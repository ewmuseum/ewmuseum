You saw it...

12 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12th May 2004 The lack of a face bore no problems for Nerezza. Since choosing to hide it behind the mask of his own hair, he’d had no issues in communicating successfully. More vocal than ever and gesturing more often than an actor with a hundred scripts thrust at him by a hundred directors, he usually had no difficulty in relaying his feelings. But staring out at the rolling waves of Sado Island – Anarchy’s “home”, as it were, he seemed lost. An observer may think he were completely mesmerized by the hues of violet in the sky or imagining himself swimming in the glistening water lit up my the sunset, he sat near the edge of a cliff, kneeling with his hands idly trailing through the grass. But something was occupying his mind… something so pressing that he didn’t mind the wind catching his hair and sweeping it from the regular position of guarding his face. “…Anarchy…” One single word uttered only in his mind. Nerezza very rarely paid attention to the people unless it was time to eat or boredom struck, meaning his interaction with females was bare bones minimal at best. But it was plain as day that back in the fun days, as he referred to them, the highlight of The Asylum was most definitely working alongside Anarchy. Even during the 26 years he spent as a human, he never quite saw the right one. But there was something about her that caught his eye. Maybe the electric blue colour that swamped her. Her clothes were always vivid and bright, creating an impression on eyes that spent most of their time either in the dark or stick behind red sunglasses that made the world monochrome to him. Failing the colour, she was never too conservatively dressed either. A skimpy bikini underneath a fishnet vest and tight vinyl pants showed off her curves… and she had quite a few to show off. And the entire package was topped off by long, slightly curled hair that bounced about whenever she moved. But that was just her visual side. Anarchy had quite a sense of humour. Every time she laughed at one of his jokes, it was like a rapture of applause to his ears from the audience of a grand concert. She didn’t recoil in horror as most did to his true face, either. To show the things that nobody else did to Nerezza – friendship beyond business – made him feel special to her. Yet he never got the chance to ask exactly how special her was to her. Akiyama had reclaimed his daughter and it had created a bitter and jealous hatred inside Nerezza, one that he kept hidden from most. But with the recent events, it had emerged and grown bigger. “She made him do things… to me… against my will. Both of them… they made me bleed, they made me cry… “ Her words echoed over and over in his head. Paranoia ran rampant. Akiyama was deluded at the best of times, with a complex of godhood clouding his mind. The condition was further amplified by his other daughter: Anarchy’s older yet identical twin, Cyclonica. She played Prophet to his God and jammed the idea even deeper into his brain. But then to the extent where their religious playtime had hurt Anarchy… The faces of all three of them flashed through the eye in his mind… Anarchy’s, sobbing tears while Akiyama and Cyclonica looked on with glee. Even those new girls, Re:plica… they were probably there. The voyeuristic spectacle Akiyama would have created would be ceremoniously portrayed, he’d want his entire following there. And then- And then a pair of arms wrapped themselves around Nerezza’s neck, followed by a chin placing itself on his shoulder. “Rezz…” A nickname long unused, sounding sweeter than ever coming from those blue lips. “Anarchy…” He wanted to scold her for being late, but he couldn’t manage to say the words. “I thought you might not have came by.” “Sorry.” Anarchy detached herself from Nerezza’s upper body and took a seat next to him on the grass. “You know… I have to tell you something. You won’t like it. Daddy’s new girls are having a math with me next week.” “Both of them? Fine. I’ll rip them apart if it puts a frown on HIS face…” Nerezza continued looking out into the waves, not budging his gaze. It was as if he was speaking with the sea instead of Anarchy. He hadn’t even turned his head to look at her yet. “It’s not a tag team match… it’ll be a handicap. But don’t worry! I’m as good in the ring as ever! Wouldn’t you say I’ve pumped up a bit?” The vampire glanced over to her and almost jumped out of his dull grey skin. “THAT COAT…!” Anarchy’s arms and body were wrapped in a fur-lined leather jacket that zipped up to her cleavage. While nobody else may have had a problem with it, especially with the display it was putting on, it struck a very bad chord inside Nerezza, for that very same coat was the one Cyclonica herself wore all the time. “Oh… yeah…” Anarchy mumbled in apology. “Sis and I still play Barbie and exchange clothes. Well, we did up until… you know… that thing. I still had her coat and it’s kinda chilly tonight. I’m sorry if I reminded you of her. It makes me ashamed to be her identical twin sometimes. Everything she did to me, it looked like I was doing it to mys-” Her words were cut short as a black-gloved hand lamped over her mouth. “mmmf?” “That’s enough… you don’t need to think about that any more.” An uneasy silence followed. Mainly because Nerezza was still looking for the right words to say to her and Anarchy was unable to speak at all. Their eyes met for a moment as the breeze carried Nerezza’s hair to one side. And Anarchy became the third person to see his face since the change…
