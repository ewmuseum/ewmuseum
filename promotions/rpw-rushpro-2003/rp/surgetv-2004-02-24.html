'ARE YOU JUST GONNA LET HER PUT HER HANDS ON ME LIKE THAT?!' Surge just stood there. He glanced between Candy and Kat, who seemed amused by the whole situation. 'Uuh...uuh....' He stuttered. Candy glared at him, awaiting a legible response. 'Well!?!' She pressed. Katarina took this as a good time to make her exit. 'I wish I could say it was nice talking to you, Ms. Dickinson. I won't trouble you anymore with my presence.' With that said, she turned towards Surge and smiled seductively. 'See you next week, Rod. Call me sometime.' She turned and walked away, disappearing around the nearest corner. Surge watched her leave in a daze, then quickly snapped out of it when Candy shook his arm. 'Did you see what that bitch did!? Why didn't you do anything!?' She screamed. Surge hesitated, then spoke. 'I guess I just froze...' Candy's eyes shot daggers in Surge's direction. He had to look away. 'That fucking BITCH!....She's gonna be sorry she did that! Come on, let's go!' Candy spun on her heels and stormed off. Surge breathed out a long sigh, then slowly turned and followed. Tension and anger still hung in the air. Around the corner, Katarina stood with a malicious smirk on her face. All was going according to plan.
