Manhunter - 2 - Lust

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: December 23rd, 2003 His eyes opened as the first rays of sunlight crashed down on his sleeping visage. He shacked off the first few seconds of confusion, remembering how much he had drank the night before. The sight of about half a dozen empty bottles of wine lingering on the side of his bed confirmed that thought, as he ran one hand through his hair, the other rubbing against his unshaven morning beard. It was too early for him, as he looked at the clock radio in front of him that displayed 11h32 in the morning, so in an effort to go back to bed, he turned to his side, and it was then that he saw her. He had not dreamt the passionate night that he had encountered after all! Wrapped in the silk covers was the naked body of Katarina Dimoni, the woman whom Belmont had met the night before, also an RPW coworker. The only thing Belmont could remember was the intensity of the past experiences. As he peered into her sleeping face, he remembered the moans and the screams that had stormed out into the air the night previous. The scenes all came back to the Serpent Prince who could not help himself from having another large urge at that said moment. As he closed is eyes, the visions of a past night appeared as clear as vision in front of him. He remembered the glow of the moon on the beads of sweat that came down from his chest and hers. The feeling of her hot skin came back to his fingers, and the taste of her lips and tongue came back to his mouth. Belmont found himself too amused sooner than later, and with only one thought on his mind. Wake her up, and drain her out. When she woke up, though, it was with a headache and body all too sore... ''Jesus,'' she grumbled when light finally caught her eyes and forced them shut again, ''Il vino è un cattivo appende sopra...'' The complaint about wine's bad hangover was in her first language naturally, because Dimoni thought she was speaking to herself...which was a mistake she didn't mind discovering when she let her head fall to one side, away from the window's light so her eyes would open. It was a pleasant, albeit confusing surprise to see the handsome face staring back, but Katarina's grin and tone both were cool and collected as she uttered a simple, English ''Hello''. The soft words reached Belmont's ears as her soft caress came towards his exposed chest. In the past, Belmont had fought the urge to fight, but now, he was fighting the urge to jump over her and repeat the previous evening. All he could do was grin knowing that all the pleasure she had given him, he had given back and then some. To say that few hours before, Belmont was trying to kick this woman out, thank god she had stayed, and thank god she had done what she did. Belmont looked at her silk covered naked body, before saying. 'Sleep well?' Katarina smiled. 'Not enough.' Belmont grinned. So did she. Both of them looked exhausted, but through the tired faces came those of relief, of ecstasy, of pure pleasure. Dimoni pushed her body up, now placing her bare back against the bed rest, and bringing the covers with her, covering her bare breast. She skewered the room with her icy eyes, spotting an open door that led to the bathroom, a place she longed to go too. The sweat had dried, and it made her feel as if a shower was needed. She got up and wrapped the covers around her before turning towards Belmont, one hand holding the covers and the other waving goodbye, winking devilishly. 'What was it that you kept saying last night?', he asked, trying to spare every moment of sight before she could get the chance to disappear into the next room. ''What?'' ''El...err...il mio...dio?'' Kat laughed lightly at both his pronounciation attempt and the question before her. ''My last name is Dimoni,'' She began, ''I was born Italian...and that means 'Oh my God'. When I'm distracted, I don't bother with English.'' Tristan was still watching her intently, though his grin grew smug and proud when she answered and then continued... ''And I don't think I'd mind keeping you around...'' The games had begun and Dimoni was playing for control. ''So for future reference...'' Her voice was almost a whisper, as if she was giving out some amazing secret though the tone was more than likely only used to reel him in further. ''Più duro is 'harder', and la fermata is a command to stop.'' Cruely teasing as she was, Katarina quickly turned and continued her trek to find the shower. It wasn't long before Belmont jumped out of bed and ran towards her, grabbing her by the arm and spinning her around before planting a kiss filled with passion upon her lips. Dimoni, trapped between the wall and Tristan Belmont, could do nothing but oblige and did so with the same intensity he offered. As Belmont's fingers caressed the Raven-haired mystery's leg, she wrapped hers around his neck. Sooner than later, however, Dimoni - manipulative and thirsty for control as she was - would beak the lip lock and walk into the bathroom, leaving Belmont alone. Few seconds passed before the sound of running water made its appearance, followed by Kat's most devilish laugh and voice... 'What are you waiting for?' Tristan Belmont grinned with satisfaction before heading himself into the steamy bathroom. -Joint effort by K&J-
