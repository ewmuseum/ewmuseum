My Quest...

19 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/19/03 I've been dealt with a burden. When I realized my goal in life is to make the world a better place for everyone else, I realized it wouldn't be an easy task. However, when one realizes he has the power to help others... it is one's duty to help out. I'd like to blame the media for the situation it has put society in. All the awful things you see on television these days have led to all the cancers of society: drug use, horrible treatment of women, and we'd be here all day if I listed all the problems in the world. It all started one day when I ran out of my supply of oven pizzas as I decided I would spend the entire day watching the entire first season of the original Star Trek. Since I required nourishment in the form of food, I had to venture out to the grocery store. As I was getting out of my car at the store I witnessed one of the most horrific things in my life... these punk teenage kids were trying to rob an old lady of her purse! I still can't say what came over me but I was blessed with a large body size and I managed to scare off these no good kids, although they required some physical resistance at first. Even though the kind lady tried to reward me with money for my good deeds, I coudln't accept... I had done it to help someone out. But I didn't imagine that actually helping another person would feel that rewarding. It was a sensation I never had felt before. For the next few weeks, I decided to dedicate all my free time into getting into better shape and training to be more physically fit. As I was deciding what path I should take in making the world I better place, I had accidently flipped past the Sci Fi channel on my television set and turned to some of the most digusting flith I had seen. It had crude treatment of women, homosexual messages, vulgar characters and even more... it was professional wrestling! Right then I understood what I had to do. Television was the negative influence I had known all along. Professional wrestling was part of the problem and I had to fix this problem. I would make my quest of ridding the world of all evil famous through the venue of professional wrestling and start by taking down all the horrible figures that children actually dare call role models that are infesting wrestling today!
