Revealed.

19 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: the day after yesterday. A bloody Peter Maxwell stands in a hospital waiting room. He's dressed well, even though just a few hours ago, he'd gone through hell at the hands of Dorn Tracer. His eyes seem distant; cold. As if he's not really there. Maxwell stands with a gauze dressing on his head, and a patch over his eye. Swelling has started under the right eye, the one that was formerly "destroyed" thanks to a car accident. He walks with a swagger, as if everything before was a lie. People sitting in the room have cleared away from him, and yet still stare openly. "Shit. This hurts." Maxwell turns to the left and looks at the large man that accompanied him to the show this evening. He stands at least a head taller than Maxwell, and is easily 3 times his size. "Hai." The man replies, simply and quietly. He removes his hat, and reveals a somewhat kind looking face. He's Japanese, and large, perhaps formerly a sumo wrestler, or something of the like. Many scars and pockmarks litter his yellow skinned face, his eyes almost completely hidden by his large apple like cheeks. "It will hurt for long time. We get back to house, I call medicine. You feel better. Sleep better." Maxwell sits on the chair behind him and sighs. "Good." After what seems like days to the blood soaked Maxwell, but in reality was only around 3 hours, a nurse walks through the large doors. "Peter Maxwell?" she calls out. Maxwell stands up again, regretting even sitting down and getting cozy, as he raises a finger, and motions to the door. The nurse smiles sympathetically and guides Peter to the triage room. He enters and again sits down. "How are you, Mister Maxwell?" The nurse asks, matter of factly. Maxwell looks at her, as if he can't believe she doesn't know who he is. He glares momentarily, and deicides it hurts too much to look evil. He resigns to the pain, and leans back in the chair. "How do I look?" "Like you just got a beating." "That's how I feel too." The nurse chuckles as she puts a blood pressure collar around the rolled up sleeve. She runs her hands along his large biceps, as she adjusts the cuffs into perfect position. "How'd this happen?" she says as she pumps the ball, listening in a stethoscope. "I'm a wrestler. Had a match, didn't work out the way I planned." "Isn't that all fake?" "Depends on who you talk to, really. Sure, some people go around in the back, and talk about what they're going to do to each other, how they're going to go about the match, but more often than not, about 2 minutes in, you forget, and you're fighting for your survival." "Mmmm-hmmm." The nurse looks at Maxwell through the tops of her eyes. She grabs a pen and writes down a few numbers, more than likely, his blood pressure. She takes the cuff off, and then inserts a thermometer into his ear. "Good looking guy like you doesn't need to be in all that mess." "Good looking guy like me isn't going to be good looking anymore if I keep winding up this way." The nurse smiles and pats Maxwell on the shoulder. For the first time in weeks, a genuine smile creeps onto his face, albeit briefly. Three hours later, Maxwell emerges from the ER, with a quite a few stitches keeping his skin together. Behind him, his driver walks stoically, looking for the car. "Anyone call?" "Dunno. I not have cell phone. It's in the car." "Ah." Maxwell says as he walks to his car. At 28 years old, he really doesn't need to have a driver, but, after nights like this, it's welcome. And since joining RPW, he doesn't need to go far week after week, a definite plus in his book. The driver opens the door to the limousine, and Maxwell creeps into the back, in definite pain. He slumps down into the seat, and looks over at his cellular phone. A single light was flashing. Maxwell picks it up and presses a few buttons. He looks at the display, and smiles a bit. He presses another button, and opens the phone, pressing it to his ear. After a few seconds he, speaks. "Maxwell. What'd you need?" Maxwell pauses as the car starts, and he rolls up the privacy screen. "Yeah. I told them you'd be there next week. I don't think Mason's too happy with it." Maxwell opens a bottle of water and takes a long drink from it. He closes his eyes as the cold beverage goes down his throat and lets out a refreshed sigh. "Well, it's something they're just going to have to live with, I suppose. He's taken everything you've given without question, and now it's time to pay the price. Right?" The car starts to move forward, and Maxwell looks out the window as the car moves away from the hospital. It's next destination is unknown, but they'll be there soon enough. He puts down his bottle of water and opens his briefcase. He pulls open his laptop, and clicks a few buttons. "Right now? He's into you for at least 3 million dollars. That doesn't even include the strings you pulled to get him the TV time, the advertising and the NASCAR deal. I'd say he's into you for a lot more." Maxwell pushes a few more buttons on the laptop and closes it. "I'm sure he'll be more than happy to see you. Be there early, and we'll see what we can do. I'll make sure I'm first out, so that Mason has the entire show to sweat it out, wondering just who the hell you are. I think the look on his face will be classic. Great. See you then…" And with a closing that will surely cause shivers to be sent down the spines over everyone, Maxwell says something that will surely be a dead giveaway to the identity of the Financial contributor. "…comrade."
