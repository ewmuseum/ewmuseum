Before Today #07 : First Impressions (Part Four)

17 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 17/01/04 'Let's see how the Princess likes slumming it, shall we?' From the confines of a stuffy closet, a calculating Magnum Wolf whispered to himself. The lens of his video-camera peeked through the slightly ajar door, with the disgruntled Private Eye monitoring every movement on the view finder, who's back-lit LCD display illuminated his moustached face. With his shoulders stooped and knees bent, Magnum ignored the aching discomfort once his target walked into view... ...That target being Karina Wolfenden. 'Slut,' the sexually repressed, having not got the hint of any action since he left FSW, Magnum sneered. The camera was filming a casual and hair-flicking K-Wolf chatting to one of PWC's backstage team, before spinning away from him as she made her breezy exit from the conversation. Seamlessly moving, fluidly between destinations, Karina was left looking at the allocation board. Despite it's less than homely appearance, the Esby Gymnasium had enough small rooms huddled inside it's walls to cater for the PWC's small, and mostly undemanding roster. Magnum Wolf knew the board very well, he'd been standing in front of it just 2 minutes before. 'Ah, Karina,' he lamented, 'they'd given you such a nice room, probably because DADDY asked them. But a top Private Eye never goes anywhere without his trusty correction fluid and pen, so I arranged a little ROOM TRANSFER.' Oblivious to Wolf's all-observing eye, Karina ran a pink and silver fingernail down the list, eventually finding hers. 'Okay,' she softly spoke to herself, as if a huge step was about to be taken, 'let's get the party started.' After slinging a brightly coloured rucksack onto her back, slipping it onto her shoulders in mesmerising fashion, Wolfenden departed, bright green eyes scanning from side to side, waiting to match room - or closet - numbers. 'Oh, don't you just look like butter wouldn't melt,' an uncomfortable Wolf hissed, easing himself out of the closet before he began to follow her. Around the corner his lens pointed, catching a rear view of Karina continuing her side-to-side looks. 'Once I catch her cry and moan about her room,' a hopeful Wolf predicted, assuming Wolfenden's reaction would fit into his plan, 'I'll show the whole locker-room what 'Little Miss High-and-mighty' is like, and she won't last another week...' As Wolf zoomed in on her, unsurprisingly focussing on the chest area - not realising that he was licking his lips over a pair of gel capsules within her bra - Karina found her magic number. Housed on a dirty white door, which was holding on for dear life to it's hinges, was the designation C11b, or as Wolfenden would call it that night; Home. 'Guess this is me,' she boldly went, walking into her new surroundings. To say that Magnum had given her the worst accommodation possible was an understatement. It was the dingiest of all the dingy rooms at not only the Esby Gymnasium, but the whole of Rowan University... And maybe even New Jersey. In fact, it wouldn't have looked out of place in a Turkish prison, although the prison would have proper drainage. 'Well,' Karina quipped, undeterred as she kicked her shoulders back to slide the rucksack straps down her arms, 'it certainly has character.' Through the view-finder, a smirking Wolf watched as she hung her rucksack from a large, protruding nail. 'But,' came optimism from Karina, 'I've crashed in worse places.' Shrugging the less than glamorous surroundings aside, Karina unzipped her bag and started to un-pack as Magnum lowered his camera and cussed under his breath. Plan A had failed, so it was time for Plan B... Wolf needed to plant the seeds of discontent himself. 'Not quite up to your usual standards?' a straight to the point Magnum asked, striding in as if he just happened to have been passing. As Wolfenden turned to face him, Magnum soured his face, finding the room's smell offensive. Taking in all of the room's desolate surroundings, he crossed his arms and shook his head. 'My, my... This surely won't do. If I were you, I'd complain, kick up a fuss!' Fixing a hair-clip into her hair, itself made into two bunches, Karina just shrugged. 'All around the world there are people living in worse places,' came Karina's passive reply as she looked around at the damp walls and cracked ceiling, 'I’m only here to change.' With a dismissive facial expression, Karina turned back around and pulled her pads from the rucksack. Taking in the more pleasurable scenery, Magnum looked her up and down her from behind, licking his lips. 'But surely, you’re not going to stand for this INSULT to your class are you?' Magnum asked, bemused. 'I’d guess they did it on purpose to ‘test the rich girl'.' After pulling on a pair of black leather fighting gloves, Karina velcroed them up as she looked back at Wolf. 'You really need to take things less seriously, ya know?' came her breezy retort, not biting Wolf's bait at all. 'There are plenty worse things than a dingy room... ...Now if you don’t mind, I need to change.' She grabbed the bottom of her t-shirt and motioned pulling it up over her head, leaving Magnum’s eyes transfixed on her before he quickly shook it off. 'Fine,' Wolf mumbled under his breath before leaving, the door slamming closed behind him... The impact causing little bits of plaster to fall off the ceiling. 'Someone is REALLY hung up on the whole 'Money Thing'...' Karina commented, giving Magnum a peculiar look as he disappeared behind the closing door. With his trouser bulge bulging a little more than usual, Magnum pulled out his camcorder once more and tried to find a hole to film her undressing through... But to no avail. 'Grrrrrrrrrrrrr...' The PWC was meant to be the re-launch of his career, but already Wolf's 'wrestling's all that matters' attitude had distracted him. While Angelo Deville, Sebastian Toomes, Jason O'Neil et al got on with the business at hand, thrusting themselves into the spotlight again, Magnum would be more concerned with his quest to rid the PWC of Karina Wolfenden. And we all know what happened there... Or don't we?
