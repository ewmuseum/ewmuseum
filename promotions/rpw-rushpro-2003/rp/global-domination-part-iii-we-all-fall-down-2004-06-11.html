Global Domination: Part III - We All. Fall. Down.

11 June 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11-06-04 “WAKE UP YOU LITTLE MOTHERFUCKER!” The booming of Hessian’s voice was more effective than any alarm clock, especially when it played on the answering machine next to the Imp’s bed. Stunned initially by the shock, Minion was quick to stop the message playing, ending it promptly and falling back to sleep in his plush custom made purple satin-laden heart-shaped bed. His stomach dropped as he heard Hessian’s voice, but sheer frustration forced him to shut the answering machine off. What he didn’t realize as he drifted back into a deep slumber was the flashing number ‘11’ on the machine. BEEP!…Minion it’s me. I’m sorry…pick up the phone. We need to talk…BEEP! … BEEP!…I can’t apologize enough. Can we meet up so we can talk about this? I feel bad enough as it is, don’t blank me man…BEEP! … BEEP!…Come on Minion pick up the phone. We can talk about a new managerial position? Don’t be pissed off because of this…Minion? Ugh, you know my number if you decide to call…BEEP! … The calls kept coming but Minion would not answer. The Global Champion sounded sincere enough, but it was too late. He could only stand so much, but being shunned as he was and expected to crawl right back was something Minion could not do. In the past month their professional relationship had deteriorated incredibly, and Hessian seemed to realize that now as he tried to get in contact with his manager…or perhaps he realized now how much more Minion could bring in the way of earnings should the two combine their skills as athlete and entertainers. Meanwhile Hessian tossed his cell phone aside, sighing disappointedly and folding his arms. He sat up and stared blankly ahead. His prolific and menacing ring attire was scattered all around the room, he lay clad in a fetching black silk robe with an Edwardian style ‘H’ on the left breast. A dazed look filled his eyes, and as his eyelids grew heavier, the Global Champion slowly slunk down under the bed sheets…and as his head hit the pillow two faces appeared next to him, two faces belonging to two very pretty girls; busty brunette and a voluptuous blonde. Hessian’s eyes grew wide with shock. “…Uhh…” The two girls giggled and wrapped an arm each around the Global Champion, snuggling into his chest and glancing up at him. Frozen, Hessian sank down into the pillows, trying to figure out why the hell two women were in bed with him. Glancing quickly at them, the Mercenary of Satan closed his eyes for a moment and drew breath. “Who…are you?” He asked, somewhat nervously. “Wow, you really were drunk last night, huh?” said the brunette in response, pulling a large empty champagne bottle up from the side of the bed, and knocking over many more smaller empty bottles of spirits. “Don’t you remember? You were flashing your cash all over the place and brought us home…” “…Whoa, wait. You’re not-…” The two girls nodded. Hessian suddenly felt exceptionally stupid and a little vulnerable. His heart was beating fast, and what man’s heart wouldn’t under such circumstances? Hessian’s fast heartbeat was due to his being nervous, far from being excited. Here he was, the Global Champion…in bed with two beautiful women. It had the makings of a generic Primetime Wrestling Academy promo, but this was Hessian. The last time he woke up next to two beautiful women they were hanging by their ankles from the rafters of his chamber, gutted like fish. Long since gone were those days when Hessian stalked the streets, slaughtering in silence and hiding in the shadows. No one ever questioned the disappearances; no one really cared enough about the street scum to bother. Though as he gained more and more notoriety in the ring, his dark rampage in the streets had to come to a halt. The chamber remained until Hessian had settled comfortably in Old School Wrestling…and then he moved. To where exactly no one knew, but it was known for definite that the Mercenary of Satan was lingering somewhere in Detroit. Finally the street scum could rest in peace. What Hessian was going through now was a complete contrast. Here he lay with two women after a night of passion and alcohol. Two women who from what Hessian could gather were ladies of the night. Certainly, Hessian had gone out and partied in celebration of his cocky prediction as to the outcome of the Global Title match. As he scanned the bedroom once more the Goliath caught sight of the Global Title itself sitting beneath a small pile of underwear in the seat of a chair. It amused him, even just slightly. For the amount of alcohol he imagined he had consumed, Hessian wondered why there was an absence of a hangover…then saw the empty pint glass with a sliver of water at the bottom. Sighing, the Global Champion prepared to make a move and get out of the situation he was unfortunately in…and as he sat at the side of the bed the two women watched on, looking slightly disappointed. “Aww…why are you leaving?” the blonde asked, pouting innocently. Hessian, putting on one of his boots, looked straight ahead as opposed to at them and thought for a moment. “I uhh…have to go, important business today. It’s not easy being the champ after all…” came Hessian’s reply. Looking to her brunette colleague, the blonde raised an eyebrow and turned back to the Mercenary of Satan. “Oh, I see. Nervous, hm? I bet Saij or Digital Mortality could handle two women at once. They’d probably enjoy it too…but it’s OK, you go on to your important business.” Hearing this, Hessian sneered. The two prostitutes folded their arms and stared at him, waiting for him to leave his own home before they did. They knew he wouldn’t however…and were equally surprised as the Global Champion kicked his boot off and leaped back between the two ladies, grinning cockily and wrapping an arm around them both. “Hail to the Champ, baby…” …Somewhere, Minion was smiling in his sleep. Paying two prostitutes to have their way with Hessian using his own money was quite amusing, but the prospect of him going one step further and then contracting an STD so close to his Global Title match was the most delightful of all. The abandonment had hit Minion so hard emotionally that he lived for Hessian’s downfall. This sick act was just another nail in the coffin. … Walking down the street, Hessian felt like a million dollars. He might as well have been earning a million dollars…but unfortunately his salary was considerably less. That however didn’t mean he wasn’t making substantially more than usual with the shiny Global Title around his waist. Today felt like a new day, and the cheerful Global Champion acknowledged passing fans, shaking hands and interacting quite happily. He was on the road to nowhere in particular, and as such Hessian roamed freely, taking in the new day. Seeing a number of picnic-style tables outside of a restaurant just ahead of him, the Mercenary of Satan grinned and approached them, taking a seat at an empty table. He waved at people as they passed by, and noticing a small group of teenagers ogling him from afar, motioned them towards him and chuckled quietly. Surely enough the group of five walked over to Hessian, seemingly fearless as they maintained a gaze towards Hessian as they approached him, as opposed to looking at the ground or around them like other nervous individuals would be. As they stood before Hessian however their confident approach quickly diminished as they mumbled greetings at the Global Champion. “Hey guys, take a seat.” Hessian said, motioning at the bench opposite him. Surely enough they sat down, looking the Global Champion up and down and trying to make sense of the situation. Perhaps they were simply overwhelmed that the PTC Global Champion had just invited them over. Of course they knew who he was; wrestling has always been and integral part of the teenage process…a common fact. “Ok. We’re going to play a little game. It won’t take too long so if you’re going anywhere I won’t hold you up.” Hessian began, the five nodded shyly in response as he put both hands on the table, one set up in an arm wrestling position and the other holding $20 for all to see. “Who so ever beats me will win $20. It’s that simple, no strings attached. Don’t worry there aren’t any TV cameras about either. Do I have any takers?” It was plainly obvious that this was no segment for Adrenaline or any kind of publicity stunt. The sole purpose of this meeting was to gain more fans and popularity. Sure it was only a group of five teenagers. But those five teenagers more than likely had one PC each. Those PCs more than likely contained at least one instant messenger service with at least thirty contact addresses and a few regularly visited forums. Hessian was about to make an impression on hundreds of people without even seeing them. “I’ll try.” The meek response came from the weediest looking teen. His arms bore little muscle; his skin was of a poor complexion. Yet as he answered the challenge by Hessian, his friends began chuckling and making snide comments. Hessian simply grinned and waited as the skinny teenager sat directly opposite him and locked hands with the Global Champion. “What’s your name, kid?” Hessian asked. “Kirk.” The boy replied. “Ok, Kirk. On your count we begin, and if you win you get the cash, deal?” The boy known as Kirk nodded and focused on his hand as it locked with Hessian’s. His friends all knew he was doing it for a laugh, after all he was probably only carrying 120lbs in weight compared to the 315lbs Hessian. As his friends watched on excitedly while Kirk slammed Hessian’s fist into the table, the Goliath tried to mask his joy, knowing he had just gained himself some good publicity. “I wasn’t ready!” yelled the champion and slamming his fist onto the table as Kirk took the $20 and got up out of his seat. “Hey you said I had to call it man! I win!” Hessian pretended to look frustrated and disappointed and gestured to the boys. “Ok, fine you win. Who’s next, what about yo-…” HONK! HONK! Hessian had put another $20 on the table, but paused as the loud obnoxious horn invaded the peace and tranquillity around him. Glancing at the street angrily, the Global Champion’s frown was turned upside down as the front passenger side window rolled down to reveal a little purple face looking back out at him and smiling. “Get in, ya big fuck!” said Minion. As another of the teenagers sat down to claim the money, Hessian jumped up out of his seat and got into the car, as driven by Minion. Taking his leave, the Mercenary of Satan ignored the fact the teenagers had just swiped $20 from him and ran off. That wasn’t important though, what was important was sitting in the driver’s seat…or rather on a stack of phone books on the driver’s seat…wearing platform shoes that barely hit the pedals. “Minion! What made you change your mind?” Hessian asked excitedly, referencing a few of his unanswered phone calls asking Minion to meet with him. “Well…” Minion said, thinking for a moment and smiling a little wider. “I guess I was just being foolish. You were right and I was wrong to do what I did and I apologize…and if you’ll have me I’d still like to manage you, or at least go under the title of manager…” Hessian didn’t have to think long about his response. “Sure! I apologize too Minion, I was just being an egotistical asshole. You can still be my manager if you want…” Hessian replied, trailing off and speaking up again while Minion looked somewhat preoccupied with his own thoughts. “…Which brings me to my Global Title match. What the fuck am I supposed to do?” Hessian looked to the floor, seemingly humiliated in asking the question. Looking equally uncomfortable, Minion sighed and swerved past an OAP at a pedestrian crossing before replying. “Yeah…well…just do what you did in the last defence, but against three-less guys. Simple! Don’t worry about the ins and outs or any of that shit…you’re the Global Champion and you’re facing two guys you’ve never heard of who haven’t been hyped in the least. What’s to be afraid of? You’ll do fine.” This brought a smile to Hessian’s face, and while he dreamt of conquests to come, Minion was busy contemplating his own situation. What was working in his little mind for all that was transpiring to do so? Glancing at Hessian, the little Imp smirked. “Yeah you got it in the bag. You’re unstoppable. You know there’s nothing anyone can say or do to defeat you Hess…the only thing people have to work with is a dominating 7’2” monster for a champion. You can’t lose!” The bullshit was on the plate. All Hessian had to do was eat it up. Judging by the cocky grin on his face…he gobbled up every last drop. “Yeah, don’t taint it Minion. Even the greatest Global Champion PTC has ever seen has to watch his back a little. Even if your opinion is infallible.” Excellent. Hessian was right where Minion wanted him. Cocky and egotistical. Just as always, only now Minion was controlling it! He could feel the championship-like glow being emitted from Hessian, happy that his long-time friend had forgiven him. All it took was an apology and Hessian was foolish enough to fall into the trap. Stalling for a while, Minion decided it was time to drop what would turn out to be a very disastrous bombshell. “Oh yeah…you better be on your best Global Champion behaviour…” “Why?” Hessian asked, confused. “…There’s a Global Title Press Conference being held today. This is your chance to prove to the world you’re a newer, bigger and better Global Champion.” Hessian grinned and nodded, going along with what Minion said. The Imp smiled, but on the inside his mind was already showing signs of tearing. Little did he know that his actions would put Hessian on the road to ruin. Little did he know he had more than likely just sealed the fate of the PTC Global Champion. The End.
