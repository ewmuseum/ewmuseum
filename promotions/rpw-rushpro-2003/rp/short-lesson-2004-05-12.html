Short lesson...

12 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Most people never took as to look into the direction of Mt. Donden on Sadogashima… that island just resonated with foreboding feelings and presences of darkness. It was there that the fabled god lived… and from his peak he struck terror down on the peasants who defied him. That is no more… the days of lore are over. In our society these days, we demand fact over fiction, we're not a people for fairy tales. Well… some of those bedtime stories you heard as a child are more real than you could ever imagine. Akiyama, on the other hand, he was the next generation folklore. The new breed of monster, shake those notions of gnarly beasts with fangs and hideous forms and presences out of your head. Today's monster was the thinking monster, the monster that took your fears, the things deep inside you that you keep only to yourself, and make them real. He brings them to life only to take yours. And Akiyama… he was that psychological demon that people had grown to fear and hate before… but become terrified of soon to come. Not many people trekked upon Mt. Donden, as it was considered either sacred or cursed… people didn't care to speak of it over the years. Only a few beings go up there, and those were the people that lived there. That of course was Akiyama, and they who followed him. “Again.” The rich, deep voice of Akiyama could be heard from anywhere within the castle. The 7 foot behemoth stood in the center of the courtyard on that sunny day, dressed in black wide leg skirted pants traditional to Japanese culture, and split-toed socks, nude from the waist up. He stood tall, hair falling about his face as his arms were stretched down at his sides, palms slightly open as he stared forward with an emotionless expression. Across from him stood Re:plica; Kaori and Kaoru Kusanagi in fighting stances glaring back at their master. While Akiyama wore little clothing, they were covered near head to toe; thick black baggy pants with heavy black boots, black gloves and a what appeared to be a lead or Kevlar vest on, They moved at Akiyama, speed walking surprisingly in all their equipment. Kaori came first, slinging her foot through the air and landing it against Akiyama's shoulder with a heavy thud. He stood there, watching as she rotated and brought the heel of her left foot around into the same point in his shoulder before ducking and moving to the side… where her twin moved into position. Kaoru rushed in, hopping up and hammering both of her elbows into the center of Akiyama's chest. She moved by her sister, the twins in range of Akiyama, and unloaded with slinging fists at his chest, ribs, and neck. They slung them more than swung because of the heaviness of the gloves they wore, weighted at the wrists and hands. Their hands clubbed into his body audibly like beating against a wall. They seemed to be doing a good job… appearances were often misleading, as the saying somewhat said. Akiyama grabbed them both by their necks and lifted them off their feet, holding them at face level giving them a stern look. “Stop being lazy. Do it.” They kicked their legs out as they slammed their arms into his wrists, trying to break free. Their kicks went straight for his face, his shoulders buffering most of the side kicks while he took shots to his neck, chin, and face head on. They didn't look to phase him much as he continued to watch and wait for something from them. Kaoru glanced over at Kaori with gritted teeth, her sister gasping for breath in the clutches of their teacher. Together they kicked into his windpipe, and followed on time with a slinging boot underneath the chin. Akiyama released them as his head rocked back, and Re:plica flipped back in the air and landed on their feet at the same time. Akiyama kept his position balanced, but he brought his chin back down glaring at his students, a slight smile on his face. He motioned for the twins to rush at him again, and Kaoru was the first to comply. She bolted at him, jumping up into the air and twisting around with an elbow at Akiyama's chest. Akiyama sidestepped the elbow and smashed his palm into Kaoru's chest, sending the girl rolling across the ground. As soon as he struck, a hard boot came cracking into his jaw from Kaori, Kaoru the distraction. Taking the hit, Aki did what came natural to him… he reached underneath her kicking leg and grabbed her by the throat, growling as he lifted her into the air with one arm and plowing her into the dirt. Kaoru looked up through strained eyes and saw her sister flat on the ground, folded legs over head from the Absolute Destiny Apocalypse. Standing up slowly, she walked over to her sister, watching her teacher lay her flat on the ground and stand over at her side. “Improvement, I guess,” he muttered, rubbing his jaw tenderly. “You've earned your gift for the day.” Akiyama looked over at Kaoru, who was still standing. She looked surprised at her teacher at the stern look he gave her. “You… you know where your result lies…” Kaoru's head lowered in defeat as Akiyama walked past her into the castle. With a smile, he walked up the stairwell towards a pair of oak double doors. Pushing one open with a creak, he closed it behind him and looked into the shady room, sunlight only peeking in from the window at the far right of the room near the master bed. “What do you think?” “I'll have fun punishing her tonight,” came the response. Akiyama shook his head, walking further into the room. “No, I mean their progress. I know Kaoru will have just as much fun as you will… though sometimes you tend to take things too far.” “Nonsense,” a wisp of smoke trailed out from the person, smoking a cigarette assumingly. “The twins are doing well, their speed increases day by day with your training, as well as their endurance and toughness.” Akiyama walked over to a table near the window and poured himself a glass of a clear red liquid, sipping it slowly. “They'll be as good as you soon to come, hopefully.” A scoff came from the woman. “Again, nonsense. There is nothing as good as me.” “Come now, dear daughter… you think too highly of yourself.” From the darkness a long leg uncrossed from the chair she sat in, standing on bare feet with red silk skirts flowing around her long limbs. Soon, Cyclonica came into the light, her red robe drawn at her waist and loose around her neck revealing cleavage. Her black hair was slicked back, her cold vision of beauty glaring at her father. “I… think too highly of myself? I, who has lowered herself to a mere servant before her own father who believes himself above humanity itself? Please, let us not get ahead of ourselves.” Akiyama lowered his glass from his lips slowly. “I'm just saying…” “You say too much.” Cyclonica took the glass right out of her father's hand and sipped its contents. Akiyama himself stared at his daughter, somewhat taken back by her pompous behavior. “What… do you make of Anarchy then, hmm? I think she says too much.” Cyclonica lowered her glass, staring at her father with widened eyes. “Anarchy says what she believes is the truth. You can't hold her fault to what she believes is the truth.” “And just what is the truth, this truth behind her truth?” “Simple,” finishing off the glass, Cyclonica poured another from the crystal wine bottle. “If you believe in something, even if it's fiction, then it becomes a fact to that person. In Anarchy's case, she believes we had our way with her… so it becomes a truth though it could very well be a false. In any case, her faith in the fact makes it real… so we're very much guilty of it despite if we didn't do it or not.” “But we cannot be guilty for crimes not committed.” Akiyama shot back. “Of course we can. If a person believes she is a victim so strongly then she will become a victim of her own doing… and finger the criminals as she sees fit. It's in the same principle that if one believes he's a deity so strongly, then that person will make himself one at all costs… though I have no idea how many times you've failed the task.” Akiyama didn't take too kindly to that remark, shooting his own glare out at her. Cyc smirked at her father, setting the glass down on the table and sauntering towards him. She stood close to him, chest to chest looking up into his piercing eyes. She looked down his frame before looking back up into his face. “… I'm naughty… aren't I, daddy?” “You need to be punished…I believe.” He shot back softly. She smiled backing up slowly from him. “No… Anarchy needs to be punished. Once our plans fall into play, you'll have your daughter back here inside the castle safely… and we can begin our games again.” She walked into the next room and slid the door behind her, Akiyama watching her walk away the entire time. He smiled at Cyclonica, a slow nod coming from him. “It'll be my dear daughter to pay in the end for her foolish beliefs… you are right as always, my Divine Prophetess…”
