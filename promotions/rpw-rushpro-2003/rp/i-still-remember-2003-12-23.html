I Still Remember....

23 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/23/03 Never before had twenty four hours seem to bring such a change of mind and place. Now instead of being strapped into the passenger seat of the 2000 Chevy Camero belonging to Chris Storm wondering how the hell I was going to get away from him, I found myself sitting on Chris’ living room couch reminiscing the past and wondering if it could ever be brought back. Ever since we arrived at his New York City apartment Chris had been a gentleman, whether it was to play mind games with me or because he really meant it I wasn’t sure. All I was sure of was that I was falling in love with him all over again without wanting to. “I wonder if he still remembers how happy we used to be together.” I whispered to myself as I dug out a picture of the two of us back in PCW after capturing the tag titles for our first time, when it seemed nothing could tear us apart. Apparently nothing was quite a powerful force though and did the impossible. I tossed the photo aside and sighed as I sank back in the couch, realizing that we would never be as happy as we used to be. However the power possessed in that single wallet sized drew me back to it. I looked down at it and had to allow the tears to fall from my cheeks. Tears weren’t the things I needed to calm myself though, what I needed was a pen which conveniently enough for me I found in my purse. Without hesitation, I began to scribble with the dark pink gel pen the words that came to mind as I continued to stare at the photo which haunted me with memories of the past. I still remember when this all started out. It was back in WIW where just like now we couldn’t stand each other. Tables. cakes, and hell is what you put me though and yet there was still something about you that wowed me. I knew somewhere inside of you there was a heart that felt the same way about me. It took an interfed match over at DWO where we got our asses handed to us for you to find it and several attempted beat downs on me for you to find it. Yet at Retribution, you found it and could not deny it, so began the relationship between the two later on to be dubbed “The Schmoopies.”. Who can forget about the closing of WIW? Even if you denied it I knew you were quite upset. Yet from there we managed to find ourselves a new career over in PCW where in just a few months we became tag champions. Sure we took beat down after beat down to get there but it was worth it just to hold the belts with you, no matter what you believe. I know you believe otherwise. You think that I only cared for you because of those belts, but that’s not true. I have all the proof in the world you need of that. Just look at my answer in Paris. Don’t you think if that was true I would have told you that a bond between us two wouldn’t work. And don’t you think that if I didn’t care for you I would have just accepted the Green surname instead of getting my ass kicked at Spring Fling just to try to get things back to the way they should have been. Regardless of what you think, I didn’t lose Spring Fling on purpose. It’s too bad you didn’t give us a chance after Spring Fling. Instead you took off to find yourself a home in DWO. I suppose that’s why no matter how badly you got your ass kicked over there all I could do is laugh. Then again being a fool in love I gave you yet another chance when we went up against the guys of FAW. You stepped into my heart yet again and you stayed in it until you lost. It wasn’t your elimination from the match that bothered me though. It was the way you acted after the match was over. Instead of being proud of me for capturing the win for Jacko and us, you stormed off without a word never to be seen or heard from again until RPW came around. Then again if I knew you were going to hook yourself up with someone as sleazy as Alyssa I wouldn’t have even bothered to grace you with my presence….but then again maybe I would. I can still see you care, no matter what you say. I saw it in your eyes the entire trip here. I knew you wanted to be the old Chris, but you wouldn’t allow yourself to because you don’t want to let down that sleazebag. That’s okay Chris, you can go on and find yourself a new life with her. Just know it will never be as happy as ours was before you left. Love…well not really, Tessa I tossed both the pen and photo aside once more before wiping a tear away. I didn’t want to just leave them there but there was no way in hell I’d be able to approach him about this on my own. So instead I nuzzled myself up under the blanket folded up on the couch and fell asleep, doing my best to keep the Chris I used to know out of my mind.
