Breathe.

22 February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: today. Breathing. It’s amazing what you think about when that’s all you can really do. Breathe in…Good. Breathe out…Good. Look at me. I’m breathing. I’m alive. There’s oxygen filling my lungs, absorbing through my bloodstream, and sending it’s sweet, life giving fuel to the rest of my body. Excuse me… Breathe in… Breathe out. That’s better. Comas aren’t so bad. I don’t have to worry about taking a shit, I don’t need to take a bath, and I’ve got a hot nurse that comes in my room once a day to wash me down. It’s not all that bad. Plus, locked inside my head, I don’t have to worry about much. The only thing I need to remember? Breathe in… And exhale… I’m not alone either. I’ve got my family here in my memories. Sure, I can’t touch them, but at least it’s something. I keep thinking over and over about my family, and how much I miss them. I can’t believe it’s been so long. Three years now since they were taken from me. I can see my daughter growing up to become one hell of a little lady. And my wife is still just as breathtaking as she was three years ago. Breathe in… Exhale… Not so breathtaking that I don’t remember to breathe. I see my father, who died when I was young. I see Cara, my doctor killed by one of her patients. I see Angelo, one of my best friends in the business. We’re all here. We’re all smiling. We’re all breathing. Breathe in… Breathe out… But there’s someone here that shouldn’t be here. There’s one face I see that I don’t want to see again. Peter Maxwell. Every time I think about my wife, there he is, soiling her. He’s on top of her, making love to her in all the places and all the ways I used to. He’s playing with my daughter, hugging her, and telling her he loves her. He’s playing catch with my father. He’s the one crying, being held by Dr. Cara. And he’s the one who took the breath away from Angelo. Breathe in… Breathe out… And everytime, he looks back at me, knowing I’m there. His crooked smile fixed upon his crooked face taunts me. He dares me to do something. He’s challenging me, even in my thoughts. With every thrust inside my wife, he challenges me to do something about it. And yet, I’m powerless to stop him. Here, inside my head, I’m inferior to him. He’s out there, walking around. He’s telling people that it wasn’t his fault. That it was all an accident. That it wasn’t supposed to happen. That… He… Wasn’t… … Breathe in… Breathe out… …ahh. That’s better. That everything that happened to me was the fault of some kid in charge of props. Maybe he’s right. Maybe I should forgive him. That’s what the Lord wants me to do. That’s what Jesus, and the Holy Spirit want me to do. “Forgive him, Jason. For he knows not what sin he commits for he has not cometh to the Lord,” they tell me. Breathe in… Breathe out… Maybe they’re right. I put myself in the position. I had to make sure Roberts didn’t get hurt. He doesn’t know better. Honor, it seems means everything to him. Maxwell showed no honor, going after Roberts with that wrench. Breathe in… Breathe out… Roberts walks now, taking up the cause that I cannot. “Vengeance is mine, sayeth the Lord” has never rang more true. Since I’m in service to the Lord, vengeance will be mine. Because when you have nothing to do but breathe… Breathe in… Breathe out… You’ve got plenty of time to think about revenge.
