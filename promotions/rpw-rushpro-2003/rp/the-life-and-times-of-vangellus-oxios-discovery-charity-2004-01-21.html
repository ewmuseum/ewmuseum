The Life And Times of Vangellus Oxios: Discovery/Charity

21 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/21/04 'OFF THE AIR?!' So we open in the temporary Van World Order headquarters. Where is that, exactly? In the simple one bedroom, one bathroom apartment located in Belmont, North Carolina. The town isn't that large, or drastically important,. which is exactly why Vangellus Oxios selected it as his US residence in the United States. He maintains his permanent residence in Crete, as his mother and father, now retired, are living at his estate. But for his time in RPW, Vangellus has chosen to take up a living space closer to his 'home arena'. After all, every RPW show is held at the Halton Arena, save for the occasional tour. In large part, it is a home-based regional federation. With the level of star power, it can be a great federation on the national scale. A business-incompetent 16-year-old owner and Magnum Wolf forcing himself into a booking position, and Peter Maxwell being nominated for a higher leadership role by Chris Storm (because everyone takes Storm seriously, and Maxwell's injuring of Gideon Poe is a great way to prove that he can run a company) aren't helping things, most likely keeping RPW as a good independent wrestling federation with a loyal cult following in the way that ECW was, or that TNA:NWA, MLW, or ROH are now. Oxios hasn't even had time to furnish the third-floor apartment in Belmont. For now, there is just a computer on the floor, a fax machine, a television in the corner of the room, and the kitchen with the standard appliances. The washing machine and dryer are down on the first floor in the corner of the building, coin-operated. As he stared out through the closed horizontal draw blinds at the overcast sky and drab surroundings of equally unimpressive aging-brick wall buildings full of single room apartments that probably weren't any nicer than his place, he burst out the beginning comment, not believing what he had heard. 'What do you mean they're off the air?' 'Simple, you know how they used to have their shows played on television? They don't do that anymore.' Adrienne was a bit more dressed down in the colors of the Irish; that's right, she received her Bachelor's degree in English from Notre Dame. She floundered in Phillip-Morris's advertising department before being released and subsequently hired by Vangellus Oxios. Her glorified title doesn't really change that she's little more than his agent. Her plain, matter-of-factly way of speaking was one of the reasons why Oxios hired her. He's got enough people to kiss his ass and tell him that he can do no wrong, and basically be a yes-man or yes-woman to him. He needs people to treat him like a normal person. It keeps him as grounded as possible for being so brash. Of course, this was a time where she needed to be a little less literal. 'And exactly how and why did this happen, O wise one?' Vangellus asked, his eyes still locked firmly on the building across of the street, where there was apparently a couple arguing in front of an open a window. An older man and a younger woman, most likely caught up in a relationship where he needed a trophy, she needed financial support, and both of them needed security that neither could provide. Harold broke in, his wild, untamed hair brushing the large Darcellus across his broad nose as he passed, making the big man sneeze. It was either that, or the pepper that he was scattering over his bowl of chicken-flavored Ramen noodles. 'Didn't you actually watch the show? Or at least catch the news on the RPW resource site afterward? Perhaps had you taken a moment to read the memo that Little Al sent to all the workers?' Turning back to his computer nerd, Vangellus said, 'I think it's obvious that I did NOT.' Harold was responsible for gathering information; any information that Vangellus needed, available to the public or otherwise. A 56-year-old hacker from Blowing Rock, North Carolina, he knew just about everything that there was to know about computers. But having grown up in a time where ever-changing slang and colloquial speech wasn't as much the norm as it is now, he can communicate things in layman’s terms, and doesn't make people feel stupid for not knowing what he knows. 'El Diablo happened. He failed to win the five-man World Title match at the Rush Pro-Wrestling pay-per-view Genesis. Since Gideon Poe won, and since he's had a great deal of mental problems that have gone untreated for so long, he finally snapped and attacked Poe's bandmates, who showed up to support him.' 'Bandmates?' Oxios said, going back to the window, finding the action on the other side of the road much more interesting. Harold nodded, sitting Indian-style in front of the Microsoft computer. Right away, he gulped down a hearty portion of noodles. 'Poe's in a band called Cadaver Theory. They're not bad, I'll loan you my CD sometime.' Adrienne interjected on a tangent. 'You don't seem like you'd be the type to listen to their music.' 'What?' Harold replied, shrugging his shoulders nonchalantly. 'It passes time on the net much better than Tony Bennett.' His attention going back to filling in Vangellus on the situation, he continued. 'There was a lot of violence involved, needless to say. The result, aside from two Cadaver Theory members physically unable to perform for the next few months, and at least one more perhaps mentally unable to perform the band is pretty much on hiatus. That notwithstanding, however, Fox Sports Network, which was allowing RPW to play its programming live on the broadcast channel, dropped them from their programming slot. That action was deemed to hardcore for their network by the executives.' 'Yes, that is FSN's official position on it.' Adrienne Diamond was herself a very knowledgeable person. Having a managerial role in Vangellus's career, it was also her business to know about these issues. And unlike Harold, she wasn't the one sitting in front of a computer all day. 'But in all common sense, the isolated incident carried out by El Diablo, which was actually cut off by rather judicious RPW-employed cameramen rather than the executives cutting the switch on our programming even if Frank Miner approved the segment in the first place, wasn't the real issue at hand. Fine, Diaz and Miner went nuts and forgot about that whole “standards and practices” thing that governs the smaller federations; it happens. What would be necessary is a simple censure or firing of the person at fault, and then everyone could get on with their lives. In fact, they did fire Miner, I believe. No one would blame the RPW for Diaz's actions.' Now having to take a more active interest in what was going on, Vangellus turned away from the window, allowing the blinds to go back to their undisturbed positions. Leaning against the wall near the windowsill, he crossed his arms over his red sweater. 'Then what's the real issue? Were they worried about a recovering drug-addict on the show?' 'No, Van, it's not you.' Adrienne always had to make it clear that Vangellus's addiction to painkillers following his motorcycle accident was not something that people were blaming him for. He was taking the steps to deal with it, quitting cold turkey on his own without dealing with any of those rehab programs for sissies, and people were commending him for it. At least those people who knew about him. 'The real problems -- plural -- for FSN were mainly the Eclipse Title match and Invincible Gabe.' Vangellus upturned his brow. 'Explain.' At this point, the young Teshigahara Emi-Sachiko, or Sachi, entered into the conversation. She and Darcellus had been standing at either side of the door, being quiet. They were security, and they were doing their jobs by guarding the door. Whereas Darcellus simply didn't care about any of the information that was being shared, and therefore couldn't give much useful lines in the conversation, Sachi was more the quiet type. But she gave her two cents, lending her understanding of the situation at hand. 'The Eclipse Title match was not a wrestling match, so much as a fairly hardcore battle between a scarred and unsettled man and a young girl. And the people ate it up. Fox Sports Network is trying to maintain a wholesome image: retaining a show that not only would put on such a physically punishing match, but that it would be supported by a bloodthirsty mob, does little to promote that image.' 'Not only that,' Adrienne added, 'but this Invincible Gabe has language that would only pass in a Jay and Silent Bob movie; possibly for a day or two each year on Comedy Central from the midnight to 3 AM timeslot. He curses too much, using the free broadcasting forbidden words as though they were normal euphemistic adjectives like 'darn' and 'heck'. RPW was live as of last week, shown at a time during the week when there are young children at home that are up at the time of night to catch the show. They heard every uncensored word, and you can be sure that any number of interest groups were contacting network executives, congressmen, and lobbyists to get RPW off of the air.' Shaking his head, Oxios pushed off of the wall and threw his hands up into the air. 'This is great, the moment I get a wrestling job and make my triumphant return to the world of physical fighting competition and the place shuts down.' He had a little bit of a victim mentality. Everything happened to him, rather than independently of him. This wasn't even the bad thing in this instance, he was blowing the whole thing way out of proportion. 'RPW is by on means shutting down, Van.' Harold finished another bit of his noodles. 'They just won't be playing anymore shows on television until they get another television deal. But between Diablo, The Eclipse Title, which might indefinitely keep the rules like we've seen this past weekend, and foul-mouthed wrestlers, plus getting dropped by a network that is famous for playing nearly anything and everything that it comes across, don't expect them to be getting a whole slew of deals right off the bat.' Sachi chimed in with, 'For now, they'll be stuck to making Adrenaline a house show.' Adrienne had a bottle of Dasani in her hands, and made an 'Mph,' sound with the raising of her left index finger to get the attention during the very brief lull in the conversation. Vangellus used to endorse Dasani water, until he actually tried a bottle and realized that it takes like clear liquid chalk. After that, he backed out of the endorsement... when the contract expired. Swallowing the gulp of water, Ms. Diamond said, 'Don't worry, though. This is very good for you.' 'How? All my SHOmaniacs won't be able to see me on Adrenaline every weekend...' Coming to his own epiphany, Van quickly changed his tune. 'Wait a minute. I own any footage of myself, even RPW footage. I have a show on the primetime slot on the E! network and in syndication on Comedy Central. I've got a website that also plays snippets of footage from The Life and Times of Vangellus Oxios. Even if RPW is off the air, I'll still be seen all over the world. I don't even have to care.' 'Yes, you do. But for different reasons.' Adrienne was very business-savvy, and she knew a great opportunity before anyone else saw it. That was why Vangellus hired her; he doesn't know everything, or recognize the things that she does. He's not the smartest guy, so he surrounds himself with people to make up for his own lack of information or attention span. 'Right now, who is the only RPW wrestler who will get consistent airtime on television? Who controls the RPW airtime? Who is the one person that the people can go to in order to receive actual footage from RPW events?' Vangellus thrust his arms triumphantly into the air, a large smile across his face. 'ME!' Adrienne nodded. 'Exactly. If you look at it in the proper way, you are the face of RPW, even more than Wolfenden or Gideon Poe. You're the one that the world will be seeing if they see any new RPW footage. For as long as it takes the federation to procure a new television deal, you are the RPW. And do you know what that means for you?' 'Yes!' After that exuberant affirmation, Vangellus lowered his arms. 'No....' Darcellus merely smirked, shaking his head. His usual stance wasn't broken, that of his right hand folded over his left, arms down in front of him, with his legs wide so that his feet line up with his shoulders. It was a simple, yet intimidating stance. Adrienne rolled her eyes. 'It means that you donate your cameras, television deal, and airtime to the federation until they can get back on their feet.' And with his trademark taking of Adrienne's ideas and kicking it up a notch to a more unreasonable proposition, Vangellus said, 'OR, I can rent out airtime to RPW on a temporary basis. I'm sure that they've got a little bit of money saved away.' Darcellus, finally adding his thoughts, said, 'Man, it's just another drop in the bucket. You got an image to keep; give away the airtime and it'll look better for you.' 'If you have to think in such terms,' Sachi broke in, 'who wouldn't want as a product endorser a man who single-handedly loans his company a television deal until they get their own? You'd look like a hero, the company will look even more highly upon you, and the contracts will roll in faster than they do already. You don't get any money in the short-term, but in the long-term, it's a much better and more fulfilling decision.' Harold agreed with a nod. 'They're right, Van.' Bombarded on all sides, Vangellus conceded. 'Okay, I'll give them some airtime. But not yet. I'll let them flounder for a week or so before I swoop in to save the day. In the meantime, if the wrestlers want to have any of those 'promos' on the show video-recorded, I'll charge them to use the Van World Order camera crew.' The Van World Order is Vangellus Oxios's official company. It covers all of the events and activities in which Vangellus participates. Adrienne is the head of the Van World Order, which has no actual base of operations. In addition to Jason, it employs an 8-person camera crew in order to have two on call and six on standby at all times. 'If that's the way that you have to do it.' Adrienne knew that once Vangellus set his mind to something, that's what he does. There'd be no getting him to make any more concessions, that's what they'd be doing. 'Don't wait until it's too late. Let the first show happen, and make your announcement to rent cameras to the wrestlers on the show. You're allowing it to them for promos and segments on the show, but you don't want that to cover matches, because if you rent cameras for matches, that makes you look bad. The backstage stuff is extra, so it doesn't matter to your image if you rent them that time.' 'Still,' Harold came in, 'offer the renting of time for segments for the first show. After that show, offer to give them airtime for the second show. It may end up that they don't even take the offers; the mere fact that you made the offer will look good for you.' Nodding, Oxios snapped his fingers. He does that when he's assured of a good thing. 'Got it. Who's hungry?' Harold raised his hand. 'You don't count, you moocher. You just ate.' 'I'm on a reverse Atkins diet,' Harold responded. Vangellus shook his head. 'Anyone else hungry?' Darcellus said, 'I can eat.' Sachi remained quiet, but she would go along. Adrienne nodded. Vangellus motioned toward the door with a waving of his hands. 'Let's go then.'
