All Falls Down...

30 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/30/04 I hurt myself today To see if I still feel I focus on the pain The only thing that's real The needle tears a hold The old familiar sting Try to kill it all away But I remember everything It had been nearly 3 years now, and Aaron Roberts could only think of the first day they'd met. He could only think of the way he had looked at her. The way their eyes had locked with one another. Aaron had always had trouble talking to women, and this case was no different. There was a crass and sexist joke. There were tires screeching. There was Magnum left standing with his jaw on the ground as Karina Wolfenden took her first steps into his life by ribbing him right back. It had seemed so innocent at first, and Wolf had tried to talk to her later backstage... but this vixen... this HUSSY... she just wouldn't have it. This was when Aaron Roberts began to hurt himself... What have I become My sweetest friend Everyone I know goes away In the end And you could have it all My empire of dirt I will let you down I will make you hurt Magnum Wolf wasn't the evil man he is today when he first came to PWC. Not hardly, in fact. If you go back to Five Star Wrestling tapes, you see a light-hearted and fun loving guy using dildos as telephones, and pretending to be a private eye. The friends he made in Five Star didn't take well to the man Wolf had become. Suddenly, all he could do was talk about Karina Wolfenden. About getting rid of her. It was like some kind of addiction, something Wolf had never had an issue with in the past. It's fair to mention that Wolf began this transformation sometime before he came to RPW. The Old School Fighting incident began his transformation... not within himself, but to everyone else. Angelo Deville begged off from Wolf, not wanting to be involved with him. Jason O'Neil left OSF with Wolf on the grounds of unfair termination, but even he tried to keep his distance from Magnum. No one wanted to catch the cancer that was Magnum Wolf. Look what it did for Karina. She was involved in a HOUSE SHOW with Wolf, and found herself fired from OSF. Magnum was alone, once again. Just as he was when he stepped into the world of Wrestling Tattler in the first place. I wear this crown of thorns Upon my liar's chair Full of broken thoughts I cannot repair Beneath the stains of time The feelings disappear You are someone else I am still right here Wolf soon began to blame everyone else for his problems. His blame game went to Ron Starr, to Paul Orion, to Ivan Stanislav, to the chumps he feuded with in OSF, and had now found it's original resting place on the shoulders of Karina Wolfenden. The critics had long lauded Wolf as the next big thing. A God Among Wrestlers. The critics had been shut up for quite sometime because Wolf had done nothing in his short career in the 'big time'. It went from an addiction with Karina, straight to an obssession. Everything else didn't matter to him. World Titles, big promotions, nothing mattered. He just wanted Karina Wolfenden to pay. To pay for what, not even Wolf could remember at this point. He started to get desperate. He started to do things completely out of character for the man that we met first in Extreme Online Wrestling in the indies. He couldn't even remember what had caused the feud. He couldn't remember why he hated Karina Wolfenden, whom he'd actually come to like over time, but he was too deep in this to let it go now. Magnum Wolf's friends were mostly gone now. Angelo Deville was dead. Jason O'Neil was a headliner in RPW. Politically Incorrect had left the business. Everyone was either moving up, or moving out... but Magnum wasn't. He was stuck. Right where he started. If I could start again A million miles away I would keep myself I would find a way But there is no chance to start again. The only chance is to end this for good.
