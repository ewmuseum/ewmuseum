Before Today #06 : First Impressions (Part Three)

16 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 16/01/04 Despite only having walked through the door 5 minutes ago, Magnum Wolf was not enjoying PWC one bit. They promised he'd be one of the stars, that he'd get a long-deserved push... But so far nobody had paid any notice to him, almost shoving him aside so they attend to others. Already disgruntled from his little run-in with a certain Miss Wolfenden, the facilities backstage at the Esby Gymnasium did little to raise his spirits. 'Ugh, what a day,' he tiredly scoffed, his trip to New Jersey resembling parts of Planes, Trains & Automobiles. 'All this and the pay-off will barely cover dinner.' His little area of the gymnasium was a dank and dreary 16 square feet, encased by bluey-green walls who's paint was peeling off... Damp releasing it from the plaster. Even the light, one single bulb hanging from it's wires, didn't seem bright enough to light the cramped, muggy room... Who's only furniture was a chipped wooden bench, and 2 loose clothes hooks. 'Welcome to the Indies, Magz,' he sighed, dropping his travel bag onto the bench... Then wincing as he heard it squelch, knowing that it was made by his clothes, and that there wasn't a radiator in sight to dry them on. 'Remember... This is just a stepping stone. Make your mark, get yourself known, then get the HELL outta here for the big pay-day.' Wolf then unceremoniously unzipped his bag and ripped the damp clothes out. 'Son of a BITCH!' he then exclaimed, seeing that his prized FSW t-shirt was soaked through with dirty puddle water, leaving it with a fine glitter of dirt. 'I bet SHE's got a nice shiny leather bag that DADDY bought for her to go with that bike,' a snarling Wolf hissed under his breath, 'spoiled brat.' With all his gear finally evacuated from the cheap, tatty bag, Wolf went to hang his shirt up to dry... Only for it's sodden weight to pull the hook from it's crumbling plaster, sending the shirt down onto the dusty floor. Wolf's face was turning redder and redder, while his fists clenched so tightly they began to ache. To get through it all, Wolf needed a scapegoat, he needed to feel better about himself... ...And the only way Wolf knew how to do that was to tear down others. 'I’ll give her two weeks before she decides she’s better off sponging from Mr & Mrs Whatever,' he seethed, carefully hanging another shirt on the one remaining hook, slowly and carefully, as if he was playing Operation. 'Wolfenden, actually,' came a familiar voice from behind him, immediately making Magz cringe. Standing in the doorway to his private little area was Karina Wolfenden, already changed into her wrestling gear and looking bright and perky... A complete contrast to the fallen former-FSW star. Shaking off the cringing look, Wolf then forced a false smile to adorn his face, turning around to face the girl he immediately loathed with a 'WOW! Great to see you again!' look. Having discarded her leathers, Karina was decked out in a Luscious Jackson t-shirt, paired with green-tinted, baggy worker jeans... In the pockets of which her hands were casually planted. Leaning against the door frame, she noticed his bag dripping murky water down from the bench, forming a puddle on the cracked floor. 'Bummer,' she quipped, 'might want to get a new bag.' A silently seething Magnum became even more tense, her casual remark winding him up further. His career, the journey, and now the conditions getting on top of him. 'Are you okay?' a genuinely concerned, yet a little confused, K-Wolf asked. 'Don't get yourself worked up, it's only wrestling, no big.' Powder keg, meet lit fuse. Lit fuse, meet powder keg. 'Only? ONLY?!?!' a frustrated Wolf howled in disbelief. 'Only Wrestling? ONLY wrestling ONLY puts food on my table, and ONLY pays the bills.' Wolf was putting his stress to good use while ranting, ringing a pair of sweatpants dry with unrelenting, tight twists. His outburst drew a peculiar gaze from Karina, who didn't seem too moved by his outburst. 'Take a chill pill,' she urged, shrugging. 'Too much stress'll kill ya.' It was fair to say her relaxed attitude wasn't rubbing off on the disgruntled Magnum Wolf. 'Stress? OF COURSE I'M STRESSED!' a wide-eyed Wolf yelled to the heavens... Or rather the peeling roof of his changing area, turning brown with damp. 'My gear is ruined, my car is stuck on a lay-by somewhere up the road, and to top it all off, I haven’t had a client in the last 2 weeks!' Choosing to ignore the rest of his words, a bemused Kari pondered the final part. 'You're a Gigolo?' 'Ye-NO!' the part-time Private Investigator then quickly corrected himself. Raising a pierced eyebrow as she looked his dodgily dressed body up and down, Karina gave him a sly look the screamed 'Yeahhhhhhh, sure.' Having had her fun with the downtrodden Wolf, Karina decided she'd better fly before steam started to blow from his ears. 'Anyway, it was, ummmm... Good meeting you,' she excused herself with, 'I'm off to see a few more of the guys, so, see you around.' 'Oh, and remember,' she added, stopping to spin around in the doorway. 'Reeeeeeelax,' a chirpy K-Wolf then advised, before bounding out of sight. With the sound of her footsteps heard skipping off down the corridor, Wolf was left to scowl. 'Damn little trust-fund baby,' he bitterly mumbled to himself. 'I bet she's never had to work a day in her life... Probably thinks my damn JOB is a hobby.' From the damp pile of gear on the bench, Wolf salvaged his wrestling boots, relieved that they were left dry as he stood them up on the uneven surface. 'Well, girlie,' Magz continued to seethe, 'if you want to play at being a wrestler, that's fine... But Magnum Wolf is now ON THE CASE!' His eyes then lit up as a plastic bag was salvaged from the pile of wet clothes. 'Ah, it's safe.' Magnum unravelled the bag to reveal his trusty camcorder, miniaturised to the size of a normal camera. 'I'm sure everyone in PWC will LOVE to see what you're REALLY like,' he beamed, a sinister sparkle in his eye. 'Let's see how the little Princess REALLY likes slumming it.' After taking off the lens-cap and pulling out the side-mounted view finder, Wolf flicked the camera on and turned it to himself... His moustached face looming large in it’s picture. 'PWC 'Case One',' Magz entitled the tape. 'Show Karina in her true colours.'
