Blink.Breathe.See.Repeat. (Walking On Hope)

18 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 18/11/03 'Fuck my sedatives!' Talking to doctors and nurses was like being a blind man in a dark room looking for a black cat that wasn't there. You just never got anywhere. 'Please Mr. Poe...' 'No! No more damn 'Pleases' and 'Can We's'! You need to get me fucking moving! You need to get my on those damn walking bars or whatever you want to call them! You need to get me doing something OTHER than resting here where all I can feel is my head resting like a melon against this fucking pillow!' I seen her shake and quiver with nerves and shock. I didn't really mean to snap at her, not that much anyway. She was a pretty young girl, probably hadn't even been here long. Hell, if I could feel anything below my neck maybe I'd even get a hard on for how tight her uniform was on her. But that was the least thing on my mind right now. She sighed and placed her hands behind her back. 'You have some visitor's, but I can only let one of them in and...' 'Let just one of us in my ass!' came the still ever so teenage hormonal voice of Maynard Sanders. Trent and Vex followed suit behind him. Vex with his Axl Rose long red hair and leathers, Maynard with his thick glam braided purple dreads mixed amusingly with his flannel shirt and jeans., and Trent, calm as always, long black hair tied in a ponytail wearing a wife beater and black jeans. The nurse looked at all three of them before looking at me. I smiled at her, an apologetic one as well as a relieved one, and nodded to her. Maybe she noticed the way I seemed to calm down as the three of them entered, and she quickly left. 'How's it going dick licker?' asked Maynard. I could sense a touch of unease in his voice as he tried to kid with me. 'Well, your mothers safe from me for a while, put it that way'. I'm sure he could sense a little unease in my voice, but all three laughed. 'You feeling okay buddy?' asked Vex. I shrugged. 'It's weird, as you can probably imagine. Doesn't really hurt, but, well... ya know' They nodded, and for the first time around these three guys I felt truly uneasy. Not by being around them, we were always so much stronger together when around each other. It was something else. We'd toured all over this country in the back of a small van, played in front of large demanding crowds and opened for some of the most intense live acts around. That was reason to feel uneasy. That was plenty reason. I'd gone out and battled in incomprehensible odds and ungodly matches in front of sold out crowds and arenas. That again, was reason to feel uneasy. But now..... it was because they were seeing me.... seeing me like this. 'When are you going to walk again?' asked Trent. The first thing he had said to me since arriving, and I already knew he was speaking for the rest of them by cutting to the chase. What he perhaps didn't know, was that he was speaking for me as well. 'You tell me, Trent. Seriously, you fucking tell me.' My voice rattled, I could feel my throat twist and clench. For a second I thought tears were going to follow, but they slid back down to wherever they managed to climb up from. I took a deep breath. 'They say I'll be ready to start some light rehabilitation soon.' 'Tomorrow' Trent said without hesitation. We all looked at him with surprise. 'Trent!' Vex snapped, like a mother scolding her son for saying a curse word in public. 'Why not?' he replied back with ease. 'If they don't get his ass walking again soon nobody is. If they won't do it, we will' 'Hold up now George Cooney! This isn't E.R' said Maynard while standing and walking to Trent. 'Jesus wake up Maynard, all of you, wake up. This isn't the flu he's got or a cold.....' We all fell silent. '... HE'S FUCKING CRIPPLED!' 'Back the fuck off!' snapped Maynard as he stormed over to Trent. 'Guys!' They ignored me, and Trent quickly shoved Maynard back into his seat before Vex could break them apart. Trent then leaned into Maynard while Vex and I watched on. 'Right, sit down and listen' He turned around and stared at us all quickly ' All of you!' We all looked at him with suspicious but attentive looks. He was our front man, he was our face, he was our leader, and right now we all needed him, and for the first time since they arrived I didn't feel excluded due to the fact that I couldn't even tell whether I'd urinated in the bed during the night thanks to the two bastards who put me in this condition. Trent finally stood up. 'If we want to make the difference between you having long or short term paralysis then we need to move now. When someone gets cancer, they remove the tumour before it spreads. Same goes for here. I couldn't give a shit what the doctors say, I'll take the Pepsi challenge against their methods for this any day of the week. Just like removing a tumour before it spreads, we remove that denial, that doubt, that self pity welling up inside of you before it spreads.' Maybe it was just in our own way, but what he was saying made sense. 'We start tomorrow. I don't care whether we have to turn into John Q to get what we want done, but it WILL get done, and there isn't a doctor in this hospital who's going to stop us.' I nodded, understanding that what Trent was saying wasn't just the truth of the matter, it was the only option I had. 'You want to get them back, then we start tomorrow'. Them...... I went to clench my fists in anger but remembered I couldn't. 'Tomorrow' I replied.
