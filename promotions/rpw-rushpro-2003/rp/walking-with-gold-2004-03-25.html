Walking with Gold

25 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Mar. 25, 2004 When a time limit is placed on your life, how do you continue on with it? The things that use to be important seemed to take a backseat to the grief that consumed you. Did life just stop for three simple words? With a Global title defense coming up, Chris had to keep his eye on the prize and rekindle the flame of being alive. “Aughh…what time is it?” Chris Storm rolled out of bed and sat up on the edge; he was still dressed in the clothes he wore yesterday. Last night Chris had passed out as soon as he hit the bed and had not bothered to change. He rubbed his forehead, trying to massage the massive headache away. Storm got up and walked to the bathroom to get some aspirin to try and tame this wild headache. The Perfect Storm paused, as he looked at himself in the medicine cabinet mirror. He looked like complete shit, something very rare for the man who prided himself on looking like a Roman God. “Maybe it was all just a bad dream…” his voice trailed off as his hand reached toward the back of his neck. Nothing could express his disappointed when his fingertips brushed against the lump on the back of his neck. He felt like he was going to curl over and throw up. “Nope, it wasn’t a bad dream.” “RIIINNNGGGG!” Chris pulled his attention from his depression to the screaming phone in the hallway. His heart fell deep into his stomach. As a wrestler who had just came off the independent scene, his phone had became accustom to ringing often but this was the first time he feared it ringing. He forced his hand steady and picked up the receiver, “hello?” “Chris!” Chris recognized the voice as the one belonging to his manager and trainer, Arizona. A sigh of relief escaped. The last thing Chris wanted was the doctor to call with more bad news. “Where are you man?” The question caught Chris off guard; he rubbed his forehead again hoping to rid himself of the pounding headache. “What are you talking about? I’m at my apartment, where you called me.” Sarcasm poured from his mouth, Chris had been in no mood to play. “You’ve got a match to win! You’re supposed to be in the ring training.” Arizona replied; trying to sound thrilled about the whole concept. “Are you stupid or just drunk!?” Chris yelled at him over the phone. “Have you forgotten yesterday? I’m in no mood to get in the fucking ring!” Arizona could not blame him, no sane person would want to step into the ring to practice after the day Chris had yesterday. Then again Chris was not exactly someone Arizona would class as sane when it came to wrestling. Wrestling is life for Chris Storm. If Chris Storm had a problem he solved it wrestling. If something bothered him he suplexes some helpless fool until he forgot what was bothering him. Yet this new problem, this life threatening problem, made him completely forget about wrestling. This was a first indeed for the self proclaimed Last Great Technical Wrestler. “I guess you’re right. We’ll take the day off, let you think, and then we can get back in the ring tomorrow” he told Chris. “What’s the point? I’m dying Arizona! D-Y-I-N-G! The stupid Global title match is the last thing on my mind. Let fucking Apocalypse have it. He can spank his monkey with it. The guy has never accomplished anything worth a damn anyways. He’s great claim to fame is drawing with me back when I sucked harder then Patti on the weekend.” Arizona had interjected into his rant, “Apocalypse is actually the new FAW World Champ, Chris.” “And that’s supposed to mean something? No one cares about FAW. If he’s the new World champ it just goes to show how the talent there has decreased at such a level.” Chris paused to allow Arizona to try and defend FAW but no reply came. “Oh let me guess Adam Jacobs was his opponent or number one contender? I made Adam Jacobs my whipping boy when he was supposed to be such a big FAW player.” Chris’ temperature started to increase as he ranted on; the emotional floodgates where opened as Chris let it all pour out. “I tell you what, Jesse Jamester can win it. Then he can go parade around GW and LOW and let them pretend like someone actually gives a rat’s ass about him. Maybe if he has a Global title someone might think he’s worth a damn. Then again he could hold this belt for a year and no one would give him the time of the day, the guy couldn’t even out wrestle his shadow!” Arizona remained silent on his end, maybe he went for a sandwich. When Chris went on his little rants it generally lasted for a while and if anyone, Arizona knew this. “Oh and don’t even get me started on ‘Little Ms. Underdog.’ The only part of that that’s right is dog. When I worked in the porn industry I saw some cracked out whores but at least they could blame the crack for being so ugly. What’s her excuse, her mom? Course she comes from 4WW, there isn’t a single decent wrestler there. No wonder they consider Patti a queen there, it’s easy to rule when your subjects are Tough Enough rejects.” “You finished yet?” Arizona asked calmly. Chris started to breathe heavily, “No, go fuck yourself, I’ll tell you when I’m done.” This was the Storm that won matches that took titles and made people look like they just stepped between the ropes from the first time. He was intense and determined to make someone pay. “Who the hell is running this show? PTC can’t pick a decent opponent for me. Jesse Jamester, Apocalypse, and Patti are by far the easiest opponent I could have asked for. Jesse Jamester has always been a second rate wrestler trying to fool people into thinking he was first rate. Apocalypse is nothing more then a fucking pussy. He talks big shit but when he gets in the ring he’ll be out classed. And Patti is by far the biggest joke of them all. Who did she blow to get into this match? She had to take a load to come from nowhere to a Global title match.” Chris paused to breathe, “Ok, now I’m done.” “Well go out today and relax, you seem high strung. We’ll skip practice until you feel up to it. But do me a favor; bring the Global title out with you. Maybe you’ll get attached to it.” The statement came off more as a plea then an order. Arizona could see where Chris was coming from but hoped being with the title would spark a drive to keep the title. Things were bleak and Chris seemed ready to hang up the towel. He had been through so much in his career and several times he was read to give up. He even retired once. But each time the love of the sport brought him back. Wrestling was just inside his blood, like all the other wrestlers. You may leave but you’ll never be gone from wrestling. It will always pull you back in later. Anyone who ever stepped into the ring knew this. Yet now Chris had came up against an opponent he feared he could not defeat. He had been with PTC for about two years and that could all come to an end soon. It seemed as soon as he finally reached his goal something had to take all his joy from him. What had he done to deserve it? He tried to make himself better which in turn would make PTC better. Was he rewarded for it? No instead he was punished. It nearly destroyed him. Chris Storm thought about what Arizona said to him. He looked over at the Global title that was displayed on his mantel. He walked up to the mantel and looked at the gold plate closely. He could see his reflection and just how bad he looked. He grabbed the title and slung it over his shoulder. “Kami, I can’t believe I’m doing this. I feel like some shitty no-namer who finally won his first title because he was best friend with the owner. I mean who carries their title with them when they walk around?” Those golden locks tossed back and forth as Chris shook his head in grief. He could not believe he was going to do this. Despite feeling like a complete retard Chris left his apartment and went out for a walk in the beautiful New York spring. Though spring had officially arrived and the sun shined brightly, the air was still cool and chilling. Chris buddle up in his nice warm jacket tightly and embraced it all regardless the temperature. Chris Storm could feel the eyes of many weighing down on him, judging him silently. He could not blame them, if he saw some moron walking around with a belt buckle the size of Texas he would make fun of them as well. “Oh, hello Mr. Storm! You here for new issue of Technical Wrestler Digest?” the small Asian woman asked him smiling broadly. The Last Great Technical Wrestler turned to see the small woman manning her magazine stand. Chris was a regular customer here, with it being so close to his apartment. The Asian woman looked at the title wrapped around his waist. “Oh nice belt bucket Mr. Storm, you play cowboy on TV now?” she asked unaware the belt bucket she was referring to was actually the most prized possession in Primetime Central. Seeing a great chance for a cover up over his embarrassing situation Chris nodded and agreed with her, “yeah I’m going to slaughter an Indian on the next Adrenaline; it will be sensational to say the least.” Chris paid for his magazine and flipped through the papers. “RPW on break of closing…fWo Survivor to start soon…PTC Cruiserweight Tournament set to start…rumors of PIT2…AH HERE WE GO!” Chris yelled as he opened the magazine to the page he wanted to see, “the predictions on the Global title defense.” That calm and fun exterior Chris wore started to fade and show a truer side to Chris. Though he had distracted his thoughts from his problems for a moment it all can crashing back down on him. “I wonder if those people that voted for me to win will still say that if they knew…I’m supposed to be the favorite but if news about my cancer got out I might lose my backing.” Chris looked down at the magazine to see just what the people where saying about his match. His eyes fell on the first reader’s comments, “I think it will still be Storm…if not then definitely Patti Anderson.” Chris’ nose shifted back in forth in annoyance. Though his ego was helped by being picked the winner it still bothered him that someone so new like Patti could be picked over the likes of Jesse Jamester and Apocalypse, two strong names in PTC. He brushed it off, at least the person was smart enough to pick him as the winner and that is all that mattered in the end. Chris looked at the next reader’s opinion on the match “I think Storm will retain in the April match.” He smiled now “this guy is a lot smarter. It will be me, no questions asked.” Having his ego back firmly into place his looked on to the next comments without worry. After all he had already been picked twice to be a sure winner so clearly the readers of Technical Wrestler Digest knew Chris Storm was the real Last Great Technical Wrester. “Patti, JJ, or Apoc. I hope” That swelling ego got knocked down a peg. Chris was quick to brush it off with insults and belittlement, “what does this guy know? He can’t even speak in clear sentences or use proper grammar. It must be one of those little net geeks who think he knows everything just because he downloads pictures of naked women he’ll never sleep with.” “The Mid-South Champion and the Global Champion. This guy’s been on a roll as of late and I doubt he’ll be slowing down any time soon.” Chris beamed as he read the next comments on the match. “But,” Chris started to get discouraged, but was never a good word when everything was going good. “I think he’ll be defeated b Patti in this defense.” “PATTI?” Chris yelled. Chris rubbed his temple as he looked over the last two comments. “How is Patti getting voted for? How do people even know who she is in PTC? She’s never even wrestler in a PTC match before.” The rippling sound of knuckles being cracked rolled from his hands. “This people think I’ll lose my belt to a woman?” He looked at the other comments. Everyone seemed to blend into the same. It was either Patti this or Patti that. “I can’t believe these people think I’ll fall to some bitch with no career in PTC. If their beloved Tessa Windsor and Chryste can’t even take me down what chance does this newbie have against me?” Chris spat off the side, showing his disgust. He took that title around his waist and unhooked it. He held the belt by its leather strap and looked into the gold plate covering its face. What made it special? It was just leather with gold studded to it. To all the other people out there it was just “a big belt buckle”. So what made it so important to the likes of Chris Storm, Seymour Almasy, Apocalypse, and Jesse Jamester? They gave years of their life fighting for the belt and proving they were worthy of it. But why give up years of your life freely? A bleak face stared at him. It showed little emotion and gave little away to him. Chris could not tell what the reflection was thinking. “They worked so hard to make this title mean something. Each one was forced to prove the deserved it through years of work and determination.” Chris spoke to the title; he had come to a complete stand still in the middle of the sidewalk. People walked around him staring at the man holding a title up to his face. It seemed as if time froze on Chris. “July 6th, 2002. This day is very special to me. It is the first day I entered PTC. February 29th, 2004. Another day that is very special to me. I won the PTC Global title on this day. That is nearly TWO years of my life dedicated to winning you.” He told the belt as he admired it. The title reflected the light that managed to get through the clouds. That belt may only be made of leather and gold plates but after remembering all the years of hard work it suddenly looked so much more beautiful to him. “I think I’ll keep you. Cancer or no cancer, I worked too damn hard to roll over now and hand it over to the likes of them. If they want you they will have to pry you from my cold dead fingers.” Chris Storm held the title out and wrapped it around his waist again. He would wear this belt proud. This belt brought people’s career meaning and purpose. It was what the strived and worked for, just like he once had. He knew Jesse Jamester wanted this title badly. It would be the reward for all his hard work. But Chris worked hard just to keep it. In the end the match would be about who worked harder for it and who wanted it more. It’s the PTC Global title; you know you want it.
