Before Today #12 : Magz, Lies & Videotape (Part Two)

2 February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 02/02/04 'Wait a second...' the bemused man stopped, trying to clarify the situation he'd found himself in. 'You're going to PAY me to do this?!' The man, hovering around the 20 mark, stood across from a domineering Magnum Wolf, who was in full temperamental director mode. Magz just blanked him at first, instead hunting around for the ideal spot to film the next 'scene' from. 'Sure,' he mumbled, still not rewarding his latest lackey with anything more than minimal attention. 'One hundred dollars.' Then, the Private Eye on a quest to film a PTC exposes for Karina Wolfenden's father saw the perfect spot for some clandestine filming. Perked up a little, Wolf turned to the man, thoroughly pleased with himself by the time they faced one another. Still not quite sure what he was letting himself in for, the man ran through the deal once more. 'So let me get this straight,' he went on, 'for one hundred dollars, you want me to stand right here...' 'Here' was a featureless corridor, who's tranquillity was only shattered by the odd passing PWC worker. '...Wait for Karina Wolfenden to walk by, then just grab her tit... And tell her to learn her place in the world?' the man finished, wondering quite what he'd gotten himself in to. Magnum sensed the hesitancy in the man's voice, realising not everyone lacked the basic morals that he was without. 'Er, mostly, yes,' Wolf replied, 'it'll be the easiest 100 dollars you've ever made... As well as the first, most likely.' This was the last scene Wolf needed for his video. He'd already shot some prime footage of the dangers lurking around the pro-wrestling scene, even getting lucky enough to catch one drunken old has-been puking over a fan. 'Need to drop the Cosby Kids off at the pool or what, kid?' Magz then probed, seeing nervousness creep across his lackey's face. 'Well, er, yeah... But that's not the problem,' the man hesitantly spoke. 'Problem is, she's, like, a pro-wrestl-' 'No she ISN'T,' a growling Wolf sliced his concern off with. 'She's in PWC,' the man then appealed, 'of course she's a wre-' 'NYIP!' Wolf blurted out, his arms motioning a cut. 'What was that...?' the bemused man asked, realising he should have said no the moment Wolf approached him. 'NYIP! She's a Snowboarder,' Magnum scoffed, still not having accepted Karina Wolfenden as a wrestler. 'Nothing more, nothing less.' Slightly frustrated at Magnum's unwillingness to even discus his concerns, the fan sighed, debating whether the 100 bucks was worth it. 'Anyway, she's a pro-athlete, and could probably kick my ass,' he tried to appeal to Magnum's better nature with. 'I mean, what happens if she gets mad about the whole thing?' Wolf rubbed his chin thoughtfully... And he had nothing. He then rubbed his moustache, hoping for inspiration... Still nothing. His hands then crept south, landing on THE PACKAGE~! Instantly, Magnum's eyes lit up. 'Tough question kid,' his silver tongue replied, 'had to really use my heard to work that one out. I'll be right over there, recording the whole thing for... Uh... Posterity, or something. If she swings, I'll have your back.' He almost sounded sincere. The fan's forehead scrunched, not entirely trusting the grinning man before him. 'Wait...' he enquired, 'didn't she already kick your ass?' Having had a nerve well and truly hit, Wolf feigned a backhand on the fan, sending him cowering in fear. 'Just grab the bitch's tit,' he growled, 'you'll get the money afterward.' Striding away, Wolf went to set-up in his 'shooting location', while the intimidated lackey just stood, wondering what he'd gotten himself into.
