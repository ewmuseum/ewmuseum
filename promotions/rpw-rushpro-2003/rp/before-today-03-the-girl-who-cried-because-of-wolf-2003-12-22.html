Before Today #03 : The Girl Who Cried (Because Of) Wolf

22 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 22/12/03 (Based on an RP originally written for the Pro Wrestling Circuit on the 5th May, 2003 - Mat) Karina Wolfenden was sodden. For hours she had been walking through the stormy night, her slender frame pelted with the relentless downfall. The pink flash that made up her fringe had lost it's brightness, darkened and matted down across her furrowed brow... The rest of her usually shiny mane had suffered the same fate, the precipitation having knocked the last bit of volume from it, making it look as limp and stringy as the wire connecting her earphones to the Discman in her pocket. The CD had finished playing, not that she'd noticed, for Karina's ears went to sleep when she should of... Hours before. Weighted down by a waterlogged black hooded sweatshirt, Karina just walked... Aimlessly. She'd probably strolled around the same corner, across the same street more than once that night, but as long as she was walking, she wasn't sitting at home... Thinking... Regretting. She didn't look alive... Deathly pale and showing no emotion, no reaction to her surroundings. Karina might as well have not be there at all, although that would of sound a pretty good deal to the dejected Wolfenden. In her switched-off state, Karina hadn't even noticed that her suede sneakers were drenched, squelching with every step... But still, she didn't even side-step to avoid the inch-deep puddles that had formed on the un-even sidewalk. At 3-30am, nobody was there to bother her, there was none of the hustle and bustle that there usually was when she set off from her apartment. Hundreds of people who'd instead be at home, deep in slumber beside the one they love, after having kissed their children goodnight... Happy families. What would Karina do after awakening from her zombie-like state, and heading home? Probably just drink. There'd be no husband, no children... No family whatsoever. In a city of thousands she was solitary. She'd never needed others, never relied on anyone else before... So why did she feel so sedated? Why were her senses barely registering? Why did her food have no taste? The humid air no scent? The sparse traffic no sound? Why did she feel like this? She knew the answer, and she knew the man who caused it all, and she knew all too well the events that plummeted her to this nadir. It seemed Karina had underestimated what having 'Wolfenden' after her Christian name meant. It was more than just a surname, it meant that she had a family, it meant every person in her life who shared that name would be there for her... But she'd thrown it all away for wrestling... And at that moment in time, if she could bare to think about it, she regretted it. Her stride was finally broken as she stopped on the curb, her unmistakable green eyes lazily scanning left to right from under her matted-down fringe. But the sights they saw did not make it to Karina's brain. The only daughter, estranged, of Lawrence Wolfenden just stepped out into the road... Where a pair of headlights illuminated her sodden body. The tyres screeched as the car braked, wet road reducing the friction, and in turn increasing the stopping distance. Through the windshield, the driver's face said it all as his body was thrust back into the driver's seat, his vehicle slowing down around him... He thought he was going to hit her. His eyes closed, preparing for the worst... Scarily, he seemed to care far more than Karina. Alive by 6 inches. Maybe if 20 minutes more of the torrential, monsoon-like downpour had fallen before she crossed the street, those would have been the last actions of Karina Wolfenden. The drama was lost on her though, her stride not even broken as she made it to the opposite sidewalk. 'Lunatic!' yelled the driver, furious that his brief moment of intense and concentrated stress wasn't even met with the slightest reaction by the woman he thought he had killed. His clenched fist hammered the horn, the intrusive sound looking like it might awaken Karina from her internal solitude... But it failed. Worry and care wasted, he hit the accelerator and sped off, taking the memory of 'The nut he nearly ran over' with him. The handful of people who had business at the early hour just stood and stared, forming their own opinions of the woman encased in the wet hoodie and cargo pants. 'Probably a Junkie,' they might of pondered, or maybe they just took her as a homeless runaway sleeping in a doorway. Funny when you considered that only a few days previous, the woman they were passing off as a nut-job was co-heir to a fortune they'd never make in their lifetime. Magnum Wolf was responsible for the 'previous'. He'd taken a few dismissive comments and began a campaign against her, he'd taken a professional difference and turned it into a personal vendetta, he'd taken a loving family and split them... ...He'd plunged Karina to depths she'd never even thought about. And wandering in the downpour, she thought he'd won.
