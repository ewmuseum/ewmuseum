Conflict of Self: Part III - Defeat

21 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 21-04-04 Hessian didn’t even bother waiting for a reply, instead hanging up on Carlisle and collapsing into the nearest chair. He held his head in his hands waiting for the inevitable to happen, while his mind was still at work. It didn’t take long before Minion came waltzing through the door singing after a successful sexual harassment spree, while a man dressed in black followed…not as happy as the Imp. Waiting by the door, he looked around; keeping his arms folded while Minion did a little jig before spinning on his heels and pointing at the man. “Wait here Chico, I’ll go fetch him for ya!” Minion pipped innocently, having no idea of what had transpired in the last few minutes. Skipping through the hotel room, the God Among Midgets searched for Hessian, finally happening upon him as he emerged from a bedroom. The Goliath glanced down at the Imp. “Go in there and don’t come out until I tell you to.” He said, motioning to the bedroom behind him. “What? Why? Something goin’ down I can’t be a part of?” Minion asked, folding his arms and cocking his head as he tried to look up at Hessian. “Yes. Now do as you’re told…” “But I-…” Minion was given no opportunity to argue, instead being pushed inside the bedroom, the door shut forcefully in his face as Hessian turned and walked to the front room where Jim Carlisle was indeed waiting, sitting in a chair and admiring Hessian’s belt from his ring attire with the logo adorning it. Glancing up at the Mercenary of Satan, Carlisle flashed a Jack Nicholson-like grin and adjusted the pair of sunglasses he was wearing. “You certainly look bigger in person than on T.V. Mr. Kelsig. Here, take a seat.” Carlisle said, motioning to the sofa opposite him. Hessian complied and took a seat, glaring at Carlisle as he placed the belt down and clasped his hands. “So…how are we going to solve this little problem of ours?” “You tell me. I don’t have $15,000 to give you and anything else I say will probably be wrong. So make whatever damn deal you have to and be on your way. I’ve got more important things to be worrying about than some goddamn debt that…my-…” Hessian trailed off all of a sudden as Carlisle removed his glasses, revealing a wicked old sneer on his aged face. It was not the sneer that stopped Hessian, but seeing Carlisle’s face. Something in his mind clicked, and seeing his gruff face, wrinkled eyes and thin-lipped sneer Hessian suddenly thought back to the night of the fire, to falling down the front steps and turning around to see the man who had started it all. Gazing at him, Angelus took in every detail before the rage set in and he attacked… Snapping out of his daze, Hessian looked up at Carlisle once again, examining his face and slowly coming to realize just who sat before him. “It was you. You…you lying son of a bitch.” He said, staring blankly now at Carlisle. “What was me, Mr. Kelsig?” replied Carlisle, unaware of how much danger he was quickly going to be in. “That night…the fire. My family’s death…you were there. You caused it! YOU SON OF A BITCH!” Hessian dived out of the sofa, charging towards Carlisle and roaring out. Lunging at the loan shark, Hessian was stopped in his tracks by a clicking sound, and something jabbing him in his gut. Holding out an outstretched arm and a balled-up fist, Hessian looked down at his stomach and saw the gun pointing directly at him. He froze as Carlisle looked up at him, grinning even now. “You took a gunshot to the arm and healed up pretty nicely if I remember correctly…think you could handle a bullet to the guts? No? Do me a favour and sit the fuck back down.” Jim said, waving the gun over Hessian’s stomach and prompting the Goliath to back off towards the sofa, and once again Hessian complied. “Now. I already told you I had nothing to do with your family’s death and I stick by that…” came the blatant lie from Carlisle. He knew fine and well what he had done, but even he had been taken aback by Hessian’s outright rage towards him and maintaining a lie seemed mandatory now. Perhaps it was Hessian’s size or the intimidating faceplate but something had him spooked, and he was thankful for the gun in his possession. “…I’m not here to be accused of murder, I’m here to reclaim my $15,000. I’m not going to sit here all day and listen to you tell me you don’t have it…so I’ll be nice and accept that fact.” He said, to a relieved sigh from Hessian…until he continued, “…So how much do you have?” Hessian threw his hands up in the air and rolled his eyes. Rubbing the temple not hidden behind the steel faceplate he sighed. “Not $15,000. What I could give you? $2,500. That’s it. I can’t give any more than that.” “That’s it? Well fuck me, I might as well take nothing!” Carlisle said, thinking for a moment and scratching the side of his head with the barrel of his gun. Hessian winced; hoping some mechanism in the gun would malfunction and send a bullet driving through Carlisle’s skull, ending his miserable life. He had only known Carlisle for little more than ten minutes and already he wished he were dead. Wasn’t the curse of humanity flowing through his veins he would have granted his own wish a few minutes into the phone conversation. He almost smiled as the thought of Carlisle’s blood spattering across the walls, yet the smile disappeared after a few moments as Jim spoke again. “Ok…tell you what. Here’s what we’re going to do. You’re going to give me that two and a half grand. There’s a guy I know back in Detroit, a bookie. The difference between him and any other bookmaker with brains is that he takes bets on wrestling matches. If my estimation of the scale of this match is accurate he’ll be taking bets on the outcome for sure. Excuse me for a moment.” Hessian had no say in what was going on, asking himself if he even wanted a say, or just do whatever it took to get rid of this nuisance. Sneering at Carlisle, Hessian rested his head against the sofa, staring up at the ceiling and sighing as Jim Carlisle held his cell phone to his ear and waited for an answer. Surely enough someone whose voice had a weasel-like sleazy tone picked up. Carlisle grinned and cleared his throat. “Yeah, Benny? It’s Jim here, Jim Carlisle………yeah I’m good, you? Hey that’s great, listen Benny I need favour. You taking any bets for this Primetime Central Global Title match comin’ up? Oh really, great…………yeah I know but bear with me, what are the odds on Hessian from RPW winning?” the conversation went back and forth as such until Jim went quiet as Benny checked the odds for the match. Hessian looked back across the room at Carlisle as his eyes lit up all of a sudden and he piped up again. “Really? Well I’ll tell ya what Benny…stick $2,500 on Hessian winning for me, alri-………yeah I know it’s a lot of money………oh no I’m plenty serious………ok good man Benny, I’ll be in on the Monday after to collect my money………heh, yeah I know, all a game of chance. Ok I’ll talk to you later Benny, thanks again…ok, bye.” Carlisle flipped his phone shut and pocketed it, glancing at Hessian and grinning an evil grin. “Well, well, well Mr. Kelsig it appears as though a lovely little coincidence has played into our favour and your odds to win stand at 7/1. So in giving me your two and a half grand and winning your wrestling match, not only do I get my money back paid in full but you also get your money back! For now though I will require it as a deposit…you’ll have noticed the ATM across the street I’m sure, why don’t you mosey on along and get it for me?” Hessian got to his feet and left the room, saying nothing. Stopping outside of the bedroom where he had left Minion, the Mercenary of Satan hesitated to enter. Was he really going to give in to a sleazy human like Jim Carlisle? He leaned back towards the front room almost as if to head back in and power through the gunshots to Carlisle, wrap a hand around his throat and choke the very life from him. Instead he simply grabbed the door handle and walked in to the bedroom to see Minion, who surprisingly enough was practicing hand gestures and poses for future venues as opposed to………charging “adult” phone calls to the hotel. “I need you to run a little errand for me.” Hessian said, almost forlornly. Minion could tell something was wrong with Hessian yet had no clue about just how much recent events had affected him. “What do you need? I just called out for some Portuguese hookers so we should be-…” He began, only to be cut off by the Goliath as he grabbed Minion’s travel rucksack, removing the contents and handing the empty bag to Minion, as well as a credit card. “Go to the ATM across the street and withdraw $2,500. Don’t ask why, just get the money, put it in the bag and bring it back here. Got it?” “Oh, this is about that debt thing isn’t it? I see………ok.” Minion replied, nodding and taking the bag and card from Hessian and exiting the room, the Mercenary of Satan following close behind. Ignoring Carlisle, Minion left the hotel room as Hessian took a seat back on the sofa opposite the loan shark. “Good. Now remember Mr. Kelsig, you better win this match. If you lose, I get collateral, namely both your legs. You don’t want that I’m sure.” Carlisle remarked as he watched Minion waddling out of the room. Turning back to Hessian he was surprised to see a new look of confidence upon the Goliath’s face. “After this? Don’t worry. There’s no way I’m going to lose.” Hessian was of course thinking in his mind of Carlisle as motivation now. It would help a lot in the ring to see him in the face of every one of his opponents, and make the process of Eternal Damnation run all the more smoothly. All the same, Hessian had just found another purpose in his match. Winning the Global title was a given, but now he had to win to pay off Carlisle. To some extent he would do it for his father and the good life he was given by his parents. He would win the match and pay off the loan shark as a way of defeating this all-too human problem. The others could do as they please. They could whine, piss, bitch, moan and gloat all they wanted and make all the threats their hearts desired. Hessian would swoop in and silence them like a hitman in the night. He wanted the belt…and he needed it. If not to conquer the problem of Jim Carlisle then to reassure himself that the hellfire still roared within, and the spirit of the bloodthirsty, demon slaying Titanic Satanic Insaniac was very much alive. Only then would his doubts be put at rest, only then would the scepticism be quelled at long last. Hessian was a marked man on all sides, and the only way to prove to both himself and his foes that he was still a formidable and powerful nemesis, to prove he was still the destructive force – the Mercenary of Satan – was to defeat. Defeat in all sense of the word. Hessian would defeat those 5 men physically in the ring, dashing any hopes they had of winning the Global Title. In winning the Global Title Hessian would defeat Jim Carlisle, stopping him from carrying out his human threat and letting him have his money at long last. In defeating those 5 men and becoming the most dominant man in Primetime Central…Hessian hoped with all his might that his dominance would in some form or another defeat the desires of Clyde and Nerezza. Hessian had to do it…or he would be defeated. Minion returned shortly thereafter, and Jim Carlisle got his money. The first half of the battle was over now, all Hessian had to do now was enter that ring against the best that LOW, XUW, GW, UWF and 4WW had to offer. Certainly the ball was in his court with all the hype surrounding him and intimidation cast upon his opponents. All that remained now was to win, to prove…and to bring Eternal Damnation to the Primetime Central. The End.
