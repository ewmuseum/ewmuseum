Weapon of choice? Where's the F***ing Rock™ when you need one?

9 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 09/03/04 “It’s about time I proved something” The Yamamura Inn, once again. The basement. Cold and dark, as dead as the owners of the property at Hessian’s hands. Propped up somewhat close to the boiler that has since discontinued it’s use, Nerezza stared at the shiny black glove in front of him. His fingers stretched out like a starfish, they cruelly pulled together as though a neck was in his palm, with a creaking sound of the material rubbing against itself. This right hand had done so much. It was the hand that inserted a key into a pair of handcuffs in Detroit. Those handcuffs held an apparent victim in place as a man was about to steal from her, then scared away by Nerezza’s red contact lenses and false fangs. The right hand shoved the same woman away as she bit him, mixing her blood with his and forever changing him. It had held the scythe that slashed NTICW’s ring apart, as Gaz Green set fire to it as a symbol of P.W.A. leaving the company. It held a pen that signed a rejected application to Most Extreme Team, as well as the successful application to PCW. It was the hand that pulled so many pranks, that replaced the sign on the men’s room as Ellie~! Handcuffed herself in place. The hand that pointed Byron Calver to the very same room. The same hand had driven the heads of Romana Dumetrescu, Alexander Miyagawa, Chris Storm and Chris Matthews into the nearest wall. It was the hand that for five times, held aloft PCW’s Tag titles. And it was the hand that for countless times, shook the un-buzzered hand of Gaz Green’s. But now what? Gaz had gone to movies. OSE had gone to the men in suits. PCW had gone to… BUNGLE. Now, what was the hand for? First, for Hessian. To batter Clyde. Then, to Clyde, to batter Hessian and try to cheat Poe. Now, it returned to its owner. “Time I proved that I am not the runt of the litter.” He dryly breathed. “Hessian, slaughterer of many. Clyde, maimer of countless. Nerezza… so far, devourer of a handful. But now… I shall surpass them and prove that evolution improves on mistakes.” Rising up from his spot, Nerezza held out his arm in front of himself, fist tightly clenched. Although he didn’t need to, he was breathing heavily, then heavier, almost hyperventilating. The tiniest gap in his hair showed a series of sharp white teeth… but not in a place that either God or Darwin would accept. The jaw line crept beyond where a man’s would end… as far back as underneath the ears. A sickly set of red gums held them in place, pulling them apart and pushing them together with the rhythm of his breath. A shiny, glistening appendage licked over the edges of the teeth, oozing from the gap and sliding back under the hair, the little drooling tip barely visible with only the colour and moving trail of saliva to give the location away. Slowly, it retreated back inside. Nerezza’s head dipped, moving his hair forwards. The signs of his extended mouth were gone, along with any other trace of his abnormality save his dark grey skin. “Since the passing of OSE, I had no reason to exist. Then Clyde came to me, telling me that Hessian would soon come seeking my help. I existed to pretend to be Hessian’s accomplice and then to turn on him… then I existed to scorn Hessian as another of his mistakes, standing alongside Clyde… but now…” The bitterness and resentment in his tongue grew, a harsh and jealous tone that oozed a threat through grated teeth. “Now… I exist for myself. I’ll prove to them both one last time that of the family, I AM the most evolved and that I AM the strongest!” The squeaking of his glove grew louder until a popping noise suddenly made him release his grip. Staring at his outstretch palm, his fingernails… no, his talons had pierced their prisons and sunken into the inside of his fist. Four little punctures sprouted blood… Nerezza’s own blood. Almost black from the lack of oxygen and thick, it slowly collected into a pool in his palm, eventually flowing over the sides and converging on the back of his hand, where they formed one single droplet and spattered onto the floor. Drip. Drip. Drip. Drip. Splat. It drew forth new ideas within Nerezza’s head. The Human Horror Film had less than a month before Hessian’s “Weapon Of Choice” match. With another inhuman smile that cut across his face, he gazed at the four sharp claws on the end of his fingers. The gears in his head began to turn and his brain once again became a factory for wicked ideas. His weapon of choice… had just been chosen.
