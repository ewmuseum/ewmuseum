Before Today #05 : First Impressions (Part Two)

23 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 23/12/03 'Where'd a chick learn ta ride like that?' marvelled the charitable fan as he stood, staring longingly at Karina Wolfenden. Oblivious attention she was receiving, Karina slid her helmet off, sending a mane of golden blonde hair falling elegantly back down, resting just short of her shoulders. Turning her head slightly, a flash of shocking pink was seen, dominating her fringe and cascading down over her emerald eyes. 'No woman rides like that,' scoffed Magnum, seemingly unimpressed with her as he wiped the muddy water from his bushy eyebrows. 'Clearly she used to be a man. Furthermore... Where the hell did a Jersey 'Girl' get a ride like THAT!' While it was the Wolfenden that everyone else was looking at, Wolf's eyes were firmly on the bike, looking at it with lust... Especially considering the less than glamorous arrival he made to the show. He didn't like being upstaged, not one bit. 'What does being from Jersey have to do with anything?' questioned the oblivious fan as he and Wolf approached her. Wolf just rolled his eyes at the man's ignorance, further more confirming that the night was, indeed, sucking. 'You know,' Magz continued, 'The joke? What's the difference between a bag of trash and a New Jersey girl?' Standing a good 6 inches above him, Wolf looked down, almost urging a reply from the well-meaning, yet quite slow fan. 'Uhm... The bag of trash gets picked up at least once a week?' came the reply, followed by a shrug, showing that he didn't really know... Or care. 'No, but that's good too,' Magz conceded, rubbing his chin and summing up the reply. 'The REAL answer is...' While Wolf tried to play comedian, the fan who drove him to the show was now lost in another world, a world where Magnum's words were just a distant, unintelligible echo. He was watching Karina, who's bare right hand ran along the top of her Ducati, like one would stroke a pet. As if that hadn't produced enough drooling from the ogling on-lookers, the K-Wolf then unzipped her leathers, squeezing her body out and letting the upper-half hang from her waist. 'The Bag,' she turned and casually interrupted, adjusting her black & pink Distillers t-shirt as she spoke. 'The B-' Wolf went to finish, before realising the young woman had stolen his punch line. 'HEY!' he then protested, furious that not even his lame jokes were going over. 'You ruined MY joke... About YOU!' Magnum stamped his soggy feet in annoyance as Karina looked with inquisitiveness at his dirty, damp clothing. 'Couldn't have been about me,' she smiled, revealing perfect teeth of brilliant white, and flashing a quick wink at her downtrodden fellow, albeit oblivious to the fact, PWC member, 'I'm not from Jersey.' The pair weren't exactly hitting it off, and the last thing Magnum needed after his hellish journey was to be one-upped in a verbal joust... Especially by a woman. Her taunting smile then dropped into a brief look of mild disgust, showing that it was just there as accompaniment to her retort. Their chemistry was awkward, both seeming naturally opposed to one another, not gelling on any levels. 'How'd you get the money for that thing?' Magz then bluntly asked her, as curious as he was envious, not thinking the youthful looking, fresh-faced woman was a day older than 20. Not deeming an answer to Wolf's question an immediate concern, Karina reached back behind her head to tie her hair neatly back... The arc of her body only intensifying the ogling stares, even distracting Wolf for a brief second. 'Easy, I didn't,' she answered, just noticing Magnum carefully eyeing up her breasts before he hurriedly snapped them away, 'Daddy did, the product of 'smart investments' or whatever...' Wolf was growling, fuming, but more and more kept riling him. 'Now, if I could please be excused from your little joke-a-thon,' she asked in her best, innocently patronising girly-voice, looking out of the top of her wide eyes at him, 'I'm already late for work.' Wolf's jaw dropped, and before he could even think up a witty remark, Karina gracefully turned and walked confidently towards the wrestler's entrance. Both he and the fan tilted their heads in unison, checking out her ass which was still being hugged tightly by the lower portion of her leathers. Looking as if he was in love, the fan nudged Wolf's arm... An arm who's hand was plunged deep into his jeans' pocket, motioning frantically. 'Ah, there's that lint!' Magz innocently exclaimed, quickly yanking his hand out showing no shame. Looking back to the Ford Contour where his gear lay, Wolf decided he might as well take the fan into the show with him... After all, his ego needed the boost of a hanger-on. Magz cleared his throat and nodded back to his bags, sending he portly fan trundling back to grab them. Not even waiting for the kind fan to properly catch up, Magz made his way towards the entrance, his 'guest' jogging awkwardly behind him. 'How much you wanna bet she either quits, or runs off home to Daddy?' a chauvinistic Wolf then scoffed disappearing through the entrance, his lackey fan in tow, 'That... Or becomes the locker-room whore by the end of the month...'
