A New Routine

29 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 29/5/04 The soaring lyrics of Sandra Nasic that blared from her earphones told the story of the last 7 days. 'It is not to high, I can make it...' With her brightly streaked hair tied back behind a black bandana, she immediately looked far from the luminous sight that appeared on RPW TV weekly. The pink and black, padded and strengthened outfit was forsaken for a much simpler, plain white vest, paired with dull grey jogging bottoms. She wasn't dressing for show, nor to feel good about herself. 'Ugh,' came a grunt that Serena Williams would have been proud of as the K-Wolf's bare fist slammed into the punch-bag. 'Ugh, ugh, ugh,' she repeated with each solid blow, her knuckles feeling that little more raw every time they impacted on the canvas. Out of 'costume', Karina Wolfenden looked plain and unassuming. No make-up, no accessories, no beaming smile. RPW's keenest and most loyal fan would have walked right past her and not even experienced the vaguest hint of recognition... Something she was grateful for. 'Take... THAT!' she then roared, putting all her 156 pounds into a straight right that exploded against the mildly forgiving target. Walking out of The Kannon Effect, Karina Wolfenden knew she wasn't meant to be RPW Champion. She wasn't even meant to be in the match. Due to exhaustion, and one too many jarring blows to the head, she couldn't even remember much of the match... Only the senses of determination and honour as she stood toe-to-toe with Gideon Poe. He had the heart, and the desire, to be RPW's premiere star. He'd carried the promotion on his back since day one. Or even Six Finger Cisco. He'd held OSW's head above water for months, and could have been the man to launch RPW's new era to greater heights than all before it. But instead, it was her... Karina Wolfenden, RPW World Champion - it still didn't sound right to her. 'Just pretend it has a moustache,' breathless words offered in encouragement as her arms burned with acidified muscles and salty sweat. 'The wind falls, into a wave. Shadow's calling, I'm the ache.' As much as she'd try to shrug it off in public, she felt the responsibility. RPW World Champion wasn't something to put on her CV, and it wasn't going to be a great topic of conversation with her friends... It meant that peoples' livlihoods were linked to her performance, her attitude, her dedication. Karina Wolfenden was no longer a care-free woman indulging in a hobby, her fortuitous win on Sunday had nullified that... She was now an ambassador for RPW. Maybe that realisation was why her knuckles were exposing fresh, pink skin. 'Grrr...' she forced out through gritted teeth while her fists continued to rain down like a cluster bomb on the bag. She was never a strong puncher, and her lack of upper-body strength had always been compensated for by her legs. But now, it was time she learned to punch someone properly. If she wasn't going to break out Exploder-Clutch Suplexes every match, she can at least land a telling thump to someone's face. For all she knew, the belt could be lost again on Sunday, but it wasn't going to be without a hearty fight. No padded gloves protecting her fists, no bracers to cushion her forearms, no well-aimed kicks to compose her striking offence, no ropes to jump and flip off of... Just a young woman, a punch bag, and a sense of duty. 'Hold me... Hold me down to the ground. And my body starts to shiver... Hold me.' It was Friday, 4:30pm. She'd started her new routine on Monday at 6.00am. Still a hobby? With one final, all-encompassing swing, Karina slammed her right fist against the bag, before collapsing forward and grabbing onto her swaying target. She was exhausted, her powerful legs almost as tired as her scorched arms as she’d danced around the bag to pick it off. She could never be the champion Gideon Poe was. She'd never be able to personify what RUSH Pro Wrestling was all about. But until somebody pinned her shoulders down for three, or made her give it all up, Karina Wolfenden would guard the belt's reputation with honour… The best trait she had to offer it. It was just leather and metal, but she would pay it respect with sweat and blood.
