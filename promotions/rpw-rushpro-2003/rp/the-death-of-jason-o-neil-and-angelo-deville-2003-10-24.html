The Death of Jason O'Neil...And Angelo Deville

24 October 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/24/03 I had been sitting in the dark for nearly two hours. It was a wonder that his security password was so damn easy to crack. I had plenty of time to back out. I could have gone running. But that wasn't what I'd set out to do. My job was simple: Kill Angelo Deville. I remember thinking about my wife, and my daughter while sitting in the darkened living room, trying to keep my head about me in the darkness. It was darker than any darkness I can ever remember. I sat calmly in the open, waiting with the silenced 9mm handgun in my lap aimed for the door. I wasn't worried about being caught. I knew full well that more than likely, I would be killed in just a few hours. But as long as I stood over Deville, and watched the last breaths escape from his body while he begged me for mercy, it didn't matter. Nothing did. At 1:22 AM, my date with destiny was just about to begin. I heard the squealing of breaks pull up into the driveway, accompanied by the low, unmistakeable hum of the engine of a Cadillac Deville. The engine died, and quickly, two doors slammed. Three voices laughed as footsteps made their way closer. Two were female, and I didn't recognize them. But the other, was the audible laughter of Angelo Deville. I'd heard it often, usually while losing blood and or consciousness. I tightened my grip on the gun, and listened as keys slowly turned in the door. It was time. The door opened and I could see the outline of the three people. Deville turned on the light in the foyer, and I scampered into the shadows. I couldn't risk being seen first, and would have been, thanks to the open floorplan of the house. Luckily, I was off in a corner, and out of their lines of site. He quickly walked over to the alarm, and shut it off. He put his arms out, and I could have sworn, he was staring directly at me, and knew I was there, as the ladies curled up into his arms, planting kisses on his neck. He grinned and deeply closed his eyes. I couldn't get a clear shot at him. Quickly, he escorted the ladies into the bar area just across the room from where I stood. Had he turned the lights on, I would have been seen for sure. Luckily, he just grabbed a bottle of bubbly, and walked into another room. The bimbos hung on his every word as they sat on a couch. Time started to blend together. I remember Deville taking pictures with a digital camera of the ladies sitting nude next to each other. He spoke nothing to each, about how their bodies looked perfect, and directed them to different poses. It's a shame he didn't look closer at his own photography. I looked at the photos and in each, the flash barely picked up the outline of my black suit. I still have the camera's flash card in my briefcase and look at the photos now and then. They really were breathtaking women. 'Come here, Angie baby!' one of the bimbos said while the other crooked a finger at Deville. He smiled down at them, and moved in like a predator, spotting wounded prey. For the next hour or so, moans and screams came from the couch. I saw breasts bouncing on breasts, Deville pounding the women from behind, and the women on top of Deville. I've got to admit. At 4 years his senior, I was never quite as verile. Such a shame that a person that could bring so much enjoyment, could also bring so much pain. Soon after the ladies had fallen asleep, Deville stood up and took a long drag on the bottle of champagne. He wobbled to the window and stared outsite, looking at the moon softly bathing the outside in it's silvery light. I hope he thought about what had been, rather than the future. Because his time was quickly running out. He looked left and looking directly at me said something barely audible before he closed the curtains for the final time. He hadn't seen me. He would have known I was there, had I not been hiding behind a table. He started to walk away. It was now or never. I crept up behind him. He stood still, watching his ladies sleep in each others arms. A smile crept across his face. and then... BANG! The smell of gun powder filled the air, and the thwack of the bullet peircing his skin was louder than I'd imagined it would be. My gun at first was aimed at the back of his head, at the base of his neck. But that would have been too quick. Too easy. Deville needed to suffer. He needed to see my final victory. His body slumped as he fell forward onto a white leather couch. He turned over as blood ran down the sofa, and he made eye contact with me for the first time since OSE. 'O...O...O'Neil...' He stammered, as blood filled his mouth, choking him as he spewed a bit of the red liquid in my direction with disgust. I certainly had to have punctured a lung, and soon, he could die, drowning on his own blood, because his heart was too stupid to stop pumping to the organ. I smiled as I bent down, looking right into his eyes. He struggled for words, and his eyes looked wildly around the room. I put my hand over his mouth. His arms swung wildly, trying to connect with my face to drive me backwards for help. 'Shhh...don't waste your final breaths on some witty comment, Deville. I won. This is the final chapter.' His eyes went even wilder as he started to choke on his own blood. It coated my fingers as I let my fingers linger over his lips. He started to sit still, accepting what was happening. 'This is the end, my friend. Your final goodbye.' His eyes started to glaze, but he wasn't going to die. His amazing physical conditioning had actually stopped the blood from rushing into his lungs, and he pushed most of it out of his mouth. His grey armani suit was covered in blood as I bend over him. Quickly, I kissed his forehead, in the way of the mafia. I whispered into his ear. 'Say hello to my wife for me...You'll be seeing her soon.' His eyes came to life as he stared at me. He knew what was coming next. I put the barrel of the silencer between his eyes, and pulled the trigger. A soft thunderous clap rang through the room, and Deville slumped backwards on the couch. He never flinched, as if he was expecting it to happen. His last breath escaped his body, and I leaned over and breathed it in. I had robbed Deville of his last breath, and taken it for my own. His eyes fixated on me, as his pupils dilated, became fixed, and soon, looked at nothing. His hand curled up, and his head floated backwards. If I hadn't known better, I'd think a small smile crept up on his lips, almost thanking me for being the one to finally get to him. He'd underestimated me. And in the end, it was I who got the final revenge.
