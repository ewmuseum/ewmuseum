What Have I Done?

23 February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/23/04 What have I become? With my head snuggled so deeply into the feather pillows wrapped in pink pillowcases that matched quite well with the white sheets covering my bed , I wondered that exact question. When I was younger I dreamt that I’d be the opposite of my mother. I wouldn’t be the type who had to rely on lies and the belittlement of others to make a good image of myself. Yet as of late it seemed as if I had begun to follow in her footsteps. I was becoming what I swore I’d never be. Everyday I’d wake up to the same question waiting to pop out of mouths of the two adopted children I had custody of. “When is Chris coming back?” They’d question with a matching pair of blue eyes, both sets equally as hopeful as the other. “Someday.” I would reply each time as I rolled out of bed, obviously trying to keep the hope that I had finally lost alive in them. I had known he would never come back, but it seemed almost heartless from my parenting perspective to drop the news on their young hearts that the man they considered a father was never coming back. The question that stood out in my mind each time this was asked though was what was the greater wrong? My lies or the actual truth? But now on top of lying to my kids I found myself lying to my best friend. Though it was not totally my fault, I still had a better part of the guilt. I didn’t have to make that bet with Chris. I didn’t have to involve Chryste in the reason that Chris and I broke up. Never in the bet did Chris mention that I had to say what I said. Yet it seemed the reasonable thing to do at the time. Chryste had help Chris spread a rumor about me, so not return the favor? Of course now I look at it and it seems like the most foolish thing I could have done. Why sink to her level if I want Malphas to realize how foolish he is for dating her? Then again some saw me as foolish for dating Chris and I never listened. At the time I was happy with Chris, and that was all that mattered to me. Perhaps that’s why I felt so low for lying to Malphas about Chryste. Though Chryste deserved it, Malphas didn’t. He just like me was the victim of his relationship. Perhaps that’s why the two of us got along so well. However as much as I liked him though, I couldn’t bare to see what I saw as an effect of the bet made. Malphas too had become a fallen victim of heartbreak. The same heartbreak I never wanted to witness again after Chris had broken my heart. Which brought me back to this same question once more: What had I become? Something I never wanted to be, a selfish liar. And the reason why I had become this selfish liar who played with the emotions of others was even worse. This all happened because I had to keep my word to a man who I knew wouldn’t keep his even if I had won the bet. It was pure stupidity on my behalf. Pure stupidity that I know I’ll end up regretting forever, especially when Malphas finds out the truth from one of us.
