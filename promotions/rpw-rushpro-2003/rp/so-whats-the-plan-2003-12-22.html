So, Whats The Plan?

22 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/22/03 It’s not too often that I found myself lost in darkness and confusion but as the glowing green pixels inside of the clock formed themselves into 12:30AM I wondered what the hell I was doing out this late at night and where the hell I was. The only clues were the blurring of street lights as the 2000 Chevy Camero I was riding passenger in sped past them and a few teenagers who dared to travel out this late at night to seek a thrill. All I could do is sit and look out the window and hope that sometime soon I’d recognize something familiar which would tell me I was safe and sound, but I doubted that considering the driver was none other than the man who turned his back on me for a still unknown reason, Chris Storm. If anything, he would take me as far as way from home as possible to further play with my mind. No longer was he the caring individual I had fallen in love with. Instead, he was the kind that wanted to toy with my emotions and make me cry any chance he could. To him having me by his side right now was a not a reward, but rather a game in which he would be rewarded richly in if he learned to make the right moves. He knew that if he toyed with Jackson Rust enough he could get him to forfeit in this game and get his first win over Jackson, not in the ring, but where it hurt more, the mind. “So Chris, what’s your damn scheme this time? Kidnap me and hold me hostage till Jackson jobs to you since you know you won’t be able to get a win over him any other way?” I broke the silence with a question I had been dying to ask ever since I woke up from the nap brought on by Chris and his chloroform soaked cloth. 'Actually I had other plans for you. You know Tessa, you’ve always amazed me with how attractive you are.” Chris responded, gazing over at me with his blue eyes which seemed to light up as they focused on me. “Please Chris, don’t even attempt to humor me. We all know that the only thing that spews from your mouth is bullshit.” I rolled my own eyes of a matching color, not bothering to even look in Chris’ direction considering more than likely after his compliment some lewd comment would follow. 'Bullshit huh? Well you were you one who swallowed it all. Hook, line, and sinker. By the way how is my boy Evan doing?' He replied back in his own witty way knowing very well that if anything would catch me off guard and manage to knock me down, it would be the subject of Evan. “As if you cared how he was. But just so you know he’s doing just fine without you.” I replied, hoping that this would end the topic considering I didn’t want this to become a game involving Evan. The poor kid had already been through enough bullshit in his five years of life, he didn’t need Chris to drag him into anymore emotional games. 'Without me? But I am his loving father and you are his ever loving mother. Come on now we are parents and we should do what parents do ...if you get what I mean.' Chris said with a wink, blowing a kiss over in my direction which only further agitated me. “Give me a break, you are anything but a parent.” I snapped back to show I was in no mood for his jokes. 'Come on Tessa Bear lighten up. We are finally together again, I would think you would be enjoying this time alone. Or do you have other plans?” Chris continued on in attempts of lightening me up so that his whole plan, whatever it was, would not be as miserable for him as it would be for me. “Yes indeed I do have other plans, such as getting the hell away from you as soon as this car comes to a stop.” I retorted, still not bothering to look in Chris’ direction since I felt the last thing he deserved was my full attention. 'Ah shut the hell up and enjoy the damn ride. You always were too much of a talker.' He finally shouted, obviously sick of my comments directed towards him and frustrated that I wouldn’t give into him the same way the girls of Sweet Illusion would. “And you were always too much of a loser. Ooops did I say that? Then again it’s not like you haven’t realized that by now.” I replied with a snicker in hopes that maybe if I annoyed him enough this ride would come to an end and I could go back home where I belong. 'Loser? As I recall I am the kidnapper and you are once again the poor helpless victim. A role I am sure you are quite use to. After all you never seem to be strong enough to stop the bad stuff. Hell you can't even stand up to your own mother, bitch.' “Excuse me? Last I recall you weren’t any more able to fight off any of the crap that came our way either. As a matter of fact you were the one who ran and hid from it, while I actual stuck around and did what I could to change things. So please Chris, shut up unless you know what you are talking about.” I sunk back in my seat hoping that our destination was not much further away considering I couldn’t stand anymore of Chris and his pointless shots at me. 'I didn't run from anything. I was just able to see being with you was getting me no where. Though you still clinged to my side. That is why you returned to RPW isn't it? To cling to me more and suck my career dry!' Chris said as he narrowed his eyes towards me as if I were the devil. “Don’t you think that if that was the case, I would have been the one kidding you?” I replied with a chuckle knowing very well that his point would be proven wrong with this very line. 'I think you came crawling back with your little buddy Jacko and since you two entered RPW you two have made it your purpose to ruin my career. But I plan to fix all that.' After saying this Chris’ lips formed themselves into a full forced grin which more than likely meant trouble. “And how do you plan on doing just that? I mean obviously having your little whores attack me week after week isn’t doing much good. Did you actually manage to think of something half decent?” I replied while pondering inside my mind what on earth Chris could be planning. 'I kidnapped you. And trust me you have no clue what I have in store for you.' His grin only continued to grow larger, perhaps even larger than the ego he had seemingly formed out of nowhere. “Oh yes I do. Or at least I know what I have planned for me. The moment we get to wherever the hell you are bringing us off to I plan on getting the hell away from you and really there isn’t much you can do about that.” I bragged to Chris, though revealing my plan probably wasn’t the smartest thing to do. 'You plan to escape? Dear Tessa take may not be as easy as you think considering I’ve got the keys to this here Camero, and you’ve got….well nothing but those pretty little legs of yours and I know that even though your quite quick, you’d never be able to outrun this beauty here.' Chris replied as he tapped the dashboard of his car. Unfortunately I realized just how right he was which disintegrated any bit of hope I had left of getting away. I guess I’d just have to suck it up and learn to deal with the new Chris, the Chris that would put me through all types of hell just to defeat Jackson Rust in the one game he felt he could, mind games.
