Before Today #08 : Reliant Independence

18 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 18/01/04 'So, tell me all about this wrestling...' Lawrence Wolfenden's voice said it all. His only daughter, little Kari, the girl who had once crazed him for 5 weeks straight for a Pony, was a wrestler. How was he going to drop that in at his next dinner party? Despite unfaltering love for his daughter, that was how the Wolfenden patriarch thought. 'There's no need to say it like that, Dad,' came Karina's answer as she realised their weekly lunch-date's conversation topic had already been decided. 'It's not like I've become a Lesbian and embarked on a career as a Porn Star.' Her dismissive sense of humour didn't go down well with an over-protective, and at times over-bearing father. 'Don't even joke,' he bluntly ordered, looking up from the wine list. Just as she was within her family, Karina looked out of place within the stuffy, conservative confines of the restaurant. All around them, smartly suited business men and women engaged in power lunches, their small talk over appetisers deciding the fate of millions of dollars. Next to the father and daughter's table sat an old couple, their dress almost regal, as if the elegant decor of the place demanded that little bit of extra effort. Even Lawrence, despite being in the middle day of an extremely rare 3 day holiday, was suited and booted, tie meticulously tied and shoes blindingly shiny. Then there was Karina. 'Well, sorry,' came her rather agitated answer, not wanting to be pressed on her newest source of raw fun all afternoon. 'You're just making a big deal out of nothing...' Yes, Karina. Her hair alone contained more colour than her fellow diners' outfits combined, as if the shiny mane were a homage to Kandinsky. Not being one for elegant dresses or authoritative skirt suits, Karina just wore a baggy, loose-stitched purple sweater and beige cargo pants, long enough to ensure that the heels of her trainers were in little danger of wearing down. Normally, she'd have been asked to leave... Lucky for her, Lawrence happened to be godfather to the owner's children. 'What am I meant to make of it?' came his bemused answer, breaking off eye contact to observe how the rest of the diners were staring at his daughter. He tried to fend some off with stern looks of his own, but Lawrence knew he too sighed when he saw Kari's choice of lunch outfit. 'I accepted a long, long time ago that you were never going to be a Lawyer like Max, or even work for me at the company... But wrestling?' She checked her watch and realised they'd been seated for the entirety of 2 minutes, at which point Kari knew that it would be a loooong lunch... And it would probably be a good idea to let the wine liberally flow. 'It's not like it's my job,' she prodded back, her tone trying to make his fears sound ill-founded and dumb, 'it's like 1 night a week... And hey, it's good exercise.' Lawrence would be lying if he said that Karina hadn't followed the path he'd wanted, that he'd paved for her in life. Stubborn from an annoyingly young age, Kari had resisted any attempts to 'mould' her into the Wolfenden family design... Storming out on an ultimatum more than once. The only thing she'd caved in on was going to College, although she didn't even last the first winter... Choosing the Alps over an exam desk. 'So is Snowboarding, so is Free Running, so are all the other things you do to actually avoid having any responsibilities,' Lawrence snapped back, clearly not approving of her blasé attitude to it all. 'Am I meant to say to my Pennsylvania client base 'Oh, and this Saturday, you can see my daughter wrestle brain-dead Neanderthals on TV'?' Immediately defending the generalisation of her colleagues, amongst them new friends, Karina shot right back with something she knew would get to her father. 'Neanderthals who've gone way further in school than me.' Their voices were becoming raised, both father and daughter realising it... Forcing both to whisper their next verbal joust. 'Well maybe they had to go through college as they didn't have their parents to fall back on,' Lawrence snapped, finally finding the words to silence his uppity daughter. Kari's head dropped, as she knew he had a point. Independence was something she'd always boasted, never letting herself be controlled or guided. She didn't want to be bogged down by the working world, trapped in it's corporate cycle, so she became a lady of leisure... Flying wherever the fun was as if a trip across the globe were a footstep... ...But she wasn't the one paying for it. Then, despite the confident, out-going exterior Karina presented, she resorted back to the one sure-fire way to twist Lawrence around her little finger; Daddy's girl. 'C'mon, Dad...' the K-Wolf faux-pleaded, showing that even she would play up her girly side. 'You know I'd go crazy stuck behind a desk or back at school... I'm just not someone who's meant to be in the working world, not yet. I ENJOY what I do, it's... It's a blast.' Finally having put down the wine list, Lawrence looked back at his daughter, seeing the sincerity. He'd always said he worked so hard, operated so ruthlessly, so his children could live the lives they desired... This was the acid test of those words. 'And this wrestling,' he enquired, still finding it a little much to take, 'is fun?' 'Yeah,' an eager Kari immediately replied, 'it's like Kick Boxing, Free Running and Gymnastics all in one!' Those were maybe not the best words she could have used, especially given the ferocity of the argument when Lawrence had found out about her secret Kick Boxing tuition... Not to mention his near Heart Attack at seeing what Free Running actually was. 'So it's dangerous and reckless then?' Karina didn't want to lose the argument. She wasn't naive, she knew that people in her father's line of work only got ahead by ruining others... Once they were set on something, they always got their way. 'Look at me,' she simply asked him, attracting his attention from the lavish decor. As soon as he looked right at her, Karina's fist shot across the small table for two, grazing past his grey sideburns. Lawrence looked more than a touch embarrassed as his daughter recoiled, the eyes of the restaurant on her... Not that she cared. 'It's no more dangerous than that.' This was hard for Lawrence, and frustrating to boot. Karina enjoyed it, and she wasn't going to come to any harm... Well, not compared to the other pursuits she classified as 'fun'. Giving his full blessing was out of the question, as he could never find the words to justify it to his friends and peers, but he wasn't going to stand in the way of what Karina, his one and only daughter, wanted. 'Fine, I give up,' he light-heartedly conceded, jokingly waving a white napkin in surrender. 'But I want to come along to one of these shows, might even be a good investment.' Oh joy, thought Karina. While one edge of the sword repelled, the other slashed... As she knew her father's opinions were liable to change once her saw the inner-workings of the PWC. Despite the fact that if a fight broke out, she'd be the one protecting him, Kari knew how protective he was... And the high standards he attached to what was deemed 'good enough' for her. 'Okay, sure...' she instinctively replied, not putting much into her words. It had ended a nil-nil at full time, but Lawrence had the clear advantage come the replay. 'So, about these people you now work with...' Lawrence Wolfenden met them soon enough, and when he did, the relationship with his daughter was changed forever... Thanks, in whole, to Magnum Wolf.
