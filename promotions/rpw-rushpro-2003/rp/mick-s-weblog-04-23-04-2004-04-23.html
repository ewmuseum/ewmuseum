Mick's Weblog - 04/23/04

23 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04/23/04 Hey all you people out there. First of all, we need a name for all out fans. You know, like Hulkamaniacs, or Gabeaholics, Jonathan Conspiracy's 2 Real Fans... the name Freakazoids kinda popped into my head, but that just seems to, I dunoo, gimmicky, or something. So, if anyone can thing of a cool name for Freak Show fans, log into the RPW official forums at http://sheepsden.com/forum/ and suggest something. With that out of the way, things, believe it or not, a looking rather grim for the Freaks. See, we have no competition. Gabe just because world champ, so the tag titles are gonna just sit right there, they won't be wrestling as a tag team, far as I can tell. Of course you all saw what happened to Team Tradition, so there out of it... who's left? I heard something about these Forsaken guys, but I've never seen them backstage. And that's it. Just peachy, isn't it. Good, solid team in the RPW, and nobody to wrestle. People think tag team wrestling is dead. I'd love to prove them wrong, but the proof is in the roster. And it's not like it's limited to the RPW, most places have a lack of teams. Can't see why, tag teams add a new dimension to the ring, lets a blending of skills redfine what the matc is. Wether is two similar guys like, or I don't know, The Hardy Boys, to two completely different styles wreaking havok, like the current champs in Gabe and... that other guy. I was hoping me and Mouse could help put tag teams back on the mat. Not working yet. Well, that's it for this blog. No real match set for AD22 yet, but then... no real team to have one. Might had to resort to a singles match or something. Take Care Mick
