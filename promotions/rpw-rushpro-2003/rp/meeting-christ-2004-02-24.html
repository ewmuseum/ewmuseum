Meeting Christ

24 February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Feb. 24, 2004 'Wha…what's going on? Where am I?' Chris asked. He opened his eyes, and noticed his vision was blurred. His head throbbed with pain. It felt like a semi-truck ran over his head. The last thing Chris recalled was being pulled over by the cop, also known as Bill, and trying to get out of the ticket. Then when Chris reached for his gun everything went blank. 'What happened?' Chris asked, to no one really. He rubbed his eyes, to get a better view of his current situation. All around him were cement walls. The wall to his side had vertical bars serving their purpose. Chris had found himself sitting in a jail cell. The dire situation Chris found himself in started to set in. His mind went into panic mode; he needed to find a way out of this situation before it was too late. Voices from down the hall stole Chris' attention. 'Hate Machine wants to see him, now.' Chris recognized the voice instantly. It had that same heavy accent the man had that was at his beating in the alleyway. A shiver of fear ran down Chris' spine. The last thing Chris wanted just days before his match with AWS Man and Seymour Almasy was another beat down much like the one he received before. Had the whole ordeal been one huge set up? Footsteps echoed off the concrete walls. They got louder and louder. Chris moved against the back wall, hopping not to be seen. 'I freakin' brought him in. If it weren't for freakin' me, this guy would be long gone. I'm freakin' bringing him in, not you.' How could Chris not know who that was? It was the idioti voice of the police officer that brought him in. 'Do you know who I am?' The Italian sounded very annoyed and angry. Somewhere deep in Chris' soul he hoped it would not be the same person from the alleyway. The police officer known as Bill appeared at the cell. A sigh of relief escaped from Chris' cold lips. When the Italian from the alleyway appeared the temperature of the room dropped. Chris could almost see his breath. The officer known as Bill looked at the Italian, dressed in his nice black suit. 'I know who you freakin' are. I'm still freakin' bringing him in. I caught him!' The two stopped in front of the steel bars. Like a second wind, Chris was hit with a wind of courage. He stepped forward and faced the Italian. Neither knew each other but there was a brew of hate very visible. There was an angel of light and darkness hanging over them, taunting them. The Italian removed his sunglasses and put them in his breast pocket. 'Do not argue with me or you'll regret it,' he said to Bill. He ran a hand through his flowing white hair. Chris reached through the bars and slapped him in the face, knocking the man off balance. The officer, Bill, reached through the bars and shoved that nightstick into Chris' ribs. The blow forced Chris to back off, doubling over in pain. This new force over Chris refused to let him stay down for a second. Chris straightened up and ran at the bars trying to reach through and grab Bill. 'You see how it's freakin' done?' Bill said with a little laughter in his tone. The Italian straighten up, a visible red mark on his face where Chris slapped him. Though the officer and mobster seemed to be working for the same goal, they did not seem to agree on who would get the prize at the end. That was something that caused conflicts among them. 'Come on you sons of bitches. There is just the three of us here. Enter the god damn cell and let's get this over with!' Chris yelled at them. He pointed to the white haired Italian, 'You had to hide behind people, dodging me. Now you don't have anything to hide behind. Are you going to be too scared to fight me now?' His finger moved to the officer, also known as Bill, 'And you! I don't even know who the fuck you are and I want to whoop your ass so hard you won't just be wearing that stupid mask, you'll be wearing an entire body cast!' Chris backed away from the bars, giving them both room to entire the cell. His anger rose causing his muscles to tense up and his breathing to become shorter and heavier. Chris looked like a caged animal, his back against the wall with nothing to lose. What did he have to lose really? They could grind his bones to dust but Chris would still be the RPW Mid South Champion. They could smash his ever limb but Chris would still be able to hold his head up high. He would not - no - he refused to back down from anyone at any time. 'My name is Chris Storm. I am the RPW Mid South Champion. I am the Last Great Technical Wrestler walking on the globe. You simply can not, and will not defeat me,' He stated, holding out both his arms. His little speech did not lure either man into the cell like he had hoped. All his words generated were applause from the Italian, who wore his fine black suit like a suit of armor. He was a modern day knight with his long white hair falling gently over that black armor, his gun at his hip like a mighty sword. 'Lovely speech, but you fail to realize that I have you right where I want you.' 'And I had your momma right where I wanted her last night, bitch,' Chris replied with a heavy tone of mockery. That calm exterior had been dashed to the side with those insulting words. The Italian tried reaching through the bars at Chris, who jumped back and laughed. Suddenly the Italian stepped back and fixed his suit. He gave a confused Chris a warm smile and shook his finger at him. 'Oh no, you're not getting me to play your game. She wants me to bring you before her. And I will do just that.' 'You must be living a fantasy if you think I'm going anywhere with you. You put one foot in this cell with me and I'll suplex your ass straight through the concrete wall behind me.' Chris turned his head and looked at Bill, 'What? You want to try something laughing boy? Go back to your little Jason wannabe club, you're nothing but a freak.' Chris' disrespectful words rubbed the thin line of their patience. The Italian leaned in against the bars, 'Listen to me, you little piece of shit. The only reason you are still alive is by the grace of Christ. Do not continue to press your luck or not even Christ can save you.' His words hissed at Chris like a venomous snake ready to strike. The words hung in the air like a massive guillotine, posed to fall and strike. All three men were silent. All the cards had been laid out. This moment decided everything, the grand battle of the three sides: the enigma known as Bill, the proper and edgy Italian, and Chris Storm - three people who never actually met before a few days ago. Now they were locked in a struggle over dominance. 'I say we freakin' go in there and just knock him senseless. Then I can freakin' bring him in,' the officer stated, finally adding his opinion. Bill started to open the cell door but the Italian cut him off, pressing his hand against the door keeping it shut. 'And give him what he wants? Go get your stun gun; we will do this the easy way.' 'I don't know how you every freakin' got as high as you did. It has to be a freakin' fluke or something. You sit on the global like king yet you freakin' dodge his challenges? Just beat him the freak up and show why you should be so high and freakin' mighty!' Bill dared him. Chris sat back and smiled, enjoying the show. Whoever the Italian was, the police officer was annoying him now. The officer, known as Bill, shoved the Italian and went to open the cell door again. If this Mafia man was too scared to beat respect into Chris, the officer would it himself. Before he could get the door open the hands of the Italian wrapped around the man's neck, the right hooking his chin. He gave him no warning. 'SNAP!' The lifeless body of Bill fell to the floor. Even Chris looked shocked at the actions. He had killed in cold blood, and it was nothing to him. The Italian even had a sick and sinister smile on his face. 'You heartless bastard, you just killed him for nothing!' Chris shouted, moving further back in the cell. The Italian took his sunglasses from his breast pocket and slid them on, turning his head toward Chris. He still had that satisfied smile on his face. 'That's business.' His words had no remorse or sympathy. They were just cold and calculated. He was a man of business, a warrior who focused on the task at hand and allowed nothing to stop him until the battle was over. He was by far one of the more dangerous types of fighters. 'You just messed yourself over though. There is no way you can take me down by yourself. Maybe if you had kept your alliance with him the two of you could have worked as a team to take me down, but now the second you step in this cell I'll break your balls and suplex you straight back to hell,' Chris told him, holding his chin up high. He had not fully believed his words, but he spoke like he had. There came no reply, just footsteps. The Italian walked out of sight, leaving Chris confused. Chris moved to the corner of the cell, trying to see where the man went. He could hear something, a case maybe, being unlocked. Something slammed on a desk. The man opened a lid. Chris tried to picture the events going on in his head by the sounds, but he just could not see it. 'Click.' Chris needed not to see this one; he knew what it was. It was the sound of a gun loaded. The footsteps came back toward him. Chris' mind again went into panic mode. He needed a place to hide, somewhere to go, but he was trapped. The Italian man stepped in front of the cell again, both arms draped at his sides. His long white hair covered most his face. His right arm moved slowly up, a gun in it. He pointed right at Chris. Chris looked around franticly for anything, something that could help him. He saw nothing. It would be like shooting fish in a barrel. His finger pressed against the trigger, the muscles in his hand tightened. They say that before you life you see your entire life flash before your eyes. Is that true? Maybe it is one review of how you have lived before having judgment passed at the gates with St. Peter. For Chris it was no judgment, it was a nightmare. Hatred - emotional and deep-rooted - was the only vision that flashed before his eyes. Some of his bloodiest battles he had endured passed before him. Moments that had often kept him awake at night. Chris was brought back to his debut in WIW. He saw how he turned on the man that gave him everything, Genesis. Chris dropped the big man with his trademark Tombstone Piledriver and climbed the ropes, steel chair in hand. Like the cold-blooded Italian, Chris had not even blinked when he flew down driving that steel into his master's face. How many bones did Chris break? He never knew; he never visited his former mentor in the hospital to find out. There was a flash of white. Chris suddenly saw behind the eyes of a mask. It was a mask that hid his own sinister smile while under the disguise of the Unknown; Chris cut the brake cables to a fellow wrestler's rented car. Later that night, after the show, the wrestle got into a fatal wreck. The saddest part, Chris could no longer even remember the wrestler's name. He just remembered he killed him because he wanted his spot on the card. Chris wanted to be the main eventer, the number one man. So he killed. Another flash brought Chris back to his New York apartment. A feeling of warmth and safety fell over him, until he saw Tessa Windsor. He used her. Chris saw a pretty, young virgin and wanted to use her to fulfil his own sexual fantasies. For months he played with her, like she was a puppet. Emotions were nothing but a means to get what he wanted from her. He failed this time; Tessa never truly fell for his tricks. That failure brewed utter hate for her, hate that still lived in his heart. Then he saw it again, that vision on the brick wall - a young woman with black raven hair, being crucified in an alley. He could almost feel her pain. It was like he knew of her tortured soul. He reached out to move her coal black hair so that he may finally see her face but he was ripped away from his visions. Chris opened his eyes; he had not died. 'Good morning sleepy, had a good rest?' Chris could hear the feminine voice but his brain ached too much to place it. His disembodied state got shocked when cold water got thrown at him. Chris coughed, spitting up water. He sat up, rubbing his eyes. The haze left his eyes allowing him to see the surrounds he found himself in. Vibrant velvet red carpet covered the floor, running to the eggshell white walls. The far wall was complete glass, overlooking what appeared to be New York City. In front of the glass wall was a magnificent desk. Solid mahogany wood, the finish was absolutely beautiful. A giant chair positioned itself behind the desk; it's back facing toward where Chris was. Questions raced through his head causing it to ache greatly. Chris tried to rise from his knees but a hand on his shoulder kept him from doing so. From behind him he could hear a man speaking. 'Sunday you face two men, correct?' The question confused Chris. What did they care if Chris had a match on Sunday? Did they want him to take another dive? 'Uh…yeah, for the Global title. I face Seymour Almasy and AWS Man,' Chris replied. 'Do you have the ten thousand dollars you owe us?' the person asked Chris. Chris knew the most he had on him and in bank accounts could be no more than $8,000. The life of an independent wrestler was not the richest of lifestyles and gave him little money to save up after car payments, rent, food, and bills. 'No…' Chris paused, catching his breath. He closed his eyes. 'No, I do not.' 'Then what do you suppose we do?' There showed no emotion or feeling in this person's voice. Almost like he was mocking Chris with this questions, like they knew the answers but wanted to hear him say it to embarrass himself. 'I'm not taking the fall.' Chris had power behind his words. There was a brief pause. 'What do you mean by that?' the man asked. Chris tried to stand again, to add even more power to his words but the hand again kept him on his knees. 'I refuse to willingly lose to Seymour or AWS Man. I will do everything in my ability to defeat both of them and actually give PTC a real Global champion instead of some joke. I am a real wrestler, not some actor. I do not go out there to entertain the fans. I go to wrestle. That is why I must not and will not take the fall.' 'Oh we don't want you to lose; we want you to destroy Seymour Almasy.' Chris knew that voice! His eyes grew as large as saucers. He looked straight ahead at the back of the chair, where the voice came from. The chair started to rotate around showing the person who just spoke. Long black hair covered the face but Chris did not need to see her face anyways. He knew the voice of Chryste better than anyone. 'Now Chrissy-poo, I could easily crush you here and now and end my little game with you, but you see, you are in the position to actually help me. I'm as surprised as you are personally,' Chryste stated. The Pretty Hate Machine kicked her feet onto the desk and leaned back. She waved her hand at the guy beside Chris, letting him know he could remove his hand from Chris' shoulder. Once the hand removed its hold from Chris' shoulder, Chris came to his feet and started to march toward the desk. The goons on the side reached into their suits, going for their guns. Chris noticed but did not stop. He slammed his fist on her desk. 'What the fuck is going on Chryste? Answer me before I snap your good knee as well!' Fire came from his words. Chris truly had hate for Chryste; it was mutual. Chryste was unimpressed with Chris' banter; she had seen it all before. She played with a pen on her desk. 'It is simple Chris, even for you. I heard of your match with Adam Jacobs and made a bet. To ensure I would win I had some of my boys ask you to take the fall. Course you didn't listen to me did you Chris? Always have to be Mr. Wrestler. Like some superhero or maybe super villain…trying to purge wrestling free of the worthless actors and deadbeat talents.' 'Cut the crap Chryste, you have more money then men on the side. You wouldn't care about a measly ten grand. Or what, you blew all your money on the ponies?' Chris could not seem to believe the words that came from her. His anger boiled with each word she spoke. 'At first I thought of just having you killed,' she said very coldly. Her words even froze Chris. 'But then I learned of your little Global title match with Seymour Almasy. Now I don't care for his silly little belt, I could easily win it even with my bum knee. But I detest going anywhere near that disillusioned freak.' She leaned forward in the chair and looked at Chris, dead set in the eyes. 'Which is why I need you.' Chris stood up straight as the Pretty Hate Machine stared at him, burning a hole in his soul. 'Chryste, cut the crap and get to the bottom line.' She just smiled at him. 'You defeat Seymour and take his precious little Global title and I remove your debt. It's that simple.' The deal had been laid out. It took Chris almost a second to make his decision. 'Deal.' Delighted, Chryste jumped out of her desk and came across the desk to shake Chris' hand. Chris looked repulsed at her. Noticing the look Chryste removed her hand from near his. 'Come on Chris, it will be easy, you know you can beat him. I'll even give you an extra few thousand if you injury him!' Chryste added. 'I don't want your money,' Chris stated firmly. 'I'm winning that match for my own personal reasons.' With that Chris motioned to leave. The two goons stepped in front of Chris preventing him from going any further. Chryste motioned for them to get out of the way. They did so and Chris again walked toward the door. Before he reached the massive double doors, Chris turned his head and looked at Chryste over his shoulder. 'You know, I saw my life flash before my eyes today,' he told her. The statement had Chryste somewhat dumbfounded. '…And?' she asked him, cautious to where this might lead. 'I saw a cold and heartless man. I saw a man who stopped at nothing to win. He gave blood, sweat, and tears to be the victor in each and every match. He cared about nothing and felt nothing but the feeling he got when he wrestled. That was the only driving point of his very being. He knew nothing else, no family, no loved ones, no nothing. He had just the ring and an opponent. And you want to know what else Chryste…' 'What?' 'I am that man.' With those words Chris turned to leave. 'Chris,' Chryste said, stopping him. 'You saw yourself going to hell, didn't you?' Chris turned around and looked at Chryste. His body gave nothing away. He showed zero emotion. Then he cracked a smile out of the corner of his lips. 'And I had the Global title with me.' Chris walked out of the doors and onto Sunday where he would compete for the PTC Global title. Three men, one belt, there could be only one winner.
