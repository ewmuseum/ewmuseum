Manhunter - 1 - Icarus

22 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: December 22nd, 2003 New job, more training...it never ended. Unfortunately for this lot of people, not a single one of them had any clue who or what this new fighter was. She knew that this new company had your plethora of run of the mill 'tough girls', but adorable as that was...it wasn't Katarina Dimoni. Whether she was more or less was subjective, but the sure thing was that a mess wouldn't be travelling too far behind. There goes your packaged introduction. The can of worms was pried open far too early, though. This was her very first day in America, getting oriented with her career and the only outfit in it with administration off-kilter enought to hand her a paycheck. The mess began the second Dimoni stepped out of the hotel room they'd holed her up in, because a wheeled rack of fine wine was moving somewhere across her line of vision and, considering how sick and stuffed of information she'd been all day, a break was necessary. She was thirsty and whoever was receiving that alcohol would be made willing to share it. So off she went, behind the bellhop attached to the cart that carried the night's entertainment in several dark glass bottles. Little did she know, however, that her day of RPW orientation was far from over...even this escape couldn't save the little Russian from it. Finally, the bellhop reached destination as he stopped in front of a two door bedroom suite. Knocking on the door, he pushed the rack of wine ahead of him, preparing himself to push it inside the room when the tenant opened the door. A few moments passed which served Dimoni's thirst to augment greatly, until finally the right door opened, and dressed in a rather expensive abyssal suit appeared none other then Maxwell's cohort, Tristan Belmont. He had a smug, satisfied grin drawn upon his thin lips as he looked down at the cart of wine. Reaching into his pocket, he pulled out a bill without looking to see the amount and stuffed it in the bellhop's pocket, which began pushing the cart into his room. It was then that Belmont saw her. Even though he had no idea whom she was, and why she was walking into his room, he still stood in awe at the marvel that strutted in front of his black orbs. She walked past him, brushing her hair as the Serpent Prince inhaled the many tastes and flavors of air that brushed by him. The Demonic Warrior finally snapped back to his senses, brushing off her thought and looking at the beauty, before dropping his hand upon her small shoulders and stopping her walk. ''I don't remember hiring any entertainment, miss.'' He said with a cold tone and a furrowed brow. Her neck-length, jet black hair almost matched his own - they'd be mistaken for siblings if not for the woman's much more delicate features and striking ice-blue eyes, which contrasted Belmont's directly as they stared in a mildly offended challenge. ''You think I...'' She smiled wryly and wrapped a small hand around the neck of one chilled bottle. It was hardly a threat, but preparation in case he didn't remove his forceful grip on her shoulder. ''That's a cute stab, but no. I'm here because I seriously doubt a man of your appearances would want to get as drunk as all of this would make you.'' The game began. Kat was of the belief that she could convince anyone of anything. If nothing else, the girl was confident in her intellect. ''It really would be embarassing to get so intoxicated that you ran through the hall in your skivvies, wouldn't you agree?'' Belmont looked at her with an amused grin upon his lips. Knowing now that she wasn't here to attack him but rather for a drink, he let go of the grip he held and slowly she let go of the bottle that she'd held so menacingly. He looked at her, up and down, examining the woman whose name was still unknwon to him before taking a step back and nodding his head. ''Do not worry for me, I'm fairly sure I can take care of my own self and my self-control is not a topic to dispute. However, your class is. Who are you to step into the bedroom of a man such as myself?'' Belmont looked down at the large cart of wine and at the usher who just stood, paralyzed by the intensity of the scene. He looked at him, and then looked at the open door, which signaled that his presence was no longer needed and without any resistance, the bell hop walked out of the room. ''As for this wine, I do intend on enjoying their aromas for tonight, dear, is a night where I celebrate many successes, and you are not one of them. You are a simple intruder.'' Belmont was not a very nice man. Indeed the Serpent Prince, though infatuated by the woman's lovely aura, wanted to keep a tough front. He sensed from her pose that she would not let go so easily, and that is why he knew he could fight her a little verbally. The truth was, he wanted her to stay. Despite his comments and commands, Dimoni smiled. ''First I'm mistaken for entertainment in the form of a sex trade worker and now I'm labelled an intruder, a criminal...'' Even her own tone opposed him - a warm, inviting contrast to his icy sneer. ''Really, for such an attractive man, you have absolutely no idea how to treat beatiful women who wander into your bedroom. Most would find it quite a happy event, and I'm just glad this wine wasn't sending me to get drunk with a fat man.'' Without being offered or asked she removed her suede winter jacket and tossed it over the back of a rather senselessly fancy chair, exposing better a black tanktop that put a fighter's body on display. She was lithe, toned, without a battle mark and all too confident. Belmont looked at her, a hint of interest finally piercing through his eyes, long enough for Kat to see it. He grinned as she returned the smile, before taking steps towards the wine cart and pulling out a bottle. After removing the cork, he dropped the crimson liquid into the glasses that he had already set out on the desk in front of him, seeing her watch hungrily as the alcohol crashed against the sides of the glass. ''It's not that I do not know how to treat beautiful women love, rather that when I look at you I see nothing of a beautiful woman. I see a sin I don't mind committing.'' And with that, the glass finally full, he handed it to the unknown woman in his apartment. She studied him carefully as they drank silently, grinning to herself inside of the glass. Dimoni loved men - moreover, how simple they were and how easy that made it to get what she wanted, when she wanted it. This one, though...he intrigued her. He'd given into what she wanted, but was obviously aware of the games she was playing... He was a mindfucking rival - equal, even - and that was something Katarina could not go without testing. -Joint effort by K&J-
