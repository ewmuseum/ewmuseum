How This All Came To Be: Part 3 'The Final Moments'

21 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/21/04 The last moments of “The Schmoopies” would be something I’d never forget. They were moments that played throughout my head everyday when I woke up and realized that the fairy tale life that I once dreamed of, no longer existed. The only thing I had left now were my two adopted children, but even though didn’t seem to care much about me anymore. The only time they bothered to associate themselves with me anymore was to ask when Chris would come back and go back to being what they looked up to him as, a father. It’s too bad they didn’t know the truth about what happened that April night, where I knew that things would never be the same again. It was the night where I knew any sort of bond between Chris and I would be crushed. It was that night that stuck out most in my mind when I thought back to how it all ended. The evening was unusually cold for April. Perhaps it was the emotions I was feeling that added that chill to the air, or maybe it was just the unpredictable New England weather. Either way, it led to me nestle on the leather loveseat that sat across from the fireplace which I rarely lit. Tonight however I felt it needed to be lit, so that the hope inside of me would still burn. Chris was due to come home today, but with the clock striking ten it seemed as though maybe he wouldn’t come home. It seemed as though home for him was somewhere else now… ...Or maybe it wasn’t. The front door swung open and in walked an obviously distressed Chris. It wasn’t the Chris I knew though. His hair which was usually combed back in a spiky manner which gave it life, was flat and muddled. His blue eyes which usually glittered with love and affection, carried no expression. It was almost as if I were staring at a zombie in a nightmare that would never end. “Chris!” I cried out in joy as I leapt off the couch and ran off in his direction. The distance between didn’t dissipate though, it only grew larger as he turned to walk the other way. “Not now Tessa.” He swatted away my arms which I hoped he would welcome warmly, obviously though this wouldn’t be a night where wishes were granted. “All right.” A sigh escaped from my lips as my head drooped back down while following him up the stairs. This action apparently wasn’t welcome though, as Chris turned around and glared at me in a way that questioned what I was doing. “I’ll leave you alone.” “No need to, you won’t be seeing much of me after tonight so you might as well cherish the last few moments.” The callous tone in his voice cause my heart to recoil in horror, the worst part of it all is that this wasn’t a nightmare that I could wake myself up from and end, it was reality. “What?” The tears may not have been present in my eyes, but they were there in my voice, causing it to quiver. I looked up at him with the puppy dog eyes which he’d usually give in to, but this time they had no effect on him. He had made up his mind, he was leaving and there was nothing I could do to stop him. “You heard me, it’s over.” He said just as bluntly as before, continuing up the stairs while leading the way to the room that we would no longer share. Despite already hearing it, it still hurt just as much to hear it the second time around. “But…why…” It was a question I’m sure Chris already knew would come from my mouth as I froze at the bottom of the stairs as the reality of this all sunk in, while my question still waited to be answered. “Because I don’t love you.” He replied within seconds of my question, giving me little time to think of what his reasoning for all this may be. But now there was no need for me to think, now there was an answer, an answer to a question that I now wished I had never asked. “In fact, I don’t think I ever loved you.” The second blow was even worse than the first. It was the finishing blow. Nothing else needed to be said, Chris had said it all. He didn’t love me anymore, he never loved me. Any further questioning would only make the impact of the blows increase. So I accepted the answer given to me and headed back to the living room to take shelter. It was over, and that was that. But the question that would forever remain in my mind would be, how could something like this happen? How could a love that seemed so real manage to have never existed? That question haunted me to this day, still waiting to be answered. So if Chris wanted to take me out of this business, than let him do that. The only thing I ask for him in return is to answer that very question while looking me in the eye, so that I can tell if it was a lie, or if he really did never care. My bet is on the first, but he won’t admit it. He’s afraid of love just as every other guy is.
