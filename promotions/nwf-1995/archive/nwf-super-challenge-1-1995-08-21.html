Matches for NWF Super Challenge 1 --the Brotherhood vs. Death Inc. w~/ Master Kim This match was signed because of the attack by Death inc. on the Brotherhood at Supercard ultimately causing the Brotherhood to lose their titles. Brotherhood stormed the ring and pummeled Death Inc. and Kim. Death Inc. got the upper handwhen the Death Bringer distracted McGriff. But the Brotherhood fought back and Jones nailed Black Magic with a forearm off the top rope for the 1,2,3.. Brotherhood cleared the ring afterwards beating up all the members of Death Inc. Winners: (7:15) Brotherhood ---Intercontinental title match Captain America Rick Dorry vs. Arabic Animal Dorry was accomponied to the ring by Carl Melbourne. The Arabic animal put up a good fight but was caught in a DDT and got pinned. Winner and still champion: Captain America Rick Dorry interview for Rugged Ron Percy and KO Kid Kevin Allan (scene opens on a wrestling ring. In the middle of the ring is a jobber lying on top of a table. Allan climbs to the top and does a moonsault onto the jobber and the table folds in half...Allan picks up the jobber and piledrives him on the chair...he notices the camera and walks over to it...) Allan: Who told you to come in here? Huh..you must not like living very much...(Allan grabs the top rope and saumersaults outside the ring onto the camerman, sending both he and the camera hard to the floor. He picks the camera up and sets it the second turnbuckle) So Mr. Airbourne "futureshock" if you will....you seem to have a problem with me. I had that pantywaist Chris Certains beat beyond belief and then you decide to get in my way? Well that aint smart...but that's okay...see you showed some balls and I gotta repect that...not many would have the guts to step up to the KO Kid...but you did...and now your a problem...a problem I gotta deal with...and I got just the solution...cmon in big man... (A straping man walks onto the set. He stands 6"8 and most of it is rock hard muscle. A beard covers his chin and his blue eyes blaze with intensity) Allan: Boys I'd like you to meet Rugged Ron, he's my new tag team partner. RR: And your worst nightmare... Allan: Ron's not big on talk...he just likes hurting people...and come this Wednesday night, he's going to lay a big hurting on you FutureShock...and as for you Certains...well that's gonna be my fun... (Rugged Ron jumps onto the ring apron and steps over the ropes. He grabs the jobber by the throat and picks him up and slams him onto the table... Allan: Good gawd are we gonna have some fun...we're outta here... (picture fades out as Ron is picking the jobber back up and slamming him again) ----KO Kid Kevin Allan and Rugged Roncy vs. Future Shock Danny Airborne/Chris Certains The match started with all four men in the ring and was a wild melee for 5 minutes with Allan superkicking Certains out off the ring. Roncy fought with Airborne out of the ring and after about ten minutes the referee called for the bell and disqualified both teams. Roncy and Allan cleared the ring and Certains and Airborne left to the dressing room. --Bryan Thomas The Awesome 1 vs. Davy Airborne Thomas won with a flying elbow off the top rope. ----8 man tag match: Armed Forces(Ricky Kernodle,Ron Johnson)/ Captain Flagg/Ultimate Soldier vs. Invader Johnny Banger/ Danny The Ace Corbin/Heria Volcanot/BarryUSAStevens - Johnny Banger dominated the match with the Armed Forces pummeling Corbin and Volcanot every time they got in the ring. The Ultimate Soldier looked impressive in his debut. The end came when Kernodle was splashed off the second rope by the 7'6 Johnny Banger he camera opens on Hilton Flames, sitting in what appears to be a nightclub, preparing for opening. He is surrounded by a wide variety of very lovely ladies. [Hilton Flames] Hilton Flames, the "smooth operator" and the NWF Heavyweight Champion, now I like the sound of that! The man with the most moves, now has the gold around his waist! I can't say that I didn't expect it, it was only a matter of time before I showed the world that I'm the "mac-daddy" in and out of the ring! I plan on staying the champ for a long time to come, and frankly I don't there is anybody in the NWF that can take this gold away from me......hell, I don't think there is anybody in the world that can take it! I will say one thing however, anytime anybody wants to take a shot at this belt, step on up! I'm the heartbreakin', dream-makin', smooth operator and I'll put your a$$ to same!!!! Heavyweight Title Match Hilton Flames vs. Mongolian Warrio - clasic match up going both ways for over ten minutes. The Warrior picked up flames in a press slam and covered for the 1,2,,,,. but Flames kicked out Then later Flamespicked up the Warrior and Snake eyed him and only got a 2 count. Both men fell out of the ring and as the Warrior tried to get back in Flames held his leg and both men were countout. Very Intelligent but cowardly way to keep the belt. You could tell the Warrior was frustrated. Flames is still the champion though. His enemies are piling up. The heel Wrestling's ruling family and Bryan Thomas, and faces Davy Airborne and Mongolian Warrior will all be gunning for the belt in the next few weeks. In an earlier match Steve Thomas defeated Mammoth Matt Newmanin 12:13.with a drop kick off the top rope. Imprssive debut for both men.
