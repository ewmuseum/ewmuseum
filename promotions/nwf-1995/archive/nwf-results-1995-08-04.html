NWF Results (Providence, RI)

4 August 1995

Wrestling Card for Friday, August 4 Providence, RI 1. KO Kid- Kevin Allan vs. Future Shock Danny Airborne - great match between these two ending in a 10 minute time limit draw. KO KId will be wrestlinr Chris Certains at Supercard. Very promising future for this competitor. 2. Death Inc. & The Death Bringer w/ Master Kim vs. Armed Forces & Captain Flagg - Death Inc. dominated in their first match in the NWF. look for them to go a long way. The ending came when the Death Bringer pinned Captain Flagg with a piledriver. Captain Flagg looked miserable in his debut with the NWF. Master Kim later in an interview requested a match between Death Inc and Brotherhood at the next TV Tapings this weekend. 3. Hilton Flames vs. The Shamrock Hilton Flames pinned the Shamrock in 5:03 after the snake eyes to the turnbuckle. This seemed like a warmup for the title match at Supercard. 4. Michael The Destroyer Corbin/ Boy Toy Stephen Michaels vs. Terrordome ( Mankiller Drake & Annihilator) - this match ended in a double disqualification after both teams used chairs and mutually knocked out the referee. 5. Mongolian Warrior vs. Arabic Animal The mongolian Warrior pinned Arabic Animal after a powerslam in 6:57. 6. Invader Johnny Banger/ Heria Volcanot w/ Danny The Ace Corbin vs. Matthew Powers/ John The Butcher Animal John The Butcher Animal pinned Volcanot with the AXE after 15:24. The Invader destroyed both Animal and Powers after the match and had to be restrained by NWF officials. 7. Heavyweight Champion- Davy Airborne vs. Steel City Warrior Davy Airborne defeated the Steel City Warrior in 6:08 after Warrior went for a rebel rack and Airborne reversed the move into a pin combination. 8. Main Event: ICC Captain America Rick Dorry Heavy Hulkster Michael Hayes Chris Certains vs. Major Ricky Slaughter Dr. V Veto Von Bricke Carl Melbourne Melbourne pinned Certains in 8:34. 9. Tag Team Title Match Champions- Brotherhood vs. Masked Destroyers Brotherhood defeated Masked Destroyers Matches for Supercard VII Invader Johnny Banger vs. John THe Butcher Animal Brotherhood vs. Melbourne/ and mystery partner
