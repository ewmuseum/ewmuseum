Sorry it took so long but July 16 TV Tapings KO Kid Kevin Allan debut against Heavyweight champion Davy Airborne. The match was back in forth with great aerial moves by both . The match ended when Allan missed a moonsault and Airborne went to the top rope and splashed him for the count. The crowd cheered both men. Great first match for the Kid. Afterwards the Invader Johnny Banger at 7'4 came to the ring and pummeled Airborne and Allan both and left them lying in the ring. According to his manager Danny The Ace Corbin he is demanding a title shot. In other matches the Shamrock debuted and got mixed reaction from the crowd. Also on the card were Michael The Destroyer Corbin and the Mercenaries (Slash and Machete) in squash matches. The Main Event was a 5 team elimination match for the vacated NWF Tag team Title. The Teams were: Z-Force ( Stevie Fever and Carl Mad Dog Melbourne) Heavy Metal Express ( Nick Simpson and J>D. Stone) Heria Volcanot & Hilton Flames Brotherhood(Curtis McGriff & Michael Jones) Armed Forces ( Ricky Kernodle & Ron Johnson) Nick Simpson pinned Ron Johnson to eliminate the Armed Forces Jones pinned J.D Stone to eliminate the Express Flames dominate the rest of the match-up destroying Melbourne and then pinning Fever with a boot to the face. He then was pinned when Volcanot accidentally hit Flames instead of Jones. The were brief words between the 2 but Danny The Ace broke them up. They later demanded next week for a title shot. TV TAPINGS July 23 1. Heavy Metal Express defeated Z- Force and in the process injured Stevie Fever left leg with a chair 2. Pretty Boy Doug York defeated Chris Valiant in 8:03 with a sling shot gordbuster. 3. Chris Certains was challenged in the next match by KO Kid Kevin Allan. The match was dominated by Certains until at the 7 minute mark the KO KId caught Certains in a high cross body block and scored an unbelievable upset victory over a member of wrestling's ruling family. Certains was livid after the match up. The crowd went nuts. 4. Main Event TAG TEAM CHAMPIONS Brotherhood vs. Heria Volcanot/ Hilton Flames Flames dominated the beginning of the match and tagged in Volcanot who continued to destroy McGriff. The Brotherhood later won when Jones pinned Volcanot with a loaded forearm smash. Flames and Volcanot left the ring seperately. In other matches in the past two weeks at House Shows to note were: In a match for the Heavyweight title Davy Airborne was attacked before his match by Hilton Flames and left lying in the ring. Later on Airborne was able to beat Heavy Hulkster Michael Hayes after Flames hit Hayes and Airborne schoolboyed him for the pin. The crowd was shocked. Carl Melbourne pinned the Intercontinental champion Rick Dorry in a non-title matchup.
