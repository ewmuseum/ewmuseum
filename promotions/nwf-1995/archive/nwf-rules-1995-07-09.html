NWF Basically it is a role-playing federation, like D&Dragons format. Each wrestler has stamina pts and a class level. When your wrestler starts he is put into class 1 and can move up to 10, which is championship level. He gates tryouts where he wrestles weaker competition to see what level to put him in. For every win his stamina pts for matches moves up also. Each wrestler has an offensive and defensive move card and specialty move and cheating sections. Outside interence, steel cage , and all other things can happen to.
