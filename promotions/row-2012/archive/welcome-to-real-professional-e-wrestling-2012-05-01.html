Welcome... to real professional (e-)wrestling.

1 May 2012
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
DEF_Vagabond — 1 May 2012 Aloha, amigo! My name is Timothy, but you can call me Vagabond if ya' want to (you might see me in the real world of wrestling some day going by that name). Whatever you choose to call me, just don't call me late for dinner. I like to be on time for that sh!t, you know what I mean?
I've been involved in both the real sport and in this hobby of ours for well over a decade. Whew! Time flies! It's been real, it's been fun. It's been real, real fun. I've made a lot of friends along the way, a few enemies, and a really, really big list of...
... yeah, I got nothin'. Point is--right here, right now--marks my first official statement as the RO!W Chief Executive Officer. Right On! Wrestling has been bubbling on the backburner for roughly a year. I'm proud to say it's about to come to life and get all real up in hurr. Fo' realz, yo.
I like to consider myself a people person; you work with me, I'll work with you. But there's a catch: Right On! Wrestling isn't your day-to-day indie promotion. We take pride in our performances, both inside the ring and out. We hold ourselves to a higher standard here at RO!W Inc. I expect you'll do the same if you're interested in joining our ranks. For the exact same reason I chose Right On! Wrestling, I need you to do it right or find the nearest exit (see that 'x' at the upper-righthand corner? Click that if you don't think you can handle it).
Of course, I'm making it seem a lot harder than it really is, but that's because I take pride in my creations, and I do like to apply a certain level of realism into my favorite hobby. It's like an actor getting ready to shoot a scene, you have to be in the zone. Know what I mean, chico?
Anyway, the first card for "W!CKED Wednesdays" will be announced within the week (meanwhile, I still have to finish putting everything together, and this will continue to be a work-in-progress). Expect to see a Roster Template put up very soon. If you're interested in joining, you're more than welcome to fill it out and send it back to me. The first ten people who sign up to wrestle at the very first WW will be placed inside a special sort of guantlet match to declare the first-ever Regional Champion (which is the equivalent to the Intercontinental Title)!
I reward those who do their best, there's no disputing that. I look forward to working with you in the near future. Thank you for your interest in Right On! Wrestling!
- Timothy J, CEO
