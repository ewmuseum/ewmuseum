Headbutt (possibly loaded) 13. Backdrop driver 14. Belly-to-belly suplex 15. Second-rope fist drop Two Preferred Submission Holds: 1. Single-leg crab 2. Leglock sleeper Two Types of Reversals/Counter-Attacks: 1. Low blow from a kneeling position 2. Duck a charge, leading to a ref bump. Signature Move(s): 1. Springboard rana (the deal being he's a big guy with a good sense of balance) 2. Hanging vertical suplex Finishing Move(s): 1. Wrathful Strike (Super Kick) 2. Corps-à-corps (Cobra Clutch Sleeper) What can you bring to the table? Years of experience, tons of enthusiasm and endless unsolicited suggestions. Tell me more about yourself... I have always enjoyed the character aspect of wrestling over the in-ring action, but I still appreciate a wrestler who may be weak in the promo department but can still tell a story in the ring. I am sadly limited in my participation, but enjoy e-fedding immensely.
