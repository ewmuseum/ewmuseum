Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Right On! Wrestling was announced on the hub on 1 May 2012 by a CEO who said it had simmered on the back burner for about a year and who promised a weekly show, W!CKED Wednesdays, and a ten-man gauntlet match to crown a first Regional Champion, the equivalent of an Intercontinental title.

Two days later it had one signing, a masked villain called Sabre, and its last held post was the CEO's promise to run the fed with or without a roster. No card survives.
