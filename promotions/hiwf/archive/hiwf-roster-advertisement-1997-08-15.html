15 August 1997

Hey, everyone, come have a look at the HIWF (High Impact Wrestling Federation), home of the best characters in e-wrestling... Chameleon "Maddog" Gnort Smilin' Doc Watson Downtown G The Acer Outlaw Justice Bucky Jones Fergus McTavish Lazarus "Ice Man" Jeff Koch The Attitude Adjuster Jesse "All American" Ewiak Psycho Stud The Stormreavers (Rend & Maim) Mayberry RFD (Big Andy Cool & Floyd the Barber) The Nobles (Jack Diamond & The King with a Club) The LA Mafia (Dangerous Jason & Dangerous Jimmy) And MORE...... We recently lost a few member to college and need replacements. Every e-wrestler is original and unique and all interaction is top-notch! We have some of the best writers on the 'net! The HIWF proves every week, that when the inmates run the asylum, it's a helluva lot more fun!
