HIWF Seeking Handlers, 25-27 June 1997

25-27 June 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The HIWF (High Impact Wrestling Federation) is seeking good role players for it's new e-fed. You can get all the info for the HIWF by visiting the web page. http://www.geocities.com/Hollywood/Lot/4700 We have 12 singles wrestlers and 2 tag teams signed. Once we achieve a 20 singles and 6 tag team minimum we will run our first card. The HIWF is a booked and simulated fed. We use TNM 6.2 to get the play-by-play but change the ending to suit angles/storylines and to accomdate the best role player of the two.
