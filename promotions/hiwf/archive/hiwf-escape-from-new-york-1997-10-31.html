Scene opens in a run down bar, somewhere in Queens. Several drunken patrons sit at the grimy bar in the smoke filled room. An old jukebox in the corner plays a long forgotten tune. At a booth in the far corner sits a man dressed in a very expensive 3-piece suit. The man obviously does not belong there. Standing beside the Well-Dressed Man in an Immense Man who is watching the room intently. The main door opens, letting in sunlight. Several of the patrons shield their eyes from the glare. A small, Nervous Man walks in and stops, allowing his eyes to get used to the dank interior. After awhile, his eyes adjust and he spies the Well-Dressed Man seated at the booth. The small Nervous Man walks to the booth and sits down. [Nervous Man]: Sorry I'm late. I got lost. [Well-Dressed Man]: Don't sweat it. I take it you got my message? [Nervous Man]: (nodding his head) I did. But I'm still confused. How can you help me? [Well-Dressed Man]: (laughs) I can help you in many ways, Rudi. [Nervous Man]: Please don't use my first name. [Nervous Man]: What's the catch? [Well-Dressed Man]: (laughs again) Catch? There's no catch. Just a small favor if you will. [Nervous Man]: (suspiciously) What "favor"? [Well-Dressed Man]: (laughs) Sure. I send them to you by courier. Now if you excuse me.... Immense Man stands aside to let the Well-Dressed Man out of the booth. [Well-Dressed Man]: Thanks for your time, Mr. Mayor. [Nervous Man]: Shhhhhh! The Well-Dressed Man turns and walks out of the bar and gets into a waiting limousine. Immense Man gets behind the wheel and starts to drive. Well-Dressed Man picks up his cellular phone and places a call. [Well-Dressed Man]: (into his cell phone) Ismael here. Round up the boys. Its on.
