Ace In The Hole #4, 7 July 1997

7 July 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The screen fades to black. The Bay City Rollers "Saturday Night" blasts in the backround. Then a constantly changing rotating multi color title appears on the screen, "Ace In The Hole: Across The Globe".) [Voiceover: Welcome to the 4th edition of ACE IN THE HOLE: ACROSS THE GLOBE.] (Scene cuts to the Ace In The Hole main control desk, behind the desk, in a blue pinstriped three piece suit along with a white tie and neatly groomed hair, is Ace In The Hole Head Reporter, Talson E. Geddy. "Saturday Night" quietly fades into the backround as Talson E. Geddy shuffles his papers. he looks at the camera, as if he was always meant to be here.) (Behind Talson Geddy, a picture of INWA World Champion Ice - with a crosshair directly centered on Ice's nose, with the huge red letters TARGET: ICE rests...reflecting the entire focus of Ace In The Hole this entire week.) Talson Geddy (Very tired, looking like he hasn't slept much): And once again, Allen "The Acer" Carter has proven that he is worthy of being called champion! This Friday, the 4th of July, The Acer defeated Ice in a non-title match earning Acer a title shot in JUST FOUR DAYS! For those of you who weren't at INWA'S Friday Night Fever of the fourth, here's the incredible pre-Fever celebration, sponsored by Carter International, that took place! (Cut to footage from last Friday, subtitle reads, "INWA Pre-Fourth Fever Special Celebration!") [Friday Night, a half hour before Friday Night Fever. Scene opens up on the set of "The Wrestling Insider", at the control desk, in a blue pinstriped three-piece suit is veteran reporter Talson Geddy.] TG: Good evening ladies and gentlemen and welcome to my last edition of "The Wrestling Insider! That's right I'll be updating you on Allen "The Acer" Carter's career from a special INWA edition of my new show, "Ace In The Hole!" However The Insider is going out with a bang as we'll bring you coverage of "Carter International's Forth Of July Fever Fest" dedicated to the INWA Friday Night FEVER!!!!" Carter International, fo those of you who don't know, is a huge corperation owned by our own Allen "The Acer" Carter! Mr. Carter could, at any moment, buy the INWA, lock, stock and barrel. However, all he wants to do is wrestle for fun and gold. With that in mind let's take you to the beginning of the celebration LIVE from the same arena that FEVER is going to be held. Dack Foren, are you there and ready to light the sky on this forth of July? [Camera switches to a Dack Foren right next to the main stage of the arena and the camera pans to see over 30,000 some fans screaming at the top of their lungs. All are waving American Flags.] Dack Foren: To start everything off The Acer is going to be the MC of the evening and introduce his NEW entrance song! The title of his new song is "Acer's All Over The Place!" And Tal and I have heard it....it jams! So....[The crowd changes volume...as the lights on the main stage click on and dance around in a multi colored display of blue, green, red and orange! Suddenly, Allen "The Acer" Carter, Lisa and Juli charge out on the the stage. Lisa takes the drums, Juli grabs the keyboard and Carter tunes up his gituar!] DF: And Acer is a very multi talented gentlemen. He's trained all week for FEVER and now that training is going to get him the INWA World Championship! He's so pumped, I'd hate to be Ice when these two lock up!!! Acer is enjoying a little fourth of July attitude adjustment! From what I've heard, Acer trains for matches in two parts physically and mentally! This is the mental part, getting his attitude straight. Looks like Acer's ready to rock! [Camera switches and zooms in on Carter, Juli and Lisa. All three are putting on the ritz, Acer's dressed in his all black tux with sunglasses while Juli and Lisa are dressed like walking American flags. Carter takes the mic and quiets the crowd. Lisa strikes an erotic pose and the crowd goes nuts. Carter quiets them down again.] Allen Carter: I'd just like to say first, welcome to The Carter International Forth Of July Fever Fest!!! [Huge response from the crowd!] Allen Carter: And now for my new entrance song, Juli, Lisa are you lovely ladies ready to set rock and roll history? Lisa: I'm ready and steady, Allen! Juli: Let's kick it in to gear, Allen! Allen Carter: I'd like to dedicate this show to my parents, without whom, I would have not reached this level of success that I have. Mom, Dad, this is for you. [A fast, upbeat sounding rock track blares in and Carter and the ladies sing in key. The song is well recieved and when Carter gets to the middle of the song and...] Allen Carter: [Singing and gettin' down!] Acer's all over the place, Acer's all over the place he wants gold his focus is Ice cold cause Acer's all over the place everyone else is in the INWA rat race but Acer's all over the place stay out of his way, don't get on his case cause Acer's all over the place and he will get right in your face Juli and Lisa: [Also singing throughout Acer's part] Acer's all over the place, Acer's all over the place! [Acer gets into the final part of the song and finishes up with a huge gituar solo! The crowd eats it up!] Carter: Now, I must go get ready to claim that which is MINE, the INWA World Championship belt! I said I was going to get it and tonight on the Fourth Of July I take a first important step to getting the gold! Along with the founding of our country....I will stake my place among history and the true winners in life. Get ready to be ACED, Ice! Lisa and Juli have a special program for all of you! [Carter turns over the stage to Juli and Lisa and leaves to get ready for his match. The two perform holiday favorites like "God Bless America" and "The Star Spangled Banner" as well as others. During each song, the crowd turns skyward! During each of the special four songs that Juli and Lisa perform, military aircraft pass overhead as the crowd is stunned! A-10's, F-16's, F-14's and even F-117A's all pass over and to top it off, just as Juli and Lisa finish up "God Bless America" a HUGE B-2 stealth bomber shoots overhead! Juli then says let the fireworks begin and for the final five minutes of the show....all sizes and colors of fireworks explode across the sky! A huge finale of almost 200 fireworks set off at once leave the crowd breathless and we get ready for INWA's Special Forth Of July Broadcast of FRIDAY NIGHT FEVER......] (Cut back to the control desk. Tal Geddy is sipping some Hazelnut flavored coffee.) Talson Geddy: Quite a weekend it was, ladies and gentlemen. Friday we flew up to Washington D.C. to set up our main studio...then off to Lambeau Field in Green Bay to see Acer win over Ice!!!!
