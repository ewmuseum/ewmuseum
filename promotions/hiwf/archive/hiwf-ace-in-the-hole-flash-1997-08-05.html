Ace In The Hole: Flash In A Minute, 5 August 1997

5 August 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Sunday 12am. Scene opens up with Talson E. Geddy (in a bathrobe, ready to call it a night) lounging on an EZ Boy Recliner, sipping some Passionflower tea.) Talson E. Geddy: Well, Ace In The Hole fans a very exciting week here for the Men With Attitudes. (Tal sips some tea.) Starting off in the INWA, The Acer has joined up with Paul Lennon and they are now known as Guitars Inc. and it's just a rumor, but their finisher might be known as "A Hard Day's Night" we'll get beck to you on that story. Guitars Inc. also attacked the Deathstalkers during thier interview we'll keep you posted on that devlopment as well. (Tal yawns.) Also in the HIWF...THE NOBLES ARE WORLD TAG TEAM CHAMPIONS AGAIN! In just their first year in Pro Wrestling, this is the second time they have become champs!!! BTW, they beat The Hate Machine for the tag titles, the team the also defeated earlier this year in the now-almost defunct NEWA for the tag titles there also. The Acer lost to "Ice Man" Jeff Koch this Saturday due to outside interference from The Hate Machine....I see a stable war coming from that! The Attitude Adjuster, lost to "The Card Shark" and rumors a plenty are flying that he may very well be on his way out of The Men With Attitudes. (Talson gets up to recieve a incoming fax.) Talson Geddy: Hot off the presses, folks, the HIWF lineup for next Saturday and looks like YES!!!!! THE ACER'S GOT A TITLE SHOT AGAINST CHAMELEON!!! I've got to call him. Also The Nobles defend thier titles against the Drummond Brothers and The Attitude Adjuster has a chance to redeem himself against Big Andy Cool from the RFD stable on Wednesday's card. (Tal reaches for the cell phone to call The Acer.) Talson Geddy: Oh, yes, The Acer has been accepted in to the EWA! Anyway, that's Ace In The Hole - Flash In A Minute...that's the way it is August 5, 1997 and I'm your worst nightmare...(Tal cracks up at his own 'Rambo' impression) seriously....I'm Talson E. Geddy. =A9 1997, Ace In The Hole Productions
