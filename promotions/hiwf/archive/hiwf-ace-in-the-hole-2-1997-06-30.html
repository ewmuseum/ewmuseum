HIWF (Jun 1997) — Newsdesk

Ace In The Hole #2, 30 June 1997

30 June 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The scene suddenly switches to the "Special Report" screen. Ran Dather stands in the picture...the studio where "Ace In The Hole" is shot is in the backround . Dather speaks.] Dather: Not even one week and one show has gone by and The Ace In The Hole in already in the hotseat. Apparently, the selection of "Saturday Night" as the show's theme song has already upset Fergus McTavish, famed former NEWA wrestler, and current CWO and HIWF wrestler. Fergus has released a statement saying that he already uses "Saturday Night" as his entrance and therefore "The Acer" should respect that. The Acer released a statement telling Fergus to perform a certain impossible anatomical act. Fergus has left threatening messages on Acer's answering machine and even hired a lawyer to pursue the issue through legal actions. Allen "The Acer" Carter is wrestling McTavish in the HIWF's debut card on July 5. We'll keep you updated as this story progresses. Now on to other news.... in Washington the Senate is currently debating on which color bathing suit Sunny should wear for the WWF's tanning contest... (The screen fades to black. The Bay City Rollers "Saturday Night" blasts in the backround. Then a constantly changing rotating multi color title appears on the screen, "Ace In The Hole: Across The Globe".) [Voiceover: Welcome to the 2nd edition of ACE IN THE HOLE: ACROSS THE GLOBE.] (Scene cuts to the Ace In The Hole main control desk, behind the desk, in a blue pinstriped three piece suit along with a white tie and neatly groomed hair, is Ace In The Hole Head Reporter, Talson E. Geddy. "Saturday Night" quietly fades into the backround as Talson E. Geddy shuffles his papers. he looks at the camera, as if he was always meant to be here.) (Behind Tal Geddy a huge sign reads, "Down with Oatmeal, Ya Buggers!") Tal Geddy: Good evening, morning or afternoon where ever you are on this planet we call....Earth. Wrestling fans of all ages....it's time to get ACED! (Suddenly, the song "Acer's All Over The Place" and Allen "The Acer" Carter walks in dressed to kill in a all black tuxedo getup and wearing sunglasses. He is wearing a VFWF Internet Championship belt and holding a gavel. He takes a seat next to Talson Geddy.) Tal Geddy: Welcome, Allen Carter. You've had a busy week inside the ring and out. Allen Carter: Mostly out, Tal. Out of all the leagues i'm in, only one is running normal right now. Tal Geddy: Yes. The INWA is the only functioning league as of right now. But, The NEWA is under going changes right now, promising to be bigger and better than before. = Allen Carter: Then, I take it, your meeting with NEWA President [name withheld] went well? Tal Geddy: Yes. I managed to get you a very sweet deal. But why are you wearing black? Isn't the OWO disbanded? Allen Carter: Besides, myself, Attitude Adjuster and The Nobles...there are possibly two others that will stay on. I'm talking with them right now, so I can't reveal the details. Tal Geddy: And I see you signed with the HIWF. Allen Carter: Yes. I'm looking foward to the HIWF and HIWF gold. Tal Geddy: You are wrestling Fergus McTavish, whom has called for your head in the press after you decided to not heed his request, on the 5th of July. How do you feel about the match? Allen Carter: I'll kick Fergus and his oatmeal. In fact after I kick his ass on Saturday July 5th at the HIWF's first ever Saturday card, he'd better just put a damn sock in it and shove it. And to quote one of my heros...."That's the bottom line." Tal Geddy: I'm sure the Bret Hart fans just rolled their eyes on that one. I notice you're wearing the VFWF Internet belt you won a few months ago. Are you still champion? What's going on with you and the VFWF? Allen Carter: [Getting a real "Stone Cold" attitude now] : That's a sore damn subject with me Tal. Here it is almost a week and a [bleep]ing half and i've heard nothing from the new VFWF President. [Just then a staffer rushes in with a cell phone and hands it to Allen Carter, on the other end of the phone is the VFWF President. We only hear Carter's end of the conversation] Well, it's about time! What in the world is going on and why are the doors on the arena padlocked? When am I going to defend my Internet title again? [Suddenly Carter's face turns to shock] WHAT? WHAT THE HELL DO YOU MEAN THE VFWF IS SHUT DOWN? I.....["Acer's All Over The Place" plays and Allen Carter storms out of the studio.] Tal Geddy [looking off in the direction Carter left.]: Whew! Now we CAN review the INWA action. Not a good week for the Acer overall. He lost in the Eurasian Tournament to Cutter in the first round as we take you back.... (Subtitle reads, "INWA Friday Night Fever, June 21, 1997 Eurasian Heavyweight Title Tournament First Round Match Cutter vs. Allen "The Acer" Carter *with Juli and Lisa*) They lock up. Allen Carter takes him down with a small package. Bought off moron counts: One, two, kickout. Allen Carter goes for a snap suplex, but Cutter counters it with a vertical suplex. Allen Carter takes him down with a Samoan Drop. Allen Carter nails him with the STF. Cutter reaches the ropes after being trapped for 5 seconds. The Superstar comes to ringside. Allen Carter whips Cutter into the ropes, but Cutter reverses it. Cutter takes him down with a jumping DDT. Cutter nails him with a flying kneedrop. The Superstar hits Allen Carter with the Singapore Cane from outside of the ring. Cutter executes a fisherman suplex. Bought off moron counts: One, two, three. The winner is Cutter. Time of match: 0:01:03 LUKE: Oh my god! Superstar showing his gratitude! *Cutter gets into The Superstars face* *Cutter executes the Power Drop on The Superstar* BILL: I think Cutter wanted that one on his own! LUKE: He ain't asking for the match to be continued is he. (Fade back to "The Ace In The Hole" studio and Tal Geddy, who is sipping some Surge.) = Tal Geddy: Not exactly a good week anywhere for Allen Carter, folks. The one bright spot? Acer talking the INWA to let him comment on a match and here's how it went. (Subtitle reads, INWA Friday Night Fever, June 27, 1997) LUKE: Speaking of merchandise, you can now order a few new T-Shirts, there's the Jhaver all over print! Fight everyone with your Superstar T Show your cold side with one of two Ice Shirts Theres the Cutter Rules the INWA shirt Allen Carter's prepare to be Aced INWA T BILL: Speaking of Mr. Carter, he'll be joining us for commentary on the next match up! *Allen Carter walks to the booth* ALLEN: Not joining, doing it myself, girls, go show these two guys to where they can sit during the match. *Juli and Lisa lead BILL and LUKE off and away* Eurasian Title Tournament First Round Match-Up Jhaver vs. J-Z ALLEN: This looks to be a good match, I can't wait to watch these "Wannabe Acer's" get it on! They lock up. Jhaver executes a snap suplex. Jhaver goes for a back heel kick, but J-Z blocks it. J-Z nails him with a backslide. Peter Gray counts: One, two, thr... kickout. Jhaver executes a bodyslam. J-Z nails him with a backslide. Peter Gray counts: One, two, three. The winner is J-Z. Time of match: 0:00:33 ALLEN: Not much resistance from Jhaver on that one. *Jhaver leaves the ring with a smile on his face* ALLEN: What's he smilin about! He isn't me, an Internet Champion, a = former World Champion, hell I even took a fed over as Prez for a while! *shakes head then leaves* *BILL and LUKE return* BILL: Those two girls sure like...... (Cut back to the studio. Tal Geddy is just finishing shoving a twinkie in his mouth.) Tal Geddy: That's just about it for this Ace In The Hole, everybody remember The Acer's email is [detail withheld] = just one more thing....[a staffer bring out a bowl with a spoon in it...inside the bowl...is oatmeal!] HEY, FERGUS, YA LADDIE [Tal Geddy throws the bowl of oatmeal at the camera the bowl lands oatmeal side up on the camera, and the oatmeal slops all over the camera and the viewers at home see nothing but oatmeal] IT IS OATMEAL!!!!!! Special thanks to INWA President [name withheld] for releasing the Friday Night Fever footage to us. And that's the way it is, June 29, 1997..I'm Talson Geddy. (Tal Geddy starts singing "Saturday Night" as the credits roll.) Ace In The Hole =A9, 1997
