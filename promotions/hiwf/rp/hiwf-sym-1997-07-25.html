S.Y.M.: Speaking Your Mind, 25 July 1997 (Countdown to Summer Slam Jam)

25 July 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(A slow motion black and white clip of Tommy Drummond getting his arm raised in victory for the World Title plays.) [DEEP VOICEOVER]: For this man, it's a life's work of blood, sweat and tears...... (A zoom clipped of a devious smile on Outlaw Justice's face plays.) [DVO]: For this man, it's a game of cat and mouse. And he vows to be the victor..... (A split screen clip of Drummond and Justice.) [DVO]: For these two men, the battle is not about the title, but about earning the respect of the federation..... (A montage clip of Mysterio getting ambushed by the masked man, of Grave Digger and Lazarus being attacked by the man in black, of various runs and interference with other HIWF superstars. Finally a clip of Miss Gray attacking Stephanie.) [DVO]: A federation where the ruthless thrive, while the innocent suffer... this Sunday the federation that is known for it's extremes shall be put to the test..... (A clip of Pyscho Stud power bombing the camera man from last week.) [DVO]: When a pair of Outsiders come to feast in the waters, like a pair of tiger sharks.......... (A dark and gloomy clip of Downtown G.) [DVO]: All that, plus what has this madman been up to....you'll find out when the HIWF takes Pay Per View to the extreme, Summer Slam Jam........ [DVO]: Think you've seen everything? You've not seen nothing yet. Summer Slam Jam. Believe the hype! (A bolt of lighting flashes the words Summer Slam Jam Sunday July 27, 1997 onto the screen.) ________________________________________________________________________ The bigger than life symbol of HIWF and two wrestler's pummeling each other senseless crashes onto your screen. Underneath it black bold faced letters appear... "SPEAKING YOUR MIND". Blaring out your speakers is 'SONG 2' by Blur. **To hear the NEW theme music in MIDI format, download it here at http://www.globalserve.net/~cracker/song2.mid (30KB).** Then the screen goes black and nice looking white letters appear on your screen. PARENTAL GUIDANCE WARNING, THE FOLLOWING PROGRAM WILL CONTAIN LANGUAGE OF A COARSE NATURE. PLEASE BE ADVISED. YADDA YADDA YADDA. ________________________________________________________________________ (The scene now changes to the HIWF Control Center. This week the desk is placed right in the middle of the canvas wrestling ring in the Indianapolis Hoosier Dome where this Sunday's Summer Slam Jam Pay Per View will take place. Seated on the left is Smilin' Doc Watson, and seated on the right is Jerry Malone. Malone speaks first.) [Malone]: Only 48 hours away from the first HIWF Pay Per View, Summer Slam Jam! [Doc Watson]: Yeah! Believe the Hype! [Malone]: Today we have many interviews from those HIWF Superstars participating so lets not waste any time. But first off lets look at the results from Wednesday Night Reckoning. Advancing to the finals of the TV Title Tournament will be Downtown G., Jesse "All American" Ewiak, Lazarus and Outlaw Justice. [Doc Watson]: Looks like Outlaw Justice's chances of getting some gold are pretty good. In addition to making it to the TV Tourney Finals, this weekend he'll be facing "Mr. Universe" Tommy Drummond. I like his odds! Of course, he'll probably just screw it up! [Malone]: Well first he'll have to get by Lazarus and then President Ismael has decreed that the final two combatants will square off in a ladder match! [Doc Watson]: The HIWF keeps getting more and more extreme, week after week! I love it! [Malone]: Now lets get to the interview. Today we have comments from "Mr. Universe" Tommy Drummond, Outlaw Justice, The "Attitude Adjuster" Geoff Bondavarsky, another "Talk With The Doc", The Outlaws, "The Natural" Mark Drummond, The Acer, Fergus McTavish and The Nobles in that order. Now, live on remote is HIWF correspondent, Bebe Cake. So let's go! ________________________________________________________________________ (We're in a bodybuilding facility in Miami, Florida where "Mr. Universe" Tommy Drummond is training for his World Title match with Outlaw Justice this Sunday.) ("Mr. Universe" is doing some powerlifting, he stops when he sees Bebe Cake. Today Bebe is wearing an HIWF Summer Slam Jam crop-top and a pair of hot pink shorts.) [Cake]: Ever since he was beaten by you in 30 seconds, Outlaw Justice has been asking for a rematch with you. How are you going to approach your match this Sunday? [Mr. Universe]: You know, Outlaw Justice!!! You've been whining and crying that your defeat in 30 seconds was a fluke and you were screwed!!! Well, you don't need me to get screwed because you can do it by yourself!!! As far as this Sunday is concerned, I'm gonna show those humanoids out there that "Mr. Universe" is the ruler of the HIWF and you, Outlaw Justice, are just small change in my back pocket!!! [Cake]: Is your brother going to be at ringside for your match? [Mr. Universe]: As always, he will be at ringside!!! Whenever my brother has a match, I go help him at ringside and with me it's the same thing, this is called the family spirit!!! Don't you think so, little girl??!! [Cake]: I must say your family is strong and confident!!! [Mr. Universe]: You bet!!! And you must not forget the "brain" of the managers in the HIWF, "Pretty Boy" Bobby Lawrence, who I learn to like more and more everyday!!! Come on in, Bobby!!! (Bobby Lawrence walks in the room and greets his champion.) [Pretty Boy]: Hey champ!!! Good to see you again!!! [Mr. Universe]: This man is the best manager in the world!!! [Pretty Boy]: And this man is the reason why everybody wants a shot at him, they want the glory, the glamour and the recognition. But this Sunday, "Mr. Universe" will show the world that Outlaw Justice is not in the same league as "Mr. Universe" Tommy Drummond is!!! [Cake]: Thank you for your precious time, guys!!! [Pretty Boy and Mr. Universe]: Outlaw Justice!!! This Sunday on pay-per-view, you're going down!!! [Cake]: Back to you Jerry and Doc! ________________________________________________________________________ [Malone]: Thanks Bebe! It sure looks like "Mr. Universe" is hyped for his match. Don't you think so, Doc? [Doc Watson]: He sure is, Jerry. And I should know. And do know why I should know? [Malone]: Yes I do. Thanks for your input. So before Doc bores us any further, let's get some comments from the challenger, Outlaw Justice. ________________________________________________________________________ (Outlaw Justice stands on an empty soundstage.) Outlaw: Boy, you beat me once. You got lucky. It was a fluke. This Sunday, I will destroy you... That belt WILL be mine! (Scene fades) ________________________________________________________________________ [Doc Watson]: (in the middle of taking a drink of water) Ugh! Oh, hell! Man, Outlaw must be going to the Jesse Ewiak school of interviewing! [Malone]: Well, maybe he doesn't want to waste any time talking. He always has been a man of few words. [Doc Watson]: Yeah, maybe. But then again, maybe he's trying to keep his interviews as short as his matches! Ha! [Malone]: Glad to see you're in fine form tonight, Doc. [Doc Watson]: You know it baby! Now here's a guy who's always in top form, The "Attitude Adjuster". (Doc starts doing the wave.) [Doc Watson]: Woooooo! C'mon, Jerry! Catch the Dream! Catch the Dream! [Malone]: Puh-leeze! Just roll the clip, ok? ________________________________________________________________________ (The scene opens up as "The Attitude Adjuster" Geoff Bondavarsky has finished another ATTITUDE CHECK program taping. Geoff is in his usual three piece suit, wearing a "Life Is Terrific" button and also his traditional Eagle tie. Cheryl, wearing a blue silk shirt with a larger than life Eagle on it and a nice blue skirt with blue spiked heels, is there telling him how he did a great job and also....handing him his bottled water.) [Attitude Adjuster]: (Drinking the water) Ahhhhhhh....thanks Cheryl! That really hit the spot! [Cheryl]: Did you really mean all that "Persistence" stuff you were talking about out there? [Attitude Adjuster]: Of course. "Ice Man" is in for the fight of his life. I came to the HIWF for gold and glory! Siding with The Acer, has brought me glory! Now I wait for the gold! The TV tournament bummed me out a little, but then I listened to Zig Ziglar talk to me for four straight hours about Persistence. That's what it's all about, Cheryl! That's what life steps aside for...and what determines winners and losers! I should be tucking my tail between my legs and running home. But, no, in the everlasting words of Og Mandino, "I will persist, until I succeed" I never know, Cheryl, what awaits after the very next step. The next step is Sunday. The next step is...."Ice Man" Jeff Koch!! I will bring the right attitude to the ring, I expect to win. Losing is simply unacceptable.....it's a PPV....HIWF Summer Slam Jam....and I'm a part of it! The Attitude of Men With Attitudes will be there and yes......I will persist and Sunday "Ice Man" Jeff Koch will go down. I will win. I will succeed. Because of persistence. Plain and simple. "Ice Man" you will catch the all powerful, all encompassing, all knowing DREAM! You will catch The Dream. I promise it. (Attitude Adjuster and Cheryl leave the set to go grab some dinner at one of the many five star restaurants that they frequent. Fade to black.) ________________________________________________________________________ [Doc Watson]: (still doing the wave) Catch it! Catch it! Woooooo! [Malone]: (looks at Doc with a sideways glance) Um, are you OK, Doc? [Doc Watson]: Of course I'm ok! I'm better than OK! I'm Smilin' Doc Watson! What could be better than that! [Malone]: Gee, I'm sure I could think of something. [Doc Watson]: Well smart guy, you let me know when you do. And while you're racking your brain, its time for me to do another "Talk With The Doc". (Doc gets up and climbs out of the ring and walks out of the Hoosier Dome to a waiting black stretch limousine. Doc climbs in and the limo drives him to an undisclosed location. Once there, Doc gets out and walks on a set that is done up to resemble a bar lounge, with highbacked red leather chairs, a long oak bar, and a jazz combo in the corner. A pink neon sign on the back wall reads 'Talk With The Doc'. The jazz combo is playing 'Get Down Tonight' by KC & The Sunshine Band.) **To hear the song in MIDI format, download it here at: http://www.globalserve.net/~cracker/getdown.mid (54KB).** [Doc Watson]: (lights up a Cohiba cigar) Tonight I'll be talking with a gentleman that manages the best the world has to offer, including the HIWF World Heavyweight Champion. Ladies and gentlemen, a man I'm proud to call my friend, a man who is truly the best manager of professional wrestling, Mr. "Pretty Boy" Bobby Lawrence!!! [Doc Watson]: (handing Lawrence a Cohiba cigar) Welcome to "Talk With The Doc", Mr. "Pretty Boy"!!! [Pretty Boy]: (taking the Cohiba and lighting it) The pleasure is mine, my good friend Doc Watson!!! [Doc Watson]: You've been putting together quite an impressive stable of wrestlers in the last couple of weeks. Now I've been saying many times that it would be good to name your stable, so tell me, do you have a name in mind? [Pretty Boy]: As a matter of fact, my man Doc, I have a name in mind and to find this name, I've inspired my self a lot from my good buddy, Bobby "The Brain" Heenan. My stable will be called "The Lawrence Family" because I treat my wrestlers like if they were in my family and "The Lawrence Family", with all due respect to Bobby who I know is watching the show right now, will be much more successful than "The Heenan Family" was in the WWF!!! [Doc Watson]: You say Bobby Heenan is your good friend? Since when is he your good friend? [Pretty Boy]: This friendship goes way, way back to our college years, we attended the same college and we did almost everything together, we played football together, we were in the same wrestling team, there was nothing we could do without each other, we really had a good time. Since those college years, we've always been good friends!!! [Doc Watson]: Your big champ, "Mr. Universe" will face this Sunday on Pay-Per-view, that big crybaby Outlaw Justice. Now, just between the two of us, we both know that "Mr. Universe" will annihilate once and for all, the big dink Outlaw Justice!!! (He starts to laugh.) [Pretty Boy]: And I know why we should know, eat your heart out Malone!!! You're a 10 time World Champion and I'm currently the manager of the HIWF Champion, we know what championships are all about!!! Isn't that right Doc???!!! [Doc Watson]: You're 100% right Bobby! You know and I know that "Mr. Universe" is the most powerful force in wrestling right now and nobody can beat him!!! But, isn't that difficult to handle so many wrestlers? Won't some of them get unhappy with the chances they get???!!! [Pretty Boy]: Don't worry Doc!!! Everything is just A-OK in "The Lawrence Family" right, all my wrestlers know that eventually they will all get a title shot either for the World Title, the TV Title or the Tag Title. In the mean time, they just try to improve their chances!!! [Doc Watson]: I've heard rumors that the Drummond brothers want to form a tag team. Can you tell me more about this? [Pretty Boy]: Yeah, they talked a lot about this with me!!! They want to have a chance eventually to wrestle for the tag team title, they want to prove that they're not only good singles wrestlers but that they can work very well as a tag team. But I told them that first, they would have to wrestle in a couple of matches together before that so they can know each other more!!! [Doc Watson]: Well, Mr. "Pretty Boy" Bobby Lawrence, as usual it has been a pleasure talking to you!!! [Pretty Boy]: It was nothing, my friend, maybe I'll come back on other occasions on your show if you allow me to!!! (He smiles.) [Doc Watson]: With a class act like you, you bet!!! (Doc turns to the camera.) [Doc Watson]: So while I'm making my way back to the Hoosier Dome, let's check out these comments from The Outlaws. ________________________________________________________________________ (Scene opens with all 4 men walking down the boardwalk of what seems to be San Diego California.) [Jimmy]: As we approach this Sunday, I can't think but wonder what does the future hold for myself, Jason, and the rest of the Outlaws. You know these are tough times for us, we don't know where Downtown G is, hell none of us have spoken to him since that night where him and that Drummond guy had a go at it for the World Title, its truly hard times. [Jason]: This Sunday the L.A. Mafia comes ready and willing to take care once and for all of those wanna be Mafia imitators the Hate Machine, 2 out of 3. We only need 2 cause they won't even get a 2 count on us, that's how good we are, and we are Too Sweeetttttt!!!!!!! [Asad]: Drummond Brothers, before I address you let me address one peasant in particular and that's "Maddog" Gnort. Who the hell do you think you are, you're not on my level, you're not even on Mysterio's level, but I'm gonna do this one for my good pal Dirty Dog Dennis Rodman! Speaking of the Drummond Brothers they wanna make a challenge to myself and Downtown G to a "Death Match"!! You gotta be kidding me, DO YOU SEE DOWNTOWN G?? Cause I sure as hell don't, if Downtown was here maybe I would consider it, but he's not so the answer is no. [Mack]: Drummond Brothers, or Sisters should I say, don't you worry about July 30th cause next week on SNSJ Asad and the Mafia are gonna take you punk ass losers out of action, and you know Dirty Dog Dennis Rodman will be there, and I will as well, so if I were you I wouldn't look past that, cause you might be watching July 30th's WNR from your hospital beds. [Asad]: Oh yeah, one more thing "Maddog" Gnort I have a surprise for you this Sunday, Its a BAD One!!! [Jimmy]: Also this Sunday I will tell you people out there in TV land more about that masked man who was on SNSJ last week. [Mack]: Its all 4 LIFE!!!!!!!!! :-> ________________________________________________________________________ [Malone]: (looking around him and noticing that Doc has still not returned) Well, it looks like Doc is taking his sweet time getting back. You know, if it were up to me, I would have fired him by now. He's rude, insulting, obnoxious, and he always makes fun of me and my Mother. He's just not a very nice guy. (Malone sighs deeply.) [Malone]: Well, with that off my chest, let's check some pre-recorded comments made by "The Natural", Mark Drummond. ________________________________________________________________________ (We're in a ranch near Corpus Christi, Texas where "The Natural" Mark Drummond is riding some horses.) (Mark Drummond stops his horse and jumps on the ground.) [Natural]: I heard that somebody in the HIWF nicknames himself "Mr. High Impact". But I saw the HIWF rankings recently and this so-called "Mr. High Impact" has a record of 0-4 so far in the HIWF!!! This chump should nickname himself "Mr. Zero Impact" instead because that's about all he can offer!!! (He laughs) But enough jokes!!! Mysterio!!! This Sunday on pay-per-view, your perfect record will be in jeopardy, you have a chance to win your first match in the HIWF but let me assure you that after Sunday, your perfect record will be intact, a perfect 0-5 record!!! And if you try to bring one of your stupid masked man, my brother will be there to break him into little pieces. In the mean time, I'll be doing short work out of you, critter!!! And when I'll snap that figure four on you, it will procure me pure pleasure and fun because I'm gonna break your stinkin' leg so you can never wrestle again!!! (He jumps on his horse and leaves.) ________________________________________________________________________ (Doc Watson walks on the set eating a Big Mac and sits down.) [Doc Watson]: Yippee Kai-Ay, Motherf#@ker! Sorry I'm late, Jerry. I got a little hungry so I had my chauffeur get some drive-thru at Mickey D's. Look, I even got you something. [Malone]: Oh, thanks Doc! You shouldn't have! (Doc hands Malone a McDonald's Happy Meal.) [Doc Watson]: There ya go, sport! Check it out! You even get a Hercules doll you can play with! [Malone]: (tosses the Happy Meal aside) Stop treating me like a child! [Doc Watson]: Hey! That cost me $2.99! You know Jerry, its like they always say, "If you can't stand the heat, get your sorry ass out of the kitchen!" [Malone]: Oh no! I'm not going to quit! I love this job, even with all the aggravation you bring me! I'll outlast you, I promise you! [Doc Watson]: You'll outlast me, eh? My friend, you've got a lot to learn, a lot to learn. But hey, learning things is what life is all about. Just ask The Acer. ________________________________________________________________________ (Scene opens up on a payphone. Zoom out to see a rather distressed Allen "The Acer" Carter searching through his pockets for change.) [The Acer]: Oh, no....I don't have any change....how am I going to tell Miss Gray to meet me for dinner? Oh, my...I just don't know what to do.... (Suddenly, Max Jerome pops into the scene and to the rescue!) [Max]: Hey, Acer, don't worry, you can still dial 1-800- COLLECT...it'll save your sweetheart 44%! And you'll be her knight in shining armor! [The Acer]: Please! (Suddenly The Acer locks up with Max Jerome and DDT'S him! Then, The Acer slaps on part two of The Acerizer, a sleeperhold! Within 3 seconds, Acer chucks Jerome's unresponding body onto the pavement. Acer then calls 911 for Jerome on his cell phone.) [Acer]: I don't know about you (Acer points to the viewer) but I've hated that jerk ever since those commercials began...I've wanted to do that for a long time... and are you ready for a special HIWF edition of ACE IN THE HOLE? (Doing his best Dude Love imitation) HAVE MERCY! ("Thunderstruck" by AC/DC plays in the background. Scene switches to The Ace In The Hole studio all set up for this special edition. Huge pictures of The Acer (DDTing Jules Winnfield and getting the pin in that historic victory in the NEWA ending Winnfield's career in a loser leaves match), The Attitude Adjuster and Cheryl (Dressed up and ready to party out on the town after an HIWF press conference), and The Nobles, Jack Diamond and The King With A Club (Hoisting the NEWA Tag Team Championship Belts after their win over The Hate Machine taking the belts from them). The Acer sits in one of two seats in the center of the set and looks ready to rock. "Thunderstruck" fades out. The Acer is dressed in his usual non-wrestling outfit, an all black tux wearing Ray Ban sunglasses. Acer turns to the camera and speaks.) [The Acer]: Welcome to Ace In The Hole! Okay, hip cats, we're going to be talking about my foe this Sunday at the HIWF PPV Summer Slam Jam! None other than Bucky Jones and hey....Bucky what are YOU doing here???? (The Attitude Adjuster "Geoff Bondavarsky" Walks on to the set dressed exactly like Bucky!! "You Light Up My Life" plays in the background.) [The Acer]: Bucky, welcome to Ace In The Hole! ("You Light Up My Life fades out.) [Fake Bucky]: Oh, Mr. Acer...look at all those lights!! ("Bucky" turns to the camera) HI, MOM!!!! MOM...LOOKIE I'm on the BEST show in wrestling!!! MOM!!!! Hey, Mom I got the care package!!! Thanks, Mom!!! [The Acer]: Care package? What was in it? [Fake Bucky]: Oh, I loved those Power Ranger fruit roll ups..I love the Power Rangers, Mr. Acer..do you ever watch the Power Rangers????? They really are TURBO! [The Acer]: (Rolls eye) Can't say that I do, Bucky. I watch shows that are beyond your grasp. Bucky do you really think that you can beat me at Summer Slam Jam? [Fake Bucky]: You are the best..I could NEVER beat you, Mr. Acer! I mean you are going to be a great champ here in the H..... um, HWIF....um... [The Acer]: That's HIWF, Bucky. Stands for High Impact Wrestling Federation. [Fake Bucky]: WOW! That's what those letters mean??? [The Acer]: That's right Bucky, really big words, Bucky. (At this point Attitude and Acer are really trying not to crack up laughing. Acer's just about ready to lose it...all the way.) [The Acer]: Well, that's for being on Ace In The Hole and who's going to win on Sunday, Bucky? [Fake Bucky]: You are Mr. Acer! You are! [The Acer]: Bye Bucky! ("Bucky" gets up and leaves.) Say hi to your Mom for me. Thanks for joining me on Ace In The Hole everyone! Good night! (The camera stops broadcasting and the whole studio crew busts out laughing!!!! Attitude comes back on to the set.) [Attitude]: Oh, Lord, Allen...I think I'm going to bust a gut after that! I could barely stop myself from losing it on stage!!!! [The Acer]: (Doing Billy Crystal): You were Mah-verlous! [Attitude]: To make fun of Bucky, or not to make fun of Bucky.... [The Acer]: That is the question! (Acer and Attitude fall on the floor laughing as Miss Gray and Cheryl walk in and wonder just what exactly is going on! The closing credits roll and right before we fade to black you hear a recorded commercial from Talson E. Geddy) [Talson Geddy (As a voiceover)]: Don't forget Ace In The Hole fans, if you love The Acer and his friends...get your Men With Attitudes t-shirts with the whole gang....the group shirt..with The Acer, Miss Gray, The Attitude Adjuster, Cheryl, Jack Diamond and The King With A Club are yours for only $199.95 call 1-800-JMV-HIWF and credit cards are accepted! (Fade to black.) ________________________________________________________________________ [Doc Watson]: (laughing) Speaking of Mom's, how's your Mom doing, Jerry? [Malone]: Don't start! [Doc Watson]: (laughing) You big baby! Man, you are so easy to get going! Maybe you're not getting enough fiber in your diet at breakfast. You know, breakfast is the most important meal of the day. Just ask Fergus McTavish, The Whirling Scottish Devil. ________________________________________________________________________ (Camera shot opens on Fergus McTavish, The Whirling Scottish Devil and the Camera-Guy eating breakfast. As usual, Fergus is enjoying a large haggis.) [Camera-Guy]: Fergus, pass the milk. [Fergus]: (passing the milk to the Camera-Guy) Oy, boyo! Ya sure ya doan want any a me haggis? Put some hair on yer chest it will! Just axe me Ma! [Camera-Guy]: Um, no thanks. I'm not to keen on the idea of a hairy chest. [Fergus]: Och, suit yerself laddie. (Fergus puts down his knife and fork and looks at the camera.) [Fergus]: So yer da strong silent type are ya Jesse "All American" Ewiak? Well, I'll tell ya whats. When ya face me on Sunday at Summer Slam Jam, how 'bout I cartwheels yer arse right inta da third row? Hows dat grabs ya? (The Camera-Guy starts laughing.) [Fergus]: So "All American", are ya ready ta tangle wif The Whirling Scottish Devil? Cause I sure am! Oh, and one last ting.... (McTavish jumps up on the table, turns his back to the camera and lifts up his kilt, mooning the camera.) [Fergus]: (over his shoulder) Gets ready for The Whirling Scottish Devil! ITS NOT OATMEAL!!!! [Camera-Guy]: (turning his head away) Geez, Fergus, not at breakfast, OK? (Camera fade, much laughing and hooting can still be heard.) ________________________________________________________________________ [Malone]: Geez, that's enough to make anyone toss their cookies. Remind me never to have a meal with him. [Doc Watson]: Ok, never have a meal with Fergus McTavish. Ha! [Malone]: Alright, I can see where this is heading. Lets go to the final interview before we turn into Stan and Ollie. ________________________________________________________________________ (The scene opens up on Jack Diamond (Dressed as Ric Flair with two lovely ladies in string bikinis at his side) and The King With A Club (Dressed as King Arthur, robe crown and all and wielding a warrior's club as his weapon). They are on a shoreline and off in the distance a fierce storm approaches and the scene is punctuated by frequent flashes of lighting and rumblings of thunder.) [Jack Diamond]: Whooooooooo! This Sunday the Horsemen are going to get the job done! [King With A Club]: Jack, er, Ric, remember our private meeting with Acer? He said that I should be the primary spokesman for The Nobles until you get your head straight. Silence when in my presence! (Jack Diamond seems almost shaken back to his former self but seems miffed. Never the less he yields to King.) [KC]: Thank you Jack. I hope we can get you back to normal soon. Now on to the business at hand. Stormreavers. I must admit that is one of the more creative names I have ever heard. And oh, yes, the MWA watched your adult temper tantrum on the air earlier this week. The reason we're respected is we started out as nobodies. Match by match, interview by interview, we rose on the ladder. We even reached the top! Hoisting gold was the best moment of our professional lives. But we grew tired of the pressure. But now, with Geoff guiding us with daily sessions, we feel stronger than ever before. And as LA Mafia found out last week, we are not here to play. We're here for keeps Stormreavers. We play to win. Victory is life. We step into the ring to begin a dynasty and you, Stormreavers are the heart of the storm. We shall prevail. Now is the time to begin. Now is the time for victory. Make no mistake, Stormreavers.....you are in our way and the gold is were we're going. You will merely be but two added to the body count. I have a perfect way to finish.... To Storm... Lighting and thunder rumblings the cold and not the warm cometh in thy foe Stormreavers we shall beat the storm... we shall.... Stormreavers....feel our thunder and lighting feel our howling winds feel our elemental mastery..... (The King With A Club bows and raises his club and lets loose a warriors howl....fade to black.) ________________________________________________________________________ [Doc Watson]: Cool! Just what HIWF needs! Some culture in the form of poetry! [Malone]: If you can call it that. Well folks, that's it for tonight. So remember to hold on to your seats Sunday night as HIWF's Summer Slam Jam will shock you like you've never been shocked before! [Doc Watson]: Hey, that's what your Mom told me the other night! [Malone]: She did not! [Doc Watson]: Did too! [Malone]: Did not! [Doc Watson]: Did too! [Malone]: Did not! [Doc Watson]: Did too! [Malone]: Did not!
