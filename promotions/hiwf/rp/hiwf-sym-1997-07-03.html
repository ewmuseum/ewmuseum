HIWF (Jun 1997) — Roleplay

S.Y.M.: Speaking Your Mind, 3 July 1997

3 July 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The bigger than life symbol of HIWF and two wrestler's pummeling each other senseless crashes onto your screen. Underneath it black bold faced letters appear... "SPEAKING YOUR MIND". Blaring out your speakers is 'Killer' by Adamski. To hear it for real in MIDI format, download it here at http://www.globalserve.net/~cracker/killer.mid (36KB). Then the screen goes black for a second and nice looking white letters appear on your screen. PARENTAL GUIDANCE WARNING, THE FOLLOWING PROGRAM WILL CONTAIN LANGUAGE OF A COARSE NATURE. PLEASE BE ADVISED. YADDA YADDA YADDA. The opening shot is focused on the HIWF S.Y.M. Control Center. Seated on the left is Smilin' Doc Watson. The chair on the right is empty. Jerry Malone is nowhere to be seen. (Doc is looking around, with his now trademark Cohiba in his right hand.) [Doc Watson]: Well folks, Jerry's not here so it looks like I'm on my own tonight. You lucky bastards! Tonight's S.Y.M will focus on the first ever Saturday Night Slam Jam which will feature the first round of World Title Tournament. The following men will beat the hell out of each other to be named the first HIWF World Champion. (Over Doc's shoulder a small television screen shows the names of the participants) Lazarus Vs. Chameleon Grave Digger Vs. Outlaw Justice 'Ice Man' Jeff Koch Vs. 'Mr. Universe' Tom Drummond Downtown G. Vs. Jermain Asad Acer Vs. Maddog Gnort Bucky Jones Vs. Mysterio Fergus McTavish Vs. Attitude Adjuster [Doc Watson]: The rules are simple, just like Bucky Jones. (Doc smiles) [Doc Watson]: Ok, every match will be a falls count anywhere match. No DQ, No Count-Out. You can only win by pin fall or submission. The tournament is a single elimination tourney. Once you've lost you're out of the running. See ya later, sweetheart! [Doc Watson]: Also on the first Saturday Night Slam Jam card will be a HIWF World Tag Team Champion. All the rounds will be featured on the card. Here are the rules for the tag team tournament. Anyone can be in the tournament. But you must have a partner to participate. Duh! Who ever heard of a one man tag-team? The matches themselves will be No DQ, No Count-Out matches. The only way a team can win is by pin fall or submission. Not too many tag teams are taking part, but the match-ups are as follows. (Again over Doc's shoulder a small television screen shows the names of the participants) The French Connection Vs. L.A. Mafia Mayberry RFD Vs. Hate Machine [Doc Watson]: Well enough of these boring rules! Let's get to what this show is all about, the wrassler's! Here's some comments from The Attitude Adjuster. _______________________________________________________ (The scene opens up on the roof of the 35 story headquarters of Carter International. Almost 50 or so reporters are on the rooftop as a Carter International jetcopter glides in for a landing. Security pushes back the reporters to allow the jetcopter to land. The jetcopter lands very smoothly and upon touchdown, five figures emerge and the reporters instantly mob them. Two of the figures are bodyguards the other two are well known HIWF superstars Allen "The Acer" Carter and "The Attitude Adjuster" Geoff Bondavarsky. Carter is more used to the public attention then Geoff...but Geoff's manager, The Lovely Cheryl, whispers in his ear and that seems to help.) Reporter #1: Mr. Bondavarsky sir, any comments on the boxing fight which seemed to use "wrestling" style tactics. The Attitude Adjuster: [Very disgusted] Why would I be concerned about that? My mind is on Fergus McTavish right now. Nothing else. Bodyguard #1 (Whispering to bodyguard #2): Not even Cheryl? That IS being fully prepared. With her body I... [Cheryl's spiked heel just happens to wind up being planted firmly on Bodyguard #1's shoe. The bodyguard is really trying not to scream.] [Cheryl smiles.] Reporter #2 : Mr. Bondavarsky, What is your stand on the formation of the HIWF after the split of the former NEWA co-adminstrators? Doesn't that weigh on your mind? Mr. Carter has refused to comment so we want your stand on the whole ordeal, sir! The Attitude Adjuster: [Thinking VERY carefully] My stand? Perhaps I should say "no comment" like my friend The Acer....but no. My stand.... ....hmmmmm....In the affairs of men there sometimes comes an impasse. A point where the relationship is no longer productive and then a split is perhaps, the only solution. Personally? I'm here to wrestle, not talk politics. The affairs of the previously mentioned gentlemen are their own and not mine to ponder on. And that's all I wish to comment on that issue, if you please. Reporter #3: On your match with The Whirling Scottish Devil. What will you do to deal with Fergus' unconventional approach to wrestling? The Attitude Adjuster: One thing I have learned about success is that one must change. From one style to another, whether one must dodge chairs or learn new ways to outsmart their foes. I don't think I'll need to outsmart Fergus much though. All he ever does is moon cameras and talk about oatmeal for some gawd awful reason. Fergus McTavish...bring your best to the ring. Bring all that you are. Top yourself and.....push yourself past every limit you can imagine. Because, it won't be enough, little man. I'm POSITIVE about that. Allen "The Acer" Carter: Come on, gang. Enough questions. This press conference is over. (Carter motions for security to escort the reporters downstairs and out the building. An employee brings up some peach iced tea for Attitude, Acer and Cheryl. Acer bends down as his cell phone rings. Acer is busy talking with Miss Gray on the cell phone as the party of five make their way down into the building.) (It's a fact, it's time to fade to black.) _________________________________________________________ [Doc Watson]: Hey, that iced tea looks good! Hey, you! (snaps his fingers to someone offscreen) Get your ass in gear and bring me some! [Doc Watson]: You know, without Jerry, this hour is going pretty well! In fact, lets rush it along 'cause I've got me a hot date tonight! (Doc looks at the camera) Like, when don't I? (Doc winks.) [Doc Watson]: So next we got the 'Goddess of the Mic' Bebe Cake live on remote with 'Mr. Universe' himself, Tommy Drummond. ________________________________________________________ [Bebe Cake]: Thanks Doc! And save me some of that iced tea! (Turns to Tommy Drummond) [Cake]: What do you think are your chances in this tournament? [Mr. Universe]: What do you mean what are my chances???!!! You're looking right now at the first HIWF World Champion, so show me some respect, little girl!!! [Cake]: Sorry!!! [Mr. Universe]: You better be!!! [Cake]: Do you have any comments regarding your first opponent, "Ice Man" Jeff Koch? [Mr. Universe]: Listen to me and listen very closely, "Snow Man" Jeff Koch!!! You are gonna be in for the beating of your life next Saturday. I don't know a lot about you but I don't care because I have no respect or no admiration for anybody! [Cake]: What is going to be your strategy against your opponent this Saturday? [Mr. Universe]: My strategy is very simple, little girl! I overpower my opponents and with a little bit of cheating courtesy of yours truly, you have a winner. What do you think about that!!! [Cake]: Seems fair to me! (Mr. Universe, who is tired of answering questions, takes the microphone and shoves Bebe Cake away.) (Bebe Cake gives him the finger, sticks out her tongue, and walks away.) [Mr. Universe]: Enough questions asked!!! I want to send a precise and clear message to all the HIWF so called stars. "Mr. Universe" has arrived on the block and believe me you don't want to get in my way because if you do, I'll crush you like a turnip just like I crushed everybody in the CSWA!!! And you Jeff Koch are gonna be the first to be squashed courtesy of "Mr. Universe". What are you going to do when my 6'8'', 358 pounds frame pounds on your helpless little body of yours. I just want to say you this, if you think you can beat me, don't say it!!! Bring it!!! If you have the guts!!! (Mr. Universe throws the microphone on the ground and leaves the lockeroom.) _______________________________________________________________ [Doc Watson]: (sipping his iced tea) Wow! Now there is a man who is championship material! And I should know! And do you know why I should know? (Just then, Jerry Malone walks onto the set. His hair is disheveled, his tie is undone, his shirt is untucked, and his hands are covered in grease.) [Malone]: (waving his hand is dismissal) Yeah, yeah, I know. 10 time World Heavyweight Champion. I've heard it a million times before! [Doc Watson]: (looking surprised) Why Jerry! Where on Earth have you been? [Malone]: You know full well where I've been! My Fiat broke down on the way to the studio and you drove right by me, even though I was screaming and waving my hands for you to stop. Why didn't you stop? [Doc Watson]: (grins) 'Cause I'm a jet flyin', limousine ridin', kiss stealin', wheelin', dealin' son of a gun! WOOOOOOOOO!!!! [Malone]: (Sits down and straightens his tie, staining it with grease) You my friend, need some new lines. (Looks at Doc's iced tea) Um, can I have some? [Doc Watson]: Piss off and get your own! Now listen up! You're late and I want this show over with so no more of your whining and bitching! So let's see what Hate Machine has to say. (Watson pushes a button on his side of the desk and the monitor behind him comes on. Doc continues to sip his iced tea and doesn't even bother to turn around.) ___________________________________________________________ (Picasso Trigger stands in the middle of a ring. He's wearing a fat suit and Raw is War leather jacket and a huge Jim Ross cowboy hat. The crowd seems receptive to his attendance) [Piscasso:] Ladies and gentlemen please welcome the Heart Burn Kid, Damian Michaels! (Damian struts his stuff to the ring wearing a complete Shawn Michaels wardrobe. He gets in the ring and does the pose and the fireworks go off.) [Picasso:] Now HBK, we haven't seen you in a while. Where have you been for these last weeks? (Picasso gives Damian a separate mic) [Damian:] Well you see, Slim Jim.......I was guaranteed to be the lowest paid wrestler in the HIWF....but I find out from a couple of friends of mine in the front office...that no good Alaskan dog, Bret Smart....was getting paid even less than me..... [Picasso:] So? [Damian:] So I made it clear the Rob' man that I wasn't going to wrestle until I was the......... (A voice comes over the loud speakers and Lucifer in a Steve Austin bald cap and wardrobe appears on the large video wall.) [Lucifer:] Cut the crap Michaels.... [Picasso:] Mr. Austin why don't come out the ring and address Mr. Michaels in person? [Lucifer:] Shut your trap, Slim Jim. I'm coming out there to whoop your ass for that. (Lucifer walks off camera. Stone Cold's music plays as he struts to the ring in his full Stone Cold Steve Austin outfit.) [Picasso:] Mr. Austin. [Lucifer:] Shut the hell up, Slim Jim....(He grabs the mic from Picasso) No you listen here Blow Toy.....you might be the lowest paid wrestler here...but I'm the toughest S.O.B. in the business....this Saturday President Gorilla Baboon that jackass [name withheld], is forcin' me to tag with your sorry ass.... [Damian:] Listen here tough man....you can go on, on and on, about how you're gonna whoop his ass (pointing to Picasso) and how your gonna whoop my ass.....it don't mean jack as long as I am the lowest paid wrestler in the HIWF.......and you take this mic and shove it up your ass.. because after I win that match for us on Saturday... you're in for some Sour Chin Music....... [Lucifer:] The only music that's gonna be playin' after the match Saturday will be my music after I whoop Mayberry RFD's ass and after I whoop your ass.....and that's the bottom line....because I'm finished talkin'! (Lucifer tosses down the mic and climbs on the second turn buckle in the corner and raises his arms.) ____________________________________________________ [Doc Watson]: (Looks at Jerry) Hey, do you think they've got an ass fetish? [Malone]: How should I know? [Doc Waton]: (grinning) Well, I thought that you of all people..... [Malone]: What does that mean? I'm not a pervert! Just ask my mother! [Doc Watson]: Ok, I will. (Doc looks under the desk) [Doc Watson]: Hey, Mrs. Malone, Jerry wants to ask you a question when you're done, OK? (Doc takes a long draw on his cigar) [Malone]: (his face reddening with anger) Enough about my mother! We've got a show to do! [Doc Watson]: (looking innocent) Hey buddy, you started it! (Doc looks back at the camera) [Doc Watson]: Lets quickly go to these pre-recorded comments from The Outlaws before Jerry blows a gasket. <snicker> ___________________________________________________________ (Interview taped from Club 69 in Las Vegas) Downtown: First of all, I better never again find your ass bothering me or any of the OUTLAWS while we are kickin back here at 69, getting away from that hectic world we live in, but here at 69 we can kick back, get drunk, smoke a fat blunt, all around cool s#@t, and if we want we can get some 69 in the back, but that's not for your ears or the ears of those little kids watching at home, but what the f#@k do I care, Im a thug high on life and other things. Mack: Dont mind him, he had one to many beers, but he will be ready for this Saturday when all of the Outlaws head to Sat. Night Slam Jam where we will wreak havoc upon the HIWF in more than one way. Asad: This Saturday me and Downtown will go at it, but its strictly business, may the best man win, but thats not the only reason I am heading there. Mysterio I still have a beef to settle with you and your punk immigrant ass can't hide 'cause I'll find you and make a f'n taco out of your ass Boy! Jimmy: This truly ain't nothing but a gangsta party and we as always are the main attraction cause we can do these things like nobody else and we will do these things to them French Immigrants this Saturday cause we want to and we can! Jason: I'm the Master Blaster, with my gat in hand, never understand why we live so. Like my rap. Its the master killa of the Outlaws along wit the Genius Asad, the Ghost face killa of this s#@t Jimmy, and the blunt smokin Method man of the Outlaws Downtown and the mind behind the madness Mack 5-0 together we are OUTLAWS 4 LIFE!!! :-> __________________________________________________________ [Doc Watson]: (to a still angry Malone) Yo yo yo homey! You gots ta chill bro! [Malone]: Why must you antagonize me? Just please leave me alone and lets finish the show. If you don't stop, I'll be forced to report you to President Ismael! [Doc Watson]: (pretending to be scared) Oh no! Anything but that! Oh please, Jerry, please don't tell on me! [Malone]: (not clueing in to Doc's sarcasm) Ok, but you better behave, or you'll get in trouble! In fact, looks like someone is in trouble right now. (a old fashioned television set drops from the ceiling) _______________________________________________________ (Everything is in black and white. Barney is escorting the camera man into the court house) [Barney:] Now Phil I'm gonna haft'a book ya' [Man:] What for Barney? [Barney:] For J-walkin'. I saw walk across the street in the middle of the block....... [Man:] But Barney..... (Barney opens a case and reveals it a finger print kit. He grabs the camera man's left and begins to ink his hand with the ink roller.) [Barney:] No Phil...no excuses...I caught you in the act... (Big Andy Cool walks into the court house) [Andy:] Barney! [Barney:] Hey Ang....I'm bookin' Phil for J-walkin'. [Andy:] Barney why are you doin' that...we've always let Phil the camera man cross in the middle of the block..so he can get his shot quick.... [Barney:] Oh but Andy.... [Andy:] No Barn.... (Andy wipes Phil's hand on off. Barney looks on with a depressed look.) [Andy:] Sorry about this Phil.... (Phil leaves the court house) [Barney:] Ang....we need to nip these criminals in the bud...we let people get away with J-walkin', then they'll start litterin' and the gambling...and finally murder...your gonna let Mayberry turn into a Sin Town! [Andy:] Oh Barney...sometimes you take laws too far...We need to get focused on them funny feller's Day-me-in and Lucy-fer.... [Barney:] I guess yer' right Ang...I'll call Floyd and get him over on the double, so when can start you guys trainin'... (Barney goes over the phone picks up the receiver.) [Barney:] Hello Sara...get me Floyd's Barber Shop...no I don't need a hair cut! (The screen goes to black.) ________________________________________________________ [Doc Watson]: (rubbing his eyes) What the hell was that? [Malone]: That was Mayberry RFD. You know Doc, you're supposed to know what's going on in HIWF. Maybe you should pay more attention. [Doc Watson]: Jerry, I'll try. I really will. But its so hard when your mom is taking up so much of my time. [Malone]: You promised! That's it, I'm calling up President Ismael right now! (Jerry reaches for the phone on the desk) [Doc Watson]: Yeah, you do that you weasel. And while President Ismael is reaming you out for wasting his time, lets check out the final interview tonight from Lazarus. __________________________________________________________________ (Camera shot opens on a mirror in a darkened room.) (A dark figure cloaked in a black robe appears in the mirror.) (An offscreen voice starts to speak. But it is no ordinary voice. It is a gruff, croaking, robotic sounding voice.) [Voice]: Because of you, I no longer look like the man I once was. Because of you, I no longer sound like the man I once was. Because of you, I no longer am the man I once was. (Suddenly a wooden staff lashes out from beneath the black robe and smashes the mirror, shattering it into thousands of tiny shards.) [Voice]: Chameleon! Chameleon, I'm coming! The ~TRUTH~ is coming! And nothing you can do will stop it. At Saturday Night Slam Jam prepare to face the ~TRUTH~! (fades to black) ________________________________________________________________ [Doc Watson]: Well folks, kindof hard to find fault with the way Lazarus feels. After all, Chameleon did destroy his voice box and left him with a face-full of steel plates. Their match on July 5th promises to be a winner. (Malone is still on the phone.) [Malone]: Yes I'm still holding. What? What do you mean he won't take my call? He's too busy? He told you I have to settle this myself? That's not fair! [Doc Watson]: Well folks, looks like I'll be around for at least another edition of S.Y.M.! (Starts blowing smoke rings) [Doc Watson]: So for the whining Jerry Malone and the ever gorgeous Bebe Cake, I'm Smiling' Doc Watson. Remember to tune us in next week when we'll have the results from the first ever Saturday Night Slam Jam. Keep it cranked to the nines, baby! Who loves ya?!?
