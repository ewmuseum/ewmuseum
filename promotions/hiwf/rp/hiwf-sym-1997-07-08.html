S.Y.M.: Speaking Your Mind, 8 July 1997

8 July 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The bigger than life symbol of HIWF and two wrestler's pummeling each other senseless crashes onto your screen. Underneath it black bold faced letters appear... "SPEAKING YOUR MIND". Blaring out your speakers is 'Killer' by Adamski. To hear it for real in MIDI format, download it here at http://www.globalserve.net/~cracker/killer.mid (36KB). Then the screen goes black for a second and nice looking white letters appear on your screen. PARENTAL GUIDANCE WARNING, THE FOLLOWING PROGRAM WILL CONTAIN LANGUAGE OF A COARSE NATURE. PLEASE BE ADVISED. YADDA YADDA YADDA. The camera shot opens on Doc Watson and Jerry Malone seated and the Control Center desk. Doc is holding his head in his hands and is moaning quite loudly. In front of him is a large glass of Alka-Seltzer and a bottle of Tylenol. Jerry looks at him in disgust. [Doc Watson]: Ohhhhhhhhh! Turn off that bloody music! My head is killing me! [Malone]: Hung over from Independence Day? That was four days ago! And besides, I thought you were Canadian? (Doc takes a big gulp of the foaming Alka-Seltzer.) [Doc Watson]: I am Canadian. But you know me. I never pass up a good party. (belches) [Malone]: Doc, I think you've hit a new low. Of all things, coming to work hung over! What would President Ismael say? [Doc Watson]: I don't know. Why don't you call your Mom's place and ask him? [Malone]: Don't get started! We've got President Ismael waiting to make some comments right now, so lets see what he has to say. _____________________________________________________ (Camera shot changes to President Ismael's office) ([name withheld] is sitting at his posh mahogany desk.) [Robert:] I wanted to take this time to announce the date of the very first HIWF Pay-Per-View. Our first will be the Summer Slam Jam. It will be held on Sunday July 27th. There will not be a card on Saturday the 26th. I also wanted to comment on the incident last Saturday involving Chameleon and Lazarus. Many viewers we're offended by the amount of blood that was shed. I wanted tell those concern viewers, BITE ME! If you don't like seeing two guys beating the hell out of each other then watch that dull boring NEWA! We here in the HIWF put our hearts into these events. We don't want whining, complaining viewers who aren't satisfied by the product we put out. I'm sure the NEWA has those "pie in the face" matches, or those "Aluminum Cage" matches. All you'll ever see on HIWF television is the most exciting, the most entertaining wrestling. If you want to see two guys using rest holds for 10 minutes in a match, then watch the NEWA. If you to see to guys oozing pints of blood trying to kill each other then you've come to the right place. I will never make any apologies for the product we put out! Oh by the way, buy our shirts and posters or I'll kill ya'. Just kidding I was just seeing if you were paying attention. _____________________________________________________ [Doc Watson]: Oooooooo, does he have to talk so loud? [Malone]: President Ismael is just making sure that everyone understands what HIWF is all about. I for one think its refreshing. [Doc Watson]: Yeah, you would. [Malone]: Tomorrow night on Wednesday Night Reckoning the Television Title Tournament Begins. Those involved include The Acer, Mysterio, Attitude Adjuster, Jermain Asad, Grave Digger, Lazarus, Lucifer, Ice Man Jeff Koch, Damian, Chameleon, and Philippe Montreuil and Antoine Dubois of The French Connection. This promises to be an exciting show! [Doc Watson]: Look, could we just hurry up and get ~THIS~ show over with? My head feels like a balloon! (Doc puts a handful of Tylenol in his mouth and starts chewing.) [Malone]: I wish I could say I feel sorry for you but I don't. You should be more like me, Doc. I don't drink and I don't smoke. [Doc Watson]: Yeah, and you don't get laid either! Let's go and see what's up with those Frenchmen, Antoine and Philippe. ______________________________________________________ (We are live in the lockeroom with the French Connection and their manager "Pretty Boy" Bobby Lawrence.) [Pretty Boy]: Listen up!!! Grave Digger and Lazarus. You better watch your back this Wednesday because my boys are very upset with that stupid referee who gave a victory to the L.A. Mafia. They're both very hungry for that TV belt. I don't know who's gonna take this belt but I know it will be surely one of my boys. Tell 'em Philippe!!! [Philippe]: You're right, Pretty One, moi et Antoine are gonna be ready for our matches and losers like Grave Digger and Lazarus are gonna get the beating they rightfully deserve. [Antoine]: Lazarus!!! I hope you're listening, if you try to do something stupid, mon ami Philippe here, is gonna watch my back and he's gonna make sure you go out as the loser of this match!!! [Pretty Boy]: As for you, L.A. Mafia!!! I'm sure we'll meet again and this time you'll get crushed courtesy of "Pretty Boy" Bobby Lawrence and the French Connection!!! [Philippe]: Grave Digger!!! You are obviously no match for the best technical wrestler in the world and I'm gonna make sure that you rest in the grave you dug!!! (He laughs) [Antoine]: The best wrestler and myself, the sexiest man alive are gonna be ready to rumble Wednesday night and one of us is gonna be surely the next TV Champion!!! Grave Digger and Lazarus!!! You're going to see why we are the best, Vive la France!!! ______________________________________________________ [Doc Watson]: Well we'll see who the best ones are tomorrow night. Lazarus is a former World Champion and Grave Digger is a former Television Title Champion. Both men know their way around the squared circle and I should know. And you know why I should know? [Malone]: (waves his hand is dismissal) Yeah, yeah, I know. 10 time World Heavyweight Champion. Blah, blah, blah. I've heard it a million times before! Do you tell your kids that story when you want to put them to sleep? [Doc Watson]: Hey, smart guy! I don't have any kids! At least none that I know of. (Doc winks at the camera) But here's a guy that seems to have lots of kids to take care of. I'm talking about that dink, Bucky Jones. ________________________________________________________ (The scene fades in on a high school gymnasium. In the background a group of boys are practicing. Standing over them is Bucky Jones. He is dressed in a sweat suit. It has his former school's emblem on the front. He seems perturbed.) [Bucky]: You boys want to win the championship, you've got to get tough! I could take you all at once and wipe the floor with the lot of you! C'mon! FIGHT! FIGHT! (One of the boys looks up from the mat on which he is practicing) [Bill the Wrestling Teenager]: You've changed... What happened to you Bucky? [Bucky]: The Acer taught me a lesson. He almost ended my career. What would I do then? Wrestling is my life. It has been since I was a small boy. I don't know how to do anything else. When the Acer hurt my knee it made me wake up... I was too excited to be in the big leagues. I wasn't taking it seriously enough. And that almost cost me... It almost cost me my future. I decided that it was time for me to fight fire with fire. I'm a NEW Bucky Jones... And I don't take poo-poo from anybody! Now get back to work guys, we got a tournament to get ready for! (Scene fades) __________________________________________ [Doc Watson]: Gee. That was really inspirational. Where's my gun? [Malone]: What's wrong with you? Here's a young wrestler trying to turn his career around and you mock his efforts! Shame! [Doc Watson]: Look, I'm not in the mood for this. Just continue without me. (Doc crawls slowly under the desk.) [Malone]: And you get paid for this? This is insane! You want me to finish the show without you? Fine! At least I won't have to listen to you. (Doc belches loudly from under the desk.) _______________________________________________________ (The scene fades in on a table in the Boardwalk Cafe, in Nashville. "Maddog" Gnort and Outlaw Justice sitting across the table from each other. Gnort has a glass in front of him. A plate of cheese sticks sits between the two of them. They seem to be holding a meeting.) [Gnort]: Good work in your match last week, big man. You won it no problem. Now, this week you face Tommy Drummond... [Outlaw]: It'll be another cakewalk. You're facing Downtown G, though. [Gnort]: Hey, I can handle myself. He's all talk and bluster. No way he can beat the greatest wrestler anywhere, ever... [Outlaw]: Probably, true. But he's facing you. I'll be there to watch your back. [Gnort]: Whoa, I can handle myself... When have I ever needed you to win a match? (Outlaw starts to answer but is interrupted as another customer walks up to the table. He's holding a napkin in one hand and an ink pen in the other. He pushes them in front of Gnort's face.) [Gnort]: Hey! This is a private meeting! I don't go around to your table trying to give you writing utensils, do I? Get outta here! [Bill the Misguided Fan]: But, I just want your autograph, Mr Maddog. [Gnort]: Mister, my autograph doesn't come that cheap... (He turns to Outlaw) Get this guys payment. (Outlaw Justice stands up, grabs the fan by his collar and bodyslams him through the table as Gnort watches. Gnort then stands up, calmly takes the pen and napkin, signs the napkin and drops it onto the fans twisted, broken body. Then Outlaw and Gnort leave the premises, both laughing.) (Scene fades) _____________________________________________________ [Malone]: I can't believe what I just saw! That was terrible! [Doc Watson]: (from under the desk) Maybe they thought it was you! [Malone]: I thought you were passed out! Just be quiet and please let me continue. Unlike you, I take pride in my work. (Doc belches the words 'EAT ME' from under the desk.) (Malone makes a face.) [Malone]: Fine. It looks like things are degrading at a rapid pace around here so I'll try to finish things up as quick as I can so let's go to some pre-recorded comments from Chameleon. _________________________________________________ (Chameleon is in a hospital room preparing to check out. He slips his shirt over his heavily taped ribs. His head is bandage on his forehead) [Nurse:] Now Mr. Chameleon, remember Dr. Johns says you're not supposed to be active for the next three months. You run the risk of damaging your 4th and 6th ribs even further. Plus, with the concussion you have suffered, you should have someone monitor you while you sleep. You could end up in coma if you're not careful. [Chameleon:] I will Nurse White.....I'm going to go home and rest for the next three months.... (The nurse leaves the room. Chameleon takes his shirt off and rips the tape off his ribs and head.) [Chameleon:] Lazarus........don't believe everything you see....I might looked injured and beaten....but I have the will to end your career...as long as I am alive....I will make sure your life is a living hell...that plastic championship belt from that other fed...doesn't mean jack..... because you didn't beat me to get it....Lazarus...this will never be over...... This Wednesday I will destroy Damian....you used to be a great wrestler... but you let a woman affect your dominance in the ring...now you and your brother Lucifer run around in identity crisis.... Damian...kiss your butt good bye....... ________________________________________________ [Malone]: Chameleon looks like he's got his work cut out for him. Damian is no pushover and Lazarus is bent on destroying Chameleon's career. [Doc Watson]: (from under the desk) Too bad nobody would destroy your career. Oh, what am I talking about? You don't have a career! Ha! (Doc slowly crawls back out from under the desk.) [Malone]: Do you want me to call President Ismael? I will you know! [Doc Watson]: I don't care. Do what you want, do anything. As long as it puts me out of my misery. (Doc grabs a big Thermos full of coffee and starts drinking it black.) [Malone]: You're a mean, spiteful man Doc. I hope your hangover hurts, I really do. But the show must go on and right now we have an interview with The Outlaws. ____________________________________________________________ (Interview starts with Downtown G. sitting down on stairs behind what seems to be a arena) [Downtown]: I'm going to be serious for a minute so don't even say a word camera man or I'll put that on everything I will kill your sorry ass. At Saturday Night Slam Jam, one of the most brutal matches in my career took place when I faced one of the top competitors in the world, Jermain Asad. The match could have gone either way but thanks to the mind behind the madness, Mack 5-0, nobody was injured and a winner was crowned, which was me. Now I look to next week, the 3 way dance. Oh yeah they say first I have to beat Maddog Gnort, but its no secret I have beat him before and hell everybody knows he does not have a chance in hell of beating me. I will carve him up like turkey and then I look on to the 3 way dance for the World Title. Who are we kidding, everybody knows its gonna come down to me and Outlaw Justice, you people out there think our little stand off was extreme or that Lazarus vs. Chameleon match was extreme wait until the Outlaws get in that 3 Way Dance. In the NEWA [name withheld] did everything to prevent me from getting my well deserved title shot, but no, Robert will have no choice but to sit there and watch me win the World Heavyweight Championship in front of everybody, you wait and see motherf#@kers. [Jimmy]: This coming Saturday will also mark a new era in tag team wrestling when me and Jason win the tag team belts. Hate Machine bring your best impersonations cause after we get done wit your asses you will need new identities cause we will embarrass your ass and that's the bottom line! [Asad]: Mysterio look into my face, I ask you what you see? Death for you. [Mack]: OUTLAWS 4 LIFE :->!!!! ___________________________________________________________ [Doc Watson]: Well I think they've pissed off just about everyone. One things for sure, don't mess with The Outlaws or they'll bum rush yo' mama! By the way, how's your mom Jerry? [Malone]: I'm not going to dignify that with an answer. In fact, I'm just going to go right to the next interview and not talk to you at all. [Doc Watson]: You big baby. _____________________________________________________________ (Picasso Trigger stands in the middle of a jammed packed arena. He is wearing his Mean Gene bald cap, mustache, and jacket.) [Picasso:] Hello Ladies and gentlemen..right now on the hotline you can hear the results to WrestleMania 2 for only $10.99 a minute. And now please welcome my special guests, here are the 2 Stallions, Double D, Damian Anderson and the Pre-Mature Boy Slic Hair! (The theme to 2001 blasts and the crowd goes wild. Damian comes out in Arn Anderson black trunks, with a 2 Stallions shirt and Arn Anderson reading glasses. Lucifer comes out in his Ric Flair blonde wig and robe. Two lovely young ladies in string bikinis walkout with him.) [Picasso:] Welcome Slic.... [Lucifer:] Scheme Gene! Woooooooooooo!!! (The crowd goes Woooooooo!) [Picasso:] Now who are these two lovelies with you tonight Slic. [Lucifer:] Well Scheme Gene..after I'm done with this interview..these two young ladies are gonna ride Space Mountain! (Picasso has an embarrassed look on his face.) [Lucifer:] That's right Gene, I'm going to take these ladies to Disney Land and we're gonna ride Space Mountain all nigh' long!Wooooooooo! (The crowd goes Woooooooo!) [Picasso:] Now Slic...this Wednesday you and Damian have to face some very tough opponents in singles competition. [Lucifer:] Well you know Gene, this Wednesday I'm goin' be facin' a noble opponent...Ice Man....I have a lot respect for you pal..... but when I get in that ring, I'm gonna dance all of you...tell'em Double D. (Picasso puts the mic in front of one of the ladies chest. Damian points the mic to him) [Damian:] You know Gene, I can see you pre-occupied...but this Wednesday I won't be when I take that piece of trash Chameleon out to the curb. Chameleon, son.....it's time the grown ups taken control things....so after this Wednesday you won't be playin' with you're make-up and disguises...because no one will recognize you anyway..... (Damian walks to the back) [Picasso:] Hey Slic, do you think maybe we can go a bar later? Because I can easily drink you under that table. [Lucifer:] Only if you're buyin' Scheme Gene [Lady:] And Gene, I'll make sure that we will "drink you under the table". [Picasso:] Whoa Nellie, I'm gonna get some tonight! Remember ladies and gentlemen you can hear the this interview live on the hotline tonight for only $10.99 a minute. _____________________________________________________________ [Malone]: (with a worried look on his face) Oh man! We're going to be sued for sure! [Doc Watson]: What the hell for? Everybody rips of everybody! Its the nature of the beast! I mean look at you. From the way you're dressed, it looks like you've ripped off that masterbator Pee-Wee Herman! Ha! [Malone]: You can say that on TV! [Doc Watson]: What? Masterbator? [Malone]: Stop it now! We're going to be canceled! (Jerry yells to someone off-camera.) [Malone]: Quick, next interview! [Doc Watson]: (chanting) Masterbator! Masterbator! Masterbator! __________________________________________________________ (Mysterio and Stephanie are eating lunch at a posh L.A. hot spot.) [Mysterio:] Do you know what bugs me Stephanie? [Stephanie:] What dear? [Mysterio:] The HIWF and President Ismael. I mean I was lured by him to join...he offered me a lot of money, saying I would be facing the toughest competition that the world has to offer....and look who was my first opponent...Bucky Freakin' Jones.... [Stephanie:] He's a nice kid. [Mysterio:] Yeah, but he was chicken scratch compared to the other guys. Guys like Grave Digger, Lazarus, Chameleon, Outlaw Justice... [Stephanie:] Well this Wednesday you get the beat up The Acer... [Mysterio:] Hopefully this is a sign that the HIWF is getting bigger names to defeat.... [Stephanie:] You know honey...that Miss Gray last week came out in that thong that showed off her flabby butt and sagging chest. If the fans of the HIWF want to see a firm and tan body maybe I'll show them a thing or two..... [Mysterio:] Acer...this Wednesday you be one of the few lucky ones who gets to experience the greatness called Mysterio...And onto this Saturday....I am going to make another lucky wrestler an offer to experience my excellence in that ring...... _________________________________________________ [Malone]: Seems pretty confident about his abilities, doesn't he? [Doc Watson]: Hey, with a chick like Stephanie at my side, I'd be pretty confident too! Va-va-voom! [Malone]: Nice. Sexual harassment too! Are there no limits to your vulgarity? [Doc Watson]: (lighting up a Cohiba cigar with a $100 bill.) Ummmmm, nope! [Malone]: I didn't think so. I'll be glad when this hour is over. We've got 2 more interviews to go so lets run them right now. First off we have comments from the deformed Lazarus. ___________________________________________________________ (Camera shot opens on a darkened room. The camera faintly picks out a seated black-robed figure holding a long wooden staff.) (A gruff, croaking, robotic sounding voice starts to speak.) [Lazarus]: Frenchman. Tomorrow night you stand in my way of the Television Title. You issue threats that you have no chance of backing up. When I lay my Terminus Heart Punch on you, you will see what it means to be in the ring with me. Retribution, swift and painful. [Lazarus]: Chameleon, blood has passed between us before. Saturday Slam Jam was not the first time and it won't be the last either. You and I are on the same track, racing towards a head on collision. (Lazarus pulls back the hood of his robe, revealing his horribly scarred face.) [Lazarus]: Look at this face! Beneath my scarred skin are plates of steel! Do you really think you can hurt me any more that I already have been? Give me your best shot Chameleon, and I'll still be standing, I promise you that. (fade to BLACK) ________________________________________________________________ [Doc Watson]: That bloodbath between Chameleon and Lazarus had to be the greatest, bloodiest match I'd ever seen! I'd love to see those two go toe-to-toe again! [Malone]: You would. That match actually made me sick to my stomach. [Doc Watson]: I'm not surprised, you pussy. Ok, I've had enough. I feel like ten pounds of crap in a five pound bag. So for Jerry Malone, I'm Hung-Over Doc Watson. Remember to tune us in next week when we'll have more interviews from the superstars of the HIWF. Keep it cranked to the nines, baby! Who loves ya?!?
