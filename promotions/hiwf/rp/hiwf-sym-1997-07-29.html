HIWF (Jun 1997) — Roleplay

S.Y.M.: Speaking Your Mind, 29 July 1997

29 July 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The bigger than life symbol of HIWF and two wrestler's pummeling each other senseless crashes onto your screen. Underneath it black bold faced letters appear... "SPEAKING YOUR MIND". Blaring out your speakers is 'SONG 2' by Blur. **To hear the NEW theme music in MIDI format, download it here at http://www.globalserve.net/~cracker/song2.mid (30KB).** Then the screen goes black and nice looking white letters appear on your screen. PARENTAL GUIDANCE WARNING, THE FOLLOWING PROGRAM WILL CONTAIN LANGUAGE OF A COARSE NATURE. PLEASE BE ADVISED. YADDA YADDA YADDA. ________________________________________________________________________ (The scene now changes to the HIWF Control Center. We are greeted by the grinning face of Smilin' Doc Watson and the serious look of Jerry Malone. Doc is smoking his trademark Cohiba cigar and speaks first.) [Doc Watson]: Last Sunday we saw the first ever HIWF Pay-Per-View and what an event it was! When the smoke finally cleared, the HIWF has a new World Heavyweight Champion and new Tag Team Champions! I must say that Outlaw Justice proved me wrong. He didn't screw up, and it looks like he's got the right stuff to be the champ. And I should know. And do know why I should know? [Malone]: (waves his hand in dismissal) Not now, Doc. Not now. Also, Hate Machine became the Tag Team Champions in questionable fashion when it appeared that there were in fact four members of Hate Machine fighting in the match. [Doc Watson]: Four? What match were you watching? I only saw two the whole time! [Malone]: You would. Good thing you're not in charge. Well on this episode of Speaking Your Mind we've sent our camera crew backstage to get interviews that took place right after Summer Slam Jam, uncut and unedited, as it happened when it happened. So sit back and relax as we start off with the "All American" Jesse Ewiak. ________________________________________________________________________ (Scene is in the training room after SSJ. Jesse's ribs are taped up and he is in obvious pain.) [Jesse Ewiak]: McTavish, fine, you beat me. I have no qualms about that. But Mysterio, I want you man. I'll take you on anywhere, anytime. You do not want to mess with me. Now, the TV Title Tourney. I will hold that gold belt up high in the air. You better bet on it. OVER AND OUT!!!!!!!! ________________________________________________________________________ (The camera shot changes as it opens on the door of "Ice Man" Jeff Koch. A hand pushes the door open and inside is the "Ice Man" himself.) [Ice Man]: I've been threatened by this organization to be fired if I did not Speak My Mind, so I thought, I'd better clear up a few things. I ain't no pansy ass in this federation to be made a fool of. I'm here for one thing and one thing only: Glory. Mack 5-0, you got in my way of the Television Title and pissed me right off. At the Pay-Per-View, Cheryl tried and I whooped her ass. I don't care who you are, if you want a piece of the "Ice Man", step in line. I will take on anybody, anytime and if this camera man doesn't stop looking at me the wrong way I might whoop his ass. Oh yeah, hey Outlaw Jack, if you are feeling generous, I take a title shot anytime, anyplace, anyday. Because I am the best that this world will ever see..... ("Ice Man" turns to walk away but turns back.) [Ice Man]: Oh yeah, with "Stable Wars" coming up, the "Ice Man" wants participants to be a part of my stable called "The Masters of Disaster". I'm looking for the top men so send entries to: [detail withheld] (As "Ice Man" Jeff Koch walks away with "Stone Cold" blaring and a Huge Face Pop he shoves the camera man out of his way and gives him a double birdie and yells " Don't Nobody Mess With The Best Son!!!) ________________________________________________________________________ (The Camera-Guy makes his way to the Hoosier Dome training facilities where he sees Mysterio is training in a wrestling ring with a couple of jobbers.) [Mysterio:] Ah, yes....Big Andy Cool you will see my new finisher called the Mangler. This move is so deadly that I can apply from almost any position or hold. I have perfected this move to fine a art. (One of the jobbers performs a spinning leg scissors on Mysterio. Mysterio recovers and executes the Mangler out of nowhere.) [Mysterio:] Grave Digger, this Saturday I will achieve my third win in my push to wearing gold. Because I am "Mr. High Impact" and I will get the 1, 2, 3. ________________________________________________________________________ (Camera shot changes again, this time panning around looking for another HIWF Superstar. In the distance, The Outlaws can be seen talking in the ring. The Camera-Guy runs up to them just in time to catch the following.) (A obvious Tommy Drummond imitator is interviewing The Mafia and Jermain Asad.) [Fake Tommy]: Ok, guys now let's try to keep the violence down cause I I I Knnooww thatt youu guys cann kickk my asss, please stoopp the the violence! [Jason]: What's the problem Tommy, can't talk without stuttering? [Fake Tommy]: Pleasee spare my li.. li.. lifee. [Jimmy]: Don't worry Tommy, we know you're sore from earlier tonight so we won't whip your ass tonight. [Fake Tommy]: God bless you guys. [Jimmy]: Anyways before we give the mic to Jermain Asad, who always has something on his mind, and personally in the mood he is in today I don't want to be around him when he starts speakin' his mind, but, Tommy I gotta surprise for you and the world tonight, maybe we should bring them out, how about it Jason? [Jason]: I agree. Ladies and gentlemen, the newest members of the OUTLAWS, here are Maddog Seth and Pain Killa..... The Maddogs of War!!!! (The Pittbulls theme plays throughout the arena, as they enter the ring) [Fake Tommy]: Oh my God, are they gonna kick my ass?? [Jimmy]: Yes Tommy, they are.... GET 'IM!! (Pain Killa picks up the Fake Tommy by his throat and throws him out of the ring through a table!) [Jason]: Guys tell 'em why you're here? (Both Pain Killa and Maddog Seth turn around and exit the ring.) [Jimmy]: Hey, where are you guys going? (The L.A. Mafia leave the ring.) [Asad]: Now that we have gotten that out of the way, let me get down to business. Hate Machine what you punk ass bi#@hes did to Dennis tonight will not go with out its payback, so at this time me and Dennis challenge you to a "Chicago Street Fight" for the Tag Team belts one week from this Saturday!!! As for Tommy and Mark, this Wednesday your asses get schooled by the real Outlaws, and now I will allow my good friend Mack 5-0 his interview time. [Mack]: What happened tonight was just what you people should expect from Downtown G, cause he is a lone rebel, but Downtown if you are listening to this, you always have a home with the Outlaws, you know that, and I promise you people he will be back, that's all I have to say OUTLAWS 4 LIFE!!! :-> ________________________________________________________________________ (The Camera-Guy leaves the ring and is once again walking backstage. Suddenly, someone grabs his arm and drags him into a dressing room. The Camera-Guy turns around and there stands "The Natural" Mark Drummond and Bobby Lawrence. They don't look very happy.) [Pretty Boy]: Outlaw Justice!!! You won that HIWF Championship. Enjoy it while it lasts, because when "Mr. Universe" will be back, he will come take what is rightfully his!!! [Natural]: I talked to my brother from his hotel room and he said that he's not done with you Outlaw Justice!!! He says he wants a rematch as soon as possible to show the world that your victory was a big fluke!!! [Pretty Boy]: A big fluke!!! You bet it was a big fluke!!! And you know why??? Because a World Championship Match should not be decided in a stupid ladder match!!! [Natural]: "Mr. Universe" is gonna be back for the next Wednesday Night Reckoning and you Jermain Asad and Dangerous Jason are gonna see why my brother should still be World Champion!!! As for myself, I'll be enjoying every moment!!! [Pretty Boy]: And if that chump Dangerous Jimmy tries to stick his nose where it doesn't belong, the Mongolian Assassin will be there to make sure he doesn't disturb my boys!!! [Natural]: Me and my brother are gonna soon have all these HIWF titles!!! Primo, my brother will win back the HIWF Title. Secundo, I will personally make sure that the TV Title will go around my waist and tertio, the Drummonds are gonna be dominating the tag team division in the HIWF and if you don't like!!! We don't give a damn!!! [Pretty Boy]: Jermain Asad and Dangerous Jason!!! You're gonna be the first to know why these Drummonds are the best!!! You're gonna go down "Lawrence Family" style!!! ________________________________________________________________________ (The Camera-Guy leaves the dressing room and hears a voice calling out.) [Voice]: Yoo-Hoo! Mr. Camera-Guy! (The Camera-Guy turns around and see Bucky Jones stands outside his dressing room. The Camera-Guy walks over to him.) [Bucky]: Ollie Keaton, you have a big mouth. Almost as big as The Acer's... You best watch what you say about me from now on, I will only stand for so much. And, Acer, the same goes for you... I will get both of you back for the things you've said and done to make fun of me... I've seen and heard every bit of it. And you will both pay for every comment and every fake Bucky! And I'll do it my way... I have a surprise or two up my sleeve, and you'll all see what it is when the time is right! ________________________________________________________________________ (The Camera-Guy is now wandering around aimlessly, looking for someone to talk too. HIWF roadies are packing up the lights and sound systems. Just then, the Camera-Guy's walkie-talkie goes off, telling him to get his ass to the Hoosier Dome interview studio. The Camera-Guy quickly runs there and sees several reporters surrounding Chameleon who is standing in the studio with Picasso Trigger, and Maze.) [Maze]: Listen up ladies and gentlemen. I am about to introduce to you the man, the myth, the legend. I am of course talking about the man who has lead many a superstars to gold, here he is Picasso Trigger. (Maze takes out a small tape recorded and presses play. A canned applause sounds.) [Picasso]: Thank you, thank you ladies and gentlemen. I know a couple of you are asking, what about Hate Machine? We mutually parted ways. You see, after I guided them to gold my contract was complete. I had only signed to be their manager until I lead them to gold. And with that out of the way, please let introduce to you the man the plan, the man of 1000 and 1 disguises, here is George Peppard, no I mean Chameleon! (The canned applause sounds again.) [Chameleon]: Lazarus! You my friend are going to never be able to live without me ruining your plans. You caused my own brother to turn against me. That's an offense that you will pay dearly for.... Grave Digger, if you think you have seen the best of my mind games, then you really don't know me that well. Grave Digger you and Lazarus will never rest in peace.... ________________________________________________________________________ (The crowd disperses and once again the Camera-Guy makes his way to the arena where he spots the new HIWF World Heavyweight Champion Outlaw Justice standing in the ring. The Camera-Guy runs up to him.) (Outlaw Justice stands alone in the empty ring. He has the HIWF heavyweight title draped over one shoulder.) [Outlaw]: Does this look like whining to you, Drummond? I don't complain, I don't whine, I get the job done. I said I was going to win this belt, and I did. This is mine now, hope you enjoyed your short little title reign... It'll be the only one you ever have. ________________________________________________________________________ (Outlaw jumps out of the ring and walks off. The Camera-Guy attempts to follow, hoping to get some more comments. He spies Outlaw leaving the Hoosier Dome and runs after him. Once outside, Outlaw Justice cannot be seen. As the Camera-Guy is looking for Outlaw Justice, he spies Andy and Floyd in Walker's drug store sipping on some milk shakes.) [Floyd]: Y...you know An..Andy we have some pretty tough comp... competition this Wed.....Wednesday. [Andy]: I know Floyd....let's not worry about them....until we finished these here shakes. [Floyd]: That sounds like a good idea. (Andy and Floyd continue sipping their shakes as the camera fades to a cool looking yellow, with purple and green polka dots.) ________________________________________________________________________ (The Camera-Guy reaches out and removes the yellow, with purple and green polka dots napkin the fell over the cameralens. Leaving the drug store, the Camera-Guy is ready to call it a night when he get word via his walkie-talkie that "Maddog" Gnort is leaving the Hoosier Dome. The Camera-Guy runs over and sees "Maddog" leaving in a limousine. The Camera-Guy jumps in his truck and follows. After a short drive, the Camera-Guy stops at a door on the outside of a building. It swings around to show a breathtaking view. It is a nicely manicured lawn. There is an Olympic sized pool noticeable in the distance. And on the far side of the pool, a huge house rises up from behind a small hill. The entire yard is fenced in. The sun shines and birds chirp in the background. A crash is heard from the direction of the door, and the camera hurriedly swings back around to cover it. More crashes can be heard, and then the door slams open. "Maddog" Gnort storms out. He stops, looks at the camera, and quickly tries to hide his scowl by smiling. It's obvious the smile is a fake.) [Gnort]: Hey, ho! Welcome HIWF to my humble abode... Naturally, you've seen inside my private gym on several occasions. This is the first time you've ever perused my palatial estate, though, right? [Camera-Guy]: (speaking for the first time) Yes it is. It's certainly photogenic. [Gnort]: It had better be... I pay alot of people alot of money to keep it this way. Why are you here? [Camera-Guy]: Speaking Your Mind would like to get your reaction to Outlaw Justice's victory tonight. [Gnort]: (The smile disappears) My reaction? No comment. [Camera-Guy]: C'mon, Maddog... I need more than that. [Gnort]: You want more? Try this... (Gnort shoves the Camera-Guy aside. The camera falls to the ground but is still pointing toward Gnort and the Camera-Guy. Gnort boots the Camera-Guy in the gut and then slaps on the Bonecrusher DDT on the sidewalk outside his gym.) [Gnort]: (He yells down at the Camera-Guy) You think you got enough that time? What do I think about Outlaw getting that title? I think he's got alot of nerve going over my head! And I think you're gonna pay for asking me about it! (Gnort kicks the Camera-Guy a few times, then picks him up and bodyslams him back to the sidewalk. Then he kicks the lens out of the camera, as the scene disappears into static.) ________________________________________________________________________ ******************************************** High Impact Wrestling Federation http://www.geocities.com/Hollywood/Lot/4700/ ********************************************
