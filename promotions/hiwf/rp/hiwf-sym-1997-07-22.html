HIWF (Jun 1997) — Roleplay

S.Y.M.: Speaking Your Mind, 22 July 1997

22 July 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene opens with a clip of Outlaw Justice and Tommy Drummond in their last match. Tommy Drummond rolls up Outlaw Justice and gets the quick pin.) [DEEP VOICEOVER:] It was the rollup seen ‘round the world. No one believed their eyes when a virtual unknown pinned Outlaw Justice in just 30 seconds. Now Outlaw will have his day, his day of Justice......... (A new clip of Tommy Drummond falling through the three tables from the top of the steel cage plays.) [DVO:] While Tommy Drummond must defend the title that he won the hard way. Drummond promises an easy win to carry on his title reign in a federation riddled with controversy. (Another clip of the man in black falling from the rafters and attacking Grave Digger and Lazarus with a golden baseball bat.) [DVO:] Not only do these two dangerous men have to defend their honor against the French Connection, but they must contend with the devious mind of a madman. [DVO:] All this and more when the High Impact Wrestling Federation on this mid-summers night presents the Summer Slam Jam.............. (A quick video montage of various clips featuring the superstars of the HIWF including "Ice Man" Jeff Koch, Bucky Jones, The Acer, Jesse "All American" Ewiak, The Stormreavers, The Mongolian Assassin, and Hate Machine.) [DVO:] Think you've seen everything? Well, you ain't seen anything yet. Summer Slam Jam....Believe the Hype! (The words Summer Slam Jam Sunday July 27, 1997 explode in a fire ball onto the screen.) ________________________________________________________________________ The bigger than life symbol of HIWF and two wrestler's pummeling each other senseless crashes onto your screen. Underneath it black bold faced letters appear... "SPEAKING YOUR MIND". Blaring out your speakers is 'SONG 2' by Blur. **To hear the NEW theme music in MIDI format, download it here at http://www.globalserve.net/~cracker/song2.mid (30KB).** Then the screen goes black and nice looking white letters appear on your screen. PARENTAL GUIDANCE WARNING, THE FOLLOWING PROGRAM WILL CONTAIN LANGUAGE OF A COARSE NATURE. PLEASE BE ADVISED. YADDA YADDA YADDA. ________________________________________________________________________ (The scene now changes to the HIWF Control Center. We are greeted by the grinning face of Smilin' Doc Watson and the serious look of Jerry Malone. Doc is smoking his trademark Cohiba cigar and speaks first.) [Doc Watson]: HooooWeeee! This Sunday get yourself ready for HIWF's first Pay Per View! So put the kids to bed, grab a six-pack, and kiss your lover goodnight 'cause this promises to be one hell of a show! [Malone]: That's right Doc! Our first ever PPV looks like its going to be a barn-burner with every, that's right, every member of the HIWF involved! Of course, not everyone has their mind on this Sunday's event, like this gentleman here. ________________________________________________________________________ (We're in the hotel room of "The Natural" Mark Drummond who is as always joined by his manager, Bobby Lawrence. They're watching the closing moments of their match versus Downtown G that took place last Wednesday.) [Pretty Boy]: Can you believe this, Mark???!!! [Natural]: No I can't, that stupid ref should have seen what he just did!!! (Downtown G pins Mark Drummond.) [Natural]: You see, this is another stupid official who was obviously not watching the action, Jermain Asad clearly interfered and cost me the match!!! [Pretty Boy]: Don't worry, big man, we're gonna get revenge and soon!!! (They turn towards the camera.) [Natural]: Because of you, Jermain Asad, my brother had to stay in the hospital all of Wednesday night and Thursday morning because of a contusion!!! When we'll get our hands on you, you're gonna pay for what you did!!! [Pretty Boy]: As a matter of fact, I would like to make a statement and I hope that president [name withheld] is listening. I would like to challenge Downtown G and Jermain Asad to a "Death Match" against those very angry Drummond brothers on the July 30 edition of Wednesday Night Reckoning. I want you all to think about all this and I expect to receive an answer to our challenge!!! I talked to "Mr. Universe" this morning at the hospital and he can't wait to get out and kick your stinkin' carcasses to the ground!!! [Natural]: And if you accept, you'll see that the Texas Rednecks are born and are here to stay!!! ________________________________________________________________________ [Doc Watson]: Whoa! A "Death Match"! That's a very intensive match where you must get 3 falls in a row to win. That's pretty hard to do and I should know! And do you know why I should know? [Malone]: (in a bored voice) Because you're a 10 time World Heavyweight Champion? [Doc Watson]: You got it baby! And you know, I really think those Drummond boys can pull it off. [Malone]: This upcoming Sunday, "Pretty Boy" Lawrence's stable will be out in full force. Here we've got some pre-recorded comments from The French Connection who'll be facing Grave Digger and the deformed Lazarus. [Doc Watson]: But before we show it, I've just got one thing to say. Hey, "Pretty Boy"! What the hell do you call your stable? C'mon man! Give me a name! Any name! Just give your stable a name! [Malone]: Finished? (Doc nods his head) Good. Roll the clip. _______________________________________________________________________ (We are now in the room of a hotel in Paris with the French Connection along with their manager.) [Philippe]: We've decided to come a la maison to focus and prepare for our match with Lazarus and Grave Digger. There's nothing like home sweet home!!! Isn't that right mon ami Antoine???!!! [Antoine]: You're right mon ami Philippe, there's nothing like the fresh air of Paris to relax and have a good time unlike those filthy United States of America!!! [Philippe]: Let's talk a little bit about you, Lazarus and Grave Digger. You're obviously no match for the best technical, good looking tag team in the HIWF!!! You two are primarily singles wrestlers, we've been wrestling as a tag team for 5 years, we have a lot more experience than you have in tag team competition!!! We're also still very upset because you cost us the chance to win the TV Title and we're gonna make sure that revenge will be served!!! (Antoine turns towards Bobby Lawrence.) [Antoine]: So Bobby!!! Do you enjoy your stay in Paris so far???!!! [Pretty Boy]: I got to say boys that this country is gorgeous, the monuments, the arts, the girls, the history, this is what it's all about!!! But the thing I love the most are the girls, they're cuter than those in the U.S.!!! [Philippe]: You got it, Bobby!!! This country is filled with beautiful girls. Eat your heart out, U.S.A.!!! [Antoine]: Grave Digger!!! Lazarus!!! Next Sunday, bring everything you've got because yours truly, the sexiest man alive and mon ami, the best technical wrestler in the world are gonna pound everything that's on our way and that includes you two!!! Vive la France!!! ________________________________________________________________________ [Doc Watson]: Well, I've got to agree with "Pretty Boy" about the American chicks! They are pretty brutal. Gimme a Canadian babe every time! [Malone]: Well my Mother's American and I think she's very pretty. [Doc Watson]: So did her brother. Or should I say your father! Ha! [Malone]: That's a disgusting comment! Take it back! [Doc Watson]: (shrugs his shoulders) Hey, its your family tree, not mine! Speaking of disgusting, we've got some comments from that goober in wrestling tights, Bucky Jones. (The monitor between Doc and Jerry comes on showing Bucky Jones.) ________________________________________________________________________ (Camera shot opens on an outdoor basketball court. Bucky Jones is standing off to one side. He's obviously been playing for awhile.) [Bucky]: Acer you think you're a bad guy don't you? Well, you don't know what bad is! And at the next pay-per-view, I'll show you just how bad I am in the ring... I'm going to be so bad that there's no way I could possibly be any worse! And that's not just a promise, it's a fact! (Scene fades) ________________________________________________________________________ [Doc Watson]: Amazing! For once I have to agree with him! [Malone]: You do? [Doc Watson]: Yep! There is just no way on earth Bucky could get any worse! Ha! (blows some smoke rings) [Malone]: Oh, I should have known it would be something like that. Next we have an interview that's in the style of Jesse "All American" Ewiak; quick and to the point. ________________________________________________________________________ (The scene opens with Mysterio is working out in a gym.) [Mysterio]: Drummond, I'm going to keep this short and simple. This Sunday I'm gonna knock you out. And if your brother decides to put his ugly nose in my business I'll clean his clock too. I'm "Mr.High Impact" and this Sunday I will show everyone why I should be #1. ________________________________________________________________________ [Malone]: And once again Mysterio's companion Stephanie is nowhere to be found. I have it on good authority that she wants nothing to with him now after his remarks last week. [Doc Watson]: Well, if "Mr. High Impact" doesn't know how to satisfy his woman, maybe I can teach him a couple of lessons! Speaking of lessons, here's one for you; don't ever, ever play cards with this man here. ________________________________________________________________________ (We're LIVE in the Casino of "The Card Shark" Randy Brandenberg in Las Vegas. He's joined by his manager Bobby Lawrence playing poker.) [Pretty Boy]: What do you have, Randy? [Card Shark]: Two aces and two fours!!! [Pretty Boy]: Not again!!! We've been playing for 2 hours and you're yet to lose a game, you're really "The Card Shark"!!! [Card Shark]: That's right "Pretty Boy"!!! Some people might say that the odds are against me versus a guy like Death on Sunday night because I'm much more older than him. But for me, there's no such thing as odds against me, and you know why??? Because I'm the one who controls the odds!!! This casino is one example, I rule and control this casino. In wrestling, nobody can decide what I'm gonna do, I'm the only one to know what I'm gonna do!!! [Pretty Boy]: This is another good example that money and power are synonyms!!! And that's certainly not the case with you, Death!!! [Card Shark]: I hope you'll bring your good buddy, "Dodo" Stud!!! It will be pleasure to put my "Full House" on the both of you!!! [Pretty Boy]: What do you say we played a little game of darts? [Card Shark]: Ok Bobby, I'll start!!! ("The Card Shark" throws his first dart right in the bullseye.) [Pretty Boy]: Man!!! You're really the king of this casino. [Card Shark]: And that's exactly what's gonna happen come Sunday night, I'll be the king of the ring and this time the bullseye will be you, Death!!! ________________________________________________________________________ [Malone]: Thanks for the advice, Doc. That's one man I'll never play 'Go Fish' with. [Doc Watson]: 'Go Fish'? Are you f@#king kidding me! How old are you, fer Christsakes? [Malone]: Blasphemer! I don't have to work with a blasphemer you know! [Doc Watson]: You wanna talk to me about blasphemy? Well before you pass judgment, listen to this guy here. (Doc points to a monitor behind Jerry's head.) ________________________________________________________________________ (We now go to Bebe Cake, live on location at Mercy Hospital. Bebe is dressed in a low cut mini-skirted nurses outfit with thigh-high white stockings.) [Cake]: I'm here to check on the status of the man known as Lazarus. Last week his electronic voicebox was damaged at the hands of Chameleon in a surprise attack. Let's see how Lazarus is doing. (Cake turns around and behind her is a doctor examining the gaping hole in Lazarus' throat. The doctor turns and picks up a fist-sized metal box and slowly places it in the hole.) [Doctor Eric Wyle]: There. Try that. (Lazarus starts to speak and an errie, gruff robotic sound emerges from the box. His lips do not move.) [Lazarus]: Chameleon.... [Dr. Wyle]: Good. All back to normal. (In a flash, Lazarus reaches out and grabs the Doctor's coat and draws him close to his badly scarred face. Again he speaks in that errie voice and his lips still do not move.) [Lazarus]: Normal? Look at me! Does this look normal? This is far from normal. (Lazarus pushes the Doctor away and looks at Bebe Cake. Cake cringes.) [Lazarus]: Little girl, let me tell you this. I was once a man of God. I fought in his name under the guise of The Reverend. And I was even a champion for him, spreading his word. And what happened? God has forsaken me and cursed me to spend my dying days as some kind of freak! A man with a scarred face with metal implants and a voice made of tin! Why? I gave up asking that question long ago. But I do know this. [Cake]: What's that? [Lazarus]: That I will not rest until the HIWF is as twisted, battered, and horrific as me! Frenchmen, prepare yourself for carnage. Prepare yourself for pain. Prepare yourself to live as I live. Prepare yourself for Lazarus. (Lazarus gets up and walks off, leaving a bewildered Cake behind.) [Cake]: Creepy...... ________________________________________________________________________ [Malone]: Sweet Jesus....... [Doc Watson]: (grinning) Told ya so! So while we've still got our camera crew at Mercy Hospital, lets check in on another patient there, Grave Digger. ________________________________________________________________________ (The scene opens with Grave Digger in Mercy Hospital receiving a checkup by a doctor. The doctor shines a light in his eyes checking his pupils. Candi is seen sitting in the background.) [Doctor]: That nasty hit to the head on Saturday has effected your vision and balance slightly. You're not 100%, but I will give you medical clearance to wrestle this Sunday. However, if your dizziness and vision get worse I will not hesitate to have you removed from that match. (The doctor packs up his things and leaves the room. Grave Digger looks at the camera with his bloodshot eyes.) [Grave Digger]: Chameleon, I know it was you in that black mask last week. You had Maze do your dirty work. This Sunday I am prepared for the falling from the rafters bit, I won't be surprised again. [Candi]: This grudge between you two has gone too far. I don't want any part of this. (Candi walks out of the room, Grave Digger limpingly follows after her.) [Grave Digger]: Chameleon, I promise you, I won't be surprised again. ________________________________________________________________________ [Malone]: Chameleon better have eyes in the back of his head with Grave Digger around. [Doc Watson]: I think Chameleon can hold his own against Grave Digger. Remember, he took out both Grave Digger AND Lazarus last week. That's no easy feat. But I for one am looking forward to the bloodshed those two will make when they meet head on. [Malone]: You would! And I think we can expect some bloodshed from a relative newcomer to HIWF, The Mongolian Assassin. Live on remote once again is Bebe Cake. ________________________________________________________________________ (We are now live in the lockeroom of the Mongolian Assassin. The place is dark and is lightened by several candles. Bobby Lawrence is standing besides the Mongolian Assassin.) [Cake]: (now dressed in an HIWF T-shirt and a pair of tight jeans) I believe you would like to make some comments regarding your proteges's match at Summer Slam Jam? [Pretty Boy]: Psycho Stud!!! You're maybe big, bad and tough, but this man right here is more senseless and tougher than you!!! You can bring you're little friend Death with you because when my Assassin will be done, you two will be lying on the mat begging for some mercy!!! [Cake]: Do you think Psycho Stud will eventually become the HIWF Champion someday? [Pretty Boy]: Let me tell you this, little girl!!! As long as I'm managing in this federation, that big lamebrain Psycho Stud will never be able to beat one of my wrestlers!!! As far as being world champion, I really don't know and I really don't care!!! Right now, the HIWF Champion is my man, "Mr. Universe" Tommy Drummond and not that moron Psycho Stud!!! [Cake]: What advantage does the Mongolian Assassin have against a wrestler like Psycho Stud? [Pretty Boy]: The Assassin is quicker and smarter than that big dummy!!! Psycho Stud relies a lot on power but my Assassin is not one dimensional, he's very sound technically and he mixes speed, power and knowledge very well!!! [Cake]: Do you have any comments, Mongolian Assassin? [Assassin]: (in broken English) Psycho Stud!!! Say your prayers, because after Sunday night, you won't be able to!!! ________________________________________________________________________ [Malone]: Geez, a lot of religion here tonight. [Doc Watson]: All that's missing is "The Attitude Adjuster" Geoff Bondavarsky to lead us in a group prayer. C'mon Jerry, Catch the Dream! [Malone]: Stop it, will you? It told you already, I'm not interested in Catching the Dream. I'd rather listen to what guys like Mayberry RFD have to say. They represent a better, more wholesome era in our lives. [Doc Watson]: Whatever. Listen, just play their interview while I go take a dump, OK? My burrito lunch doesn't seem to be agreeing with me. (Doc gets up and leaves the set.) [Malone]: That's a terrible attitude! Every HIWF member deserves our respect. Anyway, here's some comments from Mayberry RFD. ________________________________________________________________________ (Barney Fife is on the phone.) [Barney:] This is a mess. Thelma Lou, this a mess. Andy is angry at Floyd. I know. Apparently Andy is frustrated at Floyd because of their losses. Yeah I know, they've been friends for years. I don't know Thelma Lou it's a plane mess. (Andy walks into the courthouse.) [Barney:] I gotta go Ang is here. (Barney hangs up the phone.) [Barney:] Ang, I think we need to discuss this thing with Floyd. [Andy:] No. I don't want to discuss it. [Barney:] But Ang.... [Andy:] No Barney, I don't want to discuss and that's that. (The camera fades as Barney continues to talk to Andy.) ________________________________________________________________________ [Malone]: Well, this is refreshing! (Malone looks around and notices that Doc has still not returned.) [Malone]:It looks like there's trouble brewing in Mayberry. I hope they can patch things up. (Doc returns to the set and is tucking in his shirt.) [Doc Watson]: Wheeeew! I wouldn't go in the washroom for the next hour if I were you! [Malone]: (rolls his eyes) Thanks for the update. Glad to see you're keeping true to form. [Doc Watson]: Hey, don't want to disappoint my fans. [Malone]: I'm sure you don't. But sometimes, it is nice to act normal. Like these next gentlemen here. ________________________________________________________________________ (Picasso, Damian, and Lucifer are standing in a studio set. Picasso is talking on a cellular phone.) [Picasso]: Make sure you're there on Sunday Night. Yeah. Ha-ha-ha-ha. You have a camera crew there too. Okay talk to ya'. [Damian]: You see Lucifer, Picasso has all set up for us. [Lucifer]: This is pure genius. L.A. Mafia you turn out the lights, because the party's over. [Damian]: Jason and Jimmy, this Sunday is gonna be too sweet! ________________________________________________________________________ [Doc Watson]: Awwwww! They didn't impersonate anyone! What a gyp! [Malone]: Well I did say they were acting normal. Maybe you should give it a try. [Doc Watson]: What the hell are you talking about? I'm an ICON, Jerry! An ICON! I'm not changing for nothing! [Malone]: ICON now is it? We're going to be sued for sure! [Doc Watson]: Stop whining! President Rob Ismael can handle any lawsuit and then some. And do you know why? 'Cause Rob don't take sh#t from nobody! Look, just shut up and watch the next segment will you! (Doc touches a button on the desk and the center monitor comes on.) ________________________________________________________________________ (The scene changes to that plush, luxurious gym somewhere in Nashville where "Maddog" Gnort is working out. Currently he is working with a punching bag. Outlaw Justice is lifting weights in the background. After a few seconds, Gnort stops punching and turns to the camera.) [Gnort]: As I am sure all the people of the loving public are aware, this Wednesday night I am competing in the second round of the Television Title Tournament... I'm not sure who my opponent is yet, but that doesn't matter... I never pay attention to such mundane details anyway. I have underlings who get paid the big bucks to do that for me. After all, why should I care who I have to face, no other HIWF superstar has a ghost of a chance against me! (Just then, the door to the gym opens and a sheepish, geeky boy wearing thick horn-rimmed glasses shuffles through. He's holding a manila envelope in his hands. He shambles over to Gnort, mumbles something about his agent, an opponent, and Wednesday... Then, he pushes the envelope into Gnort's hands and shuffles back out the door. Gnort looks at the envelope.) [Gnort]: Well, it seems my agent has decided that I should be informed of who I face this week. (He tears open the envelope and pulls out a piece of paper.) No matter who it is, everybody knows I'm the brightest star in the HIWF's inky darkness. Nobody else has a chance... (He reads the paper. His eyes get wide. He tries to cover himself by smiling into the camera, but fails, and realizes it. He turns abruptly and walks to where Outlaw Justice is still lifting weights.) [Gnort]: Yo, emergency meeting, big guy... NOW! We need to discuss my next match... We need to discuss this thing in detail. [Outlaw]: You're the so-called "Brightest star of the HIWF," you handle the match yourself. [Gnort]: I WILL win that match by hook or by crook, and you will help me! Let's go! (Outlaw grudgingly gets up and he and Gnort leave the room... Gnort dropping the piece of paper as he moves away from the weight bench. The camera zooms in on the paper. As the scene fades a word melts into focus: "Lazarus") ________________________________________________________________________ [Malone]: Uh-oh! Looks like 'Maddog' had better burn the midnight oil planning for his match if he's facing Lazarus! [Doc Watson]: No sh#t! If 'Maddog' ain't careful, Lazarus will neuter him. RRRRIIIIIIIPPPPPPPP!!! [Malone]: (wincing) Oooh, do you have to be so graphic? [Doc Watson]: (grinning) Yeah, like you've got to worry eh, Pee-Wee? [Malone]: Stop calling me that! [Doc Watson]: Quick! Roll the next clip before Jerry starts to cry! (Doc starts to laugh as the final clip starts.) ________________________________________________________________________ (Maze is sitting at large desk talking on the phone.) [Maze]: Okay, we'll make sure that we are there this Sunday. Remember 4 wrestlers are better than 2, ha-ha-ha-ha. I got a camera crew here, so I have to go. (He hangs up the phone and notices the camera.) [Maze]: Damn, why can't you guys knock when you enter a room. I want that tape erased up and until this point, got it. [Camera man]: We weren't recording. [Maze]: Good. This Sunday my main man Chameleon has something so good, not even his own Mom would know. Grave Digger, Lazarus you won't know what happened. Plus he and I have a side project that will bring our new stable some gold this Sunday. (The phone rings and Maze picks it up.) [Maze]: Hey, how ya' doin' my man? You want in? Let's do lunch and work out the contractual agreements later. (Maze points to the camera to get out.) ________________________________________________________________________ [Malone]: Looks like "Pretty Boy" Lawrence's stable will start to have some competition. [Doc Watson]: 'Bout time too! And Chameleon sure has it in for Lazarus and Grave Digger. I wonder what Maze is up to? Man oh man, am I looking forward to this Sunday! [Malone]: As am I, Doc. Well, for the lovely Bebe Cake, and the man who's going to cost us the viewership of millions of loving families around the country... [Doc Watson]: Right! Even married women love me! [Malone]: Do you have no... (Suddenly, a loud commotion is heard off camera. Both Malone and Watson look up to see the Stormreavers come crashing onto the set, warhammers in hand.) [Rend]: YOU!!! (Rend points at Doc Watson.) [Doc Watson]:(Doc's cigar falls out of his mouth) Uh...yes? [Maim]: You are the mouthpiece for the warriors known as the Nobles, right? [Doc Watson]: Huh? What are you two Viking rejects talking about? [Rend]: REJECTS? (Rend raises his warhammer and brings it down on the announcer's table, breaking it in half!) [Malone]: HE IS! YES, HE IS! [Doc Watson]: MALONE! [Maim]: We wish to deliver words to them! [Doc Watson]: Well, then just talk to the camera! [Rend]: Camera? [Doc Watson]: That thing! (points to the camera) Just look into it and talk! (Rend and Maim step up to the camera until their faces are inches away. Behind them, Doc Watson is shown trying to sneak away.) [Malone]: Oh, is 'Mr. 10 Time World Heavyweight Champion' running away? [Doc Watson]: Uh...no! I just have an appointment...to get my Porsche detailed! [Crew]: SIT DOWN, WATSON! [Rend]: SILENCE! NOBLES! We have torn through Mayberry RFD and dismantled the Hate Machine. Now, our full fury is focused on the one thing standing between us and the glory we crave...you! [Maim]: People in HIWF speak of you two with awe and holy reverence. They say you are one of the best teams ever. [Doc Watson]: You got that right! [Rend]: DO NOT INTERRUPT! (Rend raise his warhammer, and Watson cringes.) [Maim]: If these words are true, Nobles, THEN WE WELCOME BATTLE WITH YOU! WHEN OTHER TEAMS ARE AFRAID TO FIGHT, THE STORMREAVERS WILL ENTER BATTLE WITH WAR CRYS ON OUR LIPS AND WARHAMMERS IN OUR HANDS! [Rend]: And even more so...WE WILL DEFEAT YOU AND ALL OF YOUR FRIENDS! ACER, DO NOT INTERFERE IF YOU VALUE YOUR OVERRATED LIFE! WE WILL DEFEAT THE NOBLES AND GAIN OUR RIGHTFUL TITLE SHOT! [Maim]: Here's a taste of what is in store for you, Nobles, when Twilight's Hammer falls on you! (Maim and Rend turn around and raise their warhammers with both hands. Malone and Watson scatter as the Stormreavers bring their hammers down on the camera forcefully! All that can be heard are the screams of the Stormreavers as the screen turns to static.) ________________________________________________________________________ ******************************************** High Impact Wrestling Federation http://www.geocities.com/Hollywood/Lot/4700/ ********************************************
