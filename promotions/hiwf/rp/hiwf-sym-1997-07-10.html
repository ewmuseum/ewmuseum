S.Y.M.: Speaking Your Mind, 10 July 1997

10 July 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The bigger than life symbol of HIWF and two wrestler's pummeling each other senseless crashes onto your screen. Underneath it black bold faced letters appear... "SPEAKING YOUR MIND". Blaring out your speakers is 'Killer' by Adamski. To hear it for real in MIDI format, download it here at http://www.globalserve.net/~cracker/killer.mid (36KB). Then the screen goes black for a second and nice looking white letters appear on your screen. PARENTAL GUIDANCE WARNING, THE FOLLOWING PROGRAM WILL CONTAIN LANGUAGE OF A COARSE NATURE. PLEASE BE ADVISED. YADDA YADDA YADDA. The screen shot opens on two well dressed men sitting behind a large desk with several monitors behind them. Jerry Malone is seated on the left and he is wearing a checked sportscoat with the HIWF logo stitched on the breast pocket. Doc Watson is sitting on the right wearing a black 3 piece suit and smoking his trademark Cohiba cigar. [Doc Watson]: Hey-o! This is Smilin' Doc Watson ready to bring you another mediocre edition of S.Y.M! This week, we have a surprise for you folks.....I'm not hung-over! Wooooooo! [Malone]: Like that's something to be proud of? You know, I knew I shouldn't have driven you home last time. I still can't get the smell of puke out of my Fiat. What on earth were you drinking? [Doc Watson]: (shrugs his shoulders) Hey, who knows? I just drank whatever was in your Mom's liquor cabinet. [Malone]: Again with my Mother! This is getting really tiresome. Doesn't everyone agree? (Jerry looks around the studio.) [Off-camera Voices]: NO!!! [Malone]: (shocked) Oh....well...I'm getting really tired of it and I'm sure my Mother is too. [Doc Watson]: That's not what she told me last night! Ha! [Malone]: (ignoring Doc's last comments) The roster of HIWF is growing more and more everyday. To start off this edition of S.Y.M., we have a special interview with 'The Natural' Mark Drummond. [Doc Watson]: Drummond? Hmmmm, that sounds familiar. [Malone]: Just watch and learn Doc. Watch and learn. (Malone points to the monitor between them.) _______________________________________________________________ (Camera shot opens on Bebe Cake holding a mic in her slender hand. Bebe is wearing a silver lowcut summer dress that is rather revealing. She turns to the large man standing next to her.) [Bebe Cake]: Why did you come to the HIWF? [Natural]: I've come to this federation to do 3 simple things. Win the World Title, win the TV Title and win the Tag Team Championship. I want to win all three titles and show everybody that the HIWF is just another league of hasbeens like all the other federations!!! [Cake]: Don't you think you're too confident, after all, you've never wrestled in a match yet in the HIWF? [Natural]: Me!!! Too confident!!! You gotta be kiddin' because when "The Natural" says something, he delivers. You just have to wait and see!!! [Cake]: Many people say that you and your brother have a little conflict, can you elaborate more on this subject? [Natural]: I don't know who is starting those stupid rumors!!! If I ever catch the idiot who's starting those rumors, I'll make him regret and eat his own words!!! The truth is that my brother and me respect each other a lot, there is no conflict at all between us!!! I'll say what I've said for about a million times, everyone has their own path to follow in life, and that's the case with me and Tommy. I won't get in his way and he won't get in my way, so all these bogus rumors, you know where you can stick it!!! [Cake]: Can you tell me something about your manager? [Natural]: "Pretty Boy" is surely the best manager of all time. Hell!!! He's been in this sport forever!!! 30 Years, 10 as a wrestler and 20 as a manager. Fans don't like him, but who cares about those stupid ingrate fans???!!! Bobby is a great communicator and wrestling technician, he also knows how to get under the skin of opponents and referees!!! I'm sure that me and the Pretty One are gonna soon rule the HIWF!!! And if the fans don't like it, I don't care!!! ("Pretty Boy" Bobby Lawrence arrives in the lockeroom.) [Cake]: I believe you have something to say Mr. Lawrence? [Pretty Boy]: My boys, Philippe and Antoine are very upset by the bad officiating they witnessed, that I witnessed and that everybody witnessed last Wednesday night!!! So I want to make this open challenge to you, Grave Digger and Lazarus. A special tag team match at July 27the Summer Slam Jam!!! I say special because of this stipulation I would like to propose, if at Summer Slam Jam, one of you two have the TV Belt, the TV Belt will be on the line during this match. The wrestler who will pin the other will go with the TV Title. I want revenge and my boys want revenge so at the next Saturday Slam Jam, we'll be waiting for an answer. [Cake]: Can you talk about your wrestler, "The Natural" Mark Drummond? [Cake]: What do you mean talk about him, little girl, I won't just talk about him, I'll say just how great he is!!! This man has it all, the looks, the brains, the brawns and I can promise you this, "The Natural" will soon have TV belt, the World Title belt and the Tag Team belts around his waist and I'll be the first to witness it!!! [Cake]: Thank you for your time, Mr. Lawrence and "Natural" Mark Drummond!!! [Natural]: You don't have to thank us!!! You're just lucky we've accepted to answer your questions!!! (He starts to laugh) (They both leave laughing and taping each other on the back.) [Cake]: Back to you Jerry and Doc! ________________________________________________________________ [Malone]: Thanks, Bebe! Seems like 'Pretty Boy' Lawrence is developing quite a stable. [Doc Watson]: He sure is. And he doesn't waste any time either! Already issuing a tag team challenge to Grave Digger and Lazarus. I'd have to side with The French Connection on that one, seeing as how neither Grave Digger or Lazarus has much tag team experience. (Doc picks up a blank videotape laying on the desk.) [Doc Watson]: Hey Jerry, what's this? [Malone]: I have no idea. I thought it was another one of your disgusting porno videos. [Doc Watson]: Nope. Those are all back in my dressing room. Lets see what's on it. (Doc puts the tape in the desktop VCR and presses play.) __________________________________________________________ (A shot of a misty shore is shown. All that can be heard is the lapping of water against the shore. A thin outline of a boat can be seen approaching the shore. As the boat gets closer, two men can be seen inside. The boat breaks to the shore, and the two men get out. Both are HUGE men, with black and blond hair to their shoulders. Both men are wearing full Viking battle garb, and stone warhammers are slung across their backs) [Rend]: New virgin land, my brother! (Rend takes the warhammer and slams it deep into the ground. Both men then let loose a Viking war cry!) [Rend]: This lands cries out for blood, my brother! [Maim]: So do we! This land, this HIWF, is ripe for the plucking, and by the Gods, we will plunder!!!! [Rend]: HIWF, you are looking at the Stormreavers! We have been sent by ODIN himself, given the strength of THOR, and been overwhelmed by our bloodlust! [Maim]: Mayhaps in this fed we can find worthy opponents! Those teams in XWC and BACW could not handle our fury! Maybe this league can give us foes to defeat! [Rend]: But nothing is wrong in defeat! L.A. Mafia, Mayberry RFD...there is no shame is losing! We will defeat you and sing dirges in your honor..if you are not young whelps afraid to face us! [Maim]: HIWF, a storm is raging, and Valhalla help those who get in its way! The Stormreavers crave battle and glory, and only championship will slake our thirst! (Rend takes his warhammer and slings it over his back. Both men let loose the battle cry again and walk off into the mist) __________________________________________________________ [Malone]: Looks like the Tag-Team scene is starting to heat up here in the HIWF! [Doc Watson]: 'Bout time too. I was really dreading seeing Mayberry RFD week after week after week.... [Malone]: Ok, I get the point Doc. This weekend on Saturday Slam Jam we will see the final round of the Tag Team Title Tournament when L.A. Mafia face off against Hate Machine. Right now here are some pre-recorded comments from Damian and Lucifer of Hate Machine. ____________________________________________________________ (Picasso stands in the middle of the ring. Wearing his Mean Gene bald cap, mustache and jacket.) [Picasso:] Ladies and gentlemen please welcome Hate Machine! (The crowd goes wild as two guys pretending to be Damian and Lucifer come out.) [Picasso:] Welcome gentlemen... [Fake Lucifer:] Scheme Gene! Wooooooo! [Fake Damian:] You Scheme Gene...you've known us...for long time.... [Picasso:] About 5 minutes to be exact. [Fake Damian:] Every week we come out here and put on a great show for all the great fans out there. We wanted to give back to the fans. As of right now, all you listening will each receive $500 each! [Picasso:] Wow! [Fake Lucifer:] And as part of our giving back, we want to screen 'Men In Black' right now on that video wall, hit the lights Paul! (The arena lights go out the video begins to play Men In Black. All of a sudden the sound of struggling and fighting is heard coming from the ring. The arena lights come back on to show that the impostors are out cold with Damian (In his Shawn Michaels Outfit) and Lucifer (In his Stone Cold Steve Austin bald cap and suit).) [Lucifer:] Listen here sons! You can't come out here and claim you're us without getting your asses whipped. [Damian:] You see, just as my good friend Vinny Mac found out. You can't replace talent with someone who looks the same. [Lucifer:] Shut the hell up Boy-Toy. You say one more word and I'll open another can of whoopass and kick your ass. [Picasso:] Gentlemen please a little diplomacy. [Damian:] You know Scheme Gene I am sick and tired of hearing this horse's ass talking about kickin' people's asses. [Lucifer:] Shut your ass up, before I whoop your ass. [Picasso:] Please, please gentlemen. What about your tag team match up this weekend against the L.A. Mafia for the HIWF World Tag Team Titles? [Damian:] Gene, I'm going to win those titles and then find myself a partner who wrestles better than he talks. (Lucifer and Damian begin to go at as the screen goes black.) ___________________________________________________________ [Doc Watson]: (waving his arms in the air) Oooh! Oooh! Pick me, Damian! Pick me! I'd be a great Tag Team Champion. And I should know. And you know why I should know? [Malone]: (looks at Doc in disbelief) Again? Everyone knows this by heart, Doc! Everyone? (Jerry turns to the studio.) [Off-camera Voices]: 10 time World Heavyweight Champion. Blah, blah, blah! [Doc Watson]: (smiling) Jealousy does not become you, my friend! [Malone]: Ha! Yeah, right! Man, you are so full of yourself, aren't you? [Doc Watson]: Just like our next interview with 'Mr. Universe' Tommy Drummond. ______________________________________________________________ (The camera shot is live inside a gym in Dallas, Texas where "Mr. Universe" Tommy Drummond is training for his next match in the World Title Tournament against Outlaw Justice.) [Mr. Universe]: (working on a stationary bike) Last Saturday, I showed to all those humanoid fans at the Pond in Anaheim just what "Mr. Universe" was all about!!! "Snow Man" Jeff Koch was the first to witness what "Mr. Universe" was all about and Outlaw Justice you're next in line, I'm gonna humiliate you just like I humiliated Jeff Koch. You know why??? Because nobody is stronger or smarter than me. With a no DQ and no countout tournament which I adore, those other so called stars are in for the beating of their lives against "Mr. Universe". And all you fans, broadcasters and wrestlers are gonna have to show some respect to the next HIWF World Champion!!! As far as you're concerned, Outlaw Justice, I don't think you're good enough to beat me, but if you think you can beat me, don't say it!!! Bring it!!! If you think you can!!! ____________________________________________________________ [Doc Watson]: Yes! Finally a wrestler who's not afraid of speaking his mind! Hey, what a great name for a show! <snicker> [Malone]: Well, I think 'Mr. Universe' will be in for a shock this weekend. Outlaw Justice is no creampuff. Plus, he has Maddog Gnort in his corner. [Doc Watson]: Maybe, Jerry. But Maddog has problems of his own to think about. For example, live on remote is the delicious Bebe Cake with The Outlaws. (the monitor directly behind Doc Watson comes on.) ____________________________________________________________ (Scene opens with Mack 5-0 slapping around a ring announcer, Downtown G jaw-jacking with some fans, The Mafia gettin some phone numbers from some females in the front row, and Jermain Asad standing in the ring) [Bebe Cake]: Please guys, lets get this interview under way. [Mack]: You shut your mouth, and pray to God we don't whip your ass right here right now. You're a prop for this interview so keep your mouth shut. (Bebe Cake sticks out her tongue at Mack.) [Mack]: Anyways, lets get to business. Its been a great week for the Outlaws. Last Saturday Downtown G moved on to the semi-finals in the World Tittle Tournament, The L.A. Mafia took those two French immigrants to the Extreme and are now two days away from becoming the new Tag Team Champs, and of course most recently Jermain Asad whipped some loser's ass all around the ring and is now headed to become the new TV champ, What a week. [Downtown]: Maddog Gnort, you FREAK, this Saturday your ass gets whipped and then, my Destiny, the World Title, and as for Outlaw Justice me and you almost had a go at it back in the NEWA, you claim you're gonna whip my Outlaw wannabe ass, sh!t I'd like to see you try, you ain't nothing but a washed up piece of trash. The 3-Way Dance will show that when I beat your ass all around the arena and walk out with the World Title. [Jason]: This Saturday me and Jimmy take on the Hate Machine for the Tag Team Championship. Well, Hate machine your looking at the real Maddogs of War, who will beat your asses all around the place, cause we can. [Asad]: MYSTERIO, right now me and the rest of the Outlaws Challenge your ass to a 4-On-4 Tag Team match, to a finish. Pick any 3 men you want, and get ready to R U M B L E! [Mack]: Outlaws 4 Life :-> [Jimmy]: Make room for the Maddogs of War!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! _____________________________________________________________ [Malone]: Wow! Quite a challenge issued there from Asad! A 4-0n-4 Tag Team match! [Doc Watson]: Yeah, I've done that before. But not in there ring. See, there were these triples I met in Las Vegas once and..... [Malone]: (holds up his hand) Stop right there, Doc! I don't want to hear it! [Doc Watson]: Sorry, I forgot. Don't want to offend your virgin ears. And virgin everything else, too! Ha! [Malone]: That's not true! [Doc Watson]: Ok, ok. But you probably last as long as this next interview here. (Doc touches a desktop button and the next interview starts to play on the monitor behind Jerry.) _______________________________________________________________ (Jesse Ewiak is in jeans and a t-shirt.) [Jesse Ewiak]: My name is Jesse "All American" Ewiak and I'm here to tell you one thing... I'm here to fight for the USA and it's fans. OVER AND OUT!!!!! ________________________________________________________________ [Malone]: Gee, that was kind of short, wasn't it? [Doc Watson]: And you would know! Right, 'Pee-Wee'? Ha! [Malone]:(pouting) Why do you tease me so? You don't have to be so mean. [Doc Watson]: Well, maybe you shouldn't watch this next segment then. Don't want you getting too excited you know. _________________________________________________________________ (Grave Digger and Candi are jogging down a beach path. Grave Digger is sweating heavily in his sweat suit while Candi's breasts bounce moderately in her skin tight two piece work out fit. ) [Grave Digger:] French Connection, you witnessed the resolve that I and Lazarus have. The Old World Order may no longer exist or thrive, but surviving members still hold that unbreakable bond of trust, and honor... [Candi:] Antoine, don't take what we did to you and your friend a personal matter, because if was personal, you would be in the hospital right now...(she takes a couple of quick deep breathes) (They jog pass a couple of teenage boys, all three boys do a double take of Candi's body.) [Grave Digger:] Now I move one step closer to my goal of ultimate dominance. The cycle has begun, the cycle of mindless violence has started it's path of destruction. I don't take pleasure in maiming and injuring the innocent, but if I must to achieve my goal, then I will not hesitate to do so..... (Suddenly the camera shot becomes staticy and a new scene takes its place.) (Chameleon stands on the edge of a cliff looking down a beach and a jogging path. The camera pans down to show a camera man run along side Grave Digger and Candi. Chameleon whispers as he talks to himself.) [Chameleon:] Hello old friend.......enjoy your time in the sun........ you hold something.....that is near and dear to me......we've had our differences in the past...but this goes beyond all that............. (Chameleon looks behind him.) [Chameleon]: Lazarus......your time has come and gone......you will never achieve the same success as have in the past...as long as I am here you will never see gold again..... (Chameleon looks back down on the beach as he watches Grave Digger and Candi take a rest on a bench.) [Chameleon]: Your time will come....old friend......I shall get her back...... ________________________________________________________ [Doc Watson]: Man, that Chameleon is something else. Always thinking, always scheming. Brings a tear to my eye it does. (Doc wipes away a small tear from his left eye.) [Malone]: You're a sick, twisted man Doc. You need serious help. [Doc Watson]: Whatever. You think I need help? Then you obviously aren't too familiar with the antics of this man here who's sure to be involved in this weekends "Triple Threat Elimination Match". __________________________________________________________ (Camera shot opens on Fergus McTavish, The Whirling Scottish Devil working out in a gym. Fergus is bench pressing a huge log as other members of the gym look on in disbelief. He seems to be counting sporadically.) [Fergus]: ...18, (press) 37, (press) 7, (press) 72, (press)... (Fergus notices the Camera-Guy nearby and motions for him to come closer.) [Fergus]: Ya know, (press, '61') I'm just gettin' ta enjoy meself 'ere in HIWF. 'Cept fer one ting (press, '22'). Some stupid little git is pissin' in my wind (press, '8'). (Fergus drops the log nonchalantly, not caring as it rolls over the feet of a nearby gym member who screams out in pain.) [Fergus]: Bucky Jones, I'm talkin' 'bout you. Ya tink ya kin mess wif da Whirling Scottish Devil? Well I'll make sure ya tink twice before ya enters da ring wif me! (Fergus leans over and grabs his water bottle except it is filled with Johnny Walker Scotch. He takes a big swig.) [Fergus]:An one more ting. Ya talks too much Bucky. Ya must likes da sound a yer voice cause I don't tink anyone else does. So ya gots a new attitude. So ya gots some desire. So ya gots some direction fer yer career. (Fergus looks right in the camera) [Fergus]: Big f@#kin' deal. (Fergus stands up and grabs the sides of the camera lens, drawing it close to his face.) [Fergus]: Bucky, gets yerself ready ta tangle wif The Whirling Scottish Devil! I'll tear ya a new one I will! Oh, and one last ting.... (Fergus jumps up on the bench, turns his back to the camera and lifts up his kilt, mooning the camera-guy) [Fergus]: (over his shoulder) Gets ready for The Whirling Scottish Devil! ITS NOT OATMEAL!!!! (camera fade, much laughing and hooting can still be heard) _______________________________________________________________ [Malone]: I stand corrected, Doc. You don't need help. That guy does. [Doc Watson]: Apology accepted. That butt-munch Bucky Jones will be talking out of the other side of his face when Fergus is done with him. [Malone]: You're probably right. Bucky Jones has annoyed a lot of people as of late, including this man here. _________________________________________________________________ (Mysterio and Stephanie are laying on some massage tables getting an all-over massage.) [Stephanie:] Oh this feels so goooood...... [Mysterio:] I can't believe it! I just can't believe it. [Stephanie:] What's the matter dear? [Mysterio:] I can't believe the call that ref made on Wednesday. I'm mean come on, that was a fast ten count, and that s.o.b. Bucky Jones interfering in MY match! [Stephanie:] I don't what's come over him lately, he seems to have changed somehow. [Mysterio:] Well this Saturday I'm going to have something special planned for my fans, I will show everyone that I am what I say I am, Mr.High Impact..... [Stephanie:] I can attest to that honey, You're Mr. High Impact in and out of the ring...(a smirk comes across her face) [Mysterio:] Well this Saturday some fortunate soul will get lesson in wrestling, from the greatest high impact wrestler in the business..... (The scene fades as the two continue to get massage.) ________________________________________________________ [Doc Watson]: Ooooooweeee! I can wait to see what Mysterio has in store for Bucky! [Malone]: Well whatever it is, I'm sure its not very sportsmanlike! [Doc Watson]: See, Jerry, that's the difference between you and I. You live by the rules. I don't. And that explains why your life sucks and mine is one big party. (Blows a couple of smoke rings.) [Malone]: I'm glad I don't live your life. I like things plain, simple and wholesome. Much like the following gentlemen. ________________________________________________________________ (Andy and Floyd are sitting in the barber shop. Floyd is giving Andy a hair cut. Of course the scene is in black and white; because everyone knows the color episodes sucked.) [Floyd]: Y....you...you...An...dy, I was watchin' that HIWF show on Tuesday...and there was a couple fellas' on there sayin' some mean stuff about us and the HIWF......... [Andy]: Well you know Floyd...a lot of fellar's talk a lot about being ruff'est and the tuff'est....fellar's all around....... (Barney walks into the barber shop holding what looks to be newspaper) [Barney]: Hey Floyd...hey Ang...did you see this in the paper? It says that the HIWF will be Boston this Saturday....I was thinkin' we go up there and check out some of the competition...... [Andy]: You know Barn that's not a bad idea....... [Floyd]: Ye.....yea.....yeah and we could visit the dancing girls in Mt. Pilot on our way up...... [Andy]:Okay then...we're goin' to Boston to see the H-EYE-Double U- EFF. __________________________________________________________ [Doc Watson]: EE-EYE-EE-AYE-OH! Buncha hicks! [Malone]: Must you mock everyone in this league? There are some truly talented individuals here in the HIWF and yet you choose to mock them all. [Doc Watson]: No, not all of them, Jerry. Just the ones that deserve it. Which is, well, all of them ! Ha! (Blows some more smoke rings) [Malone]: So for the arrogant Doc Watson and the lovely Bebe Cake, I'm Jerry Malone. Remember to tune us in next week when we'll have the results from the World Title Tournament Finals. [Doc Watson]: Keep it cranked to the nines, baby! Who loves ya?!?
