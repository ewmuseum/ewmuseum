HIWF (Jun 1997) — Roleplay

S.Y.M.: Speaking Your Mind, 18 July 1997

18 July 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The bigger than life symbol of HIWF and two wrestler's pummeling each other senseless crashes onto your screen. Underneath it black bold faced letters appear... "SPEAKING YOUR MIND". Blaring out your speakers is 'Killer' by Adamski. To hear it in MIDI format, download it here at http://www.globalserve.net/~cracker/killer.mid (36KB). Then the screen goes black and nice looking white letters appear on your screen. PARENTAL GUIDANCE WARNING, THE FOLLOWING PROGRAM WILL CONTAIN LANGUAGE OF A COARSE NATURE. PLEASE BE ADVISED. YADDA YADDA YADDA. The camera shot opens on Doc Watson and Jerry Malone seated at the Control Center desk. Doc is dressed in Bermuda shorts and a very loud Hawaiian shirt. Jerry is wearing his HIWF X-Treme blazer. Jerry looks at Doc in disgust. [Malone]: Um, Doc, I don't think what you're wearing is appropriate. This is a serious journalistic program you know. [Doc Watson]: (flips Jerry a quarter) Here. Go call someone who gives a sh!t. [Malone]: (gasps) Watch your mouth! At least tell me why you're dressed that way. [Doc Watson]: Well, if you must know, right after the show I'm heading straight to California to visit my newly purchased home. [Malone]: Another one? How much did you spend this time? [Doc Watson]: Only 2.6 million! It was a steal at this celebrity auction. Used to belong to a former sports celebrity. [Malone]: You don't mean....? [Doc Watson]: That's right, amigo! I just bought myself O.J. Simpson's mansion! I'm gonna practice my golf swing in the backyard. Wanna join me? [Malone]: Not on your life! You're a sick, twisted man Doc. Imagine! Buying a murderer's house! [Doc Watson]: Hey, he was acquitted! You know, if you play your cards right, I might introduce you to my new pool boy, Kato Kaelin! Speaking of cards, lets talk to the latest addition to 'Pretty Boy' Lawrence's stable. _______________________________________________________________________ (We are now joined by a newcomer in the HIWF, "The Card Shark" Randy Brandenberg with his manager, "Pretty Boy" Bobby Lawrence.) [Pretty Boy]: HIWF!!! Get ready!!! Because "The Card Shark" is comin' to town!!! [Card Shark]: That's right!!! And when "The Card Shark" plays his cards well, he never loses. Chumps like Bucky Jones, "Ice Man" Jeff Koch and every other chump are gonna know what I mean. [Pretty Boy]: This man is a very accomplished wrestler, a 20 year veteran and one of the richest man in Las Vegas!!! So, all you stupid fans are gonna have to show him some respect because money and power are synonyms!!! [Card Shark]: If I wanted, I could buy the rights for all those titles, but I wont do any blackmail!!! I'm gonna prove instead that money, wrestling ability and championships are related words for me!!! And if you don't believe me, you just have to step in the ring with me so I can make you change your mind!!! With the best manager in the world, it makes my job much more easier. [Pretty Boy]: My stable is the most talented in professional wrestling and this new addition makes it even more better. [Card Shark]: I would like to make a little statement, when I'll put "My Full House" on you, you're gonna have to say that "The Card Shark" is also "The Wrestling Shark"!!! (He starts to laugh) (They both leave laughing and having a good time.) ________________________________________________________________________ [Malone]: Well I sure hope 'Pretty Boy' Lawrence comes up with a name for his stable soon. Its going to be real hard just referring to his wrestlers as 'Those guys with Pretty Boy'. [Doc Watson]: Hey, what's in a name? I mean, its all relative isn't it? You're a simple, boring guy and so you have a simple, boring name. Now myself on the other hand.... [Malone]: Ok, Doc, I think we get the point. You know, any more of your ramblings and we'll have to extend this show to 2 hours. [Doc Watson]: Fine by me, chum! All the more for the ladies to see my smiling face! Hey Bebe Cake, you like seeing my smiling face, don't ya? ________________________________________________________________________ (The monitor behind Doc comes on and we see the gorgeous Bebe Cake. This week she is wearing a pink tank top that hugs her breasts and a pair of white hot pants that hugs her buns.) [Cake]: You know it, Doc! Oh, hi Jerry! Anyway, here we go, its interview time! Ladies and Gentlemen....Jermain Asad!!!!!! (C.R.E.A.M. by Wu- Tang Clan plays in the arena) [Cake]: Asad you requested this interview time....go ahead. [Asad]: Thank you, now down to business. The reason this past Tuesday I was not with my fellow Outlaws is cause I was at a celebration party, a party for a very, very close friend of mine. This friend was having this party because of a Huge victory this past Sunday. Sure the media will say that his team lost, but it was only a technicality. If by now you don't know who I'm talking about I'll let you in on it. It was Dennis Rodman. [Cake]: Dennis Rodman! You're friends with the Worm? [Asad]: I'm a big man in Hollywood, I know all the stars. Hell, Arnold wants me in his next movie, but I think I'll leave it to Hollywood, another close friend of mine. [Cake]: WOW!! I didn't know you were so popular. [Asad]: Well you know, not to brag, but I was invited to be a member of the NWO, but I rejected it 'cause I feel the OUTLAWS are alot better. [Cake]: You mean you choose the HIWF over WCW?? [Asad]: Yes, I did. [Cake]: Wow, that's unbelievable! But what about Rodman. [Asad]: That's just what I was getting too. Me and The Real Hot Rod has a few beers, we had a few laughs, got some ass, and other things. [Cake]: (laughs) Are you saying you slept with Dennis Rodman? [Asad]: That's were rumors get started, when idiots like you hear things wrong! I am not gay, neither is he, to my knowledge, we got some female ass. [Cake]: Ok, that's better. [Asad]: Anyways shut the hell up, and let me speak. Dennis Rodman gave me a personal promise, that this coming Saturday Night at SNSJ he would be there, with me, and he will get some things of his chest. Maybe even beat a few people up. [Cake]: Wait a minute, are you saying Dennis Rodman is joining the OUTLAWS?? [Asad]: I guess you can say that, but I will not say a word and neither will he, until this Saturday when we drop the bomb. [Cake]: WOW!!! Thank you for your time. [Asad]: Its been your pleasure. Rodman, Asad, Mack, Mafia, Downtown its all 4 Life!! [Cake]: So with that shocking news, this is Bebe Cake. Back to you Doc and Jerry. ________________________________________________________________________ [Doc Watson]: Thanks, Bebe! See you after the show, right? [Cake]: You got it Doc! I can't wait to see O.J.'s old house! [Doc Watson]: Yeah, but I hear the best part is the bedroom! (Doc winks at the camera) Later, Bebe! [Cake]: Bye, Doc! Oh, bye Jerry! [Doc Watson]: Right now, I'm going to introduce a new segment to S.Y.M. Its called 'Talk With The Doc', an informal interview segment hosted by yours truly! Take a break, Jerry! [Malone]: (huffs) If I must! (Doc gets up and walks over to a set to the left of the HIWF Control Center. The set is done up to resemble a bar lounge, with highbacked red leather chairs, a long oak bar, and a jazz combo in the corner. A pink neon sign on the back wall reads 'Talk With The Doc'. The jazz combo is playing 'Only Wanna Be With You' by Hootie and The Blowfish.) [Doc Watson]: (lights up a Cohiba cigar) Once in awhile, when I actually feel like it, I'll conduct an interview with one of the superstars of the HIWF. To start this new segment, I figured why not speak with a man who is single handedly changing the face of wrestling as we know it. Of course, I'm talking about The Acer! And with The Acer is his lovely companion, Miss Gray! (The jazz combo starts playing 'The Stars and Stripes Forever') (Acer struts in and is wearing his usual all black tux with Ray Ban sunglasses. Miss Gray is dressed in casual wear, green shirt and green shorts. Miss Gray and Acer both come in waving American Flags.) [Doc Watson]: (hands Acer a Cohiba cigar) Acer, you're facing Jessie "All American" Ewiak this Saturday on Saturday Night Slam Jam! You beat Mysterio's ass already so what are your thoughts on this match? [The Acer]: Doc, let me tell you....I'll whip his sorry tail. You talk of "All American"? Man did you see that entrance Miss Gray and I had on the Fifth of July's Slam Jam??? How much have we heard from this "All American" punk? The shortest interview in the history of the HIWF!! If he wrestles like he interviews...then it's win number two for The Acer!! I called my Mom and she sent me a special apple pie and I'm going to really enjoy it. Miss Gray has a special way of enjoying apple pie and....hehehehehehehehe!!!! Well...it's not suitable for the TV audience!! [Miss Gray]: Your Mom sent two apple pies.....are we going for a double in one night? [Acer]: Nah, I've got a better idea. Besides... after we celebrate our win Saturday....it's whip cream night!!!! Oh yeah...speaking of All American...my Mom told me to quote "kick that Jessie "All American" Ewiak's ass!!" [Doc Watson]: Reminds me of Jerry's Mom! Ha! Acer, how do you react to that goober Bucky Jones unexpected and surprising help in your last match against Mysterio? [Acer]: Hmmmm...speaking of Mothers....Bucky Jones hates Mysterio more than me....I benefited from that...and as EVERYONE SAW on this Wednesday....I tried to help that poor loser against Outlaw Justice. Well, you saw what happened. Bucky is, was and always will be a total LOSER! And you can take that to the bank. He will be ACED! [Miss Gray]: That kiss I blew to Bucky probably will be the only kiss he ever gets from a woman..other than his Mother!!!! (Both Acer and Miss Gray totally lose it laughing and almost fall out of their chairs!!!!) [Doc Watson]: You know, its really good to be around those who are close to you. So you must be really pumped to have you best friends, The Nobles, return to the ring in the HIWF. Do you feel that their status as former world champs will overshadow you? [Acer]: No WAY!!! If anything...I'm glad I was able to drag them away from Hawaii...having former world champs... (Miss Gray interrupts.) [Miss Gray]: That's WORLD with capital W. Acer and I guided them to the top! [Acer]: It was your talent, my dear. I was just a supporting role. Having Jack Diamond and The King With A Club back in action is awesome....hahahahahaha I LOVE that bit with the shovels they did against LA Mafia. The Nobles FEAR NO ONE! If anything, their first ever appearance in the HIWF should show everyone how serious they are. Also...you will see a new King. I won't say anymore. [Doc Watson]: Speaking of The Nobles....what's all this talk about a NEW STABLE??? Come on, share with Ole Doc here! [Acer]: Well, Jack and King let the cat out of the bag a bit early... [Miss Gray]: MEOW! [Acer]: Um, that's when we get back to the hotel room....but those gentleman Jack and King mentioned are top candidates for the stable..... they may or may not enter our family. [Miss Gray]: Tell Doc the new name...tell Doc the new name... [Acer]: Of course, my lady! Attitude Adjuster, myself and The Nobles shall now be know as.....Doc can we have a drum roll? (Doc points to the jazz combo and a quick drum roll starts.) [Acer]: THE MEN WITH ATTITUDES! That's all I'll say on that! [Doc Watson]: Excellent! So what are your goals here in the HIWF, Acer? [Acer]: TOTAL DOMINATION! Period. Tag belts for The Nobles, TV belt for me....for now...and Attitude the World belt. Running over anyone who gets in OUR way and dealing with anyone who sticks their nose in our business. Just like The Nobles did with LA Mafia. Nuff Said. Thanks for having us on 'Talk With The Doc', Doc! [Miss Gray]: Doc, don't you ever change! [Doc Watson]: Don't worry baby, nothing can change the spots on this leopard! So in the spirit of keeping things rolling, lets go to this pre-recorded interview with L.A. Mafia. ________________________________________________________________________ (Camera shot opens on L.A. Mafia with Bebe Cake in the locker room after Wednesday Night Reckoning.) [Mack]: Get that f'n camera out of my face this ain't the time. [Cake]: But you guys won two out of three matches! [Mack]: Yeah, that ain't the point. Listen I ain't gonna tell you again, you f#@ken idiots think that just cause you have a camera and a microphone you can go up to anybody and start askin' them questions. That's what's wrong with this country. [Cake]: Don't get Bret Hart on us. [Mack]: You're really pushing your luck. [Cake]: What's the problem? [Mack]: You want to know the problem, I'LL TELL YOU THE F'N PROBLEM!!!! First Downtown G leaves the arena right after the match, I still have not had a chance to speak with him since last Saturday, I don't know what's going on. He seems to be keeping to himself, that's not good when your talking about Downtown G. He can lose it at the drop of a hat, he's crazy like that. I don't know what's going on, and that's no good for me or anybody in the HIWF. Lets just hope he doesn't do anything to CRAZY. [Cake]: What about the L.A. Mafia? (Jimmy and Jason walk in) [Jason]: Ok, ok let me just say Nobles you have no business in any of our matches. We finished your asses of back in the NEWA, ain't no way we are gonna give you a shot here. [Jimmy]: Nobles, you big fat ugly lookin' motherf#@kers, you can interfere our matches all you want, but the bottom line is, ain't no way you guys gettin' a title shot, that's it, no more on the subject. [Cake]: Ok, Mafia maybe you can give us a word on this Saturday, the Survivor Series match? [Jimmy]: Oh, yeah I almost forgot about that. Well, Mysterio this Saturday you and your three little masked friends will get a wrestling lesson courtesy of the Outlaws. [Jason]: You know Jimmy, from what I hear the reason they are masked is 'cause they are illegal immigrants from Mexico and if the border patrol see's them its beating time. [Cake]: You guys make me sick. [Jason]: Its not our fault society is a freak show! Its not our fault that there are so many bums in this so called "Great" place we live in, it sure as hell ain't our fault that illegal immigrants are hopping the border almost every ten seconds. If it was up to me I'd put an electric fence! [Cake]: Some very political comments there by Jason and Jimmy the Outlaws! [Jason]: It's 4 LIFE! Don't forget it. ________________________________________________________________________ [Doc Watson]: And buy the shirt! [Malone]: An electric fence? That's a terrible suggestion! [Doc Watson]: What the hell are you talking about? I think that's a great idea! In fact, that's going to be the first thing I get installed at CASA De O.J.! [Malone]: You are a horrid man, a really horrid man. [Doc Watson]: Yeah, admit it, you're just mad you didn't think of it first. You wanna talk horrid, take a look at this next interview here. The fact that the HIWF lets this man wrestle at all is beyond me. Anyway, here's that dink, Bucky Jones. ________________________________________________________________________ (The scene fades in on an empty high school gym. Practice is over for today, but Bucky Jones is still there. He's busy packing up the mats and putting them back into the supply closet. Finally, he turns to the camera.) [Bucky]: Acer, you've got alot of nerve interfering in my match against Outlaw Justice! I had him right where I wanted him... I was just about to put him away when you interfered and messed up my concentration! And then the entire match went downhill for me... I don't know what you hoped to accomplish... I don't know what you hoped to prove... After the powerbomb I got from Outlaw, I don't even know exactly how to spell my name... But, all you did was make me mad... Madder than I have EVER been! You say that, soon, I'm gonna get Aced? Well, you wannabe superstar, you best watch your back or you are gonna get Buckied! You can count on it! (Scene fades) ________________________________________________________________________ [Doc Watson]: (laughing) Buckied? Is that supposed to scare us? Oh, man, I tell you, the only thing lamer than being Buckied is....being Jerried! [Malone]: Real funny, Doc! Don't quit your day job! But I'm not going to get into it with you, we've still got a few more interviews to go. Once again, we go live with Bebe Cake. Here she is with The French Connection and their manager 'Pretty Boy' Lawrence. ________________________________________________________________________ (We are now in the lockeroom of The French Connection with their manager, "Pretty Boy" Bobby Lawrence.) [Cake]: This Saturday, your boys will take on Mayberry RFD, how are your boys going to approach their match? [Pretty Boy]: It's very simple, these Brutus Beefcake and Diesel wannabes think that they're good enough to beat us!!! Big deal!!! These two morons couldn't even beat their grandmother in an arm wrestling contest on their best day, and it's not gonna be different with us!!! [Philippe]: That's right master, these two WWF wannabes are no match for the best technical wrestler in the world!!! And your stupid fans are not gonna be able to save you, because mon ami Antoine et moi are gonna beat the hell out of you and humiliate you!!! [Cake]: Antoine!!! Do you have any comments regarding your opponents? [Antoine]: Yes I have comments!!! Big Andy Cool, Floyd "The Barber" Beefcake!!! Where do they come up with such stupid names. We'll make short work out of you and after that, Lazarus and Grave Digger, we're comin' after you!!! [Cake]: Thank you for your time, gentlemen!!! [Pretty Boy, Philippe and Antoine]: Mayberry RFD, we will annihilate you for good!!! (They leave the lockeroom) [Cake]: Well guys, I'm done. See you later! ________________________________________________________________________ [Malone]: Bye, Bebe! I think the French Connection has underestimated Mayberry RFD. I think their upcoming match is going to be a lot tougher than they think. [Doc Watson]: Gimme a break! Mayberry RFD shouldn't even be in the ring with someone of The French Connections caliber! And I should know! And do you know why I should know? [Malone]: (sarcastic tone) Gee, Doc, I don't know. Would it have anything to do with being a 10 time World Heavyweight Champion? [Doc Watson]: You know it, baby! Hey, ain't nothing wrong with stating the facts! And you know me, I don't lie! [Malone]: No, of course you don't. (rolls his eyes) Well, he we go with the final segment of the night, taped earlier today. ________________________________________________________________________ (Scene, Acer's office, Thursday 10am. In the office are Miss Gray, Cheryl, Attitude Adjuster, The King With A Club...all are checking their watches...because Jack Diamond has not show up yet.) [Acer]: Where in the hell is Diamond? (Acer glances at his watch again) He should be here. (Suddenly Sunny walks in with a concerned look on her face.) [Sunny]: Has anyone seen Jack? [Everyone Else]: (At the same time) WE THOUGHT HE WAS WITH YOU!!!!!! [Sunny]: Haven't seen him since last night! (The phone rings. Miss Gray picks it up. As she talks her facial expression turns to dread.) [Acer]: What is it, what's wrong? [Miss Gray]: That was the police from Southside Hospital. Jack was brought into the ER thirty minutes ago. He's suffered a concussion. The policeman was confused as well. [Acer]: How so? [Miss Gray]: The policeman said that Jack kept hitting on the women and going 'Whoooooooooo!' [King With A Club]: Oh dear Lord. [Acer]: What? What, King? [King With A Club]: While in Hawaii, Jack was really trying to imitate Ric Flair. That bump might have... [Sunny]: What, he thinks he's Flair now? [King With A Club]: More than likely. [Acer]: (Motions to everybody) Come on, let's go. (Miss Gray swipes the keys from Acer.) It's my turn to drive! [Miss Gray]: WE don't need another speeding ticket. [Acer]: I was only going 5 miles over.... [Cheryl, Attitude and Miss Gray]: NOT! [Cheryl]: I believe, 'I feel the need the need for speed!' was your exact quote before you floored it up to 120! [Acer]: So I like to drive a little fast (Acer shrugs). (The argument continues as the gang piles in the Viper. Miss Gray starts up the engine and Acer puts in Jimmy Buffett, Cheryl wants to hear Enya, however. As the car pulls out, Cheryl and Acer are really getting into it. Fade to black.) ________________________________________________________________________ [Doc Watson]: (laughing) Oh-oh! Looks like Jack is pulling a Hate Machine! He just don't know who he is! [Malone]: Well, our best wishes go out to him. So for Smilin' Doc Watson and the wonderful Bebe Cake this is Jerry..... (Suddenly Fergus McTavish, The Whirling Scottish Devil rushes onto the set.) [McTavish]: Oy, boyo's! Och, I'm late again ain't I? [Malone]: Actually, yes, once again you are. We're just wrapping things up. Try coming earlier, Mr. McTavish. [McTavish]: Och, 'Mister' is it? Doan try sweet talkin' me ya pansy! I just needs a sec! Last time was 'ere I insults dat blouse wearing, tofu eatin', tree huggin', sissy boy Jesse 'All American' Ewiak! An what do I hear back? Nothin'! Come on! Are ye a man or some wee lassie? So 'All American', are ya ready ta tangle wif The Whirling Scottish Devil? Cause I sure am! Oh, and one last ting.... (McTavish jumps up on the desk, turns his back to the camera and lifts up his kilt, mooning the camera.) [McTavish]: (over his shoulder) Gets ready for The Whirling Scottish Devil! ITS NOT OATMEAL!!!! (Once again Doc falls off his chair and is rolling on the floor, laughing hysterically.) [Malone]: Oh, hell we're going to be canceled for sure! This ends another episode of S.Y.M. and I'm Jerry Malone. See you next Tuesday. That is if we're still here.
