HIWF (Jun 1997) — Roleplay

S.Y.M.: Speaking Your Mind, 2 September 1997

2 September 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The bigger than life symbol of HIWF and two wrestler's pummeling each other senseless crashes onto your screen. Underneath it black bold faced letters appear... "SPEAKING YOUR MIND". Blaring out your speakers is 'BRAIN STEW' by Green Day. **To hear the theme music in MIDI format, download it here at http://www.globalserve.net/~cracker/brain-stew.mid (26KB).** Then the screen goes black and nice looking white letters appear on your screen. PARENTAL GUIDANCE WARNING, THE FOLLOWING PROGRAM WILL CONTAIN LANGUAGE OF A COARSE NATURE. PLEASE BE ADVISED. YADDA YADDA YADDA. ________________________________________________________________________ (Camera shot opens on Jerry Malone and Smilin' Doc Watson sitting at the HIWF Control Center. Malone is sitting on the right and is wearing a gold blazer with a HIWF logo stitched on the breast pocket. Smilin' Doc Watson is in seat to the left and he is wearing a black dress shirt and a matching black vest with a wild design in the fabric. Doc has a large grin on his face and as usual is smoking a MonteCristo cigar.) [Malone]: Greetings fans, and welcome to another exciting episode of S.Y.M! What an incredible Labour Day weekend we just had! On SNSJ, we saw the teaming up of two veterans in HIWF, Outlaw Justice and the deformed Lazarus! [Doc Watson]: Well, sorry I missed it. But, I was kind of busy. [Malone]: Oh. And what could be more important than SNSJ? [Doc Watson]: (laughs) Like you gotta ask? Well, knowing you I guess you do. I was practicing my ABC's. [Malone]: Right. Alcohol, Babes..... [Doc Watson]: And Cigars! So Jerry, where the hell is my "surprise"? Is that Larry Flynt wannabe [name withheld] coming down to the studio? [Malone]: No, but look back there. [Doc Watson]: Look where? [Malone]: Past the camera towards the back of the studio. (indicates with his index finger) [Doc Watson]: What, you gonna sic the.....oh, sh*t. (The camera turns around to show the back of the studio. Amongst the cables and lights stand two men dressed in Viking garb.) [Doc Watson]: Hey, you guys touch me again and I'll sue! [Malone]: They're not going to lay a hand on you, Doc. You see those warhammers they have next to them? If you say one more crude, rude, disgusting, distasteful, nasty thing about me, the wrestlers, or ANYONE in general, then those two are going to throw those warhammers at the screen behind us. And if they just happen to misthrow... [Doc Watson]: Hey, if they misthrow, they're just as likely to hit you, tube-baby! (An intern comes in and fits a Pittsburgh Steelers football helmet on Malone's head.) [Malone]: (smiling) If they miss, at least I'll live. [Doc Watson]: YOU....YOU....YOU DICK! (Doc suddenly realizes what he just said and looks back at The Stormreavers.) [Doc Watson]: Look, it was a slip of the tongue! A mistake! Hey, don't aim, I didn't say anything! (Doc dives under the desk, seconds before a warhammer hits the screen behind him.) [Malone]: (smiling) Close one, Doc! To start things off, we have some prerecorded comments from the two gentlemen that are keeping a close eye on Doc, The Stormreavers. (Malone looks under the desk.) [Malone]: You can't do the whole show from under the desk you know. [Doc Watson]: (from under the desk) Sure I can! Just watch me! ________________________________________________________________________ (The Stormreavers are shown sitting on a pair of rocks on a beach. The wind is whipping their long hair around as they lean on their stone warhammers) [Rend]: The Nobles? [Maim]: The toughest fight we have ever been in, and two of the finest warriors in the land. [Rend]: The French Connection? [Maim]: Two French weaklings who should spend more time sucking at their mother's teat! [Rend]: The LA Mafia? [Maim]: Children playing warrior's games. [Rend]: The Outlaws? [Maim]: I can think of no good thing to say about those two whelps. [Rend]: The Maddogs of War? [Maim]: Mad dogs should be put down, and it is not the province of fine warriors such as the Stormreavers to do such menial tasks. [Rend]: So, every team in HIWF has either fallen, been too frightened to challenge, or has walked away scarred after a meeting with the Stormreavers! Who does that leave? [Maim]: Only the Whirling Scottish Devils! McTavish Brothers, you dare call us hornheads? These helmets represent the finest warrior society this miserable rock has EVER seen! Granted, your kilts represent a fine lineage, but the fact of the matter is, you Scots have been living under the thumb of another for centuries, while we have carved our own destiny! [Rend]: Devils, be prepared to face two devils of a different kind... ________________________________________________________________________ [Malone]: It appears that The Stormreavers have taken offense to the McTavish brothers insulting comments last week and have decided to do something about it. [Doc Watson]: (from under the desk) Yeah, well those goatherders are messing with the wrong guys. The Devils will rip 'em a new one and then ask them if they want seconds! [Malone]: I'm not so sure about that. The Stormreavers are a proven tag team while the only thing the McTavish twins have proven is their insanity. [Doc Watson]: (from under the desk) ....Yep, they're here right now. Sure I can keep them here. Great! And hurry up! (A beep from a cell-phone hanging up can be heard.) [Malone]: Doc, who are you talking to down there? [Doc Watson]: (from under the desk) None of your f#@king business! [Malone]: Ooops! That's number two! (Suddenly a warhammer flies across the studio and hits the desk hard.) [Doc Watson]: (jumping out from under the desk) Aaaaaaah! Quit it you Viking nuts! I'll see your sorry asses in prison! Just you watch! [Malone]: (smiling) Speaking of prisons, let's get some comments from a former guest of the state, Bain ________________________________________________________________________ (Scene inside the locker room before anyone has shown up. Bain is sitting on a bench.) [Bain]: It's about time I get to kick someone's ass. "Crack baby" Jason or what ever the hell you call yourself, you're the son of a bitch who gets to fight me first and when that bell rings I'm going to tattoo your ugly face to the mat with the "Execution Style".......... Oh yeah, Jimmy I don't want you to think that I forgot about you, I'm gonna kick your ass one way or the other. Jason, the bottom line is come prepared to get your ass kicked or don't come at all!! (Bain stands up to walk away then turns around and says) [Bain]: I'll be waiting for you Jason. (Scene fades.) ________________________________________________________________________ [Doc Watson]: (watching The Stormreavers out of the corner of his eye) That was a rather nice interview coming from an upstanding young gentleman. Wouldn't you say so, Mr. Malone? [Malone]: (smiling) Geez, I should have these guys here every week. [Doc Watson]: (making a face but still keeping his cool) Why yes you should! The Stormreavers are excellent wrestlers and all around great guys......Awwww, who am I kidding?!? You suck and they suck! There, I said it! And in fact...... (Doc doesn't get a chance to finish as a warhammer hits him in the shoulder, knocking him out of his seat and onto the floor.) [Malone]: That's three! And a little slow that time, Doc! [Doc Watson]: (still on the floor moaning) I'll get you yet, Malone! You and your sheep-molesting friends! Just you wait! Ooooh, my head! [Malone]: And while you're picking yourself up off the floor, let's check out these comments from Mysterio. ________________________________________________________________________ (Mysterio is sitting in his Mercedes 600 driving down Beverly Blvd in Beverly Hills.) [Mysterio]: Candi my sweet. This Sunday will be your first foray into the world of greatness. It's not because you'll be laying in bed with your husband, but because you'll be riding the mystery train to ecstasy. (Mysterio turns the corner and heads down La Cienega to Hollywood.) [Mysterio]: Grave Digger, your precious little wife will be mine and you can count on that. Remember one thing, when you mess with 1 member of the Alliance, then you mess with them all. ________________________________________________________________________ [Malone]: (shudders) Ugh! Imagine all the depraved things he'll do to her! Just thinking about Mysterio having his way with the lovely Candi gives me the creeps! [Doc Watson]: Yeah, well your Mom didn't think it was so creepy when I suggested some depraved things to her! Maybe I'll give Mysterio a call for some tag-team action! Ha! (Doc flinches, expecting to get hit again but surprisingly, no warhammer is thrown.) [Malone]: (yelling to the back of the studio to The Stormreavers) Hey! I thought you were going to nail Doc for ANY disgusting comment he made! Especially about my Mother! (Rend and Maim look at each other and just shrug their shoulders.) [Doc Watson]: Ha! Got you there, Jerry-boy! (Doc blows a couple of smoke rings.) [Malone]: (pouting) Is everyone against my Mother? [Doc Watson]: (grinning) No, not everyone. There's a ring attendant in Omaha she hasn't met yet. But I'm sure she will soon. (Doc takes a draw on his cigar.) [Doc Watson]: On newsstands later this week is the latest edition of the HIWF Magazine. We gave an advance copy to Robbie "Hitt" Mann and here were his comments. ________________________________________________________________________ (Scene opens in Robbie "Hitt" Mann's home in Oakland. Robbie is sitting on the couch looking through the September issue of "HIWF Magazine"... On the cover is Robbie "HITT" Mann standing in the middle of the ring after causing Chameleon to lose his title. The headline reads "HIWF New Home of The Hitt Mann: Hope You Like Life in a COMA!") [Mann]: Well my first match in the HIWF ended in a win for me. I had my sights set on the Ewok but I guess Mysterio had to be the first to lose to the Hitt Mann, But don't worry cause you will not be the last. Ewok, if you still want to be one of the losers that get the chance to get beat by the best wrestler there ever was then go ahead and ask the Prez for a match and I will beat you like I beat him. But this Wednesday I will get another win after I put Damian in a COMA and make my count 2 and 0. Damian, I will try to take it easy on you. I wouldn't want to hurt you too bad, an Ex Tag-Team champ and all, so I will only put you in two COMA's, and when I'm done with you maybe I will start looking for Gold Waist Wear. So you title holders better shine up those belts cause one of you losers are gonna kiss it good bye. (He flips through the rest of the book till he gets to the posters and un-folds the "Hitt-Mann Rulz" Poster.....Scene Fades.) ________________________________________________________________________ [Doc Watson]: You know, I'm beginning to think that Ewok, I mean Ewiak, is MIA. Haven't heard anything from him in a while. [Malone]: Well you know, some guys don't need to brag to get the job done. [Doc Watson]: Job. Now that's one word that truly describes Ewiak. Job. You know, its been awhile since we had a resident jobber. And I think Ewiak is just the man to fill that position. Either him or that dink, Bucky Jones! (As expected, a warhammer flies across the studio. Doc quickly covers his face with his hands anticipating the blow. Suddenly, a hand flashes out across the screen and grabs the warhammer just inches from Doc's head.) [Doc Watson]: (uncovering his face, sees his savior) Yeee-haw! The cavalry has arrived! [Malone]: So that's who you called earlier on your cell-phone! I might have known! (Fergus McTavish and his twin brother Willie walk onto the set.) [Doc Watson]: Well its about bloody time! Where the hell were you? [Fergus McTavish]: (still holding the warhammer he grabbed) Och, shut yer hole, Doc! We gots stuck in traffic! Rush hour is a bitch, ain't dat right me brudder? [Willie McTavish]: Aye, dat's right me brudder! Now where are dose hammer tossing wussies? (The Stormreavers charge across the studio and attack The Whirling Scottish Devils.) [Doc Watson]: Yes! Here we go with a preview of this Saturday's matchup! Willie McTavish whips Rend into the desk. Rend hits Willie with a jumping side kick. They're brawling on the floor. Willie McTavish knocks Rend into the camera. Rend takes him down with a flying clothesline. [Malone]: Stop it! You're ruining the set! Quick, show the next segment! Show the next segment! ________________________________________________________________________ (Grave Digger is once again sitting at the bar drinking his worries away.) [Grave Digger]: Mysterio, this Saturday you will suffer the ultimate fate. Not only have you insulted me, but you have made advances towards my wife. (He takes a swig.) [Grave Digger]: In the old days, when a man even looked at another man's wife wrong, he was lynched that evening. Mysterio, this Saturday shall be the day that passes judgment on you for your actions. You may now have friends, but I can assure you that they will not be a factor in our match. You'll understand why on Saturday when I bury you. ________________________________________________________________________ [Doc Watson]: We're back but the action here continues! [Malone]: Good Lord! This is terrible! Fergus McTavish goes for a spinning elbowsmash, but Maim counters it with a duck-down move. Fergus McTavish goes for a back suplex, but Maim blocks it. Maim nails him with a facerake. Maim executes a Hotshot. Maim catches him in a flying legdrop. [Doc Watson]: Whoooo! This is fantastic! A first for SYM! Rend and Main use their warhammers on Fergus and Willie. Fergus and Willie are busted wide open. Rend and Maim hits them with a double clothesline. Rend and Maim takes them down with a gutwrench suplex. Rend and Maim executes a stomp to the midsection. [Malone]: Looks like your boys are in trouble, Doc! Fergus and Willie grab Jerry Malone. Fergus and Willie place Jerry Malone under their arms. Jerry Malone is screaming like a little girl. [Doc Watson]: Did you say something, Jerry? Fergus and Willie use Malone's helmeted head as a battering ram. Fergus and Willie chase Rend and Maim out of the studio. Fergus and Willie still have Jerry Malone under their arms. Jerry Malone is screaming like a little girl. [Doc Watson]: (waving) Bye bye, Malone! Don't forget to write! Ha! (Doc turns back to the camera.) [Doc Watson]: The HIWF continues to attract newcomers from across the globe. Let's go to a press conference earlier today where our camera crew caught these comments. ___________________________________________________________________ (Scene opens to a press conference. Trent Wallase walks the podium slowly followed by Vic in a suit. ) [Trent Wallase]: Now all you American athletes better take a long hard look at the future of professional wrestling. He is what you hope and dream to be when you get off your lazy asses and get in shape. Vic was a former body builder and Olympic powerlifter. That is something that you American scum cannot accomplish cause you are too busy complaining about each other and getting fat. Vic is going to take this federation by storm, win all the gold and take it to Germany where it truly belongs . He is the greatest athlete in Germany and he is by far the greatest athlete in America. If any of you American "superstars" don't like what I say then just step into the ring against Vic and he will eliminate you. With my brains behind Vic, we cannot possible lose. So all you wrestlers better beware and all you so-called champions better polish off those belts and get them all clean, cause Vic is here and he is going to take it away from you. (Trent Wallase moves off the podium and Vic steps to the Podium. He screams something in German and knocks over the podium. Trent Wallase manages to calm him down.) _______________________________________________________________________ [Doc Watson]: Hmmmmm. Well he looks to be championship material. And I should know. And do you know why I should know? (Before he can answer himself, Jerry Malone staggers back on the set. His clothes are all torn and dirty and the football helmet on his head has a large crack in it.) [Malone]: Don't bother getting up. I'll make it to my seat on my own. (Malone drops into his seat obviously exhausted.) [Doc Watson]: (grinning) Have a nice trip? [Malone]: Not one word, Doc. Not one word. [Doc Watson]: Well maybe you'll think twice before bringing wrestlers on the show to do your dirty work. [Malone]: For once I'm in total agreement with you. Are we almost done? [Doc Watson]: Almost. Just one segment to go. [Malone]: Great. Then let's show it so I can go visit my chiropractor, OK? [Doc Watson]: Whatever you say, Jerry. Roll it! ________________________________________________________________________ (Chameleon is sitting in his home watching a video over and over again. He turns it off before the camera can catch a glimpse of what it is.) [Chameleon]: Lazarus, your mutt doesn't frighten me. The Alliance has been formed, and grows stronger and wiser every day. Now I will dismantle your fledgling group of misfits before it has time to get off the ground. Outlaw Justice I have beaten you cleanly before and this Saturday will be a repeat performance, except for one thing. This time I will use you as example for your new found friend, Lazarus. Justice, this Saturday I will become your judge, your jury and your executioner. Oh, by the way President Rob. I see you've come back. That was unfortunate to hear about your car accident on the PCH roadway on Monday Night. (Scene fades.) ________________________________________________________________________ [Malone:] How? How did he know it was on the PCH roadway? [Doc Watson]: Who knows? But there's one thing I learned about that guy Chameleon; some questions are better left unanswered. [Malone]: Can't argue with you there. As announced this weekend, we are pleased to report that on October 31st, the HIWF will hold it's most extreme card to date; "ESCAPE FROM NEW YORK: CITY UNDER SIEGE". The expanded show will feature matches in the most extreme environment ever, the City of New York! [Doc Watson]: Tell 'em the best part, Jerry! [Malone]: This event will be produced by none other than SPEAKING YOUR MIND producer, [name withheld]. [Doc Watson]: No no, they don't care about that clown! Tell 'em the other part. The other part! [Malone]: Oh, yes. That night will be hosted by none other than yours truly and Smilin' Doc Watson. [Doc Watson]: New York City, here we come! [Malone]: Here are some possible types of matches being discussed: (A monitor comes on behind Malone and Doc.) Hardcore Hard Rock Cafe Match Waldorf Astoria Hotel Iron Man Match Yankee Stadium Base-Brawl (Survivor Series Style) Subterranean Carnage (Battle in the Sewers) Broadway Battle Royal (Last 4 men standing get title shots at PPV.) [Doc Watson]: Later on this week, President Flynt, I mean Ismael, will announce the participants in the matches. And knowing him, he'll come up with some fantastic matchups! (Suddenly, President Ismael emerges from off-stage and wheels himself next Doc Watson at the broadcast desk.) [Doc Watson]: Well speak of the Devil and he appears! President Ismael, what are you doing here? [Robert]: I'm here to make it clear, that I am back in power. I'm also here to let everyone know the truth. My car accident, I have found out was not an accident. [Malone]: What? [Robert]: The authorities have several witnesses who have reported that certain a HIWF wrestler was seen in the other car that struck me from behind, while I was at the stop light. [Doc Watson]: Who? [Robert]: Oh no, I'm not going to tell you. He knows who he is and he'll get his dues when I'm ready. I'm just here to watch tonight's show and make sure everything runs smoothly. [Malone]: Well you're always welcome here, President Ismael. [Robert]: Look you, I'll come here whenever I damn well please! Don't forget who signs your paychecks, buddy! [Malone]: Um, um, yes sir! Of course sir! How right you are sir! [Doc Watson]: (smiling) So for kiss-up Jerry Malone, and Larry Flynt in training [name withheld], I'm Smilin' Doc Watson. Keep it cranked to the nines! Who loves ya baby? ________________________________________________________________________ ******************************************** High Impact Wrestling Federation http://www.geocities.com/Hollywood/Lot/4700/ ********************************************
