HIWF (Jun 1997) — Roleplay

S.Y.M.: Speaking Your Mind, Inaugural Edition, 30 June 1997

30 June 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The bigger than life symbol of HIWF and two wrestler's pummeling each other senseless crashes onto your screen. Underneath it black bold faced letters appear... "SPEAKING YOUR MIND". Blaring out your speakers is 'Killer' by Adamski. To hear it for real in MIDI format, download it here at http://www.globalserve.net/~cracker/killer.mid (36KB). Then the screen goes black for a second and nice looking white letters appear on your screen. PARENTAL GUIDANCE WARNING, THE FOLLOWING PROGRAM WILL CONTAIN LANGUAGE OF A COARSE NATURE. PLEASE BE ADVISED. YADDA YADDA YADDA. The screen shot opens on two well dressed men sitting behind a large desk with several monitors behind them. The gentleman on the right who is wearing a black 3 piece suit starts to speak. [MAN #1]: (puts down a large Cohiba cigar) Hey there, wrasslin' fans! I'm Smilin' Doc Watson, bringing you the first broadcast edition of SYM! [MAN #2]: And I'm Jerry Malone. We've got a full lineup tonight with interviews from most of the superstars of HIWF. [DOC WATSON]: That's right amigo! (takes a draw on his Cohiba) Lots of these fella's used to be in a Mickey Mouse e-fed, but hey, HIWF took care of that, didn't we Jerry? [MALONE]: Well I wouldn't put it that way, Doc. Things just have a way of turning out for the best. So to start things off we have some pre-recorded comments from the man who made this all possible, the venerable President himself, [name withheld]. (Malone punches a desktop button and one of the monitors on the wall behind him comes to life.) _________________________________________________________ (The scene is the newly acquired First Interstate building in Downtown Los Angeles. A crew is changing the letters on the building to spell HIWF. [name withheld] is sitting at a large mahogany desk. He is finishing a phone call.) [ISMAEL:] Yeah, Vince....no his contract is not for sale....I'm not going to give away talent..you and Eric both are trying to steal my talent and it's not going to work..... (He hangs up the phone. And just now notices the camera.) [ISMAEL:] Oh...hey there wrestling fans....it's me [name withheld], President of the High Impact Wrestling Federation...I want to welcome you to the newest and soon to be dominant force in sports entertainment. You may remember me from my stint with the NEWA.......I have grown up now and recruited the greatest talent from all around the world....This Saturday the HIWF will hold our very first card. On July 5th you're going to see the greatest the wrestling world has to offer compete in the first round of the single elimination hardcore tournament to decide the HIWF World Champion. Also you're going to see 4 of the greatest tag teams face off in the first round of the HIWF Tag Team tournament....all this coming to you live from Arrowhead Pond, in Anaheim.... My pledge to you the wrestling fans is the very best wrestling on the face of the earth......and to spice it up.......it takes place in an Extreme environment....yes you the fans can bring any item you want to the arena.....and it will be legal for the HIWF Superstars to use them.......... This Saturday won't be for the faint of heart.....but if you like Extreme then this fed is the one for you! (The phone rings, Robert picks it up.) [ISMAEL:] No, no, no.......Bischoff his contract is not for sale! (The screen fades to black) __________________________________________________ [DOC WATSON]: Now there's a man who knows how to get the job done. (Watson looks right at the camera.) [DOC WATSON]: Just make sure you don't piss him off! Ha! [MALONE]: Doc! Watch what you say! Pres. Ismael is a visionary in the world of professional wrestling. Its a honor to be employed by him. [DOC WATSON]: Yeah, yeah, whatever you say Jerry. Hey, you got some brown stuff on your nose! (Watson hands Malone a handkerchief. Malone looks confused.) [DOC WATSON]: So while Jerry is wiping off whatever it is on his nose, let's go to a live report with our 'Goddess of the Mic', Bebe Cake. (Watson touches a desktop button and one of the monitors on the wall behind him comes to life and both Watson and Malone turn to watch it.) _________________________________________________ (Camera shot opens in an unknown arena dressing room. Wearing a short leather skirt with a matching cropped jacket is HIWF correspondent, Bebe Cake. To her left is 'Pretty Boy' Bobby Lawrence, manager of The French Connection.) [BEBE CAKE]: Thanks Doc! Mr. Lawrence, could you describe me your tag team, The French Connection? [PRETTY BOY]: Of course! These two Frenchmen are the best tag team in professional wrestling right now!!! And those stupid American fans are gonna have to get used to seeing The French Connection wear the tag team straps, like it or not!!! [CAKE]: Which qualities make the French Connection better than other teams? [PRETTY BOY]: The best quality of this team is that they have such a wonderful manager to guide them!!! But besides that, I think that their capacity to communicate in a language other than English makes it more difficult for their opponents to know what to expect from them. [CAKE]: Is this team the best tag team you've ever managed? [PRETTY BOY]: That's a very tough question, I can't tell, I've had the opportunity to manage such a big number of tag teams that I can't really tell who's better than who. [CAKE]: Do you have any comments regarding your first opponents, the L.A. Mafia? [PRETTY BOY]: Let me tell you something, L.A. Mafia, I've never been afraid of the Mafia and my boys will make sure that you won't bother anybody anymore, we'll show you that we're the best and we will humiliate you!!! [CAKE]: What is your opinion of the HIWF? [PRETTY BOY]: I would say that the HIWF will be a competitive federation. But of course, like in life, you have born winners and born losers and obviously this federation is filled with born losers. So, born winners like us will take advantage of the lack of talent in the tag team division. You know why??? because we're the best and we'll prove it!!! Like it or not!!! [CAKE]: Thank you for your time, Mr. Lawrence! [PRETTY BOY]: You bet! Don't forget to say the I'm manager of the next HIWF Tag Team Champions!!! [CAKE]: Back to you Doc and Jerry! _________________________________________________________ [MALONE]: Wow! Pretty strong words there, don't you think Doc? [DOC WATSON]: No. Its refreshing to see a manager so confident in his teams ability and skills. Hey, like I always say, 'If ya got it, flaunt it!' [MALONE]: Well I think he's underestimating the tag teams here in HIFW. Pres. Ismael has ensured that he has hired only the best the world has to offer. Let's talk to some of them right now, live via satellite from a press conference in good old Compton Los Angeles, The Outlaws. (Malone leans back and points at the monitor behind him.) _________________________________________________________ [MICHAEL BUFFER]: Ladies and gentlemen get ready to R U M B L E!!!!!! Here comes my favorite, your favorite hometown boys, now ready to tear down the HIWF, you never know what they are going to say, here are Downtown G, Mack 5-0, Jermain Asad, and the best f'n tag team in the world The L.A. Mafia, all together known as the OUTLAWS!!!!!!! (2 of Americas Most Wanted plays on the speakers) [MACK]: Were back!!........... And now, myself and the rest of the OUTLAWS have made their way to this new organization called the HIWF. Well for those of you idiots out there who did not know, before coming here we were bumping heads and shooting tips in the NEWA, until your man [name withheld] took the steppin' and now has formed this new organization in which he begged us, got down on his hands and knees and said, and I quote, "Outlaws we need you, the NEWA was nothing without you, please come to us!" along with several other promises I can't say on TV. So now we are here and ready to take those World Tag Team belts that [name withheld] did not want us to win in the NEWA, and let me add he did everything in his power to keep us from getting those belts, including changing matches in the last second, he'll he probably sent the Hate Machine out there to interfere in the L.A. Mafias matches, he is a low-down dirty dog who can kiss my ass! [JIMMY of The MAFIA]: This Saturday me and Jason step into the ring against The French Connection.....oooohhhh, the big boys, well big boys we are gonna teach you a lesson you will never forget cause we are the best tag team in the world, and after we get down with you, Hate Machine your asses are gonna pay for the NEWA!!! [DOWNTOWN]: We have been here for less than a week and already they are trying to break us up, what the hell, I have to face Jermain Asad this Saturday, its a f#@king conspiracy on the part of the HIWF trying to break us up cause they know we kick ass where ever we go and the HIWF is no exception. We will fight but don't expect an all out brawl cause we are united and if its not me, he will become the new world champion! And always remember OUTLAWS 4 LIFE!! <-: ____________________________________________________ [DOC WATSON]: Now there's a team that deserves the titles. And I should know. And you know why I should know? [MALONE]: (waves his hand is dismissal) Yeah, yeah, I know. 10 time World Heavyweight Champion. I've heard it a million times before! You really should find some new stories to tell. [DOC WATSON]: Two words Jerry; BITE ME! [MALONE]: Nice, ripping off someone's else's lame insult. Folks, I don't want this to get out of hand so lets quickly go to an interview with that mysterious Chameleon. ________________________________________________________ (Chameleon stands in the ring at an empty Arrowhead Pond. The camera slowly circles around him.) [CHAMELEON:] I have been waiting for this day for a long time.... Lazarus....this Saturday...I finally get my hands on you....and guess what....the OWO will not be there to save your ass.......I don't care about the belt....I signed on the dotted line to destroy you.... (The camera has now stop on shot of his back. The arena lights dim to almost black. But he is still visible.) [CHAMELEON:] Lazarus, this Saturday shall be the say of reckoning....... (the camera slowly circles around to the front of him) For you see, everything is not as it seems. (The camera finally made it's complete rotation and the arena lights brighten up and reveal Chameleon is longer in the building.) ________________________________________ [MALONE]: Boy, that guy gives me the creeps! [DOC WATSON]: (Blowing smoke rings) Jerry, ~GIRLS~ give you the creeps. [MALONE]: That's not true! Just ask my mother! [DOC WATSON]: OK, maybe I'll ask her when she's making me breakfast tomorrow morning! Ha! Speaking of breakfast, here's a guy who's got something against a certain type of cereal. (A hidden monitor drops down from the ceiling and both Watson and Malone turn to watch it.) _________________________________________________________ (Camera shot opens on Fergus McTavish, The Whirling Scottish Devil, talking on the telephone. His voice is mired with a thick Scottish accent.) [FERGUS]: But Ma, I tolds ya already, I'm workin' fer a new league! (Fergus appears to be listening to his Ma's response.) [FERGUS]: Aye Ma! I quit dat NEWA! I likes it here in HIWF better! (Fergus is listening again.) [FERGUS]: Ma, I keeps tellin' ya, its not me fault! Da President offered me a whack a cash ta get me arse over here! Jus keeps watchin' yer telly an I'll be on soon. Gots me a match wif some goober called da Attitude Adjuster. Och, I'll give him somthin' ta adjust. (Fergus grabs his crotch and shakes it.) [FERGUS]: Ya know Ma, I miss yer haggis. Kin ya send me somes? Dis crap dey serve at da HIWF cafeteria ain't enough ta feeds a hungry Scotsman likes meself. An da lunch-ladies? Argh, are dey ugly! So how much do I want? Aye, two boxes will be enough. What's dat? Jus a sec..... (Fergus calls the Camera-Guy over.) [FERGUS]: Eh, boyo, I wants ta say somthin' ta me Ma in private so gimme a sec, OK? [CAMERA-GUY]: Sure thing, Mr. McTavish. (Camera-Guy leaves, but still has the camera pointed at Fergus.) [FERGUS]: Alrights Ma, what's dat again? Och, alrights! I luvs ya too! G'Bye. (Fergus hangs up the phone.) (The Camera-Guy giggles at this and Fergus hears him.) [FERGUS]: Right laddie! Yer dead! Ya hears me! Yer dead! (The Camera-Guy turns and runs like hell, but his camera is still aimed in the other direction, pointed at Fergus.) [FERGUS]: Dat's right ya wussy! Runs all ya wants but sooner or later, The Whirling Scottish Devil will gets ya! So gets yerself ready fer a beatin'! I'll tear ya a new one I will! Oh, and one last ting.... (Fergus turns his back to the still running camera-guy and lifts up his kilt, mooning the camera) [FERGUS]: (over his shoulder) Gets ready for The Whirling Scottish Devil! ITS NOT OATMEAL!!!! (Camera shot gets blocked as the Camera-Guy runs around a corner but much laughing and hooting can still be heard.) ___________________________________________________________ (Doc Watson is laughing hysterically and is actually wiping a tear away from his face.) [DOC WATSON]: That guy kills me! I have no idea how he gets away with that crap! [MALONE]: How? Cause he's nuts, that's how! He's a dangerous guy, with or without his haggis. The Attitude Adjuster has his work cut out for him. I think Adjuster has the confidence to get him though but we'll have to wait and see. Speaking of confidence, lets get a quick comment from Mysterio, a man who's working real hard it seems for this Saturday's event. __________________________________________________________ (Mysterio is working out in a gym skipping rope.) [MYSTERIO:] Bucky Jones.....you're a loser....you couldn't win in the NEWA and you won't win here either......This Saturday....I'm gonna get extreme on your ass....then after that...I shall take what rightfully belongs to me, that HIWF World Title.....you can count on that. __________________________________________ [MALONE]: Seems like Bucky Jones is in for a world of hurt. [DOC WATSON]: (Butting out his now finished cigar.) I hope so. If there's one man I can't stand in wrasslin', its Bucky Jones. That momma's boy should stay at home if he knows what's good for him. [MALONE]: Doc, if you don't have anything nice to say, don't say it at all. [DOC WATSON]: You make me sick Jerry, you really do. Thank God this hour is almost over. Lets go to the last interview of the night, the dark an' deadly Grave Digger. ________________________________________ (Grave Digger is standing in grave yard, on a cold rainy morning) [Grave Digger:] This Saturday.....I shall take the first step to realizing my fate... my destiny...my destiny to become champion.... Outlaw Justice you may be a strong man...but do you have what it takes...to battle the forces of evil...of hate....of your own fear and terror.....Outlaw Justice this Saturday... I'm going to bury you alive......... (Grave Digger walks into the darkness that lies in the woods nearby.) _________________________________________ [DOC WATSON]: Ain't gonna be no Justice for poor Outlaw! Well folks, that's the first edition of S.Y.M. Hope ya liked it! As a personal favour to ole Smilin' Doc, why don't you drop our producer [name withheld] a line and let him know what you thought? (Doc Watson lights up a new Cohiba cigar.) [MALONE]: (looking at Doc Watson's cigar) Where the hell do you keep getting those? They're Cubans, right? [DOC WATSON]: 'Course they're Cubans! You know why? I don't smoke sh!t! And I'm not gonna tell you where I get them cause then you'll get some. (Doc winks at the camera.) [DOC WATSON]: So for Jerry Malone and the gorgeous Bebe Cake, this is Doc Watson signing off for Speaking Your Mind. Keep it cranked to the nines, baby! Who loves ya?!?
