HIWF (Jun 1997) — Roleplay

S.Y.M.: Speaking Your Mind, 15 August 1997

15 August 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
***Some of the interviews presented here are meant to be read prior to SSJ.*** ________________________________________________________________________ The bigger than life symbol of HIWF and two wrestler's pummeling each other senseless crashes onto your screen. Underneath it black bold faced letters appear... "SPEAKING YOUR MIND". Blaring out your speakers is 'KILLER' by Adamski. **To hear the theme music in MIDI format, download it here at http://www.globalserve.net/~cracker/killer.mid (36KB).** Then the screen goes black and nice looking white letters appear on your screen. PARENTAL GUIDANCE WARNING, THE FOLLOWING PROGRAM WILL CONTAIN LANGUAGE OF A COARSE NATURE. PLEASE BE ADVISED. YADDA YADDA YADDA. ________________________________________________________________________ (The screen shot opens on two well dressed men sitting behind a large desk with several monitors behind them. Jerry Malone is seated on the left and he is wearing a checked sportscoat with the HIWF logo stitched on the breast pocket. Doc Watson is sitting on the right wearing a black 3 piece suit and smoking his trademark Cohiba cigar.) [Doc Watson]: Hey-o! This is Smilin' Doc Watson ready to bring you another drab edition of S.Y.M! [Malone]: Must you be so negative? [Doc Watson]: Why the hell not? Look at who I'm partnered with! A gutless, talentless, momma's boy hack! [Malone]: I resent that! I'm a journalist, not a hack! I even went to journalism school and I've got the diploma to prove it! [Doc Watson]: You mean that piece of paper on the wall in your office? The one that's signed by Sally Struthers? (Malone's face turns beet red from anger.) [Doc Watson]: Uh-oh! Struck a nerve with that one! Well here's a guy who's striking it rich and beating the odds, Randy Brandenberg, "The Card Shark". ________________________________________________________________________ (We're live in the casino of "The Card Shark" Randy Brandenberg with "The Card Shark" himself and his manager "Pretty Boy" Bobby Lawrence. Their taking a walk in the casino.) [Card Shark]: You see, Rend!!! This is the reason for my success, this is the reason why "The Card Shark" is able to say today that I control the odds and nobody can decide what I'm gonna do!!! I've had this casino for 10 Years and every year, I've been able to make a profit!!! But you, Rend!!! You obviously don't know what business is all about, you come from the country of the Vikings and everybody knows that Vikings were the stupidest people on earth, they're not even able to get along with each other!!! [Pretty Boy]: That's right, Randy!!! The reason why "The Card Shark" is so successful in a ring is intelligence and knowledge!!! You and you tag team partner are far from having as much intelligence and knowledge that "The Card Shark"!!! That's why the odds are against you, Rend!!! [Card Shark]: As far as that stupid crybaby, Maddog Gnort is concerned!! We're gonna meat soon and this time, I'm gonna show the entire world just what kind of hasbeen you really are!!! [Pretty Boy]: What would say if we played a game of darts, Randy? [Card Shark]: Ok!!! I'll start. (The two men turn towards the dart board, in the middle of the board right in the bullseye, they placed a photo showing the face of Rend.) [Card Shark: This coming Saturday, Rend, I'm aiming for you!!! (The Card Shark throws his first dart in the middle of the photo and the bullseye. We can hear the two men laughing for this last shot.) ________________________________________________________________________ [Doc Watson]: Gee, you know who's picture I wish was up on that dart board Jerry? [Malone]: Mine? [Doc Watson]: Don't flatter yourself. No, I'm talking about that schmuck Bucky Jones. Boy oh boy, did Ollie Keaton bring a smile to my face on Wednesday night! [Malone]: That's terrible! Bucky had to be taken to the hospital as a result of Keaton's attack. In fact, that's where our camera crew caught up with him. ________________________________________________________________________ (Bucky Jones is leaving a hospital. He has bandages on his head.) [Bucky]: Mr. Keaton, I thought I took care of you already. Apparently, I didn't do it good enough. Now I'm ready to do it for real! I'm asking... NO, I'm DEMANDING a match next Wednesday! You and me! Man on man! One on one! I through with you! I'm going to make you pay for what you did on Wednesday! I'm going to make you pay dearly! (Scene fades) ________________________________________________________________________ [Doc Watson]: I think Bucky got hit on the head one time too many. He must be nuts challenging Ollie Keaton to a match. [Malone]: Well I think Bucky's doing the right thing. [Doc Watson]: Yeah, getting himself ready for an ass whipping! Ha! Looks like there's gonna be a whipping this Saturday as HIWF presents a King of the Hood match between the L.A. Mafia and the Maddogs of War. Lets get some comments about it from The Outlaws. And remember, buy the shirt! ________________________________________________________________________ (Interview from a 7-11 in Compton L.A.) [Mack]: This past Wednesday was a very bad day for us Outlaws, Asad lost, The Mafia lost but as you can clearly see we are not done thuging, its all war from now on. (Jason is looking at a issue of Playboy.) [Jason]: What a bunch of ugly hoe's none of mine women look like this. Anyways as for the French Connection you fagots got lucky and that will never happen again, as for the Hate machine stay the f#@k out of our business you punk ass bitches!!! [Jimmy]: Maddog's of War this Saturday we take it to a whole new extreme and we will come out on top, A King of the Hood match, our type of match f#@k the rules, no ref, no nothing but 25 minutes of fighting, its put up or shut up. [Mack]: I just gotta address something guys can I?? [Jimmy]: Go ahead Mack. [Mack]: Ok, Downtown G I have been watching all of your matches and I gotta say you are better than ever, you really your s#@t together, you are always welcome in our home and since we have not talked in such a long while, I am asking you to allow me a chance to say something face to face right here next Tuesday. Please Downtown let me. (Jermain Asad has a Slurpie in his hands and is going to pay for it.) [Asad]: Hey, hey HEY!!!!!! Listen up asshole. (Talking to the clerk in the store.) [Clerk]: Yes Sir how may I help you my friend? [Asad]: How much??? [Clerk]: That will be.... 2.95 please my friend. [Asad]: 2.95!!!!!!!! Hell no, I can get this s#@t at the Kwik E Mart across the street for 1 dollar. [Clerk]: Yes my friend. [Asad]: What, what the hell does that mean you immigrant. [Clerk]: I am not an immigrant I have my green card, I pay my taxes I am just as American as you my friend. [Asad]: I ain't your damn friend you freaken terrorist. [Clerk]: Hey listen here I was not convicted of anything, you shut up and pay or I blow your head of. [Jason]: WHAT!! What did you say punk? [Clerk]: You heard me mother bitches. [Asad]: F#@K that!!! (Aasd pulls out his 22 and points it at the clerk.) [Asad]: Break yourself give me the money, the food, the jewels even the mother lovin gun underneath your desk!! [Jason]: What the hell are you doing!!! (Jason, Jimmy, and Mack 5-0 run out the store, about one minute later the police come and arrest Jermain Asad.) [Asad]: You cant do this to me I ain't Rodney King, stop the violence, can't we all just get along!!! [Police]: Shut your mouth before we beat you. (They take him away in the police car.) ________________________________________________________________________ [Malone]: See kids, crime doesn't pay. So remember to say your prayers, eat your broccoli, and go to bed at a reasonable hour. [Doc Watson]: Eat your broccoli? Are you f#@king kidding me? No one eats broccoli anymore. Gimme a Big Mac anyday. [Malone]: Well I always ate my broccoli and look at me. [Doc Watson]: (grins) Oooh, I'm tempted to say something but its just like shooting fish in a barrel. So anyway, there I am once again picking up a few things at the newsstand when this magazine catches my eye. (Doc reaches behind him and places on the desk a magazine. On the cover is an obviously intoxicated woman with her skirt pulled over her head. The title of the magazine is DRUNK CHICKS.) [Doc Watson]: Ooops! I picked that one up for President Ismael. Its this one here. (Doc holds up a copy of GQ. Acer is on the cover of the magazine, dressed in his usual all black tux with Ray Ban sunglasses. Miss Gray is dressed in her usual long black sequin dress and has her hands wrapped around The Acer. The Acer is wearing the HIWF Television Championship Belt! The Headline under Acer's picture reads.....Focused, Famous AND Fashionable!) (Doc flips over to page 4 and the cover story...) Allen "The Acer" Carter, the current High Impact Wrestling Federation's Television Champion, is a true fashion dream! Always noted for his custom designed wrestling outfit, an all black skintight costume, designed to do one thing - strike fear into the heart of his foes! And his unquestionable good taste carries over to even his eyewear, his Ray Ban Preadator sunglasses! Outside the ring, he can be found in mainly an all black tuxedo but other times walking in his casual sportshirts and wearing an official HIWF "The Acer" leather jacket. Not only does Allen Carter dazzle the ring and crowd with his fashion sense but his manager/girlfriend does as well! Miss Gray can always be found wearing her black sequin dresses that shine like diamonds because there are mini diamonds embedded into the dress! For casual wear, Miss Gray prefers sportshirts and slacks. Whether in the ring, Acerizing some hapless inferior, or defending his TV belt, Allen Carter is a champion of fashion! (Doc closes the magazine.) [Doc Watson]: (straightening his tie) I taught him how to dress, you know. [Malone]: (rolls his eyes) Yeah, right! [Doc Watson]: Hey, every lady's crazy 'bout a sharp dressed man. And I should know. And do you know why I should know? [Malone]: Because Sally Struthers told you? [Doc Watson]: Ha! Good one Jerry! Ya finally told a joke! [Malone]: (visibly upset his comment did not annoy Doc) You've got the thickest skin of anyone I've ever met. But then again, so do alot of superstars of the HIWF. Coming up, we have an interview with The Mongolian Assassin who's live on remote with the wonderful Bebe Cake. ________________________________________________________________________ (We're in a darkroom who is lightened by couple of candles. Bobby Lawrence and the Mongolian Assassin are standing side to side and are waiting to speak with Bebe Cake.) [Cake]: This Saturday, Mr. Lawrence, your Mongolian Assassin is going to face Main of the Stormeavers, how do you think your man is going to fare against Maim since he's yet to win a match in the HIWF? [Pretty Boy]: First of all, little girl!!! the Mongolian Assassin doesn't care about what everybody says about him and second of all, Maim!!! without your tag team partner, you're nothing but a gutless coward and this coming Saturday you're gonna go down!!! [Cake]: You're boys seem to have a tough time winning matches these days, how can you explain that? [Pretty Boy]: I call this hard luck or a lack of preparation, however, I know that this Saturday, the Mongolian Assassin is gonna be ready for battle and Maim, you poor idiot, you've made the mistake of accepting a match with the most heartless individual in the HIWF!!! [Cake]: The Stormeavers scored a win against your French Connection last Saturday, if Rend try to interfere in the match, do you have something in store for him? [Pretty Boy]: Of course!!! (He and the Assassin laughs) I'll be glad to make a little surprise for our little friend Rend if he sticks his big nose in our business!!! You want to know what it is, well, you just have to wait for this coming Saturday!!! [Cake]: Do you have any comments, Mongolian Assassin, regarding your match? [Assassin]: (with his cold look) Maim!!! You come from the land of the Vikings, but me, I come from the land of the gods!!! And the gods are gonna make sure that humans like you are gonna stay humans!!! [Cake]: Ok, back to you Jerry and Doc! ________________________________________________________________________ [Doc Watson]: Thanks, Bebe! Boy, I hope he kicks Maim's ass! Hey, Jerry, have you heard the latest from The Attitude Adjuster over the NEW Ace In The Hole Hotline? (Doc pumps the phoneline feed through so the viewers can hear it.) (Sound of a dialtone....then Doc dialing 1-900-The-Acer.....Then you hear "Stars and Stripes Forever" play and Tal Geddy's voice comes over the line.) [Tal Geddy]: Welcome Acer fans! This is The Men With Attitudes HOTLINE and I'm Talson Geddy....For The Acer's latest State Of The TV Championship Address, Press 1. For Miss Gray's Horoscopes, press 2. For comments from The Nobles about their upcoming title defense match press 3. For an Attitude Check, press 4. (Doc presses four and grins.) [Tal Geddy]: Allright it's time to think positive! Attitude is coming off his co-tag team victory with The Acer against The Drummond Brothers this Wednesday...here's Attitude's statement about his upcoming match against former HIWF TV Champ Chameleon! ("The Secret Of My Success" plays in the background.) [The Attitude Adjuster]: My fellow positive thinkers! My win streak is now at two! Acer and I took care of those hapless Dipweed Brothers and they were shown the all powerful DREAM! Now Chameleon awaits...and I shall finish the job that Carter started! Rest assured, Chameleon, you will feel the almighty, the allknowning ever living DREAM! [Cheryl]: Hey Chameleon! Maybe you will interface with my laptop on Saturday! (Sound of Cheryl and Attitude high fiving!) (Doc hangs up the phone and stands up.) [Doc Watson]: (doing the Wave) C'mon Jerry, "Catch the Dream!" Whooooo! [Malone]: Catch it yourself, we've got a show to do. [Doc Watson]: Yeah, you're right. Next, we got words from Outlaw Justice, the Heavyweight champ. Ok, Outlaw, do it just like I told you. If Jesse "All American" Ewiak can do it, so can you. Remember, Ewiak holds the Short Interview record at 5 seconds. Can you beat him? Lets find out! ________________________________________________________________________ (Outlaw Justice stands alone in an alleyway. Trash blows in the breeze around his feet.) [Outlaw]: Chameleon, Saturday you die! (Scene fades) ________________________________________________________________________ [Doc Watson]: (jumps up) YES! 2 SECONDS! HE DID IT! IT'S TIME TO CELEBRATE! (Balloons, streamers, and confetti fall down from the ceiling. Doc jumps up on the Control Center desk and starts to dance as "Celebrate" by Kool & The Gang starts to play.) **To hear the song in MIDI format, download it here at http://www.globalserve.net/~cracker/celebrate.mid (36KB).** [Doc Watson]: (dancing around the desk) He did it! He did it! He did it! He did it! He did it! [Malone]: Man, am I glad the show is over just to get away from you, Watson! Well, I'm Jerry Malone... [Doc Watson]: And I'm the Doctor of Love! Just ask Jerry's mom! [Malone]: Enough about my mother! (Suddenly, a loud crash is heard off camera! Doc looks towards it, and his face goes pale white.) [Doc Watson]: (stops dancing and jumps off the desk) Oh, sh!t. [Malone]: WATSON! Ok, what is so....oh, no. (Rend and Maim, the Stormreavers, storm onto the set, in their Viking battle garb. They go right past Jerry to Doc.) [Rend]: YOU! [Doc Watson]: Um....look, whatever I did... [Maim]: We would like to thank you! [Doc Watson]: I'm sorry....what? [Maim]: You distracted Psycho Stud and Death for the Stormreavers to get a glorious win! [Doc Watson]: Yeah.....yeah! I did, didn't I! [Maim]: The Stormreavers would like to repay you! [Doc Watson]: With what? A big busty blonde? [Malone]: Oh, brother... [Rend]: Close your eyes, Watson! (Watson closes his eyes and licks his lips.) [Rend]: From the Gods...to you! (Maim and Rend hits Watson with a double axehandle!) [Malone]: What the.....! [Maim]: WE DIDN'T ASK FOR YOUR ASSISTANCE, WATSON! THE STORMREAVERS NEED HELP FROM NO ONE! WE FIGHT ALONE, AND WE WIN ALONE! [Rend]: HIWF! THE STORMREAVERS ARE A FORCE THAT CANNOT BE STOPPED BY ANY NUMBER OF MEN! WE DEMAND THE RIGHT TO ENTER THE STABLE WARS...BY OURSELVES! [Maim]: WE CAN BEAT ANY FOUR MEN HIWF CAN COME UP WITH! THE STORMREAVERS ARE UNSTOPPABLE, AND WE WILL PROVE IT TO THE WORLD! (Rend and Maim go back to kicking Watson.) [Malone]: JESUS! CUT, GET US OUT OF HERE, FOR THE LOVE OF...
