[NcWa] Chadd Mathews: What Has To Be 4-27-00

2000-04-27
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Anarchy Inc. presents Chadd Mathews [. The camera opens to Minn National Airport. Chadd Mathews is smiling. His eyes are blocked by the Oakley sunglasses he has on. He's wearing a new t-shirt that just came on sale. The shirt reads "The Next Insane Champion" on the front. He has a gym bag on the right side of him. The strap is over his head and on his left shoulder. The gym bag says "NcWa". On his left side is a black case that has a strap going to his right shoulder. The black bag probably carries a computer. while the gym bag carries his wrestling attire. Chadd
Mathews is also dressed up in tan khakis and some black newly polished shoes.
Chadd is walking down tunnel B. .] [. The camera switches to the second camera and the back of his shirt reads " "The Soulja" Erik Andrews, Anarchy Inc." Chadd countiues to walk down and then stops when he reaches B-9. Chadd pulls out a ticket. The ticket reads 9:48pm destination "Detriot, Michigan". .] [. The camera switches back to camera 1. Chadd takes off his glasses and a friendly smile is no longer what you see. The camera focuses on his eyes. His eyes are full of hatred, intensity, pure anger. The camera starts to unzoom a little. Chadd's tone never changes. He opens his mouth as if to talk but his right arm tilts and water poors into his mouth. He takes the water away. Chadd looks deep into the camera. Then opens his mouth again. .]
"NcWa, Bloodbath. I walked into Bloodbath with nothing to lose and I walked out of Bloodbath the same man as I walked in. My partner Erik Andrews got the upper hand over me on this one. He's walking around with a shot at the Insane
Champion but thats fine with me. It is, im happy for him. But Psydeburn, he won the shot to take on SSD and he turned it down. No He wants to take on Ashe
Corvan for the IC Title. He just one screw loose. But I'm not here to talk about should of been. I'm here to talk about what has to be."
"What has to be is simple. I have to be the best at what I do. And well theres no doubt in my mind that I am the best athlete walking in the ring today. Sure every now and then people slip one by me, such as Psydeburn and Andrews. But does it happen often? Of course not. I suppose Tyler you could call it a fluke.
Tyler lets define Fluke. According to the dictionary, A Fluke is a stroke of luck. Tyler, the fact that Pysdeburn and Andrews beat me is a stroke of luck.
Tyler Gunz, I'm sure your a bright a star and you can figure out the odd that the chance of lightning striking twice is, almost impossible. Gunz think of the chances that lightning will strike twice, right beside eachother. Now were just talking insanity. Tyler, Mr. Gunz, whoever and whatever the f[beep] you want people to call you, your chances of taking my shoulder and pounding them on the mat or a 1...2..3 is about as slim as Lightning striking twice in the same spot, 2 times in a row. Gunz lets face it, Theres no chance in hell. What has to be, is you have to be on the ground knocked out. Waking up as I walk up the ramp, hands held high. Gunz you will look on the Jihadtorn. See me stand on the top rope, fly off and make are match a Paragon Finish."
"The past is going to haunt me, for the rest of my life untill I can put a end to it. It has to be, Psydeburn sooner or later we will step into the ring. And what has to be, will take place because just like I'm going to do to Gunz at
Fade to Black is what i'm going to do to you. Psydeburn in case you want to see the pain you'll be feeling, tone into my match with Gunz. Watch as the only
Paragon match in the PPV will take place. Watch how the only match that will end with a Paragon Finish, ends. See the look of pure terror on Gunz face. Then imagine you, in the same terror. Psydeburn, be afriad but most of all be scared to walk down a street or a ramp without a man watching out back."
"Moving back to Gunz, Gunz I dont know if you are understanding the point that
Your Role Model is trying to make but I'll lower it down to your level and use small words. The Best vs Another Guy. Chadd Mathews vs Tyler Gunz....
[. Some lady working in the airport takes the speaker and says "Now boarding First Class flight 1425 to Detriot, Michigan." Chadd Mathews picks up baggs and stands in line. Chadd hands the lady his ticket. The lady runs it threw the machine. Chadd walks ahead, the camera man does the same and runs to catch up. Chadd walks up to his seat 3A and 3B he puts his bag in the window seat. Chadd sits down, The camera man sits in 3C and 3D. .]
"Now Gunz the time is ticking down, Gunz watch the second hand turn, and turn. Sooner then later you will be meeting the Best! You will walk down the aisle, wearing your E3 shit hopeing to get a win. Hopeing.... to.... beat... Chadd
Mathews. Tyler the trick to my success is quite simple. Talent over luck.
Tyler luck happens, take Erik Andrews and Psydeburn for example. Lady luck danced with them, but for some reason I don't feel Lady luck is going dance beside you. She's mpt even going to be in are match. We all know that Lady Luck has striked in Michigan before, Detriot, Michigan is my home but thats off the subject. Tyler Gunz so that brings us back to the Skill vs Skill, Talent vs
Talent. Chadd Mathews bleeds Talent and sweats Skill."
"Tyler, at Fade To Black, May 4th in Ann Arbor, Michigan Two Men who are opening the card that are not fighting for a Insane, US, IC, World, Tag team
Title shot. Two men who are not in a title match. Two men who are opening the card, setting the standards for the rest of the night. The two men are us two.
Chadd Mathews, Tyler Gunz. Tyler I don't doubt how good are match will be. I don't doubt your talents. It's not that I don't recognize the talents you have its just, compared to mine they just don't exist. Its like taking the 1rst
Potato Chip. Big, Round, Crunchy, Fresh, this is me. Then tyler when you empty the bag out and you have the little crumbs left. Tyler thats were you come in.
Small, Edgy, Stale. Tyler though the Big Chip wouldn't be the Big Chip with out the Small Crumbs to compare it to its still the better on. Tyler with out the small people like you I can't be a star. But your there, and im here so compared we will be. But you don't have to compare us. Becase on the 4th of May we do, go in the ring. And we will set the standards for the Pay Per View.
Aswell as the NcWa finally noticed Mathews as being more then just a lucky young star. Because when I beat you, when I beat Gunz the verteran, I will be noticed because its WHAT HAS TO BE!"
[. Chadd turns and faces front, More people get on the plan, the camera notices some people. And soon the camera fades to a "Fade To Black" promo.
.]
