28 December 2008

Alyssa Lee is officially the first lady of Unplugged, not to mention the only female on the active roster. But her record has some people thinking she is just another pretty face, when in fact she has brought the Current One Shot Champion to the edge and back in her lose two weeks ago, and this past Parental Advisory earned herself a spot in the main event of Season Beatings when she made Hallow Wicked tap out. (Wicked also advances with a cover over Twilight Rayn) Alyssa Lee is the true meaning of Fatal Attraction.
But she wasn’t the only hit of the night, as free agent pick up Luther Savage earned himself a One Shot championship match on the next Parental Advisory. He will face both CJ Gates and Chad Vargas as these two epic scaffold match ended in a draw. Ari Chase wanted to demand CJ Gates as the victor, but Eric Evans had another plan and made the triple threat main event for Parental Advisory 11.
Another surprise hit has to be Porter MaCleod. Mr. Prime Time Champion himself has been on fire, at least each time the Prime Time championship has been up for grabs. As he once again stepped up to the plate and added another defense to his record as he defeated Troy Gafgen in a Unplugged first Thousand Volt match. The successful defense makes Porter MaCleod the longest regining Prime Time Champion.
The night was capped off with a epic main event between Crimson Skull, Requiem, and Jinx. It’s no secret the fragile friendship between Requiem and Jinx has took its toll of Jinx, and this week we saw the breakdown of Jinx. We also saw Jinx become the 7th name to be entered in the Circus Death Match. But the victory itself wasn’t the big surprise it was how he got the victory. Requiem reversed the Hellhound slam and dropped Crimson Skull with the Eulogy. Requiem could have easily took the victory, but instead he dragged Jinx on top of Crimson Skull giving him the victory and the spot.
Parental Advisory Ten ended on a very cryptic note as we saw video that took place two weeks ago with a mystery person entered the office and well the rest they say is history…

Percy the replacement in the match earned himself 8 stitches to the forehead, and Crimson Skull along with Hallow Wicked picked up the victory.

If you saw the match you would have never guessed these two were at one time best buds. Both superstar let everything hang out as both ended the match in a pool of blood. Requiem proving a point to Crimson Skull who was seated at ring side, snapped as he turned up the violence and beat Jinx into submission earning him the victory. The king of mind games Crimson Skull also made sure Requiem knew he was there as he attacked a recovering Jinx right before Jinx caught wind of the two enemies side by side. After Requiem and Skull left the ring, Jinx went on a fit of rage, breaking and destroying everything in his sights.
The biggest story of the night had to be the disappearance of Wrestling Unplugged’s owner Jayson Burrows. Nothing was made official, but right before Parental Advisory went off the air, we saw one of Jayson’s security personal enter his office, and what he found was chaos and no Burrows. He quickly ordered the rest of the arena’s staff to lockdown the building, but it was little to late as most of the fans were already out of the arena.
