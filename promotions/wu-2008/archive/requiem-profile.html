Wrestler name: Requiem Wrestler nickname(s): The Sorrow Wrestler Hometown: The Shadows Height: 7'5'' Weight: 376 lbs Alignment: Heel Manager/Valet: Eric Evans Wrestler Gimmick: Bringer of Sorrow onto the masses Entrance music: Boondox-Torn Possession Physical Description: Requiem, with the blackest eyes, the devils eyes, stands at an impressive 7 feet 5 inches, and weighs in at an incredible three hundred seventy-six pounds. The build of his body is most impressive for a being of his size, showing great muscle tone with his pale, white skin. The sorrow fully lives up to his name, mourning in black. He wears a black long sleeved button up, complete with black dress pants. His massive hands are covered by full fingered black gloves. His feet are also covered by the shiniest of black leather combat boots, which appear to be steel toed. His hair is mostly black, with streaks of grey, flows down onto his shoulders and into his eyes, covering most of his face at times. Under his stringy peppered hair, a black and grey goatee is slightly visible. Wolfgang, is complete with a long, black trench coat upon his entrance to the ring, but removes it before the match takes place.
Wrestling Style: Murderous aka Rough Personality: Not much is known about his personality. He doesn’t speak a lot, his manager speaks for him for the most part.
Signature Moves: Last Rites - Double Arm DDT from the top rope. Finishing Moves: Eulogy What it is: Requiem Irish whips his opponent to the ropes. Upon their return, he bends to lift them into the air by their waste. He starts to turn to position himself behind them, but doesn’t fully make the transition, instead stands to their side. Upon finishing the transition, he roughly pushes them downwards. On their way down, Requiem falls to his knee, slamming his opponents face into his knee.
Wrestler's history: At this point, not much is known about Requiem. The only thing that can be said is that he was founded by Eric Evans, former co-owner of BLWA, and brought him in to do his dirty work before BLW closed.
