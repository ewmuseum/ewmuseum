WU (Wrestling Unplugged, 2008) — News

The Feed, Vol. V (November 2008)

c. 10 November 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Its that time again, Time for me to feed all your wet fantasies and give you the inside source to everything related to the Unplugged World! After a week off I’m back with even more news then ever!
The Big story has to be Jayson Burrow releasing four wrestlers from there WU Contracts, the names are…Megan Tallet, Drake Daniels, Venom and Amy Dumas. He has also put six superstars on watch those names include the WU champion himself Requiem, Red Dragon, Cameron Drake, Stalker Knight, Nigel Riechert, and the only tag-team in WU Spiral Dragons. Guess Jayson wasn’t kidding when he said he wasn’t going to deal with talent, who were just there to collect a check and didn’t want to earn one. On the flip side of things, Jayson has signed two new faces, in CJ Gates and Alyssa Lee.
Gates is a big pick up in the sense that he is a top notch talent, who has won championships in different promotions and even ran his own top notch promotion a while back. The kid is a hottie and has some great death defying moves but has a bit of a chip on his shoulder and lets his attitude do all the talking for him. Nonetheless I look forward in seeing him in action.
Alyssa Lee is a bit different then CJ Gates, as she comes in with some history with WU Superstar Jinx. It’s rumored that these two once were a item, more or less…If you call Alyssa being a pet or a slave a relationship. Some are wondering just why is Alyssa Lee here, and is she out for vendetta on Jinx. Alyssa should be a hot ticket here in WU weather its with or against Jinx.
Also in the news this week, Jayson Burrows announced that Thankful Sins well be the final chapter in what people have been calling a Civil War. Both sides are lined up in some very big matches, and both sides have the final chance in proving really is the better group. It’s no secret the feud between the Plague of Karnage and Sex and Violence hasn’t been as big as it was, but why want to pull the plug on it now? I mean it’s clear Sex and Violence have been gaining the upper hand ever since Eric Evans made the jump, and Brandon Maddox has been on fire ever since he dropped Doom, could this be a worried Jayson Burrows making this announcement? And what is to be of the stables apart of this war? Are they going to just break up and go there own ways, or are we finally going to have a tag-team division? All questions I look forward in getting the answers to.
Speaking about superstars on Fire, Twilight Rayn has become one of the hottest talents here in WU, and just a few weeks ago I wouldn’t have known him from any other Jobber who comes through the land of the rising sun. Twilight picked up his second defense last week with the help of his partner of the night Brandon Maddox, and is just three defenses away to completing the One Shot Challenge, granting him a shot at any WU championship. I’ve gotten the chance to sit down with the Lord of the night himself:
Jenny Harris: Let me just say it’s a honor to have you on the show. Twilight Rayn: Pleasure is all mine Jenny Harris: How have you become the hottest superstar in WU, in just a months time? Twilight Rayn: Really haven’t done anything different on my part. Seems the spotlight has just begun to shine after I retried Hallow Wicked.
Jenny Harris: Speaking about Hallow Wicked, do you feel lost with out having him around? Twilight Rayn: Why would I be? Yes my goal coming here was to rid WU of Hallow Wicked, but it wasn’t the only task I had. Just the main priority was all.
Jenny Harris: Just three more defense and you could be the number one contender for the WU Championship
Twilight Rayn: How do you know I’ll cash it for the WU Championship? Maybe the Prime Time would be a championship of my desire
Jenny Harris: I apologize Twilight Rayn: No need to, because I would be a fool not to cash in on a WU championship match, it’s why we are all here. To have are name etched in gold.
Jenny Harris: Is it true you are a Vampire? Twilight Rayn: Hemes would be the correct term for what my friends and I are. Jenny Harris: Porter MaCleod issued a challenge to you on Parental Advisory are you going to accept his challenge?
Twilight Rayn: Why wouldn’t I? I don’t understand why he would challenge the one person who could defeat him. And I may never know that answer. But I do know Mr. MaCleod has bite off more then he can handle with this challenge. You see Porter couldn’t achieve what I managed. You see he watched Hallow Wicked parade around BLW as champion, but could never do anything about it, where is I rid Hallow Wicked from wrestling. And if I will do the same to Porter if he pushes me to that limit.
Jenny Harris: And if you win against Troy Gafgen then your One Shot championship will be on the line
Twilight Rayn: I suppose Jenny Harris: That is one match I cant wait to see, Will Twilight were out of time. I want to thank you for coming in and being the very first guest of the Feed
Twilight Rayn: Pleasure was all mine We are just about out of time here on the feed, but I wanted to address the issue of Jinx and DLS, am I the only one who thinks Jinx has gone to far? I mean Crystal is the wife of DLS, she has nothing to do with this…this feud, and for Jinx to lick her was totally uncalled for. I HOPE that DLS gives this freak the lesson his mother should have.
And what’s up with Requiem? Seems the trip to the nut house has broken the sorrow, sure Jinx sprung him from the nest, but the champion just didn’t seem to be himself during last weeks main event, almost as if the lost of Eric Evans has been more costly then Requiem is letting on. Lets hope he can bring himself back togther as he is facing Maddox’s poison Porter MaCleod. If not then Thankful Sin could be the new dawn of a new World Champion
Where is the Co-Owner of WU? Two weeks straight we haven’t seen Eric Evans. Is he also kicking himself in the ass for what he has done to Requiem, or is he just been in safe hiding from the monster he sent to the nut house two weeks ago?
And wrapping up on the topic of staff who is the new GM? We’ve found out a big hint last week, as the new GM sent Burrows a Dildo, not a everyday gift to give to your boss, but it did help us clear up the sex of the new GM. Burrows even went out the way as to promise Santiago that he would ‘Get some’ I for one can’t wait, and it’s about time we have some female power going on!
I’m Jenny Harris and You’ve just been Feed!
