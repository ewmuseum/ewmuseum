The Feed, Vol. IV (27 October 2008)

27 October 2008

, October 27th 2008 The Feed Vol. IV Hello ladies and gentlemen. I am Jenny Harris and I’m glad that I could grace and amaze you with my journalistic greatness for this fourth edition of the Feed. This is the place where we dive into everything that is WU. Now who would be better to kick this thing off than the theme of Parental Advisory this week: DTA.
This week we seen that during a civil war no one is safe from a good old fashion Backstabbing. As Eric Evans who seemed to be the only friend in the world to the Sorrow Requiem slap the monster in the face. Well not literally as he cost Requiem a victory over Doom when he dropped Requiem with his patented move the Ace’s Over, and not only did he stab Requiem in the back, but he kicked the plague of Karnage in the balls by joining the newly named Sex and Violence. The funny thing is now Eric Evans the co-owner of Wrestling Unplugged has just aligned himself with Johnny Hall. A man who was responsible for killing Eric Evans wrestling career back when Hall owned BLWA. So why would Eric Evans make such a bold move? Some are questioning Double E about his injury. And after that Ace’s Over I can honestly say I with the skeptics.
But the backstabbing fun doesn’t stop there as Brandon Maddox did the same to Doom. And it happened at the same time Double E stabbed Requiem. Talk about your funny odds. But I must admit the Maddox spin was a bit more stunning, as we all had Doom figured as the head of the Alliance but in one fetal night we found out it was Brandon Pulling all the strings and Doom was just the puppet. Seems like the Puppet master got tired of playing second fiddle as Brandon attacked Doom after his big victory, and not only did he attack Doom but he booked a match for this week’s Parental Advisory. And that match is a all or nothing for the part of Doom. As Doom will put his WU contract as will as his WU Championship contendership up for grabs against Brandon Maddox. The question I have is…What is Brandon putting on the table?
In other news, Jayson Burrows has scratched the month of tag wars for November as he stated it just isn’t the time to add a new division to WU. This could be a factor of Jayson being to pre-occupied with the hiring of a General Manager to even pay attention to the world of Tag-Team wrestling. He did go on record offering all the few tag-teams that WU has to offer that if they really want a tag-team division then he would book a championship match at Thankful Sins.
In other news Wrestling Unplugged bids Hallow Wicked a good-bye. The former two time BLWA champion has put in his retirement papers to Jayson Burrows on Sunday night, making Parental Advisory four his last professional match.
It’s that time of the report, it’s time for the Rumor Mill: - Rumor has it that newly retired Hallow Wicked is in talks with Jayson Burrows, The Tokyo Sponsors, and Japan film company Nikkatsu in starring in Wrestling Unplugged’s first produced film. Details are not known and again this is a rumor and could hold no truth. I for one would love to see Hallow Wicked guess star in a episode of True Blood. But that’s just me.
- Who is Mortime? The fella Jinxy introduce to the sorrow. Rumors have Mortime being Requiem’s new handler since Eric Evans became Co-Owner of WU. But just who is he? Reports have Mortime having some odd connection to Requiem, maybe it could be a connection that could put a controlled leash on the Monster.
- Is Wrestling Unplugged in talks with current IZW superstar and former GCW champion Johnny Styles? We found out last week that Johnny Styles is indeed Johnny Hall’s nephew but what factor does that hold in the 7 time world champion signing a deal with WU? But there are also reports that SBK is just to busy to even toy with a WU offer.
- How close are we in seeing a super card with IZW and WU? I know this has been in talks ever since the two companies have affiliated with each other but I want to know if there is any truth to this, and I always get what I want. This is one Rumor I’ll be getting facts to before next week.
Enough of the rumors back to the good stuff! What happened to Eric Draven? We all seen the boost in activity from Eric Draven and we also saw last week as he was taken away at the hands of Stalker Knight. And this morning Eric Draven was no longer under contract with Wrestling Unplugged. I’ve tried to get a few words from Meagan Tallet but she has yet to answer my calls
Well that’s all the time we have for today, until next week…. I’m Jenny Harris, and You’ve just been feed!
