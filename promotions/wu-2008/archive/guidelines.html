Wrestling Unplugged’s mission is to provide a place of fun where people can come and do what they love to do, and in the process create a character they can be proud of. Are goal is to give everyone a fun, fair time with a touch of competition. We focuses more on the wrestler‘s you handle, and a TON less on the drama. But like in real life Drama just seems to poke its ugly head around but we try to minimize it.
-------------------------------------------------------------- Like in every E - Promotion, we got are selves some rules: -------------------------------------------------------------- ROLEPLAYING - This is one I don't see a lot of promotions use, but then fed heads get pissed about it happening, so I'm just gonna toss it out here: No attacking staff. Again unless you talk to me and get my word on it, don't do it. As for on air talent, IE Interviewers, Ref, Commentators there all fair game
- I think this is a unwritten rule in this game: No using others characters, unless you get there Ok, EVEN then I need a PM letting me know it's ok, simple stuff boys
- There is a two week trail period here in WU. For the first two weeks you will be booked in Dark matches. If you show up for both matches, then you will be promoted to the full time roster and have a shot at the One Shot Championship. This rule has come due to a lot of people filling out apps but not rping when booked. So if your serious then two weeks of dark matches really isn’t a big deal. Also just because your booked for a dark match or in a trial period doesn’t mean you can be active with segments to help promote your character.
Exception: Would be if your first RP was fantastic and really impressed the staff, then we would promote you out the trail period and into a match for the One Shot Championship. But again the Staff and myself have to feel that you really put in a great effort to be here.
- We have a Plow rule, I have been lackluster about acting on this rule, but feel with the growth of the roster that we should act and mod this rule. And it’ll work as followed: If you rped for your match before the deadline and your foe doesn’t rp until the day of deadline, then there rp will count but there will be a untold amount of deductions of points. If no one rps until the day of the deadline then both will have One rp that they may post on the day of the deadline. This is to give people a chance to comeback to a RP. I also feel there should be no trouble with this rule as I get a new card up every Sunday night, giving you all a full 6 and a half days to rp
- The Way we book are show is a bit different then what we were doing from the start. Instead of the weeks best battles being made main-event of the week, what we well do is if your match steals the show the week before then you will be booked to headline the next show, if it’s a PPV you’ll be the Co-headliner.
Character PUSH - If you want to make an impact in WU, there are many ways to do so. If you sign during a roleplaying period, then submit a segment to introduce your character, or send me a segment so we can get you on the air of are show. Another way to gain a push is to strive for the Main Event every week, and possibly coming up with a 'story' for your match. PM your opponent, see if you can make a feud or a storyline out of your match. People who can show us that they don't need staff to hold their hands through storylines will be trusted to run their feuds. More ways to gain a push are to be OOC active, and card active. You don't just have to submit ideas for your character. Maybe help others with their storylines and let us know that you are doing so. This is a creative game and that's how we all enjoy ourselves, by creating something exciting, together.
OOC - No Recruiting. This is a unwritten rule, but after people not having any sort of respect I have put it in words. Please don’t recruit. Everyone here in WU was never recruited from another promotion by me or anyone else on the staff. I just think it’s wrong, and think it’s a dickhead move. And if for some fucked up reason you feel like you want to break this rule, then you will be banned and the email you sent out (yea I always get a copy) will get back to me, and I will make it pubic on the RK network, or the Knights boards.
- When you first sign up for an account on the boards you will notice you fall under the ranking of ‘FREE AGENT‘ this is due to the recruiting and so we could see if you want to really be apart of WU, In two weeks you‘re not feeling WU then there well be no hard feelings if you opt not to stay. Under free agents you will not be able to send PM's nor emails to are roster.
More Info: After you fill out a app and make it through the two week period, you will be bump up to the status of WU Star (unless you join a stable or tag-team or create one) Once in WU Star level you can send PM’s and or emails.
For the people in the Free Agent ranking you may IM me with anything you need. (Segments, SL Ideas all that jazz) or email me.
