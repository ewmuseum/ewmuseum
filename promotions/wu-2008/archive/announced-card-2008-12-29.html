December 29, 2008 @ 7:30 PM EST Venue: Nippon Budokan Arena, Tokyo Japan RP Deadline: December 28th 2008 Segment/Strat Deadline: December 28th 2008 MAIN EVENT OF THE NIGHT One Shot Championship CJ Gates © vs. Chad Vargas vs. Luther Savage CJ Gates was awarded the victory last week by Ari Chase, but it was a very short lived victory as Eric Evans came out to make the sides even, announcing that both parties fell off the Scaffold at the same time thus ending the match in a draw. Ari wasn‘t thrilled with the move by Eric Evans, he also wasn‘t thrilled with the main event Eric booked putting the One Shot champion CJ Gates against Chad Vargas and the number one contender for the One Shot Luther Savage in a epic Three-way dance for the championship.
A vampire vs. A Clown Hallow Wicked vs. Jinx Hallow Wicked has been on a tear, looking in prime shape going into Season Beatings. Some say Hallow Wicked hasn‘t looked this good since Barley Legal Wrestling Alliance. This week on the eve of Season Beatings he takes on the mentally broken Jinx. In what should be a classic showdown between two of Unplugged‘s best.
Last Chance for Psycho Circus Death Match Porter MaCleod vs. Troy Gafgen vs. Twilight Rayn The Prime Time Champion Porter MaCleod has been on fire as of late, and has become the longest running Prime Time champion in the short history of Unplugged, this week he takes on a man he already defeated last week on Parental Advisory, Troy Gafgen. But this time the Prime Time Championship isn’t on the line and there not the only two in the match as Twilight Rayn is also in the match. All three men fighting for the last spot in the Psycho Circus Death Match at Season Beatings.
Taking out the Trash Mason Dixon vs. Joey Bohnny After the bloody attack on Joey Bohnny, Mason Dixon looks to make his in ring debut taking out the sorry ass Elvis.
He Returns Drake Daniels vs. Chris Caliber vs. Jay Copeland Drake Daniels returns to the land of Unplugged, with a new out look on Wrestling in general and looks to send the team of Elite packing at the same time grabbing himself a spot in the Circus Death Match.
