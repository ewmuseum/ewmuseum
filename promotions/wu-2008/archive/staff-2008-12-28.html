WU Owner: Jayson Burrows Contact Info: MSN: [detail withheld] AOL: [detail withheld] BIO: Jayson Burrows was never a wrestler. He always dreamed about being a wrestler but could never really take a good bump. So he got into the promotion game. He started out working for CEO of Barely Legal Wrestling Alliance Johnny Hall. It was long before Jayson was running the show, and even merged two other promotions with BLW in JWF and RW. But Jayson wanted to branch out on his own, he felt BLW had lived up to its name and it was time to let the place go, and he did and after a week of running GCW, Jayson branched out and created Wrestling Unplugged
WU Co-Owner: Eric Evans Contact Info: MSN: [detail withheld] BIO: He received a call from Jayson Burrows, who was trying to find Requiem. Double E sought out Requiem, and together the two returned to Jayson Burrows Wrestling Unplugged, where Evans went on to manage Requiem to a world championship. Double E would eventually turn on Requiem, leaving the Plague of Karnage to join up with long time friend Johnny Hall. Before this, Burrows had made Evans the co-owner of the company. Would Burrows have done that had he known Evans would turn on him just a day later? Perhaps not, but we’ll never know.
WU Commissioner: Chris Icon Contact Info: AIM: [detail withheld] BIO: Please Come Again WU Director of Operations: Johnny Hall Contact Info: MSN: SEE BURROWS AOL: SEE BURROWS BIO: Johnny Hall came into the wrestling world as the owner of BLWA, a promotion many said wouldn't last two months, It lasted 11. And it the eyes of many could have lasted even longer. But Johnny Hall ran into a few troubles, add the stress of running a promotion and trying to keep a postive friendship with Tyler Sparks (his partner in BLWA) Hall felt it was better to close BLWA and he did. Since BLWA closing Hall has gone MIA from the world of wrestling. Which makes his return to WU a surprise. But what isn't a surprise is the hostal feelings towards WU Owner Jayson Burrows, as Jayson bought the rights to BLWA, and drove it into the ground under the name BLW
