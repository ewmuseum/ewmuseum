National Wrestling League FAQ v.1.0

What's the purpose of NWL? First things first, NWL is a play by mail fantasy wrestling RPG and NWL stands for National Wrestling League. The NWL's purpose is to simulate real-life events, the NWL is not a large company and is often looked at as the ECW of old. However, they do travel all over the country performing events. The NWL has a hardcore element to it, but we still shoot for realism. NWL is open to any kind of wrestler. Although, I would like a diverse group of wrestlers from Canada, United States, and International. The roster cap will be about 20 singles with a few tag teams. 2. Who runs this thing? (Fantasy) The NWL is formed from the finacial backers of three now defunct federations. The IWC, UWC, and CEW have combined to form the NWL and the backers have instilled their faith into William Race. Race is a 42 year old man who wants nothing in the world but to succeed. With a large backing of finacial investors, William announced NWL's arrival for August 01, 2003. William is a heavy set middle-aged man. With blackish gray, semi-balding hair. On camera he's usually clad in a business suit or casual Polo shirt and khakis. 3. Who runs this thing? (IRL) 3.1. [name withheld] The NWL is ran by me! [name withheld], I've handled plenty of characters around the RSPWF circle and have participated in plenty of feds around it as well. I know what it takes to run a successful promotion and am willing to put forth the effort to do so. 3.1.1. How can I reach you? Catch me on e-mail at [detail withheld] or on AIM at [detail withheld]. 4. What are the titles here? 4.1. NWL Triple Crown The NWL stems from three now defunct federations. IWC, UWC, and CEW's main championships have been combined to form the Triple Crown for NWL. This is the main belt, if you hold it your above all others. 4.2. NWL International The International title is secondary belt, who's purpose is to elevate the status of current stars to another level. Holding this belt makes you the number one contender for the Triple Crown. 4.3. NWL Tag Team Your standard tag team titles, these are often overlooked in other federations, but the NWL will be putting some hard work into making a successful tag division. 5. What are the cards in NWL? 5.1. National Exposure National Exposure is our full play by play event held from a different location bi-weekly. NE is aired on ESPN2 on late Tuesday, ESPN is currently testing the ratings before they decide on a weekly format. This is our main show, hosted by Louis Eckle. 5.2. NWL Cheap Shots Cheap Shots are sent to NWL-Wrestling@yahoogroups.com and are released on Tues. during the off weeks. 5.3. NWL Newsline This is our weekly update packet, giving you results, line-ups, news and rumors, and all sorts of other helpful information. 5.4. Pay Per Views NWL does four pay-per-views a month. With us being bi-weekly you must allow sufficient time to build up a pay-per-view. One every three months seems pretty reasonable. 5.5. Normal Schedule 1st week's Tues. - NWL National Exposure 2nd week's Tues. - NWL Cheap Shots 3rd week's Tues. - NWL National Exposure 4th week's Tues. - NWL Cheap Shots 6. Who are the staff members? 6.1. Front Office 6.1.1. William Race Well respected 42 year old man, known for getting the job done. Owns and runs NWL. 6.1.2. David Titan David Titan is the acting commissioner, he hands out punishment as he see's fit and is Race's right hand man. 6.2. Announcers 6.2.1. Louis Eckle Currently Louis Eckle is the only man on NWL's commentating team. He provides play by play as well as color. He prides himself on his vast knowledge of wrestling holds and moves. 6.3. Interviewers 6.3.1. Tim Mackoney Tim Mackoney is NWL's senior writer and interviewer, he's often on the recieving end of punishment from asking the tough or out of line questions. 6.3.2. Vanessa Smith Vanessa is your dumber than dirt interviewer, she was only hired for T&A, wrestlers are often found gawking at her, rather than answering her questions. 6.4. Referees 6.4.1. Frank Petrowski Petrowski is an honorable referee, who calls things as fairly as possible. 6.4.2. Jason Lovitz Jason is Dan Lovitz son, anyone who knew Dan Lovitz from the old CEW would say Jason is just as bit of a trouble maker as him. 6.4.3. Luke Hamock Luke is a young go-getter of a referee. Often times he finds himself right in the middle of the action, or worse laid out on the canvas. 6.5. Ringside Crew 6.5.1. Juan "The Mouth" Havier Juan Havier is our glitz and glamour of a ring announcer, he introduces the wrestlers on large events. 6.5.2. James Piper James Piper is head of security, he's the one who leads the charge in breaking up the backstage or in-ring chaos. 7. What do I have to do in NWL? 7.1. Roleplaying Roleplays are graded on a few things, length is not one of them. Neither is quantity, if you can get your point across in two paragraphs so be it. If it takes two pages, so be it as long as it's entertaining. You can roleplay as many times as you want for any event, but only two will actually be graded upon when determining your match. On-card segments count as a roleplay, I actually prefer live segments, rather than Cheap Shot roleplays but I grade them evenly. The following are our standard roleplay rules. - SWEARING: If Stone Cold can say it, so can you. But aside from damn, ass, etc; any other curse word should be covered with a [CENSORED] or something of that sort. - NO ATTACKS: No physical attacks on announcers/reporters/staff are allowed without the permission of the powers-that-be... a.k.a. me. If you do not ask for permission your attack won't run. - KAYFABE: No mention of real wrestlers will be allowed... But other e-wrestlers and feds may be mentioned in your RPs IF they allow you too. Also, please keep the mention of "outside-NWL" things to a minimum. It's fine to know that you gained the XYZ World belt, but we shouldn't remember it at every RP! I wouldn't want any of you saying that "EMWC sucks" or "Rosendorf's a coward" in NWL cards. Go say that in THEIR cards. - FORMAT: NWL DOES HAVE A SPECIFIC FORMAT. It's similar to that of GPW's. It goes like this: Your wrestlers last name in all caps go inside of[ ]: and all actions go inside of brackets. Example: [name withheld] would be [BLAIR]:. BLAIR would be followed by a : Then another space and writing. Action goes in [ these ]. Simple enough. Here's another example: [The camera pans around and finds [name withheld].] [BLAIR]: I'll kill all comers This format is only for NWL events, as for Cheap Shots you can use any format you like. 7.2. Strategies Strategies also count towards your grading. They should consist of pertinent information. A few spots, your entrance, and a general idea of where you'd like to go in the match, the ending, etc; Please note, that just because you send in a strategy doesn't mean everything inside it will get used. The important factors will, but please don't be upset is somethings are left off. 7.3. Angles/Feuds The NWL works on the fly, which means: you'll have a general direction for where your going, a partner to work with, and the rest comes naturally. Not every aspect will be revealed to you as a handler, as I like to keep some surprises to myself. E-feds are boring unless they have excitement, and planning things out every step of the way gets rather boring. Which is why I say the NWL works on the fly... 8. Do you need help? Can I help? I am always looking for help with writing. Full play by play is hard to do at the quality that we do it. If your interested in helping just let me know via e-mail at [detail withheld]. I am also always looking for new characters, if you have any friends referring them to us would be very helpful. 9. Okay, I gotta few questions... 9.1. What kind of wrestlers can enter NWL? Any and all sorts. Wrestlers should be kept realistic, but the more creative the better. 9.2. Can I enter a single and tag team? Or two singles and them combine them as a tag team? NOPE: NWL is restricted to one "personality" either one singles wrestler or one tag team. 9.3. Why does this fed have only a bi-weekly card? That sucks! I'd rather have bi-weekly cards that come out on time and have alot more work put into them, then have two cards a week never out on time and sloppily done. 9.4. Can I enter my BIG NAME, 10 time World champ in NWL? I prefer not. Remember that this is an indy fed... And a BIG name would probably make booking decisions more difficult. 10. Alright, I wanna join what do I do? 10.1 All you gotta do is copy and paste the application below into an e-mail and fill it out as is. You'll recieve a reply within a few days letting you know wether your in or not. If I don't know you too well, the most important thing on this application is the sample roleplay, leave it off and your application will be deleted.
