An email-based heavyweight-singles e-wrestling league founded 2 July 1995, expanding to a World and National title structure within weeks and remaining a recognized part of the community's own fed landscape into the following January, where independent fan surveys ranked it #17 and #22 on two informal Top-20/Top-30 lists. Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

EGPW began as a short recruitment note posted to rec.sport.pro-wrestling.fantasy on 2 July 1995 by an operator who signed only as “JJ,” already holding six to eight interested entrants and looking for about ten more before starting an e-mail-based heavyweight singles league.

By 14 July JJ was ready to fill the EGPW World Heavyweight Championship in a tournament the following week, and pushed for new entrants to sign up before the weekend was out. He noted the promotion's own heel-to-face ratio ran about two to one, and asked specifically for more faces to balance it.

EGPW went on to run at least one dated card that August, a “Weekly Wallop” show corroborated by a contemporary e-zine, and was still being named among the community's own active feds as late as New Year's Day 1996, when an informal fan ranking placed it twenty-second of a Top 30 — six months on from its founding, after an earlier ranking that December had it at seventeenth. No closure notice of its own survives, and how or when it ended is not in the surviving record.
