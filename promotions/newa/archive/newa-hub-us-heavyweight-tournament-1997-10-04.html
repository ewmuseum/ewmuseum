[ESW] NeWA United States Heavyweight Tournament, 4 October 1997

4 October 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Sunday 10/4/97, Uniondale, NY, 8:00 pm Card Held at the Nassau Coliseum. Empire State Wrestling presents The National eWresting Alliance's: United States Heavyweight Championship Tournament: Wrestlers from 3 NeWA promotions plus some "non aligned" grapplers will battle it out for this prestegious championship. Round 1: ESW's Bernie Roth vs. Barry Horowitz ESW's Slim Dragger vs APW's "Hot Rod" Johnny Blayze ESW's Brian Mitchell vs. CWA's Awesome Al ESW's Skip Lylac vs. Pat Tanaka ESW's Samuel Stephenson vs Alex Porteau ESW's Gary Morre vs. APW's Action Jackson ESW's Jimmy Anderson vs CWA's Rick Steamboat CWA's Dory Funk Jr. vs. CWA's Lance Von Erich. Visit the Official Empire State Wrestling Webpage for more details on this and many other events. http://www.geocities.com/Colosseum/Loge/9033/
