Ace In The Hole Special: The Year That Was 1997, 14 December 1997

14 December 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Cut back to the studio with Talson, Dack, Foss and Tiger.) (Dack Foren has obtained a bottle of Acerade during the break.) Dack: That's right folks, Acerade, the greatest drink ever made! The drink of champions! The drink that helps you think the drink... (Foss reaches over and grabs the bottle from Dack and pours it all over him. Dack gets all mad and stoms off the set.) Talson: That was overkill. Smooth move, Foss. Maybe we'll get lucky and he won't return. Tiger: Maybe he'll get hit with a truck and he won't return. Talson: Um, Tiger, if he gets hit with a truck, I know he won't return. That's redundant. Tiger: Maybe you will get hit with a truck. Foss: I thought we were going to talk about the NEWA. Talson: Yes. We are. At the time the WWA was starting to heat up, Allen Carter joined up with the NEWA and was the first wrestler to sign up! Day by day, Allen Carter watched as the ranks grew! Foss: Who knew that a man named The Reverend [name withheld] would change the life of Allen Carter? Tiger: The Acer and The Nobles hooked up with the Rev and a few others and the Old World Order was formed. Talson: Let's not forget Grave Digger or Iorn Spike! Tiger: Or Miss Gray! Hubba-hubba! The Acer knows his women! Hey, did I ever tell you that I spent the night with Becky LaRue from the IIWF? Dack (From offstage): Who hasn't? Talson and Foss: We haven't! Talson: How on earth, Dack Foren, did you get Becky to... Tiger: (Finishing for Talson) Do the nasty? Dack: (Sitting back in his seat with a fresh change of clothes) At a party. She was drunk and I said I was a wrestler. (Suddenly his face turns white) God, I hope she isn't watching. Tiger: I hope she is. Hey Becky! I'll be there along with the sailors! Talson: Please. Control yourselves. Anyone remember The Acer's debut in the NEWA? Foss: A thirty second loss to Jules Winnfield. How could anyone forget? The Acer won out in the long run. Tiger: He won several months later after a long fued with Winnfield's stable. He beat Winnfield in a loser leaves match. He ended Winnfield's career. Not much to boast about though. The fed closed down the next week. Foss: It was a moral victory, Tiger. Also The Nobles, the jobbers of the WWA at the time, tasted gold in the NEWA for the first time ever! Talson: That's right! Jack Diamond and The King With a Club took the belts from The Hate Machine and held on for a month before losing them to Midnight Rampage! Tiger: Remember the time Miss Gray and Winnfield's manager got into a tumble? Dack: Mia Wallace. Miss Gray won by slamming Wallace into a toliet bowl. Tiger: Too bad she never flushed it! HA! Talson: And the other time The Nobles stole the belts from the Black Jacks? Tiger: Should not have show another efed's belts in the NEWA. No Class. Dack: The OWO melted the belts down for rings for all the ladies of the OWO! A Classic! Genius, Mr, Carter... (Foss pushes Dack's chair over until he falls from it.) Tiger: Thanks Foss. Well the NEWA came and went. There are three people whom awards go to. Talson: To [name withheld] for forming the NEWA. You have been awarded a 1997 Acer Excellence Award! Tiger: Another one goes out to [name withheld] for the OWO idea and all his work. Congrats Jake! Foss: And last but not by any means least, [name withheld]!For his work in the NEWA and the OWO. Congrats and next up...The Acer's first gold in the VFWF. to be continued... copyright 1997, Ace In The Hole Productions (Scene opens up in The Ace In Hole studio. "Stars And Stripes Forever" by John Phillip Sousa plays in the backround. As the lights come on, we see four figures in silk Ace In The Hole blazers. The lights come on and the shadowy figures...come to life.) Talson Geddy: The year that was in e-wrestling. The year of Allen "The Acer" Carter. (Talson Geddy, THE strength and founding member of the AITH broadcast team is confidence. Out of all of the members he alone has stuck with The Acer from early on till now. He looks as if he was always meant to be there.) Dack Foren: That's right Mr. Geddy! I'm privileged to be here with all of you and thanks to Mr. Carter's kind... (Dack Foren. Intern and expert suck up. Charmed his way into the AITH crew by..."Charming" Allen Carter's Exec. Asst.) Foss Setlingar: Stow it please you incessant suck up! We're here to look at the first part of a career. And what a first part it was! (Foss Setlingar. A gentleman's champion and face all the way. 3-time former world champ.) John "Tiger" Riggins: You got that right Foss! (Slaps Foss on the back, just a bit to hard.) Man, and 1998 is going to be great! (John "Tiger" Riggins. His heroes are Traci Lords, Stone Cold Steve Austin and Steve "Soundbite" Roberts. Was arrested once arrested for indecent exposure along with Sunny. Nuff said.) Foss: Gee, did you think that rhyme up all by your self? Probably took you all night. Talson: Ahem! The career of Allen Carter started off with a whimper. Carter was, in Dec 96-Jan 96 accepted into the ranks of the WWO, WIF and WWA. Foss: The WWA under President Bob Roberts in case there is a mix up. Tiger: Yeah, but our boss got tossed from the WWO and the WIF! HA! What a way to start! Talson: All very true. Even in the WWA Allen Carter was bushwacked in his very first match against the Widowmaker. Scorpion came down to the ring and DDT Acer while he was thrown on the outside. The Acer lost the match. Tiger: And that caused the formation of The Royal Court which was the force to be dealt with in the WWA. The Acer did get belts for just about everyone in his stable... Dack: By ousting and demoting President Bob Roberts to VP, The Acer, in a sheer stroke of genius... Talson, Tiger and Foss: SHUT UP! Talson: The Acer did by out the WWA! He became president and...created a title and awarded to himself! He also stripped all the other champions and gave the belts to his wrestlers. Foss: First time I've heard of using politics to win belts. Dack: It was a major bummer when the fed shut down. The Acer's first efed just faded out. Foss: True, but Allen Carter wants Bob Roberts to know that he had won...an 1997 Acer Award! Thanks to Mr. Roberts and all of the wrestlers in the WWA! Talson: We'll be back with a look at The Acer and the NEWA. To be continued... Copyright 1997, Ace In The Hole Productions
