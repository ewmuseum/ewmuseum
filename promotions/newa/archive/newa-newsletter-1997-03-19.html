**************************** NEWA NEWSLETTER 3/19/97 **************************** ******************* LATEST NEWS ******************* -----WRESTLE WAR Wrestle War is are first Pay-Per-View. It will be held LIVE, April 6th in Salem-Winston, NC. MATCHES SIGNED : -- United States Title Match inside a Steel Cage -- (Champion) (Challenger) Mr. Irrestible vs Outlaw Justice --World Tag Team Titles Match inside a Steel Cage-- (Champions) (Challengers) Hate Machine vs The Nobles ****MORE MATCHES TO BE ANNOUNCED** ---- THE REVEREND AND THE NOBLES ??? We have no idea what is going on with these three guys. It all started two weeks ago when The Nobles came down to the ring and confronted The Reverend. No words were spoken and they didn't even blink an eye. Then all three men turn around and leave the ring together. The same thing happened this week only this time The Reverend was the one who came down to the ring. What's going on ? Are these three guys going to join forces ? I HAVE NO IDEA !!! ---- THOSE WNWA US TITLES !!! When Donovan Black and BlackJack showed those WNWA US Titles on Mat Wars this week they started a fire in every person in the NEWA. President White went on record saying that "I will NOT stand for this show of disrespect" I think if Donovan Black and BlackJack are so tough why don't they put those WNWA US Titles on the line at Wrestle War !!! ---- THE ACER'S ATTACK ON JULES WINNFIELD !!! This Saturday Night The Acer attcked Jules Winnfield after he pinned "Shotgun" Sammy Cooper to advacnce to the 2nd round of the Television Title Tournament. Jules Winnfield was on his way back to the locker room, when The Acer appeared out of no where and hit him with a steel chair. After Jules Winnfield fell to the floor, The Acer then hit him on the leg with the chair. We understand that Winnfeild's leg is hurt, but he claims he can still wrestle Chameleon Saturday in the 2nd round of the Tournament. ---- "MADDOG" GNORT AND OUTLAW JUSTICE !!! What's going on here ?!?!? I mean "Maddog" Gnort comes to the ring to shake Outlaw Justice's hand and Justice choke slams him. The same thing has happened twice now !! I think "Maddog" Gnort should have figured out that Outlaw Justice, just doesn't like him !!! ************* Champions ************* World Heavyweight Champion : Psycho Stud United States Champion : Mr. Irrestible World Tag Team Champions : Hate Machine ************** RANKINGS ************** Singles : 1.
