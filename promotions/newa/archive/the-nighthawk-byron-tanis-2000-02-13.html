NEWA — Roleplay

[NeWA] "The Nighthawk" Byron Tanis. Read, discuss.

13 February 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Again, forgive the HTML. Tanis is my other character in the other alliance in our little fantasy world. In any event, he's the current World champ. This post doesn't much pertain to any opponent, as I don't think I had one. It's called "Twenty-nine".
Tanis finished at #20 on that RSPWF list. [It's that sound you make when you step into a full bathtub that greets the darkness. It lingers. The darkness gives way - here comes the fade up.]
<CENTER><IMG SRC= http://www.hobartcity.com.au/_slides/images/nightvw.jpg ></CENTER> [This indescribable view moves from right to left, as if something was powering the camera behind it all. The city is generic in every sense of the word, but it grabs the eye with rising buildings and varying color. Moving water is still audible, but with the motion of the camera, it makes us wonder whether or not it's actually something moving through the water, rather than the water itself, that brings this sound.]
[Curiosity wills the smooth fade to the gliding yacht, skiing over still waters. Its cabin lights up the night, and shadows can be seen moving to and fro within it. The city, too, helps cast the spotlight on this lone party boat, moving through the otherwise clouded and dreary night. As far as yachts go, this one certainly is bigger than most, stretching a few hundred feet from bow to bow. With the party ongoing inside that cabin and us way out on the high seas, again it's the yearning for the camera to oblige us that comes through.]
[And, once again, it does. Presumably, it's the inside of the cabin now, with expensive furniture and expensive women gallivanting about. Alcohol flows like water and laughs ring out at a disturbing rate. Oh, it's a party - the kind we're always looking for. Women, booze, laughs, leather, music, and women. A slower song, with a rhythmic beat, takes hold of the party and men find their women - couples taking over. This act manages to highlight one particular tall vixen with long shimmering brown hair and crystalline blue-green eyes (and a really cool red dress too). She looks left, then right. A man in a cheap suit
(who looks a lot like Boaz Sity. Weird, huh?) approaches her, but she blows him off and glides toward the door of the cabin and something else that caught her eye.]
[It's a figure, standing out on the deck, leaning over the rail lazily. The woman has joined him now, leaving the chaos of an ongoing party. Her heels click on the deck as she walks, surely giving away her presence, but whoever it is doesn't bother to turn around. It's a man, quite obviously, standing out like a sore thumb in a faded black leather jacket, and tattered old blue jeans.
His face is still unseen as she comes to rest beside him.] Woman: [softly] Beautiful, isn't it? [The city is on screen again, its lights visible above and below sea level. The sky has darkened considerably since we saw it last, but the pink haze is still there - if you look. The response comes; a gruff, familiar voice.]
Tanis: No. Not really. [As his words come, his face is on screen, attractive and powerful. His half-smile is vacant though, leaving a stoic, alien feel to the Nighthawk. And there he stands, gazing upon water, when a ten of a woman stands not two feet to his left. But it's the gleaming city that is on the screen as her soft voice purrs on.]
Woman: Gosh, it's cold out here. [She pauses, and her smooth face has replaced the city lights.] Woman: [extending a slender hand] I'm Mary. I didn't see you inside. [Byron glances in her direction, looking over her frame. He shakes her hand, but turns back to the water in the process.]
Tanis: I wasn't inside. Mary: [smiling] Well, why don't you come in for awhile? Dance with me? Tanis: [turning on her now] Why? So I can get you drunk and [bleep] you? Then you can go home and tell all your friends how you flew with the Nighthawk? [The woman takes a step back, bringing a hand to her mouth in shock. Tanis only rolls his eyes, bringing his right arm above the railing for the first time to take a swig out of a green bottle.]
Mary: <b>Excuse</b> me? Just who do you think you are? Tanis: [pausing] You don't know who I am? Mary: No, of course not. I just saw a guy out here alone. [Tanis drinks, then glares at her with hard eyes.] Tanis: And what do you see now? Mary: Uhm, listen, I'm sorry I disturbed you. [She turns to leave, but Byron calls after her, his breath clouding in front of him.] Tanis: I'm going to die, you know. Mary: [turning] What? Tanis: Mary, right? Mary: [slowly] Right. [Tanis forces up a smile and she melts. He can be downright charming when he wants to be it seems.]
Tanis: [leaning his back against the rail] I'm Byron Tanis. [The woman in the red dress comes back to him with small steps, rubbing her hands together to keep her sleeveless arms warm.]
Mary: Excuse me for asking, but I've never seen a, well, white guy named Byron. Tanis: [laughing] My father's best friend was killed in 'Nam - hence the name. Mary: Do you get asked that a lot? Tanis: Nah. It's mostly just comments behind my back, in the dirtsheets, that sort of thing.
[Somewhere in the distance, the clouds mumble their anger in the form of echoing thunder. Both Byron and Mary glance up briefly before leveling their gazes.]
Mary: So you're famous, huh? Tanis: You could say that. Mary: I'm not much for pop culture. [Another clap of thunder - this one much closer. It seems to light a bulb above Mary's head.]
Mary: What did you say about dying before? Tanis: [sighing] I'm only getting older. Each day, I start to feel it now a bit more. Old enemies keep coming back. I beat them and I beat them and I beat them, but they don't stop coming.
Mary: [compassionate] I'm afraid I don't follow. Tanis: None of us live forever. I'm twenty-nine tonight. Mary: Well, Happy Birthd - Tanis: [to himself more than anything] And I could feel death when Venom had me in that room. I could <b>feel</b> it.
[More angry rumbling from the Gods.] Mary: What are you? Tanis: [looking out at the mirror-like water with glassy eyes] I'm just a man - with powerful friends . . . and powerful enemies. Mary: I meant what do you do for a - Tanis: [cutting her off with something totally off the subject] I used to love the water. Kids in Calgary look forward to the winter so they can play hockey, but not me. No, I liked going up to the lake and skipping rocks. All day long.
Mary: [reaching for his attention] I love water too. [Byron holds his dark green bottle of liquor up and looks at it - almost sadly.] Tanis: I said I <b>used</b> to. [He drinks.] Mary: What happened? Tanis: I fell in love with myself. [He smiles tightly, finishing off the bottle. Without even a thought, he tosses it overboard, watching with wry amusement as it smashes on the water's surface. Mary isn't so amused.]
Mary: So you're just gonna stand out in the cold on your birthday on someone else's boat and feel sorry for yourself?
Tanis: Wrong. Mary: What was wrong? Tanis: This is my boat. [Mary straightens, her expression tightening. Anger doesn't suit her beauty very well.] Mary: Fine, if that's how you're gonna be. It was [sarcastically] nice meeting you. [She stalks off, her heels once again ringing out over the night. A few steps from the door to the cabin, she turns back once more.]
Mary: You really are an asshole, you know that? [And she's gone, slipping back into the party where the Boaz look-alike has been waiting for her. On the deck, leaning on the rail, Tanis smiles.]
Tanis: [whispering] I know. [One last thunder clap opens the skies and the rain is quick to take advantage. The intensity builds and soon it's just the <a href=" http://www.weblust.com/sounds/rain.wav ">rain</a>, filling our ears and dominating the audio. The Nighthawk doesn't stir, holding his place on that rail, looking out over the night. His smile is gone now as the downpour darkens his hair.]
[His right hand retrieves a set of stylish sunglasses, placing them over a set of tired eyes. Tanis likes looking at the world through tinted glass.]
[And, just over the downpour, the former World champion can be heard one last time - humming the theme "Happy Birthday".] [Silence. Darkness.] [name withheld] -Bye.
