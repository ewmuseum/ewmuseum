*********** NEWA Newsletter ************ **************************** DATE : March 25, 1997 **************************** ******************** LATEST NEWS ******************* ----- NEW UNITED STATES CHAMPION !!! "Shotgun" Sammy Cooper shocked the world Saturday night at Mat Wars when he pinned Mr. Irrestible for the United States Championship. This was a strange match to say the least, Mr. Irrestible was first DQ when Grand Wizard of Funk interfered in the mat. Then President White came out and re-started the match again. The match then ended when "Shotgun" Sammy Cooper caught Mr. Irrestible in a small package. ----- THE REVEREND ???? Thing got strange at the last edition of Mat Wars during The Reverend and Robbie "HITT" Mann match. It started when The Reverend was a announced, the light turned bright white which blinded everyone in the arena. When the light did return to normal The Reverend was behind Robbie. The Reverend looked as if he hadn't shaved in a week and his eyes were bloodshot. The Reverend then attacked Robbie. The match ended when Robbie pinned The Reverend, but the madness didn't stop there, The Reverend attacked Robbie again knocking him out then he tied Robbie up in the ropes and painted a big White Cross on Robbie's chest !!!! What't up with the Reverend ??? Fans, I have not idea !!! ----- THE ACER AND JULES WINNFILED FUED !!! The Acer and Jules Winnfield fued took as different turn at the last edition of Mat Wars when Mia Wallace attacked Miss Gray during The Acer's match. The women fought all over the arena which ended when Miss Gray rammed Mia Wallace's head into one of the toilet in the ladies restroom.Then later that night during Jules Winnfield and Chameleon's match, which has a Television title tournament match, The Acer came out and hit Jules in the back with a steel chair causing him to lose the match. ----- THE LETTER O ??? Fans, I don't know anything about this. All I know is that Scott Hadley and Mike Ousley received a old scroll with the letter O written in gothic sytle. That's all I know. ----- THOSE SIX MEN IN THE RAFTERS ???
