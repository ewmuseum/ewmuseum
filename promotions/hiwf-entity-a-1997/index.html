A January 1997 HIWF that publicly retires its own name three words into its only surviving post -- 'Since everyone seems to think the name is stupid' -- and in the same breath publishes a ten-title structure with two crowning tournaments. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

This HIWF renamed itself inside the only post that survives of it, dated 23 January 1997: “Since everyone seems to think the name is stupid..It is now Hardcore Internet Wrestling Federation.” No document of the name it carried before survives to say what that name was.

The post announced ten titles at once — World, National, All-Time, and tag-team counterparts of each, a 3-man Tag Team belt, and two Hardcore championships — to be settled in a single night: a tournament crowning a “TOUGH MAN” as World Champion, a parallel tag tournament, two Battle Royals for the All-Time belts, and a no-disqualification three-team elimination tag match, “THE MATCH FROM HELL,” for the 3-man belts.

It allowed real wrestlers on its roster so long as they were heavily roleplayed — “You can have REAL wrestlers but you have to roleplay ALOT STILL!!” — and closed its pitch with “GROWING IN POPULARITY ALREADY!!!”

No roster, card result, or title change of any kind survives beyond that single announcement. Whether the ten-title tournament night this HIWF planned ever happened isn't in the surviving record.
