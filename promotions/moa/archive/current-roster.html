current roster The following are the men and women who continually fight to keep the MoA the dominating force that it has always been. As long as they continue to follow the Way, MoA dominance is ensured in their respective federations. PWA DIVISION Darren Ridel (Leader) Height: 6' 2" Weight: 230 lbs. Hometown: Los Angeles, California Style: Aerial Technician Theme Music: (weekly shows) "Master of Puppets" by Metallica - (pay per views) "Hate Me" by Tantric Trademark Move: Sweet Dreams Kick ---------- Pinwheel Kick Finishing Maneuver: "For the Glory" Frogsplash ---------- Modified High Impact Frogsplash - Ridel is one of the most devoted followers of Styx's way. He follows orders without question, and his loyalty is unrivaled. Currently the General of the MoA's PWA division, he feels that only by dominating this federation can he prove himself to not only Styx, but to his former friend and rival Abunai. Nightstryker - Bryce Knight Height: 6' 4" Weight: 254 lbs. Hometown: Chicago, Illinois Style: Technician Theme Music: "Thunderstruck" by AC/DC Finishing Maneuver: Mindbender ---------- Sit-down Pildriver - Nightstryker has been with the MoA's PWA faction for some time, and is currently one of its most loyal followers. A 3-time IC Champion, he is Ridel's right-hand man, and is undergoing training with Ridel's best friend, Jason Creed, in order to sharpen his skills. Gabrielle Height: 6' 0" Weight: 135 lbs. Hometown: New York City, New York Style: Luchadore Entrance Theme: "After the Flesh" by My Life With the Thrill Kill Kult Finishing Maneuver: Waking Nightmare ---------- Pinwheel Kick - Gabrielle is the daughter of Styx, though she dislikes her father greatly for the horrifying childhood she was forced to endure. He found her little over a year ago working in a grocery store, and offered her a position within the Masters. Silverback Height: 6' 2" Weight: 230 lbs. Hometown: Unknown Style: All-Rounder Entrance Theme: "Destroying Angel" by HT Finishing Maneuver: The Silver Lock ---------- Ankle-Lock Submission - Silverback lives within the body of his brother, McDee. Trapped within McDee's mind since childbirth, he was eventually able to overthrow his other half and take control of their body. He now follows the Masters as a way to wreak havoc on the PWA. The Rage - Brian Anderson Height: 6' 7" Weight: 255 lvs. Hometown: Chicago, Illinois Style: Hardcore Entrance Theme: "Fear" by Disturbed Finishing Maneuver: The Breakdown ---------- Front Face Lock into a Piledriver - The Rage joined the MoA when he turned on his former friend Sirus Moran. He isn't extremely loyal or trustworthy, but is a fearsome competitor within the ring. His alliance with the Masters is mutually beneficial, as he helps enforce their dominance in the PWA. Marcus Collins Height: 6' 6" Weight: 260 lbs. Hometown: Santa Clara, California Style: Aerial with Martial Arts Entrance Theme: "Nothing Man" by Pearl Jam Finishing Maneuver: Daybreak ---------- Cresent Kick - Marcus Collins fights for the MoA as its slave. He was beaten by Darren Ridel at the PWA's Battledome, and as a result is forced to do his bidding. He despises the MoA with a passion, but as of right now is powerless to do anything to stop them. Phoenix - Nicholas Ridel Height: 6' 6" Weight: Approx. 260 lbs. (hasn't been weighed in) Hometown: Los Angeles, California Style: Brawler Entrance Theme: None Finishing Maneuver: None - Phoenix is the older brother of Darren Ridel. Though he hates his brother, they have reached a compromise. Nick serves the MoA, and in returns receives a very comfortable paycheck. He currently has no wrestling contract, but accompanies his brother to the ring. The Brutal Height: 6' 8" Weight: 320 lbs. Hometown: Unknown Style: Brawler Entrance Theme: Loud Drum Beats Finishing Maneuver: None - The Brutal is Gabrielle's tag-team partner, and roommate. He never speaks, and his face is almost always concealed behind a chrome plated mask. One of the most powerful men in the sport, he uses shear brute strength to dismember anyone in his path. UW DIVISION Jamie Williams (Leader - Heir to the Future) Height: 6' 0" Weight: 225 lbs. Hometown: Boston, Massachusetts Style: Aerial/Technical/Brawler Entrance Theme: (entrance) "Never Die" by Creed - (exit) "Oh My God" by Guns 'N Roses Finishing Maneuver: Future's Wrath ---------- Modified Shooting Star Press - Jamie represents the future of the MoA. He is and always will be MoA at heart. He is a deeply thoughtful yet fully confident young man. He’s still young barely in his prime and ready to take on the world. He can, and he will. He is Styx’s heir to the future, the heir to Styx’s throne. Frost - Angelica Roberts Height: 5' 8" Weight: 135 lbs. Hometown: New York City, New York Style: Aerial Technician Entrance Theme: "Janie's Got a Gun" by Aerosmith Finishing Maneuver: Polaris ---------- Top Rope Fisherman Buster - The daughter of Kim Collins (nee Roberts) and Styx, Angelica is a rebellious teenager who feels she may have finally found a home. Angelica has become a good friend of her half-sister Gabrielle in the short time they've known each other.  She is new to the world of professional wrestling, although she has several years of street fighting experience behind her.  Although she is a 'rookie', she will undoubtedly prove herself to be truly lethal as well. Fley - Alex Faust Height: 6' 2" Weight: 230 lbs. Hometown: Claims to be New York Style: Hardcore with Martial Arts Entrance Theme: (entrance) "Cold Contagious" by Bush - (exit) "Machinehead" by Bush Finishing Maneuver: Afterclap ---------- Powerbomb into a Piledriver - The supposed and self proclaimed King O Extreme. He is the youngest Faust child. He didn’t know about his brothers’ doing in wrestling until he was visited by the oldest Faust, Jason. He was…taken to Styx and introduced into wrestling in the AXE. There, in very short order he took the Extreme title, and fights for the memory of his brother William Faust. The AOWF Hall of Famer known as, Flesh. Jason Creed Height: 6' 5" Weight: 310 lbs. Hometown: Las Vegas, Nevada Style: Brawler Entrance Theme: "Papercut" by Linkin Park Finishing Maneuver: the Endgame ---------- Inverted Death Valley Driver - The best friend of Darren Ridel, general of the M.o.A in the PWA, Creed owes his true loyalty to no one except him. He follows the MoA to help his friend, and wrestles to improve his skills so that one day the two of them can settle the score.
