WOWA Crowns A New Champion, 15 December 1997

15 December 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
**This is the WOWA title match...where they crown a new Champion. ==================== Flight of the Valkery plays ==================== Kent: MAKING HIS WAY DOWN THE AISLE...WEIGHING IN AT 259 POUNDS..AND STANDING AT 5 FOOT 9...HERE IS FOMER WORLD ONLINE WRESTLING ASSOCIATION CHAMPION....VILE VINCE VIPER!!!!!!! [Fans mostly boo as VVV comes out of the back. He is HEAVLY bandaged around the head. He goes to the ring and gets it.] Kent: AND HIS OPPONENT (The lights dim as a light drum roll can be heard.) =================================== "The Unforgiven" by Metallica starts up.... =================================== (Fans cheer as the ring explodes in a ball of fire.The lights flash back on,and "The Confessor" Rey Jeran marches solemnly to the ring.Jeran wears a hooded black sweatshirt,brown combat boots,and a pai of faded jeans.He walks to the ring,and lowers his his hood,revealing his brown hair that has been allowed to grow out.He rolls up his sleeves and stares unnervingly at Vile Vince Viper.He briefly glances at the fans,then tackles Viper and starts to pummel him with face punches) DING DING DING Mike: This match is on its way. VVV is heavily bandaged around the head. You cant see most of his face. Jeran is landing punch after punch! [Fans cheer] Vic: Jeran now picks up VVV and sends him to the ropes. VVV comes back and Jeran hits a clothesline!! VVV back up to his feet and Jeran charges and hits a Spinning Leg Lariat!!! Mike: Is VVV growing a little? Seems taller. Anyway...Jeran with more punches and gets VVV up to his feet. Jeran picks him up and sets him up. Jeran with a double underhook suplex and now off the ropes and hits a elbow to the side! Vic: Jeran picks up VVV and hits a chop and now sends him to the ropes. Jeran follows up. VVV jumps up to jump over Jeran but JERAN CATCHES HIM ON HIS SHOULDER!! Jeran turns around AND CHARGES AND HITS THE RUNNING POWERSLAM!! [Fans cheer] Mike: The World Champion is dominating so far. We also want to remind you that LOCO has the World Belt after he STOLE it last week. Jeran has the pin. 1 2 KICKOUT Vic: Jeran now gets up and waits till VVV is up. Jeran throws him to the ropes...Jeran off the other and Jeran and VVV go by each other..JERAN REACHES BACK AND HITS A REVERSE NECKBREAKER!! [Fans cheer] Mike: Both men down and Jeran now is moving and gets back to his feet. Jeran now off the ropes for a fistdrop..BUT VVV MOVES!!! VVV slowly back up to his feet and nails Jeran with some stiff shots to the face. VVV throws him to the corner and charges with a HARD CLOTHESLINE!! Vic: VVV now grabs Jeran by the eyes and starts clawing away. Jeran can barely see. VVV grabs his head and gets onto the second rope. VVV JUMPS AND HITS A SECOND ROPE BULLDOG!!! [Fans scream] Mike: Jeran is laying on his stomach NOT moving!! VVV now onto the top rope. And JUMPS AND LANDS A TOP ROPE LEGDROP TO THE BACK OF THE HEAD!!! VVV turns him over on his back and pins him! 1 2 3-KICKOUT [Fans cheer] Vic: That was close. VVV now gets Jeran up to his feet and grabs him by the hair. VVV takes him to the corner and SMASHES his face into the top turnbuckle. VVV with ANOTHER eye claw and the ref. telling him to let go. VVV has let go and is now CHOKING Jeran in the corner. Mike: VVV grabs him by the hair and FACESLAMS him into the mat. VVV now drops a knee to the head and takes a little breather! Jeran is back up to his feet and VVV charges and nails him with a kick to the stomach and now hits him with a DDT and a pin!! 1 2 3-SHOULDER UP Vic: VVV is yelling at the ref. And now gets Jeran up to his knees and now to his feet. VVV throws him to the ropes...Jeran comes back and DUCKS a Lariat and now turns around and hits a EUROPEAN UPPERCUT. [Fans scream] Mike: VVV is stunned and Jeran throws him to the ropes...Jeran off the other end and BOTH MEET IN THE MIDDLE WITH A DOUBLE CLOTHESLINE!!! Both men are now down and not moving. Vic: The ref. is up to 8 and both men are now getting to their feet. Jeran is up first and CHARGES at VVV...VVV HOOKS HIS HEAD AND HITS A FIREMANS BUSTER OUTTA NOWHERE!!! [Fans scream] Mike: Jeran is out and VVV up to the top again. VVV MEASURES UP AND JUMPS DOWN WITH A BIG KNEEDROP TO THE FACE!!! THAT HAD TO KNOCK HIM OUT!! [Fans boo] Vic: Jeran is holding his head and now VVV off the ropes and hits a back senton splash and a cover!! 1 2 3-KICKOUT Mike: VVV is really mad now and sits ontop of Jeran WHAILING AWAY WITH PUNCH AFTER PUNCH!!! The ref. yelling at him to get off of the World Champ. VVV PUSHES THE REF. DOWN!! The ref. is back up and warning VVV not to touch him. Vic: VVV picks up Jeran and whips him to the corner..NO..JERAN REVERSES IT AND WHIPS HIM BACK INTO THE SAME CORNER..THE REF. IS HIT....VVV SQUASHED THE REF. IN THE TURNBUCKLE AND VVV TURNS BACK AROUND AND JERAN JUMPS UP AND HITS A FRANKENSTIENER!! [Fans go nuts] Mike: The ref. is out and Jeran is back up to his feet. VVV is getting back up. VVV SWINGS..Jeran ducks and kicks him in the stomach. Jeran GRABS THE ARMS AND HITS A DOUBLE ARM DDT!!! Jeran covers but there is NO REF. Vic: Jeran slaps the mat and goes over to the ref. Jeran checking him out. Jeran now with an EVIL LOOk on his face! Mike: I dont like the looks of this. Jeran now HURLS VVV over the top rope. And Jeran staying in the ring. VVV is back up and is on the apron. Jeran charges AND NAILS VVV WITH A DROPKICK AND VVV IS CATAPULTED INTO THE GUARDRAIL!! [Fans cheer and scream] Vic: VVV has got to be hurt after that as he is dangling from the guardrail. Jeran now jumps over the top rope and onto the outside. Jeran now takes the steel steps from the corner. Jeran picks them up and goes over to where Viper is. Jeran SMASHES IT AGAINST VVVs HEAD!!! [Fans go nuts!!!!!] Mike: VVV was caught inbetween the guardrail and Steel steps. VVV on the ground now and Jeran now HITS HIM AGAIN WITH THE STEPS!!! [Fans cheer] Vic: Jeran now sets the steps down and picks up VVV. Jeran grabs the waist and sets him up. Jeran places VVVs head inbetween his legs. Jeran now GETS HIM UP AND DOWN ONTO THE STEEL STEPS WITH A PILEDRIVER!! VVV is out and the ref. is still not back up. [Fans go nuts!!!!] Mike: VVV has got to be out cold and Jeran could probably get the win if the ref. was up. Jeran now over by the apron and is searching for something under the ring. Jeran pulling somethign out...JERAN HAS A TABLE!!! Vic: Jeran is pulling a TABLE out from under the ring and now setting it up. Jeran sets the table up beside the guardrail and gets VVV to his feet. Jeran hits a few hard punches to his forehead and now picks him up adn BODYSLAMS him onto a table!!! [Fans cheer] Mike: This is going to get outta hand before too long. Jeran hits VVV with a few more punches and gets onto the apron. Jeran looks back to measure up. Jeran on the second rope...JUMPS AND SPRINGBOARDS BACK WITH A SPRINGBOARD PLANCHA THROUGH THE TABLE!! [Fans go nuts!!!!] Vic: Well thats as hardcore as WOWA will go. Jeran and VVV are on the ground through the table which is now in about a million pieces! Mike: Both men ARE out and it doenst look to good for VVV right now! Jeran now is getting up. Jeran grabs VVV by the head and throws him into the ring. Jeran now in the ring and gets the ref. over to VVV and COVERS VVV. This is it!! The ref. looking around and is going for the pin. 1 2 3-SHOULDER UP!! [Fans scream] Vic: I cant believe he got outta that. The ref. is looking at the table. hmm, I wonder what happened there. Jeran picks up VVV and whips him to the ropes. VVV REVERSES it and Jeran comes back and VVV hits a TILT O WHIRL BACK BREAKER!!! Mike: Both men are down and that not only hurt Jeran but it took alot of energy outta VVV. VVV now is getting up. Hes a little wobbly and now sets Jeran up on the top turnbuckle. I think hes going to preform that top rope frankensteiner!!! VVV JUMPS ON HIS SHOULDER..BUT CAN'T GET IT OVER AND JERAN TURNS IT INTO A POWERBOMB!! [Fans cheer] Vic: That was DEVASTATING!! Jeran gets VVV back up to his feet and now sets him up....GETS VVV UP AND RUNS...A RUNNING LYGER BOMB AND THIS IS IT!! 1 2 3-FOOT ON ROPE!! [Fans scream] Mike: VVV barely got outta that one also. Jeran now whips VVV to the ropes..he comes back and RAIN RIVER SPINEBUSTER!!!!! You know whats next!!! Vic: Jeran could get outta this one alive. Jeran gets him up and turns him around. Locks the head....WAIT..whos coming down the aisle? [Camera zooms in] Mike: VILE VINCE VIPER???? WHAT??? THERES TWO VIPER's..ONE NOW OUTSIDE OF THE RING AND THE OTHER ONE ABOUT TO BE PUT IN THE TORNADO DROP!!! [Fans scream] Vic: The one in the aisle has NO bandages and that is clearly him. Jeran lets go of the hold and is just STARING at the VVV on the outside. The other VVV in the ring is going to the outside and is searching for something under the ring..whats he pulling out? Mike: THE WOWA WORLD TITLE!!! Vic: WHAT? [The VVV that just got the title goes back into the ring behind Jeran and SMACKS him with the belt.] [Fans scream] Mike: JERAN IS DOWN..THE REF. DIDNT SEE IT AS HE IS LOOKING AT THE OTHER VVV. The one in the ring lays the WOWA World Title down and now gets Jeran back up to his feet. He draps his leg over Jerans head AND ROCKER DROPS HIM ONTO THE WORLD TITLE!!! [Fans scream] Vic: That was a HELLRAISER!! Is that who I think it is? Mike: He throws the belt outta the ring and pins the fallen Jeran. The ref. sees it and counts!!! 1 2 3 DING DING DING [The VVV in the ring starts to take off the bandages.] Mike: ITS LOCO!!! LOCO has been FIGHTING JERAN THE WHOLE TIME!!! Does this MEAN he wins the World Title? [Fans scream in disbelief.] Vic: I hope not...he was suppose to fight VVV not LOCO. Mike: LOCO raises his hand with the "L" and now the REAL VVV is in the ring and there STOMPING ON JERAN!!!! LOCO has the World Title and is SMASHING IT INTO JERANS HEAD!! [Fans boo] Kent: AS A RESULT OF A PINFALL...THE NEW WORLD ONLINE WRESTLING ASSOCIATION WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION.....LOCO!!! [Fans boo] Vic: WAIT...."Malibu" Mike Madison IN THE CROWD AND JUMPS OVER THE RAILING!!!! TRIPLE M IN THE RING AND AMBUSHES VVV!!! [Fans go nuts!!!!] Mike: VVV SLIDES OUT OF THE RING AND OUT COMES TRIPLE M!!!! VVV WITH A EYE GOUGE AND HITS MADISON WITH SOME HARD PUNCHES.....VVV SLAMS HIM HEAD FIRST INTO THE GUARDRAIL!!!! [Fans scream] Vic: VVV SETTING HIM UP AND WHIPS HIM TO THE STEPS...MADISON REVERSES IT AND VVV HITS HEAD FIRST.....MADISON NOW UP TO VVV LANDING PUNCHES..AND HERE COMES SOME SECURITY. Mike: THERE IS MAYHEM ON THE OUTSIDE....VVV IS BACK UP AND A BRAWL IS GOING ON....LOCO IS STILL IN THE RING AND HAS THE WORLD TITLE.....FANS WE GOT TO GO...LOCO IS THE NEW WOWA WORLD CHAMPION!!! GOODNIGHT!!! Vic: I Love this League!! YEEOW!!
