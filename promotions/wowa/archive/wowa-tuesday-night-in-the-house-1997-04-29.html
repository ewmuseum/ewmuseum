WOWA Tuesday Night in the House, 29 April 1997

29 April 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[ "Santeria" by Sublime begins to play. Suicide is shown Suicidekicking the Dragon. The Pizzamen hit Mutiny in the face with a burning hot pizza. The screen explodes into the opening graphics ] ### ### ####### ### ### ###### ### ### ### ### ### ### ### ### ### ### ### ### ### ### ### ### ######## #### #### ####### #### #### ### ### ================================= World Online Wrestling Association ================================= TUESDAY + NIGHT + IN + THE + HOUSE [ The opening graphics fade out into a stage where Cindy Lawrence and Vic Rancid sit behind a blue wrap around desk. In the background is the empty WOWA Arena where the ring crew can be seen taking apart the ring ] Cindy Lawrence: Welcome to Tuesday Night in the House. Vic Rancid: Just moments ago WOWA put on its second house show and boy was it a dandy. Cindy Lawrence: Yes, we saw many great superstars step into the ring for some hard fought battles. Vic Rancid: Yeah, Yeah, let's give them the overview Cindy Lawrence: Okay -------------------------------------------------------------------------- --------------------------------------------- Malibu Mike The Dragon w/Sensation Sara vs. -------------------------------------------------------------------------- --------------------------------------------- [ The graphic fades as highlights from the match are shown commentated by Cindy and Vic ] Cindy Lawrence: Malibu Mike came out for this one cockier than ever. Vic Rancid: That is why I like his style. Cindy Lawrence: The Dragon came out more determined than ever. Vic Rancid: He looked like he was about to cry because he lost to Suicide on Friday ngiht. Cindy Lawrence: The Dragon was vicious in the beginning of this one he went right after Malibu Mike with martial arts kicks that floored Mike a couple times. Vic Rancid: Yeah, but Malibu Mike came back. Cindy Lawrence: Boy, he did as the Dragon climbed to the top rope and went from a moonsault, Mike caught him and landed a powerbomb, but could not get the pin. Vic Rancid: Malibu Mike wasn't done yet though. Cindy Lawrence: He used his patented maneuver the Wipe Out to put the finishing touches on the Dragon. [ Winner: Malibu Mike 10:23 ] Vic Rancid: That is why I like that guy's style. Cindy Lawrence: The evening was far from over though. -------------------------------------------------------------------------- --------------------------------------------- Rage Jason Night vs. -------------------------------------------------------------------------- --------------------------------------------- [ The graphic fades as highlights from the match are shown commentated by Vic and Cindy ] Vic Rancid: This Rage looked promising in this match. Cindy Lawrence: This Jason Night is a cutie. Vic Rancid: Well, Rage got control first much like Talon did on Friday Night. Cindy Lawrence: Rage was totally overpowering Night using powerslams and piledrivers he could not put the rookie away though. Vic Rancid: Not for long though. Cindy Lawrence: Night had a series off rolls ups that could not do the job on Rage. Vic Rancid: Then Night went to the top and who comes out of nowhere, but Talon who attacks Night without the ref seeing it. Cindy Lawrence: This enabled Rage the Rage Bomb through the table and get the countout win. [ Winner: Rage by countout 8:45 ] Vic Rancid: Rage and Malibu Mike both looked good tonight. Cindy Lawrence: Well, Mike Squire's.... Vic Rancid: Excuse me, it is Squealer Cindy Lawrence: Pick to win the World Title Tournament "Gentleman" Chris Cartwright was in action next. -------------------------------------------------------------------------- --------------------------------------------- "Gentleman" Chris Cartwright Talon vs. -------------------------------------------------------------------------- ------------------------------------------------- [ Highlights of the match are shown with commentary by Cindy and Vic ] Cindy Lawrence: Talon had already made his presence known earlier and was in action against Cartwright. Vic Rancid: I don't like this Cartwright fellow. Cindy Lawrence: Cartwright dazzled us with perfectly executed suplexes from all positions. Vic Rancid: Talon got a few shots in though. Cindy Lawrence: Yeah, a couple LOW blows. Vic Rancid: Yeah, so Cindy Lawrence: Cartwright eventually used his High Angle German Suplex for the pin. [ Winner: "Gentleman" Chris Cartwright 6:33 ] Vic Rancid: What a loser, he will get beat by either Suicide, Rage, or Malibu Mike you just wait. Cindy Lawrence: Well, for the last match of the evening we had a tag match for you. -------------------------------------------------------------------------- --------------------------------------------- The Bulldogs The Pizzamen Rocky & MaDDog vs. Chip & Dan -------------------------------------------------------------------------- --------------------------------------------- [ Highlights of the match are shown as Vic and Cindy voice there comments ] Vic Rancid: I hate these Pizzapukes after what they did to Mutiny on Friday night. Cindy Lawrence: Can't you take a joke Vic?? Vic Rancid: Nope Cindy Lawrence: Well, anyway the Pizzamen tried to give the Bulldogs the same gift they gave Mutiny on Friday night, but Chip ended up getting scorched with the hot cheese and sauce. Vic Rancid: And I loved it. Cindy Lawrence: The Bulldogs then continued to double team Chip until he finally tagged in Dan Vic Rancid: He didn't fair much better. Cindy Lawrence: The Bulldogs continued to onslaught on Dan. Vic Rancid: They weren't done yet though. Cindy Lawrence: That is right. MaDDog put a figure four on Chip while Rocky applied a figure four to Dan to get the win. [ Winners: The Bulldogs 18:21 ] Cindy Lawrence: That was the night's action. So long everybody WOWA Sports (c) 1997 All Rights Reserved
