MERGER!!! WOWA joins HCW to make SUPERFED!!!

16 December 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
LOST HORIZONS TO MERGE REMNANTS OF HCW WITH THE WORLD ONLINE WRESTLING ASSOCIATION; **Merger to happen after the WOWA/NWF Pay Per View ----------------- BY [name withheld] Sports Writer CHICAGO IL. --The powers that be at Lost Horizons have decided to put what's left of the HCW into the WOWA, to create a Superfederation. The Complete merger is not to be set into finality until after the WOWA's showing against the NWF. But then the World Online Wrestling Association will close its doors for good, combining with the elements of HCW and begin operations in the New Year. This means there will be a giant federation of sorts, running out of Chicago that combines both leagues into a jumble- but how to keep the vastly different styles intact? The WOWA has long been prided on it's technical and athletic sports entertainment product, whereas the HCW has been known for it's sadistic violence and daredevil wrestling maneuvers. Reports have already been received that [name withheld], in exchange for selling WOWA to the Lost Horizons company, is headed to be the new President of the Superfederation. While we have not gained specifics on to who much else has been hired, it is apparent that AJ Black will be kept on in some fashion. There will be a Press Conference Wednesday Morning to answer most questions of the press, and I will try to get a special interview with AJ Black to probe in depth as to the reasons for the merger. Rumorhounds have already been saying that federations like the Atlanta-based SAW and the Vancouver-based EWF are in line to be a sort of- "Minor League" federation chain for the Superfed's talent that may be left out... that remains to be seen. Some report that in disconnecting HCW, Lost Horizons has also dissolved the alliances with the SJPW and ULWF, but this has not been confirmed. Check your news Wednesday for my in depth report on the Lost Horizons Press Conference, straight out of the Omni Hotel in uptown Chicago, and hopefully I'll get the word straight from the former President.
