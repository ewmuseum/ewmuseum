[ONE] Diatribe

23 December 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Yes, HCW and WOWA have evolved. This new federation will be selected of the best of both, and is also open to those who may not have been involved once before. If you are not interested in receiving our mail, simply respond with "REMOVE" in the subject, otherwise, you will receive all mail from the HQ. If you have someone in mind who you think would be interested in this, pass this to them so they can audition for a spot. Handlers, here's the deal. For the new federation, there are 50 singles spots and room for 16 tag teams. But there are two divisions, a Hardcore and a Regular division. By no means are you required to use the same wrestlers you had previously in WOWA or HCW. This Federation begins its roleplaying in early January, with a Kickoff Supercard in mid-Jan. More info will follow to those that are accepted. Those seeking Staff jobs may contact Ken Wallace at [detail withheld] We will reward your efforts, but will not play favorites. Additional Information will be provided. Titles Include; World Heavyweight Title -Up for grabs in Both Divisions World Tag Team Titles - " " " " Both Divisions Hardcore Regular Division Division =========== ============= Hardcore Intercontinental Television United States Hardcore Tag Belts US Tag Belts As before, only 25 singles and 8 tag team spots in each division, and for those confused on competition, just because you are in a different division as another, that does not mean you cannot wrestle them, That just means the match will be ranked. IF you feel you can't get in a singles spot, then by all means team up with a friend and go for a tag spot, or simply create your own team. Those wishing to transfer over will most likely be able to at a later time. ---------------------------------------------------- Schedule; Monday -SlaughterHouse (Action) Tuesday Wednesday Thursday -Thunder (Action) Friday Saturday -SlamZone (Review) Sunday -WarZone (Action) Sound Offs, will be put out everyday, as long as we have enough, believe me, we should! The Schedules, Rankings and other info will be up Only on the Webpage, on Simplenet, to be debuted in the coming weeks. All shows will be out on time, if emergencies occur, we shall let you know ahead of time, so no one is disappointed. ------------------------------------------------------------- WE will notify the list EACH day of spots left in what divisions, such as I might type on Wednesday, Regular Division- 15 spots left, singles, 5 tag team spots left Hardcore Division- 10 Spots left, singles, 6 Tag Team spots left Auditioning; [ONE per unit each] Send to us, at the respective addresses; -Which division you want to apply for. -ONE 30 Line Roleplay, as quality is our judging point. - A brief description of your singles wrestler/Tag Team -Mails sent from Hotmail, rocketmail, juno and the like extra addresses must include your original address, we will not post this on the page, but keep it for reference. If you have more then one AOL address, let us know as well. We will try not to use them. There is no limit on how many characters you enter, BUT you MUST be able to roleplay WELL with them ALL. If you don't think you can roleplay well (see rules) for EACH character, then by all means, do NOT enter more then you can "handle". We are trying to get your guys in with the least amount of hassle! IF Your singles wrestler/team is accepted, then we will send you an app and ye shall be on your merry way. If the spot you want is full, we will immediately inform you. You then have the opportunity to reapply for another spot, if you want to use the same Roleplay, by all means, do so. -Also, if you are not sure whether you will make it in to the area you want, please state FLEXIBLE in the second choice, and we will direct you to what we can to fit you into ONE. IF you are not accepted, we will place you on the waiting list AND direct you to one of our farm federations. You are more likely to make it in off the waiting list if you are in one of the Farm Federations. ...Now, saying you get in, here are the rules which ye must follow to stay in. ********************************************************** New Fed Rules ; as applies to handlers -more to come, if we see them needed... We think this is fair, so no complaining. We'll try to keep them down. ;> -If special circumstances arise, and you feel you are in danger of violating rules, please contact us immediately so we can excuse it. 1-For EACH man, you must roleplay with them 2 times a week, and no roleplays less then 30 lines will be accepted. 2-Roleplays are to be done 2 times a week EVEN IF there are no matches scheduled for your characters. Have them read the Comic Strips, but they Must rp that much. 3-New applications will be done for EVERYONE, sorry, technical stuff, necessary evils. 4-Failure to do RP one week will result in warning, by your commissioner, [name withheld] [detail withheld] and it's up to him how he handles your situation. 5-Try to make sure that the roleplays are not cut off, and fit on the lines you write them in. Do not use HTML if you think it might cut words off the lines. WE don't need to sit here hitting enter 5000 times to repair the flashes, should they be choppy or cut. 6- OOC's and Kayfabe breaking will NOT be tolerated, if you have questions, save 'em for when we are ready. It's gonna be a busy week for us staffers. 7- ALL angles MUST be approved by the Staff, however, we will now run almost any angle you want. Let us know. ************************************************************* We have other projects for the future, such as Stables, Tours, Women's divisions, Handled Managers and the like, but we do not have time to reply to individual questions right now. If it is THAT important, please let us know, but if it can wait until the rosters are done, then hold your burning questions. Thank you. With your help, this will be the best federation on the Net. Thank you for your time and patience, to be removed, simply respond with "REMOVE" in the subject, otherwise, you will receive all mail from the HQ. If you have someone in mind who you think would be interested in this, pass this to them so they can audition for a spot, but try to get these into us as soon as you can. AJ Black Ken Wallace [name withheld]
