WOWA Friday Night in the House, 26 April 1997

26 April 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[ The screen opens to an aierial shot of the WOWA Arena in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania. The full moon hovers above in the star lit sky. The lights of the city illuminate the building. The screen then explodes into the opening graphics ] ### ### ####### ### ### ###### ### ### ### ### ### ### ### ### ### ### ### ### ### ### ### ### ######## #### #### ####### #### #### ### ### =================== The World Online Wrestling Association presents Friday Night in the House ================== * * * Live * * * * * * From the WOWA Arena in Philadelphia, PA * * * [ The camera opens to a wide shot of the WOWA Arena where the sold out crowd is seen screaming various things that just comes out like a bunch of noise. Fireworks go off as a light illuminates the WOWA logo in the ring. The camera then zooms in on WOWA announcers Mike Squire dressed in a sports jacket and tie and Vic Rancid dressed in a WOWA T-Shirt and jeans. They sit at the broadcast table at ringside waiting to call the night's action ] Mike Squire: Welcome everybody to the World Online Wrestling Association's Friday Night in the House. Vic Rancid: It is great to be here, but we don't have much of a card do we. Mike Squire: Yes, we have a quite a small roster, but the president plans to sign more top stars. Vic Rancid: Well, he better hurry up if he wants to have that opening pay per view he keeps blabbing about. Mike Squire: Well, we do have three great matches. We have Jason Night vs. Talon. Vic Rancid: Talon that guy is vicious I like him. Mike Squire: We also have the Fatal Clan vs. the Pizzamen Vic Rancid: The Pizzamen look like a bunch of bad gimmicks I see the Fatal Clan taking them out. Mike Squire: Last, but not least Suicide takes on the Dragon. Vic Rancid: Is Rick Steamboat back?? Well, I see Suicide making the Dragon feel like he commited suicide when he stepped into the ring. Mike Squire: Let's get to the ring. [ The camera cuts to the ring where ring announcer Kent Washington climbs into the ring adjusts his bow tie and raises his microphone ] Kent Washington: The following contest is set for one fall. [ "Enter the Sandman" by Metallica blasts through out the arena as the fans go wild ] Kent Washington: Introducing first from San Diego, California. He weighs 235 pounds and stands at a height of 6' 3" tall here is Jason Night!!!!! [ Jason Night walks through the curtain to a large face pop from the fans. He is dressed in blue trunks, boots, and knee pads. He jogs down to the ring and climbs onto the apron and raises his hands to the fans as a bar with his name on it flashes on the screen for a few seconds. He has a look of confidence on his face as he steps through the ropes ] Mike Squire: This Jason Night is a rising star in the world of wrestling. Vic Rancid: Rising star is that another word for loser. Mike Squire: I already don't like your attitude. [ "Anti-Christ Superstar" by Marilyn Manson plays over the PA as the fans cheers turn to a chorus of boos ] Kent Washington: And his opponent from the Parts Unknown he weighs 325 pounds and stands at a height of 6' 5" tall here is Talon!!!!! [ Talon walks through the curtain to boos from the fans. He is wearing all black leather and his face is painted white with black lip paint and eye paint along with his long black hair. He walks down the isle threatning the fans who return his insults and a few spit and throw trash. He climbs onto the apron and his name flashes across the screen. He climbs through the ropes with a look of annoyance on his face ] Mike Squire: This guy freaks me out he looks like he belongs in Marilyn Manson. Vic Rancid: I like this guy's style I hope he tears Night apart. Mike Squire: We will see [ Ding Ding Ding ] [ Jason Night and Talon circle each other in the ring. They lock up and Night gets Talon in a headlock, but Talon simply lifts Night up and executes a back suplex. Talon follows it up with a big elbow drop. Talon then laughs to the fans despair ] Mike Squire: Impressive set of moves by Talon, but he better stay on this rookie. Vic Rancid: Night is nothing. Talon is going to tear him apart. [ Night staggers to his feet and Talon delivers a side kick to the jaw of Night. Talon covers 1----- 2 ----- Night kicks up. Talon brings Jason Night back to his feet and winds Night's arm up and executes a short clothesline ] Vic Rancid: I told you Squire look what Talon is doing to this punk. Mike Squire: I think this rookie has some surprises in store for Talon. [ Talon hooks Night in a front facelock and executes a DDT. Talon goes for a cover and hooks the leg 1---- 2---- Night gets a shoulder up. Talon then stomps at Night and then climbs to the top rope and raises his hands to the boos of the fans ] Mike Squire: Talon is waisting his time flaunting in front of the fans. Vic Rancid: Squire, I wish I bet something on this match with you because you would be out 50 bucks. [ Talon now steadies himself on the top rope and prepares to leap off the top rope. He leaps and the staggering Night moves out of the way and Talon eats mat ] Mike Squire: I told you so Vic Rancid: I told you so Nah [ Night covers 1----2----- Talon kicks up with ease. Night then lands an elbow to the back of Talon and scurries to the top rope. Night gets his balance and leaps and lands a splash on Talon followed by a cover 1---- 2 ---- 3 ] [ Ding Ding Ding ] Kent Washington: The winner of this contest Jason Night! [ "Enter the Sandman" by Metallica plays as Night gets his hand raised high in the air and the fans explode ] Mike Squire: I told you he would do it. Vic Rancid: I don't think Talon is done yet. [ Talon staggers to his feet and attacks Night from behind with a double axe handle. Talon then shoves Night in a power bomb position and lands a big power bomb on Night. Night's head bounces off the mat and he is out of it ] Mike Squire: That is just sick. Vic Rancid: I love it. [ Talon exits the ring to the boos of the fans as the referee tries to get Night back to his feet ] Mike Squire: None the less Jason Night was victorious. Vic Rancid: Who cares if he won the match? Night isn't the one walking from the ring unassisted. Mike Squire: That was a cheap shot and you know it. Vic Rancid: Cheap shot. Talon thought he got a shoulder up he was just continuing the match. Mike Squire: You are on BS artist aren't you. Vic Rancid: Hell no, I speak the truth. Mike Squire: Our next match is between the Fatal Clan and the Pizzamen. Vic Rancid: Fatal Clan all the way. [ The camera cuts to the ring where Kent Washington stands in the ring ready this time with his microphone in hand ] Kent Washington: The following contest is set for one fall. [ The Circus Theme plays with voice over's of somebody saying "Pizza Time" repeatively as the fans go into a small face pop ] Kent Washington: Introducing first from Nino's Pizzaria, at a total combined weight of 450 pounds, they are Chip and Dan, the Pizzamen!!!!!! [ The Pizzamen walk down the isle wearing there delivery uniforms. Chip is even carrying a pizza box in hand. Dan just slaps fives with the fans as he walks to the ring. The fans are giving a small pop some of them are asking for a free pizza. Chip and Dan climb in the ring and there names flash across the screen ] Mike Squire: You have got to love the antics of the Pizzamen Vic Rancid: How can these two geeks pass as wrestlers I can't wait for the Fatal Clan to kick their teeth in. [ "Ready or Not" by the Fugees play as the fans go into a huge heel pop ] Kent Washington: And their opponents led to the ring by Miss Havoc, at a total combined weight of 750 pounds here are Mutiny and Homicide, the Fatal Clan!!!!! [ Miss Havoc leads the team of Mutiny and Homcide through the curtain and they recieve a huge heel pop. The smaller Mutiny trails behind Miss Havoc dressed in purple baggy pants and silver boots. The seven foot tall Homicide is not far behind also dressed in purple baggy pants and silver boots. Both men are muscular and have determined looks on their face as they walk to the ring occasionally slapping fives with the fans. They climb in the ring and their name flashes across the screen and they wait for the bell ] Mike Squire: These guys are huge. Vic Rancid: What do those morons the Pizzamen doing? Mike Squire: Chip has a microphone. Chip: Well, it is the Fatal Clan. You big muscle heads we have a gift for you. [ Homicide and Mutiny look at each other shrug their shoulders and walk over to Chip and Dan. Dan stands with the pizza box open to a steaming hot pepporoni pizza ] Chip: Well, here it comes. [ Dan then lifts the box back and throws it forward and the pizza flies out and hits Mutiny square in the face. He is covered with sauce, and steaming hot cheese and pepporoni. Mutiny becomes enraged and he and Homcide attack the Pizzamen ] [ Ding Ding Ding ] [ Mutiny has backed Dan into the corner and lays into him with lefts and rights. Homicide mean while presses Chip over his head. Mutiny then sets Dan on the top rope and levels him with a superplex ] Vic Rancid: That is what the Pizzapukes get for playing a prank on the Fatal Clan. Mike Squire: It was a simple joke Vic Rancid: You wouldn't say that if the Fatal Clan had spray painted the faces of the Pizzamen now would you. Mike Squire: I dunno. [ Homicide and Chip are both back to their neutral corners and Mutiny continues to pound on Dan in the center of the ring. Mutiny whips Dan into the ropes and lands a big powerslam on Dan. Mutiny then leans over and tags in the huge Homicide. Mutiny sticks around and he and Homcide land a double press slam on Dan. Chip can do nothing, but complain to the ref. Homicide then lands a headbutt to the mid-section on the downed Dan. Homicide covers 1---- Chip climbs through the ropes 2 ---- Chip elbows Homicide and breaks the count. Homicide gets up and grabs Chip by the throat and starts taunting him. Dan comes from behind and rolls Homicide up 1---- 2 ---- Homicide breaks free ] Mike Squire: This tag team match is already getting crazy. Vic Rancid: The Pizzapukes are getting their behinds handed to them. [ Dan tags in Chip who legally enters the ring and drop kicks Homicide. Homicide staggers into the ropes and flies back into a clothesline from Chip that sends him to the mat. Chip covers 1---- 2----- Homicide kicks up. Dan climbs through the ropes and the Pizzamen kick away at the fallen Homicide. They turn around into a surprise flying cross body block by Mutiny ] Vic Rancid: That's the way I like it. Mike Squire: Oh man [ Homicide climbs to the top rope as Mutiny throws Dan to the outside. Mutiny then slingshots Chip in the air and Homcide leaps off the top rope with a flying clothesline on Chip ] Vic Rancid: That there was the Fatal Slam. [ Homicide covers 1---- 2---- 3 ] [ Ding Ding Ding ] Kent Washington: The winners of this match the Fatal Clan! [ "Ready or Not" by the Fugees plays as the fans give a heel pop. Miss Havoc climbs in the ring with a towel and whipes off the remainder of the sauce from the chest of Mutiny ] Mike Squire: Look at Mutiny he is covered with pizza sauce. [ Squire laughs a bit ] Vic Rancid: What's so funny Squire? Your Pizzamen are the ones who got laid out. Mike Squire: You have a point there. Vic Rancid: Up next we will see Suicide, Suicidekick the Dragon to the rafters. Mike Squire: I doubt that. Vic Rancid: You were wrong about the Pizzamen. [ The camera cuts to the ring where Kent Washington whips his brow and then raises his microphone ] Kent Washington: The following contest is set for one fall. [ "Dragon's Flight" by Queen plays as the fans go into a small face pop ] Kent Washington: Introducing first from Tokyo, Japan. He weighs 225 pounds and stands at a height of 6' 1" tall here is the Dragon! [ Smoke pours from the locker room as the barefoot Dragon walks through the curtain wearing green scale patterned tights. He then jogs to the ring slapping fives with some of the fans as his name flashes across the screen. He climbs in the ring and awaits Suicide ] Mike Squire: The Dragon is a great martial artist Vic Rancid: Stop sucking up Squire. You know Suicide is going to destroy the Dragon. [ "Come Out and Play" by Offspring play as the fans erupt into a heel pop ] Kent Washington: And his opponent led to the ring by his manager Miss Havoc. He weighs 225 pounds and stands at a height of 6' 2" tall here is Suicide! [ Miss Havoc leads Suicide through the curtain. Suicide is wearing baggy purpl pants and silver boots exactly like Homicide and Mutiny. He also has a muscular build with a tatoo that reads "FATAL" across his chest. Suicide slaps five with a few fans, but concentrates more on the Dragon. His name flashes across the screen as he steps through the ropes ] Mike Squire: Look at that they both are the same weight. [ Squire giggles a little bit ] [ Rancid fakes a laugh ] Vic Rancid: Very funny Squire, you have the humor of Vince McMahon. [ Rancid laughs to himself ] [ Ding Ding Ding ] [ Suicide charges at the Dragon and is met with a snap kick to the jaw. The Dragon quickly covers 1---- kick up. The Dragon gets up and Suicide leaps to his feet and the Dragon takes him down again this time with a roundhouse kick ] Mike Squire: I told you he was a good martial artist. Vic Rancid: Wow, Squire, like I care [ The Dragon climbs to the top rope and leaps off and lands a chop to the head of Suicide that floors him. The Dragon brings Suicide to his feet and whips him into the ropes and lands a back spinning kick on Suicide and covers 1---- 2---- Suicide kicks up ] Mike Squire: The Dragon is on fire. Vic Rancid: Suicide is just testing him. [ The Dragon now climbs to the top again and leaps off with a high cross body. Suicide catches him and a powerslam. Suicide viciously stomps at the face of the Dragon and then bends down and laughs in the face of the Dragon ] Vic Rancid: See Squire. Mike Squire: Wow, Vic, like I care Vic Rancid: Hey, that's my line [ Suicide whips the Dragon into the ropes and punches him in the stomach and lands a DDT on the Dragon and covers 1--- 2--- a kick up by the Dragon. Suicide now calling for the Suicidekick as he stands in the corner stomping his foot. The Dragon staggers to his feet and Suicide launches the kick. The Dragon catches his foot and sweeps the other leg and then flips over Suicide in a pinning combination 1--- 2---- Suicide barely kicks up. Both men leap up at the sametime the Dragon lunges at Suicide and is met by the Suicidekick to the jaw ] Vic Rancid: That's it Squire. [ Suicide covers 1---- 2---- 3 ] [ Ding Ding Ding ] Kent Washington: The winner of this match Suicide! [ "Come Out and Play" by Offspring plays as Suicide and Miss Havoc celebrate in the ring and the Dragon is helped out of the ring ] Mike Squire: Well, atleast the Dragon put up a fight. Vic Rancid: He just halted an [ Beep ] kicking. Mike Squire: Whatever, Vic. Vic Rancid: Well, I am the great Vic Rancid signing off for Mike Squealer. So long everybody. Mike Squire: Hey [ Fade ] WOWA Sports 1997 Copyright All Rights Reserved
