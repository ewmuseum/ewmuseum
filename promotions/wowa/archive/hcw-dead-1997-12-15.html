HCW dead?

15 December 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
HCW is no more, I have taken it apart for bigger things. HCW, Hardcore Championship Wrestling will be merged with [name withheld]'s WOWA to form a Superfed, with cards beginning this new year. You could call it a Phoenix's birth, from the ashes of the HCW arises a new and soon to be powerful force in EWrestling. So for all the "BIG" leagues in the world of eWrestling, live it up now, because I've been around for almost a year now, and the SuperFed is the revolutionary Federation of the internet, and it hasn't even been born yet! All cards Will be posted on the RSPWF and for those of you who want to join, after we have our fed handlers send in applications, then what spots we have left will be up for grabs. BUT don't think you won't be doing your part. I don't write 60+K great cards to have handlers not participate. No Participation- you're fired. And no coming back either. Also, I have been asked by Future President [name withheld] to let the RSPWF know that we are looking for another Vice Pres. to help with booking and possibly Card writing. IF you have a great imagination, and don't mind working with friendly and dedicated people, and DON'T have your Own side fed, by all means, contact him at [detail withheld] Any questions, ask me. I'll be back. BUT come next year, we'll see which federation has what on the Lists... I'm tired of seeing guys I don't KNOW on these things! ;> AJ Black ************************************************************ "You're a mean one, Mr. Grinch" "You love us, you REALLY love us!" *************************************************************
