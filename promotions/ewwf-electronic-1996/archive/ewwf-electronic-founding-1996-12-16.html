EWWF (1996) — Rules

EWWF

16 December 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hey! Want excitement, action and fun? Good. You're ready to join EWWF. Go http://www.ncf.carleton.ca/~dm870 OR... read this! Ah! So, you've decided to try out the Electronic World Wrestling Federation! It is simple to play fun to watch! EWWF is a small e-fed in which we(for the time being) only use WWF wrestlers. It uses TNM like most kick @$$ e-wrestling federations but this is a fed with a twist! It also involves a bit of business! Yes, you own a bunch of wrestlers and try to get them to the top but, you're also in it to build a giant coalition of wrestlers! You must take over the federation! (Even though I hate WCW, it's kind of like making a New World Order, isn't it ;) ) Starting out! To start out is simple! You have 1100$. Just fake dough!!! EWWF bucks... A wrestler costs 250$! Just pick four WWF wrestlers you want to control and you're in! You should also put in promos for each of your wrestlers. (To catch the prez's eye, you get A LOT of matches that way!) Just e-mail me( [detail withheld] ) with your full name, your choices and optional promos and you are in! Everything that you can do! Well, this is interesting! You can do ads, interviews, promos. You can submit cool angles, plots, and other things. You can ask for gimmick matches, feuds, or even fixed matches!!! Yes, this could be one of the coolest feds you'll ever join!!! And, soon, we'll have TNM 6.2plus! More stuff to do!!!! MATCHES Matches are the staple of the federation! They're the best part. But, unlike the saying, the best things are not free! A match costs 15$ or more if you want to win more money. You see each man puts some money into the pot! Whoever wins the match gets all the money! Now a standard match costs 15$ but, you can add other options, for only 5$ more! For example, you can have a special stipulation(gimmick) match. At the moment a special stipulation(gimmick) match can be an Iron Man Match (60-minutes, whoever gets the most falls wins.), a Death Match (You must win three falls in a row.), best 2-out-of-3 falls, a Triangle Match (Three men, one ring), which can either be one-fall(First man to get a fall wins.) or elimination (Only one man can survive!) Ladder Match (First one to climb the ladder and grab the belt wins! Note that Ladder Matches are only for title bouts!), a Steel Cage match (Two men locked in a cage, pin the other guy or be the first to escape!),an I Quit Match (Make the other guy quit to win), a Towel Match (Your corner man has to throw the towel into the ring for the other man to win!) or a Falls-Count-Anywhere Match (You can win anywhere, even across the street from the arena!!!) You can also add stipulations to matches. You can pick'n mix from No DQ, No Count-outs and No time limit! You can add these to any match! And, for 5$ you can have a henchman interfere in the match! This could lose the match for you or could help you big time. Now, you can add any of those options and you will have a bigger pot, but, that's not all! You can fix a match! All it requires is for you to pay the prez the amount of the pot( and the match is fixed! You will win automatically! Why do this? I mean, you won't gain any money, no money at all! But, you will get upped in the rankings! You could get yourself a title shot! And a title shot means dough! Also, in tag matches, six-man tag matches and Survivor Series Matches (except during the Survivor Series matches cost 20$) the fee is 10$ a wrestler. Now, I know what you are thinking, "I can wrestle myself and boost my ratings! I could have title matches with my wrestler facing another of my wrestlers!" Not so. It would be too easy to get rich quick that way... So, forget pitting two of your wrestlers against each other. MONEY Money makes the world go round! It also makes EWWF go round! You will need money to keep going in this game... How do you get it here's how! Don't worry, it's just phoney money, EWWF bucks... Get ahead! Almost everything you do will lead to making or breaking the bank! Lose a match, lose money! Put in an ad, get money, do an interview, get money! Get a job, get BIG money! Have lots of matches, you're bound to win a few! When you win you get money! It doesn't matter how you win, just win! Do an ad! If you do an ad, you get 25$ if it gets played and 10$ every other time it gets played! An ad get played once and three ads are played a show! Go on! You'll get some dough! Interviews will get you 10$! You just have to push your weight arond in the fed, beat people, lose badly, WHATEVER! As long as the interviewer is interested in interviewing you, you get $10! It works like this, the interviewer gets interested in interviewing you. He asks you to do an interview. You write it up and send it back to him. It gets published. (Uncut, uncensored) You get 10$! Get a job! Write the Prez! If you feel you can help with something, and the Prez has a job he feels you can help him with, he'll give you a job. Pay variies based on the task. Of course, the pay is just in EWWF bucks, not real dough! But, you could become one of the more poweful people in the fed! It's just a lump sum. I get 1000$ up front for being prez. You could make some big money! One other thing, if you are short on cash, you can sell your wrestlers to other people for whatever you want or sell them to the federation for 100$. PLAYING THE GAME All you need to do is read your e-mail on Saturday and Wednesday. That's when the shows come...Of course, you should participate actively... send in plots, ads, ideas, anything! Remember, the fed is only fun if you make it fun! INFO Shows: Wednesday Night- Wednesday Night WAR! Pay-Per-Views: Always at the end of the month except in February January: Royal Rumble February: St. Valentine's Day Massacre March: WrestleMania April: Rockin' Wrestlin' May: [Can't think of anything] June: King of the Ring July: Beach Blast August: SummerSlam September: Fall Brawl October: Halloween Havoc November: Survivor Series December: Season's Beatings If you can think of any better names for these events(except Royal Rumble, WrestleMania, King of the Ring, SummerSlam and Survivor Series), I'll give you 25$(EWWF bucks...)! President: [name withheld] ( [detail withheld] )
