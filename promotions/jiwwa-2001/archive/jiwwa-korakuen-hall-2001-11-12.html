JIWWA Korakuen Hall Results, 11/12/01

13 November 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

[Part 1 of 2]

Japan Independent Women's Wrestling Association returned to Tokyo today for the first time since July 7th with a solid card. It seems the JIWWA is trying to go back to a solid schedule after nearly 2 years of being inactive. Everything is building to the big December 15th show (rescheduled because of the September 11th World Trade Center attack) will feature Yori Kenyuki defending her 3WI title against Michiko "SPARROW" Yokota. "O" Denotes Win "X" Denotes Loss Match 1 ------- (O)Miki Tanaguchi (12:05 Fisherman's Suplex) (X)Saya Kawashima 2 former students of Junko Shiratori square off with Tanaguchi scoring after a swinging neckbreaker followed by a Fsiherman's Suplex. Saya Kawashima looked good despite not having wrestled in a year. Match 2 ------- Reika Kusanagi (O) Hanako Nakanishi (23:37 Avalanche Style Brainbuster) Megumi Urameshi Haruko Itzuki (X) The Cruel Angels get back in the hunt for the 3WI Tag team titles as they bring anarchy to Hanako Nakanishi. Haruko Itzuki seemingly had he match won at the 12:32 mark following a running knee to the face (Shinning Wizard) on Megumi Urameshi. The Angels recovered however as Megumi executed a backdrop driver to set-up a Reika Kusanagi Brainbuster from the top rope. Match 3 ------- Misae Suzuki (13:03 La Majistral Cradle) Angel Saito The Godesses of Beauty begin their preparation for the 3WI Tag ttile challenge on 12/15 by taking on champions Rekka Sakura and Misae Suzuki in singles matches. Score it 1-0 in favor of the champs as Misae Suzuki reverses a hammerlock and slips a La Majistral for a suprise pin of "Beauty Star" Angel Saito Match 4 ------- Princess Yamaguchi (21:46 Avalanche Dragon Suplex) Rekka Sakura The war went into the next match as Yamaguchi scores the upset on Rekka Sakura. Rekka looked way off her game in this match blowing several regular spots in her repetoire, including a springboard plancha. Yamaguchi fought a late rally with a release german suplex and then hit an Avalanche style Dragon Suplex off the top rope to score the pin. Rekka was helped to the back by Misae Suzuki and DARIA Kawashima Match 5 Main Event ------------------ SPARROW Yokota (O) DIAMOND Kenyuki (34:07) Keiko Sakura Sachie Yokoyama (Michinoku Driver II-b) Yuriko Tanaka DARIA Kawashima (X) A Hot Main Event featuring the rookie Kawashima teaming with 3WI champ Kenyuki and international star Sachie Yokoyama against The Jet Angels and Kenyuki's challenger on 12/15 SPARROW Yokota. Kawashima looked good against her rival Keiko Sakura, executing a stunning cannonball dive ot the outside ring area around the 10 minute mark and even scored the closest of nearfalls with Trapped Leg Fisherman's Buster. The real story was the kicking and punching between Yokoyama and Tanaka as both women gave a stiff display of kicking power in the ring and violent bit of brawling outside it (Sachie and Tanaka both did small blade jobs). This only fuels the fire for their 12/15 match, Tanaka's first high profile singles match. Kenyuki and Yokota, on the other hand, didn't really get to face off for long as Yokota was working a fluid match laying down double and triple teams for the Jet Angels. The end of the match saw Yokoyama and Tanaka spill out of the ring as Sakura tried to keep Kenyuki contaiend in the corner. Kawashima had Yokota set for a Liger Bomb but saw that slip away as Yokota countered with a frankensteiner and followed with a nearfall Doctor Bomb for 2. Yokota finished it by setting Kawashima for a reverse Brainbuster and executing a fast Michinoku Driver for the 3 count. A super match which saw the crowd really behind Yokota and The Jet Angels. The JIWWA makes another stand at Korakuen on the 18th of November featuring a Main Event of Yuriko Tanaka and Rekka Sakura against Yori Kenyuki and Sachie Yokoyama and a Semi-Main Event of DARIA Kawashima teaming with Keiko Sakura against The Goddesses of Beauty.
