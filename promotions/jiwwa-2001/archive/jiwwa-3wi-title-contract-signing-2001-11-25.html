JIWWA 3WI Title Contract Signing

25 November 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Camera opens on a modest ballroom filled with the hustle and bustle of reporters and photographers. A buzz of conversation is heard amongst the gathered media. At the end of the ballroom stands a raised dais with a podium at its center that is flanked by long tables covered in black cloth. The JIWWA banner is hung behind the podium. The reporters quiet as Kenshiro Matsumoto enters through a side door and approaches the dais.] Kenshiro Matsumoto: Konnichiwa, welcome honored guests and members of the press. We are here this afternoon to formally sign the contract for the Women's World Wrestling Indpendent Title match between Challenger Michiko Yokota and Champion Yori Kenyuki. Allow me to introduce the participants in this match. The Challenger on 12/15, the current BLAZE International Women's titleholder, Michiko Yokota! [Yokota enters to a barrage of flashes as she approaches the podium. She is a striking young beauty of a woman with a cute, schoolgirl like face, hazel brown eyes, and shoulder length black hair that she wears pulled back neatly. She is wearing an elegant, but simple, black business suit with white pinstripes that showcases her slender, but well developed body. Yokota acknowledges Matsumoto with a pleasant nod before having a seat.] Kenshiro Matsumoto: [returning the nod before looking back to the press] and now the reigning Women's World Wrestling Independent Champion, Yori Kenyuki! [Kenyuki enters the room and again the room lights up with the bright flashes from cameras. She is wearing a simple black suit with matching black shirt and silver tie. She is also wearing her traditional black facepaint with the kanji symbol for "WAR" in silver on her right cheek. She bows to Matsumoto and to Yokota and then sits at the table to the right side of the podium.] [Matsumoto motions to an aid who brings for the formal contract. Matsumoto adjusts his glassesand reads] Kenshiro Matsumoto: This contract signifies that on December the 15th 2001 the Women's World Wrestling Independent Championship will be defeneded at The Yokohama Bunka Gym. The Champion will meet the designated Challenger in a 1 fall 60 minute match. The Women's Wrold Wrestling Independent Championship may only vhange hands on a pinfall or submission. Should there be disqulification of ringout levied against the Champion then the challenger will be declared winer of the match but the title will remain with the Champion. Should such a situation arise the Challenger may resulst an immeadiate rematch. Both Champions and Challenger agree to these terms nd are co-signed at the bottom of this legal and binding agreement. [Matsumoto signals to his aid for the ink and brush] Kenshiro Matsumoto: First the Challenger... [Matsumoto dips the brush in the ink and offers it to Yokota who carefully signs the contract and returns the brush to Matsumoto] Kenshiro Matsumoto: and now the Champion... [Kenyuki takes the brush and deliberately scrawls her name on the contract before handing the brush back to Matsumoto] Kenshiro Matsumoto: Now all that is needed is for the reinging official to sign the contract [Matsumoto looks over both signatures befoer nodding and signing the document himself] Kenshiro Matsumoto: "Now before we begin with questions let us have opening comments from first the challenger and then the champion." [Matsumoto turns to Michiko Yokota and nods. Yokota returns the nod and steps upto the dais] Yokota: "Ever since I returned to this sport, I've been working towards my goal. I want to be the woman to carry joshi puroresu into this new millennium. I've met each challenge laid before me to this point, but none have been as great the champion I stand before today. I'm looking forward to stepping into the ring with Kenyuki to find out my true worth." [the press applaud Yokota's words. Matsumoto steps forward t othe dais] Kenshiro Matsumoto: And now some words from the Champion... [Matsumoto looks ot Yori Kenyuki, who rises from her own chair and steps to the dais] Kenyuki: "I am looking forward to a great fight from Yokota. I have been out of the ring too long and it is time for war again. I am proud to be 3WI champion and I will be a strong champion. Yokota is a worthy challenger, but a challenger I will defeat." [Matsumoto approaches the podium.] Matsumoto: "Now, you may begin with questions. Yes..?" [Matsumoto points out into the gathered press selecting one for questions.] Reporter: "Yes, this is for both competitors. How do you feel about the current state of joshi puroresu and do you believe that JIWWA can spark a resurgence?" Kenyuki: "Puroresu in general has been on a down turn lately and it has affected us all. I believe there is an audience for what we do and I hope that they support us. The JIWWA has a chance to lead the way for Joshi and I will dedicate myself to help lead JIWWA into the future. I now challenge Yokota to do the same and together we can make our battle historic and set the pace for future main events here in JIWWA." Yokota: "I hope to be able to do my part to help lead the resurgence. With the status of BLAZE once again in influx, the responsibility falls on Mr. Matsumoto's JIWWA to lead the way and carry the burden. If the fans will give us a chance I know that Kenyuki and me can win their hearts. We're fighting for the most prestigious and historic joshi puroresu title of all time. It would be a crowning achievement for me to win the 3WI title and join the elite group of women to have the honor. I'm going to pull out all the stops and I know Kenyuki will do the same. I promise the fans a match they'll never forget." [Matsumoto again points out to the press.] Matsumoto: "Yes go ahead." Reporter: "Kenyuki-sama, who do you feel would be next in line for a title shot?" Yokota: "That question might better be addressed to me." [Yokota smiles and some laughter emerges from the press. Kenyuki pauses for a moment and then a smile crosses her face briefly.] Kenyuki: "I am in no way looking past Yokota. She is the only challenger that concerns me." Reporter: "Yokota-sama you have confidence beyond your years. How will you attack Kenyuki?" Yokota: I don't believe in tailoring my strategy to my opponent. I like to play to my strengths and let the chips fall where they may. I think a lot of times people get too caught up in attacking their opponent&#8217;s weaknesses, they end up not taking advantage of their strengths. So I'm to try and wrestle my match, just like I have since my return. Matsumoto: I thank you all for attending this press conference and I think we will all see one of the greatest matches in Joshi Puroresu from these two fine athletes." [Matsumoto reaches down to the podium and pulls out the 3WI title. Kenyuki and Yokota stand and all three step out from the podium as cameras flash seemingly non-stop. Kenyuki and Yokota each take one end of the belt and hold it between them while facing the cameras. After a few moments Matsumoto takes the belt and then Kenyuki and Yokota face each other. They reach out and clasp hands and then bow. Kenyuki then pulls Yokota close to her, face to face. The tension is high neither woman gives any ground they just stare intensely into the others eyes. Finally a sly grin breaks out on Yokota's face. Kenyuki nods to her and then they release the handshake.]
