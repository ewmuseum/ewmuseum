JIWWA Virgin's Brawl

7 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Explanation: This was acard originally supposed to come out back in December of 2001. Originally planned as a 5 match card this is the only match that I ended up writing. I thought it lost some time ago. Thanks to [name withheld] for digging out the old copy of the match I emailed to him. [The camera shot opens on the ring in Korakuen Hall. Immediately the graphic comes on-screen…] Nihon Joshi Junior Senshuken Decision Match Sakura Keiko v. DARIA Wakizawa [The house lights dim, the camera cuts to the backstage curtain as music begins to play] IRUMINE-SHON mashita ni mioroshi yoru wo nobotte'ku Escalator kooritsuita BIRU no tanima wo HEDDORAITO no kawa ga nagareru [Trasnlation: Looking down below the illumination An escalator that climbs into the night A river of headlights streams between the cold buildings] [The arena is awash in flashing lights of blue, the fans already know who is coming and the chant goes out] Fans: Keiko-chan! Keiko-chan! saisho ni suki ni natta no wa koe sore kara senaka to totonoerareta yubisaki tokidoki damarigachi ni naru KUSE dokoka e itte shimau kokoro to melody [Trans: The first thing I loved about you was your voice And then your back, and your polished fingernails Sometimes you would fall silent, a heart and melody going off somewhere] [Keiko bursts through the curtain, arms raised with a wide smile on her face. The crowd erupts in cheers as she starts her way to the ring, surrounded by seconds] Angel voice na wo yonde mimi sumasu echo Shining smile kaze ni chiru hanabira to kiss Angel voice atarashii natsukashii echo Shining smile isogazu ni ari no mama kiss [Trans: Angel voice Call my name; I strain to hear the echo Shining smile Scattering in the wind; flowers and a kiss Angel voice a new, but nostalgic echo Shining smile a relaxed, honest kiss] [Keiko makes her way ringside, the cheering is getting louder as she finally approaches the ring apron. At her side is Yuriko Tanaka, already whispering in her ear. Keiko gives a slight bow to the ring before jumping up on the apron and dashing over to ringpost] tooi SAIREN sasayaki to hibiki tenshi ga kakureta NAITO BUREISU yoake made wazuka RIMUJIN no SHI-TO RU-MU MIRA- ni tsuki wo utsushite [Trans: A distant siren, whispers, and echoes... A night breeze that conceals an angel... Just until the dawn, the limousine seat... The room mirror reflects the moon] [Keiko scales the ringpost, mounting the top turnbuckle while still facing the crowd. The cheers rumble as she smiles and raises a fist in the air] ashita wo yakusoku shinakute ii mainichi au kedo omoigakenai egao de soshitara sukoshi kowakute mo ii toriko ni nante dekinai hodo nazome-ite ne [Trans: It's OK not to promise tomorrow Though we see eachother every day with unexpected smiles And so it's OK to be a little scared] [Keiko pumps her fist one last time before backflipping off the top rope and into the ring] Angel voice me wo tojite mune wo daku echo Shining smile itai TOGE itakunai kiss Angel voice kasurete mo sugu chikaku echo Shining smile nani hitotsu mayowanai kis [Trans: Angel voice I close my eyes, the echo embraces my heart Shining smile a kiss that makes a painful thorn not hurt Angel voice a faint echo that will soon draw near Shining smile a kiss given without a single doubt] Voice of Shinji Fukubi: Tonight Keiko Sakura faces the challenge of a talented rookie, who just happens to be her sister's star pupil. At stake is the Japan Women's National Junior Championship. [Keiko stands in her corner, Yuriko Tanaka whispering in her ear on the ring apron] Voice of Shinji Fukubi: Can this rising star she faces overcome the odds and get her first major win of her career? Is this the time for DARIA Wakizawa? [Camera cuts to backstage. DARIA Wakizawa stands by an air of nervousness is about her as she paces in her silver ring jacket] Voice of Shinji Fukubi: This is Japan Independent Women's Wretling Associaition! This is Virgin's Brawl 2002! [DARIA waits patiently until her music finally plays and she walks through the curtain] mirai dake shinjiteru dareka ga warattemo kamawanai hashitteru jounetsu ga anata wo kiramekaseru [Trans: I believe only in the future and I don't care if someone laughs at me the passion that makes you run also makes you shine] [DARIA is blown away by the response… The fans are cheering wildly for her] Fans: DARI! DARI-CHAN! mabushisugi, demo mitsumete itai dokka "bigaku" kanjiteru I'm really really stuck on you WOW! WOW! [Trans: you're too bright, but I want to watch you I feel "aestheticism" within you somewhere I'm really really stuck on you WOW! WOW!] [Overcoming her surprise, DARIA clinches both her fists in excitement and dashes down the aisle to the ring] darenimo nitenai yume no senaka wo oikakaete oikaketeku yoake wo yobisamasu yona moeru kimochi atosaki nante ima wa shiranai taikutsu na jikan yori mo DRAMATIC teniireru made [Trans: the backside of the dream unlike anyone's back I chase, and chase after it some more this burning emotion that feels like it brings the dawn I don't care about what happens later more than boring time until I get dramatic events within my grasp BELIEVE IN WONDERLAND!] [DARIA arrives at ringside an stops before the apron. She closes her eyes and claps her hands twice taking a stance of prayer ever so briefly before hopping on the ring apron and raising a clenched fist in the air] DARIA: IKUYO! [The fans cheer even louder] darenimo mienai yume no katachi wo tsukamaete tsukamaeteku watashi wa tsuite yukukara atsui kimochi tsujitsuma awase betsu ni iranai arifureta nichijou yori mo PARADISE mezashite hashire BELIEVE IN WONDERLAND [Trans: the shape of dreams that nobody can see I catch, and catch it some more I will follow along with your passionate feelings I don't need to make ends meet more than the typical every day life run towards paradise BELIEVE IN WONDERLAND] [DARIA steps through the ring ropes and dashes over to Keiko Sakura's corner, getting right in her face. Both girls are eye to eye as we already have a tense staredown. Referee Satoru Tendou wedges himself in and orders DARIA to her corner] Voice of Shinji Fukubi: This is the big challenge! Can the "Blue Angel" overcome the rise of the "Pure Heart Fighter"? [Ring Announcer Ryu Takada steps forward to center ring ready to do the honors] Ryu Takada: Ladies and Gentlemen, this match is for the Japan National Women's Junior Championship. It is scheduled for 1 fall with a 60-minute time limit. Introducing first from Tokyo, Japan weighing in at 127 pounds, DARIA Wakizawa! [DARIA sprints out of the corner to center ring and is covered by sliver and yellow streamers] Ryu Takada: Her opponent, from Hiroshima, Japan weighing in at 117 pounds, Sakura Keiko! [Keiko steps to center ring and bows as silver and blue streamers flood the ring] Ryu Takada: Referee for this match, Kaga Satoru! [Both girls return to their corners. DARIA stands with her back to Keiko. Keiko listens carefully as Yuriko Tanaka whispers in her ear with last minute strategy.] [The bell sounds 3 times and both girls come charging out] Shinji Fukubi: and we are underway! Shinji Fukubi calling the action with special guest color man Kazuya Asakino! Welcome Kazuya-san! Kazuya Asakino: Hora! Thanks for having me! SF: Keiko and DARIA circling each other now, we have a tie-up. Keiko backs DARIA to the ropes and ref Kaga asks for a break [Keiko backs off slightly but then quickly slaps DARIA] KA: WHOA! What was that for? SF: Keiko trying to send a message that she isn't going to play nice with her sister's favorite pupil here tonight and DARIA doesn't appreciate it! [DARIA storms forward but Ref Kaga gets in her way. Keiko raises her hand in the air and receives a loud crowd pop] KA: Keiko's comes to fight tonight, but it isn't like DARIA came here for dinner and a movie y'know? SF: DARIA getting control of herself as she ties up with Keiko again, back-up into the ropes. DARIA with a whip to the opposite side… [Keiko is shot to the ropes, DARIA rebounds off her side. Keiko rebounds from the opposite side and is met in center ring by a STIFF elbow/forearm shot by DARIA] SF: Keiko stunned by that elbow shot as DARIA follows up with a go behind grapple into a hammerlock KA: Keiko Sakura is known for wrestling at full tilt. Her matches have an element of speed throughout. DARIA wants to feel Keiko out first and in order to do that she needs to control this match at a reasonable pace. [Keiko, still trapped in the hammerlock, slaps her shoulder and then drops off to one knee. DARIA rolls off but is back on her feet quickly and rebounds off the ropes.] SF: DARIA to the ropes, rebound from the opposite side. Lariat; ducked by Keiko who responds with another slap followed by a headlock takedown. Keiko follows with a short knee drop to the stomach. Pick-up by Keiko, scoop-up into a side pendulum backbreaker. [DARIA holds her back in a sitting position as Keiko follows up with kick to the small of the back] SF: Hard kick to the back as Keiko takes command. Head grab now as Keiko drags DARIA to her feet. Irish whip to the ropes, rebound by DARIA. Keiko ducks down for a back body drop but DARIA answers with a neckbreaker. DARIA wasting no time… JUJIGATAME! KA: SURPRISE! Keiko isn't expecting a shoot-based attack from someone trained by Rekka Sakura! [Keiko yells as she squirms in the jujigatame. Finally she wriggles to the ropes and grabs the bottom strand] SF: DARIA showing off her versatility here, surprising us with submission that could've ended the match fast. DARIA on the attack now, scoop and a slam followed by a legdrop. DARIA with the pick-up and an Irish whip. Keiko on the rebound is met with running knee drive by DARIA. Keiko is up against the ropes now with nowhere to go. Chop by DARIA! [Keiko is daze against the ropes as DARIA sets her for another whip to the far side] SF: KEIKO WON'T BUDGE! KA: She's using her free arm to hold on to the top rope strand. She needs to turns this around or she's going to be upstaged by this rookie. SF: DARIA with another chop and trying for the whip again but Keiko still hangs on to the ropes. Ref Kaga now is warning DARIA to break it up. DARIA with a knee thrust to Keiko's stomach. Whip to the ropes, reversal by Keiko. DARIA on the rebound, dropkick by Keiko! KA: Keiko fought that Irish whip every step of the way and finally used it as a means to get a break in the action. Now she just has to follow that dropkick with some impact offense. SF: Keiko with a pick-up on DARIA. Whip to the near corner, Keiko follows in with reverse elbow and DARIA slumps against the turnbuckle. Keiko executes a snapmare bringing DARIA down. Keiko with a kick to the back again and now tying DARIA up… KA: I've wrestled in Mexico enough to know what she's doing here! That reverse leg vine, if she goes for the arms… [Keiko slaps DARIA's sides and grabs the arms] KA: SEE! La Tapitia! [Keiko rocks DARIA back and stretches her out in La Tapitia] SF: DARIA Wakizawa being racked out by Keiko Sakura but refusing to quit. Keiko has her suspended high [DARIA screams in pain as Keiko stretches her. Finally Keiko breaks after close to a minute of suspension] SF: Keiko in control now as she picks up DARIA. Scoop, inverted waistlock… tombstone piledriver! A cover by Keiko! Ref Kaga is there! 1… 2… [DARIA bridges out] KA: No kick out by DARIA! That's our first nearfall this match and I get a feeling we're gonna get more. SF: Keiko nods as he gets to her feet. Pick-up on Keiko, ¾ front facelock… She's trying for the Cherry Cutter! KA: One of Rekka's key moves! This is Keiko trying to get psychological edge on DARIA! [Keiko gives the signal for the crowd who pop loud as she executes…] SF: CHERRY CUTTER! A cover! 1… 2… [DARIA shoots up a shoulder] KA: NO! SF: Ringside, Yuriko Tanaka is slamming her hands on the mat telling her partner to end it! Keiko executes another pick-up on DARIA. ¾ facelock! She's going for another Cherry Cutter! [DARIA pushes Keiko off and grabs an arm and drives her down to the mat in Fujiwara armbar] SF: FUJIWARA! FUJIWARA! FUJIWARA! [Keiko screams in pain as DARIA cranks back as much on the arm as she can] KA: They're center ring! It's a long way for Keiko to get to the ropes and DARIA has it cinched in tight! SF: Keiko is struggling as she mounts to her knees; DARIA is at an odd angle with that armbar… [Keiko rolls over and through but DARIA holds onto the arm and cross wrist flips Keiko back down and slaps on the jujigatame] SF: Keiko almost escaped but got trapped in another cross-armbreaker. KA: But look.. She's closer to the ropes this time and can go for the break if she extends herself… [Keiko stretches out her right foot and hooks the bottom rope] SF: Ref Kaga demanding the break and DARIA now has that look of determination! She senses a kill on the horizon! [DARIA grabs Keiko and applies a standing front facelock] KA: Any number of big moves can be done from this position; Keiko is in big trouble if she doesn't pull it together! SF: DARIA with a front facelock and armlace… leg grapple… She's going for the D-Drop! Her cradle-trapped-leg Fisherman's Buster! [Keiko kicks her grappled leg free and lands out on her feet in front of DARIA] SF: Keiko blocks the D-Drop! Double leg grab! Slingshot catapult in to the corner! DARIA IS DAZED! Keiko now with the double reverse armbar… TIGER SUPLEX! KA: With a bridge! Where is Kaga! [Referee Kaga dives down and makes his count] 1… 2… [DARIA rolls out her left shoulder] SF: NO! Keiko is down on the mat after hitting that Tiger Suplex. DARIA is down after taking it! KA: Whoever gets up first can dictate the rest of this match but someone has to get up NOW! [Keiko rolls over, clutching her shoulder in pain. DARIA lies on the mat rolling her eyes and breathing] SF: Keiko slowly getting up now as DARIA rolls over on to her stomach. Keiko seems to be in a lot of pain, that shoulder can't be in good shape. KA: Remember she hurt that same shoulder a month ago. With DARIA working it over I wouldn't be surprised if she re-injured it. [Keiko, on her feet, scoops DARIA up and slams her down] SF: Keiko pointing to the top rope now! She's going for something big! KA: She needs something big; with that shoulder it is important she hits a big move and… uh-oh… [DARIA pops up and dashes to the turnbuckles. She punches Keiko, who was on the top rope, in the stomach and then slaps her in the face before front mounting the turnbuckles] KA: DARIA is going to get that big move it seems… SF: NO! Keiko fights back! We have a duel on the top rope! Keiko with punch to DARIA! DARIA Responds in kind… KA: Kaga is on the count… [Ref Kaga is already up to 3 on his 5 count] SF: Keiko now shoves DARIA off the top. Keiko on the top rope… DIVING FOOTSTOMP! [Keiko lands in the center of DARIA's stomach to a huge pop] KA: She's going for the cover! 1… 2… [DARIA barely lifts her shoulder] SF: I CAN'T BELIEVE IT! She's earned the name "Pure Heart Fighter"! [Keiko can't believe it as she complains about the count. DARIA rolls on over and starts to get up] KA: Keiko can't be questioning the ref like that, DARIA has proven that she's dangerous and Keiko can't make this kind of mistake! SF: DARIA is vertical and coming up behind Keiko… NO! Keiko turns! She knew DARIA was there and lays her out with an open palm blow! SHOUDA! A cover! 1… 2… [DARIA lays her foot on the bottom rope and Ref Kaga sees it. Keiko slaps the mat in frustration] KA: Keiko has got to be wondering what will put this rookie away! SF: Ringside is intense! Yuriko Tanaka is frantic as she has watch Keiko nearly win the match twice! [Keiko grabs DARIA and hooks in the ¾ front facelock again] KA: She's trying for the Cherry Cutter! NO! DARIA has proven she can counter this! SF: Keiko trying for the Cherry Cut… NO! DARIA shoves her off into the near-by ropes. Keiko stumbling back straight into a ¾ face lock… DARIA WITH A CHERRY CUTTER OF HER OWN! Kaga is there! 1… 2… [Keiko kicks out] KA: Holy Crap! SF: DARIA now getting a second wind as she scoops Keiko up… MICHINOKU DRIVER! LEG IS HOOKED! 1… 2… THR… KA: CENTIMETERS! DARIA was centimeters away from the biggest win of her career! SF: DARIA keeping the offense on, Irish whip to the far ropes… NO! ASAI MOONSAULT… INTO THE DRAGON SLEEPER! KA: Keiko turned it around again! [DARIA scrambles to the ropes forcing a break] SF: Keiko is up, boot to the face of DARIA Wakizawa. A pick-up… Keiko setting up DARIA… Shotay! Shotay! Shotay! Shotay! Shotay! DARIA is dead on her feet! Keiko with a kick to the stomach and ¾ front face lock! Cherry Cu… [DARIA slips out and hits a clean German Suplex with a bridge] 1… 2… 3… KA: NO! Keiko rolled a shoulder! SF: DARIA lifts Keiko up and dashes to the ropes, rebound… [Keiko jumps up and hits a Rana] KA: REVERSAL! THIS IS IT! 1… 2… 3?!? SF: NO! Keiko on her feet… [Keiko reels back and lets out a shout] Keiko: UUUUUUUUUWWWWWWWWWWWAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAHHHH! [Keiko scoops up DARIA in a Fisherman's Buster set-up] KA: This could finally be the end! SF: The lift! The Drop… FISHERMAN'S BUSTER! [On the outside Yuriko Tanaka is yelling at Keiko to pin DARIA but Keiko shakes her head] KA: Keiko is going up top! She wants a sense of finality to this! SF: DARIA is still down as Keiko scales the turnbuckle… [DARIA slowly gets to her knees as Keiko waits up top] KA: What the hell is she waiting for? SF: DARIA is on her feet now! Keiko off the top DIVING FRANKENSTEI… [DARIA falls back with Keiko mounted on her shoulders. Keiko falls throat first across the top rope] KA: HYPER SHOT! DARIA turned the Swinging Frankensteiner into a Hyper Shot! Shinji the winner will be decided within the next 4 moves! SF: Both DARIA and Keiko are down. NO! Kip-up by DARIA! Keiko is on her feet as well but staggering… DARIA from behind… BACKSLIDE DRIVER! D-DRIVE! Keiko has both shoulders down! 1! 2! 3… KA: HOW DID SHE ESCAPE?!? SF: DARIA is in tears of frustration! She can't believe that the D-Drive didn't get the job done! [DARIA sets up Keiko for a DDT] KA: It has to be more than this; a simple DDT won't finish it… [Keiko counters and lifts DARIA off the mat. DARIA wriggles free of what looked to be a Northern Lights Suplex and tries for a back spin sunset flip] SF: DARIA counters the counter... no wait… [Keiko blocks mid-move and swings DARIA's head under her arm and drops her Air Raid Crash style] KA: This is it! [Keiko dashes up the nearest turnbuckle and signals to the crowd] SF: Keiko up top… there is that sense of finality about this… [Keiko flips off the top rope with a moonsault but turns her legs down for the Moonsault Footstomp] KA: ANGEL STOMP! SF: Keiko with the cover… 1! 2! 3! DING! DING! DING! [Both women are on the mat exhausted. Yuriko Tanaka scrambles over to Keiko with towels and water as the other kohai attend to DARIA] Ryu Takada: Ladies and Gentlemen, the winner of this match in a time of 21:46 and NEW Japan National Junior Women's Champion SAKURA KEIKO! [Everyone tends to the girls, both still on the mat. Kenshiro Matsumoto steps up to center ring with the Nihon Joshi Junior title belt. Finally Yuriko Tanaka helps Keiko up as the other young girls help DARIA up] SF: What an incredible match Kazuya-san! KA: I'm blown away! Win or lose DARIA Wakizawa proved she belongs with the pros. Keiko is going to have to look over her shoulder in the future for DARIA who I'm positive will challenge for the title again! [Yuriko helps Matsumoto put the belt on Keiko. Keiko finally stands on her own and walks over to DARIA, still being supported. Keiko shakes her hand and both girls hug to a huge pop. Matsumoto hands the mic to Keiko] Keiko Sakura: DARI-chan… My arm hurts, my back is killing me… [Chuckles] if I could get off the hook with never wrestling you again I'd be slightly happy, but then I wouldn't be a good champion. Anytime you want to challenge for this belt… I'll be waiting! [DARIA nods, tears streaming down her face and hugs Keiko one last time]
