A fantasy-roster federation on the fwrestling.com hub whose 2009 news records a ladder match for its title and its ownership, a takeover by a stable, a new owner named by the stable, and his reported death six weeks later. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Red Star Wrestling's own home page gives its last six weeks as news. On 7 April 2009, after fifteen shows of build-up, Bone Smasher and Alex Coslov met in a Scaffold Ladder Match for the Red Star Wrestling Championship and 100 per cent of the company. Coslov poked Smasher in the eye and dropped him from the scaffold, and then the lights went out: a stable called the Bizarre Ministry stood in the ring, named a new owner, Micky Thunder, vacated the title, fired Coslov and promised the fed's first television deal, a show to be called RSW vs the World. In a statement the same day the owner said Team Ego's tag title win would count if he wished it to. On 21 May the page says the new owner, Micky Thunder, had died; on 27 May the Weird World Order announced a special show to reveal its plans for the company.

The rest of the site is its frame: rules (a roleplay at least once, three warnings and you are fired, one show a week treated like a pay-per-view, results final, angled and roleplay-based matches), an application template and a champions page showing the title vacant and Team Ego holding the tags; the roster, shows and title-history pages hold only their headings, and the forum is on a ProBoards board that is not in the collection. The hub's own league profile of the fed announces a first show for 19 January 2007, every two weeks, with pay-per-view style cards; the fed's own pages that survive are all from April and May 2009.
