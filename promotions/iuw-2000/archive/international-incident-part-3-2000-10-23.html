International Incident, part 3

23 October 2000 (part 3 of 3)
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Elliott Davis: Gavin Foster is in the ring ready to go and the referee for this match Howard Collins, is checking Foster for foreign objects.
Jordan Turner: Here comes probably my favorite wrestler in the IUW right now,
"The Val" Orange Valkyrie!
("Drown Soda" by Hole plays as "The Val" Orange Valkyrie comes out. He is wearing a long sleeve T-shirt with the logo of "Softcore Conception" written on it, a pair of orange Adidas pants with white stripes, white boots with white kickpads over the boots. He has red hair with white highlights and also looks to have a lot of freckles. "The Val" stops at the top of the entrance ramp and turns around so his back is towards the ring. He raises his arms so that he slowly forms an "O," then he turns and runs to the ring sliding in under the bottom rope. Once inside the ring he stands up briefly and plays to the crowd before going over and sitting in a corner waiting for the start of the match.)
Jordan Turner: Look at "The Val," I mean seriously, have you ever seen a more confident wrestler?
Elliott Davis: "The Val" is indeed a confident wrestler, and don't forget he is also a very good athlete.
Jordan Turner: Valkyrie is going to tear this guy in half! Elliott Davis: Howard Collins is calling for the bell and we are under way! Jordan Turner: I bet this guy doesn't last two minutes with "The Val," what do you think? Elliott Davis: You never know Jordan. What??? Jordan Turner: I told you! Elliott Davis: What in the hell was that? Jordan Turner: That was his finisher, the VaL-Driver! Valkyrie just grabbed
Gavin Foster and executed his finisher and there wasn't a thing Foster could do about it! "The Val" is the man!
Elliott Davis: "The Val" is just leaving the ring and laughing as he makes his way back to the locker room.
Jordan Turner: He has to be the man to beat here in the IUW, especially after that quick victory!
Elliott Davis: Well, I don't really see as to how I can argue with you considering what just happened. A stunned Gavin Foster is making his way to the locker room as one of our next participants is making his way to the ring. Fans our final match of the night should be a good one.
Jordan Turner: Who is that loser in the ring right now? Elliott Davis: He is not a loser and his name is Jim Gibson. His opponent for this match will be a man by the name of Triple X and here he comes now.
("The Way I am" by Eminem starts to play and Triple X walks out. Triple X is wearing green pants with black around the legs and a black shirt with three X's across the front and back of the shirt. He has water in his hands and he drinks some and spits it out to the crowd. He walks down to the ring and stands on the apron pointing at the crowd. He throws his arms up and pours water it his mouth and spits it out to the crowd and fireworks go off in the ring.)
Jordan Turner: That entrance really reminds me of someone else in this industry but I just can't quite put my finger on who that person is.
Elliott Davis: Triple X isn't going to wait for referee Howard Collins to start the match! Triple X starts off the match by attacking Jim Gibson before the bell and hits Gibson with a high knee dazing his opponent! Triple X runs to the other side of the ring and comes charging back with a spear!
Jordan Turner: Triple X just about took Gibson right out of his boots with that spear! Elliott Davis: Triple X picks up Gibson and hits him with a hurricarana and then quickly gets back up and climbs to the top turnbuckle!
Jordan Turner: Triple X executes a moonsault and he hits it perfectly! What a move. Elliott Davis: Triple X is picking up Gibson and he grabs him from behind and he's doing a rolling German suplex!
Jordan Turner: What a series of suplexes! Triple X is up and he's going back up to the top rope. Senton Bomb! Senton Bomb!
Elliott Davis: Triple X pulling Gibson over to the corner and setting him up. Triple X now walking over to the opposite side of the ring. Jordan Turner: Bronco Buster! Triple X is destroying this man! Elliott Davis: Triple X grabs Gibson by his foot and is dragging him to the center of the ring. Triple X grabs Gibson's other leg and is executing a sharpshooter!
Jordan Turner: Hey! Triple X calls that the X-plosion not the sharpshooter! Elliott Davis: My mistake then. Jordan Turner: Darn right your mistake! Call the match right or don't call it
Elliott. Elliott Davis: Triple X releases the hold and picks Gibson up and Gibson with a few uppercuts but Triple X regains the momentum by executing a pump handle slam!
Jordan Turner: That was a pathetic attempt of offense. Oh and there's X-Rated,
Triple X's finisher!
Elliott Davis: Howard Collins begins the count: one, two, three! Jordan Turner: The winner of this match is Triple X! Elliott Davis: That's all the time we have fans. Thanks for tuning in and make sure to tune in next week as we have an eight man tournament to crown our first ever International Heavyweight Champion! For Jordan Turner I am your host
Elliott Davis saying goodnight!
