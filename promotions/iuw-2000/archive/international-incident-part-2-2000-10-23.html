International Incident, part 2

23 October 2000 (part 2 of 3)
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Jordan Turner: Anderson is out on his feet. The lights are on but nobody is home! Elliott Davis: "The Goldenboy" grabs Anderson and executes a piledriver! Jordan Turner: This match is over, put him away already! Elliott Davis: "The Goldenboy" picks up Anderson and throws him into the ropes and.. Jordan Turner: Scarlet picks Anderson up into the air and drops Anderson's neck across the top rope snapping his neck back!
Elliott Davis: That's "The Goldenboy's" finishing move! He calls that the Golden Gunn! Jordan Turner: Well, I'll admit that is an effective finisher! A guy's head isn't meant to snap backwards like that.
Elliott Davis: "The Goldenboy" goes for the pinfall! Jordan Turner: Big Fatty counts: one, two, three! Elliott Davis: The winner of this match is "The Goldenboy" Christopher Scarlet! Scarlet with a totally dominating match in his debut. I don't remember Anderson getting in any offense at all.
Jordan Turner: That's because that loser Anderson didn;t get in any offense! Great, the goody two shoes wins the match. I'm so thrilled. Can we get on to the next match now?
Elliott Davis: In a minute Jordan. We have to let the wrestlers in the ring leave first. (As "The Goldenboy" Christopher Scarlet leaves the ring he slaps the hands of the fans at ringside and makes his way back to the locker room area. Some league officials come out and help Roy Anderson back to the locker room area as
Alex Collins, a participant in the next match makes his way out.) Elliott Davis: Now that's what I like to see. Alex Collins is eager to get his match under way and wasted no time getting out here.
Jordan Turner: Yeah he may be eager but from what I have heard about The Judge, Collins may end up wishing that he never even came to the arena tonight. Elliott Davis: Only time will tell. Let's watch as The Judge makes his way to the ring. (Nature Trail to Hell begins to play over the speaker system and The Judge makes his way out. The Judge stops at the top of the ramp and raises both his hands into the air and then slams them down as if passing judgement to the beat of the music. He then walks to the ring and turns back around, facing the entrance. He removes his Judge's robes revealing his wrestling attire that consists of black pants and a black tanktop that reads "Judgement Day Is Here!"
on the back . The Judge then climbs into the ring.) Jordan Turner: Now tell me that isn't an entrance that instills a sense of justice! Elliott Davis: Tim Smith will be our referee for this next match. Jordan Turner: The Judge yelling at the crowd and Alex Collins sneak attacks The Judge from behind! Elliott Davis: Collins goes to throw The Judge into the ropes but The Judge reverses it and executes a killer side buster! The Judge then quickly grabs both of Collins legs and executes a Boston Crab!
Jordan Turner: What a great combination! The Judge gets in some quick offense and follows that up with a wear down submission type move. You know what that shows Elliott? It shows that The Judge has some smarts!
Elliott Davis: Every wrestler in the IUW has smarts because here in the IUW we have the best athletes in the world!
Jordan Turner: Listen to you. These guys are dumb jocks with some smarts, not the brain surgeons you are making them out to be!
Elliott Davis: Collins gets to his feet and bounces off the ropes trying to get some momentum but he is caught with a Spinebuster from The Judge!
Jordan Turner: The Judge goes over and he's standing in the corner behind Collins as Collins gets to his feet. Collins stumbles around as is met with a diving reverse DDT! What a fantastic move!
Elliott Davis: I don't believe I've ever seen that move before. The Judge picking up Collins and throwing him into the ropes and catches Collins with a
Samoan drop! Jordan Turner: The Judge climbing to the top rope and just sitting there waiting for Collins to get to his feet.
Elliott Davis: Collins finally up to his feet and he is met with a top rope clothesline! Jordan Turner: The Judge is looking good, totally embarassing Collins. Elliott Davis: Collins laying on the mat and The Judge hits him with an elbowdrop! Jordan Turner: Here we go, The Judge is calling for his finisher! Elliott Davis: And there it is, the Judgement Day! Jordan Turner: Tim Smith goes down for the count: one, two, three! The Judge wins baby! (The Judge immediately rolls out of the ring and starts to make his way back to the locker room as the fans boo him loudly. The Judge turns to look out at the fans and passes judgement on them yelling that they are the worst fans he has ever seen. Meanwhile the referee is helping Collins out of the ring after that devestating finisher.)
Elliott Davis: I take it you like The Judge? Jordan Turner: Of course I mean look at my options. It's either like a guy like The Judge or like some goody two shoes like The Goldenboy. I'll take the guys like The Judge any day over the other alternative!
Elliott Davis: Well here comes a participant for our next match, he is Hardcoremaster! (Smoke And pryo go off, then the sound of a motorcycle hits as "Born To Be Wild" blares out over the loudspeaker. Hardcoremaster makes his way out to the ring on a motorcycle looking like a reject from the old Happy Days television show. Hardcoremaster has long black hair with a bushy beard and is wearing a leather motorcycle jacket, ripped up jeans, a t-shirt and army combat boots.
Once he reaches the ring he parks his motorcycle and climbs into the ring.) Elliott Davis: That's not a very original entrance! Jordan Turner: Yeah but it does make an impression doesn't it? (The Jumbotron comes to life and shows an image backstage. Hardcoremaster's opponent for this match [name withheld] is seen laying unconscious on the floor backstage. A big pipe is laying beside him and a little bit of blood is on the floor coming from Davis' forehead. The camera pans out and shows wrestlers Sexy
Kev and "Pure Ecstacy" Jason Jarrett standing over the fallen wrestler. Each man starts to take credit for knocking out Davis and then the two start fighting with each other. League officials quickly come to the scene and break up the two men as the scene fades back to the announcing booth.)
Jordan Turner: Well, it looks as if Hardcoremaster is going to win this match by forfeit. Elliott Davis: That's a shame too because it deprives the fans of seeing Hardcoremaster in action. Jordan Turner: Oh please, I'm sure Hardcoremaster is more than happy to take the win any way he can get it.
Elliott Davis: Probably so but one of my other questions is why are both Sexy Kev and "Pure Ecstacy" Jason Jarrett both taking credit for knocking out Jack Davis and then fighting with each other? Jordan Turner: Well, both guys want to be known as tough guys so maybe that's why. Elliott Davis: Well, Hardcoremaster is making his way back to the locker room and here comes Gavin Foster.
Jordan Turner: Here comes the referee too.
