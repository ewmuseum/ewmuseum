International Underground Wrestling Frequently Asked Questions Version 1.0 ---------------------------------------------------------------- Contents 1. About the IUW 2. Staff 3. Fantasy Characters 4. Titles 5. Events 6. Your Role in the IUW 7. Conclusion 8. IUW Application ----------------------------- 1. About the IUW International Underground Wrestling is a league designed primarily for people that have lives outside of the computer. This league is for people that have jobs, go to college, or simply just don't have the time to devote to an e- league that a younger student does. The IUW is a "booked" style e-fed which means wins and losses are based on your roleplays. We in the IUW do "NOT"
look for length in your roleplays as much as we look to see if it holds our interest and does not bore us. There will be one card a week and everyone in the league will be included on that card. The IUW will decide the outcome of your match by basing our opinions on your best two roleplays each week. If you are able to do more than two that's fine but we will only judge your best two roleplays. If you are only able to do two roleplays a week then that is fine as well. Remember roleplays do not have to be long, they have to be interesting. We also use an "open" mailing list which means that the roleplays will come directly to your e-mail from the other handlers.
This allows you to roleplay whenever you want to or when you have the time. We also only allow original, created wrestlers. After all, this is fantasy wrestling. 2. Staff Currently the league is run by one person, me. The reason for this is that I prefer to keep the league small and manageable. By keeping the league small and by small I'm saying our roster will never go above 24 wrestlers, then it makes it easy for me to handle by myself. I also learned from past experience that I demand that cards be on time and written a certain way, and people who generally want to help out aren't always dependable. My members expect a lot from me and I want to give it to them and that is why I do things myself instead of depending on others. I'm not against having people help out, it just takes me a while for people to build that trust with me so that I will let them help out. I have to know for sure that a person is really dedicated to the league before I will trust him with any important task.
3. Fantasy Characters President & Commissioner - Gabriel Storm Ringside Announcers - Elliott Davis (Play By Play) & Jordan Turner (Color) Referee's - Tim Smith, Howard Collins, Big Frank Gibson Interviewer - "Scoop" Timmy Johnson 4. Titles IUW International Heavyweight Champion IUW Junior Heavyweight Champion IUW International Tag Team Champions 5. Events There is only one card in the IUW and that card takes place on Monday Nights. The show is called "International Incident", and it's the IUW's version of RAW or Nitro.
6. Your Role in the IUW Your role in the IUW is very simple, to roleplay twice a week and have as much fun and enjoyment as possible.
7. Conclusion After reading this I hope that you decide to give the IUW a chance, join up today as spaces are limited. (Remember the league will "never" exceed 24 members.) We will do our best to get everyone over as a superstar, I don't want anyone in this league to become a "jobber". Everyone in this league will lose a match here and there, its not that we consider your opponent better neccessarily as much as it is we want everyone to have fun. Occasionally even the lowest superstar needs a win over a veteran to keep his interest in the league strong and to promote a well balanced league. Just don't complain about it, just enjoy yourself and work on your storylines! Have fun and I hope to see you in International Underground Wrestling.
