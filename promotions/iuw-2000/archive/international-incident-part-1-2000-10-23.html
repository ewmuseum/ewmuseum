IUW Card Archive

International Incident, part 1

23 October 2000 (part 1 of 3)
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
International Underground Wrestling Monday Night's International Incident 10/23/00 (The scene opens to a jam packed arena, everyone here to witness the first ever event of International Underground Wrestling. The fans are going nuts looking into the camera and cheering as a man walks out from the backstage area and begins to make his way to the ring. The fans begin to quiet down just a little to see just who this man is and what he is about to do. The man gets into the ring and is handed a microphone from a ringside attendant. The man goes to the middle of the ring and begins to speak.)
Gabriel Storm: Welcome to International Incident and welcome to IUW! (The fans all cheer.) Gabriel Storm: My name is Gabriel Storm and I am the president and commissioner of this great new league. Tonight we have a jam packed card for you, a card that will introduce you to our fantastic wrestlers who are all superb athletes.
We hope that tonight we will make a great impression on you all and keep you wanting to come back every week for more action. With that said I'd like to introduce you all to our two commentators for every event we will do here in
IUW. (Gabriel Storm points over to the announcing booth where two men are sitting.) On the left is the play-by-play man Elliott Davis and to his right is the color commentator Jordan Turner. I'll leave it to you guys now, have fun!
(Gabriel Storm leaves the ring and the focus now turns to the commentary booth.) Elliott Davis: Welcome fans and thank you for tuning in. Tonight is sure to see a lot of action and we hope to be able to bring it to you in a way that the fans at home feel like they are right here with us at this great event.
Jordan Turner: Come on Elliott, you are boring me already! Fans forget about the great athletes and forget about sportsmanship! Tonight you are going to see a lot of guys getting their butts kicked as well as a lot of violence! This is wrestling, not your mommy and daddy's weekday sitcom! This is INTERNATIONAL
INCIDENT! Elliott Davis: Thank you Jordan, for helping the critics out there who bash wrestling and giving them a quote.
Jordan Turner: Screw the critics! There are more wrestling fans out there than critics and besides, those critics are all pansies! Wrestling fans have chairs and won't hesitate bashing those critics over their heads with those chairs if they get out of line! So the critics better keep their mouth's shut if they know what's good for them!
Elliott Davis: Well, I guess you do have a point there. Jordan Turner: Of course I do! Elliott Davis: OK our first match is close to starting. Roy Anderson a participant for our first match is already in the ring and we are awaiting the arrival of "The Goldenboy" Christopher Scarlet!
Jordan Turner: Oh great, I understand that we are going to a pre-taped interview with that goody two shoes named Scarlet and our own reporter, Timmy
Johnson also known as "Scoop."
(The scene switches to the big screen where "Scoop" and "The Goldenboy" are seen standing in front of the IUW logo.)
Timmy Johnson: Hello fans, I am Timmy "Scoop" Johnson and I am standing here with the leagues premiere good guy, "The Goldenboy" Christopher Scarlet.
Welcome to the IUW Goldenboy. Christopher Scarlet: Please Scoop, call me Chris. Timmy Johnson: OK Chris. Tonight you will have your first professional wrestling match in the IUW as will everyone else. Your first opponent will be
Roy Anderson and this is the first time the fans will see you in action. How do you plan on winning this match and how do you hope the fans will receive you?
Christopher Scarlet: Well Scoop that's a question that should be obvious. I hope that the fans respond well to me and like me, after all, that's what this business is all about for guys like me. This is a business for the fans, we are here to entertain them and I as a professional wrestler want to do my best to give them the best match possible. They paid to get in here and they are paying to see me put on a good show and that's exactly what I want to do. As for my opponent and how I want to go about winning my match, well, I don't really want to give out too much information. What I can tell you is that I am more of a technical wrestler than anything else because I am not as big as the other wrestlers in this league. I will try to use my technical expertise to wear down and defeat my opponents and tonight, Roy Anderson will be the first to go down in defeat to The Goldenboy!
Timmy Johnson: Chris next week on International Incident the first ever International Heavyweight Champion will be crowned in an eight man tournament in which you are a part. Your first round match up is against a man by the name of The Judge, can you say a few words about him?
Christopher Scarlet: Well Scoop all I can really say is this, I don't know much about The Judge but come October 30 at International Incident, he can be the judge but I will be the jury and the executioner! I will be the jury because I will decide his fate and then I will become the executioner when I defeat him to advance on in the tournament. I will become the first ever International
Heavyweight Champion and I will win that title for the fans!
Timmy Johnson: Big words and big promises from a small man with a big heart. Thank you for your time Chris and good luck in tonight's match-up. Christopher Scarlet: Thank you Scoop! Timmy Johnson: Back to you Elliott. (The scene then switches back to the announce booth.) Elliott Davis: Fans it's time for "The Goldenboy" to make his entrance. Jordan Turner: Oh great, here comes Mr. Goody Two Shoes! (The lights in the arena dim to a very faint glow. "Full Nelson" by Limp Bizkit begins to play over the PA system and two spotlights appear on the curtain. As the song plays the fans all stand to their feet and hold up signs supporting
"The Goldenboy." The signs from the female fans say things like "Marry Me
Chris!" or from the guys, "The Goldenboy Rules!". After a few seconds "The
Goldenboy" Christopher Scarlet walks out from behind the curtain and the cheers erupt as he makes his way to the ring. The Goldenboy's long blond hair is pulled back into it's usual tight ponytail with a large section of it braided so that it stays out of the way during the match. The Goldenboy is wearing red wrestling tights (pants style) with white hearts on them, with the name
"Goldenboy" written across the back of them. Scarlet also wears white knee pads, red elbow pads and white wrestling boots with his initials "CS" on the boots. As The Goldenboy makes his way to the ring he shakes the hands of his male fans and gives a few hugs to the women fans. As The Goldenboy reaches the ring he walks up the ring steps, grabs the top rope and does a flip into the ring. Once inside the ring he walks to the middle and raises both his arms up into the air acknowledging his fans and spins around in a circle so everyone can get a good picture. Finally the music stops and The Goldenboy goes to a corner of the ring. )
Jordan Turner: This guy is supposed to be a fan favorite? Seems a little bit egotistical to me. Look at his arrogance, he seems very full of himself.
Elliott Davis: That's not arrogance, it's just a showman playing to the fans. The referee for this match will be the senior referee, Big Frank! Jordan Turner: Big Frank is right, how much does that guy weigh? I'm surprised he can even get into the ring!
Elliott Davis: Stop it, he's not that big. Jordan Turner: Whatever, he probably out weighs both wrestlers in the ring combined! Elliott Davis: Big Frank checking both wrestlers for foreign objects and he's calling for the bell.
Jordan Turner: I hope Roy Anderson shows this goody two shoes what a good beating feels like! I can't stand people who have that holier than thou attitude.
Elliott Davis: The Goldenboy offers to shake the hand of Roy Anderson but is refused. The Goldenboy shakes his head in disappointment and the two begin to move around the ring sizing each other up.
Jordan Turner: Come on Anderson! Elliott Davis: They lock up and "The Goldenboy" throws Roy Anderson into the ropes and hits him with a backdrop!
Jordan Turner: Wow what a technical move, a backdrop. Elliott Davis: Anderson gets to his feet but "The Goldenboy" stays behind him and catches him in an armlock hammerlock submission manuever!
Jordan Turner: Come on Roy, you can get out of that move! Elliott Davis: "The Goldenboy" positions himself and executes a backspin DDT! What a move! Jordan Turner: That was rather impressive looking, but the minute Scarlet goes up against a bigger wrestler moves like that are going to be harder to do.
Elliott Davis: "The Goldenboy" picks Anderson up and throws him into the ropes and catches him with a cross bodyblock which he then turns into a roll up pin!
Big Frank begins to count: one, two, kickout! Jordan Turner: A close one for Roy, he better get his act together and quick! Elliott Davis: "The Goldenboy" picks Anderson up by his head and then he sets him up and executes a brainbuster!
Jordan Turner: It looked like Anderson landed wrong on his neck but Anderson is getting up!
Elliott Davis: Anderson gets up and is met by a dropkick to the knee which sends Anderson right back down! "The Goldenboy" quickly moves over and gets on the second turnbuckle and gives Anderson an elbowdrop!
