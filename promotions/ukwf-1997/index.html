Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The earliest trace of the United Kingdom Wrestling Federation is a third party's own community fed directory, dated 25 September 1997, which lists it in two lines — “UKWF — United Kingdom Wrestling Federation” — alongside its members.tripod.com home page, a month before the promotion advertised itself anywhere on its own.

It advertised itself twice after that, in one-line recruitment posts to rec.sport.pro-wrestling.fantasy on 23 and 31 October 1997, each pointing recruits to the same tripod address: “Join the UKWF Today.”

No roster, card, or result survives, and no closing notice does either; its record stops with that second recruitment post.
