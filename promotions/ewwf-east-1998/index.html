A federation billed as a member of the EWWF Alliance, running weekly TV-style broadcasts around a World Champion nicknamed FBK and a Vice President storyline through the summer and fall of 1998. Five roleplays survive alongside a founding recruitment notice and a pay-per-view promo trailer. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Extreme World Wrestling Federation introduced itself on 4 June 1998, recruiting roleplayers under the tagline “Forged by Fire, Continued by Tradition... Time to Get Hardcore,” promising extreme stipulations, a fair voting committee, and roleplayers worth watching.

By that same month its own broadcasts centered on a stable, The New Revolution, built around World Champion FBK and his partner Scorpio, who feuded publicly with rival wrestlers and stables over the tag team and undercard titles; a tag team calling itself The New Wave, “Dangerous” Daniel Karby and “The Master” Ken Stone, challenged for a number-one contendership until Scorpio and FBK themselves interrupted to needle both sides.

Its own late-August advertisement for a pay-per-view, Summer Slaughterfest '98, traced the promotion's title picture as it then stood — Outsider's third reign with the United States Championship, Higor Rosa holding the Internet Championship, Guru holding the Ironman Championship as part of a stable called the Lynch Mob, and Poe and Bludstorm as Tag Team Champions — while announcing the formation of a new stable, Hell on Earth, built around FBK, Outsider, and Immortal Doomsday.

A September broadcast, Stampede Saturday Night, opened by introducing a new on-screen Vice President, Clint Cornell, reported to have already fired the promotion's own Extreme Champion, Harvester of Sorrow; the same show staged a backstage assault on Lynch Mob's Guru and Luni by a disguised “Devilman” Christian Alicea, revealed mid-attack to be performing under several rotating alter egos, and closed its surviving half with Ironman Champion Phantom retaining the title over Immortal Doomsday in a falls-count-anywhere match after a Hell on Earth stablemate, Outsider, ran in on Doomsday's behalf.

By October, FBK — still World Champion — was holding Vice President Cornell hostage on camera while feuding with challenger “Misfit” Chris Douvalas and dismissing a rival promotion and its manager. Later that month, Suicide of Hell on Earth previewed the promotion's own Rage in the Cage tournament for the Extreme Championship as its number-one seed, running down the entire field of contenders by name.

No result survives for the Rage in the Cage tournament, no further Stampede Saturday Night broadcast beyond its first part, and no document records when Extreme World Wrestling Federation was founded before this June 1998 recruitment post, or when its own record ends.
