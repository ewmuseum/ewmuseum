EWWF East — Stampede Saturday Night

Stampede Saturday Night, Part 1

27 September 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
EWWF Presents... Stampede Saturday Night!!! Live from the Corestates Spectrume, in Philiadelphia, PA! [The camera fades in, and we pan the arena, seeing a sold out crowd, in the Corestates Spectrum of Philly. "Rise" By Pantera plays loudly through the arena, playing loudly, and very very fast. It fades out, into the arena, and our commentating picks up, with Extreme Gene and Ryan Reynolds, our newest EWWF Commentator.] Extreme Gene: WELCOME EVERYONE! To a sold out crowd, here in Philiadelphia, Pennsylvania. Finally back in the East side of the states. It's great to be home, with NEW Changes in the EWWF. We have introducing our newest EWWF Commentator, my side-kick, Ryan Reynolds... Ryan Reynolds: Thanks, Gene. We do have a lot of changes, starting with a new movement in management in the EWWF. A new Vice President, who some say "bought" his way into office... a very wealthy man, by the name of Clint Cornell. We'll see what he has in store for the EWWF. Extreme Gene: Word has it, that he's ALREADY Fired our Extreme Champion, Harvester of Sorrow. I doubt that this is true, we'll see around main event time, when our World Champion, FBK will face off with our Extreme Champion, Harvester of Sorrow. That should be a GREAT Match. Wait a minute everyone, there is some sort of a diversion going on in the back locker room area... [Camera fade into the locker room hallway. There is EWWF security everywhere as they are running around the hallway looking for someone in particular. As the officials and security walk farther away from the camera, a mysterious person walks right in front of the camera. The person is wearing a big red wig with a long open sundress, and pair of black jeans underneath. The person has huge tanned arms and is carrying a fire exstinguisher. The camera follows this mysterious person into the Lynch Mob locker room.] Extreme Gene: Who is this person?? He kicks open the door where Guru and Luni are preparing their strategy. "Misfit" Chris Douvalas is no where in sight. This mysterious person is spraying everything in sight with this Fire Extinguisher. The whole room is engulfed with the white dust, and nothing is visible. The cameraman moves back a few feet where the audio picks up a loud crack! Luni is shoved by the massive figure out of the white sprayed fog, and onto the floor, where his forehead is busted open. The white fog is slowly clearing as the audio picks up loud painful screams. Ryan Reynolds: The camera finally enters in the locker room where the mysterious person is beating up on Guru. The mysterious person takes off the red wig and throws it in Guru's face to reveal long dry tangled black hair. We can't tell at this point if it's a male or female! The person starts slamming the base of the fire extinguisher onto Guru's knee. The person is yelling... Mysterious Character: He is dead, He is dead!!!!!!! NO NO... HE'S ALIVE, HE'S ALIVE... AND DEAD!!!!! [The mysterious person walks around Guru who is gripping his knee in very dreadful agony.] Ryan Reynolds: The camera is able to finally get a good description of this attacker. The hair is covering the attackers face but it is most certanly a man. The man stands right in front of Gurus head who is grimmacing in pain craddle up holding his right knee. The man sets up the base of the fire extinguisher and lightly puts it on Guru's head... Extreme Gene: OH MY GOD!!! THE MAN IS ABOUT TO CRUSH GURU'S HEAD!!! HE RAISES THE FIRE EXTINGUISHER HIGH IN THE AIR AND AT THE SAME TIME PULLING BACK HIS HAIR TO REVEAL!!!! OH MY GOD ITS "DEVILMAN" CHRISTIAN ALICEA!!!! CHRISTIAN ALICEA IS BACK IN THE EWWF!!!!!! [Alicea begins to yell...] "Devilman" Christian Alicea: THIS IS YOUR END, YOU ARE GOING TO DIE, JUST LIKE ME!!!!!!!!!! [Alicea winces and yells again this time to himself...] I TOLD YOU TO QUIT WITH THE PHILOSOPHICAL SHIT!!!!!!! Extreme Gene: Alicea is now arguing with himself which GIVES MISFIT A CHANCE TO RUN IN AND SPEAR ALICEA!!!! Alicea gets tackled but throws the fire extinguisher at Guru, which nails him in his right hip. Misfit begins pounding on Alicea but the officials and security come in and separate Misfit from Alicea!! [Alicea gets up and starts laughing at Misfit, he begins to yell at him...] "Devilman" Christian Alicea: YOU COULDNT DEFEAT ALICEA, YOU COULDNT BEAT CHRISTIAN AND YOU COULDNT GET EXTREME WITH THE DEVILMAN! AND NOW THAT THEY ARE DEAD YOU WANT A PIECE OF THEM??!?!!? MISFIT!!! YOU AND GURU BOTH SUCK!!!! NEXT WEEK YOU WILL GET YOURS.... AUNTIE PATH IS HERE AND THERE AINT NOTHING YOU CAN DO ABOUT IT!!!!!! [Auntie Path then spits on Misfit and then Guru before he is escorted out of the arena.] Ryan Reynolds: Auntie Path?!? Extreme Gene: I Believe that's what he said! I think Alicea has snapped once again. That man is deranged. He's got 4 different characters! And he plays em' all at once! I've never seen anything like that man! He belongs in a looney bin! Ryan Reynolds: I've been doing research, and I think he's been staying in Bellvue for the past 2 or 3 months... That's why he hasn't been so effective. Extreme Gene: Well, it looks like we have a whole new "Devilman" Christian Alicea.... We'll be right back after this commercial break... the Ironman Championship bout... Falls count ANYWHERE in the Corestates Spectrum... Phantom, the Champion against Immortal Doomsday, representing Hell on Earth... otherwise known as HOE. ======================== Commercial Break ======================== ****Ironman Title Bout**** ****Falls Count Anywhere**** Phantom (Champion) VS Immortal Doomsday ======================== Extreme Gene: We return from a commercial break, to a jampacked Corestates Spectrum, in Philiadelphia, Pennsylvania. The fans are cheering wildly as the generic EWWF music, "Rise" By Pantera fades to a close. The fans are settleing down and getting ready for a night of action when the Ring Announcer enters the ring. We see Immortal Doomsday standing, awaiting his opponent, in an exclusive match. Let's go to ringside... Ring Announcer, Brody Stevens: Ladies and Gentlemen, welcome to the Corestates Spectrum... and thank you for being a part of the EWWF, and our first Stampede Saturday Night card! The following contest is a FALLS COUNT ANYWHERE Match, for the IRONMAN Title. Introducing first, already in the right, to my right... from the Dark side... Weighing in at 245 lbs... representing the HELL ON EARTH... This is IMMORTAL DOOMSDAY!!!! [The crowd begins to boo, Doomsday looks around with a dirty look on his face, flipping people off, and taunting the crowd to "shut up". Suddenly, the lights shut off. A purple spotlight shines down on the platform to reveal Phantom standing their. He has on black shorts and a black shirt. His long black hair hangs wet in front of his sadistic red face. He has the EWWF Ironman Championship draped over his shoulder, and his torch named Lucifer grasped in the other hand. "Ironman" By Black Sabbath starts to blast over the PA. Phantom looks about as the heavy riffs fill the arena...] Brody Stevens: And now, our Ironman Champion of the EWWF! From Parts Unknown. Weight... Unknown. Please welcome... PHANTOM!!!! Ryan Reynolds: Phantom raises the sign of the devil as fire engulfs him from the platform. He steps out of the flames unscaved, and stalks down to the ring. As he walks over the top rope, he rushes after Immortal Doomsday. Extreme Gene: Yeah he does, I.D is in the corner, being pummeled on by our Ironman Champion, Phantom. Taking his Ironman title, out of mockery, he slams it down over the head of Immortal Doomsday! That title isn't a weapon! He'd better watch out, or our EWWF Vice President will "fire" him, like he did Harvester of Sorrow... Ryan Reynolds: You know... I don't get that. Harvester of Sorrow, one of the greatest Extreme Champions of EWWF History, and Clint Cornell just fired him with a snap of the fingers. No questions asked. Extreme Gene: LOOK AT THIS! Phantom has Immortal Doomsday over his head with a Gorilla press slam... Oh no! HE THREW HIM INTO THE FANS! The fans all moved out of the way! My goodness! Ryan Reynolds: Take a seat, Immortal Doomsday! Extreme Gene: Shyeah... Might as well, eh? Phantom, standing over seven feet tall, easily, but slowly walks over the top rope. What's he doing? Why is he coming over here? Ryan, I suggest you move. He's grabbed my partner's chair, hahaha, you look goofy standing up, with that head set on... What's Phantom doing? He's now going after Immortal Doomsday, who's laying across 3 rows of chairs, looking dismantled. Phantom raises Ryan's chair over his head. What's this?! What's Outsider doing here? He has nothing to do with this match! Ryan Reynolds: He's fending for a brother! Immortal Doomsday is part of Hell on Earth, a stable in which Outsider helped start! Extreme Gene: True that. He grabs the chair from Phantom! Phantom turns around, and is LEVELED!!!! Phantom is still up on his feet, but his head is busted! One chair shot! That tells you how hard Outsider swung! Phantom is dayzed but is still up, and out on his feet! Outsider shoves the chair into Phantom's chest... Phantom leans forward... Outsider picks Phantom up with a Crusafix Powerbomb.... OUTSIDE DEVESTATION ONTO THE CHAIRS!!!!! Ryan Reynolds: He put him in the "Outside" Devestation! What strength. Very Impressive on the part of the "Extreme Being" Outsider. Immortal Doomsday is slowly getting up with the help of the Outsider. The referee is waving for security! Outsider is being escourted to the backstage area... With this, Immortal Doomsday falls back down onto the chairs, taking a huge bump from the inside, onto the chairs. Both men are down. Referee Billy Gilmour is counting them both down. Extreme Gene: Those two men are in a world of hurt. The Fans in the Corestates Spectrum are all over this one! They are throwing food, drinks, and all sorts of debrit at Phantom and Doomsday! The referee is waving for security once again to control the fans! Immortal Doomsday is first up, and picks Phantom up by the hair. He throws him over the security guard rail, and into the middle aisle way! What's Doomsday doing? He balances himself up on the rail... A FULL FLIP, landing on his back! He missed! Phantom is up! He sees grabs Doomsday by the head, and shoves it in between the folding part of the chair! HE'S GONNA SNAP IMMORTAL DOOMSDAY'S NECK!!! Ryan Reynolds: I've seen many careers end this way! This could be devistating! He sets Immortal Doomsday on the floor... A LEG DROP ACROSS HIS FACE!!! THE CAMERA HAS GOTTEN GRAPHIC ON US!!! We heard his neck snap!!!! He could've broken his neck! Phantom looks down with a sadistic smile! What a crazed maniac!! Phantom with the cover... Extreme Gene: Referee Billy Gilmour with the count.... ONE! TWO! THREE! The bell sounds, and our Ironman Champion status stays the same. Phantom, your Ironman champion, by dropping a leg drop across Immortal Doomsday's face in between a chair! EMT's are rushing down the aisle, where Immortal Doomsday lays lifeless! Phantom will have hell to pay with HOE. Let's cut to a commercial break... next up, we have "Flamethrower" Rob Dalton facing long time nemisis "Hellkid" Johnny Lords. ======================== Commercial Break Part 2 up next... ========================
