Sky’s The Limit

18 June 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This is another stable RP except this one features the Tag Team of the stable: The New Wave (Daniel Karby: [detail withheld] ) (Ken Stone: [detail withheld] ) who are fighting this Sundayf or the #1 contedership of the Tag Titles. Once again... Comments appreciated.. -Bobby ---BEGIN TRANSMISSION--- [The arena is sold out and the fans are screaming for the next segment of the EWWF house show, which has stopped at the Cobb County Civic Center in Atlanta, Georgia. The lights suddenly go out in the small place and a bright red spotlight hit the entrance way. Above the entrance, a small video wall is setup and is showing clips and footage of The New Revolutions past and present day matches, events, etc. "10's" by Pantera begins to play in the backround. The crowd goes balistic and start the chant of "DANGEROUS, DANGEROUS, DANGEROUS!!!" over and over again. "Dangerous" Daniel Karby walks out of the entrance way, but turns back again. A few seconds go by and the chants continue. Karby then comes back out and shakes his head in disagreement with the fans. He is wearing a black "FBK Sucks Cock" t-shirt, black jeans with "TNR" spraypainted on the legs. He also has on his black wrestling boots and his hair is down covering his half-bearded face. After a few seconds of taunting the fans, he walks down toward the ring. Trash is coming from every angle as he climbs into the ring. He even picks some of it up and throws it back in a comical way. Karby sees the ring announcer standing in the corner. Just as the ring announcer comes out to hand him the mic, Karby does a short-arm running clothesline on the EWWF official and takes the mic. The music continues and the lights return to normal] Karby: Right now, I want to bring out my entrusted tag team partner. A man who I have the utmost respect for ever since he smacked the sh*t out of me with a steel chair. You all know him as "The Master" but The New Revolution only knows him as the most miserable son of a bitch to fight in the ring. He has changed nothing about himself in the past years, except for the poisonous attitude that has spread like an infectious disease! So what I want right now is for all you stupid ass nobodies to welcome one half of the FUTURE EWWF Tag Team Champions of the World....THE NEEEEEWWWWW WAAAAAVVVEE!!!!! Here is...."THE MASTER" KEN STONE!!!!! ["More Human than Human begins to play over the p.a. system and "The Master" Ken Stone steps out from behind the curtain. He has on a pair of black wind pants, and a red tank top. On the tank top, there is a "The New Revolution/New Wave" graphic. Stone's arms and chest are pumping and his face is just as psychotic as ever. He also has on a pair of "adidas" wrestling shoes and a pair of black, padded, gloves. His short, black hair is dripping wet. He stands about five foot infront of the curtain and raises both of his hands. While doing this, he let's out a VERY loud scream.He puts his hands back down and starts walking to the ring. On his way down there, he sees something. He get's a HUGE smile on his face and walks over toward the crowd. He get's a strangly happy look in his eyes and picks up a metal chair. He bashes his head with it a couple times and than drops in on the aisle-way. He continues and get's in the ring. He stands next to Karby after working the crowd a bit. He still has a very physchotic look in his eye. He just stands there and stares into the camera.] Karby: Now that we are together in the same ring, Superfreaks, you can get a look at the f*cked up individuals that are coming for your titles. Yeah, you heard me right, we talked it over and we accept your challenge to the three on three matchup. If you can get the Misfits ass up off the couch for two minutes and have him in the ring with the both of ya, we'll beat your asses and take the titles. FBK, if you have a problem with me accepting these terms, then get your ass out here now. I'm willing to do anything for the EWWF Tag Titles and that's the bottom line there. Speaking of which [looking down at his "FBK Sucks Cock" t-shirt]...these things have got to go. Whoever came up with the concept of these f*ckin shirts must have been so goddamn bored that they decided to market them and earn a few bucks. Seeing how they couldn't wrestle to earn a living. You people must be willing to spend your money on anything if you buy these damn shirts! If you're that desperate to spend money on useless things, I can give you Ms. Bobby Rollins phone number if you need it!!!! With that being said, whoever made these shirts are the ones who "Suck Cock"!! [Karby reaches down and rips the t-shirt off his body. Stone takes a lighter from his pocket and lights the shirt on fire. As it burns, Stone takes the mic and speaks up] Stone: LOOK AT THIS! This [pointing to the burning tee shirt] is what The New Revolution is all about! This fire symbolized the rage that each and everyone of us have inside. Since the Dangerous One and The Master have joined TNR, we have let out rage and anger we never thought we had in us. Outside of the ring, I used to be the calmest and nicesst guy you could meet. If you don't believe me ask Daniel. But than something happened, the best thing ever. I was brought here, by your WORLD CHAMPION the Full Blooded Killah, to help him. He taught me how to let out all this anger and animosity which I have inside of me. I used to keep it all inside, and than I would just burst. [Stone starts shaking uncontrolably. Spit flys from his mouth and sweat from his hair.] _BANG_ [Stone "explodes", frightening about 95% of the people in the arena with his loud scream.] But now, this RAGE and ANGER isn't kept inside anymore. Now, I am one f*cked up individual! And let me tell you this, right now, I am _PUMPED UP_ baby. It has brought me to a new level with my wrestling. I no longer feel sorry for the guy I just left laying on the mat with a broken leg; I now longer feel sorry for that punk who dogged my friend out and is now in a coma. I just don't give a damn anymore! I mean look at me. Right now, I am bleeding out of my head(due to the chair shots earlier), and you know what, I LOVE IT!!! I have never felt better! Ya know why? [Stone looks like he is gonna explode again, but than, he suddenly calms down. His voice is monotoned and he continues with his speech.] Stone: 'Cause I love pain. I love inflicting it and I love receiving it. It's as simple as that. South Syndicate Soldiers, the "triple S". You guys are pretty damn happy with yourselves huh. I mean, you had to come down and beat on those little losers the Mighty Mighty Posers on Warfare. Comeon fellas, these guys are little shrimps, give 'em a break, hell, I know I would... and than you beat on the "niggas" of the EWWF, Ice Pick and Koo Jo. I don't even want to start talking about them, cause I could be here the whole damn night. Okay, so.. on to the SSS. "Mr Cheesepuffs" Matt Allen and "The Real Flop" Kenneth Morlock seem to really be killing it in the tag team ranks here in the EWWF. Comeon fellas, how hard can it be to beat a team like IcePick and KooJo, or the little shrimps the Mighty Mighty Posers, or even our silent "world's tag team champions", the Superfreaks. Geez guys, me and my buddy Dangerous over here haven't had a match in the EWWF yet, and we're ranked #2. That shows all you how strong the tag team division is here. Now, when we finally get the chance to get a match, we'll show all you doubtfuls that we can, and will kick...some...ass. Now, looking past those two little punks, let's move on. The Superfreaks. Guys, guys, guys, I can't believe what you are doing to yourselves, or should I say, doing to the "suits" here in the EWWF. It must be pretty damn good, or you wouldn't still have the belts. Tellez, you're on the executive commitee here in the EWWF, why don't you drop me a line explaining how in the hell these punks still have those damn titles. [The crowd, along with "Dangerous" Daniel Karby, laughs at that comment. Stone waits for them to calm down and than continues with his speech.] Stone: Superfreaks, once we get done disposing the rest of the trash here in the EWWF, we'll get to you. You better be counting your days with those titles, 'cause there isn't gonna be many more of 'em. Superfreaks, the tension is building, and it's just a matter of time before the New Wave has what is currently yours, those tag team titles. [Stone hands the mic to Karby. Stone than starts yelling, and banging on his head, waiting for the pain to overtake his body. After a few seconds of this, he stops and stares into the camera. Karby begins to talk once again.] Karby: Superfreaks, you two assholes have pissed us off for the last time. I get so sick of watching you two come out here on EWWF TV and making a mockery of those belts you hold. You both make the EWWF tag team division look like a bunch of f*ckin circus animals. At Wednesday Warfare, we beat the hell out of Tony Tellez and proved that we are not the corporate puppets you people think we are. We don't kiss ass, and we certainly don't....SUCK IT! So, Superfreaks, to make it final, yeah we do accept the three on three matchup. When we win, FBK keeps the EWWF World Title and "The Master" and myself take those title from you two and proclaim ourselves the most f*cked up men to hold these titles. And the number one contenders, SSS....you two couldn't even dream of beating us on your best day. In fact, we'll beat your asses right here, right now!! Whatta say? We know your sorry asses are back there, so why don't you come out and show why you're number one contenders! [A few seconds go by and THE NEW WAVE waits in the ring for the SSS entrance music to cue.] Karby: DAMN! Come on boys, we ain't got all night...get your asses out here now!!! ["For Whom The Bell Tolls (The Irony Of It All)" by Metallica BLARES over the PA and Karby and Stone drops their heads in the ring in disappointment. Scorpio is weilding a chair and he runs to the ring and slides in. He is holding the chair, preparing to hit someone with it! What is this? Scorpio has the mic!] SCORPIO: "You want someone to fight?!?! I'm right here bitches! Fight this! Fight the REAL THING, not some pussies who can't stick up for themselves . . ." [Karby and Stone stare deeply into Scorpio's eyes and he returns the favor . . . then then all three men break out into a hysterical laughter! Scorpio puts the chair down and sits in it; he continues.] SCORPIO: "I could take you punks . . . [Chuckles] Well, I see the South Syndicate Soldiers are showing their New Breed pride and representing the New Breed well by being bitches. I mean c'mon . . . SSS is _RANKED HIGHER_ than you guys, doesn't that mean they could take you? Aren't those rankings perfect? Obviously they aren't because that illiterate piece of shit Outsider is ranked ahead of me. The EWWF is going to shit. They have a man who has greasy hair, and can't speak three sentences without stuttering or mispronouncing a word as the third ranked champion of the whole promotion and another man who hangs out and sleeps in his basement all day as another high ranked man, a "superstar" if you will. How can these two men standing across the ring from me not hold the tag titles? The EWWF Tag Team Champions are MUTE, and these men appear at _EVERY_ show and what do they get? NOTHING. Bullshit? I believe so . . . EWWF, there are ALOT of changes that are about to happen here in the EWWF, and The New Revolution is the one bringing them about. Want to try and stop us? Not possible . . . TNR is unstoppable. We are like a Mack truck about to slam into a Corvette . . . I'm putting my wager on the truck." [ Before Scorpio can even finish "Let Me Clear My Throat" by DJ Kool blares throughout the arena as the fans rise to their feet. FBK emerges from the entrance way wearing a "Suck FBK's Cock" T-Shirt along with his black wrestling tights with TNR on one side and FBK on the other. He has a million dollar smile on his face and he pulls a mic out of his tights. He steps in the ring and looks at the three men. He then shakes each of their hands. He puts the mic to his mouth . . . ] FBK: THE MACK TRUCK HAS ARRIVED!!!! Now NEW WAVE, I've listened to what you said, and you have somewhat of a point. You WILL beat the living crap out of Triple S . . . but the conspiracy throughout all this is why are you even fighting them? You should have that number one contender spot . . . hell, you should have the EWWF Tag Titles around your waists!!! As Scorpio said, you are at ALL EWWF shows each week and give a mic spot along with us. You speak unlike the muted Tag Champions. All they are doing is bringing the tag division down, but with the NEW WAVE as EWWF champions, the tag division will be better than ever and I can promise you that. So when you go out there and face Triple S, don't just beat them . . . KILL THEM!!!! As for the tag team of the pussy Outsider, and the "Gay King" Ricky Taylor, The New Revolution is going two for two on Sunday night and you'll fall victim to Scorpio and myself. We're the best, and look at it this way, after you lose . . . you can say you fought the best . . . AND LOST!!! One last thing, for all you lovely ladies that see me wearing the new and improved FBK T-Shirts, if you want to take on the responsibility of what the shirt reads, find me in the back. [ All four men gather close to the mic and take a deep breath . . . ] TOGETHER: Where ever there is NEW REVOLUTION . . . ALL HELL WILL BE RAISED!!!! [ FBK drops the mic as all four men strut around the ring. They then step out of the ring as women dive towards them and slap a few hands in the crowd. They walk through the curtain into the locker room area . . . ]
