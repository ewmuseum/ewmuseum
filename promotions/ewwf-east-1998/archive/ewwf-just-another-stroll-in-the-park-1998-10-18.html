EWWF East — Roleplay

Just Another Stroll In The Park

18 October 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This RP was used in the EWWF about the upcoming Rage In The Cage tournament. EWWF is currently my only fed after returning from a 3 month layoff so any ELITE feds, throw some offers my way, along with a recent RP packet. Thank you and goodnight. =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- [SCENE: A beautiful park. The large trees' leaves are changing colors, or according to Irish Michael Andrews Shamrock: colours, and the grass is green as can be. A gorgeous scenery as we scroll the landscape then our little tour of the area is interrupted by the sight of the black trenchcoat, black boot, red and black horned mask wearing Suicide. He stands with an awkward smirk upon his face, in a total standstill. He begins to walk at a leisurely pace and the EWWF cameras zoom in and follow him...] "It's a beautiful day in the neighborhood, eh? Yeah, it's great to be the best participant _BY FAR_, in the Rage In The Cage tournament. Who looks to provide me any challenge? As I was scanning the list of challengers, I found that ... NO ONE can hold a candle to me. With the rambling, bumbling, mispronouncing, contradicting, self-inflicting IDIOTS the EWWF has me placed up against this tournament should be nothing more than a walk in the park. Let me take you for a stroll on the reality of this title tournament." [Suicide ponders momentarily then resumes.] "Round one. My first match is against Kenneth Morlock of the South Syndicate Soldiers. I have a little history on this tag team and this man himself here compiled by my Hell On Earth teammates." [Pulls a slip of paper from his trenchcoat pocket. He reads ...] "Tag team from Memphis, Tennessee, hence their name. Former members of the United Wrestling Organization: UWO. Reportedly unsuccessful in the UWO, not confirmed to be totally true; unsuccessful in EWWF-- confirmed. Kennth Morlock: Weaker member of the team, known to model his style after a well known North Carolina native with an accomplished career. Uses the figure four, much less effective than this known legend's. No extreme strengths-- nothing important in his offense to watch out for." [Suicide rips the paper and drops it.] "The man is a nothing. I have seen this in my short tenure so far in the EWWF, and the biography tells it well. Kenny, I have no fears going into this match. You pose no real threat to me, you are nothing but a stepping stone in my climb to the Extreme Title. So Morlock, it's a lost cause. Use this match as a LEARNING experience instead of actually going into this match thinking you are a legitimate contender for the title. There is a point to all this, and it's simple: Inexperienced singles wrestlers don't beat legends, and Kenny Morlock _WON'T_ beat Suicide." [He continues to wearily stroll then he resumes his speaking.] "People are probably wondering if I am overlooking Kenny boy Morlock, but the truth is, I can overlook Morlock. I have prepared for this moment: the chance at the Extreme Title, for months. I am OVERconditioned, OVERprepared, and UNDERrated. The EWWF brass, including Clint, does not believe in the ability of Suicide, but of course they all will be proved WROOOONG. Morlock, the end is near." [Pause. Then we see one of Rob Dalton's little minis holding one of those retarded signs, behind him is a fierce thunderstorm ... this one says, "Oh God! He gonna kill Kenny!"] "Now for the rest of the tournament ... I still don't have a legitimate challenger. We have CrossCut, who knew he could never in his entire life better his performance in the battle royal, giving us the sob story about his knee through Clint Cornell. CrossCut knows that Suicide was out for him, and what happens? He comes up lame with a fake injury. I'm sure the knee is injured. I think CrossCut isnt showing up because he doesn't need Suicide beating up on his pride at Rage In The Cage. "Immortal Doomsday, my colleague, also seems to have fallen off the face of the earth. I believe in you Doomsday, I know you are either nursing an injury, or attending to Hell On Earth business-- recruiting possibly? Keep it up Doomsday, I'll represent you and HOE well. "'Deadly' Vic Sexon ... FAR from talented, even farther from 'Deadly'. Straight out of the school of Devilman Christian Alciea he is a name changing, personality conflicting man with a HUGE lack of wrestling ability. I can't believe the striking similarities between the two of you. So close that I can foresee you both suffering the same fate at Rage In The Cage-- A bonecrushing, heartbreaking loss. Another victim of Suicidal Tendencies..." [Chuckle.] "Is it cold out here?... oh wait that's just the gust from Shiver's air conditioner while he sleeps and lets that poser Poe speak for him. Shiver you don't have half a brain, _that_ is why you don't speak, and Poe is a brain who has no athleticism. Put them together and what does that ALMOST equal? SUICIDE! Remeber Shiver-- 'Hooked On Phonics' is helping improve eighty five percent of America's illiterates, you can be helped too!" [He stifles his laughter and continues ...] "Now X-Treme versus Luni is an interesting matchup considering both are possibly the biggest posers in the promotion. Luni I have lost all respect for you that I claimed to have. You are not hardcore. You proved to be nothing but a filler for that battle royal. You don't even seem disappointed about your loss. You show up every now and then and promise big things ... what have you done to back these up?... Absolutely nothing! You suck Luni. As much as I hate to say it, and I always hate to say it, but you suck. Why do you waste your time? Go home and cry you pathetic human being. You do not belong here. [Makes a pushing motion with his arms] Shoo, go away. "And his opponent X-Treme, the poser drug addict. You are nothing but a hyperactive lab rat for the EWWF. The same thing goes for you as it did for Luni: You don't belong here ... leave my presence before I make you leave ... and that is not a threat X-Treme, it is a _PROMISE_" [He becomes tense, and mad ... After hesitating he speaks again.] "What am I getting worked up over? No one gets mad during a stroll in the neighborhood park, right? Good. Now, Outsider, my man, good luck in the tournament. If it comes down to it, you're mine, I own you, hopefully it will not come to that but with the roll Hell On Earth has been on I would not be surprised if we met up somewhere down the road inside that barbwire cell. Your opponent, 'Firestarter' Higor Rosa ... or wait, is it 'Rebel' Dave Blood?... I'm all confused. Those two guys are so alike, it is so weird. It's like they are the same guy or something! Jesus... you never know with these clones running around the industry nowadays. Anyway, Higor, you won't get past my boy OT, so I'm not worried about meeting you in that cell." [A long pause.] "Jason Cline. A 'Prodigy'? Of who? Who taught you so well Jason? Obviously no one up to the standards of Suicide because _I_ am the number one seed in this tourney, not you. Boy, if we meet in the second round I am promising you a whooping that a mentor would give to his prodigy because you need to learn the ropes. You want to be a superstar? I'll whip your ass into shape and we'll see how far you can take it. Watch me wrestle a few matches Cline, you'll learn something that no one can teach you through words-- how to win. Now your opponent, Auntie Path. What can I say about you that I already have not said? There is not much. I have covered your weaknesses, I know your one and only strength. The video tapes do not lie Auntie Em... I mean Path. Time will tell who knows more and who holds the key to the Extreme Title. Now, if you will excuse me I want to practice for the eighteenth..." [Suicide smirks that oh-so-wicked smirk and continues his stroll through the park, leisurely and calmly. A cakewalk ... Is this foreshadowing for Rage In The Cage?... Time will tell ... FADE.]
