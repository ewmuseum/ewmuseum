EWWF East — Roleplay

FBK Roleplay

9 October 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[ Camera opens to the countryside of the United States. The area is in the middle of nowhere, just fields and fields of grain. The winds are blowing heavily and the sky is dark. There is a slight drizzle that covers the camera lens. Then the camera man reveals his face, to show FBK. FBK stares into the camera, not moving a muscle. The camera turns more toward black second by second, until nothing is seen or heard, except a black screen, which is paused for a few moments. ] [ A few seconds later, the camera re-opens. The scene; a dark cave, with an unknown location. The cave is cold and wet. Its so quiet, you can hear dripping water hit the ground. There is a small pond in the center of the cave which Clint Cornell hangs over, in a cage. Cornell is all taped and tied up. There are also some barrells surrounding the cave, with an unknown substance within. The camera man sets the camera on a barrell. The camera man still being FBK. FBK then sits in front of the camera on a barrell. ] [ FBK still wears his jail uniform, not being able to change after his escape from jail, and then EWWF Headquarters. FBK is dripping wet, looking as he just bathed in that small pond. He has a rag over his head, slightly shredded up. FBK takes a deep breath and begins to speak. ] FULL BLOOD KILLAH: I told you that you'd never find me, and you still haven't. EWWF, fans, corporate suits, and whoever the hell else this concerns, your Vice President is still my hostage, your Vice President is still my _BITCH_! [Groans are heard from the cage in which Cornell sits in.] Shut up Cornell. EWWF, I tried to be Mr. Nice Guy, I tried to be nice to the fans, and I tried to be your perfect champion face. But I just couldn't take it anymore. Hiring Cornell as your Vice President didn't help either. Me and him didn't hit it off, and therefore we stand with a bad acquaintance. And that's one thing you don't want . . . a bad acquaintance with FBK. Clint Cornell is a living example, at least not living for long, heh heh. [FBK pulls the gun out of his pocket and shows it to the camera.] But that's not the matter at hand right now, the matter at hand right now is "Misfit" Chris Douvalas. [ FBK takes a breath with pissed off face on. He doesn't look happy, actually, very angry. He keeps his breath and continues speaking . . . ] FULL BLOOD KILLAH: Well Douvalas, Hellkid once said I was the worst heel in the game today, it was a joke as FBK playing a heel. And he was right in a few ways because I can take criticism, "The Chris." Well Douvalas, even though I know you can't take critism, I'll dish some out anyways. Chris, the only thing worse then me playing heel, is you trying to play heel. Its a god damn joke! Is it just a coincidence or are you always having a corporate "suit" near you, and you end up beating on him. Does that boost up your confidence or something? Beating on a corporate suit? I gotta try it I guess, maybe it will help. Man, if you're gonna do something, make it creative. Hang the Vice President in a cage over some water or some wacky shit. But whenever I get ready to view a Douvalas interview, I'm prepared to see Krissfit Lugolus beat on some corporate suit, heh heh. [FBK takes a breath] Douvalas, we all know you're a cover up, we know you're a _TRUE_ corporate image. That's why you pull all this shit off, beating on corporate suits. If it wasn't for Clint Cornell, you'd be number two contender for the World Title and Dalton number one. You know it, I know it, Cornell knows it, and Dalton knows it. Well, Dalton would've given me a challenge, facing you is like having a day off, except just being present at your job. Douvalas, I like how you say you're the soon to be World Champ. Unless I'm "soon to be" retiring, you ain't nowhere near being "soon to be" World Champ. Misfit, I respected you at one point, but I ain't gonna bullshit you and tell you me and you are gonna have a nice, respectable match, because I'd be full of shit. I'm not going into this match to win, to walk out champion . . . I'm going in to end your _WORTHLESS_ career, put you on the shelf for life. Its time for World Champion FBK to put up and shut up, that time is now for me. I'm ready to put up Douvalas, and you're in for a cold day in hell . . . on earth that is. I've been silent for weeks now and I've done is gotten criticized for it which is bullshit. I was still at every card giving every fan what they wanna see because I'm the fucking show; the one and the only. Misfit, you are nowhere near my level, and its time I showed every piece of shit in the EWWF the truth . . . that FBK is the most dominant force in EWWF history. You may have won the UWO Title, but _BUSH_ leagues like that don't count around here; and UWO is the definition of _BUSH LEAGUE_! Speaking of bush leagues, let me move onto SEWF real quick. Shaw, as far as I'm cornerned you're term as HOE manager is through. You had to come into EWWF and bring all that SEWF bullshit with you. Shaw, I thought you were a nice guy when I hired you for the HOE managerial position, but all you did was bullshit me, and I'm not the type of person to get bullshitted, maybe bullshit, but not get it done to me. Well let me tell you this much Shaw, I'll give you the opportunity to walk and take the SEWF with you, but if you choose to deny my offer, all you're doing is signing an early contract to disband your wrestling promotion. Shaw, you step into _ANY_ ring, you're in my warzone . . . my house . . . and nobody comes into my house and pushes me around, not Misfit, not David Shaw, not SEWF, _NO ONE_. Shaw, get a tissue ready because you'll be crying your eyes out in a second. You call your promotion better than EWWF? If its so great, how come I beat your World Champion, Johnny Lords or whatever name he goes by in SEWF, not once, not twice, but _THREE_ times. If its so great how come I've beaten your TV Champion, Ricky Taylor, not once, but _TWICE_. Shaw, and if you don't play your cards right, you'll be the third man added to that list with a big fat ass kicking next to your name. So as far as I'm concerned, take your "Dynamic DDT", take your sad excuse for a wrestling federation, and take your sorry ass and walk . . . or else . . . [ FBK stops out of breath because he is going crazy. His face is bright red and he his panting like crazy. Drips of sweat run down his face until he wipes them off with the sleeve of his shirt. FBK then catches his breath and begins to talk again, still not very happy. ] FULL BLOOD KILLAH: Back to Misfit, the self proclaimed "star" of the East. I found that pretty funny Douvalas, everyone here to see you? Not only do you wanna be champion like FBK, but you have to steal his gimmick as well? No one wants to see the crappy youngster Chris Douvalas in the ring, they want to see the _SHOW_, FBK get in the ring, give them what they paid for and then they can go home all happy. But Douvalas, you bring in about one-tenth of the ratings I do, and if you think I'm bullshiting you, lets just look at my last segment on EWWF TV, where I get arrested backstage at Streetfight Sunday Night. All your ratings ever on a show probably don't even add up to that I bet. And Misfit, Tellez was not threated by getting contract offers from SEWF to his stars, because I wasn't offered one, actually I was, but it was turned down. The best move that could happen right now is Douvalas go to SEWF, it would get rid of the crying, whining, and bullshit. Three things that annoy the hell out of me in EWWF. And Clint Cornell, EWWF Front Office, there is _NO_ way I am jobbing this title to Douvalas just so you can keep him pleased in EWWF . . . if he's giving you a hard time, give him the big boot and say "buh bye baby!" We could certainly do fine without him, no doubt on my mind. [FBK pauses once again and takes a breather.] Douvalas, I simply came down to the ring during your match on Streetfight to make sure Dalton didn't get screwed because the last thing we need in EWWF is more bitching and moaning. I don't like Dalton, and I also don't like you so I didn't care who I face . . . sure I thought I'd get a few early hits in, but I didn't want Clint Cornell doing anything stupid. And as long as Cornell's up in that cage, he won't do anything stupid, heh heh. As for the spotlight, its always on me buddy . . . nowhere near you. Just remember this . . . the spotlight always is on the show, and thats what I am. If I'm already milked dry, why is the gold still around my waist asshole? They would book me week in and week out to defend my title if I was already milked dry, smart ass. They don't book me for matches often because I get the ratings whenever a mic is put to my mouth, unlike you who lacks on the mic. Sure you're an exceptional wrestler, not up to par with a guy like me, or the rest of HOE for that matter, but you do absolutely _ZERO_ on the mic. The fans usually take their bathroom breaks when you pick up a mic, its down right boring. You're the real corporate image Douvalas, all you are is a walking contradiction. You try and look like some bad ass rebel, get some guys from the back area to come out to try and stand up to you only for you to beat on them. Then when you walk into the back again they hand you the money and you seal your lips to their ass again. You're probably looking for this cave right now to bail Cornell out of here and save the day as the true corporate image, you're a poser Douvalas, a god damn poser. And by the way, Mr. Walking Contradiction, if words were wisdom you'd be speaking more . . . then why aren't you speaking still? Exactly, because you're out of words, you're smart ass catch phrases. You stepped in my way, now you've got hell to pay. Douvalas, go home, grab a tissue and have a good cry. Then after you're done with that, try and dig your way out of that big, smelly pile of critism I just buried you in. Just remember one thing . . . I . . . am . . . Spartacus . . . [ FBK puts his hand over the camera as the screen grows darker and darker with a softer sound . . . FADE. ]
