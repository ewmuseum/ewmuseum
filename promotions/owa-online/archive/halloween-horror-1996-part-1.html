Halloween Horror: The Wheel of Torture's Revenge, Part One

27 October 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
{The creepy intro, featuring the new OWA Logo, animated sequence inserted here) [Ed] Thank you and welcome to Halloween Horror. I'm Ed Bagel and with me is the non-winner from last night's award ceremony... [Biff] Shut up.. not like you won anything.. [Ed] I wasn't saying that I would win anything.. we are about to get things underway here.. we are preparing the cage for the first match in Round 2 of the Wheel of Torture Tournament.. and as of yet.. we have not heard a word from Amazon Woman.. she's scheduled to take on Consuelo Salyards inside the steel cage in our opening contest. [Biff] I'm telling ya.. she's outta the OWA for good, pal.. [Ed] Let's go to highlights of the first round in this tournement, held earlier today. ------------------------------- [Announcer] The first turn of the Wheel landed on 'Amatuer Match'.. and is was Consuelo Salyards besting Asian Invasion in that match... (Clips shows Consuelo getting the one count on Invasion, after Invasion was distracted by Johnny Fate.) [Announcer] Former OWA Champion Stacks Coltrain advanced over Gustaffson in a Coal Miner's Glove Match. This match depended on speed which was on Stack's side during this contest, in which, under normal circumstances would have put Stacks as the underdog. {Clip shows Stacks coming off the turnbuckle nailing Gus with the glove and getting the pinfall) [Announcer] In the third match, Chaos prevailed over The Shocker in a First Blood match. (Scene shows Chaos whipping Shocker into the ringpost, opening up a nice cut on his forehead..) [Announcer] The next contest saw JetStream and John Justice do battle in an Over The Top Rope match. This time it was newcomer John Justice getting the quick win, by slamming JetStream out of the ring for the win. In the fifth match, Pretty Boy Monty prevailed over former OWA TV Champ, Johnny Fate in a submission match.. (Monty whips Fate into the post, arm first.. later Monty applies the Reverse Arm Bar and forces Fate to submit) [Announcer] Match six saw Traci Lane defeated Johnny Blades in a Street Fight... {Both bloody and battered, Lane executes the Gorilla Press Piledriver for the win) [Announcer] And in our final opening round match, Anthony Hazard defeated Shogun in a parking lot match... (Hazard runs the head of Shogun through a car window.. and pins him on the sidewalk...) [Announcer] With those out of the way, we move on the Round 2.. back to Ed and Biff! ------------------------- [Ed] The cage is up.. and it'll be Amazon Woman taking on Consuelo Salyards.. Let's go down to ringside... [Announcer] Ladies and Gentlemen, this match starts Round Two of the Wheel of Torture tournament.. it'll be held inside this steel cage. You can win the match by pinfall, or being the first to escape the cage and touch the ground with your feet. Here are your opponents.. first, being accompanied to the ring by the giant Gustaffson, weighing in at 126 pounds, here is the 'Latin Lover'.. Consuelo Salyards!! And her opponent.. last years winner in this very tournament.. she weighs in at 165 pounds.. here is Amazon Woman... {Consuelo steps into the cage.. while Amazon Woman's music plays, no one actually comes through the ropes.. finally the music stops.. an official comes into the ring and consults with the referee, when all of a sudden the curtains part and Amazon Woman walks down the aisle.. she has a very determined look on her face... her hair is still cut short.. she stops when she gets to the cage and looks up and down it.. she climbs the stairs and enters the cage and the crowd goes wild... Amazon Woman approached Consuelo Salyards and extends a hand.. Consuelo shakes the hand a little reluctantly, and Amazon goes back to her corner, never once does she smile. The official steps out of the cage and closes the door. The bell rings.) [Ed] Amazon Woman is here! [Biff] Feh.. she won't stay long.. not... with Freddy around... [Ed] Lock up by the two.. Consuelo with a whip.. reversal by Amazon Woman.. Amazon with a clothesline... Consuelo takes her over with a crucific... one.. kickout by Amazon.. Consuelo off the ropes.. cross body block.. no.. Amazon catches her... [Biff] Running powerslam by Amazon Woman.. cover.. one.. two.. kickout by Consuelo... that's her finisher, but it's a little too early to be putting anyone away... Amazon off the ropes, but Consuelo catches her with a hotshot... cover.. one.. two.. kickout by Amazon.. [Ed] Whip by Consuelo.. reversal by Amazon.. Consuelo comes off the ropes with a cross body block, but Amazon ducks and Consuelo flies into the cage! Belly to back suplex by Amazon Woman and cover.. one.. two.. kickout by Consuelo.. [Biff] Amazon with a whip.. reversal by Consuelo.. spinebuster slam by the Femme.. she leaps up and begins to climb the cage, but Amazon Woman is up lifts her up with a Gorilla Press and slam.. Amazon Woman climbs to the second turnbuckle and nails Consuelo with an elbowdrop.. [Ed] Amazon goes for the door.. the outside ref opens the door.. but Consuelo hits her with a knee to the back.. Consuelo with a cross corner whip.. and dropkick.. Consuelo with a Front Flipping Leg drop from the second rope.. it could be over.. [Biff] She should get out of there.. no.. cover... one.. two.. thre.. NO! Kickout by Amazon Woman.. Consuelo whips Amazon Woman face first into one side of the cage.. then the other.. Consuelo off the ropes and nails Amazon with a flying forearm.. Consuelo begins to climb out of the cage... [Ed] Consuelo is on the top turnbuckle and Amazon Woman finally is getting to her feet.. but Consuelo is putting a leg over the cage.. here comes Amazon Woman, climbing up.. a right cross by Amazon and she rams the head of Consuelo into the cage. [Biff] Now what's she doing? Oh my.. she's standing on the top turnbuckle and she's Gorilla pressing Consuelo.. and slammed.. that had to be a good 15 or more feet.. Amazon steps over the cage and begins to descend.. [Ed] Hold on.. COnsuelo is heading for the door.. but no dice as Amazon drops from midway to the floor... [Announcer] Winner of the match, in 16:52 and advancing in the tournament.. Amazon Woman! [Ed] Let's go to Elaine Bryant and Shelly Marks with the spin of the wheel. ------------- [Elaine] Thanks Ed.. we're waiting for the cage to be taken down at ringside.. Shelly if you will please spin the Wheel and we'll see what our next match will be... [Shelly] Okay.. here we go.. (Shelly spins the wheel...) [Shelly] This thing spins so long... here we go.. and it looks like.. it'll be Stacks Coltrain vs. Chaos.. and it'll be a strap match! [Elaine] There ya have it.. back to you, Ed and Biff] ------------- [Ed] They're still taking down the cage.. so let's go to Myers Watterson who has information regarding the next big OWA Event.. A Holiday In Hell. ------------- [Myers] Welcome to this update... Holiday in Hell is the next big event for the OWA. It'll feature eight teams in a tournament format. The first round will be normal eight-wrestler tag team matches. The winners advance to the second round. The second round consists of tag team elimination matches. Only the survivors advance to the finals! The finals will consist of War Games. Now.. the following teams have been confirmed for War Games: Team Plague with Team Captain Freddy Fever, OWA Champion Executioner, and the Chiefs.. and for Team Elite, team captian Anthony Hazard, The Shocker and The Extremists. Other teams will be announced in the near future. Holiday In Hell will be held on December 22nd. Let's go back to Ed and Biff. ------------ [Announcer] This match is one fall and is a Strap Match. First introducing, accompanied to the ring by Pretty Boy Monty, here is the former OWA Champion, weighing in at 185 pounds, here is Stacks Coltrain! And her opponent.. coming down to ringside.. weighing in at 301 pounds, here is Chaos! [Ed] Chaos has been waiting for this one for a while now... The strap is being secured.. and there's the bell. Immediately Chaos nails Stacks in the throat with that strap.. he picks her up and powerbombs her on the mat and begins to drag her around, but Stacks whips around and gets to her feet.. [Biff] Stacks whips Chaos in the back with that strap and clotheslines him with it. Side suplex by Stacks.. and she places him on the turnbuckle.. DDT! She pulls him to corner one.. and tags it.. then she pulls him to corner two.. and tags it.. Chaos is up and drives a knee to the back of Stacks. [Ed] Chaos wraps that strap around her neck and chokes her with it. Chaos tosses Stacks over the top rope.. he follows her out and slam her head on a table.. Chaos is getting up on the table and pulling Stacks up.. he lifts her up and.. brainbuster through the table! Oh my! [Biff] That table is history.. Chaos rams the head of Stacks into the ring apron and rolls her back into the ring. He pulls her to corner number one.. tag.. corner two.. tag.. corner three.. tag.. Stacks is up and wraps that strap around the neck of Chaos! She's choking him down! [Ed] Turnabout is fair play.. chokeslam by the former champion.. and a piledriver.. she pulls him to corner one.. tag.. corner two.. tag.. Chaos is up, but Stacks clotheslines him with that strap. Stacks off the ropes with a flying clothesline. Stacks throws him out of the ring.. [Biff] Powerbomb on the outside by Stacks.. and she puts him on another table.. she.. chokeslam through the table!!! Stacks is fired up here.. she rolls him back into the ring.. and gives him another piledriver!! [Ed] She's going for corner one.. tag.. corner two.. tag.. corner three.. tag.. but CHaos with a trip stops her momentum.. Chaos is up.. and he's wrapping that strap around her neck.. and NO! He throw her over the top rope and is hanging her with that strap.. [Biff] Monty rushes over to try to help... but the official is trying to get Monty away from the ring.. this, no matter how crude, is legal in this match.. Stacks is going out.. and Chaos isn't letting up. Stacks has gone limp.. and Chaos finally let's her drop to the floor.. he goes out and rolls her back into the ring. [Ed] This is the Wheel of Torture, but I've never seen something like that before.. corner one is tagged by Chaos.. as is corner two.. corner three.. tag.. corner four.. he tags it.. and the bell rings.. this one is over.. [Biff] Stacks is in bad shape here.. we may need some help here.. Monty is in the ring.. Chaos moves to the next round to face Amazon Woman. [Ed] Let's go to Elaine and SHelly with the spin of the Wheel. ---------------------- [Shelly] Okay.. here we go.. it'll be Amazon Woman vs. Chaos in the semifinals. THe next match will be John Justice vs. Pretty Boy Monty.. and Elaine, if you'll do the honors this time.. [Elaine] Sure thing.. {She spins the wheel... and it goes round and round like a record baby right round.. oh... never mind..) [Shelly] Falls Count Anywhere! [Elaine] Back to you, Ed and Biff! ------------------ [Announcer] This match is part of the Wheel of Torture tournament. It will be a Falls Count Anywhere match. First, weighing in at 295 pounds.. here is John Justice! And his opponent, being led to the ring by Shogun.. weighing in at 275 pounds.. here is Pretty Boy Monty! [Ed] Monty looks a bit distracted.. probably concerned about the condition of Stacks.. lock up.. whip by Monty.. Justice bowls over Monty with a shoulderblock.. bodyslam by Justice.. Justice with a whip.. and a backdrop.. no.. Monty hits him with a swinging neckbreaker.. cover.. one.. two.. kickout by Justice.. [Biff] Powerbomb by Monty.. and he goes for another cover.. one.. two.. kickout by Justice. Monty with a clothesline, but Justice ducks down and Monty is sent over to the top to the floor. Justice nails Monty with a double ax handle from the apron. [Ed] Justice picks up Monty and slams him through a table.. cover.. one.. two.. three!! I'd have to call this an upset.. Monty was a favorite, but he's obviously got his mind elsewhere.. let's go to the ring announcer. [Announcer] Winner of the match in 7:05, advancing in the tournament, John Justice. [Ed] Let's go to Elaine and Shelly for the next spin. ------------- [Elaine] Okay.. we're ready.. John Justice will take on the winner of this next match between Traci Lane and Anthony Hazard.. and Shelly if you'll be kind enough to spin that wheel.. [Shelly] Here we go.. (the wheel spins.. and spins.. and spins...) [Shelly} NO DQ. [Elaine] There you have it.. back to ringside..
