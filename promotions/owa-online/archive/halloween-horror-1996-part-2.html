Halloween Horror: The Wheel of Torture's Revenge, Part Two

27 October 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Announcer] This match is one fall and it is a No Disqualification match. First weighing in at 194 pounds, accompanied to the ring by Gustaffson.. here is the "Black Widow" Traci Lane! And her opponent.. weighing in at 207 pounds, accompanied to the ring by Sir Goodwin.. here is Anthony Hazard! [Ed] Here's one Traci has been waiting for since she lost the OWA Title to Anthony back in the beginning of the year. Hand shake to start things out. Headlock by Hazard.. Lane with a whip.. Lane with a Gorilla Press and slam.. [Biff] Traci's got that bandage on her head from her earlier Street Fight with Blades.. that was a wild one and it had to have taken alot of out of the Femme. Lane with a Northern Lights Suplex.. one.. kickout by Hazard [Ed] Lane pulls Hazard to his feet and Hazard nails her in the ribs with an elbow.. he follows it up with Russian legsweep.. he nails her with a powerslam.. and a flying kneedrop.. cover.. one.. two.. kickout by Lane [Biff] Hazard goes for a jumping side kick but Lane ducks and nails him with a fist to the midsection... Lane with a suplex.. and then a short clothesline.. cradle by Lane.. one.. two.. kickout by Hazard.. dropkick by Lane.. but Hazard ducks and Lane misses.. [Ed] Hazard with a jumping side kick.. and he follows it up with a belly to belly suplex... and a sleeperhold.. Lane is going out.. she's too close to the ropes.. the ref calls for the break.. Hazard with a double axhandle chop.. [Biff] Hazard's setting her up.. yes.. for the Reverse Powerslam.. wait.. no.. Frankensteiner by Lane.. and cradle.. one.. two.. three!!!! Lane advances! Hazard's to his feet.. is he going to clock her?? [Ed] No.. they're shaking hands.. what a great match.. and this match completes the second round of the Wheel of Torture tournament!. Coming up next.. for the OWA TV Championship.. Miguel Thunder goes one on one with former champion Mariko. Let's get to ringside. ------------------------- [Announcer] This match is one fall and is for the OWA TV Championship. First the challenger.. being led to the ring by the giant Gustaffson.. weighing in at 167 pounds.. here is Mariko! And now the champion.. weighing in at 230 pounds.. here is Miguel Thunder! [Ed] We have lock up.. and Mariko quickly nails Thunder with a knee to the midsection.. snap mare by Mariko and a thrust kick to the head.. quick cover by the challenger.. one.. two.. kickout by Thunder... [Biff] Mariko is very determined today.. Mariko with a whip.. reversal by Thunder.. Thunder with a backdrop.. no.. sunset flip by Mariko.. one.. two.. three!!! She surprised him! [Ed] We have a new champion.. Mariko has regained the OWA TV Championship! [Announcer] The winner in 2:02 and NEW OWA TV Champion, Mariko! -------------- [Announcer] 8 teams.. 7 matches.. one steel cage.. plus an OWA Title defense.. all in one night.. On December 22nd, the OWA brings you a Holiday In Hell.. exclusively on PPV. -------------- [Announcer] This match is one fall. First.. weighing in at 400 pounds.. here is Mack Truck! and his opponent.. weighs in at 275 pounds... here is Sentry! [Ed] This is Mack Truck's first match against an OWA competitor.. let's see how he fares against OWA competition. Lock up.. and Mack Truck easily lifts up Sentry and hits him with a fallaway slam! [Biff] Truck is a big boy.. he drops an elbow on Sentry. He pulls him to his feet and body slams him to the mat. Mack off the ropes.. and splash.. no.. Sentry moved out of the way and Mack hits the mat! [Ed] Sentry off the ropes with a flying clothesline.. flying legdrop by Sentry.. Sentry pulls up Mack and whips him to the corner.. no reversal by Mack.. and an avalanche in the corner by Mack! [Biff] Cover! One.. two.. three!!! Truck wins. [Announcer] The winner of the match in 5:36, Mack Truck! (To Be Continued - Up next..
