OWA Championship Wrestling, 3 November 1996 (Part Two)

3 November 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(In a Dark Gym... no light... in the back, one man is training) (the owner of the gym stops the cameramen) [Boss] This is Xyz, but You have nothing to do here, don't go closer! {The cameramen steps back and carfully look at the man kicking on a punching bag) [Cameramen] Wow!! you see the power of this man, he is about 6'5 and 270 pounds, no hair. (The Man stops kicking on the punching bag and look in the direction of the camera.. his eyes pass from dark brown to light Red) [Cameramen] DID YOU SEE THAT!!!!!!! (Fade to Black) ------------------------- [Myers Watterson] Welcome to the first Holiday In Hell report! First let me announce that the OWA Commission has signed for December 1st, a match between the OWA Champion Executioner and the InterNet Champion Monica Brant! The OWA Title will be on the line for this match. Please note, this is a champion vs. champion match and that if the titles change hands prior to December 1st, that the new champion will be placed into this match. Now on with the update. First we know that OWA Champion Executioner will take on Wheel of Torture tournament winner, Traci Lane in a steel cage match for the OWA Title. The remainder of the card will be a 4 person tag team tournament. There will be 8 teams. The first round will be normal 8 wrestler tag matches. The second round will be elimination tag matches with only the survivors advancing to the finals which will be a War Games match!. Here are some teams: Team Plague: Captain Freddy Fever, The Chiefs and Asian Invasion Team Family: Captain Pretty Boy Monty, Rainbow Warrior, JetStream, & Shogun Team Elite: Captain Anthony Hazard, The Shocker, and the Extremists Plus this team just announced! Team Femme: Captain Rachel Ryan, Consuelo Salyards, Mariko & Gustaffson We'll have more on this big event in upcoming weeks! ------------------------------------ [Announcer] This match is one fall. First, being led to the ring by Mike Mustang.. weighing in at 243 pounds.. here is the "Disco Kid" Freddy Fever!! [Fever grabs the mic] The UNCROWNED InterNet Champion! And tonight, I'm going to take the Grape Ape and squash him like a bug!!! [Announcer] And his opponent.. being led to the ring by Traci Lane.. weighing in at 367 pounds.. here is the giant, Gustaffson!! [Ed] Here we go.. Freddy Fever attacks Gustaffson before the bell. Freddy Fever takes him down with a dropkick. Freddy Fever takes him down with a slap, but Gustaffson doesn't sell it at all. Freddy Fever executes a swinging punch, but Gustaffson doesn't sell it at all. [Ed] I think Fever's in big trouble here... those blows aren't affecting the big guy... Gustaffson executes a kick to the midsection. Gustaffson takes him down with a kneelift. Gustaffson goes for a piledriver, but Freddy Fever counters it with a backdrop. Freddy Fever nails him with a swinging punch. Freddy Fever nails him with a chop. Freddy Fever goes for a choke against the ropes, but Gustaffson blocks it. Gustaffson executes a kick to the midsection. Gustaffson whips Freddy Fever into the turnbuckle, but Freddy Fever reverses it. Freddy Fever takes him down with a spin kick. [Ed] Nice recovery by Fever.. he's gotta get offensive if he's going to beat the giant.. [Biff] Hey.. Fever can be pretty offensive... hey.. I didn't mean it like that! Freddy Fever goes for a vertical suplex, but Gustaffson blocks it. Gustaffson takes him down with the Running Forearm Smash. Referee counts: One, two, shoulder up. [Ed] Near fall by the giant.. Gustaffson executes a headbutt. Gustaffson takes him down with a jumping DDT. Gustaffson goes for a football tackle, but Freddy Fever counters it with a side step. Freddy Fever throws Gustaffson out of the ring. Freddy Fever struts around the ring.. Referee counts: one, two, three, four, five, six, Gustaffson reenters the ring. Freddy Fever executes a Hotshot. Freddy Fever struts around the ring.. [Ed] This is a mistake.. he should be going for a cover here... Gustaffson whips Freddy Fever into the ropes. Gustaffson hits Freddy Fever with a backdrop. Freddy Fever falls out of the ring. Gustaffson goes through the ropes. Freddy Fever whips Gustaffson into the guardrail. Freddy Fever reenters the ring. Gustaffson follows him back in. Freddy Fever takes him down with a snap mare. Freddy Fever executes a spin kick. Freddy Fever takes him down with a Hotshot. Freddy Fever takes him down with a vertical suplex. Freddy Fever takes him down with a facerake. Freddy Fever runs into the ropes. Freddy Fever executes a back heel kick. Freddy Fever takes him down with a fist to the midsection. Freddy Fever takes him down with a bodyslam. Freddy Fever throws Gustaffson out of the ring. [Ed] Fever is on a roll here.. he's dishing out a ton of punishment to Gustaffson. [Biff] Well.. no one ever said the big guy had any brains.. big body.. itsy bitsy brains.. Gustaffson reenters the ring. Freddy Fever nails him with a flying lariat. Freddy Fever struts around the ring.. Freddy Fever goes for a choke against the ropes, but Gustaffson doesn't sell it. Freddy Fever begs off. [Ed] Uh oh.. Gus is pissed now.. [Biff] Get out of the ring, Freddy! Gustaffson catches him in a power bomb. Referee counts: One, two, kickout. Gustaffson nails him with a spinning power bomb. Referee counts: One, two, kickout. [Ed] Freddy just won't stay down.. [Biff] He's kinda like a cockroa.. er.. never mind.. Gustaffson goes for a vertical suplex, but Freddy Fever blocks it. Freddy Fever executes a piledriver. Freddy Fever takes him down with a backbreaker. Freddy Fever takes him down with a waistlock suplex. Referee counts: One, two, kickout. [Biff] Near fall for the Disco King.. Freddy Fever runs into the ropes. Freddy Fever takes him down with a flying lariat. Gustaffson takes him down with a powerslam. Referee counts: One, two, kickout. Gustaffson nails him with a jack-knife power bomb. Gustaffson executes the Drop Piledriver and goes for the pin. Referee counts: One, two, three. [Ed] Gus gets the pinfall! [Biff] What? What a lucky man he is!! There's no way he couldn't have beaten Fever! [Announcer] The winner is Gustaffson. Time of match: 0:09:46 ----------------- [Announcer] This match is one fall. First.. coming to the ring.. weighing in at 245 pounds.. here is Johnny Fate! (Fate takes the Microphone, and Mack Truck comes to ringside) [Johnny] Asian Invasion... I told you I had a suprise!!! You thought that he was on your side didn't he? Well he's not!!! Mack tell everyone what's up!!! [Mack] Ladies and Gentlemen... Johnny Fate is a changed man!!! I went into his office last week to talk to him about beating up Invasion, and he simply smiled and laughed!!! He said that Invasion will get his soon enough... I also talked to Fate about he and I forming a tag team!! Well you tell them the good news..... [Johnny] Mack Truck and I are now going to be known as... The "Power Team"!!!! I've got all the power upstairs and he's got all the power right here!!! {Johnny points to his arm} [Mack] The Power Team is comming after everyone of you loser tag teams!!! I want to win the Tag Team Belts for all the children around the world!!! And you know when I want to win it Johnny? [Johnny] When is that big man? [Mack] I want to win it before Christmas!!!! I want to win those belts for all the kids who are cheering for us right now!!! [Johnny] We will.....We will!!!!!!! [Announcer] And his opponent.. being led to the ring by Freddy Fever.. he weighs in at 230 pounds.. here is the newest member of the Plague, Asian Invasion! [Fever - grabs the mic] Hey.. Fate.. that halo you're wearing around these days, looks a little crooked.. how about Invasion comes in and fix that for ya, punk! Hahahaah! [Ed] Invasion and Fever are cracking up over that comment... [Biff] As well they should.. Fate is a wuss for listening to the fans.. he's a moron and a loser.. and now he's just plain stupid.. Asian Invasion attacks Johnny Fate before the bell. [Ed] There's the bell. Asian Invasion misses with a clothesline. Asian Invasion misses with an elbow. Asian Invasion misses with a shoulderblock. Johnny Fate hits Asian Invasion with a clothesline. [Ed] This isn't Invasion's night.. Johnny Fate takes him down with a German suplex. Referee counts: One, two, kickout. Johnny Fate executes a dropkick to the knee. Johnny Fate executes a flying spinning leg lariat. [Biff] Fate's working on the legs of Invasion.. Fever's pounding on the ring apron, cheering on Invasion... Johnny Fate whips Asian Invasion into the ropes. Asian Invasion hits Johnny Fate with a shoulderblock. Asian Invasion nails him with an inverted atomic drop. Johnny Fate runs into the ropes. Johnny Fate misses with a kick. Asian Invasion executes a sleeperhold. [Biff] This'll slow down Fate. Johnny Fate reaches the ropes after being trapped for 8 seconds. Asian Invasion nails him with a bodyslam. Asian Invasion whips Johnny Fate into the turnbuckle. Asian Invasion runs shoulder-first into the corner. Asian Invasion executes a standing wristlock. Johnny Fate reaches the ropes after being trapped for 23 seconds. [Ed] Asian Invasion going for the submission moves. I know that Fate is suffering a sore arm from Halloween Horror and the submission match with Monty... [Biff] Oh.. you mean the one where Fate QUIT! Hahahahaha... Asian Invasion executes a flying karate chop. Asian Invasion executes a figure-four leglock. Johnny Fate reaches the ropes after being trapped for 21 seconds. [Biff] I'm not sure I understand the game plan.. Invasion is going to work on different body parts.. he should be concentrating on that arm... Johnny Fate executes a sleeperhold. Asian Invasion reaches the ropes after being trapped for 5 seconds. Johnny Fate runs into the ropes. Johnny Fate executes a Japanese armdrag takedown. Johnny Fate nails him with a crucifix. Referee counts: One, kickout. Johnny Fate executes a short clothesline. Johnny Fate nails him with a short clothesline. [Ed] Nice comeback by Fate... Johnny Fate runs into the ropes. Asian Invasion hits Johnny Fate with a clothesline. Johnny Fate falls out of the ring. [Biff] Well.. it WAS.. muahahahah! Referee counts: one, two, Johnny Fate reenters the ring. Asian Invasion runs into the ropes. Asian Invasion hits Johnny Fate with a backdrop. Johnny Fate falls out of the ring. Asian Invasion goes through the ropes. They're brawling on the floor. [Ed] I hope Fever doesn't get involved here.. [Biff] Or Mack!.. Sheesh.. blame everything on Fever.. They're brawling on the floor. Asian Invasion takes him down with a swinging neckbreaker. Asian Invasion knocks Johnny Fate into the ringpost. Asian Invasion whips Johnny Fate into the guardrail. Asian Invasion executes a chop. Asian Invasion reenters the ring. Johnny Fate follows him back in. [Ed] The action is now back in the ring and Fate is hurting.. [Biff] And that's just with his looks.. [Ed] Will you shut up and call the action.. Asian Invasion nails him with a hiptoss. Asian Invasion executes a dropkick. Asian Invasion goes for the Flying Head Scissors, but he can't do it. Johnny Fate counters it with a duck-down move. Johnny Fate takes him down with a spinning backbreaker. Johnny Fate nails him with a stomachbreaker. Johnny Fate takes him down with a grapevine. [Ed] Fate's coming back.. the crowd is cheering him on.. Asian Invasion whips Johnny Fate into the turnbuckle, but Johnny Fate reverses it. Johnny Fate runs shoulder-first into the corner, but Asian Invasion lifts his knee. [Biff] So much for that.. hehe.. Asian Invasion runs into the ropes. Asian Invasion executes a swinging neckbreaker. Asian Invasion whips Johnny Fate into the ropes. Johnny Fate falls out of the ring. [Ed] Mack Truck is beefing with the referee.. and oh no! Freddy Fever nails Johnny Fate with a brainbuster. Freddy Fever rolls Johnny Fate back into the ring. Asian Invasion covers Johnny Fate. [Biff] It's over!!! Hahahaha! Referee counts: one, two, three. [Announcer] The winner is Asian Invasion. Time of match: 0:22:49 ~~~~~------------------- [Announcer] This match is one fall and is for the OWA TV Championship. First introducing the challenger... being led to the ring by Sir Goodwin... he weighs in at 230 pounds.. here is the Shocker! And his opponent.. being led to the ring by Gustaffson.. weighing in at 167 pounds.. here is the OWA TV Champion.. Mariko! The Shocker runs into the ropes. The Shocker executes a double-axhandle chop. The Shocker goes for an atomic drop, but Mariko counters it with a bulldog headlock. [Ed] Nice counter by Mariko.. Shocker started off quickly, but Mariko put a stop to that.. Mariko whips The Shocker into the turnbuckle, but The Shocker reverses it. The Shocker goes for a forearm to the back, but Mariko counters it with a side step. Mariko executes a jumping side kick. [Biff] OH! Nice kick by the Femme.. Mariko whips The Shocker into the ropes, but The Shocker reverses it. The Shocker nails him with a bearhug. [Ed] I'm not sure how well this'll work.. Shocker's not much of a power wrestler... Mariko reaches the ropes after being trapped for 19 seconds. The Shocker whips Mariko into the turnbuckle, but Mariko reverses it. Mariko charges into the corner, but The Shocker moves out of the way. [Biff] Mariko takes it hard in the corner.. The Shocker takes him down with a flying axhandle. The Shocker takes him down with a grapevine. Mariko reaches the ropes after being trapped for 5 seconds. The Shocker nails him with a tiger suplex. Referee counts: One, two, shoulder up. [Ed] We almost has a new champion there.. [Biff] I dunno.. hey.. look up the aisle.. (There are 4 masked lady wrestlers standing in the aisleway.. they are apparantly watching this match with some interest) [Ed] Where did they come from? Who are they? [Biff] Like I'd know... Mariko nails him with a belly-to-back suplex. Mariko nails him with a bow-and-arrow submission. The Shocker reaches the ropes after being trapped for 13 seconds. Mariko whips The Shocker into the ropes. The Shocker bumps into Referee. Mariko goes for the Spinning Cresent Kick. There is no referee to count. [Ed] Tough break for Mariko.. there's no ref to count that fall. The Shocker gets back up. The Shocker nails him with the Running Forearm Smash. There is no referee to count. [Biff] Dummy Shocker didn't wake up the ref.. The Shocker executes a double-axhandle chop. Mariko nails him with a powerslam. There is no referee to count. [Ed] Finally the referee is back up.. Referee is back on the job. Mariko goes for flying headscissors, but The Shocker counters it with a throw-off. The Shocker goes for a clothesline, but Mariko counters it with a duck-down move. Mariko goes for a spin kick, but The Shocker counters it with a duck-down move. The Shocker goes for a tiger driver, but Mariko counters it with a backdrop. [Ed] Several counters there.. but Mariko comes out on top of the exchange. Mariko executes a spin kick. Mariko nails him with a flying clothesline. Mariko takes him down with a reverse cradle. Referee counts: One, two, shoulder up. [Biff] Close count by the referee.. Mariko executes a flying clothesline. Mariko catches The Shocker totally by surprise. Mariko executes the Spinning Cresent Kick and goes for the pin. Referee counts: One, two, three. [Ed] Mariko retains the title!! Next week, she'll defend against Chaos! [Biff] You mean, the Crusher Chaos.. Stacks is still in traction after Halloween Horror... perhaps we'll see Mariko carried out as well... [Announcer] The winner is Mariko.
