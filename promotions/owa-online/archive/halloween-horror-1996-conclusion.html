Halloween Horror: The Wheel of Torture's Revenge, Conclusion

27 October 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Announcer] This match is one fall with no time limit. It is for the OWA Championship. First, the challenger... accompanied to the ring by the giant, Gustaffson. She weighs in at 205 pounds. Here is "The Enforcer" Rachel Ryan! And her opponent.. weighing in at 230 pounds.. he's your OWA Champion.. here is the Executioner! [Ed] Here we go.. the OWA Title is on the line.. funny thing.. Plague leader Freddy Fever is NOT at ringside for this important event. [Biff] Well.. we heard from the Plague prior to this match and Executioner wants this match as much as Rachel does. [Ed] I believe this is the first time these two have squared off.. there's the bell.. and we have a lockup. Ryan with a whip.. Executioner ducks an elbow, but Ryan catches him with a Gorilla Press and slam! Ryan quickly executes a backbreaker and covers.. one... kickout by Executioner.. [Biff] Executioner back up a bit, but hits Ryan on her way in with a kick to the midsection and stuns her with a DDT. Executioner bounces off the ropes and nails Ryan in the head with a boot.. Executioner goes to the top and misses a moonsault as Ryan moves out of the way. [Ed] Ryan is up and hits Executioner with a choke slam.. Ryan whips Executioner into the ropes and goes for a powerslam, but Executioner nails her with a lariat... Executioner with a gutwrench suplex.. then an atomic drop.. [Biff] Clothesline by Executioner and a cover.. one.. two.. kickout by Rachel Ryan.. he's going for the Cross Face Chicken Wing, but Ryan hits him with a jawbreaker.. Ryan with a slap.. she whips Executioner into the ropes.. and nails him with a kick. Russian legsweep by Ryan.. [Ed] Cover.. one.. two.. kickout by Executioner... Ryan misses an elbow drop.. and Executioner nails her with a clothesline... and he applies the Cross Face Chicken Wing... the ref is asking Ryan if she wants to sibmit... [Biff] Give it up, babe! She's trying to reach for the ropes, but she's too far away... he's had that applies for almost a minute now.. Ryan is starting to fade here.. she won't submit.. but she's probably going to pass out.. [Ed] The referee is there.. he's checking the arm.. once.. twice... thre.. no.. she's got it up.. Ryan maneuvers around and finally puts a leg into the ropes for the break.. Executioner is up and body slams her to the mat.. He drops a knee on that arm.. Ryan is in a lot of pain from that arm. [Biff] Ryan is to her feet.. Executioner off the ropes.. Ryan catches him with a powerslam! She pulls him to his feet and goes for the Gorilla Press Powerslam.. but her arm gives out and she falls back.. Executioner is on top.. one.. two.. he hooks the leg.. three!!! [Announcer] Winner of the match in 17:59 and STILL OWA Champion.. Executioner!! [Ed] An incredible match. I'm sure that Ryan will get a rematch in the future.. we'll have to see how that goes. But right now we are ready to start the final match in the Wheel of Torture Tournament! Let's get down to ringside! [Announcer] Ladies and gentlemen.. this is the final match of the evening.. it's the finals for the Wheel of Torture tournament. It is a Death Match. The winner if the person who can gain 3 consecutive falls. There is no time limit. The winner of this match will be the winner of the 1996 Wheel of Torture tournament and will get a shot at the OWA Champion at Holiday In Hell. Let me now introduce your competitors. First, with wins over Consuelo Salyards and Chaos, weighing in at 165 pounds.. here is last years tournament winner, Amazon Woman!!! And her opponent.. being led to the ring by the giant, Gustaffson. She had wins over Johnny Blades, Anthony Hazard and John Justice.. she weighs in at 194 pounds.. here is the "Black Widow" Traci Lane! [Ed] This will be some match.. the winner will be the one to get 3 consecutive pinfalls.. [Biff] So the first to win 3 falls is the winner, right? [Ed] Not quite.. must be 3 falls in a row. [Biff] What?!? We'll be here all night!!!!! [Ed] It could be that way.. the referee is making sure they know the instructions to this match.. there will be a 30 second rest period between each fall.. and there's the bell. Lock up.. Traci with a whip.. but Amazon comes off with a shoulderblock that knocks the Widow to the mat. But she quickly gets back to her feet. [Biff] Amazon Woman is right there to catch her with a belly to belly suplex... cover.. one.. two.. kickout.. Lane is up, but Amazon catches her with a bearhug, but Lane is out quickly with an earringer.. [Ed] Dropkick by Lane.. and a clothesline.. followed by a kneedrop.. cover by Lane.. one.. two.. kickout by Amazon Woman. Lane with a bulldog and another cover.. one.. two.. kickout by Amazon Woman.. we're going to see alot of pinfalls in this match, I think. [Biff] Lane goes for a gutwrench suplex, but Amazon counters with a backdrop.. then a fistdrop.. whip by Amazon Woman.. and powerslam! Amazon pulls Lane to her feet.. and Running Powerslam and cover.. one.. two.. three!!! [Announcer] Winner of the fall: Amazon Woman. Amazon Woman with one fall. [Ed] Great first fall. Will that predict tide of the match? There's the bell for the second round. Amazon quickly moves in, but Lane nails her with a side suplex.. Lane with a legdrop.. and a spinebuster slam! [Biff] Cover.. one. two.. kickout by Amazon Woman.. Lane is up and nails Amazon with a Northern Lights suplex.. one.. two.. three!! [Announcer] Winner of the fall: Traci Lane. Traci Lane is up one fall. [Ed] There's no tying in this match.. Traci Lane is up with one fall.. she needs two more to win this match.. Amazon needs three in a row.. there's the bell for the third fall. [Biff] Lock up.. Amazon Woman with a roundhouse right.. and a kick.. clothesline by Amazon Woman.. whip by Amazon Woman.. and powerslam.. cover.. one.. two.. kickout by Traci Lane.. you can see the blood from her previous matches begining to come down into her face. [Ed] Lane quickly nails Amazon with a fist to the midsection... Russian legsweep into a pin.. one.. two.. kickout by Amazon Woman.. Lane with a whip.. reversal by Amazon.. and a boot to the midsection of Lane by Amazon Woman.. and she approaches Lane and.. neck snap! [Biff] Lane falls to the mat.. cover by Amazon Woman.. one.. two.. thre.. no.. Lane with a foot on the ropes.. Amazon with a whip.. and dropkick.. no.. Lane holds on to the ropes and Amazon crashes to the mat. [Ed] Lane stumbles over and executes a swinging neckbreaker.. she follows it up with a side suplex.. cover.. one.. two.. kickout by Amazon Woman.. Traci Lane with the Gorilla Press Piledriver and cover.. one.. two.. three!! [Announcer] Winner of the fall, Traci Lane. Lane is up with two falls, one more for the win. [Ed] This match is already taking it's toll on both competitors.. we're at about the 39 minute of this match. There's the bell starting round four.. if Lane can get the pinfall here.. it's all over... [Ed] Lockup.. Amazon Woman with a bodyslam.. and an elbowdrop.. cover.. one.. two.. kickout by Lane.. Amazon pulls up Lane.. no.. cradle by Lane.. one.. two. kickout by Amazon Woman.. [Biff] Amazon off the ropes.. no. Lane nails her with a fist to the midsection... and a bulldog... Lane sets up Amazon for a Boston crab... Amazon tries to reach the ropes.. and finally makes it.. the ref calls for the break.. [Ed] Lane is all over Amazon Woman, trying to get the third fall.. Lane with a powerslam.. one.. two.. kickout by Amazon Woman.. whip by Lane.. she goes for a backdrop.. but Amazon catches her.. piledriver.. no.. she can't quite get it.. Lane with a backdrop.. no.. she can't bring Amazon Woman over.. Amazon.. with a powerbomb! One.. two.. three!!!! [Announcer] Winner of fall four, Amazon Woman. Amazon Woman is up one fall. [Ed] What a match.. there's the bell for fall five.. Amazon with a clothesline on Lane.. she picks up Lane and executes a Fallaway slam! [Biff] Amazon Woman goes to the top.. and flies off with a cross body block.. but Lane rolls it over.. and hooks the leg.. one.. two.. three!!! Lane pulls out a win... [Announcer] Winner of fall five, Traci Lane. Lane is up one fall. [Ed] It's approaching the one hour mark for this match.. there's the bell.. both competitors are weary and Traci Lane is bloddy from her previous encounters.. Lane with an uppercut.. Gorilla press by Lane.. cover.. one.. two.. kickout by Amazon Woman.. [Biff] Lane whips Amazon.. Amazon reverses it.. Lane reverses it.. Amazon comes off with a flying clothesline.. but Lane ducks and Amazon is sent out to the floor.. she cracks her head on the guardrailing.. [Ed] She appears to be busted open at this point.. Lane rolls out of the ring and rolls Amazon Woman back into the ring.. Lane with a swinging neckbreaker... cover.. one.. two.. three!! [Announcer] Winner of fall six, Traci Lane. Lane is up by two falls. [Ed] Amazon Woman took a hard bump on the floor.. and it cost her... Lane is up by two falls.. there's the bell.. Amazon is trying to wipe the blood from her eyes.. at least Traci has some bandages on her head.. [Biff] Lane with a kick to the midsection.. and powerbomb.. she pulls up Amazon Woman.. and delivers another powerbomb.. Amazon Woman appears to be out of it at this point.. I don't think she can possibly defend herself.. [Ed] Lane with a cover.. one.. two... three.. no! Amazon Woman lifts the shoulders just before that three count.. Lane pulls her up and delivers a chokeslam.. cover.. one.. two.. thre..kickout by Amazon Woman... [Biff] She's got to be running on instict at this point.. Lane whips Amazon to the corner.. reversal by Amazon who sends Lane right into the referee!!!.. Amazon gets a running start and Stinger Splash on Lane and the referee.. [Ed] I have to believe that Amazon can barely see the referee.. Amazon Woman with her Running Powerslam and cover... [Biff] There's no ref.. no ref to count.. She's up and looking around.. she find the ref and drags him over to make the count.. one.. two.. thre.. shoulder up! Lane has a shoulder up! [Ed] Amazon Woman pulls up Lane.. no.. cradle by Lane.. one.. two... three!!!! Lane wins it! Lane wins it! [Announcer] The winner of fall seven.. and the winner of the Wheel of Torture tournament.. Traci Lane!!! Total match time: 1 hour 25 minutes and 36 seconds. [Ed] We have run completely out of time.. I'm sure we'll here more from this event in flashes throughout the week.
