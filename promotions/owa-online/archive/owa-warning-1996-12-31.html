Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The OWA Commissioner sits on a stool in the OWA Studios) This past week, we lost one of our long term players in a Retirement match. In a few moments, you will see this match in it's entirety. Before we take you to that match, I want to let you know that the Online Wrestling Alliance would NOT be consider, what the fans like to call "Hardcore". We are more of an angle based wrestling federation and our players enjoy that status. However, once in a while, you need to throw the rules aside and then the wrestlers take matters into their own hands. Such is the case with the match you are about to see. This is a retirement match, the stipulations of this match will be explained later. What you are about to see, is NOT typical OWA wrestling. What you are about to see can be considered extremely graphic. Parental discretion is advised.
