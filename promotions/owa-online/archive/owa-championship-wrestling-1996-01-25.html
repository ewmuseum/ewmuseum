OWA Championship Wrestling, 25 January 1996

Welcome to OWA Championship Wrestlign. OCW features the top names in the OWA. OCW is brought to you by the OWA Magazine web page. OWA Magazine features the top stories of the week as well as current ratings for the OWA. You will also find wrestler profiles and a special page set aside for the OWA Champion. OWA magazine web page is located at: http://www.cybergate.com/~stevie/owa/owa.html *** This match is one fall: Amazon Woman vs. Raekwon Amazon Woman awaits the arrival of Raekwon in the ring. From the opposite side, Raekwon sneaks out from under the ring and tries to cheap shot Amazon. Amazon quickly spins around and catches him with several shots to the face. The former champion whips him into the corner and rushes in with a clothesline, as Raekwon falls to the mat. Amazon with a series of elbow drops on the fallen Raekwon. Amazon whips Raekwon in to the ropes and misses a clothesline as Raekwon ducks. Raekwon with a kick to the midsection buckles the former champion. Raekwon with a high knee lift, and a series of stomps on Amazon Woman. Raekwon climbs to the top turnbuckle and leaps off. Amazon quickly moves out of the way as Raekwon lands on this stomach. Amazon Woman is quickly to her feet and scoops up Raekwon on her shoulders. She quickly climbs to the second turnbuckle and leaps off powerslamming Raekwon in to the mat... 1... 2... 3!!!!! A decisive victory in her last match! Winner of the match in 5:23: Amazon Woman!!! *** Amazon Woman is making her way to the podium here.... [Amazon Woman] I'm barely breathing heavy after taking care of that lightweight. I am not leaving until I get a return match here today for the OWA Championship against Pretty Boy Monty. Monty, you know I was robbed. I may not have been able to beat you, but I know I would have given you a run for your money. Now, I want you to come out here today and accept my challenge. If I win, I will stay in this federation and continue defending the belt. If I do not win the match, it will be my final match and I will go into retirement peacefully. There's the challenge Monty. Accept it if you dare. What a shocker! Amazon Woman here today wants a shot at the OWA Championship. We are awating a reply from Pretty Boy Monty, who I know is here in the building. Let's go now to this prerecorded interview with the Family *** [a camera slowly approaches a mansion on the hill from a distance...It slowly pans the grounds....and enter into the mansion...up the stairs...around....and around...through the hallways....it approches a door and stops....slowly the door opens..sitting there are the remaining members of THE FAMILY...The Rainbow Warrior....Executioner....Intimidator and Pretty Boy Monty.....Monty acknowledges the camera to come in.....] [Pretty Boy Monty] Well, well it looks as if we have company....come in...come in..so you have found us....look at these proud warriors I have gathered around me...it just amazes me that on little incident as the end of the INN federation has weakened us so...but yet we survive.....(sipping coffee)...as you can see we have been studying tapes of our enemies which we have many....(Pointing to pictures and tapes of the Giant Gustaffson...the Femme Fatales....Raekwon.....Colt) we havnt yet decided what to do but when we..... [Rainbow Warrior] FORGET THESE DAMN PLEASENTRIES MONTY.....CALL WHAT THEY... They used to call me Tiger, but now I am the Intimidator. [Pretty Boy Monty] As you can see....we can only get stronger....(smiling) dont wait for us we will come to you and once I reach the Pinnacle we will only.....continue to grow as a FAMILY...(pushing the camera away)....now leave us.... *** Now bear in mind, that was recorded prior to Amazon Woman's challenge today. We are still hoping to hear from Pretty Boy Month, the OWA Champion. Let's go back to ringside. *** This match is one fall. Sabin Figaro vs. Traci Lane. They lock up, Lane hits Figaro with a knee to the midsection and a short clothesline and goes for a quick pin. Figaro kicks out after only a one count. Figaro quickly whips Lane into the ropes and catches her with an elbow to the head. Sabin pushes Lane into the corner, and nails her quickly with the Fist Flurry. Lane is hurt as she falls to the mat. Sabin with a cover... 1... 2... Lane kicks out at 2 and a half. Sabin quickly climbs to the top turnbuckle and Lane is up to toss him off, clotheslining him on the top ropes. Lane with a quick cradle... 1... 2... 3!!!!!! Winner of the match in 3 minutes flat: Traci Lane! *** Next week Traci will go one on one for the OWA Championship, facing Pretty Boy Monty. That's if Monty accepts the challenge of Amazon Woman tonight and retains the title. Either way, Traci Lane will get the title shot. We are still waiting word from OWA Champion Pretty Boy Monty on the rematch for the OWA Title tonight on this show. Let's get go now to the Endurance's Evil Challenge Update. *** Here is your final Endurance's Evil Challenge Update. Before we get to the tournament itself, let's go over some of the single matches on the card: Rainbow Warrior battles Gustaffson OWA TV Champion Anthony Hazard will defend against former champion Colt Plus, the OWA Champion, Pretty Boy Monty will defend against Traci Lane. . Now, the remaining OWA Wrestlers will be involved in the four round tournament known as Endurance's Evil Challenge. Here is how this will work. There will be four rounds... Round 1: Tag Team: Your partner will be chosen at random. If you turn on your partner, you are automatically DQ from the event entirely. All teams who are victorious will move on to round two. Round 2: Singles. You will face your tag team partner in this round. Winners move on to the next round. Round 3: 4 Wrestler Iron Man Challenge Match: The four remaining wrestlers will battle each other in a Texas Tornado Style match. All four in the ring at once and the 2 with the most pinfalls in the 30 min time limit will advance to the final round. Final Round: Texas Death Match. The two battle royal finalists will battle in a texas death match. Winner is the person who can knockout the other for a 10 count. NOTE: The winner of this match is the winner of Endurance's Evil Challenge and will also get a crack at the OWA Championship. Next week OWA Championship Wrestling will be preempted so we can bring you Endurance's Evil Challenge. Let's go back out to ringside. *** This match is one fall: Denise Duncan vs. Executioner. Executioner runs at Duncan, who elevates him high with a back body drop. Duncan drops an elbow on Executioner. Executioner takes a breather out of the ring. As he tries to enter, Duncan meets him with a couple stomps to the head. Duncan pulls up Executioner and whips him into the ropes, and misses with a clothesline. Executioner bounces off the ropes and nails Duncan with a clothesline of his own. Executioner misses a leg drop. Duncan pulls up Executioner and whips him towards the corner but Executioner reverses it sending Duncan hard into the corner. Duncan falls to the mat, and as she gets up, Executioner charges towards her. Duncan ducks and Executioner sails over the top rope to the floor running into the railing. Executioner gets up as we see blood running down off his forehead. Executioner climbs into the ring as Duncan stomps him in the head. Duncan climbs the turnbuckles, as Executioner gets to his feet. She jumps off and nails Executioner in the forehead with a double ax handle as the blood run more freely from his head now. Duncan for the cover... 1... 2... 3!!! However, Amazon Woman, you stated if you lose this match you will retire, then I am afraid this will be your final match. I plan on proving that I am deserving of this title and I will defeat you soundly today. Let me go change and I'll be ready when it's time.
