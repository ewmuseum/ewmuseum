I'm not some new snot nosed punk who'll come in and demand a title shot just because I'm here. I won't go out and attack a champ in hopes of creating cheap heat for a title shot. I'll wipe everyone of my opponents across the mat and get the attention of the entire federation and fed heads to where they all say, "Hey! Mr. America is the best. He's so good that he deserves a title shot!!" After that, I'll take the chump to school and take his gold and pride. That's all there is to it. Everyone who steps into the ring with me will realize that I am the best after I hit him/her with the Made in America and that person lays helpless on the mat on his/her back starring at the arena lights as the ref counts to three and Mr. America gets another win to add to his column.
