Match time: 5:05 ------------- [Myers] This is Myers Watterson with your first Halloween Horror update. Also dubbed the Wheel of Torture's Revenge, Halloween Horror mixes the luck and brutality and turns it into an art form. Here's the concept. Prior to each match in the tournament, the Wheel of Torture will be spun, and the wrestlers will wrestle that match. Winner of the match advances to the tournament. Here are the 14 matches that will be on the wheel: Cage Match Coal Miner's Glove Match Strap Match Parking Lot Match Chain Match Amateur Rules Match First Blood Match Submission Match Falls Count Anywhere Match Normal Rules NO DQ Match Street Fight Ladder Match Over the Top Rope Match Once a match is selected, it is removed from the Wheel. All matches will be used, unless there is a draw during the tournament. Amazon Woman, who won last year's tournament, has received a bye into the second round. The other qualifyers are, Miguel Thunder, Chaos, JetStream, Gustaffson and Asian Invasion. The main even for this event will be a no time limit match for the OWA Title as current Champ, Stacks Coltrain takes on former champion, Rachel Ryan. Also, tag team Champions, the Chiefs will take on the winner of tonight's tag team match between Brawlers Inc and The Extremists.
