A May-1997 real-wrestler booking e-fed whose surviving house shows stage Vince McMahon, Roddy Piper, Eric Bischoff and a roster of other public figures inside its own in-fiction "Tuesday Fillibuster" broadcast. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

This MWA's earliest surviving broadcast, the Tuesday Fillibuster of 21 May 1997, opens on a pre-taped promo from J.J. Dillon, announcing that he had taken control of the stable Devastation Inc. and was bringing in Tully Blanchard to represent it at ringside while Dillon himself worked on title matches from the office. The post carrying it declines to name a president at all, pointing readers to the fed's own homepage instead.

Its house shows cast Vince McMahon and Eric Bischoff as a bickering broadcast team, McMahon repeatedly needling “Bischoff” on-air while Bischoff snapped back “bite me” in return. A 24 May 1997 show introduces Jerry Dammers as the promotion's new full-time announcer, on a card that put the Quebecers against the Bash Brothers pairing of Ahmed Johnson and Mark Henry.

A follow-up show on 13 June 1997 turns the format itself into the joke: Todd Pettengill shows up uninvited to host, and Dammers has him handcuffed and dragged out by security before the regular broadcast team picks the show back up.

No document from this run is dated after that show, and no roster, title list, or closing notice of any kind survives.
