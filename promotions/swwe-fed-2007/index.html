Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

SWWE Fed grew out of swwe.com.sapo.pt, a Portuguese fan site otherwise devoted to real WWE, TNA and ECW news, results and biographies, running its own weekly Xplosion! broadcasts from at least 4 March 2007 under commentators billed as JR and JBL. Its wrestlers competed under their own forum handles rather than character names, most playing themselves “in the person of” a real WWE star — one member, Lashley, is stated by the promotion's own commentary to be feuding in the person of Triple H.

By 29 April 2007 the promotion had added a third, developmental brand, the AWA, feeding wrestlers up into its two main shows, Xplosion! and a second weekly broadcast, Underaction, and staged its own first champion-versus-champion main event between the two shows' titleholders that same night.

Its own on-screen leadership was headed by a Chairman billed only as Doctor, who by October 2007 had been killed off in storyline and succeeded, in a twist the promotion's own recap called a surprise, by his in-fiction son, Eira. That month's Unsurrender pay-per-view changed three championships at once: Lashley beat Eira and Rated Xtreme to become the new Xplosion! Champion, Mr. Xtreme won the SWWE World Heavyweight Championship from Salvador and Felix619 in a Hell in a Cell that left Salvador seriously injured, and Boy Killer beat Rayden for the Underaction Championship.

No further fed-related document survives past that recap; the wider site itself later reduces to a bare redirect, but neither later capture mentions the fed by name, and how it ended isn't in the surviving record.
