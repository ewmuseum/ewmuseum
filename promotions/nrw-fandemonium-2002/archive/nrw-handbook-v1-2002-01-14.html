Who needs a simulator to decide your matches for you when you have within yourself all that you need in order to win! 2.0 - Getting Started To get started in NRW, you need to join. To roleplay, simply post messages on the NRW Message Board before and after your matches. Roleplaying is BIG in NRW, so be sure to participate. The more you participate, the better your chances are to win and the bigger your push. If you have a match, it is expected of you to roleplay for that match. If you do not roleplay, you're opening yourself up and allowing yourself to easily job, and that's not what you want to do here in NRW, otherwise you wouldn't have joined in the first place! 4.1 - Deadlines The deadlines are always Midnight Pacific Time (3am Eastern), 2 days before the card. The deadlines will ALWAYS be posted on the website so there is no reason for lateness. All roleplays late will not be read or accepted, period. The message board displays the time of the post, and if it's late, it'll not be read. 4.2 - Limits Since time is a major issue to some people, there will be a three roleplay limit for each wrestler each card. Anything more than three (unless otherwise noted) will not be counted. The only exemptions to the three limit is if you're participating in more than 1 match in 1 night. You will be notified at the beginning of the week about any limit changes. 4.3 - Profanity A lot of e-mail organizations accept it, some don't. This one does...To a certain extent . What I run here is an organization, and I always like to keep a business-like image when I run an organization. This may not be real life, but some people often tend to be offended by such language. I will allow a such word as "ass", a term which is usually very freely in the Wrestling World these days. I understand that at times a wrestler might become frustrated. But please, try and censor the profanity with "@$#%" or something like ::Bleep:: or whatever. But anything other than that, might be punishable by suspension or expulsion. 4.4 - Spell check With roleplaying and interviews, spell checking is a must. A few random errors is allright, we all make them. However, three mistakes for every sentence or two is bad. Correct spelling and punctuation will be considered in the effort category, and the more effort you put in, the better chance at a huge push. Just do yourself a favor and read your stuff over. 5.0 - Strategies Strategies helps the match writer write your match. Basically you write movies, spots, or anything else you would like to see put on the card in your match. These are commonly called "strats" and you must submit one for every match you're in. It could vary from week to week, or you can keep it the same every week to save time. It's also nice to keep it short and simple. There is a template on the webpage that you can freely follow. 5.1 - Instructions These are seperate from strats, but have the same idea. Instruction deadlines are different and need to be sent 2 days before the card is sceduled. Instructions include the following: Sneak attacks, any interviews in-ring or in the back, or just random angles going on in the back. As for interviews, do me a favor and type up the interview yourself so all I need to do is copy and paste. 5.2 - Deadline Strat deadlines are 3 days before the card. This can give the match writer some extra time to start the match. 6.0 - Rankings Unlike other federations, rankings in NRW do not constitute your status. Just because you’re 10-0 doesn’t mean you’ll be pushed. You get the same treatment is you’re 11-0 as you would if you were 1-7. Rankings are there to see where you stand in wins and losses. These will accrue permanently. 7.0 - Cards NRW holds one television card per week called Fandemonium. Our Supercard, will be held bi-monthly on the last week. Supercards are called Niagara Brawls with appropriate subtitles. The shows will be written up in a third person summary format by members of the NRW Booking Committee, and available to read in their respective areas. We will also have a "video store" in which you can read out old cards. There will also be a Results Archive, but this archive will only give the rundown of the card, not the entire play-by-play results. Also, check the NRW Control Center for info on any upcoming PPV events. 7.1 - Getting Matches NRW reserves the right to sign any matches that we feel should take place and will do so for the most part. However, we also want the wrestlers themselves to get to pick who they will wrestle. Therefore, if you want to request a match, simply e-mail me your request and we'll see what we can do. Any type of match is sanctionable in NRW, at the discretion of NRW (No DQ, Barb Wire, Steel Cage, Leather Strap, Texas Death, etc., even special stipulations which you name. Creativity counts). Be aware, though, that if you are a registered wrestler in NRW, NRW reserves the right to place you in any match we feel fit. Therefore, if you will be out of town, or away from the computer for a period of time exceeding a week, please let us know. 7.2 - Match Limits At no point is any wrestler allowed more than one singles and one tag match on any given televised show. The only exceptions to this are special events, tournaments, and Supercards. Belts are permitted to be defended on any card, but all four championships will be defended at the NRW monthly Supercard under "normal" circumstances. 8.0 - Suspension/Expulsion NRW reserves the right to suspend or expulse anyone at any given time for violating any of the rules, stated above. Also, if you have a problem with how we use your character, come to us in a friendly manner about it. Don't blatantly bitch about it, or you will job, plain and simple. 9.0 - Conclusion Aside from what's outlined here, you basically can do whatever you want. Just remember, it's only a game, and regardless of who wins or loses, have fun in NRW, because that's what this is all about :-) If you have any questions, please contact me at [detail withheld]
