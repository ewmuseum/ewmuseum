Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Die Hard Wrestling Alliance was about three weeks old by 18 June 1999, when its own recruitment post asked for more roleplayers ahead of an approaching pay-per-view.

That pay-per-view, Carnival Massacre, aired 30 August 1999, billed as “International Breakdown, Part 1” and staged live from Tokyo. It opened with a video recap of Blade's own year of World Title chases — a neck injury on his first shot, a title won and lost within four days, a second reign held “with pride” — before the returning Angel Reaper unmasked as a woman and renamed herself Angelic Reaper.

Blade escalated his own feud with a rival mid-broadcast, announcing on camera that their scheduled cage match would be ringed in barbed wire and then, moments later, electrified, and having a construction crew build both live in front of the crowd.

Three title programs ran through the card: Glorified J won the Television Championship from Kevin Shields in a hardcore “Steel Cage Drug Match” fought around a bong suspended above the ring; Johnny eXtreme retained the North American Championship against Dark Shadow after a referee's count nearly cost him the belt; and the Intercontinental Championship picture was thrown into chaos when challenger “Smoked” Shawn Stevens, declaring himself “the future” of the promotion, ambushed three rival wrestlers backstage before his own title match against the champion, the Gamer, tore through a restaurant and a parking lot.

No further document survives after this first part of the broadcast.
