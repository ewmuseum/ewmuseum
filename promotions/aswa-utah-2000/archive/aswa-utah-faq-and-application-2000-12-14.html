The All Star Wrestling Alliance The All Star Wrestling Alliance, often referred to as ASWA, is a regional e-wrestling federation. The All Star Wrestling Alliance is owned by All Star Wrestling and operated by Gene Oakland. The aim of the All Star Wrestling Alliance is to provide entertaining and innovative wrestling to the general public across Utah.
Gene Oakland is assisted by Brian Pierce and Jerry Butcher in operating the All Star Wrestling Alliance. The federation works within a very small budget and has a television deal with Fox 13, KSUT, who broadcast
Friday Night Fury, the federations flagship production, live. 2. All Star Wrestling All Star Wrestling is the parent company of the All Star Wrestling Alliance. The chief executive of ASW is Gene Oakland and his goal is to provide wrestling entertainment across the United States.
All Star Wrestling intends on owning a number of federations across North America and All Star Wrestling Alliance is their first venture. 3. All Star Wrestling Alliance History ASWA was formed in early November 2000 when Gene Oakland and his company, All Star Wrestling, spotted a rare niche in the wrestling market. Utah, strangely for a state of it's size, did not have a recognized e-wrestling federation and thanks to generous funding from
All Star Wrestling, Fox 13 and the local community Mr Oakland was able to establish the federation.
A deal was signed with the owners of Salt Lake City Memorial Hall to stage all ASWA events there and Fox 13 agreed to broadcast ASWA events.
Gene Oakland set about installing a recruitment program for both staff and wrestlers. His first recruit was Jerry Butcher who joined the organization as commissioner. Oakland later added a number of other staff members including Brian Pierce.
Gene Oakland quickly set about developing a talented roster for the All Star Wrestling Alliance and a number of vastly experienced e-wrestlers were brought in to the federation. In addition Oakland announced that he was willing to welcome newcomers to the sport and develop their skills.
The roster quickly developed in size and the All Star Wrestling Alliance announced that it intended on staging it's first ever event on the 17th November at Salt Lake City Memorial Hall. Hopes were high that under Oakland's stewardship the All Star Wrestling Alliance would become a success in both Utah and beyond.
This plan was furthered when ASWA joined the E-Fed Universe and also signed up to become a member of the RWA Interfed in December 2000.
4. Salt Lake City and the Memorial Hall Salt Lake City, the state capital of Utah, is generally regarded as one of the cleanest cities in America. It is also a Mecca for Mormons worldwide. The city has a joyful and almost noble air about it as it is cradled by the Wasatch Mountains. Around 18 miles west of the city lies the Great Salt Lake from which the city takes it's name.
Within the center of Salt Lake City lies a number of Mormon temples and tabernacles. In addition a Memorial Hall can be found in south central
Salt Lake. Memorial Hall was built as a tribute to the many American soldiers who lost their lives in wars through history. The main feature of the hall is the grand room, around 400 meters long and 250 meters wide. It is this grand room where all ASWA events are staged on a specially erected ring.
The grand room has basic lighting facilities and an average quality sound system. A small entrance ramp, around 20 meters long is erected to take wrestlers from the locker room (a small room to the side of the grand room) to the ring.
5. Championships and Titles Every wrestler wants to be able to preside over there counterparts by wearing a title belt. The All Star Wrestling Alliance has a number of titles for prospective applicants and current wrestlers to challenge for.
ASWA Heavyweight Championship The pinnacle of any ASWA wrestlers career should be to challenge for the Heavyweight championship. This title holds the most regard in the federation and heavyweight title matches are often bruising and exciting encounters. Matches for this title are almost always the main event of any card. The holder of this title represents the federation and will be expected to act in a responsible manner.
ASWA Utah State Championship To be crowned as the champion of Utah is for many people a great honor. The wrestling fans within the state look upon all Utah state title holders as their champion. By wearing this belt wrestlers open a whole new world of opportunity for themselves including supermarket openings and book signing.
ASWA Cruiserweight Championship Only wrestlers under the weight limit of 230 pounds may challenge for this title. That doesn't mean that this championship is devalued, in fact quite the opposite. Cruiserweight title matches are usually high flying affairs that excite, innovate and entertain.
ASWA Tag Team Championship Despite the severe lack of tag teams in e-wrestling as a sport Gene Oakland has decided to press ahead with introducing the tag team division. Tag team matches are often the most exciting of all as teamwork, friendship and agility are all called upon.

7. Staff Members The All Star Wrestling Alliance has a dedicated team of staff who all work together to ensure that the ASWA is a success.
President and Owner - Gene Oakland Gene Oakland is an experienced former wrestler whose career was cruelly cut short at the age of 32. After learning his trade in the legendary federation of the deep south Oakland was regarded as one of the hottest properties in wrestling. However the move to the big time never materialized and Oakland then watched in horror as a mis-timed powerbomb ended his career.
Gene retained his love for the sport however and quickly moved into the administrational scene of wrestling. After many years working for federations in the southern states Oakland decided to establish All
Star Wrestling and in turn the All Star Wrestling Alliance. Oakland is a fair man who believes in honesty and loyalty, he frowns upon wrestlers who criticize the fans and other wrestlers.
Commissioner - Jerry Butcher Jerry Butcher, a former wrestler within Texas retired in the fall of 1998 at the age of 40. Butcher, loathed by the crowd throughout his spell as a wrestler, decided that a career as a promoter beckoned. News of the All Star Wrestling developments filtered through to him on the grapevine and after speaking with Gene Oakland he agreed to take on the role of commissioner.
Butcher is a natural showman who believes that the crowd should be entertained - whatever the price! Booking Official - Brian Pierce Brian Pierce, an experienced e-wrestler with an interest in behind the scenes work, answered an advertisement for staff members from Gene
Oakland. A very professional and dedicated individual Brian has a big say in booking matches and developing angles.
Play by Play Commentators - Mick McKinsley and Jimmy "Crossroads" Callaghan The vastly contrasting commentary team of Callaghan and McKinsley provide many moments of unintentional humor throughout a match and the odd bit of thoughtful insight. Both men took up careers as wrestlers but knew from a very early stage that they were not cut out for being in the ring. McKinsley began to follow a path to becoming a wrestling analyst whilst Callaghan enjoyed a very successful career as a wrestling manager.
McKinsley provides a run down on the events whilst "Crossroads", so called due to his unnerving knack of switching sides provides some unthoughtful and unhelpful views.
Reporters - Zara Bryant, Ethan Hunter and Malice Rayne The trio of bright young journalistic hopefuls provide some entertaining and informative views along with conducting interviews with the superstars. Bryant, a stunning ebony beauty, is looked upon with affection by the wrestlers for her looks despite the fact she knows nothing about wrestling. Malice Rayne is the gothic goddess of wrestling. Her wayward spirit and depressed attitude are certainly interesting whilst Hunter, a local journalist, is able to provide basic wrestling knowledge to complement his sound interviewing techniques.
8. Events The All Star Wrestling Alliance stages one card a week and also stages a bi-monthly main event. All events will be broadcast on Fox 13, KSUT, and will take place in Salt Lake City Memorial Hall unless otherwise stated. All cards are hand written leading to an exciting and original card.
If you can help out the card writing team please send an e-mail to [detail withheld] 9. Roleplays and Flashes As with the majority of e-wrestling federations the results of matches are decided by the quality and standard of roleplays or flashes. In the
ASWA all wrestlers who have a match must submit at least one flash or strategy by the deadline.
When the card is released a deadline will also be posted and this is the date that flashes and strategies should reach the president. A flash is basically an interview with your character and if you do not have a match then there is no need to flash.
Also, every week another deadline will be set for the flash packet. This means that wrestlers can submit another flash prior to the card which is sent out to the roster along with all other flashes, allowing some heat to build up before the event itself.
We ask that all wrestlers attempt to stick by the following guidelines when posting a flash...
