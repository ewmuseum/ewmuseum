Friday Night Fury (Part 3 of 4)

13 December 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Here is part three of Friday Night Fury. Please reply with any feedback and visit our website at
http://www.efeduniverse.com/aswa for more information. End of Advertisement Break Fury returns to the air with a shot of the play by play commentary team, Mick McKinsley and Jimmy "Crossroads" Callaghan...
MM: Welcome back to a real ding dang of wrestling action! JC: Ding dang? Since when did you become Garth Crooks? MM: Up next is Tony Geller taking on Steve Norton! This match followed a brutal assault by Geller last week on Steve Norton and his tag partner, Enigma, after they had defeated Sex and Violence!
JC: Brutal? Pah! Brutal is what Bart Blair just did to Jacobs! MM: True, tonight we have already seen Frank Dayton win an excellent match against D-Man Daniels, Jeff Hendrix made a winning start against
Heath Fury and now Bart Blair has viciously beaten Isaac Jacobs!
The camera switches to a shot of the ring announcer within the ropes. He raises a microphone to his lips... Announcer: The next match is scheduled for one fall. Making his way to the ring, from Rio De Janeiro, Brazil, weighing in at 245 pounds,
Tony "Tank" Geller!
White rapper Eminem's "The Way I Am" is heard over the PA system and is swiftly met with a roar of boos and dislike as Tony Geller, dressed in the usual white trousers appears through the curtain. Geller sarcastically bows toward the crowd as the boo's intensify. The "Tank"
continues to make his way to the ring, stopping only to insult a few fans. Upon reaching the ring Geller snatches the microphone off the ring announcer and waits for the boos to slowly die down...
Geller: Good evening folks! You know, I am trying to please you guys every week, but still you boo me. I am trying to get rid of the crap that lurks in ASWA, but still you boo me. I am trying to make a name for myself, but still you boo me. You know what I say to that? Shut the hell up!
The boos rain in on Geller and he nods in agreement... Geller: Maybe I am an asshole, but I don't care. Tonight I have to beat
Steve Norton and then I get a chance at the title! And when Richard
Starr gets the Tank Wrath locked on him there ain't nothing he or any of you can do because I will be the next champion!
Geller hands the microphone back to the ring announcer and waits eagerly for Steve Norton...
Announcer: And his opponent, from Baton Rouge, Louisiana, weighing in at 245 pounds, Steve Norton!
"Kashmir" by Led Zeppelin rips through the arena and a huge cheers echo around Memorial Hall as Steve Norton, dressed in traditional wrestling attire, charges to the ring. The crowd go wild as he slides under the ropes but Geller quickly leaves the ring by climbing through the ropes on the other side...
MM: Geller exits the ring! He knows he's in trouble tonight! Norton looks around the arena before talking trash at Geller. Geller sarcastically agrees and tells the referee to take control of Norton.
The referee pushes Norton to the other side of the ring and tries to hold him back as Geller tentatively slides back into the ring...
MM: Look out! Here comes Norton! Norton pushes past the referee and charges at Geller who quickly slides back out of the ring to a chorus of boos. Norton points at Geller and tells him exactly what he thinks of him. The referee tells Geller to get into the ring and he very tentatively enters at the opposite side from Norton. Geller eventually gets in the ring and slowly stands to his feet...
MM: The fans aren't too appreciative of this! Boos still fill the arena as Norton walks toward Geller. The two men lock up and start pushing and pulling each other around the four corners of the ring. Geller is backed into a corner but manages to push
Norton into the opposite corner. Eventually they move back into the center of the ring and Norton hits a Judo like hip toss on Geller and quickly applies an arm lock...
MM: Norton utilizing some tricks he picked up in Japan there! Geller reaches out but is a long way off the ropes as Norton tries to take a firmer grip on the arm. Geller wriggles around on the mat and eventually frees up his left arm...
MM: Oh a cheap shot from Geller! JC: That's genius, good quality wrestling! Geller rakes the eyes of Norton, forcing him to drop the hold. Geller and Norton both get to their feet but Geller whips Norton across the ring...
MM: Drop kick by Geller! Norton slides back to the mat but is immediately hauled back to his feet by Geller. "Tank" pushes Norton onto the ropes and hooks his arms around the top rope. The fans are still booing like crazy as Geller starts to stomp away at the left leg of Steve Norton...
MM: Geller goes to work on that knee, he could do some serious damage! Norton winces in pain as Geller continues to stomp away. Eventually
Geller unhooks Norton, leaving him to drop to the floor in agony.
Geller then grabs Norton by the hair and attempts to pull Norton into a standing position. Norton, still very groggy, eventually reaches an upright position and is immediately nails with a bodyslam and followed in with a elbow...
JC: Gotta say that Geller is giving it out tonight! MM: Yeah you're right there "Crossroads"! Geller then drags Norton towards the turnbuckle by his legs. Upon reaching the corner, Geller slides out of the ring and grabs hold on the ankles of Steve Norton. Geller takes a quick glance around the arena before pulling Norton into the turnbuckle and right on to his crown jewels...
JC: Arggh! I can't look! Norton and the crowd wince in pain before Geller drags Norton round again and pulls him out of the ring feet first. The crowd insult Geller on the outside where he stomps away at Norton. The referee also joins them on the outside to ensure that there is no interference...
MM: Geller has his eyes set on the title shot! I can't see anything coming between them tonight.
On the outside Geller lifts Norton to his feet and hoists him up into the air. The crowd move back sharply as Geller drops Norton face first on to the steel safety barriers. Norton crumples to the floor and once again Geller lifts him to a standing position and then up into the air...
MM: Norton slides off Geller! Norton grabs Geller from behinds and forces his head onto the steel safety barrier to the crowds delight. Both men fall to the floor with exhaustion and the referee wastes no time in starting the ten count....
"1...2...3...4...5...6...7-" Both men stagger to their feet and start trashing sluggish blows. The crowd cheer as Norton takes charge and hammers away at the skull of
Geller. Norton then whips Geller towards the far side of the ring...
MM: No way! Geller ran into the referee! Both men go down! The referee slumps to the floor along with Geller and Norton quickly approaches. Norton lifts Geller off the referee and bodyslams him on the concrete. Geller clutches his back but is once again lifted to his feet. Norton pulls Geller towards the commentary table...
MM: Arggh! Look out! JC: Incoming! Norton crashes Geller's head onto the commentary table as the crowd begin to cheer again. Norton slams Geller onto the commentary table again before rolling him back into the ring...
MM: Norton is reaching for a chair! Norton grabs a chair and tosses it into the ring. Steve Norton follows the chair into the ring by sliding under the ropes as the referee recovers on the outside. Norton places the chair flat on the mat and then drags Geller to his feet. Norton grabs hold of "Tank" and prepares for a DDT on the chair...
MM: Norton goes for the DDT! Wait! Geller reverses it! Geller hits a low blow which causes Norton to keel over and the Geller grabs Norton by the hair and drives his face onto the chair. The fans gasp and then begin to jeer as Geller rolls Norton over and hooks the leg. The referee slowly rolls into the ring and begins the count...
MM: Geller should have it! 1...2- Wait! Here comes Enigma! The Enigma charges down the entrance ramp to a huge cheer from the crowd. The referee prepares to count the three as Enigma slides under the ropes...
MM: 3! Enigma is too late! Geller gets his title shot! Enigma broke up the count a millisecond to late much to his frustration. Geller raises an arm in celebration but is quickly pulled to his feet by Enigma. The Enigma kicks Geller in the gut and then hits a huge powerbomb which shakes the ring...
MM: Geller is being attacked! This is revenge for last week! Geller and Norton both lie on the mat as "Comfortably Numb" by Pink
Floyd plays over the PA system. Enigma slowly helps Norton to his feet to a great reaction from the crowd.
Advertisement Break Scene opens with a shot of a rickshaw in downtown Beijing. A small boy pulls the rickshaw which has an attractive brunette in it. The camera zooms in and it becomes clear that the small boy is in fact Keanu
Reeves and the attractive brunette none other that Sandra Bullock. The words "Speed 7 : Run Son!" appear on the screen in flashy writing and a voiceover accompanies the image of the boy running through the crowded streets...
"Keanu Reeves and Sandra Bullock are reunited in Speed 7 : Run Son!" The scene switches to a close up of a very anguished Sandra Bullock... SB: Shit! There's a bomb on the rickshaw! If you go under 50 miles per hour then this thing is gonna' blow! Run Son!
A piece of action music then plays as the voiceover returns... "Speed 7. It's the same as all the others - but it cost more!" End of Advertisement Break Friday Night Fury returns for it's final segment with a shot of the ring announcer preparing to speak...
Announcer: The following match is scheduled for one fall! Making his way to the ring, from Albany, Oregon, weighing in at 240 pounds,
Leon "Purple" Hayze! The crowd go wild as "Purple Haze" by the master of guitar, Jimmi
Hendrix, fills the air. Leon Hayze appears through the curtain dressed in neon green wrestling tights and purple elbow pads and boots. His eyes are bloodshot and he looks like he is off it as he reaches the ring and takes a position in the far corner...
Announcer: And his opponent, from Norfolk, Virginia, weighing in at 240 pounds, Phil "The Specialist" Grand!
"Flick of the switch" by AC/DC fills the air and a mixed reception greets 6' 5" man Phil Grand. Following last week's comments Grand clearly has something to prove after claiming he deserves a title shot.
At reaching the halfway point of the entrance ramp Grand begins to charge at the ring and slides under the ropes...
MM: Phil Grand is a man with a mission. He wants to be recognized as the best but he's gotta prove himself tonight!
The bell rings as Grand gets to his feet and starts trading blows with
Hayze. The swap left hands and right hands at a furious pace which gets the crowd on their feet. Grand soon forces Hayze onto the back foot and whips him across the ring...
MM: Tilt-o-whirl Slam! JC: Good God! Hayze was nearly ripped in two! Grand immediately drags Hayze to his feet and hits a bodyslam followed up by a leg drop. The crowd are really excited by the start and once more Grand drags Hayze to a standing position. Hayze is whipped across the ring and into the ropes...
MM: Grand with the spinebuster! The thud echoes around the ring and is followed by a roar of agony by
Hayze. Grand then quickly locks on a half crab in the center of the ring...
MM: He's out of the blocks like a whippet! JC: A whippet? You mean a greyhound, those buses sure can move! Hayze writhes in agony as Grand locks the crab on tighter. Grand drops the hold after about five seconds and scoops up Hayze...
MM: Backbreaker! What a start from Grand! Phil Grand applies the surfboard on an already groggy Hayze. The crowd are impressed by Grand's start and start to cheer for the "Specialist".
The referee checks to see if Hayze wants to tap out as Grand locks the hold on even tighter...
MM: Hayze may tap out here! Look at the pain he's in! Grand eventually drops the hold and pulls Hayze to his feet. Grand pushes Hayze onto the ropes and the "Purple One" slumps onto the top rope...
MM: Oooh! Big chop from Grand! And again! Hayze struggles to stay on his feet and is whipped across the ring by
Grand... MM: Clothesline attempt! Hayze ducks it! As Hayze returns he nails Grand with a clothesline of his own to buy himself some valuable time. The crowd are still cheering as both men crawl towards the ropes...
MM: Both men are down, the referee starts the ten count! "1...2...3...4...5-" Both men stagger to their feet and turn to face each other. Grand runs at Hayze who hits a drop toe hold to cause Grand to eat a face full of mat. Hayze gets to his feet and pulls Grand up by his hair. A reverse suplex is followed by a hook of the leg...
MM: Hayze covers! 1...2...Grand kicks out after two! Hayze gets to his feet and pulls Grand to a standing position. Grand is whipped into the corner and hits the turnbuckle with force. Hayze follows in with a clothesline but is met with a boot to the face...
MM: Great counter by Grand! Hayze staggers back and is nailed with a sidekick which sends the crowd wild. Once more Grand drags Hayze to his feet and pulls him to the corner. Grand sits Hayze on the top rope and follows him up...
MM: Specialist Suplex! Grand hits his trademark Superplex and then hooks the leg to a huge cheer... MM: This could be over! 1...2...3! Grand takes the honors! JC: An excellent match all round! Grand was fantastic! MM: Phil Grand has proven a point tonight! He was ready to mix it with the big boys! What will happen next week!
JC: But we still got more action tonight! Up next, the ASWA Utah State
Title is on the line as Grant Grayson takes on John Steele!
Scene cuts to a shot of a corridor at Salt Lake City Memorial Hall. Richard Starr walks along the corridor, with his title belt around his waist, Starr walks past the camera and follows a sign that reads "Ringside" as the scene returns to the commentary table...
MM: The champ is in the building! What is he doing here? Sent via Deja.com http://www.deja.com/
