Friday Night Fury (Part 4 of 4)

13 December 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Here is the final part of Friday Night Fury. Please post any replies and visit our website at
http://www.efeduniverse.com/aswa The camera switches to a shot of the ring announcer who raises a microphone to his lips... Announcer: The following bout is for the Utah State Championship, introducing first, the champion, from New Devil Hills, North Carolina, weighing in at 228 pounds, "Up Yours" Grant Grayson!
A huge boo fills the arena as "Blackball" by the Offspring roars around the hall. Grant Grayson appears through the curtain, dragging the title belt behind him. He walks with a casual swagger toward the ring ignoring the taunts of the fans as he goes...
MM: Grayson is up against it tonight! Bart Blair and Richard Starr both have designs on his ass-
JC: What? Those guys ain't gay! I am gonna tell Bart Blair you said that! MM: I didn't mean like that! Grayson steps in the ring and tosses the title to the floor which increases the boos from the crowd. The announcer speaks again...
Announcer: And the challenger, from Chicago, Illinois, weighing it at
263 pounds, John "The Liberator" Steele!
Steele appears on the entrance ramp as "Open Your Eyes" by Guano Apes is greeted with a huge cheer. Steele wears blue cut open trousers with the letters W-O-R down the legs, a leather jacket, Old Glory bandana and as he heads toward the ring he slaps hands with the fans and passes the bandana to a young boy at ringside...
MM: This match is gonna be huge! Utah State Title on the line, here we go! Steele climbs through the ropes and is immediately set upon by Grant
Grayson. The fans boo as the bell rings and Grayson hammers away at the back of Steele. Somehow Steele eventually forces his way through the ropes and makes it into the ring...
MM: Grayson is charged up for this match! Steele remains in a standing position despite Grayson's efforts to force him downwards. Grayson then tosses Steele across the ring by his hair but the big man get straight back up. The fans cheer as Grayson attempts a shoulder block which has little effect and once more a shoulder block attempt fails...
MM: Grayson will not overpower Steele! JC: Duh! That's pretty obvious when he gives up 30+ pounds! Grayson goes for one more shoulder block attempt but is nailed with a huge clothesline and Steele quickly stomps away at Grayson's legs.
After six or seven seconds Steele pulls Grayson to his feet and whips him into the ropes...
MM: Powerslam from Steele! Steele drops down and hocks the leg looking for the quick pin... MM: The cover from Steele, 1...Grayson kicks out with ease! JC: Look who it is! In the crowd - Richard Starr Sure enough Richard Starr has taken up a position in the front row. Meanwhile Steele pulls Grayson to his feet and whips him across the ring. Grayson ducks a clothesline attempt and returns with a swinging
Neckbreaker...
MM: Grayson fights back! Grayson pulls Steele to his feet and whips him into the ropes. Grayson hits a back drop which sends Steele over the ropes and smashing onto the concrete to a huge gasp from the crowd...
MM: Wow! Grayson sends Steele airborne! Richard Starr looks on with interest as Grant Grayson rolls under the ropes and drags Steele to his feet. Grayson takes Steele by his hair and pulls him up the entrance ramp and towards the curtain...
MM: They're heading backstage! Every so often Grayson punches Steele in the face to make sure he doesn't gain any advantage. However as they reach the top of the entrance ramp Steele hits a low blow...
MM: Steele fights back! DDT on the concrete from Steele! The crowd erupt in a huge cheer as both men lie flat out at the top of the entrance ramp...
JC: Both men are down! Steel is first to his feet and pulls Grayson to a standing position by his hair. The fans cheer as Steele pulls Grayson through the curtain and into the backstage area. Meanwhile, Richard Starr has left his ringside seat...
MM: Where are these guys going? JC: Wait! Where the hell is the champ!? The two men are in a large room which has a number of boxes lying around. Steele throws Grayson against a brick wall and then whips him into a pile of Styrofoam filled cardboard boxes...
MM: That's not going to effect Grayson! Steele isn't use to hardcore brawls, he may have made a mistake in heading backstage!
Grayson crawls off the boxes and kicks out at Steele. The man from
Chicago staggers back and smashes his head against a shelf on the wall.
Steele slumps to the floor and Grayson quickly approaches him, and stomps away at the challenger...
MM: This match is becoming brutal already! Grayson lifts Steele to his feet and drags him around the corner and into a large store room. Grayson takes a brush and breaks it in two on his knee...
MM: Oh no! Grayson crashes the brush over Steele! Steele slumps to the floor as Grayson goes to work on him with the brush. Grayson drops the brush and picks up a piece of 2 by 4...
JC: This could get very nasty! Grayson pulls back the 2 by 4 and cant swing it forward. He turns around and is stunned to see Richard Starr facing him...
Starr: I don't think so! Starr kicks Grayson in the gut and then then throws him out of the store room and onto the concrete floor as the fans in the main hall cheer as they watch the match on a large screen...
JC: That damn Starr! He's a cheat! Starr tends to John Steele and helps him to his feet before they both walk over to Grant Grayson. Both men start to stomp away at Grayson and he quickly slumps to the floor...
MM: Great teamwork! JC: It's cheating! Steele lifts Grayson to his feet and drags him back towards the entrance curtain. Starr bids him farewell...
Starr: I'll keep a look out for Blair. Finish it John! Steele reaches the entrance ramp and pulls Grayson through the curtain and onto the ramp to a huge cheer from the crowd. Steele pulls Grayson all of the way towards the ring and prepares to rolls him under the ropes...
MM: Look out! Here comes Blair! Bart Blair jumps through the crowd with a baseball bat in his hand. The bat is the same one he had earlier and he immediately pushes Steele away...
JC: What? Get Steele! Blair looks at Grayson and then crashes the bat across his knee with a sickening thud. Grayson's knee actually collapses underneath him causing him to scream out and fall to the floor...
MM: Oh no! That is awful! His knee is ruined! Grayson clasps his knee in agony as Blair continues to work it over with the bat. A number of fans turn away as Steele looks on in horror...
MM: I cant believe it! Grayson's career may be over! Blair rolls Grayson under the ropes and into the ring. He then looks at
Steele... Blair: You're next Steele. Any time and any place! Blair turns his back and heads backstage but as he moves up the entrance ramp Richard Starr charges down it...
MM: Now Starr is out here! Steele rolls under the ropes and drapes and arm over Grayson... MM: The cover! 1...2...3! That's it! John Steele is the new Utah State
Champion but Grayson is seriously hurt!
As son as the bell rings a paramedic rushes to the ring to tend to
Grayson and is followed by Helen Steele who charges out of the crowd to hug her battered husband in the ring. On the entrance ramp Blair attacks Starr, nailing him in the gut with the bat and then across the back with a stomp...
MM: We got a new champion! But this has been marred by Bart Blair! Bart
Blair and his brutality may have ended a career!
Fury finishes with a shot of Steele embracing his wife with the injured
Grayson behind them being attended to.
