Friday Night Fury (Part 2 of 4)

13 December 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Here is part two of Friday Night Fury. Please reply with any feedback and visit our website at
http://www.efeduniverse.com/aswa for joining information. End of Advertisement Break Fury returns to the air with a shot of the commentary table where Callaghan and McKinsley are seated... MM: Welcome back folks! We have just seen an excellent exhibition for the All Star Wrestling Alliance between D-Man Daniels and the victor,
Frank Dayton. I am being told that Frank Dayton is backstage with Ethan
Hunter, let's take a look.
The scene changes to a shot of Frank Dayton, looking suitably sweaty and ecstatic following his victory. He stands in front of a cream wall and with him is Ethan Hunter who looks miniscule stood next to the "Texas Devil"...
EH: Joining me, following yet another excellent victory, is the "Texas
Devil" Frank Dayton. Frank, that was another excellent performance, what's next for the "Texas Devil"?
Dayton responds, speaking in a gruff southern accent... FD: You know something, I've only got designs on one guy - Richard
Starr. He says he wants to be a fighting champion but I don't see any evidence of that! The damn "Tank" isn't a fighter - he's a nothing. If
Starr wants to be a true champion he's gotta' put that title on the line against a true wrestler!
EH: You've made an unbeaten start so far - do you think things will continue? FD: You damn right they will! I haven't seen anything to scare me in the All Star Wrestling Alliance, yet. So until I meet somebody who can match wrestling skills with me I will stay undefeated!
The scene quickly switches back to a shot of the ring where the announcer has taken up a position in the center of the squared circle...
Announcer: The following match is scheduled for one fall, making his way to the ring, being accompanied by Holly Wood, from Melbourne,
Australia, weighing in at 248 pounds, "Hurricane" Heath Fury! Oasis' "The Swamp Song" plays over the PA system as Heath Fury appears on the entrance ramp. He is followed by the stunning Holly Wood and he takes her hand and raises it into the air at the top of the entrance ramp. A fair number of the fans cheer and a lot of wolf whistles fill the air for Miss Wood. The attractive duo make their way toward the ring and look in awe of the setting...
MM: Heath Fury seems suitably impressed by the crowd and the venue
JC: Impressed? Jeez, I thought Australia was exciting, what with all those Lions, Tiger's and Bears.
MM: Lions, Tigers and what? JC: Bears. It's in that documentary, what's it called? MM: I haven't got a clue? JC: Jeez, something about a girl called Dorothy, she has these awesome shoes. Its the, er, "Wizard of Oz"!
MM: What? JC: Yeah, it's about some magician from Australia, it looks awesome! Heath Fury takes his place in the center of the ring and winks at Holly
Wood as the announcer raises the microphone to his lips...
Announcer: And his opponent, from Newark, New Jersey, weighing in at
220 pound, Jeff "Hammer" Hendrix!
"Sledgehammer" by Peter Gabriel fills the air as Jeff Hendrix, another newcomer to the federation appears through the curtain. He is hard faced and his whole body seems to lack emotion. He wears a pair of red wrestling shorts and boots and walks with a purpose toward the ring...
JC: What's eating Gilbert Grape? MM: Who? You mean Jeff Hendrix? JC: No, Johnny Depp, that's a good film! The camera focuses on Hendrix as he climbs through the ropes and immediately approaches Heath Fury and squares up to the Australian. The referee steps between them but both men are giving each other the eye and refuse to back off. Fury then begins to shout out...
Fury: You wanna' piece of me? Come on! Come on! Hendrix doesn't respond but glares straight at Fury. The referee calls for the bell and then jumps aside as the two men lock up...
MM: What the hell was that all about? JC: I don't wanna' know. I just wanna' know one thing. MM: What's that? JC: Holly Wood's phone number! Owwwwww! Meanwhile back in the match, Fury has applied a side arm headlock on
Hendrix, who attempts to push Fury away. Fury struggles but manages to keep hold of "Hammer" and then pulls Hendrix from behind him into the air...
MM: Front face suplex! Great maneuver by Fury! The crowd cheer in amazement at the move as Jeff Hendrix springs to his feet clutching his nose. Fury then whips Hendrix across the ring and into the corner. The Australian follows in with a clothesline...
MM: What a start by Fury! Fury pulls Hendrix out of the corner and then whips him into the opposite corner. Once again Fury follows in with a clothesline as the crowd cheer...
JC: He looks just like that guy. MM: Who does? What guy? JC: That's it! Heath Fury looks just like Mayor of Munchkin City! I told you that film was in Australia!
Fury then applies a choke hold in the corner of the ring which he applies for three to four seconds before the referee steps in. Fury applies the hold again and once more the referee steps in. By now
Hendrix has slumped to the floor in the corner and Fury begins to choke him with his boot...
MM: This is good stuff by Heath Fury! The referee forces Fury to drop the hold and he obliges by dragging
Hendrix to his feet and scooping him into the air...
MM: Ooh! Nice bodyslam from Fury! Here's the cover, 1...Only the one count! Fury drags Hendrix to his feet by clasping his hair. Hendrix seems to wail at his hair being pulled. Fury then whips Hendrix across the ring and into the ropes...
MM: Fury with a clothes-, No! Hendrix ducked the clothesline attempt and as he returns from the ropes he hits a swinging Neckbreaker and then quickly hooks a leg...
MM: What a move by Hendrix! 1...2...Oh so close! Fury sits up and shakes his head. He seems surprised at Hendrix's comeback and is soon on his feet again as the "Hammer" pulls him to a standing position. Fury is whipped across the ring once more and is met with a drop toes hold by Hendrix...
MM: The "Hammer" is fighting back again! JC: The "Hammer"? (Start's singing to himself) Can't touch this, der de de der dah da dah da, Can't touch this!
Fury crashes face first into the mat and Hendrix quickly sits on his back... MM: Camel Clutch! This could be over! Fury writhes in agony as Hendrix applies the hold with even more force. The referee is checking with Fury is he wants to tap out as Hendrix applies the hold with extra bite once again. Hendrix's face is a mirror of concentration as Fury winces in pain...
MM: Wait a damn minute! Holly Wood slides into the ring and creeps up behind Jeff Hendrix and grabs hold of the New Jersey native and pulls him off the near unconscious Fury. The referee quickly calls for the bell and makes
Hendrix the winner by disqualification but the match continues as
Hendrix manages to stagger to his feet, with Holly still clinging onto his back. JC: I wouldn't mind being Hendrix right now! Owwwwww! Hendrix then grabs hold of Holly's neck and tosses her over his shoulder, causing her to writhe in agony as her back crashes against the mat. Hendrix then raises his arms and "Sledgehammer" by Peter
Gabriel fills the air once again as Jeff "Hammer" Hendrix crowns his debut with a victory...
MM: Another fine match, Heath Fury and his partner are decimated in the ring! JC: How could he do it? How could he hurt such a pretty little thing! I better see if she's alright, I know resuscitation!
MM: Stay right there Jimmy! The action is not finished yet! Scene switches to a low budget promotional film. The promo opens with a shot of Richard Starr in action against D-Man Daniels, it is followed by action reels of the superstars of ASWA. Bart Blair, John Steele,
Grant Grayson and others. This whole section is accompanied by "Take a
Look Around" by Limp Bizket. The music and images suddenly stop and a waving Old Glory appears on the screen and a voiceover, which seems fresh from an advert for action figures, speaks over the waving flag...
"The All Star Wrestling Alliance is going on tour! Top wrestling action, attitudes from hell and much more are coming to a town near you!"
A shot of Richard Starr then fills the screen, he has his championship belt draped across his shoulder and he winks at the camera.
End of Advertisement Break Friday Night Fury returns to the air with a shot of the ring announcer ready to introduce the next match up...
Announcer: The following match is scheduled for one fall. Making his way to the ring, from Detroit, Michigan, weighing in at over 250 pounds, Bart "Blockbuster" Blair!
"Walk" by Pantera starts to play and Bart Blair comes to the ring. He is dressed in his wrestling gear and carrying a baseball bat in one hand and a microphone in the other. His hair is pulled back in a ponytail and the scar of the left side of his face is outlined with blood red paint. He casually strolls down to the ring sneering at the fans. He stops only to rip a Richard Starr sign out of the hands of a fan at ringside and tear it to shreds before climbing into the ring.
The fans are booing him quite loudly... BB: Shut your stinking pie holes you pieces of crap. The fans boo even louder and a couple of cups even fly into the ring but they miss Blair...
BB: Grant Grayson, you are going to find out what it is like to bite off way more than you can chew. Big deal your the Utah State champ. In the greater scheme of things this state is a flea on a dog's ass. You talk about the big payday your going to get because your the champ.
Yeah that big payday and a quarter will get you a phone call. Your a piece of <bleep> wrestler with a piece of <bleep> title and I am going to flush you back into the sewer you crawled out of. I couldn't give a damn about that crappy title so I let you have it. That's right I let you win the belt because in a best case scenario that belt is second best and Bart Blair does not compete to come in second place. Now as for your little pre-emptive strike last week. It was pretty cute to attack me in the back, but I am not whining. Instead I brought my friend here and as you can see your name is all over it.
Blair holds up a black bat and in silver letters it says "Grant Grayson"... BB: Your little nickname is going to take on a special significance when I shove this bat up yours. You started this war Grayson. Just remember that when your writing your memoirs from the hospital bed.
A few more cups fly into the ring some coming close to Blair and the crowd is getting louder in their booing...
BB: Now Richard Starr, you were handed that belt on a silver platter by a sore loser. I am not going to say anything else about the way you won that title but consider yourself officially challenged. In fact I challenge you to a cage match for that belt. I don't want your little buddy Steele coming down and saving your ass when I am about to cripple you and take your belt. You might wanna think about what I am saying
Starr. I would hate to see you have an accident a spend a few months watching from the sidelines instead of competing. We all know your accident prone and you tend to get hurt in the back slipping on a banana peel or dropped soap or whatever.
The crowd is getting rabid and Blair seems to be soaking it in... BB: Now John Steele, surely you realize your not ready? Once, a long time ago you might have been ready for this moment, but now your softer than a rotten tomato. Do you still have the killer instinct Steele?
When the time comes can you look across the ring at me and say your prepared to go the whole nine yards? I will beat you John. I will beat you like a dog. Your wife knows this. She tried to protect you, give you some time to prepare for the hell that will be this match. She knows what I am going to do to you Steele.
She doesn't want to see your career end early. You should listen to your wife and hide. You talk about coming back from the dark recesses and being stronger than ever? I live in those dark corners. I am the whisper of doubt when you enter the ring. I am the smell of terror when you wake up from having nightmares about me and what I did to you long ago. I am the fear of failure you harbor when you look in the mirror and think "Am I good enough to beat him". I am your personal demon and the only way your going to get rid of me is to kill me with your bare hands in that ring. You climbed out of the hell I created for you once but this time I will drag you back into it and not let you out. You see your wife knows me better than you think. Of course when I first met her she didn't really know what
her name was. She was a confused soul who needed comfort and love and for awhile I was all that and more. Lets bring out the loser I have to face tonight I have women waiting for me back at my hotel room.
The ring announcer steps back into the center of the ring and raises the microphone to his lips...
Announcer: And his opponent, from Minneapolis, Minnesota, weighing in at 285 pounds, "The Deranged One" Isaac Jacobs!
Cypress Hill, those depressed rappers, are heard mumbling over the PA system as Isaac Jacobs appears on the entrance ramp accompanied by "Insane in the Brain". Jacobs looks on a different planet as he steps through the curtain and walks with a swagger that airs neither confidence or stability towards the ring...
MM: This match promises to be action packed, two excellent wrestlers together in one ring! JC: Where? Maybe you could point them out? Jacobs climbs through the ring he twitches which makes the front row of the crowd a little nervous. The referee gives them the pre-match warning and then calls for the bell. The bell rings and the two men lock up. Jacobs takes the early advantage and the tie up moves to the corner. Bart Blair is backed into the corner and Jacobs knees him in the gut twice. However Blair manages to reverse the situation and leaves Jacobs backed into the corner...
MM: Blair sounded focused prior to the match, now he needs to apply himself to the match! Blair knees Jacob's in the gut and then hits a forearm to the face of the "Deranged One". Blair knee's Jacobs again and then pulls him back towards the center of the ring. Blair hoists Jacobs in to the air...
MM: Northern Lights Suplex from Blair! Jacobs body jumps into the air following the impact and Blair quickly hooks the leg for a cover...
MM: The cover! 1...2...Oh Jacobs kicks out! Blair looks a little disgruntled with the referee and drags Jacobs to his feet. Blair holds Jacobs by his head and continues to argue with the referee...
MM: Low blow from Jacobs! Blair didn't have a clue! Blair keels over in agony and Jacobs hits a gut wrench powerbomb which sends the crowd wild. Jacobs seems to have gained some momentum and he drags Blair to his feet. Jacobs whips Blair across the ring...
MM: Oooh! Knee to the chest! Blair crashes to the mat and looks in agony as the cheers from the crowd continue. Jacobs moves over to Blair and as he reaches down to pick him up Blair rolls out of the ring under the ropes. The fans boo and jeer Blair and he staggers around the outside...
MM: Blair rolling out of the ring, what a Jessie! JC: Well, it's the right thing to do, any good wrestler would do the same. MM: Jacobs and the fans ain't to happy about it though Blair abuses a few of the crowd who jeer him and begins to regain his composure on the outside. Jacobs casts out a few insults as Blair challenges him to come outside the ring...
MM: I'll tell you now, if Jacobs goes through the ropes I don't hold much hope for him, Blair is at home without rules!
Jacobs obliges and climbs through the ropes before jumping down where he instantly begins trading blows with Blair. The crowd on the front row go wild as Blair is soon forced on to the back foot. Jacobs grabs
Blair by his hair and drags him around the ring...
MM: Jacobs trying to whip Blair into those steel stairs! Blair reverses the whip, sending Jacobs crashing into the steel stairs, leaving both the steps and Jacobs a crumpled mess...
MM: I told you, Blair is at home on the outside! JC: Kinda like a banker in Wall Street? MM: Yeah, now what is he doing? JC: Or like Monica Lewinsky in a Presidential office? MM: He's going- What?! Blair stomps at Jacbobs before moving back around the ring. Jacobs pulls a steel chair from behind the commentary table and walks back around to what Isaac Jacobs lies. The fans begin to jeer Blair who smirks at them as he nears Jacobs. Blair pulls Jacobs off the steel steps and lies his battered body face down on the concrete...
MM: He's got a plan, it's in his eyes! Blair smirks at the crowd once more before setting up the chair next to
Jacobs. Everybody in the arena looks puzzled as the referee tells both men to get back in the ring. Blair pulls Jacobs to his feet and hits a big chop to the chest which brings a tear to everybody's eye. Blair pulls Jacobs away from the chair and toward the safety barrier...
MM: I am telling you, this is about to get nasty! Blair takes Jacob's head and crashes it against the steel safety barrier in front of some abusive fans. Blair quickly tells the crowd where to go and tries to ram Jacobs into the barrier again...
MM: Wait! Jacobs blocks and then smashes Blair into the safety barrier! Blair bounces back up, looking dazed and shocked, Jacobs proceeds to ram Blair back into the steel barrier, and again! The crowd go wild as
Jacobs whips Blair back across the concrete and straight into the steel chair...
MM: The plan backfired! The crowd are going wild! JC: That's because their animals, pigs, typical pigs! Blair crashes into the chair and flips over in the air. Jacobs reacts to the cheers and quickly staggers over to an injured Blair. Jacobs take Blair by the hair and rolls him into the ring. Blair lies motionless on his back as Jacobs follows him in under the ropes and hooks the leg...
MM: This has to be it! 1...2...Oh my God! How the hell did he kick out! JC: Pure fighting spirit, I knew Blair was made of steel! Jacobs and the crowd are silent with disbelief and look shocked. Blair slowly comes to but Jacobs hooks the leg again without even getting a one count. Jacobs tries in vain to get the cover...
MM: Blair won't stay down! He's getting up! Blair slowly gets to his feet as Jacobs grips onto his head. The crowd boo as Blair pushes Jacobs off him and into the ropes...
MM: Tilt-o-whirl slam! Blair covers! An exhausted Blair drops down onto Jacobs and the referee makes the count... MM: 1...2...Jacobs kicks out to the crowds delight! Blair is furious with the count and remonstrates with the referee. The referee argues back...
MM: No way! The referee got nailed with DDT! The crowd boo as Blair talks trash to the referee. Blair slides out of the ring and grabs the crumpled chair from off the concrete. Blair slides the chair into the ring and follows it in...
MM: He's in that mood again! JC: Time of the month? I get that too. Blair grabs the chair and lifts it into the air... MM: Oh that is savage! Blair crashes the chair onto Jacobs who's body shakes due to the force. The boos rain in on Blair and some cups are tossed into the ring. Once again Blair crashes the chair onto Jacobs' skull. Blair seems to laugh at the pain he is causing Jacobs...
MM: And again! And again! This is awful, stop this! JC: Go and stop it then! I would love to see you try! Blair drops the chair in the center of the ring and spits on the fallen body of Isaac Jacobs. Jacobs is bust open quite seriously and blood drips from a gash in his forehead. The referee meanwhile begins to lift himself up along the ropes. Blair quickly pulls Jacobs to his feet and hits a double arm DDT on the steel chair as boos fill the air...
MM: This is horrible, I can't believe it! The referee turns around in time to see Bart Blair hook the leg of the bloody Isaac Jacobs. The still groggy official makes a very slow count...
MM: 1...2...3! Blair has the victory but he went to far! It was brutal, it was anarchy, it was illegal-
JC: It was fantastic! MM: Up next is Tony "Tank" Geller versus Steve Norton! Stay tuned! Advertisement Break Scene opens with a shot of a lovely grassy meadow. A few buttercups glisten under the sun and two small children run into sight. They skip and jump about quite happily in the meadow. Until a suited gentleman approaches them. The camera zooms in on them and the gentleman is very obviously meant to look like George W. Bush Junior. The look a like of the President elect speaks with a fake Texan accent...
Bush: Say kids, you must be pretty tired after all that running about? The little children, one boy and one girl nod in agreement... Bush: Would you like a nice comfy chair to sit in? Again the little children nod... Bush: Okay, follow me! The children follow Bush to a set of two wooden chairs in the middle of the meadow. He beckons to them to take a seat and they do. Bush then takes a remote control out of his pocket and hits a big red switch before releasing a cackle. The children become locked in their chairs...
Bush: Fry you little terrors, fry! Bush hits another button and the camera focuses on his screams and cackle whilst off screen the sound of frying flesh can be heard. The camera switches to a shot of two small skeletons in the chairs and a slogan appears on the screen...
"New Bush, Same Danger!" A voice over accompanies the final shot... "Broadcast on behalf of the Gore Liebermann campaign" Sent via Deja.com http://www.deja.com/
