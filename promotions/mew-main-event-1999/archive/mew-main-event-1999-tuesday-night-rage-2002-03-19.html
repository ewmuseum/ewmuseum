Tuesday Night Rage

19 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

— Part 1 of 3 —

(MeW highlights Butch McCaul getting run down with a hummer and his exit from the hospital. It also shows Morbid Wolf asking Butch if he wants to remain on the shelf or get rehibilitated to wrestle by a special program Morbid runs.
Butch gives no answer as the scene fades out. Then MeW shows the rest of the MeW stars and various highlights that have gone on over the past few weeks.) Curt Love: Welcome to Tuesday Night Rage. And tonight we are going to kick things off with a Death Match. Our main matches are Baphomet vs Justin Tyme for the IC title and Sabbertooth vs Stunning Steve Anderson for the world title. We are in Madison Square Garden and we are live.
Pudge Michaels:Wow, Curt you never cease to amaze me. You seems to get dumber and dumber each time.
Curt Love: Any predictions for this night Pudge? Pudge Michaels:I'll save them for later Curt now lets kick this off with Superbeast vs Carnage and its a death match. Stan Wilson is ready as we go to him. [Stan Wilson: First off let me introduce Superbeast! And his opponent is Carnage!] [(The music of Carnage plays as he comes down to the ring and rolls in)] [(Rob Zombie plays over the pa system as Superbeast comes down to the ring with a 2x4 in hand)]
Curt Love: Superbeast is in the ring and he just leveled Carnage with that 2x4. Carnage is down. Now Superbeast is beating Carnage with that 2x4. Carnage is in serious pain early and it appears as if he really didn't care to get his revenge on Superbeast. Superbeast is still beating Carnage with that 2x4 and then he stops.
Pudge Michaels: Carnage looks a little too pathetic for me. Superbeast picks up Carnage and throws him out of the ring. Superbeast then rolls out and he gets the ring bell. Superbeast then places the ring bell in the face of Carnage and kicks it back into the face of Carnage. Carnage is busted open. Nothing new. He is bleeding profusely from his head.
Curt Love: Yes, this definitely a massacre by Superbeast. Carnage with a blind punch and Superbeast moves as Carnage falls face first into the ring steps.
Superbeast picks up Carnage and piledrives him on the ring steps. Superbeast picks up Carnage and throws him into the ring then slides in and covers Carnage the ref counts 1..............2.................3!
Pudge Michaels: Another effort my Carnage wasted your winner of this Death match is Superbeast!
(We go back stage as Lance Point is sitting in the locker room watching a tape of Lucas Mannings previous matches. Barry Gomez comes to up Lance Point to ask him a question.
Barry Gomez: You don't seem too enthused about Maximum Lucas Manning. Is there anything you would like to say to him?
Lance Point: Hey Lucas you think you are so damn tough. Face me in a 25 ft scaffold match if you got the balls you will accept.)
Pudge Michaels: Well his plans got screwed up at last weeks Rage but now it appears as if Lance Point is out looking for blood. What a match that would be!
Curt Love: Pudge you are right there. So lets move on to our next match. I'm sure the night will get more interesting as the night goes on. Our next match
is Adam Creed vs Silent Thunder. [Stan Wilson: First welcome Adam Creed. And his opponent is Silent Thunder] [(The music of Adam Creed plays over the pa system as he comes down to the ring he then rolls into the ring and leans against the ropes)]
[(Silent Thunders music plays as he comes down to the ring and then he slides in. He then has a stairdown with Adam Creed)]
Pudge Michaels:Both men exchanging rights and lefts and Creed gets the first knock down as he picks up Thunder and sends him to the ropes. As Thunder is coming off the ropes Adam powerslams Thunder. Thunder rolls out of the ropes to catch his breath.
Curt Love: Creed on top of the turn buckle. Damn it! A suicide dive and Thunder is down. Creed now tries and irish whip but Thunder reverses it and Creed goes shoulder first into the ring post. Thunder with repeated knee drops to the face. Creed is bleeding a little bit as Thunder now picks up Creed and rolls him into the ring.
Pudge Michaels: Thunder rolls into the ring and hits a diving clothesline. Creed goes down as Thunder covers. The ref counts 1.........2....and no Creed kicks out. Now Creed with a submission attempt and will he get it? Yes its an stf. But somehow Thunder makes it to the ropes.
Curt Love: Thunder with an irish whip but Creed reverses it and he gets a firemans carry. Creed with a mexican surfboard submission as Thunder is nowhere near the ropes he has to tap but give it up for Silent Thunder for his effort.
Adam Creed at least for the moment escapes Silent Thunder.

— Part 2 of 3 —

(Backstage the name plate on the door has Leap of Faith on it and we go inside.
Justin Tyme is talking to Butch about his match later. Justin Tyme: I can't explain it Butch but I have a good feeling about this. So how are you feeling? Morbid Wolf asked you a question are you actually going to accept?
Butch McCaul:Man, I don't even know. We'll have to see. I'm not giving any him any chances to further injure me....
The scene fades out) Curt Love: Well Morbid Wolf is patient we do know this but he is seeking an answer but I don't think he has Butch on his target list anymore. What do you think Pudge?
Pudge Michaels: Butch has a brain and Morbid is not concerned. If Butch turns down his help thats what he does but anyways lets move on to our next match.
Its fatal four way. We have to take a break and then we'll have the match in its entirety. {Commercial Break} Curt Love: We are back and now its time for the fatal four. This should be a great match. Lets go to Stan Wilson.
[Stan Wilson: Please welcome the challengers for this match they are Zombie, Hendrel, Dean Lynch, and Gangsta] Pudge Michaels:Well the action has already broken out Zombie clotheslined Dean out of the ring. Gangsta has Hendrel in a submission but Hendrel is in the ropes and is forced to break the hold.
Curt Love: Hendrel clotheslines Gangsta down then repeatedly elbow drops him. Zombie goes over to Hendrel and throws him out of the ring and pins Dean Lynch who just crawled back into the ring. The ref counts 1....2.....3! Zombie wins the fatal four way. He took advantage of the situation.
(In the parking lot Charile Krebs is just arriving for an interview concerning his tag match when all of a sudden Morbid Wolf comes out of nowhere and clotheslines Charlie. Charlie falls onto the concrete. Morbid then drags
Charlie over to a miscellaneous car and smashes his head through the car window. Morbid Wolf spits on Charlie Krebs then walks off)
Curt Love: I don't know if that was so smart, but I'm really not one to say you know. Anyways what do you think Pudge?
Pudge Michaels:I think that Krebs got an early taste of what Friday will feel like. Anyways whats our next match?
Curt Love: The winner gets a TV title. Shakin Shawn Johnson vs "The Chosen One" "Too Pretty" Jearmy Patrick. [Stan Wilson: Welcome to the ring Too Pretty Jearmy Patrick and his opponent is Shakin Shawn Johnson.] [(Too Prettys music plays as he comes down to the ring)] [(Shakin Shawn Johnsons music plays as he goes to the ring and then rolls in)] Pudge Michaels: Too Pretty with just a straight boot to the face as Johnson goes down. Johnson then recovers and clips Too Prettys knee. Too Pretty is stubbling as Johnson gives a quick knee to Too Prettys face.
Curt Love: Too Pretty is down but Johnson runs against the ropes and tries for a leg drop but misses then Too Pretty roles up Johnson as the ref counts
1........2............3! Too Pretty steals this one. Too Pretty will face Morbid Wolf. You know that will be a war especially now that it appears Morbid is looking to collect another title to increase the competition.
Pudge Micheals: Wild night so far. A night of ok matches and we saw Charlie Krebs get his head smashed through a car window that was great now we are moving on to our next match which is Marty Franklin with Lance Henson vs Mr.
Hercule Sa-ten. Now lets go to the ring. [Stan Wilson: First accompanied to the ring by Lance Henson is Marty Franklin. The Challenger will be Hercule Sa-ten.] [(The Corporations Music plays as Marty Franklin comes down to the ring. Lance Henson goes over to the commentary booth and puts on a headset.)] [(Sa-tens music plays over the pa speakers as he rolls into the ring)] Curt Love: Lance, do you have any predictions here? Lance Henson: Of course I do and Marty Franklin will win. Gurantee that. Pudge Micaheals: I guess we will see then. Marty Franklin with the cheap shot as Sa-ten goes down. Marty Franklin with a chokehold. The ref warns him to let go and he goes. Marty Franklin now with repeated leg drops as Sa-ten is still down.
Curt Love: Now Sa-ten is up with a lunging clothesline and Franklin is down. Sa-ten picks up Franklin for a hard spinebuster. Franklin is down but now Sa-ten picks up Franklin and throws him out of the ring. Sa-ten is over here talking smack to Lance Henson.
Lance Henson: Hey Herc, don't make me kick your ass if you want a damn match I will make you cannot win. Don't fuck with me buddy!
Curt Love: Calm down Lance! What the hell? Herc just punched Lance and now Lance is on the floor and is just now getting his composure regained. He has his headset back on.
Chair shot to the face of Sa-ten and he is down. Marty Franklin rolls Sa-ten in and makes the cover. The ref doesn't see Lance Henson holding down Sa-tens leg and the ref counts 1.........2........3! With an assist from Lance Henson Marty
Franklin wins! Pudge Michaels:You never punch a man with the power to make your life miserable. (We go back stage as we see Lucas Manning knocked out hanging on a locker room wall. He is knocked out with dried blood all over his face. The scene then fades out.)
Curt Love: Very eerie there as we are ready for the IC title match. Justin Tyme vs Baphomet. Pudge last minute predictions?

— Part 3 of 3 —

Pudge Michaels: No now leave me the hell alone you dumb bastard! Lets go to
Stan Wilson in the ring. [Stan Wilson:Introducing the MeW Intercontinental Champion he is Six Foot Even. He weighs 176lbs and he is from parts unknown please give it up for Baphomet. Next he is the challenger he is Six Foot 2 inches, he weighs 212lbs and he is from Orlando Floria please welcome Justin Tyme]
[(An apprentice of Satan by dark funeral plays as the arena is dark and a blood red spotlight follows Baphomet down the ramp way as he enters the ring with the
IC title in his hand the lights dim back to normal.)] [(Blood Brothers by Papa Roach plays as Justin Tyme runs down the ramp way then slides in.)]
Pudge Michaels: The IC title is on the broadcast booth as Justin tries for a suplex but Baphomet reverses it into a drop toe hold. Justin then recovers as
Baphomet grabs Justin by the hair but Justin reverses and sends Baphomet into the turn buckle. Justin with a running clothesline.
Curt Love: Not much seen from Baphomet here but maybe it will change. Baphomet with an irish whip to Justin then a sidewalk slam. Baphomet with repeated elbow drops. And also kicks to the ribs.
Pudge Michaels: Baphomet goes for the early pin and the ref counts 1....2.... and Justin kicks out. Baphomet picks up Justin and powerbombs him and then pins Justin again but the ref counts 1.....2...and Justin kicks out. Justin grabs Baphomets leg for a dragon twist. Baphomet is down. Justin then grabs up Baphomet and throws him out of the ring. Justin then grabs up Baphomet and sends him face first into the ring steps. Justin throws Baphomet into the ring and covers Baphomet then the ref counts 1.....2...... and Baphomet kicks out. Curt Love: Justin grabs Baphomet up and sets him up for the Tyme Bomb. Can he get it. Yes! The Tyme Bomb on Baphomet and Tyme covers Baphomet as the ref counts 1........2........3! Justin Tyme is the new Intercontinental Champion. A night he earned finally. Justin will be celebrating for awhile.
(We then see Sabbertooth heading for the ring as then it cuts over to Stunning Steve Anderson heading for the ring looking pretty confident he is going to win.) Curt Love: This match has started at the ramp way before we could get started. Sabbertooth with lefts and rights knocking Steve Anderson down. Steve Anderson then gets up with a lunging clothesline staggering Sabbertooth.
Pudge Michaels:Now Sabbertooth with a ddt to Steve Anderson on the ramp way. Wow this main event didn't take long to get started. Whoever keeps the world title here will deserve it. Now Steve Anderson is walking down the ramp way as
Sabbertooth runs and clotheslines him. Sabbertooth at ringside with part of the ring steps.
Curt Love:He runs at Steve Anderson but Steve kicks them back in his face. Sabbertooth is down as Steve Anderson rolls Sabbertooth in the ring. The ref counts 1.............2.......no Sabbertooth kicks out and Steve Anderson can't believe it.
Pudge Michaels:Hey dumbass you have to expect anything from Sabbertooth. Steve Anderson arguing with the ref as Sabbertooth shoves Steve Anderson into the ref and the ref is down. Sabbertooth gets the World Title and hits Steve Anderson in the face with it and Steve Anderson is down. Sabbertooth then tosses the title out of the ring. The ref is starting to stir but is still groggy enough to where he wouldn't notice much. Sabbertooth then grabs up Steve Anderson and uppercuts him as Steve Anderson goes down. Sabbertooth then walks over to the ref and stands him up.
Curt Love:What? Anderson may be out from the title shot to the face. This can't go down. Sabbertooth now has Steve Anderson in a half crab and Steve Anderson is no where near the ropes. The ref now coming to raises Steve Andersons hand up once, now twice, one more time and the match is over. The ref raises up
Steve Andersons hand again and it falls to the mat he signals for the bell to end the match and declares Sabbertooth still the World Champion. What is going to happen on Friday Fury please stay tuned. This is Curt Love for Pudge
Michaels signing off.
