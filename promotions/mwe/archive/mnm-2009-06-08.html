He grabs a hold of Hardaway’s legs and locks in the Boston Crab! Hardaway moans in agony, repeatedly hitting his head on the mat, wanting to find the strength to not tap out. At this point, the Hardaway fans are making their cases known, with the HARD-A-WAY chants. Wait… HARDAWAY TAPS! SINC IS THE MWE CHAMPION! …Kidding. Anyway, as the MWE cameras pan back to the ring, we see the sweat coming down Hardaway’s face, his hands clawing for the ropes, and he is about to put his fingers to the mat in pain… Until David Whitman comes from behind and hits Sinc square in the back of his head! Sinc lets go of the hold and falls face-first simultaneously. Whitman fans worldwide take a deep breath, as their Champion is still safe…for now. Because, out of nowhere, the at-one-time-exhausted Kevin Hardaway pulls Whitman down with a roll-up! …1 …2 …3! DOWN GOES WHITMAN, DOWN GOES WHITMAN! HARDAWAY IS THE NEW MWE CHAMPION! …Gotcha again. Here’s what REALLY happened. …1 …2 …Kick out! Ryan Adams: DAMNIT! I KNOW I USUALLY DON’T CUSS, BUT DAMN THEM ALL TO HELL! I honest-to-God thought Hardaway had it that time. Aw shucks. David Ferrera: You must be joking. Whitman ain’t going out on a roll up. Hardaway shakes his head, almost like in his mind, Whitman is just about invincible, and decides to target Sinc instead, who is still holding the back of his head in somewhat pain. He picks Sinc up slowly, before kicking him in the stomach… The Kevin Hardaway fans know what’s next… WILLIAMS DRIVER! Ryan Adams: KEVIN HARDAWAY HAS JUST HIT THE WILLIAMS DRIVER ON SINC! IT’S OVER, IT’S GOT TO BE OVER! …1 …2 ...But the count is interrupted once more, by David Whitman. Whitman, who had used almost every ounce he had to disrupt the count, is breathing heavily, obviously fatigued. Hardaway runs his hand through his hair in panic, knowing that he was inches away from being the MWE Champion. Not knowing whether to feel disappointment or to strive for it more, Hardaway unleashes on Whitman with rights and lefts, fully intent on letting Whitman know what he feels. He picks Whitman up after several dozen punches, and goes as far as to FLIP HIM OFF! This enrages the Whitman fans, of course, and right when Hardaway is about to finish off his variation of palm strikes and kicks, Whitman ducks! Hardaway quickly turns back around, trying to keep the momentum going in his favor, but Whitman quickly grabs him and hits THE BURNING HAMMER on Kevin Hardaway! Ryan Adams: BURNING HAMMER! THAT’S GOTTA BE IT! David Ferrera: Doesn’t look like Whitman is content with just that, though… Whitman indeed, does not end it there. He walks BACK to the turnbuckle, and climbs it slowly…he situates himself and has his face targeted at the crowd, with his back to the ring. He throws his finger out at the children in attendance before closing his eyes and taking…his…Final FLIGHT! Ryan Adams: FINAL FLIGHT! FINAL FLIGHT ONTO HARDAWAY, IT’S OVER FOR SURE! David Ferrera: Damn, another Whitman W… …1 …2 …BUT IT’S BROKEN UP BY SINC MERCIER, MY GOD! Sinc hit Whitman in the back of the head, preserving his chances at winning. Slowly, he rolls Hardaway outside, and keeps on rolling until he falls to the outside mat, knocked out cold from back-to-back Whitman finishers. Meanwhile, in the ring, it is Sinc and Whitman, mano a mano. Sinc uses the ropes for leverage once more, and picks Whitman along with him. With Sinc using one arm for the ropes, he kicks Whitman and… SINC MERCIER HAS JUST TOLD DAVID WHITMAN TO GO 2 SLEEP! HE CONNECTS! Ryan Adams: SINC MERCIER, WITH NO REGARD FOR HUMAN LIFE! Sinc, all adrenaline gone, falls to his knees and manages to throw a last-ounce-of-energy arm over the chest, of the fallen David Whitman… …1 …2 …… ….. ….3! Ryan Adams: MY GOD! THE BOYHOOD DREAM HAS COME TRUE! SINC MERCIER, IS YOUR NEW MWE UNDISPUTED CHAMPION! David Ferrera: Holy…sh*t. Fred Carry: Ladies and gentlemen, here is your winner and NEWWWWWWWWWWWWWW MWE UNDISPUTED CHAMPION, HE IS, SINCCCCCCCCCCCCCCCC MERCIER!!!!! As the camera pans to Sinc, title in hands and his head bowed, maybe the old Grand Mystique saying is true. Maybe, MWE might have the new generation’s Sonny Williams. Ten months ago, a journey began by a man named Sinc Mercier. After feuds with Windy Bridges and Mason Resoan, and this now-epic match… This is his time. This is his Full circle. Fade.
