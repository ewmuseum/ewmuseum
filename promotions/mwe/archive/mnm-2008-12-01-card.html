MWE Monday Night Mayhem card, 1 December 2008

1 December 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
LIVE FROM: Albany, New York - Times Union Center ALL RP'S DUE BY November 31st at Midnight CT! In what everyone expects to be an epic match to kick off Monday Night Mayhem, these two will go face-to-face in a preview to the MWE United States Championship match at Adrenaine Rush. Will Sinc Mercier show everyone why he's the U.S. Champion or will Mason sneak in with a victory before facing Sinc at Adrenaline Rush? Former number one contenders the Hollywood Hooligans were apart of a late-night trade that sent them over to Monday Night Mayhem in exchange for Sonny Williams. The Hooligans are out to redeem themselves on Monday after losing the Tag Team Championship match at Wrestle-Wars II. Meanwhile, they face a couple of MWE newbies in Scotty McKnight and Chris Cassidy. Can these newcomers to MWE show what they're made of by taking out one of MWE's top tag teams Monday night? In the opening match of the new X-Television Championship tournament, former number one contender Samuel Steele takes on the vicious Steve Cowan. For months, Cowan has haunted MWE, even former MWE superstar Jekyl by using his mask as a scare tactic. He has taken out constant superstars from behind, but can he take out the brash and less-experienced Samuel Steele when they face off in the first round of the Tournament? The other two competitors in the X-TV Title Tournament are the Mysterious G and Joe Roberts. Joe is an alumni of the old MWE, best known for his technical ability. He listed in an interview with MWE.com that one of his pet peeves are characters, and boy is he facing a character-and-a-half on Monday Night! Mysterious G has concealed his identity to all in MWE, only letting Richard Covell in on the surprise. Both men have put into contract that Covell will not tell the MWE world who lies behind the mask of Mysterious G, only if Mysterious G plans to reveal himself if he wins the X-TV Title. Because of Grand Mystique and the Maxx being drafted to Genesis, the MWE Championships now remain vacant. So who are the two top tag teams remaining on Mayhem? Darius and Boyd are one of them. D&B put smiles on the faces of many MWE fans last week with their entertaining debut promo, only to see them barely miss out on being MWE Tag Champs. Can they win Monday?Tex-Mex has gotta be one of the ''Strangest Tag Teams in MWE History'', right next to the Mayhem Mafia! They met backstage at Wrestle-Wars as Billy Joe saved Alejandro from being run-over, causing Alejandro to grudgingly feel like he owes Billy Joe something. Can they co-exist in their first tag team match, even with it being a Tag Title match? In what appears to be another epic Main Event to go down in Monday Night Mayhem history, MWE Champion David Whitman and former MWE Champion Brian Michaels square off in a battle for the ages. Michaels is 2-0 against Whitman, beating him at the original MWE Turmoil and the rematch the next night on Mayhem. Brian has constantly been the good guy throughout his run at MWEV2, however last week fans were treated to a more vicious side of Michaels, who completely destroyed D.C.'s car. Could this be just because of his hatred for D.C., or has Michaels gone back to his old ways? Whitman on the other hand, has been the consumate professional at MWE. He has defeated such top talent as D.C., Sonny Williams, and Alejandro Rodriguez in his new run at MWE. Whitman has got to be considered MWE's Wrestler of the Year thus far, but could his biggest test come against someone he's never beaten? We'll find out if Whitman can survive or if Brian Michaels can head into D.C.-Michaels III as the MWE Champion. If so, could it be a Champion vs Champion match at Adrenaline Rush, with Brian being the MWE Champion and D.C. being the World Heavyweight Champion? Or can David Whitman prevail once again in the clutch and continue his third run at the top? ADRENALINE RUSH CONFIRMED MATCHES: D.C. vs Brian Michaels - 3 Stages of Hell - Champion vs Champion? Sinc Mercier vs Mason Resoan - MWE United States Championship
