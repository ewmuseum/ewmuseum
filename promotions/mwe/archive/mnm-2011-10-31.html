MWE Monday Night Mayhem, 31 October 2011

31 October 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Show Opener: *We open to a plain black screen... a small hum over the TV as the camera is fed static. A quick glitch and hop in the static cuts automatically to... As the logo fades, the packed house of the Los Angeles Staples Center is in clear and present view. The stands are filled to the roof of the launch of the newly born Mayhem Wrestling Entertainment. Signs adorn the fans, "Welcome Back!" "M DUB E!" and other ceremonial sayings and quotes litter the crowd. Along with signs made for superstars for the night. As the crowd lets out a massive cheer, the cameras make a sweep.* Adams: Do not adjust you television, do not change the channel... Welcome Ladies and Gentlemen, to Monday Night Mayhem!!! Fererra: We're baaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaack!!! *The fans continue to let out screams but all fall into a chant of MWE! MWE! MWE! MWE!* Adams: I'm Ryan Adams, alongside my long time partner at the table, David Fererra! This is the first show, of the reformed, reinstated, and resurrected, Mayhem Wrestling Entertainment! Fererra: And we're starting things off right out of the gate, here in the beautiful city of Los Angeles! Adams: It feels like a lifetime, but we are, in fact, BACK! *The cameras are still sweeping over the fans. As a quiet fills the arena... a few moments of silence before it is broken up by... The intro notes play as people let out cheers, everyone by now, SHOULD know this song. If you don't... get out of Los Angeles, now. As the first words of "California Love" echo through out the arena, a smoke screen appears on the rampway. Through it, D.C. and Eli Silver emerge to a chorus of cheers. D is wearing a black "MWE" hoodie, with faded jeans, and a black Anaheim Angels blackout hat, backwards. Eli Silver is wearing a designer suit and tie. D hops in place with a smirk on his face a mile wide. Eli Silver can't help but smile as he looks around. D scurries to one side of the ramp to pump up the crowd, and then runs over to the other side. Eli begins his march towards the ring as D runs to catch up with Eli. The two make their way to the ring as fans go wild for the song, singing along.* Adams: And we are apparently going to be joined here in the ring, with CEO of MWE, D.C., and the General Manager of Monday Night Mayhem, Eli Silver! Fererra: Well, I'll go ahead and say it, since we all know we're thinking it... Elizabeth St. Claire made a HUGE mistake here, by putting these two in charge once again. Adams: Whatever your feelings may be partner, these are your bosses, I'd watch my tone if I were you. Fererra: You're as much a suck up as ever, glad to see some things may change, but most of it stays the same. *D.C. and Eli have gotten into the ring by now, and D.C. is standing on the 2nd rope closest to the camera waving his arms up to get the crowd to cheer. He hops down and points towards the far side, before settling in the middle of the ring, where Eli Silver has been standing the entire time, gazing into the audience with a smile. Eli hands D.C. a microphone. As D takes it, he paces around the ring a little bit as the fans react with a building cheer.* D.C.: What the f*** is up LOS ANGELES! *The fans let out a massive cheer as D.C. cracks another smile, looking around. The cameras cut to the audience to show them cheering up a storm. It cuts back to D.C. in the ring.* D.C.: For those that didn't get the memo, yes. What you are seeing is real, MWE is back... *Another pop from the fans.* D.C.: We got bought out by Tits McGee up there in the press box... *D points up to the luxury box at the top of the stands, as a spotlight shines onto Elizabeth St. Claire in a dress suit, with her arms crossed, and a forced smile on her face.* D.C.: And she has it in her mind that she has the mental capacity to bring MWE back from the dead. She believes she has the drive and motivation to bring MWE back to the number one wrestling federation in the world. *The crowd starts to pop as D.C. pauses.* D.C.: But she is doing this with daddy's money. This isn't her investment. She got MWE as a birthday present. *The crowd starts booing Lizzy as she looks on, D.C. can't help but crack a smile.* D.C.: F*** your suit and tie policy too!! *The crowd lets out another cheer as D.C. paces back and forth.* D.C.: So here is the deal, we're bringing that old fire back. We are bringing you reasons to watch wrestling again. We will prove to you why, MWE, WILL... NEVER... DIE!!! *D spikes the microphone down onto the mat and leaves the ring. Eli remains in the ring, looking down at the microphone. He slowly leans down to pick it up, and tapes it a few times, shivers running down his shoulders as the echo radiates around the arena.* Eli: I am Eli Silver... your General Manager of Monday Night Mayhem... *The fans let out a big pop for Eli, with little chants spurting up.* Eli: Well then... For those of you that are curious as to what is going to happen from here on out. We are going to be running a tournament leading to the inaugural Pay Per View, Crowning Glory. While, that Pay Per View has been technically a August event, we usually opened with CG as the first Pay Per View, so that is what we are going to continue... *The fans let out a quick cheer.* Eli: So, without further ado... Welcome back. Let's get this show started. *Eli places the mic on the mat slowly and makes his way to the back as we break into a commercial.* *A sexy looking cowgirl enters the ring with "Lenton vs Black" written on it. She then exits to whistles from the fans.* The sounds of cheers are heard around the arena. The wait in anticipation, almost in eager for him to come out. The lights flash a royal blue and suddenly "Take the Hard Bulls Over (Bliix Metal Mashup Remix)" by Rihanna ft. Jay-Z and Rage Against the Machine blares. Lenton busts through the curtains. The fans bust into a defying scream of cheers. Lenton is talking to the crowd jumping up and down on the stage, walking from one end to the next. The camera zooms in on his face, "Listen to that!" Lenton exclaims with a smirk. He walks up the ramp with a slight strut, looking out at the crowd. He stops short and looks around for a moment. He takes it all in, listening to the fans cheer for him. Some of the fans hand touch him. Stephen slaps the hands and continues to make his way up the ramp with his eyes glued on the arena again. Once he makes it to the apron, he looks looks at the ring and then climbs up on it quickly and stands up on the turnbuckle. As the song goes into a small guitar slowly with the voices harmonizing. The lights turn into a spotlight. The fans continue to cheer as the song begins to break down. Lenton forms a smirk on his face and lifts his arm in the air with enthusiasm. He wipes his mouth and leaps down from the turnbuckle and stands in the ring, circling it. He decides to hit the turnbuckle again standing on it lifting his right arm in the air the same way he did. He takes in the cheers, looking around slowly. Stephen leaps down and stands in his corner, getting ready to fight. Some Kinds Of Monster hits and the strobe lights flicker on and off Samuel Black starts slowly making his way to the ring. Halfway down the ramp and starts hitting himself in the head and talking nonsense to himself. He gets in to the ring sits in a corner and continues to hit himself and talk nonsense as he awaits for the bell to ring. Lenton vs Black is up first. The two pace around for a few minutes before finally locking up in the center of the ring. Black backs Lenton into the corner. Referee breaks them apart but not before Black takes a cheap shot at Lenton, with a slap across the face. Fans pop in favor of Lenton who comes flying out of the corner with quick punches to Lenton. The two tangle back and forth, exchanging punches. Black kicks Lenton in the knee bringing him down and quickly hits a DDT with Lenton on his knees and makes a quick cover to a count of two. Lenton rolls out of the ring as Black argues with the ref. As Lenton makes his way back onto the apron, Black goes on the attack but Lenton fends it off. Lenton enters the ring fast and goes for the 3:00, but Black avoids it and catches Lenton from behind. He drops him down reverse DDT style onto his knee, and then holds the position, locking in the Infectious Disease. Lenton taps out after a few seconds in the hold. Winner, and advancing towards Crowning Glory: Samuel Black. *Another sexy Halloween costumed girl enters the ring with a sign that reads Bowen vs Williams. She then exits the ring with cheers and whistles, as she blows kisses to the crowd.* Alex walks down to the ring staring at the fans and slides under the bottom rope. He walks over to the corner and falls down, setting in the corner he waits. CJ Williams walks out on the stage, raises his arms in the air, and leans back for a scream. He then walks confidently down to the ring, and enters via the top rope where he poses once more. Bowen arises from the corner with a furious attack on Williams. Eventually it spills out of the ring to ringside as the two exchange punches. Bowen knees Williams in the gut, he locks a frontface lock onto Williams, but CJ fights out of it with punches to the gut. Bowen escapes to the ring and CJ reenters the ring at the 7 count. Bowen continues his assault, but Williams irish whips him into the ropes and smashes Bowen down with a big shoulder block. Williams hits the ropes and lands a big body splash drop onto Bowen for a two count. Williams whips Bowen into the corner, lifting him up towards a possible Superplex. Bowen fights out of it. The two are battling back and forth as Bowen pushes Williams off the rope and onto the mat with a thud. Bowen launches off the top rope, but Williams brings his knees up. Bowen rolls in pain as Williams rises and taunts Bowen to a standing position. He kicks Bowen in the gut and nails him with the Gone In Three Seconds for the final three count. Winner, and advancing towards Crowning Glory: Carl John "CJ" Williams A stream of blood comes down from the titantron screen, and a deep brown iris burns a hole through every single soul in the arena, young and old. Fire seems to emanate as it intensifies, the course vaguely familiar as if you knew where it came from, the man’s name on the tip of your tongue. The camera zoomed out, slowly panning to reveal the man whose identity was known all along throughout the wrestling community. Zack Lifer, the one and only ‘Damned One’ of this business, was all that you could see. A monologue was coming, one that brought chills to everyone in attendance, the shiver of spines could be heard worldwide. He had that effect on people, and it was a clear circumstance that he would show the world how he has changed in the past few months away from the squared circle. “Been scared and battered. My hopes the wind done scattered. Snow has friz me, Sun has baked me, Looks like between 'em they done Tried to make me Stop laughin', stop lovin', stop livin'-- But I don't care! I'm still here!” The words were loud and clear as he quoted Langston Hughes, words which the “Forced Executioner” himself entirely stood for. Everything that went his way, he survived with an overwhelming ‘I’m still here.” The words and the idea of proving the world wrong as he lifted to the moment of becoming what he was truly born to do… It obsessed him to the bone. He would become the face of wrestling or die trying. An idea that everyone in the locker room would soon learn as they faced the man across that very ring. The date November 14th crossed the screen in a dominate streak from left to right. It would be his debut night, and it would be the beginning of an age. “The Age of Lifer is upon us, whether you like it or not.” A chorus of kids sang in harmony as it cut to dead silence: “This is finally your time… Zack Lifer.” Another random hottie, for no reason, parades around the ring in a "FBI" uniform holding up a card that says "Slade vs Dahlia", as she swings her hips, the men in attendance hold their breathes before the music from Tristan Slade brings their attention back... The cameras first get clouded with a picture of a crooked Mona Lisa for a few moments, soon it breaks, then when the words "Ten Percent Luck" start during the song "Scardonia" red laser lights shoot up from the ground cutting through a puff of smoke as Tristan strides slowly up the ramp letting his arms dangle at his sides. He takes his sweet time looking around him carelessly at the audience. He receives heavy boo's but still he continues to slowly and loosely go down that ramp. As the boo's continue, he stops almost reaching the ring, takes a few steps backwards, turns around, eyes his titantron, and frames himself in the tron admiring his image. He then breaks his fingers apart as if signifying he's breaking the frame, then reels back forwards, and rolls into the ring. He makes his way to the center of the canvas, makes that picture frame with his fingers once more, locks in on a random member of the crowd with it, motions the camera person to film his fingers around the crowd member with an extreme close up, and then breaks his fingers away signaling that crowd member is broken to him. Useless. After that, he chucks off his jacket to the outside ring and goes to a corner in a kneeling position awaiting his match with a shit eating grin. The crowd stands up anxiously waiting for the next participant. Suddenly, the lights go off leaving only a hint of light left illuminating the center of the curtains. The theme started with a heavy guitar solo radiating throughout the arena. As the bell rings the two walk towards each other in the center of the ring. Slade yells at Dahlia for a bit and is met with a slap across the face. Briefly holding his cheek, Slade kicks Dahlia in the gut and Slade hits a float over take down into a headlock. Dahlia fights out of it, tossing Slade into the ropes. Slade hangs on and Dahlia charges. Slade pulls the ropes down and Dahlia floats over, onto his feet on the outside, but Slade is already flying through the air and lands on top of Dahlia on the outside, nice spot. Slade picks up Dahlia and rolls him into the ring for a one count. Dahlia begins to stand as Slade follows him into the corner. The two tussle for a little and the referee separates them. Slade backs away with his arms raised, but pushes the ref aside and hits a quick clothesline on Dahlia. The ref in turn gets into Slade's face. The two argue for a while as Dahlia gets his barrings again. He comes out of the corner, nailing Slade with a spinning heel kick. Slade pops up and is lifted into a fireman's carry. Dahlia rolls through it, slamming Slade to the canvas, and follows it up with a fast moonsault into pinning attempt. Slade kicks out at two. He seems baffled by what just happened as Dahlia pumps up the crowd and goes back on the attack. Kicking Slade while he is down he backs up once again and taunts Slade from behind. The crowd gets pumped as Dahlia reaches out to go for Lost In Seclusion. He grabs Slade's arms, Slade fights out with elbows. Slade pushes Dahlia into the ropes and ducks under a clothesline attempt by Dahlia. Dahlia hits the ropes on the other side, into a springboard crossbody attempt as Slade spins into a 360 and nail Dahlia with the Have Palm Will Travel. Dahlia is laid out as Slade pins him for the three count. Winner, and advancing to Crowning Glory; "The Malice of Mind" Tristan Slade As we fade out from the last scene we go into a whole brand new one, but in a different kind of territory. We see Nate Claudius Rellington sitting back in a very large leather black office chair behind a very wide oakwood desk with a bull's skull engraved into it and an amber stone encased with a bullet inside it resting on the edge of it. Nate seems to be just starting to unpack his things from LA to make himself at home in his new quite LARGE office within the MWE. Before he unpacks all the rest of his stuff onto the desk he pulls out a cigar for a quick smoke smiling, eying the walls of his new office and all the accommodations and business certificates hanging on them with a freakishly fowl smirk. Just as he is starting to relax he hears a pounding on his door. He jumps from sudden fear. Nate: What the hell do you want? He doesn't hear an answer, the banging gets louder. Nate: Jesus H Christ you little shit don't make me call security on your ass! More banging. Nate: God dammit! That's IT! Before Nate picks up his phone under his desk the door begins to start being wiggled open, until after a few clicks it swings open in a creaking symphony. He hears the door open, drops the phone and perks his head up in a sweat and his cigar still in his mouth. There's nobody there. Nate: The hell? He starts feeling a breathing down his neck. Nate turns around and looks up, he see's Tristan Slade right in his face with a sharpened grin and trickles of sweat flooding down his cheeks after his match win. Nate: HOLY FUCKING BBQ! He jumps again from sheer terror and crawls backwards away from his desk as his cigar flutters out of his mouth and lands on his pant leg. Nate: OW! CHRIST! Who the hell do you think you are kid!?! Tristan creeps over to Nate and kneels down to him as Nate is sitting in a crab walk stance backed into a corner. Tristan picks up Nate's fallen cigar and places it carefully inside his mouth taking a puff and then putting it out on the rim of Nate's freshly cleaned cowboy hat. Tristan: Hello, Nathaniel. I'm Tristan Slade, and I know EXACTLY who you are. And this guy? Tristan points behind Nate as Nate looks behind him, there's Jackie Black filing his nails as he leans in the door way and waves hello in a kiddish glee. Tristan: That's JB, and we want something from YOU. JB: Pleasure to make your acquaintance mate. Nate: Oh yeah? And what makes you think I'm even gonna consider helpin two dumbasses barging in to talk down one of MWE's most honored business members? Tristan snaps his fingers and JB pulls out a handful of papers from his pockets. All of them are filled with tax Audits, subpenas, and unpaid fines and tickets. JB tosses them on Nate's lap. Tristan: Because I know your past Nathan. I know before you were manager to the one named Sinc Mercier you had ALOT of debts and....secrets...you didn't want getting out. You were bankrupt and a failing business man, so you let that half brained cum catastrophe be your escape, wanting to start fresh to help pay your debts. JB: It really is a shame sir, it would be a damn bloody murder if these were to get out. Tisk tisk Nathan, didn't you southern men have more pride than that? Nate: How'd you get those? Tristan and JB look at eachother and start laughing for a few seconds, then both stop suddenly turning from happy face to serious face. Tristan: That's for ME to know and YOU to find out. But the truth is, I can make ALL this go away IF you help me. Come now Nathaniel, don't you want more clients to help? I'm SUCH a vaaaaaaaaluable asset after all. Aren't I JB? JB looks at Tristan then at Nate nodding with a playful frowny face. Nate: Your bluffing! If there's one thing we "southern men" know it's a poker face partner! Tristan jumps onto Nate's lap prowling in front of his face nose to nose with an angered stare. Tristan: Am I? Do you REALLY want to question the one man who cannot lie? A master of mind and a genius of honesty? Try questioning me again, and see what happens. Next time that cigar is gonna be free game to be put out in that ash tray of a crotch of yours. Nate's mind is in a million and one places, not sure what to say or do. He simply just picks up one of his cigars, lights it, and shakes his head in a twilight. Nate: Awww...shit. As they meet nose to nose in the ring a chant breaks out for them to kiss, which, to make sure everyone is clear, would probably be incest. Way to support the Ol' South, you dirty redneck. When the guitar joins in, pyros explode from the stage and Lief steps out onto the stage, accompanied by his girlfriend Riley. She jumps up onto the apron and sits on the second rope as Lief makes his way up the steps and ducks through the ropes. Riley follows him into the ring and they move to opposite corners of the ring to pose to the crowd. "When Acting as a Wave" by The Dillinger Escape Plan begins bursting through the speakers abruptly. The lights starts to flicker to the odd timing of the beat as bursts of water randomly erupt throughout the arena, getting several crowd members doused. Bursts of flame also explode from erratic places (nowhere near the crowd though, thank goodness) and during all this madness Zack Evermore has appeared at the top of the ramp. He walks down the ramp at a leisurely pace, headbanging and getting lost in the song. Near the ring, he lunges inside, and jumps up and down a bit as a wide, crazy smile grows on his face. Riley goes nuts on the outside of the ring as Lief beckons the crowd to cheer for Zack. Lief kicks Zack a few times before flipping off the referee and picking up Zack. As he takes a few steps back, he blows a kiss on his right hand and sends Lief to the outside of the ring with a thunderous right hand. Riley rushes towards Lief aide as we see Zack pump up the crowd. We cut to a commercial break. Back from commercial and Lief has taken control of the contest. We see what happened during the break as Zack exited the ring to attempt to get Lief back inside. Riley provided a distraction of sorts, as she yelled, pissed, and moaned at Evermore until Lief managed to sneak a low blow from behind on Zack. Followed by another clothesline. Evermore lets out a blood thirsty battle cry as the fans light up. Evermore nails Lief to the mat with snap reverse STO, but only manages a two count to the fans dismay. Zack turns around and begins to mock her as Lief makes it to his feet. The referee tries to get Riley off the apron as Zack turns around. He immediately barrel rolls out of the way as Lief is coming straight at him with a Neutron Star Collision, but misses and he is on a dead shot right into Riley's stomach on the apron. He puts the breaks on, and you could swear to see the tire burn on his boots as his eyes go into a shock. Riley jumps down off the apron, half falling in the process as Lief turns around and gets blast with a fast left-right-left combo by Zack... Zack kicks Lief in the gut and hooks his arm, and lifts him up, bringing him down in the center of the ring with a Brainbuster, to complete the combo-finisher The High Five!!! 1... 2... 3!!!! Winner, and advancing towards Crowning Glory; Zack Evermore.
