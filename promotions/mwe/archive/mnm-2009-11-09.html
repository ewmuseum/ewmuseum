MWE Monday Night Mayhem, 9 November 2009

9 November 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
LIVE FROM: Albuquerque, New Mexico - University Stadium (18,018) Ryan Adams: It's an action packed tonight here in Albuquerque New Mexico! We have the return of Sinc Mercier against the young and flustered Zak Warner, no holds barred, it is going to be a helluva match. We also have the returning Kevin Hardaway after all his backstabbings and assaults lately taking center stage to show us how brutal he can still be in the ring. David Ferrera: Kevin has indeed crossed many lines attacking people the way he has, but now he can finally be taught a lesson as he takes on CSB for some real competition instead of cheap shot interferences. But who am I to judge, I LOVE THE BAD GUYS! Ryan Adams: Bad guys, good guys, or in betweens, this GWC and MWE War is far from over, I am sure we will see alot more interferences and attacks as time goes on, I am certainly bracing myself. David Ferrera: Whatever you believe doesn't matter, the fans love it, I love it, MWE vs GWC, let's do it! But first, it's our first match of the night! Zack Crash Vs The Virus Fred Carry: The following contest is scheduled for one fall! The lights dim just before the music starts as the crowd pops with a mixture of cheers and boos. At 00;32, Leon “The Virus” Roberts is seen rising up from the stage by platform as red fog fills the stage, the US Belt hanging lazily over his shoulder. Leon Roberts is now walking to the ring, exchanging hi-fives with the nearby crowd. Fred Carry: Introducing first, from Toronto Ontario, Canada. Weighing in at 275 pounds. He is the United States Champion…The VIRUS!!! Leon is now at the ring. He climbs up and over the top rope. He then goes to the center of the ring, raises both arms up in an X formation as lightning strikes the posts before they burst into flames. Adams: And we got one heck of an opening contest here tonight in a Champion vs. Champion Match. Ferrera: And not only that, both these men are members of Team GWC, which is due to face Team MWE somewhere in the distant future. Adams: That’s a very good point. So you have to wonder how that will affect these two men as they compete against each other tonight. A quiet, soft beginning of a rock tune fills the PA system as the crowd blows the roof of with cheers. They all jump up out of their seats, brandishing their signs as they eagerly await the arrival of the Ultimate Thrll Seeker. Suddenly... FEEL THE PRESSURE LET IT GO!!! FEEL THE PRESSURE LET IT GO!!! The song breaks out and explodes in a hard rock sound as the strobe lights begin to flash wildly around the arena. Zack rushes out from behind the curtain to a loud ovation from the crowd, the X-TV belt strapped firmly around his waist. He bounces around the stage for a moment, going from side to side, saluting to the crowd before making his way down the ramp. YOU TRY TO BUY SOME TIME CAN'T READ THE SIGNS OR REAONS WHY EVERY SINGLE TIME I COULD DRIVE IN BLIND CAN'T MAKE UP MY MIND I KNOW I'M GONNA FIND MY WAY OUT Fred Carry: And his opponent, from Charleston, West Virginia. Weighing in at 215 pounds. He is the X-TV Champion…ZACK CRASH!!! Zack makes his way down the ring, continuing to gesture to the crowd as the strobe lights are still flashing. Nearing the end of the ramp, Zack makes it a point to slap the hands of several outstretched hands. Looking at the crowd nearest the ring, he looks for the nearest person, takes off his trademark shirt and hands it to them. Then, he rushes the ring and slides underneath the bottom ring rope. CRASH YOU'RE GOING DOWN WITHOUT A SOUND I'LL PASS YOU UP BEFORE I EXPLODE BREAKING UNTIL I SEE IT THROUGH YOU WON'T KEEP ME DOWN WHEN I CRASH THROUGH YOU Getting to his feet, Zack rushes toward the nearest turnbuckle and leaps onto the highest rope, gesturing to the crowd. As several cameras flash blindingly, he back flips off the rope and lands safely on both feet in the center of the ring. Zack then goes to the side ropes and bounces up and down on them as he continues to work the crowd. HEAR ME OUT I'M NEVER BACKING DOWN HEAR ME OUT I KNOW YOU'RE GOING DOWN With this blood pumping, Zack goes for the center of the ring where he takes off hi belt and raises it high for all to see. He then hands the belt to the ref before he does some quick stretches before croucthing down, awaiting his match to begin. Adams: Crash has been put in a tough spot recently. Just barely off the victory over his rival Oblivion, he’s been forced to compete on Team GWC to fight for a fed he’s never been in, all on Eli’s orders. Ferrera: If I were anyone on Team GWC, I’d keep my eye on that one. There’s no telling if he might turn his back on them or not. Adams: I don’t think Crash is that kinda guy. The ref goes to take the two belts from the respective champions, hands them to a ring aid, and calls for the match to start. The two men slowly walk toward center ring, where the stare long and hard at each other. The crowd just eats up the building tension between these two as they both think about how they are supposed to be “teammates” despite that neither one can stand the other. But for now, there are no teammates. Only opponents. Virus pulls back for a hard right fist to Crash, but Crash is able to block it. He pulls back, ready to counter with a fist of his own, but Virus kicks Crash so hard in the gut his feet actually leave the mat. Crash falls to his knees, holding his gut in pain. Virus runs for the ropes to bounce off them and runs back toward Crash to make him eat a giant boot to the face. Crash falls backward, one hand still holding his gut, but the other now holding his face. Virus paces over Crash, stomping on his legs several times. Virus then pulls Crash up into a sitting position by the hair, only to deliver a hard kick that echoes through the arena, sending Crash back down to the mat. Adams: Virus starting this one off rather viciously. Ferrera: And he seems to be targeting Crash’s legs. That’s good strategy on the part of the US Champ. Take away the high-flyers ultimate ability to walk or jump and you got this one finished. Virus continues to work the legs of Crash, getting one leg now into a sort of modified Ankle Lock. Crash screams loudly as Virus puts some serious looking torque on Crash’s ankle. But Crash is able to roll on his back and use his free leg to kick Virus in the head. It isn’t a powerful kick, but it’s strong enough to force Virus to let go. He staggers back as Crash gets back to his feet, the one leg limping slightly. Crash charges for Virus, but Virus turns and elbows Crash right in the kisser. Crash stumbles back into a corner as a small trail of blood pours from his lower lip. Virus charges for Crash in the corner and hits a hard Clothesline. Crash seems dazed, but stays on two feet. Virus doubles back to deliver another Clothesline in the turnbuckle. But this time Crash recovers, lifting Virus up so that he face gets to meet Mr. Turnbuckle. Virus stumbles to center ring, holding his face. But Crash rushes for him, grabbing Virus’s head for a One-Handed Bulldog. Crash gets a sly look on his face as he watches Virus crawl back up. You know, the kind of smile that lets you know he’s thinking of something crazy. Once Virus is on both hands and knees, Crash rushes to bounce off the rope, runs back for Virus, uses his back as a step to hoist himself onto the top rope, and leaps backward off the rope to hit what looks to be a Moonsault Leg Drop combo. The crowd begins to chant “HOLY SH*T, HOLY SH*T” as Crash gets back to his feet, exhausted from that last move. Virus remains face down a moment before he two gets back up. Adams: Did you see that insane move? That crazy innovation of Crash’s is one of the reasons he’s one of our top rookies at the moment and why he’s been on an undefeated streak. Ferrera: It’ll also probably be the reason he gets himself and someone else killed in the ring one of these days. The two opponents take a brief moment to recover as the crowd cheers them on. Then, they charge each other, locking up in a grappling contest at center ring. But with Virus’s height and weight advantage, he easily overpowers Crash and gets him into a Side Headlock. Crash forces him off however and pushes him to the ropes. Crash waits for Virus on the return, but Virus takes him down with a hard Shoulder Block. Virus raises his elbow to drop in on Crash, but Crash rolls out of the way and Virus smashes his elbow into the mat. As Virus holds his elbow in pain, Crash leaps on the back of Virus and wrenches back the injured elbow in an Armbar. Crash pulls the arm back so far it looks like he might tear Virus’s arm right off his shoulder. But Virus grits his teeth and pushes through the pain. He forces himself to a standing position, and Crash wraps his legs around Virus’s waist, keeping a firm hold on the arm submission. With his free arm, Virus elbows Crash in the legs a few times, but Crash doesn’t let go. Virus then runs backward, colliding into a turnbuckle, smashing Crash between himself and the corner. Crash finally lets go and Virus goes for the center ring to catch his breath and nurse his arm. Once fully healed, he notices Crash still groggy in the corner. He charges at Crash, who lifts up a foot to smash it against Virus’s face. Virus stumbles back to center ring as Crash climbs the top turnbuckle. As Virus turns, Crash launches himself for a beautiful CrossBody. And Virus counters with a beautiful boot to Crash’s face. Well it wasn’t beautiful to Crash, and neither was Virus going for the first cover of the match. “1…” “2…” Crash kicks out. Ferrera: Crash just barely kicked out of that one. He’d better be careful with those high-flying moves. One more mistake like that and he’s streak will be over. Adams: Maybe, but I think Crash is more resilient than that. Virus picks Crash up and gets him into a Headlock position. He then raises Zack up off his feet and goes for a Suplex. But instead, Crash lands on his feet behind Virus, and with his arms now wrapped around Virus’s neck, hits a Neckbreaker. Both men lay on the ground motionless for a moment from exhaustion. The ref comes over to examine the two men before starting the count. “ONE… “TWO…” “THREE…” “FOUR…” “FIVE…” Both Virus and Crash get to their feet, and the ref signals that the match will continue normally. Crash goes and Irish Whips Virus to the ropes, but Virus counters and turns it into an Irish Whip of his own. As Crash comes back, Virus lifts him up for a Spinebuster. But he lifts Crash too high, and Crash is able to reach down with an arm, grab hold of Virus’s lower thighs, and gets him into a Roll Up. “1…” “2…” Virus kicks out. Crash looks distraught that he didn’t get the pin, but he gets back up to his feet and begins hitting Virus with a number of Chest Slaps as the crowd cheers for each one. Crash then runs for the ropes, then uses them to springboard off them to hit Virus with a Hurricanrana. Put Virus counters, turning it into a hard Powerbomb. Virus goes for a cover. “1…” “2…” “3!!!” No, wait. Crash was able to get his foot up on the bottom rope. Adams: That’s some veteran-like thinking on the part of Crash. Ferrera: I call it luck. Virus gets up and begins arguing with the ref, insisting that had the 3 count. But the ref refuses to go back on his decision. Suddenly, Virus is pull into a Schoolboy Roll Up by Crash. “1…” “2…” “3!!!” NO! Virus kicks out at the last possible second. Both men quickly scramble to their feet and get into a defensive position, each waiting for the other to make the move. As they stare each other down, the crowd around them cheers on the awesome match these two men are having. Finally, the two lock up in a grappling contest that takes them around the edges of the ring. Virus is able to force Crash into a corner and proceeds to hit him with some nasty elbows to the jaw. Virus sets up Crash on the top of the corner, the quickly climbs up after him. He locks Crash’s head in and tries to go for a Superplex. But Crash holds onto the ropes tightly, and they both stay stationary. Crash begins punching Virus in the ribs until Virus lets go and stumbles away to the center of the ring. Virus is dazed, so Crash takes advantage of the confusion to hit another Diving Crossbody. But Virus is too quick to recover, and delivers a hard punch to Crash’s heart as he comes down, a move which Virus calls Diablo’s Claw. Virus pulls Crash away from the edge so he can’t get his leg on the rope. But as Virus goes for a pin, Crash Snapmares Virus, then stands up to hit a Dropkick to the back of his head. Adams: Holy crap on a crap cracker! This is one of the fastest paced matches I’ve seen in a long time. Ferrera: And I’m not even going to comment on that one piece of garbage that just came out of your mouth. With Virus laying face down, Crash gets an evil grin on his face. Placing his own back against Virus’s with his legs facing his head, he hooks Virus’s legs in a Boston Crab like hold, and wraps his legs around Virus’s neck. That’s right! It’s the new Crash n’ Tap submission signature, and he’s got it locked in nicely. Virus screams out in pain, reaching out for salvation, but there is none. They’re in center ring. But Virus doesn’t tap. Using all the will and strength he can muster, he uses his hands to lift himself up and slowly crawls toward the ropes. Inch by inch they crawl, and Virus holds out his hands, knowing soon he won’t be able to take the pain anymore. Adams: It looks like Virus is about to tap out. Ferrera: Come on Virus! Just a bit further! You can do it! Virus is just within centimeters now. His fingertips are just a breath away. And then… He gets a hold of the ropes. There is a mixture of boos and cheers as Crash takes use of the refs count before finally letting go. Both men are exhausted beyond their limits, and they each take a moment to rest while the crowd chants “THIS IS AWESOME” in the background. Adams: I got to agree with the crowd. This is one of the best opening matches we’ve had on MNM in a good while. Ferrera: As much as I hate to agree with you, or these fans for that matter, I have to admit this is one hell of a match we’re seeing. Adams: No doubt. And I have a feeling it won’t be going on much longer either. Virus gets to his feet, using the ropes to help pull himself up. Crash does the same, slowly getting on his hands and knees, gradually using his knees to hoist himself back to his feet. The two of them stumble toward each other, each a bit wobbly on their feet. Crash winds up and delivers a huge Chest Slap. Virus takes it in stride, then winds up to hit a Chest Slap of his own. The two go back and forth, taking turns trading blows as the crowd cheers or boos every strike. Crash strikes…cheers. Virus strikes…boos. It continues like this for sometime until Virus is able to get the advantage. He begins striking Crash with quicker and more frequent slaps. Crash tries to hit another strike, but Virus either blocks it or is too quick with a slap of his own. Virus finally manages to force Crash into a corner and proceeds to pummel him with closed fists. The ref eventually forces himself between the two and admonishes Virus for the dirty tactic. As the ref checks on Crash, Virus steps back, and as soon as the ref is clear, charges for Crash, lifting his foot for a Corner Big Boot. But Crash ducks out of the way, and Virus’s foot gets caught up in the ropes. Now Crash sees his chance. He goes for the opposite corner and takes aim as Virus frantically struggles to get free. It seems he also knows what’s coming. Adams: Looks like the end is coming for Virus. Ferrera: Come on Virus! Move! Crash runs at full speed, leaps with both knees in the air, to hit his finisher Crash N’ Burn. But Virus is able to get out of the way with no time to spare. And Crash collides knee first into the corner. Crash is in some serious pain now. As he cuddles his legs, Virus pushes him up onto the turnbuckle facing the audience. Virus climbs up after him, placing Crash carefully on top of his shoulders, looking to deliver a Super Electric Chair. The crowd stands on their feet, eyes wide open to watch this daring move. Virus smiles evilly as he, almost Matrix-like, starts to fall backwards. BUT NO! Somehow, Crash manages to get off his shoulders, and Virus instead falls flat on the mat. But before he can react, Crash falls at just the right angle for his legs to spring off the top turnbuckle ropes as he twist his body for a Flash N’ Crash, which hits its mark. Crash covers and the ref begins to count. “1…” “2…” “3!!!” The crowd erupts with cheers, both in victory for their superstar, and amazement at the finishing move. Crash gets up, a look of shock and awe on his face, as if he didn’t expect that last maneuver to work. Fred Carry: Here is your winner…the X-TV Champion…ZACK CRASH!!! The ref comes to hand Crash his championship belt as Crash goes from turnbuckle to turnbuckle, lifting his fist in victory as several camera lights flash in the arena. Adams: And Crash’s streak continues with one of the most impressive finishes to a match I think we’ve ever seen here on MNM. Ferrera: Alright, I admit it. That was pretty cool just now. Adams: No doubt about that partner. And after yet another victory to his name, you have to wonder if anyone can stop this guy? Crash exits the ring and begins walking up the side of the ring, slapping the hands of many of his admiring fans. As Crash reaches the stage, he turns back around one final time, lifting the X-TV belt high for one more pop from the crowd. Satisfied now, Crash finally throws the belt over his shoulder and exits behind the curtain as the camera fades away to black. We go backstage and find the ever-loved Freddy Klyde standing in front of an MWE background drop. Believe it or not, he actually looks ready—as in professionally ready, to conduct this upcoming interview. Klyde: Hello fans, Freddy Klyde here once again! At this time, I am joined by one of the newer members of MWE. She had a very successful run in the short-lived second stint of ICW. She is the youngest member of the roster and one heck of a basketball player. She is....Madison Seton. (The camera pans back a little and we see Madison standing next to Freddy. As it is only a view of her shoulders and up, all we can tell is she's wearing a black shirt and we see her hair down. She has a perky smile, but you can see in her eyes that she is pretty nervous.) Klyde: Madison, how are you this evening? Madison: Good. (There's an awkward silence.) Klyde: That's it? j-just “good?” (Madison purses her lips together as her eyes brighten, giving a very spunky series of quick nods.) Klyde: Well, on behalf of the staff—welcome back to the main roster. Do you feel your time in ICW helped? Madison: Like...I think so. I mean, there's so much to learn and stuff. Aaaand—its just wrestling has so many things and people facing, uh...people I'm facing off and— (She shakes her head. This type of thing she isn't used to. She gets a semi-disappointed look.) Madison: We've got to start this thing over again. I've totally messed it up. (Klyde looks in surprise. His words aptly describe his expression.) Klyde: But......we're live. (Madison gets a look of shock as she jerks her head back and widens her eyes a bit.) Madison: I did not know that. Well, you see, it's a thing of I needed some more time to, like, work on my stuff. We all know I'm a rookie, not just in MWE but overall. Klyde: Do you feel overwhelmed? How about your first week here, when you teamed with Eric E-E-....Eric Derf? Madison: You know—it's weird. My sister warned me about steeper competition but—like, I got in the ring that evening and just didn't think. I just “did.” And it worked. I know have a reputation to build up here and I, you know, think I'm doing that well. Klyde: I can't help but notice you seem nerve-wracked right now. I thought I was supposed to be the only one. (Madison has a chuckle. It's been obvious not just by her words. Her small expression of being nervous never left—even if not very visible to the naked eye.) Madison: I just, you know, have never done, like, a thing in this way. Klyde: Do you think, being a female, you have a harder road ahead of you he-here? Madison: I'm not sure. My sister went on and on about the potential for hitting a place with a so-called 'glass ceiling.' I thought nothing, you know, of it since I did well in the small federations. All I know is that I have to work hard. Working is how you get things done. If you want to get noticed, you need to work. If you want to move up in the world, you need to work. If people around here feel I have to prove myself, you know, more than what the guys would have to—then I just keep working. I'm in it to win it and if that, like, means more hard work, and stuff—so be it. (Madison's voice begins to show confidence and courage as it heightens in power and her mind becomes more clear. Her nerves are beginning to loosen and she starts to feel alot more freedom with herself.) Madison: Anybody can talk all that bad stuff about me as much as they wish, but I will never stop believing that I can be the best without filth coming from my voice. This voice is used for motivation, for respect, and I am not letting anybody downgrade me without them facing me in that wrestling ring proving whether or not I can be defeated. Klyde: What a powerful message, did you have anything else to add before we wr-wrap this up? Madison: Yes, I am going for the gold, and I am going to do what ever I can to become a top contender. Whether I have had a bad day or a good one come my match, things will always look my way! Now if you don't mind, I kinda go to go, I want to get more training in readying myself for the big move back to MNM. (Klyde nods his head as Madison leaves the scene with her chin held high and a strong heart. With that Klyde leaves as well in a jittery strut off camera and we cut to the next match.) Oblivion Vs GM The lights dim as soon as The Night begins. At 0:20 a single spotlight at the stage shows Oblivion standing in a shroud of fog. Immediately, his girlfriend Heather, does a seductive crawl underneath his legs. Oblivion then places his foot on her back, and outstretches his arms, as two giant pillars of fire erupt from both sides of the stage. Heather gets up, and they walk to the ring. Adams: I think it's sick how he treats that woman, but it's even sicker that she's OK with it. Ferrera: She's a woman, what do you expect her to react like? Once Oblivion gets into the ring, he doesn't pose or anything, he simply turns around and awaits his biggest hurdle to date. Cover Up by Trapt plays to the arena. After a few seconds the bright house lights go off but the music continues to the arena. The house spotlights come up and are focussed on all four corners of the ring. They change colours rapidly. Then the music stops. Then both sides of the entrance ramp, there are five or six short bursts of steam, followed by small firework going off by the Titantron screen. The house lights returns and the music starts back up again. GM is in ring, sitting on a turnbuckle. He is facing in the ring with his arms outstretched and back towards the crowd. His eyes are glued onto Oblivion, who returns an equally disturbed look of anger. Adams: I don't like the look in the former World Champions eyes right now partner. He looks like he could kill. Fererra: For once we agree on something, except unlike you, I WANT to see him kill someone out here, he deserves it after being screwed out of his title the other night at Turmoil! GM hops off the turnbuckle with his eyes still glued to Oblivion as the ref calls for the bell. And as soon as it rings, both men go at each other, little hesitation, handing out lefts and rights, as if they'd been worst enemies for the longest time. Eventually, one left hand turns from GM turns into two, then three, then out of nowhere, he hits a dropkick, knocking the masked madman to his back. But instead of giving him a moment to recover, or maybe to taunt, he's right back on the attack, clenching in a headlock on Oblivion. He keeps the hold locked in for a few moments, but eventually realizes he's still got too much in him at this point in time, so he lets him go, but only for a moment before kicking him as he tries to get back to his feet. Ferrera: Grand Mystique is on fire right now! Adams: Maybe losing the World Title lit that fire under him he used to have? Ferrera: Used to? Grand Mystique has always been a threat to anyone that steps into the ring with him. As Oblivion gets back to his feet, GM waits a moment, stalking his prey, and at the perfect moment, hits a devastating neckbreaker, knocking his opponent right back onto his back. GM quickly gets back to his feet and lets out a loud vicious scream of anger and hatred. Oblivion is clearly getting beaten, so he drags himself towards the turnbuckle to try and get leverage, but this proves to be yet another mistake as GM takes advantage of the situation. As soon as Oblivion gets to his feet, he is met with a splash in the turnbuckle by GM, knocking Oblivion right back down, once again on his back, but this time near the corner. GM glances at his fallen foe for a moment, then turns back towards the turnbuckle and begins his climb. Adams: He's going up high! He's going to do a high risk- Ferrera: He's going to do his suicide dive thingy, we know. You get way to over excited sometimes partner. GM stands atop the corner turnbuckle for a moment. Only a moment though, as he leaps into the air to hit his Suicide Cannonball Dive, but in an act of desperation, Oblivion uses his instincts and puts up his knees, causing GM to land the wrong way, the hard way. Despite this being his first offence the whole match, Oblivion goes for the pinfall. 1... 2-Kickout. Adams: That was a nice reversal by Oblivion, and he needed it too. Ferrera: Why do you always seem to pick sides? You always root for the good guys. Adams: Do you see ANY good guys out here right now? Ferrera: Besides yours truly? As GM curls on the ground, Oblivion slowly makes his way to his feet, and looks down at GM. GM is slowly starting to get to his feet, so Oblivion runs towards the ropes, bounces back, and hits a Rolling Thunder on his half risen opponent, knocking him back to his stomach as Oblivion stands and glares daggers into his opponents back. He goes towards GMs head, thinking he could end it here, and locks in the Dragon Sleeper as GM screams out in pain. Oblivion has no intentions of letting the hold go, and GM is too far from the ropes to get granted a release by the ref, so he tries throwing his loose arm at Oblivion, but he's got the hold clenched in way to tight, and even a few weak punches won't do the trick. Eventually the hold begins to take its toll on GM as he looks to get weaker and weaker, and eventually lets his arm down, giving up on the strikes. The referee grabs his arm, lifts it, and lets go... It drops. Ferrera: Aww, come on! This match was going so good, don't end it on such a bullshit call! Adams: What are you talking about? Making your opponent pass out in a match is just as great as a pin or submission. Jealous you can't make someone pass out? Ferrera: Tempted me... The referee shouts "ONE" as the crowd chants the same thing, same time. He goes to lift his arm for a second time. It drops. The referee shouts "TWO" as the crowd, much louder chants alone with him. With the final moments of the match potentially a moment away, the referee lifts GMs arm a third time... ... ... ... But before the ref can drop GMs arm a third time, Oblivion lets go of the hold, causing the former World Champion to drop down like a sack of potatoes. Oblivion gets back to his feet, staring at the fallen foe as the referee shouts a few things out at the masked madman, but he's not listening much. Instead, he exits the ring and approaches his girlfriend Heather on the outside. He stares at her for a moment, grabs her by the back of her hair, pulls her back, and plants a large kiss right on her lips, despite the fact that he's got a mask on? Adams: I don't know what's sicker. The relationship those two have, or the fact that he could have just won the match, but chose to inflict more pain? Ferrera: I'm hoping for the pain. But that's the the only thing he grabs. Tucked into the back of her pants was a long, black whip. He takes it from her, then stares back into the ring for a moment. His intentions are clear. Instead of taking the quick way under the ropes, Oblivion walks around the ring towards the closest ring steps, and climbs them. He's taking his time, he wants GM to be fully conscience for the pain he's about to inflict on him. Finally in the ring, Oblivion lets the longer half of the whip hang on the ground as he holds the handle tightly in his hands, waiting for GM to get up. As GM finally begins to stir, Oblivion begins to get impatient. The referee is yelling at Oblivion to drop the whip, threatening a Disqualification if he doesn't, but right now it seems as if he's in his own world. Finally, GM manages to pull himself to his feet using the ring ropes for leverage. His back towards the ring and his face looking towards the ramp, it seems GM has no idea what's in store as Oblivion raises the whip, and with a vicious "crack!" snaps it in the direction on GM. But somehow, GM quickly side steps the whip, causing it to wrap around the top ring rope. GM turns back towards Oblivion, who's still holding the whip handle in his hand, and bounces off the ropes, flying back and hitting Oblivion with a cross body, causing him to let go of the whip as his body is brought down hard. Adams: Nice call! How could he have known what was coming?! Ferrera: Well, he WAS facing the titantron, so yeah... He goes for a pinfall... 1... 2... Kickout. GM gets back to his feet, now in control again, but Oblivion isn't one to wait, he just as quickly gets up, but ends up getting hit with an armdrag, knocking him back down again. He gets up again, and once again, is knocked right back down with another arm drag. Same situation a third time, but instead of an arm drag this time, GM gets creative and hits a short arm clothesline.
