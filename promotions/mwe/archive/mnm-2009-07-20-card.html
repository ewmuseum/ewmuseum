MWE Monday Night Mayhem card, 20 July 2009

20 July 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
LIVE FROM: Atlanta, Georgia - Phillips Arena [19,600] ALL RPS DUE BY FRIDAY, JULY 17th at Midnight CT! Jesus Cruz vs Blight Newcomer Jesus Cruz is one fiery guy, and he impressed MWE officials in his try-out match. He’s being rewarded with a match against Blight, one of MWE’s sickest and most demonic wrestlers. Cruz is an outspoken fellow, with a short patience for wrestling. He held a fantastic record in the MMA ranks, but the average fan has to question whether or not he can keep that in MWE. Meanwhile, Blight is searching for his second win. Can he show Cruz, forcefully, that wrestling is more intense than MMA-style fighting? Devlin Sharpe vs Anthony Clark vs CSB Also making his debut with MWE is CSB, who has experience in GWC, along with Kevin Hardaway, Zak Warner, and the Virus. He was the last ever GWC Champion, and the wrestlers above have had many-a-success in MWE. The question now remains, can CSB pull that off? In his first match in, he takes on Devlin Sharpe and Anthony Clark. Both men have big egos, with Clark being the new presumptive leader of Michaels Inc and Devlin fresh off a win over Eric Derf. Will one of those two pull off a victory on MNM? We’ll see. Scotty McKnight vs Peter Kaymakcian At Independence Day, these two tagged up as a team, with the common purpose of winning their ladder match, so they could go on to this MNM and have a chance to be the #1 Contender for the X-Television Championship. This Monday, their shot is coming, because the winner of this match is going to Crowning Glory to face Jay Riddick for the X-Television Championship. McKnight is a changed man after dropping Jekyl, while Peter K is trying to make Michaels Inc. proud. Who will win out? Zack Crash vs Chris England vs Vega Knight Chris England and Zak Crash were at one point partners. That point was last Sunday at Independence Day. In a last second decision, Zak Crash threw the match and leaped all the way from the top of 12 ladder crashing through Scotty McKnight and England. Now, England and Crash are against each other in a triple threat match. However, the screwball in the whole situation is the third fighter. Vega Knight, after not competing for almost two months, is going to be in the center of all this. It is like adding fuel to the fire, and this triple threat is sure to be explosive. Jay Riddick vs Joseph Sharpman Jay Riddick was not pinned at Independence Day. However, he did not pin anyone himself. Yet, here he is, right now in the present day, as the current MWE X-TV Champion. He is without a doubt one of the hottest newcomers in MWE, holding a 4-0-1 record and the X-TV title. But make no mistake, even with the gold around his waist and a wave of momentum, he has his biggest challenge yet this Monday Night. Joesph Sharpman made his return at Independence Day, revealing himself as the culprit to D.C's misfortunes. Now in D.C's path, Sharpman will wrestle under the watch of the former Unisputed Champion of the world, but he vows that it will make no difference. Will Sharpman be able to gain momentum on the road to Crowning Glory, or will Jay Riddick brush off Sharpman to continue his dominance? Only Monday will tell! Brian Michaels vs Alejandro Rodriguez Alejandro Rodriguez returned at Independence Day, calling out David Whitman and his friends. His comeback is expected to shake-up MWE for at least the next two months, and the future hall-of-famer will take on another future hall-of-famer in Brian Michaels, who is also enjoying his last few matches. Michaels, who won via disqualification at Independence Day, was betrayed by Nic Kraven. Kraven then stated that Brian Michaels would have to go through an obstacle course to get to him at Crowning Glory. This is the first match of the obstacle course: Will he succeed? Grand Mystique and Kevin Hardaway vs Sinc Mercier and Mason Resoan In an epic clash of MWE’s Main Eventers, the MWE Champion in Sinc Mercier and US Champion in Mason Resoan tag up to take on Grand Mystique, the presumptive #1 contender for the MWE Championship and Kevin Hardaway, who shocked the wrestling world on Sunday night by turning on his friend in Zak Warner. Warner is no doubt expected to show up on MNM, but will he have anything to do with this match? Can Sinc get an early W over Grand Mystique? Will Mason surprise everyone and be the best in his first Main Event in months?
