MWE Crowning Glory II, 10 August 2008

10 August 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
MWE PRESENTS: MWE CROWNING GLORY II *Fireworks begin as ''Black Suits Coming'' hits the arena with a bang. The MWE camera shows the United Center audience launch itself into a frenzy.* Ryan Adams: Can you feel it, ladies and gentlemen? MWE is BACK! David Ferrera: I've been feeling it for days, the time has come! Ryan Adams: And now to kick off Crowning Glory with a bang...we'd like to present to you, the MWE Hall of Fame Ceremony. *An MWE camera pans in and focuses on a stage with a large MWE backdrop. The camera then pans out and shows a large audience of about a thousand all standing up in suits and dresses clapping. The camera zooms in and shows several memorable faces such as Maxxamillion Mustafa, Mason Resoan, Antonio DeMarco, Arch Angel, Windy Bridges, Ace Cannon, Paige, Joey Dallas, Greg Cherry and many other notable MWE alumni. Also sitting in the front row were the new MWE wrestlers, all finely dressed and clapping as well. An elegant piano sound begins playing in the background as Richard Covell comes out, holding a large square plate in his hand. Richard is sharply dressed as the audience continues to give a standing ovation, before he leans into the microphone and begins to speak.* Covell: Ladies...and gentlemen. Welcome to the 2008 MWE Hall of Fame ceremony. *The crowd applauds as Covell bows his head down in acknowledgement.* Covell: Let's get down to inducting. Introducing first, former MWE World Heavyweight Champion...Brian Michaels! *The crowd gives a mixed reaction (rofl) as a grim Brian Michaels comes out trying to smile. He walks to the podium and shakes hands with Richard Covell. Michaels' eyes are red as he looks down the plaque for Tim Fitzgerald. He admires the plaque and holds it in his hands as the clapping dies down.* Michaels: Alejandro Rodriguez. Sonny Williams. Aiden Copane. Arch Angel. Windy Bridges. *Michaels looks out at the crowd, showing off a disappointed expression.* Michaels: Katherine Tenaud. Ace Cannon. Paige. Frank Soprano. Joey Dallas. Bishop. Mason Resoan. Jason Sincade. *Brian bites his lip and shakes his head.* Michaels: Devlin Sharpe. James Young. Everest. Genesius Annaya. Sinc Mercier. David Whitman. D...C. *Michaels pauses as scattered applause is heard for D.C.'s name.* Michaels: All of them...great in their own way. In some way shape or form, these ladies and gentlemen helped put MWE on the map...but NONE of them...can EVER...say...that they made MWE what it is today. *Michaels begins to tear up and chokes back on his words.* Michaels: Tim...Fitzgerald was a great man. He made MWE what it is today. *Michaels gives a slight smile.* Michaels:Tim taught me everything I know. Tim, in some way shape or form...influenced...the MWE you see today. He didn't have to wrestle. He was a leader. He didn't ask for your respect, you gave it to him. *Michaels looks down at the plaque and begins feeling it, rubbing around the parts.* Michaels:All these alumni, winning Titles...it all goes back to Tim. The merger with AEW was because of Tim. *Michaels chokes and holds back his tears again.* Michaels:Tim is dead. But, it's not a matter of how... just that it happened...it's enough to talk about. *Brian bows his head and takes a deep breath. Michaels points at the large screen in front of the curtain.* Michaels:He made us believe in the pursuit of a championship. He let us ALL believe that anything was possible, no matter the circumstances and for that...the MWE alumni honor him. *The large projector starts with static before showing Devlin Sharpe in a suit and his hair combed back sitting on a chair with a curtain behind him.* Devlin: Tim was an inspiration to me, always willing to help the new guys. Even the ones who didn't like his style at first, he didnt mind the flack he got, he helped all. Tim was taken from us untimely, he deserved to live a full life, he was liked by many and tried his hardest to make himself liked by all. *The projector switches to static before showing Aiden Copane, in a suit as well, with red eyes, sniffling.* Aiden: I’m usually the bad guy in MWE, I’m usually the one you people love to hate. But, that’s just not me, at least not now. Tim Fitzgerald was the father of MWE, a hard worker, and a great personal friend of mine, and he will be missed dearly. The legacy continues without his presence, but not without his soul, his heart, and last but not least, his ability, to keep MWE alive. Rest In Peace, Tim, we all love and miss you. *The projector switches and shows Alejandro Rodriguez in a black dress shirt, with his head in his hands. Rest In Peace Tim, love ya man. *The projector switches once more to a scene showing Sonny Williams leaning back against a wall in the MWE Headquarters. Tim came to me one day and offered me what I had be wanting my whole life, a wrestling contract. The man believed everyone could be a World Champion. While maintaining that aura, Tim ran this company for months on end. Not because he couldn't do it anymore, but we reminded him of his old federations. In turn, as much as he inspired us, we inspired him. We shared a common bond. Hope. Hope, that we could maintain a haven, for everyone, to come in and have a good time, and maybe talk a little shit in the process. We lived by Tim's teachings. When MWE was started, it started on my birthday. When MWE closed down, was the day Tim passed away. For me, on a personal level, seemed like fate played against us. It was a crushing defeat. Being the very last owner of MWE, and having that responsibility on my shoulder, only to watch it fall between my fingers, was heart breaking. It was like turning the lights off in a room you have no business being in. To find out, that same day, that MWE was quite literally, taken off life support, finding out that Tim passed away as well. I don't know if people could understand the underlying feeling that could have on someone. While its true, we are all characters, playing in a game... Tim, was probably the one real person amongst us. *D.C. takes a moment.* D.C.: So this is for you Tim. If not for you, there would be no D.C.. There would be no Brian Michaels. I only wish I could say the same to you friend. *D.C. takes a breath for a second and cracks a smile.* D.C.: And just so you know... Tim... Rest in peace man. *The camera flashes to static as the audience stands up and applauds.
