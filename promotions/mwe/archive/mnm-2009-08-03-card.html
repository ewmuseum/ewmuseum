MWE Monday Night Mayhem card, 3 August 2009

3 August 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
LIVE FROM: Knoxville, Tennessee - Thompson-Boiling Arena [24,535] ALL RPS DUE BY FRIDAY, JULY 31st at Midnight CT! Jai Williams Vs Oblivion As a fresh opener we are pitting two fresh superstars against eachother in an awesome way to start off the show. The recently hyped oblivion, against, get this, SONNY'S brother Jai Williams! This match will be one of both arrogance and spite. Devlin Sharpe And Blight Vs Peter Kaymackian And Anthony Clark Devlin got butchered on the last installment of MNM, but so did Peter, Anthony, AND Blight, so I guess you could say, this is a match of redemption. After all four of these guys lost, they get a chance to duke it out and vent their frustrations in a tag match against eachother, who can come out on top? Jay Riddick Vs Zak Warner Vs Jesuz Cruz A man who has recently lost to Sharpman against a man who just got his revenge on Hardaway as well as a man with unbelievable confidence in his abilities. It seems this an event of vengeful skitzo vs aspiring rookie vs confident newbie. Will Zak still blow off some steam over Kevin, will Cruz continue winning, or can Riddick be the victor and shake off his loss against Joseph Sharpman? CSB And Zack Crash Vs Scotty Mcknight And The Virus Crash and CSB team up for the FIRST time in MWE , against two other people with strong bonds as well as big dreams, The Virus and Scotty Mcknight. After just becoming a number one contender Mcknight is on fire and on cloud nine, though Virus has been slacking as of late, he still got to let out his rage by attacking his former colleague Supernova and is more ready than ever to make a statement. Kevin Hardaway Vs Mason Resoan Another big match here, Kevin Hardaway and his recent transition into a careless snob has shown MWE that this new coat of colors isn't necessarily a bad thing. His face days are over and he's looking to prove a point, but at the same time, Mason has just become US champion and has been out to define his reign as a victorious superstar, but the question is, can being a US champ be enough to beat the new found evil in Kevin? D.C. Vs Sinc Mercier - No DQ Match After running in the ring and aiding Sharpman in victory Eli took this knowledge and made his next match a No DQ. Little did Silver know Sinc would be the one knocking on his door wanting a piece of the former Rated-X member. D.C. sent a clear message to the roster and he has "the safety off" so to speak, but Sinc replied to that message in valiance and told the people that HE is their voice, and he wants to make sure D.C. knows that. It is loud mouth vs loud mouth, a true PPV worthy match, but only one of these arrogant men can win, find out on MNM!
