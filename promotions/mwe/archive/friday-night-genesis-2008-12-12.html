12 December 2008

The camera comes in on the entrance ramp as a beautiful scene of pyrotechnics erupts from the stage and ramp, all in blues as the crowd sings along with the official theme of MWE Genesis. We begins looking around the crowd seeing the many types of fans of all races, genders and ages. Each and everyone of them going wild for this MWE presentation. Stasiak: Welcome ladies and gentlemen to Friday Night Genesis! We are coming to you live from Buffalo, New York from the HSBC Arena! And have we got a show for you tonight! Jacobs: Your damn right we have an amazing night of wrestling planned ahead for the evening. We have Sonny Williams challenging Vega Knight for Intercontinental gold, as well as debuts from Shane Perry, LaLa and Derek Wellings. All leading up to our main event where the World Champion D.C will be taking on the debuting Virus in non-title action. Stasiak: Not to mention an amazing tag team contest when the World Tag Team Champions Maxamillion Mustafa and Grand Mystique take on the two men that are set to meet at Adrenaline Rush, and we are about to see the contract signing for that match.So with that we turn things over to Dick Malibu. Our focus turns away from the fans and turns to a much more serious side of things as Dick Malibu, Genesis General Manager, becomes the center of attention. He is standing in the ring in a Gucci suit, the canvas is covered in red velvet and a large desk is set up in the middle of the ring, on each side stands a large security guard. Malibu has a mic in hand as the lights dim and a spot light shines down on the center of the ring, making Malibu and the desk the focus here. Malibu: As I am sure most of you are already aware this is the last edition of Friday Night Genesis that will ever air on MWE television. Now I as a business man can realize that ratings were not what they were projected to be and the profit we generated was not nearly what Richard Covell and his band of merry men wanted. I can accept that, but to take MY show off the air without even consulting me is down right bullls*** but that my friends is another story for another day. Because I am a business man, and being a business man that is exactly what comes first here tonight. We are going to get two men to sign this contract making their match at Adrenaline Rush official. These two men have been a pain in my ass for a while now, and last week they only made things worse for themselves when they decided to involve themselves in nearly every one of my high profile matches, but I shouldn't have to explain this, just roll the damn footage. A Genesis graphic flashes over the screen as we cut to show footage of the last Genesis. First is the ending of the triple threat match between Dallas, JJ and Melinko. Dallas has been thrown to the outside of the ring, Melinko is brawling away with JJ Executioner in the ring, JJ gets the upperhand nailing his finishing move in the center of the ring, the camera switches to show Dallas trying to get himself into the ring only for Cannon to run down to ring side and lay a massive boot into the side of his head, as the referee counts the pinfall for Melinko. The Genesis graphic flashes over the screen again, as it fades away this time we see the highlight from the American Animal vs Ace Cannon match. Cannon pulls himself to his feet using the ropes and impatiently waits for Animal to work his way onto all fours. As Animal slowly does this Cannon prepares himself to launch out of the corner at full speed but the sudden sound of Joey Dallas' countdown clock hits once. Causing Cannon to hesitate, however the sound dies away and Cannon runs at full speed towards Animal delivering a stiff kick to the head for High Society! But as he stands over Animal the countdown sounds off again, this time it doesn't fade away and "My Time" hits the PA system much to the chagrin of the crowd. Cannon turns his attention to the entrance ramp where Dallas is slowly making his way towards the ring, as he gets closer the two men begin to yell and point at each other. As Dallas reaches the mat he points to something behind Ace, he slowly turns around only to meet a huge lariat from Animal forcing both men to the mat, an arm from each draped over the other. The referee makes the three count. The Genesis graphic flashes on the screen for the third time and this time cuts to last Genesis' main event match, where D.C became the World Champion. D.C is laying motionless in the ring as Cannon positions himself for the punt, as he tries to launch himself he finds that he can't move his right foot and looks down to see, Joey Dallas arrogantly smiling up at him. Cannon reaches for his hair but gets spit on for his trouble. Cannon then tried coming through the cage door after him only for Dallas to slam it in on him, Cannon turns around into the Chemi-Kill and the pinfall on him. The Genesis graphic, for the final time flashes across the screen cutting back to the ring where Malibu is looking up at the titantron, an almost disgusted look on his face. Malibu: That is the kind of garbage I would have to put up with week after week, so in a way I'm glad that this show is coming to an end, but if it's the last thing I do, I will see these two idiots kill each other once they meet each other one on one for only the second time in their illustrious careers.
