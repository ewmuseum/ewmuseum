MWE Monday Night Mayhem card, 15 September 2009

15 September 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
LIVE FROM: St.Louis, Missouri - Scottrade Center - (21,000) ALL RPS DUE BY FRIDAY, September 11th(ironic?) at Midnight CT! Blight Vs Brad Kane-MOTP Qualifier Blight takes on a recent MWE rookie in the first MOTP qualifying match, the winner, just like the next three matches below, goes on to the semifinals, in this unique match of dark and sinister vs confident and bold we will see a clash of different energies and intense physical sport engage on MNM, so be ready for a great low card event. David Renshaw Vs Jesus Cruz-MOTP Qualifier In the second match of the night, two men who both competed in CG's MITB match are going head to head to see which one can come out on top in a smaller and more dignified match. The winner goes on to round two of this very awesome tournament. Tali Preston Vs Sebastian St. Claude-MOTP Qualifier Getting no word from Tali we chose her opponent, she faces the silly and suave Sebastian St. Claude in the third qualifier of the night. Will the seal loving saviour defeat the erotic bombshell, or can the bronze of this blonde destroy him giving him his second loss of his career? Zak Warner Vs CSB-MOTP Qualifier Former GWC buds are forced to compete with one another in the final qualifier for MOTP, we here at MWE really hate to pit GWC alumni against eachother but come on, it's good entertainment, and I am sure you will all love it. So watch as what could be a match of the century, takes place on MNM. Virus Vs Jai Williams- #1 Contender's US Title Virus gets another chance at the coveted US title but he must defeat Jai to obtain it. But with all the hype on Jai and all the experience on Virus, this match is near even, after their previous engagements these two must know eachother all too well by now. So watch and be amazed as this near even matchup goes on. Mason Resoan Vs Oblivion The crazy sideshow known as Oblivion who almost was able to grab the suitcase at CG in the opening match takes on the retaining US champ in a battle of proving one's self when the competition raises and the heat gets hotter. Can the lunatic handle the righteous champ, or will he fail like the rest? FIND OUT SOON! D.C. & Eric Derf & Zack Crash v.s Joseph Sharpman & GM & Kevin Hardaway-6 Man Tag D.C, Eric Derf, and Zack Crash all have something in common. They are risk takers. All three of them are willing and ready to make a dive, take a dare, and make a jump to try and win. On the other hand, Joesph Sharpman, Kevin Hardaway, and NEW MWE World Heavyweight Champion, Grand Mystique are known for quite the opposite. All three of these men are known for being powerful, vicious, and downright mean, as they pummel their opponents all over the arena. So what will happen when these two teams collide? Well, certainly a clash of styles...but more importantly, a main event that is sure to raise some eyebrows and get some followers.
