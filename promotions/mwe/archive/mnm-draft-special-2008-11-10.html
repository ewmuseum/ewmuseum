Sinc rolls inside and waits for Sonny to get up. Sonny gets up and gets inside, and Sinc meets him with a dropkick, sending him down to the mat. Sinc quickly grabs Williams’ legs and executes an STF. Ryan Adams: Nicely executed. Sinc grabs Williams’ arms and goes backwards… he lands on his back with Williams on the air, with Williams’ back on Mercier’s knees. David Ferrera: Bow and arrow hold! Mercier releases the hold and starts climbing the turnbuckle. But Williams gets up quickly and drops himself onto the ropes, causing Sinc to fall down to the outside! Ryan Adams: What a drop! Smart move by Williams! Both men are down as the referee starts to count out Mercier. Mercier gets up and rolls to the inside, only to get DDT’d by Williams. Sonny picks Sinc up and for a powerbomb but Mercier reverses and sends Williams back-first into the turnbuckle. Sinc rolls to the outside and grabs Williams’ legs, putting one on each side of the post, then puts Williams’ left leg under Williams’ right leg. Mercier then puts his own shoulder under Williams’ right leg and pushes himself up. David Ferrera: What the hell?? Williams is screaming in pain but refuses to tap out. The referee is counting Mercier out while checking if Williams taps out…. 6… Williams: NO! 7… Ryan Adams: Williams can’t take this much longer! 8… David Ferrera: Mercier is on his toes! I’d advise him to get in but it’s too late now! 9….. The ref checks again, but Sonny shakes his head. 10! The referee calls for the bell. Fred Carry: Ladies and Gentlemen, your winner by count out, SONNY WILLIAMS! Sonny’s theme song starts playing through the speakers. Sinc releases the hold, infuriated. Sonny uses all his strength to get to his feet as he starts yelling at the referee. The crowd is now in a frenzy over the finish. All of a sudden, we can hear Sonny’s song being interrupted by a familiar voice on the speakers. HOLD IT, HOLD IT, HOLD IT! Richard Covell walks out as the crowd’s mixed reaction grows even louder now. Covell: Good God. First at Wrestle-Wars, now on MNM? Is it THAT hard to get good referees? Listen up, buddy, a count out is hardly a way of settling a title match. Smith, you are hereby officially dismissed. Bellkeeper, ring that bell, this match is BACK ON! And Carry, reverse that decision! … not in that particular order, by the way! The crowd erupts in cheers as the referee rolls out of the ring looking sad. Fred Carry: Ladies and Gentlemen, this match is BACK ON! Covell turns back around, addressing Carry this time. Covell: Oh, and Carry? Use your brain next time. Don’t just take the referee’s word. Fred Carry: But s- Covell: HEY! THAT IS AN ORDER! Covell leaves through the curtains. Meanwhile, Sinc Mercier has re-entered the ring... David Ferrera: NEARING THE FINISH LINE! Sinc and Sonny go at it with punches, left, right, left, right- Ryan Adams: It's an all out battle to the finish! Sonny gets a hard kick to the gut in, and as Sinc gets back to his upright position Sonny flips him the bird. David Ferrera: ATTITUDE TIME! However, Sinc lunges at Sonny, and musters up all his strength to execute a Swingset! Ryan Adams: SWINGSET! SWINGSET! Sinc tosses his arm over Sonny as the ref counts. 1! 2! 3! Fred Carry: The winner of this match by knockout, and your NEW US CHAMPION; SIIIIIIIIIINC MERCIIIIIIIIIIIIER!! The crowd continues roaring with cheers as Covell stands there smiling wider and laughing a little. Covell: Ok ok calm down, the fourth pick for Mayhem is someone who certainly has been...KNOWN...TO.....FLY! WOOO! From just that the crowd goes crazy as Covell is about to announce his name. Covell: Come on now guys let's settle down....because it's really not much of a big deal even though he is a FORMER MWE CHAMPION.... The crowd continues going crazy, the noise not seizing. A-Rod's music hits and he comes out running and going back and forth at the start of the ramp pointing to the crowd and raising his hands in the air as they cheer for his draft. He is a one-time MWE World Champion, one-time MWE Tag Champion, he is a one-time WZCW Mayhem Champion. He is a one-time APWC Champion, he is a one-time EW Champion, he is the former EW Undisputed Champion of the World... Covell: MAXXAMILLION MUSTAFA!
