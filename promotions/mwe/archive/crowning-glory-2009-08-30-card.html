2 Year Anniversary Spectacular, Expect It All When This PPV Takes Off! If You Wanna Know Why This PPV Is So Major, Ask About It's History, We'll Be Glad To Explain! THIS IS THE POINT OF NO RETURN PEOPLE! BE WITH US, OR BE GONE! LIVE FROM: Chicago, Illinois - United Center - (22,200) Peter Kaymackian vs David Renshaw vs Anthony Clark vs CSB vs Jesus Cruz vs Oblivion vs Shane Phillips vs Eric Derf MWE goes BIG TIME with a MITB match! Nu-MWE's first ever title opportunity for low carders. Every single one of these competitors has what it takes, but who will step up, and who will fail? Find out which one of these talented athletes hit home when the 2 year mark hits at Crowning Glory! The winner can cash it in anywhere at any time, as long as they advise the board ahead of time :p wink wink. Blight is still in a never ending struggle, but this time around he goes against a fresh newbie who seems to have a taste for french delicacies and enjoys talking to his pet seal. Plus another newbie who is also a former GWC superstar looking to hit home. Will funny conquer, will Kane shock us, or can darkness take it home? Find out in this falls count anywhere low card, make or break match up. Here's one for the masses people! The beautiful and seductive Tali Preston tests her skills against the brother of 2009 MWE Hall Of Famer Sonny Williams. With a fresh outlook on life and a beaming personality Jai hopes to take the MWE by storm with he, himself, and I. His hopes are not to hang under the shadow of his brother while Tali looks to use her natural beauty and deadly abilities to slaughter the roster one by one to get what she wants. And the winner? The winner gets to choose their opponent for the first ever Master Of The Premises qualifying match! The stipulation will be chosen by the board. MWE goes HARDCORE again with two of the most charismatic and egotistical young guns in the company. A high flying thrill seeker vs a Chaotic man trying to find his true purpose and situate his personal life. Could Riddick's backstory disasters be his destruction, or will Crash's pure mind set make an easy win to become the new XTV champ? WATCH CROWNING GLORY YOU F*CKS! These two have been raging war with eachother both mentally, verbally, AND physically for weeks on end. And now finally they face off to show eachother who's boss. The satanic creature known as Virus has toyed and twisted England's mind for far too long, and kidnapping his brother has taken it too far. Now England is more ready than he has ever been before with his brother at his side, to end Virus's escapades. Just like Virus and Chris, but a little more heated, Kevin has been screwing with Zak for long enough. And after making a statement by destroying his recent tag team partner and former GWC colleague, will Kevin prove why he's the kill switch, and why "straight edge" is better than Warner? Or can Warner tap into his rival's and former friend's mind to kill the flame burning inside the new found K-Hard. When these two face off in an Unsanctioned match, sparks will fly, birds will fall from the sky, and cats will bark while dogs meow! Err....wait, THIS MATCH WILL BE EPIC! IS IT TRUE? COULD IT BE!?!?! MY GOD IT IS! Not only does Kraven finally get a shot at the united states championship, but it is ACTUALLY being properly defended! Your dreams have come true people now STFU! Mason Resoan, friend of Sinc Mercier and loyal MWE faithful goes head to head with the passionate and emotional Nic Kraven who put up an excellent fight against the late, great, D.C. But will we see that same passion but ten fold? Find out what these two are capable of when the time comes. Yet another boiling feud that has been going on recently, D.C. and Sharpman! The former savior and current devil in disguise as he likes to be addressed is set to face off against a past world champ in MWE. D.C. is truly a legend, an icon, a true MWE original. While Sharpman may still have alot to learn, that doesn't mean he should be counted out. Joseph has been fighting hard to make his way up the card, and maybe this will be the push he needs! D.C. is vicious, Sharpman is vicious, these two will be like raptors in a fifteen foot high STEEL CAGE! There will be pain, there will be anguish, and oh yes....there will be blood? *Giggles* maybe. MWE Hall Of Famers and spectacular legendary superstars collide in a final match of brute strength. They've done it all, and their ready to end it all. Both superstars retiring after this match, both superstars giving it all they got ONE....MORE....TIME! This is the send off match folks, the mother to end all mothers, the f*cker to end all sex positions, THIS IS IT BOYS, THIS IS WAR! When these two are done with eachother, that ring might as well implode itself in jizz, cause this one's mark worthy! So if you see them, wish them luck, it's their last match peeps. The main event, but is it worthy? IS IT APPROVED BY ALL OF YOU!?!?! I f*cking hope so. The winner of this match, gets to take home WORLD gold! We could see a new champ, we could see a defending loud mouth, we could even see Oprah washing clothes for old people, but WE WILL SEE one of the most talked about, anticipated, and over speculated matches of our time. For the first time EVER in MWE history, these main eventers go one on one for the gold, for the glory, and for SPARTAAAAAAAA!!!!! One winner, one new champ, who will it be? Hell if I know.
