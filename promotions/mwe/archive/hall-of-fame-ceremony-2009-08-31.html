Ya know? *People chant "Michaels" "Michaels "Michaels," as he says his final words.* Michaels: Either way...no matter what, I will ALWAYS be connected to MWE...even with no Wi-Fi. Remember, even if you didn't love me at first and didn't think I could do it...at least you got to witness...*****es. *And Michaels picks up his plaque off the podium and holds it high in the air as he embraces the cheering of the fans and his peers alike, as the very last MWE Michaels promo finished and Michaels accepted his award.*
