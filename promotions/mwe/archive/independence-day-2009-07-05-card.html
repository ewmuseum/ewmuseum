MWE Independence Day card, 5 July 2009

5 July 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
LIVE FROM: Jacksonville, Florida - Municipal Stadium (76,877) MWE once again switches things up with the intergender match bringing together the female body, and the male specimen, it's time for SEX! Wait no....it's time for merciless boob and dick pounding in that ring. Settling their beef once and for all the former rated-x member Devlin Sharpe and the former x-tv champ Eric Derf will gruel it out in a prison cell cage match! One thing's for sure in this match, there won't be any way to win or any way to escape without pinning the opponent first, get ready for an intense match. For the first time ever Peter K and Scotty Mcknight team up to take on Zack Crash and Chris England in a fight to claim team stardom, one object of complete destruction stands in their way, a steel ladder, an object of pain and torture, question is, how will they use it to their advantage? Oh the possibilities. Kraven puts the XTV title on the line once again to prove his worth and show that he is ready for tougher opponents. MWE introduces the first ever High Road Match, if Kraven wins he has to choose one of his opponents to succeed him as XTV champion, if he loses, he remains in the division, what will happen? Let the suspense fill your cold hearts. Warner has had to endure a month's suspension and is ready to fight back with all he's got, Kevin wants him to prove himself in a friendly matchup, these two have a long history and also are current friends, only one victor to take the number one contender's spot, one winner, and one man to be that much closer for the US title. But will their egos get in the way? Or will Warner successfully prove himself and show Kevin he can hang with the best, get ready, get set, START MARKING OUT!!!!!!!!! Mason is again planning to reach for gold and reclaim the US title around his waist, but Virus has proven he is a worthy adversary after just defeating Peter K and Mason just losing to Nic Kraven, can The Canadian Classic get his groove back? Or is Virus going to prove why he's called the devil? Only one way to find out in this epic Ironman Table match. Seeking revenge and another win on GM, DC is determined to make sure his opponent is left in a heap of misery, but nuh uh, GM has JUST returned, the fun has only begun. They will fight until they make eachother bleed, and then one of them goes for the pin and takes home a nice PPV win, but will it be the vengeful loud mouth, or the now unmasked marauder? Now is the chance for Sinc to be a "fighting champion" and take down a true MWE vet and original superstar, but Michaels has shown us that he still deserves to be main event, and Sinc is a remaining rookie in his eyes. Let the trash talk and screaming egos fly, when these arrogant wrestlers face off in a lumber jack match, there is bound to be some hurt. Could there be a new champ, or will Sinc remain strong? RP DEADLINE IS SATURDAY, JULY FOURTH!
