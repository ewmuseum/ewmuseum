To BeoWOOF!; who let THAT crazy DAWG out?

Sep 26 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A camera clicks on to a beautiful Hawaiian resort. There is a large waterfall roaring down a tall, rock wall and tropical plants surrounding an outside balcony. The balcony has lunch and dining tables and towering over each table is a straw umbrella giving each table a radius of cool shade. There are very pretty waitresses’ in hula skirts roaming around the balcony handing out very tantalizing food trays. It is early morning and the sun is glistening over the beach just below the wooden balcony as Hawaiian music is also playing loudly over the area. Among these tables on the dining balcony Sinc is seen chomping down a mango as he looks intently out onto the beach. His US title over his right shoulder. He is wearing a casual dressing. A blue Hawaiian shirt embedded with tropical trees and beach imprints. He is also wearing tan cargo shorts and black sandals. His bodyguard again is near him wearing his same black suit as the sun is gleaming off his same bald head. He is completed with black sunglasses, largely rimmed, and black cackies. The briefcase is laying on the table lying between them. The bodyguard is favoring a Pina Calata as Sinc continues eating a juicy mango. The camera zooms in a bit on the two. Sinc: Oh Jekyl….that sick son of a bitch….he got me man…he really did….I thought I could beat that circus freak….I guess I was wrong. Oh well….I am sure one day I will compete with him again, but next time I will have a bigger boot to shove up his ass. Sinc says this looking at his bodyguard. Sinc then looks directly into the camera very eager to say a few words. His face is a bit bruised from his match with Jekyl. Sinc: Next week I go against a man who spends most of his time acting like a douche bag at local bars. And who thinks he has got some big cohones after his victory over DC last week. Let me tell you something. That man would not have any balls even if he was to face some midget with really big muscles and some mental issues. OH MY GOD IT’S A TINY MAN I WILL BE DESTROYED BY HIS MASSIVE PECKS! I actually think I had a fan of mine take a snapshot of you and your boyfriend: The camera fades to a picture of Aeron Beowulf and a midget next to him. The bodyguard looks kind of disgusted. But also amused at the same time. Bodyguard: Maybe lay off the balls boss….kids will see this…love the picture though funny as always. Sinc nods in agreement and looks at the camera with a frown. Sinc: Oh right right…sorry kids I meant Magical Rocks….MAGICAL….ROCKS….nothing more. Sinc looks away from the camera and looks toward the beach for a moment, a look of disappointment strikes his face as the crashing of the waves were heard for a brief moment louder than the cheesy Hawaiian music that was playing. He looks back to his bodyguard friend. Sinc: I don’t know man….Beowulf may be kind of tough for me….X-TV Champ…winning streak….do I REALLY have what it takes to beat him? I mean it’s nice to be here in beautiful Hawaii and take a load off but I just can’t help but keep that savage SOB on my mind. He’s like a pimple that never goes away. Bodyguard: You shouldn’t doubt yourself boss. I think you are very talented. The bodyguard’s voice is a very deep, Italian like tone, Sinc makes a disgusted look and grumbles. Sinc: Right…your only saying that cause I pay you….hell why not tell people your name your mouth is sure big enough at the moment huh Boize? Boize: Sorry boss….I didn’t mean to upset you. Sinc: That’s fine….I have way too much to think about right now….you can talk to that damned camera man for a bit while I think of something to say….I just feel kind of overwhelmed right now. Sinc looks at the beach again with a very blank stare, as if he is looking at the infinite abyss. Boize: Shall I sing a song? I can sing somewhere over the rainbow maybe. Sinc widens his eyes and looks back at Boize. Sinc: NO! God no….forget it anyway…I think I have a few words I can say…. Sinc looks back to the camera, as if it exists again. Sinc: Ok Aeron….out of brutal honesty we are both quite fresh in this business. Let’s not lie to each other here. Your quite frankly one of the craziest, toughest X-TV champs I have ever seen. And I am not surprised your holdin that damn title for so long. But know this, I am one of the most arrogant cocky stars you will ever see. And as a champ myself I will only give you my best. BUT… Boize: Time for the good stuff…. Sinc looks at Boize wanting him to shut up. But Boize was not referring to Sinc, just about to eat some marinated pork after a waitress set it down in front of him. Sinc ignores Boize and continues ranting. Sinc: BUT, you tend to think you can show this persona of death and violence, and make people afraid of you…I am not going to explain my whole fearless speech again but I will say this, you can say all you want how good you are, but to The Arrogant Aviator, you are a spec of dust that was missed with the final sweep of the room. So now I have to manually pick you up and toss you out. Which is what SINC MERCIER…..intends to do. Boize is now chomping down on a piece of pork as Sinc continues talking. Sinc: I could easily go on and on about a guy who says he is CLEARLY not gay. Sinc rolls his eyes as Boize laughs a bit as he stuffs his face. Sinc: And that’s funny you say you’re the- Sinc makes his fingers act like quotes. Sinc: Top Dog. Because every time I see your sorry doggy ass in the ring you look like YOU need to be euthanized. And I think DC may agree with my wording there. You honestly looked like you could be mistaken for a dog, taken into a kennel, and then released after they gave you a very PROPER physical. Sinc looks at Boize after Boize wipes his mouth with a napkin. Sinc: Maybe I should get another size fifteen boot after what I said last week with Jekyl for Aeron BeoWOOF as well. That way when I get around to it I can give Jekyl and Aeron BOTH a whooping of a lifetime. And when they get home they can sit on their asses like Beowulf and his can of coke in some slutty two bit dollar hotel room. Hell maybe Jekyl can experiment with his little buddy in there just to double check if Beowulf is really NOT GAY. Sinc makes an expression like he has forgotten something. He then looks at the camera again. Sinc: Ah yes…you say you’ve seen asian porno’s? And you’ve seen cocks in them? OH MY GOD! BEOWOOF LOOKS AT ASIAN COCKS! EGAD! Wait actually, you said it was DC’s cock….in that case that’s just….wrong…and sad….very very sad…and to question his dick size? SERIOUSLY….you have WAY too much time on your hands Ariel. Sinc puts his hand over his mouth. Sinc: I would have NEVER guessed you were into that kinky shit Mr. BeoWoof I always thought you were a standup guy. I guess I was wrong…Oh…so very wrong. And you saying Lindsey gives you BJ’s? Hardly…maybe some other person named Lindsey….like some fifty year old prostitute I think I saw was with you in that hotel room….wait…that would be YOUR mom, not DC‘s…Or was it your grandma? I am SO sorry. My mistake. Again Aeron so kinky. Boize shakes his head from side to side slapping his head with his hand grossed out. Sinc eyes Boize. Sinc: Come on Boize don’t tell me you haven’t had a little kinky sex every now and then. Boize blushes as he looks away at the ground. Sinc: Thought so. Sinc sighs and looks at the camera as his facial expression tightens into a wince. Sinc: And I remember seeing you as I was resting in my locker room first winning that title, remember, this was when I actually had respect for little shits like you. I remember seeing you win that title and almost clapping. But now that I look back on it, you lost against J.K. Valentino, some nobody that doesn’t even show his face in MWE anymore. He was a weakling. I say now that was more of a freebie if anything, just like the freebies I am sure you get from every living scum looking for a good time on this earth. Actually, you are more pathetic if anything Beowoof. You hear that dog boy? Sinc moves the camera closer with his hand in force. Sinc: PATHETIC! Sinc then points the camera at Boize wanting him to say something. But Boize is now enjoying an ice cream sundae. Sinc: OH COME ON! Boize: What? What'd I do? Boize has vanilla ice cream smeared on the left side of his mouth. Sinc then lets go of the camera as the camera man repositions it fitting sinc back into frame and focus. Sinc: Well forget about my numbskull bodyguard over here. What I am trying to say is that you Beowulf need to get a checkup because that streak is bound to end. And Sinc Mercier will do everything in his power to make sure it does. And if I don’t end it…I guarantee you…I GURANTEE YOU AERON….SOME one will…. Sinc spits off frame and looks at Boize. The camera zooms out to get Boize back in the shot. Sinc: Right big guy? Boize finishes up his sundae and nods. Boize: Right boss. The camera then swings around back to Sinc and adjusts. Sinc: Just know Aeron you are not the best and you are not the worst, you are just that average line in between that needs to be broken. And I will be the one to break it and make a new line that you will NEVER be able to step over because I will not allow it. You will just watch in a blank stare as that line gets crossed by everyone else but you, just remember I have your manhood, and you will never get it back until you can go through the obstacle that is me. And I promise you I am a very tough obstacle to pass. Sinc spits on the ground again and makes an angry grin to the camera. Sinc: Don’t test me Beowoof don’t even try to counter what I say with pointless garbage. Because everyone knows you for what you really are, and that is an idiot who does not know when he is in boiling water, I will be the one to turn the temperature up and watch your career burn. Just like Alex Bowen and Devlin the ape shit Sharpe you will be left in the dust by Sinc Mercier’s wake and you will regret ever testing me, you will not make it out in one piece win or lose I assure you, pain will be inflicted to the best of my ability. I will BREAK…YOUR…BONES! Sinc slams the table and points at the camera in anticipation as Boize sits there watching blank faced. As if un-phased by his boss’s actions. Like it’s a normal routine. Sinc: DAMMIT BeoWOOF! The THOUGHT of you just gets me riled up. I am talking about a guy that had a fistfight with some roid raged thug and says it’s a warm up! FOR GOD SAKES DOG BOY WAKE UP! WAKE UP AND SMELL A CAT’S ASS IN THE MORNING! You aren’t a badass, you never will be, and you never should think you CAN be. It is ONE winning streak, if your gonna REALLY show me what you got then put that title on the line in our match so I can become an undisputed champ and watch you whimper in agony. You deserve that! When you rumble in the ring with me it ain’t gonna be no donkey show, hell no, I know you’d like that buddy but I don’t swing that way…it will be a CRIME SCENE. Because I’d call you thinking you could beat me a crime ANY day. Boize just sits there and nods every now and then. Sinc: Ariel BeoWoof I hope your PUMPED, pumped like Arnie and a bootleg terminator DVD, because you will receive a special invitation to kissmyass, beatdown court, San Francisco California, because Sinc Mercier will be there to make sure you get all the special treatment you need. The camera zooms out a bit. Sinc: And that’s all you need to know Aeron….nothing more…nothing less….because- Sinc stands up as everyone looks at him. Sinc: SIIIIINCCCCCC MERCIIIIIIERRRRRRRRR……makes it so! Sinc continues standing there looking at the camera as it slowly zooms out more. After zooming out halfway Sinc sits back down and begins mustering something to Boize. The camera is now moving backwards away from them preparing for a fade. Very low audio is heard now between Sinc and Boize. Sinc then begins opening the briefcase still sitting on their table, the camera rushes back to Sinc and Boize. Boize stands up before Sinc finishes opening the case and blocks the camera. Sinc pushes Boize out of the way a bit to get in the frame. Sinc: In time you will see everyone…not now….sorry folks….SHOWS OVER! Sinc’s fist is then seen punching the lens. The lens cracks and the camera falls to the ground and lays on its side, wobbled. A cracked side, ground shot of Sinc and Boize is seen walking side by side back to their table. The scene fizzes out right when they reach it and get back to their briefcase.
