Ten Superstars... One Winner...; Alejandro Rodriguez RP#2

Aug 8 2008

Alejandro Rodriguez

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The scene opens at the first ever MWE house show since it’s revival. The fans are going nuts and are chanting “MWE, MWE, MWE!” at the top of their voices. Suddenly “Eye of the Storm” plays across the loudspeakers and the crowd know too well what is coming, they begin to boo as Alejandro Rodriguez jogs out from behind the curtain, in a White “A-Rod” t-shirt, a red and white mask and red bottoms. Alejandro looks to be in good spirits as jobs down to the ring smiling through his mask. He runs around the ring and grabs a microphone from the announcers table. He then proceeds to enter the ring and do a four-corners taunt to which the crowd give him a mostly negative reaction. He returns to the center of the ring with his microphone, he speaks into it. Alejandro: MWE… IS… BACK! The crowd cheer and begin to change “MWE, MWE, MWE!” again. Alejandro grimaces and waits for them to be quiet again. Alejandro: And it’s such a shame… *he pauses* … such a shame that there’s just one tiny thing that spoils it… just ruins it. He pauses again and begins walking around the ring. Alejandro: That such a great fed, with such prominent history has to return, in a place… like this. The crowd begin booing as Alejandro shakes his head. Alejandro: And I am only here for two short reasons. ONE! Because this is the first house show since the revival of the MWE. And TWO! Because I need to kick Xavier Stone’s ass, Brian Michaels' ass and Aiden Copane’s ass. The crowd continue booing Alejandro Rodriguez. Alejandro: I don’t care if you people boo me, I don’t care if you hate me. Because I resent you, you were the people who turned your backs on the little Mexican boy on the streets and for that, I couldn’t give a damn what you think, I am in this for the money and the glory, not to entertain any of you people. The sooner you realise that the better. The crowd chant “You Suck”. Alejandro: However, I didn’t come here tonight to talk about you people as much as I would love to, I didn’t come here to talk about my match tonight, I came here to talk about Crowning Glory, which promises to be the greatest spectacle in the history of MWE. We have the first ever MWE Hall of Fame Ceremony, an X-TV Title match and the match that determined the MWE Championship last time a ten-man over-the-top battle royal, as you know last time I eliminated four men and came second to David Whitman. But I don’t want to talk about last time, I want to talk about this time, and I guarantee to you that I will be more impressive that I was last time, I will hit harder, move faster and be stronger. The guys who have come from AEW have no idea how much I want this, and how badly I can fight, the 10 man over-the-top battle royal was where it all began for me in MWE, and it will be the same this time around too. And just to show my opponents how superior I am, I will access every single one of my opponents here tonight, starting with… A picture of Xavier Stone comes onto the titan-tron. The crowd begin to cheer and Alejandro waits until they calm down. Alejandro: Xavier… Stone… I know very little about you, I know that you need Ace Cannon to win a match, I know you have your son called Troy and a wife who’s name escapes me. I also know that you have done nothing to raise my opinion of you and I know that probably won’t happen. You have yet to prove yourself to everyone, and yeah you can talk about what titles you have had in the past, I could say I was MWE US Champion twice, I could also say I was the X-TV Champion and the MWE World Champion, you see, I have what it takes, you have nothing yet. I don’t care what you done, I don’t care what you say you’re going to do, lets see what you can actually achieve. Very little I presume. Anyway, I’m bored of talking about you. NEXT! The screen flashes and up comes a picture of B.G. Mancini. Alejandro: B.G. Mancini. Cool name. *he laughs* How is MWE’s resident Godfather? I’m sorry, I meant alleged Godfather. Ok, seriously now, I don’t know about you or any of your recent alleged activities, all I know is that you have a cool ring attire and a funky hat. You could surprise me at Crowning Glory, however much I seriously doubt it, you could come off with the win, maybe you get one of your mobster friends to take us all out before the big day, because besides that, there’s no way you’re getting your hands on that title. I can guarantee you that. NEEEEXT! The screen flashes again and this time it shows Alex and David Bowen. Alejandro: O...K. I really got stuck on this one, so I asked my inside source to come up with awesomely interesting facts about these two guys. And he said.. Hold on… *he reaches into his pocket and pulls out a little note, he unfolds it and looks up* Oook.. So Alex Bowen is a crazy hardcore sum-bitch… Fair Enough… And David Bowen *he turns over the note* is a big fellow who doesn’t talk. Awesome, so now you’ve heard all about them. And.. Uh.. Yeah, I hope they come up with a good scheme in the carpool. NEXT! He chucks away the note and the screen flashes to a picture of Jekyl. Alejandro jumps back five paces and clutches his chest. Alejandro: Woah.. That shit me up. He suddenly laughs. Alejandro: Where do we get these people? Did the MWE casting bus drop off at a freak show? Well I suppose why David Bowen is here too. Seriously? A mass murderer? Called Jekyl? It sounds like he be on a Jack in the box. You may be able to kill people, but I’m afraid my friend that no foreign objects are allowed, and yes… that does include knives and plastic explosives *he laughs again*, so we have yet to prove your wrestling skills, time to find out what you learned at the circus, do they teach mass-murderer’s wrestling there? Jekyl, much your life you’ve been a laughing stock, so you should be glad that after Crowning Glory, your life isn’t about to change. I think I’m done in that region… NEXT! The screen yet again flashes and this time it comes up with a picture of Devlin Sharpe. Alejandro: How exciting, we’re onto the MWE Originals! And we start off with Devlin Sharpe, I’ve seen him in the ring many, many times, I’ve seen him to be a part of one of the biggest factions in the history of MWE and I’ve seen him hold the X-TV Title. All of this looks great on paper, but truth be told I’m not that impressed by Devlin Sharpe, he’s not exactly been one to make a stand and do what he does best or die trying. The last I heard is that he’s dedicated this match to someone, and I believe that’s a wrong thing to do, just in case he fails, and nine times out of ten he will fail. That one out of ten is if B.G. Mancini hires a contract killer to kill me. NEXT! The screen now shows a picture of Brian Michaels. The crowd erupt into cheers. At the same time Alejandro claps slowly and mockingly. Alejandro: Whaddya know it’s Baz! Brian Michaels, my old chum! The multiple time MWE World Champion! Lets hear it for him everybody! *Alejandro claps slowly again* We’ve faced each other in the past, he’s won some matches I’ve won some matches, I know I can beat him, but I also know he has beaten me, but I tell you that history is not about to repeat itself. I know I can win, there is absolutely no doubt in my mind whatsoever. You can say what you want and you can do what you want Brian Michaels, you can make yourself out to be the biggest underdog in the world but it still won’t help you. NEXT. The screen fades into a picture of Sonny Williams and yet again the crowd give a very big reaction. Alejandro: If it ain’t the world’s favourite B-Boy! The man who’s so mistaken, he thinks it’s Sonny against world, he has no idea what he takes for-granted. He complains about his childhood, it’s his own fault if he gets into gangs, he’s a criminal and he deserves to be locked away as much as Jekyl. I respect him though, above all things, he has a good record against me, but he’s also against 8 other people, so he better be focused, because he seemed a little distracted earlier this week I have to say. He also dedicated this match to a loved one. Typical, isn’t it… He didn’t dedicate the match though, he dedicated the win.. I mean, is this guy for real? How the hell do you dedicate a win, before a match? I mean, unless you’re against Alex Bowen there’s no guarantee that you’re going to win. Ok, the time has come. I’m going to go onto the last but certainly not least combatant, a legend, a future Hall of Famer, ladies and gentlemen, the man, the icon, the first ever MWE Champion! The screen flashes and up comes a picture of David Whitman, who gets the biggest crowd reaction, they cheer and chant his name and it takes a good minute before they calm down. Alejandro: Please, please, the cleaner has enough to do without you all having orgasms. Alejandro laugh as the crowd boo him. Alejandro: Anyway.. Where was I? Oh yes.. David Whitman, well we certainly have a history, you beat me at Crowning Glory the last time it happened, I never forgave you for that, I did all the work and at the very end you dumped my ass over the rope as I eliminated four god damn people and you only eliminated me! Then you beat me the next day on the first ever Main Event on the first ever edition of Monday Night Mayhem, I was so pissed off then. But I accepted defeat and moved on, since then you have had nothing but my respect and I am very glad to induct you into the Hall of Fame this Sunday. But no matter how sincere I am and proud to induct you on Sunday, please know that I will hold absolutely nothing back in the ring, I think you know that, you know what I am capable of. But also know, if you try something like what you did last time again, know that I wont be as forgiving as I was last time. In fact, the whole roster should know that crossing Alejandro Rodriguez is not in good interest of your health. The crowd boo their disagreement. Alejandro: No, no, no… listen up. I’m not here to piss anyone off, I’m just here stating facts, just stating the obvious. I’m in the best shape of my life and anyone who thinks that they can even hold a candle to me, just know that they are delusional, in two minds. I will have no mercy this Sunday and everyone should know that any respect or mercy I had for them, it will be going out the window, because I openly declare that this match will be the most important match I will ever have in my life, and I’m going to win that match or I’m going to die trying. I’m not dedicating the match, not dedicating the “win”, to anybody, I’m doing this for me, because I have to and I need to in order to make a statement and put the rest of the MWE locker-room on hold. That includes Whitman, Williams, Michaels and all the other guys in the back. Alejandro drops the mic and rolls out of the ring as the crowd begin to boo. Alejandro walks back up the ramp casually. He turns around once and looks at the crowd before turning back round again and walking through the curtain.
