In the name of a Father; MoP Tournament Final vs Brad Kane

Oct 3 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
QUOTE To recap what's been going on: CSB finds out he's the father to a newborn baby with no living mother or relatives. It starts to plague his mind until he disobeys his lawyer Harold and goes to the hospital, getting caught in the moment and taking the kid and driving off, getting into a high speed chase. He gets bailed out by Harold and MWE's Zack Crash but now he must face Court to win custody of his child, but also at the risk of a restraining order and possibly prison. QUOTE OOC: Just to let you know, after the promo it switches from narrating description to Blaise's description/thoughts, just because it wouldn't work having one style for both. I've tried to make it as clear as I can in it though. Oh and my use of "***" in the middle there isn't so I can be lazy with transitioning, it's just because I wanted a closure type feeling. If you read it through, you'll see my description means I don't skip anything time wise. Enjoy guys. *** QUOTE The camera opens up to see Chris Saint Blaise sitting in an empty Court Room on the south side of Boston. Chris' face seems less animated than it usually is as the tension in the room mounts. He's dressed in smart suit attire from head to toe, except for undone top bottom to give him space to breathe. He never liked suits. The room is a huge and powerful scene. A mix of grey and brown, the only highlight in the picture is of our "Modern Day Hero" sitting in the "accused" chair, staring coldly at the ground below him. Suddenly his lack of motion breaks and he looks up the lense, somewhat of a distant twinkle in the eyes. He gulps slowly, before opening his mouth and speaking in a low, monotone voice. Ladies and Gentlemen... ...Boys and Girls... ...Ebony and Ivory... ...This may be the last time I speak to you in the free world. He pauses for a moment, clearing his throat and letting his last sentence sink in - but sink in for him, not for his audience. The realisation of what he's said has hit him but he chooses to persevere, looking back up to the camera, now more intense than before. In approximately twenty minutes, this Court Room will be filled with bare-ass strangers who get to choose my fate and my future. Whether I get to be a full time father, or whether I get to be just another number in a Boston State Prison. So whichever path I'm given, I wanna' take this opportunity to tell you why I'm here. A thousand doubts go through his mind as he finally breaks his silence on the rumours that have been sweeping through dirt sheets for weeks. In his gut he knows if he does go to prison, he wants people to know the real reason why. I live in the fast lane. Girls, drinks, maybe a couple of herb remedies that could be interpreted as non-standard behaviour, whatever. But a little while ago I learnt that my lifestyle had caused a woman by the name of Alexis Moona, to give birth to my child. Unfortunately during labour, Alexis passed away. Although he knows what to say next, he pauses for a moment, paying his respects. With no other family, that kid had either me or social services left, and like I'm gonna' leave my child with those stuck-up assholes, so I took my child home. Or at least I tried. Turns out I forgot to sign a form and that warrants being chased by four squad cars. And that's basically why I'm here. Bitterness starts to seep through Chris' cold front as he begins opening up a little more emotion at a time. He clears his throat once more as stares a hole through the camera as if it's his enemy, ready to unload verbal hit after hit to winch his frustrations out. So there's two things that could happen. The first is if I go down, I go down in a blaze of glory. I go down knowing that I could've brushed that kid aside and ignore this whole situation, but I went to do the right thing. If you disagree, I'd like to get in the last insult and say go fuck yourself. Good luck replying, since there’s no cable in Prison. I should know. Slowly his momentum starts to build as he fights through the battle and doubt in his head. He licks his lips, almost excited to say his next line. But if I come out of this a free man, God help you MWE. Because if you thought I was kicking ass before then I'm gonna' be hauling it this time round. I'm gonna' be unphasable, unstoppable, un-fuck-with-able. In short, every one of you is going to hate my fucking guts. So I guess it's fitting then that I'm meant to facing MWE's resident family man, Brad Kane. Anybody familiar with both Brad and Blaise would understand the irony as CSB looks into the distance, smirking to himself slightly. For those who don't know, Brad is very delusional. Ya' see, Brad believes that because he's famous that he needs to thrust his family into the limelight with him. Take 'em all for the ride like it's a theme park. Every week, a different promo from Brad about his daughters or how hard he bangs his wife and what kind of noise she makes, regardless of whether they want the limelight or whether Megan reads all those noises off of a cue card. Well Brad, sometimes the spotlight is a bad thing. Look at what happened to Michael Jackson. Or just look at what everybody says about you. The guys in the back, they're all really happy for you man, you got a good thing goin' on. But do you really need to bore the shit out of three million every week? Nobody cares about your home life. People might not care about mine but fuck, at least I don't go shouting about it. Chris sits back in his chair a little, relaxed at being able to cut loose again, though maybe for the last time. He allows himself to vent without worry as he knows that win or lose this case, the impact of his words get that much stronger. Of course, you're going to take all this to heart and that's understandable. When we hear harsh truths we try to retaliate, even if it's with bullshit. So criticise what you read in the dirt sheets and hear in the locker room. Call me a deadbeat dad all you want. But look at the home you bring your kids up in. A million kids, a million different mothers. Your fights with Spike and all the intertwining bullshit with Joe Ragnal and Freya. Just keep your sermons to yourself about what kind of father I'm gonna' be and what kinda' home I'll bring my son up in. But if I'm a deadbeat then Brad, so are you. In short, you've got no right to lecture me, even if I am a single father. Matter fact, fuck that. I've got a best friend who'd never turn his back on me like Kevin did to Zak, like Danny Vice did to GWC. So forget the wrestling for a minute. If I win this case, I can't wait to be twice the father you are. He pauses himself momentarily, thinking he might have crossed the line with his last remark. He then looks back up the camera re-assured, knowing he's got nothing to lose and everything to gain. He starts to get more and more animated, not just with his facial features but with his hands, putting his hands out as if to bring a point to the table. And don't even bust out all that Straight Edge crap because we've heard it all before. Straight Edge means you're amazing and can walk on water and have a huge dick. Keep telling yourself all this champ, but you preached this shit to Zak, DeAngelo, the fans - and nobody ever cared. That's because Straight Edge in itself...is flawed. Hell, technically speaking...I'm Straight Edge. Blaise keeps a straight face as he's dying to unleash his next punch line, not just putting Brad down but mocking his lifestyle the same way he expects Brad to mock his. Well let the flames begin. Well straight edge means no addictions, right? No conforming. Well I'm not gonna' conform to what you believe, to the standards you set or the make-believe status you made for yourself in GWC. I'm not gonna' conform to the critic's and I'm not gonna' conform to you. I don't lie down for anybody. Unlike your wife. At this moment, Blaise knows he's gone a step too far but by now, he disregards control and rolls with it instead of resisting it. His smirk returns. I thought Straight Edge meant no sex outside a relationship? Hmmm.... Guess I am the Straight Edge one after all. While you're no better than those reality TV stars, craving for a couple more seconds of screen time. Because you can't have it all Brad. You were the straight edge tough nut but when everybody started talking about CSB, the young upstart badass then you panicked. Your limelight was fading and you hated it. You're so fickle you had to think of something else to get people to watch you again so you decided sticking your dick in a bunch of prosthetic whores would get you a couple YouTube hits. Well mission accomplished, go fuck all you want. Have fun popping Viagra and trying to get past 3 inches. But when you're fucking, I'm training. And when you're training, I'm still training. A real wrestler, whose heart is in the game, never stops trying to get to the top. Remember that when you're fisting Paris Hilton. Chris puts his feet up on the table, almost carefree at being able to release so easily. He knows he has to do it now because for him, there may not be a later. And have you noticed I've barely even touched on GWC? Not because you got the better out of it, which you haven't by the way. I just wanted to save the best till last. Brad Kane, the face of Global Wrestling Coalition. Debatable, actually. I mean, you flirted around the main event but you never really brought it to the table the way your hype promised you would. When you finally won the Title you had it for what, a week? Two weeks? 3 tops man, you barely even got your name plate on the thing. And all the while you were achieving your life goal, nobody even noticed. Everybody was watching CSB light up The Ultimate Title division and making it the number one Championship in the whole country. Five star match after five star match, guys like Me and Creeping Death made you and the big boys look like shit, and you hated it! You couldn't understand how someone could come in and steal the attention away from you, without even trying. And the answer was simple. I'm CSB, I'm not straight edge, but I'm still better than you! Chris looks up to the ceiling, shaking his head in disbelief of what he see's as Brad clawing onto the spotlight like it's his child. To Blaise, GWC meant happy memories on the outside but it came with a dark tint on the side. And this was something he rarely spoke about. He looks back to the camera with a relaxed but serious expression. This wasn't about being funny, this was about the truth. And don't take this as jealousy either, I've got nothing to be jealous of you about. I was GWC's last ever Champion, hell I still use the belt as a coaster. Yeah you got the limelight but you got it by stealing it from the people who deserved it, like your friends. Just look at Zak. You call Zak a friend but last week when you faced his protégé', Cruz, all you did was cuss out his name. There is nothing you or anybody can say to the man Zak Warner. He's put on Match of the Year candidates, he's won Title after Title, he's toppled the biggest dogs in the business. But you don't give him credit for it, oh no. You decide to badmouth him just so you look better against Cruz. It's the same way you stole the spotlight from Me, Zak and DeAngelo back in GWC and Brad, I can't stand you for it. Blaise takes his feet off the table as the tone gets a little more serious. You can see the anger inside of CSB build but he restrains himself knowing his location. Instead he lets it seep out, slowly, but still making his intentions known. For those who don't know, us three were the anti-authority in GWC. When the Owner, Danny Vice, started acting like a giant douche, we stood up to him. We were fighting him and we were doing just fine when Brad came in and decided it was his ship now. We were renamed Uncultured Revolution. Like that makes any fucking sense or matters in the fucking slightest. Like we needed you, your labels, your t-shirts or anything. You brought nothing to our team and fell flat on your face when it all mattered. And all for what, to get a couple more weeks with a main event pay check. Well you can have your limelight, your fame, your moment in the sun. Either I kick your ass, win this tournament, and you can be its first ever runner up OR I go to prison, you face GM and everybody knows you as the winner who didn't fucking deserve it. After that, go pull whatever fucking stunt you want. Have forty more kids and make a porno with Blight. Maybe then somebody, somewhere, will still give two shits about Brad Kane. Me though? I'll still know you as the whore without a pimp. The reality star without viewers. Another opponent without a fucking chance. At this point CSB snaps, standing up and throwing the chair back behind him. He stares into the camera with a huge fire behind his eyes, pointing at the lense like he's going to rip it straight off. Brad, you better hope I don't win this case. Otherwise I'm going to make you more famous then you've ever been before! MWE, feel my fucking meat! *Fade* *** Wayne: Aaaaand Cut! Relief, amongst other things, hit me like a steam train. Dear God, this is a surreal moment. CSB: Thanks for not talking in this one bro. The camera lowers and Wayne Prez rears his grinning mug in my direction. He walks over, dressed in an Armani suit and gives me a ghetto handshake to embrace. If you're wondering why his suit is nicer than mine, it's simple. I spend my money on paying the bills. Wayne spends his money on making his wardrobe look like P Diddy's. Wayne: Don't mention in man. I'm saving all my good lines for my debut. As serious situation it is, Wayne sure knows how break the tension. I suppose that's what friends are for in predicaments like this, rather than freaking the shit out of me. CSB: Your debut huh? Wayne: Yeah if you lose I'm gonna' ask for your contract, ha ha ha. CSB: Makes me glad about the whole "no cable in jail" thing. Back in GWC, Wayne asked me if I'd train him up to be a wrestler, though the results were a little less than successful. Still, don't be surprised to see "The Prezident" in an MWE ring some time soon. Not. ... Yeah I just pulled a "Not joke". Fuck you. We walk out of the empty Court Room and down the halls to see Roldy, going through his rough notes like a schoolboy on the night before an exam. Though he's clean shaved and wearing a crisper suit than usual, Stevie Wonder could tell this is a sleep deprived man. He sits on a bench in a nearby hallway as we're separated from everybody else involved in the case. Roldy looks up and spots me, but looks down and keeps ruffling through his papers as if he hadn't seen me. Instead he calls out without looking at me. Harold: I hope you cut a real "Braveheart" moment in there 'cos I had to pull some major fuckin' strings to get you in. CSB: Yeah I appreciate it Rold. Last favour I ask of ya'. Roldy stops and looks up at me. There's a couple of seconds of silence until he snickers and goes back to his stack. Harold: Ha, you've said that before. He's right, I have. Though I think it's the thought that counts, right? He stops again and looks up to Wayne like he's a child who needs to leave so his parents can watch "Die Hard". Harold: You, get take your seat, I gotta' talk to Chris alone. We share another ghetto shake as he runs down the hall now that everybody is piling into the Court Room I made my own. Roldy takes a deep breathe and stands up in front of me, leaving the papers down on the bench so he can see me eye-to-eye without distractions. This is where things start to get tense. Harold: Chris...you're a good kid. Your heart's in the right place, but... He sighs, looking back up into my eyes. Harold: ...But in every mans life you've gotta' learn when to cut the shit, alright? What I said to Brad earlier about harsh truths has never been so fitting. The only difference is that I'm not gonna' bullshit in retaliation because I know Roldy's just saying what everybody else is too scared to say. Since all this started...I've changed, but somehow to me it doesn't seem like enough. I'm 27 years old. It's time to commit to this. I walk forward and hug Roldy because I just don't have the words to say right now. Roldy knows I understand though. He knows I’m on his side. ???: Sorry to break up this little moment... I turn around to see the smuggest expression I've ever seen. ???: I just wanted to let you know that I'm Drew Jackson, head of prosecution. Scum. This is the guy who wants to keep me from my child and if it wasn't for Roldy's speech ten seconds ago, I'd rip his fucking head off myself right now. Drew: Don't bother putting your hand out because I'm not gonna' shake it. I just wanted to let you know Chris, that I've worked for social services for the past twelve years. I've seen everything. But if there's anybody who's unfit to be around children, it's you. He walks up and looks at me, face to face. He knows I'm a wrestler, he knows I could snap his neck in a second yet he looks at me like he's not even the slightest bit intimidated. Drew: And it's gonna' be my absolute pleasure...making sure you never see that kid again. My fists are shaking with rage. I wanna' fucking kill him. I wanna' break his fucking neck. But he walks away before I get the chance. *** Harold and I walk down the halls towards the Court Room I can now call my own, going over the tactics and talking points we'd spent so much time on over the past week and a half. As we get closer I can hear the noise from the busy Court Room get louder and louder. It's funny how I don't mind wrestling in front of thousands in an Arena but when you've got a hundred people in one room and you know they're talking about you, you can't help but panic. We walk in and the room suddenly gets a little quieter. We walk through the stands and I see a flurry of familiar faces. Crash, Warner, DeAngelo and Wayne. My second family, each rooting for me as if it was the match of my life. It's weird because your whole life you go through situations where you compare it to be judged, but only when you're sitting in a seat once shared by Murderers and Terrorists do you really know what it's like to be judged. I look to the side of me and asshole of the millennium Drew Jackson sits with a few shuffle papers at his table. I look to the Jury and they stare back at me like I’m putty in their hands. My life is in complete control of a bunch of absolute fucking strangers. Bailiff: All rise for the honourable Judge Andrews. Everyone stands as an elderly white man in full apparel makes his way into the Judges booth, sitting himself down and calling order. This is all starting to seem really real, really quickly. Judge: Case 245, Social Services America vs. Mr Christian Saint Blaise. Prosecution, you may lead with your opening statement. With that, Jackson jumps to his feet like an eager schoolboy, ready to dig into me. He takes the floor and paces back and forth, displaying an arrogant smirk. I didn't think it was possible to hate someone this quickly. Drew: Ladies and Gentlemen of the Jury, I have a lot of faith in you. You all are today's modern America. People who work hard for where they are, who live healthy law-abiding lives. To me...you're what's great about America. Roldy mutters obscenities under his breathe as Drew butters up the Jury. Please let them notice it like we have. Drew: But ya' see, not everybody are like you, the average Joe American. Some people decide to take shortcuts, to break the rules. To break the law. Now if you've worked hard to bring up your children then I believe you deserve to see them grow up. Call me old fashioned, but you've done this the right way. Chris Saint Blaise, in my honest opinion as a veteran working with children, is unfit to breathe same air as you and I, let alone raise a beautiful baby boy. Chris Blaise is a danger to society, a bad example to today's youth and it disgusts me how this debate even has to go to Court to be decided. Drew looks at the Jury with an empty expression as if he's only telling the truth, but maybe in his head he is. I suppose something's not a lie if you really believe in it. He turns sharply towards me and points at me, as if I didn't stand out here enough. Drew: I'd like to call Mr Blaise to the stand. I look to Roldy like a cry for help despite how much we've been through this, over and over again. He nods at me to tell me it's alright as I rise, making the longest march of my entire life. Drew: (speaking quieter) That's right Chris, savour the freedom while you've got it eh? I ignore that piece of shit and take my oath before finding my place in the box at the head of the room. Wow. The view here is unlike any other. I could see everybody staring a hole through me. I could see the hope in Wayne's eyes, the worry in DeAngelo's, the focus in Roldy's and the smugness in Drew's. Drew: Mr Blaise tell us how you met a Miss Alexis Moona? CSB: Er..I met her in The Litten Tree bar in south Boston. Drew: Yes and according to your statement you drank with her that night before returning to your apartment to engage in unprotected sex. Is this correct, Mr Blaise? CSB: ....Yes, yes that's it. Drew: Now why did you fail to use protection, Mr Blaise? Do you disrespect women? CSB: NO! I- Drew: Then what is it then? CSB: We were drunk! We didn't know any better! Judge: Order, Mr Blaise. I settle myself back in my seat and take a deep breathe, pacing myself. I can't get worked up - that's just playing into his hands. I look back to Jackson and straightens his tie, further directing his play for the Jury. Drew: So after your night of intoxicated tomfoolery you kept in contact with her, right? CSB: No. She left the next morning and I never saw her again. Drew: So after having unprotected sex with a complete stranger you never even suspected she would fall pregnant? CSB: Well she never tried to contact me so... Drew: That's not what I asked, Mr Blaise. Compose yourself, c'mon. I take out a tissue and wipe a bead of sweat that appears on my forehead. I gulp hard. I'm going into fucking meltdown here. Roldy catches my eye as he takes his hands and pushes them down in the air, signalling for me to "cool down". It's easy for him to say over there. But before I can catch my breath, Drew is back up in my face like he wants a shot at me. Drew: Mr Blaise could you inform the Jury of your profession, please. Harold jumps to his feet. About time. Harold: Objection! Judge: Over-ruled. Answer the question, Mr Blaise. Roldy sits back down, defeated. Part of him knows he's just gotta' take the hurting in the first round so he can batter the fatigued fighter in the second. That's all well and good, but you gotta' make it to the second round first. CSB: I'm a professional wrestler for Mayhem Wrestling Entertainment. Drew: A wrestler, hmmm...Oh I was just wondering how it connected to your previous offences. Shit. Drew: Could you please inform the Jury of those offences now Mr Blaise? Harold: Objec- Judge: Over-ruled. This is a civil offence, not a broken law. Mr Blaise's background may be a factor in his future behaviour. Mr Blaise, answer the question. Shit shit shit. And I can't lie either, I'm under oath and Drew already knows what they are. CSB: ... Drew: Today Mr Blaise. Sweat starts to fall down my face like a waterfall as I flurry to stop it reaching my eyes. I look at the stands and the guys are shaking their heads in distraught. I've lost and they know it. CSB: One count of possession and one count of assault. I speak as quiet as I can, ashamed at the life I used to live. In fairness, these were back when I was 19, barely even an adult. I thought if I became a wrestler then I'd never have to work again, so a criminal record wouldn't mean shit. But here it is, biting me on the ass. I feel like the smallest man in the world. Drew: Assault and Drug Possession. Now ladies and gentlemen of the Jury, do you believe a convicted felon, someone who's violent and someone who's consistently under the influence of drugs and alcohol - Do you that kind of person has the RIGHT to be a father? To bring up a child and to teach that child right from wrong, when they don't even know themselves? The look on the Jury's faces says everything I'd ever need to know. Drew: You're a danger to everyone around you Mr Blaise. And I'm not gonna' stand here and let you endanger another life. Your honour, no more questions. Drew goes back to his chair like a victorious champion as I trudge back my desk. As I walk past the Jury they all look at me like I'm worthless, like I'm something I'm not. I can't believe this is happening. The sound of so many people talking is almost deafening as the Judge decides to step in. Judge: Order, ORDER! The Jury will take a fifteen minute recess to review events. Case dismissed. Thank fuck for that, I'm almost drenched in my own sweat here. I take my seat next to Roldy and he just sits there, staring into nothingness like a zombie. CSB: Roldy what do I do? He just sits there, like I don't even exist. For fuck sake Rold, I'm dying out there, the least you could do is acknowledge me. CSB: ...Roldy? Harold: ... We're fucked. *Fade* ***
