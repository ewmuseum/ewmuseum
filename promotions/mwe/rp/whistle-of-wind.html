Whistle of wind; jekyl RP

Aug 22 2008

Jekyl

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
An eerie whistle of wind, the creaking trees yonder crack and bend in the wind. *Creeeek* *snap* *crack* A branch falls in front of the camera, as it goes from top of view to the bottom Jekyl appears on scene. His hair moves in the wind, with every short gust his hair rustles, his smirk even darker and more sinister, his raw emotion can be felt in the air. *creeeakkk* *crack* *Creeeakkkkk* Fear, the fear of death, the fear of loss, the fear of nothingness. The smell of fear can be intense, the situation and circumstances determine the intensity of fear. Fear can show a person’s true personality, it can change them in way that only fear could accomplish. Fear is life. People live in fear and people are feared, feared for what they are capable of, for they thirst of power and the greed of kingship. Jekyl has his head down as he cocks his head to the side and looks at the camera. Jekyl: Evening…M..W…E… …My career in MWE hasn’t started out the best, but I have made an impact. The people here fear me, they know what I am capable of, of what I have done, and what I can do. *creeeeeeaaaakkk* *SNAP!* The wind blows harder, trees sway, the wind howls between the twigs and branches. The tree trunk creaks as the weight above it sways in every direction with the wind. Jekyl: This Company deserves a better class of champion, superstar and icon. I’m going to give it to them. I would like to state that my match with Aiden was nothing but a warm up. A rookie I took for granted, and yes it may have been a tournament to the US title, but alas that title to me is mediocre and I aim for bigger achievements. What makes a man strive for greatness? The recognition? The self satisfaction? The power? What makes a champion? Some say it involves many things, striking FEAR into people hearts. Some believe it’s the will power to become great. A champion should have many traits, should be able to intimidate anyone who challenges him. Should be able to want more, work hard and fight for what he wants. *BANG* A tree falls nearby. The wind howls louder. The raw emotion now riveting in every direction. It can be felt every which way, filling every nook and cranny. Jekyl: Mr. Whitman…. The MWE World Champion! I will give you a hand sir, you have won this title by beating not only me, but 8 other people, and that is something to be proud of. You have also successfully defended it against Alejandro and retained your glory. The raw emotion begins to change. Changing into something evil, beginning to obtain a sense of determination, a sense of lust. Not the type of lust you would find between a man and a woman, but a lust you find between a man and his most sacred and intense dream. Jekyl: But Mr. Whitman, what your aren’t calculating is that you may have won the battle royal, but you aren’t the one who eliminated me. Brian Michaels eliminated me because he still had a hold of me as he was in the ground, I lost my footing and had no choice but to let gravity take its toll. In all technicalities, I don’t believe that’s allowed, but I let it go. You have yet to face me one on one, and I promise it won’t be pretty, and you will title-less. Rain beings to pour, the sound of the drops echo, the sound bounces of trees and dead air. *snap* Another branch breaks. Jekyl: It makes me happy I get to be your opponent this week, yet I am saddened that I have the pathetic and incompetent Sonny Williams with me. My only message to you Sonny is stay out of my way, let me do my job. If you interfere, you will get hurt, understand? Since I have no choice that he is on my team, I don’t get to face you alone, but then again, you have your work cut out for you. It’s 2 against 1 Mr. Whitman. Can you handle the heat? Fire and passion are what complete dreams and make them a reality, to complete the common goal these elements are crucial. Jekyl: I await our match Whitman, all I suggest is that you bring your “A” game so without further a due Here…we………go… A flash blinds the scene as the atmosphere goes quiet, the rain stops, the wind stops blowing, and a dead silence causes an eerie feeling.
