MWE 24/7 — 08-09 PPV PR Archives

Peter Night Live

Jul 3 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Micheals Inc. RP *The scene opens in a room similar to that of the stage of Saturday night live. The room is empty and barren. Suddenly a spotlight comes upon the stage and we see Peter walk out and sit on a stool. He grabs the microphone by the stand and pulls it closer to his face. He shakes his hair out of his face. He begins to speak as if he was hosting a Saturday Night Live show.* Peter: Welcome to another addition of Peter Night Live or PTL for short. We have a great show for you here tonight. We will be building to my huge ladder match at Independence Day with my partner. His name no one really cares to know, but he'll still get a win after I do all of the work. I also have an interview with Chris England's brother great stuff there and I've dug up somebody for Chris' past for a very special interview. There is something for Zack Crash to, don't you worry. Sure he really isn't THAT important, but hey I even acknowledge the little dumb asses to. Well I want to start tonight off right so lets go to my interview with Johny England. *The lights dim, but before we go to full screen of "Peter's Interview" with Johny we see a shadow moving around. We are now in England where we see Peter sitting in a trolly being ridden through the streets of London. He has a sick and devious smile on his face. The camera man Devon looks scared and confused. He begins to speak in a trembling and scared voice. He sounds like the nervous friend you bring when you are about to do something illegal or bad.* Devon: Do you really think we.... we...... we should be doing this Peter? I mean..... don't you think this is crossing the line? *Peter just lets out a slight arrogant laugh right in Devon's face. He looks out the trolly window as Devon continues to try and reach his conscience. His voice still trembling.* Devon: Come on man lets just turn back now. Please I am begging you. *Peter turns his attention back to Devon and he begins to berate him. He spares no word in taking Devon down and shows no remorse for what he is about to do.* Peter: Do you really think that I am afraid of my actions? Don't answer that it was a rhetorical question genius. I don't fear England, the GM of Mayhem, or what my actions will bring upon the both of us. Last time I faced England I didn't dare cross the line enough. This time I will nuke it to the point of where it won't exist. It is time I do the unthinkable. So shut up and just film what I TOLD YOU TO! Got it? *Devon just sighs and turns his head. Peter smiles deviously as he pads his coat pocket and we hear somethings move around in there. It sounds like pills moving around in a bottle. Fifteen minutes pass by as the trolly finally stops. Both men stop out of the trolly and look at the small run down building in front of them. The sign reads, "Where your demons are put to the rest, Jolly Meadows." Peter smiles deviously as both him and Devon walk in. We enter the building and see a run down Johny England sitting in a circle with a bunch of other raggedy men. All unshaven and all look lost. We then see Peter walk in with a slow clap. He begins to speak and talks in a very sarcastic, but real sounding tone.* Peter: Why hello Johny very nice to finally meet you. *Johny rises up from his chair slowly with a scowl on his face. He begins to talk to Peter in an untrusting tone.* Johny: What are you doing here? Your business is with Chris, not with me. Leave me be. *Peter has a big Joker like smile. Joker from Batman the Animated Series(1990's original version) not some of the newer ones. He continues the conversation.* Peter: Can't a current MWE superstar pay an alumni such as yourself a visit? Johny: Chris has told me all about you. Your a SNAKE! Where ever you go pain and suffering follow. Just leave me be. Peter: You can't trust everything you here. Come on I even brought you a present. *Peter reaches down in his pocket as Johny puts up his fists and begins to slowly move back. Johny's voice is sounding quite scared and his begin to bug out.* Johny: Please just leave me be. Let me take care of me demons in peace. *Johny begins to tears up and turn his head away in pain as Peter pulls out his "present" for him. It is non-other then pain pills, the reason Johny is in rehab in the first place. He begins to shake around the bottle as Johny winces in pain. He begins to speak in a very scary and hoarse voice. Not sick hoarse, but evil and twisted hoarse.* Peter: Come on Johny don't you want your gift? Well you will have to beg for it. Come on Johny beg for it. Get on your knees and beg for IT! *Johny begins to speak in a brave, but scared voice.* Johny: Leave NOW! *Peter lets out a slight chuckle. He begins to speak in a cold and manipulative voice.* Peter: So be it, come on Devon we will be going now. *Peter turns around and begins to walk towards the exit. He has a cold smile on his face and stops once he hears someone scream something towards him.* Johny: NO WAIT!!!!!!!!!!! *Peter turns around and faces Johny. He speaks as if he is a broken man. He is on his knees, hands on the floor, and head looking down at the floor.* Johny: Please I am begging you to give me those pills. I'll do anything, please, just let me take away my pain. *Peter looks unamused. He places his right foot in the air, but just barely off the ground. His voice is cold and cynical.* Peter: Kiss my foot if you want your pills and tell us about your brother compared to me. *Johny hurriedly crawls over towards Peter's foot and begins to kiss it. He then begins to stammer in an excited voice.* Johny: Chris is a loser and a vermin compared to you. At Independence Day the only reason he is in that match is to leach off your star light. He'll lose to you just like everyone else in MWE. *Peter tosses the pills on the ground and begins to slowly walk away as Johny begins to take a few off the ground and shove them in his mouth. He turns his head and looks at Peter. He is gasping from the shock and awe of what Peter just did.* Johny: These are sugar pills! But........ but....... but why? What did I do to you? *Peter just turns around and mutters pathetic as he walks out. This scene fades to black on Johny crying and huddled up in a ball at what he has just done. We go back to the original scene and see Peter clapping with an arrogant smile and laugh. He begins to speak in an arrogant and pompous voice.* Peter: Bravo, bravo. Best interview I have ever seen. Whoever conducted that interview deserves a pulitzer or whatever interviewers get. It is time to move on. It is time that I give you a brief history of my "partner" for Independence Day, Scotty McKnight. *He cracks his knuckles and loosens his neck. He stretches out a little bit. When he is finally done he begins to speak. He is doing a very bad narrator voice. He is keeping the mood very light.* Peter: Scotty was born in Miami, Florida with a weird teething ring in his mouth. He wasn't necessarily bitting on it as he was sucking on it. Then at the age of five Scotty began to fight. Yes he began to fight the tingly feeling he got in his pants whenever he saw boys. Finally at the age of seventeen he met his idol Tito Ortiz. Tito taught Scotty how to work a "hose" and how to turn it on. It was here Scotty also learned that brown was his new favorite color. Now at age twenty-seven Scotty seems to love the submission move the STF and seems to love hanging around with lots and lots of sea men. *Peter suddenly stops. His facial expression becomes changed. The mood has changed it has become very serious. His eyes are full of passion and anger. He speaks with no remorse or sympathy towards anyone he attacks.* Peter: Scotty I know how much of a star you think you are, but you need to realize one thing. That if you get in my way you'll have the spotlight put on you, the spotlight kick. Brian Micheals chose me to be in Micheals Inc. because of my talent and because of the fact that I am going to rule MWE very soon. You know why he didn't pick you? Because you are Mixed Martial Arts reject whose only purpose in this company is to be a curtain puller. You will forever be stuck here in the midcard levels getting people ready to see the All-Star, the Show Stealer, the Whole Damn Thing, Everything you Want to be, ME! This match is my chance to become the star that I know I am. *Peter's mood is unchanged. He stops and looks at all of the barren seats. He walks over to them and he begins to walk up and down. He finally stops at one seat and decides to sit in it. He looks at the camera and begins to speak.* Peter: You see Scotty this is like rap back in the 1990's. Consider yourself one of those independent rappers, consider Brian Micheals Dr. Dre, and consider me Eminem. You know what the difference between Eminem and that independent rapper? Eminem became world wide, an icon, the biggest thing his field of specialty has ever seen. Scotty you may think you like to fight, but this isn't Mixed Martial Arts and this isn't that little prep school. Back on the street I destroyed kids like you and I am willing to do it again this sunday. *He gets up from the old dirty seat and begins to walk down the stairs. He still has a scowl on his face. He goes right up to the camera and looks at it. He goes backstage and pulls out a ladder. He climbs up the ladder and sits on top of it. He continues onward with his attack on everyone involved in his match at Independence Day.* Peter: Zack Crash don't think that I have forgotten about you even though it is pretty easy to. I bet you are thinking that this match fits you perfectly don't you Zack? Yeah your attitude towards life and your in ring style makes you believe this is a Zack Crash type of match. Well if you believe that then you are WRONG! The only person this match fits is ME! This ladder represents my career and the ladder that I am climbing to get to the top faster then everyone else in this match. England has never won a world championship, Scotty never won a world championship except at that one gentlemen's club called Color Me Rainbow where he won there world title, and you Zack who has never won a damn thing. Me I've been CWW Champion, Battlefield World Champion, ICW National Champion, and CWE Champion. I've won four different world titles for three different promotions. This sunday Zack expect to crash and burn. *Peter's eyes begin to bug out and he looks all over the arena. He finally lets out a devious smile, but he still seems angry and passionate at what has just transpired over the past couple of days however he won't tell. He continues on his rant this time sounding a little more sarcastic, but still very passionate and angry.* Peter: Oh no I made fun of Chris England. I forgot how very sensitive Chris is. Now he is going to try and attack me with the fury and bad teeth of an englishmen. I mean I should be very afraid considering Chris has never ever ever won anything significant here in MWE or even ICW. Now going into our match he is going to have his panties up in a bunch. I am literally shaking. I have no idea what to do. *Peter stops like he did with Scotty and is now serious again. He shows no remorse in attacking Chris' brother from the interview before. He looks ready to make sure Chris England knows he is not the guy to mess with.* Peter: Chris you are the type of guy to get his panties in a bunch because someone makes reference to the fact that Frank Smith only died so he wouldn't have to see your pathetic career. Or the fact that someone would give your brother sugar pills to show how truly pathetic your family line is. Chris the fact is that this business isn't right for you because you are way to emotional. You are the type of guy who can't deal with the fact that the people here have something to say about you, your drunk of a mentor, and your druggie brother. To be honest for a moment I am glad that your mentor isn't around anymore because then we would have more panzies like you in my ring. Your the first of many "legends" as these people would say or neverwas' as I would say who will be going down at Independence Day. Enjoy your few remaining days of happiness because they end at Independence Day Chris. *The scene fades to black as Peter stands a top the ladder and pulls down a briefcase similar to the one that will be hung in the ring.*
