A Point To Prove; A Scotty RP

Jun 5 2009

Scotty McKnight

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The battle of the garden of dreams. At the foot of Mount Doth sat a garden, it's beauty was beyond words to describe. It was neither day nor night. The sky was a mix of a fresh deep blue and green, the stars shone thru the sky brighter then day, a small river flowed thru the garden into a pond surrounded by small rocks covered in a soft green moss leading to the grass so lush leaves of every color carpeted the ground. Surrounding the pond stood a 17 foot tall weeping willow trees, though thousands of years old they seemed to stop growing centuries ago. In the corner of the garden stood a tree, the tree of souls. The tree was a thousand times larger then any tree ever witnessed by mortal eyes, each leaf glowed its own color, the color of the soul's aura it represents. Three figures were in the garden today, two females and a male. The female with the long white hair was laying across a branch of a willow tree dressed in a soft blue dress robe she was Sky, sister to Krad the young male laying on the grass deep in thought, dressed in a full black robe his long hair brought back in a ponytail at the nape of his neck. The other female stood atop the water, making droplets fly threw the air casting glimmers of light across everything, her long black hair was tucked in to her dark green dress robe her skin darker then the others. She had a native Indian look about her while the rest were a pale cream color, her name was Kiyal. She laughed as all the water droplets exploded into a mist over Krad,. he looks up slightly amused. Sky: Kiyal, it would of been funnier if you made a wave of water wash over him. Sky giggles an jumps out of the tree landing so graceful it would seem like she floated down. Krad: If you even were to try such a thing I'll make both of you pay. Krad smiled jokingly letting out a soft chuckle. Kiyal drifted off the water and stepped on the grass. As four crows dive down right towards her, sky looks up an smiles as they turn an go back up just before crashing into Kiyal who stands there unfazed. Krad: Damn birds Krad laughs as Kiyal looks at him enraged. Kiyal: Those BIRDS are my brothers and they are as much a part of me as you and Sky are apart of each other. The crows all let out long caws to agree with Kiyal as they fly out from the willows and land on the lowest branch that sky just slid off from. Krad: You know I love making you mad it's almost like a mist forms around you. Krad leans back an laughs just as a ball of water flies over his head. Sky looks at Kiyal and laughs hard, then runs to her to hug her and stops. Eight blood hounds emerge from the trees as if they weren't even there. Kiyal steps closer to Krad as Sky stands between them. Kiyal: They can't sense you if you're with me just try an stay quiet please an they will leave. Krad watches them as they come closer, getting up ready for an attack. Kiyal reaches out with her arm to try and stop him. Krad: No, they're gonna attack, I know it. Sky: Krad, listen to Kiyal they wont be able to sense us, they're just walking by. The hounds walk closer as one turns suddenly as the breeze blows through the trees and makes them rustle. Krad mistook the moment an jumped to attack, leaving the protection of Kiyal's ability. He attacks the hound and kills the other; seven lunge for him for an attack. Sky runs to her brother's protection and distracts the hounds, Kiyal follows Sky taking out two hounds from behind. Sky: To the tree, we can fight better with it covering our backs! Sky runs ahead as Krad and Kiyal follow the crows over head taunting the hounds. They growl viciously sending chills down there spines. The three of them make it to the tree and turn to fight. They brace themselves as the hounds charge to attack. Sky rushes for the first hound and kills it successfully, but by turning around in the fight one approaches her from behind. Krad and Kiyal yell out to her but there voices are drowned out by the sounds of battle. Kiyal orders her brothers to go after Sky and help her, they leave for the charge. Krad shoots out an attack aiming for the hound at the same time. Chohi the fastest brother makes it to Sky and gets between her and the hound just as Krads attack was to hit the hound. Chohi falls wounded as the hound bites Sky viciously, spilling her blood along the roots of the soul tree. As the roots sizzled an hissed, Krad grew enraged at the hounds. He grabbed the nearest one and literally tore it limb from limb growling like an enraged animal, the other hounds surrounded him. Angry at what they have just witnessed, this reaper doing to a member of their pack. Kiyal ran to join Krad mercifully killing one as she did so Krad finished off the other three like they were nothing. Krad: You dumb bitch! You and your dumb crows fucking got my sister hurt! I should kill you... He advances towards Kiyal, the crows go to fly to Kiyal's aid but stay still and quiet under Kiyal's silent command. She walks towards him fearless and stands looking into his eyes. Kiyal: How dare you say this is my fault? If you weren't the idiot who went to attack from so far, my brothers would have been able to handle it. Instead of taking out one of my brothers. Mist slowly rises around Kiyal, surrounding both her and Krad. Krad: I can take care of my sister just fine without your help. Krad waves his hand and a breeze blows by, blowing away the foggy mist. Sky: Stop it now! I'm fine... It was no ones fault, I don't want you guys blaming each other. Sky looks down at the two of them, blood staining her blue dress robe. Sky: Besides, we have bigger things to worry about. Sky looks up at the soul tree to see buds, thousands of years from budding, starting to glow bright. They were too high up to count how many, but at that point they all knew something was wrong with the tree, and as for the souls that were glowing, only time would tell. Krad looked at Kiyal, silent words of blame were exchanged between them, an argument that would last for thousands of years more. They all looked at the tree once more as the stars in the sky moved towards the east and the light dimmed, they left the garden. Bodies of the hounds askew, not knowing what would await them the next time they came... ****************************************************************************** Scotty wakes up in his room, sheets tossed from the bed, sweat beating down his face, taking deep breaths he struggles to differentiate reality from his dream. He looks at his surroundings and realizes he is still in his motel room. The seen creeps in past the motel room curtains as Scotty sits up from his bed and rubs his face, still shocked from the dream he just had... Scotty: What in the hell was that? A voice in his head arises out of the blue, almost scaring the crap right out of him. Sky: That was a memory of my past... Something I have forgotten for 5000 years. Scotty: So why does it come out now? Sky: After time these memories will come up, recollections of my past, which can turn up at any point in time. Scotty: Wonderful.... good to know..... *sigh* Scotty walks into the washroom, leaving the door open, the sound of drizzling water can be heard as we see the reflection of Scotty standing in the mirror. The zipping sound of metal lightly echoes the bathroom as he makes his way in front of the mirror. Scotty turns the water on and begins splashing luke warm water over his face, as he looks back in the reflection he doesn't see himself staring back at him, but a beautiful woman, long white hair that reaches to the mid of her back. Black fishnet shirt covers most of the skin on her torso while a black corset covers her bust and midsection, and to finish the ensemble, black trip pants with chains hanging all around the legs. Sky: MWE has started to give you and Jekyl a lot of criticism lately, how do you plan on fixing this issue? Scotty clenches his fists and slams them down on the counter causing the mirror and Sky's refection to shake. Scotty: I will tell you what I'm going to do... I'm sick and tired of Virus thinking he is better than me because he thinks he's the devil reincarnate... He is nothing but some nerd with lots of money and some spiffy pyrotechnics... He has a daughter who calls herself a vampire, when in truth shes an anorexic albino child, and to continue your little charade, you feed her blood? What kind of father are you? I will make an example out of you... show you what someone with real power can do. You may have beaten me and Jekyl at Tribulation but I guarantee that when Monday Night Mayhem rolls around, you will be wishing that your wife was there to carry you home....what a slut. Scotty looks away from the mirror as he manages to think of Demonica and Virus getting their freak on, but manages to clear his mind and leans into the mirror to look the reflection of sky in the eyes. Scotty: On the topic of his wife, I have to ask, since he goes to Toronto a lot, he should know the Jane and Finch area, and with that being said, to those who don't know it is the drug central of Toronto. Virus, did you by chance find Demonica there shootin' up a syringe full of heroin and decide to take her home? Does she still do this to make herself think that you are the man you say you are? You see Virus, you may intimidate MWE with your lies and deception, but you don't intimidate me...You are just some sad strange little man looking for attention and you think that having a family of freaks will get you this. I hate to break it to you brah but you got it, but its the kind of attention that Childrens aid craves and I have a feeling they will be knocking on your door any day now. Sky smirks as Scotty backs away from the mirror, his face shows his confidence well, the reflection of sky dissipates from the mirror and Scotty's reflection returns, Sky's voice remains. Sky: Your element of fire co-exists well with your passion to win. The fire in your heart burns fiercely with the passion to win, this will take you far but only as long as that fire burns. Scotty turns around and heads back into the main area of his motel room, contemplating whether he should go out into society today, or stay inside and think about his next match, when all of a sudden a knock booms from the door. Scotty makes his way to the door and slowly turns the knob. The door swings open as a man rushes in and slams the door behind him, looking through the sight hole in the door, we can only assume who this man is by his clothing, Scotty: Jekyl, what the fuck are you doing? Jekyl spins around with the eerie smile on his face, his hair rustled in his eyes, his breath shallow and fast paced, sweat beads up on his forehead. Jekyl: Yo... I need a place to hide out in for a few hours, if anyone asks, I was with you last night from 5pm to 5am, savvy? Scotty: Whatever... What did you do now? Jekyl takes a quick glance through the peep hole while he explains his reasoning to Scotty. Jekyl: Well last night I was walking down the road and I seen this nice new car that I liked, so I hot wired it and went for a spin. Well wouldn't you believe who I thought I saw... Scotty: Who? Jekyl: I thought I saw Jay Riddick crossing the road, so I sped up. Scotty's eyes widen in surprise, contemplating where Jekyl's story is going to lead to. Walking back to sit on the bed while Jekyl regains his composure and sits on the adjacent sofa chair. Jekyl: Well I sped up and hit the guy... and as he was flying across the hood and over the windshield, I realized something... Scotty: It wasn't Riddick? Jekyl: No.... he looked nothing like him... and he was black.. Scotty: How do you mix something up like that? Jekyl: Don't ask... probably cause this Riddick kid doesn't matter enough to be pointed out so easily, but my problem now is that the cops are after me... Scotty smacks his forehead and falls backwards onto his bed, eyes closed, thinking about Jekyl's current situation and remembers that Jekyl will be at ringside when he faces off against a retard and an emo, balding, self conscious freak. Scotty: Not that I don't care about your current dilemma, but I gotta get ready for my match this week. Jekyl looks over at Scotty, his facial expressions quickly go from astounded that Scotty doesn't care about his situation to proud that he is keeping his eye on the prize. Jekyl: Well we both know what Virus is about, but what do you have planned for Jay? Scotty sits up rubbing his eyes, thinking about who Jay Riddick is, his current career in MWE. Scotty: I only give Riddick credit for the few wins he has in MWE, but other than that, I will treat him just like society has done for his entire life... I will treat him like the dirt he is, make him wish that his mom should have swallowed, bring him to his last ounce of hope, I will give him the satisfaction of thinking that he beat me, then rip his last hope away. Jay believes in karma? The same karma which has yet to come his way? The idea that he has stuck in his head; “Oh if I'm a good boy, then good things will come back to me in return!”. Well I hate to burst his little bubble, but reality is a bitch and shes about to serve your papers... Jekyl smirks a little while Scotty paces in front of the bay window of his hotel room. Jekyl: Slow down you're giving me whiplash! Scotty snaps Jekyl a quick glance, his eyes concentrated, focused on one thing, victory, the expression of fierce anger cuts across his chiseled jaw. Jekyl flips on the T.V and begins flipping to ESPN, an MWE commercial begins to play. Live from Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic, MWE presents Monday Night Mayhem. Sinc Mercier faces against Kevin Hardaway and David Whitman for the long fought US title. Mason Resoan plays a special role as guest referee while Windy bridges faces off against D.C in a singles match. The screen flickers with each match being announced, Jekyl and Scotty smile at the next announced match. In a triple threat match, one half of Famous and Infamous takes on the new up and coming superstar Jay Riddick and continues a rough feud with The Virus. Can Scotty McKnight come back from his recent loss against the Black List Mafia? With Jekyl at ringside will havoc eventually ensue? Find out June 8th on Monday Night Mayhem. Jekyl turns off the T.V and looks over at Scotty who is now sitting by the window watching the sun creep up the skyline. Jekyl runs his hand through his hair, curious on what is running through his tag team partners mind. Jekyl: So not only do you have to go up against Virus, but you have to worry about some prick that goes by the name Jay Riddick, what's your game plan for Riddick? Scotty glances over at Jekyl, a grin appears on his face, the sadistic side of him becomes more clear. Scotty: Well Virus will be a little tough, but honestly, I don't think Riddick will be a problem, This guy thinks that because he has a few good wins that he is king shit, well I got news for him. I am Scotty McKnight, I am the kind of person people come to fear... Jekyl: Do the voices tell you that? Scotty: Shut the fuck up, I'm serious... I will no longer let Virus reign supreme of the tag team roster and I will be damned if some punk gets in my way of holding a title belt... You may not think holding gold is anything important, but I do... When I hold a title over my head, I hold it for me and me alone... not you, not anyone else... FOR ME... I don't need your help to get there, but don't get me wrong, I'm glad I have you as a partner, you have taught me a lot, but there are some things I will do on my own and beating these two jackholes will be my first step to glory. Jekyl: You're learning... We will see what you've learned Monday, for now, rest up... All of a sudden a loud banging noise echoes from the front door, a booming voice blasts from behind the door. Voice: THIS IS THE POLICE! JEKYL, WE KNOW YOU'RE IN THERE, COME OUT WITH YOUR HANDS UP! Jekyl: Aww shit... Scotty: Oh lawd... The scene fades as the front door to the hotel room breaks open in a hail storm of splinters, Jekyl and Scotty look at each other and run towards the door, fists clenched, ready for a fight. -FADE-
