My Love For Babies And Cowboys Pt. 2; Masterpiece?

Jan 4 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The camera focuses on Sinc as he sits down in the recliner Nate once dwelled in, and relaxes himself as he stretches his legs letting the warmth from the furnace envelope him. He begins closing his eyes then he reminds himself of his next opponent, and how he has not said a word about him. He suddenly opens them, sits up, and gazes at the camera in awareness. He begins setting himself into a mode he would usually be in if it wasn’t so late at night. He does his best to whisper his words. Sinc: So I am guessing you want me to talk some trash right? Do the dirty dirty? Sinc rubs his chin and nods. He stands up confidently and begins positioning himself into an active posture to show the camera some grand flare and energy. Sinc: You guys asked for it, now I wish I could be loud right now but I got a beautiful lady trying to sleep and her douche bag of a brother trying to jack off in his room, but I will do my best. Sinc adjusts his voice and takes off his black coat now lightening his suit. He lays it carefully on the recliner next to him and then shakes his arms out for some comfort. He takes a deep breath and smirks. Sinc: Let me just start out by saying, Mr. Cannonball, you were in one of the most treacherous, annoying, and quite pathetic factions I have ever seen in my lifetime. WAR, there is a lot to say about this pathetic attempt, first of all every last sucker in this stupid getup failed immensely and partly drove MWE to the ground with its negative bull sh*t and self righteous cry babies. Speaking of babies, I sure hope that poor soul was wearing pampers, because I would have shat myself. Sinc raises a finger in the air to gesture that he has more to add to his point. Sinc: The only thing worse than that is if the father of the baby sounded like he had the name of an STD. Leon The “Virus” Roberts….hmmm that’s close enough. But enough about the baby, I am sure you have gotten an earful of it nonstop, Liberty is in a better place now, AKA probably halfway across China. Let’s talk about you and Joey Dallas. You have a large history with him, and him getting on your berries about how he will save you and cleanse you and all that nonsense which is what he basically told me at Adrenaline Rush can be kind of tiring. But I am not going to let him nag me into thinking I am a horrible superstar, you shouldn’t either. I don’t need him, but I do think he has the potential to make me go places. As for you Cannon, all I say is him continuously kicking your petty ass over and over again until you see those green dollar signs turn black and blue is all that you will ever get out of that guy. Sinc briefly motions to the camera with a flaunting body posture that he is ready to talk some serious trash, and even tell a novel to the camera. Sinc: You two go at each other like the fat kid and the Oompa Lumpa’s in Willie Wanker’s chocolate factory. You bitch and moan at each other like the earth itself is at stake and its up to the wonder twins to activate their mighty powers and save it. Oh please, you’re a stuck up cry baby who can only get his dick hard after watching gold bricks fall out of your ass or seeing a world title around your waist which I can not ever see happening. If I was to choose between seeing Joey Dallas beat you again or seeing him try and squeeze the living life out of Mason come MNM, I would gladly watch him beat you to hell and back again only to laugh in your discrete face. I can see it now…. Sinc puts two hands in the air like he is holding a picture frame and closes one eye to look at it with a certain perspective. Sinc: I would make sure that is planted RIGHT on my wall. Your bloody, pulverized, bruised and battered face inside the MIDDLE of that ring and me standing there with my personalized microphone, framed and the picture being extra big. And while you are in that ring so badly injured you will hear two words…. Sinc tries to whisper as lightly as he can, but at the same time sounding like he is an assassin trying to snipe the living hell out of his greatest target. Sinc: You….Lose…. Sinc goes into a dark mindset of evil, grazing, thoughts. He then smiles with a vibrato in his lips. Sinc: Let me say a prayer for you Ace Cannon…..let me pray for the one who most think will have the upper hand on me. He whispers this in a maniacal tone and takes a moment to look up at the ceiling and begins forming his hands in prayer. He closes his eyes and wistfully mutters out calm, semi-serious words. Sinc: Dear powers above, I need your help, I need your help for me to decide what to do with this Cannon character. I am trying not to be TOO selfless of him, or too harsh. But at the same time I don’t want to apologize for anything that I have said. I mean, should I…..should I beat him to a pulp in that ring on MNM? Or Should I find an actor to do it for me? This is really really hard because I know in WZCW an actor replaced him because he was fired…and I don’t wanna come off as an assh*le but maybe this ONE time you can make an acception? Please don’t sick WAR on me if I do beat him bloody. I don’t want a bunch of amateurs to take me out. That would hurt wouldn’t it? Thank you and goodbye. Sinc stops his prayer and rolls his eyes as he makes an expression of carelessness and flips off the camera using the old, shoving the arm technique. He is truly showing he can care less about Cannon and his feats, he wants to show MWE he means business, as Nate has implied. Sinc: I don’t give a Dick Malibu about your football career, your best training money can buy, your steroid taking little girlfriend Paige, or your suck ass protégé’s. What I care about is beating you, getting BACK on that path to destroying Mason and taking back what is indefinitely mine. I make LOVE to that belt more than you or Mason make love to Covell every time he makes some special announcement. Oh jizz, here comes the man of the hour markout. No, he is simply the strings to this myriad of superstars. I am my own man, I can stress this all day. I CAN…I mean…I can take a rematch with Devlin The Ape Sh*t Sharpe, a LOW CARDER, and beat him senseless to a degree where he couldn’t even land a punch on me, and make it US match material. And maybe if I go beyond that, make him injured and cry to make a near main event status match. Because of ME, not him. Sinc ends his rant for a moment to try and remain calm and prevent himself from going into a hyper mode of dialogue. He also begins to gather himself to try and remain quiet as he continues his talking spree. He rubs his chest assuring himself into a calm manner, and continues. Sinc: I lost my US title yes, I feel horribly bad about that. But that isn’t going to stop me or end my trail of greatness to continue up the mountain and reach that golden belt from a dead, rotting tree laying gloomfully atop that peak. I will snatch that US belt back off that branch and make that tree bloom. Because that’s who I am. I make things tasteful, I give things energy. What do you do Cannon? You kick babies and get your ass kicked by a man who thinks he’s Jesus. And once I regain that belt, I am going to go further, and say I can become the main eventer everyone see’s me to be. It might take time, it might take some skill building, but soon I won’t be the one who has to be saved, or the one needing money for fame, I’ll be the one showing each and every MWE fan, superstar, and staff member that I am ruthless, and arrogance pays off. Sinc points to himself and smiles widely at the camera trying to show MWE that he is the next big thing, and he is not going to hide his confidence in it. Sinc: MWE, I am right here, waiting for that opportunity for another title shot, for another chance to prove my worth, for another whirl at Mason, or another whirl at the main event. If I have to shove Paige’s face up Cannon’s ass and then make Cannon sh*t her out in the morning I will not even flinch or hesitate to do it. Sinc turns away from the camera and drifts in front of the fire. He puts his hands out and begins taking in its warmth with an evil snare in his eyes. The camera swerves to the side of his face in a quick jolt. Sinc: I underestimated Mason. And I don’t want to make the same mistake with you Cannon. When Mason beat me, he attacked my weaknesses, he continued to say that I was a nothing. He bashed my ego by inflating his own. And he backed up his words and made me ultimately be the sh*t end of the stick if you will. Dallas was right about me, he saw potential, and I do have it. There is no doubt about that. And I will show him come Monday the potential he needs to see. Yes, NEEDS to see. If I can’t prove myself to a snob like him, I can’t prove myself to anyone. So you see Cannon, you now know why I need to beat you, and strive to go once again for that gold. I need to prove to Dallas and everyone else, that funny old Sinc, is not only not so funny anymore, but also not so MERCIFUL….anymore. Sinc loosens his tie tucked leniently in his buttoned white shirt once hidden by his black suit’s expensive coat, and takes it off while smoothly letting it slide into his right hand. He snickers at the camera symbolizing that the tie means something. Sinc: Cannon, compared to my life growing up, your life was gold. My family were low class people with hardly a dollar to their name. All of it spent on bills and utilities. You know what me and my brother did for fun? Told jokes and beat each other to death when we were bored. My parents would come home and see us bloody while we would just laugh tasting our fun driven red trickles of distilled glory. Blood and bruises were fun to us as kids, now imagine how much fun it could be with what I can get away with inside that ring with the likes of you. A man who has had a silver platter handed to him, the type of man that would enter an Abercrombie & Fitch and be seen as its next poster boy. The type of man that would play golf with cripples and be seen as a god. The point I am trying to make is, try stepping in the shoes of the needy, the hurt, the sick. I was lucky to get this job, you? You would have probably been handed a hundred other opportunities. The tie drifts more loosely in his right hand as it sways in grip with a taunting motion. Sinc looks at it in disgust and disrespect. Sinc: Ties, suits, uniforms, rolex watches…..it’s all the same. It’s all the epitome of what we all wish to become; Rich and famous. But inside we really know deep down that is not what we are. We are individuals hiding our own dark secrets that we wish, that we WANT to come out…but we can’t. We can’t let them because we are afraid of what could happen. Sinc then begins to dangle the tie as it slips from his grip over the fire. His expression is of pure hatred and negative aura. Sinc: Well let me tell you something, sometimes we just have to…..let go. Sinc lets go of the tie as it falls swiftly into the roaring fire. Each variant in color, each bright spark or brief speckle symbolizing a point or a moment in Sinc’s life to him. As he stares deeply into it he begins to see his life flashing before his eyes. His old comedic jokes, his echoing laugh, his devious comments, and then he see’s his father and him together staring out into the ocean with fishing rods. His mother and brother watching from the beach. He sheds no tears, or says no words. He just watches the fire and lets the tie burn. After a minute of total blankness Sinc turns back to the camera and begins walking slowly around the room. Sinc: The old me is gone and done with. This is a new beginning, a new year, a new time for me. And I have to utilize it. Not just for a victory, but for myself, for Kendra, and for pride. My new year’s resolution will become true and I WILL become the one to look out for this year. I’ll still call my enemies every name in the god damn book or let’s say joke about how their tattoos remind me of a man with skin cancer but that doesn’t mean I won’t ever take them or myself serious. It simply means I won’t be limited to being creative. He stops walking and sits on the arm of the couch inside the room. He has one leg swinging freely in the air while the other is firmly planted into the wooden floor. Sinc: Forgive me if I am sounding like Mr. Serious here, but honestly your probably going to see a lot more of that people. Funny is getting old. But I still love it somehow. So screw it, scratch what I just said. I am always going to be funny, but from now on it’s going to be a different kind of funny, a much more darker, meaner, funny. A forewarning I guess you could say. Sinc stops swinging his leg and plants both feet onto the floor and leans on the couch arm in a bland action. Sinc: Ace Cannon, you will be on my radar as the one for me to decimate in that ring on Monday. I want to see you pee your little undies when you see me walk down that ramp aiming for you. And then all the fans will see you and me nose to nose, and ask themselves if this is really a match to spend their time watching. And when they get their answer once that ring turns a crimson red they will all see that I am the real deal, and that you Cannon, are truly a former champ, and scream main event. But also, a simple puddle on my sunny day. Soon drought will consume you and turn you into vaporized air as I remain liquidated in fresh talent. You’ll become a nothing. I want our match to be amazing and something to look back at regardless. And that is a compliment my baby punting amigo. Sinc continues leaning on the couch as his eyes squint into an intrigued and very boastful passion. He shines a flash of energy and emotion at the camera as his words continue to spill out. Sinc: On a final note, you can be confident all you want, you can be full of this false pride, this false hope that you have. You can lose over and over until your confidence is finally shattered into pieces. But you can’t be so sure enough in yourself in doubting me. Doubt is the one thing that will kill you, that will drive a nail into your cold, bitter heart. I can take up protégé’s or hire actors, or kick all the babies in the world and call it talent, call it ENTERTAINMENT and STILL make it to the top. But after me and Mason, doubting is something that should never be done. So let me tell you something Cannon, I want you to wear your knee pads and your elbow pads on Monday, I want you to get ready to dance on your own grave, because in the end you’ll see me providing the final words. And my final words are concluded, and you will HEAR them ringing in your ears, because I am, and I will say it again and again until you recognize me Cannon…. Sinc tries to continue to maintain his quiet tone, but he can no longer hold it. He straightens his jaw and begins to posture himself into a pose of holding a microphone. He backs up his statement and shows he certainly does like expressing his creativity. Sinc: SINCCCCCCCCCCCCCCCCCCCC……………MERCIERRRRRRRRRRRRRR!!!!!!!!!! Sinc goes back into a neutral standing position feeling as if he has just freed himself from the clutches of silence. He acts as if he is relieved. But wonders why the camera is still rolling. Sinc: You guys can go ahead and leave now….anytime douche bags…just go ahead and you know…skadoo. The camera slowly turns to Nate and Kendra both in Pajama’s looking at Sinc with a pained fury. Sinc: Oops….uhm…that’s your cue MWE…bye bye…go go go. Nate shakes his head in pure dead end exhaustion as Kendra yawns. Nate: You take his hands, I’ll take his legs…. Kendra smiles with a tired grin and nods. Both Nate and her have drooped eyes from lack of sleep and their hair is a mess. Kendra: Agreed. Nate and Kendra then begin closing in on Sinc as he gestures to them to stay back with a worried reaction. Sinc: Come on guys…I couldn’t help myself…please just go back to sleep. I uh…I didn’t mean to…. Both continue moving in on Sinc still tired out of their minds looking as if their sleep walking almost. The camera fades out before they reach him and the scene finally comes to an end as Sinc bears a sighed sob. ______________________________________ I sincerely hoped you all enjoyed this RP as much as I have writing it.
