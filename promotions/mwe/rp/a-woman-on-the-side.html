A Woman On The Side??; Anthony Clark RP

Jul 2 2009

Anthony Clark

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The screen opens up to a black screen that reads. Michaels Inc. Production. The screen then fades to black, and then fades open. And you would see an all white living room. The flat screen tv would be on, and you’d see that Evil Dead 2 is on it. There would be a bull dog lying on the couch asleep, as in the background young Jessica Richards would be cooking in the dinner. She would be wearing a pair of pink tight sweat shorts that are very small, and a white tank as she is cooking. And the camera would turn to show Anthony Clark coming down the stairs. Unlike his girlfriend Anthony would be all dressed up. He would be wearing a pair of Affliction jeans with crosses on em, and he would be wearing a very tight button up white Affliction shirt too with his sleeves rolled up. Just like his pants the shirt would have crosses on it as well. He would put on his bandanna as he walks down the steps, and once its on he would put on a white flat billed trucker hat. He would tilt it to the side a bit, and Jess would look at him. Jessica Richards: Where do you think are off to? Anthony Clark: Umm I got umm a photo shoot for MWE to do. Jessica Richards: Without me? I’m at like all your photo shoots with you. Why not this time? Anthony Clark: Umm I guess umm they want these for the pay per view. I’m not really sure babe. I don’t know what else to tell you. Get off my back god! Jessica Richard: Whoa why you getting all offense. All I did was ask a simple question. Anthony Clark: I’m fine ok just relax. Anthony would walk into the other room grabbing his keys. As he does he would be sweating, and be really nervous. He would have a look of panic on his face. But the thing is we don’t know why yet one bit. He’d put his cell phone into his pocket, and walk over to Jessica. Anthony Clark: I’m heading out babe. Later. I’ll be home late, umm me and Brian Michaels are going to the bar afterwards. So don’t wait up for me babe. As Anthony would walk to the door Jess would say. Jessica Richards: Are you sure you don’t want me to go, maybe they made a mistake. Anthony quickly in panic just yells. Anthony Clark: No!... I mean umm babe don’t worry bout it, it’s just one photo shoot. You can be in the next one. After a few minutes, Anthony would finally get out of the house. He would run over to his White Cadillac 09 CTS-V. He would get in, and he’d close the door. Finally the camera would pan into his car while Anthony is driving down the highway. As he is driving his phone would start ringing. He’d answer it. Anthony Clark: Hey baby, no don’t leave I’m on my way. Some things at home were keeping me up don’t worry. I’ll be there soon, you can always count on your man baby. Anthony would hang up the phone, and point to the camera man. Anthony Clark: This is none of Jessica’s business you got that camera man? None of her business. The screen would fade to black, and open up again reading 15 minutes later then it would transition to this scene. We’d see a nice clean bar, and parking outside of it would be Anthony Clark. He’d get out, and fix his hat and his cloths. As he does a girl wearing next to nothing would run up to him and jump onto him wrapping her legs around him. As she does she would shove her tongue into his mouth, and start kissing him. He’d lean back against his car smiling. He’d let her down, and smack her ass. Anthony Clark: Oh Michelle Jordan how I have missed you. I haven’t seen you in like over a year. Damn it is good to see you baby. The two would start kissing, and they’d head into the bar then. They would get some shots of tequila, and go to a table as they wait for the shot to arrive. They would sit on other sides of the table when they talk to one another. Anthony Clark: So what’s new in your life? Michelle Jordan: Well after I stopped managing you in wrestling from way back when, I started modeling for awhile actually. Anthony Clark: Oh yeah? I should come see your work next time. You know I got connections baby. I can probably hook you up with a great modeling job or something. You know how I do babe. Michelle Jordan: How is wrestling going for you? Last I saw was when I was managing you for a short bit, and interviewing you in GWC. Anthony Clark: Well I left that hell hole, and moved to MWE. This place has the best competition Anthony Clark has seen in a long time. But we are going into well my 2nd pay per view here in this company, and this match is make or break for me. Michelle Jordan: What do you mean Anthony? Anthony Clark: Well its for the X-Havok championship. And the thing is, I’m going up against Jay Riddick, and Kraven. Both of which have a victory over yours truly. I don’t know what it is, or what I have to do.... But I need to beat these two. I need to prove that I’m not just some punk kid who has gotten a fair share of flukes in his life. Those titles I had back in the day weren’t bullshit. I need to show I am the true rising star of this company like I have said before. Michelle Jordan: You gotta find what you once had in you. You gotta find that Anthony Clark of old that was once inside of you. The one you left behind. The one that didn’t care about how the fans saw him. The one who didn’t care about his appearance all the time. You gotta find the Anthony Clark who wanted to make his opponent bleed, not the Anthony Clark who makes sure how his hair looks during a match. That’s the Anthony I used to love, and miss. What happened to the Anthony Clark who eliminated his own stable mates, and his life long best friend in a battle royal just for a title shot. What happened to the Anthony Clark who beat up his own trainer in a ladder match for the tag team titles. Anthony Clark: I know what you mean Michelle. I’m starting to feel that hate... That anger I felt then. These two are just like nails on a chalkboard to me. The pin falls they got on me are just haunting me night, and day. What they don’t realize is I’m a man right now who has nothing to lose. If I lose this match I am just back to where I started with these two. I’m back to being the man who can’t beat Kraven, and Jay Riddick. But if I win... Haha I’m going to be on the top. You see Michelle I will be just like Eli Manning. Mr. Clutch. I will come threw in the toughest part of the game, and win. But Jay Riddick haha, he is more like Tony Romo. He can get the wins to get to the show, but the problem is no one care if you can’t win when it matters most of all. Michelle Jordan: Oh. Very true. Jay Riddick sounds familiar. I think I was sitting in bed one night, flipping threw the stations and saw him on tv. Anthony Clark: Queer Eye For The Straight Guy on I guess? Michelle Jordan: Haha, no. He was talking to some guy named Brian Michaels in the ring. Anthony Clark: Haha... I was there to see that. I was watching it in the back. And you know from a business aspect of it Brian was making a right move for Michaels Inc. But from my aspect of it... It wouldn’t of last. You see Jay Riddick wants to be the next Jay Riddick not the next Brian Michaels. That’s all fine, and dandy to me Michelle. I mean it’s fine if he wants to be a life time loser for all of his life. What’s wrong with being the next Brian Michaels? Huh? Being a World Heavyweight Champion. When did that become a bad thing? It’s never been a bad thing. Jay Riddick made the biggest mistake of his life. Michelle Jordan: But wouldn’t he be a good strength for Michaels Inc? Anthony Clark: Haha, I’m going to prove to Michaels that he made the right choice to pick Anthony Clark. I’m going to prove to this world that Riddick made the worst choice in his life by turning down Michaels. He could of gotten the help of the best, instead he rather make it on his own. Haha it’s just sad. It’s like watching the career of Rick James. You know he is on the top, going to be the next big thing in music. And boom failure. He collapse. The only thing for Jay Riddick is, Dave Chappelle isn’t around to make a joke to bring back his career haha. Michelle Jordan: Who did you wrestle last week babe? Anthony Clark: Actually Chris England, and Jay Riddick. You see that match is where Jay Riddick made his other mistake. Jay Riddick could of eliminated me in that match. He could of kept Chris England in so he had easy picking at the Pay Per View but instead he keeps me in. He keeps a man who he knows is hunting for him. He keeps in a man who makes damn sure everyone know he hates him. If it’s some form of reverse psychology. It isn’t working. I’m not worried bout him any longer. Jay Riddick will be like a roach, and I’m just going to step on him. Michelle would laugh as Anthony talks, and under the table you would see her foot rubbing up against his leg some. He would smile at her. Their drink would get to the table after a bit, and they’d start drinking down the shots of tequila. Anthony Clark: So what brought you back to town? Michelle Jordan: Is that really even a question at all? Of corse you. Anthony Clark: Oh really now? Clark would give a cocky grin to her. Michelle Jordan: I mean once I heard you left that Jessica Richards skank, I had to hurry back. Anthony Clark: Umm haha riiiight. Anthony would kinda look away making a grim face, as he is scared he is being caught up in his lie. Finally he admits to her then. Anthony Clark: Look I haven’t left Jessica just yet ok. I’m working on that right now. It’s just kinda tough. You know we live together. So give me some time. But you know when it comes to all of this, you are all I think about babe. Anthony would wink at her, and she would giggle some then. Michelle Jordan: So what’s this other guy you are wrestling at this pay per view. Anthony Clark: Oh good ol Nic Kraven. Michelle Jordan: What’s so special about this guy babe? Anthony Clark: That’s the thing he isn’t special. He is nothing more then a clown to me. A joke. He is just like every typical guy I see in wrestling these days. These dark edgy guys. You know the ones who hear voices, the kind of guy who is “nuts”. I’m not afraid of this guy one bit. I have faced five billion guys just like him. I took of Mark Evil in XHF. I have fought Creeping Death in the GWC, and I have wrestled Scott Pillman in the EWA. Hell I took on Blight in MWE also. All I ever see anymore are these dark edgy kind of guys in wrestling. I’m looking for something new. Something fresh. I mean how many promos can people cut about “darkness”. These guys think they are dark like those goth kids in the episode of South Park when Stan goes goth haha. Michelle would start giggling when he says that. Michelle Jordan: That’s my favorite episode. Anthony Clark: Mine too haha. But I mean I’m tired of these eye liner freaks. I mean this guy wears more eye liner then you babe. It’s sick. Is he trying to be like Pete Wentz or something? I don’t know but it doesn’t make much sense to me at all. Is he trying to be a girl or something? People these days... It’s guys like this that these fans cheer for. It’s guys like this they want champion, and it boggles my mind. Nothing is normal about this guy. He is like a mix of a Marilyn Manson, and Milo off the cartoon the Oblong’s. So what he has pinned me before. That doesn’t make me feel any different bout this match. He is the one that has something to lose. I have nothing to lose at all. And do you know what that makes me baby? Michelle would smile at him. Michelle Jordan: Dangerous, Just how I like you. Anthony Clark: You’re damn right baby. If I lose I’m right back to where I started. Title-less. And you know what they say the sun will shine another day. Well I blew my first chance against Nic Kraven, shame on me. But this time I will not let this chance slip threw my finger. I’m not going to let this get by me. I have let opportunities slip by before, and none worse then this last one. This is personal for me. This is going to show that I am just as good as I said I am. Michelle Jordan: You are babe, I know you will. You are always Mr. Clutch. You come through like none other. Anthony Clark: If Brian has taught me anything since joining Michaels Inc it’s don’t take anything for granted. I took my last match for granted, as I said I blew it. But this time I plan to walk out of that ring holding the X-Havok title over my head in victory. I don’t care if I have to bring Ultraviolence to the MWE like I did to the GWC to win this title. I’ll do it again. I have no problem bloodying some people up for my success. This is the match for Anthony Clark. This is the match for him to prove he is more then just a pretty face. This is time for Anthony Clark to prove his is truly dangerous. Nic can call all the voices in his head, he can be as crazy as he wants to be. It’s not going to do him any damn good haha. Anthony, and Michelle would both laugh. Michelle Jordan: So since we have caught up, I think we should go back to my place to do some training. Anthony Clark: Well what moves do you have to show me? Michelle Jordan: Just a few you haven’t seen in a while out of me haha. Anthony’s eyes widen, and loudly he would yell over to the bar. Anthony Clark: Check! The screen would slowly fade to black then. The End.
