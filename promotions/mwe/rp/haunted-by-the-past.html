Haunted by the Past

Jun 5 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The scene opens up inside the Librería Cuesta Bookstore, Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic. The camera spins round and goes down one of the isles of the bookstore, as it gets to the end a big cue of one hundred plus people becomes visible, each person in the cue waiting eagerly with book in hand waiting to get it signed, the camera zooms in on the book to reveal the front cover, The book is The life and times of a True English Patriot by Chris England, as the camera works its way down the cue a table becomes visible at the end of it, sat behind that table is Chris England, he is casualy dressed in a smart black shirt and a pair of blue jeans. England is all smiles and can be seen laughing and joking with the fans he is signing signatures for. Twenty minutes go by and the cue is now going down, England is no longer laughing and joking with fans, instead this has been replaced by a thoroughly professional approach, not looking up to see the fans he is signing the books for until after he has signed the book, then shaking there hands and wishing them a good day, this approach is making the cue move a great deal faster, up steps the next fan, a female in her mid to late twenties, England doesn’t look up, he just grabs a hold of the book and opens the front page and readies himself to sign it. **England** Who’s it to? England picks up his marker pen and hovers it over the page ready to sign. The female is staring intently at England almost begging him to look up but he doesn’t. **Female** Its to Daniel and could you sign it lots of love from daddy. England methodically starts to sign the book speaking out loud as he does so. **England** To Daniel lots of love from...... Hang on one second did you just say daddy? A puzzled look creeps across England’s face, England looks up and as he does so the look turns from a puzzled one to a horrified one, the female he see’s standing before him is none other than Jasmine Banks, England’s former fiancée Jasmine Banks, the colour from England’s face begins to drain and beads of anxious sweat start to roll down his brow. *******************OOC**************** Jasmine Banks Rep Is... **Jasmine** Yes Chris that’s exactly what I said, and yes its true you have a son and his name is Daniel. Jasmine looks calm and relaxed, she knows that she dropped a bombshell on Chris but feels a great sense of relief that she has finally told Chris he is a dad. England just sits completely still for a few moments in total and utter shock, he takes his hand and rubs it across his face as he gathers his thoughts, he steps up out of his chair and looks directly into Jasmine’s eyes, as he does so he remembers the pain and agony he suffered at the hands of Jasmine, as he remembers this the anger starts to build up inside him, his pale face starts to quickly pick up colour as it starts to turn red, then he finally explodes speaking angrily at Jasmine. **England** What the hell are you playing at Jasmine, I haven’t seen you for five years, then suddenly just as I’m starting to get my life on track and for the first time in my life actually trying to do the right thing, you turn up and drop this fucking bombshell on me, your just the fucking same as you always where, running round like a fucking hurricane trying to destroy everything in your path, why now, why is it now that you choose to tell me this shit in a bookstore full of my fans, the people i owe so much to! Jasmine looks far from happy at the way Chris has just spoke to her, to be frank she looks rathered pissed off! She is standing with her hands on her hips and shakes her head in disgust before speaking angrily in reply. **Jasmine** Why now? Well I would of told you five fucking years ago if you hadn’t just disappeared without a trace! You just picked up and left you changed your phone number, closed your email account, the only fucking reason I found you is because you turned up in the city where I live and your face is all over the place on posters for this damn book signing, and you have the nerve to accuse me of trying to destroy you! You walked out on me Chris you left me alone and pregnant with your kid, your the one that destroys everything! England goes immediately on the defence trying to defend his actions all those years ago. **England** What did you expect Jas you were having an affair with my fucking boss! Did you really expect me to stick around and stay with you after that and besides how do I know that it’s even my kid!!! you could cut the tension with a knife, by now all the people in the cue have started to push forward and are forming a circle around Chris and Jasmine, some fans are even using their cell phones to record the argument. Jasmine shakes her head in anger at Chris’s coments and looks really angry. **Jasmine** Do you really think I would be here unless I knew for sure that Daniel is your son, think of me what you like Chris, but i would never intentionally go out of my way to hurt you i... England swiftly interrupts Jasmine, butting in aggressively and angrily now shouting. **England** THEN WHY THE FUCK DID YOU SLEEP WITH WARREN! Jasmine rolls her eyes and is almost shacking with fury at how Chris even after all these years still blames it all on her, the anger is very evident in the tone of her voice. **Jasmine** BECAUSE HE WAS THERE AND YOU WERNT! You can blame me all you like, but the truth is it was all your fault, you were too busy with your career that you forgot all about me, you devoted all your time to the fucking wrestling business and you were barely ever home, even when you were at home you were never really with me, you would just sit there in the damn basement drinking beer and watching videos of some loser you would be wrestling at the next show! England takes a deep breath and sigh’s, he hangs his head rather ashamedly as he thinks back to those days, he realises she’s right he did spend all his time on wrestling related matters and he never did give her the love and attention he really should of, he takes a deep breath calming himself down and then begins to speak in a much more calmer fashion. **England** Look us throwing insults at each other left right and centre isn’t getting us anywhere, why don’t we go somewhere a bit more private so we can talk about this properly. Jasmine realises Chris’s attempts to calm the situation and follows suit, taking a few deep breaths to calm herself down. **Jasmine** Yea ok where you got in mind. **England** There’s a room out back they were letting me use as a dressing room we can go there. **Jasmine** Ok. England waves his arms signalling to signal Jasmine to follow him, he leans over and tells the event organiser to apologise for the displays just seen and tell the fans he will be signing no more autographs. England and Jasmine walk off through the bookstore together in total silence, they make their way through the aged corridors of the bookstore before they finally enter his dressing room area. The room is dark and dingy the walls have think brown damp stains on them and all that is in the room is 2 chairs and a really age beaten old dressing table with a mirror built into it, the mood the room sets is rather coincidental as the pair are already in a dark dingy mood to start with. England and Jasmine sit down on the two chairs and England leans forward and starts to speak in a rather emotionally drained and downbeat tone. **England** Look Jas I’m sorry about how I reacted back there I really am. **Jasmine** Its ok I was kinda expecting it anyway. The pair just sit there for a minute or two in total silence just staring at eachother, neither one of them sure of what to say, England opens his mouth a few times and goes to speak but then stops himself before finally 30 seconds or so latter speaking. **England** So does he look like me? Jasmine smiles realising Chris is now starting to accept the fact he is a dad, she speaks more like her normal bubbly self. **Jasmine** Oh My God yes, he is the spitting image of you, just looking at him sometimes brings back memories of all the fun we had together. Chris cracks a smile which gets wider and wider as he remembers all the wild and fun times that him and Jasmine shared in the past. **England** Those were the days huh, it was just you and me against the world doing whatever we felt like, man I miss those days. Jasmine smiles back at Chris as just for a brief it seems as if the whole world has stoped still as the pair remember all the good times they shared. **Jasmine** You know there ain’t any reason why we can’t have more good times together, me you and Daniel. Chris seems to be in deep thought for a few minutes as he is contemplating what Jasmine is inferring. **England** Maybes sometime in the far future, but as of right now this is going to take a hell of a lot of getting used to. **Jasmine** So you’re going to be in our lives then? **England** Of course I am Jas he is my son, my flesh and blood, and I’ve got so many years catching up to do with him. **Jasmine** So you want to come meet him? **England** What now I can’t i have a match Tonight and I have a lot of preparations to make. Jasmine takes in a deep breath and lets out a loud sigh, suddenly reminded of how she always had to take a back seat to wrestling and to her it seems like nothing has changed, she replys in a rather sarcastic and bitchy tone. **Jasmine** Yeah of course you have, its always been the same with you, wrestling always takes priority, so i guess there’s nothing new there. **England** No no you’ve got it all wrong, I want to be there for you this time, this time I’m going to do things the right way, but wrestling is the only thing I’m good at and now I have a son to provide for, so doing my job and earning my wage becomes even more important. Jasmine shows a look of surprise on her face, somewhat shocked at the commitment England seems willing to make to helping with the upbringing of there son. **Jasmine** I’m surprised at you Chris you really do seem like your trying to change for the better, would you like me to leave so you can prepare for your match? England picks up a pen and a bit of paper of the dresser in front of him, he writes down his hotel telephone number and extension and hands it to Jasmine. **England** Here’s my Hotel number give me a call tomorrow morning and we will meet up and i can come see Daniel, but yeah right now i could do with having some time alone to take in all this stuff and prepare for my match. **Jasmine** Ok I will leave you to it. Both Jasmine and Chris stand up they look at each other awkwardly then share an awkward hug for a few seconds before Jasmine leaves the room, England then sits on his seat and takes a deep breath and try’s to take in what has just happened. *******5 Hours Later********** MWE’s Monday Night Mayhem is now underway but is in commercial break, the camera’s are focused in on Chris England, who is now dressed in his ring attire of red and white English flag trunks and union jack wrestling boots, he is also wearing a black True English Patriot t-Shirt, England is stood in front of the camera waiting to address the MWE nation. it is five hours since he found out he was a dad and it still hasn’t sunk in, he has thought of nothing but meeting his son for the first time ever since he arrived at the arena, but now he must focus his mind on his job, his career as he knows that he now has a son he needs to provide for. The camera man begins to motion to England with his fingers counting down from 10 till when the camera’s go live all across the world. England takes a deep breath and steady’s himself getting ready to speak to the MWE world. 8..7..6..5..4..3..2..1... **England** So here i am back in MWE once more and only a matter of minutes away from making my in ring return, by now I’m sure you all will of heard about the altercation that I got into today at my book signing, now I wont go into the details because that’s personal, but I would like to personally apologise to any fans that didn’t get the opportunity to have their book signed. England gathers himself and clears his mind ready to get on with the serious business of talking about his return to MWE. Now then on to the matter at hand, tonight I make my in ring return against Peter K, a impressive young upstart who is making big waves in the wrestling industry, a young man who no doubt has a very big career ahead of him, now I could stand here and rant about how I’m in the best shape of my life, I could tell you all how I’m going to dismantle Peter one bit at a time, but I’m not going to do that, because you see I may be the best physical condition I have ever been in, but we all know that when you’re on a losing streak that counts for nothing. As England says nothing he emphasises the point by crossing his hands then spreading his arms out wide, he then takes a step forward towards the camera. See lately I have been on the worst run of defeats I have suffered in my career, this lead me to re-evaluate my approach to life, both in and out of the ring, you see for as long as I can remember I seem to of done all the wrong things, for all the wrong reasons. Tonight all that changes, tonight you will all witness the rebirth of The True English Patriot, you see now i see what is truly important, I see that the people I really owe my performances to are the fans, after all without the fans there would be no wrestling industry in the first place. Where I have gone wrong in the past is its always been about me, for years i was so confident in my own abilities that I forgot to pay any attention to the opponent that stood in front of me, because of that I had effectively lost the match before i even got in the ring, tonight that’s just not going to happen, i have studied hours and hours of tapes of Peters matches and I’ve got to say I’m pretty damn impressed, but just because I’m impressed with his ability doesn’t mean I’m impressed with him as a person, cause quite frankly I think this man’s attitude is appalling. England shakes his head from side to side rolling his eyes to emphasise his disgust at peters attitude. Now I’m sure your all thinking this is a bit hypocritical coming from, as we all know that for a long time my attitude wasn’t exactly great, but that was then and this is now and Peter very much reminds me of how I used to be, in all the tapes I’ve seen of him he seems to do nothing but bitch about the fact that he isn’t in the main event, he seems to be constantly calling out higher level opponents. This is the reason he reminds me of myself, he is so fixated on the fact that he feels his abilities are at a higher level than the opponents he has been booked against, he totally forgets to study up on his opponent, now that’s fine with me because that plays right into my hands, You see Peter if you come into the ring tonight underprepared for the challenge that lays ahead of you, unprepared to go toe to toe with one of the best pure wrestlers this industry has ever seen, then my friend you are going to find yourself well and truly wanting. England lets out a confident smirk as his confidence in his own abilities seems to be flowing at the maximum level. Tonight Mr K your going to step into the ring with the big boys, tonight I am going to school you in the fine art of wrestling. Unlike anyone you have ever faced before i will push you to the absolute limit, I will make sure you are in the fight of your life. Win or lose tonight I guarantee every single wrestling fan all over the world, Tonight you will all see my True potential, Tonight i will repay all the support i have received from the fans since my return, This one is for all the True pure wrestling fans out there. England has a stern and serious look on his face, he looks fired up for the match more so than any match he has ever been in, he is determined to prove his worth to the fans and most of all he is determined to start his ascent up the wrestling industry ladder once more. See you in the ring Peter and you better bring your A game because you can bet your arse im bringing mine! England smiles at the camera a confident smile before turning and walking off down the corridor, the camera fades to black and the scene comes to a close as England confidently makes his way to the ring.
