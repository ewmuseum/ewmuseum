Hungry for Pizza; ...And a First Match

Jul 28 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
QUOTE Mayhem Wrestling Entertainment, or MWE as it is almost always known as, has recently open up it's doors once again. However, this time many of the once famed and fortunate originals and regulars are gone. Of course a few have returned and will continue to make an impact, but this generation of MWE is filled with new superstars. New blood is always key in any profession to keep things going, to keep things interesting, and to keep things fresh. No longer will the ranks be dominated by David Whitman, Brian Michaels, Sinc Mercier, Mason Resoan, and D.C. No, in this new generation of MWE it is time for the young guns of the world to replace the already decorated MWE legends. This isn't to say the likes of Eric Derf, Zack Crash, Zak Warner, and Ace Cannon are going to be ignored. They all still have plenty of life in them and plenty of will to keep going, but no fan, employee, or wrestler can deny the fact that the new guys are the majority now. No cliques, no clans, no alliances...everything is...fresh. People like Jack Lupin, Alexis Black, Zack Evermore, and James Preston are here to not only make impact, but make people forget about the old and recognize the new. MWE is home to people of all sizes, shapes, heritages, and backgrounds and as MWE starts new, so does Craig Zachery. THREE MONTHS PREVIOUS *Craig Zachery is seen sitting on a bench on the Brooklyn Boardwark, a rather trashy boardwalk compared to some of the beaches he is used to by his apartment down in south Jersey, in nothing but DC clothing from his hat to his shoes. He lies lazily against the back of this bench sighing, occasionally looking up to see some of the old couples that normally inhabit a beach like this. Craig is a rather tolerant person, not usually one to judge, but in this particular instance he couldn't help but look in awe at some of the people in bathing suits that frankly should not be. However, he doesn't really want to be up here right now. Being a Jersey boy for majority of his life, he was constantly in New York for various reasons and he got quite accustomed to just visiting either Long Island or Manhattan and anything else felt foreign. However, his girlfriend Crystal and he were both on a day trip up to the New York Aquarium and the Bronx Zoo. Having already tackled the massiveness that was the Bronx Zoo and being almost completely finished with the New York Aquarium, Zachery was getting rather ancy to get out of what he'd consider foreign territory. It was a relief for him to finally see Crystal walking towards him from the back entrance of the aquarium, as he sprang out of his seat to go walk next to her.* Craig: So, how was the show? Crystal: Ya know, it was alright...but it was more for like little kids. Sea lions are cute as hell though! Craig: Ha, sea lions are pretty cute I guess...but you know what is even better than being cute? *Crystal shrugged her shoulders waiting to see what he'd he say.* Craig: I'd say being relaxed is probably the best thing right now...I mean, it is like 103 degrees outside. *Craig wiped his head a little bit with his shirt, and the sweat from his forehead flew off like a waterfall crashing off the side of a cliff.* Crystal: Are you really that eager to get home? Craig: Kinda, it's like...super fuckin' hot outside right now and Wildwood's beach sounds sooo damn good. *Crystal grabbed Craig's hand and continued to walk in stride, thinking the idea over in her head for a bit. It was pretty hot, but she loved New York and it's atmosphere. Reluctantly she went along with Craig's idea though, as it probably was for the best. Crystal: Alright, it sounds good...but I ain't drivin all the way down there this time. Here, you take the keys. *she quickly tossed the keys from her purse to his hands* I'm just gonna chill, cause if I drive when we get home I'm goin' the hell to sleep. *Zachery caught the keys and gave them a jingle as they approached their car, nodding in approval and willing to drive. It was a good four hour drive down to their house in south Jersey, but at least they made the day worth while. Waking up at six o'clock and making it to NY by 10, they had been in New York for a good five hours. By the time they got home, it'd be nice and cool and just getting dim...basically perfect conditions to just lounge on the beach. Craig opened the door, put the key into the ignition, and started up his white 2005 Lincoln Aviator. He closed the door as Crystal got in, put the AC up to full blast, and checked his phone, looking at his recent calls. He drummed a few seconds on his steering wheel, placed his phone in his pocket, and drove out of the parking lot.* Craig: So, I got a call from my gym today. Crystal: Oh yeah, what about? Craig: Well, I've been talkin' real close lately to my personal trainer who knows the owner of the gym real well and apparently he's been talkin to some people too. Crystal: Alright, I'm with ya. Craig: So, basically one thing led to another and there is this professional wrestling company called Mayhem Wrestling Entertainment, they used to be real big like...late 07' early 08' I think. Yeah, well apparently they went under, but recently got bought out and are lookin for new people to fill up the rosters. Crystal: Isn't all that shit like fake and stuff though? Craig: Well, yeah kind of, or at least that's what I thought too. It's apparently still pretty physically demanding and all that, but my whole thing was ya know money and stuff. My trainer really wants me to look into this. Crystal: Well, like how much money are we looking at? *Craig tilted his head back as he sighed, stretched, and looked ahead out the window. A bridge toll was comin' up and he took out his wallet and looked inside. There were a few 20s, three 10s, a five, and four singles.* Craig: Basically, enough to fill this like...every week. Crystal: Well fuck, I mean, you ain’t never done shit like this before but I mean that's serious money. Craig: Yeah, but it eats away at your time and I have to constantly hit the gym to keep up a certain physique they want me to keep. Not that I don't like the gym or anything, it's just...I'm not to sure. I've never done anything like this before, but my trainer seriously won't leave me alone about this. *The car went silent for a little bit while they both rolled the idea back in their heads. All of the sudden, Crystal opened her mouth.* Crystal: Well...what are you thinkin? *Craig hooked left and took a ramp up onto the Brooklyn Bridge as he continued to drive. After a few seconds he runs his hand over his head and rests it on his neck.* Craig: I'm thinking money, exposure, and a shit load of traveling... *Crystal goes to say something, but he wasn't done.* Craig: ...but I'm also thinkin about public opinion and my time. There's just one last factor. Crystal: And what's that? Craig: You... *The car goes silent again as Craig takes a quick glance to see her reaction. She seems puzzled and a little nervous, but simply stays quiet. Slouching into her chair, she puts her hand on her cheek as she looks out the window thinking of all the ups and downs of if her boyfriend were to pursue a career in this field. And while the thoughts race back and forth like a NASCAR track, the drive home just had begun... -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- PRESENT DAY *With his feet up on the railing of his seaside condo balcony, Craig Zachery takes in the almost unfamiliar scent of the Atlantic Ocean. For the last several months he has been actively taking trips up to Toms River, NJ for some mixed martial arts training. Being a wrestler in high school, Zachery had some experience in grappling and physical competition, but was extremely rusty. Granted, he had only been out of school for five years, that's still long enough to lose your touch. In Toms River Zachery found a great camp that works with many great MMA fighters and he signed up, being sponsored by his local gym and his employer, Mayhem Wrestling Entertainment. CZ, as he sometimes liked to be called, wound up following through with the opportunity of joining this once massive company, but although Craig consistently lifted and was in generally good shape, it wasn't enough. Toms River was a great place to develop the right body, discipline, and conditioning needed for MWE. However, as Zachery had predicted, it was taking up a large chunk of his time. Now, after the inaugural show of MWE's third stint, the three months of work and time CZ put in at Toms River was going to be put to good use. However that wasn't until next Monday so it was time to rest. With his feet still up he just looked at the ocean in peace, not even realizing Crystal had opened up the sliding glass doors behind him.* Crystal: Hey. Craig: 'Sup? Crystal: Way to steal my question. *Craig chuckles and takes his legs off the railing to spin and face Crystal in his seat.* Crystal: What's on your mind? *Craig ponders this for a few brief seconds and can't think of anything.* Craig: Just relaxing. Tryin not to think about much. Crystal: Well, I guess that's good, but hey I was thinking, you want to take a walk on the boardwalk? *Looking directly into her eyes, Craig plays around with the thought into his head before giving it a shake.* Craig: Sounds fun, but I don't know, I'm just kind of not feelin it right now. Crystal: Alright, that's cool and all, but what do you want to do? Craig: Well, I've just kind of been relaxing, and I didn't really think we were gonna do anything. Crystal: Yeah, but aren't you a little bored? *Craig simply shrugs his shoulders.* Crystal: How don't you know if you're bored or not? Craig: I don't know, I'm not really having the time of my life or anything, but I've just been kinda drained lately. Crystal: Yeah, but we haven't done anything in like a month except have sex and lounge around on the couch. I mean, it's summer time and we live on the shore. *Craig thinks about it for a bit and tries to remember exactly what he has done for the last couple of weeks, except for train. After a few moments of silence, he realizes she's right, and tries to think of something to tell her.* Craig: Here....how about this. Tomorrow, we'll go to the beach early and then the boardwalk tomorrow? *Crystal sighs in disappoint, thinking that Craig would take her out tonight.* Crystal: Alright, if you really don't want to go out tonight, it's cool...I guess. But tomorrow night we're going out or you won't see any of this *she hovers her hand around her vagina* for a week, got me? *Craig gets a little scared and nods jokingly. He pulls her in, gives her a kiss, and then smacks her on her ass as she walks back into the house. As he watches her plop on the couch he looks at his phone and she's that it's already around 7 PM. Scrolling through his contacts he spots Dominos. He calls them up, orders a small bacon pie and some cheesy bread and places his phone in his pocket. He puts his feet back up onto the railing and relaxes for the next hour waiting for his pizza to arrive.* ???: Hey! *Craig shifts around.* ???: HEY! *Craig moves around again.* ???: Hey guy, I got your pizza! *Craig rubs his eyes and looks down over the balcony to see a delivery man from Dominos looking up at him. Apparently he dozed off and his pizza got to him already.* Craig: Alright, just uhhh...leave the pizza on that bench. I'll be down in a second. Delivery Boy: Sure, but sir, I need your money. *Craig lifts his head up again and groans. He reaches into his back pocket, finds a twenty dollar bill, and leans over the railing. He drops it to the ground below, and the delivery guy picks it up.* Delivery Boy: Sir do you... Craig: Don't worry about it, I'm to fucking lazy to get that right now, keep the change. *Delivery boy shrugs and pockets the money before getting into his car and driving off. CZ looks over the railing and sighs as he dozes back to sleep again for another half an hour or so.* *Upon waking up Craig finds himself in a state of hunger. Remembering that he ordered pizza he walked through his living room, with Crystal now gone, and walks down two flights of stairs to the place where the pizza man left his pie. Looking around for it, he can't find it.* Craig: Awesome. *Again he looks around, even going back to where the delivery man was standing and still, no pizza is to be found. Two seconds later he turns and spots a box sitting next to an overfilled trash can. He picks it up and smells the scent of bacon. Taking a look to his left he spots a smaller box in the pile of garbage and opens it to see an unopened cup of marinara sauce and crumbs of what smells to be cheesy bread.* Craig: Fucking perfect. *He walks back across the street and looks at the time on his phone now. It's currently 9:30, meaning he slept a lot longer than his intended half an hour nap. He looks outside and he realizes that it is a bit darker, but all the lights make it hard to tell. Sitting on a bench in front of his house he runs his hands over his head.* Craig: Seriously, fucking perfect. *He again looks at his phone and sifts through some pictures, littered recently with pictures of himself as he documented his body's progress through his recent training. He certainly has improved, but he still can't tell if he is ready for Mayhem.* Craig: How is it that after all this work I'm still unsure of myself. Seriously, three months of work and I still can't grasp on this. *He takes a glance at his arms and sees they've got a bit bigger and pulls himself up to see a four pack and the beginning of a six pack.* Craig: I mean, I know it isn't the best, but it's better than most people. Seriously, I just need to grow a pair of balls. *Craig shakes his head a little in disappointment.* Craig: Ya know, I think it's because I'm facing that James Preston dude. He has like perfect abs and like really super defined arms...but fuck it I guess. *Craig looks at the sky taking a deep breath before fixing his seating on his chair.* Craig: Ya know, when I joined MWE and they sent me to Toms River, I was confused as hell. I looked at myself in the mirror and was like, "Hey, I'm a pretty buff guy, I don't need all this work," and looking at you Preston, I see why. *Zachery looks down at the ground now, but lifts his head for a brief second as a dog begins to bark a little down the street.* Craig: I've never actually met someone in my life that claimed to be perfect or even had the audacity to say somethin like that, but when you say it and then I look at you I can kind of see why. But just because you look strong doesn't necessarily mean you are. *Craig now stands up and walks a little down the street following the barking faintly, but mainly, he's just stretching his legs a little bit.* Craig: Now I debut this week and I know they say a lot rides on first impressions. I've heard so many people who work behind the scenes at MWE talk about how it's about going out there and giving it your all, but to be honest, I feel like it's a bit more than that. I feel like it's more of sending a message that you're in the business to win, not just put on a great show. And ya know, this Preston guy I think shares that idea. *Craig turns the corner and looks down Pacific Avenue, a very large street. He sees a pizzeria around six blocks down and checks to see if his wallet is still on him. It is.* Craig: But I know people don't really like that guy, so I don't really know if that is a good thing or a bad thing. I do know that I want to show people that I can hang, cause it seems like this Preston dude is just gonna try to shut me down. Cause I've seen a movie or two in my day, and I think that he's just gonna try and make it look like there is no way he can lose, and I don't want none of that shit. *Craig is now around three blocks away from the pizzeria and he already takes out his wallet.* Craig: See, I don't want my matches to be predictable or anything, because I'm not an old fuck or anything...I remember me in high school. I always had to have something unique when I hung out with people, and in a way, I feel the same about this. I don't want to be the cliche underdog who is fighting for his first win cause to be honest, that's bullshit. If there's one thing I know about this JP guy is that it he wants people to believe I can't win and he wants me to believe I can't win...and while I might not be super confident or anything, I'm not gonna let him get his way, cause that's boring. I want James Preston to look at me as an equal and even though he won't, as a kind of favor for me as a newcomer, I'd like that. Cause if he looks at me like an equal, then I'll look at him like an equal. If he goes and tries to make me look bad, then I'm just gonna think the guy's an asshole and I've never even talked to him before. Ya see, I like to win, I'm a kinda competitive guy, but I know when I watch my movies or my sports shit is always best when two guys go at it and are equal, not when one guy is just way better than his opponent. *Craig arrives at the pizzeria but realizes it's closed so he puts his wallet away. He continues to walk down as he sees other signs for other possibly open restaurants or pizzerias, cause he's hungry.* Craig: And if we both think we are equal to each other then we'll wrestle like equals. And if we both wrestle like equals we'll put on a hell of a show and that's like two birds with one stone. A good first impression and we get to entertain the fans. And again, I figure this guy is gonna tell me he doesn't care what the fans think, and kudos to him, all the more power to ya. But to be honest, this isn't necessarily about the fans just yet, because I don't even think I have fuckin' fans yet. *Craig chuckles to himself as he realizes just how low he is starting.* Craig: Nah, this is for me...at least for now. I want to come in on a good note, win or lose, and if he makes this match boring, then I'll come in on a bad note. I mean, I've done some stupid shit before and I know that sometimes I let my brawn takeover and I don't want that to happen in my first match...but I know myself better than anyone else knows it, and I know it will happen like a chain reaction. He'll belittle me, I'll get angry, we'll wrestle and I'll let brawn take over brains, and it'll be a bad note to step in on. *Craig spots around 15 blocks away a Dominos pizza sign and gets a little excited as the hungry truly has sunk in by now.* Craig: I don't want that, MWE doesn't want that, and I doubt the fans want that. And I won't use that as an excuse, cause that's one of those things Toms River has taught me...excuses get you no where. So on Mayhem when we fight, I don't want you to say your head hurt or Alexis Black distracted you. I don't want anything like that... All I want is a fun, competitive, interesting first match. Give that to me James Preston and we'll have no problems and I'll leave you alone. Strip me of that privilege and I'll remember it for the next time we face. When I was a kid, I did stupid shit that'll I'll never forget. As an adult, you can be damn sure that I won't forget anything, stupid or not. *Craig spits on the side of the road as he closely approaches the Dominos he so badly wanted earlier.* Craig: So I'm coming into MWE as a man with no extremes or anything like that. I'm coming in as a man who just wants a good first match and I'm just gonna let MWE take me from there. Just like in life, I just kind of go with the flow and I do with what I'm dealt, what I can. I'm coming in with no loses and no wins against a man who claims to be perfect. So what I say to you is let's have me a perfect first match. A perfect first memory. And more than anything a perfect start to MWE. *Craig makes it to the front door of the Dominos and puts his hands on the glass as he sees it's still open.* Craig: Cause if you don't come in prepared to give me that, then for the benefit of all around, I will make sure that you live up to your perfect persona in one way, by giving you the most perfect loss of your career. James Preston I'm pumped for our match...just make sure you are too. *With that Craig pushes into the pizzeria to fill his stomach full of energy as his long two week wait for his match begins.* OOC: It feels good to be back RPing and just like most, this hopefully will help to get the ball rolling.
