QUOTE OOC: For those who are unfamiliar with this, Dark Blue is Ivory, Purple is Warner Part III Sun filtered through a partially opened curtain, the frequent attack on the shadows of the night. An explosion of sun, brought on by Zak throwing back the thin black fabric, broke apart the leftover calm of sleep and brought in a fresh warmth that only nature can create. He quickly gazed around the room, his room, as the light sunk into its contents. Nothing jumped out at him. Just a double bed, sleek black metal, two bedside tables and a painting floating above. A landscape, mountain range behind a field. Black and white, a tinge of pale yellow creeping over the hills, and streaks from through the clouds. A barely noticeable purple too? Could just be the light. Something in Zak’s stomach tried to get to it. He didn’t understand why. To tired to concern himself too much, and without enough knowledge to even try, he turned to a set of doors in the wall. He swung them open, clothes resting behind them. Taking them all in and wondering if they were his. He assumed, or hoped, they were. “Well, if they’re not mine, at least they fit.” he thought as he made his way through the rest of the apartment. He hoped that the new day would help the place recall some long forgotten memories he was sure he had, but found no clues, no images. Just a collection of furniture, some clothes and an array of photo frames without photos. And of course, Cory sleeping on the couch, a trace of drool swaying on the couch in tune with his breathing. Staring at him with what could be considered either minute contempt or irked intrigue, Zak spread the living room blinds, sending a river of sun crashing into Cory’s tightly shut eyes, wrenching them closed with even more force. Slowly and painfully, he awoke, groans and growls replacing the English language for a moment of two, but he managed to regain control of his vocal cords. “God man, did you have to do that? I mean, man… trying to sleep here dude.” Zak continued to allow the hated rays into the room while Cory lost himself in the trance of the tired, but not for to long. “Hey… Zak right? Yeah, Zak. Any food in here? Know it’s a long shot, but the stray canned good could’ve lasted this long.” No reply. “Yeah, good point. Looks like we’ll be eating out, my shout, seeing as you let me crash here.” Zak continued around the apartment, dodging white sheets and looking at the nothing that made up the various ledges, shelves and mantels. He paused in front of a bookshelf. “Com’on, no point hanging around here when food awaits out there.” Heading to the door, Cory turned to find Zak hadn’t moved anything but his neck as he searched the spines before him. He sighed with defeat. “Looks like I’m waiting.” he thought as Zak’s darting eyes stopped, resting on something that cut through the dust laden collection. “This book’s new…” he muttered. “You say something?” Zak didn’t even hear him, he already had the fresh cover in his hand. The title screamed at him, ’Memories Long Forgotten’, with no sign of an author to lay claim to them. “No, of course you didn’t. What was I thinking?” Cory’s voice trailed away, the sound of possibility overriding it. With tender trepidation Zak thumbed open the cover. He scanned the first page. Nothing. The second. Nothing. Desperate now. The third, the forth, the sixtieth. All the same. Just the hope of creation a blank page usually holds, but instead crushing disappointment. He flicked to the last page. Empty as despair, of course. Zak went to hurl the book to the hard floor when something caught his eye. On the inside of the back cover, the corner. Writing. Five little words. Five words that held so much power. They laughed into Zak’s ears. 'From the pen of Zak.' echoed in his mind, stinging it mercilessly. Rage took over. Rage and hatred. The book was airborne. A wall soon put an end to that. Cory watched the entire time. “You… you okay Zak?” Zak had lost himself in his thoughts. “A taunt… just a fucking taunt. A FUCKING taunt…” He stared at the still intact piece of hateful pages, the disgusting literature that doesn’t exist. Cory followed the sight of Zak, quickly giving the half crumpled book a look before resting his hand on the shoulder of an enraged one. “You… you wanna talk about it?” “Who? Who’s doing this? WHO?” he thought, still oblivious to the man trying to comfort him. “Zak? You there? Come on, you’re starting to scare me… Zak?” Cory tapped his palm on his shoulder blade, trying to break the trance somewhat. “Zak?!” With a start, Zak looked to Cory with distant irises. He turned to walk away. “Hey… you right?” All he got in response was a deep breath. Cory slowly began walking, not sure what to say or if he should say anything at all. “If you don’t mind me asking… I’ve always thought it was better to talk about things rather then bottle things up… what was in the book? What pissed you off so much?” Zak stopped at the door, his hand trying to crush the knob. “Nothing.” With that, he left the apartment, leaving Cory to ponder the answer. “Had to be something to get a response like that…” He looked back over to the mangled cover and pages being left for dead and followed after Zak, his anger centre in his own mind. ____________________________________________________________________ Excuse me. But first let me ask the question… If someone talks, who’s existence isn’t recognized by either the one he’s addressing or those around him…does he make a sound? And the answer, which should consist of an astounding in depth reasoning to the monsters conclusion… No. Hmmm… Guess that question was fairly cut and dry, don't you say? Oh well, get we might as well get down to business... The scene to be set is one that is unusual for Ivory to be in for a promo. The normal settings he’s involved in are always unique in one regard or another. And even if some may seem to be a bit basic, there’s always a twist to the environment that he provides that makes it stand out. Making it more than a dark room. More than a basement. More than a field. His attitude. His aura. It all intertwines within the area he’s occupying and makes it stand out. But this…there is no effort in this. Or is there? An empty arena. So generic. Ugh. And while it’s in front of thousands of fans in attendance and millions at home… Your voice falls on deaf ears. Ivory lets out a bit of a chuckle before continuing. I’d take that as disrespect… Another chuckle …but then again… Why should I care when nobody else does? Deaf ears, Shawn. Deaf ears. How.. Word after word, after word. I wish we could dance.
