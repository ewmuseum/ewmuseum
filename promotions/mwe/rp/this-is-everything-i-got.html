PART 1 - Coming to Terms A MWE camera begins to blur out a distant, blant, black image. The picture comes to perfect image but it is still very dark, and very dull. It appears to be an alleyway with a lot of graffiti on the walls. All stands out except for one area where a passage is written on the wall. This is the same alleyway where MWE fans saw the insane Jekyl cut his promo last week. All of a sudden a figure walks down the alleyway with a hoodie on making people unaware of who it is. The figure comes to a halt in the middle of the alleyway and lowers his head. He is wearing a plain grey hoodie with jeans on, but the left leg of the jeans is being held up with a red bandana. In a swift motion, the figure brings his hands to his hoodie and takes it off revealing who it is. It is none other than MWE Superstar and No.1 Contender for the MWE World Championship, the Main Man, Sonny Williams! Sonny has his hair braided back which is a change from the usual way we see him. He looks into the camera with a look of fierce anger. The camera zooms in to see two spray cans in his jean pockets. Sonny continues looking into the camera with fury and without making a blink. Sonny Williams: Ever since the 10th of August 1998 I have almost surrounded my life around the death of my brother Jordan. I admit to everyone, to anyone who wants to take a shot at me, that I have not gotten over his death what so ever. I wish he were still alive to this day, and I know that it was my fault that what happened, happened. I love him, but I have come to the conclusion with my brother. I have come to a conclusion, a conclusion that hurts me deep down to even think about it. I remember a few things in my short life I spent with Jordan that makes me smile, things that light myself up in a dark room. A picture of Sonny's brother, Jordan emerges in his head as he has angel wings and a halo over his head. Sonny drips out a tear as he continues with his eyes shut. Jordan waves back to him as Sonny makes a smile. All of a sudden the picture begins to zoom out as a flashback is shown. ***FLASHBACK*** It is 1997 in Detroit, Michigan. He looks around the scattered area around him, but suddenly makes a swift movement. The other boy raises his head appearing to be Sonny's brother Jordan.
