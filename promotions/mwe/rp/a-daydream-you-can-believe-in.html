A Daydream you can believe in

Nov 5 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The scene opens up to show the Los Angeles skyline, showing the sights and sounds of California’s flagship city. The camera then reveals the suburban neighborhood that Brian Michaels has just moved into. Brian Michaels in workout pants and a black-and-red Rated-X t-shirt. He is feverishly texting on his cell phone. The camera zooms out to reveal Michaels is at his new house. It is empty, as he just moved in. The only thing in it is Michaels and a box of his belongings. He puts the cell phone away as silence is heard throughout the house. Michaels has a perplexed look on his face. He crosses his arms across his chest and looks at the camera. Michaels: Silence… Michaels bends down to the ground, listening for some kind of signal or mixed message. Michaels: Kind of like Alejandro Rodriguez’s sex life. He gives a playful grin at the camera before grabbing his car keys on his kitchen counter. He throws them up in the air before grabbing them and walking out the door. He then stops in his tracks, wondering why he has keys when Starbucks is right across the corner. He shrugs and begins walking again. He takes a peak at his new neighbors. One of the neighbors are out mowing their lawns. One is inside watching the New York Giants vs. the Dallas Cowboys on DVR. He walks by the last house and the television is turned onto a pornographic image. A credit flashes across the scene stating ‘’Nailing Sarah Palin’’ and then shows a bustier version of the Vice Presidential-hopeful being…well…nailed. Michaels looks over his shoulders, obviously intrigued. He walks on the lawn quietly, trying not to disrupt the person inside. When he finally peers in, it is revealed to be a woman watching the Palin scene very curiously. Michaels: OH SHIT! The woman looks back, obviously startled. It is revealed to be Chloe Cornett, the assistant GM of Friday Night Genesis. She is wearing a pantsuit with some of the buttons unbuttoned. She looks around and puts a finger on her mouth, telling Michaels to hush. Michaels: OH! OH!…OH! YOU…Palin! Michaels fists his hand on his mouth, looking around. Chloe opens her window, obviously in distress and in a rush. Chloe: THAT’S NOT WHAT IT LOOKS LIKE! He gives a sly look at the camera. Michaels: ...That’s what she said. Chloe: Look, you can’t tell anybody what you saw here. I’ll get looked on the wrong way! Michaels: Don’t guys already look at you ‘’the wrong way’’? Chloe: Yes, but this is different. Michaels puts his hands on his waist in a Nicole-Ritchie-esque fashion and cranks his head back with a look of disapproval. Chloe sighs. Michaels: You’re used to sighing aren’t you? Chloe: Enough! Look…maybe you and I can come to a…compromise? Chloe leans over the window, showing off her rather large breasts. Michaels’ jaw drops as he looks around feverishly. He puts his left hand on his chin as he ponders the potential compromise. He then quickly reaches into his pocket, taking out his picture-phone and taking a picture of Chloe’s unusually large rack. He begins to run away as she looks on in disgust. Michaels: GOTCHA BITCH! Michaels runs away quickly, laughing at a fever pitch. He looks to his left, where the street is, only to see Barbie McQueen waiting in her Mercedes, pink skirt and white Brian Michaels T-Shirt. She licks her lips and puts her hands on her waist. McQueen: Um…Brian? Michaels: Yes….sweetie, who trusts me on any situation, no matter how bad it looks…? McQueen: What would you think…if there was a guy…who pulled down his pants in front of me…and I snapped a picture of it? Hmm? Michaels puts his hands on his hair, knowing he is in a difficult situation. He then puts his hands together, clapping. Michaels: I got it! He’s posing for an underwear ad! Barbie rolls her eyes in amazement. McQueen: Okay…what if you catch me in bed with someone? Michaels: It depends…if it’s a chick…I’m joining. If it’s a guy…I’d say he put something in your drink and took you to his room. Barbie rolls her eyes again, obviously not buying into it. McQueen: What, the FUCK…were you doing with a girl bending over right in front of you? Michaels: I was…taking a picture…of…my new contract. You know, I wanted to show you and David W. later. McQueen: Okay, let me see it. Michaels scratches his head again and takes out his cell phone. He quickly goes to his pictures and deletes the ‘’titty picture’’. He then forwards to an actual picture of his contract he took in the MWE Headquarters. He hands the phone to Barbie, who eyes the picture. McQueen: Wait a sec…you took this picture in an office! That last pic, you took outside! How dumb do you think I am? Michaels: Well… McQueen: Don’t answer that, jerk! Barbie waddles off in a huff, obviously mad about her boyfriend’s latest actions. She gets into her car and speeds off leaving Michaels a bit depressed. He puts his hands in his pockets and walks to the Starbucks with his shoulders slumped down. He opens the door to hear the little rattle and sees no line. He smiles and walks up to a Hispanic man in his Starbucks work attire. He gives a polite grin as Michaels squints at the menu on the board. Michaels: H.. Hispanic Man: Welcome to Starbucks, how may I hel- you? Michaels: Uh…I’ll take a Soy Latte. Hispanic Man: Desea Cappucino? Michaels: Um…No. I don’t wear caps and I don’t like casinos, no. Hispanic Man: Hokay. Michaels: Huh? So I’m a ho now, eh? Hispanic Man: No, senor. Michaels: Senior? I ain’t old, junior! The Hispanic man wipes some sweat from his brow, as he clicks in Michaels’ order. Michaels sighs and turns around, only to be hit by a blonde with a white Gucci bag and a white tennis skirt. She has a pink tight shirt on, smacking her gum loudly. Woman: I’m so hawwwt. The camera takes a look at the woman, who takes off her designer sunglasses, and reveals herself to be the heir to the Hiltons, Paris Hilton! Paris: Yeaaaah. I want like…coffee. She scoffs at the Hispanic man. Hispanic Man: Whatchu wannn? Paris: Um, gimme some moche latte…or something, I don’t know. Michaels looks around, wondering if anybody notices Paris Hilton bossing the cashier around. Michaels: Hey, walking, talkin’ stereotype…you think you can just walk in and order whatever the fuck you want and boss this guy around? Paris then also looks around, scoffing at the much-less popular Brian. Paris: Um. I don’t think I care what you, like, think? Michaels: Know what…forget it. Forget I said anything. But remember THIS…you…Paris Hilton…suck in bed. Paris chokes on her gum and takes a long pause. Paris: Excuse me? Michaels: First of all, that’s not the only thing you’re chokin’ on. Michaels gives a dirty chuckle at the camera before rearing his head back at Hilton. Michaels: Yeah, I’ve seen your work, this guy… Michaels points at the Hispanic cashier, who looks around in a confuzzled manner. He takes his right hand and points to himself in a questioning way. Michaels: Yes, even he has seen your work. You blow…figuratively…and literally. Paris’ jaw drops in disbelief at Michaels’ insult. She rolls her eyes as Michaels winks and walks to find a seat that’s empty. He finds one and sits down. He sprawls his legs and hangs his head, obviously tired and looking for comfort. He rests his head on the table and falls into a deep, deep daydream… .............................................................................................................................. The scene begins rather blurry, with Michaels wiping his eyes with his hands, clearing up his vision. He awakes and is in a locker room, on his own. He looks around the room and sees pictures and posters and advertisements of himself. He wonders where he is. He looks down at what once used to be a Rated-X shirt and now is a business suit. He looks around desperately, opening the door violently, walking out into a long empty hallway. A man walks up to him, appearing to be coordinating an event of sorts. Authority Figure: Mr.Michaels, you’re on soon. Michaels: I’m on? What the fuck are YOU smoking? The figure looks around with his eyebrows cocked up, wondering what Michaels is thinking exactly. Authority Figure: Your speech, the one you’re delivering today in L.A. to the MWE fans. They are packed out there and it is HOT! 100,000 fans man…you ready? Michaels: Um, sure. I knew that, for sure. Brian suddenly notices how heated the hallway seems. He runs his hands over his hair, thinking of what to say. Authority Figure: And you’re on, now! Brian walks down the hallway, and walks out a door labeled ‘’Exit’’. He opens the door and is amazed by what he sees… He sees a podium and keeps his head down fully knowing he is going to have to be quick on his feet with such a large crowd. He hears music… [dohtml]<object width="425" height="344"><param name="movie" value="http://www.youtube.com/v/0jtzlQOaVbQ&hl=en&fs=1"></param><param name="allowFullScreen" value="true"></param><param name="allowscriptaccess" value="always"></param><embed src="http://www.youtube.com/v/0jtzlQOaVbQ&hl=en&fs=1" type="application/x-shockwave-flash" allowscriptaccess="always" allowfullscreen="true" width="425" height="344"></embed></object>[/dohtml] He walks up to a large podium, with no papers to look at. He looks out into the crowd with a dead-hot sun staring him dead in the eye. He sees no teleprompter. He takes a deep breath and begins to speak in a loud booming voice that is heard throughout the park. Michaels: If you’re tired…of the same old….ranting…I’m better than you promos…give me an amen. The crowd easily resonates to Michaels’ words and gives him an amen, with about 20,000 mumbling it out the side of their mouths at least. Michaels begins to feel a little more confidence in his words. Michaels: If you’re tired of men like Ace Cannon…with their third-person dialogue…and their rich cars…and their good-looking girlfriends…and their obsession of World Titles…give me an amen. More and more people begin to relate to Michaels’ words as he talks with more emphasis now. Michaels: If you’re tired of Joey Dallas…with his God’s gift to wrestling talk…and his ego…and his ‘’save us’’ campaign…gimme an AMEN! All the voices combine for one large voice as Michaels say amen, booming throughout the park. Michaels has a bit of a grin and a swagger to him as he walks off the podium and begins walking on stage. Michaels: If you’re tired of Alejandro Rodriguez…and his short, Hispanic ass…talking about how at 5’9…5’8, 7, 6, 5, 4, 3, 2, 1…he is an inspiration to kids everywhere…always losing to David Whitman…gimme an AMEN! Shouts of ‘’Preach on!’’ ‘’Righteousness!’’ and ‘’Rodriguez blows!’’ are heard. Michaels: And if you’re tired of Mason Resoan…and his talks of running people over…his talks of changing MWE…give me…an amen. The crowd rises in approval as Michaels’ grin widens from side-to-side. He looks out at the horizon with a broad look on, obviously anticipating a change in momentum. Michaels: So these are my thoughts on my match at Mayhem. Ya got Ace Cannon…a man, who I may not agree with…but I respect. I respect him for what he’s done in the business…and for his still unreached potential. The old MWE fans know this…but Ace is my half-brother. For the past year, Ace and I have kept in contact. We’ve searched and searched for a promotion that could actually stay alive for more than a few months. The fans chuckle along with Michaels who puts another grin on his face. Michaels: Now Ace has been places. He’s main-evented for Revolution Pro. The audience again laughs out loud as Michaels can’t help but put a smile on. Michaels: Yeah…not too prestigious eh? But really…Ace Cannon is like…my better twin. That’s right, you heard me right…Ace Cannon is my better twin. He’s younger, he’s faster, he’s stronger…BUT…he’s not smarter. Now don’t get me wrong…I’m not going to go on a limb and call Ace a dumbass…but he talks so much shit, he’s full of it. He’s so cocky that when someone can actually CONTEND with him…he’s so surprised…he can’t come back. Michaels: So Ace...pretty boy…when I break your teeth and you go over to your playboy models and they ask…what happened? Michaels: You let those whores know what motherfucker to blame! The male portion of the crowd erupts in cheers and applause, causing Michaels to give a wink at the female part of the audience. Michaels: Joey Dallas…I’m not gonna lie…there is no one at MWE that has more potential than Joey. He can do so many things, and we’ve got so many nicknames for him in the locker room. Nicknames such as… Michaels: Joey the Actor, Joey the Cowboy, Joey the Miner, Joey the Wrestler, and MY personal favorite…Joey the Plumber! A rim shot is heard as the crowd that has heard of Joe the Plumber. Michaels: But in all seriousness folks…Joey Dallas is promising you promos as usual! He is promising you…more of the same! He is wrestling on the tried and true campaign of ‘’I’m better than you.’’ He is going to come out here and tell you, the MWE fans, that he is better than me, that he’s faster than me, that he’s better at everything than me. Michaels: But Joey Dallas…when you look in the mirror on Monday night…you look at your past…you look at your future, and you know damn well deep inside that you’re not a 3 time MWE Champion. That you’re not what you love to claim you are…so Joey Dallas on Monday night…I invite you to grow a pair and to face me man-to-man…no bullshit. The question remains…can you keep up? The crowd gives an ooh and aah as Michaels continues to walk across the stage back and forth, taking each step in stride. Michaels: Mason Resoan…the man who…made the decision…to run me over. The man who held my leg in his hand…and he broke it. Like a miserable motherfucker…he broke it. Brian takes a long, gleaming look at the camera. He looks at it with his green eyes, once filled with passion, now filled with hate. Michaels: Mason, when you look at my eyes, I want you to know that they’ll never forget what you did…They’ll never forget. I swear to you Mason…words can’t describe what I’m going to do you Monday night…but I’m not gonna kick your ass. Michaels shakes his head as the crowd looks around in confusion. Michaels: Because…I’m going to be too busy…putting it into your head…never again…to fuck…with me. Because if you do Mason Resoan, I swear on Ace Cannons’ grave, I WILL make you bleed and I will make you suffer…and that is NOT…a promise…that will be broken. Brian gives the microphone a bit of a snarl before cranking his head back and changing his emotion again. Michaels: And Alejandro Rodriguez…one of the toughest sons of bitches I know. This guy has been labeled the underdog night in…night out…and he’s still gotten the job done in crunch-time and pressure situations. He puts his hands up in the air in defense. Michaels: He's not the biggest guy in the ring, but he sure as hell knows what he's doing. Michaels then puts a finger up to show the crowd he's not done. Michaels: But just like everybody else...he's lacking something. Alejandro has the ability to shit-talk like no other...but he's missing the fight. He's missing the ability to look someone in the eyes and to beat them to a pulp. He won't do it, he CAN'T...do it. Michaels: My friends, you must believe in the ability of two 3-time MWE Champions in David Whitman and myself. As much as I hate the son of a bitch, D.C. knows how to get the job done when it matters. The crowd pops big-time for the MWE Champions' name and a mixed reaction is heard for D.C. Michaels: This Monday night, change is coming to MWE, and the lobbyists can take THAT one to the bank. Thank you, and God Bless MWE! The crowd gives a standing ovation or the former MWE Champion and Rated-X member Brian Michaels, who promptly takes a bow. The scene fades back to Starbucks as snoring is heard. Slight snorting is then heard, followed by Michaels' head rising of the table he was sleeping at. Michaels: I need some decaf... The coffee arrives at Michaels' table as the camera fades away as it then flashes the MWE logo.
