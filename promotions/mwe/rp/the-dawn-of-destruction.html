The dawn of destruction

May 20 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Blight (Damien) Bar tender Morgan Sean The prolouge The scene opens with the flame of a lighter moving closer to the tip of a cigarette. The face of the person lighting up cannot be seen. A voice is heard deep and drawn out. The sands of time pouring through their glass prison. Each grain falling into place piece by piece. At the top of the hourglass each grain of sand patienty waiting to plumet to the bottom just as their fallen comrads before them. The crisp sound of the cigarette paper and tabaco is heard burning as the man takes a large pull. The camera starts to pan out as we see the profile shot of Blight's face where there is no facepaint. Over and over the hour glass will tumble, and over and over the sand will still fall uniformly to the bottom. In this cycle a question poses. Does the past repeat itself or does it repeat the present Blight takes a rather large pull of his cigarette and blows the smoke back out. The smoke fills over the lens of the camera and when it clears we have the profile shot of the opposite side of his face where his face is painted. In my own life I too ask myself the same question. For years I have pondered the true answer. Time after time I have fallen to my demons. Time after time I have left myself for dead. Time after time my hourglass has been turned back over just to have everything repeat itself. The burning of the cigarette is heard again and a Blight lets out another puff of smoke, this time when the smoke clears we have a full face view of him. For far too long I have suffered under this pain. Day in and day out this torture on eased by alchohol. Until one day He came...and beat the sense into me. (Blight snarls at the thought of what he said) Two years were spent in my own prison, purging my self of my own sins. The hourglass will turn once more, but this time sand will not flow through it...blood will. No longer will my demons take my life from me, they will take everyone elses. No longer will my demons torture me, they will torture you. As the final sands run out of the hourglass they will show that only one thing. The sands will show that only the sinners remain Blight gets up from the chair he had been sitting in and drops his cigarette on the floor. As the camera pans up the smoke is rising thicker until finally a flame is seen glowing and slowly growing as the scene flashes white. The Scene. Bible Black by Heaven and Hell is heard blaring from a juke box as we see a large figure that is Blight walk out of the shadows of the door at a bar. He walks past a few tables where the patrons just glare at him weirdly, not bothered by them he walks up to the bar and pulls up a stool where the bartender has his back turned. What will it be tonight? Tequila and just keep them coming. The bartender turns around and places the shot in front of him. Blight picks up the drink and shoots it back like nothing. He sits there a while just staring ahead listening to whatever plays over the jukebox, completely blocking out the chatter of the other patrons. The bartender walks over and pours another shot for him and walks away again. Blight hears another stool slide up next to him but pays it no mind until he see a womans hand reach across and grab his forearm. Turning to face the woman he finds himself stunned with shock. The woman smiling says. Its been about two years and all you can do is look at me funny? Still in shock Blight tries to fathom words. You can't be real? I mean its really you, but its been so long. How have you been? I've been pretty well but I think i should be asking you that question. After that incident with Frank you kind of disapeared. Noone heard from you, noone knew where you had gone to...what happened Damien? Blight before speaking takes another shot and stays silent for a moment. To tell you the truth Morgan I don't really know...all I know is that I had a tag partner, someone who grew up with me, who was like my own flesh and blood punching me in the face numerous times and throwing me over a couch. When I finally came to things really didn't make sense. I found a note that said I needed to really clean myself up and get my head back together...so I checked into rehab. There I really learned that I didn't need to live in the bottom of a bottle...that being drunk all the time wouldn't take my problems away, that they only made them worse. And yet your still sitting in a bar? That makes sense I guess...When did you get out of rehab? What have you been doing? Its been so long since we talked you gotta tell me. I got out about two months ago, and I started wrestling again---wait didn't Frank tell you? Um we haven't spoken in a year and a half. We broke up not too long after you disapeared...after that whole thing he changed like he wasn't the same anymore and I couldn't deal with it. But why would he have told me, have you seen him? The bartender walks over and places three shots down pours tequila in them drinks one for himself before looking at Blight and Morgan before telling them its on the house. After they take their shots Blight looks at Morgan again. Well yeah...like I said I wrestle again. We both made our debuts for the same fed this past week. It was just coincednce that we both started at the same place again. We had a five man battle royal...we did some old teamwork like back in the old days we faced off a litle but that was really it. We haven't had a chance to sit down and talk really. Now this week we are in a fatal four way against each other for a no.1 contenders spot. Wow you guys always did make a good first impression where ever you were. We did didn't we....well one of the guys in this match we faced in last weeks match. And after last week I'm betting me or Sin are gonna leave an impression on his face hahaha. He truly thinks hes a gift to woman...maybe if those women were Helen Keller. I guess he really just need a reality check thats all. I'll bet that kid is still a virgin...unless his old fat nextdoor neighbor...eewww well you know what I mean. Come on Damien thats mean! Although probably true...What if you and Frank...or well Sin come face to face in that match? What would you do? After taking another shot that the bartender had refilled while he was talking. Stop him dead in his tracks...I don't care who I'm facing, there is a title shot on the line here the past means nothing, if I need to run over him aswell then so be it...nothing is going to stop me this time. Remember out of countless matches Frank only holds two or three wins over me. I still think I got his number Frank will always be Frank no matter what he calls himself he will always make the same mistakes and he will still fall in a heap. Oh...ok but what---- Hey babe there you are, I've been looking --- Who are you? Damien this is Sean...my soon to be husband, Sean this is Damien an old friend. We were just catching up, its been a while since we saw each other. Oh uh ok well I'll let you guys catch up some more I'll go put some more music on. No no its ok I need to get out of here anyway...Morgan listen maybe we will catch up some more another time...come by the arena if you want...it was nice seeing you. Oh ok, well if you guys start talking again, give him my love. Morgan give him a big hug before he turns and nods to Sean. Blight heads for the door and tires screeching loudly before a crash is head. Morgan looking at Sean goes to run for the door as the scene fades out.
