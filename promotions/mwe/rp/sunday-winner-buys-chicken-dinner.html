Sunday winner buys chicken dinner

May 19 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
off: This takes Sunday night after Backlash *The scene opens at eleven pm midnight at Dwayne Wade's mansion. He is throwing a party to celebrate his good friend Edge's victory over Jeff Hardy earlier in the evening. Everyone is dancing to Metalingus by Alter Bridge. Many celebrities are there the most notables include T.O., Jessica Alba, Carrie Underwood, Brandon Jacobs, and of course Edge. The event wear is casual as most of the guests are wearing hot button up shirts and some jeans. Although Peter doesn't follow the dress code electing to wear a Peter Kaymakcian Legend in the Process shirt, some grayish jeans, and a pair of white nikes. Peter signals to cut the music and he stands on a chair calling out for everyone to listen. Peter speaks in a cocky and arrogant voice as he gives this speech to the illustrious guests and doesn't regret a single word even though he knows there are MWE Cameras all around the place recording every word spoken.* Peter: We are all here to celebrate my bro's title win today over Jeff "Watch Me Crash" Hardy. However we are not here only to celebrate my friend's victory as we will also be celebrating my victory this sunday at Tribulation. You see the Saintz of Los Angeles are nothing more then some street thugs pretending to be wrestlers just as Cryme Tyme and LAX do. If you want to know how I know this it is because the thugs have already been kicked out of four wrestling institutions because of how much they sucked. To give these thugs a fighting chance the "genius'" in MWE management decided that I needed a partner, but not just any old partner a young gun. Wasn't I just complaining about being tired of all these insignificant newbies in my matches? Well Riddick just sit in the corner like a good boy so daddy can end this quick and pick up a win for the both of us. Alright people lets give a round of applause to both me and Edge! *As the people are clapping we notice this gorgeous blonde girl looking at Peter with "hungry" eyes. The girl is a brunette, about five foot six and couldn't weigh anymore then one hundred and twenty five pounds. She just stares at him as he mingles with T.O. and Edge. T.O. hasn't followed Peter's dress code either as he is wearing his own jersey with some blue jeans and a pair of black reebok shoes. Edge is still in his ring gear with a Rated-R Superstar shirt on. All three men stand tall and confident as they carry on with there conversation. She is to far away to be noticed, but just close enough to listen. Peter is talking as if he has already won his match this sunday.* Peter: After this February Edge we'll still be the World Heavyweight Champion, I'll be MWE World Heavyweight Champion, and Terrell you'll be the Superbowl MVP and the NFL MVP. If I where the Saintz of Los Angeles I wouldn't even show up to Tribulation and save myself the embarrassment. *Edge has a cocky grin on his face as he hoists up the World Heavyweight Championship and soaks in all of Peter's accolades. He responds with the same arrogance shown by Peter.* Edge: Tonight was my night, this sunday is your night. Both me and Terrell will be cheering you on from ringside. You see Peter once you get that belt and Terrell once you get that ring we will prove why we are the best at what we do. *T.O. joins in adding his own ego to a pot full of egos. Each men has stroked there ego and there friends ego while talking, will T.O. do the same? He speaks in a gentle and confused voice.* T.O.: Do you guys hear yourselves? I mean come dog, you two must be trippin' disrespectin' your fellow roster members. They are all just as valuable.... *All three men just burst out laughing as the girl looks on curiously. T.O. begins to speak still laughing a little, but sounding just as arrogant as the other two.* T.O.: I am sorry, I just couldn't say that with a straight face. We are the Kings of WWE, MWE, and the NFL. I am ten times the receiver Moss is. No one can step to you two in the ring. So no wonder we get bored and decide to take some time off like I did in 2005. *After there laugh is done all three men decide to go in different directions. T.O. heads to the DJ, as Edge heads over to talk to Carrie Underwood. Jessica Alba can be seen leaving probably going home to attend to her baby. D-Wade is chilling playing NBA Live 2009 on Peter's PS3. D-Wade is winning 85-79 just starting the fourth quarter. D-Wade is playing as the Heat as Ashley is playing as the Cleveland Cavaliers. Peter start to walk over towards D-Wade when the woman who has been watching Peter finally decides to talk to him. The song Confidence by Teddy Greiger is playing as the girl begins to talk to Peter in a quirky and extremely happy tone.* Girl: Hi my name is Becky and I just thought that maybe you would like to talk and hang out a bit. *Peter looks uninterested in this girl as he knows he could get any girl at this party with the snap of his fingers. Peter decides to humor Becky talking at her as if she was a child or below him.* Peter: Hey Becky how are you? *Becky has a huge grin on her face as she answers in a hyper tone. She can not stop smiling.* Becky: Oh my God I am doing fabulous. How about you? Oh wait no need I already know, you are doing great aren't you doing great? *Peter goes to answer her question, but she quickly cuts him off. She is very energetic and it is apparent that Peter is starting to get a little annoyed by her.* Becky: Duh your Peter Kaymakcian of course your great. I can't wait to see you in action this sunday as I know that you're going to win. You know what I want to ask you? Well that was dumb because how could you know without me telling you? It's like come on Becky he's great, but not a psychic. Any who I was wondering why did you quit CWE? *Peter is very annoyed at this point and time and has decided that enough is enough and that this girl has got to go. Peter speaks to her in a very vicious tone, but not a loud one as he does not want to cause a scene at the party.* Peter: Look you're going to play a game called Shut The Fuck Up and here is how you play. Becky you have to shut the fuck up and stop talking to me because you are a waste of my time. Now I am going to be hitting up with my boy D-Wade and congratulate him on beating Ashley. Now if you would excuse me. *Just then a beeping noise cuts Peter off. It is his phone telling him that he has to leave now in order to catch his flight to Italy. Peter does not look happy as he has to leave his party without getting to talk to D-Wade about there future plans for joint effort between the Heat and MWE. Peter quickly walks out the party passing by many fans and with Becky in hot pursuit. She yells out to him so he can hear her and stop.* Becky: Will I ever see you again? *Peter responds with snarky and tasteless comment to the poor girl.* Peter: I hope not. *Peter is running down the stairs and towards his Ferrari Enzo. Becky is still chasing after him. She screams out again hoping this time he will stop.* Becky: Wow you are really committed to your gimmick. No wonder MWE Management loves you so much. Look why don't we get to know each other? *Peter is wondering what does he have to say to get this girl to leave him alone. Before he can respond she quickly grabs his hand and pulls him around into a kiss. After she breaks the kiss she has a smile on her face, which Peter thinks is pathetic, and asks him a question in a sweet little erotic voice.* Becky: Was that so bad? *Peter looks at his watch and decides to make this brief. He talks to her is if she was stupid.* Peter: Y-e-s i-t w-a-s b-e-c-a-u-s-e I d-o-n-t l-i-k-e y-o-u. N-o-w l-e-a-v-e m-e a-l-o-n-e y-o-u c-r-a-z-y p-s-y-c-h-o. *Becky just laughs as a frustrated Peter turns around and unlocks his car. Peter quickly enters his car and speeds away not realizing that he dropped his phone. Becky looks down and notices Peter's phone. She bends down and picks it up. Her voice is extremely excited as she begins to talk to herself.* Becky: I knew God wanted us to get married. What will we name are kids and where will we hold the wedding? Who cares I am marrying Peter Kaymakcian! *Becky dances around in glee as the scene fades to black.*
