You Don't Get a Strike Everytime

Aug 14 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Note: Sorry for posting so late, haha. Did half of it Thursday and then went airsofting today. Got home, passes out, and woke up just in time to get working. Not to mention, I was distracted watching Gran Torino while writing the end. *It's August 13, 2009 and Mason Resoan is sitting in his backyard, by himself, tanning alone. This is a quite unusual scene, considering that for the last four months, Mason has not been seen without Sapphire. She has pretty much been around him through thick in thin in every major and little decision he has made. Whether that be moving to a different country, making a glass of lemonade, or simply suntanning. So to see Mason by himself is not so much awkward, but delighful. He is wearing very a pair of very tight boxing shorts, black, with an MWE symbol on the back. And he is currently laying face down, in eyes closed, and his back recieving light. He does not notice the two men who have suddenly entered his back yard. One of these men is current World Heavyweight Champion, Sinc Mercier, and the other, is his MWE-Comitte Member Nate Rellington. They move quickly and quietly over to the little chair in which Mason is sitting on and crouch down, eye level with him. In perfect synchronization, they grasp a hold of the four legs and heave him up into the air, so slightly, that Mason doesn't even know a thing. Walking slowly, they move Mason closer and closer towards his pool until they are within two feet.* Nate: Hello Mr. United States Champion. Would you like to go for a swim? Sinc: CAUSE I SURE WOULD! *Mason doesn't even have time to react as he is tossed into his pool in one swift motion. Splishing and splashing, trying to get his wits about him, Mason moves over to the closest ladder in his pool and holds on. He shakes his head, and his hair throws water everywhere, as he climbs to the surface. He has his back turned as Sinc and Rellington laugh hysterically behind him, and the camera pans over to Mason's face. He has a slight smirk on his face, but it is an evil type smirk as he walks grabs a towel over from the towel rack towards the end of his pool and dries off his back and legs and walks over to the duo that just tossed him in his own pool.* Mason: Alright, pretty good, pretty good. But if you don't think I'll get you two back, you're sadly mistaken. *Sinc and Nate slow down their chuckling, but are still enjoying this hardy situation. Mason takes his towel by both ends and begins to roll it in his hands, over and over again. Eventually, it looks like a long piece of cloth and Mason takes one step back and whips Nate's precious cowboy hat from his head, into the water. Quickly, he stops laughing, and Sinc erupts. Mason smiles in satisfaction, and looks over to Sinc.* Mason: Oh, you'll get some too. Haha, believe me, you'll eventually get yours. *Nate is a little ticked now, because his hat is floating in the middle of the pool, all by it's lonesome. He turns to Sinc.* Nate: Alright Sincster, what's going on for today? Sinc: I really don't have any idea. Here let me think. Hmmm...I say, we use... *Sinc looks around and points at Mason.* Sinc: His computer. *Mason doesn't really realize what Sinc has said, but as Sinc begins to march to the house, Mason snaps into reality. He remembers the last time Sinc almost got in his house. He runs forward, tackles into the grass, and stands up, as Sinc looks bewildered as Mason walks up his back porch stairs.* Mason: Don't worry, I'll get the laptop. You don't need to go in there. *Mason walks into the house, very suspiciously and sneakily and emerges a few seconds later with said laptop. He brings it out of the house, walks over to a table he has set-up outside, and places it down. Sinc walks over, bumps Mason out of the way, and quickly punches in wikipedia.* Nate: You're relying on Wikipedia to tell us what to do today? Sinc: Why of course. I am going to have Wiki work it's magic and tell us what we will do for today's events. Kind of like a magic eight ball. *Mason thinks to himself of all the bad things that could come up. He closes his eyes, crosses his fingers, and hopes nothing weird pops up.* Sinc: What the f*ck! We can't do shit with nuclear acid! No, no, no, this won't work. *He clicks a random button and let's out a whoop.* Mason: Did you just whoop...for...bowling? Sinc: Oh, you know you like to play with those huge 16 pound balls...don't even deny it. *Nate just looks on in bewilderment.* Mason: You're a wacko, you know that. Sinc: A wacko who loves to bowl! NATE! TO THE CAR! *Nate shrugs his shoulders, doesn't say a word, and leads the way to Sinc's car. They all climb in, Sinc being the driver, and begin to go to the bowling alley.* *25 minutes later, Sinc pulls the car into Miami Lanes bowling alley. He whips it around two different lanes looking for parking, finally seeing an open spot in the third lane close to the front. Sinc drives straight to it, parks the car, and kicks his door open, flinging himself out in excitement. Mason just laughs as he gets out of shotgun, and Nate Rellington slowly steps out of the backseat. They walk into the bowling alley and walk up to the front counter. Sinc, who at this point doesn't even realize the awkwardness of the situation, rings a small silver bell on the counter. A middle-aged man steps from the back staff room and looks at Sinc. He rubs his eyes, and sees the other two people to his right and left and just gasphs.* Sinc: Well, hello there Mr. Bald Man. Can we get lane 27 and three pairs of bowling shoes. I need a size 15 1/2, please. Worker: I'm sorry sir, I'm afraid are biggest size is 14. How about you two gentlemen, though? Mason: We both have bigger feet, no need to worry. *Mason chuckled a little bit as that.* Worker: Well, we won't stop you from playing... *The worker looks at each of them, slowly transitioning his eyes.* Worker: Not like would could anyway. You boys should be fine, lane 27 is open for you now. *Sinc, Mason, and Nate just shrug and continue on, with Sinc turning around, walking backwards, and talking to Mason.* Sinc: So, what is new in the wonderful, luxurious, and oh so fabulous world of Mason Resoan? Mason: Luxurious, wonderful, and fabulous, eh. I guess you could somewhat say that... Sinc: Oh, come off it, you know that we have it good. Of course, we earned it, but we still have it good. *Mason thinks for a second as the reach there lane, with their bowling balls already picked out.* Mason: You know what, you're right. But, I mean...I still got Sapphire on my ass all the time. Nate: Partner, it could be a lot...a lot worse. *Mason powders up his hand, getting ready for his turn, as Sinc rolls his first frame.* Sinc: Seven pins, not too shabby. But yeah, Mr. Big Shot over here is right. I mean, you could have some like Lindsey. Man, she went through a freakin' divorce with big old Dingleberry C over there. Mason: Still, it seems like all she does is b*tch and b*tch. I don't know, whatever. Nate, it's your turn. *Nate gets up, cracks his neck, walks forward and throws a strike in his first roll.* Mason: Damn, who new Mr. MWE Board of Director could bowl. Sinc: It was probably luck... Mason: Luck or not...hey, speaking of D.C, how'd you do against him last week. I never heard the result. *Sinc rubs his chin, pondering the question.* Sinc: Well, I didn't lose... Mason: Awesome! Sinc: but I didn't exactly win either. Mason: Huh? *Sinc pauses for a second again.* Sinc: Don't worry about it. Your bowl. *Mason gets up to the lane, and bowls an 8 and then picks up a spare.* Sinc: Well, while were on the topic off opponents...how about you. How about Mr. Resoan losing to Kevin Hardaway? Mason: I could say a million and a half things about that match last week, but the bottom line is that I lost to him. No excuses. No bull crap. No draws. He flat out beat, whether I think he could do it again is one thing...and whether it will happen again is another. All I can say to Mr. Hardaway is that I've only lost a couple times in singles competition here in MWE. And almost every time, I've avenged the lost. When I face him again, I hope he doesn't believe that I'll make the same mistakes. *Mason nods to Nate, as Sinc goes up to bowl again. Sinc, however, pauses. He looks at Mason, with a slight frown.* Sinc: What are you talking about? That's the past, this is a new week, and Hardaway is no longer your problem. I'm talking about GM. *Mason pauses this time, thinking about GM for a second, something he hadn't done all week. He shook his head and just thought to himself.* Mason: GM...GM...GM, hmm what to say about him. I mean, he's facing you at CG. Sinc: Oh come off it, I know you're itching to cut your promo. Mason: You don't know anything. It's your bowl, come on. Nate: Mason, you gotta think about GM before you talk anyway. I mean, I'm watching this guy, and let me tell ya, he's no push-over. There's a reason why he is gonna be going for the title soon. *Sinc picks up his bowl, throws it down the lane and bowls a gutter ball. He frowns, slowly losing intrest in actually bowling.* Mason: I get it...Grand Mystique deserves a little more of my time then at a bowling alley, but honestly, who cares. No one but me is facing him this week, and personally, he isn't THAT big of a deal. He's just like any other opponent I've ever faced. Sinc: Quite cocky of you, don't ya think? You've never even faced him and people have been waiting for this for a while. It's a bigger match then you realize. *Mason goes over the card in his head again and nods.* Mason: Big match or not, I'm not gonna break a sweat over it. I know he's a good wrestler and all, but you know...he's not to much to be worried about. If there is two things that GM is good at, it's cutting a deep and meaningful promo and it's getting his point across. Well, you know what, cocky or not, I think that I can take him. I've had more experience, more big wins, more titles, ect. ect. *Sinc just starts laughing.* Mason: What do you find so humorous about this? Sinc: It's just the fact that you made a pact to yourself that you wouldn't bring up the past anymore, that this was the future, and the past is meaningless. What happened to that? Mason: Well, sometimes rules and pacts have to be broken... Sinc: Just what I expected. Man, it's your bowl, Nate. *Nate goes up, but Mason ignores him, analyzing what Sinc just said.* Mason: Are you trying to say that I can't stick to my word? Sinc: No, what I'm saying is that if you want to beat GM, you need to follow what you told yourself, and changing that up...will only lead to failure. *Nate gets a second strike in a roll, as Mason looks intently at Sinc.* Mason: If GM thinks I'm gonna fail, or Nate does, or you do, you have no idea. Staying true to myself...staying true to myself. If you want me to stay true, I don't have to look at the past. I'll look at the last couple of matches lately. I've lost two consecutive. GM's won twice in a row. GM is going for a world-title shot soon, his first in MWE. I'm only defending the U.S Title. I lost to Hardaway and he has never faced off against me. So he has the advantage. *Mason is slowly getting more intesne. He walks up to the rack, grabs his bowling ball, and throws it with power down the lane. Gutterball.* Mason: But never count me out. You know, GM may be knocking people down like...well, like bowling pins. But you know what, not everyone gets a strike everytime. And f*ck it if it's corny, the expression works. *Mason picks up his ball again and throws a spare. Afterwards he cracks his neck, drops off the little lip, and returns to the seats with Sinc and Nate.* Mason: And the last couple of weaks, I've been throwing some gutters, but everyone will get that one frame...that one chance to redeem themselves. This week, I'll redeem my loss last week and end GM's seemingly limitless momentum. GM might be on his high horse right now, but after Monday, whether I have to scratch and claw, or just walk in and lay on him, I'll get the pin, and walk into Crowning glory as a winner. Sinc: See, now that's the spirit! *Sinc goes to the lane again, and goes for another frame, scoreless, somehow getting two gutters in a row.* Mason: You see, I don't even know who my definite opponent for Crowning Glory is. I have no worrries, no carries, and the only problem I have right now, is Sapphire. But you know what, that's easily taken care of. GM on the other hand has the pressure of trying to defeat me, Mason Resoan. He has the pressure of his first every World Heavyweight title match. And he has the pressure to prove to everyone that he wasn't just a fluke when he picked up all his wins. On the other hand, just like when I walked into my U.S title match, I've got nothing to lose, and all to gain. If I lose, so what...another loss. I've gotten them before. I win, that sits on GM's mind the whole time he faces you, and if he possibly wins the title, which is doubtful, then it sits then too. GM, without me even doing any work, has played mind games on himself. And I might be nicer now, but that doesn't mean I won't capitalize. *Nate finally speaks up after he bowls a third straight strike.* Nate: YAH-HOO TURKEY! Sinc: Now Mason, that is what I enjoy hearing. And GM, just so you know, if you can't beat him...you won't beat me either. We are the coast to coast connection, and we win, lose, and train together. C2C is more than just a tag team, we are an alliance that sticks together. You can't beat one half, you won't beat the other. So good luck this week, because you're gonna need it. Because Mason is pumped! He's ready! And he's the Mayhem Wrestling Entertainment United States Champion for a reason! Mason: And don't you ever forget it. You thought Mason was done, well think again OH Great Grand One. I might have been going up and down the last few months but I am rejuvenated, reenergized, and refocused. Those are the three keys to success and success is something you just won't have! Nate: And if ya didn't know... *Mason and Sinc just look at each other, then at Nate, and shake their head as the screen fades to them bowling once again.*
