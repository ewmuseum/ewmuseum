I've Seen Your Nightmares

Jun 28 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

When it doesn't become one you'll know because I do make him wake up later, I hope the things said and done in the dream make sense because again, it is a DREAM after all, don't hate on me! Also, if you haven't read my most recent CD RP read that first because you love me. And yes my RP's don't have to be 10,000 words to be good, gosh people I can write short RP's too. They say a picture is worth a thousand words. They say every color is its own….every stroke of the brush has a story. In a painting, in a masterpiece, in any work of art there is imagination and creativity…. The colors, the imagery, the appearance of it all is just so alluring, so….surreal. In a cartoon you have humor. In a drawing you have aspect. In every picture you have a story. So he wants me to be mean and ruthless like him? Dark and sinister….evil and conniving…he wants the heel Sinc back, he doesn’t want me to be the happy duckling, he wants me to be the dark gemini. But Brian, this is who I am. THIS IS SINC MERCIER, what do you expect? What does Brian Michaels wish out of Sinc? The past is done and so are the mistakes I’ve committed, we all change Michaels, without a shadow of a doubt we all go through our phases, but you are going through a special phase, one that I can’t quite put my finger on, just like your career, everything is flying by so fast, and you fail to realize it. Colors begin to appear around him slowly coming together into shapes and sizes, the entire environment is shifting under his feet, he is in an ever changing setting as various pictures of Brian whirl around him, every facial expression, every achievement, and every moment of Brian‘s entire career is at the mercy of Sinc‘s eyes. Look at the many sides of Brian Michaels, every expression and every countless moment I am looking at through heart felt envy. Each picture and every photo finish portrait is just so entertaining to examine, the stories each one of these can tell are priceless. He eyes every single image and one by one describes in his mind what he thinks each should say. He captions each piece with words that have been thrown at Brian over the years. Especially the one thing he will always cherish, the world championship. “Politicker” The time Brian lost to D.C. at Adrenaline Rush, and the pain he was in when it was all over, the anger he must have felt when his colleague and Rated-X stable mate D.C. took the championship away from him was another big moment in his career. “Failure” And finally the GREATEST image of them all, one taken straight from the tabloids, the secret life…of Brian Michaels? “Man Eater” You see, I am not trying to aggravate the living daylights out of you Brian, I am simply showing you what you already know. Every time you’d hear such words, you’d get motivated, you’d dance around that ring like the taco bell dog, you became filled with this adrenaline, and now here you are. You and me, for the world championship, one that you’ve won three times, and one that you shouldn’t feel so greedy to regain again. I can’t help but think you never cared about evolving new generations or creating this company of new breeds, that your effort into making a new line of champs is all a hoax. All you care about, is stripping that title from me and replacing the words Sinc Mercier, with Brian Michaels, four time MWE world champion, ain’t that a trip? The setting falls dark, all the rotating pictures stop and begin to disintegrate. Two lamps flicker on, lamps that appear to resemble those seen on a street or in a park. A single woman walks forward into the two flickering lights. Sinc looks at the woman in a unique glimmer, he tries to recall an event that appeared to have happened years ago, an event Michaels will NEVER forget. Remember the winter of 1999 Michaels? Remember a lovely lady named…Courtney? Sinc pauses, still staring at her in an almost lustful glare, but he looks away, he looks away in disgust. He pauses and places a hand on his chest as if he is appalled, he now describes the event of December 31st, 1999. That woman….was raped. I’ve seen your nightmares Michaels, I’ve known your weaknesses and your fearful spites. I read up on you, I’ve went place to place to your friends and family members to find out what makes you tick, and then I heard about little Courtney, the poor thing…. Sinc stares back at her, his eyes swell up, he gripes his chest now in a beating pain as if feeling what Michaels felt when he found out about this sick event, Sinc watches….he watches with an ongoing fear and worry, then it happens. A man approaches her, and snags her up in the small patch of light, she screams and she tries desperately to get away. Rape…is art? Pain…is creativity? Destruction. Torture. Sadness. She tries hard to cry for help, but she can’t be heard, the man has her tightly squeezed, within only mere seconds he takes her away into the darkness and they vanish. The lights fade out and the darkness slowly scatters as Sinc walks some ways down a small road and eventually making it to a medium sized building of some sort. It looks to be a business building, he enters it without hesitation and it is now showing the scene to be a news station, an empty and abandoned news station, but it is not a place to tilt your head at, it is more familiar than meets the eye. I’m sorry I had to show you such a horrific image Brian, but the truth remains, it happened whether you like it or not. I’m trying to prove something to you, prove that no matter what you do, or the amount of effort you give off, there will always be that one area in time that you just can’t forget. And now look at you, you make me want to hurl. Your not at all the image wrestling should be, you’re the exact opposite. What was once a good man is now a relentless anarchist. When Christina said I used to stand for something…..I ignored her. I still stand for something Michaels, I stand proud and strong to be keeping this business on its feet, you stand for the very thing you compared me to in a bathroom, a piece…of feces. Sinc walks behind a news desk just feet away from him after he steps inside and takes a seat in a worn out chair located right behind the desk and sits folding his hands atop the surface and stares straight ahead. She was RAPED Brian. She DIED! And what are you doing right now? Doing your best to hate anything and anyone because you want to be the center of attention, the pharoh of the temple, and the messiah of the new age. Let me tell you something Mr. Messiah, SHUT THE HELL UP AND FACE THE FACTS! I am so sick and tired of seeing superstars turning into monsters because they can’t stand losing, and Brian, your just like them. Admit that your hatred and your loathing came from things that were unavoidable or purely your fault. Sinc looks around him and raises his arms in the air with a smile. Does anyone remember this place? Does anyone know what happened here? He takes the time to debrief the current location and explain what it stands for. And you remember this humble abode don’t you Ace Mountain? But I guess I should tell you what this area was used for anyway to give all those wondering viewers a little wake up call. A long time ago back when Sharpman was Joey Dallas, an actual decent wrestler way back when, Rated-X did a little news cast here in this very place. Watch this…. Sinc leans forward and begins to dust off the front of the desk depicting the Rated-X logo, he looks up and makes a shocked expression like he just discovered a rare jewel and then leans back into his chair smirking in a vulgar crack of his lips. You two thought you were so clever and funny with your decisive puns and your off-the-wall phrases. This very set was the result of one of the greatest Rated-X promos of all time, but unlike you Michaels, time progresses. All you do is continue to lack and shoot promos that aren’t anywhere near the caliber of entertainment you USED to put out, am I right? So much anger, so much evil, you must have a demon inside you somewhere, either that or you’re a clone of Virus. But how could I say that, you helped found one of the greatest teams ever made. But the problem is, Rated-X WAS something, but we aren’t living in the past anymore, Rated-X is now a dying notion. Whether they returned or not that doesn’t change anything presently, MWE is a constant shift of balance and you have been out scaled Michaels. Is it fair to be attacking Brian in such a manner? Can this type of jib jab become something to treasure or ignore? When people open a history book they want to see what made America and how the culture has revolutionized our world today, but when they open a book about MWE spotlighting every werestler there has ever been, they want to see who has made the BIGGEST impact, whether Brian should be in that book or not, is up to the readers and the fans. I have to salute you for winning your way back to the top though sir, I am going to have to give you your props where it is deserved. Every time I had a chance to have a little rest and relaxation, hell even before joining this federation I always watched your wrestling matches and admired them, your skills are not to be questioned and your ability to entertain is incomprehensible. But to let you stand there and even attempt to say none of us have the balls to do what you do, that none of us can dream and then make those dreams come true, that’s not something I am going to admit. When you wish for something ridiculous or childish, you think “Hey, wait a minute, why did I ever believe I could get what I wanted?” But after grueling through various matches and watching veterans, idols, and icons continue to be the best, it makes you want to be JUST like them. Then after a while you begin to come to terms that you have the ability to become great after all, and your dreams can become a reality, but when you actually get in there with the best of the best…then…it all goes to hell, the true colors start showing. I refuse to buy into your shameful innuendos. You were what I wanted to become, you were THE guy to beat, and THE guy to be, and I can’t stress enough how much my opinion has changed of you over the months Brian, it’s such a blow, such a hard handed strike to my face to see you become like this. Richard Covell doesn’t matter anymore, Randy doesn’t matter anymore, Christina doesn’t matter anymore, not even what happened to Courtney matters to you anymore, now do you see what I’m saying? NOTHING MATTERS TO YOU ANYMORE! The only thing you seem to be slobbering over is defeating me and becoming the new world champ, and ladies and gentlemen THAT is the mountain that is Brian, ego and a half. Is this the champion you all want? A Sonny Williams remake but with a touch of heel? Because I don’t want that for any of you, I want to give you all a champion that is entertaining to watch and a man you all can be proud of. If I was watching Brian as champ right now and I was still a teen hoping to become a wrestler like him, I would have changed my mind long ago. A fury begins to rise deep within Sinc’s mind, there is a row of television monitors lined up on the front wall of the news station, he reaches for something under the desk and pulls out a remote control, he aims it at one of the TV monitors and turns it on. Brian why don’t you sit back and relax, watch what a fool you are….. He presses a button and a backstage statement fizzes on the screen. I got people in my ear telling me 'You ain't ****', you ain't '****'. You have to tell them 'I AM ****'". Whatever you say, Brian. I am sorry for being successful." - EDITOR'S NOTE: I personally say good riddance. Make like Maxxamillion and GTFO Michaels. (POLL: Are you going to miss Brian Michaels?) Yes: 36% No: 64% Sinc looks disgusted and bites his lip. He can only speculate how Michaels is taking this at the moment and how he must feel right now, but he doesn’t stop there, more contradictory news is going to be heading Brian’s way. You think that’s bad Elmer Fudd, then watch this…. He presses another button and the scene switches to a completely new statement. In the latest turn of events, he and his former "father" have reached an agreement. Michaels has seemingly sold his heart to the devil. As well as not being paid, Michaels will not be allowed a World Championship opportunity as long as he remains at MWE. He is allowed a shot at the MWE United States Championship, or MWE Intercontinental Championship, along with the Tag Team Championships. In return for this, MWE must provide Mr.Michaels with one of the following options: A ) A last match at Crowning Glory 2009, against whoever he pleases. B ) One more match against D.C., before or at Crowning Glory 2009. C ) Must provide Michaels with complimentary strippers whenever he pleases. Now to me, knowing Richard Covell, he'll probably let Michaels have the first. It's got to kill Michaels' ego, knowing he's lost three of his last four matches, and now being down a division. Michaels, his egotistical self, still sounded optimistic on Friday afternoon. "It's no biggie for me. I knew I was going to have to give up things, to move on with my life. I'm on a losing streak, and I knew it was best to step aside and make room for the future," he said at a press conference. Sinc raises a finger in the air and stands up as if a light bulb has just gone off over his head. He turns off the monitor and drops the remote, he may have just cracked this case wide open, he may have finally busted Michaels up enough to get him to see his idiocy. Do you see how stupid you really are Brian? I mean, I could find every single place you’ve ever cut a promo or have ever verbally destroyed someone, and slaughter you with each point you have made, but this cuts the cake. You wanted to walk out on this company and leave MWE in the dust, then you had to whine to daddy dearest, no I’m sorry, fake daddy dearest, to stay only to get told you can’t have a world title shot again, then go BACK to him, whine again, and get one ANOTHER time. Doesn’t that make you YOURSELF a politicker and prove what everyone says about you correct? I think it does, what a surprise. He laughs to himself and rubs his watery eyes from laughing so hard. He stops to think of something else he could show Brian as an eye opener, better yet, something to show OFF to Brian as an eye opener. He takes a few moments to muster what else he wants to rant about, and decides he knows just the thing. There is but ONE more thing I want to point out, a place I don’t think you’ll ever quite get out of your head. You cried about being on a losing streak and how you no longer being able to get a world title shot was “no biggie” yet here you are again now thinking your on top of the world. But if there is one place that is a big connection between you and D.C, it’s gotta to be the one I am about to show you. Sinc walks around the desk and leaves the station slamming the door behind him, but as he leaves the station and shuts the door he only walks into another building of some kind, and as he enters he smiles knowing right away where he is at now. He finally addresses the area and focuses on Brian again. New York Wrestling Promotions, I remember watching you tear D.C. a new one in this very place, I was so into it my man, I was so happy to see you expressing yourself like never before, but that was then this is now. But look at all these pictures Brian, look at all these wrestlers that have become something…it must hurt right? It must hurt you to see these great men succeed you because you can’t seem to succeed yourself anymore… He averts his gaze to a wall to the left of him, pictures of many wrestlers that have gone through this promotion are hung along the wall, Windy Bridges, Aiden Copane, D.C….but no Brian. It was here that you took D.C. away and took him under your wing if you will, yes I remember it all too well, you helped make D.C. what he is today, I haven’t failed to acknowledge that. You saved him when you took him away from here, you helped turn him into a somebody, but it is not him I want to talk about, it’s you. If you’ve seen him in your nightmares Michaels then I am going to say that I have seen you in mine, but I am not like you or him, I don’t get afraid of people who are secretly afraid of me, don’t hide it. It’s taken THIS long for you to finally have what it takes to face me, and D.C.? I haven’t even tussled with him yet but I do plan to one day. When you said the winner of your match at Adrenaline Rush was king of MWE though, that was a bit much. Because if you look at the facts right now buddy boy, you’ll see that you and D.C. are FAR from kings of MWE, maybe kings of BS, but that’s all I can really pick at. He gives the place one last look around, bears a grin, and leaves to once again show himself in this strange darkness outside. No walls or roofs, no shelter or smoke machines, just darkness and himself. But he doesn’t mind, he likes when he has the opportunity for this charade of solitude to be alone and clear his head, he likes to speak for himself and nobody else, the independence he has is what he cherishes most. Too bad it’s not raining, that would have really set the mood wouldn’t it Michaels? Ofcourse it was a pun, but he felt it was needed anyway. You won’t be seeing Sinc tearing his clothes off and being depressed as the rain trickles down on his face, but you will see Sinc discourage Brian to the fullest with all that he has tanked up inside him. I don’t know what routine you’d call my entire speech on you so far Michaels, but I’d call it damn near genius and powerful. You have probably heard it all throughout your time in MWE, you have witnessed every word and every attack, but the difference would be that everything said about you is nothing less than true. You’ve heard it all, you’ve been through it all, but it works Brian, it gets under your skin and it makes you PISSED! And that’s what I want from you at Independence Day, I want you pissed and ready to bang a chainless tiger. Brian I want you so mad that you’ll be willing to do anything, whatever it takes to take me down, THAT’S what I expect out of you. The truth in Sinc’s words could not be more clear or ever compared to or even stated ever again. But as he says such things, such blasphemy in Brian’s perspective, he also has a feeling the type of treatment he would get from a Michaels speech, the type of hatred he would be fed and dealt. Let me predict what your going to say for once, and I am guessing it will be something along the lines of, I’m just another David Whitman? I’m not a fighting champion? Or my favorite, I am not GOOD enough to beat you or to be wearing that belt around my waist, I can’t hear that one enough old pal. You can markout for the dozens of Sharpman clones, D.C. wannabes, and Copane has beens all you want. But the bottom line is I’m something different, I’m the unique one in this family known as MWE, I’m the one that takes the last slab of steak because I deserve it Michaels, yes me, I deserve to take that last bit of glory and shove it down your throat. You’ve already tasted it, you’ve had that juicy morsel and you’ve coveted it. But I want to covet something now, and that is that title and my fans, I could care less what anyone else thinks of me including you. That’s pretty much the truth right there people, all Michaels knows and loves is the fact that he can bring a face down because their just like someone else, because they have the similar routines and the usual tirades that can be compared to various view points, but Sinc is a man that is not going to stand for being the same as everyone else, and he is throwing it right back in Brian’s face. I am going to stand my ground and continue to say you’re the same as all the other heels because it’s true is it not? It’s the same screaming fest and that same fire and hatred that drives guys like me to remain the best or at least try to. You can’t tell me that I’m a liar because you’ve done it before MANY times, you can call me out on whatever you want but the evidence is there. I got where I am today not because of you, not because of D.C, and not because of anyone else, but because I damn well earned it, and I will keep earning every match and every type of title shot I possibly can because this is the man people need, not just another hero and not just another villain, they need unique and they need TALENT. Brian Michaels…. With every word Sinc now says he beats his chest and sharply winces his eyes. A confidence overcomes him and he seizes this emotion and passion into a brave and resilient trio of established words. I. Am. THAT! He stops the beating and takes a second to cool off and carefully arrange the next batch of nouns and verbs to be the finale to Brian Michaels and the final blow to the wound. He takes his time not rushing it, and figures out exactly what it is he wants to say. You’re a legend Brian, and I can’t deny that. But legends don’t live forever, they grow old, they wither, and then they die, the gates of hell are opening up for you, and they reserved a spot with your name on it. I hope never to see you there, and I sincerely hope you have at least once chance to redeem yourself before those gates close forever, goodbye Brian, it’s been nice knowing you. With that, Sinc mysteriously and ferociously walks away. The scene zooms out entirely until an image is coming into view, and Sinc is nothing but a tiny dot on a gigantic picture which looks like some kind of tribute to Michaels. As it becomes clear and evident, the whole picture is revealed. “Brian Michaels, the legend lives on dies.” Sinc, wake up, wake up! What the…that’s weird….why am I hearing voices? I’m the only one who was talking, go away! Get up you big lug! WAKE THE HELL UP! Oh…was I sleeping? Was that all just a…dream? I don’t want to wake up, just a little while longer… After minutes of yelling and trying to awaken Sinc Mercier he gets struck by surprise with a splash of water all over his face. He kicks and moans thinking he’s drowning but once he opens his eyes he finds out where he really is. A broken down bar, but not any bar, the bar where he did his first comedy gig and got his head hit with an empty beer bottle as his father watched in embarrassment. The faces of Mason, Nate, and Christina surround him in worriment. Mason helps him up and dusts his shoulders off, they all are on a stage, the stage Sinc was on when he did his standup. You look like a mess dude. Me? No I’m fine….what’s he doing here? Sinc looks at Nate clearly not liking his presence or even wanting him around right now. Christina and Mason give Sinc a slight scorn and give off a vibe signaling that more important issues are at stake. That doesn’t matter jack off. We have been calling and calling you for ages, then we contacted Nate and he gave us an idea on where you might be, and here you are, an old run down bar with the door handle broken off on a Sunday night in the most dangerous part of San Francisco. I’m fine, for the love of god can’t a guy just have some peace? No…because your shirt is stained in beer for one, for two there are about fifteen cans of it all over the floor, and for three you’re a f*cking idiot. Nate sighs and takes his cowboy hat off holding it in both hands to his lap in a traditional fashion and stares directly at Sinc with a saddened angst. It was really only Nate who knew Sinc had such a bad problem in dealing with depression and doubting himself, Christina knew as well, but not as much and not as deep as Nate did. I know you don’t want me near you right now but your anger with me is ridiculous. Windy was manipulating my mind and I wasn’t myself, I said I was sorry and I mean it, you don’t need to push me away like this, what do you want me to do, beg? Actually yes… Ok Sinc we are trying to HELP you, we are not trying to hurt you and neither is Nate. So why don’t you just come with us, and leave this old dump to rot huh? This is NOT an old dump Mason The Reason, this place has a lot of history with me and it helped me become who I am today. My dad and I were so close every time we’d come here, and I would always do my best to make him proud of me on this stage. But he’s dead and I have to take on an MWE veteran, a man I can’t even think of beating one on one unless it was…well…in my dreams. And knowing my father won’t be there to watch me in such an awesome match makes things worse. Mason just rubs his head making it apparent that none of what anyone is saying is reaching the brain stem of Mercier, so he decides to take the encouragement route and try and build Sinc’s self esteem again. Look man, you have the potential to beat him and we all know it. And your dad has died long ago, you were able to cope with it just fine until now. It has more to do with you thinking you can’t beat Brian, a man who mentored me and somebody that I recently lost to, but that doesn’t matter, I’ve learned to deal with it. If I can move on then so can you. You can beat him, and I know you can, because every superstar has their own way of doing things, you I have learned are able to do certain things others can’t in a miraculous showing and you need to take that ball and run with it. Drinking when your down and out doesn’t help at all. Sinc taps his chin and looks at the three faces gazing at him. A worried Nate, an infuriated Christina, and a calm and collected Mason Resoan. He then looks at himself, his beer stained white shirt, his blue jeans, and his shoes, and just sobs his head. Your right, I shouldn’t be acting like this, I have a match to focus on and a PPV to go to. I have the utmost faith in Brian to give us an epic match, and I believe me and him can really sell the show, and with that faith and belief win or lose, I will always remember Independence Day and the match Mr. MWE gave me. Let’s go guys, I need to drop the kids off at the pool. Everyone looks at him scratching their heads, and then they get it, he needs to go to the bathroom. They look disgusted and displeased but they are glad Sinc is ok, and they follow him out the door with one thing in mind, and two words to shine above them all. SIIIIIIINCCCCCCCCC!!!!!!!!!!
