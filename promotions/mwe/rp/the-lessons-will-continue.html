The lessons will continue....; A Grand Mystique RP

Nov 19 2009

The Grand Mystique

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This scene begins like every other, in a dark, dusty room. In the corner, we see a window that is covered by a blind. Despite this, this opening provides the only source of light as sunlight pours in from around the narrow slits of the sides. A shower of grainy dust shines in the light but a gloomy almost monochromic shadow is cast over that which is not within the streams of light. In the middle of the room, against the back wall, is a hospital bed. It is laid flat, the bed sheet are perfectly laid but absolutely filthy. The rest of the room is much the same, with a pair of cabinets and a mirror opposite the bed, all afflicted with a grimy shadow. It would be a very normal hospital room if it was clean but the desolate state of the room makes it seems like a relic from the Cold War. Life appears to have abandoned this room and tension fills the air. There is a slow tapping that is felt around the room. It gets louder, causing the wooden floorboards to vibrate but stops as it appears to be just outside. Then a calm breeze is felt and the blind wavers ever-so-slightly over in the corner. The camera shifts forward but the blind snaps up, causing the camera to snap around in shock and surprise. Suddenly the dark, hulking shape of the Grand Mystique comes into view. “I’ve spent more time in hospitals than care to remember. It puts my recent experiences in perspective. I come here and I remember the true lessons of loss and grief.” He is barely visible as he lurks in the shadowy corners. His dark, body-length jacket hides him almost entirely from view. His voice is dark, intimidating and threatening. His presence denotes a feeling of absolute foreboding. “But whatever I happens, I remember that I am in control. Destiny is like a vehicle. It may be currently out of control but it can be taken back. Having your hands on the wheel is not unlike having hands around a throat.” He traces his fingers through the thick covering of dust on the table in front of him. He looks at it with contempt before flicking it off. “MWE is becoming a different company right before my eyes. These punks don’t know they are made. Instead of appreciating what you have, you just talk about what you don’t. Shit has been laid on a plate for you but nobody has laid down for me. Everyone has tried to keep me down but I refuse to let it happen on anyone else’s terms.” His disapproval for his recent situation is clear. He is glad that the youth movement is working in MWE but the gratitude is not there. The respect is absolutely lacking and that is something he clearly detests. He stares out through the window into the bright blue beyond. “This streak may not come to an end at Mayhem, or even at WrestleWars but it will end soon enough. The lesson that I have learnt still needs to be heeded by those who refuse to be taught. Loss and victory are not so different. History is only ever recalled by the victor and he who falls is left reeling in the aftermath, dealing with the lies and histrionics retold by he who deems himself worthy and superior. Pain is only ever around the corner and is constantly stalked by loss. Risk is the differential.” Another breeze fills the room but this time it’s significantly stronger. It causes the dust to blow around and in a second it seems like a sandstorm has hit the building. And then....nothing.....but blinding light and a rushing noise that sounds like a thousand mile an hour wind taking everything before its path. But it’s over before it starts. Life can turn that quickly. “It’s a serious looking wound, but it was fairly shallow.” The silence of ignorance is inimitable. “You have no nerve damage but the scar could be quite significant.” We see the same room, except it is now full of colour and light. It is almost sparkling clean. We can hear a hive of human activity from just around the corner but inside the room are a doctor is his typically clean white coat and a pale young patient. He is examining the thick bandage wrapped around the head of the teen. “We stitched it up and you’ll be fine. We’ll keep you in though, for a couple of days, just to keep an eye on you.” More ignorance. Just an empty gaze into the mirror in front of his bed. “It’s a miracle you survived. Your friend and teacher are extremely ill.” The doctor finishes and leaves quietly, sensing he will not get any answers from this young man. Hardly surprising, given the traumatic events that have lead to his stay there. On his way out of the ward, the doctor asks a nurse to keep an eye on him. His silence is concerning, the after-effects of shock is difficult to determine and trauma is always difficult to recover from. The nurse comes in. “My friend, my teacher who came in with me. Where are they?” He speaks with a blank stare and no emotion. He comes across as totally drained. “I can take you around if you want.” “I want to go on my own.” “OK...they are in the Intensive Care Unit. It’s two floors up. I can get someone to take you and drop you off.” “Thank you.” A minute later, he is being wheeled up to the unit where his friend and his wrestling teacher are being treated. The porter parks him in between their beds and leaves. Alex stands up and looks over the two people who have helped him become more than just another pupil. He had found a calling, he had found himself. “I want to thank you both. You found me in the deepest hole and pulled me out.” His friend Reid is almost unrecognisable. His face is purple and swollen around the eyes and nose. His entire hairline is covered with a thick bandage that seems like it is holding his head together. Mr Wood on the other hand appears to be sleeping and you wouldn’t be able to tell much difference. He is wearing an oxygen mask which is hooking up to a machine, which is quietly pumping up and down on the other side. A large white patch covers his abdomen. Alex stands between, using the wheelchair as a prop. “It is not fair. You should not be here.” He slams his fist down ruefully on the shoulder of the chair. “What do I do now? I don’t know if I could do it without you.” “You were there when I needed you. You put me on the path and got me rolling. Everything you did was for my benefit. For that....” A pretty young nurse enters. “Are you ok?” She has a calm aura about her. “No, it should have been me.” “No....no, look these things happen for a reason.” “But this was too much. It......should have happened like this. We had plans.....this screws everything up!” “I’m sorry.” She places a soft hand on his shoulder. “You can change your plans and your friend could get back to normal.” “But they look so ill.” “They are seriously ill but stable at the moment. That’s why they are here. We will do everything we can.” She carries out a couple of quick tests on each patient before she turns and leaves, but is unable to concentrate or look at anyone but the mysterious young man who is keeping vigil over his friends. He seems so innocent and desperate for his friends to be well. He sits on the edge of Mr Thomas’ bed. “Sir, you have taught me so much. I may never be able to put into words what that means. I will never forget what you taught me.” “I promise never to back down, face a challenge head on and I will succeed and become the best.” He speaks with such a negative tone that makes him seem absolutely certain they can’t get better. Even though he was told just now that they could. “And Reid, you showed me how to become innovative, go beyond the regular and to never be boring. Our dream will live on.” “Everything I achieve, is going to be down to you two.” He stands up on his own for the first time. He is not leaning on anything. He sees both people he had called friends for so long and sees something different.”And how I took everything you said, all that you gave me and made it better. I’ve made it all so easy for you. Your minds are clear. No worries, no troubles, just absolute solitude.” He turns away and walks to the door. He hesitates for a second and it looks like he will turn around but he is struck by.... BEEP!!!! BEEP!!!! Alex looks around but sees only white coated and nurses in their powder blue outfits surround him and the beds in the room. “Get the kid out of here.” “He’s in de-fib.” “Get me the paddles.” *shouts*”GET HIM OUT OF HERE!!” Before he knows it, Alex is pulled and tugged out of the room. He can’t see anything except the hive of activity. He has to assume that it can’t be good. However....he does not seem to care. He walks out the dimly lit ward and heads down back to his room in the much busier ward that he came from. He closes the door behind him. His head falls back on the door and he allows the rush of life to pass behind him. He felt happy and secure here. Life could be ignored. This was what he needed. The aches head had become searing and his neck felt like it was being ripped apart. He walked over to the full length mirror that is two yards away from the end of the bed. Grabbing the end of the dressing, he unravels it slowly. He knows he should be nervous, knowing that this wound has far-reaching implications. And given his professional ambitions, he should fear for his future. But he doesn’t and he is relaxed. He looks at the mirror and sees the epitome of composure looking back at him. “There is nothing I can’t overcome.” He turns his neck, flinching as his fingertips gently rub over the torn edges of his skin. The wound neatly runs across his hairline behind the ear. It is almost black with blood, flakey and brittle to the touch. It tingles, sending a short, sharp burst of pain through his neck. Pain, he considered, was an excellent way of triggering reality. GM roused himself back around to a state of self-awareness. He had drifted off and caught himself remembering a long lost chronicle of his existence. Funny, because he is looking at himself in this mirror and it felt so fresh. “This nearly ended everything.” He pulls back his hair and tips his head sideways to reveal the now-faded scar just behind his ear. It looks slightly pinkish and thin. “But I fought against the serious reality of this injury and emerged the other side alone. Stronger, smarter, better.” He rubs the scarred tissue. It wrinkles up and stretches taut as he moves his finger around. “And it led to the MWE World Championship. The title that is coveted by everyone I come across in the company. Its’ fate intertwines mine with the best in the history. I’ve gone toe to toe with them all; Hall of Famers and those who will go down as being the best that has been produced by the system. And I came from outside and wrecked it all. I stood tall as the first World Champion not made in the original incarnation of the organisation. It was a reign which signalled much change in the company. Regardless of the success of that reign, the achievement stood tall. I played my part. Change has come to the MWE.” He refocuses on the mirror. His drive and determination from his dark eyes reflect back like lasers, off the mirror and into the camera. “And the match this Monday demonstrates that transformation. I have to tag with Leon Roberts, the week after I helped beat down his GWC stable-mate Zak Warner. And we have to face Sinc Mercier and Eric Derf. The successes and accomplishments of myself and the three men I will share the ring with makes this a potentially massive match, and yet the story in which it is set is diverse yet equally significant.” “I see desperate men, all around me.” The whites of his eyes become visible, as they widen dramatically. “A devil, anxious to stake his claim as a legitimate champion. An enigma, eager to show why he will become a great future and a man who is coming back from the grave to back up the ego of his legend and the legend of his ego.” “I feel sorry for you Sinc, because your spark has disappeared, just like mine. Only I wouldn’t need to arrange a three on one gangbang to beat Zak fucking Warner. I feel sick for helping because this war means nothing to me.” GM falls pale at the mention of the event. He clearly feels like he betrayed all that he stood for by standing up and picking a side in a war that he feels no part for. “You and Derf stand there, proud to represent the company, next to me. And I feel nothing but nausea. I feel like throwing up because I had anything to do with the plan that was put in place to rescue you from getting your ass kicked again.” He grimaces furiously. All of his encounters with Sinclair Mercier have brought out a distinct aggression and hostility from GM, unseen since his encounters with Windy Bridges. “Sinc, CG for you was a summary of your whole career – a giant disappointment. I took your bullseye and ran with it. The only difference is, I put myself out there and got hit.....repeatedly. And I bet you love the idea of ranting about. I know you will because you are too stupid and boring to think of anything else. You won’t take the risk of furthering yourself. You just can’t resist retreading the same boring old ground.” He shakes his head and punches the corner of the mirror. It cracks but doesn’t fall apart. His clenched fist begins to bleed slightly. “At CG I made you meaningful and memorable – something you failed to accomplish on your own. I took your spotlight and everything that means so much to you. I gave those watching enduring images, none of them feature your success. It will be etched in the memory of millions as the setting of your pain and my success.” He spreads his fingers on his bloody hand as it begins to flow a little more freely. He catches a drop, trickling down his arm on his index finger. Unusually, blood has not been shed by GM’s hand since the fateful night at Crowning Glory. “I will not suffer for your Crowning Glory failings, your inability to make a memory for yourself at that massive event. So try it, try to make this about revenge and throttle me within an inch of my life because every bit of pain that you felt at Crowning Glory will not compare to what you can do to me now. I put you through hell and you are still crawling out, clawing desperately for the light, for revenge.” He licks the blood droplet off his finger. Its’ slightly metallic flavour rings familiar in his mind and sends memories flooding. His scar, Windy, Randy Covell, DC, CSB, the damn World Title. A mixture of good and bad all brought back by the mere taste of a drop of his own blood. “Trouble is, will you remember as the one who replaced you as the chosen one or the one who got shot down? I dare you to underestimate me at your peril because the evil that faces you from across the ring is ready to explode again. I’m pig-sick of the disrespect, from you, from everyone. A former MWE champion should command absolute respect. Will you give me that?” He spreads his arms out, to a gesture that would normally command respect. “The prevailing light of your success is seeping through the darkness of my world; I will never regain the heat that people expect to see from me while you and your kind are around to extinguish the evil. Your perpetual happiness is radiating and makes me feel ill. I hate that there is a constant need for a hero, someone to stand up for the masses when the masses don’t really know what is best for them.” He points down, quietly supporting the point he is trying to assert. “So sit down Eric Derf.” He smiles and in an instant, he seems more relaxed than we have seen him in some time. “I guess you have been taking lessons from Sinc; if you truly believe was worried about you as a threat, then you’re an egotistical prick just like him. Since CG you have proved to me and everyone else why you will never be a credible champ. You were never on my mind, never a factor. I think you are truly overrated and nothing will make me as high on you as everyone else is. I will not drink the Derf kool-aid because it is full of shit.” It is very clear that GM is one of few who doesn’t appreciate Eric Derf. He speaks with an angry disdain and he refills on that contempt as he remembers Derf’s latest interview. “So prove me wrong. Show me the money Mr MITB. Give us a glimpse of what you promise to be. Show me why you are rated so highly by DC, why he is backing out out of a match with you every week and why everyone seems to think you will be the key in this GWC thing.” “You can never take the credit for something you never earned in the first place. So I want you to face up with me and rip the respect clean out of me.” GM clenches his bloody fist tightly. The fresh blood drips out to the floor is neatly appropriate, given GM’s expectation of the lead Derf-herder. He examines the deep wound with an inquisitive look. “You’re just another pretty boy type who is trying dig its’ heels into this company. We don’t need to see that now. Now is the time to prove yourself. Show us that you would tear out your own heart to get ahead and put everything ahead of yourself. If you’re going to be Mr MWE one day, then now is the time to show what you are made of.” GM is determined to get every last drop of commitment out of Derf because as he clearly sees someone who is not willing like he is to fight the cause. His tone is deep, almost a demanding growl and the blood on his hands is a very physical reminders of the boundaries that the Grand Mystique has pushed in the past. “It is time for evil to stand over all that is good and well. Your heroes are nothing but boys masquerading as men; the protagonists who are always reaching for the next level. Weaknesses are kept hidden and their strengths are overexposed. The villain is he who asks the questions, and poses a challenge. Heroes are expected to have the solutions. But on one day, the villain will have the answer and things will be made right.” Contentment surges through his system and an aggressive, sadistic smile escapes, curling his lip ever so slightly. “Or the Mad Hatter can emerge from his closet and ass fuck you all.” He pauses, allowing the brief instance of humour to be replaced by building tension. “And just like a bullet or a knife, one time is all that is needed, except for Leon Roberts.” A familiar foe, albeit from a distance, GM and Virus have similar careers, filled with success and disappointment, highs and lows. And while GM observed Virus become a dominant force for a time in EW, his recent efforts have been regularly overshadowed by MWE legends like DC. But to GM, he foresees Leon Roberts, as nothing but a minor distraction, an annoyance and in this instance, a handicap. “Leon is totally misfocussed. A pantomime villain who could be MWE champion if he took wrestling seriously. But that will never happen because he just can’t concentrate. So because this match has been booked for me to work with this lumbering piece of meat, who will probably cause us to lose this match. Sinc and Derf, don’t think this means anything. It may as well be a handicap match. What does this do for MWE? Team GWC? I’m not sure I care. It’s not that important. Not yet. But the time will come.” GM reconsiders all that he said. The impact, the potential for retribution and reprisal. It was all there and he was content that he had begun to play his part in Monday’s match being worthwhile viewing. He looks up to the mirror and sees something in the reflection which achieves both calm and concern in him. Something familiar but also not seen in some time, stares back at him. “And something to remember me by. The name is Grand Mystique. You are not my friends. I am not familiar with you and want to have less than nothing to do with you so show your superior some deserved respect.” The scene fades out to the harsh sound of a smashed mirror falling to the floor.
