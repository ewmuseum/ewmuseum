Respect

May 4 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
*The scene opens with a very focused and seeming some what angry Peter Kaymakcian sitting on a stool with his left hand under his chin in a fist and his right hand clenched in a fist of rage. Peter is wearing a white-t shirt with a leather jacket over it and some baggy light black jeans and some red and black air forces. Peter's eyes move back and forth as he looks at everyone passing by when finally a backstage personnel walks up to him. His name is Al and he is five foot four inches and his hair is fading back. He has on glasses and is a bit chubby, but not to chubby. His voice is shaking in fear as he gives Peter the news on his request.* Al: Sorry Mr. Kaymakcian, but your request for some in-ring air time was declined. You'll have to wait till next week. *Al cowers in fear as he expects Peter to hit him for bringing him the bad news, but instead Peter just shakes his head no. Peter simply gets up and walks towards the stage. The scene then cuts to an empty arena with no one in sit, but a camera crew waiting for a member of the MWE roster to make a promo. Instead Peter comes out with no music playing and he walks down the ramp and straight to the ring. Peter grabs a mic and begins to speak as if he was disrespected and his talent is being wasted.* Peter: What do I have to do to get a decent match around here? Seriously my opponent for this week is Semi? No sorry I phrased that wrong my victim for this week is Semi. I am tired of the main eventers such as Brian Micheals, Ace Cannon, A-Rod, Sharpman, and so on and so on ducking me. They go to management to make sure that I am stuck with these ICW rejects so I can't steal there top spots. So this week after I destroy Semi, just like I did all those other ICW rejects I better get a main event match because I am not a curtain jerker. *The scowl on Peter's face is still there and he just looks around the empty arena. Peter continues sounding bitter and ungrateful at the opportunity MWE has given him. He is borderline disrespectful yet he doesn't seem to care.* Peter: You fools in the front office can't protect your friends forever and for every week that I am misused more blood will fall starting with Semi this week. That loser won't know what hit him and his blood will be on your hands. The people payed to see a wrestling match, but I am going to give them a massacre. I will no longer be disrespected with opponents such a Semi. I'll hit that fat bastard with some Jenny Craig and watch him burn. The only way Semi could possibly beat me is if you promised him a whole twinkie and sex with some of the divas around here. To bad no women in there right mind would touch that disgusting buffoon. *Peter is extremely cynical and has decided to walk around the ring to clear his head as he doesn't seem of sound mind right now. Peter stops and puts his foot on the middle rope and runs his right hand through his hair. Peter continues on his rant in a monotonous tone.* Peter: I deserve respect, no I demand it. My match this week isn't the only reason why I am feeling disrespected it is also due to the fact that I am not on the Tribulation card. Instead of putting me on the card they have a scavenger match. Semi is in an unfortunate spot because it seems that my work in ICW has done nothing and it looks like Semi will be my statement this week. Those fools in the back will realize that if they keep playing with this fire everyone will get burned. The backstage personnel, the trainers, the superstars, and especially the front office. Semi will be the first of my many victims until the wrongs against me have been righted. Semi by the time that the match is over not one damn person will recognize you. You won't even look human. *The scowl on Peter's face is getting bigger as he continues on with all the injustices brought up against him. Peter's voice is filled with hatred and anger all of it directed at Semi and the bookers.* Peter: Semi you may think you are tough and you may think that you can last against me in a match, but your wrong on all accounts. I am going to show you how tough you really aren't and I am going to make you beg for mercy from my onslaught. The pain I will inflict on you may even be to gruesome for Satan himself. If you still think that you are tough enough to hang with me I'll show you why you aren't. *With that said Peter walks over to the ropes and rolls underneath the bottom rope and starts to rummage underneath the ring. The camera zooms in on Peter and it shows a sick and sadistic smile across the face of Peter, but we can't see why as the ring apron is hiding what Peter has under there. Peter slowly pulls his arms from underneath the ring to reveal that he is holding barbwire with his bare hands. Peter wraps the barbwire around his right hands turning into barbwire knuckles. Peter smiles as he looks down at the barbwire and then the camera. Peter says something in a sadistic voice.* Peter: Kids go ahead and try this at home. It won't hurt that much. *Peter looks at his right fist wrapped in barbwire and out of nowhere Peter just begins to unload on himself with the barbwire wrapped fist! Blood is pouring profusely from the top of Peter's head and the sick bastard is just smiling about it. Peter takes his left hand and rubs it on his face. Peter then walks towards the camera and smears his blood all over the camera. Peter sends Semi a deadly message with his last words. He speaks as if he has lost his mind.* Peter: Semi the things this freak is going to do to you in that squared circle. These things would throw me in jail if done outside that ring. Just think if I've done all of this to myself what will I do to you? Semi be afraid, very afraid because I don't even know what is to become of you after this match. My best guess is that you will become a shut in and be afraid of the outside for the rest of your life. You'll become a shut in with your family carrying the burden of your destroyed psyche. Have fun. *Peter just laughs cynically before punching the camera with his barbwire wrapped fist as the scene fades to black.*
