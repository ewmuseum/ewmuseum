May 1 2009

The spatter of rain drops fall on the old dirty pane of a window. A small flash of lightning cracks and illluminates the room to show an array of old liquor bottles, some peeling wallpaper and a rocking chair facing the window that is opposite of the only door in the room. Some footsteps are heard walking down a hallowed hallway. By the sound of the footsteps it seems as if the person walks with a bit of a limp. Suddenly the footsteps stop and teh dooor opens and an old feeble man is seen walking in. The walks towards the rocking chair where he sits with a huff. He stares blankly at the window dripping with riain water as he slowly reaches into a pocket in his jacket and pulls out a metal flask. A crack of lightning hits as he takes a hearty swig from it. He begins to rock back and forth just staring out of the window into the rainy night. There are more footsteps a bit more heavy and strong. The door to the room swings open wildly and a rather large shadow is standing in the doorway. Without even turning to see who is in the door the old man smiles and speaks. OLD MAN: I have been expecting you...its been a while Damien. MAN IN DOOR: (in almost a growling tone) Why to you insist on still calling me that. OLD MAN: That is your birth name isn't it. The man in the door slowly starts to walk into the room and towards the old man in the chair, he walks to the window and leans against the wall and faces the chair. MAN: Here is your money...( he throws and envelope into the old man's lap) OLD MAN: Is this more of that drug money Damien? Who did you have to beat up for it this time? MAN: STOP CALLING ME DAMIEN! AAHHG....Its not drug money...I earned it the hard way...the right way. Yes someone did get hurt for it but its not what you think. OLD MAN: Really...then what is it that should change what i think Damien. MAN: I...I found a way to control my anger...a way to vent...legally. OLD MAN: And that is. A loud crack of thunder hits as a rather large bolt of lightning flashes and we see both men fully for the first time. MAN: I wrestled for it. I have become a wrestler... OLD MAN: A wrestler? Really now? Well what is the name you go by now then Mr. Wrestler. The sound of sarcasm and almost hate is heard ringing in the old mans feeble voice. MAN: Blight. My name is Blight. OLD MAN: Well then Blight let me ask you this. Was it satifying to know that this money is still blood money? That people had to still get hurt for it? BLIGHT: Yes it was...for the first time in my life i earned something and for the first time why the hell aren't you happy for me. OLD MAN: But I am...I always knew you would be great at what you would find truly satisfying and now I can go happy. BLIGHT: What is that supposed to mean? OLD MAN: Blight this will be the last time we shall meet. I grow old and feeble, I surely cannot go on like this for much longer. The rain begins to pour harder and the thunder rolls over heavier. BLIGHT: No you can't just give up like that. You have to--- OLD MAN: I have nothing left to wait for my son. I have lived to see many days and many nights. I have seen my son grow from a young lad to finally be a man. There is nothing left a father could want. Now I am at peace with myself...and with you. The old man takes a swig from his flask once more as another bolt of lightning hits. His hand that is holding the flask slightly opens and the flask drops to the floor with a loud pang. The old man takes one last gasp of air and looks at Blight. OLD MAN: My son I see myself in everything you have done in your life from failure to prospers. Now go and be better than me. The old man's eyes close and the chair slowly stops rocking. BLIGHT: Dad...I'm sorry. Blight picks up the flask and pockets it. He then turns and picks up the old man and puts him on his shoulder and begins to leave the room. He walks to the back of the house and lays the old man down in his bed. He proceeds to pull the sheets up over the mans face. He walks over to a closet and rummages a bit before finding a shovel. He turns and looks at his father's body laying under the sheet and walks out of the room with the shovel. BLIGHT: Now truly being alone will bring the hell of life...and I will unleash only what i have grown to know...they will bleed my fury. END SCENE END SCENE.
