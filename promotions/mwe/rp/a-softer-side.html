A Softer Side

Sep 20 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A door to a decently furnished hotel room opens up. In walks the deranged madman of the MWE, Jekyl. He sits on the edge of his hotel room bed watching a tape of the promo that was made by Sinc Mercier. In his fingers he twiddles a black rose. QUOTE The camera is still zoomed on Sinc as he is still closing his eyes and crying slow tears. After a few moments Sinc opens his eyes and looks at his ex-wife again. This time she is awake looking at him, smiling at him. She slowly strokes Sinc’s cheek happily but exhausted. Sinc: I’m sorry again babe….you deserve better than me….you really do… The camera zooms out a bit getting more of Barbara in the shot as Barbara nods her head “no” slowly. Sinc laughs a bit as he looks at Barbara in a very caring way. Sinc: You always were persistent….always know who was just right for you and when he was….you chose me…and I chose you….my dad was right you know….I am an idiot…I am an idiot for cheating on you and not seeing the beauty I have right in front of me… Barbara is able to get in a small chuckle followed by a cough as she grasps her oxygen mask. Sinc leans over to hug her, he then proceeds to kiss her forehead and pet her hair with a loving gesture. Sinc: Don’t worry…we are here…together again. Nothing will ever change that. Watching on, Jekyl takes a glimpse of the rose and begins to remember a time of his life that he tried so hard to forget, a time in his life where he led a semi-normal life, the scene fades into a flashback. ****************~~~~Flashback~~~~****************** The scene fades in to Jekyl before he was the madman we know today, before he was know notoriously as Jekyl, when he was known as Ryder James, his birth name, about the age of 25. Ryder walks into his house, Oak flooring, Dark brown walls, picture frames scatter over the walls showing an early Jekyl and a strange woman. Directly across from the door is the staircase to go upstairs, just off to the right is the dining room which leads into the kitchen to the left of the stairs is the living room, a fireplace on the outside wall, black leather couch against the staircase wall, cherry oak coffee table on top of a designer rug. He puts down his suitcase, as he sees his wife, Carla, sitting in the living room, going through some old photos, with a half empty bottle of Bolinger 75. Now Ryder was no stupid man, and he knew that his gorgeous wife drank a lot of wine when she was troubled. But no matter what, this was one of the women whom Ryder could never forget. She looks up at Ryder as he walks into the living room and sits down beside her. Ryder: Whats wrong Carla? Carla is a beautiful woman, dirty blond hair, sea green eyes, pouty lips, hourglass figure. At this point she looks like she has been crying for hours. Carla: Sweetie, I don't know what to do..This relationship is going nowhere, I mean....married for almost a year, you going between jobs, the mortgage is slipping... I want a divorce.... Ryder just looks at her trying to register what was just said. His eyes blink a few times before coming back to reality. Ryder: Excuse me? You want a divorce? Carla: Yes...In the one year we've been together, you have gone through three jobs and were barely paying the mortgage on this house... A tear escapes Carla's eye and drips onto the floor. Ryder sits with his face buried in his hands, the words "I want a divorce repeat over and over in his head... As he looks up his eyes start to become red... Ryder: You gotta be kidding me... Carla the only reason I haven't had a steady job is because I haven't found the right one for me, and no matter what we always get by... Carla: I don't want to "Just get by.." I want to not have to worry about money... I'm going to my mothers tonight, I will be back for my stuff Tuesday. Carla looks at one last photograph which shows the two of them holding each other on the dance floor of their wedding night, both happy, unaware of the future... Ryder: Fine...You know what? Go...If you think our world will change by Tuesday, then you have high expectations....When we got married, we took vows...I changed myself for you, i stopped my ways, and did it for YOU... and things get rough? Get out of my sight...Before i change back to my old self... Carla Rushes out the door with her suitcase dragging along. Ryder walks to the door and leans on the door frame as Carla backs out of the driveway and speeds down the street... *************~~~~~~Months later~~~~~~************** It has been 6 months since the divorce took place... During the divorce he lost his house, his car, and most of his possessions. All he had left were his clothes and his money, kept his job and got a place of his own in the downtown Toronto area. Through the strain of the divorce and loss of his possession, Ryder started to slowly slip out of his sanity, to lose it all and gain nothing, he is alone, surviving as he once did as a child, but with a little more to his name than before. It's 3am, the apartment is dark the only source of light comes form a nearby candle, Ryder stares at the flame and begins to speak to himself. Ryder: The world is full of pain, regret, and suffering, yet once again it has hit me hard, to lose hope of all that I thought was right....People will suffer and it will be by my hands... **************~~~~~~End Flashback~~~~~~***************** Jekyl sits up and looks at the T.V which is now fuzzy with static. He looks down at the black rose, then back up at the T.V....The rose is crushed in his hands as he thinks back to the flashback, then back to Sinc's promo... The breeze from the window rushes over Jekyl, the anger and hate Jekyl feels fills the atmosphere with the intensity of a raging fire. Jekyl: Sinc, you are no different than Sonny Williams... You cry over the lost, over the people who left you...You create pain for yourself over the people who abandoned you. I know how it feels to lose a loved one in a relationship, yet I use it to my advantage.. I focus my pain and anger, unleash it upon my opponents and those who cross me...You are no different. Jekyl stands up and walks over to his hotel room bay window and looks out at the nigh horizon of the city, the city lights partially illuminate the sky, high rises scattered throughout the city. Jekyl lowers his head. Jekyl: Just like I told Sonny, you can't fight for someone else, you have to fight for your self, fight for own right. The thought of you sitting over you ex-wife's body reminds me of when Xavier was leaning over his burnt up wife's body, truly pathetic....I should of been the one to injure your wife and give you more incentive to fight... I haven't lead the best lifestyle, but I leave it because it suits my liking; The love and thrill of pain, the thrill of torture, the infliction of fear. Jekyl turns around and raises his head, a tear runs down his face. Jekyl: I'm not as most people would like to think, bad... I am like this planet...Solid hard surface that's impossible to get through, with a soft hot core...I do what I do to not only satisfy myself, but I do it to help people realize who they truly are. Did you know if you put a person in a position that they fear their own death, you realize what lengths they would go to to save their own skin? Once they admit this they truly start to live...You shouldn't be leaning over your broken ex-wife, for the sole reason that she left YOU... she did it for a reason, because she no longer loved you, yet you come crawling back to her.... truly pathetic.... Jekyl heads towards the door and stops with his hand on the knob. He turns around and smiles which would send chills down the spine of Satan himself. Jekyl: I would love to continue this promo, but I have other lives to change.... See you at MNM Sinc, just promise me you wont bring that horrid ex-wife of yours..... The door slams shut, the camera focuses on the T.V to show the lobby camera, Jekyl holds out a gun to the receptionist as the screen cuts to static. -Fade- ((OOC: I know this isn't really part of his gimmick but i wanted to show a side of Jekyl not many people see))
