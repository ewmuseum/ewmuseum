Time to start from the ground up

Jul 23 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The scene opens with a large view of a beautiful suburban cul-de-sac. There are mini vans, children running around playing, beautiful green grass, and happy smiling couples. Suddenly the cul-de-sac is shown moving downward! As we zoom out we see Peter standing in the middle of a ghetto holding a picture of the cul-de-sac we were just seeing. There are rusted and junked cars on the lawn, broken down chain link fences, the grass is brown and disgusting, all the neighbors stare at Peter angrily, and there seems to be only one working car in the entire neighborhood, his. He mutters to himself very angrily. Peter: I'm going to kill you Jason. He calms himself down and thinks well lets just check out the house first before making any judgements. He takes one look at the house and his mouth drops. He quickly takes out his phone and dials Jason's number. Jason picks up after a few rings. He berates Jason for ripping him off, but Jason keeps a calm demeanor through out the entire conversation. Peter: What the hell were you thinking? Jason: So I take it you noticed the slight differences between the picture and the actual neighborhood? Peter: YA THINK?!? You need to fix this ASAP. Jason: Sorry bro, but I can't. Peter: What do you mean you CAN'T? Jason: We'll the check has already been cleared so you're kind of stuck there, but look at the bright side.... Peter: WHAT BRIGHT SIDE? I'll be shot before I even enter the house? Jason: No, no, no, no, no, well maybe. But I was talking about after a few MWE shows you'll be able to move out and get into a better house. So just sit tight for a few days and everything will be back to normal. Also their might be a surprise when you come inside the house, okay bye. Jason quickly hung up the phone. He looks up at the sky and thought to himself what next? He shakes his head as he walks into the house. He passes by the dead grass around him and notices the broken pathway leading to the house. He is finally at the door and when he goes to open it, to his surprise Jason opens the door for him. He looks at Jason shocked and Jason just shouts with joy. Jason: Hey roomie! Peter looks at him curiously. He is unsure if what Jason has just told him is the truth or if Jason is just messing with him. He answers Jason in a question. Peter: Roomie? Jason: Yeah, well since all you could afford was this place and I needed someone to help with rent, bills, food, and well everything, so I sold you this place. Peter is now angry at Jason. He yells at Jason with passion in his voice and eyes. Peter: You did what?!? You know what I had to do to get out of this place? Jason: You used to live here? I thought you lived next door to me? Peter: NO! I meant a place like this. You know how many matches I had to win, how many countless hours of training I had to do in order to create the life I had. Jason: Let me give you the grand tour. Peter just slaps his head in frustration. He follows Jason inside the home. He walks around and notices that the place looks better from the outside. After Peter cools for about a half an hour he finally decides to hear Jason out. Peter: So how long are we hear for? Jason: For six months. Peter: Okay I think I can survive that. However dude since you tricked me into moving here, your pick up dinner for the first night. Jason: Cool what do you want? Peter: Plain large pizza. And pick up a two liter bottle of coke or pepsi, while your there. Jason: All right I'll be back in a few. Jason exits the house and heads towards the pizza parlor. Peter can't help, but think to himself he can't be mad at Jason. After everything they've been through together. Jason was the only one who believed that he could make into the business. So if living in this run down crappy house is the price he has to pay for what Jason's done for him, so be it. As Peter is sitting alone in the home suddenly there is a knock at the door. Peter walks over towards the door and opens it. When he opens, he sees a beautiful and sexy latina. She is about five foot seven, so taller then Alexis Black, has brown eyes, brown curly hair, thin, with a C cup rack, and a gorgeous smile. She begins to speak in a happy manor. Peter on the other hand decides to flirt, however this girl is to smart for it. ?: Hello I'm Natasha. Peter: Why hello, I'm the man of your dreams Peter. Now what may I do for you. Natasha: Okay then, Peter I just came to welcome you to the neighborhood. Peter: Well do you welcome all new comers like this or just the fine ones? Natasha: No just the ones with the big egos. Peter: Damn girls that was kind of cold. Why don't you stay for dinner so you can warm up? Natasha: Sorry, but garbage day is tuesday. I'm guessing thats where you found your "game"? Peter: Ouch that was a vicious burn. Natasha: Anything else? Peter: No my ego has suffered enough. Natasha: Good well then I'll be on my way. Natasha heads off as Jason comes back from the pizza parlor. They sit down and begin to joke around and relax, like two college roommates. Jason pulls out an eighteen pack from a brown bag. He takes one out. Peter: Dude come on? Jason: My bad bro, you want one? Peter: AA? Jason: Dude we'll play COD later. Peter: No dude, I'm a recovering alcoholic. Jason: Oh so I'm guessing you don't want one? Peter: You think? It was the reason I had to leave MWE, wrestling, and Kaitlin. Look I don't want any part of that stuff. Jason: Okay cool bro. Speaking of MWE, who are you facing this week? Peter: Alexis Black. Jason: Ah so you're facing Sexi Lexi. I bet you're going to enjoy pinning her. Peter begins to crack up laughing. Jason: Dude what so funny? He wipes a tear away from his eye as he begins to speak. Peter: Alexis Black is a dude. Jason is shocked. He begins to speak in disbelief as if he has just heard a juicy rumor. Jason: No freaking way dude. No one would name a little dude a little girls name. You're fucking with me. Peter: Dead serious man. Jason: You sure he's always been a dude? Peter: I don't know to be honest. I mean most other dudes in this business don't need a step ladder to reach my knee. Both begin to laugh. Jason goes along with the ribbing of Alexis Black. Jason: So what you're saying is that men of Alexis Black's stature are in short supply? Peter: Well there are people who think little of him. Both continue laughing. After a few moments a laughter Peter begins to speak seriously. Jason listens intently because he knows this is important to Peter. His voice is serious and apprehensive. He doesn't seem to be the confident Peter that used to run around these MWE halls. Peter: Being honest though Black has done a lot of things in his career. He's taken out quiet a few guys. I mean going into this match every last person has Alexis Black beating me and then sending me back out of this business. Every last person expects me to make a fool out of myself at the show against this guy. So instead of having one opponent, I'll be having millions. I just don't know if I can win. Jason looks at Peter confused. He yells at Peter. He wants to let Peter know that this is his match to lose. No matter what the odds Peter has pulled through before. Jason: Dude are you kidding me right now? Now listen here buddy, how many titles has this Black guy won? Peter: Zero. Jason: And what about you? Peter: Eighteen. So whats your point? Jason: And how many of those titles where you supposed to win? Peter: Zero. Jason: So you're telling me that you've defied the odds eighteen times and now you can't do it one more time? I mean Petey I love you bro, but your just being stupid. You never let things like that get to you before, so why now? Jason's words ignited something in Peter. He knew Jason believed him, but never thought it could be this much. He began to speak this time with so much confidence even Jason could feel it. Peter: Your right. So what if the entire world is doubting me winning against Black. Man I could care less. The only thing that matters is defying the odds one more time. I defied the odds by winning eighteen titles, by making a name for myself, and by returning to MWE. What has Black done? He crippled to jobbers, one of which ended up returning. Great job on that one Black. At least when I end someone's career they stay ended. Everyone maybe doubting me heading into sunday, but I'll take my eighteen defied titles, my best bud, and myself over Alexis Black and the one million suckers any day of the week. This monday Black is going down. The scene fades to black with Peter and Jason eating their pizza slices.
