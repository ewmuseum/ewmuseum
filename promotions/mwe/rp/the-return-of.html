But I still find it oddly symbolic of my own journey, my own climb up the rocky walls of MWE. What makes a wrestler great is his journey. The wall he climbs to get to the top of the mountain…or canyon in this case. That despite all the hardships, knowing he didn’t take any shortcuts, and although he may fall, he pushes on. Because when you’re that high, you see things differently. You see what truly matters in this business. And Virus, listen to me well when I say…you don’t matter...and you never will. At least not to me. You’re nothing but a small rock for me to climb over to get to what truly matters. And this week, what truly matters is proving that I am better than you. So get ready, The STD, to Crash N' Burn. With nothing left to say, I walk away from the edge of one goal and toward another. Because, as is my way of life, reaching the top isn’t good enough. It’ll never be good enough. So I’ll keep climbing. I’ll keep setting new, higher goals for myself because that’s what I’m all about. And though I may fall every so often, I’ll never quit. Because the view from the top will always be worth it.
