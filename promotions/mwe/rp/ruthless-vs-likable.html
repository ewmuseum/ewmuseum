Ruthless vs Likable

Jul 18 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
**The camera opens, we see a bright sun high in the sky as it bears down on the dry dessert land. As the camera pans around in the oppressive, dry desert heat we see a familiar figure to MWE standing by what appears to be a dog pen. As the camera zoom in we see the figure in a black shirt with a red Y&R logo adorned across the front for Young & Restless, he is wearing what appear to be slim fit designer jean, black Nike's sit on his feet, and as we pan up to the face we are met with the ever familiar sunglasses wrapping around the face. We now know this man is none other than Ace Cannon, the King of Controversy. He gives the camera curt smile, it comes off a bit sadistic but when doesn't it?** Cannon: MWE, I see you found me yet again. Wait, or did I find you? ** Cannon laughs to himself** Cannon: Anyway, I'm glad you found me. I didn't know if you would be able to find my new place out here in the desert. I mean since I left MWE the last time I've been out here, it's been interesting living in a barren land without anything around for miles and it's taught me something...... It's taught me that I'm crazier then any of you motherfuckers can imagine. ** The creepy, sadistic little smile appears again.** Cannon: I mean, when your in the desert and you have no one to talk to, no phone, no internet, and no TV it leaves you alone with your thoughts. I makes you think about things you can't imagine! I'd share them with you all but I'd get fired from MWE again, so you will just have to imagine what goes on in this mind or don't, you might creep yourself out. **Cannon looks over at the dog pen he's been standing by. The dogs appear to be well behaved and haven't made a sound yet as the mill about their wooden enclosure.** Cannon: You see these dogs right here? ** The camera pans to the dogs in the pen. We then pan back to Cannon.** Cannon: This is the reason why I came out here. You see, when I got fired from MWE for challenging authority I didn't know what I wanted to do. So I sat at home bored and pondering what I wanted to do next. I had already made millions with various business ventures and wrestling and I had a thought about starting a philanthropic charity organization, then I remembered that I fucking hate people! So I continued to ponder, then watching TV one night I saw these majestic creatures. ** Cannon stops, looks at the dogs and reaches his hand into the pen and tousles the head of one of them.** Cannon: I saw these pitbulls and I thought to myself, “Damn, these dogs are just like me. Misunderstood, aggressive and controversial.” Then after that thought I knew that in my heart of hearts, I wanted to raise these guys. So I got a couple males and a couple bitches, they fucked and I had puppies. Then I started raising them.... ** Cannon looks at the pen again, he smiles and turns back to the camera.** Cannon: As I raised them, I became to attached and didn't want to sell them. So then I thought about getting them into dog fighting, then I remembered what happened to Mike Vick so I said, fuck that. Then my second thought was, let me just raise them to be mean and aggressive at my command. So I started to teach these dogs commands to sick things, people, other animals you name it they're trained for it. They become killing machines at the snap of a finger, it's quite phenomenal. You want to see? ** Cannon looks at the camera for a second with a eerie smile written across his face.** Cannon: TV Land, if you said no then turn the channel or close your eyes. ** Cannon opens the pen grabs one dog by his chain collar and leads him out. He motions for the muscular gray pitbull to stay, he listens. Cannon then walks about 100 yards to a shed, he opens the door and walks in, seconds later he walks back out carrying a small cage back towards the camera. He arrives back in front of the camera and sets the cage down.** Cannon: In this cage is a jack rabbit, it's the fastest rabbit there is. It has been clocked at speeds up to forty-five miles per hour, it's impressive. Just watch..... **Cannon opens the cage and the rabbit takes off, it literally kicks dust up from the barren desert floor. After about two seconds, Cannon snaps and the pitbull takes off. Both animals are far away, we lose sight.** Cannon: Eric Derf, what just happened here is all illustration of me and you. Your the cunning little jack rabbit, the furry, cute, little creature everyone loves. Your the fan favorite nobody hates you, your a likeable guy. You mind your p's and q's, cunning a witty promo, get a few laughs and then go out there in the ring and win a match. It's a good act you have, but I'm sick and tired of it! ** Cannon lifts his glasses up and places them atop his head. His eyes gaze into the camera, they are cold and full of hatred.** Cannon: See, we have been on the same career parallel. I remember seeing you in WZCW after I got fired and thinking, “Talented kid.” Then we both ended up in Rev Pro, and that without your knowledge was my doing. I told George Prax you'd be a great asset and we brought you in and for the short time the company was around you did good. Then I came to MWE and threw your name out to Richard Covell, you know my father and long story short you were hired. But this time around you started to really, really annoy me. ** Cannon looks at the camera and shakes his head in a disapproving manner.** Cannon: You came to MWE as Mysterious G and wore that goofy fucking mask. Everyone knew it was you but you wanted to surprise people! You walked around like a big dope with that mask on and God was it annoying. Then you won the X-TV title and unmasked like it was some big fucking deal, it wasn't. Nobody cared.... They just told you they did. Anyway, once that mask came off your career took off because everyone knew the name of Eric Derf and I was part of that reason, but I digress. Anyway, you started having matches with the big boys, Brian Michaels, DC, Kevin Hardaway, and Sinc Mercier! **Cannon stops and looks toward where the dog went. He stares for a second then brings his attention back to the camera.** Cannon: At the same time you were doing all that I was the most hated man in MWE. People were looking for reasons to fire me. They were tired of what I was saying, they were tired of my antics and tired of my controversy. They boosted you up and held me down , I'd see you on TV and think, “I'm better than that clown, these should be my opportunities!” But they weren't and I fuckin' hated you. I wanted to rip your nuts off and use them for cuff links, but I never got the chance because I got canned! [** Cannon nods his head in a manner of approval and rubs his hands together.** Cannon: Then right before I came to the desert to raise these dogs you won the Money in the Bank match. I physically got sick and promised to myself that if I ever got the chance to have a match with you I'd take it and now here it is! ** Cannon cracks his neck and gets that sick smile across his face once again.** Cannon: Derf , this is a long time coming for me and I'm ready! I'm ready for the King of Controversy to kick Mr. Money in the Banks ass! I'm going to show everyone that this version of Ace Cannon is crazier, more aggressive, more controversial and more driven than ever before and there will be no better place to start than with you Eric Derf, a man who needs to be knocked down off his pedestal. ** Cannon finishes and sees the dog coming back to him. The camera stays trained on the dog, it appears the dog has the carcass of the rabbit in his mouth. As it get's closer we see this is true, the dog drops the carcass and sits at Cannon's feet.** Cannon: So Derf, before I leave take note of what just happened here. The aggressive and ruthless won over cute and likeable. This is our future fate.... ** Cannon bends down and pats the dog on its head and put it back into the pen. He then gives his attention back to the camera.** Cannon: ….. You can bank on that! ** The camera fades to black as Cannon walks off toward the horizon.**
