Self Doubt?; Anthony Clark Rp.

May 22 2009

Anthony Clark

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The screen opens up. Anthony Clark stands. He is wondering around his mansion some with a very different look upon his face. A face showing depression, failure, and self doubt. He would be wearing a pair of baggy Affliction jeans. He’d also be wearing a black t-shirt under his black and white plaid shirt with the sleeves rolled up. He’d run his fingers threw his mohawk as he paces around the room. On the couch would be Jessica Richards, and a old friend of Anthony’s Xavier Hudson. Jessica, and Xavier are trying their hardest to get Anthony to calm down some. Xavier Hudson: Bro you need to calm down ok. It’s just one match. If your going to let yourself get down about one match, what are you going to do if you lose a second one? Anthony Clark would look at Xavier in a fit of rage. Anthony Clark: Excuse me ? Second lose? I think not Anthony Clark will never and I mean never lose two matches in a row. Jessica Richards: Babe you pinned two people in that match. Anthony Clark: Jess I don’t care if I pinned all but the last one. I didn’t get that final pin, ok? I didn’t get announced the winner. Sure I got two pinfalls on guy. It didn’t matter to me, and that’s for the sheer fact my hand wasn’t raised after the match. Xavier Hudson: Ok match one. That’s all it was. There wasn’t anything important on the line. There was nothing at stake during the match. Ok so what you lost? It’s not like you were the first one to lose. You didn’t show any weakness out there, you were nothing but excellent out there. Jessica Richards: Babe you have the biggest match in your MWE career coming up... Even though it’s your second match in the MWE. But look at the bright side, you are taking on two people you have fought before. And can I be honest? They weren’t much of a threat to you at all. And your other opponent... Well I don’t know anything about him at all. Anthony would walk around the room, and you can sense he isn’t really listening to reason. Right now his worst enemy is himself. Anthony would go outside with his thoughts running a mile a minute in his head. He wonders to himself “Am I really as good as I say I am? Wait why am I questioning myself?”. Anthony has no idea what to think after the match. Anthony Clark: I’m going outside. Need some air. Anthony would head outside, as Jess and Xavier sit there not knowing what to say to him anymore. Anthony would walk out onto the balcony, and look over the property of which his house sits on. He would be looking at the pool as the shimmering of the sun hits him the eyes. He’d walk down the steps closer to the pool. As he is walking he’d pull out a cigarette, and light it. After the first drag off his cigarette he takes a long breath after blowing out the smoke. He would look at the camera, and begin to laugh to himself. Anthony Clark: MWE world... Anthony Clark is a bit distraught momentarily. You see last week you all got just a taste of what Anthony Clark is capable of... And to be honest... I wasn’t happy with my performance. Ok so I pinned to guys. You heard Xavier inside. You all heard Jessica inside. The thing is wrestling fans... It’s like entering a 30 man battle royal. You can throw 28 men over that top rope. But if you can’t get that last 1... Then who cares. You aren’t the victor. You aren’t the man in the tabloids... Your nothing but the butt of everyone’s jokes. You are nothing but the person who couldn’t back his world. And Anthony Clark is a man of his word. But last week... I looked like a liar. Anthony would shake his head again, but some odd reason laughing. Anthony Clark: You all cheered last week. Whether you love Sin or hate him. Whether you love Blight or hate him. You cheered nonetheless. Because you've all hated me above the rest. The true face of evil in this company. My abilities, my personality, and most of all my pride just all over you sick fans up the walls in hate. The fact you all can never be as good as me makes your skin tingle. It makes the hair on your neck stand. You all sit there waiting for me to make my mistake. You wait for me to fuck up, so I become nothing more then a false prophet. Along the way, I set a records, and I have taken titles.. Despite that impressive accomplishments, the one thing most men desire.. has eluded me... The respect of the fans. You see people the quicker you realize life is nothing more then a bottomless pit of sorrow for you neanderthals. The better your lives will be. But it’s you people that hold me back. You don’t want me to be top dog. You don’t want me to be successful. But the funny thing is... It’s that shit, that drives me. Anthony would take a drag off of his cigarette, and then flick the ashes away. Anthony Clark: And that people is why I need to win this match. The last thing you people want, is to see me be successful. And that is just for the reason of yet again I will prove to be better then everyone single one of you. I will get my title shot, and move on to become a champion. Something that will haunt you people. And it drives me. You people think I should respect you. You think that it’s the fans who make, or break the superstar. But you are wrong. They have no part of this. The fans are just a big or a part as the guy who cleans the throw up after kids get off the tilt-a-whirl. I can’t help that god blessed me with looks of a Greek god, and the rest of you with looks like well Elmer Fudd. My history... My future... It’s already written in stone. Anthony would get up, and start walking around the yard. You’d see a bunch of pitbulls running around the yard. Anthony would smile as he walks past them. You would see some hired help doing yard work on the property. Anthony Clark: Let me tell you kids a little story... When I was a baby...My parents decided to name me Anthony... What most people don’t know is what my father really wanted to call me. You see his idea was to name me Sirius Clark... Now I know most of you half heads out there are going “ Like the Sirius Radio company?”. And the answer is no you ingrates. Unlike you I have a brain, and know why he wanted to call me that. Now when you look up at the sky at night, of corse you see stars. Well when you look up there you will see the brightest one of them all. The brightest star in the sky... Sirius. You see even from day one... They knew I was going to be special. Anthony would take one last drag off of his cigarette before flicking it into the grass. He’d blow the smoke out, as he’d walk over to the front steps of his house. He’d sit on the front stairs, and take some time for a couple of quick thoughts. Anthony Clark: Now I know a lot of you people are going “Hey Anthony, you just jumped to fans. And now to your life story. Why you jumping around so much?”. Fans... This is not me jumping around. This is me telling you a big story. You see you all hate me for one reason. You can’t be me. You want nothing more in this damn world then to be Anthony “Sirius” Clark. But you can’t. And the thing is I have had no say in who I am ladies, and gents. I was blessed with this talent. I can’t help I’m rising, while the rest of society is sagging like the breast of an old haggard woman. And at Tribulation I will wrestle 3 men who are part of that damn society. Blight, Sin, and Delvin Sharpe. Anthony would chuckle to himself. Anthony Clark: Blight... You’re an odd man. I’ll give you that son haha. You are an odd ball. You are puzzle that can’t be solved cause all the those answer hide behind your mask. The mask that covers your face so you can hide your weaknesses, and you strengths. Your seem to be a question to yourself anymore. You are nothing more then a scared little boy under that mask. You scared to show who you truly are to this world. You are scared they won’t like you. Scared they won’t except you. So you hide behind this mask so no one can figure you out, or get close to you. You afraid of yourself... Or you have the most hideous fucking face known to man kind... Whichever you feel most comfortable going with man haha. Anthony would shake a bit in fear as he thinks about the possibility of what Blights face might look like. Anthony Clark: Sin... I haven’t seen you since we wrestled last time, and of corse that was when you were on your back for the three count. Haha. Sin you are nothing more to me, then what Blight it is. And as I just said that is nothing. You two... I don’t know really know what your story is. I guess you tagged, or you had some form of friendship. Honestly I don’t care what you two do what so ever. I don’t care if your best friends, guys that hate each other, life partner. Whatever it is, keep it away from me ok. I am not letting anything get in the way of my chance at a gold. Jessica would come out, and see Anthony on the steps. Jessica Richards: Anthony, are you doin okay baby? You left pretty quickly in there. Anthony Clark: Yeah I’m fine. Just thinkin about the match. Jessica Richards: Well I could figure that. We were inside trying to think of anything possible to help... But no luck. We saw Delvin Sharpe hasn’t even said a word about this match just like Sin. So either he is training.... Or to scared to say a damn word. Anthony Clark: Yeah I defiantly agree. I mean I can’t even tell you what he looks like, or what he is like. But the thing is babe.... I don’t care. The only thing I know about this guy is he is wrestling me. That simple. When that bell rings it’s nothing more then game on. I don’t care if my own brother from another mother Xavier Hudson is across the ring from me. Gold is on the line... Nothing will stop me from getting what I truly deserve in this world... And that’s another title to the history of Anthony “Sirius” Clark. Anthony would get up, and look into the camera. Anthony Clark: Delvin... Sin... Blight.... Tribulation is going to be the rise of Anthony Clark. Finally I will get to lay down a permanent mark on this company. Last match I left an impression... This time I leave my mark forever. The new shining star of MWE has arrived. Anthony would walk up the steps into his house, taking the hand of his girl at the same time. They walk inside hand in hand, and as they close the door the screen would slowly fade to black. The End.
