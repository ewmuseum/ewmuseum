Of Masks and Faces; RP#2

Jul 30 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Oblivion’s Journal. July 31th, 2009. 8pm. Saw the Jai Williams Promo. Must say I’m impressed. Actually looking forward to this match now. I don’t know what’s more nerve wracking. Having somebody who’s equally ‘green’ as I am to wrestling on national television face me in my first match, or trying not to kill the next person who annoys me. It’s a lot harder to do then one might think, considering, all my life, that is what I am used to doing. Then again, I am nothing more then a masked freak. Judging from what I’ve seen, most, if not all masked people, wrestlers or not, are sociopaths. Well, there are a lot of exceptions in wrestling. But I’m not one of them. There’s no way that I could ever be. As I write this, in the stall of an airplane, heading to Tennessee, It is one of the few times that in an even remote public place, my face isn’t covered. Been in here for a while. Must have been something I ate that’s made me feel sick. But I had come to realize something. After wiping off some of the excess puke, I had an epiphany. So many years, hiding my given face under various masks, it made me understand one thing. My given face, is the mask. A disguise, as nobody recognized me without it. I could do various crimes if I wanted to, and what they saw, one of my actual faces (I’m just calling it that) is what they would think. So it has come to the point, that the face I was born with, is actually a mask. Even for somebody who’s seen a lot, that’s something that even sends a shiver up my own spine. Yet, I fully embrace it. For now though, I must return. To my seat. Hopefully whatever hit me will pass before I land. I hate feeling sick like this. I know it’s not nerves. I don’t get nervous very easily. Can’t afford to, for any reason. Oblivion put his journal on the sink counter, before he turned around, and puked one more time. He dry heaved quite a bit after that. He wiped himself clean, before he grabbed his mask, and fastened it back on. He washed and dried his hands, all with a heavy sigh. Oblivion: This bites. Oblivion grabbed his journal, and left, heading back to his seat. He often got weird looks from everybody. He sighed. It had been like this since the infamous events of nine eleven. People feared he was a potential terrorist. As much as a villan he could be, a terrorist, he was not. He had no grand scheme for the world around him. He could even care less if the world around him crumbled to dust overnight. Okay, maybe that wasn’t the truth. He did enjoy wrestling, and wanted to break a few bones and possibly end careers by his own hand. Nothing he can’t do. Oblivion inwardly chuckled at the surrounding passengers, as they looked at him with fear. He liked it when people feared him. He just isn’t a terrorist though. But he let them think what they wanted. He patiently waited a few more hours until he landed. He was fine. No more upset stomach. That was a sigh of relief for him. He quickly gathered his bags, and went to his hotel. He eventually got settled in his room. It was small, but it was comfortable. He unpacked his laptop, and logged onto the internet. When he checked his e-mails, he saw that his boss, Richard Covell, had told him he wanted one of his interviewers to interview him. He was going to send Shawn Spitzer to do so. Oblivion began to type out his response, talking as he said it. Oblivion: Thank you sir. I will gladly give an interview for the MWE Universe. However, it will be held inside the Hotel laundry room. Make sure you arrange it, because otherwise, it will not happen. Sorry to sound like that, but I feel more comfortable in secluded places, rather then a lobby. It’s….how I have lived for quite some time. But then again, when you hired me, you knew that, didn’t you. Oblivion sent the e-mail, smirking as he did so. He took out some of his clothes, and put them all in a dresser. Lastly, he took out his wrestling gear. Like most wrestlers, he didn’t wear his gear around. Although, he still did wear mostly black clothing. In fact, he took off his leather jacket, and hung it. He then did the same to the black spandex gear. He then checked to see if he got a response. He smirked when he did. The e-mail said that his terms were acceptable. In fact, everything was arranged to be done in about an hour. Oblivion: Thank you Dick. Oblivion then deleted the message, before he would shut down his laptop. He then left the room to get himself a drink. Fifty five minutes later, Oblivion made his way to the laundry chute on the floor. Yes, this hotel had laundry chutes on each floor. He opened it up , and was pleased to see it was big enough to fit him. He looked over his shoulders, to make sure nobody saw him. He then climbed in, and went down the dark shaft. After a couple of minutes, he arrived in the laundry room. He was very lucky that he landed in a pile of laundry. He poked his head up from the pile, looking around. ??: That my friend, was a nice entrance. Oblivion looked around for the source of the voice. He saw his interviewer, Shawn Spitzer. Oblivion smiled, leaning over the giant basket. Spitzer: So uhhhh, you going to come down from there to do this? Oblivion: Naw I’m cool. When he said that, Oblivion was pelted with more laundry from the same chute. He sighed, as he hopped out, and landed on the floor gracefully. Spitzer: So you’re the enigma they call Oblivion. Well you sir, are…..different. Oblivion: Whatever. But do me one fucken favor man. Don’t fucken spit on me. Spitzer: *sigh* I can’t help it man. But why should it matter. I mean seriously. What the hell are you going to do? Oblivion then stared Shawn in the eyes. The menacing cold glare that he gave almost made Shawn shiver. Oblivion smirked. Oblivion: Well Mr Spitzer. You, and your camera that’s filming this, can quote me when I say, the last person who asked me that, the back of his skull caved in from my foot. Spitzer: Oh damn. That must have hurt the poor bastard. How did you know that we were filming this now? Oblivion: I pay attention to my every surrounding. Part of what I used to do. Spitzer: Right. So tell me sir, how do you like MWE so far? Oblivion: I have no opinion right now. I haven’t wrestled yet, I haven’t met any of the other wrestlers, so if you want my opinion on that, wait a couple months. Spitzer: Fair enough. But can I ask this. Why MWE? What made you want to come to MWE? Oblivion briefly smirked again, as he stretched his neck out. He chuckled for a second, leaning against the basket of laundry. Oblivion: Well, It was a few months ago. I had come across MWE. I saw something there. It was at a point where I was scouting potential homes for my change of career. I saw a brutal, and barbaric match. It was something in the archives. It was I believe DC against Brian Michaels, from their two out of three falls match back in December. Spitzer: Heh. How could anyone forget that match once they seen it. Oblivion: Agreed. But something told me this is a place full of unrelenting violence. Being somebody who’s only known violence, I’ve grown to like it a lot. I like it, when people cower into a corner, as they take a look at me. Thinking, that I am just as bad as their worst nightmares. People who do wrong, I used to hurt and cripple them, because it was my job. And to me, hearing bones snap is something I like to hear. But….at the same time, it concerns me. I might be an intimidating force of violence, but even I, have my boundaries. I figure, wrestling will keep those boundaries intact, while I get paid for it. Spitzer: Oh. So you’re in it for the money I take it? Oblivion then gave a look to Shawn that basically asked him ‘are you a fucken idiot’. Oblivion sighed, shaking his head in shame for his interviewer. Oblivion: No, the money is not worth shit to me. ARE YOU A FUCKEN RETARD?!?!?!?! Yes, I’m in it for the money. I’m in it for the violence, I’m in it for the money, and dare I say it…..I’M IN IT FOR TH GLORY!!!!!!!!!!!!!! Oblivions sudden lack of self control caused Shawn to flinch for a second. But he gathered his wit quickly, and proceeded to ask more questions. Spitzer: Okay…jeez no need to spazz out on me like that. But moving on. What do you think of your opposition this week, who is another fresh talent, in Jai Williams. Oblivion smirked. He knew his answer already. It was already written down in his journal. But he knew he would have to answer the question anyways. Oblivion: Well I really don’t care much for anyone in the first place. Yeah, he’s considered new to MWE, just like I am. But I also know that unlike me, he has more of a wrestling background, as his brother, Sonny, is a soon to be hall of famer. To me, it means that one of two things will happen. One, he’ll rely on his brother’s legacy to propel him further, or he’ll try so hard to create his own, he becomes paranoid. Either way, not good when facing somebody like me. Spitzer: Interesting to know. But answer this for me. Jai Williams is not somebody who scares easily. He’s somebody who’s been through a lot, and is a very tough nut to crack. That right there takes away one thing that you say you like to have over everyone. What are you going to do about it? Oblivion: What am I going to do about it? That’s a good question. What I’ll do, is just simply go in there, and prove exactly why people should be afraid of me. Yeah, I will admit, I’m just looking forward to facing Jai, in good sportsmanship. Do I plan to hurt him beyond just defeating him? Most likely. See, every match, or every job, I believe, there is always something to prove. The biggest reason for me leaving the previous career I had, was because it had come to the point where escalation was the only way I could prove myself to everyone who actually knew me. In wrestling, escalation is in various different matches, and moving up the ranks. I love violence, and I love to hurt people. That much, is a given. I am not a monster. Just one crazy son of a bitch. So yeah, whole I like people to fear me, it’s not going to change anything if they don’t. So if Jai Williams isn’t afraid of me, then let him stand strong. I’m not backing down. Spitzer: That’s good to know Oblivion. But a lot of people have been saying that you don’t deserve to even be a wrestler. Oblivion: Then those people can go fuck themselves for all I care. I live the way I want to live. People can say all the shit they want about me. I say, you want to talk shit, then back up your claims. Yeah, I may not have always trained to be one, but considering the physical and mental state I’m in, and had to condition myself to be, this is something that’s a sure fit. Spitzer: Well that’s good to know. Why do you wear a mask? Oblivion then smirked again. He took a couple steps forward, standing taller then Shawn was, looking in his interviewer’s eyes. Oblivion: I’ll answer that, with a question. Am I really the one who’s wearing a mask? Spitzer: Uhhh no offense sir, but it seems that way. Oblivion: That’s what makes you people so short sighted. The human face, is nothing more, then an ever shifting mask. Lies, deceit, anger, hatred, and even lust. It’s all one ever shifting mask. That’s how I view the world. What looks like a mask to everyone else, is me taking off the true mask, so that I am displaying nothing, but the real me, and the real face of humanity. Like I said before. I’m then a monster. The rest of humanity is. If anything, I’m the savior. I’m the one who is, the most honest, most fair person in all of the 9 billion people that roam this world. Shawn is somewhat shocked at Oblivion’s answer. He still maintains eye contact, even after taking a nervous gulp. He takes a deep breath before taking a step back. Spitzer: Okay then. I got one more question for you. Despite what you just said, people are still going to view you as a monster. In this sport, Monsters are feared, like you are, but down the line, they’ll lose their credibility. What is your opinion for that? Oblivion: Well I’m glad you asked, because it goes back to what I said before about escalation and proving a point. I understand that one time or another down the line, yeah, I’m going to lose some credibility. But I’m not one to complain about it. Talk is cheap, action is priceless. So if I lose credibility, I’ll take action, as it will be an opportunity for me, due to the situation having been escalated. Shawn looks at Oblivion intently. He then nods and smiled, satisfied with his interview. Spitzer: Well Oblivion, good luck with your match against Jai Williams. It should be a good one. Oblivion: Thanks Spit-fuck. But I do have one more thing to say. Oblivion turns and directly faces the camera. You can see the intensity that is within Oblivion’s eyes. He is all business here. Oblivion: Jai Williams. You can try all you want to defeat me, but it isn’t going to happen. I will not be defeated by you so easily, and that’s IF you can, because I will be going to my limits, which I’m sure far exceed your own. I want MWE to see their future, when they see me, I want them to see the black hole that I am. When they see me fight, they’ll see something beyond what they can comprehend. You’re right about the fact that I do want to be the first person to defeat you, but it’s not based on that fact alone. I just want to win, no matter what I have to do. That is the basic rule of humanity. Winning by any means necessary. And when I win, I will be having a great time. Maybe a Night out in the town, with everyone taking notice, and recognizing me as a winner, just like i always have. A few ladies if they want to experience Oblivion in a whole different way, and hell Jai….you can be my wingman if you want to be. Just make sure that during all that training, you don’t pull to many muscles, because if you are not at absolutely one hundred percent, then you will lose. It’s just the way it is. Because the truth of this world….is Oblivion. Oblivion smirks, as the camera recording is cut. Shawn offers to shake oblivion’s hand, but Oblivion just ignores it as he leaves the laundry room, heading back to his hotel room. END
