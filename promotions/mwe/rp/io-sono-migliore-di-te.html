Io sono migliore di te; B.G. Vs Dave Whitman

Sep 5 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The camera turns on and we see B.G. and Mike sitting at B.G.'s desk in his office in the back of his club. B.G. Mike we're going to do this a little differently. You're going to tell me who my opponent is and then you're gonna go out and make sure that the customers don't touch any of the girls. Mike doesn't look to happy about that but he nods his head anyway. Mike You got it boss. You're opponent is the MWE World Champion, David Whitman. Also the Boss said if you don't show up then you're fired from MWE. B.G. gets an irritated look on his face and then starts talking. B.G. Well not only am i going to show up I'm going to give the undefeated Champion his first loss. Now what are his weakness? Mike looks through the file he has in Whitman and then he looks up. Mike Like we went over before the Battle Royal at Crowning Glory, you're bigger and faster than he is. All you need to do is keep him grounded. If you use your size and strength you will beat him. B.G. thinks about how he can use his size and strength to his advantage. After a couple minutes he looks up at Mike. B.G. Don't you have a club to go watch over? I want you to make sure that everyone is doing there job and no one is harassing my girls. Mike gets up with an irritated look on his face, but he doesn't say anything as he exits the office and goes out to the club floor. As soon as the door closes B.G. gets an angry look on his face. B.G. OK so Richard Covell has decided to put in a match with the undefeated MWE Champion David Whitman. Well that was a mistake because I am going to beat him to within an inch of his life. What ever happens to him is on your shoulders. The look on B.G.'s face goes from angry to just irritated as he grabs the camera and looks straight at it. B.G. Whitman you better watch out because I want something you have and that is the title around your waist. When I give you your first defeat Covell will have no choice but to give me a shot at the MWE World Title. B.G. lets go of the camera and the cameraman backs up as B.G. starts talking again. B.G. Whitman The Revolution is here and not you or anyone else will be able to stop us. I will be World Champion and Fear and Animal will become Tag Team Champions. When we hold the Gold we will be unstoppable. The look on B.G.'s face turns from irritated to completely serious. B.G. Dave, If not for Alex Bowen I would have won that Battle Royal not you. And in Indianapolis I get to prove to everyone that I'm the better man. As for you seeing promise in me I don't care what you see in me, I will hand you your first defeat. You think I'm getting sloppy well on Mayhem I'm going to show you that I am anything but sloppy. Just because I have other things on my plate does not mean I've gotten sloppy and I'll prove it to you and the world when we step into the ring together. B.G. grabs the camera and brings it right up to his face and the fire can be seen in his eyes. B.G. You've worked hard to get where you are? I've worked just as hard if not harder than you have. The fact that we're opening the show actually makes me angry. I deserve nothing less than the Main Event. As for giving the fans something to watch I couldn't care less about the fans. I'm better than them and they hate me because they know it. The bar will be set high because I will beat you until you can't even stand up and Covell will have no choice but to give me a title shot. B.G. throws the camera away from him and the camera man almost falls to the floor. He then gets up and heads for the door to his office. A he opens the door he looks back at the camera and says one more sentence. B.G. The Revolution starts here and no one will be able to stop it. B.G. walks out the door and the camera fades to black.
