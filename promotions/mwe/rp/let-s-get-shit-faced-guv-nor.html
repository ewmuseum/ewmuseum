Let's get shit-faced, guv'nor!

Mar 28 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The scene opens inside a café in London, England. The camera pans around the café, it is fairly empty, with few people inside. Outside it is a lovely day, and the sun shines in and illuminates the scene. The camera focuses on one table in particular, where Darrel Jones, wearing a white shirt and trousers, is sat with MWE Tag Team Champion Alejandro Rodriguez, who is wearing a black shirt, a black mask and jeans. Darrel is holding a newspaper in his hands, and Alejandro is just sitting there and sipping his coffee occasionally. Darrel looks up at Alejandro. Darrel: I forgot to congratulate you properly… Alejandro: What for? Darrel: For your victory over David Whitman. Alejandro snorts and sips his coffee as Darrel folds the paper and puts it on the table. Alejandro: Not that it matters. Darrel: What do you mean? Alejandro: I mean, that the last two times I’ve faced off against Whitman, the undisputed champion, the best MWE has to offer, I’ve won both times. And yet, the next week I’m back in the tag-team division. I just don’t get how it works, I defeat the top dog, and then I’m back down to the bottom again. Darrel: Sometimes Al, I think that helping the new young guys is not going to the “bottom”, but it’s a privilege and an honour. Alejandro laughs and gets up to his feet. Alejandro: Yeah, you would say that, let’s get out of here. They walk out of the café and into the street, littering the sides of the streets are black cabs, and in the road are the big red double-decker buses which London is famous for. Alejandro: Remind me why we have to come to this city Darrel. Darrel: Well one, it’s one of the most famous cities in the world, two, there are 90,000 fans who will be at Wembley stadium to see you perform… Alejandro: …and three? Darrel: …well, Stephani wanted to go to Legoland. Alejandro sniggers and hails a black cab, which pulls up on the curb for them. Alejandro: I swear Billy Joe was more excited to go than her. They get into the cab, Alejandro shuffles over to let Darrel get in, who shuts the door behind him. The cab driver turns around, he is middle-aged and slightly overweight, with a thick London accent. Driver: Wotcher. Alejandro: Excuse me? Darrel ignores Alejandro and speaks to the cab driver. Darrel: Take us to Windsor please. Driver: Will do mate. The cab pulls off from the curb and begins driving round the crowded curbs of the street. Alejandro: So, how do you think Legoland will prepare Darrel for a big title defence? Darrel: Let him have his fun, and besides, when did you get worried about matches? Alejandro: I just don’t want to carry the whole match by myself, I have to make Billy-Joe look good as well, otherwise he wont have any credibility. Alejandro hesitates answering for a moment, as the cab takes a sharp left turn and Darrel is nearly thrown out of the cab. Driver: Sorry bout that, this arse behind me is giving me aggro! Darrel: Right…. Alejandro I understand your concern. But what do you think of the Black List Mafia? Alejandro: What about them, you know more about them than I do… I have had some history with The Virus back in EW. But while they were facing Blood Fuze and some other no-name’s in MWE, I was defeating guys like David Whitman up with the big dogs. Why should I be worried about a team who have little mental stability and little accomplishments or ring experience to back it up? Darrel: Yeah… they do seem a bit, freaky though? Alejandro: Well unlike you, Darrel, I have faced guys like this before, Kanto and Jekyl ringing any bells? Their whole game is trying to psych out your opponent before the match, so they become sitting ducks for your attack. Well I don’t play into that sort of crap, and I have never done it myself, it’s a cowards game. Darrel: Well how do you get over that feeling of intimidation? Alejandro: You just have to concentrate, and not think too much about your opponent. Do you really think I would have let Billy-Joe go to Legoland if it wasn’t taking his mind off the match? You have to face the problem when it hits you, not a week before, where you have time to doubt yourself and be worried. It’s all in your head, and I‘m not letting it get to me. As for my actual opponents, yeah, Virus is a tough guy, but unlike him and Nova, me and Billy Joe work as a very strong team, and whatever weaknesses either of us might have, the other one backs it up. Look at Billy, strong, brutal, but dumb, in-experienced and slow, and then look at me, not strong, intelligent, experienced and fast. Virus has some dark history, and he’s lingering with that, it’s time to move on, we have all had a bad past, perhaps me more than others, but I moved on with my life. Darrel: What about Supernova? Alejandro: Now, I’ve seen some strange guys come and go in the MWE, but none as strange to work out as Nova. The guy is genuinely mental, you’re telling me, that there’s two sides to him, two souls in that body? One a light-yet-tough guy and one a dark, brutal demon? You have to be joking, we had a clown who believed himself to be a murderer and now this, a mental guy, who thinks he’s a murderer and a demon? I mean, I’ve shot someone in the leg, who hasn’t. But what’s with all the murderers in the MWE? The cab takes a sharp right turn and the driver curses under his breath as Alejandro and Darrel are rattled around in the back again. Driver: Sorry again… Risk me life for this bloody job, I do! And get paid peanuts and all… Still… it’s enough for a piss-up with the lads, got absolutely rat-arsed last night! Alejandro: Great… Driver: Most of the lads are thick as two short planks, if I’m being honest with yer! At least we ain’t yobs or louts though, like most of them in the boozer! Life was brill when kids stayed in school and off the streets, all of them divvy’s with a ciggy in their cakehole and alkie in their paws, and the pigs don’t do anything about it! Can you believe that? Darrel: Uh… no, it’s terrible. Driver: So where you lads from? The north? Darrel: No, we come from the United States… Driver: Yankee’s eh? You’ll be popular here, then. Here we are… He chuckles to himself as he pulls up on the curb as they enter Windsor as Alejandro looks offended. Alejandro: What’s that supposed to mean? Driver: Nothing, guv‘nor… Alejandro: What did you call me? Darrel: Leave it Al, how much will it be then? The driver presses a button and the total comes up, he turns around to face them. Driver: That’ll be a fiver, boss. Alejandro: A fiver? Driver: Five quid... Alejandro: What? Driver: Five pounds? Alejandro: There we go… Darrel hands over the money and they both get out of the cab. The scene fades to black.
