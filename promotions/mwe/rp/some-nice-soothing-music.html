Some nice, soothing music...; Derf/England VS Bowen/Semi

Feb 28 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It's dark. Nothing is showing. Nothing can be heard, other then a light breathing. Someone is in the room, but who, is not known. A distinct sound is heard, a guitar string being plucked. A single note echoes around, followed by a second, and a third, and eventually a song is playing. The song being played by the guitar is soothing, almost enough to put a person to sleep. As the music slowly continues, echoes of voices begin to be heard, voices past spoken. QUOTE (Eric Derf) is on a slew of rave reviews and has really got some potential. QUOTE (Derf's) on his way up and it's gonna be hard for Bowen to beat him while he's on a roll. QUOTE (Derf's) been on a role and this is Bowen's first match back, it's going to be a challenge for him to get a win over the Derfster, especially now. QUOTE I see Derf winning this one. Bowen is good but not as good as some of the last couple Derf (matches) QUOTE (Derf's) on an awesome roll right now. QUOTE While it could be close, Derf has a solid grip on the momentum, and it's usually hard to break, especially when the guy he's facing could be a bit rusty. As the last voice flows through the room, the guitar hits a final note, echoing through the room with the last of the words. A single light turns on in the middle of the room, shining directly on top of the source of the music. Eric Derf is sitting on a stool holding a 12 string acoustic Fender guitar. He's dressed in blue jeans and a red button down shirt with the sleeves rolled up. His hair looks slightly shorter then before, he must have just gotten it trimmed a bit, it looks nice. Although the light isn't shining very brightly, and only on Eric, a drum set can be seen behind him, but not much more detail can be made out of it. Eric just finished playing a soothing song, but still holds the guitar as if he were to play again at any second, and although his eyes are aimed at the floor by his feet, his eyes are empty, probably staring into space. Everyone though I had the match won. Everyone though I was going to beat Alex Bowen, and continue my winning ways. Even I never gave Alex Bowen a chance at winning our match. Everyone knew the guy wasn't championship material, so no one though twice about it. No one though he'd win a title. They all expected me to win. They all expected Eric Derf to take home the gold... Eric strums a few notes, no real music, just a few random notes. A few seconds pass with the sounds echoing through the room before he continues. That wasn't the case. Alex Bowen returned, and he beat me. Even halfway through the match, my nose possibly broken, my forehead covered in his facepaint, people still though I would win... and I didn't. He did, and now he's the X-Havoc Champion... A few more notes are strummed... But I'm not going to mope around like a sore loser. I told him to bring his best, and he brought that and so much more. I got what I wanted, and he got what he came for. So, all I can do, is ask for my rematch. As a former champion, I ought to be entitled to one, so I will have my shot at getting my title back. And the road to getting that title back starts Monday, when Chris England and I face Jesse Anderson, and the new X-Havoc Champion, Alex Bowen. Eric finally lifts his head, a serious look in his eyes, as he drops his hand down the guitar, hitting a few cords at once, just for dramatic effect. Now, I don't mean to sound over-confident a second time, but I feel I've got a good advantage going into this match. A much better advantage then last time I faced Bowen. Last time we fought, I talked about my life, and you talked about yours. This is a different night altogether. Yeah, you have a nice resume of people you've beaten in a hardcore environment, but this match isn't Hardcore. We know you beat some guys, seriously hurt some guys, but I'm not one of them. You've ended careers, but you've only given me a reason to push my career further. And now, I know what you bring to the table. I know what Alex Bowen is made of. I know what you've been up to for those months you've been gone, and I'm ready to face you again, knowing what your made of. I won't underestimate you again. A few more cords hit, once again echoing... The thing that cost me that night...I missed my Sweet Shin Music. It cost me my gold... Mark my words Bowen, I will not miss a second time. You won't see it coming, you won't have time to prepare to avoid it. It will be quick, and it will hurt. And the next time you see me after that will be me standing over you, arms raised in victory, just as so many others have seen. A couple more cords. Unlike the other ones, this time it almost sounded like a song, barely. I have watched the tape time and time again, studied my mistakes, studied your stragedy, and I know it by heart. So don't think you can pull a fast one in our tag match.... Speaking of which... I'm not only worrying about Bowen this time... I've also got to keep my eye on "The Semi" Jesse Anderson. As he says the Semis name, he sort of lets it slide out of his mouth in a sly, almost cocky voice. The Semi has been rolling all over the MWE Training grounds, plowing through everything in his path. He's never been beaten down there, but how surprised can you be by that? If you've seen the guy, all he really needed to do was run into them, and they'd be flattened! Eric chuckles, his guitar bouncing on his lap. Sorry Semi, I felt a good fat joke would break the ice between us... Well, break it easier then if you stepped on it! Eric begins laughing so hard, he has to set the guitar on the ground to go from dropping it. Oh, mercy... But fat jokes aside, this guy is a force to be reckoned with. He's huge, and he can hurt a man if he wants to. And it don't take much to make him want to hurt people. Growing up, getting picked on for being tubby, must have made him a pretty messed up kid. Never happy with anything or anyone, I don't see what he's got to lose going into this match, other then the few pounds he'll lose trying to catch me. Eric slowly steps off the stool and sticks a hand in his pocket. Standing there, plainly as can be, he continues. I sure as hell hope Semi don't here me saying this. He might get upset at another person calling him fat... You know what? Screw the fat jokes. I'm willing to bet Semi is the kind of person with a short temper, the kind of guy that can't take a joke and gets hot headed anytime someone calls him fat. I'm sure I'm not the first person to call him fat, so I'm probably going to suffer the same fate as all those other guys that called him fat, right? Wrong. Semi, you're not in the little leagues anymore. Your playing with the big boys now. The pros. It's gonna take more then a hot head and a bit of body weight to take us down, anyone here can tell you that. If you want to get anywheres, you'll need to calm your ass down. But once me and Chris England defeat you, and your little undefeated streak is over, do you think you can handle it? Are you gonna be one of those guys that can get back up from a loss, or are you the kinda guy that will just walk the second people realize your not all that? Jesse Anderson, you might be huge in the ICW, but when you get to the big leagues with us, you're gonna look the smallest you've ever looked, no pun inten- Eric is interrupted by more guitar notes being hit. To the viewer this may seem a bit confusing, but Eric isn't confused, and instead reaches into his other pocket and pulls out his ringing cell phone. Hello?....Oh hey baby, what's going on?... ... ... What do you mean?... ... So he's not gonna make it?... ... ... This is just fucking great... ... I'm not taking it out on you, I'm just fucking saying... I wasn't swearing at you, can't you see I'm a little pissed?... ... I KNOW it's not your fault, just calm down... ... What are you talking about, I didn't- Eric holds the phone away from his ear and looks at it, and assumably he see's the timer blinking, referring to an ended phone call. Women... What are ya gonna do with em... But it seems, I've got me a little situation. It seems my partner, Chris England, messed up. Something to do with his Visa, or not being allowed in or out of whatever country he's from, I don't know... Long story short, it looks like I'm going to MNM for a handicapped match, which sucks. Eric slowly sits back on the stool, his thumbs the only thing still hanging in his pockets. I'll admit confidence in the fact that I feel I can beat Alex Bowen. I'll admit that I feel I could end the undefeated streak of the Semi. But not alone and at the same time. Chris England as my tag team partner, it would have been a huge upset for us to have lost. I mean, I know the guy can handle a tag team match, he just beat the tag champs alone! That's skill, it's to bad we might not be seeing it... Eric stops talking for a moment, obviously upset at what he just learned. After a few seconds of silence, he reaches down and picks up his guitar from earlier, and gets ready to play again. So it looks like I need a new plan for Monday... I'm a man of pride, and even I know the odds of me defeating the new X-Havoc champion and the Undefeated Semi are slim... So I'm not going to try to win. I believe in reality, and I don't believe I can realistically defeat both of these men alone. Instead, I'm going to have to do something else. I'm going to try to survive. This match will be about defense, to see how far I can go. Because I know I can go far. Alex Bowen can hit me with a chair, Jesse Anderson can try to choke me out, but don't think for a second that either of you can pin me. I swear, with every last string of my strength, I will not let myself be beaten. I will fight to the bitter end. Mark my words, I will not let you win, this could be he longest match either of you have ever been in. I have something to prove. Eric hits a few cords, once again soothing the atmosphere. Bowen, keep the title warm for me... More cords... Semi, enjoy your undefeated streak while it lasts, it won't be much longer... A few more cords... I'll see you two at Monday Night Mayhem... Be prepared... Eric continues playing the guitar, a soothing melody filling the room as the camera fades out.
