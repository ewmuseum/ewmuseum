Enter Suave Saviour; Funnier than butter

Aug 28 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
~Sebastian St. Claude is walking in the streets of Chicago, Illinois. The home of MWE’s pinnacle event Crowning Glory. The one event of the year that truly shakes the earth to its very core. After the occurrences of the past 12 months, it all comes down to this one event. Sebastian St. Claude has spilt his blood, broken his bones, and shedded tears to, oh wait, this is his first match in MWE. Although he did sacrifice his blood, sweat and tears.....picking a television to watch MWE and The View at Future Shop. The crowd was chanting his name as he stood face to face.....with an employee at Future Shop deciding between the Sony 80 inch HDTV and the LG 80 inch HDTV. And after it was all said and done and the dust has settled, Sebastian purchased the Sony 80 inch HDTV because nothing is more entertaining to him than walking out with the box in his hands shouting at random people that he got a Sony, the more expensive and nazi like brand. Because nothing says “Hey I’m a douche bag with money” than buying Sony electronic merchandise. Anywho, now we see Sebastian St. Claude walking on the sidewalk in a relatively low traffic Chicago, Illinois. Sebastian is wearing a fur coat surrounding his entire body, a pair of expensive sunglasses and a beret planted on his peanut-brained head.~ ::Sebastian:: Chicago, the “Windy City”, bien donc I agree. I’ve been in this city for an hour and it really blows. You see mes amis, je suis ici for a reason. I am going to walk my way into the United Center ici in Chicago, and I will walk out victorious like the French people always do. Les personnes that run MWE have made a big mistake just like when Michael Jordan was inducted in the NBA Hall of Fame. They do not realize what talent I possess. Instead of Sebastian St. Claude in the main event they have Stinky Merci & La Grande Moustache. But that is not all, look who else is getting my spots at the PPV. Davis Whitebread, Nicotine Kraven, Jesus Christ, Eric Nerfball, A “Small” Rod And who am I being put against at Crowning Glory? Regarde ici! Some ignorant peasant called “Lite Brite”? He wears a mask because he is not as good looking as moi. He looks like the man in the Hannibal except he has big hair like une femme. Which leads me to believe Lite Bright is a woman! I do not fight woman, however if she decides to jump all over me like all the women do, I will beat her off with this beautiful hands. I will beat her off good. ~Random people walking on the sidewalk walk awkwardly fast and begin laughing and staring at Sebastian like he’s a wierd freak. Which he is, but I’m his narrator and he’s paying me so I can’t really say that.~ ::Sebastian:: What are you ignorant fools laughing at? That is not funny. I will make her bleed maudit! ~Suddenly a young looking kid with a piece of paper in both hands walks up to Sebastian about to ask him something.~ ::Kid:: Hey mister, can you...? ::Sebastian:: Pas de probleme mon chere. ~Sebastian grabs the piece of paper and signs his name on it with a pen found in the coat pocket.~ ::Sebastian:: Voici mon garcon. ::Kid:: What did you do? I needed to know where First Avenue was. These are directions! ::Sebastian:: Take a turn at “kiss my ass” avenue. ::Kid:: Jerk! ~Kid walks closer to Sebastian and stomps on his feet. Sebastian starts jumping on one foot yelling in pain. His toe is throbbing.~ ::Sebastian:: You better run you little puke! Now where was I? Ah oui! You better watch out Lite Brite, looking for a fight. Well you can bite my dynamite with all your might, because tonight, you can go fly a kite if you know what’s right...PEACE! That may surprise you Lite Brite, I can rap like le Lil Wayne except I get the chicks. I mean tabernac I have my own personal freakin’ theme song! See I did not come here to MWE to be made a fool of. I am le real deal. But that is not all, I also have to face some fourreur de chien called Maxx Payne, I mean ugh....Brad Kane. Tabernac cameraman can you hold the cue cards straight? I can’t read my maudit promo! ::Cameraman:: Maybe if the bags on your eyes didn’t look like they had testicles drooping from your eyelids you’d read it right. ::Sebastian:: Oh I’m going to get you, you son of a b- ~INTERMISSION! Dog fucker Ex: Your mom is a fourreur de chien. ****************************************** WE KNOW RETURN YOU TO YOUR REGULARLY SCHEDULED PROMO! Sebastian is now in his mansion, sitting in a red leather comfy chair with his pet phoque Raphael on his lap. Sebastian is stroking Raphaels’ forehead gently and smoothly. What a lucky phoque. Behind his chair is a fire place. The sound of crackling wood and the brick around it make the room much more majestic. Sebastian then stares directly into the camera and begins to speak again.~ ::Sebastian:: You may be asking how I got from Chicago to my mansion here in France so quick, and I will tell you why. I have a private luxury jet that can go 34828 kilometres per second. Why? Because I’m richer than you and the added bonus that I am a Frenchman. You may be thinking we’re filming two different scenes but that’s totally not the case. So I was saying about Brad Kanielson. I’ve heard about you and your many championships you’ve held in professional wrestling, however your championships are like Emmy’s, they’re worth nothing. Mr. Brad, you say you fight just to support your family. However I think somebody’s got the alimony checks in the mail this week. I am a Frenchman, not a fool. What are you really spending your money on? Les prostitutes? Chuck E. Cheese? Tickets to Hanson? I bet it’s anything but you’re family. Brad Kane, you are a little boy that will live a dream this Sunday. However this dream will become your greatest nightmare. I am going to kick you so far up the asshole that my foot will be in your mouth. And that mon ami is the definition of irony. Now I am a person of the people. The people love me like a god. So while other MWE Superstars are getting the rubdown by a sweedish 300 lbs woman and signing autographs at the monster truck shows, I am going to the spot where all the true MWE Fans are found, le internet! Pierre! Fetch me my Mac! ~Pierre comes rushing into the room with Sebastian’s mac laptop on a tray on wheels. The mac is already opened on the MWE Official Fanclub Forums. Sebastian takes the laptop and places it on his lap without anything to put over his legs thereby killing dozens of his spermatozoids.~ ::Sebastian:: Watch as I search for people mentioning about the very successful and handsome Sebastian St. Claude. Ah, here’s one. This one’s written by MasonMark1983, he writes, “Sebastian is a little f-ck.” Ah I told you so my little Raphael. He called me a phoque just like you except he spelt it a little differently. Let’s see what else people have written about me. Adam66228 writes, “Sebastian is the biggest weiner I’ve ever seen.” He scores again, apparently je suis the biggest winner this person has ever seen. They all seem to need work on their grammar however. Oh Raphael let’s look at one more message, since these people really do love me. SebastianIsReallyCoolAndHandsomeAndSexyAndHeIsTheBestWrestlerInTheWorld writes, “Je suis, I mean Sebastian is the greatest man alive! He’s the Mel Gibson of wrestling!” Oh Raphael, this is wonderful. ::Raphael:: ARF! ARF! ::Sebastian:: No Raphael, I did not write this. We must both have the exact same IP addresses! ~Sebastian’s butler, Pierre takes the Mac off his lap and places it on the rolling silver tray and rolls it away as Sebastian grabs several Seal Snacks from the large bag found beside his chair. He flicks it up in the air and Raphael catches it in his mouth. That’s what she said... Anywho, Sebastian continues throwing salt in what will soon be the wounds of Blight and Brad Kane, names Sebastian seems to never be able to get right.~ ::Sebastian:: Crowning Glory will be the beginning of a new generation, a generation of grace, beauty, perfection. As soon as I step out into the ring at the biggest event of the year, I will be featured in every magazine cover, even Playgirl Magazine. I will be the talk on every talk show including the one with the overweight women who like to bitch as they go through the process of menopause and I will be the spokesperson for every new hit product. Citizen Kane and Kite, both of you will also become famous. You will both be known as the two wide open doors that will allow me to enter this industry and make my impact in MWE aka My Wrestling Eternity. Now here’s Jean Luc with the weather! ~The camera cuts to another scene in another location where the green screen is being used as the background to show the upcoming forecast. The man standing in the screen is clearly Sebastian St. Claude except wearing an odd yet classy brown weatherman wig and moustache. Because weathermen must have thick moustaches or their credibility is ruined. Sebastian, I mean ugh....Jean Luc is showing the upcoming weather forecast.~ ::Sebastian, oh wait I mean Jean Luc:: Ah oui, merci Sebastian St. Claude. As you can see behind me, the forecast for the upcoming weekend appears to be beautiful. Friday will be sunny with no overcast, 32 degrees Celsius and who cares Fahrenheit. Saturday will be just as nice except with a little overcast but Sunday August 28th there will be a storm of epic proportions. Sunday there will be blood falling from the sky, hail in form of bones that will crash down to the ground. Tears of two pathetic losers will cause a little precipitation around the night time hour because my bon ami, Sebastian St. Claude will pummel Brad Kane and Blight so bad it will storm like losers in Chicago but here’s the bright side... Early Monday morning will be beautiful with sun shining all day long. Because the world will finally come to their senses and realize Sebastian St. Claude, The Suave Saviour of MWE is the true Emperor of MWE! And now back to Sebastian! ~The camera cuts back to Sebastian St. Claude kneeling directly in front of the fireplace, looking down in a majestic pose with his hand by his head looking epic like a squirrel dropkicking a child. Sebastian looks directly into the camera with the fire making a reflection in his eyes, the burning flames. He continues on with his semi decent promo by starting off with a kick ass line...~ ::Sebastian:: I will shit out all of you here in MWE. I will consume you all, you will enter my bowels and I will shit you all out of existence. You see this flame? This flame is my heart, my desire, my strength. It doesn’t stop growing. Fire has been around for the entire time this world was here yet the sight of it still amazes you people like freakin’ Jesus. There is a reason my debut is at Crowning Glory, because I’m special. I’m a very special person. I serve as the Ambassador of Athleticism to all of you. I set the bar higher than you can all jump, my track is too long to complete, and the world will soon realize you cannot compete with Sebastian St. Claude, you just have to live with it. In closing I say, I am not scared. ~Suddenly the bottom of Sebastian’s robe coat catches a flame by the fireplace and suddenly the bottom of it ignites. Sebastian doesn’t even notice it until he sniffs something burning. He looks back to see his robe coat was caught on fire.~ ::Sebastian:: EEK! Maudit Tabernac! Get it off! Get it off! ~Sebastian quickly whips off the robe coat and tosses it to the ground. Instantly he attempts to stomp out the fire. Stomping his coat quickly with his right foot. Finally the fire dies out, Sebastian leans over gasping to regain his breath. He then turns towards the camera.~ ::Sebastian:: We can edit that out, right?
