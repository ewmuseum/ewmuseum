Powned; Zack Crash RP

Sep 23 2009

Zack Crash

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Right now, Zack Crash is in a tough spot, and that was putting it mildly. Putting it regularly, he was downright in the shitter. He ran through the mall like a madman on fire, his three friends on his heels, looking down every nook and cranny with desperate quickness. He never thought he’d have to use his speed for this. Many people were forced to jump out of his way to avoid being run over, but he didn’t care. Not even those of them that looked back to shout jeers and curses at him. He had far more important things on his mind. If he lost it, if he had to walk into Eli Silver’s office next Monday night and tell him what happened, then he’d be in some REAL serious trouble. He didn’t want to think about what his punishment might be. At the very least he’d be lucky to keep his job. As he searched, his mind began to wonder how he’d exactly gotten into this situation in the first place. It was early afternoon, and already the mall was packed like a sardine can. Both the second and first levels were so crowded you could only barely make out the variety of stores that lined the wall. From couples out shopping, to loitering friends, to screaming children, the mall was a mess of activity today. Kent: Dude, slow down! You’re going too fast. I can’t keep up in this crowd. Whined Kent Bishop. Within a sea of flesh and body, Kent struggle to keep up with Zack, who was steps ahead of him. Zack looked over his shoulder to see his friend’s face slowly drowning out in the distant throng of passers-by. Zack: No, you’re just too slow. Zack chuckled at Kent, picking up his pace even more. He fought his way through till he finally made it to an area of the mall that was blessed with some breathing room. There, he waited until Kent had finally managed to break through the crowd. Bending over, supporting his weight on his knees, he breathed heavily. He looked worn out. Kent: I wanted to go to the movies. But NOOOOOO, Zack wants to go hang out at the mall today. Geez. Zack doesn’t explain himself. He wouldn’t expect Kent, or anyone else for that matter to really understand. But the reason he loves coming to the mall so much isn’t for the shopping (not that they were doing any of that today anyway) as much as it is for the crowd. He loves crowds. The noise and commotion, all of it encircling you in a mingled chaos. Just something about being around all that makes Zack feel apart of the world. And as he is quoted to say: “When a crowd gathers, you just feel as if something is going to happen.” And Zack had that feeling of eager anticipation right now. Something was going to happen today. He just knew it. Besides, Zack always found it funny that malls sometimes had stores sitting side-by-side that didn’t seem appropriate being next to each other. Like this one time, he saw a weight fitness store next to a candy shop. Can you say irony? ???: Quit your bitching and be thankful we let you come along in the first place. ?????: Yeah PussPuss. Calls two familiar faces. Out from the crowd crawl Chris Saint Blaise (known also as CSB) and his hetero life-mate Wayne Prez. As they come to stand by Zack and Kent, Kent offers Wayne a scowling look. Kent: I thought I told you not to call me that anymore. Wayne: Awwww, what’s the matter PussPuss? Still got that stick wedged firmly up your vagina? Kent: SHUT UP! Kent and Wayne begin yet another argument while CSB and Zack just sit back and watch. While CSB and Zack have been hitting off famously, Kent and Wayne are less than friendly with each other. Zack guessed it was because Kent had a hard enough time dealing with one personality like Zack’s. Add two more, and you get a little strained. And needless to day, Wayne’s “endearing” nickname for Kent didn’t help things. But CSB and Zack kept bringing them along because they think watching these two fight is hilarious. CSB: Stop secretly professing your love for each other ladies and let’s get going. Zack: Yeah, you tell ‘em Vodka. Speaking of endearing nicknames, coke and vodka are the nicknames Zack and CSB have for each other. It was CSB’s idea really. He made the comment about how their team was like combining coke and vodka, and the names had stuck ever since. Not that Zack would know of course. He wasn’t much of a smoker or drinker, so he didn’t understand how the names applied. But nonetheless, he took CSB’s word for it. The four of them make their way through the mall, walking along the outer edges of the crowd, while Kent and Wayne continued to glare daggers at each other. For a while, the day passed on with relative simplicity. They ate at the food court, where Wayne kicked their butts in an eating contest, only to rush to the bathroon to vomit it all back up. Then they went to play games at the GameSpot, laugh at the emo kids in Hot Topic, and just loiter around acting as if they were planning to buy anything. Kent: Zack? Kent spoke up as they were coming out of another random store. Zack: Hmmm? Kent: Why are you still carrying that thing around? Kent asks, gesturing to the X-TV Championship Zack has draped over his shoulder. Zack looks at the belt with an odd expression, as if confused that Kent would ask such a thing. Zack: I have no idea what you’re talking about. Kent: What do you mean? It’s sitting on your shoulder. CSB: Oh leave him alone Kent. Let him do whatever the hell he wants. Wayne: Besides, that is some serious bling. Kent just crosses his arms in irritation. Zack smiles to himself as he uses the sleeve of his shirt to polish the belt in the sunlight filtering in through the mall’s glass roof. Zack knows that he’s just gloating by showing off his belt, but he doesn’t care. He admits it. He brought it to the mall today just to show off. So what? Even Zack needs to give himself an ego boost every now and then. And the awed stares of several onlookers is just what the thrill seeking doctor ordered. Many of which, I might add, were very beautiful ladies. Kent: Whatever. But I promise you this’ll end badly. Wayne: Like your mom. Kent and Wayne start up in yet another argument, as CSB tries to break them up. Zack, while waiting for them, gazes around examining the people. Across the way, he discovers two ladies looking at him, whispering and pointing toward his belt. Zack gives them a sly smile and wink, which causes them to wave shyly and rush off in a fit of giggles. Zack nods proudly to himself when something bumps him in the shoulder, which briefly knocks him off balance. Zack: Oh, I’m sorry. Are you– But the man hasn’t lost a step, and seems to be jogging off down the mall as if nothing has happened. Zack only shrugs. A few seconds later CSB and the others rejoin him. As they approach, Zack turns to face them and they stop dead in their tracks, their mouths gapping wide at Zack. Zack looks at them oddly for a moment, confused to their sudden silence. Finally, CSB speaks up. CSB: Uh…dude? Zack: Yeah? CSB points a finger toward Zack’s shoulder. CSB: Where’s your belt? Zack: What do you mean? It’s right– Zack tries to pat the metal belt, but his hand connects only with the flesh of his own shoulder. His face drops, as he pats again, hoping that maybe he just missed. Slowly, with dread filling his gut, he looks to find that his shoulder is now naked of the X-TV Championship belt. Zack: IT’S GONE!!! Zack immediately drops to his knees, searching the cold floor desperately for ht belt, reaching outward with his hands, stretching them in-between legs and feet. But it’s useless. The belt isn’t anywhere in sight. Zack stands up, his face already pale as a ghost, his smile gone. Zack: I lost it! Kent: How do you lose a 20 lbs. leather belt? Zack runs his hands through his hair, ready to pull it out by the follicles in frustration when he remembers something. Zack: That guy who ran into me! He must of pick-pocketed it off of me. CSB and Wayne begin looking very concerned for Zack, whereas Kent looks at Zack with a disgusted, exasperated look. Kent: How can he have pick-pocketed you? You had the damn thing on your shoulder. Zack: I don’t know, but you gotta help me find it! Kent: I told you this would end badly. CSB grabs Kent by the shirt and yanks him close, staring down at him with a look that all of CSB’s opponents should be familiar with. CSB: Just shut your pie hole and help us find it! And with those motivating words, all four of them take off. Together they search every store, every bathroom, every nook and cranny for any sign of the thief and Zack’s X-TV belt. Which proves more difficult than originally thought, since it is only later that Zack realizes that he has no idea what the man looks like. Kent: How the hell are we supposed to find someone who we don’t know what he even looks like? Kent asks the others as they meet at the heart of the mall by its luscious fountain. The sound of the running water along with its cold smell would otherwise feel soothing. But their minds are too preoccupied to appreciate the fountain. CSB: Don’t you remember anything about the guy? Zack: Nothing. He just bumped into me and was gone in an instant. I only just saw his back. Wayne: There’s gotta be something we can go off of. Come on man, think? Zack strains the limits of his brain, trying hard to remember the situation and pick up any details he might have missed. Suddenly, he recalls a smell. A very bad smell. It had something of a stench between liquor and weeks of not bathing. In fact, his memory was serving him so strongly that it was overriding the scent of the fountain. Wait a minute?! Zack: HE’S HERE! They all begin looking frantically in all directions for any sign of the thief. But they don’t know what they’re looking for, apart from the fact that he’s blending well into the crowd. But then, Zack sees a back that looks oddly familiar. Zack: THERE HE IS!!!! Zack points an accusing finger at the man, who turns around at the shouting only to find four men charging him. And the chase is on. He takes off like a bat out of hell, but Zack and the others are slowly gaining ground. He’s a middle-age man whose hair and beard are very unkempt and dirty. His body reeks of stench, and several patches of skin are covered in spots of dirt. His jaw is full of rooting, crooked teeth. His clothes are tattered, worn, and appear to be a size too small. He struggles against Zack’s grip for a long moment, but he’s not strong enough to break free. Thief: HEY! Get your hands off me! His breathe is so bad it could make the skin crawl. Zack: Where the hell is my belt you bastard? Thief: I have no idea what you’re talking about? Kent: Dude, just give him the belt so we can get out of here. Theif: Screw you! Wayne: I’ll trade you some weed for the belt. Thief: Leave me alone! Try as they might, the man just won’t surrender the belt. He keeps turning his head, refusing to face them, which may actually be a good thing considering his bad breathe. Zack Crash is about to lose it and rip the man to tiny pieces when CSB comes forward. He lays a gentle hand on Zack’s shoulder and softly moves him away from the man, a very wide grin on his face. CSB: Give me a few seconds with him. He'll tell us where it is. As if they were old buddies, CSB wraps an arm around the man’s shoulder and leads him a small distance from the group. The other look on as CSB, still with a calm expression, whispers something into the man’s ear. After a few seconds, the two return, CSB looking very proud, and the man looking very shocked. Thief: I pawned it at a shop here in the mall. I’m very sorry. The man said matter-o-factly with a slight bow. Zack: WHAT?!?! The man told Zack of the place he pawned the belt and gave off a few disturbingly polite apologies, then preceded to walk away, slow and calm. Through the whole thing he looked like some kind of drone. After the man turned a corner and disappeared from sight, everyone turned to look at CSB, who still had that proud gleam in his eyes. Wayne: Dude, what did you say to him to make him give up the belt? CSB’s face turns graven. CSB: You don’t wanna know. Without another word, all four of them rush for the pawnshop. If Zack weren’t concerned about getting to the belt before someone buys it, he might have found it odd that there’s a pawnshop inside the mall. They soon reach the shop, and it’s everything you might expect a pawnshop to look. Old, shabby, shifty, and it looks odd with a KB Toys to its left and a Walden Books to its right. Wayne: There it is. Wayne points toward the viewing window where the X-TV Championship waits to be bought. Zack releases a huge sigh of relief before dashing inside and slamming his hands on the counter. Zack: Iwannabuythatbeltinthewindowdisplay. Zack shouts off so fast it merges into one word. The man at the counter, who appears to be very slow and bored, takes this without so much as a nod. Pawnshop Worker: That’ll be $500. Zack: $500? Zack argus the price with the man for several minutes, but is unsuccessful in haggling the price any lower. So he turn to the others with a pleading look. Zack: Come on guys? I need your help. Spot me some money? Kent: No frickin’ way! I said nothing good would come of you gloating. This is your mess, so you deal with it. I’m going home. Kent goes to leave, but CSB yanks him back by the collar, nearly choking him. CSB: Empty your pockets…both of you! He says sternly as he sees Wayne trying to discreetly make an exit of his own. Wayne turns and hangs his head and pulling out his wallet in defeat. Wayne: Awww man! And I was saving that to buy some serious stuff. Reluctantly, they all empty their wallets, and are just able to come up with the $500. Zack then walks in, purchases the belt and comes back out with it on his shoulder, looking as if he can feel the belt’s weight bearing down on him. He looks both tired and relieved. Zack holds onto it now with both hands, and tight enough to where NOBODY will be able to take it from him. Zack: Thanks guys, I really appreciate this. Kent: You’d better! I’m completely broke now. CSB: Don’t worry about it. I still owed you one from when you bailed me out of jail. CSB gives Zack a hard slap on the back, smiling at him, and for that quick moment, Zack couldn’t have been more grateful for his friendship even if he tried. Wayne: I guess you got powned today huh? Kent: What the hell does that mean? Wayne: Get it? It’s like a combo of pawned and owned…powned. Silence. CSB: That is so lame. Zack: Yeah. Even I don’t think that’s funny. Everyone tired from the stressful day, they all said their goodbyes, and each left in separate directions, Kent down one way, CSB and Wayne down the other, leaving Zack all alone. His heart still pounding, he decided to just walk around until he calmed down. About halfway down one side of the mall, he came across a store so odd he had to do a double take. Promos R’ Us. Wow! This mall had everything. Thinking to himself that he still needed a promo for MNM (and a damn good one at that) he thought ‘what the hell’ and walked in as if he owned the place. He walked straight up to the counter, where a pimply teenage boy with a cracking voice waited for him. Cashier: Welcome to Promos R’ Us. May I take your order please? Zack briefly looked at the menu, then said. Zack: Can I have one wrestling promo to go please? Cashier: Would you like a side order of smack talk with that? Zack: Sure, why not. When his order comes up, Zack pays the kid, then walks out back into the ever growing crowd of people. Holding a long sheet of paper in hand that is his order, he looks down at it and begins to read. Zack: People say you never know how precious something is until you lose it. Well unfortunately I had to experience that first hand a few moments ago. But luckily for me, I was able to gain back what I’d almost lost. But I was determined. I didn’t give up, and now my belt is back on my shoulder instead of some second rate pawnshop. And funny enough, we have the same situation repeating itself again this week on MNM. Only I’m not the one in fear of losing something. No, that honor falls to Grand Mystique this time around. But what does he have to lose in this match? This isn’t a title match, so nothing’s really on the line. If that is your line of thinking, then I’m afraid to tell you you’re dead wrong. You see, there is SOOOOO much on the line on MNM this week. More than GM, more than the fans, or more than anyone else in the back can even hope to realize. What’s on the line? A spot. And not just a spot. THE spot. The spot of which Zack is referring to is the place at the top of the MWE roster. A place that exceeds championship belts and goes higher than tenure or even Hall Of Fame status. This spot is far more important than any of that. There’s not even a real way to describe it. It’s just something every wrestle knows deep inside their heart of hearts. Zack’s pace slows from a light jog to a meticulous walk. It’s the spot that all MWE wrestlers hope to have one day. And despite all the victories, all the championships, all the accolades, only a few ever make it to this level. It’s where the best, of the best…of the best make their mark on this business. And that’s what me and GM are really going to battle for at this week’s Mayhem. You know, being in this main event, I find myself looking back at my time here in MWE and see how far I’ve come. I started out with a jobber match, going up against Blight and destroying the so-called “Monster” of MWE. Casper The Friendly Ghost is scarier than him. Next, I debuted at my first pay-per-view in a Tag Match against McKnight and Peter K. with Chris England as my partner. Now we may have lost, but it was me who the fans were talking about when the match ended. Also, as a fun side note, Peter and Scotty may have won, but where are they now…gone! And after some lesser matches, I went to my first Crowning Glory where I won my first MWE championship belt…the X-TV Championship. And now, for the second week in a row, you can find me sitting atop the card in the main event. Quite a ride, wouldn’t you agree? Now the reason I took you all down this little stretch of memory lane is to draw a comparison. Well actually, it’s more of a contrast. The real contrast between GM and myself. Zack’s feet ring heavy with sound as he steps heel to toe through the mall. His body and expression exude a strong determination, which many around Zack pick up just by being near him. Several of them stop by to gaze at him in wonder, and others still jump out of his way in fear of being burned by his intensity. I know you’ve been having trouble with your contract GM. It seems you wanna be accommodated in a way that Eli and the MWE Board just won’t allow. But do you wanna know a secret? They could allow it, they just don’t want to do it. Why? Because in their eyes you haven’t really proven you’re worthy of the spot you know hold. You came up from nothing, and how long did it take you to get to a high profile match like this? A year? Why’d it take you so long? It’s because you lack any real impact. Me on the other hand, I’ve been here less than six months, and already I’m being booked in the main event of two shows. I have more impact than you, and because of that I was able to climb the ladder in half the time it takes normal wrestlers. Why? Because I have impact. That’s the real contrast between us. You have smoke and mirrors, while I have impact. And impact will trump any cheap trick any day of the week. The crowd around Zack begins to quietly disperse until Zack is almost alone in the mall. Whether this is due to the impact of his presence or whether they just have other places to go is unknown. But in the end it doesn’t matter. As much as Zack likes people, they would only be a distraction. He prefers to do these kind of things alone. That way, he can fully give himself to the moment.. Now before I go any further, let me save the rest of the fans some trouble by telling them what you’ll undoubtedly say about me. It’ll be something along the lines of, “Zack Crash’s lifestyle will eventually cost him.” Or something like, “Zack’s adrenaline will run out.” Maybe even, “He’ll crash and burn someday.” That’s about it. It’s all I ever hear. Just the same old photocopied insults just worded differently. It’s like a record player stuck on continuous loop. What, did all you heels have a meeting once I joined MWE, selected a few choice words and decide that you’d rehash those same promos over, and over, and OVER again. Just look up GM’s promo from last week, and essential you’ll get the same promo he’ll do this week. For that matter, just look up the promo of anyone whose has faced me before and you’ll have the exact same thing. Let me ask you something. If my “lifestyle” was eventually going to make me crash and burn, as you all seem to think, then why am I still standing? Why am I here with the X-TV Championship on my shoulder? Don’t answer, cause I’m going to tell you why. It’s because the only time, and I mean ONLY time I’ll ever burn out is when I retire. Nothing you say or do to me will ever make my lifestyle cost me anything, and that’s the beauty of the thrill seeker lifestyle. I win either way. That’s what being a thrill seeker is all about. It ain’t about the outcome, it’s how you get to that outcome. So give it up attacking me like this because not only is it not working, it’s boring to boot. Shut up and come up with some original lines or just shut up and stop wasting our time. Zack smiles broadly, truly believing within himself that nothing GM says will bring him down. His confidence is so high it can practically been seen by the naked eye. His confidence is soaring, and why not? Last week he beat both DC, a future Hall Of Famer, and GM, the current World Champ. Needless to say, it was something of a self-esteem boost. But I digress. I’m not here to talk about what you are GM. I think last what was said week speaks for itself anyway. I’m here to talk about our match. As I mentioned before, there’s a lot more on the line in this match than at first glance. And there’s something deeper in this match that I want to draw your attention to. This type of match is what we call a Champion vs. Champion Match. Now look at the championships being represented in this match. The World Champ…and the X-TV Champ. Now people might think it odd that the X-TV Champion is in the main event two weeks in a row, let alone going up to face the World Champ in a singles match. And they’re right. Or at least they would’ve been a few months ago. This match represents my ultimate goal to make this belt more prestigious than it’s ever been. Only a few weeks and I’m already doing what I set out to do. You got to ask yourself, why wasn’t it Mason Resoan vs. GM? Or why wasn’t it a triple threat match between all three of us? Because people are actually starting to care about this belt more than they used to. Nothing against Mason, but he hasn’t done much with the US Title lately. Zack’s grip on the X-TV Title loosens as he begins to run his palm down the face of the belt, stroking the nameplate with his fingers. He looks at the belt, regarding it as if it were the World Championship. And to Zack, it might as well be. Whether it be X-T, US, or World Championship wouldn’t matter. He’d treat them as if they were the same. Because it isn’t so much the idea of being champion, but the thrill of being champion that motivates his every action and word in defense of the title he now holds. What does all this mean? What does all this represent? What it represents is that, by just being booked in this match…I’ve already won. I’ve already won because, win or lose, I walk away with something far more important than anything GM could possibly get from this match. I walk away with credibility. I walk away with recognition. I walk away with the knowledge that I have finally made it here in MWE. And no matter how hard you may beat me GM, you’ll never be able to destroy that. So I just want you to know that. That even if by some act of God you actually manage to magic trick a victory out of your ass, I’ll still walk away the better man. And if I win, it’ll be even better. Cause if I can beat you, then I can also beat Sinc Mercier. And if I can beat Sinc Mercier, then I can beat K-Hard and Whitman. And if I can beat them, I can beat DC, Warner, Sharpman, and the list goes on, and on, and on, and on. So you see GM, this is the perfect match for me. It’s what we call a win/win situation. Zack stops in his tracks as he has just come to a cross road in the mall. Each path is lined with different stores, each path longing to lead him down a different path in the mall. Much the same could be said about Zack’s career. He is at a cross road now in MWE. One path could lead to victory and glory, while the other could lead toward defeat and obscurity. His actions for this MNM will decide which of these it’ll be. The future is unknown, but that’s the way Zack likes it. This week’s MNM will go down in history, and it’s not because it marks the day Zack Crash reached the top. It’ll be remembered as the day Zack Crash reached the top, but decided the top wasn’t good enough so he went higher. And I’m going to keep on climbing higher until I reach the moon, then I’ll climb to Mars, and on it’ll go. And no one, bot GM or anyone, can stop me. GM, this week you will…crash and burn. With everything now said and the talking over, Zack examines the two paths. Each one looks equally as tempting as the other. Zack for a moment tries to consider and analyze which one would be better. But then he remembers that thinking, rationalizing, and analyzing isn’t his style. Instead, he decides to do what he always does when faced with even a semi-difficult decision…he just wings and leaves the rest to fate. And with that frame of mind, Zack makes his choice, and once again walks down the path toward his ultimate future.
