Perfection will Conquer All; A James Preston Rp

Jul 23 2010

James Preston

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Its 8pm on Friday the 23rd of July 2010 just three short days from the rebirth of MWE with the re-launch of its flagship show Monday Night Mayhem. That being said the camera’s come into focus over a standing room only event at the United Centre, Chicago, Illinois. This right here is the first MWE house show since its rebirth. It has been an action packed show and the crowd are lively and in awe of what they’re seeing. The penultimate match has just taken place and the crowd begin to chant MWE, MWE, MWE, MWE as stage hands clear the ring of the carnage from the last match. Suddenly darkness surrounds the arena, total darkness. Welcome Home by Coheed and Cambria begins to blast loudly through the PA system, Red and blue strobe lights hit the entrance ramp as smoke begins to fill the stage. Steping out through the smoke is none other than ‘The Image Of Perfection’ James Preston, who is dressed in a black designer suite a white shirt, a pair of black leather shoes, a cream coloured tie and his signature black sunglasses. As he steps through the smoke he has his arms stretched out wide and then spins in a circle twice, after two full rotations blue and red pyro fires behind Preston. Bang, Bang, Bang. Preston then begins to make his way down to the ring with a confident smirk on his face. He feels home in an arena, this is his domain; this is where he goes to work and gets the job done. After approaching the ring in confident stride he almost effortlessly rolls into the ring under the bottom rope. The house lights come up as Preston grabs a microphone from the corner of the ring and takes up a position in the centre of the ring. Well, well, well here I am back in MWE the place where it all began and the place where my TRUE journey into the wrestling history books will begin! My first stint in this company is what most would call a steep learning curve, a journey paved with more irremovable objects than achievable hurdles. When I left this place I began a new journey, one in which I attempted to be trying to bring pride, dignity and honour back to this great industry. There’s a pop from a majority of the crowd at this point, mainly the ones that remember Preston from his brief stint in UUW where he began this somewhat honourable mission. Preston smirks and shakes his head a bit. Yeah obviously a fair few of you use to watch UUW. I bet your all expecting this mission of mine to continue and pick up even more steam here in MWE huh? An odd few in the crowd can be heard loudly screaming YEAH! Whilst the great majority kinda just silently nod in agreement. Meanwhile Preston rubs his nose whilst looking out into the crowd. Well in that case I guess each and every single one of you have me figured out (there is a five to ten second pause in which Preston just looks out to the crowd) COMPLETELY FUCKING WRONG! There is visible shock on a great majority of the faces the camera catches on camera. Where has this come from? Preston was a people’s man last time he was seen in a wrestling ring what’s changed so quickly, they don’t understand it. Meanwhile Preston is shaking with the anger of his words veins almost popping out his neck and it is clear he is far from done as he screws up his nose as his head twitches angrily. You see for three whole bloody months I kissed all your arses, I sucked up to you, I gained your respect, I told you all I was fighting for you each time I stepped into the ring and each and every single word of it was utter BULLSHIT! I couldn’t give a rat’s arse about honour respect and dignity being missing from this industry! It was just a tool I used, a tool to reel you all in and you fell for it hook line and sinker! Why would I do this you might ask? So I could see the exact look of shock and horror I have just seen in each one of your faces when I burst the balloon and show you all you have been played. Played like a cheap striper tricked into thinking the old man in the corner in a luxury smoking jacket who they thought was Hugh Hefner who was promising them a brilliant contract in exchange for sexual favours is in fact nothing but a cell phone salesman! Preston bursts into laughter in the ring shaking his head clearly in a mocking way to the fans in attendance that the majority of which are now loudly booing Preston. See I could give a flying fuck about any single one of you fans in this building and I never will! The only thing I care about is being the best, about becoming the most perfect pure wrestler in the history of the business and now that my fun and games are out of the way that is exactly what I am going to do. I am going to take the budding careers of each and every member of the MWE roster and reduce their careers to nothing more than hollow shells of what they might have been! ‘Why choose now to pull the rug out from under everyone’s arse, when I could of kept up the act and made it even more infuriating for everyone’ is a question you might all be asking yourselves. To be honest ideally I would have loved to of kept this going for a long, long time just to make you all look even more foolish for believing me. But then just a few days ago I saw a video of Zack Crash belittling me, calling me nothing but a footnote, inferring that my god given talents have been wasted on me and that just pissed me off so much I couldn’t even entertain the idea of playing Mr Nice Guy anymore. The crowd pop loudly and start to chant Crash is awesome accompanied by three claps repeatedly over and over again. Preston takes a deep breath and shakes his head rolling his eyes and lets out a sigh. Yeah he is awesome alright, awesomely fucking STUPID! You awakened a dormant beast in me Zack and now you have to go one on one with that very monster in this very ring Monday night. Not a smart move pal, you’ve just signed your very own career death certificate! I was happily strolling along playing the jolly crowd favourite getting my kicks out of all these chumps actually buying my bullshit and you brought me out of it. To use an old saying in your case the shit has certainly hit the fan! You have fired me up to dissect you piece by piece and leave you nothing but a broken shattered version of what you once were. The loud booing from the crowd stops Preston dead in his tracks as he is just about to continue speaking he drops the microphone down by his side and just stares out into the crowd, his teeth gritted firmly together and a look of stern anger on his face, he looks about one step away from visibly shacking. SHUT THE FUCK UP! CANT YOU SEE GREATNESS IS GRACING YOU WITH ITS PRECENCE YOU INCOLENT MORONS! The crowd boo even louder and Preston just stands there hand by his side now shaking with anger till it stops. Now as I was saying this Monday night Zack Crash is going to meet the toughest test he has faced to date. Going into this he sees me as no threat at all, he seems to think all he has to do is turn up beat me and leave. Cardinal rule number one Zack NEVER EVER underestimate your opponent. So I haven’t exactly hit the ground running in my career, I haven’t as of yet been able to climb up to the rung of the ladder that Perfection deserves. But I have heard you admit yourself that I have the talent to go far in this business, you will be my first scalp Zack you will be the first victim to fall to my feet. You will be the man that gets me noticed and all of this because you couldn’t keep your damn motor mouth shut. You really should of Zack, see I saw you on TV badmouthing my skills and well running me into the ground as much as you could, thing is Zack you haven’t even began to see one tenth of my skills because as of yet I haven’t unleashed them. This Monday I will unleash them and I will use them to ram each and every single one of your words down your bloody throat and make you choke on them as you ultimately submit at the hands of PERFECTION, at the hand of the PERFECTION LOCK! A slow and continuous chant of Assssssshole, Assssssshole, Assssssshole breaks out as the fans vent their distaste at Preston’s attitude. Surprisingly rather than getting angry a raised smile creeps onto the face of James Preston as he begins to chuckle. Oh please is that supposed to insult me? I mean come on this is two-thousand-ten not nineteen-ninety-nine can’t you morons think up something more original? Then again we are in Chicago a city known more for its Neanderthal idiots, thugs, murderers and low life scum so really it shouldn’t shock me that you’re all unoriginal idiots! Preston grins like a Cheshire cat clearly pleased with himself as he chuckles away. The boo’s begin getting increasingly louder before after ten seconds or so plastic beer cups and empty popcorn containers and just about anything lying around on the floor starts to be launched at Preston. He just laughs it off and kicks the unanimated objects away with his feet. Again very original of you I mean wow I can’t believe you all managed to pool together all your collective 100 brain cells to think that up! Now as I was saying before, Zack Crash you have sealed your own fate in wanting to simply brush me under the carpet. I am more focused and determined to deliver a performance of ground breaking brilliance than ever before in my career. You’ve painted a giant proverbial bull’s eye on your forehead and I always hit the BULL! Preston stands up tall and firm and makes the motion of throwing a dart as a symbol of his intent. The crowd boo but not quite as loudly as before there seemingly getting a bit fed up of it and the odd smark can be heard in the background screaming something along the lines of ‘wooo you rock’ every now and again. Your card is marked Zack, your days are numbered, it’s only a matter of days till I get my hands on you and show you up as the glorified stuntman you truly are. See you have talent there no doubting it, but it’s all high flying offence, let’s see how you fair when you step into my world and I ground all flights! See I’m going to do my best to keep you on the ground Zack, let’s see how you fair at going to toe to with a pure wrestler in a good old fashioned technical brawl, because that exactly what I plan on forcing you into, you never know you might just get lucky and steal a victory, but that’s the only way you’re going to beat me on Monday Zack I’m not going to let you get anywhere near a turnbuckle, I’m going to slow you down to my pace and I’m going to dominate your worthless as and show the world your nothing but an inferior schmuck. You will be the first but CERTAINLY not the last to feel the wrath of a man hell bent on taking all the fame and riches the world has to offer and make it all his own! Preston stands tall and proud in the centre of the ring, a huge confident grin etched on his face, this is what he truly believes is his time to shine all he has to do is reach out and grab the opportunity with both hands. The crowd are still booing again and giving Preston a great deal of heat. This Monday night Zack the high flying adrenaline junky is going to take his risk taking offense and CRASH straight into a wall of PERECTION! For him there can be no escape his fate is already sealed, the superior wrestling skills and pure brilliance of THEEEEEEEE IMMMMMMMMMMAAAAAAAGGE OVVVVVVVVV PERRRRRRRFECTIION will be far too much to overcome! Preston smirks confidently as he extends his arm and arrogantly drops the microphone to the matt. His music begins to play once more as he drops to the matt and rolls out the ring under the bottom rope. He confidently struts his way to the back up the ramp as the crowd boo loudly and the scene fades to black. …………………………………………………………………………………………………………………………………………………… Friday July 23rd 10.30pm The scene opens up outside O’Hare International Airport, Chicago, Illinois. There is a scanning panoramic view of the outside of the airport before the camera focuses in on a long black stretch limo parked in the pickup bay. As the camera zooms in you can see there is a man sat on the bonnet of the limo, this man is James Preston and he is looking rather fed up. He takes a brand new apple iPhone out of his pocket and dials in a number, impatiently taping his foot on the ground as it dials then finally he gets an answer. **???** Hey what’s up JP? **J.P** Never mind what’s up where the bloody hell are you, I’ve been out here waiting for like 45 minutes you didn’t get arrested at customs again did you. Who is this man Preston is getting agitated with, who could it possibly be? I guess only time will tell. **???** Jesus James take a bloody chill pill will you, I’m on my way out there was a mix up with my baggage. Preston rolls his eyes and lets out a deep breath; clearly he is more than a bit pissed at having to stand around waiting so long after all he is in the worst city in America. **J.P** Just get your fucking arse out here now, I don’t like standing around on streets in this shithole of a city at this fucking time of night. **???** What’s a matter tough guy; scared a few drugged up loons are gunna mug ya for ya loose change? He wasn’t in fact it couldn’t of been further from his mind however now the thought has entered his head, in this city he knows it’s a highly probable outcome he isn’t exactly scared just fucking lazy when it comes to fighting if it’s not in the ring. No the real reason he is pissed off is because his and this mystery man’s plan has gone down the pan and now he has to explain that. Just because his hot headed inner nature reared its head when he was confronted with a few home truths by his opponent this Monday Zack Crash. At this point Preston just hangs up the phone and sits waiting impatiently on the bonnet of the limo. Five minutes later a man exits the airport and starts walking towards the limo, from the distance you can see that this man has long greasy hair that’s tied back in a ponytail, however he has a black beanie hat on covering most of it up. He is wearing a black t-shirt; the writing on it can’t quite be read yet. He is wearing a pair of scruffy old blue jeans with the knees worn out and a pair of black doc martin boots and he has a large duffle bag over his shoulder. As he gets close to the limo the writing on his shirt is now visible, it reads ‘The True English Patriot Chris F’n England. Holly fuck it can’t be can it? He hasn’t been seen for a long time not since Leon Roberts ended his career tearing his kneed cartilage causing irreparable damage. What a lot of people don’t know is that England and Preston go waaaaaay back to days of training together back in London and were very close friends, well actually they still are and it looks like they might be set to wreak havoc on MWE. **Chris England** A fucking limo James? You’ve been back on a salary what two weeks and already your blowing it on fancy shit? Preston bangs on the windshield for the chauffer to get out and help England with his bag and he does exactly that. Preston drops down off the bonnet and shakes his head at England, clearly not happy with his tone. **J.P** Just get in the fucking limo Chris it’s been a long day. England obliges and heads to the back of the Limo where the chauffer is now holding the door open for him, he climbs inside the very swish limo which is kitted out with plush cream leather upholstery with rose wood inlays, and there is also a rosewood minibar in the centre of it. Preston stops to talk to the chauffer and tells him not to drive till he gives the order, a precautionary measure on his part, as he knows there’s a possibility of needing to make a swift exit if England gets pissed at what he has to say. Preston then climbs into the limo and takes a seat next to England. **England** So what the fucks got your panties in a bunch? Preston taps away on the rosewood inlay on the door for a few seconds in a nervous manner before replying, he knows England can get a bit volatile when given bad news. **J.P** Look erm I kinda fucked up our plan. Preston looks out of the tinted glass window nervously avoiding eye contact with England. Bracing him for the possible verbal and maybes even physical onslaught that might be coming his way. **England** Not the Plan, Plan? **J.P** Yes the Plan, Plan. **England** Oh for fuck sake James you mean you’re telling me you’ve revealed you’re not a Mr Nice Guy after all and I wasn’t there to bask in the beauty of all the pissed off people we created? Preston clearly see’s England is pissed, however surprisingly not as much as he had first anticipated. Maybes England is mellowing a slight bit in his old age, or maybes he I glad to be doing anything other than sitting on a sofa drinking beer and nursing a bum knee. **J.P** Yes I mean that and hang on what do you mean we created? You only came on board a fucking week ago. **England** Yes but I was part of it wasn’t I! England clearly feels aggrieved you can tell by the look on his face and tone in his voice, he wanted to share in J.P’s fucking with the crowd but unfortunately the way things panned out didn’t get the opportunity. **J.P** Can we not fucking argue about this it’s been a long day and we got a lot of training to do this weekend. Preston stares out the window once more hoping that England is going to drop it all, but he knows Chris better than that, it’s simply not going to happen. **England** Just tell me what on earth possessed you to do that? Why take away the opportunity to fuck with the peasants minds even more? And I mean fuck you did it before I arrived I never got a chance to join in the fucking fun! Preston turns and looks at England knowing the only way to stop this turning aggressive is to just get on with it and tell him what went down. **J.P** Look I saw Crash on TV bad mouthing the fuck out of me, I couldn’t take it, I couldn’t of let rip on him the way I wanted to and did I add, without blowing my cover. England chuckles a bit and now sees this for what it is, Preston being himself aka an arrogant hot head that won’t let things lie sometimes he has to vent and England understands that all too well hell he does the same himself. **England** So basically you saying your macho bravado took over once a fucking gain and you ruined the fun once a fucking gain. Preston rolls his eyes at England, it’s been no more than 5 minutes and its already just like old times, England constantly poking away at James and blaming him for pretty much everything, when in reality its usually England’s fault. **J.P** You better not be referencing Reno we swore we would never ever speak about Reno. Preston has a look of please don’t bring that up on his face as he anxiously awaits a response. **England** Ok ok, let’s not go down that route. So now what’s the new game plan? **J.P** Well I haven’t exactly thought about that, so what do ya say let’s do it old school, go with the flow, kick some fucking ass and cause absolute fucking mayhem. Mission number one tear Zack crash into a million tiny pieces and get some fucking credibility around here! **England** I’m game I may have a bum knee but I can still crack skulls with the best of em. But I’m only in as long as you throw beer and scantily clad women into that plan of yours. Preston rolls his eyes he saw that one coming a mile away England never could resist a cold brewski or women in a bikini, throw them to together in the same room and well you’re not going to be leaving that room anytime soon. **J.P** Ok man it’s a deal. DRIVER TAKE US TO THE HILTON. **England** Oooo the Hilton you posh git the money really has gone to your head. Preston shakes his sighs he knows he has plenty more of that coming over the next couple of months but really he is glad to have a familiar face around. The scene comes to a close as the limo is seen pulling away from the airport. The journey has only just begun!
