What Happened to me?

Dec 27 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Camera blinked on and focused on a slightly angry Devlin Sharpe looking around his room while sitting in a chair, on the walls were pictures of him when he first started including him in his mask, him standing without his mask, him holding the TMW United states championship in a suit, him and joe roberts holding the TMW Tag team championships, him holding the MWE X-TV championship, a group picture of Devlin Sharpe, Brian michaels and D.C Making faces at the camera, Devlin Sharpe stood up and walked over to the bookshelf, he brushed his hand again the books, growled and slapped the books off the shelf, one of the books flew and smashed against the framed picture of Devlin from the beginning of his career, he grimaced and walked over to the picture, he picked the picture and the broken frame up, a piece of glass went in to his finger, he winced and took the glass out as he stared at the picture Devlin Sharpe: So young, so full of promise... He bowed his face, he couldn't bare look at his picture, he disgraced himself Devlin Sharpe: Where did I go wrong... His mind was working feverishly as his entire career flashed in front his eyes, his first promo, his first match, his first title win, his 2nd title win, his first major feud, his first world title oppurtunity... Devlin Sharpe: ...Yes I remember now.... Triple Threat Match, David Whitman, Brian Michaels, Devlin Sharpe, what exactly happened that Day... Devlin walks towards his shelf, looks for a DVD marked MWE Turmoil I, he goes through his shelf, he looks through his DVDs, Brian Michaels: Ace of Wrestling, Ace Cannon: Loose Cannon, D.C: Always High, Paige: Head Bitch in Bikini, but he couldn't find the MWE Turmoil DVD, he looked around his room and on the floor spotted the Turmoil DVD, he picked it up, looked at the cover, Brian Michaels grinning face was staring at him, he blew the dust off the DVD box and took the DVD out and put it in, he sat back down in to his chair and pressed play, he stared intently at the tv, he skipped the other matches and went to the backstage segment before his match...he got injured right before the match...he pauses it there and has a revelation Devlin Sharpe: ...thats when it all changed, I was injured for a while and while I was recovering I focused more on my education and when I returned, I just couldn't be focused on wrestling anymore, plus MWE had changed it wasn't like it was before, the wrestlers they now employed were much more competitive than the atmosphere I had left during my injury, I couldn't cope, I couldn't take the pressure of measuring up to the new guys, so I effectively brough my brother to carry me until I got better Devlin sighes and looks back up at the Camera burying half his head in his hands Devlin Sharpe: but now everyone is saying my brother is dragging me down, Sonny Williams, Sinc Mercier, Ace Cannon, Brian Michaels...D.C...and my opponents for this week, Darrius and Boyd... He counted off everyone he said on his fingers Devlin Sharpe: ...Have all been saying that I would be better off if I got rid of him, sometimes I wonder what I should do, could they be telling the truth or just trying to dishearten me, I mean I have had my disagreements with all of them, especially Michaels and D.C, now Michaels too much of a stuck up asshole to wrestle me but I hear D.C has accepted my challenge, lets see if I can beat the guy who can fly as HIGH as a kite, he is probably the best high flyer in the MWE, he probably thinks that a guy with a losing streak is hard to beat Devlins face changes as his punches his palm Devlin Sharpe: but he's going to find out, Devlin Sharpe does not back down easily, I may lose a lot but hell that does not mean I will lose to him, I will show him I will show everyone what I can do when I am truly motivated A smirk grows on Devlin Sharpes face as he stops slouching and becomes serious still with a grin an evil grin that would send chills down people's spines Devlin Sharpe: See all these weeks of losing was just a ploy, make every one think I am weak so that I can slowly come in as a sleeper and beat everyone while they are still shocked, yes my brother did factor in to my crappiness, but really he was just a tool, but the thing is...I still don't have the motivation to win, i just wanted to know what it is that I am missing, maybe if I could find out whats missing I can go get it and fix my career once and for all Devlin grabbed the remote of the DVD player and threw it against the wall, it fast forwarded to Brian Michaels winning the title Devlin Sharpe: FUCK YOU, YOU BASTARD...YOU'VE SAT ON YOUR DADDY'S LAP FOR FAR TOO LONG Devlin jumped out of his seat and started pacing around his room, he grabbed a hockey stick and smashed the photo of Rated X, oddly it split in to Brian Michaels by himself and D.C and Devlin Sharpe together Devlin looked at the picture for a few moments Devlin Sharpe: Could this be a sign?... Devlin shook his head roughly Devlin Sharpe: Nah... He paced around his room nervously because he didnt know what to do, he couldnt think of how he could have his career saved, he just didn't know what to do... he dashed out his room and ran around his house with all the thoughts swirling in his mind, his face looked panicky and feverish as he fainted and then the camera switched off -10 Minutes later- The Camera switched back on as Devlin's phone was ring, he stirred a bit and finally woke up, he sat up a bit and answered his phone Mystery Man: Meet me at Town hall in 1 hour, I will solve all your problems... Devlin Sharpe: But who are you? Mystery Man: An Old friend.... The mystery man hung up the phone leaving Devlin Puzzled as he got off the floor and had a shower, he got out of the shower within 20 minutes and sat down on a chair, he went through his mind thinking who it could be that was calling him who was this Old friend...Windy Bridges? Sinc Mercier, D.C? Devlin Sharpe: Well we just have to go and see won't we? Devlin put on his jacket and came out of his house, he opened the door to his candy apple red nissan skyline and went inside, he also allowed the cameraman to enter his car Devlin Sharpe: You the lucky MWE fans will be able to witness just who the fuck was on that phone Devlin drove to the town hall at top speed, he was there in 45 mins, he climbed on to the aging statue of a chair and sat on it Devlin Sharpe: and now we play the waiting game, you see I have waited here before, me and an old friend used to hang out here all the tim- wait a second I just remembered who my old friend is A man was approaching Devlin Sharpe, he started climbing on the statue when Devlin shouted: Devlin Sharpe: Aiden Copane, you fucking rat bastard, scared the shit out of me? where the hell have you been? The Camera panned to MWE Alumni Aiden Copane as he smirked at Devlin Sharpe Aiden Copane: Nothing much man, just chilling with my girlfriend, BUT I have been keeping track of your win loss record and your matches and I figured out why you always keep losing... Devlin had a puzzled look on his face and just stared dumbstruck at Aiden before regaining his composure and saying Devlin Sharpe: ...and that reason is? Aiden laughed with forced then wiped away a tear from his eye before continueing to laugh Aiden Copane: Devlin, you seriously can't tell?, you keep losing because you worry too much, you worry about Ryan, you worry about what he does, so in short, his life is messing up your career, his existance is playing with your future, now the way I see it Devlin you got two choices, keep living this life of failure and misery just to keep your stupid brother who by the way I don't need to remind you, took your girlfriend, happy OR you can cut your losses and start from scratch as a singles competitor, but for that to happen you need to ...remove... Ryan from the MWE Roster permanently, if you know what I mean Aiden had one of his trademarked sadistic smiles on his face Aiden Copane: Now I am not saying that you should do it straight away hell you have a tag team title match this week at the payperview, I know you'll win those titles, I have my tickets, whether you win or lose I want you to listen to me Aiden grabbed Devlin by the scruff of his neck nearly spitting each venom filled word at his face Aiden Copane: Cut your losses, get rid of your brother, permanently, I will help get you the stuff you need Devlin Hessitated at what Aiden said, had he gone mad, although there was a method to his madness Devlin Sharpe: but what happens if I win the tag team titles AND listen to you Aiden Copane: I KNOW you will win the titles Devlin, I believe in you, EVERYONE does, Darrius and Boyd are both candy ass motherfuckers, one of them reminds me of that smug son of a bitch Brian Michaels, and about the situation? Richard Covell is a softy, if your brother goes missing he will give you time to grieve, so you wont have to defend as often AND he'll even find you a replacement partner Devlin Sharpe: Any Chance of you returning to help me out? Aiden Copane: You'll never know Aiden and Devlin laughed as Devlin turned to the Camera Devlin Sharpe: Well there you have it folks, turns out my old friend was MWE Alumni Mr Aiden Copane, tune in to Adrenaline rush this sunday to see me win the tag team titles The camera panned out showing Aiden and Devlin messing around
