Kickin' it Old-School

Nov 30 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
..A handheld camera opens up on a hotel room, where Barbie McQueen is in her white robe with a towel over her head. She has just come out of the shower and is admiring her looks in the mirror. She takes the towel off her head and begins to comb her hair. She is brushing it smoothly before hearing a loud knock on the door. She drops the comb, walking over to the hotel door and opening it. In walks an aggressive Brian Michaels, visibly drunk with a black MWE sweater and light blue jeans. He is wearing large snow boots and walks in with an extremely and unusual plain look on his face. He leans on the wall, licking his lips. Michaels: So…I guess it’s like my theme song says…my girlfriend’s a dick magnet. McQueen looks on with a puzzled face, throwing her arms slightly in the air, wanting to know what that means. Michaels: My girlfriend…she’s got to have it. She again looks confused as to what Michaels is saying, scratching her wet hair, causing some water to fall out. Michaels: So…you don’t need to go home…but you need to get the FUCK outta here. Brian gives Barbie a smug look as she finally notices what is happening, the fact that Michaels is slowly talking about his theme song. Barbie: What are you talking about? Why do I have to go home? Brian’s evil look turns into a smirk, as he stops leaning on the wall and takes several steps towards Barbie. Michaels: You don’t know what I’m talking about? You know what you did…all I gotta do is play this song. Brian reaches into his sweater-pocket and takes out Graduation, the Kanye West CD from 2007. He puts it into the old-school boombox, and skips to the 15th song. The song begins to play as he fast-forwards to the middle of the song, where Kanye’s words are heard loud and clear. It's gonna be the death of me And I always thought that you having my child was our destiny But I can't even vibe with you sexually Cuz every time I would try, you would question me Saying "You fucking them girls, disrespecting me? You don't see how ya lies is affecting me. You don't see how our life was supposed to be. And I never let a chick get that close to me. And you ain't cracked up to what you were supposed to be. You always gone, you'll always be where them boys’ll be." Got you saying, this is the first time he ever spilled his soul to me’’ Michaels: I had one of my friends remix the song…I just hope you didn’t expect me not to know about you…get out of my face. Lying whore Barbie scoffs and stands her ground, putting her arms across her rather large chest. Barbie: Ugh, no. We both paid for this hotel room, so I’m going to stay, no matter what. Brian gives a sarcastic chuckle before walking right up to Barbie, looking her dead in the eyes. He winks at her before pushing her down to the floor, causing her to land on her ass. Michaels: Wow…even the floor gets some. Michaels winks again as he slams the door, walking out in the process. Barbie scoffs as the camera again goes back to Michaels, putting a piece of gum in his mouth. He walks with a brash swagger, running his hands through his hair. He walks into the elevator only to see this extremely cute woman waiting in the corner. Michaels: Damn girl…you got big… The woman’s eyes raise in question as Michaels coughs to avoid saying what he was about to say. Michaels: Tits. Yeah, I said it. WHAT YOU GONNA DO, BITCH?! The woman rolls her eyes as the ding is heard and she walks out of the elevator. Michaels reaches down and spanks her ass hard, so the new entrants into the elevator hear it. Michaels gives her a grin as he walks down the hallway to where there are nearly a hundred people screaming, with a large steel guard rail is confining them in one large group. Michaels: The fuck? There is also an interviewer from MWE, seemingly it is Sara Patterson. She is wearing a red skirt with a red blouse and black heels. She has large earrings on as she slowly steps onto the platform, awaiting for Michaels. Michaels snarls at the fans who then boo in disapproval. He smirks and slowly walks up on stage, where there is a seat awaiting him and Sara. The camera then zooms out to show the entire setting. The platform is complete with a large MWE logo and all. The camera then pans back in on Michaels and Patterson. Sara: So Brian, what are your thoughts on Sonny Williams’ ‘’dream’’? You know, the one about you being a cab driver being obsessed with D.C.? Brian gives a bit of a chuckle, thinking back to Sonny’s promo. He rubs his chin, pondering back at the segment. Michaels: Y’know, before I get too into the promo, and totally rip into Sonny…I wanted to show you all an image I brought just for Williams. Roll it, fellas. The camera pans over to the image as the fans give a raucous laugh, many jumping up and down in laughter. It comes back at Michaels, who is trying to hid his now-visible grin. Michaels: You see Sonny is the cover boy…for Natural Male Enhancement ads. Ain’t got a big dick? Sonny feels your pain! Dial 1-800-Lil-Dick for MORE INFO! Sara covers her mouth, shocked at Michaels’ language. She staggers back, before coming back to her up-right position. Sara: That’s kind of harsh. I thought you were against people praising their genitals? Michaels: That’s where you’re wrong Sara. I’m all for talking about my ridiculously large penis, as long as it’s not too much. Sara: Yeah, I mean, how big could it be, right?! Sara chuckles at her joke, as the camera zooms in on a serious Michaels. Michaels: Seriously? Sara: Well, anything bigger than ten inches is kinda crazy. Michaels: Fuck it. Let’s get back to Williams. Am I the only one in MWE who feels that it’s a LITTLE ironic that Sonny basically had a dream about a gay David Whitman? The crowd is silent, wondering where this is leading to. Michaels: I mean…that was Sonny’s dream. It’s kind of like he WANTED our Champions’ balls on his chin. BUT…that’s not what matters. What does matter is Monday Night… Sara: Oh yes. MWE has an excellent main-event match on Monday, featuring Michaels here vs the MWE Champion, David Whitman! What are your thoughts on this match Brian? Michaels: My thoughts? You want my thoughts…okay. Brian angrily grabs the microphone out of Sara’s hands and throws it on the ground. He grunts at her as she quickly walks off the stage to avoid Michaels’ sudden rage. He runs his hands through his hair, causing his hair to become wild and crazy. Michaels: WHITMAN! It’s been man-on-man for a long time pal! You can go around and you can praise the fans, but at the end of the day, you’ve never gotten the job done against Brian, the fucking King of the World, MICHAELS! He takes off his trench coat, as his face begins to redden. Michaels: But before I get into ANYTHING ELSE…let me give Whitman some praise. Michaels licks his lips, as he kicks the chair he was once sitting in. Michaels: Ya see, Whitman’s gotten the job done when it counts. He won the Battle Royal at Crowning Glory…a match I was in. He held that Title for a month, and he styled and profiled and sold T-Shirts like crazy. Then, Mr.Whitman lost it again. I guess that’s his life story. He gives a chuckle, the fans still raining down on him with eccentric boos. Michaels: Let me tell you something, WHITMAN! When you step into the ring, Monday night, you will lock eyes with the greatest wrestler alive TODAY! The REAL fuckin’ deal, Brian MICHAELS! Brian takes his hand and runs it through his slick hair, giving a nice little dance at the same time. Michaels: David Whitman is a champion….I’m a LEGEND! Whitman has friends, I GOT BITCHES! Whitman’s got jokes, I got Rolexes. He’s got fans, I’ve got MANSIONS! Michaels paces across the stage, before walking towards the edge, near the fans, and points to his chin, daring one of the fans to punch him. Michaels: See just like this guy…he can’t touch me. He can’t touch me because he damn well knows…the second he makes contact…I just…lose control. His once egotistical tone fades away quickly as he licks his lips again, the camera zooming in on him. Michaels: Dave…you want to know what happens when I….snap? When I let my anger get the best of me? Because after all the personal shit I’ve been through in life…the LAST thing…I want…is for YOU…to piss me off. Brian walks back to his seat, picking it up and sitting on it. He begins to rock and forth now, moving his tongue around in his mouth. Michaels: I’ve been known…to do some pretty CRAZY things…You see, six months ago…I revealed to the wrestling audience in Enigmar Wrestling that my girlfriend…my love…my SOUL…was RAPED at a New Year’s party… His face is red, trying to avoid thoughts of the fateful night where his girlfriend was taken upstairs and hidden from him while he was in a fight with the bartender. Michaels: I swore that night…I would never, ever, be pushed around by anyone. Black, White, Cuban, Asian, I do not give a fuck you are…you piss me off…all I gotta do…is think back…to that night. Michaels’ intense mood switches back to an egotistical-ish manner, rising from his seat and kicking it down to the floor, hitting the rail guarding the fans from the stage. Michaels: This company…these FANS…want too much of my heart, they want too much of ME! So I’m just not going to give them anything…because they turn on you like D.C. turns on his friends… He chuckles at the comment, referring to D.C., his old friend in Rated-X. Michaels: David Whitman…can crack his little jokes about me being the boss’ son…but at the end of the day…that’s not what matters. Michaels rises to the center of the stage as the lights are all turned off. Only one spotlight remains and it is focused on Michaels, whose eyes gleam at the camera with an undying hatred. Michaels: I don’t get by because I’m daddy’s little boy…I get by because I’M THE GREATEST! I am THE SHIT, and David Whitman for the THIRD TIME…will learn that…this Monday. The audience members that remain, continue to try and drown Michaels with boos, but that appears to only intensify his arrogance. Michaels: He’s going to come out here…give you the simple good guy lines…and expect another win because he’s David Whitman…well you know what? Brian licks his lips, wanting to draw the fans closer to what he’s about to say. Michaels: I’m BRIAN MICHAELS! I’m the two-time MWE Champion! I’m the ECFW Intercontinental Champion, I SINGLE-HANDEDLY carried MWE when guys like WHITMAN…couldn’t keep up. So David Whitman can take his ball, and go home because for the third straight time…I’m going to prove for the THIRD time…that Brian Michaels is STILL… The camera zooms in on Michaels’ green eyes, still full of hatred. Michaels: Better…than David Whitman. See, because just like the spotlight is on me right now…it’ll be on you, come Monday. The headline will say ‘’Can Whitman get the job done?’’ And you know what Whitman’s gonna say….I don’t think I can. Brian gives a grin and a laugh that sends chills down the spines of all the Jonas-Brothers-and-Twilight loving fans in the hotel lobby. Michaels: He doesn’t THINK he can! Do you hear that, boys and girls? David Whitman…is like the little train that could NOT. He can confide in his little cousin, brother, friend, I don’t give a fuck…mark my words, David Whitman will go toe..to toe….with ME…and he will crumble…just like everybody else. You wanna know why…? Brian shows off his five fingers, his ring finger with a very large and expensive ring on it. Michaels: Because I’m full of… He puts down his pinky. Michaels: Attitude… He puts down his index finger, smirking at the audience. Michaels: Confidence… Brian then takes his other hand and rubs it across his chin, while putting down his finger. Michaels: Personality… He puts down his thumb now, leaving his middle finger open and visible to the camera and the millions upon millions worldwide. Michaels: AND SEXUALITY! He then uses that fist and points it at the camera, grinning from ear to ear. Michaels: And you, David Whitman….are full of SHIT! Michaels sticks out his tongue as the static comes across the screen and the scene closes.
