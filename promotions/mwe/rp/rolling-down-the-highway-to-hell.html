Rolling Down the Highway to Hell; Semi RP

Mar 27 2009

Jesse Anderson

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Don't need reason, don't need rhyme Ain't nothing I would rather do Going down, party time My friends are gonna be there too I'm on the highway to Hell On the highway to Hell Highway to Hell Highway to Hell! The scene opens with AC/DC blaring from the radio of Semi's pickup truck as Semi himself pilots it. It is a clear night in Columbus, Ohio as a beaten up red pickup truck with rust spots and scratches in the paint drives along a brightly lit street with fast food joints, gas stations, and mini marts littering its sides. Semi drives along, slightly bobbing his head to the music. He has four or five days stubble around his mouth and chin and a pleased, but as always, smug look on his face. He is wearing an "MWE Presents Highway to Hell" T-shirt and jeans. Nearly completely drowned out by the louder music, softer music begins playing. It is Semi's entrance theme. 18 wheels are rolling! 18 wheels are crushing me down! Semi leans to the left and reaches into his right pocket with his right hand. He retrieves the black cell phone that is the source of the new music, flips it open, and puts it to his ear. Semi: Yeah?... Yeah, I got it. Semi sends a sideways glance to his passenger seat. Semi: Yeah. I'll be there soon. Ok. Semi flips his phone shut and returns it to his jeans. Semi steps on the gas. The already loud engine becomes louder and Semi passes by the screen as the camera pans over two cases of beer in the otherwise empty seat beside Semi. The camera cuts out. The scene returns as Semi walks up to the door of an old, worn brown house. He has a case of beer in each hand. Semi stops at the door and begins to reach out with his right hand but realizes the predicament and stops. Instead, he kicks the screen door hard enough to make a loud bang, three times in a row. A moment later, the door is answered by a man in his mid twenties with a bald head and a large beer gut under a stained white tank top and jeans shorts. The man spots Semi and speaks loudly. Man: Well it's about time! Get your ass in here! The greeter lazily pushes the screen door open wide and steps away. Semi catches the door open with the beer in his left hand and steps through into the dark house as the door slams shut behind him. Semi walks through a dark room to a doorway with no door that's just outside what appears to be a kitchen. Man: Watch your step. The bald man steps through the doorway and begins descending shaded stairs. Semi follows him down the stairs towards light. You can hear voices coming from the basement below. As Semi reaches the bottom and starts to turn, two voices greet him at once. Voices: Hey, it's Semi! Hell yeah, it's the guy with the beer! The cameraman reaches the bottom and turns as well, revealing the bald greeter and two other men sitting around a round table that is scattered with cards and betting chips. Sitting closest to Semi, on the right, is the man that greeted him at the door. Semi turns his attention to him. Semi: Thanks for the invite, Mike. Mike: No problem. Couldn't pass up the opportunity to spend some time with the famous Semi wras'ler while he's in town. The man sitting around the table from Mike looks two or three years younger. He's short but powerful looking. He has large, muscular arms with a large tattoo of a skull on his left shoulder. A Red Sox T-shirt is stretched over a wide chest, and he has blonde hair tucked up under a backwards red baseball cap. As soon as Mike finishes talking, the man under the hat pipes up with enthusiasm. Second Man: We can do greetings later. First, toss me a beer! Semi shoots the guy a cold look and tilts his head back. He doesn't say or do anything for a long moment. Then a small grin spreads on his face and he sets down the cases of beer, rips one open, and grabs two beers from it. Semi tosses one across the table and it's caught by the muscle man. Semi: It's good to see you too, Joey. Joey laughs arrogantly and flexes his left arm as he pops the beer open with his right hand and takes a long swig. This whole scene is watched in amusement by the third guy at the table, who is tall and skinny with dark frizzy hair and glasses. He is wearing a button-up black shirt that's unbuttoned over a white undershirt. He makes eye contact with Semi and nods his head in greeting. Joey: You remember my buddy, Sam, don'cha? Semi: No. Sam: Hello, I'm Sam. Semi: I got that much. Hi. Semi opens the other beer in his hand and sits down in the only empty chair around the table. Mike: So, Jess, I heard you picked up another new title or something. Semi looks at him a little surprised. Semi: Well, kind of. I didn't really... Semi is cut off as Sam begins talking quickly. Sam: Well, it was a non title match but Semi dominated! Jack Savage, who is Semi's biggest rival in WHQ, actually came down to the ring in MWE and handed Semi the title! It was amazing! And when Semi wins at Highway to Hell, he'll be the official champion! Semi looks dumbfoundedly at Sam. Semi: Um, yeah. That's right. Joey laughs hardily and grabs Sam's shoulder. Joey: Sam's a wrestling fool! He's a big fan of your's. When I told him we invited you, he was giddy as a puppy dog to come here! Sam grins shyly and turns his eyes down to the table. Semi smiles arrogantly. Semi: Is that right? Well here I am, in the flesh, the unstoppable Semi. Sam looks up with a smile and extends his hand. Sam: It's nice to meet you. Semi looks briefly at Sam's hand then back into his eyes. Semi raises his hand, but only to take a sip of beer. Semi: Damn right it is. Same here I suppose. Sam withdraws his hand sheepishly. Joey and Mike shoot each other an odd look before their eyes fall back to Semi. Joey speaks up, still with enthusiasm. Joey: So how long has it been, man? Probably two years or more since you've been around for a beer! You been hiding from us? Joey cracks a big smile. Semi still looks smug. Semi: I don't hide from anyone. I've been right out in the middle of the open, on world wide television, kicking ass. I've been busy. Sam: Heck yeah you've been busy. Still defending the WHQ championship on Sundays, now wearing the X Havoc championship on Mondays, which was after carrying the ICW championship, and now you have a pay per view coming up! Semi points a thumb at Sam. Semi: See? This guy gets it. Sam smiles, proud of himself. Joey rolls his eyes and Mike butts in. Mike: So are we gunna' play some poker or what?! The camera fades out as Mike reaches out for the deck of cards. As the camera fades back in, the once relatively tidy card table is now covered in empty beer cans. All four men are talking loudly over top of each other. Joey is smoking a cigarette. There are betting chips piled all over the table but most of them seem to be with Sam. Joey: Damn, Sam! Are you going to let anyone else win a game? Joey turns to Semi as Sam grins and shrugs his shoulders. Joey: Sam's a friggin' math genius. I guess that's the other reason he wanted to come over. Soon enough, I'm not going to be able to afford more beer for the week! Mike: Good to see a man with his priorities straight. Mike laughs a big belly laugh while Joey scrambles his brain for a comeback. Joey: Yeah, well, at least I can spell priorities! Semi: Yeah? Go ahead. Semi raises an eyebrow. Joey looks annoyed and rolls his eyes upward in thought. Joey: P... As in piss on you! I don't have to prove nothin'! A round of laughter rises up from the table. Mike: So guess who I saw at the store the other day. Everyone gazes across the table to Mike. Mike: Remember Amanda Moore from high school? Joey: Ha! Yeah, I remember because everyone wanted more Amanda! Everyone laughs, Mike in particular. Mike: Yeah, that's her. Well it turns out now there is more Amanda to go around. She's become a cow! Joey: No way! Well, you know you would still do her! Mike looks around with shifty eyes. Mike: That's not the point! Everyone laughs again. Semi looks at the cards in his hand and tosses them down on the table with a sigh. Semi: I fold. Joey: Me too. Mike: Ditto. Sam: Looks like I win again. Sam tosses down three 10's and pulls all the chips from the middle into his large pile with a huge toothy grin on his face. Sam: So Semi, I've got to ask; how have you been preparing for your title match with Derf? Semi raises an eyebrow and shoots a look at Sam, staring him down. Semi: What's that, little man? Are you asking me about wrestling? Well hell, all this time, I thought you were the damn expert! Sam flashes a look to Joey, then to Mike. He opens his mouth to speak but just shakes his head, speechless. Semi: I think as long as you know your history, you need not worry how I'm preparing for Derf, but just what exactly Eric Derf is in for when I step in the ring with him again. Semi stands up quickly and his chair loudly scoots back across the cement floor. Semi continues talking loudly, with determination. Semi: I'm sure you read all your silly little wrestling blogs; and that being said, you should know by now that the last time I was in the ring with Mr. Eric Derf, he spent the night in the hospital getting his battered and bloodied head stitched up after I sank a steel chair into it! Semi snatches a beer off the table and downs the last of it quickly as the three men around the table look on in unease. Semi crushes the can in his hand and tosses it to the side. He puts his hands down on the table and leans in towards Sam as he continues, extremely frustrated. Semi: Not only did he sit in that hospital all night, but from what I hear, he sat there and talked trash about how Semi is unstable and didn't deserve to get a push over him. Semi looks disgusted and spits on the floor to put an exclamation point on his sentence. After a pause from Semi, Sam speaks softly and timidly. Sam: Eric did beat DC though. Semi stands straight and talks loudly and furiously. Semi: Yeah?! What's your point?! I won my match last week as well, kiddo! And that was against two men in a giant steel dome! Sure Derf beat DC, who may be a former world champion, but so am I of ICW, not to mention the current world champion of WHQ, AND the man carrying the X Havoc championship! Semi takes a deep breath and continues, bitterly Semi: As far as I can tell, they pulled me out of that match with DC when they realized that Semi isn't playing games in MWE and instead put me in Terror Dome as an insurance policy so I didn't take out one of their top money makers! Sam: What about Bowen? Semi: What about Bowen? Did you see what happened to him in that match? I'll worry about Bowen when he can walk again, let alone wrestle. Derf is the next to be run down, therefore the only one that matters at this point in time. Semi puts his hand to his head that is now flushing red and rubs it. He continues on, less angry and more matter-of-factly. Semi: The way I see it, Eric Derf has already lost this match to himself. The first thing on Eric Derf's mind is revenge. He's not going to out wrestle me and he's DAMN SURE not going to beat me down. Plus, Eric's the kind of guy that thinks just because he recently lost that title, it's still his for the taking. He thinks he's wrestling down hill. Looking extremely smug, Semi pulls the chair he shoved back under him again and continues, never taking his eyes off Sam. Semi: You want to know how I've been preparing for this match? It's simple. All I've had to do is remember that when the Semi rolls down the Highway to Hell, I AM GOING TO RUN ERIC DERF DOWN! The only thing breaking the silence for a long moment is Semi's loud catchphrase reverberating off the walls of the dusty basement. The three men around the table from Semi look back and forth between one another with worry in their eyes, not daring to speak for a while. Finally Joey breaks the silence. Joey: Did you guys know the band U2 was originally called Feedback? Everyone in the room turns confused and surprised looks at Joey. Joey: What? The new awkward silence is broken by a generic ring tone. Sam looks up and shifts around behind the table. He pulls out a red cell phone and answers it. Sam: Hello? Oh, good! See you soon. Sam returns the phone to his pocket, looking excited and looks around as everyone has their eyes glued to him. Sam: That was my sister. Her and I are going home together, but first she wants to stop in and meet people. Sam quickly glances at Semi as he says this then back down at the table. Semi gives him an annoyed look as he begins to calm down. Sam: So, uh... another hand of poker? All three other men state in unison firmly, "No!" The camera cuts out and back in as Mike is throwing away beer cans and Sam and Joey chat. Semi sits, leaned back in his chair, with his arms crossed across his chest. Suddenly, a knock comes from upstairs. Sam: Ooh, that's Lilly. I'll get it. Sam jumps up and hastily makes his way to and up the stairs. Joey turns his attention to Semi. Joey: What's up with you, man? You haven't talked to us much at all tonight, other than when you went off about your wrestling match. Semi sighs and glares at Joey for a long moment. Just as he appears he's going to respond, Sam's voice is heard down the stairs, soon followed by steps. Sam: Everyone... Sam appears as he reaches the bottom of the stairs and steps to the side. Sam: This is my sister, Lilly. Following Sam is a beautiful black haired woman with big brown eyes and pouty red lips. She is wearing a black dress that shows off her gorgeous body, lots of cleavage and long legs sticking out from a short skirt. She looks around the room and smiles wide, showing sparkly white teeth. Mike's eyes grow wide and he stops his cleaning immediately. Joey whistles. Semi looks her over but without changing his smug expression whatsoever. Joey: Hell, Sam, why didn't you introduce me sooner?! Mike: Likewise! Lilly bites her bottom lip, still smiling, and turns her gaze to the floor. Then she looks over at Sam. They meet eyes and clearly try to signal to each other. Sam then looks down at the floor and begins to speak, again, timid as a sheep. Sam: Um, Semi, Lilly is a big wrestling fan too. Sam looks up at Semi who wrinkles his brow, telling him to move along. Sam: And well, she has always dreamed of being a ring valet. We... um, she... was thinking maybe... Semi cuts Sam off by raising his hand. He sighs deeply and looks Lilly in the eyes. Semi: No. Sam and Lilly both look surprised, as do Mike and Joey. Joey: Are you frikkin' crazy, man?! Look at her! You don't want to travel with her? Semi pounds his fist on the table and shouts. Semi: I said no! Semi then stands up and looks at Joey. He talks loudly, furiously, and defensively. Semi: Are you crazy?! I don't travel with people! I don't walk to the ring with people. I don't like people! You were wondering why I haven't said much tonight, and that's why! You drunken basement dwellers are the kind of pathetic slobs that boo me all the way to and from the ring every week! Semi flashes a look at Mike as well as he says this. He then turns towards Sam and Lilly and points at Sam. Semi: YOU are the kind of think-you-know-it-all losers that are the kind of people that just want a handout of spotlight from greatness such as myself wherever you can get it! Semi then moves his finger from Sam to Lilly. Semi: And YOU are the kind no personality, sleazy tramp that relies on your fake boobs and extensive bedroom experience to get you where you want to go in life! Semi looks between every appalled face in the room another time and yells again, aimlessly. Semi: I don't need her, I don't need any of you freaks, and I don't need anyone else, either! This Semi travels the highway alone; all the way to Hell! Semi then storms around Sam and Lilly without making eye contact and to the stairs. Everyone looks around the room with disgusted faces as you hear Semi stomp up the stairs. Sam shakily makes his way through the silent room towards his seat and it mountain of poker chips. You then hear stomping again, now coming back down the stairs. Everyone averts their attention that way. Semi rounds the stairs back towards the table with wallet in hand. Still not making any eye contact, he grabs a few bills from his wallet and tosses them into Sam's pile of chips. Semi then returns his wallet to his pocket, grabs the two cases of beer off the floor, and stomps back up the stairs again. The camera cuts out.
