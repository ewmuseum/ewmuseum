3 Part Promo.; I meant it was 3 Parts.

Mar 9 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Sunday March 1st 2009, 9:51 PM. Aftermath of the Blood Fuze vs Black List Mafia tag team match. Mr Markowitz is in the ring holding a microphone. Markowitz has a look of relief on his face and he is slightly smiling. He wets his lips to speak but quickly stops. He walks around in the ring for a bit before stopping. He cracks his knuckles by stretching out his hands, which are locked together, outwards. Markowitz: Tonight, Triple G and I, the tag team known as Blood Fuze, The crowd erupts in cheers but a few boos can be heard around the arena. Markowitz stops, sighs, runs his fingers threw his black, rough hair, and tilts his head back a little. Markowitz: Settle down now. As I was saying, we beat, Virus and Supernova last week. The crowd erupts in all cheers now at the expense of Black List Mafia. Markowitz can be seen smiling and holding his mic below his waist. Markowitz: Proving, whatever we said last week was true. But now, last week doesn't matter. While Triple G has a match against Supernova, of the Black List Mafia, I have the time off. While I do wish G luck in his match this week, I don't wish him a single bit of luck in his match against... Me. Triple G, I don't wish you luck because you don't need it! You are one hell of a phenomenal wrestler and I know you have just as good as a chance as beating me as I do beating you. Markowitz has a huge grin on his face and we hear the crowd popping at Markowitz generosity. Markowitz: So, let me hold a proposition for you, whoever wins in our match, is the.. CAPTAIN, of Blood Fuze. They have the ability to overrule any decisions made. The right to us there entrance music when we come out. So what do you say bro? Deal? Or no deal? Markowitz chuckles for a minute as the crowd pops at his proposition. Markowitz puts his right hand in the air signaling he is done. He lightly tosses the microphone to the referee, who is sitting on a fold-out silver and metal chair, as he catches it with ease. Markowitz rolls out of the ring and gets to the entrance ramp. He signals goodbye to the crowd and then turns around and walks away. After a minute or two of slowly walking back with the crowd cheering, Markowitz gets to the end, screams goodbye one more time and heads to the back. Sunday March 1st 2009, 10:46 PM. The camera now shifts to Mr Markowitz backstage. He is talking to a camera guy., and we also see the MWE interviewer, Sara Paterson. The camera guy signals to Markowitz that they will be on Camera in, 5,4,3,2,1. Sara Patterson: We have Mr Markowitz, one half of the notorious tag team known as Blood Fuze. Mr Markowitz, I see you are facing your own partner, Triple G, thoughts? Markowitz is visibly staring at Sara's breasts. He is in a daze. Sara looks at him awkwardly, shakes her head, and then snaps her fingers and with that Markowitz looks up with a confused look. Sara: Markowitz? What's your answer? Markowitz: Huh? What? Sara: I asked you about your thoughts on your match against Triple G. Markowitz: oh.. Well.. well, Sara, the thing is, we will face each other, but remember, this match wont be a match to one up each other, this will be a match, to pay our respect to the former MWE owner, Tim. But, when the bell rings in our match, there is no predicting who will win but I know it will be close. That is all I have to say. Sara: I see.. I see. Well what else can I ask you, ah, your thoughts on your match last week? Markowitz smiles and signals his enjoyment, and Sara has an intrigued look on her face. Markowitz: Well, some may call it luck, and others may call it a fluke... Well, you know what I call it? Blood Fuze. Yeah, thats right. Blood Fuze. We are the future and beating Virus and Supernova proves our theory. We beat them with skill and there is nothing they could do to prove us wrong. Fact of the matter is.. We beat them! They continue to say they are better, it was a fluke, but you know what? We won and that is all that matters. During that whole speech, Markowitz was getting excited and flailing his arms around to prove a point. Sara: Interesting, I don't know what to call your win but all I know is that it was impressive. Markowitz is once again, visually looking at Sara's breasts. He is in a deep phase. Sara: Thats it, I'm out. Sara walks away as Markowitz looks up. Markowitz *Out of his phase*: Wait wha'? Sara? Camera Guy: She left. You were staring at her.. uhh.. Boobs. Markowitz: Oh SH*T! Oh well.. I got to go anyway, see ya. Sunday March 2nd 2009, 12:54 AM. Markowitz gets out of his car. His red and white striped 1969 Gran Torino to be exact. He is in his driveway. His house is painted a bluish, greenish color. The front door is made of wood, it has a square piece in the middle made of glass with different designs. The windows are rectangular shaped and green. He walks up the pebble filled driveway to the front door and puts his keys inside the hole. The door opens up and the breeze from the outside flows inside. He is now inside the house. He looks around for a bit. The kitchen door is open and he hears a slight noise. Markowitz: Dad? You home? At that moment a semi large man walks out. He resembles Mr Markowitz a bit. It is revealed that he is his dad. Dad: Hey son, were ya been? Markowitz: Oh, hey dad! I was at my ICW show! Don't you remember? Did you even watch it? Dad: You and your stupid wrestling. And no I did not. Mom was never to happy about you going into the wrestling business. Markowitz is smiling. He always likes to enjoy a chat with his dad. Markowitz: Well you know mom, she doesn't like physical violence. In anything. Mr Markowitz and his dad, both start to chuckle a bit. They both take deep breaths before stopping. Dad: So.. Did you win your fight? Or however you guys call it. Markowitz: Its called a match, dad. And I sure did! Me and Trip.. I mean Gary were partners and we beat this team called The Black List Mafia. Dad: Very swell. And about Gary... How is he doing? Markowitz: Well as I said we both won tonight, and next week he is against Supernova of The Black List Mafia. Dad: Well knowing Gary, and all his confidence and determination... He will probably lose. Dad chuckles and jokingly wipes a tear from his eye. Markowitz: Ha. And get this, me and him are both facing each other in two weeks. Dad: Really? I don't care how much your mother disapproved, I gotta see this. Two little kids going at it. Haha. Markowitz *sigh*: Oh dad... The scene ends as both Markowitz and his dad are laughing. OOC: I wrote this RP before the ICW results went up, thats why I didnt mention G winning.
