I mean, I'd die for a Title, Brad'd die for a Title. Brad fights for his kids. And Me? Contrary to everything I've done since the red light switched, I stand to my feet with all the motivation in the world. I walk off shot and the next time the MWE Universe see's me again, I'm holding a 10 week old baby boy. MY baby boy. My world. I hold him in my arms. Tight enough to keep him safe but soft enough to do the same. I sit back down in my deck chair, holding my boy as his eyes open up to me and the gorgeous skies above me. This time, my eyes are locked on him. The sea might as well not be there. ...I fight for Cian Xander Blaise! (pronounced "key-en Zander Blaze") Though you can call him CXB for short. Remember the name, he'll be the one to end my Title reign, given 20 years or so. I smile, though before long I swallow hard and do something I've yet to do today. I look up, staring straight at the camera, dead set. GM, don't mistake. Think you got me all figured out? Well I bet you didn't notice, throughout this poetic yet merciless verbal beat down, throughout this massacre of your character, your history, your wellbeing...That I haven't cursed at you ONCE. Go ahead, watch the playback. I'm CSB, good father, great example. What? Thought I was gonna' come out here playing some loudmouth game like you? GM I'm sorry, but you just don't have me figured out the way you think you do. You don't have my number. Matter fact, I don't even have a phone. I look down and place my hands over Cian's ears, similar to an earmuff effect. But for the record, I'm gonna' fuck you up and drop a douche in your mom's vag-sack this Sunday at Turmoil. Just FYI. I just thought that if I had to see Drew Jackson again I'd probably beat him with a tree, so better safe than sorry. But forget the punch lines for a minute. It sinks in. This Sunday I'm facing one of MWE's biggest names and I'm facing him...for The World Heavyweight Championship. You've had your Crowning Glory. Now, I'm about to have mine. Grand Mystique, feel my me- I look down, realising that things are a little different now. Ya' know...just feel it! I sit back in the deck chair and enjoy the presence of my best friend and only child as the camera turns off, ending one of my most passionate sermon to date. One war down. One to go. *Fade To Black*
