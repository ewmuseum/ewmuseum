Revelations

Jan 12 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
OOC: FUCKING FORMATTING ... Sorry, but this would have been up before the deadline if it wasn't for it, if I loose that's fine but I hope that since it's only a few minutes late you'll take it into consideration. I know I should have posted this a lot sooner but I've had to get back into the routine of school and everything lately and yeah ... nevermind. Anyway, the bright blue/aqua is Dallas' thoughts so you can understand that part of it. Sorry to everyone, especially Mason for this being so late. Once again The black is broken by a a shining green line darting drastically upwards, and then back down again before it trails away to the right of the screen. I find myself lying here, helplessly, and hopelessly searching my soul for the reason why. The same motion from the light as before flickers past again, beeping sharply as it does, before fading back into black. Why I let it slip so far as to risk these injuries when they were unnecessary; Why I took things into my own hands instead of just letting them play out as the cards were dealt. But thinking about it now, it all snaps back into perspective. Everything I have done, is because I have found the one person on this entire rat hole of a planet, that actually makes he fear for the well-being of something. The one person that makes me feel like they could single handily tear down an entire nation with their words, twisting them into the minds of leaders to get what they want! Everything I have done to myself is to stop something even worse happening to the MWE. Everything has been to stop, Ace Cannon! The same green glow glares across the screen, however this time flat-lining across, the beep stays and becomes a long drawn out tone. The camera soon falls victim to a sudden jerk causing it to fall to the floor, and as it lays there we watch. As Dallas has thrown himself out of his hospital bed and is in a fit of rage as he begins tearing the wires and sensors off of himself. His head turns to look at something as a team of medical personal that heard the tone from the heartbeat monitor come rushing in, seeing what he is doing they quickly grab him by the arms and try to force him back down to his bed. Yet Joey is having nothing of it and fights his arms loose, before throwing a small man dressed in scrubs down to the bed forcefully. Seconds later Dallas picks the camera off the ground (it's a camcorder folks) and points it towards himself as he storms out of the room, pushing past some nurses running in to help as he does so. As we continue to focus on Dallas we start to notice the some important details of this scene, as he is dressed in a greenish-white hospital gown that has blood stained across the top of it from the last few days that Dallas has been unconscious for. He has a fresh bandage wrapped around his head and his hair, for the first time since he has returned, is scruffy and messed, laying flat on his head opposed to his usual styling. The look on his face tells it all, he is on the hunt. Dallas continues to march through the white hallways, throwing open doors as he does so. Behind one door is a child that appears to be about twelve or thirteen lying motionless in a hospital bed, dressed in the same disgusting colors as Dallas. This was not was he was looking for and he slams the door shut and continues along his way, throwing more and more doors open, finding them either empty or with common patients. He has already begun breathing heavily and he is only a little ways down from where his journey began. From off camera we can hear people running, Dallas quickly turns his attention to them and within a few short moments he has been grabbed by the arm and is being drug back down the hallway. The camera falls once again to the floor, we watch as Dallas tries to fight off two rather muscular men dressed in scrubs as he begins yelling at them, his words coming between gasps for air, his words are loud, yet weak. Joey Dallas: Let go of ... me! You fat sons .... of bitches! Where the ... hell is CANNON! He continues to struggle, but to no avail. The injuries from the Lights Out match are too much for him to keep this up and soon enough his arms and legs become limp and the men drag him back into his room, disappearing from view. The camera remains on the ground as a pair of legs walk past and into a room near by. A few seconds later one of the men that forced Dallas emerges from the room and walks directly towards the camera, picking it up and looking into it. He moves it around for a few seconds and then we cut to black. What happened that night? What day is it now? Everything is still a blur to me and it doesn't seem like anyone around this place wants to give me a strait answer as to whether or not I put a stop to Cannon's reign of hatred. The last thing I remember was my head hitting that cold concrete floor, then everything becomes a blur. The camera clicks back on, a green tint of night vision fills the screen as Dallas' arm pulls away from the camera and ends up on the back of his head, he is running his fingers over the torn skin on his scalp trying to remember the ending to his brutal match against Cannon last Sunday. His feet are thrown over the side of his bed and he is looking down at the ceramic floor. He takes a few deep breaths before raising his head to look into the camera, his eyes are dilated from the lack of light in the room. Dallas rubs his hair and then brings his hand down to run it over his face a few times, feeling his rough beard run between his fingers before dropping his hand down to rest on his lap. Joey Dallas: Right now, I don't know where I am, I don't know what day it is, and I don't even know if I successfully completed my mission against Cannon, but the one thing I do know is that whatever the outcome of that match was, neither one of us came walking out with our arms raised with feeling of accomplishment. I know that my body has been through the most vicious and tolling match of my career. I know that no matter how bad I'm feeling right now, Cannon is dealing with the same pain and anguish that I am. Dallas pushes himself off of the bed, slowly, painfully working himself to a vertical base. As he moves his hands away from his surroundings, his knee buckles, causing him to stumble backwards, eventually falling back onto the bed ass first. He takes a few seconds to let the pain in his legs fade away, looking back towards the camera again as he does so. Joey Dallas: Cannon is going through the same problems, the difficulty walking, even standing. The discomfort of the simple act of breathing. Weakness when trying to stand. He feels like the entire weight of the world has been dumped onto his shoulders. I know that he doesn't know what happened at Adrenaline Rush either, and I know him, he is going to do whatever it takes to find out. But so will I. He forces himself to his feet, and lunges towards a small cabinet, which to him in the dark is nothing more than a vague outline, he hits it full force and drops to the floor with a thud. Dallas is now off camera, but we can here him struggling to do something from the floor. About a minute goes by before we see and arm come from the floor, flailing around on the front of the cabinet, trying desperately to find something sturdy to hold onto to. Finding the handle to the drawer, he tries to pull himself to his feet, only to bring the drawer and all it's contents crashing down on top of him. A yelp of pain and the crashing of everything that was inside the drawer breaks the otherwise silent scene, but it soon fades back into silence, only being broken by an occasional deep breath and sound of Dallas' hand smacking against the floor in anger. We hear a short grunt just as Dallas' hand reaches up to the camera, pulling it down to the floor, causing static to flash across the screen. As the scene clears back up we see the scattered contents lying across the floor. Paper, pens, a small book of common diseases, and perhaps most importantly a small cellphone. Dallas has now pushed himself into a seated position, his back leaning against the cabinet, eyes wandering through the darkness until they come to rest on the blue glow of his cellphone. He reaches towards it, finding that it is just out of reach, but we already know the resilience of this man and it comes to the surface here again as he begins to claw towards the phone, pulling himself back to the floor to reach it, taking it firmly in his hand, or at least as firmly as he can. He flips it open and looks at the screen, squinting as he waits for his eyes to adjust to the glare. January eleventh? This can't be right, I have been passed out in this damn hospital for over a week!? I missed the dawning of a new year? All because of that barbarian Cannon decided to take things into a new echelon of violence and personal torture. But the worst part is, as I try and find things out for myself, the thought that keeps crossing my mind, is that maybe he managed to avoid salvation. Dallas squints at the screen of his phone as he begins pressing buttons in a sequence, after hitting the last few numbers the phone begins to ring. A few short rings later, and the familiar recording begins speaking, just loud enough for us to hear it. "The date is, January fifth, two thousand nine. You have two new messages. Message one." There is a short silence, and then the familiar voice of Richard Covell begins to crackle from the phone. QUOTE (Richard Covell) I hope your happy, you and Cannon have run up the cost of medical insurance of this company for years to come with that little match of yours. Things like this usually don't piss me off, but from both of your conditions at the moment, I know that this fight of yours isn't over yet. So regardless of whether you get this damn message or not, I'm going to take that little wager of yours and book those matches you so desperately wanted. So on the next Monday Night Mayhem it will be Sinc Mercier versus Ace Cannon and Mason Resoan versus YOU! WOOOOOOOOO! Lovely, just lovely, I can't even get my bearings enough to walk, and he wants me to just proudly march myself down to the ring like I'm one hundred percent. It is exactly this kind of arrogance and disregard for the people that work for him that disturbs me about Covell. He sees us as nothing more than his talent, and throws us around like little toys week in and week out regardless of health. He doesn't seem to realize that we are merely human, we are of but flesh and blood. He lets out a groan of pain as he tries to push himself into a more upright seated position, straining hard on his arms and back to do so. The phone's recording once again begins playing, this time simply saying "Message Two" as it begins crackling a second time, the words are broken and unclear for a few seconds, however as time passes by slowly the voice becomes clearer and clearer, coming in as clearly as a cellphone will allow, the booming voice of Sinc coming through. QUOTE (Sinc Mercier) ... Picking up the scraps huh!? Well from that show it looks like that's all your gonna be on Monday you smug son of a b*tch! So go ahead and try to save me, but unless you can beat Resoan then you prove NOTHING to me. You may have beaten Cannon, but at what cost? .. Your f*cking pathetic! That was just the boost he needed, well not his protégée suddenly considering himself better then him, but those words, the glorious, glorious words that have begun ringing through his head since he heard them "You may have beaten Cannon" everything he needed, everything he wanted, all in one small little phrase. A small smirk twists it's way onto his face, and with chuckle he snaps the phone shut with one hand. He drops the phone back to the floor and throws his arm up, locking it around the whole where the drawer had been. Pulling himself up, limp legs gradually becoming sturdier. Dallas pushes his right leg under himself, using it to push up enough to get his left leg to a knee. With this he puts his free hand flat on top of the cabinet and with one swift motion he thrusts himself to both feet. He knew that Ace Cannon had done everything that was possible to avoid his inevitable salvation, but in the end, it was slowly but surely coming to him. But the thought of Monday Night Mayhem kept creeping back into his head, the thought of Mason Resoan, standing face to face with him, and quite honestly, it disgusted Dallas. You can see it on his face, as his mouth quivered into that indescribable shape of loathing, like a bitter taste was stuck in his mouth. Dallas lets his eyes search among the dark room, searching for the blinking red light from the camera that showed that it was on. Finally spotting it, he pushes away from his whereabouts and stumbles towards it, his bare feet appearing larger and larger as he moves closer to the camcorder. The scene abruptly begins to dart around the room, quite obviously from Dallas picking up the camera, coming to rest on his face. Joey Dallas: Sometimes, a saviour must sacrifice his own health and life in order to guide someone down the path of what is good and right. As I stand here, finally knowing that I have set my greatest nemesis on that path, I realize that it is but a small price to pay for the satisfaction that has been etched in my heart. The satisfaction that releasing someone from the evil clutches of the darkness presents upon a person. Yet come tomorrow's edition of Mayhem I face the unsave-able, a man that has been just inches away from being his own saviour, but sure enough this man failed to do so. Letting himself slip ever so deeper into the pit of immorality. He beings stumbling around the room, feeling around the walls in the dark for a light switch. But instead he finds the door, letting his hand slip down to find the knob he turns it. He steadily cracks the door open, letting it swing completely to the wall before staggering slowly into the hallway, falling against the wall yet again, using it to support himself as he brings the camera back to his face. Dallas continues to move, his free arm clawing the wall. Joey Dallas: When I take on Mason Resoan, the name of our program will be a perfect fit, because standing in the ring with a man who participates in such self-indulgence, will lead to nothing but a scene of mayhem. Mason is a man who prides himself on his in-ring ability and personal accomplishments; yet has fell so far in his career. For much like Ace Cannon, I have seen the career of Resoan grow and mature, or rather crash and shrivel. I have seen him after climbing to the top of the mountain, as he held the world in his arms as the world champion, the smug grin on his face as he look down on what would be his inferiors. Dallas continues clawing ever so slowly down the hallway, passing by closed doors. He appears to be a man on a mission as he walks slowly and painfully. Joey Dallas: He was on top of the world, and his ego followed the same path. Mason knew that he had the right to say that he was the best in the world. I am not arguing that fact, but the reality of his situation is that ever since Brian Michaels dove off the top rope and drove his elbow through both him and the stretcher that he was strapped onto that night, he has fallen, he is no longer the best in the world. The wall ends abruptly as Dallas reaches a corner, his eyes once again drift around with a dazed looked on his face. The lighting in the hallway is dim, but it is enough for him to see the crevasse of the elevator shaft. He pushes off the corner, letting out a grunt as he does so, throwing himself towards the elevator coming up just short. Causing him to fall to one knee, reaching forward he grabs the frame of the elevator as to not fall any further, after steading himself he brings his arm up and out of view, but it is quite apparent that he has reached for the button as the door dings and slides open. Dallas slides the camera across the floor of the brightly lit room that is the elevator, the night vision begins to go out of whack, everything appearing as a bright blur of green and white blobs. Crawling in after is Dallas, his skin is a slightly darker green from the rest of his surroundings, his gown is slightly lighter from the rest, but in the end he is just barely visible in the light. As he pulls his leg inside the door closes, tightly shutting behind him. Dallas remains moving, pulling himself up on the wall, struggling to get his arm high enough to push the "G" button for the ground floor. He pushes himself backwards so he is sitting next to the camera with his back propped up on the rear wall. He reaches his hand towards the camera, and for a brief moment we cut to black but come back, once again in color. Dallas' face is red from his struggle towards our current position. Joey Dallas: Mason has fallen a long hard drop from his place at the top of the world, once a proud man that could rightfully call himself a main event has found himself in a spot that I was when I first broke into competition. A man that had held the entire world in his arms is now nothing more than a common mid-carder. Yet for some reason, in the back of this brash young mans mind, this man seems to think that he still has the privilege to the fame and respect that he had once earned. His ego has grown to the point where he truly and fully believes that having a successful past instantly earns you the right to find yourself in competition for the title of being the greatest that has ever been. But I can see past Resoan's ego, I can see that deep, deep down he knows that he will never reach the point that he was once at. The same ding sounds again, this time the door slides open revealing the lobby of the hospital. Dallas takes the camera in hand again and pushes himself up. Once again he begins staggering, moving towards the main doors, the camcorder focusing on it as he continues his rant. Joey Dallas: Resoan may have gone through some of the best men in this industry to get to his first reign as the World Champion, but he does not know what it is like to go through the personal torture that I have faced everyday for the last year. The feeling of knowing that there was someone out there that want wants to do whatever it takes to put you exactly where I stand today. He does not know the realization of knowing that someone wants to end your career, that someone wants to leave you in a state where you cannot walk. However Mason still has the over bearing confidence that makes him think that he is better than me and every other person who has been thrown out the door of prosperity and fought to find themselves knocking at that same door. Only those that have put themselves through the most to get back to this role allowed through. So you see, Mason is not a candidate for salvation, if anything it is quite the opposite. Monday is a day that will forever be known as the day that Mason Resoan has been thrown into a personal damnation. Female voice: Excuse sir! You can't leave, your supposed to be in your room recovering! Dallas turns along with the camera, settling in on the receptionist. She is wearing the clean white clothing that is required in a hospital, a name tag hanging just above her left breast reads "Stacy." She has pushed herself away from the desk and is moving towards Dallas. However he turns his attention away from and back towards the door, continuing to stagger towards it. Only a few feet away from the door he drops the camera, static flares over the camera quickly, focusing back in just as Joey pushes the door open. The brisk winter wind begins to blow his hair as he staggers towards the street, a slight misting rain begins to dampen his cloths and hair. The similarities between Mason and Cannon are almost too much, both of these men's egos have become over inflated to the point where they feel they need to prove they are the best. They have gotten to the point where they find wrestling as nothing more than a child's game, that they can manipulate and twist around to benefit there own financial and historical gain. These similarities will continue after Mayhem, as I will have gone to hell and back with both of these men, and as I walk out I will once again put them into the past, as another win written into my personal testimony of why I am the man that this company needs. He has found himself in the middle of the street, both arms out stretched looking into the sky. Street lights shinning brightly down on him casting a large shadow across the concrete. His shoulders moving up and down as he breaths. His legs shaking from the cold and his injuries. I will prove to the world that I am truly the saviour, as I finally put the nail in the coffin of Mason, putting an end to his constant ego trip. I will show each and everyone of the people that doubt me that I am not a one trick pony, that Ace Cannon is not the only man that I can put an end to. I will show Mercier that my words are true, that Mason is no more than a manipulative devil. A man that uses his words to twist reality. The legs of the receptionist run past the camera. As Dallas comes back into view his legs suddenly turn to jello and give out underneath of him. Collapsing to the cold ground as the headlights of a large vehicle come shine onto his body, a loud screech of breaks is heard and then a low battery signal flashes and the camera dies sending us to black.
