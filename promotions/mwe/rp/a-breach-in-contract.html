A Breach In Contract; Virus Debut RP

Dec 9 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Chicago Illinois, Monday, December 8th, 2008, 8:30 PM. The city lights were on, helping the citizens of Chicago see in the darkness of night. However, on this one night, a sort of path of darkness was bing made, as there was this one man. He was about six feet tall, two hundred and seventy five pounds, and wore a black trenchcoat. But what was most interesting about this one man, is that his eyes glowed a sinister shade of red. Every step he took, light would shatter. This man, was Leon 'The Virus' Roberts. Thje Devil's Incarnate. Needless to say, he was beyond pissed at this moment, and the air that everyone breathed around him on this night, people felt like an Elephant was stepping on them. Why was He so pissed off? It lead to where he was going. The headquarters of MWE. About ten minutes later, he arrived at the office of the MWE owner, Richard Covell. Leon knocked on the door Covell: It's open. Leon didn't open the door, he kicked it in. At that moment, thunder echoed through the sky, as it began to rain heavily. The door was in splinters, and Covell now had a scowl on his face. But he saw Leon's sillouette, and quickly realized that his door was the least of his problems. Every step that Leon took, echoed through the room. The mere presence of such an monster shook even the bravest men to their very cores. Leon eventually stopped right in front of Covell's desk, and managed to grab a nearby empty coffee much, and held it in his hands. Leon: Give me one good reason why I shouldn't crush your head like this mug. Leon then easily crushed the mug in his hand. There was no blood, as he was wearing a pair of gloves at the time. But the remains of the mug were scattered all over the floor. Covell: What is this about Leon? Leon: About Genesis. I wasn't supposed to be booked at all for this episode of Genesis. Tell me, why am I competing, when we had written, as part of my contract, that I would not be competing until Adrenaline Rush? Leon walked around the desk, as Richard Covell rose up from his chair. Leon stared into the eyes of Covell, right when he was in front of him. Covell's lip quivered in nervousness. Covell: I'm sorry Leon, but I wasn't the one who booked Genesis. It was Malibu. I even warned him that he should not have done so. Leon smirked at that. He then suddenly grabbed Richard by the collar, and slammed him up against the wall, so where Richard was at eye level with Leon. Leon: Now you listen to me old man. I could care less about who actually made the decision to book me, because you're more at fault then anything. You won the MWE, you could have said that it was to not happen. Yet you did nothing. You are willing to bed over for anyone, more then a one cent whore. Or did you suddenly become that when you slept with the one cent whore who is Ace Cannon's mother? Richard couldn't speak. Leon had Richard in a position where he could only agree to whatever Leon was going to say. Leon: I'm very tempted to hang you out the balcony window right now, so that you can get a perfect view of how far of a fall it is from up here, but I'm sure that you will listen up. Because you didn't cancel my match, you will have to keep me very happy from now on, or I will make you regret it. Because while I'm happy for the fact I get the finally kill D.C for the wrongs he's done, I would have rather have waited. Afterall, I do possess the spirit of the Devil himself, and that being said, contracts are something that I take very seriously. UNDERSTOOD!?!?! Richard barely nods, as Leon lets him go, dropping Richard in an unceremonious heap. Richard got p, and dusted himself off, and sat back down. Covell: I-I understand where you are coming from Leon, and I can respect that you're not letting it go easily. But seriously, you should have taken it up with Dick Malibu. Leon: I would, except I didn't feel like killing somebody today, just getting the point across. Either way, this scene would have happened regardless if I ripped his head off or not. I have no qualms about doing that, because of the history that he and I share. Namely who he's managed. Your lemon of a son, Ace Cannon. What was to stop me anyways. Nothing. Covell: I understand Leon. I'll do what I can to make it up to you, how's that? Leon: You would regardless if you offered or not, because if you refused, I would have taken you to court, and taken away MWE. You're a smart man Covell. But know this. I plan to milk this for as long as I can, because as I said before, I take contracts seriously. Richard sighed nodding his head in agreement. It was then, that Leon offered his hand. Richard was a bit surprised by this, but he accepted the handshake. But when he did. He winced in pain. When the handshake was done. Leon smirked, as Richard stared at his hand. A black mark had appeared at the center of his palm. Leon: That's my calling card. I will remain in contact Richard. Don't fuck up like George Prax did. With that, Leon left the office building. Richad stared at his hand for another minute, before he adjusted his tie. When Leon reached the entrance of the MWE headquarters, he was greeted by his father, Randy Roberts. Leon had a smirk on his face still. The silver hearded man looked at his son as he got in the car. Randy: So, how was it? Leon: Better then I thought. The little bitch understood me perfectly. Randy: So did he call off the match? Leon: I never asked him to do that. This match is personal. Randy: Personal? Leon: Yeah. I've waited a while to get my hands around DC's neck and remove his head like a champaign cork. Randy smirked at that joke, as he started the car off and drove off. Randy: Why do you say that son? Leon chuckled for a second. As he calmed down, he looked at his father with a smug look on his face. Leon: You'll see. Buffalo New York, December 10th 2008, 4 am. It was early morning time. Leon was actually outside on his hotel room balcony. He had been drinking the night before, and he still was. He was also smoking a fat joint. Was he overly affected by the chemical induced states that weed and booze were supposed to produce. At the moment, he was not. After a few minutes though, he would go back inside. He was alone this time. But being that he was wide awake this evening, he decided to do his shoot. So he set up his camcorder, and sat back down, out on the balcony, while shooting. Leon: Quite the view, isn't it? I figure I'd start this shoot on a light note, as it's going to get heavy very quickly. Leon adjusted the camera, so that he was in view. He sighed, taking a big puff from his joint. Leon: Adrenaline Rush was supposed t be special. Everyone was anticipating something big to happen there. What was supposed to happen, would be my very first appearance in an MWE ring. I had built those viral promos, displayed them on MWE.com like it was hacked, but there were some people who were determined to spoil the surprise. Nevertheless, Adrenaline Rush was when I was supposed to make my first appearance in and MWE ring, and not before. That's not going to be the case. Because the General Manager of Genesis, Dick Malibu, in his idiocy and possible grudge against me for beating up his main client on a lot of occasions, decided to book me on Genesis. He at least did the right thing by booking me against the champ, the attention whore, DC. Leon chuckles, as he drinks some of the contents from the large bottle of Jack Daniels. Leon: Is it easy to see why he's an attention whore? It's his girlfriend, Lindsey. Yeah, I fucken said it. DC, If you're watching, I fucken said your ex's name. You know what that means? It means that I'm testing DC. I'm testing him, because I don't think he has what it takes anymore. He's nothing like he was six months ago. He's gone from somebody who I was cautious to face in the ring, to somebody who has become but a footnote, even though he is a world champion. How pathetic is that? I have been watching MWE for a while, and from what I saw, DC has had nothing but loss after loss, after loss. I can see it's getting to him, despite the fact he got two big wins on the previous episode of Genesis. This week will be no different. See DC, you and I do have some history, and I am about to remind you full aware of it. Leon leaned in closer, as he took another swig from his Jack Daniels bottle, and had a goodfive second puff from his joint. Leon: Our paths first crossed six months ago. You were MWE champion in Enigmar Wrestling, while I was the ever dominant Enigmar Wrestling Champion. I was champion of a company that was new, and people watched me, kick more ass then anyone else, as I rose up in ranks. You had your title practically handed to you, which was a crying shame. We then faced off, and you beat me. I admitted, that on that night, you were the better man, and I was all prepared to step back and let you carry Enigmar Wrestling, on your back, just as proudly as I did, if not more. Then it came. The day that you were named the chairman for a wee because of Mr Scott's sudden illnesses. He became ill the second you were announced the Enigmar Wrestling champion. Because while you were chairman, EW went from strong and tough, to being on life support. All because you decided to throw away the title I alone gave massive prestige to, to somebody just as bad, if not worse then you, when it comes to deteriorating companies, and in the art of douche-baggery. I'm talking about Ace Cannon. You know I was extremely livid about that ordeal. From that moment on, I promised that I would pay you back one day. This Friday on Genesis, for your sins, you will be receiving your punishment. The punishment, is career death. Why do I say that? Well let me put it to you this way. My temper is infamous for causing many premature deaths. My temper, causes anyone who dares to defy me in that state of mind to want to out run the Roadrunner, they're that scared. And all my rage will be directed towards you. Leon then chuckles. You can hear the sinister undertone to his laughter, as he drinks more of his Jack Daniels. Leon: So you basically got your wish DC, because I'm coming after you, madder then a herd of bulls. I know that you're looking for a fight DC, and you got the one person that nobody wants to fight. But then again, you got your own personal problems. Recent break up with your lover, which sucks because I know that feeling. For the record, It was another chick I dated before Demonica, so I somewhat sympathize. Yet after seeing that shoot you did the other day, I can't help but feel that there are things seriously fucked up with you. While you did sleep with that hot maid, you kept to yourself most of the night, and missed out on many chances to have a big time orgie. I was talking to Hef the other day, like right after it, trying to work a deal to have Demonica on the cover once she's no longer pregnant, and he told me about you not taking further advantage of the situation. I couldn't believe it. Leon once again takes a nice long puff from his joint, before he goes and continues. Leon: But besides that, I paid close attention to you. I paid close attention, because I knew that your trap that is your mouth had once again, something to say. I admire the fact you want me to come at you, with everything I had. I already promised I would. But the fact that you kept hyping me up. It just proves that no matter what, nobody gives a shit about you, not even you yourself DC. See you can also mention every damn thing that you said, about how I was to not push your buttons because you feel that you are that pussy ass monster from Cloverfield. I could care less if you were all the mass murderers, monsters, and every other fucken thing wrong in this world, you still would not be able to defeat me this time around DC. You said you want me to act like a man, and cut the magic. Well the magic, I can put aside for now. But to act like a man, and not the devil's incarnate that I am is simply impossible. For you see DC, your threats mean nothing to me. They just mean that you sound like some desperate attention whore who claims they can kill me. Let me ask you this DC How can you kill something....... That has already been killed before. Leon chugs the rest of the Jack Daniels down, before he unceremoniously drops it on the floor, as it shatters. Leon: Yeah. I said it bitch. I am not exactly human. The Devil brought me back to life, for the sole purpose of being his incarnate on Earth. The ultimate harbinger of Chaos. The very Chaos Theory of wrestling. While any man would consider your threats to be true to them, all I see about you is an attention seeking whore, who is like a whining and complaining child when they have their favorite toy smashed to bits, and demanding a new one. You whine and bitch because you believe, that the spotlight should be on you. Why do you think people don't give a fuck about you. Because you're nothing more then a fucken mental case, who should be put out of your misery. Because the attention wasn't on you, you decide to dump what meant the most to you, or at least did. See, I feel as if I can talk about her all day, because I can. But there was another reason I mentioned her. You threatened to harm my daughter. I'm sorry, but I am drawing a line. You want me, keep my family out of it. The moment you drag my family into my affairs, is becomes beyond personal. So here's what I'm going to do DC. I am going to knock you off your throne, for good. Because you don't deserve to be on there. You don't deserve to lead anything. What you deserve DC, is exactly what I'm going to do to you, and what I should have done to Paige. Yeah, saw that last bit. To be honest, I'm not surprised she would stoop so low to sacrifice another human being in her place. Next time, I won't be so careless The best part about the human race, is that everyone has their indulgences for primal savagery of killing. It's in our nature. It's in our blood. I am going to fulfill those indulgences on Genesis, whether they are secret or not, by ripping out your still beating heart, as you see it beating, just as you die. I will then put your heart on the barbecue, and eat it for a snack. So prepare yourself DC. I'm going to at last grant your wish, by putting you in the limelight. You will get all the attention in the world. Because you know what they say, some men are more famous after their dead. You will become one of them DC. See you at the show. END
