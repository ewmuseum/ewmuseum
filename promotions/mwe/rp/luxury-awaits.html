Luxury Awaits

Oct 25 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Carl John Williams, CJ Williams, sat casually on his couch as he watched television. His two bed room apartment was not very large and neither was his television. Luxury wasn't something that CJ necessarily ever experienced, and Mayhem Wrestling Entertainment was his one opporutnity to finally experience luxury, of any kind, for the first time in his life. Now that isn't to say that he has had it rough his whole life, because that is far from the truth. CJ has had his fair share of breaks and shortcuts to help him out throughout life, but nothing that truly put his mind at ease. He never was able to truly relax and this Wednesday afternoon was one of the very first times he was able to just sit and mull things over in his mind. It was here, on a fair weather October night, watching television, that Williams nearly forced himself into a relaxed state for the first time in months, if not years. He had been training hard the previous week, and the week before that, he signed his very first professional wrestling contract with MWE. Things were going his way, and his body needed rest in the upcoming days. Monday Night Mayhem was less than a week away. CJ: Wednesday's never have anything good on TV. CJ shifted his body on his couch to a sideways position. He stared endlessly into the picture box in front of him, completely oblivious to all things around him. CJ: I'm so flippin' bored right now. Why'd my 360 have to go and red ring for a third time. I should just buy a PS3. Ugh. Ceej, as his friends sometimes also call him, flung his legs over the side of the couch on to the floor and got up lazily to his table where he stood his TV on. He looked at his Xbox 360 gaming console and sighed again. CJ: Well I guess for shits and giggles... Williams pressed the power button on his console and listened to his video game system roar to life. He looked on with a glazed look, rather tired of his equipment breaking, but his expression soon turned to glee as his rings appeared green instead of red. CJ: Thank jeebus! What the hell, why is it working all of a sudden... ???: Better not question it. James Carrington or JC, CJ's roommate, had now entered the room from the front door. He was out running some errands and came home just to see CJ's miracle happen in front of him. CJ: I'm literally so happy right now it's ridiculous. I was honestly about to kill myself out of boredom. Wednesdays are, no doubt, some of the most boring days of the week. JC: You're preachin' to the choir dude, my highlight of the day so far has been buying Doritos. Doritos that aren't even mine, mind you. CJ: Speaking of which, go grab those bad boys and you should totally cook up some pizza too. JC: Want me to warm up some hot chocolate and give you handjob too? CJ: Piss off man, I'm gonna set up Gears and we'll play a bit. But like I need to get some Live first. So by the time you're done doing all you gotta do everything will be all set-up. James walked to the kitchen and started removing the contents of his bag onto the counter. You saw an array of different foods and boxes, none of which contained anything to healthy. Chocolate milk mix, Doritios, boxed pizza, Kool-Aid packets, a pound of sugar, and a few two liter bottles of Barqs root beer. CJ instead say back down on the couch a little happier and started buying some Xbox Live points as well as a Xbox Live Gold membership. He popped in Gears of War 3 and sat down, ready to play. James then walked over to the couch, plopped down next to him, and pulled a little snack table close. On it he placed a bag of third degree burn Habenero Doritos and CJ took a handful munching on them. CJ: So how is your mom doin'? I haven't heard you mention her in a few weeks. Carrington thought about it for a little bit, thinking about how his mom truly was doing. JC: Well to be honest, I haven't seen in her in like a week, but she was doing a little better when I caught up with her last. Right now my brother is taking care of her, and him and my sister have been rotating every three or four days of who checks up on her. CJ: Well that's good at least. I honestly forget that you even have a sister sometimes, I don't even think I've ever met her yet. JC: Well I'm a little closer with my brother than her, but that's cause she is always busy. Honestly this is probably the most I've seen of her in the last two years. She has a three old son, so she kind of has her hands full. CJ: What about the dad though? Doesn't he help out, I mean one child can't be that difficult. JC: That's what I was thinking, but her husband, Mark I think his name is but I'm not sure, is a construction contractor so he's usually gone for majority of the day. He's always traveling to Las Vegas and a bunch of other big cities like San Diego bidding on jobs. He helped work on the new Padres stadium back in 04' and ever since his dream is to work on these massive ass projects. Normally I'd laugh at that, but he brings in decent money so I guess whatever. There's a small pause as CJ kind of digests this little information about JC's sister, someone he almost didn't even know existed. They started up around of Annex in Gears and relaxed back in there seats a little. CJ: Well when am I gonna...oh fuck right off you locust prick. CJ's character just then got downed and curbed stomped by the opposing team. JC let out a little chuckle as he hid into cover and grabbed another Dorito. CJ: Anyway when am I gonna finally meet her, and while we're at it, when is your brother gonna come over again. Just then the over bell rang and CJ got up to go retrieve the pizza. The conversation didn't stop though as JC just yelled across the room. JC: I honestly have no idea man. It could be next week, could be next month, or it could be three months from now. Williams lifted the pizza out of the oven and cut in clean in half. He put each half on two separate plates and brought them back to the little snack table and handed James his. CJ: Well I hope it's sooner than later. I want to get to know these peeps a little better. JC: Don't worry, if you plan on staying with me for a while, you'll meet them. You could always take a trip with me to my mom's place one day. One of them is usually hanging around, and I'm sure my mom would do good with a little extra company. CJ: Sounds like plan man. Sounds like a plan. CJ pit into a slice of his pizza as he respawned. JC and CJ then drifted off into mindless banter about the game at hand and the surrounding elements of the game. A few hours passed and many rounds of Gears, a full pizza, a bag of Doritos, and some root beer later and CJ found himself outside on his balcony by himself alone with his thoughts. It was 11:30 PM and James had decided to go to bed, citing boredom and needing to be up early for something and his reasons. CJ was in a pretty good mood and was thinking about meeting Jame's family again, as he had only met his brother a handful of times and his mom once or twice, though JC did talk about CJ to her a decent amount. But mainly his thoughts were about JC himself and what he had done for him in the last few months as well as the upcoming Monday. Taking out his HD flip cam, CJ propped up the camera on a tri-pod outside and looked at the full moon in awe. CJ, since the age of 17, when he first placed in his state wrestling championships, had started a video journal before every major event, whether it be a tournament, a workout session that meant a lot, or a match against a rival. He would use these journals now, in MWE, to convey his thoughts to the world and his opponent, but also to stick with his tradition. CL: I developed many things from my AP Psychology class in high school that have stuck with me, and one of the things that I took was that reflection of self is one of the most vital parts to progressing through life. These video journals are a constant periodic reflection of self to allow me to develop through life. Williams took a second to look at the night sky again and pause leaning against the second floor balcony's railing. CJ: So in layman's terms, these things allow me to watch myself grow. Ceej thought about the way the stars looked in the sky on this night. It was rather empty, but there were six or seven floating around the moon which shined rather brightly. CJ: Something that was explained to me long ago was that in the wrestling world, lucky breaks don't normally happen. People are ruthless, unforgiving, and selfish and that you could either look out for yourself and rise to the top...or let people walk on top of you on their way to the top. CJ adjusted the camera so it focused on him a little more than the back drop of the rest of the apartments in the complex he was living in. CJ: But I'm not an idiot and I understand this. I understand that while awful as it is, James's mom's sickness provided me with a lucky break. It provided me with a chance, an opportunity, and a prospect I dreamed about three years ago. Ceej scratched his head as he looked directly into the lens of the camera, as if he was trying to connect with someone that wasn't there. CJ: This is a chance...a break I won't allow myself to throw away. Williams settles back into a more casual tone and actually walks away from the camera for a second. He goes back through the sliding glass doors into his house and emerges back into the shot a second later with a folding chair in hand. He sets it up and sits down, readjusting the camera on him accordingly. CJ: Unfortunately that means that I have to go one on one with Alex Bowen in my first match. So I wouldn't quite say I've gotten into luxury just yet. A strong wind blows through a CJ shivers a little bit as his arms are exposed as he only sports a plain brown t-shirt. CJ: Alex Bowen is crazy, plain and simple. He is the type of person that gets placed in a white room with various different objects and is studied to see how he reacts with them. Mentally unstable is probably an understatement, but ya know, I'm fine with that. I'm not a bitch, I'm not gonna complain that I am placed in the ring with a psycho on my first match because the way I see it...it's a good way to break me in. Williams takes a quick glance inside to see if James had walked out and seen him talking to the camera, but James was still in his room. CJ: I had to deal with my fair share of weirdos during my independent run, but the issue was I've usually always had a partner. Tag team wrestling for the longest time, and even to this day, is still kind of my strong suit. This will be one of my few singles matches in the last several months and it should be a nice way to see what the future lies in store. Another cold wind current blows through and makes CJ shiver again. He notices a sweat shirt on the couch right next to the window, so he leans over, opens the door, grabs it, and puts it on. CJ: But I was a wrestler all through high school, and a fighter shortly after that. I know what it is like to be in the ring, octagon, or mat by yourself with no help. It's like solitary confinement until you get out and I'm sure Bowen will thrive on that. Actually, I'm positive Bowen will strive on that. Ceej places his hands in his pockets and settles back into his chair, almost slouching at this point. He rolls his neck and continues. CJ: This match will be interesting though for a bunch of different reasons and I'm excited for it. Because Bowen might not be the brightest bulb in the box, he might not be the sharpest axe in the shed, but he has motivation and a drive...whatever the hell it may be, I know he has it. But I have my drive to. This isn't just an exhibition to give us something to do, this is the relaunch of Mayhem Wrestling Entertainment and its flagship show. This is the first round of a tournament for the World Heavyweight Championship. There is more riding on this match than meets the eye. CJ takes his hands out of his pockets and slowly runs them through his hair as he sits up a little more straight once again focusing in on the camera to connect with whomever is watching. CJ: Bowen I want you to know that everything I do has a reason and purpose. I'm not a fairweather type of guy on anything. If I like a sports team, I'm a hardcore fan. If I play a video game, I get into that video game. And when it comes to wrestling, I'm as dedicated as they come. Williams pauses to collect his thoughts for a moment. CJ: To me this Monday is going to the most significant match of my career, hell, my life, up until this point. I'm gonna make myself known by defeating you and moving on in this tournament. Fans of MWE who don't know me will soon find out that while I may not be doing this specifically for them all the time, that no matter what I will deliver them one hell of an entertaining product. When I'm on screen, there will not be a dull moment. CJ stands up and places the chair to side, picking up the flip cam now and pointing it only at his face. CJ: This Monday night is going to be for me, I won't lie. But it isn't just for me. It's for James for giving me this opportunity. It's for the MWE board of directors and higher ups to prove that I'm not a flake and that I can hold my own with anyone, crazy or not. This is to prove to the fans that I am THE GUY to watch on this program. And it's to show to you that you may think that by being uncanny, unpredictable, and borderline insane makes you a tough opponent to face, but I'm here to prove you wrong. I'm here to take myself to heights I only ever dreamed of and a title shot is in my future. Bowen you are in my path, and I'm not gonna claim that this is divine will. I'm not gonna claim that this is my destiny. I'm not gonna spew a bunch of bullshit about how I'm the best and that I'm unbeatable. No, fuck that, I'm here to straight up tell you that I'm going to explode on Monday. My adrenaline will be at an all time high and if you don't come prepared to take what I'm going to dish out, you WILL lose. CJ puts more emphasis on the will and continues to rile himself up a little. CJ: I'm not trying to be artsy and hipster, but there was a little man named Gandhi who said that you have to be the change you want to see in the world. Well I'm saying that rather than allowing MWE to get filled with lazy, insane, and undeserving competitors where change is needed...I'm simply going to start things off the way things should be started off. With intensity. With explosion. And with purpose. And my purpose right now is to defeat you and get one step closer to taking full advantage of the opportunity that was given to me. My purpose is to win. Williams gave a small little smirk as he closed his flip cam and placed in it his pocket, trudging back inside to watch some more TV before getting some rest.
