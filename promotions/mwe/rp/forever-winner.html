Forever Winner; Brad Kane Production

Oct 6 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Overcast skies dominate the skies of Boston sky. Rain wants to fall from the skies but there something holding the drops back. Perhaps the humidity just isn't quite right or its just one of those days. Naturally the location in Boston never seems to really change but for once, its not the giant Kane mansion. Oh no, it certain can't be a place like this. This place is a freaking death trap for quite a few families, those who have a certain deathwish. Oh, if you haven't figured it out by now, they're at... McDONALD'S The single biggest deathwish for any parents with kids of every single age. If its not the Happy Meal's, its the damn playplace. Always something at McD's for the kiddies to complain about. Either way, Brad Kane has family around a large table with a couple of high chairs for his daughters, Sarah and Jennifer. Sitting in booster seats are Lacey and Lilly. Missing, though, are Kaylee and Ryan but why exactly would you take four month old babies to a place such as this. No matter as Brad and Megan are having a hard enough time trying to keep Lacey and Lilly under control. Seeing that both of them are very hyperactive, neither of them really care to focus eating on their chicken nuggets and apple slices. Megan is busy giving Sarah and Jenny a couple of french fries while they throw them around a bit, causing a giant mess. Megan Kane: Sarah, stop that! Sarah Kane: Mama! Megan looks to be nearly at her wits end trying to deal with her twin daughters. She glances over at Brad for a moment but can tell his hands are full enough with her stepdaughters. Megan Kane: Honey, I know I'm mommy but please stop throwing your lunch on the floor. It creates a mess for someone else to pick up. Jennifer Kane: POOPIES! Oh god, that messy diaper smell begins to overtake the table. Megan stands and grabs Jennifer and a diaper bag before running off to the bathroom. Now this sets Sarah off, watching her twin sister getting grabbed off before crying her eyes out. This throws Brad into a bind, wondering if he needs to comfort Sarah or keep his focus on Lilly and Lacey. The crying one year old gets to him more then the other two. Brad stands up and grabs Sarah from her high chair, trying to help her out. Brad Kane: Sarah, Jenny will be back. She just made a messy in her diaper and mommy had to go change it. You've made plenty of messy in your diaper in your short time on this world. So please, calm down baby, your sister will be back in a few moments smelling fresh and clean. He bounces Sarah up and down a little bit, the small child starting to calm down. It really helpss once she sees her mother and Jennifer coming back, smiles on their faces. Megan Kane: Someone doesn't have a poopie butt anymore does she? Jennifer giggles as she gets kissed by her mom, Sarah becoming jealous until Brad kisses her. Sarah Kane: Luv dada! Brad Kane: And I love you too Sarah. Back in your chair you go. Brad sets her back in the chair alongside Jenny in hers. Megan gives Brad a quick kiss before noticing something is completely wrong at the table. Something does seem a little bit off. Maybe its the fact that there are uneaten chicken nuggets on the table. Megan Kane: Hey, where are Lacey and Lilly at? After taking a quick look, Brad realizes while he was busy tending to Sarah, the other two went away on their own. He bangs his head on the table, a little bit frustrated, mostly with the fact that his daughters walked off in such a manner when they know for a fact they're not supposed to. At least he knows where the two are for an absolute fact. Brad Kane: I imagine they snuck off to the playplace while I was trying to calm Sarah down. I'll be back in a minute or two... He gets up, his sandwich hardly even showing that he's ate the smallest bite of it. Megan sighs, wondering why they choose to take the kids out when its so much easier having Brad go to pick up the food and bring it back. Always such a better idea. Megan Kane: You know girls, sometimes your daddy has the patience of a saint dealing with us, myself included. So many times he'll just nod his head and put up with what we do without really complaining or uttering a word in disgust. Such a great husband and daddy, isn't he? The twins smack their hands and nod their heads, agreeing with Megan's assesment. She smiles before taking a bite of a french fry. In the playplace, Brad looks for his oldest daughters, trying to keep his cool, not yelling at them for such disregard for what they've done. Lacey's already gotten lost in a store before so she knows the dangers of running off like this but did she learn? Nah, she's three, he should give her a break but Lilly is usually so well behaved. Ever since she's turned two, she has become something of a terror. Still, even as a terror, Lilly is enough to bring a smile to Brad's face. Brad Kane: Lacey, Lilly, please come out of the play center and finish eating. No answer but he does hear a few giggles coming from the ball pit. That gives away their location, Brad beginning to smirk. Time to play a really cruel joke. Brad Kane: Well if Lacey and Lilly aren't here, I guess I'll leave with mommy and their little sisters. Boy will everyone be sad that Lilly and Lacey ran away. Darn, just darn. Guess I'll give away all their toys and clothes. My life will be so empty without them. A giant fake sigh before he begins to walk away from the ballpit. Lilly and Lacey pop their heads up, seeing their dad walk away from them all sad, not picking up on the whole joke. Once they see him open the door to leave the playplace, that's when it really hits home that he's serious or at least how they took what he said. Lilly is the first to jump out of the ball pit, running after him. Lilly Kane: Don't leave me daddy! Taking the smirk off of his face, Brad turns around and looks surprised to see Lilly hug his leg. Brad Kane: Lilly, where did you come from?! Now Lacey jumps out of the pit and runs for him too. Brad kneels down and hugs both of them before looking at them directly in the eye. Brad Kane: Now both of you know better then to leave the table at any time. Doesn't matter if you think its alright because no one else is in here right now, you just don't do it. Mommy and daddy wouldn't know what to do or think if we really lost the two of you. You have to be around to help us with the babies. Both of you love your little sisters and brother right? They nod their head. Such a silly question to ask either of them. Brad Kane: Imagine how sad they'd be if their big sisters were gone all of a sudden. Who'd teach them the things that you know that mommy and daddy don't, huh? Both of you are our special helpers, okay? Once again they nod their head, feeling bad for what they did. At least they know the feeling of remorse already. Lilly has a couple of tears rolling down her face. Brad wipes them away before kissing her, then Lacey, on the forehead. Brad Kane: Now can we go finish eating please? Daddy has hardly had any of his lunch and he's very hungry after working out with mommy all morning in the bedroom. Megan spits out a little bit of her coke after hearing him say that to his children. She follows it up with a playful slap on the arm and a sense of disbelief that her husband would say something like that. Then again, she remembers, Lacey has seen one of her uncle's penis before. Boy was Brad angry when he found that one out. Megan Kane: Are you sure you want to say something like that? I think they might be able to figure it out. He simply dismisses it without a second thought. Lilly and Lacey giggle, not really knowing what's going on, just out the fact that their parents look to be having some fun. Brad Kane: Figure out what? We can do sit ups and what not in the bedroom. Not like I automatically assumed that right off hand. Now who has their mind in the gutter all the time? Hmm? She sticks her tongue before the two kiss. Lacey looks at them and is a little confused. The tone of his voice made it sound like a fight but why did they kiss. So confusing to a three year old's world. Lacey Kane: Why did mommy kiss you? Without taking even a second to think about it. Brad Kane: Because mommy loves me very, very much Lacey. Not to the point where she lets me work out with his little sister though... There's another slap on the arm, confusing Lacey that much more. She tries to shrug it off by eating a couple more bites of her nuggets but the thought of her dad and stepmom acting like this just doesn't seem to leave her mind what so ever. Lacey Kane: Why mommy hit you now? Brad Kane: Well, you'll have to ask mommy that yourself. I certainly can't speak for her all the time but it'd certainly help our phone bill every month if I did do all the talking for her. Yep, another slap on the arm followed by a laugh by Megan. Brad acts like it broke his arm. Lacey is still just as confused as ever now. Why would her parents act like this in public. She's always been told to never hit her little sisters or brother. If Lacey only knew the word hypocritical, she'd be able to use it at a time like this but her vocabulary is still small at this point in her life. Lacey Kane: Mommy hits you a lot and not get yelled at. Why do I get yelled at? Megan Kane: When I hit daddy, its not out of being mad. This is just a way that mommy shows daddy that she loves him and makes her laugh. Lacey opens her mouth but shuts it, going back to eating her lunch. Oh good lord, Lilly dumped ketchup all over her hair. Megan busts out laughing while Brad hangs his head down. Lilly is giggling right now, happy with what she just did. Hey, at least it wasn't gum. Brad Kane: Lillian, why did you do that? Lilly Kane: Cause... Brad shakes his head, already admitting defeat not feeling like going around and around with Lilly on this situation. Lilly grabs another ketchup cup from the table and throws it at her dad. Hit him right in the face. Lilly giggles a bit more, becoming happier by the second. This whole ordeal at McDonald's is starting to take its toll on Brad. His face is bright red and its not only due from the ketchup. He inhales, trying to calm himself but this time its not working. Brad Kane: Lilly, you are one step away from going out to that car and daddy laying down the law. Do you want that? A grin is on Lilly's face before she says something that no two year old has the business of ever saying. Lilly Kane: Fuck you! Brad stands up, livid as he pulls up Lilly from her booster seat and heads towards the door. Lacey's mouth is wide open before she covers it, shocked that her little sister would say something like that. Brad is in their minivan now with Lilly over his knee. Lilly's butt, meet Brad's right hand. She begins to cry but she had it coming. Back in McDonald's, Megan eats a fry and sighs. Why does every trip to a fast food place end up like this and how did Lilly exactly learn that phrase... Thank you Zak Warner. ::...Two hours later...:: Everyone is back home at the mansion, safe and sound. The girls are all taking naps. In the office of Brad and Megan, they sit there with a camera set up for what could be a promo in a few minutes but who exactly knows with their life between the sheets. Although since Megan is holding Ryan, I think its a good assumption that there will not be any love making today. Hold the cheers for later. Brad is sitting chair, looking a little upset at himself for losing his cool earlier in the day. Brad Kane: Lilly is probably going to hate me now because I spanked her. Megan Kane: But she had it coming. All part of being a good parent I think but damn Zak teaching her that kind of stuff. Why are we friends with him again? Brad shrugs his shoudlers, not exactly sure of that himself. If Brad smoked weed, well there would be a fantastic reason. Since he doesn't, who really knows. Well Brad knows the real reason Zak likes to hang around the two at times. A couple of the reasons are serving as a kind of pillow for Ryan right now. Brad smirks before glancing up into Megan's face. Brad Kane: Well I know for a fact he wants to put his johnson in you but that's neither here or now. I need to get my thoughts out there for a bit. Got a bit to say for once and I think people'll be a bit shocked to see it in a video form. Megan Kane: You have gotten too reliant on the audio only stuff. Plus its lazy. I mean who takes the easy way out of something like that? Brad and Megan look at the camera, shaking their heads. Megan gets up with Ryan still in her grasp and begins to press a button on the camera. That little red light is on, letting Brad know its time. Basically we're looking through the camera's POV now, only focusing on Brad, if that makes any sense. "So I've made into the finals of this tournament. I've beaten Blight and Jesus Cruz to get to this point now. I'd be lying if I said I wasn't shocked to make it this far, all the way to the finals. Not more then three months ago, I was sitting in this chair, thinking of what to do with my life. Wrestling had been such a big part of it because I was that whole life long fan before getting into the business and realizing the dream thing that you hear from everyone these days. Its a little lame, I realize, but in my high school days, I was a pretty good football player. I had a scholarship to a few small schools in Division I. Pretty much a free ride for a college education and I passed it all up. I passed up the chance to go to school and continue my learning. I wanted to take that scholarship so badly but I couldn't. I had a good reason why I couldn't do it though. At the time, I thought my first love was pregnant. Turned out she lied to keep me at home with her." A small chuckle escapes from his mouth. Not really past a woman to lie about something like that at times. "Sometimes I imagine my life if I had gone to college instead of staying back in Brooklyn. I think I wouldn't be here right now. I would've gone down a different road in life. I wouldn't be looking at my wife who is holding this camera in one hand and my son in the other. I wouldn't be wrestling I think. Probably be in New York running some sort of store instead of standing here inside of my office. Had my major planned to be in business because the business type classes were the only thing I really enjoyed in school. This is something I enjoy more though, that thrill, that rush that we always get. For quite a while, I didn't get that rush, that feeling of loving what I do. I hated this business and everything about it. I hated the traveling, I hated most of the people and I hated the fact that I wasn't able to stay at home on weekends. Getting my suitcase ready to fly out to who knows where to wrestle in front of people I didn't care about was taking a toll on me. I tried to hide it most of the time because I didn't want people to think that I was so bitter, so angry at this industry." Brad gets up from his chair and glances at the wall. It has a few photos of his career as you probably could imagine. He focuses on one of them. Some of the old GWC folks will know it, its when he won the World Title last year. "This picture right here, Kingdom Come II in May of 2008. The night that I climbed to the top of the mountain that means a bit to some of the people here at MWE. Three other men in this promotion have gotten that feeling that I felt on that night in May but I'm sure none of them felt the pain, the sorrow and the frustration just a mere two weeks later. Two weeks I was at the top of the mountain in that promotion. Then that world I had worked so long to build came crashing down around me like a muddy landslide. The moment I lost that World Title last year, I stopped caring about my passion for wrestling. It was all about that money I was making. I had a pregnant wife for most of the past two years. I basically have seven kids now with their futures to worry about. As time passed, people started to know it was about the money I made. I would leave places because I felt that I was getting underpaid. I know, I'm standing here in this giant mansion and I felt like I was getting unpaid." Another small chuckle from Brad. You can see him putting his hand over his eyes, leaning up against a wall. "This house with more rooms then I'll ever need along with my family and I felt like it still wasn't enough. All the greed came through me like a shot in the dark. In March I finally woke up to the fact that I needed out for a while, I needed to get out of wrestling to recharge myself and maybe love what I do again. Five months passed. Five of the greatest months of my life. I was able to stay home, play with my daughters, watch my son be born. The moment I saw Ryan though and I think I've said this before, I knew what I had to do again. I'll always be wrestling for my family but the reasons are just so different now. My kids need a role model and being greedy isn't something I want them to grow up with. I want them to see the respect I give to people, the honor and the love. This match next Monday is my biggest match since my return into the wrestling world. My match against CSB is something that I'm looking forward to and something I'm not looking forward to." The words stop for a moment, Brad lowering his hand and re-opening his eyes. His hazel eyes start into the camera, showing a mix of excitement and nervousness. "This tournament final is just, its something I don't know what to make of. Since August, I've just gone through mostly people that my peers know I should be able to handle. This is the first real test I've had to face in a long time and I don't know if I can handle it. This whole time I've been thinking what if I make an idiot of myself in the middle of that ring and lose in three minutes. What if I mistime something and I seriously hurt myself just to win this tournament and beat CSB. All these thoughts are plaguing my mind right now and I don't exactly know how well I'll be able to deal with it until Monday night. My mind has been racing, thinking of you CSB. Your problems with the law have me thinking of my own problems with my kids. I've never told anyone that when my first son was four, he was taken away from me for a couple of months because he fell out of bed and broke his arm. His mother blamed me for it and called it child abuse. I had to fight and scrape and claw to get him back from her. Looking back on it, I should've let her keep him at that point and forget about him. But I was able to get my son back and things kept on falling from there. I've heard of your legal problems thanks to Zak and it saddens me, it really does." Brad takes a moment to look back at his wall of pictures before turning back around. He walks to Megan and picks up Ryan from her. With Ryan cradled in his arms, Brad sits back down and looks at the camera. "You need to realize something though. Even if you manage to do well in your court room affairs, you have a life like this to manage. You have to mold, to shape a person as best as you can. Its hard, I'll be going through it for a long time, Right now my biggest wish is that my son turns out to be a wrestler, just like I am. I want him to look up to me when he gets to the age when he really realizes what is what. That's the wish of all fathers, or at least the good ones. You'll have this child to mold and I don't think you're quite ready for it yet. So many times I remember seeing you in GWC with Zak and De. The three of you were always doing something. I'd be lying if I said I never tried smoking weed. I'd be lying if I told people I didn't use to be an alcoholic. But now I do things in moderation. I only have a glass of wine with certain meals, that's about the extent of my party life. Being a parent means more responibilty and I don't know if you completely get that." Ryan looks at the camera, Brad smiling at him. "As much as I want you to be a good parent if you, as much as I respect you as a wrestler, there are a few truths that you might need to hear..." Megan stands up and takes Ryan before leaving the office. This leaves Brad alone, alone with his thoughts and a running camera. "I apologize if my tone takes a sudden turn to a bit more vicious but I feel like this needs to be said. I apologize if I go back into the history of GWC too much but this applies for once. You see, when the kingdom of GWC was at it's greatest, people like myself, Kevin Hardaway were the top level guys. We were the ones that Danny Vice banked on to carry the shows. Then you had the guys that were waiting in the wings that were former champions like Zak Warner, Joe Ragnal. Then there were people stuck in the middle of the pack, pissing away what they had. That person pissing his career away in the middle was you, CSB. You stayed in the middle of the pack. You stayed with the Ultimate Title for so long, going against lazy cunts like Creeping Death. What did you ever do that made people look up and take notice of you besides making jokes with Wayne. Sure you become the GWC World Champion but look at how it happened. I left, Kevin left, Sitar left, the Ragnals left and the Strikers left. Zak was too busy banging broads and getting high. Thunder was more concerned with god knows what. You were the only person who could've done it because there was no one else there to stop you! Do you think you would've been able to become GWC World Champion if everything wouldn't have happened? Do you think people would've cared if you've beat someone like Derek Sitar or Kevin Hardaway with that World Title on the line?" A simple shake of Brad's head, letting CSB know what he thinks without having to say a word. "You're a good wrestler, nearly a great one but I've never seen you beat someone at a "top" level. I have yet to see you do anything that makes me believe in CSB as a credible threat to something like this tournament final. I understand that you're ripping it here in MWE but I haven't seen a lot of it. Sure you beat Zak a couple of weeks back, everyone has an off night. You beat me on Monday, I'll stand up, look you dead center in the eye and shake your hand. I'll show you the respect if you beat me CSB. I'll believe in you as a guy who can get it done at the top of the show. This might be it for me. This might be my last chance at getting to that main event in my career again. My love of this business is back but I'm just not the wrestler I used to be. Beating you, CSB, gets me a shot again. I'm thirty and I feel like I'm fifty some days. My oldest daughter is going to be at Mayhem on Monday night to watch me wrestle this match. I want to put a smile on Lacey's face as she watches her daddy beat CSB in the middle of the ring. I want her to cheer for her daddy as he gets the cheers from the crowd because he won the Master of the Premises Tournament." He stands back up, looking at the wall of pictures again. "All these pictures are of my past. Who I used to be. A World Champion, a top guy but am I that now? Those five months away took a lot from me. No matter what happens on Monday Night Mayhem, CSB, one of us leaves at the number one contender for the World Title and the winner of this tournament. That'll be in the record books forever. What'll never be in the record books is what we do after we get done in the ring. I'll have to go to my other job. The job that never ends, not until the day life is done with me. People used to ask me what my job was and I'd always reply professional wrestler. I get asked that now and do you know what my answer? I'm a full time parent. This job means more to me then anything else in the world. But when I get into that ring, I turn it off. My parenting instinct will be turned off CSB, don't you worry. Just hope I'll be able to give you that Brad Kane of old." Brad walks over to the camera and presses the stop button. He yanks it off of the stand before walking over to his laptop where the wires to upload this to YouTube are already set to go. He plugs it in before sighing loudly, becoming a little sad that this is what his life has become. Wrestling is just a way to make his kids to look up to him like a hero. Megan stands in the door way with a smile on her beautiful face. Megan Kane: You really think you can win this one? He shakes his head, not really feeling it. Megan walks over and sits on his lap before giving him a kiss. Megan Kane: Why are you still wrestling then? Brad Kane: I don't know. Its just like a drug to me. I want to stop but I just can't. I don't know if I ever will either. I'm sorry. She wraps her arms around him as the video has finished uploading. She shuts his laptop lid before closing her eyes, nuzzling up to him. Megan Kane: It's alright. You'll always be the champion of my heart and the best in the world to the kids. Now lets go take a nap. Sleep in the middle of the day? Brad nods his head, yawning. Megan stands up and grabs his hand, the two walking out of his office. Being a wrestler is only temporary. Being a great husband and father lasts forever and its a job Brad will always love and never think of leaving.
