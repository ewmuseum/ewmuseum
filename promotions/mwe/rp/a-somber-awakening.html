I only want to hurt people, not kill them. Oblivion calms down after his long rant. He stretches out for a second, before he walks off the view of the camera. He is only gone for a minute however, has he is seen with his tank of gas, and a barbed wired two by four. Oblivion: This match will be hardcore. People’s faces will be messed up, and most likely, a career will end. Not mine though. I’ve lost thus far in MWE, I don’t plan on making that same mistake again. If absolutely necessary I will resort to lethal enforcement just to win, because I care about wining, not if people live or die. With the stakes so high, there is only a few things that I know must happen. One, I will prove to be a dominant force in the Money In The Bank Match. Two, I will establish myself as one lethal motherfucker who will show MWE just how violent a human being can be, and three….. Oblivion puts down his piece of wood, and walks up to the camera. He shifts it, to make it look at the wooden ladder. He walks up to it, and carefully starts to empty the gasoline onto the ladder, saving only a little bit. He then goes and picks up his barbed wired weapon, and smirked. Oblivion…..Set the world ON…..FIRE!!! Oblivion carefully dumps the rest of the gasoline onto the top part of the weapon, before he takes a lighter, and sets the barbed wire on fire. He then walks up to the ladder, and stretces his arm out, so that his flaming weapon touches the ladder, which sets it on fire. Very soon, the whole thing is lit up, in a spectacular sight to behold. Oblivion tosses away his weapon, before he takes a bucket of water, to put it out. Oblivion tosses away the bucket, before he focuses the camera on him and the flaming ladder. Oblivion: La verdad, la matanza, la sangre, la angustia, y atormenta. For those who don’t understand, that means truth, carnage, blood, agony, and torture, in Spanish. Words that describe me, and what I bring to the table. Renshaw, Petey, Phillips, Clark, Cruz, and Derf, I’m sorry, but you guys are going to be disappointed when you look up, and see the true winner of the match. CSB, well hopefully you’ll be unceremoniously wheeled out of the arena by the time, I take, what it rightfully mine. Money…In The Bank. Oblivion smirks one last time, as he moves out of sight. The camera is now solely focused on the burning ladder, which begins to crumble down, and collapse. From behind the camera, Oblivion adjusts the angle, so that we see nothing but all the pieces of the flaming ladder, before it turns to ashes. From behind the camera, Oblivion says one more thing. Oblivion: Ashes to ashes…..Dust to dust. FIN
