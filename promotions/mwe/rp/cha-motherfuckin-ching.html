CHA' MOTHERFUCKIN CHING!

Aug 16 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The scene begins to blur and focus into a dull setting on a cloudy, and stormy like day. It is seen that the camera is being filmed at a cemetery in Detroit, Michigan. A man is seen wearing a white suit with delicate black sun glasses and a bunch of flowers in one hand. He is standing in front of two tomb stones. He takes off his glasses and puts it in his pocket and turns to the camera. It is seen to be MWE Superstar Sonny Williams. He turns back to the tombstones as the camera begins to come in closer to the action. The tombstones are two of his dead relatives, one being his brother Jordan, the other being his father. The text on Jordan's tombstone is beginning to fade away as it already has on Sonny's fathers tombstone. It reads "They say the good die young, and are raised up to the heavens above. Jordan was a delightful young boy, his presence will forever be missed. RIP." Sonny leans down and puts the flowers next to both tombstones. Sonny turns to the camera as his face is filled with raw emotion. He takes a deep breath and looks down at the tombstones once more before forwarding off with the camera by side. Sonny notices this and stops just in front of a big marble cross in the middle of the cemetery. Sonny Williams: I have lived a life filled with absolutely everything that falls into the bad category. I've seen people I loved, I cherished killed right in front of me. I remember a lot of shit that went wrong. No I am not whining, or carrying on, my story is something to be heard. It's something you don't hear every day. To have lived the shit life I have thus far experienced, and to be told by someone who compared to my childhood lived it up pretty well, that I am a bitch, it sends the steam right up the kettle. *Sonny looks around the cemetery and behind him at the big cross. He looks around at all the tombstones with young deaths written on them and shakes his head. He turns back to the camera with a look of fury.* Sonny Williams: All my life, I've tried not to hate and to forgive people. Until now, I tried to love my mother even though what she had done to me and my life. But now, I see the lime light. I see all the shit she has caused me, all the shit that could of been avoided had she of been a good parent and kept us under her wing. I try not to hate, but when there is someone I come across so arrogant, obnoxious and self absorbed, all it does is switch the button of hatred. One man of all that I can not stand, CAN NOT STAND THE SIGHT OF, is Brian Michaels. *Sonny smirks and makes a small chuckle before directing himself back to the camera.* Sonny Williams: I had something to say about Brian earlier this week, but he is the type of guy when you say something or do something, he will re-form it to the Brian Michaels way and pile a whole lot of bull' fucking shit on top. He says I made him out to be a rich, popular young boy. I never once said that, all I said is that he was given a decent place to lie his head on and rest. He had parents who loved and cared for him, and I know, I know he probably takes that for granted. He is a man I do not like, he is a man I.. DESPISE. Sonny Williams: Brian, I always was a fan of wrestling but that isn't what got me into this business. My ability to fight is what brought me to this business. I have adapted a wrestlers formation of skills, and I am proud of where I stand today. But this week, I'm turning back to my old ways. I'm not going to pick your body high above my head and slam you down to the ground. As much as that would crumble your body to pieces, my punches will affect you much more. You see my punches leave lifetime pain, it won't just be a two day black eye, you will have internal bleeding, your retina will displace itself. There will be prices to pay for that when you have grandchildren they will ask you, Grandad, why is your eye always puffed up and half open? You can respond to them and say, I was hit by a train. Not just any train, it had a name, and it's name was Sonny Williams. *Sonny smirks again and scratches his head before looking at the camera in a collected manner. His face begins to fierce up once again.* Sonny Williams: You like to call me overrated, a hypocrite and a man that doesn't suit this intensity level. I do not overrate myself, I am as good as I say I am, and I'll tell you, I'm pretty fucking amazing, believe me. I have gotten here into this ring, and to a high standard in this business because of my pure talent. I'm not going to put you down and say you got nothing, cause even I, hating to say it, agree that you got something. So you've got talent. Bravo. But you will never have my respect or dignity. You say I have dropped down the bar, well if that's what you think I am going to put my hands on that ladder and I will climb to a new level. Brian, nobody has seen me like they will at Monday Night Mayhem. Sonny Williams: This match isn't about who is the better wrestler. I have already answered that six months ago. This match is about who can take the most punches and still be standing to be called victorious. I will be the better man, I have witnessed enough, taken enough punches in my life and I am telling you, you're punch compared some I have taken will mean nothing. *Sonny walks along the foot path as the camera follows, he comes to a park bench and sits on it looking down at the ground for a few moments. He takes a deep breath and looks at the camera.* Sonny Williams: I've said it before, but ten years on with no sight of Jordan, it has left a huge whole in my heart. I am still paying the effects of his departure to this very day. I was supposed to dedicate a win to him at Crowning Glory, and I fell two spots too short. I was confident of a win there, of being named the new MWE World Champion. If this match between me and Michaels were to be a wrestling match, I'd dedicate the win to him, but it's going to be a brawl. I can not guarantee a win, all I can guarantee is that Brian Michaels will be in agony. Blood will be shed, bones may be broken. *A beeping sound is heard as Sonny looks into his pocket as his pager is heard. He reads it and puts it back in his pocket and looks back to the camera.* Sonny Williams: I will leave on words that I want Brian to listen to, and listen to real good. All this intensity and hype up for this match, what I say, what you say, it will mean nothing come Monday Night Mayhem. I hate you like I have never hated anyone else in my entire life, you want me to hit you, I will and I will do it with a grin and a feeling of satisfaction on my face. *Sonny pulls out his black sun glasses from his white suit and puts them on once again. He gets out of his seat and looks straight into the camera.* Sonny Williams: For all the shit I have ever been through in my life.. Sonny Williams: For my brother.. Sonny Williams: For every person around the globe who agrees that you are a motherfucking arrogant snob.. Sonny Williams: It will be my pleasure to destroy you.. Sonny Williams: In every way, shape and form. *Sonny moves even closer to the camera now.* Sonny Williams: I hate you.. Sonny Williams: AND I AM GOING TO FUCK YOU UP! Sonny Williams:CHA MOTHERFUCKIN CHING!
