Victory Through Destruction

Sep 7 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The camera begins to blur out of it's black and blant image as it takes its time to fade into a actual picture. Eventually it comes to perfect picture where it is seen to be in the Conseco Fieldhouse in Indianapolis, Indiana. A huge structure is seen over the ring which is the brutal and deadly Steel Cage itself. The arena is dark but there is one spot light over the ring itself. The crowd is empty and there is no one seen but one figure in the middle of the ring. The camera zooms in as it is seen to be MWE Superstar and No.1 Contender for the MWE World Championship, Sonny Williams standing in the middle of the ring looking at the steel cage. Sonny is wearing long blue jeans with the left leg of the jeans only over the knee with a bandanna strapped holding it up with a grey Sonny Williams hoodie. He continues to look around at the structure that he will be taking part in later in the night. He slowly begins to bring his head back to preportion as he notices the camera is with him. He stares at the camera for a bit before opening his lips. Sonny Williams: This is where I will be later tonight, in the Main Event. I will be in a structure built to break bones, shed blood. As much as the physical pain this structure puts on the human body people tend to forget about what kind of emotional effect it has. I've seen men step into this position to later have their ass handed to them. Sure, they may be bleeding and tending to a few broken ribs but the emotional effect this structure can dish out, in some cases ultimates the physicality. People have said that I try to make others feel sorry for me for my past experiences. That is not the case. I have experienced a lot of emotional and physical pain in my life, this cage match, it doesn't even compare to some of the experiences I have had. In other words, the low down is, I'm not scared one bit. Sonny walks to one side of the ring looking at the steel cage above him. He goes to a turnbuckle and climbs to the top of it. He raises his arm in the air trying to reach the cage but it is too far off the ground to be touched by anyone. He jumps off and sits on the first rope in the turnbuckle. Sonny Williams: My opponent, he is a man, if that, who thinks that his disturbing acts and creepiness intimidates his opponent. As much as I disagree with almost everything he does surrounding his life, I am not intimidated by him. I mean c'mon, he's 190 pounds and barely six foot. Look at me on the other hand, I am a monster, a destroyer of all menace. I like to destruct things, objects and people, most of all people. My opponent, he is weird, insane and extremely strange. He's more annoying than ad breaks during a live coverage. His name is Jekyl, I guess his parents chucked a strange name to a strange kid. Sonny begins to chuckle as he pulls himself up from the turnbuckle and begins to walk around the ring once again before looking back at the camera ready to continue on. Sonny Williams: I have had some emotional, and fierce confrontations already in the brink of the new MWE. Go and ask Brian Michaels what it's like to mess with me, my family and my personal business. I dare you because you will learn the exact fate that he did. Maybe you don't watch much television Jekyl so why not just show you a re-cap of that exact night. All of a sudden the camera changes angle to the titantron which lights up showing footage from Monday Night Mayhem from three weeks ago when Sonny Williams faced off against Brian Michaels in a Singles Match. Their build up was very fierce and intense. ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ Sonny is still in the ring looking at Michaels at ringside in absolute fury. Michaels begins to become cocky and goes into the crowd milking his win over to the crowd. He goes over the barricade and starts celebrating his win with fans from Detroit, Michigan. Sonny's face begins to inflate with fury as Mr. Cooper is behind him with one hand in his pocket and the other on the shoulder of Sonny. Sonny is on his knees still not up from when Michaels rolled him up for the pin. He looks into his red bandana and pulls out a pair of brass knuckles. Ryan Adams:Is that brass knuckles!? Sonny has brass knuckles! David Ferrera: Yay! This is where it gets interesting. Sonny puts the brass knuckles on his right fist and smirks. Michaels is unaware of anything going on. Sonny gets up off his knees and rolls under the bottom rope as Mr. Cooper smiles on from inside the ring. Sonny walks monsterly to and over the barricade separating the ring from the fans. He looks for Michaels who is still celebrating with fans and unaware of anything. Sonny does a slow jog over and spots out Michaels. He is right behind him and taps him on the shoulder. Michaels turns around thinking it is a fan as Sonny connects with a punch to the face with brass knuckles. Ryan Adams: OH DEAR GOD! Michaels falls to the concrete ground as Sonny picks him up from the ground and holds him up continuing to lay a load of punches on him using the brass knuckles. He begins to bleed from his nose and forehead. Michaels looks out cold on the concrete as Sonny starts stomping on his face and stomach. Sonny picks up his body and puts it on his shoulder walking back to the ring. David Ferrera: Look at the power of Sonny. Sonny walks through the crowd with Michaels on his right shoulder in a bloody mess. Sonny walks over the barricade and he puts Michaels on the announcers table. He is bleeding heavily as Ryan Adams and David Ferrera get up out of their seats. Sonny looks under the ring for weapons. He pulls out a trash can and two steel chairs. He grabs one steel chair and puts it under the head of Michaels before grabbing the other steel chair. Mr. Cooper comes from the ring next to Sonny as he taunts the crowd for his client. Sonny looks around the ring and then up into the heavens above at his brother Jordan. He smirks and slams the steel chair on the head of Michaels who is bleeding even more. Ryan Adams: Ladies and gentleman, I don't know what to say, but what we're seeing is really... disturbing. Sonny smiles as the crowd begin to boo, which is quite strange as he is in his hometown. He continues smirking as he looks under the ring again for other weapons. He brings his head out and looks at Mr. Cooper giving him an evil smirk. Sonny pulls out a table as the crowd make another boo. He smirks and sets up the table in front of the commentary table. He picks Michaels off the commentary table and brings him around the front of the table he brought from under the ring. He puts Michael’s bloody head in between his legs ready to execute his finisher, the Sonny Slam. He smiles and points up into the sky to his brother, Jordan. He brings Michaels up and... POW! Sonny connects with a spine breaking Sonny Slam on the table which breaks it in half easily by the power of Sonny Williams. Ryan Adams: This is just right out disturbing. David Ferrera: Mmm... He did take it a bit far, I agree. Sonny begins to taunt the crowd as they boo him off and his manager Mr. Cooper. The MWE doctors and workers rush to ringside with a stretcher and other tools. They all rush down to see on Michaels as Sonny begins to make his way up the ramp laughing and smirking with Cooper doing the same beside him. They both stop as Mr. Cooper raises the arm of Sonny Williams high above his head. The medical team put a neck brace on the motionless body of Brian Michaels and begin to try and put him into a stretcher. This takes four men to pick up his slumped body and put it onto the wheeled stretcher. Sonny and Cooper are still standing on the ramp looking down at the bloody mess that Brian Michaels is. The medical team gets Michaels onto the wheeled stretcher and try to clean the table up so that Michaels can be carted off safely. All of a sudden Sonny runs down to ringside as the crowd boo on. He runs up to the stretcher, pushing medical workers away while doing so, and connects with a big boot that sends Brian Michaels flying back down to the floor. He gets on his knees on top of the slumped body of Michaels. He yells at ring announcer Fred Carry to pass him a microphone. He does so in a scared manner. Sonny puts his face and the microphone very close to Brian Michaels as he smirks. Sonny Williams: I told you this was coming. I TOLD YOU! NOBODY MESSES WITH SONNY WILLIAMS, NOBODY! Sonny drops the microphone and pushes the face of Michaels as he gets up from the ground and pushes away medical workers as he walks through them exiting the arena with a smirk on his face and the crowd booing him and his name. The camera blurs out as Monday Night Mayhem goes to a break with last scenes showing a bloody Brian Michaels in intensive care from MWE medical workers. ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ Sonny laughs to himself again. He tries to calm himself once but is unable to and breaks out into another laughter. He calms down and brings his attention to the camera once again. Sonny Williams: Jekyl, I saw your promo. Bravo, bravo. But there were many, many areas you could have been the smart bloke of the pack and got your facts right. You see, I may not be a very good man, the things I do are painful to others but I get a feeling that I like when I do it. BUT! One thing I can not stand is people who bullshit. You said that I lived the life of a gang banger? In instant message language 'WTF'. Are you so crazy and perverted to even picture that? I guess so. You probably brought that up cause you were harassed as a child by your parents. Maybe we should call up a doctor, maybe get a butt plug put into place for you. Sonny Williams: As much as that brings anger to me and pops the veins out from me in fury, the way you mentioned my brother of all things. I fight each fight in dedication to him. I don't do it to bring him back, he is in a place where it is much more peaceful, a place your face will never be shown. Even before Jordan passed away, I loved to wrestle. I still walk out each and every Monday with the same passion I had years ago. To see someone so fucked up like you walk into the spotlight and make a comment that is so insanely screwed up, I will not stand for one second of it. Maybe you never had a sibling, maybe you did, I don't give a damn. Sonny leaves the ring after looking up at the steel cage once again. He walks down the steel steps and up the ramp as the camera is still filming and moving backwards at the same rate. Sonny Williams: I am a man of destruction. I know where my limits are, but you, you are just plain motherfucking weird. Everything I have been through in my life, all the pain and agony I have experienced, JUST EVERYTHING BAD, all my emotions will be brought out on your skinny white ass. My brother, he died miserably something I wish he never had to have happen. I know he is looking down on me right now, and he is wishing me the best. There are people in my life I will never forgive, and after what I do to you Jekyl I am just hoping that some day God will come to terms and forgive me for what I do with you. Sonny continues walking as he goes into the backstage arena. The camera continues following Sonny as he is walking to his locker room. Sonny has a look of extreme anger on his face. Sonny Williams: The way you have made me feel, words can not describe how much I want to snap you in half like a toothpick. You want to be out-done by someone, well that someone is coming for you tonight. Just for facts, I have faced more elite men than you have killed people. You are insane, you belong behind steel bars. As much as I'd like to see your ass in jail, I can't exactly do that but I can get you behind steel and that is where this ends. You say you can tell a person when put in a deadly situation, well I can tell the fight of the tiger from the first punch. I warn you now, my first act in that ring will be a fist to the face. Sonny Williams: Now I see you are already preparing for Turmoil where it will be you, me and Whitman for the gold. I would start preparing for something different, something along the lines of a dental plan. You say you can guarantee blood from me. Bullshit. If so fucking prove it, you're nothing to me. You're going to be spending a lot of time in pain after tonight's fight. I am yet to win, so too are you. I will come out victorious, but it's gotta end with you looking like a bitch in front of.. everyone. Sonny comes to his locker room door as the door has a gold plate which reads 'Sonny Williams' on it. Sonny puts his head on the door and his hand on the door knob. He slowly turns back to the camera and looks into it with a look of fury still on his face. Sonny Williams: Jekyl, tonight all your nightmares will be coming true. You won't be sent down to hell to meet your idol, the devil, but you will be stretchered away from this arena in a cart. Everything I have gone through, all the shit in my life will be in pain of you. I will pray before I come out tonight, I will not pray for you. I will pray to God to someday forgive me for what I do to you, because I tell you now, every hit, every knock that you take will feel like a pile of bricks to the head. I need a win, and it starts here, it starts on you. From this day on I do dedicate my wrestling career to my brother, I always have. But from today, I will be more fierce, more brutal than any other MWE Superstar ever. Sonny Williams: I will climb that steel cage and get to the top and look down at the bloody, insane mess I made of you, and I will enjoy every moment of seeing you suffer. Christmas is coming early, not for you, but for me. CHA MOTHERFUCKIN' CHING! Sonny turns and opens his locker room door before slamming it closed as the camera begins to fade out as the promo ends.
