Oblivion

Before we begin this, there is a question that should be asked. If you were to get blood on your hands, in any way shape or form, how would you react to it. We aren't talking about your own hands bleeding, or treating wounds from another. No, when we say blood on your hands, it means causing death, or even permanently disabling somebody. The results are indeed different. There are those who are afraid of it, that they develop Hemaphobia, or the fear of blood. Others, they embrace it fully, becoming killing machines. But there is a third, and probable my dangerous of them all. This of course, means it mentally fucks with the person, resulting them going insane, and extremely dangerous. That could be considered the case for Oblivion. The masked madman of MWE. He would be one of the first to say just how messed up he truly was. That being said, there are some new recent developments that caused many people to turn their heads. If you lived under a rock during the previous edition of Monday Night Mayhem, you wouldn't know. But at the end of the night, Oblivion had attacked somebody. That somebody, was Zack Crash. Now, everyone thought it was just Oblivion, just trying to make himself bigger by attacking the guy who just defeated the world champion, Grand Mystique. But there is more. From what was said from Oblivion that night, one is certain that those two share a past, but nobody knows what it was. That being said, Oblivion had a smile on his face, one week after the whole event went down. He was driving around Chicago, after just picking up Heather from her latest job. Apparently, she was becoming a bit famous in the area Oblivion had her work. So much, that she was being worked every day, all day. And was well paid for. It wouldn't be long under Oblivion had driven them back to his warehouse. When they got off of his moped, he looked at her. It was a mix of demand, and lust. Knowing full well that if she didn't comply, she would get hurt. She handed him all the money she had made that day, and he greedily took it, carefully counting all the bills, making sure the amount was correct. He nodded when it was. Oblivion: I was right in taking advantage of your talents. You're quite the money maker in this profession. Heather looked at him with tears in her eyes. He smirked quickly, before slapping her once. The impact of the hit was enough to make her lose her balance, and fall onto the ground. She tried to get up, but by then, Oblivion had his foot on her chest. Oblivion: Do not try to hide who you truly are from me Heather. I know you well enough, that you enjoyed all the sex you've been getting from multiple men. A natural born slut. Heather: Please...get off. You're hurting me. Oblivion: I am getting off by hurting you. That's the whole point of this. Heather: Revenge? Why are you so obsessed with revenge? Oblivion: Hurting people who have wronged me ignites this fire withing my blackened soul. It makes me feel as if, the world around me is being burned by flames, that are hotter then the sun itself. Oblivion then did remove his foot from Heather's chest, before he helped her up. His touch to her, was gentle. Which surprised her. Forgetting that she had the shock collar on, she doubted he was as pure evil as he presented himself. That is when she was shocked. She fell forward due to the unexpected surprise of it. Oblivion caught her before she could hit the ground. When he did, he smiled. Oblivion: You would think that those thoughts would be cast from your mind right now. I think, on some subconscious level, you're enjoying all of this. This makes things even more interesting. Heather of course, was to damn tired to respond. She was barely keeping herself awake, as Oblivion dragged her into the warehouse. When he was in there, he put her on one of his chairs, and tied her up again. She promptly passed out. He sighed, gently running his fingers through her hazel colored hair. Oblivion: You're a tougher cookie then I had originally anticipated. I don't know what it is about you, but as angry as I am, part of me still wants you to be with me. You still make me think of these thoughts, over and over. But they aren't enough to stop me. Because in the end, you will be mine. After that, then perhaps you can come to appreciate the chaotic lifestyle I've come to enjoy. Oblivion turned away from her, heading to his own bed. He was tired himself. Even a madman needs his sleep. Next morning, when Heather woke up, she could hear the sounds of something being beaten. The usual 'Morning registration' when people become aware of their surroundings, Heather was in the familiar position of being tied up. She was more annoyed then scared at this. This shocked her again, due to the damn shock collar that had linked to her nerves. But if anything, this allowed her to become alert. She used what little strength she had, turning her around by bouncing the chair, to see Oblivion, dressed only in a pair of tight black, knee length tights, furiously punching a punching bag. Mask was still on, but you could see some tresses of black hair coming out from the back of it. For a moment, she forgotten all the physical and mental torture he inflicted upon her, and she ogled him. Just from his back, you could tell just how ripped and muscular he was. He wasn't looking like he was juiced up, but he looked built to be a fighter. It was then, that Oblivion had turned around, and spotted her ogling him. He smirked. This was a breakthrough for him, that she was dropping her guard. Oblivion: Like what you see? Heather quickly turned away, with a blush on her face. But she had a scowl on her face again, which got her shocked yet again. Oblivion chuckled to himself, just over the fact that Heather was the one who kept doing this to herself. Oblivion: Okay, I'm going to admit, this particular torture has proven more effective then I originally thought. Yet at the same time, you just can't help yourself. Puts a whole new spin on paying the price. Heather sighed, trying to ignore Oblivion and his constant taunting. She then felt a pair of hands rest on her shoulders, almost massaging them. She then felt the ropes restraining her loosen. Oblivion: Take a fucken shower. Please. Heather: Oh. So you actually think that you can trust me doing a simple thing as taking a shower? She sighed, as she walked over to him, and sat beside him. He didn't even turn to her though. Heather: If you don't mind me asking, what are you watching? Oblivion: I am just reviewing a few things. That's all. Especially since I do have a somewhat of a big match coming up. He smiled, as he saw a couple more minutes of the wrestling. Heather then got up, not interested in the wrestling matches that he had on. She paced around the room for a minute, before she asked something she had to ask, but might indeed regret. Heather: What is your problem with Zack Crash? What the fuck did he ever do to you? That got Oblivion's attention. And when their eyes met, she could feel a very cold shiver go down her spine, as Oblivion got up, and slowly walked towards Heather. She tried to move, but her body just wouldn't respond to her brain's commands. She knew it was to late when she felt a taped hand wrap around her neck, and began to squeeze on it. She banged her head a bit, almost knocking her out. Oblivion: YOU!!! You...You don't...you don't even know the half of it. If there is one person, one person who might deserve equal punishment as you do, it's him! Last week, was only a small taste for what I have in mind. Last week.....I ruined his celebration, for the biggest victory of his career. A victory, he would not have had, had I not interfered. But I want more then just costing him a moment. For him...I want that title of his. I want him, to go through my personal hell. And ultimately, I want to take away something that I know he values a lot. His ability, to walk. He took Heather with him, showing her off to the various gang members there. And they all looked at her with lust. Eventually, one bald black guy approached Oblivion, with a few of his buddies. Rest assured though, I don't want to see any bloodshed here. I'm just looking for this address. Oblivion then took out a piece of paper, and showed it to the bald guy, who smirked. Being that he was a bit taller then Oblivion, he felt he could easily take him if he wanted to. Bald Black guy: Oh, and why should I tell you how to get there?
