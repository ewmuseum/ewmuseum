There is an ego called competition

Dec 15 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It’s complete silence, nothing but black is seen. It lingers for a little while and after a few more moments of nothingness, an arrogant voice is heard. ???:GET OFF ME I’M FINE! Suddenly noise booms in, phones ringing, people muttering, beeping and buzzing sounds of equipment is heard as well as the rolling and screeching of small wheels across a solid hall. The camera fades in from blackness and shows Sinc being treated by a doctor as he sits on the side of a nursing bed. His feet dangling. Sinc has a large bandage on the right side of his forehead as the doctor fiddles with it making sure its secure. Sinc is shirtless and wearing gray sweat pants and black, low cut socks. Sinc: Dammit I said GET OFF ME! Sinc slaps away the eager doctor’s hand, the doc is wearing the usual white coat, slacks, black work shoes, and a buttoned shirt. His hair a buzz cut, and brown. He is middle aged and looks somewhat like a young Robert Deniro. Except, without the mole, and the weird glint in his eye, and the kind of odd smirk that he does, but you get the picture. Doctor: I need to make sure your in good shape for your next match, I mean after all you suffered a minor concussion. That man must have really tore you down. Sinc: Yea well….let’s just say I wasn’t in the right state of mind….too much crap fluttering around in my magnificent brain it caused some leakage. Doctor: Well I’ll be right back, I got some paper work for you to sign before you leave. You seem ok now. Sinc: Whatever Doc get outta my sight. The doctor leaves closing the door in a loud clang as Sinc is left once again in silence. He adjusts himself and begins laying down on the doctor’s bed. He frowns and looks at the ceiling, as if nothing else matters but that damn ceiling….that taunting, ginormous, ceiling! Sinc: I freakin hate hospitals. Sinc begins folding his broad arms across his shirtless chest as he continues laying in the bed in thought. The camera gets a little too close as it hovers over Sinc like a reaper. Sinc: Oh please....what...WHAT WHAT WHAT WHAT- Sinc sits back up and sticks his gaping face into the camera lens with a diminished expression upon his face. Sinc: WHAT! The camera staggers back in a bit of shakiness as Sinc is very upset from not just his loss, but the fact that he has a very deep feeling he is going to lose, the feeling you would get if you knew your friend or family member had stole something dear to you. In this case, Sinc's pride. Sinc: I don't know what you want me to say....that I lost? Fine, I admit defeat congradugoddamnlations Resoan.....you managed to beat a man who has made an ass of himself in a field. But you know what? Your right, I don't care much for my fans anymore, I don't care if I go into any mental rage anymore. Because I realized something....yea I realized something. Giving the people what they want just isn't gonna win matches, it just isn't...isn't GOOD enough anymore. Sinc takes a minute to think for a second, looks at the ceiling again, and flips it off with a middle finger. He looks back at the camera with a big grin. Sinc: The only thing that matters now is me. ME,ME,ME! Those people....those sweaty, party loving, man boob flopping, SONS A SOMETHINS.....don't help me. Ya see they just seem to....bring me down. Down to a level I had only hoped I would never go....It's like uh....it's like I am on a ship at sea....suddenly a big old ball of joy named Mason Resoan boards it with some I dunno....worn down lasso wieldin rope and like some type of maniac pirate takes a mighty pink sword and stabs me with it, he cuts and he cuts until he see's the insides, the pain, the mush, the goo. Until he see's the inside of my very painful soul. But my question is what were you more focused on? The pain and the guts....or the colorful sword that would stick out the most.....it all matters how you think and what you interpret, much like the difference between someone who thinks for himself....and someone who cares what others think of THEM. The sword is simply a distraction of life, something that looks all nice and pretty but in the end can be a very deadly thing. But it can’t cut through everything, a sword is only good for one thing, but a heart, a desire, and a passion goes where the person wants it. Sinc maneuvers off the side of the bed after he sits up and now begins moving around uncomfortably like he did last week. He begins rubbing his neck and shuffling across the floor trying hard not to say the wrong thing. He doesn't want to flub. Sinc: What I am trying to say Mason is that you can't always rely on those who can't see you for who you really are, those who can’t really help. But can only cheer for you, and boost your morality only for you to be upset in the end. Sure they are my fans...they are what makes me happy, but happiness can only get you so far. Mason Call me stupid, call me arrogant, call me loud, and for the sake of my manhood and well....Mason's small ego, even call me contradictory, but for sh*t sake's don't try and say I have a fake passion. Mason, I have more passion in your mom's famous MILF salad than you do after trying to promote yourself like you were Dustin Diamond at a b*tch-your-way to the top book sale. Sinc stops pacing and sits back down on the side of the bed smugging and looks at a random corner of the room trying to take in everything Mason has told him, and everything that he had just, and previously told himself, from now and last week. Sinc: You WERE a champion Mason, keyword WERE. But you let your ego and your love for Canadian foreign soap operas with the lead singer of Green Day and Ce’line Dion get in your way. You lost what was important to you, so you resort to your lame Indie feds, you resort to your suckie boasts about beating a bunch of Englishmen only to lose to some cherry picker. Then you come back to try and regain your pointless confidence. Mason….get over yourself. Your not great, your not good, your just ahead of yourself. YOU WERE great, there’s that word again, sue me! Anyway, you were great but you got caught up in the wreckage of the first fall of MWE, forgot what truly mattered, and fell under pressure. Now here you are trying to climb the ranks again and try to go after who? ME! Your trying to go after MY ASS and blame ME for all the people who never gave you hugs when you were shedding tears over how sad your life is because your not yet in the main event scene. You sit on your cheap wooden stool and begin licking your own tears and screaming, “WHY OH WHY!? WHAT HAVE I DONE? I JUST WANT TO BE JAIL BAIT FOR ALL THOSE BUSH LOVERS!” Pucker up and keep kissing ass because your gonna need all the ammo you can get when it comes to me, shape up or ship out, or else you’ll underestimate the power of THE ARROGANT…… Sinc pauses for a brief moment and makes a balled fist as he eyes the ground. Sinc: ARROGANT…..AVIATORRRRRRRRR!!!!!! Sincs smiles as he relaxes himself and begins kicking his feet in the air playfully in boredom. Sinc: I’m not a saint….I’m not a hero to anyone…I am one person trying to make a damn living. Just like you, I know damn well a man like yourself wouldn’t wrestle for fun, you want the money, the fame, the fortune. You don’t just wanna entertain people, you wanna gloat and boast like you own the world, the whole world on your draft dodging fingertips. You know what Resoan….you can make fun of me making fun of YOU and nit pick my words to death like you were Simon Cowell at the kiddie corall. Sinc then puts his hand on his bandaged head and shifts off of the bed as he now sits in a nearby chair looming beside him, he rubs his head a bit as he takes a piece of gum from a pack he has. It is laying on a small table directly right of his bed and he then begins to unwrap the piece, and tosses the wrapper in a ball behind his head, and begins chewing the gum. Sinc: But honestly I don’t think you did a very good job trying to insult me Resoan. You missed QUITE a few things. My dad was an alcoholic, my brother is severely depressed, I get drunk off my ass when I am not being f*cking recorded all the time, I try to support my mother with whatever money I make, oh yea and I think I banged Sarah Whitaker once before in a one night stand at Applebee’s. But hey, I am sure you already knew that. Don’t go to the back seat rows, that’s where we made a mess. Sinc grins widely and looks slowly, and passionately at the camera. Sinc: Applebee’s…..eating GOOOOOOOOOD….in the neighborhood. Sinc makes a kissing sound then goes back into his arrogant mode. He continues sitting in the chair, this time twiddling his thumbs as he is eying the room in a nervous gesture and swirling the piece of gum around in his mouth thinking whether he should continue his dialogue. He nods and continues. Sinc: Ok honestly….this is honest here…don’t get your non violent “fans” on my ass for this, but why wouldn’t I make fun of Canadians, their annoying to listen to, they b*tch and whine about politics and America when they need to worry about their own country, they think they are smarter than every person in America combined, when in the end all we have to do is throw you a draft form and put a gun to your heads and you’d surrender. Seriously, why NOT Mason, why not make fun of you and your kind, I could give a crap if I suddenly get a knock on my door and a “HEY! What the hell were you talking aboot when you said you didn’t like us, EH?” What do you want me to do about that Mason, I’M THE ARROGANT AVIATOR FOR CHRIST SAKES! I’m not the peace and freedom freakin party here! If I was, you certainly wouldn’t be in it Resoan! Besides, I think your really the only Canadian I hate at the moment. NICE JOB NUMB NUTS! Your now on my SH*T LIST! Sinc begins blowing bubbles with his gum as he taps his feet on the ground feeling quite distraught while sitting down. He stands up and begins walking around the room again and is doing anything but staying still. Sinc: You don’t have a slightest clue who I am and what I am about. You only know the on camera funny guy don’t ya? The Jokester, the mic man, the loud mouthed spikey haired hoodlum! Are you too SCARED? Are you too AFRAID to continue ranting and raving about me and my life Resoan? Because if you’re a stalker than stalk me dammit, dig deeper, pretend like I’m your mother’s uterus and dig into that powdery bag of dirt like no tomorrow! Because if your not done with me yet, I would love to see you try and attack me more, it only gets me riled up Mason. You know this by now ya damn pretty boy! Sinc swipes his hand across a food tray placed in front of his bed and shuffles away all the food that was on it as plastic forks and empty foam bowls fly across the room. He rubs his neck again and paces faster around the once quiet room. Sinc: I know one god damn thing you don’t have you yellow bellied Sinc looks into the air and raises his voice as if YELLING TO GOD HIMSELF! Sinc: SACK….O…CRAP!!!!!! Sinc then falls to his knees and reaches under his bed, he pulls out his US championship. He swoops it up from the ground and slings it over his right shoulder as he stands. Sinc: I don’t think you have EVER won this bad boy….this is what you want isn’t it? TO BE….A US….CHAMPION!!!!!!!!!!!! Sinc goes to his knees again and pretends he is holding a microphone. Sinc: CHAMPIONNNNN!!!!!!!!!!!! Sinc stands back up once more and smiles as he looks at the glimmer of the title twinkling in his eyes as if it is sending him a message of dominance and destruction. Sinc: Ok yea…yea you have been a tag champ, so what, and yes, even an MWE champion, but you see….you seemed to skip this title right here. I have made this title more prestigious than it ever has been before. This title is what defines me, wearing something like this so PROUDLY is what makes me strive in the MWE. I know what your gonna say, “A guy like you can’t raise that title to any prestige, because you suck, etc etc blah blah whatever” Well our last match maybe I didn’t bring my entire mindset with me, maybe I did go a little overboard at a former MWE champ, but I know I didn’t dare lose confidence in myself and I damn well won’t now. Sinc places the title around his waist and clips it on as he stands now with his hands sternly on his hips. Sinc: Ya know people have been asking me….Sinc….why were you so mean last week Sinc? Why were you so brutal? Why would you set that helpless innocent field on fire? And you know what I told them? I told them “Because I want to make a success out of myself.” Because you were right Mason, I shouldn’t need anyone to save me, not the people, not any wrestler, nobody. But I do know I will do what it takes to retain this title around my waist at this moment. And if I have to form an alliance or smash some little helpless kid’s dreams I will. Whatever it takes I’m willing. But they also say to me “You don’t need anyone to save you Sinc, you don’t need anyone!” I don’t NEED anyone they are correct, but I do need to do what’s best for me. Because I just can’t be that funny guy to everyone the way I used to be anymore. Because its hard to be funny, when everything you've strived for... has been taken away from you. My father, he was everything I strived for, and if I lose this title and after losing my previous girlfriend….yea…that’s about almost everything right there. And until you understand that Rat Sh*t Resoan, you won’t understand why I have been acting the way I have. And if I am acting childish MY APOLOGIES….unlike you I like to have a little fun every now and then while you probably sit at home reading Reader’s Digest and watching Degrassi! Sinc disconnects his title from his waist and tosses it on the bed glancing at it a little then looking back at the camera. Sinc: There is one…last…thing I’d like to touch on here. You are the Canadian classic huh….do you even KNOW where the origin CLASSIC comes from? It means to serve in the highest rank of class. To show authority, Serving as the established model or standard, and finally….having lasting significance or worth….by the way if you want to know how I know that, it’s called leap frog, I stole it from Mason’s locker room. Sinc laughs to himself somewhat as he moves his arms around and cracks his neck continuing his statement. Sinc: Anyway….from what I’ve seen you certainly don’t belong into any high rank, your not an authority of any kind except of your masturbation sock when you smile at that shining picture of Copane every night, MY GOD….WHAT A MAD MAN….HE SAID MASTURBATION? OH DAMMIT ALL! Anyway, your not established and well….you CERTAINLY aren’t a model….and you definitely don’t have a lasting significance and aren’t worth a DAMN thing to me other than a victory. So you see….you can TRY and bring me down, but I am just going to get back up and annoy your sorry ass even more. By the way if I still have any “ring rust” to you or anything or any confusion about what “side” I’m choosing once again I’m sorry I can’t pleasure your babbling behind. Why don’t you go the DC route and try making a porno with that two cent bimbo of yours, maybe you’ll get somewhere, make the money I am sure you so desperately crave, and make it become a local hit huh big guy? The doctor that was checking on Sinc earlier enters the room as Sinc still talks to the camera. The doctor taps Sinc on the shoulder, Sinc turns around raising a brow and then gives him a few forms to sign. Doctor: Here you go just sign at the very bottom and- Sinc: I KNOW JACKASS! Did anyone come by for me yet? Doctor: Yes you have a ride waiting for you outside but- Sinc: CYA LATER! Sinc throws the papers up in the air everywhere. He grabs a white robe from a wardrobe closet near the entrance to his treating room and slides it on around his upper body, then fastens it around his waist. He then see’s his pair of black Nike sneakers in front of the wardrobe and slips them on. He looks at the doctor in disgust and discontent. Sinc: Don’t wait up Doc. Sinc rushes to his bedside and grabs his belt back as he throws it over his broad right shoulder once again. Sinc then opens the door and storms down the hospital hallway as nurses and other doctor’s and assistants move out of his way and they all look at him in curiosity. Sinc manages to get to the front reception desk as a nurse tries to stop him with words. She has brown bunned hair and white wrinkled skin. She looks in her late 40's, she is wearing a white casual nurse's outfit with a long white dress to top it off. Her eyes are like seeing the wicked witch of the west for the first time. And hairs were sprouting out of her ears such as that of a forest. But it was the atrocious hair on her lip that caught Sinc's full attention. Nurse: Sir did you fill out the paper work? Sinc stops and looks at her as he shoves a finger in her face. Sinc: Listen here Polly Pocket, have you ever thought about getting that hairy snake shaved off your ugly, shriveled, lip? The nurse feels the top of her lip as she realizes what Sinc is talking about. She makes an embarrassed expression and looks away letting Sinc leave as he makes his way to the doors and out of the hospital. He continues on as he pushes two security guards out of his way and they ignore him in anger. Sinc: Thank you for releasing me from this HELL HOLE! Sinc is finally outside as fresh night air envelopes his senses. A black Jetta is seen in front of the hospital with the ignition turned on with a female driver in it. The camera zooms in on her face. It is Kendra, Sinc’s ex, just as beautiful as ever. Kendra gets out of the car smiling and walks up to Sinc giving him a hug. She then looks to her right at the camera and acts a bit startled. Kendra: Your very welcome. Are they gonna follow us home too? Sinc looks at the camera and raises a hand at it as if trying to smack it, the camera backs away. Sinc looks back at Kendra impatiently. Sinc: Ignore them. Hard Rock Café again? Kendra laughs as she smiles at Sinc in a warm manner and hugs him tighter. Kendra: Sure… Sinc and Kendra stop hugging each other and Sinc claps his hands once and rubs them together. Sinc: Then let’s ride baby! Kendra chuckles and releases from Sinc’s grasp after the hug and walks to the driver’s door. Kendra: I’m drivin….your payin. End of story tough guy. She says this winking at Sinc and Sinc winks back with a smile. Sinc: Fine with me babe. Oh yes and to those people that are rooting for that stuck up smartass Resoan, I have a new phrase for ya “peeps”. Sinc looks at the camera, once again positioning his arms as if he is grabbing a mic and begins to shout; Sinc: Your going to EAT MY WORDSSSS…….while I INFLICT….THE…PAAAAAAAAAINNNNN!!!!! Kendra begins twirling her hair as she looks at Sinc with interest and blushes. Kendra is wearing a short black dress and silver necklace. Her earrings dangle gracefully from her ears, two silver star earrings glowing in the wet night as a full moon sits in the sky. Her hair is long and loose as she flips it in the air and enters back inside the car. Sinc follows entering the car with her on the passenger side as they both give each other a quick kiss on the lips happily. Kendra: You sure know how to promote yourself big guy, but you always knew I liked your arrogance didn’t you? Now let’s get outta here before they get too much footage of us for Christ sakes. Sinc nods his head and begins rubbing her right leg with an intrigued expression on his daunting face. Sinc: And you sure know how to get me….you know…I can’t say it with these cameras floating around, oh screw it. Sinc eyes the camera in annoyance and discomfort. Sinc: This girl is a HOT fox, and yes, she gets me very MOTIVATED, make sure Mason hears this, because I want to challenge him to who can give their lady a bigger ego, Mason and his syphilis machine or me and my Motivation! BET ON IT! Kendra: Sinc! Kendra slaps Sinc in the face lightly as Sinc rubs his cheek while gazing at her. She blushes again. Sinc: Ow…just wanted to show you off like you were my own new toy….damn. They both share an odd, awkward silence for a few moments and Sinc sighs shaking his head. Sinc: Fine fine fine….I’ll get them outta here. Sinc looks out his window seeing the camera trying to get closer, but Sinc reacts in a negative way and pushes the camera man away from the car and spits his gum in front of his shoes. Sinc: Sorry buddy, go get your pleasure pusher somewhere else, And if you didn’t know…now ya- Kendra hit’s the gas hard as the car jets off in a smoking screech as the traffic light ahead turns green and a path of smoke erupts from the back of the Jetta. Sinc finishes his sentence in a gasping yell. Sinc: KNOWWWWWWWW WOAH WOAH WOAH! BABE SLOW YOUR ROLE! DAMN! Sinc’s voice fades as the car drives off in a fast rapid succession as the camera fades with it’s leave.
