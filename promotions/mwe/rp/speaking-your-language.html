Speaking your language; Insurance RP

Aug 8 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The scene opens up with a two children play fighting. ???: Your goin DOOWWN! ???: Not EVEN Aeron! Aeron: Wanna BET Dusty?! Aeron grabs the other little boy now known as Dusty. He does a mini suplex and pins him. Aeron: ONE, TWO, THREE! DING DING DING Dusty: You counted to fast! Aeron: Nuh unh! Dusty: Yuh huh! The two bicker with eachother for about 10 minutes before Dusty's dad showed up in his truck. Dusty's Father: Come on Dusty, Its time to go home. Dusty: Aww. Do I have to? Dusty looked at his dad puppy-dog eyed hoping to stay a little longer. Dusty's Father: Cant son, how bout we get some ice cream on the way home. Dusty: Ice cream! Oh boy! The little child runs to his fathers truck and tries to open the door. Dusty: A little help dad? Dusty's father chuckles as he opens the door. Dusty jumps in and buckles his seatbelt. Dusty: Bye Aeron! Aeron: See ya later Dusty! Aeron stands in the front yard looking around. Aeron: And still the champion AERON BEOWULF! He imitates the crowd in the background as the camera spins around Aeron. It does a full 360 and about 180 degrees the scene changes with the actual crowd roaring in the background. Aeron standing in the middle of the ring with the X-TV title in his hands. Aeron looks into the camera. Aeron: I stand here today to tell you all something. The Berzerker is here to STAY! I was in GWC for a little over 5 months, I stopped wrestling for a a good year, by then I had grown some hair, So I styled it and went into APWC under a new persona and a new name. I was in APWC under Lukas Phantom, Why change my name? New persona new name, Lukas, my father had always encouraged me in wrestling so I decided to dedicate the carrer in APWC to him. Why phantom you may ask? I dissappear in the fog. APWC had been in talks of merging. While they were talking about this I was on a week vacation, I had came back and was told about this, I dissappeared again until last week where I signed up for MWE, and you know what? I am here to STAY I aint moving on I'm going to show them that my place is HERE that the champ is here to stay and he aint going no where. He sighs and licks his lips as he signals for a can of beer, a tall boy tossed to him from ringside. He opens it and starts to sip it. Aeron: Anyway more on topic of the match, J.K. Valentino, You GOT to be kidding me. He sounds like a guy you'd see in one of those mushy chick flicks, you know the ones where the guy loves the girl but the girl doesnt know it and visa versa that kinda stuff. And instead you find out hes a rapper? This guys a joke. You say your a rapper, and a DJ? So you spit the mic while turnin the turntables? Well I got something to say, or better yet. Let me say it in a way you might actually understand it. I stand here in this ring today, Well listen up cause theres somethin I gotta say, You keep on with these lies, You cant compromise, Dealin with the fact, That you gotta act. You cant just sit there, in yo underwear, So listen closely and listen good, GET THE FUCK OUT OF MY NEIGHBOURHOOD. PEACE. Aeron chugs the last of his beer as he crushes it against his head, he holds his arms up in triumph before stepping inbetween the ropes and walking out.
