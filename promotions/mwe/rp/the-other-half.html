The other half.

Aug 13 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Aeron blinks a few times as he sits in a steel chair with a bright light shining on his face and two cops standing on the other side of a table. Police officer 1: So, Your names Aeron. Respond. Aeron: Yes, and yo- Police officer 2: Just answer the questions we dont need no back talk from you buddy. Police officer 1: Is it true that you know Jekyl. Respond. Aeron: Yes, listen can I just tell you the whole story? The two police officers looked at eachother as they walked back whispering to eachother. Shortly after they return and look at him. Police officer 2: Go ahead. Aeron sighed as he licked his lips. Aeron: Can I get a glass of water before I start? The first police officer waves to the two way mirror. One of the female police officers comes in and places a glass and a jug of water beside it. Aeron pours himself some and takes a mouthful before starting to talk. Aeron: Alright it started like this. The scene goes into a flashback-type scenario. Aeron sitting on the bed in the hotel room watching TV. Aeron heres a knock at the door so he slumps over to get it. He looks through the eyehole to be met with a disfigured smile. Aeron (Mumbling): Ah its Jekyl. He opens the door as Jekyl looks at him. Jekyl: Why helloo there buuuddy. Jekyl snickers like an evil clown. Aeron: Nothin much. Jekyl: Lets go before we miss the bus. Aeron: Kay, thanks for waking me up. Jekyl nods as Aeron closes the door behind him. Jekyl and Aeron step in the elevator as Aeron presses the ground floor button. Jekyl seeing another patron in the elevator with them. The first feeling of it going down, weightless, almost made the two jump. Jekyl looks at the man thats trembling and sweating in fear of him. Jekyl smiles and pulls out a knife and looks at the man. Jekyl: Tragic things those elevator murders eh? The man takes a large gulp of air. The scene switches back to Aeron in the police station. He takes another large gulp of his drink. Police officer 1: Look at him hes sweating! Hes lyin I tells ya! Lyin! Aeron: Or maybe its this giant lamp you have shining on me? The police officer looks at him and slaps him across the face with the back of his hand. The other officer restrains him. Police officer 2: Stop maybe hes tellin the truth, lets see where he goes with this. Aeron: Thank you!...So anyways... The scene switches back to the elevator with Aeron on one side and Jekyl tormenting the man on the other. The elevator dings as it opens about two floors down as the door opens up, the man runs out panting and stumbling down the hall. Jekyl: RUN PIGGY RUN! Jekyl roars in maniacal laughter. Aeron nudges Jekyls arm. Aeron: Leave the poor guy alone, people are gunna think we're crazy. Jekyl raises his finger as he starts to open his mouth. Aeron: DONT, remark on that. Jekyl chuckles as they reach the ground floor. They walk out the door as the bus leaves without them. Aeron: God damnit, Jekyl smiles as his looks around and points to a car dealership. Jekyl: How bout we go take a car for a, "Test drive". Aeron shrugs. Aeron: Sure, this should be fun. The scene shows the officer still restraining the other. Police officer 1: So openly admitting about stealing a car!? Aeron: That was to prove to you I ai'nt lying. Aeron finishes his glass as he starts to pour another while the scene switches back. The two men walk across the street, cars honking at them. Jekyl taunts at them dancing and flipping them off. Aeron grabs Jekyls arm and pulls him across the road. Jekyl with the look of a dissapointed child on his face. Aeron and Jekyl are looking at the cars as a dealer runs towards them. Dealer: Can I intrest you fellas in anything? Jekyl points to a small smart car. Aeron looks at Jekyl evily. Aeron: I am not fitting into that clown car. Jekyl: No not that you idiot behind it. The three walk around the smart car slowly revealing a blue Mustang GTO 2009. The dealer puts a large smile on his face. Dealer: Nice choice fellas, so will that be cash or cheque? Jekyl: Can we take it for a test drive first? Dealer: Suure! Jekyl hops in the front seat while the dealer hands him the keys. Aeron closes the door to the passengers seat after he buckles his seat belt. As The dealer attempts to open the back door Jekyl laughs and locks the door, he rolls down his window. Jekyl: That was the test, now watch me drive! The tires screech as Jekyl takes off. Aeron: Yee haw! The two cruise interstate 94 as Aerons stomach growls. Aeron: Hey you wanna grab something to eat? Jekyl: Suuuuuure just gotta pick up some money. Jekyl pulls onto the curb of the bank as they close the doors to the car, Jekyl and Aeron walk in. Jekyl stands in line as frightned people let him ahead. Aeron waited at the front door. Aeron takes another drink of water. Police officer 1: So we have confirmation that he robbed a bank. Aeron: Hey, I didnt say anything about that, but yeah I didnt see much he just stood there for a couple of minutes, without even moving. The only thing I could think of was my hunger. I was wondering if we could pass Jekyl off as Ronald McDonald and score a free meal. One of the officers chuckle as the other hits him on the chest. Aeron: Then all I seen was Jekyl run out with a sack of something and I wasnt about to get ditched twice in a day so I ran with him. Aeron takes another gulp as the scene fades to black then to white as Aeron and Jekyl are driving. Aeron: Dude are you crazy! Robbing a bank in the middle of the day!? Jekyl: You should know me by now. Aeron shrugs as he furls his eyebrow. Aeron: Good point. After about half an hour of driving they pull into a small plaza by Battle Creek. They pull into a McDonalds drive through. Orderer: Welcome to McDonalds how may we help you. Jekyl: I'll have two McChickens extra McChicken sauce please. Aeron: And a double Big Mac. Orderer: Will that be all? Jekyl: Did I say that would be all? I want two large cokes. Orderer: Thank you, Your order is $14.57 please pull up to the next window. Jekyl drives and lays a $20 dollar bill that he had taken from the bag of money he had stolen from the bank on the silver window sill. Orderer: Thank you, pull up to the next window to recieve your order. Jekyl sighs as he pulls up. Jekyl: Why cant they just make you pay and give you your food at the same damn window? Aeron shrugs as the lady hands Jekyl their order. Server: Thank you and have a nice day. Jekyl: Bite me. Aeron laughs as he grabs his big mac and starts eating it. Taking breaks inbetween bites to drink. Jekyl: Damn. They call me a pig. Aeron: What? I was hungry. Jekyl: I can tell. Jekyl laughs as Aeron continues eating. After about another hour of driving they finally make it to Detroit. Aeron: Hey, just drop me off at a hotel. Jekyl: Sure. Aeron finishes off his fries as Jekyl drops him off. Aeron waves good bye as he walks into the hotel. He walks to the front desk. Waitress: Hello, how may I help you. Aeron: Yes I have a reservation from the MWE under the name Aeron Beowulf? The waitress punches in his name and smiles as she hands him a pass key. Waitress: Enjoy your stay. Aeron smiles and takes the card as he walks to the elevator. Aeron: Best service I've had all day. Aeron sighs. Aeron: Man that Jekyl is one helluva guy. The elevator door opens as he walks down the hall looking for his room. He stops infront of it and slieds his pass key as the door unlocks. He takes a sigh of relief as he lays on the bed. He dials room service. Chef: What'll ya have chief? Aeron: Cola float please. Chef: On its way. Aeron sits there as he turns on his tv. A few minutes of flicking through channels theres a knock at the door. Chef: Heres your cola float sir. Aeron smiles as he walks to the door, he opens the door as hes tackled to the floors and handcuffed by three police officers. The scene goes back to the station as he takes a final sip of what was left of the water. Aeron: And here I am, talking to you. After he dropped me off that was the last I seen of Jekyl, honest. Now can I go home? I've got to get some sleep so I can go swimming tommorrow. The two officers nod as they escort him to a driver and a car. Aeron tells the driver where to go. After a few minutes the man tells him he can leave. Aeron thanks him as he steps out of the car and back into the hotel. He goes back up into the elevator. It dings as he steps out and starts walking. He gets stopped midway to his room by Sara Patterson. Sara: Hey Aeron, before you go to bed would you mind answering a couple questions? Aeron: Sure. Sara looks around waving the camera crew in. Aeron sighs slightly as he yawns. Sara: So what do you think about your match this week? Aeron: Well Like I said before Im here to stay and Im keeping that title. Theres no way I'm letting them get a hold of it. Sara: Speaking of "them" what do you think of your opponents? Aeron chuckles slightly Aeron: Which one? Sara: Lets start with the pre-defeated J.K. Valentino. Aeron: That bozo? I beat him once I can do it again no doubt. Hes a walking talking stereo and I'm going to put him on mute, permanently. Sara looks nervously at him stuttering at the start of her next question. Sara: A-and what about Jason Fear? Aeron: Jason FEAR? Come on how corny can you get. Ooh ooh I got a better name for him. Jason Queer cause you see Aeron dont fear NO man. Cept Jekyl, than again he aint man. Sara: Good point, I think everyones afraid of Jekyl. Jeff Austin? Aeron: Jeff who? Oh, Mr."Unpredictable"? Or, Mr.Drunk you can take your pick, yet another person that doesnt threaten me. Hes probably going to show up drunk and get kicked out. Sara: And how bout the the "Greatest highflyer in history"? Aeron: Is that what that stank was? Sorry I thought it was flying chicken. I hope people have an appetite for wings. And maybe Austin can bring some beer. Sara: Well thats all that we really needed was your thoughts on the match and your opponents. Aeron: Alright. Sara and the camera crew walk off down the hall to the elevator as Aeron opens his door with his key. He yawns and stretches and rubs his eyes as he sees Jekyl on the end of the bed. Aeron: DUDE!? WHAT THE FUCK! Jekyl snickers as he starts interrogating Aeron about the cops. Aeron still half asleep not knowing what he was saying nor could he remember. He woke up the next morning to find an empty bottle of whisky and shot glasses and a card on the bed. "Why so serious?" He sighed as he tossed it in the garbage can.
