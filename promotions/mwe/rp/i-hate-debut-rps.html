I Hate Debut RPs; Brad Kane Production

Aug 19 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A giant TV screen, showing past wrestling matches. Its hard to say who it is for certain but there is a ton of kicking going on. The two men on the screen aren't backing down from one another, just kicking as hard as they possibly can. There is a busty brunette cheering at ringside, cheering on one of the two. The match stops in the middle, someone hitting a pause button on what could only be a DVD. Walking around in a giant room now with theater seating, surround sound speakers and all the video games and movies you could ever want. That same brunette is walking around again, wearing next to nothing. Her figure is quite fantastic, really knowing how to wear a tank top that's a size to small and a pair of shorts. Suddenly a hand snaps and smacks her rear end. The brunette turns around, ready to slap someone but quickly draws it back. A sly grin is on her face now, realize who smacked her on the ass. It's a man, covered in so many tattoos you might think he's an addict or possibly spent some time in prison. No matter who or what he is, the woman, who is way out of the league of a person like him, gives him a kiss on the cheek before grabbing his hand. Their feet pound against stairs, going into a giant kitchen. No one is there, the woman making her man sit down at the table. Woman: Well Bradley, what would you like for lunch today? Brad: Wouldn't mind a big breast of Megan. Ahah, funny joke. Megan giggles before shaking her head. Brad shrugs his shoulders, not really bothered of what she cooks him for lunch. Brad: Anything that you want to make me is just fine. Just no potato salad, please. Megan: I know, you hate my potato salad, just like everyone else does. Lord knows I've tried and tried to make it good. Brad stands up and rubs Megan's back, trying to make her feel a little bit better. It works, both of them opening the fridge. Brad pulls out a package of hot dogs and places it on the counter. Megan: Hot dogs? Brad: Yes, hot dogs. What's wrong with hot dogs? Megan: You know what hot dogs do to me. A grin is on his face now, getting a pan and starts to fill it with water. Megan sighs, not wanting this for lunch but she'll just have to deal with it. Lunch is cooking, Brad going back to the table and sits. He pulls out a chair for Megan but that doesn't exactly work. She instead plops down onto his lap. Being a guy and all, Brad is happy to have her on his lap. giving her a kiss on the neck. Megan: You know I don't sit in a chair when no one else is around us. I figured after being married for nearly two years, you'd know this by now. Brad: You'd figure so but I don't remember a whole lot of things. But I do know one thing. Megan: What's that? Brad: That shirt you have on is way too small. It's like you got it out of Lacey's closet. Only you would try to squeze into a shirt that belongs to a three year old. She giggles again before standing up. Megan walks to a cabinet and opens, revealing paper plates and some napkins. Two plates and two napkins are all they need before she grabs the hot dog buns. Brad watches her every move closely, hoping something pops out. Nothing does, sadly, Megan sitting back down on his lap. Megan: There, now all we need are the hot dogs and we're good to go. Brad: Yes indeed. Now, we have a busy couple of weeks coming up. We leave for Paris on Friday.. Then we come home for a couple of days. Fly to L.A. for a couple of days before heading off to Chicago. Gotta admit we never have a dull time. Megan: Not at all. I think I'm looking more forward to our L.A. trip though. No kids with us, just you, me and the camera filming us. Brad nods his head, staring into his wife's clevage as she goes on. Megan: At first I really wasn't wild about us agreeing to be in the porn industry but I've grown to love it and I know you have. Watching me with all the other girls. Then I get to watch you with the other girls. Brad: It is rather fun. After lunch, should we get some practice? Megan nods her head before they both turn their attention to the stove. The pot containing the hot dogs is boiling over. Brad gets up and brings the pot to the table, watching Megan eat the hot dogs. Well she doesn't as much eat them as she pretends to give it, you know, a bj. If this is your introduction to the personal life of Brad and Megan Kane, boy, you haven't seen anything yet. ----- So I suppose this is where I'd use some time to formally introduce myself to a new place. For those that don't know, my name is Brad Kane or Bradley Allen Kane if you really want to get formal. I've been married for nearly two years to the greatest woman in the world. Before that, I was in a five year marriage with a complete bitch. I have a twin brother and a little sister. I'm an old man at the age of thirty. I have a total of six kids right now, by three different women. The oldest is only three so if I hear anyone bitching about a lack of sleep, you die. Been wrestling for a bit over five years now and I've mostly loved every single moment of it. There are a few times where I hate this business but for the most part, this is what I was meant to do with my life. Some of us were born to be doctors, born to be lawyers, born to be a drug addict or perhaps even be born to moonlight in the porn industry. You see, not only is Brad Kane a professional wrestler, I'm also in the porn business too. If life has taught me anything, there isn't too much of a good thing. I enjoy having wild sex with my wife and all the other women in that certain industry and I love kicking people in the head in the wrestling industry. For the last couple of years, I've made myself into a man who enjoys kicking people in the head. You see, when I first started in wrestling, I was known as Reckless Jack, an insane high flyer who'd do anything to win a match as long as it involved blood and lots of it. Some people say I worked better while I was Reckless Jack and others swear I'm better going by my real name. I personally feel since I've started to use my real name, my career has taken a turn in the right direction. When I was known as Reckless Jack, I got a bit of a reputation as a guy who was a flake, who didn't care, who'd leave over anything he could find an excuse for. I was an immature guy in the wrestling world who wanted it all and I wanted it all right then and there. It didn't help that a title was basically handed to me my first month in the business. I didn't know that you had to earn anything, I always thought it was given to you on a silver platter because every promotion wanted the new guy to succeed. I thought that the owners wanted a guy like me to come in with new blood and show the old guys how to work. Then once I really got into the wrestling world, I found it was quite the opposite. I think that's why I gained my reputation as an immature guy because I was used to getting what I wanted and when it didn't happen, I'd go into this mold of a two year old and throw a huge fit. I was acting like one of my daughters because I didn't get what I wanted. Everything snapped into a huge perspective on one certain night. It was in May of 2007 and some people already know this story. I was going for a move I'd done plenty of times, a shooting star press. Apparently I didn't jump right or get enough rotation on it because the next thing I remember was waking up in a hospital room with my now wife crying her eyes out worried about it. That night showed me two things. The first is that she loved me, obviously. The second though is much more important to my career. In that one moment when I woke up, I realized that it was time to shed that immature persona that had plagued me for quite some time. That shooting star press opened my eyes to that I need to be a bit more mature. She helped me through it though. My wife, my angel, she helped me in so many ways. I used to be an alcoholic too. She forced me into rehab. Megan is the reason I'm still alive and the reason why I'm still in the wrestling business. She loves watching me wrestle. Megan knows, just like a few other people here know that I enjoy this. I like being able to stare a guy in the eyes from across the ring before I kick him in the goddamn head. But, but there is one thing that gets me more mad then anything else in the world and one of the members of this promotion happens to know what that is. I have one rule, keep your mouth shut about my family. Feel free to call me an asshole, call me a homo, call me a piece of dog crap because I've already been called every name in the book. But once you cross that line and you utter something about my wife, my children or my siblings, I just lose all control and I don't see things clearly. I've nearly killed two men I used to consider a brother because of not only what they did to me personally but also due to what they said about my family. If you ever want to know about my anger, just ask that asshole Kevin Hardaway. He knows of it first hand when I nearly took him out of the business earlier this year. That's all in the past now. Now I'm looking forward to the future. I'm looking forward to my debut here in MWE. How often does someone get their first match in a promotion on a pay per view, on the main card. I honestly didn't expect anything like this but once I got that e-mail and I saw the match listing with my name on it, my jaw dropped. I guess having a reputation does pay off every once in a while because there are a few people here who I used to work with in an old promotion you've probably heard about so many times it'll never leave your head, they got me put into this position. And for that, I thank you. All praise aside now, this match at Crowning Glory is my first chance to show this new place, this new promotion about who I am and what I can do in the ring. You've heard the stories, maybe but now you get to see it first hand. I hope I'll live up to the hype but most of the time when someone gets placed on such a high pedistial, they'll never live up to it, even if this turns out to be match of the year. I have no idea who these guys I'm facing are. I just know their names and a small bit about them. Blight, he's some kind of gothic emo kid? I don't know, I'm just trying to gain a grasp on what this guy is. He wears a mask, that I'm pretty sure of. See, this is the part I hate of joining a new promotion. People expect this junk talk about the people I'm facing but I don't know who they are so how can I possibly say anything that's good without making up anything. I hate people making up false facts about who they're facing, call it a pet peeve. So, Blight, I guess you're an evil looking guy. Nothing I haven't seen before. So take off the mask, quit being creepy and lighten up. No one likes a party pooper and I think you might fit that bill. Now this other guy, Sebastian, is French, I believe. I think I really don't have to say much else, the jokes write themselves. If it wasn't for World War II, maybe your people would have a better reputation. Personally, I don't think every French guy is a coward, just a pansy. But hey, just my opinion and I'm sure someone will rip my head off for saying that. No matter who these two people are, falls count anywhere, sounds pretty fun. Been a while since I've been in that match. Maybe I'll buy you two a taxi, we fly on a plane and we go fight in Boston. No, wait, I got a better idea, we go fight in a strip club and calls all of the women whores. Falls count anywhere, so unpredictable, so much fun and right up my style. Hell, I might not even wear my ring gear for this one. Just as long as I get to kick something, I'll be happy. Blight and Sebastian on the other hand, well, they won't be so happy when their heads are rolling on the floor. This should be a good one guys, just decide if you really want to show up to this or not. I got something to prove and when that happens, the survey says... You're dead.
