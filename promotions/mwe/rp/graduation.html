Graduation

Aug 21 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[OOC: This RP being late is Derfs fault.] The scene opens in a decently decorated room. Nothing huge and fancy, but it's also not a crap shack. In the middle of said room, is a large man sitting on the floor. That man is J.P. Smith. Looking closer, he's on the floor looking at a photo album. Why he's sitting on the floor, who knows, but he looks comfortable. The album itself is huge, probably close to 1000 pictures in it, give or take. As J.P. flips through em, he stops at a certain page that catches his attention. A large "8" appears in the center of 4 pictures, one of those pictures featuring a chubby little kid with a birthday hat on. It's more then obvious this page has pictures from J.P.s 8th birthday party. And when you turn 8, it's supposed to be a very happy time for all children, yet for some reason, J.P. doesn't look overly excited in his picture. Another picture from his 8th birthday party is his gifts being opened. Normally, a kid would be smiling opening gifts. This picture is no exception, the kid opening the gifts has a HUGE smile on his face... but it's not J.P. It's one of his brothers, opening his birthday gifts without permission. Another picture, J.P.s father sleeping on the couch, smoke in mouth, pint in hand. Typical fashion. This page of pictures, J.P.s 8th birthday, was a typical day in his life as a young child. J.P. has had enough of looking at these photos though, so he flips the page, or multiple pages at that. Ironically, the page he lands on is exactly 5 years later, at J.P.s 13th birthday party. Unlike the last pictures, J.P. seems happy here. Lots of gifts, pictures of J.P. himself opening them. Pictures of J.P. with all kinds of friends and other adults, two of which are his foster parents. But one photo stands out among the rest. There is one picture of J.P. standing next to a friend. Both friends are wearing the same shirt, an orange and red flannel shirt, very unattractive, very flashy. But it's not the shirts that make the pic stand out. It's who is in it. Standing next to a young J.P. Smith, is a young Eric Derf. As J.P. looks at his photos of what now looks like a happier time in his life, his thoughts are interrupted by a knock at the door. J.P. however is far to lazy to get up, and he doesn't want to be distracted from his photos. Either way, he was expecting company anyway, so assuming it's who he thinks it is at the door, he simply shouts. J.P. - It's open!! The door can be heard opening, someone enters. J.P. doesn't move, he's assuming he knows who it is, so he continues flipping through the photo album. Eventually his guest makes their way into the room. It's his friend Celeste. She walks into the room and sees J.P. sitting on the floor. She snickers a bit before asking. Celeste - So, why are you sitting on the floor? J.P. - I was just looking at some old pictures, bringing back some memories. Care to join me? Celeste - Sure! I love pictures! Celeste sits down on the floor excited, but J.P.s expression seems to have faded a bit. J.P. - I wish I could say the same, but for me, looking at pictures from my childhood is just a brutal reminder of how bad my childhood really was. Celeste - Oh come on, I'm sure it couldn't have been THAT bad. Are you sure you're not exaggerating a bit? J.P. - Exaggerating? Let me paint you a picture of how bad my childhood was - Wait, no. Better yet, let me show you a picture of exactly how bad some things were. J.P. closes the photo album, then opens it up to the very first page. He was a new born baby, still in the hospital. Innocent looking. But that wasn't what he was looking for. He flips a few pages till he finds some photos of when he was about 3 or 4 years old, just a young toddler. The picture he points out of one of him sitting on his fathers lap. And with J.P. sitting on the lap, his fathers other hand clutched a pint of Vodka. J.P. - When I was young, he would do this almost every night. He'd drink constantly, telling me it was his medication. He'd put me to bed while still drinking, he'd tell me bedtime stories while drinking, he'd even have a pint in place of dinner, sometimes barely making us food at all, just so he didn't have as long to wait for his next drink. And I was young enough that I believed it really was medication. Celeste is a bit shaken by this revelation, she places her hand on J.P.s shoulder in an attempt to comfort him a bit. He stares at the photo again before flipping a few more pages. This time, he points out a photo of himself sitting in a hospital bed. He's got a goofy smile and it pointing at his head. Celeste leans forward to see what he's pointing at, only to notice a wide cut closed up with stitches. J.P. - My brothers thought it would be funny to push me down the stairs. I smashed my head off of a radiator when I got to the bottom, and they let me lay there bleeding. They were scared to call the ambulance, so they sat in the living room and watched TV, pretending nothing happened. Celeste - What?! Oh my god, that is awful!! Celeste is covering her mouth at such disgust. She's legit shocked, she knew J.P. had a bad past, but not that bad. As J.P. goes to flip the page, Celeste stops him. Celeste - What happened there? She points to a picture of J.P. standing next to some classmates, but what sticks out is his arm in a sling and a cast. J.P. - Oh, that! He chuckles a bit, nice change of sounds for once. J.P. - Well I was a little chunkier when I was young. Walking home from school, I dropped a cookie in the road. I stopped to grab it, and I got hit by a car. Celeste - Oh no! That's terrible! J.P. - You think that's bad?! You should have seen the car! I destroyed it! Celeste can't help but laugh, which was J.P.s intention, as they both share a moment in laughter. Celeste takes her hand off J.P.s shoulder now and cross's them as she continues looking down at the photo album. J.P. goes from page to page now, showing many more photos of his childhood that look bad enough to be in a horror movie. J.P. - It wasn't just my brothers and father that made me so screwed up, that made my childhood so awful. It was everyone, my whole family. Aunts, Uncles, even my grandfather. None of them were really there for me as a child, and to be honest, I couldn't be happier for their rejecting me. Celeste gives J.P. a "WHA?!" kinda face, she doesn't even need to say anything, her point is made and J.P. knows it. He once again flips about halfway through the album, to the pictures from earlier of his 13th birthday party. J.P. - I say I couldn't be happier because if it wasn't for all the bad I suffered early on, I wouldn't have been put in foster care, and I wouldn't have had the great life I have had ever since then. Not to mention, I'd have never met my life long best friend. He points to the photo of himself and Eric Derf in matching shirts. Celeste - Is that the weird dude you hung out with in High School all the time? Eric? Do you even talk to him anymore? J.P. gets a shocked, almost insulted look on his face. J.P. - Of course I still talk to him! Like I said, he's my best friend. He's been a better brother then both of my real brothers, he's been there for all the good and all the bad. Hell, he's the reason I got into wrestling. He's the guy that got me hired at UUW! Now it's Celeste that has the shocked look on her face. Celeste - Wait, Eric Derf is who got you into wrestling? Wasn't he like 100 pounds soaking wet, in a paper bag? How did he get into wrestling? Does he even win? J.P. - Well actually, right now he is considered one of the best wrestlers to never hold a World Title, so yeah, he wins. Alot. He's been training me too, some of my wins are because of him actually. If it wasn't for his advice, I might not even have won a single match. I owe alot to him. He's one of the best. J.P. flips through the photo album, almost to the end. He finds a picture of himself and Eric Derf standing inside an empty arena, with a wrestling ring in the middle of it. They're at the top of the arena, far from the ring. While Josh is dressed casually, Eric isn't even wearing a shirt, and he's quite sweaty. They have their arm around each other, giving a thumbs up to the camera with their free hands. J.P. - This picture was taken a few years ago, on the night Eric made his pro debut in WrestleZone Championship Wrestling. He lost that match, but obviously he was still excited, and even then he was trying to get me work there. His first match, and he wanted me working with him, wrestling with him. Never did pan through though. Celeste - Why not? J.P. - Way to many politics. He didn't kiss enough ass there, and wasn't afraid to speak his mind, and it cost him. He wrestled 10 matches for that company, and they had him lose 9 of them, just as punishment. Celeste - Ouch, that sucks... He flips the page just once, and there is another picture, almost identical to the one just being looked at. J.P. and Eric, standing in the arena, only this time Eric has a thumbs down instead of thumbs up like last time. J.P. - This was his last night in WZCW, it wasn't the best night, but it did lead to bigger and better things. Celeste - It did? You just said he lost almost every match he had, he don't sound to good, does he? As she was speaking, he flips a few more pages. Once again, another picture of Eric Derf, this time taken from a fans perspective while he was wrestling a match. J.P. - Well he might not have been then, but he wasn't giving up. This pic was taken during a match he had in a company called Rev Pro. He lost that match because SOMEONE was blind! Celeste - I hear referees are stupid... J.P. - No, not the ref, his partner. He was blind. It was his gimmick. Not a very good one though... The two have a bit of a chuckle before J.P. continues speaking. J.P. - After that, he wrestled in a few other companys, only had a few matches here and there, before he finally decided to head to the next big thing, Mayhem Entertainment Wrestling. Celeste - Isn't that where your both signed now? J.P. - Yeah, but it was a while before I signed there, and when he joined, he wanted to do something different. He looks Celeste in the eyes, and without staring at the album, he drops his finger onto a picture. Celeste looks down at it to see a picture of a wrestler in a mask. Celeste - His different idea was to fight someone in a mask? J.P. - No, he WAS in the mask. He didn't want people to know it was him for a little while. He called himself the Mysterious G. Celeste - Sounds creepy, sorta... J.P. nods in agreement but continues to speak. J.P. - It was this idea that started Erics rise to fame. He started winning more matches, gaining respect in the locker room along the way. Hell, if it wasn't for that mask, he might not have won the first ever title he won, even though he ditched the mask right away. J.P. points to another picture on the same page, this one with Eric Derf holding the X-TV Title in one hand, and his mask in another. J.P. - He lost the title a few weeks later, but he went strait to the top from that point. Hell, this time last year he beat 7 other people in a ladder match to win the Money in the Bank contract? Celeste - Money in the Bank? J.P. - By winning that contract, he was granted a shot at the title anytime over the next year, at a time of his choosing. However, MWE closed right before he could cash in, so he never did get his chance to win the title. Celeste - Shitty... Celeste stands back up to stretch a bit, but J.P. is fine sitting down. It might be boring to Celeste to hear about her friends friends life, but J.P. is just enjoying himself, and it couldn't hurt to go over his soon to be opponent. J.P. - After MWE closed, Eric retired from the ring so he could settle down and start a family with his bitch of a girlfriend. But once she actually got pregnant, he needed money, so he went to UUW and got his own talk show, The Hot Topic. And using the popularity of his talk show and his pull backstage, he finally helped me reach my ultimate dream. He got me a contract to wrestle in the pro leagues. J.P. points to a photo of himself, in full wrestling gear, standing next to Eric, dressed in a suit and tie, with a green screen behind them both, probably for the Hot Topic set. Celeste lets out a bit of a sigh, causing J.P. to finally look away from the photos at his now bored friend. Celeste - I'm happy for you, but you just spent the last forever talking about a guy I really don't know. No offence, but if you love him so much, why don't you marry him? J.P. - Well the reason I've been looking into his career so much lately is because it started off pretty slow. Just like my career is starting off slow. Hell, like him in WZCW, I only had one victory in UUW before I left there. But now that he's unretired and we're both in MWE, he's gonna teach me everything he knows, and eventually I'm even gonna pass him on the ladder to success. J.P. finally stands up, leaving the album on the floor. He walks over to a coffee table as Celeste watches. He pays no attention to her, instead he grabs something off said table. It's another picture. He goes back to the floor and flips the album all the way to the end of the pictures. He slides the picture in as Celeste leans down behind him to see what it is. It's a picture of J.P. and Eric standing in the ring, holding each others arms in the air in victory. J.P. - Our first time teaming together, and we won. And now, it has finally come down to this. Celeste - Come down to what? J.P. sits for a moment in silence before clearing his throat of potential flem or whatever. J.P. - Something I have yet to tell you. Our next show is the biggest show of the year, and it's also Erics final test for me, we're facing each other. Everything he taught me I plan on throwing back at him. My rise to fame will come so much sooner then one would think. Just look at Erics career, it started just line mine. The only difference is I'm already doing better then he was. My whole life Eric has been there, teaching me lessons along the way. I'm like a baby bird being shown how to fly. And after Crowning Glory this Sunday when we steal the show, I will have proved to him and the world there is nothing else he can teach me. I will pass my final test. I will fly on my own. Celeste - Yeah, but doesn't he have alot more experience than you? J.P. - Yeah, but one of the things Eric taught me is that will power and determination can exceed experience any day of the week. Not to mention I've picked up a few tricks from a few other people I've fought. Example, I fought Leon Roberts, the guy fighting for the title. He showed me wrestling isn't just for the body, but for the mind. When we were getting ready to fight, he would use his mind tricks to get into my head, and it worked to a tee when be defeated me. Although it might not be the best thing to do, I was always told to do whatever it takes to win. I know a few things about Eric I could use to get into his head. I could remind him of his own father. Celeste - What about his father? J.P. - He went to one of Erics first ever wrestling matches, before he was famous. He was drunk, and ended up getting into a fight with Eric himself. Eric punched his father, and when he hit the floor, he never got back up. He died on the spot. A gasp is heard coming out of Celestes mouth as she claps her hands over it. Celeste - Eric killed his father?!?! J.P. - Not really, they say he was literally in the middle of a heart attack when Eric hit him. He was dead before Eric threw the final punch. But it's stuff like that I can use against Eric to get into his head. If that didn't work, I could remind him of his dead baby. Celeste - That's just cold hearted... J.P. - I was told whatever it takes from Eric himself. And with the skills and mind tricks I picked up from Leon Roberts, I will beat Eric. I will graduate from his teachings. Almost like it was planned to happen after his best line, Celestes cell phone starts to jingle. She pulls it out of her purse and begins replying to a text message. As soon as she finishes, she stands up, causing J.P. to stand up next to her. Celeste - I really do hope you win your match, you'll be fine wrestling without help, I'm sure. But I have to be going now, I just got a text from my mother. J.P. - Alright, but just wait here for a minute. J.P. runs out of the room for a few moments. Just as Celeste was starting to get impatient, J.P. returns with what looks like a ticket. J.P. - I know you're not the biggest wrestling fan, but I would love to see you in person at the show. It'd be nice for you to see me in my proudest moment. Celeste - I wouldn't miss it for the world! As the two embrace each other for a hug, the photo album on the floor begins to be zoomed in upon. The photo that is currently being displayed is the one of Eric and J.P. wearing the matching shirts. Two best friends, together through thick and thin, about to go through the thickest.
