Dinner and a Promo

The camera fades up, on an elegant and lavish room, which seems to stretch for eternity. It’s as if they are inside a castle, with intricate and masterful paintings lining the walls. So why is it that we find ourselves in such a place? Are we celebrating the crowning of some new royal entity? Are we finally getting to see a Angie Hardaway gangbang? DeAngelo awkwardly looks around the empty hall, the table stretched far down the room, row after row of empty seats. Where's everyone else? Everybody else? After the pleasantries are exchanged, Warner walks away from the camera, and in the background we hear the beginning of a shower running, as the camera cuts to black. -------------------------------------------------------------------------- We open up in a familiar, clichéd dark room, its inhabitants swimming in shadows, all in a desperate bid to make the scene seem atmospheric and a little bit mysterious, perhaps even holding a tinge of danger and things of a fear inducing nature. All through the house, not a creature was stirring, not even a… Footsteps echo quietly, the sound bouncing from the walls with the tenderness of your average feather falling from the sky. Perhaps even a leaf falling from a tree as a couple walk underneath. Something of that rosey tinted description. The gentle clicks of plastic being manipulated ring out like gun shots in the docile silence, until music begins to play, radiating from all corners of the room. Nothing to loud or abrasive, but enough to fill the shadows. “I love this song, I really do. ‘Angerfly’ by Cry to the Blind. I doubt many of you have heard it, doesn’t really surprise me. You’re all nothing but a collection of uncultured fuckers, constantly sucking on a stream of consciousness known as the Main Stream, content to follow whatever style or life that’s sat in front of you, perfectly happy with your blind mediocrity. Not me though, no, not me. Granted, Uncultured has been a word used to describe me on more than one occasion, but it is better suited for you all. I watch in disgust as this federation breeds more and more generic soldiers to march into war, the MWE flag held high in pride and ignorance. A generation of Semi‘s and Devlin Sharps, oh the future looks bright doesn’t it? Sure does, if a second wave of boredom and a distinct lack of life and flair is your thing. 2008, the year of boredom and the overrated. I watched as the few free-thinkers, the few artists in this company went unheard and ignored for the longest time. For months and fucking months, the few with their own voice were ignored. People like Paige and Windy Bridges get shit on, when they deserve to be the face of this company. It fucking sickened me. People with no emotion and no distinct personality were labeled as role models, people to try and be like. Not even I could change all too much, as brilliant as I am. Sure I had my followers, but they were certainly the minority, the rest ignored. But then, something happened. A few people left and one or two began to shine. People took notice at what was possible, but they saw perhaps too late. One of the trailblazers left, gone forever, but his mark was still made, no matter how small. That’s why I’m here. I’ve seen that change can occur. Perhaps this time, my ability will not be cast aside by the pathetic. Maybe this time, originality will shine. I’ve set my sights on a pedestal that will allow my rule to spread across the MWE nation. Yet before I can make my move towards the top of MWE, even though I have already proven myself in this company, they insist on starting me off at the bottom once more. He leans back in a chair, grinning ever so slightly. With no DeAngelo here, this promo seems to have taken on a more serious tone. Management has decided that instead of giving me some sort of challenge, they are going to team me with my best friend Kevin Hardaway, against two people who have never had a match together in their lives. I don’t know what MWE is thinking, because if they want rating, a decimation isn’t the right way to go about it. Now, I know the smarks were buzzing about who I was going to bring n to watch my back here in MWE, and I’m sure that it was a shock to see Kevin of all people. Most expected DeAngelo Williams, CSB, or even Random McRally. As great of wrestlers as they all are, none of them have been through what me and Kevin have. We have hated each other, bloodied each other, and had each other’s back. He took my GWC World title from me, I called his wife a no good drug addict whore. He almost ended my career, I laughed at him when he almost OD’ed on pills. Hell, he even abandoned me in a match, causing me to get knocked the fuck out. I’ll take a moment to fill in the blanks though. You see, through each incarnation of Uncultured, whether it be Youth, Society, Revolution, or Anarchy, there has always been one common goal. If you are united under the Uncultured name, you live together and you die together. Youth was born of a common goal of wanting to live the straight-edge life, society was born out of the greed of each man, Revolution was born out of the necessity to survive, and Anarchy was born to own the fuck out of MWE.
