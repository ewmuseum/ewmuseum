Rebutting the Rebutal

Sep 29 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
An MWE Camera begins to fade out of a blant and black image as it takes time to come to a picture perfect image. It finally does as it is seen to be in some sort of Entertainment room. The camera screenshots through the room where it is seen to be a pool table, arcade games, a bar and to the left a huge entertainment theatre which is currently connected to a PS3. A white leather lounge is seen in front of the television with a figure sitting down on the lounge. The camera slowly turns it around to see it to be none other than MWE World Champion, Sonny Williams. Sonny is playing a game on the PS3 with the controller in his hands. He bites his lip to show his concentration. He is wearing a white bandana, grey hoodie jumper with the sleeves cut off and baggy blue jeans with the left leg held up by a red bandana. He has the MWE World Championship lying next to him on the lounge. Sonny Williams: Oh my god, I was so close to an airstrike. All of a sudden the door creaks open as Sonny turns back in anticipation to see who it is. It is his part time manager Mr.Cooper. Sonny turns back to the television in a careless motion to Mr.Cooper entering the room. Mr.Cooper is in a stylish suit, as always. He walks up to the lounge to watch Sonny playing the game. He looks down at his watch and looks impatient. Mr.Cooper: Sonny! Sonny turns back a little shocked. Sonny Williams: .. What? Mr.Cooper: You've got an interview to shoot. I booked it for now so you better hop off your couch potato ass and walk out that door and get ready for your interview. Sonny turns back to the television and continues playing. Sonny Williams: Screw that. Mr.Cooper looks a bit annoyed. Mr.Cooper: Screw that? A few days ago you went from sorrowing to believing. Where has that man gone? Sonny Williams: He's not available at the moment, please leave a message after the beep. BEEP! Mr.Cooper looks annoyed still. Mr.Cooper: C'mon Sonny we need to go. Sonny Williams: But I'm playing a game.. Mr.Cooper: I don't care whether you're playing MWE 09 or Hello Kitty Adventure Island, we need to go now. Sonny remains silent as Mr.Cooper smirks and thinks of something to say. Mr.Cooper: Fine, have it your way. I guess we'll shoot the interview later with Shawn Spitzer instead of Sara Patterson. Sonny turns around with widen eyes as he drops the PS3 controller and picks up the MWE World Championship and heads towards the door as Mr.Cooper smirks walking behind Sonny. Mr.Cooper: .. Works like a charm. Sonny opens the door and walks down the hallway with Mr.Cooper a few paces behind to see an MWE Interview set up already done and furnished. A camera crew and all are ready in place to shoot the Interview as Sara Patterson walks into the frame as Sonny looks at her and smiles. Sonny Williams: Sara Patterson is so much better than any game. Mr.Cooper rolls his eyes at Sonny. Mr.Cooper: If you say so. Sara Patterson gets into position ready to shoot the interview she looks at Sonny, as Sonny smiles. She smiles at him back. Mr.Cooper and Sonny Williams are out of picture as the camera man uses his fingers to indicate how long until they begin to shoot. He goes down by 3, 2, 1 as they now are on air. Sara Patterson smiles at the camera. Sara Patterson: Hello, and welcome to all MWE fans around the globe. I am Sara Patterson and my guest at present time, well they don't get any bigger than this. He is accompanied by Mr.Cooper and is the current MWE World Champion.. Sonny Williams! The camera zooms out showing Mr.Cooper and Sonny Williams into the camera. Sonny looks at Sara Patterson and her skimpy outfit at the present time of the interview. Sonny Williams: .. Damn girl! Sara Patterson looks confused but didn't hear exactly what Sonny said. Sara Patterson: Huh? Mr.Cooper butts into the conversation quickly. Mr.Cooper: What my client was trying to say is that it is great to be shooting an interview with you Sara, they don't come any bigger than Sara Patterson now don't they? Mr.Cooper chuckles thinking Sara will too, but she doesn't. Sonny smirks in response to it. Sara Patterson: Now Sonny, this week you go into a very intense battle against the newcomer, the Vega Knight, and to top it all off it will be for the MWE World Championship. What are your thoughts on your match? Sonny Williams: My thoughts? I'm not gonna lie and say yeah I've been in the gym 24/7, drinking egg energy drinks and preparing all week for this match because quite honestly, I haven't been. I've been out partying, drinking and catching up on my social life. I've been so angered since last Monday Night Mayhem because that fucking conniving Vega Knight or what ever you people call him, I've been trying to focus elsewhere. He's taken this drunken, sorrowing bastard I was and flipped him over into a raging, determined man that I am. He's fucking angered me I tell ya.. Mr.Cooper butts in and looks at Sonny a bit annoyed. Mr.Cooper: Uhh, Sonny I don't think this is a suitable timeslot to be using those words. Sonny Williams: Fuck it, fuck Vega Knight. If children are watching this I recommend they switch off now because by gawd I warn you, you haven't heard this much foul language since D.C.'s fucking sex tape with Lindsey. This is gonna be more fucking crazy, more fucking insane than anything Jekyl would ever do. You know, this match to me, it's reached a step higher, one Vega will walk and one I will fucking leap. What lies on my shoulder, it's like a baby to mothers for me. I love it. I love walking around this fucking building and people automatically paying me their respect. It's fucking awesome. Mr.Cooper smiles. Mr.Cooper: This is the man I was looking for. Sara Patterson: Sonny, you say this match between you and Vega Knight has reached another level, would that be partly from what he has said in the media this week? Sonny Williams: Ab-so-fucking-lutely. I mean he angered me last MNM, but some of the bullshit he has said this week has angered me further. It's brought this man out that people around the world have been waiting all their fucking lives for. He thinks he is a funny dude, but honestly have you seen some of his insults? Don't get me fucking started. And that hair, fuck man if I were him I'd be more wanting a fucking haircut than my title. He thinks he can humiliate me, on international television. I got three words for him; GOOD FUCKING LUCK! Sonny Williams: I've out done, out last the very best this industry can provide. Who has this joke gone past to have bragging rights? JJ Executioner. Vega, he hasn't even got the right to speak yet little alone be fucking around with me. He runs his mouth like a fucking coyote, something I want to make come to a fucking halt. He better be listening to every word I say of this, because he might have done some fucking research on me and he might have fucking brought back memories but he needs to know that come Monday Night Mayhem he isn't walking into the ring against the ordinary Sonny, I am passionate, I am aggressive, I am fucking determined and do you know what I'm gonna do with those emotions all together? Sonny looks at Sara and Mr.Cooper as you can see the anger and intensity in his eyes. Mr.Cooper: You tell us.. Sonny Williams: I'm gonna fucking tear him limb by limb and let him know that this is Mayhem Wrestling Entertainment and when you walk in my fucking hood, you better drop to your knees and bow, bitch, bow. If you will let me Sara, I need to let that arrogant, fucking douchebag know something. Sara Patterson: It's all yours Sonny. Sonny Williams: I'm looking into this fucking camera as though I'm looking into your fucking eyes. You, you are one cocky fuckwit who has done and said things that as you can see, I am not very fucking happy with. Everyone wants to see the angered and high tempo man I once was, this is just a small glimpse baby. I'm not toxicated at the moment, and like I said in the past, this is Sonny fucking Williams and I'm the fucking World Champion and will be til the next fucking millennium. Vega, I don't know you and I will never fucking want to, but what I do to you Monday night will be so much more amusing than anything you had planned. I hate wrestling you, but I'm gonna love it when I fuck you up. I've said all I need to fucking say, I'm not gonna leave with a catchphrase but I will say this, I am going to kick your fucking ass. Sonny Williams: CHA CHING! Mr.Cooper looks confused. Sonny Williams: I couldn't resist. Sonny Williams walks away from the set up and back to his Entertainment room as Mr.Cooper is only a few strides behind Sonny. Sonny enters the room and walks back over to the white leather lounge as Mr.Cooper follows by closing the door. Mr.Cooper: That was insane, exactly what you needed to keep your title. Sonny turns and looks at Mr.Cooper. Sonny Williams: I'm doing it so we can fucking party, drink fucking alcohol and get fucking laid cause baby it's coming! Mr.Cooper smirks. Mr.Cooper: Like I said, you got a deal. -FADE- Promo Ends. OOC: I know it's like a bit late but I got permission from Sinc to do it. I only had one hour to do this too so I hope you take that into consideration why this RP wasn't my best. Feedback appreciated.
