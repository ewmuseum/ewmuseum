Reflection In the Fire

Aug 19 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It has been said that 'the truth will make men free'. The truth alone has never set anyone free. It is only doubt which will bring mental emancipation. Without the wonderful element of doubt, the doorway through which truth passes would be tightly shut. ~ Book of Lucifer. The sound of footsteps could be heard, echoing through my territory. That is what I hear. It disturbs me from my thoughts. Thoughts I need in order to focus about my next fight. I should have known I'd be getting company soon. Past few days, been secluded. Away from most of the world. Keeping to myself. Reflecting upon things. Even I, Leon 'The Virus' Roberts, need to do this on the occasion. I half expected my wife to show up. But knowing her, the little devils are keeping her busy. So instead, I get one of the other two people I would have expected, which are Jase, and Vega. Though I am pleasantly surprised it was Vega, especially after our last match. He got hit in the right eye...his bad eye. It was causing his vision problems in his eye again. Because of that, he wasn't going to wrestle at the pay per view. Still, from what I can see, he's able to see out of one eye, just perfectly. -Leon- What brings you here, my friend. As he approached me, infront of the fire barrel I have going, the bone chilling tone of voice doesn't seem to affect him. Doesn't surprise me anymore. He's far to used to my presence, and my mannerisms. He merely smiled. Rare from him indeed. -Supernova- Well...People were getting worried about you. So your wife sent me. -Leon- More like she's been to damn depraved. Tell me Vega...did she get so desperate, that she nearly jumped your own bones? -Supernova- Unfortunately yes. I chuckle at his response. Am I offended that he said unfortunately? I most certainly am not. I don't need to be. But I can tell he looks relieved. He knows that I was originally sore about what had happened before. But as time wears on, so does my moral degrading. Once I stop my chuckling, I see that he is looking at me, as if trying to get some answers out of me. -Leon- You came for more then just my wife's orders. You're curious about why I am secluding myself, even from my followers. Correct? -Supernova- Duh. This is not you. You don't suddenly...vanish without a trace. -Leon- Vega, my old friend, there are some things, even you don't fully understand about me. I do like seclusion sometimes. Not often, but right now, this is one of those times. Especially after our last match. -Supernova- Ugh. Don't remind me. I got enough reminder by wearing this fucken eyepatch. -Leon- Heh. You and your vision problems. They make me laugh sometimes. But this isn't a time to do that. No. Losing to Derf and Smith, made me realize, that I need to focus. Not let distractions get in my way. I do tend to get distracted easily. I can't help it sometimes. It's true. I often do find myself distracted over the littlest of things. It's come to the point where I am above and beyond annoyed with that little problem. Vega nods in understanding, as he pulls up a folding chair, to sit with me. -Supernova- You certainly got a huge match to focus on Leon. And let me tell you this. I- -Leon- -Don't say it. I know. You of all people, are looking forward to Crash's humiliation. -Supernova- Damn right I am! I will never forgive or forget what he's done to me. I'm personally going to make sure that he's not walking out of there. -Leon- Do not concern yourself with my affairs this time Vega. I only want you to watch. Your presence will be more then enough, as he will fear you trying to attack him. -Supernova- No Leon. Every single chance I get, I'm going to hurt that little bitch! The only reason why you're facing him, is because of the title. If there was no fucken title, I would have personally gone after him myself, and finished what I started! -Leon- I'm well aware of that Vega. But this isn't your fight. Not now at least. Besides...wouldn't it be more fun, when he joins us....becoming our personal bitch? I look at Vega in the one working eye. I could tell that he is thinking about it. The fact remains, I don't want my him to interfere. It's personal. I have to prove to not only myself, but the entire world, I am superior to Zack Crash in every which single way. I can see that my words are playing themselves in his head repeatedly. He seems to now enjoy such a cruel idea. He nods in agreement. -Leon- I figured as such Vega. There is one thing you must understand. When I do go into seclusion, I do it for a reason, even if forced. I don't want you to interfere, because I must do this alone. I must defeat Zack Crash. I must be the one, that makes him squeal. My...pride is what's refusing any form of help. I see him roll his eyes at me. It's almost as if he's having a hard time believing me. Well that's his problem. He knows that I'm nothing but honest, especially on some things. I wasn't lying when I said only I could be the one who could make Crash scream for mercy. -Supernova- Yeah yeah. But you mentioned that you were forced into seclusion once. What the fuck do you mean? Fuck me. That was a sore time. Don't exactly like to relive it, but I kinda have to. Judinging by how my sudden reaction was, he's staring right at me, telling me that he wants to know the story, and he's going to find out one way or another. -Leon- After my Wrestle Wars match, I had a massive meltdown. I was so pissed off, I was a lot more aggressive then usual, And that's saying something. But....it was so bad, that I was ordered into a mental ward. Oh I kicked and screamed, and even broke necks. But in the end, I was in a looney bin. A place, I expect a lot of people simply want me in still, considering that I'm not exactly known for my sanity. -Supernova- Neither of us are Leon. -Leon- I know. I spent a couple weeks in there. It was quiet, and when I don't seek it, I despise it. It was like...I had shut down, and looked over everything. In the end, I decided that, I was tired of holding myself back, out of fear. When I say I enjoy hurting people, I mean it, but that bloodlust only increased after that incarceration. Okay Leon. Remain calm. Save your rage for later. Now's not a good time. I can tell that Vega is worried, as I see him looking at my clenched hands. I took a deep breath, knowing that I had to explain everything. I had no intention of stopping now. -Leon- One day...I decided to escape. By then, my skin had gotten extremely pale, and my natural hair color, of fire red was showing through. I busted myself out of there. I saw the fear when I looked upon everyone's eyes when I walked out of there, covered in drywall dust, and a bit of blood, from a little fight I had gotten into with one of the guards. I did promise that if they didn't come after me, I wouldn't torture them. But during my time in there, I had decided, that not only was I sick and tired of holding back...I had to reveal my true intentions. -Supernova- A chaotic world. A world without rules. Only law, is survival. Yeah, I get that. Isn't that also the time you cut your hair? -Leon- Yes. Speaking of that, I still get people asking me why I cut it. would it really be surprising that I simply wanted a change from before? Apparently it is. Luckily, it would seem as if Vega did understand my reasoning. Which is good. Hate having to go into every single detail. -Supernova- Got a strategy to go against him? Please tell me you do. -Leon- You need not worry about that Vega. My strategy has been woven in place over time. I can feel his defenses breaking down. He doubts who he is. He's slowly succumbing to my words. His true self is being brought to the forefront of who he is. The more and more his darkness consumes him, the more ownership of his life I receive. Oh he'll try to use that truly sick and twisted evil part of him to his advantage, but there is one problem. Nobody knows how to properly use it, except for me. -Supernova- What about me? I think I have good control. -Leon- Says the guy who tends to have split personality moments. -Supernova- Touche. -Leon- Besides. Control is for pussies. -Supernova- No argument here. I chuckle at my friend. He put himself on the spot for that one. But once I am done, I look back into the fire. And I let myself get lost in it's visual dancing. I see all that I had done over the past two years. Championships, blood, and everything inbetween. I saw my feud with Zak Warner in GWC...it was mostly me not standing down, and going right for the bigger names...within reason at the time. I wanted the recognition, and to mean something. I knew that if I didn't, I wouldn't have been able to remain long. So yeah, it was a failed feud, but It did do what I set out to do. I put myself on the map, and hell, remembering how I nearly broke Zak's arm and neck in one match, I like to think I am responsible for getting him on the drugs. After that, I was in EW. I saw me humiliating sonny Williams, Milenko, Zak Warner, and Alejandro Rodriguez to become the only EW champion that actually mattered. That damn placed was lined with politiks. People who didn't deserve to be main event got there, and won. It could be argued I was one of them, but I know that I destroyed everyone in my way. More championships, and tough names. I was still a rookie on the pro scene still. But I was one who simply put, doesn't stay quiet for long. GWC did eventually close down in 2008, but not before I nearly killed a wrestler, by the name of Ryan Daniels, with just a single punch to the heart, to become a champion, then vacating the title when GWC did that WCW-esque relaunch...I won, and became the last Ultimate champion there. When I came to MWE, I admit, my head wasn't in the game at the time, so I didn't exactly care that I was beaten by Paige. I was more pissed off that I started earlier then I said I would be willing to. Guess that's why I was so screwed over a lot...or at least that's what it felt like. Still, I had made the name for myself. Vega and i were the last MWE tag team champions, and then i went on to retire Chris England, Jay Williams, and Mason Resoan, along with winning the MWE United States Championship. Of course, the day I lost it to Warner, I lost it completely. I felt I was screwed over...again. But thinking about it. I think I know the true problem. I have a massive ego. Probably just as big, if not bigger, then my need for violence. I guess it tends to develop over time for people like me. Well...similar to me, at least. But ti's that ego of mine, that does get in my way in some of my matches. -Supernova- Thinking about something? Shit. He caught me off guard there. I look at him, and nod, acknowledging that what he said, was correct. He knows that's the main reason I'm here right now. -Leon- Reflecting upon my career. Can't help but think that my ego is part of the reason why I keep losing. I mean....you and I both know that Zack Crash...No...just about everyone, is inferior to me, yet I keep blowing it. It's my ego, man. -Supernova- Now that's just fucken stupid man. You're letting everyone get to you. Didn't you just say you're the goddamn Virus? That you do whatever the fuck you want, fuck the consequences? I look at him, square in the eyes. How could I have forgotten already?! My lips curl up into a very slight smirk. Vega is right. I did say that the other day, at the press conference. I certainly meant it. -Leon- Yeah. You got me there Vega. But...at the same time, you know how cocky I am. You and I both know that Zack Crash can't defeat me, without help from myself. He may have won the UUW title from me, but he didn't win on his own. I took to damn long, and it cost me. -Supernova- True, that did happen. I don't know what to say man. I mean...I think you have every motherfucken right to be the most cockiest son-of-a-bitch to ever exist. Tell me somebody else who's been bullied, in the middle of a nasty divorce, beaten up by his mother a few times, raped, did drugs, and shot 14 times. Please...tell me. I look at Vega and my eyes widen in realization. He's right again. He just straight up told me that a lot of shit has happened to me, and I'm still here. Yeah, I deserve to do what I want when I want, and how I want. I chuckle, knowing these facts. -Leon- There isn't a fucken person alive who's been through the exact same shit I've been through. There never will be again either, because nobody else has what it takes to go through such cataclysmic events and survive! -Supernova- Exactly. Because of that, you deserve to be the arrogant sumbitch you are. And yeah, you're a warped individual. So am I. Big deal. We just like to do things our way. Besides, I myself, wouldn't have it any other way. -Leon- Yeah. Thank you for telling me that Vega. Because you're right. I don't give a fuck about what others have to say about me. Never have, and I never fucken will. The both of us meet eye to eye. He smiled, as the message I have sent to him, was sort of a thank you, for helping me realize that I simply don;t give a fuck about what I do, or who gets harmed. Thank you my old friend. But as I realize all of this, I am getting pumped up. Now is the perfect time to tell the world my thoughts, on both the match, and Crash. And my intentions. With all that I have been thinking about today, There is a lot to say to him. -Leon- Vega. I was planning on telling the world everything about this match today, once I was done with my thoughts. Thanks to you, that process has sped up. Please, take my camera and tripod, from beside me,and record what I must say. It is...of upmost importance. -Supernova- Gladly. I grab the equipment, and hand it to him. While he sets it up, I take out a picture of Zack Crash, and take a good look at what I am up against. Another historical day, towards my destiny of total world domination is upon me, and upon Zack's defeat, I just know it. I know he's a fool for saying such things, about me being saved upon my defeat. But he does not know what lengths I will go to, to ensure that I will not be the one who's saying those words. While I have no plan to say those submissive words to anyone, it doesn't hurt to have some insurance. Which is why I'm making sure that Vega is around, for he and I both know, that when the opportunity comes, he will strike. -Supernova- You ready. -Leon- Yeah. Let's do this. I see him adjust the camera, until he's satisfied with the angle. Once he's done, he pressed the record button, and gives me the signal to start. This is it. Time to get down to business. I can feel my anticipation levels rise, as I begin to speak. -Leon- Well, looks like we're here at last. Crowning Glory 4. quite possibly the biggest event in all of MWE's history, and to be honest, that's saying a lot. It is a dream event to many, in the sense we never thought it would happen, when MWE closed down again. But here we are, once again. For me...I have been looking forward to this for a long time. I scowl at the camera. The reason being, that the long time, is actually only a couple months. A couple of months since I did lose to Crash. I will not forget about it. It is goof fuel to this fire though. And the fire, that I can feel dancing within me, grows stronger yet, just thinking about all the limitations being off in this match. -Leon- To me, this is my chance, to show that I'm who I say I am. When I say I am a ruthless bastard who only cares about violence. What better way to prove it, then to make somebody else squirm in pain, begging for their very life. Which is what it is for me and Zack Crash. For the past few months, me and him have feuded. Light against dark, good, against evil. But really, it all depends on who you believe is right, or wrong, truly is. It's all about perspective. I have admitted that my actions are dark, and not for the faint of heart. Cold and violent. But does that make me a bad person? To you tapeworms and leeches that suck off the religious and moral ballsacks, I suppose it does. To me, I am just being true to myself, and yeah, I'm making others see it my way. Because really, my way, IS the right way. I give the world watching my trademark smirk. Something I do when I'm feeling very confident in both my words and actions. -Leon- But really, to me, I could honestly give a fuck about what I do being right or wrong. Why should I have to care about the consequences of my actions? If anyone had to experience half the shit I went through, maybe they would have a remote understanding of why I do what I do. That is why I am above such nonsense about morals and law. They only hold me back from me being me. Yes. I have admitted it. I am truly a chaotic being, who's only goal is to bring more and more chaos as I go along. See, it's all about wanting. Before, I have let my own ego, the one thing that's bigger then my lust for violence, get in the way of what I wanted, because I truly did believe that I was better then everyone. No...scratch that. I AM...better then everyone else. But now...things are going to be a lot...ALOT...worse. I lean forward, still smirking, and even crack my knuckles in anticipation. Oh these people have no idea just what they're in for. And really, they are in for a treat. For they get to see that there is such things are me doing a lot worse then what I've done before. -Leon- And with Crowning Glory Four coming up, what better way to prove it. A refresher, if you will. Last year, I brutalized and retired Chris England, in what would be a start to a violent series of dominance. Last yeah, I retired a total of three people in less than two months. Chris England, Jay Williams, and Mason Resoan. I made three names that either were huge in MWE, or promised to be huge, all nothing in a matter of seconds. And I will do so the same this year. This year, might be a battle that could be all to easy for me to win. For in the match I am in, there are no rules, no limits, and no repercussions. Only when the opponent throws up the figurative 'white flag', is when it's all over. That...or the officials declare him dead. Wouldn't surprise me if I decided to take things that far, because I simply didn't want to deal with him again. I can feel my blood starting to burn now. One of my self induced adrenaline rushes has kicked in. Both in anger and anticipation. Main reason I can't wait to see Zack Crash squirm like a bitch, is because I feel as if I'm constantly reminded about my failures when I look at his mug. I hate it. But soon, it won't matter. -Leon- To say I've been looking forward to this match is an understatement. I have patiently waited for the day that I would make my last loss against Zack Crash meaningless, and to take what will be rightfully mine. The MWE world heavyweight championship. Zack Crash. You'll find that this match will be far different then our last one in UUW. You'll find, that there is no way you could have exploited my one true weakness, to escape. This is a corner you backed yourself into. This time around, I'm going to prolong your suffering. To the most extreme. Like I did with your friend, Kent Bishop. Yeah, I had one of my lackies capture him. I had him captive for a few days. Don't think I just let him be. I used him as a personal punching bag. Although I was merciful, as I needed him alive. But the screams that were made by him, as he begged for mercy each time that he was being beaten up, It made my blood turn hot with adrenaline, and only egged me to continue. Oh it may not have looked like it from afar, but when you got up close, after all the branding I did to him, you did see the bruises and cuts. After that, I released him. My original intention was to get you to hate me. Seems that even now, you don't. You refuse to hate me. But yet, you acknowledge that I am right. That will only do it for a certain amount of time. I'm sure that those who are watching will feel disgusted in what I have just said. Even Crash himself. I feel almost as if I can sense their displeasure towards me. -Leon- Crash. There is something that has been bugging me, just a little bit though. You keep saying that...you're going to save me. Well I can tell you that's the single most stupidest thing I've ever heard come out of your mouth. Noble...perhaps. But irreversibly stupid, nevertheless. Because Crash, let me ask you this. How the fuck can you save me, when there is nothing to be saved? When everything is the way it's supposed to be, there is no so-called...saving...to be done. Maybe, at one time, in my former life, I would have agreed. But now...why should I? You mention that while you do have darkness within you, you have control over it, and you claim that is what makes you better then me. Well Crash...you are dead wrong. I will tell you this right now Crash. Control is for the weak. Control is for cowards. Afraid of what they are capable of doing. As you can see...I'm not one of those losers. I welcome the thirst for blood. I welcome the urge to go and hurt people, for no apparent reason. I embrace the high I get when I see the scared looks on people's faces when I approach them with the intent to kill, and maybe, within the moments of their own lives being taken away by me. Like I told Vega, control is for pussies. Sitting back in my chair again, I briefly chuckle, remembering some of the times of those who I have greatly hurt. Even those who wronged me first. The thought of their screams, begging for mercy, even when they know they're not going to get it, makes me smile even more. But once I am done thinking about it, ending my brief breather from me addressing this match of ours, I lean forward again, ready to explain more as I go along. -Leon- A very good friend of mine...one that you crash, know oh so well...reminded me that I truly am above all others. Especially you Crash. I can explain all that I have been through, but you already know the stories. What about you Crash? You lost your mother. Big fucken deal. Mothers aren't as great as everyone claims them to be. So really Crash, I don't feel sorry for you at all. But there is one thing that I am curious about. What is it that caused you to develop this side of you, that I'm trying to bring out? I think it could be connected with your mother, if you were very close to her. Death can hit people hard. Makes them bitter. Makes them seek an escape. Or maybe, it fills them with rage and anger. So perhaps, I may just have figured you out Crashy-boy. With the death of your mother, you became bitter, but refused to show it, and thus, you had to compensate, by becoming an adrenaline junkie. Well....more if you were before. Off camera, I hear Vega chuckling himself. He just couldn't hold it in anymore. I'm not surprised that even he would share my enjoyment. After a minute, he does calm down, and excuses himself from his sudden outburst. -Leon- But ever since we started this, ever since I personally have hunted you down, you have been giving into the true self of you. The one who simply puts...wants to show me that you're worthy of my time. Yes, I can see it. I see the chaotic being that you truly are Crash, and I like what I see. But why am I focusing so much on you. Well the answer lies within somebody who you have permanently hurt. Yeah. I'm talking about Supernova. How your actions, and not apologizing for them, caused him to go on a rampage. We've heard the excuses, but I for one don't buy them, as I know deep down, you enjoyed the sound of your knee breaking his orbital bone. You smirked like a madman when you found out that he would have permanent eye damage. You creamed yourself when you took his spot to become the champion at the place where you guys worked at. But most importantly, I can see the look in your eyes when he's mentioned, as your eyes display a taunting expression, and you loved the fact that you fucked his girlfriend. That is why I believe that what you try to show people, is false. The baby kissing, sweet talking, philosophical, stunt performing, hero figure you put infront of the camera, is all but a hoax. One which will be exposed. I close my eyes for a second, trying to pace myself. I was getting to worked up again, but this time, it isn't so much in anger. I can't help myself sometimes. Although, not that I really want to. -Leon- I've let all my intentions out on the table. No amount of physical, mental, or emotional preparation will prepare you for what you're about to go through. You had this coming for a long time. Because when you utter those two words, you'll admit to me, and to everyone else, that you're inferior, and that not even the raging monster inside of you was able to overcome me. Because in the end Crash, I know that is what I'm going to get. Your true self has come forth. I know that when you see your reflection, that I am the man in the mirror that you see. Because it was I, who got inside your head, giving you nightmares about who you truly are. You've been infected Crash. Oh how I have been waiting to use that line on somebody ever since I adopted the 'Virus' nickname. It's almost as if everyone was just waiting for it as well. But I know that the meaning behind it is true. Being that I'm basically haunting his every thoughts, he's basically been infected, and it's taking him over. I chuckle, knowing the truth behind those words is what's happening as I'm doing this video. -Leon- This is it Crash. You and me, one last time. No rematches, no going back. Heading out of this match, the both of us will leave better then when we came in. I can guarantee that. You will thank me later Crash, when I open your eyes, and show you, that the only sensible way to live, is without morals and rules. Embrace this Crash, for when you're in hell, you'll find that if you don't, you'll suffer far worse then you ever have before. Welcome to my world Crash. This is the ultimate Adrenaline rush. And come Corwning glory Crash.... You will.... Burn.... in.... HELL!!!!!! I chuckle like the madman that I am. I could even feel the adrenaline starting to leave me, as I casually sit back in my chair. I meant every fucken word of what I said. Zack Crash truly has no idea what's coming to him. If he thought facing me inside Hell in the Cell was bad, well he's in for a rude awakening. But at the end of the night, I know that i'll have gained both a championship...and a new slave. The thought of it all, is quite....orgasmic. Taking the photo I have of Crash, I get up, and put one corner of the picture against the flames, and it catches fire, quickly burning the picture into nothing more then ash. His fate...has been decided. Come Crowning Glory Four, Zack Crash and I, will dance in the flames of hell. END
