Keeping my head above the rest.

Sep 6 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Alex Bowen is standing in a tall room, there are towels over about 10 box looking things in the room. He has his black trench coat on but just a black pair of slacks on under it. He stands in the room drinking a beer and smirking at the camera. Finally the door opens and the camera turns to see David Bowen walking through the door with a case of beer and a chair in one hand, he pushes the camera man to a side and sets down the case of beer and puts up the chair and sets down. Alex finishes his beer and sandman style crushes it a few times aganst his head. Alex- Pain is a fleeting emotion. Its a dream Sinc, you should know that by now. Still you threaten myself and my brother. David- A foolish mistake indeed. Alex laughs and reaches down and grabs another beer and cracks it open. Alex- You say you have a new plan for wrestiling. What is it? I guess your to much of a chickenshit to tell us all. My plan, no the Burning legions plan is to clense the mwe as a whole of people like you. Shit talkers, snitches, assholes in general. Our plan was set into action in another promotion called Apwc, It went through without a hitch. In 3 weeks we were tag team champions. I myself beat both of dead end, both seven foot monsters. Single handedly, Sinc have you ever beaten two men back to back that are over 7 feet tall? Its no eazy task to say the least. My brother and I then proceded to beat the living shit out of them in a casket match, and take there titles. Holding them for allmost 3 months. David- Ahh yes what a glorious time it was, havoc, destruction, pain. A great time to all but the losers. David gets up and grabs the camera and looks into it then starts to shake. David- You call my brother a weakling? He carried your tag match. You know it and so do I, Your a fucking washed up peace of shit Sinc. I'm going to make sure Alex has no wepons on his person when he comes down for the match in a few days. David scratches his head. David- Why You may ask? Becuase I want to prove a point, One member of a team is only as strong as his partner. I want to prove to you and mwe that the burning legion is nothing to fuck with.... David hands the camera back to the cameraman and looks at Alex who is setting back in the corner sipping his beer in peace. David- And after he beats you, I'll come down and beat the shit out of you myself. I'll be setting in the locker room as I am now. Enjoying myself, But I've had enough of your shit, I need somone to take it out. And If mwe dosen't like it they can go fuck themself's. Alex- Very true brother. Sinc you call me week, And I also saw you like to fuck with venom. Well So do I buddie, I'm quite a fan of facing other peoples fears head on. Alex pulls off a towel of a box, It turns out to be a fishtank full of scorpians. He takes off the top and puts his hand in running it through the scorpians. He dosen't get stung. Alex- I'm a masochist Sinc, I'm the only man right now in mwe that has put his body totaly on the line. I love pain, so far no one has been able to take me fully to the limit. I've messed up my sholder 3 times, granted it hurts constantly. I've had docters, family, friends, and even my old home jcw tell me it was time to put it up. Hell I was fired from jcw because I woulden't retire after my 3 surgery, what makes you sinc mercer, think your suck tough shit now? You have more people that hate you then you think Sinc. You should allways try to make friends when you have hatred surrounding you. I have the biggest man in Mwe as my friend. He's really the only one I need. Alex reaches back and pulls off another towel. A smaller fishtank with 4 rosehair tarantula. Alex takes off the top and grabs on and lets it crawl up his arm. It ends up on his sholder feeling his neck with its front legs. Alex- Harmless spiders thats a fact. I love rosehairs, there so pretty. But this next one isn't quite. She cost me allmost 2000 dollers. From asia I would love to introduce you to a cobalt blue tarantula. David gets up and takes off a towel from behind the rose hair tank. A rather large blue spider is trying to climb the sides of the tank to no avail. He grabs the spider by its back legs and it rolls up in a ball. He places it in Alex's hand. Alex pokes it on its head to get it to roll out. Its as big as his hand, It sort of freaks out and bites Alex twice. Alex- There there little one, nothings wrong. Pretty isn't she? Sinc she's just like you, big talk but nothing but a small painless bite. Sinc your just like this spider in a way, Your in the palm of my hand just waiting to be crushed. But I'll save that for you not this spider, She actually entertains me. Unlike you with your petty insults. Alex pets the side of the spider and walks her back over to the side of the tank and slides it inside its home. He grabs the rosehair and does the same, then closes the top of the tanks. He turns around and faces the camera. Alex- So Sinc untill then this is me, come and get some. Because I'm not afraid of you like most of mwe seems to be. And James after I beat sinc, I'm comming for you. That title belt is mine. Alex starts to walk out of the room and David gets up and follows. He clicks the light switch on the way out.
