To Kill a Mother****er - The Brian Michaels Story

Mar 5 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
There is an eerie silence as MWE cameras fade into a funeral. There is a black casket, with red roses and blue flowers laid near it. A breeze in the air causes the flowers to begin flying away. There is a priest, gray-haired and wearing a black robe, standing at a podium next to the casket. The camera fades out to show that there are hundreds of seats, but only three are occupied. One is taken by Richard Covell, whose once slick facial features are now a pool of dismay and sorrow. He is wearing a warm, black trenchcoat and slacks. His position is slumped, as if all the energy had been sucked out of him. On the opposite side of him, sits Randy Covell. The youngster is making his first funeral visit and his green eyes are taken back at the emotion of it all. He remains fixated on the swaying of the roses back and forth along with the wind. It all feels surreal to him. In the middle, the oldest of the Covell children, sits Brian Michaels. His usually vibrant green eyes are filled with sadness. His black tuxedo makes him feel uncomfortable as the priest leans to say the funeral’s opening words. Priest: Heavenly father, may you bless the soul of Lisa Michaels and all of His children… The sermon is interrupted by a loud, awkward sound. At first guess, you may think it is airplanes flying over the scene, however the camera pans to show Brian Michaels, reddened facial features and flared up nostrils, yelling angrily and throwing the sea of empty chairs together, colliding in a huge bang that shocks the priest. Randy Covells’ jaw drops, and Richard looks on in amazement as well. The camera shows that Michaels has taken one of the chairs, and with the leg, written a large ‘’F U C K Y O U’’ in the now-torn grass. He angrily mutters to himself as the Covells’ rise to their feet in their designer suits. Covell: Brian…wha…what are you doing? He walks over to Covell, who is slightly shorter than he is, and stares at him with green eyes of mystique. He winks at the elder Covell, but Covell does not understand what it means. Michaels simply dons his signature grin and walks up to the podium where the still-shell shocked priest awaits. Michaels then precedes to punching the old priest directly on his temple. He nearly falls over, and manages to hang on to the podium. The former MWE Champion shakes his head in disgust before rearing back and hitting him with an Ace of Spades. Michaels: That…was for Sinc Mercier. Randy stands up awkwardly, and begins to clap with great enthusiasm. The elder Covell threatens to backhand him, so Randy once again sits down. Michaels gives an effort to hide his sadness with a menacing, monotone voice at first, but his vibrant shaking at being so near the casket of his own flesh and blood brings him back to reality. At first, he ignores his mother, not even glancing in the direction of the pitch-black casket. Michaels: First order of business, this is not a heel promo and this is not a babyface promo. I am about to put my heart and my soul into the following words today, so I’d appreciate it, if Mr.Covell would shut, the fuck up. Richard eyebrows perk up in bedazzlement. Randy continues to look at his older brother, clearly curious as to what he has planned for them today. The three-time MWE Champion clears his throat and begins to speak in a now eloquent tone, as opposed to his gruffy style the last time he spoke. Michaels: If you do not abide by the rules stated earlier, you will make like D.C. and get the fuck out. The winds swirl around in the background, and the birds begin to chirp and fly away. Several bells are heard, coming from another funeral going on about a half-mile away. The once-beautiful sunny day in Dublin has now transcended into a dark and chilling atmosphere. The setting is now zoomed out, as the camera gets a full-face view of Michaels. Michaels: Ace Cannon. My baby brother. Richard cannot help but give a ‘’WOO!’’, feeling proud of his middle child. Randy adjusts his position in his chair, now slumped. Michaels: Well…let’s look at his accomplishments, or lack thereof. Cannon, I know you’re listening to this…and I know you’re saying, I’ve heard this shit before…but guess what? I’m not going to talk about your accomplishments, because you don’t have many. He cranks his neck, then shrugs his shoulders for a second. Richard looks on, puzzled, wondering why Michaels isn’t giving Cannon a major plug and promotion. Michaels: See, your “accomplishments” are as follows…You had a three-some with a girl that Dallas fucked and I fucked, and with another girl who already fucked D.C., therefore you fucked two ALREADY fucked girls, which gives you a… Michaels pretends to count his fingers and thumb, before apparently coming to a final conclusion. Michaels: 95.45% chance at having Gonorrhea, 83.27% chance at having “Herps”, and a 99.99% chance of you having too limp of a dick to wrestle, piss, or….ever sleep with a woman again. He gives a bit of a chuckle, as Randy busts out laughing, and begins to roll on the floor laughing. Richard stands up angrily, and kicks him in the back. Covell: Get off that ground NOW. You are not a juvenile delinquent like your so called brother. The camera pans back to Michaels, who is brushing dirt off his tuxedo with a certain swagger and style that could only be dictated by the Career Stopper himself. He confidently puts his hand on the podium and speaks up amid the now-screeching winds. Several of the roses begin to fly away from Lisa’s casket. Michaels: As for Paige…trust me, I NEVER let her dictate. After all, all she could tell me is how she DICK-TAKE! Randy nearly falls to the ground again, but remembers what his father had told him moments before. He brushes himself off non-chalantly and rises to his seat again. Michaels: But, lemme get back to your self-proclaimed accomplishments…You have been successful in never lasting more than three months at a wrestling promotion. CONGRATULTIONS! You have punted a little demon girl, which apparently makes you a badass. you have been murdered on the mic AND in the ring by Joey Dallas, a guy whose claim to fame is coming THAT close to beating David Whitman. Michaels emphasizes the “that” in the final sentence, and uses it in a sharp and cutting sarcastic tone. He continues to ramble however, not allowing Cannon’s father, Richard Covell to intervene on the part of his son. Michaels: You won a World Championship in a promotion where THE HIGH KID was your TOUGHEST competition, you haven’t won a singles belt in MWE and I’m supposed to take you seriously? I’m supposed to believe you’re hot shit because you beat David Whitman? Do you want a cookie for having someone come in, and do the work for you? He reaches into the back pocket of his jeans and playfully takes out a chocolate chip cookie. He flaunts it in the air like a stripper would playfully dance on someone’s lap, only to leave them in the dust. He breaks the cookie in half and puts the cookie near the camera, only to throw it to the ground, and stomp on it repeatedly. Michaels: Do I piss you off? How does it feel, to have someone doubt your accomplishments, hm, Ace? How does it FEEL…to know that no one believes in you. I mean, how many times, do I have to repeat it? YOU NEVER CLEANLY BEAT WHITMAN. Whereas…I have. I want it, to eat at you like Paige eats pussy, mkay? He puts two fingers on his lips and spreads them out, as if to motion that he were licking a you-know-what. Michaels: Also, I absolutely love how Cannon tells people that he left Rev-Pro and EW because of “creative differences” B*TCH PLEASE! Your ass couldn’t get a title shot if you deepthroated Maxximillion Mustafa, and you DAMN well know that. Michaels pretends to deepthroat a penis, making stroking motions (think HBK in DX). Michaels: Is this promo TOO EMO ENOUGH FOR YOU YET, ACE? Is this the part where I’m supposed to cry about you being the football god in college? The part where you become the big man on campus at USC? Michaels rubs his hand on his chin, pondering sarcastically for a few seconds. He laughs it off, wagging his finger. Michaels: That’s right…big man on campus…girls…beer…parties. That is, until you went and pulled a Matt Leinart, and you just couldn’t make it past REAL strength, and REAL grit in the pros. That’s right...Ace Cannon is the Matt Leinart of MWE. Covell rises to his feet, and throws his arms up in the air, as if to say enough. He angrily walks up to the podium, making it the only barrier in between the two massive tempers, and sticks his long, bony finger right at Michaels’ forehead. Covell: You listen to ME, boy. We came to a funeral, and that’s what we’re going to DO. ENOUGH of this ranting, this is about the woman we loved… Michaels’ eyes freeze. He stands there in a single, lucid moment of reflection, reminiscing the times he had spent with his now-deceased mother. He blanks out, and ignores everything Covell is saying, flashing back to the one moment where his life was changed forever… QUOTE Michaels’ thought process is chugging like the train that could, trying to put the bits and pieces of it all together. He remembers the fateful night where his father had been doing the bills and his mother was complaining about having to be both a housewife and having to work down at the firm. A young, ten year old Michaels watches on as the argument progresses. Covell: Why don’t you get your ass to a bigger job and make some more damn money woman?! The construction isn’t paying well and we’re going through a goddamn RECESSION! You gotta come up with a way to make more than 15k a year. Lisa: Richard Lawrence Covell, I SLAVE all day, cleaning YOUR clothes, fixing up YOUR shoes, working part-time AND having to help OUR son with his homework…and you have the nerve to tell me that I need to do more?! Richard walks up to her, with a very conniving gleam to him. He is shaking, almost as if he were drunk. Covell: Why don’t you work as a prostitute? Michaels tries his damndest to remember the rest, but cannot get it into his skull what exactly happened next. However, he refocuses his attention to Covell, his father. Michaels: Who are you, to be at this wedding? Covell is taken back, rather shocked at the question, and takes it almost as a statement. He refuses to answer and there is a deathly silence for moments. Covell: It’s amazing…I’m the reason you’re even here. I gave that woman the best years of her life and you got the nerve to talk to me like that? There is a tense, emotion-filled feeling between the two. Randy meanwhile, sits back, not knowing what to say, as he was not around at the time. Michaels slowly walks near the casket, admiring and absorbing all of his deceased parent’s features. Her once enthusiastic and outgoing blue eyes, closed shut. Her lips, curved like Angelina Jolie’s hips, pursed shut as well. Michaels shakes for a second, before quietly putting his right finger over her red lips. The camera zooms out to show her full body, as she is dressed in her Sunday best, a long black skirt and her favorite white blouse. Michaels: Let me tell you something, dad… Again, he uses the word ‘’dad’’ with a sarcastic and cutting voice. Michaels; You know that book, To Kill a Mockingbird? Richard nods, recognizing the popular book/movie. Michaels: Well…on Monday night…I’m going to write my own book. It’s called, to Kill a Motherfucker. The motherfucker will be played by none other than Ace “A tarnished victory means nothing” Cannon. AND GUESS WHOS’ GOING TO PLAY OJ SIMPSON, BITCH?!?! Randy rises like they are at church and Michaels is the gospel speaker. He throws his hands in the air and waves them like he doesn’t care. He dances around a bit as well. Randy: YOU ARE! PRAISE JE-SUS! Michaels points a finger at Randy and nods his head. Michaels: You’re DAMN RIGHT. His playful pitch takes a turn for the worse, as he focuses back on his mother in her pale skin. Michaels keeps his finger over her bright red lips, as if he is telling her not to speak. Michaels: In all seriousness…there is one thing that Ace Cannon cannot take from me. Amidst all the talk of politics, and ass-kissing…there is one thing that he’ll never, ever take from me. He puts his left arm and beats his chest in a gleaming way, proud of his accomplishments. Michaels: That’s my self-esteem…and he will never, ever take that from me. So…no matter how many times, Cannon bitches about politics…or me being “Daddy’s Favorite”… He uses two fingers as if to quote Daddy’s Favorite. Michaels: The fact remains, that you, Ace Cannon, are a failure at wrestling. You succeed in the minor leagues, but you cannot make it in the big-time at MWE, and THAT’S where your anger is stemming from. The thought that your MASSIVE ego cannot stand, not beating Whitman cleanly, is where it stems from. Anger, has turned you into the sick fuck you claim you are today. Michaels points to the two sides of his head with his fingers, as if to say that Cannon belongs in a mental asylum. Michaels: But I know deep down inside…you just want a shot. You just want be like D.C., and like David Whitman…and that’s why you’re so bitter. You can’t stand the sight of me, being better, than you. Monday…you’re going to have to witness that image, over, and over again. You want to get sick? He seemingly beings to beat his brains out, punching himself squarely in the temple and slightly above his left eyebrow. There is a steady trail of blood being to boil. Michaels: This, is my legacy. You want to take the gimmick that I created and use it against me? We’ll get bloody. You want to wear Dallas’ blood? I’ll wear yours. Michaels raises his right hand from the lips of his mother, and tells Randy and Richard to back away for a moment alone. The elder Covell shakes his head no and rises up. Covell: No. I want a moment alone with her. Michaels’ head fuzzes up, as he begins to shake with anger and passion. It runs down his backbone as the memory he was having earlier is starting to be jogged back into his head. QUOTE Lisa: What’d you say to me? Covell eases up, then aggressively gets into Lisa’s face. She looks half-intimidated, but manages to stand her ground. Covell growls and spits in her face. The young Michaels stands back in shock. Covell: You already look the part… She attempts to slap him, before he throws his wrist in the way. He puts his hand at her throat and begins to strangle her slowly. Covell: Don’t you hit me, you little fucking slut. Michaels looks on still, silenced. This is clearly a traumatic moment, and Lisa is stirren. She falls faintly from the strangling and leans against the kitchen counter, her eyes closed. Her once-beaming blonde hair is now a disarray of strands covering her face. She whimpers, before breaking down into a faint cry. His eyes widen as he comes to the slow realization that Richard Covell caused his mother to kill herself. He doesn’t know how to react at first, looking over her facial features once more, before walking from the casket to Covell. They are both standing and looking at each other eye-to-eye in a fierce way. Michaels speaks up first, speaking in a low whisper, which eventually grows to resonate across the graveyard. Michaels: Domestic trauma, domestic trauma, domestic trauma, domestic trauma, domestic trauma. His haunting voice almost awakens the souls of the dead. There is literally, a feeling of corpses rolling over, as Michaels speaks in a possessed-type of voice. Michaels: Who are you…to be at this wedding? Covell throws his hand up to answer the question, but is cut off by an increasingly angered Brian Michaels. Michaels: Murderer. He shakes, trying not to let the pure emotion get the best of him. Randy Covell quietly sneaks away from the intense moment as Richard goes to rebut. Covell: I, I don’t know what you’re talking about. Michaels points to a far away location, and starts shaking his hand. Michaels: Go…unless you want to end up like everybody else….with a corpse that rots, and a soul too heartless to go to heaven. He says this with an emphatic tone. Covell gleams his eyes and squints in a challenging manner, but recognizes that he cannot keep up with the younger Michaels. He begins to slowly back away. He goes far, until he is out of camera view. Michaels, knowing that he is alone, again slowly walks towards the casket of his mother. His eyes redden at the sight of her, with her body laying in such a peaceful manner that is comparable to the waves of the sea. So at peace, he thinks. Michaels: What can I say…you were there for me when things looked bleak. You…you promised me the world. Hush little baby, don’t say a word…mom is going to buy you a mockingbird. He peacefully rings her finger to the beat of his words, as he thinks of the pain she had to witness during his childhood. He wonders if this was because of him. Michaels: And if that mockingbird don’t sing, mom is going to buy you a diamond ring. He puts her finger down near her chest, and reaches under her, clutching her in his arms. He tears up, holding his mother in his arms for the final time. Michaels: I love you, mom…you taught me to keep my self-esteem, and I won’t let Ace Cannon take that from me. He lays her down peacefully for the final time. He uses his black tuxedo to clean his tears a bit, but his eyes remain red in pain. He looks up at the sky, and puts his finger to it. Michaels: Tim Fitzgerald. You are the only reason I am here today. Your belief, in the ability to be a more perfect human being, has struck a chord. As a rookie in MWE, I didn’t understand what you meant by improvement. I thought we were only as perfect as God made us. He nods his head, and closes his eyes, thinking back to his last conversation with Tim where they discussed MWE and its progress. Michaels: I realize now that I am only as perfect as I can be. This is what I will strive to be for the rest of my life…perfect. He clutches his finger back in, and beats his chest for the final time. Michaels: Rest in peace, brother. The camera fades as Michaels is on one knee, bowing to the great Tim Fitzgerald’s legacy. Static. OOC: I know the tone of this roleplay was not the best for a tribute show. I know the "Kill a Motherfucker" line may be taken out of context because it's a show about someone who died. I just want to let people know that Tim was one of my best friends, and I respect him too much. I feel like the line isn't toeing the line, and while it may not be appropriate, I will not let it limit my creativity, the irony of being at a funeral as well.
