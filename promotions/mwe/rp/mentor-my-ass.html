I mean, if you are going to run-down the roster...do it in fashion. *Mason smiles with his cocky grin again, smirking in the inside at Michaels attempts to be bada**.* Mason: Seriously, you run-down the roster...and all you do is make yourself look like a fool. They weren't creative attacks...they were not attacks that cut deep...and there was nothing to back up the ideas that you expressed. On the other hand, you have me, who runs down the roster, targeting all the weaknesses of my opponents and peers. I didn't leave room for error...instead of acting like a twelve year old and making fun of people I resented or hated, I actually made them hate themselves. I exposed their flaws...I didn't give myself another flaw. And this is all hilarious, because guess who is given credit for a good run-down...Brian Michaels is the correct answer if you didn't guess it already. *Mason, for the second time in five minutes, looked back at the hospital bed, and saw it in that nasty look. He turned to the cameras, with the same look, as he continued to talk about Brian Michaels.* Mason: But enough of this stupid act. We have seen it all before, and to be quite frank...it is getting old. So old, in fact, that it is quite annoying. Agrivating almost. *Mason gets that crazy look on his face, and we start to see his head lean forward.* Mason: You sit there in your little world, thinking that everyone revolves around you...you think you are so much better than everyone else...and you actually believe that you mean something. Well, I have got a news flash for you...this sharade of yours isn't going to last very long. You, Michaels, are not...and I repeat NOT what you used to be...and you never will be! *There is actually anger showing on Mason's face now, as he expresses dislike towards Brian Michaels.* Mason: You would like to think because you have three world titles under your belt that you are untouchable...and like I have said to other cocky opponents...you should really think if you deserve to be cocky. Because I just don't think you are ready for that. You think that because you can transition to a heel quickly, that you really are one. You think that being the bad-guy will help you. Well, YOU ARE NOT THE BAD GUY! *Mason is actually yelling at this point, as you see the stress across his face turn to anger. His face looks pure...but his attitude is just the opposite.* Mason: You have no idea how long I have sat and watched you go from being a wrestler looking for his wits...to a guy who doesn't want anything...to a man who strives for success. Well, it is to late to choose anymore...because I am telling you that you are not making your way back to the main event. You are not thrusting yourself where you are not wanted...and you not getting something you don't deserve. I WILL NOT LET THAT HAPPEN! You think you are heel....and it so agrivates me...because you are not...you are not a heel. You are as harmless as an infant, but you just don't realize it yet...and the stupidity that you express makes me thrive to hurt someone...a feeling I normally dont have. *Mason is expressing pure hatred now.* Mason: You think your ballsy, huh? You think that you have what it takes to beat me in a steel cage...well I think you don't have anything left to face me now. I don't think you are ready to face me...because you don't know that by enclosing me into a steel cage, that there is no running and I will find a way to break out of the steel structure...and you can believe me. If it means cutting a promo in a straight jacket, like I have been doing it...but if it means... *Mason walks to the wall, and runs and kicks the hospital bed into the wall. He quickly follows that up by jumping into the wall, knocking down shelves of expensive equipment, and watching them crash to floor.* Mason: BREAKING SOME SHIT OR HURTING SOME PEOPLE...I CAN DO THAT TOO! *Mason just screams, before walking to the door, raising a foot and kicking it in. He really looks like a crazy person now, as he runs down the hall, dress in surgeon pants and a straight jacket, leaving carnage behind him. He runs down the hall still, not caring what people think, and if people get in his way, he plowed them over. Running now, full sprint, he shoved several doctors into the wall, before making his way to a staircase, in which he sped down. People dodged out of the way, as he made great leaps down the the stairs, looking like a seriously loopy guy now. Some people even screaming, so naturall when he reached the hospitals bottom floor, people were looking for the ruckus. Without missing a beat, Mason kept running, all the way out of the building, to the parking lot, in which people had now started following him. Some tried to catch him, mainly the staff of the hospital, but most just trailed this massive man. However, as some of the personal tried to close in on him, he made a b-line to the cars. Finding an open bed of a truck, he hopped up into it, using the roof of the truck as a step to launch his farther away, nowing drawing some serious attention. Car hopping, the crowd now grew to at least 50 people, and this looked exceptionally big for a hospital parking lot. Eventually, he reached a white van, launched off the roof of another car, and stood on top of it, looking around at the bystanders to his crazy-like actions. On top of the truck, he stopped, and made that cocky grin.* Mason: Michaels...people wanted different...and that is what I am giving them. Dick Malibu wanted to put me in a steel cage with you...and that is what he is going to get...but don't think I am not prepared...because whether you want to believe it or not...I AM READY FOR THIS SUNDAY! *People look at him in awe and some in bewilderment. He ignores them all though.* Mason: Michaels, this Sunday, you will see what I can really do...and if you think I need to actually train to even the playing field...then you are more naieve than I actually thought. This Sunday, you will realize that I am more brutal than you ever tried to teach me. This Sunday, Mason Resoan goes insane inside a 15-foot high steel structure...and you happen to be in my way. *Mason makes a final survey of the crowd...before dislocating his shoulder (Lethal Weapon 2 style), slipping out of the straight jacket, and throwing it down.* Mason: Michaels...that was me escaping the cage. *Mason raises his now good hand in the air.* Mason: That is me celebrating my third victory against you! *Mason smiles one final cocky grin.* Mason: AND THIS IS ME TELLING YOU THAT I AM NOT CRAZY! I AM A GENIUS! *There is a final pause, as Mason does not look at the crowd, but the sky.* Mason: And if ya didn't know...now ya know! *And the screen fades to black as a satisfied Mason takes a breath.*
