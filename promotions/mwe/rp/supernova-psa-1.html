Supernova PSA #1

Jul 22 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Is there somebody who can be your best friend one minute, and your worst enemy the next? Nobody really thinks it's possible. However, there are people out there that are capable of that. Whether they are bipolar, or just have a natural split personality. May sound the same, but there is a difference One such person, is 'Supernova' Vega Knight. As we all know in MWE, his history here's been kinda twisted. He started out as an extremely popular as just himself, but then, he did a 180, and became a total psychopath, known only as Oblivion. Of course, for the longest time, people didn't know it was him. Only when he felt he exacted revenge against somebody who pushed him over the edge, did he return as himself. But it's a new chapter in the young wrestler's life. He has not one, but two girlfriends. Sisters in fact. But the thing is, one of them, the one he is closer to, did something horrible to Supernova one time, and to this day, feels the wrath of him. Beaten, abused, and locked away. Just some of the things done to her. But of course, we all know that story. At this present day, Supernova is currently on his way to do some business for MWE. The destination he was going to was a small warehouse just outside of the industrial district of the city where he lived. He had bought it, and often used it to film stuff, or as a little hideout. It was only a half hour drive, but it was still something very important to do. Parking his thunderbird right up front, Supernova got out of the car, before locking it up. He then opened the door to get into hi little hideout. He noticed his personal cameraman was passed out on the floor. This wasn't a good thing. Supernova's right eye twitched, before he would punt the guy in the ribs. The cameraman saw him, after trying to recover from Supernova's hard kick. He got up, and went behind the camera. Supernova took off his trench coat, and chucked it aside. -Supernova- Explain to me, why you were passed out, on the floor, drunk....and you didn't invite me! -Cameraman- Sorry sir, but I did try, but you never answered. I assumed you were in another three way. -Supernova- Okay. I likely was. But you knew that we had to do shit today. Next time we're recording something, make sure you invite me along. Or at least, don't get into the good stuff. This is your only warning! The cameraman nodded, understanding that what he did was not the wisest of ideas. He began to get everything set up. Supernova stretched his arms out, preparing himself for the promo he was going to cut. The setting of the shoot right now, is just a blue screen area, so that images can be inserted as he goes. The cameraman gives the signal, and Supernova begins his MWE re-debut promo. -Cameraman- Ready? We're rolling in 3....2..... -Supernova- This is Supernova, with a Public Service Announcement, brought to you, by non other, then MWE, who completely condones resurrection from the grave, more then once. The logo for MWE is seen in the background of his head, as that is where it's zoomed up right now. -Supernova- Are you tired of the same old crap that you have to hear week in and week out by the same generic brand wrestlers? Do you need a lethal injection of excietment, you'd probably die happy? Then you're looking at the one lone exception to the majority of what wrestling has become. I mean, what hasn't been done. There is nothing new in wrestling anymore. Some guy get's thrown off the titantron...exciting to watch, but it's been done before. Crashing off the stage, overdone. People who claim to be ruthless sons of bitches. Overdone. People who act all cocky, but are really nothing more then a big pussy, that's the most overdone thing. Which is obviously why, I say, there needs to be a change in environment. Insert a new species into the jungle. And Naturally speaking, this species, is non other, then the two faced motherfucker of the decade.......SUPERNOVA! The background from what we see flashes black and white, even showing some clips of Supernova's career, both as Oblivion and as himself. Some really good spots are shown, without a doubt, before once again, stopping on the MWE logo. -Supernova- Now, all week, I've been getting various different people, from friends, to hardcore fans, to even lovers, asking me all the same question. Why come back to MWE? The answer for them, is...why not? They need me there, more then they care to admit. They need somebody who can't be planned against. Somebody, who is both, the summer, and the winter, at the same damn time. Especially since MWE is once again, back from the dead, and we need to start this shit out right. We can't afford to have whiners around, and hopefully, there will be none this time around. Those people make everyone else sick. Trust me, a lot of crap went down that caused MWE to die a second time. Which is the main difference between me and them. I don't whine and complain about my life, or the hardships of not advancing fast enough. Fuck that shit. I hide nothing. And instead of complaining, I simply just take what I want. If it's revenge I want, you're fucked. If you have a title I want, It's mine. Hell, if you got a bitch that looks totally fuckable...I'm gonna fuck her! The good, or bad thing about Supernova, as we can all assume is correct, is that you don't know what he's thinking, and thus, we shouldn't be surprised that he's merely smiling. Not smirking, smiling. He's got all the confidence within himself to actually do that. But yet at the same time, you can see the intensity in his eyes, showing he is ready for some action. But after a couple seconds, he sighs, and the intensity drops a bit, as he's become relaxed. -Supernova- But aside from that, I'm really just here to have fun. Of course, there are people who simply, piss me off. I'm not going to go into details of what happens then, because that's a story we're all familiar with. I will say this though. When it comes to certain events within people's lives, I simply don't care. I don't care if you're a millionaire, just had a kid, or even have had your family held hostage by some random douchebag, that can only be, at the most, half as epic as I am. Fuck, I could honestly care less about your own family thinking you're a loser! Oh wait. I forgot. That's the kind of person I'm facing this week. Mr. JP Smith. Somebody who's family has rejected him from day one. Beaten and abused. He claims to be strong due to that, but still, the fact he even mentions it once? Please. Spare me the fucken sob story. I simply don't care. Fuck your life outside of the ring. It's worth absolutely nothing come game day. Supernova is trying really hard to not chuckle right now, but you can hear it a slight bit from him. It's no wonder. He has all the confidence in the world about himself going into this match. the intensity in his eyes are back. He even licks his lips like the somewhat insane person that he is. -Supernova- Let's face it Mr. Smith, any chances of you becoming a worthwhile name has pretty much gone. But I won't deny you're good. You do have the heart. But not good enough man. But I suppose that's why I kinda...am actually looking forward to facing you. You have the heart for the game, but I want to show you, you need more then just a heart. You need to be the party king. You need to be the man. Walk and talki like a god. Go in with guns blazing, not giving a fuck about what happened before, but what you will make happen next. You'll see what I'm talking about, when I hurt you, inflict limitless pain, before I pin you...1...2...3! Supernova raises a finger each time he counts a number, to add some extra impact. His smirk grows wider, as he turns his entire body, showing the right side as the dominant part. That is where the smirks is most prominent. -Supernova- The real question is, how bad will I hurt you? How bad, will you be hurting in the morning? I don't even know that answer, as I'm just going along as it is. But I can tell you this. As long as you have your mind not focused on me, and doing things that will affect your focus, like possibly...chilling with friends, or whatnot....you'll only wind up hurting yourself even more. I'm sure that's the last thing any of your family, or at least what's left of them that gives a fuck, wants. More highlights from Supernova'a matches from the past show up in the background, with him hitting some amazing moves. They also show show some more of his many expressions he has been known to show towards people. As he does that, he lets out a breath he was not aware he was holding. The images finally stop, as he begins to talk again. -Supernova- The moral of this public service announcement, is that I'm showing what MWE needs. A complete overhaul. If you want MWE to truly flourish, then I'm your man. Of course, by the time I'm done, Mayhem Wrestling Entertainment will be but a name of the past. Should be now, as this place has no zing to it. Easy for a hostile takeover, by me and Virus....mostly me though. In the end, when I ultimately rule the entire place, I shall rechristian this place to an even more fitting name. Supernova Wrestling Entertainment, or SWE, for short. Now...can we have a graphic displaying that modification behind me. -Cameraman- It is sir. -Supernova- Where? I don't see it. -Cameraman- Trust me sir, it's on full display for everyone to see. -Supernova- You know, I'm not in a mood to be screwed around with right now. I got better things to do then argue about a graphic. So just display it. I don't want to see this fucken blue wall behind me. -Cameraman- Sir. It's there. I'm afraid you'll just have to accept that you can't see it right now until you watch the video. -Supernova- Alright I guess I'll-WAIT A SECOND! You just broke the 4th wall. -Cameraman- Sir, I believe you did that first, so it shouldn't matter if I did or not. -Supernova- I know I broke the 4th fucken wall. But I'm allowed to do that. You're just a cameraman. Technically, you don't have a right to say a fucken thing. But...whatever. I'm done dealing with your bullshit. The people watching this thing want to see a fucken epic pose of me infront of the SWE logo. So for the benefit for epicness, here's as ten second pose, the last image you guys will be seeing for now. And with that, we fade
