One long night...; Derf VS DC

Mar 9 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
OOC: I have this RP broken up into 4 parts. The first 2 parts were written while I was booked to be in the TerrorDome match. However, part 3 and 4 are written for my match with DC. Likwise, part 1 and 4 are both written in a different style then I am used to. So if you do feedback for me, I'd like specific feedback on the styles for part 1 and 4, so that I know if they work. Enjoy. **1** The scene opens during the tag team match between Alex Bowen/Jesse Anderson VS Chris England/Eric Derf, all four men exhausted after fighting each other. What's odd though, is that there is no sound. Nothing, that is, but the thoughts going through Eric Derfs head as the match continues. QUOTE Meanwhile, Eric gets back into his corner as Alex rolls to the outside. Bowen starts looking under the ring and pulls out a chair. What the hell is Bowen doing? This isn't a hardcore match, and I sure as hell ain't gonna let him smack me up. QUOTE Bowen walks over to Derf’s side, but Derf sees him coming and jumps off the apron and lands a dropkick, sending the chair straight to Bowen’s head! Serves him right! You mess with me, you get hurt. QUOTE Eric now jumps on Alex and starts unleashing punches left and right. Take this! Take that! You asshole! QUOTE Derf gets off Bowen to help England with Semi as they now start double teaming him. Stupid Bowen... Oh man, looks like Chris could use a hand with Jesse here... QUOTE All of a sudden, Semi just loses it and pushes his assailants off him. He hits Derf with a hard elbow to the face and spears Chris England. Ah shit, my face! Dammit that hurts! Don't show it though, don't wanna look weak... QUOTE Semi turns and reaches for the chair. Alex Bowen tries to stop Semi, but gets a stiff chair shot to the face. What the hell? He just hit his own partner? Is that a DQ? What's going on... Why is he looking at me?... Oh Shi-........................ **2** How Kevin, are they doing? Is anything serious? Well England just woke up, he's walking around someplace. Derf's got a nice cut above his left eye, nothing a few stitches can't take care off. He's still out of it. And Bowen? I haven't heard anything about him. Someone else is treating him. Hmm... I knew that Semi guy was bad news. You can't just go around smacking people with steel chairs when you lose your temper. It's not professional. It could get the company in alot of trouble. I know what you mean Steve, but I'd keep your voice down, he might still be around. I wouldn't be so sure of that. I heard when he got backstage after the match, Mr. Covell was waiting to talk to him. Rumor is, he wasn't to pleased with what went down. Well I can't blame him for being upset. Semi was supposed to be the next big thing in MWE. He had the skills, he's undefeated, he- He WAS undefeated till he lost his temper. He just put a permanent mark on his resume, and now he's gonna be considered a liability. And personally, I don't feel safe working with a guy like that. Hmm. Mhm. ... So, did you catch the game last night? Which game, the Hockey game, or the Basketball Game? Either way, I didn't. You know the hot new makeup chick? The blonde one with the huge tits? Yeah, Cindy's her name. I asked her on a date, we went to the movies, then I took her home, and then... You know... What the hell happened? Well once we got inside... Oh Eric! Your Awake! How do you feel? Eric Derf sits up on the gurney he'd been placed on, still in his ring gear, only now he's sporting a bandage around his forehead. His eyes are slightly glazed over, just as anyone would have right after waking up. He looks around to an unfamiliar area, although the pattern on the wall indicates he's still in the arena. He see's two men, one in slacks and a blue button up shirt, glasses, with short black hair, holding a coffee. The other, white pants and a doctors uniform, shaggy white hair, and a goatee. His name tag says Kevin. The first guy sets his coffee down and quickly reaches for Eric Derf, motioning for him to lay back down. What... what happened? Why do I have such a headache? You were hit with a steel chair and knocked out. You've been out cold for almost an hour now. I was hit with a... Ugh... Bowen... I'll kill him... I'll KILL HIM! Eric sits up, but the man in the blue shirt, along with the doctor, grab his shoulders and calmly restrain him. Erics eyes are still glazed over, he's clearly in no condition to do anything. Calm down, it wasn't Bowen. It wasn't Bowen. Eric looks at the man in blue, known as Steve. His face is a confused mess. What? It wasn't Bowen. Who was it then... Oh wait, I think I remember... I remember... Semi hit Bowen... Ugh... Wait, did he hit me too? There is a pause from both men before Steve answers. Look, you need to get some rest. Mr Anderson is being delt with by Mr Covell for attacking all of you. Ugh... he'd- wait, all of us? What else did he do? Well he hit Chris England as well, and then bruised up a few security officers afterwards. Rumor going around is that he's in for one hell of a punishment. Yeah, I don't think your gonna have to worry about him attacking you with any more chairs... Eric once again sits up on his gurney, but his time, he makes no attempted to actually go anywheres, nor do the doctors make an attempt to keep him in place. Ugh... Right now, all I care about is getting rid of this headache... Do you have any Tylenol or anything I can take? Kevin reaches into his coat pocket and pulls out a bottle of pills, but they're not Tylenol. I've already got you on these, they're to help your cut heal faster. I can't give you anything else, the reaction of mixing could be fatal. The best thing we can do for you is leave you alone for a while so you can get some- TERRORDOME! All three men look towards a door in the room where the shout came from, just as someone runs by. Only a blue and red blur was seen, he was so fast, but the three security officers that followed indicated it was a rouge fan trying to meet some wrestlers. Steve bolts out of the room, following the security. What the hell was that? What's going on here today? Did he shout Terrordome? Both the doctor and Derf are staring at the door, confused, although nothing is going on outside of it. Theres probably a TerrorDome match at the next show, I heard the card was posted someplace... I wouldn't worry about it. But about your cut, you're going to have to take it easy when your working out. You should be good to go for the next how, just as long as you don't bust your head open again. Yeah, got it. Also, you should probably refrain from any training sessions, for obvious reasons... Yep, I understand... Ugh... Eric holds his head again, still in pain, just as Steve re-enters the room. Just as I thought, a fan made it threw security. We got rid of him though... Eric, hows your head? Feeling any better? Still holding his head, Eric lays back down on the gurney. Oh man, my head is killing me right now. I think I need some sleep... Well before you go to sleep, I should tell you something... That kid that was just back here, he stole a copy of the next shows card. And about that card, your match is the Terrordome match he go exited about. Still laying down, Eric moans before replying. What the hell is a TerrorDome? Is it like a cage match or what? Ugh... Well Sort of. I've got some footage of one of these matches from a while back, I'll send it to you online sometime. I just thought I'd let you know since Bowen and England both probably know. Oh, thanks for the heads up... Wait, Bowen and England? No Semi? Kevin, who's been silent for a while, drinking his coffee, walks over to the door, looks outside of it, then closes it over before walking back towards Derf on the gurney. Semi's got another match on the card against someone else. He won't be in your match, it's just the three of you. I'm sorry if you were looking for revenge or something... Eric sits up again, and points at the bandage on his head. This, this cut, it hurts like hell. I would love revenge against him, I really would... but I'm a smart guy. He's obviously not stable, and I'm not gonna waste my time on a guy that's just gonna throw a hissy fit when he don't get his way. England and Bowen, as much as I dislike him for taking my title, at least I respect them. England stayed true to his word by being the best tag partner he could, and Bowen, he is what he says he is. At least he follows the rules. So, your not upset about not getting to face Semi? Not really. I've got better men to face. Eric lies back down and closes his eyes. Now, if you don't mind, I'd like to get some sleep. Rest my head. I've got alot of studying to do for a match I've never heard of... Eric crosses his arms, eyes still shut, and goes silent. Steve and Kevin nod at each other and walk towards the door. So, what is a TerrorDome match anyway? Well, it's like a giant cage... The men exit the room, closing the door behind them. The room goes silent... For about three seconds. **3** The door quietly opens again, and someone steps in, but it's out of focus, so it's difficult to tell who it is. Eric continues to lay with his eyes closed, he's obviously still awake as only moments have passed, not nearly enough time for even the most tired of people to pass out. Hey Eric, hows the head feel? Without getting up, Eric opens his eyes to see the source of the voice, and sits up in surprise when he see's the voice is coming from none other then Mr Richard Covell himself. Once sitting up, Eric rubs his eyes again, possibly because he's tired, or maybe because he don't believe what he see's. Why would Mr Covell need to see Derf? Ugh, it's still hurting... Ah, you'll be fine eventually... Look, I wanted to talk to you about something. May I take a seat? Without waiting for an answer from Derf, Mr Covell grabs a nearby steal chair and pulls it over to where Eric is sitting on his gurney. Eric simply nods slightly, just enough to cause pain to rush to his head again. Listen. Our next show is a tribute show to an MWE Hall of Famer, Tim Fitzgerald. We've got some unusual matches happening, alot of which have never happened before. One of which is your match. Yeah... I heard about that match... What is a TerrorDome anyway? Mr Covell has a sort of surprised look on his face from Derfs question. How did you hear you were in the TerrorDome Match? Haven't you been in here since your match? Yeah, one of the trainers, Steve, mentioned it to me. Told me it'd be fair that I knew since Bowen and England both already knew. You know, give me some more time to study for it, whatever it is... Well, that's why I'm here... theres been some changes to the card... They're putting Semi in the TerrorDome. Eric looks at Mr Covell with an upset look in his eyes, obviously not happy about having to face such an unstable man. Are yo- Let me finish, please. Eric shuts up, and crosses his arms. Covell can probably tell he's not happy. I had plans for your guy's tag match tonight. Whoever got the pinfall was going to get a "special treat", but since Semi was the only man standing, we had to give him the "treat". However, I wasn't pleased with his actions. You don't just start pummeling people on your first night, and I felt that if he wanted to hurt people, maybe he ought to be in a match where the people he hurt can hurt him... Which brings me to you. Knowing now that Semi was practically demoted to get into the TerrorDome, Derf uncrosses his arms, fully expecting good news from Mr Covell... Or hoping for it, anyway. TerrorDome is only meant for three people, and we still have that "prize" I was talking about from your match. I've decided to give it to you. You've earned it, your red hot in MWE right now, the fans love you, they want to see you win, they want to see you do more here in MWE. Bowen's already got the X-Havoc title to worry about, and England is to fresh to face the man I've now got you booked against. Eric slowly slides off the table and stands right above Mr Covell, looking down at him, which causes Covell to stand up as well. The two men are face to face. Mr Eric Derf, how would you feel if I told you you were to face the Former MWE World Champion, DC? The expression on Derfs face quickly goes from a confused, glazed over dumbfound look to a sly smile, one side of is mouth smiling higher then the other. DC, eh... I like the sounds of that. Derf VS DC... Has a nice ring to it. You know what Mr Covell, Thank You. Thank you so much. This is a huge honor. Eric, quite exited, starts throwing exited punches into the air, laughing, even going as far as to hug Covell, not that he hugs back. Almost as quickly as he got exited though, he stops the jumping, laughing, and once again grabs his forehead in pain. Whoa, Whoa there man, calm down, calm down... calm down... Listen, I got alot of other stuff to take care of, like trying to get my damn job back. You should get some rest, you've got your biggest match ever to prepare for, you'd better be 100% for it. ugh, Yeah I know, thanks... Mr Covell pats Derf on the back and starts towards the door, stopping halfway there to turn his head around. Good luck kid. Eric doesn't look back, he barely even moves, other then the smile on his face. Mr Covell leaves the room, slowly closing the door behind him, which once again leaves the room silent. After a moment of exitment, Eric finally decides to get some sleep. Laying on the gurney, he closes his eyes once again, and lets the silence engulf him in a deep sleep. **4** As Eric sleeps, his voice can be heard. His thoughts can be heard. All of the stuff going on in his head can be heard as if he were speaking aloud, yet he's still just laying on the gurney, arms folded over his chest like he was placed there. I get to fight DC... So many things to think about with this match. Can I win? Is he ready to fight? What does this match mean for us? Whoa Eric, slow down. One thought at a time... I still think it's awesome I get to face him. A former champion. I've always wanted to face a former champion... But am I good enough? I mean, he won the MWE Heavyweight Championship. I've never fought against a guy that has won that title. Man, I don't know if I can beat him... But I can't let anyone know. Doubting yourself is only going to give him more of an edge to go over me. Oh, but I don't know! I know I'm good, but I know he's better... Erics quietly shifts position on the gurney so that he can sleep on his side, one hand is under his pillow. Eric, snap out of it! I need to stop thinking he can beat me. I know I can beat him, anything is possible. I can beat anyone on a good day. I need to keep that confidence. If I start doubting myself now, what will become of me? Will I become an afterthought? Vanish into MWEs history books? No, I can't let that happen. I've come to far in this place. Yeah, I've beaten some people, and will continue to do so. And besides, I wanted this match. Why am I questioning myself. Ok Eric, you have to think positive. Think of how I can beat DC... This show, it's a tribute show. I never knew the guy, but I do believe DC does. Hell, I think it was an announcement DC made in my former company that brought this mans passing to light, so I know he knows the guy. Tim FitzGerald. DC will be likely be fighting for his memory, and on a tribute show where storylines mean nothing, a top guy with ties to the tribute-e has a better chance of beating an up and comer that never knew him... But I disagree. While I may not have known Tim myself, I did read about his passing. I simply thought of it as just a random kid dying, but as the months went on, I heard more and more about him. He was a legend, and I'd never heard of him. But one thing I did hear about him, is that he helped make some of our biggest stars today, guys such as A-Rod... Guys like Whitman... Well, I feel I'm an up and comer. And if I knew Tim, I know he'd want to help push a great guy like me, just as he would like to see his friend DC win. But deep down, gathering from the information I know about Tim, I know he'd like to see the underdog beat a big dog. He'd see how this could elevate both of our careers, and I feel that's what he would have wanted. Eric once again rolls over, his back now to the camera, one hand still under his pillow. Why am I thinking about a tribute when I should be thinking about how to beat DC... Ok, what do I have that makes me better then him... Well, in a flat out wrestling match, I could work circles around him, but he's a spot monkey, and like it or not, he can beat me that way. But wait a second... He is a spot monkey. But who's going to be watching for his spotiness? I mean, normally, people get into his matches, because of something that happens outside the ring. But nothings happened this time. Sure, he was in a car crash, but everyone knows that for storyline purposes. He's fine, otherwise he wouldn't be at this show. And since people know that, he won't be able to play off the hype that he survived a car crash for our match. His girlfriend, will she come to the ring with him? Is everything going on with her real, or a storyline? Everything is so confusing, I don't know what's real or fake anymore, other then my own skills... So that's what I'm going to have to do to beat DC. The only real think I can do, is simply out-wrestle him. Without any real storyline going into this match, without the help of others preparing him for this match, he's weaker. He's still a threat, but not as great a threat... But now I worry he can still beat me. I have to remember, he is a former world champion... Oh, how many times am I going to have to remind myself of that! I don't know what to do. I know I can beat him, I know I have the confidence to beat him. I have to show confidence. I need to... Actually, I need to wake up. I need to get out of this bed. This is the biggest match of my career, I can't just sleep. My head will recover eventually, I don't have time to just sit around. I need to wake up.... wake up... wake up... wake up... Wake up... Wake up... The sound of a second voice prompts Eric to slowly open his eyes, and from a first person view, the face above is blurry at first, but after rubbing his eyes, the face becomes more clear, it's Kevin, the doctor from earlier. Wake up Eric. It's time to head out. Eric slowly sits up, now fully awake, in a normal state of mind, as opposed to the dream he was having. Oh man, how long was I out for? Like 5 minutes? Ugh... Kevin chuckles as he hands Eric a duffel bag. Yeah, 5 minutes and 8 hours. You passed out for the night, we need to get this place cleared out... So, I heard Mr Covell told you about the match change? Eric reaches into the duffel bag that was handed to him and pulls out a navy blue t-shirt, continuing to speak as he begins dressing himself, even without a shower. Yeah, he told me... Listen, if you see DC, give him a message for me... Tell him he'd better be prepared for me... Cause theres not a doubt in my mind I can beat him. Eric hops off the table, pats the doctor on the shoulder as a sort of "Thank You", and leaves the room, still in his wrestling trunks. Fin.
