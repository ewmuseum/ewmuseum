A man in a suit, all black, and a mask. It becomes apperent the man is the figure known as the Mysterious G. He's not moving, it's barely noticable that he's even breathing as he's just standing there with his hands behind his back. The voice from earlier starts again, now obviously that of Mystrious G. These two men are charecters, yet they are themselves. And we love them for it. They entertain, which is their goal, so congratulations to them. But when you think about it, are we not all charecters in a sence? When people think of you, or when you think of them, do you think about the person for their names, hair colors, body weights? Or do you think of the things that they do that make them who they are? Joe Roberts. You claim to hate charecters. Yet you yourself ae a charecter of self hate. Your a hypocryte. When you go out to the ring every week, you cause pain. But when you go home, you're not a pain bringer, your just a reguar guy. But in the ring, you are a charecter. But alas, I was recently descibed as "a charecter ad a half". That is incorrect. I am a charecter, disguised as another charecter. MWE know me as The Mysterious G. But before this, I went by a different name. My birth given name. And when I'm not wandering the halls that the next MWE show will be hosted at, I'm not wearing this mask. I'm a regular guy, yet, I'm still considered a charecter. The Mysterious G, while still having one hand behind his back, uses his other hand, and reach for his mask. He grabs the bottom of it, but stops short of removing it. Joe... Me, you, and two other charecters have a shot at somthing only a charecter in a wrestlng business could hold. A Wrestling title belt. A prop, if you will. And when I win that belt, I will no longer need this mask. So when I bet you, and I beat another man and become the X-TV Champion, I will be released from the hell of hiding my face. MWE fans will rejoice in the fact that one of the wrestling worlds hottest free agents isn't really that free. But don't let my coming fool you. I will still be a charecter. But I'll be more of a man than your charecter could ever accomplish. He pulls the mask off his face, only for the white light arond him to get so bright, he becomes unseeable, as the white light has grown beyond watchable. The Mystery... Will soon be solved... And Joe Roberts... will be left in it's wake...
