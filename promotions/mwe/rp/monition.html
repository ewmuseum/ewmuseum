Monition; Another David Whitman RP

Nov 30 2008

David Whitman

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Dense foliage. Muddy earth. Twilight looming on the horizon. Almost as if a vision- a recollection of a dream I once had. I remember. I cannot forget- I remember. Don’t I? The light-green, brown, and golden leaves crunch subtly beneath my feet as I make way through the forest. Once, I would have been stepping on bedewed grass- similar. Very similar. An interchangeable experience. Yet worlds apart. Deep inhalation. Eyes closed. I can feel the crisp air- lively, yet somber. A refreshing type of duskiness. My body embraces it. Eyes open. My trek ensues. Cause; obscure. Destination; unknown. I wander. Aimless, yet on a mission. One I do not know of. I roam. The finest feeling in existence; purposeless purpose. My surroundings sharpen. The timber falls into focus. I remember where I am. I remember. But I do not know. I only remember… The feeling of standing in front of the mountain. The insurmountable obstacle. Ascending. Until I reach the top. Then, the invariable outcome; my unsought task... Tumbling down. The sensation returns to me. I can sense the trophy I struggled to obtain, and maintain, escape my tight grasp. I shiver. And I sigh. But I reinvigorate myself. This is not the mountain. This is a forest. THE forest. MY forest. My new dominion. I close my eyes once more. Breathe in. The freshest air. I reopen my eyes and continue my journey with renewed vigor. Until I realize I am not really wandering. I stop. Contemplation envelops me. I am torn. Confidence. Readiness. Avidity for retribution. Diffidence. Irritation. Fear of failure. I observe my surroundings; anticipating a resolution, yet uncertain of its advent. The trees offer no solace, their derisive silence speaking not volumes, but the language of infinity. I surrender. Divisiveness and Decisiveness live hand in hand. Wha… wh…?! Without division… there can be no room for decisions. Without decisions… there is no difference. Without wrong… right… Without darkness… light. I stand still for a moment. Hesitant. I let the doubt cloud my mind as I focus on the sounds. But they escape. I slowly lower myself. To my knees. The concentration. Fills. My purpose is clear. Vivid. Unmistakable. He will NOT triumph yet again. I WILL establish my ability. And the bygone game of back and forth will be laid to rest. Once and for all. I do not truly believe this. I genuinely know this. The towering trees gyrate as my eyes water. I drop. Subdued. Mere moments later, I am awakened by the harsh light penetrating the bedroom curtains. Vague recollections of my travel still linger as I slowly open my eyes. The forest… As quickly as it came, it had faded away. To dust. But I remember. I remember it all.
