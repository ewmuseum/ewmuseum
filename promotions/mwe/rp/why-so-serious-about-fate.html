Why so Serious about Fate?; Jekyl RP #1

Aug 5 2008

Jekyl

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A sudden maniacle laugh, A stainless steel table in a dimly lit room, walls splattered in blood, A solid steel door with a slide window the size of a ruler. the floor covered with dried and fresh pools of blood. A loud bang is heard outside the room, a loud clang and the door creeks open with a loud screech. A man flies into the room backwards, he is middle aged, Black beanie, red flannel jacket, and torn black jeans. On his neck is a tattoo but not clear enough to make out what it is. Jekyl slowly walks into the room as the man slides into the stainless steel table, a look of extreme fear in his eyes. Jekyl slams the door behind him as it makes a thunderous bang as it closes. He walks past the man and grabs the camera violently and focuses it on the man. Jekyl: Whats wrong scared? GET UP! The man does as hes told as stands up he looks around the room and quivers in fear as he realizes the red markings are actually blood. Jekyl: Now... What's going to happen is you my shaky little friend, is going to hold this here camera, and film my promo. Man: Y...Ye....yes sir.... Jekyl: Good boy! Jekyl tosses the camera to the man as the scene twirls as the man fumbles the camera in his hands. The scene becomes steady as the man focuses the camera on Jekyl, as he walks around the steel table and sits on the chair. Jekyl: Now sir, heres the deal, you are going to hold that camera steady, if you do that, you will live...Understand? The man now scowls as he tries to hold the camera steady...Jekyl licks his hand and runs it through his shaggy hair and wets his lips. Jekyl: And go......Ladies and Gentlemen, you may not know who I am and what I am capable of but I assure you, things will get messy. My life has been nothing less than interesting and exciting. I plan on nothing less to come out of my appearance in MWE.. I will rip apart everyone in my way, and I will start off with Xavier Stone, Alex and David Bowen, Sonny Williams, David Whitman, Brian Michaels, Mancini, Devlin Sharpe, and Sir Alejandro Rodriguez. All these chumps are no match for.... THE JEKYL! Jekyl pulls out a butterfly knife and holds it in front of himself, the man holding the camera begins to shake. Jekyl reaches across to grab the camera and places it on the table facing the man, the sweat pouring off his face, running into his eyes, he wipes his face nervously. Jekyl stands up and rushes around the table and can be seen behind the stranger twiddling the butterfly knife in his hands. Jekyl: Do you know why I have these scars? As a child my father liked to drink....a lot, and a fiend..,and one night he came home more drunk than usual, he became very violent. My mother grabbed a kitchen knife to protect herself, father didn't like that too much, so he went after her. Now as he grabbed her he looked at me as I watched on scared, He grabbed her knife and walked over to me and came down to my level and grabbed me by the back of my head, with a concerned look on his face he asked me "Why so serious? Why SO SERIOUS?!?! Lets give this serious boy a smile". Jekyl comes closer to the stranger and puts his arm around him, the stranger jumps a little as Jekly's arm touches him. Jekyl looks at him with a face of sorrow. Jekyl: Why So Serious? Man: NO!....N...Noo.....NOOOOOOOOOOO!!!!! He then takes the butterfly knife and cuts the mans mouth open to give him a permanent smile, then stabs him in the back of the neck. As the man falls limp Jekyl drags the man into the back corner of the room leaving a small trail of blood behind him, the then sits where the man was sitting. Jekyl: Some people just make life so serious, they don't know how to have fun. But I gues this is a time to be serious, to tell the world and the fans why I am going to win this title, and I will start off with the extravagant Xavier Stone, A man who's parents were viciously murdered, left alone to fight for his life, grew up in a trike.. I mean bike gang and ended up joining a company called APWC, which to my research found that it was no good so it merged with another laughable fed, EW which is where not only does Xavier reign from, but the Bowen brothers, Brian Michaels, Sonny Williams and the notorious Alejandro Rodriguez. Which might explain the lack of talent... Jekyl twiddles his fingers around in the air with a big smile on his face, he leans in closer towards the camera. Jekyl: Mancini, the Italian guy who allegedly has no connections to any mob or crimes...Am I the only person who can see its obvious he is a part of it? Because he is a coward and hides behind a man who can fight his legalities...Coward, 2 face and snake, if you are going to take part in my world, at least take credit for your work, or I will. Devlin Sharpe, you are a man of apparent no history, I know nothing about you, therefore I will make sure you end up not having a future, simple! Now now, David Whitman, trained by his cousin’s best friend, then his cousin, seems like your average Joe, I see you lived most of life with your cousin as his best friend, you apparently really got into wrestling when James won his first title. I also found out that David and James often travel together, along with their cast of their TV/Online show, “Top Rope Tavern”. Now it seems to me this guy has some issues, he had people tell him what to do, and not just one but 2 people...Pussy, a man grows up in life learning on his own, hit or mis, SWING BATTA BATTA BATTA, STRIKE! This one will just be another strike to your record. Jekyl leans back in the chair and twiddles the butterfly knife in his fingers once again and looks at the walls and then the ceiling. Jekyl: My biggest worry is Xavier, he knows what life like mine has been like, and it is almost intimidating, yet.....theres a certain je ne ce quois about him, might be because he's a father now and his personal entourage is growing, which slows a man down, if he were smart he would ditch the ditz and then put his bastard son up for adoption, THEN he might get somewhere in this company... i mean this guy may be the current AEW US champ, and ex APWC WORLD champ, but to me, he is just some other chump to laugh at. In AEW he had 4 world title matches and didn't win any of them, then he gets dropped to mid card and wins a sappy US title.... quite pathetic. Now Mr. Xavier I am going to touch a nerve here if you don't mind, Your son and poorly deceased Ex Troy and Kat... I think i knew Kat, she was a good fuck, and now you know this do you find it funny that you doinked a girl that another clown has boinked? shame really. Troy, the bastard son of yours.... pretty sad that you were never told about him, and she dies and hes stuck in your lap.... oh well life goes on.. Jekyl turns the camera towards the far wall which as a mirror across it, he stands up and walks to it, the air is quiet, not a peep except for the sound of his shoes and the sound of his voice. Jekyl: Fate, a wondrous thing that runs peoples lives, something people believe controls them and decides what they are gonna do, whats gonna happen and where they are going to go in life. Truth is, there is no such thing as fate, everyone makes their own fate and they decide how they are going to live or die. I discovered this as a child that I was going to grow up alone, I discovered I liked to hurt people, which is one of the reasons why I joined MWE, NOW THATS FATE. I chose what I wanted to do, where I was going to go and how I was going to do it. The only exception to the rules of fate are the rest of the men in this company, I am their fate, I decide whether they live or die, I DECIDE WHETHER THEY SUCCEED OR FAIL! Jekyl smashes the mirror and cuts his index finger. He looks at it and begins to write on the unshattered part as it says "Why so serious" as it drips down the mirror, Jekyl begins to laugh as he takes the camera, looks into it and smashes it against the wall, audio still in tact, records his voice as the Laugh of a serial killer echoes out of the room, all of a sudden a flash of a scene hits the scene, Jekyl sitting in a throne with a crown on his head and the MWE world title in his belt, blood begins to drip in front of the lens as the scene completely fades. -FIN-
