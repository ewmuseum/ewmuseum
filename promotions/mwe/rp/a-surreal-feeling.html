A surreal feeling; David Whitman RP

Aug 14 2008

David Whitman

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
WOOHOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO! Oh, Please. Please tell me that’s not- It is. David and James move out of the way, as a blur speeds past them and tosses itself into the pool. David, James, and the entire cast of the Top Rope Tavern show (Eddie Sanchez, Scott Jones, Thomas Williams, and Kate Madison) are staying at the Atheneum suites in Detroit, Michigan. As is usual whenever they’re at a hotel, they are all currently hanging around the pool area. The blur that just flew past David and James was Zack Evermore, and the reason David and James are looking very concerned right now is because Zack seemed to be naked. David: Is he- James: I think he is. Eddie: SOMEONE GET THE MAN A TOWEL! Zack exits the pool, and everyone cringes as they turn away. Kate: I second that motion, cover this guy up! Zack: FINE! Party poopers. Zack walks over to one of the chairs and covers himself up. Zack: I’m decent again, guys. David turns to look and his disgust is instantly transformed into rage. David: MY TITLE! Zack starts laughing as he runs towards the pool. David: NO! Zack jumps, but David manages to catch him mid-air with a spear, all the while grabbing the title and tossing it to the side. James catches the title as David and Zack land in the pool. A few seconds later, David sticks his head out of the water. Kate: Uhm, where’s Zack? David: Who? James: Dude, you’re drowning him, aren’t you. David: Wh- ME? Naaaaaaw. Eddie: Seriously. He can’t breathe. David: Fine, but if he pulls this crap again, I’m taking him out. Eddie: … to dinner? GAY! David feigns laughter as he swims over to the side of the pool. Zack comes up for air, coughing and wheezing. Zack: This is too much. I gotta go lay down. Zack exits the pool as everyone turns away again, and he starts walking towards their suite. He passes an old couple, who stares at him. Zack gets in between them and puts his arm around them as he walks with them. David: Oh God. James: He’s going to end up in jail soon. Again. David shrugs as he lies back on his chair, smiling. James tosses him the MWE title. David holds it up and looks at it. He admires the flag designs, and then runs his fingers against the boldly imprinted red MWE letters. Scott: GET A ROOM! David chuckles. David: You know, I really missed this title. It’s weird, I feel just like I did when I won the last time. It’s almost as if I’ve gone back in time, isn’t it? James: Yeah. You won the title at the same PPV, almost the same way- Super weird. David: Maybe it’s a sign though, you know? Maybe this is my chance to make things right. Most people don’t get a chance to rewrite the past, but that’s what this is beginning to look like. A chance to rewrite history. I could become a better champion than I ever was before. James: That won’t be hard. Everyone laughs as David just smiles. David: Yeah… Eddie: Dude, you ok? David: Yeah, it’s just so- so surreal. It’s like… I mean it truly is as if I’m in heaven right now. David lays the title across his waist. James: If you wanna have an impressive run, you’re going to have to start by making this your last day off for now. Rodriguez is gunning for you, you know. You’re not going to be able to get rid of him just by throwing him over the ropes this time. David: Yeah, yeah, I know. James: Cuz you know, I saw that match… he’s a lot more focused than I thought he’d be. David: You think I don’t know that? Every time I fight him it’s as if he’s trying to take me out for good. He keeps improving, but then again, that’s a good thing. James: How do you figure? David: You know, that keeps me on my toes. If he didn’t improve and I had to wrestle him, I wouldn’t improve either. James: Yeah… Meh, I dunno. He’s more intense. You just better be careful. David: I was BORN careful. They are all silent for a while. Kate receives a call and leaves, which is something she does often. Eddie, Scott and Thomas are playing a drinking game; they see who can hold their breath underwater the longest, and the first one up has to shot a beer. They’re already on the second case. James is wearing his signature boardshorts. David is wearing the same thing, except in white. James and company have followed David to Detroit because they are still in negotiations with MWE over the TRT show, and also because they like staying in hotels. James: Your first Monday Night Mayhem in a long while, huh? David: Yeah… and you know where it’s at? James: The Palace. David: HELL YEAH! Eddie: WHAAAAT? James and David look over at Eddie, who has gotten out of the pool and is heading towards them. Eddie: What palace? Buckingham? David: Dude, we’re in Detroit. Eddie: … Westminster? James: How drunk ARE you? Eddie: I can’t hold my breath for longer than five seconds under water. David: Ah. Eddie pulls up a chair. Eddie: So, what palace? David: You know; Auburn hills. Me and James were there about three or four years ago when that brawl broke out. James: Malice at the palace. David: Yeah. Great timing, huh? James: Indeed. Hey, don’t they have luxury suites at that place? David: Uh, yeah. James: Wait… you’re staying there, aren’t you? David: … sorry? James: WHY THE HELL DIDN’T YOU GET US SUITES THERE?! David: Wh- this place is nice! James: Are you ashamed of us? Eddie: I think he is. David: No, it’s just- Look, a lot of the MWE roster, they don’t really like me right now. In fact, I think most of them want my head on a plate. Eddie: That doesn’t sound very appetizing at all. David: Point is, if I bring you guys, they’ll get the wrong idea- they’ll think I think I can just run the place, or get what I want or whatever. James: I’ll bet you a million dollars almost everyone there has at LEAST two guests. David: Yeah, well- that’s not the point, now is it? They’re probably looking at me as an outsider- James: Dude, you were the first champion!! David: Yeah, but I left. James: But you’re back now! David: But still. Eddie: You two should be in the debate club. James: What debate club? Eddie: … I’m gonna go get a beer. Eddie gets up and walks over to the others. James and David stay quiet for a while. David: … So, what about the show? James: You just worry about not losing your title at MNM, and let ME worry about the show. David: Alright, man. David and James bump their fists, put their sunglasses on, and get more relaxed. After about a few seconds, Scott appears, tapping James on the shoulder. Scott: Uh… James. James: What is it? Scott: I uh… You should take a look at this… James sits up and looks over to where Scott is pointing; Eddie is lying flat on his back next to the pool as Kate is giving him mouth-to-mouth, and Thomas is a few feet further rocking back and forth whispering “ohmygodohmygodohmygod”. James: He drank too much and subsequently drowned, didn’t he? David: He just acted like he drowned so he’d get to make out with Kate, didn’t he? They all look over and carefully examine the event. Scott: Well, I’ve known him for quite a while, and honestly, I’d say… A bit of both. They all look at each other, and shrug. Scott: I’m gonna go check, just to make sure. James: SUPER GAY. Scott scoffs as he walks off. David: Just another day in heaven. They both lie back down. James: … Dude, you know how messed up it would be if he had to go to the hospital, and that’s the last thing you said? David: If this were a movie or something that would have been my fade-out line. You just had to ruin, didn’t you? James: … Yup. David sighs. He looks up, then down at the title on his waist, and he smiles. He closes his eyes as he lays back, relaxed, in the beach chair.
