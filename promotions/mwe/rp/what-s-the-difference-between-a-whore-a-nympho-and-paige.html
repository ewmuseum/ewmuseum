What's the difference between a Whore, a Nympho, and Paige?

Apr 12 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Read along to find out! Lights a-blazin’, people movin’, this place is buzzing, baby! Las Vegas at night is like nothing you will ever witness in your life. The lights, the energy, the movement…. If you’re not completely and utterly gratified in every single way after spending an evening in Las Vegas, it’s because you got a seizure in the process. Yes, Las Vegas represents everything that makes men happy. But it also represents everything that makes them bad. That makes them lustful. That makes them want more and more and more, until there is nothing more to take. Booze, money, women. Sex, gambling, drugs. Hell, Las Vegas even has its own CSI. All the good things in the world, gather to the various establishments of this one place, in order to do bad things. It’s just how it is. How it’s always been. And tonight, two men, two good men, have gathered here themselves, to do some damn sleazy things. “HAHA! Who needs Europe baby? UK? Forget it! Las Vegas is where it’s AT, daddy-o! WOO!” Two men walk into frame as their figures become apparent to on-lookers. And yes, they are some big names! Dick Malibu! Windy Bridges! Suited-up and read for action right here along the strip baby! And even though it’s evening, dick is wearing his sunglasses (how much cooler could this man get!). Stylish brown suit and neatly permed hair. He struts his way down the strip with a smile on his face as Windy follows closely behind, in a new, striped black with neon green trim suit, perfectly spiked blond hair and, uniquely, white sneakers. The two men laugh as they walk down the strip, passing onlookers who recognize them or are intrigued by the thought of these two very unique men walking down Las Vegas streets together. But soon, the two men end their trek as they come to a halt in front of a big, menacing building. Neon signs blind them (ironically enough, not Dick… since he’s wearing sunglasses…) as Windy and Dick try to read said sign, gazing up from their street-side view. Still looking up, Dick smiles. “Now THIS…. THIS is what I’m talking about brother!” The camera pans up to reveal the sign Windy and Dick are so aptly staring at, and yes, it is that of a naked neon lady spreading her legs like clockwork, with a flashing sign that reads “Hot Naked Ladies XXX”. The sign buzzes like a bee as the camera pans back down to our heroes. “Dick… Why did you bring me here? No wait, let me guess! You own a part of this place too! How come you don’t have your name on this place anyway?” “Oh trust me baby, my Dick’s all over this place! WOOO! WOO! WOOOOOO!!” Dick does a little strut as Windy rolls his eyes. “But seriously… I think this is the perfect setting for your next promo daddy-o! You’re lookin’ for a little bit of an edge, what edgier place is there than a strip club brother? “Edgy? More like sleazy!” “Hey! Watch it there brosef. Dick Malibu has made a name for himself supporting strip clubs all across the US-Mexico border! Are you calling me sleazy?” Windy ponders what he’s going to say for a second, as if hesitant, worried of some sort of consequence, but then he just smiles and says it. “Well, I wouldn’t be the first one!” Dick laughs sarcastically and shrugs Windy off. “Anyway… that’s not the only reason I brought you here. Think about who you’re facing at MNM for a second… You’re going to give the Head Bitch in Charge a promo to remember daddy-o!” Dick laughs as the two men approach the doors to the gentleman’s club. With a pull on the big, mahogany doors, they disappear within the confines of club as the scene fades. ------------------------------------------- I mean… really… how much of this can be described? There are minors that are going to read this! But you get the idea. A nice, dark-ish, cozy atmosphere. That’s about the cleanest thing you could say here. For the rest, all you kids can use your imagination. Scantily clad (if clad at all) women scour the place, serving drinks, dancing on stage and… um… doing other things... The camera closes in onto Dick and Windy, who are sitting at the bar, having just ordered two beers. A waitress wearing a VERY tight bikini drops them off in front of them as they grab them and toast each other, taking a long swig each. “You know this place is pretty nice.” “Almost a shame we have to be in a different country tomorrow.” “What’s worse is that I’ll have to be in proximity of Paige in the ring at MNM!” Windy cringes as Dick gives him a weird look. “You’re not afraid of facing Paige tomorrow, are you?? ” “Afraid of facing her? No… not at all! Afraid of catching an STD? For my life!”” “Woah Momma!” All of a sudden, a bunch of red lights go off and cheesy music starts playing. Windy is a little frazzled and very confused, but Dick laughs as he puts his arm around Windy and some of the scantily clad women gather around this scene. Streamers and confetti fall from the ceiling as Dick adjusts his suit in his seat. “Wow, what a scene Daddy-O! You know what that means, don’t you Win-day!?” “Actually I don’t Dick. And I have to say I’m pretty frightened.” “Well, Windy-O, your little comment there set off what I like to call… THE LIGHTNING ROUND! “ Dick pulls out a deck of cue cards from his jacket pocket and hands them to Windy. “Ya see brother, I knew the whore jokes would come up, so I took the liberty of preparing a few good ones for you to read off for the good people watching at homen.” “Dick, I don’t know…” “Come on brother! You wanted an edge, well here’s your edge. Your opponent has a clear and evident weakness, and you should take advantage of that! Here we go daddy-o! No turning away from the lightning round!” Windy sighs as he sifts through the cards and pulls one out, hesitating a little before reading it. “What did the prostitute say to Paige?” “What!?” “Hell, I don’t know! I can’t read Paige’s thoughts!” “BA BOOM CHA! Not bad, not bad! Let’s keep the ball rolling Win-Day! They’ll get better as you go along!” Windy grabs another cue card amongst the pile as he tosses the first one aside Letterman style. He loosens up a little, smiles and says this one a little easier. “Not even Flava Flav would touch that rank piece of pie!” “HEY-O!” Windy tosses away the cue card and grabs yet another. “Getting with Paige was like throwing a hot dog down a hallway!” “OUCH!” Dick makes a slightly surprised face before high fiving windy” “That one surprised even me Daddy-o! Hold on let me find you a good one before we wrap this shindig up. You still have a promo to cut!” Dick sifts through the cue cards, tossing the ones he reads aside, shaking his head disapprovingly… and trust me, there’s A LOT of cue cards… this was obviously well thought out. Or just really easy to write! Eventually, Dick settles on one and hands it to Windy. Windy looks at it… reads it… studies it even… and then snickers.” “What’s the difference between a whore, a nympho, and Paige?” Dick can barely contain his giggles. “The whore says, ‘Aren't you done yet?’ , The nimpho says, ‘Are you done ALREADY?’ And Paige says, ‘Beige. I think I'll paint the celling beige!’ Dick explodes into laughter as music hits and a band headed by a bald man begins playing a closing theme. ----------------------------------------- the scene cuts and returns to the same bar, only this time, Windy is sitting at it alone. Women are still going about their… business… but Windy sits alone, with a beer. Dick can be scene in the background, sitting by the stage, and I don’t think anything else needs to be described about that. Nevertheless, Windy is here, with his beer. He takes a swig as he loosens his tie and looks up t the camera. “Nice choice for a setting, isn’t it Paige?” Windy takes a deep breath and a long look around. Not really looking at the naked women as much, moreso taking into the environment Dick has brought him into. A part of him probably wondering what he’s doing here. “Kind of weird, don’t you think? Windy Bridges… in a strip club… with Dick Malibu… cutting a promo while wearing a suit? A little, unpredictable, don’t you say? And I mean, that was kind of the point. I bet you’re sitting there at home, or at the gym, or in your hotel room, or wherever… watching this… thinking… ‘this isn’t the Windy Bridges I got to know in WZCW… in old-MWE… in EW… or even REV~Pro… this is a completely different man…’. You must be thinking to yourself that this Windy Bridges is NOT the predictable Windy Bridges you were expecting to face. And I mean… that’s really what it boils down to, isn’t it Paige? It boils down to the fact that in my three first matches here in the new MWE, I went 0-3… because I was trying the same old crap. I was the same old goody-two-shoes Windy Bridges, saying the same old things in his promos, trying the same old moves in his matches. Well, not anymore, sister!” Windy smiles a little wider now. “You see Paige, the Windy you knew and grew to love last year is still here. I’m still a nice guy, I’m still the stand up citizen I’ve always been. But I’ve realized that if I want to accomplish anything in my career… If I want to be anything more than a mid carder… then I have to be…… well…. I have to be more like …. You! Minus the whoring, of course!” Windy laughs as Dick runs up beside him and gives him a high five, before returning to the his stage-side seat. “Now Paige, darling, I’ve never been known as much of a woman beater. In fact, you could probably attest to this yourself… I’ve always been very respectful to the opposite sex. But the moment you decided to step into the ring with men, you gave up the right to use your genitalia as an excuse. Don’t think that I haven’t seen how people react to you during matches! They’re hesitant. They’re uncertain. They give you leeway and it’s what makes you so successful. And a month ago, I may have been exactly the same. But the Windy Bridges you see before you today… the one that looks good in a suit and even better in the ring… as I said… is not the same Windy Bridges you and many other came to know and love. And Paige, whether you admit it publicly or not, you know that last part applies to you the most. We’ve never wrestled inside the ring, but baby, deny it all you want, Windy Bridges and Paige have locked horned outside of the ring, haha.” Windy laughs once again, and once again, Dick runs up and high fives him once again. “And while I did have to make a few visits to my doctor after that, you know, just in case, I never really lost respect for you. And don’t get me wrong, despite what I’m saying, I DO have respect for you. You’re willing to do anything you have to do to get to the places you need to get. And that’s really something I’m striving for… maybe not the same way you’re doing it, but nonetheless. And this Monday… you’re going to realize that Windy Bridges is no longer a pushover. That I’m willing to do anything I need to do to win. And I don’t care who, or what… stands in my way. Paige…. At MNM, it’s my time to shine. And whether you like it or not, you’re going down! And not in the way you’re used to!” Windy polishes off his beer before setting it down on the bar. He gets up from his seat, adjusts his suit, and joins Dick in gaze of the beautiful women in front of them, on stage, as the scene fades.
