Return of a loser

Apr 12 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The scene opens up with Devlin Sharpe standing in the middle of the MWE ring holding a microphone with a single spotlight shining on him "Finally, after all the waiting, I have finally returned to the MWE, returned to kick Semi's ass in a..." Devlin paused for a moment as he reallised, he tilted his head "...NON TITLE MATCH? WHAT THE HELL?!, I mean shit sure I lost, but for my grand return to the MWE I demand a motherfucking title shot" Devlin looked to the floor and sighed "Alrighty, I am sorry it was a tam egotistical of me to say that I guess but I truly believe with the right motivation that I am the best that MWE has to offer, I mean sure I screwed up a few times and lost more but to be honest who hasn't done what I have done, won a few matches, lost a few matches, went on an ego trip, lost a job, worked my way back up to the main roster with a new gimmick, its natural evolution for a wrestler really isn't it? well alright, I suppose once I win this match I will recieve an MWE title shot eventually, I mean hell a wins gotta count for something right? but I need to prove to you guys I have the mettle to be a champion in the MWE and I will do it right now so keep looking at that big old titantron up there to witness the true power of a real champion" Devlin winks and makes his way out of the ring as a group of cameras begin following him heading towards the ramp, through the curtains, then Devlin began to increase his pace as he passed through the backstage area at a nearly running pace, he is seen exiting the arena and getting in to his car as he drives off the camera get in to a van and follow him, suddenly Devlin does a handbreak turn and shifts gear as he shoots down the highway at blistering speed, the van tries to follow him, after a few kilometres, Devlin pulls in to a roadside bar and slowly gets out the car grinning "Now you'll see the fun" Devlin slowly walks in to the bar followed by the camera men and begins acting like he owns the place, shoves around the customers, one of the customers responds back with a punch however Devlin ducks and knees him in the gut causing to fall on his knees causing him to fall on his knees clutching his gut, then Devlin puts his hands up "Hey I was just defending myself", several of the guys friends began approaching Devlin as he turned to the camera "Get ready ladies and gentlemen, shit's gonna stir up" One of the guys swung at Devlin as he ducked underneath it and sweeped him, another tried to kick Devlin as he sweeped the other, Devlin did a backflip to avoid it and landed on a table and motioned for the others to attack him, as they approached him, devlin jumped and hurricanrana'd one of the guys, another ran towards Devlin but he caught him in a drop toehold, there was one final guy left, Devlin decided to have fun with this guy, so he began dodging, ducking and weaving under every one of his attacks and then picked him up in a powerbomb position, brought him to a table and bombed him through it "and that is why you don't fuck with Devlin Sharpe" Devlin walked out of the bar and outside of the bar he looked at the camera and said "Ladies and Gentlemen, we hope you have enjoyed my demonstration and I will be looking forward to your support during my redebut match for the MWE, wish me luck against that Semi competent, Semi retarded, Semi professional douchebag, Semi" Devlin got in to his car and drove back to the arena where MWE was held as the cameramen followed him in the van again. ooc: sorry for the length, to tell you the truth, I forgot I had a match, so please try to enjoy my new style of roleplay and leave feedback about the content (not the length)
