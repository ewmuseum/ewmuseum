American Chopper with the Animal

Aug 29 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Thursday August 28th at 9 P.M. The Learning Channel (TLC) aired the celebration of The 120th Episode of American Choppers with special guest American Animal. The Intro of American Choppers plays. http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s1HnHtPQePc Montage with the time 8:00 AM in the corner of the screen with Paul Sr. Paul Jr. and Mikey plays as they are getting ready in there house drinking orange juice and putting there clothes on. The announcer Mike Rowe: Hello and welcome to another episode of American Choppers this is not just any normal episode it’s the 120th episode. Pictures of American Animal appear in the screen. Mike Rowe: And we are going to celebrate it in style with the newest MWE Wrestler Bad Ass American Animal. Paul Sr., Jr. and Mikey enter the Orange County Choppers garage mechanics Rick and Jason are already there drinking coffee while wearing there uniforms. Mikey: What’s up guys? Rick and Jason: ehhhhh. Paul Jr.: why so down isn’t this new wrestling guy coming over so we can design his bike. Paul Sr. (Scream): Yup AMERICAN ANIMAL! The show enters there first commercial where they promote Pampers Budweiser and Progressive Car Insurance. Show Returns. Screen displays the front of Orange County Choppers with the time 12:00 and then displays the inside. Mike Rowe: Today’s special guests was just a little more special then others and the boys where in for a surprise. Mikey is laying down in a couple of boxes Paul Jr.: in top of his bike as Paul Sr. stands tall with his arms corrected. Mikey : Where is this American Animal guy he was suppose to be here like an hour ago. Paul Jr.: It’s true. Shit where is he? Mikey : What is he a corny country singer. Paul Jr.: Nooo He’s this new wrestler from Mayhem Wrestling Entertainment. Mikey: What’s his name. Paul Jr.: Josh Callaway. Paul Sr.: Rises his strong arms. Paul Sr.: F*** what this guy thinks we got time. As he just says that AA enters the garage with a black sleeveless Harvey Davidson shirt with blue jeans black timberland boots and a can of Pediasure milk. Animal: What’s all this fighting about? I’m here and that’s all that matters, what’s more important two chicks or a big bear and two cubs. Paul Jr. Sr. and Mikey laugh. Animal: What can I say? I was in time but than a hot bitch and her sister entered my car. You understand me. They all shake hands “bad ass” style. Paul Sr.: Why you be drinking Pediasure. Animal: Dude before I do my thing I drink monster to get energize to break some a** and then after I get a little soft a little calmer so I buy myself Pediasure. Mikey: I drink some PediaSure too! Animal: why? Mikey: Because it tastes great. Animal: You drink it because it tastes great I drink it because I’m warned out! Paul Sr.: Lets stop talking trash lets start talking about your bike. Paul Jr.: Yea let’s head out to the computer room and design it. All men except the mechanics go to the computer room that has 3 computers and a whole lot of paperwork around it with paintings of past bikes in the wall. Animal Paul Jr. and Mikey stay standing as Paul Sr. sits down in front of the main mac computer. Paul Sr.: So what do you want to be the theme of your bike. Animal: Oh I want it to be about my favorite battle ground America. Paul Sr.: looks down in demise. Paul Sr.: I can see that I should have never asked. The men design the bike in the computer selecting colors and different pieces to the bike. The show enters there second commercial break promoting Direct TV, Babylon A.D. and Valvoline motor oil. As the show returns the design of the chopper is done and printed out. They head out back to the garage room and the hand the six pages to mechanics Rick and Jason. As they go ahead and start to build the bike. Animal and the boys head to a plastic table in the garage and sit down to converse. Paul Jr.: So tell me I heard your going to make your debut this Monday on mayhem The boys get closer to animal and seem more interested except Paul Sr. Animal: Yup i was always interested in the MWE and wrestlers Jason Fear and B.G. Mancini introduced it ti me when i left jail. Paul Jr.: I heard that your match is against B.G. Manicni. Animal: Yea i met him right before i went to jail 7 years ago In a bar where i accidentally paralyzed my uncle well that's the reason i went to jail. Animal: He was my trainer so it’s going to be extra special. It’s like where going to find out if I outsmarted him or he still got some hidden tricks in his book. Mikey: Oh that’s cool. Animal: I really respect B.G. Mancini but in the ring I,m going to give it all i got and there is no such things as friends in the ring. Paul Jr.: So how do you prepare for a match. Animal: Well you wake up every mourning at noon try not to go to sleep too late and don't try to drink a tremendous amount of beer. Animal: But this time this match it’s all for a good cause and people are going to look at it like a classic match soon. Paul Jr.: Well folks remember to tune in to watch American Animal make his debut vs. his own friend B.G. Mancini. Paul Sr.: Well he going to enter in his bike right. Animal: Of course. Paul Sr.: Never really been a wrestling fan but ill sure tune in. Animal: You must. Third commercial brake promotes the LG Dare, Target (Why so goodbye and I say hello) and American Animals Match on Monday Night Mayhem. The show returns in the outside of Orange County Choppers and showcased the boys as they stand in front of the bike covered in a big black cloth screen corner displays 2:00pm. Paul Sr.: Are you ready. Animal: I’m always ready. They uncover the bike. Animal: It’s good. Mikey: What do you mean its good its great. Animals smiles and sarcastically smiles about it. Animal: OK it’s the greatest bike ever. Animal says bye and thanks and bye to all the mechanics and the Tuetel three as he jumps on the bike asks for the key and goes away. -Fade as he enters the road-
