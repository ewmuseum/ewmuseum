The Saintz are *record scratch* jobbing

Mar 29 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
OOC: Totally late on this and I know... especially Prax is gonna laugh at me. When it comes to writing a Saintz promo, the hype over shadows exactly how much material I really do have for them. The gas is on E, but I started an RP, that was supposed to develop into a promo. But considering the fact that I'm already late... and I've already lost the match, I switched up the ending. So, here is what I had, adjusted to fit the fact that the Saintz, IN CHARACTER, turn in a late promo for Highway to Hell... So everyone is clear, this does break kayfabe, and is meant to "embarass the Saintz". Think of it as a public apology of sorts lol. D.C.: What do you mean you don't have a plan?!?!?!? *D.C. stands in front of the pack, like a teacher in front of his students. Wearing a brand new D.C. hoodie in repeated succession, with black cargo pants and a Saintz of Los Angeles bandana. His arms are stretched out wide as he shakes his head in disappointment. The camera only shows four figures standing facing D.C., their backs to the camera. D stands in front of a oddly shaped car, like an old English black taxi. Tera is behind the car, on her cell phone.* D.C.: Axel? Your supposed to be the f***ing leader, and none of you got anything to say? *The camera switches to an angle, as Axel Christ, Ozzy Sampson, Diamond Moses, and Eve Magdalen are shown looking around on the ground like every little bug on the cold cobblestone street wants to be stomped on. They stand in front of the taxi, arms behind them, trying like hell to look innocent.* D.C.: Diamond? *Diamond rubs the back of his head, and shrugs big. Ozzy however, raises his hand, eagerly waiting to be called upon. He hops in place as D.C. purposely glances past him.* D.C.: Eve... come on... Ms. Thing-Thang, can't even talk about her own ass cheeks for ten f***ing minutes to at least turn over some kind of sell? What the f*** is wrong with you four??? Didn't I teach you anything? *Ozzy, still, frantically jumps in place, raising his hand like he wants to finger fuck the sun.* D.C.: WHAT OZZY??? *Ozzy's eyes widen, he stops, and puts his finger in his mouth, like pondering whatever great idea he had to say. He looks at the ground. Immediately snapping back and look at D.C. with a big bright smile. But he squints one eye, thrusting his lower lip over his top, and pretends to be smoking a cigar.* Ozzy: MEH SHEE! We gonna act like a real rough and tough group, see? And then we're gonna tell it like it is, see? MEH *Ozzy nods, with a big bright smile, dropping the act and awaiting the approval. But D.C. looks at him, like Chef Ramsey staring at a half cooked piece of meat. He holds his hand on to Ozzy and looks at the rest.* D.C.: Axel... what the f***... Ozzy can come up with something mildly retarded... and the only reason I'd let you idiots take HIS advice, is because he's the only f***ing one of you with a damn idea... AND ITS OZZY WE'RE TALKING ABOUT! *Ozzy looks up at the sky. Not knowing if that was a compliment or not. Diamond nudges Axel. Pushing him forward. D.C. hops up on the taxi's hood and folds his arms. He glares over at Ozzy, whose still looking and scratching his head.* D.C.: What, do you want a f***ing Scooby Snack? *Ozzy nods quickly, almost drooling at the proposal. D.C. shakes his head, staring at Axel holding his hand out.* D.C.: Say something!! ANYTHING! *Axel steps in front of D.C., with his head held down. He looks slowly up at D.C., thinking twice. He kinda shrugs...* Axel: Well... we did have one idea... *The camera turns to D.C., who cracks a wicked smile. The scene cuts to Axel, whose also cracking a smile.* *In an instant, horns are heard honking. People shout from the street as the Saintz are on top of a double decker bus. Diamond is leaning over the top flipping off passers as the behemoth barrels down the street. Megaphones a plenty for each of them as they throw their hands in the air. A beer cup flies out towards them, and even a rock clangs against the side of the bus as it continues its parade. The Saintz are laughing together, as the camera, blatantly shows why this ruckus is happening. They are wearing jerseys...* Axel is wearing Ozzy is wearing Diamond is wearing Eve, like the icing on the cake... *The Saintz are screaming at the people of Manchester. They are flipping the people off and flapping their jerseys at them, waving the colors of Man U's biggest rivals, with some of the worst players. Eve flaunts her Beckham jersey, which give or take, is either sexy or despicable across the faces of a few people the camera catches. Diamond is chuckling like crazy as he poses, flexing his muscles like he is neigh untouchable. Ozzy jumps in place, he does that a lot, cause he's an idiot. The camera cuts to another angle, at the drivers seat, where D.C. is shown behind the wheel of the bus (as it goes round and round, ROFFLE), an Englishmen stands behind him, making sure D.C. follows proper conduct, but D.C. whips the wheel from left to right hard, sending the bus teetering.* D.C.: This is f***ing hilarious!! *D.C. looks behind him, as if wanting some kind of confirmation from the Englishmen, who stands tall and proud, unflinching.* D.C.: You need to get laid more oft- *A bottle smashes across the windshield as D.C. grabs the wheel again. The camera switches back to the top where Axel Christ is seen with a megaphone in hand.* Axel: OHHHH SAY CAN YOU SEE! BY THE DAWN'S EARLY LIGHT! DAVID BECKHAM TOOK A PLANE! BECAUSE THE U.S. IS TIGHT!! AND THE ROCKETS RED GLARE! OUR BOMBS WILL POLLUTE YOUR AIR! AND YOUR TEETH WILL GLOW BRIGHT! WHATEVER IS LEFT IN YOUR MOUTH THAT'S THERE! *Something whizzes past Axel's head, ducking quickly to avoid it, he comes back up smiling bright. Diamond stands beside him with a megaphone as well.* Axel: Don't hate us because we represent a country you people weren't man enough to control. God save the Queen? But the b**** doesn't even have a ruling say in your politics! What the f*** is wrong with you people? Diamond: THE SAINTZ HAVE ARRIVED IN THE UNITED KINGDOM! We're gonna burn this city to the ground! Axel: ...and as you stand in the ashes of your falling, ruined city, you will know that it was US, who drove your homes into the ground Diamond: Who stole your lives, simply because we could... Axel: And you will suffer the pain and anguish, that Marko and Triple G will experience at the hands of the Saintz! *Ozzy grabs a megaphone as well.* Ozzy: TEA SUCKS! *He nods at Axel with his hand extended high up in the air. Axel looks at him chuckling lightly as another cup of beer flies past them, dousing Eve with its liquid goodness. The jersey... oh shit... its white... The half boos turn to cheers, as Eve basically says "fuck it" and rolls with it, grinding along the railings of the bus to the amusement of the hecklers and whistlers on the street. Raising her arms in the air and shaking herself in small motions, taunting the on lookers at a peak of the goods. Diamond seems to approve as he puts an arm over Ozzy and Axel as they gawk at Eve.* Diamond: I think we have a sell... *The three look at Eve...* *The camera cuts, and D.C. is hopping out of the drivers seat of the bus. They're in a large building, by the looks of it, you could mistake it for a factory or warehouse of some kind. D.C. emerges from the bus and brushes himself down. He holds his arms out as if begging a question.* D.C.: Alright... promos done then? *Axel shakes his head with a cracked smile. Diamond puckers his lips, like hes tasting something sweet, as Ozzy flaps his arms, swatting at a fly, that doesn't seem to exist.* D.C.: What do you mean no???? You f***s didn't say a damn thing up there did you? *D.C. looks at the four as Axel tries to say something, D.C. notices Eve, soaked in beer, with her now see through white Beckham jersey.* D.C. (under his breath): ...goddamn... Axel: NO... we got a better idea... we just mainly raised hell while you drove, but we came up with a good concept to attack Marko and Triple G. *D.C. seems to want to be humored, at this point, something would be better then nothing. D.C. puts his hands on his hips, looking like a chicken as he waits for the announcement.* D.C.: Lay it on me... Axel: Sex... *D.C. stands still, expecting something else to come out. But the place is silent. The only thing heard is the few drips of beer dripping off of Eve, which D.C. can't help but stare at.* D.C.: That's it? *D.C. looks at all three as they nod confidently.* D.C.: The f*** does that have to do with MARKO AND TRIPLE G! F***! COME ON! *D.C. is pissed, like he's at a press conference with Mike Brown after Katrina, asking where FEMA is. Eve rolls her eyes and glares at them. She takes a step forward. Tossing her arms to both sides, making a slap of the wetness surrounding her.* Eve: This is gay... Seriously... *She looks at D.C., "matter of factly" and turns around to face the rest of her crew. D.C. takes a slight step back and checks out her ass with a "damn shame look" as she addresses the rest of the Saintz.* Eve: You guys, we've gone through development camps, we've been coached by D... and we can't even think of a f***ing promo against two nut crumbs from New York... what the f*** are we even doing? What was the f***ing point of the bus ride... other then me having to save it by getting soaked in beer... *Its dead silent, you could probably hear a cricket, except this is a fucking warehouse. Axel lowers his head and shrugs, as Diamond nudges him, Axel turns around sharply pushing him back.* Axel: Dick... stop doing that... Diamond: Your supposed to be the mouthpiece, say something... *Diamond nudges him again, Axel pushes back harder and Diamond puts him in a head lock. For no apparent reason, Ozzy jumps on Diamonds back and tries to put him in a sleeper hold. The camera cuts to D.C. standing next to Eve as they watch the three guys tussle to the floor.* D.C.: Wow... and you guys got kicked out of how many development territories? *Eve rolls her eyes, letting out a sigh. She flickers her hair to the side as D.C. watches the three. The camera pans to them on the floor rolling and throwing punches.* Diamond: CHRIST, HE BIT ME! Axel: NO I DIDN'T YOU ASS! Diamond: NOT YOU! OZZY! Ozzy: YAR! TAKE THAT YA BLIMEY BASTARD! *The three continue their squabble as Eve rolls her eyes, not wanting to look at D.C. for fear of disapproval.* Eve: Four... not counting the one we never actually debuted in... *D.C. lowers his head, defeated.* D.C.: Can you whistle? Eve: ...yeah? *D.C. puts his hand on his face as he turns around. He waves his other arm in the air as if telling her to fix the mess. Eve lets out a loud whistle that echoes through the building. They stand up, as D.C. emerges back with a bottle of water, he drinks it slowly. Axel and Ozzy still nudge at each other as Ozzy growls like a bear on amphetamines. D.C. is also carrying a towel, he hands it to Eve without looking, and walks directly up to Axel. He takes another slip... and stares Axel in the eyes.* D.C.: What the f*** are you doing here kid? Are you cum stains coming into Mayhem... *D.C. walks past Axel and looks into Diamond's eyes.* D.C.: To take on Marko and Triple G at Highway to Hell? Or... are you going to waste yourselves away... *D.C. walks past Diamond and now looks at Ozzy, who is growling with intensity, but as soon as D.C. stands in front of him, he whimpers like a dog.* D.C.: Like every f***ing place you've tried to get into... *D.C. walks past Ozzy and stares at the far wall of the warehouse. He doesn't face the group as Eve is shown off the side drying her hair. The rest of the group stands like an Army platoon, except Ozzy, whose in his own little world.* Axel: We're trying man... damn... *D.C. shakes his head yet again, defeated. He throws his arms out in the air and rips the Saintz of Los Angeles bandana from off his head and throws it on the ground.* D.C.: Then we just wasted this entire day, because none of you could think of anything... we're turning the cameras off... and your going to go to Highway... and I hope you get some f***ing sense knocked into you by Triple G and Marko... *D.C. puts his hand on Axel's head and pushes him back. He turns and walks away as the cameras show the Saintz looking at each other, except Eve, who shakes her head as well, brushing her hair to her side.* Eve: We gotta work on this... *The camera slowly begins to fade to black.*
