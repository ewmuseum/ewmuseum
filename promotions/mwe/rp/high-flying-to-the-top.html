(High)Flying to the top

Oct 12 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Devlin Sharpe: Just a highlight reel? I seriously cant take this anymore everyone sees me just as a human highlight reel, sure I get the glory and stuff but everyone talks behind my back about me having no wrestling ability what so ever and I am trying to make up for it by flying all over the place like an idiot, when in actuality I want to prove to everyone that I can actually mat wrestle with the best of them, however the situations I am forced to in matches do not allow me to properly express myself as a technical genius and people look down on me for that, all I am ever good for is jumping off high buildings without killing myself but what people don't reallise is I put my damn body on the line for their entertainment, they still don't appreciate me as a wrestler they appreciate me as an entertainer alright and to be honest I dont really mind but I wish they would actually respect me as a wrestler but I know that that will never happen as long I wrestle the way I do, unfortunately however I do not have any plans on changing my wrestling style any time soon as it continues to help me win matches on a weekly basis provided I am not dissapearing in to thin air, I remember when I came to the MWE as a singles competitor in my little mortal kombat mask, those times were good I was picking up the occasional win but I never got to shine until i became a part of rated X, and that just proved to me that, I sucked as a singles competitor, that i would never achieve greatness as a singles competitor, after my injury rated X dissolved in to the steaming pile of crap that is the michaels and D.C feud that you can also see on continue on MWE this week, when I was planning my return I was thinking about how I would return how I could make the most impact, should I begin as a singles competitor again and try to get noticed and lost in the shuffle or should I join a tag team and slowly rise in the ranks, however the problem with a tag team is some times your partner can be unreliable and sometimes you do not gel together, tag teams work best when they think alike, that is when the idea of bringing my brother in for a tag team run came to me, with his help I could slowly improve my ring skills to the point where they are good enough to take on the best the MWE has to offer, most people see a tag team wrestler as crap because they rely on other people but relying on other people is often what gets people championships even singles ones, strength in numbers as you put it, with my brother behind me, after we win the tag team titles I can take down each and every other champion in the MWE and take their belts off them and prove to the world that I am a good wrestler in tag AND singles, I plan on dominating MWE with an iron fist and a waist full of gold...X-TV, US, Tag Team and World titles shall all eventually be mine with the "help" of my brother of course...the first step to a succesful singles career however is a win over one of my PPV opponents...the American Animal...he's a tough son of a bitch no doubt about it, however the only american animal he reminds me of is a pidgeon in new york, a pidgeon shit on me once, I crushed it with my bare hands and that....is exactly what I plan to do to the American Animal this week (ooc: again sorry for length brain functionality has shut down at this temporal stage)
