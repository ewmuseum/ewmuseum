Memoirs of a superstar

Sep 28 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
*Ryan sharpe stumbles out of an abandoned warehouse with clear bruises all over his body, he is dragging Devlin sharpe behind him, Devlin has a very big bruise on his head and is knocked out cold, Ryan continues to drag Devlin across the road to a nearby shopping mart, he bursts through the door and the clerk is shocked* Ryan Sharpe: Please, You must help me, we were attacked! Clerk: What do you need? is your friend alright? Ryan Sharpe: He's my brother, I found him unconcious Clerk: Wait I know you guys, The hollywood hooligans, you no showed the match last week man? what happened Ryan Sharpe: We were outcold for a week? quick what day is it? Clerk: Saturday, you can still make it for your match against the bowens on monday Ryan Sharpe: Those clowns again? Clerk: They did beat you... *Devlin sharpe stirs and wakes up* Devlin Sharpe: huh? where the hell am I? Clerk: Welcome to WindyMart a supermarket chain endorsed by former MWE Superstar Windy bridges Devlin Sharpe: Woah, dude you look like me, but stupider, who are you? Ryan Sharpe: I am going to disregard that due to you apparantly having Amnesia Devlin Sharpe: Ryan? Last I remember, we graduated from wrestling school... Ryan Sharpe: Oh great, he's lost his memory, don't you remember anything dev? Devlin Sharpe: Bits and pieces, my chest caving in, the letter X, a blonde girl and a red haired girl, if i saw something maybe it can jog my memory Clerk: David Whitman, Devlin Sharpe and Brian Michaels triple treat for the title, Rated X, Annie Garside, Hayley Williams Ryan Sharpe: How do you know all this? Clerk: Big fan, i have taped every show Ryan Sharpe: Would you mind showing Dev so he can remember? Devlin Sharpe: Thanks man *The clerk leads Devlin and ryan to a room in the back with a moderately large TV and puts a DVD in it opens with an area covered in smoke, the smoke clears and Devlin Sharpe stands in the middle of the smoke with his Mortal kombat like mask* Ryan Sharpe: ...Dude...What? Devlin Sharpe: I remember, i got that from a sub-zero costume, haha *Devlin Sharpe is shown on a stretcher being carried out* Ryan Sharpe: isn't this the one where you injured yourself without actually wrestling? Devlin Sharpe: Shut up man seriously... *Brian Michaels' big speech about how Devlin Sharpe helped him is shown and Devlin subconciously raises his arms in an X motion* Clerk: The birth of the best incarnation of Rated X *D.C Pokes Devlin Sharpe and pins him for the title* Devlin Sharpe: The apocalypse finger poke hehe... *Rated X are shown at a party paid for by sonny williams' wallet* Devlin Sharpe: All sorts of hijinx *Devlin Sharpe and Annie Garside are shown kissing* Ryan Sharpe: Too bad she died... Devlin Sharpe: Yeah bro *Devlin Sharpe is running towards a ring filled with 9 people, jumps to the top rope from the ground and does a shooting star press to everyone in the ring* Devlin Sharpe: The return of devlin sharpe *Hayley Williams is shown with Devlin at the statue of liberty* Devlin Sharpe: Haha boobies... Ryan Sharpe: I think he's back to normal now.... Devlin sharpe: yes, yes I am, thank you for everything my good man Clerk: Don't mention it Ryan Sharpe: Say...what was your name again? Clerk: I am suprised you don't remember me Devlin...its me...John Austin... Devlin Sharpe: Man where you been? John austin: A few weeks after i started MWE I had a career ending injury and I have been managing this, the west newberry mass. branch of WindyMart ever since Devlin Sharpe: Well sorry to hear about that man, but good to see you're still in work John Austin: Yeah, me too, i miss the rush of competing in the ring though Devlin Sharpe: I am sure I would too Ryan Sharpe: Did we actually tell hayley why we were missing? Devlin Sharpe: Oh yes, we didn't did we? hey John wheres the pay phone? John Austin: Its just outside, you sure you remember Hayley's number though? *Devlin sharpe laughes at john austin* Devlin Sharpe: of course i do dude, haha *Devlin phones hayley* Devlin Sharpe: Hey its dev... Hayley Williams: OH...MY...GOD...DEV WHERE THE FUCK HAVE YOU BEEN, I HAVE BEEN WORRIED SICK YOU FUCKING IDIOT, YOU DIDN'T EVEN CALL ONCE FOR THE WHOLE WEEK? WHAT HAPPENED TELL ME EVERYTHING Devlin Sharpe: if i knew lord knows i would tell you, but i dont remember anything, neither does Ryan, but all I can say is we'll be at MWE soon enough ready for our match against.... Hayley Williams: The Bowens....oh and I promised Michaels i wouldnt tell you this but...Rated X is Reuniting, without you Devlin Sharpe: ...I am going to kill them both... Hayley Williams: they did all the prep last week when you went missing, we tried to find you but no one could, sorry babes i tried to tell them but they said if they cant find you they can't do anything, it's a 2 on 3 match aswell... Devlin Sharpe: I shall be returning to MWE soon enough and I will confront them soon enough Hayley williams: Please baby don't fight them, I know you and ryan can take them but please you are friends...and Michaels and DC have had their own problems please don't add to them Devlin Sharpe: For your sake I won't do anything... Hayley Williams: Thank you very much baby, ill show you how much I missed you Devlin Sharpe: Meep...ill be there in 30 seconds *Dev closes the phone and runs inside the store* Ryan Sharpe: Dev, we need to go and kick some ass, Are you ready? Devlin Sharpe: Suck it? Ryan Sharpe: ...Let me break it down for you Devlin, stop making those references Devlin Sharpe: you just did one man!, what are you afraid of lawsuits? John Austin: Good luck with The Bowens guys, I hope you tear them a new one, i can't wait to see you holding champion gold again Dev Devlin Sharpe: Thanks john, that will happen soon enough *Devlin and Ryan hail a cab from the road across the WindyMart as John Austin waves goodbye, the sharpe brothers enter the cab and the cab drives off, leaving behind a neon sign that says "WindyMart Official endorsed by MWE Legend Windy Bridges, WE NOW STOCK WINDY-Os"
