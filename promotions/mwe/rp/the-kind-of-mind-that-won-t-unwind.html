The Kind of Mind that won't Unwind; David Whitman RP

Oct 8 2008

David Whitman

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I couldn’t believe my ears. They were trying to outdo each other, pick the most devastating choice for an opponent. They wanted the person they picked to obliterate the other one. I thought I was safe. But seconds before he said it- I guess I could kind of feel it coming. vs...DAVID WHITMAN! David Whitman. Indeed. Whether Michaels did it to give DC a challenge, or to give ME an @$$whooping; it didn't matter. He had done it, and it was a stroke of genius. I only had a couple of minutes to process it all, though; I had to start walking. Towards those curtains. “Good luck out there, Dave!” People. Patting me on the back. Workers. MWE workers, mostly, and some other people I didn’t know. Handing me a bottle of water, a small towel… It all felt surreal. The guitar riff started, and the crowd got loud. Almost as if they were seeing Korn play the song live. I had been through this a million times before, in a million different places, but this time felt a lot different. I was going for the trifecta. David Silveira‘s drum track triggered the fireworks along with the lights, which in turn got me walking. It all felt very fluid, very automatic; very routine. I stopped and stared at the crowd, which instantaneously put a smile on my face. I waved, waved, did my hand sign, and waved some more. Walked down the ramp, slapping some hands. Entered the ring, got on the top rope… the whole shebang. Then Carry called him out, and it finally re-entered my brain completely. The trifecta. I had to defeat Jekyl, and then the following week I would face DC, and if I had defeated Jekyl; finally Sonny. As I stood there waiting for that deranged clown to come down, I had all that on my mind. Would I be able to defeat him? I would have to, if I wanted a title shot. But even if I DID defeat him, would I be able to defeat DC? And if I do, will I be in good enough shape after that to win back my title? The second Jekyl got into the ring all my thoughts turned into one shaking ball of energy that just burst. A Gorilla Press Slam, a Pump Handle Suplex… then the bell rung. Ding. Ding. Ding. And the rest was history. Of course, I just barely got the pin on him, with that small package. I wasn’t even expecting to get the pin there. But I guess I held on tight enough. In any case, I won the match, and I would get to face Sonny Williams, old friend current foe, at Wrestle Wars a couple of weeks later. But before that, I had another obstacle. One that rocks and rolls by the name of DC. My match at Wrestle Wars is technically speaking not on the line. If I lose against DC, I won’t lose my title shot. At least, not unless Covell decides to shake things up again. That’s what I had on my mind after the match with the clown. Not my title shot at the Pay per View, not getting revenge on Sonny… But DC. My upcoming match against DC. Back in the Mayhem Mafia, we traded a couple of blows, but I’ve never faced him one-on-one before. Well, in a REAL match. This would be it. David Whitman versus DC. One of the loudest, most obnoxious guys around. Which would actually be awesome if he wasn’t such a great wrestler, cuz it would have been amazing to kick his @$$ from here to Nairobi. Of course, life never gives us easy answers, and DC HAS to be who he is. A force in the ring. Former tag champ, former World champ, the list goes on. All I’ve got to remember is that despite all his talent, he’s still… What? A loudmouth? What does that have to do with anything? Does he even have a weakness? Of course. Everyone does. And I could find DC’s, if I tried. But for some reason, I feel as if I shouldn’t. I should leave it alone. I want this match to be David Whitman at his peak versus DC at his best. A head-on collision between two trains traveling at full speed. A battle of strengths, not a hunt for weaknesses. An all-out fight to the finish. Wednesday, October 8th, 2008. David Whitman is sitting on a couch in front of a TV. It’s a living room, and his cousin James is just a couple of feet away from him and sitting in a chair drinking beer. James is looking worriedly at his cousin, who seems very attentive to what is playing on the screen. All of a sudden, the door opens, and Eddie, Scott and Thomas all walk in. Eddie: Dang man, I knew you said your uncle’s place would be hard to find, but JESUS! James jumps up out of his seat and walks over to them. James: Thank God you guys are here! He’s been like this all day! James turns to David, and the others follow suit. Eddie: What’s wrong with him? James: I have NO idea. He’s been watching this DC promo for the last two hours… He won’t budge. I think he’s lost it. Scott: Well… is he depressed or something? James: No, man, I think he’s furious. I think he’s probably gonna get up any second now and trash everything. Probably eat a small horse and set a pre-school on fire. Thomas: Ah, college… They all turn to Thomas. Thomas: Oh come on, you prudes. They all shrug Thomas off as they walk over to David, whose glare is still fixated on the screen. He does not seem agitated; his facial expression is one of pure concentration. Eddie: He looks like a little scientist, ogling his first White Dwarf. Thomas: EW! Now THAT'’s SICK, dude! Eddie: No, I’m saying- sigh. Never mind. Scott: Maybe he needs a beer or something. I’ll go get one. As Scott goes to the kitchen, Thomas sits down next to David. Thomas: Hey kid. Listen, I know you’re probably worried… Thomas pauses for a second to see if David reacts, which he doesn’t. Thomas: Scared… Still no reaction. Thomas: Uhm… happy? … HEY? HELLO?! COME ON, MAN, get a grip! Thomas slaps David hard across the face. James quickly grabs Thomas and pulls him off the couch. David slowly rubs his face, and continues staring at the screen. James: Dude, calm down! Thomas: HE’S THE ONE BEING A PRICK! Scott comes back from the kitchen with a six-pack of beer in one hand and a sandwich in the other. He sits down next to David on the couch. Scott: Dude, here, have a beer. Scott hands David a beer. David holds the beer in his hand but keeps staring at the TV screen. Scott: Dude, man. I know how you feel. You’re infuriated. You want to kick Diminutive C*ck’s @$$ and beat him down till the point where he can’t even get up if Lindsey tied her extensions to his and did a triple somersault right onto American Animal’s dick. Thomas starts laughing loudly as Eddie and James just shake their heads. Eddie: WHAT. THE. HELL. Scott: Shut up. My point is, David; that I’ve been there. I’ve been there a lot. This happens to me like, EVERY DAY. Man, if I had a nickel for every time it happened to me, I’d be like “WHERE THE HELL ARE THESE NICKELS COMING FROM?!” And then I’d probably have a mental breakdown. Everyone looks at Scott as he shrugs and continues eating his sandwich. Thomas walks over to David and sits down next to him, on the other side. He takes the beer David has in his hands and drinks from it. Thomas: You know, he’s right. If Dingle Cherry- Thomas turns to the others to see if anyone is laughing; Scott is literally rolling on the floor laughing as Eddie is walking away, shaking his head, and James is looking real serious. Thomas turns back to David. Thomas: If that punk is getting you mad, you ought to just take that and run with it. Get in that ring, and sweep the mat with him. You know you can take him. Stop stressing and get up and DO something! James: YEAH! Like drink! David lets out a chuckle. James: Wh- David starts laughing, slowly. He takes the TiVO remote control and presses pause. He laughs for a good minute as the others look at each other, not certain of what their reactions should be. Then, as abruptly as he started, David stops laughing. He presses play, and the footage of DC talking continues playing. Eddie: Uh… Eddie taps Scott on the shoulder, and tilts his head towards the door. Eddie: We got that… thing… remember, man? Scott: OH! RIGHT! Scott jumps up, and pats David on the back. Scott: Just remember to train some before your match, hmkay? Thomas gets up and follows Eddie and Scott as they head for the door. Thomas: Wait for me! I got that thing too! James: N- no, you guys! GUYS! Don’t you- James gets up as well and runs towards the door. He catches them just before they get to Thomas’ SUV. James: HEY! What the hell is wrong with you guys?! You’re not gonna support David?! Help him out a bit?! Eddie: Dude, we already tried our best. What else do you want us to do? Thomas: Yeah! And besides, he’s always getting into this crap. “Look at me, I’m depressed, I got the blues! Look at me, I’m feeling insecure because I haven’t had a girlfriend for years! Look at me, I fell on my head and got a concussion and now I’m crazy!” I mean what is UP with him?! They all look at Thomas for a second. Thomas: Yeah, yeah, I know, back seat. Thomas sighs as he hands his keys to Eddie and enters the SUV through the back door. Eddie: Sorry about that, J. Scott: Yeah, but you know, in his own crude, un-PC manner; Thomas DID have a point though, man. I mean, David’s always getting down or whatever- James: He’s had a rough couple of months! Eddie: Yeah, but he does act out a bit. James: His LIFE has been turned upside-DOWN since rejoining MWE! Scott: I think he’s using anti-depressants. James: HE’S BIPOLAR, FOR CHRIST’S SAKE! Eddie and Scott look at each other, then at the ground. James: Look, I know David isn’t the most stable person around, but he’s not some little kid. Well yeah, he’s younger than all of US, but I mean- we shouldn’t treat him like that little tag-along that always wants to hang with the big boys. He’s held the world title, TWICE! He’s been in a faction that could probably have held its own against us! Eddie: I don’t know about THAT. Scott: Yeah, you and Thomas DID train him. James: Yeah, whatever. All I know is, he got his brains kicked in by Jekyl and Sonny fairly recently. He got his revenge on Jekyl a couple of days ago, and now before he faces Sonny- for the WORLD TITLE, might I add- he has to face DC. A guy whose record speaks for itself, a guy who ran his former stable-mate over with a CAR, a guy who is psychologically probably the complete OPPOSITE of him. Scott: How so? James: You HEARD the guy? DC speaks his mind, and doesn’t hold back. David cares about everyone’s feelings. DC spits in the face of authority. David… Well, he doesn’t like authority, but has never been one to actively pursue anything resembling an anti-authority plan. DC’s got ego, or self-confidence, if you will. A hot girlfriend- Scott: EWWWWWWW! Eddie: I dunno dude, I’d do her. Scott: Then you’d be doing a million other dudes. James: I got a great line for that one, but later. ANYWAY; My point BEING, David has every RIGHT in the world to feel down, or angry, or confused, or whatever the HELL he’s feeling like. I can’t say I have any idea at all about exactly what’s going on through his mind right now, but if I were him, I know I’d definitely be thinking about a couple of things. Hell, I’d be angry at Michaels for making this happen, depressed because I knew I had a chance of having any and all momentum destroyed before my next title match, and probably just unable to cope in general. Eddie: You’re right, man. I’m sorry. Scott: Yeah, me too. We should be more supportive. James: That’s all I wanted to hear. Heck, man, knowing David, he’s probably going to stay quiet up until his match. Not a peep. Probably won’t even MOVE. Then, almost as if on cue, a very loud voice emanates from inside the house. I GOT IT! I GOT IT! The door swings open and David walks out. With a brisk pace, he walks up to Eddie and snatches the car keys from him. David: Let’s go boys, we got a couple of things to do! David gets into the driver’s side as the others hurry to get inside the car. James: Where are we going, man? David: Well, I got a great idea. You know how Covell kept telling me that I needed to d- Thomas: DAYUM, MAN, YOU TALK TOO MUCH! A moment of silence passes before they all burst into laughter and David steps hard on the gas...
