Back to the Past 1; Zack Evermore MNM RP

Zack Evermore

There are great many people in the world. A common practice is to categorize said people into groups. For the purpose of the following exercise, we will be using realities as a grouping method. As a wise man once said; there are a great many people in the world. Some walk the earth inherently content and immersed solely in the world around them; the external, the daily, the social. These people have their mind wrapped around the belief that life is solid, facts are concrete and proof is indubitable. We also have those who struggle to live in the external world. Those who have a separate reality, one inside their minds, that is different from the so-called "real" outer reality. These people, believe it or not, seem to make up the majority of earth's population. Maybe you yourself are one of them. Your mind, everything you think, feel... It is all seemingly within your own body, is it not? These strugglers tell themselves daily to focus on the external; they battle their own feelings and emotions, they strive towards making the "real" world as close to the world they've created in their mind; their "ideal world", their "happy place", their "goal in life", their "this is how it should be". There are also those who live completely and solely in the reality they've created. These people are often unable, or unwilling, to cope with the external world around them. Most often they end up in institutions, but not always; some are even known as visionaries, artists and geniuses in this day and age but were considered odd and reclusive when they lived. And we have the people who have somehow, through miracle, madness, circumstance or luck, been able to seamlessly integrate both versions of reality; the world around them collides and meshes with what they believe or decide to believe. “Is that a flat tire? Awesome! I just got a flat tire!” Why is it awesome? In our external world, it would by and far usually be categorized as "not awesome", if not way worse. But in the minds of these people, in their reality, it is awesome, because it just is. Because just BEING, in essence, is awesome, therefore any product thereof will inevitably be... Awesome. Some might call these people "blissfully unaware", but honestly, who are we to decide which version of reality is "right"? Who am I, who are you, to decide just what and who is truly aware and unaware? If anything, I propose we should envy these people. Or, better yet, try to become like them. How? Simple. Observe them carefully. Oh look, Here's one now... -- ... But he could play the guitar, just like a ringin' a bell!" The blackness slowly fades into red. It takes it sweet time zooming out, revealing to us that the red is, in fact, a square, surrounded by more red and white squares. Zooming out even further, the camera soon gives us enough of a vision to realize the checkerboard-ish pattern forms a floor. More zoomage ensues and we see similarly checkered tablecloths on tables, tables that have miniature jukeboxes on them. Walls, sparse in color but well-adorned by old metal signs, remind us that Coca-Cola is indeed Refreshing! , not to mention The Right Choice! Among these many vintage signs of smiling girls and men and even the odd child we see an ATM, and even the machine you would believe is a relic due to its old-fashioned design. The feeling that we've been transported to the past continues nagging at us as a young man in a white cooking apron and paper hat passes by, spinning a big empty plate on his right index finger. While we are slowly but surely becoming aware that this seems to be a dining establishment of sorts, we hear a chorus of voices proclaim an enthused "HELLO!" as a family of three enters, smiling and returning the friendly greeting to the staff of the eatery. The camera moves, swerving and at a bouncy pace, first passing by the plate-spinner, then by a girl in similar white and black outfit sporting a ponytail and small red bow, and then by yet another young man and woman; the male wearing a black bowtie and the female in small rectangular reading glasses and ponytail like the previous girl. What these youths have in common is not only their chipper demeanor, genuine-seeming smiles and matching white upper clothing and black pants, but also the upbeat "Hello!" each of them yells, but never too loudly, at the camera as it passes each them one by one. Soon we reach a booth near a corner, and the first thing you would think would catch your eye would be the cute girl dressed in the flowing white gown and paper hat with several signatures on it. Her hair, green and black and yellow and blue, is done up in two pigtails; one on either side, with the right one seemingly longer by an inch or two. But what in fact seems more startling is the hulking brute sitting next to her. His light pink bodyshirt contrasts his dissatisfied frown, and the paper hat placed upside-down on his bald, dome-y head only adds to the comedic effect. The camera turns an exact 90 degrees so that now we can see the entire booth. And sitting across from the peculiar young lady and the monstrously massive man is Zack Evermore. Wearing a pair of white pants and a white and black striped shirt, he almost seems to fit right into the place, except for the streak of red in his hair. And the feral look in his wide eyes. And of course, what he's doing. He is shaking, almost imperceptibly, at an alarmingly fast rate. His grin, fixed and broad, is unwavering. It is, by any and all standards, a very unsettling scene. On the table we two empty plates, on in front of the big man and one in front of the comparatively small woman. We also notice each person at the table has a big glass filled with red and white liquid topped off with cream. Gooey. Zack is now shaking more visibly. His two friend just state at him, indifferently. Suddenly and to no one in particular, the shaking Zack says something. "Yes. Just Yes." He proceeds to shake his head wildly like that one emoticon I use in MSN conversations. If you haven't added me yet it's [detail withheld]. Back to your regularly scheduled RP. After his impromptu head bang session, Zack calms down considerably. His big buddy taps him on the shoulder. “You ‘kay there, buddy? Hmm? “YESSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSS” Zack jumps up, and runs off. That emptiness simulates the amount of time it takes for Zack to return to his seat and begin staring at the camera. “This place… is Johnny Rockets. And listen to me, person… THIS PLACE IS AWESOME!!!” He headbangs again. “You know when people go “You know what’s wrong with this country?”, well let me ask you THIS… Do you know what’s RIGHT with this country?” He takes a deep breath. “THIS! Places like this, preserving the old-school fun and awesomeness of a time long gone. THIS MALT? IT RULES, BY THE WAY. But what we're here to do, except for indulge in my happy go-go living an innocent life fantasy, is to spread the word about MWE. Specifically, my next match with C! J! WILLIAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAMS!” He does that thing that Sinc Mercier does, just ‘cause maybe Sinc is reading this. Hey Sinc, sup?! "Yes yes, so let me get right down to it… My next match is against a man named CJ Williams. Though I think CJ is just a weird name, I know for a fact that this particular man is a force in the ring. AND WE ALL KNOW WHAT THAT MEANS!!!" It seems that nobody knows what that means, as Will and Zack’s nameless girlfriend just stare at him. “It means that next Monday, 19720 people are going to be sitting there in Oracle Arena using their wide gaping mouths as flycatchers, since we’re wrestling in that ring with so much love for the sport that even mentioning it just made that entire sentence sound gay!” Again with the quick headbanging. “STOP IT BELLY, HOMOSEXUAL PERSONS ARE PEOPLE TOO! YOU BIGOT!” The big man raises an eyebrow at the childlike idiot. “I didn’t say ANYTHING!” “Look, my point is… We've got a lot in common, CJ. You were trained by an MWE superstar, I was trained by an MWE superstar...'s cousin. HEY IT COUNTS! He smiles and thumbs-ups the camera. "My MWE debut match was hella fun, finally got to use the High Five, which I hadn't used in like MILLENIA! BUT, my El Cajonian friend, this next match should, could, dare I say it? WILL! Be even better! The big man turns to the girl. “First time he ever says my name, and it’s completely out of context.” “Well, you snooze, you lose!” “… what the hell does that MEAN?!” Zack is still staring at the camera, not letting up one bit. "Rumor has it; you got your contract based on pure luck, CJ. Well I don't DO rumors, do I Belly? "Will." "No, I don't, nor WILL I ever!" "No you dickface, my name is Will." "OH! Yeah yeah whatever, sorry 'bout that, mah Belarusian homeskrilla!" "Skrilla means money." "Well YOU sure know a lot about American slang for a man who shook hands with Medvedev at the winter palace!" "Goddamit Zack, I've lived in Jersey for the past twenty years! I don’t know the first thing about Belarus, it’s all a blur." "Whatever you say, White Russian!" Will mumbles “that’s a mixed drink, dumbass,” as Zack continues speaking to the camera. "This match is gonna rule. For those watching at home, a feast for the eyes. Fluidity in motion, two masters of the craft perfecting their art in the squared circle. This man has a four-win-streak as a career OPENING. Me, I won a title winning my first televised match ever. When the two of us get into that ring… It will be like seeing a swan take flight. A very aggressive and violent swan, BUT HEY SHUT UP LEAVE MY ANALOGIES ALONE YO" As Zack stares at the camera, it zooms out slowly, revealing the girl with small glasses from earlier standing there and smiling at Zack. Zack slowly turns to her, and grows a matching innocent smile. After a few oddly quiet seconds, she speaks. "And how is everything over here? Alright?" "Yes… Andrea, thank you." "Can I get you anything else?" "No thanks, you've been great!" Zack fishes in his pockets and pulls out two fifty dollar bills, handing them to the waiting waitress with a smile and a hand-signal that seems to convey something like “Keep the change”. She "Thank you so much!"es him and walks off, seemingly oblivious to the camera. But Zack definitely isn’t. "When I was younger, my parents continuously strived and tried NOT to raise me like what would basically amount to Richie Rich. The reason for this was less than noble; having seen David Whitman's less than wealthy parents squander money on useless things in what could only be described as near clinical levels, my mom and dad were scared that I could become that way by association. Actually HAVING money to squander would make my ostensible habit quite harder to monitor, and since it was their money, it wouldn't benefit them in the least." Zack coughs, as he has doing a bit lately. "Despite their reasons being less than selfless and discounting the fact that this all meant I only peripherally knew David through James Whitman even though I lived most of my life next to David, it helped me because at an early age I decided never to live my life relying on anybody else. CJ, what you did, that whole thing with your friend’s mother… HEYO! But yeah, I mean you did what you had to. You saw an opportunity and took it, ran with it. And I’m fine with that; from what I’ve seen you’re a pretty decent guy. So don’t let anyone tell you otherwise. ESPECIALLY not negative people. A deep breath. "In an age when negativity and bad vibes reign supreme, you and I fight a never-ending battle against evil! We are the last line of defense against senseless uncoolness. Come on, SON! You know what I'm saying!" Do you? Do you really? You should, because the RP ends here. No, seriously. We’ll be back after these commercials…
