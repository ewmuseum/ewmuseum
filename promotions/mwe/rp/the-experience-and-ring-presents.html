The Experience and Ring Presents

Feb 21 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The scene opens in New York, New York. In the centre of the screen is a black stretch limousine, parked right out front of a five-star grand hotel. Walking out of the hotel, carrying two suitcases, is Alejandro Rodriguez, wearing a black button-up shirt and jeans, coming out shortly behind him is Darrel Jones, wearing sun-glasses, white shirt and jeans. Right beside him is Billy Joe Barker, looking unusual with a tan, he is wearing a cowboy hat, his favourite cow-boy boots, a Tex-Mex shirt, and denim jacket and jeans. Darrel and Billy-Joe are both carrying suitcases. Alejandro huffs and puts down his suitcases and taps on the drivers window. The driver rolls down the window. Driver: Yes sir? Alejandro: Hey jackass, you ain't getting paid to sit in your damn car. Now pop the trunk and help us with these cases. The driver nods, he pops the trunk and gets out of the car, he, Alejandro, Darrel and Billy Joe get the suitcases inside, The driver then opens the doors and Darrel and Billy Joe get inside, Alejandro hesitates, and he looks into the driver's eyes. Alejandro flashes a smile. Alejandro: Good boy... Alejandro gets inside the car and the driver shuts the door, the interior of the car is of white and black design, with the seats white leather, a white roof and black floor, Billy Joe leans back and tilts his cowboy hat over his eyes to go to sleep as the limo starts to . Darrel checks his watch and then looks at Alejandro. Darrel: Our flight to Dublin leaves at five, it's an hour to the airport, so we got plenty of time. Alejandro: Ugh... Darrel: What? Alejandro: Fucking Ireland... Does Covell just throw a dart to decide where we go? In two weeks I bet we'll be in fucking Melbourne... Darrel: What's gotten into you today? Alejandro: Uh... I've just been wondering, if it's all been getting to me. Darrel: Maybe you just need a break... Alejandro: Yeah maybe, like forever. Darrel sits up fast and looks wildly alarmed at Alejandro, who is laid back in the corner. Darrel: Surely you don't mean retirement? Alejandro shrugs. Darrel: But you're in your prime! You're not even 30 for Christ's sakes... This is your dream, your boyhood dream, remember? You're the one guy in MWE who cares, actually cares about MWE and cares about wrestling. You haven't taken a break since... since... since forever! Alejandro: Yeah, well maybe a break is what I need. Darrel turns to face Billy Joe as the driver pulls onto the highway, he sees he is asleep then he lets the matter rest. Darrel opens a mini-fridge and takes out two bottles of beer. Darrel: So what about your match this week? Darrel opens both the bottles and then hands one to Alejandro. Alejandro: What about it? Darrel: Well it's against Project Revolution, surely you must be feeling something. Alejandro: I'll tell you how I'm feeling, confident. And besides, it's between me and Billy, it's out business. Darrel sits up straighter after taking a swig of your beer. Darrel: Surely it's my business if I manage you. Alejandro: I got none of your fuckin' business. There is a slight pause and Alejandro takes a sip of beer. Alejandro: Sorry... I just don't wanna take the time to talk about my opponents all day... Darrel checks his watch. Darrel: We have 55 minutes until we reach the airport, is that enough time? Alejandro sighs, he looks outside the window and then he puts down his beer. Alejandro: I suppose it is... well what do you want me to say about Animal and Jason Queer? They're hot young prospects, strong, young and quick... But they're arrogant little toe-rags, they don't show up to their matches, they don't have the experience... Back in the day if you fuck around with the business, you get your ass handed to you, so that's exactly what me and Billy are going to do. Alejandro clears his throat and drinks some more beer. Alejandro looks over at Billy sleeping and sighs. Alejandro: And besides, I don't even know why you're worrying, Darrel... These guys might look tough, but really, all you have to do is get around them, they don't have the ring presence, speed or experience to match mine, and they don't have the sheer power or natural brawling skills that Billy has. In what possible area could Project Revolution match us? Alejandro looks at Darrel hoping he would give an answer, but Darrel says nothing. Alejandro: I know I can't speak for Billy the Kid over here, but I'm looking forward to this match. Teaching young punks some respect is something that I always get a kick out of. If you can't respect this business and the people in it then you don't deserve to be in it. I would care if Animal and Fear were the most talented guys on the roster, if they fuck the MWE up, we fuck them up... That's the way it works. Darrel looks satisfied as Alejandro looks over at Billy and then back out of the window. Darrel drinks some more and then looks at Billy. Darrel: Do you think we should wake Billy, he should really sleep on the flight. Alejandro: Sure. Alejandro pats Billy on the shoulder, but the big guy don't stir. Alejandro shakes him back and forth. Alejandro looks at Darrel, who shrugs. Alejandro: Shout Chuck Norris in his ears... Billy jumps up, startling Alejandro. Billy looks around with sleepy eyes. Billy-Joe: Did sum'wun say Chuck Norris? Alejandro: Yeah it was me you big ape... Darrel: We were just talking about your match this week. Billy-Joe: Agenst, Prohjec' Rev'lucun? Darrel: Revolution... Billy-Joe: Tha's what a said, Farrel. Darrel: ...Darrel. Billy-Joe: Carrel. Darrel: ...DARREL! Billy-Joe: PARREL! Alejandro: Enough! Darrel and Billy-Joe look at Alejandro, who is holding his hand to his head as if trying to keep away a bad headache. Alejandro grabs a beer for Billy. Billy takes it. Billy: Thanks. Alejandro: So what do you think about Project Revolution, Billy? Billy: Well a dunno... They is tough an' big... But we ist tough an' big too... Plus we got da 'xperience and da ring presents... An' you're awful smart and quick Alejambo. Alejandro: With all that charisma Billy, you'll be the next Ric Flair. Billy: Gee, thanks. Darrel lifts up his glass. Darrel: Well, may I propose a toast... Alejandro: Well what are we proposing to? Darrel thinks about it for a second... Darrel: A sucessful... and long... career. Billy: A'll drink ta that! Darrel and Billy clink their glasses. And after a long stare from Darrel, Alejandro joins them. __________________________________________________ Not terrific but I'm not wasting my time on guy who don't RP.
