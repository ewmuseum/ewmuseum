That awkward first handshake...; Zack Evermore RP

Oct 26 2011

Zack Evermore

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
From pure, undiluted blackness, the view we witness slowly filters into visibility. Bit by bit and pixel by pixel, a big brute of a man is slowly becoming crystal clear, and thanks to all-new state-of-the-art MWEHD technology, soon enough we can see this mountain of fear-inducing muscle quite clearly. Staring into the camera, the monster speaks. "MWE..." He slowly lowers his head, almost as if in shame. "... I just want you all to know how sorry I am." Without warning, a stupidly grinning face pops out in front of the man. "HEYOOOOOOOOOOOO!" The goofy face backs up a bit as we hear a loud sigh emanate from behind him. Ignoring it, he snaps his fingers and shoots a fake gun at the camera. Old school. "aWhaddup, viewer?! For those of you that are ill, or nil, informed, let me introductificate myself. I am none other than MWE's M! W! E!" He stops, staring at the camera, waiting for it to sink in. "Mayhem Wrestling Entertainment's... Main! Wrestler! Evermore!!!" He looks down, shaking his head. "I apologize. That was ridiculous. And besides, i was just kidding. About the main wrestler part, i mean. My name IS Evermore, that's ZACK Evermore, even more Zack than ever!" Zack starts moshing violently to the suddenly-present Irish punkery of Flogging Molly's "Lightning storm", and we see the big man from earlier try to grab him as the camera cuts to static abruptly. The scene opens up once again after the refreshing static-break. This time Zack Evermore seems a bit calmer, leaning back in a big plush sofa. His surroundings seem nicer as well; there is a mini-fridge to his left, and to his right, next to the giant man from earlier, there is a huge gumball machine with a seemingly custom-fitted (read: bigger than necessary) dropping slot. The machine's round glass globe is filled to capacity with assorted candies, energy drinks and cans of Tac Bac.. And, despite the nicely painted walls and various paintings adorning the place, Zack is still in his board shorts and baffling black "Mayhemmingway" shirt. He slowly cultivates a smile for the camera. "BACK TO THE INTRODUCTION!" A pause for pause-sterity. "Speaking of BACK... And this time, it's a RELEVANT segue... Some of you may not know this but back in the day, I eh... Enjoyed? ... a brief stint in MWE. Very brief, in fact; I left after one match. Why? Because politics make my head hurt. And my heart, too." He stays perfectly still, staring at the camera for a few serious seconds before shaking his head around wildly. "Dag NABBIT! I just put the EMO in EVERMORE! Oh yes, the letters are there, you just gotta rearrange them! Think outside the wrestling-ring shaped BOX already, people!" He takes a deep breath, closes his eyes and mouths a 5-count. He returns to us with a fresh new smile, signaling all is right with the world. At least, with his world. "MOVING ON!" A hand appears from off-camera giving Zack papers as he takes them without acknowledging the random hand. He flips through the papers as he speaks. Note that he does not LOOK at them, he merely flips THROUGH them. "After carefully reviewing the current backstage situation, I have come to the conclusion that we are going to HAVE to do something about the motherflippin' soda machines. Because A), why call it a soda machine if it ain’t got the OG soda, CLUB soda, in it? Club soda's so OG it's got CLUB right in the name! Which brings me to B); FRUCK you if you think amma survive without mah Club Soda, flitches!" Zack suddenly turns back to his big bad buddy. "Don't you dare." The man shrugs, serious as ever. "I have to say it." "No." "Yes." "Why?" "Because it's true." "That's subjecti-" "Club Soda tastes like ass." Zack completely loses it. The cam cuts to a millisecond of static and returns. Zack is cool and collected again, this time standing next to what appears to be a moving truck. The outside of said truck is completely covered in marker scribblings; a great many words and doodles, though the most noticeable is the big "Evermore" in the middle, also of apparent permanent marker origin. "Tell her to stop messing around and get a move on, Belly!" The big man walks out from behind the truck and stands next to Zack. "Stop calling me Belly!" "Okokoko but tell her!" "She won't listen to me. Did you tell her to listen to me? Anyway, she's coming out now." A cute, smiling girl steps into view. And she just happens to be wearing a Catwoman mask. She directs her smile at Zack. "You need to get a driver. I'm not driving you and Belarus around anymore." The jock cringes. "Mr. Anderson would do nicely. Hell, even Will." She turns to the huge man with a surprised expression. "Awwww how sweet! It thinks it can talk to me after calling me quirky!" She pinches his cheeks and walks off as he sighs. "Is she ever gonna let that go?" "You should be nicer to people." "She changes her name daily! You can't tell me that's not quirky!" Zack shakes his head. "Haters be hatin." He finally returns his attention to the camera, now about 132% more excited than he's been this entire time. "SO CHECK THIS! I'm sitting at home eating Pacman and playing cereal- WAIT WHAT, I gotta calm down, I'm too excited! Anyway, the phone rings, and it's James! Whitman, of course. So I’m like AW YIEAH, this fool has GOTs to get over here, this is some MAD cereal!" Will Anderson nods slowly. "I can attest to the figurative anger of said breakfast food." Zack raises his finger, so wait hold up. "BUT! Turns out D.C had called him about the re-opening of MWE, and James was calling me to see if I'd be interested in giving it another shot! He explained the new power structure and all that jazz, and I said "Well lick my pancreas and call me slippysticks!", and then he promptly hung up on me." Zack shakes his head. "That silly frucker. But yeah! So I went through the whole process and everything, got accepted, the flitches said they like me but I gotta work on my... I believe it was... Sanity? Fruck if i know. What I DO know and was excited as all flying flipfaces about... Is getting HIT IN THE FACE with THE! MOST AMAZING! Debut match… IN ALL RECORDED HISTORY!!!" Zack stays calm, but inexplicably manages to look like he's about to explode with excitement. Yeah, that's what she said. You know I rock that. "LIEF O' FRICKAFRACKIN' NEIL, PEOPLE!” Evermore’s smile grows even more wide than usual. “If you can think of a more MINDMELTING first main event for the first new Mayhem, then SON, YOU NEEDS YO HEAD SCANNED!” He turns serious as he yells that last caps-locked bit into the camera. “Look, have you even HEARD this dude’s music? Total rulage. This one time I was out with David and them, and we somehow ended up in Ireland. POINT IS, O’NEIL, you remember that show? I think it was… Gallagher’s? Three years ago? July? Hell, I dagnab near broke my neck during your set!” Zack headbangs fiercely for 3 seconds, then stops and gets serious. “O’Neil, I envy you. My own band, “SNAP!”, didn’t make it HALF as far as you did. All the way out to Ireland, that is. Well, then again, I guess you should look at it relatively, I meant, we were big in our own state, so… WELL LOOK, THE POINT IS, if any one of you thinks this dude’s music ain’t good just because he hasn’t so-called “hit it big”, do NOT make me remind you of what popular music nowadays is like…” He raises an eyebrow at Anderson. “All I said was that MAYBE, SOMETIMES, ‘Pokerface’ could be described as a somewhat catchy tu-“ Zack shoves his hand into Will’s face, pushing him away. “O’NEIL! This Monday, there is no doubt that we will tear that roof off the arena! They are gonna have to duct tape the Staples Center back together! It’s the great FRUCK-KING, Charlie Brown! FRUCKING EPIC MAIN EVENT, THAT IS! Spreaking of fruck, fruck pumpkins, goblins, and Cheetos, SON! We are gonna TRICK these fans so hard by TREATING them to such a SICK match they’re gonna go back and DEMAND to pay MORE for the tickets they bought! DAGNABBIT LIFE O’RILEY, DO YOU NOT COMPREHEND HOW AWE-TASTIC OUR MATCH IS GOING TO BE?!” Will Anderson peeks his head back onto the scene. “The… the camera can’t actually answer y-“ “AS A MATTER OF FACT!” He slowly turns to Will, furious, mouths “Lady Gaga?” to him, and Will slowly moves away and off-screen. Zack then turns back to the camera with a mental smile on his face. “AS A MATTER OF FACT, LIEF! How about we raise the stakes? I win, you autograph my back and I get it tattooed on!” Will Anderson walks back onto the scene. The two share a slight smile, then high-five. Zack returns his attention to the camera, as Anderson returns to his usual business of not looking pleasant. Dude apparently just likes tattoos. “And if YOU win, well I dunno, you can have my dog or something. Wait-" Zack turns to Anderson, who's nodding lightly at him. "Yes, you rescued a puppy from a tree recently. No, I don't know where it is." Zack’s head drops. "EVERY FRICKIN TIME!" Catwoman comes back on screen. “Wrap it up, fool. Don’t wanna drown these people with your Zackiness just yet.” She pops back out as Zack nods, and Will walks off in the other direction. The camera zooms in on Zack’s now-serious face, as he looks to his left, then his right, then directly at the camera for 20 seconds, before yelling angrily… “BUY THIS SHIRT!” The camera cuts out roughly.
