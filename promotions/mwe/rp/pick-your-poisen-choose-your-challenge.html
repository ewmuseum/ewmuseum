Over the past few years in Efedding I've noticed that the highest graded RP's are always the one's that deal with the biggest issues. Murder, Revenge, Love etc. Maybe because it's perceived that they're harder to write. In my opinion though, they've become so cliché' that it makes them easier. So here is my attempt at writing a "real" RP, because not every wrestler has to go through drama every day to be a World Champion. Sometimes, a story can be straight-up told without all the Hollywood extra's stuff on it. I may be alone but I'm aiming for realism, entertainment and to write the story well. Not to make his every day a blockbuster. Don't get me wrong, sometimes RP's do need that big feel to it. But I think an RP actually loses realism if it's like that every day.
