The lights point to ONE man.

Nov 30 2008

(NOTE: HOW THE DESCRIPTION IS IS SIMPLY DESCRIPTION NOT A REAL VOICE. THIS CAN’T BE SEEN AS SOMETHING TO REPLY TO IT IS MERELY DESCRIPTION, THAT IS ALL!) The apple ipod….a nifty little tool gotta admit, but Samuel Steele is certainly diggin it. The sound the dance, the music, the groove, lovin the tune, lovin it all. In a mall lots of cats, walkin, talkin, wearin their fancy hats. As he is strollin and rollin through the mall he sees a computer store, big sign, very tall. “Gateway Technology” it stated, Steele looks happy and somewhat elated. A song plays on his ipod touch, he pumps up the volume and dances and sings, a bit too much. Why, why it’s Kanye West! HOT DAMN! Here we go, great tune, big vibe, the right way to go. He enters the store and all its galore. He goes to a large computer, the screen nice and lean, he wants to test it out, one of the employee’s approach him, see what hes about. Employee: Checking out our merchandise are we? Steele takes those earphones out he wants to scream and shout, but he talks, politely, seeing that what he wants to say is sweet and mighty. Steele: I wouldn’t call it merchandise old man, more like a thingy, yea…a technological thingy to fiddle with right man? Heh heh…. This employee is very thin, he has white, gray hair in a comb over, he bears small, black rimmed glasses, white slacks, red buttoned shirt, and a wrinkled smile upon his saggy face, Samuel Steele wouldn’t set such an old coon in his place. Employee: I am oh so very glad you have such interest in our computers here at Gateway young lad. Are you looking to buy? Steele: Maybe later coon. Right now I just wanna type man, I wanna jot my thoughts down like a real live pro baby! Steele opens up a web browser, Internet explorer it seems, he’s typin away by the minute, he’s on a forum? Mwewrestling.com, he seems to be posting a topic, he’s gonna pin it. QUOTE Steele: Dear MWE….I hav had some reel fon bein in da fed an all. But I tink tht ma oppont, steph cowman wil beet mee. He’s a kool guy an hee knws teh ropes. A frmer Nwf world champ an gold metallist. He’s a likable guy wif a guud hart. [/center] The employee seems unhappy and impatient not liking the pace of Steele’s typin and how long he’s takin, but these words are real and passionate, he ain’t fakin. Employee: So are you going to buy it or do you need more testing time youngun? Steele stops typing for a minute and looks at the old man in digression, and folds his arms, raising alarm. Steele: If I’m gonna damn well buy something I want to test it f*ckin out! SO LET ME TEST IT PORCUPINE LIPS! Steele’s eyes are bulged and irritated, angry and disappointed, his temper is ill faded. The old man understands and shuts his mouth, Steele keeps typing, as silent as a mouse. QUOTE Steele: N E way…sry I stalld thre for a sec an old ass ragedy ass buzard stpped mee….but N E way bak 2 buznezz. I dun waynt 2 sund lik a payne in da ass but if I am able 2 beet cowman I wuld b so gr8ful. He haz bin lozin l8tly an I tink I cann take him out if I rly try hard. He may hav bin a champ b4 but I hav the passion an the want fir victoree 2 beet em. I danc, I groov, I sing, I bling, I’m gunna mak sur I giv it my all aganst him cuz I can and I will. Today I am ma own man. No mre ma ma to guide me, no fatha besid me. I’m gunna win this for ma fans, and thse who feel tht groov lik me. No mre crak or weed, I can doo dis wif out dat shiyat. Peace an freedom. Love an respect. That’s what im bout. Lata all tanks 4 reedin. [/center] Steele is done, and he is proud of his rant for all those who care, his fans, his friends, his family, no sin or burden to bare. But that employee is still eyeing him, that vulture that buzzard, he is fryin him. Steele: Man do I look like prison bait? Look away before I beat yo ass! That shit was personal son! Steele stands up out of the chair, grey hoodie, black hat, and black sweats he wears. The old man doesn’t say NOTHIN! He just stands and watches as Steele leaves the store, turns his ipod back on, and walks singin and dancin all over again, countin his steps, ten by ten. On the way out he sees an MWE camera man right in his face, he shrugs and decides to say a few words, just in case. Steele: I did it ya’ll, I said what I needed to say, go online, check it out, you be the judge this day. I don’t got anything else to say, I’m not good with words, I ain’t lyin I hope you all understand that I can still be DEATH defyin. I can rhyme, I can wrestle, I can dance, I can sing, but I ain’t good with my self esteem. Mwewrestling.com, that’s where its at, go there read my words, love em, feel em, be em. I put my heart and my soul into it, maybe it was a mistake, yea I said it, MAYBE…a MIS….TAKE! But my words and my hand they will shake, I wanna see a victory, I wanna see a win, if I’m wrong I oughta pluck the hairs off my chinny chin chin. Lata ya’ll I gotta fly, my rhyme is done, I’m sayin goodbye. Steele walks away from the camera full of confidence and joy in his stride, he swings his hips out the mall doors, people walk by him, like he doesn’t exist, just dirt to the floors. Walk in his shoes, live in his life, you’ll see what he means, naughty or nice. He plays that Kanye song on his ipod once more, happy, nothing else, he just listens and is lovin it, for sure.
