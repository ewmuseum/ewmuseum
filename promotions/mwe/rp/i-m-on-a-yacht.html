I'M ON A.....YACHT!; ZOMG NO WAY!

Apr 3 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Shaking things up doesn’t even BEGIN to explain the plans Sinc has had ever since his sentimental return to this federation. He’s worked far too hard to give up now, and no matter how many times it’s been said, work pays off. On this satisfactory day Sinc opens the mouth of the lion, and steps inside its jaw to live in danger of a new era, and to overshadow his error of leaving, to gain the respect from his colleagues once again. So they call you, The Angel, A-Rod, and sometimes even The High Flying Menace. When I think of you there are only a few things that come to thought, pathetic, degenerate, b*tchy little man, and best friend to hopeless blue collar jobbers. But you have been around for such a long time in the MWE, and you even managed to beat David Whitman, bravo… As more of the scene is revealed, Sinc seems to be on a yacht of some sort. The paint on the boat glimmers a bright white in the intense rays, and the ocean seems to calm itself around the plentiful boat drifting it slowly on the mesmerizing waves. Sinc is wearing some simple gray swim trunks and a swaying tan buttoned shirt. A martini placed firmly in his left hand. You know your diligence and your incompetence doesn’t ever seem to get old. The ghetto Mexican making it to the big times, people just continue to eat that up don’t they? Well maybe instead of playing tic-tac-toe with a chick all day you can actually give me your best this week. I know what your capable of and I insist Alejandro, that you go that extra mile and annihilate me with your miraculous luchador moves. Embracing the day, he looks beyond the glittering salt from the water’s surface and darts his eyes to the horizon as the sun glows feverishly over the ocean view. Every breath drawn seems to be a determining factor for the day ahead. Scare me away from MWE forever A-Rod, show me you can still soar and fly in that ring all day and all night long! At FNOD I said I didn’t want to have to fall to the level of people like you and Sonny Williams again. I wanted to move up to the main event already, but I learned that it takes extreme patience to make it big, and your ignorance will be my waiting line. Symbolically Sinc breaks the martini glass over the boat’s rail edge and lets the sharp pieces of such glass drift away into the water below. Finding a way out of a business and then snooping for a new way in can be very challenging and distasteful. Working one’s way back up the corporate ladder is the only instantaneous solution to a slowing problem. When I decided to come back I knew that there were so many people I needed to face still, and though we have faced off before in a triple threat, you have never beaten me one on one, ever. Stay close to the tag division while you still can Alejandro, because only the strong survive. With a deliberate placement Sinc etches over onto a complacent beach chair folded out on the deck. He relaxes himself and puts up his legs as he leisure’s on this comfortable contraption. You clenching off of a John Wayne clone as a tag partner isn’t gonna protect you when it comes down to you and I buddy. Partners are nothing in the singles division, that ring turns into a bloody tandem when it comes to one on one’s, I fortuitously hope you understand the concept of an MWE ring, as well as pain. An acidic smirk lurches across his face as his lips cringe into a pucker of thinking sanity. The words just begin to creep out of his mouth in a flow of epidemics. I loathe and abhor your gigantic pride that you tend to pour out of your body from day to day, but I do enjoy your knack for adventure and your thirst for victory. You and your little underpaid friend Darrel remind me of Rocky And Bullwinkle when they couldn’t get a job done. You endlessly prance around the cage of self influence and pop thoughts into your mind that you are going somewhere, when you are only going in circles, circles of limitless time. You try to backtrack, try to find your way home, but that trail of blood is getting dry, and a pack of wolves are waiting for you on the other side, and THAT my friend is when life sucks. The tyrannical encampment of a ring is more than meets the eye, it can transform into your worst enemy or your best friend in the blink of an eye. Cautiously watching and interloping your worlds into a square fixation is just the beginning of its suppressed abilities, it is capable of decades more. You are so much like a bipolar roller coaster to me, up and down, angry and content, sad and jovial, I don’t think you have really captured what it means to be a wrestler yet A-Rod. You’ve lost recently to a team of idiotic hoodlums trying to tippy toe their way back to the top. They are nothing more than mindless drones trying to recalibrate their careers in a failed attempt at success. The Black List Mafia shouldn’t even be brought up as any sort of competition. Now caressing himself in a bottle of sunscreen Sinc dabs the bottle gently over his arms and neck, he protects and shields himself as the day only appears to grow brighter and more erratic. You and your little hubby of a partner keep trying with the only result being a failed victory and a blazing loss, congrats, you can depend on people, good job. But people can only be proud of you for that if they were conjoined twins at birth with a sexual fantasy of being separated to the ones they love and desire, how foul. Sure your the tag champs, but for how long? In a circumstance of question being tough as nails and somewhat edgy is a must, but to formulate every verb and every noun you try to speak, takes some time to come out right and shift into a gear of intellect. Yes you too were also an MWE world champion, but I think I proved to people that such an accomplishment does not matter in that ring with me after I defeated Sonny Williams and Mason Resoan, former world champs. Am I making you flinch yet little man? A syndication of a changing legacy permits all over the face of Alejandro in Sinc‘s brain. Sinc is dragging him through the dirt of a mental prison and showing him the vindictive imagery of a shattered human and a lone Shepherd. Coils of unorthodox images and twisted attitudes screech across every thought and every pause in Sinc’s imagination, he tries to recover his calm facial stature. Every trick, every mind game, and every move you want to try and bestow upon me I will seek out and uncover as nothing but a flashy effect. You mouth off like a school girl on how you won’t stand for people’s crap anymore, how you have suffered and clawed for survival growing up and it was oh so hard to get in this business. I have heard that same story countless times. He takes a hanging moment to recall the many words Alejandro has said in the past, and the heightened babbling he continues to spew week in and week out, this masked perpetrator has stepped in the wrong direction with Sinc. Go on, keep giving me the same sh*t that all my other opponents and so called “friends” gave me. Your not the only one who grew up roughing it out kid, you really gotta get out more. I don’t CARE what you’ve been through, quit whining and b*tching about it and start acting mature for once. Be bigger than you really are. Your biting down on beating David Whitman for far too long, you need to learn to let go and accept….accept the fact that you dwell on your past too much, and cannot handle the future, cannot handle…me. A cool, blissful, breeze splashes in a sensation by Sinc’s head soothing his nerves in a residential numb, and just as quickly, it vanishes to kiss the coast of the sunburst land ahead. Whitman is not your calling card you moron, your the calling card to your own destiny, you make things happen, not people like him. My favorite part of your FNOD promo was when you mentioned Jekyl as if he was an obstacle at all, I’m sorry but that was really quite stupid of you to say. You hurl out these outrageous accusations and you CLAIM that they are relevant and important, in the MWE, the only thing that is important is being at the top of the card with that world title around your waist, beating the top dog doesn’t make you the top dog…yet. The floundering bull sh*t A-Rod coughs up frequently gives Sinc a hefty unsettlement in the pit of his stomach. His flamboyant exuberance and shaky status flakes the ego of Sinc just a little. But when I pin you come MNM, I will have pinned yet ANOTHER former MWE world champ, and THAT…would bring ME, myself, and I….to the top again amigo. You’ll be pouting and scathing for another shot, another match with me, but I’ll already pass you by and be the victor of this game of numbers. I’ll rise to the highest point and flick you off into a falling thump. Down…. Down…. Down…… Down…….. Thud. What’s wrong? Did I strike a blow to your ego? Or is that just me being arrogant? Believe me when I say that I want nothing more than to see another competitor fall to me in that ring and demolish them to clear my path to stardom once again, scotch free. But your a little different than everyone else, you don’t ever simply give up. You’re a mind bending parasite that I can’t get out of my sights and squash. After seeing all that you’ve done for this business I admit that I am a little jealous, but if you fall to me, all my worries and jealousy will be washed away. Because your aseptic keenness will be crushed by my enticing talent. Simultaneously a graceful flock of birds flies passed the cotton puffed clouds above and harks at Sinc relaxing on his expensive and outstanding yacht, or in his case, a mini paradise of his own accord. He doesn’t bother to look at them as they fly by, having no care to enjoy the simple things in life and the miracles and happenings of mother nature, nature is nonexistent to a man like himself. People who can lick their elbows, bend their thumbs all the way backwards, or loop their tongues are superficial and unique. But how many people can say that they have made it to the highest point in one of the biggest companies to ever graze the market, a composition of various niche’s and talents, enough to build up to an entire tower? Not many A-Rod, not many at all. That is why competition can never really end and egos can never die down, this is what makes MWE great. Sorry to burst your bubble poco muchacho, but your not great and your not worth my time and energy. Sinc removes his shirt revealing a fit upper body and lean muscles. He looks at the ocean water in envy and then back at the camera with a vibrant smile. So you can either choose to vote for Pedro, or place your bets on one of the GREATEST superstars to ever grace the MWE. I don’t know if The Angel lost his wings or if he just can’t seem to find them, But I have already learned to fly, and if A-Rod can’t see that, then he will never see why they call me The Arrogant Aviator. I am the pilot of MWE, and my arrogance allows me to fly past the ranks and bust through the stern competition, somebody call the air force, because I think Alejandro Rodriguez just got decommissioned. Sayonara people. With an arched back Sinc dives off the boat and begins to briskly swim in the clear water below, his cares are gone, and his days are far from numbered, he is back again. END.
