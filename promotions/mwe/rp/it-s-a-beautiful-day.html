It's A Beautiful Day; You can't deny what lives inside you...

Jun 5 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
-One moment, that’s all it takes to change the world. How in a world so hard to make a living, so hard to make it big is it impossible to believe that everything can change in one moment? In this world, there are people who take every opportunity possible to reach their dreams or to even aspire to live a decent life. In this world, there are people who have everything they can dream of in their disposition. Within the snap of a finger, they can gain things we fight for our entire lives. One moment can change everything imaginable. A more philosophical thought is how the definition of the impact a moment holds has many possibilities, however the definition of a moment has no definition. A moment can be one single second and yet it can also be a lifetime. Talk about your aspirations, your hopes and your dreams however you’ll live to be the person you hate the most. Everything you wished you’d never become will be what you become when you get older. When you were younger, you dreamt of having fast cars, lots of money and a saviour of the world, but the sad fact is you’ll become a sore old man who’d sell everything he had for 15 minutes of fame for justification of their lives. Same goes vice versa, when you look at Jay Riddick, a man who grew up under the idealistic that he’d never become anything because the world wouldn’t give him the time of day. And not only is he one of MWE’s fastest rising stars, we now we know he has a child aswell. Not much is known about Riddick’s relationship with Alexa Girard, besides the basic rundown that she pity dated him up till the prom and couldn’t go any further living a lie, so she made the mistake of informing Riddick that she didn’t really love him. Riddick in a furious state of mind brought Alexa to a motel room during prom and raped her. After shooting an interview, Riddick walked to his car only to realize his unknown 10 year old son, David came into the picture. But there’s still missing pieces to this puzzle, how did David know where Riddick would be? What he did? How he even knew of his father? Well that’s the gift of time, and the wrapping unravels as time continues on. We live in the age that is self-proclaimed to be the age where everything that could be invented has been invented, where there is nothing new to discover. That has lead to the age of ignorance where we believe we know everything and understand all, which is truly a demonstration of the fall of man. In this lifetime there will always be something to be learnt. Riddick is walking around in his birthplace, Niagara Falls. He isn’t usually one to walk outside for long distances, but this for him was necessary. After reminiscing of that incident, he felt he needed to walk around his hometown to remember what his life used to be like. He walks long strides in the cool afterglow of the sun as it’s roughly 8 o’clock at night. He dares not to walk in the bright lights of Clifton Hill as he wouldn’t want to be noticed.- Riddick: This week, I step into the ring for the third time. Third time’s the charm as they say. I walk down that narrow ramp into a squared circle that will contest my ability of being a threat. See my career has been overshadowed by doubt. The very same doubt that shadowed over my life when I was struggling to survive. The very same doubters that claimed I would never become anything. My destiny would be my fate and that my fate would become my destiny. The first week of my tenure here in MWE, I went into MWE, into the arena, into the ring when nobody knew who I was, nobody was aware of the uprising of Jay Riddick. People sitting in their seats were oblivious to the fact that I would come into the ring and eliminate four competitors in a Battle Royal. I took out every man that stood in my way and I took the first step to making a name for myself. The second and latest week of my MWE tenure, I was put in front of an obstacle I’ve had my entire life. I was put into a tag team match, being in a position where you had to put your faith into your partner that you would both walk out victorious. Never in my life have I had someone I had to work with, that I had to have faith in. But what’s interesting was that I took my inability to have faith for others and brought home victory for myself. I’m not to be found in any danger zones right now, I expected from the start that I would be the only person by my side. When I’m down, it’d be my hope and my power forcing myself back up. When I get myself in a tough predicament, my fate will depend on me and myself only. It’s no shock to me, it’s no surprise. And it’ll be no surprise when I walk out of MNM victorious for three times in a row. First Blood Match, the name leaves much to be desired. I would raise doubt that anyone in MWE has ever seen their own blood for the first time. But that’s where things get interesting. Throughout the history of professional wrestling and all of its first blood matches, they all stopped at blood and went no further. I won’t simply drain the blood from Virus and Scotty McKnight, that’s where everybody else stops but I won’t stop there, not by a long shot. Before I drain the vital fluid that flows through your veins, I will drain you of hope till your perspectives of the future end at the present. I will drain you of power until the very moment you’re limbs, one by one surrender under your own will. I will drain you of a future until you reminisce about the past. Virus, much like your name, you are an infection that runs through the bodies you encounter. You attempt to destroy the body and ruin all hope for those you infect. But much like any real virus, the remedy will be found in a short time and after that time, you’re pinnacle point will soon descend and find yourself into the abyss of which revival is impossible. You also attempt to try and convince me that I am trying to become an apparition of duplication under your vision. Claiming I’m like yourself, claiming that I will never be you. Well a message of a revelation reveals from my own words, I do not want to be you, I do not want to be Virus. Nobody wants to be Virus because it will only lead to the genocide of your false-claiming, obnoxious type of characters. You can deny it’s my time Virus; you can attempt to understand what has happened in my life and deny that my time has arrived, but that will only ruin any hope for your salvation. You claim you will rule the world under your own terms; well we have a problem then. Because if you want supremacy, if supremacy is that addicting taste that you cannot deny yourself of, well then you’ll have two ways of taking it from me, you can pry it from my hands, pry it away from my life, or you can drain it from my veins. The choice is yours. And let’s not forget Scotty McKnight. Scott, you have arrogance that runs in your veins along with your blood, because every time you speak and every time you open your eyes I see some punk that nobody took the time to teach you a lesson. But now I’m going to take my own personal time to give you some quick pointers and lessons for the future, lessons that Jekyl doesn’t have the balls to teach you. 1- Never and I mean never should you ever decide to make the decision of poking fun at my lifestyle. You wouldn’t last a single moment, little man. 2- Never act like you can put Jay Riddick into mind games, I’ve played them all and any attempt to lure me into false sense is futile. 3- Karma is true; in all I put up with I am owed plenty. I am not scared of any man who takes advice to an incestual clown and idolizes a fighter who never understood how to be a man. A fighter that had everything except for respect for his superiors. And much like “The Huntington Beach Bad Boy” you’re career itself will end by a man who foiled his attempts to fight another day, causing a cry of happiness in the world. I walk here in the streets where I grew up, where I played as a child, where I learned how to ride a bike by myself. And here is where I fell of that bike, spilling blood from my unprotected body on the sidewalk. Take a very good look, because this is the closest you’ll get to seeing my blood this week.-How sad do you think it is when you’ve committed acts as vile as this, yet people from the public unknown to this knowledge want to idolize you? Even criminals wouldn’t want others falling into their footsteps. Riddick chooses only to walk around where he grew up. The amber glow of the clouds make visibility easier, as Riddick makes his way to a street several turns of his old house. Cars are awkwardly absent for this time as Riddick walks on the paved street careless if any car were to drive by. Out of nowhere a small red ball rolls in front of Riddick’s feet. The color being bright enough to capture Riddick’s attention as he kicks the ball back in the direction it came from only to hear somebody calling his name.- David: Jay! Jay! It’s me, David! Riddick: Is this where you live? David: Yeah, my mom is taking a nap. Did you want to see her? -A flashback plays of him and Alexa dancing to some slow song at the prom. Riddick wearing a black suit is actually smiling, as his teeth being a rare sight smile into her face as they dance across the floor. Alexa rests her head on his shoulders as they dance, but her smiling face turns to serious as she lifts her head and whispers into Riddick’s ear, saying she had to talk to him privately in their motel room. The scene switches to a simple view of a locked motel room door, nothing new to the eye for Riddick. The only thing heard is Alexa screaming at the top of her lungs in horror as only heavy heaths of breath are heard from Riddick.- Riddick: No, not today. But I have to ask, how did you find me? David: Mom talks about you sometimes, she said she saw you on TV one night wrestling. I never saw you, so I watched. Then I heard you were doing something for the show in New York before flying to Italy, so I waited for you outside. Mom had a business trip to New York, so that’s how I got there. Riddick: What does she do? David: She’s an author. Riddick: -mutters- Of course, she’s a good storyteller. Look, can we talk? David: Sure, we can go to the park. Riddick: Alright kid. -Riddick and David walk to the park, the clouds are still in flames by color only, highlighting the burning red sun light. By sailor’s rules it means the next day will be a sunny, bright gorgeous day. They’re both sitting on the park bench, Riddick won’t even look into David’s eyes, and it’s almost hard for him to talk. Riddick’s cold blue eyes suddenly begin to open up and a new side of Riddick that shines more than the burning red sun.- Riddick: Look, I’m not good at talking about certain things, but I find this is something you need to know. I can’t apologize enough that I wasn’t there to see you grow up. You may not know, but I’ve had a hard time with life. I’ve had trouble dealing with people. I’ve done some things in life I could never forgive myself for. David: Do people hate you? Riddick: Well, life is like learning how to ride a bike. In the beginning you have others to help you and push you, people that believe you can succeed, people that pick you up when you fall, and dust you off and encourage you to continue. I didn’t have that when I was growing up. When I was growing up I didn’t have anybody by my side, anybody to pick me up when I fell. So I got up myself and continued riding, although when I was riding that long tough path I started hating people for not being there for me. So it’s made me really hard on people. People like your mom. David: What did you do to mom? Riddick: I don’t think you’re ready to know, but one day you will. David: Did you hurt mom? -The flashback of the motel door with the screams being heard replays in Riddick’s mind as his eyes grow back into cold blue, eyelids shut tightly and thoughts attempted to be repressed. Riddick then opens his eyes again, he raises his face as it comes into contact with the light of the sun. He struggles to open his mouth as he attempts to continue to speak.- Riddick: Sometimes on that bike ride known as life, you come to uphill climbs and dirt patches that you really don’t want to cross, however there is no turning back in life. You take those obstacles and challenges that are in front of you. In the end, you pass by or you don’t make it to the other side, but you forget those moments by remembering the smooth travel and the beautiful things you saw on your way. There are always those days I wish I could turn around and never go to those places, but when you ride across you can turn around and make all the detours you want but you still went down that path. David: You’re not as mean as you look like on TV. Riddick: I’ve made my way down many paths in my lifetime, some rough and some smooth. But some paths I would never let people take. I may not be like myself on TV at this moment. But I would never let anyone I love down those paths I took. David: You’re not such a bad person as you are on TV afterall, are you sure wrestling is real? Riddick: -Smirks- It is, kid, it is. What you’re hearing now is the sound of change and the sound of revelation. Maybe all it took was somebody to listen to find out that my life will be alright. -Riddick and David simply stay sit on the bench looking at the sky, the deep red orange hybrid sun is scattering it’s light shadow into the clouds above, it was always a fact that red light would make the morning a sailors delight. It’s true, it always has been, sometimes it just takes us a moment, one single moment to simply look at it’s beauty in order to change who we are, and to change who we are could change the world if we make the right choices. That’s all it takes, one moment. Tomorrow is going to be a beautiful day...-
