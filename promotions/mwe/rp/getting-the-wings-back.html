Getting the wings back...

Apr 12 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Norwich, England, 10th April 2009, 1:48pm. Norwich Cathedral. The scene opens in the cobbled streets of Norwich, England. The camera pans around the area showing the city’s main attraction, the Norwich Cathedral. The Cathedral is huge, with the spire nearly reaching a hundred metres tall. It is a fairly chilly day, which seems evident due to the number of passers-by wearing thick coats, but otherwise it is a bright day. There is one man who is not wearing a thick coat, only a black t-shirt, black mask, white trainers and jeans, that man is Alejandro Rodriguez, he turns to face the camera, he smiles, opens his arms and begins to talk. Alejandro: Norwich, England… Over three hundred thousand people walk these streets every day, to visit, to go to work, to go home. Me and this great city share something in common. Back in the eleventh century, this city was the most important place in the United Kingdom, behind London, people came from far and wide to visit it. but now, cities like Manchester and Birmingham overshadow it. Alejandro begins to walk down the cobbled street, toward the cathedral, still talking. Alejandro: I used to be one of the most important superstars in the MWE. I won all the men’s singles titles there were to gain, I held the MWE World Championship and never lost it, that was in the old MWE. In the new MWE, all I have to my name is a US Title victory, which was ripped from underneath me, and an MWE Tag Team Championship, which I have failed to defend so far. Alejandro passes by a wooden bench, occupied by another two people, Alejandro sits down beside them, the two people look up at him and promptly get up and move away, Alejandro does not notice this, he is still focused on the camera and the beautiful cathedral. Alejandro: So you can understand why a man like Sinc Mercier can challenge my confidence, you can see why he would question my integrity. But he has witnessed it first hand, after all, you know, that time when I defeated him. But I shouldn’t hold that over him, the man has improved a lot, and… well, I would like to say he has matured, but I know he hasn’t, not even one bit. Alejandro swerves around in the bench, and he puts his feet up at the end. Alejandro: But now he’s back! Great, everyone cheer. To everyone he’s the next big thing, to me he’s an arrogant young punk who needs to learn a few crucial lessons, first of all respect. He says that he’s heading towards the top, with no-one stopping him. We’ve all been there, look at me, Sonny Williams, Brian Michaels, D.C., David Whitman… We’ve all rose up the ranks, and sometimes we have a few harsh words to say to our opponents, but we all have respect, and that’s because we respect other people too. Trust me, Sinc Mercier will not rise to the top unless he changes his ways. Alejandro swerves off the bench and gets to his feet again. He breathes in the fresh British air and he walks down the road a little more. He gets to the entrance of the Cathedral and pauses. Alejandro: So, Sinc Mercier’s claim to fame is a US Title reign and victories over Sonny Williams and Mason Resoan? That’s funny, because you see, I did all those things years ago, I’ve held the United States Championship twice, and I defeated Sonny Williams for one of those titles! I’ve defeated bigger names than Sinc Mercier… He’s beaten two former World Champions? I defeated our current champion just two weeks ago… Infact, I’ve defeated every World Champion there ever was in MWE. He enters the Cathedral through the entrance. He walks forward down the aisle of wooden benches and the camera follows. The camera sweeps around the huge cathedral. At the end of the cathedral is the beautiful gold cross, embedded in a wall of great wooden pillars, each one with gold stripes up them. The walls of the cathedral are illuminated by the sky outside giving them a beautiful blueish hue. Alejandro walks down the aisle, he gets about halfway and he pauses. Alejandro: So yes, Sinc Mercier, I may live in my past. I may talk about accomplishments and victories, that’s just what I do. Anyone can talk smack to an opponent, it‘s not fuckin‘ difficult, exploiting weaknesses of your opponents, and their common traits, not everyone can say they’ve done the things I have done. I would talk smack to you right now, but look around me, there is a time and a place for those things, Sinc, and this is not one of those places. Alejandro continues down his way and he reaches the great golden cross, he bows down in prayer for a moment on two, and he opens out his arms wide as the camera pans around him. Alejandro: This is a place of faith, this is a place of peace… Sinc Mercier, this is a place of hope. All of my life, my lord has watched over his faithful angel, and he has turned my life upside down, from the hell it once was, to the dream I have longed for since I was a little boy. And while you may say Sinc Mercier, that this man needs to get over his childhood, it truly was a blessing from heaven to get away from it. And while you may not need hope where you came from, in my country, it’s all we had. But I will forgive you for remaining ignorant of that, just like you remain ignorant of more important things. Alejandro steps away from the cross and the walks back toward the rows of benches, Alejandro takes a seat on one of the benches. Alejandro: To be a champion, Sinc, you have to have the heart and desire of a champion. Just ask yourself man, just ask yourself if you have that desire, ask if you have that heart. And maybe, you could be one of the greatest, but not now, because now is my time. I am officially changing my ways, I am turning my faith back into the almighty lord in heaven. Sinc Mercier, the angel may have lost his wings, but he is getting them back. And trust me this Monday, Sinc Mercier, you will LEARN… TO… FLY! The camera pans away from Alejandro Rodriguez and back to the great golden cross as the scene fades to black.
