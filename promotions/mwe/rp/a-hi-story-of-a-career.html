A (Hi)Story Of A Career; A Grand Mystique RP

Oct 17 2009

The Grand Mystique

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Boy blue Alex Wood orange Hayley Wood gold GP Dr Cleaver red I am a big disappointment. The cave-in, jobbing champion who can’t live up to promise and expectations. You have no right to expect anything from me. But I will fight and I will not relinquish. _______________________________________________________ QUOTE Twenty one years ago A husband and wife sit in front of a desk in a doctors’ office. She is quite plain to look at; not unattractive but worry lines are written all over her face which darken and wrinkle her skin. He suffers from this too but his pale skin and heavy eyes show more than just worry. They are clasping each other’s hands tightly. Apart from her the office is clean, tidy and filled with random ornaments. It is your typical GP’s office, complete with that awkward sterile odour. She bits her nails nervously as the clock ticks by. Each tick and tock seems to echo a thousand times over in her head from a grandfather clock behind her. The doctor bustles in behind them. “Hello there.” He turns around and greets the doctor with a handshake. She doesn’t answer, only giving a meek nod and courteous little smile. The doctor wears half moon glasses are perched on the end of his nose and his brow is furrowed. He carries a wedge of papers bound in a folder. He walks around to his desk and positions the folders in front of his seat before taking a seat himself. “How are you both?” “Good days and bad.” “I feel OK today at least.” “Good.” The doctor smiles kindly. “And how is that gorgeous little man of yours?” “He’s growing so quickly and so intelligent and able for his age.” ”It’s great to see.” “That’s good to know. His foot is such an unfortunate event.” “Yes. Yes it is. But he copes with it well” Their four year little boy was the apple of their eye. He adored them and he was the centre of their world. “You said that this was urgent?” “Err, yes. Sorry.” He is suddenly hesitant as if the reminder stirred something inside. He reaches into his folder and pulls out a sheet of paper. “The results of the blood test and the examinations that we did last week have come back to me. The blood markers are significant elevated, which indicate that the cancer has returned. I’m so sorry Alex.” Panic strikes across her face and a lone tear falls down her cheek. My boy, my beautiful boy. It didn’t seem possible but her husband clasped her hand, holding it even tighter. He moves around next to her. He perches on the desk and tries to consolidate her by clenching her hand. “How bad is it?” He is a picture of composure; an admirable feature as a million thoughts run through his mind. “I’m afraid it’s extremely advanced and therefore, we expect it to be very aggressive in nature.” “When can you operate?” Her voice shakes. “I’m so sorry but it’s just too risky to operate. The mass is too large. We will try chemotherapy but at this stage, we just can’t suggest a treatment.” She collapses in the arms of her husband. The weight of her despair seizes her like a crushing pincer. She cries uncontrollably in his shoulder. He had fought so bravely to see off the illness twice before that now, its’ return was the ultimate kicker, just as life dared to seem normal. It was threatening to destroy him slowly and painfully. It was here and now that he decided he would fight to his end. For her and for him. And his boy would help her and learn how to live life to its fullest and fight to get whatever he wants . His spirit must shine on despite this turmoil and stress. His strength will develop and mature within him through her. He will achieve his goals through pure determination and mental might. He will emerge stronger. QUOTE Five years later We see the same woman walking along a promenade. She is holding the hand of a boy who is walking carefully along a raised wall. He is limping slightly and is wearing a plaster shoe around his left foot. “So are you looking forward to Tuesday?” “Yeah I can’t wait to get this off. I hope they don’t have to operate again any time soon.” “Well you’ll be fine even if they do.” “I’ll be more than fine.....I’m gonna be Superman!!!” With that he lets go of her hand, runs off and jumps off the ledge. He lands cleanly on both feet and runs off – well limps - off ahead of her. He is coping so well despite his clubfoot (OOC, wiki it), which had been operated nine times in nine years. In her eyes, he is the spitting image of his father. It pulls her heart just to look at him. His vibrant spirit is identical to his fathers and it was extremely endearing. His father was inspiring in life and heroic in his passing. Even at his weakest, he fought every day just to see them one last time. She knew that he could achieve anything if he had inherited half of his strength. She was proud of what he was like even as a young child. He relished her encouragement to fight every day like father did and she endured more than pangs of loss for not being able to share the pride. QUOTE Three years later She stands over her son as he plays with his dinner with a fork. “You can’t keep lashing out like this. You have got to be the better man and walk away.” “But it’s constant Mum and I’m no coward! Everyone talks about me behind their back and calls me names. I hate it there!” He clenches the fork in a fist and drives down, aiming at a crunchy roast potato. The skin snaps, breaking the tension between parent and teenager. “But punching someone and putting them in hospital –“ “I only punched him gently; it’s not my fault he is a pansy and fell onto the wall.” She wanted to laugh. Deep inside, she could almost reason with the point. It was typical of him to use humour to make the best of a bad situation. It had cheered her up so much in the past. Life was always a struggle and she hated having to play bad cop. “But violence isn’t the answer. You need to be a grown up about it and rise above them. I’ve not raised you like this.” He doesn’t look up and only moves to take a mouthful of gravy soaked potato. “This is the second time that I got called to see the teachers and the fourth time that you got involved in a fight. You heard what they said – next time they will consider expulsion.” “I can’t afford for you to be sent to a school that is even further away than this one. Please do this, for me?” His silence speaks volume to her. He often regretted the violence that occurred in these encounters with the playground bullies but rarely spoke of it. “There was one option that the teacher mentioned to me. Something that I think you could try” “What’s that?” ”Well I’m not sure how good you could be because of your foot.” “You always told me not to let that hold me back. So tell me.” He always was defensive about his foot and after tripping or overactive playing, he would often refuse painkillers and hospital visits. Cruelly she knew he would latch onto most suggestions designed to protect it if she thought out loud it could cause him problems “Well it’s wrestling.” “Fake....wrestling? Like I watch every weekend?” “Not exactly, actually competitive wrestling. They do it at school but you would much rather play football on the same night.” He tilts his head in positive acknowledgement. “Besides, they seem to think it will be a good route to divert this aggression into something practical and positive.” “I guess I’ll try it....wait, does that mean I can’t play footy anymore?” “Well the last three weeks have ended up in punch ups involving you. A break would be good for you right?” “.....OK.” QUOTE We fade to a scratchy old video that shows a school gym, with some blue mats arranged in a large square. A couple of teenage guys, a slightly smaller one with a dirty blonde crew cut, one taller, more muscular with black hair appear from either side and step on to it and start to chain wrestle. It becomes clear after a couple of minutes of exchanging holds that this is no ordinary school wrestling exercise as the moves becomes more spectacular and impactful. They both show some skill in the moves that they are applying and it flows with impressive timing. After five minutes of stiff action, the dark haired teen catches his opponent with a low blow before hoisting him up over his shoulder and planting him with a running powerslam, showing great strength. Somewhat inevitably, when a camel clutch submission is applied, the tap out follows. The footage fades with his arms raised. _______________________________________________________ “So that is where it began.” The voice booms and echoes as we see the Grand Mystique stand in the centre of an MWE ring. The ring is barely visibly however as a solitary, coned stream of light from above that highlights GM’s stature. The camera falls slowly from above and it is now that it’s so easy to see that the dark haired teenager stands in front of us as a man. “What you know is that I am human. I came into this world. Kicking and screaming, just like everyone of you. I have history and I have flaws.” “So what, I hear you ask, makes me think I am so different and unique, so much better than you think? I’m so certain that there must be something, mustn’t there?” GM pokes an index finger into the blood red tie on his chest. He is wearing a dark navy blue suit and it glistens in the lights. This may be the smartest we have ever seen him on camera, hinting at a smarter and more professional Grand Mystique. “I have the spirit of a heroic warrior. I was raised to fight for what I want, to take what I want and do with it as I please. I was taught to use this resolve, to channel it into achieving my dream with unfailing fortitude.” “It’s funny though. I spent my whole life being told I could achieve anything I ever wanted and yet I ended up in wrestling. I got called a joke by my peers. But I didn’t care. I was the best at what I did. I took to training like a duck to water and rode it all the way to big time.” He smiles as he remembers with clear fondness some of the experiences that he had when he was learning the ropes. “Well to WZCW at least. It’s a dump and I was treated like shit but the business bit me. I knew that I had to go further, succeed and achieve. I began to dream what it would be like to lead a company as World Champion; a proper company and a proper championship.” He spits at the name WZCW, “the place that should not be named” with real bitterness. “The journey threw up disappointment and triumph, shock and victory in equal amounts like bumps in the road. But the journey never faltered and the destination remained true.” “Like fate, preordained by a higher power, I would find my way to the MWE. It seemed so right to make a name in the re-imagination of a company that made so many of the people who had made a personal impact on my professional career during my time in EW and WZCW.” For those who have seen GM develop the way he has in MWE, it will be difficult to imagine him as a talent who would have struggled to exit ICW. “In time, the fire was lit and stoked but MWE dragged me from the depths and inspired me to become better. It renewed the iron will that was instilled in me from such a young age and reminded me that I had to fight for what I wanted because no one was going to hand shit on a plate to me. So I destroyed the glass ceiling on my way up the card. I made a habit of constantly exceeding the expectations of others because no-one could even contemplate the possibility that I may actually become better than them.” He knows that in his way, he is saying that MWE saved him, that coming to face the likes of DC and Michaels and that ending his feud Windy Bridges so emphatically in his favour all contributed to the resurrection of his career. “The whole time, the world never knew who I was because of this mask.” He pulls a mask out from his back pocket.”I always enjoyed this aspect of my career and it helped because no one knew how to deal with it.” “This mask was only ever what I wanted to show the world. My deceit and lies, the path of violence and destruction that I left behind was made worse because no one knew who was pulling the strings. The fear spread like wildfire and no one knew how to handle it.” He smiles as memories of his action cross his mind. The actions seemed had more of an effect because they didn’t have a clue who was behind it all. “I’ve spent the vast majority of this year on top of MWE. Having this mask on or off, it never really mattered, because the promises I made, the threats I offered, I delivered on it. And I did it against the best. Whitman, Michaels, DC, hell......it was in MWE I destroyed Windy Bridges in the bloodiest match in the company’s’ history.” He puts the mask back in his pocket. “And here I stand as the MWE Champion, recognised as one of the best here. In doing that I’ve managed to achieve a career dream. A dream that I had for a long time. But CSB, you are new here so I want you to hear something from my own mouth, just in case that bump on the head last week caused you to forget what this is all about.” “Just because you have apparently picked up the title of the Master of the Premises, you mean shit to me.” He looks up, takes a deep breath before carrying on. He knows he has fired the first shot and is expected to back it up. “Just to clarify, my recent efforts have been so poor that I reconsidered my whole career. I lost my edge. I lost all of the impact that I had coming out of Crowning Glory. But I took a long look at myself and at this World Championship belt and felt compelled to take action. So I acted and CSB, you got the sharp end of it.” “But I’m not about to apologise. You see that just wouldn’t be me. I have nothing to apologise for.” GM straightens the knot in his tie. He has taken to showing brutal honesty since he arrived in MWE; this is no different and GM would not have it any other way. “I just don’t feel that neither you nor Kane deserved the opportunity. The Master of the Premises is Eli’s bullshit way of making stars but at the end, two GWC rejects make it? Let me tell you that while I am around you are the Master of nothing. These are my premises and the belt is my key. While I am around, you have nothing to be Master over. You can look ahead beyond Turmoil because our match will only end in your disappointment. So enjoy your ceremony next MNM because there will be no title winning celebration, only the bittersweet knowledge that you had your shot against me and couldn’t quite get it done.” His confidence seeps through. His smile and his broad chest all betray that GM is looking forward to this match. We have not seen him quite like this since before Crowning Glory. “The GWC died on your watch.....while you were champ. That guilt should kill you but all I see is you blowing smoke up our asses. And now I see you in REW, in EPW but I can’t compare any of those companies to the MWE? Now I know GWC did something right to produce Hardaway and Warner but it was a dying company and you were on top when the ship sank. I heard you and Kane bickered about whose fault it was that GWC but some blame has to lie with you, as its champion at the time.” There is no doubt that GM feels right in saying this. It needed to be said since the GWC “invasion” began. The light begins envelope from above, surrounding the ring in its’ brightness. GM shuffles slightly but stays in the centre, in about the same spot.. ”And no matter what you have achieved in GWC, REW and EPW are nothing in this business......I fail to see how your credentials place you anywhere above the mid card in MWE.” “You are pathetic, you are overrated, you are......an even worse version of DC because your opinion of yourself is vastly different to reality. In fact if you want to have a chance at the pay-per-view, I’d suggest that you get your head off of Courtroom TV and into the game.” GM points to his temple again with his index finger. “Ah yes, the Chris Saint Blaise courtroom drama, coming to screens at an MWE show near you.” He chuckles sarcastically. “Because I refuse to let my first title defence be against someone who is not ready to bring their absolute best. I know you are capable because you are here and t. I’ve seen what you can do when you put effort in. You were impressive at Crowning Glory, but you did not win. I thought you did well against Kane but” He chuckles a little again. ”You did not win.” He clearly revels in CSB’s troubles, whether they be in or out of the ring. “You think that you have impressed me? You have not been here long enough to impress. DC impresses me, Kevin Hardaway impresses me. Hell, even Zack Crash impresses me. You have barely registered as a dot on my radar. To me, you are a poor imitation of the MWE’s best.” “It may be the obvious comparison but the bland promo style, the crude, selfish lifestyle and the never-may-care attitude in and out of the ring. It’s been done before and it has been done better by others.” He sneers a lip-curling grin at the camera. He undoes the red tie and discards it behind him. He then unbuttons the top buttons of his cream shirt. “The problem with you is you don’t have the brains or the brawn to challenge me as the MWE Champion and you don’t possess the heart to dethrone me.” “There will be blood but it will not leave my body. And the fans will not see you leave with my title because it’s a part of me that I am not ready to part with. Your first MWE main event will see you suffer in style. You will wear a crimson mask that will traumatise your friends and cause them never to look at you in the same way again. A mask that I will hand to you as a tribute to remember both your suffering and my victory.” In talking about the possibility of violence, GM instantly appears relaxed yet the intensity is not lost. His eyes have not left the camera and he remains, defiant in the centre of the ring. “And I am not stupid, I know you have had Zack Crash in your ear, telling you that I am very beatable. And Warner will try and give you all the encouragement he can from wherever he is on this particular day. But the distractions of other week will not be repeating themselves. I know that my belt is on the line and I will not lose focus.” His brow furrows, his eyes narrow and his nostrils flare up. He is the picture of composure and determination. “Above and beyond that, you don’t deserve to be World Champion of this company. You have achieved nothing here. No titles, no wins over anyone significant and you go from opening the biggest show in history to main eventing in a title match. So something tells me that unless you go all out for the match at Turmoil, having put all your other distractions to one side and as a result, work the match of your life, then you will not beat me.” GM knows that he needs to channel the same energies that have seen him beat Brian Michaels, Windy Bridges and Sinc Mercier. All legends in an MWE setting. He takes a deep breath and the light of the ring begins to come back, forming shadows where there once was light. “You need to know why we are the best in the world. You can witness it firsthand. This is no GWC rehash with second rate wrestlers. This is better, we are better and I am the superior. That sucks for you but it is plain and simple destiny. You can’t do a thing about it.” The light arrives back in it original coned state above GM’s head. It then disappears completely, flooding the surroundings in darkness. _______________________________________________________ We see a final video. GM is in a room that appears to be an office. A laptop with a blue top is sat closed on a desk. He is sitting down in front of the desk on what looks to be a really comfortable leather chair. The camera backs up behind him. We can see an empty brown envelope which has Global Wrestling Coalition CONTRACT stamped in red. He is reading the paperwork with his legs crossed. He has a cartridge pen which he is twirling like a baton in his fingers. He turns over the final page and sees the space for the signature. He rests it down on the desk and gets up. He paces across the room, as he is clearly deliberating the offer. He was still working in WZCW, the UWA and Rev Pro at this point and had already accepted the offer from MWE, which was due to start in November. It takes only a minute for him to make his choice. He rips the contract up and puts it in the wicker bin by his door. He smiles, content with his decisions. MWE was the standout choice. He was certain that it would be the best choice for him personally and professionally. He felt it would benefit both him and them. If he could continue his development among talent he was already familiar with, then he could excel. It was then that the realisation hit him. If he wanted to achieve his career dream and get better, he would have to cut down his commitments. His body could only take so much punishment and the complicated schedule of work and travel was beginning to take its toll. He didn’t need the GWC anywhere near as much as they needed him. It was dying and he couldn’t cope with another suffering fed. WZCW was the pits, the UWA never cared and EW was never meant to be. The MWE was fresh and was seeking to create a new legacy within the shadow of its’ original and popular format. This MWE could be everything that he was looking for. And while he wasn’t entirely excited by the prospect of tagging with Maxx, he knew he could make an impact within their partnership. It would be a very solid base for him to start.
