Memories of an under-dog; Alejandro Rodriguez RP

Sep 13 2008

Alejandro Rodriguez

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
All I ever dreamed for was happiness. All I ever wanted was a good life. All I ever deserved is what I have now. It took a lot of heart. A lot of determination. A lot of lives. But I know I have done myself good. I now know who I am. What I am. I have things I hold dear. A family. A job. A pleasant life. But all that. Is about to be put on the line. A thin, Thin Line. I have everything to loose, with nothing to gain. But this is not about money. It’s not about my family. Stacy or Stephani. This is not about me. This is about my pride. The man is a freak. And he has no business in the MWE. It’s my job to take him out. Out of the MWE. Out of his misery. It’s my duty. Scene opens inside a rural Mexican gym, the dirty and damp walls in total contrast to the gleaming exercise equipment, the place was unusually deserted, it was the number one gym in the entirety of Mexico City, but earlier, a strange man paid the owner a huge amount of money just to have the place to himself for a few hours. The owner was baffled, the place wasn’t even worth that much money to buy, but he agreed, with that money he could set up another gym in Mexico, Puerto Vallarta perhaps? The camera slowly pans in on the man working out, doing fast sit ups on the bench, his face concealed by his elbows which lift his head upwards and downwards, barely stopping for breath, the camera pans in closer, and you see that he is wearing a mask. The camera turns around the man. You now see is face, screwed up with concentration, gleaming with sweat. He is Alejandro Rodriguez. 95.. 96.. 97.. 98.. 99.. 100. Alejandro grabs for a towel and wiped the sweat away from his face, he has a drink of water and let out a huge breath. He wonders what equipment he should use, he looks around and rests his eyes on the exercise bike, he feels a terrible pang of sadness. He closes his eyes. FLASH… His mother, beaming as Alejandro rides his bike for the very first time… …BACK No, must not think about that, have to train. Alejandro looks around again and see’s the treadmill. FLASH… His father, running out of the front door after learning Alejandro’s mother was pregnant. …BACK The punch-bag. FLASH… Kanto punching him in the face and the gut. …BACK. NO! Camera shows Alejandro beating the living hell out of the punch-bag with a series of strikes until he breaks down, leaning on the punch-bag, his eyes watering, a tear falls. FLASH… Scene shows Alejandro crying with a backpack on his back, ready to jump out of his bedroom window, knowing he would never see his mother alive again as the bandits corner her, pulling out weapons. A gun is pointed at her head as he glimpses at her for a final time, tears streaming down his face, he has to do it. A tear falls. A gunshot. He jumps. She dies. …BACK Alejandro is now face first on the floor, falling down in self-pity. He bangs his fist against the floor and sobs. For the first time he wished the gym was full, so he was distracted, not seeing these memories, these nightmares. Haunting him. Taunting him. Killing him. He pulls himself up, he walks over to the ring, he practises taking some bumps. Landing back first into the canvas. Once, twice, a hundred times. His back was sore, he did not care, he had faced far worse. He bumped into the canvas again, and then he stopped, and remembered. FLASH… Kanto slamming Alejandro to the canvas. …BACK Alejandro is breathing heavily, he takes another bump with a thud. He closes his eyes. FLASH… His mother being thrown off a tall building into a dumpster, the bandits who had done the act, standing there, laughing as she thudded against the trash that lay within, the shadow of her last moment of life still showing on her face. …BACK Alejandro is face first on the canvas, he is sobbing into it. He pulls himself to his feet slowly and he climbs out of the ring. He wipes his tears away with his towel. He leans against the wall, shaking uncontrollably for an unknown reason. Sadness? Anger? Remorse? He staggered to the nearest piece of equipment he could see. The weights. One by one, he placed numerous weights on the pole and he lay down underneath it. FLASH… Alejandro is 6 years old, he is carrying home heavy weights of food to ensure his family will be well fed for at least another day. He struggles and struggles with the weight, but he does not give up, he keeps going until he reaches the house. He finally gets to the house and drops off the goods and promptly collapses. …BACK Alejandro grabs the weight and lifts it into the air, his elbow nearly buckles out of the sheer weight of the thing, Alejandro is surprised by how heavy it is. But he wouldn’t give up, just like that little kid in Mexico who had to feed his family, now he had to train to keep feeding his family. Finally with much more effort than Alejandro could bear, the weight was in the sky, his muscles were popping and he was turning blue with the strain, he lowered the weight to go back on its stand, but only one end got there before Alejandro collapsed. BLACKOUT Flicker… Alejandro’s dad getting another pay check from the chairman of a powerful wrestling company, the two shake hands. Flicker… Alejandro’s mother begging for change on the streets of Mexico, one man takes pity and gives her money and she seizes his hand and thanks him. Flicker… Alejandro’s Dad dying while surrounded by friends and family, he smiles and they all talk and they sing hymns together. He closes his eyes, as if falling into a deep sleep. Flicker… Alejandro’s mother, dying alone, helpless and sad, a gun against her head, looking as her son jumps out of the window. …BLACKOUT Alejandro comes into consciousness on the bench, the weight had since rolled off oh him, but the taste in his mouth told him that he was bleeding. He tried to get to up his feet, but there was little feeling in his legs and he fell to one knee. He crawled to the nearest chair he could find, he fell into it and laid there to rest, he closed his eyes and left himself to his thoughts. I have to face a tough opponent, yet I have never been weaker in my life… I am scarred physically and mentally, but I can’t let that get to me… Because there is a job that needs doing, and I have to do it… Kanto is nothing more than a sideshow freak that got lucky, he has no emotion, I have too much… He only wants to cause pain, I want to provide a living for my family, to stop them from going through what I did… Kanto has no heart, he has no soul… Whatever he has, it soon will be taken from him… Because I shall not rest until that man gets exactly what’s coming to him… …what he deserves. Whatever he is, he is no match for me… He cannot even think to dream about what the things I’ve done… The things I’ve accomplished… The things I’ve lost… He fears nothing, yet he knows nothing also… He has no idea how hard I am going to hit him… Harder than anything that he’s ever witnessed in his life… In his sad, sad life… He knows not how I’ve triumphed against legends like David Whitman, like Brian Michaels. He doesn’t know of my blood battles with Sonny Williams, or MWE’s fastest victory over D.C. He knows not that I have captured all of MWE’s singles titles, he has done nothing of the sort. Least of all he knows nothing about my life, how I’ve faced demons just like him, and how I have triumphed above all that and captured the MWE Heavyweight Championship, more than my sell-out father ever did in his lifetime. I have it inside me… I know I can do it… I know I will do it… This Sunday… MWE’s Turmoil… Kanto’s Turmoil… Fade to black.
