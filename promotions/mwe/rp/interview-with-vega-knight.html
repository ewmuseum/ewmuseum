Interview with Vega Knight; Supernova's Debut RP

Sep 19 2008

Supernova

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A camera opens up to see two men sitting down in an empty lounge. One was the MWE interviewer, Shawn Spitzer, and the other guy sitting down, is a rather sharp dressed man. He is the interviewee, 'Supernova' Vega Knight. Spitzer: Hello ladies and Gentlemen and welcome. I'm here at the sports lounge in the Value City arena in Colubus Ohio, and today, I'm interviewing the MWE's newest fighter, 'Supernova' Vega Knight. Vega, how are you today? Shawn extends his hand, but Vega is looking quit surprised and still, obviously looking at something. Spitzer: You know, it's rude to not shake somebody's hand when they extend it. Vega:........I'm sorry. Couldn't help myself. I mean.....KEEP YOUR SPIT IN YOUR FRIGGEN MOUTH....shit man. Sorry. Vega chuckles, finally shaking Shawn's hand. Spitzer: Thank you. Now Vega, What's it like to be here in MWE? Vega: I gotta say, I'm impressed. I was offered a decent deal, as I'm new to the mainstream wrestling circuit, mostly working in the Indies the past year or so. I see that there are a lot of intense talent here, and I'm looking forward to having a lot of fun here. Spitzer: That's good. Any goals? Vega: Well besides eventually winning the MWE title, I'm hoping to bring a flavor to MWE that it seems to lack, and that is lightening up the mood. I mean, there are so many guys here to take things far to serious for my likings, and it's really brining the mood down here, so I feel that we all need to have more fun. Spitzer: Oh really? What makes you think that? Vega: Well I've always had this kinda, twisted sense of humor, mostly which I get from seeing all sorts of crazy pranks, not to mention some of the stuff we've seen in wrestling. Both Spitzer and Vega briefly laugh at that. Vega is then hit with some spit, and he stops laughing for a second, and wipes it off. Vega: But I guess the other part is, I grew up in a place where people were killed a lot, and even some close friends were gunned down. So I appreciate having fun. It's like, the only way to live man. Spitzer: Well you gotta be serious sometimes. Vega: Only when I'm opening up a can of whoop-ass. Vega then pulls out an energy drink that is labeled 'A Can of Whoop-ass', and drinks it. He looks at the label. Vega: Well what do you know? I just opened up a can of whoop-ass. Vega chuckles at that. Vega: No but seriously. When Wrestling, you gotta be serious. Those who aren't serious in the ring will lose focus of who they are, and will fail. So you need to be serious in the ring, and fight every single fight like it was the only thing that mattered ever. Spitzer: That's good to know. Well what abou- Vega:-Whoa whoa whoa. This isn't working man. I'm sorry. Give me a second. Vega gets up, goes into his gym bag, and pulls out a faceguard, and puts it on. Shawn rolls his eyes. Vega: Okay I'm good. Spitzer: As I was saying. What do you think abou- Vega: -I'm sorry man, but this isn't working either. Just give me one more minute. Vega goes into his gym bag again, and grabs out faceguard wipers, and attaches them, and let's the activate. Shawn looks pissed now. Vega then just gives a full blown chuckle. Vega: Man, you should see your face. But I needed that. Anyways, go on ahead. Spitzer: Are you sure this time? Vega: Yes I am. Shawn sighs, before he begins again. Spitzer: Okay. Tell us what you think of your opponent, JJ Executioner, please. Vega sighs, stretching one of his arms out, thinking of an answer. Vega: I'm gonna be honest man. The whole thing about him, it gives me a headache. It seems that there are far to many wrestlers who use the 'homicidal darkness' gimmick. Once you've seen the real deal, you just don't believe in that type of thing. I know one guy who people say has one of the most extreme versions of it, in fact I trained with him. But believe me, it ain't no gimmick, it's real. Spitzer: Oh really? Vega: Don't ask. If he decides he wants to come, he will tell you himself. Not my place to tell you. But back to JJ. I'm not scared of him. I mean, what kind of guy dresses up like a big pile of bullshit? I mean yeah, we've seen some other guys like that, but they at least reinvent themselves to be better. and When I say better I mean, not looking like a big pile of bullshit, nor acting like one. JJ, I don't think he can. I mean, I'm honestly not scared of him. I think that despite his good performance at Turmoil the other day, I simply don't think he's a serious threat. I'll still be serious in the ring with him, but I can't take the guy seriously otherwise. Spitzer: Oh and do tell why you say this? Vega: The guy that trained me, 'Outlaw', he makes wrestling trainers and marine drill instructors look like elementary school teachers. It's partly due to the fact he was trained by Stu Hart. I can honestly say that Outlaw can make even the most fearless, most toughest of trainees and wrestlers beg for their mommies. Even beg like a penny whore, hungry and crying to be paid more then a penny. So JJ Executioner is not somebody who I'm going to lose against. Spitzer: Well at least you have the focus. But everybody is still saying that you're new, you're green, and you will lose. Vega: Yeah, so? They say that to everyone. Difference is, some of us train far earlier then the rest. I mean, I began when I was thirteen years old. I spent eight years training, even in the Indies. Now, I'm here, and I'm ready to make my mark. JJ is just a stepping stone. I will defeat him, or this can right here -Points to the Can he's holding- Is not a can of whoop ass. Spitzer: I thought it was? Vega: That's the point. It is a can of whoop-ass. So it basically means, that JJ won't defeat me. Spitzer: Well I can tell you're ready. So tell me Vega. Where did you get the name 'Supernova'? Vega thinks for a second, then chuckles. Vega: It was in the Indies. A few of the guys said that when the bell starts, I just become a totally different person. It's like, they aren't just seeing a wrestling match, they see like.........an explosion of intensity that lasts, and the guys who I have faced, are never the same afterwards. One of them compared me to Supernova, almost like the one that formed the Crab Nebula some many years ago, and I guess it kind of just.....stuck with me. Spitzer: Interesting story. Well that's it for this interview. I hope you guys watching this video on the Internet now get a good look at somebody who could very well be the future of- Vega: -Hold it. It's not could very well be. It's Most definitely is. Shawn sighs at that comment, before he continues. Spitzer: Okay. The man who MOST DEFINITELY is the future, of MWE, in 'Supernova' Vega Knight. Vega then looks directly at the camera for what he's about to say. Vega: And we will see you the fans, live, right here, at the Value city arena in Columbus Ohio, regardless if it's in person, on the boob tube, or through Internet connection, we will see you, on Monday Night Mayhem. Vega then gives a goofy thumbs up and smile gesture, before chuckling at his antics again. Spitzer: He's Vega Knight, I'm Shawn Spitzer, we'll see you around. Fade
