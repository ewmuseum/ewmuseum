Apparently I'm kind of a big deal; A Grand Mystique RP

Jun 11 2009

The Grand Mystique

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
*** Prologue: “Do you remember who I am now?” An open laptop is sat on a plain wooden desk. It is showing the MWE.com site and it is already open to a specific page. The top of the page reads: “Monday Night Mayhem Moment of the Week”. It suddenly begins to play a silent video. The quality is a little grainy yet clear enough. After a few seconds, the sound comes on. Quote: The music blares over the sound system as the victor savours the moment, bathing in the atmosphere of the heated crowd. But then the lights cut out and some very familiar music, Headstrong by Trapt begins. The crowd boos at the instant recognition of this music. Fireworks go off with a loud explosion at the top of the ramp. Following that the lights come back on, standing in the corner, pointing at DC is the Grand Mystique! The former MWE Champion is on the far side of the ring but freezes at the sight of GM. Both men continue their firm eye contact for a few seconds until GM rushes down and attacks DC, who tries to counter but is struggling against the intensity of GM’s attack having just come through a tough match against Winston Bridges. An official who has come down tries to break up the collision but gets squashed by the weight of both men as they fight in the corner. GM then gets the advantage as he kicks DC in the face which causes him to collapse to the mat. A desperate Lindsay runs into the ring to attend to her partner. GM sees this and is standing over her in an instant. He grabs her hair and picks her up with ease. He looks into her eyes and meets her petrified wide eyed stare with one that shows no fear and a lethal concentration. With horrendous ease and deadly intent, he throws Lindsay over the top rope and she crashes to the floor with a crash. By this time more officials have run down to the ringside area but have not entered the ring. They huddle around Lindsay and encourage paramedics to hurry up to get medical treatment started. They have more than half an eye on GM, who lingers over DC yet, is looking at the carnage he has wreaked on his fiancée. GM turns around to see Winston Montgomery Bridges in front of him. With DC lying crushed in front of him, these two blood rivals exchange a silent stare. Bridges kneels down and cautiously helps DC slowly get to his feet. GM backs away ever so slightly as the pair that used to be known as Sick Addiction stand before him. Suddenly Bridges shoves DC towards GM who instantly hits the Big Bang on DC, who crumples to the mat. Winston then shows savage instincts by applying the Oh Canada Lock on DC in the middle of the ring!!! DC taps instinctively yet is soon unable to do so as he soon is rendered barely conscious due to the torturous pain that has been inflicted. Then the hold is unexpectedly broken as PAIGE has returned! She runs headlong into the ring, crashing into GM who falls through the ropes! She then launches herself towards Bridges who breaks the hold in order to escape the vengeful Paige. GM and Winston head up the ramp, knowing that they have made an impact at DC’s expense and shocked the world by managing to coexist. Paige meanwhile has immediately go to ringside to tend to Lindsay. DC has tried to do the same and refuses medical attention in order to be close to the woman he loves. As GM and Bridges reach the top of the ramp, DC is staring angrily up; an image which is captured on the Lexi-Tron as he cradles Lindsay gently, with Paige behind them both, looking on with a vengeful and emotional gaze. The video pauses on the lasting image of DC doubled up in pain, struggling to comfort Lindsay in his arms. The returning Paige, the former MWE Intercontinental champion is in the background. Her face contorted in the twisted torture of her anguish as she sees the girl that she loves, suffering a pain that she was unable to prevent. The man, a brother that she detests holding the love of her life. A hand reaches out and shuts the machine. *** We now see a red curtain that hangs over a theatre stage. The perfectly formed red ruffles jerk and sway slightly as it is lifted up. Revealed is a familiar masked man. He is wearing the mask and wrestling attire that has become such a fixture in EW, WZCW and most recently with great success, MWE. The outfit shows his toned and muscular figure. He stands in the middle of the bare stage with the spotlight centred on him. His shoulders are pushed back; his hands are tucked into his pockets in a totally relaxed pose. The eyes that pierce through the mask do not burn with the anger that we had seen so often. However that is not to say that the face doesn’t show emotion.....far from it. The focus and concentration coming through grip the camera and the stare holds it in place relentlessly. The eyes are of course, the windows to the soul – a popular saying, even clichéd yet in every instance with GM involved, it proves true. If there were any doubts still existing over the identity of this figure, the pale blue eyes give him away. He has not lost the intensity that made him the most feared man on the entire roster. And the blue mask that surrounds them has been the symbol of his career and the face behind some of the most vicious and brutal assaults ever seen in a wrestling ring. This is most certainly the Grand Mystique alright. He looks exactly as you would expect him to. “The MWE.......has been given its’ warning. I have come back.” And he most certainly sounds like GM. Complete with hybrid Anglo-American accent. No surprises here. “And DC and Paige are just the first in line. The whole roster will soon be very aware of what I am capable of when I am done.” GM chuckles and scratches the back of his neck. “Yes; the entire roster – there have been some major changes. People seem to have let their egos float out of control. And there are some things that need to be said.” GM walks to his right and fetches a stool from behind the edge of the curtain. He places it in the middle of the stage, where he was standing a second ago. “Brian fucking Michaels......I think the man, the freaking MWE Legend who I beat with his own finisher has had his brains scrambled by that kick to the head.” GM sits down. “I said three months ago that he suffers from a dual personality issue. Ace Mountain, for the loss. Promo of the Year.....bull shit.” GM shuffles ever so slightly on the stool. He seems comfortable to be back in front of the camera again. “Ace Cannon, Joesph Sharpman, Jesse Anderson, the men who wanted Vanguard to be the biggest deal in the industry, these men made it a huge joke. They are imbeciles. Cannon is the biggest prick in the world, a man who has no idea of what to do with his power, influence and ability. Like Sharpman and Anderson, their delusion of grandeur has taken them beyond egotistical.” “And yet, where is Vanguard now? The faction that was created to dominate the federation are invisible, nowhere to be seen.” GM allows himself a subtle smirk. “I’m sorry; it’s more than a little ironic ‘tis all. They are not alone though. Blood Fuze don’t get enough credit for leaving after the biggest win that they will ever have.” GM rolls his eyes and shakes his head.“Great timing kids.” For added effect, he gently facepalms his own forehead. GM chuckles again, unable to hold it back. He composes himself which allows him to become much more serious. It is clear that the time for joking has passed. “People have treated the MWE like a joke. My return is going to signal the end of that.” The tone is deadly stern and sombre. “It’s time to let things get really, really serious.” GM stands up, picks up the stool and walks to the end of the stage. The camera swings behind him and shows the seating area in front of GM. What it revealed is a bizarre sight. Cut out heads fill the area. On each row of scarlet upholstered seats, empty eyes look blankly in the general direction of the stage upon which GM stands. The camera moves beyond GM. On closer inspection, it is now clear - each face represents an MWE superstar. Each superstar appears a few times and there seems to be no bias in how these are arranged. The camera moves closer slightly. It now picks up the delicate pieces of string that keep the faces dangling perfectly still down from above alongside the shadows that they cast on the pale magnolia background. The camera then pans around before coming back to look upon GM as he takes in his “audience.” “You see MWE. I’ve told you that my time was coming. And I’ve proved that 2009 was going to be the year of the Grand Mystique.” GM plants on his right fist on his left palm with an emphatic thud. “It started well and got better. I am the reason Antonio Demarco left MWE. I ended the great career of the legend that is Maxamillion Mustafa. And Sonny Williams, Greg Cherry, Aiden Copane all left because they could not face losing to me! GM slaps his palm viciously. It echoes like a gunshot. “I’ve already pinned Windy Bridges and Brian Michaels, two legends of this company in the first three months of 2009. I gave David Whitman the first and only serious challenge of this apparently awesome championship reign.” GM calmly closes his eyes for a couple of seconds. He hesitates, the rant is only gathering momentum. “And all I have got to show for my record in MWE, for not being pinned is a worthless tag title reign with Maxx? A belt that I carried and then single handed placed at the doors of the Project Revolution losers just because I could. But that will not be repeated. From now on, I place trust in myself only. The Hand of Prophecy steers my career, gets benefits and reaps rewards for no one but the Grand Mystique!” GM’s frustration is clear. His tone is stern and determined, matching the vicelike grip that his stare possesses over the camera. “Now.....now, I deal in what is good for me. I am the man who will be the face of the main event in MWE for months, years to come. Ahead of DC” *GM points at a prominent picture of DC in this row of faces* “Ahead of Sharpman” *He directs his pointing digit to an image of Vanguard’s co-founder, who wears his typical smart ass grimace.* “All of these superstars, these heroes who seek your roaring approval are nothing but symbols of horrifying boredom and cataclysmic failure.” GM whirls his finger around menacingly in the air. “I am the antithesis to the MWE and the total solution for everything that is wrong with MWE.” GM moves to the right of the stage and slashes his finger violently through the air. A great swathe of paper faces fall to the floor. With one swift and silent motion, GM has removed the images of a giant chunk of the roster. With his “audience” decimated, GM moves back to his spot in the middle of the stage. He pauses, to take in his thoughts as the empty eyes that remain continue to judge the recently absent MWE superstar. In the audience, more faces fall. One by one, they gently rustle on the floor slowly until none remain, cut and fallen by his mere presence. GM kneels down to survey the mess. He shakes his head, managing to express utter disdain for the collapse of his spectators. He sees familiar faces, discarded on the floor. Mercier, Hardaway, Resoan, Kraven , Kaymakcian. All cast aside by GM. He shakes his head, a vengeful disregard for his competition, his colleagues. Yet for all their intentions, GM knows that he is different, better. What he stands for is on a totally unique plane to those who are restrained within the constraints of being bisexual or perfect or undefeated. GM stands again. His eyes sparkle in the light with experience and intellect. Behind them, his mind runs amok with thoughts about what has occurred and what is about to occur. Three faces appear. DC, Paige and his eternal foe, the wrestler who until recently was known as Windy Bridges. He stares deep into the camera yet his face, at least what is visible to show, barely flickers. More faces fall from the ceiling, surrounding GM and obscuring his face from view. Yet the faces show the same three images that float in front of the camera. They hang there and drift from side to side. The familiar smirk of D.C., the intimidating gaze of Paige and the newly discovered arrogance of Winston Bridges shines through, even in paper form. Suddenly the faces fall. Pouring down like rain, landing with dull thuds as paper and string lands on the varnished floor. One after another, after another they fall like raindrops, hitting and scattering on the floor. The camera follows this to the floor as the strings that held up these papered MWE legends. The holed eyes stare up at the ceiling. The camera moves across and soon reveals the toes of a pair of black laced shoes. The camera pulls away sharply. In an instant, the legs that fit those shoes and a muscular, tanned torso are revealed. Still the focus of the perfect stream of light from above, the ripped frame glistens with sweat. The arms, well built and taut, lie relaxed with thumbs tucked into the waistband of the dark blue trousers that fit this man perfectly. “The MWE need to have dose of their future. A prophecy of their reality is what I have returned to deliver. But the face of the future is exactly the opposite of what you all expect to see.” The hands reach out and grab the camera. They force it upwards to reveal the face behind the voice. “I refuse to hide any longer. I have no reason to protect my identity, to hide behind a facade. There is no shame behind what I have done in the past.” GM lets go of the camera and it stays in place in the new position. He moves the stool back into place and sits down, facing the camera. He pauses for a second and rubs his goatee pensively. “Until now, that mask was my identity, the alter ego of the most destructive energy in MWE. Yet I have never been ashamed of my actions and I refuse to accept anonymity any longer.” GM smiles, beaming with a seething pride. “People will sneer at me, they may prefer not to know who holds the smoking gun.” The sheer pleasure of this experience comes bounding through in all of the small lines and details of his facial features. All of which are so new and fresh. “I am here to take credit for my actions. Maxx? Windy? Yeah I did it all and I am fucking proud of it.” “And that was the past. At a time where I was rooting around at the bottom of the barrel of my potential and questioning myself, I took two of the best and beat them to a bloody pulp. It reinvigorated me and gave me reason to go beyond my past.” GM’s proud smile turns to a cocky grimace. “A flawed idea? Irrational maybe but I am vindicated.” This is familiar. Mask or no mask, this is a real giveaway that this is most certainly GM. “And whether I like it or not, DC is most certainly a feature of my past. He was there in WZCW, complaining and bitching when shit didn’t go his way. In EW, he was there, representing MWE, winning the World title as I lost the US title. And then you beat me” GM has a slight look of sarcastic regret as he looks up at the rafters beyond the camera for a second. “Yes I remember it. I made fun of you and Rated X; you called me a paper champion.” GM’s bitterness is clear as he spits out that last comment with disgust. ”And that comment, just another throwaway line from the master of that gimmick, stuck with me and continues to resonate.” GM points to his temple and taps gently. ”Because you were right you son of a bitch.” He chuckles. For a man who hates being wrong, he seems to appreciate the comment. “I mean, what have I done with my career? My championship wins don’t exactly read like a Hall of Famers CV. And then there’s you, who has beaten everyone on the roster, won all the gold and then does it all over again the next day. But I have a question for you motormouth, I have retired Maxx, destroyed Windy Bridges in the bloodiest match in MWE history and pinned Brian Michaels at the first attempt. How did the biggest star in MWE history turn into the companys’ biggest star making jobber?” He laughs again, unable to restrain himself. “For fucks sake, your win loss record is shocking and the simple fact is that you don’t deserve to be main event anymore. Just like Brian Michaels and Devlin Sharpe, you are living off the past. What have you done in nu-MWE except lose to idiots who are now living on the dole queue?” GM climbs off the stool and drops to his knees. The camera follows and pulls back slightly. “Loss after loss, Beowulf, Project Revolution, Eric Derf – a list of human crap, no-namer after no-namer has pinned your shoulders to the mat.” From his position on the floor, he raises his arms and slaps the floor three times. The booms echo resoundingly around the cavernous walls of the hauntingly empty theatre. “They have exposed you and made you look like a bigger bitch than I already knew you were.” GM looks up. He is clearly enjoying this. “A word of advice D, go back to the X TV, before it’s too late. That’s about your level now.” GM’s face changes, he knows the time for humour is over. “So I could say that what I did on Monday was because of your win against me in EW, but that isn’t the whole truth. And while you could suggest that this is jealousy or egos coming into play that would also be wide of the mark.” GM sits up to a kneeling position. ”But I can tell you here and now, exactly I was thinking.” He nips back up to his feet and turns around, away from the camera. Almost as quickly he turns right back around to face the camera. His hands are on his head, pushing back his slightly damp hair. A vicious sneer indicates his mind is full of vicious intentions, the whites of his eyes clear and wide. “I wanted to do what no one had ever done in this company or any other. I wanted to say I was the first to pin Brian Michaels AND D fucking C in the middle of the ring in the MWE.” GM’s right arm comes down. His index finger is aimed into the camera, a long and lingering point, full of intent. “Michaels is out of the picture and do you really” think that you are capable of doing what he failed at?“ He scoffs callously; knowing that he is poking at the embers of what was competitive relationship between the former stablemates. “But when I do it, pin you in the ring and leave you flat on your back, I will show you and everyone else why I will be the first person to win the MWE title in my first stint with the company in its newest form. Because Whitman knows that he has not been tested like I tested him and Sinc Mercier cannot....” GM pauses as he reconsiders what he just said. “He WILL NOT be allowed to treat that title like his own personal property and hold it forever without defending it like Whitman did.” GM hesitates again, knowing that the tangent he just started to explore will be saved for another time. The pointing arm is reduced to scratching a bit of his hairline, where an inch long scar is visible. “And while you may still be DC, the man, the legend in body, your spirit left you long ago. You are a flaccid shell of what you used to be. The second coming of the old DC that you made such a fuss over last week......is a hyped up figment of imagination. The old DC died with you on that soaking pavement in January. You think your leg is going to stay in one piece once I am done with it? Let me tell you something, scars remind us that the past is real. That accident, that left you with your leg hanging on by ligaments, ruined you as a force in this company and all that you have left is the memories of being the number one guy.” GM rubs the back of his head as he paces across the stage in front of him, kicking aside the paper faces which had fallen a minute earlier. “You used to a pretty fucking big deal around here and now look at you, more PG than Rated X.” Another smirk. “I intend on reducing you to a bloody wreck. When I am finished, you will be another casualty, just like Maxx. And most importantly, I will show you up to be one of the most worthless and over rated clowns ever to work in a wrestling ring.” GM is now as far away as he can be from the camera without leaving the stage. His figure, which is captured in full by the camera, is shown to its fullest, even though he stands on the edge of the shadowy recesses that border the stage area. “Overrated that is, with the exception of your *GM coughs deliberately and sarcastically* beautiful partner, the she-male!” GM kneels down and picks up one of the Paige faces that had fallen. “Yes, this, apparently, is Paige. Not dead, or even retired it appears.” GM throws the face over his shoulder and walks back towards the camera. “So I don’t know if you are 100% and ready to face myself and Windy.” GM throws his arms up in the air in exaggerated exasperation. “Are you even in the right state of mind to work with DC, you hate him remember? Can’t stand him, he gives Lindsay everything that you fail at. How is she by the way? One thing is for sure, DC is no Ace Cannon.” GM chuckles again as for a split second, he reminisces about some of his rants on Ace Cannon. Maybe he was right and had been all along? GM drags himself back on topic. “Well with you in the ring, I can guarantee I won’t allow anyone to upstage you. Not your whore Lindsay, your bitch Cannon or your hanger-on spare tyre, Eli fricking Silver.” GM stands with his arms out wide, in a welcoming gesture. ”You can be centre stage, for your career to be ended in grandest fashion on the biggest stage yet." GM tips his head to the side ever so slightly. Again the hint of humour that had overtaken his serious disposition disappears to a cruel stare, complete with a deriding upturned lip. “Knowing you, I’m sure that you will be 100% by the time your Hall of Fame induction comes around.” GM rubs his hands together enthusiastically. “And I don’t care who you have faced before – your resume reads like a who’s who of the never were! Wellings, Virus, Williams, Warner and only two mid card titles to show for it?” GM claps his hands together, slowly and sarcastically. The whacking noise reverberates around the empty walls. “You are the definition of career under achievement and yet, you get lauded for it again and again?” GM brings his hands together again. He orchestrates a quiet, unwavering silence with one gesture. “But I guarantee that you will never endure pain and anguish like you will witness and experience at my hands. I saw on Monday that you had a revelation. You noticed what more and more people will discover in the near future!” GM’s eyes are wide with anticipation again. “I mean you were in EW, you saw me destroy name after name around me. You saw me become the number one contender, to a title that I will never have a shot at! But guess what bitch, I am better than you ever knew then and never thought possible now.” GM drives his fist into his palm. “The MWE is now home to a significantly superior creature, improved on every level. Everything you have done is fed off the leftovers of other people. I am the one who crushed Maxx, I left him truly defenceless, yet you pounced like the bottom feeding dog that you truly are. That is not a challenge.” “I have never faced you before and once Mayhem is over with, I will make sure that you never want to ever again.” He turns away and rubs his hair fiercely so a little hangs over his face. Beneath the slightly damp and fraying strands, the camera picks out the intense gaze of his gleaming eyes. “You two will be in the ring with the future of the industry. You represent the past and that leaves one destiny for you. You will be forgotten, overtaken and surpassed and I promise that your legacies will not be crushed in vain.” Using his fingers he drags most of the hair out his face, yet his face doesn’t change and the intensity burns through the camera. “Now I can see you both, in front of the little cameramen that will follow you into your room. They will watch, gawp at you as you cut your funny little promos. You will attack us with your “Fuck the World” dialogue and it will be so fucking boring. You two need to realise that you are so old school that you don’t even know that the new generation have raised their foot and are now prepared to use it to stamp out everything that you represent.” Out of shot, GM uses the toe of his boot to knock on the wooden panelling. Again, a monotonous thumping sound booms from the stage underfoot. “I don’t promise to be perfect or to be a saviour. But I will fucking guarantee to change everything that you thought to be right and true about the MWE. The Grand Mystique returns to Monday Night Mayhem, be ready because I am partnered by revolution and destiny. Anyone incapable of adjusting to my future will be left in the past, paralysed by the inability to adjust.” GM turns around, picks up the stool that he used earlier and goes to leave the stage. As he reaches the darkest shadow of the curtain at the side, he stops. Pausing to look down, he lifts his leg up and turns his ankle. He reaches down and pulls something off his foot. An audible huff, followed by a murmured “Shit” is heard as he shuffles around. He remerges from the gloom. He holds up to the camera what he has in his hand. The picture of the man, born Winston Montgomery Bridges. “This was always going to be difficult.” He wanders back, seat in hand and sets it down centre stage. He rests his hands down on his knees in front of him and stares relentlessly at the image that he holds. “Let me make one thing crystal clear, Windy Bridges is a whiny and inferior piece of shit. I beat him half to death more times than I cared to remember and I made his return to MWE so painful that he will never, ever forget it.” With one hand he holds the picture. With the other he uses his fingernail to rub the coarse black stubble that covers the point of his chin. “But.” He pauses, clearly intending to choose these words very carefully. “But, Winston Montgomery Bridges is an altogether different proposition and one that I might just be able to get along with.” GM’s cruel grin reappears at the mere idea of working with his blood rival, the man who he stole shows with in three different companies. GM spilt more of Bridges’ blood than anyone. Ever. “You see, I can certainly appreciate Winston’s current position and views about those around him. I believe that he has accepted his destiny, accepted that being a goody two shoe bitch can and will only get you so far in this industry. He recognises that his current path will lead to bigger and better things and that is why you are witnessing the progression of Winston Montgomery Bridges in the MWE.” GM nods knowingly to himself. “And that personal and professional development is down to none other than myself. So I can guarantee to DC and Paige than you forget everything you saw about Bridges and especially about myself. Our pasts, our futures. Destiny has changed it all.” He chuckles again, that deep evil laugh. He knows Bridges has given GM credit in recent weeks. Credit that up until recently would have been implausible for the man to utter. That wicked chuckle is GM’s appreciation at how everything comes full circle in the most peculiar way. *Fade*
