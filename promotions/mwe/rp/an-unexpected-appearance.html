An unexpected appearance; A Chris England RP

Mar 1 2009

Chris England

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
****OOC=I origonally wasnt going to post up an rp due to personal problems, but ive felt alot better today and thought ide attempt to get an rp up hope you all enjoy it.**** It is 6pm on Sunday the 1st of March 2009, the True English patriot has just been deported back to Northern Ireland following his failed attempt to enter the Republic of Ireland. He is seen standing on a roadside round the back of Windsor Park football stadium; England is wearing a black hooded top, a pair of old brown boots and some blue jeans. He is seen deeply engaged in a conversation on his mobile phone and is pacing backwards and forwards, clearly not in a very happy mood, talking in an aggressive tone to someone on the other end. **England** Look I don’t care that Sunday is your day off! Just get your bloody arse here NOW! England continues pacing up and down the pavement waiting for a reply. **Man on Phone** Err, err, but err, Mr England sir it’s the only day I get to spend time with my family. England’s face starts to turn a shade of deep red as he is visibly getting even more angry. **England** Look you little shit; do you have any idea who I am? Do you know what I’m capable of doing to you if you don’t get your bloody arse here now? The man can be heard making nervous noises on the other end of the phone. **Man on phone** Ok sir, I get the picture I’m on my way, I will be there as soon as I can; but erm where exactly is there sir? **England** Meet me round the back of Windsor Park football stadium and make it snappy! England is just about to hang up when he remembers to add something. **England** Oh and don’t forget the item I requested. **About an hour later** Chris England is sat on the curb, waiting for the arrival of the man he was speaking to on the telephone, he looks like he is a bit fed up with waiting, when suddenly a lorry pulls up in front of him coming to an abrupt halt. A small and skinny man who stands at around about five foot seven, steps out of the lorry and walks over to England. The man begins to speak in a thick Irish accent. **Man** Are you Chris England? **England** It’s Mr England to you! **Man** I’m Peter, I’m here to collect your cargo and as you requested the freight box is in the back of the lorry. Peter looks around and see’s nothing in front of him but the true English Patriot, he raises his hand to his face and scratches his chin in bemusement. **Peter** Erm Mr England sir may I ask where is your cargo? England lets out a little smirk as he walks towards the back of the lorry, before turning round and replying. **England** I am your cargo! Peter looks at England a little bit confused and then starts to walk towards England, who is now stood at the back of the lorry. **Peter** Erm I don’t think I fully understand sir. **England** You don’t need to understand, I hired you to get me to limerick and that is exactly what you’re going to do, now get the back of this lorry open and let’s get on our way. Still a little bit confused, Peter opens the back of the lorry, England jumps up into the back and approaches the Freight box. He throws his bag inside the freight box and then proceeds to jump inside it himself; at this Peter raises an eyebrow. **England** Now punch some air holes in the side of this thing and seal me in. Peter does as The True English Patriot has requested, sealing England into the box then creating some air holes in the side, using tools from the tool box he keeps in the back of his lorry. Still not fully understanding why he is doing this and just going with the flow, Peter closes up the back of the Lorry and walks round the side jumping up into the cabin and starting the engine. The Lorry drives off into the distance and out of the shot of the cameras, heading off on the long journey to limerick. **9 hours later: 3am Monday 2nd of March 2009** The lorry that England hired is seen pulling up outside Thomond Park, Limerick, Ireland. Peter is seen getting out of the lorry and proceeding to walk round the back; opening the doors, he then climbs inside the lorry, he then grabs a crowbar out of his tool box and cracks open the freight box carrying England inside. **Peter** We are here sir. England slowly gets to his feet and climbs out of the box, he stretches his legs, trying to get rid of the stiffness from being cramped up in a tight space for 9 hours. **England** What time is it? Peter looks down at his watch. **Peter** It’s 3am sir. **England** Well that’s the first part of your job over with at least. Peter scratches his head with a confused look on his face, before speaking in a surprised tone. **Peter** The first part? I thought just had to get you here sir? **England** Yes Peter the first part, indeed I did hire you to get me here, but it is only 3am in the morning, I now need you to hide me in the back of here until the show starts tonight. Peter Scratches his head still a bit confused. **Peter** But you’re only paying me to get you here sir? England shakes his head from side and lets out a tut-tut sound, he reaches his hand into his pocket pulling out his wallet. Taking a wad of pound notes out of his pocket, he begins to count them out into his other hand, he then hands the wad of notes to Peter. He continues by putting a very small wad in comparison back into his wallet, placing his wallet back into his pocket. **England** Is that enough for your troubles you money grabbing little pirck! Peter count’s the notes in his hand with a look of shock on his face at how much he has been handed. **Peter** More than enough sir! ** England** Glad to hear it, now lock me in the back of here and come to get me out about 7pm tonight got it? **Peter ** I hear you loud and clear sir, see you tonight. On that note Peter climbs down out of the back of the lorry, closing the doors behind him, leaving the True English Patriot in total darkness. **16 hours later 7.30pm Monday 2nd of March 2009** The Monday Night Mayhem show is just about to start, live from Limerick Ireland, the Camera’s show MWE interviewer Shawn Spitzer walking through the backstage area, with a camera crew in tow, looking around to see if there’s anyone he can interview. Spitzer see’s someone walking down the hallway towards him, Spitzer double takes, then he triple takes, he can’t believe his eyes. It’s as if he has seen a ghost, the man walking towards him is the true English Patriot Chris England; a man that shouldn’t be here, a man that is supposed to be banned from entering the country they both stand in. Spitzer motions to his camera crew to start recording as he approaches England and begins to speak. **Spitzer** Ladies and gentlemen, I really can’t believe I am saying this, but at this moment in time I am joined by none other than The True English Patriot Chris England! Spitzer lets out a smirk of disbelief at England before continuing. **Spitzer** Mr. England, it really is one hell of a shock to see you here tonight, you’re not even supposed to be in the country, what the hell are you doing here? England raises an eyebrow at Spitzer, looking a bit confused at the question. **England** What am I doing here? Well Shawn isn’t that just a tad bit obvious, I’m here to do what I do best, I’m here to FIGHT! **Shawn Spitzer** Wow when you were denied entry to the country we all thought that was that and that we wouldn’t see you here tonight, how did you manage to get them to let you into the country? England smirk’s before letting out a loud chuckle of laughter. **England** Who said they let me into the country? England Laughs again with a sinister smirk on his face. **England** Let me answer your question for you Shawn, where there’s a will there’s a way, did you really think that I would let those terrorist leprechauns, that are the Irish government! Stop me? The True English patriot, from doing what I love the most; because if you did then you were seriously WRONG! **Spitzer** You can’t say that about the Irish government Chris! You will get not only yourself, but the entire MWE banned from Ireland! **England** Who the hell do you think you are to tell me what I can and can’t say! I say what I want, when I want to say it and there isn’t a damn bloody thing that you, or anyone else can do to stop ME! England takes a deep breath, trying to calm himself down before he does something he will regret, meanwhile Spitzer looks a bit annoyed at the tone England has taken with him; but Spitzer seemingly takes it on the chin and ignores it. ** England** And it’s Mr England to you Shawn! **Spitzer** Ok then Mr. England seen as your here, why don’t you tell us all your thoughts on your match tonight? **England** Who are my opponents again? **Spitzer** Alex Bowen and Semi, and your tag partner is Eric Derf. ** England** What? I have to face them two losers yet again? Come on I’ve already defeated Bowen once and if it wasn’t for Austin Quartz, I would have been the one to end Semi’s undefeated streak back in ICW! England suddenly gives a confident smirk down the camera lens. You know what Shawn? that is actually the best news I’ve heard all day; I spent the last twenty four plus hours hiding in the back of a lorry just to get here and I was worried after being cramped up for so long I might not be at my best. But now I am not worried one little bit, see I don’t need to be 100% to beat them two losers, they ain’t got nothing on the True English patriot; I mean come on! Last time I stepped into a MWE ring, I SINGLE handedly took out the tag team champions, so facing these two guys tonight is putting it in simple terms, going to be a walk in the park. See I know both of my competitors all too well, I have faced off against both of them in my time in ICW. First off there’s Alex Bowen, the biggest one hit wonder in the history of MWE; so at Friday Night Overdrive he just happened to beat the man that I am teamed with tonight for the X-TV Havoc title. Big f*ckin deal, that, my friend was nothing more than a fluke, a flash in the pan. I mean, everyone at some point in their career gets a lucky break from time to time and that my friend was your lucky break. Tonight Bowen, me and my partner Eric Derf, will show you and the whole entire world ,that, what happened at Friday night overdrive was nothing more than a fluke. We will show the entire world that you are indeed a one hit wonder, when I personally leave you unconscious in the middle of the squared circle out there tonight. Then we have Jesse ‘The Semi’ Anderson, a man who claims he run down ICW and now claims that he is going to do the same thing in MWE. Well tonight, this man is going to find out, that, the harder you hype yourself up, the HARDER you fall. For months, Semi has believed in his own hype, believed that he actually has the ability to run down the MWE. Well my friend, tonight the only thing you have the ability to do, is LOSE to the True English Patriot and believe me THAT is EXACTLY what is going to happen! England sends an evil glare shooting down the camera lens, with a distinct look of sinister intention and disgust on his face. You see Semi, guys like you really disgust me; you walk around here with this nonchalant air surrounding you, believing that you can run down and defeat any opponent in your path. Well tonight my friend, you’re going to run straight into the gigantic road block, that is the True English Patriot. I’m quicker than you, I’m more agile than you, I’m a hell of a lot stronger than you and most of all I’m smarter than you, you see tonight I’m going to out-think you inside the squared circle, I’m always going to be one step ahead of you, I’m always going to be that little bit quicker to act and tonight, you and everyone in the arena is going to see a even MORE brutal side to The True English Patriot than usual! England once more sends a glare of evil intent down the camera, he is visibly shaking with anger now, as he just seems to be getting more and more fuelled up. This last twenty-four hours I have gone through hell to get here and tonight, I am going to make it HELL for Bowen and Semi, when they step in the ring with me. On top of the fact that I am in one of the most angry and destructive moods I have ever been in, in my entire life , I find myself partnered with a man that is a former X-TV Havoc Champion, a man who I am sure, is more than capable of backing me up out there tonight. England smirks a confident smirk and has a look on his face that makes you believe he feels indestructible, Spitzer who has let England rant on about his match till now, steps back into the shot. **Spitzer** Sure sounds like you’re pretty confident that you and Derf are going to walk out the victors tonight huh? **Chris England** You’re damn right I’m confident, I mean come on Shawn look at me, I’m a true image of perfection and you can’t beat perfection. England flexes his muscles to show off his self proclaimed perfect physique to the camera, with a confident smirk on his face. But not only do I have the perfect body, I also love to fight, cause Chris England is my name and fighting truly is my GAME! Later on tonight, I will prove once and for all, to each and every single person in this world, that the best things in life TRULY are ENGLISH!! England flexes his muscles once more, with an even more confident grin on his face. Now if you don’t mind Shawn, I have a match to prepare for! England walks off down the hallway towards the dressing rooms, with a confident air around him, leaving Spitzer stood there all by himself. **Spitzer** Well there you have it folks, the True English Patriot is here in the building, and by the sounds of things, he sure does mean business tonight. The camera fades out to black as the scene comes to a close.
