Two Birds, One Stone

Nov 7 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
*Mason Resoan and Sara Whitaker are laying down together on a a custom bed, bigger than a king, with cotton sheets and extremely colorful pillows. Mason stretches and looks around and winds up finding himself looking at Sara again. He kisses her on her forehead and stands up. Mason is wearing nothing but his silk boxer shorts at this point and reaches for some socks. He looks at the clock and realizes how early it is and groans. Sara is still sleeping, but Mason ignores this and proceeds to get dressed. First draw he finds he opens and picks out a white T-shirt and and a black button up. He then covers this with a black Pea Coat that had been specially designed for him. Long and sleek dress pants follow this, along with his trademark gold watch and his sunglasses are next. He stretches some more, lets out his final morning yawn, and opens the door to the upstairs hallway.* *He walks down the staircase, rounds the corner, and bolts for the kitchen as he realizes that it took him almost thirty minutes to get dressed and groomed up. It is now 7:15 and Mason is in a rush. He gets a drink and gulps it down as fast as humanly possible. This gives him a well-needed kick and he starts to run around his house fixing things up. Not a single time, for over an hour, did Mason realize that there were cameras in the house. Not for an hour more did he realize it until Shawn Spitzer knocked on the door at 8:30. Shawn walked in and greeted Mason in a friendly manner. They talked for a little bit and at 8:50, Shawn left, as well as the cameras.* **********LATER THAT DAY*********** *Cameras roll up to Mason Resoan's mansion as he is leaving. The MWE truck follows Mason and Sara all the way to a local Java shop where they finally track them down. Mason is still dressed in the attire he selected before and Sara was in a sparkling dress of unique turquoise color. She looked as beautiful as ever, but was angry when she pointed the cameras out to Mason upon entering the shop. It was now 10:00 and the shop was deserted, besides the cashier who was playing solitaire in the back room. Cameras followed Mason inside and Mason and Sara took a seat. The table, as well as the other furniture in the building, is made out of all oak. It is one of the nicer Java Huts in the area and looks fancier than the others, as well. Pictures line the wall and there are computers of all sorts laying around. On the other side of the building there is a coffee machine and a cash register.* Mason: It amazes me...it really does. MWE puts so many man hours into taping me and following me around. Don't they have other things they could spend their time and money doing. MWE, I know you will be watching this, so I will say it now. You need to find someone else to follow around all the time. It gets annoying. Sara: Yeah, well they are here now, and I doubt they will leave any time soon. So, I suppose we should call him up. Mason: Yeah, you're right. I'll be right back. *Mason walked away from the table outside and pulled out his cell phone. He dialed in a number and waited, finally the person he called picked up. They exchanged words for a bit, before Mason hung up the phone and walked back inside. He smiled and sat down next to his woman again.* Mason: He says he should be here in less than ten minutes. Apparently, he is right around the corner. Finally, someone who we can rely on. Someone who isn't a jacka**. *For about five minutes Sara and Mason talk a little, until a man in a suit, which was completely white, walked in He walked straight over to the table with an extremely confident strode and sat down beside Mason. He shook his hand and Sara's hand, in which he added a polite kiss. By the looks of it, this man was quite the gentleman.* Mason: Finally, we meet. How are you Bruno? Bruno: Fine and yourself? Mason: I could be better. Well, I assume you know why you are here? Bruno: Well, I could only guess for what you hired me for. However, I must ask, who is your new girlfriend? Mason: Oh, this is Sara Whitaker. She'll be with me from now on. Sara, this is Michael Bruno. Sara: I am so happy MWE was willing to let us purchase your services. You are much better than that slob Shawn Spitzer or that creepy pedophile Darell. Bruno: Oh believe me, I am top of the line. I am not some random pool guy MWE found and dressed in a suit. And I am definitely no pedophile. Sara: Well, good to hear. Mason: Very good, well, I would like to get this over with so I can spend the rest of the day with my girlfriend. So, not to be rude, but can we begin? Bruno: My apologies, I know that new relationships need to be established. So, let's begin. So what are your thoughts on being paired in this Four-on-Three handicap tag match this week against Rated X and David Whitman? *Mason has already begun to give his evil smirk as he thinks about the question.* Mason: To be honest, it's ridiculous. At Wrestle Wars, I kept the match going. I saved Sinc Mercier's a** and put his foot on the bottom rope. I decimated both Alejandro and Sinc Mercier and what do I get for it? Nothing! In fact, instead of giving me the shot at the United States championship, they pit a man who was pinned and a man who wasn't even in the match on Sunday in the title contention. It's extremely unfair. Bruno: And who is in the match? Mason: Well, for one you have Sinc Mercier. I'll get to him in a second. However, they put in Sonny Williams. I mean what the f**k! Sonny Williams, that no talented a**clown lost his World Heavyweight Championship on Sunday. There was no third superstar in his way, the referee wasn't retarded, and he didn't have a disability and he still lost the match. Yet, he is still granted a championship match for a title he is not even in contention for. If you suck that bad at wrestling that you are lowered from Main Event to mid-card and you lose your title, well then you shouldn't wrestle at all. However, on the other hand, I was screwed over because my referee was retarded and Alejandro Rodriguez interfered, but I am put into a handicap tag match. Tell me how that makes sense. *Mason takes a breath and rolls his eyes once.* Mason: And then you have SINC F**KING MERCIER who is put into the match. Now, this...*Mason chuckles* this is the funny part. I would like to show the viewers at home something. *Mason quickly finds a computer and goes to MWE.com. He pulls up a video of his match's ending.* QUOTE Rodriguez hits the L.T.F on Mercer and scores the pinfall …1 …2 Suddenly Mason Reason grabs the left leg of Sinc mercer and places it on the bottom rope but the ref doesn’t see it …3 Mason: As you can clearly see, I saved Mercier's a** and he still lost the match. He is an imbecile and an insult to wrestling. Sara: He is more than an insult. He is a spit in the face to the art of wrestling and anything revolving this sport. And to believe that he got another United States title shot... Mason: Is just sick. It makes me sick to my stomach and should make anyone who views Mayhem Wrestling Entertainment stomach's sick, as well. And believe me Sinc, I will not let this go unnoticed. I don't care you are emotionally scarred because your brother hates you and almost killed you. I don't care if you are drunk out of your mind and are laying on the side of the road. I will make sure that you get what is coming to you. And if, by chance, you beat Sonny Williams this Monday night, well then it will make me beating you down so much sweeter, because along with the satisfaction of you paying for what you did, I will also become the Mayhem Wrestling Entertainment's new United States champion. And as Sonny would say...Cha Ching! *Sara leans in and rests her head on Mason's shoulder for a little bit as he fixes his watch. Bruno also fixes his tie and takes a deep breath.* Bruno: So, what do you plan to do for your match this week? Mason: I plan to show people exactly how dominant I am. In my mind, this week will be two birds and one stone. I got Rated X and my teammates can have Whitman. This week will actually be better than I thought, because I get to do what I said I was going to do before Wrestle Wars. I told D.C that if he couldn't get rid of Brian Michaels, then I would get rid of them both. Well, I plan to stick true to my word by completely destroying D.C and Brian Michaels at the same time this week. And David Whitman, if you so much as put a hair in my way, then I will use you to beat your teammates. I don't care how I have to do it, I will make an example out of Rated X on Monday. Brian Michaels, by now, you must have realized that you can not beat me. Upon my debut, I claimed that I would go ballistic on you and make you wish that you never had to wrestle me. Well, look what happened. I creamed you and made people wary of my sanity when I beat the living hell out of you. I left you unconscious in the ring all those weeks ago and I will do it again, with even less effort and fire than last time. This time, no chalkboards, no erasers or rules. No foreign objects, just our abilities, our fists, and our egos. They will clash, but like the United States presidential election, I will destroy you as Obama did to McCain. No contest, no work, no need for you to even try for it will be useless. *Sara Whitaker now joins in with the wicked grin that Mason has displayed so many times. She is obviously pleased with what she is hearing.* Mason: And D.C, we finally get to wrestle each other. To bad there has to be interference. I would love nothing more than to teach you how to get things done. I was confident, I really was, that you were going to once and for all beat Michaels. I figured that you were going to finally close the door of a rivalry started a year ago when we conspired to end Michael's career. Instead, you b****ed out and now I have to the work myself. I am disappointed in myself, as well. I used to think that you had some real talent when I saw you so enthusiastic about betraying your partner. When you had absolutely no remorse for making people feel bad about themselves and make them feel pain. That was when I thought so high of you. However, after this Sunday you proved to be just as useless as my former mentor's training. I can't tell if it will be a pleasure bringing you back to what you were or a disappointment that you left that state in the first place. I guess time will tell and Monday will show what you can really do. Don't disappoint. *Sara clears her throat and Mason stops in his tracks. He backs off a little bit and allows Sara to take control of the camera.* Sara: I stated before, when I was interfered that the men should do what they have to do. I said that they should compete in their matches and we should compete in ours, and I stick true that. I expect you to be by D.C's side this Monday, as I will when Mason comes to the ring. I just have to say, once more, that if you get even close to interfering in this match, that I will make sure that I personally escort you from the ring. *Sara gave a reassuring wink of sarcasm and let Mason take over again.* Mason: And as for you Whitman, I can't say anything but stay out of my way. You don't want to get in the way of me. *Michael Bruno is staring in awe at the duo of Mason and Sara. He realizes they are staring at him and jumps back into focus.* Bruno: So, now that this portion of the interview is out of they way, I have to ask what you think of being paired with Alejandro Rodriguez, Joey Dallas, and Ace Cannon? I mean, you can't be happy with this? Mason: To be honest, I could care less at this point. As long as they don't get in my way I'll be fine and they better not lose the match before I am tagged in. Bruno: No comments on Ace Cannon or any of your other tag team partners for this week? Mason: Actually, yes. Ace Cannon, I need to set the record straight. You claim to be the best competitor in the wrestling universe. You say people, like Joey Dallas ride on your Coattails. Well I have news for you, new guys should not talk so much smack. Yes, I know you have been in Mayhem Wrestling Entertainment before, but that doesn't change a thing. The fact of the matter is, you haven't proven you are worthy of any praise yet. Make an impact and then talk, win the match this week or something, but until then, no one is riding on anyone's coattails but you. Bruno: Any closing comments before the interview comes to a close, Mason? Mason: Yes, actually I have one more thing for one Sinclair Mercier. You act tough and big, you boast and pout when you lose. You wallow in your defeat and try to push your problems away. Well, some problems...you can't push away. *Everyone, including Sara, Bruno, and the camera man are confused.* Mason: And if you didn't know...Now ya know! *Mason hooks Sara's arm and they stand up and leave. The cameras follow them all they way until they get in their car and drive off.
