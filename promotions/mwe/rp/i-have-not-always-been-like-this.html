I have not always been like this.; A JJ Executioner RP

Sep 20 2008

JJ Executioner

*OOC I hope this formatting works, I may come back to edit it if it doesn't.* “I can’t believe that my message has been truly heard by all. The MWE has yet to know true mayhem.” We see the giant new MWE star JJ Executioner sitting in a very small dark room. There are two very small gaps in the stone wall with metal grates across them. As a result there is the bare minimum amount of light in there hardly and it therefore is dank and shadowy. JJ is there, sat on his haunches. His head rests on the wall and his arms are crossed. He sits in silence for a second, before getting up and pulling at something in the window. The camera moves closer to the window and shows that it is heavily razored barbed wire that is coming away from the window. The camera then focuses on JJ’s hand, which begins to bleed heavily from the multiple wounds. ]*Flashback* 20 years ago, aged nine years A small boy walks though a narrow corridor. He is about 9 years old, is thin, quite frail looking and wears an ill-fitting school uniform. He approaches a set of double doors but gets knocked back suddenly as a group of five boys, who are much older and taller, crashes through them, knocking the youngster to the floor. They surround him and he cowers beneath them. Within seconds, he has been kicked all over his body and lies in a huddled, defensive position against the wall. The group has left quickly upon causing such damage in a minimal amount of time. The youngster is scared stiff. A small stream of blood trickles steadily from his nose, which is crushed and bent out of joint. The young boy feels the trickle of blood and rubs his nose with his wrist. As he looks at his hand, he gets a petrified look in his eyes. *End* JJ is seen walking. On both sides he is flanked by workshop tables, full of unusual tools and pieces of metal equipment. As he approaches the end of the room, he begins to adjust some straps on what looks like a variation on a dentists’ chair. It is only now that the tools that JJ has walked past can be seen properly. On one side there are rusting hacksaws of many sizes. Underneath are a large vice and a dusty sanding machine. On the other is a range of smaller handheld objects, such as knives and wrenches. JJ picks up a screwdriver. He goes to use them on the chair but then begins to look at them. Despite the fact that he wears a mask to obstruct his face, his eyes show and they betray a look of familiarity. He sits down in the chair and begins to play with the screwdriver, twisting it into the palm of his hand. “So many tools, so little time. This is my workshop, these are my tools. And those who fall before me in the ring will arrive here and the job will be finished.” He then gets up and moves behind the chair. He bends down and begins to tighten something by the floor. The camera follows him, before it climbs up. Above JJ, is a pair of scythes, hanging ominously over the chair. *Flashback* 3 years later, aged 12 years This begins similarly to before. The same boy walks down the same corridor. The boy is now taller but still very thin for his age. He has an ugly looking lump in his nose and walks with a protracted limp. He turns and walks into a classroom. As he enters he sees the same group of five teens. He calmly tries to turn around and exit but there is a sixth member of the gang by the door and the youngster has the door quickly slammed in his face. An elbow to the back of the head floors the boy before the shock of stiff kicks to the midriff and head soon render him unconscious. Two hours later, he wakes up. Blood that streamed from a gashed eyebrow has dried. The boy presses it to check the damage. The pain from the wound throbs but he doesn’t look as scared. As the camera fades, a small smile crosses his face. *End* JJ walks through the shadows, completely black in his hooded outfit. He carries a prone body across his shoulders. He steps into a room that has boxes on either side. JJ plants the body without much care on the floor before walking out and slamming the door shut. JJ: “Pain......pain is a tool that I use. Thought not like a steel chair or a rope. Pain is a motivational tool. If I get punched, *JJ chuckles to himself* I kick three times as hard. If I bleed and I will choke somebody out.” *Flashback* 5years later, aged 17 years We see the same classroom as before but it looks very different. There are tables and chairs as you would expect but none in working condition as legs are bent, tops are smashed and even windows are cracked. Amongst the remains are six bodies, still and unconscious. The legs of a seventh are wrapped around the neck of a eighth. The figure stands up slowly and brushes himself down. He is barely recognisable as the young boy who we have seen suffering but he is the same person, with a shock of greasy jet black hair cut to his shoulder, a generally more muscular frame and 18 inches taller. *End* As that scene ends, we are in a graveyard. The gravestones in the middle are laid in a large circle. JJ stands in the very middle of it. He holds a large shovel across his body. The camera moves right down in front of him. JJ: “Vega Knight......this is a very simple warning for you funny guy.....the MWE is not the place for you to act like the comedian you claim to be. My very reason for coming to this company is to take out just like you, those with a lack of credibility or dedication. Choose your next words carefully, lest you not survive the night, for with every word you speak you come closer to being the next victim of the Death Scythe.” JJ begins to dig as the camera rises and fades. *OOC the editing didn't work, i wanted to centre the flashbacks, could somebody tell me how to do this?*
