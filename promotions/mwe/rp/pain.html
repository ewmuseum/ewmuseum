Pain..

May 7 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
“This I sat engaged in guessing, but no syllable expressing To the fowl whose fiery eyes now burned into my bosom's core; This and more I sat divining, with my head at ease reclining On the cushion's velvet lining that the lamp-light gloated o'er, But whose velvet violet lining with the lamp-light gloating o'er She shall press, ah, nevermore!” Prologue The war is over. The smoke is cleared. Standing with his arm raised in victory is the man known as “The Definition of Violence” Nic Kraven. In a blaze of glory, Kraven took on Devlin Sharpe and Peter Kaymakcian last week. The match was dubbed as The Battle of the ICW Graduates. Kraven came out victorious after pinning Kaymakcian with the GTV and also keeping his hot streak in MWE intact. A streak that started with Scotty McKnight and ended with Peter Kaymakcian and Devlin Sharpe. Kraven has been absolutely dominating here for quite a while now but another competitor awaits him on the next MNM. The competitor: The former MWE Intercontinental Champion and one half of Uncultured Anarchy, Zak Warner. What will become of Nic now that he faces one of the top wrestlers in the company? Will Kraven somehow pull an upset? Or will Warner beat him and break the streak as well? Is there a chance Warner's partner, Kevin Hardaway, will have any involvement? All answers lie ahead. ~~~~~~~~~~~~Inside the mind and dream of the disturbed one~~~~~~~~~~~~ Darkness. Nothingness. Nothing can be seen or heard. Not even a bulb of light can break through this unbearable darkness. Suddenly, the darkness is wiped away and light engulfs the whole scene. The dark has been driven away. Light is now shining. Ahead lies the crystal blue ocean. The waves crashes against the sand making a splash. Its so...peaceful. A hand is seen reaching out. Whose hand does this belong to? What is the meaning of this? Now it hits him, its his hand. But, whats the purpose of all of this? Now that he thinks about it, whats the purpose of..him? Who is he suppose to be? What is he suppose to be? Why must he be the man he is today? Why must his life end up like this? Questions fill his mind now. However, there are no answers. A mind full of questions yet no one around to answer it. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ SCENE #1 ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ [The scene opens up in a dimmed room somewhere backstage where no one dared to venture. There, we see a light bulb that lights up only part of the room. The other part lays completely dark. This room is just like any other room. Four bare walls, and one single window. The light from the window can not shine through though as there are shades covering it. Just a plain ordinary room. However though, this is room means more to Nic than just any regular room. This room is where he goes and ponders his thought. A room where he carefully thinks out his plan in the ring. And now, we are back in the very same room. Under the light bulb, we see a single steel chair. There sits “The Definition of Violence” Nic Kraven. He has on a black shirt with what looks like an evil clown with blood covering it, a leather jacket, and dark blue jean shorts. His head bowed with his semi long black hair completely covering his face. We can see a small book shape object at Nic's side but we can't completely tell. The camera moves closer towards him until it is standing in front of him. Nic looks up slightly at the camera for a few seconds before speaking.] “The Definition of Violence” Nic Kraven Who am I? What am I? What am I meant to be in this world? You see, for the last year or so, I have been questioning the reason for my existence. Am I placed on this world for the soul purpose of hurting people? Is there something more to this? Am I loner destined to wonder endlessly? Or is there something more I have not discovered yet? What am I? Who am I? Am I destined to be cursed all my life? An emotionless man with the only purpose is vengeance. A man feared by others. Shunned, forgotten, ignored. Am I the hero? Or am I the villain? Am I God? Or am I the Devil? Why is it that my life has become what it is? I look around and I see children that has been rescued by God. God has blessed them. They have had good lives so far. They have been raised by wonderful parents. But when I look at myself, I feel cursed. Why wasn't I saved by this God? Why was I the one that was left out?! Why was this boy..this seemingly innocent boy known as, Shawn Travis, be the catalyst of this heartless man known as Nic Kraven?! [Nic takes a moment and wipes off the sweat that was beading down his forehead while he was speaking. He strokes back his semi long black hair from his eyes and then speaks once again.] “The Definition of Violence” Nic Kraven Well? Why? Out of all these children in the world, why was I left with no savior in sight? Was this some bias situation on God's part? Was I merely just born at the wrong time? Whose fault was this? Who instigated all this pain on me? Why was I forced to become the man that I am today? I know your there. Your always around even if I don't need you. When I anger, you consult me. When I'm in anguish, your here. You...are...always...here. Answer me! I know your around here! ANSWER ME SERENITY! [A gust of wind suddenly blows across the room sending chills down his spine. A calm, delicate voice speaks out in the darkness signaling that the voice, Serenity, was here once again.] “The Voice” Serenity Anger? Tell me Shawn, what has made you rage? “The Definition of Violence” Nic Kraven Don't give me that shit. You know why. Now answer me. Why couldn't I have been saved by this god? Why does it have to be this way? I...I have become nothing more but the Devil to the people that have set their eyes on me. I am nothing more than a cold heartless demon in their eyes. What is my purpose in this world? “The Voice” Serenity Why? Its because I got to you first. I have led you away from the inner conflicts of this world. You are neither a follower or a servant of the Devil. I...I have led you away from God and at the same time, shield you from the Devil's clutches. You are above all of this. [Nic is deemed speechless with these lines. He knew it, everybody knew it. Deep down in his heart, he knew this was all true. He was just merely to foolish to realize it. All this time, he was convinced that the Devil has gotten to him. Convinced that the Devil was somehow the cause of all this. Nic remains speechless for a few more minutes until uttering a single line.] “The Definition of Violence” Nic Kraven But then...what is my purpose? “The Voice” Serenity Tell me this Shawn. What is the thing you most desire out of this godforsaken world? What is your deepest desire? Think long and hard about this and then answer me. “The Definition of Violence” Nic Kraven Vengeance... “The Voice” Serenity This is your purpose. Listen to me closely Shawn. The way you think of yourself is foolish. You do not need a God to save you and you are beyond the control of the Devil. You are neither light or darkness. You are beyond it. Shawn, I have given everything you needed. I have given you all the tools to suceed in your purpose. I have molded you into what you have been wanting for the longest while. I have molded you into a God. A God of vengeance, fury, hatred. I have given you strength and desire to make this all become a reality. [With these last words, the gust that have been blowing around the room has stopped. The chills were gone. Serenity was gone. Nic was left all alone once again to ponder his thoughts. He needed peace. Slowly, he reaches across to his side pulls out a small book like object. White earphones are wrapped tightly around it. Nic slowly untangles the earphones and presses a button on the object. A blue light flashes on and Nic presses another button once again. He then puts his earphones on. With this, he bows his head letting his hair fall in front of his face. The song slowly begins playing.] Your bottles' almost empty You know this can't go on Because of you my mind is always racing The needles' breaking your skin The scar is sinking in And now your trip begins but It's all over for It's all over for [Its uncanny just how powerful music can be at its fullest. Why, it can even soothe a clouded, disturbed mind. This was all that was needed to bring peace to Nic's mind. As the lyrics flow through his mind, he starts thinking about his match this upcoming Monday on MNM. And then, his opponent, Zak Warner.] You For you When you're on the edge and falling off It's all over for you For you When you're on the edge and falling off It's all over [Yes, Zak Warner. A former MWE Intercontinental Champion. Thoughts about the match races through his mind. Nic can barely stay at peace now. The storm in his mind has come back. Nic then strokes his hair back but stays in the same position as before.] I know what runs through your blood You do this all in vain Because of you my mind is always racing And it gets under my skin To see you giving it And now your trip begins but It's all over for It's all over for [These lyrics, its as if it was telling him a secret message. This whole song, it just fits his upcoming match with Warner. But slowly, he pulls out the earphones and sets the object aside. Nic knew he could not sit here for ever as he has something to do. Nic stands up, and then makes his way through the door and out the room taking one last glance. With this, the scene slowly fades to black.] ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ SCENE #2 ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ [The scene reopens in the MWE arena. As the camera pans around, the crowd cheers loudly. You can just tell it through the reaction that the fans are having the night of their lives. We see that some people are holding signs that says, “Kraven fears Warner!” All of a sudden though, the lights in the arena go out. We can hear some small children screaming. We then hear I Stand Alone by Godsmack blast on the PA System and engulfs the arena.] Now I've told you this once before You can't control me If you try to take me down you're gonna break Now I feel your hatred nothing that you're doing for me I'm thinkin you outta make your own way I stand alone Inside I stand alone [Nic makes his way out of the curtains and on to the stage as the lights come back on. The crowd begins to boo loudly with barely a few cheering. He ignores this as he does the infamous “Raven Taunt.” Nic continues down the ring with a downpour of boos coming at his direction. He slides into the ring and goes to sit at the corner of the turnbuckle as the lights dim. Nic calls for a mic and receives it. He sits there staring at the crowd for a few more seconds before speaking.] “The Definition of Violence” Nic Kraven Your reaction does not effect me anymore. Your hatred for me does not surprise me. Everywhere I go, I get the same reaction. I know you all hate me with a passion and I can not change this. This however, is not what I was going to address. You see, this week, I have a great obstacle set before me. The man that stands in my way, is Zak Warner. [The crowd cheers loudly at the slight mention of Warner's name.] Its no secret that the crowd is very fond of you. They see you as a hero in their eyes. Its painful to see that. However Warner, I praise you. Let me take you back to a moment in time, December of 2008. The moment you first walked into the locker room. You commanded respect and soon got it. Later on, you won the MWE Intercontinental Championship. Let me ask you Warner, how did it feel? How did it feel to be a champion? To earn respect from these people? Now let me ask you, how would it feel if everything you had worked for, disappear right in front of your eyes? It would hurt wouldn't it? The pain would consume you wouldn't it? That pain...that pain is what I have been feeling for the last 15 years. You can't even comprehend how this would feel. “The Definition of Violence” Nic Kraven You see Warner, we are a like in many ways. You are tortured by the voice that haunts you. This voice, it tells you to do unspeakable things doesn't it Warner? This voice wants you to give into your insanity. I can see it in your eyes, you suffer the same as me. No matter how hard you try, this voice keeps coming back. Its haunting, isn't it? To know that it will haunt you for the rest of your life. We have faced the same sufferings. I recall that you have lost somebody close to you before. Who was it again? Hmm? I seem to remember that he went by the name of..Jason Cross. Ahh yes, now I remember. So Warner, how did it feel? To know that you will never see your beloved friend again? Its painful isn't it? It eats at your every soul. I can see it in you, the memories you have of him haunts you to the very core. You want to see him again so badly, but that will never happen. Hes gone now, out of this world. [Nic takes a minute to chuckle at the pain eating at Warner. He looks around the arena as the fans greeting him in a downpour of boos. He takes a second to stroke his hair back and then speaks again.] “The Definition of Violence” Nic Kraven You see now? We are more alike than you know. We have seen pain and torture. We have seen death. We have both been tortured by the voices in our heads. But then, this is where the similarities end. Along your way to the road of glory in this company, you found a companion. While I don't need a companion. You see, what you fail to realize that this is a dog eat dog world. There are no friends you can trust. Sooner or later, they will all turn on you. You see, I also had a companion once. A Tag Team partner. I remember that final moments of that match. I was almost there. Close..so close to that World Champion. I with my partner at ringside against the champion. Later on in the match, he would turn on me and cost me the match. Why? Was it because he was jealous of my success? I don't know. You see Warner, I learned that day that friends are for the weak. The only person on this earth you can rely on is yourself. You wouldn't think for a second that your friend Hardaway will turn on you if he gets his most deepest desires? That he wouldn't back stab you if he ever had the chance at that World Title. If you have not had that thought cross your mind, you are foolish. You know, what they say is true, friends come and go. You need to realize this. “The Definition of Violence” Nic Kraven Ever since your Intercontinental Title reign, you've seem to fallen off the title picture. I see, that you're not the same as before. You have been forced to battle foes like Jekyl or Scotty McKnight. Both of which I have defeated successfully. Ever since, you have been cheated out of victory, or have been beaten cleanly. It appears, you have gotten soft. What is the reason behind this? Is it that you have simply lost a step? Is it because of Uncultured Anarchy? Or is it just because of your so called companions? [Kraven takes a minute to take a breath. With the crowd still booing, he smirks a bit and continues once again.] “The Definition of Violence” Nic Kraven Well Warner, for whatever reason, I will make you see the light. Your success in this companions have blinded you. Your companions have made you week. You see Warner, you may not think much of me. You may just think I'm some ICW Graduate. But tell me this, have you ever faced somebody like me? Somebody that knows, and feels what you have been through. I know your pain and I feel your torture. We are alike. Am I afraid that your partner might ruin our match? I do. But I am tired of worrying. So Warner, fight me. For this week, I thirst for pain. Bring your all to this match Warner because I see it in you. You want to destroy me. Limb from limb. You want a victory to carry momentum on to Tribulation. So do so by fighting me. Fight for your so called companions. Fight for Uncultured Anarchy. Fight for each and every fan that sees you as a hero, a role model, a god. Serenity was right, The God of Vengeance has risen. I've built my own parables and psalms. I have made my own rules. Yes, I walk through the valley of the shadow of death. I fear no evil for I am the dark god himself and this is my valley! Quote the book of Kraven! I am tired of waiting. I am tired of playing mind games. For I have come to do battle. Its due or die. Enter this battlefield and fight me. This is a crusade and I am a Dark God come to battle. Come Warner, the battle awaits. Come in here and fight me for I shall win this battle. Nevermore! [Slowly, he stands and taunts to the crowd. The fans booing wildly with a few flipping him off. But what does this matter? This is a battle that the fans have no part of. Strangely, the lights in the arena one by one crackle and goes out. The final light now goes off. With this, Nic takes one last breath as the camera shuts off.]
