Alot of shit in one

Aug 22 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The camera's turn on to a empty basketball arena. Alex Bowen Is shooting baskets. Missing a few here and there. Hitting a few two's. He grabs another ball and looks back at the camera and goes to the three point line. Dribbles the ball, Jumps up a little bit and shoots. The ball soars, But still misses the basket completely. Alex grumbles a bit. Alex- Allways miss the damn three's. Alex walks over to a bag setting on a empty bleacher and unzips it and grabs a small sized towl and whipes off his face. Then whipes down his arms and legs. He reaches back in the bag and pulls out a shirt and a small stick of deodorant, and pulls of his shirt and whipes down his scared stomach and back. He puts on some deodorant, and puts the shirt over his head. But suddenly takes it back off, and puts it back in the bag with his other suplys And points at a scar running down his left pec. Alex- This is what destroying yourself over and over will get you. Nothing but pain, and a ulgy ass scar. Ten years ago, I had maybe two. Alex turns around slowely to show the camera his scars. Scars line his back from recent Aew matches and from old Jcw wars. Scars that range from small dots to scars the run the length of his back and arms. As he reaches face he starts to chuckle a bit. Alex- Pain is nothing but an imagination. A feeling of sorts, my opponet this week calls himself the "painbringer". I was originally poised to face the supposed italian gangster, Bg Mancini this week.... Well was anyways. Damien took it upon himself to take him out. To make a name for himself, Once again in Mwe. A former champ he was. But this time nothing will stop me from becomming champ. I lost my tag title in Aew, and the X Innernet title shot that was gave to me. Prety shitty I know. But today is the dawn of a new day. A new day in the life of the hardcore juggalo himself. A new chance to win a title. The us title. Alex walks back over to his bag and sets down and grabs the towel out of his bag again to whipe his face. Alex- I'm just done messing around with people. I need to go back to my roots. I love the fans and there reactions to me. But god dammit I'm sick of losing to people I KNOW I CAN BEAT. The new and old people in Mwe can hold a fucking candle to the tallent thats stored in this old scarred body. I'm only fucking 28, I feel as if I'm 50 at times. But you wanna know what Damien? I'm mwe's fucking rookie of the year. I might be older. But this is my first shot in mwe. No one has the style I have, The ability, The everything else. Alex points at his chest. and hits it a few times. Alex- I'm the now, The future. And forever, Mwe's greatest new star. The crop of tallent we got from Aew and other promotions is unbelievable. But I'm on tope damien. You wanna know why? I'll do anything to win. Alex puts up four fingers and pulls down on one. Alex- Break a bone, I've done it many times. Alex puts down one finger leaving three, and pulls down another. Alex- Tear the ligaments in my right and left nee's? Done the left one twice, and the right one once. Alex puts down his third finger and pulls on another. Alex- Cut my body to pieces on a regular basis? Done that you've seen the proof. Do you need any more? Alex puts down his second finger and leaves one up. He gets in close to the camera and starts to wisper. Alex- And did I mention the fact that I love to inflict pain on my opponets? Ace cannion, In aew I wrapped him in barbed wire around a ring post and knocked him out. He ripped and teared to get him self out of the barbed wire. And what about another so called allumni? Sonny williams, when he dissed me in aew, I whiped his ass all over that damn arena, then I put his head through a car window. Alex puts his hands up to his face and rubs them down his face with a evil laugh. Alex- You dont know me damien, What I'm going to do to you in this match is going to be awsome for me! I'm going back to my roots. I've put plenty of people in pain, But you the so called painbringer will be my greatest feat yet. I've herd that mwe was the shit back in the day. You'r an allumni, I'm going to take a shit on old mwe and everyone that was in it when I rip you limb from limb. And I'm going to enjoy EVERY....FUCKING.... SECOND OF IT! Alex reaches over angerly with his right hand and grabs his bag and storms off to the door. He looks back and winks at the camera a little bit. Alex- And that mwe, and damien you can go to the fucking bank with
