Welcome to México Ryan Adams; Cruz/Warner/Riddick RP

Jul 22 2009

Jesus Cruz & Zak Warner & Jay Riddick

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
One knee, and out went Blight. One knee, and an impact was made. One knee, one win, one man. But, a one-hit-wonder? A mere flash in the pan? Fifteen seconds of fame? A man that people will look back on, and ponder 'What was that one Mexican fighter kid's name? Umm, Jesus something'. Nah. No possibility. Not with him. He's for real. Oh what? Don't believe me? Same ol' same ol' cliches of bull? Hey, whatever, that's fine. But when Jesús wins, and wins, and wins, and wins, and wins, then I want you to do something for me. Apologize. Because I gave you fair warning. Fade the fuck in bitches. Once again, we kick off the promo at Cruz's gym. But this time, we're on the outside looking in. The windows are a little fogged and blurred, but their is enough visibility to see the hard fighters inside, training. The camera slowly zooms back, showing the 'Xico Mezcló Artes Marcial Academia' sign, which sits atop the small but stable copper stone building. It appears rugged but clean. As we pan over, we see wrestling's ringside commentator Ryan Adams. He's looking mighty snazzy this evening, attired with black dress-shirt and khaki pants. Holding a MWE mic in hand, he begins with a smile. Ladies and gentlemen, I'm here in Xico Mexico thanks to the fine folks at Xico Mezcló Artes Marcial Academia. Co-owner and founder of the gym, Xavier York, contacted me yesterday morning. He offered me a first-class trip down here to interview MWE's newest talent, Jesús Cruz. And I, of course, graciously accepted. The glass door swings open, and Feliciano Cruz pops his head outside. Bienvenido Sr. Adams. Venga adentro. (Welcome Mr. Adams. Come inside.) Feliciano retreats back inside the gym, as Ryan stands puzzled. After a few seconds of confusion, Adams walks over to the camera man, behind the shot of view. Though he attempts to be quiet, his whispers are heard over the recording. You speak Spanish? Very little. Well, uhhh, pick any of that up? .....Nope. Feliciano swings the door back open, and peaks his head back out. ¿Qué espera usted? (What wait you?) Adams steps forward, back into the shot, still looking quite helpless. ....Err, me speaky no Espanio. ...... ¿Qué? (..... What?) ...Do you speak English? ............ (............) Feliciano peaks his head back inside, and begins to yell. ¡Yo no comprendo una palabra que este hombre dice! ¡Manuel, usted habla con este bastardo! (I do not understand a word this man says! Manuel, you speak with this bastard!) The door extends fully out once again. A young clean-cut Spanish man pats Feliciano on the shoulder, and passes him by. Feliciano makes his way back inside with a growl, as the young man holds open the door with a grin. You have to excuse my father. He just can't comprehend those north of the border. Oh, ha, I see. But father you say? Well then, that must mean... The young Spanish male extends his hand. The name's Manny Cruz; brother of Jesús. Great to meet you Manny. The two men begin a handshake, but Ryan's facial expression quickly changes. He pulls back his hand, as it is now sticky. Ah, sweaty after a long days work eh? Oh, that's no sweat. Manny clears his throat, and spits at the ground with a look of disgust. As Ryan grimaces in shock, looking at his hand, Manny places his arm around Ryan's shoulders and guides him into the gym. The camera guy follows, as the three men enter. The smell of sweat and the high temperature of heat make the place near unbearable. However, Ryan Adams doesn't even notice. Why? He's to busy vigorously rubbing his hand up and down his leg, in an attempt to completely rub off any and all of Manny's saliva. Jesus.... Actually, it's pronounced 'Jesús'. Ryan looks up to see Jesús "El Peligro" Cruz standing before him. Oh, errm, yes I-.... It's a pleasure. With one final wipe, he extends his hand. Jesús leaves Ryan hangin'. Ah, I see you've met my brother. Manny, who still has his arm wrapped around Ryan, gives Jesús a wink before walking off with some swagger in his step. Once he is out of sight, Adams drops his arm back down to his side and speaks up. Yeah... He's a real class act. No doubt. We'll conduct the interview in my father office. Jesús turns to his right, and opens up a large sturdy oak door. Sitting behind the office desk is Feliciano Cruz. Salga padre, nosotros tenemos un shoot para hacer. (Leave father, we have a shoot to do.) Feliciano gets up with a nod. His eyes stare down Ryan as he exits the room, slamming the door behind him. ......Nice guy. Yep. Have a seat. Ryan sits back in the cushy chair. It's a great chair, no doubt, but it's nothing compared to Feliciano's office chair, which Jesús claims with his ass. ........ ........ So, shall I start then? Please do. Ryan reaches into his pocket, and pulls out a paper wad. He opens it up, and tries to flatten it out. Across the top in big bold letters, it reads: Questions. Wow, you really came prepared huh? Oh, yeah uh, you see, I'm not a interviewer. I'm a commentator. Which brings me to my first question.... Why me? Why did you choose me to come here to speak with you, out of all the other options? Simple. For everybody watching at home, there are two voices heard over their televisions during a match. David Ferrera's, and yours. And after rewatching my match with Blight last week, it was clear that Ferrera understands greatness when he sees it. But you? I'm not so sure yet. Well, you did get an impressive debut win last week. Of course I did. I'm unstoppable. I could beat any MWE wrestler. D.C., Mason, Sinc, G.M., you name it. And I won't stop until I'm givin' what's rightfully mine. The MWE World Championship. What's rightfully yours?! You've only had one match! And you think you deserve the top championship in this industry?!? Yeah.... I do.... Jesús leans forward in his chair, cringing his fists on the table. You got a problem with that? ...Na.. Not at all.... Good. Next question. Adams slowly and carefully moves his eyes from Cruz to the paper. You've been booked in a triple threat match this week against Jay Riddick and Zak Warner. You are clearly the underdog in this bout, as you've never competed in a match of this nature before. What's your game plan? If I told you, then Zak and Jay would know what to expect, now wouldn't they? ......Well, I suppo- Listen... This match was most likely thrown together as a filler. I'm stepping into the ring with two men full of problems. Wether it be Zak's ongoing feud with Kevin Hardaway, or Riddick being a walking target as X-TV champion, I'm the only one with a clean slate. All I have to worry about is the bout at hand, and believe me Ryan, when I show up, I'll have a winning game plan. Can't you just give us a sneak peak of what's to come? I mean, if you're as good as you say you are, it won't matter if they know what's coming or not. Right? .......... Jesús leans forward once again, and begins to whisper. Don't play games with me Ryan. I'm not! Stop fucking with me Ryan. I didn't fly you out here to get fucked with. I didn't mean it like that! You show anymore disrespect, even a hint, I won't hesitate to make an example out of you. We clear? OK, we're clear, I'm sorry, I'll move on. ....I'd think that'd be best. Jesús reclines back in his chair and crosses his arms at his chest. Ryan Adams takes a deep breath, before continuing. Well Mr. Cruz, you cl- Mr. Cruz is my father's name. You can call me sir. ....Sir, you claim that no one in MWE trains at the level that you do, and that you're the fastest rising superstar in the company. However, "The Uprising" Jay Riddick has had to work long and hard for anything and everything that man's received, and it's debatable that Riddick is the future of MWE. How do you respond? Jay Riddick's training is shit compared to mine. You see, Riddick trains with "XXX" to become a bigger and stronger wrestler. I, on the other hand, train to expand and increase my fight arsenal. Because as I showed last week, it doesn't matter how large you are. It matters how dangerous you are. And that is what makes me a success. Just look at the guy. He was on top of the world. Hell, even I admit that he 'was' the fastest rising superstar. But his uprising halted when Kraven dominated him and Clark at Independence Day. But still, he was handed the X-TV championship out of pity. Sad really. Then last week, he was givin' the opportunity to crush Joseph Sharpman's return hype with a one-on-one match. But did the newly crowned champ pick up the W over the rusty return of Sharpmen? No. He was knocked unconscious. No excuses Riddick. You're slumping, and I'm capitalizing on it. Strong response. Hey, way to state the obvious. Ryan shoots Jesús a smile, but Jesús's face remains serious. Next question. The smile slowly fades from Adam's face. ...Kay. He clears his throat and moves on. Zak Warner, your other opponent, has some MMA training experience of his own. And in other organizations, he has had success in the Ultimate Edge and Underground division, which follow mixed martial art rules. If Zak Warner ever decided to get into professional MMA, how do you think he would fair against other opponents? Terribly. Sure he's beaten a few wrestlers, but of course, he knows some basics. However, against an actual fighter, he'd be embarrassed. Perhaps if he were to stick to his MMA training from the beginning, he MAYBE could have made a name for himself. But we'll never know will we? Because instead, he decided to adapt into wrestling. Now, he strikes some, but mostly plays to the crowd with an outrageous moveset of flips, spins, twists, and slams. He risks his body day in and day out. And for what? The roar of the crowd to feed his ego?! I don't respect anything about Zak Warner. Wether it be his attitude, his style, his over-confidence, or even those damn dog tags he wears around his neck, it's all poser to me. You show no fear, do you? Fear is for the weak. Jesús throws a jab that comes inches from Ryan's face. Ryan flinches hard, ripping his paper in half, and clumsily falling out of his chair. Heh... see what I mean? Ryan quickly gets to his feet, and dusts himself off. *sigh* Well, the interview has been lovely. The pleasure's all mine. Jesús extends his hand, and Ryan shakes it. Bad idea. Another spit handshake. Ryan pulls his hand back in anger, as Jesús chuckles. Come on dude! Damn! With a smirk, Jesús slaps the living piss out of Ryan, causing him to fall to the ground and curl up in the fetal position. Tell Ferrera I said hi. Jesús cups his hands around his mouth, and yells back into the gym. ¡Manny, Papá, les muestra a estos hombres la puerta! (Manny, Dad, show these men the door!) Manny and Feliciano Cruz burst through the door and storm into the office. Manny lifts Ryan up in a full nelson as Feliciano grabs the camera man by the shirt. The camera falls to the ground, as we see the two men being dragged out the door. Moments later, Jesús picks up the camera, and aims it at his mug. We're done here. Jesús chucks the camera at the wall, and the feed cuts to static.
