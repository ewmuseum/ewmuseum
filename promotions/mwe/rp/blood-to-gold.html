Blood to Gold

Jan 25 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Sometimes it takes a death in order for a birth to take place. An outstretched field of flowers. All colors; all sizes. The hooded man walks amongst nature’s spectacle as the wind cuts his path. Petals bustle at the gust’s might, forever displaced from their stem of birth. The man stops, surrounded by a gale of petals. A sight almost majestic, but impressive none the less. The dark figure appears to be focused. A black rose stands erected before him, his eyes dissecting the beauty. Intrigued by the matter's artistic value, his hand begins to lightly caress its petals. We are beautiful. We are masters of our own craft. We are thriving. The wind's might causes his coat's hood to drape down his back. Demanding eyes revealed. Dark, styled hair flowing in a cadence. Artsy, but you fucking love it. The hue of blue never seemed so savage. This look transcends into a stoic kneel, as though the gust is not present. As though the now pattering rain has no effect, his eyes are locked on his prospect. But we must die. The stalk is ripped from the moist earth with such haste. Such meaning. Each petal surrounding him, he stands amidst the destruction he's caused. With opened hands, and water beginning to fill them, he begins to speak coldly, as the petals vanish into obscurity. It is said that everyone that is alive has a purpose. Is it true of the dead? A look back into the direction of the petal's passing. Bill - My eyes are the two in which you will stare into as you take your plunge. Do not blink. Let your eyes water; let your eyes tear. Release the emotion, do not let it build up. Do not let the human emotion consume your black heart. It's only human to want what you cannot have. Selfish wants and selfish needs shouldn't crumble the armor of such a believable warrior. That's why I'm here, Mr. Williams. Ever since I was placed on this battlefield by my cowardly forebear, I was entitled end bringer. The relentless reliever. I have taken it upon myself to relieve you of your lie, to abolish your sin. Now you have the chance of knowing that because it hurts to breathe, that it hurts to look and to lift your head up, it's because of me that you'll be able to finally do it. I will give life, through death, and when the deed is done - I want you to look up at me from the flat of your back and I want you to see what a real man, what a great man is made of. I want you to define, for me, what I am to you. What am I to you, Bill? A concerned, curt tilt of the head. While your mindset is still fogged with self-denial, I'd like to know your answer. Do you view me as lesser? Easily toppled opposition - daft matter, troubled by overwhelming frailties? Flower easily picked from the garden by dirtied, bloodied, undeserving hand? I suggest placing that hand back into your pocket full of wishes, for you've made the wrong choice this day. There are things the feeble human mind cannot fathom, but there are things the mighty fist can induce. I will tell you what you pursue, and I will tell you what you cannot have. Victory, a glimpse you shall receive, but a full frontal view, never. Not in my line of sight. Do not let the word "why" mingle its way through your debauched mindset. You know why you won't win. I'm what you're not. Thorough. I was raised in the war; I've nothing else to learn on the battlefield. You challenging me for my badge of honor - Dishonorable. Foolish. Ignorant. You've much to learn. I've nothing to teach. Participate in the unfair exchange if you wish. A slow stride through the field. His eyes wandering amongst the others. I hold hate for fools that seek their closing chapters. Do not seek death, for death will find you. It will not be an easy task to look this man in the eye, though, for I seek the road which makes death a fulfillment. An accomplishment. Take me down that road, Billy boy? Take me down that road even though you've yet to find yourself - yet to know your fucking place on the battlefield? MY home. MY livelihood. Things that shouldn't be toyed around with. This is MY life, I suggest deciding yours before you attempt to threaten mine. I...can only end yours if you continue down this path. Millions of blocks, but they all end at one road, Billy. I'll be waiting. Lightning on cue. Delightful. I'll be watching. I'll be anticipating the proverbial final blow - knowing you're not its holder. I'll be expecting the pale rider himself to show up on my doorstep, but instead, I'll hear the weak knock of a - A mighty strike of lightning. Fucking.. A more heightened crackle this time. Bitch. Tapping at my door, promising a challenge. I'll take your empty word AND wasted effort, and make it mine. Like the last time. Remember? Accusing me of playing games. False accusations that should have resulted in three swift slaps on a mat. Broken will before my feet, the small man inside, doubled over in pain. You'd never show it; you'd never tell us how much it hurt. How much it sting. Sweat and tears flowing over an opened wound, perhaps I didn't make a tear deep enough. Part two, only this time, Bill Williams Jr, I've awakened. You‘re going to feel this, Motherfucker. You‘re going to be the first to understand the unknown… The rain’s volumes picking up now, it seems nothing can stop this man’s word. Motherfucker. His dark brown, overly styled head of hair now soaking wet, do you think he cares? This is the life I’VE chosen, and I’m going to see it through. God damn it, I’m going to see it through. No dreams hindering my sight. No fools blocking my path. All obstacles rendered obsolete. Just an understanding of my enemies. I know you, Bill. You chance for solace. You chance for hope. You chance for victory. Ever chance to breathe? Answer that question if I GIVE YOU a chance, Bill. The pressure for success, Billy, one of the most glorious strains. You cracked the last time. His voice now goes through a drastic change, an eerie whisper, almost inaudible, but you're listening close enough, are you not? Crumble. Be born again. Fade to black, but only for a moment as the voice can still be heard. God let there be light. The morning sun shines so eloquently on the barren oasis. This has to be a dreamscape, for the landscape’s beauty is edging on a plane unreal to ours. No, this is real. The sandstorm’s gust now blinding us of the beauty, placing us in a situation of panic. Lost. Left in a position to find ourselves. Where are we? Blood red ropes, pale white canvas, and black, unforgiving steel. Through the storm lies the wrestling ring. I will let there be night. The natural phenomena, the sunset. So sudden. All light is now dark, and all dust is now grounded. At rest. At ease. So cold, so lifeless. The night. Your sense of panic now transcends into one of comfort. Rest your head, and let the feeling overtake you. Don’t fight it. Dream on. Fight on. You’ll lose. God walked on water. The rain. Nature’s music. It only makes the slumber deeper. No thunder, no lightning. Just rain. So much the canvas has began to flood. The bottomless canvas where a shadowed individual walks across. An astral being, significantly holy. The creator of all. He fades. Unbelievable. I walk on blood. The pale moon reveals a bright red hue, and the shadowed being is now revealed, the halo dispersing. He treads through the blood, not once looking back. Mission mindset. No turning back now. God turned water to wine, but me: I turn blood to gold. The GWC World Title. You’re awake now. He stands in an empty arena, mirrors surrounding him, title wrapped snug around his waist, eyes focused far more than the camera’s focus. And they shut. And he speaks. Softly. Kill ‘em softly, kid. All that is left for me is my inevitable demise. A walk through the wrong door; a stride down the wrong path. Saddening it has to be this way. My own legs will take me to hell, and my mind will allow it. It was meant to be. The things I've done, I‘m no good man. All of you beasts in the back aren‘t saints either. Through the signing of the contract, we received our tickets to burn eternally. But I, I won‘t be the first. Death is the broom I take in my hand to sweep the wrestling world clean. When you all are burnt to a crisp, I will be the last to step in and bless that motherfucker. Cold. I’ve come from nothing to destroy everything. My badge of honor has been engraved on my iron fist. I’m not hungry anymore. I do not seek the shimmer of the gold encrusted leather, no. I seek the bright red contrast of blood splashing on my canvas. A brush dipped in red, perhaps, a true artist’s delight. And with a stroke of dominance, I will paint the perfect picture of despair. Agony. Torture. And when it’s finished you all will clap like fucking puppets, and I’ll love you for it, but you all will love me more. Unconditional love. Pure. Forever. I've given this business my all, and I'll be damned if I don't TAKE IT RIGHT BACK. I won't stop until I collect all the lost blood. ALL the broken bones - shattered hope, shattered dreams, I want it ALL BACK. This GWC Title, never should have left my waist. The EPW Deathmatch title, I made it famous. One thing, though. There's one thing that was NOT ONCE lost in this crusade, and that's my heart. You cannot break my heart with the infamous three count, and yet, they so valiantly try. Warriors, black knights with obligations to fulfill. All I can do is let them know that they've come to the wrong man. Swing your steel at diamond, for what? A standing ovation? A quench to your underlying thirst? Fun? Playtime's over, Bill William. The soft spoken tone is nevermore. That raspy shit hits the ears, and it shivers your spine. Billy Boy. Perhaps not you, but I've been waiting for the night that I could exchange holds with a man I respect. I could compare you to my coward father, for I respect him for what he allowed me to become, but other than that, fuck him. I respect you for the great talent that you possess, that has yet to cease in improving. The killer instinct you showcase night in and night out. I respect that, but other than that.. Fuck you. You say that I don’t have intensity, or determination. You say that I don’t have what it takes to step up and dethrone you. Yet I have proven time and time again that I lack none of what you stated. In fact, what I have is more than you could even hope to have. I've endured so much on the battlefield that I've come to realize that I just may not have a heart. I do not thrive on heartbeat, I thrive on blood-thirst. So, throw your fiber my way so I can unravel you, and finally see what a black heart truly looks like. I'm curious. I want to touch it; I want to hold it in my hand while the tempo of the beat lessens. And lessens, and slowly dies. Oh, but it's still beating. So slowly, and I will anticipate the last beat, and I will CRUSH IT in the palm of my hand. I will make you deadlier; I will render you heartless. You will thirst for redemption, and your next fall will be even more laughable than the previous. Monday 26th January 2009. Don't worry, you'll get back up. Beauty can be found in even the most tragic of scenes, don't you agree? Spilled guts, splashed blood, all for nothing, nothing for all. With denial as your burial plot, I will cast you away into the night of our doings. Through the fog lies a casket, made specifically for you. I will not drive any nails into it; I will not bolt it shut. I want everyone to see the beauty that I'm capable of creating. Busted lip, bruised eye, bloodied nose. Lovely, wouldn't you agree? I'll be sure to supply a mirror, or perhaps I'll just use my newly won Intercotinental belt's reflective surface. Shove it in their face. Show them who you are. And once you lift yourself from your coffin, I ask that you take every breathe with pleasure, because I assure you, one day I’ll be the one taking them away... With even greater pleasure. Pleasure After Pain. My beautiful liar. Eyes slowly opening, paired with the silver locked drapery. I will rid you of your beauty, for we've played this game too long. Cat and mouse - I've only yet to digest you. An acid bath awaits - with no chance to cleanse yourself and come correct. You've wronged me for far too long. Final judgment. I cast you away in the light. Undoing, unraveling, a ragged fiber to my rich touch. The full frontal. A cascade of mirrors. Nowhere to go. What have you come for? What do you wish to find here? A weak man, easily toppled? Another fight of epic proportion? It's all we're capable of. Though, the unfair exchange does not allow me to lay with a smile. I give you everything; you give me everything, and yet, I'm empty. I've found that your everything is not enough. And with a swift motion of force, a mirror is shattered. You owe me, Billy. You had the advantage; you came from the future to face your newly found past. I fought that night with a goal. My undying greed for gold - another title, another blanket. Somewhere to hide. Another name gained, one that went forgotten. A saddening process I must endure, but a life I've chosen to live. One in which I will find absolution through death. False prophets encountered along the way. They preach; they fail to deliver. Their almost cannot match my certain. They fall, I walk on. The truth is exposed, your great crusade is only a fraud. A viper striking at which that is not truly there. It just looks funny. You..you build up to that final hoped instance, the venom clearly visible, a certain cocked back deathblow. If only...your prey was there. A forceful push, the shattering domino effect. I pity you and what you stand for. Even through defeat you are ignorant to what I have told you. It's not about death, darkness, or dreams. It's about the light, the truth, about anteing up as a man, a fucking man, something you have no clue about. There's no name for what I bring to table, but understand that I am only a man. And yet I earned this chance at your gold how? By becoming lesser of a man? No. I proved that I could do as I please, and if it wasn’t for that meddling whore Paige, I would have won cleanly last week. But management was smart. The choice is, yet again, presented to the man you deem unworthy. They have me surrounded with people who wish they were the best. They surround me with imbeciles whose only talents lie on a physical plane. They surround me with such fools, such charlatans, such poorly dressed adults that I cannot help but insult them straightforward. I cannot help but throw mean words their way, for it is all that they deserve. Like scraps that a dog would have gotten in the medieval times, these fools receive the lesser of my words… They shatter, and shatter 'till they are no more. Am I angry? You might deduce from this speech that I am. But the truth of the matter is that I am not. I may be frustrated a bit, but for no reasons known to anyone else but me. If I am frustrated all will be worked out in the brawl. If I am not, it will come to me. A fight is a fight, even when it is against others, even when it is against yourself. And that is the greatest fight of all. That is the most difficult fight to fight. We are its greatest survivors, its greatest pawn soldiers. We fight ourselves and if we do not win, we undergo changes. We undergo changes even if we do win. But you... You've lost. Unstrapped gold. Tossed aside. Gone. I will leave no room for change. I'll occupy all your opened doors. Your comfort zone will be rendered as a death zone. Billy, I'm tired. Your debt lingers, and you'd toy with me this way. You'd smile in my face - you'd swear you've got my number, every move matched. No--- No...NO! NEVER! You owe me too much to be considered as an object of importance on this road. Your debt lingers, Billy... And on the 26th, the man comes to collect. Eyes closed again.
