My 15 Minutes Are Up...But I got more to say.

Apr 23 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A pitch-black scene captivates the entire camera before a sudden, yet faint knock is heard. A few moments later, there is another knock, and the scene now comes to full color. The scene that comes into focus is a busy one; assistants drinking their early morning coffee in one hand, holding several important papers in the other. The one man that sticks out, as he has in his entire career, is Brian Michaels. Michaels is wearing his Burger King uniform, which is subject to several chuckles around the MWE Headquarters International in Paris, France. He rolls his eyes and continues to knock at the door as former Chairman and still-present Owner Richard Covell opens it. Covell gives Michaels a grin, but Michaels doesn’t return it. He looks on with a death glare and steps into the office aggressively. Covell continues to give a smirk, before closing the door. The camera follows both men into the room, and explores the features. It’s virtually empty, as Covell is expected to take a leave of absence back to the states soon. The only thing that remains in the room is his large brown desk and his framed picture of MWE’s first ever Crowning Glory. There is a bit of tension between “former” father and son, as Michaels snarls at Covell as soon as the door closes. Michaels: Do you think taking away my title shots are going to make me any less of a man? The question startles Covell, as he was expecting more anger than an abrupt question. He doesn’t really know how to answer it, and begins to feel around his large brown desk, hoping that it might find an arrogant answer for him. Covell: You want to know why I took away your title shots? I love to watch you squeal. I love to see Brian Michaels being taken down a peg, and being forced to work as a mid-carder. Michaels, we all know that you want all the attention. You always have. But face facts, kid, you’re not the old Brian Michaels. You can try your damndest to try and change your gimmick but at the end of the day, you’re just like D.C; another has-been who MWE uses as a cash cow. With this, he silences Michaels for a few seconds. The now-pulsating tension in the room nearly climaxes with Michaels taking a few sudden, yet prompt steps towards Covell. He speaks in a low, chilling voice, as Covell gleams at him right back. Michaels: So, we’re all part of your master plan to keep MWE afloat. That’s how it’s always been, company-first, right? Let me ask you something, who was Main Event during MWE’s weakest point? The elder man smirks, easily knowing the answer, and fully believing Michaels is just setting himself up for the inevitable. He purses his lips, and speaks slowly yet gracefully. Covell: Brian Michaels. Michaels nods his head, knowing it was indeed, him, who was blamed for MWE’s shortcomings at a time when only ten men were on the roster. Michaels: That’s right. Who had to put up with all the shit for MWE’s failures during that time? The Champion. Who was the Champion, Rich? Covell again smirks, knowing full well that Michaels was the man. He is about to speak with an arrogance that would send even Kanye West crying foul, before Michaels interrupts with a cold, bitter, response. Michaels: Me. So I sat there, working the main event every night, only to come back and listen to people talk down on me. They told me I couldn’t get the job done, that a new leader was needed. They told me I was a failure, another guy just destined to fall into the drugs circle that every retired wrestler eventually falls to. Michaels: But I fought. I ignored the politics talk, and I ignored when people just, like, YOU… Michaels’ intensity level raises suddenly, much like a spike on Hawk’s shoulder pads. He goes to speak again, before Covell interrupts now, putting his finger in Michaels’ eye, shaking with fear and intimidation. Covell: You know how hard it is to take you seriously when you’re wearing a Burger King uniform? I mean, pardon me stopping your babyface tirade entirely, but you’re not getting your Title shot. You’re not getting a Title shot, because you don’t DESERVE IT. You are NOT the best anymore, you are NOT Mr.MWE and until you get that through your THICK-ASS SKULL, you will continue to be a shell of your former self…BOOM, ROASTED, NOW GET THE FUCK OUT OF MY OFFICE. Covell immediately pushes Michaels back, wanting the effectiveness of his words burn even worse than he thought. Michaels is pushed out of the office and the door is slammed, shut. Kind of like his window of opportunity. See, because despite what Michaels would tell people, he really wanted a World Title shot. He wanted that opportunity to at least prove to people, that he could take on MWE’s best in David Whitman. That, despite their friendship, the jealousy is not rooted so far inside that he can hide it. There were awkward moments during Mayhem Mafia’s reunion party. There was the instance where Michaels was at the bar alone, where the MWE Undisputed Championship rested. The part of Michaels that just wanted to say “Fuck you” to everyone at the party and grab the belt was dying to come out. But he couldn’t. He had a match, with David Whitman as his partner. He couldn’t look at the belt. He just couldn’t. It was like an ex-girlfriend dating this really successful guy. You know you got your turn, but you just want another ride. Just one more, he kept thinking to himself. But alas, it was not meant to be. After all, he literally couldn’t concentrate on the World Championship. Not when he was about to face the two top heels in MWE. There is a long black fade as the promo continues, albeit with a much more “WTF happened to me?” kind of emotion flowing. Not just that, Michaels has arrived at his place of peace. His place of comfort; The wrestling ring. Unlike his life, the squared circle never changed. It was always a place of energy, where wrestlers lived vicariously through the fans. It was always a place where blood ran. Whether it was the blue, evil blood of the heels, or the red, gritty blood of the faces, it never changed. Maybe that’s what Michaels was afraid of; change. TIME: 07:00 AM – Palais Omnisports Bercy How ironic is it that Pala is in the name of the arena? I mean, think about it. Pala is the dude who beat Michaels for his first MWE Championship, and created the downfall of Brian Michaels. Now, damn near two-years later, Brian Michaels is here; taking the long walk down the entrance ramp of the soccer field in Paris. He feels odd here, but knows that this is his place. The MWE production workers are doing their jobs, setting up the equipment needed for the Monday Night Mayhem. The empty bleachers don’t seem to faze him at all, as he slides into the ring in his ring attire, and lies on his back, looking at the clouds. There is no top on the stadium, which allows him to see the blue and white swirls above him. He doesn’t have to speak; the scene speaks for itself. Who woulda thought that Brian Michaels, once MWE’s most egotistical superstar could be taken this low? He seemingly talks to himself, as the workers carrying equipment around him seem to become another chapter in his life’s memory museum. Michaels: How ironic is it, that what Rich told me, is probably the same thing my opponents are gonna say about me? As he lies there, the clouds slowly begin forming. He mutters “ah, shit” to himself, before hearing pitter-patters come near him, closer and closer. He believes for a second that it is rain hitting the mat, before looking to his side and seeing his baby brother, Randy Covell, dressed in a brown suit and tie, looking very distinguished for a 23 year old. However, his facial features do not give off that same aura. Instead, his stitched-up face reveals several cuts and bruises, and his green eyes stare at his older brother in disgust. Randy Covell: Where the hell…were you? Immediately, Michaels remembers exactly what Covell is talking about. He repositions himself on the mat to face his younger brother eye-to-eye before looking on with a puzzled face. Michaels: What are you talking about? With this, Covell immediately snaps and kicks Michaels in the back of the head. Covell: You greedy son of a bitch. How could you leave me in the cold and not do shit about it? You left your own friggin’ brother to be beaten by GM and then you shrugged it off like it was nothing? I thought you changed…you’re still the same selfish prick you were your whole life… As Covell leans back, he spits in the face of his older brother. Michaels, looking defeated, takes it only because he knows he deserved it. He sits there, spit all over his face, and lying down in what can be called his only safe haven, Michaels sighs. He looks up at the clouds once more, but they form into little driplets of rain, which then turn into large drops. The pitter-patter hit the ring as all the workers, and Randy leave for cover inside the locker room. Not Brian. Nah, the rain didn’t bother him. In fact, he used to lie in the rain, dreaming about wrestling for a promotion and being one of the top stars, thinking of facing such legends as Ace Cannon from WZCW, Main Eventers from SFW, and others. As he takes another deep sigh, he allows the rain, which is now pounding harder, hit his face and dirty-blonde hair repeatedly. Michaels: Believe it or not, this is my sanctuary. I’d rather be here; soaking wet, over watching Mason or Cannon’s promo. He closes his eyes, not wanting to let the rain get into his green eyes filled with hope, yet experience. These eyes have seen many things. Sure, some of those things probably include porn, BUT…he has also seen many wrestlers come and go. Yes, even the wrestlers who claim to be “perfect”. Michaels: Why would I rather be here, you ask? It’s because I already know what they’re going to say. He rolls his eyes, before closing them again quickly. He now puts both hands under his head and takes a deep breath, letting the rain dictate his emotion and even his heartbeat. It is a slow, yet steady beat, and his emotion is vibrating with it. He even speaks with a mocking tone. Michaels: Let me guess guys…Michaels uses politics. Michaels is old. Michaels isn’t the same. Michaels ain’t nothing without D.C… Michaels ain’t nothing without Richard Covell. Michaels has never beat me. Michaels again rolls his eyes, showing his disgust for lack of creativity at MWE these days. He uses his right hand and moves it up and down as if to mock both Ace Cannon and Mason Resoan. Michaels: I swear to GOD if someone mentions that again, I WILL SMACK THEM SO FUCKIN’ HARD THEIR ID PICTURES ARE GONNA GO TO A MY CHEMICAL ROMANCE CONCERT AND ASK FOR FREE RAZORBLADES. TAKE, THAT, MOTHER, FUCK, ERS! Totally random, right? But I digress. He uses emphasis on each word, spreading out the final sentence in little bursts. His once calm demeanor is now psycho and a bit gritty at the same time, as he gets onto his knees and flips the camera off. Michaels: It has been too…damn…long…since I got to rant. The rain’s tempo increases smoothly, as Michaels begins to take off his BK uniform, See-You-In-My-Nightmares style. Michaels: MASON RESOAN? PERFECT? The only thing perfect about Mason Resoan is how he can accurately figure out where to take a piss at…Mason, get your popcorn ready because I’m about to shit all over you… He finishes this sentence in a low, menacing tone, before going sarcastic once more, in true Brian Michaels fashion. The polo shirt is now off, and the black slacks are halfway down, revealing Michaels’ red boxers. Michaels: AND YES, RYAN ADAMS, YOU ARE CO-RRECT SIR…IT IS OFFICIAL…MASON RESOAN, SHIT, IS ABOUT TO HIT THE FAN. The slacks are now off, and it is official: The setting? Try Brian Michaels flapping his arms wildly like a chicken, wearing only boxers, in the middle of a ring, at a soccer stadium, with no roof. Oh, and it’s raining. Try that for imagery. KONICHIWA, BITCHES! …Wrong country. Michaels: Mason Resoan…whether you like it or not, you are MWE’s new Brian Michaels. You can try and differ yourself from me all you want, but the fact remains, you are this generation’s Brian Michaels. Cocky, brash, and successful, you are everything I was. He sighs once more, thinking back to his deep, rooted past. His famous confrontations with legit MWE Hall of Contenders such as Aiden Copane and Windy Bridges come to mind, along with the feud of his life, against D.C. just four months ago. Michaels: But what YOU DON’T KNOW…is that everything I’m not…made me everything I am. This phrase, is almost like a summary of Michaels’ career. He says it with a somber tone, knowing that, despite how others may take it, it is the truth. Everything he isn’t, politicker, old, egotistical, has made him everything he is, or will be known for. Michaels: I’m not a politicker…yet I’m labeled one. I am a politicker, to you. The politics jokes got old quickly for him. Even the biggest smarks don’t realize what it’s like to have their accomplishments doubted, and questioned. It wasn’t by a large amount of people, or a majority by any means, but despite who said it doesn’t matter; the fact that it was said pains him the most. It hurts him to know that the company he gave so much to, thinks of him as just another wrestler. Michaels: I’m not old…I’m only 28…yet you’d call bull and I’d be 48 in your mind. I am old, to you. Michaels points at his chest, booming with pride despite the BK uniform laying directly to his left. He ignores it and continues to speak with shame at the way he’s been labeled in the past. Michaels: For that reason, you ARE me. It might sound freaky…but back in June of 2007, I was just like you. A few nice wins under my belt…I thought I was hot. I thought I was, dare I say…perfect. Again, the past is mentioned. The rain pours on, making the scene that much more dramatic as the 2007 Brian Michaels and 2009 Mason Resoan almost come to a full circle. Full Circle? Like Grand Mystique and Maxximillion Mustafa? It couldn’t be. Michaels: For that one second…when I thought I was friggin’ invincible…it was the same second I crumbled. Michaels: Somehow…you came in to the picture. I saw potential in you, kid…but just like I told you last time we faced…I haven’t taught you one thing. He holds up one, fateful finger. He told Mason this the last time they met, but Mason no longer bought into his words. Mason was now his own man, with no regard or acknowledgement of Michaels in his past. He gives Mason the same look he did the last time he tried explaining it to him. Michaels: It’s about overcoming the odds. See, Mason, you can come around and talk circles around me for as long as you want, and it’s true…you’re a good talker. You have the accomplishments to back up your smack, and I respect that. He looks on, grinning like an esteemed trainer. He feels as if he has something to do with Mason's success in MWE, but his praise quickly turns into an attack on his character. Michaels: But the arrogance you have? This, perfection, talk you’re spitting? It’s too much. If you were undefeated in MWE, you could claim to be perfect. But you’re not. Who have you lost to again? OH yeah…David Whitman…my tag team partner. The man I am honored to call my friend…forget what everyone says about me wanting the World Championship…I respect the hell out of David Whitman. Believe me or not, I’ve said it once and I’ll say it again, David Whitman is the very best MWE has to offer, night in and night out…Not one person has given the amount of effort he has…he is the Undisputed Champion for a reason. He reaches down and goes into his slacks. He reaches into it and feels around, before picking up a keychain. It has the Mayhem Mafia logo on it, with the letters MM engraved on the back. He cherishes it in his hand, before dropping it. Michaels: But…back to Mason. You see, Mason is perfect. He’s 9-2. He’s a former MWE Champion. He was my protégé, my student, my apprentice. I saw potential in you, out of a crop of new superstars, and I took you under my wing….and just like everyone else, you stabbed me in the back. But it’s not your fault. Michaels bows his head and points a finger at his own chest. He now fully believes that Mason Resoan was just another rookie before Michaels gave him all the knowledge he had at the time. He is still angry that Mason doesn’t give him credit. Michaels: No…it’s my fault for giving you that killer instinct, Mason…it’s my fault. You can hype yourself and jizz all over your tights all you want about how you’re 2-0 against me, but at the end of the day…do you really think you’re going to win? Michaels: I mean, I’ve seen you hate people before. You’ve disliked Aiden Copane. You’ve disliked me, and you’ve disliked David Whitman. But Ace Cannon, you REALLY hate him. Think. You’d rather be putting nails down his throat than tagging with him…you know why Mason? You can’t stand the sight of seeing someone else have their glory. You can’t stand seeing the sight of Ace Cannon being able to do something you couldn’t do, and that is beat our World Champion… He smirks, knowing how much it angers Mason to have to work with others. He’s always been an independent, and even rejected Michaels’ help at first back in 2007. Michaels: So before you give us your typical “I’m perfect” rant and your generic “I’m Better Than Brian Michaels Because” bit…I want you to think long and hard…because what I know…is you will get my best. Michaels: And when I say my best, I don’t mean my best like I did against D.C…no, my friend, you are on an entirely different level. Mason, I will scratch, crawl, climb, dig, shit, dodge, duck, dip, dive AND DODGE AGAIN, to win this match because Mason Resoan, you bet your ASS, I will FUCK YOU UP…come Monday. You better kiss the Jesus piece…because I just blessed you with the best goddamn promo you have ever seen, and there is no…denying…that. There’s an ego for your ass…so if you want to get into the ego debate… Michaels pretends to “pop his collar”, before giving his old-school smirk. He walks near the hard-camera ropes and points at the camera, as he is now speaking directly to Mason himself. Michaels: I am Brian Michaels, and you are Mason Resoan. I’m a three-time MWE Champion and you’re a one-time MWE Champion. I can make a better promo off the top of my head than you can think of in an hour, and I am the shit, period. I find it funny that one year ago, I told you that you had potential. But do you know what potential really means? Michaels: It means you haven’t done anything yet… In a brief, split second, Michaels grabs his boxers. He gets “that feeling” and immediately stops talking. Michaels: And as much as I’d really like to take a piss right now, I can’t. So, I gotta talk about Ace Cannon. The woman-abuser. You know, Ace kind of reminds me of that movie character who gets drunk and beats up his wife all the time. No one really likes him anyway… Michaels: Now all Ace Cannon has to do is punt a few chicks, bang Paige, and be the boss’ son to get a title shot? Michaels simulates what he believes to be Ace Cannon’s career in MWE. He takes a few steps back and punts the air, then gets down on his side and begins thrusting the air as well, appearing to have imaginary sex with Paige. After that, he gets on his knees much like a sinner who is trying to make up for their sins and begs to his invisible father. Michaels: Yeah, I went there. See, Cannon’s gonna talk to you about how I’m US Title material at best, and how I feel right at home in the mid-card. The truth is, what has Ace Cannon done at MWE? I mean, yeah, he beat David Whitman BY INTERFERENCE, and he slipped out in a match against me…congratulations. He’s done it all…at Rev-Pro. He was a beast…at EW. His face turns red, trying to hold back the laughter, before resuming his promo. Michaels: Ace Cannon is the self-proclaimed leader of Vanguard and he’s the top heel at MWE today, no denying that. Cannon can bitch all he wants about how I was Covell’s spoiled kid and how he was ignored all through his childhood, and the reason he hasn’t won a Championship is because he hasn’t gotten a shot…but let’s look at facts. Fact, Ace Cannon is good at talking stuff…congratulations…you want a cookie? He chuckles to himself, thinking of the old-school D.C. joke, before gathering himself once more. The rain begins to stop slowly. Michaels: Let me put it in a language Cannon will understand… Michaels beats his chest with both of his hands clenched in fists, much like an ape would. His eyebrows furrow, and he puts on a sarcastic angry face. Michaels: Brian Michaels no like Ace Cannon. Remember that third-person bit you had going on? You had some fans thinking you were from another planet or some stuff like that. I just say…if Ace Cannon really IS superhuman…then it’s going to take that much of an effort to bring his sick ass down. I don’t care if I have to punch the living daylights out of you and you’re still laughing, I will get mine. Believe that, Ace…I will get mine. As Michaels stands around, the rain has now completely stopped. The sweet mist makes the mat slippery and Michaels feels like an ice skater on ice. This is his spot. No Burger King and no Bars can change or modify that. This is home. The way Michaels talks, it bounces off everywhere; the titantron and speakers, and almost like a God, the production workers almost come out just to listen to him. Okay, so maybe I’m being a little egotistical. Michaels: People grow. Wrestlers grow. I went through a stage, D.C. has gone through a stage, David Whitman has gone through a state. Heck, even Mason Resoan has gone through a stage. But Cannon’s stayed the same. That’s the part about him that makes me wonder if he even is just like us…but like I said, if he isn’t…it’s going to the end of my LIFE before I let Ace Cannon beat me again. This match means THAT much to me…and Cannon knows that. Michaels says this with pure confidence, much like how he said Blood Fuze was going to be a force to be reckoned with in MWE last week. Cannon and Michaels both know that their match went to the limit, and that talent-wise, they are both virtually even. It will be a battle of wits, and Michaels’ green eyes know this. Michaels: Ace Cannon can take his self-proclaimed accomplishments and shove them. As far as I’m concerned, he hasn’t done enough to earn my respect, and that’s saying something. Michaels: Why’s that? Because I give out respect about as much as Paige gives out sex…I mean, damn Cannon, at this point, a lapdance from Paige would be a blessing. Michaels holds his ribs, laughing in pain as he slips on the mat and falls on his ass. The embarrassing stumble does not seem to affect him as he just gets back and keeps laughing. Michaels: Forget the wrestling accomplishments…Ace Cannon will be known as the man who turns straight women, LESBIAN! If that’s too emo for you, or not “in your face” enough, then take the Rev-Pro Title you probably have enshrined at your house, and look at it real good because that’s the last World Title you’re gonna hold… Amazingly enough, Michaels is able to transition from comedy gestures with Paige to talking about the now-defunct Rev-Pro Championship. Unpredictable as always, Michaels gives a bit of a growl before calming down a tone. Michaels: So whether it’s being perfect…or being a psycho wanting gold…I am human. I might not be the nicest guy, or the most humble, and I am working on that. I don’t want gold, and I don’t beat up my girlfriend. He takes his left hand and points at the MWE Titantron, which shows a two-sided picture. One side shows a photo shopped picture of Chris Brown smacking Rihanna, and the other side is Ace Cannon yelling at Lindsey, with her head buried into a pillow. Michaels: Chris Brown…Ace Cannon…whatever you want to be called…get ready. I’m not 110 pounds and I ain’t 5’5. I’m a MAN, damnit! It’s like that age-old cry…pick on someone your own size, motherfucka. He puts his right hand on top of his head, as if to motion that they are the same size and that Cannon is a bit of a weakling for picking fights with women. Michaels: I don’t lead Vanguard with an iron fist and use the stable as my way to bitch about management…and I’m not perfect. He shakes his head, climbing up the turnbuckle and throwing his arms out in the air, much like how he did when he won the MWE Championship. He feels the the energy and excitement in the arena, despite no fans being there. Mayhem Mafia will win Monday night. He can feel it. Michaels: But come Monday…with all due respect to Curt Hennig and Mason Resoan…I will try my best to BE perfect…they want the old Brian Michaels? I will BE the old Brian Michaels and give my best along with David Whitman…we’ll prove, there are none better than the Mayhem Mafia. Bar none. He climbs down the turnbuckle, as two jobbers walk down the ramp quickly and begin working out in the middle of the ring. Michaels smirks and remembers when he was their age, just trying to find a job. He slides out of the ring, respectfully, and hops over the barricade, and into the bleachers. He watches the match unfold like the experienced veteran he is. In his boxers. Michaels: Sometimes life knocks you down...there are going to be people who are bitter, and don't want to see you succeed. They tell you, that you aren't who you think you are...but I know who I am...and nobody can take that from me. Full Circle, indeed. END.
