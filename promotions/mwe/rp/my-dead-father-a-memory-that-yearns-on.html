My dead father, a memory that yearns on; RP#2(better)

Aug 15 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
*The rain lightly begins to fall as Sinc is seen kneeling in front of his father’s grave early in the morning at the Alexander Valley Cemetery. A camera man is silently filming him as Sinc looks a bit uncomfortable but he allows the filming for the soul purpose of his fans seeing the true side of him.* Sinc: Oh dad….where do I start…its been one hell of a while hasn’t it? I miss ya pops. You taught me everything I know. As a kid I was always the one picked on at school. Then when I come home….your right there….but instead of hugging me and being a softie…you slap me and say MAN UP! MAN UP SON! FIGHT YOUR FEARS! And ever since I have tried being the man you wanted rather than your little boy. *Sinc stops to take a quick breath as the rain continues tapping the shoulders of his trenchcoat*| Sinc: I got a tag match comin up….and you know its kinda funny pops. You always told me I was funny…yet you believed I had the spirit and the fire for wrestling. Most of all….in the end….you always believed in me. Never once did you doubt me or my ability. Even my skill. You always knew I’d make it somehow, someway. You knew I was capable. And for that I brought you this to kinda lighten up your grave. *Sinc takes something out of his trench coat pocket. It is a small rubber chicken* Sinc: Just because you always hated my jokes that’s why. Ironic isn’t it….to hate something that is forced upon you…I mean your de…..your…dead…You can’t…you… *Sinc sighs deeply trying to withhold the tears* Sinc: You can’t stop me now can you dad….can’t stop me from cracking all the jokes in the world….this chicken proves it…Like that old annoying Queen song goes…you can’t stop me now….and yes you always hated that song…well history repeats itself like you always told me aside from some other more pointless things that you blurted out mostly when you were drunk as a mofo. *Sinc manages a slight laugh* Sinc: When I was interviewed by Shawn a few days ago I mentioned you pops…I mentioned you when you snapped me out of my blank state at the bar that day…the moment that beer bottle hit my head I knew you were only helping me….hell you were boosting the fuckin crowd for me man….to this day I appreciate that still….through my high school years I have always been the class clown, the funny man. I made lots of friends and then when I think I had it made…when I think a had it all you shook me, shook me hard and reminded me I don’t have it all….I simply just have it better than most… *Sinc pauses thinking of what else to say….a few moments of recollecting the memories he had with his father. The camera guy zooms on him for those solid moments of silence not once had Sinc cracked a purposeful joke or even glanced at the camera for himself or the people* Sinc: One day I am gonna join you up there….or maybe for those few bad days you had…DOWN there….and I don’t mean the way you always got down on mom….HA…what a frightening sight when I saw you and mom dry humpin each other as a kid….sorry…couldn’t resist at least one gesture to ya pops… *Sinc flashed a smile just as quickly as it faded away* Sinc: Sorry sometimes I can’t control myself…at one point I drank so much I felt like my head wasn’t even connected to my body…just because I remembered when you gave me MY first drink…it was the NASTIEST thing I ever tasted…but look at me now dad…look at your son…your…only…son…drinking like crazy and lovin the feelin! *Sinc then stands up and stares blankly at the grave for a second* Sinc: I feel like I failed you pops….I never won the gold for the family….I never won the gold for anyone….and for that I am truly sorry…..you know one of my opponents is a champion…Tag Team, US, and even the X TV title holder at one point. But you always taught me to never back down against others who are higher than me on a professional level. But at the same time you said I had the heart to climb the worst and land on the best. And that is exactly what I plan to do….just for you… *Sinc reaches into his coat in the chest area and brings out a small golden locket. It has his father and him at the shore fishing* Sinc: Yea…we caught the big one there….I remember that day crystal clear. SON DON’T STUFF YOUR HAND IN ITS MOUTH! I couldn’t stop laughing and neither could you…. *Sinc grabs the locket and closes it. He kisses it and puts it back into his coat. He kneels in front of the grave again* Sinc: Ill cya later buddy….its been fun….and remember….don’t let mom find out…heh… *Sinc laughs at another memory as he gets up about to walk away when he turns around he sees his mother walking towards him wearing a long black dress and a trench coat like Sinc’s. The camera snaps around and focuses on her for a few moments as Sinc walks up to her and gives her a hug* Jenna Mercier: I’ve been so worried about you….seeing as it was in the morning one year after your father died I sort of guessed you’d be here….you never answer your phone….you worry me to an awful death Sinclair Christopher Mercier…. *Sinc nods in sorrow* Sinc: Ma I know….I’m sorry…I have been so busy with my career I havn’t had the time to see or talk to you…MWE is a pain on my schedule…. *Jenna begins crying* Jenna Mercier: You have no idea how HARD it is to see you get hurt out there….. Sinc: Ma don’t cry…come on mom… *Sinc hugs her again but tighter. The camera zooms on them for a few seconds* Jenna Mercier: Even though your father never wanted for you to be a comedian and tell your filthy jokes I always thought that would be better for you….but I also knew you had this UNNATURAL obsession with wrestling getting yourself hurt and all….so I refused to stop you….as long as your father was by your side making sure you were safe I was ok with it…. *The rain begins slowing down* Sinc: I would always be ok mom….with or without him I would always be fine….he was just there for moral support….the old douche couldn’t even stand half the time. He always brought his damned beach chair at every practice and all my matches…. *Jenna laughs with Sinc* Jenna Mercier: Hahaha….you and your father always knew how to make me laugh….half the time I couldn’t even tell you two apart….you know when he died….when…when he died…. *She bursts into tears again as Sinc rubs her shoulder to try and comfort her* Sinc: What ma….please…tell me…. Jenna Mercier: When your father died in that coma a part of me died that day….and every time I looked at you especially when you were there by his bedside I always saw him….NOTHING but him….It broke my heart….it broke my damn heart son…. *Sinc sighs* Sinc: I know…..you think you were the only one effected…I was too….I could not stand seeing my father AND my best friend die in front of me…the person who taught me everything….he was the Aviator not me. Jenna Mercier: I know he was the role model for you….he always was….you were like his shadow….now you are his light… *Sinc nods getting kind of sad hearing his mom talk like that* Jenna Mercier: Well…..its true….he always made sure he never hid anything from you…he always made sure you knew everything about him… Sinc: Yea, I guess pops must be listening right….I am sure he knows more about me even now than I know about myself. Jenna Mercier: No matter how you look at it honey….he’s always going to be listening some how… Sinc: I guess your right….he always made sure he was involved in every conversation… *Sinc then looks behind him and notices the camera….he had totally forgot about it, he continues ignoring it and turning his attention back to his mother, Not feeling like talking to the people at the moment* Jenna Mercier: You aren’t going to kill yourself Monday are you? Sinc: Nah ma…but I do got a tag match next week….but it is nothing to worry about….I don’t want you worrying about me… Jenna Mercier: Are you crazy? I will ALWAYS worry about you. If anything happened to you I don’t know what I’d do… Sinc: Don’t dare think that way mom…please…when you do that it loses my chi for my match heh… *Jenna smacks Sinc lightly across the face* Jenna Mercier: Don’t be cocky to me…especially in front of your fathers grave….I’m sorry Sinc….I just….don’t want to see you in the hospital you already know this…. Sinc: I know I know…. Jenna Mercier: I don’t want to see my baby in a puddle of blood somewhere…never to see me again…with all those steroid rumors and wresters dying out there things are getting worse… Sinc: Ma….please…I am here now aren’t I…respecting my fathers wishes….giving him my care…and I will do the same for you mom….now before I grow old can we PLEASE go get something to eat…all that moanin and hollerin is getting me hungry. *Jenna chuckles a bit* Jenna Mercier: Alright son….lets go grab a bite to eat… Sinc: How about we go kill our arteries at Mickey D’s? Jenna Mercier: No….a nice home cooked meal sounds nice… Sinc: oh man…ma!!! Jenna Mercier: What? I am not going to let you ruin your body at such a place. Sinc: It’s not ruining my body its relaxing…letting loose…. Jenna Mercier: Well whatever it may be when your with your MOTHER it is not happening. Sinc: Well me and pops always did that after practice… *Sinc stops and folds his arms looking away in disappointment* Jenna Mercier: Well I am not your father…are we really going to argue about what we are going to eat? Sinc: Ma I am just jokin with ya….lets do what you want to do…. *Sinc’s mother suddenly stops and looks into the sky in wonder* Sinc: You ok ma? Jenna Mercier: No….its just….too many things are going through my head….too many memories….too much pain….even trying to be funny makes me breakdown again….I can’t help it…its just too hard… *Sinc hugs his mom again* Sinc: Just remember….no matter what…I will always be here for you….forget about all your worries….all your troubles….and as much as it hurts…forget about dad for a while… *Sinc let go as his mother is once again in tears* Sinc: Here… *Sinc pulls out two tickets to Hawaii* Sinc: This was going to be for me and a lady friend of mine but you deserve it more….go with someone you can have fun with…go with Aunt Lissa….or one of your friends from work… *Jenna hugs her son again thankful for him and after a few moments broke away and wiped the tears from her face* Jenna Mercier: Son I want to go with you…I could not possibly accept this unless it was with you… Sinc: You know I am far too busy….I can’t… *His mom nods slowly in sorrow* Jenna Mercier: I understand…..just be safe ok? Sinc: I will mom…promise…now lets go home… *The rain stops as the sun suddenly, slowly began eroding over the ridge hill of the valley and Sinc and his mother are seen walking away as the camera slowly turned to Sinc‘s father‘s grave…and then begins to slowly fade with the sounds of birds chirping and the wind nipping in the air* E N D.
