Self-Destruct Sequence; Tex's CRAPTASTIC Tribulation RP

May 22 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
-- Thursday, May 21, 2009 -- Los Angeles, California -- Ventura Freeway -- 11:18 PM Sick. Twisted. Demented. All of these words can be used to describe one seemingly unstoppable group in this business. Three men each with their own wants and goals have bound together in a time of need for the MWE. This group is known simply as the Vanguard, a once harmless word that has been changed forever due to the actions of these three men. The Vanguard now strikes fear into the hearts of all those who cross them. However as our scene opens this elite group has not joined together tonight, and Ace Cannon is a wreck. His affliction shirt tattered on his chest he holds the neck of a bottle of Jack Daniels in one hand ... and the wheel to his rental Porsche in the other. Ace Cannon: "Jus' who the f*** does that little c*** think she is ..." Still fuming from his argument with Paige from earlier Ace takes a swig from the nearly empty 40 and lowers his foot a little further down on the gas pedal forcing the red car to take itself to it's limits. The needle on the speedometer slowly climbing past the one hundred miles per hour mark. Ace Cannon: "Who in their right mind would choose that whore Lindsey over the man that should be the nex' champ!?" As he continues to roar down the highway headlights from the passing cars nearly blind him, causing his already iffy driving to become worse. Swerving all over the two lanes on his side of the road nearly finding himself in the ditch, but Cannon doesn't care .. He's in one of his rampages again as he throws to bottle of Jack back to his lips gulping back the last of the copper liquid as the car throws itself to the side of the road before Ace spins the wheel just enough to find himself traveling down the road in a fairly safe direction. Ace Cannon: "I mean come on, I'm the only person in this f***ing place that has the balls to say things the way I see 'em .. But instead I get suspended for that, f*** that little bitch Malibu! I EARNED my chance to fight Whitman!" With that Cannon throws the empty bottle out of the opened driver side window and listens to the shatter of glass under the roar of the high powered engine of the Porsche. Ace Cannon: "There's gotta be a f***ing conspiracy to ruin my life ... All this bullshit happens at the same ti-" And the "bullshit" only continues to pour onto Cannon as he is interrupted by the sound the blaring sound of a police siren. The red and blue lights flashing in his rear view mirror as the familiar white of a cop car closes in on Cannon and his rental car. Ace Cannon: "F*** me." "You in the red Porsche .. Pull over, this is the LAPD!" The nearly muffled voice of the Italian policeman is enough to to make Cannon throw his foot down on his breaks and pull over to the curb, coming to a stop as the white police car pulls up behind him slowly, the flashing red and white lights still blinding Cannon as he lets his head hit the wheel in front of him. A few muffled drunken words escaping his mouth as the sound of the slamming car door can be heard from behind him. If you've ever been pulled over you know that just listening to those footsteps as the cop walks towards your door can be even worse then what your about to have happen to yourself. It's no different this time at least that's how it seems, the mere anticipation is nearly killing Cannon as he stares out the windshield into the dark of the night. However this is much different then your regular speeding ticket and that becomes apparent as a flashlight is pointed into the car, followed by the barrel of a high powered hand gun. Police Officer: "Get out of the car and put your hands behind your dirt bag!" Slowly Cannon reaches for the door handle, cautiously getting out of the car as he attempts to hide his drunken stance. Failing miserably while he stumbles to even stand out side of the car as the pistol of the police officer points towards his chest. Ace nearly falls forward as he takes his hand away from the red paint on his rental and raises both his arms to place his hands behind his head. Ace Cannon: "Now officer let's be reasonable about this." Words slurred the look on his face tells the story, Cannon is actually praying for help here. Just to avoid getting shot by this angered law enforcer. Police Officer: "Get on the ground!" Ace Cannon: "You have got to be fucking kidding -" He isn't even given time to finish his sentence before the officer charges at him, tackling him to the ground from behind. As Cannon lays face first on the asphalt of the highway the officer puts his knee into his spine and reaches into his belt taking out his handcuffs as he points his gun just above his knee. Police Officer: "Do I LOOK like I'm kidding you?" Skillfully the officer latches one of the handcuffs onto Cannon's wrist with one hand, the gun still held in the other pointed toward the former top contender. The officer then latches the other side of the cuffs onto Cannon's opposite wrist and proceeds to forcefully pull him to his feet. Ace Cannon: "Do you have ANY IDEA WHO THE HELL I AM!?" The drunken Cannon is freaking out as the officer begins to lead him towards the cruiser still parked behind the Porsche. Lights and sirens blaring at full force. Police Officer: "I don't know, and I don't care ... Now you have the right to remain silent. I suggest you use it." Hands held behind his back against his will Cannon is, for the first time in his life, at the mercy of someone else. It was a hard pill to swallow, going from being the most feared superstar in the world, to knowing that the back seat of a cop car, and a dim, cool jail cell were now the only certain things in your future. -------------------------------------------------- Why does it always seem like the most devoted factions are always the ones to fall apart the quickest? It has always been this way, ever since I can remember. I remember vividly watching from home as the Canadian X-Change, High Society and even the Mayhem Mafia all began to have their problems, and sure enough fall apart. Now Vanguard seems to be taking the worst beating out of anything .. We have lost Paige, Ace has gone on a drunken rampage that threatens to destroy himself .. and me .. I'm starting to lose what little motivation I have left to change this company for the better. Could it be that we were wrong in assuming that this company needs to be change? No, that can't be it .. But something is out of place, I just don't know what. Ten .... Nine ... Eight ... -------------------------------------------------- -- Friday, May 22, 2009 -- Los Angeles, California -- Van Nuys Station -- 7:43 AM Dim and cold just don't seem to be enough to describe this scene as Cannon sits, now sobering up, on a freezing metal bench leaning against the cement wall behind him in a rarely seen calmness. As he sits in this dingy jail cell his mind riffles through the recent chain of events that have lead him here. The only motion in the cell is his slow breathing and occasional glance around, that is until his day dream is broken by the loud clanging of the steel bars being opened. As Cannon looks up his eyes roll over the dark blue uniform of the local police department, a color that over the last few hours he has come to loath. Yet still looking towards the door, peering towards the officer standing there Cannon looks at this rather large man with a long of disgust. His long blond hair, his holier then thou stance .. everything about him just makes Ace want to lunge across the room and tear his heart out of his chest. But he's in enough trouble as it is. "I'm guessing you're Avery Cannon?" His tone is stern, yet calm .. Even inviting, but Cannon fails to see that. He can only see one of the men responsible for putting him here and it takes everything he has to not attack him. Instead, he clenches his fists on lap and looks into the officers eyes. That familiar stare of aggression locked on his face. Ace Cannon: "It depends who's asking." His attempt to change the officer's mind about continuing this conversation is useless, and the burly man takes a few steps into the cell shutting the large steel bars behind him as he does so. The anger boiling in Cannon is plain to see, but he keeps his cool, instead of lashing out physically he simply rolls his eyes and stares into the far corner. Ace Cannon: "I hope you realize that's the biggest mistake you have ever made." Anger still not working to the affect that he had hoped Cannon turns to look back at the officer only to see his hand extended out, looking for a handshake. The officer's face still surprisingly calm as Cannon can't help but tense up. "I'm Sergeant Sanders, and I'm going to be the officer working your case." Ace can't believe that this man is completely ignoring everything that he has said as is offering him a handshake. That is the straw that broke the camel's back, and Cannon's anger finally gets the best of him as he slaps Sander's hand away from him lunges to his feet, getting into the face of the officer. Ace Cannon: "I don't think you're f***ing listening! So clean our your ears, take your thumb out of your ass and listen to me you idiot ... I'm Ace F***ING CANNON! I'm a man that has kicked CHILDREN so hard that they're left in hospital beds .. What makes you think for a SECOND that I won't do the same to you?" Much to Cannon's surprise Sander's remains calm in this situation. This has to be the first person outside of the wrestling world that hasn't backed down from this side of Ace Cannon, but this only makes Cannon angrier .. He cocks back his fist, only to watch a smirk pop through the lips of the Sergeant. Sgt. Sanders: "Other then the fact that while you're between these bars I decide what happens to you .. And when it happens?" The MWE superstar now does that pulsing move, where you want to hit somebody but you manage to hold yourself back, you know the one. He wants to put this officer out for the count, but knows full well that the consequences are not worth that feeling of accomplishment we would get for a few moments as he watched this bastard squirm. Sgt. Sanders: "Now how about we take a seat and talk this out like respectable adults?" Sanders motions towards the still cold steel bench that sits firmly attached to the cement wall behind Cannon. Reluctantly Cannon lowers his fists and takes a look behind him to look at the place that he had called home since last night. With a shake of his head he looks back towards the officer. Taking a deep, soothing breath in he lets his lips open one more time. Ace Cannon: "Fine." -------------------------------------------------- Seven ... Six ... Look at the kinds of things we have lowered ourselves to, pick-your-poison matches where the only goal in mind is to hurt. Degrading an entire nation of people for the purpose of getting other another's skin. Tarnishing a faithful chairman's legacy by comparing him to a less than deserving champion. This was not what the Vanguard was created for ... Or maybe it is ... Maybe I'm the one that has lost sight about what this fight is really about. Maybe I'm the one that's forgotten what the Vanguard Movement was all about ... I just don't know. Maybe falling apart is what we need right now. ... Five ... Four ... -------------------------------------------------- --Friday, May 22, 2009 -- Rome, Italy -- Hotel La Residenza Suite -- 12:57 PM The shift in scenery is welcomed with opened arms as the dingy jail cell that is now home to Ace Cannon is left as nothing more than a bad taste in our mouths. Even the change in lighting is drastic as a large picture window floods this luxurious hotel room with sunlight. The bright rays seem to make the burgundy carpet glow a bright red, with the exception of a dark shadow laid across the floor. The owner of which is draped in that oh too familiar attire. Adorning a sharp black suit, his blond locks shining brightly against the sunlight stands Joesph Sharpman. Gazed locked on the beautiful Italian landscape, as though lost in a day dream. The only sound is that of a television in the background, just quite enough so that the near whisper tone of Sharpman can be heard above it. Joesph Sharpman: "What is it about me that seems to attract all of this negative attention? I'm a fair man ... I've had to endure all, if not more of the trials and tribulations that every other man in my position has had to. Yet some people still seem to think that I do not deserve the marquee that other superstars that could only dream of accomplishing the things that I have in this industry have just been handed." The next few moments are nearly silent as Sharpman stares into the horizon. Listening, waiting for an answer that will never come. He turns his head slightly so the blurred profile of his face becomes visible. Joesph Sharpman: "I am the two thousand and eight heel of the year ... I made the most incredible return in wrestling history .. And last week I made the Undisputed Champion tap out. But I am still looked at as the undeserving United States champion that I was a year ago, all the while men that have failed to even half of the things that I have in this company are given the opportunity to impress that I have been seeking since my career began." With this Joesph turns completely around, letting us see his blood red tie and the glare of anger locked across his face. Looking into the camera he once again let's his lips part to speak. Joesph Sharpman: "But apparently there is something about me that just isn't enough for the incompetent group of people we call our board of directors ... Because every time I put myself in the position to be given what I deserve they put another obstacle in my way ... They build another wall for my to break down ... And every time I do, they get more and more desperate to keep me away from that brass ring, even to the point where they throw out distractions." Eyes still focused into the camera Joesph raises his right hand, now pointing towards us as he continues his tirade. Joesph Sharpman: "Which brings me to you two .. The latest distractions that have been thrown at my feet, and the men that have failed to defeat the Vanguard on several occasions .... Zak Warner and Ivory. And just like every other time I am forced to step into that ring, I am seemingly the one with a disadvantage" That stern forefinger turns away from the camera and is slowly brought to rest on the red tie of Sharpman as he continues his gaze towards the audience. Shaking his head monotonously that routine smirk of his once again crossing his face. Joesph Sharpman: "Because when I walk into Stadio Olymipico for Tribulation .. I don't know which one of you to expect .. I don't know if I'll be wrestling with a technically adept Zak Warner .. Or if I will be in a street fight with the deranged Ivory. Human nature would usually be to wait and find out ... However, I'm not like the rest of the scum that populates this company ... You see Zak, Ivory ... I will treat this match as exactly what it is ... A handicap match." Sharpman slowly lowers his hand back down to his side. Eyes still on the camera, television still softly humming in the background. And much like everything else, his speech only continues on. Joesph Sharpman: "Because facing any other man, I am aware of exactly who I am wrestling ... But Zachary, I remember the last time I walked down that isle to meet you face to face. When I expected to be facing a pure wrestling mastermind ... Only to meet this vicious, unforgiving, evil genius that you have come to know as Ivory." He finally takes his focus away from the camera, and begins his usual strut past the camera, only for the view to follow him. Showing the gorgeous maple dressers and cabinets in this five star suite, accompanied by a rather large stainless steel refrigerator .. Not that any of this really matters, but I'm setting the scene. It's my advantage alright! Joesph Sharpman: "Any other man would fear facing you ... That unique gift of unpredictably you posses to much of a risk for the reward that lies on the other side of the battle. But you see Zak, Ivory .. Whichever one of you wants to listen to this ... That, is where I set myself apart from the rest of the men behind the curtain." Suddenly stopping his strut in the middle of the large room Sharpman looks towards the ceiling, back still turning towards the camera as he lets his mind wander. Joesph Sharpman: "Because unlike those parasites ... Who promise the MWE world that they will do whatever it takes to reach the top .. I really am willing to do whatever I have to ... I have risked and lost my body and career for this industry only to watch it continue to decay, as self-proclaimed wrestling prodigies took the short way to the top ... Meanwhile I fight tooth and nail at the bottom of the heap ... Night in and night out for the shot at glory that I deserve. So you see Zak ... I am willing to sacrifice ANYTHING ...." Joesph raises his hand above his head, brining his eyes down to his worn and battered fingers as they clench tightly above his head to form a fist. A symbol we have seen far too often from Sharpman. Joesph Sharpman: " Just to hold that brass ring just one more time .. And trust me, I will ... Even if it means I have to drag your psychotic little mind to the doorstep of the board in a body bag." This time as Sharpman pauses to let his words sink in, the volume on the television rises drastically in the background. Causing this made man to turn his head to look in the direction of the noise. "... When late last night MWE superstar Ace Cannon was arrested in his hometown of Los Angeles for speeds exceeding one hundred miles an hour as well as driving under the influence. To make matters worse the superstar was just days ago suspended from the company for breaking the terms of the standard contract which states that any racial content is banned from their network ... A sentence has not been decided on as of now, but early reports are stating that the wrestler could face up to sixty days in prison as well as an undetermined time of community service." Joesph Sharpman: "Happy freaking birthday to me ..." The heavy sarcasm in his voice is easy to hear, and even from looking behind him we call tell that he has rolled his eyes as a loud burly laugh comes from the same general direction of the television. Followed by an almost familiar voice. "Lighten up Joey, you knew it was bound to happen sooner or later." With this the camera spins towards the direction of the voice, revealing none other then Jesse Anderson sprawled out of the designer sheets of one of the two king sized beds in the room. Forced into a suit from the earlier Tribulation press conference he now lays with the shirt unbuttoned half way down and his feet in on the bed as his gaze drifts between the television and Sharpman. Jesse Anderson: "That guy was a train wreck waiting to happen, all you can do is be happy everything else didn't blow up in his face like this bullsh*t did." The fact Semi can stay relaxed in this situation only seems to set Sharpman off, as he locks his glare on him. A glare similar to the one that was locked into the eyes of David Whitman just nights ago. Joesph Sharpman: "I'm glad that you can see the bright side in this Jesse, because while Ace is sitting pretty in his little jail cell right now. We're the ones that are going to have to deal with everything negative that comes out of this ..." Jesse Anderson: "Correction ... You're the one that has to deal with all the crap that comes with the gig of being "Vanguard Leader" Mister big shot." This is what it has come down to. Jesse Anderson and Joesph Sharpman, trading stares. The tension between them could be cut with a knife (I really don't understand that saying. But I'm using it anyway) as stable mates are ready to pounce on each other until finally. The fuse on Sharpman finally reaches it's end point, and boom goes the dynamite as he completely snaps in a way we have never seen him do before. Joesph Sharpman: "See that's the problem with what the Vanguard was created for! Self-indulging egomaniacs like you! I give and give towards making your career take the course that it should ... Yet people like you still can't find that little button in your head that tells you to respect the people that made you!" Semi is just not the kind of guy to take this form of verbal abuse and he quickly leaps to his feet. Getting into the face of Sharpman, even grabbing him by the collar of his shirt and pushing him violent into the picture window as the sunlight still shines through. However the light seems to make the angered look on Anderson's face even more frightening. Jesse Anderson: "No, the problem with the Vanguard is you and your self-important attitude! What you need to realize is that MWE is not the only federations on his f***ing earth, and guess what I've done something you've only ever FAILED to do and that's win a World Championship ... Now before you go on another one of your self-righteous speeches tell me .... What the f*** have you done? HUH!? .... Semi shoves his fists into the chest of Sharpman, pushing him even harder against the glass. Jesse Anderson: "What THE F*** HAVE YOU D-" Before Jesse can finish his sentence Sharpman's hand is thrown across his face with a hard slap that could have been heard next door. Leaving a large red hand print on the much larger mans face as he drops MWE's mastermind and holds his face only to have Sharpman grab it and pull it up. Forcing Semi to look into his eyes as he sneers between his teeth. Joesph Sharpman: "What I've done is make heads turn ... I have constantly been the man in the spotlight despite whatever you did in an indy promotion that has no effect to your career in a legitimate wrestling company. I made headlines around the world because of what I have done in this industry while you were jerking curtains in ICW ... THAT'S What I've done!" A sneer crosses Anderson's face as he feels the fingers of Sharpman tightly forcing his face to look towards his, and he's had enough. With one powerful move he pushes Sharpman against the glass yet again this time leaving him as he clutches his back in pain as he storms out of sight, the last sound from him is the slam of the door. With a deep gasp for air Sharpman collapses to his knees, suddenly breathing heavily as he stares at the dark burgundy carpet. Any other man in this position under this type of situation would probably break down in anger .. However, that just isn't the case with Sharpman as he first begins to chuckle down on his hands and knees before it slowly builds to a full blown laugh as leans back against the glass. Joesph Sharpman: "So this is what it comes to? ... After everything that I have sacrificed in order to make this company what it is today, I am once again cast aside because I am a man of truth .. After all the bridges that have burnt in the locker room, I am once again exiled to fight for myself ... But let me remind you all of the legend of one Joey Dallas." An evil chuckle, a deadly smirk ... and above all else an usual picture as we look at Sharpman. Now staring back down at his hands as he sits on his feet tucked neatly underneath of himself. Joesph Sharpman: "Because no matter how much you people did to me ... No matter how far you pushed me over the edge, when I bounced back I always came back even better ... and just because the legend of Joey Dallas is long dead. That familiar legacy will live on, because with the demise of the Vanguard today ... I bring forth a new prophecy this Sunday ... Because sitting here, right now I realize something ... The only time I have succeeded to my full potential is when I was on my own. See it was these two hands ... and what I have right here." He lets one of his hands move carefully towards himself, putting out an index finger to point towards his head. That evil smile still twisted across his lips. Joesph Sharpman: "My own ability to out think any of the pathetic men that call themselves the MWE roster ... So you see Zak, while your quest for the greater good ... Your goal to destroy the Vanguard, has been seen. It has only opened up a door for me, because with the mental strain of putting mid-carders like you into the main event has been lifted off of me. And now, this is the era of a man truly deserving ... A man that has every tool he needs to change the world ... For better, or for worse ... Joesph Sharpman." And with that, and one final laugh our scene fades to black to put an end to this scene. -------------------------------------------------- For far too long I have idly stood back and watched as men take what should be mine. Opportunity. And why? Because I did nothing about it ... I waited for my chance to shine. But from this day on ... I will TAKE it ... Three ... Two ... One ...
