First day at the office

Jul 28 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
David Renshaw, the London born giant with shining gold hair resting over his shoulders, his green eyes glowed with a warm light, but fear was swirling within them as he awaited Devlin Sharpe's arrival in the arena parking lot, he was dressed rather casually a tshirt, a pair of combats and a pair of hi-top trainers, he sighed as he balanced himself on his skateboard with an aluminium baseball bat in hand, his mind was racing with thoughts of what he was about to do, was he really doing the right thing? He wanted to stand out in this company so he had to do what he had to do, however there was a deep seated apprehension as his actions may have repercussions. His mind trailed back to several days earlier as he had a meeting with a MWE scouting agent regarding a MWE contract, after years of backbreaking albeit fun training at the Dropkixx Wrestling Academy in Tottenham, London, England, he was about to reallise his life long dream, a contract with an American wrestling company, something many of his fellow students could only dream of, What haunted him forever however were the last words the agent had said to him “Make an impact” He fully understood what does words meant, but could his conscience allow him to do it? He basically had a choice to make, make a good impression by having a good match or attack someone backstage and take them out of the picture permanently, with the former being extremely hard due to the many variables involved like the fact that many of the wrestlers in MWE had him beat on experience and would wipe the floor with him, and the ones that lacked experience would put on a poor match and make David look bad therefore not allowing him to make a big impact, that is why he had to bite the bullet and do it against his better judgement he had to take out a well known MWE superstar, but the question was who? There were many well known MWE superstars David could pick from, their fame was universal, however there was one particular individual that David had known from before, in fact he had trained together with his older brother in Dropkixx Academy, it was a shame that the brother of one of the greatest students of the academy had become spoiled by America and lost his way, he did not deserve to be in the MWE, his ego had gotten the better of him and he had been on an embarrassing losing streak, David reasoned to himself that he wasn't harming him, he was doing him and everyone a favour, he was putting him out of their misery As he heard footsteps he quickly crouched and used his board to hide behind a parked car, Devlin was smart, he had heard his wheels and paused for a moment, however he made a foolish move as he allowed his girlfriend to go on ahead and leave him, Devlin slowly crept towards a group of parked cars, he analyzed each of them but he had no idea how wrong his sense of direction was, he heard the clang of aluminum hitting the tarmac, Devlin turned around suddenly towards the sound but all he could see for a split second was a blur heading towards him at a rapid speed before he was speared to the ground with the force of a Freight Train by David Renshaw David looked back and made note of his skateboard that landed a few feet away from him as he looked at Devlin laying on the floor in a crumpled heap clutching his stomach, for a moment he felt sad, he was so helpless in so much pain, David wanted to help him he really did however he relented at the last moment as he put his foot gently over Devlin's Adam's Apple regretting everything he was about to do, he slowly picked up his baseball bat, he stared at Devlin's fearful eyes before mouthing the words “I am so sorry” David picked up his baseball bat, took a deep breath, and lowered it with lightning quick speed almost like a professional golfer towards the already damaged ribs of Devlin Sharpe, as the metal hit flesh and bone it made a sickening crunch sound that caused David's insides to do somersaults, it made him sick to the stomach that he had to do this, but he closed his eyes, keeping the tears in. He took another deep breath as he next targeted Devlin's arm, although he was severely winded, Devlin tried to beg him not to do it but could only whisper, David crouched down still with his foot near Devlin's Adams apple as he took in the words Devlin was trying to say, he felt horrible, however this was his job, he had to finish it and with the precision of a surgeon, he repeatedly swung at Devlin's limbs until he was sure that they were all sufficiently damaged, not enough to kill him, however enough to make sure he could not return to in ring in the near future, he then looked down at Devlin's mangled body, he had passed out from the pain ages ago, however he had thrown up a lot of blood causing his face to be covered in it, to stop him from choking to death, he tilted Devlin's head to the side, look at him one more time then walked away He held his skateboard up, looked at it for a moment then placed it down and stepped on to it with his right foot, pushing himself forward with the left foot, he continued on for a couple of moments towards the exit of the car park, when he got to the exit, he started tearing up and threw up, he couldn't believe what he had just done, he thought for a moment then took out his mobile phone to call for an ambulance for Devlin Sharpe, David waited outside the car park until the Ambulance had arrived, he then made his way to a nearby bench and sat down Was it really worth it? He asked that question to himself every single moment after that, sure nobody in MWE really liked Devlin Sharpe, but was it the right thing to do? Would doing this haunt him forever, creep behind him like an eerie shadow, all these questioned swirled in David's mind, he held his head in his hands as he thought about it all, trying to piece it together, trying to get it to make sense. He had a meeting with Richard Covell and Eli Silver in a few hours, he had to stay calm, but what would happen? Would they approve of what he did or chastise him for injuring one of their superstars, they told him to make an impact but did he really have to go that far? His mind was racing, should he go to the meeting, what would happen to him, but he had to take this chance it was his only chance He slowly got up and stepped on his skateboard and made his way to the MWE headquarters, he passed several people who didn't notice him, this would soon change as he made his debut in the MWE, as he stood outside the headquarters of MWE, he took a deep breath as he stepped in, he had no idea what to expect from the meeting, he had to be ready for anything, he walked in to the office nervously as Richard Covell and Eli Silver eyed him dillegently Richard Covell: Nice to finally meet you, Mr Renshaw Eli Silver: I honestly have no idea why you agree to this Lunacy sir, I don't condone his actions one bit Richard Covell: What you fail to understand Eli, is that this guy, is the most promising student of one of the most pretigious wrestling school in England, plus he listens, I can mold him in to the best superstars in this company he has unlimited potential Eli Silver: And a blatant disregard for the safety of others Richard Covell: Blatant disregard for the safe- Say Renshaw, what does the W in Wrestling stand for? David was nervous and his voice slightly cracked when he said it David Renshaw: Wrestling sir? Richard Covell: Exactly, see Eli, Wrestling often involves disregard for the safety of others incase you haven't noticed, Dave here has the ruthless aggression necessary to be the best in the MWE, he has the killer instinct Eli Silver: Sir, I will no longer argue with you on this matter as I like my job however this is a bad idea Richard Covell: So he nearly killed Devlin Sharpe but let's be honest, who really cares about that guy? David laughed nervously at that joke Eli Silver: Very well Mr Renshaw, now you have fulfilled Mr Covell's requirements to be a part of this company, all you have to do is sign this contract and you will be a part of the vast MWE family David slowly approaches the contract and goes over it as Richard Covell and Eli Silver look at him patiently, he takes some air in through his nose then signs the contract as Richard Covell and Eli Silver begin clapping Richard Covell: Welcome to the company kid, you'll be at the top one day, but I have to go for now, I have other business I have to attend to Richard Covell stands up and shakes David's hand before leaving the room Eli Silver: Now I must tell you David, I don't condone your actions one bit however as they were ordered by Mr Covell I have no choice but to overlook them, if you have any questions or problems, please don't start fights in parking lots, come to me, I will guide you on anything you are unsure of David thought for a moment before he spoke David Renshaw: Sir, what is going to happen about Blight Eli Silver: What do you mean? David Renshaw: I mean I took out his partner, he will lose because of me, I hate being impulsive I just wish I could help him out you know, I would like to be his partner if that is possible Eli Silver: I have no idea, it would require a lot of paperwork and such however as you caused this, I might aswell have you replace him David Renshaw: Thank you very much, I would just like to atone for my actions Eli Silver: Yes well, if that's all, you may leave my office David walked through the open door and closed it behind him, he knew he had to mentally prepare against his opponents, as Devlin's replacement he had pretty big boots to fill, he had a very good although slightly insane partner, but his opponents were superb however he had an advantage over all of them including his partner, they had all lost a match, he hadn't, he motioned a cameraman to follow him as he walked out of the MWE Headquarters and got on to his board and made his way back to the car park he was at earlier that day, he went to the exact spot Devlin was lying, came off his skateboard and stood in the pool of Devlin's blood that had dried David Renshaw: Now you may all the wondering why exactly, I brought you here, this pool of blood is the perfect example of what I can do, in case anyone out there is doubting me Dave crouched down and ran his fingers through the dry blood before standing up and looking at the camera David Renshaw: Now many people may theorise that I am weak due to the fact I blindsided dear old Devlin but that is far from the truth, I did not seek an advantage, an advantage only presented itself to me and I took it, which is just what will happen in our match, I will just sit back and analyze the match until I figured out your weaknesses than I will strike at your crucial points He stares at his hands for a moment as they are visibly shaking, he takes a deep breath and his hand stops shaking, he makes note of the temperature in the car park as it isn't cold enough to make him shiver probably just a residual psychological effect of what he done David Renshaw: as I was saying, everyone has a weakness and to the untrained eye they are not noticeable however, we in England train differently, in America like everything else, wrestling is mainly taught offensively focussing on the wrestler's strength and skill against a lesser skilled opponent, however in England, we are taught to analyse our opponents, so even if they are bigger faster and stronger, there is still a chance they could be beaten because EVERYONE has a weakness that can be exploited to gain the victory A smile grew on his face as he had a calculating look in his eye for just a moment, the thoughts of his opponent blazing through his mind at light speed, he had studied his opponents before he just had to pinpoint their weaknesses, they may not have physical weaknesses after all not everyone does, however everyone does have psychological weaknesses David Renshaw: I have spent some time studying everyone in the MWE and I think I know how to deal with our opponents, of course I also know how to cover my partners weaknesses which would make us a very effective tag team who can work together to defeat our opponents David interlocked his fingers together for a moment making a solid wall to illustrate his point about tag teams jelling together by covering each others weaknesses David Renshaw: What my opponents fail to understand is that, I understand them, I understand how they think, I understand their motives, I understand what drives them He holds his left and up and curls all his fingers apart from his index finger in to a fist and stares at it for a moment before coming to a realisation, he pauses and looks back at the camera David Renshaw: This is Peter Kaymackian, alone, by himself, no one likes him and even less actually care about him, clearly this individual has deep seated trust issues, he acts out to others in order to make him feel better, but clearly this is wrong as he will die terribly lonely, which is rather unfortunate David seems saddened by what he just said, however as he bows his head, shadows of a smirk can be seen, as he raises his head, theres a serious look on his face once again David Renshaw: How do you propose to compete in a tag team match Mr Peter Kaymackian? How are you so sure your partner actually wants to help you, after all you don't have many friends in the MWE or in general, do you? See this is the problem you actually face, in order to defeat me and my partner, you must first face yourself, ask yourself this question, do you honestly trust anyone? Or rather, does anyone trust you? David Renshaw cleared his throat as he looked at the flickering light bulb of the car park until it stopped blinking David Renshaw: Anthony Clark seems to be tagging with you out of necessity, as I am tagging with Blight out of necessity, however unlike you and Anthony Clark, me and Blight have a mutual understanding between us, we respect each other and understand that in order to win our match we must work together not against each other, we must not let our egos clash and try to pick up the glory for ourselves He makes a fist as he smirks at the camera hitting his stride as he knew the type of person Peter Kaymackian really was, there was a glint in his eye as he kept talking with a grin on his face David Renshaw: Maybe its because of your attention starved upbringing or some other mindfuck you suffered as a child, you have the ego of a hollywood A-Lister, you must cry yourself to sleep, living a shell of a life, you pretend you are some big shot strutting around like the place like you own it, but deep down, you're just an insecure little maggot who makes himself feel better by taking out his anger stemming from his failure of a life, on other people, do us all a favour man, just come out of the closet, admit that you have psychological problems, there are services out there that can help you, don't lie to yourself, or one day this will all catch up to you and you will regret it as you can't keep up with the pressure... David crouches and looks around for a small pebble on the ground, he spots one and picks it up and looks back at the camera and crushes the pebble in his hand and lets the dust get blown away by the wind David Renshaw: And crumble in to dust... you know what the sad thing is right? You actually believe everything you say to yourself He gets in to a foetal position and rocks back and forth David Renshaw I am a good boy, I am a good boy, I am the best, I am the best He stands up and dusts himself off before looking at the camera again David Renshaw: Get this in to your air filled big head Peter Kaymackian You are not special. you are not a beautiful or unique snowflake, you're the same decaying organic matter as everything else, You're not how much money you have in the bank, you're not the car you drive, you're not the contents of your wallet, you're not your fucking khakis, you're the all-singing, all-dancing crap of the world and I genuinely hope that one day you will understand this but regrettably it will not be in the near future it seems He got back on to his skateboard and skated towards the exit of the parking lot as he wanted some fresh air, the smell of the parking lot was making him dizzy a cocktail of urine and diesel fuel, an amazing aroma that can make a Frenchman puke David Renshaw: Now I come on to the other half of the tag team, that I will face this week, clearly this person has less issues than his partner however it seems that his main concern ISN'T the match at hand, some people may even say he doesn't even care about this match, no, because obviously he is too preoccupied with his sham of a love life, I mean who honestly cares about girl that used to manage you and let you stick your dick in to her, possibly while drunk I might add, you should've reallised as soon as she let you do that, that she would spread her legs for any random individual and accepted that and moved on and concentrated on your wrestling career, if you put half the effort you put in to maintaining a relationship in to winning a match, well you wouldn't have lost last week would you? But don't worry it's not just you, this is a flaw exhibited by several members of the MWE roster, they lack the focus and the commitment to their career which is why they lose, not because they aren't good enough or their opponent is just better, but because they don't concentrate on what is truly important and until you understand that Anthony, you will always and forever be a loser David clears his throat realising he had been standing like a statue all through his little monologue about Anthony Clark, scratches his hair for a moment as he had forgotten about something or rather someone as he remembered he had a tag team partner for that match, all of the case files he had studied for the MWE wrestlers, his case file was the most intriguing due to the psychological reports David Renshaw: Finally I would like to close this segment by talking about my partner in this match, total fucking nutjob is one way to describe this man, however oddly enough, it seems me and him have a lot in common, both of us wanted to destroy Devlin Sharpe although for different reasons, this man, my partner has the brute strength to compliment my strategic attack on our opponents, of course with my intelligence I can lead us to victory, however my partner may be unstable so I would have to watch my own back should he turn on me David seems to hear something and whips his head back but it turns out to be nothing so he turns to face the camera again David Renshaw: As I was saying together with Blight the psychotic behemoth we will plow through our enemies and be on our way to victory, as that is simply the only logical choice, after all who would dare face Blight alone let alone with me in his corner, Blight may be a psychologist's dream, however anyone who faced him must have lost all sense of logic...so Peter Kaymackian, Anthony Clark, Bring it on
