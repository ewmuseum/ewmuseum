R&R; A Grand Mystique RP

Oct 31 2008

The Grand Mystique

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The camera moves in to show a MWE ring in the empty arena in Philadelphia. There is very little lighting and no movement and no noise. All of a sudden, the arena spotlights start to flicker, focussing on each of the four turnbuckles in a random order. The speed at which they move from one to the other quickens. The colours change from orange to red to blue to green, creating an incredible light show. In an instant, the show stops and there is a moment of darkness. It then starts again, this time focussing on one corner. Sitting there on the turnbuckle in a black overcoat is Grand Mystique. His mask, a sparkling dark blue, provides incredible reflections off the spotlights. GM: “Finally it has begun; Grand Mystique has appeared for the very first time in the MWE! Not only have I made my debut, but I did it successfully. The Grand Mystique and Maxx have claimed their first victims and we are your new MWE Tag Team Champions.” Mystique speaks in his raspy, slightly-lisped voice. While his mask hid any clear emotion, his voice betrays some pride and happiness. GM: “Project Revolution....” A more serious tone. He pauses, and reaches for his midriff. He undoes the jacket and around his waist is the shining MWE Tag Team. GM:“You took some of our best offense, we took yours. Yes we beat you and took what meant most to you two than anything in the world. But we expect you to come back. We demand that you earn the opportunity to face us again with these belts on the line!" “I am sure our paths will cross in the future. That has been determined by the stars.” GM claps his hands twice. The arena goes pitch black. ************************ The camera is now scrolling along a corridor. The equipment stored in the area makes it clear it is backstage at a MWE event. The camera steps through a door into a pitch black room. The doors slams shut. It is still eerily dark but there is a sound of water washing against metal. There is also an occasional deep sound. There is another two claps. The lights come up slowly. After a few seconds, the scene becomes clearer. What would be a normal locker room is laid out with various paraphernalia, such as paintings, incense sticks and aromatherapy candles lit with a rose tint, tarot and playing cards and mannequins. There is also a crystal ball on a table to the right of the bath. The room has a pinkish hue to it. A black overcoat is hung to the left. In the middle of the room, Grand Mystique is sat in a metal bath tub, filled with water and giant ice cubes. His whole body (everywhere south of the neck) is covered with the water, yet he shows no indication that the temperature of the freezing cold water bothers him in the slightest. The camera moves in to the front of the bath. GM: “So Darius and Boyd, potential new champions or pretenders to our crown?” “So you are the new number one contenders? Well you displayed something resembling potential when you took out the Ortans’ last week. But I promise that this week will be a very different experience for you two.” GM snaps his neck sideways to the right. All of a sudden, the crystal ball moves viciously to the right. It falls off to the table and smashes. The camera sees all of this; it comes back to where it was at the front of the bath and then moves in very slowly. GM: “As if I needed to remind you, the Grand Mystique and Maxx have held numerous singles titles between us and this team were always destined to succeed. Just ask Project Revolution and the Hooligans. Their bones will be aching, brains will be concussed. And the former champions have ten pounds of gold missing from the waist because they know they came across simply unstoppable.” “Now they will look at up at the beautiful mystery of a starry night, unable to comprehend how the universe handed them such a vicious destiny. A destiny that you, Darius and Boyd, will be forced to share because any challenger to our titles will be forced to endure similar punishment. Maxx will break your neck and the Big Bang will come to talk you all out because non-title or not, we do whatever is necessary.” “We are prepared to do whatever is necessary. And I am willing to tread my path to destiny through every member of the roster, so be it. The camera is now right in front of GM. He smiles, looks down and brings up a sparkling new (and whole) crystal ball out of the water. It encases a ticking pocket watch. The crystal ball then has a small amount of dark blood dripping down it. The camera focuses in on this, and then FADES. GM’s voice is heard; “It’s nearly time. Boyd, Darius – get ready because our match at Mayhem will be the closest that you will get to our Tag Team titles!”
