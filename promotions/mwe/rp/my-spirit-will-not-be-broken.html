My Spirit will not be broken; Chris England RP

Jul 4 2009

Chris England

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It’s been a long hard week, autograph signing after autograph signing, meet and greets with the fans, the whole shebang, but finally the end of Friday has finally come and all that’s over with. Now it’s time to relax and prepare for my big tag match this Sunday. I’m stood in front of a wash basin with a mirror in front of it in the hotel lobby bathroom of where I’m staying; it’s just like any other fancy bathroom, granite work surfaces, gleaming expensive mirrors and them ultra posh hand dryers that feel like there literally blowing the skin off your hands. I turn the tap on and fill the palms of my hands with warm water; I close my eyes and splash the water over my face in some kind of effort to bring some energy back to myself. As I open my eyes and glance into the mirror I can see a tall shadowy figure standing in the door way, I can’t quite make out his face but there’s something about him that seems familiar. **Mysterious Shadowy Man** Well well well. It looks as if we have ourselves somebody who is not all that he shows. **England** Excuse me? As I turn around to confront this man he has gone, he has simply vanished into thin air, who the hell was that guy and what the hell did he mean. I hurriedly walk out into the hotel lobby and scan around quickly with my eyes, there is no sign of the mysterious man, how can he just vanish into thin air? Was he even real, or was he some kind of deranged figment of my imagination, either way there’s something fishy going on here and I’m not so sure I want to find out what it is. I begin to walk through the hotel lobby still a bit confused about what just happened, suddenly I feel a tap on the back of my shoulder, instinctively I spring around fists clenched, body coiled and ready to pounce to find a short, bald, fat man in his mid thirties the man looks at me almost wetting himself my edgy reactions obviously un nerving him somewhat, he begins speaking to me. **Fat man** Whoa there big man I was only going to ask for a autograph. Thank god for that for a moment there I thought it was the mystery nut job coming to attack me, I breathe a deep sigh of relief as I reach down into my pocket and pull out my instant marker pen that’s still there from my autograph signings. **England** Oh right sure man what you want signing? The man pulls a ticket from the last MWE show out of his pocket and hands it too me. **England** So you saw me get my ass kicked at the last show and you still want my autograph. The Fat man grins at me in a pleasant but slightly freaky way. **Fat Man** Of course I do man your my favourite wrestler, the way that despite your past and all the shit you’ve gone through, you still give it your all is really inspiring dude, you got guts to try and put things right and for that I respect you big time man. The fat mans word’s have left me somewhat speechless, I don’t think I’ve ever had anyone call me an inspiration in my life, truly touched by his kind words I decide to reward them. **England** You know what that’s one of the nicest things anyone has ever said to me, what’s your name bud? **Fat Man** My names Mike Roth sir. **England** Well mike you got tickets for Independence Day this weekend? **Mike** Yeah but I’m up in the nose bleed seats it’s all I could get. **England** Well as you were so kind with your words to me, how about I return the kindness, when you go to the show speak to the stewards I will make sure you’re on the guest list for ringside seats. Mikes eyes light up as if he can’t believe I just offered him this, this is really satisfying for me, and I’m really getting to like this nice guy side of me. **Mike** Oh man holly fuck this is the single greatest fucking thing anyone has ever done for me, how can I ever repay you. **England** You already did with your kind words, but if you really want too you could always boo the shit out of my useless opponent’s tomorrow night. I shoot a wide grin towards mike who is now grinning back at me and laughing. **Mike** For sure man, I would off done that anyway. **England** Well it’s been nice meeting you mike but I really should get some rest before the big match. I extend my hand out in a offer of a handshake to Mike he accepts and we shake hands and exchange smiles before walking away from each other, that was a great experience for me, for the first time in a long long time I feel appreciated for my efforts, and I will be damned if I’m going to let Mike down this Sunday, hell if I get my way I will steal the god damn show let alone the match! I’m now stood outside the hotel lobby elevators. The lift goes ping the doors open and in I walk. I Lean back against the shiny gold plated hand rail that’s screwed onto the elevator and close my eyes. The elevator has come to a crashing halt and the lights have gone out. Oh fucking great just what I needed after a long day. After about thirty seconds the lights come back on and the advertising screen on the wall behind me starts to flash catching my eye. The screen stops flashing and the shadowy image of a mysterious figure who’s face is covered in darkness and can’t be made out appears, it’s that fucking nut job again what the hell does this guy want! **Mysterious Shadowy Man** Now I’m sure you’re curious as to why I keep bothering you, it’s rather simple really, I see every single human being for what they are. They aren't as innocent as they can claim to be. But you...you......I see something within your soul. This whole.....trying to be a good guy shit.....it's not you. What I see in you, is that you...are an addict you're no different from the druggies, the Smokers, or the alcoholics, except your choice of addiction. You can try to rid yourself of it, but in due time, you will return. You will return to being a freak like me. And I know, that you will enjoy it, because one can mask the darkness that lies beneath, but one can never remove it from their soul. The screen cuts back to showing advertisements, and the lift beings to move once more, an overpowering sense of rage is pulsating around my body at rapid speed, who the fuck does this guy think he is, does he really think he can do this to me and get away with it! I’m going to find this fucker and I’m going to tear him apart one tiny piece at a time. WAIT that’s exactly what he wants, this is the exact reaction he was hoping to drag out of me, I cannot let him win I WILL NOT play his twisted little game. I need to get calm I need to show this man that he can do this as much as he pleases but he will not defeat me. DING goes the elevator as the doors swing open; I shake my head from side to side in some kind of pointless effort to clear my mind as I step out of the elevator and into the hallway. I need to show this man that he cannot break my spirit. I need to get some rest and work on making a BIG statement to this whack job at Independence Day this Sunday! Now outside of my hotel room, I swipe the room card in the door and push it open, I dramatically flop down on the bed letting out a sigh in the process, and I close my eyes and drift off into deep thought. ************18 hours later ****************** The scene opens up inside the main arena of the municipal stadium, Jacksonville, Florida. This is an MWE house show, the last one before the blockbuster pay-per-view Independence Day. The fans are in a frenzy of excitement wondering what is going to go down next following an action packed start to the show. Reborn by Drowning pool blasts out over the sound system, the fans burst out into an excited round of cheers as the know who is on their way! As The True English Patriot Chris England makes his way onto the entrance ramp, the fans loudly chant his name, ENGLAND ENGLAND ENGLAND ENGLAND! He is dressed in a pair of blue Jeans and a white t-shirt that has the Union Jack flag on it. He makes his way down to the ring shaking hands with the fans on his way, he even stops to sign some autographs for some lucky fans in the front row at ringside. England climbs up into the ring and walks over to the corner, where a sound man hands him a microphone. With a big wide grin on his face and clearly looking somewhat overwhelmed by the amazing reception he has received he begins to speak. **England** Wow I thought the reception I got last time I stepped in a ring was great, but you guys have to be the loudest SOB’s this side of the Atlantic! As long as you guys give me this love, I will make it my number 1 priority to give you all one hell of a show each and every time I step inside the squared circle. The crowd brake out into chanting England’s name once more as he shakes his head and claps on the microphone in appreciation. So I bet your all thinking, what brings The True English Patriot out here, only 24 hours before one of the biggest contests of his career? Well its simple really, you see just under 24 hours ago this weird freak took it upon himself to stalk me, first creeping up behind me in the bathroom, then later on he somehow shut out the power of the elevator I was in and taunted me on the advertisement screen. The crowd break out into a repeated chorus of ‘what an asshole’ clap clap clap clap clap, England grins and lets out a chuckle before gathering himself to continue. See this guy has decided he wants to taunt me on my past, he says that I am just like him and the darkness within me will eventually prevail. Well mystery man if you can hear me, listen up and listen up good! You will NOT break my spirit, yeah I have a dodgy past and yeah I have done many dark sinister and evil things that I regret deeply, but you’re not going to bring that back out in me, no matter how hard you try! England stands still in the centre of the ring shaking his head from side to side, as a physical gesture that he will not be broken down, he has a stern look on his face and you can hear the emotion in his voice. This Sunday I am going to show you exactly how the new more enlightened me, responds to engagement and that my friend, is in a cool, calm and professional manner. See I could go into this match tomorrow night against Peter k and Scotty McKnight and I could go hell for leather, I could tear them to shreds in a emotional reaction to your actions, but no I’m not going to do that, because we both know that is exactly what you want and as long as there’s breath in my lungs I WONT let you win. England still has a stern look on his face, the anger towards the mystery man is evident but so is his will and determination to do the right thing, the fans let out another round of England chants as England pauses gathering his thought. Boy oh boy this guy has got me more fired up than I’ve felt in a LONG time and that can mean nothing but bad luck for McKnight and Peter K. You see guys when you step into the ring with me this Sunday, it’s going to be a simple case of wrong place, wrong time! I’m fired up and raring to go guys, I aint felt this hungry for success in years, I REALLY want that W beside my name this Sunday and not only do I want the W, I want to achieve it in sensational style. It may well be a ladder match and they may well be no rules, but you will see no chair swinging, low blowing or cheap shots from me. However what you will see is a pure wrestling master class, I will be dragging pure wrestling moves out of my locker; moves that Peter k and Scotty don’t even know exist. From the first bell to the last bell I will school you in the art of wrestling. Very much the same way in which I took Peter K to school only a few short weeks ago. England grins into the camera in a highly confident fashion, oozing with enthusiasm and more than ready for the task at hand. Now don’t get me wrong, I am by no means saying that this is going to be easy, Peter K is after all a very talented young man, a young man who I’ve said on many occasions, has all the attributes to make it all the way to the top. However as I’ve also pointed out in the past his MAIN enemy is himself! He is too egotistical he wants it all and he wants it now. Peter just like you found out last time you got in the ring with me, success doesn’t just happen, it’s not something you can command, you have to EARN IT, me on the other hand no matter what the outcome, I give it two-hundred percent each and every time I step in the ring. For your sake Peter I hope you have learned your lesson from our last match, BUT if you haven’t yet again you will find yourself in a no win situation. England pauses in the middle of the ring for a brief moment to gather his thoughts, the intensity is oozing out of him, he is in the zone, and he really feels this is his time to make his mark. He takes a deep breath before continuing. Now then onto you Scotty McKnight.........you know what, I really wish there was something positive I could say about you, but to be honest there isn’t. You’re arrogant, you’re snobbish and your one sick and twisted deranged human being. I mean yeah sure no one liked Jekyll in fact he creeped everyone out, but did you really have to stoop so low as to take out your OWN partner? The simple answer is no. No man should ever take it upon himself to intentionally injure someone, let alone their own bloody partner. Scotty you make me sick and I am going to take pleasure in giving you the hardest match of your damn life. It is people like you who give this sport a bad name and if I have anything to do with it, I will delight in nothing more than kicking your sorry arse right back into the luxury mansion, it crawled out from. England looks out into the crowd once more with stern look on his face. His expressions letting them know that he is more than ready to go to battle and that he is determined to win at Independence Day. Now that brings me to the final man in this match. My tag partner Zach Crash. Zach you’re an impressive young athlete. You conduct yourself in respectful manner; it will be my pleasure to team up with you. Tomorrow night hopefully we can put on one hell of a show, let’s do it for the millions of fans all around the world and most importantly let’s do it to show these moronic idiots that the key to victory is not in your ability in the ring, but in the ability within our own minds to create our own destiny! England throws the microphone down to the ground as Reborn by Drowning pool begins to play once more. He drops to his back and rolls out of the ring. He has a steely look of confidence and determination on his face as he makes his way to the back.
