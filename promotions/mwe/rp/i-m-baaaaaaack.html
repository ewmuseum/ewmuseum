I'M BAAAAAAACK!

Jun 11 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
** The camera shot opens up and we see Ace Cannon sitting on a couch. He is wearing a black Silver Star t-shirt, Affliction jeans and black Air Force Ones. His head is titled up and he appears to be staring at the ceiling above him. He keeps his gaze fixed on the ceiling for a long time and he doesn’t really move. The camera then begins to pan around the room and we see a simple waiting room. It has bad furniture and magazines all over the place and it overly lit with poor fluorescent lighting. As the camera pans back around we see Ace has quit staring at the ceiling and is now fingering through a People magazine…** Cannon: Man, Kate is such a fucking cunt. But I must say, after eight kids her body ain't bad. I'd give it to her. ** Cannon was referring to Kate from Jon & Kate Plus 8 if you didn’t know. As he continues flipping through the magazine he stops on another article about Zac Efron.** Cannon: Zac Efron is going to be in a remake of Footloose? Man, the original sucked but now there going let this little ass clown be in it? I wish he would just fall off the face of the earth with his little emo boy haircut. I’d fuck his girlfriend though… **Cannon shakes his head and sighs. He continues fingering through the magazine and comes across a fashion pictorial where outfits are rated.** Cannon: Man, all these skanks on here are hot. I would let any of them saddle up and ride my bologna pony…. Well, except for Roseanne.. What the fuck is she doing up here anyway? She hasn’t been relevant since ‘96.… ** Just as Cannon finishes that thought we here a door open and Cannon looks up from the magazine.** Lady: Mr. Malibu is now ready to see you. ** Cannon nods and sets the magazine down. He then follows the lady as she leads him down the hallway. She stops and opens a door.** Lady: Go ahead and take a seat Mr. Cannon. Mr. Malibu will be with you in just a moment. ** Cannon walks in and suddenly stops and looks at the women who lead him in.** Cannon: Has anyone ever told you that your fucking hot? ** The lady looks at him and gives an embarrassed type smile** Lady: No wonder you’re here Mr.Cannon… Just take a seat and Mr. Malibu will be right in. ** Ace walks over and sits down at Dick Malibu‘s desk. He props his feet up on the large wooden desk and begins looking all around the room at all the pictures Dick has hanging up in the office. In all of them he is with some MWE wrestler or personality shaking hands with a big cheesy smile on his face, but out of all the pictures Ace notices one thing…. He isn’t up there which infuriates him because he made Dick Malibu. During Ace’s thought the door suddenly opens and we see the face of Dick Malibu, the GM on MWE.** Dick: Daddy-O! It’s been a long time! ** Dick extends his hand and Cannon just looks at it. He gets out of the chair and moves over to a big white leather couch on the other side of the office and sits down.. He just stares at Dick without saying a word.** Dick: Daddy, why are you being such a sour puss? ** Cannon takes a deep breath and just glares at Dick who is still standing at the door. After a long moment of tense silence Cannon finally speaks..** Cannon: Dick…… FUCK YOU! ** Dick raises a single eye brow in the direction of Cannon’s statement.** Dick: Excuse me? **Cannon doesn’t hesitate this time.** Cannon: Dick, you heard me. FUCK YOU! I will spell it out this time for you… F.U.C… ** Dick stops him..** Dick: I get the point Ace…. But why do you have all this anger directed toward me? ** Cannon laughs sarcastically at Dick.. He just shakes his head.** Cannon: You are dumber then I thought. Why shouldn’t I be mad at you? You’re the son of a bitch who suspended me and put me out of work for a month. You fucking took my title shot away…. The carrot was right there for me to grab and you took it away. I have every right to fucking hate you. Hell, not to mention the fact the only reason you have this nice job is because of me! I brought you into this business and helped you make a name for yourself, and you repay me with a suspension… Yea, FUCK YOU! **Dick just looks at Cannon for a moment and finally takes a seat in a white leather chair across from Cannon.** Dick: Oh how easy you forget daddy-o! You didn’t get me to MWE.. I got myself here! If you remember correctly you fired my ass when you went all dark and depressed when we were in RevPro! Remember when you were getting your tattoos done and your fired me in the tattoo parlor? Remember? ** Ace just sits there still glaring at Dick.** Dick: Just like I thought, you forgot. I got here on my own accord! Sure, you did help me make a name for myself but you left me… I didn’t leave you. Now as far as suspending you don’t blame me. Blame your father, it came from him. I tried to defend you as much as I could daddy, but that promo was the straw that broke the camels back. I almost had to suspend you when you kicked DC’s pregnant girlfriend but I convinced everyone it was just an accident… But you didn’t know that. Instead you want to come in here all pissed off thinking I am out to ruin you… Newsflash……. I’M NOT! **Cannon rubs his chin in a quizzical manner while looking at Dick.** Cannon: I never say this… But I guess your right. Now why in the hell I am here? Dick: Glad you asked… I have some good news and bad news for you. Cannon: Ok… Dick: What do you want to hear first? Cannon: Give me the good news… Dick: Well, Ace Cannon…. I am happy to tell you that you will be back for the upcoming MNM. The board of directors voted unanimously to allow you back. ** A smile creeps across Cannon’s face as he looks at Dick.** Cannon: About fucking time…. I am fucking pumped for this… Who am I facing? Dick: I will tell you in a second… But you have to get the bad news first. ** The smile slowly melts off Cannon’s face..** Cannon: Alright, what is it? Dick: Well, the board of directors did vote unanimously to bring you back… But there is one condition… Cannon: AND??? Dick: You have to have someone baby sit you outside of the ring for the next month.. Cannon: What the fuck!?! I am a grown ass man. This is fucking bullshit! ** Cannon sits with a look of scorn and fury written all over his face.** Dick: Now don’t get all pissed. I got someone to come in that you have a past with… **Cannon gives Dick a quizzical look.** Cannon: So who the fuck is it? Dick: You will see… **Dick gets up and walks over to the office door and slowly opens it…. After a few brief seconds a huge African-American man with a big smile enters. He is wearing a Raiders jersey, jeans and black Reeboks.. It is none other then BISHOP! Dick pushes past Bishop and sits back down in his chair. Bishop follows and stands over Cannon with his arms open.** Bishop: Ace, give your boy Bishop hug! ** Cannon just looks up at Bishop and shakes his head.** Bishop: You don’t want to give me a hug? It’s been awhile since we have seen each other. **Cannon just looks up at the big man and chokes back his disgust.** Bishop: C’mon buddy.. Gimme a hug. ** Cannon finally lets his disgust show.** Cannon: Listen, I am not the same guy I used to be Bishop. Shit has changed and you need to understand this. I know you’re here to do a job and I respect that… But were no longer friends and we haven’t been for awhile. So do your job and that’s it… ** Bishop gets a little teary eyed and shurgs his shoulder and takes a seat next to Cannon.** Cannon: Now with this circus over who am I facing at MNM? Dick: Well, you have a tough road ahead of you in your return daddy-o… Your first opponent back is none other then Kevin Hardaway. ** Cannon looks at Dick and shakes his head in a disapproving manner.** Cannon: Kevin Hardaway.. Really? He is… ** Dick holds up his hand cutting off Cannon.** Dick: Don’t cut a promo here. I don’t have time for it brother… I have already arranged for a camera to meet you outside of here in ten minutes for promo time. ** Cannon nods at Dick and stands up.** Cannon: I am going outside to wait for the camera to show up. Dick, I will see you around. ** Cannon walks past Dick with Bishop in tow as they exit into the hallway. It is silent for the first couple minutes until Bishop breaks the silence. Bishop: So how have ya been dawg? It's been a minute? **Cannon just continues walking and not paying attention to anything Bishop is saying.** Bishop: Ya, well since you last seen me I started my own bitness as a bodyguard. I have worked for sum big stars like Miley Cyrus, Andy Dick, and even Spencer from that show The Hills... I am sad that show ended. That was my shit! ** Cannon just continues and still not paying attention. Bishop still doesn't notice his friend isn't paying attention.** Bishop: Ace, have you heard from Sincade recently? I heard he was going through some tough shit. That man was always a gangster though.. **Cannon finally stops as they get to the exit of the building. He turns around and looks at Bishop.** Cannon: Listen, I already told you once that we aren't friends anymore. I have changed and I am not the old Ace Cannon. I will respect you and the job that your going to do and thats where are relationship stops. I don't want to hear anything about the past or what your doing with you life. I don't fucking care... ** Cannon turns around and walks out the building as the camera turns to Bishop he just stands there with a distraught look written upon his face. It's like someone just killed his puppy as the camera fades out.** **We fade back in and see Cannon sitting on a park bench under a big shaded tree. He looks relaxed and at peace while sitting there. He looks around at green grass and trees the surround him. You can here the birds chirping in harmony and the leafs rustling when the wind gusts a bit. After a few moment of this serenity is interrupted.** Cannon: I’M BAAAAAAAAAAACK! ** A sly smile comes across Cannon’s face as he takes sunglasses and puts them atop his head.** Cannon: Now that I am back let me clear up a few things. The first off being that I’m not a racist. Now, I know a lot of you think when I am not around that I am wearing a hood and white sheet driving around in a pick up and spreading hate… That simply isn’t true. Secondly, I love Mexican food…. So that means I love Mexican people and thirdly, fuck all of MWE for turning there back on me. But I will address all that at another time.. Right now I have an opponent to talk about… ** Cannon stops and takes a breath before continuing.** Cannon: Kevin Hardaway… A so called legend in the wrestling a world. A man who has done everything. A man who dominated GWC, a company that many people consider the so called best of all time. . You have an impressive track record and I can’t deny that…. But I do know one thing, MWE isn’t GWC and I’m sure as hell not DeAngelo Williams or any other pansy ass motherfucker you faced there. I am god damn Ace motherfucking Cannon! ** Cannon once again the smile appears on Cannon’s face. He looks off into the distance past the camera before he continues. No one knows what Cannon is thinking about… But it is probably juicy.** Cannon: Hardaway.. Long before you got too MWE I had already made a name for myself. I was the “Franchise” Ace Cannon. I had been the most controversial man to ever step into an MWE ring. I had kicked children and been in epic matches with anyone you could imagine… I was the big man on campus around here. Then as I am about to reach the summit of my career I get suspended and from all accounts you were one of the people to benefit from that suspension. I mean, I have been told you got a title shot and got into the little backstage kliq with Brian Michaels, DC and all those guys. The funny thing is what a lot of people don’t know is I was actually on good terms with those guys and was a locker room leader… Then I get suspended and you step into the roll I left. ** Cannon stops and takes a breath before continuing.** Cannon: Now, I see you benefited from me getting suspended and I must say congratulations too you. But, I do know one thing… You can’t feel my boots in MWE! I know the ratings dipped in MWE without me and I know this for a fact. I had people come up on the street and say to me “Cannon, MWE isn’t the same with out you. The spice is missing.” I heard that over and over and then I turned on MWE one week and saw you Hardaway and I laughed. I laughed at how hooky and cheesy you were. Then I thought to myself you must have been sucking some serious dick to be a legend in GWC because what I saw from you wasn’t shit! ** Cannon lets his face turn into a slight scowl as he continues.** Cannon: There is no hype surrounding my return and I wanted that Hardaway. I told the board of directors that I’d let my track record speak for myself if they gave me a quality opponent and then they turn around and give me you! This has to be a joke, you’re the biggest overrated piece of shit in wrestling today. I don’t care how inflated your accomplishments are or how much people praise you. It’s all a farce and you have bit off more then you can chew here in MWE and I am going to be the guy who finally makes you choke and re-think your time here in MWE. **Cannon pauses and looks up at the sky and takes a deep breath and exhales very slowly.** Cannon: Now here is the funny thing about this match. If by somehow in God’s divine plan you beat me then it won’t matter. I have already hit rock bottom! But you, you on the other hand have everything to gain and lose by beating me… **Cannon stops and just looks at the camera. His eyes are now glowing with intensity and his face wears a competitive scowl.** Cannon: …You can’t knock me down any further now Penny Hardaway… But I know by me whipping your fucking ass I can knock you down a couple notches. I have heard whispers that you’re the future of MWE and all this other shit and let’s face the facts.. A loss to me, a man suspended for racist comments, a man who has kicked children, a man who has abused women, a man who may or may not be an alcholic ,a man who has caused a miscarriage by kicking a female would do nothing but hurt your career. I am the ultimate villain here and your MWE’s superhero and if I defeat you then everyone will be disappointed and they will turn there back on you and you will yet again be left with more unfulfilled potential here in MWE! **Cannon reaches up and puts his sunglasses back over his eyes and stands up from the park bench.** Cannon: Hardaway, I really hope you bring you’re A game out to the ring with you at MNM. I want some kind of test and I want you in that legendary form you supposedly possess. Because I really want to show every little piece of shit fan and every little cocksucker in the locker room that a suspension can’t hold me down and that I am back and better then ever! So K-Fed, Penny Hardaway, Kevin Hardaway or whatever the fuck your names is I am ready for you…. And just remember the pressure sits squarely on your shoulders and not mine… You have everything to lose and I have everything to gain. See you June 20th and remember NO PRESSURE! **Cannon looks at the camera and lowers his sunglasses just enough to give the camera a sleazy wink followed by a dry laugh as the camera fades to black.**
