What the fuck just happened?; A Grand Mystique RP

Dec 4 2009

The Grand Mystique

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Derf, you suck :p *OOC; I know I could have won this by just posting those three words, but here is the actual RP* The sky above is far-reaching and limitless. Absolute massive space that reaches farther than imagination will even venture. Limitless potential. Existence on Earth is a far simpler concept. One step at a time. A week, a month; it will come and go with alarming regularity. Life is predictable like clockwork. The night is quiet and unobtrusive. The dead silence of a perfect night can allow for real concentration that is key for rediscovery of a lost edge. Quiet thinking can reinvigorate and revitalise a disturbed mind. That is at least the experience of the Grand Mystique. He is taking time to refocus in the only way he knows how. The surroundings are lit up by the sparkling night sky. It is a picture perfect night with no cloud to spoil this idyllic outlook. While GM has looked sapped of his trademark confidence and bravado in recent weeks, we see him here and he looks truly at home and content, absorbed amongst his solitary solace. The stresses which have dominated his life in recent times have lifted from his face to be replaced with natural composure. “Life can only kick you in the balls so many times before you have to ask; what the fuck can you do to me anymore?” This is typical of the Grand Mystique. While his achievements have been relatively stellar, his competitive in-ring record is less than remarkable, so his ability to bounce back from a downturn in his fortunes is more extraordinary. He is jogging along a gravel track and the camera can barely keep track of him. The moon is visible from over his shoulder. It hangs low in the sky and casts a weird shade of pale orange that seems eerie and faintly demonic. He continues jogging as the incline of the track increases. The crushing sound of gravel beneath his feet has become hypnotic and relentless in its rhythm. He slows to a stop as he reaches the edge of a sheer cliff face. He takes a second to regain his breath as he looks over the almost vertical drop to the cavernous unknown below. “WrestleWars.....holy shit.” “It’s really been a fucking year in this company? Damn scary prospect. Kinda funny though. I’ve always wondered what would have happened if I came in earlier. Different time, different possibilities. But Destiny plays out in the funniest ways.” He laughs and wipes his sweaty brow with a black and white sweatband on his left arm. “And instead of seeing Maxx or Project Revolution here, it’s me. The only remnant of that tag team match left standing. Hell, even the belts are extinct.” Pushing his greasy hair off his sweat filled forehead, he considers everything that happened in that short space of time. “And that is because I never backed off and I never accepted to be second best to those clowns. I outlasted them and then I buried them, achieving more than they ever could, in a shorter time that they even imagined.” As he recalls his journey of the past year, he talks in his familiar way. A gruff growl, piqued with a determined tone. “I evolved every step of the way and became a World Champ. Regardless of the bumps in my path, I will not falter. I can still call myself a Champion, unlike half the roster who are prancing about like they are something. All I see at the US title level, in the XTV division are people who are so blind to their own ego, that they feel able to ignore their failings.” He turns directly and grabs the camera. His eyes burn bright as they catch the light and he grimaces, speaking furiously. “I could quite happily do a Brian Michaels and shoot on every single member of the roster but what would that accomplish? I have no desire to go mana a mano in a pissing contest with idiots who can’t even lace my boots.” He grins ever so slightly, knowing he passed up the chance to use a “classic” Michaels-ism in favour of effectively making his point, like a man instead of a dickhead. Fuck it, Brian was overrated. I proved that at Highway at Hell. GM pushes the camera away. He stretches his legs loosely as if he needs to move his body to keep his momentum going. “But Eric Derf, I don’t quite know how to place you among all the wasters that I have seen recruited by this company. You come from WZCW and Rev Pro, out of nowhere and manage to take the group by storm. Mr Money in the Bank has proven that he can go from enigmatic unknown to superstar at the forefront of what MWE means.” “That should command respect, shouldn’t it? You should be able to use that briefcase to get what you want.....Right?” A quizzical look from GM burns through as if he was looking at Derf himself. From someone who had power and lost it, to someone who has it and chooses not to abuse it. “But that attitude quite hasn’t flown with DC has it?” “Don’t worry though. You’ll get your rematch. On his terms, no doubt. But you will get your chance to prove to the world that your win over him was no fluke. But everyone else knows, it was not the momentous event it should have been. There was no passing of the torch; it was him lying down for you because he only wanted an easy night. So he could collect his paycheck and rest his boo-boo.” To feign shock, he covers his mouth and raises his eyebrows. “Oh noes, was that too far?” He stands up, ready to go again. He looks up and sees the start of the winding path up the mountain that he is intending to take. He steps forward but freezes as out of the corner of his eye, he sees a sparkling crystal ball which captures the twinkling stars and stands out even in this darkness. “Like I’ve said, destiny is a bitch but it is the truth.” He reaches down to pick it up. He wipes it clean and looks deeply into it. He had not seen this accessory in a long time and it has helped him in many of his matches. “Life will always follow the path laid by destiny. Your fate was set many months ago. Before Money in the Bank. Before you even started here. You were set to go right to the very top but you will never be the biggest name in the company!” GM considers the fact that Derf is in a comparable position to him only a few months ago; able to take a title whenever he wants and on a massive roll, where he seems impossible to back against. “Even now, I’m not sure if can ever take you seriously. You’re still the WZCW jobber, the Rev Pro cub who never got his bite, the Glenda in the mask that no one would be certain about and didn’t want to trust.” In one short sentence; Eric Derf summed up to a tee. “Don’t forget though, you are still the imbecilic monkey who was opening the biggest show of the year while I was making my career in the main event.” “Everything you have done, I can do better. Your losing streak in WZCW pales compared to mine....and I won a World Title in the midst of all this shit!” GM chuckles at his own sarcasm and looks as if he was going to continue but he stops. He turns his head around and standing about a metre behind him is; He bears an uncanny resemblance to the Grand Mystique prior to March this year when he returned from a two month hiatus; Following his return, he unmasked, for reasons for which have never been fully explained. Yet despite the similarities, his mask is a lot more elaborate and in many ways, very different to what we have seen before. The masked man speaks. “You have let yourself down. Your name is muck. You command less than no respect and tarnished your legacy. Congratulations, but I guess it is now time for me to salvage what I can from your poor choices.” He throws something at his feet and it lands with a thud on the dirt by his feet. GM bends down to pick it up. He pauses as he looks at it wistfully. It is his last mask, last seen just after his return, pior to the Independance Day pay-per-view. “You think you can do any better. The kids in this company would eat you alive.” “Not likely; you see there’s a higher authority at work here. And the reasoning behind the here and now is greater than you will ever know.” “So is that it? I’m done?” “You have let yourself down. Your name is muck. You command less than no respect and tarnished the legacy of the name. Congratulations, but I guess it is now time for me to salvage what I can.” GM turns around and looks over the deep chasm beneath them. “Well, it looks like it has been taken of my hands.” “Opportunity is what you make of it. You didn’t do enough of the ample chances that you were given. You have been handed success on an unprecedented level and it became a burden.” “It was no burden, it was.....it is an honor.” GM sounds disheartened. It seems like he actually realises now why this masked mystery is standing behind him now. The next few seconds fly by in what feels like slow motion. The masked man strikes a clubbing forearm blow to the back. GM stumbles but manages to stop himself falling over the cliff edge. Even as his footing gives way, he scrambles around desperately in the dirt. The crystal ball falls down to the floor and instinctively he reaches for it, in a bid to use it to prevent the assault any further. But the masked man anticipates this. He takes a step to his right before punting the crystal ball towards his foe. He catches it cleanly with tremendous force and it has a devastating effect. It makes brutal contact with the side of GM’s face less than a yard away before smashing into thousands of pieces on the dirt. Instantly, the injury is apparent. The entire left side of his face is swollen and covered with blood which pours down his face, pooling on the floor as it falls quickly. He slumps, face down and unconscious as this most brutal of beatings ends. The camera takes in the assailant, the perpetrator of this heinous attack. He is breathing heavily as he stands tall over the bloody mess he has laid forth. “I have been tasked with restoring everything that was great about the name, Grand Mystique. It once stood for something and it was feared, but now, it is being bandied about like a notch on the bedposts for the lesser light in the federation. There is no reverence about the Grand Mystique. No one feels a bitter rush of anxiety when they hear that name any more.” “This will change.” He walks away and looks up as the scene changes around him. The backdrop of this impossibly dark night becomes a canvas of lighter blue and the white moon sinks to his right. In the far distance to his left, the sun is rising, causing the horizon to be lit with oranges and purples. But the backdrop is still murky enough to make all but the dimmest of stars. He becomes calm and smiles just a little as realisation comes to pass. His plan has come to fruition and it couldn’t have gone any better. He has achieved his key aim and reached the next point in his plan. “The questions are ringing in my ears already; I can hear it all so clearly. Why here? Why now? What about WrestleWars? And if it is not already fucking clear enough, the Grand Mystique will be wrestling at the pay-per-view. And maybe you will get your answers. Or maybe not. I will decide nearer the time if I need to explain it to the idiots.” His sarcasm is biting as this mysterious masked, calling himself the Grand Mystique, addresses the camera for the first time in the fresh light of a new day. “Wrestlewars will be noteworthy for one event at least. I am going to shame Eric Derf into brutal insignificance.” “You symbolise everything that I have chosen to fight against; heroic, wholesome and clean-cut, the very antithesis of my existence. I hate that you are so good at getting those miscreants behind you, cheering your name, when they are so quick to boo those whose methods they are too stupid to comprehend. I’m not jealous – you don’t have anything to be envious of. The respect of the fans and your peers is not anything special.” Tarnishing the rest of the roster with the same bitter brush, he speaks with the same acid-tongued, vengeful tone that we were used to seeing from the Grand Mystique. Yet his manner is distinctly cold and aloof, with a holier-than-thou approach that is undeserving given he is such an unknown quantity. “Little grasshopper, you need to evolve. Become better. This is what I have achieved. Did you learn anything from when you lost your precious XTV title to Alex Bowen? How about when you pinned DC and Brian Michaels? You have this huge reputation gained by beating big names and while you have managed to be on a team that against the Grand Mystique, you have yet to beat me.” This new, masked Grand Mystique emphasises the fact that he is referring to himself. The camera moves in and exposes his features for the first time. He has a distinctly natural tan colour to his skin and a wickedly youthful exuberance that shows in refreshed eyes that bath in this new dawn. “Show me a killer instinct Eric. Prove to me that you will do whatever you have to do to get on top. Show why these fans have chosen you over all others to be the man who will lead the MWE in the future. Because will the pg-7 crowd, fan favourite Eric Derf will fight through pain and draw blood to become a champion? I doubt it because he has not been tested. I doubt there is even a scar on his damn face.” This distinctly refreshed “Grand Mystique” is clearly blessed with an aura of self-confidence. Or maybe that is just the sky behind him. Either way, he presents an incorrigible aura that shows his belief that he is capable of beating anyone on any day. “You will get everything from me because I’m sick of you and your ilk pandering to everyone. Your antics will not work and they will force you to turn on them. You will want to escape their ungodly pandering. You will be doomed to walk against them. They will ask of and expect so much that you will not be able to give what they want.” His eyes narrow and his bitter tone continues as he laments Eric’s affiliation with the MWE fans. There is no doubt that they see him as “one of their own”. For someone so normal to achieve that success is bound to get a connection with the audience. Then he thought about that phone video that showed Derf and Josh Parker in the dressing room together was anything but normal. “You believe your own hype, because no one else does. I fall asleep when I see you in the ring and day dream when you talk. Eric Derf is as vanilla as the day is long.” “This will be a war of unequivocal plight, which will seal the door on your past. And no matter what the result, it will set you up for the rest of your career.” To the right, the sun is rising slowly and brightening everything around it. If anything was missing from Derf’s career, it was the fact that he had not shed blood for a purpose. And maybe that was the intention of the Grand Mystique. “A win will ensure you will never be seen as the punk kid who won the XTV title behind a mask, who fluked a win over DC and became Mr MITB. It will show them the Eric Derf who can click his fingers and main event any PPV he wants to; because that is the power you have in whatever hand you are carrying the briefcase in on that day.” Having seen Derf rise up the card in MWE without much effort, the resentment is clear. The Grand Mystique in any form, had to work for his achievements. He walks away, further along the path ahead of him. “Do whatever you have to do to keep yourself going at the top, because you will never appreciate how easy it is to lose momentum. I doubt if you will ever appreciate how lucky you are. I’ve seen men who have lost more than you have ever won in your short, miserable existence.” He speaks with a hint regret that we have not seen until now. Perhaps it is because he feels like he would be working his way from the bottom. Or that he is the one that will need to establish the Grand Mystique as a credible threat again. Looking off into the distance, he has a distant stare in his eyes. “The name of the Grand Mystique will not be dragged through the mud anymore. It will once again symbolise terror and remind everyone why they detest the cold shiver of dread in the pit of the stomach when they hear those two words.” He speaks through gritted teeth, s deep growl that signals his fierce intentions. “The name will stand firm. And the legacy earned by that name will be set in stone, because my actions on Sunday WILL justify the reputation. Eric Derf will have to wait because I will not allow him to make his name at my expense.” He storms away out of sight as the camera fades, with the sun rising faster and faster in the colourful distance, bringing great light and warmth with it. The Grand Mystique is dead, long live the Grand Mystique??? There is little doubt to be had that change has come and MWE may never be the same again. Long may it stay that way, because this is truly refreshing. It takes millions of years for a species but this creature....a monster of epic power and destruction has done it in a matter of months. The Big Bang cometh. And it taketh away *OOC, I tried to make this a killer RP to leave on. I've been more excited about this RP than any other since CG. I hope that reading this, will make everyone hope I can be back one day. If I find the desire and the spark again, then I will. Anyways, I never whored myself out for feedback, but at one point I think I've left a comment on the RP of everyone who will RP for this show. Now more than ever, I'd hope that it will pay itself back in kind. Prove to me that MWE can carry on.*
