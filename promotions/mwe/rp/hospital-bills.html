Hospital Bills

Oct 9 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
*Mayhem Wrestling Entertainment cameras have been searching for Mason Resoan for the past three days, unable to find him. Today, however, they found him in his locker room and cornered him, asking questions and bombarded him with claims from other superstars...Today, MWE received a hospital bill for 47,000 dollars.* QUOTE Mayhem Wrestling Entertainment, we are sorry to inform you that you have just received a bill for 67,000 dollars. Several of your employees have been injured with broken bones and stitches. Here is a list of the injuries: Adam Barrameda - Camera Man - 24 stitches in forehead - 7,500 dollars Kevyn Jaremko - Interviewer #1 - Two broken bones his his ankle - 5,250 dollars Kyle Thomas - Interviewer #2 - Torn ligament in his right bicep and 12 stitches in his knee - 12,000 dollars Ryan Vanderhorn - Security Officer #1 - Broken Collarbone - 4,750 Tyler Smith-Watner - Security Officer #2 - Shattered Bones in left and right legs - 15,000 dollars Anthony Garvin - Camera Man Assistant -Two broken toes - 3,500 dollars *Cameras are rolled in to Richard Covell's office, where Richard is obviously angry. He is flipping through tons of paperwork and is counting in his head, trying not to lose concentration. Suddenly, he throws everything on his desk at the wall to his left and sits down in his leather chair, tries to relax, and puts his arms on the rests of the chair. He is taking random deep breaths and takes a spin in the chair. Finally calmed down he starts to get up, when Mason Resoan walks into the room.* Mason: Did someone have a tantrum or something? This room is a mess, Covell you should really think about tiding up some more. *Covell doesn't say a word and his face turns beat red as he looks at Mason with an evil glare. Mason, uncertain, looks at Covell and figures that he is angry, so he decides to laugh. This turns Covell purple in the face, which makes Mason laugh even harder. Covell rips off his tie and throws it into the pile, which ruffles up some of the papers. A few of them rocket out of the pile at Mason's feet, which Mason looks down at. Suddenly, Mason knows why Covell is so furious, and this makes him laugh harder yet. Now, it looks like Covell is about to spring at Mason, because Mason is in stitches at this point. Mason tries to say something in between chuckles.* Mason: You...look...like a...retard...ha ha...ha... Covell: SHUT YOUR MOUTH...SHUT IT RIGHT NOW! *Mason stops laughing and looks a Covell like he has six heads. He stares Richard Covell, his boss, up and down and just looks baffled.* Mason: Or what, you'll fire me? That's a laugh, you know you can't fire me. You know who you can fire, those jackasses who cornered me. They should know that if you corner someone, they will retaliate. And that is what I did, so f*** them. They don't deserve to be healthy and moving right now. Covell: And what makes you think that you are any better than them? Mason: For one, I don't push around a piece of equipment all day. Two, I don't go around protecting people when I am a weakling. Three, I don't go around asking people questions about stupid s*** that doesn't matter. In fact, I actually do the opposite of all those people. I beat the living hell out of people in the ring, I smash people in the head with big pieces of equipment, and I don't ask people questions, I just win my matches against them. Covell: And that gives you the right to severely injury six of our companies worker. You do realize that MWE has to pay for all of this right? Mason: Stop bitching, MWE pulls in over 5 times that bill in one house show...off of tickets alone. 47,000 dollars in chump change. Covell: Well, if 47,000 dollars in chump change than you can have the pleasure of paying the bill. I mean, it is not that much...right? Mason: Yeah, have fun thinking that I am going to pay for something that shouldn't have even happened in the first place. If you are so intrigued on what I actually think, then come interview me yourself next time. Not some jackass group of lackeys...Oh, look at that, your little camera man just pissed on your nice carpet. You scared buddy? *The camera man is shaking uncontrollably. He is very small and scrawny with absolutely no muscle on his body. He only nodded when Mason spoke to him. Resoan took offense to this.* Mason: Hey asshole, speak when I talk to you. You aren't two years old. Camera Man: Yes...yes... I am... scared. Just please don't throw me against the wall or anything. Covell (Under his breath): That's pathetic. Mason: Yeah, well I don't want to be charged for murder so I won't touch you. In fact, here is three hundred dollars...go fatten yourself up a bit and buy yourself a gym membership...you are pathetic. *Mason throws three bills at the man, which the man squabbles with before getting a grip on the bills. He then runs, as fast as possible, out of the room and down the hallway. Mason sticks his head out of the door and laughs the whole time that the man is in his view.* Covell: Are you finished yet? Mason: Why are your panties in a bunch? You are freaking out about a little bit of money and one of you camera men freaking out at the mere sight of me. What is the problem? Covell: You can be charged for assault, and possibly, be arrested. You injured six men, don't you get that. They are going to be pretty angry. I can not have one my superstars being arrested. It was bad enough when D.C. got arrested, let alone four times. Mason: So just let those men know that they will be fired if they press charges. It is as simple as that. If you don't...well then it would be your own fault if I get arrested. Either way, you are wasting my time. Covell: What else do you have to do? Mason: Watch tapes... *Mason then left his bosses room and Mayhem Wrestling Entertainment headquarters. He hopped in his car and drove off back to the airport, where he flew out to the next arena that MWE was to host a show. --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- The next day... *Mayhem Wrestling Entertainment cameras have once more searched for Mason Resoan. Again, they found him in his locker room, watching video tapes of his opponent for next week...Brian Michaels. This time however, it was only Shawn Spitzer and a camera man.* Mason: Not this again...I mean, come on, can't I just sit and watch my tapes with anyone bothering me. I mean seriously, this isn't a public locker room, this is my personal locker room. Can't you see the little gold plate on the door, it reads Mason Resoan...if you didn't notice. Spitzer: I am sorry Mason, but because the last interview attempt was a failure, Covell sent me out to do the job. He told me to tell you that if you were to do anything out of the ordinary today that you would be suspended for a month. Mason: Covell, always trying to keep me in line. When will he learn that I do what I want...when I want. If I want to punch you in the face, I will, but considering I already had my fun yesterday...well, I just won't be greedy. *Spitzer lets out a sigh of relief as he looks around the room, and does indeed spot the gold plate that says "Mason Resoan" on it. He looks a little disappointed in himself, but shrugs it off.* Spitzer: Well, let's get started shall we. Alright, well I have to get your thoughts on your match with Brian Michaels... Mason: Does anyone pay attention to what I say. I hate having to repeat myself. Brian Michaels is nothing to me. My former mentor, yes. A worth opponent, no. If someone, as big of a name as him, can be as easily fooled as I did to him once before, than this week will be a breeze. An easy win, you could say. Spitzer: Don't you think you should get Michaels a little more credit than what you are. I mean, he is a former two-time world heavyweight champion. Mason: Key-word right there is former. I mean, where is the belt now? It is not with Michaels now is it. He isn't boasting around with it on his should right now, is he? No, right now the belt is on Sonny Williams. How pathetic, Michaels would always talk about how Mayhem Mafia was worse than Rated X, yet if you compare it now, Mayhem Mafia has five total World Championship reigns. Rated X has three, and on top of that Rated X has imploded. *Spitzer nods in agreement has he sees that Mason is right. Spitzer rubs his little goatee and thinks for a second.* Spitzer: So, what do you think of the pick your poison idea, pitting two former Rated X members. Mason: I couldn't give a rats a** to be honest. I don't care if it was Richard Covell, D.C., or f***ing Bugs Bunny who pitted me against Brian, either way I am going to embarrass him on Monday. I mean, Brian is a fool. An intense bada**...that has to be a joke right? I mean, he honestly can't go around saying that he is the best when he walks around acting like a good guy. He was an intense bada** back when Rated X first started, and even then D.C. was still more outrageous then Michaels. Brian Michaels is the biggest Jacka** in Mayhem Wrestling Entertainment's history. Spitzer: So, you think that your match with Brian is going to be easy? Mason: This match is going to be easier than picking apart James Sullivan, maybe not as fun, bust just as easy. Spitzer: So, after your match with Brian, what will you do? Mason: You know, beating the s*** out of people, in specific, Sinc Mercier. Spitzer: Why is that? Mason: Why, do I need a reason. Aren't interviews supposed to right things down or even pay attention to the person they are talking to. I said that I will do whatever I want, but if you need a reason, my reason to you is to make Sinc realize that his title reign is a fluke. I mean, anyone can beat up James Sullivan. Sinc Mercier, I have to agree with D.C. when I say f*** you. You are nothing and have done nothing. I will make you humble. *Mason laughs a little at this, as he adjusts himself in his seat.* Mason: I don't really like seeing people get things they do not deserve, and one I am finished with you, I will make sure I continue to let people know that your title reign was just as bad as D.C.'s sex tape...which was pathetic...but that is besides the point. You see, when you have as much talent as I do, you feel embarrassed when you are put against people that are below you. Right, now, I should already be facing Sonny Williams. I mean, who else do I need to prove myself to? No one, that's who. I can beat anyone I want at anytime, and I make people leave the company before I even show up. Kanto, a blood loving sick son of a b**** left before I even showed up to MWE. I mean, he talks about blood and hitting people with baseball bats, but I actually had someone killed, so what did Kanto actually prove. Oh yeah, he proved that he was also a jackass along with the rest of the retards here in MWE. *Spitzer lets out a little chuckle.* Mason: And what the f*** are you laughing at. Better yet, who the f*** gave you permission to make noise? Spitzer: All I am asking is, don't you think that those are bold words for not even competing in a match yet? Mason: Are you questioning my talent? Are you honestly challenging my abilities? Do you want to be embarrassed along with everyone else in the company. Seriously, the only person in this company with half a brain is Richard Covell, and that is because he hired me. Soon, people will remember that I am the biggest asset in the whole industry... *Mason paused for a little bit.* Mason: And if you didn't know, now you know! Now get out of my sight! *The screen fades to black...*
