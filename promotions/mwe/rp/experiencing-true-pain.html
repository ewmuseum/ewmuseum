Experiencing True Pain

Jan 20 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
*Mason Resoan is sitting in a very large room, laying in a bed covered with maroon sheets and laying on several tempramedic pillows. His whole body is covered in them and his head lays gently on three as he relaxes with no one around him. On the television screen he is watching a replay of last week's Monday Night Mayhem, in which he beat Joey Dallas viva disqualification. The video plays on as Mason tries to relax, his whole body simply sinking into the fabric of his custom made pillows. The camera pans over, closer to Mason, so that there is visibility of the match. It is at the scene right as Mason is being laid out on the table. Mason re-adjusts himself in bed, careful as he does so, as to not irritate any body pains, and the camera continues to watch the video play.* QUOTE Dallas nods and both Sinc and him go on two turnbuckles diagonally across from the table, both raise a dominant fist in the air, then they miraculously dive into a double forearm drop onto Mason through the table. Mason receiving double the impact. Sinc’s forearm hitting his face, Dallas’s hitting his abdomen as all three men fall through the table. Mason now unconscious in a blackened state. Sinc and Dallas barely moving. Medics begin rushing towards the announce table leaving Ferrera and Adams speechless with blank expressions. The camera does a few final minutes of rolling as medics and EMT’s check on the three athletes. All three in great pain. Suddenly the camera then fades into an unreleased video of Sinc and Mason. *Mason looks at the video with a sense of confusion, almost, and rewinds the tape playing it once more. The scene flashes upon Mason's giant 72' LCD High Definition Television and clear as day, Mason revisits the punishment he received just days previous. He again re-adjusts himself in his bed, making sure his entire body was on the pillows, letting them soak in the relief. He cracks his neck as he looks to the side and sees two things. One, his arm, carefully placed in a sling. And two, on a oak side table, lays the Mayhem Wrestling Entertainment United States Championship. Mason looks at it ever so intently, showing no focus to anything else. He then, with a muster of strength and will, lifts himself to a sitting vertical basis and reaches over to the table, placing a hand on the title before picking it up and holding it gently in his right hand. He stares at it for a second before sliding his legs to the right and standing up out of bed. He, with slight difficulty, places the championship title belt on his shoulder as he once again cracks his neck.* *Mason also slightly shivers as he adjusts to the pain of his acing body. Not only that, but in Canada, since everything is colder, it is currently thirty-five degrees in the house, and Mason is not wearing a shirt. As he makes sure that the title won't fall of his shoulder, he rubs his six-pack trying to gain some warmth as he no longer has his expensive heated-blanket covering his body. He once again cracks his neck, trying to somehow get feeling back into it, but there is really no progress. Instead, Mason walks all the way across his room, and with a great deal of will and suffering, he bends down to open his mini-fridge, which adds cold to his already frozen body. He pulls his dark-blue sweatpants up trying to stay warm, but decides to tuff' it out as he makes painful movements to retrieve a soda, which he then placed on the table where his United States Championship once lay. Mason opened the soda, took a sip, and placed it back on the table. With a tasty sigh, Mason turned and sat on his pillows, looking straight into the camera. The camera focused in on his chest area where his arm lay in a blue sling against his blackened body, due to bruises. The video of his match plays on repeat in the background.* Mason: People have said that I don't know what pain feels like...they said that I could never really understand the concept of what it truly means to be in pain. At one point in time, I used to deny this fact and tell people that I knew exactly what pain was. I knew what it was, because I received it in my training. I tried to use conditioning and training as an excuse for what pain really was, but in all honesty, even through all my battles, I did not have a clue...not a damn clue. *Mason shakes his head in shame and disappointment. He slightly moves his arm in the sling, trying to get some feeling back, just as he did when he slowly moved his neck. The championship moved a little when he did this, but he simply placed it back in place when he shrugged his shoulder. Mason's tone was a little more ominous now, with more understanding in the pitch.* Mason: After Monday Night Mayhem this week, I realized all this. I realized that pain is an ugly thing...something I do not like. I found out that when it comes to pain, I do not exactly have the most experience...but after Monday Night Mayhem, I can now truly, with all my heart and soul, say that I know what pain is. Never before have I been reduced to pandering to my every injury, but now I am. Now, because of two men, I have to lay in bed all day, at doctor's order. I head to spend close to 10,000 dollars on medical bills alone, let alone all the s*** that came with it. On my bed right now, I have over twenty pillows I was TOLD to buy, each at 150 dollars a piece. A blanket that cost more than normal people's cars and a bed that, with the money, I could feed a small family of four for two weeks. All this money, this time, this pain...because of two men. *Mason struggles, but leans over and picks up his can of Pepsi again, taking a long drink out of it. He is clearly refreshed, but the pain still shows in every pore of his body as he tries to regroup himself. He places the can back on the table and he continues to try and move the arm in the sling.* Mason: I was in my match with Joey Dallas and we were fighting like our jobs depended on it. I called it...if you recall...I claimed it to be match of the night and it was. Despite all the hostility between us, we put on a hell of a show. Despite Joey cheap shotting me in the private area, we put on a hell of a show...and despite me getting caught in a devastating submission maneuver...we put on a hell of a show. But a show will only get you so far, a match is what I said we were going to have, but it wasn't a match. A definition of a match, in my eyes, is a competition in which one or more competitors compete to a clean finish, in which one clear victor is declared. Joey Dallas and I put on a hell of a show...but we had no match. Joey, on a future date, we will have a match, not a show...but a match, and we will see who the better man is...and next time...there will be no...and I repeat NO interference. *There is obviously tension, verging anger in Mason's voice as he takes another drink of his Pepsi. He again can be seen enjoying it, but still, wrinkles of pain are seen all throughout his body as he moves his arm about the sling. He is progressing, moving it a little more each time. Mason, taking his attention away from the sling, moves his eyes towards the giant High Definition television just as Sinc Mercier comes to the ring with a steel chair in hand. Mason points to the screen.* Mason: That man, Joey, is the interference I am talking about. He is what prevented our show from becoming a match. He is what prevented a great showdown from happening...your protege...the man you "Saved" erased all the potential that could be even mustered in one's mind when he came out to the ring...with a steel chair no less. Sinc Mercier ruined greatness and for that, I am furious. *More intensity is heard in Mason's voice, but the tension and pressure is obviously taking its effect on Mason's body, as he cringes in pain. He tries to relax again by taking yet another sip, but to no avail, it doesn't work. Mason chugs for a little bit and only a little of the beverage is left. Mason is clearly aggravated at this point and as he puts the can down, there is a sign that he almost crushed it as their are dents all over.* Mason: Sinc Mercier, a man...no...a coward, who I have beaten twice came down and pulled the plug to a match so epic, it would have made even the most great matches being contested. I had my expectations and they were almost fulfilled, but Sinc...Sinc Mercier came down and ruined it. He smashed me with a chair, technically, saving you Mr. Dallas. HE "Saved" you from going through a table and for that...you should be grateful. But Sinc was not stopped there. Instead of helping out his master, he decided that he was going to try and punish me...possibly for revenge. So, he smashed me in the head again...and again, nearly knocking me out cold. Now, any individual would have been down and out, but I struggled and was thrown into the ring, staring down a cold, unforgiving, stainless steel ladder. And this ten-foot ladder was opened up before my very eyes as my arm was inserted, with me not being able to defend myself. And then, the beat down continued. BOOM! One chair shot. BOOM! A second chair shot. BOOM! A final chair shot to my arm...which is why this arm *points to arm* is in a sling. Two sick individuals decided to inflict punishment like I have never experienced before into my body...into my arm...and you know what, I felt it. I felt it more than I ever...EVER wanted to! *Mason has a small vein in his right wrist and up his his lower left neck. He is obviously in pain. He is obviously angry. However, if you didn't hear his voice you wouldn't be able to tell how angry or intense he really was. Mason finished off the rest of his soda, but before he could put the can down, he had crushed it on accident, but didn't even notice it. The championship belt once again began to slowly slide down his right shoulder as he cracked his neck and moved his arm again. His arm movements were picking up speed.* Mason: And despite my pleas and despite my innocence, despite my goal to simply win, I was further punished as I was violently thrown out of the ring. Joey Dallas, you placed me on the same table we fought on just minutes earlier before Mr. Mercier decided to interrupt our beautiful match. You and Sinc both went back to the ring and I rested my muscles and tried to relax thinking my punishment was over, but I was soon surprised as you both took leaps...of faith? No, of pain, as you destroyed my muscles and my body with simultaneous elbow drops sending us all crashing through this announce table to the floor. Wreckage, carnage, and three broken bodies lay on the floor, with me, Mason Resoan, receiving the worst of it all. Now...days later, I am still feeling the effects of that night, where I experienced real pain for the first time. My body is still recovering from that horrible assault and I have no idea if I will be ready, but I do one thing. You guys, Sinc and Joey, have no idea about tables...you have no ideas about ladders...and you don't know about chairs. You don't know about the three weapons you used to destroy me on Monday...but I do. *Mason reaches deep inside himself and pulls together some strength as he relieves his arm of the sling. He moves it around more and gets feeling back into it as it starts to recover rapidly. Mason looks around and sees that his MWE United States title had fallen down. He picks it back up and places it on the table it sat on before as he reached underneath his bed. Underneath, nothing could be seen but black, but out from underneath he pulled out a table.* Mason: This, is something that I am familiar with. This is something that I have been through many times and something I have broken many times...with other bodies. It really does dish out pain, but not like on Monday. And ever since that crash and burn situation, I thought about tables. I thought about what they are intended for...then how they were used...and how much I like them. This, in front of me, is a standard MWE table. There are plenty found under the ring and they are probably one of the most devastating devices to dish out punishment...punishment that could hurt the chest and abdomen...or the head, which brings me to this. *Mason reaches back under the bed and finds a chair. He sets it up and sits in it as he puts his legs together and sits in a very mannerly way.* Mason: See, chairs are very interesting. Chairs are used to inflict pain to the head and to many different limbs. On Monday, Sinc Mercier, you displayed this as you smashed me over the head with one many times. And as much as that I hurt, there was still something that I can say hurt much more... *Mason reaches underneath the bed and retrieves the final object, a ten-foot ladder. He places it sideways on the top of the table and sat back in the chair.* Mason: This final object, by far, is the most painful of all. My arm can verify that and I am sure many other people can verify it as well. There is something about ladders, that for some reason, shoot fear into you. For me, ladders are wondrous objects that can be used many different ways and inflicting pain is just one of them. And that is exactly why I have these three objects in my room now...is because I need to figure out how each can be used. How I might be able to use them to get back...to show you Mr. Dallas or you Mr. Mercier, just how painful these can really be. Trust me, they are not pillows, but they are in fact objects of destruction...and destruction goes hand in hand with revenge...which, after Monday, I have wanted on you both. You see, there gets to a point where nothing else really matters and I said last week that it was Sara Whitaker's attacker. Well, after the maliciousness of you both and your vindictive behavior, I have narrowed it down to one of you...so revenge is what is on my mind. I don't care which of you did this horrible act, but it won't matter...because I get a double blast of sin, I get you two both this week. *Mason leaves the chair and resumes his position on his bed, throwing some of the sheets away so he could sit comfortably on his custom pillows. He grabs a remote and turns of the television just as the scene where he was being thrashed came on. Mason tossed the remote from his hand and instead replaced it with the MWE United States championship.* Mason: This week, I don't get you Sinc and I don't get you Dallas...I get you both. Now, I may not be 100%, as I doubt any of us are, but that is where my partner comes in...Ace Cannon. You see, I find it only fitting that I beat Dallas and Mercier beat Cannon. One win from both sides of the ring and the two teams will collide soon...very soon. Dallas, I said that we were like Stallone v.s. Schwarzenegger...well, that may still stand, but Dallas & Mercier v.s. Resoan & Cannon is like The Williams Sisters v.s. the Manning Brothers...It is to much to handle in one match, but we will try it and we will see who is better, because his two losses aside, we will win. You have both beaten Cannon, but I have beaten both of you...and if memory serves me correctly, it isn't like this is the first time Cannon has faced you, now is it Dallas. Long history...wouldn't want that to get in your way, now would you? *Mason wags his finger in the air and makes a "tut-tut" sounding noise.* Mason: But to be honest, I don't care what you guys want at this point. After Monday, all my remorse for either one of you has gone out the window. You two crossed the line and you made yourselves personal with me...a mistake that only few people make. See, if I can beat both of you, then who says I can't beat you both at the same time. What says that I can't make history by continuing to prove myself as a worthy triple crown...who says I can't keep my undefeated streak going...who says it, huh? No one, that is who, because everyone knows that I can and will make history, that I will prove myself as the best triple crown champion ever, and they know...most certainly, that I will keep my streak alive. *Mason cracks his neck once again finally getting full feeling back into his body. His blood is flowing and he is warm once more, ignoring any and all pain his body.* Mason: Ace Cannon...together, we will destroy Dallas and Mercier. Together, for one night, we will show them that we don't play around and that we will hit them with everything we got. You can have Dallas and I'll take Mercier...because Sinc, if it means sacrificing my girlfriend temporarily, I will take you out for good. You have been in my business too long and three months is too long to be dealing one someone of your stature. You are weak...you are incompetent...you are simple minded and you are foolish enough to be under the control of one Joey Dallas, even though I beat him. Next time, get help from someone else, because when you can't beat me and he can't beat me...well, something has to be wrong. And speaking of wrong, I need to speak with Ace Cannon. *Mason stands up off the bed and straps the MWE United States Championship around his waist and begins to walk toward his door. The cameras follow him as he walks down a very large hallway, completed with a polished hardwood floor. Pictures hang about the entire way as they continue down, but as they pass the camera drifts right and left capturing shots of some of the pictures. A lot, are visibly seen as Sara Whitaker's photos, however there are a few of Mason from the original MWE. His world title win, his annihilation of Aiden Copane, his days in the indy fed. The camera snaps back into focus, however, as Mason proceeds to walk down a flight of stairs, sixteen steps big, made of all oak wood. It seems that throughout the house, oak wood can be found, as upon reaching the floor of the ground floor, there is more oak in the furniture and in several doors. Mason continues to parade around his house until he reaches a small hallway in which he walks down and at the end lies yet another door that Mason walks through. They are now in the fabulous garage of Mason Resoan where several cars sit. Mason walks down some small cold steps, littered with dust, and walks to a random car. It is a black 69' Camaro SS with fine trim work and an amazing paint job. The garage door opens up and Mason drives out as the scene cuts to black...* *The scene fades back in as Mason parks his car in front of another house, a little smaller than his own. He steps outside, stretches, and looks around at his surroundings. He sees a couple of things that catch his eye like beautiful hedges and a well kept front lawn. There is also a small stone pathway a little farther up the black asphalt driveway, which is where Mason turns to walk down. He reaches a large door with a silver knocker and a doorbell. He tries the door and it is already open, so he enters without knocking or alerting anyone that he is there. Inside everything is almost as big and luxurious as Mason's mansion. There is plenty of designer furniture, custom televisions and even a bar like him. He can also see that this house has a large backyard, as well, set with a pool that is heated. He spins his head to attention as a yell is directed towards him.* ???: What the hell do you think you are doing in my house? Mason: Don't piss yourself Cannon, I am here on good terms. I'm here about our match. *Cannon is halfway down his staircase and stops, hops over the railing and lands face to face with Mason staring him dead in the eyes.* Cannon: I...don't...need...your...help! I don't need anyone's help so you can just turnaround and lea- Mason: Leave? Leave after I just spent all this time and money trying to get here...no. I am not leaving. Cannon: Well, I have nothing to say to you...I'll win our match on Monday, don't you worry your little head. *Mason had turned his back, admiring some of the nicer things in Cannon's house, but spun around and looked at Cannon. He gave a slight chuckle and a disappointed shake of his head.* Mason: You'll win this match...you, the man who got beat not once, but twice in two matches. The man who lost to Sinc Mercier and Joey Dallas. You will win this match...no, I don't think so. You see, there is more to this match than either of us can handle by ourselves. I may have beaten them both individually...but together...it is different. Together they have chemistry and the only way you and I...we can beat them is if we are just that much better. Cannon: So then what do you propose? Mason: I propose that you get your head out of the clouds and focus, because screw ups this match means a loss. I...don't...lose. *Cannon takes a quick glance at Mason's United States Championship and cracks his neck.* Cannon: And what makes you, Mr. United States Champion, believe that you won't be the one making the mistake? Why is it me? You aren't perfect or invincible. You learned that last week. *Mason is getting visibly frustrated with Cannon, especially at the mention of his terrible beat down from last week. Cannon has a "what now" smirk on his face.* Mason: Listen, we can sit here and bicker all we want, but the fact of the matter is this...Dallas and Mercier are good as a team, not as individuals. We need to break them down...we need to isolate them...and we need to destroy them. You, as you have probably been told before, are evil in every sense of the word. You will decimate anyone and everyone if you have to and that methodical punt usually does the job. We need to use that to our advantage this week. *Cannon walks over to his freezer and pulls from it a chilled mug and some unknown beverage. He begins to pour it and smirks again as he says...* Cannon: You don't think I know that? You spent all this time to get here and tell me that. Well, listen up Mason, I have known that for quite some time. I am smart enough to realize things like that and I have enough intelligence to be able to put them into action. Despite what you may think, I am not just the next cocky bastard to waltz through MWE, I know what I am doing. Mason: Then why didn't you do it at Adrenaline Rush? *Mason must have pushed a button as Cannon stops pouring the drink and walks around the counter to face Mason again, putting his nose on his nose.* Cannon: THAT WAS A MISTAKE! Nothing more...nothing less. Dallas beat me by a second and it was luck. It will not happen a second time! *Mason retaliates with just as much intensity.* Mason: Then how do you explain last Monday? Was that a mistake as well! No, you lost your confidence in your match with Dallas...and that will not happen again. This week, I am your partner and we are going to win at any and all costs. I don't care if you break your arm or if you lose a tooth or even if you lose your girlfriend... I am not losing! Cannon: You don't get it...do you? You are no better than me. I am not going to screw it up this week, this time, I am going to get the pin. Mason: Oh, you would like to think that wouldn't you. Cannon, I said it before and I'll say it again, they are ten times as good together as they are apart...we won't beat them if we don't have intensity, cooperation, and chemistry. And the only way we are going to get to that point is if you admit that this week is harder than the weeks before. Sinc and Dallas are not going to mess around and let me tell you, I am not either. *There is a small pause where they just stare at each other, letting their egos soak into each other, trying to find some common denominator.* Mason: Listen...we know what we need to do. We have all the facts laid out and all the plans thought out, but we need to put it into action. Do I like working with you...no, but I am going to suck it up, as are you. *There is a slight pause, in which Mason licks his lips and gives a small breath.* Mason: So, with that said, it is now time for us to have one small thing in common...a victory over Sinc and Dallas. Something that will finally make Sinc realize that he is not being "saved" and we shall make Dallas realize he is no savior. *Cannon pushes Mason aside for a second, looking straight into the camera with his eyes peering deep inside, almost as if he is trying to connect with Joey Dallas.* Cannon: Dallas...despite what may have happened before, despite what Mason is saying now, and despite my match with Sinc Mercier...one fact remains. That fact is my persistence. Until I have beaten you, I will not sleep. Until I have beaten you, I will not blink. Until I have beaten you, nothing will get in my way...not Sinc Mercier, not Randy Covell...no one. We have unfinished business to settle this Monday and it will only get worse from there! *Mason now retakes center stage in front of the camera and cracks his neck as he shifts his title belt.* Mason: This week, Joey Dallas and Sinclair Mercier, you will face the perfect combination of skill and brutality. Of intelligence and of power. Of wit and cleverness. You will see what happens when you take on the combination of "The Franchise" Ace Cannon and "The Canadian Classic" Mason Resoan...you will see that no matter how bad you may beat me...I am going to come back and destroy you...decimation awaits this Monday! *There is a pause as Ace Cannon is seen walking away in the background. Mason looks deep into the camera and said in his most cocky tone.* Mason: And if ya didn't know...now ya know! *Mason then pushed the camera away with his hand as he walked slowly to Ace Cannon, when then decided to proceed to leave the house to begin their training.*
