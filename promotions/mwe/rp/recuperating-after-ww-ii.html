Recuperating after WW-II; 5 Man Mayhem RP

Nov 8 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The camera turns on and we see Milenko sitting in a chair in an abandoned house. He is covers in dried up blood, scabs and burns from his Hardcore match at WW-II. He also as a gauze bandage wrapped around his head that is soaked in blood. He can't move very well but he manages to look up at the camera and he starts to talk in a whisper. 'The Great' Milenko Once again I did what I said I was going do didn't I? I said Alex Bowen would not walk out of Wrestle Wars and he didn't. I was bloodied beyond all recognition but at least I was able to walk out of the ring. Alex was taken out on a stretcher with a big hole in his chest. After that viscous match he still wants to step into the ring with me 1 more time. Thinking about Alex getting beat by him again makes him smile a sadistic smile. When he smiles scabs on his forehead open and blood starts to trickle down his face. 'The Great' Milenko This match is a 5-man Mayhem match and I only have 1 partner while Alex has 2. My partner is Billy Joe Barker and this is his debut match in MWE. As he mentions Barker Milenko grimaces and more scabs on his face open up and blood starts pouring down his face a little faster. 'The Great' Milenko I don't know much about this guy and frankly I don't really care. All I know is that he is a redneck from Texas and those 2 things are enough to make me hate him with all my soul. He stops talking and looks directly at the camera. When he starts to talk again he speaks in a normal voice. 'The Great' Milenko Barker this is your first match and if you get in my way I will make your life a living hell so just watch yourself. He stops talking about Barker ans gets a thoughtful look on his face tinged with a sadistic smile. After a little while he starts talking about his 3 opponents. 'The Great' Milenko Enough about my partner lets talk about the three people across the ring. First there's JJ Executioner. He's had many shots for the X-TV Title and he never got the job done. When he steps into the ring with me he will know the true meaning of the word pain. As he is talking he looks up at the camera and a fire can be seen in his eyes. He continues to talk but he is looking right at the camera as he does it. 'The Great' Milenko JJ you can paint rats up to look like me and chop them in half all you want it doesn't change the fact that when we step into the ring together it will be you who gets cut in half and I will be left standing tall. I want to show you a couple things in case you forgot. Milenko wheels a monitor out from the shadows and presses play. QUOTE David Ferrera: Why the hell would they come back here?! Ryan Adams: I think Milenko wants to get his hands on Alex’s weapons, David! Milenko looks around for a few seconds before putting Alex down gently. Ryan Adams: Wh- what’s this?! Milenko waits for Alex to get up slowly, then all of a sudden he knees Alex hard in the stomach. He holds him in a pedigree-like position and looks at the crowd slowly. Ryan Adams: What’s he- oh no. Oh GOD NO. This is too much. David Ferrera: I can’t believe we’re agreeing again, but a Tempest from the top of the ramp all the way to down there?! He’ll kill Alex as well as himself! Milenko gets an evil smile on his face for a few seconds before proceeding to jump off the ramp. Ryan Adams & David Ferrera: HOLY SH*T!!!! The crowd erupts loudly before an abrupt explosion is heard, followed by pyrotechnics on the side of the ramp flame up. David Ferrera: What the hell?! The camera hits a closeup and we see a complete mess down there as Milenko and Bowen are both lying on the floor in piles of blood. They broke through a table and we see some other people lying down there as well with headphones on their ears, clutching their chests or heads. We also see what appears to be a couple of computers and other machinery, most of it broken as sparks fly all around. The fans start chanting “God Al-migh-ty! *clap clap, clap clap clap*” Ryan Adams: Good lord! They just completely obliterated the pyrotechnicians’ setup! QUOTE Ryan Adams: Both men are bleeding profusely. Milenko’s face and the back of his head have been busted open at the beginning of this match, and Bowen’s back got quite some guts after the powerbomb through the back of that pickup truck. David Ferrera: And after that Tempest that ended all the way down on the pyrotechnics programming area, both men have been severely injured; I can definitely see Bowen’s face all scratched up, and the right side of his face has a few- well, what seem to be holes in them! Accompanied by slight burns marks from the sparks! Ryan Adams: Correct, and I can’t even tell if Milenko’s eyes are open; that slam must have re-opened the face wound he attained earlier as his face is now looking like a red waterfall! Alex Bowen has managed to get to the ring and has rolled into it. Milenko and Alex are now standing face to face. Ryan Adams: This is it! Alex reaches for the barbed wire bat, but Milenko steps on it. He is shaking his middle finger in a "no" fashion just before landing a thunderous clothesline. He then proceeds to pick Bowen up, and using all of his remaining energy, moves towards the turnbuckle with Bowen and climbs it. David Ferrera: He can NOT be serious! Milenko proceeds to execute the Tempest onto the barbed wire baseball bat! Ryan Adams: NO! NO! TEMPEST ON THE BARBED WIRE! IT'S OVER! Milenko turns Alex over and tosses his arm onto the blood puddle emanating from Bowen's chest. http://z4.invisionfree.com/MWEntertainment/index.php? Welcome to your control panel 1. 2.. 3!!! The bell rings. Fred Carry: Your winner... Milenko! When the monitor turns off Milenko pushes it out of the way. He looks back at the camera with that sadistic smirk on his face and an even bigger fire in his eyes. 'The Great' Milenko JJ I played that so you can see exactly what I'm capable of. I may hate Alex more than any other person on this planet but I can and I will keep my eyes on th ball and that is making all 3 of you feel the pain I can inflict. The smirk leaves his face but the fire stays in his eyes as he talks about Mason Blade. 'The Great' Milenko Mason me and you have a lot in common. We both wear a mask and we both got into this business for revenge. Where you wanted revenge for you father I wanted revenge on my brother for letting my childhood be filled with torture and pain. That aside we are on opposite sides of the ring and you will feel the pain I live with everyday of my life. you lost the #1 contenders match and you will lose this one, the only difference is if I have any say in the matter this will be your last. The smirk that was on his face comes back but this time it's a full blown sick and twisted smile. The fire in his eyes is full of hatred and he talks in a choked whisper. 'The Great' Milenko Once again I step into the ring with Alex Bowen. Even after that beating I gave him at WW-II they want to put him in the ring with me again. What is wrong with MWE management? They obviously have have no concern for his safety if they want to put him in the ring with me after what I did to him. Milenko grabs the camera and pulls it so close to his face all you can see is the fire and hatred in his eyes as he talks directly to his brother. 'The Great' Milenko Alex if I were you I would not come the Mayhem this Monday. Just think about your wife and kids. Do you want your wife to be a widow and your daughters to grow up without there father because your to stupid to know when to walk away? If you step into the ring with me that's what will happen because I will make sure you don't walk out of that ring. Milenko lets go of the camera and turns his head around and says something to someone out of camera range. After a few seconds 2 men walk into the shot and they stand on either side of Milenko. 'The Great' Milenko before I go I wanted to introduce the world to 2 of the newest members of the MWE roster. I met them while i was living on the streets and they hate the world just as much as I do. on my left we have The Wraith and on my right we have The RingMaster and together we are the Triple Threat. I brought them up to MWE to help me make everyone's life a living hell and to inflict the pain that we live with everyday. The camera stays focused on the three men as it fades t black.
