Coming In and Clearing Some Things Up; A Sara Whitaker RP

Oct 30 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
*MWE cameras are seen rolling down the streets of Toronto, Canada, to a nice home of large size. There is no sign of Mason Resoan, but there, standing on a balcony on the second floor, is Sara Whitaker, his new girlfriend. She is seen talking on the phone, most likely with Mason, and she walks inside. MWE Cameras roll up a little closer to the home and knock on the door. Darell is with the camera crew today, looking all spiffy in a new designer suit, with shiny new black shoes, and a very nice bracelet that dangles around his wrist. Sara opens the doors, whispers something into the phone, and hangs up, before allowing the MWE crew into the house.* Darell: Is this your home? Sara: Yeah, but not for long. Mason and I are moving in together. Darell: That's nice...I guess. Sara: You bet your ass it's nice, now I never have to drive 30 something miles to get to his home for whatever reason. And I don't have to worry about paying bills or anything. Honestly, it's the smartest thing I thing I have ever done. Darell: Well, I guess you know why we are here, right? Sara: An interview would be my first guess. Am I right? Darell: Yes, you are, and I hope to god this will be better than when we attempted to interview Lindsay. Sara: Don't worry, I will go easy. Just don't piss me off or ask anything that might make me question what you are up to...got it. Darell: Of course, no problem. Just one more thing. Sara: What is it? Darell: Where do we set up the interview section? Sara: Right in the backyard I guess. It's nice out there, just walk through the back doors. I will be back, let me go fix up while you set up, and by the way. If I notice anything is missing when I get back, I am telling Mason. *Darell nods and trots away. He looks over his shoulder to make sure that Sara is upstairs and he runs into her dining room. The house is extremely tidy and there are pictures covering all the walls. Pictures of Mason and Sara, just Mason and just Sara. There also framed pictures of what appear to be Sara's family. These frames are different as they are in metal frames to show significance. They walk a little further through the house into the kitchen where pots and pans hang from the ceiling and there is cooking equipment everywhere. The refrigerator, much like the dining room, has pictures covering the whole front and side of the 'fridge. They proceed a little further and find the sliding glass doors which they walk through. Upon walking out they see a very large pool and chairs and benches all of the place. On the opposite side of the pool, the crew see a large bar and some stools. They decide to set up over there.* Darell: This house must have cost a fortune. It's nice and tidy, as well, despite the gigantic size. Mason picked a good one here. *Ten minutes later the crew has finished their set-up and Sara walks back out and is impressed with the professionalism of the crew. All the cameras are perfectly surrounding the bar area and there are two stools in the middle of the whole circle of equipment. Sara walks over and sits down on the stool. She fixes her beige skirt and enjoys the comfortable seat. She waits as Darell sets up some mics on her chest, which makes Darell blush, and then his own. He checks to make sure everything is running right, then takes a seat, and begins the interview.* Darell: Sara, thank you, once again, for letting us take time out of your day to interview you. So, getting right down to business, I have to ask, what are your thoughts on Mason Resoan? Sara: Well, I expect you to say wonderful things about him, which I will, but I have to say this. He is a cocky prick...but that is why I love him. He is cocky and at times a complete douche bag, but he can back it up and that is why I picked him to be my new boyfriend. Only a man of his caliber can satisfy my needs. And as for his loss at Wrestle Wars II, well that doesn't mean a thing. Triple Threats are bulls*** and everyone knows that. In soon time you will see why Mason Resoan is, in fact, called the 'Canadian Classic." Actually, he has already displayed it, when he decimated Brian Michaels. And that, Darell, is just a sample of what he is capable of. *Darell is blown away by the sharpness of Sara's voice and her commanding attitude. She brushes her hair back and takes a pause while she waits for the next of Darell's numerous questions. Darell adjusts his shirt and hopes he isn't blushing bright red.* Darell: So, how did you exactly meet Mason. We haven't seen you before. Sara: It was after he beat Brian Michaels is his re-debut. After the show, he was in the backstage area, and being the V.I.P that I am, I of course had backstage passes. He greeted me and I greeted back and we exchanged numbers. He left, because he was tired, and I stayed up all night excited. The next day, I called him, we met up, and now I am going to live with him. Darell: Do you have any doubts about his match against Brian Michaels, D.C., and David Whitman this week on Monday Night Mayhem? Sara: Not at all, Mason could tell you this himself, but I don't mind backing him up. He will win this match, and would have won his match this week even if he, himself, was in the handicap position. D.C. is weak and couldn't do what Mason did, and that was beat Brian Michaels. This week, it will simply be two birds with one stone. As for David Whitman, he is just a distraction. If he gets in the way, I am sure my baby will have no problem tossing him aside just like he will do with D.C and Brian Michaels. Darell: Do you know his thoughts on his tag team partners? Sara: I thought this interview was about me. If you want Mason's thoughts than go ask him yourself. Otherwise, start asking me some questions. Darell: My apologies, let me try again. What are your thoughts on D.C's girlfriend? Sara: All I have to say is that if she tries anything to interfere with Mason, I will destroy her like Mason will D.C. No help allowed, not even if it is me. Let the men fight their own battles...and let the women fight ours. Darell: And how about Sinc Mercier? Why all the hate on him, I saw that you were disgusted with him last week. *Sara laughs a little.* Sara: Sinc Mercier is probably the most worthless piece of garbage in Mayhem Wrestling Entertainment today. He doesn't know how to keep a title, he doesn't know how to keep a girlfriend, and he sure as hell doesn't know how to wrestle. His attempts to make a dent in Mason was more of a joke than anything else...actually, it was an insult to Mason. Sinc, will never hold another title ever again. I will make sure of it, Mason will make sure of it, and I bet you Sonny Williams won't have it happen either. I don't usually use the "F" word, but I have no problem saying this. F*** you Sinc Mercier. You are a b**ch if I have ever seen one and I can't believe that you let Alejandro f***ing Rodriguez get a pinfall over you. That, is irredeemable, and I hope that you don't sleep at night because of it. I mean honestly, Mason put your foot on the ropes for Christ sake and you still lost. How much more can you suck? *Darell, again, is blown away by Sara. Not for her voice, but more by her passion and enthusiasm. He looks around at the rest of the crew, and they, as well as he was, were intrigued and hung onto every single word Sara spit out.* Darell: Well, you obviously don't like Sinc Mercier, so what are your thoughts on him getting a title shot after being pinned on Sunday? Sara: It's disgusting and honestly, I would like to slap the person who made that match over and over again. I mean how does that make sense. Give the person who got pinned, and the person who didn't deserve the title in the first place, a title rematch. If anything, it should be Alejandro v.s. Mason for the United States title this week. That triple threat was messy business and because of it, Mason was screwed out of a win and the title. Mason said that he was going to take that short little mexican to school, and if it wasn't for that retard Sinc Mercier, he would have. Sinc Mercier, you better hope you never get into a match with Mason ever again, unless you have an army, because if not... then you better start eating your vitamins and saying your prayers, because you will be destroyed. *Sara takes another breath and relaxes a bit. She brushes back her hair again and starts to adjust her bracelets at the same time Darell adjusts his own. Darell cracks his neck and stands up, as does Sara. They shake hands.* Darell: Once again, thank you for the interview and we wish you the best of luck with your future with Mason. Sara: No problem, oh and once last thing, don't were girl's accessories if you can't pull it off. Now get out of my house. *Sara ushers them out and walks back into her house, relaxes, and sits down and calls Mason again.* ---Fade to black---
