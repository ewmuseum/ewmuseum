Breaking the mould; MoP Tournament vs Sebastian

Sep 25 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
QUOTE To recap what's been going on: CSB finds out he's the father to a newborn baby with no living mother or relatives. It starts to plague his mind until he disobeys his lawyer Harold and goes to the hospital, getting caught in the moment and taking the kid and driving off, getting into a high speed chase. He gets bailed out by Harold and MWE's Zack Crash, and now him and his child's future is a mystery, though Harold said he'd do his best to get him custody. CSB: I'm starting to see a pattern grow here. Ya' know, me sitting in your office and you looking at me like I'm an asshole. Know what I mean? Harold: ... CSB: Roldy? Harold: ...I can't wait to retire. 10AM, Wednesday morning and I'm laid back in one of Roldy's rustic leather chairs, located in his office on the south side of Boston. Rold sits opposite me with a stack of papers reaching high enough to have any ex-convict worried and bags under his eyes worthy of any hangover. Yet despite this Disney-like picturesque scene, I can't help but keep a smile on my face. Sure, I'm in no pretty position but the past couple of days have been a pretty good stab at turning it around. My new tag partner, Zack Crash, bailed me out of jail. I hear that's how all great tag teams start. But legit, it's good to know in a business of conmen and liars that I've got somebody within it that’s got my back still. You don't make that many friends here. Or maybe that's just because I'm an ass. Then there was Monday, when I did something that's plagued my mind for so long I can't remember what it's like to NOT think about. I beat Zak Warner. Friend, brother, whatever you wanna' call him - after seeing what he did to Kevin at Crowning Glory, beating Zak has made me feel invincible. For people to see the status that Zak and Kevin hold, their ability, their fame, only for me to beat Zak clean has made me more confident than any Title ever could. But the real kicker is sitting just a short way in front of me, in all his balding glory. Because despite what he wanted, what he knew was good for my career, Roldy is here and trying to win me custody of my child. He called me every name under the sun but he was working his ass off to get me what I wanted, even after I quit on him. For that, he's my hero. Now I sit with him, near enough creaming my pants in anticipation as to what I gotta' do next. No matter how big the hoop is, I'll jump straight through that bitch. So suck on that one, world. Roldy's secretary, Alice, walks into the room with her notepad and pen in hand. Alice, in a word, is Gorgeous. Early-twenties, Brunette, thin waistline - though call me crazy but something tells me I should probably keep my penis on the sidelines for a little while. At least until we get this clusterfuck out of the way. Alice: Anything to drink Mr Babs? Mr Blaise? CSB: Hi Alice. I'll just have a Sprite- Harold: I'll have a Black Coffee and Chris will have a glass of Whiskey. CSB: Roldy it's ten in the morning! Harold ignores and looks at Alice as if to say "You still here?". Alice nods and leaves the room. What the fuck Harold? Harold: Trust me, you'll appreciate the whiskey with what I'm about to tell you. Fuck, there goes the good mood. I thought I was in the clear and now Roldy's going through his stack of papers like the Grim Reaper sharpening his scythe. Alice walks back in and places the drinks down on the desk before making her leave once again. But I don't take my eyes off Roldy as he seems to have found what he was looking for. Harold: To recap what we talked about at the Station, the only things you're being charged with are resisting arrest and speeding, which I've already knocked down to a couple of fines. They're nothing fines, chump change. That, you can forget about. Now the kid. Like I said, they can't charge you there because it's your kid and legally, you were allowed to take it home. You just must have been so eager to go home and watch cartoons that you didn't sign the release forms. CSB: Roldy, please! Harold: Basically you're being taken to court on the charges of being unfit to parent a child. The charges are from Social Services who've got your kid and are trying to stop you from seeing him. Are you fucking kidding me? Are you FUCKING KIDDING ME? That's MY child - MY FUCKING CHILD! ...Calm... I drink the whiskey in one full swoop, slamming the glass back down so hard that cracks start to appear at the side. I can't shout at Roldy for this, he's doing his best for me, so I try to keep my calm - if only to hear him out. CSB: ...So what now? Harold: Now...now we get to work. I've got my entire department working on your defence case. We're gonna' meet every day until we've got your argument down. Round the clock, we're gonna' work our fucking asses off till you look like fucking man of the year and we're win this fucking case! That...That's what now! Roldy looks like he's had a fire lit under him as I see it. This case is just as important to him as it is to me. He's angry. He wants to fight this. Rold realises his passion and settles himself back in his seat, adjusting his tie and jacket. Harold: Listen Chris, I got meetings with defence lawyers all afternoon to try and figure this thing out so go home and do whatever it is you do to stay out of trouble. Play action figures with your room mate or something. Search for porn. Whatever. I'll get Alice to call you later and let you know what's going on. He goes back to staring at the court order in his hands as I rise to my feet. I put my hand out to shake but he's so engulfed in his work he doesn't even notice I'm still there. I laugh and head for the door, knowing this case will be hard but I'm in good hands. That's when Roldy calls to me. Harold: Oh and Chris, make sure your "Court suit" is clean. I turn to him and smirk. Oh little does he know... CSB: Of course it's clean, Roldy. Still hanging up from last time. *** I walk out of Harold's office and onto the busy street's of Boston, heading towards a small nearby cafe' to meet Wayne for breakfast. Despite my notoriety I manage to go un-noticed, bar the odd point or call from across the street. Two teenagers in skater gear start their own little "CSB!" chant and I throw them a thumbs up and a smile, as I enter the cafe', hearing them mark out behind me. Inside and Wayne's sipping from...a chocolate milk. I swear, does nobody realise it is ten in the fucking morning? He sits casual, flicking through an FHM Magazine without a care in the world. I swear there are days where I really do envy him. CSB: Sup homo. Wayne looks up and smiles, closing the Magazine on my arrival. He throws me a "gangstah handshake" as I sit opposite him, getting myself comfortable. Wayne: So Roldy chop your balls off this time? CSB: Nah, he's saving that for his retirement party. My days look pretty numbered though. Wayne: Yeah? What he say? I grab the cafe' menu and browse it, not in any rush and not that Wayne's on the edge of his seat to find out. Time to eat hearty. I mean, I've already had a whiskey! CSB: Basically I'm going to court 'cos they think I'll be a bad parent. They wanna' stop me seeing the kid. Silence. I suppose a little bit of soap opera drama isn't the best way to break the ice over breakfast. Wayne: ...But Roldy's got it though...right? CSB: Yeah, yeah, Roldy's working on it. At that point, Wayne looks at me concerned. He's wondering if that last sentence was something I put my faith in. ...And I do. Yeah, yeah I do. I trust Roldy. He's got me. CSB: Yeah it's fine. Roldy's fixed stuff like this before, remember? Wayne: Yeah the police cells in Amsterdam were much more comfortable than Boston's. We laugh at the old times and how Roldy's turned "Oh shit" moments into joke material in a matter of days. I guess in that respect, I'm in good hands. I may or may not have fabricated that last part. Waitress: Whatcha' havin' fella's? Wayne: You do nuggets? Waitress: ...Yeah.... Wayne: ...Niiiiiiice. I think I'll just have the wings. The waitress shakes her head at Wayne as I laugh. If "fucking with people" was a sport, Wayne would be it's Pele. The woman looks over, prompting me for mine. CSB: Full set Breakfast and a...diet coke. Wayne looks over at me like I'm a freak. Diet coke, I know, I know. The waitress walks away and leaves us be, letting us get back to talk about normal things like TV or sports. Or not. Wayne: So let's just say you get the kid. Where's it gonna' stay? CSB: Oh I'm gonna' get a shed for the garden. In the house, where the fuck you think? Wayne: No I mean where in the house? CSB: Well I'll probably just get a crib and some toys and he can stay in my room. Wayne: What about when he's big? What about when he can run? ...With scissors? What about when he's fifty? I laugh at Wayne's nervousness though anyone can see through it. He looks at me, eyes begging for an answer and I can't mess with him any more than he has himself. I mean, being mean to Wayne is like kicking a puppy. CSB: Bro I know what you're thinking and NO, the baby isn't getting your room. Wayne: If the baby's getting my room then don't sugar-coat it, just fuckin' tell me man! CSB: The baby isn't getting your room Wayne! We got plenty of room. And when we run out, we'll just get a bigger place. Wayne seems to calm down, settling himself in his seat and having another swig of milkshake to reassure himself. He smiles, knowing he's sticking around. People laugh about Wayne but I shit you not, you'll never find a more loyal guy in the world. I'll never have to worry about him doing what Kevin did to Zak. Never. Wayne: As long as I'm not being forgotten man, that's fine by me. CSB: Forgotten? Fuck man. You're my free childcare. Wayne: Wait, what? CSB: You keep your room but you gonna' have to help me out with the kid. Wayne: Dude, I've never raised a kid before. CSB: Oh and you think I have? Look all I'm sayin' is I can't do this shit on my own. You gonna' need to be babymomma Wayne. At least until I can find a good looking nympho with their level 3 NVQ. Then she can help me look after baby...er... Wayne: OOH! OOH! SHOTGUN NAMING YOUR KID!! Wayne sits back, ecstatic with himself for nailing such a coo. The smug grin on his face like he's just whipped my ass. CSB: ...Alright. Wayne: ....What? CSB: If you can think of the most awesome name of all time, I will let you name my first born child. Wayne: Really? CSB: Really- Wayne: Bat Man. CSB: Think harder. Wayne scratches his head as he thinks to himself, muttering a thousand inaudible names under his breathe. Honestly? I'm not gonna' be one of those people who name my child after the Arabic word for "eternal beauty" or any other poetic bullshit like that. I live a simple life, so why not make everything else about it simple? Wayne picks a good name, I'll stick to my word. Somehow though, I don't see it getting much better than "Spider Man". The waitress comes over with our food and we immediately get stuck in, savouring a good meal when we see it. Suddenly Wayne lifts his head from his plate of wings and looks at me with a face of worry. Wayne: Yo Chris...What happens if you don't win the case? I put down my knife and fork. Silence. Wayne looks at me as serious as he's ever looked at me before. CSB: I guess we just gotta' wait and see, huh? Silence. I guess we DO have to wait and see. ... .....
