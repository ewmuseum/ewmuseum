Suspicious Minds; Maxx RP 2

Nov 6 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
October 30th 2008, 16:57, Golds Gym ,Detroit Maxx and the Grand Mystique walk into an empty Golds Gym, Maxx shakes the managers hand as he and gm are ready to start there workout session, suddenly GM looks over with a weird look of suspicion GM: This place seems to be a let down for a familiar brand such as gold’s gym Maxx laughs and throws his bag on the floor, he looks inside and pulls out his towel and a bottle of Evian still, as he begins to drink a picture falls to the ground, curiously GM grabs it as Maxx looks on wondering how that picture ever managed to get in the bag with his belongings, suddenly GM’s eyes widen and begins to crack a smile as Maxx looks on in horror Maxx: That’s not mine GM laughs as Maxx grabs the picture and turns it over to reveal Chloe Cornett in a rather revealing swim suite, a caption is written below, “you’ll be seeing these again big boy XXX…love Chloe” Suddenly Maxx’s face turns red as he begins to feel utter embarrassment for not noticing the picture before, he attempts to hide it as GM chuckles at his tag team partner attempting to hide his shame GM: just think the so called emotionless animal is actually starting to feel embarrassment Maxx continues to feel embarrassed as GM looks on with a crooked smile on his face, Maxx’s embarrassment turns to anger as GM gives Maxx a big pat on the back GM: whats this big surprise you wanted to show me? Maxx: well apart from the amount of money I paid so that we could have the gym to ourselves for a couple of days, I also added something familiar to enhance our training session Maxx opens an unmarked door as GM looks on with a confused look on his face As Maxx opens the door to reveal a wrestling ring, GM gazes with amazement as Maxx with joy looks on at his beaming colleague Maxx: Lets show these rookies what were made of! The screen cuts into a training montage of Maxx and GM in various scenarios as gonna fly now by performed by DeEtta Little and Nelson Pigford. Maxx looks on puzzled by his tag team partners actions as GM looks on in rage GM: WHY DID YOU LOCK ME IN THE CROSSFACE YOU BIG PIECE OF….. Maxx turns aggressive and shoves GM, they both lock up once more this time GM breaks the it up and slaps Maxx across the face with his left palm Maxx: YOU SON OF….. GM now even more angry runs the ropes and attempts a cross body but Maxx powers out and lifts him into a fireman’s carry, but Maxx as he suddenly remembers who the man he’s fighting with is Maxx: (Internal dialogue) what the hell am I doing? GM suddenly leaps down and kicks Maxx in his right ankle, he positions himself and locks in an ankle lock submission with a a sadistic smile he grabs Maxx’s ankle but suddenly stops as Maxx looks on in horror GM: Looks like my animal instincts got the better of me, for that I apologies GM holds out his right hand as Maxx looks on remembering the look he saw as his own tag team partner attempted to rip his ankle into shreds, he however reluctantly accepts as both men depart, GM hits the showers as Maxx sits alone and looks on at his ankle thinking what could of happened Maxx: (Internal Dialogue)was he lying when we squashed things?, is he out for himself, can I trust him to watch my back as I have done on two separate occasions? , this week on Monday Night Mayhem we have our toughest challenge to date with Darius and Boyd and I need my partner behind me 110%... Maxx looks up at a poster of himself hanging from the wall in the right hand corner, he is holding the now defunct EW Heavyweight title pointing out to the crowd Maxx looks on and ponders his future as the scene cuts to GM infront of the showerhead, the water sprays over his face, his hands run all over his face as the camera can only see his eyes looking on with pure anger Fin
