One Step Foward, One Step Back.; RP against Renshaw

Aug 6 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Stage One, The Rise. Jesús Cruz, a freshly signed MWE talent, hyping his own name to the world. In his debut bout, he slayed a man whom doubled him in size. He appeared untouchable. Stage Two, The Fall. The hours upon hours in the gym could not prepare him for the triple threat environment. He fought, and battled, but in an instant, got caught. Laid out. Three count. Badda bing, badda boom; game over. Stage Three, The Rebound. Reality has set in, flaws have been unraveled, and realistic standards have been reset. Jesús is beatable, but still the cream of the crop athlete. And prove it, he will. Your fear. Don't let it wither. Lets do this folks. Fade in. Look behind you Cruz! LIFE TO LIFELESS! COVER! ONE! TWO!! THREE!!!! Look behind you Cruz! LIFE TO LIFELESS! COVER! ONE! TWO!! THREE!!!! Look behind you Cruz! LIFE TO LIFELESS! COVER! ONE! TWO!! THREE!!!! The image flashes over and over again. As we slowly pan back, we see that the flat screen showing it is slanted and cracked. As we zoom back more and more, we clearly see the destruction of this apartment. The refrigerator has been tipped over, and food has been scattered all over the floor. The window, completely shattered. The couch has deep shred marks, and most of the inside stuffing has been removed. And sitting on a lone wooden chair, with his arms crossed tight, and deep black bags under his eyes, we see Jesús Cruz in a state we've never seen of him before. Confusion. Because as he continues to stare at the screen, he wonders to himself, 'How could this have happened?' Cruz rises the remote into the air. With a click, it goes black, and his nightmare appears to be over. But it's not. Tossing the remote aside, he clinches his fists against his temples, pressing firmly on each side of his head. With a loud sigh, he shakes out his hands, as he attempts to gather his bearings. He stands up quite abrupt, knocking down the chair in the process. His head turns, staring at the camera with a frown. It... It could have happened to anyone... Jesús inhales with his hands on his head, slightly tugging at his hair in frustration, clearly still upset over the defeat. ...And just like that, I'm back to square one... Cruz grunts, as he kicks the downed chair half way across the room. Again, he takes another deep breath, in an attempt to keep calm. It's... It's ok... I'm still the man... His words are all a cover. They hide the feelings that boil up inside him. His ego, deeply hurt. His confidence, lessened. His view on the sport, altered. But he refuses to admit it. He must stay strong. For his father. For his gym. But most importantly, for himself. He clears his throat, wipes the sweat from his brow, and lets out another sigh. Blocking the negative thoughts out of his mind, he desperately tries to clear his mind for the promo. As the saying goes, the show must go on. So this week they stick me against David Renshaw; a bulky skater boy from London. And I couldn't be more offended. Seriously, I'm an athlete here. No gimmicks, straight aggression. And I get stuck opening the show against a flash-in-the-pan goody goody? Look Renshaw, you just don't stack up to me. There will be no kneepads, no helmets, no protection from what I'm going to do from you. Jesús walks forward, each and every step towards the camera. He stops, with his head alone filling the shot, and his eyes staring deeply into the lens. You think your though shit, don't you David? With a sneak attack on Sharpe, then a DQ tag victory alongside Mr. Blight, you feel on top of the world? All you've done is injure a bitch, and take a blow from your own skateboard... Let me tell you boy, from experience. In a matter of seconds, the walls around you can come tumbling down. And with me, it will. Ohhh David, it will. Because defeat is a bitter taste. It's a taste I refuse to taste again. Thus, I must stop any momentum you think you may have, and finish you inside that ring. With both hands, Jesús clamps onto the camera, and pulls it in even closer. The muscles in his face tensen up, as intensity flows from his mouth. And let me warn you punk, I'm no Devlin Sharpe. Try and meet me in the parking lot, and you'll wake up to the comfort of a nurse. Because I'm not going to get blind sided by an X Games wanna-be. Got that!? Cruz shoves the camera away, which causes the camera man to stumble back and trip. He quickly scrambles back to his feet, and resumes recording. Don't underestimate me Renshaw. My size should play no factor. People consider me as 'a little engine that could'. Think again. I'm 'a locomotive that railroads'. So I don't care how big you are. To me, it's just a bigger boom to hit the canvas. I'm quicker, more conditioned, more explosive, more experienced, and simply, better. I'll admit, if it was me against you in a game of skate, you'd school me. No doubt. But, it isn't. So don't expect to leave The Allstate Arena with just a scrapped elbow. Because this will be a brawl. That, David, is MY sport. And between the opening bell and the closing bell, you're nothing more to me then a moving target. Riiiiinnng..... Riiiiinnng..... Riiiiinnng..... The noise of the phone interrupts the promo. Cruz clearly hears it, as takes another deep breath, and attempts to ignore it and finish. I like your spirit David. Riiiiinnng..... It's a shame I'll have to crush it. Riiiiinnng..... Good luck. You'll need every bit of it to survive. The answer machine kicks on. Jesús turns around with aggravation and annoyance. *Yo, you've reached Jesús; you know what to do.* *Beep!* It's Xavier... Don't do this... I know you're there, listening... I'm sure the offer upset you, but your father and I feel it's best if you accept it. Sure your a well-trained machine, but in the sport you're in now, that's just not enough. So don't be thick-headed. You can't honestly look me in the eye and tell me you can do this alone. All you hav- With a roar, Cruz viciously rips the answer machine from the wall, and hurls it across the room before the message can end. It smashes and breaks to bits. But no, the rampage doesn't end there. Jesús begins striking himself in the forehead, using enough force to draw blood. Hit after hit, pounding away at his own flesh. As a steady stream of blood makes it's way down his face and onto his white shirt, he stops and yells aloud to himself. WHY!? WHY MUST THEY DO THIS TO ME!? I'M MY OWN MAN!!! THEY KNOW THAT! Another fierce roar fills the room, before he notices the camera man has not yet exited. YOU! GET THE FUCK OUT OF HERE! NOW!! Immediately, the camera man bolts for the door. He fumbles with the handle, but manages to swings it open, and make it out. From behind, Jesús slams the door behind him. But his anger is still heard though the wood and drywall. I'M BETTER THEN THAT! HOW DARE THEY!! AHHHH!!! More bashing and banging is heard inside, but we're not sticking around. The screen bounces up and down as the camera man continues to flee, before suddenly cutting to black.
