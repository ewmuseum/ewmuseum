Steroids,Explosions,AssKissing - The Good Old Days; Alejandro Rodriguez RP #2

Sep 21 2008

Alejandro Rodriguez

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Alejandro Rodriguez is standing in his locker-room, warming up for a segment at a house show at the Value City Arena just one day before Monday Night Mayhem, he is wearing a usual ring attire, black baggy pants, a white t-shirt and a black mask. He is securing the strap on his mask when there is a knock at the door. Darrel Jones walks in through the locker room door. He sighs when he see’s Alejandro and he closes the door. Alejandro: Why the fuck do you always try and catch me naked? Wait till I fucking say “come in”… Jesus. Darrel: Sorry Al, it’s just that… Stacy’s just arrived at the arena, she looks pissed. Alejandro: Ah shit, thanks… You better get lost before she arrives. Darrel nods and darts outside the locker-room in a flash, about thirty seconds later there is another bang on the door. Stacy Adams storms into the locker room wearing a low cut pink top and ripped jeans. Alejandro: Baby… You look hot! Alejandro opens his arms hopefully and Stacy slaps him across the face. Alejandro doesn’t seem to be hurt but he half smirks. Stacy: You could have been dead! Stephani has been sick with worry! I had to send Darrel to talk sense into you! YOU’VE BEEN GONE FOR TWO FUCKING DAYS! Alejandro: Baby, I’m sorry, I was busy- Stacy cuts him off… Stacy: BUSY!?! You’ve been getting drunk every single day in that god forsaken bar so don’t lie to me you... You- oh fuck it! Stacy launches herself at Alejandro and begins kissing him full on the mouth, she takes even Alejandro by surprise and he falls back onto the floor with Stacy on top of him. She continues kissing him for a few more seconds and then she breaks off and Alejandro starts laughing, someone knocks on the door and a muffled voice comes from behind it. ???: They’re playing your music Al, its time for your promo. Alejandro moans and looks at Stacy. Alejandro: Fuck. Baby I got to do my promo now. Stacy gets off him and tosses her hair back, she gets to her feet and struts to the door. Stacy: I’ll be waiting. She gives him a sexy grin and she opens the door and begins walking back to the car park. In the locker room Alejandro gets up to his feet and straitens his shirt. Alejandro: …women. It comes to his attention that his music is still playing. He runs out his locker-room and pelts it down to the curtain. He walks through ait and receives a dynamo reception of boo’s and jeers. Alejandro smirks cockily and walks down to the ring ignoring the fans chants of “You’re an Asshole” among other chants. He walks around the ring and gets a mic from the time-keeper. He walks up the steps and climbs into the ring, he then walks over to a turnbuckle and he climbs up it and poses, soaking up all the boos and chants, he then climbs down from the turnbuckle and returns to the centre of the ring, he waits for the crowd to calm down, and then he speaks. Alejandro: Who here… Who here in this arena remember the good old days? The good old days when Brian Michaels was talented, when D.C. slapped in the face of authority unlike today where he follows it rule by rule, when we had a ‘roided up Frank Soprano, a blown up Aiden Copane and most importantly a Sonny Williams who kissed everyone’s ass in order for the fans to have an orgasm every time he entered the arena and gave them a cheap pop… *Alejandro laughs…* I remember those days, I remember when he wanted to be liked. Now that’s all flipped on his ass you see, shows you just how much he really liked you people at all, not that I blame him, I hate all of you too. The crowd rain boo’s on Alejandro Rodriguez and he laughs at their torment. Alejandro: But the thing is Sonny, the whole hating everyone and being hated by everyone thing, just ain’t working out for you, is it? Come on be honest, it isn’t. You see, these people, they used to love you, they used to respect you and worship you. I’ve always been hated. I don’t give a fuck about being hated, I love it, I love to watch these brain-dead people boo me like five-year-olds when i insult the shit out of them. But that’s not the point, you want to be a bad man? You haven’t done anything to make these people hate you, you don’t just do bad things Sonny, it ain’t as simple as that, you’ve got to really mean it Sonny, you’ve got to really feel it inside of you. And you know, deep, deep down, that you’ll never, ever… Get a reaction as good and as hated as the one I got when my music called… So let me give you some advice Sonny, and here it is. Go back to your cheap pops, not your play-ground insults. Go back to your freestyle raps, not your emo poems. Go back to kissing ass, I know you fucking love it. The crowd give a mixed reaction to this. Alejandro: Don’t you guys fucking start on me, I still fucking hate you. The crowd begin to chant “You Suck…” and Alejandro smiles. Alejandro: That’s better assholes. Alejandro laughs and then continues speaking. Alejandro: But unlike you people, I fucking hate the guts of Sonny Williams, the man is the most fucking, grade-a, annoying piece-of-shit I have ever had the displeasure of meeting in my who damn life. He fucking lies through his teeth. He can’t even count to fucking four, he said we were 4 and 0 in his last promo, but now, well it’s just fucking gone down to 3 and 0 now! The man is so full of bullshit I just can’t fucking believe it! And his repetitiveness, oh my fucking god! Play a different fucking record for once in your life! We’ve heard all the shit about your brother, your mother, your world title, how you‘re all fucking this, how you’re the main man, the fucking gangster, the pre-school raps and poems, have some mother-fucking originality for once will you? I hate the way you talk, the way you stink of shit, the way you brag and still get things wrong, I hate how you wrestle, I hate fucking wrestling you. But I love to beat the mother-fucking shit out of you because you mother fucking deserve it, because I hate you. Alejandro spits on the floor to show his disgust and the fans boo. Alejandro: No wonder your mother threw you out onto the streets you thug, anyone would have done the same. Looked at your whiny bitch-ass face and just fucking slapped the shit out of it and thrown you out the front door. I‘d fucking pay to be able to do just that, but I get paid for it, see when I get in that ring with you tomorrow, I’m going to slap the shit out of you and kick you out the MWE, just as if it was decades ago when your mother did just that to you. I had no mother because she died to save me… You have no mother because she couldn’t stand the damn whiney, bratty, ugly sight of you. You think you can disrespect me you piece of shit, earn some respect why don’t you, quit fucking talking and see who fucking wins this time bitch. The crowd continue to boo Alejandro and Alejandro draws a breath and pauses. Alejandro: You know what, I’m done fucking talking to you, I’m oing to do what you never fucking have done and shut up. I’ve just got one more thing to say to him, if he thinks he has a cat in hells chance of walking out of Mayhem without more broken ribs, then he’s got another thing coming. I’m going to beat him in the face so fucking bad his face will be too swollen for him to run his mouth, which we all know he will. So “good luck” Sonny, because you’re going to need It, when I’m through with you, you‘ll go crying back to your Mom, regardless of what she‘s done in the past. I ain’t gonna fucking say catchphrases and say “bitch” at the end. I’m just going to kick your fucking ass. Alejandro pauses. Alejandro: And you can take that to the bank... Bitch! Alejandro slaps his hand to his mouth. Alejandro: …oops. Alejandro laughs. Alejandro: Alright if you’ll excuse me, roll my music essé, I have to go and fuck my girlfriend. Alejandro drops his mic and rolls out the ring as even his theme music doesn’t cover up the heat from the crowd. Scene fades to black.
