Anything to Win

Dec 19 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
*The scene opens up, in Mason Resoan like fashion, with him walking towards a room. However, in this particular setting, he is walking to the curtains of a Mayhem Wrestling Entertainment house show. He is in blue sparkled "Canadian Classic" wrestling tights and has a gold and white hoody on. "Extreme Ways" by Moby blasts throughout the PA system within the arena, and Mason waits. he ties his boots one last time, shrugs his shoulders to warm up, and then at the climax of the song's introduction, he walks through the curtain with his hood up. The crowd boos and yells at him as he brashly walks to the ring, taunting the crowd as he walked. Sara Whitaker, as some fans noticed, was not by Mason's side this morning. Mason continues to walk down the ramp until he reaches the ring apron. He holds his hood, looks around the crowd, yanks down his hood and hops up onto the apron. He slips through the middle rope, spins, and jogs over to the other side of the ring, where he receives a microphone from the timekeeper. The crowd continues to boo, but they quite down incredibly.* Mason: You know, I have been laughing a lot lately. Many different things spark it, jokes, television shows, random billboards...all kinds of thing. You know what though? The thing that makes me laugh the most...has to be the ignorance of my surroundings. Whether it be Sinc Mercier, Brian Michaels, or the fans that attend my matches. Ignorance surrounds me, despite my pleas, and it makes me laugh. You boo me, you yell at me, and you envy me. However, when I speak...you all listen. Don't deny it...you quite down...because when I speak...you know it is important. You know it is relevant to MWE...and you know it isn't gibberish. Unlike Sinc Mercier, I can speak full sentences without studdering or making a mistake. I come up with creative jokes and insults, and I apply them at the right times. My language is tasteful and useful...and I know how to use it. Sinc Mercier...not so much. *There are a few laughs heard throughout the small crowd of about 3,000. However, the laughs are immediately drowned by the continuous boos of the audience. There is even an open beer can thrown from the crowd, which sparks security to come over and escort the man out of the building. This makes Mason laugh, once again.* Mason: And you know the funny thing about ignorance...it never fails! No, it will never fail, because for every one intelligent person, there are ten drunk a**holes that surround them. You just saw it for yourselves, and I am sure you could see it by looking around you. You can tell can't you? Who is literate and who is not. Who has a job that means something...and who works in a cubicle 40 hours a week. *People all over the Sovereign Center begin to look around. There are loyal Mason fans who obviously see what he is talking about, but then there are people who seem to be confused at what they were asked to do. There are many drunks in the crowd, despite how early in the morning it is, and the loyal Mason fans make note of this, but then turn back to listen to the Canadian Classic.* Mason: It isn't that hard to see...which is quite pathetic...but then again, it happens in companies even as prestigious as MWE. Another key example...my opponent for Adrenaline Rush...Sinc Mercier. The self-proclaimed Arrogant Aviator...which just so happens to bring me back to ignorance. You see, Sinc, people who win are arrogant. People who have established themselves have the right to be arrogant. You say you don't care what I say, well, it came back to bite you didn't it? You didn't listen to me, and I beat you...like I said I would...last week on Monday Night Mayhem. *Mention of Mason's sparks the crowd to begin to boo again. There are obviously some Sinc supporters in the building.* Mason: Oh yes, boo, please, I don't care. It is not my fault that you can't except the fact that you support a loser...a shameless human being...a man in denial. *Mason lowers the microphone a little as he slowly surveys the arena. Many people are furious by Mason's words, and some are even tempted to throw things at Mason. Mason taunts the crowd by urging them to let loose and bombard the ring with beer bottles, soda cans and anything they can throw at him. However, Mason begins to laugh and wave his finger, as he knows that he is untouchable.* Mason: You guys can't touch me...hell, you can't even speak to me. You know why...because I am here and you are there. I am in the ring, winning, and you are in the crowd, watching. So, I will continue to taunt you...because I can. And you will endure it...helpless...like children. Just like Sinc Mercier...he will watch and even try to get at me...but he will simply have to endure what I throw at him. And then when he can't bear it any longer, he will once again...yes, once again, crack, and I will beat him. And if ya didn't know...now ya know! *Mason walks to the ropes, slips back through the middle rope, and walks down the entrance ramp taunting the crowd as he went. He drew immense heat from the crowd and was satisfied, as he made his way through the black curtains. A few of the staff patted his back as he walked through, but Mason ignored them, and kept walking to his locker room. Upon entering, he grabbed a bag full of who knows what, draped it over his shoulder, and walked out of the arena. The scene fades to black... *The scene opens up once again, with Mason looking around in the middle of a store. All that can be seen is Mason, but as he backs up, you can see that the store is actually a jewelry shop. He is looking, very focused, at a small 9 centimeter plate, made of complete gold. It almost looks as though it is a championship belt plate. The camera spins in a circle, twice, before regaining control. It was Sara Whitaker shoving the camera man out of the way, so she could walk up to Mason. She was looking beautiful, wearing a black pea coat and long black dress pants. Her hair was in a pony tail as she held Mason, both of them looking at the plating. Mason, bent down to tie his brown dress shoes, as a man in a formal business suit walked over to him. He tapped Mason on the shoulder, and he stood up quickly, thinking it was Sara. He frowned when he saw it was the store clerk.* Clerk: May I help you, sir? *Sara pushed Mason to the side.* Sara: Listen, we will come over to you when we need you. For now, let us browse. *The man listened, fixed his tie, and walked back over behind the counter, where he opened up a magazine. However, he kept his eyes on Mason and Sara. Mason picked up a gold plate, showed it to Sara, and she nodded in approval. The man walked back over and butted in again.* Clerk: Listen, do you need help ringing anything up or picking anything out? I am a professional, I can help you make the right choice. *Mason, displeased, sighed and stood up. He spun around and looked at the man straight in the eyes, very seriously.* Mason: Listen, I know you can speak English, and if you didn't hear, my girlfriend her said to lay off for a little, what don't you understand? Clerk: I just need something to do. This place is extremely slow most of the time...really boring actually, and it is rare we have customers. *Mason and Sara are both in a good mood, and don't snap. Sara looks at Mason and he understood.* Mason: If everything here is so boring, simply quit. Clerk: Quit a job that pays 18 dollars an hour? Are you serious, I do nothing all day and make tons of money. Mason: Then why the f*** are you complaining? Clerk: F*** it, just tell me why your here and maybe I can help you. First off, who are you? Mason: I am Mason Resoan, the next United States Champion of Mayhem Wrestling Entertainment... *Mason stops in mid-sentence, because he heard the clerk say something under his breath.* Mason: Cocky? You think I am cocky? Why don't you speak up next time so I don't have to strain my ears. If I am so cocky, elaborate...because I am sure Sara and I would love to hear. Clerk: I know why you are here, you are going to design a new belt...you think you will beat Sinc Mercier and the match hasn't even started. Mason: Are you kidding me...did you not see me destroy him Monday? Do you not know my record or my history in MWE? I am Mason f***ing Resoan...I've already won against Sinc. I have already shown him that he can not beat me. For you, or anyone else to think that Adrenaline Rush will be any different is just madness. Adrenaline Rush will be the worst beat down Sinc will have ever gotten, and he knows it. So why should you doubt me. Just shut the f*** up and go back to work...actually, I am purchasing this...so ring it up and I will be leaving. Clerk: Whatever you say... Mason: Ha! Still running your mouth...you know, your ballsy, I'll give you that. But how far will that get you, huh? Sure, you might have money, but what are you doing with your life...nothing? You know something else, Sinc Mercier is ballsy to. He is the United States Champion, and where is he going with his life, huh? That's right...no where! Just like you, Sinc Mercier is just a push-over and a fraud. A man, in disguise. A man, that as much as he denies it, is a shell of his former self. Do you like being compared to that man? *The clerk goes to give a witty response, but Mason cuts him short.* Mason: I don't give a hell what your opinion on the matter is, actually. All you need to know, is that Sinc Mercier is nothing but a coward. A man, who instead of dealing with his problems, tries to overshadow the fact that he is a failure. And Sinc, if you are watching, I'll tell you something...you are not a leader of MWE! You are not a front man, you are not a true champion, and you are sure as hell not a history maker. I AM! Mason Resoan is a history maker, and you know what, I have an idea. Maybe, instead of getting this little sheet of metal engraved with my name, I should redo it with the words: "MWE's LEADER." And people will have nothing to argue, because after a disgrace like you as champion, I will look like a leader...more than a leader, a savior. People will sigh in relief to know that there is just a little order in MWE. At least one match of the night will be worth watching. At least one champion will have a face that people can recognize as a leader...that face...will be mine! There is no doubt about that. Sinclair...you think that just because you are losing faith in your fans and you have realized the truth...that you are going to win at Adrenaline Rush...well, think again! I have known what you just learned for quite some time now. I was the innovator, the outcast, the first person in MWE to make use of that knowledge, and that is why I am here today...facing you, for the United States title! *Mason stops as he hands the piece of metal over to the clerk, who rings it up. Mason violently snatches it out of his hand, grabs Sara by the hand, and walks out of the store. They get into their limousine, and tell the driver to head somewhere the cameras didn't hear. Upon getting into the limo, Mason starts to relax as he stares at Sara, who looks at him in the same way he does her. They don't talk the entire car, ride, but stare the entire time. Mason, of course, has a million thoughts racing through his heads as the limo ride continues. He eventually begins to enjoy the ride, but of course, the ride ends. He gets out of the vehicle and sprints out of the car up to the mansion, where he gets some things ready quickly. It is now the evening, reaching around 8 P.M.* *Mason is sitting in his room, on his bed, thinking, as he holds in his hand, a briefcase full of unknown contents. Sara Whitaker walks into his room and quickly becomes worried.* Sara: Where are you going...and what is in the bag? Mason: I am going out...there are some things that I need to think about by myself. It's nothing personal, I just need to get out for a little bit. *Mason thinks this is enough information to satisfy her, but she asks again.* Sara: But to where? Just in case I need you... Mason: You'll be fine without me for a little bit, but if you really need, be, I will be downtown somewhere. Probably by some sort of water, probably a big body of water. Sara: What are you going to do... *The concern in her voice is increasing again. Mason "shhs" her, and kisses her on her head and leaves. Sara is confused, but after a little while lets it go. Mason grabs his briefcase, swings it behind his back, and runs out of his mansion. He proceeds to walk downtown, by himself, in the night. He looks around as he sees some of the sights you can see in Toronto at night. There are many tourists, but he ignores them as he proceeds to walk and stare at some of the large buildings and stadiums of the area. He walks down further, past some of the more unvisited buildings, such as bowling allies and parking garages to far away buildings. He then sprints a little further down the city until he reaches a small boardwalk. He had no idea Toronto had a boardwalk, but he didn't think to much of it...he had to much on his mind right now. No one was around, so he walked into the middle of the boardwalk, and placed the briefcase on a stone wall that separated him from the water. He turned, saw the cameras, sighed, but tried to ignore it. After a while of thinking, he turned back to the cameras and spoke.* Mason: You know...there are a lot of things that go on in my mind in the day. I think about Mayhem Wrestling Entertainment, my profession, and my girlfriend, and my opponents for my next match. However, as of late, all I can think about is Sinc Mercier. The United States champion...a fool...as I have called him many times before. But I can't help thinking about him...even though I know I will beat him. And lately, I have been thinking about Sinc's change of heart...and I have something to say to you Sinc... *Mason takes a breath of the night air and turns and looks to the water...spins around, and looks back at the camera with his evil grin.* Mason: I told you so! *Mason smiles and chuckles a bit as he begins to sway to and fro.* Mason: I told you...from day one. Since back when you faced D.C...I told you so! I told you that appealing to crowd would get you nowhere...and you insisted otherwise. Well, I am happy to say that you think otherwise...even though it took two months to sink in. But I am sure that you thought about my words long and hard, didn't you? You wouldn't mention me, but you did think about my words...why else would you change? *Mason takes a step back and leans his back against the stone of dividing wall. The swaying stops.* Mason: You see, I don't act...I don't play...I don't mess with MWE's audience. I am truthful...honest...and trustworthy...yet the crowd hates me...because they don't want to know the truth, or, to quote a famous movie, they can't handle the truth. They can't...it is plain and simple...and that is why they still love you...despite your actions. You could burn down a forest and fans of yours in the EPA would still cheer...because they will not accept the fact you are a "bad guy." They still boo me over you, because they don't like the fact that I was right...because they don't want to see me win...because they hate me...and I embrace it. I love the fact that they hate me...because what is there to lose? Fans...HA! If I still have fans...no matter what I do...I won't lose them...because they are those intelligent people I was talking about. They have accepted the fact that I am better than you and they know that what I say is the truth. When I say I will win my match...I do. And you know what, I haven't disappointed yet. *Mason pushes off against the wall, and cracks his neck a little.* Mason: Nor will I begin to disappoint, because honestly, I don't plan to lose to you...as much as you would love for me to. I have beaten you once and I will beat you again...and take all that is dear to you...or what was dear to you...because frankly, you have already lost that belt. *Mason pokes fun at Sinc's iffy emotions and pretends to get angry...but immediately begins to cry...then laughs at his impression.* Mason: And when there is a loser...someone has to win. Someone has to walk out with the strap...otherwise...there is chaos. And you know what...we don't want chaos now do we...or at least I don't think your emotions can handle it. That winner is going to be me, and I honestly don't think I can stress it enough. This win, as monumental as it will be, is just a stepping stone to all my defenses. What is fun about manhandling a broken man? There is nothing fun about...nothing to be proud of...nothing you can look back on and compliment yourself on. However, the defenses will be spectacular...and you get to watch! You get to see that I meant business from the get go...and I will continue to mean business. You know why, because upon this win...I will have motivation...oh, yes! Sara Whitaker is enough motivation for anyone...but some people enjoy to have more. You see, my motivation will be to show people that I actually deserve the title United States Champion. That I deserve the title, Triple Crown Champion...that I deserve the title...leader of Mayhem Wrestling Entertainment. I have already established myself in this new form of Mayhem Wrestling Entertainment...but why not further my ever-growing legacy? *Mason puts back on his evil grin, which has become familiar to him. It is quite charming, and enhances his ego.* Mason: See, Adrenaline Rush will be much more than a win...it will be the start of something amazing. It will help me kick off the new year with a bang...and regain the dominance that I once had in the old MWE. 2009 will be a good year...I can tell. *Mason stops grinning and instead gives the camera a thumbs up with a smirk.* Mason: And here is another thing for you to think about, Mr. Arrogant Aviator...I am undefeated...yes, it is true. I have won all of my matches...even my house show matches...speaking of which...I saw you lost yours. What a shame, losing to Joey Dallas won't help your reputation very much, now will it? You see, I don't crack under pressure...in fact, I apply the pressure. When my back is on the mat, I turn the situation around. When I am insulted by someone, I turn the situation around...and when I am coming into a match, not a champion, I turn that situation around, as well. What do you do...you crack, you fall and I beat you. Hopefully, you can make a liar out of me...but, I doubt it. Just try not to hurt yourself. *Mason continues to poke fun at Sinc, giving the camera a sarcastic "wink and gun" motion with his hands.* Mason: Sinc, your lies won't get you anywhere with me...because I see right through them...I make sure that I expose them...and I did...so enough of you...at Adrenaline Rush we will see if you can keep your word and get the job done...you can do it against others, but will it work with me? *Mason holds up one finger and touches the briefcase that is teetering on the wall. He then takes it off, waves it in the air, and touches his palm with the same finger.* Mason: This brings me to...THE MAN OF THE HOUR! Superstar of the week and the most egotistical son of a bitch since Greg Cherry...BRIAN MICHAELS! Ding...Ding...Ding! *Mason makes the noises and has a weird face that goes along with it.* Mason: Did you like my Oprah Winfrey impression? HA! Brian Michaels, guess what you won...a blip on my radar! Way to sign your own death notice...congratulations! I hope you are fond of your new MWE Championship...because we all know how much you deserve that belt...just like Sinc Mercier...speaking of which, do you guys hang out or something, because you have no idea how much you guys are alike. I mean, all you two do is lose and lose, especially to me, and then you receive title shots, when I get what? A house show match against Sonny Williams and the Vega Knight? You don't deserve that strap and you know it... *Mason thinks for a second and ponders, even going as far as putting his hand on his chin.* Mason: Actually, before I get into you, I would like to formally apologize to David Whitman...a man who shouldn't of had to defend his gold in the first place.......WAIT! Hold the phone, I take that back. Whitman, gets your balls out of your throat and be a man...I mean come on...Brian Michaels...really? How could you lose...I mean, how much more do I have to do to this guy. I beat him once, and D.C didn't get the job done, and then D.C roughed him up and you didn't get the job done. Then I roughed him up again in our little tag match squabble, and you still failed...just quit...now! *Mason puts his hand back on his chin.* Mason: What was I talking about...oh yeah, Brian Michaels...It is kind of easy to forget about him, so I apologize to the viewers at home. Brian Michaels...I have to say, you title win really screwed you over though...I mean, come on...you know I am coming after you again...right? I said before that United States championship was just a stepping stone, but with you as champion...I mean, I have a reason to challenge now. So, say hello to your championship while you can, but make sure you write a poem or something to ease over your separation...because it will be rough! *Mason laughs at his own joke again.* Mason: But all seriousness aside, Brian, you have put yourself back onto my radar. And when we meet again, your chances of winning will be very much like my theme song. You will need extreme ways to beat me...foreign object...some sort of cheating, because I have proved time and time again that you can't beat me...and just like Sinc, I will show you that I am going to try extra hard to make you pay for taking hold of something that didn't belong to you... *Mason picks up the briefcase, for some reason which is unknown, and begins to swing it.* Mason: But wait a second, HOLD THE PHONE...again, there is one thing I forgot to mention...you have a match against the World Heavyweight Champion...how ditzy of me to forget. D.C versus Michaels III is happening as the main event of my card...how could I forget. I mean, I did start the whole thing, I should have at least remembered it, right? Nah! You see, D.C, I have a feeling, is finally going to end this feud once and for all. I mean, three chances is enough for anyone, so I am sure he will get the job done. So, you won't be able to face me for your title...how sad, right? But done worry, you could always pull a Sonny Williams and drop a class and face me. In fact, I encourage that motion, because I will simply take your place, and who knows, maybe I will win the Undisputed Title. It is about time, right? It will make sense, because who better to beat D.C, than the person to give him motivation to attack you in the first place? *Mason turns, opens the briefcase, and shuts it quickly. He places the case back onto the stone wall and looks at his watch.* Mason: D.C, I have to ask...how is Lindsey? I heard that you to were getting along just fine, right? I hope so, I would hate to see any trouble between the two of you...I mean, what would you do without Lindsey...go back to getting sent to prison? That's laughable...I mean, Lindsey completes you...and as much as you hate to admit it, you would fall to pieces without her. *Mason takes a piece of paper out of the briefcase, which he opened again. He held it up for the camera to see, front and back.* Mason: You see, D.C, I figured out how your career is. With Lindsey, your career is much like this piece of paper. Whole, complete and capable of anything. A blank piece of paper, much like a career in its prime, can be turned into anything. A poem on 9/11 or a masterpiece of art. It could even turn into a letter that could launch you farther into your career than ever before. But that is when the paper is blank...but when the paper is full, it means that your career is over. You can't improve. As of right now, your paper, much like mine, is blank. However, your piece of paper, without Lindsey would change. *Mason proceeds to rip the paper into six, holding the pieces in his hand as he continues to talk.* Mason: Without Lindsey, however, you career would be tore in half...well, six, according to the paper. That is besides the point though. Slowly, but surely, you would try and build your career back up to what it was, but it would not quite be whole again, would it? No it wouldn't, so a piece or two would be missing. *Mason takes two of the six pieces and tosses them into the body of the water. He transfers the remaining pieces to his opposite hand and begins to sway.* Mason: You would continue and you would slowly start to realize that you weren't as good as you once were...and slowly, you would begin to drop ranks. Shredding your career into further pieces. *Mason holds up the pieces of paper in his hands and proceeds to rip them into so many pieces, you can't count them all.* Mason: Even then, you would continue and try to be what you once were...maybe capturing your old X-TV title...whoop de doo! But, what would happen after you lost that? You would lose everything...your career, your title and Lindsey would have been long gone...slowly, you would deteriorate. *Mason throws the remaining pieces, that lay in his hand, into the water. The camera follows them as they fall, slowly, but surely, into the water. They stayed on the surface for a bit...but suddenly dissolved into the water, not in sight anymore.* Mason: Like I said, Lindsey completes you...and as much as you believe, you would be as good as done without her. You have beaten the best of the best...and throughout it all, Lindsey was right behind you...she was your motivation. Just as Sara Whitaker is mine...but the difference between me and you is the fact that I am in control...and I plan to keep it that way. Unlike someone like Aiden Copane... *Mason shudders upon hearing this name.* Mason: Aiden Copane...the only thing that makes me hate being Canadian...the only person to single-handily make Canada look like a bunch of retards...and he is a perfect example. He had fame, fortune, glory, glam, the nine yards...but then his girlfriend left. Then, it happened. First went his girlfriend...then went his title...shortly followed by his career. Please, for the sake of MWE, and the human race, don't go down that same road....we don't need another Aiden Copane in the world. Oh, and one final warning Aiden, if you try anything...anything at all, I swear it will be the last thing you do, not in a wheelchair! *Mason steps back, closes the briefcase, and leans against the wall once more. He then shoots off the wall and begins to pace back and forth, as the night got colder. And as the night got colder, so did Mason's attitude.* Mason: And Sinc, this all has something to do with you...and our match. Everything, whether it be championships, relationships, careers, or your win/loss ratio...it all relates back to you...somehow or someway. Like I said, I can not stop thinking about our match, and after Adrenaline Rush...I will be happy to walk out with a smile on my face and my hand raised. When I get my victory, you will see that I am a true champion and you know why? Because history repeats itself. I said this back when I beat Aiden for the title. History repeats itself, and Adrenaline Rush will not disappoint. And, I bet everyone watching has been wondering what is in this briefcase...well, I'll show you. *Mason walks back over, after the pacing stops, and opens up the case. He starts to remove some items, such as a t-shirt, a replica championship belt, and a picture among other things.* Mason: I mean business, always have, always will. Inside this briefcase were some things that some people, possibly yourself, might hold dear to them. I, on the other hand, will sacrifice anything for the win. Winning is what MWE is all about, not the fans, not the reputation, but the wins and the legacy. That is why I come to MWE, to cement my legacy as the greatest. To cement my legacy as a true wrestling phenomenon...and nothing will get in my way of that. *Mason picks up a T-shirt and a hat.* Mason: You see this...this is my merchandise. They sell this in stores, on MWE.com, and I have even seen them being sold in airports. It is everywhere, and people...like David Whitman, who still care about there fans...would do anything to see loyals where these things. On the other hand, I could care less...Winning is all that matters! *Mason tosses the shirt into the body of water, shortly followed by the hat. He then picks up, the replica championship. It is of the Mayhem Wrestling Entertainment Championship.* Mason: This is what my championship looked like when I was MWE Champion. This, and the records on MWE.com are the only things that will officially prove my reign...and while I can't erase the records on MWE.com, I can do this... *Mason looks at the gold, kisses it goodbye, and launches it, with force, into the body of water. There is a much bigger splash, as compared to the clothing. Finally, Mason picks up a box of pictures.* Mason: You see this... *Mason holds the box closer to the camera* ...these are any and all pictures of my school days and my wrestling days before MWE. It is the only thing that I have left of me after my early childhood days...yearbooks, wrestling team photos, pictures of me when I was in my indie federations...EVERYTHING! I am willing to throw it ALL away...for the victory. And I'll do it now. *Mason takes the box and looks inside...he then proceeds to put the box on the wall and slowly dump out all of the pictures and he scrapes out all year books and memorabilia that was in the box. He then shows the box to the camera, confirming that all the contents were removed. Mason places the small box back into the briefcase which he seals up.* Mason: I told you once and I will tell you again, I plan to win and become the new MWE United States Championship...and by new, I mean I will redefine what it means to win the belt. There will be a new look and everything. And just because it is around holiday times and I am feeling extra generous, I will even show you what it will look like! Mason: Do you like, I designed it myself! HA! History will repeat itself...you can bank on that! *Mason walks back to the wall, grabs the briefcase, picks it up, and tosses it off the bridge along with the picture of the new MWE United States Championship design he created.* Mason: It has been well documented, that I am dangerous in the ring. It has been noted that I tend to break my opponents down...break them down in the middle of the the match...until they crack! Until they no longer know what is going on. They have no idea where they are or what is hitting them. They also don't know how to block it or counter it. I make people angry and violent. I make them want to stop everything they are doing... and cry! They want to break down in the corner and let loose, because they realize that they have been played like fools...like amateur. Guys in ICW...they envy guys like me...who can make people step back and think twice about their career. They step back and think...what am I doing in this ring, if I can't beat him?
