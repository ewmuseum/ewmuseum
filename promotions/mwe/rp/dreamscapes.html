Dreamscapes; Kevin Hardaway RP

May 2 2009

Kevin Hardaway

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
QUOTE CODE: Kevin Angie Hayley Main doctor Random doctor Nurse Out into the pouring, drenching rain they go, where Hayley sees him on his knees, face down on the concrete, either letting the water pound on his head like a thumping headache or maybe just not moving at all. Hayley sees this and rushes over to Kevin, worried and freaking out right about now as she trips over to him and shakes him again. He doesn't move. “Kevin? Kevin? Kevin? Wake up.” No movement. “Oh my god. Kevin? Are you ok? Speak to me?!” Once again, no movement or no words coming out of his mouth. “Dammit...SOMEBODY HELP!” “I'm alive, dammit. Can't you see I don't want to be bothered with?” False alarm. Kevin puts his head back up, still on his knees though as he sees Hayley sitting next to him in the front of the hospital. A nurse comes outside after hearing Hayley scream from the top of her lungs but she lets the nurse know about the false alarm. “What the hell are you doing? You're freaking everybody out here!” “Sorry...I was praying actually. Praying that Angie was going to be alright. And then you had to come out here and ruin everything. I'm trying to do what I can right now with the situations I have before me and people have to ruin my style. Is 5 minutes alone all I ask of?” A little forceful coming out of Kevin, not so much like him at all actually. Even though Hayley has no idea whatsoever at what's going on, not even with what Kevin is doing. “You know I have no idea what's going on either. I rushed out here to see if you were ok. I care for Angie as well, but I understand how much you love her. And I know the feeling about losing a close friend, hell...a family member of yours. Because that's what Angie is for you, right?” “You know the answer to that question, so why are you asking me?” “Trying to help out perhaps. Somehow we're in this together because of who is inside there, and if you don't want to help me out, then fine. So be it. Go home and take your ball and chain with you.” Kevin finally gets up and looks straight into the eyes of Hayley, his eyes now as red as the blood that he was covered in during her car accident. Obviously, he's not too happy about what she just said. “Don't you ever say that again. What do you have with her that I don't have? Huh?” Hayley gasps in shock over the thing that Kevin just said. Complete, utter shock. “Excuse me? I have a 15 year friendship with her. I've known her since we were in the 5th grade. We've been through more things than you can handle in your relationship with her. Hell, she's about ready to die and all you do is run out here. What? Are you too pussy to see your wife die in there? Well, fucking take it.” Kevin starts motioning for a front hand right across her face, a slap. Hayley though blocks the slap and nails Kevin on the cheek with a right hook that sends him scattering a little bit. The girl has strength to her, she can tell you that. Kevin gets up though and looks at her, staring, like he's going to throw her down to the ground and kick her square in the head. He turns though and pulls out the keys to his car as he rushes towards there and gets in. Immediately, he skids his tires and gets the hell out of there in the maddest rush anybody's ever seen. So bad that a couple cars stop and pile up, as they honk their horns in a regular driver's road rage. But Hayley sees him drive off and wipes something from her eyes. A couple tears down her face. She sniffs up and heads back inside the hospital. He gets back into his car and doesn't start it up. He just sits there, listening to the words the doctor said, listening to the words that Hayley said to him before he stormed off, listening to every single thing that has happened within the past couple days inside his head, until we hear Angie's monitor going nuts as the doctor yells out for the adrenaline shot to the heart. All of it is heard in a single, insane blur. Kevin opens up his eyes to see himself still in the parking lot. He puts the car in drive and gets back on the road. He starts thinking again. Ever since Angie crashed, he hasn't heard a word yet about her condition and whether or not she's going to make it through. That paranoid feeling starts to get under his skin again. You know that feeling. Where when you hear the slightest pin drop, you expect the worst. Kevin heard it and now he's freaking out himself. He can't keep his eyes out on the road, he keeps looking around. Like something is bound to happen. Well it does. RINGGGGGGGGGG! RINGGGGGGGGGGG! RINGGGGGGGGGGG! It's Hayley. Oh no. It can't be. Angie can't die. Not today. Not ever. Not on his watch. Kevin, in a paranoid state of mind still, all of a sudden gets calm. Eerily calm in fact. He looks at the phone one more time and sees the front of the hospital nearby. But he doesn't put on his turn signal. He just offers up a smile and closes his eyes. “Here I come, Angie my love.” He turns hard left to the middle of the road. CRASH!!! His car immediately crashes into a nearby SUV and goes flying into the air, turning around on it's side, going at least fifty to sixty feet in distance alone. It finally heads back down to earth and skids along it's side as glass and pieces of the car go flying near. The car then ends up flipping a couple times before finally coming to a complete stop, upside down. Kevin opens his eyes slowly now, barely able to at all as he's hanging upside down, bloody and battered. Just like Angie was that fateful night. Faintly he can hear the ambulance and sounds of the EMT's running nearby the wreck as he unballs his bloody fist to reveal his cell. Still working somehow. He then sees a figure, upside down, next to him. An angel perhaps? Kevin tries to regain his focus to try and see who's sitting next to him but his consciousness is fading fast. The figure reaches out a hand to him, and up close, Kevin can see that the figure is a female, and has a ring on her finger. He faintly calls out to her. “Angie? Is that you, sweetheart?" The figure keeps reaching out to him, and once again, barely, he can hear the figure speaking to him. In who appears to be Angie's voice. “It....me. It's.....time.” It sounded like she said something in between, but Kevin's eyes slowly close. He did hear the words that he thought he would've loved to hear. “It's time!” It's time for them to meet and love once again. Kevin smiles as his eyes finally close, seeing his final image in life. Just as the fire department use the Jaws of Life to get him out. The EMT's rush over immediately and try to revive Kevin, as one of them puts his fingers on his neck. “He has a pulse. Alright, very carefully, get him over to the emergency room. Looks like a severe concussion.” =================================================================================== On the other end... Beep....beep....beep. “Phew. Heart rate stabilizing. She's going to be okay. Let Mr. Hardaway and his friend know about Mrs. Hardaway condition, will you, sir?” The nurse nods and opens the door to see Hayley, walking back and forth. She hears the door open and rushes over to the nurse immediately. “Is she....?" “She'll be fine. Her heart rate is going back to normal. She just had a small problem with the medication we were giving her. Apparently it was running right into her immune system, but don't worry. Everything has been squared away. Where's Mr. Hardaway?” “He went out to get some fresh air. I'll call him and let him know.” The nurse nods to Hayley as she steps away and goes to the restroom area to clean up. Meanwhile, Hayley grabs her cell and calls Kevin's number. No answer. “Shit, not this again.” It keeps ringing until, finally, his answering machine comes up. “Hey, it's Kevin. Obviously, I’m not here, so if you need to, leave a message after all of that “Wait for the beep” shit. Thanks.” And just like he said, she gets the “After the tone...” message heard on every answering machine. BEEP! “Hey, it's Hayley. The nurse just told me that Angie is going to be fine. Apparently something happened with her immune system, it wasn't feeding the drugs that the doctors gave her. Bullshit if you ask me, but, if she's going to be ok, then I’m all for it. I hope you get this ASAP and I hope we can put the past between us with what just happened. I'm really sorry that I said those things, believe me. I was afraid for Angie's death as well, believe me. I went haywire and I shouldn't of slapped you. Well, hope you return soon. Bye.” She closes up her cell phone, just as she sees a rush of doctors and EMT's run by her at full speed. Nothing like she's ever seen before. But something suddenly catches her ear by a couple of the doctors. “Car wreck out there. Dude just freakin’ totaled it.” She just had a gut instinct. She had to know, so she yelled out loud. “Did you say “car wreck”? “Yes we did. I mean, it's done for. Real nice car too from what we heard. '67 Mustang. What a beaut, huh?” She drops her jaw to the floor and rushed immediately to follow the doctors. =================================================================================== The message she left could be clearly heard on the cell, as doctors were prepping him on a stretcher. Suddenly, Kevin opens his eyes again and sees Angie, walking near him. Walking with him as he was being rolled back into the emergency room. Angie looks up as she sees Hayley running over to the stretcher and with Kevin's eyes fading away again, she tells him as clear as crystal. “It's not our time. I'm going to be fine and so are you. I'll see you soon. I love you.” =================================================================================== He snaps awake, looking around his environment. Its his bedroom now, Angie sleeping peacefully next to him. That brings a smile to his face, although when he lies back down, he starts breathing heavily, a cold sweat coming down his face as he wipes it away and closes his eyes again. But he can see an orange ring around his blank dark stare, as he opens his eyes and sees that Angie is now awake, the light turned on beside their bed, as she looks over at him, a concerned look upon her face. “What’s wrong, sweetie?” “Bad dream, nothing else.” “Aww, what happened.” “It was when you were in the hospital, and I had that heart attack. Although I didn’t have it, instead I got up, argued with Hayley, she slapped me, and then I took my car and just drove off. Then...I...I purposely crashed it, just because I thought you were dead and wanted to be with you...up there. But you came to me as I guess I was being prepped up on drugs and told me that it wasn’t my time.” “Oh my goodness.” “Then I woke up.” Angie moves over and hugs her husband tight, as he places his head on her chest, his eyes closed. He liked when she comforted him, it just put a good smile upon his face, that’s all. “I’m so sorry, I really am.” “For what? It wasn’t your fault.” “No, I should apologize. Everything that has happened, with the car wreck, and the near-death experience. You shouldn’t be subjected to any of that.” Angie is now gently running her fingers through Kevin’s hair, his eyes closed as he just rests with his loving wife. “I know, but now I have to...after all of this has happened, after who hit you, after all of the details coming out. I know that’s eating you up inside.” “It is eating me up inside, but things will happen sooner or later, trust me. He’ll pay...he’ll pay.” “Ok, love. Can we go back to sleep?” “Sure.” They both share a quick smile at one another, as Angie goes over to turn off the lamp on her night stand, Kevin still resting on her as she holds him close. Kevin reaches an arm around her and holds her just as close as she does, falling back asleep fast, just like that. Still the loving couple. =================================================================================== A few hours later... It’s morning now. 8:15 AM to be exact as Kevin slowly rises from under the covers of his bed as he opens up his eyes and looks at the clock on his night stand. Yep, same time as we just mentioned. He looks over and sees one of his favorite things in the morning. Angie, sleeping peacefully, like an angel as the sun barely shines over her, leaving a beautiful glow to her. He goes over to her and hugs her from behind, her eyes opening as well as she turns around to face her husband. “Good morning.” “Morning.” “How did you sleep the rest of the night?” “Ok. Nothing happened.” “I’m glad to hear that, love.” The two end up sharing a kiss...a long, sweet, passionate kiss. But then...the mood killer arises. Crying can be heard from the other room, as they break off the kiss, a sigh upon both of their faces. “D’oh...Kimmy’s up.” “I’ll go get her, love. Don’t worry. I was going to make breakfast anyways. Figured you could use it.” “That’s so nice of you.” “Don’t hold your breath.” The two end up sharing a laugh in the process. “Seriously...” “Psh. Fine.” As Angie gets up and puts on a t-shirt (you’re not seeing anything, so don’t hold your breath either...perverts) to cover herself, Kevin gets up as well. “What are you doing?” “As my dad says to me...shit, shower, and shave. Or in my case, promo, shower, shave.” “Your dad said that?” “I know...weird, huh?" Angie chuckles to him as she opens the door. Kimmy’s still crying, but not loud enough so the couple has to yell. “Well, you go do that. I have to deal with our alarm clock.” The two share one last kiss for now as Angie leaves the room. Kevin closes the door back up again, possibly so that during his talk, the baby doesn’t yell so loud that you can’t hear a word he’s saying. The camera is on now, as Kevin sits on the edge of the bed, trying to wake himself up, scratching the minor beard he has growing. He hangs his head down and sighs out loud. If there's one part of my life that I haven't gotten to sever ties with, it's my dream world. Yeah, ever since I left my glory days behind me and spiraled downwards, I've dealt with some deep, emotional problems. Everything from hallucinations to things I can't even explain, to even nightmares. Everything just came up tenfold then after Angie's car accident. Because after all of the things that I had to go through, she was the one who told me that everything was going to be ok sooner or later. And I knew damn well that after that car accident, she wasn't going to go away dying, just to see me completely lose my mind. That's why I think she came through everything. Usually, she's not the strongest person out there, her immune system can be weak at times. But I guess she saw what I was going through, something that couldn't be explained, she saw that. And it was because of that where she became the strongest warrior and the very reason she's in the other room making me, her, and our kid's breakfast. He gets up and opens the door slightly. Already, his daughter has stopped crying as he looks in the kitchen and sees Kimmy in her high chair, looking all around the room, cute as a button, as Angie is getting stuff out of the fridge. Kevin seems to smile at this. Everything seems to be gone now. All the hallucinations, all the weird stuff. But...there's one thing that I can't sever ties with, I can't move on with no matter how much I tried. And that's my dream world. Your dreams are supposed to be your gateway to a different place. During my childhood, if I was ever having a bad day at school, I would go home and just go to sleep, hoping that my dreams would take me away to a place away from this world, into a world that I could call my own. Where I could do anything, be anything, think anything. Half of the time now, I would like to do that. But lately, with everything going on....their have been no dreams. None at all. Every time I go to bed with Angie, I start to dream of an even more vicious world, a brutal society. I think of all of the bad things that have happened in my life. I think of things that are demonic to the human eye, things that would fit in a horror movie more than real life. Everybody should know about nightmares. Everybody's had them. And I’m sure there's been a few people who have dealt with recurring ones. This car accident...you have no idea how bad it is for not only her...but for me. You think that I would be the calm one in all of this. But no...I’m just as paranoid as her. Almost every night, I have nightmares about that fateful day. Doesn't matter the time or setting, that car crash still comes up somehow. I've had dreams set in the future, set in the past, set in a different country, set in the nighttime, daytime, rain, sleet, snow, tidal wave, I had them all and somehow...all of them involve Angie's wreck. And now I can add "me being in a car wreck" to the list of recurring dreams. There have been a couple nights where I couldn't even sleep because I didn't want to dream. How bad has it gotten to the point where your wife is worried about you? How messed up do you think when your own daughter could even see it? I can hear her crying at night, because I know that somehow, deep within her little brain, even though it's not fully functional yet. But she knows when her daddy isn't the way he is. Once again, he looks over to see Angie making eggs it looks like. He looks at his daughter once again as well and this time, she ends up looking right back at him. Even though she’s 1 and a half, she can see her daddy in the crack of the door. She ends up laughing and pointing at him, as Angie lets her know that it was indeed daddy in the other room. Kevin smiles and chuckles. I'm rambling on about something that you guys don't really care about, aren't I? I shouldn't even be worrying right now, because right now I don't have to care about the person who almost killed the person I love more than anything else in this world. But when situations come up like that, you kind of have to worry. So what I’ll do right now is take "THIS", and bundle it up into a nice little ball, and I’ll throw it into the back of my head. You see...this week, this match should be easy for you Devlin. You're facing a guy who just got the shock of his life, a guy who feels like he's been shattered by somebody who almost destroyed his world for good. I'm somehow thinking right now that you're just watching this and you're just laughing your ass off, like you don't care. Like you think "Hey, fresh pickings this week." You don't care about what's going on right now, do you? All you care about is yourself, trying to once again make a name for yourself. Tell me, how many times is this now? Three? Four? Twenty? I don't know since I've only been here for about a month and a half, but I can tell you straight up that it's probably in that ballpark. He scratches up his beard again. The darn thing can be itchy from time to time, and he doesn’t like it when it grows out. He likes to be balder than...you know, I have no analogy for that. Moving on. I've only been here a month and a half and I’ve already heard your horror stories. About how you don't even seem to have a care when you're out there, doing a promo or two. That all you care about is yourself. And you know what? It just sickens me to a point where I want to run you over and throw your dead little carcass into a volcano. People like you, you aggravate me to a point where I feel like I shouldn't even be in this business anymore. Why do people, like me, deserve to be wrestling when it's people like you who just walk in, think they know a bunch of moves that look cool can make them into a star that quick? Because they want the fame, they want the fortune. They don't give two damns about this thing we call "wrestling", they just want to come in, look like a douchebag because they can, and steal the thunder, just so they can laugh in your face. You know, I didn’t want to even compare here. But you know, I have to. And god forbid me if I end up getting into a "shoot" here, but...in my career, in everybody's career actually, you come across these people. They could be in droves, it could only just be one person. But even if it's only one person, he can still make sure to make everybody's life a living hell. And that's what you seem to be, Devlin. Some kind of worthless little nothing that thinks that by acting like a toolbox, he'll get the recognition he deserves. In Uncultured Anarchy, that's what they say the second before they get completely and utterly destroyed out there. People like you, I just can't fathom people like you. I couldn't, in all my life, and I can't here. You remind me...of somebody who I used to wrestle with. He goes by the name of Creeping Death. He goes by a real name now, but I’ll always remember him by that name. I'm not going to name drop the promotion, but I will name drop the name of the guy who I just mentioned, although it just pains me to even say his damn name because just saying it causes the entire world to just shudder up in the fetal position like they were about to be lynched by a mass murderer. And just like that...he does shudder. All of the bad memories, the thoughts, the matches he saw with him in it. The simple fact that he didn’t care. By the way, no clips of this person are being shown. If he decided to show clips, he’d probably get sued by said person...and would’ve been called an “asshole”. Kevin hangs his head down once again, a little fuming from the last time out as he cracks his fingers, the anger expressed by those cracks. I look at you, Devlin, and you look like the bastard child of his offspring. Seeing your promos, seeing the way you wrestle, seeing your gimmick, seeing everything about you, just gives me the very worse thought that maybe you two are third cousins of one another or something. You bitch about nothing, you only care about yourself, and seemingly time and time again, you seem to get more shots than your sister gets, treating her VD. No offense to the MWE at all, but that's what it seems like. Most of your moves could be finishers in a standard wrestling company, your still stuck in the past, the list just keeps on going like a damn Energizer bunny. The only thing that you guys don't compare to, is that Creeping Death is just a FLAT...OUT...douchebag. You're a FLAT...OUT...LAZY...douchebag. I know, I'm talking about a piece of my past right now, but I’m trying to make a point here. The point is that people like you don't even belong here. Like Zak mentioned in his little spiel, Uncultured Anarchy is here to make sure that these people who think they are better than who they say they are, don't even get a CHANCE to prove that claim. Because we're proving it for them that they don't have a chance in HELL. Remember the "hype" that Greg Cherry promised all of you? Nope. I made sure that his "hype" was nothing more than so. Jekyl and Scotty McKnight? They only proved that they should've left when ICW closed it's doors...now look at Scotty McKnight. Zak caved his freakin' head in and now he's out of action. The next person in this little crusade now...is you, Devlin Sharpe. And this time, it's going to be for good. I'll make sure you never come back here and make sure that you go somewhere else to continue your little charade. Maybe go to where Creeping Death is, have one big giant orgy. It's your call. Kevin laughs at the joke he just made. Even though he has a wry sense of humor, it never seems to come out whenever he’s in the ring. I guess it’s just common nature, but who really knows. He laughs again though. Something else has came up into his head... You know. All this talk about this "Creeping Death" toolbox, and you know...I never faced him in the middle of the ring. At least on an one-on-one scale. Devlin? To me, you're the closest thing I am going to get to him. Kevin sighs, as Angie calls to him in the other room. “Sweetie...breakfast is ready.” “Coming, hun.” Kevin gets up and opens the door. But he doesn’t walk out to the kitchen to greet his family. Instead, he walks back into his bedroom and sits back on the edge of the bed. Forgot to turn off the camera I suppose? Oh...one more thing before I leave... D.C., once I’m done throwing Devlin Sharpe's pimply carcass ass out of this ring...I’m coming for YOU. Do yourself a favor and watch out, 'mmkay? Thank you. Kevin winks to the camera. As soon as he said the magic name, Angie immediately turned her head to look at her husband making that imposing threat. She starts to shake just a tad, but she takes a drink of water, and it stops almost immediately as she sits down. Kevin joins in with his family and kisses his loving daughter on the head as gentle as a summer breeze and kisses Angie on the top of her head as he sits down next to her. A family moment indeed as the scene ends up fading.
