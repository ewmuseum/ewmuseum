~10:20 pm Independence Day~ Vega rushed out to ringside along with several EMTs. His best friend, and former tag team partner, Leon ‘The Virus’ Roberts was just knocked out cold by Mason Resoan during their Iron Man Tables match. Due to this, Mason had won by default. But even he was concerned for the one who was the embodiment of Sin. Vega: Leon, are you okay? There was no response. Vega grabbed his hair, and pulled a bit of it in frustration. He grinded his teeth, and the EMTs had managed to get the big guy onto a stretcher. They began to wheel him away from ring, and up the ramp. Vega followed. Vega know his friend was still alive, because he checked for a pulse himself. Cameras followed them, as Leon was wheeled backstage. When they got to the parking lot, the ambulance was waiting there already. Vega climbed in the back. He felt his blood boiling up. He was getting angry. Quickly realizing that would get him nowhere, he took a deep breath, and sighed. He was calmed down. He didn’t need a random episode with his darker personality right now. ~2 hours later......~ Vega took out his cell phone. He was in the hospital waiting room. He had just seen Leon himself, and he knew that Leon would be fine now. The doctors at the hospital were able to quickly diagnose what had happened. It was a concussion. Vega dialed Demonica’s cell phone. Demonica (phone): Hello? Vega: Hey De. It’s Vega. Demonica (phone) Oh hi Vega. Wait, that didn’t sound like you were just calling me. What’s wrong now? Vega: I’m in the hospital right now. Leon had suffered a concussion during his match. A dropkick was landed very wrong at a sore spot of his skull. Demonica (phone): WHAT?!?!?!?!?!?! Vega: Calm down. Leon will be fi- Demonica (phone):-THAT SON OF A BITCH!!! When I get my hands on Mason Resoan, I’m going to repeatedly use a fucken iron fucking ball to his balls, then I’ll fucken rip his fucking balls off by hanging him with a fucken rope, force him to eat his fucken balls, take a fucken nail gun to use him as target practice. Then I’m going to fucken shove a pineable the size of a fucken whale to shove it up his ass, before I slice his head off, and have a crack addict puke down his fucken NECK!!!! He then heard her take a deep breath. Demonica (phone): I’m sorry. I’ve been very frustrated lately with everything. The triplets, Liberty, Leon and his cavalier attitude towards life. Vega *chuckles*: That’s okay De. I’m more surprised if anything. Trust me, you’re not the only person who was thinking that. But all it was, was an accident. Nothing more, nothing less. Mason was really sorry about the whole thing as well. Demonica sighed over her end of the phone. She heard the triplets crying. She sighed yet again. Vega knew this it was the triplets this time, for he could also hear them. Vega: Well, the only thing I can say here is that we will be flown over to Toronto General tomorrow. On top of that, The doctors are going to be putting him into a sedative induce coma. They say it will help him heal faster, or something like that. Demonica (phone): Fuck. Not another episode of him being high of of that shit. I swear, he’s doing this to himself. You’ve known him longer then I have. Does he suffer from addictions? Vega: Only two that I know of. Sex and violence. I know he drinks, smokes, and occasionally does recreational drugs, on top of the fact he used to be a drug dealer to pay for his own food, as far as I can recall, he doesn’t have any serious and self destructive addictions. Demonica (phone): *sigh* Well that is a sigh of relief.. Anyways, I better go. The triplets are still crying. Call me when you get into Toronto. Vega: Will do. Vega ended the call. He pressed his thumb and index finger against his forehead. His watch then beeped. He sighed. He walked over to the vending machine, and bought a can of coke. He then took out a prescription bottle, and took a little red pill out. He popped it in his mouth, and took a sip of the coke to swallow it. He then proceeded to drink the rest of the can, before he trashed it. He belched, as he discovered his cell phone was ringing. It was Richard Covell. Vega answered it. Vega: Hello? Covell (phone): Vega. Glad I caught you. How is Leon? Vega: He’ll recover quickly enough. Covell (phone): Good. Anyways, I just wanted to let you know a couple of things. One, MWE will be paying for Leon to get home, as I was notified by one of the EMTs that he will be flown to Toronto. And second, I just want to say I’m sorry for not booking you lately. Vega: Sir, you need not worry about it. I understand these things happen. Besides, I’m still trying to sort out my head and stuff. Covell (phone): Ah. Well you will be on the next show. We’ve booked you against Chris England and Zack Crash. Vega: Nice. Thanks for letting me know sir. The conversation was finished there. Not wanting anymore calls to deal with, Vega turned off his cell phone. He decided he would check out of the hotel he and Leon were staying at, with their stuff, when they were ready to leave. ~Next day, 10 AM~ The doctors had Leon placed in a helicopter, still attached to a few machines. Vega was there with him, having just gotten all their bags packed and loaded on. They began their five hour journey up to Toronto, from Jacksonville. Vega sighed to himself. Vega: I wish I could honestly say that there was something I could have done to stop this, but there isn’t. All this is, is a freak accident. It’s so weird. Last time you were like this, we thought it was really serious. But this trumps it. I just hope that you’ll recover quickly, my friend. As twisted and screwed up as you are, I couldn’t ask for a better person outside of my family to call my brother. *sigh* But….I guess it’s a good thing that the triplets are only six months old. Still……I think even they are able to sense your pain. I promise you this though. I will do what I can to win my match coming up. May not have wrestled in a while, but I will rock Atlanta. I must. Vega smiled a bit, as he let a lone tear run down his face. One of the few times he was truly emotional. He wiped it away, and then tried to fall asleep for the ride home. ~Later 4pm~ Having just arrived at Toronto General, Leon was immediately escorted to one of the very few remaining empty rooms for him to remain in until his concussion had healed to a good point. Vega wasn’t allowed in just yet. But he smiled, as there was somebody else he wanted to talk to. He found the man who he was looking for walking right in. The man, who was a doctor, immediately recognized Vega. Vega: Doctor Meridian. Good to see you again. Dr Meridian smiled when he saw the young schizophrenic prankster. He was the one who had diagnosed Vega’s split personality problem in the past, and used to be a confidant for Vega while he was in mental therapy. Dr Meridian: Vega Knight. Well I didn’t expect to see you here for a while. How you been? Vega: Been better. My friend, Leon, was hospitalized after a gnarly accident. Dropkick to a weak spot on the head. He’s got a concussion. Dr Meridian: Really? Wow. Well I can’t say a whole lot about that. But have the prescription I gave you been working? Vega: Yeah somewhat. I’m having a much better time controlling my dark side these days, though there are some days where I do hear that voice, whispering me to strike down those who doubt me. Dr Meridian: Well Vega, I’ve done all that I could for you. The best advice I can say is, that your doubts cause your inner, more corrupt personality to grow stronger. The more you try to doubt yourself, the more you will have to deal with him. But if you can harness your condition to the best of your abilities, then you will become a more powerful force then before. Vega: Yeah. Thanks sir. Dr Meridian: No problem. Though you won’t blame me if I say that because what happened when you admitted yourself originally, with my assistant, that I’m a bit skeptical of how serious you’re taking this. Vega chuckles at that. . He then recalled what the assistant looked like. Long slender legs. Well equipped in both tits and ass, and has the most shiny blond hair that was possible. In Vega’s book, she was irresistible. But she was just another notch on his bedpost, so to speak. Vega: Yeah I get it. Trust me sir. I would love to get rid of that side of me. To become like how I was. But I can’t. It’s something I know I’ll have to deal with the rest of my life. Dr Meridian smiled, as he left, waving goodbye. At that very moment, out of the corner of his eye, Vega saw Demonica walking into the hospital. Well, it was almost more like running. Demonica soon appeared right infront of Vega. The two friends embraced each other, as Demonica let out a frustrated sigh. Demonica: Not again. I can’t stand the sight of him like this. Vega: Me neither. But he will be fine. I promise you that. Demonica: I know. Demonica looked over into Leon’s room, before she entered it. Vega sighed again. He rubbed his temples together, trying to not let the overall stress get to him. He didn’t have to wait long before Demonica walked back out. She looked a bit relieved, but still not in the greatest of moods. Demonica: I need to get out of here Vega. I need a drink in the worst way. Let’s go barhopping tonight. Vega: You want to do that? I’m surprised De. Demonica: Yeah yeah. I need to really let loose, and drinking is the best way for me. Besides, you forget. I’m from Mexico……I’m a natural party animal. Vega: That’s true, I guess. Demonica then walked out of the hospital. Vega went to grab his and Leon’s bags, before he met up with her in the parking lot. She had brought the Hummer with her this time around. Vega: Well, if you plan on drinking, leave the car at home. Between the two of us, there won’t be a designated driver. Demonica: I know. Vega puts the bags in the back, before he stepped in the big vehicle. Demonica then turned around and left the hospital parking lot, leaving the fee she had to pay for being there. ~Next Morning~ Vega groggily woke up. His vision was extremely blurry. He was definitely hung over. Way to much drinking from the night before. He shielded his eyes from the sunlight, as his vision began to clear up. He noticed a few things out of place. One, he noticed that there were a bunch of clothes on the floor. Two, that the bedroom was not of his own. Immediately, his common sense started working, and he began to panic a bit. He slowly turned around, and noticed the one thing he feared. It was Demonica. She was underneath some covers, and like Vega, she to, was naked. Though she did have a small smile on her face. Vega hit his forehead with his palm, and his head really began to hurt due to that. Vega:……I’m fucked. Vega tried to recall the events of last night. They had hit a few bars. Demonica had more to drink then he did, which really surprised him to no end. They took a cab back to her home. And apparently, he had discovered that Alcohol makes Demonica extremely horny, as when they got in, she practically jumped right on him, before leading to what did happen. He then looked around, and noticed that in the trashcan on his side, there were a couple of rubbers. Freshly used. He smiled, knowing they at least did it with protection. It was then, that Demonica woke up. Demonica: Ugh. Damn. She then noticed Vega. Her eyes widened, before she sat right up, covering herself. Vega looked over, and slowly nodded. Demonica: Holy shit. So we…? Vega nodded again. Demonica ran one hand through her hair, in a very nervous manner. Her breathing had suddenly become laboured, but she was able to regain control over her breathing. Demonica: Did we at least use a condom? Vega weakly smiled, while nodding again. Demonica sighed in relief. She was still nervous about the whole ordeal. Vega was as well, but he controlled himself better. Vega: Well, it was an accident I guess. But call me an ass, but I don’t regret it. Demonica: Given the circumstances, I don’t either. But let’s not talk about this. This will be our secret. Vega: Yeah. Our dirty little secret, I guess. Demonica scoffed at him, as she leaned over, and grabbed Vega’s orange silk boxers. She tossed them over, and he caught them, before putting them on, before climbing out from under the covers. Vega: Once this headache of mine wears off, I’ll leave. I have stuff to do anyways. I hope you don’t mind. Demonica: Not at all. Vega smiled. It was an awkward smile, to say the least. He looked around the room, and gathered his clothes, followed by putting them on. Once dressed, he left the room, and went downstairs. He quickly then called a cab, so he could go to his apartment. ~Present Day, 3pm~ Vega was in a good mood. He had just gotten a call, from Leon himself. Leon had just woken up from his coma, and promptly called Vega to let him know about it. After hearing that, Vega had popped another pill, and digested it. He looked at the prescription bottle, and immediately, he frowned. Vega: Three quarters of the way done already? Damn. But these pills do work. It’s a good thing that MWE management know I am on this stuff. I gotta get a refill on this. But……I got better things to do right now. Vega put the prescription on the counter, when suddenly, he heard a knock on his door. He sighed. He was pretty much in no mood for visitors right now. Reluctantly, Vega walked over to the door, and peered through the hole. Needless to say, Vega was Very surprised. But who he saw actually made him very happy. It was his older brother, Liam. Vega quickly unlocked the door, to let the slightly taller, black haired man come in. Vega: Hey Bro. You should have called first though, the place is a mess. Liam: Naw man. Doesn’t bother me. I just wanted to check up on you. You’ve been kind of distant lately. Why is that? Vega: ……It’s the schizophrenia again. Got to the point where I had to go back on the medication again. Almost………… Vega didn’t want to continue what he was saying. Liam noticed the sad look in his brother’s eyes, and was really curious as to what Vega could have possibly have done that would cause him to go back on the medication to suppress his darker personality. Liam: Uhhhh bro…….what it is that you did? Vega: I……I can’t. To…..painful. Liam: That’s a new one. What could Vega Knight……One of the most badass, unpredictable, humorous people I know………..could have possibly have done that made him scared enough to go back on the medication? But tell me, why haven’t I seen you around much on TV lately? Vega: Oh that…well the boss wanted me to get used to the meds, which they know to excuse from any drug tests. Liam: Yeah I’ll bet that has really pissed a lot of people off. Vega: No kidding. Vega then walked over to the fridge, and grabbed a couple of beers. He brought one to Liam, who gladly accepted it. They both opened them, and did a toast. They both saty on the couch. After taking a swig, Vega put his beer down. Vega: But yeah. More then ever now, I’m looking forward to getting back into the ring. It wouldn’t surprise me if I was almost forgotten about. It seems that people think I don’t matter much anymore. I admit, it’s a cheap way to win, but in wrestling, those kind of rules don’t apply. You win by whatever means are available. Liam: That’s true. I won’t disagree with you there. Vega: Exactly.
