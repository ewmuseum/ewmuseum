Another On-Screen Staff Member Added!

Jul 27 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A little less than a week ago, there was a Staff Announcement on MWE.com, letting the MWE fanatics know that MWE is getting some Fresh Blood. Samuel Steele and Freddy Klyde are in business and rolling trying to make a name for themselves. But that doesn't mean that there isn't more. MWE is proud to announce that yet another On-Screen Staff Member has appeared on the scene! THE METHODICAL MADMAN - RJ (Robert Joesph) Carusso - A former actor and director, this man is quite a character. He is very meticulous and knows how to intimidate his interviewers. He will ask questions that get under your skin or make you think. He won't be afraid to and will break you down and pick you apart if you aren't quick enough, because that's what he loves to do. Being associated with big time stars and small town dreamers, he has diversity in him, and can handle anyone or anything.
