Crack a Smile?

Sep 19 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It's dark... Enough light illuminates the area to show a dirty floor scattered with broken glass, turned over furniture, scattered papers, and a mad tied up behind a rusty old metal desk. The man is wearing a clown mask with no eye holes, muffles are heard coming from behind the mask. Jekyl:Ladies and gentlemen....for this promo I have1 man assisting me...He is a man you all know and love, a man you see on the funniest late night t.v talk show... Jekyl walks past the camera in front of the man, he leans over and whispers something in the man's ear, he looks at the camera with one hand in front of the mask and begins to grope it. He then rips it off snapping the mans head back, duct tape over his mouth, we see it is Jay Leno Jekyl: Jay Leno..... Jekyl rips off the duct tape resulting in an earsplitting scream from Leno... Jay: What the hell? Why am I here? Jekyl: Well, instead of coming to your studio for an interview, I decided to "invite" you to my "studio"... My apologies for the mess, but you have a job to do... Jay: And what if I refuse? Jekyl: You sir aren't leaving here until you make me smile... Jay takes one look at Jekyl and smirks... Jay: Look your already smiling.... can I go now? Jekyl makes a straight face and leans in closer to Jay. his scars become more visible as Leno winces at the sight of the scar tissue. Jekyl: Start interviewing Lone, or you aren't leaving here... Jay: FINE!!! Jekyl pulls up a chair beside the rusted desk and straightens his jacket, runs his fingers through his hair and licks his lips. Jay: Good evening ladies and gentlemen, tonight I'm joined by MWE superstar Jekyl....How are you tonight? Jekyl: I'm fine, would be better with a world title around my waist. Jay: Tell me about it, looks like you need something to draw attention away form those pants.... Jekyl shoots Leno cut eye look and tells him to continue... Jay: So what do you think of this election? Jekyl: I don't care... I do what I want, when I want, what do you think? Jay: McCain is an idiot... I remember what Obama said about Sarah Palin..."You can put lipstick on a pig... but it's still a pig" but it's more like "you can put lipstick on a cunt... but in the end it will still end up being Sarah Palin" Jekyl sits there with a blank expression... Jay: Nothin? Jekyl: Time's running out... Jay: OK ok...So with your next opponent being Sinc Mercier, who now holds the US title, What's your plan? Jekyl: Sinc.. I hear as a child he wanted to be a comedian, yet his father pushed him towards wrestling, which he ended up striving at...Yet at the same time he also became a comedian, cause no matter what anyone thinks...I think he's a joke... Jekyl smiles and chuckles a bit as Jay returns the smile... Jay: Look! Your smiling! I'm free!! Jekyl: Nice try, but I made myself smile... Leno curses under his breath and begins to look around for a minute, the atmosphere has become tense with his fear and Jekyl's intimidation. Jay has never interviewed someone so evil, so insane....so Jekyl like.. Jay: I'm not used to this kind of pressure, and it's nearly impossible to make you laugh! Jekyl: You can do it I know you can, all you have to do....is NOT act serious...have fun with it! Jay: Fine... So with this match coming up, its not for the belt but it's to see if you can obtain that belt later on... Jekyl: Yeah, I'm not done going after the world title, but for now I think a change of pace is needed, and such as the US title...Sinc will step into the ring and will shit himself, I mean look at yourself, you have the same look towards me as everyone else that sees me, they see a man who inflicts torture and pain, but these people like yourself....they don't realize the pain they give me, the pain I have gone through. When all is said and done, I will rule this company and then the world, my goal is to cause the torture and pain on Sinc, to show him my years of suffering in one single match, I will make him litterally give me the title so he can walk off cry like a baby, then head to yuk yuks to make fun of me, JUST to end up getting shit thrown at him cause of his lack of talent not only on stage, but in the ring too... Jay kind of leans back astonished at what Jekyl is saying... His face doesnt have the look of fear anymore, but insight, he is now interested on Jekyl's background. Jay: For those who don't know, care to explain some of your past? Why you are the way you are? Also give some insight on your intentions? Jekyl: My past? Alright...It all started with my father, who was a fiend and an alcoholic...he came home one night, more drunk than usual and started a ruckus, and my mother grabbed a knife out of the kitchen to protect herself, which my father didn't like. With me watching he grabbed the knife and came over to me and asked me "Why so serious?", which in turn i had no reply, he repeated "WHY SO SERIOUS?!?! Lets give this serious boy a smile...." then he gave me these scars. I know people get tired of hearing that story but it's who I am... As for the way I am, well that story lies on a different plain of reasoning. Jekyl bows his head in thought, a humming appears in the background....Jekyl lets it go without much notice... Jekyl: After my father and I had our incident, I left home, fighting for my very survival, a child of the concrete jungle... Everyday was a battle, wondering where my next meal would come from, where the warmest and driest place to sleep was... Fighting for my own... All these people who grew up with everything handed to them are the first to go, and I will be the one to end them... Sonny Williams knows what the past of war is like, he knows about regret, and that was his downfall... he worried to much about his past, dedicating his fights to the dead, which will in the end, give no satisfaction... Jay: That's deep... With Sinc, didn't he grow up with a golden spoon? Jekyl: Most likely...Most of the superstars here did...But I for one don't care... In the end, win or loss I will be the one inflicting the most pain! All of a sudden shouts are heard outside the room, the frosted glass window to the left shows some dark shadows lining up against the wall... Jekyl: What the fuck? Voice: RYDER JAMES, WE HAVE THE PLACE SURROUNDED! COME OUT WITH YOU HANDS UP! Jekyl: Well this was unexpected... Do me a favor Jay, and tell them you came willingly? Jay: I don't think they are gonna believe that while I'm tied to a dirty old chair... Jekyl cracks a smile, Jay realizes what has happened and grins widely. Jekyl: Heh you made me smile.... Now tell the nice S.W.A.T team that your unharmed and came here of your own accord... Jay: No problem...can you untie me now? Jekyl: Of course... Jekyl unties Leno and they walk to the door, As Leno is the first to open the door he is met at gunpoint. Jay: Whoah whoah boys... the man you want is behind me...He let me go... Jekyl's smile drops out of expression as he just realized what has happened, but his smile returns... Jekyl: You see officers, Jay Leno and I were scheduled to do an interview.... but I decided to have it at a place where I would be comfortable, Jay agreed... Swat Officer: Is this true Mr.Leno? Jay: No! He tied me to a damn chair after kidnapping me from my dressing room in L.A! Jekyl: This man is obviously distressed, he walked out of this room with a smile and unbound, the mess behind me is from before we got here, as you know this is an old abandoned hospital. The swat officers look around and see no sign of struggle and deduce that this is a hoax, Leno stands there unfathomed at what just happened, Jekyl just got away with kidnapping Leno, no real sign of distress, he had this all planned out. Jay: Unbelievable, whatever I got a show to do... Jekyl: Thanks for the interview Jay! The SWAT exit along with Leno...the scene goes quiet, the dense musk of years of neglect and sanitation rejection linger in the air, Jekyl paces the room in sight of something, unsure of what to find... The scene closes as Jekyl is the only one left in the building, still sitting in that dirty room... he bends down and picks up a picture and a metal tablet.... in the picture he sees a woman about the age of 32 holding a baby... the tablet reads... "Norah James M.D" Jekyl: Everywhere I go I find artifacts of my past...Oh well, I got a match to prepare for...Sinc Mercer may have been victorious on a night, where I was not, but his luck has ran out, for the show is named after my favorite pastime, in Mayhem, I will become the nightmares he had as a kid, that made his scream and cry like a little bitch, to his 1 cent mommy Jekyl gently lays the picture down and places the name tablet neatly on the desk, he then quickly glares at the camera... Jekyl: Something I forgot to add into my interview before i was rudely interrupted... MWE to me is like a ladder, at the top of this ladder is Sonny Williams... at the very bottom is the Tag Title, then around the middle is the US title, just before the top is the X-TV Title... Now to get to the top of this ladder there are steps i need to take, and just like a regular ladder, there are steps i can skip... My plans... Are to step on Sinc Mercier, take his belt, and climb back up to the top of this proverbial ladder... I will be number one and I don't care who i have to go through to get there... After Sinc I go after Aeron... and then who ever holds the Gold at the top.... I won't stop till this goal is met... Jekyl exits the room and walks down the deserted corridors.... The echo of faded screams and Jekyl's laughs reverberate off the dead hallway walls....[/i] Heeheehaa haa haah hoo hhaaaaaa hummmmmm HAAHAHAHA!!!! -FADE-
