The Man Himself.; Dedicated to Tim Fitzgerald.

Mar 9 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[OOC. The following 1000 word RP may seem short, but it's straight from the heart. I want you all to know that despite whatever differences and disagreements we may have from time to time or even the lasting ones, I'm honored to be a part of this federation, and truly believe each and every one of you are what makes this place special. And despite anything bad that might be going on in my life, I'm honestly glad that we could all join together to pay our respects and honor the man who made all of this possible. Thank you all. I mean it.] ------------------------------- The scene slowly opens up revealing MWE’s own David Whitman dressed his signature black pants and a “Tim 4 life” shirt. Next to him, also leaning on the railing is his cousin, James Whitman, dressed very similarly. The camera pans out to show that they are actually in an arena; not a huge one, but an intimate one that surely brings back memories to each and every wrestler about his or her humble beginnings. James: This… is the arena formerly known as Syndicate Hall. He turns to David, who nods approvingly, before turning back to the camera. James: For those who don’t know, this is where all of the early WrestleCrazy Entertainment shows were held. Back before even I started wrestling with the company, some amazing athletes perfected their craft here… All because of one man. James moves forward and starts walking towards the ring as David and the camera follow him. James: Later, when the fed got bigger and better, they renamed this arena to WrestleCrazy Hall. I like to think of this as the place where it all got started. James enters the old, slightly downtrodden ring as the cameraman and David follow him still. As he intently slaps the looser-than-regulation-tightness ropes, some dust shakes loose. Not an avalanche, but enough to show that a couple of years have passed since this particular ring was in use. James: Paul Timmons was the man who founded WcE, and Rocky Stevens was well-known as a huge influence within the company as well as later becoming the next owner. But what a lot of people don’t know is that behind the scenes, it was all made possible because of one man… Tim Fitzgerald. James hops up onto the top turnbuckle and sits there with his hands on his legs as David walks over to him and rests on the ropes next to him. James: A federation takes more than one person to create. It takes… a society. It takes a society to build it up so that it becomes a living, breathing entity that while needing all its parts to function at maximum capacity, can live on without them, should they fail one by one. James takes a deep breath and looks at the camera. James: Without getting into too much detail, I can tell you right here and right now that without Tim Fitzgerald, there would have been NO WcE, WWA, eWa, SWA, WHQ, or MWE. And if we’re talking about direct responsibility… Tim was the God that created both the entity known as WcE and the one called MWE. His eyes start scanning around. James: And like all creations, they each had their weaknesses and bad times, but will always be remembered for their unbelievable strengths and amazing moments. James stops talking, looks at his cousin and pats him on the back. David takes a few steps forward as he looks into the camera. David: I can honestly say that Tim has been and to this day is THE most important person to my cousin’s wrestling career, as well as my own. Without him James would not have gotten as far as he has. Same goes for me. He takes a couple of extra hesitant steps as he begins to wander around the ring slowly. David: Lately, things haven’t been so great for me. Personally or professionally. In the ring, backstage, physically… whatever. But this… match, this whole tribute to Tim… it’s something I’ve been looking forward to for a while. Finally, we can let go of our personal crap… ALL of us. We get the chance to just let go of the stress… the worrying if we’ve been training hard enough, worrying how we’re gonna do in the next match or the one after that… we let go of it all, and get in that ring for something that really matters. To honor a man who was important to all of us, whether we choose to acknowledge it or not. Tim loved wrestling. Not the holding grudges part, backstage politics, money, fame… Tim genuinely loved WRESTLING. And he always did an exceptional job bringing the highest quality possible to the people, and everyone respected and admired him for that. Besides that, he was always a good friend. A a funny, smart guy. Insightful, and, I know it’s been said a lot; a true genius. A master at his art form. Honorable, and yet at the same time down to earth. I miss the man. David looks up at the sky as he continues talking. David: He was always avid about being true to yourself. If you felt things weren’t going right, or something that shouldn’t be happening was happening, or people were saying stuff you didn’t feel right about, he would urge you to open up. He was big on being honest, and rightfully so. He looks back at the camera as his cousin jumps off the turnbuckle and walks over to him. David: So, let’s be honest. Alejandro Rodriguez is a man I have immense respect for. A guy with amazing talent and, despite anything he tells you, a huge heart. We all know the true Alejandro Rodriguez; he IS MWE. And we have quite some history together, so this match is gonna be amazing. This is the kind of match Tim could get behind. Two evenly matched athletes fighting to see who would come out on top. And both walking away with pride, knowing they gave it their all. Alejandro, you and I have had our differences in the past, and right now we might not be on the best terms… But I’m extending my hand in peace here. For one night. Like I’ve been able to do with James. I want to shake your hand, and get in that ring, and wrestle. Not for revenge or blood thirst or the upper hand… but for the love of wrestling. FOR wrestling. For Tim. The two men stare at the camera as the scene slowly fades out…
