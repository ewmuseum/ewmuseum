Settled At Last

Aug 30 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
James Sullivan: Well for the last couple of months, it has been hell for me. I mean, first I signed with Mayhem Wrestling Entertainment, then I wrestle my first match, and on top of all that I moved to a different house...to be honest, I moved into a different lifestyle. The lifestyle of a wrestle who is up and coming and who will win the Mayhem Wrestling Entertainment United States Championship if it is the last thing he does. *James is sporting his normal baggy jean shorts, but today he has a MWE T-Shirt on with a brand new shiny gold watch. He is sitting on a bench in front of his mansion and is looking around, for the first time, at his lawn.* James: Damn, I never realized what a big entrance I had! Stephen: It comes with the job. Oh the joy of benefits and money. James: Yeah, it is nice, but is there really a need for such a long driveway? I mean what is the point. Stephen: I mean yeah I noticed that too. I noticed something else also. James: What else? Stephen: You are getting distracted again. Come on, you are finally settled down, locked and ready to go. Don't get caught up in your own little world of admiration. This is yours and will be yours, you will have other times to look at your lawn. You match is in three days for crying-out-loud. James: Yeah, maybe I should say something. Alright, time to get serious, time to focus. Alright, well...where to start. Oh ok, the tournament. *James takes a breath, and Stephen takes a couple steps back to supervise. James looks at the camera, rocks the bench a little bit, and exhales.* James: So, MWE is currently in the middle of United States Championship tourney, in the second round also the Semi-Finals. I have already beaten Xavier Stone, and Aiden Copane is my next match. How wonderful is Aiden? I mean I would love to be him. He dates drug-attics and owns establishments where people go and waste their money away. I would love to be him! *Sullivan smiles a little bit, giving a devilish grin. He shakes his head, chuckles, and picks his head back up to look at the camera.* James: All joking aside Aiden, you better get your head in the right place, otherwise, I will have no competition. I mean Kanto beat you last week, your girlfriend left you shortly after, and you are still not even concerned about me...meh, what ever works...right? *James looks disgusted talking about Aiden. His face shows complete dislike for Aiden in every shape or form, and Stephen is looking on, approvingly.* James: It sickens me actually. You are so cocky, so arrogant, and so illiterate. Can you tell right from left, apparently not. Seriously, you ignore your fans. You ignore your opponents. And if that wasn't bad enough, you spend your time and money opening up Casino's. Continue with that, and you will be in for a rude awakening. See unlike you, I don't go around ignoring things. I focus on the things that matter. The fans, winning, and learning. I am sure you have done your research on me...right? If not, I sure do hope you get in as much as you can within the next three days, as I pretty know you back to front at this point. *Sullivan is smiling ear to ear now, knowing of the cockiness that Aiden Copane sports. He just shakes his head again.* James: See, my mentor, Stephen Arthur, told me that you will do whatever it takes to win. Whether or not you needed to bend the rules was my problem, but now I see my only problem is trying to find away to not embarrass you...or at least not let you embarrass yourself. See, I am not that bad of guy, I really am not. But I can be. Last week, you lost the match for our team. You tossed me to the side like I was a piece of garbage, and we lost the match. I saw you last week, before the match, talk about trying to get me out of the way, so you could win your title already. Well I have news for you, if anything, this week it will be a cakewalk for me...not you. *The cameras focus on Stephen who is definitely pleased with his pupil. He seems to think that James is confident enough to not be called cocky, and to exploit the right buttons so Aiden will snap.* James: When I signed with MWE, I didn't come in prepared to lose. In fact, I try damn hard to win all of my matches. I try to keep on nice grounds with everybody...but with you, I don't know if that will happen. You seem to believe that the only real wrestler in this company is you. You align yourself with the people in power and stay away from any real challenges. Well this week, I will show you that your cockiness will be a major flaw. You say that you deserve to be main-eventing...well prove it. This week, for both of us, will be our time to shine. And if you can beat me, I will keep my mouth shut, but I am serious man. Dead serious, and I am not scared of you or your ego. You talk about D.C and Brian Michaels. You talk about Kanto and Xavier Stone, but you said my name once. If you honestly think I didn't notice, thing again. Unlike some, I am not an ass, and I look at all my opponents as equal. You seem to think that you aren't anyone until you have won a championship belt. Well, you will just be my stepping stone to my first belt, and then you will notice me, and if you don't then I will just have to make a bigger impact. Making a statement isn't easy, but if need be, I will do just that and make you want to face me. This is me officially trying to open your eyes, because I swear...I swear if you do not bring your A-Game, you will regret it. Enough of you though! *James is getting somewhat hyper and his intensity raised by a lot. He is looking around at some trees and calms down...but only a little bit. He still sees Stephen, who is urging him on.* James: One final statement, Aiden Copane...whether you like to admit or not, this will be just as big of challenge for you as it will be for me. But seriously, enough of that. Statements are made all the time, and I did see what D.C. did to Brian Michaels. A Chemi-Kill will mess you up more than ever before. I see what the so called big leagues are like, and after this tourney, I would love to try you on for size D.C. You will be an even bigger challenge than Aiden, that is if Aiden brings his A-Game. As for you Brian, I might want to avoid the Chemi-Kill...it's not good for your health, and certainly won't you a title shot anytime soon. With MWE back on the map, I don't quite know if your settled in yet. Good luck with D.C...you might need it. *James stops looking straight at the camera, as Stephen comes over and grabs him by the shoulder. The cameras back off, and watch from a far as student and mentor talk.* Stephen: Alright, your done. You can "unwind" now. James: I was just getting started too. Stephen: Don't get ahead of yourself, you don't want to drift to off-topic now do you? That is not your battle, and I know them, you do not want to get involved right now. James: Yeah, for now. But I can feel it, there will be a time where I face D.C. and or Brian Michaels. Stephen: For now though, stick with Aiden Copane. Remember, this is only the semi-finals. You still have one more step of the way if you beat Copane. James: Yeah, but that is for another time and place. Anyway, Aiden is going to be more of a challenge anyway, more pressure is on me, and despite my attributes, I am still the underdog. In any case, Monday Night should be fun. Stephen: Yeah, that's what you think. Just wait, this match will change your idea for fun. Because while you see Aiden's cockiness, he can be very sneaky. Just watch out, that's all. If the ref's back is turned, raise your guard. *James chuckled a little bit, as Stephen grabbed his shoulder as he said that. Stephen then gave a slight chuckle as well.* James: I'll be sure to remember that... *The screen then fades to black...* -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Please, leave all comments possible.
