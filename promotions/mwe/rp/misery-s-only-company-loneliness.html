Misery's only company loneliness

Apr 2 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
*The scene opens up in an Olive Garden and we see the confrontation between Oliver and Peter. We hear the words, "I used to think we were brothers, but now I realize that we were never even friends." over and over again. The words are finally stopped by a scream and we see Peter just shoot up from underneath the covers panting and sweating. Peter has a wife beater on and the covers are covering up the rest of his body. Peter runs his hand through his uncombed hair and he begins to speak as if he is scared, but at the same time frustrated on how his life has turned out and everything that has happened to him in the past. Peter looks unsure if he even wants to wrestle anymore.* Peter: Why is that dream stuck in my head? Why can't I ever get it out? Damn it! Damn it all to hell. Why God must you take everything from me. Why?!? Give me some damn answers! *Peter's rant is cut by the sound of ICW GM Darrel Jones telling Peter that he has a promo he needs to cut in order to become apart of the MWE roster. Peter unwillingly gets up and we see that he is barefoot and wearing blue simpsons boxers with Homer saying, "This everyone's fault, but mine." Peter looks down at his boxers and mutters, "This one is on me." Peter eats a bowl of Captain Crunch and then gets dressed. He puts on a Dwayne Wade jersey and some baggy jeans. Peter is wearing some converse sneakers and walks down the stairs of the hotel where all the superstars are staying and walks towards the limo which is going to escort every ICW Superstar to Norwich, England Carrow road for ICW fan interaction. Peter opens up the limo door and he immediately sees his tag team partner for this monday's event extending his hand to shake Peter's. Peter simply slams the door shut in Nic Kraven's face and elects to sit up front with the driver. Both begin to talk about life and where men stand in this world. The limo driver's name is John and seems to have an optimistic view on man's relationship with God, while Peter holds a very cynical view on God. John is an old man with gray hair and is wearing a very nice suit.* Peter: Lets go. John: Why are you sitting up here, son? Peter: A little less questions and a little more driving. Okay pal. John: Something troubling you, son? Peter: Yeah, you keep trying to talk to me. John: Well it seems like you need to talk to somebody, maybe you should try God. Peter: So I can talk to myself? No thanks. John: No so you can find understand and maybe help you get over your problems. Peter: I used to believe in God, but then I realized that just like fairytales and cartoons. God is fake. John: Now why would you say that? Peter: If you've been down the road that I've been on then you would know why. *John simply turns his head and continues on driving. Peter looks out his window and can't help, but remember his old life before Oliver with Rachel. He sees when they first met, when they first kissed, and when they broke up. He sees the heart break on her face and the cold, unemotional stare in his. Peter mutters, "You heartless bastard. You finally got what you wanted."The limo stops and lets all the ICW Superstars out and Peter heads straight to the ring since he is first one on to cut a promo on his first match in MWE. Peter looks uncaring and walks out with no music and no anything. Peter just grabs a mic and instead of doing his usual show boating he just speaks straight from the heart and each word is filled with more pain then anyone in the arena can possibly imagine.* Peter: This week is considered the biggest week for everyone who has a career in ICW, anyone who wants to roll with the big dogs, but it isn't for me. This week has been one of the most painful weeks in my entire life and I plain on taking out this pain on both Nic Kraven and Blood Fuze. This week I plan on sending a message to anyone who thinks that they know what pain is that they have no clue what pain really is. Blood Fuze when you step inside that ring with me you won't be facing a man, you won't be facing a caged animal, and you won't be face a demon or a hell spawn. You'll be facing Peter Kaymakcian a man with nothing left to live for, but a whole lot left to prove. My question Blood Fuze is how do you plan on beating a man who is willing and ready to do anything and everything to pull out a win? Blood Fuze how to you plan to inflict pain on somebody who is already in so much pain that they actually enjoy it? Blood Fuze this is the week your career in MWE ends. *Peter stops himself because he get choked up on the pain of everything that has happened to him and all of his past sins coming back to hit him. The fans in England try to cheer Peter on to continue and he does so with the same passion and pain that filled his other speech.* Peter: I've been down and out before, but never like this. I've been beaten, bloodied, and broken, but none have felt worse then the pain I am feeling right now. Nic I am warning you for your own good, stay the hell out of my way. Nic I am ready to take out everyone and anyone in that ring and if you want that to be you, then so be it. I have no problem with ending your career, before it even begins. I'll destroy anyone who gets in my way, even the referee himself. I don't care if I get yelled at, suspended, or even FIRED!!! I don't care one single bit. All I care about is inflicting pain on my opponent. All I care about is being able to leave the ring and sit in a bar getting drunk by myself. In fact screw this I am out of here. *With that Peter rolls out of the ring and walks up the ramp. When he exits the stage and enters the backstage area he is met by Nic Kraven who has a look of wonder on his face on why Peter is threatening his own partner. Peter just ignores him and walks up to John and demands that he take him to a bar, but John simply refuses. Peter then walks towards the street and calls for a cab. When Peter gets in the cab he tells him to take him to the nearest pub and the cab driver just nods his head. The cab driver's name tag says Gabriel and he is caucasian and blonde. Gabriel passes by a the first pub and Peter speaks out in anger.* Peter: Hey idiot you just passed the pub over there! *Gabriel simply ignores Peter and continues driving and the area he drives into just keeps getting foggier and foggier. Peter just sits back and enjoys the ride. When the cab stops Peter speaks out in a sick and twisted tone.* Peter: So if you want my money, you better put a bullet in my head. *Gabriel again just ignores Peter and motions for him to follow him. Peter does so and they end up in front of a rusted gate. Peter again speaks out in defiance and a sarcastic tone.* Peter: So you drive me here to see a rusted fence? Well la-di-da. What's next a dried up river. *Out of nowhere a powerful tone can be heard blasting over Peter's voice. Not one to be quiet Peter speaks out with anger and hatred.* God: My son it is time for you to be quiet. Peter: Who the hell are you? God: I am your father. Peter: Sorry didn't realize we were at a star wars convention and here I am without my light saber. God: It is time for you to listen. Peter: No its time I got some answers, from you. God: Why do you hold onto hatred and anger? Peter: What else do I have to hold onto? God: Love, kindness, and friends and family. Peter: You took all of that away from me. You took away my family, you took away Rachel, and you took away Oliver. God: You separated yourself from them and I just finished it off. You where bad and you were punished. Peter: Makes perfect sense I screw up and you punish them. You are the biggest hypocrite you know that? You claim peace and love, but when someone doesn't do what you say, you do something drastic and cross the line. God: What have you done to keep Oliver and Rachel? Nothing absolutely nothing. You fill your heart with anger to keep our love and keep out your friends. Peter: Well maybe my family dying had something to do with that? God: I don't give anyone anything they can't handle. Peter: Is that your disclaimer? Is that what makes it easy for you to sleep at night? You judge me, but as far as I can see you are just as bad as me. You brought AIDS here. You let people die in the streets and you let skinny people starve and give fat people food. You'd let the world burn or flood just to get your point across. As far as I am concerned you can't be molded in man's image because at least men have a set and admit what they've done. God: And you whose fault is it that they get AIDS? Why do these people choose to fill there veins with drugs instead of going providing shelter to there family and food? Why should my voice have to be silenced by men who would rather see the world burn for a corrupt a reason? I've seen your life and seen all that you've done. You want to change, but you choose to live in the past. This is not your final hour, but a brief stop. *Just then a blinding white light fills the scene and then it fads to black with what happened to Peter to be left unknown.*
