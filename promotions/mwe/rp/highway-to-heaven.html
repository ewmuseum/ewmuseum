Highway to Heaven; Alejandro Rodriguez

Jan 25 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Nashua, New Hampshire, Wednesday, 21st January, 1:54 pm, Route 495 Highway. The scene opens on the Route 495 highway heading toward Massachusetts. The sun is shining bright and there is little traffic as road-users drive past the camera in their cars, one car in particular going faster than the rest, faster than should be aloud. The camera focuses on this particular car, an orange and black Hummer H3, with a custom registration plate, “L T F1Y”. The view changes to a different camera, fitted into the back of the H3, panning around to show it’s interior. The car is hugely spacious as the back-seats are pushed back to where the trunk would be, the leather seats are of a cream color clashing nicely with the darker color of brown on the dashboard, at the back of the front seats are televisions for the purpose of back-seated passengers, top-of-the range Satellite Navigation and media player systems are situated at the front of the car, and finally, the camera pans to the left to see a hand creeping down the left of his chair, opening the fridge below his armrest with a click and taking out a can of Pepsi. Darrel Jones of course, extracting a cup-holder with a push of a button, he lays his drink aside and returns his attention back to his laptop, plugged in through the cigarette lighter, he is wearing a black button-up shirt and jeans. He looks slightly agitated, he looks through the window for a moment and then leans forward to talk to the driver. Darrel: How long is this drive going to take? Someone turns around from the driver’s seat for a second from the drivers seat, beaming, the man who almost won the Undisputed Championship on last week‘s Monday Night Mayhem. He is wearing a white “A-Rod” t-shirt, a black mask and jeans. His face looks like he’s enjoying the experience more than Darrel. Alejandro: Who cares, this is great! A snort of laughter comes from another person in the back seat, Billy Joe Barker, he is wearing his favourite horrible brown cowboy boots, a black Tex-Mex shirt, denim jeans and matching jacked and his cowboy hat. He is watching TV on the screen on the back of Alejandro’s chair, he looks round his chair and scowls with disagreement. Billy Joe: But why couldn’ we fly to Masterchoosis? Alejandro swerves to avoid a car cutting in front of him and he swears under his breath in Spanish. Alejandro: Because Billy boy, I’m not always going to be here to buy you plane tickets and keep you in five-star hotels every week, you’ve got to learn the hard way, like me and Darrel did. Darrel laughs for a moment and closes down his laptop, he looks around the Hummer. Darrel: Driving him around for an hour and a half in a 50,000 dollar customized Hummer with Playstation 3, drinks and television systems is hardly teaching him to learn the hard way. Long pause… Alejandro: Shut up. Billy: An’ besides Allafantrono, I can pay for me own plane tickets. Alejandro: Only when you stop buying all that Chuck Norris memorabilia. Darrel takes a sip of his coke and there is a moment where Al just drives, Billy watches TV and Darrel closes his eyes, before snapping back awake again. Darrel: Don’t you want to talk about you match? Alejandro: Not really, but I’m sure you’re going to make me. Darrel: It’s healthy to talk about your opponents… Alejandro: Don’t be gay… Darrel: So what do you think of DeMarco? Alejandro: He’s cool. Darrel: Alejandro… please. Alejandro: What? I respect the guy, I used to watch WcE all the time. Sure I enjoyed guys like James Whitman and his tools. I used to like watching the King of Xtreme Mark Marvin, I liked watching the insanity of Eddie Freeze and Dan Stown, but DeMarco, like Samson, like Rocky Stevens... They were true wrestlers, they made the crowd encourage them instead of laughing at them like the JESTers, they didn’t use weapons to win titles like Marvin, they weren’t crazy like Freeze or Stown, they were just brilliant. I have a great deal of respect for WcE, why? Because they were all taught by the great Tim Fitzgerald, Darrel, did you know that? Darrel: I, uh, no… Alejandro nods and continues to speak as he changes lanes. Alejandro: That’s where I first met Tim, I attended a show once with my trainer, he introduced me to Tim. From first glance I knew the guy was awesome. He taught me so much, but I wasn’t ready yet to go in the ring, otherwise I would have been psyched to join WcE, it was my first true love. I watched it every week with my trainer, watching guys like DeMarco taught me a lot, so I guess I owe him for that. Darrel looks deep in thought as Alejandro takes a moment to reflect on the conversation. Alejandro: Like most federations, like MWE infact. WcE suffered a sticky period after Tim’s death, unfortunately, WcE never survived but MWE did. After WcE’s death, I tried to salvage some superstars to MWE… Guys like Freeze and Stown didn’t want to know… Marvin went back to his hometown of Baltimore to revamp his music career and try and find the murderer of his children. DeMarco and the JEST’ers came, the JESTers preferred to create their TV Show.. But DeMarco, is the only true transition from WcE. Darrel: Do you think you’ll struggle against DeMarco? Alejandro: He has the experience in the ring, but I’m not too short of that myself, let’s face it. He’s stronger than me, and some would say a little more intelligent. But I am faster and able to adjust situations in the ring like not even James Whitman can achieve. DeMarco is a hall-of-famer, and rightly so, and it is an honour to step in the ring with him, but it would be a greater honour to beat him in the ring. Darrel seems satisfied enough with Alejandro’s reasoning and he ceases with the questions. Some signs approach the car and Alejandro begins to slow down. Alejandro: Now Darrel, we can switch around at these services, I need some quality time with my and my PS3. Darrel: Whatever. Darrel rolls his eyes and places his laptop to one side. He leans back and takes a drink, Billy is watching the television in the back while drinking a beer as Alejandro pulls into a service station and parks the car in a small car park outside the garage. Alejandro and Darrel both exit the car and swap seats, Alejandro sitting in the back with Billy and Darrel now in the drivers seat. Alejandro turns on the PS3 and cracks open a can of coke from the fridge below his armrest as Darrel turns on the ignition. Darrel: We need gas. Darrel drives into the gas station and gets out as Alejandro’s game begins to load, Call of Duty 5. Alejandro: Thank god for in-car wi-fi. Alejandro starts an online game, Alejandro plugs in a USB headset, to talk online as Darrel fills up the Hummer with diesel, Darrel shouts through the window. Darrel: This price is ridiculous! Alejandro: It’s a hummer Darrel, go figure. We have the money. Now be quiet, I’m owning some noobs, as you Americans might say. Billy Joe watches Alejandro play his game, Alejandro gets angry at all the players who kill him. Alejandro: What the hell?!? Martyrdom? Freakin’ noob! Alejandro stops talking and his opponent talks back. Alejandro: Low-life? I’m sitting in my 50 grand Hummer as we speak! I’m Alejandro Rodriguez you dumb-ass American, I kick punk‘s asses like you every day! Alejandro’s face intensifies as he continues the game, then he almost jumps up with excitement. Alejandro: Hey crumpet! I just sniped your freakin’ head off! Alejandro glances at Billy Joe, who has a look of incredulity on his face. Alejandro: What? He was British. He turns back toward the screen as Darrel gets back in the car and starts driving. Alejandro continues his game. Alejandro: One more kill… one more kill… YES! DOGS! WOOF, WOOF! Eat this jerk-offs! Darrel looks round from the front seat. Darrel: You need therapy. Wait… hold on. Alejandro’s cell-phone goes off, he passes it to Billy Joe, as he is too immersed in the game. Billy Joe answers it. Billy: Haaaylo… mhmmm dats me… why, wha’s hapun’d…wha’, is he ok… Alejandro hears the tone of Billy’s voice, he removes his headset and listens in to the conversation. Billy: …we’ll bee dere. Billy closes the phone with a look of shock on his face. Alejandro looks at him with a slight frown. Alejandro: What’s wrong? Billy: It’s Bob… Darrel: Bob who? Billy: …Benson. Darrel: Who? Alejandro: Billy’s friend from last week. Darrel: What’s wrong with him? Billy: ‘e’s been taken into intensive caaair. ‘e’s been ran over. Alejandro’s mouth forms into an “O” and Darrel pulls the car to a halt at the exit of the garage. Darrel begins to turn the car around. Alejandro: Wait… what are you doing? Darrel: We’re going back to Manchester, Bob needs us. Alejandro: But we’ll be late… Darrel: We can get a flight, but right now, Billy needs to know if Bob’s ok. Alejandro huffs and puts his headset back on. Alejandro: Fine, turn us around. The scene fades out as Alejandro plays on his PS3, Darrel turns the car around and Billy Joe sits in the corner, looking very upset. Manchester, New Hampshire, Wednesday 21st January, 3:05pm, Manchester General Hospital. The scene opens in a Manchester General Hospital waiting room. The camera pans around, showing rows of patients and friends and families of those under the care of the hospital. Alejandro, Darrel and Billy are sat on one row. Alejandro is looking at ease, with a coke in his hand, he flinches when an ill person passes him, scared of catching what he might have, to his left, in the middle Darrel is looking quiet and solemnly, looking opposite himself all the time they are waiting, Billy is as white as a sheet, and still very upset. An ill young boy walks up to them, accompanied by his mother. Boy: Mom! *cough* It’s Tex-Mex! Billy Joe Barker and Alejandro Rodriguez, and look Darrel Jones! The mother hushes her son up quietly and quickly. Mother: Hush now, Tommy, we’re in a hospital, these gentlemen are probably here to see a sick friend… But the boy has already rushed forward with a pen and paper, Alejandro takes the pen, he reaches out for the paper, the boy gives it to him but not before coughing all over it. Alejandro looks like he’s holding a dead rat, and he signs it as fast as possible and hands it back. Tommy: Thanks… The boy runs off with his Mother behind him, Alejandro stands up. Darrel: Where are you going? Alejandro: To wash my hands. Darrel: Wasn’t it enough that you insisted we all wipe our seats down three times before sitting down? Alejandro hisses back at Darrel. Alejandro: That boy could have coughed friggin’ Malaria all over me! At that moment, a nurse walks into the room, Darrel, Alejandro and Billy Joe all look at her, she looks a little startled at Alejandro wearing a mask. Nurse: Are you here to see Mr. Benson? They all nod and follow her through a couple of corridors, Alejandro looks scared to touch anything or anyone in the hospital and he jumps out of the way of a few ill people as they walk toward Benson’s ward. They finally reach his room in intensive care and the nurse enters first with them following behind. The nurse stands aside so they can view Benson. Darrel: Damn… Damn is right. Bob is unconscious, his body is covering with bruises and cuts, his head is bandaged up tightly, he has two black eyes and deep cuts in the shoulder and guyt area, which are too bandaged up. Billy Joe has his head in his hands and Darrel and Alejandro too go white in the face. Nurse: As you can see he’s in pretty bad shape… The cuts and bruises will heal, but… Mr. Benson only has one kidney, he was only born with one, it’s not uncommon… This means that one kidney has grown a lot more than what it would with a normal body… When Mr. Benson smashed through the windscreen, a piece of glass penetrated the kidney, he has to have it removed. Billy, Darrel and Alejandro look at the nurse concerned Alejandro: It can be done, right? Nurse: Yes… but, Mr. Benson has no health insurance, and as far as we know, no family and the surgery costs around 50 thousand dollars. I know Mr. Barker might not be able to pay the fee… but… we thought, maybe you could recuperate the costs, Mr. Rodriguez. Alejandro: I… uh. Alejandro looks to his right, seeing Darrel looking at him very impassive, and Billy looking at him, with tears coming down his eyes. Alejandro: I don- Darrel: -Billy saved your life Alejandro… Return the favour. Alejandro leans in close to whisper to Darrel. Alejandro: My life didn’t cost him 50 grand. Darrel: He had to replace his car… Alejandro: I’m not annoying as fuck. Darrel: Do it, Al… Please. Alejandro pulls his head away from Darrel’s, and he returns his stare to the nurse, without looking at Bob or Billy. Alejandro: Fine… I’ll pay it.
