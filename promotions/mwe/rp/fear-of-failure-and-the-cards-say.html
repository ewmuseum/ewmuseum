Fear of failure...and the cards say...; The Morbid Angel

Jul 2 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Blight FreakShow Martyr Jezibel Just tell me when it starts to hurt ok? As soon as you feel you can't handle it anymore let me know ok Jezibel? The camera pans over and we are inside The Sanctuary. Blight and Jezibel are streching inside the mock ring preparing for Dark Karnival's intergender tag match. Jezibel who normally doesn't wrestle is getting ready to show that she can hang with the big boys...literally. It's ok Blight I can handle it. Its not the training and streching I'm worried about. Remember it has been forever since I've trained with you and the guys for a match. I really need to thank you though. All week you have helped me prepare, FreakShow has helped me alot, Martyr has helped me mentally prepare, its all just a bit overwhelming but in a good way. I haved learned alot and remembered everything I could have forgotten. Blight stands up from his stretch and leans on the ropes looking at Jezibel who is still on the mat streching. He gives her a bit of a smirk and shakes his head. She shoots him a look like " what are you laughing at?" and Blight starts to laugh. I'm sorry Jez I don't know what came over me. Maybe its the fact that you seem all girly right now which for your personallity is pretty weird. Jezibel gets up from her stretch and socks Blight right in the arm. He winces in pain and starts to laugh again only to be on the recieving end from a wicked spinning kick to the head that not only shocks him but floors him as well. She walks over and stands over Blight looking down at him. I told you I've been training...now is it a good idea to make dumb remarks like that? I'm more prepared for this match than anything. I just wish I could say the same about FreakShow. He was kind of off since Mayhem, I think that loss to Jekyl and McKnight might have done something to him. Blight getting up looks at her and shrugs. He reaches out of the ring to the floor and grabs two bottles of water handing one to Jezibel. He takes a swig and brushes his hair back out of his face. I thought so at first but I don't know. I mean its not like him to get bummed out after a loss. That's just not his style, he won't beat himself up over stuff like that. Maybe not being at the Carnival is making him homesick? I'm gonna get that dummy we have been using all week to work some moves. We won't go hard today just a bit of light work I don't want you to be too tired for the match. Blight jumps out of the ring and goes over to the weight machines where there is a dummy that is about five foot 6 in height and weighs about 115 pounds. He easilly throws it into the ring and rolls in afterwards. Yeah your right but still he just wasn't his fun self. He was kind of distant and just out there. Oh remember that move I told you I was working on well I call it The Lobodomy. Here lemme show you. She grabs the dummy and sets up like a fisherman suplex she spins in the direction of the hooked leg spinning and driving the dummy down to the mat hard. She gets up smiles and jumps up and down in joy. Blight smiles and claps his hands in impression. Jezibel dances around the ring for a second laughing as they hear the door to The Sanctuary open and Martyr and FreakShow yelling at each other. They really can't hear what they are saying but it looks like Martyr is trying to defend himself from FreakShow's verbal attack. FreakShow quickly goes to his room not even paying attention to Blight and Jezibel and leaves Martyr just standing in the doorway. Martyr walks over the ring as Blight and Jezibel climb out to meet him. Dude what was that about? What happened? I really don't know man, he picked me up and never said a word until we got here. Basically all I said to him was yo man you ok, and he completely flipped. He really hasn't been right the past few days....so how was training? It went well, I got to show Blight The Lobodomy...Someone really should talk to him, but I'm gonna hit the shower so I'll leave that to you Blight. Thanks for the training all week guys but its girl time now later! Jezibel gives Blight a quick hug and runs off before he could say anything. Blight looks at Martyr in confusion. Why do I have to talk to him? Not that I mind don't get me wrong, what the hell just happened?....What about you Martyr you ok? Do you think the loss had anything do do with this? I'm ok man...Yeah the loss was pretty shitty for us but I know how it goes man Famous and Infamous were no joke like you had said. Hey thats the past so to me its time for what is next and that is this intergender tag match. I'm worried man...we got Axel, Diamond and Eve, which leaves Ozzy to be floating around somewhere. If I'm tied up with one of the other two and FreakShow's head isn't in the game that leaves a huge opening for something to happen to Jezibel. The two of them look at each other for a moment as FreakShow's room starts blaring with music. Well dude I'll still be there so I can have you covered thats no problem...but what if his head really isn't there that could end badly...Damnit man...alright give me a few minutes lemme go talk to him. Blight walks over to FreakShow's room where we can hear him skimming through various Children of Bodom songs. Blight stands outside thinking of what he is going to say. Before he could knock FreakShow opens the door and just looks him in the face and steps aside allowing Blight to come in. He slams the door behind the two of them as Blight walks over and lowers the stereo so they could talk. FreakShow lays on his bed just looking up at the ceiling. Dude what's going on man? Both Jez and Martyr told me that you haven't been yourself the last few days. Is your head ok? What is floating around up there bro? I'm fine...its just...just...fuck it dude we let you down we screwed up. You gave us this chance and we fucked it up our first night out there and now its only a matter of time before we end up back at fat ass Slappy Bob's again. Dude I'm sorry. Blight walks over grabs FreakShow by his shirt and stands him up. FreakShow looking very pissed off at this stands up and gets in Blights face. Shut the fuck up! You did do what you said you would do and that was make an impact. You and Martyr took Jekyl and McKnight to the damn limit and now your sitting here crying about it because you lost like a damn litle girl. You didn't let anybody down but instead you took one of the best tag teams in MWE to the max. Dude get your head straight cause the three you got coming up at Independence day are no joke and will end you if your not careful. FreakShow looking in Blight's eyes realizing that he isn't just blowing smoke. He starts to calm down a bit and we can see his arrogance climbing again. He gives Blight an appologettic smile before jumping back onto his bed. Dude your right we did kick their asses huh? Haha. And now we got some preteen crap-wads. I'll be at the bar before drunk as hell before the High Road match is done. And dude I know they are no joke I mean come on look at them we got the mental midget the Patron Saint of staying up past his bed time Axel Christ...The Patron Saint of Jello Diamond Moses. I'm not sure which is fatter Diamond's head, Axel's mom or Eve's porky ass! I really shouldn't forget the Patron Saint of Riddelin, he'll be running around here trying to find out who ate his Lucky Charms. Blight looks at FreakShow and goes to speak again but realizing it would probably be pointless hits the play button on the stereo so it plays loudly and walks out of the door. Jezibel is standing outside of the door waiting as we see Martyr working out a few feet away. She motions towards her room and Blight follows. So I got through to him...it didn't take much I just yelled at him for a second and he snapped back to normal. He definately is worried about this match. Just a hunch I got. I figured that. Hey I set up my card reading table come sit I'll do a reading for you. Jezibel starts to light some candles and some insense before shuffling her cards. Blight sits in silence as Jezibel starts to trance out before dealing out three cards face down. She flips the first one over. The card of The Four Horsemen...this must be Saints Of Los Angeles. It seems like they will be a bigger thorn in our sides than we originally thought. If all of us aren't careful... She flips the second one. The card of The Jester...someone isn't taking you seriously. Maybe you will have to go to extreme measures to fix this problem... She flips the third. The card of The Angel. The saviour of the damned. As she says that we can hear Matyr yell for Blight and FreakShow to come. Both bedroom doors bust open and they run out. They both stop short as a man standing about 5 foot 9 wearing white pants a black t-shirt and a white leather vest is standing in the middle of the mock ring. His long black hair slicked back into a ponytail shines in the lights above. FreakShow and Martyr stand outside of the ring as Blight yells. What the hell? Where did you come from? Why are you back? I thought they commited you! Martyr, Jezibel and FreakShow quickly turn and look at Blight in confusion. SCENE ENDS.
