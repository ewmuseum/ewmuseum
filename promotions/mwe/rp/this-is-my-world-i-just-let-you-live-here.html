The Grand Mystique

*A lot of this RP was planned before I knew about Tex leaving so any in-character references are based around him being a significant part of the match, even if he won't in any way.* This RP is dedicated to: Mr Money in the Bank. Congratulations. Try it bitch, I dare you. * “No! Up a bit!” An angry voice. A voice insistent on absolute perfection. We begin in the bustling corridor of the MWE Headquarters in Chicago. The executive floor is as you would expect and as we have seen before. The artery –like corridors are bustling with middle management negotiating their way from one room to another. At the end of the corridor, are the pictures of past MWE stars. These pictures have one thing in common. They all have three features in common. Wide, exhausted smiles, sell out crowds and a bright piece of gold being raised high above their heads. Outfits and hairstyles may change, the faces all vary, even the gold changes a little by design. Yet the symbolism is clear. Put in the industrious effort, endure the blood, sweat and tears and accept the massive risk of the business and the rewards are clear and plentiful. And whether it is the absolute failure that was Pala or Aiden Copane or the overwhelming success stories of Whitman, Williams or Rated X, the reward was worth it for their place in the sun. On a grand stage where you are recognised as the elite in the company; they would all say it was worth it. If they said it was not then they were lying. These images absolutely show that happiness. A large man is trying to precisely position a new, gold-rimmed frame on the wall with another new face beaming down to him. As he permanently fixes it in place, the camera swings around. Behind him is the face in the picture. He has a very plain white t-shirt and jeans outfit on but he wears the MWE World Championship belt proudly over his shoulder “That’s better. Why couldn’t you do that first time?” The man gets his equipment off the floor and shuffles off, mumbling obscenities under his breath. The man he leaves behind is casting a smug look on the new picture. “Mr Silver will see you now, Mr Mystique.” A pretty brunette receptionist emerges from the door behind him. While still admiring his picture, he tips his head slightly in her direction. “Thank you Hayley. Say, what do you think of this picture?” “It is very good; I take it the weekend was well worth the wait?” “Oh yes, very much so.” He proudly taps the faceplate of the belt. He gives a wide smile to the cute girl but doesn’t hesitate to walk past into the room behind her. GM walks straight through into Eli’s office. The room was generally more organised and welcoming since Eli had sorted it out after his first day. He has decorated it in his own inimitable stylings to make it totally unique to him. Gaudy posters hang from the walls and expensive trinkets line the shelves. It’s both a sign of perverse male dominance and aggressive display of wealth. “How’s it going Mr Champ?” In his typically enthusiastic voice, Eli rises from his desk, straightens down his slick Armani suit and goes to shake GM’s hand. “I’m on top of the world. Nothing says you are number one like twelve pounds of leather and gold on your arm.” “Exactly and we are not sure of challengers either. They are all coming out of the woodwork and I’ve got the paperwork to prove it.” “Yeah they smell blood. Pity I’m not bleeding.” The gritty determination in his voice is palpable. He sits down in a comfortable leather chair. “That’s the right attitude to have.” “My attitude isn’t what I have come about Eli.” Eli buzzes around to his desk, stung by GM’s tone. “Errr, yeah....I got your message.” “And I bet you thought I was a greedy cocksucker?” Did GM really say cock sucker? “No, no. Of course not. I actually think this may be more deserving than most.” “Good. I think, given what I have done for the company my request is only fair.” “I can certainly talk to the board and see what terms they agree to.” “That’s not good enough Eli.” Any cordial atmosphere has gone out of the window. “What?!” “If DC and Lindsay can walk out of this building with a seven point two five million dollar deal, then don’t you think I deserve some sort of parity?” “Of course but these things take time.” “No they don’t. It’s all very simple. Unlike DC, I don’t want to leave the company where I have achieved so much.” GM is calm yet his tone is direct and to the point. “OK so what are these terms? Two million, three million?” “Five downside guaranteed.” “What the FUCK!!” Eli’s reaction is so random that his receptionist pops her head through the door. Used to outbursts like these, she leaves quiet as a mouse. GM carries on like it never happened. “Plus no more medical or legal bills.” “Oh my god...” “And twenty percent merch royalties.” “You greedy shi-!” “And ten percent annual loyalty bonus.” “You done? You want a cock and balls sandwich with that?” GM looks at him with a confident shrug of his shoulders. Eli takes a deep breath and gets up from his desk. “I like you GM. I really dig ya. And you deserve a rise, we both know it. But I can go so far on my own with this.” He pours himself both a glass of scotch from a crystal decanter. “I want to walk out of the building with this sorted Eli. Get the board down here.” “Not happening champ.” “Then let’s go.” “Huh? No, wait.” GM has already got up from his seat. “Give me until Mayhem to sort it out. Do this for me and I will make sure that by the time you walk out for your match, the paperwork will be signed.” The logic appeals to GM, who is clearly considering it. “OK, I’ll agree to that. I’ve got nothing to gain.” “Consider it done.” He gulps down his drink and slams the glass down with a degree of victorious satisfaction. “You want the Lindsay bonus?” “Nah. Chloe isn’t in my future.” There is no bitterness in his voice. In fact he seems totally content with it. “This isn’t all about cash Eli.” He says it with total sincerity. “Like fuck it isn’t.” Eli’s become a lot braver than last time he met GM. Must be a perk of the job. “It’s not that you don’t deserve it but I could have done with some notice you know. This would have been a lot easier if we could have worked it out over a few weeks.” “I thought you could have handled it Eli. Besides, I wanted this done and out the way by Mayhem.” “Yeah, yeah. Big match for ya bud.” Little patronising shit “I don’t remember the last time that I had a match that wasn’t significant.” “This may top the lot, even after Crowning Glory.” “No match is going to be bigger than that. Not until the main event of next year’s show.” Eli nods his head, displaying general agreement. It may be an arrogant statement but given the unqualified success of the event, he may be right. “You don’t think that I know this may be the most stacked six man match in MWE history? That I have to face two people who have won opportunities at this title? That I have to face two of the brightest prospects in the company on the same team as a legend?” “I think the teams are more than even than you make out, my main man.” Eli tries to play nice. But GM continues his sarcastic tone. “I know how good Hardaway and Sharpman are. But I also know that this belt is the biggest bulls-eye that I could have and they are both hungry enough to become opponents in the future. The fact is that their talent and their histories make this match more than interesting.” “And I reckon that you’re dying to get the strap off me to put it on one of your puppet bitches. And with Sinc out the picture isn’t it convenient that DC gets a number one contenders match?” Eli is visibly aggravated at this comment. His eyes widen and he seems to “hulk up” and grow in stature. “You fuck! You arrogant little prick. Do you forget that I was the one who gave you that main event spot! The same person got the main event extended because you couldn’t get the job done! And you’re lucky that I am even considering taking your contract extension to the board after what happened to Sinc!” GM silently acknowledges that Eli may have a point. “Truth is I could have made this match much more interesting. I could have made you defend it against five others. A click of my fingers and I guarantee I could make a match that would guaran-damn-tee you would lose your belt and be remembered as the worst champion since Aiden pencil dick Copane!” This is a glimpse of the true Eli Silver. The one that curses for a habit. The man at the helm of a revolution in MWE. GM gets up. He walks up to Eli’s side of the desk and looks him dead in the eyes. “Oh Eli, you just don’t understand. Crowning Glory was the beginning of a new era. You spreading your influence on the company. I became a champion and took out one of the biggest names in the business at the same time.” “It was the perfect time. The peak of a rise to absolute domination. There was no stopping this. You can kid yourself all you want. It was simple destiny that decided it was my time.” GM's purposeful stare meets Eli’s wide eyes. “So you know what Eli, do your worst. Try and take this belt off me. Make my life a nightmare. You will never make me hate being World Champion and you will not succeed.” “I worked so hard and for so long to get this feeling and it’s proven to be better than I ever thought. You could give me a piss test and it couldn’t be any cleaner. This is a natural high, the benefit of hard work and I couldn’t feel any happier about having to deal with all the shit that you can throw at me!” With that, GM leaves, confidently striding out of the office. Eli smirks slightly at the positive, defiant reaction from his World champion. As he hears the door slam, he considers how talented GM really is, how long he can remain on top as champion and prays that his reign will be good for business. GM leaves the office and as he does, he takes the frame off the wall. He looks at it pensively before tucking it under his right arm, with the belt held in his left hand. He then walks to the end of the corridor and gets into the elevator. He stands there with his arms wide open, welcoming. “So many things to discuss....oh where to begin?!” The doors grind slowly shut, hiding a smiling GM from the camera. The scene fades back in to show the giant illuminated MWE logo that is above the HQ building in Chicago. Its’ bright fluorescent tubing lights up the dark night sky. In front of stands the Grand Mystique; the new World Champion is sitting down on the brick base of the lighting structure. ++++OOC note, I tried to get the MWE logo here but I couldn't get it to work. So, sorry, imaginations required here++++ “This Monday sees six of the best in MWE face off in one ring. Our esteemed General Manager wants to display the talents of his roster for the world to see. For this is the finest wrestling company going today.” From his shoulder, the glint of the golden faceplate catches the light and glistens sharply. “And I’m the number one guy, because this says I am!” He points to the small nameplate. “I am the single most vicious and driven competitor in the company because everyone has seen what I would do to get things done......This here is my reward and for so long it was what I aspired to hold.” “But in the here and now, as I hold it as a symbol of my supreme dominance, I need to hold it longer. I need to be a better champion than Mercier and DC in MWE, than Virus and Maxx in EW and than anyone in WZCW. I need to set the standard and raise the bar, the prestige.” GM removes the belt from his shoulder and rests it across his lap. “I am sick of the doubts. Sinc was allowed to run his mouth without restraint or control. And that is why I made his Crowning Glory a night to remember. His suffering, just like that of Maxx and Windy can all be traced back to my hand.” “He had to pay for his sins and at the most crucial time; I did the world a favour and shut him up permanently! My title reign will be long and successful because I need to purge the reign of Sinc Mercier. His words and his actions have tainted this belt; it leaves a bad taste in my mouth.” He rubs his hand across his chin which has a pretty minimal amount of stubble across it. “I’m on top and it makes everyone mad because no one believes I am ready. It makes me sick.” He gets up, steps sideways and looks at the bright red sign that stands above him. The sign dominates the Chicago night-skyline. “I should be satisfied with my title win, I really should. I took it to the biggest star in the company, on the biggest night of the year and did the business; exactly what I said I would do. I promised and delivered on it. I got my hand raised, I have my title on my shoulder and I got my payday, which adds up to a great nights work, no matter what your name is.” GM speaks with total sarcasm, in an “I can’t do anything right” attitude. He turns away and looks over the edge of the building at the lights that surround the MWE building. “But no, no, no......it’s not enough. I give everything I have. And now I want more. I want more title defences, I want more fights and I want more people trying to claw the title away from me because I will thrive on the feeling that I have what everyone craves, maybe Cruz or Warner next week, DC next month.” “See Sinc had stained my reign before it even began and that is why I decided to end CG the way I did. CG for Sinc will be stained with the cause and effect of his words.” He points off into the far distance. “It is with these words that I will lay myself to anyone who is put before me. I will set my path through them. It will be stained with their blood as well as Sinc’s.” “All these jealous eyes in my back are now what drive me. My desire for this belt brought me to the top of the mountain, I now need to use the selfish desire I have to become a great champion to survive and stay on top. There is no doubt in my mind that I will face tougher challenges than Sinc or Whitman ever faced in their reigns as champ; can you spell screw job?” The defiant tone is mirrored by his actions, which are defensive and blatant. He turns to face the camera with a clenched fist. “I lived off the feeling of craving Sinc’s title for so long and now having that feeling targeted on me is what will drive me on.” He points to his temple with his index and middle finger. “It’s an addictive feeling and one I hope Kevin Hardaway can forgive or at least understand me for; to have those burning eyes in your back. And as until Sunday, I was the perennial chaser. My eyes were always on this belt and now I have it, I have no intention of letting it go.” “But I guess this feeling is going anywhere anytime soon. I expect to hear every week how rubbish I am. And maybe as I have to face three of the least discreet imbeciles on the roster. They really have no idea. I think I may have the best side of this deal.” He points at the middle of the camera and with his free arm, readjusts the title across his shoulder. “So, to all my challengers and doubters. I don’t care who you are; legends or rookies. Your pasts mean nothing, only my future is certain and assured of success it is.” “In Hardaway and Sharpman, I have two like-minded men. They are ambitious in mentality and sadistic when the occasion calls for it. I think finally I can work with these two, so long as we all know where we stand.” “While I am not even sure our opponents are worth the wasted breath, my partners have credibility and intelligence to back me up and help demolish the little wasters.” He shakes his head, clearly a little disenchanted with the choice of opponents. “It’s some kind of crazy when the XTV champion is allowed to headline any sort of broadcast with the World Heavyweight champion. Crash better not get any ideas above his station, but it certainly will get his blood pumping.” He steps to the side of the building again. This time, he steps over the ledge and looks over the side of the building, which is dizzyingly high up. “I am all for enjoying life and I appreciate the thrill of living on the edge. The buzz of being a champion is unparalleled but it won’t always be enough. Whether he wants to or not, Zack is going to go higher and faster to his next rush yet it will only ever send him a gutter" He walks along the ledge, dramatically waving his arms about. ”What Zack Crash will never realise is that somewhere along the line, the gamble will backfire and he will lose. He will lose what he craves most and as an addict, it will lead to his downfall because he won’t be able to get any higher than that first buzz.” “But like me, Zack has sought subconscious comfort in the memory of an old friend.” With the change of tone GM steps back onto the roof. The red gravel greets him with a soft crunch. “Maxx was the reminder of what I was always capable of. I no longer require his influence. Maxxamillion Mustafa is no longer a factor in my career because I have surpassed what he ever achieved.” “Maxx was a career overachiever. Just like you, DC and Derf. I mean, I don’t quite explain how lucky you two got when you earned your respective title chances. I mean, what the fuck are the chances?” “It’s been a long time coming but finally I get to face off with the enigmatic, charismatic, metro-sexual enigma that is Eric Derf.” GM claps slowly and sarcastically. “Welcome kiddo, you made it to the main event.” “Now get the fuck out of my face.” GM wags a warning finger at the camera. “I mean, you won Money in the Bank. Big whoop for you, but you know what. That suitcase is only a chance. There’s no guarantee that you will win. And if you are thinking of cashing it in on me any time soon, then you will be next on my shit list. I will make your pathetic existence miserable.” “You are an insignificant blip on my radar until you prove to me otherwise. You have always gone AWOL after achieving something special. You always appear on the verge of the spectacular but you will always sink into obscurity, just like you did at Friday Night OverDrive. Because while I was making sure of my place in the main event, you lost to Alex freaking Bowen!!” “That’s what makes me different. I don’t drop the ball.” He grabs the title so it obstructs his face from view before lowering it after making his point. “You fumble it so much now, what makes you think you can handle the pressure of being the absolute main man?” “Past events tell me that it isn’t when a case of if you fuck up, it’s a case of when it happens. Because you all screw up sometimes. You panic and shit goes tits up.” GM moves back on to the ledge by the sign and he is clearly considering his words carefully before moving onto his biggest target. “DC would have us all believe that he is the highest power and authority in this company. Biggest earner, pulling power, number one contendership.” He steps up and the camera lowers so the sign is totally framed as his backdrop. “And such, only a fool would consider that DC has lost his star power, his “X” factor. But his star is on the decline and his work reflects this. He is a very talented but incredibly angry young man. Pissed off at the world and blaming everyone else for his fuck ups instead of taking responsibility for his actions. He has taken ranting to a whole new level and I for one need to turn down the volume.” He waves his hand dismissively. Even though he daren’t write off DC, he isn’t shy of mocking him and giving the same treatment that he would expect from DC. “I’m not even sure DC is going to able to walk to the ring at MNM let alone work a match. How stupid do you have to be to flip yourself through the roof of a Hell in a Cell structure? Face facts. You got lucky. Lucky that Sharpman isn’t a patch on me. I would have made sure that you left the cell a cripple, just like you tried to do to me in EW.” “Yeah I remember our first meeting. You were Sonny’s bitch and you two put me through a cell. The fact that I am still around means you fucked up.” GM pokes a finger in his own chest. “You should’ve killed me ‘cos I ended up with your belt.” He chuckles evilly. His eyes catch the light from the sign which seems to be encompassing him slowly. “I’d expect some sort of payback from Sharpman if I were you. Then again, I know myself and Hardaway are more than capable and therefore more than able of handling you.” “You have a habit of riling people and rubbing them up the wrong way. And this Monday, it comes back to bite you in the worst way, because Sharpman and Hardaway can’t stand you and my feelings on you are perfectly clear. But maybe you should look closer to home.” GM directs an accusing finger at the screen. “I mean, can you really trust Derf. The guy bitch slapped you for everyone to see. I see no respect there. He treated you like a punk and you’ve let him get away with it. He doesn’t admire you or hold you in any high esteem. If he says he does to your face then he is lying.” He shrugs his shoulders innocently. “And what’s wrong with that, you ask?” “Anyone else in the history of the federation would have been taken down and verbally abused in the typically vintage DC way. What makes the Derf-turder so untouchable? Maybe this match will trigger the memory a little. Maybe D is going a little soft in his old age?” He pokes his finger to his head and whirls it around a little, suggesting DC may have lost it. “D, I can respect your achievements. You know that. But your methods leave something to be desired. But you were the first MWE champion I ever encountered and so you’re the standard that I am going to emulate. But don’t worry, I will emulate you. As the first MWE Champion cut from the new breed, it’s the least I can do.” Now standing in front of the sign with its’ light totally surrounding him, GM clearly believes that he is the man to beat in MWE. There is no doubt in his mind. Having the belt in his possession has clearly done wonders for his confidence. “Anyone who wants this belt can call me out. Don’t be shy about it. I don’t mind walking through all of you. I did it to get this belt. I’ll do it to retain it.” “It’s not your fault, it’s just plain and simple destiny.” With that, he steps down from the ledge and walks off past the camera. The camera looks up to the MWE sign again and fades.
