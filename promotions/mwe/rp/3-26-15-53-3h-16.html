MWE 24/7 — 08-09 MNM RP Archives

3 + 26 = 15 + 53 + 3H + 16

Apr 11 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
At Highway to Hell, the problem was solved. The code that everyone saw for several weeks leading up to it all revolved around one thing. I was making my MWE return and I knew exactly what I was going to do once I came back. I wanted the World Championship. You know, it’s incredible. I step back and look at everything I’ve accomplished and it’s nice to look at, but with how obsessed I am with being the best, nothing is ever good enough for me. I don’t settle for “good enough” because that’s against my nature. I stepped away from the MWE over a year ago roughly to solely focus on becoming the main star somewhere else and I accomplished that feat. I became their superstar of the year, an honor that very few have received and to be quite honest, no one had truly earned like I had done. I had held championship gold for 15 straight months in that company and all but the first 9 calendar days of that year, I was empty handed. So I’m sure you wonder where I’ve been? I’ve been contemplating the return for a while. It had always been in the back of my mind that it’d be nice to win the World Championship somewhere else. I always enjoyed the thrill of being the top guy, the main attraction…the person to beat. It’s a feeling I haven’t known in almost two years. Hell, I went 16 months without even an opportunity at the title, which is crippling for someone who makes it his entire career to hold the World Championship. Regardless of the company you are affiliated with, you want to be the top guy in the industry. Period. End of story. If that is not true for you, get the hell out of the ring and find something else to do with your time. That makes one wonder why I’ve come back to the MWE. I want to be the World Champion and if that means I have to go through David Whitman to do it…so be it. Don’t worry; I’m not going to take him lightly. It’s not in my nature just to throw aside a challenge especially with someone as dominant as Whitman, but I have the goal in mind. So I’m sure you wonder how the other three members of the Cherry Pit Crew are doing that being my best friend Jack Campbell, my other best friend Eddie Parker and my wife Ashley Roberts-Cherry. I’ll tell you what, since leaving the MWE, they’ve actually had similar success as I have. Jack and Eddie lived out a dream and became the tag team champions over one of my arch rivals and I couldn’t help but be happy for them. They had been robbed of the titles on so many occasions that for them to finally break through the glass ceiling was incredible. My wife also was able to live out a dream. She became the Women’s Champion for the first time in her career and although she didn’t have a long reign with the gold, just seeing her have the ability to win the title and etch her name into the history books was something that I will never forget. I was so happy for her that day that words could not express. Then you have me. For those of you who don’t know who I am, let me run down the track record. *takes deep breath* Three time SCW World Heavyweight Champion, two time SCW United States Champion and the longest reigning champion in SCW’s six year history, 2007 SCW Superstar of the Year, former two-time AWL Hardcore Champion and the longest reigning Hardcore Champion in AWL history, former AWL Diamond Champion, one half of the first ever AWL Tag Team Champions, and in my second match ever in MWE, I won the MWE X-Television Championship. *takes another deep breath and exhales* Sure, it’s just a brief overview of my career, but it’s a career that is only four years old. I stand here at the age of 22 with my best years ahead of me and all of those accomplishments are just a testament to my ability. It’s a scary thought when you sit down and actually consider what I have done and who I beat to get there. I’ve beaten legendary superstars and some of the biggest men in our business. Hell, I’m one of the smallest guys in the business and I’ve done all that. Size isn’t everything. It’s the talent that matters. Yeah, it’s slightly arrogant and I don’t mean to come off that way. I just like to present the facts. I’ll let the people make the decision about me in due time. They seemed to accept me back with open arms at Highway to Hell and I appreciate that. Hopefully, I’ve earned their respect enough that their voice can lead me to the MWE Title. The solution has returned, the Sensation will be experienced and everybody’s life…you know the saying. Ashley and I were traveling through England. It’s strange to have my first show back be overseas, but whatever’s good for the business. We saw all the sights, but found it best just to relax in the hotel and try and prepare myself for my first match back in the MWE in a long time. Ashley: How do you like your coffee? Cherry: I like it to taste like tea. Babe, you know I don’t drink coffee. Ashley: It’s really good and it wakes you up. Cherry: That’s fantastic that you think that, but that doesn’t mean I’m going to become addicted right away. Ashley: Just try it. It won’t kill you. Cherry: What if it does? Ashley: Then…oops. Cherry: Oh, that’s a comforting thought. “It doesn’t kill you and if it does…um…my bad.” That’s a really nice sentiment from my spouse. Ashley: What do you want me to do then? Cherry: If you want to make yourself some coffee, go ahead. Just make me some tea if you’re up. Ashley: You’re not going to make it yourself. Cherry: Ash, I trust you that you’re going to make me tea. Ashley: Fine, geez. I’ll make you some tea. Ashley went over and made herself a cup of coffee as she pulled out some Lipton teabags and proceeded to make me a cup of tea. Cherry: You know, life isn’t that bad here in England. I mean the economy’s suck everywhere but at least the MWE pays well. Ashley: I wouldn’t know. I don’t work for them. Cherry: You should. It’s a lot of fun. Maybe you’d get a title opportunity after a couple shows. Ashley: They don’t have a Women’s Championship. I’m not interested. Cherry: Oh come on, women win male titles all the time nowadays. It seems to be the norm. Ashley: I think I’ll be happy just watching from the sidelines this time around if you don’t mind. Cherry: Suit yourself. You could’ve been like…MWE something Champion by the end of May. Ashley: You’re not going to convince me. Unless the MWE has a significant Women’s Division, you can forget about it. Cherry: Alright, that’s your prerogative. She finished the coffee and tea and brought it over to me as we sat beside each other and watched the rain outside. We clinked mugs as we drank out of them and relaxed a little bit. My life wasn’t hectic yet. Hell, I was just making my return; the real fun had not yet begun. Until my match with Kevin Hardaway happens, my career in MWE has not started up again. Right now, I can just relax and take it easy. So, the married life isn’t overly glamorous, but hey, I’m not complaining. My life doesn’t have an overabundance of problems. I’m just a normal guy with extraordinary in ring talent, a hot wife and two great friends. Some people may be jealous of the fact that my life isn’t a constant living hell. I can’t really help that, but the way I see it…I don’t have to help that. I’m not trying to be arrogant, but that’s the way it is. I’m happy with relaxation and if it’s the life I live, then so be it. I’m solving MWE’s problems, because I really don’t have any of my own…but I’m sure they’ll come. Ashley: I wish it was a much better day outside. Cherry: Well if I could control the weather, it would be. The rain is nice, though. Ashley: I’m not a fan of it. Did you want any food while we’re just watching this sight? Cherry: Were you going to make some for me? Ashley: I was going to say, I could order some pizza. Cherry: Because nothing says a romantic evening like British pizza. Ashley: You’re lucky that I understand your sarcasm or else I’d kick you. Cherry: I’m sure you would. Ashley: I’m surprised I haven’t kicked you with all your smartass comments towards me. Cherry: It’s something that you’ve probably gotten used to. Ashley: Yeah, well you’re lucky that I have. Cherry: I’m not scared of you. Ashley: Do you want to go? I mean, we can throw down right now if you want. Cherry: You don’t want any of this. Ashley: Is that a threat? Cherry: It’s a promise. Ashley: Alright, alright. I see where this is going. You think you can beat me. Cherry: Obviously, I own a championship defense victory over you. Ashley: Yeah and it took Jack and Eddie to help you retain. Cherry: Hey, I needed them to prevent Jay Gold from costing me the title. I can’t even imagine the embarrassment I would’ve suffered if I lost to you…no offense. Ashley: Gee thanks. Cherry: It was only your second match ever. I’m not losing a World Championship to a girl in only her second match ever. Ashley: Well now that I’m more experienced, do you want some of me now? Cherry: You can bring it if you want, but chances are, the result is still going to be the same. Ashley put down her coffee and I put down my tea and we started to wrestle a little bit. We didn’t try to hurt each other; it was just playful like couples have the tendency to do. I managed to roll her up a couple times and tried to count my own pins, but she kept kicking out. She got a lot better at this than she used to be. After a few minutes, both of us got really tired and as she stopped to take a breather, I rolled her up and counted a quick three and started to parade around my room. She shook her head and looked at me in disbelief. Ashley: I can’t believe that you would fast count your own wife. Cherry: I’m a competitive person, you know that. I do whatever I can to win. Ashley: You fast counted me. A real competitor would count fairly. Cherry: You’re telling me that you wouldn’t fast count me? I know you would, because you know you can’t beat me. Ashley: We’ll see about that. Cherry: Yeah until then, I’ll worry about my competition in the MWE. As I reached over for my tea, Ashley rolled me up from behind and tried a fast count but I managed to roll through in another pin and this time did a fair count and counted three again. Ashley slapped the floor in frustration. Cherry: I was fair that time. You can’t complain. Ashley: Okay, okay. You win. Cherry: That’s what I like to hear. I finished off my tea and set it down on the table in front of me. It’s been a while since I got to speak to the MWE faithful, so I figured I would give them the opportunity to listen to me. The person who should pay the most attention? You guessed it: Kevin Hardaway. Cherry: You know, it’s an amazing thing. I stand here ready for my first match back in the MWE in nearly a year and a half and I get a short little one-liner from my opponent about our match. That’s it? I was hyped up for nearly two months and that’s all he has to say about me? He may not know who I am, but damn man, do some research. Sure, I may not have had a long history here, but that doesn’t mean that I’m only worth 10 seconds of your time. It shows that you have no idea what you’re getting into. You have won World Championships somewhere else and quite honestly, so have I, but just because you’re in a new place doesn’t mean you can just run rough shot over it because you were big somewhere else. I won the X-Television Championship in my second match in the MWE. Apparently, the title is now the X-Havok Championship, but regardless of the name, I was able to capture it that quickly. Maybe in my case, I was given an opportunity after an impressive showing in my MWE debut. Your MWE debut…less than stellar. Sorry Kevin, but debuting at a Pay Per View event, especially one like Highway to Hell and starting off with a loss is not too good for the career. I mean, how quickly did you want your credibility shot down? If you start out with the company, at least win your first match or nobody is going to care about you. It’s the cold hard truth. Now I appreciate the fact that you were willing to give me a history lesson, but the fact of the matter is, I don’t care a lot about what you did in your past. Not to sound harsh, but this isn’t the other places and I understand that. You don’t have to care about my World Title wins if you don’t want to, it doesn’t really matter to me. However, I proved myself here as well and I’m a big name around these parts…you really need to do some research. With that being said, let me introduce myself. I’m the solution to all of the problems that go on in the MWE. I’m the Pennsylvanian Sensation and a former champion in every company I’ve ever been in, including this one. My name is Greg Cherry and I’m not someone that you just want to give a passing glance to. This is a lesson that you’re going to have to learn the hard way, it seems. You see, Kevin, while you may have done well for yourself against Windy Bridges, you didn’t do well enough. You didn’t truly impress me. I watched you compete and you were decent but you have a long way to go. I find it insulting that regardless of how much you know about me, you basically put me in the category of “Oh yeah, I have a match against Random Guy B.” For those that know me, they know for a fact that it just pisses me off when somebody doesn’t take me seriously or seriously enough. It doesn’t really matter that you don’t know a lot about me, hell, I don’t know a lot about you other than footage I’ve seen of you and your match at Highway to Hell, which in all honesty was a poor decision on your part to show me that. You’re lucky that I’m telling you what I can do instead of showing you. I don’t give away free information like showing past clips so my opponents can study it. I’m not the type of guy who lets you get one up on me. I’m competitive and full of pride…it’s a sickening combination that when put together is nearly unstoppable. Kevin, I hate to be the bearer of bad news but your ineptness in this situation, regardless of the troubles in your life is going to be your undoing. I’m not saying you’re not talented, you are that. You have a lot of potential to go far in the MWE…but you have got to learn that you can not just show up and win a match. This is not the type of thing that you’ve got “in the bag” regardless of who you face. With all of that said, I hope your personal problems in your life get resolved. I don’t want you to let this bring you down. I enjoy a challenge, don’t get me wrong. Therefore, I don’t want to face the shell of a man whose personal troubles caused him great sorrow. I want the competitor which I know you have in you. If I want to be the best, I understand I’ve got to beat the best. My journey to the MWE World Championship starts with you, Kevin. As much as I respect you, I’ll be damned if you’re my first roadblock on my way to the top. And if you don’t like what I say, then your life is the pits, deal with it. I reached over and grabbed my mug because I was thirsty for another cup of tea. As I washed it out and prepared to get myself another cup, I thought to myself…was this really going to work out as planned? Was I really going to be the one to knock off David Whitman if he got that chance? Wishful thinking is nice, but I haven’t had a straight up World Championship match in a long time. It’s been too long, but there was time to rectify that injustice. I took a sip of my hot tea and rejoined my wife to watch the continuation of the rain outside. This was the life I live…let’s hope it stays this way for a while. And…scene.
