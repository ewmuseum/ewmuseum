The Comparison; hell yea

Aug 20 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
*The cameras fade in as Sinc is seen standing in front of a chimpanzee cage at the San Francisco Zoo. He is leaning on the cage eying the chimps with great interest. He then looks into the camera and smiles* Sinc: Great timing! Today we are going to explore the WONDERFUL experience of the chimpanzee. a FASCINATING creature that seems to enjoy lounging a little too much and throwing dung at innocent bystanders when threatened. *Sinc leans off the the cage and begins pointing at it* Sinc: Now Devlin Sharpe jumps around the ring alot. This monkey jumps around with ring WORMS. Devlin Sharpe is short and annoying. This monkey is short and...wait...hes actually alot less annoying then Devlin nevermind. Devlin talks about his dick alot. This monkey plays with its dick almost every day. But hell, at least it has one. Now if you compare the two….there really is no difference…need I say more? *Sinc folds his arms and looks at the ground in thought* Sinc: You know....I have often wondered to myself...how could a person hate someone like me....then I remembered...I just don't give a shit. I don’t care because I have grown up a careless, lonely, and sometimes even heartless bastard. My father’s death didn’t make it any better either. But theres one thing that I have that most people don’t. And that is the heart, the guts, the BALLS and the glory to speak my mind, to do what I want, when I want, and to have fun with it all in the end. Cause if you aren’t having fun you aren’t living people and that’s a fact. *Sinc gestures to the cage again* Sinc: I mean look at this sorry creature….its quite pathetic really….trapped in a cage in the middle of a busy, polluted city. It can’t break free or have fun in the wild like it used to. Its freedom, its DIGNITY all stripped away in the blink of an eye. To lose something like freedom, something that means SO much to a person is for the most part unbearable. Yet it strives to continue living, it strives to survive for the good of its pride and for the good of its kind trapped along with him. A career is A LOT like a cage. Its meant to trap those who are not good enough to hold their job, yet they are only released from it to feast on those few free opportunities they have to get out into the big times. And those are opportunities you just gotta take. Cause if you don’t, it only hurts you in the end. *Sinc scratches his head and laughs as he moves closer to the camera* Sinc: You know Devlin Sharpe says how great he is and how he thinks of me and others as lesser people. Hes lost his last two matches. And hes blabbing his mouth to me and my people that I am basically a nobody and that hes a somebody. Please. Me and Bowen proved Devlin was a nobody in that ring that night even with Mancini’s tactic they STILL lost. And recently I find out theres not just one douche bag named Sharpe, theres two! Two chimpanzee looking idiots running around. Man their mom must have been REAL lucky…. *Sinc puts his face to the camera* Sinc: NOT! Those are the two UGLIEST sacks of crap I have EVER seen! They need to be in a circus for no talent twin wrestlers. But what would they be? Janitors? The new face of Numb Nut advertising? Ones the left nut the others the right? *The chimpanzee in the cage screams in a rhythmic tone* Sinc: Someone seems to agree with me. *Sinc steps back from the camera making a serious look on his face again* Sinc: As for my match…lets think about it….while I am comparing him to a chimp….and if I was wrestling an ACTUAL chimp instead of that nobody….I think the chimp would have a better chance. I mean come on….look at us…two different, unique individuals in the ring….it just so happens that one has better wrestling genes and better looks…your lookin at him people! *Sinc flaunts himself* Sinc: And Devlin’s girlfriend….I gotta admit I have a thing for red heads….but I’d rather date Rosie O’ Donnell with dyed red hair AND herpes then even THINK about going for the skank Devlin has. I know harsh…but necessary… *A zoo keeper walks up to the chimp cage and opens it to feed the chimp as the camera zooms out to get him and Sinc in the shot* Sinc: Hey buddy mind if I go in? Zoo Keeper: Sorry, Hank here is a very shy monkey….its not a very good idea…. *Sinc smugs* Sinc: Well can you at least give me a little insight on that flea bag….I mean Devlin Impersonator…I mean chimp… *The Zoo Keeper nods* Zoo Keeper: Well….its name is Hank….its shy around people it doesn’t know….it has an eating disorder and doesn’t like to eat too much even when we force it it would just walk away and hide. It does love hanging from trees and generally just relaxing…. *Sinc rubs his chin* Sinc: Sounds like Devlin to me… Zoo Keeper: Who is this Devlin that you keep referring to? And why is a camera filming you here again? Sinc: Don’t worry about it go feed Devlin, I mean the chimp. ANYWAY…. *The camera zooms back in to get only Sinc in the frame* Sinc: Another thing Devlin is full of other than shit…is well….more shit. Devlin is like a BIRD full of so much crap it can’t even walk….its too busy leaving a trail of feces. Kind of like his girlfriend on her period. But worse and more messy…well not really about the same actually… *Sinc pauses a moment to think* Sinc: I am so sorry I almost forgot…the comment Devlin made about how ‘MWE Legends such as HIMSELF get pushed around in some meaningless matches’ so technically he’s more than just a bird full of crap….he’s an annoying….SQUAWKING….WHINING….PATHETIC BIRD! One of those birds you shoot on the street with a BB gun as a kid….its too fat to dodge the bullet….and too stupid to realize its coming…just like Devlin before he knows whats coming I give him a taste of his own medicine and break his damn body to pieces! *Sinc’s facial expression turns to anger* Sinc: Devlin Sharpe….listen to a word of advice from The Arrogant Aviator ok? Watch your little midget monkey ass because I will make sure I beat you again. And again. AND again to get you out of my way for championship gold. You will NOT EVER be a threat to me. YOU are the nothing amigo, YOU are the nothing. Not me or even Alex Bowen. I am the fresh talent MWE needs and I am the talent they expect. And what they expect they will get. You are the fire and I am the water, and your girlfriend is the cave creature from the under BUSH that just so happens to be there with her octopus like, messy, DIRTY, sex capades watching it all. Think that over monkey boy in the meantime I, SIIIIIIIIIINCCCCCCC MERCIERRRRRRRR, will see you next week. *The noise buzzes out as Sinc storms out of the zoo the camera zooming in on his storming leave and fading with the sounds and the images of nearby zoo animals*
