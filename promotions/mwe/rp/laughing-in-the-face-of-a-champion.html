Laughing in the Face of a Champion

Nov 24 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
*Mason Resoan and Sara Whitaker are walking down the streets of Toronto, Ontario, Canada next to each other. They are not talking at all, but definitely seem to be unhappy. They round the corner and walk into a shop, in which Mason respectfully holds the door open for Whitaker. They enter in and it turns out to be a clothing shop. Mason walks over, looks up and down the aisles and smiles. He whips out his phone and makes a phone call, smirks again, and hangs up. He and Sara walk up to the cashier.* Cashier: Hello, how may I help you two today? *The cashier has a very delicate voice and is wearing a teal long-sleeve Abarcrombie and Fitch shirt with brown dress shoes and a silver chain around his neck. His voice is extremely high pitched. Mason looks around and over the counter to look for something, and can't find it. Mason: Does someone have your balls in a vice or something? Cashier: What did you say? Mason: Oh, nothing...nothing at all. Um...see that whole wall of clothing? Yeah...um, *Mason looks at Sara, and she nods.* we would like to buy it all. Cashier: All of it? Sara: Did he stutter? Get your a** over there and tell us how much we owe. And hurry the f*** up. Cashier: No need to get fies... *The cashier stops short as Sara gives him a very evil glare. She is almost growling at people at this point, as the cashier trots in a girl fashion over to the wall. He is counting and checking tags, as he continuously look back at Mason and Sara. Sara catches him looking back at her and calls him out.* Sara: Listen, you stupid little butt pirate, if you look back here again I am going to have Mason beat the s*** out of you. I know you aren't looking at my tits, so stop trying to scope out Mason's penis you queer. *The cashier is highly offended at this point, and is almost reduced to tears. Sara is looking for fierce than ever and is getting increasingly more angry by the minute. Mason is keeping a little distance in case she swings at the guy. The cashier makes a finally count as he sniffles and types some numbers into the register. He finally gets a grand total, with tax and everything, and prints out the receipt. He hands it over the counter, and Sara snatches it out of his hand very violently. The cashier is obviously scarred out of his wits, and Sara is showing absolutely no remorse for her insults. She grabs Mason by the wrist and tells him to open the door, which he does. Sara walks across the store, as two men in black suits walk in. She points to the wall and some carts and marches away, taking Mason with her. They get into a limousine and begin to drive. Mason makes an attempt to talk to her.* Mason: Sara, you really shouldn't get so worked up over this. I can handle myself, this match will be fine. Sara: That isn't the point. You should not have to wrestle against this imbecile in the first place. You are perfect, in almost every way. Articulation, looks, talent, skills, senses, and in bed. However, you are being placed against a man who loses one match, and gets wasted on the side of the f***ing road and cries until his friends have to come console him. *Sara is rocking back and forth in her seat in uncontrollable rage. There is a small vein in her neck that can be shown, and she tries to relax. She lays back on the leather seat of the limo, and takes a deep breath.* Mason: Listen, I know what I did...I was there. You need to calm down though. All you have to do is escort me to the match, root me on, and I will decimate him...simple as that. Sara: Don't you want more? He caused you a championship that you rightfully deserved and now he is wearing it. Doesn't that get to you at all. Mason: Yeah, it does, but you have to realize that by freaking out, you are acting like Sinc, himself. *Sara smacks Mason in the face, pretty hard. A big red-mark is imprinted on his face, and he rubs the place where he was hit. He has a puzzled face on and looks at Sara in disapproval. She looks back at him in anger.* Sara: Don't you ever...and I mean ever compare me to that piece of filth...ever...ever again. If you do, I will make sure that your voice is as high as that cashier's, but to make your voice that high-pitched...it will hurt. *Mason covers his groin area and cringes, in his mind, at the thought of what is girlfriend would do. They don't talk for the rest of the ride to the mansion. They arrive, the driver gets out, and Sara kicks open the door. The back swing knocks the driver over and Sara struts to the front door in a very passionate manner. Mason smirks his evil smirk the whole time as he watches her slam the door of the mansion shut. He steps out, cleans off his pants, and throws the driver a 50 dollar bill. He jogs to the front door and enters, as you hear the limo screech away in the background.* Sara: Get over here right now. *Mason, reluctantly, obeys.* Sara: Sara: Do you have a cigarette? Mason: You smoke? Sara: There are some things I do hide from you. Now, do you have a smoke, or will I have to go all the way to my room to get one. Mason: No, don't worry about it. There are some leftover from our last party. *Mason points* they're over on the table in the dining room. *Sara walks over, opens up the pack, takes out a lighter, and lights the cigarette. She takes a puff and walks outside. She motions for Mason to follow and he does. Outside, Sara calms down, and lets out a sigh of relief as the cigarette clams her down.* Mason: So, what else do you want? Why am I out here right now, it's getting cold. Sara: You need to tell me why you didn't say something at the meeting? Mason: What do you mean? What was there to say. We went to Texas, had the meeting, I found out my match, and left. Quick, easy, no problems. Sara: So you are telling me that you are fine having a match against scum. You're telling me that you wouldn't rather just go straight for Whitman or at least try to do something more with your life than just toy around with a man named Sinclair? Mason: Well, I figured that I could get a shot in on him? *Sara sighs once more. She shakes her head, takes a drag, and taps the cigarette.* Mason: What, you're going to try and sit there and tell me it is wrong to go after the man who cost me the title? If you haven't realized, he won the title again, and it is going to be me versus him at Adrenaline Rush for the championship. How sweet would it be to get consecutive wins over him...embarrass him a little...and win the title? It would be perfect payback for having him cost me the match at Wrestle Wars. Sara: Don't tell me you are going soft? *Sara has the "wtf" look on her face. Even more so, Mason has a confused and dazed look on his face.* Sara: You do realize that you tried to run over Alejandro Rodriguez after your match...you do realize that you were the one to start the whole D.C. versus Michaels feud. You also completed decimated Devlin Sharpe and James Sullivan, the latter of which you ending their career. Sullivan did nothing but lose and you showed more emotion towards him than you do now for Sinc. Devlin and A-Rod didn't do anything either, yet you showed emotion for them? What is going on? Mason: Listen, I do everything for a reason. I pick my spots, I do my business, and I get s*** done. Why do you think people hate going against me. They know that I know what I am doing...plus, I am the only man that doesn't fear D.C. I will handle Sinclair Mercier...don't you worry. I will also deal with winning the United States Championship. I have everything under control, and you just have to trust me on this one. Sara: You better, because I don't want to have double-cross my word. I will do what I have to do. Mason: And I will do what I have to do, now let's go inside. It's cold as hell. *Sara and Mason both walk inside, as there is a knock on the door. It is the moving crew with there newly purchased clothes. Mason and Sara take them upstairs into their room and shut the door... *...The scene re-opens: Mason Resoan is walking through this mall, with a very big stride. Many people look and turn around to see what all the ruckus is, and it is simply the fact that Mason is being followed, as always, by Mayhem Wrestling Entertainment cameras. He struts throughout the building, in his newly purchased outfit. He is wearing a silver chain down his neck, brown Oakley sunglasses, shiny polished brown shoes, and his traditional gold watch. He is also sporting a white button up T-Shirt with Levi Blue Jeans. As he walks through the mall, he looks at all the colors and the adds that are all to familiar to his everyday life. He is disgusted by the sight at some of the people he has seen, especially while walking past the food court. Finally, he made it to a back room on the far side of the mall, in which he met with the mall's manager.* Manager: May I help you? *The manager has an aggravated look on his face. Like Mason was not supposed to be in this area of the mall.* Manager: You do know that regular customers are not allowed in this section of the mall. Mason: Listen you ballsy f***, you shouldn't be talking to someone who is twice your size and intelligence. Next, I do belong here because I am picking up the keys for the car I purchased here not to long ago. Manager: Does this look like a car dealership. Get out of my sight before I call security. Mason: Listen, you dumb a**hole. I purchased a car you had on display here. It has been waiting here for me for about two weeks now. *Mason pauses, looks over the store managers head, and sees the wall behind him. His face becomes even more distorted than before, as he looks at a hook with keys on them. He points and the manager turns around. The manager immediately blushes, rushes to the wall, and hands the keys to Mason. Mason takes them and walks out of the store to the parking lot in the back where his car is waiting for him.* *Mason pulled out of the parking lot and drove away at a high speed to a local morning radio station. Mayhem Wrestling Entertainment is popular in this part of Toronto and he was asked to do a show. In style, he pulls up to the station, gets out and walks into the station. As he walks in, he is immediately engulfed by the setting of electronics and awards on the wall. Many levers and buttons are around him on very expensive looking pieces of equipment. Lining the walls in the main hallway are records, casettes, and CD's of all different music styles. He could tell that this station was old and had a very prestigious history.* *As he continued his walk, he began to see more and more pictures and records. Some of the most world renown artists and actors had graced the building. Robert De Niro, Slash, and he even saw some wrestlers like himself up on the walls. He continued to gander at the scenery as he walked around the building, until he stumbled upon the main recording room. Inside sat several men, majority of which were waring baseball caps with the station's name embedded on the front. They were relatively friendly, but kept quite only nodding or giving a slight wave. He knocked on the glass and they pointed away. He followed their fingers into an even larger room with less people inside. Only three people were in this room, but Mason knew he was in the right place. A rather small man walked up to him and greeted him with respect.* Man: Mason Resoan, glad you could make it. We are about to start the show in about 20 minutes. In the mean time, get situated and we will set up all the equipment so we can get under way. If you have any questions, ask them before the start of the show, and we will be happy to answer them. Our main DJ, DJ Slack and our productions manager, Kyle Adams are sitting in the room already. And by the way, I am Kevin Rothman. Nice to have you here. *Mason was a little taken back by the extreme friendliness of Kevin, but accepted it and walked into the room. He sat on a stool and began to mingle with DJ Slack and Kyle Adams. 13 minutes later, Kevin Rothman was back and everything was ready to go. He sat on a stool to the left of Mason and was primed and ready to ask questions. The clock turned 9:00 and Kevin Rothman flicked a switch.* Kevin: Hello ladies and gentlemen, this is Toronto Morning Radio Show, or TMRS. Today, our special guess is a man you may...actually, you should recognize as hometown hero Mason Resoan from Mayhem Wrestling Entertainment. Today, we will be having a sit-down with him before his match with Sinclair Mercier, the current United States Championship. However, before we get started, we have to introduce ourselves. I am Kevin Rothman, the man with the questions. DJ Slack: I am DJ Slack, bringing you all of your musical needs. Kyle: And I am Kyle Adams, the man that will broadcast to you all you need to know. Kevin: And with that, let's get this program underway. We will be accepting any calls...oh and we already have one now. Hello, yes, caller. What is your question for Mason Resoan. Caller #1: *muffled* Mason, this week you face Sinc Mercier and then again at Adrenaline Rush for the gold. Why don't you just take him out now and take his championship. I mean, it is not like he is going to beat you anyway. Mason: I know that, you know that, Sara knows that, but Richard Covell has his head to far up his own a** to realize that. So, whenever he gets the memo, I will be destroying Sinc Mercier only once and maybe then...maybe I will be happy. Kevin: Thank you caller number one. We already have a second caller. Yes, caller number two, ask your question. Caller #2: Thanks KR. Now, Mason, what do you think of Brian Michaels getting a title shot this week on Monday Night Mayhem? Mason: I am disgusted...honestly, Covell, you have made some bad decisions in your career...but this...this is a new low. I mean, he loses his match to me when I debuted. He came to a draw against D.C at Wrestle Wars II, he lost a second match to me in a tag match, yet he gets a title shot. It's just like with Sinc Mercier. He does nothing but lose, but he gets all the rewards. I am sorry, but if you have to sink that low in your career that you have to start relying on connections to get somewhere, than you should just give up altogether. If Brian Michaels can pull out a victory this week, I am sorry to you David Whitman, because you should not have even had to defend the gold in the first place. And as for you Michaels, you will simply be putting yourself back onto my radar. Kevin: Some strong words from a strong man. Nice! Now, in this portion of the show, we would like to let you go freestyle. Talk to us about MWE, tell us what is going up...or down for that matter. *Mason cracks his neck and smiles at this. He thinks of Sara and what she would be saying right now and continues.* Mason: I don't know if you wanted this or not, but either way, I'll tell you what is going on. Adrenaline Rush, our next big Pay-Per-View is around the corner, and I get a shot at the United States title. Sinc Mercier is going to be my opponent, and I have to say, it will be nice to get what I deserved in the first place. *Mason looks at the three other people in the room for a second, then zones out, and looks into the cameras that followed him to the station. He lowers in closer to the broadcasting microphone.* Mason: Sinc, I would like for you to know that what I will do to you on Monday...and at Adrenaline Rush is nothing compared to what my girlfriend wants to do to you. So, piss me off, and you piss her off...and that just means that morals, rules, and anything that she stands by is completely null and void at this point. However, what I will do to you is beat you...to win. I want nothing to do with you and if you suck me in further into your life, then some things might happen to your family, friends, and co-workers that you didn't want to happen. I mean, look at Devlin Sharpe. He didn't do a damn thing to me. Not one thing, but he was to close to Brian Michaels...and look what happened. I left him bruised and beaten on a concrete floor in the back of an Arena. And you might be asking yourself, "What does that mean for me?" What it means is that you better not have anybody to close to you...hmm, say your brother for instance, because he might just get hurt. *Mason backs away from the mic and catches his breath for a little bit. The intensity in Mason's voice was growing towards the end of the speech.* Mason:However, to avoid all of these potential problems with family and friends, I suggest that you don't start with me. *Mason retreats a little with his tone and also adjusts a button on his white T.* Mason:Sinc, you need to grow up. Honestly, to me, you look like a teenage girl. You go from pissed, to happy, to sad, to in love, to pissed again. Are you going to start cutting yourself. Grow up you stupid b****. Crying to your brother isn't going to help you in your match against me. It won't help you realize your dreams. And it sure as hell won't help you hold onto that United States Championship that you obviously hold onto, oh so dearly! I have no responsibility for what you do outside our matches, I can only watch, but if I was you I might consider...uh, I don't know, training for a match. Actually being prepared before you step into the ring without knowing what your up against. This Monday, I might hold back. I won't lie, I just might. Because, I want you to have just a little taste of what I can do to you physically and psychologically when we wrestle one-on-one. Then, at Adrenaline Rush, I will show you what it means to be a real wrestler. A real superstar. Someone who actually makes things happen and gets things done. After Monday, maybe you will open up your eyes. If not, well then I will just have to laugh. *Mason laughs a little. To himself, it is quite normal, however to Kevin Rothman, Kyle Adams, and DJ Slack, it is a very sadistic and evil laugh. A laugh that sends chills down your spine and makes your bones cringe. A very serious chuckle...like the chuckle of a madman.* Mason: Yes, laughing in the face of a champion. Laughing in the face of a so-called MWE Elite. Champions are supposed to be the front men of our industry...of our profession. But I am still here laughing...having a good ole time. I just find it funny I guess, to know that I am in a company in which the normal superstars are really the leaders. Not the captains, not the champions. Actually, I don't know how we function as a company with such horrible superstars in the first-line. The army wouldn't work if the Captain was below the private. It would make no sense and that is how MWE is running right now. That...is why I laugh in the face of our so-called elite. I just hope that I can help MWE run more smoothly when I take over...as an MWE elite, as a captain, as a leader, and as our front man. Sinc, you are an embarrassment to our company and I hope that this Monday... I really do hope that I can take you to school so that Adrenaline Rush won't be so boring. So I can actually see why you are champion, because as of right now, you are just a transition. *Mason gives off another laugh and Kevin shudders a little.* Kevin: Intensity folks, that's all I can say. Well, we will come back to this segment in a little bit, but for now we have to take a little break so here is some music in the meantime. Play it for them DJ Slack. *DJ Slack fires up some music as Mason gets up and takes a drink. Mason walks back to the main room and once again zones out to admire the scenery around him Kevin Rothman then taps him on the shoulder. It was time to resume the interview again.* Kevin: And we are back, once again with Mayhem Wrestling Entertainment's #1 Contender for the United States Championship; Mason Resoan! And Mason, you have given us some powerful stuff so far, but we have to hold you up for just a second. We have another caller and this will be our last. So, caller number three, make this question a good one. Caller #3: So, Mason, you talk a lot about Sinc Mercier. But, what about Friday Night Genesis? I mean, D.C, a man you say you don't fear, is now champion. Mason:D.C...Is a joke. His run will be a joke and Genesis is a joke. One thing I am sure that both Sinc and I can agree on is that by far, Mayhem is the dominant show...and we will show that this statement is true by Tuesday of next week. D.C, you got drafted to a second rate show. A show where I don't know who half the people are, and you pride yourself on being the show's champion? To me, that is more of a disgrace than anything. And now you feel special, special because at Adrenaline Rush you get the opportunity to do something you should have done over a year ago. I bet you feel very proud...well you shouldn't. In fact, I would be in depression to know that I could not beat Brian Michaels. *Mason just smirks and gives a slight cough.* Mason: Beating Sonny Williams for a shot at the gold...not something that hard to do. And then beating who in the main event? A biker, a freak, and a man with an ego the size of f***ing Russia. Congrats., honestly, you just won a belt that takes your career lower than when Aiden Copane started dating Carrie Marie. *Mason frowns at the mention of Aiden Copane's name.* Mason:Speaking of Aiden Copane, I have to say that this special referee match is utter garbage. I am looking for a loss with Aiden Copane as a potential Special Guest Referee. I mean, I beat the hell out of him, became more successful than him, and actually found a girl that would have sex with me, that didn't have a genital disease. I swear, that Kim girl had a sign above her head that read "STD's here" and like a jacka**, Aiden jumped all over it. In fact, I would rather not have Aiden as the referee not because he will screw me over, but because I don't feel like having to get close to a man that burns when he pees. Aiden Copane, f*** you and get out of my sight. And then we have Pala. Former member of the Mayhem Mafia. Former MWE World Heavyweight Champion. A man I beat the hell on multiple occasions. I even beat the hell out of him and prevented him from winning back his title, which I wound up winning. So why should he be objective in our match. Why should he be fair when he probably hates me. He won't and that is why I say f*** you Pala. Go get a job at McDonalds or something because no one wants to see you anymore, especially not me. *Mason thinks for a second, and almost as if a light bulb went off in his head, he began to talk again.* Mason: Finally, a special referee that might actually work. Katherine Tenaud, an objective superstar or former superstar who can't hold a grudge against me. A woman who would actually make sense to have as a referee, because we know that she would make everything balanced. I hope, for Christ sakes, that KT gets in as the referee or so help me, Adrenaline Rush will be hell. *Mason slows down a little and looks at his watch. Only ten minutes left on the show.* Mason: Oh and Sonny Williams, I would just like to laugh at you for a second... *Mason gives a little chuckle.* Mason: So close, you were so close, and then you let yourself get crushed by D.C. Way to go, have a wonderful career at that second rate show. You jumped ship and now look what happened. You lost against D.C and now you are back in the grabs for the Intercontinental Championship. Best of luck to you, because you might need it as of late. Kevin: Well, it seems that the show is coming to a close. Mason any other closing words of wisdom before you go? Mason: Yes, just one last thing. Sinc, I know I have said it before, but I will say it again, you need to shape up and worry about me. You b**** and moan about D.C, Whitman, your dad, your brother, and everyone else, but you don't even think about me. I say, make your priorities and then b****, because it could be a fatal mistake you may regret. Oh and Sara says f*** you. *Mason holds up his phone with a text-message from Sara Whitaker that says f*** you on it.* Mason: And if you didn't know...now ya know. Kevin: And that was Mason Resoan people. Good luck with your match against Sinc Mercier and do Toronto proud. And now, more music from DJ Slack. *Kevin flicks a switch again and Slack starts to play music again. Kevin hands Mason three hundred and fifty dollars and Mason shakes his hand. The part without words and Mason walks out of the building to his car and speeds out of the parking lot without a fear in his mind. The screen cuts to black.*
