History According To Zack; (Z)ack Crash WW RP

Dec 1 2009

Zack Crash

First off, you’ll have to forgive me. I’ve never really done this kind of thing before, and I’d be lying if I said I wasn’t a bit worried I’ll screw this up. But then again, since when as the idea of failure ever stopped me from doing anything. If I did, I wouldn’t be the Ultimate Thrill Seeker. And I’ll try any cheap thrill once, and this seems to be the popular way to do this sorta thing these days, so here I am. But I’m getting off topic. The reason I’m doing things differently this time is because I feel it’s important you understand things from my perspective, especially considering the situation it involves, which is more than complicated. Nobody really understands the deal with Oblivion and me. They may think they do, but they’re just clueless. But that’s why I’m here today, hopefully, to help to truly understand what happened. Right now, at this very moment, I find myself walking once again through the lonely hallways that were once the Shooting Star Wrestling Academy. It’s funny that I’ve been trespassing here twice now and nobodies stopped me. I guess that shows how much anyone cares about this place. They won’t even front the money to bulldoze this building to the ground. But that actually makes me happy. I don’t know what I’d do if this place was torn down. Walking through these familiar halls fills me with a kind of goodhearted nostalgia. Memories unfolded before my eyes like living specters, and it brings a smile to my face. Being here and remembering my roots makes me look back on my career from where I started to where I am now, and it really puts everything in perspective. The place smells thicker of dust and mold than before, and the light seems stuck undecided between light and darkness. But even if it were pitch black, I could still navigate my way no problem. Are these changes just in my mind, or is it because of something else? I meant it when I said I was no longer afraid of Oblivion or the past. That much is true. But what grips me now is something much worse; something that, no matter what happens, I’ll never be able to completely shake. Guilt. Because somewhere within me, I wonder how things would have be different. Because somewhere within me, I still wish we were the friends we used to be. But those thoughts are meaningless. What happened…happened, and there’s nothing that can be done to change it. I’ve always been a man who believed in fate. I don’t necessarily have to like it, but it was how it was meant to be. I walk slowly into the gym, my feet echoing forebodingly like in the horror flicks. There showered in the room’s solitary pool light, like a beacon, is the ring. What was our old nickname for the ring again? It’s funny what you remember and what you can’t right? I walk over to the ring, almost as if it is calling me to it and crawl through the ropes and stand center ring, feeling the old and worn springs of the ring mat beneath my feet, bathed in the cleansing light. It was, in this very ring, in this very spot even, that everything that was right turned dreadfully into so terribly wrong. And standing in this ring that holds so much history in its mat, I understand a certain truth. I walk over to one of the corners and sit with my back against it. Feeling the pads of the turnbuckle curve up my spine somehow manages to relax me. I close my eyes for some rest, and I begin to see history play out before my shut eyes as if it were repeating itself. History is a funny thing. There’s the saying that, “history is written by the winners.” But in this particular history lesson…I’m not sure if there are any winners at all. Many Years Ago… It was a day like any other. The Academy was a mess of activity. Only difference was it isn’t in the shambles that it is today, but I guess you already knew that. One of the things that amazed me most about the place when it was in its prime was how clean it always was. Even with all the sweat, the floors and walls were clean enough to eat off of, but again, I’m getting off topic. The place always felt more like a club than a wrestling school. You’d walk in and everywhere you’d find people leaning against walls or sitting on benches talking, people carving their bodies with the weight machines, or just in the ring mastering their craft. It was just a very social environment. One this day, I’d just come out of the locker room dressed in my ring attire, ready for some quick training when I heard a voice call from the front lobby. ^*&@: Hey Zack! Said the man who you all would one day know as Oblivion. He rushed up to meet me and we shared a friendly grasp of the shoulders. Zack: Yo dude. We exchanged the usual quick greetings like we always did, just bullshitting about meaningless stuff. You all know how it is. ^*&@: You excited about tonight? Zack: Hell yeah you know I am! What we we’re both so excited about was the fact that the CWF (Catalyst Wrestling Federation) was having one of their shows tonight at our school. They brought their shows to our place every so often, and it always gave us newer guys a chance to shine. Me and the man who would become Oblivion had belong to the small promotion for about a year now, just getting our feet wet to the business. It didn’t pay much, but neither of us cared. Not only were we doing what we loved, we were doing it together. Zack: But I bet you’re even more excited than I am. It had been decided that ^*&@ was to be given the CWF Title that very night. And it was all he could talk about every since. ^*&@: I don’t know. I’m actually more nervous than anything. ^*&@ turned away, as if ashamed of himself for feeling the way he did. I just smiled and shook my head dismissively at him, patting him so hard on the back he lurched forward a bit. Zack: Don’t worry. You’re going to be great as the World Champ. And not long after this, you’ll be heading toward the big time. ^*&@ looked back at me with a gracious smile that was turning slowly back into playful. ^*&@: Thanks man, I really appreciate it. But what about you? I had almost expected him to ask about this. He didn’t even need to specify what he was talking about. We’d always had a friendly rivalry with each other. We even at one point made a fun bet over who would win gold first in CWF. But now that he was possibly moving ahead of me, he felt a bit like he was leaving me behind. It really showed you that, when worst came to worst, we were in this together. But he did deserve it, and I was honestly very happy for him. But that didn’t mean I couldn’t tease about it a bit. I crossed my arms across my chest and put on a mock frown, turning away from him. Zack: Well…I guess I’ll let it go and forgive you this one time. But you owe me for making you look so good. ^*&@ let a short burst of laughter. ^*&@: You make me look good? Due, anyone would look good next you. In the middle of the hallway, we locked up in a pretend grappling battle, forcing others to move out of our way. We were sure we were getting some ugly and annoyed looks, but we didn’t care. “Screw them” was always our motto. We were having fun, and that was all that mattered. ???: If you don’t mind, please refrain from having one of your…sexual encounters in the middle of the hall. It’s so undignified. Our playful scene turned sour as THAT voice sucked the energy from the room like the vacuum of space. We both turned around and there, leaning stupidly against the door to the locker room…was JT Hunt. JT Hunt: If you two gentlemen…a term I use it the very loosest sense, want to see a real wrestler, then I suggest you watch me tonight. Both of us scowled at Hunt. Even back then he was a bigger dick than Kayne West at the MTV Music Awards. And even then, he was just as mediocre. He hadn’t even moved past the low card, and he’d been in CWF longer than we were. But of course, his fragile ego wouldn’t allow him to admit being second-rate, which was probably being too kind. It still amazes me that he was ever in a company with the kind of legacy GWC has. Zack: Don’t you have someone else to bother right now? I shot at him. I know, not the best come back in the world, but I wasn’t in the mood to deal with him right now. Hunt just gave me a condescending snort of laughter and walked off. I’ve always hated that stupid little laugh of his. I think it makes him sound like a retard. Me and ^*&@ just glared at him while he walked down the hall, parting the way of people like Moses and the Red Sea. Hunt was never well liked by anyone in the academy. He was always just so off-putting to everyone. And Hunt himself didn’t seem to care what people thought of him. Which was good for us at least. That way, everyone could be open about how much they hated him. ^*&@: Just ignore him. He only does it because he knows it gets ya. I bite down on my lower jaw, feeling my teeth crack underneath my silent anger. Zack: Easier said than done. There was a moment’s silence as ^*&@ let me vent my anger. Hunt just has this way of getting under my skin more than any other person had. Even now, I’m not entirely sure what it was. ^*&@: Anyway, you up for our usual practice match? The effect was instantaneous. Hunt vanished from my mind like the insignificant douche he is. A smile spread so far across my lips I felt my cheeks might burst. Zack: You’re on! So after ^*&@ got himself dressed in his ring gear, we headed for the main gym, and the ring. Seriously…what the hell did we call that thing? Anyway, we always did practice matches together before one of the CWF shows. Practice matches are kinda weird for me. They’re really the closest things to fun as you can get in wrestling. Not to say a real wrestling match isn’t fun, it’s just that the competition can…do things to ya. You can lose yourself in the rush of competition, and winning becomes the only thing that matters. But in a practice match, there isn’t any of that. No winners or losers. Just good ol’ fashioned fun. The two of us waited until it was finally our turn to use the ring. We took a few moments to stretch ourselves in our respective corners before turning to face one another. ^*&@: I’ll go easy on ya this time. ^*&@ said as we got ourselves ready in the ring. I smiled slyly and countered with my own clever banter. Zack: I thought that was my line. With that, we tied up in the center ring. The match went back and forth as our practice matches usually do. We weren’t hitting each other with our moves too hard, taking it in turns to practice our moves and taking bumps. It was really more of a way to keep our skills fresh, but it was still fun. But in this particular match, something sudden came over me. I felt the urge to try a new move I’d only just learned recently. A Shooting Star Press. We’d had this unspoken agreement not to try moves that were too difficult for guys at our level, but come on! It’s the Shooting Star Press, and this was the Shooting Star Wrestling Academy. You don’t need to be a rocket scientist to connect the dots on that one. Something about the rush I was feeling told me to fly. So I whispered into ^*&@ ear what I want to try. He protested at first, but eventually gave it. So we set up the move, and I began to climb the nearest turnbuckle. As I did, I felt like I was climbing Everest, and I felt on top of the world. Thing is though, when you’re on top of the world, it also means the fall is much harder. Zack: Time to fly motherfuckers! And I jumped. Instantly, I knew something went wrong. I’d practice the move enough times to know how the landing should feel if done right. But I’d landed at an odd angle, and a strange vibrating sensation ran through my knee and up the entire length of my leg. It felt like my leg had received the kick from a shotgun firing. And I heard a screeching sound in my ears that was probably just as loud. My first thought was how that couldn’t be a good sound. Zack: Dude, are you ok? He didn’t answer me. He probably couldn’t hear me over his wailing. A hand was pressed up hard against his eye, and I feared the worse. But even that was as bad as what I saw next. Blood began pouring through his finger like water, and for the briefest second, his hand move away from his eye. Or at least, what I assumed was his eye. Half his face was covered in red. I couldn’t move. I couldn’t speak. I was frozen was a few of the instructors pushed me aside and knelt down beside my friend. Instructor: Someone get the doctor! You, get some towels now! We need to stop the bleeding! Put some pressure down on it! The instructor kept shouting orders, and various students obeyed with instant responses and a team of instructors surrounded him. But I couldn’t do anything. All I could do was stand there in shock and look on as my friend’s blood began to stain the mat. Bloody Mat. I remember now, that’s what we used to call the ring. And standing there, I thought the namely felt grimly appropriate. A Few Hours Later… Zack: How is he doing? I was sitting on one of the benches inside the changing room as one of the instructors and the doctor who had examined ^*&@ finally entered after a long period of me internally beating myself up. They loomed over me like accusatory shadows. The room felt colder than usual, and I felt confined under their understanding, almost pitying frowns. The doctor cleared his throat before continuing. Doctor: Unfortunately your friend has suffered some major trauma and damaged to his retina. It’s nothing serious, although he’ll be lucky if he can ever see out of that eye again. He should make a full recovery in time, but there’s no way he’ll be able to compete tonight, or for a long while for that matter. I hung my head, ashamed of myself. Great, not only do I nearly kill him, but I ruin the biggest night of his wrestling life. I hadn’t even called him yet to check up on him, too filled with my own self-guilt and pity to even try. Instructor: We just told the bookers at CWF about this, and they just informed us they’d like you to take his place. I look up, shocked beyond a response. Oh yeah, this day just keeps getting better and better. All I could manage was the stuttering of one word. Zack: Wh-what? Instructor: They said they want to give you his World Championship spot. They need someone to replace ^*&@ for tonight and apparently they came up with a new angle for you getting the championship shot. Words failed me. Yet, as if from someone else’s body, I heard myself speak. Zack: I can’t. Instructor: What?! Why? Don’t you know what a huge opportunity this is? I looked away from his tempting eyes, not trusting myself to answer. Because something told me, if I kept looking at him, I just might have to admit something to myself. Admit to fact that I, somewhere deep inside myself, really wanted that title match. Zack: I just can’t. I’m sorry. I hear a pitying sigh escape his lips, almost as if he understands what I’m going through or how I feel. But he doesn’t. How can he, when I don’t understand it myself? Instructor: Look, I get that you feel bad about what happen, I really do. But you have to understand that in this line of work, accidents happen. And you just have to learn to roll with whatever happens. Now this is a HUGE opportunity Zack. And if you pass it up, there might never be another one. Slowly, but surely, I feel my voice of conscience give way to my voice of desire. The only answer I give him, at least still able to feel a bit of shame for what I’m about to do, is a small, almost unnoticeable nod of the head. Later That Night… Sitting in the back room after the show, I began to truly notice how heavy the World Title felt over my shoulders. It was more like a ball and chain than anything to me. Every slap on the back felt like a punch to the face. Every word of congratulations felt like an insult. I was so sick with myself I felt literally ill and dizzy. Here I was, injuring my friend with a wrestling move, jumping on what was HIS opportunity like some kind of vulture, acting as if nothing was wrong. I couldn’t stand it. Eventually, I had to get out of there. I stood up and offered some reason for me to excuse myself. I ran for the back door, bursting through it and to the outside in just enough time to hack up some of my lunch on the pavement. The night air felt suffocating. ???: You were great out there tonight. The voice was so quiet and calm it made me jump slightly. I turned to find a girl standing before me. No, not a girl…a woman. A woman whose features, even in the shrouding darkness, were so beautiful and perfect she might was well as been an angel. Zack: Thanks. I said weakly, rubbing a bit of my vomit from my mouth, hoping she didn’t see. Something inside me told me I shouldn’t be looking like this in front of her. So I straightened myself up as best I could while she slowly stepped into the light of a nearby street lamp. In the fullness of the light, I recognized who it was. Zack: Heather?! And while I expected her to be at ^*&@’s side, here she was, looking up at me with what I saw to be lust in her eyes. Zack: What are you doing here? Shouldn’t you be with ^*&@ ? She didn’t answer me. Or at least, not with words. She comes close and rubs a finger up my still bare chest. As I smell her heavy perfume clog up my nostrils, my heart begins to race like it’s on speed or something. I’m at an even bigger lose for words than I was before. What exactly is going on? Heather: Oh, I find hospitals to be sooooooo depressing. I’d much rather be here…watching you…if you know what I mean. She smiles up at me with her charming smile, and I look deeply into her eyes. And looking at her, I’m struck with an urge. The urge to kiss her. To hold her. To undress her. Wait, what?! I gently back away, smiling and playing ignorant with her hoping she’ll quit tempting me. I look away, noticing within the shadows of the arena, Hunt looking at us with evil satisfaction. Zack: Wh-what do you mean? But she doesn’t back off. Instead, she’s right on top of me again…literally. Her body is pressed up so close to mine I feel our heart beats might merge to become one. And that’s not all I’d like to become one with her – wait, stop it! I try to fight the feelings I have right now, but I can sense my defenses give way. Back In The Present… I open my eyes as the memory thankfully comes to an abrupt end and I once again find myself back where I started. The Shooting Star Wrestling Academy is once again back in shambles before my eyes. Sitting in one of the corners of Bloody Mat, I rise to my feet and step into the center of the pool of light, reaching down from the rafters to the ring. Standing there, with the memory now fresh in my brain, I come back to that truth I now understand. It’s more ironic really. A wrestling ring was where this all started, and it would be a wrestling ring where it would end. Wrestle Wars…was a place for endings. Zack: They say that, eventually, all good things must come to an end. And I suppose that’s true. Because when you think about it, it’s the only way we can truly appreciate anything. If something stays with us forever, you’d just look past it and never notice it one way or the other. But with the possibility of losing something, then you really start to appreciate it and what it means to you. Yes, all good things must come to an end eventually. And that’s exactly what happened, because last week on MNM, my long standing winning streak came to a crashing (pun intended) end. Am I bitter? Not really. If anything, I’m just happy to have fought side by side with my friend and World Champ CSB, to have stood toe to toe with the MWE legend DC, and to see the rebirth of the old Zak Warner. Who has stood toe to toe with some of the best in MWE and come out on top…me. And who is everyone talking about…ME! We’d finally come to the big one. I turn back around and look back at the spot on the mat where Oblivion’s faded blood still stains the ring. They never were able to get it out completely. I allow myself one moment of grief and guilt to take me, as the shards of a tear form in my eye. But only for a moment. Then…it’s gone. Replaced by hard determination, and an equal hatred of my own. So now, everyone knows the full story. No more secrets, no more mysteries, and no more forgiveness. I’ll admit, at Turmoil, I still had my reservations about fighting you. But do confuse that with fear. Oh no, not anymore. It was more out of sympathy. Sympathy and sadness over what the two of us lost together and what we could have been together. But apparently, that wasn’t meant to be. We were built up just so we could fall. And you know what…I’m just fine with that. Because after reviewing everything, I now know what kind of man you really were back then. What you were in the past, THAT was your real mask. This mask we see today isn’t a mask at all. It’s your true face. A face of anger, bitterness, greed, contempt, and all those other nasty words rolled up into one violent package. One little mistake is all it took for you to destroy everything. And yes, it was you who destroyed our relationship, not me. All it would have taken for this to end before it began was a simple “I forgive you” or something. But I guess that was too much to ask. So yes, I had my reservations. But that was Turmoil, and this is Wrestle Wars. And come WW, there won’t be any need for reservations. I beat you once, and I’ll do it again. Not only that, but I plan on removing your mask myself. I think it’s high time you showed the world who you really are and stopped hiding behind this facade of yours. And it’s a face you all know very well MWE. Now I could just tell you right now, but where’s the suspense in that? Besides, it’ll be a lot more fun to beat him, then rip that mask off his limp body. And then after that, just maybe, this whole thing will be over and done with. My heart is racing now at like 1,000 beats per minute. Ok, maybe I’m exaggerating things a bit, but you get my point right? But there is a sorta adrenaline you get from anger. It’s a release really. A release of our most hidden emotion, now free of the boundaries we place upon it. Even at the risk of sounding like an old Star War fanboy, hate and anger are powerful and useful weapons. But only in the hands of those who know how to use them properly. I know how to use them. Scotty and Oblivion…not so much. That’s been you’re whole problem from the start. You keep bringing up the past, and what wrong I did you and all this other drama. Yeah, I did wrong, but you’re missing the point. What happened back then doesn’t matter anymore. The only thing that matters is the now. I realize that now. That’s why you didn’t beat me at Turmoil. You were so busy with your back toward me, looking to the past that all I had to do was step up behind up and whack you upside the head. You keep concentrating on stuff that’s insignificant at this point in the game. Because no matter how much you hurt me, no matter how many bones of mine you may break, no matter even if you take this belt away from me (which I promise won’t happen anyway), it won’t change what happened. And until you realize that, you’ll never win this little feud of ours. So it’s in your best interest to just let it go. I could go on forever, but the time for words is over. And now, it’s time to bring this symphony to a close. I look down one final time at the blood stained mat. And it has no more power over me. Lifting my foot high, I stomp down hard and crush the stain underneath my boot and grind the power of the memory away into bitter dust. And as I take my foot away, I can almost swear that the last bits of the faded blood has been wiped clean from the mat. But in the end, one of these men has to become the new X-TV Champion, and chosen by me no less…if I win of course…which I will. It’s basically a lesser of two evils choice. Or, I can pick Oblivion, and basically reward this sick freak for making my professional and personal life a living hell for the past few months. Not an easy choice to say the least. So who will I pick to succeed me? Only I know for sure. But I promise you this. Scotty McKnight will finally get a foot up his jaw, which will hopefully shut him up for good. And Oblivion…you say the truth is oblivion? You don’t even know what the oblivion really is. At Wrestle Wars…I’ll show you. Apparently, after being in the dark for so long, my eyes are somewhat dilated. But soon, my eyes begin to readjust to the light. Because no matter how thick the darkness, it will always be pierced by the light. On December 6th, I go to war against the darkness. Bring it the fuck on.
