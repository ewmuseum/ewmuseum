MWE 24/7 — 08-09 PPV PR Archives

Bring Your 'A' Game

Dec 18 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
An MWE Camera begins to fade out of the black and bland image it is currently set in. It takes time for this to do so but it finally does, leaving it with a picture perfect, clear image. It seems to be based in the wrestling arena where Adrenaline Rush will take place later that night. The camera does a screenshot around the arena. All is dull and dark, the arena is empty and the silence is near overwhelming. All of a sudden "Adrenaline" by 12 Stones, which is the official theme song of Adrenaline Rush begins to play very quietly around the arena. A faded light gradually shines over the centre of the wrestling ring as the camera pans down. A figure is standing.. That figure appears to be newly crowned MWE Intercontinental Champion Sonny Williams. He is wearing a tight black shirt with the Adrenaline Rush logo printed on the front, with blue long jeans and stylish shoes. The Intercontinental Championship is resting on his shoulder as he looks straight into the camera as the camera zooms back to see amongst Sonny in the ring, two tall ladders set up and standing, a few tables scattered across the canvas floor and steel chairs absolutely everywhere possible. Sonny looks into the camera still, about to open his mouth and let out his words and thoughts. "Can you feel it? .. Not the beat of the music but the anticipation. Can you feel the excitement.. The fear.. The anxiety.. The rush. This is what adrenaline defines. The excitement of new prosperities, the fear of the match concepts, the anxiety of the wait and the rush, to win. To win is to be successful, and to be successful is to be a champion. But being a champion just doesn't mean or make you successful instantly.. There's a certain way to making it that far. You need to defend your title with respect, you need to face each and every sole challenge with dignity and you can NEVER, give up or give in.. It doesn't matter whether you're a week-long champion or a decade-long holder of the title, as long as you do each and everything I mentioned, you're at least doing something right.. But lets, lets leave title talk for later." Sonny looks into the camera fiercely but with concentration with the words that are coming out of his mouth. He reshuffles his MWE Intercontinental Championship resting on his left shoulder as he looks behind him and walks to one of the many steel chairs scattered amongst the ring. He picks it up by his spare hand and shoulder and firmly opens it and places it flat on the canvas floor in a seat position. Sonny sits down abruptly as he looks straight into the camera recognising the environment surrounding him. "T..L..C.. Most people think these three letters stand for Tables, Ladders and Chairs. But not me.. Not Sonny Williams. You see to me, those three letters stand for something completely different, something much more chaotic, something that causes the addition of brutality, it causes something well known around here known as mayhem.. But to Sonny Williams, to me, TLC stands for Torture.. It stands for Labour.. And Crucifiction." Sonny swiftly stands up from the steel chair he was sitting on as he turns to one of the tables set up in the ring. He looks into the camera as he puts his hand on the table and feels up and down it, moving his hand slowly on it. "These tables were designed for men to be slammed straight into.. These ladders were designed for men to jump an illustrious leap of fate onto their opponent to result in pain, and to result in agony.. These chairs, well they're pretty self-explanatory, you whack the other guy in the head with it. But all in all, these items, these objects, these WEAPONS, were invented to cause pain on the human body." "This is an environment that was created, that was designed, to punish the bone structure of the human body. Blood is supposed to fall, sweat is supposed to burn and chaos is absolutely guaranteed. People think chaos is a bad thing.. Well it's supposed to be, for most people. But when you put chaos and Sonny Williams together, there's bound to be some sort of path of destruction.. There's bound to be some sort of momentum, and fury. Because that's one thing about me, like having a 400 pound man run 100 miles at you, you can't stop me when those two paths become one." Sonny re-adjusts his MWE Intercontinental Championship which is still sitting on his left shoulder as he slowly walks away from the table he had his hand on as he walks towards one of the two ladders set up in the ring. He rotates his hand on one of the ladder platforms as he slowly walks up each platform, to finally sit at the top. He re-adjusts his MWE Intercontinental Championship once again as he looks into the camera with that same look of determination he had before. "But there's more to the person people call Sonny Williams than what meets the eye.. I'm determined, I'm brutal.. I motivate myself to higher lengths.. I AM a man of my own. The voice of authority, the articles in magazines, all of that, it didn't create the man you see in front of you right this second, no.. NO. I didn't come into this business to be the next DC, or the next David Whitman.. I came to be the man I've learnt to live as over my lifetime so far.. I came into this business to share my dream, to share my life's story, with the millions of viewers across the globe." "This is WHO I am.. This is WHERE I am meant to be.. I'm supposed to be at the top of the ladder, and I am. I am to who I am, and unlike one of my opponents.. I don't need to compare myself with a movie character, a former wrestler, a past mentor, a man, A LEGEND.. Because what you see with Sonny Williams, is exactly what you get. I don't compare myself with anyone else, because there is no one, and I repeat no one who can compare themselves with me." Sonny rubs his chin for a moment or two as he turns to the side, away from the camera, with a smirk on his face. He turns back to the camera with his head down and his hand still rubbing on his chin as he suddenly stops and looks back up at the camera with a more determined and anger expression on his face than before. "Who can tell me they lived on the streets for 5 years!? 5 YEARS! Does anyone else know what it's like to be rejected from the homeless shelter on a night where rain is pouring down, sudden lightening bolts are connecting and where the loud bangs make you feel like this world, is the real Hell? Who can tell me they saw their only love one, THEIR ONLY BROTHER, die in front of them!?.. NO ONE CAN. So you see I don't compare myself to be any different than what I am.. I'm not like a man named Vega Knight." Sonny pushes himself off the top platform on the ladder as he steps down one platform and jumps off onto the canvas floor of the ring on his two feet. The ring shakes a bit as Sonny re-adjusts his MWE Intercontinental Championship as he looks straight into the camera again. You can see veins in his neck indicating his frustration and emotions from bringing up what he had just said. "Vega Knight is the same man who told me, who told the entire face of this universe that he'd beat me at Friday Night Genesis and that he'd kick my ass into the next millennium. This is the same man who told me I was a has-been, and who told me that I no longer had what it takes to take me places in this business... But do you want to know what I told him? I told him to bring his pencils, to bring his books, to bring his rulers because I was taking him back to school." "I turned my style and went old-school.. I resorted to my old tactics, the same tactics that helped me win titles, win over people I once broke relationships with, and win and put my life back, back on track.. But that wasn't all my idea at first. You see the same man, I defeated gave me that same word of advice to bring back the tricks, and wipe off the dust. He called out the man deep down inside of me, and thought, he, HE could beat that man.. And you know what, I did exactly what he asked and brought that man out to play." Sonny continues to look into the camera with now a more steady and calm expression in his facial features than before. He looks down at the MWE Intercontinental Championship and puts his right hand and rubs the gold plate with his name printed in big bold letters. He re-adjusts the title and looks back into the camera again. "I made the change, I decided to take the chance and the rest goes down as history. I know it, you know it, the MWE Roster knows it, the little African without a TV knows it.. the WHOLE WORLD knows it, that I am now officially the new MWE Intercontinental Champion, and I did it the old fashion way, I wasn't handed glory.. I worked for it and at the end of the day I did what I had intended to do and that was to beat Vega Knight." "But the thing is, I had intended for more of a challenge, more of a struggling battle between two men fighting it out for a prize of absolute glory. But Vega, Vega didn't give me that, he backed my words up and proved he didn't have any respect for the belt.. He proved to the world that he's not at a stage in his life to compete against me or to compete in this division as a whole. And after all the words he told me, ALL those insults and sly comments it only dug him a bigger hole." "He proved to me that he's just another wannabe whose bark is bigger than his bite. And I can't respect a man who can say he's gonna do this, or that he's gonna do that because when the day arrives he doesn't exceed to his words of wisdom, or to his expectations. You see, unlike Vega I've learnt a thing or two over my wrestling career.. I've learnt that you can do everything you say, only, ONLY if you put your mind to it. So whether Vega listens to this or not I ask him a question, I ask him one sole question which will answer my nightly ponders.." Sonny smirks to himself as he once again re-adjusts his MWE Intercontinental Championship which is still resting on his shoulder. He scratches his head and looks into the camera, about to continue with what he was previously stating. "Vega, tonight, when you tell the entire world that YOU are going to beat ME, just promise me this.. Promise me that you'll walk the walk because I want to.. No.. I NEED to see you beat me. I want you to promise to me that you're going to beat the same man who came from rags to riches and became one of the most inspirational, determined and vigorous superstars MWE has ever seen. So Vega, tell me you're gonna beat me but more importantly.. Put your mind to it, and beat me." Sonny slowly walks back over to the steel chair he was once sitting on and sits in it again abruptly. When sit he re-adjusts his MWE Intercontinental Championship once again and continues to look into the camera. "But Vega, Vega isn't the only human being I need to express concern about later tonight. You see, this match, this battleground, this JUNGLE of infurity and destruction is not just your ordinary one-on-one fight. No, you see there's another man who has elevatored himself into this match, to add-on to the chaotic environment. This man, this superstar.. He's aggressive, he's strong, he's mobile, and he's determined to prove a point unlike Vega could prove a point to me. This man, is otherwise known as.. Derek Wellings." "Now before I hear this guy spilling claims and shouting insults right into my ears, I think I should hold a history lesson so he learns a bit more about the man who has just broke out of my skin and seen the world surrounding him, the same man who he is gonna have to try and beat to ever be called the MWE Intercontinental Champion. Derek.. I've held many titles, I've wrestled many future legends, I've beat some and lost to others.. I've came from living in disgust to living in almost royalty. I don't like bragging but when the time comes to face the facts, and to face my face you should consider researching my reputation.. Because you wouldn't mess with a man holding a gun.. So I'd advise you didn't mess with me, that you don't mess with Sonny Williams." Sonny gets up from the steel chair suddenly and turns around and heads towards the ring ropes. He puts his leg through the middle rope and lowers his head to get through the ropes. He stands on the apron and jumps onto the mats surrounding the wrestling ring. The camera follows Sonny's progress as he re-adjusts the MWE Intercontinental Championship which is still on his left shoulder. Sonny lowers his body stance and puts his head under the ring looking for weapons. Suddenly, Sonny's head pops out with a sledge hammer in his right hand as he looks into the camera again with that same determined expression he had before. "You may think that if you've got a sledge hammer in the gripes of your fingertips that I won't mess with you either.. But, that's not how I roll, that's not the Sonny Williams way.. That's the cowardly way, a road I choose not to pass through. I'd break my neck to keep my chin up than be a coward. But that's not what I'm trying to get at.. My point is I'm not gonna give up or give in.. I'm gonna win, or at least do my best to get that far.. This title, it needs to be held by someone who knows how to hold Singles gold.. It needs it's prestige, it needs to be treated correctly." "I'm sick of losing, I'm sick of seeing titles that were once known as prestigious being thrown around like a children's toy, I'm sick of the same old, same old in MWE. That's why this change came, this is why I am going to make a success of myself and show people, show doubters that I'm a true champion, whether I'm holding MWE gold or not. I've fought through enough in life's journey, so when I see guys getting title shots from every angle for no reason, much alike Derek, it angers me, because I was brought up in a time where we had to earn everything.. So much has changed, but so little has helped." Sonny drops the sledge hammer on the mat and slides under the bottom rope, and back into the ring. He re-adjusts his MWE Intercontinental Championship when standing and shoves away the steel chair that was set up in the middle of the ring as he looks into the camera continuously. "So tonight, it's Man vs. Man vs. Man.. It's Warrior vs. Warrior vs. Warrior.. It's Solider vs. Solider vs. Solider.. And it's Vanilla vs. Vanilla vs. Chocolate. I am that man, I am that warrior, I am that solider and I am that sole chocolate.. That's what makes me the odd one out, the odds are stacked against me.. All my life I have been the underdog, and all my life I have been defying the odds and breaking the barriers. Tonight is no different, as I will prove to the world that I'm back, that I'm a new man and that I am the MWE Intercontinental Champion and that I will defy the odds!" "So better yet, I won't end with my usual 'Cha-Ching' but I will end on this.. Derek.. Vega, forget about confidence.. Acceptance and perseverance.. Forget about how your going to win or who you're gonna pin because tonight I don't want anything but a challenge to serve me right.. So please I ask you bring two things with you tonight.. I'm not talking about your boots, or your luck, or even your finishers.. But better yet bring what hangs between your legs.. Bring your balls because I swear on the grave of my brother that you're going to need them. Man up to your words, and do what you can to beat me because it's what I need, it's what I thrive and crave on." Sonny moves closer to the camera with that same look of determination and anger from the words he is speaking. His veins in his neck are seen to be twitching as he continues to stare down into the camera with the arena still empty and dark. "And if you choose to ignore my words, when all is said and done, and I'm standing here with my hand held high be sure to.. Remember.. The.. Name." Sonny makes a stern look into the camera as he suddenly turns and walks away from the camera. The camera turns to the arena where all is still bland and dark. The camera begins to fade into a visible black as the promo finally ends.
