Like Spending X-mas in a Homeless Shelter

Sep 20 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
An MWE Camera is seen to fadely walk into a hospital shown in small timely screenshots. The camera itself is in a hazed setting to acclimatise to the setting of a hospital. The camera is still in a black fade as it begins to become clearer and clearer. The camera enters a room to see a sleeping figure in a hospital bed with wounds on his head. It is the newly crowned MWE World Champion, Sonny Williams. He is sleeping in a hospital bed, still unconscious from the brutal attack that Jekyl gave him after his match at Turmoil. He has wounds on his head from the brutality of the attack and is being monitored well by the nurses. We see the MWE World Championship next to Sonny's bed in a well furnished case to keep it in tip top condition. All of a sudden Sonny begins to opens his eyes as he squints from the brightness of the light above his bed. A nurse quickly walks in to see Sonny awake and smiles to Sonny to see him well before leaving the room. Sonny looks shocked to where he is. Sonny Williams: Where am I? What the hell am I doing in a gown in a hospital bed? Sonny tries to get out of bed but brings his back up but suddenly begins to hold his abdomen in absolute pain. A nurse rushes into the room and gets Sonny to lie back down his hospital bed. It takes time for his arrogance to acknowledge that but he finally does as the nurse says. He lies back down as she goes to his bed noteboard to read what is wrong with him. She raises her eyebrows. Nurse: Mr.Williams, it appears you were brutally attacked after your match last night at Turmoil by Jekyl. You have 3 stiches in your forehead, 5 in your arm and are tending to 2 broken ribs. I advise you lie down and rest in here before you even think of exciting. A look of shock comes across Sonny's face. Sonny Williams: Did, did I win last night? The nurse smiles and moves to the side as she is standing in front of the MWE World Championship. Sonny sees it and looks at it blinkless for a few moments. He makes an evil smirk to himself. Nurse: You did, congratulations Mr.Williams. The nurse leaves the room as Sonny slightly gets up in a slow motion and picks up the MWE World Championship from it's case. He looks at it with a huge gaping evil smirk on his face as he can see the reflection of his face in the championship. He looks at it more and reads the golden plate on it which reads, 'Sonny Williams'. He takes it to his hospital bed and takes his time getting back into bed. He is sitting up in his bed with the MWE World Championship resting on his shoulder. He notices the camera is in the room and has an evil smirk on his face. Sonny Williams: So here I am, I said I would win, I told absolutely everyone around the globe that I had a feeling, a feeling of luck and certainty. I had never felt so confident before a match until Turmoil, I just knew that it would be me walking, or in my case being carted out of Turmoil as the new MWE World Champion. So many people doubted me, and yet again I prove them all wrong with a historic win in the world of Mayhem. Sure, I can't remember any bit of the match but I do remember the build up to it. I remember all the hype, the bookies and everything. I went into that event as the least likely to come out as the new MWE World Champion. I proved them wrong and I will continue to do so. Any face chucked in front of me will see the same Sonny, they will see the true MWE World Champion. Sonny Williams: It's no longer me running rampage trying to claim something that a lifetimes work has gone into, I have done it and done it for the second time. I may be battered and bruised, but I am over the moon. I did exactly what I said I would, I said I would do this for me and I have. Right now, I don't care whose looking up or down on me, because from now on I don't care about anyone but me, your new MWE World Champion. Sonny looks down at his MWE World Championship with the same evil smirk on his face. He re-adjusts his title before looking back into the camera. Sonny Williams: Now, even though just mere minutes ago I woke up from being unconscious, I've got other issues to deal with, something more serious. That issue is Jekyl. Jekyl, you are one insane motherfucker with the most screwed up lifestyle I have, EVER, seen. I beat you, and even though I can't remember how I did it, but then again we're talking about me, so I am sure I did it in style also. It seems you have a problem with me being crowned the new World Champion, well to me if you got an issue I can offer you a tissue and if that's not good enough I can give you something else, maybe something along the lines of a beat down like you gave me. I am not out partying tonight for my win, and it's all because of you and your tantrom. This is like spending Christmas in a Homeless Shelter. Sonny Williams: I proved that you are nothing to me, that I can beat you, David Whitman and any other man on the MWE roster. I've gone through good and bad situations, I know how to capitalise the moment. I am here at a place in my life I have been waiting years for. You and everything that surrounds you is something I can't stand. I want you in the ring, I want to see next month at Wrestle Wars that it be Sonny Williams vs. Jekyl for the MWE World Championship because I can guarantee that these ribs will heal, these stiches will fade, but nothing will ever be able to make you forget the night that I make you of embarrassment. Sonny rubs his forehead and finds where the stitches are using his hand. He brings his hand down and rubs his chin then looking down on his MWE World Championship. Sonny Williams: You know Jekyl, you may feel special that I am talking about you rather than the rest of the MWE locker, because now as I am the World Champion I got everyone running after me. I don't need to care about you Jekyl as much as some other guys. I have beaten you, what makes you think I can't do it again. I'm gonna break it down between you and me in a language you might understand. You see I do freestyles, but you have never been around that type of environment to understand it. A poem maybe? My life continues to follow, As I pay the ultimate price. I am now the World Champion, But I remain rolling the dice. I've done something spectacular, Which for once is a change. I've completed a hard mission, And it took a long range. Jekyl is not a human, He is what I call superficial. His skills are of a low standard, But that's already official. Now I wait for a challenge, Will one happen to appear? That is the grand question, Someone the crowd will cheer? I'm pulling out a Kanto, Something pretty weird. He may be a freak, But I am more feared. To you Jekyl I leave on this, You are nothing to me, I am the Main Man, Of the MWE! Sonny has an evil smirk on his face as he continues to look down from his hospital bed at the camera. He puts his hand on his title and gently rubs it. He smirks a tad more. All of a sudden, a nurse comes rushing into the room with a cell phone in her hand. Sonny turns and looks confused at her arrival. Nurse: It's for you. I'm not 100% sure, but it sounds a lot like your boss, Richard Covell. Sonny Williams: .. Okay. The nurse hands Sonny the cell phone as Sonny slowly brings it to his ear. He looks a bit confused as it is odd to be called by his boss like this. The nurse turns away and leaves the room as she closes the door shut on her way out. The camera audio comes in to the phone so we can hear the conversation. Sonny Williams: Hello? Richard Covell: Ahh, Mr. World Champion. How are you? No, don't answer that because I'm almost certain you are over the moon, but at the same time looking for revenge. Well, first of all I congratulate you on your victory. It was tight, but you came out as the man. You now know as the World Champion you partly hold this industry on your shoulders. Sonny Williams: Yeah, yeah, yeah. If you're going to give me the big 'you gotta do us proud' lecture, I've heard it millions of times. I know that the federation is only as good as it's World Champion. Since I have won now we've taken a ultimate step from good, to great. I'm going to take this industry to a bigger and better place. You said yourself before, you were proud to have David Whitman as a champion, you should be prouder that I am now. Richard Covell: Well it seems you understand that, which is good. Now I didn't call you just to praise on your victory, maybe you'd remember that I am the father of the son you brutally attacked. Brian Michaels ring a bell? Sonny smirks. Sonny Williams: Ahh, yes. I've had some good and bad memories in my lifetime, but I gotta say that the attack on Michaels probably would rate somewhere in the good section. Richard Covell: Listen here asshole, you may be my biggest worker in this industry now but that still doesn't give you the right to speak to me in that manner. Have you forgotten that I am the Owner of MWE. I can stripe you of that MWE World Championship title any moment. Sonny Williams: Jeez, calm down. Richard Covell takes a deep breath which is heard through the phone. Richard Covell: That better be the last of that. Anyway, I know you're in a bad condition but your the World Champ and I need you on my show and you will be, because you have a match this week. Sonny Williams: Are you kidding me!? Richard Covell: No, I don't kid kiddo. You have a non-title match this week and it's against a man I think you're very known with. Sonny Williams: God damn it, hurry up and spill the beans. Richard Covell: Alejandro Rodriguez. Good day and good luck. Sonny brings the cell phone away from his ear as he looks on into the distance. He looks pissed off and a bit shocked at what Richard Covell said. He flips the cell phone over and throws it as hard as he can at the wall. It breaks into pieces as Sonny starts clutching his ribs in pain. He re-adjusts his title and looks back into the camera. Sonny Williams: So it seems it is me and Alejandro Rodriguez once again. We've had a lot of fierce battles over the past year. Some people have even said that they are some of the most even battles they have seen in recent time. Those people, well they're insane. Why you might ask? Well if you look at the record between myself and Alejandro you will know that I come out, far superior. Now just for stats, and parshly bragging rights, I am 4-0 in wins against Alejandro Rodriguez. BUT, we have had one draw. Something I should mention or he will have something along the lines of a bitch. Sonny Williams: Now I am coming out of a big match, a match that ended with even more brutality. What did Alejandro come out of? Nothing but a freak with face paint. You could even say that he has the advantage over me. But am I going to back down is what the million dollar question is? No, I am Sonny Williams. Sure I might have a few broken ribs, a few stitches but that's not going to keep me out. As much as a week off sounds so good seeing what I had to go through, I'm down with a match. Sonny rubs his chin. Sonny Williams: My opponent, he gives nothing to me of intimidation. He's small, he's annoying, he is everything I am not. I may have not won on Monday Night Mayhem before but I have stepped up a platform on the ladder of success. I just won the match of my life, I didn't do it for anyone but me and I am going to hold this title like a little girl holds her doll. I'm pretty sure Alejandro held his little rag doll well as a child so that must make him a possible contender for this title. Sonny Williams: Alejandro, I won't back down, I will never give in and you better believe it from past experiences. You and I have a long history dating way, way back. You know who is the better man, and now all this match matters is for you to get some needed dignity. I may be in a hospital bed while you be out partying the streets of your new called home of America, but let me make this clear everything said before this match, this main event, not one word will matter when we lock arms. People laugh when I say I hate the build up to matches, they say that I am one of few who seem to look like I enjoy it. I don't. I was built on wrestling, not promos, not trying to make the most hype there can be. Sonny smirks down the camera with his arm tightly holding the MWE World Championship. Sonny Williams: So Alejandro, I want you to know loud and clear that this match isn't about me. It's about you. It's to see whether or not you got the balls to step up to the plate and take it by two hands and test the best. When I say the best, I mean me because right now, I am the man.. And like a legend said "To be the man, you gotta beat the man". Alejandro, it's time for you to be a man and grow up, like you never did. All the pressure is on you, can you handle it? Sonny Williams gets out of his bed and kneels on the bed as he has the MWE World Championship on his shoulder. The camera zooms in as Sonny tries to hesitate pain. Sonny Williams: CHA .. AHH! Sonny clutches his ribs. Sonny Williams: CHING! The camera begins to fade out with last pictures of Sonny Williams with the MWE World Championship on his shoulder and clutching at his ribs in absolute pain from the attack at Turmoil. The camera fades as the promo ends. OOC: Feedback appreciated big time.
