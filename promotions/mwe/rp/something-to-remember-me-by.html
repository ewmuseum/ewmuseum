Something To Remember Me By...; This IS for you Tim...

Aug 23 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hermosillo, Sonoran Desert, Mexico 6 years ago. Left foot, right foot, left foot, right foot… Collapse. The scene opens in a desert somewhere in Mexico, the sand fills the whole scene but for the sky which shines blue, with the occasional mountain or outline of a cactus in the distance. A man, a young adult infact, is lying face down in the sand. He is breathing heavily and is exhausted, he is carrying a plastic carton, which once was full of water, now empty. With great effort, the man turns around onto his back and sweeps sand from his face. The man’s name is Alejandro Rodriguez. He struggled to get to his feet, he was so tired, but he remembered why he was here, to live the American dream, to live the life his mother so bravely gave hers for. He couldn’t stop here, he had some 154 miles to Nogales, the border of Arizona he was planning to cross. So he carried on walking, he dropped the carton, it seemed useless to carry it now, he had very little hope of finding any water. Left foot, right foot, left foot, right foot… Collapse. This time, Alejandro didn’t get up. Chicago Illinois, United Center, USA Present Day Are you FUCKING KIDDING me? The scene opens inside Chicago’s own United Center. The camera pans around the arena, which is currently being constructed as the stage of Crowning Glory III, the biggest event in the MWE Calendar. Two men are stood behind a load of workers constructing a ring in the centre of the arena. The camera is not far away from these men, and it focuses on them, the two men are Alejandro Rodriguez, and Darrel Jones. Alejandro is wearing a black mask as per usual, with a white Crowning Glory III vest featuring him and a picture of David Whitman on opposite sides of the shirt along with a pair of dark jeans, he seems angered about something and beside him is Darrel Jones, Alejandro‘s friend and manager, he is wearing a smart black shirt, jeans, blazer and white trainers. Darrel: Good, isn’t it? Alejandro gives Darrel a shocked look and he holds his arms aloft at the arena. Alejandro: No, this is not good! This is the biggest pile of wank I’ve ever seen! Darrel: Al, the Chicago Bulls play here. Alejandro: I don’t give a fuck about Chicago Bulls. This is Chicago’s bull-SHIT stadium. This seats… what, 22,000? I might as well have retired in a fucking Bingo Hall! Darrel: Come on Al, many greats have performed here. Alejandro: Bite me… Darrel begins counting them off his fingers. Darrel: Madonna, Green Day, Bruce Springstein, Kanye West, The Eagles… Alejandro looks puzzled. Alejandro: The who? Darrel: Yep, The Who… Um… who else? Bon Jovi, Nickleba- Alejandro cuts across him, by actually physically shaking Darrel’s shoulders. Alejandro: Alright, I get it, some legends have played here… Has an icon retired here? No… Lets get out of this dump. Alejandro and Darrel turn around and walk up towards the end of the ramp, walking away from the ring and the arena as the scene fades out. Hermosillo 6 years ago. Despierta, amigo! (Wake up, friend!) The scene pans across the desert once more, and Alejandro opens his eyes. Stood over him is a tall Mexican man, wearing a ripped blue shirt and trousers that look more like rags. Exhausted, Alejandro allows himself to be pulled up to his feet by this man. The man hands Alejandro a carton of water. Usted necesita beber… Tome todo lo que quieras. (You need to drink… Take as much as you like.) Alejandro takes the water in his hands, he tries to drink but his hands are shaking so much that some water spills over the top. The man steady’s Alejandro’s hands with his, before raising the carton to Alejandro‘s mouth. Tranquilo, amigo. (Easy, friend.) Alejandro drinks all of the water from the carton. He holds the carton over his head as the last drop falls in his mouth. Alejandro leans on the man, still exhausted. The man chuckles, takes Alejandro’s weight and begins walking with him. Que nunca han llegado a la frontera. Ven conmigo, un grupo de nosotros se va en un camión. (You would have never made it to the border. Come with me, a group of us are going in a truck.) The man walks on and Alejandro see’s the truck about 50 yards away. A group of Mexican’s get out from the back to assist the man, they lift Alejandro into the back of the truck and into the shade. Alejandro takes another look around at the people around him, and he passes out again. Chicago Present Day. What are we doing here? I need a drink… I’ll tell you what you need to do… The camera shows Alejandro and Darrel walking into a bar, Alejandro walks up to the bar, with Darrel walking behind him babbling about all the things he needs to do. Alejandro sits on a barstool, and Darrel does the same while still talking. Darrel: …and there’s the press conference, and you need to cut a promo too, an- Alejandro ignores him and talks to the barman, a grubby looking man, who is wiping glasses with a rag. Alejandro: Two shots of tequila, when you’re ready. The barman walks off to fetch the drinks, and Darrel keeps on talking. Darrel: -eed to talk to the committee and ready your Hall of Fame speech, and when you’ve don- Alejandro: Give it a rest Darrel, I’ve been working all week on all that crap. Darrel: But you need to- Alejandro: What I need to do is have a drink, before I go insane. I will shoot a promo later today, ok? Darrel: Under the influence of alcohol? The barman returns with the tequila, Darrel eyes it nervously while Alejandro pays. Alejandro: Darrel, the Mexicans invented tequila. I may be a lightweight in the ring, but not in the bar, remember that. I’m sure I can handle a few shots. Darrel: Well, I don’t want one. Alejandro: Good, they’re both mine anyway. Alejandro drains one shot, then looks at Darrel. Alejandro: So, what’s your problem today? Darrel: I don’t have a problem Alejandro, just a shit-load of things to get done. Alejandro: When was the last time you had sex Darrel? Alejandro looks seriously at Darrel and Darrel looks away. Darrel: I don’t see how that’s any of your business… Alejandro: Well you’re just so damn uptight lately, you need to get some… release. Darrel: Well excuse me, I just have to spend my entire day juggling with doing the work I need to do and keeping you in line so you don’t forget every damn thing you have to do, so excuse me if I don’t have any time for “release”… Alejandro shrugs and downs his last shot, he thanks the barman and gets up to his feet. Alejandro: C’mon, Mr. Manager, we got a promo to shoot… The scene fades out as they walk out the door and into the street. Caborca, 1 Mile from Nogales 6 years ago. Es despierto, le da cierto margen.. (He’s awake, give him some room…) Alejandro opens his eyes and finds himself surrounded by people in the back of the truck. The one who rescued him is leant over him, with a look of concern on his face. All around him, there are Mexican people, all of whom are with their families, all closely embraced as if someone might be lost in an instant. The rescuer speaks to Alejandro. ¿Cómo te sientes? (How are you feeling?) Alejandro sits up and the people around him are surprised by how young he is. Alejandro takes another look around at everyone, the truck is moving, and it begins to rattle around a bit, the truck is cramped, and you can‘t see the floor of the truck as there is too many people and too many things. Alejandro: Hambriento… (Hungy…) The rescuer reaches to the back of the truck a pulls a little bread and gives it to Alejandro, who eats it immediately. The rescuer looks at Alejandro and sighs. Es todo lo que tenemos me temo, pero vamos a estar en la frontera pronto. (It’s all we have I’m afraid, but we’ll be at the border soon.) Alejandro: Gracias. (Thank you.) Angél: Mi nombre es Angél, ¿cuál es tu nombre? (My name is Angél, what is your name?) Alejandro: Alejandro Rodriguez. Angél: Donde es su familia, Alejandro? (Where is your family, Alejandro?) Alejandro bows his head down and he wipes his face with his hand. Alejandro: Muerto. (Dead.) There are several gasps of horror around the truck. Angél puts a hand on Alejandro’s shoulder. A tear falls down Alejandro’s face, he wipes it away. Alejandro: Angél, el ángel… (Angél, the angel…) Angél chuckles and ruffles Alejandro’s hair. Angél: Tú eres el ángel, mi joven amigo. (You are the Angel, my young friend.) Alejandro dwells on these words for a moment as Angél gets up and walks towards the driver. Angél returns and gathers the people’s attention as the truck comes to a halt. Alejandro looks round, the people look scared, the families draw closer to each other and the loners look determined, and begin collecting their things as Angél speaks. Angél: Escuchar a todos, nos llega ahora en la frontera, la policía ha sido alertada, que llegará pronto, y pueden ser armados, por lo que tiene que ejecutar, y no debe dejar de publicarse hasta que son seguros. Familias, debe separar, no mira para matar a los niños, y no van a disparar si alejarse. No tenemos combustible para regresar, y aunque hemos tenido, no tenemos los suministros para mantenernos con vida hasta entonces ... No tienes elección, buena suerte. (Everyone listen up, we are arriving at the border now, the police have been alerted, they will arrive soon, and they may be armed, so you must run, and you must not stop running until you are safe. Families, you should separate, they will not look to kill the children, and they will not shoot if they get away. We have no fuel to turn back, and even if we had, we have no supplies to keep us alive until then… You have no choice, good luck.) Terror rings through the cabin as Angél draws close to Alejandro, pulling some things out of a sack. Angél: Alejandro Ponte esto, no ver su cara. Escúchame, que se ejecutará, y no parar, estamos rodeados de árboles del que se ocupará de planificar emboscada a nosotros, tratar de ocultar al verlos, y cuando llegue la oportunidad de correr. ¿Me entiendes? (Put this on, Alejandro, they will not see your face. You listen to me, you will run, and you will not stop, we are surrounded by tree’s, they will plan to ambush us, try and hide when you see them, and when you get the chance, run. You understand me?) Alejandro: Si. Angél hands Alejandro a wrestling mask, and Alejandro puts it on, hiding his face. The back of the truck opens and the refugee’s run out the back, it was a mad rush, as the parents push their children to their children to the front. Alejandro, being small and agile, managed to slip through the panic-stricken families. He ran for his life, anywhere away from the truck, he headed right, what he supposed was the east, the others were not far behind, but they couldn’t keep up with him. Alejandro heard the distant sound of police sirens, but he kept on running, hopping over logs, dodging branches, never stopping. The sirens’ noises didn’t deter him, but the noise of the bullet did. Alejandro stopped in his tracks, and turned around, 200 yards behind him, the police had surrounded the refugee’s, and in the struggle only one had been shot, only one who tried to protect them all, Angél. And then the tears began to fall, and Alejandro struggled to stop them coming as he kept running. Two now, two dead in the attempt to save him, two dead in the attempt to give him a better life. The thought almost overwhelmed Alejandro, but he had to keep running. Left foot… Right foot… Left foot… Right foot… Chicago Present Day Crowning Glory III, the greatest spectacle in the history of the MWE. The scene opens up on set of Alejandro’s promo. He is stood in front of a huge Crowning Glory poster featuring himself and David Whitman, and just visible is a D.C. & Joseph Sharpman one to the left side and a Sinc Mercier & Grand Mystique one to the right. Alejandro is wearing different clothes, a white blank tank top over the top of his ring attire, which consists of a black mask and matching pants. Alejandro: Crowning Glory, where it all started. Where it started for David Whitman, myself, and others known to be later known as the MWE Originals, including Brian Michaels, D.C. and Sonny Williams, legends in their own right. It is at Crowning Glory, where on the past two occasions, the entire roster was given the chance to win the biggest prize there is, the MWE Championship, a chance which I almost achieved. The first Crowning Glory, the tensions were high, and you could feel the anxiety in the air. That was for most people… me? I was excited, I went out there and performed my very best, but it wasn’t good enough. I eliminated more than anyone else did that night, four men, but I was thwarted by David Whitman. The second was no different, it was the restart of the MWE, a night I looked forward to like no other. I was determined to do it that time, but it wasn’t to be, again, it was down to me and Whitman again…. And again, he came out victorious and with the MWE World Title. Alejandro walks along the wall to focus on the Sinc Mercier & Grand Mystique poster where Alejandro points out the MWE Title, which is prominently featured in the middle. Alejandro walks back to his poster. Alejandro: And where it started for myself and David Whitman, it now ends for us, our MWE Careers end for us. Two careers that have intertwined throughout MWE History, two careers that have captured every piece of MWE Gold between them, two careers that together have held the MWE Championship five times. It seems only fitting at the biggest event in MWE’s prestigious history, that the biggest and the most prestigious superstar will retire… And I’m not talking about myself… No, I am talking about David Whitman. The man is without a shadow of a doubt the finest superstar in the MWE. He is the most coveted, hugely celebrated, impeccably athletic wrestler to walk this earth. And I think it’s safe to say that at Crowning Glory III, where the tens of thousands in the stadium, and the millions watching around the world rise out of their seats, it will be for good reason. It will be for David Whitman. Alejandro pauses for a minute and takes some time to patrol along the wall. He passes another mammoth poster, promoting the Money in the Bank Ladder Match containing all eight men, looking up at the briefcase that they will fight for. He turns around and walks right back to his poster. Alejandro: But this match is as much mine as it is his, the fans may not be cheering me on Sunday when the bell rings for the first time… But by the time I am done wrestling my heart out, by the time I have bled for them and exhausted myself to put on the greatest wrestling match in the history of MWE, they will cheer. And they will give me and David the biggest standing ovation of our careers. They will scream our names until their faces turn blue, and they will be filled with an euphoria that will last them the whole evening, their whole lives infact. And they will chant, “Please don’t go, Please don’t go…” He says these last words slowly and he lifts one fist in the air as he says it, bringing his head up high. He stops, and brings down his fist and his head slowly. Alejandro: And as much I have said those words in my lifetime, the closest people to me still seem to go. I have seen my own mother killed to save my life. I saw the man who gave me the name “The Angel” slaughtered while I was running for my life. I saw the man who gave me my first wrestling boots, the man who trained me to wrestle, the man who let me in his life and treated me like a son, die in my arms. And most recently, I heard about Tim Fitzgerald’s death, the man who saw the potential in me, the man who saw what I would become, the man who gave me my first job, taken away from the earth, far before his time. “The good die young,” is the saying... Mom, Angél, Santino, Tim… They were more than good. They call me “The Angel”, but they are the true angels, and I am so sorry that they had to die before they could see what I have become, before they could see what I have made of my life… But this is all for them, and I hope they’re watching down, because I dedicate everything I have to them, because without them I would not be the man I am today. For that I will forever be in their debt. A tear falls from Alejandro’s eye without him realising, and it takes him a minute to compose himself. He looks up the ceiling and puts his hand on his heart, as more tears threaten to fall. Alejandro: David Whitman, you may be stronger in your body, but I am strong in heart, and the strength I feel, knowing the people who love me most are watching from above, will give you one hell of a fight. We have had some of the most brutal, legendary battles in the history of MWE, but it will be nothing… absolutely nothing compared to the blood, sweat and tears that you and I will experience on Sunday. I promise you, every MWE fan, every wrestler, every announcer, every cameraman or worker… You will watch on Sunday, and you will watch in awe… David Whitman, you are one of my greatest friends, I love you, I respect you… We will go out there on Sunday, and WE WILL TEAR THE DAMN ROOF off the United Center! We ARE NOT going out without a spectacle! We will steal the whole damn show and show THEM ALL how it‘s supposed to be done! Alejandro looks at the camera without an intense fury and it takes him a moment to cool down. Alejandro: And for everyone who has been there along for the ride. Darrel, Billy-Joe, Stacy, Stephanie, Michaels, Williams, D.C., Resoan, Copane, the entire MWE Roster… I thank you so much. The is the End. The scene fades out. Green Valley, Arizona 6 years ago Death is a terrible thing. That was all that could run through Alejandro’s mind for the last few days, all he could think about. And it was true too, death had stripped him of his mother, and The Angel. He had barely escaped with his life, he had not stopped running for miles. He managed to hitchhike a ride for half the way, and he stopped off at Green Valley. Which is where he is now, sat in the corner of the street, his face masked with tears and his wrestling mask on the floor. He is sat on a street, people walk past and cringe away from Alejandro, other than the occasional one who feels sorry for him, who spares him a pitiful glance before walking away. All with one exception, one young adult, perhaps the same age as Alejandro… 16? 17? He walks over to Alejandro, and looks at him in pity. He is holding a sandwich in his hand. He is wearing a white t-shirt and jeans. He gets down to his knee’s and looks at Alejandro in the face. ???: Have something to eat. The man gives Alejandro his sandwich. Alejandro takes a bite and moans with hunger. Alejandro: Gracias… Alejandro eats more and the man smiles. David: My name’s David… Alejandro cannot tell what he is saying, and just looks at him in confusion. David: You come from Mexico? I live in Phoenix. My cousin James and his friend Thomas took me down to the Green Valley for the weekend, dunno what for… there’s only trees down here. Alejandro tries to make out what he is saying, but just looks more confused. A moment later, two other men walk up to David, and Alejandro looks up at them, they are older than David, they were James and Thomas. James: There you are, we’ve been looking all over the place… Thomas: Who’s your friend? David: Um… I dunno his name, I think he’s from Mexico. James raises a hand and waves at Alejandro. James: The name’s Whitman… James Whitman. Thomas: Dude… you’re not James Bond. James smacks Thomas upside the head and they have a miniature fight. David rolls his eyes and Alejandro smirks. James: Come on Dave, we better get going. David: But… Thomas: David, this guy can’t tell a damn thing you say. Lets go. Thomas and James begin to walk off and David watches them, he turns to face Alejandro. David: I’m sure we’ll meet again someday, bye. David runs after James and Thomas, who have already reached the end of the street. Alejandro: Goodbye. The scene fades out as Alejandro rolls onto his back and David disappears round a corner. ___________________________________________________________________________ Kinda ironic, huh?
