Birthday Hangover

Aug 11 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The sudden clanking of glass bottles and tin cans suddenly awaken Blight who is sprawled out on his couch in his home. He looks up at who made the noise and FreakShow is standing over him giving him a weird smile and says oops. FreakShow continues to go around the room picking up bottles and cans as Blight tries to lift his head up but puts it back down as if it weighs a ton. He rolls over and moans and streches in the process. He is still wearing his boots and jeans from the night before but his shirt is no where to be found. He sits up holds his head for a second and pushes his long black hair back. He looks at FreakShow still blurry eyed and looks around at what is left of the mess of his house. BLIGHT: Damn that was one hell of a party. What time did people leave...rather did people leave at all? FREAKSHOW: Haha yup that was one party bro! Well I stayed the night to look after you, you did put yourself out of commision. Martyr took Jezibel back to the Sanctuary. Talon disapeared about halfway through with some girl. As for everyone else....I haven't the slightest clue! Blight looks up at FreakShow once more before trying to rub his eyes clear again. He forces himself to stand up kicking more bottles in the process and almost knocking over a bucket next to the couch where he was laying. BLIGHT: What is the bucket for? And what did I drink my head feels like a thousand pounds? Uhg never mind I think I know what the bucket is for..... Blight quickly turns around and drops to his knees and starts to vomit in the bucket. FreakShow grabs a few more cans and bottles among other garbage and puts them into the big garbage bag he is carrying before scurrying off to the kitchen. He comes back with a rather large bottle of water in his hand for Blight to drink. FreakShow: Here drink this its just water. Well lets see you started with some beers...then Martyr showed up with some Jack Daniels...Jezibel broke open some Southern Comfort...we started some Vodka shots...oh yeah and Talon had some Jose Cuervo with your name all over it. By the time we sang Happy Birthday to you, you were fucked out of your mind bro. BLIGHT: Holy crap man...I think there is a racoon in my stomach. Ok from what ever else do you remember have the MWE called yet to let me know if I have a match this week? Oh shit wheres that bucket??!!! Blight starts to heave as FreakShow grabs Blights cell phone out of his leather jacket that is hanging on the wall. He opens it up and starts hitting some buttons hands it to Blight who is sitting back up again before turning to finish cleaning. Blight puts the phone to his ear and listens to the voicemail that was left from the night before. As he is listening to the message Jezibel opens the front door and lets herself in. She walks over rubs Blight's shoulders slightly before helping FreakShow finish the cleanup. By this time most of the work is done and almost everything looks as if there was never a party. BLIGHT: Hey Jez...my damn stomach...well FreakShow this week is gonna be damn fun. I got not one...not two...but three opponents. Chris England, Peter K. and the new girl Tali Preston. I didn't realize Peter K. was still walking after last week...looks like I'm gonna----- JEZIBEL: Well your not going through with it are you? Right? Blight and FreakShow just look at Jezibel blankly, with Blight trying to figure out which one of her is talking. Once he realizes the Jezibel in the middle is the right one he stands up and stretches. BLIGHT: What are you talking about? The match? Of course I'm gonna go through with it, I have a job to do ya know. JEZIBEL: So that means having to fight a woman really? So then next week if they put me and you one on one you would actually wrestle me? FreakShow: Jez that is a different story though, Blight wouldn't let that happen. Even if---- JEZIBEL: Shut up FreakShow of course your gonna make excuses for him your his best friend...now answer me Blight are you defected or something? How can you go into this match and wrestle a woman? Blight now starting to see straight finally still just looks at Jezibel blankly shakes his head and walks over to his big arm chair and sits down. FreakShow following Blight walks over to the couch and sits and just stares at the irrate Jezibel trying to figure out what the problem is. BLIGHT: Are you done now? Now I can explain myself...first off I have a job to do and if I don't do that job I don't get paid, and I'm not a happy person if I don't get paid. Second off Tali Preston is one piece of hot ass. FreakShow laughs in agreement but quickly shuts up as Jezibel shoots him a deathly look from across the room. BLIGHT: But...she probablly has a bigger dick than I do. Thus making the bitch a man. Once that match is over the only time anyone in the MWE will see her...him...it is when she is standing on the corner where Eli found her. She wants to be the Rock n Roll Princess, she wants to be The Sweetest Temptation...she wants to be something. If it is the last thing I do I'm going to make sure she isn't going to be apart of the MWE for much long...unless FreakShow here decides he wants a trophy wife and can afford her wages. FreakShow starts to smile at Blight's comments but stops and looks at Jezibel to make sure she isn't going to hurl something at him for doing so. Instead in anger she flips off both Blight and FreakShow as she storms out of the house. FREAKSHOW: What the hell was that about? What does she want you to do about it you have to take orders. Dude did you say Peter K and Chris England too? BLIGHT: Yeah and? Look last week I left Peter K laying out in that ring along with Anthony Clark. I just wonder why the hell MWE would put him in the ring with me again. I seriously think he has a man crush on me man. I saw the way he was checking out my ass in that ring. Everytime we locked up it was like he was just trying to cop a feel bro...what the hell do I have to do to keep him away. I need to find the kryptonite for the MetroSexual Superman. Blight stands up and makes a superhero pose before acting like he is fixing his hair and checking his nails as FreakShow laughs hysterically. BLIGHT: I wonder who is more womanly Peter or Tali. Now Chris England on the other hand maybe I should worry about....maybe. Proud English Patriot my ass, the only thing he is proud about is that his teeth aren't as fucked up as the old cliche goes. Although I haven't had a chance to put my fist through his mouth yet eigther. I'll have to make sure to wipe the fish n chips off my boots when I finish with him. Ya know how people say if your around someone long enough you take on some of their habits? I wonder if I'm in that ring with England long enough will I start running around like a Brit screaming out different cereals? Cheerios, Fruit Loops, Frosted Flakes!! Blight starts jumping around but quickly regrets it and clutches his head as the hangover is still in full effect. FreakShow laughs so hard he slides off the couch and lands on a missed beer can. He rolls over and peels it from his ass and sets it aside. FREAKSHOW: Dude all that is funny as shit on a stick but your going in there with three serious opponents. Dude your gonna have to watch your back and wrestle smart. One false move and that could be it. You really have a chance here to prove you can hang with the big boys of the fed. BLIGHT: Yeah dude I know what I'm getting myself into. But do they know what they are walking into? Highly doubtful. I have seen it all...I have felt pain uncomparable to anything they have known. I have put more blood and sweat into this buisness than they could imagine. I have fallen from my moutain top, but now its time for me to rise back to the top. Now its time for me to show how dominate I really am. I have been face to face with the Devil himself. I have faced my sins and destroyed my fears. Now its time for the three of them to see what it is like to stare death in the face and kick him in his balls. Blight walks out of the room into his bedroom and comes out with a large duffle bag. He puts it on the couch pulls his hair into a ponytail and grabs a set of keys off of the table near the large arm chair. BLIGHT: England, Tali, Peter, smile pretty for the devil because your about to take a walk through Hell. Blight motions to FreakShow to head for the door. Outside Blight mounts his Harley and FreakShow climbs into his Camaro. FreakShow pulls out onto the road and heads into town, as Blight rides off into the sunset over the highway. END SCENE
