PORCUPINE! NOSTRILS!

Mar 27 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The scene cuts to Mr Markowitz. He is wearing a plain purple shirt and blue jeans. He walks into a hospital waiting room. Markowitz sits down on the second green chair on the right side. He has a look of relief and confusion on his face. He is the only person in the room at that moment. He is looking around for a few minutes, and at that moment, a familiar old man walks in. After close examine, Markowitz realizes who this man is. Markowitz: Mr.. Mr Talbot? Is that you? Mr Talbot: Yes...? Excuse me, but I don't think I know you. Markowitz chuckles as Mr Talbot looks confused. Markowitz: Yes, you taught me.. in 9th grade! Mr Talbot: You look familiar...Markowitz.. I remember you. Markowitz, chuckles, as Mr Talbot has a small smile on his face. Markowitz: Yea, I was always the one kid, who always who got sent to the office. Markowitz laughs, as Mr Talbot is smiling. Markowitz: Anyway, what brings you here? Did anybody..god forbid..get hurt that you...know? Mr Talbot looks down and bites his lip. Mr Talbot: Umm..yes..My daughter..she, was riding her bicycle, and she was hit by a passing car. She is in a coma, I..We..don't know when she is going to wake up. Markowitz: Oh My God. I am so sorry. Well, my dad, he had a stroke, he is ok, but needs rest. Mr Talbot sighs, and looks at Markowitz and gives him a little pat on the back. At that moment the same doctor from last week comes in and call Markowitz to his fathers room. After walking down a small corridor, passing by several rooms, Markowitz walks into his dads room. The moment Markowitz sees his dad, he runs up to him and kisses him on his cheek. Markowitz: Dad! How you feeling? Markowitz' dad coughs and is speaking in a low voice. Dad: Okay. I..*coughs* I, just woke up a few hours ago. Just been *clears throat* watching TV. Markowitz: Cool. Well, I really hope you are doing OK. I can't wait till you come back home, good as new. They told me I could only stay a few minutes though. Markowitz looks down and wiggles his feet a bit. Dad: Well, I love you son. Remember that...Anyway, I thought you said Gary was coming? Markowitz cracks his knuckles and stretches out his arms. He smiles at his dad. Markowitz: Yea, he told me he couldn't go. Didn't give a reason. Dad: Oh. Yea.. Well you better get going..There giving me lunch soon. Markowitz: See ya dad, I love you. I will see you tomorrow, and everyday. The scene fades as Markowitz is leaving the room. ************************************************************ The scene cuts to Mr Markowitz. He is wearing a black shirt with a red tribal design on the front, blue jeans and a yankee cap backwards. He is staring at a picture of his father on his wall. The camera zooms out and it shows Triple G and Mr Markowitz both sitting down on Markowitz bed. it is a king sized bed, with blue sheets and a blue and white blanket and matching pillows. Triple G is wearing a blue shirt and green and white shorts.. He seems to be smiling and patting Markowitz on the shoulder trying to cheer him up. Markowitz: They wouldn't let me see him! They told me he needs rest. I promised him I would see him everyday. Markowitz is visibly upset. Triple G is smiling, trying to make him feel better. Triple G: Yo, Marko, I told you, different subject, lets talk about... MWE. Remember? You beat me! Your the captain of Blood Fuze man! Markowitz looks up at Triple G and starts to grin a bit. But, the grin quickly goes away after the thought of his dad. Triple G tries to comfort him, but, Marko pushes him away. Triple G: Come on man, don't be like that. Your dad will be fine, don't worry. They only said you cant see him for an hour. Markowitz, who has calmed down, is talking slowly with anticipation. Markowitz: My dad... could.. have died. Now... they wont let me see him again? Triple G: Yes, I know that. You think I was happy when I couldn't be with my mom, when she had cancer? Markowitz: I know. Those were rough times like now. I just wanted to see him. The last few memories I have of him, is seeing him on the floor, and and seeing him, for a short amount of time, in a hospital bed... Lets say... God forbid he dies.. Markowitz starts to tear a bit. Triple G tries to get him back to a good mood by talking about MWE. Triple G: Anyway, what are you gonna do, now that your captain? Markowitz looks up at Triple G and grins, forgetting about his dad for a moment. Markowitz: We decided whoever won was captain. I won... Markowitz grin suddenly turns into a smile, he completely forgets about his dad. Triple G: Exactly why you are the captain. Markowitz: Yes, and after thinking about it, I decide.. no. Triple G, smiles but quickly gets confused. Triple G: What..What do you mean? Markowitz: I mean that we are Blood Fuze! There is no captain, there is no leader, because without the other guy, we could never be Blood Fuze! One man does not make a team and I certainly believe it. I need you, you need me. I believed this from the beginning, and knew this all along. Triple G smiles, claps his hands for a second before gesturing for Markowitz to come. Triple G: One thing I don't get is, why did you say all this if you didn't want to be captain anyways? Markowitz: Simple. So you would fight harder. Triple G: I see.. Triple G is smiling and shaking his head. Markowitz: What I don't understand, is why my underwear doesn't put the leaf on his letter opener, if my lightbulb isn't opening up its can of chicken noodle soup. Triple G: THE FUCK? Triple G scratches his head and wonders what Markowitz is talking about, as Markowitz is laughing. Markowitz: Do you ever pay attention? You know our opponent? Ozzy Sampson? Yeah well, he is sorta messed up..in the head. He screams out random shit like, PORCUPINE! or..NOSTRILS! It's actually sorta pretty funny. Anyway Ozzy, I know you have Diamond Moses and Eve Magdalen on your side. But little do you know, I have a secret weapon of my own, coming at HtH. Actually, we, Markowitz points at Triple G. Markowitz: Do. They both laugh. Triple G: So true, and speaking of chicken noodle soup, lets go downstairs, I'm hungry. The scene fades as they quickly and quietly walk out of the room. ************************************* *The Next Day* The scene cuts to Triple G sitting on a bench in the park. He is wearing a white Abercrombie shirt, with blue stripes and jeans. The bench is a light brownish color and has dark black metal screw in it. It is a nice and quiet morning. The birds are chirping, the sky is blue, the sun is out, and the wind is blowing freely. But, most importantly, there is a girl sitting on a bench 20 feet away from Triple G. He is staring at her, she doesnt notice, because she is reading the book Twilight. He is staring at her. She doesn’t seem to realize, so he just continues. Triple G *To himself*: Come on! Just go and ask her! You can do it! She will say yes! Triple G stares at her again. Those beautiful eyes. The beautiful hair. The beautiful everything. Triple G *To himself*: You can do it… You can do it…. Come on, go! Triple G gets off the bench slowly. He pretends not to notice her and goes over to the bench she is on and sits down. He takes a deep breath and talks. Triple G: Hello. Girl: Hey. Triple G: How are you? Girl: Good. She isn't paying much attention to him, as, she is reading her book. Triple G: What might your name be? Girl: Allison. Triple G pauses and stares at her. He smiles. Triple G: Cool. I’m Gary. Allison: Cool. Alison is still not paying much attention to him, but Triple G is staring at her in amazement. Triple G: How is that book? Allison: Huh? Triple G: How is the book? Allison: Oh, it’s good, I guess. Triple G: Great…. Allison: Yeah. Triple G sighs and takes a deep breath. He then proceeds. Triple G: Umm…. Would… You… Like… Triple G pauses for a second. Triple G: Would you like to go to to get lunch or something? She looks up at him and smiles. Allison: I thought you would never ask! Come on let’s go! Triple G smiles as she takes his hand. They start to talk to each other until they reach to his car. He opens the door and lets her in. He then goes to the other side and gets in. He starts the car. They start to move off. They see a struggling old man walking down the street. But, he quickly fades away as they drive. They pull up next to a cafe. They get out and get inside. They see a tall man. He is wearing a black suit with a red tie. He is wearing a white undershirt and is looking around. His hair is a bit grayish and whitish. He looks to be around 50 years old. It is very dark inside. There are many people talking amongst themselves. A lot of spoons stirring. It is very loud. But, the smell is like coffee. Triple G goes over to the man. He is holding a clipboard on a brown stand. He reserves a table. Man: Your table will be ready in 15 minutes. Triple G: Thank you. Allison: Let’s go wait outside a bit. Maybe get to know each other a bit. Triple G: That sounds great. Allison: What do you do for a living? Triple G: Well, I am a wrestler. Speaking of which do you want to hear who my opponent is? Triple G looks at her and smiles as Allison nods her head, yes. Triple G: Well his name is Axel Christ. He is basically a copy, of one of the most famous, and best wrestlers in MWE. DC. You see, he was trained by him, and since I guess he is to dumb to think for himself, he has to copy another guy. And, he also has his own "lacky" in Ozzy Sampson, who is also my opponent. He.. well he is..well..special, I guess I could say. They both walk outside and start to wait. Triple G looks at her and stares. He starts to think to himself. Triple G *Thinking*: She’s so beautiful. She’s one in a million. I have never felt love like this before. Triple G’s quickly forgets about that as the man calls them in. The scene fades away as Triple G and Allison are walking into the café. OOC: Remember, this RP is for both of us.
