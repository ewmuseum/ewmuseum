Rage and Awesomeness; A Virus and Scotty McKnight joint RP

Jul 25 2009

Leon Roberts & Scotty McKnight

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
~Toronto, July 21st, 3pm~ Leon Roberts had just pulled into his garage. Inspite of the recent concussion he suffered, he did indeed attend Monday Night Mayhem, and from what we all witnessed, beat up his best friend, Vega Knight. Nobody knew quite yet as to why he did it. There have been rumors going around, but nothing concrete. He took his luggage out from the truck of his Hummer, and went inside. A scowl was very much present on his face. He went in, and proceeded upstairs. He put all his bags down. He was about to leave, when he saw Demonica leaning against the doorframe, with a look of disgust on her face. Leon Sneered at her. Demonica: What the fuck is your problem Leon? Why did you attack Vega like that. You had no right to do so. Leon: I don’t need a right, nor a reason, to do any motherfucken thing I please, or have you forgotten that? But there is a reason. A reason, which would drive any man to do worse then what I did. I was merciful believe it or not. Because……I KNOW ABOUT WHAT HAPPENED WHILE I WAS IN A FUCKEN COMA!!!!!!!!!!!! Demonica gasped. It was clear she knew exactly what Leon was talking about. She pulled her hair a bit, confused and stressed on how Leon could have possibly have found out about her and Vega’s one night stand. Demonica: How did- Leon:-I find out? I found out a couple of ways. One, you obviously forgot to take out the garbage in our room while I was out, because when I did, I saw two semen filled condoms in the pale. Two, when we went out the other night, the babysitter for the triplets, when we hired her again, pulled me aside, and told me what she saw. Demonica was starting to hyperventilate due to Leon’s ever growing rage. But, that is when He smirked. She saw that, and for a good five seconds, she stopped breathing altogether. He slowly walked towards Demonica, almost slithering like a snake. He chuckled as he got within inches of her, and swiftly grabbed her neck. He dragged her pin, before pinning her against the wall. She struggled, hitting the arm that held her throat repeatedly. Leon: You know, that’s going to hurt you a lot worse then it will me. But then again, maybe that’s what you deserve. Pain. You deserve to be treated the way I found you three years ago. LIKE THE WHORE THAT YOU ARE!!!!!!!!!!!! Leon tightens his grip to the point when Demonica is having a difficult time breathing. It is then, that he takes his other hand, and delivers a solid blow to her stomach. She was very lucky that she was not pregnant. Leon dropped her to the floor. She began to cough up some blood. He then knelt down, and smacked her down. She landed with a bit of a thud, and her eyes partially glazed over. A small stream of blood trickled down her lip. Leon then heard his cell phone go off, playing the song ‘Lick it up’ by Kiss, it was an indication he had received a text. It was from Scotty McKnight. It read: Yo Leon. Come on over later. We’ve been put in a match together against Crash and CSB. Let’s chill, drink some, smoke a pound of weed lol Scotty Leon smirked at that. He put his cellphone back in his pocket, before, taking one last look at Demonica, as she was getting up Leon: I will be back. And if you aren’t here, I will find you. So don’t do anything stupid. Leon immediately left. He got in his hummer, and drove to Scotty’s. It would take him about half an hour. Needless to say, Leon hadn’t exactly simmered down yet from his encounter with Demonica. When he arrived, he got out, and went to the front door, pounding on it. Scotty answered almost immediately. He saw the unpleasant look on his friend’s face. Scotty: Yo, what’s the damage? Leon: Ugh....you don't want to know. Scotty: Well considering the partnership we have sort of created in the past month, and not to mention our match together, I would like to know, maybe I could help. Leon: True. Well I've been bottling it up all week anyways. But ever since Monday Night Mayhem, have you been at least a bit curious as to why I beat my best friend, Vega, up? Scotty looked at Leon curiously, as he let him inside his house. He was thinking for a second, as he shut the door behind him Scotty: Now that you mention it, I have been wondering. Leon: It's because I found out that he and my wife Demonica, while I was in a motherfucken coma, THEY FUCKED!!!!!!!! A deep growl from Leon’s throat indicated that he was not going to be a happy camper anytime soon. Not to mention his knuckles were turning ghostly white, and he had drawn blood from both of his hands. Scotty: Wow. Here, let me get something for that. Scotty left the room. As he did that, Leon also sat down on the couch. He took a deep breath, before emitting a low growl. He shook his head, before he looked at his partially bleeding hands. It was then, that Leon felt an emotion he thought he could never feel. Remorse. He knew he had crossed the line this time. He knew it wasn’t so much the physical torture that would have affected Demonica, but the mental torture. He knew that he crossed the line by calling her a whore, because of her past. He let a single tear loose, before he buried his face in his hands. He was ashamed of himself for what he had done earlier. Leon: What have I done? She didn’t deserve any of that. Scotty comes back, and sees Leon in this state. He immediately sits down beside him, with a worried look on his face. Scotty: What the fuck? Are you alright? Leon: No. The thing is......Well.......I really exploded on Demonica. I mean....Beyond normal. I choked her, punched her, and smacked her.......and called her a whore. A real sore spot for her. Scotty: That's pretty intense, what caused it? Leon sighed. He took the band aids from Scotty, and put them on. He looked at the ground for a second, before he began to speak again. Leon: I was just way to mad at her because of her sleeping with Vega. Scotty: Boosh... Well, she knows why? is there any malice between you two? Leon: No there shouldn't be.......I don't get it. It just...felt as if they waited for this. I mean yeah, I knew that Vega had a crush on her.........I don't know.........I have every right to be mad, yet I still feel this way. Scotty: Well I don't know how much my opinion matters in this, but I think your still pissed at the fact that he slept with her and she allowed it. Leon: Well....I don't know. I'll ask her exactly what happened. But...the thing is, when Vega had two personalities, he almost raped her. That's what's bugging me the most. Leon briefly massaged his temples, so he wouldn’t feel the stress of what’s been recently happening so much. It’s very rare indeed that Leon would ever feel guilty about anything. Leon: *sigh* Just...give me a second......I'm going to text her. Scotty: Do what you must man, I’m getting a beer. Leon: Okay. Uhhh.....got any Jack Daniels? I need something hard. Scotty: I think so, let me check. Scotty left the living room again, as Leon took his cell phone out. He began to write a message to Demonica. The message read: Demonica. I know that I’m probably the last person you want to even think about, and I can’t blame you. I am so…sorry, my love, that I….snapped like that. I had no right to. I can only hope for you to quickly forgive me, but I don’t expect it. I know that it wasn’t the physical pain that would make you not forgive me, but what I said. I know I hit a sore spot, and I didn’t mean to. If you can let me explain why I went off the deep end like that, I would be most happy. Leon. Leon sent the text, just as Scotty came in with a beer and a tray full of Jack Daniels shots. Leon smiled. Scotty: Will ten shots be enough? Leon: Oh more then enough.. Gotta say though, this match of ours coming up, it could go either way. Leon takes his first shot, as Scotty raises an eyebrow in confusion towards what Leon had said. Leon: What I mean is, You recently had a match with Zack Crash, while I have dealt with CSB. So in a sense, we already know each other. So our best bet, is to strategize. Scotty: I hear you bro. I hear you. The two began to trade knowledge of their opposition back and forth, while they drank. This went on for about an hour. Leon polished off all of the shots, and was pretty much drunk. Now he was drinking a lot of water to keep himself hydrated. Scotty: Dude, you okay there? Leon: Yeah….just….need the H2O, know what, I’m sayin’? Scotty: Yeah. Dammit I knew ten shots was to much. Leon: Yeah yeah….Well then I knooowwwz who I’ll be sending the fucken hospital bill to if I…..am poisoned. Both men laughed at that joke. Scotty knew Leon was kidding, so he didn’t worry so much. As he laughed, Scotty went to get his camcorder ready, so they could actually do a shoot on CSB and Zack Crash. Scotty: Yo V, We need to show those motherfuckers how WE ROLL!!!!!! Leon: SHWEET!!! Scotty immediately came back with his camcorder and tripod. He set the tripod up, before hooking the camcorder up. Leon came back into the living room quickly, looking a bit more sober then he was before. Leon: Say uhhh…Scotty. I’ve been meaning to ask you since you ditched the clown. I’ve been wanting to…expand Black List Mafia for a while. Regardless if I…hadn’t of kicked Vega out, there are still plenty of openings. So what you say? Scotty: I’ll think about it, how about that? Leon: That’s cool. Leon went back into the kitchen, to get some more water. Scotty immediately presses the record button on the camcorder. He sits down, facing towards the camera, ready to begin this. Just by looking at him, anyone could say that he was both very much determined and excited for this upcoming tag team match with Leon, against CSB and Zack Crash. Scotty: If my match at the previous edition of Monday Night Mayhem was an indication, then everything that I’m doing, must be right. I did what I said I was going to do, and I soundly defeated Peter Kaymackian, to get what is truly rightfully mine, and that is an XTV title shot at the mecca MWE pay per view, Crowning Glory. When I get the three count over Jay Riddick, It will be the next step, of me heading to the top of the MWE mountain. But this week, me and my boy, Leon ‘The Virus’ Roberts here, will be, for the very first time, teaming up, in what will be, a match that will be nothing short of skull caving awesomeness. Leon (off camera): Yo Scotty. Where’s the weed? Scotty: What? Leon (off camera): I said…..WHERE’S THE MOTHERFUCKEN WEED?!?!?! Scotty: Oh. That can wait dude. Leon (off camera): Fine. Leon would come out from the kitchen, to join Scotty on camera. He sat down beside Scotty, with a semi distant look on his face. Scotty: Now, we know who we’re facing. We’re facing the so called, ‘Ultimate Thrill Seeker’ Zack Crash, and Chris Saint Blaise, or CSB as he is called. Both Virus and I, have experience against our opposition. He with CSB in the late, great wrestling company, GWC. Leon: Oh hell yeah. Scotty: As for me, I am still fresh off of a victory at Independence Day, over him and Chris England. Needless to say, I’m not exactly happy. Leon looks at Scotty oddly, even raising an eyebrow. Scotty knows that Leon is confused. He takes a deep breath, before he continues. Scotty: Let me put it to you this way. It constantly I keep getting matched against people I already beat. But why should I, when I am moving up the ladder like a speeding bullet. I mean seriously... Zack Crash? His name suits his record against me, all his did was crash beneath my greatness. Crash, I beat you once, With, or without Virus’s help I will do it again, You will be just another win to my record, just proving why I will hold the X-TV title come Crowning Glory. Leon: Well said Scotty. Scotty: Thanks. But there is still more. Crash, I will give you credit that you deserve. You have proven that you will do anything to entertain. But, just because you think that because your such a big thrill seeker, it will get you respect. HA! Well Zack, your going up against the biggest thrill killer in MWE, You want a fucken thrill? Well you found it, and it will be the last thrill you will ever get. Even you know that with the way you are, it will be your own demise. Come Monday Night Mayhem, if I actually fail, to end you, I know that you will be dealing with Virus. Your end, is almost certain. Leon’s distant face lights up like suddenly, as he gives his trademark smirk, his eyes, focused entirely on the camera. Leon: But, let’s not forget about what else we must deal with. We are dealing with the last person to hold the GWC world title. Yes. I recall the match where it happened. It was the match, that inevitably spelled the beginning of the end. Not only, because of our careless General Manager at the time, but because of you CSB. In a four round match, where the Ultimate, Underground, United States, and World titles were up for grabs, we both had secured victories. I, would be the last person that would hold the Ultimate title. A title, which I will begrudgingly admit, you give value back to it. Leon pauses for a second, to take a quick gulp of water from the glass he had brought with him. A bit of the content leaks from his lip. But he lets it fall, as a single drop falls off from his goatie. Leon: But you did nothing for the world title. In fact, with the way you are, you should consider yourself lucky. Because all I see, is nothing more then a foul mouthed, lucky, retarded, douchebag. You and your fuck buddy, Wayne…you can watch all the porn you want, you can drink, say anything you want, and smoke a fat pound daily, it still won’t make a difference. That just means you’re a normal sumbitch pile of monkey shit. How pathetic. Seriously, because when was the last time that you dared to go beyond the boxed limits, and turn heads. Like, deciding to go on a city wide joyride, cause property damage, and keep a straight face while doing so? You might be considered greatness in the eyes of the fans. While I do love the fans myself, they are all extremely short sighted sometimes. While they see somebody who swears, and is entertaining, I see a guy who’s all talk, and little action. Yes, I will admit, you are skilled for some of your accomplishments. CSB, part of your game plan is to get into people’s heads. Well that don’t work on me. Leon takes a deep breath, as he pauses to collect his thoughts quickly. Scotty is agreeing with all of what Leon has said thus far about CSB. Leon: But like Scotty said about Zack, your end is near. Had you faced me before this upcoming show, maybe you would have had a chance for survival. But this week, there is none. For good reasons as well. Leon’s smirk fades away, to a full fledged scowl. In order for him to show the full effect, he had to recall the events of the past week. Regardless if he felt guilty or not, this is something that would make any man’s blood boil to a fever pitch. Leon: The reason is, goes back as to why I took my best friend, Vega Knight, out for good. See, I found something else he and my wife did. Yeah, I know you’re going to talk about it. Well go ahead CSB, I dare you. I will laugh at all of your words should you. Scotty: Umm Virus….you’re getting off topic. Leon: I know, sorry. But anyways. The reason was, because I had found out, while I was in a coma, they fucked. My wife, the single, most amazing woman I met, betrayed me. I don’t know what caused it. Were they drunk, or did Demonica feel way to lonely. I don’t know. Perhaps I never will. But the rage I felt when I learned the truth, could not be contained. I first hunted Vega, and I took him out. I then made Demonica cower before me, as I wrapped my claw around her neck, taking the air out of her lungs, before dropping her down on the floor. If I’m capable of doing that, to people I actually care about, what makes you think that anyone else would have a chance for survival. Scotty: OOHHH!!!! Yeah I really don’t want to piss this guy off even further then he already is. Anyways, I’m getting a drink. Leon: Heh. At least my partner knows better then to get on my bad side. So CSB, Zack Crash. We’ve said everything that’s needed to be said. We dare you to say anything. We know just about what you’re planning to say. But let me ask you this, to the both of you. What are the things that you guys cherish most? I want the pleasure, of taking it all away. Scotty comes back with two drinks, and a couple of rolled up joints. One was a Smirnoff for Leon, and the other, was a Budweiser. He gave Leon a joint at the Smirnoff (which was already opened), while he put his beer down, and lit up his joint. Leon: Thanks dude. In the end, there is only one thing that can describe this match. Our opposition, might be good, but they are far from being me or Scotty here. It will be match of the night for sure. In the end, Scotty here Rocks, I, The Virus rock, CSB and Zack Crash suck… Scotty: AND I LOVE SLUTS!!!!!!!! Leon: HELL YEAH BROTHER!!!!!! Leon takes a big swig of his drink, before he lights up his joint. Both Scotty and Leon smoke their joints, as they raise their drinks in an early celebration. FIN
