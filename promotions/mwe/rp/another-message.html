Another message......; A JJ Executioner RP

Oct 9 2008

JJ Executioner

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Knock knock Voice: “Come in!” The oak door opens. Slowly and hesitantly. “Errr, Hello Mr Covell.” Shawn Spitzer pops his head around the door. His cheek is swollen and he has a split lip. “Shawn Spitzer! Come in, come in! It is so great to see you and in one piece too. How are you?” Shawn steps through. His eyes look heavy and weary. “Good sir, thank you. I am glad to be back at work though.” “It’s not too early for you?” “No sir.” “And you are OK about the interview?” “Of course, who’s doing it?” “Sara Patterson.” “Oh, OK.” Shawn blushes and shuffles uncomfortably on the spot. “Can I have it?” Covell breaks the short uncomfortable silence. “Of course.” Shawn hands a small packet to Covell. “Great, we will get some questions out of the way then we will play this. Let’s get you miked up.” Covell puts a comforting arm around Shawn’s shoulder. Together they walk out of the room and turn a corner into a small studio. Shawn freezes as he sees the studio lights, as they are frighteningly similar to the ones used by JJ Executioner during Shawn’s recent ordeal. Covell nudges Shawn ahead and pair of overall-clad, headphone-wearing technicians approaches them. Shawn stares, bewildered at this sudden burst of focused attention. Shawn is ushered over to a brightly lit area. It has a blue backdrop with a series of red and white MWE logos scattered diagonally across. There are two large office chairs, positioned across from each other in front of one camera. “Shawn!” An enthusiastic voice brightens up the room. Sara Patterson, Spitzers’ fellow MWE interviewer and incredible ball of energy bounds up. Shawn feels his cheeks flush instantly. Shawn regarded her as the single prettiest girl in the wrestling industry and had a thing for her from the moment that they had met eighteen months ago. She is wearing a strappy pink vest top and low-riding black denim jeans. “I am so happy to see you are OK!” Of all the warm greetings that Shawn had received on his first day, this meant as much to Shawn as anything else. He felt an instant pang of ....emotion. He tries to hide it. He fails miserably. His attempts to talk to Sarah are pitiful and end in Shawn just gawping open-mouthed about twelve inches south of her eyes. Covell quickly notices Shawn’s blank stare and tries to restart the conversation. Covell: “So Sara, are you OK with the set up?” Sara: “Of course.....a few questions, then we play the tape. And you are OK with that Shawn?” Shawn: “I think so......what about the tape?” Covell and Sara look at each other and then back at Shawn. Sara: “I wanted to get a live reaction from you, as we were filming...” Covell: “But I don’t think that you are quite ready for that. It would not be fair to you.” Sara: “So what do you want us to do?” Shawn has a look of shock on his face. Now that he has the option to see what JJ may have said or done to him or where he was kept during his ordeal, he is not sure that he wants to. Covell senses his hesitation and tries again to reassure his young employee with a fatherly arm around Shawn’s shoulders. Covell: “We do not mind whatever you say. I am sure it is important that you see it but so long as it is under your terms.” Shawn: “It’s OK. I want to. See it now I mean.” Covell leads him to a 14’’ LCD TV with DVD player set up in a corner. Within seconds, a grainy, wavering image is playing. Covell brings a chair and gestures for him to sit down. Sara and he stand behind the chair, with intrigued looks on the face. Shawn becomes pale as the picture on screen steadies. The images appeared to have been filmed with a handheld camcorder placed on a table. Quickly, JJ Executioner is shown on screen as he takes a seat in front of the camera. He is clearly visible against the dark background. JJ rests the MWE X TV title across his lap. He adjusts his mask slightly and moves his greasy, black, shoulder length hair out of his face with a giant hand. JJ: “Shawn. No doubt you will be watching this from the comfort of an MWE studio somewhere in middle America. I am sure you have questions. Where were you? Why did I take you? What purpose did it serve? In the build to the biggest match of my career, such questions from you are insignificant and pale in comparison to what I have to say.” JJ moves the belt up on to his right shoulder. He taps the gleaming faceplate with his left knuckles. “Because at Monday Night Mayhem, I will become the second MWE X TV champion of the ‘nu’ era. And I am going to bring some credibility to this belt, something lost in the reign of Aeron Beowulf and those before him.” “Speaking of Aeron, it seems the champ isn’t here. I reckon that he lost his bark and his bite when I took his belt. It sounds like that crazy dog has packed up his balls and left at the thought of facing me. *JJ reaches across and off camera, picks up a pair of rusty scissors and gestures with them at the camera.* Or maybe he is having his balls surgically removed. *JJ throws the scissors off camera* Either way, I am going to take his title and destroy what was an impressive unbeaten streak in MWE.” “And the third factor in our match, Vega Knight, the Supernova. This guy has the biggest ego in the industry, forget “resident loudmouths” DC or Sinc Mercier, Knight could not get his head down the ramp to share a ring with those two! His confidence, his self-belief is incomparable, albeit unjustified for someone that has done nothing of any significance.” “But no doubt he will be a significant force in this match and in the MWE. I can’t believe that he won’t be up for this match. He will want to prove to the world, to himself, that his defeat to Sonny Williams was a one off that won’t be repeated. But in the end, beating him will be all the sweeter because this *he points to and then raises the belt towards the camera* is on the line.” JJ places the belt across his lap and rests his hands on it. JJ: “Shawn, you are probably wondering why I chose you as the vessel for this message. By taking you like I did, I saw you were innocent enough to understand my intentions in the entirety. There is no need to manipulate you. I don’t expect you to understand. I am not in the MWE to be understood, I am here to become the next X TV champion. On Monday night in Philadelphia, the judgement becomes final and I officially become the X TV champion!” “We will talk again Shawn.” JJ stands and lunges at the camera. The film ends with a big JJ boot smashing into the screen. The bizarre and sudden ending makes Sara and Covell jump. They both take a deep breath in relief that it is over. In front of them, Shawn is ghostly white and wide eyed. Covell lets out an exasperated sigh. Sara waves her hand in front of his eyes to no reaction. Covell: “Shawn!! Shawn!! Oh god, I need him to interview Mason Resoan tomorrow....Shawn!! Wake up mate.....” **Fade** *It ends slightly differenytly to how I planned but this accomodates an appropriate timeline to fit in with the second Mason RP*
