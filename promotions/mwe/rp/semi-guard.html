Semi-guard; Semi RP

May 8 2009

Jesse Anderson

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The scene begins in your typical, every day diner. A red and yellow tiled floor stretches across a small restaurant under several booths, a jukebox in the corner, and a chef station and cash register in the front. You can practically smell the pancakes. There is quiet chattering heard with soft country music playing over-head. A middle aged waitress with dark auburn hair that is put up in a bun, and wearing a yellow dress with a nametag that says "Betty" walks down the aisle with a pot of coffee in her hand. She walks up to a table and fills a cup before continuing on down the aisle. The newly filled cup of coffee belongs to the XTV Havoc Champion, the Semi. Semi is wearing a Hard Rock Cafe T-shirt and jeans. Semi is sitting across the table from his personal assistant, Dewey, who is wearing a white polo shirt and tan slacks. Semi, who is reading a newspaper, reaches out and grabs the cup of coffee in front of him and takes a sip, not taking his eyes from the newspaper. Semi: Better than the coffee at the arenas. That crap tastes like piss. Dewey flashes a look a Semi, then turns his attention out the window beside him. Semi takes another sip of the coffee and sets it down on the table in front of him. He continues to read. Dewey turns his eyes again to Semi quickly and back out the window as Semi catches his look. Semi lowers the newspaper a little and glares at Dewey. Semi: What? Dewey looks back to Semi with wide eyes. Dewey: I uh... nothing. Semi scowls and looks down his nose at Dewey. Semi: Don't give me that crap! You've been giving me weird looks all day! What did you do? Dewey snaps back quickly. Dewey: Nothing! ...I just... Dewey looks down at the table in front of him and bites his bottom lip. Dewey: I just don't know how you could... Semi: I could what?! Dewey: How you could join those people. The words spew from Dewey like the sealing of his own death sentence. He is very timid as always, with his eyes down and his arms crossed under the table in front of him. He looks clearly intimidated by, if not completely terrified of Semi. Semi glares at Dewey for a long moment. Semi: What people? Dewey: Va... Vanguard! A sly grin sweeps Semi's face momentarily before he replaces it with smugness. Dewey continues talking quickly. Dewey: Those guys are such jerks! Just last week they tripped me and... Semi cuts Dewey off abruptly. Semi: Whaaa! You better get over it real quick! Fact is, you don't get a say in my career path, you understand, twirp? I can leave you here with no job and no way to get home right now if you have a problem with that! Dewey shakes his head quickly and puts his hands up in front of him in haulting fashion. Dewey: No no no no! I'm just saying... Dewey is cut off again. Semi: You're not saying anything any more! I joined Vanguard because I wanted to, so deal with it. Dewey sighs and looks back out the window, but Semi continues talking. Semi: Here's a lesson for you, skinny. Make a friend or two everywhere you go. Who you know is usually more important than what you know or do. Semi talks a little less gruffly at Dewey and Dewey looks pleasantly surprised. Semi continues. Semi: You know what? Yeah, they are jerks. But hell, I find it much easier to see Cannon and Sharpmans' side of the coin than people like David Whitman and his grab-ass gang, or the lovable retard cousins. Dewey's eyes widen a little more at the last comment and he looks nothing short of miserably sad. Semi: Guys like Ace Cannon and Joseph Sharpman have not only stood on the top of the mountain but they've propped up their feet while they spit on the people below them. That right there, is the kind of people I want to associate myself with when it comes to my career; because quite frankly, people like you just don't make it very far. Dewey's eyes dart straight to the floor and his bottom lip quivers slightly. Semi sees this and backtracks a bit. Semi: Look, kid, I wasn't saying... Dewey snaps his frightened eyes back towards Semi's as Semi barks across the table loudly. Semi: Look dammit! You've done pretty good carrying my bags and getting my coffee. In fact, you've done everything I've told you to. All I was saying is that you wouldn't be much in the wrestling business. Taking orders and staying out of the way isn't the way to make it around here. By this time, Dewey has perked up a little and is watching Semi as he listens. Semi: To make it in this business you have to make yourself known. Well known. You have to put your face and voice everywhere it can be seen or heard. You have to stand out from everyone else. You have to do whatever it takes to make sure that the eyes and the ears of every person in that building and every person watching on TV are turned towards you. Some people like to do that by praising the fans and doing their bidding; but guys like me just like to take action. When it comes to taking action; kicking ass and making names, I'm rolling with the guys that kicked the pregnant bitch. I like that. Semi stops talking and turns his eyes up to meet the waitress, Betty's. He looks around quickly, embarrassed, and turns smugly back towards Betty. Semi: Well it's about damn time! We're ready to order! Betty loudly sighs and smiles sarcastically. Her face drops again as she lifts a yellow pad a mini pencil. Betty: Go ahead then. Semi: I'll have the steak and eggs special. Semi turns his attention towards Dewey. Semi: What about you, squirt? Dewey: Um... scrambled eggs and bacon. Wheat toast. Betty scribbles on her pad, turns, and brisquely walks away. Semi wrinkles his forehead at Dewey and talks loudly. Semi: Scrambled eggs and wheat toast? Really? No wonder you look like a lamp post. I need to teach you to eat like a real man! All this time, I just thought you were a crackhead. Semi takes a sip of coffee as Dewey's jaw drops and he shakes his head, 'no!' Semi takes the newspaper on the table in front of him and refolds it. He tosses it to the side of the table against the wall and it flops lazily on top of a wire basket holding salt, pepper, ketchup, and other various eating essentials. Semi clears his throat and turns back towards Dewey, looking thoughtful. Dewey notices Semi's expression and looks as if a weight has been temporarily lifted from him. Semi: Anyway, as I was saying; I like these Vanguard guys. They're all about righting all of the wrongs in the wrestling business today. Man, there's a lot of them. I mean, just think; Semi isn't even world champion of MWE. How rediculous is that?! Dewey rolls his eyes and releases a small gasp of air through his nose. Semi: Um, I digress. But I'll tell you one wrong that's going to be made right at Monday Night Mayhem. The Semi is going to make sure no one ever claims to be the top ICW graduate in MWE again. Dewey: Uh... I don't recall Peter saying... Yet again, Semi cuts Dewey off in mid-sentence. Semi: Shut up! Another thing I'll teach you is to shut up and listen when I speak! Semi shouts loudly enough for the whole restaurant to hear. Dewey's eyes grow very wide for a moment and most of his face turns pink as he lowers his head. Semi: NOW! ... The point is, guys like Peter Kaymakcian underestimate me because they too were a part of something only a few people have done, and that was move to MWE from ICW and stay there. Guys like Peter K think that puts them in my league. Dewey nods his head in agreement but still looks afraid. Semi: Guys like Peter K, Blood Fuze, Chris England, and the other ICW grads need to get this down right now. The Semi is the measuring stick to which they are compared. I was the first ICW champion. I was the longest ICW champion. I was undefeated in ICW and am now undefeated in MWE. I am the XTV Havoc Champion and the WHQ World Champion. Dewey continues nodding with his eyes off to the side, like he's hearing this for the thousandth time. Semi: My credentials alone are enough to crush Peter Kaymakcian. I mean come on! We're talking about a guy that's typically seen walking around in cartoon underwear and thinks he can talk to God! This is what he should be doing right now. Semi begins talking quickly, to his hand. Semi: Yes, hello, God? Hi. Please don't make me face Semi at Monday Night Mayhem! I know I've talked a lot of trash about you and Semi and everybody else, but I know that it's just because I'm insecure and have self-confidence issues. But if you just make this match go away, I'll stop drinking and jerking off, I promise! Semi drops his hand back to the table and lets out a quick laugh at his own joke. Dewey cracks a smile as well, but as he does, Semi begins talking again, now with anger in his voice. Semi: Peter Kaymakcian may have had some luck and success in the past, but he's going to suffer the same fate as everyone in his place before him. He is going to be ran down by the Semi that is now fine tuned by the Vanguard. Semi smiles a big, sadistic grin as Dewey looks worried, but is still nodding his head at Semi's every word. At this point, Betty walks back up to the table, carrying two plates of food. She sets them down in front of Semi and Dewey. Semi: Finally! Did you raise the damn chicken to lay the eggs first? Betty: No sir, we just had to wait for the food to actually cook. I apologize. She says this with a very dry wit that obviously angers Semi. Semi: Get me some new coffee while you're here! Betty: I'm not carrying the coffee pot, hun. I'll fill it up when I can. Betty storms away and you can hear her swear under her breath. Semi shakes his head and grabs the fork from beside his plate. Dewey spreads some grape jelly on his toast. Semi cuts and stabs a large chunk of gravy-soaked steak and some egg and holds it up towards Dewey. Semi: Now this is real food! Dewey bites the corner from one slice of toast as Semi shoves the heaping fork full into his mouth and begins to chew loudly and sloppily. Semi tries to talk as he eats and bits and pieces of steak and egg spray Dewey as he squints and leans back in his chair. Semi: I'n gona fix you wight up, scwooey! You betta coun on it! Semi swallows his food loudly as the scene fades out.
