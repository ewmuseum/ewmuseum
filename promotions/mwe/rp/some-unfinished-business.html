Some Unfinished Business

Jul 23 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A lone cop walked into an abandoned warehouse. He wasn't what you called a good cop. He was very much, a bad cop. A young gun to be exact. Hated playing by the rules. His clothes, (not a cop uniform, as he just always wore a suite) had some bloodstains on it. He was in a bit of a scuffle, and had shot his target at close range. But for what reasons was this one cop here for? Well it was because he was part of something bigger. At least, in his opinion. However, before he could enter into the place of the warehouse he wanted to enter, he was stopped by a hooded figure. -Hooded Figure- Your entry pass to this place...? The cop rolled up his sleeve, to reveal a Pentagram brand. The hooded figure nodded, and let the cop pass. The cop smiled. He continued to walk, until he came to the back of the room, where there was a beheaded statues of religious men, and broken crosses. In the middle of the mess, and lit up candlesticks, was a man, in the middle of a massive pentagram circle. He wore mostly black, and spikes were on his shoulders. He rose up, and his fire red hair, could be seen. He turned around, and smiled, as the two men embraced each other. The man, that had the fire red hair, was Leon 'The Virus' Roberts. -Leon- Glad you could find some time to return Jase. -Jase- Well you know I wouldn't abandon you, big brother. -Leon- I know. You're looking like you had a good day at work. -Jase- Would have been better if I shot the fucker in the head. The thief busted my balls for a minute. -Leon- Yeah, he deserved to be hurt majorly. Even a shot in the head. I myself would have pointed the gun south of the border. -Jase- I'll think about it next time. But what's this I hear about UUW? Is it....? -Leon- Yeah. It's been bought out. MWE is back. -Jase- Hmmm...you don't seem all that excited for it. -Leon- You're right, I'm not, See...I thought I left that part of me behind. Back when I didn't have so much focus on the task at hand. -Jase- Ah. Well I personally can only see nothing but good...for us...come out of this. -Leon- I don't. MWE is full of people who have it out to prevent me. It's going to be a hard struggle to make the ideal world according to my predecessor. -Jase- Well there isn't much you can do about it, except push back harder. Adversity has never been something you concerned yourself with. Especially since your rebirth. -Leon- My rebirth....HA! Yeah, it's all been good. I wouldn't have such strength if it weren't for it. Leon sighed. He stepped out of his symbol, and rubbed the back of his hands. The self inflicted brands he put on them were symbols of who he is. But there was something bothering him. Jase sensed it. -Jase- This isn't about MWE re-opening, is it? -Leon- No. It's about unfinished business. On one hand, yeah I got plenty of unfinished business with MWE, but even more so with UUW. I had Zack Crash right where I wanted him. Where he was to be humiliated, and beaten, and exposed for the villain he is. But he somehow fought back. He wasn't supposed to, and now, he's dealt his cards. But at least now I don't have to worry about going good. -Jase- You....good? What an oxymoron. HA! -Leon- Yeah. Well...his version of good. The type that kisses ass, and helps people. My good, is all about having fun, and raising anarchy, so that the world I want can exist. But if Zack Crash is willing to still fight, under the same circumstances, I guess I can oblige and keep my end of the bargain. -Jase- I know you won't lose. But you know, with MWE, your goals will now have a higher platform to to use. So just think about it. With a well known name, and more travel, your message will be reached to a much wider audience. That way, eventually, the world will be at your mercy a lot quicker if it was UUW. Hell, a lot of it has already thrown itself into anarchy. Remember how much fun we hade at the G20 summit? We helped with those riots, and it was nothing but pure anarchy. I loved it. Only wish it was even more violent. -Leon- Got that right Jase. Thanks. You certainly have helped me see things in a clearer perspective. -Jase- Anytime. -Leon- Now...I do indeed have a message for MWE. And for my cocksucking opponent, Zak Warner. Let's send it. It's time they paid more attention to me, whether they like it or not. Both men walked away from the ritual zone of the warehouse, and into Leon's office. When they were in there, Jase pulled out a camera from a cabinet. Leon sighed, knowing that Jase somehow knew where the camera was all the time. But it didn't matter. Right now, he had a message to send. -Leon- So this is where we find ourselves once again. Back on the wings of a phoenix. A phoenix, that has a name, echoed through the wrestling community, like a myth. That name, is MWE. Oh sure, a lot of people are happy about it. But what about those who aren't. Especially since the guy who apparently owns MWE now, is who I believe, is the reason it's second run ended in the first place. Yeah, I'm talking about D.C. I have little doubt that he will run the company into the ground. But then again, why the fuck should I care about that? I'm the goddamn devil's incarnate for fuck's sake! Okay. This might be a little early for Leon to get worked up about something. It was only noon afterall. Yes, Jase didn't exactly have a full day yet, but who cares? Leon's pissed off. Yeah, it's a normal, but it's more expected in a couple minutes. But again, not surprising. What is surprising, is that he is able to regain his composure, before his eyes begin to flash. -Leon- But MWE is back, and with that, the end of UUW. Another company that was shafted due to lack of success. People had lacked the motivation, or were to fucken intimidated by me to work properly. But oh well. Things happen. It's all part of the universal chaos theory, that shit happens. But you know...Perhaps I can use MWE once again as my platform, in reforming this pathetic world into a better one. Oh yes. My world, of anarchy and chaos. Fairness at it's most extreme. Survival of the fittest, being the only rule. Leon stopped for a second, to give that ever dramatic pause that every good promo needs once in a while when they happen. Although he had other intentions, as he pulled out a chair, and sat down, before looking straight at the camera, a smirk ever present on his face. -Leon- As we all know, I have been trying to create this world for quite some time. Why do I want to do this? It's because everything else is a big fucken joke. Morals, religion, laws...easily bent to one's will just to gain an advantage. Stuff that needs to be exposed. Even the so called good guys and heroes. They're nothing more then the biggest frauds of them all, because when it comes down to it, they're not doing what they do for the people. They're doing it for themselves. Don't believe me? Then let me ask you this. If Jesus Christ really was the son of god, he could have easily have escaped from his fate, but he chose to die, to show that he and his father were there for the people. It made him famous. He did it for the fame. That's right bitches....I just exposed the fucken truth about the crucification. Don't like it, suck my super sized balls for a while. Leon stopped for a minute, his smirk extremely twisted now. He just went there. He just crossed a line, and didn't even think twice about it. Of course, Leon being Leon, he personally felt that the morality and laws that exist are nothing but bullshit anyways, so he felt that not only was it his right to do so, but he was more then awesome enough to do so. But then, all of a sudden, the mood really darkened, as he stopped smirking, and snarled, almost growling like a lion on the hunt. -Leon- But enough about that. Because right now, I have to focus on somebody who's been nothing but a thorn in my side since I began my career. Zak fucken Warner. I hate him so much. The fact that this pompus assfucker gets so much hype and attention just for being around sickens me. This is a guy who works when he feels like it, and for some fucken reason, when he does, everyone praises him for it. But when he decides to just fuck off, people are always optimistic he'll return. What about the hard working ones like me? It's because of people like him, that forces me to go to upstart places to dominate. To elevate them. And I hate it so fucken much. I hate the fact that people like him have the world in their hands, while doing absolutely nothing to obtain it, other then just being witty when they get there. Yeah, at one time he may have worked hard for his achievements, but...he doesn't give a fuck about them much now. Well....no...maybe he does give a fuck, and perhaps somewhat deserve some, but not all. Leon stopped there. He had to think things through here. He had to pace himself, for he knew the answers to his problems with Warner would eventually come to him. Luckily, they did, as a slight smirk appeared. The camera almost didn't catch it. -Leon- But perhaps this goes beyond him just being regular old cockblock. Maybe the reason he makes sure I don't succeed, is because I chose him as my first target. He's pissed off, that some little wrestler, who everyone knew was destined for greatness, chose him to be his stepping stone to become bigger then he was. Get over it Zak, I wanted somebody who I felt I could benefit from facing. From defeating. From harming. That was you Warner. Although, I get that if you were politiking to screw me over constantly, it's a bit personal. It's not the fact I went after you first, it's the fact, that I almost killed you in the ring, the first time we went one on one. Not in the sense where you got humiliated by me, but the fact that I did almost kill you, when I gave you multiple injuries that one match of ours, even nearly snapping your neck. When I heard those bones pop, and you writhed in pain, it excited me. You knew I enjoyed wanting to seriously hurt you. Hell, you and I both know, that the entire reason you're so doped up all the time from the amount of drugs you do, is because of me. Before, you were drug free. Straight-edge. After that one fateful night, back in early 2008, you needed pain killers. It brought you back into your addictions of the past. But even pain killers weren't enough, as you have smoked more marijuana and snorted more cocaine then anyone else I knew, even me. I should know, I once had a deep love affair with the white lady, when I was only fourteen. It's an escape from the reality of the physical pain I already put you through. Oh I know Zak. You will say that what I did hardly affected you. But you and I both know the truth. That your life was changed that day. Since then, we've shared wins and losses. Leon began to chuckle like mad. Hell, even his fingers began to dig into his wooden desk, leaving deep marks in it. His anticipation was rising in numerous levels, and he honestly could not wait for his match. He had planned on doing more damage then last time. -Leon- But now, your final match against me, or maybe even your final match in general, is coming close. Once I massacre you worse then ever, even far fucken worse then what I did to you during our last match....which I begrudgingly admit you won...you'll be so fucked up, that what you do to yourself now, to get away from all the physical pain that you suffer through now, will be far worse, that you'll overdose like Andrew Martin did, and we'll never have to deal with your fucken sorry ass again! And I'll brag about it, that I was basically responsible for it. People will hate me for it, but I want them to. I get off on people hating me, for my actions. It just proves to me how pathetic and feeble people are. And it makes me more and more justified to proudly say that I am above humanity. I have proven time and time again, that I do whatever the fuck I want to do, and the consequences can kiss my ass. And come our match, well it will just be another day for me. But this day will be special, because I refuse to deal with you again Zak. I have much bigger things to deal with. In the sense, that I plan to expose Zack Crash for the villain that he is. He's no better then me. When I finally get my revenge, and bring him to my level, one of the many parts towards my plans for a new world will finally be completed. Leon pauses for a minute. It's mainly to catch his breath, and to cool off the the self induced adrenaline high he put himself into. He had to pace himself, because he didn't want to get to excited over beating the fuck out of Zak Warner. That's not to say he didn't look forward to it, as he obviously is very much so. -Leon- Right now Warner, you're just a flyspeck. At the most, you're an annoying itch I can't seem to get rid of. After I dealt with you Warner, I bring Crash down to my level, and then, after I have Crash under my command, I shall take what will be mine, and that, is the MWE world heavyweight championship! Then end of Warner is upon us all. Freedom from an annoying prick, is but a sweet feeling. You'll thank me later, and I'll just say that I told you all so. So Zak. I'm talking to you one last time boy. Clean yourself. Detoxify your ass. Because you'll need to be sober to face me, and maybe defeat me...by disqualification or countout. I'll see you then Zak, because right now, I myself, am going to prepare myself, just to make sure I deal with you...once and for all. Leon chuckles one last time. Oh his eyes are flashing between crimson and gold now. If anyone else was in the room, aside from Jase, they would have shit themselves due to the sheer feeling of killer intent that Leon is emitting. It's a gnarly feeling, as you know Leon would kill you if he felt like it. But it is clear, that Leon was done with his message. Now, he had to wait. But patience was not one of Leon's strong points, so don't be surprised if something is badly thrashes something Fade.
