Micheals Inc. *The scene opens in Peter’s mansion in South Beach Florida. The weather is beautiful and there are three gorgeous women running around Peter’s backyard. He has on black sunglasses and seems to be working on his already near perfect tan. The girls run up to Peter. The other girls laugh as thinking that will put them in with Peter.* Peter: Did you even try looking? *Alicia in a scared and confused voice.* Alicia: No. Peter: How'd you expect to find it? Next time look before you ask dumb ass. After your done leave my mansion! *Allison and Jennifer are standing there looking seductively at Peter. Allison is blonde, while Jennifer looks like she is from Hawaii or from some part of Asia.* *Just as Peter goes to make an advance towards the to beautiful ladies Becky comes rushing in. Peter's stalker and obsessed fan comes in to ruin another great chance for Peter to make love to some beautiful women. Peter is still getting around, but this is the tenth time this week Becky has interfered in Peter's plans. Becky runs in and knocks Allison down first. This isn't a promo Becky, this isn't anywhere near a promo, it is all........ME! Do you comprehend? *Becky just smiles at Peter and slaps him on the arm. In a cute and vague tone Becky responds.* Becky: I got you. *Becky walks towards where Jennifer is lying down in pain. She looks down at her furiously. Becky begins to yell at her just as she did with Allison.* Becky: Hey, you get off my future-husband's property. He hates you and hates everything about you. Leave NOW! *Peter looks at Becky stunned and wonders how someone can be so oblivious to the others around them. He shakes his head and then walks off towards his limo. Becky runs after him screaming from down the hall.* Becky: Where are you going? Can I come? Okay I'll just meet you at the hotel. *He ignores as he opens the front door of his mansion. He walks down the gray steps and towards the limo waiting right in front of his mansion. He passes by two gargoyles that sit on the right and left side at the bottom of his rail for the steps. He walks towards the door of his limo where the limo driver/assistant named Paul opens up the door for him and lets him in. Paul stands at about 6'4" and doesn't look very intimidating. He has on a green jacket and hat and looks fairly old. He gets in on the driver seat of the limo and they start to drive off. After a few minutes Peter's phone begins to ring and it is none other then T.O. calling him.* Peter: My boy, T.O. how's it goin'? T.O.: You know what I do? What about you? He flips his before answering and Paul mutters, "Idiot."* Peter: I am facing some fool named Virus or Bacteria. It's whateva though, man because his in ring skills is the equivalent of Romo in the playoffs. *T.O. responds in a high pitched comedic voice.* T.O.: Damn that bad? Man, MWE needs to recognize your talent. Peter: You telling me? Man, I have to go up against Romo and Jessica this week. I feel like you on the Cowboys, un-utilized and disrespected. Seriously everyone knows who I am, but does anyone have a clue about who Virus is? Better question, does anyone even care about Virus? I guess MWE figures that after I beat Virus I'll go after the tag titles and single handily save the MWE tag team division. Just wait for the things I say about, religion, family values, and other stuff on the tonight show. Alright dude I'll call you when I land. He reluctantly decides to confront Peter about his plans for the tonight show. He talks in a cool and calm voice.* Paul: Listen sir, I don't think that it is in your best interest to talk about these things on the tonight show. Don't you think that you've started up enough trouble with your comments on England, God, and so on and so on. *Peter's smile turns instantly into a frown and he lashes out at Paul. The normally calm and joking Peter begins to berate Paul and his God.* Peter: Listen to me jackass, you're paid to drive, not to TALK got it! I will SAY whateva the hell I please. YOUR God is a pathetic figment of your imagination. All those "stories" are nothing, but a bunch of lies or misconceptions. Oh father I know that they are KILLING ME, but please forgive them. Also if Jebus was God's son why the FUCK would he have him get tortured like that? There is no God and there is no higher being then me Peter Kaymakcian. GOT IT?!? *Paul just shakes his head in anger and he puts up the black window that separates the driver side of the limo and the passenger side. Peter lies down in the back seat of his limo and decides to take a little nap.* {A few hours later}(this includes getting on the plane and the plane trip) *We see a jet begin to land at JFK International. The jet stops and there is crowd of people sitting there with cameras and there are plenty of interviewers. The most notable thing we see is a stretch limo in Buffalo Bills colors and the number 81 on it. It seems T.O. is interested in picking Peter up from the airport. The jet door swings open. We hear the sound of a thousand cameras taking pictures going off. Camera lights going off everywhere. The plane stares finally connect at the bottom of Peter's jet door and Peter walks out with a leather jacket on and sunglasses. He is now wearing a plain white-t and some loose fitting jeans. Peter then continues on joking around. This time he is mocking Virus.* Peter: Alright so I'm like a vampire, who is "friends" with some dude named Vega. Do you know who I am? *T.O. starts to crack up as Peter is in a goofy, but serious looking face. T.O. while laughing tries to answer.* T.O.: Count....... Suckula? My God I am always forgetting the non-important people. Um your name is Bacteria! *Both men begins to crack up as T.O.'s limo driver yells we are here. Peter stretches and exits T.O.'s limo.
