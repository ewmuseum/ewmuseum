Just a little pick me up.; Alejandro Rodriguez RP ©

Sep 20 2008

Alejandro Rodriguez

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The scene opens in a bar in Columbus, Ohio. The camera pans around the bar showing groups of friends laughing over drinks, slutty girls trying to woo college guys at another table, drunken bums at the bar nearly falling over themselves to collect one dollar that lay forgotten on the floor and one man, drinking alone, just with himself and his thoughts, this man was Alejandro Rodriguez. He is wearing his usual generic clothing, jeans, a t-shirt and a black mask which concealed most of his face. He continues ordering drinks from the barman. Alejandro: Another shot, when you’re ready. The slightly hunch-backed barman came stumbling along and poured Alejandro another shot of whisky. Alejandro stared at the barman and drained it in one, still looking at the barman, he slammed it down. The barman looked at Alejandro too, and for a few seconds that was all that they did. Alejandro: Another. One of the college guys, who had just disentangled himself from his pack of friends, slumped himself into a chair behind Alejandro with his drink as Alejandro was served another shot. Alejandro took no notice of him and drained his shot in one again. The college guy cleared his throat and Alejandro turned the face him. When Alejandro spoke his voice was slurring. Alejandro: What. CollegeGuy: You’re Alejandro Rodriguez. Alejandro grabs the man’s drink and drains it in one. Alejandro: So what. CollegeGuy: You lost to Kanto… Alejandro seizes the man by the scruff of the neck and pulls him close to Alejandro’s face. The man tries to get away as Alejandro’s heavy breathing smelling of alcohol and his angry face prove too much for him. His friends all look concerned as Alejandro pushes the college guy off his stool. The hunch-backed barman, who seemed not to notice this, poured Alejandro another drink. Alejandro: Thank you. The doors of the bar burst open and closed again, Alejandro seemed not to notice this until Darrel Jones slumped on the opposite side of Alejandro, and he seemed concerned at the college guy on the floor, his drunkenness and the force of the fall seemed to have knocked him out cold. Darrel: Hey Al… God what happened to that guy? Alejandro: Must have slipped. Hey barman, get this guy a drink. The barman nodded and poured Darrel a shot and slammed it down in front of him. Darrel looked at it as if it was poisonous, he smelt the drink, and then put it down immediately. Darrel: What are you doing here? Alejandro doesn’t answer this immediately. Alejandro: Drinking. Darrel: But why? Alejandro: Drowning my sorrows. Darrel: Because you lost to Kanto? Come on Alejandro, you’ve faced worse than this before. You’ve got to concentrate Al, you’ve got a big match coming up, Sonny Williams is the MWE World Champion! Alejandro’s face contorts up in confusion. Alejandro: Sonny’s the World Champ? Darrel: You didn’t know? Alejandro: I had no idea… Darrel: Well, that’s exactly why you need to concentrate! Alejandro pauses. Alejandro: Wait, what are you talking about? Darrel sighs. Darrel: You’re facing Sonny Williams this week! Alejandro: … oh. Darrel: Oh!?! Come on Alejandro, Sonny is a serious threat! ARE YOU EVEN LISTENING TO ME!?! Alejandro: Sorry? … lets get out of here man. Alejandro gets up, drains his own drink, drains Darrel’s and slams down 100 dollars on the bar counter. Alejandro: Lets go. The two of them walk out of the bar, we find that it is mid-day. Alejandro has a slight stumble in his step but apart from that he doesn’t seem too bad. Darrel hails a cab, which stops right in front of them. Alejandro gets in first, followed by Darrel. Alejandro: Take us to the strip club on Lake Street. Darrel looks at Alejandro in partial disgust and he talks to the cabbie. Darrel: No.. Take us to the Value City Arena. Alejandro rests on the window ledge and closes his eyes. Alejandro: …party pooper. Darrel smirks for a second. Darrel: It’s for your own good. Alejandro ignores him, he sits up straight as the cab begins driving by a lake. The cabbie looks in the mirror at Alejandro. Cabbie: You’re Alejandro Rodriguez. Alejandro sighs. Alejandro: So what. Cabbie: You lost to Kanto. Darrel puts his head in his hands as Alejandro gets riled up. Alejandro: Alright, that’s it.. Pull over, now! The cabbie pulls over as told to, the cabbie looks at his pay-meter. Cabbie: That’ll be… Alejandro: Fuck that, lowlife. Alejandro continues the rant even after he leaves the car, with Darrel, by shouting through the open window. Alejandro: Why don’t you get a real fucking job. I was taken the piss out of people like you when I was living on the streets, and you still do it now! Well fuck that you god-damned- Alejandro cuts off when the cab begins to drive off. Alejandro runs as best he can after it, stumbling and almost falling over. Alejandro: FUCK YOU! Alejandro walks back towards the lake, where Darrel is sitting on a wall, Alejandro joins him. Alejandro: So why did you come and see me anyway? Darrel: Well Stacy wanted me to. Alejandro: Stacy did… why? Darrel: Well you haven’t seen your daughter and your girlfriend for a day and a half because you’ve been drowning your sorrows in that shitty bar. Alejandro pauses and bows his head. Alejandro: I’m sorry. Darrel: It’s not me you should be saying sorry to. Alejandro: I know. They remain in silence for a while. Alejandro: Why did you want us to go to the arena then? Darrel: Well, seeing your family isn’t the only thing you have to do, you’ve got to do another interview segment with me. But now I‘m not so sure… Alejandro remains silent. Darrel: I should ring Covell and tell him you can’t- Alejandro: I can do it. Darrel: I’m sorry? Alejandro ignores him. Alejandro: I just need a pick me up. Darrel: If you’re talking about alcohol- Alejandro ignores him again and gives Darrel 100 bucks, he points to a clothes shop. Alejandro: Go into that shop and get me some fresh clothes. Darrel looks startled. Darrel: But… why? Alejandro: Don’t ask questions, just do it. The camera focuses on Darrel, who shrugs and walks across the road. Alejandro talks from off-screen. Alejandro: And keep this safe for me! Alejandro’s mask hits Darrel on the head, Darrel grabs it, looks astonished and he turns to face Alejandro. Alejandro is standing on the wall, facing the lake, the back of his head is visible, he has very short hair. Alejandro dives off the wall and a splash confirmed he has jumped into the lake. Darrel looks shocked and he runs towards the clothes shop. An Hour Later… Alejandro and Darrel are stood inside the Value City Arena. Darrel is holding a microphone and the camera crew seem to be taking the last minute checks for the interview, Alejandro is wearing his fresh clothes, a white shirt and black pants. The camera crew seem ready to start, Alejandro and Darrel both not and the cameras roll. Darrel: Ladies and Gentlemen, this is Darrel Jones reporting from the Value City Arena alongside with Alejandro Rodriguez. How are you Alejandro. Alejandro: I’m not bad Darrel, not bad at all. Darrel: Well that’s great to hear Alejandro. Now last week, you came close to defeating Kanto, but close was not close enough unfortunately, how do you feel about your loss. Alejandro: That loss really hit home, no-one’s ever done what he has done to me in the ring, I just started doubting if I could really beat him at all. Poeple in the street now fucking know me as the guy who lost to Kanto, I'm sick of it. But I’ve totally realised that his victory this last time, was my mistake, not his triumph and that‘s what makes all the difference. I know that if I stepped in the ring with him again, I’d be more successful, I’ve learned things about him, now I know what to do, I know how to beat him. Unfortunately for now, I cant. He was released to deal with his personal life? God, talk about 15 minutes of fame. I’m surprised he even has a personal life, who’d want to counteract with a freak like that? I’m not sorry to see him go, and I doubt anyone else is for that matter, so lets not talk about him, lets talk about the future. Darrel: Ok then. This week as you know you face against a guy who you’ve had a lot of history with, none other than the World Champion Sonny Williams, what are your thoughts on him becoming the MWE Champ? Alejandro: That guy has already disgraced this company with his title reigns once before, and I hate to say that it’s going to happen again, but I guarun-damn-tee that I will do everything in my power to make sure I knock that guy off the top-shot as soon as I can, because I can tell you and I am sure a lot of people would agree with me that he doesn’t deserve to be there at all. All this smack he talks, it’s going to come back and it’s going to bite him right on the ass when I shove my foot up it this Monday. That ain’t a threat, it isn’t a promise, promises like that of which Sonny Williams never lives up to, it‘s a guaranteed fact, Sonny Williams is going to get his ass handed to him. Darrel: Pretty strong words there Al, earlier in the week, Sonny had a lot of strong things to say to you. Alejandro: Yeah, like I didn’t already see that coming. Same old Sonny. He talks a load of smack and he doesn’t back it up. He thinks we’re 4 and 0? Ha-ha. That figure gets higher every single time I look at it, didn’t it like used to be 3 and 0 like a week ago or something? And the good old line “apart from that draw that counts for nothing” you mean the draw when I knocked him out cold after delivering the most devastating move in all of MWE history on him? Oh yeah, must have been that one, Sonny has never knocked me out? Shows you how much of a champ he is. Well good luck champ, because whatever bullshit figure you say next, it’s going to have a big fat 1 next to it, and when I get my title shot, a huge number 2 and then he‘ll want his re-match, it’ll be a juicy 3 and then because I know he loves to complain and moan till he turns blue in the face he‘ll get another shot, so then it goes up to mother-fucking four. And I will tell him this, I will knock him out all those fucking times. You can mourn your brother all you want, you can cuss your Mother all you want, you can run your mouth all you fucking want. And in time, you will ... LEARN… TO… FLY! Alejandro glances at Darrel and laughs. Alejandro: CHA CHING! Alejandro walks out of view and jumps back into view. Alejandro: …bitch! The scene fades to black.
