Lights! Camera! Action!...I guess?; Anthony Clark Rp.

May 6 2009

Anthony Clark

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The screen opens. When the screen opens we see a dark wooded area. There is trees all around the area. The leaves all over the ground. The full moon is peaking threw the foggy air threw the trees. You can hear the faint howls of the owls outside in the woods. Soon those hoots of the owls are interrupted by screams of a young teenage girl ranging in her late teens. She is running threw the woods screaming. He jeans are all ripped drenched in blood. Her white beater is soaking wet, and also covered in blood. She is screaming in terror as the camera shows a undead man following her. She falls down the ground, and the zombie walks over to her groaning. The Girl: Oh god....Oh god no!! The zombie gets closer to her as he groans. His cloths are ripped to shreds, and his face is decaying as blood, and puss pour out of his skin. Some of his skeleton is showing off as he is leaning in towards the girl. Zombie: Brraaaain! BRAIN! As the zombie leans down to bite the screaming female. You hear a loud noise. BANG! A loud shot gun pop fires across the screen knocking the zombie off the screaming girl. The zombies head blown into millions of pieces it looks like, and the girl is doused with blood yet again. Walking over you see a young man walk over. He has on a cut off sleeve red, and white plaid button up shirt with a pair of ripped up jeans. He blows the smoke off the barrel of the shot gun as he looks at the young girl. The Man: I’m Anthony Clark...Come with me if you want to live. Anthony would shake his head then, and say. Anthony Clark: Wait wait wait. Cut! I’m not saying this line. That is terrible. I’ll just be ripping off Arnold from Terminator if I drop that line. Do I look like a joke? The camera would turn to show who Anthony is talking to, and you would see an entire film production crew. The director would look pissed off at Anthony for ruining the shot, and wasting the film for the shot. Gus The Director: Cut! God dammit Clark! You are way to picky of an entertainer! Anthony Clark: No Gus, I’m not! I’m a true entertainer! I know what the fans of Anthony Clark want. I know they want to see me dropping the lines like I always do. They don’t wanna see me ripping off some hack who can’t act anymore. Gus, don’t you know who I am. I’m the next big thing in the entertainment business. I’m the next big star. I mean hell I’m getting paid more then you. The crew would be cleaning up the set trying to get ready for re-shooting. The once zombie would get up to show that he is an actor in make up. You would see the actor take off the fake prosthetic head as he gets up showing off his real face. The young girl would get up, and as she goes to walk away Anthony would smack her ass. She’d walk away showing a look of disgust on her face then. Anthony walks away grabbing a towel, and bottle of water. He’d wipe the sweat off of his face, and take a drink. Gus would run over. Gus The Director: Anthony do you know how much film you have wasted during shooting of this film!? Anthony Clark: Gus... Perfection usually takes time. And believe me I know perfection. And the only reason I keep making it look bad, is cause you are screwing it up for me. These lines are terrible that you gave me. The writers might as be a group of 10 year olds. I’m not a bad actor Gus. Your just a shitty director. Anthony would smile big as he walks over to his trailer. As he does you would see cast, and crew run over to stop Gus who is about to attack Anthony after taking month of Anthony’s ridiculing abuse. Anthony has no idea as he walks away grinning from ear to ear. He would walk into his trailer closing the door, as you would hear Gus screaming outside the trailer. When Anthony walks in you would see a lush, high dollar trailer. He would sit down on his black leather couch, and he’d sit down beside his girlfriend Jessica Richards. She would be sitting there in a jean skirt, with a white beater on. She would be texting, as she is holding her tiny dog. Anthony Clark: So what’s next on my schedule. Jessica Richards: You are wrestling in MWE. You have to be in Paris France for it. Anthony Clark: God I have France. It’s so dirty. A bunch of cowards live there. Probably where all of my opponents are from haha. Anthony would lean his head back, and close his eyes. Anthony Clark: You know wrestling is something I have known for years. I know that, you know that Jess. This whole world knows that. I took a break for a while. I got into film. I became more of a movie guy. Everyone knows what happened with XHF, and GWC. I mean I was a champion in both. I was taking titles left, and right in both. I was once half of the longest reigning tag team champions in the GWC. I won their Ultimate title. I put my body in hardcore matches for that title. I brought my own version of hardcore known as Ultraviolence to GWC. I was starting a revolution there with a group of others while I was there. He would take a deep breath, and he would lean his head down some. Anthony Clark: XHF... I didn’t do as much as I did in the GWC. It was more like a second home. I mean I won the Phoenix title there, and I won their European championship there. I wasn’t a joke ladies, and gents. And I didn’t take things lightly. You know I put myself threw blood, sweat, and tears in this industry... And I still keep out the best looking in the ring, and best looking physically. Anthony would look up with a cocky smile on his face then. He’d take a drink out of the water, and use his towel to wipe his mouth. Anthony Clark: Now I arrive to this new fed.... This new place of sorts... The MWE. Do I call it home yet? No. Do I call it the new place to trust? No. Do I call it the new place where I reign supreme as the best looking man in the company? You better believe it son! Haha. You see I don’t just bring my in ring ability to this company... Which well lets face facts is a very important thing. But the most important thing I bring to this company is a face you can show off. I’m not a barbarian looking goof that the MWE posts of their poster. I’m not a clown. I’m not a muscle bound freak. I’m all American. All natural. And all around better then everyone else. If the first thing I have noticed about my first match in the MWE.... It’s I’m more focused then my own opponents. Anthony would get up, and walk outside of his trailer. His girlfriend Jessica would follow as they go over to a limo parked outside of the studio. Anthony Clark: People think I’m too busy with my movie career. They are highly wrong. All I’m doing is building press for myself. Why you ask... I’ll tell you why MWE fans. Anthony Clark is worth the press. I’m worth every second of it. Just like my films, my matches are blockbusters. I’m not just the Total Sexstasy. I’m also the Total Money Maker! The cocky, and very smooth Anthony Clark gets into his limo with Jessica Richards. The camera man quickly follow them in. Anthony Clark: So I look at my opponents, and my first impressions... Not impressed. Not impressed one bit. First off there is young “dark” Blight. First off what’s with the name. B-Light? Randy Blythe of Lamb Of God. Bright Light? Hmm I did like Light Brights. But back to Blight. You walk around with the mask on for what? To cover your face cause this world is just to dark, and cold for you? You keep the long hair to cover your eyes from the pain? Is that what it is? If anything all you really seem like to me is a really bad album made by The Cure. Which is weird cause I like them. Hmm....Getting off track again. Anthony would give a little chuckle then. Anthony Clark: So you have a bloodlust huh? So what I have a lust for women. But I don’t tell them. I show them. Which is what I do every night all night haha. What is it Blight? Are you a dark misunderstood person? Frankly I don’t know why I’m wasting time on you. Go put on your guy liner and cry. Anthony would look at his girlfriend who isn’t paying attention as she is texting still while reading a magazine. Anthony Clark: Apparently “Sin” has a history with this Blight guy. I don’t know, and honestly I don’t care. I’m more worried about the girls I’m with after I win the match. Not your boring history. And who said any of us are being “hardcore”. You know there are a lot of these guys who have taken a chair shot, or got thrown into the steps and they consider themself “Hardcore”. Hardcore is a word that is thrown around in wrestling freely, but never done right. It takes any idiot to get whacked by a chair. I learned that the hard way. You wanna show your skills. Try getting a technical match fellas. Lose less brain cells too haha. Anthony would lean over to the bar in the limo, and get himself a glass of scotch. After he makes it he’d take a drink. He’d swallow it down, and the swallow is followed by a big smile. Anthony Clark: And what’s up with the name huh? Sin? Do you know what a sin even is man? Or are you trying to be dark like Blight? Boy I have committed more sins last night with two girls in my hotel room last night, then you did in your entire life hahaha. You wanna say I’m from the “Real World”. That’s fine. Cause I am in a real world. Not this fantasy world guys like you and Blight are where you think you can win against me! He would take one more drink out of the glass, and toss it out of his sunroof in the limo. The camera would show threw the back window, and you’d see the glass shattering all over the road. Traffic stopping do to it. The young arrogant Anthony showing no sign of care. Anthony Clark: Now we are on to the first one to speak. The first one to step up to the plate here. A Mr. Riddick. Hmm wonder if he likes the movie Pitch Black... Or Chronicles Of Riddick. Jessica Richards: Anthony baby. No one liked that movie. Anthony Clark: Good point haha. So Riddick you talk about hearing this ringing all the time. This ringing in your head or ear or something. My answer to that... Go to the doctor. That can’t be healthy at all man haha. How did he get medically cleared for this match haha. But then the young man talks about madness, and everything. My reply to that. Son you don’t need a wrestling ring, you need a damn padded room hahaha. And you also talk about this dark cloud upon you. Let me just say what is with all you dark people in the MWE. I thought I came to a party. Not a funeral. You see this is why the MWE took me in people. This is why they need a shining light here. To get rid of these dark soulless people. After a while the car would pull into a huge luxurious housing estate. The white gates would open up then. Anthony Clark: MWE... Your new shining star has arrive. The new young buck is here, and kicking. You will see you will get more bang for your buck out of me. I don’t bring in the male wrestling fans. I bring in their girls, and their cougar mothers as well hahaha. Anthony Clark... Total Sexstasy has arrived. Girls be prepared for the ride, and boys get ready to cry. Anthony would smile as him, and his girlfriend get out of the limo together. The screen slowly fades to black. The end.
