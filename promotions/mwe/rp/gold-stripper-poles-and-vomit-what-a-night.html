Gold, Stripper Poles, and Vomit. What A Night!; Anthony Clark RP

Jun 4 2009

Anthony Clark

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The screen opens. We would see Anthony Clark sitting on his white leather couch inside of his gorgeous Los Angeles home. He would be sitting back relaxing with his legs up on the glass coffee table. And his pet bulldog sitting beside him watching the T.V. with him. Anthony would be wearing a pair of all black Nike sneakers, a pair of Affliction jeans, with a black beater on. In the background you would his girlfriends Jessica in the background in the kitchen. As Anthony is watching the T.V. the camera would pan over to see what he is watching. You would see on the T.V. some coverage of MWE. He would be watching the latest breaking new story of Zak Warner, and how he just recently entered rehab. Anthony Clark: Holy shit Jess, Zak is going into rehab. I mean he talked about it last night but I mean... I didn’t believe it one damn bit. Jesus. Jessica would walk into the room, and sit down on the couch beside Anthony. Jessica Richards: Didn’t you go out with him like last night? Anthony Clark: Yeah. We all went out to party. Zak said it was his final hoo-rah I guess you can say. I was just thought he was joking but I guess he wasn’t. Jessica Richards: What even happened last night Anthony? Anthony slowly kinda looks up to the ceiling some, as the thoughts would run threw his head. The screen would fade to black, and the words would come up on the screen. LAST NIGHT. The screen would then open to a white Cadillac escalade pulling into a parking lot of local Los Angeles strip club. The windows would be down, and the song “Don’t Trust Me” by 3OH!3 would be blaring out of the windows. The car finally parks, and getting out of the car on the driver side is Anthony Clark, and behind him in the back seat Xavier Hudson. On the other side is Kevin Hardaway, and from behind him Zak Warner. They all hop out finally, and you can see Anthony’s face is full of excitement. Xavier the same. The camera would pan over to the pissed off looking Kevin Hardaway, and the very calm Zak Warner. Kevin Hardaway: Come on, do we really have to come here? This place is a bunch of booze hounds, and well whores. Anthony Clark: Whoa whoa whoa they are woman of the arts. They are trying to make a living just like me, and you do in the ring ok bro. Xavier Hudson: WOMEN! Zak laughs, and kinda pats the back of Xavier. Kevin Hardaway: Oh god I’m in for a long ass night. Zak Warner: Chill out man, it won’t be that bad. The screen would pan to another black screen saying 1 HOUR LATER. The screen would pan to all 4 sitting at the stage. Of corse the already hammered, and almost passed out Xavier Hudson can barely keep his eyes open. We then see sitting next to the very happy and all smile Anthony Clark is the very grouchy, and pissed Kevin Hardaway. Kevin Hardaway: I can’t believe this. I’m going for World title contentions, and I’m stuck with you three in a strip club for the night. My woman is going to kill me! Anthony Clark: Here’s a five dollar bill, go have some fun man. You are really bringing me down man. Xavier how’s it going on over there? Xavier Hudson : Man wheea and the flopp. Cinnamon toast. Anthony, Zak, and Kevin: Wow. All three gentleman stare at each other in shock of speaking in unison. Finally Zak would shake his head, and kinda laugh. Zak Warner: I’m going out for some air guys. I’ll be back, how bout you come with me Clark. Anthony Clark: Ok man, that’s cool. Kevin Hardaway: Dear god no, don’t love me here with the whores. And well him. The camera pans to Xavier who at this point can’t stand up, and has his shirt neck around his head like a head dress. Zak Warner: Haha later bro. The two would walk outside, as Kevin puts his face into his head feeling helpless and miserable. When the duo of Clark, and Warner get outside Clark lights up a cigarette. He’d offer one to Zak, but he’d waive him off a no. Zak Warner: Things will be the death of you man. Anthony Clark: I know but hey we all gotta die sometime. Zak Warner: Good call bro. Talking bout dying. You gotta match against Kraven coming up. Anthony Clark: That isn’t talking bout dying. I don’t fear that guy one damn bit, ok? Did you forget who I am dude? I mean you fucking trained me. So did K-Hard. You need to have some faith in what you taught. You guys trained me well enough to know when I don’t have a chance. Zak Warner: Yeah, but you also tend to have that ten cent head that you head when I first met your scrawny ass. Anthony Clark: Look I’m young there is nothing more for me to do then grow experience man. And I’m doing it now. True in my own mind I am the best there is. But it’s not like I’m going to walk out to the ring against Kraven with self doubt. Okay, the guy is on a hot streak. Well so am I. I mean I’m going on to my third match here in the MWE. And it’s for a title. I mean you have that record of the shortest matches to win a title. You won a title in three match. And believe me bro I’m going to tie that record. Zak Warner: I don’t have any doubts you do, but sometimes your too cocky out there man. Don’t make the mistake of under estimating your opponent. I know but I remember you in the GWC too. I mean that’s where me, K-Hard, and hell Reckless Jack trained you. We told you numerous times your head gets to damn big for your body. Anthony Clark: Zak I’m doing fine ok. Kraven is going up against the young, and fastest rising star pro wrestling has ever seen. Zak Warner: No you see this is the shit I’m talking about man. Sometimes you get ahead of yourself. You think you’re ahead of the game. And you haven’t seen what it truly is yet to be faced with a challenge. Anthony would get kinda frustrated. Anthony Clark: Oh yeah. Challenges well I think I was the one who had his hand raised last time I saw you in the ring Zak. Zak Warner: Oh yeah you mean when I busted you open with a staple gun, and then shut the cut closed with the staple gun. That match? The one where I scared your damn body up. I toughened you up that day son you got that. That wasn’t a match that was a lesson I was teaching you. Not everyone here is going to go out there and lie down cause you think you’re a gift to wrestling. This is a political business. You have no one to count on but yourself. The better you realize that... The better off you are. You’re going against Kraven ok. That guy isn’t right in the damn head. I mean I’ve beaten you senseless Clark. And I’m sane. What do you think a whack job is going to do to you? Anthony would lean against his car, as he takes all this into his brain. Zak Warner: Just think about that. That’s all I got to leave you with before I hit rehab man. Just remember that. Zak would walk back inside. As the strip club door opens from Zak’s entree in you would hear a mix of loud music, and a yelling Xavier Hudson. Anthony would sit outside as the camera man continues to film him. He’d seem a bit bewildered by the words of Zak. Not really sure what to think of them at all. Anthony Clark: Kraven... Tonight I’m partying... But come Monday Night... I’m going to be partying again. But you see this is going to be for an all together different reason. It will be for me winning you XTV title, and well K-Hard winning the world title... But mostly my XTV title win. Kraven I’ve seen plenty of your type. You’re a little crazy. Not together upstairs in your head. Hell you’re probably on six billion medications. You probably worship that title cause well it’s the only thing you have ever let close to you in life? Am I in the ballpark boy? Anthony would chuckle taking a drag off his Camel cigarette. Anthony Clark: Dad wasn’t around when you were a kid? Mom never hugged you? Didn’t make the baseball team in high school? So now you are a dark, and tormented soul. I’ve seen your type before. I wrestled your kind. Blight, and Sin are those dark eerie type too. And guess what I did to them at the Pay Per View. I beat em to move on to get my number one contendership.... For your title. I beat three other men to take you on. One man... I have beaten three, and now all I have to do is beat one. Hell if I can take on three, then one should be a piece of cake then. Anthony would sit on the curb, and watch the traffic go by then. Anthony Clark: You see last time I was speaking to the camera I told you all what my destiny was. I told you all how there is nothing but greatness for me... Well I got another history lesson for all of you. You see gold is something that people see a royalty in some cultures. The Egyptians used to burry their kings in golden tombs, to symbolize their royalty. And when Jesus was born he was given gold as one of his gifts. And now.. It’s my time. I am here to prove I am one of the best in the wrestling world today. And step one is taking what is rightfully mine Kraven. And that is your XTV title. I’m going to prove to you, to Zak, and everyone I am just as good as I say I am. Kraven I feel no sorrow for you. I feel nothing for what I’m going to have to do to you. It’s nothing personal.. It’s just business for Anthony Clark. Soon or later everyone will be a victim to the shining star Anthony Clark. It’s just the question of who wants to step up to the plate first. And well it looks like you’re the next due up. You can talk about your depression, and your cold dark worlds. Well hey this world is cold... So bundle up partner. All of a sudden you’d hear a big bang, and the camera would look over to see the strip club door wide open. And you’d see Hudson sprinting out with his hand over his mouth. But before he makes it all the way to the parking lot he would throw up all over some poor girls shoes. The girl would scream in horror, and Hudson would wipe his mouth and stand back up. He’d take a deep breath, and look at the poor girl. Xavier Hudson: Ummm sorry? Kevin finally walks outside then. Kevin Hardaway: Ok night over. We need to get out of here. The owner is like pissed at Hudson. Anthony Clark: Oh god lets roll. As Clark gets up, the screen would pan back to Anthony sitting on the couch with Jessica. He’d shake his head a bit in amazement, and trying to get his thoughts back together. Anthony Clark: Wow that night was a bit nuts to say the least. I remember it all like it was yesterday. Jessica Richards: It was yesterday! I’m still cleaning the mess of vomit Xavier left threw this damn house! Anthony Clark: Oh yeah. That’s why he is still here. The camera would pan over to Xavier passed out in a Lazy Boy recliner with nothing on at all except his baseball hat covering his unmentionables. The screen would fade slowly to black. The End.
