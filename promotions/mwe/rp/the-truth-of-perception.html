The Truth of Perception; Hardaway RP; World Title Match

May 29 2009

Kevin Hardaway

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
There are several photos that Kevin Hardaway carries inside his wallet to remind him of those people who may have had some kind of impact upon his existence; and every now and then, he'll take them out and spend an afternoon staring at them in silence as he remembers the events that occurred in the moments before these pictures were taken- and the reason why he came to hold the people depicted in these photos in such high regard in the first place. Sometimes, Kevin will gaze for hours at a time at a snapshot of his one and only true love, Angelina Hardaway standing behind a wheelbarrow of some kind (even though the both of them have no idea what it is) in the dead middle of winter, near a park in Baltimore, Maryland. How Baltimore had beautiful parks like that, it was beyond the both of them. Kevin always gets a smile out of that picture. If they ever had a fight, or a long standing distrust between one another, that picture always showed up at the worst time. Perhaps because the picture of her was taken right before he got down on one knee and asked his love if she wanted to spend the rest of her life with a beaten-down, broken professional wrestler. The magic of her smiling and her eyes sparkling like a Christmas tree was one of the most joyous things he ever had a chance of seeing. Whenever Kevin regards this photo of her with the most perfect smile, he always finds himself transported back to that day, and always feels gratified about the fact that the look of adoration and happiness that's apparent on the face of the golden blonde-haired beauty that is shown balanced against that wheel hasn't diminished in the slightest in all the time that's transpired since the moment when that picture was taken. Of course, there are times when Kevin will choose to examine some of the other photographs in his possession. He will occasionally stare at a picture of Amy Skye-Williams struggling to keep a straight face as she watches her lover, Jay Williams smirk into the camera's lens and salute the photographer with the bird on each hand; and in that moment will understand why he still considers these two to be his closest friends, Jay being one of his brightest students. He wonders what they are up to ever since they both retired from the wrestling scene to raise their daughter, Ellie. There are other pictures inside his wallet as well. There are photos of Kevin in the company of various people that he has befriended throughout the years, and in the presence of members of his family that remained in contact with him after the members of his immediate family had passed away. In each case, can usually express to others the exact nature of the relationship that he had with those individuals who are pictured; and the exact nature of the influence that they had upon his life. Yet, at the moment, the Kevin’s main focus isn’t upon pictures of Zak, or Jay, or any other person that he could possible claim to have known, for that matter. In fact, the current object of his attention is a photo of somebody that he never got a chance to speak with; someone who is impossible to understand in his life...honestly because he only had a chance to speak with her for such a short period of time in his life. But, recent events that have occurred in his life, and in the life of the woman that he love have compelled him to re-examine the question of just how important this particular person might have been; and that's why he is now staring at a faded colored photograph that happens to be the only evidence that he has that this person ever existed. The picture features a very young Kevin seated upon his mother's lap; and the curiosity in his stare is apparent as he watches his mother stare down with love in her eyes at the tiny baby girl that's being cradled in her arms. He looks at it for a long period of time, the smile on his face glistening as a small drop splashes on the picture. A tear drop coming from his eye. He wipes it away, his thumb gently sliding across the baby girl’s little face as he decides to turn it over. It reveals a small note, written by what looks like his mother. Quote: Feb 2, 1984 Dearest Rachel: Today would have been your second birthday, and to honor that, I'm writing you this note so that you'll know just how much your mother, father and brother still love you. Where ever you are, Rachel, please know that we shall never forget you...Not even for a single moment. With all the love that we have, Linda, James, and Kevin Kevin proceeds to let the air out of his lungs with a prolonged sigh and shakes his head as he struggles to confront the guilt that he feels welling up inside of him as a result of reading his mother's letter to her daughter. For, if he's to be truly honest with himself, he would have to admit that he really hasn't done a very good job of keeping the promose that his mother made on behalf of her familiy. Granted, the reason why he has failed to do this might be easiliy understood, given that his sister's life lasted for all of a handful of months and that simply didn't give a young Kevin enough time to bond with her in any meaningful fashion, or to develop any memory of his sister that was vivid enough to be stored away in the back of his mind for future reference. Still, Kevin knows all too well that, for all the validity of this explanation, the fact remains was that Rachel Hardaway was his flesh and blood, and there should be nothing that would be able to excuse him from the obligation that he has to acknowledge her existance and to honor it so that her life might continue to retain some measure of value close to two and a half decades removed from the date of her death. It really is a good question, he thinks... I just wish that I knew the answer... At that moment, the sound of a familiar female voice clearing her throat captures his attention, and causes him to whirl around and note the figure of the woman...his loving wife, as she stands in front of him with bags of groceries in each hand. He notes the mixture of concern and curiosity in her eyes as she puts the bags onto the counter and reaches out to take one his hands in her own, and then addresses him in a sympathetic tone of voice. Well, maybe I might be able to help you to answer whatever question that you're asking of yourself at this moment, my love. And, if that's the case, then you know that I'll be thrilled to provide you with the information that you desire. Remember, if there’s anything that I can do to help to answer this question...whatever it might be? Kevin takes a quick glance down at the photograph in his hand, takes another deep breath, and then slowly nods his head and he places it on top of Angie's lap. Yes, Angie...I really think that you might be able to provide me with some assistance in this matter. So, with that in mind, I'd like you to take a look at this picture, and tell me what you see... Angie ends up taking a couple of moments to peer at the photo, looking at the smudge that was left by Kevin’s thumbprint and smiles up at him. Why, I see a picture of you, Kevin, that seems to have been taken when you were all of...what? Nineteen or twenty months of age? Yeah...something like that. She ends up breaking out into a broad grin. Oh, don't you look sweet in this picture? Check you out with that t-shirt of yours...Is that really an AC/DC t-shirt that you're wearing? It is... Yes, Angie...yes it is. But... Angie is paying no attention though as she gives Kevin a playful shove on the shoulder. Weren't you just the cutest little headbanger back then? Once a metalhead, always a metalhead, right, Kevin? Oh, this picture is great! Does Amy know of this picture? Jay? I am so going to show it to them. They'll get such a kick out of it! Kevin’s voice remains the same and yet gets a little louder in tone. Angie... Oh, come on, Kevin! This is a great picture of you, and your Mom, and that lovely little baby in Linda's arms... She looks a little closer into the photo, still smiling and still keeping an arm around her husband’s. She's also quite the cutie, isn't she? You can already tell that when she grows up, she's going to be quite the heartbreaker. After all, she's got the same beautiful blue eyes that your mother had... Angie's smile freezes in place as she stares intently at the photo in her hand for a moment, and when she looks up at Kevin, he can note to look of realization that has just dawned over her features. Kevin...this baby has the same ice blue eyes as Linda did...that you have...this baby...this baby has your eyes...Kevin...the baby in this picture...this baby is...is.... This baby is Rachel Amanda Hardaway, Angie. This baby is my younger sister. Angie drops her jaw in complete and utter disbelief as she looks deeper into the picture and back up to her husband’s face, still in complete shock. Kevin! Why didn't you tell me!? I thought you were an only child... Well, in a sense, I was, Ani...because Rachel died five months to the day after she was born, and I was the only child in the household from that point forward.... Your...your sister died!? How!? Cancer. Really?! Yeah...she had a tumor located under her lungs. It was removed, but it ruptured before we could get it out. We did get it out and we thought everything was going to be okay, but it wasn’t. She ended up getting cancer out of it, and there was nothing we could’ve done. We tried everything, but it just didn’t work. Oh my god, honey...I don’t know what to say. Umm...do you mind how she died? Like I mean, was it peaceful or was she in a lot of pain? One night, she just...stopped breathing...while she was sleeping in the crib. I kind of remember that my mom came in to check on her...and then when she checked on me, she had the most horrified, anguish-filled expression that I've ever seen on a face in my life. It was horrifying. I...I don't really have that many memories of Rachel. My mother and father would always change the topic whenever anyone mentioned her name...and I kind of got the impression that was because they blamed themselves, and one another, for what happened to her. So, after a while, I kind of forgot about what happened to her, because there were so many other issues going on in the household...so many other things that I had to deal with...as it turned out, I spent so much time dealing with those issues that it seemed like I could never find any time to remember her...and, as a result, the memory of her faded from my mind... So...that's why I didn't tell you about Rachel prior to now, Angie...Because up until the time when you and your sister were trying to work your way back to one another, I just never could bring myself to try and recall the memory of my sister when I didn't have any memories of her to draw upon... And, that is what has led me to the point where I am now asking myself- and you- how I should go about doing something that should have been done a long time ago...How do I find a way to honor my sister when I have no idea of who she was, or even the person that she might have been had she survived to this point and time? Kevin watches the woman that he loves remain silent for an extended period of time as she nods her head and reaches up to hug her husband. For a longer period of time, these two just sit on the edge of their bed, hugging and holding one another, her comforting him as much as she can. She lets go of the hug though, holding his hands as lovingly as she can. Well, you may not have gotten the chance to know your sister, Kevin...and you may not have had the opportunity to see the person that she would have become, either. But, you can still honor the person that she could have been, my love. You can honor the person that you would have wanted to have had as a younger sibling. That does sound like as good a plan as any that I'm likely to hear, Angie. Now, do you have any suggestions on how I should go about doing so? She smiles at him, actually getting a decent smile back from her husband’s face. Of course I do, Kevin. But, before I offer them to you, let me ask you a question...What type of person would you have wanted your sister to be...? Kevin, after a few moments spent considering his response, he answers Angie. I think that Rachel would have probably been pretty smart...pretty kind...and pretty strong-willed... Angie’s smile grows a little wider this time. Much like her brother... Kevin grins back to Angie. Perhaps... Perhaps? I think that my sister might have been a little less intense than I was, and maybe a little more inclined to rely upon her charm, and style, and sense of humor to resolve any difficult situations that she would have experienced throughout the course of her life. In truth, I can't say for certain exactly what type of person my sister might have been had she lived past infancy... Well I’m sure she would’ve been not only a great sister, but a even greater friend. Not just for you, but maybe for me and Kimmy. Yeah... A long period of silence follows as Angie keeps looking at the picture, smiling up at Kevin who has a tear running down his face. Angie slowly takes her thumb and slides it across his eye, wiping it away gently. I miss her, you know. I know, baby... With that, she gets as close to him as possible and gives him what seems like the world’s most comforting hug ever. Another extended period of silence follows this, letting everything just sink in as much as possible. Angie lets go and kisses her husband on the cheek and wipes more tears away, coming down his cheek. It’s all about redemption. Not just for Rachel, but for me as well. Everything that has happened with me this past year. All of my sins and all of my past mistakes, true redemption only comes when we are given absolution for our sins; not being returned to some previous state of existence. I can sense that feeling inside me, but I can’t even touch the feeling until I redeem myself on Monday. Angie gives him another kiss on the cheek. You do what you need to do. Okay? Okay. I love you. From the bottom of my heart. I love you too. She gives him another kiss, this time a long, sweet kiss on the lips, sliding her thumb across the stubble and the roughness of his cheek as she stands up and walks out of the room, leaving her husband to be outside on the porch, still looking at the photograph of him and Rachel, wondering, hoping... Everything comes full circle on Monday. Salvation, redemption, everything. He can not fail, he can not die, he can not hope that hope never comes. Everything happens for a reason right? Well this is his reason. This is why he does what he does best. But he starts questioning himself as to why he does these things... What does it take to be champion really? First of all, it's in your nature. No wimps can apply. You have to have the ability to kill ingrained into you. There's no time for sympathy or useless emotions. You can't just walk in off the streets expecting to be a champion. Trying something as foolish as that only guarantees that you find yourself...well, truth be told...you'd probably be laughed out of the building, bringing shame to yourself and your family. Not to mention the complete murdering you’d get from said champion. It's in your heart too. It's got to be something you strive to be. In our business, champions are regarded as the best fighters and warriors on the planet. So when someone asked what I wanted to be when I got older...I said, "I want to be a champion one day." Nobody knew what I was talking about, no one knew a damn clue what I said when I was just a little kid. See, even then, I had my priorities. And of course, my priorities have been made obvious here in the MWE recently. Champions are the definition of strong...and in our business, only the strong can survive. The strong chase the weak away, leaving only the elite to step into greatness...and why wouldn't I want to be one of the elite? He’s absolutely right. You can’t be bothered to go inside of anything cold turkey. It’s all about the way you act, the way you feel, the way you look. Going inside of a war zone without any training could leave you in a gory, gruesome pool of blood. You need to know, not only the basics, but anything that may seem advanced. Ever since I was three years old, I knew that I wanted to be something. Look at me and I will tell you that in all my days and years learning in elementary school, middle school, high school...I didn’t amount to damn near anything. I wasn’t the all-american quarterback on the high school football team. I didn’t get straight A’s on my report card and become valedictorian. I never got the girl that every guy wanted there. I just thought of myself as a regular old pawn in a chess game. I didn’t care. I didn’t want to be “Joe Everyman” like everybody else. Why didn’t I care you ask? Well between afternoons of detention, I was busy trying to build my very own life. A life in this business. And you know what everybody did when I told them what I wanted to do? They all laughed in my face. They all looked at me and thought I was some kind of a joke. Up until late 2007, everybody thought I was a damn joke. He hears the laughter inside of his head, everybody cracking up. Doing the things that he wanted to do, nobody approved of him, nobody wanted to accept what he wanted to do. Not after hearing the horror stories of people involved that ended in tragedy. Only a few people didn’t really mind and accepted the career that he wanted. One of them was Angie...she accepted anything his plate set down. She was the only one who accepted his cause of being straight edge. But we’re getting too far here. Until one day, I decided to step up. It took a lot of time from my life, but becoming a champion, becoming the person that you wanted to be, it always takes more time than what you expect? Nothing comes for you overnight. It took me nine years of my career, from the time I started in this business in 1998. It took me 27 years of my life in general, from the day I was born. The amount of frustration, the agony of defeat, the humiliation of being second fiddle to people who you thought couldn’t pass you, you realize how much that ripped me apart inside. This industry isn’t as fun as you may think. This business is like Wall Street. Sure, you find a couple people who you can trust on the inside, but then you also have your Gordon Gekko’s. People who will do ANYTHING and I repeat...ANYTHING to make sure they’re up top and you’re not. I’ve met both kinds. The thing is though, it didn’t stop me from finally stepping up and finally dethroning the Gordon Gekko’s of the world. He can sense the scum of everybody who has caused him agony and suffering throughout not only his career, but throughout these past several months. Those people, the “Gekko’s” he liked to call it, thought they blackballed him out of the very thing that he loves most, but the only thing they all did, was put him into a slumber, a coma if you would call it that. Just now though, he’s woken up from that sleep and gotten back on his feet. He looks into his wallet and pulls out another picture and looks at it carefully. A picture of his family. His current family. Kevin...Angie...and little baby Kimberly, placed happily on her mother’s lap. The picture shows that it was actually taken on the same step as the picture from eariler. The one with his sister, Rachel. He starts to think back, about his family...and how they were there throughout his “coma”. Back to Earth now, for the past eight months of my career though, I’ve hit a snag. Everybody hits snags in their careers, it doesn’t matter who you are. I’ve hit snags before in my career, but not like this. This snag has caused nothing but controversy, blackballing, you name it. Here’s the $64,000 Question though...have you ever been in a snag in your career, Whitman? Because by the looks of it, I don’t think you have, man. You may have lost a couple matches, a couple titles, but I don’t think I could ever recall from the archives of you going into a complete cold slump. You’ve always gotten back up, dusted off the dirt from your shoulders, got back into the swing of things and always reclaimed your spot on the top of the throne. See, a real champion is one that can outwit and outlast any type of scum that walks their away. And not just scum, anybody. Being a real champion is not just being the “best wrestler in the world” but being the smartest man that you can think of. You do things that no other man can think of, you start getting new strategies, you need to think differently in different environments. And throughout the entire history of this company, our World Champion, David Whitman has been exactly that. He’s not just one of the best wrestlers that this company has ever seen, but he’s the smartest person in this company today. He knows the feeling, hands down. And I’m sure he (along with everybody else) knows that the current MWE Champion. It’s like the weirdest game of chess that you will ever play. You have to make your move at the right time. One false move and game’s over, you walk away holding your head down low, away from everybody who thought you would yell out the words, “Checkmate”. But like I said, only the strong can survive in this business. There is no time for making friends. There is no time for worrying about these people you call your “partners”. Everybody has learned that the hard way, but it is a lesson we all must learn. Trust me, I know. Ladies and gentlemen, this is a dog eat dog world, there is no room for alliances. You can't trust anyone because in the end, greed will screw you over. Everyone has one thing on their mind, and that is to be champion. I don't care what you say, but everyone has the desire to be champion and will do whatever it takes to get to the top of that mountain. I, for example, have been given the opportunity of a lifetime. I have been given the chance to prove that I am who I say I am, and there's not a damn thing Mercier nor Whitman will do to stop me from proving it to everybody in this world. Whitman has the bragging rights at the moment because he holds the prize at stake here. The MWE World Heavyweight Championship. However, in due time that will all change my friend. My rise to the top of this business has been the talk of the talk around the ol’ water cooler. Kevin looks around the room, seeing all the plaques and the trophies, and the replica titles he has. Everything from the XHF United States Championship, all the way to his crowning glory, the GWC World Heavyweight Championship. Engraved in his name, he takes a good hard look at the belt. Not because he wants to remember the past, but because he wants everybody to know that he ain’t some hot shot rookie in this business, and that’s he’s been around the world one too many times to count. Both of you guys, Mercier and Whitman, you may be fooling many people, but you do not fool me. You can't fake greatness, or any of the things that come with it. The wisdom, the power, or the success. Those are credentials you cannot make up or sugarcoat. To be a great man, you have to earn each and every credential. Yeah, you hold the highest prize in the company, Whitman...I'll give you that. You’ve also made your claim at being one of the best to ever lace up a pair of boots and step inside that ring outside. But somehow, someway, I have you figured out. I saw the fear in your eyes when you fought Jesse Anderson at Tribulation. It was as if you had met your match, even though you came out of it on top. You have gone around this company throwing your weight around for far too long, kid. It's time for a new elite to rise, and take the throne. For man is most deadly when another challenges him over a simple piece of tin, for that defines the man. The common, those so simple, even as their pride is wounded, they gather an unbelievable strength and can achieve accolades none would believe. A young boy walking through the halls of his school, verbally castigated by his peers classmates, soon he strikes back and the classmates are taken as they realize blood is trickling from their jaw, they had pushed the pride of the schoolboy over the edge and retaliation was inevitable. A young lover accompanied beneath satin sheets by his mistress, physical encounters, and then as the encounter ends and he asks if she was satisfied, she neglects his very attempts to please her and wounds his pride. He swears that the knife found not a sheath in her chest as a result of a madman's mental state. In fact she was stricken because she had struck first upon him, not drawing forth the fact that it was his wounded ego and wounded pride that she struck and not him. So how does one fare when their pride is wounded as they come to the realization that they have toppled from the pillar they have placed themselves upon, soon enough the answer shall come… What the hell is he saying? What the hell is he thinking? How does this have to do with a title match, a match that could signify what he’s been dreaming of for almost a year? It means so much more than one person can think of. It’s an odd comparison though. You see...Mercier...Whitman, man gives up a lot of things for pride, whether it is at a job for a local software company. Or on the gridiron of a grassy plain, divided by lines of 10 yards, the field stretches 120 feet from end zone to end zone. Warriors step onto this field in different conglomerates across the United States. Pride judged on this field is decided by how many big plays a person can come up with to define you as a hero. This occurs every Sunday from September to February. Moving away we come towards the alternate battlefield. One decided inside a squared circle with 3 ropes upon all four sides with post on each edge connecting the ropes. The lights illuminate, the crowd roars to a deafening sound, cheering for the guy who acts more like them and doesn’t have a cocky attitude to back it up, or cheer for the bloodshed. Moving away from the wrestling ring, we move to a swamp marsh, a desolate city, for which buildings and areas, which show significant importance, are scattered by pieces, some on the brink of total collapse. Men with weapons and tin hats with mesh coverings of their body walk through this area. Some frightened some that have seen everything possible. And some that openly would want to shoot the first thing they actually see. Bullets are heard flying, men screaming out in pain. The blood, the destruction, the apathy for man’s soul, the crescendo of death is beautiful for it plays like an angelic symphony. Simply, this matter this global cause to stop another from gaining power is called in simple term effects, war. Plaguing each and every one of us. Kevin puts his hands over his face, wondering, thinking about the past wars he has faced through in his life. Wars involving people going after him because he doused his girlfriend with beer, wars involving people who wanted to make him suffer, wars involving people who knew he couldn’t beat them, and wars involving nothing but two men wanting to rip apart one another until they couldn’t breathe through their mouths. These battlefields shed the simple fact that pride is decided on these fields. But, pride could be taken a little bit too seriously. For one single mistake could change the way we see ourselves as a humanity. We’ve killed on the football field, on the battlefield of war, and inside of the squared circle. Death creeps around us at every walking moment. Defining death is just as complicated as defining pride. Its consent envy’s the noblest man to a furious passion. Most of us think that we have everything figured out. That we are the center of attention towards everyone else, becoming a big shot, teaching others what you have learned. An entity of one’s self being. Actually seeing them as something they are not. Everybody wants to prove something, that they are the cream of the crop. That one man is standing over all others victorious on the battlefield. Some of us however can’t even grasp the concept of what to actually do in a wrestling ring. What a person shall do with words, may not do in action. Thy self is complicated thy pride is questionable. Thy self is afraid of what he can and cannot do, showing others that he is not a person who loses and doesn’t learn from these losses. Pride, one man’s goal, which is another man’s problem… He’s had to face pride square in the eyes, losing trust with everybody on several occasions, trying to find whatever was necessary to make sure everybody could either believe in him or make sure he could tough it out with the best of them. That’s pride...something that he feels like he has again, but not his fellow opponent. Sinc Mercier...I can see that in your eyes. I can see that you want to prove something to the entire industry, let alone the world. At Tribulation, you fooled Windy Bridges and you became the US/IC Champion. Wow, what a shocker. What is this...your 4th reign as United States Champion? And yet, you seem like you’ve been here for quite the long time, am I correct? It’s pretty damn amazing to look at you, look at you on TV and actually feel like that I’m seeing myself in the mirror, yet when I look at the mirror, I don’t see me currently...I see myself a couple years ago. I see that self who couldn’t reach up the top of the ladder into the big time, who was just one, itsy-bitsy rung short of breaking the glass ceiling. You see everybody laughing, and pointing fingers at you, and whispering sweet little nothings into their friends ears. You don’t think that they’re talking, but in all honesty, they actually are. About how you’ll be nothing short of what everybody called me back in the day...a “glorified midcarder”. Nothing else, nothing more, Sinc. You tried to prove to everybody that you could hang and yet you’re still trying to reach up, high to that ceiling reaching for nothing but the breeze that passes you by. You know how that feels, Sinc? Because I know that feeling all to well. Guess what though? I proved to everybody that I wasn’t some mid-card loser. I actually did something about it, and I stepped up. And what did you do? Participate in some “scavenger hunt” to win those belts. What did I do? I took D.C. to the limit. Something that very few people can say that they did in their careers. Have you? He rolls up the sleeve on his shirt to reveal a few minor bruises and scratches. Tribulation was the rare occurrence where he wasn’t busted open like a grapefruit nor wasn’t he physically scared and broken up. The only minor injury caused to him was not being able to answer the 10 count. Like we said before, it’s like a game of chess. And well...Kevin made one mistake and got caught for it. Not this time though. He’s studied both Sinc Mercier and the current champ, David Whitman. He doesn’t know everything that there is to know about both men...who could know, but he feels like he knows enough. This coming Monday at Mayhem, I get to do something that I haven’t done in a long time. I get a chance in my career to once again step up and make very damn sure that I will be the first to reach the top of the mountain. I’ve waited too long for my redemption song. Too long. For a few minutes, I thought my redemption song was going to be me standing last against D.C., but it wasn’t meant to be. And now, I get a chance at glory, at not only becoming the World Heavyweight Champion, but finally getting a chance to be the one in forever history to join both Sonny Williams and Brian Michaels in the annals...the ones who dethroned David Whitman. Sinc, you’re also in my way as well. Doesn’t mean it’ll be any easier than it once was. But when you learn that when one man will do anything to achieve not only his goal, but his hopes and dreams, that said man will do ANYTHING IT TAKES to make sure he’s a success and not a failure. And I am not a failure, never will I be one. All the good men and all the bad men who have smiled and laughed at me lately will look at me come Monday and they will either smile more or laugh more. I really hope for the former of the two. Because I want to call myself champion again. I want to make every single little soul-less piece of crap that has called me out for what I wasn’t for the past 8 months...I want them to see me and die a little more on the inside. Those people know who they are. I want to shock the world and make everybody that is watching that very moment to remember it for the rest of their lives. I WANT REDEMPTION! Silence. Barely can you hear a pin drop. And this is my song... While shutters enclose the boundaries of my heart. I hold my breath till the steady beating is shut out. In silence I wait for what is real. For I know the truth of my perception. Nothing in life matters more, more than You. Nothing more, more than You. When first feelings fade, I will still be here. (As I Lay Dying // The Truth of My Perception; 2005)
