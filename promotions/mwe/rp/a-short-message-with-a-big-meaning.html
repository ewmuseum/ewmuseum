A Short Message, with a Big Meaning

Oct 10 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
An MWE camera begins to fade in from a blant and black image it currently is set in. It takes time for it to come to a picture perfect image, but it finally does. The camera is shown screening around an arena Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania. It is shown to be a House Show. All of a sudden, "Can't Be Touched" by Roy Jones Jr blasts through the arena through the PA System. A mixed reaction is heard from the crowd, but there is more so booing than cheering. Out walks Sonny Williams and his manager, Mr. Cooper. Mr. Cooper points to Sonny Williams as he raises the MWE World Championship up high as he gets a mixed reaction walking to the ring. He puts the MWE World Championship on his shoulder as he walks done to the ring with Cooper by his side. He walks up the steel steps eyeing off into the crowd at a man holding a "Sonny sucks!" sign. He walks through the ropes and circles the ring for a few seconds before being handed a microphone from an official. The music stops as a loud boo begins to hustle throughout the arena, followed by a small cheer from a group of people. Sonny Williams: I didn't come out here tonight cause I had to, I came out here because I want to. I don't really "do" in-ring promos, but I thought that I'd go back to basics. You see there is one face painted, yellow belly freak who looks like the stuff you shit after eating two servings of Indian, who thinks he can beat me. Yes you heard me people, that motherfucking, sorrow wanting, dick chugging son-of-a-raped-bitch thinks I am scared, and that he can somehow beat me. Does he really know who the fuck I am? No, but what he should do is type my name on YouTube and see what pops up. Milenko I dare you, because I bet my sorry black ass that the first video will be something along the lines of "Sonny destroys so and so" or "Sonny shocks the world". That's who I am. Sonny has a malicious look on his face as he pauses for a bit looking on to the camera with an evil grin. The crowd make a small boo, as Sonny chuckles. Mr.Cooper is beside Sonny with a grin on his face with where this is heading. Sonny Williams: But seeing as you don't really know who I am Milenko, let me give you a history lesson. I'm the type of guy that keeps coming back at you, I'm like a fly in the summertime, I just won't piss off. I am dominate, I am destructive, I am a force field, I am the Main Man, I am the fight, I am better.. THAN YOU! Oh, and did I forget to mention that I am the fucking MWE World Champion? Sonny chuckles as does Mr.Cooper. Sonny Williams: You know, apart from the fact that you wear face paint like you attend a children's party every Monday Night, there's something else I hate even more about you. You introduce yourself, not as a superstar but as a coward, as a man who is in deep need of an appointment with Dr.Phil. You're a fucking bitch who wants nothing more but to have people feel sorry for your past. I used to dread and walk trounces in my past, but that is day that has flown away. You know what that makes me.. A Man, something you will never be able to put under your job application. Sonny Williams: But wait, it gets better. You see Milenko, I'm not that bad of a guy. I attempt to respect my opponents and give them credit where it is due, but there are some people that fail my attempts. Milenko, I'm not gonna lie and I'm just gonna break it down like I see it, you're a conniving load of shit, and I will never feel sorry for you. But here's my question to you, would someone who talks of being abused daily by his step-father and having a mother who was raped do any of what I'm about to show? Sonny makes an evil smirk on his face. Sonny Williams: Roll the footage. Sonny Williams: You see that is exactly why I don't respect you. You're so fucking fake that Pamela Anderson's implants seem more realistic than you. Not to mention that you can't dance, rap or sing for fucking shit. Jesus Christ, I'd rather watch D.C banging Lindsey in the back of his Nissan Skyline than go through that shit again. Mr.Cooper chuckles as Sonny has an evil smirk on his face. The crowd boo during the pause as Sonny licks his lips in a sinister manner. Sonny Williams: But this is it.. Milenko, you know I don't like you, but you're not just my opponent anymore, you're also my warning. My warning to every single man, women or freak backstage who wants to lace their boots up and get in my face, because do you know who I am? I'M FUCKING SONNY WILLIAMS! And come Monday Night I am going to grab you, rip your nose of your face, and shove it right up your candy ass so you can smell the shit that you're full of. And you wanna know why? It's because it's who.. I.. AM! Sonny looks into the camera and then makes a sudden evil smile. Sonny Williams: CHA CHING! Promo ends. -FADE- OOC: I thought this was a quite decent RP for it's size.
