Voices tell me..

Sep 21 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
An MWE camera begins to fade out of a black and blank picture. It takes a few moments for the camera to gain a normal clear picture. It is night so the picture is still dark. The camera is situated at a dark and emptied warehouse in Columbus, Ohio where Monday Night Mayhem is occurring this week. The camera can see a figure in the middle of the emptied warehouse with his head down in a rocking chair, rocking to and fro. The camera slowly moves closer to the figure. A glare of the moons light shines above the figure as he raises his head. It appears to be current MWE World Champion, Sonny Williams. He is wearing a grey Sonny Williams hoodie with baggy blue jeans with the left leg of the jeans held up by a red bandana. The MWE World Championship is resting on the lap of Sonny. He has a look of focusness on his face. You can see the stiches on his forehead from the brutal attack he had at Turmoil. He continues to rock to and fro on the rocking chair. Sonny Williams: I hear voices. Voices that are trying to change who I am, what I stand for and by any means changing me as a person. Each night, at about 9:07PM an angel speaks to me. I can't hear it aloud, but my head hears every word. It hears the deep sound of God's worker. The doctors say it's because of the recent bumps to the head, the physiocyratrists say I am going through a time in my life where I doubt myself. I remain answerless. I do remain clueless but I do have a thinking for the cause; my recent losing streak on Monday Night Mayhem. It burns me inside each week, it's the only thing that has been keeping me from partying from night til morning. Sonny looks down at his watch on his left arm as it reads 9:06PM. Sonny suddenly stops rocking on the rocking chair and looks into the distance with a look of confusion and shock on his face. Sonny Williams: Shh.. I think I can hear something. A voice is heard within the head of Sonny Williams. The voice is a the sounding of a deep voice. Sonny holds his head trying to get rid of the voices in his head. He can't stand it. People look down, On your sultry soul. Lose after lose, You're on a bad roll. You remain winless, As you begin to burn. Your soul is dying, It's time for you to learn. You may be on top, But success does end. Let it fly freely, Let the success mend. A challenge awaits, The devil mess you are. Listen to the crowd, They will take you far. You can achieve more, More than you think. Your journey is waiting, Don't let it shrink. With confidence of all, And the ultimate certainty, Live your life well, Because it may end at thirty. Look down the mirror, Look into your eyes. The potential is there, Ignore the many cries. Your soul requires help, Help that can succeed. To visit your brother, If wanted of need. The heavens above, Ponder your sins. Whether you are good? The dice still spins. Where there is hope, Faith beckons beneath. Do never give in, Or be left with the grief. Luck is waiting ahead, For you to hold. Grab it with two hands, And never fold. You may ask the question, Who am I to say? I am the God's angel, May this be your day. Sonny still has his head buried in his hands as you can see the veins in his neck from the power of the message. He slowly brings his head back up looking into the camera. He takes many deep breaths as he tries to calm himself down. A rat runs across the floor of the emptied warehouse making Sonny look in awareness. Sonny Williams: These voices, they're driving me crazy. The other night while I was purchasing something from a bottle shop as it stroked 9:07PM and I broke down both physically and emotionally. The owner almost had to get an ambulance. It was horrible. These voices are supposably trying to make me a better person, but with all this I seem to be coming out the other end more screwed up than ever before. I can't stand it. Sonny Williams: When I mentioned to a physiocyratrist that it was an angel that was talking to me first thing that jumped to her head was my match this week, against the self-proclaimed angel, Alejandro Rodriguez. She thinks I am being ultimately intimidated by him and that my losing streak is getting to me like never before. I don't know what to believe. But what I do believe is this.. Sonny begins to slowly rock to and fro on the rocking chair once again after becoming calm. Sonny Williams: Tomorrow night here in Columbus, Ohio I walk into the very ring I won what lies on my lap and I don't just walk into it with just a match ahead of me, I walk into it with a quest ahead of me. A quest that could either continue a run of glory or end in absolute embarrassment. Right now, I don't know who exactly I am. I do know that tomorrow night, it's not all about Alejandro Rodriguez but it's also about me, it's to see whether either man can step up the ladder and reach a level higher. Sonny Williams: This match is bigger than words itself, it can't be explained. My match is the Main Event, and rightfully so but what concerns me is whether my match will run on til 9:07PM. Will my guardian angel give me another visit? I don't know. I'm lost for words. I'm lost for words bar one topic right now, Alejandro Rodriguez. Sonny rubs his chin with a evil smirk. Sonny Williams: Alejandro, I am not too sure whether you are my cause of insanity right now. You could be, you're a former MWE World Champion, a contender to what I hold and always a challenge. You've gone through misery too, you've paid the price of loss after loss and I know how it feels. I know what it's like to feel emotionally battered, even more so than physically. It hurts right? It hurts a lot more than anyone would understand. I acknowledge you as a serious contender, and maybe that is why I am freaking out. I know I got skill, you know I do too but whether I can back it up after brutality, that's yet to be decided. Sonny Williams: I've beaten you before, three times to be accurate. I know I can do it again, but I'm not getting at anything. No feelings of certainty or luck are coming to me. This isn't going to be easy, but through good and bad I will out shine you, or at least I will try. You've never really succeeded in this business, you have said I am a disgrace, when actually I bring grace to the place. There are some guys I don't like around this joint, your sorry little ass falls in that category. Sonny stops rocking on the rocking chair and puts his arms on the arm handles. Sonny Williams: So tomorrow night I will stand toe-to-toe with a man I have come to know so dearly over the past year. Fire will uprise, the atmosphere for wrestling will absolutely be in the air. Alejandro, I'm not 100%, I have broken ribs, stiches and am in pain, but I don't like you, you're existence and whether or not you are the cause of recent things in my life. If I get my way, you will live the same fate I did at the end of Turmoil. It's time to determine who is going up and who is stepping down a level. I rise to the occasion so my prediction is that I will rise above all and .. Sonny Williams: LEARN.. Sonny Williams: TO.. Sonny Williams: FLY! Sonny Williams:.. bitch. Sonny gets up from the rocking chair and puts his grey hoodie on as he puts his hands in his pockets and walks away from the emptied warehouse. The camera begins to fade as the promo ends.
