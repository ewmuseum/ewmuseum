Blind; Oblivion RP

Nov 20 2009

Oblivion

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
There is an old saying amongst everyone. That people don’t appreciate some of the things in their life until it’s gone. Even for madmen, that is also correct. To be more specific, the masked madman himself, Oblivion. And what he had lost, or rather was losing, was his vision After his victory over Grand Mystique, Oblivion had an extremely difficult time seeing. He knew that his vision was going again. That’s right, his vision had once faded, but he took some proper corrective surgery. In fact, right now, because of some he had no choice but to get, a gauze was wrapped around his eyes. Which ultimately, leaves us to where he is now. Back in his warehouse. Heather had not left his side since he had his surgery. Luckily, it was a minor one. QUOTE Flashback: day after Monday Night Mayhem. Oblivion had managed to get back to Chicago, to see his eye specialist, Dr. Sheamus. The 40 something, brown haired man had thoroughly checked over Oblivion’s eyes. He sighed. Oblivion: I’m going to need immediate surgery, correct? Dr Sheamus: I’m afraid so. Oblivion: Fuck. I can’t afford any time off now. Dr. Sheamus: I don’t think you will need to Oblivion. See, you came to me early enough. Given a few more months like this, you might have gone completely blind. You’re one crazy person, but one can’t discredit you for wanting to take care of yourself. Oblivion: Thanks Doc. When will I be able to have this fucken surgery? Dr. Sheamus: I can get it done for you in two hours. It will be the basic kind, so you won’t miss any action. Blind for about a week, then you’ll be fine. But I highly recommend you come back in a few months after to get the full correction. Something you failed to do last couple times. Oblivion sighed. He knew better this time. He had this exact same operation done to him before, so he understood why he had to get a full surgery in a few months. This one, was to just relax the eye nerves a bit, so they don’t make him go blind. Oblivion: Yeah yeah. Probably for the best anyways. But only after I’m done dealing with everything. Dr Sheamus sighed. He knew the full story. Being that Dr Sheamus was a kind hearted man, he didn’t see why Oblivion was so obsessive over destroying a man’s life. But this doctor wasn’t usually one to pry. Oblivion: I know you’re not somebody who gets obsessed…like I am. But all things considered about this, I think even you would understand. Dr Sheamus: I know. Zack Crash messed you up badly by accident. I know. I know there is other things as well, but you have to let it go. Oblivion: I’ll let it go when I give him a permanent injury. Don’t forget…because of him, I’m seeing you, to get my eyes fixed. Dr Sheamus: Yeah yeah. End Flashback. Right now though, it was early morning. Oblivion actually was working out, doing some weight training, with the weight machine where you had to alternate between arms for each rep. That is when Heather walked into the living room, and gasped. Heather: OBLIVION!!! Oblivion: Jeez woman. You want me to go deaf as well? Heather sighed. He could hear her walking towards him. He let go of the handles he was gripping, and when the weights hit the ones unused, there was a semi-loud clank sound that was heard. Heather: Sorry. But I don’t want you to get hurt because you can’t see. You know how sometimes people say stuff they shouldn’t have said. Well this was one of them. Heather knew it to, when Oblivion shot his head up, and slowly looked towards her, as if he knew where she was. On top of that, he was growling. Yeah, he was pissed. Oblivion: I’M NOT FUCKING USELESS! Heather: I didn’t mean it that way. I meant…I’m just concerned. Oblivion sighed, and nodded. She was just being a good girlfriend. Nothing he could do about that. Oblivion: Regardless. Dr Sheamus said it would only be a week before I could see. Well it’s been a week since the surgery. Heather: Oh, you going to take the gauze off? Oblivion: Not yet. Heather: Why not? Oblivion: I think…not seeing has allowed me to relax a bit. More so then I have in a while. Dunno why. Heather: Okay. Oblivion smiled. He began to feel around the air for any objects in the way. He even managed to grope Heather’s boobs a couple times (he wore a shit splitting grin when he did), before he found the couch at last. He sat down. Heather joined right beside him. Oblivion: How ironic is my situation. I’m blind in pretty much every sense. I can’t see, I’m focused soley on killing Crash, and my rage is pure black, that it prevents me from seeing things clear. Heather: I know. Oblivion: I know I should let my rage go. But I won’t. Without my rage…what would I be. Oblivion could hear Heather sigh right beside him, and could even feel her smile. She then kissed him, which had surprised him a bit. He didn’t push away though. Heather: You’d still be a great man. A man who gave me reason to fall in love with, twice. A man, who knows his flaws, and accepts them. You’d still be amongst the best. Your rage can fuel you, but it hinders you as well. Oblivion: Yeah. Get me the camcorder. Heather sighed again, before she got up, and got it. It would only take her two minutes for her to get the device, before setting it up. She activated it, and let him know, by snapping her fingers. It was time. Oblivion: Everyone has their strength, and their weaknesses. There is no exception. Non…whatsoever. I know mine. My eyes. They are so fucked up, that every so often, I have to have surgery done on them. Recently, I had my third one done. And believe me, it’s not fun, walking around blind. It hinders somebody like me. Somebody, who enjoys seeing the pain and suffering on people’s faces. Oblivion tilted his head upwards, so that it looked like he was looking at the camera, through the gauze. For the most part, it was hard for anyone to tell what kind of mood he was in, as his eyes more or less always gave him away. Oblivion: But then again, I’ve always thought that it was everyone’s flaws that made them interesting. Something that made people like or hate you. My biggest flaws, is that I’m obsessive, compulsive, and my eyes are fucked. But what about people who claim they have no flaws? That they are so called ‘perfect’? I can name your biggest flaw out right now, that is equal to all three of my flaws. Arrogance. Oblivion began to chuckle that that statement. He even began to scratch the side of his head. What is the masked madman thinking? Oblivion: Which brings me to my opponent this week. James Preston. He called himself the ‘Image of perfection’. Well Preston, I have a few things to say. You’re a fucken jackass to even think you’re perfect. Such arrogance will kill you, especially to a guy like me. To me, you’re nothing but a plaything. And like most playthings, you’ll get broken, and discarded. Like nothing. Which is what you are. Nothing. Perfection is nothing. Nothing is perfect. Therefore…anyone who dares call themselves perfect is absolutely nothing. Not even the tiniest form of bacteria. But...I have a feeling Preston, that if you’re watching this, you may think to yourself ‘HA! This guy will be so easy because he’s blind’. Well Preston…if you love that, well you’re going to love THIS! Oblivion reached behind him, and frantically began to take the gauze off of him. He took off the patches as well. He then opened his eyes, and smiled. Oblivion: I’m not blind bitch! The surgery, like the ones before, were a success. A Minor one, which I’ll eventually have to get again, to preserve my eyes. But not before I get what I have wanted for so long. For Zack Crash to suffer on the same level that I have for so long. Yet, you people watching, you have no idea just how bad it truly is. NO IDEA!!! Only three other people know the full story, besides myself. Heather, Crash, and JT Hunt. Oh yes. My obsession with taking out Zack Crash will not be denied. I have suffered enough bullshit in my life, that any other bullshit, is obliterated. In due time, people will understand, that their extreme hero is nothing but a vulture. Somebody, who waits for the most opportune time, to get what he wants. Patient…likely. Greedy…fuck yes. And you know, I am that bad karma, that will always follow you around, fucking you over at every turn. You can’t deny what you’ve done But back to Preston. I don’t even feel threatened by him. Why. Because he is nothing. Simple as that. But if he insists, I guess I’mm just have to do what I do best, and whoop his ass, until he begs for his mother to rescue him, then…I’ll FUCK HIS MOTHER RIGHT IN FRONT OF HIM! Oh yes. No we can tell what kind of mood Oblivion is in. He’s definitely in his craziest of moods. His now uncovered and repaired eyes give it all away. He was looking for a fight. Heh…get it? Looking…HE WAS BLIND PEOPLE! Oblivion: You can say you have no flaws. No problems in your life, or even chinks in your armor. Well I’m going to exploit them all. I’ll find them, show them to you, and then use them to kill your career. You know it’s true. Stop being arrogant, and start being driven. Because you’re only holding yourself back with your attitude. It’s a simple, fair, even…dare I say it…blind…truth. And everyone should know by now, that the truth…is …OBLIVION! Oblivion began his mad chuckle again. He clearly was back to his normal, frightening self. There was no denying it. He had reason to be confident himself. Recovering quickly after surgery that he wouldn’t miss any time was no laughing matter. Now, we just wait, to see just how bad Oblivion plans to destroy James Preston.
