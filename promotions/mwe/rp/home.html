** The camera opens and the sun is setting across barren desert land. The sky is painted with vibrant shades of pink, red, and purple. As the camera pans around we gain our bearings, we are back on Ace Cannon's land, his desert sanctuary where he raises ruthless, blood thristy pitbulls. As the camera pans back around we see a car zooming across the desert, it's moving a a rather good clip and kicking up huge amounts of dust. As the second tick the car grows closer and closer to us, finally after about five of six seconds the car comes to a stop right in front of us. The car is a pink Cadillac, looks to be a 1975 Pink Cadillac El Dorado convertible with bull horns attached to the hood. As the dust settles a man gets out and it's none other than Dick Malibu! If you don't know who this man is then your missing out, he own a chain of ultra successful tanning salons in the greater Des Monies area, friend to the stars and most importantly Ace Cannons former manager and business partner. As Dick stand by the car surveying the area we still see he is a sharped dressed man, his feet are covered in the world finest snake skin boots, his jeans are tight and designer, his shirt is made only the finest cotton found in Indonesia, his sport coat is silk and yes, it was made in Italy and lets not forget the sunglasses their of the aviator style and made by Armani. Dick slowly lifts his sunglasses off his head and his face distorts into what can only be a look of disgust.** Dick: Jesus, Ace has truly lost all his marbles. It smells like three parts dog shit, two parts regret and one part failure. **Dick begins walking toward what looks to be a rusted out trailer. It is truly a shabby mess and the only person who would live in their would be a relative of DC. Anyway as Dick gets closer a loud comotion is heard, the camera pans to the trailer. We see Ace Cannon standing in the door way holding as shotgun...** Cannon: Who the fuck is that? ** Dick is frozen in his tracks and not sure what to say.** Cannon: You have three seconds to open your fucking mouth. One...two.... Dick: IT'S ME! IT'S DICK MALIBU! ** Cannon keeps his gun trained on Malibu for a second, he really considers shooting him. He decides against it and steps down from the trailer.** Cannon: How the fuck did you find me? ** Dick walks closer to Cannon and stops a couple feet away.** Dick: That's what your going to say daddy-o? No hug? No handshake? No, “Hey long time no see?” ** Cannon just looks at Dick with a blank stare before answering.** Cannon: No, I think your a piece of shit. Why would I want to patronize you? Dick: Well we're friends, right? ** Ace looks at Dick and laughs right in his face.** Cannon: Dick, we were never friends! It was strictly a business arrangement. So back to my original question, how did you find me? Dick: I see how it is.... Now how I found you isn't important. I'm here to discuss business, can we go inside? It's hotter than a blistered pussy in a pepper patch out here. ** Cannon looks at Dick for a second and then reluctantly heads to the door. Both men step inside the trailer and it's very minimal. All we see is a little kitchen, a couch with clothes all over it and a lawn chair. Cannon cleans a space off and sits on the couch, Dick on the other hand looks around the trailer in utter disbelief at how Cannon is living. Once Dick gains his bearings he takes a seat in the lawn chair.** Cannon: Ok, you have five minutes to talk business. If I don't like it I'm kicking you the fuck out and I may or may not punch you in the face. **Dick looks at Cannon and shakes his head.** Dick: Ok, fair enough. Now, I'm here to talk MWE with you. I saw your promo for Eric Derf the other night and Ace, I feel like your not the man you used to be. You seem likes something is missing and you talk about being ruthless? A ruthless man doesn't live in the desert raising dogs and if I don't say so myself, Ace you seem like a neutered man. ** Cannon glares at Malibu.** Dick: I mean what I saw in that interview was the utter shits. I know that in there somewhere Ace Cannon, the baddest motherfucker on the planet is screaming to come out. I know the Ace Cannon that screws women, drinks champagne and throws money around like it's nothing wants out! If that Ace Cannon doesn't come out I know that your run in MWE will be miserable and worthless, jack. So I'm here today to bring you back to Hollywood, rebuild your career and rebuild your empire. What do you say daddy-o? We may not be friends but as business partners we are unstoppable! ** Cannon stares at Dick for a few seconds and he's not sure what to say. He just looks at him...** Dick: Ace, I'm telling you this is what you need. You may not be my friend, but as your friend you need this. You need to get the fuck out of here, Hollywood is your home! Being some dog farmer in the desert isn't your calling! **Cannon is still sitting there. His looks is still the same, it's blank.** Dick: Daddy-o, do what's right! ** Cannon stands up and walks outside and Dick follows. Cannon walks toward Dick's car but stops, he is halfway between the trailer and car. Cannon looks from one to another, one represents his new carefree laugh where society and what it thinks doesn't matter and the other represents everything that has given him trouble in the past. He looks back and fourth a few times and finally he makes his decsion.... He walks toward Dick's car and gets in. Dicks face is full of excitement and happiness, almost like a kid on Christmas.** Dick: You won't regret this! MWE is our pussy and it's waiting to be fornicated with..... ** Ace slowly shakes his head and begins to smile. The camera fades out as Dick climbs in the car and they drive off.** BACK IN BEVERLEY HILLS ** The camera opens back up. The camera pans up and we see palm trees, a blue sky and a high sun. We must be back in Beverley Hills. As the camera pans down we see Ace Cannon, his skin looks bronzed and sun kissed, he has a glass of champagne in his hands, black Silver Star boardshorts on, and as always sunglasses adorn his face as he sits back poolside. Oh, did I mention beautiful women surround him? A blonde with big tits on his right and a brunette with even bigger tits on his left.** Cannon: This is the fucking life.... ** Cannon looks at both the women and he takes a sip of his champagne.** Cannon: What the fuck was I thinking sitting out in the desert raising some fucking dogs? These hand are far to precious to do manual labor. I'm more suited to be sitting poolside, drinking champagne, having a threesome with two hotties and letting my money work for me! **Cannon looks at each woman again.** Cannon: Speaking of threesomes, can you ladies go to the bedroom? I've got some work to do here, give me about 20 minutes and we'll have some fun. ** He lowers his glasses and winks at each of the women and they giggle a bit. Both stand up and walk away in their very, very small bikinis. Cannon put his glasses back up around his eyes smiles and lets out a big sigh of relief. But all good things must come to an end, Ace hears his name ring out and the voice is familiar, it's Dick Malibu.** Dick: Ace, daddy-o how's life? **Cannon looks at Dick and just smiles.** Cannon: Dick, it's not often that I say this but you were right. Beverley Hill is my home, this is were I grew up and this is my element. Dick: HA! I told ya. You are Beverley Hills and Beverley Hill is you. Sure, your a sick fuck but high society is what you know, not some dog farming. Cannon: I agree. Now, what made you stop by? Dick: Brother, I dropped off some business plans in your office and to see if you know anything about Eric Derf putting out a promo. Cannon: I don't know anything about Def putting out a promo. I have been to busy hitting the clubs, having one night stands and sitting out in the sun. Eric Def doesn't concern me, he was a joke then and he's probably still a joke. He's cheesier than Windy Bridges at a karaoke bar. ** Dick shakes his head in response to Ace's statement.** Dick: I hate to be the bearer of bad news.... But Eric Derf has grown up. ** Cannon lowers his glasses and just looks at Dick Malibu, his look basically says “get real.” He puts his glasses back up and lays back.** Dick: Daddy-O, I'm serious. He isn't that goofy jokester type anymore, he is going to be a force here in MWE. **Cannon just lays their in his chair soaking up the rays.** Dick: Ace, I've got things to do but you better take Eric Derf serious. Cannon: Dick, I get the point. Dick: I hope you a loss in the re-birth of MWE for it's most controversial figure wouldn't be good for you. ** Dick looks at Cannon waiting for a response and doesn't get one. He just turns his back and walks away and exits the lavish backyard. Cannon goes back to basking under the glow of the hot California sun, but the camera pans back around and he's got another visitor. This man is a real familiar face to MWE, he's wearing a fuschia colored polo shirt, light khaki pants, light brown boat shoes, and his bleached mane of hair is slicked back. It's Ace's father, former MWE owner and chairmen Richard Covell!** Richard: Ace, get up. **Cannon doesn't move he just sits there, he is hears his father but he's ignoring the old leathery skinned bastard.** Richard: Listen you son of a bitch, I know you hear me. So cut the childish bullshit out and listen too me. **Cannon motions with his hand for this father to continue.** Richard: Ace, as you know I lost MWE and it's no longer mine. This is the biggest regret ever in my life, I lived and breathed this company and now it's no longer mine. Now there is a silver lining in all this, your back in MWE and you can carry on the family legacy in MWE... ** This grabs Ace's attention and it makes him sit upright in his chair, he takes off his glasses and his eyes are glowing with anger. He stands up and gets almost face to face with his father.** Cannon: How dare you come here and patronize me! Come here and say that I'm going to carry on the family legacy in MWE..... Well I have news for you, DAD! I don't want this family legacy, it sucks and is full of failure and regret! **As Cannon finishes his last sentence Covell slaps him across the face! Cannon is stunned, he grabs his face and fury and rage light up in his eyes yet he doesn't hit his father back.** Richard: ACE! Everything you just said is true and I know this. I'm here to help you, I want you to succeed here in MWE and I want you to re-write this famlies legacy and it starts with Eric Derf weather you realize it or not! You have all the tools to be the top dog here, you've been close and this time around it's your turn, but your sitting here pissing it away. **Covell stops and just looks at Ace before continuing.** Richard: Son, get your head right. I saw Dick when I came out and we both agree Eric Derf is not the same guy as he used to be, he's gotten better and you need to realize this. Get your head in the game, I'm going to leave but remember this is yours to lose and no one elses. **Ace looks at his dad and slowly nods his head in agreement. Ace offers his hand to his father to shake and Richard take it, but as he does Ace hits him with a devastating right hook sending Covell down to the ground. Ace bends down and talk to his father.** Cannon: Listen, DAD! I don't need a pep talk from you, if I wanted a pep talk from a failure I'd go watch the movie RUDY. So do us both a favor and never talk to me again and get the fuck off my propery.... And one more thing, me succeeding in MWE is for myself and myself only... Fuck you and our family. **Cannon walks off leaving his dad, Richard Covell laying on the ground. Cannon has a sick smirk on his face as he enters the house. He walks to the living room and sits down on a huge and plush brown leather couch. He just stares at the ceiling for a minute before talking.** Cannon: Derf let's face it, that last promo I cut was soft. Hell, it was softer than baby shit and it wasn't my best product, everyone knows it. When I came home the other night I watched and then compared it to my other work from MWE and it wasn't on par, one of my worst promo's ever and it's because I wasn't in my element. **Cannon stops, he scratches his chin pondering what his next words will be.** Cannon: Sitting here I'm in my element and my mind is where I need it to be. Out in the desert I was trying to act ruthless and aggressive and it wasn't working, it's something that must naturally come to me. My buttons must be pushed and as you just saw out there my father did that, my aggression exploded and I knocked his old ass out! Now me having to face you is also pushing my buttons, so I know that aggression will come out in the ring and you have never, ever, ever faced someone as ruthless and aggressive as me. ** Cannon sits up on the couch, he gets closer to the camera and his face is looking deadly serious.** Cannon: You see Derf, I know two things in life. One, living high life and number two is being the best wrestler to ever set foot in that ring. Sure, I don't have the accomplishments, but call up Joseph Sharpmen, DC, Brian Michaels, even David Whitman and they will all tell you that I'm a monster in that ring. **Cannon stops and rubs his chin. He averts his gaze for a moment but he is soon staring back at the camera with that cold ruthless gaze.** Cannon: If you don't want to call them go review the tapes. Go look at my vicotry over Whitman, go look at my victory over Michaels, go look at my EW win over DC, or go watch the closely contested bloodbaths me and Joseph Sharpmen had! All of thoes matches showed my potential as a world champion! Yea, in the past I thing outside the ring get in my way but that's who I am. I'll continue to be the controversial character but this time it won't stop me! ** Cannon stands up and continues looking at the camera. His eyes are giving out that can be described as a laser intensity and focus.** Cannon: If want to kick pregnant women and kids then I will! If I want to call everyone and their brother out I will! I still don't care Derf and I never will. You know why? Because, in the immortal words of Eminem.... I JUST DON'T GIVE A FUCK! ** Cannon takes a deep breath trying to regain his composure.** Cannon: The four main things in my life are champagne, glamor, sex and respect. Derf, I have the first four and come Monday you'll give me my respect! If you don't then that lady I've heard you knocked up will receive a visit from me and that visit wouldn't be pretty. It would consist of me kicking your demon seed right out of her! I've done it before and I WON'T HESITATE TO DO IT AGAIN! ** Cannon stand up and smiles, it's a sick smile one that Cannon is known for.** Cannon: Now I'll see everyone Monday and Derf, I hope to God that your ready. I'm in my fucking zone and you better be as well, if not the King of Controversy will chew you up and spit you out! **Cannon begins to walk off but he stops and turns around....** Cannon: Oh and one more thing... You can bank on that! ** He continues walking off and enters a room and the camera fades out to the sound of girly giggling.**
