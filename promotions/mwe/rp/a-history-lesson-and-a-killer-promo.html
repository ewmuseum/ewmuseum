A History Lesson and A Killer Promo; A Grand Mystique RP

Nov 5 2009

The Grand Mystique

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Alex Wood Reid Reynolds Mr Thomas Eli QUOTE Twelve years ago The echoing slap of perspiring skin battering on plastic around the small school hall is inimitable. “Again.” Slap! “Again.” Slap! We see two adolescents, a blond guy and his taller, raven haired partner. They are soaked with sweat but obey the command nonetheless. The shorter of the two shoots for a takedown and after a brief struggle, he forcefully turns him around and slams him down on his back. He leans back and offers a hand to his partner who accepts the gesture as they rise to their feet. “Time to call it a day, gentlemen.” An older man with greying dark hair in a blue tracksuit and top stands up from a bench at the side where he has been observing his charges He allows them both to regain their breath and wipe themselves down with towels. He hands them a bottle each and a leaflet each. “Now as you know the school has cut the funding of the department, but what I hadn’t told you is that this would be our last class.” “You have got to be kidding me!” Reid shouts loudly as Alex throws his bottle against the nearest wall. The teacher raises his hands calmly in a bid to calm his two students. “Just have a read of those leaflets.” “The Kick Punch Slam Academy is looking for new recruits to train in cage fighting and wrestling. Come to our tryouts this weekend.” Alex read it aloud, almost unable to take in the meaning of the words. “That’s great.” “Yeah....I guess. Thank you Mr Thomas” He seemed happy at how the gesture was received. “You two have made this worthwhile. This is just the next logical step. This class being stopped may in fact be the best thing – you two are too good to keep only under my training and the KPSA is the best thing for you. They don’t come down this way too often.” The concerned stare of the two young men had turned into enthusiastic smiles. “Now go get changed.” No more than five minutes later, having broken their record for getting changed, they were standing outside, chatting excitedly about the future as their coach locked up. “I can’t believe that we are going to the KPSA.” “This is going to be awesome!” “You ready lads?” The trainer emerges then turns to lock up the door behind him. It was then that the darkness came. From out of the shadows, a clutch of giant faceless figures emerge and attack the two boys and Mr Thomas. Before the rush of self defence can kick in, the teacher gets clubbed from behind and smashed headfirst into the glass of the door that he was trying to lock. The glass shatters down onto the paving and becomes ruby red in the stream of blood that pours from him. The boys get isolated and are overpowered in seconds by the numbers. They are brutally pummelled against the corners of the wall. Soon, blood covers their beaten faces, dripping onto the floor and staining the brickwork. Suddenly a bolt of pain shoots through Alex’s face. In what feels like slow motion, he clutches his ear and feels a hot rushing sensation running through his neck. He reaches out and realises the wound gapes behind his ear and the flap of skin brushes by his fingers. The blood is now gushing onto his palm. Alex sees his friend unconscious and his face is covered with blood. Behind him, Mr Thomas, the man who had become his mentor, was lying against the fractured glass door among a pool of his own blood. An intense surge of adrenaline pushes through Alex’s system with every increasing heartbeat. He lashes out, filled with desperation-fuelled rage. His friends destroyed, his dreams ruined; his aggression is focussed and channelled. We see the scene ending abruptly with him, charging into the group of thugs who were still in the area. QUOTE Six months earlier We see the two lads just as they finish getting changed. Reid reaches into his backpack and pulls out a blank video tape and puts it on the side. “You watch wrestling, right?” “Yeah occasionally, I’m not keen on it though. It’s too cartoony for me.” Reid smiles and nods. “Yeah I hate it. They’ve ruined it for me.” He picks up the tape. “You should try this. It’s Japanese, it’s a little weird and you can’t understand the commentary but the action is beyond awesome. The stuff they show over here is nothing like this.” Alex looks at it warily. He only watched wrestling for a laugh at the garish outfits worn by the over the top characters. Even since he had been forced by his mum and the school to give wrestling a try, he had found the whole escapade even more ridiculous. On his way home, he found his breath lighting up in front of him. Yet despite the cold, he was kept buzzing and warm by the mere intrigue of the tape. Reid had enthused so much about the Japanese style but Alex couldn’t let himself believe it was that good. But Alex was willing to try anything once as life had been good in the six months that he had been going to wrestling class. There had been fewer arguments and no punch ups, which had pleased everyone. While Alex wouldn’t put it solely down to his after school interest, he would concede that he had enjoyed it. He had a real friend in Reid and that had helped. School and life were both very rewarding at present. He walked in to the small apartment that he shared with his mum. He knew he was on his own for a while tonight, and instead of housework or homework, he put the tape in. Their dusty old TV fuzzed into action and their video player whirred into gear. Within seconds, among little to no razzamatazz, these wrestlers were performing. It lacked the production values of the American programming he watched yet he sat two metres away from the screen, enthralled by the blistering action and the magnificent story they were able to tell. Even in a foreign tongue, it was memorable. The final match on the video was between two men who wore these bizarre face-covering creations which were able to hide all but their eyes. These wrestlers had an endearing, mythical quality that Alex found totally endearing. These masks served to only heighten their personality and accentuated the good versus evil battle that was on show. These Japanese wrestlers were simple characters, understated and unique but able to let their ring work do the talking. We see a projector that lights up a dim room. The machine hums in the background and is paused on the close up image of a stocky man, wearing a black wrestling mask and a matching body suit outfit. “That’s when I knew. I knew what I wanted to do with my life. It didn’t matter that exercise left me in agony or that we had little to nothing with which to fund this crazy dream.” “Yet I felt compelled, to get to wear one of those masks, to tell that story and be the guy who was on these kind of videos.” The camera falls away from the screen. In the dim room, we see the Grand Mystique slouching in a low arm chair. He speaks in a dead serious manner, which betrays his mood of the past few days. “And now I am here, before you as exactly that man because although I am on the low of the low, I know that in the ring, I can and I will continue to run rings around anyone.” “i’m not champion anymore because I neglected to seize the opportunity presented on a plate to me. I know that I am better than what has been shown over the past few weeks. Crowning Glory is a career highlight but since then I have failed to fulfil my destiny. I may never reach that level again.” He rubs his nose, which is swollen and bruised at the same point which broke and bled, causing his blood to be the reason that he is no longer the Heavyweight Champion of the World. “But I know that no matter how badly I fail, my life will not suck as much as Oblivion, my opponent on Monday and perennial ghost of Zack Crash.” Oblivions’ grimacing face appears on screen. His wide eyes show a desperate man, trying to be intense and intimidating but managing to fail on so many levels. “Oblivion hides his face from the world, for no other reason than he enjoys the luxurious anonymity it affords him. I am not one to criticise that lifestyle choice but this obscurity is protecting him while he allows a perverse obsession to sicken everyone. He has gone too far but he doesn’t care. He has gone so far past the point of being reasonable; he can’t see it on the horizon. He has mistaken this anonymity for power, when truly, he has in fact lost control of any power he never really had a long time ago.” “Protecting your identity from the world works both ways. Just like he uses his mask to act out fantastical desire, he has created a world with no boundaries so he can go over the top and do things most people would consider extraordinarily vicious.” We see the prevailing image of Oblivions career so far. He stands over the brilliant Zack Crash, with blood on his hands. GM nods knowingly at the achievement and the comparison, given how he lost the World Title, he has to chuckle a little. “I should know; I mean everything Oblivion does, like torturing Zack Crash or haunting those women to a nauseating degree, is a painful reminder that I have lost the fear factor. Oblivion has that in moderation and to his credit he deserves his place. But when he has replaced me as the most menacing wrestler on the roster, that tells me that I need to sort myself out and get back to where I used to be, because it won’t be far to go. The picture of Oblivion and Crash fades, to be replaced with a similar one of the Grand Mystique standing over Windy Bridges. Bridges wears an intense crimson mask and GM’s chest is discoloured by the blood of his nemesis. He smiles at the memory. “However if I was the ultimate villain, Oblivion is the pantomime dame compared to me. The violence that took place at my hands took place in the ring and nothing has compared. Not now, not ever.” We see another picture appear on the screen. We see GM having been back suplexed off the ring apron. He has landed awkwardly with the top of his neck and his head making contact with the mat first. GM rubs his neck ruefully as he addresses the camera directly. “Just in case Oblivion has not got the picture. NO ONE cares about your damn neck injury! You’re loopy if you think that anyone has real sympathy for anyone in this business. This is wrestling and it’s real; serious injuries will happen!! You have no long term effects of an injury that happened years ago– you can wake me up when you struggle to sit up in the morning because until then I don’t need to care!” He stands up slowly, as if to demonstrate his final point. He is clearly in some discomfort himself. “At this point, I have pain in every moment of my life. I dare you to even think that you can make me suffer any more than I have already.” His grimace speaks volumes for his attitude at this point. His tone is beyond negative. He walks up the projector screen and takes in this picture, this memory of himself at his most memorable and successful. “Talking of pain, your self-serving addiction to Zack Crash will do nothing for you or your career. He is just better than you; you have nothing on him. And while he soars higher, your jealousy and desire for absent revenge will only paralyze you and leave you lying at the bottom.” He speaks with a hint of renewed enthusiasm. “Unfortunately you are oblivious to the ways of life outside of your little bubble, where you rule all from your dingy little warehouse. However your pimping powers are as effective in the real world as a chocolate teapot. I suggest you begin dealing with plain and simple fact. And the fact is that I am coming off a crushing loss, but I promise never to make the same mistake. I leave no one standing, because I will claim your soul, wreck your destiny and show that you are truly irrelevant in MWE. I will show you true pain but you will never experience loss like I have.” When he speaks of pain and loss, his voice trembles with grit and determination. This is the Grand Mystique at his most raw and vulnerable, dare it be thought that he is not extremely dangerous at this point. He turns off the projector, allowing the light of the room to show his gaunt features. “No one cares about you, your face, your purpose or aim in life. But if you think you matter, then you really are oblivious.” “I’m beyond caring about what you can throw at me, because I know I am that much better. When it is all said and done, Oblivion and whoever you are behind the facade will fail to nothingness.” He walks off as the camera fades. QUOTE Two days ago GM storms through the office leaving the receptionist stunned in his wake. He walks up to the desk and drives his fist down onto the table. The bang is loud. The receptionist shrieks and hides. Eli, meanwhile, is a picture of calmness and tranquillity. He opens his eyes and sees GM really close in from of him. His eyes are clearly ready to burn through anything, yet Eli stares back with peaceful and relaxing sentiment. “I want a rematch Eli, at WrestleWars. It’s really fucking simple. Make it happen” Eli takes a sip of the drink to his right; still amazingly calm. “Really? Now let me guess, you’ve got a big argument about why you should right?” “No. I shouldn’t need to.” Eli raises a questioning eyebrow. “No?” “NO!” “Well let me make this real fucking simple GM. I thought you were a clever sumbitch but I’ll slow it down for you.” “Now look Eli!” GM’s notorious lack of patience appears to be wearing thin. Yet Eli sees this and decides to continue. “No you look, you grand waste of time! You’ve come in here every other week when you want something and I’ve had fucking enough. You lost fair and square at Turmoil, CSB is just better than you. Just like Zack Crash was before him and like Mason Resoan was before him.” “Dammit, what the hell are you trying to say Eli?” GM’s temper flares but Eli matches him. He stands up and although he is shorter than GM, his posture makes him much more formidable. “You had your chance.” “What?!” “I’m saying that you were a massive waste of space as MWE champion! You need to step back, sort out your shit and get back what made you. You have gone backwards quicker than my whang after I shoot my load! What’s up with your shit at the moment?” Eli’s tone is demanding and accusing. GM looks down, slightly disheartened. Outtalked by Eli Silver? Yeah it just happened. “You know what? I’m....” “You’re what? Quitting MY show? GM....you’ll do what I say.” Eli was digging for answers for sure, but was GM ready to give them? “This is my show and you are a part of it.” “Yeah, you put me on a team with men that won’t trust me. That’s brilliant management.” “Oh I know you didn’t just question my choices.” Eli is immediately defensive as he has had his business decisions questioned and the utter professional in him is hurt. ”It is what’s best for the federation. You have a problem; you deal with it like men. Shoot some pool, fuck some bitches, I don’t care!” “Yeah let’s see how that goes. Last time I saw Sinc, he was broken and lifeless at Crowning Glory thanks to me. And let’s just say that DC and I will never see eye to eye. Plus Derf, ugh, I can’t believe you think this will work. Besides, what is going to stop me ruining this whole experiment?” GM is aware that he is provoking Eli. It seems at the moment he doesn’t care. “Your precious contract. I could cut you in a second. And I could make sure you never get a sniff of any title.” “Eli, I don’t give two hoots at the moment and for the record, the contract was never that important. Everything I thought I cared for and thought that was worthwhile is gone. At this exact moment the entire damn roster is on notice because I couldn’t give a fuck anymore. So Team MWE, the GWC fuck up that has brought this on, I don’t care what goes on.” His tone is so serious, monotonous. Eli can’t tell whether he is serious or not. Yet given his reputation and the outcome of recent events, he has to assume that GM means everything that he says. Eli steps up to GM and calmly begins to talk. “All I’m going to say is think very carefully about your actions. Anything I don’t like will have consequences. So STOP FUCKING UP!” “Trust me, I’ve done too much thinking. Just know my priorities have changed and your consequences are not top of the list. Monday’s match is going to be a rebirth” It’s been a while since Eli has seen this attitude from GM. Indeed it’s the kind of thing he would encourage to see more of. “OK?” “You don’t want to give me a rematch? You want me to go through with this bullshit excuse for a war?” “Yes that’s exactly what I want you to do.” Both men rise and form intimidating shapes. GM’s aggression has riled Eli’s ego of authority. “Then Oblivion will know that the consequences of my actions will hurt a lot more than the consequences of his. And then the toll of casualties will fall on your shoulders. The pain, the agony will be down to you and the darkness will flood the MWE as you know it. You are going to have no fucking choice but to give me a title shot.” “Well it’s my show, so it’s my choice whether you like it or not. So get the fuck out of my office.” GM chuckles and shakes his head. He knows Eli doesn’t need to pander to his needs or demands now and maybe his new contract is not even worth the paper it’s written on. Not that he cared. “You’ll regret this Eli.” The door slams shut behind, which leaves a seething Eli, frustrated by the entire conversation. The glass he was drinking from previously gets smashed against the wall and he turns the air blue with curse.
