Changes; Part 1

Sep 7 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The camera fades in to show stars. At first sight you'd think it was the camera looking up at the sky but suddenly something looms over it, a huge metal object is seen. As it passes closer to the camera you can hear beeping and soon a hatch is visible with three letters on it. USA. The satellite continues to move slowly as it passes completely over the camera and the beeping seems to get farther and farther away in space. The camera moves to the left a bit to show the earth spinning slowly in the emptiness. The camera slowly gets closer to the earth and accelerates faster as time passes until its going faster than a freefall and a city is seen. As the camera continues to descend faster and faster it suddenly stops right above a street full of people doing their morning commute. The city is Indianapolis and the man the camera is slowly descending upon is Aiden Copane. The camera slowly switches angles to show Aiden from the front. He is wearing black Armani sunglasses and a full Armani suit. He is being engulfed by people walking around him but hes not moving an inch. He then leans against a wall He takes off his sunglasses and then looks up at the sky from where the camera came from and then looks back into the camera. Aiden Copane: Welcome ladies and gentlemen, before you ask, you might think that I haven’t made any changes in my life, and if you did think that, you’d be wrong. I decided I’m gunna do something to keep you guys tuning in. Instead of the previously mentioned change I was going to make, it’ll be the first of three changes I’m making to shake things up a bit, the first, will be revealed. In fact, maybe we should get our asses going. Aiden gets off of the wall and gets into the traffic of people walking in the same direction as the camera follows him. Aiden Copane: I think this week’s addition to my life is the most important one, but don’t worry the next two are going to be just as good, but maybe a little less juicy. *Laughs* Moving on to more serious matters, Aeron Beowulf, damn, to be honest I didn’t think you’d make it this far in MWE. For that I have to congratulate you. Too bad you won’t be moving any farther, you’ve reached the wall, the wall of Aiden. Let me make this as clear as I can put it, I’m going to walk out of that ring tonight, with gold around my waist and a body lying in the ring, or outside of it, depends on how the match goes. Ah, here we are. Aiden looks up at a rather tall building which is visibly cleaner than the rest of the apartment buildings around it. Over the door written in golden letters it reads “Trump Golden Tower”. Aiden steps into the doors and walks over to a board near the elevator and finds on which floor he’s going to be stopping at. He finds what he’s looking for, the 14th floor. He presses the button for the elevator to come down and get him but one was already at the lobby floor. He stops into it as the camera follows and the doors close. Aiden Copane: Holy shit… what a slow moving elevator, when I meet Donald Trump for brunch later this week I’ll have to let him know to fix this shit. Well…since we’ll be on this elevator for another two years I might as well comment on Aeron’s own promo. I watched it, since I always like to be a step ahead of my opponent but this “thing”. I’m not even sure what it was. I mean, where was he? He was in a field…with sheep. What the fuck man? Another thing, what kind of god damn cab company comes to wherever the hell he was to pick up people with paint on their faces? That’s just weird… For fuck sakes what floor are we on? 6th? Are you kidd…I don’t know what else I should say... Camera Man: *Whispering* James Sync... Aiden Copane: James Sync? Who’s that? ... Really? ... You don’t say!? Wow... I guess I’m not always a step up on my “opponents”. I’ve just been informed that it’s a triple threat match. Is this new? No? Wow, I didn’t know...Well, I can’t really comment on James... Who is he? Rockstar? Are you serious? Well in that case you can go fuck yourself James Sync, you stupid motherfucker. Ding Aiden Copane: Holy shit! Salvation... Who wants to talk about James Sync anyway? I know I don’t. The elevator door slides open and a few steps ahead, behind a glass wall are several female teens practicing their ballet. They were all graceful, but when you looked at them, one of them was more graceful than the rest. Suddenly they all turned in the direction of the glass wall, according to the instruction of the ballet teacher. The most graceful dancer of them all looked over at Aiden and a huge smile came across her face. She disregarded all of her teacher’s pleas to stop as she ran towards the door leading to where Aiden was. She swung the door open and Aiden smiled at her, she ran at him and lunged up and jumped on him. Michelle: Baby! You finally made it to one of my practices! I’m so glad you came baby, just like I’m going to be with you when you fight those guys tomorrow. Aiden Copane: Baby, you really didn’t have to run out of practice, I think she’s gunna be all pissy now. Michelle: Oh, I don’t care, she’s a bitch anyway, I’m just really glad you’re here. The ballet teacher makes her way over to the door and swings it open. She walks over to Aiden and Michelle and attempts to rip Michelle out of Aiden’s arms. Teacher: *With Accent* What are you doing to her? She’s just a girl, and how dare you take her away from me in the middle of practice! She has to get ready for her competition. Let her practice. Aiden puts Michelle down onto the floor behind him and turns around to face the ballet teacher who is now starting to slap him in the chest for touching her. Aiden Copane: First of all you bitch, she’s 18, she can think for herself. Second of all, MWE is more important than your stupid ballet class. Third, don’t touch me again. The ballet teacher starts to back away from Aiden and he begins to turn around and face Michelle before he goes back, charges at her and spears her down to the floor. Aiden gets up and examines his work. He turns around to see Michelle smiling at him when suddenly he hears static. He turns around to see the TV in the ballet lobby on. He walks over to it and turns it off then he turns around and he hears static again. Aiden Copane: What the hell is with this shit... not again. First at my mansion, then on my jet, and now, here? Why does this keep happening? Aiden punches in the screen and walks back to Michelle, grabs her hand and pushes the elevator button. Static. --- Yes the tv's have a significance. you'll see.
