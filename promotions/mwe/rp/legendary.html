Legendary; Dedicated to Misawa 1962-2009

Jun 18 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
***I apologize for not continuing my "Son/Mother" storyline, however this roleplay is dedicated to Misawa so I don't want to override that with the storyline. It's only for this week.*** “What lies behind us and what lies before us are tiny matters compared to what lies within us.” Walt Emerson, -What can be said during the living days of the legend? The days that create, that processed this legend. Nobody can predict who will be a legend. There is no obviousness that make it likely who will become one. A legend has a thousand definitions, yet no instructions or manual to read on how to become one. Success? Being unique? Passion? There have been a myriad of concepts such as these that really make us ponder on what the secret ingredient of legend hood. Look at any legend, you’ll find some rich, some poor, some big, some small, some that work forever, those that never work. That’s the thing about legends, those who are cannot describe it nor even accept the fact they are. And the rest of us wouldn’t have a clue because it is so abstract to what our usual lives throw at us. In the world of wrestling, a major way of making your statement towards becoming legend is by becoming champion. To excel beyond all other competition to prove that you are a dominant member of your division. The path to becoming a champion is filled with rough patches and obstacles. Obstacles that will attempt at destroying your career, permanently. And as Riddick makes his way down that path, there are two obstacles that lie in his way to touching the surface known as the MWE X-TV Championship. Those two people are the arrogant Anthony Clark, and the rough-houser of MWE Chris England. But in this case, one of these obstacles will leave and the other will cling on for one last life at becoming X-TV Champion at Crowning Glory. In this 3-way match there is one winner who moves on, the man who does not get pinned or submits that moves on, however the one that falls at the end loses the possibility of becoming champion at Crowning Glory. Riddick is sitting at a computer desk typing and reading at his condo in Niagara Falls, reading and typing as the clicks and tapping of the buttons is all that is heard as the sound echoes and bounces off the walls. The sound of wheels rolling as he sways back and forth reading the messages. Riddick is on the MWE Fan Club Forums, reading one particular thread about predictions for this weeks episode of MNM. He sighs while reading it as he being to speak.- Riddick: It happens every week, every day, every moment of my life. It isn’t enough for people anymore. I’m honestly surprised I hadn’t seen this coming. I’m not an idiot, I’m not oblivious to technology, I know that wrestling fans and so-called “critics” wander around these sites thinking they know what goes on in the ring and who’s really in it. And what really ticks me off about this whole situation is that they believe they know Jay Riddick, and according to their “expertise” my uprising is over, done, finished forever. -Riddick rolls out of the front of his computer, showing the poll results as to who they believe will win between him, Anthony Clark and Chris England. With Chris England having a big lead ahead of everybody else.- Riddick: Do I look like a joke to you people? Have you not seen my in that squared circle demolishing every bit of competition they put in front of me? See this occurrence of people never realizing how good I am has happened all of my life. I was told I’d never be the best at anything, hell, they told me I’d never be somebody. It was never Jay Riddick’s moment, it was never Jay Riddick’s time. Every time I had my shot to prove myself, I trained to make it excellent, I trained to show people that I was for real. But every time I got shafted for some idiots to get their 500th shot. Why? Because nobody cares for Jay Riddick, nobody cares about me. Well I’m going to tell you people something, forget about the downfall of the economy, forget about viruses, forget about everything in 2009, because 2009 is the year of “The Uprising” Jay Riddick, which will set forth my destructive path into the future years. -Riddick is walking away from the computer as the camera fades to black. The camera then shows Riddick outside of the condominium in the parking lot, he strolls through dozens of cars as he continues to speak.- Riddick: All I have to do now is defeat Anthony Clark, something I’ve done before quite easily. See Clark, you’ve failed at defeating Nic Kraven last week, once again I got shafted. They wasted the title match on you Clark, and what have you done to deserve it? Nothing, absolutely nothing. And what did I do while you wasted your opportunity? I was busting Virus and Scotty McKnight’s head till they bled. And now I’m at the spot where I’m still fighting to get my shot. So Clark, it’d be in your best interest to stay out and not get involved in the match. Because if you can stay out of the ring and not get pinned than you’ll get what you got last week, an unearned title match. Because god forbid should I find you in that ring this week or I’ll do to you what I did to you last time and I will make you submit. -Riddick finds his modest silver car, and enters. He turns it on and drives it off and out of the parking lot, as the camera fades to black once again. Only for the camera to show himself by the majestic beauty known as Niagara Falls. Riddick stands against the barrier only to see the Horseshoe Falls directly behind him. He begins to speak once again.- Riddick: Chris England, I’ve seen you in that ring before, and I know your style. You’re your own contrast, big but fast, rough yet smooth and agile, it’s almost impossible to come up with a game plan to match your style. But of everything you are, you’ll never be Jay Riddick. Because being Jay Riddick, I’m going to do what I do best and I’m going to kick some British behind. See that’s why I took myself here, this isn’t just Niagara Falls, this is the line between Canada and the United States, this is where I feel neutral. I’m near both of my hometowns but more importantly there is a contrast here that I want to explain. -Riddick turns and looks back at the falls only to look back into the camera. His face already tells that he has a story, a theory or even a revelation about to be told.- You and me Chris, we’re so much different, our styles are different, our nations are different, and our appearances are different. But yet it’s those differences that make us the exact same. We both like to get our hands dirty with blood of others, we both like to win and we both have that drive. But sadly, your drive is nowhere near mine. I want to be champion very badly, I would do anything to be champion, but I would never forget who I am. And that’s where I find you’ll wind up. You will do anything Chris, even if it meant forgetting who you are, and that is why you’ll never be any greater. So here I am at the Falls by Peace Bridge, I’m at the line between one extremity to the other. In life I’ve crossed those lines and I’ve stayed back away from crossing them. But I never forget about who I am as a person, I’m not just a fighter, I’m not just a warrior, I’m not just a champion, I am and will always be Jay Riddick. This week I win the three way match, and at Crowning Glory, I assure you I will become 5 and 0, and I will also become your new MWE X TV Champion, why? Because this may be the only chance I will be given and failure is NOT, I repeat NOT an option. -Riddick simply turns and glances towards the falls simply witnessing its inspiring motion. It’s no different this day than any other day, but still for years and years it’s been observed as a sight of inspiration. And yet it’s been unchanged for over one hundred years. Time changes, but sometimes people don’t. Sometimes people do change with time but that comes with a price of forgetting who we truly are. People corrupt themselves by seizing every opportunity at any given price. In the wrestling world, people have tendencies to change drastically, or they never change at all. So it all revolves around this one question we pondered this last little while, what makes a legend? Is it the smile you put on the faces of those who watch you? Is it the tingling sensation you get when people chant your name? Being a legend means being remembered forever, and those who are never forgotten are those that are cautious on how they change as the times change. And that’s where people are forgotten. When they corrupt themselves just to get a shot at glory that even they can’t recognize themselves. Those people have their names drawn in the sands of time, only to be blown away eventually. Nothing to remember as it’s no longer there. Legends have their names engraved in stone, never to be blown away in time, never to be forgotten.- ***Roleplay is dedicated to the memory of Mitsuharu Misawa 1962-2009***
