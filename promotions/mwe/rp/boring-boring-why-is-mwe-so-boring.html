Boring, boring, why is MWE so boring?

May 29 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
*The scene opens on a friday afternoon in Peter's hotel room. He is awakened by the sound of his doorbell ring franticly. Peter sits on the right side of his bed and looks down on the floor staring at his feet and the beautiful Armenian carpet on the floor.* *Peter looks in the mirror and sees his gorgeous reflection in the mirror. He smiles at his reflection, winks, and then does the cocky I'm hot gun thing to the mirror. Peter gets up, stretches, and lets out big yawn and the sounds of his back cracking is heard. Peter is wearing nothing, but his boxer briefs as he walks towards the door. Peter walks by his dresser deciding against putting anything on while answering the door. He walks by a leather couch and finally answers the door. To his surprise Becky is standing outside his door and she stares at him looking from head to toe and back again. She is wearing a red skirt with a pink top on. She has ruby red lipstick on and not to much mascara. The always excited Becky yells out in delight as a huge smile comes across her face and she comes right along in Peter's suite. She looks at the suite in amazement. Finally she begins to speak in a very fast and excited tone. Peter looks at her and thinks to himself, "This bitch is crazy."* Becky: Oh my God this suite is amazing. It definitely looks better inside then from outside on the roof of that building? *Becky points outside as Peter cautiously backs up away from Becky, not knowing what she might do next.* *Becky continues to speak in a very excited tone as Peter is behind the suite's bar pressing the security button.* Becky: So anyway I bet your wondering why I am here, other then the fact that we are getting married in June. I am here to bring you back your cell phone! *Becky puts her hand in her purse and she begins to fumble around in there looking for something. Peter immediately drops down to the floor thinking she is going to pull out a gun. She finally pulls out Peter's Venus from D-Wade's party celebrating Edge's title win over Jeff Hardy.* *Peter carefully gets up hearing no gun shots. He looks around the room and sees no bullet holes so he gets up. Becky over to the bar and sits on a stool as she hands Peter his phone. Becky begins to speak in a seductive voice, as Peter looks at her in terror.* Becky: You know what we should do? We should ignore the priest's advice and just have our way with each other. Wouldn't you like that? *Peter finally has enough and talks to her as if she were dog. Becky just smiles and nods her head with widened and excited eyes.* Peter: Listen you psychotic bitch, we are not going out and we sure as hell aren't getting married. Leave me alone. I do not like you and I never will. *Becky backs up devastated and puts her right hand over chest and her left hand over her right. In an over the top dramatic tone Becky begins to speak at a puzzled Peter.* Becky: How could you say such a thing? I though what we had was special? *Peter looks at her puzzled. Becky begins to speak in a worried and frantic tone. Her eyes show fear and regret.* Becky: Was that not good? I am sorry guys lets take this promo from the top. Peter maybe you should hit me you know to really get the fans against you. *Just as she says that a beautiful woman walks out of Peter's bedroom with nothing on except some pink bunny slippers. Becky yells out in anger and hatred at this women.* Becky: WHORE!!!!!!!!! *Luckily before Becky can act on her emotions Peter's security stops her. They pick her up and carry her out of Peter's suite. The girl walks over to Peter as he explains to her what happened in a very egotistical voice.* Peter: You see Isabella, that girl is a psycho stalker who believes that we are going to get married in June. Every time I tell her its not going to happen and that I don't like her she says I am doing a good job staying in character. *Isabella looks confused, but just shakes it off after looking at Peter's abs. Isabella decides to address the situation the only way she knows how. She talks to Peter in a seductive voice.* Isabella: Well why don't we alleviate your stress like you alleviated mine last night? *Peter just shakes his head no and explains to Isabella the problem with that in a sort of down voice, but not to down of voice. Sort of like how Jesse, from Full House, talked to Rebecca.* Peter: Sorry, babe, but I got a house show I have to get to. Listen after the house show and my meeting with my D-Wade I'll come back here and we will get rid of some stress. *She nods her head in agreement and they share one more passionate kiss, before Peter gets ready to go. Peter grabs a button up shirt which he decides not to button and he puts on some levy designer jeans that were designed just for him. He walks out his door and down the hall to the elevator. He presses the up button and waits a few minutes and the elevator arrives. He presses garage level five as he listens to the elevator music. He walks out of the elevator and into the garage. He looks around at all the hideous cars, but finally finds his. Black mustang convertible.* *Peter enters his car and drives off to the arena. His hotel isn't that far from the arena and there is little traffic because it is already three o'clock. The roads in the Dominican Republic are very bad and that puts Peter in a very bad mood. He arrives at the MWE arena at three fifteen. He gets out of his car and locks it and as soon he turns around he is met by MWE Backstage Personnel Mickey. Mickey talks to Peter very cautiously as he has to be the bearer of bad news.* Mickey: Peter, your match against Sinc Mercier for his US Championship won't be happening as Management has decided that you'll be facing Jay Riddick. *Mickey cowers down thinking Peter is going to take his frustrations out on him, but Peter just smiles and laughs. Mickey begins to laugh to, until Peter pins him up against the wall and stares at him with angry eyes. Peter begins to yell at Mickey as he pins up against the wall.* Peter: You tell MWE Management Riddick versus Kaymakcian won't be happening! *Peter tosses Mickey down and he begins to walk out towards the ring. He is handed a mic very quickly by a member of the backstage personnel, knowing he has something to say. He walks out to the stage with no music playing as the crowd begins to boo him. He walks down the ramp and looks at the MWE fans with disgust. He rolls into the ring and begins to talk down at the MWE fans.* Peter: You people all disgust me, but that is not why I am out here. I am out here for one reason and one reason only and that is to talk about Chris England. How dare MWE Management take away my title shot, the very same week they make me go up against that has been Chris England. Chris' last win in MWE came over a month and I don't know how you even consider that win when Semi got himself disqualified in that match. England thinks that he can save his pathetic career, but he can't. He can't save his pathetic career because you need to win to save your career and Chris has shown that he can't win. Mayhem won't be a match, it will be an embarrassment. I will embarrass Chris in that ring, but the biggest embarrassment will be when Chris places his hands into his face and cries out I'll get it next week, knowing he won't. *Peter stops talking as he takes in all of the boos by the fans. It is as if the fans are feeding him, by booing him. His' eyes are closed and his' arms are stretched out and his' fingers waving towards his body. He is egging these fans on and they are going right along with it. He begins to speak again this time with a little less aggression and with a little more arrogance.* Peter: This will be a repeat of the civil war. America kicking Britain's ass. England how can you proud to be a citizen of a country were dentistry obviously doesn't exist? Sorry I might have confused you fans with those big words haven't I. Okay let me rephrase that. Chris how can you like to be a member of a country were teeth doctor don't exist? Was that simple enough for you morons? Chris while we are wrestling please don't smile as I do not want to be blinded by the yellowness of your teeth. I'll show you fans a picture of Chris smiling. Peter: Oops I guess I gave them the picture of Chris' wife. My bad, well actually Chris it's your bad for marrying that. Back onto the matter of hand, Chris I had to place a bag over your daughter's head before we had some "fun". Don't worry Chris it was before Tribulation and I made sure I wore a condom because I would not want to knock up that ugly bitch, wait I mean whore, wait I mean herpies for slurpies. *Peter is stopped by the fans booing as they feel he has crossed a line beyond the point of talking trash. They believe he has done the most vile tactics ever by bringing Chris' wife and daughter into play. He just waves them off and begins to exit the ring. His left leg is on the ring apron and he is between the middle and top rope when he stops and says one more thing in an arrogant and sickening voice.* Peter: Chris by the way I wasn't gentle. *Peter exits the ring with the fans booing louder then ever. Some fans are even throwing food and drinks at him as he just ignores them. He enters backstage where he sees a bunch of faces all staring at him with disbelief at what he had just said. He walks down the hall and towards his car with a stride in his walk. He gets to his car and he unlocks he door. He sees a note in his window and it reads, "Can't wait for tonight. XOXO you know who" *He smiles and he enters into his car. He begins to drive to the cafe where he will meet D-Wade. It is only five forty-five when he arrives and he sees D-Wade sitting there drinking his coffee. There is a cup already there as he takes a seat. D-Wade and Peter begin a friendly little conversation.* Wade: I got you a hot chocolate. Peter: How'd you know? Wade: Because that is what you always order. Peter: So what did the Heat Owners say? Wade: They said you could do not. MWE can have one match at our home opener during the half time show. The only condition is that you have to be one of the guys wrestling and a title has to be on the line. Peter: Cool looks I'll be defending my MWE Championship in the Continental Airlines Arena. *They both share a laugh which is cut off by Peter's phone ringing. It says he is being called from his own suite. Peter puts the call on speaker so Wade can hear the conversation to. Peter answers in suave and sexual tone.* Peter: Isabella babe daddy will be home soon. *Isabella cries out in fear.* Isabella: No, you need to come home now. That psycho is here and NOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!!!!!!!!!!!!. *The call becomes disconnected as Peter and Wade look at each other. Both head for there respective cars as they speed towards the hotel Peter is staying at. They park there cars in the garage and they run towards the elevator. Peter presses the down button as he begins to talk frantically.* Peter: Come on, come on, come on. Damn it, come on! *The elevator finally comes down to the garage as Peter and Wade both run into the elevator. Peter presses the level twenty-seven button and the elevator slowly moves up. After about three minutes the elevator finally stops at level twenty-seven. Both men get out of the elevator and run towards Peter suite. When they enter it, they find it shambles. The leather couch is flipped over, all the bar stools are knocked down, windows are broken, cereal boxes on the floor, and the plasma screen tv is busted. Both of there attention is taken towards the bedroom door where they sees Becky walk out in a wedding dress. She has a disturbed smile on her face as if she had just convinced herself that something wrong is right. She speaks out in an understanding tone.* Becky: Don't worry babe, I understand completely. You were testing our love by sleeping with that tramp. I am glad to say that we passed that test and that I got rid of that whore. We will be together forever. *Becky slowly walks out of Peter's suite. Peter quickly closes the door and locks it. Wade and Peter stare at each other as the scene fades to black.*
