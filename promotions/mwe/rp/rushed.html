The camera fades up on Warner, sitting at his computer. He has a bottle of Labatt Blue in his hand, and he seems to be reading some message boards.[/color] Hello boys and girls, today we are going to play a little game. It’s called “Let’s see what all the idiotic internet smarks have to say about MWE this week!” It’s a fun game that anyone can play, provided you have the internet. All you have to do is go to the message boards, find an idiotic post, and then respond! Let’s begin! Originally posted by: TNAGUY1543: So what do you guys think about Paige being a champion? I mean yeah, she is hot…but you got screwed man. Well TNAGUY, you bring up a very good point about last week’s match. What most people don’t know is that none of the people involved wanted such a finish, but it draws ratings so we can’t really complain. To be honest, I’m surprised that someone who is a TNA fan was able to process such a coherent and logical thought. Our next post is by someone with the totally badass username of “iMark4Sinc”. Jesus I’m scared to read this. MAN UP! Sit in the corner DeAngelo, and don’t speak unless spoken to. Originally posted by: iMark4Sinc: I’m tired of seeing how MWE management keeps pushing people like David Whitman, and keeps letting him hold onto the belt. There are so many other guys that deserve that title…like I don’t know…SINC MERCER!! I can’t blame him for walking out on the company like he did. He was the best thing MWE had! Who else was as AMAZING as he was???? IT WAS A GIANT FUCKING CONSPIRACY!!!!! ….Is this guy serious? It really wouldn’t surprise me if it was lil ole Sinc himself, sitting at home typing that because his boyfriend is too busy working the corner to support the two of them. Everyone can bitch about Whitman being champ, but all that means is that you people still talk about it, and still tune in hoping to see him lose. In the long run, having him as champ has been more beneficial then any other champ. Granted he might not be the most technically sound wrestler, but name one guy in that locker room with more charisma than him. You…Hardaway…the crackhead who shines your shoes… It was a rhetorical question De, you weren’t supposed to answer. Seriously, did your mom do crack when she was pregnant? ….yes. Now it all makes sense. Our next post comes from “The Real Tits McGee” Nice username by the way. Originally posted by: The Real Tits McGee: So is anyone else loving the Tag Team Division? We have the Vanguard Fucks…the Saintz of Jobbing…Uncultured Anarchy….a couple more and we could have a bonafide division. Tits, you bring up an amazing point. When I first joined MWE, the tag division was AMAZING. Then something happened along the way, and it got pushed to the backburner for a bit. I’m so happy that teams like Tex-Mex and Uncultured Anarchy are coming in, because now we can get the division back to the way it used to be. Look in the near future for some epic matches, hopefully between Billy Joe Fuckstick Alejandro, Kevin Hardaway and myself. As for the American Animal and his little boyfriend….I don’t know if you all have heard, but they have reportedly been released from the company due to something having to do with Ollie Williams. Yes, I’m talking about the fictional character from Family Guy. THEY GOT FIRED! Yes they did, and if you don’t shut the fuck up you will to. You wouldn’t fire me; Affirmative action would be on your ass quicker than me on KFC. Fuck Affirmative Action. I won you fair and square. Stop interrupting me, I have smarks to bash. Next, we have the lovely yet quite disturbingly named “MyNameIsn‘tDCandISuckAtCallOfDuty.” I’m not sure if reading this is the smart thing, but I can’t go back now. Originally posted by: MyNameIsn‘tDCandISuckAtCallOfDuty: I can’t believe how awesome the booking is nowadays! All these run-ins, stables being formed, and TNAish swerves make me feel like I’m watching WCW all over again! ….Our next poster is “JeykleForPrez”. Great, I feel like cutting myself already.. Originally posted by: JeykleForPrez: Liek OMGz, Jeykle is sooooo shexy, I wish I could go to a show and meet him. He made being emo soooooooo ubercoolz! He make me lulz all the time! I mean, I even carved his name into my arm with a dull spoon!! I iz his #1 hardcorest fan!!!! People like that are the reason I’m pro choice. Dude….not cool. My momma tried abortioning me. Yet you somehow survived and blessed us with your infinite wisdom. Well, I only have time for one more post, this time from “Tera‘sKidIsReallyMine”. Originally posted by: Tera‘sKidIsReallyMine: One thing I’ve always wondered is what the wrestlers are like backstage. I can honestly say that this is the best group of guys I have ever worked with. There are a couple douchebags that try to ruin the fun, but we don’t let it. Ribs make the business fun, if you don’t like it then get out of the business. Well folks, that’s all the time I have today, see ya next time. Fade…but only for a moment. Images flash wildy across the screen. Xavier Cross tapping out in the first Dark Crusade. CXF holding up the tag team titles, with Nikki Venus at their side. A sunset flip, leading to a second TV Title Reign. An elimination chamber, with one man standing in the middle, a world title held over his head. That same man, holding the first ever Underground championship, tears streaming down his face. That man, is Zak Warner. There are no jokes this time, no limos filled with weed and women. Hell, even DeAngelo can’t be found this time. Shit’s gettin serious. Now I’m sure most of you are wondering why I chose that specific opening video. Was it to feed my ego? Partially. Was it to prove to Sharpman, and his band of bitches that I have accomplished more than either of them can ever hope to accomplish? A little bit. Mainly though, I played that video so when I go home and watch this, I can be reminded. I can be reminded of the fact that I am one of the best damn superstars in the history of sports entertainment. I can be reminded that I have overcame the odds, when nobody else thought I would. Granted, I might have slipped up a bit in the past few weeks, but that is all behind me. People doubt me and my abilities now, just like they did back then. Nobody thought that I would be able to win a single fall in the Dark Crusade, just over a month after I joined GWC. I can gaurentee that nobody saw me making the TV champ tap out, thus taking his own belt from him. Just like nobody thought I would sacrifice my body, by driving it through Lexington Royale just to prove a point. Ask any fan of GWC, hell even any of the stars that were around when Felipe and I won the tag titles from one of the greatest teams to set foot in the ring. Ask em all, and few will say that they thought we would come out victorious, setting in motion one of the greatest tag team reigns in GWC history. After I lost the TV title, people thought I had fallen too far to get back up, but yet again I proved them wrong. I beat three men, including Xavier Cross once more, to take back the TV title. I overcame the odds that people thought were insurmountable, just as I had so many times before. Yet for whatever reason, people still doubted me. That all changed, when at Day of Reckoning, I overcame the greatest odds that had been set before me. I beat 5 other men, including not one, but TWO hall of famers, and had my hand raised as the GWC world heavyweight champion. I had finally reached my ultimate goal, when everyone doubted me all along. Even here in MWE, I have been underestimated since the day I stepped in the ring. I wasn‘t supposed to win my first match…I wasn’t supposed to draw with Paige and Sonny, and I sure as hell wasn’t supposed to win a title in my third match. It is obvious that Warner isn’t in Norwich yet, as with each word his breath comes out in a white smoky mist. As he walks along, it becomes clear that he is once again back in his hometown of Syracuse, walking along the streets of downtown. There are billboards featuring some of the greatest athletes to come out of Syracuse, from Carmelo Anthony, to Marvin Harrison and Dwight Freeney, to David “Luckiest Catch in Super Bowl History” Tyree. Looking up at these billboards, makes me proud to be from Syracuse. One day I hope to be up on those, next to the other great athletes that have come from this city. This place is my true home, where I can turn when shit gets heavy. After what happened at the Pay Per View, I came back here to do some thinking. You see, I never expected to find out that every single person in MWE had somebody watching there back. I never expected Paige to kiss me, and kick out of my finisher not once, but TWICE before beating me fair and square in the middle of the ring. Which, by the way Paige…I just wanted to say good fucking job, and you earned that victory. There were two things that night though, that I did expect. One was the formation of Uncultured Anarchy. You see, I knew that I had to find someone who had my back, just like I would have theirs. The smart choice would have been to bring in DeAngelo Williams, but he has retired to being my cameraman. Instead, I turned to the greatest star in GWC history, Kevin Hardaway The other thing I expected, was for some shitty booking to happen after the pay per view…I just didn’t expect to be put into a match with Joey Dallas…I mean, Joseph Sharpman. You see Joseph, we are quite similar, you and I. We both fight until there is nothing left inside of us, and we refuse to quit. We both love inflicting pain on other people just as much as we do on ourselves. The thing with this match though, is I have nothing to lose. If I don’t win, its just another loss for me, and I find another way to start my climb back up. But if you lose, not only is your pride and ego damaged, but you lose that respect you hold so near and dear to you. How will you feel, knowing that by agreeing to this match, you signed your own death certificate? It must eat you alive knowing that you are going to finally reap the results of your actions. This week, I take the first step in adding another title to my already storied legacy. Whether it be the United States title, or the Havoc title, I plan on further cementing my legacy as the most dominate wrestler in MWE History Joey, you can take your pipe dreams of “Vanguard”, and shove them right up your ass along with your pet gerbil. Paige, after you lose your title, I’m sure that I can arrange a night to take you out to a nice seafood dinner, and I might even call you back the next day. I hope you boys are ready, because I know that Kevin sure as hell is, and so am I. Fade to the billboards of Syracuse Legends.
