Drunk

Sep 3 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The scene opens in a dingy, scummy looking bar. Large, leather clad biker looking fellows are sitting around, playing pool, just being really loud, causing a ruckus in the joint. At the bar, a waitress grabs a platter with two pitchers of beer in it and walks away. When she leaves, the man behind her becomes clear. It's JP Smith, washing his troubles away with a small glass of some sort of alcoholic beverage, on the rocks of course. JP drinks his drink, reliving the last moments of his Crowning Glory over and over in his head. He lost his match. He lost to his teacher. He didn't hold up his end of the bargain made before the match. He went onto the grandest stage of them all and left the sorest loser of them all. This realization forces him to down what little he had left, only to realize he's still in the mood to drink. JP - Hey barkeep... Refill... The bartender glares at him for a moment. It's a skinny man in glasses, he looks more suited to be a computer designer then a bartender in a biker bar. He walks up to JP and takes the glass from him, but instead of refilling it, he places it in a mini sink at the other side of the bar, next to all the liquor bottles. Bartender - Man, I'm gonna have to cut ya off. You've had- JP - Don't you DARE tell me when I've had to much to drink! He turns around after his outburst to make sure no one heard him. The bouncer, obviously a VERY large, well build man, is already busy "bouncing" someone out the door, so feeling confident, JP turns back to the skinny bartender. JP - I don't want trouble man, all I want is another drink... So you're gonna go over there, grab the bottle, and leave it in front of me, and then you're gonna go about your night, are we clear? 'hick' His little hiccup came from nowhere, but it didn't calm the bartender down any as he grabs the bottle and places it right in front of JP before walking towards the other end of the bar to serve another customer. JP grabs the bottle, takes a swig, cringes at the taste, and sets it back down. Breathing heavy, he continues to think about what happened, and what he's going to do about it. ??? - You should quit that drinking. You're gonna do something... not like you. JP swings around in his bar stool, his head half an inch behind the rest of his body, flimsy from the juice. He looks around, looking for whoever just spoke to him, but no one can be seen. Confused, he turns back to the bar, slowly, and as soon as his eyes reach the back of the bar again, the source of the voice reveals itself, kinda. Now standing behind the bar is a man in a black suit, red shirt, and a black tie. But it's not what he's wearing that shocks JP, it's who it is. As JP stares into the mans eyes, he is staring into his own eyes, because right in front of him, is himself. JP - Who? Who the fuck are you?! ??? - Isn't it obvious? You're so drunk, you're starting to hallucinate. I am you... well, at least a part of you. I'm what some would call your better half. I know what you know, I see what you see, but unlike you, I know what we both want, and I will actually strive to go for it, not beat around the bush about it. JP - What do you mean? When I want something, I 'hick' I take it! ??? - Oh really? Then why did we lose to Eric Derf at Crowning Glory? We both wanted it, why didn't you take it? You wanted the spotlight, but you didn't really want it that bad, you didn't wanna feel like you betrayed your friend, did you? At this point, JP is pretty much talking to himself from the view of anyone else in the bar. Luckily, other then the bartender, who couldn't care less about this drunken fool, no one seems to notice. Or care for that matter. JP being one of those people not caring, he keeps talking to himself like it's normal. JP - It's just not fair. I could have beat him. Hell, I had him beat anyway! Fuck! I didn't come this far and work this hard for nothing! I'm bigger, stronger, and way more aggressive then he will ever be, or ever has been. But even as kids, he's always been the one who ends everything looking like King fuckin Shit. 'hick' As if he wasn't drunk enough yet, JP takes another quick swig of whatever it is he's been drinking. His other half is leaning down on the bar, with no disregard of leaning on this filthy surface with his expensive looking attire. ??? - Well why do you let him embarrass us? I mean, I know why, but are you willing to admit it? JP - It's just always been this way. He's always trying to act like I'm his student. He's the one that always tries to get the spotlight, even if it means for me to stand behind him, in the shadows, as long as it makes him look better, puts his name higher on the list. Anything for a boost in ego. The bartender is now noticing JP is having a full conversation with no one, since no one else can see the 2nd JP. He walks over to the drunken fool with a glass and a towel in his hands. Bartender - Hey man, you doing Ok? JP slowly slurs his face towards that of the bartender. JP - Of course I'm aright. I'm just having a conversation with 'hick' with my friend here, can you leave us alone? The bartender looks next to himself, which is where JP has been focusing his attention for quite some time. He notices another bottle matching the one JP is drinking, so without even asking otherwise, he grabs the bottle, pops the top, and just sets it in front of JP, assuming that's the friend he's referring to. JP barely even noticed the 2nd bottle of liquor in front of him though, he's more focused on what his other self has to say. ??? - So, what do YOU want to do about your situation with Eric Derf? JP - What do you mean? There isn't much I can do. I might get mad at some of the stuff he does sometimes, but he is still my best friend. I couldn't- The hallucination slams his hands down on the bar, making a very... non existent noise, but you get the idea. His face is red with rage, his fists clenched. ??? - Alright, listen to me for once. Eric Derf is not a real friend, we both know this. The only person you can truly trust is yourself, so you need to listen to me for once. Eric Derf has an ego. He only calls himself your teacher to make himself look better, in reality, what the fuck has he ever taught us? He don't care about you, he don't even care about the fans. He only cares about himself and his reputation. We shouldn't be budding with him, we should be exposing the fake that he is. You know you feel it JP, deep down inside, you know what I am saying is true. The hallucination leans back up, and slowly calms down as JP takes another drink. It's hard to think to hard when your thoughts are literally talking to you. ??? - But right now, you've got bigger fish to fry, don't you? You need to stop worrying about Eric Derf, and start worrying about Windy Bridges. Another guy, just like Derf, he couldn't care less about the fans, yet he plays up to them anyway, and they all eat it up like cereal. We both know if Windy was a smart man, he would have stayed down after what Ace Cannon did to him. Now we can appreciate that, can't we? JP gives his other half a stink eye, completely in denial of what's being said. JP - Are you fucked in the head? What Ace Cannon has done to Windy Bridges, it was fucking sick! ??? - Well guess what? Maybe I am fucked in the head, but if I am, so are you. You need to realize again, what I represent, it's everything you truly feel, but right now, you're brainwashed into thinking otherwise. We need to follow an example set by Ace Cannon if we truly want to make it anywhere here in MWE. JP - Oh yeah? What would that example be? 'hick' Although he spews it out in a sarcastic tone, JP Smith is still quite drunk, obviously, and also in denial. The hallucination, however, simply smiles and even laughs a bit. ??? - We need to be ruthless and aggressive! But we need to kick it up a notch. When we finish with Windy Bridges, we're gonna do something NO ONE has been able to do, we're gonna finally put that mutt down for good. I want to aim to break every bone in his body, bruise every ounce of flesh, beat the living pulp out of him. I want to show him, and everyone else, once and for all, that JP Smith isn't the man to be messed with anymore. JP - You're acting like you want to end his career, and I KNOW I wouldn't do that. I know myself better then that, you're not making any sense. The hallucination shakes his head in disapproval, before leaning in really close to JPs face, the two almost eye to eye. JP is sweating in this heat, while his hallucination couldn't be cleaner. ??? - You know you can't fight these feelings forever JP... JP - What feelings?! I don't feel anything! 'hick' ??? - You know the feeling I'm talking about. Everytime you get mad, everytime you think you hate something, you can't delay the inevitable. I am those feelings, I am in you, and now I am telling you I need out. You need to let me out so we can put the MWE world on notice. They need to know there is no more underestimating JP Smith. We need to make an impact, starting with Windy Bridges. We need to hit him where it hurts. He's gonna play off his heart like he usually does, we need to play on his emotions and we need to bring him down. Windy Bridges is a ticking time bomb when it comes to his emotions. You can beat the hell out of him, and eventually he'll just snap and come back at you. You can't let that happen. Don't make the mistake so many others have made. You need to take him out, but you need to do it so bad, getting back up isn't an option. You need to go out there and prove yourself. Over the years, you know dozens of people have tried to take Windy out, and you're next in line. You need to make sure you're the last in line come the end of the night. He's already lost his friend Spud McKenzie because of Ace Cannon. He's gonna want to "avenge" his friend. Don't let him do that. If anything, you need to destroy Windy, then find Spud and destroy him even more. Watching that happen last time, you know you enjoyed watching it. Deep down, you liked what you saw. JP stands up, knocking his bar stool behind him, and he gets right in the face of his hallucination. JP - Fuck you... I can beat Windy in a clean match, I don't need to attack his friend. And I don't need to attack my own friend either. ??? - What did we say about Eric Derf? He is not your real friend. The only person you can truly trust is yourself. Your real self. JP stares at himself, glaring, furious. Finally, after realizing the scene he just made, he turns away from the bar and makes his way towards the far corner of the bar. He gets there and finds a payphone. As he fumbles with his change, looking for a quarter, his "other self" isn't very far behind. ??? - Giving Celeste a call now? She can't help you either tonight. JP - How the hell do you know who I'm calling?'hick' ??? - Seriously? You still don't get it. I am you! Everything in your head, every thought, it's all me. Even your disagreeing with me, that's all your conscience. And I know you're calling Celeste right now, you're going to ask her for a ride home because you've been drinking, but what you really want is... how do I put this lightly... you want to plow her? JP - Oh, go fuck yourself! ??? - You first... oh wait a second!!! The hallucination simply chuckles at his own joke as JP holds the phone for a moment, really thinking about what he's doing. But eventually, he dials the number, and waits for an answer. As it rings, the hallucination continues laughing. JP - What is so funny anyway? ??? - Do you even ever realize how much of an ass you look like? You're piss loaded, calling the girl you wanna fuck, just to get a ride home. You know she don't wanna fuck you back, or else it would have happened by now. You're too scared to tell her how you feel, and too shy to let anything happen even if she came clean first. You're pathetic. You need to listen to me once in a while. JP - Shut up! Just as he shouts at his hallucination, he hears a voice on the other line and shuts up himself. Celeste - Hey, it's Celeste. I'm not around right now, leave a message and I'll get back to ya! JP sighs, but doesn't hang up. Hoping she's around but not answering, he decides to leave a message. JP - Hey, it's JP. I need your help. Please, I just need someone to talk to... The hallucination reaches out and grabs the phone out of JPs hand. (Use your imagination people.) He puts it close to his mouth and just stares at JP for a few seconds before breaking his awkward silence. ??? - Actually Celeste, you know what? Forget what I just said. I don't need you. I want you, I would love to do things to you, dirty, perverted things, but quite frankly, you're just not worth my time anymore. Have a good life bitch. As the hallucination delivers his last line, he chucks the phone towards the receiver, landing it right in place. JP just stares at him in shock. ??? - Dude, don't act so surprised. All I did was tell her exactly what your thinking, minus the sugar coating. And you know it. That's why you're not fighting it. Deep down, you know you don't need her. You don't need Eric. What you need is yourself. You need to show the world you're better then they give you credit for. That is all you want, and all you need. JP doesn't respond. He simply walks back to the bar, picks up the stool he knocked over earlier, and sits down. The bartender has since taken his bottle away, but seeing him return, the bartender returns with a glass. Bartender - Sir, I really think you've had enough tonight. JP ignores the bartender, he just sits with his head down in shame. ??? - You need to leave now JP. You need to go home and prepare for your statement on Monday. You need to prepare to show the world what you can do on your own. Bartender - You're falling asleep, I'm gonna have to ask you to leave. ??? - You don't need friends. You just need yourself. Snap out of this fucking funk and just do what you know you gotta do. Bartender - Sir, I said- JP gets off the stool and grabs the bartender by the scruff of the neck and pulls his face close to his own. JP - I fucking heard you! Get out of my fucking face! JP pushes the bartender back, causing him to hit the bar behind him, but not hard enough to knock anything over. It was loud enough to get the bouncers attention though as two men, both as large as JP, approach him and grab him by the arms, and drag him backwards. Bouncer - Alright buddy, out you go. JP - Let me go you fucking dipshits! I'll beat the hell out of both of you! 'hick'Get off me!!! JP is talking alot of drunken talk, but he's barely putting up a fight at all. The bouncers get him outside and pretty much chuck him towards the sidewalk before returning inside, leaving JP in the cold alone. And when I say alone, he really is alone this time, his hallucination seems to have disappeared. Alone and cold, JP Smith slowly gets to his feet and begins to stumble home, or wherever, he's to drunk to care. After a few blocks, JP finds a bench on a sidewalk, which he claims as his own by dropping onto it, and all but passing out, had it not been for a car pulling up, lights blinding him. He lifts his hands to his eyes to block the light a bit, but he can't make out who is walking towards him, but he can hear the voice. !!! - JP Smith? That you? JP doesn't recognize this voice, but if they know him, maybe they can help or something. He sits up, only to fall back down again. The person in the light runs towards him to help him up, but the light is casting a shadow, making the figure impossible to detail. !!! - I got ya, I got ya. Come get in my car, I'll take ya home. The person helps hold JP up long enough to get to the passengers side door of the car, where they open it, and JP manages to get in himself. The person runs around to the other door and gets in. Once inside the car, they turn on the light on the ceiling and stare at JP. It takes a few minutes for him to realize where he is or what's going on, but eventually, he looks around, then to the person who just helped him. When he see's who it is, he almost jumps a foot in the air. JP - You!!! What are you doing here! Although the person is still undetermined, they speak anyway. !!! - I've been following you for a few weeks JP. Sort of like your personal stalker. JP stares stunned and confused. JP - You're the person that has been stalking me? What? Why?! What's going on! I'm confused! The person starts the car and reaches behind themselves to grab the seatbelt. Right before driving off, they look at JP dead in the eyes. !!! - I need your help.
