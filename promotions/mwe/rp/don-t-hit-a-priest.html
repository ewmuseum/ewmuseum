When I looked at people I saw something I hated, but now when I see their faces, I see something I love, something worth fighting for. But what am I to do with myself when I am thrown into a sporadic situation where a priest confronts his altar boy? The room air blows so cold, so nimble, it’s still fresh to the tongue. How can I defy odds and bring down my teacher, my god, my enormity? In the bible Joseph, son of Jacob, is one of the best-known figures in the Torah, famous for his coat of many colors and his God-given ability to interpret dreams. Due to jealousy, his brother Judah sold him into slavery for 20 pieces of silver. SELLING his own brother? How sick and sadistic that is, but if I had to sell Dallas as my brother, I would understand…. Eventually he worked under the Egyptian official Potiphar, but was freed and became the chief adviser to the Egyptian Pharaoh, allegedly during either the Hyksos Era or the Middle Kingdom of Egypt. A coat of many colors, an ability to interpret dreams, or in this case, shatter them. It sounds all too familiar. Joey Dallas, AKA James, AKA Joseph Sharpmen or whatever the hell this heckler wants to be named is all just a lie. Fondling of the fingers, wrapped around a rainbow coated world, Sinc knows the it factor and the way the game of wrestling is played, Mr. Dallas is just there for the ride, and to cash in his final performance. A man who wishes to rule the entire universe and ride on a jet of colorful air waves is still living off the circulation of his flaw, notoriety. I realize I am starting to sound like my former colleague but I’m proving a point that anyone can talk using big words and quivering dialect, it’s not hard. A stepping stone of wonder, the exploration of something bigger, and more worthwhile crosses the mind of Sinc Mercier. Joey boy has tried to build to an entity of great destruction and power, but all the dreams he’s had, all these walls he’s hurdled or tumbled down, came right back up to him. His golden opportunity, wasted. Wasted to a fun loving child named David Whitman. Laughing voices and pestering calls begin to overcome Sinc’s thoughts, his entire independence is questioned as he pours his heart out to someone he once trusted. One can only speculate what was going through Joey’s mind when he fell to the current world champ, maybe he wanted to cream his pants, maybe he wanted to end his own life, or maybe he was just coming to the realization that he JUST can’t beat everyone. See, I don’t like liars, Mason can easily tell you that. Little Joey doesn’t seem to understand that the people around him are not the sinners, no, he is the embodiment of all these sins he has said the people, the Sinc merciful’s out there, and myself have committed. One lick of the finger, and a swift comb of the hair, the man is stylish and composed, but the things around him continue to purge into his emotions as though they were a toss up of cat and mouse. He is content about his words, and truthful of his actions. It takes an army to bring down a city, it takes a single person to bring down your hopes. Joseph is that person, bringing down someone’s hopes with his snake like language and fork tipped tongue. Words strike like blades and fire like rifles to the striking horizon ahead. Talking and chatting isn’t the same, the wound is deep. But he must continue on, this man, this rugged man, must not give up. He claims that people are suffering and he is the only one worth watching in this industry, HE is the key to the blockade of doors. What drives you to define such imagery Mr. Mercier? What makes Joseph need to see this truth that you have driven into your brain? Jesus could be a heel for all I care, but it was the blood he shed that created a horde of faces, and that horde is coming after Dallas with everything they got! He thinks he can relinquish me like a fly and swat me away when I’ve buzzed around his noggin for a prolonged stay hoping to annoy him into a euphoric distaste, and fly away again when his gaping hand swipes at my stinted body.
