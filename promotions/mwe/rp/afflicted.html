Michelle Madison: Your winner at 19:49 and STILL GWC World Heavyweight Champion, Kevin Hardaway!!! The scene opens up in the hotel where Kevin Hardaway is staying. Kevin is sitting in the rental car that he had gotten from the airport earlier. He had never turned his head even while the cameraman sat there, waiting for his words to speak to the viewers. His eyes were locked on what was in front of him --- seemingly lost in his own world, zoning out...whatever it was that you wanted to call it, that’s what it appeared that he was doing at the moment. So he blinked a few times before giving the camera a quick glance to let the people know that he was aware of everything around him. After a few more seconds of silence, he had finally began speaking. I didn't decide to show those clips of me in action, in my prime as a history lesson to the folk out there who don't know me all too well. To everybody, it wasn't what I'm made of. At least not now. The only reason why I was watching those clips was for one reason...because I look at all of those clips and it makes me tighten up inside. It makes me feel like everything that I once previously shared, owned, or held, is nothing more than a distant memory. Everything is just gone now, and the only thing I can really do is try and reach for it, only to come so close and so far to the dream I originally turned to reality. It would only be a dream again, and nothing else. Nothing more. I actually compare this business, to love. Yeah, love. The feeling that two people share. It's quite similar, because you love to work in this business, making people proud to be a fan, working at the best shows of the year, and yet trying to achieve your own goals as a person. You try to achieve that dream of that first kiss, that first taste being recognized. It's very similar. And it's like your love life in the beginning of your career too. You don't know all the buttons and the in's and out's of somebody, just like you don't know the in's and out's of the business. And if you don't know that, then you're not going to have a good time finding somebody out there. Especially was like mine. I mean, you just realized that there's somebody out there for anybody and dammit, you want it now. Well, you can't get it straight away. It's what I learned in this business my very first month in my career. Did some things wrong, said I wasn't the person who I thought I was and all hell broke loose. It was that simple. He paused for a moment, a scowl clearly seen on his face as he thought about everything that has happened so far. A lot of it made him feel like he was the one at fault for everything that was happening. Then when you finally think you have everything figured out, somebody comes along the way and BAM! Your complete perspective on life is changed forever, for the rest of time. Believe me, have you ever had that one girl you saw in high school? The one who say behind you in math class, and the very second you looked at her the first time, your eyes lit up and you couldn't believe what you were seeing. You were looking at a goddess it seemed like. But it was that point where no matter how hard you tried to get her to talk to you, it never really happened? Or maybe you two were good friends and you just couldn't get the courage to tell her how you feel? Well, it's a mix of the two, but that's how I felt wrestling. You can call me stupid for comparing this, but all you need to do is just think about it. It'll come to you, trust me. Anyways... You realize that she's the ticket. She's up there at the top and that's where you want to be in this business. You want everybody to love you, everybody to respect you, and it starts with her. Then...something happens. You finally get that nerve to take that extra step that you've been wanting to take for the longest time, you're ready to tell her how you feel and you're so close to her and you can finally touch her and tell her everything and...she rejects you. She tells you that it won't work out, or that she's seeing somebody else. All that time you spent trying to tell her your feelings deep inside, the things you want to accomplish and somebody tells you, "Sorry, kid. It won't work out." Yeah, you know how heartbreaking that makes you feel? And after all that, you don't get it through your thick head. You keep on trying for her affection, only for somebody else to come and swoop in and steal all of your glory away from you. He gets her, why not me? Only for her to completely ignore you in the long run until she just leaves out of nowhere, for you never to see her again. His head turned towards the camera with a smirk starting to cross his face. He had a plan...he was here for a reason, but that reason has yet to be revealed. It would be, though. So you just feel like moving on, not trying to worry about the one that got away and instead, trying to just worry about your own damn self. And it starts working. People give you advice in the long run on how to do things and you just put it in the back of your mind, like it means nothing. But then, somebody comes along. Somebody who you don't even expect to come along. Some random girl walks up to you and your friends and asks to join you in a card game or something. You don't mind, you let her join in, it's being nice. But during that game, you and that girl start talking and you start clicking. Next thing you know, she's playing in all of your card games you have with your friends because you want her to join, so you can continue talking. And one thing just leads to another, you're really getting along with this person. And all of a sudden, it happens. You two hit it off without any failure whatsoever. And that "ticket" that you were reaching up so high for in the past, only to never reach it? It's right there in your hands, finally there for you to grasp on to it and to hold it tight. It was there for the taking. And then, the sky is the known limit. Anything and everything right happens to you. You feel happy, you feel confident, you feel that all is right with the world. You can go up on the rooftops and scream that you are the happiest person in the known universe. You finally have that first love you can call your very own. The tone of his voice spoke with more enthusiasm. But that all of a sudden, turns into a grim dose of reality. But it's when you're feeling the happiest, is also when you can expect nothing but the unexpected. You feel like everything is ok with you and her, even after so long. But then, you start seeing signs that maybe everything isn't what it seems like. You stop getting calls, you rarely see her anymore, you expect she's off doing other things. You start getting paranoid and with paranoia comes fear. And then that day happens when you either get an e-mail, or she tells you flat out, not caring who is in the room with you and her. She tells you that it's it. It's done and it's over between you and her. And that feeling...heartbreak is an understatement compared to what you feel. You feel like somebody just gave you a shot of adrenaline and sent you back down to planet Earth after you had what can be described as a "near death experience". And you don't know how to react. You don't know what to feel, how to eat, how to drink, anything. It's like you just got punched in the chest by a cannonball. It comes back to what I said to you guys eariler...you wake up in the morning and try to shield yourself from the blinding sun. You don't want to get up. You don't want to do anything. You're too lazy to do anything. Your self-esteem is shot through the window and out in a bloody heap alongside Route 66 and you don't feel like picking it up and trying to fix it. That's the way you feel. And no matter how bad you even try to get up to fix up the broken pieces inside of you, they just crumple and re-shatter back out of you for the entire world to see. A snicker comes from him as he had paused for a moment, making sure that the camera could look directly at his eyes. He finally got out of the car, as well, looking out at the night time sky. And trying to mend those broken pieces has been me for the past 7 months, trying to find the courage and strength to move on and try, try again. Yeah, so I lost my debut match. Big whoop. Most people would already be walking out that door right now because they couldn't get the shit they want on a silver platter. But i'm not like that. I used to be like that the very first couple months of my career, but not anymore. I think I finally reached that platform of "dusting my shoulder off, getting back up, and trying again." It's taken me a long ass time to do it, but I think redemption is finally coming. It may not be now, or in the next month. But for some reason that only I know about, I can see it in the clear distance in the blinding sun. My 9th grade English teacher told me one day when I was feeling down one day...she goes, "Even the smallest of lights can brighten up the darkest of rooms." But first, I need to win a match here for god's sake. Something I haven't done in general in a long time. Comes back to mending a broken heart. But enough about that for now, because I have to worry about this coming Monday. To my opponent this week, Greg Cherry...well, I know absolutely nothing about you. If you have a good track record, then more power to you. But all I know is that you're making your return to this place, so good for you. And i'm pretty damn sure you know nothing about me either...expect those clips I just showed you, and my track record elsewhere up until all hell broke loose. But it doesn't seem important. You know what...scratch that, it actually does. Because you're going through the same thing as I just mentioned. The frightening part about that is...my love life is eerily similar to my career in professional wrestling the past couple years. And I can possibly tell that you're feeling the same thing. So we both have something to prove in that ring on Monday... Redemption. He smiles to the camera for a final time, still looking directly at it as he walks off and into the hotel where he's staying at...letting the sliding doors come across him as they close behind him, us seeing his back as we cut out. =================================================================================== QUOTE OOC: The code again. Please give us an insight into your work here and give us time to deal with this circumstance if it is to turn south. As Kevin recovers from his worried state he makes his way back to Hayley and placing himself one seat down from where he sat before, making sure he didn’t wake Hayley in her peaceful state. =================================================================================== "5 Years Later -- 11 Hours After Midnight" Three hours has passed since. Kevin has all of his forms filled out and given to the nurse. He gets up for a few seconds and starts to stretch out, accidentally waking up Hayley in the process. "What’s the time, Kevin?" Kevin quickly looks down the hallway to catch another glimpse of the blue clock. "It's 11:42." In hearing this, Hayley builds up the energy to find her handbag and reach into it, removing her silver flip phone. "I’ll need to call my boss and tell him I’m not coming in today and probably the rest of the week as well. You mind getting me a coffee then, while I make this call?" "Sure." "Two sugars please?" "Gotcha." With that, he struggles to lift himself from the chair and performs a few quick stretches and eventually heads down the hallway to find a coffee machine, however just before he turns the corner he turns around to peek at Hayley, who now had her mobile phone against her ear seaming to be crying as she tells her boss the gravity of this situation. He starts to talk to himself. "This must be hard for her, no other family either, Angie is everything in her life, surely somebody can see this." With that quick glimpse Kevin continues to walk down the endless hallways in search of a coffee machine. With this thought he makes his way to the front entrance of the hospital and slowly makes his way outside getting some fresh air. After minutes of roaming the hallways Kevin finally comes across a coffee vending machine. As he puts in his selections and places the coins in he waits for the coffee to be poured, however in this time rather than diving into deep thought, he just listens. The sounds of the hospital machines working endlessly, the conversations of different people near him, kids crying because of their injuries and of course the vending machine, but the one thing that he wanted to hear wasn’t there. "I’m listening now. Talk to me; tell me my purpose through this hard time." In keeping an open mind and still listening to all the noises around him he waits, wanting to hear or see anything that could be a sign, however the only sign that he got was the small beep from the vending machine as it had finished making the first coffee. As Kevin continued the process for the second coffee he looked around more, paying special attention to the colours of the hospital and the amount of plants in the hospital. It seamed to be a calming place with the walls coloured by a pale pink paint and the plants as green as they could be, it seamed to be a relaxing place. As the vending machine beeped again Kevin removed the two coffees and headed off again trying to navigate through the hallways. As Kevin finally reached Angie’s room he saw that Hayley was talking to an elderly grey haired man dressed in a white coat and black pants, no doubt a doctor. In seeing this Kevin slowly approached looking for any sign from Hayley to whether he should give them time or listen in, in not receiving anything he made his way to her and was greeted by a forced smile and an outstretched hand. "Thanks." As he passes the coffee to her and stands in between her and the doctor, completing the triangle. After taking one sip of her coffee, Hayley looks up to Kevin. "This is Doctor Anderson, he is Angie’s head doctor." "How are you Mr…" The doctor pauses as if to ask for his name. "Kevin, remember? We spoke eariler." "How are you holding up?" "Yeah, keeping it together one step at a time." "He’s been great, keeping me from breaking down." "Good to hear. So, Kevin I’ve just been telling Hayley about the latest news." "Oh yeah, what’s happened now?" "Well she hasn’t improved too much, her liver is still in its withdrawal stage and the rest of the organs are starting to react as well. It is getting quite serious now." In hearing this, Hayley bows her head and withdraws from the conversation, while Kevin stays fixed on the doctor's face, eagerly waiting for any more information. "So what steps can be taken to fix this then?" "Well, we have found a suitable donor for the liver so that will be the main focus of the surgery, hopefully if that goes without a hitch then we might be able to solve the problem." "Fantastic, and what’s the risk in this operation?" "Well we have the best transplant team in this state alone so they should perform it without a problem, however there is always a risk." By this time, Kevin is showing more of a positive expression and Hayley is wiping the tears out of her eyes and focusing on the conversation. "So what is the best result from this surgery?" "Well, best case scenario is that the transplant goes well and we see Angie gain her normal color and hopefully be close to normal in a couple of days." "Ok. So when is the operation going to happen?" "Well we are still waiting for the liver to arrive, so anytime within the hour." "That's good to know." The doctor takes a look at the forms that Kevin filled out for Angie as they wait for a response from the doctor. "Looks good here. So with this I’ll schedule an operation room." "So, does this operation room have a viewing platform?" "No, but we will tell you as soon as possible when anything happens. Is there any more questions?" "None from me." "No. Thank you very much, sir." With that the doctor gives an over exaggerated smile and leaves Kevin and Hayley to dwell on the circumstance. "You think Angie will be all right?" "I’m sure she will pull through it. I know her, she's a trooper." "…I hope so." With the end of this conversation both Kevin and Hayley go dead quiet as they both stare endlessly at nearby items, focusing all of their thoughts on the situation and nothing else. =================================================================================== "After the Operation" Upon entering the room Kevin instantly notices both a young blonde haired nurse checking Angie’s vitals, and of course Angie in her new state. The difference between what she looks like now compared to 15 hours ago was tremendous. Angie’s skin still has a slight yellow tan and her mouth and nostrils are covered by an oxygen mask, however that isn’t what catches most of Kevin’s attention. As the surgery had just recently finished, Angie is laying on her bed without a shirt on showing the huge incision in her chest, which is sowed up with roughly 30 stitches. "Whoa, what has happened? Her skin, her chest? Baby, what is wrong with you?" In staring at this, Hayley starts to cry again but rather than just crying on the spot she hurriedly leaves the room with the doctor signalling for the young nurse to follow her. Yet at the same time Kevin can no longer hold back his tears as one slips past his eyelashes and falls down his right cheek. In letting Kevin observe Angie a bit longer, the doctor grabs a bandage and starts to wrap over Angie’s stitches. "Well there you have it, the operation itself was a success. No problems with the transplant or the incision, so there's no need to worry about this, but like I said before just got to wait and hold off until the other organs have been found." Kevin wipes the tears off his face and out of his eyes as he now stares down the doctor again as he finishes wrapping the bandages around her and abruptly directs a question to the doctor. "Listen doctor, be honest with me. What is your honest opinion on Angie's situation?" The doctor gives a brief look at Angie and then reconnects with Kevin’s solid glare. "Well I wouldn’t hold my breath. We don’t know what has caused her organs to shut down. There will be a gap in between now and when we can give her the other organs, so if this illness was to advance further, it could kill her." Kevin now starts to lose the color in his face, as he fully understands the severity of the situation. Just as the doctor pats down the bandage, he goes over Angie’s vitals, however his reaction was different from the nurse who just checked on her. All of a sudden, Angie starts twitching around like the hell below them possessed her with the devil's spirit. The doctor pushes the red button near her bed as three nurses rush in and start prepping Angie as Kevin looks on in utter horror. "SHE IS CRASHING. SOMEONE GET THE DEFRIBULATOR AND GIVE HER 20 MILLIGRAMS OF ADRENALINE! STAT! Sorry, but we need to take care of this Mr. Hardaway." The doctor pushes Kevin away and shuts the door immediately as he stands outside, in what feels like himself, as Hayley comes back with the nurse and sees Kevin standing there. He turns his head to her to see Hayley rush over, what appears to be slowly. It all has to be some kind of bad dream. It has to be.
