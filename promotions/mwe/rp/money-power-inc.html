Money & Power Inc.

May 9 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
OOC: So, I was actually kind of angry. I had this finished Thursday Night, and was in the process of posting. Well, I had to go and take a shower and I take like 45 minute showers (get off my back) so when I got out, I figured I was cool and it was posted. So, if you saw me in the Roleplay section for hours, that was why. Well, either way, I went to bed and all that fun stuff, and it turns out it didn't post, and yesterday, I woke up, went to school, and went directly to my friend's house, who I crashed at. It turns out that the RP never did post, because I never posted a fucking title for it, so there was an error message. So, I get home about an hour ago, look, and I see this error message. If you don't believe me, go ahead, but I am telling you that is exactly what happened. *Sapphire Garvin is looking around a big open space, what looks like an old office building. The carpet is the traditional faded blue, with white walls boredering in what some say is a prison. She knows how cubicles feel, and as she looks, she frowns, walking to a small step-lader, and climbing it. She reaches upward, pops up a ceiling tile, and a bunch of dirt, dust, and sheetrock gruble fall out in a mess on the floor. She coughs as she jumps backwards off the step-ladder, and frowns once again. She walks across the huge open space to Mason Resoan, who is currently staring out a window, at nothing.* Sapphire: What are you looking at? *Mason does not answer and simply continues to look out the window, aimlessly.* Sapphire: Hello...are you there? *Mason snaps out of his aimless like trance that he often finds himself falling into when he looks through windows. He quickly spins around, looking at Sapphire deep into her eyes. He is wearing a very classy white suit, which he accompinies with black polished shoes and his signature gold watch. His sunglasses perch on his head like a bird, staring at the ceiling. He looks at her and smiles a weak smile, as he walks past her.* Mason: Oh, it was nothing...nothing at all. Anyway, what do you think of the place. We've gone through...what is it now? Four floors? It seems pretty solid to me, don't you think? Sapphire: Yeah, but what could we do to improve the look. This won't work if we can't shape this thing up to be as nice as possible. *Mason puts his right hand on his chin in a very classical fashion, as if he was the great thinker. He spun a couple of times, looking around at some of the fundmentals of this story.* Mason: I am sure we will find something to do with it. Possibly a paint job. We don't want to spend to much money on something we have never done before. We'll start with some basic stuff, of course replacing the ceiling tile and the caprets with something that sticks out a little more...and then we start moving in the equipment. It might take a little bit, but if we can fix this place up a little bit, I think it could be successful. Sapphire: Well, do we have an option? We checked out several other buildings, and none of them were quite what we were looking for. Mason: Yep, this seems pretty solid. I think we're settled. *Mason walks to the opposite side of the room, which took nearly twenty seconds, showing the size of the floor itself. He ran his hand across the plain white walls, making his way to a big, brown, boring door. He swung it open, held it open with his foot, let Sapphire walk through first, and then proceeded to walk through himself.* ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- *Mason is now in a black collared shirt, retaining the glasses, shoes, and watch. His pants are now black, as well, and he seems to be by himself now, with Sapphire Garvin no where to be seen. In fact, the entire scene looks quite peaceful, as Mason is sitting by himself, eating something out of a blue and beige bowl. It appears to be Apple Jacks, as there is a green box and the cereal is red and green itself. He enjoys spoonful after spoonful, not really caring about anything, when his phone begins to ring. He places the spoon back into the bowl, spins on the stool, takes the phone out of his pocket, and begins to walk to the other side of the room.* Mason: The papers are being finalized. Gotcha, well, I am home, and the cameras are here....Yeah, I know the deed won't be transfered until I bring the money and sign the contract...listen, I'll be there in a little bit. Don't worry about me, I will be there soon. Alright, love ya, bye. *Mason clicks end on his voyager, looking in a sigh of relief as he finds the camera. It doesn't take him long, and he leads them out his backdoor, past his pool and back room, but rather to another small building behind it. He swings open the rusty metal door of this shack, of sorts, and leads them through his gigantic backyard/extra patio area. They are now on a slanted hill, leading down to a lakeside view, a scene never seen before. The lake seems as though it stretches on forever from left to right, but directly across, a building is seen. Mason points to it.* Mason: I wanted to show you that...because you are beginning to see the beginning of a new company...a company that is expanding every day. The real estate industry has never really been something I have looked into...until now. With Sapphire Garvin at my side, you will start to a see a more...business side of me. Not that you haven't seen me do business before, but this is serious. No playing. No jokes. No sarcastic remarks. If you thought I was successful, watch me...I am more than you will ever know. *Mason takes an arm and places it in front of the camera, blocking its view of the lake. He then slowly sweeps it to his back, as though unveiling the lake that is Mason's backyard. He smiles a quick grin.* Mason: This lake, is not public property you know. It is only for the owners of the lakeside house owners. As you can see, I am one of those owners, but I am more...see that house across the way. Well, as of last month, we have expanded across the lake. And as of today, we will be expanding even further...straight into the heart of Toronto! We are making our prescene known, not only in the wrestling world, but also in the world of business and industry. We are more than Sapphire Garvin and Mason Resoan, we are Money & Power Inc. and we are more than you will ever know. *Mason gives a slight chuckle, as he takes a seat, looking out to the crystal blue lake. How fitting it is, for Mason Resoan to be sitting on a lush green hill, staring into a crystal blue lake, in a clear sky, on a sunny day. A perfect picture, for the self-proclaimed "perfect" wrestler. He looks intently at his own reflection in the water, as he continues.* Mason: And when I say that we are more than you will ever know...we are not just talking about our business aspects. We are not talking about our mentalities. We are talking about everything, including wrestling. Whether it be in a game of mental chess in the ring, or in a pyshical confrontation between me and another competitor, you should and will never know what is coming. Being perfect is a hard reputation to keep up and the way I keep that reputation alive is by hitting my opponents with something new everytime I compete with them. Wether it be a new move, getting under their skin, or simply striking them from a direction they would have never seen coming. So, when I see the card this Monday, and see myself pitted against two opponents I have never faced before, it makes me smile inside. New talent is like opening up a present on Christmas, because it could be anything, but you know it will be good. That is what this match will be for me on Monday. *Mason waves a hand in front of his face over and over again, still concentrated on the reflection of himself in the mirror. He makes an akward face, lays back, and stares into the sky, leaving himself looking like a kid, in a way. He is completely masking all actual emotion he might be displaying right now.* Mason: Mr. Markowitz and Triple G are new blood of MWE. They are fresh, young, and ready to be taught in any which way their masters please. David Whitman and Brian Michaels dictating their every move and showing them the tricks and tips of the business. And that is my present. Knowing I am going against two inexperienced superstars, taught by two men I have faced before. Triple G, the weaker of the two, but definitely the more risky will be more like Brian Michaels. So crafty, yet flawed enough to let me pick him off. And Mr. Markowitz, will be more like David Whitman. Safe, effective, strong, and fearless. He will come at me with all that he can until he can topple me...and this is where I have my fun. *Mason rolls to his stomach facing the floor, clenches both fists, and pushes himself up with an impressive fist push-up, which he jumps up from. He is now standing look omniously over the lake, as if he was an all knowing being. He has no grin, no smirk...in fact, he has no expression at all. Just a blank face, as he continues to pick apart his opponents.* Mason: Mr. Markowitz is a resliant, yet persistant man who will not lose...like David Whitman. He could be dominated the whole match, and come from no where and end it...like David Whitman. *Mason, for the first time since he began to talk, now looks at the camera. However, he only turns his head, and not his body.* Mason: Whitman was a mistake I won't make with you Markowitz. David's tricks will not work for you. And if you try, you will be shot down before you even know it. I don't care what Whitman, or Michaels for that matter, taught you, because it doesn't matter. The only way to beat someone like me, is to throw something I haven't seen at me before. You need to be what I will be to you...a suprise. You need to be able to shock me and catch me off my guard, otherwise it is hopeless. That is something I doubt you can comprehend. To be honest, I don't even think that MWE Champion, David Whitman could teach you, nor would he. Very few people can be a surprise all the time, as they give themselves away. The slightest emotion will allow me to predict your next movement, and Whitman is almost as secret, if not more secret, than I am. So, Markowitz, I wish you good luck...but I am letting you know right now, that when we square off this week, it will be like nothing you have experienced before. *Mason contemplates his words of wisdom and advice for a moment, before turning back to the water again. Upon seeing his reflection, he remembers Triple G and this time spins his whole body to face the camera.* Mason: Triple G...I hope you were listening, because I don't repeat myself. However, I will give you a piece of advice, as well, because I need to keep it fair, right? *Mason chuckles a bit at the retorhical question, but moves on quickly.* Mason: You may have your master, your mentor, and your friend, Brian Michaels in your corner judging and helping you throughout the match, but let it be known that he has never beaten me. I was once his student, or so he likes to take credit for, and look at where I am now. How do you suppose you will be beat me acting like him, when he, who is far more advanced than you, can't beat me? If you can answer that question with a valid and sensible answer, than maybe you will have a shot this Monday. However, until that point, you don't stand a chance. It will be as if you are walking in the ring with your father when you were sixteen...trying so hard to best him in a game of strength and wit...but failing miserably. Good luck, because Monday, you and Mr. Marko are going to need it! *Mason sighs as he goes to open his mouth, but just as he does, his phone begins to ring again. Once again it is Sapphire. He reluctantly answers, and immediately is bombarded by questions.* Sapphire: Where the hell are you? Mason: Listen, I told you.... Sapphire: No, you listen. You always tell me to ignore it...but I am not like Sara...you will actually listen to me. Get your a** down here now...we have some deals to close. *Mason would love to retort, but knows that he would be shut down by his girlfriend. So he reluctantly follows her order, and makes his way back to the house. There, he checks himself in the mirror, brushes his teeth, and grabs a black briefcase. Moving with a purpose now, he makes his way to his garage and is stuck with a 1978 Chevrolette Corvette. Sapphire took is custom 67' Black Camaro GT, and for that, he was slightly irritated. Nevertheless, he threw the briefcase in the backseat, started the engine up, and screeched out of the garage to Sapphire's location. ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- *Mason pulls up to a building like earlier. Gray, with a mulititude of windows laying on outside walls of the building. It gives the dull sense of a barless prison and Mason personally hates it. However, he reaches to the back of his wonderful car, picks up the briefcase, swings open the door, and slides his shades over his eyes. He is looking extremely classy as he stares around the open parking lot. Only three cars sit here...and ironically, they all add up to over half a million dollars. Mason laughs at the scene, and proceedes to the soldi, cold, gray, metal doors that are then entrance to this buidling. Inside, it looks much like an insane assylum, as the walls are bright white, with all the ceiling fixtures illuminating the hallway as though the sun itself was in the room. Mason ignorned the blinding light, however, because he had his shades on, and proceeded down the hallway. Right. Left. Left. Straight for two corridors. Right. Left. Left. Right. Right. Right. And one final left, and Mason finally reached the room. It was much like a maze, and if he didn't recieve Sapphire's text of directions, he would have surely gotten lost. As he swung open the door, he stared at a pre-school like round table in the middle of the room. It was perfectly circular, with three chairs, and a oak-wood finish on the top. Mason walked past Sapphire, giving her hair a quick, yet gentle stroke, before he took his own seat. He then slowly, but surely lifted the briefcase up onto the desk. Shappire sighed in relief, as did the man in a black business suit in the other seat. Mason looked inside the briefcase, smiled, and then looked to Sapphire who slid him four pages clipped effectively onto a small little board that had a pen attatched to it. Mason skimmed through the first two pages of legal gibberish that he knew Sapphire had already read. He quickly read the third page and finally made his way to the last page. There were three dotted lines. One line said Sapphire Garvin. One line said Michael Zimmerman. And one line was blank. Pen in hand, Mason attempted to sign the contract, but the man piped up.* Michael: I hope you understand the entire contract fully. And I hope that you have the required information in that briefcase. Would you like to double check before you sign? It could be important. Mason: No, no thank you, I am fine. I just want to sign. Michael: Listen, I don't think you know who I am. My name is... Mason: Michael Zimmerman, former real estate extrodinary...business degrees of bachelor level and a ton of great recommendations. See, I already know this, which is why I am not afraid to sign this contract. So, if you wouldn't mind, just let me sign. Thank you. *Zimmerman, who had stood for a handshake, instead diverted the hand to the briefcase, which he pulled over. He clicked it open, with a skillful spin, and looked inside to see a single piece of paper. It was blank, with nothing but a few numbers on the back.* Michael: Wait a second...hold on. What the hell is this! *Mason sighed and put the pen and clipboard down once more. He looked at the man with a look of disbelief, disappointed in what all the hype about this man wound up being.* Mason: Listen, you incompetent fool. I know what the contract says. I know the deal to be made, and if you wouldn't jump to conclusions, I was going to tell you to flip the bill. It is a check, for $175,000 dollars, what we agreeded on for the office space. All you have to do is cash that check, and you will be set. Now...will you please....PLEASE shut-up and let me sign! *The so-called well-respected business man sits down once again, as he exams the bill carefully. Mason takes the pen and with an extremely fast signature, signs the finally dotted line. He puts the pen back in place, flips the few other pages back over, and places the agreement on the desk. He then slides it to Sapphire, you takes the papers off the clipboard and places it in her purse. They look at the man, stand up, shake his hand, and leave. Pulling out the text message, they trace their way back to the parking lot, in which they hop in their respective cars and begin to drive off.* ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- *Mason and Sapphire pull up to a wide open black parking lot, completely empty. It can't be seen as barren, as there is hustle and bustle on the opposite side of the road. A powerful plaza surges through the area, with much activity and many people seeing where Mason and Sapphire are. They also see what the couple just purchased. As they step out of their cars, they look up at the giant building they just purchased, for a very good price. Being in Mayhem Wrestling Entertainment definitely had some advantages, and great deals is one of them. However, Mason was concerned about the good deal, who was watching him, or anything else in the area. All he was focused on was this big building. Mason: It looks like your idea is coming together quite nicely. Excpet for the incompetent business men we had to deal with, this wasn't exactly the hardest goal to accomplish. *Sapphire is looking and inspecting the outside of the building, but as she is looking at, she is able to slip something out.* Sapphire: Remember, we aren't finished. *Mason sighs heavily as he leads in front of her, with great stride, taking a key from his pocket and unlocking the two double glass doors, that serve as the entrance. He walks in and sees a very empty room, recently eliminated of all its contents. Shaking his head, he began to think of plans for the building.* Mason: Hey, Sapphire...what if we did something different for every level. Like this level could be a drum clinic or something? *Sapphire shakes her head and continues looking around at the floor with a speculating eye.* Sapphire: No. *Mason seems a little taken aback by her bluntness.* Mason: What do you mean...no? Care to elaborate. Sapphire: Listen, just make your way to the top floor or something. This is most likely going to be a office building. Mason: Why the hell would we make it that? Isn't that what it was? *Sapphire had been walking around looking at the material of sheetrock and foundation of the building, as ideas surged through her head. She was now close to Mason, as she walked over to him. As she looked into his eyes, she put a hand on his shoulder.* Sapphire: Listen, I can't really think of why we should keep a building like this for something that might actually want to branch off into. So, we are going to make it an office building, load it up with cubicles, desks, and pointless fake plants and things of that nature. That will hide the terrible foundation and possible rotting of the walls. We make it look nice, look into a big company like Apple or something, and sell it to them for double the price we bought for, along with everything we put into. So I am expecting a 450,000 dollar sale or so. That should get us enough money to buy an actual building. At any rate, I am designing the floors in my mind right now, so go to the top floor and relax. You don't need to worry about this. *Mason likes the idea now, but is still a little displeased that he can't help out. However, to avoid arguing, he makes his way to the stairwell, and begins to jog up the stairs. Upon reaching the top floor, he swings open that same big, boring, brown door from before. He makes his way to the area he was in earlier. Looking around at the surrounding emptiness, he makes his way to the window, sitting down, scrunching up his knees, he looks out. He thinks of earlier, when he was talking about Marko and Triple G. He laughs.* Mason: You know, I am thinking about it...and this company, this idea, this movement that Sapphire and I are doing, is in the earlier stages. Much like you, Blood Fuze. We are new to the business, unexperienced, but on a roll and ready to get pushed. However, you don't understand that we are actually able to progress...and we don't get hit with obstacles we can't face. If I wasn't enough for the both of you this week, then just take a look at my partners, and you would see why you wouldn't stand a chance. I am not a tag team competitor, but I used to be, and as a result, I know a good tag team when I see one. The MWE Tag Team Champions, Supernova and Leon Roberts, are going to take care of you this week. It will be as though they are taking out the trash of the tag team division. *Mason shakes his head in disappointment, and thinks about Marko and Triple G. He thinks deeper into his match.* Mason: And if you think about it, we have you outmatched in almost all ways. Leon Roberts could tare you apart Markowitz. And Supernova...well you wouldn't stand a chance Triple G. They are more than just a tag team...they are the tag team champions, and based off of that fact alone, there is no way that you could even do any sort of damage to that team. It is as though you are leaving your master alone to face the three of us...and we all know how that is going to turn out. *Mason stands up, and begins to pace.* Mason: I could go on and on about how much I despise Brian Michaels at this point in time. He takes credit for things he never did, he makes himself look as though he is the king of the squared circle, and he forms alliances that he doesn't like. However, I am not going to sit here and bash him. I won't even promote myself over him, because frankly...that is getting old. I'll try to make this clear as possible...the Mayhem Mafia is broken! *Mason stops pacing and leans against the window, crossing his arms across his chest, letting out a large sigh.* Mason: Everyone knows that Mafia factions are some of the most deadly, crazy, unpredictable groups in the entire group. So, how does it work that I know how your Mafia works, without even being in it? How do I know the positions and power standings...how do I know what Marko and Triple G are learning? How? *Mason quickly shakes his head.* Mason: It is because you are broken. You aren't a well-oiled machine...you aren't a faction to be weary of...you don't threaten or intimidate anyone. You don't swear...or break the rules. You are no mafia. A mafia is willing to do anything to look out for their members, as well as help them in anyway possible. So, when I watch a Brian Michaels match, and I see him out there, alone, with no one in his corner, I get to thinking...how good is this mafia anyway? Not very good, obviously. *Mason begins to contemplate how broken they really are, and comes to another conclusion.* Mason: And that is why, you will not be able to beat us come this Monday. It is not because you are weak...it is not because we are better than you individually...it is the fact that we, even though we don't know each other, will have each other backs. We will make sure that we don't let our unity be broken. We will show you that it doesn't take much to work together. All I can see.....all I can ever see is watching the Mayhem Mafia implode...like last time. *Mason puts that evil grin on his face as he uses these words of the past to try and get in the head of the Mayhem Mafia.* Mason: Wait...Michaels, don't tell me you don't remember that? Sonny Williams breaking off from the group, and Pala disappearing? They just, took a turn for the worst, and left each other to die...like animals. And you honestly don't think that will happen. It will get to the point where you are sick of no title shots, or GGG and Marko don't like each other any longer. Or they might more power than you think they can handle...and it will slowly, but surely detierate in to a faction of nothingness. Like it never even exsisted. All teams must come to an end, and I really wish people would start listening to me. *Mason lets out a massive chuckle and he squeezes a fist. He holds up one finger and looks at it intently.* Mason: Let us start, with my own group. Mason Resoan and Aiden Copane, in the old MWE, we were the top dogs of the company. He had already been a United States Champion and in the main event picture, and I was the most successful and most undderated superstar of our company when I came in. However, slowly, Aiden and I started to do things to make us known. Calling out people, not having a care in the world. I was turning from underrated to recognized as a force to be reckoned with, and he was getting primed and ready for a main event push to the world title. The gold was in his grasp, and he started to think a little to highly of himself. We ceased hanging out...we didn't plan anymore...no more jokes...no more fun. We were an ununited group of tag team champions. Not to mention his slut of a girlfriend who just put the frosting on the cake. Eventually, I was tired of his little act, and you know what I did. I smashed him in the face with a steel chair. I left the douche bag by himself, bloodied, battered, broken, and betrayed in the middle of the ring. The Canadian eXchange...was over! *Mason looks at the finger that he had held up. He joined it with his middle finger, looking at another team in his mind.* Mason: So, maybe CX wasn't the most successful, because we imploded...you could say, but it did come to a crashing hault. Even so, it was neccessary and done, but it wasn't the only time like that happened. I know you will remember this one Michaels. Rated X, breaking up in the most contreversial endings of a faction I can say that I have ever seen...or helped plan. I was on a hell bent path to MWE gold, and by any means neccessary I was willing to do something to take you out of the picture. And it just so happened that your suppossed close friend D.C was building up a burning hatred for you. Together, we ended what was supposed to be known as the greatest MWE tag team of all time. But I am not finshed! *Mason is getting on a roll, as he holds up a third finger, and beigns to move around a little more, back and forth, back and forth, to and fro.[/color] Mason: Let us take a look at earlier this year...oh, now I know you couldn't have forgotten this. My old foe, Sinclair Mercier was on the prowess. After some impressive wins, he began to feud with me, being taken under the wing of one Joey Dallas. Joey took him in, almost as a son, but turned him into an obedient drone. He told Sinc how to act, when to act like he was told, and how often to act like he wanted him to. He has Sinc under his control, like a puppet on strings. Sinc was a sheeple, but decided that enough...was enough. He began to show signs of rebellion. New Found Sin was about to break through and start showing signs of real, powerful like...but what happened. Joey Dallas, the coniving b***rd pulled the plug, and he beat Sinc to a pulp in the middle of the ring. He thought that he needed to learn a lesson, and New Found Sin...was destroyed. *Mason holds up a fourth finger, not even looking at it, he simply holds it up in front of the camera.* Mason: And probably most relevant, we have Mayhem Mafia, of old MWE. They were full of winners and success. David Whitman was already a mult-time MWE Champion...Pala was the current MWE champion, and they had MWE by the balls, knowing the strings to pull to get what they want. However, Sonny, like I knew he would, decided that it was time to end things. He wanted an MWE Championship run, as well, and Pala couldn't handle the thought of facing his own friend. So, as he David and Sonny clashed, Pala lost confidence, dropped the title to Aiden Copane, showing how broken the Mayhem Mafia was. Mayhem Mafia was dead...and it will repeat itself. *Mason places his entire hand on the camera as he shoves it back. Wobbly, it looks back at Mason, as he gives a chuckle and sits with his evil cocky grin.* Mason: Four teams...four implosions...four broken unworthy teams. Come this Monday, you will show me that Mayhem Mafia is going to be the fifth, because there is no way...in god's green earth that you will be able to beat the Black List Mafia and Mason Resoan. The Mayhem Mafia is broken, on the verge of imploded...and most of all....the Mayhem Mafia is...IMPERFECT! *Masons smiles as he walks back to the door that this whole day started out in.* Mason: And if ya didn't know...now ya know! *Mason swung open the door, much like before, and disappeared once again, leaving people to ponder the strength of Mayhem Mafia.*
