Win at all costs

Aug 8 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
An MWE camera blurs and focuses to a scene backstage where it sees Sonny Williams in a retro-like room. In the room consists of a pinball machine, a huge plasma television and many more cool objects. He is sitting on a leather lounge with two smoking hot women on both his left and right whilst smoking a Cuban cigar. Sonny is wearing a his usual backstage attire being long grey/blue jeans with one of the legs out of the jeans only over the knee with a bandanna strapped holding it up and a grey Sonny Williams hoodie. He has his arms around both women who have their heads on his chest and very close to him. He notices the camera and takes out his Cuban cigar and takes his arms from around the smoking hot women and put them in its usual position. Sonny Williams: I guess you’re wondering, why I am here living it up with two of the finest women across the city, smoking a Cuban cigar and well living a better life than you. I would too. I mean we’re only two days away from the biggest wrestling event of the year and while you’ll probably see Alex Bowen and his echo head teddy bear David Bowen working their guts out in the gym, I will be sitting back relaxing in a mental focus for Crowning Glory. Again, why am I not in a physical preparing state for Sunday? It’s simple, I have trained my entire life for something like this. I have shed blood, sweat and tears and yet show no remorse to step back and turn away. A lifetimes preparation has gone into Crowning Glory, an extra few days seems absolutely pointless. Sonny Williams: I’m sure you all saw the footage, promo, whatever you’d like to call it about my dead brother. I know you all may have felt that I needed a head to rest on, someone to talk to and you probably all felt incredibly sorry for me. Don’t. I don’t want you to feel sorry for me. Why? Well plain and easy, your existence means squat to me. I love my brother, don’t get me wrong but after what I have been through in my life would not compare to what you have had. You may hear from someone backstage that they’ve heard the tough childhood story a thousand times. Mine isn’t fiction, mine was for real. *Sonny looks down at the carpet ground but not completely, only a tad so the camera can still viseably see his eyes.* Sonny Williams: Jordan, he always wanted the best for me, and I wanted the best for him in return. I spent the last year trying to entertain a group of people with no morals and no integrity. *Sonny looks back fully at the camera* People just like you. You thought that Sonny Williams was the joker, the man who made you think you really could live your dreams. I am not that same man, I have matured, I have grown up. Exactly what three quarters of the MWE Roster hasn’t done. My main priority is no longer you people, no. I have done enough hard yards, gone through enough tragedies to know that at the end of the day, you gotta please yourself. And that era begins. And it begins now. *Sonny looks over his shoulder a bit to see the blonde on his left. He leans over as she whispers something in his ears. He raises his eyebrows and rubs his hand on her knee and goes upwards. He suddenly stops at her thigh realizing the camera was still in the room.* Sonny Williams: So now you’d think that I had no preparation for my opponents come Sunday. You thought wrong, because I have. Half the men in this match I have either beaten, and the other half I have never challenged in my life. And the half I have beaten are supposably known to be incredible athletes. That has got to say something, now doesn’t it. I’ll work down the list, because yes I do have higher risks to face in this match, one of which isn’t this clown, Jeky? Jeki? Jekyl! That’s the one. Now just a like all of you, I have seen what this guy has been doing and saying. The guy is a freak, fullstop. He makes me sick to my stomach, the way he killed a man on set and raped a beautiful young girl it’s just sickening. Even me living in the streets of probably the biggest crime capital, I even find what he did unacceptable and disgusting. Onto my match though, do I find him to be someone who could take this thing out? No chance in hell. The minute that freak steps foot in front of me, is the second he’ll be out of the match. Sonny Williams: David & Alex Bowen. I have never faced either, am I looking forward to it. Pfft, no. They are no name superstars. Guys you see appear maybe once or twice and are never seen for the rest of a lifetime. They have absolutely nothing that scares me, and if Alex wants to tell his big teletubby brother, or whatever connection he has with him, to attack me I say bring it. I am the monster, the powerhouse, the unit of MWE, not David Bowen. No, it just wouldn’t be so. Sonny Williams: Apparently I hear there is some wog out there who is big. Wow lets all run away from Italian wannabe Mafia loser. You’re trying to copy me buddy, and turn me Italian. Not-a-liking. B.G. Mancini, you don’t scare me. I have gone through so much in my life, I have seen the biggest and I have seen the smallest. I’ve seen guys under 6ft that intimidate me more than you ever will. You will not walk away MWE World Champion, I will make sure of it. Sonny Williams: The last non MWE original, Xavier Stone. Gotta say, have never been fond of you, your actions or whatever you involve yourself with. You are a cocky, skinny white ass boy who can only get a half decent pop from his son Troy. Let me send a message out to you Troy. Your father, your dad, he will be in serious pain after Sunday. Not only will he suffer from the mentality lose of things that he won’t be the MWE World Champion, but the pain I bear on him if given the chance will be very bad, mark my word. I’m sure daddy has told you some stories, maybe some myths of a boogeyman. I am not a freak but by gawd I will make you shit your pants just as bad, if not even worse. Xavier, you bitch, come Crowning Glory whether you bring your whole family or not, someway or another they are going to see the ass whooping I pull out of the can onto you. Your antics are as sick as Jekyl, actually I can sort of depict a similar mind thinker to be quite honest. *Sonny makes a confused look into the camera and then shakes his head and looks back* Sonny Williams: Now we make it to the MWE men, the men I begun my career with. The men I have almost all beaten! Devlin Sharpe, you begin my process squid. To my pardon I am not even too sure why I brought you up, I mean you’re half my size and no one has seen any sign of you lately. You got no chance in hell, and you never did have what it takes to become a MWE World Champion. Sonny Williams: Alejandro Rodriguez. Life hasn’t been quite the same without you, I mean the many, many times I had countless wins over you, hasn’t been the same since then. But now, all returns. You and I, we had probably one of the most fierce rivalries in MWE History before the place crashed down and burned. If I am correct the winning score was 3-0 in my favour. No, no, no. Sorry before you reply and say it, yes you and I shared one draw on one occasion but three wins shits all over that. I’m not going to lie, no matter how small you really are, you are someone I do consider a threat in this match. I have beaten you three times, but all times it was close. Now I hope you don’t jump to conclusion that I have come to like you coming from these, well compliments, because I hope you remember, even when I was a good guy I hated you, that is one of the few things that haven’t changed since returning to MWE and having a new attitude. None of this Xavier Stone, Devlin Sharpe or Alex Bowen business, you are one of three men I consider a threat. But don’t take that all well, I know how you move, I know how you work in the ring. I know your weaknesses and I know how to depict them. And I will. You see for all you people on the screen the image you’re about to see is exactly what I will re-act at Crowning Glory to Alejandro Rodriguez. Sonny Williams: Alejandro, you know me I know you. I hate you and well you probably hate me back, but I will bring torture to you and your body. You will not complete a dream of yours to win this match. I just will not allow anything like it. You got no dedication, you have done your dream to become a wrestler. You haven’t got the desire that I do. *Sonny Williams scratches his head and looks back at the camera to continue* Sonny Williams: David Whitman, wow I never expected the day would come.. But I am glad it has, as much as I admire your in ring work and everything you have done, I still have never beaten you and before my career is in dirt and dust it is something I want to be able to say. You and I used to be good friends. You misleaded yourself and are now trying to please a group of people with no morals or integrity. I wouldn’t turn that corner for these people. Not any day from now on. Anyway, I am inducting you into the MWE Hall of Fame, a place where I will be some day, I admire and respect you but I will beat you at Crowning Glory. Even in Mayhem Mafia I knew my skills were far better than yours, I will prove that on Sunday. My long road to Crowning Glory is much bigger than yours, I will beat you. I will prove that I am the superior Mayhem Mafia member. You better believe it. *Sonny stands up from the leather couch and puts out his Cuban cigar. He looks into the camera with a fierce look on his face* Sonny Williams: Brian, Brian, Brian. You and I we have hit it off in the ring a number of times. You can even re-call our last meeting when I defeated you to win the MWE World Championship, and you think it will be different this time around? That, that is just stupid talk. Last time I met with you I was still developing my skills, since then I have improved unlike yourself. You can not bear my skills, time and time again small indication make my theories correct. You and I, me and you, we hate each other. It’s plain simple and it’s publicly known. Now what have you got on me that could say you could win this match? Sure, you’ve had some good fights in your life but you got nothing on me pal. Just look at me, I am a force to be reckoned with and this environment for Crowning Glory suits me the best out of all ten me who will enter the ring on Sunday. I prepare to destroy you, like I have and like I will do in this picture on screen. Sonny Williams: You see my fists, they bring pain. My shoulders, they’ll charge right at you. There is no escaping this match, if you want to be the best you gotta beat the best and right now I am claiming that I am the best. Just like I have said for every other superstar, Brian, I will not let you win this match. You’re time is up, you’re only still here because of your father. I got here with natural skill. Sonny Williams: To every superstar in this match, do not pass me by because be ready to take a beating like a man. I must win, I will win, and I will do so for my late and great brother Jordan. Dedication is a bigger word than win, but I will do both and be the new MWE World Champion! Sonny Williams: You feelin me?
