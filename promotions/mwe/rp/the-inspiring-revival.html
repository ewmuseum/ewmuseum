The Inspiring Revival; Properly titled, my fire is back!

Aug 24 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
THUD! BAM! POW! -Riddick watches from behind the curtain at an independent wrestling show. Watching a classic between two guys he’s trained with, Ashton Flame & Adam Bomb. The crowd erupts with excitement as the sound of bodies crashing ignites their spirits for the very profession they paid to watch. Riddick turns around from behind the curtain to a simple stage behind the curtain surrounded by chairs and empty water bottles on the ground. Some guys are preparing for their match, doing squats or even sitting in the silence prepping themselves for their upcoming match. As he turns around he finds himself staring face to face with his trainer, the multi-time World Champion & a man who Riddick himself has essentially became to pay a true tribute to him. For those who do not know XXX, he is one of the best all-rounded wrestlers to walk the face of the planet. Somebody who uses the power of self inspiration to overcome any and all competitors in any of his matches. Seem familiar?- XXX: Hey man, it’s nice to see you. Riddick: It’s been too long man, it’s nice to come back to where I started. As you know I’m featured in the upcoming PPV “Crowning Glory” the biggest show of the year for MWE. XXX: That’s good, what’s your match? Riddick: I’m going to be defending my championship in a hardcore match against Zack Crash. And the problem is lately this championship has been giving me nothing but problems in my life. XXX: Care to explain? -Riddick turns towards the curtain, the red thick curtain that separates backstage from the action in the ring. And in some ways it’s separating the life of Riddick, his stressful emotional breakdown as of late to where he wants to be.- Riddick: This championship I hold, it’s tainted me. The fact I was awarded the championship by the previous champion, Kraven. The fact that I’m being considered as a transitional champion. It’s ruining my life and it’s taking me out of my game. -XXX puts his hand on Riddick's shoulder, he looks down and then looks directly into Riddick's eyes, he takes a short breath and then speaks to Riddick.- XXX: Let me tell you a story, a story of a younger XXX who competed in GWA. They were crowning the first ever GWA World Champion. They held a tournament where I won the first round against Jay Phoenix, I hit him with my Burning Hammer and I moved on. Only to lose to Thorn Darkken, the unstoppable force within GWA. This man was the person everyone put their chips into. This man was who they believed in. I was out of the tournament, but then, something happened. A new General Manager came in place and took the four men left in the tournament and took two more people that were previously eliminated. I was one of those people he brought back. He took us six, and placed us within the confinements of one of the most brutal matches imaginable. He placed us in the Lords Of Chaos Dungeon. A six man elimination match where anything goes and the last man standing is the winner. I was the dark horse within this battle. Everyone thought I was thrown in to sacrifice myself to the lions in that match. Everyone placed their money on the unbeatable, unbelievable Thorn Darkken. I could’ve stepped down, I could’ve laid on my back and not be retired to this day. I would’ve lived to step into that ring again. But I didn’t. That match took alot of years off of me. I’m not even 40 yet but that match is the reason I no longer step into that ring. But I can live with myself to this day, because I did not quit. It all came down to me and Darkken as the final two. He gave me everything he had, but what kept me going was the roar of the crowd. The people in the audience that were speechless. He picked me up, without a belief he would not emerge victorious. He forced me up but then I dug into my soul and I found the strength to give him a Burning Hammer and to prove to everyone that this dark horse was now a World Champion. I won the whole thing because I didn’t believe in what people thought of me, I believed in what they saw from me. That’s my story, I may never be able to step past that curtain to hear the crowd cheer my name anymore. But I will never live a day where I can regret anything in my career. -Riddick is looking at that small space between the curtains revealing the ring and the rabid crowd as he hears the ref making the count. 1 . . . 2 . . . 3!- Announcer: Here’s your winner, Adam Bomb! XXX: All your problems go away in the ring Riddick. It’s not the past that makes the crowd gasp in amazement, and it’s not what’s going to happen in 20 years, the crowd reacts to what they see in the present. Riddick: I understand. It’s clear to me now. -Riddick doesn’t turn to XXX, he keeps staring into the space between the curtains to the ring. This is where he began and at sometime in his life this is where he will end. The lights gleaming into the ring shows dust particles coming off the mat from all the slams and moves being done in it. This is where the crowd goes insane. This is where they find who is their hero, their inspiration and their favourites. This is they find the magic in wrestling. Both Adam Bomb and Ashton Flame eventually made their way to the back. Resting up their bruises and wounds from their match and the ring is completely empty with the audience anticipating what’s next. XXX taps Riddick on the shoulder and Riddick turns to him.- XXX: This is where you heal Riddick, let the crowd decide what will happen. Riddick: You don’t mean... XXX: You may not believe it Riddick, but they do believe in you. You are a hero to them. Riddick: I’ll give it a shot. -Riddick takes a deep breath, whether he’s in a large arena or a small hall it’s the sound of the crowd that worries him. One day there’ll be fans chanting his name and the next day it can change. One day nobody will want to see him anymore. This is the true anticipation of a professional wrestler. Riddick finally goes through the curtain. The crowd explodes in excitement seeing the X-TV Champion, a MWE Wrestler at an indy show. The smiles on the fans, the people cheering his name. The people around long enough to remember this is where he started. Riddick walks down the wooden ramp built by hand and high fives the fans by the rail. He sees the happiness on people’s faces, their wide eyes and the sound of “Riddick” being chanted over and over. Riddick steps into the ring and the ring announcer hands him a microphone. Riddick walks around the ring, feeding off the reaction from the crowd. Almost a little bit of a tear in his eye from the appreciation of the crowd. The crowd dies down, and Riddick begins to speak through the microphone.- Riddick: Bet you didn’t think you’d see me here tonight. Especially with MWE Crowning Glory being two days away in Chicago, Illinois. You know, they call Chicago, the windy city but I may have to think about that because the cheers and applause you’ve people have given me here in Niagara Falls has blown me away! -Crowd erupts vigorously as Riddick once again continues to make his statements on the mic.- Riddick: If I could be serious for a moment, I want to speak the truth to you people. I want to let you know I did let alot of you down lately. I know that maybe the truth is I don’t deserve the MWE X-TV Championship, I was handed the championship. No matter how much I want to believe I earned it, I was handed the championship. However this is what makes Crowning Glory the summit of professional wrestling. Crowning Glory is where dreams become reality, where legacies become solidified, where the greatest stories of glory are defined by the blood, sweat and tears that pave that way for others to share the reality that dreams can become real. There was a moment not even a month ago where I asked myself if this was real. I asked myself if this ring where we tear our bodies to pieces and we defy what we believe and what you people believe is impossible. This Sunday at Crowning Glory, I will face Zack Crash one on one in a hardcore match. And what makes this important for me and what makes this important for every one of that believes in me is me emerging victorious not for bragging rights, but to prove to myself that I earned that championship. What you people may not realize is that I am willing to do anything to prove to myself and to prove to you people that I deserve to be champion, I am willing to shed every pint of blood that runs in my veins, I am ready to break every bone in my body, and I am willing to wrestle as if it were my final match, as if it were my final moment, till the moment I only have one breath left. I will emerge victorious because this is Crowning Glory and the world will be watching. Zack, I respect your dedication, I respect your faith within yourself, I respect every move you make, but I only need to do one thing Zack, I need to walk out of the United Center after the bell rings with the X-TV Championship in the tight grasp of my sweating hands and the crowd cheering my name. Zack, you jump off ladders, you put people through tables, and you live your life in the fast lane. Crowning Glory is the end of the road. Crowning Glory is where the dead end lies. Zack, this is the biggest spectacle of the year, this is the climax of MWE. This is where you pull out everything you have left and we see who the best man is. I wish I could tell you I am the champion and I will do anything to keep my reign as champion going, but in my eyes, I realize this championship is as much mine as it is yours. I am going to fight for this championship like I’m the challenger. There is no champion’s advantage, there is no concept that makes either one of us look better. You truly make me think Zack; you constantly amaze the crowd by not only your high risk arsenal, but watching you destroy yourself, watching you get punished. It makes me ponder Zack, your economy of pain tolerance as a whole is thinning each and every time you step into the ring, so how much do you believe you’ll have left when you face me at Crowning Glory? I know I can say all of this and you’ll point out how much punishment I’ve taken these last couple of weeks, however I’ve come to a revelation given to me by an old friend. No matter what happens to me, no matter what goes on, one occurrence cannot define me as a whole. I will never base myself off of one fault, I will never step down because of one concept that effects my life. I’ve said this many times, at the beginning of a new day when the sun rises it doesn’t matter how badly it stormed last night or how terrible it was outside, today will be a beautiful day. Crowning Glory isn’t about me, it isn’t about Zack Crash, it isn’t about anyone in MWE. It’s about the people who sit in those seats, the people who watch at home, and the people who continue to support MWE that give us hope that dreams can become a reality. Without your faith we cannot find the power within ourselves to believe in ourselves. -The crowd erupts maniacally in joy and happiness over the words Riddick has supplied them. The applause carries the chants for Riddick by the youngest and the oldest, the men and the women, the tall and the small, everyone’s support for Riddick brings tears to his eyes as he gives the mic to the ring announcer. Riddick hops out of the ring and high fives the people in the crowd standing in the front row. He makes his way all-around the crowd and finally up the ramp where his mentor XXX is standing on top with his arms wide open as he and Riddick hug at the top of the ramp. Riddick then walks to the back receiving applause from the wrestlers in the back. Riddick then walks out of the back entrance with solid foot steps being heard as the sole of his shoes smack the concrete steps and the parking lot ground. He sees a dark figure by his car however he can’t make out who it is so he makes his way closer to his vehicle. Riddick searches his pockets and finds his keys with the clicker. He unlocks his car and the light from the inside makes it easier to see who’s standing beside his car. Suddenly a voice emerges.- ?????: It’s about time Jay. Riddick: Who are you? ?????: It’s Alexa, I need to talk to you. Riddick: What is it Alexa? Did you get a restraining order put in place? Do you want to yell in my face how I’m a failure to you? What do you want, Alexa? I’m really tired of this. What’s this message you wanted to tell me? Alexa: The message was....*gasp*...it was... Riddick: WHAT DO YOU WANT TO TELL ME!? Alexa: I believe in you Jay. Riddick: What? Alexa: I was wrong Jay... I wanted to tell you earlier but I couldn’t tell you for the longest time. It isn’t easy to admit I was wrong. Riddick: Do you realize what I had taken away from me Alexa? I told those people in there it was the championship that was screwing my life up but the truth is it was you. You took away my child, you kicked me out of the life I’ve always wanted! Alexa: I know it’s hard for you to forgive me, but I lifted the restraining order between you and David. It’s just that... -Tears form in her eyes, as the moonlight shines off her pupils.- Alexa: He misses you Jay. He’s only known you for a very little time, but he misses you Jay. You mean the world to him. He can’t live another year without a father Jay. And I know you can’t live without him. He tells me about the conversations you’ve had. But awhile ago, I was talking to him before he went to bed, and he told me about what you told him that a beautiful day could make you forget about your problems and realize what’s truly important in life. That the past is called the past for a reason. I said good night and I shut the door, but I could hear him talk, and he said he misses his dad. I heard him talking about how he misses you Jay. He said he doesn’t care if we’re separated or not, he wants you around to talk to him and he wants to learn from you. Riddick: You know Alexa, you’re a good storyteller that can make people believe in your stories. But I don’t believe you for one minute. Alexa, you do not know how much you’ve hurt me, how much you’ve tormented my life. Why should I believe in you all of a sudden? Alexa: I may be a writer but I’m not an actor, Jay. I can’t show different emotions with the snap of fingers. I can’t lie when I look into your eyes Jay. You want proof Jay? Look into my eyes when I say this. Look! -Alexa forces Jay to look into her eyes by forcing his face to look deep into Alexa’s eyes. Suddenly a connection was made.- Alexa: Jay, you can live your life and never forgive me till the end. But you cannot make me suffer by having my son wishing you were there in the middle of the night. You won’t be hurting me as much as you’ll be hurting David. Jay, you look directly into my eyes and you see the misery of not only me. You see the tears David shed when he misses you. He doesn’t even remember your face Jay, all he remembers is your voice, your voice that helped him understand. Are you telling me you’ll think I’m lying and your son, yes, YOUR SON will go his entire life not knowing your face, only knowing a voice that he may one day believe is imaginary. If you don’t believe me Jay, out of the millions of people in the world that chant in your name, out of all the people that believe in you, there will be one person that won’t, and that will be David, the son you will have let down. Riddick: I forgive you Alexa, I want my son back. I will not let either of you down. -Riddick also grabs Alexa by the back of the head, looking into each other’s eyes. They suddenly move in closer and closer. The electricity, the energy between their eyes is eccentric. They move in to the point where Riddick and Alexa share in a romantic exchange under the moonlight. The camera cuts to black and the next time we will see Riddick is walking past the entrance way into a packed United Center in Chicago, Illinois defending his MWE X-TV Championship against the number one contender, Zack Crash. Crowning Glory is everything that professional wrestlers dream of. From when they were young children watching immortalized legends on television with the energy of millions behind them. From the heroes that we idolize to the enemies that we frown upon it was the people that paved the way for the wrestlers of today to make their mark in front of the biggest stage in the world. For Jay Riddick, it’s a dream of a lifetime. He’s lived his entire life in the shadows of society. Nobody believed he’d amount to anything, and now he’s loved by countless people and ready to step into the ring at the climax event of the year. Riddick has spoken to us about belief, defiance, faith, revival, revelations and many other concepts. This has brought him to the big stage where he will defend his championship against Zack Crash. The path he’s taken has been a challenging one much like his life, however he’s told to us before that he’s not one to lay down and die, he’ll dust himself off and continue riding down that path. Crowning Glory, has many surnames to describe it. Where the blood, sweat and tears that the wrestlers of the past and present have shed to create the path to the future. The climatic occurrence of the year. The finish line. However you define it, it will be a night nobody will ever forget because the echoes of the event will last a lifetime.-
