No need for luck...; Alejandro Rodriguez RP

Aug 30 2008

Alejandro Rodriguez

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The scene opens in Alejandro Rodriguez's hotel room in the center of Bloomington. Alejandro is sat in a cushdy armchair, wearing an A-Rod t-shirt and ripped jeans. Stacy and Stephani have gone to see the sights in Indianna, leaving Alejandro to have an interview with Darrel, who is sat adjacent to Alejandro, wearing a black suit and tie. The camera begins to roll and the interview begins with Darrel facing the camera. Darrel: G'day MWE fans, Darrel Jones here inside Alejandro Rodriguez's hotel room and here with me live is none other than the man himself, Alejandro, how are you? Alejandro: I'm good thank you. Darrel: How do you feel about your loss to David Whitman two weeks ago, I never got your thoughts on that. Alejandro: Well no-one is going to feel too great after a loss, but I'm not that bad to be honest, I have alot of respect to David Whitman, but I believe I took him to the edge and he certainly took me to mine, in the end, he caught me off guard, what can I say. All credit to him, he pinned me for the three count, not many people have done that, and he's done it twice, it certainly is a good feat so he is certainly very lucky to be holding that title right now, but none the less he is deserving of it. Darrel: And what about last week, when you defeated Brian Michaels in a first blood match. Alejandro: Well it was certainly tougher than I expected it to be, but Brian had it coming to him to be honest, he's everything that I despise, arrogant and up his own arse. The guy grew up with a silver spoon in his big mouth, and I had nothing and no-one. He calls himself an underdog and that to me is completely laughable. The guy doesn't know what an underdog is, not knowing when your next meal would be and not knowing where you would sleep that night. I was the better man last monday, I had more heart and determination, and it was Brian Michael's turn to have nothing, nothing he could throw at me, he was completely out-classed. Darrel: And this week, you will be in a match with two of MWE's bright young stars, Kanto and Sinc Mercier. Alejandro: Well I had a look at Kanto's match this past week and he was quite impressive for the look of the guy. Please! How many freaks do MWE hire? I thought we'd seen the last of it with Jekyl and Aeron Beowulf. I mean, is the job center merged with the circus or something? Richard Covell really needs to do something about them, and fast. But the guy may prove me wrong, but I can certainly without a doubt tell you one thing, if I do not win this match, I can certainly guarun-damn-tee you that I will NOT be the guy getting pinned, certainly not by a freak and some punk who looks like he's walked in the wrong door. Alejandro pauses a second to recollect his thoughts. Alejandro: And this Sinc Merkier - Darrel corrects him. Darrel: - Mercier. Alejandro: Yeah, whatever, Mercier... What can I really say about him, other than he's arrogant, cocky, obnoxious and mediocre to the last degree. He doesn't deserve to be in MWE, he never has earned his way into the history books, even before we died he was good-for nothing. And yet he can afford to say how great he is and how he'll do this and that, has he done anything worth recognition yet? Nope, didn't think so. He's worthless, and if he thinks that he will beat me I will just completely laugh in his face, because he can't hold a candle to me, he doesn't even deserve to lace my boots, but judging from his behaviour, I doubt he could even tie up his shoes without his Mommy. Darrel: I take it that you're confident in this match? Alejandro: I have every damn reason to be confident in this match Darrel, those guys are nothing compared to me, they have never acheived anything and they never will. I could beat both of them even if my mask had no eyeholes, thats how pathetic these guys are in light of me and all of my sucess and achievements. Whatever they will become, they will never be as good as me, and I'm not saying that to be cocky, it's damn true, and they know it. They know it better than anyone, I pushed David Whitman to the limit, I've beaten him before, I've beaten Michaels at HIS own game! And they don't think that I will beat them? Then they're more damn stupid than they look. Darrel: What are you reactions to D.C. testing your loyalty to Brian Michaels and then putting half a million dollars on Michaels' head? Alejandro: You've got to understand this Darrel; it's not my loyalty to Michaels, it's my loyalty to the MWE. No-one fucks with MWE, I couldn't give a damn about Michaels, but when D.C. thinks that he's bigger than MWE after all that it's done to him, that deserves a big slap in the face. And I can truly say now, that if anyone betrays everything that MWE stands for by ATTEMPTING to win that 500,000, I'll put them out flat on their back, not for Michaels and not for money. Because I have pride and because I'm not a damn sellout to the company thats earning you six-figure wages every year that you work here. D.C. is making people do his own dirty work, get in that ring yourself and do it D.C., you god-damn pussy. Darrel: Ok, well that concludes our interview, thank you for joining me Alejandro, and good luck in your match this week. Alejandro: Thank you Darrel. But I don't need luck. Alejandro walks out of view and the scene fades.
