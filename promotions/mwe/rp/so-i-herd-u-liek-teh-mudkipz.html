so i herd u liek teh mudkipz?

Nov 21 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
OOC: Ok, I know that it is well past the deadline now, and if I loose my match because of that I completely understand but I just wanted to get his up to show everyone that I was working on something. Being the idiot I can be at times I of course procrastinated like no other and ended up falling asleep around 4000 words into it last night. ===== “To forgive is to set a prisoner free and discover that the prisoner was you.” ~ Lewis B. Smedes As the scene opens the Boardwalk Hall is dark, the only light coming from the occasional cellphone and iPod glowing brightly in the crowd, we slowly pan around seeing the slight amount of light shining through the pitch black before we come to focus on what is to be the vocal point, the ring, however there are no spot lights shining down on it, and we can barely make you the vague outlines of the ropes and general area that the canvas takes up, everything else remains pitch black. We remain like this for a few brief moments before an oddly familiar voice begins to boom over the PA system. It's been over a year since I last stepped foot inside an MWE ring, over a year since the last time I heard the MWE fans slowly build their cheers up for me or against me. It's been over a year since the last time I strapped on the old boots, locked up and won under the Mayhem banner. A spot light flickers for a few seconds in the middle of the ring showing a dark figure sitting in a steel chair in the middle of the ring, the amount of light in scarce and we cannot make out who this is, the camera slowly moves around the ring, still trying to focus in on the center of the canvas. Yet the only thing that remains in our view is the dark shapes of the ring and, as we zoom in, the dark outline of the person sitting here. Two months ago I made a triumphant return to wrestling, however somewhere along the road of my personal recovery I made a wrong turn, and signed away my soul to a company that would slowly fall from their self-proclaimed throne at the top of wrestling, before plummeting themselves into a deep hole from which there was no return. Some may say that my departure was the final blow to an already dying company, but I would be lying if I told you that was the truth. Because honestly that shit hole of a company was already six feet under, all I did was throw the last shovel full of dirt on top of the grave. This is where the greatest company in the world comes into play, the MWE. A true company, one that knows how to treat it's wrestlers the people that put asses in seats around the world to sit down and watch your product. Mayhem is a company that can only rise in the ranks to become the greatest wrestling promotion on history. And for the first time in my life, someone else has reached a hand down into the hole that my career always seems to fall into, and pull me back onto level ground, for the first time in my life ... The spot light once again flickers, the titantron starting to glow dimly as it does, showing the steel chair sitting in the middle of the ring, the man still there, head down, wearing a dark pinstripe suit. This time the light stays on, the man slowly runs a hand through his short blond hair, the other hand clutching the microphone pressed lightly against his lips. He raises his head slowly to look into the camera, revealing that this man is the one and only Joey Dallas! His light blue eyes seem to pierce through the camera and look directly into ours. Joey Dallas: I have been saved. Dallas sits still in his chair as the crowd around him sits in awe for a few brief moments before the majority burst into cheers realizing that this is the second man that had "jumped ship" from the former rival promotion, he sits there soaking in the appreciation and excitement of the crowd, the spot light and titantron remain the only source of illumination in the arena. Although while there are a strong number of cheers in the arena we can still make out quite a few of the fans here booing the former MWE United States champion. However Dallas remains still and just takes the bad with the good, the microphone still pressed against his lips. As he begins to open his mouth we are suddenly cut out by static, a few moments later a small graphic appears on the screen stating that we are experiencing technical difficulties. We now open a new scene; this one is a little bit more ... malicious if you will. For as we begin to focus in we see dark dirt trails leading between piles of dirt each accompanied by a tombstone, it is obvious now that we are in a graveyard. Sitting atop one of the tombstones with his feet dangling just above the dirt covering the grave is Joey Dallas, dressed in the familiar pin-stripe suit, his head is down. As we move in closer to him he slowly raises his hand and wipes away a few beads of sweat from his face before his lips slowly start to move, forming words softly, yet with purpose. Joey Dallas: I'd like to take you all on a trip down memory lane, back to the MWE , and the Joey Dallas of a year ago. We now begin to gradually circle around the front of Dallas, his emotions have gotten the best of him here in the early going and he has already began to shake uncomfortably on his seat on top of the pale grey tombstone, as we now reach the far side of Joey we zoom into his face, however the lighting is bad and his face becomes nothing more than a silhouette on the cold mid-day sky. However we can still make out the quivering of his lips as he continues to speak. Joey Dallas: Back to the days of an egotistical mad man, a childish and poor excuse for a man. A person that claimed that they were God's greatest creation and was crafted by his hands for the sole purpose to save the fans from the war that had been created by the inner workings of a corrupt company. A look back at the self-centered dreams and aspirations of this so called man, his journey to the top of his world and his plummet to the depths of his own personal hell. As he speaks he slowly raises his hand, looking at it as though it holds all of life's deepest secrets, but as he finishes his thought he clenches his fist tightly causing his entire arm to shake with emotion. He takes a deep breath in and slowly releases his fist, exhaling as he does so. He begins moving his fingers, in and out still staring at his hand as though all life's problems could disappear because of it. Joey Dallas: As well as a tale about a promotion that was crumbling from the inside out and sure enough fell to the ground shaking the foundations of the wrestling world as it was known. The blame for it all, can be placed and carried squarely on the shoulders of one group of people. A group of people who let their egos get out of control, who only worried about themselves and getting their hands clasped tightly on the top rung of the ladder we in this business call success. These memories ring oh too true for Dallas, and reliving them as he relays them onto us is no easy task for the man that could be the first World Champion. Our focus remains on his face for a few seconds as he begins to push himself off of the tombstone. A few brief moments later he is standing tall, his back turned towards us as he peers out to the horizon. Joey Dallas: That company WAS MWE, and that pathetic excuse for a human being was Joey Dallas, that man ... was me. Just incase you forgot who I was; let me make it very damn clear for you people. My name is Joseph Dallas, and I am an MWE original. I was the man that wanted to single handily end the war that had grown way past personal between the MWE superstars and Windy Bridges along with the rest of those WAR bastards. However to say that I was any different then those already at the top of the company looking to achieve their dreams would be completely ludicrous. Joey looks up and into the sky, taking in the beautiful site of it as he continues to relive the good memories of his past; however keeping his mind off the horrifying recollections of the past is not an easy task for him. He now half-turns towards us, keeping his attention in the distance, yet letting us know that he is not done with us yet. He takes a few deep breaths still looking into the air before gradually lowering his head towards the dirt at his feet. Joey Dallas: Every man that steps into this business has that goal, that dream to one day make it to the top of the company and the only way to do that is to have your arm raised high each and every chance you have, and finally taking the gold and strapping it proudly around your waist. The Joey Dallas of a year ago, that egotistical mad man, wanted nothing more than to hold onto the belt and call himself the top dog. Joey now readjusts his suit making sure he looks as near perfect as possible before finally turing his full attention back to the camera. Joey Dallas: But the fact that I am standing here today is testimony enough that people can change. Sometimes it takes an instant for a man to snap into reality and realize that he was wrong. While others take years to change, and sometimes it takes some sort of drastic measure. Some times it a brutal beat down, leaving you an inch from death. We move closer and closer to Joey's face as he stares into the camera, a look of intense anger beginning to twist its way across his features as we do. The closer we get the more visible a scar on the neck of Dallas becomes, reaching from just under his ear and down under his jacket. A scar that wasn't there the last time Dallas had a successful run in the MWE. Joey Dallas: Sometimes it takes not knowing if you'll ever be able to walk again while you lie semi-conscious in a hospital bed, with your very livelihood in the hands of people you've never met. Sometimes it takes a daily routine of needles and pain killers. Sometimes it takes that hard, quick push down the ladder for a man to change. Because that is exactly what the MWE superstars of a year ago did to me. They took me under their wing, kept me safe as I rose through the ranks, and at the sight of the slightest bit of animosity towards them they through me out like I meant nothing. On that faithful night they brutally attacked me, using every advantage they could as they tore me apart from one side of the arena to the other. Breaking my body, mind and ultimately my spirt as they took a sickening pleasure to my anguish. We suddenly zoom out, seeing Joey standing still, peering once again, down at his hands. Joey Dallas: They held my career in the palms of their hands, and like playful children with a shinny new toy, they snapped it. However realizing the final damage they had done, made them feel accomplished. Knowing that they had put me in a wheelchair, that they had potentially ended my career. Amused them. Dallas had tightly clenched his fists closed as he spoke, however he takes a few more deep breaths relaxing himself as he does so, closing his eyes as he draws in a final breath. Slowly exhaling he tilts his head slightly towards the sky again, opening his eyes as he does so. Joey Dallas: But knowing that they felt no remorse for their actions only drove me to recovery, I fought harder to get back into an MWE ring then I had for anything else in my life. Not dreaming about the success, or the glory. Not striving for championships or even victories. No, my goal was simply to prove to those who had put me down and left me their for dead, that the passion I have for this sport is stronger than any damn one of them. The confident man, is still looking upwards, but quickly snaps his gaze towards the camera as a twisted smile slowly works it's way across his lips. He lets out a slight chuckle as he slowly begins to move towards us along the dirt trail which had now become accustom to us. Joey Dallas: That, is the reason why I was drafted second to Genesis, the reason why I was trusted to turn this brand into something amazing, but when your drafted as the number two pick to a brand there is instantly a world of pressure on you to measure up to the hype, because God knows that there are plenty of others down the line that are ready to take your spot and prove that the general managers choice was the wrong one. That pressure was only made worse when it was announced that on the shows debut program there will be a match, for the World Heavyweight Championship. That is where my opponents this week come into play in this game of ours. Two fresh young superstars in Milenko and JJ Executioner. Dallas stops a few feet away from the camera before crouching down to the dirt at his feet. We watch him as he slowly takes the loose dirt from the top of the path in his hand, letting it slowly slip between his fingers, as the last of it falls he picks another handful of it, however this time he holds the dirt in his hand as he begins to move his lips yet again. Joey Dallas: Two men that could potentially be the future of this company, they both have the ability to get it done in the ring, but both lack one major detail of a world champion. Neither man has the self confidence it takes to make it far in this business. Just take one look and it’s clearer than the hand in front of your face, which is really the problem here. Neither man has the confidence to show their face to the public. I understand that the mask is a tradition carried on by the lucha libre as a sign of respect to those of the past, but from what I’ve seen neither of my opponents has studied the art of the lucha. From there it becomes evident that these men are hiding themselves from the fans, and at the end of the day, hiding from their selves behind an indescribable mix of fear and self loathsome. To become a champion you need to be yourself, not run from it, and for Milenko and JJ the time to embrace yourself for who you truly are is slipping away. Dallas let’s the dirt from his hand slowly trickle through his fingers, running like the sands of an hour glass. As the last of it slowly floats away from his palm he pushes himself back to a standing position and brushes his hands off on his pants. Once again he turns his attention away from us, walking back towards the tombstone from earlier. Joey Dallas: A wise man once said that the bigger they are the harder they fall, and JJ is about as big as they come. The man weighs in at a measured in at a shocking six foot eleven and is well over three-hundred pounds, but I’ve heard the story of JJ Executioner, and I believe that the mans past weighs a much larger burden on him than wrestling him could ever be. Dallas has reached the grave and is now kicking some of the dirt that had fallen off the top back onto the pile before turning back towards us looking directly into the camera with a meaningful stare. He runs a few fingers through his hair before motioning for us to come closer. Joey Dallas: Imagine this if you will, a young boy the age of ten, tormented by the older kids, the bigger kids, the girls. Well this young boy had only one dream in his life and that dream was to be bigger than all those old neighborhood bullies so they could get exactly what they disserved. Well my friends, that young boy, was JJ Executioner. It’s truly a pity that even after the kind of misery they put you through and pain you suffered at their hands. That you turned out exactly like them, nothing more than the common bully, picking on people smaller than you, attacking somebody, and brutally beating them just because their different than you. You aren’t a wrestler, you’re a f***ing brutish beast that deserves nothing more than the lose I’m going hand you this Friday before I move on to the World Championship. Joey takes a long look at the worn writing on the tombstone, trying to read what it says, but the erosion on it has made it ineligible. He crouches down to get closer to the gravestone then proceeds to brush away the dirt from some of the letter, making out a few unimportant letters. Dallas sits for a few moments running his fingers over the letters; remaining crouched he slowly moves his hand away. Joey Dallas: There is one other man that stands in the way of that match however, his name, is Melinko, but when I look at him, I can’t decide wither I should laugh, cry or run away screaming. You see, this man is like the mutant offspring of the Undertaker and a clown. He tries to be this frightening creature, but in the end it all adds up to nothing more than a load of Saturday mourning cartoon bullshit! With this a microphone falls from the right side of the screen, catching our view as we try to focus on Dallas, who’s attention has now turned to a man that has run onto the screen to grab the fallen piece of stage equipment. A second man has also run across the screen to help him and together they pull the microphone up and off screen. Man: We’re live asshole! Dallas is still focused on the two men off screen, his serious expression has now turned into an annoyed look as he slowly turns his attention back towards the camera and begins to open his mouth, before he can get another word out the backdrop of the graveyard falls down in the background crashing to the floor with a loud crack. Joey storms off screen as a large group of workers run in to fix the set, they begin to lift the backdrop up, but as they do a few of the tombstones fall to the ground as well. A few of the workers run from the backdrop to the tombstones before the screen cuts to black. The next thing we know we are in a small, dankly lighten room. In our view is the length of a coffee table, on top of which is a shot glass and an opened bottle of Jack Daniels, at the end of this sits a large Lazy Boy chair. In which is sitting Joey Dallas, his suit jacket is missing, his tie thrown over the arm of the chair and the first few buttons of his shirt are unbuttoned. The expression on his face shows that he already has a few drinks in his system as he sits there with his head in his hands. Dallas lets out a deep sigh and looks towards the camera. Joey Dallas: Well obviously the help around this place is nothing but a bunch of f***ing incompetent pricks. Now where the hell was I? Oh yeah that Melinko bastard that’s right. Let me tell you something about myself Clowny M, and shut up because I know it’s a bad nickname, but there is a reason why I call myself The Salvation. It’s not about winning world titles, being the salvation of this company is about showing punks like you what the definition of the word respect is. If it wasn’t for people like me paving the road of the MWE you would be out of a job right now. So in reality Melinko, I am your salvation. Dallas shifts uncomfortably in his seat before reaching out for the bottle of Jack, pouring himself another drink before knocking it back quickly and slamming the glass back down onto the coffee table causing the camera to move slightly to the left yet still keeping Dallas in frame. Joey Dallas: As a matter of fact, me and every other superstar that were in the original MWE are the saviors of you un-respectful pieces of sh**. And that includes you and those two lackeys of yours Melinko. But now I have this disgusting feeling in the pit of my stomach from just spending this much time even talking about you so if you don’t mind me I’d like to worry about the big boys now. He now shuffles around for a few moments reaching into his pocket searching for something. He pulls a folded piece of paper out, he takes a few seconds to unfold it and take a glance of it. His eyes moving back and forth across the surface of the note. Joey Dallas: There are much larger things at hand when the cameras start rolling this week on Genesis, everyone knows just as well as I do that this is the debut of the brand on MWE television and the night is a huge step forward for the company as we name a new world champion. Almost every man on the roster has an opportunity to become that champion. However there are a few of those men who I would like to address tonight. People like Vega Knight, Sonny Williams, D.C. the American Animal, and … Ace Cannon. Joey pauses for a second; he has already folded the paper again and thrown in onto the table. He relaxes in the chair, leaning back and taking in his calm surroundings. Joey Dallas: All the men I mentioned have the chance to make it into the main event where only one man will be left standing above the wreckage, his arm raised in the air in victory, and perhaps most importantly, have the gold strapped around their waist as THEE man on Genesis. First of all I’d like to address the “Supernova” Vega Knight. The glorified mid-carder of the century. Vega is the kind of man that needs to sit back and take a look at what has happened over the course of his career, you see, Vega has had these types of opportunities in the past yet each and every time he comes up short. But I guess that's the story of his life isn't it? Always having to settle for second place while the people who truly know how to use technique to their advantage take the bull by the horns and fight out the victory. The man now slams his hands on the coffee table, grasping each side tightly as he leans towards the camera, he once again as that oh so familiar smile of confidence on his face. Joey Dallas: So Vega I want you to continue thinking that you have what it takes to be on the same level as the rest of us, because it will make it so much more satisfying when I knock you off your perch and watch you fall miserably, clawing hopelessly for that top spot you so desperately want. I know that your shaking nervously about having the shot once again to take it. Hold it in your hands, protect it as though it were your a child. But you know as well as I do that just like the last time, you'll be just one second too late. Vega I suggest that instead of trying to prove yourself, you just stick to what you do best, being the perverted hack of a wrestler that you are, and if your lucky, I might extend my hand to you, offer you the help and training you'll need to EVER be a main eventer in this company. The choice will then be handed over to you Vega, just hope to God that you make the right one. He begins pushes himself back up into a sitting position using the coffee table. Not letting his eyes slip away from the camera for even a second. The sly smile has once again faded from his face as he thinks about who to take on next, as he comes to his conclusion on who to address the smirk slides it's way back onto to his face yet again. Joey Dallas: Next I would like to take the time to talk about one of the few people in this little tournament of ours that actually has the past experience of being a world champion, he has truly seen the top of the mountain and stood over the MWE with an iron fist, until he let the gold slip through his fingers to his former best friend. Sonny, I respect your accomplishments in this business as much as the next man, but all those titles mean nothing after selling out your people. Sonny you came into this world as the fresh young face of a people, having all the in-ring abilities that man had to offer. You had the charisma, yet you lacked personality. What you did to obtain it is something that even I can't forgive, you sold out your own people and caved into the stereotypes that so many African-American's before you had fought so hard to put in the past. You became this anger ridden gangster, raised on the streets with a love for rap and the hunger for success, but the real Sonny Williams was still at the core of this stereotypical black man, waiting to get out, but the longer he waited the more and more he became like the character he portrayed on a weekly basis. Anger filled your soul didn't it Sonny? Knowing that you would never amount to anything as long as you continued to freestyle your way to US titles. As you watched your best friend in David Whitman take the gold while you waited in the back of the line just waiting for your shot. Sonny, that title shot didn't come because the fans adored you, it didn't come because of your gimmick. No, that shot came because Covell knew the kind of anguish it must have taken to throw the people you knew to the side and become what you were. But that anger boiled inside of you Sonny, didn't it? You couldn't take the constant criticism over yourself, you felt that the people only loved you, because the media hated you, and that anger finally boiled over causing you to finally snap and take matters into your own hands, you decided to break away from the Sonny Williams of past, but creative found a way to market that to the fans too. Which is exactly where your sitting now. Your a lost cause Sonny, and you getting that championship would only prolong the inevitability of you slipping away from the limelight. Dallas pushes himself off the chair, the leather sticking to his cloths tears away as he pulls himself away. He takes a few steps to our left getting, now standing next to a wall. He leans his forearms against the concrete and rests his head on top of those. Dallas only needs a few short moments to think about his next target. Joey Dallas: And then there's the most hated man in the world, coming second to only one to absolutely no one. Unless of course, he is facing off against his most bitter rival, Brian Michaels. That's right, the man I am talking about is D.C., the baddest mouth of the south, but that's really the only thing you've had going for you lately D. It's been so long since you won a match on your own that I can't seem to remember the last time that I saw your hand raised. You've been on a bit of a loosing streak lately haven't you D.C.? You went from being one of the only wrestlers in the world that people were afraid to stand across the ring from, to this, this sad excuse for competitor. You've become the very thing that you proclaim yourself to be, D, your overrated. I've begun to think that the reason you were so successful in the past was because Michaels was in your corner to support you. Keep you focused on the task at hand, rather then the task that was going to be on her knees later that night. You've lost all hope at ever being the D.C. of Rated X, and maybe you've even lost all hope of ever being that top man again. But trust me when I say that I hope that just isn't the case because I want you to go to the limit with Sonny and advance into that cage because I want you standing across that ring from me, surrounded by steel so I can finally pay you back for getting involved with my match against Cannon back in Revolution Pro. And when I finally get my hands wrapped around your scrawny little throat I will tear you apart. Joey pushes himself away from the wall, leaving his hands still pressed against the cold cement of them as he turns his head back towards the camera, staring at it like a man on a mission. He begins to open his mouth, but is interrupted by a quick knocking, he turns his attention into the distance as the sound of a door cracking open can be heard. From off camera we can hear the voice of Joey's limo driver to the show. Limo Driver: Mr. Dallas we've got to get going soon if you want to make it in time for your match. I'll be waiting for you by the limo when your finished here. We hear the door lightly shut, Dallas again turns his attention back to the camera, the same stare as just a few moments ago in his eyes. Joey Dallas: I'll make this quick then, because there is one final man I want to talk about right now. Ace Cannon, I want you to look my in the eyes right now so you know that I mean every word I'm about to say to you. Cannon I have known you since my career began, I've seen you change over the course of a year, I've seen you come from the insecure leader of a mediocre faction when you took the men of High Society under your wing and taught them all the skills they needed to make themselves into the superstars of tomorrow. To the crazed commissioner of this company, pitting me against some of the most talented wrestlers I have ever had to compete against, I took away the most important aspect of your life and in return you took away the most important in mine. Ace I have seen you evolve into one of the most malicious men that the wrestling world has ever seen, a man that would do as far as to punt a child square across the ring. But Ace, I have seen you fail miserably at everything you have tried to accomplish over the last year. James Young and Jason Sincade, the two men you modeled in your image, are no where to be found they followed you into a brutal WAR and they lost, crushing all their dreams of ever making it to the top, and you, instead of consoling them telling them that the dream wasn't over, you threw them aside like they meant nothing to you. I have sat back and watched you tear apart this company from the inside as the commissioner using your spot in the company for your own sick pleasures, may I remind you Ace that this company was written into the defunct federations while you sat on a fat wad of cash that could have turned it around for the better! Dallas is now shaking in anger talking about the man that he had beaten just a few short weeks ago when he made his triumphant return to the ring. He moves towards the camera, bending down so his face is the only thing that we can see as he continues to twist his lips around forming words, sentences and verbal attacks on Ace Cannon. Joey Dallas: If nothing else Ace, then I shouldn't have to remind you that you are the man I came back to tear apart limb from limb, and the last time I got my hands on you inside a wrestling ring I beat you in the middle of the ring one, two, three. Since that day you have become a sick human being. Cannon winning the world title would be meaningless for me if it meant that I couldn't stand across from you again and show you the kind of person a saviour is. The bottom line Cannon, is that I don't respect you as a wrestler and I sure as hell don't respect you as a man. I'll see you Friday Dallas picks the camcorder off the table and throws it against the wall shattering it and as it does sending us into a frenzy of static before cutting out to black.
