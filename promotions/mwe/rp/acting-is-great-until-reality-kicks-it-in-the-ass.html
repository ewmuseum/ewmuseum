Acting is Great, Until Reality Kicks it in the Ass!

Aug 28 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
*The scene opens up as Mason Resoan sits on a small black chair in beige cargo pants and a dark blue button up t-shirt. He has one leg resting on the other, as he observes the room around him. In front of him are huge black poles and vibrant different color lights hanging from the ends of each. He gives a big sigh of relief, making sure that he was fit for whatever he might have to perform. He seems to be the only one in the surrounding area, but as the camera turns around, there is a few people mingling at the other end of the room. He walks towards them, but not directly at them, and makes his way to a brown door which he swings open. Inside, the room is much different and much smaller. It's an office of sorts, with a standard sized oak desk sitting in the middle of the room. It's one of a kind.* Mason: If I was you, I'd jump on me. I'm weak, vulnerable, and fragile. Easily broken, and I'll be the first to...well, rather foolishly, admit that. But that's alright, it is. *Mason gives a slight chuckle, which is almost comforting to him at this point.* Mason: What is not alright, is passing up an opportunity of a lifetime. Pissing away a perfect moment. Flushing down a championship win, and in the end, ruining what could have been, the best moment in your new, young, and prosperous career. That is what is not alright. Darkness will only get you so far and you know what, in every movie, in every war, in every form of entertainment or real life experience, the good guy always wins. The light always bursts through the dark clouds. The superhero always...always defeats the supervillian, and what I want to ask you is...what will make this match of ours any different? *Mason stops and pauses, to regain himself without rambling.* Mason: Kraven, what I'm trying to say is to lighten up. I'm reaching my hand out there, despite the bad or the problems in my life, and letting you decide on whether you want to shake it. I'm not one to sit here and tell you how to live your life or work your career, but what am going to tell you....is that this Sunday, I don't care if someone dies in my life or yours, if someone gets thrown in jail, or someone new walks straight into our respective lives...I want us to steal the show and make this the best that it can be! *Mason stops one finally time.* Mason: I am no savior. I am no saint. I'm just a guy who knows, now, that running will get you know where but to more problems. I'm just a guy knows that being bitter isn't pleasant. I'm a guy who knows that darkness and despair, pain and suffering, and the overall destruction of someone is not right. I want you to stop the running, stop the hate, and learn from my mistakes...because if you don't..you will fail. Because even though I may be nice, I'm not stupid, and I see an opportunity when it comes my way. Please, "Sic" Nic Kraven, realize that life isn't so bad if you make it good. Life is how you make it... *Mason drops the mic and simply raises his voice.* Mason: THAT'S IT...THAT'S ALL IT IS. *And with that, Mason walks out of the ring, leaving everything behind, including a tape of some of the most raw emotion every caught on video.* Spielberg: I think...I think he has the job. *And with that, the camera pans one more time to see Mason walking out the door after his lover.*
