A Letter of Warning; Sonny Williams RP

Dec 5 2008

Sonny Williams

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Saturday December 6th, 2008 03:21AM "Dark clouds seem to follow me, Alcohol that my pops swallowed bottled me, No apology, I walk with a boulder on my shoulder, It's a Cold War - I'm a colder soldier, Hold the same fight that made Martin Luther the King, I ain't usin' it for the right thing, In between Lean and the fiens, hustle and the schemes, I put together pieces of a Dream." These lyrics describe the way I feel about life.. It's how life treats me, it's how God judges me. The worst always ends up in front of me, on my plate, but I try to attempt to live a regular life.. I have a dream, a dream that one day I will wake up and everything will seem fine. All that was ever bad in my life will be washed away as though it never happened.. Now putting pen to paper is never anything ordinary for a guy like me.. But for now, it feels like the right thing to do, to express the way I feel because never in my life have I doubted myself as much as I do now. I've never felt so disappointed.. So forgotten. I'm used to being up in the lime light, winning matches people would never imagine I could progress in. But now, all has changed and it feels.. Awful. To have been a man who made such an impact in the lives of people, and to have made people turn their heads while I was there saying "Hey, I'm here and I'm doing my thing".. To now, here and now, being a man after all I said and proved, who is now nothing but forgotten. It sucks.. Life sucks. You see I'm also finding it hard to find the motivation to wake up in the morning, I feel as though the voices of the media that are saying my prime is complete, are beginning to play an influence in my mind. Those same voices tell me that I am not the successful man I once was, and that I'm slowly, bit-by-bit, moving down the ranks in the land of Mayhem. And I'm starting to think to myself about it, asking myself the same question over and over.. Are they right? No.. Well not yet they aren't.. One man once told me the key to success is the way you go about things. He said if you keep thinking to yourself that something is gonna go wrong, chance is that it will. You need to go into everything you can with three things.. Confidence, Acceptance and Perseverance. You've gotta be confident with yourself and the things you do.. You've gotta accept sometimes that you won't come out on top as the best man, but the best way to take it is to the face. And last of all, you've gotta persevere and never, EVER, give up. It's something a man named Tim Fitzgerald once told me, and to this damn day I have gone by it like a prophet or religion. Because those three words aren't just letters, they've all got a meaning behind it. But that, that is a-whole-nother story. I'll tell you what.. I'm trying. I'm trying to continue using those three words as a guidance in life, but I'm losing hope. Ever since I lost that MWE World Championship to Whitman, I've been on a skateboard down a steep, rocky hill. I've fallen over, time after time but have jumped right back on that skateboard.. I've got scars that are not just physical but also emotional. Those same scars are like tattoos, they're permanent and they'll be coming along with me to my grave. But that isn't what I'm trying to get across.. My point is that I've never given up, or given in. I've experienced a life filled with trauma and suspense. I've seen people I loved, die in front of my naked eye.. I've seen way too much to ever be scared of anyone or any superstar in MWE. Period. Life has always been in some sorts a mission to complete so one day, when I have kids they can live a better life than what I did. MWE is like life, it's a mission to complete. It's a mission to win, a mission to succeed, a mission to become a Champion. Not many people have the true power to finish the 'mission', and only a select few ever make it to the top of the pack.. Maybe people have forgot, but I have done exactly that. I've completed that mission of making it to the top.. I've been the MWE World Heavyweight Champion not once, but TWICE. I have succeeded in an industry where you need a rich upbringing to make it to the big time.. But not me, I started from scratch.. I made it this far on my own and I'm not gonna lie, I give myself credit where it is due. And by just pointing those facts out, I already feel more.. Confident. But those that have no morals, no integrity who are known as people, have forgotten those few facts.. They've forgotten who the real Sonny Williams really is. They've forgotten how I came from rags to riches and that I was once nothing but a match filler. The guy CEO Richard Covell would call up if another superstar had issues to tend to.. That's what differences me and most of the MWE Roster.. They were handed success, while I had to earn it. I had to fight for a salary, I couldn't just lose because I knew deep down if I did that I'd have to start looking for a new job. It was tough, you wouldn't imagine how tough it really was. But I can't say it didn't help, because in the long-run I came out on top.. I became the man people once dreamed to be. I had a legacy that was unique, a legacy people were inspired by. And that's what has me luring in front of a sheet of paper with a pen in hand at four o'clock in the morning. Remembering that I once was 'that' guy.. 'That' guy who had more momentum than Whitman had before he lost to Michaels.. One question I am constantly asked is, where is 'that' guy? Did he die? Did he disappear? If so, where too? How can we find him? WHERE, can we find him? Does the new Sonny Williams know where he is? Of course I do. And I could find 'that' guy if I tried. But for some reason I feel as though I shouldn't.. I should leave it alone. I'm not crazy nor am I on a rollcoaster ride of emotions but I want the rest of my MWE career to be a challenge. My whole life has been a challenge, I've completed challenge after challenge and this is just another for me to pass by.. I want to complete it, because as much as the build up to it sucks.. When you're done, that feeling of satisfaction is just.. indescribable. I want each of my following matches to be a head-on collision between two MWE Superstars at their full speed.. A battle of strengths, not a hunt for weaknesses.. An all out fight til the finish. It's gonna be hard to complete, and I thoroughly know that.. But if I can go from sleeping next to a revolting dumpster in the harsh streets of Detroit, to living in almost royalty, I think I am more than capable of at least attempting to complete this task.. I'm not going to be TOO confident about it either, but I know I can do it from past experiences.. I know that guys like Brian Michaels, Alejandro Rodriguez, DC, Aiden Copane and many more would back me up on that one from what they've already seen. And it starts this week.. No.. It starts today, and it will start on.. The Vega Knight, for the newly introduced.. MWE INTERCONTINENTAL CHAMPIONSHIP! First thing that pops to mind is a second chance.. A chance for me to redeem myself of my current woes and put gold on my shoulder once again. This is taking one small lunge up the ladder I once owned.. But it gets better.. The guy I gotta defeat to officially be the Intercontinental Champion, I've already beat before.. Which helps, right? Right. But my question is, how can I take this guy serious? He's more concerned with getting his genitals wet than defending his slab of gold. He's his own biggest fan and that drives me insane. He thinks his jokes are funny, he thinks his looks are charming and worst of all, he thinks his wrestling skills are of a good standard. I can not make it more crystal clear that no this guy is not funny at ALL, no even if I was a homosexual hobo with no arms and couldn't jerk the engine, I would never be that desperate and there is no logical thought that this guy is a valid wrestler or champion. From what I've seen of the guy, he should change his name from Supernova to Super-push-ova! Hold up, hold up.. How is this guy a self-proclaimed "Supernova", when really all he has done so far in this industry is beat a few jobbers who called themselves legitimate wrestlers. The only prosperity of a challenge this guy has had thrown in his face was a very talented man, a very intellectual man, a determined, vigorously harmful man.. A man otherwise known as.. Sonny Williams. But Vega, Vega knows how that all went down.. He knows exactly what dose of medicine I can kick into his or anybodies ass. He knows that I have an obsession with destruction, an obsession to cause chaos. But what he doesn't know is that gold, gold can almost dictate every move I make, it inspires me to do anything.. ANYTHING, to get that gold around my waist.. That's an obsession right there.. It's not the shinny gold plate with my name printed in ideal letters that makes me want it that bad, it's the prestige, the honour. The honour of holding a title with dignity. Vega Knight, he knows nothing of that, he didn't have to earn that title.. There was no 10-Man Battle Royal, no Singles Match.. Nothing. Unlike I have ever had done to me, he was handed success. He was just handed it, and that, that is wrong. This is a business where when you screw up, YOU SCREW UP! No matter what no one deserves to be just handed a championship title, no matter what the problem is or was. This is an industry full of grown men, not children. Look at my case, I lost the MWE World Championship to David Whitman.. Did I get a re-match? No, as much as I would have liked to, I had to face the facts that I lost, I didn't come out the man. And I accept that, but Vega Knight, he needs to learn some rules.. He needs to headbutt a brick wall and kick some sense into himself because at the moment, he's more concerned with who sells the most MWE Merchandise, who makes the most gags and who has the most fans.. But that doesn't matter.. What matters is if you're holding gold, but you can't just 'hold' it.. You need to do it with respect, dignity, and most of all confidence. I don't like the Vega Knight.. I've tried to look behind all those basic jokes and see the potential he may have in him, but I can't.. He's too much of a dick, he's too much of a person I REALLY do not like. I've said it before to many people, I've said that I didn't like them.. But most of the time that's because I haven't tried to. This Vega Knight guy is a whole different story. So it's now 5 o'clock in the morning and my eyes are beginning to squint.. I'm that tired! But I will leave on a few final notes concerning my match this week.. This Friday at Genesis, I want the Intercontinental Championship, but what I want more is Vega Knight coming at me 100 miles an hour. I want him to prove something to me.. I want to prove something to everyone. I want to show everyone that I am still champion material, that I am still a man to worry about.. I don't want to be forgotten, I won't let it happen! So Vega, he will learn this.. He will realize that never again will he be handed success, but that he will have to fight an illustrious fight for it.. Remember, gold is precious, it's powerful, it's what a man desires.. And.. I.. Am.. That.. Man! Exiting, Sonny Williams. PS: CHA CHING BITCH! OOC: The short verse at the start of the RP were lyrics from the song "I Have A Dream" by Common. This RP was fully dialogue, so that's why it wasn't too long. Feedback wanted/needed
