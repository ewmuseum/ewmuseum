The Saint Of Hero Hill; Fight for what you believe in...

Jul 16 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
-Whether you fall or rise there is always a revelation to take in and to learn from it. There is no exception to the rule. And those revelations that you will obtain can either be painful or enlightening however it will come to good use. In the aspiration and journey to dreams there lies an infinite amount of obstacles and rough patches that challenges you to surpass and defy. And along these journeys you’ll find people who have fallen to the challenge, and those who have risen above. Each step closer to accomplishing your dreams and aspirations is a mountain that lies in front of you, in order to conquer you must rise to the occasion or find yourself lost at the bottom, the abyss of hope. In life, there will be people who are fortunate enough to be born at the top of the mountain, no obstacles to overcome, no difficulties, no merit of heroism. They say a man who stands on top of the mountain is somebody who’s seen it all. Someone who possesses memory and experience that makes themselves more enlightened than others. However I challenge that statement. The difference between a man who climbed the mountain and a man who begins at the top is perception and wisdom. A man is stronger by the lowest he begins and the higher he reaches. While those who are born at the top can never say they’ve witnessed what it’s like to face a challenge. In life there lies lights and shadows, and no matter who you are or what you’ve done there will always be at least a spot of shadow that hangs over your head, daring you, challenging you, failing to believe in you. Shadows that can ruin a person’s hopes and dreams forever. The dark cloud of doubt. But then there’s the lights of defiance, lights that touch your skin that makes the blood in your veins accelerate, the wind that chills your skin, and the fire in your eyes ignite. Those lights can contain power that cannot be defined or calculated, power that can make you do things you’ve never believed were possible. The old expression goes, what doesn’t kill you only makes you stronger. That statement must be true when you consider “The Uprising” Jay Riddick’s career that has defined defiance in every way shape and form. Riddick is sitting in a bar stool in the Casino Niagara Bar back in his hometown of Niagara Falls. Because of the falling state of professional wrestling, nobody really recognizes Jay Riddick and who he is. Maybe some glances by a few people but nothing much else. Riddick is sitting in his stool nursing a tall frothy glass of beer as the look on his face signifies he isn’t very thirsty. Like others, maybe he sits with a blank expression on his face so somebody may give a damn and ask him about his life. After finishing his beer, he begins to speak.- Riddick: Another beer please... -Man beside him seems to have said something.- ????: Never thought I’d see you here like this. -Riddick looked over his shoulder with his head hunched down only to catch a glance as to who was talking right beside him. He connected his peripheral vision seeing a bald man with coke bottle brown eyes and a familiar face.- Riddick: Ricky? Is that you? -Just so you know, Ricky Wyatts is a professional wrestler called XXX a multiple-time World Champion almost everywhere he goes, aka the man who has trained Jay Riddick. You can see an odd resemblance between him and Riddick, both bald, good shape, strong jaws.- XXX: Yeah it’s me. What’s been going on? You look pretty depressed. -Barkeeper brings Riddick another tall glass of beer as Riddick spares no time taking a huge chug out of it before replying to XXX’s question.- Riddick: Everything you can imagine... XXX: That doesn’t sound like the guy I trained several years ago. Riddick: It’s nothing, I wrestle in MWE now and I’m undefeated so far, but each and everytime I overhear people talking, people in the back people I sign autographs for, everybody seems to believe I don’t deserve my spot, that everything I got was a fluke. -XXX takes out his wallet and opens it up, flipping through the leather pockets of the wallet searching for something that must have somewhat relevance to the conversation. He finally spots what he’s looking for and pulls it out.- XXX: You see this? -Riddick looks at what XXX is holding. It’s a picture, a small wallet sized picture of what appears to be a picture of Riddick’s graduating wrestling class, and XXX has his index finger pointed at a younger Jay Riddick.- XXX: This is the man who came to me and took everything I threw at him, through tooth and nail and everything in between. This man is the man that would refuse to give up no matter what people thought. Riddick: I remember this, I remember how I almost barely couldn’t walk out of the facility. XXX: You’re right on the money. My mission during that time was to make sure you didn’t pass. I wanted you to fail because I didn’t want you thinking that this would be easy. But you made it, you defied my perception of you Riddick. When you came into my school all I saw was a guy acting tough and complaining to make himself seem tougher. But look where you are now? Undefeated, and I read that you’re now the X-TV Champion. Riddick: You’re right man; I just gotta do what I always do in that ring. I have to do what I do every week and defy the crowd and my opponent. I guess that’s what I was meant to do... -XXX stares into Riddick’s unchanged expression, his expression of depression which is noted by his weary eyes and his subtle sigh that came out of his mouth. Clearly that was not the true dilemma Riddick possesses within his soul.- XXX: Look at me Riddick, look at me and tell me what else is wrong... -Riddick looks from his tall beer into XXX’s eyes. He cannot lie, he cannot pass off as if there’s nothing because deep down inside is a cancer that is tearing his heart apart. A cancer that may ruin his life if he doesn’t do something.- Riddick: Yes, there is something else. You see, it’s a long story, but a very tragic one. The big picture is that my ex-girlfriend doesn’t think I’ve changed, and because of that, she will not allow me to see my son ever again. XXX: What’s your son like? Riddick: He’s so smart, full of questions and always thinking. He isn’t afraid to talk and he’s the only person I can really talk to. And I feel like I’m the only person that he can really talk to. XXX: Sounds familiar Jay, I can tell he’s alot like you. Riddick: Yeah, but unfortunately I’ll never get to see him again. XXX: Why is that? Riddick: My ex is banning me from talking or even seeing him, I don’t know what she’ll do if I’m caught. XXX: You don’t get it do you? -Riddick stares into the eyes of XXX who appears to be looking at him with a very strong expression of concern and worry. Riddick is pondering over what XXX is talking about and what he’s not getting.- XXX: Me teaching you for all that time, throwing everything I could at you so you’d learn properly. You did it all Riddick, everything I told you to do you understood it all but yet you didn’t know what you were learning. Riddick: What are you talking about? You were teaching me how to wrestle... XXX: No Riddick, I was teaching you about life. Except I taught it in form of professional wrestling. Think about it, never turning away, never backing down, fighting until death, this is where you have to use what I taught you. If you want to see your son again you’re going to have to fight for it. If you back down from this not only are you letting your son down but you’re letting yourself down. Is this where you want to be for the rest of your life? -Riddick’s eyes suddenly open wider. A revelation has entered his mind, and that revelation is ceasing the growth of the cancer eating away at his heart and everyone he loves. Riddick stares back into the deep eyes of XXX as he continues speaking.- XXX: You realize you`re standing inside a casino? There are people all over the place, people who won, people who lost. People gain lives here, others lose lives here but the difference is they took the chances. The chances were never pretty but they took them because they believe in themselves. Do you believe in yourself Riddick? Are you willing to take the chance to get what you want? -Suddenly the room starts spinning; darkness fills the sides of Riddick’s peripheral vision and suddenly around the room all Riddick can hear is the subtle words last said by XXX... “Do you believe in yourself Riddick?” Riddick is falling down an abyss of darkness, his mind follows behind himself as he falls down deeper and deeper down that very same abyss of darkness. “Are you willing to take the chance to get what you want?” Riddick’s arms and legs are flowing down as the wind his body is breaking is causing pressure, making his arms and legs flail all over. Riddick looks straight down and sees pitch black nothing. He cannot see where he is falling, or even rising as all around the world has become a jet black world. Riddick suddenly stops and a loud thud is heard, suddenly his world is restored. A blue sky saturated with white puffy clouds. A bright sun shooting its rays of light onto Riddick’s body. He suddenly sits up looking around at his surroundings.- Riddick: How did I get here? What am I doing here? -Riddick is suddenly remembering the words that have occurred in what may have been his dream, the words his trainer XXX told him. “Do you believe in yourself Riddick?” “Are you willing to take the chance to get what you want?” Riddick brings himself to his feet in a standing position. And he sees ahead of him is a land full of grass and nearby is a tall hill, a hill named Hero Hill that must be at least 20-30 feet with a tree planted in the middle of it. Riddick walks over to it, staring at the hill. And suddenly he hears a song playing in his head, his theme music. “Grab the bull by the horns the old adage goes. Nobody tells you where to go from there. Seems like fate's pulling you. Decisions have to be made. The best path is the hardest earned.” That song brings true as Riddick looks to the peak of Hero Hill.- Riddick: I see an obstacle in front of me, a hill. I stand here at the bottom and will find myself at the top eventually. I was not born at the top of the hill with the sun highlighting the peak of the hill with brightness; I started out like many others in the shadows that are found at the bottom of the hill. -Riddick climbs to the very top of the hill, taking slow but steady steps as he heaves his body to bring himself up at each step. And eventually when he gets to the top, he looks at the bottom of the hill where he started.- Riddick: So this is what it’s like. To find myself at the top of the mountain. Sharpman, you and I are different. On your first night, you became the MWE United States Champion. You were born into MWE at the top of the mountain and throughout your entire career, you’ve never been to the bottom. You don’t even know what it’s like to be living in the shadows of others. How can you even claim to be the devil in disguise Sharpman? You’ve never witnessed hell in your life. You’ve never lived a life below those who were great. Nevermind to mention that I’ve listened to the people, I’ve listened to the guys in the back, and 99.9% of the people believe that I cannot defeat you. Well when we meet in the ring, none of those people that believe in you will be in that ring. All of those people that doubt me, they’ll be on the outside and they will not have a factor in our match. I’m the X-TV Champion now Sharpman, I won it because I wanted to show every person that believed in me that I wouldn’t let them down. But in our match, there will be only one mission for me, I want to drag you down to hell and show you what the real devil looks like, what hell looks like, I want to show you everything you’re not. Sharpman, I want to bring you down and show you what the bottom looks like. I stand on top of this hill, and every blade of grass on this field represents the people. There may be none of these people that think I can beat you, but it doesn’t matter. I don’t care because they won’t be in that ring when I face you. I will face the risks if it means fighting for what I want because I am the man who started from the very bottom and now I stand at the top of the hill, I am “The Uprising”! I am Jay Riddick!
