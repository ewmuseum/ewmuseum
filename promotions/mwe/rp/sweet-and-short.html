Sweet and Short; Sonny Williams RP

Nov 8 2008

Sonny Williams

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A camera begins to blur into focus from it's black and blant image it is currently in. It takes time to do so but when done, it is a picture perfect image to see. It shows to be in a New Jersey park at the night time. The camera does screen shots around the park as it sees one figure on a park bench hunched over with a hoodie on. The camera comes closer as the figure brings himself up revealing to be none other than Sonny Williams. Sonny is wearing his usual out of ring attire which is long blue jeans with one of the legs out of the jeans only over the knee with a bandanna strapped holding it up, he is also in a grey Sonny Williams hoodie. Sonny is without Mr.Cooper which is odd, and has very fierce and stern look in his eyes as he stares deeply into the camera. Sonny Williams: You've all seen my life, heard my stories and witnessed my moments of glory, and my moments of not so glory. Life has always been a mission to complete for me, to start over so one day when I have children they can live a better life than what I did. I couldn't give a damn if people like or hate me, because I know I am a bad man but even at that, I have some set rules myself. As babies most children are taught to crawl, but I was taught not to lie and to this damn day, it is something I have still stuck to through blood, sweat and tears. But now, there is one man who is sticking his ear into my life and making false claims. Sonny continues staring into the camera with a look of absolute anger and annoyance in his eyes. You can tell, he isn't in a good mood and has something he needs to let out. He rubs his chin for a moment or two, still staring down deeply into the camera. Sonny Williams: Sinc Mercier, you turn my stomach. You make me out to be an emotional bitch, an absolute failure and a motherfucking bullshitter and that, that is something I do and never will stand for. It's not hard to call up a camera crew and make a few jokes you found off the internet or in movies you've seen, now isn't it. You sicken me with all the shit that comes off your tongue and out your mouth. You don't know anything about my life, and you don't know what I have experienced so don't you ever attempt to torment my gimmick or my actual life.. But you, a loudmouth? Honestly you're worse than DC. At least he can make me laugh from time-to-time, but you, you're a lot like John McCain attempting to be a stand up comedian, it just doesn't work out well. Sonny Williams: So Sinc, when you say you're this and that all you are doing is contradicting yourself of anything because until you've got a record like me is the day when I won't take your words through one ear and out the other. I have lived a more catastrophic and tormented life than you ever will, and to this very day I have trouble sleeping at night. Sonny's veins are seen to be slightly seen in his neck indicating the anger and aggravation coming out of what he is saying. He brings his hands to his head and slowly takes off his hoodie. He looks into the camera still as he begins to make a slight, unknown chuckle. Sonny Williams: Sinc, you motherfucking obnoxious, arrogant, and self absorbed douchebag, you think you can joke about me and my life in ways that not many people have ever dared to do. You think it is okay to go into personal fucking business and make claims that you yourself can not even answer. Well I did my research too, and I actually got the facts. So do you want to know why I think I am better than you, better than everyone else in this fucking business called MWE? Well I will tell you why Sinc. Sonny Williams: You lived a great childhood compared to me, you had loving and caring parents. You had a life to look forward to. But, you had every expense needed for wrestling paid for at easy will. You're life was given help by others to pursue your dreams and to pursue a life. Sonny looks away and scratches his nose and makes a small laugh to the distance. He then turns fast and looks straight into the camera with a look of fierce on his face. Sonny Williams: I HAVE GOT TO WHERE I AM TODAY BECAUSE OF ME!] I created the man I am today. I never went to college, I never had the chance to become something great.. But I did. I was too busy working at the local fast food shop, working 7 days a week on a $7.69 hourly rate salary to afford to go to wrestling school. I've faced men like Brian Michaels, David Whitman but working like that was even fucking tougher, you wouldn't understand how tough it really was. Sinc, you make me sick. Everything you stand for makes me feel ill, and I bet my sorry black ass if you lived the life I had, you'd either be in the side gutters or three feet under the ground. Sonny Williams: You got to where you are in this business today with the help of your parents, their money out of their back pockets are what have you here today earning a $645,000 contract a year. My journey and success was all me, if there were a movie on my guidance to wrestling success all the credit would be directed to me. Call me hypocritical, call me a slimy back stabbing son of a bitch, write a fucking book on your hatred for me because it has no importance what so ever. You think you're big and tough with words, but let me remind you that all you are is skinny white boy trying to put on boots five sizes too big. Sonny Williams: Yes, I hope you listen to what I am saying here and now Sinc. You, you don't know what is coming at you. I have never beat you, only because I have never faced you. This is history, Monday is gonna be history. I don't just plan to take you out, but I plan to become the next MWE United States Champion. I have been waiting for Monday Night Mayhem all week, it is set on my Calendar and it says, today is the day I make history, today I beat Sinc Mercier and become a 3 time US Champion. Sonny smirks but still has that fierceness in his expression. He pushes himself off the park bench and begins to walk down a path way through the park in New Jersey. It is a husky night as the camera follows Sonny walking down the path. Sonny looks on forward, knowing the camera is there still. Sonny Williams: Sinc, I don't want just a wrestling match come Monday Night Mayhem, I also want a all in brawl. I want something I grew up on being apart of. Something you may have only seen a few times. Sinc, you have crossed a thin line that not many people have ever crossed in a lifetime of depression. I like to play dirty, and I am going to do everything I can to leave that ring as the sole standing man. Whether I win by pinfall or submission, it doesn't matter because I will win, and I will do it in a way that you will suffer and let all the 8 year olds around the globe know that their "Sinc Mercier" is nothing but a coward and a man that talks and doesn't walk. Sinc, it will be my pleasure to leave you in pain, I am going to grab your albino hair and hold your head back and show you what it feels like to ride this train. I hope you like soup, because when I knock your teeth down your mouth it's all you will able to eat. Sonny stops walking on the foot path and turns to look deeply into the camera. He looks more fierce than ever. The camera stops suddenly to see Sonny and his expressions expressed. Sonny Williams: I will leave on this note Sinc, I meant every word of my promo. Whether it was not entertaining to you, I couldn't give a fuck. This isn't just any match, no. This match has got an extra vibe that doesn't come around every day. This match may not be as great as DC vs. Brian Michaels but it's gonna spark debate, it's gonna spark epicness. Sinc, I hate you and all my lifetime agony and anger, it's going to be unleashed on you. Look into my eyes, look at my face, take a good close look because this is the face of the next MWE United States Champion. I swear so. Sonny stops talking and continues staring down into the camera. He suddenly turns and puts his hoodie back over his head as he walks down the pathway. The camera sees Sonny walking down the pathway normally. The camera begins to blur out as the promo ends. OOC: It was short but it was all that was needed to say. Feedback would be more than appreciated!
