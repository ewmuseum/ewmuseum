Meeting Christina

Aug 24 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
*The camera opens up to show the door of a condo in Miami, Florida. ‘’I kissed a girl’’ by Katy Perry is blasting throughout the room. The camera quickly shows Brian Michaels, with a visible black eye and large flowers in his hands, grinning. He reaches into his pocket and takes out a tic-tac, putting it in his mouth. The music is then toned down.* Woman: WHO THE FUCK IS IT? *Michaels is shocked by the language, and stands back a bit. He looks around, then clears his throat to speak.* Michaels: It’s…um…me, honey. *A loud trashing sound is heard, as the woman angrily stomps loudly toward the door. She shrieks and violently pulls it open, to see Brian Michaels, her ex-girlfriend. The camera looks at the woman, who is Christina Darby, Brian’s ex-girlfriend.* Michaels: …Hi. *Christina’s face goes red as she screams and slaps Michaels across the face, hitting his bad eye. Michaels rears back and holds his black eye in pain.* Michaels: Goddamnit, woman! You couldn’t have slapped me on the other side? *Michaels begins to rub the left side of his face, clearly in agonizing pain. Christina is still fuming.* Christina: You think…you can just go out of my life randomly, then come back whenever you want? *Michaels shrugs.* Michaels: Yeah. By the way, what are you doing listening to I kissed a girl? Christina: What, now I can’t listen to it? Did you make that rule up too? You're so....controlling! Michaels: My God, you sound like a certain Cannon I know...These women and their bitching… Christina: Excuse me? Michaels: I didn’t say anything. Now, I wanna take you out to a nice restaurant. Come on. *Michaels goes to grab Christina’s hand, but she swipes Brian’s hand away. She gives him a wrongful look as Brian pouts.* Michaels: C’mon. Let’s go somewhere, it’s Miami! Let’s go to the club…SOMETHING, woman! You’re so boring, you never want to go anywhere. *Christina puts her arms across her chest, shaking her head in the process.* Christina: I’m not going anywhere. Go date one of those wrestling hookers, no one wants to see you. Michaels: But I’m cute… *Michaels winks at Christina as she attempts to close and lock the door. With amazing ability, Brian puts his arm between the door and stops it from closing.* Michaels: I will break my foot off in your ass woman! Get your ass out here and let’s go to dinner. *Christina moans in displeasure.* Christina: Why don’t you go away already?! Michaels: Because I’m persistent, bitch! Now get dressed, we’re going out. Christina: Is this because of what happened with you and D.C.? *Michaels runs his hand over his mouth. He grimaces at hearing the name and tries to hold the anger back by biting his lip.* Michaels: No…its not because that…jack off…lied to me. It’s because I’ve got a match against Alejandro Rodriguez and it’s gonna be intense and I need a way to relax. *Christina again tries using her force to close the door, but to no avail.* Christina: So you’re going to sleep with me to relax yourself? NO! Michaels: Goddamnit, why are women always assuming the worst? I seriously need a way to get my mind off things. I knew you’d be close-minded. Forget it. *Michaels lets go of the door and slowly walks away with his head hung low. The door closes for a minute as Michaels reaches the bottom floor. Michaels is downstairs in his black 2009 Mercedes-Benz and is about to hit the ignition as Christina walks out with a pink mini-skirt and a white Hollister shirt.* Michaels: Now THAT’S what I’m talking about. I knew you’d fall for it. Christina: Yeah…just shut up and take me to dinner. Michaels: Whatever you say, playboy bunny. *The Mercedes-Benz is seen driving at maximum speed, cruising down the highway to Mancini’s Italian Restaurant. The top of the car is off, as Michaels and Christina converse.* Christina: So who are you facing again? Michaels: Alejandro Rodriguez. The Mexican Mighty Mouse…agh. D.C. pisses me off. I mean, honestly, MWE MADE HIM. MWE MADE D.C. D.C. did NOT make MWE and he thinks he has the right to go off talking about how I always wanted to be in the spotlight? I call bullshit. D.C. was always going to be the future of MWE. The son of a bitch was impatient and felt he had to run ME over, of all people? That I was holding him down? Can you believe that shit? *Christina seems to be interested in other things, such as twiddling with her hair and changing the radio station.* Christina: And I thought women bitched too much… *Michaels looks over at her in an odd way, as the wind puts the hair in her eyes.* Michaels: What was that? Christina: Nothin’. *Michaels nods and pulls over to Mancini’s. They slam the doors of the car and walk into the restaurant.* Michaels: Mancini’s…sounds familiar. *They stop after walking through the front door to see an elegantly-dressed man waiting for them at the front desk. He speaks in an Italian accent.* Waiter: Table for two? Michaels: You know, why don’t you speak regular American? Waiter: I do not know what you are talking about. Michaels: Oh, shut the hell up. You know what I’m talking about. I can spot a fake accent when I hear one. How about you just take us to the goddamn table and let us order our food? *The waiter looks over at Christina.* Waiter: Someone hasn’t been getting ‘’any’’ *He shares a laugh with Christina as Michaels’ facial expressions worsen.* Michaels; Boy, I will slap the shit out of your mouth so fast, call your manager and have him send you to Italy SO damn fast… Waiter: I will get you the table now! Thank you! *The waiter takes them to the table, muttering things about Brian in the process. He seats them in a booth on purpose. Michaels grunts at him, as he and Christina sit on opposite sides and look at their menus.* Michaels: See anything you like? Christina: Nope. *Michaels takes a deep breath. He licks his lips, before seeing an Alejandro Rodriguez-look-alike pass by. He yells in fury and jumps on the look-alike, punching him in the face.* Michaels: I did it! I beat Alejandro Rodriguez! *The Alejandro Rodriguez look-alike gets up, taking his mask off and crying. It appears to be a child of 10 years old. Michaels ‘’oohs’’ the thing and passes the child 30 dollars. The child stops crying and smiles. He then runs away as Michaels shrugs.* Michaels:Just like Alejandro. Give him some money and he’ll shut up. *Michaels takes a seat as drinks arrive.* Michaels: Who ordered these? Christina: Me. *Michaels shrugs and takes a drink out of the margarita.* Michaels: So I’ve got a match against HIM. Alejandro Rodriguez. Alejandro… *The camera shifts to Michaels’ face. The camera gets a close up on his black eye as he twitches with anticipation.* Michaels: Alejandro…you badmouthed me. You laughed at me. You kicked me when I was down…and I’m supposed to believe…that in a match, you’re going to beat me? *Michaels takes a sip of his margarita.* Michaels: Apparently, beating me once…means you’re going to beat me again. I can assure you, Mr. Small Luchadore…this is not the case. You wanna know why? *Michaels licks his lips and winks at the camera.* Michaels: Because…this is a First Blood match. I excel…at this kind of match. No, no…wait. I don’t excel at this kind of match. I make…myself bleed. And…I make my opponent bleed. Alejandro Rodriguez, I don’t care if I bleed first…because…I’m not going to bleed last. *Michaels looks at his right hand and puts it on his chin, shaking his head violently.* Michaels: I’ll give you a little fact. I’ve bled on five different continents! I’ve had my eye busted open by Sonny Williams, I’ve been beaten to a bloody pulp!….and what makes you any different? What makes you, Alejandro Rodriguez, different from the rest? Nothing. *Michaels shakes his head in disapproval as the camera begins fading out.* Michaels: You’ll find out, Rodriguez. You’ll find JUST like the rest of them. You’re gonna pay a price to get in the ring with me…you’ll see. *The camera goes to static.*
