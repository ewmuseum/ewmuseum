Setting the Tone

Aug 6 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
*MWE cameras fade in on a cloudy day in downtown Chicago, Illnois. Cars are seen driving around, parking. People of all races are seen walking down sidewalks rapidly, and some cars are seen passing red lights. The camera then focuses on Richard Covell, owner of MWE and father of Brian Michaels. He is holding a Chicago Tribune newspaper in one hand and Starbucks coffee in the other. He is wearing a gray trench coat with a black hat and adjusts his newspaper, but it falls to the ground. He mutters to himself and picks it up. He looks to the left hand-side and sees a man in boxers and a blue tie spreading his legs (that sounds awful) and scratching his dirty blonde beard. Covell looks intrigued (not like that) and looks at the man, and recognizes it to be Brian Michaels, who is now snoring. Covell taps his arm and gets no response. Covell then throws the Starbucks coffee at Michaels and Michaels immediately jumps up.* Michaels: OH SHIT! What was that? Covell: What the HELL are you doing sitting out here on a bench? Michaels: Long story short...Christina kicked me out. She's pissed off that I got fired from AEW, she was good friends with everyone there, yada, yada. *Covell looks over Michaels and shakes his head. Covell squints his eyes at Michaels as Michaels gets the wrong idea.* Michaels: What? Is there a girl behind me? Covell: What happened to...Brian Michaels? Who took my son? Michaels: Well Courtney took my virginity when I was 17, if that's what you're asking. *Michaels shrugs and scratches his head as Covell looks down on Michaels.* Covell: Brian Michaels...is a three-time MWE World Champion. You're not the Brian Michaels I know... *Covell gestures at bottles of tequila and wine thrown on the floor, cracked.* Michaels: That wasn't me. That was some dude...he came over here, pissed off...yelling something about his balls not being big enough....wanting to take my pants...blah. Covell: You're not Brian Michaels. Brian Michaels wouldn't be laying here, whining LIKE A LOSER! Where....is the PASSION?! Where...is the INTENSITY?! You've lost it, kid. You've proven to everybody in the wrestling world that you were a one-time thing. You haven't been a World Champion in a goddamn year! You've been settling for MEDIOCRITY! You're drinking, you're out of shape! *Covell pushes Michaels down to the bench harshly, causing Michaels to sit back a little.* Covell: You're getting pushed around by women, you're getting fired...you're pathetic, you miserable piece of shit... *Michaels bows his head down and looks at the bench and the area around him. He puts his hands on his head, pondering his next decision.* Covell: Wipe yourself up. Get your life together...you've got a Title match coming up. This your ONE chance to redeem yourself. You prove to me, to MWE, and to YOURSELF...that you're still the BEST. Otherwise...you're useless. *Michaels takes a deep breath and shakes with emotion. Covell shakes his head once, and begins to walk away. Michaels grabs his hair, pulling it violently. He shakes his head and goes back to sleep as the camera begins to zoom away into goes pitchblack.* PART TWO *Michaels is seen on the bench, with his head suddenly waking up. He yawns, then slowly moves head upwards.* Michaels: My opponents are formidable. They all have history...just like me. Except...I'm back. I'm not back as the 2008 Brian Michaels. I'm back, 2007 style. *Michaels grins to himself.* Michaels: David Whitman. David Whitman. You were the beginning of my entire story. It all traces back to you Dave...You were a good man. *Michaels nods his head and licks his lips.* Michaels: But last I checked...I beat you twice. I may have lost my job...and I may have lost my girl...but I haven't lost the killer mentality. *Brian walks to the counter and grabs a towel. He wipes his face with the towel, burying his face in it.* Michaels: I still have the will to win...Whitman...I've beaten you once...and I can do it again. David, you’ve got my respect, but you’ve yet to beat me…I’m gonna keep it that way. You’ll see, I’ll show ya. *Michaels takes a deep breath and sits on the counter, taking out a bottle of water. He begins to drink half the bottle, before throwing it down.* Michaels: B.G. Mancini, you wanna act like a mobster? You go ahead…because you don’t know what it’s REALLY like living on the streets. You don’t KNOW what it’s like to not know where your next meal is coming from. I used to live the high life…but this past week has been hell. I’ve seen women abused, I’ve seen guys overdose…and it makes me sick to my stomach to see all these lives being thrown away…it’s bullshit… *Michaels grins and gives a slight chuckle.* Michaels: And we’re supposed to believe you, B.G. Mancini, is gonna kick MY ass? FUCK NO. The fact of the matter is that I WISH you could come up to me at Crowning Glory, look me dead in the eyes…and tell me you’re hardcore. Because you’re not B.G…You’re not. You, my friend, are an insignificant PRICK who gets his rocks off by acting like he’s a tough mobster. Of all people, you would bring DISGRACE to that Title… *Michaels gets up and stretches, and begins texting on his phone. He looks back at the camera after sending the text and smiles.* Michaels: Oh that’s DEFINITELY who you thought it was. My life needs to be turned around. No more bullshit. SPEAKING OF BULLSHIT…why don’t we move on to Xavier Stone? *Michaels gets on the phone, pausing. His words are audible, however. He then hangs up the phone.* Michaels: Xavier Stone, you are…a bitch. You…overrate yourself. Quite frankly, you…talk…WAY…too much. I mean, you’ve got a kid, man! Be a good example, be a FATHER…you go around punching people in front of your kid? Ridiculous. The fact is, I want this more than you do. I’ve seen the way you walk around…it sickens me that people like YOU even have a spot on this roster…X…if you want my respect…EARN IT. In the middle of that ring…on Sunday night…we’re going to see if you can bring it. *Brian runs a hand through his hair.* Michaels: Xavier…you can bring your kid…you can bring your burnt wife…Bring your whole FUCKING FAMILY…Bring Crispy, bring the kid from the Zack and Cody show, BRING THEM ALL and I will PERSONALLY…show them…how weak their father/husband…REALLY is. *Michaels says this last line with a cold voice, as a cab pulls up in front of him. He throws his wrestling bag in the backseat and sits in the front.* Michaels: So, Jekyl…is a CA-RAZY character. *Michaels says crazy with a sarcastic tone. He begins to twitch and chuckle.* Michaels: Because see, Jekyl had a rough childhood. How many times have I heard THAT one? For all we know, Jekyl probably lived in a rich neighborhood, and played football at USC, and went on to start Malibu Enterprises and… *Michaels realizes his mistake.* Michaels: OH. Wrong character. Shit happens. Jekyl, you’re a clown…as a kid, I’m not gonna lie…I was deathly afraid of clowns. At least…till I kicked one in the balls and ran off and laughed. Sound familiar? *The taxi cab has been driving for around 5 minutes, and Michaels notices that the tab is at $9.00. He looks at his wallet and sees nothing. Michaels mutters ‘damn’ and looks at the taxi driver as they continue to drive to MWE Headquarters in downtown Chicago.* Michaels: Alex and David…THE Bowens…One looks like a bit mental…the other IS mental. Alex, you can hide behind your big man…but that’s gonna show everyone how little confidence you’ve got in yourself. Do us ALL a favor, grow some balls, and get rid of the Hellboy look-a-like, hmmm? Michaels: Speaking of the Hellboy look-a-like, David Bowen! How the hell are ya, man? *Michaels waits for a response, by giving a dead-pan stare at the camera.* Michaels: You’re not much of a talker are you? I think he’d be the most charismatic MWE World Champion since Sonny Williams! *Michaels ponders the comment, then raises his finger as he just got an idea.* Michaels: Speaking of Sonny Williams…seems like we’ve switched roles, right? I mean, I used to be the bitter guy who thought that he was too good for MWE. Not to mention, I pulled it off better. See because now…I have something to fight for. I NEED to prove that I’m still the best. *The taxi driver looks back, beginning to reverse park.* Michaels: Sonny Williams, the last time we got in the ring…you beat me. I’ll give you respect, but that was nearly a year ago, things…have changed. The Sonny now…likes to flaunt his past history. The fact is, everyone knows it, you’d be NOTHING without John Austin. Yeah I said it, John Austin is better than Sonny Williams EVER will be. Sonny, if you’re as good as you say are…you’ll win. If the hype is worth it…if you’re not just another big muscular wrestler with marginal skills? Or are you going to lose? Are you going to choke it away, hmm, Sonny boy? I guess that answer is up to you, then. *Brian reaches back to grab his wrestling attire from the backseat.* Michaels: Devlin Sharpe, we go way back to when you were a kid. Not many people may know this, but Devlin Sharpe was one of the original Rated-X members. Devlin, was always a talented wrestler…but I always doubted him becoming a World Champion. See, back at the original MWE Turmoil, the World Title was on the line between Whitman, myself and Sharpe. I knew Whitman was a beast, but…Devlin Sharpe? This skinny kid? *Michaels chuckles to himself.* Michaels: The kid could pack a punch…but apparently the size of the dog is what really matters, because I fucked him UP! My prediction? The same will occur on Sunday Night. *Michaels grins as the taxi comes to a complete stop.* Michaels: And finally…Alejandro fucking Rodriguez! How’s your sex life? Still short on love? It doesn’t matter. Short jokes won’t matter once we step in the ring. Alejandro’s an athlete…that can’t be ignored. He can do some things I’ve never seen before, true that. *The taxi driver sticks his hand out, waiting for the $30 that Brian owes him, as Brian gestures ‘one second’.* Michaels: I’ll give you your props too…you beat me too…but it won’t be enough this time, Alejandro. The past is the past, and we will leave it like that. I am changing my ways, I am no longer an arrogant snob. I am Brian Michaels. *Michaels flips off the taxi driver, and unlocks the door, running out. The taxi driver gets an angry expression on his face as Michaels begins running down the sidewalk.* Michaels: And if you’re not down with THAT…just kidding. Brian Michaels has been reborn, and I’m bigger, better…and stronger…than ever before. *The MWE cameras zoom out as Michaels continues running away from the Taxi driver.*
