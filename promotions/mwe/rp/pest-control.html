Pest Control; A JJ Executioner RP

Oct 30 2008

JJ Executioner

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
* I would recommend reader discretiion but at the same time, this is not meant to be gory or horrific. I hope you enjoy it.* At first we see nothing at all. Only the piercing, whistling squeals of a sixty mile an hour gale can be heard. Then small hammerings of metal on metal and harsh sounds of wood being roughly shaved. As the scene begins, the camera slowly fades in to what looks like a workshop. Shelves line the walls and are filled with random artefacts, most of which are covered with dust. There is a dim light in the background. In front of it is a large man, huddled over a large table which is laid out like a work bench. He is leaning over a large box like object, gently filing it on one side. The camera moves in slowly. We can see that he is surrounded by tools on all sides, discarded lazily after use. Sawdust and larger wood cuttings lie scattered on the floor, with the occasional footprint in them. As we get closer to the man at the table, he stops filing and reaches across for a small pot and a thin brush. With a surprisingly delicate touch and an almost caring attention to detail, he begins to add colour to an object within the walls of the box. The wind howls up again. Almost as if disturbed, the man stands up. As he uncoils from his position of total and utter concentration he reveals himself to be a giant. He moves around to the far side of the room and switches on another light. This brighter light fixed to the ceiling reveals the physical details of the man known to the MWE as JJ Executioner. As always JJ stands tall and proud, his imposing muscular structure showing clearly. He is only wearing a very tatty t-shirt and a dusty pair of trousers, ripped at a three quarter length. However despite this relatively relaxed exterior, JJ stills wears his mask which along with his long hair obstructs most facial detail. JJ: “At MWE Mayhem, we may well see the biggest clusterfuck in the history of wrestling.” JJ picks up the file he was using a minute ago and starts firmly rubbing away on the edge of the box. “Five Man Mayhem it is being called. And myself included, those four men are probably the biggest misfits in the company. So why not put them all in one match?” JJ: “But what nobody seems to see is that the winds of change are blowing through the MWE. And I intend on being at the forefront. So if four other freaks think that they can come into my ring, then let them try. If I am feeling generous they can each leave the same way as Supernova left WrestleWars. I have suffered, more than anyone else and now it is time to share.” JJ bends down. He opens two large doors that arm built into the base of table that he is working at. Carefully he lifts out four small boxes. JJ: “Now let’s talk about my opponents.” JJ reaches into two boxes and pulls out a pair of large brown rats, dangling and struggling as he holds them by the tail. “Firstly the Clown Freak Brothers, Alex Bowen and Milenko.”On closer inspection as the camera draws nearer, it becomes clear that these rats have their faces painted white and black, although it has merged into a grubby grey. “These two men, who have no chance of being physically ready to face me after they damn near killed each other at WrestleWars, grew up together; they know each other inside and out. Together they could make a fearsome team but individually, their blood lust will blind them to only see each other. They are surely easy targets.” JJ places both rats in opposite corners of the large box that he building earlier. He then throws off the containers on the dusty floor. JJ: “Now unfortunately, I can’t have the best seat in the house to watch those two beat the livings shits out of each other again. It would make winning that much simpler.” JJ picks up another rat out of a third box. This one is even larger and shaved completely bald but is painted (top) half red and (bottom) half blue. He also has a large cowboy style hat on, which obstructs its’ face and caused it to really struggle. JJ: “This Brokeback Mountain extra speaks more shit than Vega Knight but makes as much sense as Windy Bridges, which is saying something on both counts......I don’t know what to expect from this Texan punk other than hope. Hope that he will bleed willingly and easily, as he becomes yet another victim.” The rat is placed in a third corner. One box remains and JJ takes great care removing an enormous rat, which is similar in size to a small cat. This one is painted in red and black stripes but is relatively docile, almost as if drugged. JJ: “And Mason Blade, I am going to do you and everyone watching the biggest favour of all. You will fall to the Death Scythe and I will remove you and your shit from the MWE. Your desperation for revenge has led you right in to my sights and you are now trapped.” *JJ holds this rat up close to his face* You, Mason, are a monster who scares precisely no one. Your mask, your size is only intimidating on a dark alley. You are out of your depth in the MWE, in my ring.” JJ places this rat in the last remaining corner which has a larger space left for it. JJ: “You all would be so much more comfortable in your own little worlds. But at Monday Night Mayhem, Five Man Mayhem will see four strangers out of their comfort zone and within striking distance of the Death Scythe. I am truly looking forward to wreaking damage on those stupid enough to consider stepping up to me in my environment, where I feel so at home, leaving pain and destruction in my wake.” JJ steps around the desk and removes a large Perspex lid from the box where the rats have been placed. With the lid, many dividers are also removed. The rats are free to roam around this maze-trap within which they have been placed. Above JJ are three chains. JJ: “But know this, at Mayhem next Monday, JJ Executioner will be left standing tall.” JJ pulls a chain above his head. Suddenly a massive pair of blades swing down like pendulums over the box. On their first swing, they crash through the sides of the box and the two rats that were painted to look like Alex Bowen and Milenko are cleaved in half. JJ: “The Juggalos can’t stop me.” JJ pulls the second chain. The huge Mason Blade rat which didn’t move that far is fried as a massive electric shock plunges through it courtesy of a metal stake, which falls from the ceiling. JJ: “Mason Blade is big and tall but if he has the bottle to show, i will show him that all men are similar when lying on their back on the floor.” JJ pulls the third chain. Nothing happens. He pulls it harder. Still nothing. He pulls it again, so hard that it falls from the mechanism that was holding it. He looks at it, bewildered for a split second as to what to do with it. Then he moves to pick up the rat and wraps the thick chain around it, squeezing harder.....and harder. JJ: “And you, BJ Texan, your debut will see the life squeezed out of you. Slowly and yes it will be painful. So painful that your little Mexican jumping bean friend will feel it too out of pure sympathy and relief that it was you and not him that has to suffer.” Within a few seconds the rat is dead. JJ drops it and the chain to the floor. JJ: “The MWE needs to take note; what is left is of these four men at Mayhem. Well that may not be worth looking at” JJ storms off past the camera, leaving the room. He has an evil glint of a smile on his face. A smile of satisfaction and anticipation as he takes his leave to prepare for Five Man Mayhem and the three hour Draft special of Monday Night Mayhem.
