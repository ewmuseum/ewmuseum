Epinephrine Surge; David Whitman Adrenaline Rush RP

Dec 23 2008

David Whitman

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
QUOTE (Preface "This Cheer? This Cheer right here?") "There is a very big probability that I will NOT be home for Christmas. As a matter of fact, you can go ahead and forget every Christmas carol you've ever heard. A white Christmas in Phoenix is very unlikely, so keep on dreaming. A red nosed reindeer is the most red-f*cking-iculous thing I’ve ever heard of. And there won't be any roasting chestnuts. Besides, I hate nuts. Oh, and Santa isn't real." James Whitman hangs up the phone. Thomas: Spreading Christmas cheer yet again, I see. James: My sister's raising a f***ing moron, I swear to God. She's gonna end up just as hopeless as her mother. Thomas: Yipe. The casually dressed James walks over to the couch and sits down next to Thomas, who puts down the book he’s reading. James: I know what it sounds like, but come on. That kid messes with my mood. You know as well as I do that Jess just- Thomas: Dude, it’s not that. I know about all that. It’s… the kid, man. She’s just a little kid. She’s got nothing to do with any of it. And it’s the holidays, for Christ’s sake. They stay silent for a bit, and then suddenly both begin chuckling at the unintended pun. James: Yeah, yeah. Thomas: And poor David hasn’t talked to his own cousin in years just because of you and your- your whatever. James: Poor David? What is this, A Christmas Carol? They both chuckle again. Thomas: No, seriously though. James: This isn’t about David. All of a sudden, James’ phone rings. He takes it out of his pants pocket and looks at the screen, then at Thomas. He opens it up and puts it to his ear. James: Hey Kate. Yeah, I- What? Slow d- okokok, but- James takes the phone away from his ear and looks at it puzzled, then closes it and puts it back into his pocket. Thomas: What did she want? James looks at him. James: It’s about David. Thomas gets a “no way” look on his face. James: Dude, I couldn’t make this sh*t up if I was writing it myself. Listen, I gotta go. Call Eddie and Scott for that thing later, alright? Thomas: Yeah, that little “errand” you were talking about earlier? What’s THAT about? James gets a sly smile on his face. James: If I told you- Thomas: No, I'm bored already, shut the f*ck up and leave. James chuckles as he grabs his keys and leaves the room. GODDAMMIT! James Whitman is standing outside of his cousin David’s house, along with their good friend Kate Madison. Both are dressed in their casual gear. James: Yipe. Kate: I know. They’re both very distressed, as they can hear David going crazy inside the house. Kate has been standing there for about half an hour, while James just got there. James: And he’s been like this…? Kate: For a while. Well, actually, after Monday Night Mayhem, we met up and he was calm, and quiet. I spent a couple of days with him, he barely talked, but he didn’t seem upset or anything. I just went out today to get some groceries and when I got back… James: He was tearing the house down. Kate nods. Kate: I’m scared as f*ck to go in there. They both look at the house for a minute, thinking deeply. Screams of “MOTHERF*CKING B*TCH@** SH*T C*NT” and other, even less sense-making not-even-half-sentences emanate from the house as the otherwise quiet Arizonian town suddenly feels out of place among its neighboring towns. James: I gotta get in there. James closes his eyes, taking a deep breath. As Kate pats him on the back, he nods at her, and starts walking towards the front door at a strong and decided pace. Just before he can place his hand on the doorknob, a loud crash, accompanied by a “MOTHERF*CKEEEEEEEEEEER” as non-melodic backup, stops James in his tracks. He lowers his head, takes another deep breath, and opens the door to the house. As he enters David’s humble abode, James is met by a sight so different from what he is used to that his mind does not even seem to register it as he calmly walks past the overturned sofa, the numerous broken chairs and picture frames, the ex-television set, and the pile of unrecognizable objects mixed with broken glass. He enters the kitchen. James stops as the kitchen, which is usually halfway presentable, is now not so anymore. It is now the epitome of cleanliness. No empty beer bottles or dirty plates, just spotless counters and floors- almost as if right out of a freshly-printed kitchen catalog. And sitting at the table in the corner, wearing his long white baggy pants and with a glass of water in front of him, is a shirtless and sweaty David Whitman. He has a random cut above his left eyelid, and his expression is one of frustration mixed with Zen- almost as if to convey the calm before the proverbial storm. James walks over to the glass kitchen table and takes a seat in front of his obviously distraught cousin. They sit in silence for a while before James says the first words. James: … I thought you’d probably trash the whole house. David’s face twitches, ever so slightly. David: Balance. James: Uh… David gently places his right hand on his glass of water, as he shifts his focus from the clearness of the table to the confusion of his cousin. David: The living room is hell. The kitchen needs to be heaven. It’s all about balance, James. There is evil in the world, and there needs to be good. It’s all about balance, cousin of mine. James raises his right eyebrow. David: I win the title on my return match; I then lose it to Sonny Williams. I win it BACK from him, and I lose it to Brian Michaels, the only person I seemingly can’t beat. James: No, wait, but Aleja- David interrupts James by slamming his fist hard on the table, causing some of the water in his glass to fly into the air and land on the glassy surface of the kitchen table. David: WRONG! All wrong. Alejandro Rodriguez had a reason for doing what he did. James holds his hands together in a “time-out” motion. James: TIME OUT! Ok wait, so you get up from a finisher, and this guy- one of your supposed closest friends, comes out and smacks you in the face with a steel chair- and you wanna sit here, SHIRTLESS none the f*cking less, and tell me he had a REASON for doing what he did? James leans in closer to his cousin and proceeds to put emphasis on every word he is saying. James: YOU. LOST. THE. TITLE. David leans in as well now, and tilts his head a little to the left, as if to mock James. David: NOT. HIS. FAULT. They both fall back into their chairs. James shakes his head lightly as he rubs his face with his right hand. David is now staring at the ceiling and cracking each of his knuckles one by one. David: It’s Michaels. It IS Michaels. It is MICHAELS. He CAREER STOPPER-ed me, and I kicked out. Alejandro did what he did- James: -For a reason, right?- David: -, and Michaels saw a shot to get out of the match and took it. I would have won. Without missing a beat, James shoots his cousin an incredulous look. James: He did what anybody else in that position would have done. Look, I just- I can’t understand why you’re completely BYPASSING Alejandro here. HE F*CKING HIT YOU WITH A F*CKING CHAIR! They both stare at each other for a moment, seemingly trying to understand each other’s point of view- and both failing horribly. David: It seems you just can’t wrap your head around this idea, but I don’t blame Alejandro for doing what he did. As a matter of fact, I completely understand why he would want to rip me apart. I knocked his F*CKING GIRLFRIEND OUT! WITH A WEAPON! James: IT WAS AN ACCIDENT! Accidents frickin’ happen. James leans in closer to his cousin again. James: And hey, besides; let’s look at the timeline; Alejandro costs you your title by knocking you the f*ck out with a chair during your match against Michaels. AFTERWARDS, angered by HIS actions, you knock his girlfriend out ACCIDENTALLY. While venting out, frustrated because of something HE did. David rolls his eyes slowly, and then just as slowly rolls them back again, almost as if he were watching a tennis match or something. David (in a sing-songy voice): I, knocked Stacy ouuuuut- James leans back in his chair again, this time putting his hands behind his shaking head and looking down at his sneakers through the clear glass table, before he lets out a sigh. James: You just keep telling yourself it’s aaaall your fault. You set this unstoppable chain of events into action. You are the butterfly that caused the effect. Go ahead. Blame yourself. No, really. Go ahead. Once you’re in that ring at Adrenaline Rush with that backstabber you call a friend though, you’d better change your attitude. ‘Cause I KNOW you’d never throw a match. I know you’d never let anyone kill any commitment you have to any match and I know you’d slaughter anyone for even THINKING that- I know you. And I know that once you get into that ring with Alejandro, regardless of your “friendship”, regardless of it being a Pay Per View, regardless of you feeling at fault for Stacy needing stitches, regardless of anything and everything- You’re gonna give it your all to win. And you know what? It’s a First Blood Match. And that means that to win- James stops his little speech as he notices David’s surprising smile. David: You don’t need to explain the match stips to me, cuz. I’m still IN a federation. James’ expression turns to one of surprise before he bursts out in loud laughter. James: Wow. Mood swings much? And also- MEAN much? The cousins share a good chuckle. David: I’m just saying. You’re right. I WILL give Alejandro one hell of a match. But like you said, that’s going to be because it’s who I am, and it’s what I believe in. I’ve faced Alejandro countless times, and I’ve beaten him, and he’s beaten me. We’re I know this match can go either way. But I’m just gonna get in there, and give it my all. James taps on the table with both his index fingers a few times, while he thinks about what his cousin is saying, and how he personally feels about it. James: You know what, fine. We’re not seeing eye-to-eye on this. But it doesn’t matter; the end result is the same. David shows genuine intrigue at the comment. David: And that is? James raises his left eyebrow lightly, as if to gauge how David is going to react. James: You’re going to have to beat Alejandro to a bloody pulp. The expression on David’s face slowly turns into one of confusion but halfway through it abruptly turns to one of contemplation. He moves a bit back into his chair, now looking up towards the ceiling pensively. He calmly answers his cousin’s non-question. David: I guess you’re right. James: Yep. James also moves back a bit in his chair now, as he also looks up at the ceiling. James: Stalemate. David and James are now both gazing at the asymmetrical designs in the off-white ceiling. As David slowly nods without shifting his upward stare, James turns to him again. James: So, you wanna tell me what all the screaming and sh*t was about? David quickly turns his attention to his cousin as he gives him a look of curiosity. David: Say what? James: Come on. Seriously. The older Whitman cousin now looks a bit angry as he points to the direction of the front of the house. James: You DO remember Kate, right? Your friend? The one who spent these last few days looking out for you, making sure you’re ok? Yeah, well, she’s out there, scared f*cking sh*tless, thinking you’ve gone completely mental. David stares at the glass of water in front of him. James grabs the glass and moves it a bit to the side, breaking David’s stare. James: What happened, man? David shoots his cousin an emotionless gaze. David: Balance. James drops his head as he lets out a huge sigh. James: Right, right, I forgot. David bangs on the table a couple of times with his fist, commanding his cousin’s attention. David: I’m serious. Look, I was in a bit of a… okay, just look at it this way; After… the whole Alejandro thing, the whole, you know, Michaels thing… I got knocked into emotion limbo. And after that, I was IN emotion limbo for a while. And today, when I finally got some time alone to really think for a while… I guess that just knocked me back to reality. James: Emotion reality? David: No, reality reality. And I had to deal with all these feelings of anger, resentment, and facing myself- James: You sure that isn’t emotion reality? David: Stop interrupting me! And yeah, I was… I had to deal with so much in such a short period of time… I dunno. I had to- Look, it’s like this. When I was in limbo- James: Emotion limbo, right? David: Right- my emotions weren’t there. And when you have no emotions, you’re just… empty. And when I came back to reality- David pauses for a second, thinking James will interject. James: No, go on. David: - when I got back to real feelings, I had to go to one end of the spectrum, and then to the complete opposite end of the spectrum, so that there could be… David pauses without really realizing it. James: Balance. David: Yes. I couldn’t just- for some reason, I couldn’t come back to just the middle, to the, the healthy zone, you know what I’m saying? James is pensively staring at the glass of water, which is now in front of him. James: Yeah. After a few quiet seconds, David taps the glass. David: Hey, I’m fine, you know. James: Dude, you’re old enough to take care of yourself. I’m not going to tell you what to do and all that. But you might seriously want to get this whole Rodriguez thing into perspective. This isn’t about emotions and thoughts and opinions- this is about FACT. And the fact is, you need to blame Alejandro. You can’t blame Michaels, and you can’t blame yourself. You lost the title, and you know who’s at fault. James gets up from his chair. James: And I HOPE you’re fine. You know what doct- David: HEY! David gets up as well now, confronting his cousin. David: I just told you, all that crap was about A, Balance, and B, Michaels. James expresses heavy confusion. James: Dude, think about THAT. About what you just said right there. And hey, you know what, you might be right. Alejandro MIGHT somehow be completely innocent. I don’t know HOW, but hey, you never know, right? David: …Right… James: Just do me this favor. Think about your first match against Rodriguez ever in MWE. Then, think about a couple of weeks before the three on four tag match on Monday Night Mayhem where you faced him. What I’m saying is, think about your rivalry, then your friendship. Think about how long your history is; you’ve been at each other’s throat since your first match any time you get into that ring. Think about THAT. And finally, think about what he cost you during that match against Michaels. If you STILL feel, after all that thinking, that Alejandro Rodriguez will go into this match at Adrenaline Rush with the notion that you two are friends, then… I just don’t know what to say. But chances are that after all that thinking, you’ll understand that this is not just some friendly competition; this Pay-per-View match will be the culmination of years of clashing between two sworn rivals, for good or for bad. The cousins sit there for a moment until all of a sudden, David begins chuckling silently. James still has a serious look on his face as he addresses his cousin once again. James: I’m serious, David. If you can process all of that, and come out calmly, it’ll mean your mind will be clear again. You will be on the right path, for the first time since… a while. But David, if you think about all of that sh*t, and you still come out with the opinion that Michaels is the one who is to blame for your title loss and not Alejandro, it will only prove that everything what I’ve been saying is correct… David: And that is? James: That you’re A; Very, and B; F*CKED UP! David chuckles as his cousin gets a distraught look on his face. James: You’re not focused. David’s smile slowly fades a bit. David: I am. James: You’re not. He points to David's chest. James: What would you say to Alejandro, if he were here, right now? David pauses all of his movements for a moment, before taking a deep breath. David: I would say… that at Adrenaline Rush, there is a First Blood match. And that match is between two of the fastest, most aerially adept and unbelievably technical wrestlers in MWE history. Two men with enough history between them to fuel the match on that alone. But even so, this match won’t just be about that. This match will be about… everything. This match will bring it all full circle. Alejandro Rodriguez… You and I will Learn To Fly… and we will have our Final Flight. David takes another deep breath, almost as if to convey that he is struggling to find his words. David: The past couple of weeks have been a blur, Al… And this next match will be what will knock everything back to a standstill. Our little meeting in that ring won’t be just another couple of wrestlers duking it out… about some titles, some records… it won’t be a meeting of the minds… It will be… epic. David takes a final deep breath. David: If two identical trains were to ever be traveling at the same speed and have a decimating head-on collision, that incident would be named the Rodriguez-Whitman Adrenaline Rush Match, because that is the epitome of what it is. Despite the posters, the commercials, the articles, the stipulations, the sequence of the matches, even the official Pay-Per-View match card… Despite any of that, the moment the last Adrenaline Rush match ends, there will be one truth, one UNDENIABLE truth… And that… is that Rodriguez-Whitman will have been the MAIN EVENT. The cousins both stay there for a moment, enveloped by the silence. James: Yeah, well. Just know, that that still doesn’t mean you’re focused. This isn't a Godd*mn promo. YOU need to know how you feel about your opponent. YOU need to make sure you're stable. You need to get everything else out of y- David just gets a smile on his face, which stops his cousin mid-sentence. David: Dude. I promise you, right here and right now, I WILL think about it, all of it, for real. But whatever my ultimate verdict is, I want you to understand one thing. And that’s that I’m fine. And I’m going into this match against Alejandro focused, and prepared. He pats his cousin on the shoulder. David: And I’m going to be fine. James now pats David on the shoulder as well before turning around and leaving the kitchen. He calmly walks past the pile of unrecognizable objects mixed with broken glass, the ex-television set, the numerous broken chairs and picture frames, and the overturned sofa as he reaches the living room door. He opens it, turns around to see his smiling cousin’s “thumbs-up” gesture, and he returns the gesture before leaving the house. As he closes the door behind him, James smiles before turning around and walking towards his car. Kate Madison, who was lying on the hood of the car fiddling her thumbs, sits up quickly and turns her attention to James. Kate: I thought he killed you. James chuckles. James: Nah. We talked. I mean his moods are kinda- well, you know. And his views are still a bit… off. But you know what? All in all… I think he’s fine. I think he’s really going to be fine. Kate and James both smile with each other for a while, content, before the quiet and pleasant vibe is once again interrupted by a deafening crash, followed by loud, unintelligible cursing. James drops his head. James: God*mmit.
