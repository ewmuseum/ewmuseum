A Christmas Miracle [Reader Discretion Is Advised]; A Joey Dallas Christmas Role Play

Dec 25 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
===DISCLAIMER=== The follow role play uses a symbolism to further a storyline planned out between Ace Cannon and Joey Dallas. The symbolism used may be deemed as offensive and inappropriate as the character Joey Dallas portrays the part of The Saviour. This role play is not meant to offend but to entertain, and if lines are crossed then I am truly sorry. I hope that everyone realizes that this is not reality and is merely a character doing and saying these things. Thank you, now that this is out of the way, I hope you enjoy the read. ===DISCLAIMER=== It takes only a second for our scene to fade into view as we find ourselves running quickly down a long hallway, large greenish-grey bricks are the foundation of the walls, the lighting is dim and the moisture on the concrete floor can easily be seen. As we continue down the hallway our destination comes into view, at the end of the corridor is a small room, the walls here are made of the same colorless brick as the hallway, there are words painted in black along them as well. Words such as 'Passion' 'Respect' and 'Destiny' fill the walls and images of them flash by the camera in split seconds moving onto the next one as quickly as the last. After looking at these words closely our focus changes to a wooden chair sitting in the middle of the room, a bright stream of light from a window several feet ahead of the chair shines down on our subject. Sitting in the chair, staring out the window, is Joey Dallas. He is dressed, very unusually, sporting loose fitting pants and t-shirt, both white in color, ratty and torn. On his head are the bandages covering the stitches from his brutal brawl with Cannon, and loosely wrapped around his head, is a length of barbwire, forming a sort of crown if you will. All of Dallas' focus remains on staring out of the decrypted building that has been made the home of our promo today. He has not made any motion since we have entered the room and it is unclear if he is even aware of the MWE camera's presence. He continues to look through the window for a few brief moments before slowly shifting his gaze towards his feet, a short distance ahead lays one of the World Tag Team championships. Joey Dallas: This championship symbolizes everything that I try to. It is a symbol of my constant struggle to the top of an industry, a metaphor for the agony of my greatest defeat being abolished, the belt that lays at my feet represents my own personal salvation. Yet Covell seems to think that by tearing it out of my hands that he will be able to douse the fire that has been lit beneath me, but he needs to realize that his efforts to shroud my intensity going into Adrenaline Rush are all in vain. Stripping me of my accomplishments only throws more fuel on the fire, it only adds to my already unstable emotions. Dallas reaches to the ground, taking the title belt by the strap and lifts it up. He begins to stare at the glistening gold of the championship, his name engraved on it. He knows full well that the MWE.com report has officially made him and Ace Cannon the shortest reigning World Tag Team champions that the company has seen. His eyes stay completely focused on the belt in his hands as he begins moving his lips again. Joey Dallas: The first mark of success that I have come across since my return to this company, the symbol that states that Mayhem Wrestling Entertainment is the most important thing in the career of Joey Dallas. You want to rip it from my tightly clenched hands, take the crying child from my arms. To accomplish what? Putting the crown back onto an undeserving pair in Maxx and Mystique? The two men that had the nerve to say I was trying to feed my own ego, only to find a way to convince you to take away a title that I've saved. A championship that had fallen into the hands of two men that will only tear apart the division just to keep, this, around their waist. Dallas raises his free hand up to run his fingers along the design of the belt, slowly moving towards the plate where his name had been etched just a few short days ago, he drags the tips of his fingers across the engraved gold surface. His eyes still not leaving what is in his mind still his championship belt. Joey Dallas: I have taken this championship and gave it a new meaning simply by engraving my name into. I have taken the world tag team championship to a place where it was never thought possible, people did not want this belt, until it was saved by me. However this belt has since become a thing of controversy, as the other half was taken to a place, that even I don't think I can save it from. So take it, tear it away from me, because when I walk into Adrenaline Rush, I will have a much more important task at hand. Dallas pushes his arm out, locking it once it has been extended as far as possible, the belt hanging down from his hand. We switch our camera angle to the floor looking up as Dallas releases his grip on the championship, letting it fall. It seems to move in slow motion as it does, it hits the floor leaving the camera in a shroud of darkness. The camera angle switches to the ceiling looking down at the title belt as a dust cloud forms in the air, the thud from it falling onto the cement floor echoing through the room as our current camera slowly watches Dallas, who has not moved a muscle as we slowly drift back to the same position we were in before he dropped the belt to the ground. Joey Dallas: At Adrenaline Rush I face the task, of cleansing a soul that has been tarnished and corrupted for twenty-five long years. Twenty-five years of greed and selfishness, twenty-five years filled with rage. Twenty-five years of an uncontrollable lust for women. Twenty-five years. An era that has been left to rot away in the unseen depths of the darkest evil. I face my greatest challenge since returning to this company when I have to save the soul of, Ace Cannon. Dallas has now let both of his arms come to a resting position at his sides. He let's his focus finally break from the title that once again lays at his feet, only to let his gaze once again drift back to the window where the ray of lights still shines through on Dallas, making him appear to be glowing from our position. Joey Dallas: I can already be quoted as saying that I've seen the evolution of Ace Cannon, but just those words do not nearly begin to give what I've witnessed first hand justice. He pushes himself off of the chair and begins a slowed pace towards the window, carefully stepping over the belt sacrificed at his feet. As he gets closer and closer to the only source of light in the room it gradually becomes darker in the room and his body starts to become blurred against the light leaving vague outlines of him against it. Dallas finally comes to a stop a few short steps from the window. Joey Dallas: I have sat at home and watched a man that thought so highly of himself that he thought he could lead an entire revolt against a company, trying to take away all the hard work and sacrifice of the superstars before his time. While making an industry that so many make their passion and sole desire to be apart of, out to be nothing more than a hobby. He made this sport into his way of making an easy buck. This man thought so highly of himself that he brought his proteges into his battles and lead them into unknown territory not knowing if they would make it through the fight with their livelihoods in tact let alone their careers. Claiming that they would become champions, because they 'learned from the best.' That same man, took his girlfriend, and strung her along through it all as though she were nothing more than a lowly puppet. My morals and personal beliefs wouldn't just let me stand back and watch as he made a mockery out of an industry I had loved since I was a child and the people that had that same passion I had, that fell into his grasp only to be lead down a path that would see this vial human being take all the glory for himself. So I took it upon myself to save an industry and it's people. The camera slowly begins to move in closer to Dallas, trying to get a good look at what Dallas keeps turning his attention to outside this abandoned building, but the glare from the light shields it from our view. The camera rests a few paces back from Dallas, continuing to focus on him through his recollection of what has brought us this far. Joey Dallas: And so I found myself in the crossfire of a war, trying to play peace keeper, but that wouldn't last long. Because like any person would, when I found the perfect opportunity to strike I made the best of it, and took out the oppressing leader. Admitting that is not something that I am afraid of. I did what was the right thing for this company to survive to see the end of the war and I took out Ace Cannon. I took the cold steel of that chair, and a wrapped it around his knee until it left a perfect imprint in the metal, and as I watched him squirm trying to tear away the padding that bound his already beaten and battered knee, I rose that chair high above my head and let it come crashing down, tearing the ligaments in Cannon's leg. While I stood above the carnage, the fans that witnessed that faithful event grew quite, because even though it may not have been apparent they knew in the depths of there hearts that what I had just done had saved them. Saved them from months of watching this man tear an industry apart from the inside out. Dallas continues to stare out the window for a few seconds before slowly turning around to face the camera, the light surrounds him making it so that we can still only see a blurred silhouette and the very vague features of Dallas against the dark of the room. He begins walking towards the camera, stepping carefully over the title belt again, as he moves closer we begin moving backwards. Dallas' face comes in clearer and clearer as he moves away from the window, letting more light into the room. As he reaches the chair, still sitting in the middle of the room, he stops and turns back to the window. Joey Dallas: But the fans weren't the only ones I saved that night, you see on the same evening I had the pleasure of offering my guiding hand to someone who had found themselves following a path of sin and wrongdoing. For that was the night when Ace Cannon realized that the woman in his life had found the light, as he looked up from his agony he saw Paige in the arms of her saviour. Now any other man would have just packed up and gone home after such an event, but evil knows no bounds and Ace Cannon found himself in a position of power. Being named the commissioner of the MWE, but while Covell hoped that he would use his knowledge of the business to help the company pull through a time of economic crisis, Cannon had only his personal revenge in mind. So Cannon came limping back, and just as I made Alejandro tap out to claim the United States championship, his sinful voice rang through the arena, and as I held the United States title above my head, just as I had done with the steel chair that should have ended your career, you announced your new alliance, with the man that would come to play a very important part in this little rivalry of ours. You had made a deal, with Windy Bridges. His words ring out through the dank, dark and cold of the room before he moves his attention away from the window and finally comes to focus on the camera, his face showing the pure emotion of anger in his face, that rings out with his words. Blood has soaked through the bandage that covers the battle scars on his forehead. Joey Dallas: Cannon I did not want this war between us to happen, my intentions were to stop an even greater battle from raging out of control, yet you couldn't put your own fame and success aside to see the reality of things and you made this personal. You went on a power trip, taking every thing that I had worked so hard to accomplish away from me, first it was my title. Then it was my career. Don't believe for one second that I don't know that it was you that ordered that vicious attack on me. You may have forgotten that night but I remember it crystal clearly. As Joey begins to pace down the hallway the camera flies to the far end to watch as Dallas strolls slowly and carefully towards us, the white of his clothing and the bright blond of his hair seems to shine against the light glaring behind him. He pays no attention to the discolored walls or damp floor against his bare feet. Joey Dallas: I relive that night every time I lace my boots. I re-experience the throbbing sting of having my body thrown into the concrete barricade again and again, every time that I slide on a knee pad. I remember the days that I lied motionless in bed, in too much pain to even attempt to walk. The memories still haunt me to this day, and it was all because the ego trip of Ace Cannon refused to be derailed. The long nights of sitting at home, nursing my injuries, while I watched the foundations of MWE crumble and the hierarchy fall from their thrones at the top of the industry as the company was put to rest. He stops walking and turns to face the wall of the corridor, he reaches his hand up and slowly runs his fingers over the green of the brick, and as he does the green fades away while bits of it crumble and fall to the floor. Dallas rests his hand flat against the stone and looks at the wall, he soon becomes lost in thought as he continues to explain his side of the rivalry with Ace Cannon. Joey Dallas: If that weren't bad enough, as I sat at home, slowly regaining the feeling in my legs, I watched as the stars of MWE found themselves in EW. Taking themselves to the top of a company the same way that the WAR superstars had done to them, pushing away the people that had made the company what it was, demanding shots at their champions, and like a caged animal finally set on the loose they began to tear apart Enigmar Wrestling just as they had done to the company that gave them all the hype and fame that they had earned. But the worst was yet to come, as I continued to watch, Ace Cannon found himself in a championship match. The television screen glared against the darkness of my bedroom, my eyes locked on the picture as the man that I had so desperately tried to save wrestling from held the World above his head. Dallas pushes himself away from the wall so that his hand lays flat and his arm is locked in place. He drops his head and stares at the wet ground, his feet shaking from the cold. After a few moments standing like this he lifts his head, lets his arm drop from the wall and then turns back towards the camera and continues to walk down the hallway. We eventually walk out of a large oak doorway and down a long cement staircase. Dallas pauses at the top, the sun shining down on him still keeping that glow to him. Joey Dallas: What he held in his arms that night, was not just another piece of gold to go in his resume, he held the symbol of my greatest defeat, for it was the moment that the lights in that arena shone down across the gold that I knew that I had failed, but while most would have given up, that was the event that drove me. Drove me to walk, to run and ultimately to find myself across the ring from the personification of evil. Drove me to finish the mission that I had started nearly a year ago. That was the night that I took my first steps since I was ruthlessly beaten and left for dead. I rose to my feet, and as Ace continued his celebration I put one foot in front of the other, toke baby steps, and threw that television across the room leaving it broken and destroyed, just as I was left by my peers and co-workers. That is the day when I decided that I would stop at nothing until I have saved the world from the man that had written the closing page on the novel of my career. Dallas takes a quick look into the bright mid-winter sky, before looking back into the camera. He slowly begins walking down the steps of the abandoned building that has become familiar over the course of this promo. As we move further and further away we can see the rotting and broken wood siding of the building. He reaches the last step and hesitates for a second looking at the cracked sidewalk that waits for him, he gazes over at the dying grass that lays on either side, the same yellowing color of it protruding through the larger cracks in the sidewalk. The camera zooms into the cement just as Dallas reaches his foot down, as his foot connects with the ground a small stream of steam can be seen rising from below his foot. The camera zooms back out showing Dallas walking down the pathway with the same emotional strut we had seen through the hallway. Joey Dallas: But this story refuses to end so abruptly, for where is Camelot without King Arthur? What is England without Churchill, and what is wrestling without Joey Dallas. For every story, every event needs it's hero, it's saviour, you see if was that same night when I a revelation was made apparent to me, no one else was willing to take on the responsibility of being our saviour. So I re-taught myself to walk, and walked back into the ring, starting from the basics and teaching myself all the abilities I would need, for the time would come, that the world would be ready for the second coming. He continues to walk down the sidewalk going through a broken metal gate, the door hanging open. On either side are large, eroded and damaged stone lions standing proudly as the day they were made. Dallas stops to run his hands over the cold stone surface of one of these statues. The camera moves in as he lets his fingers glide over the facial features of the proud and noble animal. Joey Dallas: The home of which would be Revolution Pro, for Cannon had found himself in the small indy promotion just a few short months after dropping his championship, bound on being what lead the company into the main stream, however he was not counting on what was to become of him. For weeks the message was buzzing, discrete warnings sent to him and his pathetic agent Dick Malibu, and when things seemed to be at their darkest, I showed them a glimmering light of hope. Then, just as every prophecy has foretold a hero emerged to save the people, put an end to the reign of terror headed by the ultimate evil. My debut in Revolution Pro marked the dawning of a new era, for it was the night that revenge was in my grasp. Yet just like everything else that I have found in my life, it was torn away. Joey runs his fingers up the nose of the symbol of courage standing in front of him, as he finishes his sentence he clenches his fist in anger. The camera catches his face, showing the intense look across his face. Dallas keeps his eyes locked on the lion statue as he continues along with the recollection of the Dallas/Cannon saga. Joey Dallas: Torn away by a man that me and Ace Cannon have come to know well over the last year, as D.C, found himself involved in matters that he had no place being in. I may have earned the victory, but it's a bittersweet one, for the dirty hands of our so called champion have tainted it. The feeling it left was not one of accomplishment, no the match left a bitter taste in my mouth one that needed to be cleaned away. Yet the bookers held us apart, they had thrown me into a gimmick team with Windy Bridges, who is so nice it's obnoxious, on the other hand they had booked Cannon towards the world title, pitting him against Virus. This is where the transformation of Ace Cannon gets interesting, for his pent up rage and frustrations against me finally got the best of him, and with one felled swoop he became more fiendish and cold-blooded than I had ever thought possible. He found himself barred inside a steel cage, Virus standing across the ring and as their battle raged on, Cannon found it necessary to use a kick that could knock out a full grown man, to punt a child clear across the ring leaving her motionless and destroyed and as her father cried over her. He simply walked from the ring, and was named number one contender. All this while I was held back, forced to compete in tag wrestling, forced to watch as Cannon got the opportunity I rightfully deserved, and given no chance to do anything about it. Dallas releases his fist and pushes away from the statue beginning to walk around the house, as we round the corner we see a large hill. Atop of which we can see a vague shape however from this distance it is near impossible to see exactly what it is. Joey continues along his way, taking a path of dead grass towards the hill. Joey Dallas: I may be the saviour, but even those that do no evil have a breaking point, and as I sat back and saw my greatest adversary continue to be thrown into the spot light just because he was a "big name" I slowly started to reach mine. Deciding what would be best for my career, I left. Leaving the company for dead and returning to the place that I had set out to save in the first place. Now apparently daddy's little boy heard that I had signed and quickly followed after me. As I'm sure has been made obvious by now that it was not long before things once again imploded and we found each ourselves at each others throats. Now for the first time since our meeting in Rev-Pro we face off against each other. Only this time, there will be no running, no surprises, and no interference, this time our blood fued ends! He has walked halfway to the top of the hill by now, and the shape that had caught our attention earlier is becoming clearer as we draw closer, the shape appears to be that of a cross, and it becomes clear that it is that as we reach the peak of the hill. Dallas pears out across the lake that lays on the other side of this land form as he reaches up to his head and slowly peels away the bandage that covers his stitches. Joey Dallas: You see Cannon, this bad blood between us finally comes to a head this Sunday as we step foot into Adrenaline Rush, an event I know oh too well. In case you have forgotten, it was at the first Adrenaline Rush when I claimed the United States championship in my debut match. It was also that event where your ego ignited the fire that has blazed for over a year now. This year's result will be no different, however this time the fire dies. And with it so does your time of casting a shadow of hatred over this company. This year, the people that have become lost in the haze of corruption that has followed you for all of your career, will finally be saved. I only wish I could say the same for you. Dallas clutches the bandage in his hand before letting his fingers slip away, the wind catches it and takes the bandage flying off into the distance just as Dallas turns to look at the camera. The cut, just above his eye already has signs of healing and dried blood surrounds it. He peers towards the cross that stands tall just feet in front of him, taking those few steps towards it he reaches his hand up to meet the wooden surface. Joey Dallas:Ace Cannon, you are the personification of the some of the worst sins known to man. The embodiment of greed; your excessive need and lust to be the main attraction where ever you go. The feeling that the world should be handed to you on a silver platter, while people like Sonny Williams and David Whitman take a back seat to your reign. Pride; believing full well that you are better than the next guy, that your abilities are greater then that of legends. Seeming to think that your fame and fortune make you deserving over the hard work and sacrifice of other men before you. Envy; still clawing towards the top spot that people before you had earned wishing, hoping that you could get your hands on the golden accomplishments and accolades that would seal your place place in history. All these sins punishable by eternal damnation, yet nothing you have done can compare to the new found sin that will be witnessed this forthcoming sabbath day. When I will suffer for the sins that have been committed over the last year, however Cannon, the sins I am paying for will not be mine as you have wrongfully claimed, no they shall be the sins committed by you. Dallas drags his hand down the cross in front of him before bringing it back towards his head, he grasps the loose end of his stitches and begins to slowly tear them away, writhing in pain as the gash along his forehead reopens. Blood slowly starts to trickle down his face as he gets the threads further out, he eventually pulls the last of his stitches out and looks directly into the camera. Blood now pouring down his face and onto the pure white of his clothing leaving a large stain trailing down his chest. Joey Dallas: But as I have already said, my task is not to defeat you this Sunday, it is cleanse your soul. However I can only make take first step on this long and painful journey, so the choice falls into your hands. To sacrifice your career in hopes of being reborn as I was, or to accept my gift to you. The gift of salvation. Nearly no white can be seen on Joey's shirt as the blood continues to pour down his face, dripping into a small puddle on the ground in front of him. The camera zooms out to show the full body of Dallas standing tall and proudly in front of the cross as the sun begins to fade away behind the horizon. He closes his feet, placing them side by side as he drops his head to look at the ground. He raises his arms out to a position matching that of the cross as the camera begins to fade away. Joey Dallas: Merry Christmas The camera fades to black and our scene closes.
