Eat **** and DIE.

Jan 2 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
MWE cameras zoom in on a sight, not too familiar. It is a press room, with photographers and news reporters. Flashing lights fill the room, as a podium is seen in the middle of the room. There are two tables stacked to the right and left of the podium, empty with nothing but water bottles. Voices fill the room with sound, mainly coming from news reporters. The cameras pan out to once again show the details of the press room, with its white walls silent. The whispers come to a climax, as loud clacking sounds are heard. The whispers begin again. He’s here, they say. Who’s here, others whisper. The clacking of boots become more prominent as a fully-bearded figure now comes into the scene. The camera pans into the average-sized man, who is wearing a black cowboy hat, blue jeans and black boots. He is in a black tuxedo with a blue striped tie on. The camera reveals it to be Brian Michaels. His once-energetic green eyes are now a pool of sorrow and disappointment. He runs his hand through his now-scruffy light-brown beard and takes the slow, woeful steps to the middle of the stage, where the wood-brown podium awaits him. As he finally arrives at the podium, and adjusts the microphone in front of him, he is instantly bombarded with questions and comments. He looks down, almost embarrassed, before looking off to the side at his agent. His agent gives him a thumbs-up, as Michaels dejectedly turns back at the swarming press members. He looks at them, angry at himself, before clearing his throat and leaning towards the microphone. Michaels: For 26 years, I’ve told myself that I was the best. I told myself, that no matter what stood in my way…I could get the job done. He takes a look around, squinting his eyes at the flashes on the cameras. He puts his right hand up, using it as a way to cover his eyes from the flashing lights. They pause for a moment, and he puts his hand back down. Michaels: For all of MWE’s existence…I got it into my head, that I was the best MWE had to offer. I got it into my head, that no matter how much other wrestlers would come around…talking about how great they were…that I was THAT much better. That I was THAT much faster, that I was THAT much stronger. His head falls below his shoulders, as his tone is completely lifeless. Michaels: And after my match…I thought one thing… All the news reporters take out their notepads and pens, ready to quote Michaels on what he is about to say next. He clears his throat and takes a deep breath before completely changing his tone and vibe. Michaels: Who took a piss in Devlin Sharpe’s cereal? Whispers are heard, from ‘’Did he really say that?’’ to ‘’Who takes a piss in cereal?’’. Michaels rises from his sitting position and promptly flips off the media, who are standing at attention. Michaels: I’m sick of this shit…from D.C. and Lindsey’s Real World-ish problems, to Paige’s Bi-Polarism…Devlin Sharpe…I have to ask you ONE question….HAVE YOU TAKEN YOUR FUCKING PILLS TODAY?! Do you KNOW who you’re FUCKING WITH!? He begins to unbutton his tuxedo in rapid motions, showing off that he means business. He finally takes the full tux off, revealing a blue dress shirt. He reaches near the podium and take the microphone off its grip, putting it near his lips. He walks around holding the mic with his left hand as he unbuttons his cuffs with his right. Michaels: What I hate…is how…I’m supposed to be CRAZY…yet…I see promos from guys, pretending to be Jesus Christ? Promos from guys, who think they’re mass murderers? Promos from guys, who mess around with other guys? The camera pans into Michaels’ face, as he looks on with a look of horror. His eyebrows perk up, similar to the Rock’s, and his facial expressions reek of craziness. Michaels: What?! NO! This isn’t Mayhem Wrestling Entertainment, this is MTV. I’ve seen many things in MWE. From blowjobs, to car crashes, to broken necks, but NEVER…have I seen ANYBODY, go around messing around with other guys…while watching a DVD of me? He shudders at the thought of Devlin and Aiden Copane ‘’messing around’’. He walks off the stage the podium is set at, and begins to walk towards the large red EXIT sign at the back of the press room. He throws up his free right hand, and shows off his five fingers. Michaels: Fuck D.C… Michaels pulls down his thumb rather quickly, setting the media in attendance up for his trademark. Michaels: Fuck Lindsey… He now uses his left hand, still with the mic, and pulls his own pinky down. Michaels: Fuck the fans… Again, using his left hand, pulls down his finger this time around. Michaels: Fuck Rated-X… Now with his right leg, he superkicks the back door, opening it, and feeling the cold breeze against his blue dress shirt. He pulls his index finger down, leaving only his middle finger visible to the media. Michaels: And fuck…Devlin Sharpe. He exits the room, middle finger still up in the air. He immediately feels the bitter Chicago wind blowing across his face, and attempts to cover it up by putting his hands across it. The media left in the press conference rapidly fetch their cameras to attempt to follow Michaels, but only a TMZ camera escapes, with a rather large male reporter with the camera. They follow Michaels, who is now walking down the sidewalks with a bit of a swagger in his step. The male reporter comes up to Michaels and is now in the camera’s view, wearing a green TMZ sweater and sweat pants. He is almost jogging, in an attempt to keep up with Michaels, who is now walking full-speed. Reporter: Uh, Brian? What was that all about? The camera shifts from the report to Michaels, who has a smug look on his face, as he licks his lips. He gives a faint, yet still audible chuckle at the reporter. The camera zooms out a little, to show that they are in downtown Chicago. The camera pans back over at Michaels, who now stops walking. Michaels: Get out of my fuckin’ face. He begins to walk off with an angered-look in his eyes, before the persistent reporter catches up to him again, along with the cameraman. Michaels once again stops in his steps. He grimaces, before looking at the cameraman again. Michaels: I ain’t even gonna say nothing, man. Leave me alone. Once again, he begins to jog, only for the damned reporter to come up to his face again. This time, he doesn’t let the camera stop rolling, but he gets into the face of the large man, who is already having problems keeping up with Michaels. Michaels: The camera can stay…but you…have to leave. The rather large reporter and the skinny cameraman take a second to discuss the proposition in the corner, before they begin nodding. Reporter: Alright…you can use Steve. Michaels grins from ear-to-ear, thinking of where to go next. He aggressively grabs the camera and begins to pull it towards a locked door. The skinny cameraman, Steve, nearly fumbles it, but manages to pick it up in mid-air. Brian reaches deep into the pocket of his blue jeans and pulls out a key. He inserts the key and opens the locked door, as the camera zooms out to reveal that they are now at the first floor of MWE Headquarters. The camera pans out again, to show what the first floor looks like. Framed pictures of the three MWE Hall-of-famers, Katherine Tenaud, David Whitman and Tim Fitzgerald lie across the gray walls of the first floor. There is also a marble desk with a ‘’Front Office’’ label on it. Michaels has his back turned to the camera, and silence is heard for a moment. After the silence, flat-out laughing is heard coming from Michaels, as he turns around slowly. His cheeks are a puffy red, as he holds his stomach to try and fight the pain. He stops for a moment. Michaels: Devlin Sharpe? Man, get the fuck out of here! He says Devlin’s name in a sarcastic manner before he explodes with laughter again, almost getting down and rolling on the floor. He calms down long enough to speak, after several seconds. Michaels: Who, are you? Who are you…to question MY accomplishments? To question MY work? Do you think you’re some king, Devlin? Oh, that’s right, you do… His signature grin now becomes visible, from ear-to-ear. He makes a gun sign with his hand, almost as if to say ‘’shoot.’’ He winks, almost as if he were acknowledging that he is giving a ‘’shoot’’ promo. Michaels: Well, since you DO have such an ego…let’s go look at your track record. He rubs his chin with his right hand, and gives a sarcastic look, acting as if Devlin’s record is longer than his. With his left hand, he signals that he may have remembered. Michaels: First of all…your claim to fame.. is Rated-X. That was the ONE time in your life, when you were just BARELY above mediocre. He makes a ‘’little’’ sign with his fingers, mocking Devlin’s success, or lack thereof. Michaels: And it drives you INSANE. It drives you insane, because you know…Rated-X was your 15 minutes of fame. Rated-X was the ONE time in your life…where people looked at you and noticed you. It was the ONE time where you weren’t stuck in the mid-card…and you actually got attention. Now…one year later, you want it back. YOU CRAVE a Rated-X reunion…because that…was the only reason…anybody gave a shit about you. Brian gives a death glare at the camera, his green eyes filled with cruelty and emotion. Michaels: Let’s see…you were IN Rated-X, shorter than when Cannon was IN Paige…you’ve NEVER won a World Championship, you’ve main-evented ONE show in your career, and you call ME out? You’re telling me that I’M not good enough? Brian Michaels isn’t good enough for Devlin Sharpe?! That’s ridiculous. The comedic mood is now gone as shades of the passionate Brian Michaels begin to come back. He walks around the room, and walks over to the three framed pictures of the MWE Hall-Of-Famers. He softly rubs his fingers onto them, feeling the life in his fingers. Michaels: The three people behind me, are honorable human beings. They’ve done NOTEWORTHY things in this company, and were honored for that reason…what have YOU done Devlin? What have YOU done to compare yourself to someone like Tim Fitzgerald? Someone who laid their LIFE, for this company…while arrogant fuckers like YOU…stood around, running your mouth, and not doing shit. He again, does the gun gesture, and smirks. However, his facial features turn serious once again, as he names the accomplishments of the three MWE Hall-of-Famers in front of him. First, the camera pans to David Whitman’s framed picture, which has David standing awkwardly in a white suit and tie, seemingly from his Mayhem Mafia days. Michaels: David Whitman…three time MWE Champion…winner of both Crowning Glory Battle Royals…Two matches that, you were in Devlin…and you didn’t win. Current #1 contender for the Undisputed Championship… He grimaces for a moment, thinking of a what-if situation if he were in D.C.’s shoes, and Undisputed Champion. He shakes his head, trying to get the thought of his mind, while walking to the middle picture, which is Katherine Tenaud in a pink skirt and white tank top, with her trusty guitar across her chest. Michaels: Katherine Tenaud...FIRST MWE Women’s Champion, honorary member of Rated-X…never lost a match in MWE. After eyeing Katherine’s picture for awhile, Michaels takes slow, yet emotional steps towards Tim Fitzgerald’s picture. He glares at it, trying to get a feeling for Tim’s one-of-a-kind smile and always combed black hair. He takes the time to think of what he is about to say. Michaels: And finally…the man, I consider my mentor. The man, who never had to step in the ring to prove his worth…Tim Fitzgerald. Tim was the reason, that guys like you, ever made the MWE Roster. He told us to never hold anyone down, and to believe everyone has unlimited potential… His once-emotional tone, once again seeps into a comedic mood, as he has problems imagining Devlin in the Main Event scene. He turns away from the last framed picture. Michaels: But, I don’t think he was talking to you, Devlin…also known as, the guy dumb enough to fight his brother over the Paramore chick… Michaels gets a disgusted look on his face, trying not to imagine Dev and Ryan's manager. Michaels: Listen…little man…it’s okay to feel bitter over never being above mid-card…but…don’t you DARE…come to ME, telling me I haven’t earned anything. You know how long it took me to become a Main Eventer? He simply holds up one finger, causing the cameraman Steve to try and guess how long. Steve: A year? Brian simply shakes his head, looking at Steve in disbelief. Michaels: One month. Steve ‘’oohs’’ him and throws him a thumbs up. Michaels rolls his eyes, before getting back into his ‘’promo mode’’. Michaels: When was the last time someone looked at you as a legitimate opponent? When was the last time people looked at you, as, the man? When was the last time, someone signed up to MWE, to face Devlin Sharpe? Motherfucker, when was the last time someone bought a PPV for Devlin Sharpe? He grabs a random blue hat with the words ‘’Thinking Cap’’ labeled in the middle in white. He rubs his chin and puts on a thinking posture before throwing the cap angrily to the floor. Michaels: Oh, that’s right…never. NEVER. You’ve NEVER been the man, and you NEVER…will be. I swear on my soul….you will not be at Monday Night Mayhem. Why’s that, you want to know? He grins before reaching into his pocket and taking out a piece of gum. He takes it out of its wrapper and rapidly begins to chew it, feeling the intensity of the moment and match ahead. Michaels: Because, to borrow a quote from an old friend…you were the cum that your mom should've swallowed. Brian sticks his tongue out, almost as a signal to Devlin’s mother. He pulls his tongue back in, seemingly satisfied, like she was, according to Michaels. Michaels: And as Maury would say, I AM NOT THE FATHER! He gets a little chuckle after mocking the talk show host Maury, who lets guys know if they’re the father of their girlfriend’s children or not. Michaels: Devlin, if you wanna make yourself known...I'll slap you in your FUCKING face. Michaels takes his right hand and gives himself a light slap on the cheek, portraying Devlin's possible future. Michaels: But, before I go…I want to talk about Devlin, and politics. At the sound of the word ‘’politics’’, Michaels begins to twitch. It starts in his green eyes, and now the camera and MWE fans watching recognize how much that accusation irritates Michaels. Michaels: Devlin Sharpe said…that I need to get off of…Daddy’s Lap… The term ‘’Daddy’s Lap’’ sends his whole body into overload, as he frantically begins to shake with craziness. The twitching is heard from his tone, as he tries to speak slowly. Michaels: You know…I’m not jealous of other peoples’ accomplishments…but Devlin…when you try and demean my accomplishments…by telling me that I cheated to get them…it sounds a little bitter…a little jealous…and a little typical…from such a little boy. A gritty and intense feeling is felt by even the cameraman, as Michaels walks closer and closer to the camera, wanting Devlin to hear every word. Michaels: After all the SHIT I’ve been through in my life…this is it. For someone like YOU…to challenge someone like ME…is absolutely fuckin’ CRAZY! For you…to call ME out…on cheating to get to the top? No Devlin…no. I made it to where I’m at today because I BEAT PEOPLE, THAT YOU CANNOT BEAT. I beat Sonny Williams, he crushed you! I’d MURDER Sinc Mercier, he obliterated YOU! And, despite all this evidence, that you’re nothing more than my punching bag this week…you expect me to believe that you’re a contender? He simply shakes his head, not wanting to believe Devlin's statement that he is a legit. force to be reckoned with. Michaels: Fact is Devlin…you’d probably have to pay ME to watch one of YOUR promos…I’m an emo bitch? YOU’RE the one looking for Aiden Copane’s help. YOU’RE the one who wishes you could be the ‘’old Devlin’’. Fact is Devlin, I don’t have to look back into the past…to feed on my ego. I’ve done more shit in the past three months than you’ve done in your whole fucking CAREER. He continues to hold up three fingers, almost as if to reiterate the fact that he did more in three months than Devlin has ever done. Michaels: Main-evented…check. Won a match…check. Cut a decent promo…check. Fucked a girl....check. Beat David Whitman… check. In conclusion, I wrote up a little rap for you, Dev. I actually took 15 minutes of MY time, to talk about you. So, enjoy your 15 minutes in the spotlight. He grins widely, showing his crystal-white teeth. He reaches deep into his jeans and finally pulls out a folded piece of paper. He unfolds it, and clears his throat. Let me tell yall a story about a boy named Dev He wasn’t shit, he even lost to Project. Rev He hates on me, accuses me of pol-i-tics It’s funny, everyone who accuses me of that is on my dick Why, because they ain’t got nothing else to say Watch as Dev becomes the NEXT wrestler to just fade away Face facts Dev, your life is fail And come Monday night, better ask Ryan to post bail, Because I’m about to take you down, down to IC-DUBYA Fucker, I’ll whup your ass so bad that NOT EVEN the PARAMORE chick will love ya. All of a sudden, the rage boiling inside Michaels lets out, as he suddenly picks up his leg and kicks Steve straight in the jaw with an Ace of Spades. He tilts his head to the side, and cranks his neck. He reaches down to pick up the camera, and continues to chew his gum loudly. His twitching returns now, as he looks to the camera with passion and a grin at the same time. Michaels: Devlin…EAT SHIT, AND DIE! He spits out his white gum at the camera, completely covering it. Static is heard before the screen goes completely blank.
