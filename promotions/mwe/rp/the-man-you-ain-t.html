The venue was called “Rumble Rookies” designed to put as much pain and punishment humanly possible to any and all superstars who have the guts to slate themselves on the roster. Getting his shit together was hard enough after being so used to the classy life of promoting venues such as these, but now he must endure what each athlete he helped to promote has underwent. Nothing can sway Casey’s mind once he is competing. He becomes initially IN the moment. The classic jingle of “Baby By Me” from renowned music artist 50 Cent helps guide Casey out to his opponent with the aid of cheering fans and the honking of air horns. Bradley stands almost in a trance like state feasting his eyes on Casey whilst the blood vessels within the sockets begin to pour towards his pupils. During the trials of a match between rookies, everything is on the line. Casey’s future with the MMA and his high octane fans hang in the balance of how far the kid is going, while Bradley’s obsession with being the best and fighting for his country of Brazil fuels him to go all the way. Both men have track records of being undefeated in this MMA rookie circuit, but it wasn’t until now that Casey competed against a man who was twice his size. Delgado places his mouth guard and drains all thoughts or contemplations of the outcome to this match by keeping a tuned mind on how essential victory is currently, and how rewarding the idea of moving on to a major Mixed Martial Arts company would be. Mr. Patrick wasn’t playing around, Casey witnesses his tenacious might and goes on the defensive. Several minutes into the fight and Bradley is already exhausted, Casey’s tactic is working. No successful strikes have been hit, it has become a bout of stamina and fatigue. Bradley appears to let his arrogance and self esteem get the better of him, Casey has the upper hand. Within the final moments of the match Bradley only grows more fatigued and winded, but it only takes a single one of those moments, to turn the tides. In Casey’s mind he had the win in the bag, Bradley would give in any minute now with sweat pouring from his face. The time was soon right for Casey to cut a match deciding punch. But during all of the commotion and tension within this life changing moment in time, and through the whistles and shouts of the crowd flooding around the octagon like savages, there was a separate happening only feet away. A small huddle of people surrounded an unconscious and bloodied figure, it was a male body being tended to by security personnel and a small group of worried fans. Casey becomes slightly distracted by this commotion and glues his face to the walls of the octagon to examine it. He knew who it was. His brother had been a victim of some kind of attack, and the blood that oozed from him became the result of Casey’s worst nightmare. Everything froze if only for a second, and his tears were all that could be moved. As Casey turned around with his eyes filled with a moisture of misery, one quick strike from the paw of Bradley Patrick struck him dead on. Camera flashes seemed to shine across the glint of Bradley’s glove as Casey tumbled to the ground in a blackened spiral. Like the falling of dead rose petals the act of crashing and colliding with a physical impact can only bring suffering and torment to that which was alive and full of life. Soon after the referee makes the winning count, Casey was done. He blacks out and fails to obtain a contract, or a rallying cheer, or even the infamous hand raising gesture winners of any athletic ability love so much. And through everything that had occurred…..he could only think of his brother’s safety. When he came to his senses what happened to his brother meant everything to him.
