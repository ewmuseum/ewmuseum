What am I doing wrong?; Currently formatting, don't judge yet.

Oct 23 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Scene opens inside the Juniata golf course in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania. Alejandro Rodriguez is seen walking along the fairway, he is wearing a blue polo shirt, white golf trousers, white golf shoes and a black mask. Walking alongside him is Darrel Jones, wearing an open necked shirt, black trousers and white golf shoes. Walking behind them are their two caddies. The pair reach Darrel’s ball, which is slightly behind Alejandro’s. Darrel stands up to take his shot first. Alejandro: The doctor said golf would calm me down, how the fuck can I calm down, you’ve won the past four holes. Darrel: Did I ever tell you I was golf champion at my university? Alejandro: You’ve told me three times in the last half-an-hour, just play your shot. Darrel swings and hit’s a good shot 150 yards which just slips onto the green. He steps back and gives the club back to his caddy. Alejandro: Nice shot. They walk forward another ten yards where Alejandro’s ball is, Alejandro is given a club and he steps forward to his ball, Alejandro swings a practise swing. Alejandro: So, have you gotten me a meeting with Covell? Darrel: Alejandro, I’m not your manager. Alejandro ignores this and he hits his ball, he hit’s a great shot down the fairway and it lands about seven metres away from the hole. Darrel whistles and the pair walk down towards the green, chatting more. Alejandro: I’ve been meaning to talk to you about that, I don’t really need an interviewer every week, and I have you every day, and rather than having two jerk-offs follow me around every day, I’d much rather have one, you‘ll be getting paid top dollar, more than MWE staff losers anyway, whaddya say? Darrel doesn’t quite know how to respond to this, the pair continue walking and they reach Darrel‘s ball first, Darrel turns around to face Alejandro. Darrel: I suppose I have nothing better to do, ok, I suppose I agree. Darrel is given his putter by his caddy, Darrel lines up the shot, getting right down to check the slope of the green. Darrel straightens back up and takes aim, it is a hard shot to make, about 17 yards from the hole. Darrel takes the shot, it rolls along the ground at a fast pace, it curves inwards towards the hole and it drops in. Darrel: For what it’s worth, I did get you the meeting with Covell. Alejandro curses loudly and walks over to his ball, which is nearer the hole than Darrel‘s was. Alejandro: So where did you play golf at university? Darrel: Right here, Al… Right here. Alejandro hits his shot, it goes about a foot past the hole and rolls into the bunker. Alejandro: FUCK! Alejandro throws his club into the tree’s and walks off the green, with Darrel following suit trying to keep a straight face, Alejandro turns round and glares at him. Darrel: I’m not laughing! Alejandro: Shut the fuck up, when is the meeting? Darrel: Tomorrow, just before your promo. Alejandro: Alright, lets go, I’ve had enough of this shit. Scene fades to black. Scene opens at the Wachovia Centre, it is a lovely day, it is around mid-day and the sun is in full flow, shining down on . Alejandro exits his car wearing a black shirt, jeans and a black mask. Exiting with him is Darrel, who is wearing a black suit and tie. They walk towards the back door entrance of the centre. They walk through it and walk down a hallway crowded with workers making last minute checks on microphones and cameras. Darrel stops in his tracks. Alejandro: What the fuck are you waiting for? Darrel: Damn… is that Lindsey? Alejandro turns around. About 20 yards away from them is Lindsey, wearing a white tank top, ripped jeans and a baseball cap which does nothing to hide her cheek, which has swollen out and turned red. Alejandro turns to Darrel and keeps his voice low. Alejandro: Jesus… Did D do that? Darrel: I have no idea… Should we say something? Alejandro checks his watch and shakes his head. Alejandro: No, we need to get going, if we’re late you’re the one who’ll have a swollen cheek. They turn left down another corridor and then right again, they finally reach a door which says on it “Chairman’s Office”, Alejandro knocks and waits. Darrel: Do you want me to wait outside? Alejandro: No, I want you to come in, but I’ll do the talking, ok. A muffled voice is heard from the other side of the door saying “Come in!”, Alejandro opens the door and walks inside, the office is a medium sized room, MWE posters and all replica titles are hung up on the wall. Covell closes his laptop and indicates the two seats in front of him for Darrel and Alejandro. They sit down in the seats and Covell leans back in his chair. Covell: So, gentlemen, to what do I owe the pleasure? Alejandro remains in silence for a while. Alejandro: You know, I have an easier time getting a face to face with Osama Bin Laden… Covell: I’ve been very busy… Alejandro: Signing a guy who attempted to kill your son must take up a lot of time. Darrel talks before Covell gets his temper up. Darrel: Alejandro wants to talk about his future in the MWE. Covell looks directly at Darrel. Covell: So why the hell are you here? Alejandro: He’s my new manager. Covell smirks a bit but does not laugh. Covell: What would you like to say? Alejandro takes a pause before speaking. Alejandro: In the past month, I have defeated the world champion in a non-title match and I have destroyed Jekyl, enough so to take him out the MWE. So how the hell do you work out this US Title match at Wrestle Wars, how the hell do you put me in this match with a rookie punk and a washed out dickhead with less charisma than an orange? I should be champion, I should be in the Main Event, tell me what I have done to be in mid-card, TELL ME what I am doing wrong, tell me why Sonny Williams is getting away with barely beating jobbers every week when I destroyed him and Jekyl in a matter of weeks, are you proud to have a paper champ? All I want is an explanation, so give me one. Covell pauses a second… Covell: Alejandro, do you mind if I had a quick word with your manager? I’m sure you’ve got to get ready for the house show anyway. Alejandro, as if waiting for a lame answer, stands up immediately, pushes his chair over Alejandro: Sure. He storms out of the room and slams the door shut. 30 minutes later. Scene opens inside Alejandro’s locker room, he is dressed in his ring attire except for an A-Rod shirt. There is a knock on the door, Darrel walks into the room, now looking a little tired. Alejandro is just fixing his laces up and he looks up towards Darrel. Alejandro stands up straight… He half-raises his hands. Alejandro: Well? Darrel: Your promo is next. Alejandro: Lets walk and talk then. They exit the locker room and walk down a hallway. Alejandro: Do I look ok? Darrel: Uh, yeah, fine… Anyway, Covell was banging on about how I needed to control your temper and how you’re getting too big for your boots, but he did say you had a point and he would look into it… Alejandro: Blah.. Blah.. Blah. Same old, same old, heard that kind of shit all my life. Have no fear Darrel, we’ll get back on top. I mean, how many asses do I have to kick to get a title shot round here? Alejandro laughs and grabs a bottle of water from the table, opens it and drains it in one, they reach the curtain and shakes himself off. Alejandro: Showtime. “Eye of the Storm” by Bullet for My Valentine plays on the other side of the curtain, Alejandro steps through it and receives a chorus of boo’s from the crowd. Alejandro laughs and beams, he walks down the ring at a fast pace and he taunts the crowd, aggravating them even before he starts talking. He walks around the ring and grabs a microphone from Fred Carry, he rolls back into the ring and stands in the centre. He waits for the crowd to calm down and then he begins to speak. Alejandro: Everyone here in this arena, everyone who watches MWE every single week worldwide, everyone everywhere knows that I’m on the best form of my life. I’ve beaten the World Champion Sonny Williams and I’ve taken out the man who I swore to get rid of, Jekyl. And what do I have to show for it? I’m in a United States title match with two of the most over-rated stars in this business, one is a rookie punk with no respect and one is a high-maintenance ungrateful and washed out imbecile and the worst champion apart from Sonny Williams. Alejandro begins pacing the ring as the fans boo his remarks at his opponents. Alejandro: And what have I done, to deserve this position? I should be in the Main Event, not David Whitman, what has he done to deserve that spot? Beaten Jekyl, hell, I took Jekyl OUT of the MWE! He hasn’t beaten Sonny Williams in the new MWE, a feat I achieved just a few weeks ago. David Whitman has had his time, and now it’s his time to step aside and stop hanging off Covell‘s arm, just because Whitman is Covell’s second-favourite poster boy does not give him rights to have title shots every four weeks. Alejandro takes a few moments to think in another pause, he stops pacing momentarily and continues again as the crowd boo impatiently. Alejandro: If there’s something that I hate more than anything, it’s people that make MWE look bad, right now, Sonny Williams is making MWE look bad, he’s making himself look like an idiot. He’s barely walked out alive from wrestling two absolutely unknowns back-to-back and he‘s lost to me. The man hasn’t defended his title against anyone formidable since he’s won it. He’s a paper champion, he was before, and he is now, he’s disgracing that title, disgracing the MWE and disgracing himself. If he comes out on top, all credit to him, but he knows deep, deep down that I deserve a title shot more than David Whitman, more than anyone. The crowd boo their disagreement and Alejandro turns around 360 degrees as if staring out every single member of the crowd. Alejandro: All this drama and under-treatment, has deterred me from what I should look to be achieving now, as much as I hate America, I’ve held the United States Championship twice before, nothing gives me pleasure in holding it, if some of you remember, I threw it in a trash can and burned it the first time I held it. As I said, I don’t enjoy holding it, but I suppose if it keeps the title away from the claws of Mason Resoan and Sinc Mercier. The crowd begin to boo. Alejandro: Oh what? I’m not allowed to save the MWE once again? I’ve had it up to here with people talking about Sinc Mercier, wearing Sinc Mercier shirts, holding up signs, both of those people, Sinc and his mother, are extremely annoying. The amount of shit that comes out of that man’s mouth, it‘s dangerous, and the worst part is that he is actually believing his own hype. Get a grip, dude, you’re not the greatest thing since sliced bread, infact, I wouldn’t say you’re that great at all. The crowd do not back up Mercier, but they continue to boo Alejandro anyway. Al keeps patrolling the ring in his particular style while speaking. Alejandro: And now that he has accomplished something, even if it’s not much, you bet he’s going to be cockier, more obnoxious and more egotistical than ever before, not something I‘m looking forward to. So I truly am going to take pleasure in beating him down, taking his title and putting it’s prestige back where it should be, where I originally put it, you see, Sinc Mercier has dragged that title down into the mud. But I’m not under-estimating him, he is a good wrestler, knows how to defend himself and get some offence in, but if he thinks he can beat this, then he’s kidding himself even more than he has done. Alejandro looks out into the crowd and points to a row of the audience who all hold letters which spell the name “M A S O N R E S O A N”, the crowd see this and begin to cheer. Alejandro: Mason Resoan, a man who’s made a lot of difference in the MWE, not necessarily for the good, but still, a lot of difference. After the entire MWE original period I don’t think I’ve ever faced that man One on One… Does anyone here remember when Mason was World Champion? When he was tearing it up with Aiden Copane in Canadian Xchange? No, neither do I… That’s the thing about Mason Resoan, whatever he was, whatever he became, he was always one thing… Completely forgettable. The crowd boo Alejandro but Alejandro laughs and then raises his arms for them to be quiet. Alejandro: What? Oh come on people… You can’t be serious… You’re telling me, that if Ed Ferrera and Ryan Adams hadn’t have said anything about Mason that night when he debuted in the new MWE, damn, I‘ve already forgotten that date, you’d know exactly who he is? Exactly! You wouldn’t know jack about him. Which reminds me, seeing as that I ain’t got that great a memory and because Mason is so average, I don‘t know jack about him either, so Darrel put some notes together to remind me about him. Alejandro pulls some pieces of paper out of his pocket. Alejandro: One, bunch of bananas… Two, boxes of Fruit Loops… uh.. hold on, this… is my shopping list. Alejandro throws the list onto the floor and pulls out some more notes. Alejandro: Aha, this seems a lot better. God damn Darrel your handwriting is shit. Mason Raisin.. uh.. Resin… no.. Razing, Resoan! Ok, Mason Resoan is a former… uh… seriously I can’t read this.. Ah, who gives a damn. You see Mason Resoan, I don’t need to read shitty notes off a piece of paper, I already know what you are, you’re nobody, nothing, a has-bin… infact, you never has bin anything, you‘re the only MWE Original that everyone forgot, who has achieved very little and done nothing since, you‘ve been hanging round, taking credit for running over Covell‘s douche bag son and winning the MWE Title in one of the worst times it has ever faced.. Alejandro throws the notes on the floor as the boo’s and chants start coming down again from the crowd, whom Alejandro ignores, Al leans on the ropes and talks directly into a camera. Alejandro: My opponents are jokes, and I shall make a mockery out of them on Sunday, because I can and because Resoan and Mercier make MWE what it should not be, classic face and classic heel, one kissing ass and getting the cheap pops and one loud-mouthing with his playground insults, have some diversity bitches. But one thing, both of you could learn something this Sunday, but it ain’t something you’ll want to experience and it will not be fun, but Mason, this will be a moment to remember. Because Sinc Mercier and Mason Resoan, in time you will… Some of the crowd chant along as Alejandro says his punch-line. Alejandro: LEARN, TO, FLY! The crowd boo as Alejandro gets onto the top turnbuckle and holds the mic to his mouth, Sinc Mercier style. Alejandro: And if you didn’t know… now ya know! Alejandro laughs and jumps from the turnbuckle as “Eye of the Storm” by Bullet for my Valentine plays on the loudspeakers, Alejandro faces all sides of the crowd and takes a dramatic sweeping bow to the audience. Alejandro exit’s the ring and walks backstage with the crowd still booing and throwing insults at him. Scene fades to black.
