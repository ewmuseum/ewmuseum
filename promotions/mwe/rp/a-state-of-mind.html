A State of Mind; David Whitman RP

Aug 23 2008

David Whitman

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The camera slowly opens up on David Whitman as we see him leaning against a railing. He’s wearing a black shirt that has “THERE IS NO SPOON!” written on it in plain white letters, and long black pants. The left side of his forehead is adorned by a big bandage, and his face is sprinkled with numerous stitches. David looks around nonchalantly for a few seconds before turning his attention to the camera. David: Oh, HI! Didn’t see you there. We hear a voice coming from behind the camera shouting “yes you did!” David: Shut up, James! David taps the camera, which sways lightly before focusing back on the now moving David. David: ANYWAY, as I was saying, I’m David Whitman. And for those who don’t know me, this is a little intro vid just for you. David passes his hand over the camera lens and the scene transitions into the inside of a bar. James (from behind the camera): That was soooo cheesy. David: Shut UP! Anyways, as you can see, we’re in a bar. BUT! This is not just any bar! We see a hand appear in front of the camera doing a thumbs-up motion. David slaps it away. David: James if you’re gonna be like that, why are you manning the camera in the first place?! James: You know what, you’re ABSOLUTELY right! The camera turns to static for about half a second, and it opens back up on the exact same spot but now James is standing next to David and has a beer in his hand. David (whispering to James): dude, we don’t even know that guy whyd you let him use our camera James: RELAX! Any customer of the Top Rope Tavern in bound to be an amazing human being! David sighs. David: Anyways, as you can hear, James loves this bar. That’s because he owns it. That’s right, my cousin James Whitman owns the Top Rope Tavern. But here’s something even MORE interesting; I created, write, direct, AND star in a show based on this bar! James: That’s right! I’m in it too! David: It’s called Top Rope Tavern, named after the bar itself of course, and right now we’re shooting the 7th episode. I recently started talking to people over at MWE to see if we can strike a deal to show TRT on MWE.com… but that’s all up in the air right now. James: Up in the air? Who SAYS that?! David: ANYWAY, moving on… Right. Wh- AAAAH! David grabs a hold of his head and falls to his knees. James: What th- James signs to the cameraman to shut off the camera as he tends to David. The camera turns to static and stays that way for a few seconds before coming back. This time, we see David and James sitting at a dinner table eating what appears to be a traditional thanksgiving meal. There are a couple of other unknown people there; it appears to be a typical family of a mother, father, daughter and son. The camera is shaking a bit, until David finishes his bite of turkey and shoots the camera a menacing look. The camera is now perfectly still. David: So yeah, where was I? James: You uh… well I mean, you were talking about Jekyl and Sonny, and then you told the uh- Hey, what’s your name anyway? James look in the direction of the camera, and everyone does the same. A shaky voice from behind the camera says something, in a raspy whisper. James: Did- did he say Bud? Right, well then, you told Bud to turn on the camera. David smiles at James, and pats him on the shoulder. Then he starts talking, and focuses on everyone a bit, as if he were talking to everyone there except the camera. David: Hey, everyone, everyone, listen, I’ve got to face Jekyl AND Sonny this week, right? At Monday Night Mayhem, this week in Michigan! What?! INSANE. I know, right? So here’s what I’m going to do. David takes his fork to another piece of turkey, holds it up in front of him and looks at it for a while. David: I’m going to get those mother*ckers. He eats the piece of turkey, looks at the mother, and then the father, smiling. David eats some of his mashed potatoes, but everyone else is just looking at their plate. David looks around at everyone. David: Oh come on, you guys. Let’s TALK! WE’RE A FAMILY! Girl: We don’t know y- David: I SAID WE’RE FAMILY! James: Dude, calm down man- let’s uh, talk about your match. David: Right. Monday Night Mayhem indeed. You know, when I got the pin on Alejandro, all I could think was “Thank GOD this is over!” I was ecstatic. Not just about keeping my title, no, but I was genuinely happy that the match was over. I didn’t think I could take ONE MORE MINUTE in there. Alejandro took me to the limit. I was relieved I got out of it alive. David smiles and goes to put some more turkey into his mouth, but he abruptly drops the fork holding the turkey onto his plate. David: BUT NO! We all know what happened then, right? My ol’ buddy Sonny came out and kicked my @$$, and opened up my head even more with those f*cking brass knuckles! James: Calm down, man. David: Oh, I’m calm. David quiets down for a while, before spontaneously getting up out of his chair. He starts pacing around the room. David: But THEN! THEN! Then this- this, this CLOWN- David walks over to the little boy and pats him on the head. David: And yes, son, I mean LITERALLY. Like Bozo. Boy: Who? David: You HAVE heard of Bozo the Clown?! The father strikes his son with an urgent look. Boy: Y- Yes! David: Oh, thank God. David starts pacing again. David: So yeah, this CLOWN attacks me too. For NO REASON! HAS THIS WORLD GONE MAD?! David slams his hands hard on the table, causing much of the food to jump around and land on different people. David suddenly sits down, silently. He puts his head in his hand. James walks over slowly, and taps him on the shoulder. James: Hey, buddy? You okay there, buddy? Hmmm? David: Sonny Williams. Sonny is the last person on earth I would expect this from. We used to be in the Mayhem Mafia together, you know. James looks confused. James: Uh, yeah, dude, I know. David: We used to have a lot of fun. Sitting around, laughing and joking around, freestyle raps… He was in our movie, too, James, remember??? James: Dude, yeah, I remember. I wrote half of that movie. David looks around at the family members. David: Any of you seen the movie? Bulletproof JEST? They all shake their heads “no”. David: Man, it was fun. He was funny, there, too. He played himself. Not a lot of people can pull that off. Everyone looks confused. David: Sonny… man. This is so weird. I’m going to have to kick his @$$ and beat some sense into him. He’s a good man, James. He’s just forgotten what this is all about. It’s about- It’s about- Wait, what the f**k was I just talking about? Dad: It’s about. David: Right, it’s about the fans. It’s about honor. About loyalty, respect- James: Hustle? David: What? No, that just makes it sound super gay. James: Right. But can you SEE me? David nods his head while looking serious, even though it seems obvious that he has no idea what James is talking about. David: Yes. Yes I can. But I can’t see Sonny. I have no idea where he is. Where he’s gone. The man who took me out with those brass knuckles- That wasn’t my long-time friend. And I’m not just saying that because he attacked me. The old Sonny would never have stooped so low as to attack someone from behind. But apparently, the old Sonny is gone. Well, I’m not gonna rest until I find my old friend and bring him back. James sits back down. David takes a sip of his water, as everyone else does the same, nervously. David: And until then, I’m going to have to- James: Yeah, yeah, kick his @$$, we got it. You ready to leave now? David: Yeah. W- David shakes his head. David: Where the hell are we? The camera turns to static before opening back up in a white, clean room. It appears to be a hospital room. We see David asleep in a bed, surrounded by Eddie, Scott and Thomas. They’re all looking at David. All of a sudden, James enters the room. Scott: What’d he say?? James: The doctor says the beatdown David got at the hands of Jekyll and Sonny, especially after his match with Alejandro, apparently caused a rare form of Mild Traumatic Brain Injury. The doc said he’ll probably get some short bouts of temporary insanity, but they should stop after a couple of days. Thomas: Wait, but… Should he wrestle? James: Well, I mean, even if he stops going nuts in time for his match, I don’t think he should face those two again, and especially in a handicap match. But try telling him that. Thomas: We actually SHOULD try telling him. James: Dude, NO. They all stare at David for a while. Thomas: What the hell is UP with that Jekyl character, anyway?! James: I have no idea, man. I don’t wanna get all cliché and call him an insane freak, but- Eddie: He reminds me of some crazy comic book villain who’s hell-bent on taking over the world. Scott: No way, dude, he doesn’t seem interested in power. Thomas: First off, there’s no WAY he even REMOTELY resembles a comic book character. Eddie: He COULD resemble a comic book character that’s been taken to the big screen and changed completely. Like, you seen Fantastic Four? Silver Surfer? COMPLETELY different. Scott: Naw dude, I was thinking more of Ba- James: WHAT THE F*CK, MAN! Why the hell are we even TALKING about this?! These people broke my cousin’s brain, dude! Let’s just forget about that backstabber and that clown, and focus on keeping David in here and getting him healthy. Scott: … but what if he isn’t ready for his match when the time comes? James: I dunno, we’ll deal with that problem when we get to it. In the meantime, let’s try to heal his mind. Everyone looks at each other for a while. Eddie: Uh, how do we do THAT? James: Man, I have NO idea; I just stuffed a bunch of sleeping pills in his drink when he wasn’t looking. They all shrug. All of a sudden, James turns to the camera. James: Wait, WHAT?! Have you been recording all this time?! Bud (from behind the camera): He told me t- The camera starts moving quickly. James: HEY! GET BACK HERE! We see the floor as the “cameraman” is running away. Eddie (from afar): TACKLE!!!!!!!!!!!! We see a big blur of images as the camera hits the ground and then turns to static.
