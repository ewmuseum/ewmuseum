*The MWE have come to terms over the release of James Sync. We would like to wish James all the best in his future endeavours.* A supernova (plural: supernovae or supernovas) is a stellar explosion. They are extremely luminous and cause a burst of radiation that often briefly outshines an entire galaxy before fading from view over several weeks or months. During this short interval, a supernova can radiate as much energy as the Sun could emit over its life span.[1] The explosion expels much or all of a star's material[2] at a velocity of up to a tenth the speed of light, driving a shock wave[3] into the surrounding interstellar medium. There are hundreds of twinkling stars in what was a peaceful and serene view. The camera drops to show the ruins of a castle in the distance. As the camera closes in, there is a human shape, perched on top of the tower. The picture begins to stutter and a deep, commanding voice is heard over the images. Suddenly images of James Sync are flashing quickly. They start with his debut in MWE but quickly move on to his most recent match, his final in the company where he was destroyed by the newcomer, JJ Executioner. Against a giant at 6’11’’ and 377lbs, Sync was easily and quickly squashed. The final images are him being choked, slammed and then dragged out of the ring by JJ and a noose wrapped around his neck. This last picture is the enduring image and fades slowly. “What makes you think that you are capable of avoiding a similar fate?” The scene is now on top of the tower. JJ stands there, with his back to the camera, looking at the vast expanse of sky and my soulcountryside before him. He is holding the noose rope in two hands. “Too many victims, not enough time. Not enough pain to go around. At Turmoil, I destroyed a man’s career.” An image of Sync unconscious in JJ’s rope is shown as the giant dragged him out of the arena. “AND I LIKED IT!” He laughs, although it sounds like a menacing cackle. “A beast has woken in the cesspit that is my soul. And like a cancer, I have come to ravage the very heart of the MWE.” “You will not survive to know your potential or how bright your star can shine. You are going to be swallowed up by a black hole of pure hate and anger.” “Vega Knight, the Supernova, you will see why JJ Executioner is going to the very top of the MWE. You will also feel the pain of your eyes popping out of your head, your pulse racing out of control due to the sheer dread of not knowing when you will have your final breath, courtesy of the Hangmans’ Noose.” “And when the Death Scythe falls, your time is up and you will join Sync down there. ” For the first time, JJ turns towards the camera. He points down to the courtyard, where a lone body is hung from a noose, swaying in the breeze. *OOC - Too short again for my liking but I feel this is a step again in the right direction with this character. Hit me with your comments please fellas.*
