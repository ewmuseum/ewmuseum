Debut Of A Champion; Milenko VS Sonny Williams (MWE Title)

Oct 8 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The camera turns on and we see a Milenko sitting in a dark corner of the Mellon Arena. he is wearing his usual backstage attire which consists of Calf length black shorts and a black trench coat. You can't see him very well in the shadows but he starts talking in a whisper anyway. Milenko Some of you know me and some of you don't, but all of you know my brothers, Alex & David Bowen. What you don't know is the fact that the 2 of them are cowards. Milenko leans out of the shadows so you can see his head. The look in his eyes. can only be called murderous. He takes a deep breath and starts to talk again, Milenko The truth is Alex and David are only my Half-Brothers. Our mother was raped by some scumbag and I was born 9 months later. What was really screwed up is that my father always blamed me. I was beaten by my Alcoholic Step-Father almost everyday and locked in a cubby hole under the stairs when ever he didn't want to look at me for days on end sometimes for as long as a week with no food or water. I ran away when I was 13 and never looked back. He leans back in the shadows but you can still see what appears to be a maniacal glint in his eyes when he starts to talk again. Milenko After I left my Step-Father beat my mother and Alex walked in during the beating and punched him right in the jaw and got sent away. David also ran away and spent his teenage years living in the sewers until Alex found him and brought him out. He never even bothered looking for me. The glint in Milenko's eyes gets even more crazy as he talks in a normal voice. Milenko I followed Alex around ever since he got out. When he became a pro wrestler and joined WZCW I also joined. I wanted to make him pay for not helping me when we were children. I waited and made him as miserable as I could until finally chocked him out and beat him in a Juggalo Death Match at Kingdom Come. I have not and will not forgive him for what happened to me but i have bigger goals now and those goals are beating Sonny Williams and winning the MWE World Title. I knew B.G. Mancini wouldn't be able to get the job done so i went to Richard Covell and demanded that I be put there in his place. Milenko smiles a sadistic smile as he mentions Sonny Williams and the MWE World title. The glint in his eyes is still there and still crazy. Milenko Sonny the 2 of us have faced each other before in EW. The difference is that I'm better now than I've ever been. You've gotten scared since you lost to Alejandro Rodriguez. You won it in a grueling match against both David Whitman & Jekyll. Then when you lost in a non-title match you were afraid that you would lose in a title match so you started this Invitational you got going on and you started with Supernova. I watched that match and I knew if a newcomer to this business could come that close to beating you then I know without a doubt in my mind that I can beat you and I will win that title you hold so dear. the smile lessens slightly as the glint in his eyes turns to a burning fire and his voice gets deadly serious. Milenko Sonny if I'm willing to almost kill my brother what do you think I would do to you to win the MWE World Title? Simple, I would do anything to beat you. I've been fighting to survive my entire life just to survive and i don't expect that to change anytime soon. When I hold the MWE World Title I will inflict the same pain I've dealt with my entire life on all of my opponents. Milenko grabs the camera and pulls it right to his face. The fire in his eyes gets even brighter and his voice gets even more serious. Milenko Sonny hold on to that title and shine it up real nice after Monday Night Mayhem it going to be mine. Milenko throws the camera away from him and gets up and walks away. As the camera fades to black a picture of the MWE World Title is shown.
