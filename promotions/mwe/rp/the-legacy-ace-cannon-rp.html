The Legacy Ace Cannon RP; Posting what he sent me for him

Dec 5 2009

Ace Cannon

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
((OOC Note: This is not Cannon's best by far, he was meant to make a much better and longer RP but due to being busy with work and stuff I have to post this for him, his rough draft RP)) ** The camera opens on a newly returned Ace Cannon who is leaning against a black Cadillac Escalade as he stands in the hot Los Angeles sun. He is dressed in a black Silver Star Casting t-shirts that has two silver guns on the front, he also has a pair of Ed Hardy jeans on and Oakley’s adorning his face. He chats away on his Blackberry for a few minutes before acknowledging the camera. He finally finishes his call and tucks his phone into the pocket of his jeans, he then looks away from the camera for a few moments before talking.** Cannon: I am back once again… I mean, me and DC’s herpes have two things in common. The obvious being that we have both been pressed against Lindsey’s oh so sweet pu**y lips and the second being we both always go away, but we manage a comeback and that comeback is always more intense than the other. ** A sickly smile comes across Cannons lips.** Cannon: Now get in the car, I got shit to do. **Cannon turns around and opens the Escalade’s door and gets in the driver side. The camera follows and gets in next to Cannon. The camera gives a profile shot of Cannon’s right side and was can see some of the wear and tear on his face over all the years of wrestling and stress from the business clearly taking it’s tool. Cannon starts the car and puts it in drive. The Escalade slowly begins to roll as Cannon begins to speak.** Cannon: This last year has been crazy for me. I came back last year at Wrestle Wars and made a big impact by destroying the tag teams of Darius and Boyd and the Ortan Brothers. I made my presence known, and I was back with a fire that had never burned so strong before. Time wore on and I had vicious and bloody battles with Joseph Sharpmen. They were a series of matches that made there mark on MWE. Joseph and myself than realized we were killing each other for all the ugly fans and we soon joined forces and started the Vanguard. **Cannon continues driving and hops on the 405 and begins driving at what appears to be a relatively fast speed. Yet he continues to talk with no regard to the traffic around him as he begins changing lanes and driving rather recklessly.** Cannon: The Vanguard was poised to be the biggest and best stable in MWE’s long and illustrious history. Joseph was out there kicking major ass, and I was out there beating people such as Brian Michaels and David Whitman. Paige was with us and it was like old times. Hell, I even got a threesome with Lindsey and Paige at one point! I was on top of the fucking world, and then it all came crumbling down and it came faster than I expected. **Cannon takes an exit on the interstate and takes a deep breath before continuing.** Cannon: It all came down when I was only two weeks away from my first world title shot in MWE. After all blood, sweat and tears over the years I was finally getting my due….. But I made mistake. A BIG mistake.. ** Cannon shakes his head and continues driving. He doesn’t say anything for what seems to be five minutes or more. The Escalade comes to a stop and Cannon puts it in park. He just looks at the building for a few second and than continues.** Cannon: This is where the mistake happened. This is the Immigration building I brought all the Mexicans to that day in my promo for my match with Alejandro Rodriguez. When it happened, I thought it was funny. I thought it was the most entertaining promo I had ever done and was ready for the world to see it. Yet, when they did it wasn’t the same feeling I had. Instead people were pissed and I was in a shit load of trouble…. ** Cannon just sits back in his seat and looks at the building again. He shakes his head in disapproval over his actions of that day.** Cannon: The fact I got in so much trouble made me say “WOW”. Hell, in my career I had kicked children in the head, kicked pregnant women in the stomach and done countless of other horrible things and never got in trouble but this time it was different. I had crossed the line and it started my downfall. I was suspended for a month and had my title shot taken away from me! What I had worked some hard for was taken and people were predicting me to win. I was about to cement my legacy in MWE…. But it didn’t work out that way. ** Cannon reaches in his center console and pulls out a pack of cigarettes. He extracts one of them and puts it between his lips. He than grabs a lighter, lights it, and takes a couple drags before talking again.** Cannon: I came back from my suspension, apologized to the locker room and told them was a changed man, but I lied. I jumped back into what was left with Vanguard and me and Joseph kept plugging along. Paige dumped me and I was upset with that, and what I had lost professionally. Than to compound all of it my buttons were being pushed in the locker room and I lashed out. ** Cannon takes a couple of long drags off his cigarette and starts the Escalade back up and begins driving again.** Cannon: I lashed out at someone who had been fucking with my head for a long time. Someone who was my “brother”, someone who was a multiple time world champion, and the wrestling business greatest politician. I speak of none other than….. **Cannon stops just short of saying the name. He takes a few drags of the cigarette and than pushes it out the window.** Cannon: I refuse to say this persons name, but many of you know who he is. Anyway, I lashed out at him and gave him a piece of my mind. When I did that, I got canned and banned from any MWE event. It finally came tumbling down faster than a house made of playing cards. I sat around my house as a broken man, I know longer had what I loved and it killed me. I finally got back in the saddle and discussed starting another company with George Prax, but that fell through. Than I was going to a company called Wrestle Headquaters, but I didn’t feel it there and that fell through as well. So I went back home and just sat around and started reflecting upon my life and I came to a conclusion.. ** Cannon stops the car once again and we are seen in front of big building. A building that is probably ten or so stories high and a lot of people are milling about the street. Cannon turns and looks at the camera. He lefts his glasses of his face and puts them a top his head a an evil smile comes across his face.** Cannon: That conclusion is…. I DON’T REGRET SHIT! Did I intentionally do a racist promo? Nope, but it added to my legacy. Do I regret calling out Brian Michaels? Not a fucking chance. He deserved and people saw through his bullshit in due time and I’m back and he’s not. Now the question is why am I back? Well, MWE is having some issues. The rating are down and the people in charge rather be listening to Lady Gaga instead of building this company up. The talent here fucking sucks and none of the new guys understand the legacy of MWE. Then the veterans who understand the legacy are just letting these clowns shit on it and it all makes me SICK. ** Cannon looks at the camera with a disgusted look on his face. One that is full of contempt and disgust.** Cannon: So I am here to restore ratings and hopefully fire people up with my controversy and that’s why I kicked Nate and Eli in the head! They are spineless bastards just like everyone on this roster. I will be the spine in the land of the spineless and if you can’t handle that than change the fucking channel because I am back and more fucking pissed than ever. ** Cannon shuts the car off and gets out. He walks over to the sidewalk and stands in front of the building he just pulled in front of. The camera focuses on Cannon who just stand there as people walk past him on the sidewalk.** Cannon: Since MWE is in trouble I am at a place that can cure it….. I’m at the California Regional Cancer Center. If you can’t read between the lines that means I think MWE is full of cancer and I will be the cure. **Cannon walks into the center and the camera follows. He walks to a bank of elevators and the camera follows. Cannon hit’s a button and the elevator begins to go up. The elevator stops and the camera pans up and it says LABORATORY. Cannon continues walking and enters a room. He stands between multiple shelves…** Cannon: Look at these shelves…. It’s the MWE locker room. **The camera pans around the brightly lit room. Shelves are covered what appear to be nothing but samples of cancer. It is an eerie sight an almost sickening sight.** Cannon: In case you haven’t figured it out, this is cancer. It’s what’s killing MWE and starting at Wrestle Wars, I will begin my mission to cut the cancer out of MWE. The first cancer to extract goes by the names of James Preston… ** Cannon picks up a jar and examines it closely. He just stares at the cancerous tumor he holds in his hands and begins to talk…** Cannon: James Preston, the Image of Perfection. I read a lot about you, and how your this hot and upcoming rookie in the business. I also read about your background, and if I do say so myself you remind me a lot of a young Ace Cannon. You grew up rich, got what you wanted, made good grades and thought you were the most perfect motherfucker to ever walk this green earth. **Cannon sets the jar back on the shelf in its rightful place.** Cannon: Well James, your not perfect. Nobody is and that is the attitude that is killing MWE. You walk in here and think you’re the fucking shit…. I got news for you, YOUR NOT. You’re an unproven sack of English shit. You have done nothing in MWE and if I went on the street and asked an MWE fan who you were I bet they’d reply with… “James Preston… Can’t say I heard over him. Maybe he is in New Moon?” Jimmy, call me chemotherapy because you’re the first bit of cancer I plan to get rid of. **Cannon runs his hand through his short hair ad he looks around the room. He looks at all the sickness around him. A creepy, sadistic and sick smile comes across his face.** Cannon: Jesus Cruz, about the time I got kicked out on my ass you were coming in. So I never got to see you wrestle. Now, I don’t think I missed anything because you’re a fad. You come in MWE with all these little swift kicks, flying knees, armbars, kimura locks and all this other MMA bullshit. You come here because you think pro wrestling is a joke and will expose us with all your little Bruce Lee moves…. ** Cannon stops and shakes his head….** Cannon: Fuck, if I wanted to I could buy a Tae Bo dvd and learn all the same shit you do. You don’t impress. Professional wrestling has been around a whole lot longer than your mixed martial arts bullshit. We are a proven entity and you’re a FAD. Your attitude of thinking pro wrestling is a joke illustrates my point of MWE being full of little pricks who don’t appreciate the legacy. Cruz, I will make you appreciate pro wrestling and I will make you understand that there is a legacy here to be followed. So step up and be ready to be humiliated and if I were you come with some real wrestling moves… Leave the Jackie Chan shit at the door. **Cannon walks down the line of shelves and comes to the biggest jar of cancer. He stands there and looks at it. He then looks at the camera and than back at the jar. He nods his head in approval.** Cannon: Here is the biggest sample of cancer in this room. This sample symbolizes something to me… It symbolizes GWC. MWE has been infiltrated with these guys from GWC…. These failures from GWC. They came to MWE after there company folded and talked about how good it was and tried to press their ideals onto MWE and slowly they took form and at that same time MWE began to falter. ** Cannon stops and just takes a deep breath.** Cannon: They came here and tired to ruin the legacy of MWE… Well, I’m lucky to havw in my match The Stargazer a former GWC wrestler. A former GWC heavyweight champion! I relish this opportunity to crush him in this match. I relish the opportunity to show all the little GWC cancerous fuckers that MWE is nothing to be taken lightly, and we have an undeniable legacy here and I will make an example of that when I destroy Meatgazer. Now Meatgazer, I want to tell you something right now and I want you to listen closely. So turn up your TV and put down the back issue of GWC magazine and get in the present because this is relevant to you. ** Cannon pauses so he knows out in TV land he has Stargazers attention.** Cannon: Now that I have your attention, if you knew what was good for you than you wouldn’t show up at Wrestle Wars. You need to stay home because I have my eyes set more on you than the other two clowns of this match. I want to expose you to the world and show them that the Kevin Hardaways and Zak Warners on GWC have nothing and I mean nothing on Ace Cannon! So Star, if you do show up you won’t leave that ring walking…. Instead you will leave rolling out on a stretcher. ** Cannon begins to laugh a little.** Cannon: Stargazer the only thing that can save you now is wishing upon a shooting star that Ace Cannon doesn’t kill you. **Cannon stops and rubs his chin for a second.** Cannon: James, Jesus, and Stargazer I hope your ready for December 6th. It will be a day of redemption for me and the day that I put MWE back in the right direction by cutting out the first piece of cancer. A new day will begin, and that new day will be called the Cannon Era. **Cannon grabs a jar off the shelf and opens it. He slowly reaches his hand in the jar and pulls out a sample of cancer. He looks at it for a second with no emotion on his face. He slowly lifts it to his mouth and puts it in! After a few chews he swallows it and his face still looks blank with emotion.** Cannon: I am crazier than ever…. And just remember. I’M YOUR NIGHTMARE! **The camera fades to black has Cannon stares at the camera with a look of sheer and utter craziness.**
