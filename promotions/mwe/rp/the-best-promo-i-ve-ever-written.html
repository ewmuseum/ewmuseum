The best promo I've ever written.

Aug 16 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
OOC: The best (and shortest) promo I've ever typed up. For those looking for tips on how to write an intense promo...look no further. *An MWE camera zooms in on a dark room, showing all its features. A soft chuckle is then heard as the camera slowly turns to show Brian Michaels rocking back and forth on a rocking chair with a light bulb over his head. Michaels’ eyes are red, and wet from tears. He squints his eyes at the camera, shaking his head slowly in the process. He has a scruffier beard than usual, with his hair tied back. He is wearing boots and old-school jeans. He looks down, pondering his thoughts. He slowly looks up as the camera fully shows Michaels’ eyes.* Michaels: Apparently…the life I lived…is a lie. Sonny Williams subconsciously turned this ‘’Liar, Liar’’ thing…on me. He called me, and I am quoting him…a rich, skinny white boy. So now along with fitting wrestling stereotypes…Sonny Williams has become a living, breathing stereotype. Sonny Williams is the kind of guy…who says something and does something else. *Michaels begins to rock on the rocking chair, putting his hands together. He looks up, licking his lips in the process.* Michaels: See to Sonny…I grew up in a rich complex apartment in New York. Apparently, I didn’t even live in Brooklyn or Queens or something of that nature. No, I was born in Manhattan to him. I went to a prep school, with prep friends. *Michaels bounces his head as if this is all typical.* Michaels: According to Sonny, I was living in a condo by the time I left high school and had my parents pay for all my wrestling classes. I was elected valedictorian in High School and Most Likely to Succeed in…yada, yada. *Brian’s facial features start showing a little impatience as he counts to five with his fingers, before speaking again.* Michaels: After being Most Likely to Succeed in everything, I decided I wanted to become a wrestler. Of course, being spoon-fed everything in my life, my parents paid for a years’ worth of wrestling classes. Anything to make their baby boy happy… *Michaels begins to fidget around in the chair, trying not to snap violently before taking several deep breaths, calming himself down.* Michaels: So I took wrestling matches…and my dad paid off the promoter…and I miraculously…won three World Championships because of money and lies. *Michaels grabs the camera in his right hand and picks up his left hand slowly, bringing it near his left eye.* Michaels: The lies…the bullshit….is gone. It’s done. *Brian puts on a menacing and gruffy voice, looking around violently.* Michaels: Where are YOU, Sonny?! Hmm?! If you’re so fucking pissed off at the world, come HIT ME. Hit ME, motherfucker, HIT ME! *Michaels takes deeps breathes, as he looks up with a devilish grin.* Michaels: Sonny…your words…are just words. Ever heard of the catchphrase…actions…speak louder than words? *Michaels then switches the camera over to his left hand, leaving his right hand open. He then takes the right hand and repeatedly punches himself in the right eye, opening up a huge gash. Michaels leans forward toward the camera, again squinting his eyes.* Michaels: Sonny Williams…your intensity level is nowhere near mine. I am focused soley on this match. I will give the fans everything I can BECAUSE…I am Brian Michaels, and I don’t back down from some washed-up former World Champion who doesn’t think I can take a PUNCH. *Michaels sets the camera down on the floor, slightly slanting it upwards. He sits down back in the chair and leans back, rocking it. He rolls his eyes into the back of his head, giving a slight smile.* Michaels: But, but, but Sonny...I can take a punch. I can take...a lot...of punches. *Michaels smirks to himself sinisterly.* Michaels: And therefore…we are onto Sonny Williams’ supposed story. The guy who…according to him…lived on the streets. He faced depression…crime….murder…drugs…sex…lies…rap music…having a job…no money to go to college. Where have I heard THIS, before? *Michaels sarcastically puts a finger on his chin, pondering for several seconds at the roof, before leveling back down, staring at the camera with a dead eye.* Michaels: You said you faced depression? Ever heard of pills? *Michaels pretends to pop pills into his mouth.* Michaels: Crime, murder, drugs? That’s a lifestyle. Don’t come to me crying like a whiny motherfucker because YOU couldn’t get a scholarship. *Brian pretends to write something on a piece of paper.* Michaels: All this…was self-induced. No one FORCED you to do any of this…and you’re bitchin’ about your childhood? You think you had it BAD? Okay. Sonny Williams…if you thought that your childhood was bad…I don’t even want to think about the scars you’re going to have after this match. Cuts that can’t be removed with bandages or stitches. *Michaels shakes his head, closing his eyes in the process, imagining Sonny’s cut up face.* Michaels: These…will be scars that will haunt you. From now…until forever. You and your mind games…you and your ‘’please feel sorry for me, I was abused a child’’ bullshit won’t cut it. You lived a tough childhood…don’t complain. It makes you sound like an even BIGGER son of a bitch. *Michaels shakes his head and grabs the camera again, putting it right next to his now bruised eyes.* Michaels: THIS…is the price I pay for being a wrestler. THIS is the pain I feel after every match and you have the audacity to tell me that I haven’t been through shit? Sonny Williams, you are the typical example of a man who overrates himself. You ARE a hypocrite because you come on, talking about your outrageously tough childhood….yet you’re too important to stay in a motel? *Michaels again shakes his head, disgusted at Sonny’s actions.* Michaels: You mean that you as a kid…would beg and beg for MCDONALDS’…and now you’re too good for a DENNY‘S? You’ve forgotten yourself… *Michaels stands up, stretching out his tight jeans.* Michaels: I mean hell, Sonny…your ego is the size of my dick… *Michaels peers down at his pants, smiling.* Michaels: And it looks pretty big. *Michaels shrugs, and again sits down, leaning back.* Michaels: But that’s not the point. The point is Sonny Williams…I am sick of your motherfuckin’ complaining… Michaels: On this motherfuckin’ E-Fed… Michaels: Talking about your motherfuckin’ childhood… Michaels: Telling me that I hired a motherfuckin’ actress to play your hot mom… Michaels: With your motherfuckin’ whore of a girlfriend… *Michaels slightly chuckles, as he puts his right hand on his chin to suggest he is thinking.* Michaels; I’m not going to sugarcoat this Williams…I’m going to break you down mentally and physically… *Michaels turns out the light bulb and stops rocking in the chair.* Michaels: MOTHERFUCKER! *The camera goes to static as Michaels ends the promo chuckling maniacally.*
