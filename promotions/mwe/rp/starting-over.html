Starting Over; And more...

Nov 11 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Prologue: NOT A Good Start... Wow, just--wow...here I was, believing I was going to come into my first match in MWE with a victory...only to lose to some guy who looks like he needs all of his rabid shots and his favorite chew toy. Not gonna lie, I kind of dropped the ball that night. The return of MWE...had me excited, and I just...DROPPED everything, the one time I could make a decent impression and I blow it, JUST like that. Somehow I'm gonna have to make it up to D.C. and the reps, on the other hand I need to get my ass in gear and start turnin' some heads quick...or my career here is over. Well that's enough of the wake-up call! Let me try and introduce myself the right way. Most of you have seen me in ESPN, the rest of you...well get a fucking T.V. The name is Stephen...Stephen Lenton. I'm what you call...a late bloomer so to speak. I didn't start this because I was just some shithead who couldn't make it to the pros (I'm looking at you Tarzan...) I came here, because I am INDEED a fan of professional wrestling. So I took the opportunity to come to this company...and they accepted me. SURE, I fucked up last time, but this time...I think I've got a pretty legit chance at winning this. That's if these two don't focus on me, and decide to beat the shit out of each other. Then my chances of winning are REALLY high. I'm not afraid to say that I'm looking forward to beating them both down. I don't shed a tear over it, I'm not going to suck them off about their abilities...leave that to someone who thinks kissing ass is going to get them a championship shot. No, why I'm here is to prove that I am THE BEST...in this company. There's no need for a gimmick for me. I don't need to act like I'm sort of prophet sent down from God himself to cure wrestling of it's "disease". No thanks. I'm here to prove to the world that I am the best there is...and that shouldn't be that hard...not to me at least. So let's be real here, come the time to throw down...YEAH...I'm goin' to be prepared to cave some fucking skulls in. There can be...two of them...three four...they can be blue, red, green, black, white--I don't give a flying tit. Come Monday night, this guy...right here...? Is going to set the standard of wrestling. For those of you who don't believe me, WELL...fuck you. For those of you who want to enjoy the ride, buckle up, kick your feet up and watch me run through two guys, I don't give a flyin' fuck about. Enjoy it while you can boys and girls... That's All I Gotta Say. I was going to try my best...I was goin' to go out there, try and turn a few faces upside down, and probably get a few praises. That wasn't the case. Somehow I wished I was still playing football, I mean you can't beat the rush you get when you're out on the field. People screaming your name, you being apart of a well oiled machine...one unit. That's what being a Virginia Tech Hokie...being able to bond with your brothers in arm...and cracking some fucking skulls. Once that ball was kicked, it was like you got a mental boner...it was the most pumped experience you'd EVER feel. Look at me...I'm literally having a wet dream over this shit...let's talk about wrestling. Stephen Lenton: HIKE...HIKE!!! Or not...three wideouts...an easy touchdown pass--fuck it...I'll just run it. Another friendly game of football with the boys from the neighborhood. That big dark bastard was James...he was the "artist" of our little group. Went to Old Dominion University and majored in engineering. He was a country boy...came from North Carolina, wrestled pigs and all that shit. Should have seen him when he played Rugby...he hits hard. Tried to recruit his ass for football, but he said it was a little too "tamed" for him. James: STEVE, I'M OPENED!! Stephen Lenton: Stop standin' in one spot Darkness!!! James rolled his eyes and ran another route, while I stared down Aaron...my little brother. He wasn't even little, he was two inches taller than me and weighed enough to tilt the planet. He was still college...he goes to Virginia University, he's their starting Nose Tackle. I tried to shake him, running away from him as best as I could... Aaron: Come on Steve, stop bein' a little bitch! Stephen Lenton: Oh I'm not--UMPH--runnin' because I'm scared of you...I'm--mmph--runnin' because you might eat me. Aaron: FUCK YOU MAN! That stopped his big ass in his tracks...now. Stephen Lenton: REESE!! Aaron: Aw shit, YO AUG, MO' IS OPEN!!! Maurice "Roderick" Mason...I call'em Roderick...everyone else calls him Reese. He's a little shorter than me, but he was built like an athlete...just like me. Maurice got a scholarship to play football at West Virginia, he majored in sociology. Since he was a dick, he usually got a reaction from everyone...go figure he chooses the profession where he can say something stupid and get people to act out about it. I threw the ball DEEP down the field, where Reese was standing. He caught it and ran all the way to the touchdown. He did some stupid victory dance and taunted Aaron's team... Reese: Shoulda never left me deep! Aaron: Yeah well it won't be happenin' again. Reese: Yeah right... Stephen Lenton: Hey Aaron, wanna know what'll make you run faster to catch Reese...? I'll tie a sausage to the his back and you can chase'em! Everyone broke into a laughin' fit, Aaron sarcastically laughed and then frowned. His friend Augustine caught up with us. Stephen Lenton: Let the record show...you young cats don't know a damn thing about football! HA! James: Good game fellas...good game, even though ya'll sucked, but good game. Aaron: Man James you ain't even do nothing, but run around and look lost. James: That's call strategy m'brotha! Stephen Lenton: Strategy woulda cost us the game... These guys had been around me since I was a kid, man. They never stirred me wrong, they didn't try and screw me over...these guys WERE my family. With them around, we were like a pack...couldn't be separated. Even when we all went to college, even when I left Aaron to deal with the headache of mom and dad and their nagging...we didn't stay apart for long. I mean shit...they helped me with this scandal B.S. The news wanted to make me out as dirty...like I was unclean, which was totally false. BUT...that was for another time. I picked up the football and twirled it around in my hand, walking with the group towards our cars.... Reese: Yo Steve, that was a hell of a pass... Stephen Lenton: Damn, you make it seem like I lost my touch or something. I haven't been out of the game for that long... Reese: Yeah, I know...I'm just sayin'...I wish you were still playin'. I gave a half smile and shrug my shoulders, I opened up the trunk of my SUV and sat down on the edge, opening up my bag and taking my cleats off. I put them down inside of the bag and look at Roderick. Stephen Lenton: What are you gonna do...y'know? I can't fight what they say, and I knew this was going to happen sooner or later the news got out. I mean I wanna play, I really do, but I got a new job. James: Tusslin' with sweaty dudes. Stephen Lenton: Why must you make everything sound so homo...? James: Everything is homo, you ain't searchin' hard enough. I shook my head. Stephen Lenton: Look, I'm absolutely happy with the decision I made with this wrestling thing. I mean it was better than sitting around the house getting big like Aaron. I needed to DO something...and if I didn't go it quick I would be stuck in that lifestyle. Now I have a job...a good one...one that involves putting foot in asses and walking them dry. So I'm good. Aaron: Fuck all that...you shoulda taken your suspension and been a man about it. Now I gotta make the paper in the family by gettin' drafted! Stephen Lenton: Please, you jump on the hard count, no team's gonna want some big ass who flinches all the time. Aaron: Hey, don't try and play me, I'm on the verge of being an All-American man! Stephen Lenton: Oh you're ALL of somethin' alright. Yo go home and tell mom and dad I'll be over in a bit. Aaron: Shut up, you don't tell me what to do...! He was always a dumbass, I took the football and threw it right at his big ass head. He rubbed it and looked back at me, I waved at him with a sarcastic smile and then give a straight face. Aaron: Okay, okay, I'm goin'...damn! C'mon Aug. Much better...I turn to Reese and James. Reese: How long you staying? Stephen Lenton: Tomorrow morning...I think. Reese: Damn man, I wish you could stay longer, we ain't spoke in awhile... James came out of nowhere and put an arm around me and Roderick. James: Know what this means boys...STRIP CLUB. Always with the strip club...this dude spent more money on seein' tits than he did payin' for his books in college. After a test...? Strip club. Out of school early? Strip club. Just had sex...?! Strip club. I don't know WHY he thought I liked going, but I just went with it. I couldn't lie, the girls in that place looked pretty decent, if only the thought of them having a C-section didn't turn me off. James: Come on Steve... Stephen Lenton: Look, I don't want to party tonight, I wanna spend some time with the family...talk y'know...? James: Titties ain't never hurt nobody... I looked at Roderick. Reese: He's got a point...I like tits personally, what about you? Great, a majority vote, I wish Aaron were around right now. On second thought....it would he unanimous. I just gave up and threw my hands up, shaking my head some at the idea. Stephen Lenton: Fine, fine, fine I'll go...but you guys gotta have me back before nine-- James: WOOOOO, TITTAYHS!!!! James did some kind of victory lap and gave me some skin. I couldn't help but crack up, James was a funny dude...that's what was cool about'em. So we hopped in our cars, and went out separate ways, and it came to me I had a 3-way dance coming up on Monday night. Maybe going to the strip club wasn't a bad idea...it'd take some stress off of me. ... Who the fuck am I kiddin' I'm going to be stressed regardless of the situation. I see it like this: One of us is going to have to lose, and I sure as fuck don't plan on losing. Maybe it was that stubborn football player inside of me, kicking at the right side of my brain, but I didn't care. Monday was my night...the only night I needed to stand out. On My Way To Kicking Some Ass. My flight's come on time and it's about time you all get to see MR.ENTERTAINMENT in action on Monday Night where I take on ONE newcomer (At least I think he is) Zack Lifer and a MWE ORIGINAL, the O.G. of Mayhem Wrestling Entertainment...Eric Derf! Boy oh boy, have they thrown me a curve ball in this match. See for some reason, I had a knot in my stomach. I had this queasy feeling...like I was going to puke. All of that was fixed today when I had a pair of D-cups rubbed in my face and watched a girl bend her back until her head was hitting her ass cheeks. So I calmed myself, I got some sleep, and I woke up and made my way to San Francisco, California where the sun is bright and the hipsters FLOCK...ready and willing to take on anyone they put in my way. So they put me up against an original...and some new guy...who's as FRESH as I am...but look at THIS...he's been around the block a few times and he's got a few TRICKS up his sleeve. Good...that's real good, I like tricks...it's my favorite cereal when I wake up on Saturday mornings. See I'm not going to take a lot of shit, because people can REALLY take offense to that and get anal. They don't want me to...go on and on about some practical shit, because then they can call me generic. I just want them to realize that if they step into that ring and believe for a moment I'm gonna let them do to me what King Kong did to me weeks ago...think again gents! Oh NO, I'm not going to allow myself to fall in such a deep ass hole. I'm going to fight my way into the light one more time, and if you try and drag me down I'm going to kick you away from me. This is my time...no one is going to take it away. I don't care if you're a veteran here, Eric Derf; I could care LESS if you've been in this company longer than I have. I mean you can go on...and on...and on...and on...about my history. People do that when they face a true athlete. But y'know what...? It's predictable, you're going to have to do something to impress these people. They wanna see ERIC DERF, beat down two no-named fucks who joined your company. I know Lifer won't let you stomp on him like some little bitch, so I guess I should take the same approach. THOUGH, I'm going to stomp on you BOTH...it really doesn't matter at this point now does it? The time has come for me to stop talking, to stop spewin' so much bullshit...and to step into the ring, where the fans will cheer for me and wish me luck. I'm going to feed off of that and use it as motivation. Competition is my Gatorade. Period. Fade.Static.End.
