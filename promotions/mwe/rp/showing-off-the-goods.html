Showing Off the Goods

Aug 15 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
*Mayhem Wrestling Entertainment cameras fade on as they roll up onto a huge gym. It is quite a marvelous sight, however looks somewhat familiar to the camera man. They continue to investigate for about a minute, all the while showing off the looks of this gym. The camera man walks in and sees some pictures on the walls. Some really fit men and on top of all them, former MWE World Heavyweight Champion, Mason Resoan. The camera men paused for a moment, and looked around, with everything becoming seemingly more familiar, but decided to once again shake off his idea. He continued to move the camera back and forth down the title floor. Counters were made out of marble, weights were everywhere, and the exercise machines were clearly top of the line. Finally, after about a half of minute of wandering, MWE cameras found two men at the end, and most deluxe part of the gym.* ???: Are you serious, do you honestly expect to win your match this week if you keep giving up every two seconds. See that is the difference between you and Mason, see...Mason kept going. He never stopped, he pushed himself to the next level every time he hit the gym. Why do you think he was a former tag-team champion. He carried the team through the lows and the highs. And why do you think he became MWE World Heavyweight Champion? It was because he was damn good, and he could walk any walk that MWE through at him. Yeah, he talked a lot of shit, but he could back it up. James...You need to keep pushing yourself. DON'T STOP! James Sullivan: Stephen, come on, honestly, you try being in my shoes. You try training this hard. Stephen: Sullivan, shut the fuck up. I took you under my wing just as I did Mason, and just like Mason, I am going to train you, give you advice, and stick with you until the end. I have gone through this before, I paid my dues, and I don't have to do it again. I am a former four time eXo World Heavyweight Champion for crying out loud, so I do not want to here you bitch and try to tell me to put myself in your shoes. I've been there and I have done that. *Stephen sighs and backs off for a little bit. He takes a look at Sullivan and realizes the potential, the power, but also notices the youth and lack of experience. He chuckles to himself a little bit, and calms down a little bit.* Stephen: You are young, you have know idea yet. The same was with Mason, but over time...he learned. As will you, as long as you train and listen to me. If you were facing me this week, you would get your ass kicked. I saw straight through your little interview this week. You are nervous, I can tell. Naturally, I can not blame you, but I will not let this continue. I picked you, out of all the other possible students, and I will not have you fail me. Mason trained until he puked. He trained until he was so tired he passes out on the weight bench. And with this training, he won the world title, and he was able to brag, boast, and laugh. You, at this point, do not have that privilege. Sullivan: So what do you propose I do? Stephen: From this point on, I will not propose anything. I demand and order you to do what I want. If I tell you to eat spam and lint rolled in a tampon, you better do it. If I tell you to attempt a 550 pound bench-press with no spot, you better do it. And if I tell you to train, like you have never...ever have trained before...well, there will be some horrible consequences if you do not. I am going to go now. You have two hours of free time, and when I get back I am going to whip you into shape! *Stephen Arthur throws a towel in his face, which slides to Sullivan's lap. Sullivan looks angry, but throws his anger away and looks at the towel, as a door is heard slamming in the distance. The camera man, finally switches his position to the front of Sullivan. James, finally gets angry one more time and does 20 250 lbs. bench presses in a little over a minute. He sets the bar down and sits up. Sweat is pouring, as he wipes himself down. He looks around, spots the camera, and puts himself in a calm mind-frame.* J.S: This week, I face Xavier Stone in a match, as you should already know, that will let me know whether I will make it big or lose it all. Lately, I have been a little to cocky. Not training, eating the wrong foods, and not getting enough sleep. No, this is not a Pay-Per-View, no this match is not for a title, but it is my debut, and I will not let my mentor down. Stephen Arthur is a great man, and out of 400+ people, he chose me to be his next "project." He has been teaching me many useful tricks, and in return, I have been slacking off. Now I realize how stupid my actions have been, and I have to apologize to everyone. In my mind, if I disrespect my mentor, I am slapping my fans in the face, and that is not my intentions in the MWE. My intentions, like I stated earlier this week, are to come in this Monday Night, on Mayhem, and give Xavier Stone the challenge of his career. *James Sullivan gets up off his weight bench, walks a to a oak door, and turns the knob to enter a gigantic room.* J.S: While challenges are fine and dandy, I don't plan on just pushing Xavier, I plan on winning, because starting today, I am going to push myself harder than ever before. I see the wrongs of my ways, and I am going to stop them before they have a chance to get going. Xavier, I am telling you right now, there is no way...in hell...I will ever let you beat me. The United States championship will becoming around my waist, and your just another bump in the road. *Sullivan apparently owns a mansion, and the camera man is observing his fine home as Sullivan walks upstairs.* J.S: I have noticed, since you arrived to my home and gym, that you have admired my possessions. And if you have not already guessed, this used to be Mason's old mansion. Some of the bet promos were cut here, but I could care less. After what Stephen Arthur just told me, this mansion could be burned to the ground for all I care. I am focused, I have a mission, and I am going to make sure I return from this mission, successful. *James Sullivan laughs as he thinks about Monday Night Mayhem. He also thinks about some other future opponents as well.* J.S: But, while we are at it, let's take a look at some of the people I may have to face next week. Starting with, Aiden Copane. Convenient how we we just talking about Mason Resoan, huh? Let's see, former X-Television Champion, um, former United States champion, former MWE World Heavyweight Champion, and former MWE Tag Team champion, and the first one at that. Well, let's see, your resume is good on paper, but it's been a while hasn't it? Do you think you can still live up to what you used to be. Your bad self. Your multiple championship reigns aren't just a thing of the past right? But, let's take a look. Mason Resoan and yourself have some history, am I right. And correct me if I am wrong, but Mason was trained by Stephen Arthur, the same man training me. I watch old tapes, and don't deny the facts. If Mason could beat you, then why wouldn't I be able to? I mean Mason beat you for the world title. So I can beat you for the U.S. title. And when you were in your tag team, I think there was a reason you guys did so well. Oh yeah, Mason Resoan! Mason always had you beat, and you knew, and if you beat Jekly, then next week, I will show you that I am just as good as Mason...if not better. J.S: Speaking of Jekly, I have to say, he creeps me out. Mass murder, always causing mayhem, and always making a statement. Pretty impressive, but your act will be no good against me. Believe me when I say this, I am not easily intimidated, and just like Xavier, you will just be another stepping stone to my United States Championship reign. My fans, will be behind me 100% when I go to face you one on one for a chance to get that much closer to the title and let me tell you this, if you didn't already know, I do not disappoint my fans. I have a special J.S.B for you, and you are not going to like it. *James Sullivan motions for the belt as he makes his way back to the gym.* J.S: Because when you show no fear... Stephen: You can't be scared, ya, ya, ya...cut the shit your training starts now! Think fast... *Stephen throws a 25 pound weight ball at him to start with. The cameras stay on until they get into the gym, where a minute of training is shown. the screen then fades to black.* ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Again, I know it was short, it is just really hard to adjust to a new gimmick like this. For anyone who has never been a face before, it is hard as hell. Anyway, I hoped you liked it, and hopefull, it didn't sound heely at all.
