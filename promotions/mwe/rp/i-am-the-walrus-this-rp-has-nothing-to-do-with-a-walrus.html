I am the Walrus! (This RP has nothing to do with a Walrus); Derf VS Oblivion

Oct 9 2009
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Dispatcher 911, What is your emergency? Eric Derf I'd like to report a kidnapping. Dispatcher Ok, Please hold. The line suddenly goes quiet as it's on hold. A few moments go by, nothing. Eric Derf What the hell? The system sucks. That was the voice of Eric Derf, as we can now see it's he that is on the phone. He's sitting in his now familiar living room, laying on the loveseat. He's dressed quite casually, jeans, t-shirt, no shoes. For someone who is on the line with 911, his facial expression seems quite calm. Across from Eric is his friend Josh Parker, sitting upright on the couch. Dressed in light brown shorts and a tucked in wife beater, he goes to "yawn" and while doing so, strikes a pose, for no reason at all. However, it does catch the eye of Eric, who's still waiting on the phone. Eric Derf Showoff. Josh Parker Yeah... You only wish you looked so good. Josh is referring to his amazingly chiseled body, one that could have belonged to a god in a past life. He lays back against the back of the couch. Josh Parker Just think Eric. In a few days, I'll have been steroid free for three months, and I feel bigger and stronger then ever! I feel I could do anything! Hell, I wrestled a show the other night down at the gym, and I lifted old Stan Johnly right above my head! Erics eyes get quite wide eyed for a moment. Eric Derf Stan "The Man" Johnly? Isn't he like over 300 pounds? Josh nods in agreement, a cocky smirk crawling across his face. Josh Parker Yep. And I lifted him up, held him above my body, and dropped him... And then the fucker rolled me up. He won the match with a fucking roll up! Eric snickers for a moment, then makes an annoyed "ugh!" sound. Eric Derf Holy crap, do these guys ever take care of emergencies? Josh Parker Do you really think calling the cops on Oblivion is going to help any? You don't even know where he's got that girl held at, other then it's a hotel somewhere in America, which don't help your cause much more considering we're sitting in the east coast of Canada. I don't think this is the best way to go about your attacks on the guy. Eric hesitates for a moment, thinking about what Josh just said. He knows it is true, he has no idea where this girl is being held at. There really isn't much he can do, and like a referee in the wrestling business, the rest of the world just happen to be looking the other way when something important happens. So, he decides to hang up the cellphone. He tosses it onto the coffee table that sits between himself and Josh, and lets out a "sigh". It would have been an interesting way to start off a promo against his next opponent, but what are ya going to do. Josh Parker So, that's out the window... Any other ideas on how you can attack him? Eric Derf Oh, I've got plenty. But, you don't just blurt out random insults at your opponent. I mean, I could go and say something like "What kind of freak wears a mask everywhere?" But that's just generic, plus, I'm sure he'd have some smartass rebuttal to it. Actually, I know he would. I've been studying him for a few weeks. This IS our third match after all. Josh begins to nod, and that nodding quickly becomes jamming out as his cellphone begins pumping out one of hiss favorite songs. Josh nods along for a moment or two before Eric cuts in. Eric Derf You going to answer your phone? Josh snaps out of the daze quite abruptly and grabs his phone, shooting Eric a dirty, yet goofy look. Josh Parker Hello? Josh Parker Yes, this is he. Josh Parker Oh! Hello Mr Silver! The name "Silver" caught Erics attention, as he's now on the edge of his seat, trying to overhear what Josh is talking about. Is it by any chance that the "Mr Silver" on the phone is none other then Eli Silver of MWE? And if so, why would he be calling Josh, as opposed to Eric? As Eric sits there confused trying to figure this stuff out, he barely noticed that Josh got up and went into a different room. Not to be overly nosey, Eric leans back against the couch. He looks around the room, bored. He just stares out into space, eyeing his belongings. His 37" LCTV (which, if you've been paying attention to my roleplays, you will know is always a different size lol), the window that reveals a very tall building outside, the sign on the building that says in bright letters "HAPPY THANKSGIVING" (because we in Canada are a month before you), a liquor cabinet on a far wall, a picture of Eric Derf holding his first ever XTV Championship, with the title itself hanging above said picture. He also notices his current prize, the Money in the Bank briefcase. It's just resting against a wall, next to the empty duffel bag he would use to go from show to show. As he glares around the room trying to help his bored mind, he decides maybe it's time to cut his promo. And he knows that somehow, even without a camera around, someone will see it. Like on shows like Survivor, there is always someone watching you, somehow. Eric Derf So, I've got a match against the man I've already beaten twice in multi man action, Oblivion. There's really not much I would have to say right now, you know, since I beat him twice. They say the third time is a charm. Well I would have to agree with that, but not for his sake. Because this is the time I finally beat the masked freak in one on one action. The guy has lost every single match he's ever had, hold one match, in which he faced a man that shouldn't have even been in the ring in his condition. Oblivion, there isn't alot I can say about you. You're a loser, always have been a loser, and always will be. Come this Monday, when we enter that ring, one of us will leave victorious, and the other will fail. And believe me, I won't fail. Bank on- Josh Parker DUDE! Mid promo, Josh barges back into the room, cell phone in hand, smiling ear to ear. It looks like he just got the best news of his life, which shouldn't be a surprise once you continue reading what he just got off the phone for. Josh Parker That was Eli Silver from MWE, they saw some of my work, they offered me a contract! They want me to fly to the headquarters ASAP to sign with them! Eric Derf Dude! That's awesome! Eric is excited for his friend, but not as excited as Josh himself has become. And when Josh mentioned ASAP, he wasn't kidding, because before you knew it, the two of them were sitting on a plane heading to... Wherever the hell MWE headquarters are. Really, does anyone actually know the answer to that, or am I just dumb? Anyway, like I said. Now they're on a plane, and despite being uber excited earlier, Josh is now... sleeping. Next to him is Eric Derf, MP3 player in hand, headphones in ears. It's dark outside, almost everyone on the plane is asleep. Besides Eric, a few other people have MP3 players or books, something to keep them occupied. One guy has a laptop out, who knows what he's doing. Maybe playing Solitaire, maybe working on a business document, maybe he's writing an e-fed roleplay. Who knows? A stewardess begins walking up and down the isle, wheeling along a cart of refreshments. Eric notices this and hits a button on his MP3 player, one would assume the "pause" button, as he yanks the headphones off his head and quietly waves down the stewardess. Stewardess May I help you Mr Derf? Eric wasn't surprised she knew him. Sure, he wasn't flying first class, but he is a celebrity after all, his face gets noticed. Eric Derf Yes Ms... He waits a moment for the girl to reveal her name, but she doesn't. Maybe she's half asleep as well. Eric Derf Anyway, I was just wondering. What are your rules on masks? Stewardess I beg your pardon? Masks? Eric Derf Yes, if I wanted to put on a mask right now and just casually go about my business, would I be aloud to? Stewardess Umm... I don't believe so. I think that would be a security problem myself. Eric smirks and nods in agreement. Eric Derf That's what I though. That's all I wanted... Oh, and a Pepsi. Eric leans back and puts the headphones back on as the stewardess pours the can of Pepsi into a Styrofoam cup, and hands it to Eric. Eric mouths the words "thank you" as he takes a sip and leans back into his chair. Anyone that doesn't understand the point Eric just tried to make should go back and read "Of Masks and faces", a Roleplay by Oblivion. You'll get the obvious shot. The plane continues on, people continue to sleep, wake up, get drinks, go to the bathroom, all that fun stuff. Nothing overly exciting happens till just after sunrise, which is when the plane finally lands at "Whatever airport is closest to MWE Headquarters". Eric and Josh gather their luggage, make it through the crowd, which is made harder by the people that recognize Eric getting in his way. Finally, they make it outside and hail a cab. The cab ride is about a half hour, quite an exciting half hour as well. It turns out the cab driver was in attendance at Crowning Glory, so that gave everyone something to talk about, but nothing of any real importance came of this conversation, which is why I'm not going to bore you with the details of it. When the cab finally arrived at the headquarters, Eric and Josh got out and simply stood there in front of the building for a moment, taking in the feeling. Eric Derf Wow... I've been with MWE for almost a year now, and this is my first time here. This building is- Josh Parker Kinda boring looking from the outside. Josh interrupts Eric with a bland voice, which causes Eric to let out a hardy laugh. Eric has since changed his clothes for his first visit to headquarters, he's actually wearing a beige suit with a half unbuttoned shirt. Josh however never changed at all, still wearing the light brown shorts and muscle shirt. Eric Derf Dude, you should have dressed nice. What are you thinking? Josh just looks on towards the building, confident in what he's about to speak. Josh Parker Eric, look at the current MWE roster. You've got some serious talent. I mean, great talent. But NO ONE in MWE right now is as big and strong as I am. I go in there dressed in a wife beater, sure it shows I don't care for dress code, but it also shows off the body of a god that I have. Besides, are they gonna really be that pissed at me for what I'm wearing? (Obvious CM Punk pun right there) Eric Derf I guess so, but for your sake, you'd better hope you're as big as you think you are. The two continue talking, not about anything important though. They begin walking towards the building, into the front doors, and right up to the receptionist desk. The girl behind the counter looks quite blonde and bubbly, you're stereotypical secretary. Secretary Hello, may I help you? Josh Parker Yes, I have an appointment with Eli Silver. The names Josh Parker. The secretary pops her red colored bubble gum in her mouth as she begins to fiddle with her computer. After a moment, she looks towards Josh. Secretary Yeah, umm, just go through that elevator right there to your left, go to the 7th floor, and someone will be there to meet you. Josh Parker Thanks. Eric and Josh begin to go towards the elevator when a familiar voice calls out. ????? Eric?!?! Both guys turn around to see where the voice came from, and while Josh is confused, Eric is white as a ghost. He knows exactly who calls him, he can see her standing right there behind the secretary. Eric Derf Katie? What are you doing here! The secretary quietly gets up from her seat and walks towards a back door as Katie continues to stand behind the desk, her face glowing with excitement that she's looking at Eric. Katie I got a job here, I'm a new secretary! I'm just starting my shift right now, good timing! Eric Derf Oh yeah, what do you know!... But, you're timing isn't that great, I was just going upstairs with my friend Josh Parker here... Eric turns to point at Josh, but notices he's not there. He turns around to see Josh standing in the doorway of the elevator, laughing at Erics misfortune, all while the door closes, making Eric feel like he's trapped, despite being OUTSIDE the elevator. But, he's not a complete dickhead, so he decides maybe it's time to finally confront Katie once and for all. By the way, for those of you that don't know this "Katie" character, she's an older, chunkier woman who is seemingly obsessed with Eric Derf. He's never really taken the time to tell her they're not what she thinks they are though. Now you're pretty much up to speed. Anyway, since Josh is gone, the lobby is pretty much empty, just Katie behind the desk, and Eric halfway between the desk and the elevator. And oddly enough, a camera crew set up right there in the lobby, maybe it was already used for someone else? But ignoring that for the moment, Eric approaches Katie. Eric Derf So... You're working for MWE now I see. Katie Yeah, it's a great job, I get to meet all kinds of nice people, all you big strong wrestler types, it's great! Eric Derf I bet it is... I'm assuming because of your new job, you also have access to everyones phone numbers, am I right? Katie I sure do! Thank god as well, you never know when I'm going to have to get ahold of you for something! Eric knows this all too well. Katie had been texting and phoning just about everyone lately, just in hopes of getting ahold of Eric. As Eric thinks about this new development in the soap opera that is his life, an idea pops into his head. Eric Derf Say Katie... Maybe I can get you to do me a favor? Katie ANYTHING! Katie jumps up out of her seat when Eric asks this of him, the fat under her shirt giggling when she does. Ugh. And to think, Eric had drunken sex with this woman! She's like double his weight AND age! Bet that put a bad picture in your head! Anyway... Eric Derf You can get anyones phone numbers... Can you find me DCs num- Katie 555 -**** (Gotta keep it censored so you other people don't stalk him) Eric Derf Wow, that was quick! Katie I was going to give it to you next time I found you, I figured you could use it as ammo, you know, since you're trying to get that match against him... And I'm sure when you do, you'll kick his ass... Eric Derf You're damn right I'm gonna kick his ass... Eric turns his back for a moment as he begins using his cell phone to send DC a text message. One way or another, Eric is going to get that match he wants, and if he has to annoy DC just to get it, he will. He finally finishes typing it before reading it out loud. Eric Derf "DC, this is Eric. I want that rematch, and I will eventually have my way, so we might as well get to talking so we can figure this out. Otherwise, you'll see me at ringside again, and again, and again..." Katie Hahaha That's really awesome! Eric Derf Umm... Yeah... Eric is embarrassed for himself at the over excited Katie as she comes around her desk, Eric not noticing as he's still got his back turned. Too bad for him, as she wraps her arms around him from behind, as if it were the hug she'd been wanting her whole life. Eric Derf What the- Eric quickly pulls away from her, startled by her sudden closeness. Katie looks at him concerned for a moment, wondering why he pulled away. But Eric knows it's now or never to finally confront her about his feelings. Eric Derf Look Katie... I don't think we're going to be able to work out. Sure, we had a really good night, but we were also really drunk, and- Katie You were really drunk. I was only drunk on love. That one statement was really just too creepy for Eric to handle. He tried being a nice guy, but it wasn't working, so now he's going to have to be mean. Eric Derf Ok Katie, it's simple. You're old enough to be my mother, and I'm sorry, I don't like you that way. You and me, it's not going to work. So, stop calling me, stop harassing me, and just leave me to live my life! Despite such nastiness, Eric only had a small hint of anger in his voice. But he really meant it, and to prove his point, he turned around and left the building, not looking back to even see how Katie was handling the news. Eric walked outside and began walking alongside the Headquarters building. One of the more unique things about the building was that all along the front walls were some of the most famous pictures from MWE history, all enlarged and framed. There was a picture of DC and Brian Micheals having a staredown just before their huge match at Adrenaline Rush, a picture of David Whitman standing tall after winning the World Title on of many times, even a picture of the former Vanguard stable standing tall in the middle of the ring. As well as many other photos of popular MWE history, including one that caught Erics attention. Among the many pictures along the wall of historic moments, was a picture of Eric Derf, standing atop a ladder, holding the Money in the Bank briefcase. A moment that was the highest point in Eric Derfs career, has become a historic moment for all of MWE. Eric Derf It's nice to see your face in history, isn't it? You do something noteworthy in your life, and you get recognized for it. I won Money in the Bank, and now my place in MWE history has been secured. And if you ask me, there is no better feeling, excluding the one I will have when I cash this bad boy in. He goes to hold up his arm, but stops midway when he realizes he doesn't even have the case, he left it at home. No biggie, the world still knows what it looks like. So he continues to speak. Eric Derf Oblivion claims that his mask is his face. Yet I doubt we'll ever see either mask nor face up here in the history books. All he has done since he got here is guarantee victory each and every week, only to come up short again and again. No pun intended, but that sort of makes him two faced, doesn't it? Oblivion, I'm not going to make a joke about you wanting to cover your face because you're ugly or anything. I've studied you. You claim that we're all the ones wearing masks, hiding behind emotions at all times, masking our true feeling. Ya got me. We all do that. We feel angry, but we hide it when it needs to be hid. We feel excited, yet some people continue to hide their joy to milk someone elses pain. Everyone does it. But despite what you claim, you're no better then the rest of us. If all those emotions are masks, what does that say for you? You have so many emotions, most of which even you claim can be seen right through a mask. Wouldn't that make you a hypocrite? You mock us for hiding our true identities, yet you and I know that's a bunch of psycho bullshit. Flat out bullshit. You hide your identity not because you feel it's your true identity, but because you're ashamed to reveal who you really are. As he continues walking along the building, he comes to another poster on the wall. This one is of The Grand Mystique holding the World Heavyweight Title at Crowning Glory. Of course, by this time, he had removed his mask, and besides winning that title, has been going downhill since. Eric Derf You use that mask as a crutch, just like Grand Mystique did. But the difference between you two is, he actually wins matches. He can back up his words. All you can do is make victims out of minorities. Woman, smaller men, ex girlfriends, even ex girlfriends sisters. Which, by the way, congrats on man, that's awesome! Boning sisters! Eric cracks a smile when he says this, he seems genuinely happy for him. For a moment only, before he goes back on the attack. Eric Derf But even then, it don't take much does it? Your ex girlfriend looked like she could take a dick or six at once, maybe her sister just wanted to follow in her footsteps. And you know what they say, you gotta start everywhere... at the bottom... I mean dude, you nailed sisters. So have I. But it's no big deal if they're legs are always open! He continues walking, meeting up with another picture on the wall. This time, it's The Grand Mystique standing over Windy Bridges after their brutal encounter almost a year ago. Eric Derf But your sex life with woman isn't important right now. Because come this Monday, you and I finally get to go at each other, one on one, man to man, face to... mask. And for some reason, I bet you feel you can beat me. Yeah, you were never pinned in our first ladder match, you took a big bump, didn't have time to recover. And Yeah, you walked out on your shitty partner last week, left him to be picked apart, gave up the match just to let us cause him the pain you so rightfully wanted to cause to all of us. You might have some piss poor excuses like that, but the way I see it, is in this company, there are no excuses. While you were lying there in that ladder match, you knew someone else would capitalize. You knew last week when you walked out the record books were going to have you as a loser that night, because really, how the hell could Milenko beat me, let alone with a partner? I manned up these last few weeks, and I have been on fire. You haven't even sparked since you debuted here in MWE. You beat Mason Resoan, big deal. He was injured, and besides, if anyone was high enough on you, why wouldn't they make that a title match? Huh? They knew you'd just flake out, so they gave the title match to someone who could actually do something with it. And that same night that someone else won that title, you lost your match. You can claim your partner and you weren't getting along, but when I look at it, and the rest of the world look at it, from "behind their masks..." Me covers his face with his hands to represent a mask here. Eric Derf ... You completely bitched out. Oblivion. Maybe you should go back to your life as a hitman. At least with that job, when you failed, you didn't have the world raining down on you with Boo's. At least with that job, no one had to look at your ugly mug, or the masks you use to cover it. At least with that job, MWE will be rid of one more waste of talent like yourself, we need to make room for the rising generation here. And not that you're much of a stepping stone anyway, but I will beat you on Monday, and I will continue to rise here in MWE till the day I cash in my Money in the Bank and win the World Heavyweight Title. And not you, you're ex girlfriend, the guys that fucked her, her sister, you're dead wrestling coach, the dozens and dozens of people you've "claimed" to have killed, NONE of them... can stop me. Bank on- Josh Parker DUDE! Eric turns back towards the front door. Without realizing it, he'd left Josh behind in the building, and has pretty much walked the equivalent of a street block next to the building. But hearing Josh coming behind him, he turns around to meet him half way, and when he finally does, he snaps Josh a glare. Eric Derf Dude, that's the second time you interrupted my new temporary catch phrase! Josh Parker Dude, that's not important right now. Guess what is! Josh holds up a brown folder and waves it in the air. It is a copy of his contract. Josh Parker Yes! Josh Parker has made it to ICW bitch! Eric Derf Don't call me bitch, you bitch. Josh Parker Haha I'll call you whatever I want to once I pass ICW, go on to MNM, and make you my bitch one on one someday! Bank on it! Eric Derf You dickhead!
