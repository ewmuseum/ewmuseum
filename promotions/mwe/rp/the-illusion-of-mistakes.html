The illusion Of Mistakes.; Sorry it took me a while

Feb 12 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Mistakes, everybody makes them. People have warned Sinc time and time again that him turning his back to the fans was a mistake. Woosh. That him thinking he could defeat Mason once and for all….was a mistake! Woosh. That him becoming US champ was nothing more than a simple, undeniable, pain staking, annoying, IRRITATING, MASSIVE, BURDENING, MISTAKE! WOOSH! And finally, that Sinc joining an alliance with a man that is single handedly known for deceiving, for lying, and for manipulating was a clear, mistake. Woosh. A cold, damp, basement is seen as Sinc begins playing darts with Nate Rellington at his side. A dartboard is placed upon his basement’s wall, colored with a red and green overcoat and filled with darts mainly pinned in or around the bulls eye. Boxes of odds and ends such as metal scraps, pictures, clothes, and old toys are seen layered around on the floor near Sinc and Nate. The walls are grimy and mucked with a deep brown paint and are barely seeable due to the large amounts of varied cardboard boxes laying around. The camera gets a good look of the basement, it is not much of a sight to see. The stair well is small and rickety to the right of Sinc and an old television set is seen at the far right corner of this jagged basement tucked away beneath two towering boxes. Its old antennae crooked, the knobs on its right side loose and kinked. The screen on the set dusty and ravaged. But the thing is, the TV seems to be the only lively thing in the room. Even with such a mundane and dusty structure, it is still emitting some kind of taste and livelihood than how Sinc and Nate feel at this very moment. WOOSH! Another dart hitting the board, but oddly, this time Sinc completely misses the bulls eye, and hits the outer most ring of the entire board. The board vibrates from the impact Sinc has impaled. He then finds one of the many and endless boxes around the room to sit on and rests himself atop one. Nate at this point after Sinc’s upsetting loss can no longer stand to be silent and watch him mope in such a derailing manner. He decides to step up and give Sinc some man to man talk. Nate: Ok kid, you lost. It happens. I can’t stand to see you lower yourself like this. Sitting around in a white tank top and a ripped pair of jeans, come on, you didn’t even shave today for lord’s sake. Now all I ask is that you stand the hell up and leave your house’s lonely little basement and start grabbin a drink with your new business partner. Clean yourself up and have some fun, your not bettering yourself by doing what your doing. Your showing the people and the other superstars you’re a quitter, and you give up when things get the best of ya. So go ahead, stand up preacher boy and start showing me you can move on! Sound like a plan….PARTNER? Sinc nods calmly, but not at all happily. He leads himself to his feet and looks Nate right in the eyes as they stand face to face right in front of each other. Nate can tell that Sinc did not take his words lightly. Sinc is breathing in and out through his nose expressing his steamed rage for what had happened to him last week, and what he could have prevented had his partner decided to actually help. Sinc: YOU DON’T KNOW WHAT IT’S LIKE! Sinc shoves Nate away as Nate stumbles backwards hitting a pile of boxes as they fall to the ground. The scene of the boxes as if serenely showing Sinc’s career in the blink of an eye. Falling boxes. One by one they fall, one by one they crash to the ground. All the same size, all the same fate. An image Sinc refuses to accept, but has to, he has to accept such a distinct and vivid fate, that his career will remain a lie. That his career will remain a slope, or maybe that his career will REMAIN a box. Sinc: You don’t know what it’s LIKE being seen out there and marked out for with such a big dream to win then being screwed and destroyed. I HAD to go pro. I HAD to be driven to a high standard, then brought to ashes. You don’t know what it’s like to be a professional wrestler Mr. RELLINGTON. It’s hard work, harder then what most people can accomplish. So don’t you tell me to perk up and get a bloody mary with a cowboy that gets a BONER over the stock market! Sinc is still in Nate’s face as Nate almost cowers against the pile of fallen boxes and tries to back himself up more but can’t. Nate just remains in awe of Sinc’s words and emotions as he is crumpled into the boxes. Sinc’s eyes demanding Nate’s attention as they lock into a destructive stare of certainty. Sinc then turns away from Nate and sits himself down again in sorrow. Nate stands himself up leaning off the boxes and rubs his right shoulder. The camera focusing on him for a moment showing that he is wearing a dark blue suit and a pair of polished black wingtip shoes. No tie is present, emphasizing a casual wear suit. His signature hat still placed atop his head in a tan glaze. Sinc: Look….Nate, sometimes I wish I never became a pro wrestler. I appreciate you trying to help a sorry man like myself but really nothing can help me right now. Not a thing. Obviously I am in a depression. I have said to the people and to the world that I will become a new man, that Sinc Mercier will become the next big thing MWE has ever seen. And I was hoping an assh*le like Joey Dallas could help me get there. Now….now it’s all gone. EVERY SINGLE bit of it….GONE! Before our VERY eyes. I was making a transition into something great, something big. And it all went to hell. Maybe turning my back to the people and the fans was a bad idea, yea I will agree with that one. They were the ones that truly believed I didn’t need anyone, not even a righteous jackass like Joey Dallas. I mean what was I thinking….oh yea, he is going to save me. Real genius of me to go along with that one! Life is full of mistakes and I hate it. I am sick of being in the middle of the biggest sh*t storm since Katrina. It is not fun being seen as the lesser guy and then getting screwed over. Just shows how idiotic I was in the end to believe such a bastard. And now….now I have nothing. Nate tries not to show any hint of sympathy to Sinc being upset after his little tantrum but seems to do so anyway. Nate sighs and curses that he was born with a heart. He sits on a box across from Sinc and politely as well as slowly takes his hat off placing it on his lap. He ruffles his hair a little and then begins to motion to the point Sinc was making. Nate: Good lord! Sinc, wake up man. There will always be times where things aren’t always YOUR fault. Things turn out like a burnt ham, the ham can not be eaten, and the pig had to suffer for no reason. In youthful terms, you can’t become an instant success, and through mistakes you will suffer, but there will always be another chance or opportunity out there to get another ham, or in your case, get another shot with the fans or with the US title. Always an alternative kid. But understand this, this is business buddy, and business is not always an easy thing. You will be brought down and you will be brought up, but you will never be tossed aside. Not until you CHOOSE to be. Sinc, you have the potential and the skills to go SO far, with the few matches I have seen from you, you’ve shown me that, easily, you’ve definitely shown me that. But it won’t always be a victory, it will take some more time and effort until your finally at the top of the ladder, trust me. Sinc looks up to Nate’s words of advice but begins to contemplate some argumentative points himself. He adjusts himself in his seat and twiddles his thumbs thoughtfully. The room seems to turn to a blackness as he recalls what happened with his last few matches. Sinc: I have failed to show Mason that I can in fact beat him, I have FAILED to show Mason that I am not a complete dumbass, I have even failed my fans by thinking I could be somebody with Dallas. I was wrong, Mason was right, he was always right. This ENTIRE time who knew such a dick like Mason was the one who was right all along, and I have to take in his eccentric words with an open mind. I mean as much as I hate to say it, I beat the living crap out of him by doing the dirty work of a cowardice, disgusting man like Dallas. I destroyed Mason with that DQ because I thought I could trust Dallas if I helped him out, gave him the nod. I put the miserable guy through a table and nearly broke his arm for a selfish animal with emotions made of rock. It was ALL for that stuck up bible hugger. I will repeat myself, I was an idiot, and I have NO clue what I was thinking. Most of my fans hate my guts by now, my US title is gone from me, and my record with Mason is laughable. What is a guy like me to do in such a blatant mess? Nate, being a good friend, continues to listen letting Sinc vent freely. Nate sadly cannot relate to Sinc’s endeavor but does seem to show some understanding of such a mess. Sinc: Surely not go have a drink at some nice bar, or get a strip tease and call it a day totally forgetting about my career. I honestly don’t know why Mason saved me from Cannon’s deranged move, and I really don’t care. I just know that whatever trust or feelings I had regarding a team with Joey is done, and that Mason is still my priority at Friday Night Overdrive. If I can FINALLY overcome him, I can finally show the world that I am not just another fool. It won’t be the same hatred, and it won’t be the same chemistry, but it WILL be a match to witness, and one that people will flood in to see. Greater than the match between me and Cannon, the majority will understate and trample me with boo’s but I will fight on in this estranged match. I will do what I can to regain my place in MWE and become the champ again for those that need a role model, and Mason will become a thing of the past. If I cannot prove my worth any longer to Mason, then he will continue to infect and piss all over MWE’s reputation like pissing out a game of Sudoku in the snow. That…..I cannot allow to happen. The room falls completely silent for a moment as Nate peers into Sinc’s acorn like eyes and tries to as if grab his thoughts directly out of Sinc’s mind, and implant them into his own. Nate has always wanted to get more into Sinc’s head, and what drives him to go from a low self esteem superstar to a sudden confident titan. But he has failed to do so, and is forced to accept Sinc as the numskull he truly is. Nate: You know I am going to be completely honest with you, sometimes I don’t know if your right in the head. Just a few moments ago you rant about being this low class superstar, and now your like Kenny Rogers with a brand new guitar, make up your damn mind. It is good to keep your confidence kiddo, but don’t ever lose it. You have a tendency to do that. Forget about all the suits and ties, forget about the upper class giants and high tide lords, you make yourself someone special, and something of legends. Don't fall into the crack of riches and goodies, it's nice but it ain't everything. I don't have the god given miraculous talent you do, you have earned that yourself, and you have earned my respect. If you can do that, I am sure you can regain the fans respect as well. Don't be like me and turn into a greedy sumbitch, turn into someone that can give great things, and receive greater rewards, rewards that a price and a label cannot dish out. He then stops to look at a picture of his father standing in front of a large dome like building with a sign that says; “Mercier Strength Training” he takes from a nearby box laying upwards. The picture as if yearning for Sinc to take a peak. Sinc does not take his eyes off of it for the longest time. Sinc: Like you said it is a business, and alot of the time businesses can be some pretty liable places for backstabbers and opportunities for ultimate deceit. I’m not the smartest worm in the book, I’m not the strongest, hell I will even say I’m not the funniest. Do whatever you can in that ring, say whatever you can say to the camera to make Mason eat your words while you- Sinc sighs with a jolt of the eyeballs wanting not to finish Nate’s explanation but feeling obligated to. I am going to have to bring my best game at Overdrive, follow my instincts, and open up a black void of varied pain. No more Savior to fog my mind, no more false promises and false hope to fuel me. Because in one corner we have one man who has been put through a table, who has had his arm smashed in by the clutches of a steel ladder, who has had an elbow driven into his chest on a stretcher, who has had one of the worst beatings of a lifetime under the handicap that is….that WAS….Sinc Mercier and Joey Dallas. Sinc raises both his hands to help depict a scene of him and Mason in the ring as a sort of body language. Sinc: And then in the other corner you have a man that has had his pride stripped from his very roots, who has had the burden of family and personal issues infiltrate his reality structure, who has been confused over who to trust, and who to take out. And there goes that old saying again. Take me on, or take me out. One that lives true to this day, and one that has ringed in the people’s ears after I faithfully followed their calls and pleas. I don’t care how many times I have to take him on, or what I have to do to make him see me as a challenge, but when I get the victory I’m gonna rub it in his fat face like Oprah to the people when Obama won, I will make sure Mason’s lips are sewn SHUT. Sinc clenches both his hands into an upward set of closed fists as he grinds his teeth in anger. Sinc: I want to see him bleed those tears and wrench out all the arrogance and all the loving his momma ever gave him as a kid, because I have more potential then he has the amount of ladders under his bed. He had to suck the life out of others to racketeer his way to the top. He had to form teams and leave them to make his singles career a success, he had to squeeze the juices out of Aiden’s pit hairs to make entertainment. Let me tell him and you something about entertainment Nate…. Sinc shoves his face right in front of Nate’s as their pupils lock in on each other, dilated into an eternal abyss. Sinc: It doesn’t…come…easy. Sinc then looks directly in the camera recognizing it as it floats around in his basement watching his every move like a cop when the crime is high. Sinc: I’m gonna show you Mason, that being loud and proud, is what wins matches. And returning myself to the people, showing them that I can come back full force, will bring success back to my career. You may not have screwed me over like everybody’s little….savior…. did, but hear this, right now your still enemy number one, and until I watch your sorry self get beaten in that ring I will continue to see you as such.
