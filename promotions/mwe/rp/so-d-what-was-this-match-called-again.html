So D, what was this match called again?; A Grand Mystique RP

Jul 1 2009

The Grand Mystique

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Pitch black. Thousands of hushed voices. Then the music hits. I’ve been here for ten seconds and when the music starts, they all know what’s coming. My pulse races as fireworks explode behind me and pierce the thin darkness. The volume increases. Boos and obscene gestures cascade upon me from all angles; flooding my ears and offending my eyes. My eyes, which continue to look at DC, never leave the deer-in-the-headlights stare that he has. His straggly, paint strewn, greasy hair is unable to conceal what he thinks. “What....the fuck is he doing here?” That look alone....priceless. Punch after punch, I’m pretty certain that I break his nose within seconds. I’m not sure I care too much. His status as an MWE Legend is being shattered and the crowd hates it. Even taking out Maxx never got this much heat. Damn official; get out of here. SMACK! The laces of my boot whack his cheek. He crumples to the floor. That couldn’t have gone any bett.....Lindsey, what is she doing? Crazy bitch....but she.....is quite amazing up close and she.....gets thrown over the top rope. What the hell am I thinking? This is not the time. That can wait. I look around, expecting to see DC at my feet and then I see him. Those eyes, that unflappable gaze – Bridges stands over him and he’s helping him up? Damn redneck, does he want to suffer at my hands again? There it is; Sick Addiction – both of them will regret this. Windy then makes a very sensible decision. His eyes, wide and white with excitement, they tell me what he expects to happen Bang. Big Bang. If this was a match it would be over. Finis. End of. The thud of him tapping is inevitable. *Fade Flashback* Knock knock. “Enter.” “Mr Silver, this is ” “I know who he is babe.” Eli gets up from his plush executive desk and plucks at the pleat of his suit. “I’ve seen Mr Mystique rise through the ranks of this industry, just like me.” He steps over an obstructing pair of cardboard boxes and reaches out, shaking GM by the hand; he gets a firm grip in return. “So what can I do for you on my first day in the job?” “I’m here to appeal to your ego Eli.” They both sit down. Eli reaches under his desk and out of a crystal decanter, pours two glasses of golden whisky. “I knew you were familiar with my work, G. You know I’m not a tough cookie to rub up the right way.” Eli slides one glass over to GM, who catches it right on the edge of the table. “Exactly, but I thought that you would like to find the ultimate way of starting your tenure as the General Manager of MNM with a big bang.” “A threesome?” His excited voice squeals and forces a fake smile out of a wincing GM. “Not exactly the kind of thing that I can help you with.” “I’ll call someone.” He reaches for his phone but GM interrupts him. “That won’t be necessary; it’s not that kind of deal.” “Oh.” The disappointment is absolute and honest. “But it will do your position as a company man no harm at all. It will be solely for the benefit of yourself and the entire MWE. One decision, one call and you, Mr General Manager, can make it happen.” Eli beams. “Mr General Manager – I love it! Ya know, I’m going to get shit made up – badges, posters, it’ll be the shit.” “Eli?” GM gulps half his drink confidently. “Yeah G?” “Don’t.” “Mhm, k.” Eli takes a meek sip of his own. “In all seriousness, I have a proposal that will revolutionise the MWE and I know that a man like you is going to want to accept this idea and run with it.” “Where?” GM takes a smaller gulp of his drink and smiles with utmost self belief in the idea that he is presenting. “All the way to the main event of Crowning Glory.” “You?” GM shrugs his shoulders and opens up his arms in a gesture of ridiculous confidence. “Bitch puh-lease!” That cocky reaction causes Eli to laugh loudly but stop suddenly when he realises GM is thoroughly unimpressed and totally serious. He tries to make nice. “You have this Bloody Jeopardy match with DC at independence Day first. Aren’t you getting ahead of yourself?” GM sits back in his chair, completely relaxed. “I know DC is psyched for the match. In fact I am fucking expecting that he comes at me all guns blazing and kick ten rounds of shit out of me.” Eli is visibly shocked. “And at Independence Day, I expect the old DC, the one who destroyed a hotel room and caused thousands of dollars worth of damage just because I laid a finger on his girlfriend -” “Wife.” Eli interrupts, almost without consideration. “I....don’t.....care!!” Eli retreats, almost defensively like a snail, into the seat of chair. GM stands over him in front of the desk. “So when DC makes me bleed and then drags me on a tour of destruction around the annals of that stinking arena, he can try and finish me off all that he wants.” GM gets up. “But you know something, he will be grateful that I am going to beat him unconscious so he doesn’t feel them doing the stitches!” He walks around the table, tapping on the table with his fingernail as he does. Eli watches him carefully out of the corner of his eye, careful not make eye contact. “And when DC is carried out on a stretcher, bleeding like Lindsay and Paige at the wrong time of the month, you will have no choice but to accept me as the best choice to feature in the Crowning Glory main event with the World title on the line.” When he reaches Eli’s side of the desk, he reaches out for the decanter and pours another drink in his glass “Because I have beaten the best on the biggest of occasions and everyone else has been crushed like the insignificants that they are” GM drinks the whisky in one gulp and with forceful impact he smashes the glass on the wall behind Eli. He jumps off his seat in shock at the impact. “DC is the only thing that stands in the way between me and Crowning Glory, between my present and my future!” GM’s face is now right next to Eli’s pale features. “But soon he won’t be standing and there will be nothing you can do except book your main event!” Normally so confident and brash, Eli has clearly been a little humbled by the confrontation, but he chooses now to speak up. “Erm, Mr Mystique, the MWE will not be held hostage to bribes or deadlines.” GM smiles and taps Eli gently on the side of the face. “I’m going to say this real slowly for you Silver.” GM has lowered his voice to a whisper. “This is not a bribe and I am not forcing you or the bloody MWE to do a thing, except open their eyes and take notice.” GM continues his intimidating low whisper. “It is really not a challenge. All you have to do is acknowledge how simple the future will be if it happens. Everyone’s a winner, except of course DC and whichever poor sap is World Champion at the time.” “I.....am going to have to talk to my... err...superiors.” His voice quivers his fear. He rubs the bridge of his nose in an almost unconscious nervous gesture. “I....errr....can’t make that decision on my own.” “Talk to whoever you have to, Ellie.” He smiles; a cocky white grin. “Just now, that this is not EW and I am not prepared to be overlooked again.” GM rubs his hand disdainfully across Eli’s short, poo-brown hair. “The MWE needs someone to steer them in the right direction in the future.” GM stands up, straightens out his jacket and heads toward the door. He turns to Eli who is still rooted to his chair. It’s clear he wants the whole episode over with. GM, however, couldn’t be happier and his smiles broadcasts impervious audacity and bravado. ”Who better than me?” * “So why do the things that you do?” The camera opens up to a room. The walls are lined on two sides with thick leather lined books, which manage to fill the room with an instant sense of importance and knowledge. There is a large old fashioned red leather chair in the corner where the bookshelves meet. As the camera approaches, we see a suited man sitting in the chair. He appears to be about fifty, with a typical silver fox appearance, with horn rimmed glasses, coarse silvery facial hair and slightly creased skin. He is cross-legged and is writing onto an A4 pad, with a cartridge pen that scratches as he writes. “Never having to say sorry is great.” The man has a neutral look of concentration on his face and his forehead occasionally furrows as he scribes. “You have done some things which have been described as reprehensible, brutal, and wild.” A chuckle comes from off camera. “So, I ask again. What makes you do it?” The tone is demanding, yet tolerant and encouraging at the same time. The camera swings around. We see GM, relaxed and sprawled out on a longer chair that is made of the same red leather as its’ smaller counterpart. It is close to a wide, slightly opened window that allows a small breeze to come in as well as bright sunlight that streams in. His feet are raised at one end and his head is rested on the other. His hands are behind his head. He is wearing a black suit jacket and trousers with a white round neck shirt. The shirt is a little ruffled yet his appearance and the sunshine that surround him seem to be in perfect tandem with his relaxed mood. “I went from nothing to someone by just knocking someone out with a beer bottle. I got made after I pushed someone else off a stage.” GM’s voice is jovial as he clearly revels in recalling in some of his moments of infamy. He smiles a toothy white grin at the ceiling. “I have become who I am based on absolute physical domination. By taking out one rival after another. I’m no monster; I just do what is best for me Doc.” The man known to GM as “Doc” stops his writing and rests his pen on the pad. “So losing the MWE US title to Winston Bridges and failing to reclaim it made you the man you are now?” A probing question, it is spoken slowly and deliberately. “Absolutely.” A terse yet assured reply. “And you seriously injuring Maxx, causing him to retire and taunting him by his bedside were all....” GM turns around to face Doc and raises his hand, which causes Doc to stop. “In my best interests. I thought that would be perfectly clear.” GM sits up and swings his legs around to a sitting position, facing Doc. He looks down at the floor. “Windy....Maxx....hell even DC, all had it coming.” His voice is low and intense. ”Their mouths had gotten them into trouble and they could no longer back up what they were saying.” He looks up, making a point to have eye contact. “Legacies mean nothing to me. Who cares if DC is a three time World Champion. All I see is the same as Brian Michaels; Brian Michaels, a man that I beat the life and soul out of at Highway to Hell.” “The thing about Rated X is that, together and separately, they have dominated the main event scene in every company that they have been in. Until recently they had always been the top of their games, the pinnacles of the industry and star making innovators” “But now they are what I have exposed them to be; they are broken, pale imitators of the stars they once were. Broken by me.” “You feel no regret?” “Oh yes. I’m not completely inhumane.” “How do you feel when you get these feelings of regret?” “They don’t last long.” “And why is that?” “I have the ultimate cure.” He pulls a black wallet out of his inside jacket pocket. He opens it up and looks fondly at the contents. “This is what keeps me going, gives me strength and temporarily cures all my self doubt issues.” He doesn’t show it to the camera but he hands it off to the doctor who nudges himself forward to meet GM’s reach. He looks at it briefly before handing it back. “And that drives you?” He sits back again and strokes his silver grey goatee with the plastic tip of the pen. “That is my future. That is what I dream about when I sleep and what I focus on during each and every day that I spend away from it.” GM allows for one last lingering look before he returns the wallet to its’ original place. “It’s good to have something to aim for.” Doc gestures with the pen, from the rim of his glasses to a point just in front of them, as if pointing to the future. “I’ve found it works for me.” Doc shuffles again. “So how does that lead you to hitting an innocent woman like Lindsey?” GM’s response to that is mixture of disdainfully raised eyebrow and a slightly shocked gawping expression. Shit; he actually went there. GM pauses, quite unable to believe that this man, this shrink, who had never been met with GM until today, had the gall to ask a question like that. He stood up and walked around the couch. He stands by the window, enjoying the warmth that the sunlight provides so amply. As he ponders what was going through his mind when he encountered Lindsey in the ring at MNM. Her delicious smell, her naturally beautiful curves, her smooth skin. But most of all, her spirit. Her burning spirit, which fought with all her might to resist GM. He could see it in her eyes. All her love for DC, the pure strength that they gave each other. And he wondered if he could ever have that. And in that moment, when his mind was perfectly clear, he could give no reason why he had to hurt Lindsey in the way he did. Time seems to have stood still. GM could see Lindsey right next to him, just like she was when he hurt her. That sweet strawberry smell wafted through the air; that don’t-fuck-with-me look of hers that mirrors D.C.s’, it was so clear to him. His return and the build up that epic tag match were huge for so many reasons, not least the history between those in the ring. Against the backdrop of GM’s attack and Lindsay’s injury, it’s not surprised that it took a while to get over. Not just today but the past couple of weeks all seemed to make sense. And yet the future, the destiny of which he had spoken about so much, was his to steer. And he knew the direction to go, and where he needed to divert his energies. “Where are you going?” The Doc’s voice rips open his daydreaming scene of distraction. GM has his hand on the door handle and is apparently ready to leave prematurely. “I....erm, have something to attend to. Something important.” “But we are not finished.” He is clearly perturbed by this drastic change in events; this had raised even more issues and caused him to furiously scribble a whole new page. “I don’t care. I’m going to war on July 5th and I don’t think anyone cares if I am in a suitable mental state.” The scene fades with the door being slammed loudly. * Ping. A lift grinds open and GM walks out. He steps into a corridor. The walls are decorated in powder blue and the carpets are a darker, royal blue. As he steps in further, we see doors made of light brown oak. He addresses the camera in front of him. “I’ve been told that I am sick, not all there and that I need help.” He is quiet and almost humble. “Maybe things have gone too far, but I know nothing and no one can help me.” He rubs his stubble. “This will make things worse, but it needs to happen.” He steps towards a door and places his palm calmly on the bronze handle. He looks pensively at it before entering. He silently steps into a total darkness of the room. The door clicks shut. The camera can barely pick up his shape. GM knows this and steps towards the blinds. He pinches them ever-so-slightly open and peeks through. The room is high enough to get a good view of the city below and the busy hive of activity that is criss-crossing on the streets. GM then looks back, not at the camera but at the shape that is now visibly present to the right of the room. His hands are still on the blinds. He appears to be playing with them to see the effect of the lights against the wall. The camera notices this and observes neutrally as he changes the shape on the wall. It seems like he has created a halo-like oval shape above the bed which he is fascinated by. He lets go of the blinds gently and they fall peacefully back into place. He then walks around and stands by the bed. The camera looks over him and looks at what he seems to be particularly taken by. In the bed, curled up in the quilt with only a shock of untidy blond hair and the top of a Blood Spatter Rated X hoodie visible is Lindsey! “I know that in doing this I will incur his wrath.” He whispers so quietly to her that it is barely audible. “I felt compelled to do this because no matter what, being used a pawn in a war is not fair.” GM leans over and reaches out gently to touch her hair. She turns around 180 degrees in her quilt cocoon and suddenly she is facing him. He recoils instinctively in surprise but as she doesn’t seem to wake, he moves closer again. “You have been hurt and it was totally unnecessary. The pain that we suffer and inflict should not be part of your life.” With a feather-light touch he moves a stray lock of hair out of her face. “You don’t deserve to share our common fate of torturous agonies. He will experience pain like nothing else but you will be a mere spectator. This will be a war and I can’t expect you to understand.” He inches closer still. “You won’t be there on the front line and the television will prevent you from feeling anything real. You may never realise how lucky you are.” He kneels down and they are almost nose to nose. “But I hope DC appreciates how fortunate he is to have you.” Ting Bzzzzz. Lindsay’s mobile vibrates, it pierces the dark with its’ artificial light. GM turns to leave but realises that Lindsey hasn’t stirred, not even a little. He looks back at her and then at her phone. He sees it clear as day, almost taunting him. QUOTE 1 new message D.C. 01:55am Then GM does something so unreasonable that he can’t believe. He lifts up Lindsay’s sleek metallic pink handset and presses Read. “What am I doing? What have I done?” QUOTE Message from D.C. I miss you <3 01:55am GM bits his lips nervously. He sees this message, a clear communication of love from one to another. But GM is here and he isn’t. GM scratches the side of his head as he contemplates another quite frankly insane idea. He then exits the messages function and goes to the camera. He hold the handset up and moves the camera into focus, not too close but just far enough away to take a nice picture. Her perfectly plump lips, her round cheeks and even her pretty little nose; he manages to capture it all in a picture which is almost a two tone black and white. Without thinking of any reason to hesitate, he attaches the picture to a message and sends it to D.C. He places the handset back on the table and leaves without undue uncertainty. Shit just got real and GM started it. This is all going to add up and make Sunday really fucking interesting. * The scene starts in a white room. The camera moves round to reveal it is small and square. It is padded on all sides, is clean and artificially bright but empty. There is no obvious light or air source, or an exit. As the camera returns, having taken in the whole space, the body of a large man comes into view. He is dressed only in a grubby white straightjacket, which not only forces his arms to sit across his chest but also his legs are crossed at the ankles and bound together. He lies in a corner on the floor with his back straight and legs outstretched. He turns - or well, he rolls around to face the camera and it is the Grand Mystique. Newly unmasked and all. “I’m so used to being trapped; being held within the constraints of expectations.” In his position on the floor, his stubbled jawline rubs against the padding as he talks. “But I don’t have that anymore. No one expects DC to lose because I rubbed him up the wrong way, just because I’ve laid hands on Lindsey. But hands up in you think I care!” He smiles a grin of pure, seething evil. “The thing is D; this match is going to be so violent that Lindsey’s will be deathly pale in comparison. It’s going to be more face-breakingly brutal than any other in MWE history.” He rolls around slightly, trying to get comfortable. “I’ve been thrown through glass by Windy fucking Bridges – what are you going to do to me that I have not already done to someone else before?” “I won’t hesitate kicking your ass over the stage because I’ve done it to Maxx.” He shuffles down, away from the wall at the side of the room. “I mean two of my most famous victims; two men who I have beaten up all over this sorry country. They are two men who have beaten you.” “I recall Bridges pinning your ass a few weeks ago. I even recall seeing Maxx nearly rip your neck off in EW.” Slowly and surely, he stomach crunches himself to a vertical position, grimacing as he does it. “Shit.....if I had seen the little bitch do that, I would have come out and have a shit right down both your freaking necks.” He is now facing the camera as his sits across the room, still trapped in his constrictive jacket yet in a position that appears to be considerably more comfortable. “Because this isn’t a place like EW or even the old MWE; they were places that welcomed the old D.C. His attitude, the old Rated X way of never giving a crap is dead. DC is not da man here.” “This is my world and the King of Fuck You, the Master of Ye Olde Middle Finger has no power.” “The MWE is entering my orbit, within my jurisdiction and soon I will be on top, at the summit as the pinnacle of the industry. You will be the bottom feeder that you always were before Brian Michaels picked you up and allowed you to hang off his ass.” GM begins to pull violently. We can his elbows moving frantically trying to tug the arms off his stubborn vice-like jacket. “The MWE as you know it is dying.” His tone is now more agitated and his brow glistens a little from the effort. “Only recognition that I am the number one name in the company will help rescue it from the same fate that befell EW. And the same fate that will inevitably topple Rev Pro once again.” He pulls with massive force, with almost enough momentum to topple himself over. “Because I ask you to do the same thing I have asked Maxx and Bridges. The same thing that Brian Michaels asked of you at Adrenaline Rush.” We hear a loud rip. “Do something fucking different!” He roars with deafening intensity. “Because the same old DC schtick will not rescue you.” The tips of his fingers crawl out of the frayed rip on the jacket. ”It will not stop my fists tearing the fibres of your skin. It will not cease your blood from dripping onto the floor because that is inevitable.” He speaks with increasing defiance as he struggles free. ”Our blood will flood the streets, cover the walls and stain the sewers of Jacksonville." GM has taken his time but we now see that he has fully escaped from the ties that seemed to bind him so fiercely. He stands up and the camera rises with him "Those sewers are where I will leave you and your career when I am finished with you as I take my leave to become the main event star that you all knew that I would be.” He walks past the camera and the scene fades on the empty white room.
