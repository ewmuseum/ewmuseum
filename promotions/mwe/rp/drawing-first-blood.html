Drawing First Blood

Dec 4 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
** The camera shot opens up and there is activity all around. The stage is lit with pink neon as music pumps for the PA system and the club smells of cheap perfume, cigarette smoke and Aqua Net. As the camera pans around women dance on stage to the music and there all in various states of undress as they work for that money. The camera pans around again and we see men sitting at tables enjoying drinks or enjoying lap dances as smoke makes the air all around look thick with smog. Finally the camera begins to move around the club and we can see more men getting lap dances or laughing with there buddies over drinks and cigars. Finally the camera comes to a big man in a black suit standing in front of a door. The man really isn’t paying attention and instead he is watching the women work for that mighty dollar. The big man finally notices the camera man and opens the big wood door letting him in. As the camera enters the next room we see the backs of two women giving a lap dance to an unidentified man as he sits on a couch. As the camera stays transfixed on the scene at hand the women pay no attention and continue to dance to the thundering song as the man gropes them. The song stops and the women giggle as they stop dancing. They each give the man a kiss on the check and giggle once more as he gives them money. The women walk off and the camera moves closer to reveal Ace Cannon sitting on the couch with a drink in his right hand and cigar in his left. He has a smile on his face as he notices the camera.** Ace: Well, I see you guys ended up finding me anyway. ** Ace takes a drink from his glass and then a pull from the cigar before he continues.** Ace: I knew you guys would find me out here celebrating my birthday and usually I’d be angry but tonight I’m not. It is a celebration. It is a night for me to enjoy myself…. **Ace pauses and the look on his face changes from a happy grin to a look of utter disgust.** Ace: …. But three things are making it impossible for me to enjoy myself. **Ace stops again and shakes his head. He takes a drink to wet his lips.** Ace: The first thing that is killing the festive mood is me not having the Genesis title sitting right her next to me enjoying all the big fake tits and drinks with me. That title was as good as mine but I came up short and DC won it. **Ace stops and looks around the room and takes a pull from the large cigar he has. He exhales the smoke before he continues.** Ace: Now usually I would say something really negative about DC but he won and he did a good job. So DC, much respect from me to you man and I really hope you whip my brothers ass at Adrenaline Rush so you can unify the titles because I would really love to be the first man to defeat the Undisputed champion and DC I would love for that to be you and that is no secret. So good luck because if you win there is going to be a huge target on you back that I will be gunning for. ** Ace finishes his drink and sets it on the table next to him** Ace: Now the second thing holding me back from enjoying all these tits in my face is Joey Dallas. A man who essentially cost me the title and I really don’t appreciate that and now I have to tag with this Jurassic slapass?!?! Well, Dallas you’re a brave man to screw me over. I mean out of everyone here in this company you have seen how gold makes me lose my mind! You know that it is my obession and you know it makes me to drastic things. Hell, you were there on the night I punted Virus’s little rugrat across the ring in the name of gold and you were there the night I did the same thing to that little punkass THK! So as far as I am concerned you have signed your death warrant…. **Ace takes a drag and blows a few smoke rings before continuing.** Ace: So Dallas, look over your shoulder everywhere you go, check under your bed, check in your closet, looking in the backseat of your car when you get in because you never know where I might pop out from! But this week I will let it slide because I like to win and this tag team match coming up is very important to me and that leads me too my third reason why I can’t enjoy these tits. ** Ace takes a few puffs of the cigar before continuing his thought.** Ace: This tag match doesn’t worry me. I mean yea, Maxx and Grand Mystique are tag champions but thats all elementary too me. But there is on thing that just makes me want to go in this match and go crazy… Let me take that back… There is one man in this match that makes me just want to fucking kill and his name is Maxx! **Ace takes a few pulls off his cigar before he leans over and puts it out. He sits up and scoots to the edge of the red velvet couch. He takes a deep breath before he speaks.** Ace: You see, in case you didn’t know Maxx and myself have a bit of history back in EW. We had a fatal four way match to unify the EW, MWE and APCW world titles. I was the holder of the EW title, DC held the MWE title and Maxx had the APCW title. We went into this match along with Virus and to cut a long story short Maxx won and unifed all three titles. I mean it was a tremendous day for him but ever since that day I have had a horrible taste in my mouth about that match. A taste in my mouth that has made me think about quitting the business. ** Ace stops and rests his elbows on his knees and looks down at the red carpet for a few seconds. He eventually looks up and continues.** Ace: You see, it made me want to quit because on that day I felt Maxx politicked his way to the top of EW. Hell, for that matter he must have got on his knees and swallowed a lot of load to get to the postion he was in and I bet the rug burn on his knees hurt. **Ace cracks a sly smile before he continues.** Ace: Now here we are at present day and Maxx has new problems and I am not talking about that whore he has a hard on for named Chole.. I am talking about the cancellation of Genesis. Now, when he heard the show was going to be canceled he started crying more then the lesbian community after Paige died. Why did he start crying? Well, he was going to a new brand where he would have to start over and when this prospect was given to Maxx he wanted to quit and run off like the bitch he is. ** Ace stops and cracks another sly grin.** Ace: Why did he want to quit? Well, that’s an easy answer…. He doesn’t want to get on his knees again to start sucking his way to the top but Maxx doesn’t know something… He doesn’t know that I am going to do him a favor when we step in that ring and that favor involves me kicking his fucking skull in. I will once and for all end the politicking, I will end the swallowing of loads and rid the company of what I feel is nothing more then a cancer. So Maxx, make sure you try really hard to fuck that whore named Chole because I plan not to make you pay. **Ace looks into the camera. Once more and his face transations to a look of determnation.** Ace: So as I wrap this up let me say right now that I know I didn’t mention Grand Mystique and it’s not that I don’t find him threat because I do find him a slight threat… But this shit between me and Maxx is more then personal it is something that has been festering deep down inside of me for the last six months and come Genesis it will rear it’s ugly head and I guarantee that I will beat Maxx within an inch of his life and as that inch makes you want to get up and fight Maxx I will come out of nowhere and BOOM! **Ace yells the words and lets them sink in.** Ace: So Maxx see you at Genesis….. And you too Mystique. ** Ace stands from the couch and walks past the camera as we fade out.**
