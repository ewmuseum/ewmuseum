Redemption an Ace Cannon Production

Apr 2 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
** The camera opens up with Cannon sitting on the edge of his hotel room bed. He sits there with his arms resting on his knees and his head looking down towards the ground. He let’s out a loud sigh as his mind runs over the events that happened at Highway to Hell. He thinks about kicking a defenseless pregnant women in the stomach and thinks about crippling DC…. FOR A SECOND TIME! After these thoughts cross his mind he looks up and laughs, his laugh is chilling and sinister all at the same time. Cannon quits laughing as there is a knock at the door. He stands up and walks across his expansive hotel room to the door. As he reaches the door he looks through a peep hole and a smile crawls across his face as he sees who is on the other side. He opens the door and we see the visitor is Lindsey.** Cannon: Come on in… ** Cannon walks back across the hotel room and sits back down on the bed as Lindsey follows him.** Lindsey: Where is Paige and Eli? ** Cannon looks at her and gives her a creepy look.. A look that a rapist might give his prey.** Cannon: Paige and Eli went to a local radio station to appear on a Gay and Lesbian talk show. ** Lindsey nods her head.** Lindsey: So why am I here? ** Cannon continues to give her the creepy look before he answers.** Cannon: I got us a car and were going out for the afternoon. ** Lindsey looks at Cannon with a look of disapproval.** Lindsey: What do you mean were going out? ** Cannon stands up from the bed and takes a deep breath before answering Lindsey.** Cannon: Listen cunt, it means were going out and your buying me what I want… I know you got some fucking money. I know you have access to DC’s money and were using it… ON ME! **Lindsey get a slight look of terror on her face.** Lindsey: I…I…I… ** Cannon all of a sudden reaches out and grabs her by her arm and pulls her close to him.. He lowers his face and puts it inches away from hers.** Cannon: We are going to go out and have a good time.. There is no getting out of this you little bitch! You will do what I fucking say! Is that understood? ** Lindsey nods her head in agreement as Cannon kisses he on the cheek and lets go of her arm.** Cannon: Good.. The car should be downstairs right about now. ONE HOUR LATER ** We see Lindsey and Cannon standing in a clothing store. Cannon is looking over all the Affliction and Ed Hardy t-shirts. Lindsey stands there looking un-enthused and upset as Cannon continues shopping. After a few moments he picks out a shirt and holds it up.** Cannon: How does this one look Lindsey? ** Lindsey looks at it for a second.** Lindsey: It looks fine. ** Cannon gets a twisted look on his face after Lindsey’s statement.** Cannon: Listen bitch, have more enthusiasm when I ask you something. If you don’t then there will be some problems. Is that understood? ** Lindsey nods at Cannon.** Cannon: Good, now lets try this again… How does this shirt look? **Lindsey looks at the shirt once again.** Lindsey: It will look really good on you. ** Cannon has a smile that washes over his face at what Lindsey just said.** Cannon: Now that is what I am talking about. ** Cannon winks at Lindsey and begins walking away. As Cannon walks away Lindsey follows him toward the cash register. Cannon lays a few shirts and a couple pairs of jeans up there for the cashier to ring up.** Cashier: Is this going to be it today? ** Cannon looks over then beautiful buxom high school aged cashier named Becky before answering.** Cannon: It sure is Becky. ** Becky smiles at Cannon as she rings up the clothes.** Becky: The total is going to be $376.56. ** Cannon looks over at Lindsey.** Cannon: Lindsey, what are you waiting for? Pay the beautiful girl. ** Lindsey gives Cannon a look of disgust as Cannon looks back at Becky and winks at her. Lindsey opens he purse and pulls out her wallet and gives Becky a credit card. Becky runs the credit card and gives Lindsey the receipt and her credit card back as Cannon takes the clothes and begins walking away. As Lindsey puts away her credit card she glares at Cannon who’s back is to her but as she is doing this Cannon turns around and sees this. A look of anger comes over his face. Lindsey’s face quickly changes as she starts walking toward an angry Cannon.** Cannon: What the fuck was that look for you filthy cunt?! ** Lindsey just looks at Cannon like a sad puppy dog.** Lindsey: Nothing.. It was nothing. ** Cannon looks at Lindsey and nods his head.** Cannon: Exactly like I thought. Let’s go get some lunch being with a bitch like you makes me hungry. ** Cannon and Lindsey continue walking and get on an escalator toward the food court. Once they get to the food court Cannon takes a seat at a table. Lindsey sits down on the other side of Cannon.** Lindsey: Why did we sit down? We don’t have any food yet. ** Cannon looks at Lindsey and shakes his head in disgust.** Cannon: I know this.. I am waiting for your ass to get up and get me some food! ** Lindsey looks at Cannon and is almost in tears.** Cannon: I am going to ignore the fact that your about to cry… Now, got get me some Taco Bell. I want two hard tacos and a bean burrito. ** Lindsey nods her head and begins walking toward Taco Bell and as she is about halfway there Cannon starts yelling her name. She stops and turns around..** Cannon: DON’T FORGET MY FRUTISTA FREEZE EITHER… BITCH! ** The crowd in the food court is appalled at what Cannon has just done. He looks at all them with a mean scowl written across his face. Lindsey with tears streaming down her face now walks up to the Taco Bell to place the order at the behest of Cannon.** Lindsey: Can I get one hard taco, two bean burritos and a frutista freeze please. ** The guys behind the register types all this in and looks at Lindsey for a moment.** Guy: Sorry ma’am, but you look very familiar… ** Lindsey looks at the guy and shrugs her shoulders. She really doesn’t want a fan to realize it is her and see her in this condition.** Guy: I GOT IT! Your Lindsey from MWE! ** Lindsey looks at the guys and gives him a slight smile.** Lindsey: Yea, you got it. ** The guy behind the register smiles at her.** Guy: Can I ask you something? ** Lindsey nods her head.** Guy: Why are you with that asshole Ace Cannon? ** Lindsey looks at the guys a smiles a little bit. She is glad someone has called Ace Cannon an asshole.** Lindsey: I don’t know… I just don’t know. ** Lindsey looks down at the ground ashamed of herself because she doesn’t know why she continues to associate with him.** Guy: I have been hearing on all the internet wrestling sites that he has been treating you bad and I just heard at him yell at you across the food court and all I can say is you need to stand up too him. Your to beautiful to be treated like this. ** Lindsey looks at the guy and just smiles once more.** Lindsey: How much is the food going to be? ** The guy looks at Lindsey and feels bad for her. He sees a broken women** Guy: Don’t worry… It’s on me. ** Lindsey just smiles at this act of kindness. It is the best thing that has been done for her all day.** Lindsey: Thank you.. ** The guy hands her the food and she begins walking back toward Cannon as the words “Stand up for yourself.” Rattle through her head over and over again. She arrives at the table and puts the food down in front of Cannon. She sits down across from him with out saying a word. Cannon goes through the food and sees his order is wrong. He sees two bean burritos instead of two hard tacos. Cannon looks enraged as he looks back and Lindsey.** Cannon: What the fuck is this shit?!?! ** Lindsey looks at Cannon bewildered.** Lindsey: Is there something wrong? ** Cannon takes a deep breath and exhales through his nose.** Cannon: Yea… Yeah there is! You fucked up my order you stupid bitch. I wanted two hard tacos not two bean burritos! Are you fucking stupid or something? ** Lindsey just looks at Cannon and starts bawling. The tears are coming quick and often as the patrons of the food court now have there attention focused on Lindsey and Cannon.** Cannon: JESUS CHRIST! Now your just going to sit there and fucking cry! You’re a stupid fucking cunt. I don’t know why DC never kicked the shit out of you! If you were my women I would beat some fucking sense into your dumbass! ** Cannon stops berating Lindsey and just stares at her in disgust. The crowd still has there eyes glued to them as there not sure what is going to happen next. Finally Lindsey just looks at Cannon and stands up she then takes his precious frutista freeze and tosses it in his face! The crowd cheers wildly at this as Lindsey hurries away from Cannon as she fears the possible repercussions! Cannon sits there angry and shocked all at the same time.. He stands up and looks at the crowd who are all laughing at him.** CANNON: THIS SHIT ISN’T FUNNY! FUCK YOU ALL! I SWEAR TO GOD IF I SEE ANY OF YOU ON THE STREET I WILL SNAP YOUR FUCKING NECK! ** FADE OUT** ** We open back inside of Cannon’s hotel room. He is sitting in a chair with a laptop in front of him and he appears to have changed and showered from the events that happened earlier today… But he still looks super pissed.** Cannon: GOD DAMN IT! ** Cannon just shakes his head.** Cannon: No one embarrasses me like Lindsey did today! It is simply unacceptable and not something that happens… But I digress. ** Cannon takes a deep breath to hopefully clear his mind over what happened with Lindsey and to get on with the matters at hand.** Cannon: Now, first things first… Something that is on a lot of peoples minds is the controversial formation of The Vanguard. ** Cannon gets the now trademark chilling smile of his.** Cannon: This has been a long time coming. Joseph, Paige and myself have been sitting on this proverbial time bomb for a long time and at Highway To Hell we exploded onto the scene and made an impact! A impact that will never be forgotten and we have cemented ourselves as a group of individuals that need no one but each other on our climb to the top…. And as a group we will get to the top and our collective reign of terror will be one for the ages. ** Cannon looks at the camera and shakes his head as if to say “Sorry about your luck.” He then turns his attention to his laptop.** Cannon: As I logged onto MWE.COM today I saw two things that involved the same man… and that man is Mason Resoan. A man who I actually had some respect for and a man who I have actually tagged up with once before but I saw something regarding Mason and myself that made me angry. ** Cannon stops and just looks at his laptop.** Cannon: I saw that Mason beat me in the MWE Legends Tournament. I saw that the fans voted him over myself and this is what some of them had to say about Mason… QUOTE “Interesting match, They've never faced each other, but Mason has done something Ace couldn't do, and that's beat Joey Dallas (so far). I think both are impressive, and both are about the same level, so this one is hard, but I'm going to go with Mason, barely. “ ** After Cannon finishes reading that post he stops and thinks about it.** Cannon: Yeah this guy is total idiot. I may have never beat Dallas in a singles match but I did beat him in a tag match. Sure, Mason and myself were a team but if you go back and watch that match you will see I did all the work and you will see that I am the one who pinned Sinc in the middle of the ring for our victory! ** Cannon stops right there and lets that sink in. He knows Mason will see this and he wants to make sure that he understands every word that Cannon says.** Cannon: This little Mason fan boy is also wrong about another thing… Us being on the same level! We are not on the same level… I am better then him and I know this because I beat David Whitman and he couldn’t at Highway To Hell. I mean if Mason was so good he would have beat Whitman… But he didn’t! He fell short and that is something he can’t change. ** Cannon looks at the camera and laughs at Masons failed attempt to beat David Whitman. He then turns back to his laptop.** Cannon: Now here is what another little Mason fan boy had to say… QUOTE “I have to go with Resoan Also, his wins, and losses, and championships are a lot more impressive. “ ** After Cannon reads it he once again stops and thinks about what this little fan boy said.** Cannon: Mason is does have a very impressive win loss record and I can’t deny that.. But I can certainly poke holes through that impressive win loss record… ** Cannon stops and sits on what he is about to say for the moment.** Cannon: A couple months ago Mason and myself were having a talk.. I was disgruntled about things that were going and he proceeded to tell me that he gets a lot of his wins because he is part of a kliq. ** Cannon stops so this revelation can hang there like a cloud of smog over LA.** Cannon: I bet a lot of you are out there saying.. “Cannon is full of shit. He is just jealous.” Well it’s the truth and I am not jealous. I can guarantee Mason won’t admit to it but it’s true and in my opinion it devalues all of his accomplishments… Even his titles. ** Cannon pauses for a few long seconds so Mason can digest what was just said.** Cannon: Yea and the only reason the kliq didn’t get him the title is because Whitman is higher on the kliq food chain… Sorry about that Mason… I really am. ** Cannon shuts the laptop and takes extra time before continuing to talk. He really wants to make sure that Mason understands what was just said.** Cannon: I bring all this up because this week at Mayhem I take on the aforementioned Mason Resoan. Now this is a big match for me… Sure, I beat Whitman and Michaels in back to back weeks and those wins meant an awful lot but this match means more then the previous two. ** Cannon stops because he wants everyone to realize the magnitude of this match.** Cannon: This means a lot to me because I want to show all you pathetic and ignorant fans that you were wrong! I want you to see first hand in this match that I am better then Mason and that he will never be on my level no matter how hard he hit’s the gym or how much tape he watches…. It just isn’t possible for him to be as good as me. ** Cannon once again pauses.. But this time he pauses and reaches across the table and extracts a cigarette from a pack and grabs a lighter. He lights the cigarette and takes a few puffs before talking again.** Cannon: See, what I just said about me beating Mason is important but not as important as me making him pay for a wrong he committed against me…. Let me refresh everyones memory out there… ** Cannon once again opens his laptop and clicks around. He turns the laptop around and a video is queued up. QUOTE “Whitman puts his hands up in self-defense, but Mason kicks him in the balls. Whitman collapses to his knees as Mason snickers. He wakes up the referee, and slides him into the ring. Cannon slowly gets up as well. He takes one of Whitman's arms, and grabs the other arm and does the same before jumping into the air and driving his knees into the spine of the champion with Money, Power, Respect! Whitman falls to the mat only for Cannon to hook his leg for a cover. ONE … TWO … THREE!” Cannon turns the laptop back around and closes it again. He sits there for a moment with a look of annoyance written across his face.** Cannon: Mason, I can never forgive you for sticking your nose in my business. You see my getting involved in my match you made me look weak. By kicking Whitman in the balls to give me and advantage is unforgivable…. I was well on my way to winning that match and then you gave me the advantage and it made me look weak and like I couldn’t be Whitman! ** Cannon stops and the look of annoyance turns to a look of anger.** Cannon: I had Whitman beat… I dominated him that entire match and was on my way to making everyone realize that a Ace Cannon was a forced to be reckoned with and you ruined that! By doing that you signed your death warrant, you put yourself atop of my list of hate… A list that isn’t a good place to be…. It is a place you should be very afraid of. ** Cannon takes a few puffs of the cigarette.** Cannon: So Mason, I will beat you and show everyone that I AM a force to truly be reckoned with and after I am done with that your name will be off my list of hate and that’s a good thing for you but I don’t know if you will be able to recover from the beating you will suffer at my hands. ** Cannon takes a few puffs of his cigarette and puts it out in the ash try sitting by him** Cannon: Mason, do yourself a favor before Monday. Make sure you give Cherry Daiquiri or whatever her fucking name is a list of your next of kin because she will need to notify them after I am done whipping your ass! ** Cannon lifts his right thumb to his throat and does a slitting motion across his neck.** Cannon: And Mason remember one thing… Don’t blame the Vanguard… BLAME YOURSELF! ** The camera fades to black.**
