The views of Kanto do not reflect upon my own. ------------------------------------------------------- Flash to the narrator, So that I can tell you, In poem form, What Kanto did do. Kanto was normal, Back in the past, But with all his trauma, That never could last. Now this next story, Will be hard to consume, So if you have a weak stomach, Please leave the room. For it is a tale, Of Kanto before, So be prepared, Or leave though the door. Flash to the day, Of April 0-nine, The day that put Kanto, In a deep bind. His girlfriend proclaimed, In the passion and wild, That they have created, A personal child. Flash two weeks later, Kanto ponders a sin. He considers, An abortion. As he wishes for, The baby to die, He would not tell, For his girlfriend would cry. Flash past three months, And the girlfriend is showing. Kanto watches, Her stomach is growing. As the baby is forming, In the girl's nest, Kanto is getting, Very depressed. Flash another three, And the girl has no clue, Of what Kanto, Is planning to do. He has an idea, But will not tell, He looks towards the future, And he can see hell. Flash to the day, The baby is born. The father sees him, As no more than a thorn. With his plan, He has little doubt, As he sees it, As the only way out. Flash back to the house, Where the girlfriend's asleep. He snatches the baby, Without even a peep. He holds it close, Up to his chest, As he goes to the garage, To do what's best. He places the new-born, Onto a stool, As he searches the shelves, For a blunt tool. He finds a hammer, As tears roll down his face. He knows that it's time, This is the place. He realizes now, There is no turning back. Then what was heard next, Was a sickening crack. The crack was the hammer, And the child's head. Kanto has done it, The baby is dead. He dumped the child, Into the trash. He left the city, In a Flash. Flash to the present, Where he is to blame. He can never look, At himself the same. For fleeing the scene, Along with his wife, He punishes himself daily, With his flesh and a knife. Because everyday, He ponders that sin. Don't expect him to speak, Of the matter again. Flash to darkness. Welcome. It seems this darkness belongs to Kanto. Let's tune in, shall we? Today. September eleventh. Seven years back. A time people dwell upon. Innocent suvillians had their lives taken. The U.S. feared for their safety. The country was in chaos. And how did we respond? We took suvillians. We made the middle-east fear for their safety. We brought the chaos. Revenge... isn't it a sweet taste? Pictures begins to flash across the screen. Flash. The twin towers, burning to rubble. Flash. The Pentagon, up in smoke. Flash. Osama Bid Laden, smiling. Who here... is truly the bad guy? Flash. A strand of bombs falling out of a plane, onto a village below. Flash. A pile of Muslim soldiers, deceased, stacked atop one another. Flash. George W. Bush, smiling. To think we are the righteous one in this situation, would be ignorant and incorrect. For we are both evil nations. And I love it. What a great world we live in. War. The answer to global problems. Flash. An American soldier, clinching his chest, looking onto the heavens. Flash. A member of Al Qaeda face down in a pool of his own blood. Yes, I support the troupes. But not just the Americans. ALL soldiers. For they fill me with envy. How I wish I could kill for a living. It seems oh so simple, and ever so much fun. But... To pick a side... Who could I choose? On one side, we have men who use high-tech equipment, and up-to-date machinery, to slaughter the enemies with ease. The other, using wit and insane comicazi tactics to get their point across. I would no doubt sway with the more cruel side. But tell me, won't you? Which side would that be? Flash. The proclaimed terrorists, capturing soldiers for torture. So twisted. Flash. Americans, pumping the land for oil, keeping the supply for their own selfish ways. Such greed. No need to stop this war. In fact, let it grow. Let it spread world-wide. Forget McCain. Forget Obama. Vote Kanto, as the supreme ruler. Flash. A grenade is seen flying through the air. Flash. From the opposite direction, comes a pipe bomb.
