MWE 24/7 — 08-09 PPV PR Archives

Focusing

Feb 12 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
OOC: Alright, so, my computer fries as I am finishing up my first RP and I am like fuck fuck fuck! Well, this was because of me not saving my work or doing it earlier, and we had 60 mile per hour winds. So, my internet is out for majority of today. Well, about an hour and a half ago, I get on, and rather than not posting, I made this. It is a little over 3000 words and I thought it was pretty good for 1 1/2 hours. I hope, if nothing else, I can at least get a nod of approval for going through all this work, and I am sorry I didn't give you better competition Sinc. Anyway, my RP: Mason: Madison Square Garden is an arena that has held many historical bouts. Championship boxing matches, Stanley Cup Finales, NBA Championship games, and they have even held MWE events before. And out of all these great sports, performances, and moments, there lingers a feeling...an atmosphere that only this arena can give off. By using this arena for Friday Night Overdrive, we are solidifying our legacy Sinclair Mercier. This is our third match and with the help of Madison Square Garden, we will put on a show fit for only kings. We will take each other to the limits and push us as hard as we have ever pushed before. Without a doubt, our match will be match of the night. *Mason is sitting in the backstage area of Madison Square Garden, by himself in the locker room. It is hot, with no ventilation, and he is sweating, but only slightly. He moves his head around, looking at a place he would return to in two days, and smiles. He then puts his head back down, as he drapes the Mayhem Wrestling Entertainment United States Championships across both of his hands. The sweat on his forhead is slim, but enough to form a bead that falls onto the title. Mason smiles as it falls to the gold of the belt and hears it smash the floor.* Mason: When we clash in two nights, it will be greater than any match you or I have ever been in. It will mean more than a United States Championship match. It will be bigger than the main event and it will surpass any of our previous bouts. Friday Night Overdrive will be a great event...not just for me...not just for you...but for everyone, as they will witness a bout that will shake the foundation of MWE, redefining what a ladder match really is. *Mason stands up, places the MWE Championship over his shoulder and walks out of the hot, windowless room. He is now in the backstage area, but all is quite. The normal hustle and bustle of events is not present right now, as the reigning MWE United States Champion walks through the backstage area alone. He stops, dead in his tracks as he takes a deep breath and looks around in this change of setting.* Mason: Right now, not a soul is in the building, besides me of course. There is no activity of backstage politics. There are no screaming lucky fans. There are not interviewers trying to get a word...well, at least not right now. And even without that atmosphere, there is still an evident presence about. There is this feeling, that no matter what the event or who the crowd may be, that the show will be terrific, at the very least. This building, with so many memories, gives the impression that win, lose, or draw you will go out there and wrestle or play the hardest you ever had. It gives you that sense that despite anything you might have done, all that really matters is the match that you have that night. And I can feel that presence. I can feel it just as clearly as I could feel a cold wind come over me. It is clear as day and it makes me warm, because I know that my match will be epic...win...lose...or draw. *Mason continues to parade around the splendorous backstage area, and as he does so, he admires everything he sees. The pictures on the wall, the paint job and murals of famous people and bands that have been here before him. He admires everything, down the the pattern of brick, noticing how picture perfect it is. Words can not describe the awe Mason really is in right now, as it far surpasses the sight he saw out of his hotel room weeks ago. He just continues to walk though, throughout the entire backstage area. It looks as though he is making mental notes, but it all stops when he gets to the curtain. The dark, black, ominous curtain. As Mason stares at it, it stares back, and even though it does not have eyes or a nose, despite it not having a mouth or words to express itself, it stares back with more intensity than any human could ever have. And Mason takes it, looking at this curtain with great passion.* Mason: You see, this curtain is more than just a curtain. It is a divider. This curtain splits the world in two, because you are a different person, without a doubt, when you step through it. Right here is where you make the decision of whether you are going to win...or lose. You decide whether you have courage and intensity or if you are a coward and weak. You can either walk through this curtain pumped up, energetic, confident, and cocky...or you can walk through it nervous, unsure, and a bit gloomy. The choice is that of the individual, but it does make the difference, believe me. Now, Sinclair, we have both done this countless times before, occasionally through this curtain, but this time it is different. Far different. *Mason looks at his United States Championship that he has been caring by his side this whole time. He flips it up and over his shoulder, so perfectly that you can see the nameplate that reads, "Mason Resoan." It glows, almost as if it has an aura, and in all reality, it simply rests on Mason's shoulder.* Mason: See, this time when we make it to this spot, we have to think about the rest of our career's. After this match, our lives will change, either for the better or for the worse, depending on your outlook. You see, because after this match, there a turning point in which you realize what you want to do. You realize that if you win, you will have reached twenty high feat in the air to grasp a title belt. You will have realized that you beat your opponent so bad, they could not even stop you. You will realize that you either tried your best or not hard enough. It will all happen so fast...but one way or another, you will have an Epiphany. A real Epiphany. Joey Dallas, he is a man of many words, most of which are propaganda to get you to believe that he is better than he actually is. He is a man who lies, cheats, and steals and lectures people about hypocrisy, even though he himself, is a hypocrite. He is man, who with his words, could sell ice to the Eskimos, and you have to have strong will to fight back. And the reason I brought him up, was because he gave you a false Epiphany. He told you things that were not real, and you bought into it, trying to find a way out. Well, the thing you realized last Monday is that there is only one real way out...and that is if you do it yourself. And that is exactly...exactly what you will do this Friday. You will, by yourself, find your own destiny and realize what you are meant for. This is more than a match, it is a career changer. *Mason has his ever evident cocky grin on his face as he looks still into the black curtain. As it stares back, it becomes to much and he turns away for a second, catching his breath and taking a break.* Mason: And it all happens right here...in front of this curtain. And as you make this choice and your music begins to play, you have to cross over into another world. You will change into a completely different Sinc Mercier. There is a Sinc Mercier that has real problems and faces tough decisions everyday...and then there is the Sinc Mercier that entertains the world with his mic skills and athletic ability. And the difference between the two is that the latter has no problems and will only have one objective...win. But, like I said, it all happens before you cross this barrier. If you cross confident and cocky, then maybe you will win...but if you enter as you have the last couple of weeks...you will get nothing. *Mason switches the shoulder in which his championship belt rests and turns to the curtain once more. With a look of determination, he puts a hand on the curtain, and gets some chills he backs up, looks around, and gets serious. He then walks through the curtain, throwing it to the side as if it is nothing and looks out to a completely empty arena.* *He is looking extremely well, in perfect physical condition as he six-pack chest heaves in and out with his heaving breathing. He looks around the empty arena and puts a hand to his forehead, looking around like a telescope. He holds the title high above his head and the atmosphere is wild, bursting with energy. He walks down the ramp, cocky and arrogantly as he rolls under the bottom rope, championship first. He poses on the top rope, stretches with the title, as he poses his long black Canadian Classic wrestling trunks. He holds the title high above his head and smiles as he places the gold on the floor.* He jogs over to the top rope to his left and simply sits on top of it.* Mason: Now, that is what an entrance should be like. Sinc, when you watch this, think about it...you could feel the energy...the excitement...the adrenaline rush even though no one is there. And that is what Madison Square Garden does. It gives you that rush and the burst of energy...something you should really learn to control. If you don't, I won't blame you, but let me remind you of one thing...when there is a winner...there is also a loser. Why wouldn't you want that boost? *Mason is sweating once again, but simply runs his hands through his hair to wipe the sweat away as he hops down from the turnbuckle. He is now standing over his title, his cocky grin stretching ear to ear.* Mason: And this is a match that you don't want to lose. Like I said, this is a career changer...for the better or worse...and I don't think you could handle another loss, so pay attention. Use MSG to your advantage...take control of that boost and make sure you are the most focused you have ever been, because you will need it. Put it this way, I remember that beat down that you and Dallas gave me a couple of weeks ago...and I am not going to let that slip. I am focused. And let me tell you this...don't think that you can beat me off of revenge alone...don't use your hatred towards Dallas against me...use your hatred towards me against me. Focus. Use everything that you have, because the thing that I have in common with ladders is that we are both unforgiving. I will take you and throw you...I will crush you under the ladder and I will make you pay for that beat down. Focusing is something that I know how to do and I know what I am going to do to you already, but you need to realize that you can't be so unsure of yourself 15 feet in the air...because I will break you down and throw you out. I will destroy you with the ladder, I will beat you with ladder, and then I will win with the ladder. Why? Because I am focused! *Mason walks over to the ring ropes, places a hand on the top turnbuckle, and launches himself over the ropes, landing on the apron. He walks down the staircase and under the ring, he pulls a ladder. It is 15 feet long and he slides it into the ring, hitting his championship in the process. He rolls in after the ladder and picks it up, places it again on the ropes and sits back down on the top turnbuckle.* Mason: This, right here, is what we will have to deal with. 15 foot high ladders of destruction...of pure unforgiving steel that can, if we want it to, make each other bleed. It is possibly one of the most painful weapons under the ring. Fall from it, and get hurt. Get hit with it, and get hurt. Hit someone with it, and get hurt. It is heavy...it is mean...it is cold...and it hurts. Sinc, have you ever experienced a ladder match before? Well, I have, and out of experience I will tell you that very few matches even come close to being more painful. And that is why you must be focused...get hit with this and it is as good as done. *Mason hops off the turnbuckle, picks the ladder up, and demonstrates how fast it can be swung. He then places it back down and hops back up on the turnbuckle.* Mason: See that...it is like being hit by horse kick. It can knock you out, break a bone, or give you a concussion...all in one blow if used right. And that is just one swing...imagine two or even three. Imagine having it dropped on you from ten feet. Imagine falling on it from ten feet. Imagine your arm in it as a steel chair rips it apart...sound familiar? *Mason puts his hand on his arm and shakes in disgust. He now has a new found intensity in his eyes as he continues.* Mason: Last week, I said that I had changed. I stated that I was better than I have ever been before and I proved it as I continued to be undefeated. I beat you and Dallas, both individually and as a team and now we are back to one on one. After a win like that, I can only go up...and that is why you have to take in account that each week, much like you, I get better. Stronger, faster, quicker, and more athletic. I become more tone and I gain intelligence and ring awareness. I become that much closer to being called a wrestling king, because as of right now, that is what my record is implying. And, if there is one thing that I can not preach enough, it is the fact that history repeats itself...yada yada yada...same old song and dance, I know, but listen. That has been your number one mistake...not listening. If, for one second, you could get your head out of your a** and pay attention, then maybe...just maybe you could beat me. But, listening isn't you deal...is it? *Mason takes a short breather and laughs at his own joke.* Mason: But in all seriousness, if you would pay attention and listen, then you would realize what you have been doing wrong. You see, there are certain things in which you rely on emotion. Relationships being one...but not wrestling. When you wrestle, you rely on practice and hard-work...and some natural talent. You base your attack off of things you know and things you have seen. When you paint, you rely on your creativity to take you to your next project. When you do paperwork, you rely on your intelligence to guide you through the entire concept and hopefully make it out without to much pain. It is written in stone, yet you continue to let your emotions control you. Take control Sinc...focus...and then maybe you could beat me. But, as of right now, not even in my darkest hour would I allow you to beat me. There are only a few things that could save you from your inevitable loss...one of those being able to control yourself in and out of the ring. Which just goes back to what I said earlier...when you cross the curtain, your morale and mood will effect your performance in the ring. You, unlike a lot of people, allow yourself to let your emotions from the real world into your mood before a match, and then you turn everything into a battle of emotions. *Mason once more drops from the turnbuckle and goes down to the middle of the ring and picks the title up, placing it on his shoulder.* Mason: And I see it...I watch it unfold in your matches. At first, you start off strong dominating your opponent every which way. Then, they make one offensive move and you loose security and fall apart. You let yourself go and start this battle with yourself. And I don't want to hear any BS about not being able to control it, because you are far past puberty. You have grown up and you know how to, you just choose not to. We, despite me wanting to say, are very alike in our physical qualities, and by utilizing those, I can control myself...so you can to. *Mason sets up a ladder next to the turnbuckle, climbs to top, drapes his feet over and sits there instead.* Mason: But, I am finished here. I am finished with you and I need no more to talk about this match. Yes, it will be epic. Yes it will be amazing, but to talk on it anymore would be to ramble. So, with that... *Mason slides the ladder down, moves it to the center of the ring and climbs back up. He hangs the Mayhem Wrestling Entertainment United States Championship high above the ring and hops down.* Mason: Let us put on a show to never forget and hopefully...you will be more of a challenge this time. Sinc Mercier v.s. Mason Resoan III at FNOD will, without a doubt, be match of the night. *Mason takes a breath, turns his back, and begins to leave.* Mason: And if ya didn't know...now ya know! *And with that, Mason leaves the ladder and his MWE United States title in the ring until Friday Night Overdrive.*
