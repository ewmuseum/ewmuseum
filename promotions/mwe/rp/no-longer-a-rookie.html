No Longer A Rookie; Zack (C)rash RP

Nov 19 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Kent: I don’t want to do this! Wayne: Oh come on! Don’t be such a baby! CSB: Yeah man! Live a little! The roar of the plane’s engines were so loud that the small group of people huddled aboard had to nearly shout to be heard above its loudness. As the plane climbs upward, everyone’s ears being to pop due to the pressure. A few of them even tried in vain to relieve the annoying pressure in their ears with wide, open-mouth yawns. Outside, the land grows smaller and smaller until the horizon blends into a mess of color. Clouds encompass the aircraft on all sides, and looking through the window, you’d feel as if you’re flying through heaven. Our scene begins in a place that might evoke a feeling of déjà vu. And rightfully so. A tight metallic cargo space in the back of a small plane. A few wooden crates are stacked here or there, and one large, momentarily closed hatch door, that seems both foreboding and oddly tempting. Not so different from the scene where we first met Zack Crash. How long has it been since Zack’s debut? (* For more, see “A Thrilling Promo” in the RP archives) So much has happened. And he has grown so much in that short time. But I digress. We have more important things to attend to than sentimental nostalgia. In the back of the plane, we immediately recognize the images of Kent Bishop, Wayne Prez, and the MWE Champion CSB, all of them in the process of getting into jumpsuits. (Note: CSB went to extra lengths to make sure he got sleeveless jumpsuit) Kent looks out the window, and his expression immediately drops to panic stricken once he gets a glimpse of how dangerously high they are. He backs away from the window, as if afraid he might dropped through it, swallowing deep breathes. Kent: That’s what I’m trying to do. “Live” a “little” bit longer by NOT jumping out of this plane! CSB gave him an exasperated while he continued to put on his gear. CSB: You’re a wimp, you know that? Wayne: Yeah PussPuss. Just jump with us. It’ll be fun. Kent was feeling so nervous that he didn’t even remember to ostracize Wayne for using that nickname he so hated. Instead, he grounded himself safely onto one of the plane’s seats, crossing his arms with determined finality. His body was shaking so badly it look like he might vibrate his bones off their sockets. Kent: Jumping out of a plane flying thousands of feet in the air does not sound like fun to me. More like suicide. Wayne: Oh, so I guess that’s what this is about. You’re afraid of jumping out of a plane? Kent: YES!!! I am! I am afraid of jumping out of a plane. What about that sounds so odd to you people? CSB: Because it sounds totally awesome and cool! Wayne: Exactly! We’ll be flying like Superman…only instead of super powers, we have gravity. Kent buried his face in one palm, shaking his head in mild disgust, his voice intoning a feeling of vexation. Kent: Of all the people I could hang out with, I pick to hang out with a bunch of crazies. CSB: Some friend you are. Zack offers to pay for us to all go skydiving with him, and you can’t even show a little appreciation. Right Zack? Chris turns for a response, but doesn’t find Zack at his side. For a moment, the three of them are confused, and their heads begin to twist in a daze to locate him. Soon, they spot him in the far back of the plane, sitting in a chair, fully dressed and ready for jump with his elbows resting on his knees. With his back hunched over so far, you couldn’t see his expression. But then again, no of them needed to see it. Because when you sit like that, it can only mean one of two things: either he was getting cold feet (which was less than unlikely)…or he was deep in thought. And judging from how hunched over he was, and the fact that he hadn’t said a word since takeoff, he probably wasn’t pondering about fluffy pink bunnies or other happy thoughts. The others put their insignificant squabble on hold and walked back toward Zack, who was so deeply immersed in his own world he didn’t even seem to hear them approach with the clanking of their shoes against the metal floor. Even when they stood directly in front of him, their feet and shadows now surely in his peripheral vision, he still didn’t look up. Looking down at him, the others thought he looked so isolated. CSB: Hey? You ok? At this, Zack finally look up. His expression was somewhat hard to decipher. It looked somewhere between depressed and lost, not entirely one or the other. If anything, he looked more introspective, as if struggling inside his head with some silent dilemma that had no solution. Zack: What? Zack replied faintly, trying to mask his emotions, making it look as if he was simply daydreaming. The others either shook their heads or shot him with raised eyebrows. If there was one thing Zack wasn’t good at, it was hiding his emotions. The man was practically a giant billboard that said: “I’m feeling down. Please talk to me.” CSB: What do you mean “what”?! CSB sounded completely appalled at the flat out, and painfully obvious lie. CSB: This was you’re idea to come up here, and you’ve hardly said a word since takeoff. What’s wrong? Zack turned away, refusing to look at any of them directly. Zack: Nothings wrong. I’m just tired is all. Wayne: Has anyone ever told you should never play poker? At this, Zack actually turned to look at Wayne with a sidelong glance, taken aback at how this question could have any possible significance on their current conversation. Zack: No. Why? Wayne: Because you’re a terrible liar. You got no poker face man. Zack turned away, the sides of his face flushing an embarrassed scarlet. If any of them needed more confirmation that Zack wasn’t doing alright, they certainly had it now. Now, they weren’t going to let up. Pilot: We’ll be at the drop zone in a few minutes! Get ready for standby! Called the pilot from the front half of the plane, but nobody paid him even the faintest attention. They were keeping their attention fixed on Crash with quiet stares that told him they weren’t going anywhere until he fessed up. Crash sighed in his defeat and turned his head away again, as if ashamed of what he was about to say next. Zack: It’s just what Hunt said to me a couple of days ago has been bothering me. That’s all. There was a moments silence (apart from the rushing wind outside and the plane’s humming engine) where Crash half expected some response from the others, but received none. He chanced a quick side-glance, only to find the others now staring at him with new looks. Their looks of worry and concern, now appeared to have transformed into looks of disappointment. CSB: Is THAT all that’s bothering you? Wayne: Man, what a rip. Kent: Seriously man. Crash didn’t know whether to feel surprised or offended at their blatant mocking of his problems. Soon, Zack could feel his face being to blush. Whether this way due to embarrassment or fury, he didn’t know. Zack: What do you mean “is THAT all that’s bothering me”? Wayne: Dude, we thought you were going to tell us something interesting was bothering you. Like, I don’t know, you got herpes or something. Everyone shot Wayne a quick disgusted look. Zack Crash with herpes? Now picture that image and try to get to sleep tonight. Zack: Ok first, that’s disgusting. And second, I’m sorry my problems aren’t interesting enough for you. Kent: Don’t start getting emo on us again now. We’re just trying to help. Zack crossed his arms and turned away from them, looking very childish as he did so. Zack: I don’t think laughing at me is very helpful. CSB: Dude, we weren’t laughing at you. Just at how you’re reacting to this. Zack: And what’s so funny about it? CSB look at Zack with a strained look. It was clear he was trying to keep his temper even, trying to understand that Zack must still be under a lot of stress with his more than troubled history with Oblivion coming back to haunt him. And now with Hunt there at his apartment to taunt him must have only added fuel to the fire. But even still, Zack’s attitude was starting to get tiresome. Even still, CSB kept his voice cool and even. He took the seat next to Crash, looking him dead in the face to where he could see his own reflection in Crash’s emerald green eyes. CSB: Well for starters, the fact that you’re letting a little shit like Hunt get under your skin like this. You’re better than that man. And also the fact that you’re still worried about this whole Oblivion thing. Zack: Why wouldn’t I be worried? CSB: Hell man, you beat the guy at Turmoil. I thought you were over this. What, you afraid you won’t be able to do it again? Zack: No, that’s not it. CSB: Then what? Zack looked down at his feet, twiddling with his thumbs, looking as if he was struggling to find the words to express what exactly he was feeling right now. His face was scrunched up, and every moment appeared to cause him a great deal of his inner fire that was his trademark both in and out of the ring. It didn’t even look as if Zack himself fully understood what his emotions on the subject were. The others were silent a moment, giving him the time he needed to gather his thoughts. Zack: It’s just…after Turmoil, I was hoping this would all be behind me. That it’d be over and done with. But now, I’m starting to think this might follow me forever. I mean, I know my mistake will follow me forever, and I made peace with that. But what worries me is Oblivion following me like this forever. I can’t keep looking over my shoulder, wondering if he might randomly attack me for the rest of my career. Silence hung over the group of friends like a thick fog, filling them all up completely. Nobody had any words of comfort to offer Zack for a long while. Even Wayne seemed to have no clever quips to help lighten the gloomy discussion. Surprisingly enough, it was Kent who spoke first, getting down to one knee to look Zack in the face as he hung his head low in a mixture of shame and self-pity. Kent: Look, you and I have talked about this before. But let me ask you something, you did beat Oblivion once before right? Zack: Yeah. Kent: Ok, then you can surely beat him again. And if he comes back for more after that, then kick his ass again, and again, and again, and again until he gets the damn message. What’s so hard to understand about that? The wrinkles lining Zack forehead relaxed slightly as he processed this bit of advice. Zack seemed to be taking it well, as he began sitting up straight, the lines around his lips forming to what might be considered a smile soon. But Kent wasn’t done just yet. Kent: Whatever this “other thing” is that happened between you and Oblivion is, if you don’t want to talk about it, nobody here is going to force you. But sitting here feeling sorry guilty or afraid isn’t going to help any. And who cares what Hunt says or thinks? If he knew anything, he’d be the X-TV Champ instead of you. So just get over it, have some fun, and worry about all that stuff later. Like wave of cool refreshing water washing over him, the worry that had weighted itself on Zack’s shoulders for the past weeks was gone. Zack’s body never felt so light before, and wasn’t sure what to say or do to thank his friend. It seemed Kent was proving to be a source of great emotional support lately. So Zack contented to turn and Kent, giving him a friendly pat on the shoulder and a grateful smile was all he could offer. CSB and Wayne stared blankly at Kent, surprised by his sudden public speaking skills. CSB: That was…pretty impressive man. Wayne: Yeah! Good job there PussPuss. Wayne held out his hand for Kent to high-five, but Kent’s face was turning a bright scarlet red. With one swift motion, he jumped out of his and lunged for Wayne, who just barely managed to get out of harms way in time. Soon, Kent had begun chasing Wayne around the back of the plane. Kent: I SAID NOT TO CALL ME THAT!!! Everyone (with the exception of Kent) enjoyed a good hearty laugh, and for the first time since Zack didn’t know when, he felt completely like his old self. No worries or concerns about a future he couldn’t control or change…only the present. And he planned on living in that present to the fullest like he always did. Pilot: We’re nearly at the drop zone! Get yourselves ready back there! Kent stopped so sudden his feet slide across the metal floor a few inches. In all the commotion, he’d nearly forgotten about where they were, or why they were there in the first place. Kent looked around to find himself surrounded by the others, all staring at him with evil grins, their hands outstretched like claws. And soon, it dawned on him what they were planning. Kent: Don’t any of you even think about it! They did think about it. All three of them pounced on Kent and began dragging him to the hatch. They threw it open, and a huge blast of wind smattered them. Kent: NO! STOP! SERIOSULY, THIS ISN’T FUNNY! Wayne: Yes it is. CSB: Don’t forget to pull your cord. Pilot: First jump…NOW! And with that, they hurled Kent out of the plane, listening to his girl-like scream carry above the wind. Zack looked out as Kent disappeared into the clouds below, enjoying every minute of Kent’s tumble. Pilot: Next jump get ready! It was Crash’s turn now. He took a moment to look out at the expansive view before him, breathing it into him. And as he stared out at that beautiful sight, he saw not only an endless scope of land. What he saw was, much like when we first saw him jump out of a plane, the infinite possibilities laid out before him on the horizon. And it was all waiting for him to take it. He bent his knees, waiting for the words he knew were to come next. Pilot: Second jump…NOW! And so he did. This is Zack Crash in the rush of the moment. After a long free fall, and a brief moment of hovering in his parachute, Crash finally lands on solid ground with enough force to buckle his knees and send him into a tuck n’ roll on the ground. He instantly uses that roll to right himself up onto both feet, untangling his parachute from his back. He drops in on the ground, brushes himself off, and begins pacing up and down. His blood is pumping red-hot adrenaline through him now, so powerful it is like an unknown force that can’t even keep his body still. It’s so much more potent than usual it almost startles him. He feels almost like he can’t control this rush he’s feeling, and he needs to utilize it into a useful outlet. He looks up and finally sees another body falling in toe not far from him. As he watches the person release themselves from their parachute after landing, Crash finally notices that it’s Wayne. Perfect timing. He rushes toward him, his steps long and robust. Zack: Get the camera out now! Zack demands of him. Wayne glances over at Crash, both shocked at the order and slightly worn out from the rushing of his own body, so it takes him a while to respond. Wayne: Huh…what? Zack: Camera. I need to do my promo while I still got this rush. Hurry up, hurry up! Wayne smiles to himself as he now understands what Zack is requesting. It may be a little different than what he’s used to, but he’s always ready for a good ol’ verbal smackdown at the expense of others. He reaches into his discarded backpack and pulls out his trusty camcorder. Yes, he brought it on the plane, and yes he didn’t break it on the fall, so get off my back, this is my story and it’ll go the way I want it. Wayne: Ok dude…let it roll! Wayne says eagerly as the little light flashes red. The exact same color Zack feels within himself right now. He takes a deep, focusing breath, and begins. Zack: Jay Riddick at Crowning Glory…DC & GM on MNM…GM on MNM…Zak Warner on MNM…Oblivion at Turmoil…and The Virus on MNM. What do all these people have in common? Well they have many things in common actually. They’re all talented athletes, all of them driven and motivated to succeed, and they’re all vicious competitors who demand and garner respect. Some are psychos, some are legends, and some are even former or current champions. Wayne: They’re all also members of the Hannah Montana Fan Club. I checked. But most importantly, they’re all members of a very prestigious and continually growing list. And that list, is the list of men I’ve laid down at my feet broken and bloodied. Despite all their talk, despite all their insults, all their telling me I was too big for my shoes, despite all their prophesizing that I would cause my own demise, despite all their assurance that THEY were the better athlete, here I am…still undefeated. And yet with all this, there feels as if there’s something missing. Zack can still feel and hear the rush of the wind around him. He almost feels as if he is still freefalling through the surge of energy that runs through his veins as if it were the very blood that pumps blood into his heart. And now, as he focuses his words, he can feel that rush, that energy starting to center itself as he grabs it by the neck and directs it back at his opponents. With all these accomplishments, with me being the most talked about X-TV Champ in the belts long history, I’m still hearing myself referred to as a “rookie.” Well that’s all well in good, but there needs to come a time when you realize that the future isn’t sometime down the road, it’s right here, right NOW, staring you dead in the face. Wayne: Yeah, and the future really could use a Mentos. So people have been asking me, “Zack, what’s next?” Well let me tell you what’s next. I plan on continuing my climb to the top and beyond. I plan on having my winning streak continue for a very long time. I plan on becoming a World Champion. But most importantly of all, I plan on proving to the MWE, and the world that Zack Crash is no longer a rookie. That he isn’t the next David Whitman or Sinc Mercier. He is Zack Crash, and he is NOW! Zack’s heart is pounding so hard against his ribs it hurts. But he fights through the pain, because this is the moment. This is when Zack feels the most alive; when he feels the biggest rush he’s ever felt in his life. Every jab is like climbing the face of a mountain, every counter the equivalent of flying through heaven, and every promo the equal amount of ten…no, twenty adrenaline rushes put together. Does that make him a junkie? You bet your sorry ass it does. But that’s precisely what makes him so damn good at what he does. And all that begins this week on MNM where, good news for all of you out there, you get to see the debut of Crash Saint Blaise. You may now mark out to your hearts content. Wayne: FINALLY! WOOOOO! What can I say about my partner CSB. Not only is he one of the best wrestlers I’ve ever known, but he more importantly happens to be one of the best men I’ve ever known. As wrestler, he’s probably more accomplished than I am. Hell, he just may be the most accomplished MWE wrestler ever! I mean think about it. My first belt here was the X-TV Title I carry to this day. CSB…World Champ. In the time it took guys like Sinc Mercier to get to the main event, I did in half the time. And in what time it took me, CSB did in half of THAT. I hear all this talk about how fast I’M rising up the ladder here when, if you ask me, HE deserves it probably much more than I do. But the true measure of CSB isn’t in his accomplishments in the ring, rather than outside the ring. He’s the type of man who sticks to what he believes in and who doesn’t take no shit from nobody. And most importantly of all, he’s a family who I know has an undying love for his son, and who would do anything for him. So CSB, I just want you to know how much I respect you as a wrestler, and how much more I respect you as a person. I consider it an honor to have you as my partner, but an even greater honor to have you as my friend. Wayne: No homo. Wayne’s comment doesn’t derail Zack for even the slightest second. Adrenaline rushes have a way of giving you what some may call “tunnel vision.” Basically it’s when you can only see straight ahead of you. You don’t look back and you don’t let anything distract you. Complete, and utter concentration so deep it’s as if the whole world has been shut out. Which is more than I can say for either of our opponents. Both of whom, I might add, I’ve beaten before. But here, we find ourselves in an odd situation. You see, it was supposed to be K-Hard and Warner versus me and CSB. But apparently, K-Hard’s balls finally dropped off, so he freaked out and went home crying, his body now producing enough estrogen to restart the feminist movement. It’s funny, because when I first heard DC was our new opponent, the first thing that popped into my mind was…WHAT THE HELL DOES DC STAND FOR ANYWAY?! So I went and did my research, and now look no further, for I have an answer for you all. It’s rather ironic actually, but it stands for both Dick and Cunt. Wayne: Ouch! Harsh man, really harsh! Zack speaks his words, and to him, they turn into venom that poisons his adversaries. And his poison seeps into the consciousness of his opponents, and in the quiet of there minds, his poisonous words reveal to them a deadly truth about Zack Crash. That no matter what they say or do, there is nothing that’ll keep this man down. At least, not for very long anyway. DC, you have got to be one of the biggest hypocrites I’ve ever seen in my life. All you ever talk about lately is how you hate MWE, or how you’re against management, or some other anti-establishment “something I stole from a Green Day song” nonsense. And yet, here you are, still wrestling in the company you claim to despise. Half the time I can’t even understand what the hell you’re saying you contradict yourself so much. That’s the real reason management sent you on that vacation. So they could use the quiet time to try and figure out what the hell you were saying. Wayne: Yeah! Chris has actually has been using DC’s promos to help put Cian to sleep. Works like a charm. All that you have to say is like everything else most people here have to say…just the same old, recycled out bullshit because nobody can think of anything new or interesting to talk about. And all this talk about competition? Please! That’s just your brain in self-defense mode to protect that fragile ego of yours thinking your MWE’s Mr. Hot Shit. But do you remember who it was that beat you and GM in that tag match not long after CG. It was me and Eric Derf, two of the future superstars of MWE. Scratch that, along me us and CSB, we ARE the future of MWE. And that’s what this is really about DC. You’re old news DC. People care more about the younger guys like me, Derf, and CSB more than they care about you. Face it man. Your time is over. And my…our time is now! With DC’s pride tore into pieces so small it’s practically nonexistent, Zack redirects his rush at the other opponent. He turns his head slightly to the right, as if he can see Warner’s just over Wayne’s shoulder. And Zack speaks those words as if Warner was right there listening to him. And maybe, just maybe, his words can in fact reach beyond distance and find Warner wherever he may be. And lastly, the man who gives GWC a bad name Zak Warner. Just hearing that makes me wanna change my name to something else. You know after our match I was kinda hoping I’d inspire you to do something about yourself. That I’d maybe kick the cobwebs out of that stoned dead brain of yours. But no! You’re still as stagnant as when we last faced off. I mean, is there even a reason why you’re still here. Hell, DC may have his ass where his mouth should be, but at least he’s motivated. Wayne: I always wondered why DC’s breath smelled like ass. But you, you do it just to get a paycheck. You do it not because you’re passionate about the business, but because it’s your job. And there’s the problem. If you want to succeed here, you can’t look at it as something you do because you’re obligated to do because of some contract that’s probably paying you more money than you deserve. You do it because you’re passionate about it, because you love it. Persistence trumps talent my fine-feathered friend. It doesn’t matter how talented you are, if you have no passion, then you’re just a waste of God given space. Which is why you’ll never beat me. Because I’m hungrier and more passionate than you are. And if you think I’m wrong, then I dare you to prove it. Because I still have hope that, somewhere under that you are now, there still lives the old Zak Warner. You still have a chance in joining us to become the future of MWE. Otherwise, at least stay out of our way. Because if you don’t…well use your imagination. Zack’s heart rate slows, his breathing becomes heavy, and the last tingles of the rush now starts to fade into fatigue. But he has to get one final shot in before it’s gone. So Zack steels himself for what is probably the most important part of the promo. To quote a movie, “the only thing that matters is the ending. And this one, is very good” Oh, and before I forget, I know you’re watching this Oblivion. So while I have your attention, allow me to inform you that you no longer have any control over me. My friends have shown me that I have no reason to fear you, or the past you keep holding over my head. You got nothing on me. Because I refuse to let the past control me, unlike you. All I got left to say to you in that regard is the same thing I said last time…GET…OVER…IT! You want a rematch so bad? Hell, I want a rematch! So if you want me, come on over and get me! The die cast, the stage is set, and nothing left to do but what for the uncertain future. But the funny thing more about the “tunnel vision” the rush provides is that, if you look hard enough, sometimes even in the darkness you can see the end of the tunnel and beyond into the daylight. This is Zack Crash in the rush of the moment. They call this the NU Generation of MWE, but we’ve yet to see that nu generation. But we have yet to see that generation step up. Right now, it’s still the old generation hogging the spotlight at to themselves. Wayne: *snickers* I get it now. It’s NEW spelled with an N and a U. Well this MNM, we’re going to see that new generation. This week we’ll see a passing of the torch, or spotlight in this case. DC, you wanna talk about factions and takeovers. We get ready, because one is certainly coming your way. Because when Crash Saint Blaise steps between those ropes, we’ll show you and the entire world that the old guard is dead. And the new guard, will finally begin its takeover. We’re no longer rookies. We are the M…W…E!!! Wayne: And…CUT! With the last of the adrenaline rush finally leaving him, Zack begins to feel the weight of fatigue slowly creeping up upon him like a snake. He bends over onto his knees, feeling as if he’d just run the 100 meter dash ten times over, all within a few minutes. His throat is raw and he could really use a bottled water right now. Wayne: Huh…dude? Wayne says suddenly, sounding confused, even worried. Zack turns to look at Wayne and finds him staring blankly at something. So Zack follows his gazes and is confused himself for a moment that what was confusing him. All he saw was the vast expanse of green that seemed to go on into eternity that was the Amazon rain forest. Zack just stood there, admiring the serene beauty, but slowly, gravely he began to notice what was confusing Wayne. Or more accurately…what was missing. In the heat of the moment, he’d completely forgotten about Kent and CSB, who were know where in sight. Wayne: Where are the others? TO BE CONTINUED…
