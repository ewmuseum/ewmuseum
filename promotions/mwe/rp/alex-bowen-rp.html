Alex Bowen RP; Posting it for him.

Aug 9 2008

Alex Bowen

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
*The camera fades into Alex and David Bowen, setting behind a big news casters desk. David is in a suit and tie and a very horrible toupe, And is holding up a white cue card with MWE on it. Alex is setting with his legs proped up on the desk in a black tuxx shirt drinking a monster.* Alex- Well ladies and gents, I would like to welcome you to the channel four news room. But today its the juggalo haven. Today I'm here to talk to you about... *Alex points to Davids' first cue card, MWE.* Alex- Thats right ladies and gents. Mwe, the age of the burning legion. The hardcore juggalo, and one of the biggest men in pro wrestiling today. *Alex points to the camera and gives it a wink.* Alex- Its on you, the fans. And the people who pay our hefty saleries. *Alex claps his hands together and drinks the rest of his monster and tosses it to the back of the room. David begins to look through his cue cards and brings out the first one behind mwe. It reads Sonny Williams.* Alex- Sonny is in MWE? What the hell here?! We need to go to some video tapage. *Alex weels around in his chair and grabs a tv set and pops in a tape and grabs a remote.* Alex- Now guys this is gonna get a little bit scary. But look what I've done to sonny in the past. *Alex starts the video and it shows him beating up sonny after losing his EW Havoc title to Chris Cassidy. It then shows him throwing Damien Thorpe and Sonny into the grill of a brand new ford 2007 truck.* Alex- God I never get tired of seeing that shit. So sonny the gangster. Mr. 1 time mwe champ, I'm putting the stop to your bid for the second mwe title. Right now at crowning glory. *David throws the card that reads sonnys name behind him and brings up B.G Mancini.* Alex- The aleged gangster himself, blah blah blah. This and that, If I beat you are you gonna get daddy to put a hit on me? Shit dude I'll give you my address if you got the balls.... Whatever *David throws back the cue card and brings out Xavier Stone.* Alex- HAHA X is in here too?! *Alex starts to slap his hands on the desk infront of him in a fit of rage and laughter.* Alex- Xavier Stone huh? What are you gonna get little troy to wrestle me. OR are you gonna go death racers on my ass? How is the saying, You can burn me and shoot me but I'll never die! Haha a very true saying from a man in that movie... Shame he died like 20 seconds after he said it. You need to walk your ass back to that race track buddie. Because when your in the ring with the hardcore juggalo, You don't have a chance. Really... *Alex pulls out a wad of money out of his pocket.* Alex- Heres a grand buddie go use it for gas. Serously get of out the ring. Your washed up, go back to racing so you can set on your ass for a job. Because you definitely won't make it in MWE with me here. *Alex rolls his eyes and gives the thumbs up to david who pulls out his own name from the cue list.* Alex- David Bowen huh? Ware the hell is that dude when you need him. Ha hell I don't know ware he is but I've herd that fucker is HUGE.... Oh hell who the fuck am I kidding. *Alex reaches over and pulls up davids toupe. Points and laughs!* Alex- But anyways, Theres a small thing you should know mwe. Win or lose the Bowen brothers stick together. We have the upperhand in this match. You wanna know why? MWE allumni or not, none of you like each other. I'm prety much damn sure about that. Me and david have been tag champs before, and brothers all of our life. And we don't fight we don't bicker. We have the upper hand ON EVERYONE HERE. Trust me when the burning legion steap in this ring at crowning glory. One of us shall walk out the new MWE World champ. *David grabs his card and goes to throw it, but puts it in his lap and smiles instead, and brings up Alejandro Rodriguez's card.* Alex- Ahhhh the little man, the plague of society. Serously who the fuck let this guy in? *Alex stands up and puts his hand about neck high and shakes it as he talks.* Alex- You need to be this tall to ride ale. This tall dude, so why don't you just walk your little ass back home. Because I don't feel like wrestling a midget today, or tomorrow. So whatever. *David tosses alehandro's card to the back of the room and brings up David whitman.* Alex- Ahhh the loveable drunk. No one cares about a old washed up champ so he's out. *David tosses David whitman's card behind him and brings up Devilin Sharpe's card.* Alex- Ahh the speed demon of MWE. He's from awesomeville comon people. This kid really is on crack. Awsomeville?! Jesus dude come the fuck on are you really that stupid? Just no toss the card David please get it the hell out of here. *David tosses the card behind him and finally brings up Brian Michaels. Alex starts to laugh a little bit.* Alex- I have a little bit of a history with him and my buddie D.c.. Oh its damn true, D.c is the shit, But what really has brian done other than ride his coat tales for a few years? Mwe champ blah blah blah. This is gonna be eazy. You might of beat me in our x-innernet match in aew brian. But this time The hardcore JUGGALO WILL REIGN SUPREME OVER MWE! That the fucking bottom line to buddie.... Because I said so. *The cameras fade out with David throwing Brians' card behind him and Alex laughing*
