Be careful what you wish for

Jul 16 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Micheals Inc. *The scene opens inside Peter's loft in New York City. The outside weather is sunny and the time of day is 7:45 am eastern time. We see a tired Peter drag himself out of bed and walk past his open bathroom door. He passes by a stack of magazines knocked onto the floor and goes straight into his kitchen. He goes to his mini fridge and pulls out a corn muffin. He places it on the counter and he unwraps the thing that you put on the bottom of a muffin and cupcake. He grabs it with his right hand and takes a bite out of it. He lets out a sad sigh and looks down at the floor. he mutters in a lonely and depressed voice.* Peter: Taste just like hers. Damn. *He picks up the muffin and tosses it into the trash can with anger and hatred. He begins to stomp into his bedroom and he looks ready to pick a fight with someone, anyone. He sees no one in his room and he looks around at every little thing in the room. His attention is finally caught by a picture. He walks over to it quickly, you know how you walk sometimes when your angry. We can't see who is in the picture, but he seems very angry at whoever it it. He begins pointing his finger at the picture his eyes show fury and anger and his tone is able to match it.* Peter: This is all your FAULT! Why in the hell did you do it? WHY?!? Look at you now. Where are you now? No where that's RIGHT. You think you have success? You have nothing, absolutely nothing. The only thing I can applaud you for is how you use your pain to your advantage. That aside your PATHETIC! *He slaps the picture down and the frame breaks and the picture slides out, but before we can see who was in that picture an alarm goes off. He quickly realizes that T.O. will be here in a little less then ten minutes. He quickly gets changed and begins to walk out of his loft. He walks down the deserted hallway and decides to take the stairs today. He goes down the three flights of stairs and thinks to himself that it wasn't so bad. He walks into the lobby and he sees T.O.'s limo pull up and T.O. has big smile on his face. He walks over to the limo and him and T.O. do the ghetto hug thing where they shake hands and use the free arm to hug. both men quickly get into the limo. As always the mood is light when these two stars get together. T.O. starts off the conversation in his normal voice.* T.O.: Man can you believe that Joanna? I guess I am just like all of T.O.'s other teammates. Peter: Look T.O. I am sorry she was PMSing, but we both know we have to keep our cools at the place we are going to. T.O.: Bro you don't need to worry. My man I am calm, cool, and collected. These kids better have there popcorn ready. *The camera John interrupts. His tone is very snarky and kind of outline since he is a camera man.* John: You two doing charity work with kids? Everyone better have there popcorn ready. *Peter looks at John with anger. His eyes at there boiling point. Suddenly a smile comes across his face and he begins to laugh slightly. His tone is that of an arrogant person who knows that they are holding all of the cards.* Peter: Well John I am glad the you feel that way. Just to remind you that MWE told me that a camera man was optional for today. So I am going to do an option and fire your ass right now. Get the hell out of the window. *The limo stops and John is forced out of the limo on forty second street. Peter and T.O. continue there light hearted conversation.* Peter: Man this sunday I learned why you don't get along with your teammates or partners. It's because they are all incompetent and you end up doing all of the work. T.O.: I know that man. Jeff was no started, Donovan was a sissy, and Romo put big cock in his mouth every December and January. Hopefully this Edwards kid can keep up. If not his time is up. Peter: I know what you mean man. When me and Scotty's match ended his ass was on the ground and I was the one on top of the ladder holding up the briefcase. I was the one who won the match not him. It gets frustrating having partners who are incompetent. T.O.: The best part about not being a Cowboy is that now Jessica won't be going to the games. Peter: Why because her and Romo broke up? T.O.: Well that is what the media believes, but isn't funny that they break up after I leave Texas? She liked her a chocolate lollipop if you know what I am saying. *Both men begin to laugh at the conversation and all of the jokes being played on former teammates of theirs. The limo suddenly stops and TO. and Peter look at each other and nod. Probably code for lets get this over with. Both men exit the window and walk into the hospital where they have all of the kids. Both men walk over to a little kid who is lying down in bed. He is very pale and looks to be very sick. However the kid seems very excited to see Peter and T.O. by his bed. In a very excited tone, young Matthew begins to talk to his heros.* Matt: Oh my God your Peter Kaymakcian and your T.O.! You guys are awesome, the best, no one can touch you guys. *Peter starts off the conversation like the complete ass that he is. By his tone you can tell that he hates this place, but the kids probably even more.* Peter: Especially you. *Matt looks confused at what Peter has just said. He doesn't get that Peter was insulting him. T.O. is looking over the kids chart to get a brief history of the kid because he doesn't want to talk to the kid either. T.O. finally decides to speak. His voice is pretty much the same as Peter's.* T.O.: Pete man, I think this is Scotty McKnight's kid. Peter: What makes you say that? T.O.: The kid is pale white, he "likes wrestling", and he likes Tito Ortiz. Peter: Plus just like Scotty he freaks people out with the way he looks. *Matt interrupts there conversation. His voice is full of enthusiasm and excitement.* Matt: Hey guys can hang around you guys while your here? *Both men want nothing to do with any of the kids here. Peter in a somewhat nice tone, but really more of an insincere tone.* Peter: Sure just get out of bed. *Matt looks at them confused. He speaks in a confused tone.* Matt: You guys do know I am paralyzed from the waist down. *Peter decides to take the low road. His comments come out like daggers to this poor kids heart.* Peter: Sorry, no wheelies. *Both men turn around and walk away from a heartbroken Matt. Both men are smiling and T.O. says to Peter in a sarcastic tone, but somewhat serious.* T.O.: I told you he was Scotty's kid. Just like his dad he spends his whole life on his back. Peter: Got that right. *Finally one of the nurses walks up to the men. She seems nice, but she doesn't seem to like either one of them. She looks to be of latin descent and is wearing pink scrubs. She speaks at them in a tone that makes both men feel that they are being ordered around.* Nurse: Alright in room 305 a couple of people set up a Play Station 3 so you guys could play the new MWE game with Mark. Look I am really busy so please try and be decent people for once and do the right thing. *Both Peter and T.O. look offended. They decided not to rock the boat and get into anymore trouble and just head for room 305. They walk into room 305 and they see Mark. His ages seem to range from eight to ten. His reaction is the same as Matt's. He is in awe to see two of the greatest performers in there respective sports. His voice sounds like someone who is in awe of something else.* Mark: Peter and T.O. in my room? Going to play MWE vs The World 2009 with me? This has to be the coolest thing to ever happen to me. Come on Peter you first. I am going to be Sinc Mercier he is the best! *Peter looks at the kid with a look that says strike one. In an offended tone he begins to speak.* Peter: If that is your opinion fine, but I am going to be the guy who is really the best. *Peter picks himself and the match begins. At first he is playing around with Mark and lets him win. He seems genuinely happy at first and continues to let Mark dominate the match. However in the back of Peter's head he is saying strike two. Unbeknownst Mark, he is about to make strike three by speaking. In a happy, but non-threatening or gloating tone Mark lets out a cry of joy.* Mark: Yeah me! *Peter looks at Mark with anger. He says something in a bitter tone. You know the tone when you get mad because your losing and your ready to turn it up a notch.* Peter: Yeah you? I got your yeah you. *With that Peter begins to dominate the match. Peter hits a combo strike on Sinc with a drop kick to the knee, followed up with a tiger feint kick, followed up by a springboard tornado DDT, finally into a K-Lock. In an arrogant tone Peter again begins to speak. Not arrogant as in I am better then you, but arrogant as in your dominating someone in a game.* Peter: Come on Mr. Yeah You, wheres your game at? *Mark looks frustrated and confused that an actual celebrity is taking this game so seriously. Peter now has Sinc in the corner and he is placing his shoulder into Sinc's midsection. Sinc wobbles out of the corner and Peter hits him with a spike DDT. T.O. finally begins to speak. He is cheering Peter on all the way and at the same time Mark begins to look disoriented and extremely sick.* T.O.: Come on show his punk ass how its done. Pete don't become a Scotty McKnight on me. *Peter begins to signal for the spotlight kick on Sinc. Suddenly we hear Mark calling out in agony.* Mark: Call...... the nurse. *Peter not even paying attention to Mark has just hit Sinc with the spotlight kick. Peter is doing the normal taunt he does after a spotlight kick. T.O. again speaks and he isn't paying attention to Mark either.* T.O.: Mark dude I don't think you want anyone to see this ass whipping be placed on you. *Out of nowhere the nurse bust through the door and screams at both T.O. and Peter. She seems very angry.* Nurse: What is going on in HERE? *Peter still not paying attention to anything, but the game has just hit Sinc with redemption. The nurse is now checking up on Mark and it seems Peter and T.O. finally realize how serious the situation is. Peter in a sincere tone.* Peter: Mark, man you alright? Mark: Yeah I am now. Thanks to you guys. *Peter and T.O. smile and then Peter begins to speak as he was before.* Peter: Well not that I brought you back I am about to bring your ass down. *With that said Peter picks up Sinc and hits him with the revolution. He covers, one, two, three. The game loads and then it shows Peter holding up the MWE Championship. Peter throws down the controller and smiles as he says.* Peter: Now what Mr. Yeah You? Aint so good now are you? T.O.: Just like Donovan wasn't so good once I left. *Both men walk out the room laughing as Mark mutters something. His voice sounds both sad, disappointed, and angry.* Mark: You two suck. *Peter and T.O. are now seen talking to the Chief of Medicine. He is talking as a desperate man now and seems to be begging Peter and T.O. to do something.* COM: Please, for the love of God, please leave this hospital. You two need to leave right now. The kids hate you, the doctors hate you, the parents hate you, the nurses hate you, everyone in this place hates you. Peter: Sorry dude, but I need to do at least two more hours of community service or I won't be able to compete this week. COM: I'll tell them you did all of your hours. So please just leave. *Both men just shrug there shoulders and begin to walk out of the hospital. We see that Peter seems kind of sad and looks to be hurt. Without saying word Peter begins to walk away from T.O. and heads down the street towards a local internet cafe. He passes by a bunch of stores. He finally gets inside the cafe and sits down and looks at the computed. He notices a camera on the on the computer and decides right then and there to do an webcam interview for MWE.com. He goes onto the site and goes through all the technical stuff like signing on and clicking on the webcam button. Finally the feed begins and he begins to speak. His voice is arrogant and confident.* Peter: Scotty do you honestly believe that you have a chance at beating me? Come on man think about it for a second and look at the facts. Your a little prep boy who hasn't won a damn thing since coming to MWE. I was raised on the streets who is a former ICW Champion. You know the championship that even Jekyl couldn't beat me for. Kind of find it funny how when Jekyl leaves you show up Scotty. There are certain similarities that you two have. He was a loser and you are a loser. He was never a champion and you haven't won one single title. He was unoriginal and well prep school white boy has been done. Just ask John Cena, Dolph Ziggler, or Jack Swagger. *He just shakes his head as he notices people looking at him while doing this interview and he just blows them off. He continues on with his little quick attack on Scotty McKnight. His voice is just as arrogant as before.* Peter: Another reason why you can't beat me is because of last sunday. Last sunday I carried our team to victory while you where flat on your back when the bell rang to end the match. I had to recover from your screw up when you knocked me off the ladder when I was about to win the match. In essence Scotty, I am the only reason that this match is taking place. I am the only reason. *He stops and looks down at the keyboard and then back up to the camera. He looks ready to end this interview, but wants to end it the right way. He speaks slowly and meticulously.* Peter: Scotty be ready to face something you couldn't possibly understand. You will face a man who uses his pain as ambition and turns his tragedies into triumphs. Believe me when I say this. I have plenty of pain and plenty tragedies in my life. I am taking MWE by storm. *Suddenly the scene goes back to the picture Peter smashed earlier. We finally see who the picture is of. The picture is of a younger Peter. We hear Peter say, "Looks like you finally got everything you've always wanted, didn't you?" The scene fades to black on the picture of Peter.*
