Silence of the Laughs

Dec 5 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Every Champion prepares in their own way. Floyd Mayweather trains in front of audience to push him that extra little bit. I heard Pele used to practise his close-control with an egg. But me? My methods are a little more home-grown. CSB: Jack Daniels and coke, buddy. What? Don't look at me like that. It's been four months since I sat in my lawyer's office, shitting myself at the thought of being a full-time Father and to everybody's surprise - yes, even mine - I did it. Now if you'd pardon mine and Crash's little skydiving escapade last week, this is the first time in months I'm able to kick back and enjoy myself. The only reason I'd say forget the plane thing is most of my "fun nights out" don't include hanging from a tree, catching a snake and listening to Kent, the man who personality forgot. Note how I said "most". Weed can do funny things. I suppose that's why I've tried to stay my distance from anything that'd push me off my rails. I've played the fun-loving junkie before but now I've got a son I can't exactly hotbox the baby room. Still, there's no harm in cutting loose once in a while and that's why a night of old tricks is exactly what the doctor ordered. That is of course, if the doctor was called Zak Warner, PHD. It's a Saturday night, the first I've seen out in a while and I've managed to rope Wayne - yes, Wayne - into babysitting for the evening. Truth be told, Cian had hit the hay before I'd even left. All Wayne's gotta' do is not set the house on fire. Seen as though there's plenty of "Family Guy" on tonight, I'd say that's improbable. Note how I said "improbable". Wayne can do funny things. But with Wayne guarding the fort for the night, it seemed like a good excuse to catch up with a man that I can't help but feel I've grown apart from. You'd think with us being so alike, Management would take it into consideration. Instead, we're still yet to team and we've faced each other twice in two months. Conspiracy or not, the added bonus of me going borderline straight edge and being in and out of my court suit has meant we haven't gotten to see the good times roll like we once had. That leads us to tonight, where I stand in a packed nightclub on the south side of Boston. "The Fire", filled with poorly-tanned douchebags and enough whores to keep me and Zak busy till Crowning Glory, 2014. Though credit where credit's due, this is a pretty nice location. The lights are tinted a smooth shade of blue and the music, for the first time in nightclub history, is not half bad. The sound of "Chase & Status" destroys eardrums in that beautiful kind of pleasure and pain as the place is populated to the brim. I missed this. The barman slides me my drink and tries to shout over how much I owe him. I can barely hear myself think - let alone him - so I pass over a crisp ten and be done with him. I look around in all directions and gather in a cocktail of faces, none of which belonging to Zak, funnily enough. Teens with fake ID's, women on the edge of passing out. I definitely missed this. (OOC Note: The next part would obviously be shouted over the music but I understand a lot of people hate reading everything in capitals, so feel free to implement a little imagination here. It's not a major deal.) ???: Dude, aren't you CSB? I turn to the side to see, yes, a teen who looks no older than 17. Average jocky kinda' guy, polo shirt and what looks like a JD and coke in his hands. Well, I guess I could make his day and talk to him for a minute, at least until Zak decides to show up. CSB: Yeah, that's me. What's up man? His face lights up like Christmas decorations. It puzzles me how people in our business can do something like this, to brighten somebody's existence just by being there, though I suppose I should be grateful I don't need to do that much to do it. It's not like I have to buy flowers to get a girl into bed anymore. All I gotta' is flash my drivers licence and flex. Teenager: Dude you're like, my fucking hero man! When you kicked GM's ass it was all like "WOHHH!". Don't get too stereotypical now eh, kid. I look into his eyes and his excitement is so apparent it's like he's replaying the match in his head, cheating reality and getting a second helping of celebration. CSB: Haha, nah I just had a good day, that's all. As much as I try and usher him down, this guy's adrenaline looks unphasable. I could probably tell him I throw kittens in wood chippers for fun and he'd still wanna' buy me a drink. Teenager: But dude, you even kicked- *BZZZZ, BZZZZ, BZZZZ, BZZZZ* I jam my hand down my jeans and grab my cell phone as it goes apeshit in vibrate mode. Like I'd be able to hear anything over this music anyway. And as nice as this kid is, I'm afraid our meeting has to be cut short. Though his friends'll love the story either way. CSB: Sorry bro, Zak Warner on the phone! Teenager: ZAK WARNE- CSB: Nice meetin' ya bro. And with that I head to the nearest exit. He stands there, dumbfounded and with a smile like he'd just eaten shrooms on toast. I push past people as quick as I can, trying to catch the call before Zak hangs up but luck being luck, my phone stops vibrating just before the door. The rush stops but I carry on anyway since I might as well just call him back. I head out the back door into a quiet alley, a breath of fresh air in comparison to the heat and the noise inside the club. I guess they call the place "The Fire" for a reason. Around me is nothing pretty. Trash cans, parked cars. Nothing Disney, that's for sure. I find Zak's name under "Missed Calls" and hit redial, walking around and waiting for it to connect. Ringing, ringing, ringing. Then it hits his fucking voicemail. CSB: Zakery, Zakery, where are you man? You're late and there are smarks on my ass and- ???: Chris... I turn around and... Fuck. There's Zak. Lying in the corner of the alley by the trash cans. His eyes...He's not even moving. CSB: Zak! ZAK!! Zak, fuckin' speak to me man! I pull his body up and lean his back against the wall, grabbing behind his shoulder blades and his back. But when I pull my hand out from behind his back, it's covered... Zak's bleeding. CSB: ZAK ZAK PLEASE, what the fuck happened? Zak: Chris...Ch-Chris... I reach my arm back around and as I get to the wet patch on his body he winces and grits his teeth, the most he's moved since I found him in his broken state. Zak's not just been beaten up, he's been stabbed. No, not Zak, not fucking Zak. I need Zak. Wait, this isn't real. This can't be real. Dear God, don't let this be real. I look around for somebody, anybody. Why can't I fucking see anybody? CSB: HELP!!!! HELP!! PLEASE, PLEASE HELP!!! PLEEEAASE!!! Zak coughs the cough of a man in a bad way. The worse his body gets, the more life he seems to show. CSB: COME ON!! ANYBODY PLEASE!! I strain my voice to levels I knew it could reach. Anything to be noticed right now, anything. Should I go and get help? No, I can't leave him here bleeding in a fucking alleyway. He doesn't deserve this. Nobody deserves this. Teenager: Dude could I get a photo real qui- The kid from inside walks out the back door, oblivious to how one of my best friends is dying and I'm having a fucking heart attack. Suddenly he freezes when he spots Zak lying against the wall. The kid's mouth moves but nothing comes out, he's shell-shocked. CSB: KID YOU GOT A CAR? Teenager: Er...Yeah I- CSB: WELL GO AND FUCKING GET IT!! COME ON WE DON'T HAVE MUCH TIME! But he still stands there, staring at my best friend dying. What part of this don't you understand, kid? I get up and go to knock his fucking head off and he snaps back into life, running round the corner to go fetch his car. At least he better be. I don't know how much longer I can do this on my own. *Cough* I turn back around and a thin stream of blood flows out of Zak's mouth, his head jerking with every hit. I never thought I'd see Zak like this. Yeah I'd seen him beaten up by opponents, victimised by the masses, hell even manipulated by his own lifestyle. But it kills me to see someone so strong look so at the mercy of time. Zak: Chris...I- CSB: Zak no, you shut up and save your fucking breath, alright? We're gonna' fix you up man, good as fucking new! Two months ago, a man by the name of DeAngelo Williams told me when hard times face you, you've got two options. Step up or die lying down. Zak's in no state to make that choice now. I've gotta' make it for him. An engine fills the silence as a shitty little Alfa Romeo speeds round the corner at a pace I never knew one could hit. At that point I know I can't waste any more time so I put my head under Zak's arm and lift him up to his feet. With his energy sapping out of him like rain on a window, I have to lift all his weight just to get him over to the car. The kid leans over and opens the door as I help Zak into the back as quick as I can. I guess this is what happens when adrenaline kicks in. I jump in and slam the door behind me as the kid goes to pull off but suddenly stalls. CSB: Are you fucking kidding me? Teenager: Sorry I was in third. At this point, my breathing starts to get real heavy. Maybe the adrenaline isn't as strong as I thought it was. I come out for the first time in months, my best friend gets stabbed and we're stuck in a car with a guy who probably doesn't know where the fucking clutch is. Oh God, now my breathing is getting heavy. We speed off towards Boston General Hospital, my head being a blur the entire time. I think back to my time in GWC when I came in as a nobody and Zak, a man who had GWC in the palm of his hand, went out of his way to make the company my home. Showed me where to go, what to say. As much as I did my own talking in the ring, Zak helped make me, me. And now look at him. Sitting in the back of a stranger’s car on a Saturday night, head leant back and leaning against the window, mouth open and eyes...empty. Blood stained shirt and none of the animated quips and insults you'd expect from a man who'd seen it all and nailed its mother. Zak starts to mumble in a low and patched voice, something about me driving and I hear something about money. He's hallucinating. Fuck, hurry up kid. It hurts me, so damn much, just to look at him like this. To see him on the edge of life and death and to have to not break down and be just as vulnerable. But I can't look away and I can't break down in front of him. I can't hyperventilate and have a heart attack in this broken-down shitbag of a car. I can't say no and pretend this is all a dream, 'cos it's not. I've made my choice, and if I'm not gonna' lie down and die, then neither will Zak. CSB: Kid what's your name? Teenager: Er, my name's Dean. CSB: Right, Dean take a left. Dean: Nah man, I got an idea. We get close to a dirt road when suddenly Dean swerves the car to the right, taking out a fucking fence and storming the car through a field. Horses scatter like wildfire as we go mud flies across the car windows. Dean seems to be a victim of his own adrenaline as he speeds through the grassy terrain, though it forces Zak's chest to jerk and blood runs out of his mouth as free as water. His chin's covered. CSB: Hold on man, only a couple more minutes alright? Dean: HOLD ON!! *Smash* The car takes out another fence as we appear on a side road. CSB: What the hell man? Dean: LOOK! We follow the road round the corner and see....Holy shit, it’s the Hospital. Dean smiles a sick grin as he knows his shortcut worked. The adrenaline's hit him. Dean stepped up. Suddenly Zak goes into a chronic coughing fit, the most he's moved since I found him. His chest about to explode, I try to hold him still as he struggles and jerks away, blood now covering his shirt and his breathing going insane. Zak no, we're so fucking close, no fucking no!!!! Dean swerves into a parking space and as soon as we stop moving I practically tear Zak out of the car, Dean jumping under the other arm as we do everything in one last ditch attempt to get him into the Hospital. The automatic doors slide open and as soon as we get inside, nearby medics rush at us like a Blitz attack. They take Zak straight out of our hands and carry him off in the midst of a flurry of medical terms and mumbo-jumbo. My adrenaline dies back down. Rest. Now we wait. ... The next three hours were three of the longest in my existence. I sat in a cold waiting room, recalling every memory I'd ever had with Zak Warner, man and wrestler. The wars with The Machine, the jokes with DeAngelo and the battles in MWE. As sad as it sounded, it was like a fairytale coming to an end and with Zak's life being so hectic and stories, ending tonight doesn't seem like it would have been cut short at all. My mind starts to shift to the business and how fickle it really is. In a world where so few understand our way of life, the higher ups seem to love nothing more than to shove us into each other and create more hostility. Not once, in three fucking federations have me and Zak ever got to wrestle side by side. Yet MWE made us fight for each others blood twice in the last two months. Maybe that's why we haven't spoken as much lately, because the way we are, what we do...we're just not meant to. Dean sits beside me quietly, not even asking the silly fucking questions I expected of him back in the nightclub, and not even updating his Twitter. I owe him a lot for getting us here. I start to over think everything that's happened in the night, how I could have avoided things one way or another. What if I met him earlier? At his place? Or if I was quicker getting outside? Why did I fucking come out in the first place? I know it makes no sense beating myself up about possibilities I'll never get to see the ends of but fuck it, what else can I do right now? And then it happened. Redemption. ... A doctor emerged and told us Zak was stable but the knife had punctured his kidney, causing him to lose enough blood to remake "300". Turns out he'll need a transplant... ... The morning after and everybody had piled into this small Hospital room. Wayne, Cian, De, Crash, The Easter Bunny, you name it. Everybody came to see Zak Warner, sleeping off a near death experience (only the way Zak could) and me, feeling like I'd been hit by a fucking truck. I suppose if this whole stepping up thing was easy, everyone'd be doing it. The Doc's told me that for a while at least, my boy will be weak as shit in the face of Alcohol. My longest friend and casual tag team partner against all kinds of life's challenges. And here I am, sacrificing it to see my best friend live another day. I guess when I said last night could have been his fitting ending, I wasn't bargaining on a chance at rewriting the script. Truth be told, being a Father had restricted my chance to be The CSB of old but I'd never buried that side of me in the hope that once in a while I go to a bar and unwind, maybe with a co-worker and maybe avoid any knifings. But even though one of the biggest parts of my life was on the line last night, there was no damn hesitation as to whether I was gonna' do it. No thinking it over, no balancing the arguments. It was a "yes" and that was it, because a true Champion can fight the adversaries. For anybody who's ever called me a junkie, a waster or a bitch to my own substances - I'm CSB and I'm a slave to nothing. Despite how much the silence of the waiting room rocked the shit out of me last night, I needed just a little more time in the midst of nothingness. The room empty bar me and the saved soldier to my right, I stare into the lense of a camera, knowing that no video could ever do my life's story justice. Oh well, worth a shot. Ladies and Gents, Boys and Girls, Ebony and hospitalized Ivory... A Jack Daniels and Coke really sounds like a real nice idea right about now. I lick my lips and stretch my neck back, knowing that everybody watching the video probably already knows the score. Hell, Gerweck.net probably knew before I did. And as much as I'd love to indulge in some home-grown anaesthetic, I thought I'd take the greater glory and look towards being the first organless World Heavyweight Champion. Somehow, Straight Edge just isn't so unique anymore. Gotta' stay optimistic and take the positives out of the negatives, ya' know? Matter fact, never again will I complain about a concussion or stitches or catching my finger in the fucking door anymore. Right now I feel like...Well I feel like someone's just stolen one of my fucking Kidney's. Nice going, Zak! Ha ha ha. I look over to see the future Hall of Famer in a state of concrete sleep, heartbeat ringing through the monitor in the corner of the room. If anyone has ever owed anything... If you're wondering what's happened, it's simple. We were supposed to go to a bar in South Boston when I got a call from Lindsay. She invited us both over and we contracted "Super Herpes". Zak passed out upon hearing the news. I look over to Zak as he lies motionless and free of pain. What? Might as well have some fun before I get into full-on Braveheart mode. Ok, seriously for those of you without access to TMZ.com - Yes you, afternoon Scotty McKnight - both Zak and I are fine and are still frothing at the mouths at the chance to compete at WrestleWars. You're probably asking yourself "But Zak just got stabbed and CSB just lost a kidney", to which I'd reply "That isn't a question, go back to school". We appreciate the concern, but if Leon Roberts is the fucking devil then I'm sure we can cope with wrestling in scrubs. Hell, maybe that's just The GWC way. Even when we look dead, sound dead, stop breathing and are pronounced as gone with the fucking wind, we're still ready and waiting to kick your ass. Some things are simply too good to die. Maybe this is what this is all about. The stuff with Danny Vice, the five on five war, it all makes sense. The spirit of The GWC, a spirit that Team MWE would choke on dicks to even get a glimpse of. We've got it. Nobody else. There is nothing in this world like the spirit of GWC. No tomb Indiana Jones could find, no weapon of mass destruction. Fucks knows what happens if we lose this war but that's one thing that could never be on the line in some shitty little tag match. You've either got it or you don't. And who am I to disappoint good ol' Morpheus in the corner there, I'm "The One" and there ain't nuthin' no MWE has-been can do about it. So I hope you're finished cutting up Eminem lyrics because you're about to be the recipient of a reality check, Mr C. Because this is what happens when Bad meets Evil. I sit up in the bed and stare out the camera like its existence was my threat. We've had our fun. Now, now it's time for Braveheart. I wanna' start off by saying last week you were right. Guys like you, guys who'd been through everything...yeah, you paved the way for guys like me. If it wasn't for you, I wouldn't have been able to do what I did. But my fair DC, oh wise sensei you are, you've forgotten something. Yes you paved the way for me, but when you pave the way for someone they have to take that path. You can't set them up and then take it all away and that's why by your own words and your own sword, you know in your subconscious that your part in this puzzle has already been set. You bragged about your past but in front of you stands the future and even Rocky Balboa knew when he was a blubbering wreck in a pair of red shorts. You can't have the past and the future, DC. The latter is for me to claim. Sinister grin, scene two, take one. Action. If you could do it, why can't I? We've both seen what the bottom of the barrel looks like, tasting some semi sweet victories in between but why the hell not the masterpiece? Though you gotta' know that with every dream comes the subsequent nightmare and as magical and orgasmic as your past wins were, every parade needs its rain. Sure the trend can continue and I could retain the Title, go celebrate in Burger King and get stabbed in the throat for my Rolex but fuck it, the smallest high is worth every grain of the lowest low. At least I could take comfort knowing I was part of Mufasa's circle of life, right? Retain the Title and get stabbed in the throat? I'm a man on the edge of good and evil, DC, I'm willing to take that chance. Wait a minute. That's not right at all now is it? Actually fuck that, you haven't seen the bottom of the barrel for shit! Who have you ever fought for? Besides yourself just to squeeze a couple more pennies out of Eli's pockets. Beautiful. You've had it so fucking peachy, having your biggest worry being how many fucking cars you get to buy. Well stick your Lambo up your ass. I faced Prison 48 hours before I faced Brad Kane and I fucking dickslapped them both. The Judicinal system ain't got shit on me. US Law? Blow me, DC! So when we're half an hour into this match and you're digging deep inside of you, looking for something, anything to keep you going - I hope the camera catches the look on your face when you realise you have NOTHING. And after WrestleWars, you'll still have nothing. Because you are nothing. But once again I must hand you the septor of tainted victory. You're right, I'm not the perfect Champion, but I'm not gonna' sit around and let Eli "politik" someone else more fitting into my place. Are you really happy to get a shot like this? Throw a couple hissy fits and walk your way to the main event? Yeah, and I'm the threat to MWE's image I'm sure. A fighting Champion who took out the best? Pfft. I'm sure all the kids wanna' grow up to be DC, complete with shrooms, bribes and a side order of hypocrisy. And no, I don't need to know who you faced for your first World Title. Why would I need to that kind of crap? Unless the match featured some sort of insane nipple spot I could take advantage of, it'll be one on one this Sunday and the best man's gonna' win. End of fucking discussion. Though for the record, not even Brian Michaels was good enough to win The World Title barely seconds after wiping his feet on the way into the company, ala CSB. Though I did get that name from Wikipedia, so citation needed. I smirk to myself, knowing the legacy that Michaels had left behind but just the thought of DC's disgusted expression brings flavour to my world, even at the expense of sounding like a jackass. Bitter sweet, but sweet all the same. I look over to Zak again, taking an abstract look at a man so powerful and yet, right now so weak. It's funny isn't it? How Zak's career, future, even life...just fell straight into my hands. Something DC is about to feel very familiar to. So tell me D, how much do you know about me, huh? Who'd I beat to win The GWC Title? Don't know? I'll tell you. The entire fucking roster. All of 'em, poster boys, tag teams, Hall of Famers. Fuck 'em all. First ever Midnight Crusade match and I made sure everybody got their turn playing "Saw" victims. But hell, at least now you know, since apparently that's such a big fucking deal in this match. Oh and while we're filling each other in on our favourite colours and giving each other fucking Facebook pokes - "making fart jokes"? Are you fucking serious son? I'm not you, DC. I don't walk around inserting animal references just to get five minutes of fame, just to get people to listen to me. I mean shit, man, you started up your own Oceans Eleven just so you could bust a press conference and play with paint ball guns! Lemme' guess, you guys all went Go Karting after, right? Shared a smoothie and watched "Saved by the Bell"? For a man so hung up on the truth, the truth sure makes you look like a great big virgin. In case you weren't aware - who am I kidding, you don't have a clue - I speak how I want because it's my way of proving that I can't be vetted, censored or shut the fuck up. You do it in the hope of a "featured video" tag on Youtube. Go bone the star wars kid and get the fuck outta' here. I lick my lips and and speak colder and more blunt than I ever have towards a camera before. If he didn't think I was serious before, he fucking knows now. And for future notice, don't just read some noobs posts on a dirt sheet forum, fucking tune your ass in to the reason MWE's ratings have shot up like Viagra the past couple months. I could retire from the ring, keep cutting promo's and I shit you not, put us against CSI and I'll blow the fuckers out of the water! Fuck your hype and your hysteria. You could dry-hump Barack Obama for thirty minutes, backflip into the pit of doom and we'd still barely pull a 2.5. I'm so fucking big I could bring back Myspace! And that shit's been dead since last February! Fuck it, I'll bring back GWC, wrestle, book the cards, work the camera's and hold the fuckin' crowd signs! You wanna' talk to me about hype? DC, I'm better at being you than you are! I look back at the past couple of months. I've achieved it all. Beaten the best, won the Gold to prove it, became a man, a Father and reinvigorated my spirit for the fight in GWC's name. This isn't just a threat or promo anymore. To me, this is all real. This is the truth and I'd die before I lie about it. This is the belief that will defeat DC! But there's just one thing holding my name back, and it held me back for so many years. Then there's the drugs. Drugs, drugs, drugs. DC, the second you opened your mouth about the hidden herb of this world was the second it became clear - You're the victim of your own delusions. I mean, you bashed Crash 'cos he didn't know who you beat for some Title back in 1844 yet you, you fucking hypocrite, still think I'm some toked up idiot who stumbles down to the ring after hotboxing his living room? You fool. You've got no idea how serious I am about crippling you with intent this Sunday night. About how bad I wanna' just take that arm that's raised so many Titles before and just snap up the ligaments, just tear open your skull, and for fun, too. You think someone like me raises a kid and doesn't change the way he is? I can't believe I'm the one saying this DC...But grow the fuck up. Seriously. The reason I'M gonna' kick YOUR ass is simple. I've evolved and you haven't. I WAS the drugged up playboy everybody loved to see get extreme but when I saw something needed changing, I manned the fuck up and changed it. I looked at what was at fault and I beat it. I'm CSB, Version 2-point-Fucking-0. But you? You haven't changed since you first laced up the boots. You were the punk who rode the hype and you're still that punk, playing the same tunes and cracking the same jokes. All you do is change the opponents name and variate your "fucks" and "shits". When you lose, you flip your lid and blame everybody else. When I lose, I'll work and work and work until I'm not even me anymore! Till I'm a fucking Megazord who shoots fucking lasers and you can't even RECOGNISE my ass anymore! Drugs? You wanna' talk to ME about drugs? I AM THE MIRACLE DRUG THAT EVERY PROFESSIONAL WRESTLER DREAMS TO HAVE!! I AM THE LIVING, BREATHING STEROID OF THE HUMAN RACE AND YOU'RE NOTHING TO ME!! YOU HEAR ME? FUCKING NOTHING!! I raise out of the bed like a beast from the sea. Wires hanging from every limb, veins going apeshit and look on my face like I've seen death and it looked familiar. DC, I pray for you. AT WRESTLEWARS...I'M GONNA' STUB - YOU - OUT!! My breathing heavy, my heart rate monitor going through the roof. Week after week, year after FUCKING year, I rise to the top of this business only to be told it's not...quite...right. People were missing from the scene or I beat the wrong guy or the Champion was on his fucking period. There's always someone there to tell me that my greatest moments, the moments that make me, are worthless. Well no fucking more. I HUMILIATED the star of Crowning Glory, the biggest event in your company's calendar and you still have the nerve to tell me I don't deserve this? I don't deserve...this? Well I tell you what DC, I'M GONNA' FUCK YOU UP at WrestleWars and then - oh you wait for it - THEN I'll have the right to lie in a fucking hospital bed, threatening a man who's never beat me and tell him why I'M THE ONE WHO'S GONNA' WALK OUT TALL!! SINC MERCIER WANTS TO FUCK WITH FATE? BRING HIM ON TOO! THE STRIPES AND BARS HE'S GONNA' WEAR THIS SUNDAY AIN'T NUTHIN' TO THE ONES I'VE SEEN IN MY LIFETIME, SO STICK TEAM M.W.E UP YOUR ASS AND GET READY FOR THE REAL! I WOULD DIE FOR THIS, DC!! THIS! THIS BUSINESS! THIS FAMILY! THIS CHAMPIONSHIP!! I CHANGED MY LIFE FOR THIS!! EVERYTHING ABOUT ME!! I walk back over to the bed and lie back, crushing my eye lids together before opening to the light of the new world. I speak calm. So go ahead and tell me... What would you do for it? I close my eyes and seep into a state I haven't seen for so long. Rest. I guess I have to. 'Cos in a few days, I'm fighting for everything I've got. *Fade* Zak: Wait. What I miss?
