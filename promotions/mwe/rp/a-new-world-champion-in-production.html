A New World Champion? In Production; A Grand Mystique RP

Aug 23 2009

The Grand Mystique

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The scene opens to the exact same scene that ended the last promo. GM and Chloe Cornette are in a hotel room. She is asleep. Meanwhile he is awake, having been disturbed by a nightmare. The reality for GM is far more concerning. Because what he sees before him, real or not, is the man that stands in front of him is Maxxamillion Mustafa. Maxx would be credited by many as GM’s inspiration. A teacher of sorts. Many would say that GM, for his part, got made by ending Maxx’s career at Adrenaline Rush. In wrestling, it's funny how the student / teacher dynamic works isn't it? So maybe they were right. So why had he chosen now to see Maxx as the figment of his subconscious? Because to GM, Maxx represented mistakes and Crowning Glory meant no mistakes. This distraction was highly unwelcome. Maxx was not a factor. They had not spoken at all since Friday Night Overdrive. GM was grateful of that at least. It was here and now that brought back memories. The crushing, claustrophobic feeling for being held down. He didn’t want to feel those feelings and even now, he loathed Maxx for his simple, subconscious presence. However at this time, he wasn’t able to be sure how much of this was a dream or reality. Maxx: “What?” GM: “You’re playing innocent?” Maxx: “I’m not here to discuss me.” GM: “Ah yes. Go ahead, explain your reason. Tell me all about why you’re here.......now.” The indignant and doubtful tone in his voice is clear. Maxx circles behind GM. “You know that you need to trust me – like you trusted her. There will be no reason, no explanation. You have to explore the emotion that you detest most of all.” His tone is quiet but bouncy and sing-song. ”You have to trust me.” Immediately GM’s eyes betray that he is totally unconvinced. While he makes a point about Chloe being in his life for the moment, this was extremely different. “I don’t need you and I never have done. And yet I’m perfectly aware that there is a reason for you showing up here, now.” Maxx cocks his head to the side to acknowledge GM’s point as being reasonable and valid. “So are you going to tell me the real reason?” “Well given that I am merely your subconscious, I can’t refuse can I?” He turns and walks out of the room without saying anything more. GM puts his head around the door and sees him already ten metres down the corridor by a lift. He drags his jacket off the hook behind the door and runs down after him. “Well?” “I’m going to show you.” “What?” GM has caught up to him just as the lift doors grind open slowly. “This is ridiculous”. He mumbles to himself, annoyed at this very unnecessary diversion. He looks around at the lift and considers how pathetic he feels at this moment. Feeling like he is being held captive by his self, within a steel box that seems to be pulling down the pit of his stomach while pulling his body weight up. “This is so, so symbolic you know?” “Maxx, what the fuck?!” In an instant, Maxx then reaches out and grabs GM by the throat with his right hand. GM feels the air squeezed out of him, slowly, like how a boa constricts its’ prey. Despite this he is still able to hear the low growl of his former teammate. “I saw you start out at the low of the low. WZCW.......Murfish, Ben Legend, Milenko. You were the furthest thing from a World Champion that I had ever seen!!” “Even in EW, it took months for you to become someone! Windy Bridges made you! And I am the only reason that you are someone worth watching in MWE now!” Maxx steps back and releases GM. He gasps for air and falls to the floor. GM looks up with raging vengeance, quite unable to believe his ears. “What the hell is this? I’m not the damn mood to be demotivated! Not now! This is not the time! You want to think that anyone BUT me is going to take credit for my place in MWE? He gets up and looks at him, eye to eye. “I’m fed up of this shit. You are the reason I am so much better? Bullshit! People can try and say I’m not good enough, that I am not worthy or ready. They can say that I have been given chances before I am ready.” “I had to fight for my opportunities – I was given nothing! And I have earned everything!” He grimaces fiercely as he recalls his pasts; the successes and failures. “I have overcome EW folding, the failure of Rev Pro and the UWA. I have become who I am despite all the shit in WZCW. You think I wanted that? You reckon that I wanted EW to close when I was on the verge of greatness? You being here and now is no more significant than reminding me to not repeat your mistakes by taking a giant shit on my legacy!” GM has become as emotional as we have ever seen him, verbally abusing the spectre before him. “You’re wrong!” “Why?” “Because this isn’t about me!” Maxx attempts to sound defiant but fails. “Bollocks. This is me making sure that I am not just another reminder of how fucked up EW was. How stupid it was that it happened the way that that it did. I have made it in MWE and ultimately it all shows that its’ death wasn’t meaningless! I am going to be its’ ultimate success story!” “You are here to remind me that I have to do whatever I need to, to make sure it is never repeated and I never lose it. I will never suffer the professional embarrassment that you did. I have worked too hard. I couldn’t give myself a World Title shot, remember.” GM is making a point of reminding Maxx of his failures. He clearly is hitting some raw nerves. Max quietly backs away a couple of steps. “You weren’t always such a threat.” “Huh?” “You are so close to being on top – that you need to revisit the dregs of your past to realise how much this means to you.” “You know I get it now. This is about me making sure that I don’t suffer your mistakes. I have to take the opportunities that I have earned instead of wasting them like you did!” The lift’s bell pings and Maxx shoves GM towards the door. Expecting to be harmlessly stopped by the door, he reaches out with his arms. However his palms are sucked through the metal and his momentum causes him to fall through head first into black nothingness. . . . . . . . . . . With a crash and a bang, GM has landed. The scene has instantly changed and it takes a few seconds for him to realise quite what has happened. And how the mighty has fallen. The camera has pulled back to show GM in a dirty green rubbish bin. It is filled with paper bags and cardboard that is overflowing with rotting food and many other disgusting things that feel horrifying and smell even worse. He feels something moving around by his feet and reckons that it is probably a rat. It only adds to the very nauseous feeling that is rising up from the pit of his gut. “Wow, this is really is the low of the low; this shit is a lot worse than WZCW!” He tries to pull himself out but realises his hands are covered with this dark coloured slime. A rotten iceberg lettuce flies by his head and hits the brick wall behind him. “DDDDDDDIIIIIIIIIIIIIINNNNNNNNNNNKKKKKKKKK TTTTTTUUUUUUUUUUUUUURRRRRRDDDDDDDIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIEEEEEEEEEEEEERRRRRRRR!!!!” As he is wiping it off, he gets interrupted. Looking up, he sees this grubby old hobo in the shadowy corner of the alley. He is curled up in a sodden mound of cardboard, hugging a bottle of vodka. He stares at GM with wide bloodshot eyes. “I said...... DDDDDDDIIIIIIIIIIIII” “I heard what you fucking said!” GM rubs his hands against a paper bag and begins to drag himself out. “I can’t believe this guy is back.” From his place on the floor, the hobo reaches up, as if desperately reaching for the cookie jar from a top shelf, yet he obstinately refuses to shift from his position on the pavement. GM has jumped out of the bin and discarded the now filthy black jacket he was wearing. He is now fully dressed with blue stone washed jeans and a sky blue white t-shirt. “This is what could have happened.” GM addresses the filthy old man who gazes at him with a wary and confused look. He hugs his vodka even closer to his chest. “EW dies, Rev Pro fails and within months, I could have ended up as a bum. Out of work, dying a slow and painful death.” “Even though I was the number one contender, destined for greatness but the plug got pulled and that was that. Opportunities earned through effort, taken away by politics.” He spits out the last words with passionate anger. He hated the term and everything it stood for in the industry, yet he could not escape it. The hobo scrabbles around by GM’s feet. Annoyed, GM kicks him in the ribs for his troubles. Unfazed, he begins to prance about and yelling nonsense at the top of his voice. GM turns his back in ignorance to him. “So I had to start from the bottom. Alongside Maxx and Project Revolution, for the tag belts of all things.” Looking back, he wonders if American Animal or Jason Fear were in the same predicament as the man behind him. In fact, almost certainly they are, he has little doubt of that. He walks out of the alleyway, escaping the drunkard, the mess and the stench behind. He moves out towards the street, which is relatively quiet and dimly lit. He looks at his watch and realises that it is quarter to seven in the morning. That explains the lack of traffic and minimal street lighting, as well as the unusually coloured sky as the sun peeks out of the horizon, trying to wake itself out of its’ slumber. GM strides on. Full on energy and drive, he realises that Crowning Glory is so soon. He is pounding the pavement, heading down the street of this small anonymous town. “I had to get past that level to finally make a name for myself in this company. And by tapping into the depths of my potential, I did it. I took out name after name, fighting at new heights, against new names; ending careers before they started and finishing rivalries that had spanned across federations.” Soon the red bricked buildings that line the pavement are nothing but an unnecessary distraction that GM quickly blocks out of his mind as his focus is set in stone on one thing. “The MWE has seen my rise from obscurity first hand. From obscurity to verging on supremacy! This is a chance to reach the highest of the high after suffering the lowest of the low.” “Just like Sinc Mercier, I have found my rightful place. The main event in this company will never be the same again. No matter how many old faces retain their spot and despite the constant influx of new blood, the future of the MWE has been set up, with me on top.” He is clearly happy with his rapid rise up the card as his tone is full of pride. He crosses the road without need to pause for traffic. “Mason Resoan, DC, David Whitman – none of them has been able to kill my momentum, my destiny.” At the mention of Sinc causes GM to become particularly aggressive as he speaks. “Sinc has had a similar past. I have seen Sinc go from curtain jerker to main eventing champion, jizz spouting jabroni to poster boy. He has had snorefests with Devlin Sharpe and electrified millions against the likes of Mason Resoan and Brian Michaels.” “He is now so confident and happy yet has been wracked by loss, self doubt, guilt in his youth. A champion, a proud champion and he is enjoying his run at the top of the best company going.” “But as high as he can be now, is how far he can fall in three seconds. Indeed, they say pride becomes before a fall. Sinc’s pride is based off a false premise. He promised to be a fighting champion but this will be his first title defence.” He reaches another crossroad and receives a rude awakening of sorts. A powerful Ford F50 wagon screeches to a halt by his feet. Clearly alert, GM glares a silent apology of sorts to the driver and walks around the vehicle. It takes a while before he begins to speak again. “See, I could respect Sinc. He has walked the path up the card and deserves to be World Champion. He has revitalised his career and is reaping the rewards. But now, he has betrayed himself for the approval of the fans.” “He refused to put his belt on the line. He is too afraid to risk it and by doing so, he is damaging it, its’ prestige and legacy.” “And finally at Crowning Glory, you really can’t escape. All your fans on the biggest stage of them all will see a new star form before them.” GM speaks with a little disenchantment in his voice. He is used to the fans being absorbed into the ways of others. They seem to be happy with the efforts of those who go out of their way to entertain them. “Ah yes....the fans. Sinc should feel guilty about misleading them. But he won’t and they won’t mind a jot. It’s just the way he is; just the way the fans are.” “And they can’t accept that a new face is ready and waiting.” ”I have hidden for so long, too long. I nearly betrayed myself, I was so nearly lost in the shuffle but I am not prepared to let that happen now.” GM’s walk has taken him past many buildings. He now has come to a row of trees with steel fencing. He walks through a gap in the fencing into an area that looks like a park. Colourful flowers line the path of gravel. GM takes a seat in the middle of a black iron wrought bench. The camera is now in front of him. It falls slightly, lowering itself to capture his full form. He is relaxed and open. It’s unusual for us to see him quite like this but the setting encourages him to appear relaxed even if his tone is familiar and assertive. “I’m not A Rod or Mason. This would not be the defining moment of my career, a crutch on which I will rely on. I cannot allow that to happen. This will be my crowning glory, a lasting memory of triumph, but something that will allow me to build a legacy and an emphatic statement of intent that for the future because this is the first yet it will not be the last!” GM thought that comparing himself or saying that he is better than others who have already won the World title may be a little premature. Yet he feels absolute and sure in himself – just like he always does. “I promise that at Crowning Glory, there will be no denying my place as a champion. If I said it once, I’ve said it a hundred times; the future of this business is about to explode. I am the man ready to carry MWE to the new era, prepared to toil and take the bullets and emerge bigger and better for the effort.” GM leans forward and pokes himself in the chest a couple of times. “I’ve been through too much pain to let myself down and it will be very much worth the wait. The delay has been torturous, the hype unprecedented and no one expects anything but a successful title defence to establish you as a great champion.” He drags some of his hair out of his face and he winces slightly because of the sun. Some of his scars become visible. These scars he wears like war medals. Hidden for so long, he can show them to the world. Emblems of victory and survival. “I’ve seen the desire in your eyes. You want to put yourself on the next level. One of a privileged few to defend the belt. This event is crucial to establish yourself as the champ that you want to be.” You can tell GM is excited. He is gesturing quite animatedly and pointing to match what he is saying. His driven enthusiasm is remarkable and his comments are waspish and to the point. “I have fought legends, those who have carried this belt and those who have hungered after it almost as much as I. I am finally ready, having endured hell itself and suffered momentous victory and crushing defeat. Since arriving in MWE, I have had a steady slew of characters put before me, road blocks all ready, willing and able to stop me and block my dreams.” “Have they stopped me? No, they tried.....they failed and they only succeeded in making me who I am today.” GM suddenly gets up. He looks around as he feels like he is being watched. He leaves the bench and begins to walk out the way he came through. The town is now a little busier as the day progresses. The sun is becoming brighter; there is substantial traffic on the road. People are on their way to work but GM just ignores them and continues to walk away from them. “Sinc Mercier’s career is a combination of everything that is bad about the MWE. Look at his past, like cutting through a rotten tree to check its’ age. Devlin Sharpe, James Sulliivan are barely dots in the history of MWE. As he walks he grabs a stray branch from an untidy bush and rips off a leaf. He folds it and roughly begins to rip it up, talking while he does. “But then you get to the cream of the crop or so you would think; Windy Bridges who was never able to reach his best against anyone but me because he was trying to be somebody he could never be. And Mason Resoan, a man who can only see perfection when he wants to instead of when he needs to.” He allows the bits of leaf to fall to the pavement. “He made his name and achieved his dream on the back of wins against those men. A dream, that he has aspired to be since his first match here. He will say that I have the pressure on me – the challenger. He will say that I will suffer in the burden of a championship match in the main event. But there are no nerves, the only pressure is that which I place on myself.” He pensively rubs his chin, as he contemplates this thing called pressure. “These expectations are my own, no one expects me to actually triumph. I don’t share disappointment or success with anyone. They all believe that I will fail, that Sinc will be victorious against me and then become the figurehead of the company for the next generation.” As GM considers his current position and what the next few days hold in store for him, he considers the possibility that Sinc may never be ready to deal with this pressure. The MWE champion needs to be prepared to deal with anything and everything. Sinc has shown that he is not that man. He looks off to the distance. He has come to the outskirts of this nameless town without realising. The sun is bright and in the distance, can see a winding road through the shimmering heat haze. “So I go on my road to CG, expecting to have to endure an absolute war. I expect nothing less than yet another struggle and that will see Sinc trying to hold on to his title for dear life.” GM smiles as he realises how many people actually want him to fail. Failure with this much on the line just isn’t an option to him. The idea of always being known as the one to emerge from CG defeated makes him come over all queasy again. “And he will fail....because what is at stake is a place in legend.” “To be the only man other than David Whitman to walk out of the main event of CG with the World title draped over your shoulder, the man, Mr Number One, the hunted one who is looking over his shoulder for the next twelve months – that is a true honour and a Hall of Fame worthy achievement in itself, something that would put the man who has his hand raised in legend of the MWE.” “We both want that spot. That honour.” As he speaks of this honour, his tones hushes with respect. And it shows a side to him that he has not demonstrated in a long time. “The survivor, the man who is able to walk out will carry the burden, we will have to prosper under the pressure because the entire fed will be for that one man to represent and be held accountable for.” GM turns and realises that he is alone. Exactly how he likes it. He scratches just below his neckline as he looks around. He addresses the camera again. “That is what the winner will receive and I want it. I want the desire, the expectation, the pressure, the strain, the sleepless nights. I want the entire roster just a step behind me, breathing on my neck, their hand almost on my shoulder.” “I am in that position now. I know what it means to have the hunger for a title, to be unable to sleep properly for the sheer ambition that perforates every pore!” “To win and put myself on top of the world, that would be the single greatest achievement since David Whitman returned from nowhere to dominate the new MWE. I need to put myself in the annals of MWE history; those are my expectations because I know what I am capable of.” The sentences reels off his tongue like a shopping list, which gains momentum as he speaks. And as he speaks quicker he becomes more emotional and enthused. He clenches his fist and grits his teeth. “It’s time for us to see whether Sinc Mercier can resist the temptation of cracking too many jokes for the biggest occasion, because here is where it all matters. If you can’t let it all hang out at CG, then you don’t deserve to even be here....seriously GtFO.” GM points off to the faded distance. “Sinc Mercier can deny it all he wants but he is immature, loud and brash and he doesn’t know how to be decisive and calculated. He has continued to make astonishing errors of judgement and character. He has just never learnt!” “But Sinc isn’t the resident prankster anymore. He doesn’t believe in the power of jokes. Now Sinc doesn’t need to be gesturing and swearing like DC but it may his promos easier to stomach, his reign easier to tolerate. Sinc believes that sheer conviction of beliefs will pull him through, that his faith in good over evil will suffice. I say evil has waited long enough for a win on the worlds grandest stages, I say the monster needs to stand triumphant over the hero for a change.” GM smirks at the very image, which goes against the imbued grain of the human belief system that is based around good and evil. “Does anyone really believe that Sinc is ready to be the man? Has he really grown up and become the mature and reliable figurehead that the MWE need? I can’t see it, even if he talks a great game.” He raises his eyebrow in absolute doubt. “He abandoned the US title twice and did it with disgusting adolescent outlook, disguised as ambition. I couldn’t care less if he has heard this before. He has never heard it from me. It was stupid then and with hindsight it still wasn’t the best career decision. You’re going to be begging for the US title when I have finished with you, because you will want to have something to cling to, to hold as you go to sleep, tired and frustrated because you can’t sleep without the reassurances that you are the man.” “Well Sinctista you are the man – the man who is holding what I have desired for ten months now. You are the man who is in the way of me accomplishing my dream, my desires and CG signifies the end of the tether, the last of my patience.” “How’s that bullseye feeling on your back? Feeling paranoid yet??” GM smiles at the thought of finally taking what he has spent hours dreaming about. He is determined that his victory and the subsequent title reign will allow him to be the catalyst to not just a new year in the MWE but a brand new era. Old names leave, new ones arrive and some stars will get bigger and brighter. He is also enjoying poking fun at Sinc. Mr Mercier has been nothing but a beaming ray of smiling, laughing and joking light since his last title defense and truthfully it made him feel quite sick. GM is planning to enjoy giving everything that he has to ensure Sinc’s title reign doesn’t continue beyond CG. First Sinc, then anyone else who wants to try it. But definitely Sinc first. “This is something that will get the people interested in you once again Sinc. Whether it be what you wanted or how you meant it to work for you, I couldn’t really care. That’s the thing about heroes Sinc, people love to see them triumph and rise valiantly to defy the heinous odds stacked against them. Yeah they go wild for that. But occasionally, when it comes down to it, it’s just the best thing for business when things don’t always add up for the good guys – they’re a special and unique breed.” “Because the really good guys, who feel pure of heart and clean of soul, end up being selfish and aspiring for too much, too soon, reaching far beyond their means and that is when the nefarious villain comes in, because he knows that path and that too much of a good thing is bad for you and will lead you down to his level. I should know, I tried to please the fans and it got me nowhere!” GM doesn’t like being reminded of his past failures, so he is quite sarcastic as he talks. To him it feels like he is given credit to someone else for his change in attitude. “And yet I alone turn down this path, leaving a trail of blood and broken glass behind me. And now I am main eventing the biggest show in the company’s history! Sometimes, doing the right thing isn’t always most beneficial. I am the living proof of this.” “Everything I have seen from his pissing about with Mason in a swimming pool or a bowling alley to a kiddies’ scavenger hunt around Rome, it all points to it being exactly the same.” “A childish brat does not belong at the top of this company. Sinc has treated the fed like his own personal sounding board for too long and I have tried so hard to ignore the yelling but NOT ANYMORE!” GM’s simmering determination has now piqued into an explosion of passion. His eyes now trap the camera with laser accuracy and deadly intent. The build up and intensity of the past few shows has been building to these moments. “If Sinc reckons that he can get away with costing me matches and shouting off about me to the rest of the world, he is so very wrong. I’m going to show him true fear and very real agony.” “Mason got away with murder on the last Monday Night Mayhem. That fluke was Sinc’s fault. I had Resoan in the palm of my hand and he was there, looking after his boyfriend. He didn’t need to be there. That was an unacceptable distraction. The fact that it cost me the match is one of many crimes that he has committed and for it, he will suffer.” GM smirks again yet it instantly drops from his face, replaced by a stern grimace and wide bloodshot eyes. Eyes that seem incapable of blinking or betraying the slightest notch of emotion. “I told you!!! In January, this shit would come full circle. The prophecy was told many moons ago.” He laughs bitterly at what he said and the reaction that he got when he said. “I warned you and everybody else that I was the one who was ready to take over, go onto bigger and better things. I know you all thought I was full of it at the time and that I haven’t backed it up since but you are oh so wrong. It has shown you all to be damn ignorant.” While he talks, he shakes his head disapprovingly. Knowing that he deserved this opportunity months ago, he figures that he must make the opportunity count and back up his ambition. “I know I am better than you Sinc. But you know why I am certain of this? Everything you do, I can do that much better. I will punch you so hard your teeth will rattle and kick you in the head so much that you will forget what day it is. I wrestle rings around you and stretch you in ways that you never knew were possible.” He pats his belly, right where a belt would sit around his waist. “And then I will take the thing you desired most, that signifies you are the number one guy, the one on top. Your title is the only reason you are considered main event. I can’t wait to take your spot, your championship and make you wish you were on a film set with DC again for the easier life.” GM grimly clenches his fists as if pulling tense a piece of rope. “I’ve had flashbacks that have tried to remind me of my past. Apparently Maxx is trying to remind me what I have, how easily I can lose it. But it hasn’t worked, because all I see is a faceless hoax, who was never good enough to get his shot.” He grabs the camera to him and brings it close to his face. “And just like Sharpman, Mason, DC and Virus to name a few, they haven’t been good enough to earn this opportunity, not at this time. But I have. It’s been a long time coming but finally, it is time for a new voice to be heard. To turn the whispers into a deafening roar that will not be ignored because I WILL NOT be ignored.” [IMGhttp://i104.photobucket.com/albums/m200/DevilSoprano/Wrestling/l_793ef29fac05d34f773034999b38e806.jpg[/IMG] *** “You gotta aim high, baby. You gotta aim high. You gotta ask yourself the question sometimes, “Why was I put here? Is there a grand scheme to things?” You gotta pursue those brass rings. And if it turns out to be heaven’s gate, then at least you went down in flames.” *** **OOC**I can’t source this quote.
