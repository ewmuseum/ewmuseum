Protemplation; A David Whitman RP

Nov 8 2008

David Whitman

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The camera slowly opens up. We see David Whitman, dressed in his signature black pants and a plain gray shirt, sitting on a dark blue sofa. He has the MWE world title slung over his right shoulder. His stare is directed at the TV set in front of him- which is turned off. His face expresses indifference, or some form of emotion close to that. The camera pans out a bit and we see this is some sort of living room, as there are posters hung on the walls and chips carelessly sprinkled across the carpeted floor. The door on the far left of the scene opens, and James Whitman, dressed pretty similarly to David, walks in. he takes a look at David before turning his attention to the camera, and then to whoever’s behind the camera. James: Dude. Not again. Voice: What?! I didn’t do it?! James: Thomas. Get serious for a minute. Thomas: All I did was tell him about his upcoming match! James: … ooooh. James shakes his head. He then starts walking towards the couch. Before getting there, he stops abruptly and turns back to Thomas, who is manning the camera. James: Wait, what the hell are you doing? Thomas: His bouts of insanity translate well to the screen. What can I tell ya? He’s charismatic when he’s clinically insane. James sighs. He shrugs and walks over to David, sitting next to him. It almost seems as if he’s mimicking David at this point as he is now also staring at the blank TV screen. David: You DO know I heard every word you idiots said? James: WH-WH- WHAAA? David: I’m fine. I’m just thinking. Thomas: He is NOT fine. James: I hate to agree with Michael Moore over there, but he DOES have a point. You tend to take things a bit hard, and this time you actually have a reason to, so- David: This match. Covell is a genius. It’s- It’s not even funny. James: How so? David: I mean think about it. Everyone is intricately linked to each other- Michaels is to me as D.C. is to Michaels. Michaels made my first run in MWE a living hell. D.C. and him are sworn enemies. I just battled D.C. in one of the toughest matches of my career a couple of weeks ago. And the three of us have to co-exist. THAT, by ITSELF, is a stroke of genius and no doubt orgasmic for a firestarter like Covell. James turns to the camera and mouths “Orgasmic?” with a weird look on his face. David: BUT! Do you know who we have to face? James: I know, but I think it’s best if you get it all off your chest. David: Shut up and let me talk then. James seems offended by David’s remark, but just shrugs it off. David, meanwhile, still has not moved at all from his position. David: I’ve been through hell and back and somehow miraculously managed to become close friends with one of my most heated former rivals. Alejandro Rodriguez is a man I have immense respect for. James: There’s no denying that. David: Resoan was Michaels’ pupil. They nearly killed each other in that Student VS Teacher match. But he also nearly killed Alejandro a couple of weeks ago. For real. James: Yeah, but Tex intervened. David: …Much like YOU are doing right now. James gets a look of shock on his face and pulls back his arm slowly as if to punch David but the view runs up to him and Thomas grabs his arm. James sighs. The camera turns and is now showing the side of David’s face. David: Dallas took the US title off Alejandro in his debut match. And I know if anyone did that to me I’d want to completely destroy him. NOT team up with him. As if that weren’t enough, Dallas beat D.C. in HIS first match in MWE, if I’m not mistaken. With me so far? James: Actually, I think you ARE mista- David: Ok. Now how about this; Cannon. Michaels. Need I say anything else? James: N- David: HEY NOW HERE’S THE KICKER! This match is beautiful, in that most of us want to rip apart one or all of our teammates, but we still don’t want to see our opponents win. And that- is the genius of Covell. He made a soup out of all this sh*t, and it’s going to turn out to be explosive, one way or another. James: Wow. Ok, so are you done? David: Yes. James: Well then, I’ve got something else to throw in your little bullsh*t stew. David: It’s sh*t soup- aw man. Forget it. You ruined everything. James: D.C. just messaged me during your little rant. An intrigued David turns to James, who is holding up his cell phone. David: That’s MY phone. James: Well he said Whitman in the text, so I assumed it was me. Thomas: Don’t you have to open the message to rea- James: SHUT UP THOMAS! David: Whatever, what’d he say? James: Uh… something about a meeting. At his house. You, Michaels and him. David: WHAT?! David grabs the phone from James and starts dialing. James turns to the camera and points to David with his thumb. The camera makes a nodding motion as Thomas heads towards David, who keeps swatting at Thomas trying to make him go away. David (to the phone): What’s this sh*t about a meeting?!... Wait, what do y- Yeah, yeah, I know- WHAT?! So what, it’s gonna be you and your woman and Michaels and his woman? You don’t think I’d feel out of place?! David turns and looks at the camera, then turns again and tries to cover himself as he starts mumbling. David: lookiknowi’mnotgoodwiththeladiesyoudon’thavetogoandhurtmyfeelingsiju- (normal voice) Yeah. Ok, FINE. But aren’t you and Michaels still… iffy? You SURE?! Well, if you two start brawling or whatever, I’m out of there. Wh- alcohol? James & Thomas: WE’LL DRIVE YOU. David: Ok, so- yeah. H- David moves his phone a bit from his face as he looks at it, then at James and Thomas. David: He hung up! James: WHO CARES! I heard alcohol! David: Dude, we’re just gonna go meet, and discuss how we’re going to co-exist, and then how we’re gonna handle our opponents. James: Yeah b- David: YES, you and Thomas can come. But you WILL behave! The camera starts jumping up and down before David stops Thomas. David: Alright. Look, this is serious. If this goes well, we might end up putting aside our differences. If we can do that, we’ll be one step closer to making more sense of this match. James: Well I hate to break it to ya cuz, but despite all your good intentions and hopeful thoughts, this match is going to be a train wreck. A disaster of unimagined proportions. A calamity on a grandiose scale. Because even if you decide to put your differences aside for a moment, any little thing can trigger a reaction, and within seconds you’ll be attacking your own teammates. If that doesn’t happen, it’s still four on three. That ALONE is enough t- David abruptly puts his hand in front of James’ face, mere millimeters away. David: Shhhh… let me get through this meeting first. THEN we can ponder over the inevitable crumbling of my upcoming matchup. James: Okay. David turns around and walks away as the camera turns to James, who is watching his cousin leave while looking dumbfounded. James: I don’t know what to say. He’s being irrational. Thomas: I dunno, man. James turns to the camera. James: Well yeah, I mean it’s a lot to digest. But I thought he would’ve gotten over all of that crap. He’s the MWE champ again! The camera moves from side to side in a “no” motion. Thomas: Teaming up with D.C. and Brian Michaels will be hard enough. Respect isn’t everything here. You can respect somebody without being able to work well with them. Despite that, he also has to think about his opponents, and how they are all linked to his partners and him. He has to think about Sonny, who still wants revenge. He hasn’t even gotten a chance to enjoy being champ again. He- James holds up his index finger and points to the camera. James: I don’t remember any cameraman talking so much. Ever. A loud sigh can be heard emanating from behind the camera. The view shifts to the side as we see David walking out of another room wearing a different shirt and stuffing his wallet into his side pocket. He taps the camera on the lens as he passes. David: Let’s GOOOoOoOo! James turns to the camera and shrugs. The camera shows James as he starts following David. Thomas yells out to James. James: What?! Thomas: Do I HAVE to be the cameraman? I wanna drink too… James: Dude, the camera was YOUR idea! The view doesn’t move as James turns around and heads out of the house. The camera abruptly falls to the ground as we see Thomas’ feet pass in front of it before it goes to static.
