What is in a name?; ID rp Scotty

Jul 4 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I did what I had to do... I got rid of the most feared man in MWE... I'M FREE!!!! Scotty: Last week during Monday Night Mayhem I rid myself of the man who held me back, I destroyed him, his career and his life. I rid MWE of a menace, a man that did nothing but manipulate other for his own gain, but best of all, I'm free of the man who almost ruined my career and almost ruined my life. A knock at the door breaks Scotty's concentration. Making his way to a Mahogany door with ivory inlay Scotty replays his last moment with Jekyl in his head. Opening the door he sees a man, wearing a pair of denim jeans, black t-shirt and a leather jacket. He stands in front of Scotty casually smoking a cigarette, the heat outside so sweltering its a wonder how Virus can tolerate it. Scotty: Virus? Virus: The one and only... Gona invite me in? Scotty rubs his chin in confusion, The fact that Virus is standing in front of him bewilders him, but the fact that his mood towards him is so friendly baffles him even more. Scotty: What is this about? I thought you hated my guts... Virus: Nah, I actually quite like you... The hatred was more for show than anything. Scotty: Okay? Well I'm just in the middle of shooting my promo, so just stay out of my way. Scotty and Virus enter the house where the walls are painted grey, the floors covered in what looks like stone, white crown moldings border the ceiling which is painted black. Picture frames are scattered along the walls in an organized fashion. Virus looks at them as he walks by, almost amazed at the house. Virus: Is that Tito Ortiz? Scotty: Yeah, he was the one who trained me to fight, which reminds me, I have to call him and send him tickets to independence day. The man in the picture beside that is my father, the man I have always wanted to be. Virus: I'm assuming you're going to send him tickets too? Scotty lowers his head but quickly raises it again with a smile. Scotty: I would but there wouldn't be much point... Virus remains confused but continues to question why. Virus: And why is that? Scotty: He died about 10 years ago, so I don't think MWE would like a dirty rotting old dead guy lying around. Virus: Uhm... Yeah I would have to agree with you on that one. Scotty and Virus continue down the hallway into what appears to be the living room. A 52” flat screen T.V rests on the wall, a black leather sectional couch borders the back part of the room, a cast iron chandelier hangs form the ten foot ceiling with unlit candles arranged inside. The living room floor is covered in a black carpet, the walls remain the same color. Virus: Kind of a mid-evil look... Scotty: Yeah, I've always had a passion for that time era, you should see my dungeon! Virus's eyes widen, his jaw nearly drops. Virus: You have got to be kidding me... A dungeon? Scotty chuckles a bit, Virus kind of gets the joke but is unsure if he is right. Scotty: No, just a basement, but it's close enough. I have exact replica's of a lot of weapons from that era... swords, axes, scythes... all that kinda shit. Virus: Sweet, so there's a reason I'm here. Scotty: And what reason is that? Virus takes a seat on the leather couch and removes his jacket. His facial expression shows a mix of concern and excitement. Scotty leans against the opposite wall with his hands in his pocket, a cool breeze blows in through the window. Virus: Well I seen what you did in your last match and I must say, I'm impressed. You corrected a situation that caused a lot of problems, not just for you, but for MWE in general, and I want to personally congratulate you on that and you recent win. Why is he congratulating me? Before he wanted nothing more than to destroy me and now he is sitting in my living room acting like he's my best friend... Scotty: Uhmm thanks... Someone had to resolve that situation and I took the opportunity. Virus: So with that said, now you have a big match, tagged with someone who is as new as you are and probably just as good, facing off against Chris England and this newbie called Zack Crack...Cripple....uhmmm CRASH, that's right. You have got to have some sort of plan. Scotty: I usually make it up as I go, but before we talk about that, I have to ask... What the hell was going through your mind when you stole that cruiser from the cops? Virus rubs his chin with an evil smirk on his face as he thinks back to that vivid moment. Rustling his hands around the top of his head he gives Scotty a sarcastic smirk. Virus: Well I hate cops, and their incompetents. Besides, things had gotten oh so boring, that I needed to....jump start something. So I decided to steal that cruiser, to go for a joy ride. Plus, I'm the kind of guy who just does what he wants, and not give a fuck otherwise. Just what I would expect from someone like him, either way, he answered my question so now it's my turn to answer his. Scotty: Brilliant, well I guess I should answer your question. Scotty paces around his living room, watching the walls, keeping his ears in tune with everything around him. A violent breeze blows past the house cooling the immediate area instantly, almost in coincidence. Scotty: Well for starters I am tagged with Peter Kaymeckian. This man is new to MWE, but from what I seen, he can hold his own which is a plus, not to mention the man has talent and might go far in MWE, but that skill can only go so far and the moment he is in the ring with me as an opponent, I will do to him what I did to Jekyl... end his career. This man thinks that because he has money he is the best, but he's not, all I have to say about him is that once we step in that arena, were partners, but I promise him, if he gets in my way I will end him, Zack and Chris.. Virus nods his head in agreement. His eyes hold his deep thoughts of his own, his expressions tell his current emotions. Virus: Well first of all, he works for Brian Micahels. That means he must suck so bad, that he's learning how to be a professional cocksucker by MWE's legend of cock sucking. On top of that, my daughter, Liberty, could probably cut a better promo then he could....Like you, he has a lot of money. Unlike you, he can't invest it in anything worth shit, even though what he does invest in, he believes is a godsend. My advice, after you've carried your team to victory, do us all a favor and just waste him. Scotty smirks at Virus. The thought of making waste to Peter Kaymeckian after their match is a thought that brings him great pleasure. Scotty: Well I seen his promo and he thinks that because I was born with a platinum soother with diamond studs around my neck he thinks I am some big shot wannabe? Tito Ortiz is a great man and one hell of a fighter, he would do well to take some lessons from his school of hard knocks. Virus: Alright, enough bantering about Milometer Peter...What about who you are facing. Just what do you have that could indeed make you the true winner of this match? Scotty: Simple, I have already beaten Chris England once already. The funny accented son of a bitch got scared off and decided to stay in the box he was delivered in, he is probably still sitting in his own waste, making more while he shits his pants in fear. Zack Crash? What is so special about a retard who was handed a contract in gift wrapping and handed a match? He thinks I'm some sort insane lunatic that hears voices... Well yes, yes I am... I guess I'm so damn crazy... But here's the difference, The voice in my head makes sense and doesn't tell me when I need to pee, what is right and what is wrong...The voice in his head is as retarded as him and even the voice inside his head has a lisp. You're right in not underestimating me, I'm a dangerous mother fucker... in fact, I'm so damn dangerous I will crush you under my over sized novelty wallet that I can actually fill with money... BEAT THAT BITCH! Virus begins to laugh out loud, Scotty manages to get a chuckle in the midst of his thoughts. A warmer breeze passes through the room, Scotty's hair flutters in in the breeze as it passes him. The warmth feels good on his face. Virus: Well your opinion of Crash is valid enough. you were starting on England before, why did you stop? I'm curious as to what you have to say about him? Scotty: Chris England? There's not much to say really... The guy did a few good things, then went up against me, got cold feet and took off for a few weeks. He came back against my oh so loved tag team partner milometer peter and managed to win... So, Peter lost to England, I beat England for fuck sakes. Then in his last match he got demolished by Jay Riddick and was pinned the 1, 2, 3 right there... Virus: You're pretty good at remembering your facts... but between you and me, what do you think of the type of match they put you in? Scotty looks out the bay window and watches the trees sway in the calming breeze, the tranquility of the outside world calms Scotty down quite a bit. His expression sort of changes, his general aura shifts from a dark angry color to a medium neutral blue. Scotty: To be honest, I'm looking forward to this match. I may be stuck with a reject, and facing off against a retard with a lisp, and a yeast infected cum bubble chicken shit, but the thought of taking that 11 foot ladder and using it to do things that it was not made for.... well lets just say that I don't care if Peter is my partner, if he gets in the way of the steel 11 foot step ladder, he will get his teeth knocked down his throat too. Virus cracks a smile at Scotty, his teeth begin to show between his lips as his eyes shut, trying to imagine the thought of someone literally having their teeth knocked out by the top end of a steel ladder. Scotty makes a quick walk to the kitchen, Virus remains unmoved. The counter top is authentic granite, black appliances are placed in strategic areas of the kitchen, while a matching granite covered island stands in the middle of the room. Stainless steel pots, and pans hang above the island as an array of professional grade kitchen knife sets scatter the counter top while a double stainless steel sink sits underneath an average sized window; the breeze continues to blow through the house. Scotty: Want a beer? Virus sits up attentively and makes his way into the kitchen. Virus: Can you make it a Smirnoff I....holy fuck, what a kitchen.... Who designed it, Martha Stewart? Scotty: Good god no... Designed it myself... I actually quite enjoy being in the kitchen... cooking is one of my side hobbies, but sadly with MWE I haven't been able to get anywhere close to my kitchen. But yeah, I think I got some Smirnoff Ice in here... Scotty opens one of the double doors of his fridge, several shelves lined with food can be seen. The bottom two shelves are designated for beer, Liquor and other types of alcohol. Scotty pulls out a Smirnoff Ice and a cold Budweiser and hands Virus his drink. Virus: How about going to a bar? Drinking at home just doesn't seem right. Scotty: Preferably not, the last time I was in a bar I ended up in handcuffs and in jail. Virus starts to chuckle. Virus: I can't promise anything but we can go get a bite to eat and grab a beer while were there. Scotty takes a moment to think as he rests his beer on the island counter top. Virus leans against the fridge awaiting Scotty's response. Scotty: Sure, but if we get arrested, your paying the bail... Virus: Deal... lets get you and that voice in your head drunk before Independence day... Scotty: You got your car, or bike? Virus: Got the chopper out today. Scotty: Cool, park it in the garage, I'm driving. The scene begins to end as Scotty places the alcohol back in the fridge and the two head off down the hallway. A loud roar of Virus's motorcycle ends the scene. We will wait, wait for independence day to find out if I truly have what it takes to be a champion in MWE, to hold gold, to gain the glory of carrying a title belt. Zack... Chris.... Peter... see you at independence day...
