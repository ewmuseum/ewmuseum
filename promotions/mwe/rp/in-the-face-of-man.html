In the face of man

Aug 30 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The camera's fade into view of Alex and David Bowen setting on the ground outside of a 1700's cemetery house. The night is cold, but they both look quite content in the cold of the palace of the dead. Alex- People can debate, say, or yell what they feel. But a very powerfull man in the united states history once said the only thing thats true are death and taxes. That was a fact back when Ben Franklin said it. But today its not true, you can cheat on taxes, not pay them, or stiff the irs. But with death, it comes to every man. No mortal who walks the earth today can live longer than say 120 years? The fact that I'm trying to get to Ryan and Devlin is this... your jokes, humor no one but you. You name calling is at best a minor blemish on the burning legion. Nothing you, either of you do can ever compare to what the Bl past, present, and in the future will do. Your two idoits who got signed to mwe because the bigger guys need somone to job to them. So both of you I appalud you for your heart. But please just lay down come our match. Alex looks at david and gives him a small wink Alex- Because I don't even need to get in the ring, I could just let David take you both out. Get a chair, set it up on the ramp and get a beer to see the match. Hell I might as well buy a ticket, because what mwe is paying me this week is a joke for me to get in the ring with you. I love it, but please aganst a team who barely outweighs one member in the team. David laughs a little bit and stands up. 7 foot of a monster slowely raises off of the ground and brushes itself off. David- Brother are you making fun of my weight? Alex- No David- Just comparing huh? Alex- Seems like it so just set down. David sets back down. Alex- So like i've said before why really show up? What have you done thats so great. Your twins? Who cares, I can bench both of you. Not at the same time, but still both of you. David holds one of his fingers up and lets out a snort. David- I can do both. Alex- Shut up Daivd. You also have a foot and a hundred pounds on me. David- Don't worry brother I know my place. Were both here to wreck the ring, but I'm here more for the power. Boy of boy will It be fun getting in the ring with these guys. Its going to be the scotish highlands in there. I'm going to hammer throw them both across the ring. God I love wrestiling cruserweights, but really mwe I held my own aganst The Maxx in Aew and I'm getting 300g a year. When a idoit like daivd whitman gets perk after perk and 800g a year? Comon maybe i'll have to go talk to richard and get that shit settled out,' Alex- Yes we both will. Alex motions David to get up and they both stand up and start to walk down a hill to a gravel road conecting the graveyard together. The crunch can be herd all the way back about 5 yards from them. David- Thousands of years ago, the same fate was met for many of men. Rome was the center for all types of wrestling. My favorite infact, pure wrestling at its best. I love submission boys. Nothing like making another man tap out. And when I sink in one I'm going to make you turn blue, and get off when I say I get off. I dare the ref to say different. Alex and daivd continue there walk down the gravel road and meet the main intrance ware a old car is parked idoling. They walk over and lean aganst the car for a second, then open up the doors. Alex looks back Alex- Hollywood hoolagans, If thats what you like to be called. I've sent my warning. And come when the tag devision is opened. The burning legion will reign supreme, we shall whipe all of the deemed unworthy off of the roster. Alex and daivd get in the car and speed away, spraying gravel in the face of the cameramen.
