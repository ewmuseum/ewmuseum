The time Sinc stood still...

Nov 20 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The sound of a large door closing is heard as the scene connects with Sinc closing the front door to his house. He looks at his watch as he is seen dressed in a nice tan suit for an autograph signing event. It is mid day and he is almost late. He shakes his head and taps his feet. After a few moments of waiting Sinc hears an engine coming around the corner of his neighborhood street. Sinc: About damn time. Sinc then sees a taxi cab pull up in front of his house instead of a limo. The cab driver waves hello as Sinc looks displeased and walks to the car. Before he gets in the car he walks over to the driver. Sinc: And why the hell is there a cab in front of me right now? The cab driver is of Spanish descent, he bears a small mustache and is partially heavy. He is wearing a brown hoodie and jeans along with grey track sneakers. He is also harnessing a white cowboy hat on his black, ruffled hair. Driver: Sorry mi amigo the limo’s were all rented out by the other superstars. Sinc: You kidding me? Hey, do I look like a low profiled version of Fabio here? Come on Tiny Tim where’s the freakin limo. Driver: I’m serious my friend we have no limo’s. The company sent me here amigo. Sinc shakes his head again and kicks the ground. Sinc: Alright buddy boy I’ll play ball with you. Just get me to where I need to go. Sinc opens the back door of the car and enters the cab unhappy. The driver starts the car and they head off down the street to downtown San Francisco for Sinc’s autograph signing. Driver: Hey uh…you see the opening match for Mayhem last time? It was good huh? Man Whitman and Rated X were top notch. Even if they lost amigo it was still a match to see eh? The driver looks into his wind shield mirror as he steers and Sinc is in the backseat now even more unhappier than ever. He leans forward to talk in the driver’s ear with udder intimidation. Sinc: Are….are you kidding me? First off, quit calling me AMIGO! Comprende? Second off I am tired and exhausted after hearing so many times about how David Whitman and D.C. are so grand, are so GREAT. It pains me. It makes my ears bleed! You understand that Por’favor? And now I am stuck on a sh*t can on wheels with a man who literally smells like Oscar The Grouch! I was the freakin main event on Mayhem buddy and I get this? COME ON! Sinc lays back in his seat and rubs his head as if trying to get rid of a migraine. Sinc: Are we almost there or what? Driver: Bad day huh….Amig- I mean Mr. Mercier? Sinc: No just a really fed up one. I get dropped down to a low card match and then get pulled around in a yellow submarine what more could I ask for? What’s next is Sonny suddenly gonna come at me with a fake MWE title and claim that he is the best like out of some strange dream? Driver: Must have been a REALLY bad day…. After a few moments the two were done talking to each other and a few minutes later they arrived at the San Francisco Hard Rock Café in Pier 39 where Sinc quickly gets out of the cab, throws a wad of cash at the driver, and walks toward the café doors. He enters to a waiter standing at a podium with a smile on his face. He is of average height and has balding brown hair with horn rimmed glasses. He is wearing a black Hard Rock Café work shirt and black work pants. He stares at Sinc in an awkward manner. Café Worker: Welcome to the Hard Rock Café, your name please? Sinc rubs his head again and then puts his hands at his waist. Sinc: You don’t know who I am? Café Worker: I know you’re a customer here and we here at the Hard Rock Café sure golly gosh appreciate our customers. Sinc makes a sarcastic laugh as his eyes widen. He then tries to mimic the worker. Sinc: Well sure golly gosh you sure are one of the biggest dumb asses I have EVER MET! I’m on the VIP guest list, Sinc Mercier, I am here for the autograph signing Charlie brown, where the hell is Lucy, did she kick your balls today? The café worker begins looking at the guest list and shakes his head in confusion. Café Worker: Sorry sir but your not on our list. If you want me to get the manager we- Sinc: YES I WANT YOU TO GET THE MANAGER SO I CAN B*TCH SLAP HIM LIKE SNOOP DOGG AS SHUG KNIGHT! The café worker is sweaty now and is wiping his forehead with a handkerchief that he took out of his pocket as he frantically checks the guest list once more. Café Worker: Sir I assure you your not on here. Sinc: So your telling me that there is no autograph signing at ALL scheduled? Café Worker: We had one about….half an hour ago but it was for Dr. Phil. We had someone else booked but it seems the MWE cancelled the event so we had to find a last minute replacement. Thankfully dear old Dr. Phil came in for us. What a sweet man. Sinc’s face is red with anger and frustration. Sinc: Cancelled? They f*cking cancelled my signing? WHAT THE HELL! This is ridiculous! There has to be some mistake! Café Worker: There is no- Sinc gets in the worker’s face as veins begin popping out of his neck as he yells in his usual loud arrogant way. Sinc: IT WAS RHETORICAL YA FREAKIN JELLY BEAN! Why don’t you go to the jelly belly factory and pick yourself a nice juicy Harry Potter ass tasting special! Because that’s what your acting like, an ass! Café Worker: Sir please just relax…calm down….take a seat. Sinc: I am calm baldy! But Dr. Freakin Phil? You can do better than that! What did he have to do psych examine you til your brain explodes and your hair falls out because that looks like what he did to you Baby Huey! He’s bald too isn’t he? Hell put your heads together you’ll make two perfect sex tools as dildo rods! The worker is now in tears as the other customers in the café look at the ruckus going on, some confused, some irritated. Sinc takes a deep breath and tries to calm himself. Sinc: Look. Just get me your manager and we will be all good here ok? Before the worker could respond Sinc feels a tap on his shoulder as he turns around still angry without even realizing who it is. Sinc: WHAT! Sinc then realizes he is yelling at someone very familiar. It is his brother, once again as if to haunt him. His hair is slicked back and he is wearing a stylish leather jacket, stylish jeans, and grey T-shirt completed with a dog tag type necklace possibly from coming back away from the war. Jason Mercier: Is there a problem? Sinc takes a moment to take in the encounter and then suddenly is able to fully calm down as the worker then stops crying and begins drying away his tears. Jason Mercier: What’s wrong, it looks like you’ve seen a ghost. Sinc: No….just a little…surprised. It feels like I have just been trapped in Whoopi Goldberg’s hair though. Jason Mercier: Follow me little brother. Let’s uh….catch up on old times. Jason directs Sinc to a table, he then stops as the balding worker begins to speak again. Café Worker: Sorry Boss I just couldn’t handle him. Jason Mercier: That’s alright Carl you did your best to hold him off for me. Jason then finishes directing Sinc to a table as Sinc looks dumbstruck and shocked. Sinc: You manage the place? Sinc and Jason now sit at a front table in comfort as Jason then begins fidgeting with a toothpick he takes from a dispenser on the table. Jason Mercier: I own it Sinclair. Sinc: You what? Jason Mercier: You don’t seem to understand that when I found you drunk as a dying cow that night I was going through a lot of sh*t. Then I find out dad actually did not forget about me at all. He had a large sum of money waiting in the bank for me. He probably knew you would be successful in a wrestling career so he let me take that cash. Sinc: Wait a second wait a second. You were the guy on the street the night I was drunk? All I could see was a blurry figure with a gun! WHAT THE HELL MAN! Jason Mercier: Hey hey hey. Chill out. I was only trying to knock some sense into you. I thought you had forgotten about me. I was also going to lose my job, my house, my car, everything. I figure you’re the only one I could let out my anger on, and I know your always hanging around downtown. You don’t understand Sinc…you don’t….you don’t understand how much I HATED you when we were growing up. You were the epitome of my hatred. Sinc looks very very confused. It looks as if he has been caught like a deer in the headlights. But his eyes focusing winced as if his brother was those blaring lights. Sinc: So you wanted to kill me…because of that? What were you thinking? I was drunk I didn’t know any better, hell that night I was so hammered I didn’t know a rock from a garbage can. Jason Mercier: Maybe so, but I know you haven’t contacted me for months now. I told you you were going to start explaining things, but I already know to be honest. You had a hard time choosing your wrestling career over your comedy lifestyle that I understand, but you spent little time with your family, all dad wanted was to make you the best wrestler you could be, he had no time for me or mom, it was all you. And that is hard for me to accept, especially now….especially now since he’s….dead… Sinc: I…know that. I didn’t expect him to spend so much time with me. I told you and mom that I did not want to be a wrestler, but when dad looked at me with those beady fire filled eyes, I knew I had no choice. But if it weren’t for him Jason, I wouldn’t be where I am today or as successful as I am. You know that. Jason sighs as he then breaks the toothpick and throws the remains of it on the ground as a waiter comes by and picks them up then walks off to throw them away. Jason Mercier: You know….if I didn’t know dad had money saved for me I would be beating your sorry ass all over fifth and main. But he did….and that at least shows he still had some family values. And that he….that he still believed in me. Listen, After investing some of that money into stores and bars around this city I have been making plenty of money. If you still want that comedy career over that MWE crap then I can easily get you some local gigs with open arms. Sinc makes a look of disgust and disbelief as he tilts his head and makes a crooked frown as if he had just been told one of his workers or friends couldn’t get a job done. Sinc: So your saying…you’re the one who has been telling the company to hold off on the leisure and opportunities? That is low…real low J! Jason Mercier: What? I don’t know what your talking about. Sinc: You know DAMN WELL what I’m talking about. You’re trying to get me to leave MWE by taking away all the privileges and excitement of that company from me! It ain’t gonna work buddy boy! Jason grows quite upset from Sinc’s sudden outburst as Sinc stands in a huff. Jason Mercier: Sinc, SIT YOUR ASS DOWN RIGHT NOW! Sinc: SCREW YOU! Jason then stands and shoves Sinc back in his seat as Sinc then just lays his head down on the restaurant table and keeps it there in a depressed manner. Jason Mercier: I know your not quite happy with yourself right now but you have to understand what MWE does is their business. I have nothing to do with your anger issues or your lack of luxury or main event spots. But I do know you can do better than this. Sinc is still sobbing on the table in carelessness as Jason talks. Jason then slaps his head and he sits up, his face blank and containing a lack of care. Jason Mercier: You listening dip sh*t? I am offering you something better than MWE. No one can last forever in a career like that, at least in comedy you have something more to work with, a different type of crowd to entertain. You can be yourself Sinc. Sinc says nothing and puts his head back on the table. Jason Mercier: God dammit brother pay attention! Sinc slams the table and raises his head once more. Sinc: I’m not leaving MWE J! I’m staying with it. I don’t care how many spots I have to fall down, how much ignorance looms around me, how many people I have to stomp on, I will save myself from the clutches of failure. And if I have to fight Canadians as a show starter than so be it dammit. But I am not going to sit around and let myself, for lack of a better god damn word, sink down into oblivion. Pops wanted me to be successful, he wanted me to be the best damn wrestler I can be, and I am going to give it to him, I don’t care how many people, how many jelly bellied dumb asses or plate smashing buffoons I have to go through I am going to do it to show all those MERCIful fans out there that I am still in the game. Jason shakes his head in disappointment and throws his hands up in the air. Jason Mercier: What ever. Do what you want. I tried, just know, next time I see you your going to be singing to a different tune brother. Jason then stands up and puts twenty dollars on the table and walks out the doors. Sinc just sits there with his face in his hands in agitation. After a minute of sobbing with himself a waitress walks by his table and stops in front of him. She is rather tall and is seen wearing a white apron and white dress and shirt. Sinc looks up only to see a beautiful brunette haired woman looking straight into his eyes. She is another ex of his. Sinc: Ah sh*t…..dammit dammit dammit. This is just freakin awesome. Sinc says this looking back down and cursing under his breath. Waitress: Hello there Sinclair. Long time no talk. She then swoops into a seat across from Sinc and smiles at him. Her teeth a glowing white. Sinc: Hello Kendra, nice to see you again. Sinc suddenly snaps into a neutral mode trying to fake a bad smile. Kendra: I hardly ever see you around here anymore. But then I saw you blow up on one of our workers and I knew…I knew it was you Mr. Sinclair. Always the wild one. What brings you here? Sinc lets out an exaggerated moan and then follows by making a large upset sigh. Sinc: Long story short. I was supposed to have an autograph signing, but the company cancelled and Dr. Phil replaces me. Just wonderful. Freakin great isn’t it? Kendra: I doubt that’s the reason. Tell me why your really upset. Sinc nods and begins scratching his hand and ears in a nervous and saddened tick. Sinc: Well the truth is….my brother ends up being the man who almost kills me one night, I’m missing my dad incredibly, MWE seems to be having more important things on their minds, and I lose control of my emotions. What else is new? Kendra: Well your usually in those big shot interviews and promoting yourself all the time Sinc come on, why would anyone think any less of you? Come on now money man, you always knew how to make yourself heard, why stop now? Sinc nods in agreement and shrugs a bit still in a rather neutral state. Sinc: I guess I just feel stressed about this whole wrestling thing, still haven’t gotten over my pop’s death, have had next to zero time for family, Thanksgiving is coming up and I have no intention for any family time whatsoever. You know, a good friend of my mother’s who passed away a few years back once told me, “You’re a gentlemen and a scholar from you’re a**hole to your collar.” And to this day I take that to heart. I never really thought of myself as a gentleman but I always thought of my self as an a**hole. He was real creative with words that guy. Kendra then puts her right hand over Sinc’s arms and shows a look of empathy on her face. Kendra: You know….you really don’t give enough credit to yourself. You’re a champion, you’re a great guy, and we all know your funny, I mean come on, you always made me laugh when WE were together, what makes you think your not making your thousands of fans laugh and have fun with you out there and across the world huh? Sinc laughs and grabs her hand and squeezes it. Sinc: You know, your right, you always had this way with words that killed me every time, I couldn’t even retaliate because you were so damn smart….and so damn beautiful. I should have never fell in love with my career more than I fell in love with you. Sinc and Kendra both smile enjoying each other’s company. Kendra: Well you know I still watch you on the TV sometimes. How else would I know you’re a US champ right money man? Sinc grins and nods continuing to hold her hand. Sinc: Yea…I honestly didn’t think you’d still watch MWE when we broke up. Kendra: You kidding me? Pfft, gimme a break! I watch it all the time. You got me into it and for that I can never forgive you. She laughs in sarcasm as Sinc just listens with an interested gaze. Kendra: How about I pretend I’m your good friend Sean SPITZER and ask you about your match huh? They both laugh as Sinc questions her idea. Sinc: Hmmm….I don’t know….it may be a bit stingy. Kendra: OH PLEASE! Kendra slaps Sinc in the arm playfully as he rubs it and fakes an “OW!” expression. Sinc: Ok fine fine shoot away. Kendra let’s go of Sinc’s hand and flips her hair back as she straightens herself in her seat and tries to copy Shawn’s voice. Kendra: Well Sinc tell me about this Mason Resoan dude what’s his deal? She sounds like a very low toned woman trying to make a horrible impression. Sinc chuckles to her try at his voice and nods. Sinc: Ok that was pretty damn good woman. I’ll give you an A for effort. Do you really want me to answer your question? Sinc and Kendra are both in relaxed and calming moods now as she nods and laughs at Sinc. Kendra: Of course I want you to. Come on don’t be shy! Sinc shrugs and looks her blankly in the eyes with a hidden passion. Sinc: Ok fine. Well….Mason is a uhm…sensitive guy I guess you could say. He gets all choked up and all that mumbo jumbo about me not caring for MWE and all and how I am a fraud and junk. He has an ego bigger than his Canadian pride I guess you could say. And I don’t blame him because I was thinking of matching him up in the wrestling ring with my dog, he sizes up to his penis doesn’t he? And dogs are serious…you know…grr and all. Canadians can’t grr….just watch hockey and pretend they care. Canadian pride is priceless, but their annoyance is forever. I think Jekyl and Mason proved all that already. Kendra laughs at Sinc slapping him playfully again. Kendra: Come on Sinc that wasn’t very nice! Tendra tries to go into her SPITZER tone again. Kendra: Gee Sinclair, he sounds like a VERY tough opponent. But what about his big bad talk about taking you to school? Haven’t you graduated Sinc? Wasn’t it with Kendra Washington you graduated with as your girlfriend? Sinc continues to be amazed at Kendra’s impersonation. Sinc: Well SPITZER. Heh heh…I don’t see why I should go to school….I mean if I am correct Mr. Resoan was apart of an Exchange group of some kind. What was it? The CANADIAN Exchange? Does that mean he’s transferring to America from Canada? Because if he does I can pretty much…well…ALMOST guarantee he will probably see Osama Bin Laden and Lil Hitler there too waiting to make small talk with him. I think they will exchange a few blows together. That was serious. Kendra goes closer to Sinc as she almost begins flirting with him now and running her fingers through his spiky hair. Kendra: One….Last…Question money man….Will this….Canadian Wrestler….beat you at the Pay-Per-View do you think? Or will I have to beat you first. Sinc laughs in a low tone as him and Kendra are almost touching face. Sinc: I think his craving for maple syrup and plates ought to be enough to make him beat himself….if you know what I mean… Sinc stops facing Kendra for a moment and stares at the camera with a wink. Sinc: And if you didn’t know….now you can get the hell outta here. They then go for a slow, soft kiss and once they are kissing Sinc then puts his hand over the camera lens causing blackness and the scene cuts out.
