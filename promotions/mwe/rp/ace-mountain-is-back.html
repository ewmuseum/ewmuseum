Ace Mountain is back.

May 8 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I’ve been on this journey. It’s been eight months, thirteen days and fourteen hours. MWE has lost all its creativity. Our wells have dried up. It’s a state of emergency. I have accepted the mission to search for new inspirement. It’s lonely out here. My only companion is my ego, and this is our last mission before we head home. A female robotic voice comes over the screen. Systems, check. Engines, check. Navigation, check. Are you ready, MWE? All systems…go. Wait. We’ve been hit. Checking systems. Please wait. Please wait. Please wait. We’ve been hit by a meteorite. It claims to be a former Champion. It was part of some kind of “exchange” between Canada. Now two more. One has had tons of success in hitting other spaceships but is now washed up and is trying to knock out spaceships it can’t take. The other just likes to fuck other meteorites and party. Pitch black. Welcome back Mr.Michaels. You’ve been missed. You have now returned to… Ego World. Michaels: NOW WHERE ARE MY FUCKING PANCAKES? A scene quickly opens up in an iHop, where Michaels is holding a knife in his left hand, and a fork in his right hand. His lips salivate at the thought of a pancake in his mouth, which would be the first he’d had in months. His hair is tied back in his old-school form, in a ponytail. Michaels: I WANT THEM, NOW. A head or two spin their heads ‘round to ponder who’s making all the ruckus. Michaels: GIVE. He uses both hands to slam his table, causing the plates and drinks to jump up, almost tipping over with the orange juice in them. Michaels: ME. Michaels: MY. Michaels: FUCKING. Michaels: PANCAKES! The waitress now takes a look and comes to try and settle Michaels down, only for him to flip her off. She turn away and waddle off in a huff, looking disappointed. Michaels: In case you gap-toothed, rasslin’ loving motherfuckers have NOT noticed…Brian Michaels has been reborn. His smile and traditional grin are now back. He no longer has to cater to the fans or pretend to be someone he is not; he can just be himself and he is glowing. Michaels: I’m sick of this Mayhem Mafia bullshit. Michaels: I’m sick of watching Mr.Markowitz and Triple G waste talent because wrestling isn’t “fun” anymore…so I took care of them. As far as I’m concerned, Mr.Markowitz and Triple G bailed on me…all I did was take ‘em out. He shrugs his shoulders, as if it should’ve been done a long time ago. He doesn’t even stop to think that he could’ve crippled two young promising wrestlers. Okay, maybe not promising, but I gotta build em up a LITTLE bit here, y’know? Michaels: I mean, for fuck’s sake, Blood Fuze is lucky MWE even took them in and gave them a shot to shine…I saw their matches in Rev-Pro and I can honestly say, they’re the reasons Rev-Pro died, NOT Maxxamillion Mustafa! He chuckles, loving the Maxxamillion references in almost all of his promos. Michaels: David Whitman? Your shit is coming. Your shit is coming, don’t you worry your little hero, all-about-the-fans, emotional ass about that. Michaels: MWE, in case you’ve forgotten, I’m kinda like a big deal… He is in a full heel mode. His pancakes have just arrived, and he snarls at the waitress, who rolls her eyes at about the tenth customer that morning who had given her lip about being late with their order. Michaels eyes’ glaze at the pancakes, but he puts them off. Michaels: Virus and Vega Knight. You two are the biggest pieces of shit I have ever seen. Literally, if I could go to my toilet, and search my toilet’s archives, if it had archives, I wouldn’t be able to find two-meely mouthed pieces of shit as big as you two. He knows that talking about shit and other disgusting material could take away from his appetite, so he tries to tone it down a bit. Michaels: Wait a second…Vega and Virus must be in the sewer. Which means, the sewer is APWC right? APWC, the biggest disgrace of a wrestling promotion I’ve ever seen? Where Vega Knight is their World Champion? But he couldn’t resist comparing APWC to a sewer. He just couldn’t. Michaels: That’s laughable. Really. Michaels: Honestly, I have not seen a bigger group of pricks than you two. Virus walks around acting like he’s still somebody…newsflash Virus, you couldn’t even hold onto Peter Kaymackican’s jockstrap. Michaels: You walk around with your crack babies, Liberty, who’s bound to be a bigger whore than her own MOTHER! You have Demonica, who’s probably spending her hours that she calls being a “housewife” masturbating to ME, a guy who doesn’t have to use Viagra to, ahem, stay up. Michaels: Virus, take your Canadian ass back and watch yourself some hockey because I’m going to knock you on your ass worse than any of those hockey jerkoffs could. I think you forgot that I’m just about the best wrestler this company has ever seen and if you couldn’t get it done last time, tough shit…you won’t get it done this time either. I am BRIAN FUCKING MICHAELS, A 3-TIME MWE CHAMPION, and I am STILL the best this business has to offer, bar none…no D.C., no David Whitman and no Ace “I’m a racist because I’m too uncreative to talk about anything else” Cannon can tell you shit about THAT. He knows that he has taken a shot at Ace Cannon, and he loves it. You can tell the way his green eyes glow that he is in his happy place once more. Michaels: So put that on a stick and put it up your ass because I’m about to commit a sin Monday night…I’m about to kill a motherfucker. Michaels uses his right hand and knife and puts it very close to his throat, showing Virus a sign of his future Michaels: Because guess what, Virus? Your APWC Championships and GWC Championships, your accomplishments, won’t do anything come Monday. I do what I do Monday, and your accomplishments don’t mean shit…your basic insults, don’t mean shit…I can hype you to high hell and talk about how your lightning and fire creates too much for me to handle… Michaels: But I’d be bullshitting ya, and everyone knows you can’t bullshit a bullshitter. With this, he speaks in a very low tone…but what the camera doesn’t know is that Michaels is about to tell a little bit of a story. Michaels: Virus…after Mayhem, what I want you to do…is go on home to Demonica… Michaels: Fail to please her for about the thousandth time… Michaels: Then jerk off to yourself quietly… Michaels: And when Liberty comes to your bed, because she can’t sleep… Michaels: I want her to see the smallest dick she’s ever seen in her young, whorish life, being stroked… Michaels: Because then…and only then…will you know, how pathetic you REALLY are… With that, Michaels knows he has torn The Virus a new asshole. Figuratively, there is no comeback to what Brian Michaels has just said. It’s over, as far as he’s concerned. Not even Virus in his prime could top that insult in his mind. Michaels: As for the fuck buddy, Vega Knight…Vega, let me ask you…how great did it feel to win the APWC Championship last week against Gus? He bows to his pancake, as if beating Gus was the most prestigious honor in professional wrestling. Michaels: I bet you felt REAL good…I bet you went out to a club for about the millionth time in a week and got some “tail” that you totally didn’t pay to sleep with you, right? Michaels rolls his eyes, knowing that Vega really couldn’t get a girl if his life depended on it. Michaels: Vega Knight, I am not Gus, in case you haven’t noticed. I’m not a whiny bitch who left MWE because I wasn’t put in the World Title shot. I didn’t go to APWC because they promised me the World…I am the shit, not shit. He remembers Gus, who indeed did that. Gus, like most others who left MWE, left because they didn’t get what they wanted. That usually means they didn’t win their first match or didn’t get a title shot on their first show. Michaels: Listen up ya two-faced pussy, you know what I’m trying to say. Your APWC World Championship, it might mean something in your mind…it might bring you to orgasm, but no one in MWE gives two shits. Well, not even one shit. Y’know what’s funny, Richard Covell ACTUALLY told me that he could see Vega Knight become a World Champion because his gimmick could appeal to people. Michaels shakes his head, knowing that while many people have two-faces, it’s usually not good vs evil, but right vs wrong. Michaels: REALLY? Vega Knight, you’re worth about as much as Milenko to MWE. You serve no point, and you deserve to be fucking annihilated. He says this with complete seriousness, knowing he will give Vega Knight the same amount of effort he would if he were facing a D.C. or a David Whitman. Michaels: Mark my words…after this Monday, Vega Knight will cease to exist. Michaels: As for Mason? Michaels: Perfect? Michaels: Get the fuck out. Like, seriously. Get, the, fuck, out. He begins to bust out laughing, causing almost everyone at the iHop to stare at him as if he was retarded. He ignores them and just continues having a good time, thinking of Mason being perfect. Michaels: If Mason Resoan is perfect, then he’s Cable TV and I’m high-definition… Michaels: If Joseph Sharpman is Vanguard, then I’m the motherfucking atomic bomb that’s about to fuck up your military plans… Michaels: If D.C. is MWE’s Mike Tyson…I’m Muhammad Ali. Michaels rears back and pretends to punch the camera, chuckling to himself at comparing D.C. to Mike Tyson. Michaels: If Peter Kaymakcian wants a Title shot…tough shit kid, I GOT HERE FIRST! Michaels: If Kevin Hardaway is the future David Whitman, then I’m the future me, because I’ve beaten Whitman, three, count ‘em, three times… Michaels: If Zak Warner wants to talk about how he’s got someone always lurking around him…I’m that lurker…because you can’t get me out of your head, because I’m absolutely, FUCKING AMAZING. Michaels: Paige…you don’t like Michaels inside you? You MUST be LESBIAN! Michaels: If I had a penny for every time Joseph Sharpman told himself he was going to win superstar of the year in 2009, I’d have as much money as MC Hammer… Michaels puts his hand on his mouth, realizing that MC Hammer is indeed broke and not rich at all. Bad analogy, he thinks. Michaels: Oh WAIT! Michaels: Semi…Jesse Anderson, run ME over? All I gotta do is lure a burger over the ring, and the second your fat-ass smells it, you’ll think about gold…but not gold, as in championship…but the gold, sweet, honey mustard… Michaels: Jekyl, why don’t you go make a movie and just leave us the hell alone? As far as I'm concerned, you still sell Windy-O's in the corner, and no one buys it because you know what, Windy? IT TASTES LIKE CRAP! Michaels: Anthony Clark is going to take my woman? TAKE HER, SHE’S A CUM-GUZZLING BITCH ANYWAY! Michaels: Alejandro Rodriguez is a shell of his former self…his revival ain’t got anything on my revival. Michaels: Billy Joe Barker is your partner?
