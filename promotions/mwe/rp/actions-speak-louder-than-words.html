Actions speak louder than words; A James Preston RP

Sep 3 2010

James Preston

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It’s the evening of MWE’s greatest event of the year Crowning Glory. The show is almost finished and the penultimate match has just come to a close. ‘The Image of Perfection’ James Preston has just lost to Alexis Black after passing out during a submission move. We join the cameras in the backstage area where James Preston lays unconscious on a stretcher. Stood by Preston’s side is two EMT’s and behind them a half dozen event staff. This is the backstage interview area, to one side of the stretcher is a table, and on the table are a couple of microphones, some backstage passes and a monitor showing what’s going on in the ring. On the other side of the stretcher is the interview area, a big MWE backdrop and a mini titantron and lots of standing lights to light up the area. One of the MET’s leans over Preston with a bottle of smelling salts in his hand. He positions the bottle under Preston’s nose and almost immediately Preston springs into an upright position, blinking profusely and shaking his head. He has no idea where he is, last thing he remembers is being in the clutches of Alexis Black. As he remembers passing out it becomes clear he lost the match. A match up he worked his ass off to get in shape for, a match that he deep down felt he must win. As each second goes by his face gets more and more red, his anger at losing boiling up to uncontrollable levels. Visibly shaking now Preston reaches out and grabs the closest EMT by the throat and spins off the stretcher to his feet. He head butts the EMT square between the eyes sending him crashing down to the ground holding his face. He turns to the other EMT who has his hands out in front of himself pleading with Preston not to do anything. Preston smiles evilly at the EMT before quickly hitting him with a thunderous European uppercut square on the chin sending him two feet into the air before crashing down to the ground in a heap. Preston then turns and stares at the six event staff, the guy at the front puts his hands out pleading just like the EMT did. Meanwhile the rest of the event staff turn and run for the hills, unknown to the poor guy at the front that he has been left all alone. Preston approaches him and kicks him hard in the nuts sending him down to his knees; he then grabs his head and drives him into the concrete with a DDT. He then drags the guy up by his hair and irish whips him into the mini titantron sending the poor guy clean through it as the scene fades. We catch up with James Preston twenty minutes later sat on the floor of a utterly destroyed changing room. The wooden bench that once sat up against one wall of the room now lays scattered around the room as nothing more than dozens of pieces of broken wood. The doors have been ripped clean off the metal locker on the other side of the room to Preston twisted and dinted as if they had been the victim of a vicious sledge hammer attack. There is a circular hole about the size of a soccer ball in the far wall where Preston had thrown a medicine ball clean through it and the dozen or so items of clothing that once lived in the locker are now randomly scattered around the room. Preston is sat blankly staring at a spot on the wall as if it was the most interesting thing in the world. There is a knock at the door; Preston doesn’t move it’s as if he didn’t even here it. There’s another knock at the door, again Preston takes no notice. Out in the hallway is Chris England, having heard what went down when Preston awoke from being unconscious he has come to check on his long-time friend. **England** James its Chris if you’re in there let me in. Having got no response he knocks on the door once more. Meanwhile Preston is still sat in the exact same position, still paying no attention to the knocks at the door or even his best friend’s voice. **England** You better be decent I’m coming in. England opens the door and walks in, finding the chaos Preston has created, it doesn’t shock him too much, after all it’s not the first time Preston has destroyed a changing room. What does shock him however is the look on Preston’s face is. A look of utter blankness as he just stares away into what might as well be space. He approaches his friend and still nothing, Preston’s still blackly staring at the wall. England places his hands on Preston’s shoulders and shakes him profusely still nothing no response at all, he just continues to stare at that same spot on the wall. **England** SNAP THE FUCK OUT OF IT MAN! England slaps Preston square in the face, FINALLY a response as Preston looks up at England. However this isn’t the response he was hoping for no, this is a blank empty evil stare. Preston’s face starts to turn red with anger as England takes a step back. Preston pulls himself to his feet and starts to walk England down who is still back stepping, he walks England back till he can no further and his back is against the wall. England puts his arms out and pleads with his friend hoping he can get through to him. But this is not the James Preston England knows and loves, no this is a James Preston that will destroy anything that gets in his path and unfortunately for Chris right now that’s him. **England** Look man don’t do anything stupid, were mates we go back a long way. This attempt to talk Preston down does nothing at all. Preston could care less who he is right now, as of this moment Chris England is nothing but an obstacle that must be removed. He knees England in the gut and England doubles over, he then viciously elbows England in the back of the head knocking him out cold in one swift blow. He stares down at his long-time friend with a cold look of anger and hatred in his eyes. He has snapped he has gone to a place in his head that no one ever wants to go to and there isn’t a damn thing he can do to change that. Preston exits the room without a care in the world and heads off into the distance. Two Weeks Later Its Sunday the fifteenth of September Two thousand and ten, it’s the day before MNM and nobody has seen or heard from James Preston since Crowning Glory. James Preston is scheduled to face Craig Zachery in a little under twenty-four hours. Having not been able to get in touch with Preston to tell him he decides to call one of Preston’s known friends, and independent circuit whiz kid Quentin ‘Razor’ Sharpe. Ring Ring **Sharpe** Sup your through to the razor. **Eli Silver** Hey its Eli Silver here in just calling to…. Sharpe cuts off Eli Silver he has no idea who he is and thinks it may be one of them annoying speculative calls where some ass try’s to sell you something you really don’t want even though you keep saying no thanks. **Sharpe** Who? **Eli Silver** Eli god damn Silver the general manager of Monday Night Mayhem, Jesus kid if you ever want to get into the big leagues’ you better start doing your fucking homework! **Sharpe** Ooooo so what you want, finally realised I am the best freakin free agent in the business today and want to kiss my ass to get me to sign on the dotted line? **Silver** Keep dreaming kid. No I want to know if you have heard anything from James Preston, his phones been turned off for two weeks and he has a match with Craig Zachery tomorrow, I sent him an email but I’m not sure if he got it. You know where he is? **Sharpe** Preston yeah I know where he is, the same place he has been for the last two weeks. Locked up in his apartment staring at the wall, the guys lost the plot, I mean he went fuckin loopy after CG and knocked out his best friend for fuck sake. **Silver** Yeah I heard about that, but look kid I really need you to get the message to Preston that he has a match tomorrow. If you do that for me I will give you a try-out with MWE whenever you want one. **Sharpe** Yeah I can do that but don’t expect the guy to show he’s gone bat shit crazy, but I will see what I can do. **Silver** Thanks kid and like I said I owe you a try out. Two hours later Sharpe is stood in the hall way of a modern apartment block outside number 27, James Preston’s apartment. He has already knocked on the door a couple of times but there has been no answer. Luckily a few weeks ago Preston had given Sharpe a spare key to his apartment and permission to use Preston’s home gym whenever he pleases. Sharpe pulls the key out of his pocket places it in the door, opens the door and enters the apartment. The place looks like a bombs gone off, empty take away cartons and beer cans are strewn all over the place. The lights are out in the living room but there is a distant light on coming from the gym so he heads for the light. Sharpe Enters the gym. **Sharpe** What the fuck! On the far wall in front of Sharpe is a huge printed out image of Craig Zachery, there is a knife through where Zachery’s nose is holding it on the wall. Written in huge letters above the image are the words DEATH TO ZACHERY and it appears that they have been written in blood. The gym stinks of sweat as if Preston has been doing nothing for the last two weeks but working out. Sharpe is really freaked out by the image and words and turns to leave. However as he turns James Preston is stood right in front of him holding a knife. He must have heard Sharpe speaking and ran to see what was going on. Preston pushes Sharpe right through the gym till he is up against the far wall, the picture about a foot over his head. He holds the knife to Sharpe’s throat and evilly stares into Sharpe’s eyes. Preston’s lip is quivering with anger and he isn’t the least bit pleased to see Sharpe in his place. **Sharpe** Woaaaah James it’s me, your friend Quentin. The expression on Preston’s face doesn’t change one bit, this James Preston has no friends. All this version of himself wants to do is cause pain and suffering. He wants to destroy everything in his path. But deep down the old James is still in there and that’s what makes him let Sharpe go and as he does Sharpe makes a bolt for the door, he runs straight out the apartment and just keeps on running. Back in the house Preston turns and faces the picture of Craig Zachery an evil smirk rises across his face and then he drives the knife in his hand straight into the picture. He then breaks out into a sinister laugh as he stands there staring at the picture and imagines all the pain and suffering that he is going to rain down on Zachery. The scene fades out as we see Quentin Sharpe running like Forrest Gump off into the distance he just keeps running and running and running.
