I hate babyfaces and humble pie; A Grand Mystique RP

Jul 14 2009

The Grand Mystique

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Don’t you love nice summer days? Not even a fluffy white cloud; the kind of day that warms your very being by being wholly bright and positive. On the horizon, we see flowing green countryside. Hills rise and fall like a perfect green carpet. Fields intersect it like a horticultural patchwork. From our slightly elevated position, we also see a small lake, which sparkles like a silky sheet. From it, a wide stream winds, twists and turns into the hillside. You just don’t see many sights like this anymore; not many at all. It’s a stunningly hot day and with the slightest breath of wind to barely offset the simmering heat. The breeze makes it just the nice side of barely tolerable. It’s a really nice day. Did I already say that? Anyhow, it’s far too pleasant for it to be ruined by something as petty and inconsequential as a bad mood. However the man who is shuffling along is doing his very best. As he skulks in a very skulking fashion along the dirt track that lines the field, he is doing his very best impression of Eeyore. He is suffering heavy facial bruising but is trying to hide it with the wide lenses of aviator sunglasses. He is wearing a typically sullen and dull outfit; a blue t shirt and three quarter length trouser combination. It’s minimal yet given the heat it’s also suitable. As he limps closer, we see bruises that line his arms and a swollen wound above his left eye that is roughly stitched shut. The bruising around the swelling is a particularly vibrant variety of purple and looks tender and sore. It just doesn’t seem right that this grand misery of a man cannot enjoy the most fair of days. We can clearly tell that this is the Grand Mystique. Beaten up, feeling extremely sorry for himself and engulfed in his own depression. Without a mask on, he can hide this from the world no longer. The weather does not match his mood at all. GM, having come back has gone to a non finish with Paige and DC before being taken to the absolute limit by the latter at Independence Day, is enduring the physical consequences of those limits. He kicks out at a loose stone that is on this track. It skids off ten yards ahead before hitting the edge of the tree bark and disappears down the other side of the slope. “That is where I thought my career was going to go if I didn’t beat DC. The toilet.” “But now I’ve been told that I am the presumptive number one contender to the MWE Heavyweight Championship. That title is currently in possession of Sinc Mercier.” He walks up to the tree. He hears birds chirping and tweeting contented and safe within the pleasant shade of the branches and wishes he could sound as at ease. “I should be on top of the world.” He rubs his finger along his bruised brow, onto his hairline, as he examines the bark. “But all I can think about is the past and it’s relation to my future victim.” He walks away from this lone tree and casts his gaze over the uneven landscape. “When I see Sinc and Mason, Christina and Sapphire, all I see is Maxx and.....” He looks to his right and takes in the figure that has appeared next to him. “You.” The camera pans around GM in dramatic slow motion that blurs the surroundings yet frames the surprise on his face. Next to GM is a face that we in MWE have not seen in about six months now. Her perfectly styled brunette hair dances gently in the breeze and in a flattening black dress, her skin radiates pure energy. “Are you still jealous?” She is Chloe Cornette, the former girlfriend of his tag team partner. She is his one remaining memory of their tandem – GM and Maxx were doomed but she was a distinctly pleasant thing to reminisce. “No – you are a figment of my imagination, Chloe. Besides, I am merely comparing your relationship with Maxx.” He looks at her and then looks away. The shock on his face fades away to nothing, as if her presence is totally normal and not at all a little disturbing. “You, Christina, Sapphire – all absolutely top of the league. Stunning. Gorgeous. And nothing but an arm piece, a public display of affection to satisfy the inner geek.” He walks away from his hallucination, brushing by the bark of the tree. He continues on down this dirt track, kicking up more dirt as he goes. “And yet Maxx, Sinc and Mason are bargain basement. Overhyped and desperate to flaunt any sort of success. That’s why I expect to hear nothing but how bad my weekend is compared to theirs.” He turns and sees Chloe directly behind him again. He looks Chloe up and down generously to check that this being is truly all there. “Why have you chosen now to show?” “I’ve always been here.” “But why can I see you now?” GM is confused and concerned at the timing. In his mind, it could not be worse. “Because Sinc and Christina, Mason and Sapphire remind you of Maxx and I. Because you are jealous of their success, personal and professional.” Her tone is bouncy and matter-of-factly as it has been the entire time. “You’re manifesting yourself as my jealousy?” “Can you think of anything better?” “I should be jealous more often.” He says it quietly and then wonders why he is trying to hide perversion from this illusion of Chloe. “Your jealousy will handicap you.” “I’ll handle it.” He says this through gritted teeth in utter determination. “You can’t kill Sinc and Mason like you did Maxx. They won’t give up as easily.” “......I know.” He is hesitant and reluctant to agree to this point of view. “So what are you going to do?” “I will use their good luck against them.” He bends down and picks up a particularly large chunk of brown dirt. “What do you mean?” GM pauses, contemplating what to say and quite how to say it. Chloe moves behind him. “The simple fact is that the outcome of their Independence Day was the result of circumstances outside of their control.” His voice is almost too quiet as he plays with the dirt in his right hand. With his left he wipes his perspiring brow gently with his wrist. “And you lost.” “Sinc Mercier as World Champion.” He gets louder, looking up at the sky as he does. “I can hear the rant now. What he won’t say is that he didn’t retain his title. Nic Kraven stole it from Brian Michaels and dumped it back into his undeserving hands.” “But you lost.” “Mason Resoan wins the US title because Virus can’t keep it up for thirty minutes. Maybe he should get that seen to. That can’t be healthy.” GM squints his eyes as the broad, burning sunlight pierces his wide sunglasses. “And yet I lose to DC after he beat the living crap out of me.” GM removes his glasses to show his facial wounds in full, bloody HD.”After I took him to the absolute limit; no one has tested him like that since Adrenaline Rush. But I’ll guess they will just neglect to mention that small fact.” “And I guess that for the time being they will not consider me a serious threat to Sinc’s World title. They will not share the view that DC is ten times the legend that Sinc Mercier will never be.” GM places his glasses on the top of his head “It will be a matter of time before their luck runs dry.” He takes a deep, pensive breath. Chloe floats gracefully to his right. She leans forward and whispers in his ears. Her lips sparkle in the sun and brush by his ear lobe. “They won’t lie down you know. They have proven themselves in this company.” “I know.....MWE through and through” GM whispers, equally delicately. “More than you will ever be.” Their faces are practically touching. GM stays there for a second, enjoying the moment. Until he remembers the situation. He pulls away and walks away, down the road. “They have lived and breathed their careers here. Endured failure and massive successes.” “And dare I say they actually bear a comparison to you and Windy Bridges.” Inevitably she follows him as he leaves a stream of dust in his wake. He walks away, trying to come to terms with the fact that he is effectively talking to himself. He realises that he doesn’t need to have this going on, not with the biggest matches of his career coming up. “I know that Sinc and Mason have a storied history.” He plays with the dirt pebble in his fingers, similar to how a poker players plays with his chips. “In many ways it is akin to the battles that I shared with Windy Bridges. Back then Windy had a future. He had something that he believed in and we brought ourselves up to a level where few would match us.” He approaches a steep bank that has begun to run parallel to the track. A stream of water trickles by at the bottom. He seems entranced by it. “The level to where you can main event Crowning Glory 2009.....the biggest event in the history of this company and dare them, all of them not to take you seriously.” His tone is low and deadly serious. “And I have not sold out along the way. I have been true to myself the entire time. Mercier and Re-ozone sold their own personalities to fans just to get over with them.” “You wouldn’t have done that?” She appears again. He had almost forgotten about her. “Ugh. The idea of it makes me sick. I am who I am and it is for the better.” “And you are not the only one to realise this are you?” GM drops his dirt pebble into the stream. It causes a small ripple and causes a pair of minnows to dart away from the impact and the cloudy water it has provoked. “It is always great to see revitalised characters, Whether they be legends or not. But even I never thought I’d see Kevin Hardaway have the drive and desire to make the impact that he did at Independence Day.” “To see that happen at the expense of his best friend.....It was refreshing to see such brutal honesty dished out so effectively.” GM seems happier when talking about Hardaway, considering how his turn had so many comparisons. “That reminds you of Maxx doesn’t it?” Her voice betrays no emotion. “Of course. I mean who saw it coming? It was a total shock that no one expected.” He speaks with a bouncy confidence, as he often does when reminded of the incredible events prior to their Tag Title match at Adrenaline Rush. “It’s going to be a pleasure to be partnered by him and I hope that he is able to bring some more of that refreshing attitude to our match on Monday night.” “Because unlike Sinc and Mason, Kevin Hardaway has proven himself to be anything but a failure. One could say he is a GWC legend making a legendary impact in MWE. He is a proven draw, a wrestler of the highest calibre and his presence will only improve the match and our chances.” GM’s respect is clear. In this instance, respect truly is earned as Hardaway’s reputation preceded him and GM knew he can appreciate that. “He has impressed with his hunger and desire. He is not content with resting on his GWC achievements and he clearly intends to continue his recent and impressive form. That is very good to see and reminds me of myself.” He seems revitalised, consoled for a moment by the prospect of main eventing Monday Night Mayhem in another PPV worthy match against opponents with real pedigree. “On the other hand, Mason is coming off the back of a US title win and I am sure he is very happy. But he is in possession of a belt, on top of a division that is insignificant, thanks to the decisions of his partner. What does that say about Mason?” The difference between his opinions on Hardaway and Resoan is evident. GM’s tone is cut with sarcasm about Mason’s recent achievements. “He can barely win a match in the main event and when he gets relegated, he is given a title win. He is not good enough to hang with this competition. Sinc has four United States title reigns – but that doesn’t indicate that he made the belt meaningful. It has not been special or relevant since I held it in EW where the talent made it mean worthwhile, the next step on the ladder.” GM’s experience with the United States title used to be set in his mind as losing the belt in EW to Windy Bridges. It was a bitter pill to for him to swallow but recent experiences have tempered his take on the past. Just like the sediment in the water in front of him had begun to clear, he was able to get over it. Given that, GM clearly feels that he is relevant enough to judge the new champion. “Hardaway, unlike Sinc or Mason, does not know how to settle for second best. He will not accept an indecisive outcome and he, like me, would prefer Merciers’ pitiful excuse for a title reign to be ended sooner rather than later.” “He has also rid himself of the humble pie gobbling ways of the babyface. It’s always refreshing to see actions reflect the true madness behind the flesh.” When GM begins to talk about Sinc and his title, GM speaks with decidedly more intent as he knows the importance of every work that he utters. His eyes become mere slits and his lip curls slightly as is the way of so many bad guys before him. “Nobody gives us a chance. No one considers me able to beat Sinc full stop. This is where I begin to prove people wrong! – That is what people are saying of me at the moment but you know what.....fuck ‘em . Let them talk. I don’t have to listen anymore.” GM pokes the temple of his head. His finger presses the large bruise but he doesn’t wince even though it must hurt. “I don’t need to listen. Soon, you will be silenced – the great talkers of our time. I need to find the mute buttons for the future of this business. But I am sick of their bullshit. The fans can’t see the future for all the shit coming out of their mouths.” “Sinc, I know you have suffered to get where you are now. But trust me, when I am done and our journey to Crowning Glory is over, you will see my madness and feel its’ full force.” GM stands up again, and as he does, he once again surveys the rolling hills and valleys around him. He sees the road he is on and the stream next to it. He is able to see where he has come from and where it will go. He stands up, wipes his brow again while checking his stitched wound. He then begins again to walk down this path. “And I promise you that the road to Crowning Glory will live up to these grand expectations. Our match at the end of it will be one for the ages and at last we will see a brand new World Champion. It will make you the legitimate champion that you crave to be but your loss will be my gain.” Now in front of the camera as he walks, GM slaps his chest and the dark blue cotton clings to the perspiration on his chest and the impact is visible. He is becoming more and more motivated, practically salivating at the mere prospect of Crowning Glory. “Someone who was not around in the original incarnation of the company. Someone who had to work from the ground up to get the shot will become the pinnacle of this industry.” GM pounds his fist into his palm as his verbose chat gains momentum. “It will be the passing of the torch – the dawn of a new era and a celebration, to give hope to all those who aspire to be someone meaningful in life.” “The MWE title has become a burden to you and you need carry it for only a few weeks more. It is a burden that I WILL be relieving you of. You only need to be patient young comedian.” GM pats and then rubs his right shoulder, signalling that is where the strap would lie. “I have forgotten what it feels like to have the weight of a belt on my shoulder. It was a nice feeling and I have not felt it in the longest time! But you know I am not even sorry that it’s you that that I have to take it from because even if I have to pry it from your cold, dead hands, I have no qualms in doing just that!” He stops and looks around, realising that with no Chloe, he is totally alone. One being, in total sync with himself towards one purpose. “It’s not your fault. I mean, it came all too soon – you just weren’t ready. You have not been at your best. You are trying to use bigger words and funnier jokes and you are just showing yourself up to for failure of epic proportions.” “This opportunity has been one year in the waiting. Do you really think that I wait that long to throw it away?” GM becomes calm. He slows his breathing down and swipes the back of his hand again his forehead one last time. He looks around, knowing that no matter how fast he talks or how hard he dreams, Crowning Glory will not come around any quicker. He beams a generous smile, clearly enjoying the fact that he is getting into his stride. “Every week to Crowning Glory, you will experience a test. I will test your being like never before and by the time Crowning Glory arrives, you will be ready to crumble before me. Your soul will refuse to take any more punishment. Your destiny will catch up with you. My destiny finally comes forth. Enjoy the time you have left.” GM is now composed; certain that he is in control of himself, his career, his life.....his destiny. He checks the wound on his brow with a gentle fingertip before refocusing on the camera, which is now directly in front of him. His eyes are wide open now, ready and waiting to take every little detail in. “You have always been a disappointment Mercier. Mason, you are not much better – you two suit each other. Your fans deserve to fall over themselves for you, because you will never be loved by them as much as you two love each other.” “My advice is simple - let your world fall out of your control. It was never in your hands.” He turns around and walks down into the valley. As he walks away, we can hear one final comment from GM. “I hate tag matches.” With an understated arrogance, it is a sign. An indication that GM has taken a turn for the better. This confidence is what we are so used to seeing from him yet it has taken all this time for his self belief to show signs of resurrection since Independence Day.
