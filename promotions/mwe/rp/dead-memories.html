Dead Memories; Brad Kane Production

Aug 31 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The sounds of yawning in the mid morning, probably around nine or so. Standing around in a pair of red gym shorts, looking out the bedroom window happens to be Brad Kane. Megan is beginning to stir behind him as it looks like the two had a rough night with the kids. Lacey, Brad's oldest daughter, is jumping on their bed now, rain pouring down from the skies. She's giggling like crazy before Megan grabs her and gives her a kiss on the forehead. Brad spins around to see his wife tickling his oldest daughter. A smile comes across his face before he leaves the room to check on the other babies. Lilly is awake and is looking at her little sisters sleeping. That smile is still on Brad's face before he goes to check on Ryan. Ryan is sleeping himself, Brad going further into his room. He looks down at his son with a stuffed tiger right next to him. It's a little worn and worse for wear but Brad keeps himself from trying not to tear up. ----- *SIX YEARS AGO* Busy street in New York City. Walking around with his son grasping his hand is Brad Kane, although, you know, younger looking. His son, Chris, is only four and the two are laughing. They come across a bench and they sit down. Brad looks at his boy and smiles. Brad Kane: Having fun today Chris? Chris Kane: Yeah! We are we gonna do next?! Such a cheer in Chris' voice. Brad can only shrug his shoulders to his question though although a sly smile is on his face before he looks back over at Chris. Brad Kane: Well that's all up to you. We can either go back home or we can go to a certain toy store and a certain kid might get himself, oh I don't know, a toy of his choice. That puts a smile on the kid's face, a huge one in fact. Chris nods his head before the two get up and start walking again, going to any toy store that's around them. Sadly there isn't a Toys-R-Us around this area so they settle for a smaller store. Barely sees any business as the lack of other people in the store showcases. Chris looks around before running off to the stuffed animals. All little kids like a stuffed animal or two but he isn't looking at the bears. Instead Chris is looking at a tiger. Think of what Hobbes looked like from the Calvin and Hobbes series. Chris picks up the tiger and Brad nods his head, letting him know it's just right for him. ----- *PRESENT* It's now around eleven in the Kane mansion. Brad is working out in the gym basement, Megan working alongside with him. Lacey and Lilly are both in the gym with them although they're just running around and playing in the wrestling ring they have down there. Brad is spotting Megan on the bench press right now. She's not lifting too much but not too bad for a woman of her size. Just enough to maybe pick up her body weight but that's about it. Megan stops her lifting and looks for a towel to wipe the sweat from her face. Brad sits down on the bench now before leaning back and getting ready to do a few sets at the weight Megan has set, just to show off to his wife. Megan laughs as she sits on him, trying to make his lifting that much harder. Megan Kane: Okay Mister Macho, let's see you do this weight with me on you! He grins before doing a few reps. After he sets the bar back on the rack, Megan leans down and kisses his bare chest. A smile is on his face again before turning his head to look at Lacey and Lilly running around with no regard for their own safety in the ring just like how he did back in his old Reckless Jack days. ----- *FOUR YEARS AGO* A rabid crowd, booing the hell out of someone. Brad is on the mat, bleeding a small bit from his forehead. He looks out into the crowd, seeing his son in the front row along with his ex-wife. She's pretty much disinterested in what's going on but Chris is yelling for his dad to get up. Brad winks at him before kipping up to his feet. His opponent isn't much larger then he is so Brad is able to hit a superkick before bringing him in and hitting him with an ace crusher. Brad then goes to the top rope and hits a giant frog splash before pinning him for the win. The crowd explodes with cheers as Brad stands up, getting his hand raised in victory. Brad leans down on his opponent and nods his head. He rolls out of the ring and grabs Chris out of the front row before putting him in the ring apron. Chris goes nuts and runs around in the ring before Brad whispers something into his ear. His son nods his head before he jumps onto the prone man with a splash of sorts. Brad drops down and slaps his hand on the mat three times before the crowd goes even crazier. ----- *PRESENT* Lunch time now. All the babies are in their high chairs, Lilly and Lacey in booster seats. Brad sits at the head of the kitchen table, looking at all of his kids. Megan is at the microwave, getting Lacey and Lilly their lunch of a hot dog and some french fries. Now Brad gets a plate given to him with a turkey breast sandwich on it along with some chips. Megan has the same thing but she's busy feeding Kaylee before she can manage to get anything eaten. Brad looks around at the table and feels a peaceful calm overtake him. ----- *EIGHT YEARS AGO* A small apartment, just enough for a couple that's become newlyweds and a baby. Brad is sitting at the small table with baby Chris in his arms, trying to feed him but it’s not working out too well. Chris is refusing everything that Brad tries to give him. Brad switches his grasp on Chris, letting his head be on his shoulder. Slowly the child begins to calm himself, ready to eat. Brad switches him back to his lap and picks up the spoon. Now Chris begins to eat a little bit. Brad kisses his forehead, smile on his face. ----- *PRESENT* Middle of the afternoon, call it two thirty or so. Brad and Megan are still in the kitchen but they're fixing a snack for Lacey and Lilly. Out comes a few apple pieces for Lacey while Lilly gets some grapes. Both of them actually look thrilled to be getting fruit instead of candy. Pretty shocking but whatever. Brad stares into the fridge, looking at an empty bottle of vodka he keeps in there as a constant reminder of what he used to be... ----- *THREE YEARS AGO* Hotel room, Brad is sitting on the bed with a bottle in his hands. His son is sitting on the floor, watching TV while his ex-wife is at the table in the room, reading a book while pregnant with Lacey. Chris reaches for the remote and puts it onto Cartoon Network. Brad blinks a couple of times before taking a drink, setting the bottle down on the small table. He leans forward and shakes his head, upset with his son and obviously drunk. Brad Kane: The fuck you doing? Chris turns his head back, a little confused as to why his dad would be so upset at him watching cartoons. Not like he never changed the channel in the hotel room before. Chris Kane: Just gonna watch cartoons, dad. That's it. Brad Kane: I was watching Sportscenter you stupid little shit! Put it back right fucking now! Lucy, the ex, raises her head from her book to see Brad becoming angry. Chris reaches for the remote but in his haste, he accidentally knocks it underneath the other bed in the room. He begins to scramble for it but by then, it's all but too late. Brad is standing up and yanks Chris up by his shirt, ripping part of it. The first blow comes in the form of a backhand across his cheek. A fire is in Brad's eyes, picking a now crying Chris back up and slapping him again. Lucy: BRAD! STOP IT! Brad Kane: Shut the fuck up bitch. This is my boy! Brad hits Chris in the right eye this time, Chris going back down to the floor, crying his eyes out, Then Brad looks at his hand and sees blood on it, his own son's blood. He slumps down to the floor, beginning to weep himself over the monster he let take control. ----- *PRESENT* A clock says its eight at night now. Brad is back in a pair of gym shorts again and a white t-shirt as he's reading a story to Lacey, trying to get her to sleep. All her sisters are sleeping soundly but for some reason, Lacey is having a problem tonight. Might just be one of those nights for a three year old girl. Lacey Kane: Daddy, I love you. A smile comes across Brad's face, closing the story book. Brad Kane: Love you too Lacey. Brad sets the book behind him on her little bed. Lacey yawns for a moment, a curious look popping onto her face. Lacey Kane: Who Chris? Mommy talked 'bout him. Without showing a trace of emotion... ]Brad Kane: Chris was my first child. Lacey Kane: Where he? The expressionless look is off of Brad's face, replaced with a frown. Lacey is a little scared that she upset her dad. Although she's still young, Lacey knows about her dad's problems with depression. Brad sighs before looking at his oldest child. Brad Kane: He's... he's just a dead memory honey. That would naturally confused a three year old. Lacey Kane: What that mean? Brad Kane: When you're older, I'll explain it to you. Lacey Kane: 'Kay. She yawns again, getting tired now as Brad stands up from her bed. The blankets get pulled up for her, stuffed bear next to her head. Brad kisses her forehead before leaving the room, shutting off the light behind him. He goes back into Ryan's room and stares at him. ----- *NINE YEARS AGO* Giant hospital. There stands Brad, looking through a giant glass window, looking at his newborn son for the first time. He looks at the label on the little basket/bed thing. "Christopher Bradley Wallace" ----- *TWO MONTHS AGO* Different hospital, same situation. A smile and tears of joy are streaming down Brad's face this time, looking at his newborn son again. Once more he looks at the label. "Ryan Keiichi Kane" ----- *PRESENT* Back into the giant bedroom that belongs to Brad and Megan Kane. The lights are on, showcasing the various pictures of the two and their children. Both are dressed for bed but neither are in the oversized sleeping area. Instead the two are seated in nice reading chairs. Brad has the latest issue of ESPN magazine while Megan's bare feet are propped on his lap. In her hands is a Cosmo magazine. Neither of them say anything for a few moments, letting the silence fill the air. Megan lowers her magazine to look at her husband. Before she can say a word, her eyes catch the picture that will always be placed over his chair. Once again, it's a picture of his son, Chris. A frown creeps onto her beautiful face. Megan Kane: I wish I could've met him. He lowers his magazine now, beginning to pay attention to his wife. Brad Kane: I know. It's still hard on me after the past two years. You don't know how often I want to wake up and see him looking at his baby brother. I know Chris would've been a great big brother to all of his sisters. He would've been looking out for 'em when they get to the age of boys and stuff. He would've been their protector when I'd be older and less likely to be threatening to some jerk. But he was taken from me by my own selfishness... Megan shakes her head, disagreeing with her husband. He places so much blame on himself for Chris' death that it always worries her when he begins to talk about it. Megan Kane: It was a horrible accident Bradley. You were sober when it happened and thousands of miles away from him. No matter how much you blame yourself, his death wasn't your fault! He stares off into space, letting her words go on deaf ears. Brad feels like it is his fault because his son died. If he never touched alcohol, he never would've put Chris in the position to be with his mother thousands of miles away instead of being with his dad. The blame he places on himself almost ruins him some nights. He'll stay awake and stare at that picture, wondering what his first born could've been. Brad Kane: You just don't seem to understand. It is my fault. If I had never started drinking then he never would've been back in Iowa in the first place. Because of my problem, my addiction, I killed my son because I struck him and made him cry. I made him and I destroyed him in a matter of six years. It was six years I had him in my life before I forced him off. All because he wanted to watch a cartoon. Sometimes I can't live myself because of what I did. Megan Kane: But hey, at least you have Lilly out of this now. If Chris hadn't of died, we never would've gotten Lilly, we both know that. And because of Lilly is why you have another son now. It all balanced out honey. You can fix the mistakes from Chris with taking great care of Ryan. We both know that Ryan is going to be your favorite because he is your son. You'll have more help raising a son this time because he'll have a better mother then what Jessica or Lucy was. We both love this boy, not just you! You owe everything you have now to Chris' death. This house, your six kids and me. Isn't that enough to fill the void he left in your heart or are we just not good enough to you? Brad begins to open his mouth but he quickly closes it, just nodding his head instead. Still, even with those words from Megan, that burden will always be a stain on his soul. He caused so many people pain and it all ended the moment Chris died. Perhaps having Chris was all but a lesson in not how to act before starting a family. Furious Styles said it best in the movie Boyz N Tha Hood. "Any fool with a dick can make a baby, but only a real man can raise his children." Brad is no longer a fool with a baby. In the two plus years since his son passed away, he's grown up from an immature twenty eight year old into a man, finally, at age thirty. That's why he treasures his wife so much, his angel sent from heaven to protect him and guide him into the right direction. His six children, all of which are still at a very young age, the oldest only being a little over three. Brad knows he must be a man now. He let down one kid before and broke his heart. He'll make damn sure he'll never do it again. ----- Two years ago on July 31st, as some of you may know, my son was struck by a drunk driver. Middle of the day and he was just blitzed out of his mind. Some seventeen year old kid, didn't know what he did wrong when he heard a thud. He simply passed it off as an animal. Never mind the supposed shrieks that my son let out. Never mind that this kid got off on the charges because there weren't any solid witnesses, only some old lady who's seen the truck before. My son's killer was never brought to the justice that he deserved. He had a huge smile the whole time during his court date because he knew there wasn't a single way he was going to jail for killing my son. I remember sitting there with Megan and Lilly, crying because this motherfucker never got what he had coming. Last I heard, he's starting his second year in college and is still getting blitzed whenever he can. There are nights where I just lay in bed and I wonder about what my son could have been. He could have been second generation of the Kane name in wrestling. He could've been a teacher like his eventual step mother. I'll lay there and I'll cry about it because my son meant the world to me. Just like my brother and his son. Just like any one in MWE who has a son. You see so much of yourself in him it's just scary. Chris loved wrestling. He loved watching me do all the crazy stuff I used to do, he loved watching some of the people I called a friend wrestle. He loved the industry, maybe more then I did at his age. *Brad's voice trails off for a moment before he clears his throat, continuing on.* I remember the night I won my first World Title. He was backstage that night and I remember the match was against a guy named Dark Prophet in a ladder match. I remember pulling that title down from the top of the ceiling and celebrating. I go backstage and the first person there was Chris, waiting to give me a huge hug because I finally did what so many wrestlers want to do. They want to be called the best wrestler in the world. I've called myself that moniker before. Then I a got a certain court order from another promotion saying I might get sued if I continue to call myself that. But my son loved watching wrestling. I had a match a couple of days after he died and it was the hardest match of my career. I can tell you the exact people who were in it and how I lost that match. I tagged with a great friend of mine to go against another great friend and his tag team partner that night. It was my first match in over two months. My first match since my son died and I gained a daughter. Everything was such a blur, I don't remember most of it. All I remember is getting a standing ovation for the simple strength I had to go out there and continue to do my job the only way I knew how. I did the things that made me the former Reckless Jack. Think of all the success I had with that name. People in MWE know about it, those who were unlucky enough to work with me before anyways. All the titles, the awards and the accolades that Reckless Jack brought me. They think of my stables, my turns and my matches that Reckless Jack brought me. Stepping back a few more weeks though, to when I nearly ended my career. That shooting star press, a move I haven't done in over a year to the outside like I did that night. Chris loved that move. He told me that when and if he became a wrestler, that'd be his finisher like so many others that use it. So the night I nearly broke my neck, he called me while I was in the hospital. He was crying over the phone saying "Dad, I hate that move. I never want to see it again!" *You can almost feel the tears coming down his face as he speaks into the microphone. So much emotion in his voice.* I said okay and we talked about life a little bit. I said that after I got out of the hospital, I was gonna drive to Iowa and take him fishing. I was gonna put him in a boat and explain some of the facts of life to him. I was going to teach him how to be a man, how to stand up for yourself and how to respect women. Instead, I was selfish and got drunk for the last time before I was put into rehab by my guardian angel. My last memory of my son was never fulfilling that last promise on taking him fishing. I'm almost certain he was heartbroken. I'm almost certain that when I was at his home, getting his little sister, the poster of me was torn down because I had lied to my son again. People always ask me why I dropped the "Reckless Jack" name. I never give a response to them. I try to switch the subject instead of explaining how Reckless Jack was part of my old life that I left behind two years ago. Reckless Jack was the thing I hated inside of me. He was a drunk, he beat on his son just like my foster father did to me. Everything evil in my life was because of Reckless Jack. So after I got out of rehab, I started to use my real name more and more because it was almost a re-birth. It's like Brad Kane had become a new person. But the memory of never fulfilling that last promise to my son haunts me to this day. The thought that I never got to take him fishing and explaining life continually burns me up inside. It's almost a year later now and I can't shake it from me. I probably never will either. Memories are impossible to escape. You can try to hide them, you can try to block them out but they always come back. Just like the memory of that kid who killed my son. I see his smile and I think about how much I hate the fuck for never taking responsibility for his actions. I'll never stop hating him either, not until I see him face to face and I'll just look him in the eye and say, "All I want to hear you say is I'm sorry." I don't dream of killing him because it wouldn't bring Chris back. All I want from this kid is to know what he did was wrong and that it burns him up inside like it does me. *Now Brad sighs, trying to shake his head clear of these thoughts that still plague his mind after two long years.* But that's just how life goes and I need to move forward now. I need to keep honoring Chris in the best way possible, wrestling. Sunday night, I made my debut for Mayhem Wrestling Entertainment in the greatest way. I was able to pick up a win in my first match in this new promotion. The feeling that I get after joining a place and I'm able to pull off getting a win just makes me happy. Here I am, an unknown to most of the roster here and I was able to beat two other men after I really got my rear handed to me quite a bit. Those who do know me must remember my ability to take a giant beating and come back from it. I know its a little cliché to say something like that but its the truth. That's just part of being a wrestler. So thanks to Blight and Sebastian St Claude. I think we gave everyone a good match. But I need to move forward and keep my head down. Down in a sense that I can't allow my head to swell due to the fact I was able to win a single match. A single match is nothing when you're trying to make an impact in an unknown place. In places past, a single win was all I needed to put a petition in for a title shot. Now I really don't care if I wrestle for a title, not like I used to. I used to think that the title a man holds makes him into the wrestler he is. Then I began to find out that holding a title only leads to more problems. I've seen people quit wrestling because they failed to win a title that they wanted. I've seen people throw a fit because they lost a title. Is that how I want my legacy defined? *You can almost hear him shake his head.* My legacy in this business I want to be defined by wrestling, not by holding every single title possible. If I never got a shot at a title here in Mayhem Wrestling Entertainment, that's fine. It won't bother me. This is the first time I really feel at peace with myself and my career. Maybe it was seeing someone else shut up Kevin Hardaway. Good job Zak, you wanted it more, just like I told you. But now I get the chance to work my way through a tournament here in MWE which is a perfect way to make myself into known player here in this promotion. A little ironic that my first opponent in this tournament is one of the men I faced in that three way at Crowning Glory. The good ole big monster Blight. You know Blight, I've seen many a men like you. Always claiming be what, a dark soul or a giant that feels nothing in the way of pain? Hasn't this be done a million times by now? You leave so little to my imagination that its a little scary to think that if you're who they're matching me up against, this should be pretty interesting. A lot of my career has been spent fighting men like you. The type that claim to be a "monster", a "sinister fiend" and what have you. People like you are just a dime a dozen in professional wrestling. So I have an idea for you Blight, stop listening to Cradle of Filth or whatever you listen to and go outside. Look at the sunlight and catch yourself a tan. Then do yourself a huge favor and get laid. I don't know many dark souls that get laid two or three times a week. Why do you think I appear cheery at times? I get laid twice a day. So you see Blight, this just isn't my Monday Night Mayhem debut, this is my chance to show the front office and the people that don't know me how I can wrestle in a one on one match. You got a small taste of Brad Kane at Crowning Glory and let me tell you Mister "Badass", you aren't anything but another monster for me to slay. That's all you are. You don't scare me, you don't frighten me. I think my three year old daughter would laugh at you and that's saying something. When our match is over and you'll find yourself flat on the mat, looking at my hand getting raised, you along with MWE realize why I'm hyped up from the Global Wrestling Coalition guys. You'll know why people call me one of the best wrestlers in the United States. Mayhem Wrestling Entertainment, this should be a fun ride.
