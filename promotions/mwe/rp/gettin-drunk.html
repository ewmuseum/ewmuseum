Gettin' Drunk; All night long..

Apr 5 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Imagine this room with a bunch of people in it) The scene zooms in to Mr Markowitz and Triple G. Markowitz is wearing a black and white button down shirt, and jeans. Triple G is wearing a pink collared shirt, and jeans. They walk into the room, and quickly go over to a group to a couple, standing together. They are in casual, party clothing. Markowitz: Yo guys! Girl: Hey boys.. Markowitz gives a wink to the girl and the girls winks back. The man to the left of the girl, gives Markowitz a strong and mean look. Man: Hey man! Thats my girl, back off bro. Markowitz laughs and looks at Triple G who is also laughing. Markowitz: Oh really? Markowitz puts his hand around her. He smiles and casually winks at the man. The man rears back and punches Markowitz weakly, in the nose. Markowitz: What the fuck? Markowitz stares at him and the man flinches. He waves at him and then BOOM! Punch to the face and the man falls down. Markowitz looks at him, on the ground. Markowitz: Nobody… Punches…. Me…. Girl: And by the way Chris, were threw. After this, Triple G walks over to Markowitz. He laughs and gives him a pat on the back. Triple G: Need some help? Markowitz: Nahh man. And whats there to help? I just beat the shit outa that guy. Triple G smiles at Markowitz but Markowitz is looking over him. Markowitz: Now where is that girl. Triple G: She is over there. Triple G stares at the girl and shakes his head. Markowitz quickly runs over to her. Triple G: Man, I wish Allison was here. Triple G smiles and takes out his phone. He dials a number and puts the phone to his ear. Phone Call: Allison Triple G: Hey Hunny. Allison: Hey baby. What is it? Triple G: You free tonight? Allison pauses. Allison: Yes, yes I’m free. Triple G: So, you wanna come to this party I'm at? Allison: Yes! I would love to. Triple G: See you there. Triple G is about to hang up the phone. Allison: Wait! Where is it? Triple G: Oh right. It’s at..... 42 MacWoldensen Road. Allison: Alright! I’ll be there. Cya Hunny! Triple G: Bye Allison hangs up. Markowitz and the girl are now engaging in a small conversation. Markowitz: So, how long have you known that guy? Girl: We WERE dating for about a month, he was a real jerk though. Markowitz gives her another wink, and then looks at Triple G who is waiting at the door. Markowitz: Cool. Anyway, what's your name? The girl blushes, and looks away for a few seconds. Girl: Samantha, but just call me Sammie. Markowitz: Okay, Sammie. Well my names, Sammie cuts Markowitz off, looking confused. Sammie: WAIT! I actually know your name. Took me a while to figure this out. Your a wrestler, I saw my brothers watching it last week, and I saw your match. Your friend over there is Triple G and your Mr Markowitz! Markowitz: Yea. But just call me Michael. Or Mike. Whatever you want. Sammie is staring at Markowitz. The two engage in a small kiss but then pull apart. Markowitz: Woah. Sammie: Yea. They both look at each other for a few seconds. Triple G is now shown, and still waiting at the front door. Suddenly the door flies open and Allison walks in. She is wearing, long dark pants, and a dark shirt, with a big black coat. She is holding an umbrella as it is raining outside. Triple G: You made it! Allison: Hey, It’s raining outside. Sorry for being a bit late, traffic. Triple G: It’s ok. All that matters is that you’re here. They both start to kiss, as we see Markowitz kissing Sammie. Triple G and Allison stop kissing. Triple G helps her in. He takes off her coat and puts it on the coat rack. There isn’t any other coats. They both walk over to a few people, and one of them hands Triple G a can of beer. A man comes over and looks at Triple G. Man: Let’s Go. Triple G pauses and looks at Allison who is looking away. Triple G: Your on! Whoever passes out first looses. Man: Deal. Winner gets 100. Lets both put in 50. After a few minutes of downing a bunch of shots. A few people come over. Crowd of People: DRINK! DRINK! DRINK! After about 20, Triple G pours some more. Allison starts to watch as it is starting to get interesting. Triple G: Come on old man! Can you do any better? Man: Ye- The man faints and falls to the ground. The crowd starts to cheer. Triple G then looks at Allison who is smiling. Triple G then drops his head. Allison runs over to him and drags him to the couch. She puts him down. Allison runs over to Markowitz and Sammie. Allison: Marko! Triple G… had a drink.. Well, he’s drunk. He’s on the couch. I need help. Markowitz: Ehh, he fine. Allison walks away and goes to Triple G who is sitting on the couch with his hand on his head. Allison sits next to Triple G. Triple G falls asleep on her shoulder. We see Markowitz and Sammie still talking. Sammie: Hey… Michael… What about your match this week? Markowitz: I'm against Nic Kraven, and this guy, I think its Peter Kaymakcian. I am also partnering with Gary..I mean Triple G. Sammie: Who? Markowitz quickly glances at Triple G who is holding his head and looking around and sort of talking to Allison. Markowitz: Well, I don't know these guys. There like new guys from ICW. But what do I care? We should have no problem beating these guys. Kraven? He talks to himself. He'll probably hit himself, in all of his confusion. But to be honest, he's good. And judging by his past 2 matches, he is a beast. But with Triple G on my side. We'll win. Sammie: Talks to himself? What a freak. And what about the other guy? Markowitz: Kaymakcian? Ehh, this guy is the ICW champion. I never faced him, but baby, when I do, expect me to slap him across the face. This guy is just annoying. He acts so tough but in the ring, he gets lucky. Markowitz and Sammie look at each other and nod there heads. We go to Triple G. Triple G: Man, my head hurts. Allison: I told you not to. You should listen to me more. I’m always right. Triple G: Yeah you are. Allison: Hey, to get you to stop thinking about that headache, how about we talk about MWE? Triple G: Good.. Idea…. Allison: Who are your opponents anyway? Triple G: Well…. My opponents are.. Triple G pauses as he is in obvious pain. Triple G: My opponents are, Nic Kraven, and Peter Kaymakcian. Allison: Interesting…. So what do you think about them? Triple G: Well, Nic Kraven… This guy talks to himself is all I know..And he looks tough, I mean he is undefeated in ICW But, with Marko on my side, I can do it. Shouldn’t be too hard anyway. Allison: What about that other guy? Peter, is it? Triple G: Yes… Peter. Triple G is clearly still in pain. Markowitz is talking with Sammie and kissing her. They seem to be talking about MWE, but aren’t heard clearly from where Triple G and Allison are sitting. Triple G: So yeah. Peter is the ICW Champion. I’m very impressed. I couldn’t get the championship. But then again, I never got a chance, never. I guess he is just luckier than me. Actually, he isn’t luckier than me. I’m luckier than him, because, I have you, Allison. Allison smiles and they both start kissing. After about 15 seconds, they stop. Allison: So is that all? Triple G: About my opponents? Yeah, I mean I was in ICW for a while and I never crosses paths with Kaymakcian, and went Kraven went to ICW, I was in MNM already. So being honest, I don't know much about them. But, what I do know, is that we will win. We have the experience factor on our side, and since we are an official tag team, and they aren't, me and Marko actually get along and know each other well. The scene cuts back to Markowitz and Sammie. Markowitz: So yeah, as I was saying, Kraven and Kaymakcian? Pshh... No chance. Markowitz then puts his arm around Sammie, and looks her straight in the eye. He shifts around a little as she looks at him and could tell somethings up. Markowitz: Sammie, just to make this thing final, do you want to go out with me? Markowitz laughs and looks at Sammie, she laughs to. Sammie: No. Markowitz: What? Sammie: JUST KIDDING! haha, yea, of course. Markowitz smiles, and rests his head on her shoulder. Markowitz: Maybe next week, me, you, Triple G, and Allison can see a movie or go to the diner or something. Triple G and Allison walk up to Markowitz and Sammie. Markowitz: Anyway, do you guys wanna go to the diner with us or something? Maybe next week? Allison: Yeah..I guess. Triple G and Allison both kiss each other. They are interrupted by Sammie and Markowitz. Markowitz: So, it’s a yes right? Triple G: Yep. Markowitz: Nice, see you guys there. Were leaving. Sammie: Cya guys. Triple G: Bye… Allison: See ya. Markowitz and Sammie, go to the coat room, pick up there coats and walk out the door. We see Markowitz lead Sammie into his car, and then himself into the car. We see red lights go on and the car drive off. Triple G: Uhh… Allison… Can we go? I don’t feel to w- Triple G falls to the ground. Allison quickly gets down and helps him up. She feels his head and it’s warm. He clearly has a fever or headache. Allison drags him to the car. Reluctantly, it stopped raining. Allison reaches the car. She puts her hand in her pocket, pulls out a key, and opens the door to her car. She puts Triple G inside and puts his seatbelt on. She puts the key in and turns it. The car starts and she puts her foot on reverse and she puts her hands on the wheel and starts to drive. Allison: Oh Gary. She drives backwards and then goes down the driveway and switches. She starts to drive forward and they are off. The scene fades away as we see Allison driving down the street with Triple G fast asleep. OOC: This RP is for both me and G
