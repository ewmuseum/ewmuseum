A Dream, A Nightmare, and a Promo

Jan 9 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The silence is deafening throughout the tightly enclosed area. A basement. The stone blocks that make up the walls are rotted, bound to break as the sands of time weigh heavier on their decrepit frames. The stench of mold is intense, burning the nostrils, almost to the point of making one want to vomit. A bit of light seeps in through a tiny window. Artificial light from a street lamp, as it’s clearly past midnight. It illuminates a bit of the center of the room, revealing a large object. And no, it’s not the main attraction. Instead it’s something completely random. A grand piano. While everything else looks like it’s about to fall apart, this is new. No dust. No marks. Perfect condition. The keys have a beautiful pearl color and the frame of the piano is sleek and black as night. The scene is unchanged for several seconds before a figure begins to walk out of a darkened corner. He moves around the piano so that he can sit on the bench in front of it. A deep sigh can be heard bellowing throughout the tiny space as he rolls up his sleeves and crosses his arms. He stares at the piano, hair covering his face, before extending his hands and placing them on the keys. The opening of “Moonlight Sonata” begins. For the first time something beautiful being played by someone who has made a living doing anything but beautiful things. And as he continues to play and the song gets darker, his intensity rises, his body heaving at every note. Feeling satisfied after several minutes, he slows down before coming to a complete stop. The beauty is gone. Now it’s time for the beast. “This music is suppose to be soothing me, yet, I still can’t help but feel agitated. Here I am, playing one of the greatest classical music pieces ever assembled, yet all I hear, drowning it out, is the same song that every single victim has sang before. And I have to be honest, it’s getting sickening and monotonous. What makes you so different Sonny, from all the others that I’ve faced before? I still have yet to see it. And while you’ve done your research on me, it’s clearly evident that you’ve made a few crucial mistakes for which I’d like to correct. And not just correct because you’re wrong, but to make sure I break apart whatever false sense of confidence you have when you step inside that ring with me. Now, don’t get me wrong, I know Paige will be in the ring with us too, but that isn’t what this is about. Not yet.” He steps away from the piano, sighing contently. It is obvious that this is a different man than the one that stood before the camera last week. “For one, the past is wonderful. My accomplishments are ever long, as are yours. You have held a bunch of titles, as have I. We are both former World champions, and that is something we should be both proud of. But while it’s great to reminisce, I find more comfort in the present and future. And my future is just beginning. At the age of twenty-five, I’m just about to start hitting my prime…where as you, Mr Williams, are nothing more than another reject from the street who is doomed to repeat the mistakes of his past. But you do have one thing right. My past shouldn’t frighten you. It’s what I’m going to be doing in the present and future that really should strike fear in anybody crossing my path. Especially you, because it’s going to get a lot bloodier from here on out. And I’d just like to mention you’re little shot at my match last week and how I “Barely scraped past Billy Joe Barker last week.” You're mistaken. There was never any doubt that I would walk out victorious, even with the odds against me. I was a newcomer to this place, where as he had the comfort of knowing there were people in the back that looked out for him. I entered his world. So not only did I take him apart, I beat him in his own, true element.” Enough defense, time to go on offense. “But while you have been watching me for years, I’ve had my eye on you for a bit, ever since that fateful match back in EW. I was wondering, dare I say HOPING that you would bring that match up. You see, I had already told them that it was going to be my last match, and they knew that there was no way I would go out and actually try. So you can sit there and brag about how you beat me, but it was a hollow victory. Have you ever faced me in a place that actually mattered? No. Have you ever gotten a victory over me when I actually cared enough to try? No. So I wouldn’t pop off at the mouth about how my victories are tainted, when your record is less than commendable. I will give you credit where it is due though, you have improved greatly since the last time we met.” Warner cracks a smile as he begins to pace around the room, taking a cigar out of his pocket. The flicker of a lighter illuminated his face as the streetlight flickered momentarily basking the room in utter darkness. The flame flickers once more, lighting a candle that was on top of the piano. “I commend you on your research, but what you’ve learned, you’ve ignored. Enigmar Wrestling. Billy Joe Fuckstick. Yourself. All stepping stones to something much greater. My first tour in this place is going to be a lot more destructive than anything you could have ever imagined. Look at GWC. It collapsed because I held the championship, but not because I couldn’t draw a crowd…it’s because there wasn’t anyone left willing to gaze into the abyss and partake in their own demise. I did every single thing that there was to do in that place, and when I finally walked out it crumbled in my wake. Granted, they tried to hold their own, but what is there to do when the best damn thing to happen to your company up and leaves. You should have taken their approach. Stayed in the shadows. My shadow. Cowered in fear. But you chose not to and you’re courage is what'll get you killed. So keep chopping wood...” The fury returns. “I'll need it to go with the nails for your coffin.” Warner begins to play the piano again, his devilish laugh injected into the melody as the scene fades, but only momentarily as we snap back to reality. It seems that everything we just saw was only a dream, yet it seemed so realistic. Sweat, cold and beaded, raced across Zak's quivering flesh and dripped to the dirt below as realities danced intertwined in his mind. Pictures of the past flashed in his mind and fought with the present as he struggled to regain his grip on his mind. The venomous burn of his previous failings returned to rip at the skin and muscle of his consciousness and attempted to poison any trace of self-worth. “You're useless... A hack! You. Have. Nothing.” Twisting his neck and clicking his spine, Warner pushing the thoughts from his head as he willed himself to his knees. He slicked his hair back to push some of the mud like sweat from his scalp as he gave his surroundings a hurried glance over. Isolation. He kept waiting for a shadow to dart from tree to tree or for the dry leaves on the ground to stir, but no nature was disturbed. No shattering twigs, no trickling stones. Just silence. Nerve destroying silence. The perfect scene for the voice in his mind to truly torment. “Scared huh, Zak? Don't blame ya, I would be too if I were you. You can't handle something like this, this aint your scene. You haven't got the guts, you haven't got the skill. You're dead and you don't even know it yet.” Warner shook his head as he finally rose to his feet, his right side almost giving out as he did. He rubbed his thigh and felt a rising welt beneath his grime covered pants. Forcing the pain deeper inside him to a place he can properly ignore it and lifting his gun to his chest, Zak ran deeper in the forest, picking up his senses and heightening them above his thoughts. A chilling wind cascaded past him, causing the hairs on the back of his neck to stand higher then they could when backed by paranoia only. The sudden sensation startled Warner, making him quickly spin as he ran, only to see nothing but the world behind him. Turning back, he shook his head again, disgusted at himself for letting being shocked so easily. “See, Zak? You just haven't got what it takes to be out here. This is man's country, the place of the fighter. The land of a warrior. You, despite what you may think about yourself, aren't any warrior.” Warner laughed to himself as he pressed his back up against a nearby tree, some of the bark flaking and falling under the pressure from his spine. He peaked his head around it carefully, reading his weapon as he did so. He absorbed every detail of what laid before him, every piece of rustling flora and even the smallest example fauna was acknowledged. Assured the land was clear, he returned to running, nature crackling beneath his boots. “Bullshit... I'm a fighter. More fighter then most. Just look at my job.” “Hahahaha! Zak you fucked up little fool. You're not the wrestler here. I am.” Zak stopped running, stunned. His heart did the same, his blood freezing as his body tried to make sense of what he had just heard his mind say. “What?” “Hahaha, shocked Zak? Did you think that Ivory was just a gimmick you could play now and then to feel like a big man? Please. There's more to it then that. Can't control me forever!” “Bullshit...” He took off running again, this time less purpose and conviction. He wasn't sure what he was running to or from this time, the battle he was engaged in had suddenly become less important. “... I'm just losing it, this can't be real. I'm just exhausted or something. Just a little shook up.” “Heh, believe that all you want Zak. You're basically just admitting what I've been saying. You can't handle being out here. You don't deserve to be if one little fall or one little injury makes you loose your mind. 'Oh no, I've been out here on a battle found for an hour or so, it's making me go insane! Boo-who.' Can't handle it? Leaving fighting a war to the people who can. People like me.” “People like you? You are me! I made you! You're just a fucking part of me!” “That's right Zak, you made me. You thought this all up. Congratulations, you can state your own actions. But lets just think about this, why did you make me?” “I needed a gimmick for wrestling, obviously.” “That's right, but it's nowhere near that simple. You couldn't be just plain Zak Warner in the ring, you needed to be someone else. You needed to be me. Why? Because you were afraid. Zak Warner just can't cut it in a fight. No one will ever care about Zak Warner. Zak Warner isn't tough enough. Zak Warner, he's pathetic. Ivory, on the other hand, Ivory's strong. Ivory's fast. Ivory, Ivory's just plain better. Ivory's a winner. I'm everything you're not Zak. I'm everything you ever wanted to be.” “You are me! I am you!” “Oh wake the fuck up Zak! Just open your fucking eyes! Ok, maybe you and I were one in the same at one stage, but please, you can't for one second tell me that we've grown apart. Every time you took to that stage and hit that ring, I became a little more real. Every time you said the name Ivory, I became a little more different. Eventually, you went to another place and I took over. You felt yourself loose control for a little while. You felt yourself become a winner. Something better then everyone else in the world. You know it Zak. I'm more then just a character now. I'm just as real as you. You need me to be. You can't survive with me. I can handle the world and the people in it easier. I'm the hunter you need to be to live.” A figure ran to the cover of a tree, just catching the corner of Zak's eye. He maneuvered to the nearest piece of landmass he could and hid himself from any possible view. He showed just enough of his face to be able to survey the land, trying to catch a glimpse of the running shape as he softly pressed his finger against his trigger. “You want to prove to me you're a fighter Zak? Gotta prove you're a savage animal able to take down another human being like I am? All you gotta go is shoot that person. Beat them. Accentuate your might and burn your rule into their forehead.” Warner clenched his teeth together as he crouched down and sprinted as silently he could to a close tree, a shot firing towards him as he did. He dived, barely covering the distance, swearing at himself as he rolled to safety. “Already lost the element of surprise, aren't you a little bitch.” “Shut the fuck up...” “Oh, sorry. I'll be quiet if you think it'll help stop being a little bitch.” Zak rolled his eyes as he peered around the pillar of bark and foliage that stood between him and his would-be victim. He pointed the barrel into the open and fired several rounds, hoping to send his opponent into hiding, before darting out and around, dodging from tree to tree, shooting every fee moments to ensure his secrecy. “Not bad Zak, but you still gotta make the kill. You're screwed.” He shook his head again, taking his eyes from the path in front of him for the briefest of moments, the distraction almost sending him to the dirt below. “What the hell you doing boy?! You want to fail? You're pathetic, let me take over for fuck's sake.” Dragging his balance back to the forefront, Zak regained his composure, trying almost in vain to ignore the anger rising in the well of his emotions. He cast his eyes back towards where he believed his target to be. There was nothing but the breeze in motion, but he waited with intent. Soon, he saw a trace of black in the shape of an elbow, and then a head, slowly lean out from behind a tall tree. He jerked his eyes back to safety and slid to the roots that had dug themselves to the surface of Earth. With focused precision, Zak crept out from his hiding place, his irises locking onto his victim. Avoiding their line of sight, he managed to get a few feet closer, sliding behind cover before risking himself more. Pausing for a breath, he felt his heart assaulting the inside of his chest. Carefully looking back, he saw his opposing chess player wander out into the clear, believing all to be safe. “Hah! Idiot! She thinks you're gone! Take her out Zak!” Confident, Warner twisted away from his guarding organism and away from the woman. Then, dashing towards her, he fired a shot, barely scraping her shoulder. “Fuckwit! Don't bloody miss!” He pressed the trigger again, this attempt blazing past her turning head. She retaliated and let loose her attack, but Zak dived, sending another blast towards her as he did. He saw her hide herself as he head towards a landing, one that resulted in him crashing into a thick growth of wood, bark splintering and soaring on impact. He closed his eyes and winced in pain, his teeth felt like they were going to shatter each other, such was the force he brought them down. When he opened his eyelids again, he felt a different spark behind them. A smirk spread across his face. He stood, confidence fueling him. Moving with a defined grace, he ran towards is goal whilst managing to avoid sight. Staying low, he soon found himself able to see the woman he was aiming to take down. She was beautiful, but he refused to let that distract him. He made a final dash and she was finally aware of his presence. She fired, but he weaved, the bullet just missing but leaving its mark on his clothes. She tried to shoot again, but he was close enough to send the barrel skyward. In one swift motion, he was behind her and had pressed her chest up to the tree she had hidden herself behind, he weapon pressed to her stomach. He laughed softly as he raised his lips to her ear and pressed his gun into the middle of her back. “When you get to hell, little lady... Tell 'em Ivory sent ya.” She squeezed her eyes shut as tightly as she could as she felt three shots dig into her back, bullet after bullet after slow, drawn out bullet. When he was done, he turned and tossed her to the ground below. She returned her sight to the world and watched him rub his eyes, a look of surprise and anger on her face. “Zak, what the hell! Someone takes paint ball way to seriously!” He looked at her, confusing racing in his mind. “Hayley? I'm... sorry? Did I hurt you?” “Damn right you did, think I landed on a jagged stick...” She stood and tried to get a view of her lower back before turning away from Zak. “... Am I bleeding or anything?” He barely looked at where she was gesturing to, his concentration was purely introspective. “Nah, you're fine.” She looked back to him and shrugged, a trace of irritation still lurking in her expression. “What happened to you anyways? It was like you were someone else.” He looked at her, although he was in his own world. “Guess I was just lost in the moment...” “Yeah, guess so, wouldn't be the first time. But whatever, lets just head back. Screw our teams, I'm bored with the whole nature thing. Don’t you need to cut a promo on that lesbian bitch anyways?” “Yeah, sounds like a plan...” The two began to walk back to the base they had started at earlier in the day, Hayley hummed a tune absentmindedly, and Zak returned to his mind, waiting for some kind of explanation or bout of bragging. He didn't have to mentally idle long. “Like I said Zak, you're just a steak in a world of hungry strays. Me, I'm the alpha-fucking-male. The king of the pack. And no one, not you or any other person that wants to try and claim my superiority. I'm everything you're not Zak, and everything you, and everyone else, want to be. You're the sheep, they're the dogs. You're all deluded. I'm in control. I'm the only motherfucking king wolf. Don't forget it.” A slight twitch can be seen in his eyes, as he waited for Hayley to set up the camera. When she was done, he looked square into the lens, a lopsided grin on his face. “You know, people always doubt me. Doubt my intentions. Doubt my reasons. 'Oh, he's just here to show off. Look at him, he just wants attention.' Why? Is it because... They think their causes are more noble? Or is it because they can't believe I doubt myself? Yeah. That's gotta be it. They... the people, the fans, they've lost their sense of reality. They think I'm the unstoppable juggernaut I paint myself up to be. Newsflash ladies and gentlemen: The brush is a liar. No matter what I frame myself with, I'm still human. I can still break down. I can still lose hope and sight of the past. I can still wonder about the present. I can still fear the future. And that's what this is about. I haven't slipped yet, but I can feel it coming... Like the wrath of a God it's coming. But I won’t let it happen. You see, I've had it quiet for a while now. My time has been spent in a gym, not in front of the swarming masses. I haven't been where I belong. The life is cracking. In a gym... at home... on a couch... in a park... on a train... watching TV... Feeling each and every second... tick past... tock past... my foot in time with every one... It's like a whisper in your ear twenty-four/seven. Just waiting for you to scream so it can push it's face against your mouth to relish the agony. But no, no no, I won’t let myself fall by the wayside. I sat on the sidelines for months last time, but then I had a reason. My health required it. What's the reason now? Simple. There isn't one. I won’t play to house arrest, I wont hang around and give myself a higher chance to slip and fall. I won’t let my mind be racked with self-doubt. So, ladies and gentlemen, still think this is just a ploy for attention? Realize this: You don't know anything. But I will give you something to consider... My name is Ivory. And I promise that not a single thing has changed.” He leans up against the building behind him, his hands in his pocket. His eyes wander around almost absentmindedly, a loud sigh emitting from his closed lips. “There are a few things in this industry that, in all honesty, I hate. The catering is always bad, the people are arrogant fuckwits and the pot is always calling the kettle black. Now, I'm not usually a hateful guy, but these things drive me to the crumbling edge of insanity. But worst still, and perhaps worst of all, is the simple fact that nothing ever changes. John Cena still gets pushed to the moon. Randy Orton still has no personality. Paige is still trying to sleep her way to the top. More importantly, I'm still being overlooked. In an industry where I'm considered a god at what I do by the masses, I'm still overlooked. Is it something I said? Did I do something wrong? Is it because I wasn't I employed when MWE was just making a name for itself, so I couldn't earn a top spot before the booking team became a creature of habit and started recycling the same collection of names through its main event? Was I to good to just use as filler for a spot to avoid seeming overly repetitive? Who knows really? We're all just victims of circumstance, aren't we? Suppose the only thing to do is make the best of what the ignorant hand you out, even if the ice cream isn't your flavor. Take me for example. Even though I'm stuck in an admittedly pathetic midcard, facing off against people labeled as 'the future of the company', and have had that taped onto their lapels for the better part of a year, I'm still delivering five star matches and I'm still going into a match to get a shot at a title that's considered to be one step below the World Championship, yet nobody has held the title long enough to make it worth anything. Gotta save it, you know. Can't give away platinum ratings like that on free TV! Go me, right?” He reaches into his pocket, pulling out a stainless steel zippo. The top is flicked back and forth, the flame being lit and extinguished for no apparent reason other than self-amusement. He continues to do this for a moment, before speaking once more. “So instead of having my name in lights in the main event like I deserve, I'm facing who? Your stereotypical ‘Gangsta from the hood’, and some carpet munching, Cannon swallowing jizz trough. Watch the world watch in quiet awe, the ground is soon to quake. ‘Ivory’s up against him? Why in god's name?’ ‘I know, it makes no sense whatsoever? Just bad booking. Why make a title look bad by having the champ job? Especially to someone who doesn't even need the credibility.’ ‘Ivory is better then this, he really is.’ Sonny, you're a victim of a machine here. Clutch your Intercontinental title close to your chest and say it isn't so over and over. Close your eyes and blind yourself to the hideous reality staring you in the tearing face. You are worthless. Just another body for the brass to swing around, just another number for the coroner to count. The management thinks I need to be built up before I walk into the back and take the World Title, so they feed me a secondary champion. Paige, sadly for you, even after the supposedly improving Billy Joe Barker, and even knowing that I will beat Sonny quite easily, I haven't had my fill. Notice that in only my second match here, I am already facing two of the so called ‘top MWE stars’? Management decided that people should see that you people can live up to my standard. Who will get you next? A returning Kanto who needed to remind people that he can still go? Or will it be some other unheralded star, needing a footstep to the top?” A cigarette appears in between his lips, and the tip of the flame ignites the paper. Ivory takes a moment to let the sweet smoke fill his lungs, before exhaling softly. “You know Paige, you were right when you sat there and spoke about how I let my demons take over, and how I have a tortured soul. Yet you think that those…’issues’ somehow make me weak? You think that I will just curl up in a ball and take a beating? I can see why you had to see a shrink. Yes, I have made a few stops in my life, trying to find a way to distract myself from the emotions I hold inside. What you fail to comprehend is that I have already lived in hell, even if you find that hard to believe. What you need to realize is that we all have our own personal hell. Yours would be being locked in a room full of women, with no tongue of your own to enjoy yourself. Sonny’s hell would be a world without KFC. My own hell though, was wrestling for a company that let my best friend die. For you to compare my demons, with the bullshit that Virus tries to pull is nothing more than a slap in the face to the thousands of people in the world that deal with the same sort of shit that I do. You can’t honestly look at me and tell me that you have never heard a voice in the back of your head telling you what to do. We all go through it Paige, just some of us more likely than others. The tobacco has all but burnt up, yet the filter still hangs between his lips. He finally flicks it away, slumping down to the ground. He brushes the hair out of his face, the grin barely noticeable on his now stoic face. Even after culling the stalled future in Kevin Hardaway back in GWC, I've still got room in my consciousness for another ego shot. People have been ranting for well over a year now that Sonny is the wrestler of tomorrow, the headline in next month's sport section. Who better then MWE's consistent next to boost the wrestling profile of a man who needs no real introduction? OK Sonny, time to take the title from your chest and to wake up. You're nothing more than another rung in my ladder. Sucks, doesn't it? The man who talks about how much better he's getting. The man who's supposed to lead MWE into tomorrows sunrise. The man who people think is a champion. All beaten by Ivory. The dumb will finally think I'm great. Don't think I asked for this. I'm tired of playing games, the routine has worn very thin. I'm sick of being forced to prove what's been obvious to every-fucking-one. You think this is your big chance to prove your greatness isn’t just masked mediocrity? Well guess what, it isn't. Nobody gives a shit about you. No one cares where you came from, or what gangs you were in, no one thinks you're dangerous because of it. You. Are. Fodder. You're something to get thrown in my way to realization, nothing more then a tool to piss me off. And oh how you've fulfilled your lowly potential. But don't think I'll be taking this lightly despite seeing through it all, no no. It's time to make the people booking this shit to see that I'm beyond their image of me. It's the hour of waking up, even if I have to drive your skull through a clock face and move the hands forward myself. The future may not come for anyone in this place, but I'm not just anyone. I'll be taking my career by its throat and dragging it where I want. You, you aren't in my grand plan, and for good reason, you don't exactly deserve to be. That doesn't mean I can't take a break for a nap and to shatter some dreams. Again. Sonny, Paige, this isn't your moving up in the world. This isn't your chance to shine through the mud you've covered yourself in. This is you living up to your potential. You're bath will never come. After all, nothing ever changes here. Luckily for me, I've always defined my own world. How about you? Do you have the courage to stand up and try and be counted, only to realize the man with the abacus isn't looking? No one is. It's time for eyes to be on me.” He stands back up, walking slowly and methodically towards the camera. "The evolution is coming, a revolution has begun. So shoot up my Addicts, the flag of Zero will soon be flying high."
