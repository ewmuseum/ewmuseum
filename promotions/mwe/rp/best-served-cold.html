Suddenly a blatantly fake look of surprise crosses her face. Allison Durden: "Maybe it's because James Preston isn't perfect?" A playful tone quickly changes to one all too serious for a girl who was just minutes ago pretending an empty arena was packed to the brim. Her eyes and face quickly follow suit as she turns back to Alexis. Allison Durden: "Maybe the self-proclaimed Image of Perfection finally took a long hard look at himself in the mirror, and admitted that even the son that God left behind got under his skin? Just maybe ..." Durden looks over her shoulder as she takes her spot next to Alexis, that coy smirk of hers coming back into play. Allison Durden: "But I doubt it ... He's still convinced that the world revolves around him, but I have a feeling that his date with destiny Sunday night will finally pull that self-pretentious stick out of his ass." She lets out a little laugh to herself at that last line, feeling full well that she had torn a limb off of Preston with her verbal onslaught. Her confidence is obvious as she stands tall next to Black. Who's expression tells an entirely different story. He's back to that serious glare at the camera as it brings our focus back to him. Alexis Black: "I don't understand how you did it James ... Week after week you went out to the ring keeping the same mindset you had a year ago, and week after week it's was same result. Even after you lost the belt you created you came out claiming the same hypocritical bullshit about your perfect life, But when I had Chris England ..." Alexis raises the arm farthest away from Allison and locks his eyes on his hand as he brings it up above his head. A sinister look coming over this goth wanna-be's face as he brings his eyes back down, continuing to talk to Preston. Alexis Black: "Strung up like a butchered piece of meat ... I saw something inside click. You finally realized that you are far from perfect and that's what I needed to see Preston." Alexis slowly lowers his hand and brings his index finger to his temple, that same eerie look of joy in his eyes. He taps his fingertip slowly, using the time old symbol for 'Got you.' Alexis Black: "Because now I know that I'm so far into your head that I've already beaten you. I saw it in your eyes last Monday, the way you're already questioning whether beating me is possible and it's not ... Because you know that come Crowning Glory I'm out for blood, and I will not rest until I'm satisfied! The cost of war is a hefty one Preston, and this Sunday the price will be paid." Allison Durden: "See you Sunday, James" Almost a play off of the old Mafia tradition of "The Kiss of Death" Alexis blows an eerie kiss towards the camera as Allison runs her hand down his back with the same coy grin that's been on her face this entire time. Leaving the 'picture perfect' couple standing tall in the middle of the ring as we fade to black.
