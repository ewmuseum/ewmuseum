Silence will always be golden...; ...But chaos will ALWAYS ensue

Aug 15 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The scene opens up to the ring where this years Crowning Glory was held. Aeron is sitting in the middle of the ring on a stool hold a mic with the tip of it on the ground. The arena was empty, it was silent, so silent you could hear the pop cans rattling down the aisle or the left over signs that fans had brought and decided to leave behind for the janitor who never did show up. The only sources of light were the door that was propped open for ventilation and a single dull spotlight that shone on Aeron. He looked up at the cameraman and held the mic up to his mouth. Aeron: Silence is golden. Silent like this arena, like the ring, like my thoughts. When everything is silent, everything is calm, everything is fine. Silence will always be golden. Aeron tosses the stool out of the ring as the doors suddenly shut and the spotlight flickers like a broken street light before it fades into darkness. The cameraman is breathing heavily as footsteps can be heard. He feels his way off the ring and to the announcers table which he hides under. Five spotlights shine brightly as the cameraman sits on the announcers chair still shaken like a martini, not stirred. It is now seen that Aeron in the middle of the ring with one of the spotlights on him. Aeron: But when the silence is broken, chaos erupts. Hearts pound, foreheads sweat and your lips tremble in fear. Madness is sure to ensue but what you dont know is when it will end. When the silence is broken and when the chaos begins. I stood here in this very ring after beating J.K. Valentino to grab hold of this title. Aeron holds up the X-TV Title. The spotlight shines off the title, causing it to light the arena in which a way it makes it look like its full. The shadows of the row of seats casting on the ones behind it. Like a tree casting a shadow over a section of a playground on a hot summers day. Aeron: Before the match, my mind was stirred, I couldnt tell up from right and down from left. Aeron coughs. Aeron: Err, Up from down and left from right. But after I held that title high, covered in sweat everything started to clear up. I could see straight and everything was silent, or so I thought. Silence IS golden but only if it lasts. Now silence is just like alchohol it only numbs the pain for a small time. And now once again chaos has erupted, its going to be me verse four other competetors. Aeron wipes sweat off his eyebrow. The camera pans out to reveal he is surrounded by four other men in a circle. Aeron in the middle. Aeron: The effects of silence seem fine as if nothing bad is to happen. Four spotlights shut off, Aeron only being seen as he holds his arms out the camera rotates around him as if he was trying to show them something. Aeron: But when that chaos erupts, your knees feel weak, you cant walk, your stomach churns. Fear takes over your mind. It starts playing tricks on you, and the next thing you know...your detained in an insane asylum The spotlights shine over the four men that were hidden in darkness moments before, each cracking there knuckles. Aeron: This is what goes on in MY mind, see what I see, feel... what I... feel. The four men look at eachother as they nod. One of the men step forward closer to Aeron. The man has long black hair and a black beard, dressed in camo pants and a black wifebeater. Aeron: Gus, the greatest highflyer in pro wrestling history. Is this because you've been tossed out of the ring so many times? Or is it just cause your light on your feet. The man that had stepped forward looked angry as if he took what Aeron just said to heart. He rushes at him carelessly. Aeron takes advantage of this and lands a solid clothesline. He picks him upinto a firemans carry as he walks over to the ropes he tosses him over the rope. The man crashes to the solid floor below head first, he remains motionless. The other three look at eachother before glancing back at Aeron. The three mens knees start locking up and sweating. Another man steps foreward slightly as his kness start wobbling. This man is larger in muscle terms than the other men, he has longer dirty blond hair, his eyes burn like the sun. Aeron: Jason Fear, you think your all big and tough? You think with your muscles instead of your head.Your like the monster truck that runs over those cars that are all just waiting for you. Or the bowling ball that gets pushed around by some old guy that gets his thrill at blueberry hill. Things get just lined up for you until something unexpected happens. Like one of the cars blows up in your face or one of those pins are actually metal, painted and melded to the floor. I am that pin, I am that explosion. The man cracks his neck and knuckles as he walks up to Aeron and attempts to punch him. Aeron grabs his fist and starts to engage in mercy with him. Aeron headbutts him as the man drops his grip, Aeron applies more pressure as the man starts to clench his teeth in pain. Aeron puts a twisted smirk on his face as the man drops to his knees. Aeron lets go and turns around and walks a few steps. The man is rubbing his hands as Aeron turns around again he walks up to him he knees him in the face. The man starts to bleed from his nose and mouth. The last two guys are leaned against the turnbuckles, sweating in fear. Aeron starts to look at the two as he laughs. Aeron: Who would I rather talk about next? Jeff Austin or J.K... Tough choice. Aeron holds his chin as he furls his eyebrows in though. The two look at eachother still wondering if this was a good idea. Aeron turns around still in thought as he smirks and turns around and points at the man in the bottom left turnbuckle. Aeron: You, step forward. The man gulps as he points to himself. ???: M-Me-e? Aeron nods as the man looks at the other and steps forward. This man, bald unlike the others with a goatee. He wipes the sweat off his forehead. Hes dressed in shorts and a black t-shirt. Aeron: Jeff Austin, Mr. Unpredictable. You think this but you see when I get in your head, you have no where to run, I know everything your going to do. I'll get in your head, like a scary campfire story seeping into a young childs brain. Theres' nothing you can say, do, or think without me knowing it. You see, I am everywhere, but nowhere. I hear and see everything without being there. My only downfall, is I dont know when to stop. I have no off button, no power cord. The only thing that fuels me is rage. Everyday I get fueled more and more. He walks over to the man who starts shaking, his knees wobbling. He starts backing up. Aeron looks him right in the eye. Aeron: ......Boo. The man slips under the bottom rope running to the door. It takes him a few seconds of him attempting to open the door, the rattling of the door, the sound of the metal hitting the metal. Aeron starts laughing as he looks at the last man sitting on the turnbuckle. Aeron: Whos last, ah thats right. J.K. Valentino. The walking stereo. You see, I dont even know what to say that someone hasnt already. Theres a list that goes on, and on, and on, and on. Hmm, first and foremost, no one likes you, no one ever will, so go back to Italy and cook some pizza for me why dontcha? Beating you makes me quite hungry. Then theres the shut the fuck up no one wants to hear it, or the, go read a book or the best yet, go cry emo kid. But enough of my ramblings on lets get this done and over with, I've got places to be and people to see. And no, you aint one of them. He walks towards the man who is now trembling in his boots. Hes drenched in sweat and you can see the noticeable lump in his throat as he attempts to swallow. Aeron looks at him and gives him a quick punch to the stomach. The man grabs it in pain. He starts to weep softly. Aeron gives him a quick elbow to the cheek. The man falls through the middle ropes, his head hits the steel steps. Aeron chuckles as all of a sudden theres a loud noise. Its the door opening as Aeron turns and looks to see the man that had run away and about 15 other people. ???: GET HIM BOYS! Aeron smiles as everybody bumrushes the arena. Footsteps echo through the arena. The cameraman hides under the table. Nothing can be seen but many things can be heard. Chaos. All that can be heard are various grunts, and skin in skin contact as punches land, people yell but suddenly everything is quiet. Nothing is heard, nothing at all. Then laughter can be heard. A roaring, maniacal laughter. The cameraman stands up and all the unconcious bodies are piled up in the middle of the ring. Aeron is seen with a leg on one of the bodies. Aeron: I am the reason children check their closets and under their beds. I am the reason kids need nightlights, I...AM...BEOWULF. The scene fades with Aeron still triumphent with the leg raised and placed on the body. Holding the X-TV Title high in the air. The sillhouttes of the crowd made from the chairs have faded. The light flickers softly and Aerons panting is all thats heard. (( Does this sell the match enough? Had to sacrifice description to do this one. Oh wellz ))
