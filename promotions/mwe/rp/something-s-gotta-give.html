Something's Gotta Give; Cruz RP for MITB

Aug 19 2009

Jesus Cruz

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Up. Then down. Then up once more. Odds say, back down again. What am I rambling on about? It's Cruz's MWE success. Is this what Jesús is doomed for? Riding on this inconsistent line graph of a roller coaster he calls a career? Even I, his narrator, realize that the way things are going, "El Peligro" will be nothing more than a win-some lose-some mid-carder. But that's not going to happen. Because after Crowning Glory, things for Jesús Cruz will never be the same again. "If you're headed for a cliff, you have to change direction." - Barack Obama Fade in. The United Center in Chicago Illinois. The place where MWE's two year anniversary spectacular will take place. The place where Sharpmen and D.C. will be locked inside steel, until one man is can continue no longer. The place where Rodriguez and Whitman will have their final match, as 22,000 strong root them on. The place where Grand Mystique and Sinc Mercier finally lock horns for the top prize in the land. Augest 30th, sold out, fans will witness history. And to start this huge show, it will be eight men in biggest match of their careers. Money in the bank. Seven of those men are eager to step foot into this arena. But one is already there. Cut to camera dos. Inside, it is dim but still visible. As the shot scans around, we see hundreds of seats. All of them, empty. And then, we cut to the ring. There he is. Standing strong. Jesús Cruz. And then, he points up. The briefcase dangles above. That... That's going to change my life. All those hours of training will have finally paid off. I'll have my opportunity at the world title. I can see it now. I'll climb that ladder with a smile on my face, looking down at all of the fallen opponents as I scale each step. I'll reach the top, and pull down the briefcase with ease! And then... *LET'S DO THIS!* A strobe light flashes a hard red, lighting up the arena with a crimson-glow, as "War" by Sick Puppies hits over the PA system. Jesús cups his hands around his mouth, as he raises his voice to speak over the blaring music. My song will blare! And wether you people like it or not, everyone will take notice! *Faking falls Stopping tall Take it all Fact is that I'm taking mine I'm taking mine Time is up Out of luck Should have stood up But you're out of time But you're out of time LETS DO THIS!* Monkeys in the back, cut it! The song ends, and the red lights stop flashing. Back to dimness we go. But one thing shines bright. Cruz's smile. You ask yourself... how can I be so sure? Well, allow me to break it down for ya. And with that, a new song hits. Not nearly as loud as his entrance before, but enough to hear the lyrics clearly. *Oh yeah, Oh yeah Open wide and they'll shove in Their meaning of life Oh yeah, Oh yeah But not for me I'll do it on my own Oh yeah, Oh yeah Open wide and swallow their meaning of life I can't make it work your way Thanks but no thanks* Jesús holds his hand into the air. That's enough. Cut that shit off. The music stops, just like that. The tune is "Meaning of Life" by The Offspring. It belongs to the joke of the company. The man who parades around, talking a big game, with a skateboard in one hand and his dick in another. The man I made tap out last week. Yeah, you guessed it. David Renshaw. I honestly don't need to explain why he stands no chance. You know it, and I know it. He's in this match for one reason and one reason only. To be a target for big bumps. Nobody, including the retarded and the insane, would put money down for Renshaw in this match. Cruz points towards the rampway. NEXT! *Did you realize that you were a champion? Yes I did So I packed it up and brought it back to the crib Just a lil somethin', show you how we live Everybody want it but it aint that serious Mhmmm thats that shit So if you gon do it, do it just like this* Cruz grimaces as he listens along. But enough is enough. Aw, rap music... simply terrible... Cut it! The music ceases. "Champion" by Kanye West. The song to none other then Peter "the only exercise move I know is a crunch" Kaymakcian. Now here's a kid who has his contract for his looks. He's eye candy for the gals, and toss in a bit of athleticism, and you've got Peter in a nut-shell. But he is a good fit in this type of match. He takes big risks, and does some sick shit. But when it comes to closing out a match, and actually leaving with the W? He can't get it done. He lives for the rush, and seems to care-less about the actual victory. But hell, win or lose, he still gets his check. So why should he care right? I truly believe money is what feeds that kid's ego. And with that type of attitude and in-ring style, you'll never become cream of the crop. Lets move on. *We are young We are far Born in this world as it all falls apart We are strong We don't belong Born in this world as it all falls apart* Alright, cut. Cruz takes a moment to chuckle under his breath. The shear thought of this guy brings a smile to the fighter's face. Folks, that's "Young" by Hollywood Undead. Cue the arrogant and cocky L.A. kid, "The 21st Century Playboy" Anthony Clark. I've never seen anyone in my entire life who is more full of himself. If Clack didn't have ribs, he'd suck his own shaft. Competition-wise, Anthony is and always will be a bench warmer in this organization. Sure, the kid has swagger and confidence, but nothing to deliver when it comes to pure talent. I could see him as an actor, or possibly a fashion designer, but when it comes to fighting... leave it to the professionals. And I think he clearly showed that last week, as he got his brains scrambled with a chair. So don't get in my way Clark, or I'll finish the job Hardaway started. Cruz mimics the conchairto shot. Damn... he could make a decent mime. Alright, next track. *I remembered black skies The lightning all around me I remembered each flash As time began to blur Like a startling sign That fate had finally found me And your voice was all I heard Did I get what I deserve* His hand goes up, the music goes off. It's like magic. Well, that was "New Divide" by Linkin Park. But who does it belong to? It sounds familiar, but I can't put a face to it... Hmm... Cruz pauses rubbing his chin. Boop! There goes the light bulb above his head! Ah, no wonder I couldn't put a face to it. It's 0-2 masked man, Oblivion. This mysterious man attempts to intimidate opponents with his fetish talk of pain pleasure. Well, it obviously isn't working. And after Crowning Glory, he'll be 0-3. The dude brought in hype, and he couldn't deliver. But that crazy son of a bitch keeps on trying. I don't want to dig at the poor guy too much though. You can tell something is obviously wrong with him. Wether it be being dropped on his head as a baby, a strong case of A.D.H.D., or actually losing his marbles due to something he did or saw somewhere down the road, I actually feel bad. It's like bashing on a cripple. With a clap of the hands, the next song starts. *No one goes today No one worry get some rest But I don't know how to lose me* Yuck, CUT CUT CUT! Jesús gags, clearly not liking the song at all. He spits out some flem over the top rope before continuing. "A New Hope" by Interference. I go from demented crazy to silly crazy. We're talking about fan favorite, Eric Derf. Derf is just like the United States economy. It's top notch at first, and everyone is happy. Then it hits a bush (pun intended), and he drops into a recession. Unfortunately, the money in the bank will not be his bailout plan. He'll go from 2008 Rookie of the Year to the 2010 unemployment line. So he can talk about all the dinosaurs and Chuck Norris jokes he wants. But the old Eric Derf has moved on by. The fifteen minutes of fame have shriveled up. His recent matches have proved that, and in M.I.T.B., you will all realize it as well. Next. *Love your hate, your faith lost You are now one of us Love your hate, your faith lost You are now one of us* Stop... Stop... Cruz shakes his head, clearly displeased. Well that's "Miseria Cantare" by AFI. But wait, that song doesn't sound familiar? Who does that belong to? I have no idea, and you don't either. And you want to know why? Cruz grabs the top ring rope and shakes it in anger as he yells. Because the guy hasn't even had a match yet! And management thinks it's fair to give him a debut for Money In The Bank?! Cruz releases the top rope, and crosses his arms with a grunt. He takes a moment to control and collect his anger, before speaking again. He's Chicago's own, Shane Phillips. Clearly a filler to get the crowd excited. And no threat to actually winning. I mean, he's a former singer... He got into wrestling when Paige, the most overrated and overhyped MWE wrestler in history, put him in his place. I mean hell, if Paige tried to mess with me, I'd send that bitch crying back to the kitchen where she belongs. But no, oh no, Phillips took a'whoopin. What a twat. But whatever. The kid will come down with a chip on his shoulder and show us what he's got. And then, I'll knock him out... You're welcome. Last song. Hit it! *Is this it? Stop me, oh, oh, oh, stop me Stop me if you think that you've Heard this word before Stop me, oh, oh, oh stop me Stop me if you think that you've heard this word before* Hand up, song off. Lather, rinse, repeat. That's "Stop Me" by Mark Ronson. It belongs to, who many consider, the front runner in this match. C.S.B. I'll admit, he's my toughest challenge in this bout. He's got skills. He's got momentum. He's got the attitude. He's got the heart. But he's missing one key element. Intelligence. For I have a strict game plan coming in to this match. He, on the other hand, is going to come in and just try to whoop ass 'till their is no more ass to whoop. Well Blaise, I'm standing in your way. I am the one thing that is stopping you from winning this match. Not the rookie Phillips. Not the wacky Derf. Not the freak Oblivion. Not the fancy-pants Clark. Not the self-absorbed Kaymakcian. And not the bitch Renshaw. No, me. Jesús Cruz. Try and lock horns, I'll fuck you up. But take your eyes off me and the match can end just like that. Me sitting perched op top of the ladder, with the briefcase held high. Bring your camera. It's going to be a Kodiak moment. The lights fade to complete darkness as "Still Fly" By The Devil Wears Prada begins to play. What the fuck is this? I didn't ask for this song! Cut the music! *What's up Fresh, it's our turn baby Gator Boots, with the pimped out Gucci suit Ain't got no job, but I stay sharp Can't pay my rent, cause all my money's spent But that's OK, cause I'm still fly* Hey, idiots, stop jackin' around! You're fucking up my promo! *Gotcha car play gems on shine, said it's mine, get a mink, baby girl lets ride You da Numba 1 stunna, and we gonna glide* The lights slowly fade up, as Zak Warner is seen strutting down the ramp with a smile. Jesús is clearly shocked and angered as he looks on. With a mic in his hand, Warner slides into the ring. What the fuck are you doing here!? I want no part of you or the offer! The music cuts off, as Zak holds the mic to his mouth. Guess what kid. You didn't have to. Your father accepted it for you. And he's paying me fifty G's to do it. A furious Cruz begins to boil red in anger. NO! I DON'T WANT YOU! GET OUT OF MY SIGHT! Oh, I'm not going anywhere until you listen. Cruz turns around. He can't even look at him. He begins to take deep breaths. In and out. In and out. In and out. Zak couldn't help but laugh. Look kid, I've been in your shoes. I was a mixed martial arts star turned wrestler. But you have to adapt to the environment around you. So when your father called me up, offering me $50,000 to become your full-time manager, I undoubtedly accepted. But first, he wanted your approval. So he approached you with the idea. And what did you do? Run out of the gym like a drama queen. And you haven't been back ever since. Hell, you won't even return his calls. So yesterday, your father called me back and accepted on your behalf. And let me tell you, papa knows best! Cruz turns around, and gets directly into Warner's face. He can't do that! I can do what I want! I don't have to listen to you! Warner, still smiling, pats Jesús on the shoulder. I tell you what. Seeing as I'm a gamblin' man, I've got an offer for you. If you win the money in the bank match, which I see you're so confident in, I'll give you the check for fifty grand, and get out of your life forever. Warner's smile slowly makes a flip, as he moves in nose-to-nose with Cruz. But if and when you lose, I'll keep the cash, and you'll be my student. You'll do whatever I say, whenever I say so. Zak takes a step back, and extends his hand. We got a deal? Jesús hesitates. Looking at Warner, then down to his hand, he quietly mumbles out one word. Deal. And with that, we fade away as the two superstars shake hands with a look of intensity. The bet's on.
