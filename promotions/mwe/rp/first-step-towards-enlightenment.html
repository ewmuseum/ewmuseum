First step towards enlightenment

Jun 19 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Tuesday the 9th of June 2009, 9am The scene opens up inside the hotel room of The True English Patriot Chris England, the lights are out and the only light in the room is the light that’s creeping through the gap in the curtains. This room has a soulless feel to it, this is your typical business class hotel room, it’s exactly the same as every other business class hotel room you will ever stay in, the only thing that ever changes is the pictures on the wall. The camera’s zoom in on Chris England who is flat out asleep in the king-size bed, suddenly the phone begins to ring and England begins to stir from his slumber, he slowly rolls over still half asleep and reaches for the phone, England picks up the phone and clears his throat a few times before speaking. **England** Hello? England sits up in the centre of the bed phone in hand and the duvet still over his body, he stretches and rubs his eyes with his free hand, he lets out a loud yawn as he stretches once more. **Jasmine** Good morning sleepy head, did I wake you? **England** Oh hey good morning Jazz, yeah you woke me but I needed to get up anyway I’ve got a lot to get done today. England rustles around in the sheets as he stretches once more then lets out a yawn. Jasmine lets out a little giggle before speaking in an authoritarian but sarcastic tone. **Jasmine** Then get your lazy ass outta bed and get to it solider, don’t think I can’t hear you rolling round in the sheets. Both England and Jasmine let out some light-hearted nasal laughter. England jumps out of bed quickly throwing the duvet off of him, now on his feet he strikes a military salute as he replies in a military style posh English accent completely forgetting Jasmine can’t see him. **England** YES MARM RIGHT AWAY MARM! Suddenly realising he is doing a military salute to a grand audience of two flies and a spider, he shakes his head thinking to himself what the hell am I doing. Meanwhile on the other end of the phone Jasmine is busy laughing her ass off at Chris. Jasmine gathers herself taking deep breaths before she gets to the point of why she actually called him in the first place. **Jasmine** Still got your charming sense of humour I see, anyway I’m calling to you know arrange so you can come meet Daniel like you asked me too. England now on his feet in the centre of the room leans over picking up a plain black T-Shirt and a pair of smart blue jeans of the floor, he slings the shirt onto the bed and then pulls his jeans on. England being his sharp, witty and sarcastic self then reply’s in a very sarcastic tone. **England** No way really? I thought you were calling to try and sell me a bible or perhaps a new set of dictionaries. On the other end of the Phone Jasmine lets out a little snigger as she shakes her head from side to side with a raised smile on her face laughing slightly the laugh coming through in her voice. **Jasmine** Oh come on Chris can’t you just take something serious for once in your life. Although Jasmine is saying this she doesn’t not even for one moment actually want Chris to do it, you see it is his wit and sarcasm and the fact that he always tries to turn everything into a laughing matter constantly, which attracted her to him in the first place. It is almost like some weird can of reverse psychology as she knows just because she appears to hate it, he will keep doing it. **England** Oh lighten up Jas you know you love it really. England smirks letting out a little nasal laugh, he is completely clueless to the fact that Jasmine does indeed love it, he leans over the bed picking up the T-Shirt he pulls it on over his head then sits down on the edge of the bed. **Jasmine** So do you want to meet Daniel today then or not? **England** Of course I do Jas, where and when do you want to meet? **Jasmine** Well why don’t you just hop in a cab and come here? **England** Yeah sure where’s here? England steps up and walks over to the dressing table, he picks up a pen and a note pad that are laying on the table, he takes the top off the pen in anticipation. **Jasmine** Its 525 este Ramirez street and it’s in the Santo Domingo Este district. England enthusiastically scribbles address down on the note pad with real intent. **England** Ok I got it written down see you I will be there about midday if that’s ok? **Jasmine** Yeah that’s cool, sooooo then I guess we will see you then. **England** Indeed you will, see ya then. England puts the phone down then suddenly out of nowhere BANG, his emotions hit him, his mind starts to race at a million miles an hour, negatives thoughts racing through his mind one after the other, what if he doesn’t like me?, what if he has seen me on TV from my bad days what kind of role model would I be then? Hell it’s only been a few months I’m not even sure I’m a changed man. Immediately he springs to his feet and walks to the wardrobe, he pulls out his suitcase and slings it on the bed, as quickly as he can he begins to pack his suitcase literally throwing the things in his suitcase, by now England is in a full on state of panic, having packed his suitcase in close to world record time he makes a beeline for the door he exits the door in a hurry then it slams shut behind him. **10 minutes later** England can now be seen chewing his finger nails stood on the street corner outside the hotel, seconds later a private hire cab pulls up to the curb, England quickly jumps in the backseat before the cab has even came to a complete stop. **Cabbie** Where to? **England** Airport! As England sits there in the backseat of the cab he takes his cell phone out of his coat pocket and begins to write a txt message. QUOTE Jas I’m really sorry but I just can’t do it, I can’t meet Daniel, not yet at least, first I need 2 prove to myself that I can change and be a good role model to my son, first I must sort my own head out and get my career on track, I’m going back to America on the first flight out of here I’m really sorry Chris xxx England hits send on his phone then quickly switches it off so he doesn’t have to deal with seeing any reply that Jasmine may or may not send, he places it back in his pocket lets out a big sigh and sits back in the seat and closes his eyes. ********Two Weeks Latter*********** The scene opens up at an MWE House show inside the American airlines arena, Miami Florida. The last match up of the evening has just finished and the fans are starting to make their way to the exits, then suddenly Reborn by Drowning Pool hits on the sound system and the crowd pops loudly, they know what this means they know who is coming, they all start to rush back towards their seats as The True English Patriot enters on the ramp way and begins his journey to the ring with microphone in hand. England is dressed in a long sleeved skin-tight black top with a lose white t-shirt that has True English patriot written on it in spiky black writing, a pair of black Armani jeans and pair of smart brown leather shoes. He confidently struts his way down to the ring, he rolls under the bottom rope and takes position in the centre of the ring. The fans begin to chant England England England, he soaks grins and smiles and claps his hand on the microphone in appreciation of their support. **England** Thank you all your so kind, you know it heart-warming that you all have taken me into your hearts so quickly, see you all know and I’m damn sure I know that for a lot of years i was nothing more than a loud mouthed, bitter, self obsessed, Nazi, but I realised the errors of my ways and I saw how they were costing me not only my friendships in my personal life but my career in the ring in front of all you lovely people. The crowd start to chant England’s name once more smiles and grins clapping on the mic once more. You see I saw the error of my ways and so I took action, I vowed to myself that I would do everything I possibly could to make myself a better man and a better wrestler, I made a promise to myself that no matter how hard things get, no matter how much shit I have to go through I would do my best to put on a excellent show for the fans, and tomorrow night in this very arena I’m going to do exactly that, despite the fact that the last two weeks have been an emotional hell for me, you see two weeks ago I found out that I have a son I know nothing about and like a coward I ran away from the situation, but this was purely out of anxiety I mean look at the man I used to be such a short time ago what kind of role model would that person be to a child, so I ran but not because I didn’t want to see him believe me I did, No I ran so I could return to wrestling, so I could turn myself into a better person, so I can prove to myself that I am a good person. The crowd is pretty silent at this point, their all not too sure how to react to England’s outpour from the heart. England is now oozing emotion, his cheeks read each and every word spoke with a deep meaning. Tomorrow night I start to build my legacy, a legacy that I will build based on passion, emotion and sheer dogged determination, but no matter how hard the obstacle put in front of me is I will NOT resort to underhanded tactics to get the job done. See that’s something that the old me would do but not the new me, no the new me will dig deep down inside his soul and give every ounce of his being to get the job done but will NEVER break the rules, I would rather lose than have to cheat to win. The crowd breaks into a round of applause as they chant England’s name once more, and once more he claps on the mic applauding them in response. So as I step into the ring tomorrow night against Jay Riddick and Anthony Clark two of the most talented young superstars in this business today, I step into the ring knowing that as long as I don’t get pinned, I get myself a X-TV Title shot at Independence Day in the First ever high-road match, and don’t get me wrong boys I will be going all out for the win, but it doesn’t matter how talented you guys are, There is more chance of the Cincinnati Bengals winning the super bowl than there is of either of you two pinning my shoulders to the matt tomorrow night, Tomorrow WILL be the first step on my path to enlightenment and my first step on my battle against evil, Clark and Riddick you bet be prepared cause tomorrow night you’re going to be in the fight of your lives because you can bet your arse that I’m going to bring my A game! **England drops the mic to the floor as his reborn begins to play over the speakers once more and the fans chant his name as he rolls out of the ring and makes his way out through the crowd shaking the fans hands on the way out of the arena. End
