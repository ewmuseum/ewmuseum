Life is so weird.; David Whitman RP

Aug 28 2008

David Whitman

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It’s 4:38 in the frickin’ MORNING. Life is so weird. I mean, one second I’m in bed, still dreaming about butterflies and FRACKIN’ rainbows, and the next I’m down here on my kitchen floor. Staring at the light bulb. Funny, yes, but definitely not “ha-ha” funny. I can’t believe that just last week I had a nervous breakdown! Wh- That light’s not supposed to flicker like that. I ought to get that fixed. Where was I? Oh, right. I kinda lost my mind last week… now for some reason I can’t help but think in third person. This is so weird. But at least NOW, I can control myself. I don’t black out anymore and hear afterward that I terrorized some innocent family. At least, not that I know of. Meh. Crap. Sonny, man. I can’t believe it. I don’t think he’s playing. I think he’s really gone over to the dark side. I know he’s still a good guy deep down inside, but I’m not sure that good guy will ever be back. I’ve tried to, but I just can’t seem to figure out a way to do it. To snap him out of it. There used to be a time when everything was much simpler. When good and bad was as clear and day and night. When the answer was always in front of you. When you KNEW everything was going to work out, no matter what. Where the HELL has that time gone? Man. That was good. I ought to write that down. I should write a book. But then Sonny’d probably smack me over the head with it. I miss the Mayhem Mafia. Maybe if I- nah, that’d never work. How late is it? I don’t wanna open my eyes. That’d probably hinder my sleep. Then again, I’m lying on the frickin kitchen floor… Meh. I should be using this time to figure out what to do about Sonny. And that Go#!@*$n clown! Special referee my @$$! I know there’s backstage politics involved. Or at least he knows someone who pulled a few strings. Like a puppeteer. Heh. I oughta write that down for my stand-up act. QUOTE “I always knew it felt like the bigwigs at MWE were running a CIRCUS, but C’MAAAAAHN!” Ha. Hehe. I’m funny. If I want to beat Sonny I’m gonna have to pull out all the stops. Then again… do I want to BEAT him? Not really. Well, that’s not my intention, anyway. I’m going to have to try to get him to see that he’s not doing the right thing. For the first time in a while, winning isn’t the top priority. Heh… funny. Life’s balances. 5:40 already. Time flies. Life is so weird. I mean, one second I’m in bed, still dreaming about butterflies and FRACKIN’ rainbows, and the next I’m down here on my kitchen floor...
