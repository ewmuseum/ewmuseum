I Don't Think We're In [s]Kansas[/s] Mexico Anymore; Cruz/Renshaw MOTP RP

Sep 11 2009

Jesus Cruz & David Renshaw

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
OOC: Sorry I posted so late. My computer crashed so I had to rewrite the entire thing, which was a pain. So if this RP feels a bit choppy or rushed, I'm sorry. But I think, in the end, the roleplay turned out decnet enough to post. So enjoy. “Nobody can go back and start a new beginning, but anyone can start today and make a new ending.” - Maria Robinson We fade in to a filthy apartment. The smell in the air could only be described as a bitter, pungent, foul stank. However, you can’t argue with the smell of victory. Because spread out on the couch, shirtless, with a slice of pizza on his chest, lays a man who came out of Crowing Glory with his head held high. Of course, we’re not talking about Jesús Cruz. He left in shame and dissapointment. No. A winner. Cruz’s new mentor. Have you figured it out Einstein? We are talking about Zak Warner. He sits up, as the pizza slides down his chest and to the floor. But he doesn’t seem to mind. But why should he? Zak is on top of the world. With a smile on his face, he stumbles up from the comfort of his couch. He wipes away some pizza sauce with his hand, and rubs it on the couch cushion. He then heads to the bathroom. The cameraman doesn’t follow (thank god). Soon, we hear the shower begin to run. Knock, knock, knock. Come on in. The door slowly swings open. Suspicious eyes peek into the room, before entering. It’s none other then Jesús Cruz. …Hello? I’m in the shower. Make yourself at home. I’ll be out in a minute. Cruz glances around the room with a grimmice, disgusted with his surroundings. He begins to sit on the couch, but springs back when he notices the red sauce stains. Clearly repulsed, Jesús decides to instead lean against the wall. Noticing the remote near-by, he picks it up. Bad decision. His hand flings back and drops the remote to the ground. If you didn’t know any better, you’d of thought he just touched fire. Dude… What’s this sticky stuff on your remote?! What? ……… What? Not wanting to repeat himself, along with not really wanting to know the awnser that awiats him, Cruz decides to change the topic. So you got any plans, or are we just going to sit around and slack off? The shower shuts off. Seconds later, Warner’s wet head sticks out. You’ll do what I tell you to do. You lost the bet, so I make the rules. Comprende? Cruz raises a middle finger, but it’s too late. Warner’s head has already popped back in, and the bathroom door quickly shuts behind him. Cruz crosses his arms in anger, and lets out a sigh. I got rematch with Renshaw in a few short days, and instead of hitting the gym, I’m here. What good could this do? This is a waste of fucking time. The bathroom door opens up, and Zak struts out with a towel wrapped around his waist. Dude… Stop being such a bitch and chill out… Cruz rubs his palms across his eyes, clearly aggravated. But it keeps it to himself. Let me go throw on some clothes slim. Then we’ll chat. Zak walks out of the camera’s view. Soon after, Cruz begins to talk to himself. I’ve beaten David Renshaw before, so what am I worried about? Hell, I made the kid tap like a drummer boy… …But before, I underestimated him. He came at me like a ton of bricks. This match qualifies us for The Master Of The Premises, so he’s gonna come at me full steam… And I’ve been loafing around like a buffoon ever since losing the Money in the Bank match. Fuck this! Zak, come on! We have to get to the gym, PRONTO! There’s a pause, before Warner walks back into the scene. He’s shirtless, with a torn pair of jeans, and a black hat with the bill towards his back. We ain’t going to a gym Jesus. Hell, we aren’t even working out. Excuse me!? Zak laughs. Cruz doesn’t find it funny. Look dude, you’re over-thinking things. Just relax. How can I relax? I have a bout coming up, and I’m not prepared! Fuck game plans. David’s a joke. You know that. We all know that. Seriously, are you always this stupid or are you making a special effort today? I just don’t want to screw up. Then settle down, and keep your mind at ease. Settle down?! Tell me, oh wise one, just how am I supposed to do that?! Warner smiles. With this. Zak revels a thick plastic bag, filled half way up with “green”. What the fuck is that? It’s your new best friend. *Ten Minutes Later* The room is foggy with smoke, as Cruz and Warner are reclined back on the stained couch with a smile. Jesús takes a drag from the blunt, and begins to cough. After the coughing, he begins to chuckle like a school girl. You’re a lightweight kid. Nah-uh, I’mma middleweight; 185 pounds. Look it up! No… tha-… lawls, never mind. Having fun? Ooooooooooooohhhhhhhhhh Yyeeeeeeeeeeeeeeaaaaaaaaaaahhhhhhhhhhh!~ Good. ‘Cause now that you’re in the right mood, I can tell you about the changes I’ve made to your career. Cruz pauses, confused. Oh? What changes? Well, for starters, your name is Jesus Cruz. It’s actually pronounced Jesús. Not anymore it’s not. What? My name is Jesús, not Jesus! The accent is everything! Dude, everyone calls you Jesus anyway. This just makes things easier. ……Fine. Warner nods before taking back the blunt. Also, at the moment, the crowd doesn’t seem to care for you. Yeah, well I don’t care for them either. You will. 'Cause, by order of Ivory, you’re now a full-fledged face. How am I supposed to do that? Aw, it’s easy. Just give some support to the crowd. Greet them, thank them, tell them you couldn’t of done it without them, etcetera. I will do no such thing! I worked to get where I am, and they had nothing to do with it! …Just act like it then. Have you ever acted? …Hmm… Well, in third grade, I played a bean stock. …… A bean stock? Yeah. In Jack and the Bean Stock. …… Ooooohhh kaaaayyyy. Did you have any lines? Bean stocks don’t talk. Don’t be silly Zak. …… Whatever, just act... Oh, and you’ll be wearing these. Zak reaches into the couch cushion and pulls out a pair of red, white, and blue shorts, and tosses them at Jesus. It smacks him in the face, and lands in his lap. I AIN’T WEARING THIS! Oh, but you will. This week, we’ll be in Missouri, which is straight up middle-America, so those shorts will pull some pops from the crowd. Jesus slowly lifts the shorts, and inspects them. There’s a blue waist-band, with white and red stripes everywhere, and a yellow star on the crotch. I’m not ready for all this Zak. Well you’d better get ready. You’re going to march into St. Louis as the new and improved Jesus Cruz. You’ll head to the ring with a smile on your face, with those lovely patriotic shorts, as you slap hands with audience members on your way down. The bell will ring, and you’ll go at Renshaw with no game plan in mind, and let your body adapt to free styling to the cheers of the crowd. And when it’s all said and done, you will stand tall. And if I don’t? Then I get to kick you straight in the crotch. WHAT!? THAT’S NOT FAIR! Life’s not fair kid. Get used to it. Jesus crosses his arms once again, and sinks into the couch, pouting over the situation. The camera slowly zooms in to Zak’s mug, as he lets out a quick chuckle before taking a deep drag on the blunt once again. Then, he blows the smoke at the lens, as we: Fade to smoke.
