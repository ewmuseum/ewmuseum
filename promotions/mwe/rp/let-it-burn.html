Let it burn...

Nov 28 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The scene fades in and it is bright morning as the hot red ball of fire known as our sun burns high above the clouds over the rocky surface. Sinc is standing in a dry grassy field of wheat as the heat stresses on Sinc. Sinc is wearing a white T shirt over his wrestling attire. He raises his hand high into the sky and a microphone stretches down, towering over him. The camera is only on his upper body as he has a very serious wince on his face. He grabs the mic and darts his eyes like a panther into the camera. Sinc: You know….and I know….that as a champion of ANY caliber, you need to have a duty, a duty to carry the championship PROUDLY and never look back at your past mistakes. But Mason know it all Resoan can’t seem to pry his grubby little fingers off that idea. I went a little calm about him last week. But today…..is another story. I was too busy relaxing myself with a very….fine….ex of mine. But no, he has to go on a Canadian radio show with his prissy little attitude only to find he was going NOWHERE with trying to promote himself on a local radio station. Sinc licks his lips quickly and laughs to himself a bit as he then begins pacing back and forth with the mic still in hand. Sinc: Let me just start by saying that you need to get off Covell’s dick for a second and actually think about what your saying. Beating Sonny Williams is never that hard to anyone. All you need is a fake record label to throw at him and tell him sweet dreams every now and then and he is perfectly harmless, like a new born dumbass he’ll walk away thinking he is the greatest thing to ever NOT be main event material. So let me tell you this, I didn’t worry about Sonny Williams, or Alex Bowen, Or James Sullivan. Why should I worry about someone who obviously can’t even worry about himself. You see….your too busy playing pin the syphilis on the Sara while I am worrying about more important things. The difference is I stand here a champ while you sit there eating pancakes or eating pre-processed, spanked, whipped, and pressed pussy in the janitor’s closet of some run down radio station! Pussy or pancakes, pussy or pancakes, wait what if the pancakes are fluffy? What if their light? Will they be like my mother’s home made wonders? But wait….what if that pussy is warm and STD free because I just LOVE talking about sexual diseases because I MASON THE RESOAN want to teach Canadian sex-ed some day! OH MASON IS IT THAT HARD?!? Sinc takes a breath and taps his chin for a moment. Sinc: Mason you take my breath away….you take it away SO much in fact you cause me to stand in an open field to see something more blank and more boring than your sorry ass. Listening to you is like listening to a Ben Stein lecture, blah blah blah, I look like a f*ckin clone of a character from the Goonies. There has been a saying going around that all assholes are created equal, and Mason if that’s true, then all men MUST be created assholes. Because the way you rant and rave about how your SOOO much better than me makes you look like you have an asshole right in the freakin CENTER of your forehead. It’s like your shouting “HEY LOOK AT ME INSERT SOMETHING PLEASURABLE HERE!” Sinc slaps his head as if to try and reenact what it would seem like and then begins running back and forth then stops and laughs as he slaps his knee. Sinc: That may make me an asshole but I’M PROUD to be an asshole to some people, hell some people even like it, but Mason, I’m sorry to say it doesn’t suit you well. Because unlike you I can play one well and still be liked. But I will be talking about my reputation later… Sinc then begins to sit down in the vast empty field and begins looking at the horizon with the mic in his hand. Sinc: What else is there to say about you Mason? You’ve ran over my favorite little ball of tacos A-Rod with a car, you’ve been hit with a famous MICHAELS elbow drop through a stretcher. And when was that elbow drop rammed through your hairless Canadian chest? AH YES IT WAS ADRENALINE RUSH 07! Unforgettable. Seeing as I don’t think I was AROUND then But yet I am already US Champion, how odd wouldn’t you say Mason? OH YES, I almost forgot YOU EVEN WERE ABLE TO BEAT FORTY FIVE, Count em people, FORTY FIVE Englishmen over some ropes for an indie title! I hope you counted your god damn crumpets and were sh*ttin teabags because man with that many sweaty men in the ring that MUST have felt good. Did you kiss the queen’s ass too? HUH? FOR THE SAKE OF HUMANITY RESOAN, WHAT WERE YOU THINKING? I don’t know how many times I have to say it, scream it, or PLEDGE it, but your CANADIAN! CAN-A-DI-AN. I’m surprised they didn’t burn your ass with a brander and ship you to the US so I can beat you then RE ship you back to Canada! But the sad thing is, the forty five men you beat in an INDIE federation was useless, because the indie star Kyle Summerslam or whatever his name is, beat you only to make you look like a DAMN fool and keep his title. That’s a real shame Mason. Sinc raises a finger in the air as he winces. Then he just sighs. As if suddenly a darkness looms around him. He shakes his head and makes a deformed frown. Sinc: But yet, you were able to become a true world champ and for that I have no choice but to respect you. That is something I have yet to achieve, but I promise you one day I will. I’ve beaten a world champ, his name was Sonny, you all know the rest people. And to me, your just another Sonny, another know it all thug in my eyes. And What I want to do is make sure you know my name, good and well, right after I beat you to a bloody mush in that ring next week. I want to see you squirm, crawl, BEG for mercy. And then you know what I’m gonna do to you? I’m gonna smack the taste out of your mouth, drag your sorry behind all around that ring, act like it’s literally a walk in the park, and give you the biggest SWING SET I can muster until you realize you just couldn’t land that last jump off that swing Mason. Because I am a man of no remorse, at this point I really can’t care about my fans, I care about seeing you suffer. I’m sorry you have to be the first, but after everything that has been running through my mind….it looks like your going to have to be. Kiss Canada goodbye, hell, kiss the WORLD goodbye because where your going your gonna need an alternate passport to pain. Sinc stops and looks at the ground as he lets go of the mic and it lures back into the air. He suddenly makes a sinister gaze and a devious smile. He then looks back at the camera in confidence. Sinc: If you haven’t noticed by now, I am pretty pissed off. I am mad at every piece of living scum that ever shunned or doubted me. I am sick and tired of being seen as a person of low magnitude. Sure, fine, whatever I am US champion, but for how long am I gonna sit and pick my ass all day to see others getting their SHOT at that world gold. That’s why I am going to crush Mason, I am going to become my own lord and savior! Sinc is enraged as he is pointing and gesturing harshly at the camera, all that is needed now is the drool and foam of hatred to boil down his mouth. Sinc: IF I am ranting people I am SORRY. Because it’s god damn obvious I am not yet seen as worthy or successful to many of you, but I WILL be, I WILL become something more to you all. You will all see a dark wall enclosing around me soon because I am tired of being the funny jerk, I want to become something more. I want to become someone with the passion and the ability to create a level beyond pain. Beyond suffering. I want to engulf myself in my childhood rage, I want to look back on the past just as Mason seems to, look him in the eyes, and tell him…two can play that game. The future of MWE is bleak and you will see a dark cloud over that ring next week, your going to be facing a man of war and anger. So Mason… Sinc goes off frame for a second and grabs a can of gasoline as he begins pouring it into a pattern. He does this in a quick, aggressive way. Sinc: You can do your prissy little radio shows… Sinc stops to edit his gasoline design. Sinc: Your English brawls… Sinc stops once more to focus on what he’s doing almost done with what he wants to do. Sinc: Even your rants and promotions….. Sinc finishes and throws the gas can onto the ground in anger. Sinc: But you can’t….as respected as you are….you can’t change or bring down a man who can only save and change himself….and I'm not going to say this to the fans, I am going to say this for me.... Sinc rasps his voice in a deep agony. Sinc: SIIIIIIIINCCCCCCCCCCCC........MERCIERRRRRRRR!!!!!!!!! Sinc grabs a match from his right boot and strikes it on the bottom of it. It lights on fire in a small, amazing blaze. He then throws it into the pattern as he bounces away and stops covering his face with his arms. The camera focuses on him for a minute or two more as he continues eying it devilishly. It then goes to the flames and shifts to a sky shot from another camera in a zoom out. Sinc walks off frame blankly as the sky cam gets a shot of the fire. Finally it finishes burning through Sinc’s gasoline design. And it makes the words “Save Me” in large letters. The camera then zooms out a bit more and fades to utter black.
