The memorial.

May 3 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
On a crisp January afternoon in Upstate, New York we see Blight riding his custom Harley. His hair blowing back in the wind, the cold air nipping at his face turning it very red. Blight rides up a winding hill where we see a cemetary. He rides through its front gates and far back to a large tree that is providing shade for a grave that reads Cory Chevelle: Beloved brother, husband and friend Blight takes a knee on the hard cold ground and bows his head in a moment of silence. He begins to break down before regaining his composure and starts to speak. BLIGHT: This is the first time we have seen each other in six years my friend. I am truly sorry for not coming sooner...this is something that is still to hard for me to do. Blight pauses for a second almost as if he is again trying to regain his composure. BLIGHT:Since day one of my career you have always been there for me. You took me under your wing as a rookie, as a jobber...and you made me into an unstoppable force. Then tragically the world lost you...I lost you. I retired for a while but one thing that you always said to me was that wrestling was in our blood, and that has proven true. I have decided to come out of retirement. Cory you were my friend, my brother, my mentor and to honor you I with your blessing will use the move that I had fallen victim to. In fact I was its first victim...the Bloodlust. The squeaks of a squirrel on the tree branch above quickly draws Blights attention away from the grave. He stands up streching out his legs before turning his attention back to the grave. BLIGHT:My new home MWE has lined up my debut match. Its a five way battle royal of all new blood. For most of us in that match we don't know each other from a hole in the wall. I have someone who is too busy trying to get his nut off to anything that resembles a female, to even focus on the match. Then theres probably the most technichally sound man in the match, but I'm not going to stress him too much. We have someone who really has no history at all. He kinda stays to himself...they always say the quiet ones are the most dangerous hahaha. Finally someone we both know. Frank Stephens--- Suddenly theres a voice from behind that startles Blight. He whips around to see who has approached him and is greeted with a sort of pleasant suprise. TONY DANIELS: Dude it is you! We haven't seen you since Cory's funeral. How are you doing man? What are you doing here? TY BAHR: Tony I don't think he is throwing a beer bash ya' know. TONY: Shut up Ty. Damien its been so long...we knew you took it hard, and when you disapeared and noone could find you. We have been holding a memorial here every year since Cory's passing... Blight, Ty and Tony look at each other for a second before Ty speaks out again. TY: Wow you too can really bring a rooster off its rooftop...yall are too gloomy for me. So Damien we heard you say something about Frank Stephens, were we right? Blight still looking at the men in disbelief that they are standing in front of him snaps out of it. He looks up and sees that it is starting to snow a bit and motions for the men to follow him. BLIGHT:Yeah you guys heard right. I have come out of retirement and found a new home, Frank is in my debut battle royal. The last time we were in a ring together he was able to sneak one away...I'm going to make sure this time is different though. TONY: Wow man student surpassing teacher possibly? Hahaha I'm kidding buddy, just a joke. Ya know...maybe we need to get back into the ring with you its been a while. Plus I think Ty is still jealous that he never got to beat you. The men reach the curb where Blight has his bike parked and where Tony's car is. TY: At least I tried to beat him Tony, you never wanted to face him. Tell him Damien tell him! Blight just smirks at his redneck buddy, and heavily pats him on the back of his head. Tony's cell phone starts to ring. He answers it and walks away. BLIGHT: It was good seeing you guys again. I really should get going before the snow gets bad I have a long ride. TY: Damien...why did you disapear? TONY:Maybe another time Ty we got to go as well, that was the boss on the phone he needs up to check out some jobbers. Damien be safe dude, hopefully our paths will cross again. TY:Cheers bro! Ty pulls a beer out of his coat and starts to drink as both men get into Tony's car. Blight sits on his bike starts in up and looks back into the camera. BLIGHT: Dexter, Riddick, Mr. Sextasy or whatever the hell your name is...there is no dodging your Bullet Ride...but Sin I have a Bloodlust waiting just for you. Blight takes off on his motorcyle and roars away as the scene fades out.
