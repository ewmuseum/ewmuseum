The Irony of it All; David Whitman RP

May 23 2009

David Whitman

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The scene? Well… just imagine your typical bar. No cameras, photographers, fans or anything of the like. A couple of patrons, maybe, but they’re just finishing up their drinks and leaving. Right about now, the only customer left is David Whitman. The Phoenix, Arizonian, who is sitting at the bar, and has a glass in his hands. The familiar atmosphere that he would usually feel when he is in this position is not present; instead, a contrasting feeling hangs over him and the area surrounding him. That might just be because he’s NOT at the Top Rope Tavern; rather, he’s at Bill’s Bar in Columbus, Ohio. And the MWE champion, dressed in all black, both his shirt and his signature pants, is not drinking his usual beer, but instead sips on water on this day. David’s pensive stare at the glass is broken abruptly as Bill, the bartender and proprietor of the place, taps on the bar. Bill: You got some nerve, you know. David looks up at him slightly, a surprised look on his face. David: Uhhh… Excuse me? The bartender now walks over to David and stands in front of him. Bill: I didn’t want to say anything before, you know, in front of the other customers. But you have got some nerve comin’ in here. A hesitant and very light chuckle is David’s first response. David: Yeah, I was expecting you to defend Anderson. Bill scoffs, in a way that lets you know exactly what he thinks of Semi; “That troublemaker?”, or something along those lines. Bill: What? No, I just meant, you know, if he caught you here, I doubt you’d make it to the pay-per-view. David semi-smiles (get it?) and now talks to Bill almost as if to a fan. David: Ah. Well, you know how I operate. I like get into the shoes of my opponent. Bill shrugs it off, not quite getting where David is coming from. Bill: To be quite honest, I DON’T know how you operate, but thanks for the tip. Although… try to aim for monetary next time. David breaks slightly. He takes a sip from his half-empty glass of water before speaking to Bill again, this time with a hint of respect in his voice. David: Thing is, I KNOW Semi. I’ve tangled with him, to say the least. Bill: Don’t remind me. David: It’s just… I don’t know. Everything’s been going downhill. First I get f*cked over by Williams, then by Blood Fuze- Bill: I thought that wasn’t on purpose? David scoffs and shakes his head. David: Right. Anyway… then Michaels turns all evil on me… although I gotta admit, that was more of my fault; I shoulda seen that coming, what with our history and all. But still, you’d think it’d be ME who turns on HIM, if anything. But yeah. It sucks. And that’s just the people who were supposed to be on MY side. Bill, who is now cleaning a glass with a towel, shakes his head in a “tsk-tsk” manner. Bill: Harsh. David takes another sip of his water as he lightly nods in agreement. David: Yeah. And here’s the thing… I mean with, say, Alejandro Rodriguez, things’ve gotten PAST complicated. Sharpman completely transformed himself into something else. Cannon was always scheming and sicker than he led on. Resoan was always lying to himself, pretending his faults weren’t there… David sighs as he finishes his water in one gulp, and places his glass back on the bar. Bill puts the glass he was just cleaning next to David’s, and proceeds to instinctively reach for a bottle of whiskey. David doesn’t even acknowledge this as he goes on, still gazing at his now empty glass. David: What I’m trying to say is that, I guess in a way, you know, Semi’s the only person I can count on to be consistent… He’s there to f*ck you up and run you down, and he never fails to do it. Bill: Yeah, I guess that’s one way of looking at it. David: So now the question is… how do I WIN? I mean… I’ve never actually BEATEN him. Bill: You sure? David: Well I mean, I’ve pinned him once, but- I mean that was like when Cannon pinned ME, you know? It doesn’t count. Bill chuckles as he pours whiskey into the two glasses. David stares at his half-empty glass of whiskey, turns to Bill, then back to the whiskey. He downs it in one shot, barely shuddering. Taps on the bar. A sign for more. Bill refills the glass. Bill: Semi… heh. Well, I haven’t heard from him in a while. Truth is… he hasn’t been around since he went crazy in here… I had to force him out. There are times I regret losing my cool that night, but if there’s one thing I know, it’s that people don’t change. He’s probably the same ol’ tickin’ time bomb he always was. David: Heh. People don’t change… ain’t that the truth. David picks up the glass and swerves it around in his hand, swirling the brownish liquid inside. David: If it’s true in every sense, this next match is gonna be messed up. It’s always a struggle to keep up with Semi. I mean, I’ve faced powerhouses before, but Semi’s this insane mixture of power and skill… I mean, I’m faster, but if he’s got me beat on power AND skill I’m pretty much not gonna make it. Bill: And sitting here drinking is gonna help you out… how? David shrugs, and downs his whiskey. David: Who knows. It usually does, though. I mean, getting into the routine of my opponent… It makes me feel more… at one with them. I can predict their moves better. I can counter more fluidly. It sounds weird, but… I dunno. It helps. A shrug from the bartender. Bill: Whatever works, I guess. David is now tilting his empty glass from side to side as he speaks, this time with a hint of reluctance in his voice. David: Speaking of inexplicable things… you know what… I’ve had this ominous feeling for a while… like something really bad is gonna happen. Bill: Thanks for the explanation, though I DO know what “ominous” means. David: Yeah yeah sorry.Heh. It’s just that, I dunno. I guess I always thought, you know, once I get over the mayor hurdles in my life, like, mentally, psychologically speaking… once I got over that, everything would just be better. And instead I get to the top of the mountain only to find out it’s crumbling beneath me, and HAS been ever since I started nearing the top. You know what I mean? Bill: Sure. The bell dings, sounding faintly familiar to David as he cracks a slight smile. He finally rests his glass back on the bar and slowly gets up as he extends his right hand. David: Well, it’s been nice, Bill. I think I’ll be going now and let you get back to your customers. They shake hands. As David turns to head out, he is met by an unexpected face. Kate: Hi. --- Mere minutes later, David and Kate are sitting in David’s car. David, in the driver’s seat, is seemingly staring at the steering wheel. Kate is gazing outside the window, not concentrating on anything in particular. The silence that has accompanied them there for a while finally overwhelms them and, as it often does, inexplicably forms itself into words. David: So… we just gonna sit here, or… No reaction. Kate: … David: … Alright, fine. I’ll just ask. What on earth are you doing here? An angry Kate turns to David. Kate: I could be asking you the same thing! David sighs. Another silent moment passes… David: Okay, look. You haven’t been yourself since the whole life swap thing, and now you go and follow me to Ohio- Kate slams her fist on the dashboard, catching David completely off-guard. Kate: I DIDN’T FOLLOW YOU! You have any idea what I had to go through to even find out where you were? David sighs yet again. David: Listen… you know I can’t deal with this emotional crap. Especially right now. I am just drained. Completely. After that loss to Sharpman, I have to face Semi, who, to be honest, I don’t really see myself beating right now… It’s all just too much. Why don’t you just explain to me what’s going on? Another long pause. Kate fiddles with her thumbs. They share a look, and David knows instantly that Kate also knows about that ominous feeling he’s been having. Just as he begins to wonder what it all means, Kate rubs her eyes and begins talking. Kate: I just… Okay. Here it is. When I found out you kissed Lindsey, it just felt weird. I mean, you know, I don’t know WHY, but I kind of felt betrayed. We’re just friends and all, and it’s all my doing, yeah, but still… I don’t know. And ever since then… you haven’t been the same. And you haven’t been getting better, either, as you’ve been saying. I don’t know, and I can’t explain it, but you’ve been changing… into something I can’t quite put my finger on. And it’s not your fault. I understand that. She stops. A couple of seconds go by before David tries to say something, but Kate holds up her hand, stopping him. Kate: I… I didn’t know what was happening to you. And I still don’t. I doubt even YOU know. Though… what’s been happening recently; your losses, both in your life and in the ring… your lack of concentration… I think you’re beginning to see it now. I don’t know WHAT it is, but when you figure it out, I want you to tell me. David: … Kate, I still don’t- Kate: I heard Semi was on his way to the TRT. David freezes as confusion overcomes him. Kate: Yeah. I don’t know why, or what he’s planning, but it doesn’t feel right. The fact that you decided to visit HIS favorite bar, I don’t know if that’s an ironic coincidence, or what, but… yeah. I know you respect him, but when you get into the ring with him… You can’t hold back. You CANNOT hold back. He is not just going into this looking at it as just another match, and you know that. Silence. As soon as David realizes Kate is done, he speaks up. David: Kate… I understand what you’re feeling. You’re scared. Not just about what comes next, but about my wellbeing. We’ve been THROUGH this. WHQ- Kate: This ISN’T WHQ! And this isn’t the old MWE either. Kate sighs. A second later, David releases a sigh of his own. David: I know. Kate: I know you do. Look… it’s all very unclear right now, and I’ve never been more scared about the future. Just promise me you’ll make it through your match with Semi. A very tense moment takes its toll as they both stare at each other, knowing what David will answer but also knowing that the answer won’t be as true as it should be. David: I will, Kate. I will give it my all, and I will make sure to watch myself. Kate: Good. And after Tribulation… David: After Tribulation, we’ll sort this whole thing out. All of it. The dark feeling… the whole thing with James… My apparently self-destructive path… And US. We’ll figure it all out. Both David and Kate get their first genuine smile of the night, small, but genuine, as David’s right hand squeezes Kate’s left hand gently. David proceeds to place start the car, and looks out in front of him. Kate, still smiling, once again stares emptily out her window. They leave the parking lot and drive off. All of a sudden, Kate turns to David. Kate: Oh yeah, and uh, I heard something about a birthday party for D.C. coming up? David: Oh? Kate: I believe you’re invited? David: Ah. Should be fun. ===
