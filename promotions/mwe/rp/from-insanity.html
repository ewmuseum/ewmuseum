From Insanity...; A David Whitman RP

Sep 27 2008

David Whitman

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It’s 3:28 pm on a Tuesday, and James Whitman, dressed in his usual garb (long pants, matching black "DeathTrap" shirt) is walking around in his Cousin David’s house in Phoenix, Arizona. James: David? Daaavid? Davidinkaaaaaa? James stops for a moment, and concentrates hard. He tries to register the noise he hears, but it's not loud enough to make out exactly what it is. James: What the f- He walks up the stairs, towards the wailing sound he’s hearing. He passes a couple of rooms before getting to the one where the sound is coming from. The door is slightly ajar, and he pushes it open and slowly enters. The sight of the room startles him a bit; it seems to be an unused guest room. The room gives off a strange mixture between a sterile and a broken-down vibe; it’s mostly white and empty except for a broken chair in the corner, and an old beat-down bed on the other side of the room, with a couple of empty bottles of whiskey next to it. And, of course, David. David Whitman, dressed in his long black pants and wearing a tattered old t-shirt, is sitting on the floor leaning on the bed. He’s wearing dark sunglasses despite the fact that it isn’t particularly bright, and he is playing a harmonica. James: WHAT. THE. HELL. David stops playing the bluesy riff he’s been playing and takes the harmonica away from his mouth. He looks up in James' direction. David: Hey, man. James: Seriously? Really? David: What? I got the blues. James: Good grief. James takes David by the shoulder and drags him up. David pulls himself loose. David: Hey, calm down, man! James: NO! You have every right to be a bit sad, but if you’re so much as even THINKING about being depressed again, I will kick your- David: Hey, hey, hey! I’m not depressed, man! James gets a confused look on his face. James: Wh- David puts his arm on his cousin’s shoulder and leads him out of the room. David: I said I got the blues, man. I’m not sad at all. Yeah, I’m disappointed that I lost the title at Turmoil. But that’s no biggie, ace. I’m getting a chance to win it back. I’ve just got this bittersweet feeling right now. I mean, you know. It’s like a kind of down, but still hopeful feeling. It’s- James: Alright, BB, settle down. I get your point; you’re fine. So, let’s get a beer. You need to loosen up a bit before you start practicing for your match with Jekyl. David: WHAT?! WHAT MATCH?! James slaps his cousin without a moment of thought. David: WHAAAT THE F- James: You IDIOT! Don’t you remember?! Covell made the match last week??... YOU WERE IN THE RING! David: … James: How drunk are you right now? David: Extremely. 8 pm, same day. David is waking up. He is in another room, his bedroom, on a nicer bed. The bed is surrounded by empty water bottles. Across from him, James Whitman is on another bed, reading a book. He sees David is getting up. James: Hey there, Muddy. How you feel? David: Not bad, actually. James: Oh, right. You don’t get hangovers anymore. I guess that whole brain injury thing DID have a silver lining. David sits up, and looks around, as if trying to remember something. David: DUDE! I have to face Jekyl! James: Uh, yeah. James tosses David a bottle of Fiji water, which David promptly opens and takes a drink of. David: Thanks. But what do I DO?! Jekyl’s insane. I HAVE to win this match. If I lose, I won’t get that title shot! James: I know. And did you see that big screen trick? He’s a smart one, too. David: Thanks for your help, cuz. James: No prob, knob. David downs the rest of the water in a few big gulps before tossing the empty bottle at James, getting up off the bed and leaving the room. James tosses his book aside and follows his cousin. He catches up to David in the hallway, and holds him back by grabbing him by the shirt just before he can enter the living room. David: Wha- Hey look, this is not a good time for you to come out of the closet, douche-fag. I’m gonna go in there to watch some TV and clear my mind. James: Look, I just want you to know that Thomas, Scott, Eddie and me only arranged this surprise for you because we care about you, and we want to see you win this thing. Good luck. James turns around and walks away, heading back to the bedroom. David: James?? JAMES??? Sigh. Well, whatever you did, it can’t be worse than leaving me stranded to walk a million miles… David opens the door and enters the living room. As soon as he takes a step, the door slams shut and a locking sound is heard. David turns around. David: Whaaaat the f- A stiff chair shot finds it way to the back of David’s head. He falls to the ground, rolling around in agony, as the lights all turn off, filling the room with a chilling, unsettling vibe. David: Son of a B*TCH! As David gets up, a green glow falls across the room, and a creepy laugh sounds loud throughout the room. David: Oh for the love of- you have GOT to be kidding me. “I walked with a zombie” by Wednesday 13 starts playing, before it abruptly stops. Two voices are heard. I distinctly remember you saying track THREE! How the hell can you mix up four and thr- look, just skip to the next track! “Bad Things” by Wednesday 13 starts playing now, as the room is now covered by flashes of purple alternated with the green. David: Oh come ON! I HEARD YOU GUYS! Scott, Eddie, come out here! Stop it! This isn’t FUNNY! Or productive, for that matter! All of a sudden, three men appear from behind the large couch in the center of the living room. They are all dressed the same; in a blue tattered dress shirt and green vest, with purple-colored velvet dress pants and black dress shoes, with white face paint and green hair. David looks at each of them, then at all three together. The song stops playing, and the room is filled with silence for a few seconds. Then, all of a sudden, David bursts into laughter. One of the “clowns” takes a step forward. Clown #1: HEY! STOP IT! This isn’t funny. David falls to his knees, unable to control his laughter. David: Thomas… I can t-HAH… I can tell it’s you… you all look RIDICULOUS! David is now on his hands and knees, laughing hard and slamming the ground with his right fist. The clown turns to the others and nods. They hand him a chair, and he slams it across David’s back. David: AAAAAAAAH! David turns, and before he gets hit with the chair again, rolls out the way. David: What the HELL is wrong with you guys?! He gets to his feet. David: Stop this! I know it’s you! Do I need to prove it?! Thomas, remember that one time you told me about the girl you dated who liked wrestling, and when y- Clown #1: HEY! SHUT UP! Ok, yes, you win, it’s just us! But listen, you need to act as if we’re Jekyl. David: Can you please tell me what kind of SENSE this makes?! Thomas: Well look. WE’RE not insane. David: I’d beg to differ. Thomas: Well all I know is, we’re not insane like Jekyl. But we watched all his matches, and promos, and learned all we can about him. And if we only learned a THIRD of what makes him tick, then three of us equals three thirds, and that’s one Jekyl! David: That… is unbelievably ridiculous. Scott: Yeah, plus, look at it this way, if you can beat THREE Jekyls, you’ll definitely be able to defeat the one Jekyl for your title shot! Thomas turns to the other two "Jekyl"s. Thomas: Dude, why are you going against what I said? Now you’re making me look like an idiot. Scott: That’s Eddie. I’M Scott. And besides, you already look like an idiot. Eddie: Yeah dude. I’M Eddie. Can’t you tell us apart? Scott’s been tag teaming with you, for- Thomas: HAVE YOU COMPLETELY LOST IT?! WE’RE ALL WEARING THE SAME MAKEUP! Eddie: Oh, so now you’re saying all clowns look alike? Real nice. Scott: Yeah, you frickin’- Is that racism? Clownism? Uh… YOU FRICKIN’ RACIST! Thomas: Oh, again with this! IT’S NOT THEIR REAL SKIN COLOR, IS IT?! I AM NOT A RACIST! Will you just stop it? Noone likes a broken record. Scott: DJs probably like broken records. Eddie: Yeah, they can use them to scratch! Ew-rie-ew-rie! Scott: Although… Eddie: What? Scott: I mean, if the record’s really BROKEN- Eddie: Yeah, but it’s NOT broken, like, in HALF. I mean the whole broken record thing refers to when the records WORKS, but keeps getting STUCK, right? Scott: Oh, RIGHT! But then you CAN use that to scratch, though, right? Eddie: Yeah, definitely. Ee-ree-ew-ree. Scott and Eddie both turn to Thomas, who is just staring at them, bemused. Thomas: Are you done? Is everybody happy? Scott: I dunno about HAPPY, but- Thomas: LET’S JUST DO THIS THING! They all turn to face David, but instead find an open window on the other side of the room. Thomas: Oh for- They all run towards the window, look outside, and see David running away. Thomas lets out a loud sigh. Thomas: Eddie, get the car.
