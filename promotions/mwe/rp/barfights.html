Barfights; Who needs 'em?

Aug 6 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The scene opens up with Aeron sitting on a stump of an old broken weeping willow. The dead of night in the graveyard gives off an eerie aura almost as if Aeron wasn't the only person there. Crypts and Mauseleoms are scattered scarecly over the plot of land with rows and rows of tombstones each engraved with a different name, dates of their births and deaths and their own little dialogue about that person. A low fog rolled in as clouds started to cover the entire night sky, now it was almost a pitch black. Night noises was all that was heard. Owls, mice, the wind whistling through the rotten tree branches. Aeron stood up off the tree branch. He sighed as he stood looking around as if waiting for someone, or some...thing. A few minutes later a bright ray of moonlight shone on a single tombstone. Aeron paced slowly towards it humming. He got to the tombstone that was illuminated with the light of the moon. Aeron kneeled down to look at it, it had no writing on it at all. As he looked at it, it seemed as the entire world around him had stopped. The clouds stopped moving, the wind blowing dead leaves were caught mid-air, time itself stood still. Aeron picked up a rock that he had found lying around near the tombstone, he picked up a larger more blunt rock and he started to chisle words into the tombstone. Now all that was heard was the grunts of effort and the sounds of the two rocks hitting eachother. Aeron stood up and let a sigh of relief out as he dropped the rocks and walked away. The camera pans in on the tombstone. The only words that are seen are "Aeron Beowulf v J.K. Valentino" two lines leading from both names up and then into eachother then a final line leading up where a faded imprint of the MWE X-Television Title. The camera pans back to the near Aeron who stopped abruptly during his walk back to the streets. A heavy gray fog rolled in covering everything and just as fast as it came, seconds later it had dissappeared. Aeron, no where to be seen. The cameraman starts to walk back to his van. Walking through the desolate land. He started to walk faster in fear of being alone in the graveyard. He made it to the parkinglot lookaing around for his van. A small peice of paper was blown towards him. He picked it up and looked at it and read it aloud. Cameraman: "Dear Idiot, taking your van wether you like it or no- Aeron Beowulf", like it or no? Sheesh idiot doesnt know how to spell... The camera cuts out and screams are heard. Aeron sitting in the van driving to a bar. Radio: So I herd u liek mudkipz! A new catchphrase from the famous "Interwebs"! Aeron: What the fuck does that queer listen to? Aerons switching through the dials trying to find something interesting. Radio: Freak thunderstorms coming from the south. Aeron checks the GPS unit seeing where south was. He took a look and there was the large thunderstorm coming overtop of him. Lightning flashes and thunder booms was the only thing Aeron had with him at the time. He looked across the countryside looking at all the farm animals panicking. He snickered slightly. He kept going eventually he made it to the "Rusty Spittoon", a local bar. He parked the van and walked inside, the storm still going on. Floorboards creaking beneath him as he sits down and orders a beer. ???: Hey punk, yous be sittin in my seat. Aeron: And who are you? Aeron stares at the man. Dusty: The names Dusty, now get out of my seat! Aeron ignores the man as he takes a slow sip of his beer. Dusty obviously drunk, picks up a chair and attempts to toss it at Aeron only to hit someone on the other side of the room. A full blown barfight breaks up, chairs flying, tables tipped over. Aeron still sitting in that chair drinking his beer not caring about the barfight. Bartendette: Arnt you worried!? Aeron: Nope, Not my problem. One of the local drunkards swung his fist at Aeron but missed him and hit his beer shattering the bottle. Aeron: Nooow its my problem. Aeron stands up and cracks his knuckles. Dusty seeing that Aeron had gotten up decided to punish him for stealing his seat. Dusty swung, Aeron ducked under the punch and thrusted his hand into Dusty's stomach. Dusty falls over on the ground as the same drunkard that broke Aerons beer broke a chair over his back. Aeron turns around and looks at him. Drunky: STOP *Hic* SPEAKING *Hic* SO LOUDLY *Burp* Aeron shrugs as he picks up a bottle off the counter. He looks at the drunkard and pulls the classic open-to-close hand "Bring it on" sign. Drunky: ILL TEACH YOU *Hic* TO SLEEP WITH MY WIFE! *Hic* Aeron still confused about what the drunkard is rambling on about. The drunky rushes at him only to be met with a beer bottle over the head. Dusty had gotten up and yelled to everyone. Dusty: LETS GET THE NEW GUY! Everybody drops everything and looks at Aeron. They all shrug and run at him yelling. Aeron decides to pull a "Starwars Kid" something he had heard about over the radio about the "Interwebs" on a site called "Youtube", He picks up a broom and breaks the head off of it and starts swinging at the people smacking them across the head, some on the knees. Everybody stops and looks at Dusty. Crowd: We aint fightin that guy! Dusty: Cowards! Alot of the fighters are on the ground, some bleeding, some hopping around on one leg grabbing the other in pain. Aeron smirks and decides to walk back to his van. Aeron smirks and smiles as the van drives off. Aeron: Just like the good ol' days The van idles in the parking lot as the fog that looked like the fog from the graveyard rolled in and just like before as it came it dissappeared with Aeron in it.
