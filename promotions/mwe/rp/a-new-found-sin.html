A New Found Sin?

Jan 25 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Space, it is truly the FINAL frontier. Stars, planets, galaxies, the great unknown as it is called is yet to be fully explored or fully seen. There will always be that curiosity, that feeling of looking into the sky and wondering what in the living hell is actually out there. There’s a reason for astrology, just like there is a reason for physics, chemistry, and mathematics. Most of us hate all those subjects, but yet we cannot help to tease ourselves into finding out about them. We may fail overall to understand, but we secretly learn what we ultimately strive for, knowledge of the unknown. What is a big bang? A white dwarf? A cosmos? Hell, what is a star? We once did not know any of these things, but as we grow, as we evolve into bitter beings and suffocate in society, we realize none of it matters because earth is our true home. And as the economy crumbles the roar of time envelops us in age and rot, and before we can get a hold of life we deteriorate. And after we die the earth itself will slowly, in the brink of limitation and all its petty problems(world hunger, STD’s, pollution, global warming, terrorism, drugs) will crumble. And we are left to wonder, where to go after death. Do we depict a scene of the dead at the feet of Jesus looking at the cross in a star gazed awe and plead our sins, or do we relive forgotten memories over and over in a nailed coffin? Enough questions. Let these two men of class and bravery answer them. Two appropriately dressed men stand tall in the middle of a room that shows the entire galaxy lined up all around them. The ceiling revealing the center of the galaxy, the walls harnessing many planets and bright stars. The floor, yes, the cold floor is where Earth sets itself, beneath the men’s feet. The scene pans upwards in a dramatic context making Sinc Mercier and Joey Dallas plainly in sight. Dallas with his signature pinstripe suit and black, polished steel toe boots. Sinc with a newly unique dark, charcoal like textured suit and loose grey slacks. And to extrude appreciative insight both men seem to be wearing a fitting red tie. They stand side by side in such a demanding posture, their hair slickly gelled into a nu style of sorts. The glowing of the barraging galaxy around them illuminates their faces as the room is glittered with its fluorescent variation of lights. It is of course a glow in the dark room, why these men are standing here is out of the question. Dallas: The outside world is a big place….ah but space…that’s much, much, bigger. We stand in this superb room of astrological perfection to express ourselves that even space can be seen as nothing but a business. The elegance of such a vast place leads to so many unanswered questions and political squabbles it makes my head want to fall off. I don’t need nor do I want to know what ill faded jewels rest out there. I am not even done living out my wait on this sorry excuse for a planet. Filled with naïve minds and complacent backstabbers this hellhole is hardly what I call home. I stand here in this holographic room of space because the business MWE to me, is like space itself. Full of those little bright stars that try but never could make their way to the tippy top of the sky. And that ring, that ring is like earth to me, the core of my essence and girth, the core of my hatred for all that is unworthy and distrusting. And I will be the one that holds it in the fate of my hands. Dallas extends his hand outward as if he is cradling something and looks at his hand in great thought. He tries to imagine earth as a small, round, imitation within his grasp and smiles defining it in his mind. Sinc is content and listens to Dallas wondering what he can learn from a man like himself. Sinc too looks at Dallas’s hand in interest and thought. Dallas: You see what me and my, well, associate I could say, did to Mason was a message that not everything can be perfect. Not even Mason. His impure extravagance made me want to puke. I hated his goody goody ways and his deafening words trying to bury me and Sinc in verbal insults. And you need to know something Mason, Sinc is MY protégé, MY student. You couldn’t teach him a damn thing. You couldn’t show him how to dominate this business or be a real man even if you tried. I have already seen a change for the better in him. Dallas turns to Sinc and straightens his collar with a grin of extreme accomplishment. The energy between the two can only be described as electric. The ferocity of their careers and the image of their words has stretched across MWE in such a big way, and it can only continue to do so. Dallas: And Cannon? Please. The best he can do is throw a dollar bill at your face and tell you to handle the match yourself. After Sinc beat him to no end Cannon is nothing to me. I believed Cannon could be a real challenge for a man like I, but after that PATHETIC loss he is just a piece of gum on the streets. Maybe we will face each other again Cannon, but for now you are not even a spec in my eyes. And Mason, I hope you know that Cannon will only drag you down, you may pull off one of the greatest rants in history, but he will be the one that puts you down and hands us the victory. You gaining the upper hand on me was nothing short of a miracle. I don’t know how you managed to tweak out a near victory but I assure you that will not happen again anytime soon. And if me and Sinc have to send another little message to you again like we did last week, It would be OUR pleasure won’t it Sinclair? Sinc nods his head deviantly in agreement and dusts his shoulders off as he positions himself as somewhat of a showman to the camera. Dallas takes a step back and tucks his hands behind him letting Sinc say a few words. Sinc: Ah yes, Mason. I wish I could say you are close to a nothing like Cannon, or that you are no longer a challenge, but then I would surely be lying my ass off. You are by far the toughest wall I have to break. You have something that belongs to me, that has been destined to be mine ever since I stepped FOOT into this federation. And once I get it and finally defeat you that will be the greatest moment in my entire life. Having FINALLY beat Mason Resoan. My greatest rival and my most hated figure. I am sure it will take more than a chair shot to make that brain of yours have a reboot and see things my way. Sinc now begins to walk around the star filled, glowing, room in great interest. The glowing essence of the room still exploding its lights into a various dance of proportion on his sleek face. Sinc: Let’s go all the way back to my match against James Sullivan for the US title. The man you took out and sent to the depths below. Let’s be real, the guy clearly sucked and he was the immediate version of Vanilla Ice except with a failed record and an ego the size of Mount Everest. Now I shouldn’t boast over someone who sucks so bad he can make a prostitute lose her job but Mason, that is how you made this loser feel. And after you thoroughly ended his career that is what made me hate you from the start. When you go after my targets, my opponents and not let ME be the reaper to them, and take the glory, you just put your name on my sh*t list. Just like Cannon did. Then I lose my match against Rodriguez and you, Mason, in a triple threat for that US title by a referee’s stupid mistake. He STILL needs to get his beady little eyes checked because that match was MINE! Dallas looks at Sinc listening with much intent to pay attention and imagines a powerful image in his words. He looks at Sinc as if he is a true success, and a good idea to take him in under his wing. Sinc continues to explain his mighty words to try and shatter the much heated competition. Sinc: And dare I say it, after that you destroyed me in the ring on MNM due to distractions in my wake that continued to flood into my life as a stretch of failure and interference. Despite the issues with my past wife and rekindling a relationship with an ex girlfriend of mine, I was a fool to think that you should be seen as a lower class superstar. You had the guts, the ambition, and the will to take me on AND take me out that day, and I cannot help but respect that. And I will admit I was truly aggravated about you beating me senseless that night, I wanted to break your neck when I had the chance, but then I changed my mind knowing I was still US champ after the loss. And finally…. Sinc stops and looks to the ground beneath him and see’s the milky way galaxy lit up under the soles of his feet. His eyes are gazing below as if he is a shark looking at a helpless fish swim in a near endless sea. He hesitates and eyes Dallas real quick in worry. Dallas is complacent and smiles at Sinc with a wink signaling for him to continue on. Sinc looks up and places a smooth hand on the wall of the bright space room wiping it over a row of stars as his hand ignites in a starry brightness. He then turns to the camera in a snap of impatience. Sinc: Finally you defeated me at Adrenaline Rush. Set to be one of the greatest Pay Per Views in the Nu MWE. And that…. Sinc clenches a fist as he drives his nails to the end of his balled palm and looks at it with vengeance. He manages to take a sighed breath and calm himself before he over plays his talking role in this rant of a promo. Sinc: That is just unacceptable to me. Your like a melody that continues to ring in my ears and make me bleed from the inside. A nocturnal, never ending, suicidal, melody. So why don’t you change up the tune, get a hammer and start bashing your own head in so I can hear your skull cracking into pieces. Now THAT is a melody I would love to hear. And as I listen I hear the cheers and endure the happiness of my fellow peers. Sinc closes his eyes and raises his arms into the air as if taking in the sound of silence. It appears as though he is picturing himself in a sonata of melodic appreciation in his favor, harnessing the United States Belt around his waist. Nate Rellington is there, and Kendra is there both clapping and cheering him on as this wondrous….eternal….stupendous sonata plays for his favor. He stops his vivid thoughts of imagery and shakes his head in anger. However noticeably as his peers stand behind him in support, Dallas remains still, simply crossing his arms across his chest as his protégé continues. Sinc: And then you try and convince me that you are better than DC, better than Michaels, and better than me. Beating someone over a victory does not make you better, as long as the losers continue to try, try, try again and overcome you with confidence and permanent strife then you are not better Mason. You try to imitate, hallucinate, and congregate with yourself that you are better than Michaels or DC, that you ARE Michaels or DC. Step into the real world and succumb to the vortex of truth and reality you fool, quit living in the magical land of wand wielding four eyed nerds and show us that you can actually be your own man. Do you even know where DC’s little “finger” gesture came from? Greek mythology. It was to signal extreme disliking to the lower class and high end assh*les. And it was also the much needed finger to wield a blade in such a century, I bet you did not know you needed such a finger to grab hold of a sword or blade or any other handled weapon. Without it you would be defenseless, it was known as the dominant and most needed finger in the middle ages. And to carelessly wave it about like it is a toy be careful who you point that at, because YOU Mason may be at the end of it sooner or later. That’s a guarantee. Sinc raises a middle finger not pointing at the camera and looks at it freely pointing at a wall with a bored smirk and shrugs. Sinc: Who even knew that such a small thing can become so great or so powerful of a gesture. Just like me being in the MWE, going from nobody to somebody, now that, that was great. I didn’t try to leech off other’s success like you, I didn’t try to b*tch my way into becoming a big success. Hell I don’t even recall forming any tag teams or being in any worthwhile tag matches. But come MNM, that will all change. The team of Sinc Mercier and Joey Dallas will be one of the greatest stables of all time. Better than Cannon’s little cream filled experiment that was High Society. High Society will crumble to us, and will be nothing but fabled history in our books. We will rewrite the theory of MWE and create a whole new system of entertainment with what we have planned. And yes Mason, I will say it, it will become what Canadian Xchange could never be, a success. Sinc laughs in a quick flash of humor at the thought of such a failed stable and then continues to go on about the ambiguous Aiden Copane. Sinc: Jesus Mason, it seems like your life is FULL of failures. First you had Aiden Copane to deal with, a real big failure, then you had Sullivan to deal with, also a big failure, NOW you have Cannon to deal with, now a failure in my eyes. How many failed partners and ex-friends are you gonna run through before you finally get your head straight? Aiden was the biggest failure of them all. He came in MWE, left, came back, left again. He is a quitter and a non-logical sperm donor to helpless mothers across the world. Injecting his disgusting juices to diseased women then coming to MWE again and spreading it to us, hell he probably created Leon “The Virus” Roberts, who knows! When CX failed, when he failed, he was done and MWE was nothing but a trail of dust in the wind. Maybe Cannon can join him, Sullivan, and Virus and have a baby kickoff. It will be called the Little Tikes Superbowl sponsored by Fisher Price, sounds like a plan to me! Dallas looks at Sinc unhappy with his decision to go back to childish jokes. Sinc senses this after staring Dallas in the eyes and tries his best to flow back into a more serious picture. Sinc: The point is your surrounded by failures Mason. And what can be surrounded by failures help with? Absolutely nothing. You continue to pride yourself on upsets and let downs like these people and think you can still pull out a victorious career, guess again. Me and Dallas will show you that you cannot pull anything out except another wood chip out of your head from being put through a table. And that’s a fact. I want you to show us that you are one of a kind, you beat us both, that’s all fine and dandy, but until you beat us as a team, and beat us without ranting about Michaels and DC and how you wanna be a big boy like them, you aren’t anything too special little man. Just keep yapping and talkin up a storm because at least you still have your confidence, after MNM you will be praying for a miracle. Dallas reverts to folding his arms trying to make himself more comfortable in such a standing manner to listen more on what Sinc has to say. After a few moments Sinc walks to the center of the room, kneels and points to the planet Earth below him and Dallas. Sinc: And to end my little rant Mason. This world you see here. This is our world, we are the New Found Sin of this world, the lie that outdoes all truth’s of power. Because we are the power. We are the law. We are the voices that cannot and will not be heard. And you, you are the one that has not fallen to our feet because of your fogged mind. Who do you wanna be like Mason, huh? You wanna be the next DC, you wanna be the next Michaels, YOU WANNA BE A GOD OR A LORD! Sinc stands firmly and presses his hands against his suit fanning down any noticeable wrinkles. He adjusts the cuffs of his suit coat and snickers. Sinc: You can TRY and be whomever you wish, but you cannot become what you cannot see as threats. Because just when you have your back turned, those FORMER threats in your hopeless mind will come back and nip you in your royal ass. And I will watch you fall and crash into the flames of redemption. And I will laugh while seeing you crawl to our feet in mercy. And when you ask for us to save you…we will whisper…f*ck no. I’m done wasting my breath. Sinc turns his back to the camera and stands next to Dallas placing a hand on his shoulder and gesturing him to say a few words of his own to the people. Dallas nods his head at Sinc and steps forward, a look of arrogance and retribution as if scanned into his facial presence. QUOTE (Tex) Joey Dallas: As you can plainly see, my protégé has already become a better man then either of the men that stand before us as obstacles. Since I have taken him under my wing and shown him a path to true enlightenment, my protégé has evolved from a dimwitted loud mouth, to speaking with a savvy and grace that only someone on the path of righteousness is capable of. Now I know I have thrown that term around a lot over the last few months, evolution, and many of you people do not have the mental capacity to understand exactly what it means. Why I use a term that has refereed to the human races progression through time to explain what my enemies have turned into as I have sat back and watched. Dallas tilts his neck and head upwards, taking a brief look up a the many constellations still circling above him. His cold blue eyes focusing on the first few stars that come into sight. Taking a few deep breaths he slowly pivots his head to look down at the floor, taking in the lush blue and green picture of the globe beneath him. Joey Dallas: However the term is also defined as the gradual process in which something changes into a different and usually more complex or better form. The key word in that definition is of course usually for the evolutions of Ace Cannon and Mason Resoan have not resulted in them becoming better men then when they began on their respective journeys. Instead as these two men have evolved, they have become vile and corrupt. They have seen trials pass them by just like the rest of us, but instead of taking them in stride and continuing onto the next task at hand, these heinous human beings have done everything in their power to go back and change the past. Keeping his feet planted firmly on the ground he turns hips so that his upper body is turned slightly behind him. Raising one of his arms Dallas gestures to the man behind him. His expression unchanging from the look of a coy arrogance that has almost become a trademark of his. He keeps his eyes on the camera for a moment before gazing behind himself, taking in the other person that has made a mark on this scene today. Joey Dallas: If Sinc and myself had lived in the past and tried to change our destinies then we would not be the extraordinary specimens of human beings that stand before you today. Had my protégé attempted change his future by changing his past than he would not have become the biggest threat to the United States that this company has seen since my days as an egotistical child in the first incarnation of Mayhem Wrestling Entertainment. Turing back towards the camera Dallas moves his arms toward his suit, adjusting his tie a little. Making it just slightly looser as he straitens it out. Pulling a misplaced thread away from his jacket he lets his eyes drift back to stare directly into the camera. Joey Dallas: While if I had kept my mind and focus solely on my past and had continued to be the immature, self-absored, narcissist of my earlier days then, I, would not be the enlightened individual that I am today. For if I had not changed, had I not evolved into what I am then where would I be? Once again letting his head drift to the ground beneath him Dallas studies the globe. Locking his eyes on a point of interest he takes a few short and studied paces forward before bending his knees, squatting as he looks up into the camera again. He is atop of a large mass of the green that represents land, moving in closer we can see that the mass of body is shaped in the form of Texas. Using his right hand he extends his index finger, putting it firmly against the ground in the general area of the Dallas area. Joey Dallas: If I had continued to be the Joey Dallas of old, then I would still be sitting aimlessly at home in Dallas. Feeling that my accomplishments were enough for me to be remembered by. That the effort I had put into making this company to make it what it has become today were all futile. Yet, that was just not the case and I moved on and I am currently the greatest asset this company has ever had. Dallas puts his hand flat on the ground and then clenches his fist tightly pushing himself with his feet using it. Standing proudly on his feet he looks back towards the ceiling, spreading his arms apart as he lets his eyes lightly shut. His chest moves up and down slowly as he breaths. The camera angle switches above Dallas, focusing in on him standing still, the globe still slowly rotating beneath him. Joey Dallas: In case it wasn't obvious enough by the surroundings here tonight, this is all just a represent a cold hard fact. While everything, and everyone else revolves around in the universe there is but one focal point that keeps things in balance. In our galaxy it is the star that shines the brightest, here in the galaxy that is the MWE it is no different. Even tho people, our stars if you will, come and pass by, there is but one star that shines above them all. Keeping everything within a perfect equilibrium. Keeping the fine line between superstars and jobbers clearly defined. That star is of course the man that has single handily built this company up to being the most prestigious in the world, and the man that could crush it in his palm just as easily. The camera has now switched back to the front of Dallas, as he slowly drops his head and arms, bringing his left hand to rest his hand flat in front of him. Staring intently at the center of his palm as he talks. Finishing his sentence he once again clenches his fist. His eyes drift back to the camera, as a sly smile crosses his face for the first time tonight. Joey Dallas: This galaxy, all of it's stars and projects revolve solely around me. It is a testimony that rings truer than any other. The MWE films wing of this company, the merchandise sales, and most importantly the trademark show, Monday Night Mayhem are all ruled over by the Salvation. The only thing that remains to be brought into the light is the one thing that has evaded me throughout my career, the Undisputed Championship. And what irks me about it, is that while I have not been given a proper opportunity to achieve that goal. Both of the men that stand against the New Found Sin this week have received an ample amount of fair shots to hold the world in the palm of their hands, and each of them have realized that goal. Yet in the end, they have both fail to bring it to the heights that a title with that name deserves. So let the message that is given this Monday ring clearly through the minds, eyes and ears of every man that sits in the back awaiting their chance to shine, to every man that has passed through their time in the spotlight, and every man, woman and child that support them. Ace Cannon, Mason Resoan, you have both already been used as a prime example as to what I am caplable of achieving when I enter that ring, and both of you will be put to that use once again as you fall victim to what the movement of righteousness that is, the New Found Sin. As Dallas stares intently at the camera with his sly smile, a sly look almost identical to it slides onto Sinc's in the background. As the camera slowly and surely fades to black.
