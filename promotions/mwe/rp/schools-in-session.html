Schools in session.; Pain 101

Sep 19 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The scene opens to a dark and dingy old school hallway. Most of the doors are closed, one just open enough for sound to seep through. The old rusted lockers, broken floor tiles and roof falling apart. The camera moves up to the door thats just open, crumbling ceiling tiles fall infront of the camera. A thin red fog is rolling into the hallway from the sides. Written on the broken glass in blood, "Pain 101". The door is pushed open, the hinges cringe and squeak loudly as it opens. A classroom full of kids with tattoos, ear and various other peircings, all detainees. Aeron standing infront of a old molded blackboard, scrolled down just infront of it a tattered and torn overhead mat, grabs the bottom of the scroll and pulls it just enough to have it move up just pass a large picture with a sticker with a large white question mark placed over something. Aeron picks up a meter-stick and slams it on the front desk, the loud bang echoes throughout the halls. The students who a moment ago were bickering and talking amongst themselves stopped and had sat straight and looked at Aeron. Aeron: Now class, can anyone tell me why your all here? Vandal: Cuz we be cool. Yo anyone got any smokes? Im bled drrry. Emo: Did someone just say bleed? Punk: No, shut up you fuckin emofag. Aeron chuckles before slamming his hands down on the desk. Aeron: All of you, -Raises his voice- SHUT UP. They all give Aeron a blank look before one of the people in the back raises their hand. Goth Girl: Excuse me, sir. We're here because we we broke the law. Aeron: -Sighs lightly- Thank you, atleast someone here has some common sense. Anyway -Aeron clears his throat- Does anyone know who I am? Highschool Wrestler: You Aeron Beowulf, MWE X-TV Champion, who doesnt know you? Some of the kids murmur under their breath. -Aeron shakes his head.- Aeron: Right, now what am I doing here you may ask? Well, I'm cutting a promo. Now Mr.Smartass, who am I facing this week? The retired highschool wrestler, needle pricks in his arms, his lips white, his nose red. Obviously has been on drugs speaks softly. Highschool Wrestler: D.C. Aeron: Correct. D.C. The man from Cali. Anyone here from Cali? Three students raise their hands. Aeron: Ex-fuckin-actly. D.C. Your a good guy, I'll give you that. Tag team Champion, and of course, longest reigning X-Television champion, -He stops for a second before licking his lips- or, was the longest reigning. Cause you see D.C. You may have been able to hold it for 6 weeks, but as you see, I've been able to keep it around my waist fo- The highschool drug addict interrupts him. Highschool Wrestler: 5 weeks in a row. Aeron looks around before picking up a peice of chalk. He looks at it and as he winds his arm up and releases the chalk flies and hits the man in the arm. The man rubs his arm, not saying anything. All the kids have a slight look of concern on their faces. Aeron: Never interrupt me. Now where was I? -He opens a drawer of the desk pulling out a bottle of water, opening it and lets it rush down his throat.- Yes, 5 weeks. And after this week, it'll be six and oh, the next week, seven and oh and on and on. You get me? Aeron slams the desk drawer shut, the desk practically falls apart, the vandal had got up and started to head for the door. Vandal: Fuck this! Im outta here! He scuffles with the door before shuffling by it and running down the hall, the screams reverberating, echoing fading to a silent whisper. Aeron: Now lets take a little look into D.C's past shall we? -Aeron chuckles- Remember that night, you drove down and destroyed Alejendros car? Pissing in the gas tank, banana in the fucking tail pipe? You were arrested in your own mischevous act. You had Michaels bail you out. You tried to get rid of Alejendros' ride yet you barely made it their yourself. -He smirks.- Self stupidity at its finest if you ask me. By know most to all of the students had vanished besides a few students who were either shooting up or asleep. Aeron obviously not caring continues on anyways. Aeron: That sex-tape, I seen it, disgusting dude. I've seen asians in pornos with bigger c**ks than yourself. The tape itself, should be labeled "Da Clown with no balls". Ooh that had to hurt. Anyway -Aeron takes another large gulp of his water before laughing slightly-. Now to move on to those titles that you had won. The light in the room flickers softly, illuminating the room red. Aeron: The X-TV title, you seemed to have nicknamed everyone, one of your, hobbies. Yeah lets call it that, anyway you had beaten Alejendro after completly totaling his car. Than you had to keep it around your waist in a 4 way. I commend you on that, those are hard to compete in, beating loser after loser gets tiring doesnt it? You threw a party, hurray stupid hats and plastic plates. You robbed Sonny to pay for it. Kinda sad, but he does have too much of that. He steps into the ring and the crowd covers their eyes to block the "Bling-Rays". Back to the topic. -Aeron shrugs slightly as he leans back against the chalkboard.- You defended your title against a friend, a teammate. You had him take a dive for you, a DIVE D.C. Even if you were friends, a friendly compitetion is nothing right? Not only that, after he took the dive you MOCKED him for it. He did you a favour and you MOCKED him. Can anyone say "weak"? Than you chased after Mason, just cause he said he took out Rated-X. So if I was to say I slipped your mom the sausage you'd come after me? Its like a dog chasing a ball. You vacated the title, where after MWE had died off and reborn, I held high. Crowning glory two. He sighs, his face red as a beet before finishing up the rest of his water. He put the lid back on before throwing it at one of the asleep students. Drunk: Whuugh? Aeron: Get out of here you dork. The drunk stumbled up out of his seat and out the door. Aeron groans loudly before returning to his incandescent ramblings. Aeron: Enough of the X-Title. Onto the Tag title, the one your so proud of having "The shortest reign". You partnered up with someone who had gave you the old Canadian Beatdown with a hockey stick. Your partner already held hold so you got the short end of the hockey stick and got mauled. There isnt much to talk about this title than. Aeron looks confused, resting his head in one of his hands. Aeron: The match, yes thats what I had forgotten to talk about, the wonderful match. D.C. You've seen why I'm still champ. Im a beast, a monster, a bulldozer in a childs playground. Nothing can stop me, I havent been stopped and Im not stopping now. My gas tank is full and its running on redbull. Watch your back, and tell Lindsey that her BJ's have gotten sloppy ever since that kick to the face. He laughs and stops abruptly before he starts to think, noticing that all of the class had gone, all but rats and the creak of the door and the crumbling ceiling to keep him company. He decides to get out of there for the time being and walks to the door. He turns off the flickering light before slamming the door shut, the question mark sticker had slowly peeled off, the red fog surrounded it. Written behind it was "6-0". Aeron is seen walking into the distance as the school slowly starts to fade out of existance. Aerons voice is heard. Aeron: D.C. You just got schooled. -FADE- ((One of my not-so-good RPs but I cant squeeze anything out :/ I think its safe to say unless D.C. No shows or I get my act straight in my second RP my reign has come to an end))
