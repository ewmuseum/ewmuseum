Step to the Plate and Face the Ace

Aug 30 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
We fade backstage, where a nervous man stands shaking, outside a locker room. The plate on the door reads: KANTO. He takes a deep breath, and turns the door handle. The door slowly swings open with a loud creak. The room, of course, is pitch black. Nothing inside is visible. Ummm... Kanto? Enter the room, and close the door! But... I can't see a thing... DO AS I ASK, OR SUFFER A FEIRCE RATH! The man trembles into the room, shutting the door behind him. Ah, very good. Welcome to my mist. A silence falls over the room. Now, who dare disturbs me? Oh, I'm ever-so sorry. I'm a local interviewer, Luke Falks, filling in for Shawn Spitzer, and I've been sent here to ask the tough questions.... Do you mind? A short silence is interrupted by a loud rustle, followed by the sound of a wooden chair falling, a few quick-paced footsteps, then a deep inhale from the mysterious yet deadly Kanto. Beware the Jabberwock, my son! The jaws that bite, the claws that catch! Beware the Jubjub bird, and shun! The frumious Bandersnatch!! Ex-... Excuse me? Haha! Let me ask you a question boy. Have you come searching for trouble? Oh... No... No way. Heh.... Then you've come to the wrong place. Another long silence creeps over the room. Is this... a bad time? It's always a bad time Luke... You may ask away. Luke Falks is heard clearing his throat, as he is no doubt scared and confused. Alright, well dude, to star- STOP! How DARE you address me by 'dude'! Do not belittle me with your slang! I expect 'Kanto', 'master', or 'sir'! My apologies sir. Let me start by asking, how do you feel about your big win last week? How do I... feel? Nothing in that match made me 'feel' anything. And why should it? I told the world I was going to win, and I did just that. I had no worries or doubts, and I was proven to be a force. But, aren't you happy in the slightest? A loud grunt echoes throughout the room. I am not familiar with that emotion. For you see, my mind is in a constant power outage. ...Would you mine explaining? For who? The ignorant? Such as yourself?.... Fine. Well, for those who have ever been through a strong storm, sometimes the power cuts off. Oh so many appliances that you desire, and rendered useless. And the clocks, they reset. Think of my mind as those clocks... Constantly flashing twelve... For I always live in midnight. So here in the darkness I dwell. Not because I'm a coward, but because I'm a loner. My surroundings are for me, and me only. Invasion of that privacy is trespassing, and will result in pain. ...Another awkward silence... Some say silence is golden? Well, they obviously never met Kanto. Luke is again heard clearing his throat. O-... ok then.... Well, umm, how about your match this week? Are you excited? I suppose you can say that. Have any comments for your opponents? Tap, tap, tap. The constant sound of fingernails tapping in a rhythmic pattern is heard clear as a bell. Let me first send a message to Sinc Mercier. The tapping ceases, as another grunt roars out of Kento's chest. Your life, is something I just cannot understand. It is as if you do not take life seriously. Your obnoxious joking, and arrogant attitude is simply confusing to me. I do not find you comical, whatsoever. You have the sayings of a pre-pubescent teen. It's time to grow up Sinc. This is not a playground. You no longer goofing off on the jungle gym, or swinging with your friends.... Your playing with Kanto. And playing with me, is like playing with fire. Expect to be burned. You're undefeated? Not anymore. Tap, tap, tap. The tapping has returned. Very well put du-... Kanto. How about Alejandro? Tap, tap, tap, tap. That... That is a man I respect. Why you ask? Well, I have many reasons. For one, he has the fighting style of a crowd favorite, yet he has a mine of his own. Also, he as well finds it necessary to cover his face. He, no doubt, has something to hide, much like myself. But most importantly, he gained my respect last week. It's what he did to Brian Michaels. The blood gushing from Brian's head, was caused by Rodriguez's actions. I loved that blood. And on my first night, the first match I saw, I had the honor to witnessed it. It made me feel comfortable, relaxed, accepted, and gave the rush I needed for in my later match. Thank you Alejandro Rodriguez. Kanto begins to laugh under his breath. So... You and Alejandro have an alliance? Fuck no, not at all. You are foolish to make such accusations. Respect never comes before punishment. And despite me liking the little guy, he stands no chance against my powers. He likes opening his mouth? This week, I'll attempt to swell it shut. Tap, tap, tap, tap, tap. The tapping has quickened. Thank you very much Kanto. I really appreciate the time. Oh? But we're not finished... Tap, tap, tap, tap... ..... Silence. I... I have no more questions... Yet, I have a few for you my boy. The door is heard shaking furiously. HEY! UNLOCK THIS DOOR! Luke pounds on the door with force. HELP!! SOMEONE HELP!! Scream all you want... Noone is coming... What do you want with me?! Let me ask you a few questions, then I'll set you free. Inhale, tap, tap, exhale, tap, tap. First question. A flashlight flickers on, as the light shines into Kanto's eyes. When you were twelve, you played on a baseball team called "The Hammer Heads". Is that correct? What?! How?! How did yo- Do not blunder! Respond! ...Yes... On that team, you used a bat, much like this one, right? The light reflects off a bright blue aluminum bat. Ye...Yeah.... The light cuts off, as the room again falls dark. Remind me again... How many strikes until you receive an out? Listen, Kanto, I'll give you anything, jus- AWSNER ME! ...Three... Three strikes... .......... .......... Luke, you're up to bat. WHAT?! NO! I DON'T WANT TO! Oh, but you must. I thought you said I could leave after you ask me the questions!?! Let this be a lesson to you. Never trust a stranger. Luke is heard crying aloud, as he fears for his life. Kanto please! Man, I'll do anything! I just want to go home, and see my family! ...So do I Luke... So do I... CRACK! Strike one! AHHHHHHHH! PLEASE NO, DON'T DO T- CRACK! Strike two! ....aw... ugg.......Pl......Ple- CRACK! Strike three! ................................. You're out. *Fade to the sound of a body being dragged.*
