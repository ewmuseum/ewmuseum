Hello David Whitman...

Feb 19 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
OOC: The point of tis RP is too show the 2 faces of Cannon and the second part also plays into something Shanti is doing. ** The screen is black, pitch black for that matter. Nothing can be seen, but the sound of a door shutting can be heard and it echoes through out the dark room. Now we hear footsteps and one by one they get closer and closer until they finally stop. Once they stop we hear nothing for a good two or three second and then the dark dissolves just like that…. A light, a single light aluminates the room and under that light we see Ace Cannon sitting in a metal folding chair. As Cannon sits there his face and shirt are covered in what appears to be dried up blood. He sits there in the chair with a distant look in his eyes and a maniacal grin sits upon his face. All of a sudden Cannon a chilling laugh begins to bellow from Cannon, but as soon as he begins laughing it stops.** Cannon: Here I am, five days removed from the havoc I created on Friday Night Overdrive. A night in which I, Ace Cannon made the world notice just how much of a sick fuck I am. ** Cannon stops and a slightly terrifying grin crosses his face as he re-collects the events that took place on Friday.** Cannon: I started my night by kicking that no good piece of shit Maxx in the head! That has been a long time coming. As a matter of fact, that has been something I have thought about doing since our days in EW when an undeserving Maxx was booked into being the first undisputed champion of EW. Ever since that day he won the title I have hated Maxx with every fiber in my being and when my foot connected with his head I felt a form of gratification I have never felt before. Hell, I have kicked a lot of worthless pieces of shit in the head but when I kicked Maxx it was truly better then any sex I have ever had. ** Cannon stops and gives a little wink to the camera.** Cannon: So Maxx, I’m glad to see that your gone … I also heard your trying to get into the MWE Hall Of Fame, and quite frankly I don’t blame you. I wouldn’t want to go out the way you did with me kicking in your skull. But sometimes your dealt a bad hand in life and you got one… It is truly a shame… Well, not really. Your glory came when you won the EW title and you should hold on to that because you don’t deserve anything else and that’s including the hall of fame. ** Cannon waves “bye” to the camera as he chuckles to himself at the fate of Maxx.** Cannon: Now, onto why I am sitting here covered in blood five days after my attack on Joey Dallas. Well, it is a simple answer, and was stated the beginning of this promo… I’m a sick fuck and there is no way around it. ** Cannon takes his right index finger and puts it to his tongue. He takes his wet finger and runs it down his cheek where dried up blood is caked on. He then takes his finger and puts it back on his tongue so he can taste the blood. He then lets out a sinister type laugh.** Cannon: A lot of people are saying what I did was too barbaric and over the top… Well, I don’t care. I mean look at this face… ** Cannon stops and pints to his face.** Cannon: … That is my “I don’t give a shit face.” I mean, if I gave a shit I wouldn’t have been in trouble with the law and wouldn’t have spent time in rehab. A lot of people think Joey Dallas drove me to this, but he didn’t. I brought myself to this place and I enjoy it more then anyone can imagine. I mean the signs where always there that I was off my rocker. I mean, I did punt a fucking child so obviously this wrinkle has always been in my personality and I enjoy exhibiting and making people fear for there well being. It excites me and is what now drives me in the wrestling ring and in life. ** Cannon looks at the camera and really shows no emotion before he begins talking again.** Cannon: Speaking of fear this brings me to my opponent on MNM.. He is a five time MWE World Champion, MWE Hall Of Famer, he gets the money to produce promos like his name is George Lucas and he has more men hanging from his man bag then one could humanly imagine…. I am speaking of none other then David Whitman. ** Cannon reaches down and he is no longer bathed in light. He soon sits back up and he has a flask in his hand. He unscrews the lid and takes a long hard drink of whatever is in there. He screws the lid back on and sets it back down before continuing.** Cannon: I know I am not the most decorated guy you have ever faced here Davey. I know that I am not necessarily the most talented either, but I know one thing and it’s that your scared of me. You want to know how I came to this conclusion that MWE’S golden boy fears me? ** Cannon stops so the question can hang in the air and build anticipation.** Cannon: At Friday Night Overdrive I looked into your eyes and saw the fear as I assaulted Joey Dallas. You didn’t do anything to stop me, you stood there paralyzed by your fear and not sure what to do and at that very moment the holy grail of MWE got a chink in his armor. ** Cannon cracks a slight grin and rubs his chin before he continues.** Cannon: I know a lot of you out there must be asking yourself, “Why isn’t Ace pissed he’s not getting a title shot?” Well, it is a simple answer. Gold no longer drives Ace Cannon. Gold doesn’t make you a good wrestler, and when your no longer the hunter but rather the hunted you become weak and I can also see that weakness in you Whitman… It is another chink in your golden boy armor. ** Cannon stops and reaches in his pocket and withdraws a pack of cigarettes and a lighter. Cannon pulls a cigarette out puts it between his lips and lights it. He takes a few pull before continuing.** Cannon: A lot of you are asking what weakness is this crazy bastard talking about? Well, that weakness is stupidity. You see, Whitman has taken a match against the craziest man in MWE. He didn’t have to take it, he is MWE’S meal ticket and he could have politely declined and told my dad he didn’t want this, but he didn’t. Sure, maybe he is brave for stepping in the ring with me, but it is stupid for a man of his caliber to even risk his career by getting into a ring with a loose canon like myself and if I get the chance I will try and kick Whitman in the head… ** Cannon stops and takes a few more pulls for his cigarette. He blows the smoke upward toward the light and the smoke hangs in the air.** Cannon: So Whitman, I hope your ready for are encounter… It will be MWE’S golden boy against MWE’s black sheep. It will be a dream match-up sorts for the fans… But for me it is a chance to prove myself to the world and I will do that. I will show the world that win or loss I’m nothing to be taken lightly because from here on out in MWE I will be piling the bodies to the sky… AND NO ONE CAN STOP ME! ** Cannon stands from his chair takes one last drag from his cigarette. He drops it on the ground and looks at the camera.** Cannon: Say your prayers David… You will need Jesus to be your homeboy! ** Cannon grins at the camera.** Cannon: One more thing… OH SNAPPAH! ** Cannon reaches above his head and turns out the light. We are back to complete darkness. We hear the footsteps of Ace Cannon and then we hear the door shut.** PART 2 ** The shot opens inside of a private jet. Cannon is now all cleaned up and looking over a stack of paper while he sips on some sort of cocktail. The camera pans over to Paige and she is looking at a HUSTLER magazine. She examines every picture with a HUGE grin on her face. The camera pans over once more and we see Lindsey sitting there in an Ace Cannon E.O.P hoodie as she reads over a COSMO.Paige closes her HUSTLER and lets out a sigh and looks over at Cannon** Paige: Ace, where are you taking us? ** Cannon sets his papers down and takes a drink from his cocktail and looks at Paige before he answers.** Cannon: I am taking you to paradise. ** Lindsey then sets down he COSMO and looks at them.** Lindsey: Is this for Paige’s birthday? Cannon: It sure is. **Paige grabs Cannons hand and looks at him with a childish grin on her face.** Paige: Can you tell me why were going down here? I will make you a happy man… ** Cannon looks at Paige and shakes his head.** Lindsey: Please tell her, she has been annoying me like I knew. ** Paige pulls he hand away from Cannon’s and moves it to his thigh and begins rubbing his leg. She then leans over and whispers something in his ear. He smiles and looks at her. He then takes a drink of his cocktail and look at Paige.** Cannon: Fine, I will tell you, but you must deliver on what you just promised me. Paige: Have I ever let you down? ** Lindsey looks at the two of them with a quizzical look on her face.** Cannon: Alright, if you must know I bought you a huge mansion in the Caribbean. ** Paige gets a huge smile on her face and gets up from her seat between Cannon and Lindsey. She gives Cannon a kiss on his cheek and whispers something once more in his ear and then walks up the aisle. Lindsey then looks over at Cannon.** Lindsey: Ace, you may do some of the most fucked up things I have ever seen but your really a sweet guy. Cannon: Thanks… But this can’t be getting out, it will ruin my image. ** Both Lindsey and Cannon laugh.** Cannon: By the way that is a nice hoodie your wearing. **Lindsey looks down and blushes a bit and giggles a little. ** Lindsey: Thank you… So you have a big match with Whitman coming up at MNM… Are you scared? ** Cannon looks at Lindsey and takes in her sheer beauty before he answers.** Cannon: Not really… Whitman doesn’t impress me too much. I think he has gotten a lot of what he has because he is the golden boy of MWE. He isn’t controversial, he doesn’t kick children, and he is a wholesome guy who MWE can market and for that reason he stays on top. Lindsey: I can kind of agree with all that. I mean when I was with Whitman in that stupid Life Swap I thought he was a nice guy but other then that he wasn’t much. Cannon: Yea, well he won’t be all wholesome and all that crap when I am done with him… When Ace Cannon has something to prove there is not letting up and if I get the chance I will hurt David Whitman… **Lindsey just looks at Cannon for a few seconds before responding.** Lindsey: Can I ask you something Ace? ** Cannon nods at her to continue.** Lindsey: I mean, I knew what DC was all about. But, all the times I have been with you in different companies I have always thought of you as a complicated person and when everyone thought they knew what made you tick you changed it up on them… So, I guess I am trying to figure out what drives you? ** Cannon just looks at Lindsey and then he finishes his cocktail and sets it down.** Cannon: Causing pain is what drives me Lindsey… **Cannon just looks at Lindsey as she begins to look a bit intimidated and unsure of this man in front of her.** Cannon: You see, people used to think it was money and gold that drove me and that was just a front. I have always taken great pleasure in hurting other people and I always held something back and one day a switch was flipped.. I can’t tell you what or who did it but nothing else no longer mattered to me except inflicting pain on people, and since that switch was flipped I haven’t let up and I don’t plan too. ** Lindsey just looks at Cannon with a blank expression.** Lindsey: That is scary… Cannon: Well, you don’t have to be scared. I would never hurt someone as beautiful as you… And I mean that. ** Lindsey smiles at Cannon and reaches out and puts her hand on top of his. She then realizes what she just did and quickly removes it.** Lindsey: Well thank you Ace Cannon and good luck against David Whitman.. Cannon: Thanks… And that pain I talked about inflicting will happen to David Whitman and you might not want to watch Lindsey.. It is going to be violent and something you may not want to see. Lindsey: I will take that into consideration… Isn’t Paige waiting for you in the back of the plane? Cannon: Damn, she is… If you want to comeback there with me and… ** Lindsey looks at Ace and smiles at him with a school girl type grin.** Lindsey: Nah, I am good. Thanks for inviting me though.. **Cannon stands up and looks at Lindsey.** Cannon: Your going to be missing out on a good time… Lindsey: I sure I am.. Have fun. Cannon: Oh, I will. **Cannon walks past Lindsey and the camera follows him to the back of the plane where he reaches the lavatory in the back.** Cannon: Whitman, this will be the last time I will say anything till our match at MNM… So just remember no matter how sweet I am on Lindsey or Paige don’t think it means you have been granted a reprieve… I still plan to pile the bodies to the sky, but it is time to collect on what Paige has for me. ** Cannon enters the lavatory and the camera fades to black.**
