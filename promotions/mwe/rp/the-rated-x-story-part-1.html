the Rated-X Story part 1

Sep 28 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
OOC: This leads into D.C.'s promo. The MWE camera opens up to show a building which looks to be MWE’s Headquarters in Chicago, Illinois. There is a vicious wind outside, as the camera slowly goes inside the office, showing an elevator door which slowly opens. It appears to be Brian Michaels, dressed slick in his white dress shirt and slacks. He cracks his neck and pops his collar before stepping out of the elevator and walking down the long hallway on the top floor of MWE HQ’s. Michaels reaches into his pocket and takes out a pair of Gucci and Gabana shades and puts them on. He continues walking down the hallway. He stops midway to look at a pretty executive woman in high heels and a business skirt. Michaels: You seen Covell? The woman nods and tilts her head to the left. Business Woman: He's in his office. Michaels nods back and walks toward the left. You cannot see his eyes but his facial expressions show that he is in an angry mood. He sees a large door and knocks violently. It is seen that the door leads to the office of his father and MWE Chairman Richard Covell. Covell comes to the door and opens up. Michaels: What the FUCK are you doing? Putting me in a match with D.C.? Are you out of your friggin’ mind? Have you not been paying attention for the past month? Covell is wearing a leisurely suit with slacks as well and puts his hand up to refrain Michaels from bursting in front of his employees. He gestures to Michaels to come in, and closes the door behind him. Covell; Now who are you to barge in on MY office and throw a bitch fit? Son, sit down for a minute, I need to talk to you about finances. Michaels rolls his eyes and throws himself down on a chair, which is directly in front of his fathers’ desk, which is loaded with papers. Covell moves the papers out of the way, clearing space for an armrest. He crosses his arms and looks at Michaels with a curious face. Covell: Mayhem Wrestling Entertainment…is a business. We make business decisions, which means we are constantly looking for the best matches possible. Rated-X…is a dominant, and nostalgic group that is GUAREN-DAMN-TEED…to draw money and ratings. You pit that…against the three members of Project Revolution? How much blockbuster can you possibly get? Michaels looks at Covell with the damnedest look and shakes his head in disapproval. Michaels: You mean to tell me that I’m tagging up in a match against the son of a bitch who ran me over…because you think people LIKE that shit? Covell nods with a grin on his face. Michaels: Wait…where were you when D.C. ran me over? Covell: Well, erm, I was…I was in the process of getting a lawyer for your accident… Michaels’ head tilts as Michaels suddenly takes on a sarcastic tone. Michaels: Really, dad….I remember when I was in the hospital…and I didn’t know if I would ever walk again…and you never showed up. You weren’t like the other dads, you weren’t there for me….dad. Covell rolls his eyes at Michaels. Covell: Listen kid, you’re lucky to have a roof over your head! You couldn’t walk? Tough luck, get a wheelchair. This is the problem with whiny bitches like you…you can never shut your damn mouths. Stop being a primadonna! You’ve been a Champion and that’s not good enough for you? Michaels: Don’t turn this on me…my bad. I’m just pissed off that I have to face off with D.C. It’s like, a part of me wants to team up and bring back Rated-X, but the other half wants to kick him and his bitch in the face…can we coexist? Covell looks on and gives a grin. He rises from his desk and chuckles. Covell: Look, why don’t we talk about this over lunch? I know this restaurant that just opened up down the street and it looks pretty good. Michaels shrugs as he rises and they walk out of the office together. They share a little banter as they walk over to the elevator and wait for the door to open. Michaels: So, why does everyone say let’s do lunch? Do they literally…do lunch? Can you even DO lunch? Covell: Hey, David Whitman, why don’t you stop talking so fast. I’ve been in a crapload of meetings and I don’t need your questioning right now. By the way, I don’t know if you forgot in your insensible fit of rage, but did you know you have a match this week? Michaels again rolls his eyes as the elevator door opens. They see a rather old man standing to the side, minding his own business in a trench coat and hat. He keeps to himself and stands to the side, reading a Chicago Tribune newspaper. Covell walks near him and begins to smell something funny. His facial expressions show a look of horror. Covell: HEY! The man pretends to not notice Covell as Covell grabs the hat and takes it off. Covell: HEY! YOU SMELL LIKE SHIT! Get the fuck off my elevator, you’re stinking it up. The man looks away, noticeably embarrassed. The elevator door opens as Covell and Michaels awkwardly step out. Michaels: About my match, how do I describe my opponents? Let’s start with Jason Fear. Jason Fear…you are the Kim Kardarshian of MWE. Kim has all that ass and can’t shake it…and you have all the potential, but you just haven’t stepped up yet. You’re a tough opponent, I can’t lie. You’re on a winning streak and with your buds behind you, confident? Mancini…you gave Whitman a helluva fight, and if anybody can do that…has my respect. I’ve gotta bust my ass off to beat you this week. American Animal? Truthfully, the guy is a monster. He’s a physical specimen and if I’m not careful, the dude can tear me up…gotta watch my back. Michaels runs his hands through his hair as the father-son duo walk into the MWE Headquarters Parking Lot. They see a black lamborghini, seemingly a 2008 version. They walk into the car as Michaels hops into the driver’s seat. He turn on the car, as they begin to drive to the restaurant. Michaels: I don’t know dad, I can’t rely on experience here. I’ve never been in an handicap match before, and honestly, I can’t trust D.C. The bastard might just sneak up on me at any given minute…I’m on my own in this match. Covell; Well, if you wanna be the man, you gotta pay a price. Michaels: Yeah, you’ve told me time and time again. The lamborghini pauses and arrives at their destination. They hop out and score the restaurant for open seats. They find a few and sit down. They begin to converse when all of a sudden… -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- OOC: Yes, I know, a 2.5 star-ish promo. Not a lot of effort went into this one boys and girls. A hectic week and pure laziness can be contributed to this. Sorry to my opponents and D.C. for not giving yall a quality effort.
