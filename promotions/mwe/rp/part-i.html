OOC - As per suggestion, a few things to keep in mind: Purple=Warner Dark Blue=Ivory Italics=Thoughts ------------------------------------------------------------------- Part I Day Unknown, Fall 2006 With the intention of chilling every aspect of a human being, a wind swept across the land, an echoing wail its voice. Murderous intention on its behalf threw both sand and debris into the face and eyes of a lone man sitting on the beach despite the bitterly cold weather. He looked into the ocean with cold, distant eyes, the shade of blue mirroring the ocean, searching for the answers to the questions he wasn’t all to sure of. With a defeated sigh, he gazed over to a shallow dent in the sand, the mould of a body partially obscured. One last look at the body before him carried him onto his strangely weary feet. Although he had no knowledge of his destination, legs of iron and steel carried him there. The rhythmic pounding of rubber souls onto wooden stairs rung out as he made his way slowly towards the twilight stars that covered not only the horizon, but everything else as well. Each agonizing step brought a new building top into the sky, roofs piercing the heavens as if the city itself had slain the sky. Iris burning lights, sounds that could make ears bleed and smells that would make even Alex Bowen himself cry flooded the man’s senses, his mind racing into overdrive to try and make sense of it all. He clutched his forehead and stumbled forward quite unpoetically as images as familiar as those around him flashed and exploded in his head with no rhythm and with even less reason. Despite being swamped by the unknown surrounding him, the man felt bizarrely and confusingly calm in amongst the question marks. Soon, he discovered the location he didn’t know he was after. It was a club operating under the banner of The Lost, at least that‘s what the flashing dark pink and red sign said. From the outside, one could quite comfortably assume it was an unspectacular place. The grey concrete walls were grimy and almost brown with dirt, vomit stains the only things breaking up the monotonous color scheme. But, any negative connotations were blown away by the simple act of people walking in, for whenever the door swung open, even in the slightest, any and all standing outside were assaulted by a wall of music, people and interior design at its peak. A short wait in line brought him to the door, and closer to whatever goal existed inside. He went to enter, but a hand across the chest stopped him. Looking up slightly brought a tall and wide man into view, a face of boredom and mildly hidden contempt painted onto his head. “Got ID?” He simply looked at the bouncer, quiet confusion in his eyes. “You know, the shit you keep in your wallet?” Laced with sarcasm, the bouncer mocked the unmoving man. “Wall-et. In. Your. Back. Pock-ettt.” An over the top reach into the back of his own slacks came with the bouncer’s taunt as a few others chuckled along with the little entertainment before them. The man ignored the laughs and reached around to find, to some surprise, a black leather wallet. He looked at it with careful suspicion before handing it to the bouncer. A well practiced flick of the wrist had the item open and the closest form of identification into his hands and in his eye-line for several scrutinizing seconds. “Zak Warner, eh? You’re good to go in.” With a soft throw, the bouncer put the wallet back into the possession of Zak, signaling his entrance into The Lost. The melodies of a live band tore Zak’s ears to pieces without remorse. His tired eyes and tattered mind struggled to absorb his new surroundings. Retro furniture, along with a distinct and developed old-school vibe, that contradicted the modern rock emanating from the stage, flooded Zak’s irises as he followed slow and meticulous steps to anywhere that wasn’t where he was standing. Once more, he followed the feet that seemed to know more about the world then he did. With the flashings of a strobe light a substitute for natural light, Zak cut his way through a crowd with the tenderness of a blunt axe, but found his way to the bar. He watched the barman serve the customers that were bordering on rampaging with the type of grace, hospitality and ability that only come after a lifetime of experience. He eventually came to Zak, sweat teetering on his brow. “What can I get ya?” Staring was Zak’s response. “Well mate? What will it be? ‘Aven’t got all night.” Silence fell from Zak’s partially open mouth, the quiet obviously irritating the barman. “Alright then fuck you mate, piss off from the bar.” “ZAK!!” A feminine scream from behind, an arm around the shoulder of Zak quickly following. “Um, he’ll have what I’m having, and that’s a Caribou Lou. Thanks love.” A small smile from the barman chased her words. “Coming right up.” He quickly went to his work, while the woman followed her arm and made her way around to Zak’s front, looking him in the eyes with a bewildering passion. “Long time no see stranger, how ya been?” Comprehension ran in Zak’s mind, he quickly found he had no response. “Really? Me too. I guess you’ve been busy, could explain why you’ve ignored my calls…” Two drinks appeared beside them, a twenty dollar bill falling between them. “Keep the change babe.” In two swift motions, the money was swept up and the glasses were airborne, soft hands guiding them to their destinations. She sipped hers while Zak watched, quickly doing the same. They looked at each other, he with curiosity, and she with tenderness that struggled to hide an overwhelming desperation. “Look, Zak, I don’t blame ya for not wanting to talk to me, fuck, I know I deserve it. But please, I need to talk to ya. Need to get a lot of shit of my chest, and for once it’s not my top… You know my number, please please call me. Soon.” With that, she sauntered away, her hips shaking almost in time with the music, a few men pausing their lives to watch her denim. Zak, however, had his eyes locked onto her hair, his mind racing, bewilderment fuelling it. She found the group of friends she was with, the circle they had created defending her from anguish and pain, at least she liked to think so. For a moment, she lowered her guard and looked back to Zak, hoping for any trace of emotion to pass through his eyes or his lips. Instead, she saw him sip his drink and begin to walk, and soon, she watched him close the door to The Lost between her and him. Outside, Zak looked to the sky, the number of his questions rivaling the stars in the sky. Present Day Fade up to a cloudy night sky, and the MWE IC Champion. A half smirk comes across Zak's face before going back into the depths of his mind again. The cracks are almost looking back at him, mocking him in such a manner that he brings his head up as he tilts back to look at the clouds. There are a few spots where the stars manage to peak through as he keeps his gaze upon them. Sticks and stones may break my bones, but words will never hurt me… When will MWE Management can never learn to pull their heads out of their asses can they? I’ve heard over and over again from the guys in the back how great MWE is. Yet they can’t fathom why WCF, EPW, and even a recently dead GWC are more talked about on the independent circuit. I’ll tell you fucking why. Booking. Are you listening Windy? Did my catchy monologue attract your attention? Pay close attention because here is where it starts to get good. Just because you place your biggest draws in the main event, doesn’t mean you’re going to have a five star match. It takes two to tango. You have one of the hottest superstars out there right now coming off a huge win for the Intercontinental Championship and what do you staffers decide to do? Book me against some perpetual mid card wrestler? Where is the excitement? Where is the challenge? Paige is still raising hell, Mason Resoan is showing why people see him as a future champion, and MWE is on the rise once again. But you need more than the superstars. You need the match-ups. Zak Warner versus Windy Bridges, maybe they’ll blindfold me to keep it interesting. A slight chuckle escapes his lips as his eyes close for a moment. His voice remains steady, never wavering or faltering. Instead you give me one of MWE’s finest or so they say. Funny no one gives a shit about what you did in the old MWE or your worthless title reigns back then. Just because you have held a MWE Championship, doesn’t mean you ARE a MWE Champion. You have to prove yourself night in and night out. A superstar isn’t defined by the belt; the belt is defined by the superstar… D.C. is the most talked about MWE wrestler for a reason… Remind the audience at home who you defeated back then for your piece of leather, your so-called “championship,” Windy. I would use your first name if I knew what it was, but that would involve me caring and I sure don’t give a fuck. Aliases aren’t “cool” Windy; it just shows your cowardice. You beat Grand Mystique, yet the most amusing part is that it happened under the AEW name. So therefore, have you even technically won a MWE Singles Title? And for all of you that couldn’t afford the gas money to drive 2000 miles to a little worn-down school in California, allow me to illustrate how it most likely went. Windy told GM that his face paint looked sexy, GM offered to blow Windy after the match, and they lived happily ever ever. He sits up finally, a fire burning in his eyes. You are a disgrace to the US title Windy. Great stars have held that belt under the MWE name, yet you claim a win in another fed as being on the same level. How can you even think that you are anywhere near as great as Aidan Copane? As Sonny Williams, a two time US champ. I believe the transitive property applies here. Zak Warner > Sonny Williams. Sonny Williams> Windy Bridges. Therefore, Zak Warner > Windy Bridges. I hope you don’t mind me calling you out on this, but it is very important. I do have one question though. How can someone be in what was it… Five different companies, and never make it out of the midcard scene? You’d have to be real good at sucking to at least not make it up to the IC title division. But I suppose I was always a talented man. Warner gets to his feet, pacing back and forth. A cool breeze flutters through the night, causing leaves to fall around him. Hard work and sweat of brow are substitutes for none. Within a month of debuting I was already being hailed as a future MWE World Champion. The women were at my left and money on my right, but I gently pushed them away. The only thing that mattered was me holding the gold at the end of the day. As you can see I am well on my way and it is only a matter of time. I started out in the same place you are stuck, but now I’m the main eventer and I’m the guy calling the shots. Maybe when you’re picking yourself up off the mat on Monday you can write a little haiku about the great Zak Warner and how well talked about he will be one day. But back to the point Windy. A change of scenery doesn’t actually change anything. No one forgot your past. You’re still the same man you were last month. Your girlfriend still doesn’t want anything to do with you after seeing you with your pants down. Your attempt to get to the next level has only brought further discredit to your name. Hey, at least your reputation can only sink so low, right? You have a change to change all that this Monday though by stepping into the ring with the fastest rising star of MWE You keep telling that to yourself because words will all you have left after our match on Mayhem. A champion worth his weight in gold versus a Canadian redneck who can’t even hold a title for more than a week? That’s like LeBron James matching up against Carlos Delfino; it’s like having Curtis Mcelhinney try and stop an Alexander Ovechkin slapshot… it’s not even a contest. And don’t even begin with all that stepping up bullshit because you are in the match across from an icon. You don’t become great overnight boy. Unless you’re gifted with the total package like myself… Too bad you’re not. Do you realize that I am still undefeated here in MWE? Do you realize that in my third ever match, I beat a surefire Hall of Famer, and basically ran him out of the company he helped build? Even on his worst day Sonny Williams could take most of the MWE roster with his eyes closed. Yet I beat him in the middle of the ring. At least I am still standing here to talk about it. Talk is cheap Windy; anyone can say bold things hiding in their locker room. It’s what you say in the ring that matters… Enjoy the main event scene Windy, your stay will be a short one. Fade to fucking annihilation.
