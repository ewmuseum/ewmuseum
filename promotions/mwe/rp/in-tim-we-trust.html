In Tim We Trust; Vanguard RP II

May 5 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"Don't give me that bullshit Dick!" Dressed in a loose fitting t-shirt and a tight pair of jeans we open our scene to a furious Paige. Bandage wrapped tightly around her head she has slammed her hands down on top of the clutter of Dick Malibu's desk as he sits calmly on the other side in his usual attire puffing away on a Cuban cigar. Dick Malibu: "Look, we've been over this a hundred times baby face .. I'm the General Manager and as the GM I gotta do what makes money .. And the people wanna see Dallas- " Paige: "It's Sharpman." Taking his cigar in his right hand Malibu takes a puff off of it, sending a smoke ring into the air as he blows it out. His body language is enough to tell that he's not going to be persuaded from whatever it is he's decided on. Dick Malibu: "Dallas. Sharpman. Whatever Ol' Joey wants to call himself now, the point is that the people wanna see him EARN a title shot. So honestly doll, I could care less if he's your ... What did you call it?" Malibu raises his free hand and does the cheesy quote motions in the air as he looks Paige square in the eye. Dick Malibu: "'Hand picked replacement.' Because if I don't give the fans what they want, then I'm gonna lose my job .. Got that sweetheart?" As this conversation goes on our attention is turned towards the far corner of the screen as Eli Silver, in his usual business attire has a cell phone pressed against his ear. He is caught up in the familiar "pick up your damn phone" pace as he waits for whoever is on the other end to answer. Meanwhile Paige is still infuriated, and looks as though she is about to pounce across Malibu's desk and tear his heart right out of his chest. Paige: "First of all, I am NOT your fucking sweetheart and second of all do you really think I care about what the fans want!? It's my title I should be able to pick anyone I want to represent me in my defense!" Malibu can only laugh at this aggressive side of Paige, after all not only is she going to be out of a job by the time this day is over but she's a breath of fresh air compared to his usual meetings ever since getting the position as General Manager. Dick Malibu: "Your a precious little thing .. You really are. Look if I cared about making my former talent happy then I'd be handing your title over to Sonny Williams right about now. But that's not how this business works, as you lose the dead weight you gotta please the new talent or they'll walk out on you. If Joey wants the intercontinental title so bad he can earn a shot at it just like the rest of my roster is going to start doing .. And if you have a problem with that, I really don't care." A sharp movement from Eli catches our attention again as he snaps up quickly, cellphone still pressed hopefully to his ear as he manages to blurt out one word, and only one word. Eli Silver: "Ace!" -------------------------------------------------- Joesph Sharpman vs David Whitman Many people would consider this the rematch of the century. After the amazing battle that was raged just months ago during MWE's Friday Night Overdrive, however when that battle was fought I was a different man. That was a match between David Whitman and Joey Dallas, that is a match that will never happen again. Instead a war will be raged between Mayhem Mafia Leader David Whitman ... and Vanguard Co-Founder ... Joesph Sharpman. -------------------------------------------------- "Don't fret precious we're here ..." Dark ... Nothingness "Stop dreaming of a brighter future, because hope finds you today." A small glimmer appears in the center of the screen breaking up the otherwise black void. "We're there to protect you .. From the lies, and choice, and other poison devils .. Because they don't give a fuck about you ..." That hope .. That glimmer in front of us fades away as quickly as it became visible. Leaving nothing but the black yet again. "Like we do." We seem to fly forwards as a bright light comes into view, rushing into middle of it with a blinding rush of speed. As the light fades out three men come into view, standing proudly in the middle of an otherwise empty room. On the right is Ace Cannon, dawning his usual affliction t-shirt and jeans as well as his evil glare as he looks into the camera. One the left, is Semi X-Havok title strung over his shoulder .. He is wearing the "Blood is Thicker" Vanguard t-shirt and a glare as angry as Cannon's. However, between them could be the most important piece of this scene. With a dark blue suit covering most of his body, and his bright blond hair held firmly in place glistening against the light, stands Joesph Sharpman. Joesph Sharpman: "We are the Vanguard. The forefront of the movement that will bring the people that only harm this company to their knees. Starting with the men that forced this movement into motion ... The mafia." Cannon raises his right arm furiously pushing Sharpman to side as he steps forward, anger on his face as he starts on an enraged rant. Ace Cannon: "And that means I'm coming for what should already be around MY waist! I already beat you once Whitman and come Tribulation I'll do it again! See I beat you when you were at one hundred percent ... So try beating me after you deal with my choice of poison ... JOESPH SHARPMAN!" He is seething with anger as he stares at the camera, ready to burst into a rampage at any second as he announced his choice for the Pick Your Poison match this Monday. How better to pick, then the man that has taken even him to the limit? Joesph flattens out a few wrinkles in his suit before gently pushing Cannon back with his forearm, stepping back in front of the pack. Joesph Sharpman: "You see David, for far too long we have let you reign supreme in this company. Your reign as champion was supposed to come to an end at Overdrive when I fought you for the title, yet with my mind focused on bringing the Vanguard into play I misjudged your desire to hold this company in your palm ..." As Cannon now paces behind both of his Vanguard co-members in the background Semi cracks his neck, eyes focused on Sharpman who remains calm his words flowing in a way that seems to make him omnipotent. Joesph Sharpman: "Now trust me when I tell you .. When I promise you .. That I will not be as misguided when we meet again this Monday, because the movement is now in full motion. There are no distractions from the my goal this time .. There are no championships to defend ... There are no feuds to cloud my vision and most importantly, the man you faced two months ago for the Undisputed Championship ... Is not the man that you will be stepping into the ring with this Monday." His calm demeanor is miles away from the rampage that Cannon is on as he punches the wall, trying to calm himself in the only way he knows how. Honestly Sharpman takes very little notice to either of the other men in the room, he seems to be intent on telling David Whitman the reasons behind their next match. Joesph Sharpman: "The last time we met you were taking on Joey Dallas ... The man hell bent on showing you a path to enlightenment, taking your fans through the guilt trip of their lives and robbing you of what you cherish most ..." This is beginning to sound a lot like the man we use to see every week, but I guess that's the way Sharpman wants this to sound. Explaining every little bit of why Sharpman isn't Dallas, trying to persuade the people that they are different ... Or is he tying to persuade himself? Joesph Sharpman: "But somehow I decided that what you cherished most was your title ... Well I was wrong then, what you hold closest to your heart isn't the title ... No, your personal claim to the top is that in mind you think your the best that the industry has to offer ... No matter how much you want to deny it." Wait .. Aren't don't the Undisputed title and being the best in the world go hand in hand? Isn't that what being the championship is supposed to represent? Well let the man explain and I'm sure his message will become clear. Joesph Sharpman: "What you cherish most ... Is your ego ... Ever since I lost the biggest match of my career to you, I have been keeping an eye on everything you do ... And that is exactly what the Mafia about, feeding your unfulfillable quest to be a true legend ..." He pauses for a few seconds to look backwards at the Vanguard, Ace Cannon, the man that he founded the Vanguard Movement with in order to change the face of wrestling. And Semi, who we're still unclear about in this situation. A grin slides across Joesph's lips as he looks back towards the camera. Joesph Sharpman: "And why? ... Because, you are jealous of the man you praise to be the greatest asset this company has ever seen ... You want to be Tim." As if on cue a cellphone begins to ring from Cannon's pocket causing the entire room to look in his direction as he pulls it out. Taking just a second to look at the number on the display he flips the phone open and holds it up to his ear. Ace Cannon: "Talk to me." Par the slight murmur heard from the phone the room falls into silence, all of them positive of who should be on the other line. A few seconds pass, the murmuring stops and Cannon once again begins shaking in anger. Ace Cannon: "Yeah, I'll be there it twenty ..." He snaps the phone shut and stares at it for a few moments. Before clutching his fist tightly around the small piece of technology, turning towards Sharpman he buries it back in his pocket. Ace Cannon: "That was them ... So wrap this thing up and meet us there when your done. Let's go Jesse" Joesph nods towards him, giving Cannon and Semi the green light to walk away disappearing from sight as they do so leaving Sharpman alone. Making this scene all too familiar. An empty room, a camera .. and Joesph Sharpman the way he made himself known in the first place. As he turns back to the camera the trademark glare is on his face, one that David Whitman should fear with every ounce of his being. Joesph Sharpman: "So David, this is the way it will be come Monday. Just you, me and the man that separates you and your ego from completely tearing apart what I have worked so hard to defend ... Tim Fitzgerald. The man you desperately try to mold yourself to be ... " This is something that has never been done before, a man is using our late founder as an attack on someone else. Comparing someone to Tim should be a compliment, but Sharpman has once again managed to find a way to twist even that into a verbal beat down. Joesph Sharpman: "It is easy to look at you and see someone who cares about the future of this company ... However I see past that clever lie of yours to the truth ... Everything you do is about your legacy, trying to earn the right to be held on the same level as the man that created this company with his own hard work ... See, I now realize that the Mayhem Mafia is about you trying to let your name live on through the people you teach to be superstars of this industry ... The way Tim did for so many." He brings his hands up and cups them just below his waist, looking at them intently as he continues this harsh onslaught of words against the champion. Joesph Sharpman: "You see Blood Fuze is your Sonny Williams ... A fresh young talent filled with all the potential needed to take this company be the helm once people like you move on, talent that just needs to be molded and guided into something better then they are ..." Twisting one of his hands around he acts as though he holds a ball a ball of clay in his hands, acting as if he were flattening it out he slowly raises his hands towards himself. Without even looking towards the camera he continues speaking. Joesph Sharpman: "But what you failed to realize is that I created Blood Fuze ... I am the man that took Markowitz under his wing and nurtured him into what he has become today, I am the man that persuaded Triple G into joining his cousin in Revolution Pro ... You see, I brought them into this world ... and if I so pleased ...." Still staring at his hands he brings them back into that cupped position from just moments ago, before letting his eyes drift into the camera. A sly smirk on his face. Joesph Sharpman: "I could crush them ...." With that he clenches his hands into tight fist in the universal sign for "I crush" as a small chuckle escapes from his lungs, the smirk grower wider as it does. Letting his hands drop Sharpman keeps his eyes focused towards the audience. Joesph Sharpman: "But I'd rather .. Crush you" Fingers still tense his fists unclench, only to wrap around the collar of his shirt as he pulls the first few buttons loose Joesph Sharpman: "I'd rather break your spirit ... Hinder your ego and above all else .. Watch you squirm like the pathetic little worm you arm after the Vanguard takes everything from you. I want you to know how I felt when I lost against you two months ago. So I can finally look down on you and know that I am justified in saying that you do not deserve the legacy the people give you ..." With the same hand he begins loosening his tie, the dark red fabric clutched tightly in his fingers as the tight knot soon hangs inches away from his neck. Eyes stilled focused forward Sharpman can't seem to tear apart what he believes Whitman is enough. Joesph Sharpman: "Because you will never accomplish the things in this business that a man like Tim Fitzgerald has ... You simply do not deserve the accolades that more influential people have earned ... David, I want you to look on your wall, and see what hangs proudly there ... I want you to remember Crowning Glory David, because that is the night that this egotistical side of you began to shine through ... It was the first night in history, that you played second fiddle to Tim and it ate you up." Could it possibly be true? Was the man that Whitman has idolized ever since beginning his career in MWE the reason behind everything he has done? No, it couldn't be ... But Sharpman wants you to believe it. He is sick of seeing Whitman as a threat instead of just another victim of the Vanguard and it's eating him alive. We can see the tension in his eyes as he tries to force feed us his propaganda about the champ. Joesph Sharpman: "The night that should have been known as your greatest achievement, is instead remembered as the night that the MWE remembered the efforts of a true legend in this business ... And as you stood on top of the world, once again champion the lights would fade out ... On Tim's night. That's when you decided that no matter how much it would take, your's would be the name that is solidified with Mayhem Wrestling Entertainment ..." At this point Sharpman looks like a mess, his tie undone hanging above his jacket, the first three buttons on his shirt ripped out and scattered across the floor and his usually unmovable hair is scuffled and out of place. But his eyes tell a different story, they are filled with a confidence we have not seen in his eyes since he defeated Ace Cannon at Adrenaline Rush. The blue in them seems to shine evilly as his smile does the same. Joesph Sharpman: "And to do that, you decided in your twisted little mind .. " Slowly he brings a finger towards his head, pushing it lightly against his temple as though to say "Your brain goes here." Joesph Sharpman: "That you needed to outdo Tim's legacy ... You needed to surpass him in every aspect of his career, whether it be the careers that were bettered through his involvement or the fact that everyone in the back credits him for the success of this company ..." He lowers his hand back down to his side, eyes unmoving from the camera. The smile fade from his face now as he stares as though looking into the eyes of Whitman. Joesph Sharpman: "So you created the Mayhem Mafia ... You created what you thought could bring your career to that level. What you thought would elevate your status in MWE past that of the overrated world champion ... To a legend." As he explains his last point Sharpman holds his hand at his waist to represent where he thinks Whitman's career should be held and brings it up above his head. To the level of Tim, to the level of a true legend in this federation. Joesph Sharpman: "And while you have taught Blood Fuze to show a side of themselves that I never thought possible .. That confident side, that self-righteous side that you yourself have shown me in the past, all in hopes of bettering them so they give you the praise that people like Sonny Williams and Alejandro Rodriquez give to Tim ... You fail to realize that Mr. Markowitz and Triple G will never reach the level that Tim's pupils have, and if they do they will turn their backs on you as quickly as they did to me ..." Sharpman stares at his hand raised high above his head, the first time his eyes have moved away from the camera in what seems like forever. Joesph Sharpman: "And just as quickly as they turn their backs on you ... Your career, your reign as the godfather of the Mafia ... And your dreams of being a true MWE legend ..." Finger by finger Sharpman monotonously closes his hand into a fist and then drops his hand to his waist, still staring at it. Joesph Sharpman: "Will crash and burn just like your title reign come May twenty-fourth. When you fall victim to the unforgiving kick of Ace Cannon ... and the Vanguard ends the hopes of another hero." He now lets his hand hang loosely by his side as he turns his eyes back towards us for a few moments before looking towards the ceiling. Joesph Sharpman: "You see Tim, they don't give a fuck about you ..." Using his opposite hand he now brings his finger up to his chest and points towards himself, still looking up towards the heavens .. Towards Tim if you will. Joesph Sharpman: "Like I do." The scene slowly fades out to black as Sharpman begins readjusting his suit, trying to get himself in a presentable appearance for what was about to come. What is that you ask? Well, keep reading and find out. -------------------------------------------------- I have been considered the underdog for longer then I care to remember, so why should it be any surprise now that the mindless sheep of fans are behind David Whitman yet again? In all honesty, I couldn't care less ... In fact, I want these people behind Whitman with ever fiber of their being ... I want them emotionally attached to Whitman come Monday night, because I want them to feel the same thing that he feels. Failure. -------------------------------------------------- "Listen to me you piece of ignorant shit!" As the scene comes back into the office of Malibu we can see that there has obviously been quite a commotion up until this point. His desk has been thrown out of place, chairs are left toppled over and lying around the room, and Ace Cannon has the GM pinned against the wall. His fists wrapped tightly around Malibu's shirt as he stares him in the eyes. Ace Cannon: "I'm going to let you go, and you're going to take a seat in or little GM's chair and you will write Joesph Sharpman into the United States title match .. Am I understood!?" He loosens his grip on Malibu a little, still keeping him pinned against the wall just not as violently. Eyes filled with anger as he stares down his former manager. Dick Malibu: "And if I don't? This only pisses Cannon off even more as he shoves his arms into the chest of Malibu, tightening his fist as he pulls him up. Forcing Malibu to go on his tip toes as Cannon gets in closer to him, the intimidation factor obviously working wonders at this point. Ace Cannon: "Then you're gonna get your fucking head kicked in!" "Put him down Ace." Their little encounter is interrupted by the arrival of Joesph Sharpman, suit now straightened out, hair redone and the confident swagger back in his step as he allows himself into the office. Taking a look around the half-destroyed room he sees the rest of the Vanguard, an uncaring Semi sitting in one corner on the only chair that seems to still be stand. The still infuriated Paige in the other as Eli tries to calm her down, and Ace Cannon still tense as he once again shoves her arms into the chest of Malibu before slowly backing away for a few steps. Joesph Sharpman: "He's not worth the effort and whatever the problem here is isn't worth losing our shot at the title over." Dick Malibu: "Thank you!" Joesph Sharpman: "Don't get ahead of yourself either Dick ... In case your forgetting we can still dismantle your entire career if we so please." Once again peering around the room Sharpman readjusts his suit for what could be the hundredth time today. Just from the look in everyones eyes he can tell that this has gotten under the skin of his associates, and in turn makes a suggestion of his own. Joesph Sharpman: "Now if you'll excuse us I believe we need a brief meeting of our own ..." All eyes in the room shift towards Malibu, an evil stare on everyones face (par Eli of course) as the room falls into a awkward silence. All of the Vanguard waiting for Malibu to get out. Dick Malibu: "It's my office!" With that Joesph turns to look at Semi, and with a simple nod he takes into action. Pushing himself up his eyes stay locked on Malibu and without so much as a word he slowly begins walking towards him in a stalking manner, forcing a frightened general manager to scramble around the cluttered chairs only to find himself running into the chest of Sharpman. Unmoving Sharpman looks down at the fearful Malibu. Joesph Sharpman: "If you'll excuse us ..." Dick Malibu: "I'm going already!" Semi grabs his arm from behind, twisting it around into a hammerlock of sorts on the general manager as a look of pain twists across his features. Dick Malibu: "I said I'm going! F***!" Still locked in Semi's hammerlock Malibu is lead towards the door before being thrown out into the hall, door slammed quickly behind him. Leaving the five members of the movement in the room, tension running rampant as that same silence from just moments ago fills the room again. Only broken by Semi as he leans against the door and folds his arms across his chest. In the tense silence Sharpman's head once again twists around to look around at the room filled with nothing but anger at the situation that was going on just moments before he arrived. Joesph Sharpman: "Well?" Silence as Cannon slumps himself against the large window behind Malibu's desk, leaving Sharpman with an unanswered questioned and a brewing anger of his own. Joesph Sharpman: "Anyone want to explain to me exactly what the hell this entire little scene of ours was about?" Ace Cannon: "Tell the man!" His tone is filled with raw anger as he looks towards Paige, cocking his head towards Sharpman as he does so. However this tone doesn't sit well with an already infuriated Paige as she now attempts to lunge towards Cannon, only to be held back by a suddenly brave Eli Silver. Paige: "I will f*cking kill you!" Joesph Sharpman: "Both of you calm down!" Cannon eggs his girlfriend on from across the room, using his hand in that famous Rock motion of "Just bring it" as Sharpman raises his voice towards his peers for what could be the first time. Joesph Sharpman: "Look at yourselves ... Is this what we want? To be clawing at each others throats because of some absent minded decision that Dick Malibu made? We are better than that ... The Vanguard is better than that." Eyes shifting between the two lovers turned war enemies Sharpman tries to calm the remaining tension in the room. Slowly succeeding as the time passes by slowly, that silence once again looming over the room, only finding itself being broken by Sharpman again. Joesph Sharpman: "Now I want someone in this room to tell me, why we're sitting here arguing with each other rather then going out and doing what we promised ourselves we would do with this movement." Letting out a groan it is surprisingly Eli that speaks up for the rest of the room as he gently pushes Paige back towards the corner of the room that he had been trying to calm her down in just a few minutes ago. Eli Silver: "The problem is that since Paige isn't cleared to wrestle in the unification match at Tribulation she was told to pick a replacement, she picked you as that replacement and now Malibu has booked you against Alejandro and Warner in a number one contenders match. Breaking the terms of Paige's contract" Paige: "That no good little horse fucker" Sharpman shakes his head disapprovingly again as he looks around the room, seeing Ace and Semi both stilled propped up against their respective walls, arms crossed across their chests in a similar fashion. In all honesty, Sharpman is ashamed of this scene. Joesph Sharpman: "If that is really what this entire day has been about, then I think we have all lost sight of what the Vanguard was created for ..." This last remark sparks the attention of Cannon, who now raises his head and pushes himself away from the wall, eyes now locked on Sharpman in a way we haven't seen since their confrontation months ago. Ace Cannon: "Oh have we now? ... If I remember right we made the Vanguard so that MWE would see a new patch of champions instead of the same old main event that has been fucking up this company since the day we started here ... So tell me your holiness, how is getting you a shot at the US title going against that fucking mission statement!?" Cannon has now worked his way towards Sharpman, so that they are now only a few feet apart. Eyes locked and that tension once again building in the air as Paige and Eli look on, not wanting what they think is only inevitable to happen. Paige: "Guys don't do this .." Joesph Sharpman: "Your taking this the wrong way Ace! It's not that I don't appreciate what you three are trying to accomplish, it's just that if we do it this way it means nothing to the people we vowed to take down ... If those people feel we haven't earned the right to call ourselves champions then they will never respect us ... Trust me, I want the same thing you do ... But we need to approach this in a way that will accomplish what we set out to do." Eyes still locked Paige tries one more time to try and stop these two former rivals from going at each others throats one last time. She takes a few steps forward, putting herself right in the middle of them and turns to Joesph. Paige: "So exactly how do you wanna go about doing this then?" Ace Cannon: "Yeah Joey ... Enlighten us." Paige spins around and violent pushes Ace into the wall as his glare at Sharpman remains unwavering. Paige: "Give it a rest already Ace!" Sharpman takes a small look over to Paige before moving his eyes back towards Cannon, his expression more aggravated then angry as he takes a few steps closer to his Vanguard Co-Founder. Joesph Sharpman: "This is how it's going to work ... This Monday I'll beat David Whitman, and Ace here will make it two for two on the night after he kicks Alejandro's skull in ... Then come May twenty-fourth .. When the dust is settled and the blood is spill, the Vanguard will be standing tall above the rest of this pathetic roster in victory ... The way it should be." A sly grin crosses Cannon's face for a few short moments before he bursts out into a maniacal laughter. Forcing all eyes in the room once again to shift, this time to the insane co-leader of the faction. Ace Cannon: "Now that's the Joey I know and love!" He pushes himself off the wall yet again to a sigh of collective relief from both Paige and Eli as they watch on as he walks past the now former intercontinental champion and gives Sharpman a quick man hug. Ace Cannon: "Just make sure that you pin that Mexican Power Ranger wanna be, because I want to see his poor little face when he realizes that Vanguard is here to stay." Cannon now takes a look over the room, seeing the members that were destined to leave the stable in person only to stay in spirit, and the newest member (even though just the "Insurance policy") the man that would be built up just from associating himself with the Vanguard. Ace Cannon: "I want to see the peoples faces .. When it finally works it's way through their skulls that they can break our members physically .. But they can never break our drive to drive away the scum that rots this company from the inside out .. And why? Because we're just that fucking sick of the bullshit people like Dick Malibu want to promote .." Joesph Sharpman: "Speaking of that hack of a General Manager .. Jesse, if you wouldn't mind." Sharpman and Cannon both turn their heads to look at Semi, giving him a nod as a simple signal that it was time to open the door and allow Malibu back into the room. His demeanor still unchanging from any part of this promo he moves away from the door, slowly swinging it open as he does so to reveal a mumbling Dick Malibu pacing back and forth in the hallway. Dick Malibu: "Who the fuck do they think they are, commandeering MY office ..." Just as he closes his mouth and stops shaking his head he realizes that the dulled voices from inside of the room had stopped and he takes a moment to look up. Seeing the entire room looking out at him from inside what used to be his office. Taking an opportunity to poke fun at his rival Eli Silver steps forward once again. Eli Silver: "The Vanguard will see you now." Eli bursts out laughing at his own joke, in that awkward, your the only person laughing thing. You know it, where everyone just sorta stares at you well you look like an idiot. Scratching his neck in embarrassment he opens his mouth again. Eli Silver: "Yeeah, you can come in now." Dick Malibu: "Obviously." Malibu has that confident tone back in his voice as he lets himself back into the room, staring down his rival as he walks back towards his desk only to find himself running into the large chest of Semi forcing him to fearfully side step his way around him. Ace Cannon: "Don't be shy Dick, have a seat." Cautiously Malibu makes his way around the desk, refusing to turn his back towards the Vanguard in fear of something much worse then having his office taken over. Dick Malibu: "I'm fine." Joesph Sharpman: "I believe the man offered you a seat, now I suggest you take the offer, before we MAKE you take the offer." Semi takes an aggressive step toward Malibu only stopping as the General Manager nearly topples over the chair behind him as he plops down to take a seat. These actions cause the rest of the room par Semi to chuckle at Malibu. Cannon and Sharpman close in on the desk as Semi backs off, Cannon putting a stern hand on the neck rest of the chair as Joesph stands in front of the GM, leaning back to barely sit on the desk. Joesph Sharpman: "Now I want you to listen closely, because we are only going over this once ... This Monday, we will deal with respective matches .. Then at Tribulation I will go on to face Zak Warner and your personal body guard Alejandro ..." Sharpman points towards Cannon standing beside Malibu, his arm keeping him from moving anywhere. Joesph Sharpman: "And my associate will face the champion. In two matches that will once again be dominated by the men you have come to know better than we would like you to ... And once we have secured what is rightfully ours in the ring .. We will be coming back .. We will be taking this office, and we will be taking the rest of the power in this company .. There is nothing you can do to stop it so don't try, and until we see you in this office again this meeting ... Is over." With that Cannon throws the chair and Malibu violently towards the window, a loud crack sounding off as he hits it. The Vanguard taking their turns exiting the room as the scene finally fades to black for the final time.
