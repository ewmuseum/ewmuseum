Q&A; Semi RP

Apr 11 2009

Jesse Anderson

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The scene opens as the MWE XTV Havoc Champion, The Semi, is leaning back in an office chair with his feet propped up on a large, wooden desk in front of him. There is a bookcase behind him with only a handful of books in the oak shelves. There is sunlight coming from the left and a fake plant in the corner. Semi is wearing an MWE T-shirt, jeans and white tennis shoes that are laying lazily across the desktop, next to his folded MWE championship belt and his WHQ championship belt. Semi is twirling a pencil in his hand and looking up at the ceiling, thoughtfully. After a few long moments, Semi begins to speak to the ceiling. Semi: You won't hear me say this very often, so don't get used to it. I was wrong. A few days before Highway to Hell, the world heard me proclaim this. The scene flashes to weeks before. Semi looks around a dark basement between two men sitting around a table scattered with cards and a man and woman standing at the bottom of a flight of stairs. Semi seems furious and yells aimlessly. Semi: I don't need her, I don't need any of you freaks, and I don't need anyone else, either! This Semi travels the highway alone; all the way to Hell! The scene cuts back to present day Semi, still with his feet on the desk and now with his eyes on the pencil in his hand. He now talks to the pencil. Semi: Of course I wasn't all wrong; I never am. I DIDN'T need her. I DIDN'T need any of those freaks. But not anyone else, either? Semi wrinkles his forehead and squints his eyes slightly in doubt. Semi: You know, I thought about it for a while and I decided that I actually could use a hand in my career and in my life. Semi tosses the pencil down to the desk. It hits the desk, but continues on and rolls off the desk to the floor. Semi pays no attention and takes his feet off the desk and plants them on the floor in front of him. Semi looks into the camera for the first time. Semi: And that is why I am here today. To hire a personal assistant. Semi grins wide for a moment before continuing. Semi: That's right. A personal assistant to the one and only Mr. Momentum of MWE, Semi. The grin disappears from Semi's face and he looks serious and frustrated suddenly. Semi: There's a lot to do and take care of when you're a championship material athlete. I have to carry heavy luggage all over the country, including all this gold. I have to do all of my own planning, scheduling, traveling while training and wrestling. I have to pay the bills and keep up with the fan ma.... Never mind. I have to feed myself and wipe my own ass! So it's time for Semi to get some help! Semi shoots a snake-like grin at the camera just before there is a knock at the door. Semi turns his attention that way. Semi: That must be my first applicant now. Come in! The camera pans to the door as a man walks in. He is dressed to kill with a suit and tie. In his left hand is a silver briefcase, just below a golden watch. The man makes his way to Semi and extends his hand. Man: Mark Carter. Great to meet you, sir. Semi, who is clearly bewildered, shakes the man's hand and motions for him to sit down. He does. Semi looks Mark over with curious eyes. Semi: So... You want to be my personal assistant, huh? Mark: Yes sir. I believe I can be an excellent member of your team. Hiring me is something you will not regret. Semi: Do you have a resume? Mark: Yes sir, I do. Mark opens his briefcase that is in his lap and shuffles through it. He pulls out a few stapled pages and hands them to Semi. Semi snatches them from Mark's hand and flips through them. Semi stops on one page and reads for a long moment. He shifts his eyes back to Mark. Semi: It says here you graduated fifth in your class from Princeton University with a business degree and have four years experience in computer networking. You also have three years experience in telecommunications... whatever that means. Mark nods his head. Mark: Yes sir. Semi: Don't you think you're a little over qualified? Mark: Sir... I apologize. I do not understand. Is this not the assistant business coordinator for Semi-tech position? Semi looks dumbfounded, then angry. Semi: No! This is the assistant-to-Semi position! Hell, if you want to carry my bags for me, you've got the job! Mark looks appalled. He stands up quickly and snatches his resume from the desk in front of Semi. Mark: You can kiss my ass! Have a good day. Mark storms out of the room and throws the door open. He turns and brisquely walks off. As he disappears completely, a woman's face appears in the door. Woman: Semi? Semi looks at her with intrigue. Semi: Yeah. The woman steps into the room and closes the door behind her. She is a very fit and beautiful woman with blonde hair and blue eyes. She is wearing a low cut black tank top and jeans. She walks over and sits down in the chair across the desk from Semi. She looks confident. Woman: My name is Danielle . I'm here for the assistant position. Semi watches her with promise in his eyes. Semi grabs a pen and taps it against a piece of paper sitting in front of him. Semi: I see. What makes you the person for this job? Danielle: Well, being the daughter of a rock star, I'm used to being on the road. She raises her eyes and stares dreamily off as she recalls. Danielle: They taught me how to have a good time while on the road too. She bites her bottom lip a little before returning her eyes to Semi's as she blushes slightly. Semi scribbles something on his paper and grins. Danielle: Um, anyway, I've spent the last five years of my life as a personal fitness trainer and would be more than happy to offer my services in the gym as well. Semi nods, pleased, with a satisfactory look. Danielle then leans forward and begins talking with passion. She motions with her hands the whole while. Danielle: And dude, wrestling is my thing! I've watched since I was like two. I was there when Hogan slammed the giant! I was there when Bret got screwed in Montreal! I was there when David Whitman became the very first MWE World Champion... both times! Semi interrupts her with a loud "pffft!" Danielle: I was there when you won this, too. Danielle puts her hand on top of the XTV Havoc Championship. Semi's eyes follow her hand before looking back up, looking impressed. Danielle retracts her hand and nods her head up and down. Danielle: I know that at Monday Night Mayhem, you're facing Devlin Sharpe in a non-title match. Semi raises his chin and looks down his nose at Danielle. Semi: That's right. What else do you know? Danielle: I know that Devlin Sharpe doesn't stand a chance. I know that right now, the Semi is Mr. Momentum. You put a man across the ring from the Semi and that man falls every time. Second match of every show for months. Danielle turns her head slightly, but doesn't take her impassioned eyes from Semi's now puppy dog stare. The pen in Semi's hand falls to the floor. Danielle: Devlin Sharpe on the other hand, hasn't had a match in a long time, and frankly, had difficulties winning a match before he went AWOL. This poor bastard has no idea at all what he's up against here! Devlin Sharpe is merely one more week's momentum under the Semi's wheels. Semi appears absolutely taken aback and smitten. Danielle: You know what else I know? Semi shakes his head and his eyes grow wide. Danielle slams her hands on the desk and her voice explodes through the room. Danielle: THE SEMI IS GOING TO RUN DEVLIN SHARPE DOWN! Semi's eyes grow even wider and more entranced as Danielle stands and extends her hand. She speaks through a smile and clenched teeth with all of the confidence in the world. Danielle: And I would be nothing short of honored to be in the passenger seat. You and I will drive right to the top. Semi immediately stands as well. He firmly shakes Danielle's hand as they stare into each others' excited eyes with matching evil smiles. They finally release their hands and Danielle rests her fists on her hips. Danielle: So, the first thing we need to do is get you on a better gym regimen. We need to work on this weight issue. The scene cuts to the front of a small, age worn white building with a large window next to a red door in the front. Taped to the door is a big sign reading "Assistant to Semi interviews today." The words quickly vanish as the door is yanked open from the inside and Danielle stumbles, frightened, through the door and onto the sidewalk. Behind her, Semi's voice floods into the streets. Semi: AND STAY OUT! The door slams hard behind her. The scene cuts back to Semi, inside. He has his head down in his hand as he is slowly making his way back to his desk. Along the way, he mumbles. Semi: Bitch. Semi sits back down in his chair and rests his elbow on the desk. Semi sighs deep. There is a meek little knock at the door. Semi stares down at the desk with his hand in his head and shouts. Semi: What is it?! The doorknob slowly turns and the door opens partially. In slips a very scrawny young man with messy dark hair and nervous blue eyes. He is wearing a suit jacket that is too big for him and a black tie. Semi looks up to see the tiny man and groans in disgust. Semi: The anorexia ward is down the street, kid. The boy looks up with surprise and begins to stammer. Boy: Uh... Um no. I'm here for an interview. The Semi assistant job. Semi groans once again. He impatiently begins barking at the young man. Semi: Well take a seat then! Haven't got all damn day! The boy drops his eyes to the floor and quickly shuffles to the seat across from Semi and replies. Boy: Yes sir, of course not. Sorry sir! Semi then declares matter-of-factly. Semi: You're over dressed. You look stupid. Boy: Yes sir. I don't know what I was thinking. After he is seated, Semi snaps again. Semi: First off, how old are you, Smalls? Smalls: Nineteen. Semi: You have a name, Slinky? Slinky: It's Dewey. Dewey Smith. Semi: Like the duck? Dewey: Um... I guess. Semi lets out a loud "ha!" Semi: Where's Huey and Louie? Hell, a duck? You remind me more of the Geiko lizard. Semi laughs hardily at his own joke. Dewey cracks a smile and laughs a little himself. Semi stops laughing instantly and stares a hole through Dewey. This halts Dewey's laughter as well. He shifts large eyes around the room and stops them back on Semi. Semi: You think something is funny here? Dewey shakes his head frantically. Semi: Good! Because this is not a ha-ha job! This is serious business! Do you really think you have what it takes to help carry the weight of greatness? Do you honestly believe that you have it in you to be the right hand man of an undefeated champion? Semi stares at Dewey through squinted eyes. Dewey swallows and answers calmly. Dewey: Absolutely. I'm a big fan of MWE and a fan of your's too. That's wh... Dewey is cut off by an again barking Semi. Semi: There is no need to kiss my ass! This job isn't about that! It's about doing what you're told when you're told to do it! You understand? Dewey quickly lowers his eyes to the floor and mumbles a response. Dewey: Yes. Semi: Can you do that? Dewey: Yes. Semi smiles a sick smile and looks down his nose at Dewey. Dewey looks up and Semi quickly changes his expression to that of contemplation. He nods his head and scratches his chin. Dewey turns his nose to the floor but keeps his eyes glued on Semi. Semi then extends his hand. Semi: You're hired. Dewey shakes Semi's hand while a very evil smile spreads on Semi's face as the scene fades out. OOC: Dewey Smith will now be regular character with Semi. His rep is DJ Qualls.
