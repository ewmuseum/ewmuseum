Not So Perfect; Zack Crash RP

Jul 23 2010

Zack Crash

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Its funny how things turn out. One day youre on top of the world, living the dream of your lifetime. After the long, ravenous journey you have finally come to the end of the road, for you have finally achieved your goal. You feel invincible, you feel unstoppable, and you feel as if there isnt anything you cant do. And then, in the smallest of instances, all that can vanish like dust in the wind. Reality gives you a hard backhand across the cheek, letting you know just how much pleasure it takes in denying you everything. And then you come to realize just how much of a fickle bitch life really is. Soon you find yourself questioning if you ever lived that good life, or if it was only ever just a dream. Yet sometimes, in the darkest of places, we can find a small ray of light that somehow shines brighter than the sun. Yes, its very funny how things turn out. Zack Crash was, for the moment, completely alone. At 3:47 in the morning, his only company was the silence of the gym equipment that lay scatter from wall to wall. The only sound that could be heard was the clattering of weights and his grunts of exertion. The AC made the room’s air feel practically frozen, but that didn’t stop the beads of sweat pouring down Zacks face. The exercise was therapeutic, and at the moment, Zack was happy to be alone. He was on good terms with the owner, who gave him special permission to use the facility after hours. Zack had never enjoyed working out with others nearby because he never got anything done, unless you count signing autographs and answering questions exercise. So he most often came here alone, and whenever he felt uneasy. Something by pumping iron made Zack feel as if he was expelling all his anxieties with his perspiration. Zack:...33...34...35 Zack counted off out aloud as he lay back bench-pressing. The weights he was using wouldnt have impressed anyone, especially not the bigger men in the MWE locker room, but then again, he wasnt doing this to impress anybody. He was so well practiced at this by now his mind could freely wander while he continued his usual routine and sets. Zack:....41...42...43 He thought about his feud with Virus and his declaration to save him. Sure, with both of them resigned to MWE the feud would continue, but something just felt…awkward about it now with the change of setting. Not to mention how much interest the UUW fan base had behind their feud; interest they would now have to rebuild with the MWE fan base. But more than anything, he thought about his conflicting feelings with the rebirth of MWE. Zack:...56...57...58 His time in UUW was important to him because there he had finally achieved every wrestlers dream of becoming World Champion. In UUW, he was the top dog. And now, with MWEs sudden and under-the-radar buy out, his championship was as worthless as a leather belt bought a Good Will, and he was back at the bottom of the barrel, his spot taken by the likes of Cannon, DC, and other well-deserved competitors. But then again, he was happy that MWE, the place were he had the most success (UUW aside), was returning. He had cut his teeth in that ring, and achieving success again in MWE was certainly as appealing, perhaps even more so, than success in UUW. But somehow he felt torn, and he didnt even know why. He was thankful then that his cell phone started to ring. It was a convenient enough distraction from his own thoughts. Zack: Hello? ???: Hey dude! What is happening? Zack smiled at the sound of that voice. It was a voice he hadnt heard in a long time, and it most certainly was a welcome treat here tonight. Zack: CSB!!! Hey, how are you man? What they hell are you doing up this late? CSB: Trust me, when you have yourself a kid, you have no choice but to stay up this late. Little bastard wont fall asleep for more than two minutes. Zack could hear the love and affection in Chris voice. He had to admit, he was a little upset that his old buddy wouldnt be returning to MWE. It would have been fun to reform the team of Crash Saint Blaise. But he guessed he had more important priorities now. Zack: How is Wayne been doing? Zack asked, trying to keep the conversation light. CSB gave out a small, audible chuckle on the other line. CSB: He is fine. He misses teasing Kent. They both laughed out loud at this statement. Zack could remember fondly all the times the four of them hung out together and how Wayne would always go out of his way to pester Kent, even going so far as to lovingly nick-name him Puss Puss. The time Zack lost his X-TV Championship, the time all four of them went sky-diving together, all good times. There was a moments awkward pause, until CSB asked... CSB: You looking forward to MWE reopening? Zack groaned under his breath. Why did he have to bring that up? Zack: Yeah. Zack answered unenthusiastically. CSB must have sensed something was off because there was another momentary pause before he went on. CSB: Im sorry I wont be coming back. But hey, I hear you may be in the title match at Crowning Glory IV. Zack had also heard that rumor, but then again, he heard a bunch of rumors about who might be in the World Championship match. Warner, Virus, Cannon, there was practically a rumor about everyone getting a World Championship match at Crowning Glory IV. And Zack really didnt give that much stock into rumors. He paused for a moment, wondering how to react to this, until CSB interjected first. CSB: Is something wrong? Zack: Kinda. CSB: What is it? Zack considered it a moment, not really sure himself how to word it. Zack: I am happy MWE is starting up again, I really am. But in UUW, I finally became World Champion. I have never achieved that kind of success when the other feds I was apart of closed, so those times did not bother me as much. But now, it just seems as if all I achieved in UUW was for nothing, and now I will have to start over again. Zack immediately felt better as the words left his lips. He had not talked about his concerns with anyone, not even his best friend Kent, and it felt good to put them into words. Over the phone, through the silence, he could almost see CSB contemplating his own response. CSB: I had the exact same feeling when GWC died. I was World Champion when GWC went under, and for a while, I also felt as if my victory meant nothing. But then I realized that was bullshit! Doesnt matter if the fed died, or was bought out or whatever. A World Championship is a World Championship. So your success wasnt for nothing. And if you achieved that in UUW, then you certainly can in MWE. Zack paused as CSBs words started to register in his mind, slowly bringing with them a small, but profound revelation. CSB was right. Zack had been concentrating too much on the setting of his fed and too little on his own success. Sure, the scenery may be changing, but he forgot that his success in UUW would certainly come with him to the Nu-MWE and hold some weight. Zack: Thanks. The two held a friendly conversation for another few minutes before fatherly duties pulled CSB away from the phone and he was forced to end the call. As Zack put his cell phone back into his pocket, he was reminded of why he respected CSB so much. It wasn’t so much for his abilities in the ring (though that was part of it), but outside the ring, he was a man of immense integrity. At that moment, like a bolt of lighting, Zack was struck with an idea. He quickly glanced at his digital watch, reading the date in the upper right hand corner. Wed. July 21, 2010 MNM wasnt for another couple of days. With that in mind, Zack gathered his things and left the gym, locking the door behind him. As he stepped into his car, he noticed the smallest rays of the morning sun just coming over the horizon. He turned the ignition and rolled the car out onto the streets and headed for the airport. This is the return of Zack Crash. It feels so long ago, yet it also feels as if you never left. The United Center is deserted save a few crew hands walking pass, but they are far too busy to give your presence so much as a glance. You stare nostalgically at the ring that already stands erect in the center. And hanging proudly like a cape off the side of the ring is a banner bearing only three simple letters: M...W...E Where once hung the banner of UUW, now hangs the letters of the reborn Mayhem Wrestling Entertainment. You slowly toward the ring and climb inside, feeling the same rush you did when you first stepped into an MWE ring. Yes, it was so long since you were last in an MWE ring, but it feels as if you never felt. And perhaps, in spirit, you never did leave. You look toward the camera, ready for a return that nobody will forget. Zack: There is an old saying, "the more things change, the more they stay the same." I used to think that saying was nonsense because it made no sense to me. How can something that has changed be the same as it was before? But recently I think Ive come to understand just what it means. Because here I stand, in the ring that will soon mark the rebirth of MWE. And MWE under new management, new rules, and a completely different set of faces; some old, some new. But looking at this rebirth, I only see the glory days that were the old MWE. I still see a federation that stood above, no...towered over its competition like a colossus. Things may be different, but truly, once we get this rocket going, it’ll more of the same. Same old action. Same old mayhem. Same old awesomeness! You begin to pace around the ropes of the ring as some of your old MWE matches play out in your consciousness. You vs. Blight, your debut match against The Monster of MWE. You vs. GM, where you proved you were an asset by defeating the then World Champion. You vs. Oblivion, where you settled an old score on the war fields of a wrestling ring. So many matches, and so many memories. As these matches play like video footage in your head, you wonder what new wars you face in MWE now. Perhaps you face the likes of Ace Cannon, Eric Derf, or maybe one of the countless rookies will step up with unimaginable potential. The possibilities truly are endless. And on July 26, we the MWE roster will usher in this rebirth in the best way we know...the best damn wrestling you ever seen. But there is only one problem. Right now, MWE has no shining star to lead this company. Gone are the days of Sinc Mercier, David Whitman, A-Rod, and DC. Those names are long in the past. With this rebirth, we need a new leader, a new captain to sail this ship. And that is what this week on MNM will be all about. Wrestlers competing against each other, all in the hopes of proving to management that they are worthy to take up the call, rise up, and guide this ragtag group of superstars back to the top where they belong. And who will answer that call? ......... ......... ME! You speak the words, and you feel the truth behind them. You were once the brightest rising star in MWE. But that was long ago. Now, you are no rising star, now you are the sun. And you will shine brighter than any man in MWE today, or every was. You will take your place atop the others. You will become what you always knew you’d one day be. Not a World Champion. Not the face of a federation. A legend. When I was in MWE, I was called one of the most promising rookies they had ever seen. Week after week and match after match, I impressed everyone with my arsenal of suicidal moves and my refusal to ever give up. I was X-TV Champion, I had a 2 month undefeated streak, I was the 2009 Gimmick Of The Year award winner. Everyone, from wrestlers, to management, to the fans, were all waiting for the moment when I would eventually become the MWE Champion. Well unfortunately I never got that chance, but now that we are back, I get my second chance at destiny. And I will prove that destiny is real when I go up against the so-called "Image Of Perfection" James Preston in the first main event of the Nu-MWE. James Preston. The name brings with it a certain nostalgic feeling. You did not cross his path much in MWE, but you certainly remember his voice. That loud, obnoxious, ever arrogant voice that only talked about how perfect he was. And you remember how often you wished for nothing more than to shove your fist down his throat and let him choke on your own brand of awesomeness. As they say, good things come to those who wait. Now I am going to be honest here, but I was a bit shocked to see Preston here in the main event with myself. I was even more shocked to see how our match was being advertised. Our matched is billed as follows: QUOTE With the re-debut of Monday Night Mayhem just around the corner we're more then happy to reintroduce you to two former MWE superstars in Zack Crash and James Preston. Since the downfall of our company these men have both gained a lot of momentum in there careers. Preston has seen self-made success with his "Master of Perfection" title which was defended in UUW before the buy-out where as Zack Crash finally made his way to that ever taunting promised land of pro wrestling known as the World Championship. However with both of these men back in MWE it's a brand new ball game and on Monday Night Mayhem we'll see if perfection can topple a former champion. Now I consider that almost a personal insult to myself. The fact that anyone could look at our two different careers and for even a sliver of a second think that we can be compared to each other. Give me a break! James Preston, your career is nothing compared to mine. All you are is wasted talent. Nothing but a waste of God given ability. You have the skill, but all you have done since your debut in MWE is squandered it. What exactly have you done in MWE James? As a matter of fact, what exactly have you done in ANY wrestling federation? Allow me to answer that for you. What have you done? ABSOLUETLY NOTHING!!! You can almost hear James silence in the distance between you two. They say we have both obtained "mometum", that we have both tasted "success." Well there is a difference between my success and your success Preston. I want you to note something in the billing of this match: QUOTE Preston has seen self-made success THERE!!! Did you see it? The key word is "self-made" success, which is not at all the same as "self-obtained" success. They may seem the same and of equal value, but in truth, they are two totally different things of vastly different values. Your success came from you creating a championship belt that had little to no value at all in UUW. That is not success Preston, because you did not have to do anything to earn. It is like giving yourself a present on your birthday; it is only a display of how arrogant you are. And you may fool others with why you made that belt, saying that no championship was worthy of you, but I know the truth. The reality of it is you got it backwards. It is not no belt was worthy of you. YOU werent worthy of ANY belt. You let the words hang in the air a moment, allowing their poison to sink into the mind of your opponent. You know Preston. You have seen him in matches before. And you know he suffers from the same weakness all men of arrogance suffer from. Confidence. Or lack thereof. Although they may hide behind words of perfection, in the end it is all a game to hide from themselves. Arrogance is nothing but a clever disguise for weakness. But you intend to expose that weakness. You intend for your words to seep into Prestons blood and freeze away his façade. Then, he’ll have nowhere to hide. From you, and more importantly from his weakness. You could not win a championship by your own merit, and you know it! So instead you created your own belt and called it success. You just wanted a hand-out, could not get one, so you made your own. And what is even funnier about that is you could not even defend that belt successfully. Not even once! K-Hard whipped your ass like you were his little bitch and then dumped the belt in the trash just to prove to you how much nobody wanted or even cared about your Master Of Perfection Championship. If I had that belt, I wouldnt even use it to hold my pants up. The simple fact of the matter is that you have always been, and always will be a failure. That is why you had to create your own success. Because you will never obtain it like I did. Success. You wonder what the measure of that word really is. Can what you have done really be considered success? Are you really as successful as you claim to be? What exactly is success in the wrestling business? Is it the number of championships, number of opponents beaten; you do not know. But what you do know is this, that whatever success may be, you have certainly done it a lot better than James Preston. You look out into an empty arena, knowing that within mere days this arena will be filled to the brim with faithful MWE fans, cheering as they help Harold in the rebirth of this great federation. And you know, many of them out there will be loyal thrill seeker fans, applauding their hero…you. I fought from the bottom up to get to where I am today. I faced monsters, legends, champions, and old grudges just to prove that I was worth it. And when I left MWE, I used what I learned to obtain opportunity, which I instantly capitalized on and became a World Champion. Anyway you look at it that will always trump your silly nothing championship. A real champion does not make his own success, he simply takes the success that’s already there waiting for him. Men like myself, Virus, Sinc, DC, Windy, whether or not we agree or even like each other, in the end we all stand above others like you in the wrestling world because we dont need to slap the success label on ourselves. We let others do that for us. Through our actions, we are labeled successful. We are the best of the best, the faces of MWE. Whereas you are nothing but an afterthought. An asterisk in the MWE record books. Because the difference between us is clear. You only make success. I let success make me! Your words have been spoken. Your message has been delivered. So with that you step under the ropes and walk the path back up the ramp toward the stage. As you walk, you know that in the not so distant future, you will be walking down and back up this same ramp exactly as you’re doing now. But will you do so as the victor, or the loser? You do not know, and you decide not to dwell on it. A thrill seeker lives for the moment, not the future. As you reach the stage, you turn back for one final message to your opponent. In the end Preston, you are no image of perfection. All you are is a shadow. Nothing but a shade of what could have possibly been a great name in the pages of wrestling history. You were nothing in the old MWE, and you will be nothing in the Nu-MWE. Because the more things change, the more the stay the same. And while this may be a new MWE, you are just the same as you always were. Second-rate. Second-best. And not so perfect. And with that, you disappear behind the curtain as the tech crew, preparing for MNM, test the titantron. On the screen, the giant logo of the new MWE flashes several times before the screen finally fades once again to black. Welcome to the new thrill. Welcome to the Nu-MWE. Welcome back everyone.
