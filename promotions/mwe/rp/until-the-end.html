Until The End..; A Nic Kraven RP

Apr 23 2009

Nic Kraven

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
“Open here I flung the shutter, when, with many a flirt and flutter, In there stepped a stately raven, of the saintly days of yore. Not the least obeisance made he; not a minute stopped or stayed he; But with mien of lord or lady, perched above my chamber door. Perched upon a bust of Pallas, just above my chamber door, Perched, and sat, and nothing more.” Prologue Trusting somebody isn't so easy as It seems. April 13, 2009 proved so as Nic Kraven and his tag team partner Peter Kaymakcian faced off against the team of Blood Fuze. Early on the match, there seemed to be communication problems between the two but it was quickly solved. Working together, the two defeated Blood Fuze as Peter Kaymakcian pinned Mr Markowitz to get the team a victory. This week however, doesn't involve trusting anybody. Its a matter of getting out alive. April 27, 2009, The Battle of the ICW Graduates. Nic Kraven will face off against Devlin Sharpe, and his partner from the week earlier, Peter Kaymakcian. What exactly will happen in this battle of the former ICW Superstars? ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ SCENE #1 ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ [Our scene opens up in the early morning hours in a somewhat plain bedroom. The walls are plain white and the window is partially covered by drapes. Outside, a thunderstorm is brewing. The rain comes down hard as if bullets from a gun. Thunder sounds through the whole neighborhood without an end in sight. BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! Like fireworks, it continues. Its a coincidence that a thunderstorm would happen on this day. A coincidence that the rain would come today of all days. A coincidence, that the incident had just taken place the other day... The camera pans over to the bed and there sits a big figure. He sits there on the edge of the bed with his head bowed and his long black hair covering his face. As the camera slowly comes closer the figure is revealed as none other than “The Definition of Violence” Nic Kraven. He is shirtless but has on dark blue jean shorts. Nic sits there with one question burning at his mind..how? Could this really be it? He sits pondering on how this untimely death has came to be.] ~~~~~~Inside the mind of Kraven~~~~~~ How? This man that I have dreaded all of my life is gone now. How could this happen? Now, now that I am finally strong enough.. The bastard dies. Now that I am brave enough...he had to curl right up and die! Never will I see him again. All of my hatred towards him has been for nothing. Hes gone now. Rotting in his grave...his flesh being slowly eating away by maggots. I am left to nothing more...than a lost man without a path. Hatred and vengeance still alive deep within my heart.. What must I do now? [Confused on how to take this, Nic remains seated with his head bowed. What happens now? Without his father, who will he turn his hatred to? The camera moves closer...and as it moves closer, we can see a slight tear come to the eyes of Nic.] ~~~~~~Flashback~~~~~~ [The scene reopens in the same room as it did earlier. This time, it is silent. Only the sound of birds chirping can be heard. Theres no rain. No thunderstorm. Just a silent morning. The camera looks over to the bed to see Nic Kraven sleeping with the covers over his head. He is tossing and turning as if having a bad dream. He screams slightly but then...*RING, RING, RING.* The sound of a telephone ringing sounds through this quiet morning. Nic suddenly sits up awaking. Sweat beading all throughout his face. He turns to the telephone still ringing contemplating whether to get it or not. He decides against it and just lets the caller leave a message. The now rings out through the darkness. A man's rough deep voice speaks out.] Man ….Hey kid, its your Uncle Jack. Listen, I know we haven't spoken in years and I doubt will speak anytime soon. I just called to let you know, your father is dead. He died this morning of a stroke. The police found him dead in his bed. I know you don't give a shit what happens to him but all I'm asking is that you come to his funeral. A few other will be there as well. Just think about it kid. The Funeral will be held in Fresno. Alright....bye. [After hearing the message, Nic looks up with a stunned look on his face. Could it be? Could the man who destroyed his childhood has died? Slowly, the flashback comes to an end and fades to black] ~~~~~~Back to the Present~~~~~~ [Should he go? Should he go to the funeral of the man that made his whole life a living hell? His father had never cared about him and neither did his mother. His father, a drunk, a drug addict beat him to an inch of his life each and every day. Now, he dies. What meaning would his father's funeral hold to him? Nic finally looks up towards the camera, he stares at it for a second and then begins speaking.] “The Definition of Violence” Nic Kraven All my life, I have only depended on one person, me. From the start, I have been on my own. Even if I did live with my bastard parents, I was on my own. Fighting for myself, and even feeding myself. And my father didn't make it any easier. Beating on me night in and night out. I was the one that had to suffer his rage. I was the one taking his punches, absorbing his blows. Now...he dies. How ironic it is. Now that I am practically strong enough, strong enough to take my revenge on the man that has destroyed my whole childhood...he dies! Why must I be this unlucky! Now, my uncle, who was never around when that bastard beat me, invites me to his funeral?! Why must I go?! My father's funeral is of no importance to me. To add more to my troubles, I am to face Devlin Sharpe and Peter Kaymackian this week on Mayhem. My grief never ends, nor does my anger for this world. Arggghhh!!!! [Nic grabs his head and bows. In rage, he pulls out some of his hair maniacally. Suddenly though, he looks up as if somebody has appeared in the same room as him.] “The Definition of Violence” Nic Kraven Its you once again. Why must you disturb when I am most vulnerable? [A ghostly woman like voice answers out of the darkness] The Voice I only come when you are in need of me Shawn.. “The Definition of Violence” Nic Kraven When I am in need of you? I don't...I don't need you. I don't want to hear you. I want to be alone. The Voice Aww, are you trying to get rid of me Shawn? As I have told you time and time again, you can not get rid of me. I will always be there.. [With that one single line, Nic is speechless. “I will always be there.” Nobody has said anything like this to him before. Nic was always alone, and alone is all he wanted to be. But now...this spirit. This spirit that haunts him, that will not leave him. All he wanted was to be alone. But somewhere deep inside, he knew this was not true. With Nic speechless, The Voice could only continue to speak.] The Voice So I hear, your opponents this week are the recently graduates of ICW? “The Definition of Violence” Nic Kraven I am... Peter Kaymakcian and Devlin Sharpe. Look at them, cocky, arrogant, not yet facing the real demons of this world. They don't know the demons that haunts this world. You see this world, as glorious not knowing the pain and sufferings going on all around you. Its funny..that you two have had good lives while I, have suffered. Its funny.. [The Voice remains staring closely at Nic. Wondering...just wondering. The Voice continues just to stare a few more seconds again then speaks.] The Voice So I hear, that your father's funeral is coming up. Will you be attending? “The Definition of Violence” Nic Kraven Why...why must I attend? What of use will my father's funeral be to me? Where does it all lead? What does it all mean? The Voice Shawn, you want vengeance don't you? You want vengeance against the people who have made you this way? Go, go to your father's funeral. Expose him, for the man he is. Expose him. [With those final words, Nic has realized why now he must attend the funeral. Nic is now feeling at peace. Vengeance was all he ever wanted in life, now he will get vengeance. There is only one thing to do now.] ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ SCENE #2 ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ [Alas, Nic steps foot into Downtown Fresno. With what seems like an eternity, Nic steps foot into his hometown yet again. He has on a plain black hoodie, dark blue baggy jeans, and a pair of Jordans. Nic stares at the city with a disgusted look. He thinks about how the city has gotten even more hideous since the last time he step foot here. He now makes his way to the graveyard where his father's funeral will be held. As he makes his way down the street, some people couldn't help but stare at him. Thinking, whispering to their companions. Nic takes no notice of it as he continues down the street and putting on his hood at the process. Within a few short moments, Nic arrives at the graveyard. He enters the gate and sees quite a few people there including family members. Nic recognizes a few but continues walking ignoring them. Here, he sees the spot where his father will be buried. Seats are all layed out and there, we see the gravestone. Nic stands there, frozen in silence at the sight of his father's gravestone.] [Next to the gravestone, lies his father's casket and inside, lies his father. Nic stares at his father in awe. Nic hasn't seen him in over 10 years and now, here he lies, dead, in the casket. As the funeral begins, Nic makes his way over to a tree, standing under the shade in silence. The people makes there way over to the seats as a man in a black suit with a red tie makes his way in front of all the people. He looks at them for a second and begins to speak.] Man We are gathered here today, to mourn the death of James Travis.... [The camera moves afar to the tree where Nic stands. The camera looks up at him where he appears to have a disgusted look appearing on his face.] “The Definition of Violence” Nic Kraven Pathetic..mourning over a man that is cowardly as they come. [The camera moves back over to the man where they have just finished a moment of silence. He looks up at the crowd of people and speaks.] Man Is there anyone who has anything to say? [Nic begins walking over to the man. As he walks, people stare at him as if he was a ghost. Nic simply ignores this, pushes the man away, and faces the crowd.] “The Definition of Violence” Nic Kraven You people..you people are idiots. Fools, why would you mourn over a death of my father? He was nothing more than a cowardly drug addict who knew nothing more, than to just beat me night in and night and night out. I was the one unfortunate enough to succumb to his beatings. I was the one unlucky enough to feel his rage. You saw it, each and every one of you people saw it! You did nothing! Nobody stood by me! Nobody ever gave a shit about me. Why couldn't I be saved from my father? Why was there nobody out there to help me when I cried for help? Now, I look back and I know why. In my world, there are no saviors. There are no gods. There is just...darkness. This world, this world is as ugly, as hideous as they come. And for you people to mourn over somebody like this, its pathetic. You all are pathetic. I know now, I have no family, nor do I want one. And as for my father... [Nic grabs of what appear to be a lighter. He takes a rose from nearby, and begins to light it on fire. Nic then now walks over to his father's casket, and sets the rose on it. The casket begins to catch on fire as the people from the crowd rushes over to try to put the fire out. Nic just stands there, staring and laughing. Nic walks back to over to the tree and sits down. He watches the people like fools trying to put the fire out. After a while, he looks at the camera, and begins to speak.] “The Definition of Violence” Nic Kraven So..you've all seen my father's funeral. You all know the story. Now, I can put this in my past and look straight at my future. Peter Kaymakcian, Devlin Sharpe, the battle of the ICW graduates. An obstacle has been set in my path. For this, I must hurdle over this obstacle to move on. Before me, stands a man I have yet to face. Last week, he was my partner, and now, he is my opponent. Kaymakcian...the recently, before he was stripped of it and burned to ashes, the ICW champion. How does it feel Kaymakcian? To be rid of your most prized possession? I bet it haunts you. It eats at your soul. I can feel it, and I like it. I see it in you, you are bothered that you are not champion anymore. Even if you don't show it, I know. Kaymakcian, I see the way you act. Cocky, arrogant, you act like your the king of the world. A god If you will. You are driven by pure emotion, pure uncontrollable emotion. You see Kaymakcian, your cockiness will soon be your downfall. Once your greatest weapon will now be your greatest demise. After our little tag team match we had with Blood Fuze, you let your ego blind you. You act as if you were the only one facing them. This...this is an example of your over cockiness. And as I said, this will be your demise. In our match, I will slowly bring you out of that light, and into the darkness. I will make you realize...the errors in your judgment. Kaymakcian, you pose no threat. [Nic takes a second to look back at the casket. It appears its still slightly on fire. He smirks at this. “Burn in hell father” he thinks. He strokes back his black hair and speaks once again.] “The Definition of Violence” Nic Kraven And now, we have obstacle number two, Devlin Sharpe. I have tested your abilities before and I have beaten you senseless. I have placed my hands on you before and that was the outcome. What makes anybody think this will not happen again? Sharpe, you struggle with self-identity. First, you were a madman. Now, you are a man who questions himself. You have second guess yourself more than you know. I see it in you, you are not confident, and you are not self-assured. You are basically just on a journey of self discovery. There are worst things in this life than staring at death. And in this match, I will show you. With death of your recent loved one, I can feel your pain. And honestly, I thrive on pain. I feel your pain and I like it. And now, I want more. Here I stand in the shadows. Come after me. Fight me Sharpe. Fight me! “The Definition of Violence” Nic Kraven Blood...this week, I thirst for blood. I thirst for pain. Fight me. Kaymackian, Sharpe, come at me with everything you got. Come at me, as if I was the demon that haunts you. Come at me, as if I was your enemy. I know what awaits this match. The fans...I know who they will be cheering for in this match. I have always known. Kaymakcian, the fans look at you as if you were a hero. They cheer you. They worship you. They boo me. They boo me as if it was their job. What hypocrites they are. Last week, I heard something I thought I would never hear. They were cheering for me. Cheering for me. Why? Because I was teaming with you. This week, I'll give them something to boo about as I destroy you with my very hands. I can hear it now.. Sharpe, you are somewhat the same as me. They boo you. I hear it. But I wonder...what will happen if I do to you the same as I did to you before. Will they boo me? Will they cheer me? But alas, I don't care. The week is ticking down to its final hour. As I speak, I am getting ready. I await you Sharpe and Kaymakcian. My thirst for pain grows more and more of the passing minutes. Come..come join me for this war. As the smoke clears, there will be only one victor. It is here that I will win this war. [With these final words, he walks away from the cemetery. With every steps he takes, his family members stare at him. Does he really care what they think of him? No. He is his own man. Nic makes his way out the gate and down the sidewalk as the cameraman stands there. Step by step. Soon..he appears out of site. With this, the camera finally shuts off.] OOC: Feedback would be much appreciated..
