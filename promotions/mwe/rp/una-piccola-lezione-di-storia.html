Una piccola lezione di storia; Project Revolution Vs Rated-X

Sep 28 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The camera turns on and we see B.G. sitting behind a huge oak desk in a very deep comfortable leather chair, he is holding a manila envelope. He is also wearing an expensive Giorgio Armani Suit. He is reading what's in the envelope and he doesn't notice the camera is there. After a couple minutes he starts talking without looking up at the camera. B.G. It seems that on Monday Night Mayhem the 3 members of Project Revolution are facing one of the best tag teams in this business Rated-X, Brian Michaels and D.C. B.G. waves at the envelope sitting on his desk as he looks up at the camera. B.G. I've been studying up on you two in preparation for our match. It says here that you used to be a manager D.C. You got into the ring after Michaels said you had potential. shortly after that the two of you joined up with MWE and officially started Rated-X along with Downward Spiral and Michaels' old girlfriend Amber Von Dresch. B.G. leafs through the papers until he finds what he's looking for. B.G. Michaels was already the MWE world champion and D.C. quickly captured the X-TV Title. Because you two didn't trust Downward Spiral and is affiliation with WZCW you guys beat him down and kicked him out of the group. B.G. puts down the folder and looks up at the camera with a weird look in his eyes. B.G. After that you guys kicked Amber out of the group right there in the middle of the ring while she was wearing her wedding dress. Not even I would do something that cold. B.G. picks up the Envelope again and continues to leaf through it until he again finds what he wants. B.G. During the 2nd incarnation of Rated-X D.C. won the MWE TV title, but Michaels lost the MWE World title to Pala. After Wrestle_Wars Michaels was hit by a car and was out indefinatly, leaving D.C. to carry the Rated-X banner. B.G. looks up with a look of astonishment in his eyes. B.G. You 2 have had one hell of a run haven't you? Hit by car and 2 titles so far and I'm not even done yet. There's still plenty more history between the 2 of you. B.G. picks up the folder and picks up where he left off B.G. With Michaels gone MWE had a little problem with WAR, but D.C. made the best of it by winning the TAG Team Titles with Windy Bridges. Rumors then started that Rated-X was over, until Michaels returned a month early. It was clear after a while that the Relationship between Michaels and D.C. was strained and Michaels lost the MWE world title to Sonny Williams. Losing his love for the business as D.C. was becoming a legit Main Eventer. Late in January Michaels left MWE and D.C. and Lindsay left for EW. B.G. keeps his head down as he continues to read from the folder in front of him. B.G. In EW D.C. managed to become The MWE World after Richard Covell sold the Company. B.G. turns to the last page and scans it real quick as a smile comes to his face. B.G. Now we're at the part that I've seen with my own eyes. MWE came back and Michaels was the first to join up but soon after that D.C. came back and said it was him who hit Michaels with the car after Wrestle_Wars, which started one of the biggest feuds MWE has ever seen. B.G. closes the folder and gets up from his chair and gets up close to the camera and you can see the fire in his eyes. B.G. Enough of the history lesson The two of you hate each other and that is going to be your downfall. Michaels I would feel the same way if someone who was supposed to be my best friend hit me with a car and put me out of action. D.C. you need to be looking over your shoulder in this match don't think for a second that Michaels has forgotten, or will ever forget, what you've done to him. B.G. backs up from the camera but he talks with a passion in his voice that wasn't there before. B.G. The three of us, me, Fear and Animal are closer than brothers, I know that the other 2 have my back just like I have theres. That is what's going to win us this match. B.G. turns his back on the camera and walks out the door as the camera fades to black
