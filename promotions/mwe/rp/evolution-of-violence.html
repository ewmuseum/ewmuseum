Evolution of violence; DK/Famous and Infamous rp

Jun 18 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Key FreakShow (Scotty) Martyr (Leo) Jezibel (Joanna) The scene opens at the back of The Sanctuary once again. We see all three members of The Dark Karnival run out of FreakShow's Camaro and sprint to the back door to escape the pouring rain. A loud crack of thunder hits and the three run faster. FreakShow pulls out a key and unlocks the door. Once inside he turns on the lights and the three try to wipe the rain water off their faces. He motions for them to stay put, and runs into one of the rooms and grabs a few towel for them. Damien gave me the keys, and two copies for you guys. This is where we train from now on, we have full access to everything...the kitchen, the showers, the equipment everything. He even has set up three bedrooms for us to crash or whatever we need to do. The three throw the towels into a pile off to the side. Wow this place is huge. He really put alot of work and money into it. The three walk around The Sanctuary before FreakShow leads them over to the three doors that lead to the bedrooms. FreakShow opens the door leading to his and the three enter. The walls are painted white with a bunch of black tribal designs running on them. FreakShow walks over and closes the window to stop the rain from getting in even more. To the far right of the rather large room is his contraption that he enjoys handing from his flesh on so much. He really got your essence in here huh Scotty. How did he fit that damn contraption in here anyway? Scotty he really went all out for us didn't he...I wonder what our rooms look like, lets go! Jezibel quickly turns and leaves FreakShow's room and heads for the second door. FreakShow jumps in front of her as if to block the door from her. He waves his finger in a no matter in front of her face and points to Martyr before opening the door. I have checked out both mine and Leo's rooms. I haven't gotten a chance to check out yours yet Jo. FreakShow opens the door to reveal an equally large room, painted all white with blue carpeting. There are a few swords and things that Martyr would use in the carnival during his fire eating show hanging on the walls. To the far wall is a fire extinguisher just in case. In the middle of the room is a zen like waterfall with pebbles lined around the bottom. Martyr walks over and kneels infront of the waterfall putting his hand into the water. Dude...I don't know how we can pay him back for all this. First the contracts, then the keys to The Sanctuary and these kick ass rooms. Scotty, Jo we owe Damien alot for this. I tried to offer him some money but he wouldn't take it. I offered to give him whatever he wanted but he still refused. He just kept telling me it was no trouble that its what he wanted to do. The three just looked at each other for a second before Jezibel runs over to the third door and tries to open it. FreakShow throws her the key to it and she eagerly opens it after fumbling the keys a few times. She opens the door and her jaw drops in awe. FreakShow and Martyr head over to the room and stand behind her. The two of them are as shocked as she is. OH MY GOD! This...can't..be...real. The room is twice as large as the other two. The walls are painted black with hot pink trim, the floor is covered in thick soft black carpet. There is a 52 inch plasma tv hanging on her wall. On her pillow there is a note. She walks over to it picks it up and reads it outloud. In the closet there is another gift. Put it on and be ready by seven. I have owed you this for four years now and its time I came through on my promise. She looks at the note and looks at FreakShow and Martyr and rushes them out of the room understanding everything the note had said. The two laugh and head over to the mock ring. FreakShow gets in and sits ontop of one of the turnbuckles, and Martyr sits cross legged in the middle of the ring. So Scotty...you ready for Monday? This is our chance man. Of course man...we really need to do our thing out there. I'm nervous as hell though...I mean in the carnival we preformed night after night in front of crowds of at most 500, but now we have the center ring its all eyes on us and there will be alot more people. True but come on your FreakShow I have seen you do things to people and yourself that should be means to put you in jail. But now you can be that genocidal murderous freak you are legally...but this time don't put our bosses head into a toilet in a drunken rage again haha. The two sit there for a second they can hear Jezibel messing with the stereo system in her room shutting it on and off and gigling loudly in joy. She really missed him huh...well at least she's happy and away from Slappy Bob. Martyr stop disctracing me we need to focus on our match!!! Martyr just looks at him blankly for a second. Ok well heres the plan I thought of...we are basically going against two guys who are just like us in that ring. McKnight is very ground based and more of a pure wrestler while having a mean streak. So I'll take him on we will be evenly matched. As for you my crazy friend. Jekyl has no fear, he can careless about himself nevermind his opponent. Maybe you should give him a taste of true chaos. Hey where have we seen a lunatic clown before?...Oh yea we only traveled, lived, slept, and ate with them. Come on man Jekyl is the same thing we lived every day of our lives. The only two things that seperate him from them are his balding lover boy who thinks he hears voices. Oh yeah and he got blown up...and came back...hmm I had heard of a certain billionaire who had done the same thing a while back. Don't underestimate them Scotty, we have alot to prove in this match and the only way is to take out two of the most violent men in the fed...so we need to be faster, stronger, and more deadly than them. The two look over to the sound of Jezibel's door opening. She steps out wearing this hot black dress, her hair all done up, looking just hot. The two look at her in awe. She is on her phone telling Blight she will be right out. She waves to the two in the ring and hurries for the door. Damn dude was that really just Joanna? Wow...well anyway I really hope Famous and Infamous bring their best...I hope when everything is said and done forgetting us will be impossible. Martyr its time for them to smile pretty for the devil cause they're about to take a walk through hell. Oh yes my brother, oh yes... The two look at each other and both stand in the middle of the mock ring. They stand there as a crack of thunder rocks the building and the lights flicker. END SCENE
