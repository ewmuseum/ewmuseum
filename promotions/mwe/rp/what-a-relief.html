What a Relief

Oct 7 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Mr.Cooper: YOU DID WHAT? An MWE camera begins to fade into picture from the blant and black image it is currently in. It takes a few moments to do so but when finished it is seen to be a picture perfect image. The camera is in a luxurious hotel room located in Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania. The camera does a screenshot around the room to see the many luxurious things it contains. Mr.Cooper is in a casual business suit as always, standing in front of the bed with his hand on his forehead in annoyance. He is looking into the room where the laundry is. All of a sudden a figure pops out of the room. It happens to be MWE World Champion Sonny Williams who is wearing his usual out of ring attire being long blue jeans with one of the legs out of the jeans only over the knee with a red bandanna strapped holding it up with a grey Sonny Williams hoodie. He has the MWE World Championship resting on his left shoulder. Sonny Williams: I accidentally put a red clothing in with the whites. Mr.Cooper shakes his head in annoyance. Mr.Cooper: Are you insane? Sonny Williams: No. Mr.Cooper: Well then why did you put a red piece of clothing in with the whites? Honestly, it's just common sense. Sonny shrugs as Mr.Cooper's eyes widen. Mr.Cooper: Oh no. Sonny Williams: What? Mr.Cooper: My white suit, I think I put it in the wash. Sonny ducks his head back in and goes through the washing machine. He pulls out the suit and shows it to Mr.Cooper with a smirk on his face. Sonny Williams: Is this it? Mr.Cooper puts his hand on his forehead. Mr.Cooper: Yes, that thing cost me 500 dollars. Sonny Williams: They say red is the new white. Mr.Cooper looks confused. Mr.Cooper: No they don't. Sonny Williams: Well they do now. Sonny pops his head back into the laundry room and goes through the washing machine trying to find what piece of red clothing did all the wrong. He looks through, scuffling for the piece of clothing. He puts his arm deep in and pulls out the piece of clothing and smiles. He shows it to Mr.Cooper, as it is a red G-String. Mr.Cooper: .. You can not be serious. Sonny smirks. Sonny Williams: Oh, let me tell you.. Last night was pretty damn serious. Mr.Cooper: Now's not the time for joking. Sonny Williams: I wasn't joking. Mr.Cooper sits down on the bed. Sonny Williams: Anyway, I'm gonna go hit up the clubs.. I'll be back before 6. Mr.Cooper looks confused. Mr.Cooper: But it's 7:30PM now. Sonny Williams: I know, I meant 6 in the morning. Sonny begins to forward out towards the door as Mr.Cooper gets up from the bed and stands in front of it. Sonny looks confused but chuckles at what Mr.Cooper is doing. Sonny Williams: Do you mind, I'm going to get my dick wet? Mr.Cooper smirks and scratches his chin. Mr.Cooper: No you're not. You're staying in tonight. Sonny looks confused. Sonny Williams: Why? Mr.Cooper: Why Sonny? Why? I'll tell you why. You see ever since you're loss to Alejandro Rodriguez you have been going out at night to clubs and getting back at insane hours in the morning. You are the MWE World Champion, you are what the public percept of this industry. Believe it or not, when you screw up, MWE does too. Now you better get your act together because you've got another big match this week too. Sonny looks awfully confused. Sonny Williams: A match? Mr.Cooper: How much did you drink last night? Sonny Williams: I don't know. Mr.Cooper: HOW MUCH DID YOU DRINK LAST NIGHT? C'mon Sonny, this is exactly what I am talking about. You're gotta get you're head straight. You're the World Champion, you've got near 30 men watching your every movement. Sonny looks Mr.Cooper in the eye and forwards over to the bed and sits down on it. He puts the MWE World Championship in his lap. Sonny Williams: So, about my match, who am I going against? Mr.Cooper forwards over closer to Sonny. Mr.Cooper: Milenko.. For the MWE World Championship. Sonny smirks. Sonny Williams: Well that's a relief. Mr.Cooper looks annoyed. Mr.Cooper: A relief? You've got to see each match as an individual challenge. Honestly, Milenko is talented, but so are you. You need a clear mindset. Sonny shrugs. Mr.Cooper: Get up and go look yourself in the mirror. Look into your eyes and tell me that you are the best you can be, because I already know the answer Sonny. Sonny Williams: .. And what is it? Mr.Cooper: No. Sonny begins to breathe deeply through his nose as he gets up from where he is sitting and puts the MWE World Championship over his left shoulder. He walks over to the toilet where the only mirror is in the hotel room. Mr.Cooper looks on as he can see Sonny through the open door. Mr.Cooper: Milenko is not going to be no walk in the park Sonny, tell me.. Tell me exactly how it's gonna be. Give me the lowdown. Sonny looks into the mirror as he licks his lips in a malicious manner. Sonny Williams: I'm looking into this mirror as though I am looking into the face of Milenko. My eyes are blazing of fire, I know what awaits when we meet on Monday Night Mayhem. I've been the MWE World Champion for near one month, it's not a big sum but it's getting there. Now, you've just been signed to the MWE and honestly, you haven't even shed one word as of yet. But I know you. I know that you're gonna bring up your past, make people bow to you in sorrow.. Reminds me of a man I used to be. But that is said and done, it's ended. I'm not like you Milenko. You see I too had a bad past, but doesn't make me have to wear face paint and stupid wolf claws.. Your pathetic. You should be in the circus rather than a wrestling company. Mr.Cooper smirks. Sonny Williams: You know what humbles me most, is how people like Alejandro Rodriguez say that I am a paper champion, that I am a disgrace to this business. I won this fucking title against two of the best superstars in wrestling today and yet I am still dissed and bagged consistently. I am sick of all this bullshit. I've proven myself time and time again but yet people continue to pass my judgement. I'm angry, I'm more angry than a pissed of Samuel L. Jackson. That has got to say something. But Milenko, I know not much about you, but I can sense I hate you. I can sense the vibe I get when I mention your name. It's not a fucking good one might I add. Mr.Cooper: This is more like it.. Sonny Williams: You've faced me before, you've felt what I can dish. BUT, this time I aim to leave you worse off than the American economy. I'm not like you one bit, you're, you're insanely weird and it just seems that each week I'm up against the same type. You don't know who I really am, don't you? I'm the MWE World Champion, I don't give a fuck about the past.. Whoever and what ever has touched this title, it's my time to shine. I'm bringing dignity to this title like the likes of David Whitman, Brian Michaels and Alejandro Rodriguez haven't done. You see no man has held this title over two months, it's an incredible fact really, but what I do is firsts. There is always a first for everything. Sonny Williams: Milenko, I defeated you the first time and you think just cause the stakes are higher that the outcome is gonna be different? It's not logical to even think that. Honestly, when the day comes that you put pen to paper and decide to write about your career, I am pretty certain there won't be a chapter on you winning the MWE World Championship. You are not built for me, you are not built for wrestling. Now, I'm not drunk, high or intoxicated with any illegal substance, but I mean business. Everyone on this roster is my opponent. And a word of advice, I recommend you shut your face before you regret what you say. Milenko, I am going to beat you so hard that from now on you're going to be complaining about the assault I gave you rather than your father. Sonny turns and looks at Mr.Cooper. Mr.Cooper: That's more like it! Sonny looks angered/confused. Sonny Williams: Ohh, you haven't seen nothing yet.. Promo Ends. -FADE- OOC: Not my best, and I know it. I was kind of lost for words at parts and didn't have a whole lot of ammo to work with, so yeah. Feedback please.
