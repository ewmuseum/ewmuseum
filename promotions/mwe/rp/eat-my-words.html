Eat my words.

Oct 23 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Sinc is seated at the Philadelphia Wachovia Center. The place is empty and blank. There is little light and many shadows flooding the entire arena. The camera pans across the many empty seats looking for Sinc’s face. Sinc is actually sitting in the front row staring blankly at the empty ring that could make history for the MWE. The camera settles in a focus on Sinc, Boize is gone, his suitcase comfortably laying on his lap, his hands firmly cupped over his mouth and nose. A streak of worriment strikes his face. The camera moves ever closer as Sinc is fitted in the frame. He is wearing his most recent Sinc Mercier shirt. White with pictures of him and his motto, “Take Me On Or Take Me Out.” Sinc then stands up and hops over the barricade and into the ring. The camera follows him and fixes itself on him. He is completed with brown cargo pants and white sneakers. His US title over his right shoulder, his suitcase now in his right hand. He places the suitcase down. He looks at the camera, his eyes have an exhausted stare. Sinc: The things on my mind at this very moment can not even begin to describe the constant CRAP I hear spilling out of everyone’s mouths. My vacation is over, and my game face is back on. I am here stating the fact of the matter right now, MWE is in my blood and my blood is in MWE, no matter how cocky I sound, how arrogant, how uncivil, how foolish. You can look into my eyes and see my truth. Call me whatever THE hell you WANT. But my feelings for this company and my passion will always remain the same. They will never EVER die. Sinc straightens his title as his voice echoes through the empty arena. Shadows flood the ring with only a single light streaming down from above glowing beside Sinc as he stands halfway into its brightness. Sinc: I can go on and on and on how a guy with a mask made a united states title a Mexican title just to show he can. Or how a Canadian plate smasher can talk so much about his opponents it’ll make your head spin. But there is the sheer facts that their time has come and gone. Where mine is just beginning. They have yet to realize that! Sinc begins walking around the ring and contemplating with himself a few moments then steps back into the light and smiles at the camera. Sinc: Yes Alejandro you’ve beaten a man who raps better than he wrestles, you’ve beaten a man you’ve fought so many times you can’t say he will learn to fly because I am sure by now he has already brought a parachute with him. Even though his brain capacity would still cause him to crash, but what can you say about YOUR self huh? Can you fly? I don’t know, can someone so full of himself even move? If anything you’re worse than me. You say I am the cocky one I am the one full of myself, SINC MERCIER is the one who needs to fly. Well I see a title on my shoulder, I see a young man going places, you? I see you as a washed up mask wearing version of Rico freakin Suave trying to remember the lyrics of some song that has been remixed so many times it would become some house mix to an experimental frat house! Sinc looks at his title again and begins to rub it a bit as if trying to clean it. He looks back at the camera and shrugs. Sinc: As for the mud part, I see no mud. It looks as clean as the immigration papers you must have signed to get in the US to me. Or possibly the whack jobs you must have negotiated with. Who the hell knows, maybe you flew on some purple dinosaur with pearly whites and green spots to get over here. Did he teach you to fly in your little kinky mask wearing imaginary world? I think someone has been poppin one too many pills buddy. If I wanted to fly all I need to do is snoop around your house I am sure I’ll find something to “make me fly” in a stash somewhere. And me making MWE what it should not be? Buddy, I am making MWE better! Here you are a short man who thinks he can sprout wings and summon his purple buddy to take him away to lands far far away. Believe me if I was that short and believed in puff the magic f*cking dragon I would have dreams and goals to jump off my little feet and fly my ass as far as I possibly can. But I just can’t think in terms of impossibilities. I am sorry. Here I am a champion and in possibly the best shape I’ve ever been in my entire life more ready to take on the world than I ever have been before. Or is the world too big for you? That wasn’t a pun that was actual truth. Sinc claps his hands as all the arena lights blink on very brightly shining over the entire arena revealing all of the empty seats and rows and all the little details of the ring and beyond. Sinc: So you see Alejandro Pet My Dragon Rodriguez. I may sound like a kid at times, I may act like a kid at times, my insults may not phase you, but as long as I am still champion and as long my fans and friends and family still believe in me no one, no Canadian broke backs, short dragon riding Mexican knights, or anyone else is going to change that. And Alejandro Rodriguez will learn, to, shut, the, hell, UPPPP!!!!!!!!! Sinc was about to call his little ramble quits when he thought of Canada and how one man made it look like such a bad state. Sinc: Mason Resoan. Now it wouldn’t be exactly fair if you weren’t talked about would it? A while back you said you will beat the sh*t out of me? Well seeing how Canada is a very non-violent state I can see you whimpering in fear and calling it beating the “sh*t” out of me. I am sorry if I cannot think of anything else other than that. It is just hard to see you actually wrestling. Make ME humble? Hey, dumba** back to reality buddy. You have JUST returned to the MWE and have entered dangerous territory where you my friend are the prey. You had your chance just like Pill Popper Rodriguez. Now your in a whole nother generation that you are going to be stuck in. And just like maple syrup you’ll stick there for a very long time. Most people seem to truly think I am nothing. And I feel bad for them because the nothing I am became a something and that something will shatter dreams and pin goals. It already has. Alex Bowen, James Sullivan, a man you clearly had to act like you were cat woman or something to brush out of the way, and of course Devlin, who could forget that crap wielding chimp. And then there is Aeron, an unfortunate failure indeed. What makes you think you will do Canadians right and get a title that doesn’t even represent the state your from how can it make any sense truly. Unless your going to be like Alejandro and change it entirely, good luck gaining any respect from that, shall we call you a disrespectful commy? Who knows. I guess we will find out buddy boy. Sinc thinks back of times from Mason’s and Alejandro’s history and begins to recall some very not so great memories from the old days. He leans against one of the corners as he rubs his chin. He then leans off of it and talks. Sinc: So over all we got a man who has fought the same guy too many times to really call himself a diverse superstar, who also apparently thinks he can fly and I guess thinks someone like Jekyl is competition. Tighten your mask and make your eye holes bigger you aren’t seeing the picture here. I am fresh talent, you are old talent, I have had more opportunities then Thomas Edison on roids and you have had less opportunities then Menudo on crack. You say the same thing over and over. I will make a mockery out of my opponents I’ll teach them to fly I’ll beat them all, ARRRG I’M A FLYING DANGEROUS MAN WITH TOO MUCH TIME ON MY HANDS! ARGG! Unless you’re a sacrifice for some demonic blood orgy I wouldn’t count on it. It’s like mixing JJ The Jetplane with Elmo and calling it good entertainment. And a guy like Mason who thinks smashing plates and besmirching every American wrestler who he can think of is a way of insult is just sad. Mason, you going for the US title is the real insult here. But Alejandro was right about one thing, you are COMPLETELY forgettable. That’s the fact and the truth. Come Wrestle Wars I will show you all that fresh talent and a little charisma can not only get you places, it can create a whole new window of opportunity. Let me show you all something. Sinc nods his head and points to the titantron as a very strange looking image appears on the screen. Sinc: Holy hell! Look at those two! Like they were meant to be! Sinc makes a very disappointed look into the camera. Sinc: In the end I could care less how you put it, Mason and Alejandro I am anything but a nothing. I am the hammer and nail to your scrotum, I am the reckoning to your end of days, I am the big mouthed reaper to those doubtful. I can clear a path with a wave of insults while you two couldn’t even clear a gutter even if you tried to talk your way out of it. You both have had some major matches, and good ones at that, but everyone has their ten minutes of fame, now it’s my turn. Step aside, do your little “I’m better than you” rant and take a bow. Because that is all your ever going to get out of me. You can try to impress whomever and whenever you want but you will never impress me until you manage to strip this title from me with some dignity and without telling everyone for ONCE how bad an opponent of yours is. Whether your Canadian Mexican, Caucasian, Black, Chinese, Chin Chang, Indian, Cuban, PINK, PURPLE, YELLOW, BLACK AND GOD DAMN BLUE SINC MERCIER WILL ALWAYS BE THERE! Waiting to tear down yet another obstacle….once again. Now SINCCCCCCC MERCIERRRRRRR!!!!!! Has to take his leave. Sinc heads to the ropes to leave then looks at his suitcase and then into the camera. Sinc: And for those of you wondering….questioning…BICKERING over it. The suitcase’s secret is simply this. The camera zooms in on the suitcase. Sinc opens it and finally it reveals what looks to be a new championship title. Sinc: That’s right. I, with my own money, and after contemplating this with Mr. Covell, came up with a new title. Rodriguez you say I lack DIVERSITY? Well this is as DIVERSE as it gets. For two weeks now I have been talking to Covell about this and finally he came to terms. This title is now an OFFICIAL MWE championship. I am not the official holder as of now it is vacant people. That’s right VA-CANT! The only way to win this beauty is by a knockout. Brutal knockout! Take a look at this video taped a few weeks ago. Sinc points to the titantron again as the camera shifts to it and a video began playing of Sinc and Covell talking in his office. Sinc is seen wearing the same attire he is now and Covell is wearing a black suit and red tie as he drinks a cup of coffee while leaning back on his office chair. Covell: So tell me again why you wanted to talk? Are you not being recognized enough or something? Sinc: Haha….funny…but no. Covell: Damn that’s usually the case with most people but continue. Sinc: Well I was thinking that we could introduce a new belt. Whatdya think? Covell: A new belt huh….well I don’t really see anything really wrong with it…tell me more. Sinc: Easy really. The belt will be vacant and the only way to win is by knockout. You ok with that big man? Covell begins pondering a moment as he turns his back to Sinc and twiddles his fingers. Sinc: Hey we could forget about it if you want just an idea. Covell turns back around to Sinc and smiles deviously. Covell: No…no I like it. Keep the idea. DAMMIT KEEP IT! WOOOOO! This is JUST what MWE needs! With the plans we have coming up its perfect! Sinc: Well thank you big man. I really appreciate it. Can’t wait to get this bastard into the MWE. Covell: Just know you can’t be the holder as long as your US champ buddy. Sinc: I know. Thanks again. The titantron fades with a handshake between Covell and Sinc and the camera goes back to Sinc in the ring as he nods in gratitude. Sinc: And that would be that. I don’t care who hates me for it but this will be a new era in entertainment. Thank you and good night people! Sinc closes the suitcase back up with the belt inside and winks at the camera. Sinc then exits through the ropes and up the ramp as the camera cuts to black and the lights fade. NOTE: ACE GAVE ME PERMISSION TO INTRO A NEW TITLE! PLEASE DONT BASH BECAUSE OF IT!
