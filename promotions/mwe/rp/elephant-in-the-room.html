Elephant in the Room; A Zack Evermore RP

Aug 6 2010

Zack Evermore

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
QUOTE The atmosphere is intense. The bright, miniature explosions of lights emanating from the hundreds of cameras are blinding. Signs wave enthusiastically in the arena as overeager crowd yells loudly. The camera moves back to reveal the ramp. All of a sudden “Right Now” by Korn bursts through the speaker system and the fans seem to erupt. Former MWE champion, David Whitman, dressed in his signature black pants with the MWE title around his waist, runs out from the curtains and dashes straight towards the ring. Somehow, inexplicably outside of this reality, a calm, toneless voice iterates a surprising statement. Man, I still can’t believe David’s dead. The scene abruptly stops and turns into a motionless panorama as the words “PAUSE” and “CONTINUE” appear on top of David Whitman. The atmosphere is, in fact, not very intense at all. The rank, foul odor emitted by day-old beer, warm energy drinks and mold-ridden sandwiches gently caresses the air it merges with as it successfully morphs into quite possibly the most disgusting breaths any human being could ever reasonably be expected to inhale. No fans. No cameras. Just a couple of idiots sitting on a musty old couch playing Xbox. Why’d you stop the game?! Why do you keep saying that stupid crap? David’s not frucking dead, Stosh. Ok, I’m sorry, man. I’ll stop. Now press the goddamn start button. Okay. The usually chipper and slightly off-kilter Arizonian known as Zack Evermore and his non-smoking stoner friend Stosh are both staring lifelessly at the TV screen placed in front of them as they tap away at their controllers. F*ck David Whitman anyway, that sellout. Stosh is dressed in an "EverMore" Shirt and beach shorts that evoke mental images of a Hawaiian beach, as well as flip-flops that help to that end. Whaaaat? You DO know I should kick you left and right for that comment, right? Zack, ever the defender of his long-time friend David Whitman, is outfitted in a stylish ensemble consisting of Donald Duck boxers and his ever-present “3WE” pendant-on-a-string. His lack of decent apparel, or much of any apparel for that matter, is most of the reason why the two are sitting at opposite ends of the couch. And I mean kick you straight in the face. Both carelessly throwing around voices coated in a rich tone of indifference, the friends seem unable to cease their pointless arguing. Look, I’m just saying. For the first time in a while, Zack’s voice deviates from its monotony, presumably beginning a chain effect; one emotional sentence can break any dull-toned conversation. Ok Stosh, listen. FIRST off, let me just remind you of how fruckin’ stupid you are. David’s not DEAD. He never was. He just got kidnapped by Lester’s flunkies. Everyone knows this. What do you mean, he was never dead? How could you possibly know that? Ignoring Stosh, Zack continues his little theory. You know what the last thing he said to me was, Stosh, you freaking quarter-wit? He said “Hey man, I think something’s not right. If anything happens to me, I want you to get more active and create a bigger name for yourself. Then use your fame to help me.” He was pretty specific. Well, I mean, he WAS on acid. Ah. And weed. Of course. And opium. BUT THAT’S NOT THE POINT, IS IT, STOSH? It should be. Zack cracks his neck as he breathes deeply through his nose. Eyes closed, he lets out a deep breath before going on. The point is that he was right, because not even a week after that Lester got to him. And even now with the JESTers searching high and low, nothing’s been happening. Thanks for the update. Zack grabs the remote and turns off the television set. As he leans back into the couch, a small puff of dust leaves the tattered fabric before he opens his eyes again. While not necessarily devoid of ALL silliness, his mood seems more subdued than usual. The usual spark, the zest for life, the energy… it all seems to have taken a vacation. Say, are you okay though, Z? Another deep breath. This one, however, transforms into a small, almost undetectable smile. Yeah, life’s… funny. Look, let’s just go over my opponents. Okay. You’re the boss. Stosh reaches over to the table placed next to the sofa. Scavenging his way through bottles, cans and assorted Japanese key chains, he finally finds a coffee-stained folder which he grabs and opens. I’m ready. Let’s GO. Stosh lets out a beer burp that Zack tries to wave away. Okay. First off; Casey Charisma. Is that right? Yesm, he’s the collector of Cool. He collects cool? Like, in little bottles, or…? Zack holds his right thumb and index finger out in front of him, imagining a three-inch bottle. Stosh, being used to Zack’s ridiculousness, promptly carries on like a champ. Charisma actually does sound like a pretty cool guy. Look at all these snapshots, man. Stosh opens the folder up more and shows it to Zack, who nods approvingly as Stosh goes through a couple of pictures. Different chick each time. And they don’t look half bad. I never understood that term. I mean if they don’t look half bad, does that mean they look half good? And what the HELL does the other half look like then?! FOCUS! Charisma beat Lupin last time. Who-pin? JACK LUPIN! The other guy you’re facing! Zack jerks back with a surprised look on his face. ANOTHER GUY?! What is this, a tournament?! Dude, it’s a threeway. HEYOOOOOOOOOOO! Stosh drops his head into his hands as Zack starts doing a little victory dance. Goddammit, Zack. HEY! Remember the rule, fool; no swearing at me, just around and across me. That doesn’t even make any SENSE. Your FACE. Sigh. Descending slowly down the side of a nearby aluminum can, a drop of condensation seems to flee the ridiculous conversation held by the two friends. Zack punches Stosh lightly in the shoulder. I’m just messing around, man. I know these guys. I saw them wrestle. ..You fu- Calm down, spanky. Stosh wipes his face as he closes the folder. He proceeds to turn and face Zack with a serious expression. Seriously though… Told ya. Aren’t you nervous, Zack? I mean, think about it… these two guys, they literally brought MWE back from the dead. They faced each other in the first match, and you’ve never even stepped into an MWE ring in your life. They’ve been through the pressure already, they know what to expect. You said it yourself; you’ve SEEN what they can do… Zack shrugs. I don’t know. I saw the match. Tivo’d, snitch. These guys both like to fight dirty. Spitting gunk? Choking? The Ecstasy Within? Man, sounds like a date with my ex-girlfriend. Should be a lot of fun, but that doesn’t mean they’re great wrestlers. The two stay quiet for a moment before Stosh breaks the silence with an attempt at changing the subject. So yeah, how’s that new girl? Zack reaches to his left and grabs a half-empty water bottle, gulping it down until it’s only a quarter-full. As he nonchalantly tosses the bottle across the room, he re-focuses his attention to Stosh. Girl… oh, you mean Betty? Is that it? Well, she WAS Betty yesterday. I haven’t spoken to her today yet. I really don’t understand how you can spend so much time with someone who refuses to tell you her NAME. Well I mean, technically, she never refused. She just likes to… I dunno. She’s different. I respect that. Suddenly, another voice chimes in from seemingly out of nowhere. Hey, hey, HEY, WHAT is going ON here?! Stosh nearly jumps off the sofa turning around to see the girl in question standing right behind him. Dude, what the hell. The girl, clad in a zebra-print skirt and a handmade “Where’s Whitman?” shirt, shrugs as she looks at Stosh through her polka-dot-patterned glasses. You know, from Saved by the Bell. Yeah. Stosh grabs his heart, trying to calm down from the scare, as the girl smiles innocently and tries to explain. Cuz his name’s Zack. Li- YEAH, I GET IT. Wait… Wh- have you been standing there the whole time?! Well, I wouldn’t say the WHOLE time, but I guess that depends on when exactly your period of “whole time” would begin. See, if- Zack waves his hand in the air, cutting the conversation short. YEAH. Anyways, what are you up to, fool? She looks up as she begins explaining, as if trying to recall her thoughts. See, I’ve decided to be a Jenzy for the rest of the week. It was between that or Bob, for the fun factor. But then I realized that wasn’t really funny at all. So yeah, Jenzy it is. That’s a weird name. Fitting then. Quite. Also, I want ice cream and a tennis racket. I’ll be back in a few minutes. Laters. Jenzy slaps Zack upside the head and walks off. Not two seconds after she’s gone, Stosh turns to Zack with a mixture of confusion, pity and apathy on his face. Dude, I honestly mean it when I say that you guys are the weirdest couple on the face of the earth. Zack shrugs. We’re not necessarily a couple… Stosh raises an eyebrow. Doesn’t she live here? Kinda. I mean, you know, we basically just do what’s easiest. She likes the couch in my room. Says it reminds her of her high school days, whatever that means. Sometimes she’s in a bed mood though, or a floor mood. But wait, do you sleep together? Sometimes. Stosh gets a wide grin on his face, and now it’s Zack’s turn to raise an eyebrow. Ever the gentleman, Stosh asks what’s on his mind in a polite manner. Is the sex good? Zack looks confused. Sex? What sex? … I don’t get it. Another shrug from Zack, who doesn’t seem to know all that much. Man, I don’t know what we are. All I know is, we’re both bored of normal people and all the conventional crap. It’s stupid. Expectations, you know, relationships, all of that. Silence slowly envelops the room as the two lazily stare at the turned-off TV set for a moment. … Am I still your friend? Another moment of silence. … If you go get me a Powerthirst from the fridge you are. Stosh rolls his eyes as he gets up. And if you get me TWO, I’ll even suck y- HEY! About two hours later… In a room with random puddles of water on the floor, there sits a once-white, now beige sofa. This raggedy article of furniture and its two occupants are seemingly held in place like books by the bookends that are the tables on either side. The tables, which themselves serve as little worlds for the aluminum, plastic and glass inhabitants filled from very little to very much with varying liquids, were once presumably nice and dark, the color of expensive wood. Now… not so much. Yeah. It’s the exact same place as before. I just wasted twenty seconds of your life with an unnecessary fake-setting jump and needless added description. BAM. You know they have a new clause in the contract, right? Zack, not looking up at all from the book in his hands, mutters a reply. What’s that? Like, it says that 30% of your paycheck will be based on your promo. Zack finally looks up at his friend with an incredulous expression as he drops the book to his side. …what? So like, if you don’t do a promo, I guess you’ll lose like 30% of y- Yeah, I understand the concept of percentages, STOSH. I just… wow. Yeah. Well fruck that. I don’t have time to think about any of that. I’m not gonna stand there and rehash some speech that’s probably been used thousands of times. I’m a wrestler, Stosh. I understand, but- No. Shut up. Today is a beautiful day. I don’t wanna get bogged down by ridiculous rules. The sun is shining, man. The sun is, indeed, shining. Whatever effect that may have on you if you’re sitting inside all day is anyone’s guess. Well I mean, we haven’t really DONE anything t- YEAH, but it was by CHOICE! Fruck that! Listen to me Stosh. This Monday, MNM is going to have the exact same opener match they did last time, except for one small detail. That detail is I, my friend. I see. Yep. And what I brought to WHQ, I’m bringing over here too. By the time the match is over, they’re going to be begging me not to do any promos because my in-ring insanity is so intense. Either that or you’ll be put on a very short leash regarding high-risk maneuvers. Yeah, or that. Or you might get fired for being too much of a liability. Insurance and whatnot. Yeah. But you know, that’s how I roll. I gotta go nuts in the ring, man. Stosh lets out a resigned sigh. Alright, man. Do what you must. Just… be careful. A moment of silence passes. Zack and Stosh suddenly burst into laughter. Loud, roaring, gut-ripping laughter. The kind that can’t be stopped. Unless it’s by a very startling and unusual sound. Like an elephant. What the f*ck was that? The two sit up straight and look at each other. Honeys, I’m hoooome! Zack gets a wide, stupid, child-like grin on his face. Stosh, on the other hand, has an expression of complete horror. Zack jumps up and runs outside as Stosh tries to mentally pick up the pieces of his shattered brain.
