Streets of Pittsburg; Supernova MNM RP#2

Oct 10 2008

Supernova

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Vega Knight is currently cruising around Pittsburg in his Cadillac, just enjoying the scenery. He currently is smoking a cigarette, when he flips on the Radio. it's on the local sports channel Sport DJ: Don't forget folks that October thirteenth is the official day where MWE Monday Night Mayhem will be held in the Mellon Arena. Alot of big matches are there, but there is one that stands out in my mind, and that is the Triple threat X-TV title match between Aeron Beowulf, JJ Executioner, and the man my money is on, 'Supernova' Vega Knight. Vega smirks at this mention, as he flicks his cigarette away. Hearing enough, he shuts the radio off,before pulling into the hotel he was staying at. About 20 minutes later, Vega is currently inside the weight training room. He is currently benching around two hundred pounds. Jeff is there to bench him. Jeff: So Vega, think you got the message across with your promo you did a couple days ago? Vega: Of course. I mean it had everything. Vega lifts the barbell onto it's stance, having completed a set, and taking a brief rest before starting again. Jeff: Well I heard that JJ had some choice words from his most recent promo......though I doubt if you can call it a promo. Vega: I saw it. Vega continue to bench until this set is done. Once it is done, he places the barbell on its hooks, and takes a huge breath, before taking a major gulp of gatorade down. Vega: It was mostly just Shawn there. I mean.......I applaude how well Spitzer's acting skills are, but c'mon, how much did he have to get paid do even faint like he did? Jeff: I don't know, nor do I care. What I know is, that was quite possibly, the most lamest promo I've ever had the unfortunate pleasure of viewing. Vega: Tell me about it. I mean, what can JJ really do? He has yet to really impress somebody in forms of a real promo. He's a monotone retarded piece of crap, just like I've been saying all this time. Vega shakes his head in disappointment. He notices a tear in his wifebeater, and just tears it off. Everyone else in the room stopped to look at Vega's chiseled physique. All he had on now, were a pair of knee length, skin tight black shorts, and training gloves. Jeff had a similar set of clothing on, except he still had his shirt on. Jeff sighed, at the reaction from everyone in the room. Jeff: It's not hard to believe that at all man, but people still buy into it. Now your most recent promo. Now THAT was good. It had everything you could ask for. best friends playing cards, women, and pure, raw emotion, coming from a guy who likes to joke around, but is serious when it comes to work.Vega smiled at that. He takes his towel and pats himself down. The two friends leave the weight room. Now there were some females in the room, and when Vega left, the two friends could hear the women give out wolf whistles. Back up in Vega's hotel room, the three buddies recently got dressed up into their bar hopping gear. Matt: Shouldn't one of us be the designated driver for tonight? The three laugh at that sudden question. the only designated driver the three of them needed was a cab driver. Matt got the camera, as the three headed out. Once outside, and the three were walking, Matt turned the Camera on, ready to get more of Vega's thoughts. Matt: Yo Vega. I'm sure MWE would love to know more about what's going to happen this coming Monday Night Mayhem. Vega: Let's see. It's Triple Threat match, for the X TV title. In One corner, the title the three want to take home. In another, is the champion, Aeron Beowulf, and I just can't seem to wrap my mind around why the fuck he specifically dresses up like a freak. I mean, this guy has so much more potential, yet would rather waste his time looking like a freak. Mind games are good, but his attempt at is just amazes me on how fucken pathetic it can be. Vega takes a breath after saying that, before he continues. Vega: In another corner, we have JJ Executioner. A man who can only be described as......shit I can't think of anything to describe him in a simple manner. What I see him as is a man that doesn't belong. He seeks attention worse then a child does, even going as far as dressing up like a madman. I would believe him if he actually put something call emotion into it. I cry when I see one of his promos. It's just.....bland. The man has a bad inferiority complex, it isn't even funny. He has sought attention so badly, he went to as far as stealing the title belt, knowing full well that he would never be able to win one. Vega then stops for a minute. All three friends smirk at the pure truthfulness of Vega's words. Vega: And in the last corner is me. The future of MWE, 'Supernova' Vega Knight. A man who people say was outclassed last week, but proved he is more then capable of going the distance. See, I proved that I can hang with the big boys very easily. Also, have any of you guys ever seen a newcomer like me get such a crowd reaction? Both Matt and Jeff look at each other, and shake their heads, indicating that they haven't. Vega: Thought so. See I'm a mixture of many famous wrestlers. I have the prankster attitude of every member of DX that has ever been. Combine that with the 'fuck you' attitude of Steve Austin. Along with charisma like The Rock. Jeff: Don't forget you were trained in a dungeon like environment. Matt: Yeah, not to mention that JJ was right about one thing. Vega: And that is? Matt: You have a bigger ego then DC and Brian Michaels. Vega: Well I don't like to sound like it, but fuck yeah. Of course I got a bigger fucken ego then them. I could have told you that before. Anyways...we're here to have some fun, let's get some booze. The three walk into a nearby bar. Vega orders them a bear each. That is when a random dude, albeit a semi-strong one (wore what looked like redneck clothes) came up to Vega. Redneck: So you're uh...Vega Knight..right? Vega: I am. You want my autograph? Redneck: Ah hell naw man. I just wanted to say that I don't believe that you got a chance in hell to win the match. Vega sighed, shaking his head, before chugging back his beer. Vega: You're entitled to your opinion, but keep it to yourself man. You just spewed crap all over my good clothes. Vega then did a dusting off motion. Matt (still filming), Jeff, and the Redneck all followed the motion. Then, Vega curled his right index and thumb right at his crotch. The three saw it, and Matt and Jeff got it. Vega: BALLGAZERS!!!!!!!!!!! Vega, Matt and Jeff all began to laugh out loud, while the Redneck just stood there, stunned. The Redneck took a massive swig of his beer. Matt: Man, now you can say you....OWNED the world Vega: Damn right, I OWN the world bitches. I also own the local redneck here. The redneck had enough, and he punched Vega across the face. The whole bar heard it, and went silent. Vega looked a bit stunned. Vega: You know, I can sue you for that if I wanted to. Vega then finishes his beer, and smirks. Vega: On the other hand...... Vega responded to the punch, with one of his own. The two were now both up, and fists were flying everywhere, as the redneck kept launching punches at Vega, but only a couple hit, including one to the gut. Redneck: HA! If I can beat you here and now, what makes you think that you will win your match? Vega: Is that the best you got? If it is, well then I'm just gonna git-er-done quickly. Vega then connected with a fist to the solar plexus, before grabbing the guy, and slamming his head into the wall. Matt: That's gonna leave a mark. Jeff: Think we should stop Vega? Matt: Hell no. This is real entertainment. The Redneck looks a bit dazed, as Vega jokingly looks at his watch. Vega: Now would you look at that? You're about three seconds away from me pulling your britches over your head..........DING! Vega then gives the Redneck an atomic wedgie, before slamming him against the wall again. Vega then looks into the camera. Vega: JJ, Aeron, see this mess? This is what will be happening to you come Mayhem. Pay close attention, as what's going to happen next is what will be part of your fate. Vega then gives a curb stomp kick to the back of the redneck's head, making the redneck's head bounce ff the wall. Bartender: Heh......it's no wonder. That guy keeps coming in here to fight. It's about time he gets his just dues. Vega: I guess so. I shouldn't have taken it so far tho, but fuck...I'm pissed off. Oh well. Vega then opens up his wallet, and takes out a thousand dollars, and stuffs it into the redneck's pocket. Vega: That's for the hospital bill. Vega then climbs up onto the bar. Matt focuses the camera at the right position, making it seem like Vega was a godly figure. Vega: Listen up y'all. I am 'Supernova' Vega Knight. I am the most entertaining motherfucken wrestler that the MWE has ever seen. I'm the one guy who will do what I just did, and talk about it proudly for quite some time. Vega then signals for another bottle of beer. The Bartender hands him one, as Vega begins to chug it down. Vega: I'm the kind of guy who will not hesitate to start a food fight if I felt like it. I am going to be 'The Man' of MWE, and bring it to newer heights that without me, would never be possible. Vega then chugs some more of his beer down, leaving it at half full. Everyone in the bar is fully paying attention to him. Vega: Above and beyond everything else, I am the next motherfucken MWE X-TV champion, and without question, I'll put both Aeron Beowulf's and DC's reigns to shame, while I continue to break bodies, rake up wins, and satisfy the violent needs of each any every one of you, and the lust from all the ladies. AH HELLS YEAH!!!!!!!!!!! The whole bar gave a huge standing ovation to Vega, just as he hopped down, chugging the rest of his beer. Vega: Now who wants to outdrink me? Vega sat down, as a biker dude then came and sat down. The Bartender then places a bunch of different open beers and two big plates of various flavors chicken wings. Jeff then moves infront of the camera for a second. Jeff: Ladies and gentlemen, boys and girls, children of all ages. What you're about to witness, is the real sport of the drinkers. A chicken and beer contest. Things are about to get MESSY!!!!!!!! A few minutes later......... The Biker dude was having a hard time keeping his beer in, while Vega had little problem. In fact, Vega was now chugging beer from a pitcher. the Camera could see at least tw plates of chicken wings done on both sides, while the biker had fifteen beers down. Vega had 20 finished, and was drinking from a fricken pitcher! Bar: CHUG CHUG CHUG CHUG!!!!!!!!! Vega did finish the pitcher. By now, the Biker guy had passed out, as the whole bar erupted in massive cheers, as a couple of them raised Vega's arms in victory. Jeff: Here is your winner. 'Supernova' Vega Knight!!!!!!!! Matt: That was good. But we have to get him ready for Monday. Jeff then slung one arm of a very much drunk Vega over his shoulder, helping Vega walk out. Vega: That was fuuuuunnnn. Matt: We Know Vega. But hey, when you take the X-TV title, we're gonna really party hard Vega: FUCK YEEAAAAAHHHHHH!!!!!!!!!!!! END
