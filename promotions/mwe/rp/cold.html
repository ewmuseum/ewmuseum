Cold; Darkness Falls, Insanity Rises

Aug 14 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
***Sorry about the formatting, something unexpected happened to me today so I didn't have time to do so.*** -In life we’re told to always look into the future, to utilize the future as a tool to help your present. The past is something to ignore but to never forget. When we look back into the past, we look into the greatest moments of our lives and we look into our downfalls. The past is exaggerated; the past is black or white without a shade of grey. Sometimes we lead ourselves away from what we used to be, back when we used to be successful, when we used to be comfortable in life, when we were at our best. We take decisions that alter our results, which makes us realize that we were born to live in our own individual style. The ceiling fan is running, the blades keep turning making the most consistent noise among the living room of one Jay Riddick. The only other sound is the silent footsteps that emphasize the creaky hardwood floors. Riddick finds himself right next to several drawers of his office desk. Seeing as he has nothing occupying his time he decides to peruse his way through whatever can be found in those drawers. He takes a seat on his comfy black leather office chair and opens the drawer revealing several content to be found inside of it. The first item that catches his eye is a picture. He places it in his fingers, a picture of his child, David. Suddenly his eyes begin to glimmer as the picture really speaks to him. A child he could’ve raised, someone that could’ve looked up to him. Somebody who could have faith in him and somebody who could believe in him. He then remembers about the text message he’s received from Alexa, the message read: QUOTE I can no longer live a lie, and if I can’t live this lie than I can no longer bare to tell you. Please come back Jay, and you’ll see. One day Riddick, one day. Alexa Riddick is pondering on what that particular message could’ve meant. But all he could do is look at that picture of his child, and suddenly negativity took over his mind. All he could think about it what was taken away from him. Not just what Alexa took away from him, but what the world has taken away from him. Dignity, respect, and a chance to become somebody people cared about.- Riddick: What can it be Alexa!? What lie do you keep within you that you cannot tell me!? Am I not a real human being with real emotions!? Am I the reason for your misery!? Are you sorry you ever got involved in my life!? Tell me damnit! Tell me! -Talking to someone that isn’t there, not only talking, yelling at the top of his lungs hoping someone will hear his plea, he wants Alexa to hear it, he wants her to absorb each and every word he has said. His veins are exposed to plain sight. He’s breathing heavily, his eyes are wide open. His world around him has all turned on him through his eyes. His lips are sealed as his tongue is as willing as it ever has to speak, however Riddick is stopping himself, he knows that he is near the point of crossing the line. It’s no use, temptation defeats his strength inside. His blood is boiling, his muscles are tensing and much like when the world started with the big bang, a new world of thought was created by the office desk being thrown over.- Riddick: GOD DAMNIT! -Riddick goes on a rampage, breaking objects, throwing things, and destroying anything in sight. Breaking items such as his computer, his television, his glass ornaments around the room, his furniture, and his portraits on the wall just to name a few. He finally came to a halt, falling to his knees in the middle of his living room, livid, breathing heavier than his own body weight. Everything has been demolished, everything has been destroyed. The only thing in tact was the ceiling fan making an air noise throughout the room as it always has. The sun is gleaming through the window, shining a light onto his left side of his face. But at this time not even Riddick can accept the light into his house or even into his life. He crawls ever so slowly to the window, he goes to pull the curtain over the window but the curtain rips.- Riddick: God damnit! I don’t want the light! Stop! -Riddick takes the flipped over coffee table and places it over the window blocking whatever light was coming into the room from the sun. Darkness is all that can be seen by perception. Not even Riddick can be seen. The havoc, the mess, the destruction he has caused like a storm raiding into a room cannot be seen because of the darkness. Not only is the visual darkness is being witnessed but the darkness of thought shadows over Riddick’s corrupted world.- You wanna bring me down!? I’ve heard you people talk! I’ve heard you people talk... My friends, my peers, the people I step into the ring with. I’ve heard them talk. I’ve heard them talk.... I heard them... They didn’t even have the balls to say it to my face... They said that I don’t deserve my championship. They say I was handed the championship without earning it. I’VE EARNED IT DAMNIT! I’VE....EARNED...IT!!! I went undefeated up into my championship match, does that not count for anything!? The words you say, those words they hurt me. And it gets to the point where I don’t care about defending the honour of this championship. All it makes me want to do is collect your blood and to savour it on my lips. You heard me! I want your blood! I want the taste of my opponents blood to make my skin linger, to give me new life, to give me a hunger, to control temptation. I want to be addicted to it. I never want to be away from the blood of others. I am not a monster, and I don’t belong in this cage. People forget... They forget all the time... But they remember... They remember my losses... They remember my losses! -Because of the darkness, sound has become more heavily defined. The sound of Riddick’s heavy breathing is almost as loud as his words. And once again the humming of the ceiling fan is heard casting it’s wind within the room.- A man once said, let he without sin cast the first stone. I am not the only one who has never tasted the bitter sweetness of defeat. Among me are billions of others in this world that have tasted that failure. I want to be free from this torture, I want to live my desired life once again! And in order to do that, I have to do something for myself. -The table being held up against the window tips over, and the light is once again exposed within the room. The bright light of the sun beaming into Riddick’s eye distorting his vision. Causing Riddick to look away from the light and there he sees it. In the middle of the room, a book sitting on the floor that has been thrown out of the desk drawer. The light beaming onto it almost as if a sign from a greater power. A brown hardcover book lying on the floor. Riddick crawls over moving his hands and knees cautiously as if he were afraid something were to happen if he made any sudden movement. He finally reaches over to the book, grasping it within his grip. He opens the book and lays it on the floor wide open. His body is over the book on his hands and knees almost as if he pounced it straight to the ground. His eyes beam onto the words revealing to himself that the book is his chronicles, his stories, his memoirs. Riddick is reading the passage out loud to a room where he is the only one there.- QUOTE Journal Entry: June 1st 2008 Two types of people stand in a divide, They may be worlds apart, Or the may be directly beside each other. One will be told where to go, To be told when to leave or where to hide. The other will stand alone, He will make his own independent decisions. I am the type of man that stands alone. I am a man that lives and dies to be alone. My success if found within the confinements of my own personal advice and psychology. I write in divided sentences because I want each statement to be evident. Nobody will deny me of my rights. A soldier is only as good as his reactions that lead to his fate. With the gun pointed in his face he receives no dictation from his brother in arms. The hype, the final moment of judgement relies on your greater instincts. I am the one man army. I do not want brothers in arms, I don’t want an excuse. If I fail I want it to be my responsibility, not those of others. These are the words I live by, Jay Riddick Riddick: The truth has been spoken! Finally the truth has been spoken! A revelation heard, and a revelation understood. My one failure that has been occurring lately has always been trust. Virus, Oblivion, this week you step into the ring with me as both your partners. And as partners it should be obligatory that I watch both your backs and you watch mine. That we have to work together as a cohesive unit to defeat our opponents. However the truth is when we step into that ring, I am going to do what I was always good at. Watching my own back, doing my own thing not giving a damn about either of you. Because the truth is I don’t have to hear you, I don’t have to see you because I already know that none of you believe in me. THIS IS MY TIME DAMNIT!!!! I worked my ass off for a long time going undefeated, and now I’m falling, falling because not one of you or even anyone cares or even believes in me! I’m going to do this on my own, stay out of my way! Now as far as my opponents go, Warner, Jay & CSB. I’m not going to waste my time with this anymore. I’m not going to target you people individually because you’re all the same! You’re all the same damn people as everyone else in this world! If this were months ago, I’d sneak my way into your minds. I’d manipulate you mentally and make you imbeciles crack before our match up. But what is the point? The insider reports will claim I’m boring, they’ll claim I’m not doing what every wrestler in the MWE has done before each and every match up by having a war of words, but you know what? I have a weapon. And I believe in this weapon so much that I am willing to tell you three what it is. Zak, Jai and CSB, my greatest weapon is a two in one contraption. My secret weapon is all three of you as one. Don’t understand, you think I’m going crazy? I’m not going crazy, I’m seeing as clear as day. This light outside shining into my vision was blinding me physically but mentally opening a new window of belief. But perhaps, just maybe I am crazy because I’ll explain to you how this weapon will work. You three much like my partners going into the match this week much like everyone that has ever known me, none of you people believe in me. But wait that’s not where the ride stops, you see at one point when I kept on winning and winning and winning you people had faith in me, you people believed in me whether you like to admit it or not. So that leaves us with a question to ponder, if I once possessed the gift of your belief, does that not mean I have the capabilities? Maybe it’ll be this week, maybe it’ll be now, or maybe it’ll be in the future. But god damnit I will have it back. I can rise from the ashes once again, and you can claim I don’t deserve to be champion because I was “handed” the championship, but until somebody beats me for that championship, none of you can say a god damn thing! -Riddick continues breathing heavily after venting his anger for a long period of time. His words made such an impact, they were so pronounced that not a thing occurred or was even noticed during that time. The room may not have been dark, but all concentration was on Riddick. Riddick is alone. Alone in a room that he has destroyed himself. You can believe that every man can find somebody to talk to, can go somewhere to raise his social status, but Riddick is a man that even he’ll admit is an army of one. Perhaps his words were overwhelming, perhaps he truly is mentally ill as every muscles in Riddick’s body has numbed. Riddick collapses on the floor. Laying there out cold and unconscious, alone in his living room. Several hours later, the one thing that awakens Riddick is his cell phone going off, Riddick is groggy and has forgotten where he is. He checks out his phone to read the message.- QUOTE Jay, I came to your door, finally having the courage to tell you the truth so I came to your door knowing you were there because your car was parked in your driveway, but you didn’t answer. What does that say if you can’t respect my courage? What value do you put on truth? Maybe it’s too late Jay, maybe you’ll never find out. I’ll find you, someday... Alexa -Riddick looks in shock at his phone as he forces himself to stand on both his feet. He stumbles as he attempts to rush his way to the front door but his body keeps falling against the walls. Using his hands to hold himself up by grabbing various objects. Riddick finally makes his way out the front door and runs to his car parked in the drive way. He opens the car door and tumbles into the driver’s seat chest first, climbing into the seat as fast as he can. He searches his pockets for his keys and pulls everything out until he finds them. He pulls them out of his pocket and tries turning the car on as quick as he can but the engine keeps stalling, unfortunately it looks like he blew his transmission. Riddick starts pounding his fists on the dashboard.- Riddick: Fuck! God damnit! Why!? I have to see her! What do you want to tell me Alexa!? Tell me what you want!? Tell me! -Riddick begins to shed a single tear, in his car, much like his living room, isolated from the possibility of somebody caring for him. In his many years of life, nobody has ever knocked on his door that has cared about him, and he can tell Alexa does.- Riddick: Army of one in this war we call life. In this world born to be isolated, born without love... This is my world... But this world is a cold place... It’s a cold place... It’s cold...
