Out to lunch; be back later.

Aug 21 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The scene opens with Aeron laid back in a recliner. A TV is seen in front of him, the tape that Fear had aired. Aeron still smug about it, chuckles to himself as he yawns loudly and stretches. He looks around his messy hotel room. Dishes scattered everywhere, papers and wrappers all over the desk. He gets up slowly as he kicks the leg rest back into the chair. He walks around rubbing his eyes as if he had just woke up. Aeron: Fear, I commend you on the actor, but do you really think that people would think that I would make out with Gus' girlfriend? I'll let him as well as Jekyl take care of your little comments themselfs. Aeron smiles as he walks into the bathroom. He picked up his toothbrush and turned the cold water tap to start the water. He looked around for the toothpaste, he looked in the medicine cabinet where it was laying against the side. Aeron: Ah, there we are. Aeron takes the cap off the tooth paste and squeezes the tube and lets the toothpaste fall on the toothbrush. He starts to brush his teeth, his words slurred. Aeron: And do you really think that "Your a bitch" and "That was hurtful" is going t- He pauses and spits the mixture of saliva and toothpaste into the sink and continues. Aeron: Going to affect me at all? Your giving me what I want, your giving me the satisfaction of knowing that I hurt you. It fuels me like gas fuels cars. He spits the rest of the toothpaste into the sink, running his toothbrush under the running water to clean the brush off as he places it back in the holder. He takes a small cup and fills it half way with water. Aeron: This reminds me of an old saying, The optimist sees the glass as half full but the pessimist sees the glass as half empty. You know what I think? It doesn't matter what you think, you could be the optimist or the pessimist but either way, it all ends up the same way. Aeron taps on the glass with his finger a few times before taking a large gulp of it. He forces it down his throat. Aeron: Wow, the water here is horrible, remind me to talk to head office about that. Anyway back on track, it ends in one way, with me on top. Knowing your going to attempt to make a sexual reference on that to turn it against me, let me just say that if you have to stoop as low as twisting words like a contortionist. You must have been dropped on your head by your mom as a kid, Oh wait, no thats right, your mom just thought you were crazy and your dad wanted you killed. Or was it the other way around? Trying to drop a SOB story over live TV, may do well with the lady viewers, but think about it, how many girls actually watch and like wrestling? Exactly. Aeron cupped his hands and placed it under the cascading water. He took his hands out from under when it had some water in it. He closed his eyes and splashed the water on his face. The water splashed everywhere as if he was hit was a water balloon. He wipes his face dry with a towel as he walks out of the bathroom and opens the walk-in closet with a single hand as he starts talking again. Aeron: Leaving home at 16, what teenager hasn't? Just cause your mom was crazy and got you two into a car crash, probably scrambling that egg head of yours into nothing, doesn't mean people are going to feel sympathy for you. Sadly only one survived, even worse, it was you. Aeron pulls out a suit and compares it to himself before walking back into the bathroom locking the door. His voice not as loud being blocked by the chestnut door. Aeron: The outfit, why does a man wear earrings in both ears when its supposed to be placed on the left ear. That chain too, the one that says FEAR, something sort of like this. Aeron steps out wearing a tuxedo and walks towards a buffet opened a drawer and shuffled through it and pulled out one of the Fear merchandise chains. He holds it up and grasps it in his hands and starts squeezing it. A snap is heard as Aeron opens his hand letting pieces of the chain fall the the floor. He slaps his hands together getting rid of the dust. He smiles slightly as he exhales. Aeron: Too bad that wasn't the real "Fear" but it will be, cause I'm tired of these poppycock shenanigans. Aeron sighs he rubs some dust off the knees of the pants before opening the door. He walks out closing the door behind the cameraman. He stops at the elevator and presses the call button as he fixes the cuffs. The door dings open as Aeron steps in, he waves the cameraman in. He presses the ground floor button as he taps his foot waiting for it to stop. The elevator stops a few floors up from the ground level. Someone steps in and stands silently for a few seconds. The door closes as the person stood there in a shirt and some jeans. He spoke frantically Fan: Hey, I recognize you, from MWE! Aeron Beowulf! Can I have your autograph? Aeron groaned as he pulled out a pen from the tuxedo pocket. He looks at the pen, than back at the fan and repeats it a few times like hes trying to decide something. He puts the pen back in his shirt pocket and pulls out a ring. The fan gasps loudly and looks at Aeron wide-eyed. Fan: For me! Aeron: Sort of, Aeron smirked as put the ring on. The fan looked confused as he looked down. Aeron pulled his arm back and then punched the fan in the arm leaving the ring shape and the starting of a bruise. The fan jumped back in pain and started rubbing his arm. Fan: What was that for!? Aeron chuckled to himself as he pointed to the fans arm. The fan gave him a wierd look before looking at his arm. He seen the ring imprint. The door dinged open as the fan walked out smiling. Still slightly wincing in pain, but happy nonetheless. Aeron laughed as he fixed his collar and started to walk out. He continued on walking towards the door. The automatic door slid open squeaking slightly as he stepped outside and pulled out a pair of shades and put them on. He walked to the parking lot and walked up to a red Ferrari FFX with a white racing striped own the middle. Aeron smiles as he pulls out his keys. Aeron: I dunno about the rest of you but I'd rather have my ground transport paid for than spend time in a plane. He unlocks the car and opens the car door for the cameraman. Shortly after, he walked to the driver side door and opened it. He closed it and put the keys in the ignition. He turned them quickly and set up the air conditioning and buckled his seatbelt. He pulled out of the parking lot. Cameraman: So where are we going? Aeron turned and looked at him, letting the sun glasses slide down his nose. Aeron: To lunch, where else? The cameraman shrugs as the two drive for about twenty minutes where they pull into a parking lot of a large plaza. Aeron pushes his sunshades back onto the top of the bridge of his nose with a single finger before unbuckling his seat belt and turning the car off after pulling into park. He opens the door and closes it as he walks across the front of the car towards the cameramans door. He opens the door and helps the cameraman out. He closes the door before scanning the plaza for a restaurant. Aeron gives a small enlightened sigh as he starts walking towards an East Side Marios. The two approach the front door as he looks up and smiles. They walk through the front door waiting at the desk for some service. A waitress walks towards them. Waitress: Welcome to East Side Marios', Can I help you? Aeron: No, only here to browse. Aeron rolls his eyes as the waitress starts to walk off. Aeron: No, I'm just kidding, a table for 2 please. The waitress scoffs at Aeron before picking up two menus and showing them to their booth. Aeron slides in one of the seats, the cameraman slides in at the opposite side of Aeron. The waitress' places the menus in front of them and walks off with a smug look on her face. Aeron picks up the menu and starts browsing through what they had. The cameraman put down his camera and had it facing Aeron and the edge of the seat. He also started to look through the menu. The waitress returned a few minutes later. Waitress: You two ready to order? Aeron looked at the cameraman who nods. Aeron: Yes, I'll have a chicken caeser salad with a balsamic vinaigrette dressing and a Smirnoff. The waitress scribbles the order on her pad as she look at the cameraman. Waitress: Anything for you sir? Cameraman: Yes, I'll have a crispy chicken wrap and a cola please. The waitress nods and walks off. Aeron: So, Fear, do you really think that a big ape like you can really beat me? I beat you last week, and I'll do it again. Cause you just don't have any strategy. Your like George Bush. You don't think about your actions or their consequences. You just rush at things like a tank. Bum rushing people wont get you anywhere in this business. You shouldn't even be here, you should be back in the jungle eating bananas and throwing your own shit at other apes. Some of the other patrons that had heard the comments Aeron had been making shivered and spit into their napkins in disgust. Aeron was tapped on the shoulder by a man behind him. Aeron turned around and had recognized the face right off the bat. Aeron: Hey, aren't you that cop that arrested me in my hotel room? Officer: Why yes I am. Aeron: Oh well in that case by all means...Screw you. Aeron turns around laughing heartily flipping the cop off. The waitress returned placing the appropriate meals in front of the two men. The cameraman starts wolfing down his chicken wrap as Aeron starts jabbing his fork into his salad and pulling it out and shoving it in his mouth as he bites down on his fork and scraping his teeth against the cold metal making a shrieking sound. Everyone around them had shivers sent down their spine. Aeron opens his Smirnoff with the pen he had in his pocket which was seen earlier. The cap went flying into the air and angled backwards and hit the cop in the back of the head. Officer: Hey punk, want to go back? Aeron: What for? Officer: Hitting me, what else you idiot. Aeron sighs and puts his head in his hand tsking the officer. The cameraman takes a large gulp of what was in his mouth washing it down with cola and raised his hand. Cameraman: I've got it all on tape. The cameraman smiles ear to ear before switching the camera to an angle where both Aeron and the cop could see. He hit the rewind and play buttons at corresponding times showing the Smirnoff lid falling down from the air. Both Aeron and the cameraman were smiling. Aeron: See! Officer: Shut up.... Aeron started laughing and choking on some salad. He coughed a few times beating on his chest. He swallowed what was in his throat before giving the cameraman props. The two laugh and talk for a few minutes before the cameraman finishes his wrap. The waitress comes by, Aeron stops her. Aeron: Bill please. The waitress sighs and walks off promptly not wanting to be near him for long. Its a quiet silent near him with just forks, knives and cups hitting the tables, or the plates as families reminisce. The waitress comes back and slams the bill down on the table before walking off angrily. Aeron and the cameraman look at each other and then at the waitress and then to the bill and back to each other in unison. Aeron shrugs as he raises an eyebrow pulling out his wallet. He shuffles through it pulling out thirty dollars worth of tens. He puts them on the bill slate before standing up, stretching with a large yawn. He picks up his salad bowl and taps on the cops shoulder. The cop turns around sighing lazily. Officer: What do you want. Aeron smiles and places the bowl and the rest of its contents on the cops head, with the balsamic running down his face. The cameraman and Aeron share a joyous laugh as they walk out. The cop tries to stop them in his seat but gives up quite quickly. Officer: At least he has good tastes... The cameraman runs back to their table picking up the camera. He points it at his face and smiles. Cameraman: Almost forgot you guys! He runs back to Aeron who's already at the car tapping his foot waiting. He finally gets there as Aeron sits in the car and unlocks the other door. The cameraman steps in and wipes his forehead of sweat. Cameraman: Damn these cameras get fuckin heavy. Aeron: Wimp. Aeron rolls his eyes putting his air conditioning on full blast. Aeron: Back there, there were families, something you complained about Fear, saying you ran away, your dad hated you, your mom was a psycho, but your lucky. You had a family, my dad died at a young age and my mom dropped me off at summer camp and never came to pick me up. I braved the wilderness until I came across a nice big bar, they let me stay in one of their rooms for a small fee which I earned from working there. What job did I do you may ask? Bouncer. Aeron flexed his muscles as he puts the ignition key in and turns it revving the car up. Aeron: If there was a fight, I broke it up, and one night. A fight that had broken out had a manager of an underground street fighting league. He was impressed, but not as impressed as the bar owner was. He was like my parents. He was the only respectable father figure around. Of course he was sad to see me leave but everyone has to make sacrifices. I went into that underground fighting league coming out on top on 70% of my matches. Aeron licks his lips wishing he still had his Smirnoff with him as he takes a hard left as the cameraman jolts against the door. Aeron laughs to himself. Aeron: Shit, you ok? Cameraman: Yeah... Aeron: Good, keep filming. Anyway I went back to the bar eventually. Dad as I called him, was very happy to see me again. But it wasn't at his bar, it was at a funeral where he was buried. Someone had burned the bar down one day. He had a smile on his face while he lied motionless in his casket. I wasn't there to protect him so I blamed myself. I wanted to right my mistake. But I couldn't so I'm going to right another mistake, you not being dead in that car crash. I will make you suffer, I will make sure our paths never cross again. The scene fades with Aeron doing doughnuts in an empty parking lot.
