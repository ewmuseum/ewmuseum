Main Event Worthy; Here we go

May 29 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
There is a black void of darkness and nothing can be heard or seen. But if you listen closely, you start to gradually apprehend the sound of an object, possibly a blunt object, being slammed or shoved on a rough surface. The sound stops, and after four seconds of blank singularity a light bulb is flicked on. This creates a shortened ray of light beaming over a dirty and splintered wooden table. But the table is not alone, it has company, the company of a man that has shown time and time again, what he’s made of. The man is Sinc Mercier, he is in nothing but a pair of white sweats, and the object that has been banging against the surface of this table, is a very sharp steak knife. One more swift work of the hand and…. THUD! The knife is thrown a final time into the bewildered wooden mass and sticks there like a thorn to a side. Next to the stricken knife is a black rag, the perfect length to put around your head, but what could this be for? The explanation is upon us. He leans back in his chair and bathes in the light from the bulb as everything about him at this moment, seems to be boiling with twisted pleasure. His big blue eyes seem to brighten up and perk into two well rounded illuminating dots, his hands tense up into two shriveled raisins, his legs stagger in a waver of rigid conception, and his hair isn’t even gelled or messed with at all. He is settled, and his words begin tantalizing all over the room walls. I’ve finally made it so it seems, the infamous MWE world title, is now but a victory away. But there are those who don’t believe the world title belongs to me, because the US title is more “my level” right? That’s some stereotypical nonsense if I have ever heard it. Let me point something out….. Reaching under his furbished chair Sinc bears his hands over something large and pulls out his United States title. He plops it on the table and stares at it for what perceives as an eternity, an endless gaze into the only title he has ever won, four times no less, but this doesn’t seem to please him. Every story ever told has always had an ending, and every ending ever finished, has always been questioned or pondered over more than once. This story, has no ending, it starts with a kid on his path to comedy stardom, and continues with a middle aged homosapien making it to the highest peak in his chaotic career. But he’s heard the voices, the words, the whispers…..in the eyes of the star struck and bitter media, he isn’t human at all. He is a horn dog and an animal trying to get laid and aiming to make some quick money, and that my friends, just isn’t acceptable to him. You think….that because of this patriotic center piece…that I could never and WILL never get a world title shot? If you think that, and this goes for anybody, then you have another thing coming. He stands self reliant and malevolent, it’s like a surge of electricity has just been shot down his spine and he has just realized something very important. For years this kid has grown up thinking he will never live to see the day he can make himself into a famed figure, and for years he has been spoon fed the same lies and fables that a kid would take to heart in a split second. Now he makes his own choices, this is HIS life….people need to stay out of it and LET….HIM…LIVE. HE IS NOT the pudgy faced boy on the streets of California anymore, he is his own franchise now, and it is just starting to stand out, finally his time has come. The US title begins to laugh at him as it sits in a secretive threat on the table at his side, he picks it up, stares it down with a saddened and prolonged wretchedness, and then powerfully tosses it at the wall in front of him. The gold plating clacks against the hard bricks as dust and rubble withers away from the connecting impact. THAT filth, that colorful toy, has been holding me back for centuries! I don’t need it, I’m sick and TIRED of it always being a part of my career. Every time I walk by the only thing people remember me by is winning one title, that same title for a quartet of time. I can’t take it anymore! I can’t sit there, and be humble for another nine months, and I guarantee you no one else in this business can either without at least being agitated in some way. The belt is now ignorantly sitting in a pile of dirt and dust specks on the floor in the corner of the room. The darkness overtakes it as it becomes vanished from the small radius of light the bulb up above is giving off. Breaking down and massacring himself into a heap of emotion, Sinc hunkers down in his seat again and places the fingertips of both his hands on his forehead as he looks to the table in a dazed mind frame, everything he has ever been known for….has always been revolved around that damn title, and of course his comedic personality, but he wants something more, something you can’t easily obtain, something more deserving and rewarding, and that something is the MWE title. I want to give the fans something spectacular….something worthwhile. At last I can shove all the angry evil doer’s and ego smashers aside, and have a decent, straight to the core, all face all the time, main event match. I could not have ASKED for a better main event, than the one I am partaking in right now. I don’t have to deal with gigantic egos, or the I hate you, you hate me ritualistic rants, all I have to deal with is the nerve racking process of winning and wrestling with some of the best competitors MWE has to offer. And that….is how the business should be, that is how it should always be. Sinc grasps the handle of the knife and pulls it out of the wood, the sharpness of such an item has left a large gash to the rough surface, splinters begin to expand out of the gash and branch to different directions. He looks at the knife with a deprived empathy, he looks at it feeling an instantaneous troubled emotion deep inside of him. He begins to twirl it around in his right hand and play with it for a while with his small, nimble fingers. This hypocrisy, this conviction in his hand, may be all that he knows now. He becomes the knife to the sword, he has a small chance to many, but he also has the speed and cunningness for an upper hand, and it could lead to a surprising result, Sinc hates surprises, but in this case, he doesn’t seem to mind at all. While the sword has the power and the popularity of a favored weapon, it still isn’t a sure winner against the keenness of a knife. My purpose is not to make my opponents hate me, my purpose is to make them understand that though their arrogance should always remain strong, they should NEVER under estimate the under dog, because they just might come back and bite you in the ass. Picture the MWE title as the ass, and me biting it as hard as I can, and not letting go until I can get the whole chunk. Because what’s the point of attacking, if you can’t bring the enemy to a full circle demise? Surely you can’t let them stand afterward, because if you do, they will come right back at you with revenge filled eyes and take you out in a perpetual strike, a hindered blow, or a turn of the head, maybe even a stab…in the back? Sinc turns his attention to the black rag on the table and picks it up as he places the knife down firmly. The rag begins to look like a dark form of meaning, a meaning that Sinclair is trying to get across to a lot of people. He closes his eyes as the rag begins to be wrapped around his head, his hands freely at work bundling up the piece of cloth on the head of himself, he has become thrown into a sensational bliss, the predictable and lovable Sinc is now sporadic and unconscionable, his eyes become covered in this hellish blindfold. I want to play a little game with all of you….this is merely for effect, nothing more. I want to show you all how interesting and unpredictable I can become. If you can predict a match, the time of an unforeseen event, or even the future in general, then I dare you, no, I CHALLENGE you to predict this….. He feels around for the knife on the table and thrusts It back into his hand, he spreads his left hand out on the wooden top and stretches out his fingers. There is a good cushion of space between each finger, but the space still seem rather small, the gaps just don’t appear to be wide enough. After he’s done counting and situating himself, he shows a sense of steadiness, everything in his body seems to become solid, and he is not shaking at all, his nerves have settled. And my intentions are not to offend, but to demonstrate one of the biggest points in my entire career, maybe even my entire lifetime, the point that the unpredictable can not always be a bad thing. Let us begin. Slowly Sinc begins to tap the surface and spaces between his fingers with the knife one section at a time, six gaps, six taps of the knife, each time getting faster and faster. He does not sweat, he does not shake or grow weary, he becomes one of the most content people to ever grace this dangerous game. Hazardous and impetuous, this game becomes one of the highest risking maneuvers ever to be attempted, but luckily, he loves taking risks.
