Achieving is Believing; Darius & Boyd RP

Nov 4 2008

Darius & Boyd

A picture is seen which is in a current black and blant image. It takes it's time to fade into picture but it finally does and it is seen to be a picture perfect image. It is seen to be outside a nice general house located in Miami, Florida at about 10AM in the morning. It's your usual house, with the flag pole in the front yard with the American flag hanging loyally from it. All of a sudden a figure walks into picture. It seems to be current MWE Superstar Boyd. He is wearing blue jeans with a red Darius & Boyd hoodie on. He walks up the front yard path and up the steps to the front door of the house. He looks down at his watch and knocks on the door, looking impatient. There is no answer.. He knocks again, but still no answer. Boyd puts his hand on the front door handle and slowly twists it, entering the house. He gets inside and slowly closes the front door behind him. He looks around where he is standing as there is a stack of stairs leading to the second floor of the house, and picture frames on the wall. It seems to be the house of Boyd's tag team partner, Darius as there are many pictures of him on the wall, many of him and his parents. Boyd: DARIUS!? ... DARIUS!? Nothing is heard for a few moments as Boyd begins to be more quiet to see if he can pick anything up. All of a sudden running water is heard. Boyd: DARIUS!? Darius yells back to Boyd. Darius: YEAH? Boyd: WE'VE GOT A PROMO TO SHOOT, LET'S GO. Darius: WAIT A FEW, HAVING A SHOWER HERE. Boyd: OKAY, MAKE IT QUICK. Boyd looks at his watch in impatience as he walks into another room of the house. The room is very much so delicate with clean and non-tarnished pieces of furniture. Boyd softly sits down on a white leather lounge which is behind a small coffee table with the latest edition of the official MWE Magazine. Boyd notices the magazine and swiftly picks it up from the coffee table. The front cover has many notes on articles featured in this magazine, some including Sonny Williams, Project Revolution and the situation with DC and Brian Michaels, but the one that Boyd notices most, reads "DARIUS & BOYD; THE NEXT GENERATION OF GREATNESS?" Boyd's eyes light up as he swiftly searches through the magazine looking for the article on himself and his tag team partner, Darius. It is about mid-way in the magazine as he brings his face closer to the magazine in anticipation to read. QUOTE They're New. They're Young. They ARE Talented! A report on the exciting new duo in Mayhem Wrestling Entertainment, Darius & Boyd. Written by Dylan Read The new and fan-fonded superstars have crashed in the land of Mayhem ready to take the world by excitement and thrill. Their names may be a bit unknown to some at the moment, but in time you will learn to know that they are two very funny and loveable superstars. As for their name, they go by Darius & Boyd. Many people have referred these two young men as extreme potential to rise up the ranks in MWE in god given time. Richard Covell made a statement after he signed the two to his roster that he has put them under a plan to develop as he sees greatness in both of them. The CEO even hinted a possible Darius vs. Boyd Match down the line, but we won't see that for a long time to say the least from given reports. But for now the two have their mindset on making an impact in the MWE Tag Team Division, with more and more teams making the division much stronger than ever before. You might have seen Darius & Boyd in action at Wrestle-Wars when they went against the now forgotten tag duo of The Ortans which saw them win, but I warn you, they've still got plenty more to offer. When we caught up with the tag team duo of Darius & Boyd we asked them a few exclusive questions to help you, the MWE fans get to know a bit more about this exciting and up-rising talent that has been injected into Mayhem Wrestling Entertainment. "What interests you in being apart of Mayhem Wrestling Entertainment at it's best point, EVER?" Darius - Speaking on my own behalf, my interests in being apart of MWE are pretty plain and simple. This is the best federation in this industry at this moment, when you've got guys like David Whitman and DC lacing their boots as your workers, you know you're doing something right. But that's not all of it. I grew up wrestling, it's in my blood and it's something I will always be proud of and to be in such a federation where I can do what I love to do each week, and get PAID, is just awesome. Boyd - Yeah what he said.. I could pretty much say the same except he missed out a pretty vital part why we're here in this industry. I know what it's like to be the person sitting in the crowd watching wrestling, I used to be that person as a kid. I know how satisfying it is to leave the arena entertained with what you saw, that's a big reason why I am here. "Have the two of you got any set goals for your time in this industry?" Boyd - I'd like to say yes because everyone has aspirations and dreams to aspire to but honestly I'm gonna try and take everything as it comes. Who knows really, I'm only 22 years old which means I could possibly be around for another 15 years+ if my bones treat me well. Who knows what could happen, in one years time I could be the MWE World Heavyweight Champion, but I don't know. Darius - A bit similar to Boyd on this too, I've always dreamed of becoming a World Champion, it's every wrestlers dream to be honest. But at the moment I'm trying to focus on the tag team division, there's a lot of fierce competition running around so we really do need to concentrate if we ever want to aspire to bigger and better dreams. "What are your thoughts on the current MWE Tag Team Division?" Boyd - It's strong, no doubt about it. I thought Project Revolution were a really talented tag team and to have seen them knocked off the top of the division was a bit of a surprise, but they certainly lost to two great superstars in Maxamillion Mustafa and Grand Mystique. Darius - It's going to be a tough challenge for the pair of us, for sure, but I think we've got what it takes to pull through some wins. We've developed a bit of a fan base already and hopefully they can help us push through this difficult and tight division that it is. "Finally, what can you promise these fans out of you?" Darius - Hope, Victory and Entertainment. Boyd - Exactly that! This duo of superstars surely have proven their case as worthy superstars to watch week-in-week-out on Monday Night Mayhem. Watch Darius & Boyd this week on Monday Night Mayhem when they take on Maxamillion Mustafa & Grand Mystique in a Non-Title Match. Boyd closes the magazine and gently throws it back onto the coffee table as he looks down at his watch still impatiently waiting for Darius to come down to go to their scheduled promo. He gets up from the lounge and walks towards the front door. He doesn't use or go near the front door, but in front of the stairs awaiting for Darius. All of a sudden a smoking hot women walks from around the corner of the second floor and down the stairs. Boyd notices her and looks incredibly confused. Her hair is wet, indicating she had a shower with Darius. Boyd: Samantha? Samantha: Boyd? Darius turns the corner swiftly after hearing what is going on. He is wearing blue jeans and a tight white Darius & Boyd t-shirt for his promo the two are going to shoot. He has his hand in his hair, quickly gelling it up in a rush. Boyd looks angry and notices Darius and turns to the front door, ready to leave. Darius: I can explain. Boyd turns and looks pissed off. Boyd: I think I've seen enough for any explanation. Samantha: Let me explain. Boyd, me and Darius, we're, we're in.. Darius puts his hand over the mouth of Samantha in a fast motion interrupting her from finishing what she had to say. Darius: We're in a play, she was just over here rehearsing her lines with me. Samantha and Boyd both look confused. Boyd: You, in a play? Bullshit! Samantha: What Boyd said.. Darius scratches his chin, knowing he has been caught in the act. Darius: I'll explain it to you on the way to our promo. Boyd: Whatever, let's just go. Boyd opens the door and walks out as Darius sneakily kisses Samantha on the lips and walks to the front door. Darius: This is our little secret. Samantha smiles and bites her finger and nods. Darius closes the door and walks down to the front of his house where a taxi is seen to be parked out the front. Boyd is already talking to the driver through the window. Taxi-Driver: You are already twenty minutes late. Boyd: Yeah and I said sorry. Taxi-Driver: Just get in. Boyd turns around and signals for Darius to get in also. Boyd opens the door of the taxi and enters in as Darius follows. Darius closes the door shut as the taxi begins to drive off and to the destination of the promo. Darius looks at Boyd who is looking out the window gazing. Darius: I can explain man. Boyd slowly turns to look at Darius. Boyd: Explain? Dude I don't think I need to hear one because while you're putting an extra bone in my ex, MY EX, I have been focusing on our match this week, I've been focusing on MWE. I don't think there is any explanation behind what you did, you shagged my ex and it's done. Darius: I've been focusing on MWE too man, but just give me a chance to explain this out to you. Boyd: Here's your chance, don't blow it like Samantha blowed you. Darius scratches his chin whilst looking at Boyd. Darius: Okay well you know that party last night, the one I invited you to and you knocked back? *Boyd nods* Yeah well I was drinking, and truth to say I ended up getting wasted. I didn't know what was going on, and then came along Samantha. She was looking for a good time, so I gave her what she wanted, but I didn't know it was her, it could have been Rosie O'Donnell and I probably still would have done her. Boyd seems to look a lot more understanding now of what had happened with Darius and his ex Samantha. Boyd: Hmm, okay I believe you. But, if you're gonna go around shagging her just keep it a secret from me. Darius's eyes widen up as a smile comes across his face. Darius: Are you giving me permission to, you know with her? Boyd: No I'm not giving you permission.. But anyway our match this week, it's a toughie. Darius: I could say something, but I won't. But yes I agree, Maxamillion Mustafa and Grand Mystique are two very good competitors in-ring. I'm not the smartest guy or nothing but I'm pretty sure if we can edge these twos out this week we will be in line for a shot at them straps. Boyd: Yeah what a story it'd be, only two weeks in MWE and we could be the new MWE Tag Team Champions. Darius puts his hand on Boyd's shoulder. Darius: Calm down Boyd, it'll come in time. Boyd: I know, I know. Darius looks out to the window and then turns back and looks at Boyd. Darius: Ace Cannon.. Boyd looks at Darius with a more serious facial expression. Boyd: What about him? Darius: Nothing I'm still just a little pissed off about what happened after our match at Wrestle-Wars. Boyd: I know how you feel, but it's said and done. That guy is a low life, self centred moron who gets the pleasure of beating down on two superstars that he knows, are only just into their careers and not at his level just yet. You wouldn't see the guy beat down on a face like Brian Michaels or David Whitman. He is a coward, and always will be. Darius: Yeah, I wouldn't mind getting myself some revenge to be honest.. Boyd looks to Darius and makes a smirk. Boyd: So would I, but I know that the best way to get him is in the long run. Sure it'd be great to plant a fist in his face but that wouldn't prove a hell of a lot. Let's just build on from this week and develop our skills, I know for a fact, one day we'll be up against the coward he is. That's when we get payback. Darius: Dude.. Gay. Boyd: Gay? What are you on about, there's nothing homosexual about that? Darius: Nah, except the part where we are trying to live the fairytale story of revenge. Boyd: I thought it was a good idea.. Darius: Well.. Boyd interrupts Darius speaking. Boyd: We're here! Darius: It's about time.. The Taxi-Driver turns around and eyes off at Darius. Taxi-Driver: Excuse me? I was driving as fast as I possibly could sir, there was no way we could get here faster. Darius chuckles at his remark. Darius: Yes there is. Taxi-Driver: Legally? Darius looks at the back of the seat and rubs his chin. He then looks back up at the taxi-driver. Darius: Ahh, no. Taxi-Driver: Point proven. Now the total sum comes to forty six dollars and fifty five cents. Boyd's eyes light up in surprise from the total sum which is much more than he had expected. Boyd: You've got to be kidding me!? Taxi-Driver: Well, I'm not the joking kind. Boyd looks at Darius who is trying to pick something out of his nails. Boyd taps Darius on the shoulder. Darius: What? Boyd: Do you know how much this cab ride is costing us? Darius: Yeah.. And? We're MWE Superstars, we can live the high class ways of life. We've got the money to go blow a few thousand on Rio Tinto shares. Boyd smirks and realises that fact. Boyd: If you say so, you can shout the cash on this one. Darius: Fine, but you can shout the limo ride home. Darius puts his hand into the back pocket of his jeans and pulls out a wallet. He flips open the wallet and searches through to find a 50 buck note. He pulls it out and then flips his wallet back into place before putting it back in his back pocket. He hands the 50 dollar note to the taxi-driver who has a smile on his face now. Darius: You keep the change. Taxi-Driver: A good man you are. Darius: Yes, I've been noted for doing good things with myself. Boyd has a stern look at Darius. Boyd: Don't push it. Darius: What do you me-.. Ohh I get it. The two men step out of the taxi and close the door behind them as the taxi-driver drives off with his vehicle. They look out in front of themselves to see one of the many MWE Headquarters locations. They walk ahead and towards the door of the building. Boyd opens it wide as the two of them walk inside. A counter is seen once you walk into the building with a few workers behind it. Boyd: We're here for our promo. A worker notices Boyd and gets up from her seat and comes around the counter. Worker: Come with me please. Darius and Boyd walk behind the worker who is guiding them towards their promo location. Darius: This is an interview we're doing, right? Boyd: Right. Darius: I'm feeling lucky, I think we're gonna have Sara Patterson interviewing us. Boyd: I hope so, I don't want the guy with waterworks. The two continue following the worker to their promo location. They are about 3 metres behind the worker as she stops at one corner and looks around it, she looks back at Darius & Boyd and notes to them that their promo is taking place there. Darius has his fingers crossed as Boyd has his eyes shut, praying for Sara. They turn the corner to see a interview set up and... Shawn Spitzer. Darius: You have got to be kidding me! Boyd: He's not the joking kind, Darius. Darius & Boyd walk over to the set up, ready to do this interview but still a bit pissed off with the outcome of whom the interviewer is. Shawn notices the two are ready and smiles to them. Shawn Spitzer: Welcome guys. Darius puts his hand on his cheek, wiping away the spit Shawn sprayed at him when talking. Darius: Yeah.. It's just great to be here. Boyd prods Darius to get his attention and then whispers to him. Boyd: Let's just get this over and done with. Darius: We think alike. Shawn Spitzer gets into position on camera as the audio mics go up as the interview is just about to begin in.. 3, 2, 1. Shawn Spitzer: Hi, I'm Shawn Spitzer and today we have two very electrifying superstars on hand ready to answer some questions. I introduce to you, Darius & Boyd! The camera zooms out to see Darius and Boyd standing beside Shawn Spitzer. Boyd: It's good to be here. Darius: Yeah another week, another promo. Shawn Spitzer: Let's get straight to it, your match this week against Maxamillion Mustafa and Grand Mystique. Thoughts? Boyd: This match is gonna be hard. The two men alone have more achievements than any tag team I have ever seen in my life. I can't talk from experience but I can talk from knowledge of my love of wrestling, these two are something special to this industry. Darius buts into what Boyd has to say. Darius: Hold up, hold up. Let's not prance and wallop on about how good these two are. Sure, they've got a good record and sure they've got a career overwhelmed with experience, but we've got what it takes to take them out, to put them into their rightful places. They may be the MWE World Tag Team Champions, but so what. Project Revolution was and they gained a similar reputation to what these two have, only to have it crushed. I know we can do this. Shawn Spitzer: But what do you ha- Boyd interrupts Shawn Spitzer from speaking on. Boyd: Hold on Shawn, your right Darius. And you know what, I saw what they had to say about us and honestly I couldn't give two flying fucks about these guys now that I think about it. I mean one of them thinks he can get a few laughs out of our names, they say we're rednecks when really we're far from that shit. Shawn Spitzer: The draft; your thoughts? Darius and Boyd look sternly at Shawn Spitzer. Darius: Shawn, now is not the time for that. We've got two men, who think they've got the guts to back their words up. Hell they make jokes of us, how bout we pull a few out? Boyd: Alright. What do you get when you throw a midget with a mask and an English man in the ring? Darius: You tell me.. Boyd smirks. Boyd: One fucked up tag team duo. Darius and Boyd laugh as Shawn looks on. Shawn Spitzer: That wasn't funny.. Darius and Boyd suddenly stop laughing and look at Shawn. Darius: No but it was true. We're usually not the cocky, confident dickheads you see waltzing the backstage that are so full of themselves, but going up against these two we need to be different. Sorry about the cliché but we're not gonna put up with shit, no, we're not. Boyd moves closer to the camera with fierce seen in his eyes. Boyd: I look into this camera, as though I'm looking into the eyes of both Maxamillion Mustafa and Grand Mystique. Boys, you may be older than us, you may be more experienced than us and hell you may be better than us.. We'll see about that but what I'm trying to point out is, we won't give up. We don't back down, and when the going get's tough, we will go tougher. Darius: Shawn, if you would just let us finish this off. Shawn agreeingly nods. Darius: This may be just another match to the pair of you, but to us it's a mountain, a journey we need to achieve not only to prove a point, but to prove to ourselves confidence wise that we can do this and we will. So Maxx, Mystique I bet you two are still just waking up in your jammies, watching this with a glass of milk and a bunch of cookies by your bedside but know this, we will beat you, and after this week we will take your championships. Boyd: We won't end this with some homo catchphrase like Cha Ching or Fuck You.. But we will end with this, we're gonna kick your ass and make you feel worse than when John McCain lost Ohio to Obama. That's exactly like that case, these people want change and I say we gonna give it to them. This is our week, this is our time.. This is your time for failure! Darius: Quote and unquote. Boyd and Darius smirk at the camera as Shawn Spitzer is out of picture. Boyd: Bitch.. The scene begins to fade as Darius and Boyd walk off. Promo ends. OOC: I'd really like some feedback on this.. I know it's not my best, but it's something. Feedback please.
