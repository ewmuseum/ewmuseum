Ryan Sharpe: I Don't care I will destroy the big guy you can take skinny, little bro Devlin Sharpe: Are you insinuating that I couldnt take David Bowen on? Ryan Sharpe: Well I dunno Devlin Sharpe: Ill show you Ryan Sharpe: Well tell you what bro? You take big Dave Devlin Sharpe: Batista? Ryan Sharpe: And I will take Weird Al Devlin Sharpe: Yankovic Ryan Sharpe: Cut it out with your crappy references you're starting to tick me off Devlin Sharpe: I am your little brother it's my job to piss you off Ryan Sharpe: Well you are achieving your goal alright...
