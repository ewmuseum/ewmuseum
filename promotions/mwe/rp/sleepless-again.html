Sleepless again.; Cg Rp...The nightmares start.

Aug 28 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It is 2 am and there is a mega storm raging outside. Rain blankets the windows to a house set back into a wooded area. The roaring rolling of thunder cracks as if 40 tractor trailers race down a road like Nascar drivers. Emense lightning bolts light the area like huge stadium lights. Settled behind the rain covered window is a small boy about the age of 10, hiding under his blankets from the thunder and lightning. As he sits under his make shift shelter he can hear his bedroom door creek open. He slowly pulls the covers from his head to see who is coming in. A very tall and slender man wearing a black t-shirt and black jeans stands in the doorway. He starts to walk towards the boy's bed slowly, the boy tries to call for help but no sound comes out him. The man stands at the foot of the bed and just looks at the boy who is now crawled back towards the wall behind his bed as far as he could go. The man extends his hand for the boy to come. Once again the boy starts to scream for help but again not a sound. The man puts on finger to his lips to hush the boy. He stretches his arms outward to his sides as a massive crack of thunder strikes. Almost immidately a large flash of lightning brightens the room as two large white wings stretch from the mans shoulderblades. As soon as the flash came it fades and once again the man extends his hand to the boy to follow him. Still very frightened and now confused the boy takes his hand and starts to get out of bed, but before his feet touch the floor the man yanks him up and out of the bed and starts to rush into the hallway and through another door. They are standing in the boy's parent's bedroom. The man motions to the boy with one hand to stay where he is and he walks over to his mother's side of the bed. He stands there for a moment with his arms folded looking down at the sleeping woman. He extends his left hand out to his side and with a loud crack of thunder and a flash of lighting and large scythe appears in his hand. He looks at the boy and then at the woman again. The boy tries to scream one last time but still to no use, as still no sound escapes him. The man lifts the scythe above his head and swings downward at her. The boy starts to scream as loud as he can, the blood curdling noise waking Blight who realizes he is screaming himself. He quickly turns and looks around wiping sweat from his face and trying to stop his heart from bursting out of his chest. The first thing he sees are FreakShow and Jezibel looking at him. BLIGHT: Where am I? What just happened? JEZIBEL: Babe, your on the plane, we are about to get off you were having some sort of nightmare. BLIGHT: Landed...I finally fall asleep and it happens again. FreakShow pulls down some luggage from the overhead compartment and passes Jezibel a small bag before taking his own and putting Blight's over his shoulder. Blight stands up half hunched over so he doesn't hit his head. He starts to wipe his eyes and push his hair back into a ponytail. FreakShow still looking at him a bit worried tries to hurry him off of the plane. The three walk out into the airport to await the limo MWE is sending for them to bring them to the hotel. JEZIBEL: What did you mean it happened again? BLIGHT: I haven't been sleeping lately. I just can't and I don't know why. I haven't slept for almost a week and everytime I do I start to have that nightmare. I know what is going on in the dream but once I wake up there is no way for me to explain it. And if it isn't the nightmare its---- A nearby tv at the terminal exit catches Blight's eye. There is a commercial for this weeks MNM and is showing some clips from last weeks. Blight just so happens to see the clip of Tali Preston getting her hand raised in their fatal four way match from the week before. BLIGHT: That shit....If its not the nightmare its last week playing over and over in my head...eating away at me. I should have won not her. JEZIBEL: But you should know that in a match things happen you can't always win especially in a situation like that where you have three opponents and not one. Wait how come this is the first time your saying anything about these nightmares? If its been a week then you should have said something maybe we could have done something maybe got you some help. BLIGHT: What do you mean get me some help? I don't need any help Jez I need fucking sleep...I can live with the nightmares its the rest of the crap that is killing me. Where the fuck is this limo? Jezibel just looks at Blight quite shocked at him snapping at her the way he did. The limo pulls up and FreakShow quickly turns and loads up the trunk with the luggage. He grabs Jezibel by the arm and leads her into the limo but putting up his arm to stop Blight from getting in. FREAKSHOW: Dude chill out she is just trying to find out what she can do to help you. So try to cool your ass and keep your attitude in check. Don't take this shit out on us, we are here to help you remember? Now fuck these dreams and forget last week just look to this week. FreakShow moves his arm and enters into the limo. Blight shakes his head and follows after. The limo pulls away to head to the hotel. For most of the ride there is silence until FreakShow breaks it. FREAKSHOW: When have wins and losses counted to you? Ever since I know you all you ever wanted to do...needed to do was dominate, was destroy, you wanted to leave an impression and 9 out of 10 times it was a physical one on someone's face. Now you lose a match that you totally kicked ass in and you cry like a bitch about it...what the fuck dude? Blight staring out of the window turns and faces FreakShow his face red with anger just glares at him. Jezibel looks at both men wearily before taking her ipod out and quickly putting it on choosing not to get involved in this war. BLIGHT: I am here in MWE for a reason and that is to completely destroy. I am here to purge MWE of its sins. To make all of those bastards feel the pain they have caused others. I am here to be on top...and that means win by any means possible. But it seems that I can say that alot easier than pulling it off lately. Two weeks ago I was the last man standing...Peter K taken out...Renshaw taken out...Clark taken out. Last week I walk into a war with Tali, England and Peter K again. I dominated, I destroyed, but in the end I choked. My arm wasn't raised and that isn't good enough. FREAKSHOW: Listen to yourself...listen to what you just said. You walked in and you were a juggernaut. You were unstoppable. I will bet my balls that Tali in her debut will not forget that she had to survive the devil himself. That Chris England did not walk out of that match with some pep in his step. That Peter K will not forget that two weeks in a row he walked into a ring with you and did not walk out under his own will. Blight just turns and looks out of the window again mutters not good enough. A little while later the limo pulls to a stop and the doors open. The three pile out of the limo and grab their luggage and head to their room. Once in the room the silence is broken again this time by Blight BLIGHT: So I have one of The Mountie's groupies this week. Sebastian St. Claude I believe is what they will be putting on his gravestone when I'm done. Well no matter what his name is it will just be another one to add to the list of the damned. I really shouldn't over look him though that French bastard will probably try to tounge me when I'm not looking. Well at least he would have made history with MWE as the worlds most worthless grease spot. Jezibel smirks a bit at Blight, and FreakShow just shakes his head. BLIGHT: Now the saddest part of this week is Brad Kane. It truly is a shame that someone with so much talent and promise will be wasted. The lockerroom is buzzing that the golden child has arrived. It truly is a shame that lil Lacey will have to grow up the rest of her life not knowing who her father really is. Her mother telling her that he was a hero to the wrestling world until he met the one demon he couldn't conquer...Blight. Speaking of Bradley's wife at least she can start telling people what it is like to be with a man that actually has penis once Kane is out of the picture for good. Jez shoots Blight a dirty look for the rude comment as FreakShow is trying to hide his laughter. BLIGHT: FreakShow...maybe your right. I need to stop making excuses and start doing what I do best...breaking people. DC has issued his warning...Kraven has put his fear into other people's eyes...Riddick and Crash have been more than impressive...but now MWE needs to watch as the entire world crumbles around them and I stand tall wearing the crown. Jez looks at Blight and nods showing that she is with him as FreakShow walks over and stands face to face with him and simply says. FREAKSHOW: It's about time. END SCENE
