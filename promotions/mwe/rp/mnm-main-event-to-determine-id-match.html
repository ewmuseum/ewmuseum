MNM Main Event to Determine ID Match

Jun 21 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Mayhem Main Event to determine Independence Day Match MWE.com - It has been made official that Eric Derf will get a shot at revenge against Devlin Sharpe at Independence Day in a one on one match. However, what has yet to be made official is what kind of match they will face off in. Dick Malibu has determined that instead of choosing a match himself, he will let Sharpe and Derf fight for the right. In the Main Event of Monday Night Mayhem, Sharpe teams with former Rated X partner, Brian Michaels, while Derf teams with the man who just won the World Title, Sinc Mercier. And whoever is a part of the winning team will get to choose the match type. Both men have spoken to MWE officials, and have both chosen match types already. Devlin Sharpe was quick to answer with a choice of match. "You see, for my choice, I have picked Falls Count Anywhere, it takes a true mastermind in order to use everything in his surroundings to his full advantage and Eric Derf lacks the balls to utilize that advantage." Upon hearing this, Eric Derf was just as quick to choose a match type. "Dev wants that kind of match so he can run and hide, then attack me from behind again. So I want him in a Prison Cell Cage Match, where there is no running or hiding, just me and him in an unescapable structure." Seeing all the action throughout the arena, or kept entirely in the ring, this match is sure to see both men go through intense measures of pain to reach their eventual goals.
