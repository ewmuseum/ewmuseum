Just a promo this week..

Aug 14 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
People must wonder what kind of luxurious setting we will have this week. Perhaps a posh restaurant, or some sort of high scale club? Nah, that would be far too easy. Don’t get it twisted though, we won’t be in some cliché darkened basement, or some empty arena. Instead, we fade into the same apartment we saw before, with a familiar feeling. A half-packed bong is on a table, and that immediately clues the viewer in to the fact that this can only be one person’s apartment. Who is that person you ask? Well Zak Warner of course. What other MWE superstar would have the balls to have a bong right out in the open, knowing full well the consequences that might occur due to MWE’s drug testing policy? The couch is empty, but only momentarily as Warner walks in and sits down, a grin on his face. “You know…normally I would open up with some sort of witty catchphrase, or something to make the viewers laugh…but not this week. Maybe it is the fact that I haven’t smoked in a while or maybe it is the fact that even after all this time, I am still pissed off as all hell yet I have to wait to do anything about it. Instead of giving me Kevin this week, the higher-ups are deciding to make everyone wait until Crowning Glory. I mean, don’t get me wrong, from a marketing standpoint, that is fucking brilliant. But from MY personal standpoint, it’s a load of shit. The only thing keeping me from hunting down Kevin right now, is the fact that at CG we get to do whatever we want to each other, no rules, no boundaries. Instead of Kevin, I get a 6 man tag match. My partners are quite the odd pair, and I’m not quite sure how to feel. CSB is one of the few people left in this place that I trust, and until he gives me a reason not to, I will have his back through thick and thin. The ironic part, is we are teaming with a black guy named Williams for one final time. Yet instead of DeAngelo, it’s Jai. I still don’t know if I can trust the guy yet, considering I pretty much ended his brothers MWE career. If he can look past that though, I have no issues with him personally. If he wants to hold a grudge though, I have no problem making this a 4 on 2 match, and taking him out right along with the other three. Speaking of the other three, what a motley bunch of misfits we have. We have a guy who thinks he is the devil, whose girlfriend is such a whore that she fucked his best friend, and claims to have a vampire thing for a daughter, teaming up with a masked guy who nobody in the company knows about, or even gives half a shit about. Oh, they have Jay Riddick too, but I can’t think of anything witty to make fun of him about.” He shifts his weight, leaning back on the couch. He eyes the bong with a longing gaze, but focuses back on the camera. His voice is eerily calm, yet you can still sense the emotion. “Oblivion, I really don’t know who you are, and to be perfectly honest I really don’t care. To me, you come off as “Generic Masked Wrestler #2456”. Mr. Dark and Mysterious, acting like we should all be afraid of you because you hide behind a fucking mask. I love how you think that you actually stand a shot, when you haven’t proved yourself in the slightest. Unlike Cruz and Riddick last week, I have absolutely no faith in you. Both of them, I could see holding titles within 6 months. You on the other hand…the only place I see you in 6 months, is sitting on your ass at home, collecting an unemployment check. You haven’t shown a DAMN thing in the ring to make me think that you have what it takes. You have no skills, no technique, and no fucking stamina. I would honestly be surprised if you lasted longer than five minutes in the ring, without your partners having to carry your ass. That’s what separates people like you, from people like me. I don’t need a damn person to carry me, and I never will. The only reason you are even in this match, is the fact they needed a 6th person to make it even. Basically, you got the bad luck of the draw, and you will have to reap what you sow. I’m sure you are one of the many people who want to get on me about my lifestyle, and how I have supposedly lost myself due to my addictions. The thing is, have you ever taken a look at your partner, Mr. Virus? In case you haven’t there will be a reminder a bit later. For now, let me move on to your other partner in the match.” His phone flashes from next to him, and he pauses to answer a text. He chuckles slightly, shaking his head ever so softly before tossing the phone back to the cushion. “Jay Riddick…poor, poor Jay Riddick. If getting your ass beat last week wasn’t bad enough, this week you have to step right back in the ring with me. The best part is, I didn’t even get to pin you last week, or make you tap. I was kind of disappointed in that, because I wanted to feel your bones snapping, I wanted to hear you scream out in pain. Sounds a bit sadistic, I know, but that is the only way you will ever reach your potential. You think I made it to the top of GWC without any pain? I’ve broken more bones in my body than anybody I know, and I would do it all again if it got me to the top. I’m sick and tired of people walking into a company, and thinking that they should get a title shot based on name alone. You got lucky last week, but I won’t let it happen again. The only reason it was Cruz that got knocked the fuck out last week, was that you let your guard down and got chucked out of the ring. That seems to be a trend for you lately, doesn’t it Jay? You have everything right in front of you, yet you waver and falter, and before you can reach out and grab it, POOF it’s gone like a cloud of smoke. Remember last week, when I said you were a fluke of a champion? Well what have you done to prove me wrong. You had a chance to prove yourself yet again, but you failed. You said I wasn’t in your head, but I obviously was. You can sit there and talk about faith, and beliefs, and reference your lord and savior all you want, but it won’t help you win. Last week, you said something to me, that I actually took to heart. Do you want to know what it was?” He reaches onto the table, and grabs the remote. Hitting a button, he starts up a video on the screen, and we see Jay Riddick talking. QUOTE You see Zak, all my career I’ve been telling my mind to shut up. My mind is what is telling me to submit or to keep myself from kicking out, but I’ve overcome what my mind is telling me. I’ve made mistakes like any other person Zak, and deep down inside Zak, if you search within yourself, I am sure you’ll find at least one big mistake you’ve made recently. Zak, ignore your mind and I can tell you what you’re true belief is, and I do not question your true beliefs. Zak, I know what you’re thinking, and I agree, I am unbelievable. He hits pause, and leans forward grinning. “You are right Jay, I have made one big mistake lately. I believed in you. I thought you had what it took, but week in and week out you are making me second guess myself. You have your faith, and your beliefs…but I say FUCK YOUR FAITH, FUCK YOUR BELIEFS. I know what my true belief is, and it is that I am better than you, and you shouldn’t have a title around your waist. Can you step up and prove me wrong? Can you shut me up like you try to shut your mind up? Or are you going to prove me right? Are you going to make a mockery of your faith, and a mockery of all those that believe in you. The best part of it all though, is that you aren’t even the most pathetic guy on your team. That honor goes to Mr. Leon “Rawr I’m a scary Demon” Roberts, a.k.a. The Virus.” He pauses for a second, and in the background we hear a couple voices, too faint to make out. It must sound familiar to Zak though, because he starts walking towards the door only to be stopped by his phone yet again. The sound of his phone is enough to snap him back to reality, and he sits back down. He allows himself to indulge in a bong hit, and the familiar glazed look is back in his eyes. “I wondered how long it would take for us to be in a ring together again Leon. I figured you were ducking me, seeing as how you have never been able to get a victory over me. How many times have you tried, only to fail? Countless times in GWC you stepped up to me, only to get knocked the fuck down. Do you remember the Underground title match? In case you forgot, let me refresh your memory.” He hits play on the remote once again, and the tape cuts to a GWC event back in early 2008. It’s an Underground Title match, between Virus and Warner. QUOTE Warner then dragged himself over to Virus as he applied the Anklelock, both men having little left as Warner used the smidgen of energy left to hurt Virus more. Virus trying all he could to stay in the match, but Virus managed to drag himself towards the ropes, sitting only mere inches away as he places a firm grip upon it, as Warner is made to release the hold. Virus then uses the ropes to stand as Warner does the same, as his limp is still evident. As Virus stumbles trying to regain his blanace, Warner sneaks up behind him, and hits the Life to Lifeless, immeditaly following it up by locking in the Rusted Embrace. The Virus does his best to fight off, refusing to submit. Finally, the ref is forced to stop the match, as he passes out from the lack of oxygen. He hits pause yet again, chuckling as he exhales from yet another hit. “How sad is it, that with all my drug use I was still able to outlast you. I’ll be the first to admit, I’m not in the best of shape right now, but back then was even worse. Yet I was still able to slam you on your fucking head, and choke you out like it was nothing. No matter how hard you tried, no matter if you had Demonica, Jablez, Aaron Beowulf, or anyone else in your corner…YOU COULD NEVER FUCKING BEAT ME. I always have been, and I always will be better than you Leon. No matter how high and mighty you act, you will never be on my level. I could care less if I get criticized about my drug abuse, at least I’m open about it. How many times have you been addicted to painkillers? Yet you sit there and talk down on my drug use, just like you did back then. At least I come out and admit I enjoy getting high. You have to rely on a prescription just so you have an excuse. That’s even worse Leon, and you know it. Then again, what isn’t worse about you when compared to me? My career is better than yours, my women are better than yours, and even my friends are better than yours. I mean hell, I hate Kevin now, but at least he never fucked my girl like Vega did yours. Nor has CSB, DeAngelo, or any of my other friends. As a matter of fact, have you double checked to make sure Jay and Oblivion didn’t slip her the ol’ double team while you weren’t around? Face it Leon, this match will be like all the others. There is nothing you can do to prevent the inevitable. You can bring your sideshow special effects, your demons, and your whore. Riddick can bring his faith and beliefs, and Oblivion can bring…well he has nothing to bring but you get the point. For one last night, CSB, Warner, and a black guy named Williams will stand victorious in the middle of the ring, and there is nothing you guys can do to stop it. So shoot up my Addicts, because my flag will be flying high once more.” He reaches over and hits stop on the camera, and he relaxes a bit. It’s obvious that he hasn’t slept in awhile, but who can really blame him? There is a knock at his hotel door, and he walks up and takes a peek through the peephole. A smile breaks out on his face as he opens the door, and standing there is Angie Hardaway. She has a few bags in her hands that Warner takes and sets down, and he goes to let the door close behind him. Before it can shut though, a hand grabs it causing Warner to turn around quickly. There, standing in his doorway with a look that could kill, staring at him and Angie, is Kevin. “Oh fuck..” Fade.
