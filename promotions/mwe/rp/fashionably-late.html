Fashionably late

Aug 17 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The scene opens up to a dark but dimly lit locker room, the lights flickering. Xavier Stone sits on a bench infont of some lockers, his head down in his hands. He looks up and gives the camera a look from the brow of his forehead. Xavier: James Sullivan, a name I have never heard before, and by the end of the week, a name no one will remember. This promo will be just you and me brother, no kid, no girlfriend, just the seriousness between superstars, and now I am going to be totally serious. Xavier pauses for a moment to regain control of his temper. Xavier: A man who began training at the age of 20, is now 22 and has been in the business for less than a year… Now he has joined the ranks of MWE and is put in a tournament to go for the MWE US title. Seems to me like this kid’s bark is worse than his bite. Xavier Stands up and opens his locker, gabbing his black and green W10 hoodie and shoes. He sits back down and starts undoing his boots and begins to put his shoes on. Xavier: James, you are stepping into a ring of pit bulls and you sir are the Chihuahua. You do not stand a chance against me, Current US champion in AEW, former APWC World Champion, and King of the Mid card, you sir should go home and beg to job and get some experience, cause son, less than a year isn’t going to cut it in the league of big boys. Xavier begins to tie his shoes and then pulls his hoodie over his head, he sits down once again. Xavier: Son, let me tell you something, man to man, amano amano, amigo to… uhm…. Whatever… My point being that you are just a pup and you may be younger than me and be faster, but kid, you sure as hell aint stronger, and I would fel bad if I hurt you, like say I beat you and I hurt you bad, I’m gona have to visit your mom and explain I had to hurt her son because he willingly got into a fight with someone almost 10 years older than him and 5 times stronger. I would feel bad, so bad that I would fuck her just to get my mind off it and he mind off it too. Think of that and try sleeping at night. Xavier packs up a gym bag with his stuff and tosses it on the ground and pokes through his locker a bit, his voice echoes from the locker. VXavier*head in locker*: You know what James, come into that ring, and I will let you put up your fight. You think you are good enough o go on the line with and ex world champ and current US camp in AEW, than go right ahead, I want the world too see that all you can do is let that bulldog mouth run that puppy dog ass… See you in the ring, I’m done telling the world the obvious and trying to convince you to step out… bring it punk… [/color] Xavier pulls his head out of the locker and slams it shut, a small white rectangle paper flutters off the top of the locker. Xavier bends down to pick it up and comes to the conclusion it’s a picture of a naked middle aged woman, average looks. Xavier ogles it for a moment before turning it over. The writing on the back reads in cursive writing. “To: Richard Covell. Mr. Covell this is a reminder on what you got earlier this week, hope you remember what we talked about and hope to see you again. Signed Mrs. Sullivan” Xavier: Good gawd, that’s his mom? And she’s sleeping with Richard “duck for cover” Covell? *shudders* Things that make you go “buh-h-h-huh”, no wonder this kid got into the tournament, he got his mom to sleep with his boss. Disgusting…better question... WHAT THE FUCK IS IT DOING IN MY LOCKER ROOM?!?! Xavier crumples the picture and throws it in the garbage, the camera zooms in on the crumpled picture as all it shows is her face and crumpled white parts. The camera fades to nothing as Xavier turns off the lights and shuts the locker room door behind him. -FADE- {OOC: Sorry its 3.5 hours late, but its in, short but sweet, enjoy pplz)
