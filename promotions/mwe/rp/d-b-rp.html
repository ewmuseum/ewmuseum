MWE 24/7 — 08-09 PPV PR Archives

D&B RP; posting for dylan due to PC problems

Dec 28 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A camera begins to fade out from the bland and black image it is currently set in. The camera begins to become clear as it pans around where it seems to be, at a wrestling arena packed with fans. It seems to be a House show as suddenly So Happy by Theory of a Deadman hits the PA System as 3 big firework bangs are heard on the stage. The crowd roar in response as Darius & Boyd walk out with their MWE World Tag Team Championships resting on their shoulders, milking their appearances to the crowd as they cheer. They walk down to the ring slapping the hands of the diehard fans leaning over the firm barricade. Boyd slides under the bottom rope as Darius walks up the steel steps and through the ring ropes. They go on to the turnbuckles and raise their hands high as a cheering response is heard back. The music stops as they stand in the ring for a bit smirking at the positive cheers they are receiving. Boyd walks towards the edge of the ring and is handed two official MWE microphones from one of the many officials at ringside. Boyd walks back to the middle of the ring and hands Darius the other mic. They continue to look at the crowd with a smile on their face milking the chants and cheers. Darius: People told us we were gonna be something, people commented on our abilities saying that we were going to be the next big, people thought we would be champions.. But they didn't think we'd be champions this quickly. You see, winning what we hold now even shocked us because really hate it or love it we're still rookies around this joint, and rookies winning titles usually doesn't gel together, but that was a different case for us, Darius & Boyd. Boyd: Last week I said that at Adrenaline Rush you didn't need to call us Darius & Boyd, but instead call us the new MWE Tag Team Champions, and we made a promise to our fans and the many million MWE viewers, and we did it, we accomplished something great quite frankly. We beat a former MWE World Champion and a man who is on the uprising of the MWE, we beat them, we pinned them, we proved that we are gonna be something to recognise, that we are going to be the next BIG thing. The crowd cheer in response as Boyd readjusts his title on his shoulder as they both pause smirking at the crowd. Darius: But now, we need to stop ranting on about how we won our titles because this Sunday at Adrenaline Rush is our first actual step forward as champions, because at the Adrenaline Rush we, Darius & Boyd will be defending our titles against two men, two brothers who have been in this business longer than us but have never seen the light of day when a Championship was resting on their shoulder.. Of course, I'm talking about Devlin Sharpe and his identical twin, Ryan Sharpe. Darius begins to chuckle as Boyd looks at him with a rather odd and confused look on his face. Boyd still looks quite confused as he brings the mic closer to his face. Boyd: What are you laughing about? Are you laughing at how Devlin Sharpe barely talked about us in his promo or how he told the whole world on MWE Radio that he'd beat us, and that he is a new man with a new face for competition? Or how he called us a pair of candy ass motherfuckers, and worthless to the MWE World Tag Team Championships? Darius: All of it.. But my question is, when was the last time this guy actually won a match? He calls us worthless, and gives us maybe a line or so in his promo and shows all his concern to guys like Brian Michaels and DC, but what he really should be concerned about is what's going to go down this Sunday. Sure, we're rookies, and sure he's got experience but there's one thing I can say without trying to put us over, and that is that we are better, and we can prove it, we will prove it! Boyd chuckles and looks over to Darius as he readjusts his title resting on his shoulder. Boyd: But that's what makes me laugh, how he doesn't give a damn or doesn't show any concern to us.. He thinks we're just another tag team in the land of Mayhem, but if he had any sense between his ears he'd know that we've ALREADY proven that concern should be issued. We beat guys he hasn't beat in his near to two years in this business, and at the end of the day it answers your question Darius, the old Devlin Sharpe has died.. Because I remember the old Devlin Sharpe, I remember how he was once a threat to guys around here, and how he was actually someone to worry about.. But now, now he's nothing like he once was.. He's changed, and usually change is good but in this instance it's bad.. Very, bad. Darius: I remember those days too, back when we were just in a local federation.. You see with most people in wrestling you tend to improve as you notch up more years, but Devlin is another story.. And most people would think if he's dropped down that much that it gives us an easy win, but that's not what we want.. We would have stayed in that local fed if that's what we wanted, but we came to MWE not for the money, but for the challenge. If Devlin can't give us that then I'll be damned, but if Dev can bring back the old man he once was and if he can actually challenge us.. Then I'll still be damned because through the many fades, I can't picture it. Darius re-adjusts his title as he looks into the crowd at the many signs for him and his partner Boyd. Boyd walks a few steps before putting the microphone to his mouth. Boyd: That's what we want, we want an Adrenaline Rush.. We want to be taken to the limit, we want to lose from time to time because that's how you improve, that's how you climb that ladder. You work off your mistakes, and this is where Devlin needs to start.. He needs to work off his mistakes and take us to that very limit. Darius: So at Adrenaline Rush we plan to be taking home what is ours, but that's not the real reason we're gonna show up.. We're gonna show up to see if Devlin can change, if he can bring back the same man who could win matches, the same man who was once apart of Rated-X. So I ask him and his brother Ryan one thing, when the light is shinning in your eyes, and we're standing over you are you gonna get up and put up a fight or are you gonna lie there whining and waiting to be pinned? But either way, challenge or not, mark my word, we will be walking out the MWE Tag Team Champions.. Boyd: Because that's how we roll! Darius: Isn't that Mason Resoan's old catchphrase? Boyd chuckles with a smirk on his face. Boyd: Keyword "Old". Darius smirks at Boyd as they drop the microphones and exit the ring. The scene begins to fade out as Darius and Boyd leave the arena with a positive reaction from the crowd. The camera begins to become black as the promo ends.
