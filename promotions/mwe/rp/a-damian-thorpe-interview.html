A Damian Thorpe Interview; A Grand Mystique RP

Nov 5 2008

The Grand Mystique

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
*Please bear in mind that this RP directly continues on from the diner segment featuring GM in Maxx's RP. read that and this will make a lot more sense.* October 30th 2008, 15:20PM Outside Jays Diner, Detroit The Grand Mystique storms through the glass double doors at the diner. GM turns and walks off around the corner. He takes a seat on the bench, puts his head in his hands and lets out a deep sigh. GM: (hushed)“What...the...hell....is going on....?” “Erm....GM?” GM looks up. In front of him, thoroughly out of breath and with his hands on his knees, is Damien Thorpe. Thorpe: “I....need to.....talk.” GM: “Sit down.” GM moves over. Damian collapses on the bench. Thorpe: “I’m sorry.” He eventually breaks the long, uncomfortable silence. “I’m a reporter, an interviewer. I’m doing something I have always wanted to do but sometimes, sometimes I don’t engage my brain before talking.” GM: “It’s OK.” Thorpe: “It’s something I’m trying to work on.” GM tries to suppress a laugh but fails. He allows a smile to pass over his mouth. Thorpe: “I know you wanted a World title shot. But I thought you were happy to be tag team champion with Maxx.” GM goes to speak but pauses. GM: “On or off the record?” Thorpe: “Up to you.” GM: “Tape recorder?” Thorpe pulls one out of his jacket pocket and presses record. He lays it to rest on the slats of the wooden bench. GM: “It’s no secret that everything that I have ever done was based around becoming a World Champion. It was destined to happen in EW. It was going to happen in Rev Pro and it’s a matter of time before it happens in the UWA.” Thorpe: “You’re the best superstar in this company never to have held a World title.” GM: “I know.” They share a laugh. GM quickly resumes his intense train of thought. “It’s no surprise that I harbour dreams of being a World Champion, whether it be in the MWE or not. But I am not here at this time to be the champion, the one, the man to represent a brand and carry a company on his back. At this time, all I have on my mind is the World Tag Team titles.” Thorpe: “You and Maxx, for lack of better words are a super team.” GM: “They are the perfect words. I could not have said it better.” Thorpe blushes at the compliment. “That’s why we are here. To introduce our talents to the MWE and within minutes of our in ring debut, we are champions, dominant in fashion and in possession of gold.” Thorpe: “The titles mean a lot to you?” GM: “Of course, but it’s a benefit of hard work and the instinctive partnership that I have formed with Maxx. The title win was just academic. Project Revolution have all the potential in the world but they are full of brawn and don’t know how to use it yet. We are simply smarter.” Thorpe: “So Darius & Boyd, they seem to be another real up and coming team. What do you think of them?” GM: “So they went to the best wrestling school that they could afford. Unfortunately all the teachers in the world cannot give them what they are lacking; the complete skill that Maxx and I naturally possess. Their total lack of experience will let them down when they are face to face with us in the ring. We are the real test. A pair of natural main eventers holds the titles that every tag team in the industry aspire to hold.” “With every match, our play to win attitude goes up another level and we are one step closer to being totally dominant in the MWE, just as tag team champions should be, no matter what brand we are on.” Thorpe: “So you are planning to send a message to the entire company?” He checks the recorder to make sure it is OK. GM: “You can be damn sure that we go all out to make sure everyone watching sits up and takes notice and then says to the mother, brother, sister, mother or the little bitch sitting right next to them “Damn I’m glad it was those two hick SOB’s that had to get in the ring with those and not me, cos they are on fire!” GM was talking faster and faster and with a real fire-in-his-belly kind of intensity that Damien has not seen from him in months. He takes a second to calm down. Damian allows him a few seconds. He has clearly learnt from his previous encounters with GM GM: “I could talk about our individual, single achievements. We have held belts in every company and main evented shows in every company we have been in. Match of the year candidates, show stealers. We have gone toe to toe with the very best and taken them to the very limit. And I could talk about our title reigns or the feuds that we have survived. Or I could say that the Grand Mystique and Maxx are the dominant stars to arrive in the MWE. We will continue to be dominant until somebody worthy enough rips the Tag Team title belts from our cold, dead fingers. Why? Because we are proud to have come to the MWE and show this company what a proper team are capable of.” Thorpe: “You’re very hyped up for a non title match up.” GM: “Yeah. I know. But I think it’s because I know if we can beat those two, then me and Maxx may be seen to have a little credibility in this company. Not that we need it. The ten pounds of gold around our waists should be enough.” Damian nods in agreement. Thorpe: “Maxx got a little carried away with his promo.” GM: “Pig fuckers?” They share another comfortable laugh. “Well I thought he did too but did you see that chick from the shower? Damn.....she reminded me so much of Miss Piggy" *GM gestures to his nose* "it was scary. So maybe his comments were justified as they have been sharing her around and playing “Catch the STD” at the same time?” “Heck, if they showed some balls, ditched the bird and focussed on wrestling then they may have a better chance against us. As it is, they have as much chance of winning as McCain.......oh wait, that happened already.” Another hearty laugh. Maxx steps out from the diner and sees his two friends sharing a laugh. He is very surprised as he walks up to them. Maxx: “What the f*ck has gotten into you two?” GM: “Pork wart sandwiches.” GM plays it totally straight faced but Damian fails miserably and bellows loudly in laughter. Maxx: “What!?” GM: “Never mind.” Maxx: “I got something lined up, to prep us for the match, you up for it?” GM: “Ummmm.....trust huh? Well, every great team needs it.” GM offers his hand to his partner. Maxx shakes it firmly, without hesitation. Damian steps between them and places an arm across each shoulder. Thorpe: “So fellas, where are we off to?” GM and Maxx walk off up ahead, talking quietly. Damien is left standing, speechless but with a smile on his face as he knows he has got his first great interview with the Grand Mystique.
