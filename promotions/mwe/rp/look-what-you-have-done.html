Look what you have done...; Alejandro Rodriguez RP

Dec 26 2008

Alejandro Rodriguez

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Downtown, New York Monday 22nd December, 4:30pm New York General Hospital. Scene opens inside the New York General Hospital, the camera pans around showing patients being carted from their rooms by nurses, a young boy crying as a doctor examines his knee in a particular room and a row of people sitting outside on benches, waiting to talk to doctors, reading newspapers and drinking coffee. And one man, patrolling up and down the corridor is none other than Alejandro Rodriguez, he seems unable to stay still, he is shaking all over from tip to toe. He lets out an audible sigh as he see’s a doctor emerging round the corner, he has grey balding hair and a moustache, and he is holding a clipboard, Alejandro rushes toward him. Alejandro: Can I see her? Doctor: I.. um… of course. After you… He beckons toward a door on the right. Alejandro opens the door and walks in, at the sight of Stacy he nearly bursts into tears and falls back into the wall, but, pulling himself together, he pulls up a seat next to her and he sits down. Stacy Adams is on the bed, unconscious and unmoving, wires all over her body and a oxygen mask planted over her face. Alejandro looks at her for a moment, and reaches out for her hand. The doctor looks on. Doctor: She’s unconscious at the moment, we can't tell when she will wake up at the moment. She’s in a bad state, she has three cracked ribs and there’s a lot of bruising, but we expect her to make a full recovery… The doctor hesitates for a moment as Alejandro looks at him. Doctor: I doubt she’ll be out of the hospital by Christmas… Alejandro begins to shake in anger and he places his head in his hands. The doctor backs nearer toward the door. Doctor: I’ll leave you to yourself. The doctor leaves the room and Alejandro keeps his sight on Stacy. He holds her hand again and looks at her in pain, tears start to come from his eyes and he wipes them away. He kisses Stacy’s hand and looks up at her, he doesn’t bother to wipe away his tears this time. Alejandro: I promised, when I met you… I’d protect you… I’d defend you and make sure no harm came to you. I’m sorry… I’m so, so sorry. I failed you… Look at you, you’ll miss Christmas… our first Christmas, with me, you and Stephani, all of us together. And it’s been ruined, all of it, by one man. Alejandro wipes his eyes which have more anger in them as he changes the topic to Whitman, he doesn’t look at Stacy, but the wall opposite. Alejandro: David Whitman. The only man I respected in MWE. He was my friend, he was an inspiration. But he lost, he lost and he couldn’t take it, he couldn’t take loosing his World Title, he snapped, the sore bastarding looser, and he took it out on you... He struck you down, you we helpless, innocent. What had you done to upset him? You did nothing… and now you’ll spend Christmas in this shitty hospital, alone, without your family in Canada, your brothers and sisters, your mother and father, all expecting you, home for Christmas, how can I tell them? Alejandro‘s voice cracks under the strain, he clears his throat, bows his head an continues. Alejandro: How can I tell them, their daughter was injured, injured in my line of work. Injured at the hands of one of my friends, they will never forgive me, they will try and turn you against me, I don’t blame, I’d do the same for my daughter, Stephani. Whitman has turned my life around and made it hell. How can I recover from this, how will you recover? Alejandro looks at Stacy with intensity etched into the very pupils of his eyes. He looks again with fury at the wall, and then back to Stacy with a softer look as the camera pans in to her awful injuries. Alejandro: But I promise you one thing, I will make Whitman pay for what he did to you, when I face him this Sunday, all the respect I had for him, is going straight back out the window, I will see to it that he goes through as much pain as you have, I will avenge your loss of Christmas by making sure he never gets to see the new year, I will make him suffer. I will crush all that is what’s left of our friendship and I will make him stop breathing. I will make him bleed and win the match in your name. Alejandro stands up and strokes Stacy’s hair, he pulls it across her blacked eye she suffered from the fall. He bends and kisses her across the forehead and then straightens up. Alejandro: Rest easy. Alejandro turns round and walks out of the room as the scene fades to black. Long Beach, New York, Wednesday, 24th December, 3:20pm, East Atlantic Beach. The Scene opens on a cliff face over-looking the beach, it’s a windy day and it’s not surprising to find that the area deserted, all for one man, Alejandro Rodriguez. He is wearing a black button-up shirt which ruffles in the wind, with jeans and a black mask. He looks out toward the waves that come along and crash against the surface, occasionally spraying him with water. A car pulls over behind Alejandro, who doesn’t turn around. Darrel Jones steps out of the car, wearing sunglasses, a white shirt and black trousers, he walks up to the point where Alejandro is standing and Darrel stands beside him. For a moment nobody speaks. Alejandro: We came here after our first date, we lay here on the beach. We talked and laughed while soaking up the sun. Now look what’s become of her, she’s spending Christmas in a hospital ward because of me. Darrel: It’s not your fault Alejandro. Alejandro: I should have never interfered in Whitman’s match. How do you think her parents shall see it, will they tell me it wasn’t my fault, do you think they will feel sorry for me? Darrel: They- …it’s only natural Al, you would feel the same. Alejandro nods his head. Alejandro: That is why I mustn’t see her again, we have to split up… Alejandro turns around and begins to walk toward his car, with Darrel in hot pursuit. Darrel: You think that is going to solve anything? Alejandro stops in his tracks and faces Darrel. Alejandro: Do you know how much risk I put my family in. I bring them to an arena of men who fight for a living, men who hate me and love to see me break down like this. What if it had been Stephani? Do you think she would have survived, Stacy barely survived! Darrel: The doctors say she will be fine, I saw her this morning. Alejandro: But she’s not fine, is she? It’s Christmas tomorrow and she is still unconscious from what David did to her, she’ll never be the same again, she’ll be a nervous wreck! Now, are you coming with me, I need to train. Alejandro gets to his car and opens the door, but Darrel has no intention to get in. Darrel: Everyone needs a break Alejandro, especially at Christmas, you need to stop thinking about your match. Alejandro: You know Darrel, what Whitman did, it turned my life around. I’m going to make Whitman pay for what he’s done to my family, I’m going to destroy him, and I don’t care that you say that he didn’t mean any of it, the deed has been done, and now David Whitman needs to suffer the consequences. Darrel: Alejandro, you know Whitman, you know he would never do anything like this on purpose. Alejandro: I thought I knew him, I thought he would never dream of hurting innocent people, but I’m human, and we all make mistakes… Because I was very wrong about David Whitman, he’s not the person everyone thinks he is, the funny, out-going, high-flyer… I see him now, for the first time, what he truly is. Alejandro enters the car without another word and starts the engine and the scene fades to black. Hempstead, New York, Wednesday, 24th December, 11:50pm Hempstead All-night Gym. The scene opens in a 24 hour gym, the camera pans around showing expensive equipment all around, few people are in there except a few staff beginning to clean up, but none dominates the scene more than Alejandro Rodriguez who is training vigorously. He has changed into a white tank top and shorts, is doing mass numbers of everything. He is doing pull-ups on an iron bar and is not pausing for breath or anything. Darrel walks in through the double doors with two bottles of water. Darrel sits down on the bench opposite and watches him. Darrel: Have a drink Al, you’re going to kill yourself. Alejandro hops down from the bar, catches the bottle and has a huge swig from it, he then sits down alongside, Darrel. Darrel: Good, you’re taking a break. Alejandro: Nope. Alejandro reaches behind him and pulls out two huge weights, which he lifts with each arm. Darrel: Jesus Al, I couldn’t lift one of those with both my hands. Alejandro keeps going with strain on his face and muscles bulging. Alejandro: That’s because you’re puny, Darrel. Darrel laughs and gets up from the bench. Darrel: Alright man, I got to get going. Alejandro stops for a second. Alejandro: Where are you going? Darrel: It‘s 11:50, Christmas is 10 minutes away, I need to get back to my family. Alejandro drops the weights and stands up. Alejandro: I can- Darrel: You keep training man, trust me. But go light on the weights, you’re going to pull something. Alejandro sits back down on the bench and places his head in his hands as Darrel walks out. Alejandro sits up and has another swig of water as he notices the camera. Alejandro looks at it as if it’s a strange creature. Alejandro: Come to watch me, have you? You see, that’s not a very good thing for Whitman, is it? Because he’ll be watching this… Alejandro taps the screen of the camera. Alejandro: ...at home, drinking beer with his buddies, one of them, probably Zack, smoking pot somewhere in the background. And he’ll see, they’ll all see, how hard I’m training, you wondering why I’m here when Christmas is ten minutes away, it’s because I’ve never been more determined to beat you in my life David. Alejandro closes his eyes for a second as if in very deep thought. Alejandro: You see, David, this isn’t about Championships or awards anymore, it’s not even about my pride. You come away from your match, and you attack someone close to me and expect me to forgive you? You‘ve made this personal Whitman. How would you feel, if I beat up, James or Scott, Thomas or Eddie? You wouldn’t like it at all Whitman, you’ve taken my one thing I love the most in life, and you’ve crushed it, in a single stupid act. The camera goes from side to side slowly and Alejandro looks at it from left to right. Alejandro: I don’t care if you come to Adrenaline Rush, humbled, apologising and absolutely remorseful, I don’t give a damn. I don’t care what friendship we had, that’s clearly gone now, out of the window. Anything you have done for me in the past is forgotten, in this one act of yours, I don‘t care about anything else, when you laid your hands on Stacy you cost your entire future in wrestling. Alejandro laughs and looks maniacally toward the camera. Alejandro: Because I know, Whitman, that’s the one thing in life you love more than anything, your reaction to loosing last week proved that. You put your heart and soul, hell, you put your life into wrestling. So know, that is that I’m going to take from you, this Sunday, I will reach in and rip the chance for you to wrestle out of your very heart. So that you will never hurt anyone, ever again. Alejandro bows his head down and takes a drink of water before continuing, he looks at the clock, it‘s five to twelve. Alejandro: How can I lose this Sunday, when I’ve already lost everything in my life? I come in this Sunday Whitman, and I have everything to win, and you have everything to loose. You will come in humbling and weaker, and I will come in determined, faster and stronger than I have ever been in my life, all because of you Whitman, look what you have done to one family, you’ve torn us apart, and now …I will tear you apart. Alejandro rises from the bench and he grabs to camera to pull right in his face and for the first time, anger is very visible through the mask. Alejandro: I guarantee Whitman, I will make you more than bleed this Sunday, I will make you feel for what you did, I will make you pay. I will end your career. The clocks ring twelve times announcing Christmas Day. Scene fades to black as Alejandro gives the camera back and does some more weights.
