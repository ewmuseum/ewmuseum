Strip The Labels

Jul 22 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A large cherry blossom tree is shown standing against the glistening sunlight, however the natural beauty of this tree is gone. It has die and decayed, and in it's wake has left nothing more then the skeletal remains of what was once an amazing sight. Now, one could argue that it is still alive, as several of the bright white and pink leaves and flowers still cling loosely to the dark brown branches that are protruding from the trunk. However it would be a moot point. The lack of care and nurturing for this tree is easy to see, branches hang ready to break and most of the once beautiful blossoms have turned into a dark brown resembling the death of their home. "I'm not a champion, I'm not a hero. I'm sure as hell not a superstar ... I'm that dog chained up in the yard, taunted by everyone that walks by with the freedom to do whatever they damn well please. I am the mistake your parents made on prom night ..." As that familiar dull toned voice breaks the otherwise serene silence of our scene the view begins to pan down. We take in the dark, dying brown of the bark covering the tree's trunk as we slowly make our way towards the roots of the cherry blossom. "I'm the bastard child of the world ..." At the bottom of this tree, dressed in a white 'beater and blue jeans sits Alexis Black. His knees are tucked towards his chest and his black hair hangs over his face as he looks towards the ground beneath him. Alexis Black: "For as long as I can remember I've been told that I will never amount to anything. Anyone with a position of superiority over me has pushed me aside in order to attend to people 'more fit for society' and after a while a guy gets use to it." Keeping his head tucked down Black raises his hands for his last little spiel, using the ever famous finger quotes as he describes the normal members of society. In his mind, Alexis Black is a misfit, an outcast. Even a freak in some aspects of the word. He pauses for a moment as the wind picks up a little, blowing his hair wildly revealing the small red tuft in the front. Alexis Black: "To being cast aside, judged, and forgotten. After so long it seems like the natural occurrence of a lifetime, like your whole life your destined to be a reject. No matter how hard you try to be a success people will never see past the label you were given as a child." The wind dies just as a few of the blossoms from the tree float past Alexis. The bright pink and decaying browns contrast beautifully against this man's dark setting as he lets his hands drop back down to rest on his legs. Alexis Black: "Now as one of the labeled there are two things you can do. You can accept that no one will ever see you as a threat. You can roll over and let them step all over you or you can do something to strip yourself of that label. You can turn the tide on all of the people who abide by the labels and show them that a sleeping dragon rests beneath them." With that Black lifts his head, taking his eyes past the camera and up to look into the sky. Bringing the top of his head to a rest on the trunk of the tree still standing tall behind him. He lets out a small chuckle before speaking up once again. Alexis Black: "Week after week, day after day I am told that I'm not the kind of guy that's going to make an impact in the wrestling world. Yet for the second time since I became a part of this industry I have left an empty shell of a man lying lifeless at my feet. Destroyed any chance of them walking out of the building, and in doing so I left thousands speechless, and to top it all off I did it all with the tools that I have at my disposal every single day ..." With a sly grin on his face Black looks at us for a second before lifting his hands from his sides to hold them up in front of him. He stares at them and all there little parts: His palms, to his fingers, to his painted black fingernails. It's obvious what he meant by "tools" as he rolls his wrists around in front of him. Alexis Black: "Yet after the way I victimized James Preston and Derek Sitar people still want to label me as an idiot that will never amount to anything more a curtain jerker, but they just don't get it do they? I already sent Sitar rolling into the unemployment line in a stretcher, so what makes them think I won't do the same to anyone else that decides to try to belittle me?" Keeping his hands in front of himself Alexis looks back up at the camera with perhaps the more serious look we've ever seen on his face. His cold blue eyes staring intently towards us as he rants about the injustices of his life. Alexis Black: "What makes them think that they're safe ..." There's a kind of uncertainty in his voice, not in the way that you would expect. More so in the sense that he doesn't know if he can control something, like there's a monster trapped deep within him that at any point could get the best at him. We can't know for sure what's going on inside the head of his man, but we can tell that there's something deeper in his words. Black now let's his head turn to look out into the open field that reaches as far as the eye can see. His eyes stare forward as his lips slowly begin to open once again. Alexis Black: "What makes them think that anyone is safe?" A sudden depressed state rushes over Alexis as he let's his head drop again, his sleek jet black hair falling over his face. Yet only for a moment before he pulls his hand up from his knee, using it to brush his hair back and in an instant he pulls himself to his feet. Turning his back to the camera Alexis now leans his forearm against the trunk of the cherry blossom and lets his eyes drift upwards to look at the branches protruding randomly towards the sky. Alexis Black: "If there's one thing I have learned from my life, it's that you can never expect things to stay peaceful for a long period of time. Coming here is just a reminder of how things can change from heaven to hell in a heartbeat ..." Black pushes himself off the tree a little bit, now resting his palm against the rough bark instead of his forearm. Still gazing upwards, his eyes starting to glaze over with tears from a distant memory. Alexis Black: "This is the place I used to come when I was little to get away from reality, but it always caught up with me. Especially that night ... The night when I lost everything. The night I began living my own personal hell." Letting his eyes drop back down to stare out towards the wild nothing that stands behind the tree, Alexis drops his arms to a rest at his side. His shoulders rise slowly as he fills his lungs with air, taking a few short steps into the acres upon acres of open field before him. What does he mean by 'that night?' His posture alone tells us that we may never find out, it's something he wants to keep bottled up deep within himself until he can use it to his distinct advantage. Shoulders slumped over, head held high using every ounce of inner strength he has. Alexis Black: "Which brings me to this Monday, you see MWE management has decided to pit me against Peter Kaymakcian, a man that claims he resides in his own personal hell." With this Black pivots in just seconds to stare at the camera again, his freezing cold blue eyes seem to pierce through it. As though looking into our eyes leaving a feeling of intimidation. Eyes filled with emotion Black lets his hair blow freely as the wind picks up again, blowing even more of the cherry blossoms off their branches and far off into the landscape. Alexis Black: "Your own personal hell? That's cute, it really is .. But I don't think you know anything about personal hells Peter, because I've been fighting out of a personal hell since the day I was born!" It's strange how quickly someone can go from depression to rage. In the blink of an eye Black's emotion shifted. His body tenses up from the limp posture just seconds ago, fists clench at his waist as he stares forward tears drying up instantly leaving nothing but an intense glare into our eyes. Alexis Black: "I don't think you know what it's like, to have to claw your way up from the very depths of hell, to achieve even the mediocre level success I've seen in this industry ... See, I remember your last run here, and to tell me that your suffering in your own personal hell, is a pathetic excuse of a lie to achieve some sort of sympathetic reaction from the crowd." With a forced chuckle Alexis raises his right hand, letting the fist slowly open. Only to point towards the camera as he directs every motion, every word, everything in his entire being towards Peter Kaymakcian. Alexis Black: "I remember you, the cocky young protege to Brian Michaels. And while the success of many of that man's proteges may speak for itself, you were the exception. You were the one with a future in this company, a former National Champion in the now defunct ICW. In fact, the first Nation Champion of ICW ..." It's true, the accolades of one Peter Kaymakcian speak for themselves. The man has, or at the very least had, the potential to do great things for the wrestling world. If you remember Peter K was a man that many considered to be a future World Champion in this company. So what changes things now? Well, in Black's opinion nothing. There's no movement from him as the breeze begins to settle, just as a few clouds drift overhead bringing in a few shadows. Which leave Alexis looking damn near ominous as he stands tall in anger. Alexis Black: "So you leave and return a year later with a little less hair, and a little more meat on your bones and you want to tell me that your living a personal hell? Maybe the drones of fans will fall for it, but I won't because I know better then that. I know your history. I know your achievements and for you to belittle them is the mark of a desperate man." With this, all goes silent for a few moments. Alexis stands still, just looking towards the camera with a glare as though he's ready to pounce at any second. It's the kind of stare down you would expect in an old western movie right before Sheriff Woody drills a round between the bad-guy's eyes. Alexis Black: "I can see past the charade and to the real reasons behind this new act ... You're afraid." With a shake of his head Black turns again, showing his back to us as once again he begins walking away from any sort of civilization around. Finally the dark tone of his voice cuts through the silence. Alexis Black: "You're afraid that people won't accept you like they did before. You're afraid that the old antics of Peter Kaymakcian aren't acceptable anymore. So you've adopted this elaborate new persona, one that you think the fans will get behind because of the sob story your trying to sell as your life." He stops, leaving his feet shoulder width apart as he stands facing away from our cameras. Though we can't see his face, we can feel a disgusted smile creep it's way onto his face. Alexis Black: "See, that's where you and I are two very different people Peter. Because while your making up a tragic fairy-tale of a life to pander to the fans, I tell my story for reasons far beyond them." Alexis pulls his arm up from his waist, towards his head. Letting his hand run through his hair Alexis moves his eyes up to stare towards the sky. Alexis Black: "I tell my story, because in the end it will be what saves me from myself ... Because without my story, I'm nothing. I tell my story, because it's what drives me to step into that ring week in and week out ..." Once again the winds speed picks up, blowing a thick layer of clouds over head. Leaving just a few rays of sunlight to shine through, just enough to see the darkened silhouette of Alexis as he continues on with his speech. Alexis Black: "And whenever I step between those ropes there is always that one thing that never changes. Win or lose, there's always that constant drive in my heart to leave my opponents in a destroyed heap of flesh." Alexis stares out towards the horizon, his back still towards us. The sight we see would be inspirational if it wasn't for the way this man was talking. His silhouette stands tall and dark against the sun setting in the background. Alexis Black: "So Peter, come this Monday when the blood has settled I will leave you exactly like this place. Lifeless, hopeless and forgotten ..." With this Black let's his neck turn, the shadows on his face are now a slightly visible against the rest of the scene as he stares our camera zooms back to where we began our scene. Here, still standing tall is the skeleton of the cherry blossom. The once beautiful tree where Alexis played as a child has now been stripped of it's life. There is no other color then the dying brown of bark that stands alone against the sun.
