The steam coming off his now blue and unhealthy-looking hands completely evades his attention as he shifts his focus from the stream of water to his own blue-green eyes in the mirror, and then back to the flowing water. David: Alejandro Rodriguez… cost me my title. David looks back up at himself in the mirror, almost as if surprised by this revelation. His stare turns into one of determination. David: Yes… he did. He cost me my title. David’s stare becomes even more determined. David: Alejandro and I… we go a long way back. Back to the Lexington Era. Back to when Michaels and D.C. being in the same room meant comedy and not drama. Back to before I even got used to the IDEA of being in the main event. All it took was one face-off in that ring, and both of us, along with everyone in the WORLD, KNEW, for a FACT… that a Rodriguez-Whitman match would never, EVER be easy to call. We were too evenly matched. He pauses, tilting his head a bit as he reminisces. David: That’s why we always hated facing each other. It’s a wild card. Who knows what could happen. A four-second-long pause causes the atmosphere to gain a bit of tension as David takes a deep, silent breath before continuing. David: And then… we became… friends of sorts, from out of nowhere. It was unbelievable. Not just to him, or me… but everyone around. How the hell could these two bitter rivals, who go at it in that ring and try to rip each other’s throat apart like their lives depended on it, become friends? It didn’t make sense. It made no sense to anyone, not even Alejandro, not even me… until that match at Adrenaline Rush. David closes his fists tighter. David: That night made it clear to me how we could have struck up a faux-friendship… It was because of our in-ring equality. We never faced an opponent in that ring against each other, we faced ourselves. And during that first blood match, when Alejandro was out for my LIFE, and the tables didn’t turn but went out the f*ckin’ window… I realized that it was over. We would never face the same people in that ring again; everything changed that night. WE changed that night. I have to live with what I did to him, just as he had to live with what he did to me… somehow; I think he’ll be having a lot less trouble falling asleep. David raises his right eyebrow, and looks at his dark red hands. David: I can’t change the past any more than I can know the future, but I CAN change myself. It may not be easy, but I have to. And I have to make sure that when it comes down to the new Alejandro Rodriguez versus the new David Whitman, I don’t come out on the losing end. He pauses again. This time, he closes his eyes, and his face expresses a search for calmness. David: I have been D.C. for the last few hours. I have come to the conclusion that… I am NOT D.C., any more than D.C. is me. Yet at the same time, this contradicts a lot about my knowledge of him. Because if D.C. is really that guy… that nobody likes, that gets under everyone’s skin, that causes trouble everywhere and all the time… I wouldn’t even be here. The truth of the matter is that we both know we’re not enemies. We played videogames together and drank together. I don’t know if you’d call us friends, acquaintances, colleagues… but I do understand that it doesn’t matter for now. Because on Mayhem we will get into that ring. And both of us will want to win. All THREE of us… will need this win. Hell, this triple threat is going to be… insane. Alejandro, D.C. and I… we each have our own reasons for wanting to win… whether it be to prove that we deserve it more than the others… to give the others the fight of their lives… or simply because without the win, our self-doubts will have been right all along… This match is important. This is bigger than any Main Event, bigger than any MNM, bigger than any pay-per-view. This match is the struggle for what we’ve been searching for… and for what we’ve worked so hard to earn, and defend… David passes his hands through the hot water again, this time only for a second, and then he passes his hands over his face. David: D.C./Rodriguez. Rodriguez/Whitman. Whitman/D.C…. He passes his hands through the hot water one more time. David: Thank God for this Life Swap. And for this match. Because David Whitman, as you all may very well know, needed a kick in the @$$. And this was it. I understand D.C., I understand Alejandro. And you know what; all of this brings me to the main point of my little rant… He runs his wet hands through his hair in a frustrated manner before firmly placing both hands on the sink again. He turns to the camera and looks at it with a dead-serious look. David: I F*CKING hate David Whitman. He turns to the mirror, looking himself in the eyes yet again. David: But that’s going to change. Because I have to defeat Rodriguez, and I have to defeat D.C., and I have to get that gold. Turn it all around. Set everything right. Win. Once and for all. David turns off the faucet with his red right hand. The abrupt stop of the water flow shifts the tension in the atmosphere to a higher level as the silence now gives off a powerful, almost eerie vibe. David: … I have to change. He stands up straight, looks at himself for another few seconds in the mirror, and then at his fists. He looks back at the mirror, and turns, facing the door. He unclenches his fists, almost reluctantly, and leaves the fancy bathroom. The camera cuts to outside the restrooms as David walks over to the table and signals to Lindsey that it’s time to go. Lindsey gets up and follows him as they go outside and get into the car. Lindsey looks at David with an inquiring look at David just stares ahead of himself in a deep thinking mode. He starts up the car and the camera backs up and starts fading out as the car speeds off. The camera opens up again at D.C.’s house, in the living room. After a couple of seconds or so, the door opens and Lindsey enters. She takes off her jacket and tosses it onto the coat rack as David enters the house and closes the door behind him. He is still quiet, but Lindsey seems to ignore this as she stops walking and looks up at the stairs. She thinks for a bit before turning back. Lindsey: So about the sleeping arr- Lindsey stops moving and talking as she is met by a very serious David. They look each other in the eyes before David leans in a bit. Lindsey leans back slowly… a bit too slowly. As their lips touch, she puts her left hand on his shoulder, pushing him away lightly, too lightly… before she moves her right hand up to his neck. As she still tries pushing David away with her left hand, her right hand slowly caresses from the back of his neck to the side of his neck. They both pull back simultaneously, and are left looking at each other. As an awkward vibe starts to fill the deafeningly silent room, David decides to say something. David: … I’ll take the couch. Lindsey lets out a nervous giggle. Lindsey: No, that’s okay, you can sleep upstairs. She raises an eyebrow at him. Lindsey: Just don’t get any ideas. She still has her eyebrow raised as she points an index finger at his face, in that “watch it” manner, prompting David to let out a chuckle. Lindsey then starts heading upstairs as David turns to the camera with a blank expression on his face. He then slowly starts heading up the steps, holding the banister with one hand while wiping his face with the other. The camera zooms out of the house, all the way so that you can see D.C.’s car in the driveway as well as his house in its entirety. It then slowly fades to black.
