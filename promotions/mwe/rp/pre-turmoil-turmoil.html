Or else it wouldn’t be called a path; it’d be more of a stroll. An effortless, tireless stroll through no trouble, just a brisk walk down to the end. A path though, most of the time, is meant as a long road, full of obstacles, problems and hardships. It has been said that anything worth doing is no doubt going to be hard, and thus we deduce that the path works hand in hand with this concept; you go through hard times and then are rewarded with the spoils, if you will, of victory. Paths can differ in length and size and even type. For example, I myself have been on a path, like most, to get to a certain point in my life where I don’t have to struggle anymore; this is what I like to call the main path. Because almost everyone is on this path, knowingly or unknowingly, willingly or unwillingly. It starts when we’re young. It’s the reason we work, do favors, try to make money and try to make people happy. We’re trying to get to that point where you DON’T have to work, or worry about anything. Why we think that point even exists, is another story. But I digress. I am on other paths as well; my path as an MWE star which ends, hopefully, on a day that people will look back at my career, pitfalls and all, and smile. I am also on another path regarding MWE; that is the path which I am most focused on right now. Since my return to the Mayhem Wrestling Federation I’ve won the title and successfully defended it a couple of times. Right now, I am looking at what’s right ahead, and that is the Pay Per View. MWE Turmoil. My first PPV since winning the Heavyweight title. Such a short span of time, yet, a long and hard road.
