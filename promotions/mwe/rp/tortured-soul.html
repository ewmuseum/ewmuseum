Tortured Soul; Debut RP

Dec 27 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
“You ever dream you were alive only to waken feeling dead? i have many a time i have so, really this wont be to much of a change will it?” A man who went by the name of Jason Cross once spoke those words. Cross was a brilliant man, one of the best the industry had to offer. He was the reason people like Zak Warner got into the wrestling world. Sadly, the world is no longer graced by the presence of Jason, as the wrestling world took its toll on the poor man. Let’s flash back to that night actually, December 20th, 2006. A simple night, forever tainted by the aftermath of one of the greatest matches EPW had ever seen. “Cross tries to hold in, the pain in his eyes becoming more and more apparent. He reaches for the ropes but finds them far out of reach. He tries to counter but just wrenches his arm more. Finally...sadly...he submits. Luke: The winner of the third fall and NEW Primetime Champion... Brad Payne! Dan: ... Larry: Are you okay? Dan: I thought he could do it. Payne slowly rises to his feet, staring at his newly gained title. He then looks down at his rival, his friend. Cross is in tears. Payne helps him to his feet, and looks him in the eyes for a moment. He takes his hand and shakes it in respect before allowing himself a moment of emotion and giving him a hug. Cross holds him tightly, as though they are actually brothers. Larry: Wow... Dan: A rare moment of pure and true emotion here in an EPW ring. Payne gives Cross one last glance before leaving him alone in the ring. He makes a quick jaunt to ringside to grab a microphone and a bag, rolling back into the ring with them. Cross: So here I am. One last battle and I blew it. Congratulations to our new Primetime Champion. I promised this would be my last match and he made it perfect. A true work of art...a tragedy almost. I will always respect and appreciate him for that. At the end of the day though, I still lost. One last chance and I failed. So I guess there’s only one thing left to do. And with that, he pulls out a small gun from his bag, placing it against his temple and cocking it. There is a loud bang as the broadcast suddenly cuts to black.” Yes, what you just saw really happened. A tortured soul blew his brains out on live television, where the whole world could see. Why did he do it though, that is the real question? Could it have been that losing finally drove him to the breaking point? Or was it something deeper, rooted where only he could see? We fade to black, but only momentarily. The camera comes back, as it always does. Outside, the skies were following the example of the mirror. They wept onto the city, people feverishly escaping the liquid depression. The various attires of the fleeing public all seemed the same, dull and largely lifeless, the mood and feel seemingly mimicking or altered by the world itself. Beads of water slowly trailed down the windows in Warner’s apartment, the journeys captivating him. Drip, drip, drip. Umbrellas and newspapers rose above the streetwalkers heads and made their way towards cover while children danced in the downpour, the miserable wallowed in the drizzle, and the hopeless romantics braved the rain. Drip, drip, drip. A roll of thunder tore across the dim clouds. The speed of pedestrians increased on cue, a few paused and gazed into the heavens, some in disgust others in awe. Drip, drip, bang. Light struck far off ground, the expected crack accompanying nature’s light show. More people tilted their necks out from under cover to witness the blade of the gods carving the earth, but still the majority ignored the simple beauty of it, instead seeing the lightning as something created purely to make their lives that little bit worse. Drip, bang, crack. Warner stood and slowly made his way over to a window, grace lacking in his movements. His finger chased a drop of water as he gazed down into the street, his forehead against the chilled glass. “Raining, it’s always fucking raining.” He backed away and made his way into his kitchen, passing the glass balcony doors as he did, creating a faint reflection. The flash of lightning made the reflection disappear, but only momentarily. So many times, Warner has heard the voice inside his head, but now it seems as though it has taken on a shape, for once. “Oh come on Zak, why so serious? Could it be that you are scared of losing your debut in a new company? Or is it that you are finding yourself slowly traveling down the road that Jason Cross went?” As the echo faded from his ears, Zak turned to a nearby table and gripped an empty bottle of whiskey. In one, less then fluid motion he once again faced the glass and hurled the bottle through, glass joining the rain in the downpour upon the balcony. “Did you really think I would leave so easily? Come on Zak, you are smarter than that.” Unfortunately for Zak, there was more glass then the ones in his door. Although he was certain he had more then enough alcohol bottles to destroy each and every window, not all of them were empty. “Just fuck off.” That laugh, that mocking, trauma inflicting laugh. It is one that pierces every crevice of your head, making you gasp for air when you don’t even need to. “You know I will never go away Zak, so why do you fight it? Embrace your own insanity, before it drives you over the edge. You know that you already hold the gun, you are just missing the trigger.” The laughter begins once more, causing the room to spin at an increasingly nauseating rate. It stops spinning eventually, but only after Warner had collapsed on the floor. Static cuts across the camera, and when we finally come back into focus, the floor is empty, but the bits of shattered glass still remain. Draped in a chair is Zak Warner, a sadistic and cruel grin on his face. Yet even so, a bit of sadness can be seen. “It’s been over a year and a half since I watched my mentor, and good friend Jason Cross end his life before my very eyes, and the eyes of professional wrestling fans around the world. That night still haunts me to this very day, to the point where I wonder if I am going to suffer the same fate that he did. Day after day, night after night, I go out and give it my all…not only for the fans, but for myself, and for Jason. You see, the night that he died, a lot changed for me. No longer did I care about how much money I made, or how well my t-shirts sold. I just wanted to be able to live up to the standards that he set, without suffering the same fate he did. What I never understood though, was how people reacted after it happened. Some thought it was a cheap ploy to gain viewers, but those people weren’t there. The people who witnessed it knew differently. They saw his blood go flying out into the first row. They saw his brains explode from his skull like a bat out of hell. People said that for what he did, he should be forgotten. Those people are part of the reason that guys like Cross go nuts. Why would you forget a man who went insane, with nobody there to help him? How can you forget a man who killed himself, moments after the best match of his career, against his close friend?” A single solitary tear makes its way down his face, but he makes no attempt to hide it. His voice is full of emotion, a side of him that has never been seen. “What everyone fails to realize, is that even though as wrestlers we are in better shape then most, even though we have luxuries that others can only dream of, we are still vulnerable to the same emotional toils as normal people. Knowing everything you do now, can you blame me for doubting my own sanity? Fuck, this week I have to start fresh in a new company, in a place that I’m not even sure I belong in. Once again, I find myself put into a situation that I never wanted to be put in. Just like when I had to go up against Kevin Hardaway, flashbacks of that gun being shot off echoed in my head all night, and I lost. Any time I step into that ring with someone close to me, I can’t help but wonder if it is my last match. Could I snap and blow my fucking head off, just like he did? It wouldn’t surprise me if I did. Yet, at the same time, I know that I won’t suffer the same fate that he did, because we are two totally different people. The stress got to him, and he turned to different drugs and shit to calm his nerves, and they finally overtook his mind. Yea, I smoke, and I drink, but that’s the extent of it. Do any of you people know what it’s like to watch someone tripping on a mix of coke and heroin? Do you know what its like to watch someone fight for their life, knowing that there is nothing you can do to help? I didn’t think so.” “Enough about Cross and suicide Zak. Focus on MWE. Focus on The Hick you are facing” The tear remains on his face, but his voice is steady. It is as though Jason Cross has been wiped from his mind, and no longer does he worry. “I know most of you are expecting me to sit here, and talk about how I am going to dominate Billy Joe Fuckstick. The thing is, I can’t sit here and guarantee that anymore, because I don’t know what the fuck is going to happen around here. You see, this is a new place for me, and I have no idea if I can make it here. I see people like Joey Dallas, and I wonder if I’m still on that level. Yeah, my past accomplishments are awesome and all, but there is a reason they are all in the past. I haven’t been the same wrestler for months now, ever since GWC started to change. When I saw people I considered family getting screwed, I knew shit had changed.. It was at that point, that I wondered if anyone backstage was safe. When a fucking prick like Danny Vice can sit there and bitch about how people don’t like him, or don’t care about him, right after he screwed over Brad Kane, that is a serious fucking issue. When someone like Brad Kane, a legend in the business and a man I am proud to call a brother can be stabbed in the back by his own fucking flesh and blood, all over a bigger paycheck, how do I know that I won’t suffer the same fate. This isn’t GWC though, this is MWE and right now I have nobody. I am by myself in this place, and I don’t like it. I’m used to having people watching my back, and knowing that Billy Joe Fuckstick has his buddies waiting backstage has me wondering if this is going to be a fair match. Don’t mistake my worry for weakness though, because I will do whatever it takes, regardless of who is watching or who wants you to win. If Ollie tries to screw me out of this match, I won’t think twice about caving his fucking skull in. If ANYONE tries to interfere in this match, whether it be for you, or me, they are going to get knocked the FUCK out. This match needs to be between me and him, and nobody else. Before I end this little ditty of a promo, I think it’s time I run down the rest of the card, like I tend to do. Considering I haven’t been here all that long, I will explain my reasoning for each pick” He pulls out a list of paper, clearing his throat. This is always amusing. First off, the opening match of the night is my own. I’m a cocky sonofabitch, so of course I’ll pick myself to win. The second match involves one guy I’ve heard of, and one I haven’t. I was in a fed with Milenko once, and for that reason alone I pick him to win. Inferno matches aren’t my cup of tea, so I give that guy MAJOR props for walking into one of those. After that, A black guy takes on a guy with a mask. Mexican versus African…aren’t we all losers in that one? Nah, I’m just fuckin around…contrary to what some people think, I’m not a racist. Uh…lets go with the Mexican on this one, highflyers are fun to watch. The following match is one I don’t give a fuck about, as long as Maxx doesn’t win. As a matter of fact, if someone in that match manages to injure that bitch, I will personally pay them a very nice sum of money. Next, in what should be a very fun match to watch, Alejandro Borderjumper against David Whitman. I can’t really justify picking 2 different Mexicans to win in one night, so Mr. Whitman, you have my vote of confidence. Although, I will say having a mask helps in a first blood match. My advice, go for the back David. Sonny Williams is going to beat Vega and Wellings, just for the simple reason that I want to be the one to take the belt from Sonny. I will actually guarantee that Sonny takes this match, just like I will guarantee that I will be taking that belt soon. Elimination Tag Match…nobody wins. It’s like choosing between the Detroit Lions or the OKC Thunder for worst sports franchise ever. Paige versus Virus should be a good one, and has my vote for sleeper match of the night. I have seen Virus at his best, but I don’t think it will be enough to dethrone Paige. Not to mention she is the hottest bitch I’ve ever seen inside the ring. Paige…Call me. Mason versus Sinc for the US title…it has to be Sinc winning. We can’t have a Canadian winning the US title, that just isn’t right. It’s called the US title, not North American title. Sinc wins, or I riot. Ace Cannon and Joey Dallas face off next, and as long as there is blood, sweat, and tears…we all win. Well, except for Ace Cannon, because he will lose this match. There is just something about Joey Dallas that evokes confidence, so I pick him. Don’t like it? Suck my balls. The closing match of the night is sick. Plain and simple, I know I will be one of the people in the back glued to a monitor. There will be broken bones, people will bleed, we might even see a career or two ended. Michaels is good, there is no doubt about it…but D.C. is just that much better. I hope it doesn’t go to the third fall, because it would suck to hear either man say the words ‘I quit’. So, in closing, I hope you are all ready for a war, because that’s what this PPV is going to be on all accounts. The camera begins to fade, but stops halfway through as Warner points to the sky. This one’s for you Jason. Warner gets to his feet, and turns the camera off. He takes a nice deep breath, glad to be finished with it. He goes to lay down, when that unmistakable laugh echoes in the building once more. “Just fuck off.” That laugh, that mocking, trauma inflicting laugh. “I think not Warnerpoo, I quite like it here. You really shouldn’t have moved here, way more windows then your old place, and since it’s a new apartment, they’re all sparkling clean! I love it, but I don’t think you will. You should‘ve stayed in New York City.” Zak ran his hands through his hair, thinking more then seemed necessary. “Maybe I should’ve stayed there, god knows I wanted to, but I couldn’t alright? I just couldn't. So if you don’t mind, crawl back into whatever fucking shadows you came from.” “You couldn’t eh? Haha, I thought as much. You’re a glutton for mental anguish aren’t ya? Couldn’t stay away from the demon that clawed at your throat and singed your mind? You love the rush of torment.” “Shutup…” “You do! You fuckn’ do! You pathetic little bitch.” “Shut. Up.” “You just loooove playin’ the victim don’t ya Zak? As long as you can cry and hide and say ‘Woe is fucking me’, even if no one ever hears, just so long as you can say it, you’re happy aint ya? You are beyond pathetic. No wonder Suzanne fucked that guy behind you back.” He breaks. Rage set in, crimson covered his mind and eyes. And soon his hand and wrists as a bare fist shattered glass. He stood in the remains of his broken mind and it slowly returned. The chill on his throat, so frozen it burnt. He could almost feel the hand that accompanied the ice, but it never came. He dropped to one knee, ignoring the jagged edges of glass that peeled his flesh. “Come on Zak, pull it together. You’re just imagining things… there’s no frozen hand here… just your mind screwing with you more then usual. Where’s DeAngelo when you need him? Well Zak he’s probably off fighting some war and slaughtering hundreds to kill some time. Come now, that’s mean. I know. He’s off living his life, so it’s all you. Can’t disappoint the man, gotta get through this round of insanity. You can do it. Yeah.” Warner paused his rambling just long enough to feel the bitter chill gone from his throat. He didn’t move, waiting for it to return. It didn’t. He still didn’t move, waiting for the burning eyes to meet his. They didn’t. Relief washing over him like a cool breeze as he stood. Glass shards fell as he did so, the pieces that clung to his pants and skin were soon brushed away, blots of blood their gift to Zak. Through an increasingly hazy perception of his apartment, Zak staggered towards his bathroom. He barely reached his destination and threw open the painted black mirror that covered a small cabinet. A large amount of gauze quickly covered the mouths of the rivers of blood flowing from his arm, as well as the smaller cuts on his leg. He felt himself beg to fall to the ground, but he didn’t let himself. He dragged his feet across the floor, not letting the blood loss and mental drain win over his consciousness. He fought to his bedroom, questioning why he took himself there, before falling forwards onto his bed. He didn’t even get a chance to pull himself up from his mattress before pitch covered his eyes. -----~ Memories danced through his mind, blurred happenings of the past. Parties, drugs, alcohol, women. One blonde in particular whose name he never knew…[/B] “How’s going ‘dsome?” Her words dropped in and out of Zak’s ear, partially due to the noise surrounding them, mostly due to her slurring and his blood alcohol level. “How coul’ it net be f’n awesome!?” Both laughed at pretty much nothing. “Ya wanna go ‘ave some, some fun ayy?” Zak tried to speak, but his tongue failed him. Instead he nodded, barely. She led the way to the best of her ability, both her and Warner tripping over passed out bodies they couldn’t see, dancers that couldn’t dodge and inanimate objects they never saw but claimed jumped out at them at. As they did what they couldn’t call walking with any great confidence, Zak’s hands explored her body, not an inch left uncovered, neither of them would allow that. They soon reached an empty room in the house, and the instant the door was closed their clothes were off. She quickly jumped onto the bed and beckoned Zak over. He got on top of her and she pulled his lips to hers. The memory quickly turned black and made way for cinder eyes. -----~ Zak’s eyes sprung open, the customary sweat dripping from his forehead. His hand throbbed with pain as he used it to push himself up, his injuries slipping from his mind as he tried to comprehend what he’d just seen. “Who the fuck was that girl? Did we actually do it? Could’ve been just a dream… but I dunno, felt real enough to me… man I’ve gotta stop drinking… when the hell was that?” His head pounded along with the gashes in his skin. “Those eyes, what the hell is up with those eyes?! Why do I see them?” “Hate. They are your hate incarnate.” A voice wafted, entwined with the air. Zak searched for a source but quickly gave up. He’d dealt with enough mysterious voices to know that he could never find the person it matched. “Oh? And how do you know that?” “I know all about you Zak.” “Well soorrrry Mr. Omniscient Voice. If you know everything could you please tell me how I get rid of them?” “You can’t.” "That just plain fucking sucks doesn’t it?" “Not until you remove all you hate.” “I don’t hate anything or anyone.” “Not even yourself?” “Hell no.” “Really?” “No.” The room fell soundless as Zak thought deeply. He waited for the voice from the shadows to make itself known again, but it stayed in silence. “I don’t hate myself.” “Whatever you say Zak.” “I don’t…” Once again, quiet. “Haven’t heard you around for a while, you been getting your mask clean or something?” No reply came. “Why do you wear that thing anyway? I know you’re me.” “I wear it because that’s the way you imagine me.” “Fair enough…” Warner sat on his bed, trying to arrange everything in his head so he could make sense of it all. He struggled in vain with the information, the pieces not coming together for him, be it due to lack of knowledge or repression and a refusal to believe some things. “How do I remove hate I don’t have? I don’t hate anyone and I haven’t taken the drug in a long time. There’s not a single trace of hate in my being, none, nada, zip.” He put his hands on his good palm and shook his head. “I don’t fucking know… I need a drink.” Within moments Zak had a long black coat on and was making his way out off his home. He wasn’t totally sure how blood loss and alcohol would mix, but he decided that drinking in public would help minimize any risk posed. He briskly made his way through the generically inoffensive corridors of the apartment building, halls so bland and uninspired that they were almost sickening. They practically screamed retirement home they were that beige. “No way, he didn’t?!” “Oh he did.” “Get outa here! No way?” “Way.” “Ohhh mygod!” “I know.” Zak passed two neighbours gossiping about the latest exploits of some random person, not acknowledging their existence. They both stared at him as he made his way past, putting their conversation on hold. Once he was out of earshot, one gestured for the other to come closer. “How can someone that looks like that afford to live here?” “Apparently he’s rolling in some major money, wrestler or something.” “Get outa here! No way?” “Way.” “Ohhh mygod!” “I know.” Warner was soon inside the apartment building elevator, trying to avoid his reflection in the three mirrors that made up its walls. “You can’t hide from me Zak, you know it. So why try?” Warner just staring at the crack in between the doors in front of him. “Ignoring me eh? No matter, I know you’re listening. You don’t have to pay attention to me for me to say what I want.” Zak breathed deep. “You will crack Zak, you will give into your hate. Whether you admit it or not, you loathe everything, especially yourself. It’s festering inside of you, knowing away at your mind and your heart, wearing you down. You ignore it, but you love it. You love the feeling of despair and utter hopelessness it gives you. You relish every second of it. That’s why you drink. That’s why you do drugs. That’s why you fuck whores and sluts. That’s why you never go into rehab or get any form of real help. It all feeds your self-pity.” The sound of Warner tapping his foot with increasing speed echoed throughout the small enclosing. “Just admit it Zak, let it all flow. Let it take over, let it end you current existence and become something new! Something greater. It’s tried once before, and it’ll try again. And again and again and again until it succeeds. You can’t beat it Zak, you’re pathetic. You can’t beat yourself and your addictions, you’ll stand no chance against yourself and your hatred.” A ding and the doors slid open with mechanical precision. Wasting no time, Warner walked out, irritation and a hint of hidden fear in his step. A doorway later and he had left the cool, calm and faux serenity of the lobby behind in favor of the obnoxious, abrasive and heinous streets of. A far cry from the life of the neighborhood of his current residence, but he preferred this setting for a reason unknown. He carved his path through the crowded streets but saw or felt no one. His attention was occupied by the nothingness in his mind, his thoughts were occupied but he was thinking of nothing. He was simply lost in a moment of time as he tried to distract himself from something he couldn’t understand. He didn’t look away from in front of him until a silent voice called to him from within an alleyway. He stopped walking and gazed into it to see a woman standing in the shadows, facing away from him. He watched for moment, only to see a bullet tear through her skull, sending a mess of skull and brain to the concrete below before she could even fall. She dropped to her knees, her back then arching backwards so he landing on the top of her head, finally revealing her face. It was Suzanne. Horrified, Zak looked up. His eyes meeting them of the shooter. It was him. He tried to yell, or make a noise of any sort but his vocal cords froze as an invisible hand gripped them with a frozen touch. He felt the air get caught in his throat as the eyes of the hallucination in front of him combust and become a burning red. The body of Suzanne leapt to its feet, and along with the gunman that was he, begun to lurch towards Zak. He grabbed his neck and tried to pull away the hand that wasn’t there, the rest of his body unable to move. He watched in terror as the two things that should be there got closer and closer, their teeth and nails growing and sharpening. Other people stopped next to Zak and looked into the alleyway as he did, his expression of sheer terror intriguing them, but walked away disappointed when they saw nothing. Through fleeting and minute gasps of air, Zak tried to call for the help he knew would do nothing. All he could do was watch the creatures of his mind creep closure, inch by deadly inch and feel the life escaping him with every diminutive puff of air he could muster. Suddenly, the beasts of his imagination leapt into the air, the fire that was their eyes leaving a trail behind them. They brought their claws back in preparation to strike the target that couldn’t move. They swiped at Zak face and he closed his eyes moments before impact. In the darkness his eyelids created, he wondered if the fiends had slashed his face to red and if the black he saw was death. To answer himself, he opened his eye doors too see the life he had just thought he’d left. Suzanne and himself were gone, vanished as if they had never existed, and the hand that was strangling him couldn’t be felt. It was just him and a few people staring at him strangely. He quickly left the scene. Deciding to abandon the idea of getting drunk to hide from his troubles, so he instead found himself sitting in a taxi who’s target destination was a graveyard. After thirty minutes of avoiding his own reflection in the rear-view mirror, Warner was were he half wanted to be. Throwing the driver more then enough money to cover the fare, he stepped out and towards to what he considered to be the answer to his problem. “Get rid of all hate eh? I’ll give ya getting rid of hate. But I’ve got no hate to get rid of do I? Nooooo. So here’s the one thing I hated the most, oh yeah. This’ll work, it’s the only thing that can. So it will, fuck yeah it will. It’s here, I know it is. I was told it was, so unless I was lied to hate’s lying six feet under in this very yard of death.” After what seemed like an eternity of searching, Warner found what he was after. He stood in front of a small tombstone, the carving reading Carolyn Warner. His mother’s grave. He let his knees fall to the soft grass as he stared at the stone monument. “For years I told myself you were still alive, and that you the greatest, most vile bitch on the planet so I’d never want to come see you. I convinced myself that I loathed you, that you were alive, so I wouldn’t have to admit to myself that you were dead. But you are, I found you dead. I saw you. But I never wanted to know it. It’s time for me to stop running from what I know, I have to admit the truth to myself. I never hated you mom, I… I loved you, and still do, no matter what I tell myself.” Zak closed his eyes as he held back a few tears. After a moment or two of the calm only a graveyard can create, he open his eyes and stood. He spent another second looking at where his mother lay before turning around and walking away. What stood before him stopped him dead. Mere inches away from him stood a man with the exact same dimensions as him. Height, width, depth, everything was identical. With two exceptions. The man before Zak was entirely black, as if he was constructed from shadows. And his eyes, they were as red as an inferno. Warner couldn’t believe it, his mind all but shut down. “I… I what… fuck… no. No. No no no. I fixed this. You can’t be here, no. I did it. I cleaned the hate that was fogging up my spiritual window. I did it. It’s fixed. You should be gone. Gone gone gone. Gone!” With inhuman like speed, the shadow had his hand wrapped around Warner face and had lifted him unto his toes. Zak could feel the shadow’s fingers and hand cracking his skull, he could almost feel his bone shattering into his brain. He tried to kick the darkness, but hit nothing but air with each full contact hit. He gazed into the the blaze that was so close. He felt them burning, but no heat came. It was just sheer emotion that singed his flesh and scorched his consciousness. His life fading with each second, Warner waited for the end to finally come and for his mind to hit the proverbial rewind button so his life can flash before his mind's eye. Instead, he felt himself lunge backwards as the creature threw him, his feet catching the top of his mothers grave and sending him back to the soft ground. With a quick yet poorly executed backwards somersault, Zak was back on his hands and knees. He crawled quickly back to the tombstone and peered over it. The abomination was gone. Warner’ mind had broken. “What the fuck do I have to do! What… can I do? Someone, anyone, please tell me what I do to make this thing go away… for the love of god… save me.” He began pounding the top of the stone with his fists as the first few tears began to roll gently down his cheek and chin. “What… what do I do now? Please, just what?”.
