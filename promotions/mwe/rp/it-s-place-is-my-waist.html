It's Place is My Waist.

Oct 25 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A sunny day, where even the stars are shinning because the bright light. The majority view is green as in plants. Plains, hills, roads and stones are spread around the territory. Small children circling the environment as the play “Tag”. Parents sit on the benches as they’re proud and happy to see their children play joyfully. The music of the Ice Cream truck send sounds to every little kid’s brain and as the ice cream truck gets closer to the kids the kids run closer to the truck. It’ finally ready to start selling ice cream to grateful future. All the parents stand from their perspective seats as they head to their child’s location to reward them for their good behavior. The odd part was that everyone stood up to buy ice cream except for one. Jason Fear was in one bench alone looking towards the kids and smiling. Jason Fear was seating looking at the kids and decided to stand and walk up to the little kids. Fear: Free Ice Cream for Everyone! All the kids: Yay!!!!!!!!!! Fear with a big smile laughs at the little children and asks each on e what flavor or what type would they like. He then tells them to stop screaming because he has to give them an important message. The parents are looking at him thinking about this stranger buying ice cream for their children out of the blue Fear: Kids slow down please. Just one question to all you small people. Who watches wrestling? All the kids: MEEEEEEEEEEEE!!!!! Parent 1: Don’t put my son up to your immaturity. Fear: What immaturity? The parent and her child walk away in loud footsteps. Fear: What was that all about? Any ways kids I am a professional wrestler. Kid 1: What does that mean? Fear: I’m a real wrestler. Don’t you know who I am? Kid 2: I know who you are? You’re the guy who lost to Aeron Beowulf a long, long time ago. I like that match. Fear: No you’re confusing me with……oh whatever just leave. Fear walks away without caring about that loss back in his first days and just smiles. Fear: It’s just extremely beautiful out here. I’m enjoying the sunlight the kids playing and the sweet smell of fresh plants roaming through the air. My day is going just fine. But there’s a small little problem that’s been bothering me and ready to solve it. \Fear: Wrestle Wars II is going to be an epic event full of amazing battles and atrocious defeats. Me as proud as I am of myself and my accomplishments, I assure you Jason Fear is going to be included in both categories (amazing battles and atrocious defeats). As I have said many times before, I Fear no one and when you here Maxamillion Mustafa trying to act tough on Mayhem by trying to show of all his glory you already know that he’s starting to regret entering this match. He’s using his accomplishments to scare me but as you can all see it didn’t work as both I and Calloway manhandled Maxx and his titles weren’t there to save him. Now all you people watching this are asking yourself why I don’t say a word on what happened to me and Callaway afterwards. Well that is not a very interesting topic to be talking about so keep it to you. You’re going to forget about those events on Mayhem and remember the disturbing moments that are going to be occurring at Wrestle Wars to both Maxx and GM. Fear: Maxx you should retire because your juice in professional wrestling has already been spilled. You’re not the same Maxx who was the Hulk Hogan of EW or the same Maxx who became the EW World Undisputed Champion. You’re just worthless at this point. Maxxamillion you are not familiar with the new and younger folks like me. If it’s possible I would tell the MWE Chairman Richard Covell to tie on of my arms to my back in the match this Sunday and MAYBE, just maybe that will be fair. I mean not even Grand Mystique could help you out in this match. He’s still that little dorky fag that can’t get good reputation. That’s the only reason he’s teaming up with you Maxx because you have the popularity. GM and Maxx it’s not just going to be the beating of your lives but included a month or two out off MWE because of your disabilities in the ring. Fear: Let me not get started on the hooligans. Jason Fear’s status against those clowns…UNDEAFEATED. I have no idea how Richy keeps giving them chances. Enough is enough damn it. No more of that bull shit. If anything goes wrong in that match it’s because they mess up PR’s chances and Project Revolution is not the faction you should interfere with, especially when it has not been a good day. But look at the bright side. You lose oa Wrestle Wars never get another chance at the title and never again would you have to face us. That is most likely the best way to keep both your ass on your feet. Fear stops at a hot dog stand and buys himself a hot dog with ketchup and mustard. He takes out his wallet and all he finds nothing. Fear: Oh shit where the fuck is money. Devlin: You should of never said that. Devlin punches Fear in the jaw. And Fear falls over the hot dog stand (poor hot dog stand). Then Devlin throw Fear’s wallet back at him and walks away. PS: Extremely Confusing Ending I know.
