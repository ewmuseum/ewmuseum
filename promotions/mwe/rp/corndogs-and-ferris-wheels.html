Corndogs and Ferris Wheels

May 30 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Blight (Damien) Dr. Chris Charlie the Guard Joanna (Jezibel) Scotty (FreakShow) Leo (Martyr) Prologue The scene opens up into a room with a couch, a rather large desk with a large cushy leather chair and a large window with the drapes tied off about half way so the sun light brightens the room. Sitting on the couch is a young boy with long black hair. He looks rather distressed and aggravated. On the bottom of the screen the date of October 25, 1989. Ok Damien my name is Dr. Chris Hambrook; I’m here to help you. Here is a piece of paper and some crayons do you mind drawing me a picture. Dr. Chris slides a blank piece of white paper and a box of Crayola crayons towards a young Damien. The child looks at them blankly for a moment before taking out a crayon and starts to doodle. So Damien what do you plan on drawing for me today? The boy doesn’t answer and just keeps doodling with his paper covered as if he is hiding something. Ok so you want to keep it a secret and show me when you’re finished, that’s absolutely fine. Let me ask you Damien what do you like to do in your spare time? Do you draw often, or do you play sports, listen to music? What is your favorite thing to do? Damien slowly lifts up his head and stares at the doctor. His cold blank eyes looking straight through him. The awkward silence starts to make the doctor a bit uncomfortable before he speaks again. Uh…are you done with your drawing Damien? The boy nods his head and slides the paper face down back towards the psychiatrist. The doctor picks it up and studies it quite puzzled for a moment. The colors of black and red are seen smeared all throughout the image. Damien can you tell me what you drew? Who is this person here? That’s you… Ok and who is this here? That’s me… And what is that red blob in your hand? That is your heart…. FADE TO BLACK. SCENE: The scene opens to Blight standing outside on a dirt road. There are dozens of families with a dozen more litle children running around. Blight looks up and reads the sign above his head, it reads SLAPPY BOB’S MOVING CARNIVAL. Home sweet home… He starts to walk into the carnival but his steps studder as he almost trips over a litle girl running to her mommy with some cotton candy. Blight takes a look around and sees the dunk tank with a middle aged man taunting all of the kids to drop him in until finally a small boy does so. The smell of corndogs fills the air as he passes the food tent. The glee full screams but young children just enjoying their childhood at their favorite place that comes around once a year, are overcome by gasps of shock and soon after applause. About twenty yards ahead of where Blight is walking is a rather large tent with a large sign outside that reads SLAPPY BOB’S FREAKSHOW! COME ONE COME ALL AND SEE EVERYTHING YOU’VE NEVER SEEN BEFORE! Instead of walking in through the front opening of the tent Blight decides to walk around and into the back where there is a dressing area. There he is met by a plump security guard. I’m sorry sir this is for employees only. No access. Blight just looking at the man continues to walk past him. Sir I said no access, now please go back the other way. Blight still ignoring him continues to the rear entrance. The guard runs up and grabs Blight by the shoulder and spins him halfway around. I told you NO ACCEE------ Oh my god your back! A woman who had seen and heard what was going on through the opening of the rear entrance comes running out. Its ok Charlie hes my guest you can go back to your post now. The guard just grumbles and wobbles away weezing. I figured you would be here Joanna, are you still headlining the show with your Jezibel the All Powerful Seer act? Damien of course I am did you expect me not too? Oh wait you have to come inside theres someone you need to see, come on follow me hurry up! She grabs his arm and quickly drags him into the dressing tent. There are about a dozen and half of people all practicing for their acts. Right by the entrance are five midgets practicing some sort of acrobatic act, the one keeps falling and making the others fall. A few of the other acts find this funny and openly laugh until they see Blight walk in and quickly shut up. Silence falls over the tent only to be broken by Jezibel’s wisper. They see you as a regular…they don’t know any better yet. Yo guys give him the right treatment…this man here is a carnie legend! Blight turns around and faces the far corner where a man about six foot two is hanging from a contraption with hooks bearing out of his back. Ladies, gentlemen and things we arent sure of yet, this is Damien Stevens. He was one of us back in the day…hell I’ve seen do things we wouldn’t have the guts to do.Hey Damien you still have that bite mark from that bear on your ass? Holy crap Scotty FreakShow Maggard! What’s up bro? Its been a while man. Blight starts to walk towards the contraption as a few of the others move out of his way most of them not paying attention anymore, the rest still giving him dirty looks. Not much man just…hanging around hahaha. Hey gimme a hand getting out of this thing will ya? Oh hey Jez can you clear the room for a moment maybe we should all just chill just us. Ok everyone OUT! Shoo, go somewhere else just not here. Out out out! The tent starts to clear as Blight is helping unhook FreakShow, there is a sudden burst heat across Blight’s back. Damnit Leo watch where your blowing that thing won’t you. Well I figured I would welcome our old buddy back with a warm welcome. Without turning around Leo you grumpy pathetic fire eating bastard! Blight whips around and his met with a hug that lifts him off his feet. The group still all happy about seeing each other moves along to a large lunch table and sits down as Jezibel brings over some beers and sits down herself. Fifteen years Damien, its been fifteen years since you’ve been in this tent with us. It was like you decided to really go through with wrestling…made it to fame, true fame…and forgot us. Why? Jo, come on drop it. Shes right Scotty…but Scotty is right as well Joanna. I don’t think now is the--- Guys guys its fine, she deserves and answer all three of you do. But I can’t give you guys one…just an appology. The group sits in an awkward silence for a moment before Leo lets out a vicous burp and the four just start laughing. So Joanna and Scotty have been doing their thing here, what ever happened to your wrestling carreer Leo? Well remember that promotor? Remember Becky my wife? Yeah well, I found them screwing in the parking lot at one of our stops. Lets just say, that guy is a few olives short of a martini for a while…and four years later they let me out of prison. Leo smiles and starts to chug his beer. Wow dude I would say sorry to hear that but something tells me you really don’t care anymore so…I’m just gonna have some more beer. Blight walks over to the cooler that he saw Joanna go to and takes out four more beers and brings them to the table and sits back down. So dude we saw you on tv a lot for a while doing your thing in the ring, and then you disapeared, what are you doing now? Yeah well I’m still wrestling just for a different organization. A few more of the actors walk into the back and grab some food and stuff, they sit at a table a bit farther away. So where is it? Yeah man where is it? Come on let us see it! What the hell are you guys talking about? Dude the belt, the title where it is? Yeah Mr. World Champ! Hahaha oh ok, yeah not yet guys not yet. I’m only about to have my third match, although I should have a number one contenders spot but things happen. Things happen? Come on Damien that’s not like you. Ok well the first match was a five man battle royal, so basically it took four other guys to put me out. Then I had a fatal four way for a number one contender stop at a title called the X Tv Title. Lets just say the guy im facing this week Devlin Sharpe…well hes the ass that lost the match and not me. So who got the shot? Does it matter its only a matter of time before Damien has the belt right bro?! Damn right man, but one of the guys who was in the battle royal as well exept the messed up part is he never beat me. Plain and simple, but that’s another story. Now the sob story in all of this is that guy Devlin I mentioned. Why is that? Is he a gimp or something? Does he have like an arm for a leg and a stick for an arm? You are both idiots! I do not know how nor why I deal with you! Blight laughs loudly which gains him some more dirty looks from the other actors who came in earlier. Blight shrugs them off and grabs another beer before finishing. Nah word in the lockerroom is, is that hes on a downward spiral as of late. So basically I’m being fed this bump to finally put him down. In other words I’m taking care of the company’s dirty work, and they are giving me just another soul to ruin. Joanna gets up and clears the table of empty bottles, she walks over to some other actors and starts clearing their garbage as well. Ya know Damien…I miss wrestling with you and Leo, that was really fun. You two were the most brutal friends a guy could have. Don’t start crying Scotty cause I swear I’ll beat you with a shovel. But yeah Damien it would be fun to get back into it all Well how bout this… Blight reaches into his pocket and pulls out three pieces of folded paper and an envelope. I have already spoken to my boss and put in a good word. I had hoped I would find you guys and obviously did. Here are three contracts on for Scotty “FreakShow” Maggard, one for Leo “Martyr” Pryce, and one for Joanna “Jezibel” Sanchez. If you three are serious, the show will be in town this week, come by and drop them off on my boss’ desk. If not here in this envelope are three tickets at least come and watch me waste this pathetic waste of life Devlin. Damien…you understand we really need to think about this. I mean the carnival is all we really ever known. Is this a good idea. Look the travel schedule is the same, the people we have to put up with are the same…except when we wake up we won’t smell like horses. Well Scotty will of course. The three begin to laugh as Scotty just shoots Leo a dirty look. A voice over the PA startles the group. PA: Attention loyal patrons Slappy Bob’s Moving Carnival will be closing shortly, we will reopen tommorow at four pm for your entertainment needs. Before everyone leave we encourage you to stay and watch our very own freakshow where we promise you will see everything you have never seen before with our main attraction Jezibel the All Powerful Seer. Well dude that’s our cue. We should start getting ready. Listen why don’t you stay and watch the show its been a while since you’ve seen us. Yeah babe comeon it will be like old times Only if I can get free beer! Of course put it on Leo’s tab haha! The scene fades out with Leo chasing Scotty out of the tent and Blight heading for the stands.
