The Sanctuary; Blight/Crash rp

Jun 18 2009

Blight & Zack Crash

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Key Blight Richard Covell FreakShow Jezibel Martyr Prolouge We see a video package start of last weeks match between Blight and Devlin Sharpe. Back and fourth action continues to play in chunks until finally the ending where Devlin gets chokeslammed straight to hell repeats itself numerous times. Witch Hunt by Kittie is just barely heard playing as Blight walks off of the stage in celebration. He walks into the back and gets to his locker where he sees a note that reads. My office after you match...NOW!! Richard Covell Blight crumples the note and walks out of the lockerroom again. Blight walks down the white halls of the arena past a few stage hands who if they aren't congradulating him they are looking at him wierdly. Finally he reaches a door that has Richard Covell's name on it. He knocks hard three times before being answered to come in. Blight enters and sees a large desk with an even larger leather chair in the middle of the room with the man himself the boss Richard Covell. You wanted to see me boss? Blight...there are a few things we need to adress. Mainly your performance here in the MWE. I am sorry to say that we must go our seperate ways based on the fact that your talent isn't as up to par as we would like. Wait what? Mr. Covell what is going on here you can't be serious?! Blights voice slowly starting to get higher and you can hear how nervous he is as the moments go on. Sweat starts to build on his forehead, his eyes wide and mouth open slightly in awe. Blight I am truly sorry your time here was so short but buisness is buisness...we will be mailing you your final check in about a week or so. There has to be something I can do! One more match let me prove myself! The panic in Blights voice is very noticeable at this point. Blight again I am sorry. Covell extends his hand for a handshake, Blight takes a second still absorbing the fact that he is fired shakes his hand and screams in pain. Blight quickly pulls his hand away as Covell laughs hysterically and reveals a joy buzzer taped to the palm of his hand. Blight looking very annoyed and confused at the same time just glares at Covell. The side door to the office where Covell's secretary sits swings open as we hear more laughter and a voice mocking Blight is heard as three people enter the office. "Oh no Mr. Covell don't fire me, you can't" HAHAHA! Yeah Damien you actually looked scared. Dude you are pathetic... Blight, I took you word on your three friends and their contracts were approved immediately, The Dark Karnival are officially apart of the MWE. Blight still looking utterly bewildered at what just happens starts to laugh at himself and what has just happened. FADES OUT. SCENE STARTS We see Blight sitting on his Harley in some parking lot behind an old firehouse. He is wearing a pair of black jeans, boots, an Iron Maiden shirt and a pair of dark sunglasses. The silence is broken as the loud rumbling of a 1969 cherry red Camaro rolls up. The engine cuts off a FreakShow steps out wearing a pair of blue jean shorts a pair of black vans and a sleeveless Slipknot shirt. He takes his sunglasses off and hangs them on his shirt as he walks over to Blight and shakes his hand. A half hour late as usual Scotty haha. Are you ready to start? Yeah yeah Damien get your shots in now. Let me ask you...you said we were gonna train right? Well I don't see a ring, where are we going?center] Just follow me, and keep up. The two men start to walk around to the side of the old firehouse where there is a small set of stairs leading into the building. Blight takes out a key and opens the door and steps in with FreakShow following. The shade of the building feels rather nice compared to the summer heat outside, although the smell of stale air fills their noses. You will get used to the smell...or just puke from it. He hits the lightswitch on the wall a few feet from the door, as dozens of lights come on and illiminate the building. FreakShow looks on in suprise as the lights reveal various weight machines, a few tredmills strewn about and a mock ring set up deadsmack in the middle of the room. Blight takes his shirt off and looks at FreakShow. Welcome to the Sanctuary, here is where I train. There is a bathroom over in the far corner, and the door next to that is a shower. If you go off to the right I have a kitchen, theres some food but the fridge is mainly filled with beer. I also had a few spare rooms left that I put some beds and stuff in for you Martyr and Jezibel if you guys ever need a place to crash. Wow dude this is amazing! How did you manage to get a hold of this place? He takes a few steps in circling himself just looking around smiling. He takes his shirt off as well and runs for the mock ring jumping in and hitting the ropes a few times. He jumps out and looks around some more. Well they were going to demolish it after they moved the firehouse across town. I was able to buy it about a year or so ago. Lets hit the weights first. They head over to a bench press machine where Blight adds about fifty pounds to the already 200 on the bar. FreakShow lays down and grabs hold as Blight spots him. This really is gonna be like old times man, traveling the road together, drinking, having fun, and the best part wrestling together again. FreakShow after about ten or so reps puts the bar back up and switches places with Blight. Hell yeah man, and you got this sweet place to train and stuff. Listen I really need to thank you for giving the three of us this chance man it means alot ya know. Especially for Jo. Since the divorce she had just been really down and stuff and you basically got her to snap out of it. Blight pumps the bar a few more times before racking it back up. He sits up and wipes the sweat from his face before turning back to FreakShow. Dude don't worry about it man you guys are like family to me. When is her divorce final anyway? They start walking over to the tredmills. The paperwork should be done within the next few weeks and then her and Slappy Bob Sanchez are no more. She really could do alot better than that fat slob. They get over to the treadmills and start them up the hum of the motors sound louder than normal as they echo off the walls. Yeah dude she really can...Hey man i gotta thank you for coming with me this week to train. Of course why wouldn't I, we both could use the boost. Both men start to run faster almost as if they are racing. Well I've heard alot of good things about this kid Zach Crash, he has quickly become the buzz of the lockerroom. Although these guys haven't seen you hang yourself from your needles and shit. Haha yeah they will get a kick out of that. Hey the kid may be good but you should know for a fact that you shouldn't listen to what other people say. They start to run even harder sweat pouring off of both their faces. They keep this up for a few more moments before stopping. They both get off the machines panting and gasping for air Blight motions to FreakShow to head for the kitchen with him. They walk into the kitchen and FreakShow jumps up and sits on the counter as Blight grabs two bottles of water from the fridge. Both men chug them down and start to cool off. I don't know if this kid Crash has more balls than brains or what man. He says he has no fear, and that he knows he is out matched...yet he still wants to go through with it. I don't know man. He thinks he is a daredevil...well lets see what he thinks when he stares the devil in the eyes. Damien you know first hand how guys who don't care about their own well being wrestle. They can be the most dangerous off all. Just look at me, you know how I do it. Blight now taking a few beers out and leaning on the fridge opening them. Yeah and I can guarantee that he has had to have as many reconstructive surgeries as you have just from crashing and burning...no pun intended. I don't know man one false move and I will make that kid a grease stain on the mat. FreakShow takes a large swig from the beer. Yeah true I just think you need to be carefull out there with him he can be unpredictable. His main purpose is to be the biggest thrill in MWE...but at what cost. The world will find out on Monday when he steps into the ring with the creator of fear. I really hope Crash knows what he is getting himself into. I have said for a long time, you cannot kill what you did not create...and Crash you cannot kill evil. Blight takes another swig of beer before throwing the bottle against the wall. The glass shooting everywhere and beer running down the wall. Blight looks at FreakShow who is sitting there staring at the floor afraid to look up at Blight. FreakShow that beer spill on the wall represents what will be left of Zach after he takes his Bullet Ride. FreakShow's cell phone starts to ring, he looks at it and looks at Blight. Its Jo i told her I would pick her up from her spa treatment when she was done...maybe we should get going. Blight looks at him and looks back at the beer splatter before heading for the door. FreakShow looking at Blight leave makes a face like whoa before following. The scene ends with FreakShow getting back in his Camaro and pulling off with Blight following him on his Harley as the Sun sets with a red color in the sky. END SCENE
