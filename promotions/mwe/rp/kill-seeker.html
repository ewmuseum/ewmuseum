Kill Seeker; A Really, Really Bad [Alexis Black] RP

Aug 6 2010

Alexis Black

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"This is the final boarding call for United Airlines, flight 346. Nonstop service to Washington. All preferred passengers should be on board at this time. Thank you." People of all sizes and types breeze quickly past our camera. Everything between suit clad businessmen and t-shirt wearing, 'party hard' type teenagers, all carrying or rolling luggage behind them try to rush through possibly the slowest system in America. To say that todays scene was one of eerie silence would be the farthest thing from the truth, in fact it's the complete opposite. We open to the controlled chaos of hundreds, even thousands of travelers scurry around the Chicago O'Hare International Airport as the PA system's power clicks off, leaving the sound of hurried footsteps and frantic voices to attempt to ease us into today's events. Through the blur of people we slowly begin to focus in on one in particular, who's outfit looks as though it was carelessly thrown together this morning. A nearly bald head and goatee accompany the white wife beater thrown over his muscular physique. This is the kind of guy, that just by looking at him you know that he thinks the world revolves around him. "Hurry up!" With the concerned and angered look of running late on his face this man looks behind himself, having to slow his quick pace and come to a halt in the middle of a busy section of the airport. He slings his luggage off his shoulder and let's it fall beside his feet as he stands tall with that "What the fuck" expression just as a second one of these assholes walks up. This next guy looks younger, but is pretty much a carbon copy of our first subject. Same clothes, same haircut only this time he's clean shaven. His expression tells us that he's about fed up with the older man standing before him while he takes his time to get with in talking distance. "You really need to calm your shit Matt. We still plenty of time to blow before we take off." This Matt guy grabs the younger one by the neck of his wife beater and pulls him in close to him. It's not really a threatening kind of grab though, we can see in his eyes that he's not about to hit this kid in the middle of a crowded place, but he's pissed off. Matt: "Don't tell me to calm my shit Jake, we've got fifteen minutes to make it across this damn place and get on board, and I'm not in any kind of mood to let with your slow ass fuck up this deal!" With that Matt gives the younger kid, Jake a soft shove backwards. He holds back a laugh, but a little smirk manages to make it onto his lips as though he's amused by the way he's pissing off his older brother. Matt just shakes his head, throwing his luggage back over his shoulder as he begins walking off in the direction of their terminal. Our camera remains on the face of Jake who finally lets out a little chuckle now that he believes Matt is out of ear shot. Matt: "Now hurry your ass up .. And wipe that smile off your face before I do it for you!" We notice Jake mouthing the word 'Shit' as he slumps his shoulders in that way a scolded dog does before taking a hurried pace to catch up to Matt. Our cameras shifting focus to follow behind the duo. We simply follow them for a few minutes, both of them keeping silent as they try to rush their way through the crowd. Weaving in and out of people rushing the opposite way or simply walking too slow for their liking, until suddenly Matt stops dead in his tracks. Matt: "Shit!" Not paying attention to what's going on Jake ends up walking right into his older brother, which snaps him back to reality. His head shoots up as he realizes that he's actually walking through an airport rather then whatever his little day dream was about. Jake: "Whoa! What the fuck man?" Matt doesn't seem to hear as he frantically looks around, glancing in every direction. Quickly he swings his bag off his shoulder and thrusts it towards Jake, hitting him square in the chest forcing him to grab it. He looks at the bag, and then to his brother with a questioning look, he opens his mouth to speak but before he can Matt cuts him off. Matt: "Run." Jake: "What!?" Matt: "You heard me ..." Matt grabs Jake's shoulder and pulls him forwards to the point he has to push him sending him stumbling forward a few steps. The kid looks back over his shoulder, looking for an answer. Matt: "Run, and if you even think about looking back I'll kick your scrawny ass!" There's a certain hectic panic in his voice that Jake picks up on, and with that he just gives his brother a nod and bolts off leaving Matt standing alone as people brush past him. All the while looking around in desperation as our cameras finally catch sight of what he's trying to avoid. Standing about 20 yards away, dressed in a blue uniform are two members of airport security. One leans over to talk into the others ear for a second before backing off and pointing in the general direction of our wife-beater adorning subject of attention, much to his displeasement. With a sigh he let's out one last remark before shit gets crazy. Matt: "Here we go ..." He takes a deep breath in and then it begins, he dodges past an older couple and begins running for dear life. Top speed, he attempts to avoid the inevitability of getting caught. Matt glances over his shoulder every chance he gets to make sure security isn't following him, however he does it once too often and ends up colliding with another traveler. Almost as if in slow motion we watch as Matt hits face first into a man his complete opposite. This new arrival wears almost all black, from his D.C. shoes, to his cargo pants all the way up to a sleeveless t-shirt adorning a blood red skull right out of the Punisher movies. A tuff of mid-length black hair clouds his face as he plummets to the ground, hitting the smooth yet hard tiles with a thud just as Matt does the same. The impact sends us back into full speed with Matt's face grinding across the floor, leaving a huge red mark across his features as he pushes himself to his feet. Looking down at this new man in disgust. Matt: "Watch where your going you emo piece of shit." Now holding his face in visible agony Matt starts to slowly limp his way off again, as always watching over his shoulder trying to keep away from security. Meanwhile just a few short feet back we watch as a man in his mid-forties attempts to help this 'emo piece of shit' back to his feet, only to be gently pushed away when he reaches his knees. Hair still thrown over his face he looks up towards Matt. "You're fucking pathetic." That line catches Matt's attention, and he swings around to see that the man he just insulted is none other then Alexis Black. However from the look on Matt's face, we can tell that he's completely unaware of what this man is capable of doing. Matt looks infuriated, ready to blow at any second as Black pushes himself off his knees and stands back on his feet. He stands tall despite the pain, and looks his accidental assailant in the eye. Matt: "What the fuck did you just say to me?" Pissed, he begins limping as quickly as he possibly can towards Black. Fists clenched by his sides as though ready for a fight, trying to intimidate the smaller man. But Alexis stands tall, not letting this other guy get to him. Alexis Black: "I said that you're a pathetic excuse for a human-being ..." Nostrils flared like a bull, Matt's adrenaline begins pumping at a whole new level. Rampaging towards Alexis he grabs him by the throat of his shirt. He cocks his fist back ready to throw a punch towards "God's Unwanted Child" Alexis Black: "What!? You didn't think people would know what your doing here? .. Well news flash asshole, looking over your shoulder for security while bolting through the place isn't exactly incognito" Matt cocks his fist again, but that last line sets off a reminder in the back of his head. With that 'awe shit' expression he peers around making sure security is no where to be found before pummeling this asshole. Instead he finds that the duo is surrounded. A group of on lookers ready to see a fight, it sets him off and he shoves Alexis backwards towards the bystanders. Matt: "You don't know shit!" He takes a step back and squares up ready for what he thinks is going to be an easy fight, but little does he know that's not about to happen as Black simply pushes himself off of a couple of people in a crowd and let's off with another verbal onslaught. Alexis Black: "I think you'd be surprised at just what kind of shit I do know.. See, I know that you just threw your little brother under the bus to save your own ass!" Black steps forwards, causing Matt to take a step of his own back as his actions from earlier replay in his head. Shoving that bag into Jake's arms and telling him to run, but in his mind Matt did that to protect him. Alexis Black: "I don't care what kind of fucked up drug you had in that bag .. That's right, I saw your little hand off buddy, and it's bad enough that you've got yourself into that kind of crap .. But to drag your own brother down with you is something you deserve to be locked up for." There's a new kind of passion in Alexis' eyes that we haven't seen since his days in ICW as the self-proclaimed "King of the World" as though a less brutal, and more rebellious kind of side is starting to shine through again. Matt: "I-I don't know what your talking about ... You don't know me, you don't know what I've been through!" Matt's expression on the other hand shows one completely different. He's still in a rage, but in his eyes we can see that Black is striking a nerve that sets him into a certain state of emotional agony. Alexis Black: "You don't think that I've never had hardships in my life!? Well let me tell you, that's all my life has ever been .. But that's no excuse for becoming a cracked out, drug dealing junkie like you. You can try to deny it, but it's clear as day that's what the hell you are, and there's something you need to learn ..." Black begins circling around Matt who just stares back, tear filled eyes and an expression of anger slapped over his rugged features. Matt's fists are still clenched tightly as Alexis casually walks around, just staring back at him. Matt: "Yeah, and what's that exactly?" Alexis Black: "With every high ..." An evil smirk crawls over his face as he lets out a chuckle. He takes a small step sideways, still looking Matt in the eye. Alexis Black: "There's a crash." "GET ON THE GROUND DIRTBAG!" As if on cue two security guards break through the crowd, guns drawn and pointed towards Matt who's face instantly shows an expression of fear. Raising his hands he slowly drops to his knees. Both guards now rushing towards him he looks up at Alexis, a look in his eyes asks how he knew but MWE's own Emo Warrior simply begins to walk away as Matt is thrown face first to the ground. "You're under arrest for suspicion of possession of cocaine with the intent to traffic." The arresting officer pulls out his hand cuffs and slaps them on the wrists of Matt, the other officer still has his gun drawn and pointed towards the drug dealer. "You have the right to remain silent, anything you say can and will be used against you in a court of law." Matt: "Where's my brother?" Matt's voice cracks as he asks this question, tears now streaming down his face as he realizes exactly what he's got his family into. His terrible decision to pedal drugs has landed him in a whole heap of trouble, and he's dragged Jake down with him. We may never know however as our cameras now begin following the dispersing crowd, more specifically Alexis Black as he throws a black duffle bag over his shoulder. A few yards away he stops and turns to glance back at the scene he was just moments ago a part of, and very much responsible for. As he looks back, a tear slowly falls down his face/ Feeling the wet sensation drip down he looks towards the sky. Alexis Black: "I'm sorry mom ..." This adds a whole new level of wonder to why Alexis just did what he did .. Why is he apologizing to his mother? And if he's really 'God's Unwanted Child' then why was he looking towards the heavens? There are so many questions left unanswered here, but as usual with Alexis Black we can get the feeling that finding the answers isn't going to be easy. With that our scene slowly fades out with Alexis staring back at Matt being hauled through Chicago's airport in handcuffs. Adrenaline means you won't even feel the pain. Until it's too late. Our next scene opens with the bad quality of a handheld camcorder stuck in the face of Alexis Black. Down the right side of his face the once perfectly applied eyeliner is now smeared down his face, dried and left there carelessly. His long, black hair is slicked back and just as the sound of a roaring engine breaks the silence a ray of sun beams over his hair, causing the black and red to shine in a brilliant radiance. Letting out a sigh he turns his head and let's the rays of light shine over his face, and as quickly as it came the sun is gone again. Hidden behind a sheet of clouds. Black moves the camera around revealing to us that he is seated in a nearly empty jet bound to Detroit for the next edition of Monday Night Mayhem. Several rows behind him are empty, and even the seats that aren't find themselves homes to passengers that have either fallen asleep or are zombified, lazily staring at the on-flight movie. Alexis Black: "You know Zack, this is usually the part where I start spewing off a bunch of bullshit similarities between me and whoever I'm supposed to wrestler. But this time it's gonna be a little different ..." He swings the camera back to show himself gazing out the window, down towards the earth beneath thousands of feet beneath him. There's a certain glow in his eye as he speaks, a look of remembrance of a horrible memory on his face. Alexis Black: "This time the similarities find themselves in you, and the low life pile of shit I just had to deal with. Now you may be asking yourself how poor little, co-number one contender Zack Crash is anything like a hardened drug lord, but it's all pretty clear ...." Dragging his eyes away from the view out of the window, Alexis stares into the camera in a way you know it's time for the preverbal wrestling promo. Cold blue eyes piercing through the camera and right into the eyes of Zack Crash. Alexis Black: "I saw a look in his eyes, that I see in yours every time you step through that curtain. The look of a junkie getting his fix, see Crash every single person on this planet enjoys the rush of adrenaline pumping through their veins. Yet you strive for it, you pride yourself in being 'The Ultimate Thrill Seeker' .. See you've made your problem as clear as a heroin addict walking into his own intervention with a needle sticking out of their arm. You've become addicted to that adrenaline rush." Black leans back in his seat for a moment and let's his neck stretch over the leather headrest behind him. Eyes closed he takes a deep breath in, trying to hold something deep inside of him from getting out. Feeling as though it's controlled he pulls himself back into the same position as before, leaning forward with the camera in one hand and the other laid across his lap. Alexis Black: "See Zack, I watched your addiction grow from it's very roots and it blossomed once you strapped the UUW World title around your waist ... You knew what it felt like to be top dog, you felt the target slap itself on your back and it gave you a rush you could only ever dream of before ... And just like that" In the blink of an eye Alexis pulls his free hand up off his lap and snaps his fingers in front of the camera, you know that over used way to show just how quickly something can happen. All while still giving that piercing stare through the camcorder. Alexis Black: "You were hooked .. So when the final curtain dropped on UUW it took your trip with it, but that just wouldn't do for you Zack. Your high wasn't over yet! It couldn't be, you refused to let the facts play out and instead your letting your addiction get the best of you Zack ..." He uses the same hand from second ago to point towards the camera, point towards Zack Crash. In that listen up style that many people have used in the past, his face is stern as he let's his words flow once again. Alexis Black: "The once fan favorite of MWE has been reduced to throwing insults to whomever decides to get in the way of his next fix .. I've sat back and watched as you've gone from the extreme, fun loving X-TV Champion. Only to degrade yourself to my level ..." Black let's that finger slowly turn to point towards himself, a sneer crossing his face as he finally compares himself to Zack Crash. Alexis Black: "A level of negligent destruction. However while the reasons behind mine are still even too much of a mystery for me to solve. Your destruction is a result of your own merits .." Suddenly he finds himself unable to look at the camera, that sneer now twitching across his lips as he pulls his eyes off of us and begins glancing down. Towards the floor of the plane letting his arm drop back down to his lap as he does so. Alexis Black: "Doing anything you can to achieve that level of satisfaction, even if it means killing yourself in the process. The worst part about it though Zack, is that you can't see that your doing it. You believe that what your doing is to add to your legacy, because that's what you want to believe." Black let's out a forced laugh as he turns to look back at the camera, tears filling his eyes. The eyeliner now starting to stream down the other side of his face as well. He brings his hand back up from his lap for just a moment, long enough to wipe his face and smear the black makeup across his face. Alexis Black: "You're in control over your high Zack, but this Monday your going to find out exactly what happens when a junkie goes on a trip he can't control. You're going to experience rock bottom, and then when all is said and done I'm the one that's going to have to deal with the violence that results from the realization that you aren't in control anymore." His voice cracks a little, almost breaking into tears. Letting his head turn Black turns his head to stare out the window, trying to hide his face as that same look from earlier crosses over his face again. The question is why? Clearing his throat Black steadies himself to continue his attack on Crash, leaving even more questions to be answered then were left from the scene in the O'Hare International Airport. Alexis Black: "See your riding on Cloud 9 right now Zack, you've got your title shot in the bag and many consider you the favorite going into Crowning Glory. You're living the dream .. But when I snap you back to reality, when I force you back to planet earth and make you realize that the trip is over. Then you and everyone will see what Virus and myself already do. Your fifteen minutes is up." He peers out the corner of his eye to look at the camera, kind of forcing himself to finish his promo before time is up. Alexis Black: "So this Mayhem, when I wrap my arm around your throat and bring you to an unparelleded state of Euphoria. Don't be angry. Just consider it your intervention." With that Black reaches his free hand forward yet again, there's a few seconds of blurred picture and then our scene cuts away to darkness. [/RP]
