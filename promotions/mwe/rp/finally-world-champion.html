Finally World Champion; Triple Threat Singles RP

Nov 19 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The camera turns on and we see Milenko in the same alley The Wraith and The Ringmaster were in. The only difference is it's night and Milenko is standing above a Metal drum with a fire started in it. Beside Milenko on the ground is a sack but It's not evident what is in it. 'The Great' Milenko I haven't been doing to well recently so I decided to bring the only two people on this planet i can trust to MWE, The Wraith & The Ringmaster and together we are the Triple Threat. We will dominate the title picture on Genesis and it will start with The Wraith and Ringmaster winning an opportunity to become Tag Team Champions and it will end with me becoming the MWE World Heavyweight Champion. Milenko smiles his sadistic smile as he warms his hands in the fire. 'The Great' Milenko Before I'm able to win the title in the Main Event of the first episode of Genesis i have to win a triple threat against Joey Dallas & JJ Executioner. I've never been in the ring with Dallas but I know he is nothing compared to me and I will destroy him. Milenko bends down and reaches into the sack at his feet and takes out three wrestling championships. At first you can't see what titles they are but then the llight from the fire hits the face plates and you can see replicas of the MWE US and XPW Tag Team Titles. 'The Great' Milenko Joey I know it's your dream to become Champion and you accomplished that in you first match by becoming MWE U.S. Champion. But your dream came crashing down when you lost the title to Windy Bridges and were forced into retirement. That didn't keep you down though, a year later you joined XPW and you again became became champion by wnning both halves of the XPW Tag Team Championship by yourself which is not an easy thing to do. Milenko stops talking and he caresses the 3 titles he has in his hand as he continues to talk. 'The Great' Milenko Joey you call your self The Salvation but you have never been World Champion. Without being World Champion there is no way you can be the salvation of anything. I may not have won any World Titles but at least I've been in a few World Title matches. Milenko lifts the titles and looks at them with a smile on his face when all of a sudden he throws them in the fire. The leatehr straps char, crack & peel as the face plates turn dark and smokey. Then Milenko takes a piece of paper out of his pocket and thorws that in the fire as well. As it is burnig you can see the words Joey Dallas' Wolrd Title dreams. 'The Great' Milenko Joey with me in this match there is no way you can win so I suggest you don't even show up. If you decide to show up I will make sure you don't wak out of that ring. MIlenko leans down to take something else out of the bag as the 3 titles in the barral pop and spark as they continue to burn. He comes up with a JJ Executioner mask in his hands. Wether it is the actual mask or a replica can't be determined. 'The Great' Milenko JJ you think I cost you the 5 man mayhem match? That may be true but that's only because I don't like you. Yoiu have had plenty chances ot become a champion by beating that punk Vega Knight and you couldn't even do that. What makes you think you can beat me? You call your self hardcore but I've been in more Life threatening Hardcore matches than you can dream of. Milenko stops talking as he messes with the mask in his hands. he turns his head the fire light catches his face and a huge bruise is visble from his hairline to his chin. 'The Great' Milenko When you jumped me backstage you started something you won't be able to finish. You call your self Hardcore but you're nothing! My entire life has been hardcore. From being beaten a tortured on a regular basis by my alocholic Step-father to being thrown off the roof of a steel cage in E.W. to the 5-Man Mayhem match that took place a few days ago on MNM I have seen more carnage and dealt with more pain than you can ever dream of. Milenko turns his head back to where it was and he grips the mask tighter but he doesn't do anything with it yet. When he looks up at the camera the fire light reflected in his eyes coupled with the sadistic smile on his lips makes him look like Satan himself. Milenko JJ this Bad Blood between us is far from over and I will make sure the pain and torment you will suffer at my hands is slow and drawn out. If you have any loved ones I would call them and tell them not to watch our match if I were you because there will be a lot of blood spilled. All of a sudden Milenko throws the mask in the fire on top of the charred and ruined titles and watches it as it catches fire and pops embers into the air. 'The Great' Milenko You see I will beat these two men to a bloody pulp and then go on to win the World Title. No one can stop me from getting what should have been mine a long time ago. All of a sudden Milenko gets a look of rage in his eyed and he grabs the barrel and tips it over. The titles and pices of mask go all over the alley as they are still on fire. Milenko You see it doesn't matter who is in this Fatal-Four-Way I will take them all out on my rise to the top. I have yet to hold gold in my career and it will be the MWE World Ttle. I don't care who I have to maim or cripple to get that title, it will be MINE. Milenko looks around at the fires still going on the gound and the huge mess he caused with a huge Jokeresque smile on his face. Milenko Ace Cannon, you're mad that you have to face American Animal? Well if you manage to make it to the Main Event you will have a much harder time winning the Championship because I will be there to take you out and make sure you don't win. Milenko starts to walk around and look at the titles and mask pieces that are still on fire as he starts to talk again. Milenko Jason Fear and Vega Knight, both of you have held titles here in MWE but inly one of you can go on to the Main Event. It doesn't matter which of you former champions makes it into this match because I am hungry, hungry for gold around my waist and to be called a Champion and neither of you will be able to stop me. Milenko gets a thoughtful look on his face and an even bigger smile on his fase as he thinks about the last bock match on the Genesis card. Milenko D.C. and Sonny Williams, I wish both of you could be in this match because I would love nothing more than to maim and hurt both of you on my way to becoming champion. D.c you beat me back in E.W. to become MWE Chamion and it's eaten away at my soul ever since. I would love nothing more than to take that World Title right out from under your nose and avenge that loss. Sonny not only did you beat me for the MWE World title in my first match on Monday Night Mayhem you also beat me in E.W. I'm sure you remember the cage match we had for the Internet Title where i got thrown off the top of a Steel Cage. If you make it inbot the Main Event I will avenge both those losses and finally become World Champion. Milenko stops talking and walks away as the smoke from the ever growing fire obscures the cameras view. After Milenko can't be seen the camera fades to black.
