Est.6.6.6.; BLM Trib RP

It was a hot May day in Canada, just after Victoria Day. and that meant a lot of things. A lot of people partying outside, even after the holiday ended. For the two faced warrior, Vega Knight, he was simply driving along to Leon’s house. When he did get there, he looked into the back yard, to see Demonica sitting outside, enjoying the evening, along with her step daughter Liberty, and her newborns, Kieth, Anthony, and Courtney. He walked there after he got out of his car. Demonica sensed him coming and smiled, while Vega was busy putting on his porcelain black and white mask. Demonica: Hey Vega. Vega: Hey. Gotta say, you look....delicious tonight Although barely anyone could hear it, there was a slight chuckle after what Vega said. Demonica heard it, but she really paid no mind to it. Demonica: Yeah yeah, keep your snake in it’s cage. Not like you’d be able to get me off if you tried. Vega's right eye twitched, as he continued his really silent chuckle. It's no surprise that he lusted for Demonica. The darker side of the soul was almost obsessed with her, and that desire was amplified by the lihter side's own admiration for her. Vega wanted her, whether she liked it, or not. Vega: Damn. Insult to our ego, eh? Demonica: Well it’s for a good reason. No man truly can. But thanks for the compliment. Anyways, I assume you didn’t get Leon’s call, right? Vega: He called after inviting us here? Demonica: Yeah. Apparently, he was summoned by his predecessor for some special training. He’s meditating somewhere, which has sent his conscious into hell right now. It's helping him getting ready for the match coming up. Vega: Oh right. Yeah we are defending them against a bit of a somewhat formidable team. But not to worry. There shouldn’t be much of a problem. Especially since after a couple of losses to a certain dick wrinkle, we’re feeling a bit blood thirsty. Demonica then tried to stifle a laugh. Vega noticed it, and was very surprised to see what she found the humor in it. Vega: What’s so funny? Demonica: Oh....nothing. Vega: Oh really? Then why are you trying to stifle a laugh like that. We don’t exactly like that. No. We want to know. And We will find out. Vega then began to chuckle. His head twitched to the right for a couple seconds, before he hung his head. Slowly, he raised it. In the exposed part of his mask, you could see the sinister smile of Dark Vega. He then slowly approached Demonica. She slowly backed away. Demonica: Vega, there’s no need for the hostili- Vega smirked to himself. this was the perfect time to act upon his desires, yet make it seem like he was just pissed off. What Vega did, was he quickly grabbed her throat, and then presses her up against the wall of the house. He forcefully holds both arms above her, as he releases her throat from his grip. Dark Vega: NOW TELL US YOU BITCH!!!!! Demonica is a bit scared right now. But she kind of looks over Vega’s shoulder. Vega doesn’t notice this. But he smirks, divulging in Demonica’s fear. It is much higher then normal then anyone else in this position, and Vega sees this. Dark Vega: You know, Leon always talked about how you had a dark past. I’m assuming that this position is quite familiar. Well then....How’s about you revisit those memories while I shoot down what you said about me not being able to satisfy you. Vega then delivers an extremely powerful punch to her abdomen, causing Demonica to crumble to the floor. Demonica coughs up a bit of blood. She is crying, in both fear and pain. Dark Vega: My lighter half may try to deny it, but both sides of us, wanted to fuck you for a long....long time. The more the lighter side denies his desires, the more stronger I become. And Demonica......I'm going to enjoy hearing you scream our name. Vega grabs Demonica by the hair. He brings her head to waist level, as he unzips his zipper to his jean shorts. He chuckles at Demonica's physical and emotional pain. Suddenly, he feels a sharp pain in his leg. Dark Vega: AWWW FUCK. What the hell?!?! He then feels more pain, and feels blood coming down his leg. He turns around, to see Liberty biting his leg. But not only is she biting his leg, but actually puncturing skin, and the holes are fairly deep. Liberty repeatedly bites his legs. Dark Vega: Get off me, before I kill you. Liberty: This is what you get for doing what you’re doing. Dark Vega: Oh yeah. Well it’s mighty brave of you, Liberty. But regardless. Back off, or I’ll punt you skull in. Just like Ace Cannon did. Except when I do it, I’ll kick both sides in!!! Liberty scoffs at that. But the murderous look in Vega’s eyes, and that sinister grin, sort of told Liberty to back off a bit. That is when Vega was hit by a somewhat recovered Demonica, in the balls. He immediately goes into the ‘bent position’, and falls onto his side. Vega was stopped from doing anymore harm. Liberty: MOM! You okay? Demonica collapses, and Liberty rushes over to her side. But before that, she removes his Vega's mask, and delivers a hard punch to his face. It was a rage enhanced punch, which for Liberty, still being a young child, was only strong enough to knock Vega out. Liberty then rolled Demonica onto her back, and held her step-mother’s head in her lap. Demonica: Lib. Thank you. Liberty: Your welcome mom. How are you? Demonica: Hurt a bit, but overall, not bad. Liberty smiles at that, knowing her true mother figure in her life was fine, despite coughing up a little blood. But Demonica noticed that Liberty’s incisor teeth were pointed and sharp. Not to mention full of blood. Demonica: Lib....was it a feeding night? Liberty: Yeah. I was originally just going to just use a syringe on him, but after the way he was treating you, I had to attack him. I managed to get my helping. But I do have a question. I know his darkside took over. But why was Vega talking like a normal person. Demonica: Oh....well when one side is more dominant, it just becomes one person basically. When he’s talking like he normally does, that’s when neither part of his soul is dominant. As you can see, he went kind of....crazy. Liberty: Heh. well that's an understatement. Well, I did capture it on video. At least it will show just how messed up my godfather is. Dad's going to be pissed off. Demonica nodded, slowly getting up. She could hear the triplets crying out. She stood up, clutching her stomach in pain, as she limped over to the triplets. Once she appeared over them, with a smile on their faced, they immediately stopped, all three smiling. Liberty: I think they sensed your pain mom. Demonica: I think you’re right. Come here. Demonica grabbed Kieth and Anthony from their carry seats, while Liberty picked up Courtney. Demonica and Liberty both sat down, both with smiles on their faces, happy that noting to bad happened. With Leon........... Leon was currently at a cliff a bit of a distance away. He was in the lotus position, meditating. It’s what he did when he was summoned to hell, as he projects his mind into the underworld. The area in hell where Leon's mind was, destruction was seen all around, for if there was one thing that people didn't want to do to Leon Roberts, was piss him off. You could ask the forest that WAS there, where Leon stood. He was in his half transformed state, not quite in his devil form. His skin had adapted a crimson color, and hardened to the same hardness of dragon scales. His normally black straight hair was now silver, longer, and spiked wildly. His normally gold eyes were now red with golden slits. His hands were now claws. He still had his standard normal pair of jeans on. But what was the most prominent features, was that he had a pair of massive demon wings, and a giant scorpion tail. In his hand, he held a six foot long katana. But it wasn't any ordinary katana. It was one that was made out of crystal and bone. Leon: Goddammit!!!! This is BULLSHIT!!!!!!! Leon then slashed a tree that was in front of him, right down the middle. It split. Clenching his right claw tightly a black flame engulfed his hand, before he shot it at the tree, making it turn into two massive flames. He then heard a clapping sound. Leon turned around, and saw that it was the one entity that made him the Devil’s incarnate. It was Lucifer. Lucifer: Very good Leon. I see that your focus has been redoubled. That is good. Don’t hold back your rage. It makes you complete. Leon: Shut the fuck up old man. Your words mean nothing to me. Especially since you can’t do shit. Lucifer: Hmph. I did bring you back to life, and I certainly don’t regret that, my young successor. Leon: Yeah. It also shows you just how truly pathetic you are, compared to me. You might be a wise being, but you’re not me. You ruled hell for two thousand years, and two world wars were produced, but that only made the world stronger. I’m the devil for only five years, and many things have been happening. The World’s economy collapses, The Swine Flu pandemic is happening, along with unemployment rates skyrocketing, and crime rate spiraling out of control. Lucifer could only chuckle at Leon’s words. Leon smirked, knowing that he spoke the truth. Lucifer: I will admit, you’re better then me, because with the world, you’re not even trying. But in MWE, well you have to try harder. But don’t get me wrong. You have fought well against Brian Michaels. But your losses to him have compiled more rage within you. I know what you plan to do with that rage. Leon nodded. His spikes began to retract. His muscle mass shrank, and his skin tone returned to normal. He was done in his devil form, and now, was in his human form. Leon: Yeah, take out Famous and Imfamous, for good. Other then the fact that my own thirst for blood this week is more then enough to take them both out, they aren't even in the league of Black List Mafia. Leon stretches out for a second, before taking a deep breath. Leon: I mean....I can’t see them as much of anything. From what I see, one is some serial killer wannabe, while the other believes that he is possessed by two spirits Lucifer: Ah yes. Jekyl and McKnight. Well, as unfortunate as it is, They are, who they are. And yes, Scotty is really what he says. However, just because he is the supposed container of my son, as he claims, doesn’t mean anything. He is just another obstacle. Leon: Okay. But tell me more. Why him. Why did you so called ‘son’ choose Scotty? Lucifer sighs. Even he didn’t know the answer to that. He began to walk away, but made a hand gesture for Leon to follow him. Leon did catch up, and walked beside Lucifer, as they went back to the Grand Hell Palace. Lucifer: There are many things I do not know. I couldn’t control who my son chose to act as his container. But I did know something had to be done about it. That’s where you, come into play, my young successor. Leon: Oh, really? Lucifer: Yes. You see, there is no way I’ll allow god to fully control this world. My plan was to originally capture this world when god was at his weakest. But alas, when Damien started expressing that he might not help me, I knew drastic measures had to be taken. I knew, that it was my time, to bring forth, a new ruler for Hell. That’s where you came in. Somebody, who understood that in order to succeed, you must do whatever it takes for survival and success, consequences be damned. Leon: Yeah, that’s me alright. Lucifer: But, despite not having the best career in MWE, that doesn’t matter. You’re a huge name. People love you. So it doesn’t matter if you win or lose. But I know you. You hate to lose, and the things around you suffer because of it. Leon: Point taken. So essentially, you believe that with me, not even your son could stop me? Lucifer: Correct. I don’t care who rules hell, so as long as hell rules the world. The world that needs to exist, is the very world you have been preaching about. Leon smirked at that. They had reached the pyramid that lead up to the Grand Palace. Both devil’s sat down on the stairway. Leon rubbed his chin for a second, thinking for a second. Leon: You do understand what I've been saying. The world that I talk about, is but the only way to exist. A world, without rules. A world, where it’s either kill or be killed. In a twisted sense, we are the true good, because we know the true way to live. God is the dictator, not us. Lucifer: Exactly Leon. But you only told me about what Scotty is. But tell me, what do you think of the both of them? Leon sighs, while pressing his right index finger and thumb against his forehead. He is still feeling frustrated about what’s been happening to his wrestling career in MWE, but knows that he must do better. Leon: Out of the two of them, the only one I’ll take seriously is Scotty McKnight. Other then the fact that he has a reaper and your son attached to his soul, because I really don't give a fuck either way if he does, Scotty is at least is a brutally honest person, and wrestles seriously, and with little fear. I respect him for that. But that does not make him anywhere near what I am capable of doing. Jekyl on the other hand, is one of the biggest jokes I’ve ever seen. Lucifer: Interesting. Do tell. Leon looks directly into Lucifer’s eyes. Black eyes with red slits meet gold eyes with silver slits. Leon sighs, before he stretches his neck out a bit. Leon: All I see in Jekyl is a scared little boy. He tries to show us that he’s some crazy ass Joker wannabe. Immitating a fictional villan’s clothing, talk, hair, etc. He even shows us violence, and supposed death. Yet, when was the last time we heard about anyone he’s killed? When was the last time he was arrested? I don't think he has that kind of record. I think, that what Jekyl really is, is a poser. Lucifer: Yes, I agree with that. Leon sighs at that, and shakes his head. While stress might have been getting to him lately, he is at least calm right now, able to think clear. Leon: He’s a good actor, don’t get me wrong. For a while, it seemed believable. But after after looking at this little head case closer recently, and some research, all that it told me that Jekyl has no kills to his name. Heck, I’ll bet that video he showed us a month or so ago, was just a set up, inspired by The Dark Knight. Lucifer: You can’t deny the fact that it’s one of the best movies ever. Leon scoffed at that comment. Leon mentally agreed with what his predecessor said about the movie, as Leon would never disrespect that movie. But as it would seem, that movie did generate a lot of obsessive fans that tried to be like The Joker. Leon: Well it’s created an over obsessed fan. To scared to actually show who he truly is. I see Jekyl......no.......Ryder James, as that is his real name, as a man, who was so generic, he knew that he couldn’t succeed in this cuthroat business without an edge. Once Dark Knight comes out....BOOM........he thinks he can develop an edge, by becoming the Joker. I mean.....This is what he looked like before. Leon then took out a picture of what Ryder James used to look like before he changed his gimmick into Jekyl. Leon: I’ve seen some lame things in my twenty two years in existence, but........Ryder with his gimmick just blows them all away. It's not to much to ask for him to be original, but I don't see that happening ever. Lucifer: You can't deny he's managed a couple of good wins. Leon sighs, before he stares at his predecessor, with eyes projecting a message that could be seen as 'you have got to be kidding me'. Leon: A couple of big wins doesn't mean much. It's when you show that you can constantly pull out big wins, is when you matter. He's never been anywhere near what I've accomplished, and I don't care if he insults them. Fact is, yes he did beat a former MWE champion in Sonny Williams. That's his biggest win ever. Guess what? I kicked Sonny Williams's ass twice, en route to becoming the first, longest reigning, and only EW world champion that actually fucken mattered. In the end, what can Scotty McKnight and Ryder do? They can try to think they can defeat the Black List Mafia. They think they can take our titles. But what Black List Mafia will do, is stare those fuckers in the eyes,raise some hell, thrash Ryder James and Scotty, then leave Famous and Imfamous for dead, never looking back. Lucifer smirks at Leon's ranting and raving. He knew that making Leon do some extra physical and mental training would refocus and free some of the pent up anger that Leon was holding in. He was happy, with how Leon viewed his upcoming opponents. Lucifer: Good. I see my work here is done for today Leon. You have a clearer mind, and thus, you are able to channel your rage properly. Leon: You're right. I do feel better. I don't feel the urge to destroy everything in my path again. Rather, just who will be sent to the slaughterhouse known as a wrestling ring, with me in it. Lucifer: Yes. That's the kind of attitude that shows you are worthy of being the lord of the underworld. Now, go back to your hot wife. Go and adore your children. Go and continuously train, and send at least one of your opponents this week to hell. Preferably Scotty, just to make sure that Damien won’t help God. Leon then looks at Lucifer one last time, and a faint, but most certainly sinister chuckle could be heard from Leon. He had just about every intention of fulfilling his predecessor's wish Leon: You need not worry about what could happen. What will happen, is that I will free this world from it's shackles, and let everyone live the way they are meant to live. Not under some guidance of somebody who does nothing. Leon stands up, and bursts into black flames. He slowly disintegrates. When he is nothing but ash, it is a signal, for those who watched, that he has left hell, and has emerged back in his body. Leon gets up, and begins to head back to his home **Leon’s House** Leon would arrive early in the morning. He proceeds upstairs, to check in on everyone. He did know Vega was likely to crash at his place, so he knew he would see him. In the first room, he was a Kodak like moment, with Liberty, on a makeshift bed, sleeping, with the triplets on top of her. Leon smiled. The next room, he looked in, to see Vega out cold. He did notice that his right leg was all wrapped up, and that there was some unwashed blood on his face. Leon knew this, as he didn’t see Vega wearing his mask. Leon then smiled, and went into the master bedroom, where Demonica was just getting up. She smiled at Leon, before she winced in pain. Leon: Sug. What’s wrong? You pregnant again? Demonica sadly smiled at Leon. She shook her head. She tried to get up, but her abdomen still hurt. Leon helped her up, and embraced her. Leon: Please tell me what’s wrong. Demonica: I...I can’t. It was....just so...surreal. Leon sighed. Looking out the door, he saw Vega stirring, slowly trying to become awake. Suddenly, Leon saw Vega bolt upright, and hyperventilating. Leon sighed, as he let go of Demonica, and went to check on Vega. When he got there, He saw Vega sobbing. Vega heard Leon enter. And he looked at him. Vega: L-l-l-leon. I’m so sorry. Leon: For what? Wait......you’re talking like a normal person. The light side, right? Vega: Y-y-y-y-yes. Leon sighed. He knew that the light side hardly ever was around, and knew he had to simply go with the flow of things, before Vega was his two faced self again. Vega: Leon. I can’t.......I’m sorry. I can’t believe.........I....... Leon: What? Spit it out for fuck sake! Vega then just let the tears roll down his face. He buried his head in his hands in shame. Vega: I...My Dark side............took over last night.......I couldn’t keep him at bay. He acted on my.......no both sides attraction to Demonica.........and.......almost.....r-r-raped her. Leon was stunned by this. No scratch that. He was angry. He was shocked that such an occurrence almost happened. He then gave Vega powerful backhand slap across his face. Vega looked into his best friend’s eyes, fully accepting the slap across the face. Vega: I....I don’t know why it happened. I was neutral, just being my flirtatious self, when suddenly, I became aggressive. But.......you should really reward Liberty. She kept biting me with what looked like fangs, in my leg. It distracted me, even having my attention turned to her. I was then knocked out. Oh Leon..........I’m sorry. Leon sighed. He was frustrated again. This time, at his own tag team partner, and somebody he considered a brother. He didn’t have anymore time for all this bullshit that was happening. He then placed his right hand on Vega’s shoulder. Vega looked at Leon, still ashamed of what his darkside did. Leon: It’s okay. The Vega that I trust would not do that. I know your darkside is erratic and dangerous, and very much evil, and I accept that. But you need to learn how to control it better. I know you can. But...even still. I am ashamed of you, and my trust in you has gone down a bit. I know you’ll redeem yourself. You always do. Just, try to not let your hormones mix with your darkside again. Just do me a favor. Let your darkside out come game day. I need you to really help me against poser clown and Mr Wiccan, to just shut them up for good. Vega sniffled one last time, before a smile was seen on his face. Vega: I will. Thank you Leon. I must say though.......I feel.....very lucky to have you as a friend. Anyone else who tried to do what I did, would have choked on their own balls, before being hung by their intestines. Leon: Yeah......lucky you. Vega then tries to get up, but he is also in pain from Liberty’s attack. He sees the wrapping, and chuckles. Vega: At least they took care of me. But I’m curious......she could have electrocuted me easily, yet she only bit me. Why is that? Leon then sensed that Liberty was up, and standing at the doorway. Leon looked over, and smiled. He knew Liberty would answer that. Liberty: It’s because I’m a vampire, and It was a feeding night for me. I just needed some blood, or I would have felt really sick until I got some. You, just so happened to be at the right place, at the right time. For me, that is. But, your leg will heal fast. Vega: Yeah....thanks, I guess. Regardless Lib.........I’m very sorry. Liberty: It’s okay....I heard what you and dad said. You’re forgiven....for now. Vega: Thank you. Vega then slowly fell back don on his bed, still feeling a bit dizzy from being knocked Out Leon got up and left the room. Despite all that what happened, Leon knew that he and Vega, were not going to lose the MWE tag team championship at Tribulation. He had to personally make sure they didn’t. It would be very embarrassing if they did.
