A deal made in hell; Virus MNM RP

Oct 9 2009

Leon Roberts

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It was a Wednesday. It was almost the weekend. To most, it meant that they would get a break from their daily lives. But for Leon Roberts that was a different story. See, the life of a pro wrestler working on Mondays, usually meant the weekends is when he has to get to whatever city he's performing in. In this case, it's going to be in Dallas Texas. And as such, Leon was booking his itinerary for the show. However, in some strange, and possible cruel (or awesome) twist of fate, just before Leon confirmed everything, he heard the phone ringing, which had ultimately distracted him from booking the itinerary. He was surprised to see that it was non other, then Nate Rellingong, one of the senior members f MWE's board of directors. He picked it up, wondering what the deal was. Leon: Roberts residence. Virus, speaking. Nate: Leon. Just the man I wanted to talk to. Listen, I have a deal you might be interested in, and I would like to speak to you in person. Leon: Sure, but we'll have to do that in Dallas, as I'm about to book everything. Nate: Well then champ, my suggestion is that you cancel everything, because I am taking the MWE private jet to Toronto, for tomorrow. You, and your family, are going to be going to Dallas with me tomorrow, as we discuss this deal. Everything for the entire trip, will be taken care of, and paid for. Trust me when I say this, there I something I want you to do, and I will gladly compensate. Leon was very surprised. He was invited to go down to Texas, with his entire family, on the MWE private jet, and his whole trip would be taken care of. He was not passing this one up. Not one bit. Next day, he was driving to the Pearson International Airport. He smiled, looking at the family of six, in the van he now drove around. Hey, at least it was repainted to more his style, which included a flaming pentagram on the hood of the car. But the family of six had just arrived. Leon and Liberty had hopped out, proceeding to the private MWE jet, while Demonica got the triplets out. Some body guards went over to help Demonica, which she greatly appreciated the help. After a few minutes, the kids settled into a part of the jet that was like a movie theatre, Nate, Leon, and Demonica all sat down. Before any discussion could be made, the private jet took off, heading towards Dallas, as everything had been cleared to go immediately. One hour into the flight, Nate,k Demonica and Leon all shared some wine. But when Leon tasted it, he got a bit of a curious look on his face. Leon: Don Perium seventy five. One of the finest aged wines in the world right now. Must be more serious of a deal then I had anticipated. Nate: Really? And why do you think that champ? Leon smirked. For the first time in a while, it was true. He had finally won the MWE United States championship, and he quite enjoyed the damage he had done to Mason. Leon: Well this wine is thirty four years aged. On top of that, you're taking my entire family to Dallas, for Mayhem, a few days in advance, hotel fully paid for, including a round trip back, not to mention a few thousand dollars spending cash. I must say, that whatever deal you're trying to butter me up for, it must be serious. Nate: And correct you are. But I'm surprised to find that you were able to read into this so easily. Leon: Wrestling isn't the only thing my father taught me. It's his business sense as well that makes him the perfect candidate to run ICW. Naturally, he taught me everything he knew. Plus, it's also a bit of a sense that I picked up after a while. Demonica: Trust me sir, he ain't kidding. Almost nothing gets by him. Nate: Well that's a good thing. But yes. What I want you to do, isn't entirely my idea. Rather...Most of the board of directors want this. In fact, the only person on the board who doesn't want what I'm about to ask you Leon, is Eli Silver. Leon: Really? Well then, do tell me what you need. Nate: Very well. As you know, DC, Paige, and David Whitman have recently formed a new alliance. Now, normally the board of directors would have no problems with this, except for the fact that recently, there has been an incident during a board meeting. DC, Paige, and Whitman all basically threatened, embarrassed, and humiliated everyone on the board there, just because they felt like having some fun at our expense. Now, I myself have never been to fond of DC, but he's gone to far this time. Leon: You think that's bad? I blame him for the destruction of EW, because all the hard work until he was the champ, he shat on it all. Nate: Exactly. He has no respect for wrestling, and true hard work. He, like Brian Michaels, can claim all they want how they earned their way to the top, but you and I, we both know the truth. I for one, am sick of letting him roam around free, thinking he could do anything he wants. And basically, since he and Paige have Eli in their back pocket, he could easily at any time, say that he wants the world title, and it will be granted to him. Leon: I know. But what are you getting at this? Nate sighed, finishing off his own glass of wine. Demonica also finished hers, at the same time, trying to understand what could Nate Rellington need from Leon. Nate: Simple. After viewing your performance from last week, it's clear to me, and the board, that the match that we made between you and DC this week, isn't going to be just a match. We know, that for what we want, and need, you're the man to go to. Leon: And? Nate: I'm getting there. Now, normally, I would have simply fired DC from MWE, but he pointed out that it would cost MWE a shit load more if we did fire him. However, what if we was to leave on different terms. One for two. Both, I know, you are capable of. Either he quits, or dies. Leon was very shocked. So shocked, that his eyes nearly popped out of the socket. But then, he begins to smirk. He sees no hint of lying in Nate's expression, so he knows that whatever Nate is telling him, is the truth. Nate: All you have to do, is beat him. That's the basic of it. I plan to put him through so much hell if you don't this week. I have a somewhat suitable opponent lined up for him right after. One who simply wants to face him, so he can say he beat DC on more...fair grounds. Leon: I see. Well what's in it for me? Nate: Well, along with the whole thing that I've set up for you now, you won't have to worry about traveling expenses for six months, and not to mention that you'll have your monthly salary raised by a full grand. Leon: That sounds fair. I'll do it. But you have to make sure that you fulfill your agreements, because as the devil's incarnate, I share one thing in common with my predecessor. I always fulfill my contracts, and if need be, I include fine print. Should MWE in general mess up anything in our agreement, it's on your ass Nate. I hope you know that. Nate: I'm well aware of that Leon. I'm glad that you're not just a destroyer, but a smart business man as well. You have my word, that I will not screw you over. You will be doing us all a favor when you humiliate him. Leon: No problem sir. I'm just glad that you're giving me the go ahead to do whatever the hell I want to DC. It's been a long time coming. And he will get what's coming to him. Oh yes. Nate: Good. Now. I want you two, along with the kids, to enjoy yourselves while in Dallas. Speaking of which, it should only take us another couple of hours to get there. Leon: Thank you again Nate. Well like Nate said, it would only be a couple hours before the family of six would arrive. They immediately went to their hotel room, to get settled in. All of them were tired, so on that day, they didn't exactly bother to do anything, except for rest. Next day though, Leon wanted to take everyone out for supper, so he was looking around some places in Dallas. He drove around, until he went into a mall. Just looking around and stuff. One of Leon's favorite stores to look at was the electronics stores, so naturally, he went into one. A few people recognized him, but he was not in the mood to deal with fans. Unfortunately, one really tall, looked to be around eighteen years of age male fan, approached Leon. Fan: DUDE! You're Leon Roberts, from MWE! Leon: Yeah, and what of it? Fan: Well I just wanted an autograph from the new United States Champion. Leon: Perhaps that's okay I guess. You caught at a-WHAT THE FUCK IS THAT SHIT!?!?!?! Leon was looking at the shirt. It was actually a DC shirt. The fan shrugged it off. Leon noticed this, and trying to control his legendary temper (he really didn't want to be kicked out of a store over a shirt) he snarled. Leon: Kid, what's you're name? Fan: Wyatt Leon: Wyatt. Well Wyatt, that's a DC shirt. You do realize I'm facing him on the next edition of Mayhem, right? Wyatt: I know. Leon: And you do realize, that I have an even bigger beef with him then I did with Mason Resoan. Wyatt: I didn't know. Sorry. Leon: Take that shirt off. Wyatt: huh? Leon: You heard me. Take. That. Fucken. Shirt. Off. Right. NOW!!! This got the attention of everyone in the store, and even had a lot of people backing away from the two. Wyatt reluctantly responded, and began to take off shit shirt. Leon was surprised to see the hair the guy had. In fact, he was more or less grossed out by it. Leon: Sweet shit on a stick, put the shirt back on right now, inside out. Wyatt sighed, and did as he was told. Leon smirked, knowing he didn't have to see any chest hair, or some fucked up graphic of DC. Leon: Kid. You want to do something useful for me? Get a camcorder, because You're recording my next promo, right here in, this very store. Parents, I suggest you get your kids out of here now because there will be an excess of colorful language here, and I can't stand holding back due to you dumbasses. Jeez, swear words are just words in the end. It's all good. Some of the people did leave. Surprisingly, The manager of the store surprisingly let Leon do his thing. Wyatt grabbed a camcorder, and started recording. Leon: I knew this match was going to happen again eventually. A third time, I have to face a man who I consider a true disease to wrestling. I might be dubbed 'The Virus' but that's only because I spread anarchy and chaos. I am the true definition of wrestling, not like DC. But that's beside the point. Or is it? Leon's first smirk of the promo is seen. I's not a one side smirk like it usually is, but more of a double side, truly cruel smirk that he shows. The ones reserved for the really crazy ones. He then chuckled for a brief second, before going back to his rant. Leon: Let me remind you all about the history me and DC share. EW, in June, of last year. DC had defeated me, to end the longest EW title reign in it's short history. A company I can honestly say, I built with my own bare hands, as I was the main event, and the whole damn reason people tuned into it. Because I fought harder, hit harder, and even fucked harder then anyone else. Hell I still do. But then, DC wormed his way into the main event, beat me for my championship. I'm not mad over the fact he beat me, I'm mad over the fact he disregarded it so easily, that an equally big douchebag had won the title a week later. Yeah, Ace Cannon. And Ace Cannon as a world champion is like saying Bruno is the greatest movie ever. Wrong, and makes no sense. In one single move, DC threw away everything that I built, like it was a half cent whore. Leon's eyes began to flash red. This ain't no joke. It's one of the many signs his tempter is flaring out of control, and it is not a pretty sight to behold. Though he was trying to calm himself down, which essentially, could be considered credit on his part. Leon: But DC, that's all in the past. Or at least for the most part. I'm sure if it were anyone else, they'd let it be. I'm not like everyone else. I take what you did to EW was far worse on a personal level, then any injury to me could have happened. I want you to remember last Mayhem, the very same Mayhem I Became the United States Champion. En Route to winning it, I had thrown Mason around like a ragdoll on fire, before breaking his arm in two places. Leon was breathing heavily now, with half of his left eye closed. A sign he was getting excited Well who could blame him. It was a chance at revenge afterall. Leon: That being said what makes you think that you can survive this encounter? Ask yourself that, knowing that you're facing somebody as sick, twisted, and heartless as I am. Sure, my track record in the past may not be the greatest, and you will most likely doubt me, but that is what you get for being way to fucken cocky. Also, word out on the street is that you and your politicking ways have made you safe from being fired. Safe from being sued, as it would cost MWE a lot more money. But what if you quit? Surely, it's on your own terms, and thus, your contract doesn't, and will not be fulfilled. How do I know of all this. Well to be honest, I got two words to say. Nate Rellington. Oh yeah, he specifically sought me out. I would even say, after my performance against Mason last week, plus our history, is exactly why he sought me out. I usually don't like orders, but if it benefits me, then I got no problem. And I do benefit from this one a lot. All I have to do, is hurt you as bad as I so please. No limits, no stopping, and no stopping your blood from flowing. Because I'm even allowed to go as far as bringing you as close to death as possible. All I have to do, is make sure you are hurt, and think twice about doing what you're planning with Paige and Whitman. Leon looks around, to see that a lot of the people that were still in the store were farther away from him then they originally were. He sighed in annoyance. It almost had a look on his face saying 'sometimes it sucks to be this feared'. But still he continued with the promo. Leon: Thinking about it now, you're no better then the old fogies at other wrestling companies, who can't seem to get over the fact that they are yesterday's news, They keep doing whatever they can in management, to keep their spots. To reign supreme, way past a time when anyone could care about them. And yet, you're still popular, just for being you. All of them, like you, are disgraces to this sport that I love so much. Unlike you, when I reach the top, I ain't going down, because in all honesty, despite a couple of hiccups this year, I can't be stopped. There s no prime for me. I simply will always push forward. You keep flaunting your money around, and your ego as well, that it makes me sick. So what if you have a playboy playmate for a girlfriend. I don't give a fuck either way. Nobody does. So why bother to keep yourself around. Do us all a favor and just get the fuck out. However, I know that you won't, so it's up to me to force you out, and I will gladly do so. Over rated and hated isn't just a slogan for you. It's the truth. You need to get over yourself. You're nothing but a piece of crap, who will never get by, unless you somehow sweet talk people into seeing your way. Not only am I going to defeat you this week, I'm going to break your fucken jaw. That's what I've decided to do. Break your damn jaw, so that nobody has to hear you go on and on with juvenile shit that you constantly puke out of your mouth. Quite frankly DC, everyone will be thanking me. And I'm sure you probably will have something to say about my daughter being kicked in the head before. Well guess what....she's bounce back tougher then ever, in case you didn't fucken know. In fact, I'll go as far as saying, that's the best thing that's happened to her, as she's twice as viscious as she was. Makes me proud. But I'm not here to talk about my family, I'm here to fuck you up, like you should have been a long time ago. Leon is once again taking a deep breath, to calm his nerves, which were getting excited. His right eye twitched, which if you don't exactly know by now, is a clear sign of him looking forward to whatever carnage he was about to cause. Of course, he was going to wait until Monday, unless something provoked him. Leon: Defeating you...no....DISABLING you DC, will be a great pleasure. Over a year of pure hatred towards somebody, and pretty much legally allowed to do anything I fucken want to do to them, well that's just perfect for me. But to be honest, I think I will keep you alive afterall, so that you truly feel humiliated. Because when I'm done, there will be nothing functioning for a while, making you softer then Elton John at the playboy mansion. Oh but don't worry. Send your bitch my way, because since you won't be able to, I'll personally make sure she gets the best damn fuck she ever has, because everyone knows I can fuck and fight all night long, ever single fucken day. So the last thing that I guess I'm going to say, is that your gravy train, has officially been derailed. Because I truly am the worst type of opponent you could ever face, in the fact that I have no regard for another man's fucken life. I'd cripple my own father if he pissed me off this much. But YOU DC....YOU will truly be coming very close to having a meeting with the grim reaper, because I am amongst the best of what I do. Leon made the cut throat signal, which Wyatt took as a sign to shut the camcorder off, to end the promo. He smiled before turning to the manager, and thanking him. Leon: So Wyatt. You planning on going to Mayhem? Wyatt: Yes sir. And I wasn't planning on wearing this shirt. Leon: Good. Listen, for what you just did, I thank you, and.... Leon took out a piece of paper and signed his name on it, along with making a pentagram on it. Leon: For your autograph collection. Now If you don't mind, There's still quite a few things that I have to do. Leon left the store. He did indeed hear the big sigh of relief from everyone there. He laughed it off, knowing he had that effect on people. Didn't really bother him much. But he had a lot to look forward to, especially since he was well accommodated. Leon *Thoughts*: I meant every word I said in there. Come Mayhem, DC will indeed wish that he was never born, or at least never fucked with me. God won't have mercy on his soul, because God doesn't give a fuck. So who will have mercy on DC's soul? Nobody will. Especially me.
