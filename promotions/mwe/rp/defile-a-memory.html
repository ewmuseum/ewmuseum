Jekyl

(((((OOC: CLICK ON LINKS FOR PICTURES!))))) Ashes to ashes, dust to dust Here lies someone you wouldn't trust. Though I walk through the valley of the shadow of death The Lord is my shepherd, I shall not lack. He lays me down in green pastures, and leads me beside still waters. He restores my soul. Indeed, though i walk through the valley of the shadow of death, I shall fear no evil, for He is with me. His rod and staff comfort me.* He lays a banquet table for me, before my enemies' eyes, and anoints my head with oil. My cup overflows. Surely His goodness and mercy will follow me all the days of my life, and I shall live out my days in the house of the Lord. ~ R.i.P Jordan William ~ http://img397.imageshack.us/my.php?image=jordanwall1ca5.jpg This passage reads on a large wall, the wall is marble stone with indented white lettering. Jekyl walks on scene with a small bag with what seems like metal cans, they clang as he drops it in front of the ground. He turns around and fixes his jacket, twists his neck around to crack it and licks his lips as he fixes his hair. Jekyl: Tonight is a very special night, not only for me, but I will mark history in MWE.... and lets just say it will be "written in stone"....so to speak... Jekyl turns around and we hear an unzipping sound as Jekyl does a little jump and we now realize he is pissing on the wall... piss runs down the wall and onto the ground, Jekyl turns his head backwards. Jekyl: Don't mind me sonny, nature calls... Anyways right now my main focus is you, be grateful because normally i wouldn't give a shit about anyone, alas...I need to get through you to get that escaped retard David Whitman...So lets begin shall we? Sonny Williams, You have lost a few times in a row so far, yet so have I...But the difference between you and I is that you have something to prove, to some little shit that's rotting in the ground underneath me most likely....Me? I have nothing to prove other than I'm better, I'm not doing this for titles, for fans... I'm doing this for myself, for the fun of it.... To get to the top to say that I beat the best, I took down the strongest soldiers.... Jekyl finishes and zips his pants up, bends over and grabs one of the cans inside the bag and looks at the camera. Jekyl: You want to show brother how much you love him by beating people and winning gold? Watch carefully how i physically show your brother how much you love him with this spray paint... Jekyl spins around and sprays a line at the top of the wall. Jekyl: This line represents the day he died, you showed how much you loved him by dragging him into a gang war and giving him a loaded gun. Jekyl sprays another line beside it.... Jekyl: This line represents that same day where you did nothing while you stood there and watched that 9mm bullet pierce his chest....pretty strong love you showed there.... Jekyl spins back around and sprays a 3rd line... now starting to chuckle. Jekyl: This line represents your first loss at crowning glory, you were in the final 4 yet you gave up and fell apart..so close to victory but gave into Alejandro and lost.... *tsk tsk* Your love really shows sonny.... Jekyl sprays a 4th line on the wall... Jekyl: This represents your second match, and your second loss to Brian Michaels, and then you show your cowardice by attacking him after the match.... truly pathetic sir... Jekyl sprays a fifth line to cross out the 4 lines..... Jekyl: This line is one of the worst on this wall, because it represents last week when I was forcefully tagged up with you in a handicap match against Whitman.... and you tried to take over and we STILL lost... against one man...we lost... because your pride took over.... you make me laugh...But lets recap shall we? 5 strikes on your brothers wall to show your "love"... 3 losses dedicated to him and the beginning on how you showed your love for him while you stood there and did nothing.... golly you really do love your brother don't you? I bet you anything hes spinning in his grave right now... Jekyl drops the can and begins to dig through his bag for another can... Jekyl: Didn't realize the other one was so empty, must of been the one I used on Whitman's house last night... AH! here we go... Jekyl sprays some words on the wall in more red paint... Jekyl: Sonny you are a miserable failure who thinks they can succeed by using the dead as a pity call.... not gonna work on me... Out of all people, it wont work on me... your 5 times the failure to your brother, and I will make sure the world sees it... Jekyl steps back with a grin and steps aside so that the camera can see the paint...it reads... "You're 5 times the failure ~R.i.P Sonny~" http://img67.imageshack.us/my.php?image=jo...ll2paintmk0.jpg Jekyl tosses the can aside and begins to laugh maniacally as he walks away... Jekyl: To take a man down, you not only have to beat them physically, but emotionally and mentally, and I'm sure this will do the trick...My intentions are clear. Sonny Williams, You will be with your little brother, and you will both be pathetic losers together...HAHAHAHAhahahahahahaha....hahaha....heee...haaa.hhooooooo A flash illuminates the scene and the paint is gone from the wall, all that's left is scratches at the bottom of the wall that read... "Brother of a Failure" http://img259.imageshack.us/my.php?image=j...ll3finaldb4.jpg -Fade to laughter-
