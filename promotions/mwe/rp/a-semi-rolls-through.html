A Semi rolls through; Semi RP: Part 1

Mar 14 2009

Jesse Anderson

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The scene opens, showing only blackness. Before you even realize the scene has started, the silence that matches the darkness is shattered by two loud blasts of a horn. The blackness fades into desert. The sun is burning bright in the sky over hills of sand as far as the eye can see, with scarce cacti and other vegetation scattered about. You only see the desert for a brief moment before a large semi truck flies by the camera view, roaring through the wind. Attached to the truck cab is a long white trailer with a red and blue logo. The camera view changes for a quick moment to the front of the cab; just long enough to catch the irony. Through the wide, bug-splattered windshield, you see that the semi is being driven by... The Semi. The enormous mass of gray steel and hot rubber tears down the desert road with a cloud of dust flying up behind it. The view shifts to the passenger side of the semi cab, to reveal the crazed look on the face of it's driver, the current WHQ champion and former ICW champion. Semi's eyes are wide and the half amused, half unnerved grin on his face is unsettling. Semi: Dallas, Texas; here we come! Semi howls a crazed howl as the semi zooms passed the camera and hauls ass down the middle of the desert road towards the horizon. The camera cuts back to MWE's newest competitor as he pilots his momentous force through the desert. Semi keeps his wild eyes on the road as he begins talking to the camera. He talks loudly to be heard over the roar of the engine and the road under foot. He speaks with great excitement. Semi: Now I know what you're thinking! But no; this is not The Semi's preferred means of transportation. You see, I got into a bit of a bind trying to get from Ireland to Texas at the last minute and found some fortune in the form of ten tons, eighteen wheels, and 500 horsepower! Semi clenches his muscles and steps down hard on the gas pedal, sending the engine into a loud rev. He continues talking, now more aggressively, still with his eyes glued to the oncoming road ahead. Semi: I'll tell you; there isn't much worse than getting a flat tire on a desert road several miles from any signs of life. It just so happened, that first life sign was a truck stop, inhabited by careless truckers. Semi grins wide, still with crazed eyes on the road. Semi: I have a show to get to, and I'm not going to miss it. I'm not going to lay down and die in a damn desert, either. Nope, instead I'm going to do what I do best. I'm going to roll into Dallas and I'm going to kick some ass! Semi pushes his side window open, forcefully, letting the howl of the wind into the cab. In the same motion, Semi leans his face to the window and yells out to apparently no more than the sand and sun. Semi: YOU HEAR ME?! HERE I COME, LIKE IT OR NOT! Semi growls through clinched teeth as he leans back into his seat and slams the window shut. Semi checks his rear and side mirrors, then reglues his eyes to the road. Those eyes are now very bitter, but still wide and anxious. Semi: I am truly a man with momentum on my side. Right now I am traveling at 103 miles per hour with a forty foot trailer packed full of clothes. If anyone dares to not get out of my way, I think we all know how that will turn out. The sick grin slides back onto Semi's face, but only for a moment before he continues. Semi: And when I reach my destination and step into that ring, I will have that same momentum. I have the momentum of two years and three federations without a loss sitting in my passenger seat. I have the momentum of the hatred and anger of the warped minds of thousands of puppet fans booing in the back trailer. But most importantly, I have the momentum of desire driving this unstoppable force. Semi shoots a quick look of crazed determination into the camera. Semi: Right now, that desire is pumping through me like it never has before. I would enjoy nothing more right now than to get my hands around someone's neck and continue this momentum. That's why when I step into the ring this Sunday, someone WILL be ran down! After another mirror check from Semi, as a furrow of worry raises his brow, Semi begins talking to the camera again, but still watching the desert road ahead, complete with heat waves in the distance. Semi: This Monday, I step into a devastating structure called Terror Dome. I step into a vial, cruel structure that has been shortening careers as far back was WCE, where it was made famous by men the likes of Rocky Stevens. There are only a handful of guys I've seen in this sport tough enough to even dare step into this kind of brutality. Rocky was one of them. In WHQ, I worked with a man named John Austin. He was another. Semi pauses his speech for a long moment and stares straight ahead; most of the bewilderment and emotion drained from his face. Semi: Terror Dome. This kind of match is truly appropriately named. Another quick pause from Semi. Semi: ...But not for me. Semi cracks a barely noticeable grin from the corner of his mouth. Semi: Terror is truly a proper name; a proper emotion... For the two men foolish enough to step into that dome with The Semi. Semi glances in the mirror again and soon after, you hear the sound of the gas pedal contacting the floor as the engine screams out and the desert background becomes little more than a blur through the window behind Semi. Semi: Alex Bowen! Chris England! I hope I didn't bust you guys' skulls up too bad last time because I'm ready to take you two punks out of my hair for good, and I can't be listening to you whine and cry about a bump on the head afterwards! After a lick of the lips and a crack of the neck, Semi begins talking loudly. Semi: Alex Bowen, you may be the X-Havoc champion, but last week, when you were supposed to be a partner to me in the ring, you proved how weak you really were. As far as I could tell, I was in that ring alone. If that match had continued on the way it was going, your incompetence would have gotten us beaten. I was not about to allow a pin fall or submission on my record, in my first MWE match none the less, even if it was on my lousy partner. That's why I took that steel chair and I proved to everyone watching that I was the most dominant force in that ring. I made sure the momentum from that match came with me. Now that momentum is on it's way to Dallas Texas and to Terror Dome. Semi lets out a growl and reaches his right hand to the roof. Semi grabs a cord and gives it two quick tugs. Two loud blasts of a horn briefly drown out the sound of the speeding truck and they echo across the desert plain. Semi yells loudly straight ahead with fury in his face and voice. Semi: NEVER GUNNA STOP ME NOW! Semi quickly relaxes before continuing his speech. Semi: Bowen, it's real simple. You like to fight. I like to fight. You like things hardcore. I like things hardcore. Monday night, we're stepping into Terror Dome, and there, we can see who thrives on dishing out the pain more. Semi shakes his head, slightly, in tsk-tsk fashion. He takes a big, loud breath and lets it go before continuing. Semi: Though you might get lucky and come out of this alive this time, Bowen; because this time, my target is not you. My target is Chris England. Semi sneers an angry sneer. Semi: Listen up, Chrissy boy. This is going to be the third time we've stepped into the ring together in four shows. I've got to admit; you've made a name for yourself. Good for you, you Queen-loving English cigarette. Semi smirks a little but only before his face falls into deep anger and he begins talking loudly, in a rage. Semi: You've come far, England, but that's my problem with you! You made YOUR name at MY expense! If it were not for the fact that you have stepped in the ring with the Semi twice and have not only survived, but have not been cleanly beaten by him yet, you would be nothing! The first time we met, it ended up being two on one when Austin Quartz couldn't quite swallow his pride and decided to give you a hand. You still didn't beat me. Last time we met, my partner turned out to be worthless and the match more or less became a two on one again. You still didn't beat me! Semi lets out another sigh as his face falls. He tones down his voice and fills it with confidence. Semi: Face it England, you're never going to beat me. You are done making your name at my expense and it's time for me to finally make my name at your's. Chris England; history doesn't happen in matches; it happens in moments. When I walk out of the Terror Dome as the victor, sure, it's going to give my career a boost, but when everyone sees what I do to you, I will go to the top and no one will EVER forget The Semi! Semi looks as if he's trying to find his next words and gives another glance to his mirror. A burst of anger washes across Semi's face. He visibly forces himself to keep his cool before continuing; his words now piercingly menacing. Semi: As I've said, I've got nothing but momentum behind me now and the only thing the MWE could find to try to contain that momentum is a ton of black steel called Terror Dome. I'll make my strategy for this match crystal clear. I am going to take the hardcore boogeyman out of the equation quickly, then I'm going to show Chris England why I am pumping on ten cylinders and why I am Mr. Momentum in the MWE! Semi looks long and hard into his rearview mirror as his face turns red with fury. He nearly foams at the mouth as he yells madly to the road ahead of him. He returns his crazed eyes to the road and booms his voice through the cab. Semi: MONDAY NIGHT, I AM GOING TO RUN TERROR DOME DOWN! The camera seems to make its way out the passenger window and the Semi driven semi roars passed the camera, sounding like a shotgun blast, and leaves a large cloud of dust in it's wake. As soon as the sound of the semi fades, it becomes drowned out by an equally drilling sound. The piercing sound of sirens. The dust begins to settle over the desert road as waves of heat dance through the sand-filled air. The scene is broken from the left by a white and blue Texas State police cruiser with lights and sirens blaring. It speeds by, kicking up more dust that is quickly penetrated by another cruiser. A third, then a forth cruiser follow. The bright cloud of sand becomes blackness as the scene fades out.
