The Semi rolls into MWE; Semi RP

Feb 28 2009

Jesse Anderson

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The scene opens, panning over a huge, rowdy audience. Tens of thousands of screaming fans are filling in an arena with an MWE wrestling ring in the middle of them all. There are signs of all colors and flashes from cameras popping out of the crowd from every direction. There is a certain electricity in the air as the sea of heads chant and cheer while everyone waits for the show to begin. It is not show time yet, but that time is approaching as even more people pour into the already packed frenzy of wrestling fans. The enthusiastic, speratic chatter of the fans fills the arena with an anxious buzz from the basement floor to the tiles of the roof. Suddenly, that buzz is drowned out, as it is instantly over-powered by loud rock and roll music. Guitars blare through the halls and drums are felt deep in the stomach of every person in the building. Eighteen wheels are rolling! Eighteen wheels are crushing me down! Running me down! The fans, who were once scurrying around like ants on a hill have now stopped and turned their attention to the entrance way on the floor of the arena. The show isn't scheduled to begin yet and this music is unfamiliar. What is going on? Cars are crashing! Cars are crashing all around me! Running me down! A large figure, shadowed from most of the crowd and cameras, makes it's way out onto the top of the ramp. As the figure makes it's way further out, a wave of light washes over him. It is a massive man with a sour look on his face. It is the former ICW champion, The Semi. Jesse, The Semi, Anderson is relatively unknown it would seem by the crowd reaction or lack thereof. A few loud boos echo through the arena, but most fans just watch as the huge man makes his way down to the ring. Semi is wearing a sleeveless gray shirt and black shorts with a red stripe on either leg. His medium length brown hair is unkept and messy. There is a bit of stubble around his chin and neck. His most notable feature, however, is the look on his face that says either this man is furious and disgusted by something or someone or that he ate some bad fish. Semi only looks out to the crowd twice as he walks down the ramp to the ring. His attention is focused straight ahead. Semi gets to the end of the ramp, turns to the left, makes his way to the ring steps and climbs to the ring apron. Semi stops on the apron and looks around to the crowd. His lips curl into a disgusted sneer before he steps over the middle rope and into the ring. Semi steps to the middle of the ring and pauses. Again, the fans give little reaction. Semi then raises his arms and lets out a loud roar to the thousands in attendance. A small wave of boos hits him in the ring along with one or two cheers. Semi again looks around to the crowd, then steps to the far corner of the ring and reaches out. The timekeeper sitting at ringside jumps up and hands Semi a microphone in the ring. Semi grabs it and quickly yanks the microphone from the timekeeper's hand and flinches forward, causing the timekeeper to flinch backwards. A small, devious grin pulls the corners of Semi's mouth back for only a moment before he takes his place in the center of the ring. Semi holds the microphone to his mouth but doesn't say anything at first; just glares out at the fans. After a long moment, Semi raises his left hand that is not holding the microphone and uses it to smack the top of the mic a few times before Semi begins to speak. Semi: Testing; testing; one, two three! Can all you bastards here me? A much larger wave of boos hits Semi in the ring this time, which brings another arrogant grin to Semi's face. Semi: Good, I guess so. Semi begins to speak slowly in a matter of fact fashion. Semi: Please allow me to introduce myself. I am a man of wealth and taste... A loudly mixed reaction, mostly positive, emanates from the crowd and Semi stops. His grin curls further before it drops from his face completely and he continues loudly and angrily. Semi: On second thought, to hell with that Rolling Stones bullshit! Everyone shut up and sit down! It's time to listen to me, g*ddammit! Semi pauses again while yet another barrage of boos hits Semi in the ring. Semi: Most of you don't know me because apparently the majority of you MWE fans live under rocks. My name is Jesse Anderson, but every one of you uninformed, self-important fans can call me The Semi! That goes for everyone in the locker room as well. Semi lowers the microphone as a low but steady stream of boos arise. Semi perks up his ears and puts his hand to one, signaling he can't hear them. The crowd then boos louder. Semi cuts them off. Semi: I'm not finished! Semi pauses again and takes a deep breath before continuing. Semi: As I was saying, I am The Semi and soon enough, not one of you will forget it. Since I was a school boy, I have been fighting tooth and nail for everything I've gotten. No, I don't just mean I had a rough time, I mean I fought. I have been beating up worthless punks that have gotten in my way for most of my life, and now I make a living doing it. First I went undefeated in a little promotion called WHQ for two years. Then I went to another promotion that some of you may be familiar with, the ICW; and I went undefeated there too. Now, I am here in the MWE and I plan on continuing that tradition. Semi pauses just long enough to get a good breath to continue but that's all it takes for another wave of boos to rise up. Those boos quickly turn into a chant all too familiar to wrestling fans as the audience, from floor to rafters, tells Semi, "You suck! You suck! You suck!" Semi's eyes widen in surprise. He looks around the arena for a moment before his look of surprise turns to one of rage and he continues, loudly at first to drown out the screaming fans around him. Semi: I suck?! Me?! I suck? What did I do that was so bad? I came out here as the next great wrestler of MWE to introduce myself and list my credentials, and you already think I suck? But what does that say about you people? Most of you have never heard my name before tonight. Even fewer of you have seen me wrestle. I was only beginning my promo! Yet every one of you has already passed judgement and has decided that I'm the bad guy, the heel, and that I suck. Semi lowers the microphone and steps to the ropes left of entrance and looks out to the sea of heads as some cheer and some boo, most seemingly unsure how to respond. Semi turns his attention to the back side of the arena and the thousands of heads that way, before continuing on, still looking and sounding very bitter. Semi: You know what, maybe I do suck. But if I do suck, it's only because I'm one of you people. Semi points out to the audience, and pans to most of it with his extended left pointer finger. Semi: The fact of the matter is, people suck. MWE's newest prospect takes a few steps around the ring as he is hit by all sides with hisses, boos, and swears. After making his way back to the center of the ring and facing the right side of the arena now, Semi continues quickly. Semi: People suck! People are stupid, angry, violent, selfish, gullible, destructive... Semi turns to face the backside crowd but doesn't stop or even slow down. Semi: People lie, steal, kill, cheat, manipulate, and abuse every day, and don't tell me that you don't! More boos erupt but only momentarily before Semi cuts them off. Semi: I'm not finished yet! You people are actually stupid enough to believe your fairy tale religions, to believe your criminal governments, and to believe your brain washing media when they tell you how to live your lives and what you should believe in. Semi lowers the microphone from his face and looks out at the crowd and nods with wide eyes as people boo and yell. "It's true!" Semi says, barely heard, but easily read from his lips. "It's true!" Semi returns the microphone to just below his chin and continues. Semi: The powers that be in this world have you foolish people running on a hamster wheel for them day after day, year after year, life by life, by dangling money and sex in front of you until you die as some statistic or another. Shame on the greedy, twisted people that control and manipulate this world and more importantly, shame on you people that are stupid and scared enough to just let it happen. Semi looks out to the crowd and beckons with his left hand as people start booing the moment he stops talking. He drops the microphone further from his face and returns boos towards the crowd and rolls his eyes. He then returns the mic and goes on. Semi: Yeah, yeah, boooooo, booooo... I get it. You people are so predictable. That's part of what makes you all so easy to control. Semi pauses just long enough again to draw more boos. Semi opens his mouth to speak again at first but stops and closes it as the fans have their say. Semi's face becomes twisted in frustration and he snaps the microphone into place in front of his face again and growls into it. Semi: I can see I haven't made my point yet. You want to judge me? I'd like to think I'm a good judge of character too. Semi drops his hand with the microphone to his side and hastily stomps towards the ropes at the entrance way. Semi drops to his side and rolls under the bottom rope with microphone in hand. He stands and raises the microphone again. He points out a fan, standing in the front row, and quickly makes his way to him. The fan is a large, middle aged man with long, black hair, a shaggy black beard, and glasses. He is wearing a David Whitman T-shirt, jeans, and a hat with the Northern Ireland Irish Football Association logo. He is also wearing a goofy grin on his face. Semi: You! You for example! Semi makes his way up in front of the guy on the other side of the barricade as he talks. Semi: Look at you with your potbelly, your soccer hat, and your T-shirt with your favorite wrestler on it, who is, may I add, one of the sorriest competitors I've ever stepped in the ring with. You're the kind of guy that likes to show his "individuality." Semi makes quote marks in the air around the last part. The fan smiles and crosses his arms in front of his chest. Semi continues on. Semi: You go out to the world saying 'this is who I am, and I want to be accepted for it!' But you're not, are you? What did that individuality get you? Let me guess; it got you 35 years of life and still living with your mother, didn't it? You're the kind of guy that sits at home and chats on the Internet with your wrestling buddies all night after jerking off and eating Cheetos all day. You haven't had a job in years and haven't been layed in even longer. During the course of Semi's rant, the man in the front row goes from all smiles to horrified. His face turns red from embarrassment and anger, looking as if maybe Semi was pretty close. As Semi finishes, the fan shakes his head heavily back and forth and repeats himself over and over again. Red fan: No! no! nope. No. Semi lowers the microphone and begins walking further down the barricade and around the corner while looking around to the fans as some are booing, some are laughing, and some are in disgusted silence. Semi: Who else we got here tonight? Just as Semi finishes asking the question, he spots his next victim, or victims. Semi walks up on a standing couple in the center of the first row. Wearing a blue evening dress is a pretty, young brunette who had obviously spent too much time on her hair before the show and is wearing heavy makeup. Standing with her is a tall, skinny man of similar age with short red hair and a goatee. He is wearing a button up black dress shirt and tan slacks. Each of them is holding a beer. He looks to be having a good time but she just seems upset, apparently from the comments of the debuting jerk. Semi darts his eyes between the two of them a couple times quickly, then chuckles to himself. He licks his lips before he continues. Semi: Look at the happy couple, everyone! We've got Romeo, here, who brought the lovely Juliet out to the professional wrestling show for their second date; how nice! Both of the fans in front of Semi look offended but Semi doesn't stop long enough to let them respond. Semi: I'll bet I know what you're both thinking. Romeo here is thinking he's got it made tonight. He gets to see greatness such as myself wrestling tonight and then he gets to take this pretty little thing home afterwards. Semi pauses for a moment and grins as the man grins himself and shrugs his shoulder a little. Semi only pauses briefly though. Semi: Because, you know... You payed a lot of money for these front row seats, not to mention that beer in her hand. She pretty much has to! A loud OOOOH! comes from the crowd behind and Romeo's face becomes bewildered as Juliet's becomes enraged. Semi: And Juliet here is thinking, 'you know, he did pay a lot for those tickets, and I didn't do my hair up all damn day for nothing. I pretty much have to.' Juliet swears loudly at Semi and swipes her hand out across the barricade. Semi is too far away and leans back a little with a sick grin on his face. Romeo puts his arm around his date and tries to calm her. She shoves him away with her shoulder, turns around, and flips him off. Romeo looks horrified at first, then angry at Semi. Semi begins to walk away and comments over his shoulder back to the couple. Semi: Ooh, sorry if I ruined your chances, pal. I would be careful about that anyway, man. Semi grins and looks down at the ground as he walks a few paces. Semi: I think that means I'm three for three, folks. Semi takes another step, raises his head, and begins to speak but stops, rolls his eyes, and opens his mouth. Semi: Oh, this one is just too easy. Semi walks up to the barricade in front of a seated young teenage boy with brown hair and thick glasses who is wearing a Styx T-shirt. Semi: Let me just say this in a way you'll understand. Semi begins talking in sing-song rhythm with a robotic voice and holds the microphone up with his elbow at 90 degrees. Semi: Homo arigato, Mr. Roboto. Homo... (Semi makes three buzzing noises) HOMO! Semi immediately turns his back to the kid and takes the remaining steps to the ring and rolls under the bottom rope as the apparent father of the kid leans over the barricade and yells obscenities at Semi. The kid just looks confused. Seemingly every fan in the building erupts into boos and hisses as Semi gets to his feet and to the center of the ring. Semi: You see what I mean? You people are predictable and easily led on. You damn people want to try to judge me, but you get angry and want to persecute me even more when I do the same to you? You're a bunch of damn hypocrites and that's what pisses me off the most. Semi's face is clinched in anger and frustration. His eyes dart around the fans in the arena. He licks his lips and seems to relax a little before continuing. Semi: But you know what? I prefer it that way. The more ugliness, fear, and anger I can pull out of you people, the more each and every one of you pisses me off. The more pissed off I get, the more dominant I am when I am in this ring. Loud boos begin to rise from the crowd as Semi stops and drops the microphone to his side. Semi motions to the crowd to bring it. They begin to boo louder and louder still. It becomes deafening. Semi raises his head to the sky and lets out a primal roar. He then returns his attention to the crowd with wild eyes and clinched muscles. He begins to talk loudly into the microphone. Semi: Tonight... I make my MWE ring debut. I team with Alex Bowen to face Chris England and Eric Derf. The fans have mixed reactions as Semi watches on. Semi: Chris England... You and I know each other well. You were to be among the road kill under the Semi's wheels in ICW, but due to interference, that was not to be. I made sure you and the wrestling world knew that if you ever wanted to step in the ring with me again, I would gladly accept so that I can beat you officially and take care of you for good. Semi stops and looks around the arena with a very smug expression. He begins again, slowly at first. Semi: Well it looks like that night is tonight, England. You're big and strong, but I'm bigger, and these 26,000 drones and their combined hatred make me 26,000 times stronger. You say everything English is better, but you're about to see what it's like under the wheels of an American made Semi, wearing a shirt made in Taiwan as you're being smashed into Irish asphalt. You'll just have to hope your Queen will lick your wounds for you. Semi pauses and grits his teeth as he steps around in the ring while staring into the nearly endless rows of fans. Semi: England's partner, on the other hand, is new to the run-down list. Eric Derf. Derf is apparently a pretty new face to wrestling because from what I understand, he's spent most of his wrestling career so far hiding behind a mask and calling himself Mr. Mysterious or Mysterious Guy or something equally stupid. The look of annoyance on Semi's face subsides momentarily as the corner of his stubbly mouth curls. Semi: Though in this case, I'll say Derf, taking the mask off certainly wasn't in anyone's best interest. Semi visibly shudders and squints his eyes. Semi: Correct me if I'm wrong, Derf, I'm just paraphrasing your own sob story, but I hear you had dreams of owning your own great promotion at one time and had your foot in the door. Then, when push came to shove, all of your top names abandoned you and came here to MWE. So what do you do? You follow them here and were too afraid to show your mask when you walked in. You're weak Derf. That's why you hid behind a mask, that's why your wrestlers left you, and that's why I'm going to leave you lying in the ring tonight. I see you've gotten lucky a couple times lately, Eric, but you better be kissing the Irish Blarney Stone right now and Richard Covell's ass after tonight to ever get an opportunity again, because I'm going to single handedly beat that luck right out of you! Semi rests his hand on the top rope and looks out to the fans as they drown him with boos. Semi motions to turn up the volume and they do. As soon as they do, though, Semi continues, cutting them off. Semi: I Guess I got the luck of the draw tonight, because my partner happens to be none other than the guy that just beat good ol' Eric and now calls himself the X Havoc champion, Alex Bowen. Bowen is a guy that likes to fight, and likes it even better when there are no rules. Not to mention he's a guy with momentum on his side. There's nothing wrong with having a guy like that in your corner. The fans boo from all sides. Semi: It's real simple, Bowen, here's our strategy for tonight. Make sure nothing unfortunate happens to me while I take care of our opponents tonight, and you shouldn't even have to break a sweat. Likewise, I'll make sure you don't do anything dumb and get us beat. But remember, Alex, I have your back tonight, but after this match is over, I have my eye on that prize around your waist, among other trophies around this place. After pausing and taking stance back in the center of the ring, Semi raises his microphone and the volume of his voice high. Semi: I am out here tonight to issue a warning. Chris England knows that warning and I'm sure he would be happy to tell his tag partner all about it over some tea and crumpets. Alex Bowen, anyone else in the back stage, or any one of you losers in the audience tonight or watching around the world only have to watch any single episode of ICW or WHQ to know it. And that warning is simply: yield. Yield because if you get in the way of the Semi, I WILL RUN... YOU... DOWN! Semi tosses the microphone carelessly over his shoulder in the general direction of the timekeeper's table. The microphone falls to the floor outside the ring with a hissy thud that echoes through the arena. Semi steps to the ropes in front of the entry way and falls to his back before rolling under the bottom rope and out to the floor. Semi makes his way brusquely up the ramp with the smuggest of looks on his face and his hands clenched into fists. Semi makes his way to the end of the ramp, turns and leaves through the curtain, all the while, being booed out of the building. The scene fades to black.
