The scene opens all that is seen is big old abandoned house that looks like it should be condemned. There is suddenly a gust of wind and the front door to the condemned house swings open. The inside looks a hell a lot worse than the outside, scratch marks all over the wall, broken picture with the glass shattered all over the floor. The smell of the house is horrifying it smells like dead flesh near the foot of the stairway leading upstairs a pack of dead rats that have been what looks like gutted and drained of their blood. Leading up the stairway on both sides of the wall are blooding hand prints and on the top of stairs are more dead gutted rats. Along the walls of the upstairs hallway are more bloody hand prints. The prints all lead to a room it the end of the hallway. In the room is what looks like to be the rest of the dead rats and a man standing facing the wall whispering to himself and using the blood of the dead rats to make what looks like a countdown the man begins to talk to himself out loud. Do you know how it feels to be so alone having no one there to gide you while you are going through the most agenising pain in your entire life. To have everyone you love and thought loved you just abandon you in the blink of an eye. The man turns around he has this crazy look in his eyes like his not all there, the man then begins to scream to himself. Samuel-No No you have no fucking idea it’s like do you! The man calms himself down and for some reason picks up one of the many dead rats just lying around. Well here let me give you a glimpse of what it feels like and you might but able to share some of my pain. The man then with both hands around the dead rat begin to squeeze the dead rodent It’s like an infection spreading from your head all the way down to feet. Left over blood begins to fall from the rat and soon enough blood cover both of the man's hands and forearms draining all of your energy, hope, faith, desire, life, and all the soul that you have left. Until you just become nothing but an empty shell something not even human. Can’t you see it written all over my face? The man than wipes the blood from the dead rats that is on his hands and forearms on to his face and neck till his face turn in to a crimson mask. The man begins to start screaming again. Can you see it? Well can you? No you can’t and do you want to know why because you had the easy life the fun life all the friends you wanted family never abandoned you! The man begins once again to calm himself down.[color] You had the world in the palm of your hand but no you got to greedy you already had more than one person could ever ask for. You didn’t mind asking for more you fucking greedy bastard well karma is a bitch. Now you have almost fell down to my level but still haven’t really felt very much of my pain but it won’t be to long till you how I feel. Just a few days now on Halloween which is my favourite night of the year because it’s the one night of the year where I won’t be the only freak walking the streets and on that night I’ll show you just how much of a freak you really are.Your Pro wrestling career will be just like your football career... nonexistent you will feel the infectious disease run though your body until you give in, give up, surrender. That also goes for the rest of the poor fuckers that fall into my path on my way to the MWE World Heavyweight Championship you’ll all feel my infection.[color] The Man grabs his head and begins to feel a pain running through his head he starts to scream. Fuck! It’s eating my fucking brain ahhhhhhhhh! While screaming the man begins to punch holes throw the walls. The man finally stops putting his fist through the wall he then looks at his hands and he finds that his hands are full of his blood he then gets this sadden look on his face and falls into the corner. There’s just a little preview of how this infection keeps draining me. The man’s saddening face suddenly turns in to a smile and the man starts to laugh. Hahaha and it’s also a preview of how this infection makes me unstoppable. It makes me less human then you anything I feel besides this infection feels so good. So bring everything you have bring all your pain I want to feel it, it’s gonna feel as good as sex. You’ll try to take and feel my pain but I will fuck you up and by the end of the night we’ll find out who can last longer. The man stands up with this aggressive look on his face and begins to yell. My name is The Infection Samuel Black, Stephen Lenton along with the rest of MWE Prepare to be INFECTED! The man Now Known as Samuel Black turns back to the wall and using some more of the rat blood adds another X to that he was making earlier. The scene then cuts to black
