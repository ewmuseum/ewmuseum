Thank you

Sep 28 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Devlin Sharpe appears on the titantron Devlin Sharpe: For years people have been questioning my ability for various reasons, Maybe because I am british? maybe because I am small, maybe because I am not strong? I do not know why, but I have always been pushed down, pushed up, pushed in, pushed out, pushed in all sorts of crazy directions by all kinds of people that do not like me for whatever reason, politics have been playing me around for ages and in this rough and tough wrestling business you need friends in the locker room or else you are nothing, a jobber, forced to lose to everyone just because someone high up hates you, even after you escape backstage politics there is pressure from the crowd, they want the superhero look, they want you to pump your body full of drugs to become a huge some sort of hulking tower of muscle mass and its fucking disghusting that people would destroy their bodies just for that, aside from the drugs, you also have to be able to talk like a pro, I admit it, even myself flub up a few promos I have cut in the past but the pressure on an average MWE superstar is quite spectacular, I do not think theres a single MWE superstar except Brian Michaels Covell that didnt work their ass off, lose blood sweat, tears and all sorts of other bodily fluids to make it to the very top of professional wrestling, mayhem wrestling entertainment, you see small wrestlers such as myself often get looked down on so we have to add daredevil stuff to our arsenal which often results in tremendous injuries if messed up, not many wrestlers are as lucky as I am to land every move perfectly, then again I am not perfect either, I also make mistakes I am not saying that I don't, but being a wrestler you must do things that attract attention to you so that the crowd would like you, crowd reaction is very important to a wrestler, it makes the difference between a win and a loss, It doesnt matter if you are slapping your opponent around for 2 hours, if the crowd stops caring for you, you will lose not matter how bad your opponent is, people badmouth wrestling however they do not understand the important integral parts to it, everyone from the wrestlers to the crowd are involved in a match, they all co exist together like cogs in order to run smoothly as an entertainment based program for everyone to enjoy, one without the other will feel incomplete and I am just trying to explain to some very ignorant people that there is a very intricate balance in delivering a successful wrestling show, every single one of the MWE superstars have worked MANY MANY years throughout the globe honing their skills for you, the MWE audience the best damn audience wrestling has ever seen, you have truly made MWE the great wrestling promotion, without the fans MWE and Devlin Sharpe would be nothing, so each and everyone of you fans I would like to thank you, you're the reason MWE is back, you never gave up, you bought the DVDs, you bought the T-shirt's you bought the windy Os, you even bought lindsay's panties!, thank you for supporting MWE even when we were dead! *Devlin bows to the fans then the titantron closes*
