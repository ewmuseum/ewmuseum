In memory of Annie

Aug 8 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Devlin Sharpe and his new girlfriend Hayley Williams walked towards the couched area which was surrounded by cameras, they were about to be interviewed by shawn spitzer about his thoughts on returning to the MWE Devlin Sharpe: Before we start this interview I would like to dedicate this interview and the rest of my Career in the MWE to my fiancee Annie Garside, who passed away recently Shawn Spitzer: Care to tell us about annie? Hayley Williams: Listen you insensitive bastard... Devlin Sharpe: Relax baby ill tell him anyway...Well to be honest spitty, you don't mind if I call you that do you? of course you do but no one cares, I am not telling you, I am saying this for the benefit of my fans, all of them, the reason is, that Annie Garside was killed in a car crash, a car hit her while she was crossing the road Hayley Williams kisses Devlin Sharpe Hayley Williams: you really didn't need to do that Devlin Sharpe: I did I need to explain everything right now, after Annie died, I lost my mind, I was admitted to a psyciatric hospital for a few weeks, Hayley was Annie's best friend and the first person to comfort me when she died, she always had my back, she was also the reason I regained my mind, she is my everything now... Hayley Williams: Aww Thanks Devlin Sharpe: Now moving on to the subject of me coming back to the mwe, few monthes ago, Brian Michaels called me up regarding a possible reunion show for Rated X, I could not attend due to Annie's funeral however, he also mentioned that he is working hard with his father to bring back the MWE and that he would keep me posted regarding its status, when I heard that i worked non stop to get back in to shape as Annie's death had both psychologically and physically messed me up, a few monthes later, he phoned me up again begging me to come to a meeting with other former MWE superstars regarding bringing MWE back, I went of course in my ring gear no less, i looked stupid because everyone else was in suits, I just said i was dressed for my business which was wrestling, which got an applause from everyone in the room, well after that everyone set off on their own pathes, they were just told mwe would be reforming and they can turn up if they want, if they wouldnt richard covell wished them luck on all their future endeavours which brian michaels said was a nice way of saying "If you don't want to come back, then fuck off douche" Hayley laughed at that line Hayley Williams: aren't you forgetting something honey? Devlin Sharpe: Oh right, right, Well right here I would like to announce that my girlfriends Band Paramore will be singing my NEW theme song: "We were born for this", LIVE at Crowning Glory during my entrance, I also would like to remind you all Crowning Glory is available on Payperview at £14.95 thats $29.99 American and 37.99 Turkish, just thought i'd be multicultural, everyone deserves to see good wrestling, MWE do not discriminate Hayley Williams: Yay, I will not let you down Devlin Sharpe: I know baby I know Shawn Spitzer: What are your first on your first match Devlin Sharpe: Well slightly pissed off, i only agreed to come back if I got a title shot, Brian michaels agreed but didn't tell me that the title shot would be with 7 other people...but eh, no big haha Shawn Spitzer: Most MWE members remember Rated X, you, Dc and Michaels used to be a group that took MWE by storm, what can MWE members expect, can they expect the return of Rated X? Devlin Sharpe: Well... DC is unknown at this point, me and michaels have been trying to get him to come back but he's too busy working as a neon sign in vegas, but maybe me and Michaels, can with a bit of new blood bring back a bigger better badder Rated X just for the MWE fans Shawn Spitzer: When can we expect this new Rated X Devlin Sharpe: Now now I do not want to ruin the suprise, who knows they may be helping me win the title... Hayley Williams: You arent gonna cheat are you? cheating is bad Devlin kisses Hayley Devlin Sharpe: Not if you don't want me to Devlin looks at his watch Devlin Sharpe: Uh Listen Spitz, we have to go get "prepared" for my match if you catch my drift, seeya later, everyone watching Hayley Williams: Bye Shawnie Shawn Spitzer: B... Devlin Sharpe: Get that thought out of your head now... Devlin and Hayley leave the room leaving Shawn who turns to the camera Shawn Spitzer: well there you have it folks, Devlin Sharpe and his new girflriend, the frontwoman of the Band Paramore, Hayley Williams
