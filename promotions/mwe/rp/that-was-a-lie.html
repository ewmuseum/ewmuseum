That was a lie

Nov 23 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Devlin Sharpe and Ryan Sharpe walk up to a Podium dressed in suits and sunglasses, Ryan talks as Devlin stays silent Ryan Sharpe: See Many people ask us why we were released from MWE, we quit because we were sick and tired of being on Monday Night Mayhem, we asked for our release from MNM to go to friday night genesis, but we were denied....so what else could we possibly do but quit? Devlin Laughed like a maniac and took his brothers place Devlin Sharpe: This means we are free... he glared at the camera and spit out each word Devlin Sharpe: to... do... whatever... we... want He paused as he listed everything on his finger Devlin Sharpe: We could, Join the WWE, Join TNA, Join ROH, Ressurect WCW OOOOOOOOOOOOR the most popular choice...Wrestle on Friday Night Genesis... He sighed and look at the camera Devlin Sharpe: You see me and my brother feel that with Michaels being the bosses son and a gigantic douchebag Devlin and Ryan both grinned sheepishly Devlin Sharpe: Will never relinquish his hold on the MNM main event with his bestest buddy D.C Devlin and Ryan make kissy faces Devlin Sharpe: You see Brian and D.C are best friends and Brian is just giving D.C a little rub of his glory and they have a terrible strangle hold over the MNM main event He makes a strangling motion as he litterally chokes out each word Devlin Sharpe: This is why we want to go somewhere where we can advance and not be held down by daddy's little boy and his boytoy, we want to be on Friday night genesis damnit! He punched the table then swapped with his brother Ryan Sharpe: We did actually consider quitting all together but then we saw an advert for Adrenline rush...The thrill of competition that is what drove me and my brother out here tonight to announce our return Devlin grabs the mic Devlin Sharpe: The Hooligans are back baby and all 4 of our opponents better be ready for us! Ryan snatches the microphone off Devlin Ryan Sharpe: ...And we will never retire... Devlin: Wooooooooo! Ryan Sharpe: Why'd you do that for? Devlin Sharpe: Dunno but I do know, what we feel is like a total adrenaline rush!
