Following Through

Aug 19 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
*MWE Cameras appear in the all to familiar setting that is James Sullivan's mansion. They walk up the stairs, and find Sullivan talking on the phone. They try to get in closer to hear what the new poster boy has to say. He simply holds up one finger and walks away. The cameras simply zoom in to keep track of him, and this starts to anger him. He hangs up the phone and walks over to the cameras. He is wearing, like always, a nice t-shirt, a nice watch, and baggy jeans shorts. He poses a little for the camera, and then chuckles. He notices that the camera man is not amused, and suddenly gets serious. Immediately though, he begins laughing at the fact that he just tried to appease to a camera man.* Sullivan: Man, I told you. MWE, I told you. Xavier Stone, I told you to. And guess what, I won my match this week. I debuted and I won. I am moving on farther into this United States Championship tourney. And even with this victory, I am not pleased. See, I was not pleased with my victory, because frankly, the crowd just didn't give me the reaction I wanted. That makes me believe that my fans were not into the match, and that just won't work. I dedicated my whole match last week, solely to my mentor, my master, my trainer, and my friend...Stephen Arthur. And now that I have done that, it is all about bigger and better things. My fans will respect the fact that I keep to my word, and I like that. And frankly, if anyone doesn't like the fact that I praise my fans so much, well they can take it up with me. Because just like I did to Xavier Stone, I will hit them with the J.S.B, and pin them, and pick up another victory. *James is looking around and spots a drink. He takes a few sips to refresh himself, as the camera man looks on. A little more pleased, he still seems angry that he is being so upbeat. He frowns, and Sullivan actually gets angry this time.* Sullivan: Listen man, you need to lighten up. I am trying to be upbeat, and you are just trying to rain on my little parade. Stephen: Actually, you have it all wrong, I am going to be raining on your parade. You are being to cocky as of right now. *Stephen walked in on James Sullivan. Stephen was wearing his normal street clothes, a button up shirt and some long jeans.* Sullivan: What are you talking about man? Seriously, I called the victory...on my debut match. And I won. Xavier almost had me in that match, and I rallied back, for my fans, for the tourney, for career, and won the match. Why should my celebration be tainted...by anyone? Stephen: Because you have to realize that Xavier Stone is only step number one. He was your first challenge, but it only gets harder from here on out. You need to realize that one victory is nothing to celebrate about. *Sullivan is getting angry that his mentor, Stephen Arthur, is embarrassing him on camera again. He brings this up.* Sullivan: But what you have to realize is this was my Mayhem Wrestling Entertainment debut, and I won it, fair and clean. A celebration is only... *Stephen cuts him off mid-sentence...only by holding up and single finger and shaking his head.* Stephen: So what, Xavier Stone was the guy you beat. He is just a stepping stone, you have to realize this. That was literally... round one. You beat him, and now you have to face other people who have advanced in the tourney. Other people, who like you, won their match. This week you have it easy. You are facing Stone again, as well as a newcomer. Sullivan: So what are you saying, I should focus all my time and energy on this match, even though I already beat one half of the opposing team. As well, I have Aiden Copane, who won his match, also on my team. You are saying I should honestly be worried? Stephen: You are being cocky again. But what I am saying is simply this: continue your training. Just keep pushing yourself, because after this week, you matches will not be so easy...believe that. Plus, you aren't even in the big leagues yet. You aren't facing Brian Michaels, Sonny Williams, or David Williams. You are facing guys who are trying, just like you to reach the top. What makes you think that you can get their faster than them? Sullivan: Strength, determination, and fan support are just a few things that will motivate me to victories and my first piece of MWE gold. Stephen: But at the same time, you will be facing people like Aiden Copane. Their motives are a little different than yours, but they will be trying just as hard, if not harder. In his case, he will do anything for the victory, and his motives are less humble and his reasoning will be more realistic. He has greed to motivate him. He will cheat for a victory, and he is only looking to please himself. Furthering his career is the only thing that matters for him. Sullivan: Even with those motives, what makes him believe that he can beat me? Stephen: Why are you being so incredibly foolish today. You are not looking at the big picture.You will have much tougher opponents in the future, so when you beat them, you can celebrate. For now, however, you will not celebrate. You will train. AS for Aiden Copane, look at his title history. Actually, look at his history in MWE. He knows how tag team wrestle, and you were wrong when you said Mason carried the team. Mason was actually equal with Copane at the time. He is a former Tag Team Champion, a former X-Television champion, and a former MWE World Heavyweight Champion. But what you also have to realize, is that he won the United States title in the very same fashion as he could now. He beat out many different other competitors in a tourney, similar to this one that is being held right now. Sullivan: There is a difference though, I am in the tourney, and I will not let him beat me. No way...no how. Seriously, if I were to lose to Aiden Copane in this tourney, I would never forgive myself, and I don't think that my fans would forgive me either! I could never let myself disappoint like that. Stephen: This is so laughable, I see right through your act. Listen, it does not mean a thing when you give your little speeches about the fans. Aiden Copane will not care that you try to please your fans. In fact, it would make his day that he beat you in front of your fans. You are so weak, Mason... Sullivan: Who the fuck cares about the fucking has been that is Mason Resoan. He is a thing of the past. If he was so great, how come you are not managing him? All you keep doing is talking about Mason this and Mason that. Honestly, I am getting fucking sick and tired of it. *Stephen chuckles and laughs. He gave a very evil grin and looks directly at Sullivan.* Sullivan: Are you seriously laughing straight in my face right now. Do you really think that I am going to just sit and watch you do this to me. Do you get pleasure out of trying to embarrass me on television? Stephen: What are you going to honestly do? Sullivan: I am going to kick your ass. And when I am finished with you, you will see why I have been so cocky. Stephen: Is that so? That makes me laugh, because I could make you tap before you could finish your next blink. Sullivan: Fuck you man, I'll show you. This week, if I can't beat you down, I will simply decimate Xavier Stone and his little partner. *James Sullivan walks off, storming in angry. Stephen just laughed and continued to watch his protege get angry. After he was far enough away, he turned to the cameras.* Stephen: This week, you will see the real James Sullivan. The intense James Sullivan that I took under my wing. When you see this James Sullivan, you will finally understand what I talk about when I say the Sullivan will be a force to be reckoned with. Xavier Stone, if you though last week was tough...well then you are in for a big surprise! *MWE cameras fade to black as they see Stephen Arthur laughing as he goes to find his angry protege.* -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Hopefully this one was a little longer, I forgot to check. The ending was a little rushed as I had to get a haircut, so if the ending sucks...sorry. Anyway, I think I am starting to get the hang of this face/tweener gimmick. Tell me if I am doing a bad job, I want to know. Oh, and thanks for reading.
