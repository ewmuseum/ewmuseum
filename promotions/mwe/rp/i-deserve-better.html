I Deserve Better; I WILL BE BETTER

Jun 15 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Scotty And Jekyl are seen sitting in the back corner of a murky looking bar, the stench of stale beer lingers through the air, the gloomy corner is dimly lit. The bar itself looks like it hasn't been properly cleaned in 5 or 6 years, a pool table sits not but 6 feet away, stained with multiple strikes of ancient missed shots. A waitress walks by wearing a sweet scented perfume,a pleasant change from the odor of the atmosphere. Scotty clings to a brown bottle, Jekyl sits in the corner of the booth rubbing his lower jaw. Music plays within the bar, faded and low. Scotty plays scenes from his recent match in his head, he grips his bottle tightly with anger. His blood boils at the thought of losing this past Monday, his facial expressions show his fierce emotion as Jekyl looks over to see. Jekyl: You know, you keep making that face, it will stay that way... Scotty glares at Jekyl from the corner of his eye, his stare pierces all forms of tension and reaches Jekyl's dark stare. Sky speaks up in Scotty's mind, her voice soft and sweet, but a tone of anger arises in her speech pattern. Sky: Since your career started in MWE, Jekyl has been in your life, and ever since that moment, your career hasn't been the best., only one win to your list of accomplishments. Do you want that record to continue? Remember before you came to MWE you were a powerful business tycoon? Now your the chewed gum underneath this clowns shoe... Scotty begins to speak out loud, Jekyl confused remains unmoved but listens attentively from his position. Scotty: No... I want better. Jekyl: what the fuck are you talking about? Scotty remains inattentive to Jekyl and continues his motionless daze. Sky: End your partnership with Jekyl, go solo and regain your proper honor. Scotty: Yes... That could work well. Sky: End his career, ruin his life like he did yours... Scotty:YES! Scotty snaps out of his trance and realizes that Jekyl is speaking to him. With his new state of mind about their partnership with Jekyl, his blood begins to boil. Jekyl: Would you stop talking to yourself... I get too much attention drawn to me as it is. Scotty snaps back at his partner, stunning him in his verbal tracks. Scotty: Shut the fuck up. I'm in no mood for your bullshit right now. Jekyl sits up, intrigued by Scotty's attitude towards him. He places his hands flat on the table, his stare unmoved from Scotty's eyes, the tension in the bar could be cut with a knife. Jekyl: What's on your mind? Normally it's nothing. Scotty turns his head to fully look over at Jekyl, he glare could scare the religion out of a nun, his face as still as stone, his eyes seem almost hollow as he stares into the blackened eyes of his tag team partner, contemplating on what to say and how to say it. Scotty: You wanna know what's on my mind? DO YOU REALLY WANNA KNOW?!?! You make me sick... You go around and do nothing but cause shit to other peoples lives... Jekyl: I thought you liked causing havoc... Scotty: I do but I don't bullshit them and lie to them. I don't manipulate their minds to make them my bitch.. Scotty has begun to raise attention in the bar, everyone around them stops what they are doing to watch the argument and see how the situation ends. The bartender stays behind the bar, waiting to signal the bouncer to intervene. Scotty: Ever since I left ICW to Monday night Mayhem and joined up with you, every match I have been in has turned out in a loss. The way I add it all up, you are the sole reason for this. You have held me back, preventing me from reaching any form of glory I should have. You have been dragging me down with you since day one! Jekyl slides out of the booth and stands in front of Scotty, fists clenched. Scotty follows suit and stands up, staring down Jekyl, eye to eye, fists ready to fly. The bartender stands ready to react, the waitress leaves into the back room in a rush, customers begin to flee the bar. Jekyl: You ungrateful son of a bitch... Who the fuck do you think you are? Without me, you would be nothing... nothing but a sad, pathetic, psychopathic, bi-polar, lunatic with a voice in his head... Without me you would be sitting in the back of the stadium in the changing room, twiddling your thumbs while arguing with yourself on what your going to make for dinner. Scotty grits his teeth, his fists clenched so tight that no man could break the grip. Scotty grabs Jekyl by the lapels of his purple jacket and brings him in close, so that only Jekyl can hear his words of warning. Scotty: You think throwing me in a burlap sack and dragging me to a dark room is helping me? You know what? go fuck yourself...Do I wanna beat your face in? YEAH! So don't tempt me you insufferable fucking simpleton... Scotty releases his grip, but Jekyl doesn't move. The bartender give the signal for the bouncers to move in but keep their distance until something drastic happens. Jekyl: Try something, I dare you, I will hit you so god damn hard, you will fly back to Miami, one way, first class... Scotty grits his teeth and raises his fist, ready to hit. Jekyl: You think you got the balls? I made you, I can destroy you... make your move pipsqueak. Scotty has had the final straw. Cocking his fist back he takes his opportunity and viciously starts attacking Jekyl with a barrage of punches to the face. Scotty: You think your ugly now? Mother fucker, wait till I'm done with you. Jekyl flies onto the booth table, the bouncers step in to make short work of the brawl. The first bouncer grabs Scotty by his right shoulder, but get vigorously tossed onto the pool table. The second bouncer comes in to the brawl and ends up with a broken nose from McKnight's solid fist. Jekyl gets up and rubs his face in pain, blood runs down the corner of his mouth and down his upper lip from his nose. He begins to attack Scotty with a few kicks to the gut while the bouncer struggles to hold him down. The second bouncer grabs Jekyl by the arm and throws him across the bar, he lands in front of the bartender. A click is heard as the bartender holds a 12 gauge shotgun over the sadistic clowns face. Bartender: Listen here... Whatever business you two have together, take it somewhere else, not here. Jekyl starts to laugh out of control, the bartender confused continues to aim his gun at Jekyl. Jekyl: You think you scare me? It's not like I haven't had a gun pointed at me before. Bartender: You listen here. Shut your mouth, the police are on their way and you two clowns will be out of here fer good. Scotty shouts from the back of the bar. Scotty: DON'T YOU DARE COMPARE ME TO THAT FREAK! Minutes later after short arguments back and forth between Scotty and Jekyl the police arrive. An set of officers enters the bar and begins to break up the scuffle. An officer attempts to grab Scotty by the forearm and receives a right fist to the face. More officers arrive and finally manage to break up the fight with a stun gun. The scene fades to black as the promo ends. What we do to survive and conquer varies between men, their goals and aspirations become top priority and will fight tooth and nail to accomplish these goals. We do what we must to gain our own sense of honor and nothing can stand in our way. With the next match so close to the heart and mind Scotty will fight to his last breath to win, to end his partnership with Jekyl and ruin his life for good. The final scene cuts to static, finals words echo through the static. “I will not be someones tool...”
