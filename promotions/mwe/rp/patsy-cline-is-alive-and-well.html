Patsy Cline is alive and well...; *shrug* you tell me...

Oct 8 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
*The scene opens up to show D.C. and Lindsey sitting at a bench on a sunny day. D.C. is wearing a white bandana across his head that has the top exposed. He isn't wearing a shirt but has black cargo pants and white tennis shoes on. He looks over to see Lindsey wearing a WWDCD tank top and a flood pants. She is texting on her cell phone as D.C. looks up towards the sun as it beats down on them. A few palm trees are scene in the view as a couple people pass beside them. The camera pans out to show the surroundings as it turns towards the ocean. The beach is littered with people all splashing in the water and some sun tanning as it comes back over to show D.C. and Lindsey once again in the picture.* Lindsey: So, what do you think? D.C.: I dunno... if you wanna do it thats your own business... *Lindsey leans in to D.C. and looks at him as D.C. stares off towards the beach.* Lindsey: But if you don't want me to do it, then I won't... *D.C. lets out a chuckle sarcastically.* D.C.: And when we were with Kim? I didn't say anything about that... so whats the difference between a sex tape, and Playboy? *Lindsey takes some of the hair thats in front of her face and brushes it back with her hand.* Lindsey: Well, they called and wanted me to get back to them by the end of the week... So, its hit or miss time... *D.C. looks at the camera with a smirk on his face as he slowly begins to stand up. He begins to turn towards the camera as Lindsey shuts her phone and looks up at D.C.. D.C. looks over his shoulder a little at her as he turns back towards the camera.* D.C.: Alright... but, lets do this first... *D.C. lets out a sigh and shuts his eyes. As he opens them up slowly, he raises two middle fingers towards the camera. Lindsey joins him while still setting on the bench.* D.C.: F*** you Sinc Mercier... *By the time you read this... D.C. is lowering his hands and is about to explain. (thumbs up).* D.C.: YEAH YEAH YEAH, I know my match this week is against David Whitman and I will get to his ass in a couple minutes, but Sinc... *D.C. points a finger into the camera.* D.C.: See I was on MWEShop.com yesterday, and saw that they had my book up for sale with a cheap plug... and then you came out with a book for sale with a cheap plug... the only difference between the two is... *D.C. stands up straight and tilts his head back looking down at the camera.* D.C.: When the f*** did YOU... become the Resident Loud Mouth? *D.C. holds his arms out to the side. He looks back at Lindsey who is already shaking her head. She shrugs her shoulders as starts to blow a big bubble of... well bubble gum... it pops...* D.C.: See I don't ever recall lending that claim to anyone Sinc. I don't remember, anyone actually taking that from me. So I don't f***ing care, if it was YOU that wrote your own plug... or if it was someone else... See I don't mind people TRYING, to be like me... I don't mind people TRYING to defend me... but the f*** if I need anyones support right now... *D.C. lets out a chuckle.* D.C.: So if you think, that just because last week when you TRIED to back me up in my match against Beowulf during your promo, that that suddenly makes us ok, and good friends... F*** YOU! *D.C. grabs his crotch and sticks it out towards the camera* D.C.: You couldn't be me even if you got the plastic surgery. You wouldn't even know what the f*** to do with a vagina if the b**** came with instructions tattooed on her inner thighs... *D.C. begins to sway slightly as he looks at the camera tilting his head to the side.* D.C.: Sinc I single handedly tore through this roster once before. I'm not the one choosing to run through it again. Blame Covell for keeping me around this long. BUT, if this federation wants to keep making you seem like the alternative to MY LEGACY... then you just got a big target painted right across your f***ing forehead... I don't give two s***s and a f*** if you even believe you are better then me... You can't step to this... *D.C. holds his arms out as Lindsey comes up behind him and rubs his abs.* D.C.: I am a walking ego trip... I am everything you would want to be... ask Sonny Williams, ask Brian Michaels, ask Aiden Copane, Alejandro Rodriguez, Greg Cherry, Dev, Whitman, Pala, Ace Cannon, Paige, Windy, George Prax, the list goes on and on... I will jack your f***ing jaw son... I will punch your teeth so far down your throat you'd have to chew through your belly button... *D.C. gets a furious look on his face as he inches closer towards the camera.* D.C.: I've been arrested four times... I've started wars... I've won championships... and I've ended careers... what the f*** have you done? Won the United States Title? I MADE THE X-TELEVISION TITLE MORE THEN THE US... when I was the X-TV Champion, my matches were in higher demand then any champion that carried that belt... so who... the f*** are you?... *Lindsey throws up middle fingers from behind D.C. as they pose for a second. D.C. stands up straight and tilts his head back looking down at the camera.* D.C.: But your not the one I'm facing off against this week. So I consider you lucky. This week I have to take on the person who not only won the first MWE Championship back in the original MWE, but the man who also won the belt the second time around... The living legend himself. The man who has shown what it means to be a fighting champion... David Whitman... *D.C. holds his hand up extending his index finger asking for a moment.* D.C.: You were inducted into the MWE Hall of Fame at Crowning Glory... you went on to beat everyone in the middle of the ring to show everyone just who the f*** they were dealing with. You put your stamp on this nu-MWE by turning everyone attention to you and carried that belt, for all it was worth, until just recently you lost it... *D.C. lets out a small chuckle as he looks away from the camera. He turns back towards it with a cracked smile.* D.C.: And what happened after you lost the title back then Whitman? You choked.... You couldn't rebound from losing the title the first time. You became a fraud, a fake. A has-been hanging out with have-nots. Pala surpassed you, and after a while, Sonny surpassed you... while you were left to pick up whatever scraps the bookers would throw at you... f*** inbetween that time they even threw you at me when I was X-TV Champion, and I beat the hell out of you and tossed you to the side without ever looking back... So, whats gonna make this time any different? *D.C. stands straight up and Lindsey stands up beside him. She puts an arm around him and rests her head on his shoulder.* D.C.: Is it because your still considered a Main Event figure in the nu-MWE? Am I supposed to be afraid of the person that I watched drown himself in shame the first time around? What? Is this supposed to be some kind of f***ing retribution match? People are honestly expecting YOU, of all people, to beat me and go back to the MWE Championship and win it again... aren't they? *D.C. shakes his head slightly.* D.C.: Not this time Whitman... this time, history is going to repeat itself. I'm going to beat the f*** out of you in that ring. I'm going to show people what I plan on doing to Michaels at the PPV, by taking out the FORMER, MWE Champion. By taking out their beloved class clown... *D.C. lets out a chuckle for a couple of seconds. Lindsey takes her arm back from D.C. and walks a bit in front of the camera.* Lindsey: What are you going to do Whitman? Come out with another episode of MWE's Brady Bunch? Are you gonna do a show with Barney? D.C.: Or are you gonna bring back Fraggle Rock during your promo and hope to pull one over on me? Lindsey: You can do whatever the f*** you want, because we all know the only people that cheer for you, are the same people that are fat enough to look like the Stay Puffed Marshmallow Man. *D.C. stands back for a second with Lindsey.* D.C.: See Whitman, had this been a year ago, I would have loved to be in this match against you. I would have killed to have the opportunity to go against you while you were still considered one of the best, but you fell off. But this time around... I don't want to do this match because, while I respected who you used to be back in the Original MWE, I also remember the Mayhem Mafia. *Lindsey puts a hand over her mouth and makes an innocent look on her face as she rolls her eyes.* Lindsey: Oh my God... *D.C. holds his hands out towards the camera like he is about to explain something.* D.C.: Now for those that don't know or remember. The Mayhem Mafia, played second best to Rated-X. And while Michaels, myself, and Devlin Sharpe were slapping the taste out of peoples mouths... the entire time... Mafia tried to play catch up. Now, truth be told, every single person that was in Mayhem Mafia, went on to become World Heavyweight Champions... I guess Devlin never got the memo... *D.C. covers his mouth quickly and looks at the camera with wide eyes. Lindsey* D.C.: I don't respect you anymore though Dave. Back then I had all the respect in the world for you. But you've choked more then Carrie Marie ever did. (Only Original MWE guys will get that). *D.C. cracks a smile like only he can.* D.C.: But I know whats at stake Whitman... You are the top figure in this company right now... I've lost most of the matches I've had here so far... but this time its different... I'm not going to let anyone come between me and you. Because I came here, with the INTENTION... of killing MWE... And thats exactly what I plan on getting back to. The match against Beowulf was a train wreck because of the bulls*** that surrounded him... The tag match? That was a joke and a half because Michaels wanted to play his own game... *D.C. shrugs for a second.* D.C.: But this week... its me and you Whitman... I'll make sure of it. I don't care if I even have to pay Covell to make sure Michaels and everyone else in MWE, stays backstage, I will do it... its one on one... Ego, versus Innovator... D.C. versus Whitman... D.C. versus MWE... *D.C. points a stern finger at the camera.* D.C.: If your going to go through with this match... then make sure you have the faith in everyone in that locker room to motivate you. Because as far as I'm concerned... MWE's hopes and dreams, will be crushed, right along side of you when I lay your a** out... one... two... three... *D.C. hunches back as Lindsey sneaks up in front of him.* D.C.: And if you don't like that Whitman... Lindsey: F*** YOU! *D.C. and Lindsey spit on the camera. The picture goes blurry as D.C. reaches behind him and picks something up from the bench. Lindsey flicks her head back putting her hair behind her as they begin to walk off. The scene fades to black with a blurry picture of the sunny sky as Lindsey and D.C. are heard laughing as they leave.* OOC: It ended quicker then what I'd wanted it to go to.
