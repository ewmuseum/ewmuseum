Glory Road?; High Road Match

Jul 3 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
OOC: And its finally done. Its long though. Around 5,100 words or so. Looks like my supposed "Creative Writers Block" ended. I had to cut the CD in the end short, I was afraid it might drag. So yeah, enjoy. QUOTE Nic "Shawn" Kraven Cassidy Uncle Trent Detective “Presently my soul grew stronger; hesitating then no longer, 'Sir,' said I, 'or Madam, truly your forgiveness I implore; But the fact is I was napping, and so gently you came rapping, And so faintly you came tapping, tapping at my chamber door, that the scarce was sure I heard you' – here I opened wide the door' - Darkness there, and nothing more.” PROLOGUE It's that time once again. It's Pay-Per-View time. The time where stars will become superstars, and superstars become legends. And for Nic Kraven, it's another step to glory, and another step to total domination. For weeks, Kraven has shown he deserves to move up along the ranks. Even pinning the former World Champion, Mason Resoan. Well now, he finally gets the chance. It's put up or shut up. Independence Day lies ahead. Kraven will have to battle it out with two up and coming stars, Jay Riddick and Anthony Clark in a first ever High Road match. Simple really, Kraven wins, he names his successor for his X-TV Title and moves up along the ranks. He loses, then it's a step down and he stays in the division. Can Kraven pull out a win in this match? Or will Riddick or even Clark reign supreme. Answers lie, Independence Day. <<Produced by Michaels Inc.>> #01 [Darkness. We can't see a thing. But out in the looming abyss, we hear sounds, noises. It's more like footsteps really.] [Clack.] [Clack.] [Clack it goes. And finally, like time and time again, the scene opens. Colors, shapes, sizes, hallways, doors, everything appears until finally, it makes a gloomy gray prison. No..no, Kraven is not in jail. On the contrary, Kraven is shown in his normal street clothes walking down the noisy hallways with cells all around him, and out to freedom. Sweet, sweet freedom. It's a shame though, that most people here in this prison, will NEVER see the daylight again. But let it be known, people need to pay for their sins done in this world. Kraven's attire includes a leather jacket, a white tank top, and black baggy jeans. The camera suddenly turns away and looks behind him. And it appears though, Kraven has just come out of a room as there is an opening door behind him. However it is not just a lone door, a man is standing there as well. He has on a black suit with a red tie. His expression explains it all. It's like hes about ready to rage. His face is slightly red and beads of sweat are running down his face, to his 5 o'clock shadow, and down his neck. He opens his mouth and shouts out at Kraven.] “Listen punk! We ain't done yet! We ain't done yet, ya hear me!? I know you have something to do with your sister's disappearance. And sooner or later, I'm gonna find out the truth. Then, you'll hit rock bottom faster than Clinton's reign in office! It ain't over, I want you back here next week!” [Faster than Clinton's reign in office? Interesting. Kraven glances back at the man while walking. He rolls his eyes a bit before speaking in a boring, uninterested tone.] “Yeah, sure. And old man, let it go. We both know that I had nothing to do with this. You'd have to be a freakin' moron to think I had anything to do with the kidnapping of my sister. Go find the REAL perpetrator instead of wasting my time.” [The man opens his mouth to speak again but Kraven is to far away now to barely hear anything. So, it seems like the words coming out of his mouth are, “Blah, blah, blah, blah.” Kraven continues walking until finally coming to a stop at a lone baby blue door. He quietly turns the door knob as light is let in engulfing the dark scene. Soon, it fades away revealing the police office. Wooden desks, police officers everywhere, and...Cassidy and Uncle Trent. Trent, with a black Hollywood Undead T-Shirt, and some old wrinkly jeans, appears to be signing the release papers. Cassidy, with a bright pink hoodie and blue jeans, smiles slightly as she sees Kraven. She speaks as she goes up to hug him.] “Hey! You okay Shawn? You look a little beat up...and a little tired.” “Yeah, yeah Cassidy. Don't worry, I'm alright.” [Cassidy lets Kraven go and gives him a concerned look. Quite typical of Cassidy though. Shes always been the caring type of person. Kraven gives Cassidy a slight grin, a “Don't worry” grin so to speak. He now looks up staring at Trent as he finishes the release papers.] “What are you doing here?” “What do you mean what am I doing here? I'm bailing you out of course. Look kid, me and Cassidy were worried about you getting yourself hurt in there.” “No. I mean why are you here? Aren't you suppose to be out in a strip club wasting all your money or something? And don't give me that crap. You never cared about me or Cassidy. Your just an old rich fool who cares about nobody but yourself.” [Kraven gives him a cold hard stare. He can feel his anger rising. What he just said was true. He never cared about anybody else. Why should he change now? It's to late. Trent looks back at him looking like he hasn't done a thing wrong.] “Hey, hey, hey. Lets not go there now. Listen, I know what I did wrong and I'm here to make it up. And I think I deserve some gratitude for bailing you out of jail. We both know there isn't a single soul out there that would help you besides me and Cassidy.” [Woah. Wait, back it up. Gratitude? Is he kidding? Kraven begins shaking a bit, reaching the boiling point. Cassidy sensing Kraven's rage, tries to hold him back.] “Gratitude? GRATITUDE?! GRATITUDE FOR WHAT?! For bailing me out of prison?! I could have gotten out on my own! Tell me...tell me why you SHOULD DESERVE ANYTHING?! You NEVER GAVE A SHIT ABOUT ME OR CASSIDY UNTIL NOW! YOU-” [Just before Kraven can finish his sentence, Trent cuts him off. Other officers, sensing the disturbance begins to get up and walk to them to escort them out. Cassidy, almost at the point of tears, can barely restrain Kraven.] “Woah, woah, woah. Calm down now kid. This is no where to be having an argument. We could get arrested. Now listen son-” “No. YOU LISTEN! You, and this whole family are f*cked up! Caring about nobody but yourselves, me and Cassidy had to survive on our own! And it's because of you and our parents, THAT WE WERE RAISED LIKE THIS! YOU COULD HAVE BROKEN THIS DAMN CHAIN! You could have gone out of your way and helped us. Raised us as if we were your own. But no, YOU are like everyone else. IT'S ALL YOUR FAULT! And now, now after so many years, you walk back in to our lives and expect everything to be alright?! Well f*ck you! I don't need this! She doesn't need it! It's to late. You can't change a thing!” “Shawn, calm down. Stop...” [Kraven, not wanting to have anything to do with this anymore, walks away. He mutters something under his breath along the lines of, “Lets go.” Kraven walks out of the police department pulling Cassidy behind him. The camera cuts until a moment later. Kraven is outside with Cassidy standing besides him. Trent, barely out of the department, rushes up to him trying to reason this whole situation out. Trent roughly grabs him by the shoulder and turns Kraven around only to be slapped hard across the face. Kraven's head swiftly turns to the right as his cheek reddens a little.] “Now let me explain damn it! I know I was a bad Uncle but I want to change. I want to be here for the both of you. I-” [Kraven wasn't going to give him the chance to talk. He grabs him by the shirt and lifts him up looking directly into his eyes with that ice cold stare.] “No, these are just empty words. No man can change completely. Now you listen to me, STAY OUT OF...MY...LIFE.” [With this, he lets him go. Trent, eyes wide, simply just stares at him in shock. Kraven turns and walks away not caring what happens to Trent or Cassidy. He knew she was safe and he just wanted some time in peace and quiet. He didn't need this. This is quite possibly the last thing he needs to worry about. The afternoon sun shines down on him as the scene fades away.] #02 [Our scene opens up back in Kraven's fairly large house. 5 pm in the afternoon on Wednesday. The camera pans around the house revealing lamps, glass tables, leather couches, and right in front of the couch lies a 50' inch HD TV. You can say that Kraven's done well for himself despite his past. Kraven is shown laying back on the leather couch, his attire fairly different now. He has on a white wife beater and dark blue jean shorts. The house is empty of course. He lives alone. He stares up at the ceiling thinking. Thinking about a lot of things really. His life, the incident from earlier, and his upcoming match. He randomly reached over for the remote set on the glass table besides the couch. Kraven turns on the TV just flipping through channels for anything worth watching. Kraven finally settles on Sports Center. He focuses on the TV for a while before turning to the camera and speaking.] “Glory. A praise, or honor. Now, sooner or later, one will have to reach glory. For me, in just four months in this company, I've reached a status that few in my position have gotten to. I've beaten many. Heck, I even beat the former World Champion. And I'm still going strong as the X-TV Champion. But good things can't last forever. You see, this Independence Day, I'm in the first ever High Road match against Jay Riddick and Anthony Clark. One both whom I know very well and one...that is just strange and mysterious as me.” [Kraven looks away from the camera for a moment, looking at the TV. ESPN seems to be showing a little documentary of the Laker's Championship Win. He finally looks away and turns his attention to the camera. Kraven strokes his hair back with his left hand before speaking once again.] “Riddick, when I look at you, I see another version of me. You're strange, calm, collected. You're aggressiveness is like no other. And like me, you grew up as a loner and drifter. Hated and shunned by society. I know how it feels Riddick, I've been through the same. Riddick, you an I are both mentally unstable. But this...this is an advantage. We see the world in a different light. We both know the world is an unsafe place. This world is full of deceit...lies.” “Jay, I would ask you to join me. We're two of the same people. You should join me so we can both take revenge on this world. But then, then I look back to the Mayhem from a few weeks ago. You said, that you wanted didn't want to be the next Brian Michaels, you wanted to be the first and only Jay Riddick. Thats quite noble of you Riddick. Quite noble indeed. This is when I take my offer back. Yes, you want to be Jay Riddick? So be it. But when you look at it again, you can't be the first and only Jay Riddick. Because you see, you're already the second Nic Kraven! Yes, don't you see it?! You and I had our dreams shattered in our childhood. You and I had some traumatizing experience in the past. [It's quite clear now, Kraven has become a bit psycho. His eyes wild and lit up. His worn down arms are shaking. It looks as though something inside of him has snapped.] YOU AND I are just...a bit...delusional. Yes, delusional! You can't deny it Jay, we are both alike down to our very core. You can't deny it. Nobody can deny this!” [Kraven starts laughing like a madman for a few moments. He finally regains a little composure. However though, hes still shaking just a bit. A horrendous sight really. Kraven stretches his neck a bit and opens his mouth to speak. His bright teeth shown.] “Last week Riddick, you had declined an offer to join Michaels Inc. Thats fine Jay, but this could be the biggest mistake you've ever made. What's wrong with being the next Brian Michaels? You said that he was everything you despised. But everything you had despised was what made Michaels a 3 time World Champion. And one thing you can't deny, is that you want to be a World Champion. You've said it yourself, you want to be a champion. And that's no surprise as I see it. If I remember correctly, you've never held gold before, am I correct?” “You say you work hard, more than most people should. But that hard work never really pays off does it if you can't win a single title? What does that show you? It shows you that you're not good enough. You can work hard all you want. You can trash talk all you want. But the fact of the matter is, your nothing! Nothing if you can't win a single title in your career. Nobody can deny because they know it's true. Me, the first time I stepped into this company, I was a nobody. I was just a low class wrestler to them, nothing more. I was virtually unknown. It took an upset over Eric Derf and a X-TV Title victory to even get them to notice me.” [Kraven sits back on the chair spreading both arms out wide. He sighs a bit and shaking his head. Hard life eh? As Kraven turns back to the TV, Sports Center goes on a commercial break. A commercial on the upcoming Independence Day Pay-Per-View is shown. Kraven smirks a bit as it shows the matchups.] “Riddick, tell me. Who have you beaten so far in this company? Who? It's virtually no one worth mentioning. The Dark Karnival? A group of jobbers who've decided to pan together because they know they can't make it big in this company? Anthony Clark? A delusional man who thinks hes god's gift to women? Riddick, it comes down to this, you've beatened no one worth mentioning besides Chris England. But even then, England was only a mere X-TV Title contender.” “Jay, I know you want my title. You thirst for it like a wild Tiger on its prey. Your eyes show it all. But can you really beat me Riddick? Can you step up your game when the time calls for it? Or will you choke like Tony Romo in the playoffs. Good in the regular season, but when its the big time, he gets...”stage fright” You've never won a title and might never will. Even if I do choose you as my successor, you've still haven't earned it the right way. It's a shame you'll never be better than your mentor XXX. It's a shame that all that hype might be just... talk.” [Kraven slowly starts to stand up right, making his way to the kitchen. The camera pans around the rather bright kitchen. It's really not dirty at all. Kraven reaches out and grabs open the refrigerator door. Inside, lies fruits, vegetables, and well...cake. However though, as he looks down, he finds a water bottle. That should do. He closes the door quickly and walks over to a wooden table drinking his water. There lies, the X-TV Championship. He stares at it, cold eyes and all.] “The X-TV Championship. Clark, Riddick, you want it. Theres no doubt you thirst for it. But here I ask, how bad do you want it? Huh? Do you want it so bad that you'll step in the ring with me? Me. The Crucifix Phoenix. I took out two of the best this company had to offer for god sake! I left them lying in the ring out cold! I've left more people lying in their own puddle of blood then I can count. Sailing my way to glory. Glory that I freaking DESERVE!”[A quick flashback is played as Kraven stands there drinking his water. The scene changes to a ring. Red ropes all around it. It's an unknown company. A company that Kraven was in long before his tenure with MWE. The crowd in the arena is unbelievable. Theres so much energy and excitement. In the middle of the ring lies the words, “XXX and EAW Wrestling.” And there, there stands two men battling it out. An unknown man and Kraven.] [Kraven in his usual attire but he appears to be bleeding heavily out of his head and the man in...a hillbilly attire? What the...? The man bounces off the ropes and runs at him with a clothesline. Kraven ducks and turns him around hitting a DDT. As he goes down, Kraven looks at him with hatred in his black eyes. As the man gets up, Kraven finishes him with the Awakening Sins. Pin, and...it's over. Kraven is then kneeing look up at the ceiling. The referee hands him the title. Kraven takes it away smiling looking quite like a psychopath. The scene ends with Kraven on the ground holding the title, blood running down his face, laughing. It opens once again in Kraven's home. Kraven still standing with the water bottle in his hands.] “What is my point you ask? My point is, you can't stop me. Clark...Riddick, you can't stop me! I'll continue rising up the ranks and sooner or later, become world champion. This Independence Day, you both have to bring in the American Mafia, Russian Mafia, Irish Mafia, Jewish Mafia, Italian Mafia, and even the Yakuzas to even think of taking me on!” "Because you two and I know, that I'm not going down without an all out war happening! But take a moment...Clark. You go hold and kiss whatever girl your with now. Hold her close, and ask her...ask her if she loves you. Then ask her, if she can go on later in life without her precious boyfriend by her side.” "And theres you Riddick. I want you go to your son. Go to your son and tell him you love him. Apologize. Apologize for raping his mother like the crazy f*cking bastard that you are!"[Kraven lets out a small chuckle. He knows very well that hes getting into Riddick's head.] Spend a day with him. Taking him out for ice cream. Buy him some video games. And then hold him, hug him like you'll never see him again. Then ask yourself, can you beat me? Can you or Clark beat me?! Take a moment...take a moment and ponder.” [Kraven finishes up his water and throws it in the trash nearby. He walks back over to the couch and looks at the TV. Jonathan Coachman is seen talking about a Yankees game from a while ago. He hops onto the couch and just...looks up at the ceiling.] “Riddick, you're a crazy bastard aren't you? What kind of desperate, lowly, disgusting human being rapes his girlfriend? She said that she never really loved you so you go and rape her? Why? Because you knew you wouldn't get some? Were you so desperate for love that you would go to these lengths? You're lucky you're not rotting in a jail cell right now. But really, Riddick, thats what I like about you. You're totally crazy! You once said, “One moment can change everything imaginable.” Well that moment, that moment changed your entire life forever. And that moment, shall be your downfall. It's eating you alive inside. That memory haunts you like a cancer. You can't think straight. Your heart beating fast, faster than it normally should. Then you start to sweat. Beads of sweat falling from your head, into your eyes, blinding you. And I'll be there...to put you out of your misery.” “A man once said, you can't live in your past. But he was wrong. Dwell on it Riddick. Use it to your advantage. Feed off the anger and aggressiveness from that night. Bring it to Independence Day so I may diminish it. Step into that ring so I may break every single bone in your body. You want my title? Well come get it.” #03 [Mind games. This is what Kraven likes to do with his opponent. Enter their mind, and break them down one by one. He knows hes gotten into the mind of Riddick. Now, he must break him down. Kraven cracks his knuckle before continuing.] “And then, we have Anthony Clark.. A man who I have beaten once before. A man I know well now thanks to our match. Clark, you don't get it do you? You just don't get it! Do you know what your downfall was in our match? It was your over cockiness. You thought you were just going to walk in there and take away the X-TV Championship from my grasps that easily?! You were cocky, you underestimated me. Have you learned your lesson yet? This Sunday, you have another chance at my X-TV Championship. But this time, you have another man standing in your way. Jay Riddick himself.” “And there really isn't a doubt in my mind. We are both better than you. What have you done to deserve another chance at my title? Nothing! Absolutely nothing! You've beaten no one worth mentioning and yet, you get another chance. Well, thats fine. Because it's my pleasure to show you you don't belong in the title picture. It will be my pleasure to wipe that arrogant smirk off your face. I don't care if your in Michaels Inc. As a matter of fact, I have no idea what you're doing in Michaels Inc. in the first place. You just seem like a lost little boy. Having no idea what you're doing. You're not world champion material. You're not going to be the next Brian Michaels. It's simple, you're just...a clown.” [The corner of his mouth turns into a slight smirk. In his mind, Kraven knows Clark is just a clown looking for some attention. It's sad...pathetic. Putting people down is one of Kraven's specialty. Kraven starts changing the channel, tired of ESPN. He stops A&E, they seem to be doing a documentary on the life. Interested, he sits back watching not really facing the camera.] “You know, I'm sick of it. I'm really getting sick of this. Clark, I'm really starting to get sick of YOU. It's just getting really repetitive. Clark, each and every week, you talk about your opponents, put them down calling them talentless scum, and talk about yourself as if you are a god. Then when the week roles by, you go out there...and you lose. This all is just really getting tiresome. You can talk a good game yet when it really is time you put up or shut up, you sit back cowardly like a helpless little puppy and let your opponent stomp you to the ground. Whenever you're in an interview or when you have that mic in your hands, you jabber on more than a poet. You make empty promises...empty threats.” [Kraven takes a breath and pauses sighing to himself. A flashback is played of Anthony Clark speaking.] QUOTE Clark: Kraven is going up against the young, and fastest rising star pro wrestling has ever seen. QUOTE Clark: Kraven... Tonight I'm partying... But come Monday Night... I'm going to be partying again. But you see this is going to be for an all together different reason. It will be for me winning your XTV Title.” [With this, the scene of Clark fades and opens back up into the MWE Arena. The crowd seems to really be getting into the match. Battling it out is Clark and Kraven.] QUOTE *This was the case, as Kraven turned to face Clark, who jumped up looking for a big dropkick. Swatting him away, Kraven lurked behind him, and got ready to hit his finisher. Kraven Impact! ....1 ....2 .....3 Fred Carry: Here is your winner and still, MWE X-Television Champion...NIC KRAVEN! [The scene ends with Clark out in the ring as Kraven is handed his X-TV Title. Kraven goes to the top rope taunting to the crowd. A mixture of boos and cheers are pouring down on him. The flashback fades and the scene continues with Kraven still on the couch staring at the TV.] “Clark, you're like a broken record. Repeating the same things over and over again. Making the same empty threats and promises. All of these, all of these make you seem like a joke. See, maybe you weren't paying attention during your training sessions with Warner and Hardaway. Maybe you didn't have you're head in the right place during training. Whatever the reason, It'll be my pleasure to diminish you once and for all. I've had enough of your voice Clark. Every time you open your mouth, it just irritates me. I suggest you just shut up and wrestle for once in your life.” [You can tell by his expression that Kraven is disgusted. His blood is boiling just talking about it. He strokes his hair back with his right hand but quickly pulls it away. He looks at his hand with a wondering face. Sweat.. But it didn't seem that hot earlier.. Kraven shrugs it off and stands up to turn on the air conditioner. He sits back on the couch moments later and speaks once again.] “Clark, let me take you back to your days in GWC. You were the longest reigning Tag Team Champion. Do you get my point? Clark, this means you always needed help. This just prove my point about you. You always needed help help because you were crashing and burning as a singles competitor. You knew you couldn't do anything by yourself so you settled on someone else doing your dirty work. This just shows how pathetic you are Clark. Undeserving of the X-TV Title. You, so cocky...so arrogant, yet you haven't done a thing worth mentioning. I mean, George Bush did more in office than you have done in MWE.” “It's never going to change. You'll always need help. You lost against me, Riddick, and you lost again last week in your triple threat match. And sooner or later, the fans will grow weary of you and soon will your so called girlfriends. If you've needed help in almost your whole tenure in GWC, what really makes you think it won't be the same here in MWE? What makes you think you'll actually beat me, not even mentioning Riddick, for the X-TV Title?” [Kraven finishes speaking and shuts his eyes, his head looking up at the ceiling. His shoulder length black hair blows a bit due to the air conditioner. The memory of the confrontation from earlier still lingers inside his mind. Why cant he get it out of his mind? Was he wrong in telling Uncle Trent to stay out of his life? No... No that can't be it. Kraven puts his left hand to his face in a “facepalm” motion trying to get it out of his mind. Still with his eyes shut, he speaks.] “I've asked this many times before. And I'll ask this just one more time. Why does man need to be at the constant center of attention? What more can a man want? Can't you live your life the way it is now and step down from the spotlight? No...but you wouldn't do that would you? You're too blinded by the glory handed to you by this world. Blinded by the fame and fortune. Now, you've been handed on a platter to me. And it's my job, to diminish your light. Show you the true evils of this world.”[Kraven finally opens his eyes. Looking at the TV and back at camera seeming kind of uninterested.] “Clark, you're unorginal. Yes, I said it. Clark, you're like all the other men I've faced in my past who claims to be “the shit.” Arrogance, cockiness, self-centered, willing to put people down if chance has it. Its the same thing over and over again. It gets tiresome over time. You know, you kind of remind me of somebody. Now who was it again? [Kraven puts a hand to his chin stroking it, blinking his eyes. Suddenly, he makes a sarcastic face as if a light bulb clicked on inside his head.] Oh, thats right, Brian Michaels. You're like Michaels in some way. The smirk, the huge ego. But the thing is, he was a World Champion while you, rely on your partners.” [[Kraven, yawning a bit, reaches into his jacket pocket and brings out a small rectangular blue object with white headphones wrapped around them. You're slowly losing your attention you once had. And soon, after this match, that thread will be cut and you will fall...fall into the dark abyss. This Sunday, its that time once again. No...no not Superbowl Sunday. It's a new day, a new pay-per-view, its Independence Day. Its the beginning for some, but the demise for others. For me, it's another step to glory road. Another step in another division. Another step closer to my salvation. As a great man once said, “Its Showtime!” Clark, Riddick, bring your best! Riddick, bring that demon inside of you. I know theres one inside of you. It was present during your promo night was it not?! Bring it so I may destroy it and leave you broken...as a man and as a father. Clark, bring all you've got. I know you thirst for a victory so its time you show it. People often labeled me as demon, hell spawned from the very depths of the earth's crust. Well, so be it. Let's bring out the demon this Sunday! Because, its time, its time to take another step on my glory road." [Kraven chuckles a bit as he finishes his sentence. And then, what seemed like minutes, the song finally comes on. Kraven sits back and relaxes as the song drowns out all the noise around him.] “This the type of song that make the angels cry, i look up in the sky and i wonder why? why you had to go, go I know its better on the other side, you were chosen from the start never gon let you go,” [The song continues giving Kraven a sense of relaxation until...] [DING DONG] [Kraven opens his eyes annoyed. He sighs as he stands up and goes to open the door. And there, stands Cassidy in the same clothes from earlier. She smiles a bit before speaking.] “I need to talk to you about what happened earlier.” [Kraven looks her with regretfulness in his eyes. No, not the incident, for opening his door. He shakes his head in displeasures and lets her in as the scene slowly fades to black.] STATIC
