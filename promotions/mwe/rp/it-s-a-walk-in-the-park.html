It's a Walk in the Park; Jason Fear RP

Sep 28 2008

Jason Fear

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
*The lights shine from the roof like the sun on a grateful day. The seats circle all around like the numbers on a clock. In the center you could view the dedicated employees doing what they do best, build. The battle ground is set and the construction workers head out to help the others preparing what would be the entrée to the next Monday Night Mayhem. The view point returns to the location in where the seats on the second deck where. It aim directly at section 108 are. From the wired cameras perspective the seats are dull color and made out of cushion. The camera moves to the left direction quite fast. It was so fast it went around the whole arena three times in just 20 seconds. It suddenly stopped on section 98. Coming out of the entrance was Jason Fear with his Tag Team Championship on his shoulder, popcorn on his left hand and a large soda on his right hand. He had a casual attire which included He walked up the steps to take a seat. He places his soda on the cup holder then grabs a handful of popcorn and stuffed it in his mouth. While he’s biting he notices the camera aim at him.* Fear: Oh shit! What’s up folks? I don’t think this is the best time for me to deliver but whatever. I’ll start. *Fear finishes eating the popcorn in his mouth and takes a drink of soda. He takes a tissue out of his pocket and wipes his mouth with it.* Fear: So here we are in Charleston Civic Center. This is where Monday Night Mayhem will be presented this week. Now as for the fans with tickets on hand I am going to give you small information on this place Mayhem is going to home for a night. *Fear smiles and takes out a white Xerox paper with written words on it and also he takes out some eye glasses. (He never wears eyeglasses)* Fear: This Coliseum open its doors in the year 1959 which was the same you’re my mom was born. It was then renovated in the five years later in 1964 and then again in 1980. It holds up to 13,000 customers which are going to be present this Monday. This place also contains three different sections in one department. The Civil Center Coliseum, the little theater and the Grand Hall. It’s located on 200 Civic Center Drive Charleston, West Virginia. It has had many good events through the years like Presidential debates, basket tournaments and graduation ceremonies but it hasn’t had a great event like Project Revolution against Rated X this Monday. *Fear Folds the paper and places it back on his pocket* Fear: Rated X is considered the greatest faction in the history of MWE and AEW. I would agree to that statement because this faction has had the best wrestlers in the industry team up as one. But to tell you the truth I really doubt that this statement is going to stand as a fact for long. Rated X was a group of young up and comers just like Project Revolution who had the feel that they could become great images if they worked together as a team. Well their goal became true. Now both Brian Michaels and D.C. are former World Champions. This is to say in the pace that we’re going sooner than later we will become greast images ourselves. B.G. Mancini found potential in me so we decided that teaming up was the best thing that could happen to me. Well look at me now. A world champion and also sharing with him the Wrestler of the Week award. So As I thank B.G., D.C. you should thank Brian Michaels instead of trying to kill him! *Fear takes a sip of his large soda and takes a deep breath* Fear: Why would you want to kill Mr.MWE? You were a fucking manager for god sakes and now you’re getting paid big bucks by his own dad and you want to kill him. All I know is that you’re still that troubled little boy from California. You haven’t gown yet to take matters into your own hands. Lindsey told me that she decides when you two do sex. Come on bro your like 23 year old and you can control your own woman. She also told me that you have to win your match are she won’t sleep in your bed in the night. You’re getting handled by everyone who surrounds you. That’s the reason you’re in prison half your life. Worst of all you will be getting man handled in the ring on Monday by me. Former World Champion, Former X-TV champion, and Former Tag Team Champion and still you don’t feel inspired to leave the bad influences and do what you do best which is wrestling. Any ways, Rated X has had other pieces that have created the puzzle to its history like Devlin Sharpe a guy who I have decimated more than once, Windy Bridges who is a disgrace to the MWE nation and the one and only Downward Spiral. Those guys were like fill-ins to me. They were just there to have their name under Rated X. In Project Revolution we are all a team and no one is left out. *Fear takes another handful of popcorn and chewed like a pig will sip soda at the same time* Fear: That popcorn is pretty good I should say. Fortunate does who have their reserved seats for Monday. MWE is what it iis now because of one man, Brian Micheals. This man holds the record for the most MWE World Championship reins and is the co-founder/creator of Rated X. This man I have the most respect for what he’s done by himself. He has treated people like his slaves (D.C.) because his daddy owns the business, He has cheated his way to World Championship victories and he survived a death defying crash. Must you say that that’s a resume to be proud for? The greatest thing of all is that he likes to be called the “Career Stopper”. That nickname doesn’t match with Michaels. D.C. is do one who’s about to stop Brian Michaels Career. Brian Michaels you are in my book a “Has Been” wrestler. You’re explicitly not the same wrestler as you were before. Not quite as dominant and aggressive as you were. Where is the Bad Ass in you Brian? You were a Legend and now you have become a failure. You’re trying to be all nice and kitty with the fans but it’s not working for you Brian. You’re not the crowd favorite. D.C. is running over you like a monster truck. R.I.P. Brian Michaels. *Fear laughs at himself imagining Brian Michaels on his knees begging him to stop. All bloody and sticky almost about to have a seizure* Fear: I have an image on how the results in the first twenty minutes of Mayhem are going to be. Project Revolution going easy on the Sons of bitches, then Rated X starts beating on us and they get the fans pumped up. But there’s still that grudge between the two so sadly enough we take advantage of their stupid maneuvers and we raise our hands up high as we beat the Greatest faction in their own game. See last Thursday I took a girl I had just met called Johanna on a date to the Wax Museum in New York City. We observed many featured structures of famous people like Nicholas Cage, Julia Roberts and David Wright. Then down the hall I saw a logo that read “Mayhem Wrestling Entertainment Display. Johanna was looking at her favorite singer Chris Cornell and told her to wait a minute. As I arrived to the location in had five MWE Wrestlers on display. Brian Michaels, David Whitman, Sonny Williams, D.C. and Tim Fitzgerald. That’s when I thought to myself it would be great for Rated X to return and versus Project Revolution. I quickly informed Richard about this idea and he felt like that would draw many audiences. So He made you assholes team up. You might ask yourselves why am I here and that is a good question. This is a habit I have that when I arrive to a new stare for Mayhem I like to visit the place first before making my appearance. I really don’t know why but it has worked for the last for weeks. *Fear’s cellphone rings* Fear: Oh it’s Callaway. Time to train in the gym. Gotta Go. *Fear stands and drops his popcorns. He shrugs and leaves.*
