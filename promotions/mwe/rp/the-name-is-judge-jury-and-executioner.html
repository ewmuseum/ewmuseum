The name is Judge, Jury and Executioner; A JJ E RP - you know my work

Sep 12 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Prologue: A shadowy, dank room. There is a shadow sitting against the wall that can barely be made out. Voice: “You think you are funny but there will be nothing funny about what will be happening to you” Fade. *** Voice: “Will the court please rise?” The scene opens to a courtroom. Standing by each bench is a man, one wears a grey suit, one a black one. A judge dressed in full attire, enters from a door opposite them. He takes a seat and nods at both men to sit down. Judge: “This is the trial of a James Sync. Mr Sync you are charged with multiple crimes to music and wrestling as well as two counts of absenteeism, how do you plead?” Lawyer 1: “Erm sir, my client is not present at this time. I cannot vouch for his whereabouts at this time.” Judge: “Very well.....he knocks the mallet......Mr Sync is hereby sentenced to face Judge, Jury and Executioner at MWE Turmoil next weekend....where his fate will be confirmed......trial suspended....” Lawyer 1: “But your honour, I -” The judge knocks his mallet down again, more violently this time. He goes to speak but smiles politely instead. Out of nowhere a funeral dirge begins to play and the room goes dark for a minute. When the lights come back up, there is a giant of a man standing in front of the judge. The small crowd gasps in awe. He wears black from head to toe, complete with a black leather mask that obscures his pale face and greasy, straggly long shoulder length hair. In his left hand, he holds a short, thick rope that is tied in a small loop at the end. The giant walks forward. As he reaches the door, a tall but gangly thin man with long bleach blonde hair crashes through them. He has a cowboy hat, large sunglasses and going by the fact that he cannot walk straight, is quite inebriated. In his right hand, he clutches a Guitar Hero Stratocaster, which he raises with a goofy smile on his face. Man: “Danbury, Nantucket, I THANK YOOOOU!!!!” The giant man in front of him grabs the Stratocaster with a gloved right hand and in one fluid motion, smashes it over the head of the wannabe rocker. He looks scornfully at the unconscious victim as he surveys the damage for a few seconds. He then kneels down and begins to tie the rope as a noose around the neck of the man, who shows barely any signs of life. He stands again, rope in hand and shakes his head. JJ: “Mr Sync, your luck has just run out. There will be no one to cease your damnation.” JJ steps over the prone body and walks away, dragging the prone body behind him with ease. JJ: “At Turmoil, your MWE dream ends and the reigning nightmare of JJ Executioner begins. Enjoy your last hours.”
