Burn Milenko, Burn!!!; A JJ Executioner RP

Dec 22 2008

JJ Executioner

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Off the back of his third place elimination at the hands of Ace Cannon in the steel cage main event of Genesis, JJ Executioner ought to be satisfied with his efforts. A deep voice plays the omnipotent narrator as a camera follows JJ backstage. “I walked through two men to get to that spot.” But satisfaction is not the feeling that overwhelms him as he lies down in his locker room following the show. “I saw off two others in the cage match too. I even made that insufferable son of a bitch Vega Knight tap out!” No, JJ is angry. Angry at losing the spot that he believes was rightfully his. “Ace Cannon didn’t even deserve his place in the main event. But he got it because he whined his way into the affections of our General Manager. I should have been the one to face DC and I would have done a much better job than Ace fucking Cannon!” As he lies down, JJ’s thoughts pass over his time so far in MWE. He ended James Sync’s tenure in the company and had a major part in the end of James Sullivan too. He encountered one nemesis in the shape of “Supernova” Vega Knight but failed to pin him on three occasions, including two matches where the MWE X TV title was on the line. While JJ made an impact in those matches, it will not be what he is remembered for in the MWE. “When it is time for careers to be ended, I am the one that is turned too. But this…..this is special.” That moment is still to come because JJ Executioner will be remembered as the man who faced Milenko to decide the Havoc champion in the MWE’s first ever Inferno match. “The very definition of a match that threatens careers and lives because of the risk and the sheer volatility of the elements involved.” JJ is sat back up now. His hands are clasped together and he hunches forward. “The very possibility of being scarred permanently makes this treacherous to your health but no other match combines so many dangerous factors.” Milenko is a man with a reputation for excelling in these types of matches. “The crown freak Milenko may be many things but he has a hardcore pedigree that will come to the fore in matches like this.” But the Inferno match is so, so different to a normal hardcore war. JJ releases his hands to show a lighter, which he clicks. The small flame wisps from side to side, dancing in the darkness. “Unfortunately Milenko’s luck has run out. I could not have chosen a better match than this. It’s fair to say that I am looking forward to it. It will be a match where the brutality will be on a level not yet seen in the MWE, only to be matched by the pain and damage that will be incurred.” Matches with no rules where blood will be guaranteed to flow freely. And someone will be burnt, permanently damaged, potentially scarred beyond recognition. “The rules that protected Vega Knight for so long up until now will not be in effect. Knowing it is all legal will be what helps me burn Milenko and bring me the Havoc title.” He has made his career in beating those in matches where the rules don’t matter. From WZCW to EW to MWE, Milenko always steals the show in hardcore matches. “Did you think I wouldn’t know about your past? I know you followed your step brother Alex Bowen from company to company, facing him countless times. And whether it was in a Juggalo Death Match in WZCW or in the King of the Death Match tournament in AEW, you always left your mark on him.” It was during a five man match on the special Draft episode of Monday Night Mayhem that JJ and Milenko first faced off. And JJ did something that even Milenko was unable to do. “I destroyed Alex Bowen. He will not be coming back to the MWE any time soon.” JJ shuffles in his spot and rubs his chin “That must rankle with you Milenko" *JJ laughs* "to accomplish something in seconds that you failed to do for months! That must be the one thing that keeps you awake at night.” The encounters between the two have been hard hitting and intense as would be expected. But nobody will expect what is going to happen at Adrenaline Rush when they face off, live on pay-per-view. “Milenko can build himself up all that he wants. But when I am done with him in he won’t be any thing more significant than a pile of ash and dust. Over it will stand JJ Executioner, the first MWE Havoc champion.” The lighter drops to the floor as JJ walks out. The flame never dies as the camera fades.
