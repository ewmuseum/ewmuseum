Il tuo nuovo campione del mondo B.G. Mancini; B.G. Mancini RP #2

Aug 8 2008

B.G. Mancini

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
B.G. and Mike walk back into his office and take of their coats before they sit down at B.G.'s desk before Mike starts talking. Mike OK you heard your Uncle he wants you to win the MWE world title not only for yourself but for The Family. If that doesn't happen then he's going to be very angry. B.G. OK then lets get started who's next in that stack you got there. Mike picks up the next folder and reads through it before he starts talking. Mike Next up is a name you are going to find familiar, it's Brian Michaels. He's from right here in NYC. He moved when his girlfriend died back in '99 and before he started wrestling he started Boxing and MMA so he is not someone you want to go toe to toe with. He leafs through the folder until he finds what he's looking for and then he starts talking again. Mike He's held the MWE World title 3 times so he knows what it's like. I'm sure he would like nothing more than to hold it a fourth time. He was also the XCW Intercontinental Champion before he vacated that title. B.G. That's all well and good but does he have any weaknesses that I can exploit in the ring? Anything at all that would give me the edge? Mike looks through the file some more before he looks back up at B.G. Mike It says here that he hates losing. I don't know how you can use that ion the match but if He doesn't win you can use it after the match. B.G. gets a disgusted look on his face when he hears that last bit. B.G. That's fantastic, who's next? Mike Next up we have David Whitman. He'd from Phoenix, Arizona and was trained by his cousin and his cousin's best friend. He was the first ever MWE World Champion so you know he's someone good He leafs through the packet until he finds what he's looking for. Mike Here's something that's gonna help you it says here that he's fast and aerially adept so what you need to do is use your power to keep him grounded. If you wear him down you can do whatever you want to him. Mike outs down the Folder with Whitman's info and picks up the next one in line. Mike Up next we have Devlin Sharpe. The only info we have on him is that he used to be in the old MWE and while he was there he held the Tag titles, US title and the X Title. All he needs to get is the World title and he will have held every title this company has to offer. You need to be careful because this guy is hungry for the one title he hasn't held yet. B.G. That's all you got on that guy? I guess someone who knows more about him will have to take care of this guy for me. Who's next? Mike picks up the second to last folder and leafs through it quickly before he starts talking. Mike Up next is Alejandro Rodriguez. He hails from Mexico City. Not much is known about where he trained or who trained him except for the fact that he is a Luchadore. He has held all the titles in MWE except for the tag titles but I'm sure he would love to be world Champ a second time. B.G. All this is well and good but what are his weaknesses? I need stuff I can use against these guys in the ring. Mike We know he's a Luchadore an that all Luchadores rely on their speed so what you need to do is wear him down with your power. Just like with the other guy if you wear him down you can do what you want with him. B.G. leans back and starts thinking as Mike grabs the last folder from the pile and leafs through it. Mike Last but definatly not least is Sonny Williams. He was trained by one of the greats in this business The Rock. He has held the MWE World Championship. He is a 2 time MWE United States Champion. HE has held the NWF World Championship and the EW Internet Championship. Before B.G. can say anything Mike cuts him off and starts talking again. Mike As for his weaknesses he is no good in Submission matches. Neither are you so you 2 are very evenly matched with Sonny getting a 14 LB weight advantage. Mike puts down the last folder and leans back in his chair as B.G. starts to talk. B.G. So that's all nine of my opponents huh? None of them can beat me, they all have weaknesses I can exploit. I will win this match and I will become the new MWE World Champion. Now lets go out on the floor the customers customers think it's OK to play grab ass with the girls if I'm not out there. B.G. and Mike get up and walk out the office door as the camera focuses on the stack of folders before fading to black.
