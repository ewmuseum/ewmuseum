--The Camera zooms high in the view of Weber’s Inn. A four star hotel located on 3050 Jackson Ave, Ann Arbor, MI 48103. The Camera zooms back down to the entrance of Weber’s Inn as Jason Fear Walked out. He’s wearing Yves Saint Laurent 2239 sunglasses with a tight red shirt. He was also wearing black Adidas sweatpants and Nike trainers. He looked at his Dolce & Gabbana Rhythm men’s watch and runs to Bally Total Fitness which was the nearest Fitness area there. He walks in and shows his Identification Card. He walks down the hall to a running machine and formats speed to 3.5 to start off.-- Fear: Aeron, Aeron, Aeron. Man you can brag all you want about beating me but we all know that was a total fluke. I could of single handedly destroy you. I know this was all planed by you and Richard. For some reason Richard hates my attitude and tries to place an obstacle in my path to see me fail. That is the reason in which he placed me in this fight. But no one could stop Fear. Not even you Aeron. And you can f**k the freak show Jekyl and baby Gus. They could both kiss and then wipe my ass. You think that one match could stop me from getting in your way well then you must be fucking stupid.. --He speeds the running machine up to 4.5 speed. Then he picks up his Poland Spring water bottle of the running machine’s cup holder and takes three gulps. He places the water bottle back and speeds it up to 5.0 speed.-- Fear: I guess you still don’t know me. Hey Aeron have you ever met my friend sledge. He’s dying to meet you. I'm gonna fuck your face up with my sledge hammer so I can find a reason for you to be wear your stupid make up. It makes you look fucking ridiculous. Oh yeah Thanks I know I should be a Grammy winner by now. You saw how I made out with Veronica. You should try it one day. Anyways back to wear I left of. You don’t intimidate me. You were talking about my mother and…. --Fear looks out the window in front of him while running on the machine. He picks up his water bottle again and drinks it all. He looks at this woman with her child holding her hand. He looks back at the camera.-- Fear: Dude I rather have no mom than having a beast taking care of me. Aeron I ain’t asking for sympathy I’m asking for respect which you don’t have. Fuck your mother I don’t give a shit. Oh and that bitch you called your father figure. He’s the one who fucked you up in the first place. He made you what you are today. I mean look at your self. Homeless jerk. What’s even worst was that you had to hire a guy to play as your fan to show off that you have a better life. Well guess what ladybug you ain’t fooling no one with that filthy bullshit. If you think I’m just a fly in your field take me on next week. --Fear stops the machine and steps off. He grabs a towels and wipes his sweaty face and walks up to the weight seat. He picks up two 50lbs arm weights and sat down.-- Fear: Aeron this is what I do to hurt my victims instead of eating lunch with the camera man. That’s the reason you could only win 70% of your matches. This time I’m working harder to let neither you nor Richard escape me. Richard favors you as you see he awarded you for screwing me up with the Wrestler of the week. But he ain’t screwing me this time. Brian Michaels as his son should stick some sense into his old man’s head. But then again he’s also a punk. Aeron just beware I have a force behind me as you have Richard behind you. His name is B.G. I’m coming to get you. Trust me I know your gonna try to hide behind the boss. But then you’re putting his life on the line too. I’ll F-5 Richy and break his spine and you, you Aeron have yours waiting for you. --Fear stands up and places the weights on the floor. He walks up to a lady who’s working out. She was wear a hot pink sweat top and sweat shorts. She was blond with blue eyes.-- Fear: Hey girl what’s your name? Lisa: My name is Lisa Crandall. I’ve seen you before. Fear: Maybe on TV? Lisa: Yeah what’s your name? Fear: Jason. Maybe you saw me wrestling on TV. Lisa: Oh I don’t watch wrestling but my little brother does. Fear: Oh that’s a good thing. Lisa: Xavier Stone is hot. Fear: What do you think about me? Lisa: Umm. Yeah Fear: Can I have your number? Lisa: um ok. It’s [detail withheld]. --Fear looks at the camera with his thumbs up. He takes out his I phone 3G and marks down the number.-- Fear: alright babes call you tonight. --He walks away as he is victorious.-- Fear: See Aeron that’s how I roll. Aeron you think it’s bad luck that I didn’t pass away in that car crash was unfortunate well me too. I don’t need to live this life filled with shit. The only reason I live is to punish assholes like you. Aeron it’s all part of the plan. You’ll see, ill show ya. When you screw me like you did on Mayhem ain’t no one going to stop me from making your life a living hell. Let's make a deal hre. How about if I win this coming Monday you will have to defend your X-TV title the Monday after that. If I lose I won't mess with you anymore. I am going to leave you with the thought in your head. Don’t Fear me im not that bad. Peace I got a girl to b**g. --He walks out the Fitness area and leaves.--
