The pain

Sep 14 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The camera comes through an open door to a drab looking white padded room. Rust stains from the metal backing runs down the pads. The room has been out of commission for almost ten years. Alex spent some time in this old room when It was still apart of a boy's home/ mental health hospital. He's in a town about thirty-five miles from his old home of Montpelier Ohio. The camera turns down from looking at the walls of the room and looks at Alex; he is rocking back and fourth humming something. We can't here it but the camera man's assistant moves forward and puts his big mic closer to Alex. We can here it a famous song by Jonny Cash, the ring of fire. Alex- I.... fell....into a burning... ring of fire... Alex continues to rock to and fro, more violently with every rock. After the rocking becomes too much, he flips over and lands on his back. He stares up with a grimace, as the camera moves into place. Alex- And it burns, burns, burns..... The ring of fire. Alex gets to his kneeʼs and moves to a wall and hits it a couple times with a closed fist, then with an open palm or two. He tries to hit it again but ends up bursting out in tears and slides down the wall and slumps into a corner. Alex- Dad why did you have to do this to me, six days in here after just turning thirteen. What did I do to deserve this? Six days?! I was only thirteen, God damn it, what did I do to you to piss you off so much A large shadow is seen on the wall coming from the doorway, Its Alex's brother and tag team partner David. He comes into the room and sets down next to his brother and puts his arm around his shoulder. David- Dude its all right, what the hell are you doing here anyways? I thought you wanted this damn place torn down. Alex- I know I did, but this was the place ware my hate for the man all started. The hate that still fills me to this day. The hate thatʼs going to help up win the mwe tag team titles. Alex looks down at the pads on the floor; he starts to trace imaginary circles in the pad with one of his fingers. Alex- For six days I sat in this very cell, singing that very song you herd me sing. I listened to nut jobs screaming that shit was crawling over them, or something was trying to eat me. Then you had the idiots coming through on there way down the halls beating on my door. I had no clue why my dad left me here. Six days later my mom came in our old pickup truck and got me. Apparently my father tried to lye to my mom and said I ran away! David- I always hated that fucking dick. Alex- I know we both did didn't we? I' m sick of this place lets get out of here; I've got something to talk to you about. Alex and David get up and start out of the room, Alex stays back and spits on the walls of one side of the room and whispers, Alex- Peace of shit. They continue there decent down the hall until the reach a large steel door with a turn style handle. David is first and reaches out and with a screech the door opens and light floods in the hallway. They continue there walk down a cracked and weed overridden walkway. They make there way down to an old gravel parking lot Alex falls to his knees and begins to punch the ground below him. *flash back* A large man with a bit of a beard is getting out of a small old beat down car. Beer cans and chip bags fall out of the side of the car and roll down the driveway. He gets to the side of the car and opens up the door; a small Alex is setting in the passengerʼs seat. The man presumably his father grabs Alex by a black coat he is wearing, and tryʼs to jerk him out of the car but Alex grabs the sides of the car and wont let go. Y oung Alex- No dad don't do this to me! Father- Just get out of the fucking car god damn it!! Young Alex- What did I do wrong? The man strikes Alex behind his head with a fearsome blow. Alex is instantly knocked out. He grabs him before he drops to the ground and heaves him up on his shoulder; He walks up a clean walkway with stones and flowers on the side of it and opens up a door on the side of the hospital. He walks into the hallway and opens up a door to a waiting room. He walks into it and puts Alex down into a chair and walks up to a service desk with a rather large woman behind it. Woman- Whatʼs wrong with him? Man- I'm not to sure he just wouldn't wake up, I'd like to put him here for a lengthy stay, his anger just has the whole family in an uproar. His mom and me our just at our whites end and need a break. Woman- Well sir this isn't a baby sitting facility, but I think we can help him a bit. The man has a smile on his face and walks over to Alex and gives him a fake kiss on the cheek and taps him on the shoulder and whispers something to him. *End flashback* The flashback ends and we come back to Alex Bowen thrashing the ground with a fury of blows, David moves to him and picks him up in a bear hug. Alex still continues to thrash about. David- ALEX! END THIS NOW! HE'S DEAD. Alex- I want to kill him David, He put me through to much, and He did it to you, mom, and Cory. I need it David. Ware is he! David- HE'S FUCKING DEAD ALEX! SNAP THE HELL OUT OF IT! Alex- I don't care David, I need this! David puts down Alex and delivers a massive backhand slap to Alex. He's shocked but seems to come back to his senses. He looks down to his hand, its a crimson red color with small rocks and dirt dug into it. He wipes it on his jeans and they continue to walk in the old driveway. David- We mask the pain at most times, but all in all we take it out in the ring boys. The Hollywood hooligans and project rev have NO CHANCE. I beat down 2 men with my brother Alex in apwc that were bigger than all four of our opponents. What makes them think they can put us in more pain then we already are? I've busted myself open begging for my last breath. To put the suffering to an end. Thatʼs over now; we all have a purpose in life. And mine is to cleanse Mwe of the scourge known as the whole roster. No one is safe from me except for my brother. People might think the bigger the man the harder the fall. But I'll tear you apart on my stomach as much as I do on two legs.D avid looks at Alex who is nursing his slowly swelling hand and pats him on the back. He grabs him by his shoulders and pulls him to his side. David- We'll put some ice on that when we get home. Alex- I'm fine David, I've been through worse in my times. This is nothing but a parting memory of the pain Iʼve been in for a long time.... Alex looks down at the ground then moves away from David. Alex- For almost twenty-three years my father never approved of anything we ever did. Thats a long as time for a person to hole a grudge on someone. Let alone his own flesh and blood. Like David all the shit I put myself through is the for the hope that it will all just end. Maybe then he'll be looking up from hell and finally sigh out a good relief for himself knowing I'm dead. But until this pain passes. My opponents will be the ones hurting. I'm done playing the funny guy. I loved making people laugh, but that side of me is dead, long gone never to return. Mwe put something into me that EW and Apwc couldn't do. They put the hate back in my life. The people that like to make jokes on others like the hooligans, to be quite frank make me fucking sick. IT seems like the wrestling community as a whole has lost the spark, condoning the acts of sinc mercer and the hooligans. Who bash there opponents with words, not with actions. Alex looks back at David and gives him a bit of a grunt and points out a curb and they go and set on it. Alex- And this Project Revolution? Who the fuck do they think they are?! B.g and whoever the fuck he could find setting in his bar until it closed I guess. Your named project revolution, but what is your revolution? We've already made ours very very clear boys. And you and the hooligans will be the second group on our way to that goal. Sinc mercer was my first target; he might have won our match. But he's learned respect for the burning legion. I gave him more than a beating; I let him out of that match with his life. You can say all you want, but I held back in that match, because I have bigger plans as of right now then to fuck with him and James. Itʼs a plan for the whole mwe. Itʼs a plan thatʼs going to end all this bullshit, all of this shit. I'm going to have a bounty on my head before itʼs over with. Because all of Mwe is included, from the top of the show to the bottom. You choose to fuck with us like Sinc mercer and these two tag teams Mwe and you shall be dealt with in a swift quick action. I'm not fucking around anymore people; I'm just that full of it. David points at the camera. David- So take those words to heart boys, you can be as nice to us as you please. But never cross the Burning Legion. Because the last thing you will see on your way out of Mwe on a stretcher will be myself and Alex. The proof will be shown at this pay per view when were Alex reaches in his pocket with his not swollen hand and starts to pull something out, but the camera slowly fades away before we can see what it is.
