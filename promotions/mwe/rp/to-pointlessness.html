... To Pointlessness; Another David Whitman RP

Sep 25 2008

David Whitman

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The scene slowly opens up. As it comes into focus we see David Whitman, James Whitman, Eddie Sanchez, Scott Jones and Thomas Williams all sitting around a table, wearing small lavalier (clip-on) mics. They all have a couple of beers in front of them. David has a black eye, as well as a bandage on his face and some on his shoulder. David: I still can’t believe you did that. I swear, you just give me ONE more reason to kick your @$$, I won’t hesitate. Eddie: DUDE! I said I was SORRY! I had face paint on! I couldn't SEE! Look, Thomas told me to get the car, and then when we were driving and finally found you, he- Thomas: HEY! Let’s not point fingers here, alright? It’s just- wait, are we rolling? A hand doing the “thumbs-up” motion appears on the screen, then disappears. David: Son of a- Well, HI! You all may know me as former MWE World champion, David Whitman. I’m sitting here with my cousin, James Whitman… James raises his hand and smiles exaggeratedly. David: And his fellow stable mates in the JESTers; Eddie Sanchez, Scott Jones and Thomas Williams. Eddie does a thumbs-up, Scott does a double thumbs-up, and Thomas slaps the camera. All: DUDE! Thomas: Sorry! I was going for a high five… David sighs. David: Anyway, I’m proud to present to you MWE viewers with this new, innovative, cutting-edge, high-tech show… The Pointless Preview! The title card appears on screen. David: Godd@*&it! Who drew on the title card?! Scott: You know you all love my shark, b*tches. Thomas: I did NOT know you could draw that well. My aliens suck in comparison. James: Yeah, they do. Thomas: Shut up, James. That 7-up guy or whatever it is you were trying to pull off; it ain’t workin’. Eddie: Haha. James: Whoa! Hey, Eddie, did you draw that- that guy with the thing? Eddie: Yeah. That’s a parrot coming out of the glass, and I drew a pencil so it looks like I drew myself drawing that. They all start clapping. James: Uhm, by the way… David, what’s with the… thing… the "featuring" thing…? David: What? I was going for a BAM effect. What? You guys don’t like it? Eddie: Dude, it looks weird. The size, and with the font… Scott: And the colors?! It looks like a Christmas decoration gone horribly wrong! David: Look- STOP IT! Hey! We’ve already wasted like 10 minutes with this crap. James: FINE. The title card fades away as we see the guys again. Every time someone talks, the camera focuses on them. David: Alright, so uh, this is the Pointless Preview, where we’re basically gonna talk a bit about my upcoming match. Scott: Listen though; first off I wanna say Covell is a freaking GENIUS. David: WHAT?! Scott: Project Revolution versus Rated X? Instant classic. Eddie: OOOOH SNAP! Best opener ever! Thomas: Hey, guys, can we talk about the match DAVID’s gonna be in? Eddie: Spoilsport. James: Yeah man, Jekyl VS David Whitman. Your title shot at Wrestle-Wars on the line. What are your thoughts on the match-up? The camera starts with a far shot of David, slowly moving into a close-up. David: Well, obviously, this is a pretty important match for me. If I win, you know, I’ll get a chance to get the title back. The problem is, Jekyl’s improved even more apparently, as his match against Mercier proves. And of course, he’s still prone to his mind games, so I’ll have to watch out for that. But I’m feeling fairly confident that I have a chance at winning this. And the harder he tries at stopping me the harder I’ll work towards that pin. In the end, I guess, the fans’ll be the real winners. Eddie: Not cliché in the least bit. David: What th- Scott: MOVING ON! Have you seen Jekyl’s promo? David: Yes. James: Any thoughts? David: Well, apparently, Jekyl thinks I’m an actor. James: Uh… you ARE. David: Well YEAH, I AM, but I mean, he thinks I’m JUST an actor. That I can’t FIGHT. Thomas: That’s INSANE! You’re not JUST an actor! You write, too! David: Well yeah bu- Scott: And his directing skills are pretty good. Eddie: Oh, no doubt. David: WAIT! Do you guys ALL feel that way?! That I can’t fight?! James: Come on, man. Don’t let this guy get to you. You’ve been undefeated since your return and you’ve held the title since your debut… Way I see it, if your acting is good enough to defeat him in a wrestling match, then- that’s insane. David: Yeah, but I HAVEN’T really beaten him yet. James: Well then, you’ll just have to do that, won’t you? David: Yep. David takes a big swig from his beer. The camera cuts to Thomas, who is flipping through a book. Thomas: Did you guys know that West Virginia has a city named Thomas?! That’s AWESOME! James: What the hell are you reading? Thomas: “All about Virginia”. Eddie: Is- Is that right? David: HEY! Eddie: Oh, right, sorry. Preview, match, got it. James: Wait, that West Virginia talk reminds me, this beer is GREAT! Where’s it from? Thomas grabs his beer and holds it up while talking back. Thomas: Oh, you mean this Pale Ale, which is brewed by the Mountaineer Brewing Company right here in West Virginia?! The camera cuts to David, who has a very serious look on his face. David: Seriously? James: Hey, if you can get free beer for a little name-dropping, then why not? Besides, it’s really good. James smiles at the camera. David: Ok ok ENOUGH! Let’s do this thing RIGHT! No more diversions! Others: Fiiiine. David: Ok. So… James: What’s your exact plan for taking out Jekyl? David: I can’t say that, what if he’s watching? Eddie: He probably won’t watch this. David: Roll the clip. QUOTE Jekyl: …and you see after watching all you promo’s… James: Ok, point. But speaking of Jekyl’s last promo, you got anything else you wanna say about it? David: Well, I mean he basically drew on my picture in the jet, said Top Rope Tavern sounds like a gay bar which to me makes about as much sense as calling his “Smiley” finisher a “brainbuster on the gay”, and said I can’t fight. Eddie: Brainbuster on the gay… that’s good. David: What he said, though… it’s got me confused. Let’s compare clips for a moment, shall we? QUOTE Jekyl: what was it again? Ah yes, the “Top Rope Tavern”... sounds like a gay bar to me.... all I ever see in that bar on your show is men with cowboy hats, chaps and.... I’m not even going to continue that sentence.. David: Okay, okay… Now roll the clip from the first episode of TRT. QUOTE Thomas: Sweeeeet. Guy: Speaking of sweet- They all look at the door. The girl walks in and sits in front of James, right next to David. She points at the tequila in front of David. Girl: Whose leg do I have to hump to get some of that tequila you’ve been drinking? David: Hm. And that clip from episode three? QUOTE Kate pulls out her wallet and takes out a picture. Guy: … Kate: Ok, you're obviously surprised. Wait. David: Hm. Ok, so, what about that clip from episode Five? QUOTE James: Yeah, you heard me. You’ll get paid. Ok. See ya. James closes the phone. Eddie: So, how’s the business life? James: Meh. Why the hell you asking me, anyways? We own a frickin’ company together. Scott: Yeah, but come on, it’s not like owning a bar! James: We made a friggin movie. Owning a bar is way easier than that was. Thomas: Well, I bet it’s harder than, say, making a sitcom! James: I guess it’d depend what the sitcom was about. Scott: What about a sitcom ABOUT owning a bar?! James: OH… MY… GOD! ... What kind of an IDIOT comes up with an idea like that?! They all look around again, for no apparent reason. David looks confused. David: Ok, who got the wrong clip? Eddie: I like that clip… Eddie looks disappointed. David: Sigh. ANYWAY, you get the point; either he’s watching the wrong show or Mr. Jekyl needs a new pair of glasses. Scott: Isn’t that DOCTOR? David: What? Scott: Uh… Nevermind. James: Wait, okay, but what about episode three's title? David: Shhhh… Eddie: Oh, right! “GUY IS GAY!” David: Look, the POINT IS; Jekyl says he’s going to come out on top. But I think his version of coming out on top might be as twisted as his idea that the TRT is a gay bar, or his idea that the former world champ, David Whitman, is more of an actor than a wrestler. I am going to give Jekyl the match of his life on MNM. I am going to match his power; I am going to match his insanity. I am going to give it more than my all. I am going to make him pay for everything he’s put me through since knocking me into insanity a while back. And then, I am going to walk away the number one contender to the World Title. That’s all there is to it. David raises his bottle. David: To victory! They all raise their bottles. All: TO VICTORY! Eddie keeps his bottle in the air. Eddie: And may David become a man soon as well! The others snicker as the camera cuts to David, who has a very serious look on his face. The camera then abruptly cuts to the outro screen.
