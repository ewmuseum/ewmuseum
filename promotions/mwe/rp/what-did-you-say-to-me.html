What did you say to me?; Alejandro Rodriguez RP.

Oct 3 2008

Alejandro Rodriguez

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The scene opens at a car-dealership in Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania. The scene pans in on Alejandro Rodriguez who is talking to a car dealer. Alejandro is wearing his black A-Rod shirt, jeans, black mask and white trainers. Alejandro’s phone rings, he walks away a few yards from the dealer and he answers the phone. Alejandro: … hello. Stacy: Hey baby. Alejandro sighs. Alejandro: Hey, what’s up. Stacy: Where are you, what are you doing, you were gone when I woke up. Alejandro: That’s because you’re asleep till three o’ clock nowadays. Stacy: Ha-ha… So what are you doing? Alejandro looks at the dealer, who shrugs. Alejandro: I’m… buying a car. Stacy: Another one?! Alejandro, we talked about this. Alejandro: I’m not living on the streets anymore, Stacy... I’m earning 540,000 dollars a year, I’ve been busting my ass against Whitman and Williams every damn week, and now I’m giving myself a treat. Stacy: Wasn’t there a house show scheduled for today? Alejandro freezes. Alejandro: ...house show. Fuck. Alejandro hangs up and looks around. He sets his sights on a Mercedes-Benz SLR Coupé. He runs over and gets inside. Alejandro: I like this one, I’ll take it. Alejandro pulls out his check-book, writes something down and gives it to the dealer. Dealer: …but sir. Alejandro: You’ve seen my driving licence, you’ve seen my face beneath this mask, I’ve given you the money, now give me the keys to my car. The dealer sighs. 15 minutes later. Alejandro is queued up in traffic in his new Coupé. He is strumming the steering wheel of the car with his fingers. He revs up0 the engine and there Is still no sign of movement. Alejandro: All this horsepower and no-where to gallop. Alejandro cuts across a car and into a side-road. He races down that and pulls a sharp right at the end of that road. He rides down another road and turns right to meet the queued road and he pulls left and skips the entire queue as he heads on past the traffic lights towards the arena. Alejandro: Now, we’re talking. Alejandro pulls Into the car park a couple of minutes later, he locks his car and runs towards the arena with his gym bag. He arrives in front of the production team, sweating and heaving for breath. One of the production guys steps forward while looking at his clipboard. ProductionMember1: Ok, Alejandro... do you know your match for this week’s Monday Night Mayhem? Alejandro has his breath back. Alejandro: No. ProductionMember1: Ok, well… It’s against Jekyl, and it’s number one contenders match for the US Title so tonig-... Alejandro pauses. He turns to face the production crew angrily. He speaks slowly and loudly. Alejandro: WHAT DID YOU SAY TO ME!?! The production member backs away immediately. Alejandro storms off past him. Alejandro: US Title, what the fuck… Alejandro is walking towards the curtain. Production don’t know what he is doing and they panic. ProductionMember1: Where are you going!?! Alejandro: To blow off some steam. ProductionMember2: Play his music! Eye of the Storm by Bullet for My Valentine blares over the PA System as Alejandro Rodriguez walks out from behind the curtain to a horde of boo‘s from the audience. He still looks angry and he takes heavy strides towards the ring. He storms around the ring and grabs a microphone, he rolls into the ring and he walks into the centre. Usually he would wait for the crowd to calm down, but this time he does not. Alejandro: Shut… the… fuck… UP! The crowd are taken back and they boo as Alejandro begins to pace angrily. Alejandro: I came out here, because I just discovered… That my match this week on Monday Night Mayhem is against Jekyl, for the number one contenders spot to the US fucking Championship. It’s absolute BULLSHIT! Two weeks ago I beat the current World Champion Sonny Williams in a singles match, and now I’m CONTENDING for a second-rate title which I’ve held twice before? I’ve kept that belt to what it was and now I have to pick up the fucking pieces? The crowd don’t know how to take Alejandro’s anger, and they give mixed reactions. Alejandro: Is Richard Covell blind? What the hell is he thinking? Putting me against a fucking clown for CONTENDERSHIP to a title which I could win in my god-damn sleep. I BEAT the World Champion! Doesn’t that give me a title match? Doesn’t that mean Sonny is the paper fucking champ for going against jobbers every week in his stupid-ass invitational? He’s an absolute disgrace to this company, if you be the champ, you defend it against the worthy and you beat them, you suck up losses and walk back in stronger than ever. You win matches, against the right people and you set the right fucking example. The crowd are still clueless as to how to react. Alejandro: SO here’s a mother-fucking ultimatum Sonny Williams. Why don’t you have ME on your god-damn invitational next week, and then see how you fare against the Angel. You haven’t got one mother-fucking chance in hell and you know it. I know you’ll probably back away and beat some jobber next week, but just know Williams. Anytime, anyplace I’m ready for another chance. If I do it with the US Title then so be it, because Jekyl hasn’t got a chance in hell, Sinc Mercier hasn’t got a chance in hell and you haven’t got a chance in hell either, Sonny. The crowd boo Alejandro. Alejandro: Which brings me onto my opponent this week. The freak that has taken MWE by storm, please, what the hell has he done? Won a few matches, loose a World Championship match, and no apparently he’s the greatest thing since sliced bread. He’s nothing but a clown, and I don’t care if it’s for US Title contendership or not because it won’t affect your chances, I’m still going to pin you 1...2...3. You’ve out-stayed your welcome, Jekyl… It’s time for you to go, so I guess it’s up to me to escort you out. Because if it’s one thing I hate, it’s that no-one fucks with MWE and no-one makes MWE look bad, and you’re doing the later. Alejandro pauses and stops pacing, he seemingly has calmed down a bit now. Alejandro: Seeing as I’m pretty much guaranteed to win the number one contenders match this week, I think I should talk about my opponent for when after I‘ve beat the clown. Sinc Mercier, the cocky asshole with a mouth bigger than his brains. The man more useless than a chocolate fireguard. How he came to be US Champion, I have no fucking clue. But, I hate a bad champion. So I will take that Championship and I’ll build up it’s prestige, just like I did before. Because Sinc Mercier, Sonny Williams and Jekyl, they in time… will… LEARN... TO... FLY! Alejandro’s music comes back on and Alejandro leaves the ring to negative reactions from the crowd. He walk up the ramp and disappears backstage. Scene fades to black.
