Let The Cleansing Begin!; Chris England RP

Aug 14 2009

Chris England

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The scene opens up as a camera pans in on a park bench situated in the famous Grant Park, Chicago Illinois. It is a dark and cold night, the only light coming from the full moon in the sky. The darkness in the park somehow illuminates its beauty, the glistening moonlight bouncing of the trees. The moons reflection in the water fountains. As the camera gets closer to the park Bench a shadowy figure becomes visible. As the cameras continue to zoom in it becomes viable that this man is dressed in a black hoodie, the man has his hands tucked into the front pockets of the hoodie. He is wearing a grey beanie hat and his long hair is dangling freely down the sides of it. The man is wearing a pair of torn blue jeans and his face has five o’clock shadow stubble on it. Now the cameras are close enough its clear this man is Chris England. There is a dark and gloomy look on the face of the true English patriot, we haven’t seen this kind of look in England’s eyes for quite some time, he is sat bent oven on the edge of the bench his elbows now placed firmly on his knees and his chin resting on his fists. As the camera is now in full focus on England he looks up into the lens and begins to speak. Here I am sat here surrounded by the cold and darkness once more. The oh so familiar feelings of despair and anger boil deep down within my soul and the reason why Leon ‘The Virus’ Roberts. For the past two months Leon has stalked me in a mysterious disguise, trying to convince me to embrace the darkness within my soul and return to my evil ways. Two weeks ago I was stood in the ring at Monday Night Mayhem, about to announce my retirement from wrestling. Things have just been going from bad to worse for me lately and it seemed like the only viable option. But no Leon wouldn’t let that happen. Instead he had to announce himself to the world as my mystery stalker and tell me how he won’t let me retire. England straightens himself up from his hunched over position on the park bench, he leans forward his eyes connecting with the camera, there is a look of extreme darkness in his eyes. I look of grit and determination creeps into his face before it is swiftly replaced by a raised smile and a dark laugh starts to creep out, England begins to clap for a few seconds before speaking. Congratulations Leon, you have accomplished your mission, you have forced me into having to embrace the darkness that lies inside me once more. Only I’m not sure you quite understand what you have awoken my friend. You have refuelled my determination and given me a goal to work towards in this business, what happens this Monday is solely down to you! England reaches into his hoodie pocked and pulls out a packet of cigarettes, he reaches into his jeans pocket and pulls out a lighter, he slowly pulls out a cigarette from the box and lights it with the lighter before placing both the lighter and the cigarettes into his hoodie pocket. England takes a long cool drag on his cigarette before leaning into the camera and slowly exhaling the smoke out, through his nose. You see Leon you have gave me a new mission here in MWE. A mission to rid it of sad pathetic wastes of space like yourself. I’m sure your thinking brilliant I have done it; I’ve awoken the beast I wanted to. Eh EH! No chance fucko! See the beast has been awoken, but this time I will use my powers of darkness for the greater good! I will not do this for myself and I certainly won’t do it for the likes of you. No I do this so the long suffering fans of the MWE no longer have to put up with shit from guys like you! England Eyes are locked firmly on the camera and his face is still oozing the intensity of the last words he delivered, He takes a long deep drag on his cigarette calming himself down. He throws the cigarette to the ground and stubs it out with the sole of his boot. So my new mission begins this week, when I step into the ring with Tali Preston, Blight and Peter Kaymakian in a fatal four way match. Unfortunately for my opponents FATAL is exactly what it will be for them especially Peter K. England lets out a smirk as he remembers the wars he and peter have had in the past. He grins a bit feeling a new found confidence in his ability to take out the trash so to speak. For Chris Peter K is the main bit of trash he wants to take out. Not only taking out a man he can’t stand but delivering a clear message to Leon Roberts that he means business. A look of cool calmness sweeps across England’s face as he steadies himself before speaking. Peter and I are no strangers when it comes to in ring competition. I’ve said it before and I will say it again, Peter K has all the talent in the world, his in ring ability is right up there with the top guys in MWE, but his attitude will ALLWAYS hold him back! This Monday Peter I am going to make an example of you. This Monday your arrogance and self confidence will be worthless when you step into the squared circle with me. I am going to take whatever cocky, arrogant insults you throw my way, then I’m going to beat them down your scrawny little throat and choke you to death with them and I’m going to do this for the fans. You see Peter while your also busy blowing your own trumpet about how brilliant you are and how you’re going to take the whole world by storm and become a MWE mega star. All that yours truly and every single MWE fan is thinking, is when will this guy SHUT THE FUCK UP! Mark it down in your diary’s folks Monday the seventeenth of August Two-Thousand-Nine cause I guarantee you all, this WILL be the night The True English Patriot FORCES Peter K to shut his trap once and for all! England shoots a confident smirk into the camera. The confidence that has been lacking for quite some time seems to be flowing at full steam ahead once more. He takes a breath and strokes his chin before focusing back in on the camera. Now I’m fully aware that Peter K isn’t my only opponent in this match up. I also have to step into the ring with that Masked Freak Blight. Blight a man who prides himself on taking things to the extreme. A man who truly wants’ to be remembered as an innovator of violence. Now don’t get me wrong I have allot of admiration for a man that does whatever he can to kick things up a notch. But this Monday Blight you are simply going to find yourself in the wrong place at the wrong time. You see while you are busy looking for your opportunity to kick things up a notch and take it to the extreme, I will be methodically taking apart not only you but everyone in the match in a frenzy of controlled aggression. England smirks a confident smirk once more, he stretches his arms out at full stretch connects his fingers together and cracks his knuckles as some sort of sign that he means business. He then focuses his attention back to the camera. So that brings me to my final competitor in this contest, Tali Preston. A Psychotic Rock chick who dreams of being a Rock star. Hang on am I hallucinating or did I actually just say that? Me The True English Patriot in a Competitive match up with a chick? Is MWE struggling for ratings so much that they deem it necessary to appeal to wife beaters? Look I am many things but I will never ever lay my hands on a woman unless it is 100% the last available option. So my message to you Miss Preston is stay out of my way and you won’t get hurt. I urge you to keep out of my path, because this Monday all the pent up aggression that has built up over the past few months will be released. Now if you do however take a psychotic turn and crazily decide to attack me, then you will leave me with no option but to take your scantily clad ass and knock you the fuck out. But I’m sure if you’re smart it won’t come to that, cause I mean come on I’m six-foot-four, Two-Hundred and Eighty-Four Pounds of pure athlete, do you really want to anger a man that could squish you like a little bug? For your sake I hope the answer is no. England just sits there looking into the camera; the look on his face is a look that is almost pleading with Tali Preston to Stay out of his way. England stands up off the bench and spins around in a circle with his arms at full stretch as if pointing out around the dark and gloomy Grant Park. What you see around me is an incredible combination of Darkness and beauty. Two things that you will rarely use to compare something and two things that are somewhat a strange combination. But darkness and beauty is EXACTLY what I plan to deliver this Monday night. I plan to combine the forces of good and evil that lay within my soul to make a darkly beautiful combination. This Monday I will use my combined forces to begin my quest to rid MWE of the Pathetic Lowlifes that infest it. Mission objective number one: Commence the cleansing of MWE. The scene comes to a close as Chris England is seen walking off into the dark beauty of Grant Park, Chicago Illinois.
