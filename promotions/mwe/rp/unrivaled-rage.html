Unrivaled Rage

Jul 3 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
We see a figure in the shadows walking down the street seemingly determined to reach the building ahead of him, Her Majesty's Prison Holloway In London United Kingdom The figure steps under a street light momentarily and is revealed to the a muscular young man with long blonde hair, a lot had changed since he began his career 2 years ago, his experiences had shaped his body as in extreme ways, scars could be seen throughout his body, bruises from the various wrestling matches he had, cuts on his face, he had bulked up considerably during his time spent in the mental hospital, most noticeably however several wounds on his wrist from his failed suicide attempts However one thing that had not changed, the determination etched in every detail of his face, the fire in his eyes was burning with such ferocity that was unseen from him previously, his teeth were bared in rage, every so often his eye would twitch perhaps from anger perhaps from his past mental health issues The rage and determination emanating from this individual was second to none, his mouth wasn't moving but the expression on his face was obvious, a big fuck you to the world, this world that didn't believe in him, this world that believed he would amount to nothing, the bitterness had manifested itself in total unbridled rage which was kept inside him, his body wouldn't have you believe he had such rage however his eyes gave it all away, if you didn't notice the necklace he was wearing you would be hard pressed to recognise this guy as the “Jobber with an ego the size of a planet” that he was known to be throughout the latter half of his career. It was Devlin Sharpe, but not the same Devlin Sharpe everyone in MWE was used to, his body just gave that aura of rage and intensity never before seen from him. Devlin Sharpe: MWE will not know what hit it when “The One” returns He still had the same arrogant tone to his voice although it was peppered with rage and slightly fractured as he literally forced the words out of his mouth He continued towards the ominous building, he slowly entered the building, he looked down at the officer standing there and nodded at him, the officer sat up and motioned for him to follow him when they arrived at the cells he looked at his surroundings, darkness punctured by the light of the bulbs high in the air, he was then led to the cell he spent a year of his life in, he looked around, not much had changed, he sat down and brushed his hand against the wall and felt the bumpy texture as memories began flooding in Devlin Sharpe: Sure, I may have lost the tag match on monday night mayhem, sure I could blame it on the fact that it wasn't a one on one match, that I had an old guy that should've retired ages ago on my side? Or the fact that you teamed with a big mouth who's talking distracted me BUT did it ever occur to anyone, that I lost the match on purpose? You see Michaels had an undefeated streak which made him better than me, well I couldn't have that could I? Devlin had an evil grin on his face as he glanced at the metal bars of his cell, he was used to it, he had spent ages here which eventually began to amuse him Devlin Sharpe: And if there was another reason for me to lose on purpose? That little stipulation Eric Derf put on our match, I could have easily won my match and gotten the match type I wanted but I figured his match choice was better, a prison cell match! Devlin walks over to the prison cell bars and taps a melody on the prison bars as he looked outside the window which had bars over it as the moon shined brightly through it, the light bouncing off his hair Devlin Sharpe: I mean come on Eric, you're a rising star in the MWE sure but just by making this silly little mistake, you just proved that you are nothing but an over hyped rookie, you may win a million matches but if you make the same silly mistakes again and again, only when you stop making these mistakes, that is the only time you will beat me Devlin sits back down on the bench thinks for a moment then lies down and closes his eyes Devlin Sharpe: You clearly underestimate me Eric Derf it's obvious, you think you are in for an easy ride, you are used to the old me, who would just lie on the floor for you to pin me, sorry there derf but you missed your chance, you pissed off the wrong person at the wrong time, you brought back the main eventer, the devastator and know what? He's here to stay for the glory of it all and to kick your ass of course, but go ahead dude keep thinking you're going up against the old Devlin Sharpe but from my first move you will reallise your mistake, this is a whole different ball game and I am at an entirely new level... Devlin sat back up and put his hands in his pockets Devlin Sharpe: I know you have been improving rapidly, I am not disputing that however the thing is Eric Derf, how can I improve? Please tell me, how can you possibly improve on perfection Devlin smirks and claps before being serious again Devlin Sharpe: Everyone in the MWE treats me like a joke, a has been who has become nothing but a jobber, well this pay per view is going to change all that get ready because after the pay per view, the landscape of MWE will change FOREVER Devlin stands up and glides his fingers over the bars of his jail cell as the camera shifts showing Devlin behind bars if for just a moment before shifting again showing it was all just a joke Devlin Sharpe: You see Eric, its funny that you picked a prison cell match because for a few years after I killed my brother I spent some time in this very cell, so I know prison cells very well, this gives me an added advantage because I know how to use these bars to my benefit, they will help me in beating the shit out of you Devlin exited the prison cell and walked towards the exit, as he walked out of the door he had a flashback to the day he was released from prison, he shook his head and breath in the cold night chill, he walked down the road towards the Halifax bank, the road was empty as it was nearing morning now, a few cars past now again but generally all was quiet, when he reached the ATM, he reached his back pocket for his wallet, he took his card out and inserted it in to the ATM, he then casually entered his pin and tapped the button to show his balance Code: Your Current Balance is £764,528 Your Available Balance is £76,510 Would you like another service? Devlin pressed a button and went to another screen Code: Your Available Balance is £76,510 How Much would you like to withdraw £10 £50 £20 £60 £30 £70 £40 £80 Other Amount Devlin pressed a button and the screen shifted again as a £10 note came out of the machine after Devlin took his card back, Devlin then walked over to the bus stop and momentarily waited for the 29 bus, he got on it when t arrived, then then sat in his seat and closed his eyes until he had reached Finsbury park station, he came off the bus, took a deep breath and looked around, the arsenal shop looked awfully creepy at the moment Devlin Sharpe: What everyone in the MWE doesn't reallise all of this was just a clever charade, now is the time where I am serious, this is my time now... Devlin kicks a can so hard it lands on the other side of the road Devlin Sharpe: I may have had a bad beginning to my return to MWE, however from now on its all different, now I a man on a mission, I will take out every single undeserving leech that has joined MWE recently you know why? Because this is MY show, I was here first damnit I will not let you overtake me by sheer luck you understand? Devlins left hand curled in to a fist Devlin Sharpe: I am a MWE Original, I have been here longer than most of the roster, I am a future MWE legend, hell I already am the legendary loser, I have lost to nearly everyone on the roster Devlin smirks Devlin Sharpe: Brian Michaels, Sinc Mercier, Kevin Hardaway hell even Paige wanted to beat the shit out of me Suddenly his tone changes Devlin Sharpe: I am sick of being some puppet. some platform for people to think they can start their careers for in this business. Come Independence Day, Eric Derf will see just what kind of monster he is dealing with, when we are locked inside that prison cell. Everyone will have no choice but to take notice, because there will be nothing short of muder, that I wouldn't do...to get back to the position in this busness that I deserve...ON FUCKEN TOP!!! When I do, all of you will tell me I was right, Only I can do what I have done, can do, and will, do, all with a straight face......So Derf, this is the part where you have to ask yourself...do I feel lucky? Devlin gave the camera a quizzical look Devlin Sharpe: Oh and you know at the end of the match? When you scream my name, pleading for the pain to stop?, you may call me...The Dev
