Experience

May 8 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Out into the pouring, drenching rain they go, where Katy sees him on his knees, face down on the concrete, either letting the water pound on his head like a thumping headache or maybe just not moving at all. Katy sees this and rushes over to Devlin, worried and freaking out right about now as she trips over to him and shakes him again. He doesn't move. Katy: Devlin? Devlin? Devlin? Wake up No movement. Katy: Oh my god. Devlin? Are you OK? Speak to me?! Once again, no movement or no words coming out of his mouth. Katy: Dammit...SOMEBODY HELP! Devlin Sharpe: I'm drunk, dammit. Can't you see that I am wasted? Devlin stands up and bursts in to laughter, he hugs Katy, as the scene expands to reveal that the whole scene was created in a studio, Devlin slowly walks over to a machines and pushes the button to make the rain stop, he then grabs a towel from a chair and walks off the set while laughing, he is wearing a overcoat over a vest and a pair of jeans, he then slowly exits the studio in to the bone chillingly cold darkness of the night Devlin Sharpe: so sorry dude, I had to do this really, it was so funny, seriously I had to fuck with your head, I mean seriously it so was cliché of you I had to parody it you know like those annoying movies, I just wanted to call this "Wrestling movie" you know because like its a parody and loads of parody movies are called something movie and it gets annoying and stuff? Devlin walks out from the studio and begins to walk as the camera follows him, Devlin faces the camera while walking and starts to talk Devlin Sharpe: Normally this is my time where I talk about why I am better than my opponent, but to be honest, I am not better than my opponent, so instead I will tell you why I will beat him He brushes his hair away from his eyes as genuine rain begins to pour down and causing his hair to become humid and puffy, he tries to straighten it for a moment then continues speaking Devlin Sharpe: Now Not many people know this but I am one of the 3 TRUE MWE Originals that are still active, Devlin Sharpe, Brian Michaels, Sonny Williams and no I don't mean MWE as the MWE that Tim Fitzgerald brought Devlin shakes his head repeatedly as rain is speckled everywhere by his hair Devlin Sharpe: No no no, this was way before it all this was all the way at the beginning, hell we were not even called Mayhem Wrestling Entertainment, it was April of 2007, we were Total Mayhem Wrestling, a bunch of ragtag misfits brought together by dreams of grandeur, dreams of super stardom He stands under a lamp post for a moment as it gives him a holy glow in the dead of night and then he continued to walk Devlin Sharpe: problem was, most of us barely knew the basics of wrestling and as were were a small federation we didn't have the amazing production values of today, so I was there from the very beginning and I saw everyone around me elevate their game to a superstar level, when we started, Brian Michaels and Sonny Williams didn't know shit, now look at them, former world champions Devlin shivered as the rain hit him hard, he closed his coat tighter over him as he spoke again Devlin Sharpe: You see this arrangement was before Tim Fitzgerald took over and made MWE what it is today, in the beginning sure we wanted stardom but we did it for the fun of it really, but during that fun, all of us TMW Veterans got some irreplaceable experience, I have seen shit you wouldn't believe, I've seen people die, I've seen people come back to life, I also got some very valuable advice from tim Fitzgerald and other MWE alumni advice that you can't possibly get Devlin ran his fingers through the wet hair and licked the wetness Devlin Sharpe: I have seen GLOWING wrestlers, invasions, wars, you name it I probably saw it, I experienced it, I felt it, I engulfed myself in that experience to better myself, what do you have Kevin, one month of experience, a couple of matches in ICW some in MWE? really? that's it? the kind of shit I have seen would scar you for life dude I mean Devlin's eyes pop out and he holds his hair back Devlin Sharpe: I Killed my own brother didn't I? I had the guts to do that, what do you have Kevin, you don't have guts you don't have experience, you know I bet you couldn't even spend one night alone with Jekyl without going Bat shit insane, that dude even creeps me out sometimes and I am used to his shit Devlin accidentally steps in to a puddle and makes a disgusted face as he slowly shakes his foot to allow it to dry a bit Devlin Sharpe: you see? the other 2 MWE Originals have used their experience to reach the top, I have the same experience however I have not utilised it like they have if I did I would be equal or just below them right now and you know what the sad thing is Kevin? you can NEVER experience 99% of the stuff that happened to me, because that was in MY past, all those wrestlers have gone by, I have been here since the beginning, I will be here till the end, I may have taken 6 months off however, MWE is my home, I am MWE, MWE is me, I have been in MWE for all of my 2 year career and I will be here till the very end of the company or of my body and this kind of dedication only comes from one thing...Experience
