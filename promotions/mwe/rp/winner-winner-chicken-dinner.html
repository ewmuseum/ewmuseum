Winner, Winner, Chicken Dinner

Apr 16 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
*The scene opens up at a lucky-in motel with the sun on the horizon barely inching past the lucky-in motel. We see a green door that says apartment C-4 with a do not disturb sign hanging on the door knob. Inside the dirty little room we see two people under the covers one Peter Kaymakcian whose chest is exposed with the sheets covering his abs down and the other an unfamiliar blonde girl who is completely covered from the neck down. Peter just stares into her almond shaped hazel eyes. Finally Peter gets up showing off his Family Guy boxers featuring the many faces of Stewie through out the series. Peter picks up his jeans and puts them on quietly so he doesn't wake up the mysterious woman. He looks around for his belt because he can't wear his jeans without a belt. He looks around for five minutes checking under the bed, on the couch, and the drawers, until he finally notices it on the ceiling fan. He quickly grabs it and tries to find his shirt so he can head over to the MWE Arena to get a quick workout done. He finally finds his sky blue shirt that says "Hi Hater" on the front and "Bye Hater" on the back. He walks quickly writes down a note with his number on it and leaves it on the nightstand next to her. Before leaving Peter simply mutters, "Bye Sammy." He walks down the blue balcony towards the stairs so he can get into his car. He walks down the stair and towards his black sudan with red doors because that is the only car he could afford on a rookie wrestler budget. He drives down Route 35 for about a half an hour and starts to get a little hungry so he decides to stop at a Dunkin' Donuts. He goes through the drive through because it was so crowded inside because there was a mother there with about twelve children and no one knew what they wanted yet. Peter speaks to the drive through window in a tired tone while being answered in a fuzzy unclear response from the drive through window guy named Mark.* Peter: I would like a plain toasted bagel with butter. Mark: Did you say a plain toasted bagel with butter, sir? Peter: Yeah, that's it. Mark: Okay your total is 1.57. *Peter drives up to the window and gives Mark his money. Mark then hands Peter his order and Peter continues on driving down Route 35. He passes many signs the only one that sticks out to him though is an MWE sign that says, "MWE welcomes three bright stars the only way they know how. In a triple threat match. Which ICW graduate will reign supreme?" The poster features a picture of Peter, Nic, and Devlin. Peter smiles at the poster and thinks to himself that match is in the bag. Peter starts to get a little bored so he decides to turn on the radio to station Z 100 where he hears Devlin's interview where Devlin talks about how he beat Peter and how he is the best which just makes Peter laugh. After the interview was over the song Womanizer by Britney Spears came on which just made Peter sick to his stomach so he changed the station to the classic station where he heard his all time favorite song Bye-Bye Miss American Pie. Peter finally reaches the MWE Arena after an hour and a half drive down Route 35. Peter parks his car mutters "Crap!" after he sees all the photographers and interviewers knowing they would want his opinion on the match. Peter grabs his Dunkin' Donuts bag so he could warm up his bagel inside when the reporters swarm him. Ralph talks to Peter in an inquisitive tone.* Ralph: Mr. Kaymakcian Devlin Sharpe says that he will be the one who gets the win in the battle of ICW Graduates this week. He goes mentions how he beat you. What is your response to this? *Peter lets out a slight chuckle to Ralph's statement. Peter answers Ralph question in a tone that shows he feels Devlin Sharpe is no threat to him and won't even be a factor in the match.* Peter: The Dev, yes that is his nickname, my have beaten me in my first match in ICW, but as I recall from that point on I only lost two more matches, beat him, won my MWE opening match, and struck gold. The Dev after our first match lost to me and hasn't won a match since. Also that trend will continue on this week because I will be the one standing tall when that bell rings. To add to that point I have another thing to say to the Dev. That's your excuse? Really? I mean come on. I've only been losing because I am better then everyone else. That makes perfect sense, just like I am only poor because I am richer then everyone else, or I am only fat because I am skinnier then everyone else. You see Dev when your a winner like me you don't need to make excuses and even if you do it would certainly be better then that crap. *Peter attempts to walk into the building thinking they would be satisfied with that one quote, but he was wrong as they wanted a quote for Nic Kraven. Peter stops to answer one more question from Susie Colbert from Fox Five news. Susie asks Peter this next question in a demanding tone leaving Peter to looks at her like she has just lost her mind.* Susie: Peter Fox wants to know how do you plan on matching up with Nic this week? I mean most MWE and ICW fans feel that you are only holding onto your title because you have yet to face Nic Kraven. *Peter gets a little angry at her question because he feels like he never tried to duck any contenders in ICW. Peter answers this question with some flair, but he still sounds a little laid back as if he has already won the match.* Peter: One I never ducked anybody and if Nic wanted a shot at my title he should've earned it like I earned my title shot. Susie I have a question for you. Did you really just ask the champ how he would match up with Nic Kraven? The better question would be how long will it take me to beat both of those chumps. Last time I checked only one person got a win last week who is in this match and that was me. Dev lost and I was the one who got the one, two, three for the team of Peter Kaymakcian and Nic Kraven. Nic stood in the corner like a good little boy and let the real man take care of business. *Peter exits the field of reporters and walks into MWE Arena where he exhales a sigh of relief. Peter walks down the hall and finds the kitchen area. Peter looks around and finally sees a red microwave. He places his bagel in the red microwave oven and puts the timer on for thirty seconds. Peter sits on the kitchen counter top waiting patiently for the thirty seconds to get done. After the thirty seconds is done Peter grabs his bagel and begins to eat it while walking towards the locker room area so he can get changed for his work out. Peter finishes his bagel and enters the locker room area where he sees it is barren and just shakes his head in disgust from the lack of activity in the gym. Peter changes into some black shorts with a red and white stripe running both the left and right sides and a plain white-t. Peter places his stuff on the bench since he is the only one here and he has nothing worth stealing. Peter walks into the gym and it is as barren as the locker room. Peter walks past the running machines and straight towards the benching. He places two forty-fives on each side and does ten sets of twelve. On his last sets Peter's eyes go bloodshot red from the ridiculous amount of work he is forcing on his body. After bench pressing Peter walks over towards the dumbbells and picks up two one hundreds and does fifty hammer curls and fifty regular curls. Takes a five minute break to rest his muscles. Peter then walks over towards the Smith Machine(Squating) and places three forty-fives and each side and does ten sets of twelve. After this Peter's quick workout is done and he heads over towards the showers. Peter opts not to use the showers as he is not a fan of public showers and elects to take one when he gets home. Peter turns left and walks into the locker room to pick up his stuff and makes another left and walks out of the MWE Arena and heads straight for his car. Peter gets in his car and drives towards the KFC on 6th Avenue so he can pick up some lunch. He decides to eat inside the establishment and is pleasantly surprised with whom he meets in there. It is his old girl friend Rachel and Oliver. Peter gets his order and decides to sit at there table. Both give him a look telling him to move, but Peter elects to ignore there looks and instead tries to make conversation with the two of them. Peter talks to them as if nothing has happened.* Peter: So why are you guys here? *Oliver clearly not happy to see Peter answers in an angry tone.* Oliver: I have a business conference here. Why are you here? *Peter answers politely hoping they can forgive him for his past sins.* Peter: MWE Event. *Oliver still angry decides to make it uncomfortable so Peter has to leave. Rachel isn't sure about her feelings for Peter and sounds in decisive about anything she says or does.* Oliver: No why are you at our table? Rachel: Maybe we should let him sit with us? *Oliver snaps at that very idea.* Oliver: NO! If you want to stay here with scum like him be my guest, but I am gone. *Oliver and Rachel both get up and walk out the door leaving Peter sitting there feeling angry and sorry for himself. Rachel turns back one time with a look of regret upon her face, but quickly walks back to Oliver. Peter finishes his chicken and hops into his car and drives towards his apartment. Peter enter his apartment and sits down on his brown couch. After ten minutes of sitting Peter grabs a beer bottle and mutters "You're my only true friend" as the scene fades to black.*
