Don't Smile For Too Long, It'll Get Stuck Like That; An RP For DingleberryC

Jul 28 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
“Why live a dream that’s tainted with trouble and less than it seems?”-Emilie Autumn Sinc: I have waited for this day….throughout my entire career. He looks through the large windows of his top floor hotel room and scopes out the sky eying the buildings lined together beneath him. The city of Knoxville has never looked so beautiful and full of meaning until tonight. The night where Sinc must be so fully concentrated on his match with an MWE legend, a man who claims to be the very best, there is little time to think about what is to come, you simply must take action. Sinc: D.C…..I don’t even know where to begin. Should I start with your obsession for being a foul anomaly in this game of superstars, or should I start with your loathing to everything and everyone that can’t seem to handle you. You are locked in this train of thought that you can beat anyone at any time, well let me give you a heads up, or “reality check” as you would like to call it…. His eyes tighten into almost snake like slits and he gazes right at the center of the camera lens. Such an object of expression needs no words, he is making it crystal clear that he is coming for D.C. and there is nothing he can do about it. The camera seems to play the fiddle to a candy land of words directed at the person who appears to treat the company as his own sweet tooth, Mike Weaver. Sinc: You haven’t. Beaten. Me. How DARE you throw everyone on the roster to the dogs and thinking you have the balls and the guts to do whatever you want, whenever you want, how dare YOU Mike. How does it feel to be the daring one for once? How does it feel to be scolded with your own words? You made a bold move to call out the roster like that, and I will be rooting you on in your efforts, but know that I will never take your side. To think that Brian made me sick was just petty talk, to say that YOU make me sick, is brutal honesty. That car crash screwed with your head Weaver, because look around you, people are still standing and passing you up on top of that. After Whitman destroyed you your ignorant and arrogant self can’t admit defeat. You can’t move on because you want time to remain stopped at your will. How come I can defeat him with so little trouble, on my first try, yet he was a struggle for you? This isn’t final fantasy or some video game where you appear to believe lives and power ups are infinite, this is real life, and you are trapped in your glory days of “old school” rants and understatements. And I am going to say it, you were NOTHING without Brian Michaels. He clasps his hands into balled fists. His finger nails are almost puncturing his skin, they are close to making the soft flesh bleed with envy. But D.C. is a master of promoting himself, and a king of depleting his opponents down to nothing. In return Sinc has evolved under the aid of failed partnerships and endless betrayals, he has learned how to improve, that’s what Mike doesn’t understand. Sinc: I’ve had to rely on myself in my career, I didn’t get a chance to have some hot mess of a girlfriend guide me to the edge of the world, I didn’t get a chance to be in a team and become a famous name, I NEVER HAD THAT CHANCE. I have no faith in people that have no faith in me. I have no desire to cater to individuals who hate me, my desire for them is to let me prove them wrong and watch as I pull through and break my back to keep the earth on my shoulders. All the other superstars backstage can think what they will of this match between you and I, but they can’t throw a stone without a steady hand, and I’ve managed to dodge them all. You have so many different things to say D, and you manage to say them and back them up with prestige and an attitude that you are unstoppable. You sit back and watch things happen before your eyes and let others take care of the hard stuff while you sip chardonnay and kick your feet up because no matter how much one tries, you think life is just an easy ride….it’s not. He stops himself to once again gaze at the skyline ahead. All those people, all those innocent and synchronized pedestrians just simply attending their daily business and satire. Traffic lanes clogged into a long stream of lights and florescent paint jobs, trying to find somewhere to go only to find out their going to be late….again. He tilts his head and huffs. Sinc: Do what you will with me Weaver, but I ain’t backing down. You can throw your silly references at me and explain that I am but a measly worm to your nest of birds, OR you can toss up your arrogant meter and explode into a raving chatterbox with your typical trash talk and greatness shtick. You can even rely on your posse of idiots to talk me down through the power of friendship, and possibly put your wonder rings together to transform into giant crap piles to start attacking me with meaningless words and thunder bolts. Harry Potter style. Or finally you can be a man and come up with some new material to REALLY scare the bejesus out of me for once. Take your pick buddy, time won’t stand still forever. Because compared to me I AM father time and your mother nature, did it feel good when I stopped Lindsey’s period? I bet it did. He places his hand carefully on the titanic window in front of him. The glass is so spotless it appears that his hand is placed on nothing but thin air. Sinc leans on it watching the flickering lights of Tennessee enchant his mind with colorful blips and intriguing rays. The rush of being able to see the world under him begins to feel dignified. Sinc: But maybe….D.C….maybe I should just slow my role and calm down a minute. It could be that I am not sending you the right message, maybe I am out of my element for attacking you that way. Should I have….no….NO! Forget it, I am right in what I said. You have been opening that mouth of yours long enough. You have intertwined me into this inconsiderate man because when I picture you in my mind, all I see is rage and anger, you’ve made it obvious that the usual face and play-it-safe way will not be your destruction. I do not need to give you a history lesson of mine because it would be pointless, you outshine what I have accomplished this is true, but you have not outshined what I am presently, and that is a guardian. I am the single shimmer of hope left to end your moronic charades and constant bumbling. Open the oven and step aside Mike, because I’m coming out blazing. Sinc shuffles in the pockets of his tan cackles and takes out a lighter. His back is now leaning on the glass as he turns around and waves his left palm lightly over it. The flame boils under the skin radiating his palm with warmth. He begins to feel calm and at ease by the motion of the fire and smiles. Sinc: I don’t need to burn down a bar or make gestures at my genitals to form a statement. I have the ability to adapt and overcome, an ability your losing quite fast. I did enjoy how much fun we had when we encountered each other in various silly puns and pranks. The movie we both took roles in, the day we both got xbox achievements left and right, and the day I got to see you yell at the very pregnant Tera upstairs. But there is only so much that we can keep subtle and sane before it gets old and boring, and Mr. Weaver….your still old and boring. And you going back to your old school self is not going to help any. You ARE overrated, and you ARE hated because you make yourself to be that way, you made your choice, now live with it. Live with taking down wrestler after wrestler to satisfy your daily dose of pain and affliction, live with this abnormality of abhorrence and filth during your entire lifespan, D.C….live with the fact that your ten minutes of fame is here and now, but it will soon end, very soon. He clicks off the lighter and dumps it back in his pocket. His arms begin to fold and he stares at his hotel bed, topped with fan mail and unpaid bills he snickers. No matter where Sinc is he knows he isn’t safe from the cunning and slick cut words of his opponents, enemies, and rivals. They travel through the wind like razor blades and cannot be avoided, they follow you wherever you go, until the day you enter that ring and shun them all away. Sinc: Brian scooped you up from a no name federation and cradled you, breast fed you, hell he even idolized you up until Rated-X’s end and then you really came into your own. If you want me to give you a cuss fest and satan in a hand bag because that’s what you expect, don’t bother. My words and my faith are powerful in itself, I don’t rely on conjunctions and offensive slang to get me by anymore, you apparently still need it to sell your character, just like you need Lindsey to make people actually listen to you. Do you honestly think they are staring at you and trying to hear you out? Sex is what sells, if anything Brian didn’t make you she did, your weak alone. QUOTE me on that, PLEASE D.C. give me some of that spark I know you have, I would LOVE for you to show me that you can take me out, only to be proven wrong. Think back braniac, I mean REALLY think about how you got to become so good in the first place. If you pay attention to your own trail you will see what really created the beast in you, it was not D.C. it was everything he came across. Your matches, your achievements, all due to holding hands and singing off key songs to rejoice over what? NOTHING. I came from the VERY bottom, and I built myself up, no matter if I vacated a silly title or not. I wanted something, and I got it, and if I want to beat you, I will. Sinc walks over to the bed and shuffles through the letters and bills and simply throws them all over the room in handfuls. After the bed is cleansed of all the papers and envelopes he sits on it sighing his head in a slight sweat. He loosens the tie of his Armani shirt and chucks it to the floor. What he’s wearing seems to signal he just got back from some sort of conference or meeting. Sinc: All that fan mail, all that paperwork is just typed up feelings. I don’t need to read them to motivate myself to beat you. I already know the fans respect me, and I admire them with all my heart, but this time, I am relying on nothing but my mentality and physical capabilities, because you and I, we will DEFINE professional wrestling in that ring. It may not be a PPV, but we will make everyone wish it was, that’s a promise. At this point you seem to realize that no one can save the roster from your perpetual killing spree, but your dead wrong. Win or lose, I am going to make sure people don’t have any need to fear you, because all it takes is grasping a goal, and going for it. And Weaver, your not much of a goalie these days. As Michaels said, the ball is in your court, now what are you gonna do with it? You gonna run? Or do you wanna stay and play awhile? Because if you really want to tango, then you got a whole roster full of smug looking behemoths willing to take you out, you’re a criminal and we are all the hit men, I hope you know that. But I am sure you do, cause this is exactly what you were wishing for, welcome my friend, your dreams are coming true. Sinc closes his eyes and raises his face as it points at the ceiling. He tries to imagine reigning over D.C. with an iron fist finally defeating a god among men, a jewel in a barrel full of duds. One foot over his chest as he is demolished in the ring, and Sinc’s name is chanted. Sinc: I would love nothing more than to relive a victory over you again and again, I wouldn’t just love it, I would make sure you NEVER forget it. If I could I would do my best to make sure your in the most intense pain of your life, so much pain that it feels like your in the midst of dying and staring down the jowl of death itself. I would curse you out with everything kempt inside me and demoralize your every fiber. But I can’t do that, then I’d be like you right? No, I’m not a monster, I’m the slayer, your just in my way. I NEED to show people that I can stand a chance in that squared fixture with you D, I need to let people know that there is still an area where you have your guard down. I am going to uncover that area, and shred it apart until you become speechless and weakened. Right now your better than me, but your not THE best, not by a long shot. I have proof that I can call myself the best right now, my world title, but I refuse to, because I still have people I need to face including yourself. Sinc lays down onto the bed and adjusts himself to rest on his back. He turns his head and grabs a mini alarm clock off the dresser to his right and begins to toss it up and down in the air to keep himself entertained as he chats. The rhythm of the up and down metronome comforts him in the most traditional way, the clock has become a toy to this stressed out champion. Sinc: But I am surprised by something, tell me D, how is your little team sideshow project going? The Saints of Los Angeles were they? I am guessing you training such a low quality team probably sparked Brian to create Michaels Inc, except Brian’s team was a lot more successful than your hardly carded baboons. Didn’t one of them have a talking duck or something? You must get off by training such unstable imbeciles. The only thing that comes to mind on why you would waste your time with that group of low lives is because you probably don’t think you can do much else due to the fact that you’ve done it all correct? Nuh uh, I am still wide open as someone you have yet to defeat D.C. so that can’t POSSIBLY be true. There isn’t alot left you can do Mike, and it is just really sad to watch you go on your usual tangent like this, SO SAD, that if any tears were shed for you, they would be made of coal and brimstone. I actually wondered whether you were the type of guy who could hold back how he really feels or not, I guess I was wrong, your another short fuse waiting to be desecrated by a stable athlete with a focused aim, and that aim is to continue to rid MWE of people like you. He stops tossing the clock and catches it one last time in his hands. He blankly blinks for a few moments and then slams it down back on the dresser. Sinc winces and wipes a hand across his cheek feeling a ruggedness indicating a new beard growing. He gets up and walks over to the bathroom and looks deep into the mirror, he sees a man with a lot of issues that are still left uncertain, but he remains adamant and continues to fight his demons. He has overcome this much, and if he can survive this long, he can survive til the death of the company. Sinc: I beat Sonny, then he left. I beat Cannon, then he left. I beat A-Rod, then he left. I beat Sharpman, then…he left. A-Rod and Joseph were the only ones to come back so far, I’ve beaten MANY more Mike, and I intend not to stop there. And who’s to say, when and IF I beat you, that you won’t leave too? I know it’s coming, you will eventually leave because you can’t deal with losing, or you will leave because you just can’t seem to get motivated anymore. Whatever the case may be, until you win against me, I think of you as the guy who just can’t say no…to anything. Once we go head to head, all that is going to change. I only see one solution from you Weaver, and that is to give me your very best, because that is what I am going to give you, if there’s anything you should expect it is that I am going to push my self to the hardest level and overcome the legend you make yourself out to be, just like that. And don’t even speculate on pulling the “you left” card on me. That was the ONLY break I have ever gotten, and I deserved it. But go ahead and use that against me anyway, I know your desperate D, so do it. Or maybe the classic blab about me vacating the US title, REAL freakin original, how many times have I heard that? He cringes at the thought of people forcing themselves not to forget his past mistakes and watches as his reflection mimics him. Sinc looks impressed with himself and his image and basks in his appearance for a brief period of time. He then focuses on his rugged facial hair again and brushes his fingers through the bristles with a pattern of movement. Sinc: See, I am POURING out everything you could ever stab at me for, because it doesn’t process in my mind. I know you’re an MWE original Mike, but I could care less. Things change, this is something you gotta get used to. When you channel surf and scan all the hot new shows and movies you’re the man that scratches his head because you never found out about them or even have interest in them. You want to watch eighties flicks and nineties blockbusters, and that just won’t cut it in this present age. You got to aim big, the box office crashers, the big time sellouts, that’s what’s worth watching, not recycled classic genres. You’re a genre in itself, an untamed actor looking to make it big, well big in your terms these days is very small, again, your fame is going to be over soon enough. When the walls come down, and the gloves come off, you won’t be able to make a peep about me or anyone else for that matter, you’ll start running out of fresh material, and then you’ll go get in another car crash and wish you could dream up some new stuff, sucks to be you. And if you think for one second that I am actually afraid…of you? Sinc grabs a bottle of shaving cream from the counter of the bathroom and lathers it all over his face. He applies it carefully and makes sure he gets it just right, and with that he picks up a shaving blade and begins to trim his rugged shadow. He stops for a second to finish his statement and smirks in the mirror. Sinc: Think again. He finishes up his shaving and washes the blade in the sink. Afterward he quickly grabs a towel from a rack and cleans up his face. He contemplates whether to douse some aftershave on and decides not to do so, feeling rather exhausted from all his ranting he shrugs and drops the blade in the trash can nearest him. As he leaves the bathroom the camera is immediately in his nose. Slightly annoyed he maintains himself and goes on. Sinc: Your turning the safety off Weaver? How amusing, how absolutely THRILLING! Your apparently a human weapon now, what kind of gun are you, BB or potato? Maybe you’re an air gun, because all you ever do now is blow hot air. The whole time I’ve been running my mouth AND proving myself in MWE my safety has always been off, and your saying you’ve had yours on the whole time? I must applaud you sir, I did not expect for you to be such a….. Sinc sarcastically laughs then pauses gazing at the camera with a tightened anger. He speculates how to finish his dialogue and itches his eyebrow in an arrogant fashion. Then after figuring out the exact thing to say, he heightens his lips with a scowl. Sinc: Great liar….. After he’s done with his little phrase he averts himself and clashes back down on the bed currently sitting at the very end of it examining the carpeting in boredom. He picks dust off his arm and lets his brain tickle his senses a little for something a bit more promising to blurt out. Sinc then jots his head with a grin. Sinc: Is…is there supposed to be like a difference between the old D.C. and the new one anyway? Is the new one more into never ending pointless conversations with a posse of band wagon junkies while the old one enjoys talking a lot more trash? I could be wrong, but that’s the only difference I can really see at the moment. I apologize if I seemed to have missed something here, but coincidentally, I don’t think the two sides of D are really that different after all, pity, you could have really proved something to me if there was a decent difference to take place. Oh well, there’s always next year, maybe. Sinc’s voice holds a small bit of sarcasm during what he just said. Feeling the sleepiness overcoming him now he gets somewhat flustered and stares dreamily into the lamp beside him. The light begins to resemble an invigorating illumination of simplicity and intricacy. Before getting too out of it he wipes his eyes and messes up his hair to keep himself awake. Sinc: I want you to notice something though Mike, I want you to recall what happened to your precious Lindsey when Cannon got a hold of her, he pushed her around like a rag doll and basically made her his personal slave, and where were you to protect her? Why did Paige have to handle it herself, oh that’s right, you were injured on some hospital bed. If I were them I would never forgive you. But of course you have a charm about you, you have this way with words that gets everyone jumpin on your man bag….how quaint. While your playing save the princess I’m continuing to develop myself in this company. I was the start of the Nu-MWE craze, I was the very first Nu-MWE original that planted myself upon a mountain and soaked in every piece of advice I was given, and then I achieved becoming not only a four time US champ, but a world champ to boot, and for that I am truly proud of myself and the fans, for always believing in the impossible. And now I know….impossible is nothing. The roster needs a leader Mike, when they look at me that’s what they see, when they look at you, well, as you said, they fear you. But now that I am in your way, they are going to be laughing at you, not shuddering in your glory, because you’ll become a joke. Sinc now rubs his wrists and begins to relax himself. He goes from his wrists to his neck, and then to cracking his knuckles. He tries to lay down again but D.C. and their match will not leave his mind, the thoughts and outcome possibilities will not stop haunting him. He grunts with a heave and slams the bed in frustration. Sinc: I wish I could just get this over with and pin you right here and now just to show everyone how vulnerable you truly are. Then again, you may not just have two sides to you, you may have five or MORE sides to you, your not an easy dude to figure out, you know that. But I’ll be damned if I am gonna let you slaughter me and start your winning streak off with vengeance, I am not going to be your target of venting, I will not allow it. If I am just a punching bag to you, then you are just a sack of beans to me, and I am going to rip you wide open. You were right about only ONE thing…. He dips his head and chuckles to himself while clasping his hands in a thoughtful gesture. The hairs on his neck seem to stand up and his spine starts to tingle, the air around him makes him shiver and his dimples grow pale. The next few sentences he says he wants to make very important. He suddenly dips his head back up and bites his upper lip. Sinc: You can be undefeated if you want to be right now D…..but I am in a hotel room in Knoxville, your probably halfway across the world, right now things are neutral and colorless, but when we meet face to face, well….that’s all gonna change. That’s the future, that’s not now, and I AM the future, you better believe it. And to all the two year olds and whining babies back stage that approve of D‘s message to the roster, I have only a small rebuttal, if you don’t like me, get used to it, because your idol D.C. is going to be defeated. Your either with me, or against me, choose wisely my friends, is it worth smiling for the paparazzi, or is it about the effort? You decide. In conclusion he ends his talk and lies down in full bliss onto his bed spread. Just before he is about to go to sleep and turn off the light, he hears a loud banging on the door. He barely jumps up to the sound in both shock and confusion but braces himself. He takes a deep breath and marches over to the door, he looks through the peep hole but he sees no one. Curious and wondrous he swings it open, as he peers beside him there is still not a soul to be found. He gives up and closes the door only to turn around and see Nate in his face, Sinc shakes in terror. Sinc: JESUS CHRIST! Nate: Sorry partner, didn’t mean to scare you senseless. Sinc: It’s a little late for that….now what is it you want? He takes a seat and sets his hat down hating to be the bearer of bad news, but he prepares for the worst. More and more people are wanting to get him into plentiful storylines, commercial promotions, and merchandising campaigns. He grasps the casing almost crushing it and puts it back in his pocket. The issue with having a manager is putting all your trust in the person, so much trust, that being patient at times isn’t an option. Take everything you’ve ever learned, ever wanted to achieve, and put it all into one focused analogy. Use your weakness as a strength and shoot for the top of your class again, become a dream and live your imagination. D.C. is not going to be number one forever, not unless you LET him. God works in mysterious ways Nathan, and I find it fetching that his ways aren’t working out for me. Nate: D’s fame is what fuels him. If you take that away from him, his life is gone, he goes from high flying celebrity to low life bum once he loses to you. If you beat him Sinc, it becomes one of the biggest wins of your entire career. Win. There’s that word again, staying still like a stiletto piercing the nerves and veins in Sinc’s brain, puncturing the very center of it until the structure erupts. Achieving a victory is meaningless if you can’t prove to do it ten times over the next day. One win, at any moment in time, can be simple “luck” or maybe a “fluke” and it can even be known as a “good day”. However you slice the cake, that word, that figment, is what everyone wants on their record. Sinc: I see your still talking to me like I’m a rookie, that’s understandable. But people forget that I’ve bulldozed through obstacles bigger than that pinnacle of selfless arrogance. I’ve eaten the canvas of that ring more times than I can count, and I’ve went through hell in high water to get a cheer or a hoot. It’s just a playground for him, and it seems no one is worthy enough to be in it EXCEPT him. Whether I am approved of or not, that doesn’t stop me from doing what I do best. Wrestling creates a passion and a personality that cannot be defined as anything else but lifestyle. This creation of a revolutionary sport changed how everyone thought of original mat wrestling and revamped it into a world wide phenomenon. The fans and the photo opps are just bonuses that are not recommended or required to get some cash and fast build up. There’s jobbers, there’s money makers, and then there’s the competitors in a popularity contest, which can be described otherwise as the main eventers and top contenders. The final divide is fans and fame, there is no in between, you only switch from one to the other when you get bored and tiresome of the usual stint. Sinc: The only claim Mike can make to me is that I haven’t made a name for myself as much as he has. He can have the fame and all the good ratings he wants, but I got my confidence and my independence, I don’t need to go main stream and put myself out there. I know this is what your suggesting Rellington, and it’s probably the best thing to do in my case, but I will not stoop to being the next poster boy, I’ll save that position for some other sucker. D.C. keeps his stardom and heat by being himself, because there are still fans out there who believe he’s better than me and that he always will be. I’m still the safe haven to little kids and religious nuts due to me not being as off the wall as my opponent is. That’s not what I want them to think of me, I want to show them that saying a name and keeping it cool and collected isn’t all that I’m about. I have more to give, and D’s gonna see that. With a straying gaze Sinc grabs his coat from a chair near the bathroom and slips it on. He hesitates and raises a finger signaling he has one last thing to say to Nate, but he just shakes his head and opens the door to the room with a smug. Sinc: I’m going to walk outside to think for a few minutes. You know how to see yourself out, don’t be here when I get back, and if you see Christina, tell her I’m sorry for whatever I did, IF I did anything, she doesn’t deserve a man like me. He finishes his conversation with his former manager and shuts the door. He leaves Nate alone sulking in his own thoughts. Somewhere inside himself Nate believes he may have said the wrong things, or revealed information he possibly shouldn’t have. He rubs his dimple and sighs. Nate: The kid can’t be brought down….I’ll give him that….
