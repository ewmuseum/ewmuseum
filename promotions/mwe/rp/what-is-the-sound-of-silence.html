What is The Sound of Silence?

Sep 6 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hello darkness, my old friend. I've come to talk with you again. Because a vision softly creeping, left its seeds while I was sleeping. And the vision that was planted in my brain, still remains... Within the sound..... of silence... The song plays clear as day. However, there is no daylight. Here we are... Colorless and sightless. This promo, belongs to Kanto. James Sullivan. You've felt my wrath once before. Having to suffer through it twice? That's a death sentence. Poor unfortunate soul. In restless dreams I walked alone. Narrow streets of cobblestone. 'Neath the halo of a street lamp, I turned my collar to the cold and damp. When my eyes were stabbed by the flash of a neon light, that split the night, and touched the sound..... of silence... You may be capable of capturing the U.S. title. But my lad, that division is well below me. For I am in a world of my own. And anyone who dares to visit that world, will suffer an invasion of epic proportions. Do you dare to visit? And in the naked light I saw, ten thousand people, maybe more. People talking without speaking. People hearing without listening. People writing songs that voices never share, and no one dared, disturb the sound..... of silence... You're overconfidence will be your demise. Even if you came out prepared, walking down the ramp with a game plan in mind, one hundred and ten percent, fresh, ecstatic, and on top of the world, and put on the greatest performance of your life... You'd still be crumbled at my feet. Why? 'Cause you don't have the heart kid. And even if you did, I'm merely rip it out with my bare hands. "Fools", said I, "You do not know. Silence like a cancer grows. Hear my words that I might teach you, take my arms that I might reach you". But my words, like silent raindrops fell, and echoed, in the wells..... of silence... You got away safely our first meeting James. Don't expect it again. I want bones to snap, blood to flow, and cries to be heard across the land. It'll be you! You James! You will be the victim for my message! WHY!? WHYYY!? Simple! Wrong place... Wrong time... And the people bowed and prayed, to the neon god they made. And the sign flashed out its warning, in the words that it was forming. And the sign said, "The words of the prophets are written on the subway walls, and tenement halls". And whispered... in the sounds..... of silence... ............................................................ No more Paul Simon. No more Art Garfunkel. No more musical notes or instruments. Truly, this is.... The sound of silence. And what is the meaning of all this? Where lies the core? Let it be clear. Message target: ....Mr. Rodriguez..... No more silence. No, not at all. Many noses are heard throughout the mysterious dark. A pitter-patter scamper of an unknown animal. Then, the screech of a owl. Followed by, the howl of a coyote. What is this place? Where are we? Only Kanto knows. Alejandro, we as well are scheduled to meet... once again.... How I loved squeezing the life out of you, but the chance to do it twice? Those emotions are indescribable. And, don't think otherwise. There's no avoiding this. I will be the man who knocks you off your pedestal as a top competitor in the industry. It is the world of supply and demand. When something goes up, something else must go down. I will be the specimen going to the top, as I bring you down in a burning heap! I will climb that ladder to stardom, as I watch you plummet to the earth in tears! I will supply the punishment, and demand satisfaction!! Think of yourself as a condom. 'Cause after I'm done fucking with you, and then trashing you, I'll never look back. You're of mere insignificance, and despite the lies your mouth will disperse, you know it. And though my messages are short and blunt, often imitated but never duplicated, I think the question that remains is quite obvious. Alejandro Rodriguez, won't you be my stepping stone? Silence. More silence. Silence interrupted. "The Sounds of Silence" begins to softly play once again. *Fade away from the creep of the tune*
