Fly on the Wall; Hardaway RP

Jun 15 2009

Kevin Hardaway

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The setting? Syracuse, NY...or close to it. About 20 miles away from the big town of New York NY. The real setting? Phoenix House. A rehab center. Phoenix House has been helping people of all ages from all walks of life lead fulfilling, drug-free, lives. Phoenix House operates more than 30 residential and outpatient programs for adults and adolescents in New York City, upstate New York, and Long Island, treating some 2,500 individuals every day. Not only that, but support has come from celebrities, and sports athletes. But today, they never expected a celebrity to actually be IN rehab here. About a month ago, fellow MWE wrestler, Zak Warner was suspended and forced to enter rehab for a positive drug test. The people involved right now. One of Zak’s best friends. Kevin Hardaway. The man who he helped enter popularity, the person who has been there for him through thick and thin. Now it’s time to repay the favor. Too bad...because Angie doesn’t want to go through with it. Ever since his title loss, Kevin hasn’t been the same. He’s never the same after not winning a title, at any promotion. You’re just best to let him be for the next few days and let him worry about it. It’s just too bad that Angie has no god damned clue. Why the hell are we here? Because I wanted to talk to a good friend of mine. One that I haven’t seen since in quite awhile. Is that ok? Could’ve you just waited until he got out? Rehab facilities always have creeped me out. It’s just the...weird feeling when you step inside. Well do you want to wait in the car? You could’ve done that. You could’ve just stayed at the hotel with Kimmy and Hayley. Yes, Hayley is here with them...well somebody had to take care of Kimmy while Kevin and Angie visited Zak. Angie wanted to do a favor...it’s just that she didn’t know the gist of the information. Now that she knows what’s going on, she’s having second thoughts. Well that’s what I wanted to do. Then why the hell didn’t you say anything? How the hell was I supposed to know? You’re the one who can’t let sh*t go, Kev. Zuh? Here comes the sh*t hitting the fan. Of course, all Kevin should be able to do is step to the side of the fan. He’s dealt with 15 years of “special education” programs at school. He’s well known with the crazies. But, he lets the sh*t hit him of course and puts his foot into his mouth. Now how did that just come up? Oh, I dunno...you haven’t formally talked to me in a couple days, you spent all your time watching TV and surfing the internet. I don’t exist to you right now, you’re in your own little world. Is that a problem? It always is with you. Same ol’ Kevin...when you lose a big time match like what happened at Mayhem, you feel like the end of the world is happening. Cry me a f*cking river. Sigh. Insert foot into mouth. Might as well get her out of his head while he’s at it. Wouldn’t mind much. You know what...just get back in the car. If you’re going to be Mrs. Independent with her f*cking period, then do it when I’m not around. Fine. Fine. He turns around and so does she, as they walk their separate ways. Well, that’ll get her out of his hair for awhi...ok, now the couple’s Chevy Cobalt is driving off. Angie drove off and left Kevin behind at the rehab center. Now how’s that for miscommunication? Whatever, Kevin starts talking to himself...like he has an imaginary friend...ok, there’s some bad memories. Oh, what the hell? Dammit...you know what, deal with it later Kevin. The nerve of her. Muttering to himself now, he enters the center and immediately somebody’s there to help him. How thoughtful? Yes, I’m here to see a “Mr. Zachary Warner”, please? Hold on one second. Type type typing away on her computer as she...well I guess looks if Zak set up an appointment to meet up with Kevin. He has no idea how this works, he’s never been in a rehab facility, he doesn’t do this kind of stuff, why should it matter to him? But nonetheless, the receptionist smiles at Kevin. Ok, everything checks out. Somebody will be with you immediately to take you to Mr. Warner’s room. Kevin nods...well, that was easy. Too easy. So easy that he thinks all is well...until a big, giant security guard comes in and tells him to follow. Yeah, little too easy my ass. Then again, this ain’t a prison escape. Anyways, the guard shows Kevin to Zak’s room, walking down the white and uneasy hallway. For a nice rehab facility, this place is pretty weird. No problem anyways. The guard opens up the door and lets him in on a little piece of information. “If you’re done with him or any other emergency happens, knock on the door, I’ll be able to hear it. I can trust you, right.” Kevin nods. Yeah, he’s definitely going to trust you, to make sure he doesn’t get squashed by Bigfoot. Comedy aside, he opens the door to see Zak Warner laying on his bed, looking up into the ceiling as Kevin taps on the wall. The guard closes the door as Zak sees Kevin, running up to him, secret handshake, and hug, and other weird stuff. People and their weird society. Zak’s sure glad to see him though. HARDY, MY MAIN MUTHAFUCKIN’ DAWG!!! Very glad. Yeah...what’s going on? Same old shit, different day...you know the feeling, right? Yeah. Angie stole the damn car on my way inside. Zak finds that very hilarious as he starts laughing...hard, really hard. HA-HA! Now you’re really starting to feel the effects of marriage and shit. Yeeeah, I guess so. So what brings you here, you crazy bastard? Just checking up on you, making sure you’re doing well in the rehab. Pft, it’s nothing really. You just sit around, doing Origami and paintings, watching TV, and eating food. It’s just like home, although...at home, puffing some weed makes everything that I said better. A confused look comes across Kevin’s face, like he didn’t just realize that Zak just said that. So what does that mean? It means I’m lying through my teeth. You know how hard it is to go 30 days without a trip of acid, or a puff of the reefer, or a line of the white powder. Whoa...Kevin closes his ears, completely baffled at what Zak was saying. Dude, I can’t believe what I’m hearing here. You’re telling me you haven’t changed one bit? Nope. I count the days though of when I can finally go home and bask in the glory of a nice blunt and just watch the colors go by. I’m flabbergasted. Zak laughs hard again. What? “Flabbergasted”. That shit’s funny. Kevin keeps looking at Zak. Now that it’s been mentioned, Zak does have a weird, disheartening look upon his face. Either he doesn’t care, or he’s actually stoned out of his mind right now. I can’t believe you, man. I can’t believe you at all. Can’t believe what, the fact that I love the ganja? YES! THAT! Two years ago, you came up to me and introduced me, clean as a whistle. And during that conversation, you said something that gave me all the damn confidence in the world. You said that I was going to be a future World Heavyweight Champion. And I did that exact thing, by beating you clean in the middle of that ring. Kevin pauses for a few seconds, trying to get his bearings straight. But on Monday, I was at least expecting a card or a message of you saying the exact same thing, only here in the MWE. Guess what? IT DIDN’T HAPPEN! And I made myself look like the stupidest god damned idiot in the world on Monday. There’s times where having some words of courage from a good friend of mine actually helps. I didn’t get any of that because you were too busy trying to trade off a joint...in rehab nonetheless. Kevin walks towards the door now, about ready to knock on it, signifying that he’s done talking to his friend...former friend...whatever, he has no clue. What? Your wife ain’t doing the words of courage anymore for you? She’s doing words alright. She’s screaming, “Fuck me, Zak. Fuck me hard.” Ok, that pissed him off. And with a football like tackle, Kevin gets Zak onto the floor and starts to choke him out. Surprisingly, the doctors don’t see Kevin’s random, senseless act of violence. Zak, meanwhile has the same disheartening look on his face as Kevin gets straight to the point. You say that one more time and I swear to god... What? You’ll hurt me? No. I’ll take my hands off of you, like so. He lets go of the choke hold and lets go of him in general, sitting back on the cold tiled floor, as Zak gets up, breathing heavily, trying to regain his composure, as Kevin as sure regained his. Then, I’ll just leave. Without saying a god damn word. You want to know why? Because right now, everybody thinks you’re nothing more than a piece of low-life scum walking the streets at night. And right now, where nobody cares...only one person cares, and that’s me. You were there when my career almost ended, now I’m doing the same for you. Kevin places his hand on Zak’s shoulder now. I believe that you can make the right decision. Let’s just hope you can follow through on that. Now if you excuse me, I have to call Angie to get a ride home. ‘Later man. ‘Bye, Kevin. That’s it? That’s it. Kevin pats his good friend where it should count, on the shoulder and sits up, knocking on the steel white door, as a doctor grabs the doorknob and opens it up, letting Kevin walk out the cold, shallow halls. During the walk, he gets his cell phone from the basket and heads on out. Turning it on, he fires up his “Contacts” page and slides down the list of numbers he has on his list, including pictures of said contacts. He finally gets to the one he’s looking for. A picture of a smiling Angie, holding their baby daughter in her arms. He takes a look at the picture, and sighs. He realizes that he was an idiot, a douchebag. Guess it’s time to make ends meet. He presses the “Call” button and lets it ring. One ring.... Two rings... Three rings... Four rings... Five rings... Six rings... Click. “Hi, you’ve reached Angie. Sorry I’m not available right now but if you leave a name, number, and said message, I’ll get back to you ASAP. Thanks.” After the tone, record your message. When you are finished recording, hang up or press the # key. A receptionist will help you shortly. BEEP! Angie, it’s Kev. I’m done talking to Zak, so you can come back and pick me up so we can get home. He sits down on the step that goes to the door of the rehab center and waits. And waits...and waits. 10 minutes later, his cell phone goes off and he slides it open. A text message from Angie. Walk back to the hotel, Kevin. It’s not far. What he said earlier...the nerve of her. So they had a little argument, no big deal right? Every couple has had one. They’re not the first. A sigh of disbelief is in the air though, as Kevin just remains sitting on the bottom step. Guess since he has to do a promo, he can do it right here and right now. I could honestly sit here, removing myself from the world today, and just start to go on and on and on and on about how I suck, how I didn’t win “the big one”, and how there’s still a new champion in the balance. I could rant and rave about how I built myself up to be this big giant force to be reckoned with. All I hear from everybody is about my “experience” in big-time matches, about my days in the old time. About how I was the best that there was in this industry. That’s all I hear. I don’t hear about how good I am today, I only hear about how good I was yesterday. And I know I reference my past way more times than somebody can count on their fingers, but I can finally set free of the single fact that I can let it all go. The past is the past, the present is the present, and the future is the future. I’ve said it before and I’ll say it again. Nothing in this world angers me, drives me up the wall more, than people, who end up just leaving because either they couldn’t win “the big one”, or they didn’t get a chance to complete in “the big one” immediately. I mean, is that what you want me to do? Walk out? Go, “Yep, I won’t be winning the World Championship...peace out!” Is that so much it, at all? Heh. Then I’m guessing you don’t know the real me do you? You don’t know the man at all. Am I depressed? Yeah, I’m a little bummed out. You know I just lost an opportunity to become the MWE World Champion. Kind of a big deal. Everybody should be bummed when they lose something like that. I mean, you think I’m feeling bummed. Look at our former champion, David Whitman. The second I came to the MWE, this man was on the biggest roll of his life and he wasn’t looking back one single solitary second. He made the biggest roll of MY life seem tame in comparison. He was just at another level, and at the last minute, he made one little mistake and he lost his baby. I’ve been down that road before. Whitman deserves a break, as a reward for being pretty damn impressive with that belt. Hope he doesn’t take any offense to my actions this past Mayhem. You get that adrenaline rush and you do things you don’t want to. And I guess I should offer up my congrats to our new champion, Sinc Mercier. Yeah...good job. Now onto other business... REWIND THAT... And I guess I should offer up my congrats to our new champion, Sinc Mercier. You’ve apparently been here a long time and not only did you become the new champion, you also created the biggest dick move in the world by winning the US Championship the week before. Why is that a dick move? I have no idea. Maybe it’s because you vacated it, but hey...them’s the rules. Pretty sure you’re ok with your consolation prize. But hmm...that means the United States Championship is vacated once again. Foreshadowing perhaps? Then again, I have bad nightmares the last time I held a belt that said United States Champion on it. All joking aside...you shared the exact mirror image that I did. You broke that glass ceiling. Hell, you didn’t break it, you shattered it into a million pieces and stepped on the remains. Too bad that’s the only thing you’ll share in common with me, if you catch my drift. I would keep on to that belt tight if I were you. You never know when somebody could strike. Not throwing my name into the hat, but...there’s other crazy psychos out there in this company. But now, I have to get on my feet and do something here. Because the last two times, I’ve seen nothing but the ceiling. Certainly it isn’t going to get any better now is it? Of course not. It never does. When you get the amount of stuff on your plate as you’re getting now, you expect to step it up a notch. Everybody’s expecting you to be this great wrestler, this great warrior and within the past month, it’s been nothing but sh*t. Who was the last person I defeated? Devlin Sharpe. Devlin FREAKIN’ Sharpe. Now you know how I feel. And now, I have to face this guy, the returning Ace Cannon? Yeah, it certainly doesn’t get any worse from here on out right? I have to go and face quite possibly the biggest racist and the biggest hypocrite I’ve ever laid eyes on. Sure, we haven’t met in the ring, we haven’t met in the back, hell I haven’t seen him anywhere since I came here. Quite possibly because as I was getting started, he had to make quite possibly one of the stupidest damn moves I’ve seen in quite some time. And I’ve seen and done some stupid things. But not like this. Not like this at all. I mean, in my time I’ve have witnessed some pretty ludicrous promos from some people. People in hot dog suits, people acting like their some form of devil or superhuman beast, the cliched “I’m a big guy so I should squash bitches”, I saw a feud form out from a promo where somebody kidnapped a girl, tied her up, and forced her to watch him strip naked. But never, have I seen a promo so scathing, so senseless, so idiotic. What the hell are you trying to prove to the world, Cannon? That you’re Mr. Tough Guy, that you can do whatever you want, say whatever you want, be some kind of elitist douchebag that only cares about nothing but themselves? What the hell are you then? Well, according to this past month....YOU’RE NOTHING BUT A FLY ON THE WALL! You had the ball in your hand, you were about to go out there and prove to the world that you could’ve been World Heavyweight Champion by beating one of the best in the business. You had everything that you could of ever hoped for in the palm of your hand and look what happened? You threw it down, and stomped on it because you wanted to feed your damn ego. You wanted to feed and look what it did. Your title shot? Vanished. Replaced by a mid-carder that got a chance to prove himself to the entire world and that’s what he did. Your dignity and your respect? It’s now gone out the window. Believe me, I did some things and had to earn my trust back, but in my honest opinion, I don’t think you’ll ever get that trust back as long as you live. Love them or hate them, there are people out there that probably respect you and guess what, it’s gone now. And as you’re coming back, people are proving themselves to this business. People like Eric Derf, who went toe to toe with Brian F’N Michaels just recently, people like Nic Kraven who is on the biggest roll of his life, people like Sinc Mercier, who after months and months, finally achieved his dream. And of course...people like me, who still has something to prove. The second I stepped foot back into this company, my road to redemption started. You’re looking at somebody who was shot down, dragged, kicked, and beaten around and still got the hell up. Seriously, who’s laughing now? Nobody is. They’re all stuck in la-la land waiting for a bus. Last Monday, I took the next step up those stairs. I proved to the entire world that while I lost, I still could hang with the best that this world has. And that’s easy to say when compared to you, Cannon. You fell off that ladder and you’re about ready to take the longest, slowest climb back up. You’re that fly on the wall. Ace, you don’t know that I’m just getting started here and already people are calling me one of the fastest rising rookies in the HISTORY of this company. I know my limits, dammit. I know this ain’t the GWC, I know you aren’t DeAngelo Williams, nor Joe Ragnal, nor Brad Kane. I already know all of that. I already know that my time was a couple years ago, and I’m not afraid to deny that everything happened for a reason. But you know as well as I do, that people are still talking about me, people are still noticing me on the streets. Not just for past accomplishments but for actually giving a damn about the business still. Unlike you apparently. The reason there is no hype for your return is simple. Nobody cares about you anymore. You’re a bonafide scum sucking piece of crap on the floor. And maybe by mistake, somebody will step on it and notice you, but it’s then they’ll realize it’s nothing more than a piece of crap. You want to call me “overrated”, “hooky and cheesy”, “Penny Hardaway”...wow, real original there. How about I use some one-liners to describe you. How about “liar”, “self-centered”, “egomaniacal”, “douchebag”. Is that better? Does that make you laugh? People like you piss me off. People who think they’re still worth a damn when in reality, they shouldn’t even be around. Your time is officially over, Cannon. No more. Somebody else is doing that around here. Have no idea who, but somebody took your spot. It makes absolutely no sense as to what you’re saying. I’m not with this so-called “kliq” that you mention with Michaels, or D.C., or any people like that. Why would they want to be in that “backstage kliq”? Like I said, it makes no sense. And guess what? It isn’t going to get any better for you, you understand me? You may think you have nothing to lose if I beat you, but you actually do. Sure, I lose to Ace Cannon...but if you lose, you lost to what you think is the “biggest overrated piece of sh*t in wrestling today”. Now THAT would be a farce for you. Time’s up, Mr. Cannon. I think a job at the DMV has opened up for you. Kevin decides to get up, finally done with what he had to say, as he slowly begins to walk, slowly beginning his long walk back to the hotel, back to his family, back to Angie. Whatever is waiting in the wings for him, one can only hope. Whatever it is though...god only knows. Fade.
