The return, or lack thereof; David Whitman RP

Aug 8 2008

David Whitman

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
D: Alex Bowen? BG Mancini? Jekyl? Dude, I don’t know half these people! J: Last time I checked, that wasn’t a problem at all. David Whitman is sitting in a diner with his cousin James Whitman, who wrestles for another company. Also sitting with them is their friend Zack Evermore, who also wrestles for James’ company. David: Yeah but look at this… The fans like Michaels? My old buddy Sonny has gone over to the dark side?! What’s going ON!? Zack: David, David, David… Life is a journey. Almost everything has to change. All you can do is hold your breath and jump into the water. David and James look at Zack. David: Uh…? James: Oh, he’s just really really high right now. David: Ah. David takes a sip of his coffee as James takes a swig of beer. David: I don’t know. I don’t feel like this is going to be- James: You gonna start with that crap again?? Look, it’s going to be an amazing night. You’re being inducted into the Hall of Fame- David: By an ex-foe and an ex-partner who has turned his back on the fans- James: Details, details. Anyways, you also have a shot at the World title! And on your first day back! David: Against nine other wrestlers. James: Wasn’t it the same thing a few years ago when you won the first time? David: Yeah, and what do you think the chances are that history’s gonna repeat itself? Zack: History- David & James: SHUT UP! Just as David goes to smack Zack upside the head, the diner door swings open. It’s Eddie Sanchez, James’ stable-mate (wrestling stable, don’t get any weird ideas). Eddie: GUYS! I found Alejandro! David jumps up and points to the door. David: LET’S ROLL! James, Zack and Eddie look at him. James: Dude, that was SO lame. Eddie: I’m embarrassed for you and your family. Zack: Yeah what are you, high? David hangs his head low as they walk out and towards the car. A few minutes later David: STOP DRIVING LIKE A LUNATIC! Eddie: Then stop shouting like one! Eddie Sanchez suddenly stops the car. Eddie: THERE! Inside the gym. Alright, Zack, you take this camera and you go shoot some footage of him practicing. Zack takes the camera, nods, and gets out. Eddie: Wait, WHAT?! THAT’s why you had me stalk this guy? I thought we were gonna go kick his @ss, SOPRANO STYLE! James: No, I think David just wants to know how his opponents are doing. David: Yeah. Eddie: Well why this guy? David: Well any of them would have been okay, really, but I just wanted to give you the one that I thought was gonna be the easiest. I mean, I know where he trains and all that. James: So uh, now you wanna go find the other eight guys? David: … you know what, you’re right. This whole idea is pretty silly, huh? James: I would have gone with “stupid”. David: I should probably go train myself and stop worrying about these other guys. James: Yep. David: And hey, Cuz? Thanks for not stopping me and telling me to get my head in the game earlier, and letting me waste my and Eddie’s time with this idiotic thing. James: It’s what cousins are for. Besides, it’s fun to see you fail and struggle. James smiles exaggeratedly. David: I hate you. James: Who doesn’t? David: Point. Anyways, let’s get crackin’! Eddie, to my house! STAT! After a few seconds, both David and James look at Eddie, who is looking inside at the training. Eddie: What? Ohm sorry… I just… see so much of myself in that Alejandro guy. A few seconds pass… David & James: GAY! They drive off, as James and David keep pestering Eddie. Back at David’s house, in the gym are, 4 hours later David is lying down on the floor sweating and breathing heavily. James is sitting on a bench eating chips. James: Dude, that’s pathetic. David: Hey, that treadmill is NOT as easy as it looks! James: Yeah, but you spent like 3 hours watching the Godfather and an hour or something drinking beer, you really think 5 minutes of walking in place counteracts that? David: Hey, the beer was YOUR idea! James shrugs. James: What can I say; I love to see you fail. David: I hate you unbelievably much. James: See this is why our family gatherings are always a big success. Suddenly the door opens. It’s Zack Evermore. Zack: Dude, what the f*ck? David: Oh, right, uhm- Zack: No. Just get up. It’s time to get serious. James: I see someone’s come down. James tosses a chip at Zack, who swats at it wildly. Zack: David, do you want a real chance at winning the title or not? David: … Yeah, but the odds are stacked against me. I mean, who’s to say the fans’ll even remember me? And there’s a tag team in this thing, these Bowen guys. And who is this Stone guy?! And Sharpe’s in there, and- James: You know, for a professional wrestler, you’re a real whiner. Why don’t you shut the hell up and train like a man? Get it together, man. David: This coming from the guy who’s tossing chips at people. James tosses a chip at David. James: Only the charred ones, David. Only the charred ones. Zack: Now come on, James. Let him get some practice done. James gets up and tosses the entire bag of chips at David. David: Oh, real nice. James: Hey, and remember, you need to do a press conference later on. Or an interview, or whatever. David: Aw, COME ON! See, this is why- James: Yeah yeah, whine whine whine. Look, you’re going to have to accept the fact that wrestling has changed. You need to get your name out there. Look at me and the JESTers, we were nobody before we got our own shirts and merch. Now we have a movie out, a TV show, etcetera. You HAVE to sell yourself, man. At least a little. How else are the fans going to get to know you? David: … myspace? James: … You’re lucky I’m not drinking coffee. James and Zack leave the room as David sighs, gets up, and walks over towards the weights. - --------------------------------- (OOC: Sorry about the lack of formatting but it's just way too hard to format here, especially what with all the changeups in dialog, lol... also sorry if it's not all that good, I'm a bit rusty lol)
