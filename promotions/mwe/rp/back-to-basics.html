Back To Basics; A little old school shit talking...

Sep 13 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
OOC: I have to play the role placed before me... just because I say it... doesn't always mean that this is the opinion of the real me... I gotta say shit that sometimes I just don't mean... and I gotta take shots at people that I have respect for... *The scene opens up to show D.C. sitting in front of a window on a stool. His hoodie is black with a "WWDCD" design on the front. He is wearing black cargo pants with white shoes. His head is tilted down, but covered by a Los Angeles Angels red hat that is turned backwards and slightly off to the side. Lindsey stands beside him in a tattered and patched (that weird ass "trash" design on clothes) white hoodie with torn pants exposing her legs every other gap. Lindsey has white aviator glasses on as D.C. looks up to show his face. His ears sparkle a little as the diamond earrings he wears twinkle in the light. Lindsey puts her tongue down on the inside of her bottom lip and tilts her head towards the camera (that damn thing people do when they think they're better then you.). D.C. tilts his head back as if looking down on the camera.* D.C.: Michaels... for the first time... I admit it... *D.C. looks up at the camera. He has a calm face as he looks into the lens and takes a deep breath.* D.C.: I'm afraid... *D.C. looks around off camera. He shakes his head a little and lets out a slight chuckle.* D.C.: Seriously... I'm kinda of lost as to what even to say to you. I don't even have a reason for keeping this away from you for almost over a year... *D.C. slightly shrugs as Lindsey lowers her head.* D.C.: I honestly don't have a clue, as to how to respond to the promo you cut. I don't even know what to think, about watching you kick Lindsey in the face on MNM... *D.C. looks up at the ceiling as he brings his head back down to look at the camera and turns away. He once again looks around off camera and finally manages to look into the lens.* D.C.: Michaels... *D.C. shuts his eyes for a second, as his voice is a little chocked. He clears his throat as water begins to fill his eyes.* D.C.: Brian... I'm sorry... *D.C. bites his lip as he looks down at the ground again. Lindsey walks up to D.C. still looking at the ground. She forces a smile, sympathetic to fit the mood. But then she nudges D.C.. He finally looks up once again.* D.C.: I'm sorry for everything... for almost ending your career. To setting you up for the past year and half, and making you believe that Mason Resoan was the one that wanted you out of the picture... because when you think about it Michaels... *D.C.'s face changes... it goes from the sad look he had... to a cracked smile. He hops of the stool he was sitting on and reaches his arms out to the side. He leans into the camera.* D.C.: WHO THE F*** ELSE COULD IT HAVE BEEN????? *D.C. seems to have a new beam of light in him as he lets out a chuckle that echoes throughout the room they stand in.* D.C.: Seriously... if you f***ing fell for all that s*** that I just said... your a f***ing retard. *D.C. lets out another chuckle as he walks a little close towards the camera.* D.C.: I don't give two s***s and a f*** if you love me or hate me. I don't give a f*** if you think, that what I did, was the foulest thing a human being could do. You all, can take a sugar coated frosted f***, off the tip of my d***. *D.C. flips off the camera and grinds his hand in front of his crotch.* D.C.: Because Michaels, since day ONE, in MWE, I told everyone, EVERYONE, that includes you.... *D.C. points at the camera with a "yeah... you too" look on his face.* D.C.: That I was going to do everything I could, to get the very best out of people. So Michaels, I haven't lied about a damn thing. So don't sit there and tell me, that all I had to do was ask you, to get a shot at you. F*** that... that isn't me... that isn't D.C.. *D.C. cracks a giant smile on his face.* D.C.: Selfish? Patience? Brian... come the f*** on... since when have you EVER known me to have a lick of f***ing patience. I mean, lets face it... who was the one that was going out and starting ninety nine point nine percent of the fires in MWE???? *D.C. points to Lindsey and then points at the camera waiting for an answer. He points outside of the window. He points to himself with a surprised look.* D.C.: If I DIDN'T want this to drag on for so long... I would have told you it was me that ran you over, right about the time you tried to bury me... OHHHHHHH remember that little ditty??? *D.C. lets out a big laugh as he hunches in front of the camera once again.* D.C.: I know you do, "Brian Covell". Because you handed me your Main Event ticket. You told me to take the ball and run with it. Selfish??? YOU HANDED ME THE KEYS TO THE CAR!!! NO PUN INTENDED, THANK YOU VERY F***ING MUCH!!!.........If you were stupid enough to give me the opportunity, then I was gonna laugh my way to the top. And I did. And its because of you... *D.C. flips the camera off again.* D.C.: F*** MWE!!! *D.C. brings his hand back and grabs his belt buckle.* D.C.: Michaels, YOU KNOW I'M RIGHT! MWE wouldn't be S*** without D.C.. Because... anyone? Anyone? *Lindsey raises her hand. D.C. points to her and waits patiently. Lindsey stands up straight and tall and clears her throat.* Lindsey: Cause he is better then anyone of the little s***s you got on the roster... *D.C. snaps his fingers and thrusts his hand in the air.* D.C.: BINGO B****! I have no reason to fear you... or anyone else on your roster... I mean, for f***s sake, LOOK AT YOU!!!! *D.C. holds a hand out towards the camera.* D.C.: YOU USED TO BE THE CAREER STOPPER! THE HEART BREAKER! THE ACE OF SPADES! THE KING OF HEARTS! You used to be THE MAN, or so we thought, THE MYTH, or so we hoped, THE LEGEND, we.... all kind of expected you to be... but now? *D.C. gets a disgusted look on his face.* D.C.: Your a b****... a whining, pissing, moaning, crying, little f***ing emo chick... You went from being hardcore... to being the next guest on Doctor Phil... *D.C. lets out a loud chuckle as he looks around amusingly.* D.C.: S***, if I'd have known hitting people with cars turned them into emo chicks I'd have ran Windy Bridges off the road to save myself the Canadian version of the Blue Collar Comedy Tour. *Lindsey lets out a chuckle as D.C. raises a finger at the camera.* D.C.: And Jekyl??? *D.C. leans in towards the camera holding his arms out as if saying "come on, seriously?".* D.C.: A retarded sword swallowing clown who cut his cheeks by choking on "The Big One". *D.C. looks around puzzled. He turns to Lindsey who lets out another chuckle as D.C. looks back at the camera. He puts a hand forward.* D.C.: OR BETTER YET! KANTO! How the f*** can I be afraid of a man, who would dangle a tampon in front of a camera, to show the people watching what dripping blood looks like. I'd rather slam my d*** in a bathroom stall then listen to him pretend to be a coffee shop college kid who thinks his poems actually "send a message". Kanto, I fear herpes on a door knob more then you. *D.C. lets out a chuckle as he looks towards Lindsey. Lindsey laughs as she walks her way across the floor. The camera followers her as she walks towards a refrigerator, she opens it up and looks in it for a second. She grabs a can of Mountain Dew that was resting in there and cracks it open taking a drink. She walks back towards D.C.. She takes a seat on the stool that D.C. was sitting on at the top of the promo.* D.C.: BG??? The Meatball Monster!!! Was Frank Soprano booked???? Aiden Copane???? I don't know what shocked me more... him offering your a blow j- I mean "protection"... or the fact that that f***er was still alive!!! Alejandro... must have been fired from Taco Bell... and David Whitman... re-hab closed early I guess... Sonny Williams is a HEEL???? WHOA!!! What the f*** happened!!! Did he finally realize his rap skills amounted to Vanilla Ice at the Apollo!!! *D.C. gives a sturdy thumbs up to the camera.* D.C.: Way to go Michaels... you managed to rustle up the biggest f***ing waste of space ever assembled in a wrestling federation, and slapped MWE's name on it... *D.C. again pumps his hand and gives thumbs up again. He rolls his eyes and leans back a little bit letting out an irritated cry.* D.C.: OH I'M SURE TIM WOULD BE PROUD!!! *D.C. looks up at the ceiling.* D.C.: I didn't want to come here, because I have absolutely no business here... I didn't HAVE to tell you that I ran you over. Because even if I didn't kill your career THEN... staying here would surely kill your career NOW... *D.C. stands up straight and points a finger at the camera.* D.C.: But you crossed a line Michaels... *D.C. eyes look up for a second as he thinks about what he just said. He cracks a smile.* D.C.: OKAY OKAY... AFTER I CROSSED THE FIRST LINE!!! *D.C. nods and turns around to face Lindsey and shrugs, she closes her eyes and nods in agreement and takes another sip of Mountain Dew from the can.* D.C.: You kicked Lindsey, almost jacking her jaw from her face, and then you looked at me, like you REALLY didn't expect me to do anything about it... *Lindsey lets out a chuckle as she stands up and walks up to D.C.. She wraps an arm around him and looks into the camera.* Lindsey: Brian... I don't take offense to you kicking me in the face. We all know why you did it... *D.C. gets a confused look on his face as he turns towards Lindsey. Lindsey looks at D.C. for a second and then back at the camera.* Lindsey: He did it because I'm just prettier then his girlfriend... *D.C. looks at Lindsey with an almost pissed, but slightly amused look on his face.* D.C.: Are you trying to make me look like Gus?? *D.C. and Lindsey stop for a second and freeze frame themselves, they both look at the camera slowly.* D.C.: ANYWAY!! *D.C. reaches up and grabs the side of the camera. He begins to walk past dragging the lens across the way to follow him. D.C. passes by a picture, he doesn't look at it as he just randomly smashes it with a fist. A smash is heard from behind as the camera turns to see Lindsey tossing the stool she was sitting on through the window they were standing in front of. D.C. continues to walk around a corner as the camera struggles to catch up. D.C. comes across a room that has black all over the place. D.C. turns his around and looks at the camera with a puzzled look as he walks into the room. The camera stays back and turns to the right to see Lindsey walk into view holding a hammer that she is using to place holes in the wall like a connect the dots puzzle. The camera comes back over to show D.C. walking out of the black room with a weird look on his face. He raises his eyebrows and lets out a whistle. Lindsey walks to him tossing the hammer through another smaller window in the room. She kisses D.C. on the lips wrapping her arms around him.* Lindsey: Anything else? D.C.: Yeah... *D.C. nods his head towards the black room as Lindsey walks towards it. D.C. looks at the camera.* D.C.: Michaels... with all the joking in the world aside. I can't wait to face you. For the first time in a long time... I'm actually psyched about a match. You and me BOTH, have beaten some of the biggest names in the sport. And each time, became bigger, and brighter stars. To say that I've lost that aura, to say that I've lost my edge... TO SAY THAT I DON'T HAVE BALLS!!! *D.C. lets out three sharp "tisk tisk" noises from his mouth.* D.C.: Have you really forgotten who your dealing with??? No balls??? Brian everything about your promo was TOTALLY wrong. I haven't lost a God damned thing. You on the other hand, have lost your f***ing mind! Maybe that car crash did something to you, because obviously your memory isn't filling in the blanks... *D.C. kinda slides his head in a circle, he holds his arms out to the side.* D.C.: Who the f*** is Brian Michaels to D.C.? *D.C. lets out a chuckles as he looks around.* D.C.: And this shit, about proving you to, that I'm not... Underrated? *D.C. sucks some air in like he just got a skinned knee (hard to describe).* D.C.: Nope... Strike two Brian, you may want to get your head checked... because its.... *Lindsey comes back into the picture behind D.C..* Lindsey: Overrated and Hated... *D.C. curls his bottom lip and points to his head and points to Lindsey. He gets a regular look on his face.* D.C.: I hope you live up to the promise though. I hope you keep getting back up. I hope to f***ing HELL, that you keep reaching down into your soul, to keep fighting... too keep coming back... TO KEEP STANDING UP AFTER BEING KNOCKED DOWN... TO KEEP BOUNCING BACK, FROM EVERY SINGLE SHOT FIRED!!! *D.C. takes his hat off and gets right up close to the camera.* D.C.: BECAUSE I DON'T WANT THE CRY BABY B**** THAT YOU'VE BECOME TO SHOW UP AT TURMOIL!!! I DON'T WANT THE SOFT SIDE OF BRIAN MICHAELS!!! *D.C. waves a hand in the air, almost as if pointing to a wide audience that isn't there.* D.C.: The slew, the masses, and the fans that will fill that arena, want nothing more. Then to watch, these two icons, that the business has placed in front of them, tear each other apart. *D.C. holds his hand out reaching towards the right side.* D.C.: The Judas... D.C.. *D.C. reaches out towards his left side.* D.C.: Against the Martyr, Brian Michaels. *D.C. smiles at the camera.* D.C.: Gives you goosebumps, don't it? *D.C. tilts his head to the side looking for an answer from the people watching as he takes a step forward.* D.C.: I mean, f***, COME ON PEOPLE. WE ARE TALKING ABOUT D. MOTHERF***ING C., VERSUS BRIAN MICHAELS... *D.C. cracks a cocky smile.* D.C.: But it'd be in your best interest, to do what you did today Michaels... because I came to look you in the eye and tell you I'm better then you... *D.C. glances at Lindsey and shrugs his shoulders once again. He turns back to the camera and cracks a classic D.C. smile.* D.C.: But you aren't home... *Lindsey holds up what remains of a picture of the S.S. Rated X and a charred remains of a picture showing D.C. in front of Alejandro's car.* D.C.: Remember, who your going against Brian... cause you of all people should know exactly where to find me if you don't want to wait for Turmoil... I'm anywhere you don't expect me to be... sometimes... quite literally... close to home... *D.C. winks at the camera.* D.C.: And if you don't like that... *Lindsey leans in tossing the burnt pictures at the camera.* Lindsey: F*** YOU! *D.C. and Lindsey spit on the camera as Lindsey lets out a loud WOO! and throws her arm in the air. The picture shows a blurry D.C. and Lindsey kicking through a door as light shines through it, as the scene fades to black.*
