OOC1: Most of this RP was written throughout all of last week. The plan was that I would keep a journal mixed with my real life events, mixed in with the pretend life of Eric Derf. It's different than most RPs, as opposed to going with a mix of Dialog and description, it's almost all inner dialog. *** OOC2: Read "Eric Derfs House Broken Into" before you continue on, for a better idea of whats going on. http://s4.zetaboards.com/MWEntertainment/topic/7627816/1/#new *** MWE.com - As previously reported, there was a break and enter in the home of MWE Superstar Eric Derf. One of the items stolen was a Journal the superstar kept with him at all times. Well, almost all times. That journal has now been leaked online for all the world to see.
