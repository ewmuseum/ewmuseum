MWE 24/7 — 08-09 PPV PR Archives

Brace for impact

May 22 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Devlin Sharpe was sitting in his hotel room typing frantically on his laptop, it was nerve wracking for him because aside from his wrestling career he also had to worry about his education and sparse amounts of free time that he had due to his dedication to bettering his life Do you know why I am angry? Do you think I am not angry after what I’ve heard? Are you fucking shitting me? I mean are you guys fucking serious? You genuinely assume that I am not going to fucking flip out when you tell me shit like this? I mean seriously, apparently based on what I heard I am not good enough for several of the superstars in the locker room to even care about me, well show some damn respect, it's people like me that MADE MWE, we pioneered this company so scum bag like you can earn a decent living, no matter how much you don't deserve it because god damn it you're the biggest fucking jerk I have ever seen, you have no respect whatsoever in your body, do you? He sighed and closed the screen of the laptop as he looked outside the window to the adjacent buildings everything in this area seemed much nicer than what he was used to. I mean sure I may have hit some hard times I may have had a few losses but seriously dude what you are doing to me is just unfair singling me out like that, I mean I know I may have come off as a sore loser and a has been that's whining about the glory days but no I am just here reminding each and everyone of you that without me, MWE would not be what it is today, He stood up and opened the door to leave his hotel room, luckily the lift was across the corridor, so he walked across and pressed the button, he waited for a moment for the lift to arrive and stepped in. I am not trying to be a condescending bastard and call everyone hypocrites of blood sucking leaches because lets face it, I didn't get fired 3 years ago then throw someone through a TV screen or anything did I? No no nope, see I am not a has been that returned and basking in my former glory or shitting on the audience because I realised no one really cared about me like some loser, I actually care for my audience because they seem to care about me or at least that's what I feel when I walk in to that arena each and ever day, believe when I say that the feeling that I get is amazing its unbelievable I mean damn it WHAT A RUSH of adrenaline flowing through my body like WOAH its like an explosion inside my body He continued walking until he reached a bridge which was used to get people across the lake, he stopped at the beginning of the bridge and looked at the other side to the excel centre I just can't wait to perform to entertain to soak up the support that the millions and millions of fans give to me every day to hear them screaming my name cheering me on, you see that single solitary feeling is why everyone in MWE has joined the MWE to get that feeling, they work countless hours to perfect their craft and try to experience that moment for even just a second because that feeling is just bliss utter unadulterated bliss and millions of people all over the world would beg for this chance like my opponents at the pay per view and I myself have been lucky enough to experience this feeling for more than 2 years since the beginnings of this company which is just amazing really. He then slowly strolled to the middle of the bridge before pausing again, collecting his thoughts and observing the people passing by But some people with big heads have taken it upon themselves to declare that they are better than me, that they are above me, due to the fact that they've won more matches or more titles, to be fair those are fairly big accomplishments but they should never forget without me, THEY ARE NOTHING, MWE wouldn't exist without the efforts of the first superstars, if MWE didn't exist would the so called big heads be as famous as they are now? His eyes were fixed at the excel centre as he continued towards it with a slight swagger but the anger was still noticeable so his movements were slowly jagged causing a random walking pattern Would they have the wins, would they have the championships because I garuntee you at the skill level these so called top superstars had at the beginning of their career no other company would take them however MWE took in these happy go lucky losers and molded them and trained them in to the greatest legends in the history of professional wrestling and that ladies and gentlemen is a fact that NOBODY can ignore, if MWE can turn sad sacks of crap like the losers who mock me in to MWE champions, they can turn anyone in to champions . It wasn’t the convention day yet so there were not a lot of people around who might notice that he was acting rather oddly and alert any autorities but to be fair he wasn’t doing any wrong…yet This is what my opponents are hoping to do, all 3 of them promising hopefuls in the MWE however unfortunately I will need to beat them in order to get my title shot which also means that I cannot allow them to win this match if I want my title shot, now even though they may lose this match I am sure there are other matches that these fine wrestlers can be in, in the future as MWE is known as the place where there is always an oppurtunity, you just need to grab it and never let it go and use it to reach the ever lasting glory of an MWE champion. Devlin relentlessly and almost systematically walked towards his target, the one location that had captured his attention for more than 6 monthes See this is what makes MWE unique they help people who want to better themselves if you show promise they look put you in situations that will benefit you and improve you and that is how everyone in the MWE improves so rapidly because in the MWE being active and trying hard will get you noticed and when you get noticed the guys upstairs do whatever they can to thrust you in to super stardom however you have to work extremely hard, make several sacrifices dedicate your life to MWE because if you aren't totally focused in MWE you will be overtaken by someone who is, just like I was for several months. Devlin reached the bottom of the stair case that leads to the excel centre and stopped to calculate the necessary time required in order to reach his target and to catch his breath However I am back and ready to kick ass and take back my place at the top of the MWE pedestal and if that means I have to beat the flying fuck out of my 3 opponents even if it circumvents their career aspirations if it benefits me I might as well do it right? I mean it is after all only one loss for them that will catapult me in to the XTV title scene and with this I will eventually reach the top and be a star just like I always wanted to be because you know that's the only thing I really deserve after years of toil and service for this company. His walking style changes once again as he slowly creeps up the stairs while looking shiftily at anyone who ever dared gaze at his weird actions Don't you think I deserve like at least a pity title shot I mean come on, I have been here since the beginning doesn't that count for anything or oh wait, you want new guys who cut brilliant promos etcetera etcetera well I may not win all the time, I may not even cut good promos but god fucking damnit I am LOYAL to the MWE this is my home and theres nothing on the fucking planet that will ever change that, ever. He was in at the middle of the stairs when he got a headache and paused for a moment as he held his hand to his forehead and massaged it, after a few moments passed he continued onwards I am not like people who constantly leave and come back every few minutes for some crappy cheap pop, I don’t need that I earn my popularity or lack thereof through my actions in the ring not some stupid little publicity stunts, speaking of publicity, let me talk about my opponents for a while you know give them some mention make them known make them feel like people acknowledge their existence, that’s right I mean, Blight, Sin and Anthony Clark Once he reached the top of the stairs and imagined what it would be like tomorrow with the crowd around him, just like an MWE event You guys have a combined MWE serving record of 14 weeks that’s 66 weeks less than yours truly and that shows to me that my experience outweigh yours, hell it would take all 3 of you to work together in order to try and beat me which of course you wouldn’t be able to, everything you guys learnt in your wrasslin’ schools of yours, I probably know 1004 variations of each moves that you know. He looked to his left and noticed the fox bar with a few of its patrons inside and a large screen TV showing an MWE rerun Brian Michaels vs. Devlin Sharpe, he shakes his head because of the bad memories associated with that match You know how I know?, I have had so many opponents, I have studied their styles I have incorprated my styles in to my style I have adapted to become a superior wrestler and you must understand Blight blight oh Blight so bright, that its an absolute Sin for me to lose to Anthony Clark or the rest of you wannabes that think that they can defeat me after winning a bunch of matches, you know why you won the matches kids? They give you easy matches to gauge your ability then they hit you with a brick wall such as myself, well get ready guys, brace for impact Devlin turned around a punched hole in to the wall of the convention room causing the security to look at him, Devlin walked away while apologising but as soon as he turned the corner, he arched whole body back and started cackling with manic laughter
