The truth is messy; Shocking RP 0_0(feedback for feedback)

Sep 11 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Dim lights flick on as the camera pans around in an empty theater with dozens upon dozens of seats, red, velvet curtains, a large black wood stage, and a balcony two stories up inside the dome like theater. Amidst of the panning is Sinc sitting in the front row bottom center of the theater in front of the stage, staring blankly into nothingness. On top of the stage is a single, dusty, wooden stool as the spotlight shines upon it from the balcony. The camera stops panning and begins to fix on Sinc in a slight zoom. He is wearing a white T shirt, black cackies, and black wingtip shoes. The same black suited, bald, bulky man from last week is there with him, sitting next to him, with the briefcase. The camera zooms on Sinc a bit more as Sinc continues staring blankly. After a few moments he finally looks into the camera with content. He then claps lightly in consistence. Sinc: Bravo Alex. Bravo. You managed to lose yet again. I am proud of you. Sinc’s bodyguard with the briefcase coughs purposely. Sinc: Sorry…I know…once again, sarcasm. Sinc shrugs. Sinc: Sorry Alex I didn’t mean to trick you there pal…I just think there is no difference between you and Sullivan. Really, truly. Except for the make up part, and the obsession with scorpions, and the…oh hell. Who am I kidding you two are COMPLETELY different. But wrestling wise, your both the same to me. No skill, no talent, and like that cheesy dating show on MTV, all I say after it’s all over is, NEXT! The bodyguard laughs a little. Sinc turns to him. Sinc: Well think about it man… Sinc begins counting his fingers in order. Sinc: One, Sullivan is a no good street thug who thinks he’s got what it takes to be a champ, Two, He has lost to Kanto not once but TWICE! Once in a tag match and once in the singles match last week. Oh man I remember that highlight, BRUTAL. Just plain brutal. And three he can’t wrestle for sh*t. But that’s just my opinion. But remember it’s…. Sinc stands up. The bodyguard looks at him in confusion. Sinc: SIIIINCCCCCC MERCIIIIIIIERRRRRRR’s opinion…. His voice echoes through the entire theater as his echo then fades after a few seconds. He sits back down. Sinc: And most people respect my opinion Sullivan. And I hope you come to respect it too, it’s a very good source for the truth, because I am a VERY truthful man. The bodyguard nods and Sinc smiles. Sinc: Now my match with you I simply cannot worry about. I have too many things on my mind such as, getting my car washed, walking my dogs, watching the football game on Sundays, and relaxing in my hot tub. See Sullivan? Way too many things to worry about your sorry ass right now. It just doesn’t fit the criteria on my to do list, and to do, I mean to do every girl you have ever been with, if you have even ever been with a girl that is. As you can tell I like to be ARROGANT, I like to SHOW OFF, and I like to use my CHARM, in every and any situation I can. That’s is how you get a fan base Sullivan, that is how you make it in this business SULLIVAN that is how you be a GOD DAMN MAN…. Sinc stands up again. Sinc: SULLIVANNNNNN!!!!!!!! His voice once again echoes through the theater. It fades and again he sits. Sinc: You and everyone else watching this are probably wondering why I am in a theater right now. Well because here in The San Francisco Culinary Theater, is where I did my very first stand up comedy show. This was right before I entered MWE, and after doing local gigs at bars and clubs with my father. I bring this up because I could have chose this path instead of a wrestling career, but I knew what my father wanted, and what I want. Deep down I envied wrestling with a flaming passion and I went for it. I can’t see you doing that Sullivan I just can’t picture it. You say your fearless, you say your strong. Guess what….Alex said the same thing and we ALL know what happened to him. Same thing will happen to you buddy boy. The exact…same…thing. Let me make you realize what I am saying more. Sinc gets up and walks up the stairs to the stage. His bodyguard remains seated. He sits on the stool of the stage and stares into the empty corridors. Sinc: There was once a dude named James Sullivan. Trained by some unknown loser named Stephen Arthur. They trained and trained until they grew exhausted. James With a whole THREE WIN STREAK, amazing as it sounds, Sullivan comes to MWE only to lose, lose, and lose some more. Sure the crowd likes him, his so called “Fearless” ways and what not. But then came a man named Kanto, someone who later whipped his ass, made him black, blue, and red all over, and then made him cry in a corner to his trainer Stephen. He cried and he cried, and he never stopped. “Stephen….I thought I was strong…I thought I was fearless…I THOUGHT I COULD DO ANYTHING LIKE SUPERMAN ON THE TV! You lied to me…you lied to me…I hate you Stephen I hate you!” Then I came in the room and said hey man, don’t cry. Just quit MWE, have a bite of some fruitcake, and never return. He never took my advice, now he regrets it, and now he is going to get the beating of his life. Thank you. The bodyguard claps as Sinc exits the stage and goes back to his seat. Sinc: Thank you…thank you. Truly appreciate it. Sinc looks to the camera again. Sinc: Sullivan…listen to me very clearly…I say all that stuff because you talk like a two year old, a kid! Sinc attempts to try and sound like a kiddish Sullivan. Sinc: I love my teacher Stephen Arthur because he taught me so much…and he gave me a lollipop…and we played hide and seek together…and got ice cream..and and.. Sinc stops in an immediate change of attitude as he quickly makes a bored look on his face. Sinc: Please dude….that isn’t going to fly around here. There is only one person you can trust and count on, and that is yourself. If you don’t become independent and self reliable, you aren’t going to make it in this business. This is not shooting the sh*t around this is real life. Real business. Real entertainment. Real dedication. If you don’t have what it takes, find the nearest door, walk out of it, find the nearest fast food joint, apply, and start flippin those patties boy because sooner or later those things will fry and be ready for the next fat man who is on his way to a faculty meeting that you will NEVER be apart of. Imagine a rollercoaster full of lard and office supplies, and imagine yourself under it all in the back of the coaster, trying to fasten yourself in. Because your falling buddy, your falling if you think you can make it. And when you fall your going to fall HARD. Suddenly the entrance doors to the theater room swing open. A sweaty man with a red vest, glasses, a white buttoned shirt, black jeans, and brown loafers walks in with balding hair. He looks both worried and fearful. Sinc and his bodyguard look at him confused and a bit angry. Sinc gets up and marches to him as the camera follows him. His bodyguard doesn’t move. Sinc: There better be a DAMN good reason why your interrupting me. Sinc whispers to the man with a smug on his face. Man: Well as theater manager I must uhm…I don’t know how to put this…but I must inform you your ex-wife wanted me to tell you that she is on the roof of the theater waiting for you. Sinc: What? Sinc looks at his bodyguard. Sinc: Stay here. Manager: Yea she said- Sinc marches away ignoring the manager with anger in his eyes and his mouth muttering mumbled words that aren’t really heard on camera as the camera follows Sinc swinging open the doors and down a long, red carpet hallway filled with old play ads and movie posters. The camera shifts to a front view of Sinc walking up, long, concrete steps in a hollow corridor up to the roof. He opens a door to the roof and stops in shock as he sees his ex-wife on the ledge of the roof. The camera zooms out moving around to get both people in the frame and stops, fixing the frame on them. Sinc: Barbara what the hell are you doing!?! Barbara: Dammit! You said you would stop dating girls and make it work with us you dick! Your still doing it and your still running your arrogant mouth on TV like your careless about everything around you. I am SICK of it. I can’t TAKE IT anymore! Sinc: What makes you think I am not willing to make us work again? Barbara: The phone calls from random girls each night….The “You were great in bed” messages each day. Your pathetic Sinclair Mercier, pathetic! Barbara is a rather tall woman wearing a brown business top completed with a short black skirt and black high heels. She has long blonde hair and brown eyes topped with pink nail polish. Sinc: Woah woah woah….We live in separate houses now and I changed my number…I doubt their anyone I know. Plus we are still broken up…I thought you hated me after I cheated on you. Barbara: Oh f*ck off will you! I know what you’ve been doing. And I told you I would try and forgive you! But you blew it! Don’t lie…don’t try and hide it a**hole! We are done now! And because of you I have to do this! Sinc: Honestly I don’t know what your talking about…I don’t even know why they called your phone and not mine…Oh sh*t…forget I said that. Barbara: MOTHER F*CKER! I HATE YOU! Barbara looks down from the ledge at the cars passing by and the street lights gleaming in the night. The moon is half full in the night sky as it is surrounded by bright stars and a hazy fog. Sinc: Now Barbara come on now babe…you don’t want to do this…don’t do this…We can work it out Barb. Come on now. Sinc slowly steps toward her. The camera shifting between him and her every now and then. Barbara: Don’t you dare get any closer! Sinc stops. Sinc: Come on be realistic here. Don’t do this especially on live TV! People are going to see you do this stupid sh*t Barbara for christ sakes don’t do it! Barbara: Is that all you care about Sinc? Your PEOPLE! Is that ALL you care about! Sinc: No, of course not. What do you want me to do to make this better? Huh? What? Barbara: I want you to stop god damn LYING to ME! I want you to stop sleeping with every woman you come across! Sinc: I will ok I promise. I am sorry ok…I really am. Barbara: Those women aren’t even women…their all whores! All of them! Sinc: I wasn’t dating them honey they were crazy friends of mine ok we didn’t take it far! And they weren’t whores. Barbara: yea right f*cker! And they were whores just by their voices I could tell! Barbara balances herself on the ledge looking down again. Sinc: Come on…please…don’t do this. Please. Barbara: Why…you don’t want to put on a show for your fans? With that f*cking camera there! Sinc: You want me to turn it off? Barbara doesn’t reply still looking down. Sinc: Ok listen listen I will turn it off ok? Just for you babe. Sinc turns to the camera man, then the camera, and forces it to fade to black. The sound was still heard. The camera man is heard saying “What the hell man…”slightly and the sound of bumping and static is slightly heard. The sound continues rolling. Sinc: Ok look babe? The camera’s off. See? Look. Barbara: You’re a f*cking liar! Sinc: It’s off dammit it’s off! Barbara: Ok fine. It’s off so what. Still doesn’t change you does it? DOES IT! Sinc: Just get down…and we will work this out...Just you...and me... Barbara: We will never be able to work this out Sinc…I loved you…I LOVED you… Sinc: I know I know…please get down…please… Barbara: Do you know what love means? Do you really even know? Sinc doesn’t say anything for a moment. Barbara: Thought so… Sinc: Ok maybe I still need to learn a few things about “love” but I can make it work for you and for us I can change! Barbara: You had your chance….You f*cked it up! You will NEVER change! Suddenly the sound of heels shuffling across cement was heard. Followed by Sinc’s worried voice. Sinc: Why…WHY WOULD YOU DO THIS TO ME! The audio stops as a click is heard. It turns to silence.
