A li'l roughed up, but still kicking ass; Virus MNM RP #1

Jun 12 2009

Leon Roberts

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Leon was very happy to be done with the European tour in MWE. He did travel back and forth between the places, and needless to say, it had affected his performance. Main reason is, was because he just wanted to be with his family as much as possible, but unfortunately, couldn’t take them on the road with him. That in mind, he wanted to celebrate. He was just to hyped up for finally being back full time in America, plus the fact that he could easily chill with his best friend and tag team partner, Vega. Yet he had no plans on going to a bar to celebrate. No. Ever since that attempted robbery, plus the first blood match he almost was on the official losing end of, Leon knew that his appetite for destruction had indeed come back to the surface. He needed to do something that would cause some mayhem, in order to sate his appetite. Whether it’s harm inflicted to himself due to some crazy hell raising stunts, or he inflicts pain upon others. Luckily, there was a solution for that. The solution was simple. It was a demolition derby being held just outside of Toronto, in a summer fair. At the current moment, Leon had just finished signing up for some of the competitions, and was looking forward to it. However, there was something else that Leon wanted to do first. He wanted a Ferris wheel ride with Demonica. The night was there, and the stars were showing, and despite all the carnage and anarchy that Leon often preaches about, he has a romantic side to him, and this was one of those times when he just couldn’t help himself. It was a good thing that Vega was there with them. Lately, Vega had been trying to make his two personalities become one, and thus far, it was working. He started to talk like a normal person again. But he was still prone to split personality moments. Leon: Thanks for coming out with us Vega. I really appreciate you looking after the kids and such. Vega: Meh, I was bored anyways. Plus I feel as if I owe you guys anyways. Have fun you two. Leon and Demonica went on the next cart on the ride. Vega sighed. Liberty sensed the distress in Vega, and wanted to know why. Liberty: Uncle Vega, what’s wrong? Vega chuckled to himself. He really didn’t feel all that deserving of Liberty calling him that. But, he wouldn’t deny the sweet little vampire’s wish to call him her uncle. He shook his head, not exactly in the mood to talk about it. But Liberty did know. Liberty: If it’s because of what you did a few weeks ago, you should just let it go. No sense having it haunt you. Vega: Yeah. --With Leon and Demonica-- The ride had suddenly stopped right when they were at the peak position. Demonica was startled due to this, but one look at Leon, who had a really smug look on his face, Demonica knew that it was deliberately stopped. Demonica: You just couldn’t help it, could you? Leon: Nope. What can I say, when you love somebody, you try to make some romantic moments. Well, at least I try to. Demonica blushes at that, before she snuggles into Leon’s chest. This was sort of what Leon was aiming for when he had arrange this deal. Demonica: Yeah. But....don’t you have a match you should focus on? I mean, your opponent this week is not somebody to take lightly. Leon: I know. Hence why I’m doing all of this stuff now, so I can get it out of my system, and focus on Kaymakian. But from what I hear, he’s doomed for failure regardless. Demonica: How so? Leon: His mentor is Brian Michaels. Considering Michael’s history, ol’ Petey is going to be kicked down to jobberville one of these days, if I don’t save his sorry ass by injuring him. Demonica: That’s true. Wait. How is injuring the guy going to help him? Leon: Multiple reasons. First, because he’s new, and needs to be taught a harsh lesson. Two, it will prove that Michaels won’t save his sorry ass. And Three.....because I said it would. Demonica chuckles at that last statement. She leans up, and kisses Leon on the lips, as the ride begins to come down. They keep kissing, until the ride finally stops. They get off the ride, and head towards the derby. Just before the derby started, Demonica had given Leon one more kiss, for good luck. Demonica: Now you be careful. Despite what you are, you could still get hurt. Leon: I know. But hey, no pain, no gain, right? Demonica: True. It's just...my instinct are just telling me I should tell you that. Leon smiled, before he kissed Demonica. he opened up the car door, and stepped in, and closed the door. Demonica immediately left, so she could watch the derby. It would take her about five minutes to meet up with Vega and the kids. Just in time as well, as the derby had started. It didn’t take long for all the junk cars to go and crash and bash, and slam into each other. Vega: Well, looks like this is entertaining. Totally can understand why Leon wanted to do this. Demonica: Yeah. After a few minutes of watching, there was a nasty crash for one of the cars, that sent the driver flying. Demonica gasped at that. For the car that was brutally ‘gang banged’ by the other cars, was labeled ‘666’ That was Leon’s car. Demonica: LEON!!!!!!!!! Liberty was shocked when she heard Demonica shout out like that. Liberty was even more shocked over the fact that her father had been pulverized like that. Liberty immediately began to run through the stands. Demonica decided to follow. Vega sighed, knowing he would have to watch the kids again. Demonica and Liberty managed to get ast the security and ran to where Leon was. Despite under a helmet, the two ladies knew Leon was knocked out. Ambulance managed to arrive, and got Leon onto the Gurney. He began to stir. Leon: AWWWW FUCK!!!! THE PAIN!!!!!!! GODDAMMIT this is fucken BRUTAL!!!!!!!!! The medics loaded Leon into the ambulance, as he was still screaming in pain. Liberty could not stand the sight of her father in such pain, that it was making her cry a bit. Sure, she’s seen him in some brutal wrestling matches, but at least there Leon had more control and could easily fight back. This was not the case, and for the first time since she could remember, Liberty was actually down right scared. Liberty: Isn’t there anything that you guys can do to make him not be in so much pain? One of the medics, who’s name was jim (it was sewn into his shirt) sighed, and pulled out a medical kit, just as they loaded Leon into the ambulance. Jim pulled out a syringe, and loaded it with some clear liquid. Jim: Guess we could give him some sedatives. But this stuff we have here is pretty strong. Worst case scenario is that he’ll be knocked out for a couple of days. Demonica: Don’t care. Just do it. He has a high tolerance towards drugs, so for him, the worst will probably be hallucinations. Jim: Okay. We’ll give him some on the way to Toronto General. Demonica: Okay. The ambulance closed the doors, and drove off. Demonica grabbed Liberty, and they ran back to Vega and the triplets. The triplets were still asleep when the two ladies arrived, but Vega was panicking. Vega: How is he? Demonica: He’s in pain alright. Vega: Dammit. This is almost as bad as the last time he was taken to a hospital. It was when he was shot down. Demonica: Damn. That is bad. Anyways. We better get there, to make sure he’s alright. Vega: Yeah. I’m going to meet you guys there. His dad will want to know about this. Demonica nodded, as she grabbed onto the triplet’s stroller, and pushed them, heading to the car. --Two hours later-- Demonica, Liberty, and the triplets were in Leon’s room. He was currently asleep, after the sedatives finally did their actual job of getting him to sleep. That was when a doctor came in. He was about a 5'11 blond male, and looked to be around twenty seven. Doctor: Well Mrs Roberts, it seems like he’ll be fine. Just a few bruised bones and muscles, nothing to serious. Mostly it was shock that was causing him the pain. But from what I heard on the way here, he was doing some really screwed up version of ‘Staying Alive’. Liberty and Demonica couldn’t help but blink in confusion in what the doctor said, before they burst out laughing. It would take a minute for the ladies to calm down, but they did, both somewhat smiling at the comment made by the doctor. Doctor: I’m not joking. But I hope you don’t mind, but I would like to keep him here overnight, just as a safety precaution. Also, just in case, we’re going to be giving you some of the sedatives just in case he does feel any pain within the next week or so. I really don’t want him to miss his match. Demonica: That’s good to-Wait. You know about my husband’s wrestling match? Doctor: Yeah. I got the ‘I mark out for Black List Mafia’ T shirt, along with the ‘Hellbound’ Skull cap. I’m so looking forward to him kicking Kaymakian’s ass. Demonica: Well, I’m gonna have to tell him about it. The doctor smiled, just before he left. Demonica sighed, sitting down. She smirked to herself. The last time she was in a hospital, was when she gave birth. Like Leon is doing, she stayed overnight, as Leon was right by her side the whole time. --Next day-- Leon was released from the hospital, and was taken home. Currently, he is actually playing Guitar Hero: Metallica, with Liberty. He insisted on being the singer. Except he didn’t do to well, as he was high off the sedatives again. Obviously, Demonica predicted this was going to happen. Leon: Damn, this is some good shit. Liberty rolled her eyes at that as Leon began to badly sing again. Demonica could only chuckle at that. She sighed, as she got herself a beer. She was having a drink with Vega, as he had come over. He had shared more of the story of when Leon was shot down. Demonica sighed at that. But she decided she needed to know what drove Vega to attempt to hurt her. Demonica: Vega. You’ve always been honest with me. Can you just please tell me why you tried to rape me? Vega looked at Demonica for a second, and blinked. He then sighed. He knew She was going to ask sooner or later. But he didn’t feel comfortable talking about it. It was a good part of why he was trying to re-merge his personalities. so naturally, he tried to change the subject. Vega: Well.......You know.........Transformers two looks awesome. Looks like it’s going to kick a lot of ass in the theaters. I got some tickets already reserved for it. Demonica sighed, pressing two fingers against her head. Her patience level had dramatically decreased. She should have tried to convince Leon to not do the derby, or he wouldn’t be acting like a total jackass. Vega looked at Demonica, and saw she was not going to accept his answer. Vega: Look. It’s a real...........sensitive subject for all of us, so you can understand why I’m so uncomfortable talking about it. But...if you insist, it was because for many reasons. One, you are amongst the few truly beautiful goddesses left in this decaying world, and the douchebag part of me just wanted to be with you......then there was my darkside, who obviously tried to take things a bit further. So yeah, to sum it up, I have a crush on you, but I know your untouchable, and I accept that. When he said that, Leon suddenly staggered into the kitchen, looking glazed over with a big, albiet crooked smile, on his face. He was still high off of the sedatives. Leon: Heyyyyyy you two. Damn I’m feeling soooooo awesome right now. It feels like I could......take a piss in holy water, and not give a fuck. Wait, that don’t make any sense. Because I wouldn’t give a fuck if I pissed in holy water anyways. Hell, all I’d really be doing is....giving my owwwn blesssing....heheheheheh. Leon began to madly chuckle, as he staggered towards the two. He had the sedatives in his hand. He stumbled forward a bit, and Vega caught him before Leon could hit the floor. However, Leon would immediately jab the syringe into Vega’s arm, followed by pressing down, to administer the drug to Vega’s system. Demonica went wide eyed after seeing that. Demonica: Oh.........shit. Vega began to madly chuckle along with Leon, and even the two began to start moving their heads around, almost as if they were trying to act like Stevie Wonder. Demonica immediately left the room. But she noticed that Liberty was filming all of this. Demonica sighed. He felt a headache coming, so she left, and immediately proceeded to walk upstairs. Demonica: *sigh* With Leon on those damn meds, things have been crazy. Could this day get any worse? Demnica shivered at that thought. She really hoped that there wasn’t, but in situations like these, more bad things will inevitably happen. Demonica was about halfway up the stairway, when suddenly......... Leon: I SEE I LITTLE SILHOUETTO OF A MAN! Vega: SCARAMOUCHE, SCARAMOUCHE, WILL YOU DO THE FANDANGO!!! Leon and Vega: THUNDERBOLT AND LIGHTNING, VERY VERY FRIGHTENING ME!!! Demonica slapped her forehead with her palm after hearing both Leon and Vega make total assess of themselves, by loudly, and badly singing Bohemian Rhapsody. The loudness of it all caused the triplets to wake up and cry. Demonica could hear them crying, and sighed. She quickly massaged her temples, trying to calm down. Demonica: I swear, one of these days, I’m gonna kill those two jackasses. *Sigh* Of all the times I had to be right about something, it turns out to be the single most fucken annoying thing I’ve had to deal with. But at least the little ones will keep my mind off of it for a while. Demonica continued upwards towards the triplet’s bedroom. Just as she entered, she noticed that Vega and Leon were about to hit the head-banging part. Then....she heard them fall to the floor. She rolled her eyes, not wanting to check on them right now. -The Next Morning, around 10AM- Leon woke up in a cold sweat, around noon. His breathing was a bit shallow. He held his head in a bit of pain. After being awake for a minute, his brain began to function properly. He checked his arms and ribs for any damage. While there was some, it wasn’t as bad as he thought it would be. Leon: Fuck. What the fuck happened to me? I remember etting ganged up on in that Derby, and getting roughed up there. But why am I home so soon after that? More importantly, why do I get the feeling that I made a total ass of myself? Leon sighed. It was one of those times he didn’t know the answers, and that is soemthing that always irritated him. He suddenly felt a light tap on his shoulder. He turned around, and received a massive slap by Demonica. Demonica: That Leon, was for acting on your need for destruction and it biting you in the ass. A whole two days of what you normally are supposed to be doing was wasted due to you feeling the after effects of sedatives. Not only did you make a total ass of yourself, but you embarrassed the fuck out of me. Leon: Oh. Damn. So it was all real. Fucken hell. Demonica: Yeah. Look, because of the last two days being high and shit, you’ve been set back. Not by much, but still. People are going to wonder if you’ll be able to make it to the next show. Leon: I will, I can assure you that. In fact......I’ll prove that. Right now. I got something to say to MWE anyways. Demonica sighed, before she nodded, and left. Leon got up, and pulled on a black muscle shirt, and a pair of jeans. Leon then made his way downstairs, and went to his computer. He was going to do a webcam promo. He patiently waited, stretching out his stiff limbs from lack of training. He got the program set up, and began his promo. Leon: Hello fans of MWE. Not sure if this has hit the news yet, but just in case, you must know. A couple days ago, I was involved in a nasty car wreck in a demolition derby. I was roughed up a bit, and to be honest, the last two days have been a total blur to me. Rest assured, I will not be missing any time, no matter who says what. I am not hurt bad enough for me to miss any time, and I doubt that I’ll be any less then perfect condition by the time my match comes around That being said, if you haven’t heard, I am facing what is supposed to be one of MWE’s brightest rising stars, in the form of Peter Kaymakian. I know all about Kaymakian quite well. He was a huge success in ICW, practically ruled the entire place with an iron grip the entire time he was there. However Petey, there is something that you need to understand. This isn’t ICW. Despite your success here and now on the main roster, you are nowhere near a top talent, and the likeliness of you ever reaching that point is very slim. At first, it seemed as if maybe, you had a chance, but now, you have no chance at all. Leon sighed, catching his breath from all that he just said. The look on his face showed a bit of depression, as naturally, he was still a bit shaken up from his ordeal. Deciding to calm himself down, Leon opened up his desk, and took out a cigarette. He placed it in between his lips, and took out a lighter and lit it up, before taking a puff of it. Leon: See Petey, there are many challenges you must face if you want to aim for the top. One of those challenges, is to push your physical endurance to levels you never thought possible. You have to push yourself in a lot of matches against some of the most, fucked up individuals, where it will come to a point in the matches, where you will depend on adrenaline alone. If you can prove that you are just as sick and violent of a fuck as any of us here are, then maybe, you will go far. But then, there is the challenge of you yourself, and the choices that you make. Unfortunately, in this world, there are many things that can screw you over. People who have high positions in MWE, or even somebody very close to you, will chew you up and spit you out like the most rancid tasting piece of meat that anyone has ever tasted. This situation I’m actually talking about, is your partnership with Brian Michaels. The bane of MWE. Petey, I don’t know whether to respect you, or have you committed, because you aligning with Michaels is either one of the most ballsiest acts I’ve seen, or just the single most retarded act I’ve seen. In case if you forgotten, Brian screws over all of his so called prodigies. The only exception to the rule, was DC, but that’s because DC was smart. But yeah, look at people like Devlin Sharpe. That guy, is the official president of Jobberville, mainly because he was screwed over by Michaels. Hell, ask Triple G and Markowitz. Their careers are over thanks to Michaels. Just like yours will be if you continue to side with him. It’s pretty much aguarentee. But I wonder though. Will you take me seriously? A lot of people haven’t, saying that who I am, and what I do, is not real.. Well Petey, those people are short sighted, and I have little doubt you’re one of them as well. I hate those people, and they will get exactly what they deserve. It may not seem like it now, but those short sighted fools, all of them, possibly even yourself Petey, will get crippled by me. Why? It’s because of the simple fact, that of all of the truly messed up individuals in MWE, nobody comes close to my level of violence, and yet, nobody believes it. Taking another drag of his cigarette, Leon blows the smoke from his mouth out. When he does, a sadistic smile forms on his face. We may have seen this smile before, but this one, is a bit scarier then normal. Mostly because his lips are partially parted, and there is also a very faint growl noise coming from Leon. Leon: Because unfortunately for me, MWE management has held me down, trying to constantly prevent me from unleashing my true nature upon everyone. The nature, that loves to torture his opposition. You’re talking to the guy who humiliated Ace Cannon when he somehow managed to become EW champion. You’re talking to a guy who could easily brush off multiple gash wounds upon the entire body, big pieces of glass stuck inside, ribbed by barbed wire, and has his gut torn to shreds by a weed whacker, and stand up the next day almost as if nothing happened. Leon’s right eye begins to visibly twitch, just as Leon begins to chuckle. As always it’s a chilling and scary sight to behold when Leon is acting like this, yet at the same time, just as captivating. After a minute he finally calms down, and takes another drag of his cigarette. Leon: My point is Kaymakian, I’m sick of being held down. Being that, we’re almost upon the summer itself, come our match, it will be the first thing, to show that the Summer of Anarchy 2009 has begun. And it will last for as long as I want it to. No more games. Just blood. Lots and lost of blood, from everyone, flowing on the wrestling mats and all over the world. Courtesy of me. But what will I do with you Kaymakian? Well, I have some ideas, but they won’t be revealed. No sense in making you actually prepared for what will inevitably happen. No. You got enough on your plate, trying to ward off a psycho fan. I don’t feel sorry for you at all man. Leon takes another drag of his cigarette, before flicking the ashes into an empty can of coke that was on the desk. He is still smiling after he takes that drag. The anticipation is definitely building up inside of Leon. He just simply can’t wait for the next show to at last, come around. Leon: So that leaves you in a bad situation Petey. If Michaels doesn’t end your career, I will show you a whole new level of pain, when we fight. Because it has come to a point, where I need to take control of everything. So the question for you Petey, is a simple one. Are you ready for the worst damn night of your life? Well don’t bother answering it, because it’s a rhetorical question. There is no way to prepare for it. All the supposed training will not pay off. I promise you that. If you want to succeed against me, you’ll have to find a strength you never thought you had. I want you, to think of you losing against me, be like death. It’s inevitable. However, sometimes people are known to cheat death. So if you manage to actually get the better of me, well how it happens is still up for debate. So use your remaining time wisely Petey. Make sure that your true girlfriend is fine. Comfort her. Show her the love she deserves. Because every second that passes by, is every second that closes in on what could very well be, the end of your life as a wrestler. But, if you happen to come back very soon after what is coming to you, then I will have gained a whole new respect for you Petey. But we shall see, shall we, eh? Leon took one last drag of his cigarette before butting it out. He switched off the recording, done with the promo for Mayhem. He leaned back in his chair, with a barely noticeable smile on his face. Because despite being a little banged up, Leon felt something he hadn’t felt in a while. A passionate fire, that burned within him, begging for the violence he had promised to unleash.
