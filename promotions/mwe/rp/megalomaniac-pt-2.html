Megalomaniac pt 2

Nov 20 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
OOC: The transation from the first rp too the second happens here. Then half way through there is another transation that happens after Cannon is released from jail. This strictly covers the potential finals match I might be in. **The camera opens up in a dimly lit room. The walls are bare, white and appeared to be padded. In the middle of the room sits a single metal table and a metal chair on either said of the table. No one appears to be in the room until a door swings open and we hear a bunch of commotion and it sounds like a struggle is ensuing. After a few moments of struggling a group of guards finally drag in a man who has his legs shackled at the ankles and he is handcuffed. They take him over to the table and force him to sit down on one of the chairs at the table. The men who appear to be guards walk out and leave the man sitting there. The camera eventually pans around and we see the man shackled and handcuffed at the table is Ace Cannon! Ace sits at the table looking pisses off and staring off at on of the padded walls. As he sits there staring at the wall he continually shakes his head and then begins to yell.** Ace: LET ME THE FUCK OUT OF HERE! ** Ace continues the yelling for a good two or three minutes until the door opens and an older man in a white labcoat walks in. The man appears to be in his early 60’s and losing his hair but what he does have is all gray. He looks at Ace and stops and shuffles through a binder he has in his hand. After going through the binder for a few brief second he pulls out the paper he needs and takes a seat across from Ace at the table.** Ace: Who in the hell are you? Man: I am Dr. Leo Compton. I am the psychiatrist on staff here at the jail. Ace: I don’t need any kind of damn kooky head doctor. Doc: You may not need me but your arresting officer noted that you might. Ace: Well, you can leave now because I don’t and that is a lying piece of shit pig. Doc: He said you told him and I quote “ Will commit mass murder if I am locked up in you fucking jail and I will slit your throat if ever given the chance.” **The doctor lets the what Ace said hang in the air. Ace sits there and laughs to himself.** Doc: Is that funny Mr. Cannon? ** Ace still laughing.** Ace: I find it quite hilarious. Doc: Why do you find that hilarious? **Ace sits there for a moment and digests the last question.** Ace: I am not answering you damn questions. You’re trying to get in my head and I’m not down for that. ** The doctor puts both his elbows on the table and leans in toward Ace.** Doc: Listen, I know who you are. I know you’re a pro wrestler and I know you have a show you must get too by Friday. So if you don’t cooperate with me I will make sure to mark down suicidal thoughts on my report and they will haul your ass down to the psych ward and you will be there till atleast Saturday morning. Now, it will be in your best interest if you get on the same page as me. ** Ace sits back on his chair and just stares at the doctor.** Doc: So are you going to cooperate or should I leave now? **Ace continues to stare at the doctor and doesn’t break his stare or even blink. The doctor shuts his binder and gets up from his chair and heads to the door.** Ace: Damn it! I will cooperate with you. **The doctor stops and turns around. He goes back and sits down in the chair and opens his binder back.** Doc: Good choice Mr. Cannon. Now, I am not even going to ask you about what you said to the cop. He is an asshole and I understand but I want to know why you punched that tow truck driver. Ace: He was towing my fucking car. Doc: I understand that but something must have caused you too snap and I want to know what that is. Ace: Nothing caused me too snap other then that grease monkey towing my car. Doc: I don’t believe that for one second. Something in your head is making you separate yourself from reality. You have something on your mind that drove you too punch the tow truck driver and yes that cop is an asshole but something drove you to say what you said as well. Ace: I’m telling you nothing is wrong! Doc: I don’t see that. Something with your career has driven you to do these things. Do you have any important matches coming up? Ace: A possible world title match this Friday. Doc: Really? Have you done anything else in your career that is out of the norm when something of prestige is on the line? ** Ace sits there and cracks a smile.** Doc: What is making you smile? Ace: Well, in a one week span in the name of the world title I beat a man so bad he was near death and then a couple nights later after I secured a world title shot I kicked a child in the head. ** The doctor begins writing in his binder before he begins to speak.** Doc: Interesting, and you did this because of the world title? Ace: Sure as hell did. Doc: What makes you get so obsessed over the world title? **Ace lifts his shackled hands onto the table and ponders the question for a little bit.** Ace: I like the power, respect and prestige that goes along with being champion. To be honest with you I get satisfaction off having a title then I do having sex with ten of the most beautiful women. Doc: Does a world title define you? Ace: Absolutely. Doc: Interesting… I think you suffer from a mental condition called megalomania. Ace: What in the hell is that? Doc: It is an illness that is marked by delusions of greatness and wealth. ** Ace sits there and lets it sink in.** Ace: Listen doc, with all due respect these aren’t delusions. I know I am great and so does everyone else. **The doctor writes some stuff down in his binder.** Doc: Listen Ace, I would venture to say when you beat that guy within an inch of his life and kicked that child all you saw was the world title. It was what was clearly on you mind and nothing else. That was the delusions playing into your mind. That was the megalomania at work. **Ace sits there and thinks about it before he speaks.** Ace: Listen doc, you might be right but the sad part is I like this feeling. I like being consumed by the thoughts of a world title. Doc: I know you do and that not healthy. I am afraid for your safety and the safety of those around you. Ace: Look doc, lets cut to the chase here.. Are you going to put in a good recommendation for me to be bailed out or not? Because lets be frank here, that’s all that matters here. ** The doctor sits there and thinks about the question Ace has just asked him.** Doc: I am going to suggest that bail be allowed for you. I don’t think you are capable of killing anyone over this title and I think most of the damage you can do will be contained to a wrestling ring. Ace: Good, I need to get out of here and achieve my glory. So can you please go do whatever you need to? ** The doctor gets up from his seat and pushed his chair in and head for the door. He reaches the door and turns the handle and begins to open it before he turns around and says something.** Doc: Ace, I hope your opponents have a good life insurance policies because your consumed with megalomania and what you can do should scare them. ** After finishing his statement he exits the room and Ace sits back in his chair and starts laughing. The camera then fades out on Cannon laughing.** **The camera fades back in and we are seen in a parking garage. There are a few cars around in various parking spaces. The camera pans around and we see a limo coming up the rap toward the camera. As the limo draws closer we see it is your standard limo. The car finally comes to a stop and the driver puts the car in park and gets out and walks around and opens the door for his occupant. As the man exits the car we can tell that is is Ace Cannon. Ace gets out and he is in a black Affliction shirt, jeans and has a pair of black shoes on. In his hand is a duffel bag. He walks around the back of the car and past the camera. The camera follows as he reaches the doors to enter the arena. He walks a little ways down the corridor when Damien Thrope suddenly appears in front of him with a microphone in his hand.** Damien: Ace, can I get a few words with you? The wrestling world is buzzing about you recent arrest. ** Cannon looks at Thrope with an angry look on his face.** Ace: Sure.. Fire away. Damien: Well word has it you were arrested yesterday afternoon for striking a tow truck driver for towing your car from in front of MWE Headquarters. Is this true? Ace: Yea that’s the story. Damien: Where you charged with anything for you crime? Ace: Yea, I was charged with assault and battery. I have a court date sometime after Christmas. Damien: Well with this happening do you think it will affect you in pursuit for the Genesis world title? **Ace laughs at this question.** Ace: Not in the least bit. A little time in jail doesn’t have my mind taken off the ultimate goal. Like I told you yesterday winning a world championship in MWE is my life long dream. It is my obsession. I lay in bed and think about it at night until I fall asleep. I mean when most people are counting sheep so the can fall asleep I am counting world titles. It is that serious for me Damien. Damien: Well with that said do you have anymore comments on American Animal? He put out his promo last night. Ace: I covered him yesterday. I have nothing left to say about him. I made it abundantly clear yesterday that he is not in my league. Damien: Alright then. What about some of your possible opponents from the block A match? Ace: Remind of who those so called wrestlers are. Damien: They are Logan Race and… **Ace interrupts.** Ace: Isn’t Logan Race that new guy? Damien: Yes he is. Ace: Ok, he is the guy from Japan with a rich white boy name isn’t he? Damien: Yea, that would be him. Ace: Well, I spent my time in Japan and never heard of anyone named Logan Race. Much less have I ever seen a guy of Japanese decent have a name like that. But the point is Logan Race is a nobody right now and if he gets to see me at Genesis he will suffer the beating of his life. It will be so bad in fact I can guarantee he will retire from wrestling and take the little bit of money he has earned and open up a Japanese hibachi restaurant so far from a wrestling ring that his time in the business will be nothing but a distant memory. Now continue… Damien: We also have Jason Fear… Ace: He was just named lead character in MWE’s first movie wasn’t he? Damien: That is correct. Ace: He must be on cloud nine. He got his first Hollywood role and his star is rising in and out of the ring. That is tremendous but the deal is this. He just like his buddy American Animal will never be on my level. Don’t get me wrong there talented but not as talented as me and for that matter I seriously doubt Fear gets out of Block A. So who else is there now? Damien: James Trevel. Ace: Who is that guy? Damien: He is in the match. Ace: Has he ever accomplished anything here in MWE? Damien: Umm… Ace: Exactly… Nothing. So who is left in block A? Damien: There is Vega Knight. Ace: He is the X-TV champion correct? Damien: Yes he is. Ace: Finally someone worth there fucking salt! I don’t know much about this Vega Knight character other then him being X-Television champion and that is kind of impressive and to tell you the truth I am putting my money on him coming out of block A without a problem. But when he gets to the finals and sees me there then he will have a problem. I mean there is a reason why he is the X-Television champion and that’s because he is nothing but lower mid-card trash. He is the guy that should be opening up for me and this will be the biggest match of his career but it is a shame that it will end in a loss. So who is in block b now Damien? Damien: Milenko is one of them. Ace: I remember Milenko from EW and I always thought he was a good wrestler but that whole clown make-up is a bit obnoxious. Wouldn’t you say so Thrope? Damien: Well it technically isn’t clown make-up. He is a juggalo. Ace: A who? Damien: A juggalo.is a fan of Insane Clown Posse. Ace: Oh God, that horrendous rap group? Those guys have fan? Man, that is hard to believe there music such so damn much. The fact Milenko reps them and wears there face paint makes me want to beat his ass. I now hope that he advances so I can get my hands on him and slap that stupid paint of his face and also kick the shit out of him for liking such shitty music. I mean if your going to listen to music out of Detroit then it should be some Ted Nugent or Bob Seger not this ICP garbage… Tell me who else is in that block because the thought of ICP and there fans makes me want to vomit. Damien: There is also a guy by the name of JJ The Executioner. Ace: Well, I doubt he has ever executed somebody and by the looks of him the only thing he is good at executing is a stack of ham sandwiches. There is no way he wins this block… Actually if this was a boiled egg eating contest then I’d put my money on him but it isn’t. So besides this ham and egger who is the other man in that block? Damien: It’s Joey Dallas… ** Cannon’s eyes grow wide.** Ace: Seriously? Damien: Seriously. **Cannon nods his head.** Ace: Well, we know the winner out of block B and his name is Joey Dallas and this actually kind of makes me a little happy. You see, back in a previous fed me and Joey Dallas were in we locked up in a match and he came out on top and since that day I have been dying to get my hands on that little piece of shit again but it never happened. You see, he dodged me he started running around with a guy by the name of Windy Bridges and they formed some goofy tag team called Redneck Express and they sat around living out there childhood fantasies by doing vignettes of old sitcoms and it was rather cheesy. They were dominating TV time with this bullshit and I have always thought Joey Dallas took part in this so he could avoid me but he can no longer avoid me. **Cannon rubs his chin.** Ace: Dallas, you can’t avoid me any longer. I am almost positive our paths will cross and that is destiny in my opinion and you don’t want this to happen and I know this. I know that there is a little voice telling you “Cannon is angry.” And that voice is right! Your victory against me was a shame DC cost me that match and ever since then you have ran and hid but it will all come to an end on Genesis and you don’t want to be on the receiving end of this because I am driven for that title. You see, I can see that title within reach and I will stop at nothing to get and if that mean bashing your pea headed Texas skull in then so be it because that title is mine and not yours. So Dallas bring everything you got to the table because I am telling you right now that you will need it. ** Cannon smiles at the camera and winks.** Damien: Those are some strong words Ace. Ace: Damn right they are. I mean everything I said. Now who is in this last block? Damien: Well, this last block is Sonny Williams against DC in a Cage match. Ace: Man, that is a match worthy of a main event on a pay-per-view. I am impressed with the book but I am not with either of those two guys. Damien: There both legends in MWE. Ace: No there not.. First let me tell you about Sonny Williams. Sonny is the biggest piece of dog shit ever to wrestle in MWE. He is a hack and nothing else. I mean when he was first in MWE he was the rapping wrestler that everyone was so polarized by. But, I saw right through it. It was an act and nothing more then that. You see the black community doesn’t like Sonny as a matter of fact my good friend Bishop told me one day that people on street think Sonny is nothing but an Uncle Tom. **Ace lets that hang in the air for a few seconds so everyone can digest it.** Ace: When Sonny came to MWE he wasn’t just a black guy who rapped. He didn’t rap actually but he got here and creative told him that was a good way for him to get over and Sonny bought it and sold his soul to the white devil and became an Uncle Tom for the riches and championships and that’s a shame. I mean I think Sonny is a decent wrestler but other then that he is nothing but a sell out and for that I can’t respect him and the titties may not be retarded but Sonny is for being nothing but a sell out. You make your people proud Sonny keep up the great work. Damien: Ace, can you back up what you just said about Sonny? Ace: What a stupid question. Yea, I said Sonny was a decent wrestler but I am still a better wrestler. Damien: Well, with that said what are you feeling about DC? I know you two have a very storied past. Ace: Well my feelings on DC have never changed. I still think he is the biggest piece of trailer park shit ever to get in a wrestling ring. The reason I hate DC is well known, but for all you people who don’t know let me tell you a little story about Ace Cannon’s first stint in MWE. **Ace stops for full effect.** Ace: The first time I was here in MWE I worked my fucking ass off in and out of the ring to elevate myself here in the company so I could get title shots but I kept getting held down. So I am sitting there wondering why my career is stuck in a rut when I am the most talented man on the roster. Guys in the locker room are telling me that DC doesn’t like and he politicking to hold me down because he doesn’t like my attitude or some shit. So one day I go up to DC and ask him what his fucking problem is and we get into it and people in the locker room have to pull us apart and so fourth. Then an order from the top comes down saying me and DC are not suppose to ever have a match with one another because it is going to turn into something more then a wrestling match. So as time wears on I leave MWE and cut my losses because they didn’t know what kind of talent that had under there nose. **Ace pauses before he continues.** Ace: So after a little time off I show up in EW and guess who is there? It is none other then DC. So I mind my p’s and q’s there and so does DC. Then we have a match over the EW and MWE titles. It was a hell of a match with lots of hatred and wasn’t much of a wrestling match it was more or less a straight up fight but in the end DC came out on top. I was pissed because this guy I fucking hate just beat me, but next thing you know DC is saying he doesn’t need the EW title and says if I beat Xavier Stone I will get it. So I go out and beat Xavier Stone and I take the title and then the road to unifying the EW, MWE and ACPW title starts. **Ace stops again.** Ace: A big match is held between DC, Maxx, Virus and myself to unify the title and I came out on the losing end and shortly there after EW closed down and the DC Vs Cannon chapter seemed to be over…. BUT WAIT! It isn’t along comes Rev~Pro and me and Joey Dallas are having this incredible match and low and behold look who is setting ringside with his whore… It is once again none other then DC. So I hit Dallas with High Society and then her comes DC into the ring and he nails me with a chair and Dallas comes too and end up covering me and getting the win. Now, this enraged me! I demanded a match with DC but he went off and did some stupid shit and got banned from Rev~Pro thus finally ending the DC versus Cannon chapter. **Ace once again stops.** Ace: But it doesn’t end there. I leave Rev~Pro and end up here in MWE and once arriving I am told there will be two brands. Now, DC is named as the first pick of Genesis and I am sitting in the back thinking there is no way me and him end up on the same brand. Then as the picks go by I end up finally getting drafted as the third pick of Genesis and I sat there thinking to myself DC just doesn’t go away. I mean DC is like something he has and knows very well…. **Ace leaves Damien wondering.** Ace: He is like herpes. Just when he goes away and you don’t think you will see him again he ends up coming back more annoying then ever. But unlike modern medicine not having a cure for herpes I have a cure to rid us of DC and it is called a good ass whipping. I have been waiting to get my hands on DC again for a long time and I might finally get my chance on Genesis and if I do I plan to kick the living shit out of you DC. I mean you have been feuding with my sorry ass brother and you have taken beating from him but let me tell you the beatings he gives you will pale in comparison to what I am bringing to the table. I will whip your from pillar to post and show everybody in attendance and the millions watching at home that you’re a nothing and that Ace Cannon is everything plus some. ** Ace stops for a second to let it sink in.** Ace: Oh and DC, bring that little whore of yours to the ring I can’t wait to see her face as I kick the living shit out of you and win that match and raise the title high above my head in victory fulfilling my destiny in MWE. Damien: You pulled no punches Ace and said some very inflammatory things about ever body in this tournament and there is nothing else for you left to say unless you can think of something. **Ace thinks about that for a second before he answers.** Ace: Winning this title on Genesis will be me fulfilling my destiny. In WZCW right before I had my world title match I was run off and fired over bullshit and then in Rev~Pro right before I was going to have my title match I was run off due to the owners big ego right before my title match. But this time the only thing that will stop me from winning the title on Genesis will be an act of God because I swear to everything that is holy that I will prevail and be champion and that isn’t just a statement…. It is a guarantee for all of you in TV land and in the locker room that you can take to the bank because my mouth has just writing checks that my ass can and will cash. ** Ace looks at the camera and winks at it and then turns his back to the camera and walks off.** Damien: You heard here folks.. Ace Cannon has guranteed a victory on Genesis. So tune in on Friday because it is sure to be a hell of a show. ** The camera fades out .**
