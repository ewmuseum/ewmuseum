Welcome to the Middle of Nowhere

Dec 10 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"This one needed stiches and This one came from rings This one isn't even there, but I feel it more because you don't care" ~Stone Sour "Made of Scars" Black. Dark. Desolate. All are words that could be used to describe our scene tonight as we open to see ourselves seemingly in the middle of nowhere. We look around slowly, seeing nothing more than the bleak sights of the dark horizon of distance hill tops meeting the pitch black night sky. There is no movement, no light, nothing, however off screen we can hear the distance crackling of a fire, the camera pans over now making this our main focus, sparks dance through the air like fireflies, contrasting against the dark sky for a split second, before dying out. We continue to slowly move over until we see a dark silhouette slowly becoming the focal point of our scene. We can make out that this person is wearing the familiar pin-stripe suit of Joey Dallas. The fire flashes brightly, making the bright blond hair of Dallas sparkle against the darkness, before fading back to the dark silhouette of just moments ago. Dallas continues to stare at the blaze set in front of him as he begins to speak softly, a hint of anger in his voice. Joey Dallas: Listen to me, and listen to me well. I should be standing here tonight as the World Heavyweight champion. The dream I've had since I was a boy, was taken from me, and as I watched helplessly it was crushed, destroyed and ripped apart. It was ripped from my hands in an instant; one brutal kick took all the passion and drive I had to write my name into the history books and burn the image of me standing alone, the belt in my hands, into the minds of every person watching that night. And turned it all into one pure emotion fueled by rage and intensity. And it's all targeted towards the man that has been a nagging thorn in my side since I walked into this industry. Dallas moves his hands up from his sides and adjusts his suit before slowly turning around so his face can be seen by the light from the fire. As his face slowly comes into view, it looks entirely normal, that is, until we see the side that fell victim to the vicious punt at the hands of Ace Cannon on the last edition of Genesis. This side of his face is bruised, his skin scrapped and cut. The emotion on his face seems to match his description to a tee. Joey Dallas: Take a good long look at what you did to me Cannon! The way you scarred me physically and, with the same foul kick, managed to break something much more precious than flesh. You broke my spirit. I had an opportunity to do what I shamelessly mock others for not doing. Yet is the only thing that has managed slip through my fingers. Dallas slowly moves his hand up so the light from the fire catches it, seeming to make it glow in against the crisp night air. He stares at the lump of human flesh in front of him, the scar on his face still in clear view. Dallas moves his hand slowly towards his face, softly running his fingers over the torn skin and dried blood. Running his fingers along the longest of the scraps, he comes to let the tips of his middle and ring fingers rest lightly on his lips leaving his index finger pressed against his face. He slowly turns his head even more towards the camera, staring into it intently as though he were looking into the eyes of Cannon. We can almost fell the pure emotion that he is giving off of himself as his eyes focus in and he drops his hand from his face back down to his side. Joey Dallas: I had the opportunity, to stand a top that metaphorical ladder of success and lead this company to heights it has never seen. But once again, there was one man that found a way to delay the inevitable salvation of this company. Cannon your personal vendetta against me has clouded your vision, think Ace, of what you want more than anything in this world. A legacy! You want people to remember your name into the unforeseeable future, and you think the only way to make sure that happens is to get the one thing that eludes both of us. Dallas turns his face away from us, back towards the fire still burning brightly. He stares at the beautiful glow of the flames in front of him breathing deeply in, then exhaling slowly. He repeats this a few times as he watches the sparks dance through the air landing softly on the burning branches in the blaze. Joey Dallas: Yet on Genesis we both sign away our souls with the mere thought of grasping that brass ring. You know just as well as I do that we have done it before. Signed our very lives away on a dotted-line as the Devil's henchman twisted that evil little grin onto his face knowing full well that we would stop at nothing until we had torn each other limb from limb. The thoughts going through his mind, no different then those of the Devil we sign our souls to this time. The money, the fortune and fame of being the man that finally set as at each others throats. There is no difference from Revolution Pro at all Cannon, except this time, the Devil wears Prada. Dallas still continues looking into the blaze as he steadily moves his hands towards his jacket, unbuttoning it from the top down making sure that he doesn't let a single thread go out of place. When he finally unbuttons the lowest of those on his jacket he slips it off, allowing it to fall down his arms. Leaving the upper body of Dallas covered in only a white dress shirt. He slips his left arm from the jacket before using his right to throw it into the dark. The inner layer shining in the light before hitting the ground, still glistening when the limited amount of light hits it. Joey Dallas: And that same son of a bitch is asking us to just set our differences aside, for one night, while we take on the pair of Maxamillion Mustafa and the Grand Mystique. I have told you before, and I really hate to repeat my doctrines, but Cannon, I have utterly no respect for you, the things you accomplish and your means of achieving them. What I do respect is this business! The blood, sweat, tears, heart and god damn soul that I have put into this industry is much too great of a price for me to just throw it all away because I have to team up with a worm of a human being like you! He now throws his tie over his shoulder and begins to unbutton his shirt carefully, just as he had done for his jacket. However after the first few buttons are undone he grabs the knot in his tie with his index finger, pulling on it to loosen the tie before pulling it off and throwing it in the same direction of the jacket. Joey Dallas: There are certain people around here that have been asking that they be taken a little more seriously then they are, people like the Grand Mystique. People who haven't done anything to see that this company survives to see another day. Mystique must feel very highly of himself if he thinks he can just walk into a business and automatically gain the respect he had in another. You fought day and night, for the sake of EW. You bled, for EW. You would have died for EW. Well I'm sorry to be the one to bring you down from cloud nine where you can keep respect from one company to the next, but this isn't EW, this is MWE. A company that I have seen grow, a company that I have bled for. A company that I sat in a f***ing hospital bed, without the use of my legs for a GOD DAMN YEAR FOR! Joey had continued to unbutton his shirt as he talked and has reached the final button, however his emotions have gotten the best of him and instead of being careful with this one like he had with the rest he grabs each side of the shirt that has already been thrown apart and simply tears the last button out, sending it into the fire as he does so. Dallas now rips the shirt off in a fury and throws that into the direction as the jacket and tie before turning into the camera again, tears of anger building up in his eyes as he points towards a scar that runs from the top of his neck and mid-way down his spine. Joey Dallas: Take a long, hard look at this! This scar should be a constant reminder to you and everyone else, that I gave everything in the world to do what was best for this company. But I guess people like you just don't care about that do you? No, all you people have ever cared about was yourselves. While I fought for the future, people like you only looked out for their best interests, and what did it get me? A metal plate stuck into my neck and eight months of rehab! Dallas traces his fingers down the length of the scar and back to his neck before clenching his fist and slowly drags it down over his heart. He holds it there for a few seconds feeling his quickening heart beat as he twist a sick smile onto his face. Dallas silently laughs. Joey Dallas: You see Mystique, the world championship isn't about me. It's about the future of this company. I see the envy in your eyes, the strive and selfishness of wanting to be that man. But I know you well enough now to know that you couldn't handle the pressure of being the champion, strapping that target onto your back and having the weight of the entire company strapped onto your shoulders. Mystique, the fact of this matter is, that you don't have the moral strength to carry a company into a new era. I can see the personal benefits in being known as a champion, but unlike you I know that to see the young superstars ever earn respect that this company actually has to make it into that new generation. I have already sat back and watched this company crumble without me once, and I'll be damned if I let happen again! Joey now slowly moves his hand down his chest, tracing over his peck and stomach before dropping his arm to a rest at his side. He now slowly begins to pace around the fire, turning his head to look at the base of the flames as he does so. Once he has walked half way around the inferno in front of him he stops and looks into the sky, the bright stars shining above. Joey Dallas: Now Mystique you claim to be from the deepest, darkest side of the cosmos. Explain to me, how exactly I am supposed to take you seriously when you cannot even tell me where your from, because you know, and I know that when the announcer says hailing from the darkest side of the cosmos that it is nothing more than you trying to deceive the fans again. If you truly want to be taken the a serious member of this roster you need to drop the bullshit, tell the people where you're really from. Take off the mask, show the your face and maybe, just MAYBE, I'll start to see why I should take a man like you seriously. But until that you are nothing more than just another person that has been thrown in my way on the path to the saving this company. The camera now circles around the fire as well stopping just behind Dallas as the glare from the blaze makes beads of sweat running down his neck and arms glisten against the black of the night, seemingly making him glow as he continues to look up at the stars. Joey Dallas: ]Saving it from people like you, and people like your tag team partner, Maximillion Mustafa. It's people like the two of you that cause the downfall of a company, your envy and resentment of champions and the people that made your very careers possible. Wishing for that spot light to shine down on you during your time in the MWE. Maxx I saw how having the Undisputed Championship stripped from you, crushed you. No longer having that symbol to prove that you were one of the best in the industry, it tore you apart. But you needed that slap across of the face to snap you back into reality. Dallas is still looking up into the sky, his eyes focusing in on a single star. He brings his hand up and traces it along the cut skin on his face again, bringing his head down and looking at his hand as he does so. A few brief moments pass before looking back towards the same star as he had been looking at just a minute ago letting his hand slip down to rest on his throat. Joey Dallas: Because, you are just like one of these stars Maxx, you may have shined brightly in the past. But you have run your course, and imploded. While you still shine for a time after, you slowly begin to fade out, fade into obscurity and become nothing more then an after thought. That is your biggest fear isn't it Maxx? Finding yourself no longer being one of the people in that top spot, and the EW championship was that last bit of your glory shining through. But that light, that prestige, was snuffed out when that title was taken from you, and you were left as nothing more then a tag team competitor. That, Maxx, is the your star, fading out. The camera begins to look up into the sky, watching the same stars and planets sparkle in the sky that Dallas has been for this time. His words seem to echo, as one of the brightest stars in the sky slowly begins to fade before completely blacking out, leaving just another blank spot in the sky. The camera then slowly moves down to once again show Dallas' face, as he moves his gaze down from the sky and into the camera, both arms back at his side by this time. Joey Dallas: So now the title of King of the Universe, and your short reign in MWE as a tag team champion, are all you have left, so I invite you to continue living off those accomplishments as long as you possibly can, because in the eyes of a saviour you are just the after thought of an era that has passed on. Because as the rest of us move on, you will only continue to dwell in the past, because your time of nobility remains there. A memory of times past, as you will remain. As you watch students become teachers and teachers become legends. Because my intentions will stay the same. To take this company from it's fresh new beginnings and carry it onward into the unforeseeable future. Only, this time, those who stand against me shall feel the stern hand of a savior raise against them and leave them defeated in the wake of an uprising. But those who are willing to be saved need only close their eyes, reach their hands into the sky. And let me lead them, not to temptation, but to salvation. Dallas pushes the camera backwards, stopping about ten feet back from him as he turns to once again face the fire, the light shining off his body as he closes his feet together and raises his arms to his side. Holding them strait out, as the scene fades away to black, the crackling on the fire is that last thing we hear before we finally close our scene.
