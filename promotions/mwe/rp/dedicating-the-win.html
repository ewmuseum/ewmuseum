Dedicating the Win

Aug 5 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
An MWE Camera is focuses on a scene backstage. Sonny Williams’ manager, Mr. Cooper is seen walking the backstage arena in search of Sonny. He sees the camera and smirks. They walk up to a locker room door as it says on a shinny gold plate ‘Sonny Williams’. Mr. Cooper points to the gold plate with his clients name on it. He opens the door as it begins to make a creaking noise. The room is relatively dark yet not too dark. Sonny is seen leaning against one of the lockers with his head down on the locker. He is wearing long blue jeans with one of the legs out of the jeans only over the knee with a bandanna strapped holding it up and a grey Sonny Williams hoodie. Mr. Cooper spots Sonny and turns on a light which gets the attention of Sonny. He turns and stares into the eyes of Mr. Cooper. Mr. Cooper walks up to Sonny. Sonny takes a deep breath. Mr. Cooper: We need to go Sonny, the media are awaiting your.. Sonny cuts off Mr. Cooper as Mr. Cooper looks on in disbelief at Sonny. Sonny raises his head and softly shakes it. You can merely see the water in his eyes, knowing he won’t break out into tears but is near of it. Sonny Williams: No. Mr. Cooper: But we need.. Mr. Cooper is cut off again by Sonny who continues to stare at Mr. Cooper. Mr. Cooper looks at his watch and begins to look incredibly inpatient. Sonny walks over to a folded chair and puts his head in his hands. He turns a little to slightly look at Mr. Cooper and thinks over his words before saying so. Sonny Williams: Just go, please. Mr. Cooper turns off the light again to once again make the room rather dark. He then leaves the room in a bit of a stir up but without saying anything. The camera stays in the room as Sonny still has his head in his hands. He looks up and stares at the wall in front of him before slowly turning to the camera. He still has water in his eyes and you can just feel the raw emotion in the room with the akward silence. Sonny Williams: I guess you’re wondering, what’s going on. I would too. You see as a kid I grew up on the streets of Detroit, literally. My father died when I was 9, my mother left me and my brother abandoned when I was 11. I was a year older than my brother. I looked out for him. We lived a tough life, but we coped for a few years. I got a job and saved money to keep my brother in school, it was hard but worth every cent. I loved my brother, I loved Jordan. He and I weren’t the best of kids. We got into a lot of trouble, we hanged with the wrong people but we managed to live on, but until one day, 10 years to today when the bad turned to the worst. Sonny turns from the camera and puts his head in his hands once again. The camera begins to blur out and it starts to focus into a new scene. It was the 10th of August, 1998 in Detroit, Michigan on a warm cloudy and dusky night. A group of thugs are seen standing on the corner of a street with an old, washed up industrial building behind them. The place was deserted, no one ever came down this end of town. A boy looking to be about 16 years old who is wearing a thug-like bandana, with a sleeveless white shirt with the words “B-Boyz” printed on both the front and back and short baggy grey jeans. He is also wearing a stack of bling. This is shown to be young Sonny Williams. The thugs are still in a tight group. Two of the boys, one including Sonny Williams collect two spray paint cans and begin to tag up the industrial building writing ‘B-Boyz’ all over the building. They finish up and throw the spray paint cans away before walking back over and laughing in a group. There are seven people in the group. The members of this group appear to be a gang of which is called B-Boyz, all of which are holding baseball bats and around about the age of 16-20. But, there is one that is smaller than the rest. He is wearing a similar outfit to what Sonny is wearing. This is the younger brother of Sonny, Jordan. He looks to be very shy, and has a black eye which is pretty badly bruised. Tonight isn’t just fun and games, a gang war was on the beckoning of occuring. One of the older boys by the name of TJ who appears to be the leader or co-leader of B-Boyz makes silence in the group. He hushes at them all to be quiet. He takes off his large music headset for his walkman. TJ: Yo, boys, boys. Tonight we do this fo us, I’m sure we have all had enough of the shit that Randy and his crew (the other gang, their name is DSK, Detroit Shire Killers) have given us. I sure have, and what they did to Jordan last week, it just means war. Sonny, Jordan, did youse bring the 9mm with you? Sonny raises his shirt as the 9mm handgun is seen in poking out of his pants. He takes it out and loads the gun ready for whatever is coming their way. TJ smiles as Sonny looks at his brother, the scared Jordan. Sonny smiles unwillingly back at TJ. TJ: Good work. Now do you know how to fire, get a steady aim? Sonny nods. TJ puts his hand in his pocket as he pulls out a little cereal watch to check the time. It says the time is 9:43pm. The group had expected that DSK would have arrived a lot earlier and are surprised that there has been no arrival as of yet. TJ puts the cereal watch back in his pocket as he looks around unsteadily. He gathers silence once again. TJ: I used to know these guys, and I used to know them well. We need to get somewhere secure, these boys are very dog in the act of a fight. They’ll come at us in the most cowardly way possible, but it’ll be to their advantage. Brad, go check and see if that door for the shit hole of a building is open. One of the B-Boyz members, supposably Brad walks up to the industrial building with the tags all over the building that they had just done. He tries to open the door but it is locked. He looks back at the rest of the gang and shakes his head. Sonny looks at Jordan again, in a scared state, and does a jog to the door. He gets to it and tells Brad to move away for a sec. He uses his baseball bat to try and break it but all it does is leave a few dents on the door. He had failed for his first try at it. He backs up and runs at the door with a big kick which sends it flying down. He looks back as the rest of the group begin to walk up to the industrial building and inside. They get inside and a large amount of mice run down the crack of the building at the arrival of the B-Boyz. They step in slowly over the litters of the mice. Brad: What tha fuck! I just stepped in shit in my new fucking shoes. Sonny Williams: Let that frustration out in the fight tonight. We’re gonna need some of it man. They continue walking and find some stairs which lead to a open window upstairs. They sit there and continue to wait. The silence is overwhelming. You can sense the emotion inside of everyone. They all know that by tonight’s end, it wouldn’t be the same. Sonny looks to Jordan and brushes his hair. Sonny Williams: You okay Jord? Jordan looks at Sonny Williams with and makes a smile. He is scared, probably the most of the lot after what happened to him the week before when he was bashed by two of the DSK members. He scratches his back for a sec. He looks uneasy. Jordan: Yeah. Jordan lies to Sonny. Sonny is aware of it and knows he is in a state of fright. He looks around at the other B-Boyz members to see if they are looking on, but they all have their heads stuck out the window awaiting for DSK to arrive. Sonny looks back at Jordan and gets the 9mm handgun out of his pants and hands it to Jordan. Jordan has it in his hands unsure of what to do with it and what had just happened. Sonny smiles at Jordan and rubs his hair once again. Sonny Williams: Don’t tell anyone bro, just be careful with it. It’s loaded. Jordan nods and puts the 9mm gun into his pocket. TJ puts his hand back into his pocket once again and pulls out the cereal watch. It says that the time is 10:30pm, and he makes a look of confusion as the time had passed so quickly. The silence continues on with all but Sonny and Jordan having their heads popping out the open window. TJ and the other four members with their heads out the window begin to become very impatient. TJ turns to Sonny who is staring uneasily at the wall in front of him. TJ clicks in front of his eyes trying to get his attention. Sonny makes a sudden movement and looks at TJ. TJ: Why aren’t you looking on for DSK to come? I thought you liked fights Sonny. Sonny Williams: I do but.. Sonny is cut off by a very impatient and emotional TJ. Sonny looks confused at what had happened as the two were quite good friends. TJ: Pass me the 9mm... PASS ME IT! Sonny stands up with anger on his face as TJ follows too and gets up into Sonny’s face. TJ pinches the shirt of Sonny and raises it to see if the 9mm handgun is poking out of his pants. It isn’t as he lets go of the shirt. He stares straight into Sonny’s eyes as Sonny does the same. It looks as though a punch was beckoning on being thrown. Jordan looks frightened knowing that he is the cause of this blow up. Jordan has water in his eyes and sheds a tear. He stands up and yells.. Jordan: STOP IT! He puts his hand into his pocket ready to hand over the 9mm gun to TJ but all of a sudden the motor of a car is heard down the other side of the road, maybe 400m or so away. They vrooming of the car continues to be heard as the silence begins between the B-Boyz is on-going. Brad stands and spots a white van down the end of the road. Sonny and TJ get out of each other’s faces as they all run down the stairs of the old industrial building with the baseball bats in hand. The car noise becomes louder and louder as it suddenly stops on the corner of the street outside the old industrial building. The B-Boyz can hear the van doors slam open as the leader, Randy is heard. Randy: WHERE ARE THESE MOTHERFUCKERS!? TJ indicates to Jordan for him to hand him the 9mm handgun. Jordan does so as Sonny looks over Jordan not too happy about what had just happened and still pissed off at TJ. TJ checks and fully loads it before putting it in his pants and raising his eyebrows to the rest of the B-Boyz who have their baseball bats ready for war. All of a sudden TJ slams open the door as it gets the attention of DSK. Sonny, Brad and TJ are the front runners and come charging with baseball bats. Sonny Williams: GET THEM B-BOYZ! The others forward out after and not too far behind. Sonny starts pumping two of the DSK members with a baseball bat which sees one of which land flat on the ground with blood running from his mouth. DSK Member: Ahh! The other hits the van which keeps him from falling onto the ground. Jordan looks scared and is the last to come out. He spots one of the younger members of DSK and begins to wack him with the baseball bat. The two younger member’s trade blows. Sonny sees what is going down between his brother Jordan and the other member of DSK and comes in and lines up the kid before being hit in the back by Randy. Sonny Williams: FUCK! *says in agonising pain* Brad, TJ and another member of B-Boyz run to the scene where Sonny is in agonising pain but still on his feet. They each smash Randy as he falls to the ground who begins to bleed from the top of his head. The injured DSK members from the ground get up and one pulls out a gun from his pants and shoots it in the air to get the attention of the B-Boyz. TJ pulls his gun out also as all but Sonny, TJ and Jordan run away from the scene. Sonny drops to his knees from the pain as TJ begins to fire his gun around but keeps missing. He shoots one of the DSK members in the leg as Jordan runs to Sonny trying to gather him to his feet. Jordan: Get up Sonny! Get up, we need to go. Please, come I know you’re in pain but come. Sonny gets to his feet as his face is seen to be incredibly red trying to pull through the pain he is currently in. All but two of the DSK members get back into the van. All that remains from B-Boyz is Sonny and Jordan, and from DSK is Randy who is unconscious on the ground, and the member with the 9mm. TJ had ran from the scene as his gun ran out of ammo. Sonny and Jordan begin to run before Sonny drops once again to his knees. Jordan gets on his knees and tries to inspire Sonny to continue on. Jordan begins to shed a few tears. Jordan: We’ve been through a lot Sonny, we can push through this. I love you and I know you can do this. Sonny slowly gets to his feet again as they start to run away to where the rest of their gang is at. Sonny looks to be at a steady pace now, as he is a bit behind Jordan in running, but not a lot. They run across the road and almost around the block before Sonny looks behind to see the DSK member with the gun chasing them. He begins to shoot and continues to miss. Sonny and Jordan can see TJ on the corner of the block. TJ: RUN! But all of a sudden Jordan trips on one of the holes in the road. Sonny is unaware of this before he hears the yelling of Jordan he turns around. Sonny Williams: JORDAN, NO! The DSK member with the gun gets to Jordan and kicks him the head, TJ yells at Sonny to keep running. He does so. Sonny turns around and stops and sees the man put the 9mm gun to the head of Jordan. He looks up at Sonny and has an evil smirk on his face... BANG! Sonny Williams: NO!! He shoots at the head of Jordan and takes the ammo out of the gun and drops it. The van had already started and comes speeding to pick up the DSK member. They drive off in a hurry and away from the scene. Sonny drops to his knees and puts his head on the road ground and begins to cry. TJ slowly forwards over with tears running down his cheek. It begins to rain. Sonny gets up and shakily walks over to Jordan. He drops to his knees once again and continues crying. He looks up and sees TJ behind him also shedding a few tears. Sonny kisses the head of Jordan and then looks up into the sky of which is pouring down with rain and into the heavens. Sonny Williams: WHY!? God dammit! Sonny takes off one of his chains and put it on the dead body of Jordan. He continues to cry on and on as TJ leaves for a second ringing Brad and the guys on the bad news. Sonny kisses the head of his brother, his last loving relative. The camera focuses out and ends the flashback as it shows Sonny Williams in the folded steel chair with his head in his hands. He looks up at the camera again and sheds a tear that drips down his cheek slowly. He wipes away his tear and takes a deep breath. Sonny Williams: I loved Jordan. He made me want to live each day on. I will never forget that day, the day my life became hell. I dedicate my life to Jordan, the kid that had to live a bad life. I have lived a eventful life, somewhat could say a fairy tale life. What I say to them, is no, I lived a long period of time in depression. I had no relatives, I had some friends also killed. But nothing compares to what happened to Jordan. Sonny Williams: I am over the moon that MWE is back, but this match isn’t for any of the fans. It’s not for me. I am going to dedicate and win this match for Jordan. This is for him and all the shit I have gone through in my life. Enough is enough. Jordan buddy, I love you and I know you are looking down on me right now. At Crowning Glory I am dedicating a MWE World Championship win to you. Rest in Peace brother! Sonny turns from the camera once again and places his head in his hands. The scene begins to blur out as it becomes darker and darker each second. The End. OOC: I really liked this, it was a really emotional RP and I hope you can sense it too. Please leave feedback.
