The almost quiet day

Sep 19 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Beep…..beep….beep….the sound of a life support machine is heard as the camera fades in the setting of a dim lighted hospital. There are rows by rows of dim light shafts as the halls are near empty, a few tired doctors roaming them here and there. The secretary at her desk on the third floor is tired, her eyes saggy, her face pale. She looks sick and seems to have come down with a slight cough. She taps her fingers away at her desk computer, tap…tap…tap….but there is no one who cares enough to hear her. The doctor’s are exhausted and tired themselves walking endlessly, checking on their patients endlessly. Coffee in hand, forms and papers stuffed in their pockets mindlessly. The tiled blue and white floor is screeched with black marks and dried blood from yester year. Doctor’s just marking up the floors even more in a rush for their patients health. One doctor enters a nearby room, the door is cracked open, he sees Sinc sitting with his ex-wife at her hospital bed. Sinc’s bodyguard is not around, neither is the mysterious briefcase. The doctor looks worried as he opens the door, the lights in the room are faded, only a lamp light on a desk near the hospital bed is clicked on giving off a gloomy glow. The walls are an ugly, plain, white with a few cracks and peels. The room itself is messy with doctor’s equipment and medical supplies as well as old flowers, balloons, and “Get Well Soon Cards” flooding near the bed. Sinc looks at him, seeing an average stature doctor with the usual white coat and glasses, the doctor also has blue cackies and black orthopedic shoes. He is middle aged, his hair brown and curly. The camera focuses on the room with the two eying each other with fierce tension. Sinc: Doc….tell me the results. Good or bad I don’t really give a damn right now. I just need to know if she has a chance. The doctor sighs and rubs his forehead after taking his glasses off in grief. He puts his glasses back on and looks at Sinc again with worriment. Doctor: Well…her heart is not in the best of shape, we are going to need to give her heart medication, but its nothing we can’t fix. I am so happy that she didn’t jump. I know you didn’t mean to give her a panic attack after you prevented her from jumping but you really have to take care of her. Sinc stands up, broad shouldered, facial expression screaming anger, and walks to the doctor with his eyes widened. He then grabs him by the collars and shoves him into a wall. Sinc: Don’t you EVER tell me that this was MY fault EVER again. And I DO take care of her punk! I LOVE her! Sinc looks at the doctor’s name tag, his eyes honed on the name wanting to remember it for the rest of his life. Sinc: Now…Harry? Sinc looks confused for a brief moment then turns back to anger as he looks at the doctor once more in utter disgust. Sinc: Alright Dr. Harry Dickson?….I want you to leave us the HELL alone….And Don’t you come back in here unless you have something smart to say instead of bull shit. Alright HARRY?!? Harry: Ok ok…I’ll go I’m sorry…and it’s actually Harry Dicksan with an…an a sound…not an- Sinc: I don’t give a DAMN! Now scoot your hairy, saggy, cellulite ass OUT of this room NOW! Harry makes a position of fear as he has his arms raised against the wall and his face looking as if he had seen a ghost. Sinc lets him go as the doctor leaves in a hurry and shuts the door behind him. Sinc: Asshole…. Sinc whispers to himself as he sits back down near his ex-wife grabbing her hand as it dangles from the right side of the hospital bed. He is looking at her with sorrow. The camera gets an angle on the side of his face as the camera sits on the desk where the dim, desk lamp lights its lens. Sinc is wearing a black T shirt, blue jeans, and black Reebok shoes. He kisses his ex-wife’s hand softly. Sinc: I’m sorry Barbara….I am REALLY…REALLY sorry….I didn’t mean to do this to you….I just meant to save you…to snap you back into reality…. Barbara doesn’t reply. She is laying in her bed hooked up to an oxygen mask and life support, her eyes are closed. She looks as if she is simply resting as the light continues illuminating on her face. Only the side of her is seen. She is wearing a patient’s gown. Pure blue. Sinc: I could care less if I won my match last week and became US champion…I want the best for you first…and I promised you that night on the roof last week and I will promise you again…I will only love you from now on. You and you alone. How I acted that night was asinine and unacceptable. When the cameras cut I should not have screamed at you. I sincerely apologize Barbara I really do. Sinc takes a minute to close his eyes and mourn. He is taking deep breathes trying to hold back his tears. But he couldn’t. He began remembering his father and how he was as a kid and then how he is today. The camera zooms on his face as he recalls a fallen memory. ***************A memory is recalled************** Sinc is with his dad in their garage. There is a large black wrestling mat laid out on the cold concrete floor. The garage is messy and surrounded by boxed junk and broken pieces of cars, toys, bikes, and other various trinkets. The garage door is open and it is a sunny day as the warmth envelops the garage insulation. Jace is clothed with black, loose shorts, black sneakers, and a white tank top. Jace is a strong, bulky man. His shoulders are wide and masculine, his legs are very muscular, his eyes a deep brown, his hair short and brown. He is average height and his arms are quite lengthily. He is middle aged. Sinc has a white tank top as well, blue loose shorts, and white sneakers. Sinc is in his teens, kind of short, rather skinny, his blonde hair is blooming out like a mushroom. His bangs covering his eyes and his ears. He is rather weak looking. Jace Mercier: No ya’ idiot. That’s not how you do it. You hook the arm then go for the suplex. Your grandmother does it better than you. Sinc: I’m trying dad. I am too weak to suplex this stupid dummy. Jace: Come on you can do it its not hard! Dammit step it up! Sinc continues trying to lift a pure white manikin dummy and suplex it to the mat. He is finally able to but falls with it in exhaustion. Jace: No you weak ass kid! Jace said helping his son up. And then slapping him a few times across the face lightly as he talks. Jace: Are you a man? Are you a MAN? Sinc: Yes…. Jace: Say again? Jace slaps Sinc harder as Sinc makes a distinct frown on his face. Sinc: YES I AM A MAN! Sinc is slapped into seriousness holding back his tears as best he could not wanting to cry in front of his father. Jace: Good NOW DO IT AGAIN! HARDER THIS TIME! ***************A memory ends************** The camera is still zoomed on Sinc as he is still closing his eyes and crying slow tears. After a few moments Sinc opens his eyes and looks at his ex-wife again. This time she is awake looking at him, smiling at him. She slowly strokes Sinc’s cheek happily but exhausted. Sinc: I’m sorry again babe….you deserve better than me….you really do… The camera zooms out a bit getting more of Barbara in the shot as Barbara nods her head “no” slowly. Sinc laughs a bit as he looks at Barbara in a very caring way. Sinc: You always were persistent….always know who was just right for you and when he was….you chose me…and I chose you….my dad was right you know….I am an idiot…I am an idiot for cheating on you and not seeing the beauty I have right in front of me… Barbara is able to get in a small chuckle followed by a cough as she grasps her oxygen mask. Sinc leans over to hug her, he then proceeds to kiss her forehead and pet her hair with a loving gesture. Sinc: Don’t worry…we are here…together again. Nothing will ever change that. A knock on the door is heard. Sinc: Hold on a second sweetheart. A camera follows Sinc as he proceeds to the door in frustration and slams it open only to see Shawn Spitzer. Sinc: Oh my god…you again. Look man, if this is an interview I am not interested. We are friends and all and I’ve known you for a while but really…this is personal shit if you don’t mind. Sinc begins trying to close the door. Shawn sticks his foot in front of it and stops it. Sinc is now more than aggravated. Sinc: What? Shawn: This is totally off the record man…I just want to know how your doing and how she’s doing. That’s all. Sinc wipes some wet goo off his cheeks. He looks rather upset. Sinc: Even when you talk in an off the record manner you still proceed to SPIT all over me…anyway…she is doing fine and I will tell her you said hi. I just need some alone time man, I can call you later or something. Shawn: Alright man. Just checking up on you two, after last week it was kind of a scare. For all of us at the MWE. So uh…I was wondering….hehe… Sinc moans in annoyance of Shawn. Shawn is sweating bullets as he begins getting worried about Sinc’s reaction. Sinc: I knew it! What is it now? Shawn: Do you have any material or anything you can say to me in ANY significance to your MNM match this Monday? It would help me give MWE some feedback on your status man… Sinc grumbles fed up with Shawn now as he continues spitting. Sinc: Look buddy…I really need to be with my ex-wife right now ok? Just use that video I made last night and sent you for Covell to show the other staff members. Now if you could please let us be I would appreciate it. Shawn: You don’t have anything to say about your opponent? Or anything? Sinc: Not much to say….he’s a good competitor in the ring and he almost won MWE World gold…but he’s a crazy clown with no taste and continues his career by going after a man he knows is a worthy champion. He is in a graveyard where the dead walk, he is going to face a tough wrestler, me. But the guy has got some serious problems and mental issues. He is too unstable to be in MWE in my opinion. And you know what I usually say next about my opinions. Sinc Mercier’s opinions always matter and are always truthful. He doesn’t have much talent that I can see his father was a drunk, and guess what buddy, mine was not any better! He can go on and on about his past I frankly would rather hit myself with a two by four than listen to him talk about his past. He is not that interesting. Now please…. Shawn: Got any other input we could use? Sorry Hehe… Sinc: Ok now your starting to piss me off. Barbara is waiting for me to comfort her after she had a FREAKING PANIC ATTACK! Ok? I could give a rainbow colored SHIT about a stupid god damn clown who needs to get back in his little tiny car, keep his stupid jokes to himself and start driving off a CLIFF! He isn’t going to prove to me he is opponent material unless he steps up and shows me he is mature enough to start acting like a full grown man rather than an STD sportin, little kid scarin, JOHN…WAYNE…GACY..WANNABE! NOW LEAVE! SPITzer! Sinc’s voice roars down the calm halls as a few doctors look at him in confusion and discomfort. Shawn now has spit all over him from the yelling and hollering of Sinc’s words. Shawn looks a bit taken back by what he just heard. Shawn: Sorry again man will do…thanks for the input and I wish you two the best. Sinc: And one other thing….since you got me on a role here...if I see that crazy psycho near my ex-wife or my family or any of my close friends I am going to take his bag of shits and giggles and shove it RIGHT down his throat. I will not tolerate some laughing weirdo that sounds like a hyena when he or she, knowing as much about Jekyl as I do, is getting banged by a bicuriois, mentally ill, one legged, sorry excuse for a mate. He says he had amnesia? I will do something he will TRULY forget about. Why so serious? I will tell you why because I hate seeing people like him in careers like this. WHY SO DAMAGED? Because when I am done with him his career is going to be close to over! Sinc’s voice suddenly becomes louder. Sinc: REMEMBER……..THAT!!!! Goodbye! Shawn is now standing there as Sinc shuts the door on his face as no cameras follow Sinc back in the room. Shawn: Jeesh….that was a toughie…Talking about having the last word, he had a REAL bad day. Shawn then is seen wiping sweat off his forehead and then walking away down the halls of the hospital and towards the third floor elevator as the camera fades zoomed out on him and cuts to the video Sinc was mentioning.
