Eminem, others, to collaborate on Brian Michaels song, Soulja Boy to do Sinc Mercier song

Jun 16 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
MWE.com (AP) - Three of rap's biggest names, Eminem, Jay-Z and Kanye West, are making a mashup of three of their songs and it will be dedicated to MWE's Brian Michaels. The rumors going around are that Eminem will be using a verse from "Way I Am", Jay-Z will be using a verse from "Death of Autotune" and Kanye will feature with a verse from "Dreamkillers". The track is slated to be released sometime before Independence Day and a music video is expected to be taped tonight. In other news, Soulja Boy is remixing one of his own tracks to dedicate to Sinc Mercier. Stay tuned to the character development section for the songs.
