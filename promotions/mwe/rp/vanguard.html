Vanguard: A Definition; [Hello Me; Meet the Real Me]

Apr 12 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Vanguard as defined in the Webster English Dictionary; Is the forefront of an action or movement. That is what this stable is. The Movement; to dethrone the men that have been at the top of the food chain since this all began. The men and women of this industry that believe in change. The people that are sick of the same old crap that has been seen since 2007. The people that have been cast aside, rejected as "top contenders" The Vanguard, is the movement ...... For Domination. ------------------------------------- A large red double-decker bus which England is famous for pulls up to open our scene on a beautiful day in Norwich. The sun is shining dully in the early mourning sky, making the fresh dew of the grass glisten on the lawn of the Blue Boar Inn. It isn't often that you see a day like this in England, but when you do it is truly a sight to behold. The superstars of Mayhem Wrestling Entertainment should feel privileged to be here. They should feel ecstatic for an entirely different reason however as the early rising fans have managed to find the hotel of the world famous wrestlers and now wait behind a small barricade guarded by the finest police that England has to offer. Almost every one of them is wearing a t-shirt or holding a sign for one of our workers as they wipe their eyes in the early breeze that blows through their hair. All in hopes of catching a glimpse of their favorite superstars, and they were not about to be disappointed. As the door to the bus swings open a wide variety of our staff members are already walking down the steps, bags either slung over their shoulders or dragged behind them as they come out of the doors. Most noticeably are Ed Fererra, Ryan Adams and Fred Carry as they slowly walk past the crowd, chattering amongst themselves as they do so. The fans paying little attention to them, and getting none in return as they reach the door quite quickly. Turning back to the bus the next pair off the bus is the dynamic duo of Triple G and Mr. Markowitz. Dressed almost carelessly in just blue jeans and the basic MWE t-shirt each as they drag their bags off of the bus. Wide smiles on their faces after an amazing turn out against the Saintz of Los Angeles just days ago, their first win as a tag team as members of the roster and an amazing way to do it. In style. "Marko! Marko!" A little boy at the front of the crowd calls out, trying desperately to get the attention of his two favorite superstars. Holding a makeshift 'Blood Fuze are Awesome!' sign in front of him as he calls out to Mr. Markowitz in particular. Waving a free arm in the air he calls again. "Mr. Markowitz!" The team finally break off their conversation after hearing the high pitched voice calling out for them. Their smiles grow wider as they drag their bags over to the small barricade and up to the kid. Markowitz crouches down, getting as close to eye level as possible with the young kid, who now has an ecstatic smile on his face. Mr. Markowitz: "What can I do for ya little guy?" In shock the kid can't manage to get any words out, jaw dropped he simply holds his homemade sign up towards one of his heroes. It's the universal sign for 'Can I get your autograph?' The question only makes the grin on Mr. Markowitz's face grown wider, brighter, more confident. There is no way he could turn down an offer like this as he reaches through the barricade he ruffles the young boy's hair and takes the sign, carefully pulling it through the metal bars. As he stays knelled down next to the kid he turns his head to look up at his cousin. Mr. Markowitz: "Hey G. You got a pen on you?" The young boy is now giddy, almost shaking with excitement as Triple G pats down his pockets trying to find a pen. Unfortunately, he comes up empty. Nothing more then a simple shrug and an apologetic look towards the child as he says. Triple G: "Sorry Kiddo." It's funny isn't it? How it takes so much to get you to fall in love with something, and in just a few words it can leave you completely heart-broken. It takes an unbearable tole on your emotions just to see someone with a broken heart, and it's even worse when that person is only a kid. His dream of a whole week was now shattered and now he stands here, distraught, tears building in his eyes. All because of a pen, or the lack there of. "Hold on I think I have one mate" One of the larger policemen chimes in with a thick English accent as he too pats his pockets. Reaching under his jacket before a small shimmer of black against the morning sky shifts the emotions right back around. As the man hands a small Sharpie branded marker over the barricade the young boy wipes away his tears, his smile now back on his face as Mr. Markowitz let's the marker glide across the homemade sign across his knee spelling out his name. Triple G: "Hand that here Marko." His voices causes both Markowitz, and the kid's attention to turn towards him. Smiles on both of their faces our young superstar hands the sign and pen to his cousin as he takes the pen as well, signing his name across the opposite side of Marko's signature. Ending with a hard dot at the end he hands it back to Markowitz who slides it back through the barricade and into the waiting arms of the kid. Mr. Markowitz: "Thanks man" He hands the pen back to the policeman as he pulls himself back to his feet using the top of the steel barricade. reaching over the railing as he gets his balance he once again ruffles to young boy's hair who is clutching for dear life onto his new prized possession. A wide grin on his face as his idols follow suit of the MWE staff and walk away and through the doors of the hotel, disappearing from site. Clang. Clang. Footsteps down the metal steps of the bus as we look back towards the large red bus. Zak Warner appears through the door. A large black bag slung over his shoulder and a look of embarrassment and anger on his face. He can't believe that everything that he had held so dearly had all drifted away so quickly. His undefeated streak and his intercontinental title were both taken from his grasp by Paige. Fighting back the thoughts of failure and rejection as he walks past quickly paying attention to nothing more than his footsteps.
