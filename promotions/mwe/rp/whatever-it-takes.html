Whatever it takes

Oct 9 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The scene opens up in Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania, showing the rough steel mills and the factory workers sweating it out at their jobs. The camera then shows a picture of the Pittsburgh skyline and shows off a large hotel room. It zooms on a large poker table that has three men sitting at. The camera shows the first face, a dark-skinned man who has his eyes focused on the cards in his hands. His eyes suddenly shift to the left to show a lighter-skinned man who his eyes fixed on the television watching Sarah Palin in the Vice Presidential Debate. The camera then rolls to Brian Michaels, who rolls his eyes at the site of the sharply-dressed Palin. He has his new manager Tera ’’Barbie McQueen’’ on his lap, with her eyes focused on his chest. She is wearing a black Brian Michaels T-Shirt with a pink skirt and black boots. The camera pans over at Joe Biden, who is also sharply dressed as he strains to keep himself from correcting Palin. The camera then pans back at the poker table, where the men continue eyeing their cards. Guy #1: I’m out, man. My deal sucks. The camera then pans over to the second man who scratches his head, pondering his cards. He looks over at Michaels who is cursing silently, and then looks at his pot. He gives the camera a smirk before grabbing his hundred dollars. Jake: All in. Michaels: Do you really want to do this? Jake: It’s on like Donkey Kong. Michaels and Jake pause for a dramatic moment as the Debate goes on in the background. Palin: and Dadgumit, we’re going to bring change, but not the change Mr.Obama wants! Ya see, John McCain and I are MAVERICKS! Lemme repeat that for the American people, we are MAVERICKS! Barack Obama, is not a maverick. We need change in America! But gosh, we need realistic change, don’t we?! Michaels rolls his eyes again in disbelief at the folksy tone that Palin uses in what should’ve been a gritty debate. Michaels: Dude, Alaska blows. Palin doesn’t know what she’s talking about. Hell she said her foreign experience means that she can see Alaska from her house. We don’t need a She-Bush in 2008. Pay attention to the game. Jake: Wait a second, She-Bush?! Are you friggin’ kidding? That name is SO wrong on SO many levels. I can’t believe you’d stoop down that low. Michaels: Hey, don’t tell McCain we found out about this, but we saw a picture of him looking at Sarah. Let’s just say the SURGE…was working. Enough said. Jake: It’s hard not to look at Sarah man. She’s so energetic, her teeth make your ratty-ass television look HD. You should be grateful, this woman is our future V.P. Michaels: If that…thing…is our future V.P., I’m packing up and moving to Canada. Jake: Dude, the Maverick and the Milf! What could be tighter?! Michaels looks at Tera, who shakes her head in disbelief. Jake shrugs and puts in his cash. He throws down a Queen, and two Kings. He smirks at Michaels as Michaels shakes his head in disappointment. Michaels’ look of shame then turns into a grin as he lays down an Ace, King and Queen. Jake throws his arms up in defeat as Michaels spreads his arms to take the cash. Michaels: Always nice facing you in this, kid. You’ll get better with experience...kinda like Mason Resoan. Jake: Really? Tera: No. jJake hangs his head down in disappointment as he walks away from the hotel room in defeat. The other man chuckles and slowly stands up from his chair, leaving the room as well. Tera hops off Michaels’ lap and grabs the money, putting it down her shirt. She goes over to one of the large cabinets and takes out the car keys. She tosses them to Brian, who then catches them with his left hand. Tera: Let’s go to the mall and waste this stuff. I’ve been meaning to get myself some new lipstick and some lingerie. Michaels: Michaels likes. Tera: Yeah, I might even let you get a sneak peak later. Oh, by the way, I want to drive so toss the keys back. Michaels stands up and shrugs, showing off his black dress shirt and loose blue jeans. The scene fades out. PART DEUX The scene opens up at the local mall in Pittsburgh. It is full of people, mainly in their twenties shopping around looking for clothing. The camera pans over the mall to show several different stores, such as Macy’s and Sears. Michaels and Tera look around, eyeing the different stores. Tera: So, what do you wanna do? Michaels: Well I was guessing you could do what you said you planned on doing and spend the money I won in the poker game here. See anything you like here? Tera looks around, her eyes skipping the electronic stores nearby as she peers over to the Victoria’s Secret store. Michaels looks over at the camera and shrugs. Michaels: Go figure. Michaels gives off a grin as Tera playfully runs over to the Victoria’s Secret store. Michaels puts his hands in his pockets and takes a deep breath, slowly walking by over towards a nearby bathroom. He closes the door and leans against the cold hard wall. Michaels makes a beat with his head rocking back and forth, each time hearing a pounding sound against the wall. Michaels: Mason, Resoan. It’s been a long, long time since you and I stepped in the ring. Hell, I’m not even sure we ever faced each other one-on-one. See for those of you who don’t remember what happened between Mason and myself…let me give you a LITTLE reminder. Michaels runs his hands through his hair, and his new scruffy beard, chuckling at the thought of Mason returning to MWE. Michaels: Over a year ago, when I was one of those guys who didn’t give a shit about what anybody said…there was an up-and-comer. There was a guy, who I’d pay SPECIAL attention…to watch on Monday Night Mayhem. It was a wrestler with, unlimited potential. His name was Mason Resoan, and he was the baddest son of a bitch this side of D.C! I’ll give him that much. Brian tilts his head and gives another chuckle. Michaels: I thought to myself that you could be the new Brian Michaels when all was said and done. You were younger, smarter and stronger. So, I took you under my wing. You moved so quickly up the card. From the opening match to the Main Event, just like me. You were JUST…like….me. Maybe, just maybe, that’s what went wrong. Michaels looks at the mirror in the bathroom, admiring his facial features slowly and slowly touching them while maintaining his cool. Michaels: Maybe, just maybe, I’M…the reason you turned out the way you did. Maybe I taught you too well. Maybe I was the one who told you to have a killer instinct, and not to settle for anything but the best. I was the one who told you to create hell for your opponent, to look up their weaknesses. Mason Resoan, I HAVE NO WEAKNESSES! What drives me crazy…is that I created the student. I created the monster, and you turned your back on me. Michaels opens up his eyes as he comes to the sharp reality of flashing back to the night when he was run over. He shakes his head, not wanting to relive the memory. He bangs his head across the sink and then punches the mirror, shattering it. Michaels: And then…and then… Resoan claims to have come out with the ‘’master plan’’ to take me out, to run me over. Mason, I haven’t been the same man since that accident, since that one fateful day. I’ve never been able to look someone in the eyes and trust them again. I’ve never been able to look at my opponent the same way. I place the blame on you, Mason. Brian takes a deep breath before looking back up at the camera with his large green soulful eyes. He has hate in them as he shivers with confidence. Michaels: Resoan, I’ve been known…to do some crazy, motherfuckin’ things. I’ve been known…to get a little antsy, to be a little impatient…and I’ve been known to come out big in a big-time match. He looks around as some yelling is heard outside of the bathroom, and the commotion is coming closer to Michaels and the bathroom. Michaels: Hear that Mason? Each one of those knocks...is your heartbeat. It keeps going faster and faster...until...it stops... There is a sharp banging at the door, as it appears to be the police. They are at the door, knocking impatiently again. Michaels locks the door and stands in front of it, blocking it to prevent any officers from coming in. Michaels violently looks around and finds a pipe, putting it between the knob and the sink. Michaels: If there’s one thing that I know, it’s that Mason Resoan has accomplished a lot of things in MWE. He’s busted his ass off…but I’ve poured my heart, and my SOUL into this profession. Mason Resoan is in MWE for the cash and for the chicks. I’m in this…to prove to everybody out there, the doubters, ESPECIALLY D.C….that I’ve still got what it takes to be the very best wrestler in the World today. Michaels’ eyes begin bugging out, as he begins to yell over the police officers who are about to barge through the door. The officers are now in a full-blown scream, trying to control the rampant Brian Michaels. Michaels: Remember my lesson, Mason? Remember what I said, about analyzing your opponent’s past? That someone’s track record would ultimately teach you everything you needed to know? The officers finally barge through, smashing the door down in the process as Michaels attempts to fight off the police. Michaels: I’VE GOT NO HISTORY MASON! I’m a NEW man! Nothing that you have EVER seen can protect you from this Monday night! NOTHING MASON! The camera closes in on Michaels as the police put him to the ground and begin to put handcuffs on. Michaels: I’ve been through too much shit, because of YOU! Mason, I hate you so much I can’t put into motherfucking WORDS! THIS MONDAY…this Monday… Michaels’ yell turns into a bit of a sinister laugh as he begins shaking violently on the ground. Tera walks into the scene with a worried look on her face as the police officer goes to talk to her. Michaels: This time…Mason. There is no running…there is no D.C…there is nothing on God’s GREEN EARTH…that could prepare you for this match. Throw the lessons out. Throw the teaching out. This is the real FUCKING DEAL. Michaels…Resoan. One on one…this Monday, you’ll get yours Mason. You’ll…get…YOURS. The camera pans over to Tera, who kicks both officers in the balls as they bend over, grunting. She walks over to Michaels, helping him take off his handcuffs. Michaels: Know why, Mason?! I’ll face you ANYWHERE. I’ll face you in the ring, in jail, motherfucker, I will face you in the goddamn church! You’re going to feel the pain that I felt last year…and you…Mason Resoan…are going…to LOVE IT. Tera takes the handcuffs off of Michaels as Michaels punches the cameraman in a fit of rage, causing the camera to go to static. END.
