It's Always a Party With Zak; Cruz/BK RP

Sep 24 2009

Jesus Cruz

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
We fade in to a wooden door, with the golden numbers ‘21’. ......... And… we wait… ......... Well it’s… a clean door; seems sturdy. ......... I’m no wood expert, but it looks to be made of oak. ......... Or maybe it’s pine. I can’t tell. ......... ......... ......... Damn it, I may be a narrator, but how in depth can I describe a simple fucking door!? Somebody do something already! Knock, knock, knock. Thank you. Three hits break the silence, as the camera pans back to show thy knock-bringer. Cue Jesus Cruz. Come in! Jesus twists the handle and shoves the door open. Inside, it is pitch black. Uhh, you called? The lights shoot on, to show an apartment full of smoke-filled balloons. Standing in the middle of the room with a party hat on his head, and a plate full of special brownies in his hands, is Zak Warner. SURPRISE! What the hell? Congrats kid, you beat Renshaw! Big whoop-de-fucking-doo. Well, we need to celebrate, don’t cha think? Suuuuurrrrrreeeeee. How about we start by pinning the tail on the donkey? The smile disappears from Warner’s mug, as he clearly detects Cruz’s sarcasm. He tosses the plate of brownies aside, and turns around. Hmph, fine then. I guess you don’t want your present. Right then and there, Jesus changes gears. You… you got me a gift? Yep. Oh, you didn’t have to. It’s fine man. I found a few bucks stashed away. Warner reaches into his pocket and pulls out a wallet. He opens it up fully, and turns it upside down, to show it’s complete emptiness. See, not a cent left. Dude, that’s MY wallet! Warner pauses, and inspects the wallet quickly. Oh... I guess it is... Zak tosses the empty wallet back at Cruz with a smirk. Well, it went to a good cause. There was like fifty bucks in there! Don’t worry, you’ll like your gift, I promise. Cruz crosses his arms, clearly pissed, but is able to keep it to himself. After a deep breath, he asks: Well… where is it? Take a seat. It’ll be here shortly. Jesus sits back on Zak’s stained up couch, as Warner tosses Cruz his party hat. Wear it. Cruz grunts, as he puts the pointy purple and pink hat on. Perfect. I'll be right back. Where do you think you’re going? I’ve gotta get my camera. For what? I want to see the look on your face when the gift arrives. Umm, ok? But we have our own camera man right here. An arm comes into view from behind the camera. It waves at Zak. Well...Yeah... but we need more than one angle. More then one angle?... Why? ...Don’t you worry about it. Just sit back and relax. And with that, Warner walks away leaving Cruz puzzled. ...Whatever... Jesus reclines back in the chair, and puts his arms behind his head to get comfortable. Come here camera guy. Let’s shoot this promo. The shot straightens up, and zooms into the fighter’s face. It’s time. I’ve got Brad Kane this week, with the winner to move on in the tournament. The hype around this guy is huge. But am I scared? Nah. His shallow and unrealistic threats leave me unfazed. “You take a shot at me like that and I'll break your arm’’ “You'll find yourself in the same position as Blight did” “I'll make a former MMA star tap out right in the middle of the ring” “You'll end up broken and beaten” You really think you intimidate me Brad? You talk about your past fame and glory, and how your experience will play the deciding factor in our match. Well, the past is the past. This is the here and now. And from watching your matches here so far in MWE, I fear nothing. Think about it. We’re comparing me, a man with years of intense training in violence, against you, a man whose only workout is ‘thrusting’. Cruz perks up in his seat, now sitting right along the edge. This tournament is the world to me. From day one, I stated that I wanted the World Title. And now, I’ve been given a chance. Cruz abruptly stands up. I refuse to be overlooked any longer. It’s not a bump on the road Kane. It’s a dead end. So you need to take a different route. I can’t guarantee that I’ll run right through you. I can’t say injure you, and leave you a broken man. I can’t say I’ll bust you wide open. Or knock you clean out. I don’t make meaningless promises. But I can make one. I will walk out the victor. Knock, knock, knock, knock, knock. Cruz looks up. Uhh? She’s here!! …She? Zak runs to the door with a camera in hand, and opens it up. Filling the doorway, is a big, black, cherry-lipped, manatee-sized prostitute. B.K. made his porno, and now you get to make yours. Jesus’s eyes bulge out of his head. He attempts to speak, but instead gags. Settle down, and let ME do the gagging! The large whore begins to approach Cruz, but he leaps back and falls over the couch to the floor. NO! GET THE HELL AWAY! What’s wrong hun? Cruz gets to his feet, and gets in her face. You’re an ugly fat slut, that’s what’s wrong! SLAP! Cruz hits the floor! She towers over him, and begins to yell. Big women struggle so much, and we don’t need men like YOU degrading us! No no, uh, sorry! And um, yes, big women can be sexy! So what makes me ugly then!? ‘Cause I’m black?! Aw Cruz, you racist bastard. Hey, fuck you Zak! The prostitute kicks off her heels and puts her fists in front of her face. Come on fighter, let’s do this. Show me what you’ve got. Jesus scrambles to his feet, and the hefty female swings a baboon-like right hand, which Cruz ducks. She continues swinging bombs, as Cruz backpedals, dodging everything she throws. Suddenly, Jesus sees an opportunity and capitalizes. Her dives onto the ground, and picks up a brownie that Warner had dropped earlier. He waves it in front of her face. See it fatty? See the brownie? Cruz chucks it right out the door. GO GET IT! She chases it like a dog to a bone. Cruz runs over, and slams the door behind her, bolting it shut. The obese huh?… They just can’t resist chocolate… Cruz doesn’t find it funny, obviously. What the fuck was that about!? It was a test. And you passed it. And now, you're your own man. I'm no longer your manager. Really?! ...Noooooope. I'm having too much fun to stop now. This clip will bring in a couple million views, easily, online. NO! YOU CAN'T! Zak holds out the camera. Snatch it from my hand then young grasshopper. Jesus waits. Waits. Waits! He swipes for it! A MISS! You have failed me. Now, you will suffer the embarrassment of youtube viewers. Before Cruz can comment, Zak struts off with the camera in hand. Jesus drops his head and clasps his hands over his face, as we fade to close.
