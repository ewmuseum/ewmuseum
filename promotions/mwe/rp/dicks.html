DICKS!; Development/Funny RP Time!

Apr 5 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A coffee shop has to be one of the most common and recognizable places in the world. It’s the morning fix for the nightly bug of exhaustion and drunken hazes. If coffee didn’t exist there may be more people late for work or asleep at the wheel so to speak than the amount of people there are unemployed at this day and age. Coincidentally this is the ideal place Sinc quite enjoys to visit on his dragging mornings and dead end days off. Little did he know that he would meet a familiar face of a long lost friend, but first let’s go with the usual waiter. Sir how many clumps of sugar did you mention you preferred in your coffee on this fine morning? Now just a few minutes ago Sinc had told this waiter two times how many “sugars” he wanted, but this uncanny beagle can’t seem to raise his ears enough to pay attention to it’s loyal companion. Are you…are you freakin kidding me right now? I told you two clumps buddy, now be a happy camper and go sweeten that cup already, if there’s one thing I can’t stand it’s when the deaf, dumb, and blind do a better job than your local short term memory loss waiters. The waiter just rolls his eyes and parts ways with Sinclair in a bustling undertone of shuffling back to his work area. But as the waiter moves himself from Sinc’s radius, Sinc sees someone he hoped never to see again, hoped that this familiar person continued to think Sinc was gone from MWE, and if this person saw that Sinc has returned, he would never leave him alone. God dammit first I meet Kendra a few months ago at a restaurant now I meet Nate here, I gotta stop going out one of these days. The anxiety and nerves just now begin to set in Sinc’s blood as his veins run cold and his past with Nate Rellington begins to come alive once again. Sitting about 5 tables in front of Sinc’s window seat he hopes Nate doesn’t notice him as he covers his face with the usual newspaper trick. Just as he does this the waiter confronts Sinc with his coffee and manages to address him firmly. Here’s your coffee, two clumps as you wanted Mr. Mercier…. The waiter is not happy as he almost slams down the cup in front of Sinc as some of the hot liquid splashes on the paper causing Sinc to let go of today’s influential news in a furious flinch. Those who personally know Sinc know he has quite a short fuse, and this was not ok with him. Sinc stands up and pokes his head right in front of the waiter’s nose staring him down righteously. Nice goin jackass! Now beat it before I demand my money back! Nearly everyone is watching what’s going on and sadly so is Nate. The waiter marches away as Sinclair wipes down his navy blue sweater and his black jeans snuggly fit around his waist. After a few moments the people go back to their business around the shop and continue to nibble their croissants or sip their hot drinks. But at this point Nate is already approaching Sinc in an alarming pace. Brim tan cowboy hat as per usual, and a nice stand out brown suit. In this point in time Sinc knows it’s unavoidable and gives in. Hello again cowboy. Once again you seem to find me….somehow. I don’t know if it’s fate or what, but your like my old jock strap when it comes to finding me, you can’t seem to slip off my personal junk. Nate never laughed at Sinc’s jokes, especially jokes directed at him. As promiscuous as Nate is talking to Sinc again, he musters his business mindset and casual approach that he uses on all his clients to try and persuade Sinc to become his friend and manager again. I can understand that you and Kendra want some time apart, and that you did not want to let me in on your return, but man we were partners buddy, and you disappeared off the face of the earth and ended our partnership and friendship in a heartbeat, you skewed the meat and butchered the chicken, we were the end all BE all. Sinc was getting a migraine just listening to Nate’s voice. He did not want Nate back as a manager, Kendra was controlling Sinc’s life way too much, and their recent break from each other is needed. To slap a mofo like Nate across the face right here and now is not too far out of the question. I don’t think I want Kendra’s lackey on my back anymore. I don’t want anything that would remind me of her right now. I have a match I got to focus on and I’m quite enjoying my secluded time alone Nate thank you very much. The thought of Nate annoying Sinc right now was an anomaly, it wasn’t the usual sarcasm or the friendly chatter, it was more of an acquaintance now, and both individuals realize this. Nate doesn’t give in just yet. What you and Kendra are dealing with right now does not concern me. I was always in it to help you go big time, and you choosing to leave the MWE was purely your choice, not mine. It is understandable that you would want to leave, but after hearing of your return, I have been wanting to be by your side again for old times sake. I thought you wanted your friend back, but I guess I was mistaken. It was all an illusion right Sinclair? I never helped ya, I just hurt ya. Sinc is mourning himself at the udder denial Nate is in right now and the grief he is emitting as they both stand broadened by reunions and blinded by memories. I know you mean well, but I think re-accepting you this early into my career is a bad move bro. You’ve been a good confidence booster and a great manager so far, but at this part of the game I just don’t know how it will all end up. I need to sit down. Sinc seats himself back down as Nate continues standing dry eyed and dazed waiting for a more prominent answer. Sinc eyes Nate pathetically. What are you waitin for? Chuck Norris to come and break your jaw? Go ‘head sit down cowboy. Nate nods with a willful tip of his hat and joins Sinc at his table. Sinc seems quite stressed and unsure about what he wants for his future and whether coming back to MWE was the right choice or not. The mist of life seems to nip the end of a clueless person when they least expect it. You may be a bit hesitant now Sinc, but I guarantee you if you continue to let me help you in your career I can still push you to the top, it’s not too late, it’s NEVER too late. When I was your manager you got lots of wins kid, you were on your way to greatness. And the MOMENT you won that US title you vanished off the face of the earth and that was a blow to my heart kiddo. I thought that’s what you wanted, fame, recognition, and respect. Thick and thin I was there man, I don’t know what happened since. Sinc draws a deep and vagrant breath almost inhaling life, and time itself. This breath is followed through with bold, and sagacious words. You know I respect you. Your like my very own version of Willie Nelson….on your good days. And your right when you said we were partners, and friends. My only concern is that when I turn my back will you stab it? With the whole shindig going on with Lindsey and Ace Cannon as well as Joey Dallas’s new man ego Joseph Sharpman coming out of the closet it all seems like people are hard to trust these days. My old tag team with Joseph is a prime example. I need to trust that you will have my back 24/7.…if you can’t do that, then we can’t remain aligned in business and life. Not a year goes by that Nate never seizes an opportunity, or does some dirty work for a quick cash payment, but such antics are needed for survival, and Nate is simply a survivor. And like Sinc said to A-Rod the other day, only the strong survive. If I backstab or betray you in your career, I promise you that whatever money I make, whatever stocks or bonds I have saved up, their all yours. I would never turn my back on a partner and now a considered friend. To betray you would be like betraying my own brother, or my own son. No matter how cheesy I sound now, it will all pay off in the end. Can we learn to trust each other again? Sinc is now feeling a sense of sympathy for his “considered friend” and decides to give this another shot. After all Nate had done no wrong, and he was there for Sinc the entire time. Alright Nate Claudius Rellington, you got yourself a partner again. But I got bigger problems, Alejandro “A-Hole” Rodriguez. I’ve already basically cut him down to size, no pun intended or no homo, the other day when I talked some smack to his lifestyle and his history in MWE. I am intrigued to really see what he’s capable of rebutting to me after that horrific massacre of a promo, but I could be wrong, and once again underestimated him, but we’ll see. Nate hides a smile as he shows a sense of joy becoming Sinc’s go to guy again. Like a lost puppy finding his home, Nate has claimed his place in this world once again. He flickers his eyes and adjusts around the context of what Sinc had mentioned. I know you’ve done a great deal of harm to A-Rod with your words, and I won’t question the things you have said, but just remember that he plays the heel and you play the face, now who do you think has more freedom in their words? Just be prepared in what A-Rod has cooked up for you Sinc, it may be more than you think. Sinc can only laugh at the suggestive lyrics sensually poured out of this toneless song Nate had just explained. But A-Rod is known as one of the greatest competitors in the MWE, Sinc has acknowledged this historical fact, A-Rod is the stick and Sinc is the stone, who wins? The guy has fallen down some in the MWE. He is in cahoots with someone who is nowhere near memorable or worthwhile in a ring, and has an IQ of a pebble. Their facing low end tag teams and Prima Donna alliances. Now you tell me how hard I should try on a man who dwells on his constant past and can’t get back in the present of winning streaks and big time card boosting victories. I have challenged him and called him out to give me his very best, will he? No one really knows. Something clicks when you tell someone they can’t do something, or cannot move on to bigger and better things. This is the type of aspect Sinc hopes to bring out of A-Rod, his tip top athleticism and untapped abilities. Little bastards like him wanna go to the moon or meet Michael Jackson so they can replace his chimp, you my friend, make dreams happen. Alejandro? He just keeps dreamin and he won’t ever stop. Now explain to me why you would ever doubt yourself with a pubasaurus like him? Nate trying to say funny lines is like a fat kid trying to run the track, it just doesn’t feel right. But Sinc understands the humor is just to explain the difference between a degraded troll like A-Rod and a professional athlete like Sinc. If I doubted myself I would be low card and biting my nails for a victory. Don’t be stupid like Alejandro Nate. I like you, but I don’t like you that way. The thing is, A-Hole hasn’t stepped it up enough for me to really take him seriously. He has dived into big time matches, won a few, and then tries less, and loses even more. People like him make me think about what the sport of wrestling has become, a scamming, effortless, pool of nonsense. Does he stay because he wants to win? Does he stay because he loves the sport? Or does he stay for the jobbing money, you tell me. He needs to prove himself to Sinc Mercier in order to get RECOGNIZED by Sinc Mercier. He just hasn’t done that yet. Nate raises a finger to Sinc with a curious grin on his face to a word Sinc seemed to forget to emphasize at the end. Key word there I believe is YET. But what if he does it in this match with you, then what? Your screwed you idiot. THAT’S what. If you turn your cheek away from this guy now it’s all over, all your words and your cute little phrases will be gone, kaput. So be a man and accept that. Because unless you give it your all every match and at every arena, then you got your guard down bucko. Every time Nate seems to hit Sinc hard with his truthful words which always irritated Sinc in more ways than one. But in the end it helped him as well, so he takes it with open ears. You know I hate it when your right, but I can’t shoot you down on your points Rellington, you got some good ones. Luckily I got a nice little trick up my sleeve. And your involved, come with me. Sinc gets to his feet and drags Nate by his collar to follow him. Nate reluctantly jolts his head and his eyes and follows Sinc in a forceful strut. The scene fades into an odd movie trailer of some sort. Coming to a theater near you….two guys just trying to have fun…are called into a task force of extreme circumstances. When there’s no one else willing to do the job, you can count on them. Scene comes into play with two agents of D.I.C.K.S. spying on a drug cartel. We got a problem Officer Renzo. PROBLEMS!?!?! SON, THERE'S NO SUCH THING AS PROBLEMS!!!!!!!!!!!! Sinc Mercier as officer Tiny Renzo and Nate Rellington as Sheriff Wow Bazaar. Scene shifts to Sheriff Bazaar in his office with his secretary. Debbie I told you for the last time we need to get them outta there! The situation has gotten a lot worse, violence has erupted in the sound city of New Mexico, the cartels are out of control! When all hell breaks loose, sometimes you got to take one for the team. Scene opens to Officer Renzo holding a bomb in the middle of a mob of drug cartels. I have the power!!!!! I AM YOUR MASTER MOTHA F*CKAS! BOW TO M-*GIANT EXPLOSION* Gary Coleman as the one legged hobo. Scene fades into the hobo talking to a citizen of Mexico City. Look man, I’m just tryin to make a damn livin, the judges are ON my ass dawg! I only have like, ten bucks bro. WHATCHOO TALKIN BOUT FOOL!?!?! Hard times in the streets can lead to new beginnings. Tom Cruise and Danny Devito as the mob kings Don Guato and Harold Dutchess. You know Don it feels GOOD to be king. HOT DAMN! YOUR TELLIN ME! THIS NEW SH*T THEY JUST SENT ME MAKES ME GO CRAZY!*Lots of giggling and movement*I’M THE BEST! I’M THE BEST! And you can bet that this movie will be ACTION PACKED! Random cycle of lots of car explosion scenes. And a very special appearance with Ronald McDonald as Orlando Succubus. Scene switches to Succubus in a room filled with drug dealers. I like making kids happy, and when THEY smile, I smile, you see me smiling right now? NO! I NEED KIDS! AND I NEED MY COKE! Fix that heavenly powder up right now, before I start LOVIN IT all over your sorry ass! Are you ready for one of the most controversial movies of all time? We sure are. Scene opens to Sheriff Bazaar falling to his knees slowly while dramatic music plays, Officer Renzo is right across on his chest in pain. Destroyed cars and dead corpses everywhere. NOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! Sheriff Bazaar falls flat on his belly, a group of drug cartels begin surrounding the corps in slowmo and they begin to take his stuff one by one. Shoes, belt, weaponry, ammo, and gear. And in super slowmo Officer Renzo screams again. NOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! Join us as the hardships and heartfelt scenarios come to life in this wonderful adventure. Scene opens with Officer Renzo helping give birth to a baby with a widow in a safety tent. It’s a beautiful baby boy*Hands baby to mother* OH GOD! THIS BABY IS THE DEVIL! PUT IT BACK IN! So when the time comes, WILL YOU WATCH IT!?! The odd video fizzes out and the real scene ends. FIN.
