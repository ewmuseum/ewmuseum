One Beggining, One End.; Mysterious G vs Samuel Steele

Dec 21 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The only thing in sight it the cloudy sky. Not overly cloudy, it's bright blue, but there are clouds. But there is also somthing very odd about the picture, it all looks waivy. As if there was somthing in front of the camera causing the picture to become distorted. And then somthing does step into frame of the camera. It is The Mysterious G.... upside down. He is standing there, wearing a pair of very dark blue jeans and a tight t-shirt, possibly that of a famous profecional wrestler, and then his trademark mask, which without he would look like everyone else in the world. He begins to speak in a very monatone voice, with even himself being distorted by the cameraview. Come here, you damn camera fool... I am not a jive turkey. I am a man dammit. I am a human mother fucking being. He chuckles after saying this as he places his hands into his pockets. He's still upside down for some reason. Does anyone remember who said that? Do you remember the man responcible for those words? Can you dig into your memory, and relive that moment? He shakes his head. I didn't think so. Neither could I, to be honest. But those words, as proper as I may have made them sound, were the very first words the MWE world heard from the man I will be facing at Adrenaline Rush, Samuel Steele. Those words have been followed by some of the most confusing and utter stupid things I have ever heard from one person. And please excuse such a simple word like "Stupid", but I personally feel there is no other word better to describe this situation. He begin pacing back and forth, still upside down. The camera is still quite distorted. His hands, still in their pockets. But onto the matter at hand... The fans of MWE have decided that one of the two of us must suffer outside of the ring following Adrenaline Rush. You see, if I lose this match, Samuel Steele wins the X-TV title, and I lose my hidden identity. I lose this mask. But you know what? I'm ok with that. Wether you know it or not, I've come here and I've done what I set out to do with this mask. My mission is complete. I no longer need it. He stops walking, but is still standing with his left side facing the camera. But then again, who needs anything in this life? He begins walking again. Still distorted, still upside down. There is another possible outcome for this match. I can win this match, and the X-TV title, and in doing so, I will be ending the career or Samuel Steele... Now I am a fair man, and I would never wish this upon another human being. However, the fans of MWE have choosen this fate, so it shall be. But just for the sake of history, lets take a look at the career of Samuel Steele... And it just so happens, I'm standing in the very place is started. The Mysterious G stops walking, and the camera begins moving up, which is actually revealing his feet, as he is upside down. But as soon as the camera reachs his feet, the upside down, and the distortedness, become clear. Things are right side up, and the reason for that, is because the camera was recording everything through the reflection of a pond. A pond with many ducks swimming in it, as well as a few other birds here and there. Next to the pond, we see a park bench, as well as multiple other park benchs off in the distance, some occupied, others empty. Most of the occupied benchs are surrounded by pidgons, possibly because of bread crumbs being fed to them. But the focus isn't on the other benchs, it's on the one right in the middle of the frame of the camera, where the Mysterious G has decided to take a seat. It wasn't really all that long ago that Samuel Steele sat in this very bench. Once a distraught man with drug and alcohol problems, he sat here, and began his wrestling career by falling back into the hole he was in when he decided wrestling was the way out. He got drunk, and he smoked, and god knows what he did off of the camera. He reachs under the park bench, and picks up what looks to be an empty bottle of Captain Morgan. This bottle. This caused his first downfall in life. He managed to get away from it, from the drugs, the girls, the bad life. He wanted to start clean. And for that, as wierd as the guy may act, I respect him. However... The Mysterious G stands up, and tosses the empty bottle into the pond. Multiple ducks scatter from the area the bottle hit, as ripples begins flowing through the water. He tosses his respect away, just as I just tossed what took his respect away. Now standing, he begins walking down the path towards the other benchs. People off in the distance stare at him, a man wearing a mask, but he doesn't look towards them, he still got one eye staring into the camera. Samuel Steele, your career has ended before it really had a chance to begin, and you have no one to blame but yourself. Everyone has a story. Everyone has demons they must fight, and you have lost that fight, just as you will lose the fight for the X-TV title. But because I am not a bad man, I want to help you. And by beating you, and having you removed from your position of employment, I will be helping you. When your back on the umemployment line, remembering those few moments when you were drug free and happy, maybe that will be added motivation, added ammo, to fight your demons yet again. Maybe you will realize the fault of your ways, and try to correct yourself again. Maybe... The Mysterious G is inturupted by a pidgeon. Obviously the pidgeon hasn't said anything, he mearly dropped off a gift on The Mysterious G's t-shirt. The Mysterious G stops walking, and stands perfectly still for about two seconds, barely breathing, probably unaware of what exactly just happened. Finally, realizing the gift that had been recieved, he takes the expensive looking t-shirt off, revealing a wife beater underneath, and holds the t-shirt out in front of him, staring at the "present". Well, this is ironic. You see, Samuel, everyone gets shit in their life. Everyone. Life isn't about how you avoid it, because it is going to happen, one way or another. It's how you react. You react with anger. I react with anger, but unlike you, I'm not making a scene. I'm not making people disrespect me. You, on the other hand, have lost about all the respect you have ever had. The Mysterious G casually drops the t-shirt, and stares right into the camera. A young couple in the background appear to be laughing at what has just happened with the pidgeon, but The Mysterious G pays them no attention, instead he chooses to focus on the camera. At Adrenaline Rush, we both have somthing to lose. But it's not my mask, and it's not your job. It's the X-TV title. And right now, it has more prestige then you do, and thats why I want it. Samuel Steele, I want you to bring yor best. And if you beat me, then by gosh, you may earn some of that respect I was just accusing you of losing. But if I beat you... That'll give you alot more free time at home. Maybe you can use that to actually clean up a bit? Get your life on track? Maybe get another job? Who knows. But what I do know... is good things happen to good people. And I'm going to get mine. The Mysterious G walks off camera as it slowly drops to the water, causing the same distorted effect as earlier.
