Euthanization imminent.; What the FUCK is a snozberry?

Sep 21 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Scene opens with Aeron sitting in a hotel room, a TV remote in hand. Beside him, a nightstand with a can of cola and his cellphone. Aeron clicks a button shutting the TV off. Aeron: D.C, comparing me to a childrens movie character? I dont think Disney approves of the devil in his cartoons. Aeron sticks his hand behind the couch and pulls something out. Its a calender, Aeron flips through it. Aeron: I know you have a problem counting. Two months, eight weeks, fifty-five days, eighteen thousand seven hundred and twenty minutes. How far do you want me to go? I know how long two months is. The only "Fruity loops" I have, is the fuckin cereal with that fucking toucan as a representitive. You may have defended it weekly, but you know what? I didnt ask not to defend it, and it wouldn't of mattered. So even if I DID have it up on the line every week, I would have retained, every, single, week. Aeron stops for a second before grabbing his cola, taking a large sip. He puts it down and lets his hand trace the nightstand looking for his phone. He picks it up and opens it, a Kyocera Switchback KX21. He runs his finger over the middle button, searching for a name, Dusty. He clicks the innermost circle button. He starts to press some of the buttons as he speaks again. Aeron: You defended it in Ladder Matches, Table matches, Battle Royals, Triple Threats, Singles, Hardcore matches, Falls anywhere. Whoopdy fucking do, want a god damned cookie? You may have grown up in the bad part of Cali, but you know what? I grew up in the bad part of the world. I defended my ass in every scenario your peanut-brain can think of. In bars, in warehouses, in parking lots. I've lived through cage matches, grudge matches, knife fights. I've been through hell, and it was alot more interesting than this. You think I have the loudest mouth in MWE? Look who runs their mouth non-stop in their promos. I took a dive into your past, into your personal existence. I wasnt teaching those crack-addicts, I was reminding you about yourself. I just didnt think your mental capacity could handle all of that stupid. Being hit in the head so many times. Aeron sighs again as he looks down at the phone. Him and Dusty, texting while hes talking. Aeron: No one even said anything about checking you out. I do realize there were two hermaphrodites in that film of yours. I dont look forward to our match, Im looking forward to beating your skinny self to a bloody fucking pulp. You want to know who are professional wrestlers? Sinc Mercier, Brian Michaels, Kanto, Sonny, Myself and Jekyl. We are pro wrestlers, we show no mercy, we give no second chances. Nothing at all, no remorse, no emotion. Aeron chuckles as he stands up, coke in hand, cellphone in pocket. He walks towards the door, opening it, letting the cameraman through before closing it and walking down the hall. He clicks the elevator button waiting for it to come up, pacing back and forth on the red velvet carpet. Aeron: As I've said before, I've lived through hell, like I'd care if you did anything to my brain-dead parents. I packed my bags and high-tailed it out of my family when I was thirteen. The elevator dings, Aeron steps inside, the cameraman right behind him. He runs his finger over the buttons before pressing the ground floor. Aeron runs through D.C's promo in his mind. "D.C.: what the f*** would have been the point. Maybe you should have BEEN there, before you start ASSUMING things... See I went to the hotel, that every single wrestler was staying at... and destroyed Alejandros car in the parking lot of said hotel... THEN sat it on fire..." Aeron: The point? The point D.C. is that your afraid, like a children at a sleepover without his nightlight. You were AFRAID of Alejendro, you were afraid he was going to whoop your sorry ass. The elevator door rings open, he walks through it, a five star hotel. He walks through the front sliding door, passing the pedestrians. He steps on someones shoe as they turn to look at him. Pedestrian: Hey, dickhead, you stepped on my shoe. Aeron walks up to him, they stare down for a few seconds. The man quickly averts his gaze and walks off. Aeron chuckles slightly before continuing on down the road. Aeron: I assume things more than you change your mind. That is hard to accomplish, anyway setting it, the car on fire, like a pyro. -Aeron pulls out a lighter and lights it.- Fire, is a dangerous thing D.C, if your not careful, your going to get burned. -Aeron holds his hand over the flame for a few seconds before letting it go.- Aeron searches through his pockets, pulling out two knucklecut gloves. He slips them on, one hand at a time, than pulling out his phone. He dials a number waiting for someone to pick up on the other end. Aeron: No one even said anything about teaching those fucked up kids, they were there, I was there, if I was teaching them, I'd be teaching them math, or some stupid shit. My gimmick? A street fighter, you cant just get hooked up as a street fighter. Just like last week, Im going to show you my gimmick. That job, the one I was offered? Remember? y- Dusty: Yo, Aeron. Aeron: Yah, Im almost there, tell them to throw in a second. Dusty: You serious? -Aeron laughs- Aeron: Yeah man, double up. Dusty: Alright than, I'll meet you at the door. Aeron hangs up the phone and puts it back into his pocket. He cracks his knuckles continuing. Aeron: Yeah, anyway, Im suprised you remembered. Anyway, I took that job part time. Turns out they got this jig set up everywhere. Aeron walks down a spiral staircase, Dusty sitting at the bottom holding the door open. Aeron just nods at Dusty as the two walk in. In the background the announcer introducing the competitors. Aeron lifts his shirt up and over his head and tosses it to Dusty. Aeron: D.C, do you think Im afraid of Sonny? I mean, sure he can wrestle, but he aint scary. All you gotta do is take shots at his brother and his fucked up life and you'll get by like nothing. A hot knife through butter. Aeron walks into a large warehouse like arena. More fans screaming, Aeron at his end of the arena. He steps on a tooth, knocked out of one of the competitors, it crumbles to dust under his foot. He glances around for the other opponents. At the end of another long hallway, stands two large african american males, both in blue wifebeaters and shorts. Aeron smiles and looks up at Dusty whos smiling. Aeron: Street fighter, handicap. Two v one. See D.C, falls count anywhere, no rules. Announcer: ...And the challenger, excuse me... ChallenGERS. Bob and Vincent Richardson. Aeron cracks his neck side to side before giving it a full spin. The two men look at eachother before running straight at Aeron. Aeron readies himself for the fight, the two men throwing hard rights and jabs, Aeron serpantining between them, blocking the rights. He throws a quick knee up at one of them, Bob. Bob recoils as Vincent looks back sparingly, still focusing on Aeron. Aeron: Am I the only one who -Grunt- notices that D.C. has Lindsey -Grunt- say alot of his shit for him? Aeron throws his arms up, protecting his face from a barrage of rights and lefts. Someone from the croud yelled something out, the two men come to an abrupt halt. They walk backwards laughing as one lifts the other up one of the sides, he recieves something from the man that had yelled out. Announcer: Whats going on here? Crowd assistance! The man jumps down handing a switch-blade to his teammate. Aeron groans and looks up at Dusty who tosses down a bat that was concealed in Aerons shirt. Aeron taps the bat on his foot. Aeron: Lindsey, I love girls just as much as the next guy, but I dont get distracted like you and Clown. Overrated and hated, Even if I was paid, I wouldnt want to be you D.C. The two men now with their switchblades readied, walk towards Aeron who readies his bat. He runs at them, swinging his bat ferociously, landing a hit on Vincents arm, causing him to release his knife. Bob rushes at Aeron, who is still swinging his bat. The camera is forced to shut off but screams of pain and grunts are heard. Announcer: And here is your winner still undefeated, AERON BEOWULLLLFFFF. Aeron is seen walking out with his shirt around his shoulders, being trailed by Dusty. Aeron: Asking me to blow you? I aint gay, your gay for asking me to do such a thing D.C. I've chewed through grizzle tougher than you. And heres a little geometry lesson for you. The wrestling wring is square, not a circle, good luck "wrestling circles" around me. I am the immovable force, IMMOVABLE, you know not able to be moved by human force. D.C, no longer shall you with that whore of yours into MWE like your the top dog. Euthanization imminent, date of injection Monday. Aeron starts a hollow laughter as the camera dims to black.
