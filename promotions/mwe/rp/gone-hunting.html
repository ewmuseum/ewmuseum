Gone Hunting...; A JJ Executioner RP

Sep 25 2008

JJ Executioner

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
****OOC: Again this looks short but for some reason the page seems wider. It's because of the quotes I think*** “If the Executioner doesn’t get you Sullivan, someone will.” The camera opens up in a garden outside a large mansion. There are a couple of lights on as it is 9pm and pretty much pitch black. The camera descends onto the turf and then into the bushes. It turns and faces the house. Through a large set of patio doors is a gym. We can clearly see a rowing machine and a weight bench. Closest to the window is a treadmill, which is in use. The picture moves up to reveal James Sullivan. Dressed in baggy red shorts and brand new trainers and listening to an iPod that is tucked into the pocket, Sullivan is running steadily and rhythmically pounding the equipment. His eyes are covered by a sleek pair of sunglasses but he has a tense look of pure determination and focus on his face. Voice (whisper): Run little Jimmy....run faster!” The rough voice with a harsh London accent is JJ Executioner, however he cannot be seen. JJ: “Does this look like a man who knows no fear, suffers no nerves, knows no intimidation? I see a nervous prey, a creature who does not know which way to turn. Sullivan, you know you are on borrowed time and for a man who claims not to suffer nerves, I see past you and your charade.” The scene suddenly cuts to a wide angle view of the castle, where we have seen JJ in recent promos. Only the building itself is visible, JJ is nowhere to be seen. There is a gale blowing and autumnal leaves whip around. JJ: “And I am going to take advantage. You have earned no respect and deserve precisely none.....just like your mentors *evil cackle* you will suffer at my hands. Your trauma, your pain will go a little way to correcting the misjustices of my last week” We see the mansion again. Sullivan flicks off the running machine and steps off, taking off the iPod headphones and placing it on a fireplace. He makes a quick phone call from a mobile then picks up a remote and points it at the far wall. From the far wall, a large 64’’ television moves down from the ceiling. Outside, the camera moves closer from out of the bushes. It is now jittery and unsteady. Sullivan takes a seat on a large recliner. He lies back as far as he can and pats himself down with a small towel that was lying on the arm. He tries to relax but shuffles uncomfortably. JJ: “You have known no challenge like what you will be facing at Mayhem. You claim to back away from nothing and no one but your eyes tell a different story. I see you James. I see you doubting yourself and your ability. You will be desperate to beat me James but your desperation will be your downfall. For your attempts to beat me at Mayhem, to stay away from your mystery man will be as futile as the attempts of your friends Stephen and Mason. You will all end up on the same path James. Try as you may, this is one challenge, one obstacle that will beat you.” The camera again cuts back to the castle. This time we see the yard with the gallows. A loud voice can be heard. QUOTE Ryan Adams: BURST LIMIT! BURST LIMIT! [/center] JJ: “And whoever wants you out of the MWE” QUOTE *The arena goes dark. The titantron starts a countdown, 5...4...3, and then it stops. The screen goes dark again as Sullivan looks up confused. A small light on the titantron flicks on and the outline of a man can be seen. Only his sweatshirt can be seen and it says "You are next." Sullivan went from pumped up and angry to confused and scared.* Adams: “Who in the world is that!” ???: “Sullivan, poor Sullivan. You seem to be confused. You seem to not be able to win your matches. Why is that James? Is it because you lack talent? Do you lack motivation? Are still distraught at the fact that Stephen was killed? Well get over it, or I can guarantee you that you will follow in his footsteps.” [/center] JJ: “Whoever wants to destroy you.” QUOTE ???: “ You will find out don't worry about that. What you should worry about, however, is shaping up. Next week, you will have a match, against who I am unaware of, but you better win that match. Bad things will and can happen to you if you lose. People don't like me. They flee in terror like bugs. Kanto heard about me and look what happened to him...he left! Gone he will not risk being embarrassed by the ultimate force MWE has ever seen.” James: Is that so, and what do you know about Mayhem Wrestling Entertainment. What makes you the ultimate force? You are full of it. You have not even stepped into the ring and you are going to sit there and tell me that you are the ultimate force. Come into the ring and reveal yourself and then boast to me about being the best. If you can beat me, then I will admit, yes, you are indeed the best. ???: “Beat you, that is no problem. Actually having a match with you though? That would be a waste of mine and your time. Honestly, if you could actually remember for a second...well then you would be able to realize that stepping into the ring with me is an instant loss.” [/center] JJ: “They will need to step in line and pick up the pieces that I leave. Ultimately taking you out of the MWE will not be a difficult task following what I plan on doing to you!” Eventually the camera is back again outside James Sullivan’s mansion. The camera is now even closer, barely a yard outside the patio doors. Sullivan seems to have fallen asleep in the recliner. Suddenly a phone rings so loudly it can be heard outside. The camera moves back hesitantly for a second. Sullivan wakes with a start before realising what it is. He picks it up, talks quickly and hangs up before quickly picking up a white t-shirt, sliding it on and heading it out of the door. JJ: “Treasure each and every breath James but don’t struggle, as that will only make things worse. It will not be long before you feel your last gasp slip away, courtesy of the Hangmans’ Noose.” The camera turns away from the house. Facing away, the person holding the camera is revealed. It is JJ Executioner, he continues holding the camera and in his other hand, he raises a rope, tied in a noose. He begins to laugh manically. The scene ends abruptly as the camera falls to the floor and smashes.
