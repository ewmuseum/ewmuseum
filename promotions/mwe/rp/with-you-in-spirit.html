With you in spirit

Apr 15 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Devlin Sharpe is shown riding on his motorcycle down a highway suddenly he jerks left and heads towards a bridge, before reaching the bridge however he stops and parks his bike and gets off and begins walking on a bridge in pitch black darkness, only his face can be faintly seen by the little amount of light provided by the single lamp post on the bridge, slowly as Devlin approached the lamp post more of his outfit could be made out, he was wearing a black hooded top, a pair of black jeans and a cream coloured pair of Hi Top Converses, he approached the edge of the bridge and stared out to the water as he was deep in thought of his life and his future in the MWE Devlin Sharpe: Battle of the MWE Graduates eh? well isn't this just dandy, Peter KMac who I already defeated before and Nic Kraven who to me is a nobody for the MWE and I can say that because I am... The street light glitters in his eyes for a moment as a smile grows on his face Devlin Sharpe: The best damn wrestler in the MWE and thats a fact Suddenly his face sinks as he remembers the last time he said that, the scene shifts to a time in the past as Devlin Sharpe is at a press conference as he is surrounded by a bunch of reporters and flashing lights, Devlin Sharpe is in a suit and is standing on a podium, he looks around at all the cameras and begins to speak Devlin Sharpe: Ladies and Gentleman I know I have been losing recently however that is only because I have decided that I am better than anyone else therefore I don't need to bother fighting anyone else, all I have to do is beat a select few that I deem worthy of facing me therefore I have a list of superstars here and I plan to beat every single one of them in a row week by week Reporter: What happens if you can't? Devlin Sharpe: then of course I allow the MWE Management to fire me The reporters all gasped as flashes went off in Devlin's face as photos were taken and he smiled a smile of uncertainty, the weight of what he just said just about sinking in, he had to keep his feelings underneath and not show weakness in front of the reporters Devlin Sharpe: But of course that won't happen, trust me, I believe in my skill I believe in my ability I believe in my tr-... He paused and bowed his head as he reallised he was going against everything his trainer told him, he closed his eyes as he tried to think of what to say then his eyes flew open and he smiled Devlin Sharpe: Devilishly good looks Devlin smiled bravely at the cameras as he allowed the press to take pictures of him, if you looked carefully you can tell the fear deep inside Devlins eyes as he himself was unsure of his own ability and wasn't sure if he could do what he promised, what if he lost his job, what would he do? he had no idea his mind was racing Devlin Sharpe: Thank you ladies and gentlemen for coming out here today for my announcement He walked away from the podium and walked away after a few moments after when he was out of the sight of the press, he turned around the corner and sighed as a tears grew in his eyes Devlin Sharpe: I am so sorry, for being everything you tried to stop me from becoming..I just wish you were here right now, tell me what to do, because to be honest I am stupid...I know I was a fool to do this, to be an arrogant jerk to challenge the best, to risk my fucking career for some stupid ego stunt Devlin turned around and banged his fists on the wall in anger and frustration then collapsed on the floor and began sobbing Devlin: help me... After a few moments Devlin pushed against the floor and picked himself back up, sniffled and took a deep breath as he steadied his nerves and walked away as the scene shifted back in to present time Devlin Sharpe: You see I turned my back on everything my trainer, my brother, Mikey told me all through my life, not to let my talent get to my head, to be humble not to bite more than I can chew and what do I do? EXACT FUCKING opposite and guess what happened, I ate my words The scene shifts again as Devlin Sharpe is standing in the ring, groggy, the vision is shifted to his vision is blurred he cant faintly make out the outline of his opponent, the vision shifts from Devlins vision to the view of the ring from the side as Devlin is shown wobbling in the ring and then he gets hit by a big clotheslined and gets pinned, it sinks in, Devlin Sharpe just got himself fired, he stands in the ring motionless as his opponent leaves the ring and Medics come out, Medics try to pick up Devlin Sharpe but he shrugs them off, gets up slowly, waves a tearful goodbye to the fans and walks out of the ring, he walks to the backstage area towards the locker room, from the locker room he gets his hockey Jersey and pulls it over his bruised and scarred body and walks to the exit of the arena, takes a deep breath of the crisp cold night air and get on his motorbike and rides off down the highway Devlin Sharpe: Seeya Later, Mayhem The scene shifts again to present time as Devlin chuckles sarcastically and he stares at the light of the lamp post Devlin Sharpe: See if only I listened to what my older brother told me to do, Id have a championship by now instead of being fired then rehired and treated like shit by everyone else in the company, lets face it everyone in this company hates my guts, everyone has been wanting me gone ever since I fucked up with my big headed press confrence to be fair Brian Michaels spoilt me by putting me in Rated X, I got a shot at the big time and like lost control of my ego which grew to epic proportions causing me to claim to do things I couldn't really do, I don't know how I let this happen to myself, all I wanted to be was the best that I could be, but instead I turned in to an egomaniac who thought he could beat everyone, which is of course...BULLSHIT Devlin looked over the edge of the bridge but he could see nothing except deep endless dark nothingness, he imagined if life would be better if he just fell in and dissapeared in to nothingnesss, would anybody really care? does he mean anything to anyone? Devlin: no...I won't kill myself After the death of his two brothers, Devlin was sure that his family could not handle another death in the family so he decided for the benefit of his parents that he wouldnt kill himself, he didn't want them to lose another son Devlin Sharpe: Believe me I will make my parents proud...I will win my match in the MWE because I will change how I think, I will follow everything my brother told me instead of just deciding what to do on my own, I will consider the outcomes of my actions before I do them, I will not fuck up this time, I will slowly claw my way back to the top in the MWE Devlin looks up to the moon and smiles Devlin Sharpe: I just wish you were here to see this view bro, its fucking amazing, I really miss you man, I need your guidance I need you to tell me what to do because man I am just confused I don't know what to do, I will make mistakes again, I just know it, please man just come back and tell me what to do, bail me out like you always do, I am just a kid, I don't know what to do.... The scene shifts again as Devlin and Mikey are standing on the same bridge but a few years earlier Mikey Sharpe: How is the training coming along bro? Devlin Sharpe: it's really good mikey, thanks for helping me out but I don't know what I will do in the ring, I mean I will be by myself and I just can't do it alone, I need you to be there with me, I am shit without your help, you need to help me Mikey Sharpe: Now remember Dev, I won't always be here fore you, you need to learn to stand on your own two feet and make your own success and since I trained you I know that you can do this, I believe in you, our parents believe in you and somehow deep down, way fucking deep down, so does Ryan, in that ring even if you think you are alone, you actually aren't, we are always with you in spirit, long after we're dead Tears well up in Devlin's eyes Devlin Sharpe: Don't say that! You won't die! Mikey Sharpe: Everyone dies someday kiddo, I am glad that you actually give a shit about my life unlike your brother who tries to take my life every 15 minutes Devlin huffs, turns around, arms crossed, eyes closed and nose up Devlin Sharpe: He's a loser anyway Mikey Sharpe: Anyway forget about him...to be succesful in wrestling listen to this carefully Devlin puts his hands on the bridge and uses them to hold up his body as he slouches and turns his head towards Mikey Devlin Sharpe: Yeah bro? Mikey Sharpe: No matter how good you get, don't let it get to your head, don't inflate your ego, trust me you will self destruct so fast, your head would spin and this last one is important, don't bite off more than you can chew, if you know you've been losing for a while, don't be a dickhead and challenge the best, you WILL lose and look like a fool Mikey grabbed Devlin by the shoulders Mikey Sharpe: Promise me little bro...you will never EVER do this, if you do I will be dissapointed in you, in the way I taught you, the time I wasted training you to wrestle, it will all be wasted if you decide to go against my rules and throw away your career, understand? Devlin Sharpe: I promise bro The scene shifts again as Devlins eyes tear up again Devlin Sharpe: I promise bro-ke, I am so sorry I should have listened to what you told me maybe if I did I wouldn't be such a loser now, I would actually be succesful in the MWE but I didn't listen amd I ended up like this, a lowlife piece of shit loser who can't beat anyone in MWE or ICW, but now that I have reallised my mistakes I know how to fix my mistakes therefore I know how to be a wrestler I know what to do, I know what to do to be what my brother wanted me to be, a sucessful wrestler here in the MWE, yeah thats it, I'll make my brother proud Devlin grabbed a stone from the ground and throws it at the water and watches it skip repeatedly and sink to the bottom of the ocean as Devlin's mood sunk with it Devlin Sharpe: I wonder how far I can go before I sink like that pebble, face it, I will sink like that pebble, I am a weakling I am nothing like my brother, he's strong, capable and good at wrestling unlike me who isn't good at anything Devlin bows his head and buries it in his arms as he suddenly hears something Voice: Don't be discouraged, don't fall off the path, stay strong and true little bro Devlin suddenly popped his head back up trying to find out where the voice came from however all he could see was a wisp of white light in the sky for a split second Devlin Sharpe: Thanks big brother, if you believe in me I am sure that I can win because after all you are with me in spirit, even if you and Ryan are dead you're with me in my heart you two are what drive me to better myself and be the best in the MWE because damnit I know that what you two want me to be and I won't let you guys down, not anymore, I will change my ways and one day I will be the motherfucking MWE Champion Devlin used his hands to support him up again and looked at the moon as the light of the moon shined off his eyes Devlin Sharpe: See, this is why Peter Kaymacian and Nic Kraven will lose against me, they do not have the support of their family, at least not like I do, together with the spirit of my eldest brother, I will have enough motivation to beat the crap out of my opponents and pick up a decisive victory on my path to eternal MWE glory even if Nic Kraven and Peter Kaymacian double teamed me I still fail to see them having a a chance at beating me because correct utilization of my talents will allow me to beat them both and now with the motivation provided to me by my brother I am sure to win and that right there is a fact Devlin looks over the bridge and sees the sun beginning to rise as the water begins to turn orange from afar Devlin Sharpe: Ill be back soon bro, and next time I come I will come back a champion to make you proud of me Devlin Sharpe smiled at the sunrise and walked away from the bridge and got on to his bike and rode off towards the highway, behind him a white wisp can be seen slowly taking form of a long haired teenager Mikey Sharpe: You can do it bro...
