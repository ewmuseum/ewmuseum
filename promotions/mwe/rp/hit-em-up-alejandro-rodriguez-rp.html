Hit em Up (Alejandro Rodriguez RP); AR RP

Aug 23 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The scene opens inside a dark room. The room Is illuminated by a flickering dim light bulb, not much can be seen inside the room. Apart from a single leather armchair in the middle. Alejandro Rodriguez is sat on the armchair. He is wearing an A-Rod t-shirt, covered by a black blaizer which is buttoned up, he is wearing ripped jeans and white sneakers. Alejandro swings in and out of sight as the light bulb swings left and right. The camera pans into Alejandro and the light bulb stops swinging. Alejandro: Brian Michaels. You egotistical, cocky, obnoxious mother-fucker. You think that you’re bigger, that your better than everyone. You’re fucking washed out man. You used to be something, you used to be a force to reckon with, nowadays you’re just a joke. You used to be a world champion, but now you’re getting beat up by Sonny Williams and eliminated from a Battle Royal by fucking Doink the Clown. Alejandro chuckles to himself and speaks again. Alejandro: You’ve beaten me, and I’ve beaten you. But now, you’re old, slow and pathetic. I’m as young as ever, I‘m quicker and I’m stronger than ever before. I‘ve got more determination than you, and I‘ve got more heart… You don’t give a shit about anything, I see right through you. The crowd may have orgasms every time you try and get a cheap pop, being all fucking patriotic, I get it, I’m Mexican, I’m proud of it. You harp on about my size, but that’ll come back to bite you in the ass, getting your ass kicked by a guy half your size, now that’s tragedy. The light flickers again. Alejandro: You think you’re everything that MWE represents, you think you’re bigger than MWE. MWE made you, MWE made me, you wouldn’t be fucking anything without MWE. Why the fuck should you represent MWE above anyone else? You may be a fan-favourite. You may be daddy’s little boy. But you sure as hell aren’t the best thing that MWE has to offer. You should be retired, out on a beach somewhere with your twenty-something hookers. Playing baseball with your daddy. You shouldn’t be here anymore, there are younger and better people who should be above you. Of course, your daddy being the Chairman will help you out a little bit. Alejandro laughs again. Alejandro: You don’t deserve to be in the MWE. You and your boyfriend D.C. can take your squabbles outside because it’s got no place here, we come to fight, not to be drama-queens. D.C. should also know that is D.C. does indeed interfere in my match, I’ll kick his ass too, I’ll make him bleed, just like I’ll make Brian Michaels bleed, because if you interfere in my match, it’s my business and I‘ll let you know that you‘d just made a big mistake. Alejandro pauses a moment. Alejandro: And I assure you that you will bleed, Michaels. I‘m going to take my fist and pummel you in the head, I‘m going to kick you in the face, I‘m going to slam your head into the ground untill you split wide open and bleed like a stuck pig. It’s going to pour down your forehead and you’re going to be able to taste it in your mouth, you’ll be able to taste your own blood. Have you ever had that feeling before… Who am I kidding? Of course you have. Alejandro lifts both of his arms to his sides, and the entire room comes into focus, at the back of the room, a huge picture of Brian Michaels bleeding is spread out across the wall. The camera pans out to see all of it. Alejandro begins laughing and keeps laughing for a moment before eventually calming down. Alejandro: It seems that you and me both have tasted our own blood on more than one occasion, and I’m glad that you’re familiar with the feeling. Because the first time, oh, that’s just the worst. I’m sure you’re familiar with all kinds of pain though, being run over must be no walk in the park, man I’d pay to see that again. Daddy’s boy not getting what he wants just one little time, it must have been heartbreaking. Though I’m sure your daddy bought first class care for you… My mother never had that treatment, she was fucking shot in the head saving my life you spoilt prick. You’ve grown up with everything you’ve wanted, I had nothing until I found MWE, and you act as though your better than them? Fuck you. Alejandro’s face intensifies with anger. Alejandro: You’re an obnoxious piece of shit and this Monday I’m going to sure as hell make you bleed and I’m going to beat you once and for all, you can talk smack, make midget and racial jokes, but you know that I’m going to fucking bring it to you and I‘m going to make you bleed, but even then I‘m not going to stop, I’m going to make you cry for your daddy, and I want him to see the egotistical bastard that he brought up. I want him to be ashamed of what he brought up, because you take everything for-granted Michaels, would you even be here if it wasn’t for your Father? I didn’t think so. The room dims again so its just the light bulb flickering again. Alejandro: I don’t care how I do it or how hard it is, but I’m going to make you suffer, because even deep down you deserve it, you’ve had everything in your life but nothing can help you now. Fuck you Brian Michaels, in time, you will… LEARN! TO! FLY!
