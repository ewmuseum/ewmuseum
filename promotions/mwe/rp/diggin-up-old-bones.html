Diggin up old bones; So to speak

Sep 6 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The scene fades into a dark street, cold, damp and eerie, the camera focused on a dark alleyway where a teen about the age of 16 wearing a black hoodie and baggy black jeans with silver chains stands at the mouth of the opening....In front of him is a dark figure, no noticeable characteristics, just a dark sweater and dark pants. They exchange words as the camera pans in to get a closer look, which shows the dark figure hand the young teen something that look somewhat like folding pliers, The teen then reaches in his pocket and hands the figure some cash. As the figure nods in approval the boy backs out and walks away. We now see the boys face as he passes underneath the streetlights, his face pale, dark around the eyes, a smile on his face, scars on the sides of his mouth. Green hair peeks out from underneath his hood. After passing 3 blocks on a dead street a newspaper drifts across the road and a crumpling sound comes from the alleyway. Jekyl carefully walls into the alleyway to investigate, step by step he hears the rustling of some paper, then all of a sudden a crash is heard and a cat runs out between Jekyl's feet. Jekyl convinced that the cat was the source of the noise begins to exit the alley. All of a sudden he is tackled to the ground. Figure: This is my turf kid, give me your loot and get the fuck outa here before I decide to hurt you. Jekyl: Turf? Yours? You think I'm afraid of you? Figure: You think your brave eh? Lets see how brave you are with a 'gat you your forehead. Jekyl then pulls out the item he received from the person before which we now see is a butterfly knife, he then thrusts it into the figures stomach. The figures eyes clench shut in pain as Jekyl pushes the figure off of himself. Jekyl quickly gets to his feet, knife in hand, he bends over and picks searches the figures pockets for the gun, to his satisfaction he finds none. As he leans of the figure he rips off his hood to find out the man who just attacked him is the same man who sold him the very knife he just stabbed him with. Jekyl leans in close to the mans head and begins to talk in his ear. Jekyl: You think it's fun to sell a kid a knife, take his money, then try to rob him not even 10 minutes later? Think of it this way, the same kid you just tried to mug just stabbed you with the very knife you sold him, the irony could kill on it's own. The camera sees a twisted smile from underneath the hoodie as the man eyes begin to show fear. Jekyl begins to laugh. Jekyl: People like you make me sick, trick the innocent and then think they get away with it, I don't want to hear a single word out of you until I'm finished talking, nod you head if you understand. The man slowly nods his head in fear. Jekyl: You have 2 options here. Either you give me back my money, go home and recuperate and fix yourself up and think about what just happened., or you can lie here and bleed to death...BUT these options depend on your next choice of words, so choose them carefully....Speak....... The man takes a gulp as he clutches his torso in pain. Man: I.....I'm...sorry...... Jekyl: You're sorry? It's funny how a man's last words will show cowardice most of the time, faced with life or death, you take the coward way out and beg for forgiveness....Yet were you sorry when you tried to rob me? I told you to choose your words carefully and you chose the wrong answer, welcome to option 3, see you in hell. The camera pans out to show Jekyl in the darkness kneeling over a figure, his arm raises as the figure screams for mercy, yet no one is around to hear his cries for help. ************************End Flashback*************************** As the scene fades into the present time, Jekyl is leaning against a wall on a abandoned street. Green shaggy hair, pale face, red face paint around his mouth and his eyes dark, wearing a purple suit he has his head down. He takes a deep break as a small gust of wind blows by, his hair slightly flutters in the cool summer nights breeze, he cocks his head up slightly. Jekyl: I keep hearing controversy on why I am first in line with no wins.... You see its because I don't care about wins or losses, I care about inflicting pain, and every match I have been in, there has been pain and blood, which makes me happy and it makes the fans happy. I bring a certain je ne ce quois to MWE, and that's what people want, they want to see a better class of superstar, and I'm gonna give it to them. The wind dies down and Jekyl straightens his jacket and begins to walk down the deserted street. Jekyl: Sonny Williams, i face you in a 12 foot high cage, and I'm a hungry dog and you will be trapped in my cell, there will be no running away, there will be no mercy, only pain and blood. You will be lucky to live after our match, maybe I will do you a favor and end your pathetic career, your mediocre life, and send you where you belong.... with your pathetic brother...in hell. Jekyl begins to laugh as he walks down the road. Jekyl: You sir lived the live of a gang banger in your younger years, you dragged your brother into that life and when you went against a rival gang, your brother got shot, you did nothing to help him except cry your puny little heart out on his lifeless corpse. You say you do all this for him, but why, winning a fight will not bring him back, winning some belt will not bring him back, you are fighting for all the wrong reasons, and that is why I personally am going to rid MWE of your sad sorry excuse for a wrestler. Jekyl stops end faces a dark alleyway... Jekyl: About 3 months ago and maybe even a couple years ago, none of these other superstars would dare cross either Whitman or Williams, but what happened... you would whoop their ass...and my solution to this equation is simple, you see to balance out the big time from small is a matter of math, you divide the amount of jobbers by the main eventers, this equals world title, yet lets add to that, lets add a lunatic who will go all out without regret of loss.... you get me. Now after all the math we have done so far, you get a final world title and add in me... this equals a balance of power, a common denominator... The one man who will rip a world title from Mr. Whitman's hands and wear it. But I dont care about the title, I could car less about wearing a gold belt around my waist, I would use it as a belt to hold up these expensive slacks. Jekyl peeks into the alleyway and begins to laugh... AHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHhahahah... hehehe...ho-ho..haaaaaaaaaa Jekyl: Speaking of slacks, I'm not even close to being finished with you Sonny... You see this is my opportunity to guarantee blood, to guarantee your screaming cries of pain and anguish, you will scream for me to end you miserable life, you will beg me to bring you to your pathetic brother, you will gravel in pain for death.... but I won't give it to you... I will drag you to the top of that 12 foot steel cage and dangle you by you skinny little ankles and see what kind of a man you truly are. Jekyl begins to step forth into the opening of the alleyway, darkness consumes him, his eyes slowly adjust to the darkness, the camera's light flicks on to illuminate the cluttered area. On the left are 2 dumpsters, a foul stench fills the air. A few broken boxes scatter the ground, newspapers flutter around on over breeze that passes by. He looks left and right as if looking for something. Jekyl: You see Williams, people show their true emotions when faced with a deadly decision, and ironically this is the same alleyway where i proved this exact point to a man when i was no older that 16. You see True men will live and die by their word, not the spoken word, but the power and bond that runs their beliefs. You see I bought my first knife only a few alleyways back when i was 16, about the same age as you were when your brother got shot, heh still makes me tingle inside as I wished it was me who shot him....Sorry....I'll continue.... But after I bought this knife, the same man who sold it to me, tried to mug me not even a few minutes later... With the same knife he sold me, I used it in self defense and i ripped this man a new blow hole. Jekyl continues to walk deeper and deeper into the alleyway, the street on the other side gets closer and closer by each step. he makes. He suddenly looks to the right and stops. Laughing and pointing he continues. Jekyl: You see I gave this man 2 options, he either give me back my money, go home and recuperate and fix himself up and think about what happened, or he lie here and bleed to death. HE said he was sorry and begged for my help which is where i introduced option 3 which caused his death... Ironically, this murder was never solved because his rotted corpse still lies here. The camera focuses on a decayed skeleton lying behind a small dumpster, facing the sky above, jaw open, hollow skull....This explained the stench. The camera rushes out of the alleyway as it looks back to see Jekyl emerge from the darkness, holding his stomach and laughing. Jekyl: Mr. Whitman, after I'm done toying with Williams from the top rope of the cage, your next.... I not beating the hell of you 2 because I want the gold belt... I'm doing this because its fun....You see David, you are among the elite, and I am the kind of person who enjoys taking down the elite, to gain the status of best.... Those who think they can out do me can go right ahead, but it will all end the same... But I think Whitman, Sonny and I are going to do battle for a long time.... Whitman will fight for his honor, and Sonny will fight because the other night I asked him where his brother was... he said he was dead and started crying...... ********Reenactment scene********* Jekyl: Hello, Sonny? Voice: Yes? Jekyl: Is your brother there? Voice*sobs*: My brother is dead.... Jekyl: I GUESS THAT'S WHY HE DOESN'T VISIT YOU MUCH?!?! The voice on the phone begins to sob more and more as Jekyl hangs up and begins to laugh... The camera turns to see Aeron sitting on the other end of the room.. Aeron: That was cold....... Jekyl*laughing uncontrollably*: NOT AS... .COLD.*HAHA* AS HIS BROTHER IS!!!! **********End Reenactment************ Jekyl: That night was hilarious....But then again the face your gonna make this coming MNM...... Well.... lets just say Youtube is gonna get a lot more viewers! Anyways I have some previous engagements to attend to, fireworks tonight....... A loud boom echoes from a few blocks away, Jekyl jumps backwards startled and begins to run, in the distance we hear his laugh and voice. Jekyl*fading*: Whoops, thought i set it for an hour, not 45 minutes!!!! HAHAHAHAhahahahahahahahahahaaaaaaaaaaaaa -Fade-
