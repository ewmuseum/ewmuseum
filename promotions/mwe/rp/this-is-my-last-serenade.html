This is My Last Serenade

Mar 23 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"Here is your winner, Joey Dallas!" ~Fred CarryDon't you know me, I'm the savior "He did it! Cannon has beat David Whitman!" ~David Ferrera And it's time we, discussed behavior "I WILL REWRITE THE FUCKING BIBLE IF I HAVE TO" ~Joey DallasDon't you know me, the messiah "YOU WANNA BE JESUS!? THEN I'LL BE YOUR FUCKING GOD!" ~Ace CannonAnd it's time we, set your soul on fire "The final battle between good and evil, light and dark; As we are lead to believe. Will begin the end of the world. We as a people are told that, but we rarely believe the hype. Will we believe it, when the end is staring us in the face?" ~James Nicolson~ In the beginning, there was but one. One world; One hope; One man. Woe until thee, you thee subjects of this man for there remained but one evil. One evil that shrouds the light. One evil that stands before this man; One evil that has already ended the hope once before and tries to once again. Thus begins the end. An unrivaled fight between good and evil; The battle between the light and the dark; An unparalleled rivalry between Joey Dallas and Ace Cannon. History tells of the child like men that began to shroud Mayhem Wrestling Entertainment in a vail of hatred. As the in-matured hero of the people started his journey along a trail that found itself leading only to the path of Ace Cannon. History reminds thee of the day of the beginning. Where the object of lust and the other of steel prematurely ended the career of the wealthy; The day that a woman and a chair found themselves in the hands of Joey Dallas as he stood triumphantly over the defeated Ace Cannon. A strike to thee ego and the knee of thee true personification of evil. Yet like a snake, the evil shed it's skin, firing back with a strategic advantage as he was placed into one of the highest positions of power that Mayhem had to offer. Finding himself a pawn to attack the man he couldn't, a willing subject to rival the attempts of Joey Dallas to swing the balance of power in his favor. This man was known simply as Windy Bridges; The bright young Canadian was willingly corrupted in return for a chance to be glorified as the object of his desires was on the line; A single piece of gold, that at the time was strapped proudly around the waist, of Joey Dallas. Yet in the end, evil prevailed and the solider of Cannon was victorious. However the fall of Dallas was not concluded yet as he was brutally stripped of his dignity and livelihood. For one year, Dallas was out of sight and out of mind of the evil. Unaware of the hero's will to survive; His need to retaliate. For one year, Cannon was atop of the world. Ego fully intact after successfully removing his greatest threat from his life. One year past and revenge was finally put into motion. A mysterious figure emerging from the dark battered Cannon; A revelation created in a weeks time as the mystery was revealed and the unexpected figure was unmasked. Thus unveiling. The Second Coming. The boy had matured into a man; The man had evolved into a Savior. His sole purpose, to rid the world of the one true evil; To save the people from an inevitable downfall. His mission, simply to defeat Ace Cannon. Success was found; Yet victory was tainted, through the unclean hands of another rival; D.C. ego getting the best of him yet again injected himself into the battle. Throwing the final blow in the first encounter of this blood fued. Both of these adversaries uneasy with the feeling of their encounter being tarnished they set off for a new start in a new company; Leaving Revolution~Pro behind they found themselves where it all started, back in Mayhem Wrestling Entertainment. Where their rivalry would only become far more violent. Yet the war was held off as the many rulers of Mayhem forced them together in a different manor; The rivals were placed unwillingly into a tandem. Nonetheless their inability to work together somehow found them at the top; The World Tag Team Champions. After a year of hatred and anger towards each other, that night marked their success as a team. Even so, the celebration would not last long; A brawl in-suing just moments after where the thirst for blood was quenched for each of them. The days following would lead towards what would be the inevitably destruction of both of them; As light and dark clashed again in a battle rightfully deemed the Lights Out match. And as it was told our hero prevailed, but at the cost of his own well-being. Risking life and limp Dallas forced back the evil if just for one night; For one taste of victory. The skirmish that faithful night left our savior and the evil battered and bloodied; Injured, unknown to the world if either would be able to compete again. Yet the victory, that untainted feeling of empowerment was enough to propel Dallas to a never before seen hight of competition. Woe unto thee, the night that our hero met the brash young, David Whitman. The object of both these men's desires placed on the line. As Dallas attempted to save the world with another victory, yet the young Whitman had a heart of gold as he pulled through for his people. Winning the challenge and keeping a firm grasp around the World Championship. Sending Dallas back down to his mid-card obscurity. At this point our story takes a drastic turn for the worst; As for the first time since their battle months ago Cannon made himself seen. Brutally attack the already tired savior; Wrapping a crown of barbed-wire around his head as he was forced to suffer for the sins of Mayhem. Bled out like a pig as he was strung from the rafters. Now to every beginning; There must be an end. As Dallas and Cannon embark on their final journey together down the Highway to Hell. The final destination is quite clear, but the road has many twists and turns that will only be revealed as this story finds it's ending. The hand written words tell the entire story of the amazing rivalry and war that has waged on for the last year and a half. Each page of this story written neatly upon a thin piece of paper, each tinted gold so the dark ink is completely visible against it. A slim hand runs over the still wet ink, a thoughtful glide across the page smudges the words as it travels to the edge of the paper. The hand, now tainted with the black ink across the fingers, flips the small bundle of paper over, revealing a title written boldly across the front page. THE HOLY BIBLE King Joseph Edition A wider view of this scene reveals Joey Dallas sitting at a small table. He is dressed in his usual attire, the dark suit accompanied by a brightly colored tie. His hand resting gingerly atop of his latest accomplishment, fingertips stained black with ink. Eyes following suit he stares at the front page, his emotion clearly visible as he does so. His usual confidence and disgust have been replaced, by a look of remorse. Joey Dallas: "Ace, our final battle is drawer closer and closer as the days pass ..." Shoulders shake and rise as he draws in a deep breath, letting his fingers slide across his own version of the scripture that has changed many lives, more the story of his life than anything else. Dallas let's his neck raise, refusing to look towards the camera as he does so. Instead turning his head he looks towards one of the white walls that surround him. Joey Dallas: "And I am reminded of the fact everyday. As I see the tabloids all reading our names ... The headlines saying Dallas versus Cannon three .... Every wresting website posting our faces across the world ... Like it wasn't enough that I am forced to look in the mirror everyday of my life ... Dallas clenches his fingers across the writing on the table before him, wrinkling the paper as he pulls his eyes forcefully away from the wall and into the camera. Eyes glimmering with emotion, hands shaking with either anticipation or worry he points towards his forehead. Making every scar and scab across his face agonizingly visible as he does so. Joey Dallas: "Only to be reminded of the wars that we've gone through ... Reminded, of the blood we've shed in the hope of leaving the other lying in a hospital bed for the rest of their life! All because I want, I NEED you to feel the kind of agony that I did when you tore my life away from me!" No longer able to take the emotional strain of looking towards us Dallas tears his eyes away, hanging his head as he looks back towards the wall. Dragging his away from his scars and into his hair, clutching his blond locks between his fingers as he forms a fist once again. Joey Dallas: "But as that last match gets closer and closer, and can't help but realize something ..." His fingernails begin digging into his scalp as he tries to calm himself down, only being able to do so as he takes a deep staggering breath. Slowly turning his shaking head towards the camera for the second time. Joey Dallas: "I can't help but to realize that without Ace Cannon there to push me and shove me to be the man that I am today ... Then I ... Then Joey Dallas, has nothing." The sudden realization that everything in his life has revolved around ending the career of Ace Cannon has left Dallas in a state of shock. Lips quivering he lets his hand slowly run over his face, fingers pressing lightly against his bottom lip as his eyes drift away from the camera, head still turned towards us. Joey Dallas: "Everything I've done in my career, no .. In my LIFE, since I first showed up in MWE, has been about you ... It's been about getting under your skin ... It's been about one upping you every chance I got ... It's been about making you feel, what I've felt." He runs his hand over his face, feeling his rough bread in his palm, the holes in his flesh that were left from the stitches that were only there in the first place because he had to wage this war with Ace. Every one of them, a reminder. Of just how hard he has fought in order to keep one man down, and how everyone else has never seen the best side of him in the ring. Joey Dallas: "It has always been about showing you the harsh side of reality that you showed me a year ago ... But now that it's so close to my last chance to do that, I'm not entirely sure if it was worth all the effort. But at the same time ..." With a loud thud Dallas slams both hands down on the table in front of him, looking down at them as he forces them into fists yet again. Eyes now focused intensely towards the camera, the look in his eyes is the same stare that we have seen him use many a time while looking into the eyes of his greatest rival. Joey Dallas: "I know that I would have to kill myself before I let you get away with the sins that I have seen you commit first hand!" He sweeps the table clean with his forearm, sending the bundle of papers flying everywhere. Scattering apart in the air before calmly drifting towards the carpeted floor. All while Dallas pushes himself to his feet, kicking the chair he was just moments ago sitting on into the wall behind behind him. Joey Dallas: "From your greed and self-centered acts from when you were playing the part of the spoiled little rich boy .. All the way until you hung me to bleed out like a slaughtered animal from the rafters just a few weeks ago!" Walking around the table he lets his hand glide across the smooth surface, eyes still focused intently on the camera. This is not the same Joey Dallas that we have seen over these past months. This is a broken man, this is a man who has finally had his own personal revelation. That Ace Cannon ... Made Joey Dallas. Joey Dallas: "I refuse to let myself just sit back and watch as you continue to do these evil actions to other people ... And be rewarded for it. You could kill a man ... And the Board of Directors would have you nipping at my heels, while I actually EARN my chance to be the man!" SLAM! His hand hits the table again, twisting back into a fist as Dallas looks down at the carpeted floor below him. The pale beige color would be enough to make anyone sick after a few minutes, but Dallas isn't able to keep his composer at the moment anyway. Joey Dallas: "Tell me Ace ... Do you think that's fair? That no matter how hard someone else fights their way from the bottom up, that you can just do that egotistical little strut of yours down the ramp ... And get yourself a shot at the champion with one, little kick ... Oh but that's right, life isn't fair, now is it?" Keeping his neck hunched over Dallas turns his head to look back at the camera. His eyes filled with anger and raw emotion, something that is rarely found with "The Savior." This feud with Cannon has already brought out a lot in Dallas, and this appears to be the final step towards his evolution. Joey Dallas: "No .. Because no matter how hard I crawl and I fight to be in the positions that I have been in, people like you can just stroll in and take it all away from me ... And why!? Because any press is good press. That is the kind of unimaginable lies that Richard Covell tries to force feed people like me, while he caters to you ..." He lets his neck slowly roll backwards, now looking towards the ceiling as he breathes in deeply. Trying to calm himself down, but to no avail. As he wraps his fingers around the tie that hangs from his neck he gives it a quick yank, loosening it drastically. Joey Dallas: "Richard Covell has handed you everything you have ever earned in this company ... Because he's afraid you might blow your lid again, but every time you're given that shot ... You lose, when you're in the spotlight for your fifteen minutes of fame, you fall flat on your face. Time and time again ..." In one, not so fluid motion Dallas let's his head drop back to eye level and turns his body towards the camera. His tie now tangling around his neck like a loosened noose of a man who was about to be put to death. Joey Dallas: "So I guess in that respect, we have a lot in common ... We've both been to the biggest stage that this industry has to offer, and we have been thrown to the lions because of the people that have been the namesake of MWE since the beginning." Joey now unbuttons the jacket of his suit, much like we have seen him do before. Instead this time, it is not to show a scar, it's not out of anger. No, this time it is for his own comfort as he lets out a forced chuckle, finally able to relax. Joey Dallas: "Those lions .. Those people, the very ones I came here to protect from you ... Never seem to realize that every single one of the people they call heroes, are all just putting on a mask so that they will always feel like they are relevant ... Meanwhile people that show their true colors, their real emotions. People like me and you .. Are stabbed in the back by those filthy rodents we are forced to call fans" Now this is the Joey Dallas that we have come to know, and ... love? Over the last year of his career. This is the confident, unemotional, unfeeling Joey Dallas that has made a name for himself as a top contender in the MWE. As he finishes loosening his tie so both ends tangle freely by his sides next to each side of his jacket, Dallas leans against the table behind him. Joey Dallas: "We are the people that want .. Or in your case, wanted ... to help those people, but just like the rats that they have become they turned on us. They boo us, as though to say 'Now what are you going to do' ..." He now unbuttons the top few notches on his shirt as he relaxes more and more, his muscles are less tense, and his breathing has steadied as he gets back into this sort of uncaring state that has become the Joey Dallas trademark as much as being the Savior has. Joey Dallas: "But the people that have pushed past the relentless boos that they throw down every week .. Are the people that looked right back at them and said 'Get out of my way' as they continued along the journey to legendary status .. Now you are a different story entirely. Instead of just brushing past the fact that they hate you .. You have to keep doing these vial things that only make them hate you more, and for some reason, the Board loves you for it." As his eyes search the room they locked onto something on the floor, his biggest work of his lifetime. His "Bible" scattered across the floor, Dallas moves away from the table, crouching down to pick up a few of the sheets from the carpet, looking quickly at them before turning his attention towards the camera again. Joey Dallas: "Now this rivalry that has been the focus of my career for as long as I can remember ... It's no longer about trying to save the people .. It's not about trying to save anything ... It's gone far past that ..." As he looks back towards his scripture he reads a few lines here and there, before crumpling the paper in his hand. Destroying his work and the hope of future generations looking back on this blood filled war with a new respect. Slowly opening his hand he lets the paper fall back to the floor in a heap. Joey Dallas: "It's become about making sure people like you, never get the chance to write your name into history as one of the greatest of all time ... It's become about making sure the Board of Directors sees the hard work that men like me do to be at the top and stay there!" He pushes his legs up so that he is once again standing, however the camera stays on the floor as Dallas lifts his foot, the light reflecting off of his black shoes as he drops it down on top of the golden paper that was once his life's greatest work. As he grinds it down into the carpet the camera moves up to see that Dallas now has that evil snarl across his face as usual, looking down at his destruction as though his Bible was the body of Ace Cannon. Joey Dallas: "Now the last chance to make sure that happens is at Highway to Hell ... The final destination for this feud, and I can guarantee that come the end of our match .. One of us, will NOT be walking out of it." Dallas looks away from the paper below his feet and turns back towards the camera one more time as he sort of chuckles to himself. An evil grin on his face as he looks into at us. Joey Dallas: "Highway to Hell, is the last time .. That I'm going to pay the price for your sins .. Highway to Hell marks the last -" He pauses mid-sentence and shakes his head disapprovingly. Joey Dallas: "You know what ... Fuck this" Dallas pushes past the camera, leaving the room, par his scattered scriptures and furniture, completely empty as we here a door swing open from behind us. The unfamiliar voice of the cameraman chiming in. "What the hell are you doing?" The door is slammed shut and we cut to black.
