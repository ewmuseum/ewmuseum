Your Saviour Has Arrived; Antonio DeMarco RP #01

Jan 8 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
OOC: Feelin' a little rusty so apologies if it doesn't seem as higher standard as usual. I know I usually pride myself on my RPs, but can't say it's one of my best. That's my opinion anyways, you guys might think otherwise. Seems to me though that I wanted to throw so many ideas out there that's it's almost a bit too much of a mish-mash and doesn't flow properly because of it. Feedback is greatly appreciated. Silence except a regular thump, thump, thump. The sound of a punch-bag. Thump, thump, thump. A dimly lit low-roofed room – a basement. Standing next to the swinging red sack, a sack that has clearly seen better days and is patched roughly in many places with gaffer tape, is Antonio DeMarco DeMarco is decked out in a less than familiar manner for long-time fans.He is wearing a simple grey tank-top whose darkened sweat patches outline his abdominal muscles and pecs. His legs are covered by his long ring pants, which are decorated by an Italian flag and his name on the outside legs and his feet remain uncovered. He continues. Thump, thump, thump. His forehead glistens with sweat, his face red and his knuckles darkening with every additional bare-handed shot. The light is provided by a small series of spotlights embedded in the ceiling, not lighting the punch bag but illuminating with a hazy glow much of the rest of the room. A row of gym equipment; various weight lifting machines, a row of dumb bells, a treadmill, a bike, a rowing machine and a sole bench in the shadow of a bar loaded with some major weight. All the equipment is new, sleek and modern – quite the opposite of the punch bag that continues to get a pounding. Thump, thump, thump. The floor is almost entirely covered in amateur wrestling mat – possibly the only sign that this is a wrestler’s hide-out and not a bodybuilder or boxers. The constant thumping continues, though DeMarco turns to the camera between shots, maintaining a calm demeanour despite obvious tones of anger in his voice. Antonio DeMarco: How should I feel to know I have basically got a bounty placed on my head? How should I feel to know that practically every man in that locker room is gunning for my blood? The bag swings back towards DeMarco, he dodges it with the speed of a professional boxer, turning around it so he is face-on to the camera, though obviously partially hidden by the tattered bag. Thump, thump, thump. Antonio DeMarco: How should I feel to know that every man in the back secretly wants to take me out, to put me down like a dog? I should feel proud that’s how I should feel. I should feel like I am, in fact I know I am one of the legends of this business. I know they’re gunning for me in a bid to increase their reputation, in a bid to give their names more prestige. He swings at the bag, not with anger but almost in a contented manner, like a dog given a bone. He almost cracks a grin momentarily, but soon returns to his more serious demeanour as he turns to hit the punch bag once more before continuing to talk. Thump, thump. Antonio DeMarco: They want the glory of having beaten a 2-time World champion, a Hall Of Famer, a legend of this business. He resumes punching the bag momentarily before stopping in his tracks, catching the bag in a bear-hug and letting it loose. The bag swings in a pendulum-like manner for a few moments before resting still. DeMarco stands behind it, his face almost entirely in the shadow of the bag as he stares down at his reddened knuckles. He grimaces slightly before walking away towards a flight of stairs. The camera crew follows behind eagerly wondering where DeMarco is leading them. DeMarco heads through an open doorway, closing it with some force though not slamming it behind him in the cameraman’s face. --- He soon returns however, wearing a casual t-shirt, suit combination; a much more familiar sight. He is ready for business now. He is followed as he heads out of the front of the house and climbs into a silver Mercedes, pausing momentarily to don his sunglasses as he reverses down the driveway of an immaculate looking California mansion. He leaves through electronic gates and hits the highway seeming almost oblivious to the world as he cruises in his flashy car. A short drive ensues until finally he pulls up at his destination – it appears to be nothing more than a disused patch of land over-run by grassy weeds emerging from the dry, cracked concrete. He is greeted by three bulky men, one carrying a briefcase, this man is decked out in a classy suit, the others are obviously the muscle as they are decked out in black tank-tops and black jeans that scream out to anyone looking ‘we’re ready for business’. Antonio steps forward reaching out a hand, still red and sore from his earlier gym session; the suited man reciprocates offering a firm handshake and beckoning DeMarco to enter a nearby building that also appears rather derelict. The suited man motions to his colleagues to wait outside as he enters followed by DeMarco through a kicked-in fire exit on the south side. The inside of the building matches it’s exterior; a pool of stagnant water collected in the middle fragrancing the place with a pungent odour, one wall has been almost completely covered in graffiti, whilst another’s white plasterboard has red, bloody looking streaks running across it’s cracked surface. DeMarco lifts his feet, his eyes fixated on the back of the suited man’s head as he follows him up a flight of metal stairs into a simple office; a desk, two leather chairs, a filing cabinet and no computer just a simple manilla folder upon the desk. Suited Man: I’m glad you came, Mr. DeMarco. I have a proposition for you, an offer I’m sure you won’t be able to turn down. I hear that you’re headed back to the business, so I figured you’d want some back-up. After all everyone’s gonna be after you. You’re gonna be a hunted man. Antonio DeMarco: Carlo, I don’t need goons to do my dirty work. Headed back to the business? I already made my return; didn’t you see the state I left Sonny Williams in last week? Carlo: You have returned already? I believe you still had a job to complete. You’re in breach of contract if you’ve already signed on with those greedy bastards! Antonio DeMarco: I’d agree to their contract if they actually bothered to offer me a salary that my status justifies. Until then, I’m a mercenary fighting for my own justice. Carlo: Excellent. In the folder is the name of the next client, he’s from down-town and he’s a big party man. Best bet is to catch him unaware at one of his hot-spots. They’re all listed in there. Antonio shifts his weight slightly looking almost nervous in the chair. He reaches out for the folder, but is stopped in his tracks as the suited man, who has now lit a cigar and is puffing smoke slowly through his nostrils speaks once more. Carlo: Oh and DeMarco, do a good job of this one or your return may not last too long. Don’t forget I can end your career like that. Carlo clicks his fingers indicating the speed at which he can supposedly end DeMarco’s career. DeMarco meanwhile shifts once more in his seat, before snatching up the folder and heading for the door. Carlo grins a wicked, sick smile as he turns to the doorway and yells after DeMarco. Carlo: The whole job this time! Antonio almost runs out of the derelict building, paying no attention to the muscular men standing by the fire exit entrance. He is quick to make his way to his car and screeches away leaving a short trail of rubber and tyre marks on the crumbling pathway. He tosses the folder into the passenger seat, speeding towards the bright lights of Los Angeles. Halted at a red light, he leans over and flicks open the folder. Staring at him is a polaroid of a large, burly Russian looking man. DeMarco ponders on the photo for a moment before he is interrupted by loud honking. The light has turned and DeMarco’s Mercedes remains stationary backing up about eight cars behind him. DeMarco quickly accelerates, pulling off the road and into a back alley where he reads the information inside the folder. He puts it down momentarily, clearly thinking about it’s contents but obviously decides it is of little importance as he tears the folder and papers in half, dropping it into a large garbage bin and pulling out of the alley way before speeding away into the darkening evening. --- A new day and new business is on DeMarco’s agenda. He has obviously been awake a while despite the clock on the oven in the kitchen, directly behind DeMarco only reading eight AM. DeMarco is pouring a glass of orange juice which he quickly downs, carefully replacing the glass into a stainless steel dishwasher set underneath a shiny granite worktop. DeMarco is decked out in a fine suit and shows little of the fear or anxiety that we fleetingly saw the previous evening. That is because today he is officially placed in a match-up, today he gets to find out his opponent for his comeback. DeMarco is on red alert awaiting the moment when his BlackBerry buzzes with the email detailing who he shall face. He is followed by the camera as he enters a room connected to the open-plan kitchen. The lounge room we enter has three lavish leather lounges surrounding a huge plasma screen mounted on the wall. DeMarco sets himself down on the leather but is interrupted. Bzzzt. Bzzzt. Bzzzt. His BlackBerry buzzes to attention; he quickly sweeps it from his pocket and reads the email that has just arrived. Your opponent on the 01/10/09 broadcast of Monday Night Mayhem is The Grand Mystique. The match will be a singles bout. DeMarco pauses and gazes upwards. He then returns his stare to the BlackBerry, almost as if he is re-reading the message. He mutters to himself. Antonio DeMarco: Who in the hell is that? My triumphant return to the ring, my grand re-emergence, my comeback match and I am facing a complete unknown. He slams the BlackBerry down and storms from the room, entering a study whose walls are completely covered in DeMarco memorabilia, from framed photos of him in action to the obvious pride and joy of the collection as noted by their immaculate casing, his replica title belts. Two WcE World Heavyweight titles, two WcE International Heavyweight titles, two WcE Intercontinental titles, the eWa Pure title belt and the WWA HardKore title belt. He is quick to power up a MacBook Pro that is situated on a large mahogany desk in the centre of the room. He brings up the mwewrestling.com site and it’s quickly obvious what he is searching for. He finds what he wants, pulling up information on The Grand Mystique. Again, he mutters to himself as he reads. Antonio DeMarco: A geeky, fat handicapped kid is Antonio DeMarco’s return match? He wears a mask? Who in the hell does this guy think he is to be stepping into the ring with me? Antonio is interrupted once more as his watch alarm bleeps. He quickly closes the laptop, muttering a couple of expletives and a comment about being late under his breath before racing down a flight of stairs and jumping into the same silver Mercedes he drove the previous day. A long trip down the highway ensues before he arrives at the airport, racing through customs and the various security checks before boarding a plane. The camera doesn’t film on the plane instead catching him after the plane has touched down at his location and DeMarco has jumped into a waiting chauffeur-driven car. He straightens his suit in the back-seat of the car, rummages momentarily in the classy gym bag he carried off the plane and pulls out an energy drink which he quickly downs before the car pulls up in the parking lot of an arena – obviously the location of a MWE live event. --- DeMarco heads out onto the ramp carrying a microphone in his left hand to a hugely unpopular welcome from the crowd. Although a few cheers may be heard and a very, very brief chant of ‘welcome back’ is also heard, the crowd is generally jeering or booing the former two-time world champion. He stops almost soaking in the bad reception before speaking. Antonio DeMarco: Mayhem Wrestling Entertainment... Fear not, your saviour has arrived. No longer do you have to watch shows starring second-rate superstars as the true original superstar has returned. Antonio DeMarco is here! The crowd volume goes up about three notches as the jeering over-powers the microphone and DeMarco has to wait for the crowd to calm before continuing. Antonio DeMarco: Yes, that’s right. I am back and I’m not here under contract, I’m here as a mercenary, I’m here with no obligation to follow the rules, no obligation to play fair, no obligation to do no harm to anybody. What are they gonna do, sue me? I am Antonio DeMarco, I don’t get sued. People try to fight me but give in because they know they are fighting greatness. They know they are fighting one, no wait, the best in the business then, and though I have yet to prove it, the best in the business now. I’m unbeaten here... I’ve never been pinned, never submitted and that record won’t be changing this week because earlier this morning I got news of my opponent on this week’s Monday Night Mayhem. Fear not my loyal fans, I shall not be pinned, hell I doubt I’ll even break a sweat... I’m facing the handicapped guy in a mask, the little fat man trained by people he calls legends. Legends... Ha! The legends of this business are people like Crak Cokain and Rocky Stevens, none of this grave-digger or Olympic bulls**t! Chants break out for both Crak and Rocky, the ‘Rocky’ chant lasting a very long time before calming down. DeMarco smiles at the mention of the name but quickly engages a much more serious look as he speaks again. Antonio DeMarco: Monday night, I face The Grand Mystique and the only thing grand will be the grand style in which I mark my return with victory. Mystique? They’ll be nothing mystical about the way I pin him; one, two, three. Reading star-signs? You’ll be seeing stars, buddy! Antonio drops the microphone to a chorus of booing and jeers and heads backstage.
