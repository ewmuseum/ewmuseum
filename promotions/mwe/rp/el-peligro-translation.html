El Peligro. Translation: "The Danger".; Cruz/Blight RP

Jul 7 2009

Jesus Cruz & Blight

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
OOC: Here is my first roleplay. I'm still trying to feel out this new character, but once I do, I think my RPs will become more smooth and should flourish from there. But you have to start somewhere. So enjoy folks. Jesús Cruz in orange Feliciano Cruz in green Juan Garcia in brown If you feel the need to translate the Spanish into English, here's a good site: http://www.freetranslation.com/ --------------------------- Jesús... who? Son of Alicia and Feliciano Cruz. His mother, a former gym teacher. His father, a former professional boxer. At a young age, Jesús began exercising daily to live up to the high standards set out by his parents. In 2003, Jesús Cruz became a professional mixed martial arts fighter. In 2008, after a 14-1 record, he was fired after attacking a referee in his final match. Eight months later, he signed a contract to MWE to expand his talents towards a new sport. Wrestling. Is their more depth then that? Sure. But hey, I'm a mere narrator. I'm not as all-knowing as most people assume. It's called Wikipedia folks. Use it. Alright, enough chit-chat; lets paint the picture. Fade in. Ah, much better. Here we are at Xico Mezcló Artes Marcial Academia. The gym that molded Jesús into the man he is today. The shot slowly pans over to see the man himself, Jesús "El Peligro" Cruz, bouncing up and down, very light on his feet, facing a punching bag. To his right, his father, Feliciano Cruz, who is also a co-owner of the gym. Feliciano begins to yell out number-coded punching instructions. ¡Uno, uno, dos, uno, dos, tress, cuatro! Left, left, right, left, right, hook, hook! The punches hit the bag with force! ¡Ocho! A right roundhouse kick rattles the bag! ¡Nueve, diez, nueve, diez! Left forarm, right forarm, left forarm, right forarm! ¡Termínelo! ¡Estallar dos! Jesús rears back, and BAM! A huge right hand lands with fierce perfection! This brings a smile to Feliciano's face, as he places his arm on his son's shoulder. Bueno mi hijo. Eso hará para ahora. Jesús shoots a thumbs-up, as he watches his father walk away proud. With a smirk of his own, he slowly removes his gloves. As he does this, he swipes his arm across his forehead, hoping to remove any access sweat from dripping into his vision. He grimaces and pulls back his right hand to tender his wrist. Feeling the effects of a hard day of training, he begins to repeat his family's motto. Pain is weakness leaving the body... Pain is weakness leaving the body... Pain is weakness leaving the body... His head slowly turns, as his eyes glare straight into the lens of the camera. MWE, you're in too deep. With a limp in his step, he walks himself to the boxing ring. He turns his back to it, and sits along the edge. Yeah, I've seen the sport. Professional wrestling. But who couldn't do it? It hardly takes any athleticism at all. Just look at the roster you guys have here. It's filled with punks, freaks, and posers of all shapes and sizes. So what makes me different from all of them? Dedication. Experience. Knowledge. Skill. Coming for a spear? I'll catch you in a guillotine choke. Have me up for a powerbomb? Expect a straight punch to the face. I'll roll a clothesline into an armbar. I'll block your big boot and tweak your knee for your troubles. Give up your chin, you'll be put to rest. I don't play games. Period. Jesús inhales deep. With a groan, he gets up to his feet. He stretches his arms in the air, then loudly exhales. Now look at me. I stand at 5'10, which is the same height as Paige. And at 185 pounds, I'm 25 pounds less then "The High Flying Menace" Alejandro Rodriguez. But even though I may appear like a twig in a rain forest, I'm by no means a small guy. You see, it's called cardio. MWE, you guys should really try it. Dumbbells and protein shakes don't make a proper all-around athlete. I mean hell, you wrestlers get tired after a 10 minute match. I, on the other hand, can pace myself to go all night long if necessary. Hear that ladies? Yeah, that's right. Allllll niiiigghhhhhtttt looooooooonnnggg. With a wink, he points to the camera and nods. My first stepping stone goes by the name of Blight. Just like I stated earlier, here's a perfect example of how a strange and absurd man can whip on a mask and call himself an athlete. You call this entertainment? He's nothing. I'm everything. Yet, the odds are in his favor. I mean sure, this may appear like a David and Goliath story. He stands 10 inches above me, and nearly doubles me in weight. And he'll no doubt cut a promo, in an attempt to intimidate me with his deep raspy voice and big powerful frame. Unfortunately for him, I'm not afraid. From behind, his father tosses a towel around Jesús's neck. "El Leñador" Juan Garcia estará aquí próximamente. Entonces usted ambos combatirán para la experiencia. Sonidos padre bueno. He hands Jesús a gym bag and again walks off. He tosses the bag behind him, and refocuses on the promo at hand. I train, and I train, and I train. And then after that? I train even more. And what comes from it? Results. And I'm not talking about doing some stretches and throwing a toy dummy around the ring like a buffoon. I mean real training. Hours and hours inside the gym, working with other real athletes, continually trying to improve my already incredible fight-game. Now THAT makes you dangerous. Not gimmicks. So I don't mind being the underdog. People can think what they want. Because when I step into that ring, I'll look the beast right in the eye, and I'll show no fear. Then, I'll end him. Knockout or submission, it won't matter. You've been warned. ¡El Leñador ha llegado! The camera pans over, and the entire shot is filled up by a large hairy chest. It slowly zooms back to show a huge muscular man, both tall and wide in stature, with a clean-shaven head and a Grizzly Adams style of facial hair. Fear the beard bitches. Meet my training partner. Juan Garcia. Six feet, nine inches, and three hundred and thirty pounds of man. This is how I plan to prepare for Blight. Juan closely resembles Blight in both weight and size. However, Juan is an actual athlete, not a boogeyman wannabe. So if I can handle him, Blight will be like a gentle stroll in the park. Who says you'll handle me Cruz? Haha! Jesús chuckles under his breath, in an attempt to hide the fact that he's sore. He turns around, opens up his gym bag, flips it over, and out pours sparring equipment. We'll just see, now won't we? Jesús flings the headgear at him, which hits him in the face. Juan snarls, clearly not amused. Jesús rises up, and stares the bigger man down. ...Let's do this... Feliciano steps in the way of the camera, and places his hand over the lens. ¡No más cámara tiempo! ¡Usted necesita para salir! ¡AHORA! ¡AHORA! Feliciano begins to get aggressive with the camera man, pushing and shoving until the promo cuts to black.
