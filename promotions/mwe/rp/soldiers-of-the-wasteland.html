Soldiers of the Wasteland; Kanto Debut RP

Aug 20 2008

Kanto

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The scene is darkness. Pure black. Nothingness. No light, whatsoever. And with Kanto, don't expect anything else. This is his common setting. With no of vision, our other senses heighten. Sense of sound: Drops of liquid in the distance. Is it water? Spilled milk? The blood of decapitated lamb? Noone knows. Sense of smell: Very damp, dusty, and gloomy. Much like the smell of a graveyard. And sense of feel: Cold. Oh, so cold. Chilling straight to the bone. We hear footsteps grow near. Closer. And closer. Then, they stop. Everything does. No more footsteps. No more liquid dripping. Just an utter silence. I am near. There's no hiding your fear. Kanto has arrived. And with that, the silence is broken, as the intimidating sinner begins to speak. Allow me to do a quick run-down on my past. Age six, I saw my father beat my mother half-to-death. It made me feel... loved. Age eleven, I became best of friends with a razorblade. We'd meet up, every night. The blood running down my arm made me feel... safe. Age seventeen, my father shot himself in the backyard, leaving me as the man of the house. I held his position, just as he had, as I occasionally beat my mother to keep her in line. I felt... proud. Age nineteen, my mother snapped. She pulled a gun, and shot me multiple times, once in the abdomen, and once in the shoulder. She then took her own life with a bullet to the brain. I felt, and knew... I was invincible. For I survived. She didn't. Her loss, not mine. Nevertheless, from then on out, I've been apoplectic. Kanto is now heard breathing in anger, in and out, fairly loud. Enough about me. Onto my match. Xavier Stone, a fair warning to you, I don't want or need your alliance. You are a pawn in my game of chess. And how much can a mere pawn help the king? Extremely little. So please, stay out of my way, or you will suffer the same fate as the opponents. Inhale, exhale, inhale, exhale. The unsaintly breathing is heard quite clear. Drip. Drip. Drip. Drip. The mysterious liquid begins again, splashing onto the floor. The two men standing across the ring are both tough and confident fighters. However, they in no way, shape, or form matchup to my in-ring aggression. They want to punch me in the temple? Kick me in the crotch? Bash a chair off my skull? Go ahead. I welcome it. The pain gives me a rush. As the saying goes 'Whatever doesn't kill you, makes you stronger'. That statement pretty much sums up my life. Kanto inhales through his nose, then releases it with a grunt of anger. I'm not main eventing, which is a shame. The truth is, no other match that is following my own will live up to the night's standards. Because if everything goes as planned, the Ann Arbor crowd won't have to wait 'till the main event. I plan to have a blood bath of my own. A slight rustle is heard, followed by the sound of knuckles cracking. Sullivan... Poor, poor Sullivan. The lies you have told, bring a smirk to my face. You will not decimate me. You won't even come close. Your mistake is underestimating my power, for I will crush you, along with your ego. However, Aiden Copane, I must say, you looked sharp in your return match. And of course, your experience will have to carry young Sullivan throughout the match. However, though all your past battles, you've never faced an individual of my stature. Let me straight up tell you... You don't have what it takes. Neither of you do. I promise victory. ..... Drip. ..... Drip. ..... Drip. ..... A long silence keeps us waiting. Seconds seem like eternity, before the deep voice returns. I have said, all that needs to be said. Nothing else is necessary. Now the question is... how shall I end this visit? The classy thing to do, would be to wish you two boys luck. Unfortunately, with me, luck never plays a factor. A strong finishing line could also be 'pray to your heavens'. However, that would do nothing but give false hope, for no such place exists. So instead, I'll close us out with one word: Goodbye. Fade to the sounds of unknown dripping liquid. What is it you ask? ...Tears of innocent civilians...
