New Found Confidence

Mar 28 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
“ENOUGH IS ENOUGH!” This time, his voice is loud. His voice is angry! His voice is upset. It’s aggravated. There are many adjectives you can use to describe it, but words cannot do justice for the mood Windy Bridges is in. This isn’t the depressed, emo, Windy Bridges that could have very well jumped off a bridge a few weeks ago before he lost to the Grand Mystique. Nor is it the discouraged, however determined Windy Bridges we saw before he lost to Zak Warner and Mason Resoan. No, this time it’s different. “I’ve had enough of losing to people I know I can beat. I’ve had enough of being brushed aside. And most of all, I’ve had enough of being good ‘ole Windy Bridges, everyone’s favourite wrestler!” The anger is almost ferocious. Never has anyone heard Windy like this before. Sure, he’s been angry, sure, he’s been riled up. But this is genuine, hate-fuelled anger. “I pride myself on being a good guy. I pride myself on doing the right thing when faced with tough choices, taking the right sides when tempted. But more than that, I pride myself on being a competitor. I pride myself on striving to be the best that I can be, and I pride myself on trying to win every match I’m put into.” Clearly the words of a man who’s sick of losing. A man who might succumb to some sort of drastic measures. But it isn’t rash. It’s methodical, it’s thought out. It’s clear that Windy has done some soul searching, and that he is really saying what he wants to say, and he’s saying it exactly how he wants to say it. “And in the last few weeks, I’ve sure as hell been thrust into some interesting matches. First, I get called out by a guy who’s crazy as all hell, a guy who’s literally TRIED TO KILL ME on more than one occasion, and I get thrust into a non-sanctioned match, barely weeks after that same man lifted my truck up 20 feet into the air, with me in it, and dropped to the cold, hard, concrete floor. And we had a battle GM, probably the best battle you and I have ever had in our long history. In fact, I don’t think that we could top that one if we tried. And after that gruelling match, as if I were clearly ready to wrestle, I’m thrust into a match with the intercontinental champion. And two weeks after that, I’m thrust into a match who was being lined up for an undisputed title shot.” Windy is fuming! Red face. Heavy breathing. Like a mutated tomato on steroids (maybe not the best analogy, considering the industry, but hey, you get the idea). “And now, here I stand. Three different matches later. Three very different opponents later. One very similar result, in all three cases. In all three instances, I was lying square on my shoulders, in the middle of the ring, as the referee made a three count. And people will tell you that I looked better and better with each match, but looking good doesn’t change the record books. I stand before you, less than two months into my run in the new MWE. Windy Bridges. A former 2 time United States Champion, a former Tag Team Champion, a man who never officially lost any championship that he has held in his professional career. A man who just about went undefeated in his career before this point… is now 0 and 3 in the new MWE!!!” The camera pans out. Windy is standing in front of a white concrete wall, indistinct other than a chip here and there. You can’t see anything around it, really, but the sun protruding from behind the wall and the shadow making the white and even more indistinct greyish color imply that we are fairly late in the afternoon. The camera pans until it reveals the length of this wall, and sunshine protrudes from each side other than that of the ground. We now see Windy entirely, as he stands as tall as three quarters of the wall in height, and with 5 meters on each side of him. Dressed as we’re normally used to seeing him, in a t-shirt, jeans, and work boots, Windy begins pacing. From one side of this wall to the other. Back and forth, at a furious pace. Not really going anywhere, but unable to stay in a single place. Motionless movement, if you will. His eyes are locked on the ground, following his feet… As if searching for something. Windy laughs. But it’s a sarcastic laugh. A snicker. “And now… heh… now, because of my losing streak, I have to face some rookie at Highway to Hell. Despite the fact that I’ve been around a long time, that I’m an MWE veteran, if you will… despite my record and my accomplishments… I don’t get the benefit of the doubt. I don’t get a free pass, for my first realy pay-per-view in the new MWE. And you know what? It blows! ” Windy stops his pacing. Now, he stands straight, back against the white wall, standing up straight, he looks directly into the camera. “It truly does blow. I’m the kind of guy that works best against adversity. I’m the type of guy who needs the big time match, the big time name to put on a good show for all the people. But instead of getting the main event calibre match I SHOULD be getting, I’m stuck with… Kevin Hardaway… I’m stuck with a man who claims fame in other federations but knows nothing of those in MWE. A man who says that he’s so good, but has never been a round with some of the best. Kevin, you’ve never been in the ring with the Mason Resoans of the world. The Joey Dallases of the world. The Grand Mystiques, the Ace Cannons, the Brian Michaels’ and the D.C.s of the world. ” Windy smiles again. This time, it’s more sly. It’s almost mischievous/ “And most importantly, you haven’t been in the ring with the Windy Bridges’ of the world. All those names? I’ve faced them. Numerous times. And I’ve beaten most of them. And these aren’t GWC nobodies, these are world renowned superstars. These are guys who have their faces on magazines and movie posters, their names on businesses and products, their likeness on nerdy dungeons & dragons style internet wrestling sites. And as cocky as it may sound, I’m right in that category, brother. I have my name on cereal boxes and beer bottles. I have my face on movie posters and CD covers and concert listings. And most importantly, when Highway to Hell rolls around, the marquee isn’t going to be reading “Kevin Hardaway vs. Windy Bridges”. It’s going to read “featuring Windy Bridges in Action”. Sure, you’ve accomplished some good things in your career, and I applaud you for what you’ve done, no doubt. But Hardaway, after years of toiling away, scraping towards something better than what life has handed you, you’ve reached the major league. And you’ve signed on to the New York Yankees of the wrestling universe. The Montreal Canadiens of wrestling. The Boston Celtics of wrestling. The Pittsburgh Steelers of wrestling. And hey, since it’s topical, the Liverpool FC of the wrestling world. This is MWE baby, the cream of the crop, the best of the best, and I don’t know what you had to do, but you got one of the best in your debut. And I don’t know whether you should consider yourself lucky, or whether you should be worried, but you can bet, at Highway to Hell, whatever needs to be brought, will be brought. Because I’m tired of losing. And unfortunately for you, you stand in the way between Windy Bridges… and redemption! . ” Windy takes a deep breath and smiles. A sigh of relief. He’s said what he needed to say. But the worst is yet to come at Highway to Hell. Suddenly, that look of relief turns into one of complete surprise “Wind-ay! ” Windy jerks his head and the camera pans to reveal Dick Malibu (at least his head) popping up behind the side of the wall Windy has been standing in front of. That slick smile we all know and love, the perfectly sprayed blond lock and expensive sunglasses. “Wind-ay! Get your Canadian ass over here brother. And bring money!” The scene cuts to inside a clothing store. Presumably the contents of the white wall windy was just standing in front of. Fairly small and fairly bear, small amounts of chique looking suits, designer jeans and unusual-looking shirts hang from the walls in all colors. Light fills the brightly coloured room, a marketing tool used to make the clothing look good and to make the shopping experience a positive one. A couple of store employees fold and place clothing, while a third stands in the middle of the room with our two subjects – Windy Bridges and Dick Malibu. The camera quickly zooms on Windy’s face and nothing else, who seems a little perplexed. At the very least, a little pensive. Hand on his chin, brow furrowed, Windy motions to speak. “You know Dick, I know I hired you to be my new image consultant… and I trust you… but I don’t know if this is me…” The camera zooms out to reveal Windy, in front of a mirror in the store, looking at himself. More than anything the suit he’s currently wearing. In pure Dick Malibu fashion (no pun intended), Windy’s wearing arguably the tackiest suit ever seen. Bright yellow and purple chequered blazer with matching pants, white shoes and a white button-down shirt with the biggest collar ever, buttoned down half way, revealing a lot of Windy’s hairless chest. “You know, I think you might be right daddy-o. You don’t have enough chest hair to do that suit justice. And you’d have to grow a nice handlebar. I can picture it now. Ya gotta cut down on the shavin’ cream brother. The first sign of a good man is a little chest hair, trust me.” Windy cringes as the saleswoman/employee snickers at the understandably funny scene of Windy Bridges dressed like Dick Malibu “Ya I don’t think so, about the handlebar moustache. I have a feeling if I did that then I wouldn’t get booked for too many children’s birthday parties.” Dick looks at Windy a little weird. “Windy… you do children’s parties?” “Well… ya… I mean it’s pretty good money and I love seeing my fans on their birthdays! Dick shakes his hands in the air and shakes his head as he begins to pace around the store, both looking at other suits on the wall and back at Windy and the attractive saleswoman, who has to hold her face to prevent her from laughing --- and really… who can blaim her? “No no no! You can’t be doing children’s shows bro. Nuh-uh. Those have gotta go if you wanna be edgier. You wouldn’t catch Dick Malibu anywhere near a child. Windy’s eyes widen, obviously a little disappointed. “Well, I have a booking tomorrow before Highway to Hell, can I still do that one?” “Cancel it and any other birthday party bookings. You’re not a clown, brother. And if you want people to take ya seriously, you’re going to act seriously. Got it, daddy-o?” “Ya, sure… I guess…” Windy pouts as he adjusts his new suit. He pulls down the blazer a bit and adjust his pants before indiscreetly scratching certain… personal areas... “Hey Dick, can I take this thing off already? It’s making me itchy where the sun don’t shine.” “Ya, go for it. I think it was a little too bold for you anyway. We need to tone it down a bit. This clown talk got me thinking. If people are going to take you seriously, you need to look like a mean motherfucker. And I got the perfect get-up.” Dick smiles like only Dick can. Windy, worried, scratches some more and goes back into the changing room, closing the door behind him. Dick takes some clothes off the rack and throws them over the changing room door. “There you go daddy-o, lemme know how that fits, okay?” “Dick, I’m not sure about this…” “Relax, Windy, it’ll look fine. Put it on and let us have a look-see.” Dick waits as Windy rustles on the other side to get the clothes on. Dick sizes up the saleswoman, who isn’t laughing anymore, but has an ear to ear grin on her face. He steps up to her, lowers his sunglasses to get a better look, and adjusts his custom-fitted lord-knows-how-expensive baby-blue blazer before clearing his throat. “How you doin’?” But before Dick can finish hitting on the pretty saleswoman, Windy emerges from the changing room. And he doesn’t look happy at all… Bright white pants and a puffy shirt… YES, a puffy shirt! YES, white pants! Who wears white pants? The sales woman can barely contain herself as Dick, gleefully, approaches Windy. “Now THAT’S a shirt. What do you think brother?” “I think I’m gonna puke.” Dick, if you can believe it, looks perplexed as he takes off the glasses and looks at Windy, completely serious. “What are you talking about daddy-o? You look great! That’s a five hundred dollar shrit brother!” “I think I look like an idiot.” “You look like a pirate!” “BUT I DON’T WANNA BE A PIRATE!!!!” Dick puts his hands on his hips and paces a little more, switching his looks from the clothes hung up on the wall, once again, and the cute saleswoman. “What do you think baby?” The saleswoman looks at Dick a little surprised, still smiling (from her laugh about Windy’s shirt). “Me? Mr. Malibu it looks like you have this all under control.” “Nah, baby, I’d love to hear what you have to say. I mean, Windy’s a good-lookin’ brother. What do you think would look good on him?” “Well… you talked about wanting people to take him a little more seriously… I think you need to tone it down a little bit.” Windy smiles as he scratches himself again/ “heh… I think if we wanna tone it down we might have to go somewhere else.” Dick’s mouth drops, surprised. “Hey, watch it brother, I own 50% of this place. There’s a reason they call it ‘Dick’s premium menswear’.” “Dick, is there anything you don’t own a stake of? Dick seriously ponders the question for a second. “Well I used to own 20% of Dick’s Sporting goods… But that’s not the point… are you questioning my knowledge of men’s fashion? Need I remind you I’m the MC for many children’s beauty pageants all across the bible belt states!” “I’m sure you are Dick. And you certainly have an eye for fashion but this just doesn’t suit me. I look goofy enough as it is. I’m going to take this off… if I’m going to buy I suit I’m going to need some more… um… agreeable material.” Dick smiles once again. “Gotcha brother. You head on in there, I got just the thing for you, daddy-o. Dick grins, and his tooth actually sparkles, as he turns back and rummages amongst the clothes racks and Windy goes back into the changing room. ------------------------------------------------- The scene cuts back to the outside of the store. The white walls are the same ones as the one where Windy cut his promo in front of only an hour or so earlier. The sign in front of the store reads “Dick’s Premium Menswear”, as was expected. Signs in the windows indicate sales as pre-dusk light gives the store almost a glow. Suddenly, the glass door swings open. Out of the stores appears Dick Malibu, looking sharp, as usual. He puts on his sunglasses and runs his hand through his hair as he steps out of the store and holds the door. Next out is none other than Windy Bridges, looking pretty styling himself. Brown pants and a matching brown blazer, light blue shirt covered with a brown tie and brown vest, Windy is looking good, and looking expensive, and, thankfully, a little more toned down than what he was looking like in the store. He puts on a pair of sunglasses himself and steps out of the doorway. Almost to the beat of the music, Windy and Dick begin walking down the steps and down the sidewalk in front of the store. After a few yards, the reach the camera. With the setting mood of the store and the setting sun behind them, Windy takes off his sunglasses and addresses the camera. “You know, a lot of bad things have been said about my in my career. Trust me, I’ve been on the receiving end of some bad verbal abuse. But at least all those people had reasons to hate me. This guy, Kevin Hardaway, doesn’t even know me. Well I’m putting myself out there brother. I’m making my name known, once again, to people all around the world. I’m an institution in Canada. I’m a businessman and a damn fine professional wrestler all around the world. My career is covered in gold and accomplishments. But lately… it’s been marred with defeat. Well, that’s all about to change.” Windy smiles as Dick nods approvingly next to him. “Standing next to me right now is Dick Malibu. I HOPE that that’s a name you know. Just like me, Dick is an institution in the world of professional wrestling, and a very successful businessman, ain’t that right Dick?” “That’s right Daddy-o! You see, I’ve made a name for myself getting in people’s faces and associating myself with controversial and successful people. I own several tanning salons in southern Nebraska. I own a successful steakhouse in Salt Lake City… finest ribs you’ll ever taste. But this isn’t about me and my success, but what I’ve accomplished is relative to this discussion. You see, Windy Bridges here has hired me as his PERSONAL image consultant. He’s hired me to make him lean, to make him mean, and to give him an edge. And I plan to do just that, in several easy steps.” Dick holds up his hand, extending one finger. “Step one… buy a fancy, expensive, good looking suit. Check. Step two… make sure windy gets an attitude… and seeing as the suit comes with an attitude, check!” Dick holds up a second finger, and then a third. “And there are many more steps brother, but step number three… accomplish what has been haunting and escaping Winston Bridges since he came to the MWE. And that’s get a win under his belt, daddy-o. You see, first, it was GM, then Zak Warner and Mason Resoan. Now, tomorrow night, LIVE and only on pay-per-view! Windy faces off against… Kevin Hardaway?!?! What’s up with that!?!?!” Windy shakes his head. “You see Dick, some of the MWE brass have decided that I’m no longer the Windy Bridges I once was. That I’m no longer capable of beating anyone, of winning any gold. I’ve heard some people say that I will never amount to the World Champion I hope to someday become. So I’ve been pushed down the card… down to a glorified curtain jerker, and forced to work with some rookie who’s claim to fame is being a hall of famer in some other organization. Well, Dick here will back me up, some other organization is not MWE.” “That’s right daddy-o. MWE is the big time! It’s an international organization. It runs shows all around the world, and it’s made some people into BIG stars. And I won’t like, if it wasn’t for MWE, Windy Bridges and Dick Malibu wouldn’t be in the positions they are in today. Kevin Hardaway, you can boast all you want about past accomplishments in other promotions, but those promotions aren’t MWE!” “And I realize I might be doing the same thing… living off my past accomplishments in MWE. But at least people know what I’m talking about. But I know I have to build myself up from the ground up, because that’s what’s been decided I need to do. No matter. Starting tomorrow night, you will see the new Windy Bridges. You will see the Windy Bridges that will win his way to a world title. And with Dick consulting me, and what I have up my new… $500 sleeve… no one will be safe… from the New… and definitely improved… Windy Bridges! And tomorrow night… live… and only on pay-per-view… Kevin Hardaway…. It starts with you!!!” Windy puts his sunglasses back on. Brimming with confidence, he looks at Dick, and sort of unexpectedly, they high five. And you can bet, it’s an epic high five. The two well dressed men walk past the camera and into the dusk, as the scene fades.
