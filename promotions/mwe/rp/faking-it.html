Faking It: A rant on Sonny Williams.

Aug 14 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
*A scene opens up on a set of mansions in Detroit, Michigan. A handheld camera is shaking, violently moving around, with another man in the scene yelling at him to straighten up. The camera straightens and reveals Brian Michaels who is wearing khaki shorts and a TapouT shirt. He licks his lips and looks back at the mansions. Michaels stares at them before looking back at the camera with a pondering look.* Michaels: WAIT A SECOND. I thought Sonny Williams grew up on the ‘’mean streets of Detroit’’? Whatever happened to that gimmick? All I see around here are mansions…pools…preps…hoes…recovering alcholics…former gangbangers…Well quite frankly this an interesting scene. I here, have in my hands… *Michaels pulls his hands out of his pocket and takes out a card, however it is blurry.* Michaels; And in this card, I hold the address of Sonny Williams’ mothers’ house. Therefore I am going to walk up that driveway, knock that door down and let that lady know how terrible a son she raised. *Michaels walks across the street to a large brick mansion. He looks around at the outside images, the large windows and large expensive door. He knocks hard on it, almost breaking some of the glass. A woman, in her early 40’s comes out wearing a pink tank top and black tight workout pants. She waves hello and bends down to put her cap on the table. Michaels’ mouth opens in disbelief as Mrs.Williams’ shows off her curves and the camera zooms in.* Mrs Williams: Well howdy! Are you one of Sonny’s little friends? *Michaels gives a wink at the camera.* Michaels: Well good golly, Miss Molly! You are looking awfully pretty there Ms Williams, I mean Mrs.Williams! *Mrs.Williams does a pose for the camera, smiling at herself while she does it.* Mrs Williams: Well you look awfully hungry there! Michaels: Well Golly Gee, I was working up a sweat, who knew it could get so hot in August! *The cameraman coughs as Michaels continues smiling and hits him in the abs with an elbow, hinting to shut up. As Mrs.Williams has her back turned, Michaels bends down and frames her buttocks. He holds back the laughter as she quickly turns around.* Mrs. Williams: So how about something to eat? Michaels: Um, yes, I’d like some milF and cookies. Michaels: OH! *Michaels realizes what he has said and looks around in horror.* Michaels: I meant MILK and cookies, you know. *Mrs.Williams gives him a smile as she walks into the kitchen to pour the milk and take out the cookies. Michaels breathes a sigh of relief and mouthes off ‘’FUCK’’ to the camera. He begins to whisper, holding the handheld in his hand,.* Michaels: That was a close call. DAMNIT! Why doesn’t that woman wear more clothing? She's starting to distract me and I forgot what I was going to say! Shit! *Michaels gives a sly smile at the camera as he makes a gesture to spank Mrs.Williams. The cameraman stifles a laugh before the 40 year old turns back around.* Michaels: So Mrs. Williams, you had Sonny at a pretty early age then eh? Mrs.Williams: Yes, I was only 16. *Michaels feverishly eats his cookies, prompting Mrs. Williams to ask him to slow down.* Michaels: But it’s delicious... Mrs. Williams: I know, I know. Michaels: Can I have more for the road? Please, please, with a cherry and a 50 dollar bill on top? Mrs. Williams: Oh fine! *Mrs. Williams smiles as she goes and prepares a bag to place 3 cookies in.* Mrs.Williams: So what are you here for, exactly? Michaels: I actually came to mourn with you about your other son, who got shot. It’s always a tough loss to lose someone in the family. *Mrs. Williams looks at Michaels with a wondering eye.* Mrs.Williams: What do you mean? Michaels: You know, your other son…Jordan? Mrs.Williams:I have no other son… *Michaels stops eating the cookies and throws them down on the plate.* Michaels: Oh…okay. Forget what I said, I'm not hungry anymore. *Michaels grabs a napkin to clean up his face. He looks at what he’s doing and frowns as he throws the napkin away and stands up abruptly.* Michaels: If you’ll excuse me…I’ve got to go put my foot in your son’s ass. *Michaels bows his head and leaves the mansion, slamming the door in the process. He walks out to the front yard where he sees a baseball bat. His fingers begin to twitch, and he begins mumbling as he walks near the bat. Michaels bends down and grabs the baseball bat, eyeing it. He licks his lips and gets a serious tone in his voice.* Michaels:So Sonny Williams lied. Sonny told the World that his brother died. *Michaels nods in disapproval.* Michaels: Sonny Williams, you used to believe in things. You used to believe in honor…dignity…respect. But this, this is a new low. Faking a death? FAKING…A…DEATH. *Michaels’ facial expressions show that he is about to start ranting, his face turns beet-red. He walks outside the mansion and sees a bat laying on the front yard. He looks at it and slowly creeps toward it, looking back inside to see if Mrs. Williams is peeking.* Michaels: YOU made EVERYONE believe that you had a brother who got shot…and you don’t even have a brother. This is a new level…even for you. This is flatout…despicable. Dare I say…disrespectful to those who have had REAL deaths. *Michaels begins to rub the baseball bat up and down, admiring its features. He walks down the neighborhood and walks up to the front door of a seemingly empty house.* Michaels: See, unlike you…my girlfriend actually died. She died...and I stil haven't fully gotten over it. *Michaels stares at the bat once more, before swinging it at the door, breaking the glass. An alarm goes off as Brian feverishly walks to the house next door.* Michaels:I felt REAL emotion. REAL pain. And you try and replace that with a cheap spinoff? Sonny Williams, you’re pathetic. You had to sink to a level where you had to fake a death…for fake motivation. Your LIFE is fake, Sonny. Your Title runs…are FAKE. And if for one second, you think you’re going to pull the typical Sonny Williams bullshit…the ‘’I’m too good for you’’ shit…I will not hesitate to kick your teeth…DOWN…YOUR..THROAT. *Michaels begins to chuckle as he swings the bat at the next door, breaking it as well. The alarm goes off as Michaels slowly walks away. He continues to mumble to himself incoherently.* Michaels: I don’t appreciate…a guy like YOU…sending in a video to get fans to feel bad for you. *Michaels smiles to himself for a second.* Michaels: In FACT…Sonny Williams, you suck so bad, you couldn’t even use fake motivation properly! You LOST THE MATCH THAT YOU DEDICATED TO YOUR FAKE DEAD BROTHER! You miserable, miley son of a bitch…You’re not HALF the man you used to be. *Michaels walks to the next house, again swinging the bat but the door doesn’t budge. Michaels ponders his next decision. He drops the bat and takes a step back before super kicking the door, causing it to fall down. Michaels runs his hands through his hair, twitching feverishly.* Michaels; Sonny, I can get REAL gritty. If you want a wrestling match, go call someone else. You’ve pissed me off…you’ve crossed the line…and there’s no apologizing. There’s no going back. I’m not going to wrestle, I’m going to brawl. I’m going to give you a black eye. I’m going to make you SUFFER…the physical and mental pain that Courtney’s death brought on me. *Michaels gives a deep laugh as the camera zooms in on his face, which is full of emotion. He swallows deeply and breathes hard.* Michaels: Sonny Williams is not a man. He's a coward. This new Sonny Williams is a shell of his former self. You are not the same Sonny Williams from Mayhem Mafia. You’ve taken a step down and hear me out when I say this…I can not…I can NOT…wait…to get into the middle of that ring with you. I’m going to punch you DIRECTLY…square in the eyes. You are going to bleed…and I’m going to love it. Sonny Williams, you are a disgrace to MWE Originals everywhere. Of all of us, you ARE the weakest link. *The cameraman stifles a laugh and Michaels gives him a wondering look, causing him to be quiet.* Michaels:From the days of ’’White Suit’’ Sonny…to long, uncreative promo Sonny…to now. The still-bland, uncreative MOTHERFUCKER…Sonny. The one who lies about his brothers’ death. You try to ripoff of a REAL death. You’re pathetic, bitch. See…Unlike your life…unlike your brother…unlike your title runs…the pain in this match will be real. The suffering…will be far…far…from fake. In fact...you're not only going to hope...you're going to PREFER losing a brother...then getting in the ring with a fuckin' pissed off Brian Michaels... *Michaels shakes his head in disapproval as he bends down and picks up the baseball bat again. He walks toward the next house, grinning still.* Michaels: See, Sonny, I hate your guts. I hate what you stand for. You're an insignficant prick. I still remember...I still remember the days of John Austin. *Michaels grins to the camera as he knocks down another door after swinging the bat three times.* Michaels:John Austin was a good man...who made Sonny Williams look like hot shit, when Sonny Williams couldn't lace up anybody's boots. One year later...and he's saying other people can't tie HIS boots? Sonny...here's a history lesson. *Michaels ponders what he is about to say for several seconds before seemingly coming up with an idea and putting a finger up in the air.* Michaels:Approximately one year ago...Sonny Williams was kicking the show off. He was a bright up-and-comer and a fan favorite....his claim to fame was that he was one of the BEST damn good guys out there. He had charisma, he had wrestling ability...he was fully there mentally. Now...he thinks he's larger than life. It makes me sick. *Michaels throws the bat at the window of another house, shattering it completely. Michaels nods his head and walks towards his black 2009 Lamborghini, custom made. He climbs into the front seat. Michaels holds his hair, trying to rip it out.* Michaels:I am SO motherfucking sick…of people like you. Those slimey, sneaky snakes like YOU. People who come up to you in the locker room and say…hey…great match. Then the next day? You spread shit about them. You’re such a hypocrite and with this fake death…You fit and live out all the wrestling stereotypes. *Michaels turns on the engine, revving it, causing smoke to come out of the exhaust pipe. Michaels looks straight ahead before looking back at the camera.* Michaels:WAIT! THIS TOTALLY FEELS LIKE A SONNY WILLIAMS PROMO, RIGHT?! Just a lot of rambling… *Michaels’ clever tone goes back into a serious theme as he grits his teeth at the camera.* Michaels:Sonny Williams, I used to have respect for you as a man. You defeated me…you pinned me in the middle of the ring, one two three. That was nearly six months ago. This is a business…and history doesn’t always repeat itself. Sonny, unless you step into that ring and beat me again…you’re nothing. You’re a thing of the past kid… *Michaels reaches down and grabs a pair of sunglasses from the bottom of his seat. He puts them on top of his head and also grabs a piece of gum.* Michaels:So this was my promo…and I meant EVERY SINGLE WORD…the question now is…do you, Sonny? *Michaels pops the piece of gum in his mouth. The engine continues to roar as Michaels yells over it to be heard.* Michaels:Sonny Williams did not make MWE...MWE made you. MWE MADE YOU, you overconfident, egotistical, brash, obnoxious, MOTHERFUCKER! MWE made you...and now Brian Michaels is going to piss on your career. Bank on it! *Michaels frowns and hit’s the acceleration hard, driving off into the sunset as the camera goes to static.* OOC: I don't know what was up with the purple being bold
