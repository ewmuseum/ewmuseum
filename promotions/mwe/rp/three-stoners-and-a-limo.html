Three stoners and a limo

Jul 2 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
QUOTE Purple=Zak Warner Dark Blue=Ivory Orange= DeAngelo Williams Red=CSB We fade up into an empty basement, with a single solitary light hanging from the ceiling…. Nah, fuck that cliché shit, that’s not how Ebony and Ivory roll. We fade up into the back of a smoke filled limo, filled with girls, booze, and of course, the greatest Tag Team to never step foot inside the MWE Ring….DeAngelo fucking Williams, a.k.a. Ebony, and Zak fucking Warner, a.k.a. Ivory. But, if they are both on camera….the real question is…..who the hell is running the camera? Hello, my name is Wilford Brimley, and I have diabeetus. CSB you drunk fuck, you don’t have diabeetus….herpes simplex 2 maybe, but that’s about it. Ah, who else could put up with those too, other than Mr.CSB[i.[/i] Ya know what Mr. Narrator man….you sure do say “fuck” a lot…you should watch that language, kids could be watching. Well it seems as though DeAngelo doesn’t appreciate my language, so maybe I’ll just stay quiet for the rest of this promo. Yea, damn right you better stay..But then again….I’m the fucking Narrator, I run this shit. As I was saying, Ebony and Ivory, and their newest….associate are driving around in the back of a limo, enjoying the company of some lovely women. Wait, as a matter of fact…. needs a colorful nickname…I shall call him….Copper. Copper? Why the fuck is called copper? Who the fuck you callin Copper? You wanna fight? So help me god I’ll…I’ll….shit, I got nothin. Hey ….you ever been stoned before? Uh…well….of course I have man, I mean, come on, all the cool kids do it.. As Copper babbles on with his little lie, let me describe the setting a bit more. This is no ordinary limo, its an H2 limo. Would you really expect anything less from these guys? Now, I think I will shut up for a bit, and let these guys do the talking. CSB….it’s ok if you haven’t, hell…DeAngelo only got laid for the first time last week. Yo fuck you man, why you gotta front on me like that. You know I was with Leon whateverhisnameis’s girl a few weeks ago. So you admit you only got laid for the first time not even a month ago….DAMN. Wow….and here we all were, thinking that DeAngelo was some sort of mack daddy, when in reality he makes LeeRivvy look like fucking Casanova. ….fuck you Narrator…and fuck you CSB, you know what I meant… Alright, will you two shut the fuck up for a bit, I got a promo to cut, and I wanna get this shit done so I can sober up before I get home. After all, I got a kid to raise. Yea, I was gunna ask you…where is that little Porch Monkey? …oh HELL no, what the FUCK did you just say? Chill out man….I’m taking it back. AS I WAS SAYING, I have a promo to cut. Oh, and Toby is with Kev tonight, I pawned the little guy off so we could have a guys night out. Homies before Ho’s, knamean? Ah, you gotta love the references to shitty Adult Swim cartoons. Now, it is time to talk business, to talk about MWE. As you all may know, I haven’t exactly had the best of luck lately, and I have only myself to blame. I lost the Intercontinental Title, and then at the next Pay Per View, I got busted for drugs and suspended for a month. To make matters even worse, my opponent at the PPV Mr. Joey Sharpman decided that he was too good for a title shot and up and quit like the bitch that he is. None of that mattered though, because I couldn’t control what happened. Hell, I almost got fired and I probably would have if I hadn’t agreed to go to rehab to appease the higher-ups. The sad thing is, it wasn’t really weed that got me suspended. Hell, I can almost guarantee that over half the guys in the back toke up. No, they suspended me because apparently some supplement I took ended up on the banned substance list. They didn’t want this news to get out, so they blamed it on weed. Nonetheless, everyone knows I‘m one of the biggest advocates for legalization, so I don’t want this bunch of bullshit to be spread. So yes, I went to rehab, and as most of you might have heard I got a nice visit from one of my best friends, Kevin Hardaway. Kevin…I wanted to take the time right now, to apologize for the things I said. I stepped over the line just a bit, and I know that. But to think that it was ok to fuckin sit there and CHOKE me…did you think I wouldn’t get pissed? Damn dude…what the fuck has happened to us? I know I should have been there for your match, but I was stuck in that hellhole of a rehab center. You think I purposely kept myself from offering words of encouragement? They wouldn’t let me write out of the place cause they knew I wasn’t taking it seriously. You KNEW damn well that I didn’t want to be there and I only did so I didn’t lose my fucking job and my only way of supporting my kid. At this point, his eyes flare up and it is as if a whole new person has taken over. DeAngelo and CSB remain quiet, as he goes on his little rant. You were right Kevin. When I met you two years ago, I was clean. But I was also naive and stupid. I was young, reckless, and out of control. So I did what I had to do, and if it meant turning to a blunt to steady myself, so be it. I didn’t care, so long as it helped me…and you know what? It fucking did. I turned to weed, and I won belts. I got over with the fans and was one of the top draws in MWE, and even through all of it, you stood by me. Yet when you came at me like you did at that facility…I could tell things had changed. Even if you don’t see it…they changed. You came here to MWE because I asked you to, because I needed my brother here. I needed the man who had my back when nobody else did. I needed the greatest MWE champion ever here with me. But that‘s not what I got Kevin. No, instead of being the start I KNOW you could be, you seemed to have fell off. You want to stand there, on Mayhem, and say I am nothing but a shell of myself? How about you turn that judgmental gaze on yourself Kevin. Have you ever looked in the mirror, and been ashamed of what you saw? Even after everything that happened with Angie through the years, you stuck with her. You let her hold you down, and it‘s fucking pathetic. The first thing you said to me when you saw me, was how she took the car. The old Hardaway wouldn’t have let that happen. So I don‘t know what it is man…maybe you have gotten soft. Maybe that‘s why you still don‘t have MWE gold around your waist. So stand up on your judgemental platform all you want Kevin. Sit there and say how I NEED TO PROVE MYSELF, WHEN YOU NEED IT JUST AS MUCH AS I DO. His voice raises in pitch ever so slightly, the intensity quite obvious. CSB and DeAngelo look on worriedly, but make no attempt to calm him down. 30 minutes Kevin…30 minutes in which you think I will either prove myself, or fail trying. Do you realize that no matter the outcome, I have nothing to prove? People expect you to beat me, so if I lose it is no big surprise. If I win, I get a shot at yet another title to add to my name. If you beat me, people will think of it as just another win for you. You are always the favorite Kevin, so it comes as no shock. Yet if you lose, you will be considered a bigger failure than Creeping Death’s soon to be born kid. You will have lost to a burnt out, rusty wrestler. So tell me Kevin, who REALLY has to prove themselves this week? He shakes his head a bit, as if trying to drain water out of his ears. When he looks back at the camera, his eyes are bloodshot as normal, and when he speaks his voice is back as it always is. It is as though something had taken him over. Now, keeping with tradition, I think it’s only fair that I give my own, completely biased opinion on the rest of the PPV card. The first match is one of the first inter-gender six-man tag matches I have ever witnessed. The ‘Saints of Los Angeles‘ will take on The Dark Karnival, and to be perfectly honest, I could care less who wins. We have a Motley Crew ripoff, versus some guys who look like their mother‘s didn’t hug them enough as babies. Based SOLEY on the fact that they have the hotter woman, I guess I‘ll pick the Saints to win, although if all 6 got hit by a bus, I don’t think I would shed a single tear. Next, we have some shit called a “Prison Cell Cage match”, involving Derf and the resident W.I.F.E. of MWE, Devlin Sharpe. Derf is a cool ass dude, and personally I think that Devlin should have been swallowed, so I’ll go with Derf on this one. Call me crazy, but I am a TOTAL Derfherder. He takes off his jacket, revealing a ‘Derfhearder’ t-shirt, available on MWE.com right now. Next we have a match that can only be described as “4 Jobbers and a ladder“. Scotty McKnight, Peter “I can’t pronounce his last name so I won’t even try“, Zack Crash, and Chris England are the men involved, and I couldn’t really tell ya who I think will win. Crash stole my name, so fuck him…Scotty McKnight is just German for “I love to job out“, and Chris England is British, so that is full of fail. So Peter K, you have my vote! The next match is one I actually see stealing the show, as it is the first ever High Road match, for the MWE XTV Title. Now normally, I would pick Clarke to win since I have known him forever, but since he decided to douche out on me and Kevin, I hope he gets his head caved in. Between Kraven and Riddick, it‘s an honest tossup but I see Riddick pulling off the upset and walking out with gold. Oh, if either of you knock out Clarke, I will PERSONALLY hand you ten-thousand dollars. I don’t need to talk about my match, so I will just move on to the US title match. Virus versus Mason…that is a no-brainer. If Mason somehow loses this match, I will sit through 2girls1cup for 24 straight hours, on an endless loop. I’m not even kidding, you can hold me to that. If Mason Resoan loses this title match at the Pay Per View, I will watch that video for 24 hours. DC Versus GM, should be a decent, blood filled match. I honestly have found myself marking out for GM on multiple occasions, so I kinda want to pick him to win…but DC has just done too much to bet against. This match could go either way, so I won‘t pick a winner. Call me lame, or talk shit, but I just can‘t choose one.. After allll of those filler matches, comes one of the matches I am looking forward to the most. Hey fuck you, I ain’t no filler yo. …No, but you are WAY to white, and WAY too stoned to be talkin like that…not to mention you aren‘t even on the card. Will you two slapnuts shut up and let me finish? Good. The MWE title match will be one for the ages, as Sinc takes on Brian Michaels. Personally, I think that Brian needs to retire, and let the youngblood of MWE take over. Don’t get me wrong, I respect everything he has done for the company, but his time has passed. Sinc is the champ for a reason, and I don’t see that changing. So, like I said….this PPV isn’t anything to write home about. I‘m doubting that any title other than the XTV will change hands, a bunch of jobbers will get destroyed and embarassed, and I will single handedly prove why I belong in here. All in all…a pretty boring night if I may say so. Oh, by the way guys....Save a Collar, pop a cherry. Now CSB, cut the camera, I’m done with this shit. CSB sets the camera down, but the level of fucked up that he has reached is preventing him from realizing that he didn’t shut off the camera. And DeAngelo is too fucked up to hear me speaking right now, so let’s listen in on the guys, shall we? Holy shit man…that Devlin shit you said was funny as fuck. I can’t believe you called him the WIFE of MWE. This might be the drugs and booze talking, or maybe it’s because of the woman who has her face in my lap right now, but what exactly is a W.I.F.E? You don’t know what WIFE stands for? Washing, Ironing, Fucking, Etc? Good god man, get a grip on yourself. Speakin of getting a grip….where the fuck is your woman lately man? I ain’t seen that hot ass in a minute. ….Let’s not talk about her anymore… Oh this should be juicy, thank god the stoners left the camera on. SHIT CSB, YOU NEVER TURNED THE CAMERA OFF. OH shit, the gig is up….lets fade to….hm…how about we fade to Chartreuse, such an underappreciated color, if I may say so. Fades to Chartreuse….damn right.
