Second Fiddle

Oct 12 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Ryan Sharpe: It seems that in life, whatever I try to do I will always end up in my brothers shadow, now this may confuse some people because as you can see I am older and wiser than my brother, but the thing is, he always stands out more than I do, I tend to stay in the shadows, I don't like getting noticed but that is sometimes the downside as my brother gets all the glory even if I do the work, see this is why I don't like being in a tag team and would eventually like to break out on my own as a successful singles competitor leaving my glory hog brother behind because to be honest he has no wrestling ability, he cant chain wrestle his way out of a paper bag, he is a spotmonkey and even his spots sometimes I have to like set up for him, it makes me sick how everyone is like OH MY GOD DEVLIN SHARPE HAS DONE THE IMPOSSIBLE, well anyone can look great if they have a good partner backing them up, I feel like no one appreciates me anymore, its all Devlin Sharpe this Devlin Sharpe that I am like damnit its not all about Devlin Sharpe you know he's not like the best there is, the best there was and the best there ever will be, I mean he's not even canadian! he's not sleazy, he's not a hitman, he's not a millenium man, he's just a simple kid from London England that got famous because he can jump from high places without dieing, I am however the more talented brother yet I do not get noticed, you know what, I stopped caring about it a while, but with this tag title match rapidly approaching I am wondering where I am in my life there is only so much things you can do in a tag team division you know? its like really weird as a tag team its easier to wrestle but harder to prove that you are good in singles competition aswell, you see a lot of flack tag team wrestlers get often revolves around the fact that just because someone is in a tag team it means that they must suck as a singles competitor and must work as a unit with another wrestler in order to function properly, this is a common misconception that often leads to people looking down on tag team wrestlers, that is why you see so many tag team wrestlers breaking out of their tag teams early in their career and failing as a singles competitor, I will not lie I probably suck at singles competition, I will develop my skills and gradually learn to be more independant in tag team matches in order to slowly become great at singles competition and let this be said now, I will never turn my back on my brother I will never backstab him, if we agree to part it will be a mutual agreement and we will often reunite for nostalgia, so although I am not quite ready for singles competition I gotta start somewhere, so bring on my opponent, Jason fear but let me remind you, I fear nothing but everything fears me... (OOC: Sorry for the lack of length, my brain is like mush at 3am)
