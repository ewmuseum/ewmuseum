Derf Devided

Jul 19 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I'm back. Bitches. The scene opens in an apartment setting. A blue couch sits in the middle of a casually decorated room. A coffee table in front of it, and a 37" flatscreen in front of that. It's not a huge room, big enough for one person to be comfortable. Next to the couch is an end table with a white plate sitting on it, it appears to have crumbs on it. Someone must have been eating there. This isn't any huge lavish looking room, more of a welfare apartment. And then someone appears. Eric Derf (marks out!!!) walks into the room, dressed in a pair of comfortable looking jeans and a blue polo t-shirt. He's got a sandwich in one hand and a can of Pepsi in the other. He walks to the couch, drops down, picks up his remote with the same hand as his sandwich. He turns on the TV, flips through a few channels till he finds something he likes. Maybe it's The Simpsons, maybe it's Mythbusters, maybe he found a movie. What he's watching isn't important. After a few moments of blankly watching TV, nothing happens. His face is blank, as if there is literally nothing going on in his head. No expression, no thoughts, nothing. Exactly what "they" say TV can do for you. And then everything freezes in time. Yep, that just happened. As everything stops moving (and goes black and white for good effect) a small version of Eric Derf himself appears on one of his shoulders, just like one of "devil vs angel" situations. This smaller version of Derf is wearing a black suit, black shirt, black tie, very dark and serious looking. He's just casually standing there, waiting to speak his mind. MWE... back from the dead... I wish some things were as easy to bring back. But when something real is taken from a man, he changes. He is no longer the man he once was. A piece of him disappears. And he can spend his whole life trying to fill that hole, he can try to pre occupy himself to forget it, but it will always be there, eating him from the inside out. When MWE closed all those months ago, I was literally days from reaching my dreams. Days away from cashing in my Money in the Bank. I would have been World Champion. The world would have been in the palm of my hand. But it wasn't to be. This version of Eric Derf is quite serious, he hasn't cracked so much as a smile the whole time. His presence is cold, a huge downer. He's dead serious about everything he says, he means business. When MWE closed, I left wrestling for a while. I had some downtime, I met a girl, and we settled down. And only a few short months into our relationship, she broke the news to me. I was going to be a daddy. It was the happiest day of my life. Despite talking about this, still no smile. Nothing. Just cold, point making delivery of his speech. We were going to be a family. I even gave up wrestling to keep myself healthy. I took a job in UUW as a talk show host, just to bring in the money to support my family. But nothing I did was good enough for that girl. Yeah, we loved each other, but nothing was ever going to be OK. But we toughed it out. For our baby. My first child. The one thing I can ALWAYS be proud of. The suited Eric looks towards his feet, his voice getting raspier. This can't be an easy topic to discuss. Then that day came... We had a fight, one of many. She asked me to leave, and not thinking twice about it, I obliged and left. Didn't see her for a week. Needed to clear my head. And around this time, UUW was going to shit. I was in fear of losing both my girl and my job, but I still had my child to hold on to. Or so I thought. When I finally got back to my girlfriend about a week later, it wasn't in a way anyone wants. I decided the fighting was pointless, so I bought her flowers to say I was sorry. I drove to her house, walked in without thinking twice about knocking... and I found her with another man. In an instant, I was crushed. I didn't stick around to hear her excuses, I left immediately. Haven't talked to her since. But her cheating was nothing compared to the pain I am in now. Three days after I found her with another man, I got an e-mail from her mother, quite possibly the most gut wrenching e-mail anyone has ever read. Due to the stress levels of getting caught cheating and losing me, she had a nervous breakdown, and she lost the baby. My world was crushed. I had lost everything. My job, my girlfriend, and worst of all, my baby. I felt for the longest time I had nothing to live for. And then I got a second chance... As Eric looks back up from his feet, a familiar voice calls out into the frozen, black and white environment. Dude, eat a fucking cookie and chill out! As the suited Derf looks around, confused, he notices another Eric Derf standing on the other shoulder. Because, you know, like the devil, there is an angel to counter it. Except while one Derf is wearing an all black suit, this one is wearing nothing but black socks, flannel boxers, and a clown wig. Quite possibly the complete opposite of his counterpart. I don't want this emo sounding serious ass being the Eric Derf that comes back into MWE! I want the princess fucking, Bon Jovi killin, dinosaurs bait canuck fuck coming back! I wanna give these peoples what they want, what everyone wants! And not just a good show either,I'm gonna be a good sportsman, show some class. I'm gonna kiss hands and shake babies! The serious Derf is annoyed by now, as his face has grown in smugness. Right now, I don't have time to make jokes. I've had a rough couple of months, and I have a ton of anger that needs to be released, and I plan on taking it all out on Ace Cannon in a few weeks. If you're going to act like an immature fool, you won't be able to beat him. Ace Cannon? Ain't he the guy that once kicked in infant into a crowd of people? He got suspended for being a rasist? I remember him... I always though he was the homosexual little brother of a Hell Angel. His big brother, a huge burly biker, and all he wanted to do was figure skate... This is NOT the time for jokes! The serious Derf is pissed right now, although that isn't much different then before. But the clown Derf couldn't care less. What do you mean, no jokes?! We're the same person, you and I. We're like two pee's in a can. Or a pot. Whatever. If we split up, we ain't beating anyone. YOU need to learn to crack a smile once in a while, and I need to get my panties in a bunch more often. And we need to work together to defeat Ace Cannon. I don't need to act like a fool to defeat him. He is one of the most heartless men ever in wrestling. He will stop at nothing to defeat me. Us. This man once punted a small child into a sea of wrestling fans, just to prove a point. You know what they say about immature boy's picking on girls. He probably just had a crush on her. Maybe he though she was cute. Maybe he was just being flirty! Seriously man, you're deranged. And you're boring as a stick. As the two's bickering comes to a boiling point, they suddenly freeze in place as everything around them comes back to life. The REAL Eric Derf, aware of the thoughts going on in his head, doesn't know what to do, which traits he should bring back to MWE with him. But he's not ready to decide yet. He gets up off the couch and manually flicks the TV off. He walks out of the room, empty handed, but grabs a magazine as he heads past an end table. Continuing his travel through the apartment, he reaches his destination: The Bathroom. He goes in, and begins to "do his business". And while sitting there, it all goes frozen again. Black and white again. And of course, Serious Derf and Clown Derf are right there, still bickering in Derfs head, or technically, on his shoulders. Ace Cannon. We've never really had any confrontations before. Never really had any business with each other whatsoever. Maybe it's because we're polar opposites. I might not be in the mood for jokes right now - Speak for yourself homie! - but at least I have the emotion to express how I feel, when I feel it. You, on the other hand, act like everything in your life is great, and you couldn't care less about it all. You live your life with the best of things, fancy clothes and nice cars, tons of money to boot. Hell, your daddy even gave you a job here in MWE, one of the only reasons I think you could have EVER made a name for yourself. But come this week, Eric Derf will show Ace Cannon who the real superstar in MWE is. While you were out slewing racist remarks and getting suspended, I was working my ass off here. Perfecting my skill. And you know, maybe when you left, you were the better superstar. But you ain't no more, bitch! This is something I think, no matter the mindframe! I've become to good between the ropes since the last time you were around, I feel confident in saying I could use a dead fish as a weapon and STILL be able to beat you! Hell, I know High School Musical stars that could beat you! Then again, they probably did beat you in auditions, didn't they? The serious Derf isn't paying as much attention to the jokes being spewed by his counterpart. He has his own message to send. While the clown might be pondering to the fans, Serious Derf has a clear message, meant only for one man. Love me or Hate me, you better respect where I have come in this business in a short time. And that respect has been earned, unlike you. I made a name between these ropes, you made your name for acting like a tool outside the ropes. So when we step "between the ropes", who do you feel has the advantage? You can spew insults before our match, play politics with daddy afterwards, but inbetween those bells, you're ass is mine. AH! You said ass! Ass ass ass! Ace Cannon is an ass! I really don't care what you have been doing in the last year or so. Monday, I will destroy you, I will dominate you. I will make you wish you never came back to MWE when I kick you square in your head, just so you know how so many others feel! Roadhouse! Both Derfs cross looks, then look back towards what would be considered the camera. They begin to fade away as the scene opens back up again. Derf is still sitting on the toilet when he looks up. Bank on it. And I think you know who you're banking on to be returning too... Without needing to wait for an answer, a T-Rex eats Derf. End.
