Is anyone even going to read this?; Just thought I'd ask before you looked..

*The scene opens up to show D.C. walking sternly towards a door. He white hoodie reflects into the camera in the mid-day's sun as he walks past the camera pushing the doors open. Lindsey keeps a steady pace behind D.C. also in front of the camera, she is wearing a hoodie as well with a short plaid mini skirt on. As they walk into the building they approach a podium with a man in a suit standing behind it. A little light adorns the podium as he steps to the side and in front of D.C..* Man: Hello sir, welcome to th- *D.C. puts his hand on the side of the guys face and shoves him aside sending him into the podium he was standing behind. As the man falls back making a loud crashing noise D.C. continues to pass through an arch way as the camera keep stride. The camera turns back around for a second to show the man crawling from the rubble of the podium as he picks up a phone and mumbles something into it. As the camera turns back around D.C. is weaving his way through tables of people dressed in black suits and women in fancy dresses, eating food as the setting becomes clear that it is a restaurant. As the camera finally catches up with D.C. he stands beside a table looking at two men. As Lindsey stands beside D.C. the camera shuffles to the other side to show Richard Covell and Brian Michaels sitting at the table. *D.C. flips the table on his side. Plates, glasses, food, and silverware crash to the carpeted floor below. D.C. stands and looks down at Covell still sitting in his chair. He grabs Covell by the collar and hoists him up in to eye level. Michaels starts to stand up, but Lindsey places a knee high heeled boot on his chest and pushes him back into his chair. He lets go of Covell's collar and raises his arms up slightly. He looks at Covell's shoulders and dusts them off. Covell doesn't say anything as he looks at D.C. almost as if this was completely expected. Richard looks up at D.C..* Covell: What is it that you needed to say, that would make you come into this fine establishment... *Covell leans in towards D.C. and gets a face of complete distaste. As he angerly whipsers.* Covell: And try take a s*** on my parade. Sit your ass down... *D.C. tilts his head back looking down at Covell and grabs a chair from behind him. He turns his head back towards D.C. with an unmistakable smile... like he really just wants to rip this kids head off.* Covell: Son, did you ever hear the story about Paul Revere? *D.C. looks at Covell with a "wtf" look. I respect your ability, and I love my son... and I'll be damned if you two are the death of this fed because of something that should never have happened... *D.C. leans in closer to Covell. Almost getting right up in his face.* D.C.: Your senile, aren't you... just tell me there is something that would make me believe your just a crazy old man... fart some dust or something... have a heart attack... *Covell lets out another chuckle as he brings himself away from D.C.. Michaels again places his hands in front of his mouth.* Michaels: Maybe you should ask what happens... if we don't win... *Covell lets out a snicker as D.C. turns his head towards Michaels and then looks at Covell. Not one single foot. I will order my entire roster, security staff, and plain clothes officers... to attack on site, and remove you from the premises. You won't even be able to spit on our cameras ever again... *D.C. cracks a smile as he stares at Covell.* D.C.: Is that a fact? *Covell nods his head in compliance. How can I defy that authority??? Lord knows I've been itching to get me a brand spanking new MWE contract... *Covell realizes the sense of mockery coming from D.C.. He sits up a bit with a look of slight irritation. D.C. stands up from his seat as Lindsey looks on.* D.C.: You must have reached back into my mind because YOUR SO RIGHT! I totally want to tag team with someone... *D.C. looks at Michaels.* D.C.: With someone that I couldn't help but want to be rid of. EVERYONE SHOULD KNOW, that I'm just dying to find retribution, and save myself from the path I'm taking myself on. You are like my own personal Moses Covell!!!! GUIDE ME!!!! *The tone of D.C.'s voice begins to grow as people attention starts to shift towards them once again.* D.C.: YOU ARE SO RIGHT!!! I WANT NOTHING MORE, THEN TO REDEEM MY ACTIONS!!! I WANT EVERYONE KNOW, THAT HITTING YOUR SON WITH A CAR WAS A TOTAL MISTAKE AND PEOPLE SHOULD LOOK PAST THAT!!! *D.C. sits back down and quiets himself and points a finger in Covells face.* D.C.: You wanted Rated-X in a tag team match to try to help ease the tension... but there is nothing thats going to stop the f***ing storm thats coming... you know it... *D.C. stares at Michaels with a fire in his eyes.* D.C.: And we know it... *D.C. looks back at Covell with the same look.* D.C.: D.C. and Michaels is far from over... If you want to hire the one person that you know deep down inside, has the potential... and has even attempted, to kill your son... then the devil isn't in my corner anymore... the bloods on your hands now... She looks down at Covell and glances at Michaels. She shakes her head with a flip of her hair and a smug look on her face as she looks as Michaels shakes his head at her. As D.C. turns to face towards the entrance way there is a wall of black shirts staring at him. D.C. cracks a smile as he glances around the room. The attention, yet again, is on that table as D.C. stands in amusement looking over the crowd placed before him.* D.C.: THE F*** YOU ALL LOOKING AT? *A couple disgusted looks come across peoples faces as D.C. holds his arms out. He slightly turns and faces back towards Covell. He gives him a wink and cracks a smile, like only he can. He begins to walk towards the black shirts as he mockingly holds his wrists together in front of them as he approaches. Instead of placing him in arms or anything they simply part. D.C. a bit confused walks between the shirts and out of the restaurant as the camera keeps in tow with him. Outside the sun once again glares down on D.C. and Lindsey as they start to walk towards the parking lot area. D.C. begins to laugh as he bring his hand up to his mouth. As he wipes his mouth off Lindsey comes up beside him. The camera comes in place in front of the two as D.C. looks into it.* D.C.: Rated-X reunion... funny isn't it? Lindsey: What? *D.C. lets out a smug laugh as he continues to walk a bit.* D.C.: Not but a couple months ago, people were chanting Rated-X, and all that good s***. And when we finally bring the truth to them... they still would pay to see use team, even though we want to kill each other... We could be laying in a pool of our own blood... but people would still pay to see us back in action... *Lindsey lets out a chuckle as she looks up thinking about it.* D.C.: I mean, I can't say I regret ever being a part of Rated-X... but why now? Why attempt to reunite us now... THAT is the part that doesn't make a lick of f***ing sense to me... *D.C. lets out a deep sigh.* D.C.: Oh well though, I guess its settled... D.C. and Brian Michaels... once again teaming up, to take on MWE's Tag Team Champions... *D.C. looks off in the distance.* D.C.: No offense... but... umm... why? *Lindsey lets out a chuckle as she looks at the camera with her head resting on D.C.'s shoulder.* D.C.: Out of all the possibilities, out of all the competition... we are going to go toe to toe... with not one... not two... but three... of the biggest wastes of sperm that this planet has ever seen. How you doing? Eat any good meat balls lately? Or just balls? See I've made a living, out of being the one person... that defies anyone, that thinks they can take me. I will talk circles around you, and come up to you, face to face, look you dead in the eyes... grab my nuts and flip you the f*** off for the entire world to see... That chill that ran down your spine. That sinking feeling in your gut, that told you your about to get your ass handed to you, by the two people that you know damn well in your heart are simple, that... much... better... *D.C. scowls at the camera.* D.C.: Fear doesn't get scared? I take it back... I'm not gunning for Animal... I'll come right up to you, and look you eye to eye... and we'll see who blinks first.
