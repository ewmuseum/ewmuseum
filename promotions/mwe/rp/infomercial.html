Infomercial

The following thoughts, views and opinions expressed by Mr. Ace Cannon are his and his only. MWE does not sponsor nor endorse his thoughts, viwes and opinions. The following has been air time has been purchased by Ace Cannon LLC. ** After the opening message is shown on the television the camera opens and we see Ace Cannon. He is sitting behind a big oak desk, behind him a cheesy city backdrop much like something you see on a late night talk show. Ace is dressed in a navy blue suite jacket, white dress shirt and a blood red necktie. He looks to be glowing and not drunk like one of the last times we saw him. He cocks a half smile and begins to talk.** Cannon: I welcome you all to my infomercial. **Applause is heard, but there is no one in the studio.** Cannon: I thank you all for coming to my infomercial. In this time I have paid for I will discuss all matters MWE. I can do this freely, without any repercussions or a corporate noose around my neck choking the life out of me. I know a lot of you might think this is a shoot interview, and it will be one of sorts. So, if your feelings are easily hurt then turn the damn channel, because I don’t care about feelings. This is the no pussy zone, and if you’re a pussy then cya. **Cannon pauses and takes a drink out of a coffee cup that is sitting on his desk.** Cannon: Now first things first… There has been a lot of talk about my little brother Randy Covell coming to MWE. Now, Randy is a bitch. He is following a lot of the same footsteps of Brian, who everyone knows I think is the biggest pussy that walks around in MWE, but that is beside the point. Now, when Randy gets here he won’t last more then a week, I am calling it. He doesn’t have the drive for this business, he doesn’t have the heart and he isn’t a great athlete like I am. So Randy, don’t bother showing up and you would be doing yourself a HUGE favor. **Applause is heard again but it is obviously an audio track.** Cannon: Now, there has also been talk that me and Devlin Sharpe have beef… Well, it is the truth. We have more beef between one another then the USDA, and with that said I think Devlin is simply a boil on the dick tip of society. He runs his mouth like he is some big shot around MWE and he is a nothing. He could have been something at one time, but fucked it all up and is now in ICW setting up the ring and going on road trips with Triple G and Mr.Markowitz, and that speaks for itself. So Devlin, I hope you comeback I know was coming up on your little hit list and I am more then ready to mop the arena with you. So, I hope I see you soon, REAL SOON! ** We get another applause track.** Cannon: Onto something else… A man I like to call Maxx. A man who quite obviously is a bigger backstage cancer then anything that has ever stepped into a wrestling company. I know a lot of you are saying that he isn’t here anymore and I shouldn’t be rubbing this guys nose in the preverbal pile of dog shit… Well, word has it he might be coming back for one night only to make a farewell speech. Now, I pose the question what in the fuck has he done in MWE to get a farewell speech? Politick? Cause trouble? Talk shit? Man, those are some real AWESOME credentials! ** Cannon gives a thumbs up to the camera.** Cannon: I don’t like Maxx coming back, I am opposed to the idea but, if it has to happen then fine. I will let bygones be bygones for one night only and if he ever tries to comeback to be active I will block him for trying do so and it might cause me to leave. ** A new audio track is played and this one is boo’s.** Cannon: But I seriously doubt the company will pick Maxx over the one man dynasty known as ACE CANNON! **We get another applause audio track and this one last for longer the usual.** Cannon: Moving on, I have been on a losing streak of late. That is something I am not used to at all. I’m used to winning and it is something I have done all of my life weather it has been scoing with the hottest girl, winning football game, hitting large sums of money at the dog track or winning wrestling matches. I mean it is something I do but as of late it hasn’t worked out. **Cannon pauses take a drink out of his coffee cup as another audio track with boo’s is played.** Cannon: I don’t plan for this streak to last. I actually plan to end it this coming week and Monday Night Mayhem were I am booked in a tag match with Mason Resoan against Joey Dallas and Sinc Mercier. ** Boo’s fill the studio and the audio track last for a long time.** Cannon: Now Sinc Mercier and Joey Dallas are the two men that have sent me on this losing streak of sorts and there in for a hell of a night on mayhem and this leads me to the little coalition that Mercier and Dallas have entered into where Dallas is his quote un-quote savior. ** The audio track once again feels the studio with a chours of pre-fabricated boo’s.** Cannon: Sinc Mercier, I want you to come real close to the television and listen to everything I am about too tell you. I will be doing you a favor with what I am about to say, and hopefully it will open your eyes to the error of your ways. I don’t want to be your savior, but I want to be the man that saves you. So I hope your ready to listen Sinc. ** Cannon reaches for his coffee cup and takes a drink before he starts.** Cannon: You see Sinc, people like Joey Dallas have dotted history as we know it. Crazy zealot types who claim to be the savior of man. Who claim that there the next Jesus Christ or claim that they are Jesus Christ himself. ** No audio track is played as Cannon pauses for dramatic effect.** Cannon: I mean there was Jim Jones. A man who started his own religion and convinced weak minded people much like yourself to follow him to South Africa and build there own community where they could live. As Jones was declining in health he decided that his followers should go with him and when he decided this arrangement were made. Thoes arrangements were to give his people kool-aid that contained poison. His followers and himself drank them and they all died. ** A picture of the Jonestown Massacre comes on the screen** Cannon: A few years later in Waco, Texas there was a man by the name of David Koresh. He had a group of followers that were called the Branch Davidians. Koresh claimed he was the so called son of God. Koresh then pissed off the United States government by not complying to certain thing and he knew that they would eventually come get him. So he barricaded himself and seventy-one of his followers inside of a building and when the FBI started the seige on the building all hell broke loose and the building caught on fire and all seventy-one followers and Koresh died and just like Jim Jones he took his people down with him. ** A picture of the Branch Davidian Compound comes onto the screen.** Cannon: A few years later another man came to fame by being a crazy zealot leader and his name was Marshall Applewhite. He told his followers that the earth was about to be recycled and to make it to the next level they would have to commit suicide. He made all men castrate themselves and gave all women and me a suicide punch. They all drank it and then proceeded to tie black bags around there neck where they would suffocate to death and they all did. ** A picture of all the dead people in bunks from Heavens Gat Cult is put on the screen.** Cannon: Now Sinc, you with your pea sized brain you may not understand what I am getting at. Well, it is quite simple. When a weak minded person such as yourself finds a savior bad things happen. The savior has his followers do his bidding and fight his battles... But no matter how much the follower does for his savior the same result happens everytime... And that result is paying not only for your sins, but the saviors sins aswell! So Sinc, think long and hard about this follower and savior relationship you have.... Because it will only lead to your certain demise! ** Cannon reaches for the coffee cup on his desk and take a long drink before continuing.** Cannon: Sinc, I hope you see that Dallas fit’s the mold of these leaders and you fit the mold of weak minded follower, but I hope I have opened your eyes and if I haven’t it is no surprise. You are blinded by the white light and for that me and Mason will make you pay and that should show you the ills in your way concerning Joey Dallas. ** A new audio track with the combination of cheering and applause is played.** Cannon: Now that I have exposed Joey Dallas as the maniacal leader that he is let me expand on him more… Let me expand on him on a more personal level. **Cannon once again take a drink from his cup.** Cannon: Dallas, you’re a fraud, you’re a hack and this whole savior thing is bullshit. I know the real Dallas. The real Dallas likes to hit up the titty bars in every town we visit! The real Dallas like to drink Jack Daniels and drive American muscle cars and pick up on girls barley over eighteen. This whole shtick that you have now Dallas is nothing more then to propel you into getting the undisputed title! ** Cannon pauses and no audio track is played. He just gives the camera a cold stare.** Cannon: See Dallas, I wouldn’t be surprised if my father talked you into this gimmick. A lot of people don’t know that me and you used to be pretty tight and we used to be boys. We would drink beer, go slay chicks and have a good time and then you came to MWE and something changed. You have become something your not. The so-called SAVIOR has sold himself to the corporate devil just to win a title and that is a shame… A DAMN SHAME! ** Cannon once again stops and stares into the camera. His big green eyes glow with anger.** Cannon: So now Dallas, once you win that title you better best believe I want to be the first challenger. Sure, we have a blood feud but that doesn’t matter. I want to be the one to show the world you’re a poser and a fraud. I want to expose you and I will do it! The first steps will begin on Monday Night Mayhem when me and Mason destroy you and your half-wit follower Sinc Mecier, and Joey Dallas... This will simply be another chapter in our bloody history and this one will go in my favor and it won't be the end... The end is at Highway Too Hell! ** Cannon stands up from his chair as an audio track feeds in applause and the chants of “Cannon, Cannon, Cannon.” Cannon walks off stage and the camera fades to black.**
