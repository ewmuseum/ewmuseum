The laundry list of Mwe's true hardcore legend.; Alex Bowen's Fnod return rp

Feb 11 2009

Alex Bowen

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The cameras fade in front of Alex Bowen's house, he is standing in front of the camera with a pop in his hand, and a large black backpack on his back. Orange light fades in through the sky and hits Alex about chest up. The sun is going down; a large oak door with a plate on it that says 288 stands hard right behind him. Alex is in a large black coat, a white Devil driver tee shirt, and Cargo pants. The temperature outside is enough to see his breath but still walk outside, about 40 degrees. He flips his hair and looks at the camera. He grins A little bit, as he see's the cameraman push A button to zoom in on him a little bit, and he begins to talk. Alex- Friday night overdrive huh? Many people are putting me down against this Derf kid. Alex looks to his side and scoffs at the name. He spits on the sidewalk like its Derf himself. Alex- This kid does not know anything about the world of pain that is coming to him. I have been training night and day for almost two weeks now. Alex pauses and looks at the sky. Alex- I guess as hard as one man can. This match coming up here is not a match you can train for, I KNOW! I have been doing them for almost eleven years now. Alex looks down at his hands and counts out ten fingers. Alex- Almost eleven long years man, I said after I couldn't count them on both of my hands I would quit tearing my body up for good. I had thoughts of leaving the biz after EW closed its doors. However, all my friends and family got me in MWE.... Alex points to his chest; he hits it a couple times and squints his eyes at the camera. Alex- They wanted ME to come show people like you what it is like in the business. I am the man people call when they have a fucking cock robin like you. You have won four matches in a row in MWE. Won a title, big deal kid. There calling you, MWE's golden boy now. Well this is what they call me Derf. The MOST HARDCORE MOTHERFUCKER ALIVE! Alex is breathing heavy and pointing at the camera with an evil grin. Alex- I have more stories than days’ you have been alive son. Ask anyone, hell ask one of MWE's top superstars today Ace Cannon about me. He will tell you about the time I wrapped him in barbed wire around a ring post. I saw the look in that man's eyes when he woke back up Derf, he was knocked out cold from one of my baseball shots to the back of his head. I could have pinned him like that. Alex snaps his fingers. Alex- But I didn't, I dragged his ass to the outside and slid him up against that ring post and wrapped some of the ole' standby around his biceps and chest. I wonder if he still has the scars on them from trying to rip himself out of it. It tore into his arms and chest. Such A pity, I sat there and laughed at his ass the whole time. While he literally sat there, cried, and clawed at the barbed wire, which by this point had blood running down his whole body. He got out and I eventually won the Havoc title from Gus. I gave them both war stories that they can tell there kids. Wheres your war stories Derfball? Oh that is right man you do not have any. But I’ll give you one to tell your kids when and If you have any. Hell from what I have heard you might just have to take the Rosie O’d way and go adopt some kids there man! Alex walks off the porch and waves for the camera to follow him. He's walking down his front driveway to the mailbox. He gets to it, opens it, looks inside, and grabs a couple of letters. He looks at them with disgust and throws them back into the mailbox. Alex- Nothing but damn bills anymore. Alex walks away from the mailbox, brings his hand up with the pop in it, unscrews it, and takes a drink. The camera comes up to him as he's taking away the bottle from his face. He looks very refreshed; he undoes a flap in his cargo pants and puts the bottle down in his pocket. Alex- Alot of bills, just like alot of people have been saying Alex Bowen can't beat you Derf. Alot of people have money on you to win this match. But it seems to me people are just looking at your win streak, and it seems to me not your past. Have you ever even been in a hardcore match Derf? I looked back and books don't lie son, you haven't! Alex looks at the camera and nods his head a couple of times then puts on a smile and starts to loosen up. Alex- Oh but its not a big deal huh? Derf's MWE's golden boy. Derfball can do nothing wrong in those fucking smarks' minds. He's the greatest of all time, he's going to win the MWE heavyweight title in six months and knock off Whitman. Alex stops and gets right up in the camera and points out to the masses. He's getting his finger right into the glass. He taps it with his long fingernail as if it’s Derf himself. Alex- Do you want to know why they gave me the name the hardcore juggalo? It’s not just a fucking nickname; it’s my way of life. I'm A madman Derf, I've ripped peoples ears off, I've caused a man to go blind in his left eye by throwing him on A concrete floor, I've done everything that everyone wants to do in there lifetime and more. I put a kid just like you in the hospital for eight months Derf with brain trauma. He was just like you brother, he was JCW’s golden boy; they thought he would be their next world champion. Just like today but on the other side of the scale. He wanted my title, just I like I want yours. He was getting cocky. So I put him out of the business forever Derf. How would you like that? A baseball shot to the back of the head. I beat the kid in four minutes, well the ref stopped it because he was knocked out of it, and he had blood dripping from every hole in his head. But still the same will happen to you if you don't know whom you’re messing with. Alex stops and tries to recompose himself he wipes his hands down his face and lets out a big gasp of air. He looks at the camera and with a roll of his eyes he moves back up and gets back up into it's lens, he taps his fingernail right on the glass again and begins to walk and talk. Alex- You think you know shit huh boy? Do you see everyone else in MWE doing what I've done for longer than a decade? I'm pure mental toughness in human form. Alex points down the road with a shaky hand at a picnic booth. It has a few tables in it and they continue walking. Alex looks pissed right now. Alex- I've made a fucking lifelong carrier out of making people look like shit man. I've shit on just about everyone I’ve faced man. People want to get in my face after matches. Ask Sonny Williams about the time I threw him out of EW? I tossed his ass into a Ford F150 truck grill. I knocked his ass and a reporter out. What makes you think I won't do that to you? That would really fuck up your chances in life. No one likes a kid with a fucked up grill now do they? Alex and the camera reach the park with the couple of tables in it. There’s some snow on the ground and a couple of the tables. There’s a pop machine in the corner. It looks dead as its winter, but a couple of trashcans are still chained to the outside of the buildings. Alex reaches A Park bench with no snow on it and sets down. He crosses his feet, opens up his mouth, cracks his jaw A bit, and begins to talk. Alex- Derf what makes you so special? What gives you that edge? Alex tilts his head as he looks at the camera. Alex- What makes people think that you can beat me? Do you have heart? Or is it guts? Or the big pair in-between your legs? Well I have something you don't. Alex stands up, throws his arms across each other, and then back out again. Looking directly at the camera, he says.... Alex- I have that ruthless aggression that everyone wants. Some people won’t to be able to beat up there bullies, or slap around a drunk dad. Or even fuck with an interviewer or two. I’ve done all that and I didn't care who was there. If you looked like you were with them, your ass was getting beat. Alex sits back down, opens up his legs, and leans with his arms back against the bench. He gives the camera a grin and begins to talk again. Alex- Now it seems to me from your promo, yeah man I saw that. You're all worried about who's on the card, what the matches are and this and that. Good for you, but you need to keep your mind on the prize of the night. Alex points to himself. Alex- That’s I Derf, Joey Dallas isn't your worries, neither is Zak Warner. Nor is your drunken father, Oh that's right! I killed my father. He was a drunk, seriously kid? Are you trying to copy me? Besides the whole killing my father, you have my dad and family down to the same shit. Alex puts up five fingers and pulls down on one looking at it. Alex- Your father like mine was a drunk. Alex puts down the first finger and grabs a second. Alex- He beat his wife, wow Derf seeming similar to me here. Does every person beat his wife? Or is this just the luck of the draw here? Alex lets go of the second finger, pulls up a third, and starts to laugh. Alex- Fuck 5 reasons, this is the kicker here. He broke your little brother’s arm. Alex reaches behind him and pulls off his backpack. He opens up a zipper and looks inside, nothing. He opens another and pulls out an old weathered picture and puts it up to his face and gives a big smile and points to a large burly man slumping in the back with a beer in his face. Alex- Look! It’s my dad! He's drunk in the family picture. Holy shit what a surprise! Alex points to a small child beside two older people obviously his grandparents. His arm is in a sling and he doesn’t look to happy. Alex- Ahhh brother Cory, his arm is in a sling. Must be broken just like your brothers too huh? See the similarities, it might just be coincidence. But I really don't think so. Alex puts the picture back in his backpack and zips it up. He slides in next to his leg, grabs his wrists, and pulls them up to the sky stretching, giving out a big yawn. Alex- Honestly Derf, I heard someone say you were gay. I don't give a shit if you want to get a stiffy while watching Batman and Robin cartoons every Saturday morning in your Hello Kitty bed spread. That’s your shit man, good cover-up with that hot ass chick man. Really pulling it off, making people believe you aren't gay. Alex gives a cheeky grin to the camera and two thumbs up and begins to talk again. Alex- Back on topic now Derf, I've been seeing as of late that your tarnishing the havoc title above your fire place. It was A title I worked so hard for and your just letting it set there and collect dust? I gave that title its name today; some people have gone on to call me the Ric Flair of the Havoc division. Alex sits up a bit and reaches in his back pocket. He pulls out a half empty pack of Newport cigarettes, flips one up, and grabs it with his mouth. He tosses the pack on top of the park bench and reaches in his left thigh pocket he brings out a red bic lighter and lights his smoke. The fire dimly lights his face and outlines the many scars around his eyes and forehead. When he's done, he inhales deeply and tosses the lighter up with the almost gone pack of smokes. He leans back in his chair and looks to the camera man and points to the camera with a lit cigarette still in his hand. Alex- I defended that title with every fiber of my body. It took months for anyone to even put up a challenge to me, shit Chris Cassidy wasn't even a challenge. I tossed his ass all around that steel cage. But you on the other hand win it from a fucking hack in Melinko. I defended that title for 3 months every fucking week under hardcore rules. You have it and you've never even been in a real hardcore match. Alex Bowen laughs and takes a drag off his smoke; he starts to laugh again and starts to cough. Smoke comes out through his nose and mouth and winds up his face. Alex- My hardcore lifestyle comes over any match that I've been in . . . . I proved that in APWC when I owned both of Dead end. Seven foot tall both of them, me being the underdog and I beat both Phantom and Mark evil. Phantom's back in APWC, but wheres Mark Evil? He's probably still suffering from amnesia after I went Louieville slugger on his ass with a fucking shovel. I remember hearing after that match that it took fifty-nine stitches to cover that gash in the back of his head. God damn that felt so fucking good to do that. Alex's cigarette is shaking in his hand; ashes are dropping on the cement below him. He slides his foot over the now large ash pile below him and tries to recompose himself. He takes one last drag off his cigarette, reaches over, and grabs the Newport pack off the bench behind him. He puts it out smoke curling upwards and cellophane twisting in a lightly smoldering hole. Alex- But fuck it man, I can drop names all day. From The Maxx, to Sonny Williams, to Murfish, to fucking Ace Cannon. That doesn’t really make me tough shit. But my actions do homie, I've gone over them time and time with people. Hell I even got both of my brothers into the hardcore lifestyle. Alex leans back and rubs his neck. Alex- Shit happens and time goes on Derf. I am a monster that you are going to have to man up to right here and right now man. To be honest I want to put on the best match that I can right now and get back the title I never really lost. Alex reaches down and grabs his black backpack. He clears his throat as he puts it on his lap and unzips the large black pouch on the front of it. He pulls out a red velvet pouch with a drawstring on it, unfurls the strings, reaches in, and pulls out this. Alex wipes off the front of the title belt, puts it on his lap, and clears his throat again. Alex- I sat in bed and laid around for almost two years after I was fired from JCW. It was shitty when I put my body in real danger almost every week for A little over seven years for them. Nevertheless, that was their loss. Alex looks down at the belt in his lap and smiles and shifts his hips A little bit then balls up his hands and blows into them. It is almost dark and it is getting a bit colder out now. Alex- When I first saw what you were doing with the havok title I went nuts dude. I put everything into this title. It was no bullshit, all hardcore. I've been called the Ric Flair of the havoc division dude. Ric won his title sixteen times man, I won this belt twice. That's a real honor when people call you that when you've only done what I've done. It doesn't even compare to a man like Ric Flair, but still I gave prestige to that title. Just like Ric did man. That title was the lower card title in EW bro, I made every match with that title seem like it was a heavyweight title match. Alex looks down and pat's the gold in his lap. A light slap can be heard over the camera. Alex- All I want for this title is the best, and what your doing to it is a fucking shame. Alex picks up the belt with one hand And points to it, he's breathing very heavy now. Alex- What I want you to do at FNOD is bring your Belts, both of them. IF I win I want both of them, not just the X TV title man, I want both of them. In turn I'll bring both of my Havok titles. Titles that i've bought and paid for, about seven thousand each. Money out of my pocket, I'm willing to do this because that's how strongly I feel about it man. Alex puts the title back on his lap and tries to control his breathing. Alex- I'll let you keep both of my Havok titles seeing how much you like to warm yours above your fireplace. You can put them above it too. That's my challenge to you Derf, I'm willing to put my body at risk once again in a hardcore match. I'm willing to give up two titles that mean so much to me. Two titles that I've bled over, for titles that you've barely even defended. Two titles that you've beat no one who's as good as me. Alex jolts up out of his seat. He grabs the EW Havok title and straps it around his waist. He points to the camera and yells. Alex- NO ONE WHO'S EVEN HAD A CHANCE AGAINST ME DERF. Alex points to his title belt and stands up strait. Alex- I am a legend in the hardcore world. You're nothing to anyone who's a real fan of it. I've knocked people out, came close to killing people, and your great claim to fame is beating my brother. A hack who's not even in this company any more. I guess he went back to that shit hole WZCW full time, he probably just couldn't keep up with being my shadow in so many different places. Trying so hard to be just like big brother Alex, trying to make himself into a hardcore legend by winning a few titles belts. HA well O brother of mine, you have a long ass time to go before you ever reach this level that I have. Because just like all the rest in the past. You have failed, fallen, kaput, your no more, nothing, A whisper in the wind. Just like i'm going to make your name nothing in this company Derf. Alex looks in the camera with a stern face. Alex- I'm going to be ready Derf! But WILL YOU BE? Will your family be ready when you come home damn near dead after FNOD? You will be just like the countless others that I've faced Alex turns around and slams his fists on the park bench behind him and grabs it and flips it over. Alex- I'm going to make this unbeatable aura around your head go away. I'm going to shut up the whole Iwc, all those fucking armchair jockeys who are calling me a jobber. Because I might lose some matches Derf. But I've gained respect in this business, because all of those losses I have mean nothing, I wasn't decimated. I dished back out what they gave me and more. They all came out of there matches with respect for the name Alex Bowen. Alex points at the camera one more time and laughs. Alex- You say you want to steal the show. Were not going to steal the show, I'm going to. Were not putting on a show for those fucking puppets. Because Derf, this isn't wrestling, A hardcore match IS WAR!!! Alex grabs his backpack and slings it on his back. He walks away from the camera. As he passes the flipped table he kicks it . The camera fades out.
