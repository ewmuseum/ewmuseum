You Want To Know Why I'm Here...; I can tell...

Aug 20 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The scene is quiet, it’s dark a pitch black screen. The person who might be watching this may think their T.V. is busted, but it’s not. No, it’s far from being busted. The scene slowly begins to reveal something. The colors can be seen, but they’re blurred. Soon the color solidify and the object is visible. This object is a mack, the solid color is black, the trim around it is red with designs on it. Soon enough, the color begins to become clear. This mask belonged to someone but who’s? Suddenly there was a thumping sound. Like a heart beat, the rhythm seemed similar. The mask pulsates, like it’s alive. Soon the eye-holes of the mask begin to open. Silver hues peer through the wholes and the eyes blink twice. They quiver around, looking around it’s surroundings. It’s eyes darted forward, staring at something. Soon the camera slowly zooms out. There he appeared, the masked man. His origin is unknown. No one knows what he wants. His name wasn’t known, only his nickname, El Sainto. The darkness in the room began to dissolve. Soon thousands of masks appeared on the walls on Sainto’s sides. He peers up with his crystal eyes... El Sainto: You don’t need to know who I am. Truthfully if you tried to know, you’d be confused. If you honestly wish to know who I truly am I would lie to you. This mask...this mask is who I am. All you need to know is that I’m a man. Not any ordinary man, a man with a purpose. I live by a code, this code is my religions. Wrestling, the religion I go by. His voice was solemn, but had a hint that he was young. In his early twenties at the least. A spotlight slowly hit his frame. It revealed he was sitting on an altar. Behind him sat a chair--no...some type of throne. The mysterious masked man was sitting it seemed. Not budging from his spot he tilts his head to the side, glaring at the camera. El Sainto: Wrestling is my life, it’s the reason I breathe. Is it a morbid obsession? No, not if you’re someone like me it isn’t. You see, most people don’t realize this, but back then wrestling was a holy sport. Wrestling was apart of a time where there was honor, respect, dignity, pride. All I ask is what happened to that pride? Pictures appear in the background of the altar, the pictures of battered and bloodied wrestlers. El Sainto puts his hand over his mask and just rubs it. He put his hand inside of his long hair and glared up, his eyes glowing in the dense room. El Sainto: Wrestling is becoming extinct. Pure talent is drained away by fans who cry for blood. My lifestyle is in danger, because of ruthless men who’ll stop any nothing until gory...and genocide is bestowed upon this business. I can’t let that happen. I can’t watch as more of these...people try and destroy this sport. He pauses, his mind wrapped around the idea. He slowly gets up from his spot near the steps of this altar. He turns his back towards the camera and looks at the pictures. They fade...in...and out...like a filmstrip. Sainto clenched his fists, the pictures lighting a fire in his soul. El Sainto: ...Lohm Cassidy... Sainto says the man’s name in a calm tone. He was a man who had honor, and knew when to be civil. He turns back to the camera, the pictures fade away and in the center stood a picture of Cassidy. El Sainto: I’ve come to believe we’re two different people. I can tell that you’re ruthless; you don’t want to leave any prisoners in your matches. You want to get the job done as fast as possible. But for what? Glory, maybe a title shot, or maybe for some fresh tail. Me...I’m in it for one thing, and that’s for the competition. That’s all the motivation I need. Sainto stares up at the bright spotlight, he lowers his head down towards the camera for a final time. El Sainto: I’m here to make sure wrestling is given back that fresh breath of air it’s needed for so many years. And Lohm Cassidy it starts with you. Crowning of Glory, I will show the world what pure wrestling is. And maybe--if the people aren’t ignorant as I expect--will open their eyes. Although, you never know...this game we call fate may play on my side...for once. Xalapa, Veracruz, Mexico Two Months Before Signing With MWE From dance halls to the states... "Y en este rincón, un peso de esta noche a las doscientas libras, El Santo!" The scattered cheers rang out in my ear. My eyes were down on the canvas, my back to my opponent. The men in my corner were giving me quickly telling me advice. They poured water on my back and turned my face towards them. They wanted me to focus, though I already was. These people in this small place had no idea who I was. Although I wanted to keep it that way. I’ve been wandering Mexico trying to find my way. Each travel I try to learn something new towards Lucha Libre. I took a deep breath, inhale...exhale...I turned. My opponent was stocky, he hadn’t a mask and he didn’t seem to be happy for some reason. The bell rang twice and I stepped out of the corner. He lunged at me like a wild animal. He was sloppy, he had no discipline. This meant this wouldn’t take long. He lunged at me once again with a right fist, I roll under and run at him. I shift my body, quickly I took him down with a headscissors. He got up gingerly, what in God’s name was this? Did they pull these guys off of the damn street? I turn to my corner, they looked at me like I committed murder. Cornerman: ¿Qué estás haciendo? [What are you doing?!] What did he mean by that, I turn back and get hit with a stiff left arm. I eyes wandered around at the lights that swam around on the ceiling. The voices were calling out to me. The people chanting for me to get up. Some praising the guy for rattling me. He picked me up and threw me hard towards my corner. I winced some, there was no padding on the posts. This was like a human cockfight. This...this was NOT what I agreed to! color=#090]El Sainto: ¿Qué mierda está pasando? El jefe dijo que no se suponga que va a este ritmo hasta más tarde en el partido. [What the fuck is happening? The boss said that we weren't suppose going at this rate until later in the match.] Cornerman: ...Simplemente seguir luchando, vamos a hablar de ello más tarde. [Just keep fighting, we'll talk about it later] What he said just made me angry. We talked about this match, I sat down with these men and we discussed the match. How could they do something like this...?! How could they be so crooked?! My opponent grabbed me by my hair. I let out faint grunts, he posed with me, like I was some trophy he just killed. I elbow him hard in the ribs. He doubled over, his eyes closed tight. I take a running start and launch a kick into his chest. This place was shit, all of it was bullshit! I came for wrestling not to be some freakshow for these people!! El Sainto: Oye, no me importa lo que te dije, yo no voy a ser puesto en ridículo en público .... [Listen, I don't care what He told you, I'm not going to be made a fool of in public....] He was on his knees, he clutched his chest. I cock back and kick him again in the same spot. He grunts and rolls around on the ground. El Sainto: Y yo desde luego no le dejará un cadáver mediocres trates de hacer como alguna pieza débil de la basura ...[And I sure as hell will not let some mediocre corpse try to make me out as some weak piece of garbage...] He looked up at him, he was gasping at air. Good, I wanted to let people know what true wrestling is. Wrestler: No puedes hacer esto! No se aparte del plan de mierda! [You can't do this! It wasn't apart of the fucking plan!] I lean down in his face, I read him. His face was mixed with anger and confusion. The anger for getting put down so easily. The confusion, because he thought that he would win, and now...he was on the ground? It didn’t fit in his weak logic. El Sainto: Después de ganar este partido, voy a tener una pequeña charla con el "jefe". [After I win this match, I'm gonna have a little talk with the "boss".] Wrestler: No se supone que debe gan-- Before he could finish I kicked him in his skull. He dropped like a rock, I covered his body, hooking a leg. The referee looked scared. El Sainto: COUNT! He looked at me, my eyes were in a rage. He got down on his knees and started to make the count. Referee: UNO! DOES! TRES!!! He motioned for the bell and I stood up.This was just a disgrace, I didn’t come for some B.S. promotion. I exit the ring and looked at my cornermen. El Sainto: ¿Dónde está? [Where is he?] The pointed down the hallway. I shoved them out of the way and headed down towards it. If this bastard thought he would screw me out of a match then he was in for a BIG reality check!! I looked at the doors on this hallway, searching for some sign as to where this son of a bitch was. My eyes wandered, and soon I saw a sign. It was him, I could feel it in my blood. I bust the door down and march up to his desk. El Sainto: Así pues, usted pensó que usted podría intentar un movimiento para tirar sobre mis ojos sin que yo lo sepa. No sé quién coño te crees que eres, pero si crees que me voy a permitir que algunos no calificados sin nombre intente y me golpearon abajo, tienes otra cosa que viene ...! [So, you thought that you could try to pull a move over my eyes without me knowing it. I don't know who the fuck you think you are, but if you think I'm going to let some unskilled no-name try and beat me down, you've got another thing coming...!] He glared up at me, calm, too calm. He had a cigarette in his mouth. He chuckled and pulled it out, letting the smoke in his mouth pour out slowly. He finally decided to blow it all out towards me. Boss: No sé lo que estás hablando. [I don’t know what you’re talking about.] El Sainto: No alimente a mi caballo de esa mierda! [DON’T FEED ME THAT HORSE SHIT!!] I knock his papers off his desk and glare into his eyes. He looked on, seeming like he didn’t care if I tore this entire dance hall down. He looked at his cigarette, as if examining it for some type of mark. Boss: Usted vino a México sin nada, muchacho. Cuando te encontré, usted estaba fuera de dinero, sin trabajo! Y usted viene y me habla como si el propietario de este lugar ...? [You came to Mexico with nothing, boy. When I found you, you were out of money; out of a job! And you come, and talk to me like you own this place...?] He laughs lightly at me. He mocked me, he knew why I came, and he knew I didn’t have anything. There wasn’t a cent to my name when I came here. He put the cigarette in his mouth quickly. The end of it burned fiercely, he took it out of his mouth and blew a smug in my face. Boss: Vamos a decir sin rodeos, ¿de acuerdo? Usted trabaja para mí, si cambio de planes para un combate, eso no significa que tengo que advertir de ello. De hecho, no tiene que decirle a usted acerca de su mierda de partidos que no es lo suficientemente importante como para mencionarlos. ¿Tenemos claro ...? [that doesn't mean I have to warn you about it. In fact, I don't have to tell you shit about your matches that isn't important enough to mention. Are we clear...?] He talked to me like I was some kid. Like I didn’t have an opinion on what the hell goes on in my match. This place was crooked, as crooked as his arrogant smirk. I just wanna wipe it off of his face, make him see what happens when he crosses me. But I didn’t need law trouble. He just smiled at me, in his mind he won. I slammed my hands down on his desk and walked out of his room. Boss: Usted tiene el partido de mañana, la próxima vez será mejor ser feliz que se llega a poner los pies en mi lugar! [You have a match tomorrow, next time you better be happy that you get to set foot in my place!] He let out a laugh as I walked out. SLAM!!!!!! I slammed it as hard as I could. If that wasn’t enough I kicked and punched at his door like a mad man. I stormed off towards the back where the other luchadors got ready. I went to my locker. I stopped myself from opening it up. Something was wrong, the paint on the side was chipped off. I know...it doesn’t seem like much of an issue. This room was coming apart, it was old, and it had this sour smell. But it looked like someone broke into my locker room. Shit! No, no, no!! I flung it opened! A sigh of relief, nothing was taken. However...there was this small piece of paper. I unfold it: “To Whom It May Concern, We don’t know if we have the right person, nor do we know if you’ll acknowledge this letter. We do know who you are and what you can do. Yes, we know who you are. We just don’t know why you’re hiding it. It’s not like you’re some kind of outcast. Anyway, we’ve sent this letter to you to give you a chance at wrestling again. See, we think that if you were to come to our company you’d make a great acquisition. And please, don’t bother asking how we found you. In two months we’ll be waiting for an answer. If we don’t hear back from you, then we’ll respect your decision, and you won’t hear from us again. We just hope you make a right decision. Good bye, and good luck with your choice. A Hopeful Friend, MWE” That named clicked inside of my brain for a moment. I thought hard, and soon it came to me. Yes...I remember that place...at least...I remember hearing about it. Something else clicked inside of my mind. I turned slowly towards the exit and crumbled the paper up. I walk off towards the “Boss”’s door. I kick it down, he looked at me. Before he could say anything I leaped over the table and grabbed him by his collar. El Sainto: Here’s the deal, I’m done with this little dirt water place. I’m done taking crap from people like you. And one other fucking thing, I...QUIT!!! Understand me...? He looked at me like he didn’t understand English. El Sainto: DO...YOU...FUCKING...UNDERSTAND ME...?! He nods his head and me, a sign of obedience and fear. He shove him back into his seat and leave this God awful place. I'd have to get set, and get ready for a new life. The scene comes back to the domain of El Sainto. The masks, still in their rightful spots on the wall. El Sainto looked on at the black background. He turns to the camera. He sits down on the throne-like chair. El Sainto: ...Because of that letter, something snapped inside of my psyche. I realized, that wrestling today is shit. Wrestling today is what makes me want to quit, but I won't give those who are raping it the satisfaction of know they've turned another soon-to-be non-believer towards the door. No, they won't win this fight. I won't allow myself to be treated as such. He pauses for a moment. He leans his body forward, his hands clasped together. El Sainto: MWE sent me the invitation to come. They've given me the chance to express to them, why I'm one of the best. I represent the true wrestler. The wrestler who has the heart of a warrior, who's wise, and who will not be treated like some low species. LOHM...CASSIDY...! See I know you type of wrestlers. You want nothing, but to get ahead. You want to be seen. And in order to be seen you have to create this...this hard shell of comfort to shield yourself from a harsh reality. Wrestling Lohm, it isn't for someone like you. It isn't for a man who wants nothing, but glory. No, you're misguided by lies. This sport that I praise so much, you're one of the poisons that's killing it. And me? I'm the cure; the vaccine if you will. I'm here to make sure that you die off of MWE slowly. He leans in more, the camera zooms in on his face. El Sainto: That's why I'm here. To watch your kind die away from this sport. See, I get to use you as an example. I get to make you see, what happens when you abuse my passion for your on self-gain. Nor will I watch others do the same. MWE is my new sanctuary now. It's my new home now. I won't allow anything to ruin my new home. I won't allow you Lohm to ruin it. I don't care who you are, nor do I care of your intentions. You've given the opportunity to face me, and show me that I'm wrong. But I doubt I am Lohm, I really do. Tonight is mine, and it will be mine for a long...long...time... The screen begins to fade away. El Sainto: Be ready... His body is gone in the darkness, only his mask it seen. Soon he bows his head, and there's nothing. It was over. Fin.
