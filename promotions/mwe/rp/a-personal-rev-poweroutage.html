A Personal Rev-*POWEROUTAGE*; Joey Dallas RP V. 2.0 ... FUCK OFF D!

Feb 12 2009

Joey Dallas

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Space, it is truly the FINAL frontier. Stars, planets, galaxies, the great unknown as it is called is yet to be fully explored or fully seen. There will always be that curiosity, that feeling of looking into the sky and wondering what in the living hell is actually out there. There’s a reason for astrology, just like there is a reason for physics, chemistry, and mathematics. Most of us hate all those subjects, but yet we cannot help to tease ourselves into finding out about them. We may fail overall to understand, but we secretly learn what we ultimately strive for, knowledge of the unknown. What is a big bang? A white dwarf? A cosmos? SCREECH Throw on the show breaks, didn't we go over all of this last time? Well as a matter of fact, we did. However everything Dallas said last week has come to fruition, everything that Dallas has worked for, everything that he has promised us that he was going to do. Is now sitting right in front of him. The peak of the figurative mountain was finally in reach and most importantly, the Undisputed Championship was just one pinfall away from finally finding itself strapped around the waist of the man that has every right to already call himself a champion. Mayhem Wrestling Entertainment, the Undisputed Championship ... the world were all about to be ... SAVED Opening our scene much like we had closed it last week as the cameras shot begins above a slowly orbiting globe filling with the lush greens of land and the deep, rich blues of water. Standing almost exactly as we had left him is Joey Dallas, dressed sharply in a jet-black suit, the jacket to which is opened and hanging down in front of a clean white dress shirt, but perhaps most noticeable is the blood-red piece of velvet that hangs from his neck. With his arms spread apart as tho he truly stood on top of the world, eyes closed lightly and the rest of his face remains free of any emotion. Dallas: I am a man of unmatched intelligence. Lips twisting gingerly into a slight smirk as he lets out a small chuckle to himself. Dallas stands perfectly in place, the only thing moving is his lips. Dallas: Everything that I have done since stepping back into this little ... Taking a brief moment to think of how to word himself, he lets his smirk grow a little wider, a little brighter and a lot more, arrogant. Dallas drops his head and gazes at the colors circling around his newly shined dress shoes, pitch black against the floor. He motions around himself as his words seem to flow from his mouth freely. Dallas: World of ours, if you will ... Has all been a part of my divine plan to hold it .. Our camera angle switches instantly to view the front of Dallas as he brings his hand just a short distance away from himself. Twisting and turning his wrist he looks at his hand intently, something that has become quite common with our "Savior" Dallas: In the palm of my hand as I stand on top of that mountain of success that is constantly thrown around as though it were a significant term. One that gives anyone that finds themselves at the peak of their career the instant gratification of being a legend. That is just not the case, let me take you all back a few weeks to when I met the biggest challenge of my life head on ... Eyes drifting away from his hand and back towards the camera. This man is obviously talking about the war that waged between him and Ace Cannon in their Lights Out match that took both of them to their absolute limits. Dallas: To when my legacy should have already been branded into the thick skulls of everyone of you fans. Because when I took part in what is seen by many as the best match of my career, I came out of it, although battered and bloodied, with the victory ... But you people looked past that, and continue to praise David Whitman, a man not fit to even hold himself in the same regard to me ... Giving him a legacy that he has yet to earn. Was this man really serious? David Whitman is the greatest wrestler alive today, the only active wrestler and performer that has the most prestigious spot in any industry, a spot in the Hall of Fame. Whitman is a five time World Champion. And what was Dallas? Absolutely nothing ... his greatest accomplishment? A reign as the United States champion that ended just as quickly as it began? It wasn't even deemed fit enough to be recognized. His hand now slowly dropping to his side, running gently over his sports-jacket to come to a rest. Eyes still focused intently towards the camera. Emotions running fully rampant over his face now, a disgusted snarl directed towards us. Dallas: You fans continue to buy his t-shirts ... chant his name ... support him through everything he does. And where does it put him? Do you think your cheers help him become a champion? ... That just because you show him that you care about him, he can achieve anything? A small snicker escapes between Dallas' lips before he lets out a full fledged laugh at the thought that the fans could possibly help anyone to reach the top of their came, when in his mind it was the fans that needed him to get by. Dallas: It is really quite amusing that while you think you are helping this man succeed ... You pathetic people are only digging his grave deeper and deeper. You put the pressure on him to entertain you, but it only forces him to push himself to his breaking point. Making it only inevitable that he will eventually ... Just like the twig that he is ... Dallas holds both of his arms out in front of him, clenching his hands into loose fists as tho holding something between them. Just as he begins on his little tirade once again he twists his wrists down as tho breaking something in half. Dallas: Snap ... He hears you all calling his name, egging him on to the very edge and it's only a matter of time .. Before you send him plummeting from the top and right back where he belongs. Letting his fingers relax and unclench, Dallas brings his arms out and down, motioning to towards the floor as he keeps his feet constantly in place against the beautiful, life-like globe of the room. Dallas: Groveling at my feet, like every single one of you sheep already should be. Looking up towards the eternal light of salvation that stands before you today ... But instead, you insolent little maggots choose to beg and plead with your modern day heroes. He reaches towards his neck, loosening his tie slightly, leaving a small gap between it and the bare flesh at his throat. Staring still cold and emotionless seemingly through the camera and into the eyes of everyone watching. Dallas: All because you want them to wrestle for you ... To entertain you, yet you give them nothing in return except for a constant doubt in themselves ... Because when they lose .... They don't please, you people. Dallas let's his hand drift away from his tie, only to point a cold, stern finger towards the camera, indicating that he is talking to each and every person sitting on their fat asses at home, watching as this man belittles and destroys their opinions and cheers. His face now twisting into a frown of utter disgust he once again let's his words flow fluently through his lips. Dallas: You people are only continuing the corruption of an already bastardized world ... Where Lawyers defend the guilty and the judges prosecute the innocent ... Where the righteous and confused are ostracized and put on the long path down to hell without so much as a second thought. Yet the priests continue to commit the darkest of sins ... It has now become quite apparent to me, that you people don't need to be saved from the world ... The world ... Throwing his arms out to the side as the camera violent swings upward to look down at Dallas from an angle. He seems to be showing the Earth that still rests beneath as tho it were a new miracle product. Dallas: Needs to be saved, from each .. and every damn one of you. Dallas brings his right hand back up, pointing it once again back towards the camera. The snarl on his face getting even more twisted into a look of utter disgust. Dallas: Because you people are like a virus. ... You find one person to cling on to. You then take this man and suck the very life out of him until he breaks, and then move onto someone new, simply repeating the process. But what will happen to you, when there is no one left for you all to cling to ... To live your dreams through? .. What will happen to you parasites, when the only people that reach the pinnacle of success, know you ... That same, cold, stern finger that had been violently pointing towards the camera, is now twisted and turned until it points directly into the face of Joey. His equally cold sneer breaking for a brief moment for him to let out a small chuckle before parting his lips to let his words fluently escape from his mouth. Dallas: Like I know you. When they finally realize that you people are like a leech ... That nurtures itself through the success of your so called heros ... You insolent little slugs of this race need to get it through your thick little skulls that at the rate these men are falling, the well will eventually run dry. Leaving nothing for you people to live off of ... The last remaining drops of hope for you, will be buried deep below the surface, of people like me ... People that refuse to let you sink your claws in ... Firmly, yet at the same time somehow gently, Dallas grabs his bright white dress shirt in two fists. Pulling them apart quickly he reveals his chest, the blood-red velvet of his tie hangs down between his pecs as the shirt hangs loosely down his body. Dallas: And tear them apart, from the inside out. Just as I have sat back, and watched you do to the man that calls himself the champion, as he strove his entire career ... To impress you people, and for it David Whitman has seen himself at the top, far too often in his ... Dallas pulls his hands away from his body and brings them up, bending them at the elbows. Using the index and middle finger of each of them he does the quote motion. Dallas: "Illustrious" career only to be dethroned by men that put themselves before anyone else .. David Whitman drove himself to success, but then found himself pandering to you people, always trying to go for that one extra step, to entertain you people .. Only to find himself being thrown off the top of the mountain by people like Sonny Williams. Dropping his hands once again he runs his fingers along his chest, holding his hand lightly against himself, his heart beating slowly and calmly as he continues to tear apart every accomplishment that our champion has got to his name. Dallas: Who, although himself tries to please you people by selling into the stereotypes of his people to give each and every one of you a laugh, knew that it took more then your cheers and smiling faces to take that piece of personal glory for himself. He saw that the champ was still had you in the back of his mind, and he exploited it, and took the most prestigious piece of hardware in this company from your precious little hero. He clutches his fist once again over his heart. This was a side of Dallas that we have never seen before, he had become a man that resented our fans. He had changed from a man who's only goal in life was to show our fans a way to "save themselves" to this, and it was truly disgusting. Dallas: Yet, Sonny soon fell victim to the heart and passion of Whitman ... Which I will never deny that he has ... And he took what means the most to him. Once again to hold it over his head as you fans gloated over HIS hard work, and once again took a stranglehold over his emotions ... Taking him on a roller coaster ride of ups and downs until he was yet again taken advantage of, and lost everything yet again. As Brain Michaels took the title and momentum away from him, like that. Arms moving effortlessly in front of the number one contender he snaps his fingers in the symbolic fashion that is seen all too often. Dallas: And why? Because just like every other time he has been at the top of his game, he tried to satisfy YOU! He let YOU get under his skin and give him this cheap imitation of happiness that was ripped away from him once you people began cheering for someone new. Dallas takes moves his hand once more, this time raising two fingers as tho in a "peace" sign, only to turn them towards himself, pointing into his cold blue eyes as his emotion has now developed from one of disgust, to an emotion that almost resembled pity. Dallas: These eyes have witnessed as you people took a bright young star, and crafted him to fit your mold of what a superstar should be, and while that may be the case ... I am not a superstar, I am not even a wrestler .. I am a true hero, the type of men that you hear about in fairy tales and legends. Joey Dallas is a man, that was crafted through his own morals and judgments to become the man that will one day ... Once again as his gaze towards us remains unbreakable Dallas puts his arms out to show the globe still circling at his feet as he stands at the peak of the rich colors that represent Mother Earth. Dallas: Save this planet from the filth of a nation that is slowly destroying it little by little. The man, that will be forced to rescue this beautiful planet from the shattered remains of the people that you pushed too far, only to break them ... Both physically AND mentally .. And that is exactly where the precious fans of MWE have put my opponent, David Whitman. Dallas reaches up, between the knot in his tie and the bare flesh of his neck and pulls. Breaking the firm grasp of the two ends of the long strap of velvet only to let it hang exactly as his jacket and shirt already are. Dallas: Because the David Whitman of today, is a broken man ... His emotions and mind have been twisted and played with for far too long, and he has simply come to terms with it ... The man that had once been full of juvenile glee and excitement whenever he stepped into the ring for the fans .. Is now no more then a children's toy that is on it's last limbs. Letting his hands fall to his sides, we can see in this mans eyes that he does not feel the disgust he had earlier when he was degrading the efforts of the fans, nor is it a look of pity towards the champion. Instead it is a look of slight concern for the champion, if that is indeed possible from a man of like himself. Dallas: You have played with him for too long, and his colors are fading. His parts are wearing down and not quite working as well as they should be .. And as soon as you find the hot new thing in town, then you will simply throw him aside and go to playing with your next toy .. Your next, victim. To David, I have heard you ask yourself the same question again and again as you look at yourself in the mirror. Murmuring to yourself one phrase that I would have gladly helped you escape, as you say to yourself "Is this what I have become" Taking in a deep breath Dallas finally lets his eyes move away from the camera, looking down at his feet and everything that lays beneath them, knowing full well that he has already covered everything that it stands for. Keeping his neck arched to look at the ground he continues on. Dallas: You just need to remember, that if it wasn't for you giving into the cheers and chants, then you would still be the David Whitman that I could respect. He lets his words echo through our heads, and maybe even trying to get them through his own thoughts as he does so. Letting his head bob back up to look at the dark ceiling of the room littered with seemingly random spots of light representing stars. Dallas: But when you decided to let the fans decide your destiny, you lost every single shroud of respect that I could have possibly given you. You could have had the chance to be a champion of unmatched potential, but when you chose to entertain instead of fight that gleaming bit of hope ... As his last sentence flows through his lips he raises his right hand slowly, staring at it as though he held the very universe at his fingertips. Holding it still just slightly above his head he brings every finger in tightly in a fist causing the dim lighting of the solar system to instantly vanish. Dallas: Ceased to exist. Dallas lets his right hand drop to his side yet again, keeping his neck arched to look into the black of the room as tho it held the answers to all of life's greatest questions. Dallas: Face it David, you've always had the heart and passion to become the greatest man to ever achieve the legendary status that the fans give you ... But instead, you let them control everything about you. You let them take this industry and make it into a joke, by taking you to the top too quickly and then ... Every time you are put into the spotlight, you fumble, and show us exactly why you never deserved to be put in that spot in the first place. He finally let's his head lower and stare at the camera through the darkness, his features have become nothing more than vague outlines against the black. Yet somehow it seems to make his words seem even more powerful, it makes him seem even more omnipotent. Dallas: And I have this sneaking suspicion that you know it just as well as I do. That David, is why you are in the position you are now ... That is why your spirit is a shattered mess that has been thrown at my feet for me to simply push away into obscurity as I take the spotlight that I rightfully deserve ... And you're perfectly willing to let me take it. A single light begins to shine at Dallas' feet, lighting his body up just slightly, in an almost angelic glow. Which allows us to see the evil little smirk that has become a sign of the worst when it has been worked onto his face. Standing still and tall, hands limp by his sides, he let's his lips spread apart, Dallas: Your suffering David, you may not want to admit it to the precious fans that put you in that position, but I can see it in your eyes. I can see it in the way you walk, hear it in the way you speak ... Your spirit, much like a racing horse's leg, has been broken .. And just like that horse, you need to be taken behind the barn. Dallas raises his hand, putting out his index finger, and raising his thumb forming the shape of a small handgun the way that children do. Holding it out in front of him, pointed towards the floor he continues on with his speech. Dallas: And put out ... He lowers his thumb and pulls his arm up, symbolizing that he had shot through the eyes of the champion. Dallas: Of your misery. Don't be afraid David Whitman ... because the end .. Is near. As Dallas finally closes his lips for the last time the light under his feet fades out, only for the entire room to light up playing a personal tribute to the champion for his challenger, and saviour. You came to me, in a dream last night. You were standing, in a pillar of light. One minute here, but the next you were gone I thought you could stay, but I was so wrong The end is here. The game is over No more pretending. No more! NO MORE! The end is here. The end is here. The end is here. The end is here!
