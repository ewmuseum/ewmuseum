The rootenist tootenist Tag-Team ever!; Tex-Mex RP

Nov 29 2008

Tex-Mex

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The scene opens in a light, plain room, unusually light and plain, infact. With white walls and a white floor. There is not a lot in the room, just a very large opening behind white curtains. There is a white couch at the back of the room facing the curtains, on that couch is none other than Billy Joe Barker. Who seems quite happy to sit there wearing a cowboy hat, denim jeans and a black “Tex-Mex” shirt. The room doesn’t seem to be real, but Billy Joe doesn’t look concerned. Suddenly the white curtains part, and the first thing you see is a great black horse trotting through, but as he gets nearer a beautiful woman in the sexiest cowgirl outfit ever comes into view. Her unbelievably long legs either side of the horse. The horse comes to a stop in front of Billy Joe, who gapes at her as she swings her foot over the horse and jumps off in front of him. Cowgirl: What’s up cowboy? Billy Joe stutters and gasps at his field of vision, he instead looks at the great black horse. Billy Joe: …beau’iful! The cowgirl blushes. Cowgirl: You really think so? Well I do keep myself healthy. Billy Joe looks puzzled. Billy Joe: A’ meant the horse! The cowgirl looks offended and she puts her hands on her hip. Billy Joe: You’re lukin priddy good too, though! She smiles. Billy Joe: Can a have a ride on ya’ mighty fine horse? Billy Joe looks hopeful as she climbs onto the couch. She crawls onto Billy Joe’s lap and she swings a leg either side of his body. She gets close to him and almost whispers. Cowgirl: How about I ride you? Billy Joe splutters again in astonishment. Billy Joe: Well, as my Great Uncle Jim-Bob Jackson use at say… There’s two theories of arguin’ with a woman, but neider of dem work! The cowgirl looks pleased, she leans forward to whisper in Billy Joe’s ear. Cowgirl: Wake up… Billy Joe: A beg yur pardon? Cowgirl: Wake up, hick! Billy Joe looks astonished at the cowgirl, who is standing up now. SMACK! The cowgirl has slapped Billy Joe across the face. Billy closes his eyes in pain. He opens them and he see’s different surroundings, he is backstage at an MWE house show in the afternoon, and in front of him is an irate Alejandro Rodriguez, wearing a Tex-Mex shirt and jeans, who has obviously decided that Billy Joe isn’t awake yet. Alejandro: WAKE UP DANGNAABBIT!!!! Don’t make me slap you again! Billy Joe wipes the sleep out of his eyes. Billy Joe: Alrigh’, Am awake… happy? Alejandro: No, I’m not… You’re still not ready for Mayhem… Billy Joe sighs. Billy Joe: Bud Allesliceio, a won ma match las’ week! Alejandro: And I single-handedly took my team to victory against a hall-of-famer and two future hall-of-famer’s, and I’m still not ready! Billy Joe stands up from his chair and cracks open a bottle of water, he drinks some and then puts it down and faces Alejandro again, who is looking thoughtful. Billy Joe: What’s botherin’ ya, Allesanchino? Alejandro: Uh… nothing, nothings bothering me… Billy: Oh come on Aldibandrono, a know you very well, and a know that’chur hidin’ sumin’ from me. Alejandro: Well… we’ve never worked as a team before, I don’t know if we’ll have chemistry together. Billy: Gimme a break, if we wur more alike, we’d be tha same purson! Alejandro doesn’t looks so sure about this, he walks to a chair and sits down, Billy Joe walks over and sits next to him. Alejandro: I’ve never worked in a team before, I’ve always been solo, I don’t work well with other people, I’m a control freak. Billy Joe: I ain’ got no problem wit chu leadin’ the pack. As ma great grand pappy Josh Whippersnao always usse to say to me… We can't all be heroes… because someone has to sit on the curb and clap as they go by. But tha’ won’ be us Allosackologo, because we’re headin’ straigh’ for the top! And we’ll work together to get there. Alejandro: Thanks Billy, for what I understood of it, it helped me out. Billy: Any time, Allcoholino… anytime… Billy Joe gets up and goes back to get his bottle of water as Darrel walks in the room wearing a suit and tie, he sits down next to Alejandro. Darrel looks at the look on Alejandro’s face. Darrel: What’s up, Al? Alejandro: What? … oh, nothing. Darrel: Good, because I just got you a promo at this house show, it’s after the first match, which is just starting I think. I think it would be a good chance for you two to grow together more as a team. Alejandro: Thanks Darrel, I just hope he’s ready… They look over at Billy Joe, who picks his nose and eats the bogey. Alejandro smacks his head, Alejandro: We’ve rushed into this. Darrel: Give me a break Alejandro, you’re more than a match for Darius and Boyd, even if you or Billy were in a handicap match with them. Alejandro: I hope so, I'll do most of the talking anyway, excuse me, I’ll get him ready. Alejandro gets up looking distressed. Camera focuses on Darrel looking on as Alejandro smacks Billy Joe lightly over the face for trying to pick his nose again. Billy Joe looks excited as he hears the news of their promo, an official walks over to Darrel. Official: Are they ready? They’re on in two. Darrel: Yeah they’re ready. Five minutes later… Scene opens inside the Times Union Centre, the camera pans across the arena showing it sold-out even to the nosebleed seats. Suddenly “Eye of the Storm” by Bullet for my Valentine blares across the speakers and the sell-out crowd gives a mixed reaction, that soon changes when Billy Joe Barker walks out with Alejandro Rodriguez, they walk down either side of the ramp, slapping the hands of the fans on either side. Alejandro climbs in through the ropes as Billy Joe walks around the ring to grab a couple of microphones. Billy Joe climbs in the ring with the microphones as Alejandro raises his arms on the turnbuckles to another mixed reaction. Alejandro dismounts from the turnbuckle and grabs a microphone from Billy Joe as they both go to the centre. Alejandro pauses for a moment before speaking. Alejandro: I’ve said… and done a lot of bad things. And I hate to look at the past and see it affect the present, but I’m not sorry… I’m not sorry for prejudicing against Americans, however wrong that was. And I can’t promise that that won’t happen again, because it probably will. But I just want all of that, to be swept aside, because in it’s place is going to be the best mother-fuckin’ tag-team in the world! The crowd cheer and Alejandro and Billy Joe clap along with them. Alejandro: This team, is the best team because, we have everything that is required to be legendary. We cover all the aspects of tag-team wrestling. We are both speedy and powerful. Serious and amusing… David and Goliath, in one tag-team. When we’re apart, we’re great… when we team up, we’re unstoppable. We are totally different than any other tag team that has set foot in the MWE, or any other federation for that matter. It’s time that this tag-team division got a good tag team, but they’re not getting a good tag-team, they’re getting a great tag team. The crowd cheer as Billy Joe steps forward. Billy: When Flarius an’ Droid, step inthee ring wit’ us, there’s going to be nothin’ that they will be able to do to stop us. You cant’ ground Tex-Mex, you can’t beat them with speed, you can’t bea’ dem wid strength, you cann’ee beat them! So lemme tell you somethin’ Garius & Lloyd, you come ta Mayhem, an’ a promise you somethin‘, you better come figh’in, otherwise, we gunna bring the fight to ya! The crowd begin to chant “Tex-Mex” as Alejandro steps forward and takes the lead again. Alejandro: We keep talking, how we’re so different than them, because you won’t find a more generic tag-team than Darius and Boyd. Can anyone tell them apart? Which one is Darius, which one is Boyd? I have no freaking idea, I don’t care who’s ass I kick, the only thing that matters to us is those Tag Team Titles. For the past three weeks, we’ve been busting our asses to not only train Billy Joe, but to win our matches, last week, we both won our matches. How can you hope to find better tag-team champions? Billy: Now, seein’ as we talkin’ bout these titles an‘ how we‘re differen‘ from any other champions, lets take a look at there history. A picture appears on the titantron of Mason Resoan and Aiden Copane. The crowd give a fairly bad reaction towards them. Alejandro: Yes, the Canadian Xchange, the first ever MWE Tag Champions, back in the original course of MWE. Of course… they were the only real tag-team in the Original MWE. Rated X were a stable and going their separate ways, and we were not yet introduced to the Siamese queer Ryan Sharpe. What connects them… of course their Canadian heritage and their uncanny habit of masturbating with Maple Syrup. The crowd laugh but Billy Joe looks on seriously. Billy: Isn’ your girlfrien’ Canadian? Alejandro puts on a look of shock. Alejandro: Oh, shiiii- The crowd laugh again as the picture fades on the titantron and comes up with Jason Fear and American Animal. Alejandro: Now, seeing as I couldn’t care less about EW, we’re just going to skip straight ahead on the new MWE. So, here they are, Project Revolution. Only a mad-man would say that they don’t have a good future in the MWE. They’re red-hot prospects who already have a ton of talent, but I’ve been around the block a little more than they have, they aren’t ready to play with the big boys, they have bad habits, now, considering Billy Joe has come from my tutelage, that has not happened to him. Pro Rev are way too showy, they aren’t ready to focus on a goal and stop at nothing to achieve it, you can’t just say it’s another day and you’ll do your best… You have to live in the moment, and they aren’t doing that. The crowd remains neutral as Alejandro turns to face the titantron which fades into a picture of Maxxmillion Mustafa and The Grand Mystique. Alejandro: Not much I can say about these guys. One’s British and one wears a mask. What I do know is, they don’t have the wow factor. You don’t buy tickets to go and see Maxxmillion Mustafa and The Grand Mystique, you go and buy tickets to see Tex-Mex are in action. Because Tex-Mex do the impossible. We slam revolving doors, we judge books by their covers, we kill two birds with one stone, we order a Big Mac at Burger King and we get one, we make two wrongs a right… We do what we want, and take what we desire. Another chant of “Tex-Mex” breaks out amongst the crowd. Billy Joe: And remember… Billy Joe and Alejandro look at each other before hitting the punch-line. Alejandro: If at first you don’t succeed… Billy Joe: …you’re not Tex-Mex! The scene fades out as "Eye of the Storm" by Bullet for my Valentine hits the speakers and Alejandro and Billy Joe make their way out the ring, up the ramp and behind the curtain.
