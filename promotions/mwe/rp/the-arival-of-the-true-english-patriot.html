The Arival of The True English Patriot; a Chris England RP

Feb 12 2009

Chris England

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The scene opens up as the cameras begin to focus in on The True English Patriot Chris England. England is seen walking towards the camera, down the infamous 7th avenue, New York City, New York; Times Square is seen sparkling in all its glory behind England, as he makes his way towards the camera, he is dressed in a pair of torn blue denim jeans, his trade mark black True English Patriot t-shirt and a blue denim jacket which has the sleeves torn out of it; on the back of the jacket centrally placed is a big sewn on black patch, that has motorhead written on it in white writing. As England passes by the camera the camera follows him and shows him from a side on view. **England** For ten whole years I have travelled all around the world, putting my body on the line; week in and week out, fighting against some of the toughest bastards ever to step in a wrestling ring; some weeks leaving blood sweat and tears behind. Theres a dark sadistic look deep in the eyes of The True English Patriot, its as if deep down inside, leaving his blood sweat and tears behind in the ring, is simply doing whatever is necessary to get the job done. Sometimes I ask myself, why do I do this? Why do I constantly subject myself to mental and physical trauma? Well the answers a very simple one; I do this with no other goal in mind than to uphold the honour of my beloved England and prove once and for all to the entire world, that the best things in life TRUELY are English! England comes to a standstill, he then takes a packet of cigarettes out of his jacket pocket; he swiftly opens the box taking out a cigarette and places it in his mouth. England places the cigarette box back in his jacket pocket, then pulls a lighter out of his other pocket and lights up his cigarette. He takes a deep drag on the cigarette, then slowly blows the smoke out through his nose, he carefully places the lighter back in his pocket; then moves the hand holding the cigarette down by his side, before beginning to walk down seventh avenue once more. Now finally, after ten hard years of busting my arse, I find myself climbing up onto the top level of the wrestling industry ladder; I have finally arrived in Mayhem Wrestling Entertainment, the best bloody company in the wrestling industry today! England takes another deep drag from his cigarette, once more blowing the smoke out through his nose. And you know what? Now that I’ve finally hit the big time, you can bet your arse I’m here to stay! I’ve worked too bloody hard to let the opportunity that lays in front of me go to waste! England takes yet another drag from his cigarette, before suddenly the camera pans up to show that he is now stood in front of the world famous Madison Square Garden. So here we are Madison Square Garden, the place where dreams become a reality and it couldn’t of been a more welcome coincidence, that my day of reckoning, my debut in MWE just happens to be taking place inside the garden. England looks up at the building that is Madison Square Garden, taking time to soak up all the emotion of this moment in his life, he takes a drag from his cigarette before continuing. Now a lot of people have been asking me; how does a highly self reliant individual like myself, plan to co-exist with the young, brash and cocky Austin Quartz well enough to defeat arguably the best tag team in the MWE? England begins to walk down the side of Madison Square garden. Make no mistake about it, I really DON’T like teaming up with other people; however on this occasion it is for the greater good, you see me and Quartz have the same goal in sight and that is to announce ourselves in the MWE in spectacular fashion. We go into this match as under dogs, but in my mind this actually works to our advantage, you see we’re expected to lose this match, so really we have everything to gain and fuck all to lose. As long as Quartz can hold up his end of the bargain, I am sure we will have no problems at all co-existing and getting the job done. England comes to a halt and pulls a swipe card out of his jeans pocket, he turns and faces a side door entrance to Madison Square Garden, he swipes the card through a card reader to the side of the door and the door swings open. England takes one final drag from his cigarette before throwing it to the ground and stamping it out beneath his boot, he then enters the building through the door into a brightly lit corridor and as the camera follows him in, he proceeds to walk down the corridor. However if you were assuming that me and Quartz will be becoming a permanent tag team, you will find yourself way off the mark. You see we will band together in this very building on a one time only basis; we were offered the opportunity to star in the first ever ICW vs. MWE showcase, but only if we teamed together and any business man will tell you that you just can’t pass up this kind of opportunity. So we decided to put our differences to one side and team up for the greater good; but Austin Quartz, don’t think for one minute that this means I like you, because you and me, we still got some unfinished business to take care of sometime in the future! Having by this point weaved his way through the corridors of Madison Square Garden, England stops in front of a doorway. He opens the door and walks through it, suddenly he finds himself in the main arena of the venue, about 1/3 of the way up, he is standing with a side on view of the MWE ring; he takes a few steps to the left before sitting himself down on the closest arena seat to him, he soaks up the atmosphere for a few moments before speaking. So this is it, the arena in which my lifelong dream of wrestling for Mayhem Wrestling Entertainment becomes a reality. Madison Square Garden, the place where legends are born; well, this Friday the Garden will play host to yet another event that will go down in the history books, you see this Friday the entire wrestling industry will bear witness to the birth of one of its all time greats; as the True English Patriot takes the first step on his journey to greatness. A raised smirk starts to show on Englands face, at this point he is clearly oozing with confidence. Now at this point I’m sure you’re all thinking, oh great yet another egomaniac with a hugely inflated ego; but, I wouldn’t really call it inflated ego, I would say it’s more self confidence and it’s pretty hard not to be confident when the first task that lays before you, is taking on two average Joe’s that go by the name of Project Revolution. Don’t get me wrong, as a team, Project Revolution are a force to be reckoned with; but if you focus on isolating them and taking out their tag team cohesiveness, they suddenly seem a lot less threatening. England unconsciously takes his cigarettes from his pocket, he removes one from the packet and puts it in his mouth; suddenly he remembers where he is and realises it’s a no smoking building, showing a total lack of respect for the rules, he takes his lighter out of his pocket also and lights up anyway. England takes a drag, placing his cigarettes and lighter back in his pocket, he exhales slowly before continuing to speak. So first let’s look at the so called, Most Feared Man, in MWE today, Jason Fear. I mean come on, seriously, does this gut really think he is fearful; for Christ’s sakes! he looks like he belongs more in a seventies porn flick than he does in a wrestling ring! His is hardly the image that haunts an average man’s nightmares, never mind a finely tuned athlete such as myself. If you really think that when push comes to shove and me and you are stood toe-to-toe inside the squared circle; that I’m going to fear you, then you’ve got another think coming Sonny-Jim. Instead it will be you who has the fear in your eyes, because deep down, in your heart of hearts, you know that your in-ring ability is by far inferior to the finely honed skills of the True English Patriot! England chuckles displaying alternate, confident and sadistic expressions, shaking the now spent cigarette in his left hand, looking at it expectantly and realising that he had smoked the whole thing without noticing it. He dashes the stub to the floor, then forages around in his pocket for a half empty pack of chewing gum, he finds it and pulls out a strip then places it between his teeth, chewing with aggression and intent. So Mr UN-Fear-ED, this Friday you better prepare to quake in your boots before me, for this Friday, Chris England is going to show you the true meaning of fear. Because this Friday I alone, will be the only man in the ring that is the embodiment of FEAR! England Chews his gum and again grins with sadism and confidence, he leans forward and scratches his nose; as he raises his head and catches the camera lens, the deep dark coldness in his eyes portrays an aura of evil intent. Next we have American Jackass, oh sorry I mean Animal; no I don’t I mean Jackass, hang on I mean the American Animal is a Jackass! What a coincidence that a man who calls himself an animal, just happened to have a career as a pig, what a pedigree? All brawn and no brains! Which was never more evident than when you were dumb enough to end up behind the bars, you should have been standing in front of. Shaking his head with true disbelief at the man’s stupidity, England laughs out loud to himself. He knows deep down that this man has real weakness to his character. So this Friday, American Animal, the animal you will be is the pig that deep down you are and I will be the clever and cunning boar hunter, anticipating your each and every move always thinking one step ahead. England taps his fingers against the seat beside him, to the beat that he can hear in his mind. His persona appears confident of his ability to out-wit the animal; a smile begins to creep up his face and fills it with an uncommon warmth, as he realises that Project Revolution should be an easy target for a man of his own abilities. His smile broadens as he concludes that combining his own abilities with those of Austin Quartz, a man whose skill he reluctantly admires, may well create an unstoppable force. England becomes absorbed in thought as he prepares his final words. So this Friday Project Revolution, as the smoke clears and the dust settles, when all’s said and done; do you really think that you have the ability to be the team standing with their hands raised in victory? Cause you can bet your arse that I don’t! Cause everything that you two clowns have done around here will all be forgotten and you will forever become known as, them two average Joe’s who were The True English Patriot’s stepping stone to greatness and remember boys; not only do you have to deal with me, but you have to deal with Austin as well, he is just as young and hungry for success as myself. As for me, I always have been and will always be, one of the toughest bastards that will ever step inside the squared circle. Not only will you have to cope with my superior wrestling skills and intelligence, but you will also have to cope with my willingness to put my own body through hell and back to get the job done. Because whatever goes down, whatever the final outcome is, I guarantee you that you will find yourself in the fight of your life. England springs to his feet with aggressive intent and takes a step towards the camera, his upper lip twitching as a forerunner to the outburst to come. YOU SEE! Chris England is the name, and you can bet your bloody arse that FIGHTING is MY game! England lashes out at the camera, sending the cameraman and his camera crashing to the floor. As the image fades to black, England can be heard laughing with evil tones, his footsteps resounding with clarity as he strutted away.
