F**K YOU BRIAN!

Mar 4 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
OOC: I met Tim one time and it was very briefly in a MSN chat, so with that said it makes it hard to tribute him. But, let me take this OOC time and say that I am grateful for him coming up with MWE, otherwise I wouldn’t have met so many great people and had so many laughs. So Tim, I may have only met you once, but thank you. ----March 2nd, 10:01pm.---- ** The camera is transfixed on the ring in the crowded arena in Limerick, Ireland. We hear the fans booing as Hunt You Down by Saliva echoes throughout the arena after Ace Cannon’s big win over David Whitman. Cannon slides out the ring with a look of utter disdain written across his face as he stomps up the entrance way ignoring the insults fans are tossing at him. Ace continues walking up the ramp and through the curtain leading into the backstage area. He stops and looks around at all the pyro guys, producers, stage hands and any other person who is backstage. He stands there for a few moments with the same look of disdain on his face before he begins to yell** Ace: WHERE IS MY DAD??!?! ** Everyone has the same look, a look of a deer caught in the headlights. They just stare at Cannon who is now seething with anger. Cannon moves to his right where some production assistants are sitting. Cannon then grabs the table there sitting at and flips it over. He then grabs a young production assistant by his shirt collar.** Ace: WHERE IS MY FUCKING DAD?!?!? ** The scared kid points down the massive corridor. Cannon tosses the assistant to the ground and storms up the long corridor in the direction he was just pointed. As he continues to move up the corridor he passes many doors until he finally spots the one he wants. Cannon stops and looks a the name on the door for a few seconds and then spits on it. He takes two steps back and kicks the door open and we then see Richard Covell sitting behind a desk with a beautiful buxom blonde sitting on his lap. Richard whispers something in the blondes ear and she gets up and gives Richard a kiss on the cheek and then quickly exit’s the office.** Covell: Who in the hell do you think you are? ** Cannon just looks at his father and says nothing. He just gives him a cold and deathly stare.** Covell: Well if your not going to answer my question then why are you in my office? ** Cannon just looks at his father with same cold stare. He takes a few steps forward until he is right at the desk his father is sitting. He puts his hands on the desk and leans over and places his face only inches away from his fathers.** Cannon: You know god damn good and well why I am here! ** Covell leans in a little placing his face closer to Cannon’s.** Covell: Actually, son, I don’t know why you’re here. So how about you enlighten me. Cannon: I know your getting senile in your old age… But let me jog your memory… It happened about five minutes ago in my match with MWE’S golden boy David Whitman… **Cannon stops and lets his father think about it.** Covell: I still don’t see what your getting at. Cannon: Fine, let me put it to you this way. I know you sent Mason out there to kick Whitman in the balls and tarnish the clean victory I was about to earn! Covell: Son, you are delusional! ** Cannon shakes his head from side to side and begins to laugh sarcastically.** Cannon: Fuck you, dad! You can sit here and bullshit me all you want but I know damn good and well you sat back here on your little throne and called the shots. You didn’t want the black sheep of the Covell family to shine! You didn’t want the most controversial man in MWE to beat your golden boy… In your eyes that would have been bad for business! ** Covell rolls his office chair back and stands up. He walks up to his desk and looks Ace Cannon square in the face.** Covell: Listen son, none of that is the truth, but if you want to believe that to fill in some kind of personal illusion then that’s fine by me. ** Cannon lets out a loud sigh.** Cannon: This is not an illusion. It is the fucking truth… Let’s look here.. You have your favorites you like to push around here, David Whitman, DC and my dear old brother Brian! ** Covell shakes his head.** Covell: Ace, you truly are crazy. You are really under some kind of delusion. Cannon: Really, I am? How many title shots have I ever got here in MWE? ** Cannon stops so his father can think about it and after a few seconds there isn’t an answer.** Cannon: Exactly, zero. The best thing that ever happened to me is when MWE folded and proved myself in EW and in RevPro. Every time I come work for you I get held down. I don’t get what I deserve and that is going to change sooner rather then later… DAD! ** Covell looks at his son who is clearly angry.** Covell: Fine, you don’t think I give you a fair shot? Well, lets change that. Your last two victories have been over a tag team of Sinc Mercier and Joey Dallas and this victory tonight over David Whitman.. So logic would say that you should be a top contender, right? ** Cannon takes a step back and looks at his dad.** Cannon: That’s what I would like to think. Covell: Well then, the next show coming up is the Tim Fitzgerald Memorial Show, and your brother is returning to pay his respects to his good friend and wrestle a match. Now, I don’t have an opponent for him but since you feel so slighted I will give you a match against him, and if you win you will get a shot at the Undisputed title at a date to be determined. ** Cannon looks at his father for a few long seconds before replying.** Cannon: Be ready for me to embarrass your favorite son. ** Cannon walks toward the door of the office with a smile on his face…** Covell: Son, don’t you have something to say? **Cannon stops and turns around with a smile across his face and looks at his father.** Cannon: Yeah, if I ever walk in on you having a stroke I am going to kick you in the ribs and walk away you old son of a bitch. ** Cannon turns around and walks out the door as the camera pans over to Covell who is standing behind his desk with a look of disdain written across his face as the camera fades out.** ----March 4th, 12:15pm---- ** The camera opens in a well lit conference room. The walls are bland and white with nothing on them, a big oak conference table sits in the middle of the room with 6 large chairs on either side. As the camera pans down to one end we see Ace Cannon sitting there with a cigarette in his mouth. He takes a few pulls and blows a couple smoke rings.** Cannon: Where in the fuck is this clown. ** Cannon takes a few more pulls from the cigarette and then puts it out in the ash tray. He gets up and walks around the conference room and over to a wet bar. He stops at the wet bar and pours himself a drink and goes back to the table and takes his seat back. After a few minutes and a whole drink later they finally show up.** Cannon: Glad you could FINALLY make it. ** We pan around to see the very attractive Sara Patterson. She is dressed very casual in a black tight fitting sweater and a pair of blue jeans. She takes a seat to the left of Cannon and puts down her leather portfolio.** Sara: I am sorry Mr. Cannon. The weather out there is horrible and I didn’t know where I was going. Cannon: Yea, that’s a shame.. Let’s just get this interview started. Sara: Fine.. First things first, you had a huge win over David Whitman at MNM, what are your feelings on that? Cannon: I should sit here and say that I am glad to have beaten David Whitman, but I’m not. I’m not happy because I had the match won fair and square and was about to show the world that David Whitman is overrated. Then here comes Mason Resoan sticking his nose in my business. Now, I know Mason didn’t come out on his own accord, I know my father sent him out there because if I won it would be bad for business to have the child kicking MWE bad boy beat the MWE golden boy. Sara: So your not happy that you beat Whitman? Cannon: No, not at all, but David Whitman can’t dodge me. We will get a rematch, and I will beat him fair and square in the middle of that ring. Sara: How do you feel about the upcoming Tim Fitzgerald Tribute Show? Cannon: I have no feelings on this show. To me this show is just another day in the preverbal office. I won’t go out there and send Tim a tribute or any of that hokey heart-warming bullshit. The only person I pay honor to when I step into that ring is myself and that’s it. Sara: Well you know your facing your brother, Brian, right? Cannon: I am aware. Sara: What are your thoughts on facing him for the first time in singles action? Cannon: I don’t care if this was the first time of the tenth time. I am going to come out full of anger and violence like I do for every match I have. I don’t care that he is my half brother or any of that shit, I have never liked Brian and those feelings will be shown in the ring when I whip his ass from pillar to post. Sara: You are aware that he was really good friends with Tim, right? Cannon: Yea, and I don’t care. I know he will come out inspired and ready to pay tribute to his friend by beating his brother in the middle of that ring, but it’s not going to happen. Sara, Brian is a has been. He is washed up, and his career is spiraling down faster and faster with each an every match he is. Sara: Maybe so, but he is still a legend here in MWE. Cannon: He is, but all legends must die, and I plan on killing the legend of Brian Michaels on a show where he wants to prove that he still has it. Sara: Am I going out on a limb here to say your doing this to embarrass your brother? Cannon: I would love nothing more then to embarrass Brian. Sara, all he has done here in MWE is embarrass me. Every time I was on the come up about to hit my stride and make a mark here in MWE here comes Brian… He goes to our father and politicks someway to hold me down and elevate himself above me. It is a known fact backstage that Brian is the most politicking man in MWE, and that’s because he has a direct line to our father. Sara: So you don’t think Brian has earned anything he has achieved here in MWE? Cannon: Not at all… See, before our father was in charged here he did nothing but politick and hold people down who were clearly better then him, like myself. Then like I said earlier when our dad got here he had direct line to him and when Brian wanted something he told dad and it happened. It is all really unfortunate because Brian is talented and has always been United States title material and he would have made a great name for himself there but he got greedy instead and got himself pushed to the top where the fall from grace is longer and harder. Sara: My sources have been telling me that Brian while on hiatus has been telling many people that he lacks the confidence he once had here in MWE. Cannon: I don’t blame him.. He knows that his skills are dissipating and not what the once were. He knows that this is the most talented roster MWE has ever seen, and he knows that his brother is an unstoppable force right now. He also knows that if he crosses me when he returns to MWE that I will not hesitate to kick him in the head. Sara: So I take it that after this match family holidays will never be the same again? **Cannon looks at Sara and lets out a laugh.** Cannon: Family holiday? I never went anyway, it always been about Brian. I would show up and our father would go.. “ Brian really looked good in his last match.” or my little shit head brother Randy would say something like “ Brian is really kicking ass right now.” It always disgusted me so much that I spent my time on holidays drinking and watching a Christmas Story, now that didn’t bother me, I hate my family. Sara: You are full of anger for your brother and I suspect that’s what will drive you in your match. Cannon: Damn Sara, your are a smart one… Of course that’s what drives me in this match and the fact that once I pin Brian in the middle of that ring I finally get what is mine in MWE, and that is a shot at the undisputed title! Sara: Any final words? ** Cannon looks straight into the camera and his eyes widen, and a psychotic looks comes across his face.** Cannon: Brian, MWE is no longer about you, your washed up. Your career is slowly starting to look a lot like the movie that just came called The Wrestler. A old blonde haired guy who sold out Madison Square Garden and won titles left and right and was on top of the world and then just like that he fades into oblivion and no one remembers him, and Brian that is slowly happening to you weather you realize it or not. So Brian, enjoy this match why you can because your days are numbered. ** The camera fades to black as Cannon continues to stare into the camera.** ---- March 4th, 5:09pm---- ** The camera opens up and we see Paige, Lindsey and Cannon all sitting in front of a TV. None of them are saying anything, they all continue watching the television and a few minutes later someone finally talks.** Paige: Damn, that was some fucked up shit you said about Brian. Ace: Yea, so what. You know what else is fucked up? **Paige looks at Cannon and shrugs her shoulders.** Ace: You letting him fuck your brains out. ** Paige gives Ace a scornful look.** Paige: Fuck you, asshole! ** Paige gets up and storms out the room. She leaves Lindsey and Cannon sitting there….. A lone. ** Ace: So, yeah… How’s life treating? Lindsey: Good, and yourself? Ace: Can’t complain. So what did you think of my promo against Brian? Lindsey: It wasn’t very nice. Ace: Oh really? Lindsey: Yeah, he is your brother and all. It just doesn’t seem right. Ace: Well maybe if you were in my position you would understand. Lindsey: Maybe I would. Ace: When I was out there busting my ass to get what was mine Brian was out there politicking for himself and your boy DC so they could get what they wanted in MWE. Lindsey: HOW DARE YOU BRING DC INTO THIS! YOU BASTARD! Ace: Really? I am a bastard? Well, you’re a fucking slut.. Remember the other night when you let me fuck your brains out? Lindsey: YOU’RE A FUCKING ASSHOLE! YOU TOOK ADVANTAGE OF ME! ** Cannon looks at Lindsey laughing.** Ace: I did? I remember you whispering into my ear “ YOU FUCK SO GOOD!” and then screaming my name. I must have been better then DC ever was. ** Lindsey stands up over Ace with tears streaming down her face. She rears back and tries to slap Ace but he catches her arm by the wrist. Cannon stands up and backs Lindsey into a corner and put his face inches away from hers and we can see the psychotic anger written all over Cannons face** Ace: Listen here you little cum dumpster, you ever try and hit me again so help me God I will put you in a hospital bed right next to that piece of shit DC! ** The tears are really streaming down Lindsey’s face now. We can still see the psychotic anger written across Cannons face.** Lindsey: PA--- ** Cannon puts his hand over Lindsey’s mouth to stop her from yelling.** Cannon: That wouldn’t be wise. If you ever tell Paige about any of this I swear to God that I will gut you like a fish! ** Cannon then licks the tears off Lindsey’s face and walks out of the room. Lindsey stands there for a moment scared and unsure of what to do. Cannon walks back in and looks at Lindsey with a broad smile across his face.** Cannon: One more thing.. The Chemi-Kill is now my move! I will call it the TUNDRA in honor of the truck that hit DC and left him paralyzed, and I will use it this week against his best friend Brian Michaels. I hope you enjoy it sweetie. ** Cannon blows Lindsey a kiss and walks out again. Lindsey with tears streaming down her face walks over to the table and gets a cigarette as we fade out.**
