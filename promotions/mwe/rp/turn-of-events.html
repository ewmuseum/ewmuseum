Turn of Events; A Billy Joe Barker RP

Nov 5 2008

Billy Joe Barker

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
UHYEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEHARW! You have GOT to be kidding me. The camera slowly opens up as we see Billy Joe Barker, dressed in all-out cowboy garb, sitting in the passenger’s seat of David Whitman’s car. Sitting in the driver’s seat is, yep you guessed it, David Whitman. Both men get out of the car and we see Alejandro Rodriguez walk up to Billy Joe. Alejandro: Where the F*CK have you been?! David: Tex here wanted to get his lucky hat. And boots. And pants. An- Alejandro: HOLD UP. I give you 15 minutes to cool off and you go drive around for half an hour?! BJ: I wez jest- DID SOMEONE SAY JEST?!?!?! The camera pans out quickly and we see James Whitman and Eddie Sanchez standing on the left side of the camera, and Scott Jones and Thomas Williams standing on the right side. They’re all dressed in JEST Kiddin’ shirts and jeans, while Alejandro is dressed in a black A-Rod shirt and wrestling pants. Alejandro: Will you fools stop it with the camera already? The JESTers all look around, as if searching for something. Eddie: What camera? Alejandro: I’m serious; go cut your promo somewhere else. David: Wait, who’s cutting a promo? Alejandro: I thought you were. Scott: Actually, we thought, you know, since we had the cameras anyway, we should get a little footage of Tex for the fans. Eddie: Yeah, he needs exp- Alejandro: He doesn’t NEED exposure. What he NEEDS is to become a wrestler, and he’ll do that by wrestling, not by making a fool of himself on camera. Eddie: Wait, speaking of Tex, where the hell is he? The camera follows Alejandro, James, Eddie, Scott and David's glares as they all turn towards the car. Billy Joe is sitting in the driver’s seat as Thomas is sitting next to him. Everyone is screaming “NO!” as Thomas tells BJ to step on the gas. BJ does so and the car screeches off as we hear the group’s rants of “OH GOD, MY CAR!”, “MY CD COLLECTION!”, “MY WALLET’S IN THERE!” and “you know, they say existence is the world of which we are aware through our senses but does that mean that if someone is deaf, mute, blind and unaware of tasting and touching that they technically don’t exist?” Everyone turns to Scott, who gets a surprised look on his face. He takes a slow step to the left, followed by another one and continues until he is off-screen. The camera cuts to inside David’s car. Billy Joe and Thomas are both singing along to the CD player, equally out of tune. “If it takes me aaaaaaaaall night long, ‘am gonna get my drink on! Drink on! YEAH!” Thomas: OH God, the solo! I LOVE THIS SOLO! Thomas starts headbanging wildly and playing air guitar as Billy Joe laughs. BJ: Should we have taken Davey’s car without askin’, though? Thomas: AH, COOL IT! He LOVES lending friends his car! And besides, you gotta learn to drive some time, right?! BJ: Yeah, but Olliejandree-o said I gotta train t- Thomas: LISTEN! This isn’t about training! This is about- LOOK OUT! Billy Joe steps on the brake hard as the car comes to a screeching halt. The front tires are smoking and as the smoke slowly fades we see Alejandro standing in front of the vehicle. Billy Joe and Thomas get out of the car. Alejandro walks up to BJ, shooting Thomas a glare of anger. Thomas slowly starts walking off before hitting a running pace. The camera stays focused on the running Thomas for about 10 seconds as he runs off into the distance. All of a sudden, James pops up in front of the camera. James: Well, that was random, wasn’t it? The camera cuts to the inside of the gym, where Alejandro is pulling Billy Joe by the ear. BJ: AHWAHWAHWAHWAW Alejandro: What the HELL is wrong with you, you stupid hick?! BJ frees himself from Alejandro’s strong grip and holds his ear with a sad expression on his face. Alejandro just stands there, annoyed as all hell, waiting for an answer. All of a sudden, Billy’s face turns to one of contemplation. He looks around, thinking hard, before getting a stupid, big, mouth-half-open smile on his face as he turns to Alejandro. BJ: Alleemandrio, it’s like my gud ole’ uncle Jimmy Bob used ta’ say; when ya give a personal lesson in meanness to a person, don't be too surprised if they learn their lesson! Alejandro’s face turns to one of confusion. Alejandro: What the f*ck does that mean?! BJ walks over to Alejandro and puts his right arm around him. BJ: There, there, Malliejondrie. Not ev’rwun can be as quick in the cerebrul airee-ah as GOOD OL’ TEX here! Alejandro puts his head in his hands as BJ continues smiling. The camera suddenly cuts to inside the training ring which is now filled with magazines and papers. Tex is lying on the mat, reading, and Alejandro is observing him from the top turnbuckle. He is sitting calmly, with a big closed book on his lap titled “Wrestling Holds and Maneuvers”. Alejandro: Tell me something about Alex Bowen. BJ looks up, pensively. BJ: He wuz in that crazy hardcore match at War Games! That match- bwoah, I don’t think I cudda thought it up better muhself! Alejandro: FOCUS! BJ: Uh, he’s good with weapons’n such, and I oughtta keep’m in the ring at all cost. Alejandro: Good. Milenko? BJ: THES THE GUY HE WUZ FIGHTIN’ WITH! HIS CUZIN! Alejandro: … brother. BJ: Thet yer hulk howgin impresshun? Alejandro sighs. Alejandro: Never mind. Just remember; they look alike, and they fight alike; dirty. So watch out. BJ: Got it. Alejandro: What about JJ Executioner? BJ: He’z creepy too. He’s alsow uh… hardcore? Alejandro nods. BJ: ‘N he’z gonna want sum weapnz too so I gotta watch out for that again. ‘N he’s good up top rope- Alejandro sighs again. BJ: Uh- I mean he’s… fast? Alejandro sighs, slightly more agitated this time. Alejandro: No. Just- Forget about that. What can you tell me about Mason Blade? BJ: Well uh, he’s from Cana- Alejandro: AAAAAH! He goes to toss the big book at BJ but stops as he drops his head, remembering how BJ saved his life. Alejandro: No, BJ. That’s Resoan. That’s the one who almost ra- BJ: HEY! BLADE! HE’S FRUM DAVID’S HOMETOWN, INT’E?! Alejandro gets a spontaneous smile on his face as BJ realizes that means he’s right, and he gets a big smile on his face as well. BJ: He’z a BIG ol’ freak! You sed he’z got a lot in comm’n with me’n mah rasslin’ style, but I know I can take him, cuz if it’s one thing mah great great granpappy Billasayah once tol’ me is that if one thing’s bigger in Texas than e’rrwhere else it’s heart and powah! Alejandro’s expression turns to confusion as he tries to decipher what Tex just said. Alejandro: … Well, whatever the hell you just said, I think you’re a lot closer to being ready for the match now. Let’s go grab a burger! BJ looks hopeful. BJ: With a li’l bit o’ fries on the side??? Alejandro: NO, YOU FAT PIECE OF HICK SH*T! BJ looks real disappointed. Alejandro: … WITH A WHOL’ HEAPIN’ O’ FRIES, BWOAH! BJ gets a huge smile on his face. They both get up and exit the ring. The camera stays fixated behind them as they leave. As they walk towards the exit of the arena, BJ pats Alejandro on the head. BJ: Yer’ not sitch a bad guy like they say you are, Bellychandrow. Alejandro: Just wait till we get back, hick. Just wait till we get back. They continue walking as the light shines from outside the door and the scene slowly fades to black.
