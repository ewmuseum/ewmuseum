Cherity and PAIN

Jul 17 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PART 1 On relatively busy downtown looking area we see a black Ford Fusion pull up in front of a very big but semi- rustic looking building. There is a grassy courtyard directly beside the building, people are sitting in wheelchairs, smoking cigarettes in clothing reserved for hospitals. Scotty steps out of the car and looks around as he locks the door and walks around the car. Scotty: Is this seriously a hospital? Scotty looks up at the building and sees a giant white “H” on the front of the building, a smaller sigh inlayed into the brickwork reads. “Toronto General Hospital” Scotty: Guess it is... Scotty puts the keys in his pocket as he heads up the stairs to the hospital lobby. The air is clean, the same clean smell that emits from every hospital. Patients in scrubs are scattered around the lobby, the wheels on their morphine stands echo throughout the large area, civilians and visitors line the walls waiting to be seen, Scotty makes his way to the help desk. Attendant: How can I help you sir? Scotty: I'm here to pick up Leon Roberts... Where can I find him? The attendant pulls out a large book with “patients” written on it in black sharpie. She starts flipping through the pages, here glasses near the tip of her nose so she is looking over them. Attendant: Ah yes, here he is... Would you like me to call him and tell him you are waiting? Scotty: Yeah but don't tell him who it is, I wanna surprise him. The attendant puts on a beautiful smile as she picks up a matte white phone and begins dialing. Attendant: Mr. Roberts? Yes, there is someone here to pick you up, your release papers have been signed, please come to the main lobby. *Click* The attendant hangs up the phone and smiles at Scotty. Attendant: Please wait over there until Mr. Roberts arrives. Scotty: Whatever We see Scotty making his way over to a row of seats, where many other waiting civilians sit patiently for their loved ones. Scotty is wearing a black t-shirt and low cut denim jean shorts. The squeak of his Etnies shoes echoes behind him. Scotty sits down and looks over his left shoulder where he sees a father and son whispering to each other. The child looks to be about the age of 7 and cannot break his concentration from Scotty, the boys childish voice becomes more clear. Boy: Daddy! That's Scotty McKnight! It's REALLY him! Father: I think so too, but remember, his is like you and me so he needs his time away from fans, so lets just leave him alone for now. The child droops a sad from from his bright cheeks, His father looks disappointed, but he knows he did good. Scotty stands up from his seat and walks around the set of chairs and sits beside the boy and his father, the look of excitement from the boys face could cause world peace, the innocents just illuminates the boy; Scotty leans in towards the little boy who no looks kind of scared, while continuing to look forward, he whispers in the child's ear. Scotty: I'm Scotty McKnight.... The boys eyes light up and he begins to pull on his dads shirt. Boy: DAD!! DAD!!! HE IS SCOTTY MCKNIGHT!!! The father looks over at Scotty and begins to smile. Father: I think hes your biggest, yet smallest fan! He watches you all the time... Scotty: Well it's good to know that I'm appreciated by even the smallest fans. Scotty cracks a decent smile as he leans over to shake the fathers hand. Father: The name is Tyler, Tyler Holmes... This is my son Chandler... Scotty: Nice to meet you Chandler, and good to meet you Tyler... Virus steps on scene as he sits backwards on the chairs so he is facing Scotty and Chandler. He is wearing his black bandanna, leather jacket and denim jeans. Virus looks kind of pale but well none the less. Virus: Well who do we have here? Chandler draws back as Virus speaks and nudges in closer towards Scotty. Scotty smiles and places his hand on top of Chandlers head. Chandler: That's The Virus.... he scares me! Scotty: Don't worry bud, he's harmless once you get to know him, plus I will kick his butt if he tries anything silly, OK? Chandler sits forward a bit, confident of Scotty's reassuring words. Virus leans in to shake the boys hand who is reluctant to do so, but then does it with a smile; Virus follows by shaking the fathers hand too. Virus turns around and rummages through his duffel bag and pulls out a MWE magazine, on the cover reads... “Up and Coming, Shooting Stars Scotty McKnight and his old tag team rival Virus...” Virus then pulls out a black sharpie and signs the cover of the magazine, Scotty does the same. Handing the autographed book to his fan, they stand up at the same time. Scotty: We gotta go, but here you go, something to remember us by... Oh I nearly forgot... Here's 4 tickets to Monday nights show... tell the ticket chick that Scotty is paying for your entire night, souvenirs, 3 nights in a hotel of your choice as an all expenses paid trip and I will cover your air fare. I will leave your information at the Air Canada Center for pickup, It will be ready for when you're done here. Tyler: T-thank you... that's the kindest thing anyone has ever done for us, God bless you sir! Scotty: No problem, see you at crowning glory. Virus grabs his bag and stumbles a little, still weak from the sedated state he was in. Scotty grabs his bags and helps him out the door. The two MWE superstars get into the black Ford Fusion and drive off down the road. ---30 minutes later--- Inside the car we can see the scenery whiz by as Scotty accelerates down the 401 highway to the Lester B. Pearson Airport. Scotty takes a quick glance over at Virus who is now regaining color to his face. Virus watches traffic as they drive along side them, some passengers don't notice who he is and some nearly cause an accident because they lost their concentration. The evening sun lies not far from the gound, ready to set, the orange light illuminates Scotty and Virus from the chin down. Scotty: So... Virus: So what? Scotty: So... You weren't surprised at all? Virus: About you showing up to pick me up? Kinda... would of guessed Demonica or MWE limo, but if it wasn't them, would of guessed it to be you... Scotty begins to chuckle a little as they drive up to the entrance of the airport. Both MWE superstars exit the car and grab their bags from the trunk and make their way to the check in counter. Putting their luggage on the weighing table Scotty orders the tickets. Scotty: awesome, thanks...Okay V-rod, lets go... Virus: What about your car? Scotty: It's all good, airport rental... Virus: Oh.... Virus and Scotty are seen entering their plane, taking off into the distant sunset. The Next Day We see a big 52” LCD TV, the images so clear that it must be in HD. We see a small segment from Independence Day play on the screen. -------------------------------- ----------------------------------------- -------------------------------- *Scotty and England have now begun to battle on the outside. Trading punches, kicks, and attacks of their own. However, all focus is on Crash and Peter. And Crash takes a major advantage. He pushes the briefcase into the head of Peter, and kicks his ladder down. Peter falls to the mat in the ring, and in pain, looks as Crash climbs to the top. However, perched on the top of ladder, Crash looks at Scotty and England. He looks at them, and then the briefcase. Them...and then the briefcase. Finally, the crowd realizes what he is thinking. They erupt in cheers and he looks to the crowd. With a final pose, he abandons the win, leaps, 15 feet below to the outside of the ring, and takes out himself, England, and McKnight!* Fans: HOLY SHIT! HOLY SHIT! HOLY SHIT! HOLY SHIT! HOLY SHIT! *They all just sit in a heap, exhausted and in pain, as they breath heavily and the crowd goes wild. But then, Peter K is seen, crawling his way to the ropes. He pulls himself up, rope by rope, until he is on his feet. He looks at the ladder, then the carnage, and then the briefcase and climbs. Rung...rung...rung...rung...rung...rung...rung. He looks at the case, then the carnage, the case, carnage, and the fans start cheering like they were before. He chuckles, looks at the crowd, and flips them off. With a slight tug, he rips the case off the hook, and wins the match. The boos are immense...IMMENSE!* Fred Carry: Here are your winners, Scotty McKnight and PETER KAYMACKIAN! -------------------------------- ------------------------------------------ ----------------------------- The segment ends as the MWE logo fades out and starts the next match, but the TV gets turned off before it can continue. Scotty sits in a familiar room, Grey walls, cast iron chandelier, and a black leather sectional couch. Scotty leans forward and places a converter beside him and looks straight into the camera; His eyes deep with determination, anger, and excitement. Scotty: Independence Day was a big night for me Peter; Even though we won our match, you began to hog all the fun for yourself, and even still you think you dominated that whole match on your own. Well let me put a little more light on the subject... You could not have won that match if not for me... You couldn't inflict as much pain as I did, nor could you have the balls to do what I did with that ladder. When Chris England flew into the ladder, you assumed that I tried to delay the match so I could get the win? Listen buddy, you have your head shoved so far up your ass that you could literally give yourself CPR. I told you not to get in my way, then you fuck something up that was so simple that you almost cost our match. Virus: Yo Scotty, mind if I grab a Smirnoff? Scotty: It's 1 in the afternoon dude! Virus pops his head out from around the corner, with a Smirnoff Ice in hand and a smile on his face he steps out into the living room. Virus: It's 5 o'clock somewhere... Scotty rolls his eyes and blatantly waves his hand up at him, Virus cracks a creepy kind of smile. Virus: Thanks... Scotty shakes his head and continues his rant on Peter Kaymeckian, but gets a quick thought. Scotty: I don't think you should be drinking man, especially so soon after coming out of that coma... Virus: I'm fine... Scotty: Whatever man, I'm not driving you back to the hospital. Virus: I think you should be more worried about you match. Scotty Stands up and walks over to his bay window overlooking the backyard. The calm breeze creeps through the windows and causes the outside trees to sway, Scotty begins to ponder different scenarios in his head, some of the match simulations in his head lose, most win... Scotty: Peter.... Virus: Kaymeckian? Scotty: Yeah, Milometer Peter..... Scotty: What about him? Scotty turns from the window, a smile across his face, nearly sadistic. He begins to pace back and forth between the wall and couch, Virus wits on the lay-z-boy couch. Scotty: This man thinks he has the cohonez to beat me for that number one contenders shot? He may have been my partner, but I don't know if he remembers what I did to my last one... I ENDED HIS CAREER! Do you know why he can't remember? Cause he took most of the beating at independence Day, an who did most of the work? ME! Peter, I head what you said about me, and you have all these allegations and supposed facts about me. First, get your facts straight... You name it a fact that I haven't won a damn thing since my career started? Lets Look at my current record, I won my last 2 matches, the match where I put Jekyl out of his misery, Last week when you got your ass handed to you and I did most of the work. I lost 2 matches and lost them solely because of Jekyl, and had one draw... But if we want to get technical I can add my undefeated streak from ICW. Does that ring any bells shit 'fer brains? I don't see how you think you carried us to victory, what did you do? Weasel your way into getting that briefcase...[/color] Virus: You tell 'im bro... Scotty: Please don't interrupt me... Peter, I held England back so you could take out Crash and get that case, but what happened? He took the fucking thing and beat you with it... YOUR PATHETIC, then you weasel you way somehow to the top of that ladder... Don't you dare come up with some bullshit saying that I was too weak to get it, I was buried underneath 2 men, unable to move. Virus: Yeah that would be a pretty weak attempt... I think he said something like that in his promo... Scotty: No, he was talking about earlier in the match when I got hit into the ladder and knocked him off. Well I apologize, while you were trying to gain a few feet off the mat with that ladder, I was doing some actual work and fighting.. Virus: ...And by the looks of his promo, he didn't really have much to say about you. Scotty: Figures, this man doesn't know me, He thinks he is hot shit in a champagne glass when he is really like cold diarrhea in dixie cup. Milometer Peter, I will see you Monday night, and I guarantee you that when you step in the ring with me, I will End you like I ended Jekyl, in cold blood. Virus starts to laugh hysterically at Scotty. Virus: AH HA HA HA COLD DIARRHEA.... DIXIE CUP! HA HA HA HA HA Scotty: It wasn't that funny... I need a beer.... Scotty walks into the kitchen and opens the fridge and grabs a Budweiser. The scene ends as Scotty and Virus give each other a cheers, the screen fades to black. -FIN-
