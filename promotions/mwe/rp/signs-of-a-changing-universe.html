Signs of a changing universe; A Grand Mystique RP

Oct 23 2008

The Grand Mystique

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The scene starts in a bedroom. Noticeably the room is decked out with posters of MWE superstars’ such as Brian Michaels and Alejandro Rodriguez. In the corner, near the bottom of the bed, is a beige PC. It is stood on a pine desk with matching desk lamp, speakers, keyboard and mouse. There is an open window to the right, through which the moon is shining large and bright. Suddenly the monitor which was on standby gently pings to life. The screen fades from black to show a blank page from Google. The cursor ticks in the search box for a few seconds. M...W......E....dot.C.O......M The screen flashes briefly as it searches. It returns to the Goooooooogle results page. The top result is: QUOTE “MWE.com, featuring Originals like Brian Michaels and David Whitman and hot new stars like Project Revolution: come to the MWE for the latest news, info and show previews and reviews from the hottest company in pro wrestling.” A mouse cursor moves around then clicks on this items. Again the screen flashes with activity. A small progress bar appears at the bottom of the page and fills up steadily. The MWE.com home page lights up the screen, full of items. There are links to the right, such as MWESHop.com, MWE on pay-per-view and MWE TV. On the left are more links, which lead to MWE Superstar Profiles, each with a link for every member of the roster. In the middle are news items. The latest is: QUOTE “DC issues volatile response before the First Blood battle at WrestleWars II”. The picture that accompanies it is of the double Ace of Spades superkick that ended the match at Turmoil. After a few seconds, the mouse cursor moves up to the main toolbar. It hovers over the Refresh icon and clicks it. The same page returns but with the Michaels’/ DC story and picture moved down the page. Now the top news item is “The Grand Mystique promises Project Revolution will lose their titles to the Big Bang”.The mouse moves to the hyperlink in the title. As the page refreshes, the whole page is taken up by GM’s mask. The mask hovers on screen. Off screen, there is a click and the gentle sound of radio interference. The camera moves to the right. The speakers are now on. There is a knob labelled “Power/Volume”, which turns again and increases the volume slowly. The camera moves back to the screen. We can now see the Grand Mystique in a large window on the screen himself and he speaks: GM: “So this is the MWE ; where the big boys play, the determined succeed, the best will prevail and soon where a new pair of Tag Team Champions will be crowned.” The camera backs away so slowly we see more of GM’s body. GM: “WrestleWars will be memorable. Not for Sonny Williams or D fucking C. It will be renown in history for being the start of Grand Mystique's reign over the MWE." As we see more of GM, we can tell he is standing. The background is black but GM is seen clearly as he is standing underneath a light. GM:“But I am not alone........no, for this special company I have a very special partner, the King of the Outback, Maxx. He led the Nation of Islam in Enigmar Wrestling before becoming the King of the Universe in the APWC. Finally he won the AEW World Heavyweight title, a belt he never lost.” As GM lists some of Maxx’s achievements, we see pictures of them playing faintly in the background. GM:“Together, we are two men that you really don’t want to see working together but that is exactly what we are doing. Everything that we have achieved as singles competitors is about to be taken into the tag team arena. We have already made an immediate impact. At WrestleWars, we take advantage and by the end of the night, you know that you are looking at the new tag team champions.” GM can be seen in full profile now. He is wearing a new style of mask which is still royal blue as well as a new outfit that shows him streamline, tanned and more muscular. He holds a crystal ball in his midriff which sparkles as it catches the light. GM:"So the champs are Project Revolution. I have been here less than a week and already I can’t stand you. You two are one of those teams who we hear boasting about how great they are, how they are the ones to change the industry and destroy those before them. But know that your future is pale because you have not learned to respect your superiors. In becoming the first MWE tag champions, you have overcome everyone before you. You beat the Bowens, a circus freak act to get the belts. You even beat Rated fucking X a few weeks ago. But you two need to know one thing; as good as the past few weeks have been to Project Revolution, I don’t see anything special in the future of the American Animal and Jason Fear!” The crystal balls flashes slightly. A strange small light flashes insides it and begins to dance around inside it. GM:“And to the Hollywood Hooligans, a nippy pair and the number one contenders, they need to know that they are pipsqueaks in a world of heavyweights. They need to stand up and be counted! No matter who stands before us in the tag team division, whether it is the Ortans, the Bowens or Project Revolution, you have never seen anything like Maxx and the Grand Mystique!” The light in the crystal ball slowly becomes more powerful. GM begins to pass it between his right and left hands. GM: “We will show the world that we are the team for this company to build a division around. We intend on becoming World Tag Team Champions because it is simply our destiny to do so.” “The Hand of Prophecy is key to the future of the MWE. I can determine that your future is in my control but the outcome is in the stars, the brightest of which is one half of the future MWE World Tag Team Champions, The Grand Mystique.” GM throws the crystal ball at the screen. The camera pulls away from the screen and the picture fades quickly to a dot. The screen has a large crack down the middle. The camera swings away to show the room is now covered with posters of Maxx and the Grand Mystique. A blind now covers the window. GM: “Wait! I’m not done!” His voice can be heard but not seen. The camera steps over the window, next to the computer. The blind slams up. GM sits outside the window on a thick tree branch. He holds another crystal ball. GM:“The Big Bang is coming and it is coming to take the Hollywood Hooligans and Project Revolution out!”
