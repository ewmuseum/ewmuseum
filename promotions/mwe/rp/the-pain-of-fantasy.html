The pain of fantasy; Sinc RP

Aug 29 2008

Sinc Mercier

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The camera clicks on the scene as Sinc is seen standing in front of a star wars convention in Sacramento, California. He is wearing a shirt that says, “I heart Nerds” and some black workout pants. There are lines of people inside as Sinc walks in the camera follows him. A roar of voices are heard and seen are a large variety of people dressed as their favorite star wars characters. Sinc has a large grin on his face as the camera continues following him steadily. Sinc stops and looks into the camera as the camera stops as well. He pulls out an MWE mic and speaks into it. Sinc: Well hello there guys and gals and welcome to this excellent convention held in Sacramento California. A haven for little brown puff balls and purple sword wielding weirdo’s. The camera pans across all the people at the convention, some posing for the camera. The camera fades back to Sinc. Sinc is seen looking behind him and points at a Mexican dressed up as Mace Windu. Sinc: Hey I think I see Alejandro Rodriguez. Lets go talk to the little rebel shall we? Sinc walks over to the Mace Windu look alike and puts his arm around his shoulder. Sinc: So tell me…does it feel good to dress up like something you will never be? Mace Windu Kid: And what would I never be? Sinc: Black…. Mace Windu: I find that racist and immature... Sinc: I find you stubborn and annoying... The camera cuts to Sinc drinking punch from a Bobba Fett bowl and looking at a picture cutout of 'STAR WARS: BABES UNLEASHED' Sinc looks at the camera in surprise as he pauses shocked he is being recorded. Sinc: DAMMIT! The camera cuts and goes to Sinc talking to a very husky Darth Maul look a like. Sinc: So who are you really? Show us your true identity…. Darth Maul Man: Sorry that is truly confidential my good sir. Sinc: Are you hiding something? Darth Maul Man: I don’t know what your talking about… Sinc: Come on don’t hide I won’t tell anybody… Darth Maul Man: I have nothing to hide. I am what I am. I am Darth Maul. Sinc: No you’re a coward and a fat man who is denying the truth. Darth Maul Man: WHAT I SAY IS THE TRUTH AND I HAVE A RIGHT TO PRIVACY! THIS INTERVIEW IS OVER! The man storms off slowly. Sinc: I thought Kanto would be cooperative…he's kinda like a tow truck. Seriously, I am halfway through a sentence and he is still barely walking a few steps behind me. Sinc said shrugging as the camera cuts again and fades into Sinc standing next to a person in a Chewbacca suit as he stares at him for a few moments. Sinc: Hey I had no idea Devlin was jumping around in here.... Chewbacca Impersonator:*wookie roar* Sinc: I feel your pain....it must be hot under all that hair....thats why I choose...NIVEA FOR MEN, the only working Hair control system. The camera zooms on Sinc as he gives a thumbs up and winks fixing his hair with his other hand. Chewbacca walks away. The camera cuts to Sinc following around a short person in an ewok costume. Sinc: Hey can I get a word please? The ewok begins power walking away from Sinc. Sinc: Hey come on! come on man! The ewok begins running away. Sinc: COME BACK HERE YOU LITTLE SHIT! THIS ISN’T COPS! The camera fades to Sinc in another scene as Sinc is doing a sit down interview with a Storm Trooper and a man dressed as Darth Vader. Sinc: So tell me….what do you think my chances are in my Alejandro and Kanto match? Darth Vader: The force is strong within you…. Storm Trooper: Niner Niner. Sinc: Ok good. Good…I like the response…Now what do you know of my opponents? Darth Vader: They exceed the limits of the sith…. Storm Trooper: They disobey the empire. Sinc looks at the light saber handle on Vader’s hilt. Sinc: WOAH MAN! Can I use that thing? Darth Vader: Your request is forbidden… Sinc: Give it to me… Darth Vader: No… Sinc: Come On bro Im not gonna steal it….I just wanna see…. Darth Vader: No… At this point the storm trooper walks away. Sinc: I swear to god...I will give you money if you let me hold that nerd antique. Darth Vader gets up ready to leave. Sinc kicks his chair and gets him in a sleeper hold. Sinc: You...never...say no...to Sinc Mercier....take it in man…take it in…don’t fight it…don’t fight the pain. A sigh is heard from behind the camera as it fades to Sinc standing, scratching his head, confused on where to go now. He then spots a star trek fan out of the star wars haven. He walks up to him. Sinc: Hey man, what are you doing here? No offense but you don’t quite fit the scene of things. Trekky: I am simply proving to people that star trek costumes are way cooler. The man is dressed as spac. He is incredibly old and his skin is flabby. Sinc: Have you seen a luchador around here by any chance? Trekky: No I have not. Sinc: What about- Sinc looks to the left of him and sees the ewok again. The ewok notices him and begins running. Sinc: DAMMIT! Not this time little bastard! The camera shakes as Sinc runs off chasing him. The camera does one final fade showing the ewok suddenly in a headlock. Sinc looks at the camera. Sinc: Well I have learned many things and seen many things here today. Good and bad. For example, short furry savages like this person here are tough to chase down. The ewok snickers and grumbles. Sinc: AND! That Alejandro and Kanto better know who their up against. Me being here proves I am willing to do anything at anytime. Sinc signals the ewok to run along. The ewok walks off the scene. Sinc: You see, When I have a passion or a want. I got for it, I strive to climb far past the heavens and into a higher kingdom where I rule. And where hell is so far below me it fears me. I am not afraid to face the best or be the best. It is something I just have to do. I’m up against a man who thirsts for blood and looks as if he is from a movie. He is a child. He is nothing. He hides his background like a coward and does not show the true side of him to the fans because he is afraid of what he really is. A spineless, GUTless, coward. Sinc stands up making a face of discontent. Sinc: And Alejandro Rodriguez is just the same. Hiding behind a mask of shame. I feel for the deaths of his loved ones, but when my father died I learned to move on. I stand PROUD of myself today than I ever was to honor him. Not to hide behind something that would shame him. In the beginning I stood broken, but now…now I stand proud of who I am and what I have become! I will not- Sinc hears a rustling near him, he looks to his sides and sees the ewok going through his wallet. It looks at Sinc and runs off. Sinc: CHRIST IN HARMONY! That’s it…THAT IS IT! I'M GONNA SKIN YOU! The camera zooms out capturing the ewok taking off. Sinc takes one of his shoes and throws it at the ewok’s head. It falls to the ground. The camera zooms back in on Sinc. Sinc: As I was saying… Suddenly a crowd of people in costumes circle Sinc after seeing the chaos he caused while at the convention. They all look at him in anger and their arms are crossed. Sinc looks around him. Sinc: Hey people…uhm….sorry…I…I uh….I gotta go… Sinc darts through the crowd and rushes out of the convention door as the camera follows and the crowd is seen chasing him down the street. Sinc: You guys are freaks man! FREAKS! Sinc says this while still running as the camera follows him as best it can with the crowd still on his tail down the road. The scene cuts off in the middle of the chase, the screen and sounds blur out.
