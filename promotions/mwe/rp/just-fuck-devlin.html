Just Fuck Devlin!!!!; Jason Fear RP

Oct 12 2008

Jason Fear

The scene opens up as Jason takes a sip of his Budweiser. He places it back on the cup holder of his couch. He picks up his control and surfs through the channel looking for something interesting to watch. He was wearing the new Project Revolution t-shirt with light blue jeans and black boots. He stopped surfing when sports center appeared on the screen. He looked at the breaking News and then the highlights came up. "Have you seen the super bowl champs play these last few weeks? This will explain it all. The New York Giants win another game. They had the offense the dirty work by scoring 44 points in the game while the defense kept it to 6 points for the Seahawks. They are just storming through the season like champions would." Jason Fear: Wow that's an under statement. Like me and Callaway would say. We have been champions for the 4 weeks and we are not planning on removing it from our waist. Let that be a message sent to all the ears listening to this statement including you Ryan. All these new comers such as Darius and Boyd are all promoters not wrestlers. They are here to make the tag team division look good. To make Project Revolution look good. Jason Fear smiles and takes a drink of his beer. He gets up from the couch and walks with his beer on hand up the stairs and stops in front of a door. it was made of wood and it hade a sign that read "Do Not Enter" in big letters. He swiped out bundle of keys. He picked out the key to unlock the door. It wasn't a big room but it had many different pictures and memorabilia. Jason Fear: This room is what I call "The Jason Fear museum". This is where I keep my memorabilia from my greatest past times. See right here this picture was the picture a camera man had taken of me right after I had signed a contract for two year in the MWE. This other picture was when I was training kickboxing with my trainer Mr. Neigam. This picture was me sitting next to D.C. and Lindsey will watch Crowing Glory II. This is my Wrestler of the Week™ Trophy which I shared with my boys from PR. Jason Fear walks up to the corner of the room and grabs an object extremely shiny with a spark Jason Fear's body was covering it so it was hard to identify Jason Fear: And this is what all you posers are trying to capture from me. It's just sad how you suckers try so hard to win but just fall like rain drops...quickly. As for me and Callaway we're doing our job. I and Callaway are equal to the Project Revolution faction unlike you Ryan. You are the man behind Devlin Sharpe the Explosive Fantastic. Ryan is referred as the Mr. Dirty Work. While Devlin has the time of his life with his British strippers at home you do what he tells you to do so he's the one who looks better than you. Ryan, step up because your brother is trying to take you down. He can't fail and see you pass, He can't strikeout and sees you hit a homerun, he can't see his life fucked up and see you making money, and He can't see you as the better man. He places his tag team title back on the counter were he picked it up. Jason Fear: The first time I actually noticed your issue was when your own twin brother was pushing you around after the triple threat tag team match at Turmoil. You should know by now that you’re best way out. The route to go is to become a one man show. You’re even going to enjoy your losses like this loss this upcoming Monday. If you disagree with this advice then you can suck my d***. I will enter that ring more determine than ever. I’m going to attend Mayhem to destroy you. And as for your brother Devlin, he should be much more afraid than you. He hasn’t met the real Fear. If my last name were to be Sharpe I wouldn’t think twice to not attend Mayhem. It’s just a matter of logic. Fear laughs as he picks up a picture and kisses it. Then he reveals it to the camera showing Hailey Williams then he rips it.
