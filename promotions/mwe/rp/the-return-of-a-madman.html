The return of a madman; Vs Eric Derf & Madison Seton

Sep 24 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The camera turns on and we see 'The Great' Milenko standing in the shadows of the basement of the Ford Center in Oklahoma City . All you can see are his eyes as he stares at the camera before talking. 'The Great' Milenko: Mayhem Wrestling Entertainment, I honestly never thought I would be here after I left over a year ago. That being said I am back and I plan on picking up where I left off. Milenko gets a sick smile on his face as he thinks of his upcoming match. 'The Great' Milenko: Which brings us to one of my opponents, Eric Derf. Eric my last match in MWE you took the Havok title from me and your career skyrocketed while mine stagnated. If you hadn't beaten me that night I'm positive it would have been me that skyrocketed and not you. Management in their infinite wisdom decided to give us each a partner because they knew what would happen if they put us in a 1-on-1 match again. Milenko gets a look of fire in his eyes as the smile on his face gets even more twisted. 'The Great' Milenko: Speaking of Partners lets look at them a little bit. You're partner is a woman. Not only is she a woman she's only been wrestling for a couple months. She has absolutely no idea what I am capable of, what I am willing to do to my opponents to win a match. The smile on Milenko's face gets even bigger as he thinks about who his partner is. 'The Great' Milenko: And on the other side you have my partner Oblivion, a man who has faced you before and quite possibly hates you as much as I do. Either one of us is capable of taking you out Derf and when you put us together and give you a green, untried partner that just makes it easier for us. Milenko turns and starts to walk away but as the camera fades to black he says one final thing. 'The Great' Milenko: You and your partner better get ready Eric because the 2 of us are coming with everything we've got.
