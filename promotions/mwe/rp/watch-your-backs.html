Watch your backs...

Aug 17 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Devlin Sharpe and his girlfriend are standing on the golden gate bridge taking the time off their busy schedule to give their thoughts on everything that had happened during the past week devlin and hayley did not look happy, devlin was visibly shaking and hayley was doing everything in her power other than to physically fuck him in the middle of the bridge to calm him down but it wasnt working properly but it was obvious that it was working to some degree as devlin was on the verge of a meltdown due to what happened last week Devlin Sharpe: 3 simple words describe my feelings right about now...WHAT...THE...FUCK Devlin outstretched his arms Devlin Sharpe: What kinda crazy ass mickey mouse run operation is this?! first of all they promised hayley here Devlin kissed hayley on the lips then turned back towards the camera Devlin Sharpe: ...that she would get a spot on the payperview with her group, i spent my own money getting their group ready for their payperview debut and guess what? they started the match without them...strike 1 Devlin Sharpe raise his index finger to signify his point Devlin Sharpe: Brian also promised me that i would win the match...he said he would sort it out for me...but what happens? a big dumb ape named browny silliams or something just tosses me out like im nothing and whats worse? my so called rated x team mate Brian Michaels doesnt even attempt to help me...only AFTER i get eliminated he attacks donny rilliams and gets his ass eliminated too...strength in numbers you idiot...Strike 2 Devlin Sharpe raises his middle finger to signify his point, Devlin Sharpe: Brian then said I would be inserted in to the runners up United states title tournament? Strike 3 and youre out Devlin Sharpe raises he forefinger to signify his point and points backwards with his thumb Devlin Sharpe: and guess what he sticks me in some tag match like a has-been or a newbie...AM I EITHER OF THOSE?! The last words he screamed at the camera Devlin Sharpe: as far as I understand...MWE has become twisted...corrupted...crippled in to its current state where MWE legends such as myself get ignored and pushed in to some meaningless matches with title shots few and far between MWE has become about the new breed of wrestler...random kids that just came out wrestling school and not had any blood sweat or tears to their fucking name... Devlin stared at the camera his face alone filling the whole area with a bleak coldness Devlin Sharpe: Well from now on everythings gonna change...wipe the slate clean motherfuckers because the explosive fantastic will take the crap thrown at him...let it build up then fire it back at everyone like a motherfucking bazooka...I am going to win my tag match...but not go for the big gold belt... yet...I am slowly going to build up my gold collection starting small...then moving up up and up...I will become the first to hold all of the titles simultaneously...now MWE has some crazy ass rule about titles not being held by the same person...but I believe that is just a barrier and I aim to overcome that barrier... Devlin smirked Devlin Sharpe: I hear that there may be some tag titles coming to MWE so after I win this tag match and prove to everyone of you disghusting sceptics that I can work in a team to achieve my goal...however Devlin extends his arms in a gesture of welcome Devlin Sharpe: Gentlemen an open invitation to you...I am looking for a tag partner for a run at the tag team titles...if interested please do not hesitate to contact me... Devlin Sharpe suddenly turned seriously Devlin Sharpe: a hearty congratulations to our new champions David suckadick whitman and Aeron MMORPG reject Beowulf and be careful Aaron Aeron Eggyhart Apple...however you say your name I am snapping at your heels...remember that...hell All champions remember this...just when youve beaten everyone...I will appear...at your weakest possible moment I will come and rip your title away from your broken bodies...
