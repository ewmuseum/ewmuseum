No clever Title today

Sep 11 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Have you ever had the feeling that no matter how hard you try, or no matter what you do, that it will never be enough to get you over that one last hurdle? If you have, then welcome to the last two years of my life. No matter what I did, no matter how hard I tried, I was always in his shadow. Even when he came to MWE, I was shunned aside to make room for him. But not anymore. Last week, in front of the entire MWE universe, I finally did it. I exorcised my demons, I overcame my hurdles, I proved ALL the doubters wrong. At the biggest Pay-Per-View in the short history of Mayhem Wrestling Entertainment, I came back from what most people would have considered a potentially career ending injury, and I pinned Kevin Hardaway. Did I honestly think that I was going to be able to pull it off? Fuck yes. I knew that I would win, because I wanted it more. He might have needed it, but I WANTED it. Like I said back then, I wasn’t just fighting for myself like he was. Enough about the past though, it’s done and over with. I’ll be the first to admit, I have no idea how the hell I finished that match. When I hit the Life to Lifeless off of that trailer, I thought I was never going to walk again. My spine was on fire, and I couldn’t feel my legs. I knew Kevin had to be just as bad off as me, and that was what pushed me to get up. I remember pulling him out of the ambulance, and then it’s all fuzzy. I vaguely remember kicking his head in, and my hand being raised and then darkness. All of that led to where we are now. Hear that beeping in the background? See all those tubes and cords hooked up to a bunch of machines? Well I couldn’t tell you what any of em do, but I do know that they are what’s keeping me in this hellhole. Rosemont didn’t have a good enough hospital to treat us, so we were both airlifted out. I went to St. Louis University Hospital, and I could give a fuck less where he went.. Yeah, we could have gone to Chicago but fuck that place. Plus, the next Monday Night Mayhem was there, so I had to be ready ASAP. For the first time in my career, I honestly though I might be done. My mind was willing, but my body was weak. I half expected Management to give me a show off, considering what I did for them. We brought in the highest ratings, we both nearly died, but as soon as the card was released guess what the one huge glaring issue was. Not only is Kevin Hardaway right back at the top of the card, in the main fucking event, I’m regulated to a Tournament opener. To top it all off, I don’t draw a guy like Blight or Dev Renshaw. Hell, I don’t even get that French sumbitch or the hottest chic in MWE. Instead, two weeks after taking out a brother of mine, they throw another one at me. Now isn’t the time or place to discuss that though, because I have no blunt nor a camera crew. Plus, being laid up in a hospital makes it kinda hard to shoot a decent promo. At least the doctors were nice enough to get me high as fuck off of some morphine, although it was starting to wear off. A nap would have done just as well, but as soon as I was about to close my eyes, a nice loud voice hit my ears. Lacey and Lilly: UNKY ZAK!!! Two high pitched screams, that were actually somewhat soothing to my ears. The two girls were Brad Kane’s oldest daughters, which meant that he couldn’t be far behind. I was right, and as they busted through the doors he was standing there with a grin on his face. They jumped up onto the bed, and it was nice to be able to give the girls a hug. They were the closest thing I had to family, ever since the incident with my son. Brad: Well well well, isn’t this a familiar sight? How many times have you ended up in a hospital bed because you are too stubborn for your own good? Zak: Uh, I think this makes about 16 or 17, I lost count. Brad: So what’s the damage report? Zak: Not as bad as it seemed…concussion, couple cracked ribs, bruised tailbone, and roughly 19 stitches…hows Kev? I know you had to have gone to see him. Brad: He is a bit sore about the loss, but he will live to fight another day. That brought a smile to my face. This meant that sometime down the road, whether it be 3 months from now, or 3 years, there would be a third match between Kevin and myself. Laying down was starting to get a bit painful, so I reached over and adjusted the bed so I was a bit more upright. The girls couldn’t help but to giggle at the moving bed, and the very sound of their laughter kept a smile on my face. It reminded me of my own childhood, when things were so much easier. They were so nieve, and so full of innocence that I truly envied them. I wish I could go back to those days, where the only thing that mattered was cartoons. I wanted to go back to the time where family was first, and you had no worries. Sadly, I knew I could never go back to that and I had accepted it. Zak: Hey Brad, can you go out in the hall and get me a wheelchair? I wanna go get some air but they don’t let me walk around yet. Brad: Yeah, I can do that I suppose…come on girls, lets go find Unky Zak some wheels. As the three of them left, I grabbed my phone and shot a quick text message to a local buddy of mine. I needed to get the hell outta here, and I was going to do it under my terms. By the time Brad and the girls got back, I had already tucked my phone away. It was going to take about 20 minutes for my ride to get here, so I had a bit of time to kill. I had nothing of my own in this hospital anyways, except the bloodstained clothes I showed up with. My personal belongings were in a waiting apartment, not too far from the hospital. Ever since my GWC days, I had purchased a couple apartments in various cities and St. Louis happened to be one of them. I had Brad help me into the chair, and sat the girls on my lap. Lacey: Come on daddy, push us fast! Brad: You have got to be kidding me… Zak: You heard the kid, giddy up! Had I not had his daughters on my lap, I’m pretty sure that Brad would have shot me into a wall with the force of a Mike Tyson right hook. Thankfully I was spared, and we made the quick walk out to the front of the hospital. The girls were smiling and waving at everyone, and all Brad could do was shake his head. We finally got outside, and he sat on a bench next to me. Brad: So this little Tournament that MWE set up…how’s it feel to know you will be coming in 2nd once again? Zak: How can I come in second when my only competition is going against me in the first round? Brad: You got jokes don’t you? Remember last time we had a tournament for a title, I came out on top. Zak: Yeah, politicking can do that for a career, can’t it. I had to duck out of the way as Brad’s open palm went whizzing by my head. Before he could get a second chance, his phone rang and he groaned. He looked at the text, and shook his head. Brad: That’s the wife…apparently she wants to go to dinner so I gotta get the girls home. Zak: I understand bro, it’s gotta be hard to resist when she pulls on the leash like this. Brad: You know man, if my daughters weren’t around, I’d slap the living shit out you right now. It was true, I was pretty lucky that I hadn’t gotten hit yet. I wondered what I was going to do to kill the rest of the time, but then a brilliant idea came to mind. Zak: Hey Brad, you still carry around that little FLIP camera that you bought in Denver last year? Brad: Yeah, I always keep it on me incase one of the girls does something recordable. Zak: They take after their mom and dad then? Yeah, I knew that would get me hit but it was so worth it. For those few who don’t know, Brad and Megan do porn. Graphic, disgusting porn. Sure enough, my arm was numb and Brad was shaking his hand. Brad: That was a good one, but I still owe you…but yeah, I suppose you wanna borrow it? Zak: Yeah, I figure I might as well shoot a promo since I got nothing better to do. Brad reached into his pocket and grabbed the little camera. He tossed it to me and told the kids to wave goodbye. They did, and both of them ran over and hugged me goodbye. They drove off, and I took a moment to gather my thoughts. I wasn’t used to such an uncertain feeling, but there was a first time for everything. I propped the camera up on the bench, and leaned to the side to get a bit more relaxed. First and foremost, let me say that after what happened at Crowning Glory, I never expected to wrestle again. I went into that match fully aware that it might be my last one, and I was surprisingly ok with that knowledge. I had accepted the fact that both Kevin and I wanted to kill each other, and if we tried hard enough we would. Thankfully it never got that far, and I was able to come out victorious. I did it for Joe, I did it for Brad, bust most of all I did it for myself. One would think that after a win like that, at the biggest event of the year, that I might get some sort of reward. Hell, I would have been satisfied with a week off, but that didn’t happen. Instead, I have to help open the show, while the man I damn near killed is headlining. Tell me, where is the fucking logic in that? How does that make ANY fucking sense at all? I have sat here in this bed for days, on every painkiller known to man, and it STILL makes no sense. Even after the biggest win of my fucking career, I STILL GET NO RESPECT. When Eli Silver first spoke about this tournament, I had no interest in it. As I have said time and time again, titles don’t mean as much to me as they used to. While it would be nice to have another belt around my waist, this isn’t how I go about getting it. Fuck a tournament, where someone like Blight or David Renshaw has even the slimmest of possibilities. What is the purpose of devaluing the title to this depth? What if by some miracle, CSB, myself, Brad Kane, and Jesus Cruz all die in a horrible plane crash? Then someone like Renshaw or Sebastian St. Fucker would be the new #1 contender. To let people like that even ATTEMPT to win the title is a fucking joke, and it reeks of horrible booking. Speaking of horrible booking, Not only did I get shoved into a tournament that I wanted absolutely nothing to do with in the first place, but they put me against CSB, my best friend, bar buddy, and recent father. Congrats on the kid Blaise, I k now what it’s like tryin to raise a kid when you are in this industry, and I wish you the best. But that isn’t the point. This isn’t about kids, or anything else. This is strictly about me versus him, in a match that I will admit I am in no shape or condition for. After the war I waged, the battle I went through, if anyone actually thinks that I am ready for a match of this caliber, you must have run into DeAngelo Williams and bought some chronic. Now I know I said I didn’t want to be in this tourney, but that doesn’t mean I want to lose either. If I’m in it, I am in it to win it. I would never give anything less than 100%, even if it means I have to push my body past what it is capable of. Normally I would be confident in this match, but right now I honestly don’t know if I can perform to the best of my abilities. I feel horrible for the fact that I don’t think I will be giving Blaise my best, and he deserves it. I will give him everything I can though, and that is a promise. I had to take a moment to catch my breath, as a sharp pain shot up into my skull. Concussions were a bitch, and I just hoped it would pass before Mayhem. Blaise, we have been working together for over a year, and we have never stood across from each other. We have rode together, we have gotten arrested together, and we have kicked ass together…but never have we fought. I gotta say though, I’m a bit worried. Not because I think I can’t win, but because the last time I stepped into the ring with someone I called Brother, it ended up leading to the most stressful and career threatening part of my life. I can’t afford to go through that again, and I really hope it doesn’t have to go that way. I look at you, and I see a lot of familiar things. I’ve been in your shoes man, and I know it’s tough to handle. People see you as a mini-me, and you want to be your own person. The thing is, I never thought of it like that. You have ALWAYS been CSB, “The Executioner”, the best damn GWC Ultimate Champion. We both love what we do, we both love smoking weed and getting drunk, and we both love to fuck fine bitches. That’s where the similarities end though, and it isn’t a bad thing. You deserve to stand on your own, to be recognized as a star in your own right. Unfortunately for you, in order to do that you have to get past me. You have to prove to people that you aren’t in my shadow, even though you have never been. It must suck, having lies like that fed to you every time you look around. I’m not here to stroke your ego though, I’m not going to hold your hand and tell you that you re special. You want to be the man, you have to beat the man. I’m not in this business to make anyone famous, and that includes you. You are a former GWC World Champion, but your reign was a joke and you know it. Being proud of that is like David Arquette being proud of his WCW title reign. You won a belt in a company that was dead and meant nothing anymore. Had you been in the company 6 months earlier, things might have been different. But for now, you are just known as the guy who held the belt of a dying company. Fuck man, even here in MWE you haven’t done what you needed to do. That MITB briefcase was YOURS, and you couldn’t do it. Every time you have something big within your grasp, you fall just a foot too short. The old saying goes “Insanity is doing the same thing over and over, expecting a different result”. So tell me, does that make you insane? Are you going to build yourself up, just to fall short once again? Or are you going to break the cycle for once. Can you be that man that defies the odds, or will you fall neatly into line like all the rest? Trust me man, I’ve heard what people say, how we are only getting these shots because of our past. Well fuck that, I never have nor will I ever want anything handed to me and I know you feel the same. Before I can say anything else, the wonderful sounds of screeching tires and shitty rap music hit my ears and I know that means only one thing. My ride has arrived, late as always. I flip the camera around to show a nice little Lambo with the one and only Ebony behind the wheel. I get to my feet, the pain obvious in my eyes. As you can see, I got shit to do, weed to smoke, and a Mexican to train. Blaise, I really do wish you the best of luck, and if you so happen to beat me the first round of drinks is on Wayne. If I win, drinks are still on Wayne. Good luck Brother, I will see you in the ring. With that I turned the camera off, and hopped in the car. For another night, even if only for one, Ebony and Ivory was going to ride again.
