a Flight to Remember; RP 1

Sep 24 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It is a late September day, the sun begins to hide behind the clouds as a Boeing 737 jet with the MWE logo pierces the sky, destined to land in the Yeager airstrip in Charleston West Virginia. Inside the jet sits a man we have all come to know and hate, the standard purple suit with green and white vest underlay, the white smudged face paint, smeared red mouth paint, the grunge black around the eyes, and to top it all off the crooked smile held up by scars of the past...This man is Jekyl... The jet has been on flight for about three hours when Jekyl got the idea of filming his next promo for his newly appointed opponent. Jekyl: *ahem* I would personally like to thank Mr. Covell for letting me use the MWE personal jet... Jekyl looks around in satisfaction and smiles valiantly and raises his arms. Jekyl: Because of my most recent win against Sinc Mercier, Mr. Covell has taken a shining to me. He has always liked my work, not because I would win or lose matches, but because I brought ratings, I brought fans, not to mention I brought pain and my own self satisfaction. During the show I found out that I get the prestigious opportunity to face of against the EX-WORLD Champion David Whitman for his chance to get his title back, but he won’t if I have anything to say about it... Jekyl reclines in his seat and sets his feet up on a leather stool that sits in front of him. around him the floor of the jet is black, a few windows line the jet walls, pictures of MWE superstars scattered around the plane, attendants stand in the corridor to the kitchen watching Jekyl with fear, confusion, and curiosity. Jekyl turns his head and sees the female attendants watching on, he signals for one of them to come over. The attendant closest to the front slowly walks up to Jekyl; she is wearing a high cut black skirt, white blouse, black high heel shoes, strawberry blond hair, and a white ascot. Jekyl: Come, there's no reason to be afraid... The attendant creeps closer to Jekyl as his scars become more visible, she stops in fear, knowing what he is capable of. Jekyl: I said come here! Once again the attendant creeps closer, keeping an eye on the deranged man. Jekyl sits up and he begins to look up and down the attendant, a smile cracks from the side of his mouth as he sees the cleavage this specific attendant has, which in turn makes the woman gain some confidence as she bends down and uses her hand to lift up Jekyl’s head so they are now staring into each others eyes. Attendant: What can I do for you Sir? Jekyl smacks his lips and runs his hands through his hair as the attendant gets a disgusted look on her face. Jekyl: Well I would like to know when we are landing, secondly I would like you to follow me for a moment. Jekyl stands up grabbing onto the female’s arms rushing her over to a picture of Whitman on the wall, black frame, dark background, and gold name plate saying “David Whitman”. Jekyl: Do you know who this man is miss..... Woman: Samantha.. .my name is Samantha.... and that is David Whitman... Jekyl: Well Samantha, what do you think of this man? Samantha looks at the picture, searching for the right answer when Jekyl interrupts her thought process. Jekyl: WHAT do you think of this man? Samantha: Well to be honest I don’t watch the show, I just work for Covell, but he has been on this jet a few times. Jekyl: I see, how did he treat you? How did he act? What DO you know about him? Samantha: He treated me fine, never really said much, mainly slept or asked for drinks, but that was it. I know he was a world champion or something like that. Jekyl: You silly girl...David Whitman was a World Champion for almost 5 weeks straight... He maintained gold for quite some time... Samantha: What’s your point? Jekyl: Well He lost his title a little over a week ago to a man by the name of Sonny Williams... Samantha: I remember him, all he did while he flew on this plane was sit in a dark corner and talk to himself, occasionally cry about his brother, all the other girls and I didn’t like him much. Jekyl: I wouldn’t expect anything more from him... Jekyl pulls out a few markers and begins to draw on Whitman’s face to imitate his own and writes his trademark saying “Why so Serious” on the top left corner. Samantha: Hey you can’t do that! That’s MWE Property! Jekyl: Listen sweetheart, why don’t you waltz your pretty heart shaped ass back into that kitchen and make me a sammich... The attendant smacks Jekyl across the face and storms off to the back of the plane where the other girls begin to ask her what happened, as Jekyl looks back with a smile they close the curtain. Jekyl removes his jacket, sits in his chair and begins to clap. Jekyl: Whitman, I applaud you, you retained that title for quite some time, but I have done some research and some math, and you see after watching all you promo’s I have come to realize that you are nothing more than an actor... And my first clue was that show of yours that you film in your cousin’s bar...what was it again? Ah yes, the “Top Rope Tavern”... sounds like a gay bar to me.... all I ever see in that bar on your show is men with cowboy hats, chaps and.... I’m not even going to continue that sentence.. Jekyl sits back once again with his feet up and his hands behind his head, eyes closed, he remembers all the David Whitman promo’s he’s seen. Jekyl: My point being is that you are an actor, not a fighter, you act like you can fight which is a good thing on your part, but you see, you can act like a world champ, you can act like you care.... You can even act like you’re the greatest thing to walk this earth....but in the end, you are still an actor, and it will be me who ends that career of yours. Jekyl calls back to the attendants once again, head leaned back, arm raised with three fingers pointed and motioning forward. Jekyl: Get me three fingers of Scotch please? Also could you kindly tell me when were landing? Samantha: GO FUCK YOURSELF! Jekyl: Oh, and that reminds me, I’m still waiting for my sandwich! Chop Chop mademoiselle... He chuckles as he continues what he was saying as he puts his hands back behind his head. Jekyl: As I was saying... When you and I step into the ring, mono-e-mono, your going to step into the ring with a man who will see through the act, and come out on top...So to speak... Samantha storms up behind Jekyl and stops beside him. She pours the Scotch onto his pants and splats the sandwich into his face. Samantha: Here asshole, enjoy your drink and sandwich... we will be landing in 5 minutes! Samantha storms off as Jekyl removes the sandwich from his face, he begins to laugh. Jekyl: HeheHAHAHAHAHAHA. She’s got some fight in her.... I like that! Anyways, we are landing now so I must shut the camera off, see you shortly.... The camera shuts off and Jekyl sits up as a voice comes over the intercom. Intercom: Please be seated and fasten your seat belts, we are now landing at the Yeager airport in Charleston, West Virginia, Thank you for flying in the MWE company jet. Jekyl: At least the pilots nice.... After a smooth landing Jekyl exits the MWE jet and is met by a Lincoln Navigator limousine, the scene fades to the limo’s tail lights fading off in the sunset. -TO BE CONTINUED-
