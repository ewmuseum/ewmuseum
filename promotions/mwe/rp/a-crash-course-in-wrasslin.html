A Crash Course in Wrasslin'

Nov 1 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Just to clarify, this is an Alejandro RP. __________________________________ The scene opens in a rural gym in Atlanta City, New Jersey. It has been rented out for the full week by Alejandro Rodriguez, who is there to train his protégé Billy Joe Barker in the art of wrestling. It is Day 3 of Billy’s training and Alejandro has reluctantly agreed to let David Whitman and his friends the JEST’ers, James Whitman (David‘s cousin), best friends Eddie Sanchez and Scott Jones, and Thomas Williams (James’ best friend) to help with Billy’s training. And MWE have come along to film the process, there is an entire MWE Crew of cameramen who are filming everything which takes place, including a diary section. The camera pan in to the JEST’ers, who are standing in a corner drinking beer while Alejandro and David show Billy some holds in the ring. All of the JEST’ers are wearing JEST Kiddin’ t-shirts, plus jeans and trainers. Scott turns to the others. Scott: Why did we agree to come? All of them shrug and drink more beer. Eddie: Because we’re getting paid. Thomas looks at Eddie. Thomas: We’re getting paid? Eddie: We’re not? James is trying to crack open another six-pack of beer. James: I dunno, ask him. He nods towards Alejandro, who is wearing a black “A-Rod” shirt and wrestling pants and is shouting at Billy how to reverse the boston crab which David is applying on Billy. Eddie: Hey, Alejandro! Alejandro turns round. Alejandro: What? Eddie: Are we getting paid for this? Alejandro pauses and looks confused. Alejandro: Not if you sit around your ass all day. The JEST’ers all sigh and look at the crater of beer. Thomas: I’m good with not getting paid. Murmurs of “Yeah..”, “You’re right”, “Absolutely” and “I think I lost a quarter.” come from the JEST’ers. Alejandro turns back towards the ring, where Billy has successfully countered the move. The camera pans back on the JEST’ers. James steps forward and taps the weights. James: Don’t you think while we’re here, we should at least work out? The rest look at James as if he’s crazy, James shrugs. James: Come on, a lot of us haven’t seen action in a while. Scott: I’m on my day off. James: Dude, when do you ever have a day ON? Scott drinks more beer. Scott: That James, is the beauty of life. Eddie: I’m with you James, if we ever want to work again, we have to keep in shape. David’s the only one making any money around here. Thomas: That’s not strictly true dude, JEST Kiddin’ is doing great… I mean come on, we have our own TV Show! James: It could be better, we can’t even afford cameramen or editors, we have Eddie and Scott do it! Scott: We are still here dude. James shrugs and cracks open another beer. Camera switches over to the ring. David is in the corner of the ring, wiping his forehead on a towel, dressed in his usual ring attire. In another corner is Billy Joe, wearing a black tank top and jeans and his cowboy boots. Alejandro: Alright BJ, that’s ok. But you’re going to have to do better, ok hick? Billy Joe sighs and looks over towards Alejandro. Billy: Ya know Allyhandro, you’re gun make me real narked with those nick-names of yurs. Alejandro bangs the canvas with his fist. Alejandro: Well that’s good, annoyance leads to frustration, frustration leads to anger and anger leads to aggressiveness. Alejandro rolls into the ring and stands up. Alejandro: Alright, I want you to do what you did last time, but this time I want you to do it to me. Excuse us David. David nods and gets out the ring, he walks over to the JEST’ers and gets another beer. The camera focuses back onto the ring. With Alejandro still giving a pep-talk. While this happens, David heads over to the diary-cam section of the MWE Camera set which lets a person express their individual thoughts. Whitman has put on a t-shirt and is breathing heavily. David: He’s a big guy, and he’s hard to move around the ring, but he’s getting the hang of it thanks to me and Al. David takes a swig of beer and sighs. David: He’s a very strong man and can lift me up easily, he has a lot of potential, he can apply some strong holds and he can reverse strong holds as well. He takes another swig of beer and pauses for a little longer. David: He has a great mentor, Alejandro has a very.. Uh… what can I say... Heavy routine, he likes to insult Billy to get him fired up, whether it’s getting him fired up or it’s just upsetting him I don’t know, but I do know that either he will have to change, or Alejandro will, or it’s not going to work. Someone off-screen from MWE asks a question. David: Yeah, I think they’ll be a very good tag-team, interesting to say the least. Billy’s a naturally funny guy and Alejandro’s usually very serious so I think it will be a good combination. David pauses again as another question is asked. David: What do I think of their relationship? Uh… well. They both have very different personalities, anyone can see that, and those personalities clash all the time… But deep down they share a close bond, Alejandro owes Billy his life and Billy is living his dream because of Alejandro. Thomas: GAAAAAAYYYYY!!! Thomas is in the doorway with the rest of the JEST’ers laughing hysterically. David: What the fuck! There’s only one person allowed in here at a time! Camera switches back towards Alejandro and Billy Joe. Billy charges at Alejandro but Alejandro sidesteps and nails Billy with a hip toss. Alejandro begins to get angry. Alejandro: Stand up, hick. *Billy does as he is told.* Now I want you to listen to me, I am spending my time and money so you can be ready for your match this week. You are becoming too emotional, these guys will walk all over you if you do this on Monday, they have been training for years, and you have only been training for two days. You have great potential, and the reason we all have turned up today is to make sure you succeed. Now do it again, this time, show no emotion, I’m not your mentor, I’m an enemy, I will treat you as such. Alejandro gets into his defensive stance. Billy backs ups and charges. Alejandro side-steps and Billy tumbles over, Alejandro sighs but then Billy sweeps his legs! Alejandro hits the floor with a crash and despite the pain, he smiles as Billy Joe pulls him to his feet. Alejandro: Excellent stuff man, excuse me. Alejandro gets out the ring and heads over towards the diary-cam section, he steps inside and sits down, he is breathing heavily but looks pleased. Alejandro: Billy Joe just showed some great initiative there, I didn’t know he had it in him. He’s doing very well considering he’s been training for two days. If we get our heads down, I’m sure he’ll be ready enough for Monday, I believe in him. Someone off screen asks how Alejandro thinks they will do as a tag team. Alejandro: I think we would make an out-standing tag team, charisma-wise Billy’s very funny and I do serious-talking in my sleep, wrestling-wise Billy is very strong and I’m very speedy, it’s going to be great. Billy’s a good guy and I know that we will both watch our backs very well, we will be unstoppable. Alejandro steps out of the ring and wipes sweat off his body with a nearby towel. A bang comes from the corner of the gym as Darrel Jones walks into the gym with his arm full of McDonalds meals in bags. The JEST’ers and David roar their delight and run over to Darrel, they grab their meals, pat Darrel’s back and cheers of “Thanks Darrel. “Cheers Darrel.” “You did it again, mate.” and “Those are my Chicken Nuggets!” are heard from the crowd. Billy Joe exit’s the ring and goes to walk over to Darrel when Alejandro holds out his arm and shakes his head. Alejandro: You don’t want to eat that crap, you’ve got work to do. Alejandro looks at Billy’s sad face, Alejandro sighs and lets go of him. Alejandro: Go on then. Billy looks delighted and turns to face the others when Alejandro grabs his arm. Billy turns to face him. Billy: Yeah? Alejandro: No fries. Billy laughs, nods and runs over to everyone else, who had already begin their meals. Darrel smirks at the sight of them and walks over to Alejandro. Darrel gives Alejandro a Big Mac, Alejandro looks at it and puts it on a nearby table. Alejandro: No thanks, I brought my own lunch. Alejandro opens his bag and pulls out a bowl of pasta which has been covered with foil, he takes it off, grabs the fork which is inside and he begins to eat, sitting on the canvas alongside Darrel. Darrel: How’s the training coming along? Alejandro swallows his pasta. Alejandro: He’s getting there. Darrel: Do you think he will be ready? Alejandro shrugs and eats more pasta as Darrel nods. Darrel: I told Covell that he wasn’t ready but he wants Billy to debut immediately. Alejandro: Covell doesn’t care if he’s not ready, but I do. Darrel pauses and looks at Alejandro a little concerned. Darrel: You sure are doing a lot of work for Billy, you need to focus on your match too you know. Alejandro turns to Darrel. Alejandro: What can I do Darrel, Billy saved my life, I’ve got to make sure he’s ready, I owe him that much. I don’t care about my match this week, hell… I don’t even know what match I’m in this week. Darrel: You’re against David- Alejandro cuts him off as he looks over to David, who is picking gherkins off his quarter-pounder with cheese burger. Alejandro: …shit. Darrel: And Rated X- Alejandro: -what! Darrel: You’re teaming with Ace Cannon- Alejandro: -fuck off… Darrel: And Mason Resoan- Alejandro: WHAT THE- Darrel: and… He pauses. Darrel: Joey Dallas… Alejandro: -ARE YOU FUCKING SHITTING ME!?! Alejandro throws his bowl of pasta to the floor with a smash and the rest of the group look on as Alejandro storms out of the gym with Darrel following suit… David lets out a low whistle as a cameraman follows them outside. Alejandro is leaning on a barrier watching the traffic as Darrel is behind him twiddling his thumbs. Alejandro: I’m teaming with the biggest piles of shit in the MWE, Darrel... I’m teaming with a man I’ve hated since his career began, with the un-wanted Covell son who no-one likes and a guy who last week nearly killed me. And I’m up against three of the most decorated superstars in the business, two guys who I respect the hell out of and one of my best friends, David Whitman. Darrel: I’ve scheduled you an interview today, so you can take a break from training Billy and you can focus on your match and vent out some feelings. Alejandro: I ain’t doing any fucking interview… Darrel: Well… why not? Alejandro turns round to face Darrel. Alejandro: Because Darrel, you don’t know how it feels like to have your life saved, it’s a stronger feeling than nearly having your life taken, Billy showed bravery beyond anything that anyone I have ever known has shown and I need to help him, because he helped me. I need to give him a career, because he saved mine. Darrel: You’ve got to relax man, we’ve got an entire camera set here, we’re going to shoot it here, it will take ten minutes, David and the boys can cover training for ten minutes can’t they? Alejandro sighs and pauses and then nods his head. Alejandro: I guess you’re right… Ok, I’ll do the interview. Alejandro and Darrel walk back into the gym, everyone is sitting around, either eating their meals or chatting away. Alejandro walks towards the ring and he begins shouting. Alejandro: Hey, hick! Get in the ring, layabout! We’ve got to stretch you big-time to burn off those fries. Billy sighs as he gets off his chair and jogs over to the ring as the scene fades to black. TO BE CONTINUED…
