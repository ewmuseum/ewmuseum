Q & A - Rod; Alejandro Rodriguez RP

Nov 8 2008

Alejandro Rodriguez

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
CONTINUED FROM: A CRASH COURSE IN WRASSLIN’ Scene opens inside a rural gym in Atlanta City, New Jersey. The same gym which Alejandro has rented for the week to train Billy Joe Barker. In the background James, Eddie, Scott, Thomas, David and Billy are taking part in a practice six-man tag-team match in the ring. Alejandro is standing in another corner of the gym with Darrel Jones, it is past mid-day and Alejandro has changed into a suit without a tie and Darrel is still wearing a suit. They are preparing the camera set for Alejandro’s interview. Alejandro glances over to the ring and see’s that they are having a tag match, he walks over to them shouting. Alejandro: NO! STOP! Tag-team, are you kidding me? It’s every man for himself on Monday, have a free-for-all match! Alejandro walks back over to Darrel, who shakes his head. Darrel: Jesus, Al. Lay off him a bit, they’re just having some fun, they’ve been working all day. Alejandro: Don’t talk to me about wrestling Darrel, you’ll only embarrass yourself, now help me get this damn camera set up. They finally get the camera set up, and they get into position. Darrel grabs a microphone from an official and the camera begins to roll. Darrel: What’s up MWE fans? Darrel Jones here with Alejandro Rodriguez, who this week has been training Billy Joe Barker in the art of wrestling, how has the training been going Alejandro? Alejandro: It’s going very well indeed, we’re only half-way through our training sessions but I believe Billy Joe is more than half ready, I have never seen anyone pick wrestling up as fast as this, it takes some people years to master it, and it’s taken Billy Joe just a few short days to get the basics, he’s extra-ordinary. He has great potential, if he keeps improving at this rate then I have no-doubt that he will win his 5-Man Mayhem match this Monday. Darrel: And how have you been preparing for your match? Alejandro: Well as you well know Darrel, I only found out who was in my match this week was when you told me earlier today. The only real exercise I’ve been doing is getting in the ring with Billy Joe every day, so it’s probably more than I do anyway. Billy is hard work, and I’ve got no time to be selfish now, this is Billy’s time to shine, and he must succeed, I owe him that. It’s not every day that someone saves your life, I’m just glad I can pay him back in something more valuable to him than money. Darrel: Would you like to talk about your opponents in your match? Alejandro: You know what Darrel? Just for you, I’m going to talk about my opponents, and my partners, because I have just as much history with my partners as well as my opponents. They were all a part of the MWE last time, so I know them all very well. Where would you like me to begin. Darrel: Well lets start with your opponents shall we, so David Whitman first. Alejandro: Sure. As you know, I have a close relationship with David, he’s a very good friend of mine, actually he’s standing right behind me. *He looks at the ring, where David and Billy lock up again.* He’s a great guy and a great wrestler, but he and I both know that we not hold anything back from each other in the ring, we have faced each other numerous times, and he and I have both come out victorious in singles encounters between us, but, as even as I say that… this is not a singles encounter, and I am confident that my team’s strength in numbers will overcome their team. Darrel: What about D.C.? Alejandro: What is there to say about D.C., other than the fact he runs over his best-friends and he abuses his girlfriend, what must people see in him? The man is one tough guy though, and he has beaten me before, back in my green days, I never really got him back for that Here we have an over-rated loudmouth with an overly proportionate fan base, he feels the need to use wise-cracks and playground insults to get his point of view across and has long been seen as the most charismatic man in the MWE. But what does that all mean? What does that all direct away from? …wrestling! This is a WRESTLING show! And he has little wrestling achievements to his name, other than holding an MWE Championship because I didn’t want to, the title which he lost without winning it. Alejandro laughs his head off for a moment before regaining control. Alejandro: But I shouldn’t say that, no, he was the best X-TV Champion to date, he took that title and lifted it to such a level that people tuned in just to see his matches. But all of that doesn’t matter, it doesn’t matter because whatever has happened in the past, it wont matter because whatever happens, happens. I respect D.C. and all that he has done for the MWE, and his team has a better chance of winning with him on it, it is to be said for every partner… well I say that… Joey Dallas might slip on a banana peel or something and fuck it up. Darrel: And what do you have to say about Brian Michaels? Alejandro: To be honest, nothing that I haven’t said before. I respect the hell out of the guy, and I would be an idiot to under-estimate him. But I’m more mature enough not to take the mickey out of a person’s height or other things along those lines, but I have said it from the start, the man is a daddy’s boy. He can’t handle himself in the big bad world of wrestling, where has he gotten without his father? Ran over, betrayed and left to suffer in hospital out of action for months and months. And by the way, the man who did this to him is now being paid over 800,000 pounds a year to continue to wrestle Covell’s son, by Covell himself, despite the fact he almost killed him. Alejandro pauses and shakes his head. Alejandro: But look where he has got with his father, three healthy world-championship reigns taking on people like Pala and Sonny Williams to keep his reign to a considerable amount of weeks, just goes to show how far family will get you in the world of wrestling. Like I said before, I do not under-estimate him, I do not disrespect him at all. But he hasn’t proven to me that he can stand on his own two feet and know that he must face what’s coming. With that being said… If that man goes down, he goes down with a fight, Mason doesn’t know that, Ace doesn’t know that and Joey does not know that. I know, and I know very well that Michaels will bring his all or bring nothing at all. Darrel: Well that’s you’re opponents done, how about your partners? Lets start with Ace Cannon. Alejandro: Ace who? I’m kidding… I did my research, ok? I know exactly who Ace Cannon is, and what he represents. Some would say he’s the forgotten Covell brother, some would say he’s a great tag-team guy, some would see him as the founder of the great Team Malibu, I personally think he’s a snobby prick. No, no… I shouldn’t have said that. To tell you the truth, he is great at tag-team matches, and another guy like him on the team makes a big difference, but I don’t need partners on Monday, I‘d do a much better job of it on your own, MWE just requires that I have them. He says he respects me, but thus far he has yet to earn my respect, after Monday, he may have my respect, if he does not, I suggest he stays out of my way. Darrel: How do you feel about Mason Resoan being your partner? Alejandro: Ah yes, Mason Resoan so forgettable I even forgot he was in the match. That lowlife piece of shit tried to kill me last week, and now I’m teaming with him. DING, DING! It doesn’t make sense, Covell. You team up your son with a guy who tried to kill him and you team me with a man who almost crushed me last week because he can’t handle his battles in the ring. And all this shit about the U.S. Title, MWE.com answering this as major news, “What will Alejandro say!”, NEWSFLASH! I don’t give a damn, I don’t give a damn about that stupid little title and I couldn’t care less about it’s future. Mason Resoan needs to back off, it’s not like I banged his chick or screwed him out of a World Championship match. He lost a United States title match and he tries to kill someone. That’s how low his life really is. Alejandro pauses to laugh and Darrel smirks a bit too before Alejandro continues. Alejandro: I have one instruction for Mason in this match, stay out of my way, if you put one finger on me, I will take that finger and shove it straight up your ass. I’ve had just about enough of you, if you stay out of my way, I might just not kick your ass, but if you do one thing to piss me off, it will be the last thing you do. And I won’t fuck it up like you did, I will make sure that I will take you out the MWE for good, just like I did with Jekyl, and unlike you Mason, it won’t be forgettable, that moment will haunt you to the very day that you die. Darrel: You’ve had some history in the past with your final partner, Joey Dallas. Alejandro: Yes I have Darrel, and I’ll be the first to admit that Joey Dallas has beaten me before, I’ve worked with him before… and you know what, it sucked. One of the low points of my career it was, I was kept down and forced into the mid-card to go against guys like him, guys who grated on my nerves and made my life a misery. I’m not undermining his talent, which is, I have to say, somewhat great, but he just isn’t raw. He isn’t on the same level as the whole upper-half of the MWE roster, not yet anyway. Alejandro picks up a bottle of water, he opens it and walks over to a nearby chair, he sits down and drinks half of it in one, he gulps it down and sighs. He puts it on a nearby table and continues to speak. Alejandro: The thing… About teams, the first thing I think of, is friendship. I am not friends with any of my partners, hell I don’t even like or respect most of them. But the same can be said for Michaels, D.C. and Whitman, what else do they have other than mutual respect for each other, but they, have got it. However much D.C. and Michaels loathe each other, they were a damn good tag-team back in the day. David Whitman spent his entire child-hood wrestling with his cousin James and his buddies. But our team has that too. I’m a soon to be tag-team wrestling with Billy Joe, Ace Cannon is a successful tag-teamer with Team Malibu, Mason Resoan was a part of the Canadian Xchange, who were a key tag-team in the Original MWE and Joey Dallas was with Jason Anthony in a tag-team that did ok. Alejandro takes another gulp of his drink and he pauses again to enjoy it before carrying on drinking. Darrel walks over and sits beside him as the cameras get nearer. Darrel: One final question then, are you confident you can win? Alejandro: I’ll tell you one thing Darrel, I’ve never been less confident in my life. If the match was a 3 on 3 match without me on the team, they would surely crumble. With me on the team, it kind of eases it out a bit, but Dallas, Cannon and Resoan would still most likely crumble. People like Dallas, Cannon and Resoan have only faced guys like Whitman, D.C. and Michaels in PPV matches once in their lifetime, matches which they have lost. I have beaten Michaels for the MWE World Championship at Highway to Hell, I have beaten D.C. in the shortest ever match in the MWE and I have stolen the show countless times with Whitman and I have beaten him too. You talk about a man with the experience and intensity to look those three men in the eyes, you talk about a man to lead this pathetic team to victory, you look towards Alejandro Rodriguez. And that is all I have to say about that. It’s time for me to go and do some training. Alejandro abruptly walks off screen leaving Darrel sat on his own. The camera stays on Darrel though Alejandro’s yells at Billy Joe’s slightly sloppy work is heard in the background. The scene fades to black as Darrel pours himself a glass of water.
