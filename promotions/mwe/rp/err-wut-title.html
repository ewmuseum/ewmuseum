Err...wut title?; vs. Anthony Clark

Jun 4 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
QUOTE Nic "Shawn" Kraven "The Voice" Serenity Uncle Trent Police Officer Cassidy Flight Attendant Cab Driver “Ah distinctly I remember it was in the bleak December, And each separate dying ember wrought its ghost upon the floor. Eagerly I wished the morrow; - vainly I had sought to borrow From my books surcease of sorrow – sorrow for the lost Lenore - For the rare and radiant maiden whom the angels named Lenore - Nameless here for evermore.” PROLOGUE May 24th, Sunday, 2009, Tribulation. Titles changed hands. A star was born. And a legend has done it once again. More importantly, on that night, Nic Kraven became the new X-TV Champion after the battle with the returning Eric Derf. After months of dominating, Kraven proved once more that he is somebody to look out for. Kraven won't have long to celebrate though because this upcoming Mayhem, he will face another up and comer, Anthony Clark. Clark proved he was champion material as he defeated Blight, Sin, and Devlin Sharpe to be the number one contender. Can Clark defeat hot sttreaker Nic Kraven or will Kraven reign supreme once again? The answers lie ahead. June 8th, Monday Night Mayhem. #01 [Darkness. Yes, but out of the solid darkness, we can hear footsteps.] [Clack.] [Clack.] [Clack.] [Faster and faster it went. Until, light is let in and the scene opens. It appears the camera is closing in on a lone door in a dark deserted corridor. Nothing special, just a plain gray door. The camera then comes to a complete halt just inches away from the doorknob. And if you listen closely, on the other side of the door, we can hear somebody breathing silently. Hesitantly, a hand reaches out and slowly turns the doorknob. Eeeekkkk... The door opens and a plain room is revealed. Nothing at all interesting. A light bulb hangs from the ceiling lighting only part of the room. The other, remains dark. And just as expected, we see the new X-TV Champion Nic Kraven sitting on the dark side. His head bowed with the title laying right besides him, his left arm rests on his knee. He currently wears a leather jacket with a white tank top, dark blue baggy jeans, and Nikes. Nic looks up uninterested at the camera and turns his focus to his newly won title. He knows he doesn't need it. A title means nothing to him. But somewhere deep inside his very core, he feels like the title has become a part of him. He continues staring at it...emotionless.] “Shawn...” [The voice. The same voice that has come to him many times before. The soft feminine voice that is Serenity. Yes, it can be no other. A breeze picks up and blows across the room sending chills down Nic's spine.] “I see you've won your match.” “Y-Yes.” “Congratulations...” “What do you want Serenity?” [What did she want? Of all times to come, she comes now. He just wanted peace and quiet to clear his mind. What a pest she has become. Kraven slowly looks up and strokes his hair back with his right hand awaiting a response from Serenity.] “Whats wrong Shawn? Something bothering you? You should be celebrating, you've won your title match.” [Yes, something was bothering him. Her. But that wasn't all. There was something more, something that had been bothering him since Tribulation. Up until then, he hadn't really thought about it. But now, now that hes relived it, Cassidy's kidnapping so long ago has brought pain to him once again.] “...” [No response from Kraven. Serenity speaks again, her voice louder this time around.] “I said, what's wrong Shawn? Wait...I know now. Its Cassidy, its your sister Cassidy isn't it? [Quite amazing. She figured it out without even having to think about it. Wide eyed, Kraven just stares. Seconds pass before Kraven before regains composure. He takes a breath before speaking.] “Clever of you... Yes, that is whats bothering me.” “ You fool!” [Kraven is a little taken aback by this. A fool? What the hell is she talking about? Kraven fights back the urge to insult her as he continues sitting there...staring.] “How so?” “Your pathetic. You need to stop thinking about her. Shes gone, gone forever. Get her out of your mind and start thinking about your upcoming title defense. Her memory is only bringing you do-” [Temper rising. Kraven can feel his temper rising. He knew Serenity was right. Cassidy was gone forever. But...theres still a part of him doesn't want to believe it. Denial. Kraven speaks out his voice loud interrupting Sereniy as she is about to finish her sentence. Unable to control his anger.] “Shut up! Just be quiet!” “But you know its true Shawn. Stop denying the truth. Shes gone. You'll never see your sister again. Get her out of your mind!” [The breeze starts to swirl violently blowing at Kraven's hair. Kraven shakes his profusely not wanting to hear anymore of her “nonsense.”] “Just shut up and leave! I don't want to hear anymore! Go, leave!” “But-” “NOW!” [And just as quickly as it began, it ended. The breeze blows no more. Serenity was gone. Kraven sits there...slightly shaking. He glances down at his X-TV Title and picks it up, staring at it.] “It's funny really, I tell my self time and time again, I don't need a title. I don't need it. It means nothing other than a mantle piece to awe at. Just because one has a championship does not mean they are superior to others. Superior in terms of all around skill. It puzzles me as to why people like Sinc Mercier try so desperately to get the gold.” “Really, championship gold just blinds you. It blinds you from the thing that are occurring around you. And like an object of desire, it can slowly...drive you...insane. You see, have you ever wanted something so much, that you would do anything to get it? Well what if you couldn't get it? And then...your anger from not getting what you desire the most, will over come you driving you mad. It's like this...what if no matter what you do, no matter how hard you try, you just can't grasp that gold in your hands? Wouldn't that drive you insane like...like me? You see championship gold, is a curse.” [The words flow through his mind like a silent metronome. “It's a curse,” “It can slowly...drive you...insane,” “I don't need it...I don't need it.” No...he doesn't need it. But he feels as if, its become a part of it. Does he really doesn't need it? Or are his words just...empty, meaningless. Kraven grasps the title in his hands, and throws it across the room. The title...it was taunting him. Taunting him like Serenity has done countless times.] “And like championship gold, stardom can also blind you. A perfect example of this is Anthony Clark. Look at him, hes young, cocky, and a complete fool. Hes been blinded by his success...his stardom here in the wrestling business. A former XHF Phoenix and European champion. A GWC Ultimate and Tag Champion. Its simple, his career so far is oozing with success. And with success brings cockiness.” “The thing is Clark, cockiness will not get you anywhere. And it surely won't take the X-TV title from me. Clark...you are corrupt. Arrogance screams from your soul. You immediately put down anyone who you think is unworthy. It's funny really, you're just so blind and corrupted that you fail to realize that nobody in the locker room gives a shit about your fame or accomplishments. Clark...hear me, you arrogance will be your downfall. [Disgust can clearly be seen in Kraven's expression. Disgust of this arrogant man who seems to think he is God. Anger runs through his veins as he quickly spits on the floor. Kraven slowly strokes his back and continues speaking trying not to let his anger get the best of him.] “Clark, you disgust me. Every time you speak, every time you rant, ever time you cut a freakin' promo, all I hear coming out of your mouth is, “I'm better than you all.”Better...better how? Better than making a complete fool out of yourself? You ramble on and on endlessly claiming you are the best in this business...best in this world. But don't you see...your just making a fool out of yourself in these people's eyes? You've only been in this company for only two measly weeks and now your rambling on about how your gonna be the future of this company? Are you shitting me?! [He chuckles to himself at just how foolish Clark can be. Only 2 weeks and he thinks hes better than everyone. While Kraven...who's been in this company for over a month is still being underestimated and booed by these parasites. Kraven's mind starts to wander away as he thinks about what had occurred earlier. He quickly shakes it off and speaks.] “Tell me Clark. You think you're the best this company has, who have you beaten? Blight? Sin? Two who are as violent and deadly as me yet seems lost here in MWE? Devlin Sharpe? A man who I have beaten here before and seems to sruggle with his self identity. Thats exactly my point, you've beaten nobody worth mentioning. Then, when you faced Jay Riddick, a man that was even remotely like me, you lost. You talked as if he was inferior to you and you were going to put him in his place, and what did he do? He beat you. Really Clark, your ignorance disturbs me.” [Kraven gently puts both of his hands on his forehead and runs it down his face as if hes washing his face. Kraven arches his head back and stretches before continuing once more.] “And then Clark, we have that little slut you bring to ringside. What does she do? Taunt opponents with her assets? Tell me, are you that afraid of losing that you would hide behind your girlfriend during a match? Now, some people would call having your girlfriend at ringside to distract your opponents smart. I call it pathetic. You fear losing so you bring her to ringside to distract opponents to get a cheap win. It's pathetic really.” “Coward... Why don't you stop bringing that girl of yours to ringside and finally step up? Step up, and prove to everyone that you're not a coward. That you're not some arrogant prick that can't back up the things that you say. You have the opportunity Clark to prove yourself against a champion. They say, when adversity stands in the way of man, man steps up. I have stepped up. When I first entered this company, I was a low-carder. I was nothing. But now...I sit here as the new X-TV Champion. So Clark, can you? Can you step up? Do you honestly have what it takes? Or is there self doubt? This isn't GWC or XHF anymore Clark. Around here, your a nobody. Time to shine Clark. I'll be waiting.” [Prove yourself. Proving yourself is something Kraven has done time and time again. It just goes to show you that when adversity stands in the way, man CAN step up. But can Clark? Kraven looks out into the dark hallways. Deserted. He had hoped somebody might wander here, lost. But alas, no avail. Nobody ever dared venture this way. Kraven yawns a bit before continuing.] “Its a coincidence really, you were trained by Zak Warner and Kevin Hardaway. Warner, a man whom I have yet to beat. But it is so like me in every little ways. And then Hardaway, a man that I have even yet to face but already one of the faces of this company. They're both successful, they're both in MWE, and they both trained you. Its a shame really, that they didn't train you hard enough, or beat you hard enough to knock that attitude out of you. Because really, your attitude is one of the main things wrong with you.” “Success. Everybody wants to be successful. Whether it would be to own a large successful company or to be a World Champion. Everybody wants to be successful. Your trainers Zak Warner and Kevin Hardaway have done it. But can you? Can you possibly even over come your trainers? But then, what if you can't? Then, all the things you claim to be would be just talk. And then, you'll eventually be the laughing stock of the whole company. Doubt...doubt will then haunt you everywhere you go. Everything you do. It will then drive you insane. Heh.. Can you prove me wrong? I dare you...no..I challenge you to prove me wrong! I want a challenge!” [Kraven slowly rises to his feet. He stretches a bit and looks out at the window lying on the right side of the room. The bright side to be exact. Dark out. Its midnight. The moon seems to be almost full. Kraven walks over to the door while speaking.] “Monday Night Mayhem. A title on the line. Will you emerge from our battle victorious? The clock is ticking down to our match. As I wait...I'm preparing, physically and mentally. For this Monday, I thirst for a challenge. Throw all fear and doubts aside. Clark, I want you to go to your girlfriend now. Hold her hand, hug her, kiss her as if you'll never see her again...because maybe you might not. Leave her backstage and come ready to battle. Go into the ring, step forward, oppose me. Soon, you'll realize that arrogance will soon be your demise. And then, the light that blinds you so, will be diminish by The Dark Phoenix himself. When we meet, show your true hideous form, before The Awakening Sins puts you to rest.” [Kraven walks out into the hallway. He turns swiftly back looking at the room before closing the door. He now slowly walks down the hallway and eventually, his figure is immerse into the darkness. The camera cuts and the scene is now engulfed with static.] #02 [The scene opens in what appears to be a living room. Just like any normal living room, it has furniture, a lamp, and tables. A phone lies on it's wooden surface. A 40' Flat Screen stands in front of the camera. Directly facing the TV is a large leather couch. And sitting on the couch, is none other than Nic Kraven. He has on his usual leather jacket, a black shirt with what appears to have a cross on it, ripped, baggy jean shorts, and a pair of Nikes. However he does not have his head bowed down like usual. He has head up and stares up at the ceiling as he leans back against the couch. Thoughts roll around in his head like pinballs. Thoughts about Cassidy. The phone suddenly rings snapping Kraven out of his 'trance.” He, however, does not decide to get it letting the answering machine get it instead. A beep commences and a deep rough voice sounds out.] QUOTE “Kid...yo kid. You there? It's your Uncle Trent. Yeah, I know you don''t want shit to do with me but I got something to tell you. It's Cassidy...they found her...” [Silence. Kraven swiftly snaps his neck to the phone. He stares at it wide eyed. “It's Cassidy...they found her...” Can it..can it be? No. Cassidy is gone forever. It had to be a mistake. Kraven continues staring at it not believing what he had heard. The voice sounds out again.] QUOTE “Yeah...can't ya believe it? They really found her after all these years. Well...she just got to my house a while ago. Shes asleep now but I maybe you can fly down here now to see her. Alright, all I had to say, see ya kid.” [Kraven clumsily gets up off the couch fully alert. Hes over joyed. The one person that had gave him happiness was alive. Cassidy was alive. Kraven quickly exits the living room almost knocking down a chair along the way.] .. .. [The scene is immerse in light as it opens. Kraven is now shown sitting on a green bench at LAX with his suitcase already packed. People rush pass him trying to make their flight. Just then a soft voice rings out on the speaker system. “Flight 401 to Sacramento is now boarding.” That was his flight. He quickly stands up and makes his way over to where the plane is ready to board. A gleam in his eyes as he walks. Happiness..] .. .. .. [The scene reopens on the plane. Kraven is seen sitting on a soft velvet seat. People around him are either reading a magazine or sleeping. Some kids talking loudly from the seat behind him. A flight attendance in a blue and white uniform walks up to Nic rolling a cart along with what appears to be food on it. Shes young, doesn't look a year over 25. She looks at him with a bright smile and speaks.] “Sir, would you like something to drink?” “No. Now go. I want to be alone.” [She walks away looking a little hurt and offended. Kraven doesn't really care as he sits there with his bowed. His mind wonders way from the flight. Questions are running through his head. How did they find her? Is she okay? How is she going to react when she sees me? Suddenly a smooth voice rings out onto the speaker.] “Ladies and gentleman this is your captain speaking. We are about to land in Sacramento in about 10 minutes. We hope you have enjoyed this flight” [10 minutes. 10 minutes until he finally lands in Sacramento. Kraven takes a deep breath, sits back, and relaxes awaiting the land.] .. .. [About 20 minutes pass as the scene reopens once again. Kraven is now seen pulling his suitcase into the Sacramento International Airport. He looks around at the large place slightly fascinated. Just over at the bench, there stands 2 officers apparently waiting for him. One of the officer steps forward.] “Excuse me sir, are you Shawn Travis?” [Kraven looks at him suspiciously stepping back a bit as he does.] “Yeah. What do you want with me? “Sir, I'm afraid you'll have to come with us. Your wanted in questioning of the kidnapping of Cassidy Travis.” [Kraven is left stunned at this sentence. Questioning? Him? Why? Why would he have anything to do with the kidnapping of his own sister? Kraven takes another step back before hesitantly speaking.] “No...no. I didn't do anything involving the kidnapping of my sister.” “Sorry sir, you'll still have to come with us.” [The other officer steps forward grabbing Kraven's wrist with handcuffs at hand. Kraven looks around the airport. People are staring at them as they walk by. Quickly without thinking at all, Kraven snatches his wrist away, elbows one of the officer, and starts to run with his suitcase at hand. The officer lets out a uh! and falls to the hard floor The other runs after him but he was slower than Kraven. Nic makes his way out of the airport and into the seat of an open doored taxi cab nearby. “Get me the fuck away from here! To Richmond Grove now! Go! Hurry!" "Wait...why? What the hell happened?" "Don't question me! Just go." “Alright, alright! Hold on.” [The cab speeds away with the officer barely at the door. Kraven turns his head back seeing the officers. He chuckles a bit. Anticipation fills him. Just a few minutes away, he will finally be reunited with his sister once again. Nothing to do now but relax. He takes a deep breath and sits back against his seat.] .. .. [Minutes pass as the cab finally pulls up to a house. The house looks practically sparkling. The door is painted bright white with a colorful glass window on it. The house is also painted white. The windows are spotless and lawn is mowed well. A garage is nearby. Kraven gets out of the car and pays the driver. He looks up at the house with a disgusted look on his face. So rich but never thought to have helped Nic at least once in his life. Kraven walks up the steps and knocks gently on the door. A nearly balding man with an after shave and a slight bear belly answers the door.] “Woah. Hey kid! Good to see ya! You've grown pretty big. Whats with the face? Damn...give your Uncle Trent a hug! “Get out of my way old man. Why are you being so nice all of a sudden? I know your just acting so just stop wasting my time. I have nothing to say to you.” [Kraven pushes him out of the way and enters the house. Just as he thought. A big house with a well cleaned kitchen and a nice living room. The house is spotless. A breeze blows around making Kraven shiver a bit. A 50' Flat Screen is seen nearby along with leather couches and paintings. Kraven starts to get sick to his stomach. Why had he never helped him in a time of need? It's because Trent is a selfish man who cares about nobody but himself. Kraven walks over to the couch with his suitcase at hand and suddenly yells out.] “Cassidy?!” [A soft feminine voice answers behind him.] “Shawn...” ^__^ Amy Lee <33 BLACK OOC: I'm ashamed of my promo >__>.
