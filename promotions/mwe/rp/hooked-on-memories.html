Hooked on memories; PPV RP

Sep 12 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A dark cold room,a dim powder blue light lightly illuminates the room, hooks and chains hang from the ceiling, a frosty air, chilling down to the bone sets the scene. A puff of smoke slowly creeps across the screen Jekyl stands off camera taking in the moment of a chill running up his spine. Jekyl: It is time for a new Champion, one that will retain over and over, one that will not disgrace MWE with shenanigans of complete fuckery, and one that wont cry when he looses it after a week...Yes Sonny that was directed to you, got an issue? Want a tissue? Well your not getting one, so go fuck yourself. Whitman does nothing to deserve the title, sure he beat a few people, but he doesn't take it seriously. For example, last weeks promo, imitating the god father? common man, grow up. We need a champion that will give the gold an honorable name, one that will give it a permanent home with no bullshit.... Sonny obviously can't do that, he's still crying over me painting on his brothers memorial wall....and Whitman isn't fit enough to hold the gold. Jekyl begins to walk about the cold dark area, passing between what looks like red pillars. His breath, warm, moist and foggy in the cold atmosphere, his hair beginning to get small bits of ice on them, he runs his hands through it to loosen it up, each individual hair was stiff as a board. Jekyl: Whitman has earned the title to its current standing, he has won both battle royals and a few matches in between, but NOT ONCE has he face me one on one.... The same battle royal he was crowned champion that I was a part of, was by chance, since he was not the man to eliminate me. Alejandro did that job for you, you had no opportunity to face me, you had not PRIDE, to take me on, and you sure as hell don't have the balls to do stand face to face to me and speak the words "I...am..world heavyweight... champion" because soon as those words exit you mouth, you will not longer be a champion, you will be jobbing to the likes of a man who can't win a match who we know as Sonny Williams. I know I can't preach because I only beat Sonny, but my matches were meant to deal pain and suffering, not a win, which I accomplished, but now I have a goal, not to take a title, but to beat a man who has a high regard, and Sonny is in my way. Jekyl straightens his jacket and regains his composure, as he reaches in his pocket to pull out his butterfly knife. As he reaches the other end of the cold dark room he flips a switch, illuminating the dark, mysterious room... only to reveal that its a meat locker. Dangling from the chains left and right are hunks of pork, beef and one that stands out from the corner of the room.......a man... Jekyl: Whitman...in your last promo it took you 3 takes to get your promo going right. in the first take you acted like the godfather, when you have a jawbreaker in your mouth. Second take you and your mentally handicapped friend start fighting over a water pistol....Like seriously? what are you a couple of retarded monkey arguing over who's poo your gonna fling? Go out, buy a real gun, THEN put it to your skull.... You may be champ now... but you got sonny to worry about, and you got me... Jekyl runs his fingers through his hair and smacks his lips. His breath once again takes form of smoke as he exhales. Walking slowly towards the far back corner, brushing his hands against the cold meat as he passes by. As Jekyl walks closer and closer to the man on the hook we realize its a corpse of a random man dressed up to look like David Whitman. Short black hair, a black shirt with "Time to Burn" in flaming letters printed on it, fingerless gloves, and black pants... the corpse hang lifelessly, skin cold with the surrounding room. Jekyl: I kinda "borrowed" him from a funeral home you see, since he's already dead I didn't see any problem other than his family missing him, then again...HES DEAD! hahaha they are gonna miss him for a loooonggg time...hee hee haaa haaaaa.......... But back to our subject at hand, lets just assume, for the greatness of science, that this is the future look of David Whitman after I'm finished with him at Turmoil.... Jekyl leans in closer to the corpse, with his arms flailing around the body like a game show girl showing off the prizes. He looks at the camera smiling with the sadistic look only an insane clown would have....Jekyl looks up and around looking for something in particular... Jekyl: What the fuck was that? pondering now with a confused look stands up searching behind the big chunks of meat looking for the source of his confusion. Jekyl: Who the fuck is talking? I thought I was alone in here?!?! As he wanders the room, now questioning his very sanity hears a strange voice....wait that's me! Jekyl: WHO ARE YOU? Jekyl now stands upright with his knife in hand, yelling at thin air. I'm the narrator, for the past 3 months I have been narrating your life in your promos. Jekyl: Why have I never heard you before? You haven't been listening hard enough...But I have been here for quite some time now... Jekyl: Well well well, I think I've gone insane! Well, clinically? Yes.... just ignore me and finish this promo that will make both Whitman cry to his momma, and make Sonny find a razor and hide in a corner until this PPV is done... Jekyl: Fine... but be quiet....this is a masterpiece in progress. He then walks back to the corpse, ready to finish his promo.... The voice fades into an awkward silence into the cold meat locker as Jekyl tries to remember where he left off... Jekyl: Ah yes... This will be our so called "Future Whitman", and he will be our demonstration.... First lets start off with who this man is... Jekyl stands up as the camera pans to show the top half of the corpse and Jekyl. We then hear a sudden zip sound and the clanking metal of and open belt, a sudden sound of sliding cloth against a metal wall is heard, Jekyl has obviously taken the mans pants off. Waiving his arms and smiling he begins to speak in haste. Jekyl: Before you judge, this isn't what it looks like, I'm going somewhere with this... The deranged clown shows his knife as he reaches down and makes a swift cut... what looks like walnuts wrapped in a thin dough... Jekyl: David is a man who now literally, has no balls... He makes another swift jerk of the hand. and we see what looks like a shriveled up ancient hot dog fling past the camera. Jekyl: A man with no balls has no need for a....uhm.... "rooster".....*ahem*....Mr. Whitman.... could you please cough for me? The corpse remains still as Jekyl begins to laugh at his own joke in that cold room. he smacks the corpse in the face which causes it to swing back and forth on the meat hook. Jekyl: Now him and Sonny should be a tag team...maybe we could call them "The Harlem No Ballers".... nah too lame.... fuck it.... As Jekyl begins to cut up the corpse in random spots, he puts his hand to his chin to ponder. Jekyl: I wonder... This part of our little "demonstration" could represent both Sonny and David, you see... These two men have heart, but that is their weakness, they have a heart, they have compassion, they have the trait that causes many men to fall in a fight, they are afraid to take another life in their own hands. I am a sociopath, I don't have regrets, I don't have any form of compassion for anyone, I care only about myself, and that is what will cause me to retain over and over once I beat the shit out of both Whitman and Sonny, I will have the ability to hold the gold and not care about the pain I inflict upon people. Jekyl stands back and begins to look at the corpse who is now cut up along the arms, legs, and face. the corpse slowly spins on the meat hook as Jekyl chuckles to himself. Jekyl: Ladies and gentlemen, I now present to you, the future look of David Whitman. He dances and claps at the sight of the cadaver hanging in limbo. But he stops and begins to walk towards the exit. Jekyl: I find it too cold in here now, the thought of this match brings chills to my spine alone...no need for a meat locker to add on much longer... Jekyl picks up his pace and begins to walk down the hall, dark, dry, warm and humid, the camera lens begins to obtain fog on the lens from the temperature change. Pictures on the walls are broken, the walls stained with the blood of time, water stains cover the walls, paint faded, musk odor of time itself lingers in the halls. Jekyl Begins to whisper in the hall but no words can be heard, the dead silence consumes any form of emotion, a rat scurries across and down the call rustling old pieces of paper, he then exits to a street, a faded red mark in the ground, a broken baseball bat and empty 9mm shells scatter the ground in various places. He stops to take in the moment. Jekyl: SONNY WILLIAMS!!!! do you recognize this place? Recognize the bat? Recognize these shells? Recognize that hole in the ground? This is the very spot you sad pathetic brother of yours was shot...Take a deep breath, breath in the moment and let your tears flow. Yes Sonny, I bring you back to the very place your brother was murdered, the very place where you started as a failure to yourself, your family, and your brother. This is the same pool of blood that came from your brothers hollow skull, and i say hollow with great respect because a man of your stature thinks it was pretty cool to bring your little brother to a gang war.... your a sad strange little man Sonny, you always were, you still are, and always will be. With a sigh of relief he walks over and picks up the baseball bat, blood smeared over the top, he smiles as he looks at it with great regard. Jekyl: You see, come Turmoil... I Jekyl will become World Champion, and you will have no way of stopping me. I beat you once last week Sonny, and I will do it again this week! Mr.Whitman....you have awaken a force that should not be dealt with so lightly... You stirred the beast in its cage, are you ready for the consequences? See you 2 ugly sons of bitches at Turmoil.... Jekyl swings the bat as he walks off into the dark distance, his laugh can be heard form blocks around, an eerie wind blows a newspaper onto the camera as we see a picture of Jekyl on the front page holding up the World title. The caption reads... "A new champion was crowned. Jekyl defeated the highest regarded superstar and the low class bitch of MWE Sonny Williams, we expect great things from this man....will anyone be able to stop him?" Now THAT ladies and gentlemen...is a promo... -Fade-
