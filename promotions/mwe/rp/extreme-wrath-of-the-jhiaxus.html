Extreme Wrath of the Jhiaxus; This title has nothing to do with the RP

Nov 19 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
QUOTE Orange is Warner, Red is Kevin...got it? The one thing I’ve always liked about life on the road, is getting to stay in all the different hotels. I’ve been banned from a fair number of them, especially after the little fiasco with Kevin, Angie, and that crazy ass therapist who I still haven’t called back. Sadly, I wish I could say that this current hotel was up to the previous standards but it wasn’t. Maybe it was just because I hate warm weather and the sun just seems to hate me back. What can I say, growing up in a place like Syracuse makes you appreciate the cold weather just a little bit more. Phoenix though, was nothing but sun, skanks, and shitty food. Especially in this hotel where I was staying. Room service wasn’t doing shit for me, and I was hungry as hell. Smoking a bowl with DeAngelo probably hadn’t been the best idea, but he had stopped in town on his way to some court hearing. I guess there was some lawsuit pending with a restaurant that had overcooked his chicken, he flipped out, I don’t know the full story but it was typical DeAngelo. So there I was, sitting on my couch, owning some face in Modern Warfare two. I was sniping left and right, pissing off some n00b with the gamer tag “xXMasterSpaceXx”. I mean come on, the name just practically screams “Shoot me in the head six straight times please”. Anyways, I was about to snipe him yet again, when I heard a knock at the hotel door. I hadn’t ordered room service, or any hookers, so I wasn’t about to answer it. For all I knew, it was some crazy fan wanting a lock of my hair or something. Right as I got another kill, there was another knock so I couldn’t exactly ignore it any longer. I walked over to the door, peeking through the eyehole and guess who was standing there? My good ole buddy Kevin fucking Hardaway. I couldn’t help but to instinctively curl my hand into a tight fist, but I relaxed and took a deep breath. There was no way that he was going to start shit again, especially after it had just been released that he was done with MWE. Basically, he wasn’t my favorite person at the moment because I felt abandoned yet again. We had shit between us, but the GWC bond had always been there and he had bailed. I shouldn’t have let him in, but I did. “Honestly Kevin, tell me one reason I shouldn’t cave your fucking head in right now. I shouldn’t even be talkin to you yet here you are.” “Nothing you say can make me feel any worse about the shit that’s happened, but if it makes you feel better then by all means just swing away.” I stood there, part of me screaming to knock his head clear off his shoulders…but I couldn’t do it. Even after all the blood we shed, the tears we cried, and the battles we waged, he was still a brother. Maybe that’s why it hurt so much to see him bail. Maybe that’s why instead of pure hatred, I actually felt pity for the guy. He had been through hell and had taken it better than most. Even though I wanted to slam the door in his face and laugh at how far he had fallen, I couldn’t. All I could do was step aside, and let him in. He sat on the couch, and I didn’t move. The silence said more than any words could have and I could tell he was struggling with something. There was only one question I had, but I didn’t really want to ask it. It had to be done though, so I moved a bit closer before speaking the question that wasn’t only on my mind, but CSB’s, Zack Crash’s, and every man, woman, and child ever associated with GWC. “Why man, why now? After everything that’s happened, why the fuck did you have to bail on us now?” As his eyes met mine, I could tell this wasn’t the same Kevin Hardaway I had spent the past two years battling with. This wasn’t the confident, cocky superstar that people loved to hate. Sitting before me was a broken man, a shell of what he used to be. It killed me inside to see him like this, and it took everything I had to keep eye contact. “I can’t sit here and act like you shouldn’t hate me, because I hate me right now. Shit’s gotten worse the past few months, and I’m surprised I made it this long. Dealing with bi-polar depression while watching your life crumble isn’t exactly and easy thing.” There it was, the root of everything. Kevin wasn’t just a stereotypical jackass like most people thought. He wasn’t just a dick for the sake of being a dick. He had shit that was wrong with him and that he held inside for god knows how long. I didn’t expect this to happen, not to Kevin. Even with everything that had happened, he had still always been the one who seemed invincible. He was the guy who could bounce back from anything, yet here he was sitting on a shitty hotel couch looking like he hadn’t slept in days. I don’t know why, but I just couldn’t find the right words to say. It seemed like he could tell things were taking an awkward turn, so he stood up and looked at me with the most hollowed out, sunken eyes I had ever seen. It looked like he had gone on a three week smoking binge, and had it been anyone else I would have toked up right then and there. “Man, I had no idea…I know I’m probably not the best person to help with this, but is there anything I can do to help?” Yeah, because he was really going to turn to me for help. We had damn near killed each other, and now I was offering to help him. What could I do though? I couldn’t offer therapy, it wasn’t like he needed money, and I sure as hell wasn’t the person he wanted to sit around and spill his feelings to. He thought hard for a moment, before looking at me square in the eyes. “Win. Show why GWC deserves to be mentioned in the great companies. Be the man I couldn’t be, and stand up for everything we believe in. Fight tooth and nail, make those fuckers bleed, and do some fucking work. Plain and simple, do what I can’t and Man Up.” He put his hand on my shoulder, and I returned the gesture. It was at this moment that I knew what had to be done. I couldn’t just fight for myself, and the GWC name. I had to do this for Kevin. He was going to be vilified by everyone in the damn company, and there is a fair reason for that. But nobody else was going to see the Kevin that I saw that night. Nobody else was going to know just exactly how he was being torn apart by his decision. The silence between us spoke volumes, and it was just the motivation I needed. There was no need for anymore words, and he just walked out of the room, shutting the door behind him. I sat back on the couch, staring at the TV where the demo had started up yet again. I looked at the coffee table, and noticed the camera sitting there, untouched in weeks. There wasn’t going to be a better chance to say what needed to be said, so with a flip of the switch, I began to speak. “Now I don’t have a witty entrance phrase like CSB does, I don’t have the lavish settings like some of the other superstars…but I do have one thing that most people lack. I have a realness about me that is like a lost art form in this business. I won’t sit here and put on a fake smile like some people will, I won’t act happy when I’m not. This past month has been hell for me, and you people deserve to know the truth. I haven’t been myself lately, and there are reasons for that which will remain to myself. All you need to know, is that I am here to stay, and I won’t be going anywhere anytime soon. I won’t bail like Kevin did, and anyone who thinks otherwise can come find me and I’ll prove just how loyal I am." Loyalty, the biggest thing in this match. It’s kind of ironic, that even after all these months, a common enemy like Danny Vice can bring together a group of people who can’t really stand each other. I cock my head to the side, questioning the very word. “Yes, I said loyal, which is something that has been doubted for awhile. People saw what happened with me and Kevin, yet they acted like I turned my back on him. They act like my loyalty wavered, even when it was quite obvious that I did the right thing the entire time. Look at it now, He has packed his bags and moved on, turning his back on the very fucking company that made him what he is. I shouldn’t really be all that surprised after what happened with us, but I still thought this common bond was stronger than this. Hell, I can’t fucking stand the Virus, but I know that we have a common goal to fight towards and I’ve accepted that. Yet Kevin can just walk away without a second glance." I should feel bad for throwing him under the bus, considering what I know, but fuck it. GWC means far too much to me, and the more I think about it the more pissed I get. I don’t let the anger show though, gotta keep it PG-13 for now. “All Kevin did was prove every single thing I said about him the past few months right. He showed that he couldn’t be trusted, and that when the going got tough, his ass got going. Maybe he finally realized he was past his prime. Maybe he did what Sinc, DC, and the rest of the vets couldn’t do and accepted the fact that his time was done. Or maybe, just maybe, he knew that he would be out shadowed." Complimenting my opponents for the week? While I might be facing them this week, come the Pay-Per-View they would be my teammates and I needed to be confident. “I said it before, and I’ll say it again…the people you see on team GWC, the people who hold EVERY SINGLE FUCKING TITLE in the damn company, are the future of this business. They have done what EVERYONE said they couldn’t, and shown why they are the best. Virus beat me fair and square, as bitter as I might be about it. Zack Crash took out Oblivion, and continued his winning streak like the monster that he is. And CSB, my friend, former drinking buddy, and newest father, showed why he is the only man to ever hold both the GWC and MWE World title. To put it simply, we are pretty fucking sweet. I think Kevin realized that, and knew he couldn’t hold a candle to us. But enough about Kevin…he isn’t in the match anymore, he isn’t the man standing next to me this week, and he doesn’t deserve another word. Instead, I get to fight alongside another man who I took out right after I was done with Kevin.” To talk shit about my “partner”, or to be nice…decisions decisions. On one hand, dysfuctionality could hurt us…but if we are both in it to win it, it could help us. I kicked my feet up on the table, a bit more relaxed now that I was done talking about Kevin. “D fucking C, I have to say I was shocked to hear that they had named you as Kevin’s replacement. All that talk about me leading a revolution, how you had some sort of twisted respect for me…maybe it wasn’t all bullshit. Maybe you truly meant what you said inside that boardroom, and if that’s the case then this just might work out. Then again, you are also part of Team MWE, which makes me wonder. Did Eli and the board put you in this match to scout us just a bit more? Are you going to pull a Kevin and bail on me in the middle of the match with hopes of a beat down? I suppose I just have to trust you, which is hard considering your track record. What choice do I really have though? I can’t really afford another loss, even if it is to Crash Saint Blaise. The rest of your team already sees me as the weakest link on the GWC team, and hell you might even see it that way too. This match is my chance to prove myself, and I will do it with or without you. If this is some sort of plot, some sort of plan to weaken our team even moreso than Kevin did then just stay in the back. I’d rather face them two on one than have a teammate who is just out to sabotage me. You had all of those great things to say about me, so it’s time to see if you meant any of it." Enough about him, he isn’t who I have to beat this weak. I’ve already proven that I am better than him, and if he turns his back on me it will be no big surprise. I take a moment to reflect on the upcoming match, lighting a bowl as my thoughts swirl like the smoke down the chamber. I exhale slowly, letting the sweet scent fill the room. Too bad you cant record smell, or this would be much more enjoyable for you all. "Now, as for the two men standing across from me at Mayhem…I would be lying if I said I was looking forward to this match. Two men, both who have beaten me in the past three months, standing as a unified team while I’m stuck in an IWC mark’s wet dream of a tag team. One man is like a brother to me, the other is someone I just can’t grasp. One man made up part of the heart and soul of GWC towards its end, the other is only on the team due to some history with the biggest joke of GWC not named Lee Rivvy. Chris, you know I would go to war for you any day of the week. I was there when you had your trial, I was there when you needed me and I know you would do the same. This isn’t a match I want, but it has to happen. I can’t sit here and talk shit about you, but I can say this with 100% confidence…you will be underestimating me come Mayhem. Hell, I’m already doubting myself so why wouldn’t you? First I compliment the oponnent, then I tear down my partner…now I’m sitting here doubting myself? I must be baked out of my mind, or just bat shit crazy. Either way, Once I start talking you know I don’t stop. Even if it means I have to go against yet another best friend. I said in the beginning of this thing that I haven’t been the same. The real Zak Warner would be holding the US title in my arms right now, cocky as can be. Yet the past three months have been hell. You beat me fair and square and it propelled you to the top of the company. That loss sent me on a downward spiral, culminating with the loss to Sinc two weeks ago. We are two totally different men then when we started in this company…kinda funny isn’t it? You came in as the fresh face, no outstanding expectations and yet here you are as the top dog in the company. I come in with all the fanfare and hype, and fall short and become the laughingstock of the business. There is absolutely no logical reason why I should win this match. Logic…it’s one of those words, like loyal, that hold different meaning to different people. Those who are logical tend to see in black and white, while people like me tend to see in Technicolor. Logic is a funny thing, depending on how you look at it. Yet when have I ever been logical? Was it logical that me of all people, would be the first and only grand slam champion in GWC history? Was it logical that I would beat Sonny Williams in my third match here and hold MWE gold? Was it fucking logical that I would overcome Kevin and finally write that chapter in my career? Logic doesn’t seem to apply to my life just like it doesn’t apply to yours. You weren’t supposed to win the GWC title, yet you did. You shouldn’t have your son right now, but thank the lord you do. GM was supposed to walk out with the title, yet he didn’t. You are supposed to beat me this week, but you won’t. You see Chris, it all makes sense. Logically, being illogical is the only way we get through life. If logic dictated the way our lives went, we would both be out of a job, banging one legged whores for a turkey sandwich. We are both fighting for something bigger than ourselves Chris. You are fighting for Cian, for everything that little bundle of Irish joy stands for. I’m fighting for GWC, for the people who are watching wondering where we all went. You were a big part of GWC I will admit that, but you don’t know what it was like to be there as long as I was. I was there when GWC should have folded and been done with. I was there when people wrote us off as a D-list show. I helped build that company up to the powerhouse that it was when you joined. Without me, there would be no GWC. Logcially, without me there would be no Chris Saint Blaise in MWE…yet logic defies us both doesn’t it?" I take another hit, and hope that my words sink in. Is it possible to trash talk someone by complimenting them? I sure as hell hope so, or I’m going to come off as a complete kiss-ass. "Zack Crash…the wildcard of Team GWC. I’ll admit, I had no fucking clue why you were put on this team, then it was revealed that you knew JT Hunt. Right away, that makes you fucking sketchy in my book. That man was the definition of mediocrity and the fact you have history with him worries me. Yet your record here in MWE shows otherwise. By all means, I should be glad to have you on my team…but truth be told, I’m not. This is Team GWC, not Team GWC and a guy who may or may not have slept with a former superstar. You are a friend of CSB’s, and if it wasn’t for that I wouldn’t trust you in the slightest. He is vouching for you though, so I have to take his word. I know you won’t stab me in the back…but I don’t know how much heart you have. Is this just another paycheck for you? Are you just fighting with us to make a bigger name for yourself? When it comes to skill, you have very few equals. You beat me, just like CSB did, and I can’t hold that against you. But can lightning strike twice, even for a man as extreme as you?" Ha, extreme…another one of those funny words, just like loyalty and logic. Notice a common theme in this promo? Because I sure as hell do. Maybe it’s just the weed talking, but I think I’m making some damn good points. "You want to talk about extreme? You honestly think that just because you have fun jumping out of planes, that you can be considered extreme? Anyone can jump out of a plane Zack. Anyone can pull a string and glide down to the earth. But can anyone take a pistol whip with a stapler, then have the wound stapled shut? Can anyone take seven straight chair shots to the head and still walk out with a win? I know you THINK you can, but you couldn’t. You are the X-TV champ but you haven’t shown what being extreme really means. Extreme is putting your life and well-being on the line for the sake of personal gain. It’s doing things that no normal man would do, all for the sake of a win. You want extreme? Go look at when I won my first ever professional wrestling title in EPW. I knocked a man out with a goddamned baseball bat without a second thought. I was thrown through glass, slammed through tacks, and even driven through the fucking RING yet I still walked out victorious. Go look at old GWC footage. Look at the first ever Dark Crusade match where I sacrificed a title shot for the sake of furthering my career. I took a 30 foot plunge through a barbed wire table, because I knew it would get me over. I have done things that would probably kill an ordinary man, and never once have I called myself “extreme” until now. I normally hate that word, but to see you throw it around like its something you earned makes me sick. People like myself and CSB made our living on being extreme, and you are a disgrace to that moniker." If the fire in my voice doesn’t show how serious I take this, the fire in my eyes sure as hell do. GWC is my heart and soul, and I don’t want ANYONE trying to take that away from me. "You are going to be my teammate come the 10 man match, but that doesn’t mean I have to like you. It doesn’t mean that I have to trust you, or have any faith whatsoever. All it means is that I won’t be kicking your ass that week. This week though, you are all mine and I WILL have my retribution. If there is any doubt about how I feel about this business, if you have any questions about my heart and desire they will be answered this week. Not only will this serve as a reminder to the rest of my team, it’s going to be a notice to Sinc, GM, Eric Derf, DC, and whoever the fifth member of the team turns out to be. Whether it be Alexis Black, Madison Senton, or anyone else who thinks they have what it takes to step up…they will be put on blast. They will see the Warner of old, the one who beat Sonny Williams, the man who beat Kevin Hardaway. MWE has been going through a withdrawal as of late, and it’s time I fix that. So shoot up my addicts, for this week I will give you all the fix you have been craving. I will provide that relief that you so desire. This week, your cravings will be satisfied and you will once again experience the bliss that you all need." With a simple flick of the switch, the camera clicks off and I can once again relax and enjoy some mindless video games. Within minutes, I’m sniping, fragging, and all-around owning. For the first time in a while, I can honestly say that it is good to be me.
