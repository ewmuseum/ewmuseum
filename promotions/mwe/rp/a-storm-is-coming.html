A Storm is Coming; MNM RP

May 8 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A loud wail could be heard. That is what woke up a man. Of course everybody who has seen or met this one particular man, knew otherwise, that he was beyond a human being. But what was this man exactly? Was he a physical specimen? Was he an assassin, or a vigilante? Was he deformed? Did he possess supernatural powers. Most of those fact are true. He isn't in any way shape deformed. No. He was a 6'6 powerhouse, god-esque man. But he's probably kill you for calling him that. This is non other then one of the single, most craziest of men to ever walk the mortal existence. He is one person, who while is very well liked, and is easy to get along with, he is also the last person you ever want to piss off, because he will kill you, and he will do so in a way to send a message. For this man, is the Devil's incarnate on earth, Leon 'The Virus' Roberts. But you wouldn't think he was a bad person, for what he was doing right now. See, he had in his arms, one of his three newborn children. This one, was Courtney Roberts. The newborn calmed down, before being passed to her mother, Demonica. Demonica: Looks like this one's hungry. Leon sighed. Like most new fathers, he had no idea what the newborns could tell him. While this might be his first official experience as being a new born father, he already did have a five year old daughter, which until a few months ago, he knew nothing about. While Demonica was feeding Kieth, his two other newborns, Kieth and Anthony both woke up. He carefully picked them both up, and gentle rocked them. Demonica: Amazing. I honestly still have a hard time imagining this gentler side of you Leon. It's....really going to take me a while to get used to. Hopefully one day, you can be who you used to be again, one hundred percent of the time. Leon: Well if it’s any assurance, I plan to rip my opponents to shreds this week. You and I both know both know I will, because I can, and I love it. Demonica: At least that aspect of you will never change darling. If it did, I don't think I'd be able to recognize you. Leon: Yeah, I wouldn't be able to look in the mirror and see myself like that either. But to think, all it took was fourteen bullets and a blessing from the previous devil for me to become who I am. Demonica and Leon carried their children to the living room. While Demonica sat down, still feeding Courtney, Leon went to get 2 bottles from the kitchen, and warmed them up. It would take a couple minutes for them to get ready. Once they were, he did feed them their bottles. Once the three were done, they were placed in their living room cribs. It was then, they heard running footsteps from his five year old daughter, Liberty. Liberty: Hey guys. Liberty ran into the kitchen, unaware of the amount of noise she was making, as she fix herself some breakfast. Leon was thankful that the newborns were a bit awake, so they weren't to disturbed by Liberty's hyperactive nature. Leon: Lib, think you could tone it down just a little bit? I don't think that your siblings would appreciate it if you woken them up. Liberty stopped eating for a second, and gave her father a semi-disgusted look Liberty: Whatever pops. Those three crappers have been keeping me awake since they got here. I'm a little riled up due to that. With that being said, Leon and Demonica was surprised at Liberty. Leon then stood up, and walked over to the dining room table. He glared at Liberty, his golden colored eyes starting to change into blood red. Leon: Well, I can understand that, but do you have to talk to me with that attitude? Liberty: Yes I do. It may have been months ago, but I am still non to pleased on what happened to me. Leon: .......Liberty. WHAT THE FUCK HAVE I SAID ABOUT THAT INCIDENT?!?!?!?!?! Liberty snorted at her father's behavior. Her eyes to, changed color, and she smirked at him. Liberty: To never talk about it. Whatever. Say dad, can you drive me to school today? Leon sighed. He nodded. He needed to go out today anyways. Two hours later.......... Leon was outside in the forest that was behind his house. He was wielding his crystal blade katana, slicing up random things, while smoking a cigar. He smirked. Leon: Hmph. I’m sure that despite Michaels’s current behavior, he must be afraid. He must have recalled just how close I was to crippling him the last time. Well this time, I’m gonna personally shut his rapper-wannabe ass up. I mean seriously, where the fuck is that fucken attitude coming from. Just because he’s sucking off the champ’s cock, he thinks that he’s the shit again. Well him sucking off the champ’s cock is the closest he’ll ever be to that title. Suddenly, his cell phone rang. Leon sighed. He looked at the caller, and smirked. It was Demonica. Leon: Hey sug. Demonica: Everything is ready. I suggest you leave now. Vega has been calling here non stop, wanting to know when the fuck you’re gonna arrive. Leon: Yeah, I’ll be right there. And you can tell Vega the next he calls, to tell him to shut the fuck up before I have to plant my foot up his ass. Leon was then engulfed n black flames. It was his way of teleportation. He re-appeared infront of Demonica. Demonica: There is something I want to show you Leon. Leon: Oh, really? Demonica: Yeah. There is a big pile covered in a white sheet behind Demonica. He takes the sheet off, and reveals there are a bunch of Brian Michaels merchandise there. Leon: Demonica. I'm very disappointed in you. Demonica: Well truth be honest, after I remembered that he tried to feel me up one time... Demonica sneers at that thought. She can recall when Brian Michaels tried to hit on her. She had just discovered she was pregnant. Luckily, she did teach Michaels to not fuck with her, as she tossed him through a few windows. She smiled when she recalled that bit. Demonica: I was gonna set them on fire. Every single one of them. Leon: Oh. All of a Sudden, Demonica pukes all over the merchandise. Leon is both amused and disgusted by this sudden action. Leon: Ummmmmmm........... Demonica: I guess that's how I really felt about them. Leon: *chuckles* Yeah. Here, let me. Leon got into a sort of fighting stance. His right pal was open, as he gathered his magic into his palm, creating a spark of lightning. He then thrust it forward, which caused the spark to turn into a massive stream of lightning. It struck a lot of the books, instantly lighting them aflame. Soon enough, the entire pile was turned into ashes. Leon had a sinister smile upon his face. Demonica: That may have cost me a cool grand, but it was so worth it just to see this. Leon: Yeah. Demonica: You know, I think he's hiding the fact he's lost his manhood a long time ago. I say this with the way he constantly has to hang around the reigning champion, plus having those two jobbers personally kiss his ass. Leon: Yeah. Instead of going out of his way to spew whatever nonsense his mouth can say, he should instead actually focus on growing a pair of balls and actually become something, because it’s come to the point where hardly anybody can take him seriously. Without management in his back pocket, he will never be able to do shit anymore. Demonica: Yeah. Anyways, good luck. Leon: Thanks. Anyways, I better go. Leon kissed Demonica, before he went to the garage. He got on his motorcycle, which had all of his bags packed. He fired it up, and sped off. When he was a safe distance away, he opened up a portal, which lead to the actual highway to hell. He sped along it, smiling the entire time as he heard the all to familiar moans and screams of the corpses that lined the highway. A lot of them, he personally put there himself. Eventually, the scene changed to another forest. But this was outside the city he was wrestling at. After one more hour, he arrived, and checked in. After putting his luggage in his room, he called up Vega. Vega answered after a minute. Vega: Yo Leon. Where the fuck are you at? Leon: I’m here now. I took my way. Didn’t feel like wasting cash on a jet, plus the kids had a bit of a cold. Vega: Ah. Well then it makes sense that you wanted to see them get better. Well you ready to whoop some ass? Leon: Vega. This is me you’re talking to. I am a master ass kicker. I was born to do this. But....I sense a storm approaching. Vega: Really? Well the weather looks perfectly fine. Leon: Not that kind of storm. The kind of storm where blood will be the form of rain, and it will hail bone shards. It all depends on what happens this week. Vega: Oh really? Do tell. Leon: Well I’m still pursuing my mission to humiliate and destroy all the top talents in MWE. It is one of the next steps of throwing this world into anarchy, although I must admit it’s the first one I’m taking. The first four, simply happened. Vega: What were they? Leon: It goes back even before I became the devil. It started back in 2001 when the twin towers were hit. Then, Bush declaring war on terrorism. A war that was supposed to be swift, but has turned out to be a huge fiasco. Then there is the economy collapse and the swine flu, which have both recently happened. The four events are affecting the entire world in ways people can’t imagine. It’s making all the mortals insecure, and desperate. All I have to do, is raise more hell, and keep raising it. All the wrestling fans in the world, they want me to be the MWE champion, as they want to see just what I’ll do. Which is why the first sacrifice must be Brian Michaels, as he’s one of the original MWE Superstars of supposed greatness. But we all know why. Vega: Yes. How he was damn near handed everything. No matter what he will claim, people will always bring that point up. And also the fact he almost fucked a guy. Leon and Vega burst out laughing at that joke, as it was very much true. They recalled that Michael’s former manager, Barbie McQueen was actually a guy. Leon: Well regardless, I’m counting on both of you, and Resoan, to get the win. I’ll do my part, but you guys have to help me in the first step on brining MWE under my rule. Vega: Yeah. Say before we destroy most of the Mayhem Mafia, we should get some drinks. Leon: Totally later man. Leon hung up his cell phone and tossed it on the bed. He smirked. He was mentally prepared for his match, and his bloodlust was starting to go haywire again. But this time, he was going to let it run loose.
