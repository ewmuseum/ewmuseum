Well, Hurry up, I wanna see this! The second voice is obviously that of Eric Derf, and as the scene opens, we're in the now familiar room that is Eric Derf's apartment. Large 37" LCTV hangs on the far wall, a large window with a great view of the buildings outside, and a collection of many different empty liquor bottles lines a shelf in his room. And sitting on the large red leather sectional couch (which he just bought with his Crowning Glory bonuses) is Eric Derf himself, dressed in baige shorts and a red t-shirt. He must be working pretty hard on something, or at least he was up to this point. Eric looks at the letter for a moment before walking back towards the couch, sitting down, and opening it. Josh follows and looks over his sholder as he reads the letter alloud. Eric keeps looking at the letter, probably re-reading it in his mind. Dozens of questions flowing around, the most obvious one is, Who is Katie? I don't know any Katies... And she might be cute. And who knows, it's been forever since you've been with a woman. Eric snaps a glare at Josh. Dude, I told you about that Snow White chick at Disneyville! Yeah right, you slept with a Disney Princess. I don't believe that for a second! Unless you get me proof, I won't believe it. Eric is already ignoring Josh as he continues to stare at the letter, wondering who "Katie" is. Maybe it's just a crazy fan. Eric, I think you should meet this person. Just say Fuck it, if she's weird or crazy, just come back here. And wear a bulletproof vest when you meet her. Eric can't help but chuckle at this, and that's when he realized something. Eric, this video could take a little while... So, you're going to go for it then with this "blind date of destiny"?
