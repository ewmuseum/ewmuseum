A Sitdown with Ace Cannon

Nov 2 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
** The camera opens up and we see Damien Thorpe sitting in a director’s chair. He is dressed in a dark suit with a blue shirt and red tie finishing the ensemble. In Damien Thorpe’s lap sits a clipboard he looks at it then a bright light hits him and he looks back at the camera and begins to speak.** Damien: Hello MWE.Com. I am joined today by Ace Cannon for an exclusive interview. **The camera pulls out from the tight shot on Damien and to a wide shot of both Damien and Ace. Ace is sitting in his director’s chair dressed in a black Affliction T-shirt, blue Affliction jeans, a pair of black shoes on his feet and black Oakley sunglasses covering his face. Ace nods at the camera.** Damien: Well first things first Mr. Cannon, where have you been for the better half of the last 4 months? ** Ace looks over at Thrope.** Ace: Well, I was in a company called Revolution Pro. It was a rather forgettable experience and I put that company on my back and carried it. I was putting people over and elevating there careers but I never got my just due from the owner George Prax. Then finally one day I decided to leave and four days after there biggest star left the company closed it’s doors for good. Damien: Well didn’t they have any other stars there to hold up the company? **Ace is sitting there laughing a little bit.** Ace: They had Jeff “THK” Jordan. A guy I made, a guy I wanted to push but once I left and the company was on his back he crumbled like a two dollar folding chair under a five hundred pound man. Damien: Joey Dallas was in Revolution Pro, correct? Ace: Correct. Damien: Why did Dallas jump ship? Ace: He is riding my coattails. It was my idea to bring him to Rev~Pro and when he got word that I walked out of there he was on the phone calling my father, Richard Covell and negotiating a deal for himself. Damien: Does that anger you? Ace: No, I make more money then him and I am the better wrestler then he is. Damien: Then why do you think he came over here? Ace: He knows that when Ace Cannon is apart of a company that something big is going to happen. He knows that with me being here ratings go up and due to that it raises his level of exposure. Damien: Another hot topic is something that made ripples through out the wrestling world and that is none other then you kicking a child in the head. What is the back story on that? Ace: Well, first let me say right now that me kicking a child in the head was pure payback to a wrestler named Virus. You see me and Virus have a history that dates all the way back to another company called Enigmar Wrestling. Now, while in Enigmar when I was there champion Virus came out and savagely attacked me. He did a bunch of underhanded bullsh*t. So before I got my payback EW went out of business, but our paths crossed again in Rev~Pro in a cage match for a shot at the world title. So during that match kicked the ever loving sh*t out of eachother and after it was said and done I was the winner. Now, while Virus laid in the ring knocked out cold his little daughter scrambled into the ring to check on her poor little daddy. So as that little girl checked on her poor father I sized her up, ran across the ring and BOOM! Kicked he right square in her ugly little mug! Damien: A bit controversial and over the top wouldn’t you think? Ace: Maybe so, but as the old adage goes…. PAYBACK IS A BITC*! Damien: Now another big question is what made you want to comeback too MWE? Ace: Well, when someone says MWE what comes to mind Damien? Damien: A paycheck? Ace: No, jackass! When the name MWE is said people automatically think great wrestling and what name is synonymous with great wrestling? **Damien scratching his head and thinking.** Damien: Ummm….DC? Ace: No! Damien: Uh….. Brian Michaels? Ace: No! Damien: Well what is it then? Ace: Ace Cannon is synonymous with great wrestling and that’s why he came to MWE. Damien: A lot of smart marks are saying that it is because your father owns the company and gave you a big contract. Ace: That’s a load of crap. I don’t care about the money, I have plenty of that in the bank! I came here for the simple fact that I wanted to compete I wanted to solidify my legacy and win a world championship here in the greatest wrestling company in the world. Damien: Last time you were here there was a lot of personal heat with you and a lot of the guys in the locker room. What was up with that? Ace: Well, first off there wasn’t a lot of heat with me and the locker room. Secondly, me and the heat you have mentioned is was basically between me and DC. Now, that stuffs in the past and we made cool when we were in EW. Damien: Before we talk about your match on MNM how do you feel about the upcoming draft that will take place on the show? Is there a particular brand you want to go too? Ace: I am not sweating this draft what so evere. If I am drafted to Mayhem then I can boast the ratings on the flagship show. If I go to Genesis then I can build them into the better brand. Either way it is a win win situation for which ever brand selects me. Damien: Now in your first match back it is three on four handicap match. It will be you, Mason Resoan, Alejandro Rodriguez and Joey Dallas on one team. What are your feelings on your teammates? Ace: Well I kinda remember Mason Resoan and Alejandro from MWE. There both very talented guys and it is honor to work with them, but to tell you the God’s honest truth they should feel more honored to be working with me. As for Joey Dallas I already stated that he rides my coattails. Damien: Now onto your opponents the first of which is DC what are your thought and feelings on them? Ace: Well, I know DC. Like it was talked about me and him have pretty much squashed our beef. We had quite a few good matches against one another in EW and they were all epic, but when all is said and done I am still better then DC on my worst day. Damien: What about David Whitman? Ace: The MWE legend David Whitman. Our paths have never crossed anywhere in MWE at anytime. When I was apart of the company he wasn’t but now our paths will cross and I am excited about this because all I have ever heard is David Whitman this and David Whitman that and to tell you’re the truth it made me sick to my stomach! So when our paths do cross I will show him up and everyone will then say….. “David who?” Damien: Now what about your brother, Brian? Ace: What about Brian? Brian does nothing but suck-up to our father. He is so far up our dad’s ass that at Christmas time all I ever see of Brian is him hanging out of dads ass. It is ridiculous to tell you the truth. Hell, I hope in his feud with DC he gets killed because all he is milking my dad for is future control and money from this company and it is obvious. Damien: What about people who say he is the better of the Covell brothers? Ace: There full of dog sh*t. He may have more titles and all that kind of stuff but that’s because Brian is a world class ass kissing suck-up. So when we step into the squared circle and me and him get a chance to lock-up I will show everyone which Covell is the better of the two. Damien: You had some strong words for all three men. Do you have any reservations about that? Ace: Not at all. I can back up the sh*t I talk plus some. Damien: With that sad do you have any final thoughts? ** Ace lefts his sunglasses and puts them on top of his head and looks into the camera.** Ace: Now that I am in MWE the tide is turning. The Cannon Era has begun and when I step into the ring on Monday Night Mayhem I will prove to the entire world why I’m the best wrestler in MWE! Damien: Well there you have it folks! Tune into Monday Night Mayhem on November 10th and see Ace Cannon make his MWE in-ring debut and find out which brand all your favorite MWE superstars are drafted too! **The camera fades out.**
