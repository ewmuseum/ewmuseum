Big Mistake

Aug 13 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
David Renshaw marches towards the ring with a serious look on his face, he isn't riding on his skateboard but instead he is holding it in his hand, he approaches the ring apron and tosses the skateboard in and rolls in to the ring under the bottom rope, he looks out at the crowd and his expression changes to that of a pleasant one as he raises his hand triumphantly and makes a peace sign, he walks over to the edge of the ring and leans over the top rope while motioning for a microphone “You may be wondering why I have decided to enter the ring in this way, after all this isn't normally the way I enter the ring now is it?, now I will explain that later, don't worry about it but what I really want to talk about now is what happened to me last week, as you all saw, I won my debut match here in the MWE” The crowd cheers with a deafening roar praising David's initial success but they are cut off by David as he begins to talk again “Now don't go all hooping and hollering and all that fancy stuff just yet, you see, I won, but I didn't win by pinning, I didn't earn the victory, I didn't get satisfied, instead what I got, was my motherfucking skateboard smashed across my head, that's right I got a disqualification win due to the fact that two idiots were two scared to face me and my partner and had to resort to using weapons like the absolute pussies that they are” David grins because he knew he was right, although the crowd have a mixed reaction there is a majority of cheering David sighs and continues speaking nonetheless “However, I still won, I am still undefeated and I am damn sure to keep it that way and you guys will be helping me stay undefeated for as long as possible because with the support of my fans, I will destroy any and all opposition that comes in my way" The crowd explode in to a cheer as David is praising them, David then points at each and every person at the crowd which causes a Mexican wave to be started by the audience to the speed of David's pointing “Now what I understand is my opponent is some guy called Jesus Cross? Now, now who remembers the last Monday night mayhem, I of course won, but Jesus Cruz my god he fucked up didn't he? David starts laughing wholeheartedly and so does the rest of the crowd apart from those faithful to Jesus Cruz who begin to boo him, David is taken aback by the boos “Jesus cruz fans in the hizouse lets raise the ceiling hoot hoot, how drunk are you guys honestly, the dude, got too fucking arrogant, you slipped out of a finishing move big fucking whoop dude but you forgot one thing, in a triple threat there are 3 people, always watch your back dude, what are you gonna do next? Celebrate getting a two count?” The crowd laughes at the joke as David rests on the ring ropes while grinning as he begins to speak again “You know Jesus, you're gonna pray to your father...” David grins at his cheeky joke “See what I did there?, anyway, you're gonna pray to him, because guess what dude?” Almost instantly. his facial expression changes in to one of anger and all his muscles tense up as his rage emanates from his body like an evil aura “You got matched up with me on the wrong fucking day, you see I didn't get to unleash the full potential of my awesome power against those two retards because they were scared of me” David gets a menacing look in his eyes as he bares his teeth “And you know? I guess they were right to be, but you see, that skateboard to the head and being knocked out? It made an unbareable rage build up inside me, its like I couldn't think straight the anger was clouding my judgement and to tell you the truth, it still is, so guess what Jesus Cruz, you just hit the pain jackpot man, when I am done with your mangled body you're gonna wish I was still out cold from that skateboard shot” David walks over to the skateboard, picks it up and looks at it for a moment “You know for a little piece of wood, this hurts a lot, well I guess its time for me to hurt it” David holds the skateboard in one hand and punches it with another causing it to break off in to two pieces as David smiles and looks out at the crowd “Anyone want a piece of history, this is the skateboard that knocked me out cold” The crowd goes wild as each and every audience member fighting for a chance to own the the broken remains of the sacred skateboard, David throws the pieces with the skill and aim of a professional discus thrower, however one piece lands on an audience members head and he is out cold and David winces “Got wood?, sorry there man, but look on the bright side, now you have experienced what I experienced last week” David flashes a quick grin at the audience and begins pacing inside the ring before he continues speaking “As many of you, should know, I am fairly new to this company but you must understand that does in no way mean I am inexperienced at all, you see, many people think they are better than me just because they have been here longer, I mean if you have a wrestler who has been losing for 12 months or a wrestling who has been winning for 12 hours, which would you really support, quite frankly and I am not really tooting my own horn here, but experience doesn't count for jack shit, Exhibit A Devlin Sharpe, probably the 5th longest member of MWE? And when was his last win last never?" David pauses for a moment and he thinks “Even in the short time you have managed to expose your own weaknesses, you have the attention span of a lemming and really that is no good because I can use that against you, I can destroy each and every fibre of your being I can rip off your skull and use it as a cup and I intend to do just that, last week I got screwed over but this week, all that rage and pent up aggression will be unleashed on to you as I pummel the shit out of you" David filled with Rage and aggresssion slams his fist against the top rope and becomes increasingly frustrated at the thought of his opponent “You know its people like Jesus Cruz that make me extremely angry people that act without thinking, honestly how do you think you can ever win again with that kind of foolish behaviour you know you make me sick, you make me physically sick that they would consider an idiot like you to be a MWE wrestler, you know I cant actually believe they let someone so irresponsible become a MWE wrestler? Truly mind boggling" David shakes his head and covers his face while smirking “You do know that my match was cut short before I could demonstrate my full capability and that's what you should be afraid of Jesus, fear of the unknown, no one really knows what I can do right, no one knows what happens when I win, no one knows HOW I win” David's eyes narrow and become almost likes slits on his face as he had a piercing glare “And that's exactly what you have to worry about dude, hell have you even seen my finishing move, do you know when I will strike, do you know how to counter it, the answer to all these questions is of course no, HOWEVER I have you well scouted my friend, you see unlike me, you have actual matches that I can watch, actual matches that will reveal your weaknesses to me, which I will use to help me in my victory” The crowd is in hushed silence as everyone is confused as David gestures to his fans while sighing “And of course, with my millions of fans behind me, I will crush, destroy and humiliate Jesus Cruz and make sure to send a clear and concise message to anyone in the MWE that you don't fuck with me and if you do, well you better be prepared to back it up or face a whole world of pain and misery which will have permanent effects on the way you live the rest of your miserable little life, the pain and anguish reminding you each and every day that you shouldn't have crossed my path, that you shouldn't have angered me” The crowd explodes in to cheer however after a few moments David silences them and begins talking again “But those warnings, would be a tad too late because all is already said and done, I have already beaten you, I have caused a shower of your blood to rain down like droplets on the canvas of your life, I win, you lose, your body is lying on the mat in a lifeless state as I look on to the crowd cheering my name, applauding me, congratulating me, for utterly and convincingly destroying you, because you made the biggest mistake of your life, the mistake of thinking you can ever hope to defeat me, the mistake you will regret for the rest of your life”
