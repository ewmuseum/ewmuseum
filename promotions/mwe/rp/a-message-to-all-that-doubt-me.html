A Message to all that Doubt Me

Sep 4 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The camera begins to fade into image where it is seen to be a dark and husky room. A steel chair is faintly seen as a figure is in it, the figure of what seems to be a person. A string is seen hanging from above the chair as the person slowly moves up to pull it down. He does so as a light bulb brightens over the person who appears to be MWE Superstar and No.1 Contender for the MWE World Championship, Sonny Williams. He is wearing a white sleeveless shirt with a design of Sonny Williams on the front. He also has on long baggy blue jeans. Sonny has his head burried in his hands but slowly begins to move them up as he is aware of the camera. Sonny Williams: I lost.. again. I let down some people.. again. People call me a screw up, others say I have lost my passion for wrestling. All I can say to that is, no. Yes, I may have not one mere win under my belt, and yes I have let down my brother Jordan but I have still got the heart inside of me. The names I have faced, they are some of which that will be remembered ten to twenty years on for what they have done. I may not like them, but I do feel as though my loses to them are much more valued than say a lose to someone like Alex Bowen or Devlin Sharpe. Sonny scratches his head and makes a small laugh as he turns away to the side away from the camera for a few moments. He turns back and has a more fierce face than opposed to when he turned away. He looks straight at the camera once again giving it his full and utter attention. Sonny Williams: I am the same man who walked into last weeks Monday Night Mayhem, I have the same insights, the same attitude... everything. But one thing that I have slightly changed inside of me is my dedication to this company. A wrestlers years can be limited, only few make it through for many, many years at the top of the ladder in full flight. As much as it hurts me deep down inside to say it, I may only have a year or so left in me if that is the case. I am going to cherish each win, each moment and every step I take into the ring much more than I did when I came to MWE. Sonny Williams: I am still the Main Man. I am still the ultimate destroyer of superstars that MWE has to offer. But from now on, I am going to be putting more effort into each match, each promo and whatever to make it clear to all my critics that I am still the same son of a bitch who beat Brian Michaels last year for the MWE World Championship. But of course I have a new and crude attitude to top it off. It all begins one week before a match that I want to win, that I gotta.. win. And it all begins to some freak, a clown, a weird, insane motherfucking physcopath, and his name is Jekyl. Sonny puts his hand on his chin and begins to rub it in a thinking type motion. He makes a small chuckle and then turns back to the camera with the light bulb over him merely showing his anger in his face. Sonny Williams: Jekyl, I have never, ever faced you one-on-one. I've tagged along side with you and you contributed to one of my four loses so far in MWE by being pinned by the Hall of Famer David Whitman. I don't like you. I may have done some very bad things in my lifetime but never anything as creepy and disturbing as you. You raped a girl on the night of your prom and stupidly blew up her next door neighbour's house instead of hers. You are an idiot, a pathetic loser. How you got signed to the MWE humbles me, it really does. Sonny Williams: Jekyl, I have beaten you to a pulp before where all that surrounded us was three ropes. This time what surrounds us is much more brutal and environmentally friendly.. for me. We are going to be surrounded by a steel cage, a structure born to brutally hurt the human body. Hurting is a sport for me, it's a factor that got me into this business. Don't be surprised to see that skill of mine lashed out on you. Sonny scratches his head and then re-directs back to the camera. Sonny Williams: I've got critics who want to see me fail. People who would just love to see me lose to you and give them bragging rights to critically bag me out constantly. I am going to prove each and every one of them absolutely wrong. I am going to win this match Jekyl. I am going to abolish your body that come Turmoil you will be still aching from the pain I bare out on you. You're not just going to experience your every day Sonny Williams, no, no way. I am going to make you swallow any of the teeth that remain in your mouth. There will be no escape for you. None what so ever. Sonny Williams: I have someone else to mention also... David Whitman. The man that is loved by every 8 year old around the world. He was always given more attention than me in Mayhem Mafia. ALWAYS! He beat me last week and I still feel the pain of that lose, not the hurt of his skills. David, I know I can beat you and at Turmoil I will do exactly that. I am not the fun loving man I was in Mayhem Mafia, so please do everyone a favor and get over it and move on. I will be the next MWE World Champion, so hold that title nice and tight and polish is every night so when I have it resting around my waist, you can know you did a good job in maiding it for me. Sonny Williams: So to all the people who can't trust me in this industry.. FUCK YOU! And to everyone who has critised me.. FUCK YOU! I will win at Monday Night Mayhem! I will win at Turmoil. And if you're not down with that, too bad. CHA CHING BITCH! The scene begins to fade into a black and distant picture as the promo ends. OOC: Not my best at all but it's something.
