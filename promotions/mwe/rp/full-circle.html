Full Circle; A Grand Mystique RP

Jan 4 2009

The Grand Mystique

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The hushed buzz around an Intensive Care Unit is unmistakeable. The one at Flushing Hospital Medical Center is no different. Nurses huddle around the bright central station of desks and talk in hushed whispers. The room would be silent if it was not for the beeping machines that are positioned by the beds which line the bleached white walls. Occasionally a visitor, a nurse or a doctor will come through the large double doors from the main ward, causing light to pour through from outside but it is normally kept quite dim. From the front of the large, open room, the camera moves to the back where there are a suite of isolated rooms. These are kept for the most seriously ill of patients who needs round the clock attention. This part of the room is even quieter; even with the constant and activity that goes on within, only the clipping noise of shoes spoil it. A nurse heads to the first room on the right, followed by a pair of nurse, one blond and one brunette and they both look younger than the bossy looking one in front of them. They all wear matching blue outfits but the older looking nurse looks significantly older. They all take care to wash their hands at the soap dispenser outside before going into the room. Nurse: “This patient is our biggest concern at this time.” She studies at the file as the blond nurse checks the machine displaying the pulse and blood pressure. “He came in three days ago.” She points to the right leg which is in plaster and lifted up in traction. “His leg and arm were both broken but have been fixed by Orthopaedics. The really serious injury is the throat. He has received serious damage to the trachea and windpipe. He has already had to go back to the OR twice because of recurrent bleeds. Therefore the medically induced coma continues until further notice.” The nurses carry out some checks on the patient’s dressing as the camera moves to the head of the bed. It moves to focus on one particular point on the wall above the frame of the bed. Written on a small whiteboard, in big black writing is the patients’ name. [Maxxamillion Mustafa] The camera moves back around to the older nurse who is in the frame of the door about to leave. She addresses her two colleagues. “As you may or may not be aware, Mr Mustafa is a public figure. As such any inquiries about his condition should be referred to myself in the first instance.” “He is allowed one visitor at a time. Normal rules apply. I believe his girlfriend is almost a constant presence at the moment.” * Richard Covell, sitting in his plush office at the MWE’s HQ in Chicago, has an agitated look on his face. He has just spoken with the orthopaedic specialist at Flushing Hospital Medical Center, Dr Mathers. Mathers is an old friend who treated Richard’s son Brian Michaels after DC’s attack last year. He had just advised Richard of the severity of Maxx’s injuries. The nature of these details had caused Covell to become pale. Covell knew that good sportsmen could return from a broken leg. He also knew Maxx was no ordinary competitor. But the severity of the injuries would almost certainly keep Maxx out of action for a serious amount of time, maybe even permanently. Reluctantly, Richard allowed the man who injured Maxx to enter his thoughts.. Although it sickened him, Covell knew GM had fulfilled his promise; that he had made a reverberating impact and that it would have far reaching repercussion beyond Adrenaline Rush. Suddenly the colour returned to his cheeks. Covell, as a man in his position should, had a great idea. * The Grand Mystique tended to be someone who found you, not the other way around. But Damien Thorpe was one of the only people to have a contact for him. Damien was sat in his New Jersey apartment, dressed casually in jogging trousers. He reached for his mobile and chose “GM” from the contact list. Three rings later. “Damien?” “Hey GM.....I have a request.” “Damien, I am not interested.” “Not from me.......from Richard Covell – he said to tell you that you don’t have a choice.” A pause; short enough for Damien to believe that GM is considering his response. “What is it Damien?” “An interview.” “OK” “Where?” ”I’ll let you know.” * Knowing that Richard Covell would want answers and had sent Damien on a mission to get them, GM knew that he would have to give them at some point. Short of Covell making his life hell which was a hassle GM didn’t want or need, GM knew he needed to figure this shit out and talk it over with someone. When Damien rang, GM was already on his way to the perfect place. GM was outside the IC unit at Flushing Hospital Medical Center. * GM steps through the double doors and begins to walk over to the middle. He nods politely to the nurses, who have no reason to consider him as anything other than a regular visitor and signs the visitor book at the desk. He steps around the desk and heads to the far side. He sees the isolated rooms at the back and heads into the room where Maxx is lying. The former EW Undisputed Champion is comatose, with his leg in traction, his throat heavily bandaged and his face severely bruised. GM pulls a chair from the side and sits it really close to the bed. He leans back and tries to relax, but shuffles uncomfortable. GM: “I hate hospitals.” GM stands up. GM: “How ya doing partner?” GM moves the chair away and looks at his prone former colleague. GM: “Not doing too well are ya?” GM shakes his head. GM: “This is what a pigheaded, holier than thou attitude does to you.” GM leans forward and places his palms on the edge of the bed. GM: “You were a weakness, a load that I had to carry and I hated every second of it.” He leans forward so his head is a little bit closer to Maxx’s. GM: “But you wanna know something......friend?” GM’s head is now right next to Maxx. GM (whispers): “I am fucking glad I got you out of the way.” GM springs back up to an upright position. He walks around to the end of the bed. GM: “I am really, really glad because you and your stubbornness held me back. I always have been the future. It’s just the old guard like you could never see it!” GM now walks to the other side of the room. With his hands on hips, he looks out the window. After a few seconds, he turns to face Maxx again. GM: “I am the new blood and I am ready to destroy the old guard that is continually playing swopsy with the World title. I started with you and I don’t care who is next.” GM steps closer to the bed again. “What happened to you was just a taster. I have ended you; I will end whoever decides to cross me” GM turns the machine that shows Maxx’s blood pressure and pulse to face him. He looks at it attentively, for a few seconds before looking at Maxx again. “They can look at you and learn from your experience. They can learn from your pain and know that this is what I do when I want something.” GM kneels down and rests his forearms on the bed. “I am going to continue making impacts and rewriting the future in MY favour. Not having you around is going to make it all that little bit easier!” GM stands up and brushes himself down. He gives Maxx one last, lingering look of evil revulsion as he walks to the door. “So long Maxx. It was your pleasure to be in the ring with me. To be carried by me. To bring me to the MWE. To see me go onto bigger and better things. Goodbye old man” And with that, GM leaves. He shuts the door behind him. Outside, he takes a deep breath before walking away. As he leaves the main door into the ICU, GM bumps into Chloe Cornett. * Chloe is looking beautiful as per usual, in a tight-fitting pink and black striped shirt and black pencil skirt. The outfit perfectly shows her curvy figure and the shirt is buttoned loose to show her pert cleavage. She has a petite black and white fur jacket in her arms. GM has to remind himself to be civilised and stop staring at her figure. She seems to ignore GM, as she wouldn’t recognise him without the mask. She almost walks past him before he gets up to speak. “Miss Cornett, your beauty is wasted on him.” “Excuse me?” She almost dismisses him as a random talking head. “A medically induced coma means that you could be wearing a Santa Claus outfit and he would not know the difference.” Chloe tilts her head, and the instance of recognition is clearly obvious. Her eyes widen. “You!!!” “Calm down. You were one who was riding that knackered old c*nt to the glue factory.” “But did you have to damn near kill him?” GM’s pulse is racing. He loves this exchange. “I don’t have to justify myself to you. But I did what was best for me, not you.” “But I lov-” “I....don’t....care. You were nothing just another distraction, a weakness, you being with him made the decision so much easier to make!” Chloe gives him a scornful look. GM rolls his eyes. “Yes....I am calling you a burden. You are eliminated from my life with him. I feel so free.” Chloe goes to slap GM but he grabs her forearm in mid air and with it, pushes her to the side of the corridor. They are nose to nose, both breathing intensely. GM closes his eyes for a second to allow himself to enjoy his present position. Her pushed cleavage presses against his chest and a sweet, rosey perfume wafts all too briefly from her taut neck. Eyes open and GM stares at Chloe to find intense passion being fired back at him. He notices a recognisable fighting spirit within her eyes. “No apologies, no regrets. What I have done is no different to what DC did to Brian Michaels. Maxx would have done the same in my position. I control my destiny; no one else.” He releases Chloe’s arm but doesn’t back away immediately. After lingering for a second, he takes a step back before walking away at some pace. GM had an interview to attend. And he was thirsty. * Having exited the hospital GM had surmised his thoughts and felt suitably prepared for his interview. While he didn’t like doing it on Richard Covell’s terms, Damien Thorpe had at least allowed him to decide the venue. GM walks outside the main hospital and crosses the street. He enters a small coffee shop and buys a creamy white tea. He walked outside and leans against the lamppost. As he stood there, blowing on the paper cup to cool his drink, he allowed his thoughts to pass back over the event that was Adrenaline Rush. All but two titles changed hands and the card was stacked from top to bottom with great matches. GM thought about where he wanted to be and started walking along the pavement. His thoughts linger over the impact made by his opponent for Monday Night Mayhem. Antonio Demarco came to the ring after Sonny Williams retained his Intercontinental title and left the champ lying in the ring. Clearly Demarco has gold in his sights and GM knew next to nothing about him (almost as much as he knew about the New York traffic – he nearly stepped off the curb into oncoming traffic a couple of times). While GM was certain that Demarco would be asking questions about him, Demarco was a truly unknown quantity who had not been seen in any company for a few months. GM knew that Demarco had considerable success in the original incarnation of the MWE but had clearly taken advantage of not immediately appearing in MWE in its newest form. This, combined with his actions at Adrenaline Rush, tell him something more. Antonio Demarco is cold and calculated, probably hungry for success and eager to prove a point. He doesn’t care what he needs to do to get to where he wants to go. There was something else that GM had to consider as he continued on what appeared to be a mindless wander. There must be a reason why GM was the one chosen to face Demarco at Mayhem. While GM at first believed it was punishment, he now considered it to be a test. If he beat Antonio Demarco then surely an opportunity would open up soon after. That is what GM wanted – the chance to prove himself against the best. GM decided, there and then, that he would go to Monday Night Mayhem and use Antonio Demarco as a test. “A test I know I can win; a springboard to greater achievements. Antonio Demarco will be the first of many victims.” GM had been thinking so much that he didn’t realise quite how far he had walked. He looked around, trying to get his bearings and saw a sign that gave him the perfect destination [Madison Square Garden, -> 2050m] GM pulled out his mobile and rang Damien Thorpe. “Hello?” “Meet me at MSG.” “Errrr sure. I’ll be 25 minutes.” * For such a famous venue, MSG felt small to GM. He couldn’t to appreciate the special and unique aura of the place. From the pictures that line the entrances to the versatile arena flooring and close, intimate seating made it truly different to the massive scale of most North American venues. If anything, MSG reminded GM of stadia in England. GM took his seat in the first row of balcony seating. He knew Damien would see him from wherever he entered, so he allowed himself a little more thinking time, to consider the forthcoming match at Monday Night Mayhem. There’s a reason why GM hated facing debutants. “Such an unknown quantity.” When there was hype around the newcomer, it made it difficult to know what to take a face value. For Antonio Demarco to come after Sonny Williams and presumably the IC belt, there must a degree of truth to what he had heard about the ability of the guy. His strength and the power of his punches don’t appear to be in question, not from what GM had seen. “If he is built like a tank, I need to immobilize him, ground him – to neutralize the power. If I can out manoeuvre him then I can use my superior technical skills.” GM’s mind was working overtime. He normally had scouted opponents and had them figured out easily enough. But this was different and GM was working almost blind, “I have to beat him; that much is clear.” Damien taps GM on the shoulder, making him jump and bringing him back to reality. * Damien gets a slightly annoyed look from GM and takes a seat quickly and without fuss. Damien: “So, why choose MSG for this interview GM? We are in New Hampshire for MNM, I expected you to be there.” GM: “Have I ever done what you expected?” “But I-” GM continues in ignorance of Damien’s interruption. “I wanted to come back here, to this Mecca of sports and entertainment, because this is where GM truly arrived in MWE.” “Oh, you’re talking about what happened to Maxx aren’t you?” “I want to talk about the future but first let’s consider the past.” “How so?” “I chose to do what I did to Maxx because I could not take the feeling of being choked by being associated with him anymore. I was tired of being known as his tag team partner. I am better than that. I became better than him. It was simply time to become a more powerful, singular entity in MWE on my own.” “So you planned all along to turn on him?” GM tips his head to the side. “I planned nothing. It was.... simple destiny. Maxx was stubborn, stagnant and I did what was necessary to branch out and grow. I was sick of Maxx and tired of the bullshit talent like Project Revolution who think they are the next best thing when they were looking at the MWE’s next breakout star the whole time.” GM itches his eye through the mask. “I am proud of what I have done. The whole world saw it and could do nothing to stop me. There was no hiding behind the tinted windows at the wheel of a car for me. I wanted everyone to know what I have done.” Damien is listening attentively. He knows very well that getting GM to talk but what he does say is often meaningful. “The MWE’s old guard have blocked the way for too long. I am going to shatter that blockage and create my own legend. And I do that by facing Antonio Demarco at MNM.” Damien’s eyes nearly pop out at the mention of MWE Original Demarco returning and facing GM. “Demarco is nothing but an old vet trying to reclaim his spot by any means necessary. I may not see eye to eye with Sonny Williams on a lot of things but to be laid out like that after a TLC match stinks something rotten. It smells of someone trying to get themselves over after being nonexistent for so long.” GM’s notorious attitude was beginning to show here. “And I refuse to be just another victim. Demarco is a ‘roided up freak whose balls have shrivelled up so much that he didn’t want to make his comeback in a regular match for fear of defeat. He had to make an impact at the expense of an exhausted champion.” Damien nods understandingly. “So this is my message to Antonio Demarco.....and I trust you to get it to him Damien, if he wants a shot at Sonny Williams or any champion, then he is going to have to go through me first.” “I don’t care how ripped you are, what you have done before or how many times that you can kiss your guns before the audience throws up!” Damien tries to stifle a laugh at this comment. “Kissing yourself is not the characteristic of a champion. You don’t see Eric Derf making out with his belt. And Mason Resoan doesn’t make it publicly know that he kisses the US title after defending it!” GM allows himself a quick smile but it doesn’t last longer than a second. “You have done nothing in this company Demarco and when you’re in my ring, you’re an obstacle on my path to becoming a champion! And after everything that has happened recently, everything that I have gone through with Maxx, I am not going to be derailed at the next hurdle just as I am about to establish myself.” GM’s tone is serious and focussed. Damien daren’t interrupt him. “Antonio, I don’t care for what you have achieved in the past, in WcE, in EWA or WWA. I see no impressive achievements from you now. You have not earned your shot at me in the new MWE. And I intend on showing you exactly why as you were and as good as you think you are now, I am better!” “You are everything that is wrong with the new MWE. The old guard, who refuse to give up their spots, are unable to make new stars and hold onto their spot like it’s a precious metal!” GM shakes his head disapprovingly. “So if everyone is kissing your ass, telling you it is a walkover and you believe them, think again. If you think you are the favourite going into this match. Then don’t bother turning up.” “I refuse to play second fiddle anymore, especially to those undeserving of such hype and hysteria like you!” “Instead you are in line for the special treatment that I give to obstacles in my path, to destroy any delusions of grandeur. And just like Maxx, just like Windy Bridges, you will end up flat on your back and in hospital, a physical wreck courtesy of the Grand Mystique and the Big Bang.” GM pauses as he gathers his thoughts a little. “Everything I have done is about looking out for me and mine and that continues with his suffering. Antonio Demarco is going to find out what it feels like to be made an example of and have an impact made at his expense. MWE needs me to become a champion. It needs...... a better class of champion and I am going to make it happen.” GM stands up as if he is done and finished with the interview. He turns but immediately stops. He gives Damien a ‘wtf’ look. “Shit that line’s been used before.” “Ah fuck it, the guy who said it means nothing to me.” GM chuckles to himself and spreads his arms out. “The MWE needs an injection of new blood to take over the reins of the old guard. I don’t just make history, I create it. The future of the MWE is all about the Grand Mystique. 2009 is my year and I am going onto bigger and better things. It begins with a victory over Antonio Demarco at Monday Night Mayhem next week.” GM gets up. Reluctantly, he offers Damien a handshake before turning to leave. “Oh, GM. I forgot to tell you something. Mr Covell was very specific about giving you this message.” GM stands there expectedly. “Well.....erm......there is someone who is in negotiations to join the MWE.” “Well who the hell is it Damien?” “Erm.....well it’s Windy Bridges!”
