With Friends Like These...; Semi RP

Apr 23 2009

Jesse Anderson

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The scene opens in a locker room as Semi laces up his boot that is propped up on a bench. The XTV Havoc Champion is getting ready for his big non-title triple threat match later on Monday Night Mayhem. He finishes tying his boot and puts his right foot back on the floor. Semi cracks his neck and stretches his arms as a creaking sound is heard. Semi turns his head and his face falls smug. Semi: Well it's about damn time! The new assistant to Semi, Dewey Smith, makes his way in front of the camera and towards Semi with a steaming cup in his hand. He reaches it out to Semi. Dewey: S... Sorry. There was a line in front of the coffee machine. Dewey looks extremely nervous and ashamed. His sad puppy dog eyes along with his small, scrawny stature makes him look like a Chihuahua. Semi looks down at the hot cup of coffee in Dewey's outstretched hand for a long while before snatching it up. Semi sniffs the coffee. He then puts it to his lips and tilts it back, taking a long sip. Semi's eyes wince and he spits the coffee all over the side of Dewey's face. Semi: This is WAY too damn hot! Dewey yelps and immediately starts smacking the coffee away from his face. The scene fades out. The next scene fades in as Dewey walks out from the locker room restroom after clearly washing himself up, holding his left hand to the left side of his face. Semi is sitting a steel chair with his arms across his chest. Semi: What the hell are you covering your face for? ...Actually, I understand perfectly! Semi lets out a long, hardy laugh at Dewey's expense. Dewey lowers his hand to reveal the whole left side of his face is red and burnt. Upon seeing this, Semi's laughter becomes much louder. Semi slaps his knee as he bellows and tries to manage another insult. Semi: It looks like a tribal tattoo! Semi begins to make Indian noises with his left hand to his mouth and acts like he has a spear in his right hand. This turns into another hard knee slap, that accompanies another barrage of laughter. Semi laughs a good long time before letting out a long breath. As soon as he controls himself, Semi points a thumb at the door and shouts harshly at Dewey. Semi: Now go get me another damn cup of coffee! This one tastes like piss! This time, use twice as much sugar! Dewey: Couldn't I just add sugar to that one? Semi: No! Get a new one! This one's too hot! Dewey: But wouldn't that... Dewey gets cut off by a loud barking Semi. Semi: JUST GO! Dewey jumps a little and scurries out the locker room door quickly, quietly closing it behind him. After the door is closed, Semi takes a long, loud sip of the coffee. We now see Dewey, making his way through a long, brightly lit hallway. He walks quickly with his head down and his eyes to the floor. A chattering is heard. Dewey looks up to see two men walk around the corner. Two cousins. Blood Fuze, Mr. Markowitz and Triple G chat excitedly as they spot Dewey eyeing them as his head is turned to the floor. Triple G: How's it goin' man? Markowitz: Hey hey! What's up? Dewey smiles and replies in a mumble. Dewey: I know you guys. You're Blood Fuze. You guys are cool. Markowitz shakes Dewey's hand and Triple G pats Dewey on the back, with enthusiasm, making Dewey's eyes bulge momentarily. Markowitz: Thanks man! Always good to hear from a fan. Triple G: Love to stay and chat, but we have a match to get ready for. Sharpman's going down! Markowitz: Yeah he is! Triple G and Markowitz high-five and continue on down the hallway as Dewey has his head down, after ducking the high-five. Dewey glances over his shoulder and continues shuffling on with a half smile on his face. Dewey makes his way through the halls and into a large area where there are tables set up with assorted snacks and drinks scattered on them. Standing at the end of one of the tables is another pair of men. These men are known as Vanguard. Ace Cannon is stirring what appears to be a cup of coffee and Joseph Sharpman is sipping from a bottle of water. Sharpman: There's no way those punks are going to beat me tonight. Cannon: Obviously. Dewey nervously makes his way over to the table with the coffee maker and is spotted by the dynamic duo. Ace looks to Dewey. Cannon: Oh good, I've been waiting for you. Fix some new coffee. This tastes like piss! Dewey's eyes grow wide and he just stares at Cannon for a long while, not able to speak. Dewey: Um... I uh... I'm not the concession guy. I'm here to get another coffee for Semi. Sharpman jumps in. Sharpman: That fat idiot? Can't even make himself a cup of joe now? Pffft, what a waste of a whole lot of space. The comment brings a grin to Cannon's face. She darts a look at Sharpman then returns his look to Dewey, who is shakily trying to get a styrofoam cup from the stack beside the coffee machine. Cannon: So the big ol' Semi chose you to do his dirty work for him, huh? He might as well have gotten a helper monkey. Sharpman: Or a carrier pigeon. The two chuckle and make their way over to Dewey. Sharpman grabs the cup that Dewey is still struggling with and lifts it off the stack and places it on the table. He flashes Dewey a cocky smile and pats the top of Dewey's head. Cannon's eyes widen as he notices the red, blistered patch on the side of Dewey's face. Cannon raises his right hand and points his finger at it. Cannon: Ouch. That looks painful. Cannon presses it with his outstretched finger. Dewey squints his eyes in pain. Cannon: Does that hurt? Cannon presses it again, this time with enough force to push Dewey's head back. Dewey moans an "ow" under his breath. Cannon: I guess so. Ace and Joseph watch on as Dewey pours a cup of coffee and puts in a lot of sugar before stirring it up. Dewey is slow and careful through the whole process before finally lifting the cup to take it. Dewey turns to face Sharpman, who stares through him with cold eyes. Dewey turns to the other side to face Cannon, who has a matching stare. Dewey: Well, I should get going. Dewey turns around slowly and begins to take a step. At that point, both Ace and Joseph stick out one foot and Dewey trips over them both. Dewey falls to the floor on his hands and chest, and the coffee in his hand goes flying out in front of him. Cannon and Sharpman laugh bitterly and high five before walking way, Cannon, stepping over Dewey to do so. Dewey slowly gets himself to his hands and knees. He wipes the front of his shirt and then the side of his face. Dewey gets to his feet and sighs heavily. His eyes are to the floor and the corner of his mouth is trembling. Dewey gets another cup and fills it with coffee. Again, he slowly adds sugar and stirs. Dewey then grabs a napkin and blows his nose in it before throwing it in a trash can under the table. He mumbles under his breath. Dewey: I think I made a big mistake. Dewey turns around and begins to head back down the hallway from which he came, but stops. His eyes open wide and glaze over slightly. The camera turns to see that he is looking at a dark figure that is draped from behind in a divine white light. There is a bright glare with a golden hue over the shoulder of the figure that belongs to the MWE world champion, David Whitman. Dewey stares in awe and shields his eyes. You can not see Whitman's face, but he begins talking soothingly. Whitman: Do not be discouraged by the strange and often frightening characters you will meet along the path; for they are only there to test your strength. Dewey's mouth is now agape and his eyes even wider. He nods slightly. David then steps to the side and Dewey jerks his head to the side after facing the blinding spotlight that is being tested by crew workers down the hallway behind where David was standing. Crew Member: Yep, it works. Whitman grabs a bottle of water off of one of the tables and continues speaking to Dewey. Whitman: I haven't seen you around before. Where'd ya' come from? Dewey answers like he is talking to an old friend. Dewey: I'm Semi's personal assistant. God that guy's a pain in the ass! Whitman raises an eyebrow over the Undisputed Championship belt on his shoulder. Whitman: Semi, huh? Dewey nods his head. Whitman: Pain in the ass is right. I have my fair share of bones to pick with him too. Just don't do anything to piss him off too bad and you'll be okay. You just have to stay strong. This is no picnic in the psycho ward we're talking about, this is the world of professional wrestling! Stay strong, and you'll stay on your feet. Understand? Dewey nods again. Whitman returns Dewey's nod before turning to search the snack tray. Dewey starts to scurry away. Whitman turns and stops him though. Whitman: Oh, I almost forgot! Dewey stops and turns, almost spilling the coffee from the cup in his hand. Whitman: I heard the camera crew a few minutes ago asking for Semi. I think they're wanting an interview. Dewey: Ok. Thanks! David waves as Dewey hurries his pace down the hallway back to Semi's locker room. Dewey turns the corner to see Semi storming down the hallway towards him with the XTV Havoc Championship on his shoulder and an angry look on his face. Dewey: Oh good! The camera crew is looking for you. I guess they want an interview. Semi looks at him, disgusted. Semi: Hell, I thought you went and got lost or croaked or something! And I know that! Where do you think I'm going now, shrimp-o?! Semi storms up to the point that Dewey has stopped and bumps him out of the way with his wide shoulder. Dewey dances around wide-eyed, trying to balance the cup of coffee in his hand. Luckily, none spills and Dewey spins around to catch up to Semi. Dewey: Here's your coffee. Semi looks to Dewey's outstretched hand. He then grabs the cup and throws it against the wall beside him, not slowing pace at all. Semi: Like I said, that coffee tasted like piss. Dewey watches the coffee-splattered wall pass as he keeps pace with Semi. Semi: Ok, here's the deal. Stay out of the camera view, and stay away from the ring. You're my personal assistant, not my valet. Got that? Dewey: Uh... yeah. Yeah, I got it. Semi: Good. At this point, they round another corner and walk into the interview area. There's an MWE backdrop with bright lights and cameras pointed at it. Several people in MWE polo's are scrambling around. One looks up to see Semi and Dewey and declares. Crew Member 2: Oh thank god, you're here! You ready to shoot this thing. Semi: Why else would I be here? Are YOU ready? Crew Member 2: We've been ready for ten minutes. Go ahead. He points his thumb to the set. Semi glares at him for a long moment and takes his place after being handed a microphone. Semi: So what, you want me to talk... In mid sentence, the camera angle changes to immediately in front of Semi as the interviewer's cameras role. Semi: ...About my match tonight? Semi turns his gaze forward and his eyes grow wide for a moment. Semi: Oh. Semi's face then becomes that of his typical very smug self. He flares his nostrils and gazes deeply into the camera. He begins to speak slowly and calmly. Semi: I come before you tonight standing at six feet, three inches tall. I come before you, weighing in at two hundred and seventy six pounds. I come before you as the undefeated, unstoppable XTV Havoc Champion and as Mr. Momentum of MWE. Semi adjusts the belt on his shoulder as he mentions it. He pauses and continues staring into the camera before continuing again, this time a little quicker. Semi: Tonight, I am in a triple threat match against Eric Derf and Greg Cherry. If Derf wins the match, he becomes number one contender for my title. Likewise, if Cherry wins, he becomes number one contender. Semi tilts his head back and little and wrinkles his forehead. Semi: But when I win... who knows? Semi then tilts his head back towards and camera and begins talking loudly and bitterly. Semi: They seem to have a problem around here of lining up competition quickly enough for the Semi to run through. When I was thrown into ICW, I became the first ever ICW Nation Champion by winning two fatal four-way matches in a row. I defended that title in a triple threat match then had a four-way battle royal the next week. Then I made my debut in MWE and made my name at the expense of three more people. Then I took this championship in a triple threat Terror Dome match. Semi shakes his head a little and talks confidently. Semi: I just keep picking them off, two and three at a time. And tonight, I'm going to pick off two more. Chances are, next week, they'll find a couple more yokels to go under my wheels. Semi starts motioning along with his hands. Semi: And for further proof that they just can't find me any real competition, look at who I'm in the ring with. First of all you have Greg Cherry. We have a guy who's claim to fame is winning the title that I am holding right now in his second match with the company. That's Impressive. But oh wait, so did I! Semi looks to the left, then the right. Semi: Well, sort of. Anyway... What did he do from there? Nothing. Eventually he was stripped of the title, lost his rematch, and went home for a year to cry about it. At this point, Semi is talking quickly in a matter of fact fashion. Semi: Then, Cherry has the audacity to make all kinds hype, walk back in, and declare that all he wants is the Undisputed Championship? Semi says this, sounding appalled. Semi: You know what, Greg? Stand in line, pal. I have a longer list of fallen victims than half the roster and I'm still only sitting on the Havoc title. And that's fine with me. As long as they keep throwing arrogant punks like you in the ring with me, I don't care what shade of gold my rims are, I'm satisfied with the fact that I get to add YOUR world championship aspirations to the momentum under MY wheels. Semi shakes his head again, this time looking and now sounding a little bewildered. Semi: And all of this is not to even mention the fact that you LOST your first match back. Sorry to tell you man, but after tonight, it's going to be tough to climb that world title ladder when you're at zero and two with a loss to the Havoc champ. Semi punctuates his sentence with a glare into the camera. He takes a long breath and lets it out through his nose before continuing on, sounding degraded. Semi: And in the third corner tonight, Eric Derf. Of course. Because I certainly haven't proven his incompetence enough already. Whatever. Semi goes back to smug and continues bitterly, but without enthusiasm. Semi: Yep, good ol' Derf again. Once again, I get to step into the ring with the guy that was eaten by a dinosaur. Yep, that guy. Semi sighs and shakes his head as his eyes slip to the floor. He looks back into the camera with his confidence restored and talks sharply. Semi: Listen here, Derf. You're an entertaining guy. You really are. You go out of your way for a laugh or a cheer every time, and it always pays off. That kind of attitude and charisma can take you very far in this business. Or at least it would if there wasn't always a Semi cutting you off! This will make my fifth match in MWE and my third time facing Eric Derf. For the brain dead television mutants at home that didn't catch the term 'undefeated' earlier, that means I have beaten Eric Derf twice. Semi nods his head and adjusts the title on his shoulder. Semi: Eric, I can't make up my mind about you. I can't decide if you've got a big heart or a little brain, but either way, something is wrong with you if you are going to continue to agree to wrestle me. It's certainly not doing good things to your career, Eric, in spite of how many Pokemon battles you've won! Semi pauses and lifts his chin high. A cocky grin slides across his face. Semi: As far as tonight goes, it's just another day at the office for me. Just another two splatters of road kill to be left behind the Semi as he travels the road straight to the top. Greg Cherry; Eric Derf... I AM GOING TO RUN YOU DOWN! Semi storms away from the camera and it cuts. We now go back to our original camera that shows Dewey clapping for Semi. Semi looks at him coldly as he walks up to him. Dewey stops clapping and Semi pushes Dewey's shoulder and barks at him as he passes. Semi: Let's go! Dewey scurries after Semi and drops his eyes to the floor. Semi: Actually, I'll be in the locker room. You get me a water on the way back! Semi turns the corner to the right and Dewey turns left. Dewey then stops and his eyes widen as he gasps. The camera turns to see what has stopped him. It is the sight of who else but DC, standing with his back to Dewey and from around the side, you can clearly see that he is holding the pot of coffee up to his waist. You hear the sound of a running stream and of DC sounding very relieved. You then hear the sound of a zipper before DC puts the pot of coffee back in the coffee maker with a mischievous smile on his face. DC turns towards Dewey and begins to take a step but stops dead in his tracks as he sees the look of shock on Dewey's face. Neither man says anything for a long moment. DC shoots a look at the coffee pot then back to Dewey. DC: What? The bathroom has been out of order all night! ...When you gotta go, you gotta go! I actually think it tastes better that way. ... If you don't like it, fuck you! DC walks by Dewey brisquely with his nose held high as the scene fades out. OOC A couple things: I asked permission of everyone I used. DC, I hope that punchline worked for you. Also, is there a way to get more shades of colors? I didn't even realize I had that many people in this until I was forced to use colorless as a color, and use the same color for two people. Also, this is going up without a re-read. Sorry for I'm sure quite a few typos/grammer errors, but otherwise, I wouldn't be able to post it till tomorrow. Hope you enjoyed.
