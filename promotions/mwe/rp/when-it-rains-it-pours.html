When It Rains...It Pours

Apr 24 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
So my MWE re-debut didn’t exactly go as planned. Sue me, I was hoping that my return to the ring was going to be something special, but it wasn’t to be. Thing is, I have a chance to redeem myself and get a shot at the MWE X-Havoc Title. I also look around and notice all the vacated championships and think to myself that is it possible that I could just hold a title and nobody else would be the wiser? This is still frustrating to me. I lost to Kevin Hardaway and people are saying that I’m all hype. I can’t live with the fact that people refer to me as such. Maybe my frustrations boiled over into something else as I bumped into Jack on my way to my hotel room. Cherry: Remind me the next time I have a bad week to just shoot myself in the head. Jack: Well, when it doesn’t help that you didn’t have a match in forever. Maybe if you didn’t hype yourself up to be the next big thing… Cherry: Shut up, you know for a fact that he got lucky. You have no idea what it would have done to me if I was completely embarrassed. I think I made a good showing of myself…not good enough obviously, but whatever. Jack: It wasn’t pleasant to watch, but Greg, you have to realize that these people aren’t going to take it easy on you. Cherry: Take it easy on me? Are you freakin’ kidding me, Jack? You have been my trainer or you were supposed to be, but you haven’t done a damn thing in who knows how long and all you can tell me is that these people aren’t going to take it easy on me? I know that I’ve only been in the wrestling business for four years, but don’t start calling me stupid now. Jack: Hold on a second. Are you going to blame this on me now? Cherry: You’re not helping me too damn much, because all you do is sit there in your Armani suit on the sidelines when you are supposed to do something productive to assist me. Do you understand me, Jack Campbell? Your motivational speeches aren’t really doing me that much anymore. Step up your game, because if you don’t want to stand by ringside and coach me, then how the hell am I supposed to get better on my own? You’re the teacher, you know. Jack: You are blaming this on me, but you know what? It’s not my fault that you haven’t won a match in MWE in over a year. Cherry: And you are completely blaming me for my losses? Jack: No, I’m not, but unless you want to go back to being Greg “the Great” Cherry of the lower mid-card days from when you first started, then stop blaming other people for your losses. It goes down in the record books as a loss for you, so once you wise up your 22 year old punk-ass instead of pretending to be the whiny 19 year old who I couldn’t get through to, then maybe you can be something around the world of wrestling again. Until then, don’t put blame on me for something that was more your fault than mine! I’m out of here! Jack walked out of my room and slammed the door on his way out. I put my face in my hands and rubbed my forehead as I felt another migraine coming on. Man, I’m going to tell you that my life has been full of stress for about a month now. Maybe I am obsessed with being a champion, but ever since I first experienced a taste of gold, I was hooked on becoming the best. Cherry: I’m going to bet myself five bucks that this day can’t get any worse. Just then, my wife came through the door, Ashley walked into the room with a somewhat discouraged look on her face. I think I was about to lose five bucks to myself… Ashley: Did I just hear you and Jack yelling at each other? Cherry: Yes, he and I had an argument and apparently he wasn’t happy with something I said. Ashley: Did you insult him or something? Cherry: I don’t think I did, but I did tell him that he wasn’t helping me any. Ashley: Why would you say something like that? Cherry: Because it’s the truth. Ashley, do you realize that with the busier work schedule that I have, I don’t need a motivational speaker, I need a trainer or a coach. Yes, Jack is my best friend, but if he cannot help me become the best that I can possibly be, then he really isn’t helping me as much as I’d like. Ashley: Are you blaming him? Cherry: What is it with you two? Why do you insinuate that I’m blaming him for all this? Ashley: That’s exactly what it sounds like! Cherry: Are you taking sides now in this thing? Ashley: I’m not taking anyone’s side! Cherry: Yes you are. It sounds like you favor Jack in this situation. What is up with this? What did I do to deserve this? Ashley: Honestly, you have been bitching way too long about losing nearly every match you’ve been in lately and I’m tired of it. You shouldn’t dwell on the past, because it will bite you in the ass if you lose focus! Damn it, regain your focus! Cherry: I can’t believe you aren’t taking my side in this! You’re supposed to be my wife! Ashley: I am your wife, but I don’t have to agree with everything you say or do! I’m my own woman when it comes to making decisions. Just because I love you doesn’t mean I have to agree with everything that you say. Cherry: Well, what help are you then? Ashley: Excuse me? You’re yelling at your loving wife now? Listen, I don’t know what happened to you, but whenever you figure it out and resolve it, you can let me know. I don’t think I want to talk to you right now. Ashley left the room disgusted as I rolled my eyes and plopped down onto my bed. I let a few days pass to see what would happen. I had a triple threat match that I had to worry about and I knew that Jack would say that I need to regain my focus. It’s the same crap he’s been spewing for years, but maybe he has a point… Cherry: Okay, I get it. I didn’t exactly get the job done against Kevin Hardaway. I know, yes, I brought all this hype upon myself and then lost in my return match. That’s not exactly the most spectacular way to come back to the MWE. I get it. So shut up about it. Let me start off with Jesse Anderson, the X-TV Havoc Banana Split Peanut Butter and Jelly and…damn I’m getting hungry plus add thirty more names to the end of the title Champion. Really, Semi? You insult me for saying that I want the MWE Championship? Did I say I deserve a shot? No, I didn’t say that. I said I wanted it and now you’re jumping all over me, because I want to be the best. Well, sue me for wanting to be successful, but damn, it’s just a dream I have. Lay off. You’re also bragging about being undefeated in MWE when you’ve only had four matches. Is that exactly something you want to proudly display on your chest? Hey, I’m undefeated after all four of my matches! That’s like a baseball team winning 4 games out of a 162 game season and gloating about how they’re unstoppable. You have no idea what you’re getting into. By the way, I went 5-0 when I first started in the MWE as the X-TV Champ. You don’t exactly hear me go “BRAH! I’M UNBEATABLE! I’M THE SIZE OF A TRUCK! BLAH!” That’s mainly because I’m not…on either of those, but you get my point. Listen, Semi, before you start honking your horn ready to run us over, you better realize that Greg Cherry is going to be a giant pothole in what you consider your one-way street to the top of this company. You’ll hit that pothole and you will be stuck dead in your tracks with a blown tire and you won’t know what to make of it. As impressive as you are, don’t underestimate me, my friend. Eric Derf, you haven’t had a lot of luck lately in the ring, but you do have the ability to go in that ring and that’s something I won’t take away from you. However, I’m not going to let my respect for you cloud my in-ring judgment. I’m not going to underestimate you like someone else in this match already has because this is an opportunity that I cannot screw up. Winning this match will give me the chance to be a two-time X-Havoc or whatever the hell the title is called now Champion. All three men in this match have held that title at one point or another and I’m going to make it a point to use the X-Havoc Championship as a stepping stone towards the greater good. Mark my words that one day you will see Greg Cherry stand atop the mountain as the MWE Champion. It’s my goal, it’s my destiny and I refuse to let anything stop me now. The match against Kevin Hardaway was lost because I figured I was good enough to beat him. I don’t want to be just good enough. I want to be the best. That’s what I’m going to have to be to take down Anderson and Derf. Simply put, at the end of the night, they will receive a Sensational Experience unlike any other. If you don’t like what I say, then your life is the pits, deal with it. However, the match is not the number one thing on my mind right now. I hadn’t seen my best friend in over a week and I have no idea what happened to him. I decided to have Ashley, Eddie and myself booked on a flight to Pittsburgh, just so I could fly home and see if he was there. We drove back to my house and stayed there for a little while before heading over to Jack’s house to see if he might have come home. However, I never expected what would eventually happen in the coming hours… Cherry (knocks on door): Hello? Is anybody home? Ashley: Maybe this isn’t such a good idea. Cherry: What makes you say that? Ashley: He might still be really mad at you. Cherry: He’s in his mid-twenties. If he can’t get over a stupid fight like that in over a week, then that’s his problem and not mine. The door opened and Jack’s wife Sarah was at the door. Ashley: Hey Sarah! Sarah: Hey girl! I haven’t seen you for a while. Cherry: Hi Sarah. Sarah: Hey. Um, what’s this all about? Cherry: As much as I’d like to stay and visit, I want to ask you something. Sarah: Sure, go ahead. Cherry: I just want to know if you’ve seen or heard anything from Jack in the past week. We were in another city for a show last week and we had an argument. Sarah: What were you arguing about? Cherry: That’s inconsequential. Ashley: It was probably the same thing that we argued about. Cherry: It was close. However, we were hoping that Jack called you or something so that we would know his whereabouts. Sarah: Are you telling me you lost my husband? Cherry: We didn’t lose him…we just can’t find him. Ashley slaps herself in the forehead and shakes her head at that remark. Cherry: Listen, I figured that since you were his wife, you would have some information on him or maybe he called you during last week. Sarah: Honestly, I haven’t heard anything from him since you lost the title. I don’t know why, but… Cherry: Well, we don’t have to go into detail. Sarah: To answer your question, no, I haven’t heard anything. I’ll keep you posted if I do. Cherry: Okay, thanks, Sarah. Sarah: No problem. The three of us left Sarah’s house and went back to my place. We all chilled for a couple of hours and then turned on the late night news. Ashley: Why do you have the news on? We never watch the news. Cherry: I know, but I need something to help me fall asleep. Just then, I heard quite possibly the worst news that I’ve had in years. News Reporter: Last week there was a fatal shooting that affected an entire organization, killing one man and severely wounding another. The man who was shot and killed has not yet been identified. The severely wounded man is identified as Jack Campbell, a native of Pittsburgh and an employee of Mayhem Wrestling Entertainment. This attack occurred just 15 minutes away from the venue in which a wrestling show was being held. No comments have been made from the organization about the incident. Ashley: Oh my God… I just sat staring at the television until the story finished airing. I turned the television off and stood in front of it for about 15 minutes not moving or turning around… My best friend had been nearly fatally shot in the head last week and I didn’t know about it. Why didn’t I know about it? Cherry: I can’t believe it… Ashley (through tears): Greg…Jack almost died last week. Cherry: I know. I was emotionless. I didn’t know what to think. How could something like this happen? Was it all because of our argument? Had we not argued, would he still be standing right beside me? Damn it… Ashley: We have to go see Sarah. Cherry: I know we do. Twenty minutes later, we were back at Sarah’s house and needless to say, Sarah was a wreck. We comforted her as she sat on her couch and continued to cry. For a half hour, no one said anything. I had nothing to say that would have helped out. In fact, I was speechless. I didn’t know what to say. Then again, what does one say when their best friend is nearly killed in an act of violence like this? Ashley: We are so sorry, Sarah. Cherry: He’s going to be alright. I know he is. He’s being treated right now as he’s being flown back home to Pittsburgh. He’ll be fine. Ashley: Yes, we just need to pray for him right now. Cherry: That’s all we can hope for. We continued comforting Sarah for another 20 minutes as she continued to cry at the recent news of Jack’s near fatality. I whispered in Ashley’s ear that I needed to go for a walk which is exactly what I did. I had to clear my mind of what just happened. This can’t be happening now…my best friend is in critical condition and I was absolutely powerless to do anything about it. His friendship is worth more to me than championship gold…I really hope that I’m not the cause of all this. This is all my fault. And…scene.
