================3 Way Dance=============== The gym has always been THAT place guys just like to go to and blow off some steam, or in some cases, blow something else; like having a secret affair that they don’t want their significant other to know about. But for Casey the gym is a getaway from a perilous lifestyle where he can hone his skills and have some peace as well. And the ever grimace Benny Ten has decided to escort Casey to the gym today for the simple fact that he had nothing else better to do. The two men of course have an agenda today for their workout and maybe even a side dish of smack talking and getting laid after sweating their asses off. But life isn’t all about boats and hoes though, oh no, it’s about working hard and pumpin iron. For the sake of being a top contender in the MWE, Casey Charisma himself will be incorporating both in more ways than one! With Benny on the treadmill and Casey working the weights, we bring you to the beginning of a glorious day. ?C!Oh.C?! COME ON BLACK THUNDER, your burnin daylight! Benny Ten Man fuck you. What are you liftin anyway, twenties? ?C!Oh.C?! None of yo dayum businazz! I lift what works for me, you on the other hand need this workout more than I do. Benny Ten And look at that, my thirty minute jog is done, now let’s go get some grub before I get fuckin pissy. ?C!Oh.C?! Gimme a few seconds, I gotta finish my set. Benny hops off the treadmill and starts to wrap a towel around his face in hopes to wipe the large amounts of sweat from his skin. Casey finishes chugging his cup of water and then winces in an unrealistic pain lifting the cup up and down like it’s a powerful force. Benny gives his colleague a look of pure distaste as Casey treats the cup as a heavy burden and slaps it out of his hands causing it to tumble towards the floor. Morning was almost over and Benny wanted to make sure he gets some breakfast before most restaurants would stop serving plates, and Casey on the other hand barely had a mindset for food while his match played at a constant in his mind giving a meaning of an important focus. Casey kinda looks to be at a loss for words though while he gazes at his cup with some deep sense of understanding and sorrow, as if remembering something that would make a person lay down in their beds and start rehearsing parts for a drama. Benny Ten Will you quit being such a pussy for two seconds and just be normal. ?C!Oh.C?! Sorry, but that cup could of had a family. Benny Ten Come on before I lose it and start eating my own damn fudge packed fingers. ?C!Oh.C?! That’s pretty gross dude. Benny Ten Let’s go already! ?C!Oh.C?! FINE FUCK FACE! Sadly for Casey, Benny is paid by Casey’s mother and father to keep an eye on him and take him wherever he wants to go. After a DUI a few years ago The Collector Of Cool was never permitted by his parental figures to drive again, and that if he DID, there would be no more checks in the mail that he can mooch off of. Even though Benny was meant to be just a paid guardian to Casey, he came to turn into a close friend over the years and the whole tough guy role vanished over time. Once Benny makes it to the driver door he nudges Casey to the side and jumps in. Preparing for Charisma’s whining, he waits nonchalantly staring through the windshield of a near empty gym parking lot for Casey to get in the passenger seat. ?C!Oh.C?! Hey man! WHAT THE FUCK!?! Let me drive fool! Benny Ten(yelling out the window) No! Get in or I buy an all you can eat ticket without having to see your ugly face munching on some health bars. ?C!Oh.C?! Wait…where we goin? Casey now understands that he’s been tricked and folds his arms giving his supposed friend a nasty look as Benny shrugs off Casey’s gaze and goes on to roll down the road. Apparently a ride to iHop after a subtle argument while blasting Katy Perry along the back roads is just another day in the city. New York is where dreams are made of, and also where pancakes are the SHIT! So argument or not, there’s always a nice meal to look forward to at the end of it all. Like any good family or group of friends, everything always seems to sort of come together in harmony over some nice chow time. You can cope, move on, and feel relieved all at the same time after just eating a good old fashioned heart attack on a plate, because that’s what America is all about! After a few minutes of traffic delays and awkward silence, Benny and Casey seem to have finally arrived at their destination. Benny Ten Here at last, I could eat for days! ?C!Oh.C?! Don’t overdo it big man, just like me you got an image to keep. Benny Ten Yea whatever, I’m still hungry like a freakin wolf over here. ?C!Oh.C?! Well then…..after you. Both men leave the vehicle, but it was Benny who made haste to the restaurant first. With Casey casually following behind, the food spot had an instant smell of deliciousness once they walked in. The mixed aromas of syrup, crisp potatoes, and freshly scrambled eggs enveloped their senses. People from all walks of life and different appearances all sat around eating and discussing talks of excitement and jubilation. Not one sad face was to be seen. Benny finds some nice window seating as he motions Casey over and they find themselves comfortable while the sun shines through the streak free glass towering over their heads. A waitress greets them while handing them their menus and smiles. Waitress Anything you’d like to drink before you order? Benny Ten I’ll take a strawberry shake. ?C!Oh.C?! Uhm, water’s fine…. Waitress You got it! Are any of you ready to order yet, or would you like more time? Benny Ten I’d like a stack of pancakes with a side of bacon and eggs, oh…and a small glass of milk to top that shit off. Waitress Gotcha, and for you sir? ?C!Oh.C?! I’ll just take a fruit salad if you have it. Waitress Alrighty, I think we can do that. I’ll be back with your plates. The waitress wonders off to the kitchen as Casey and Benny just kinda look around at all the uber happy faces and uproarious laughter flinging around the room. It was almost surreal to see such a place filled with overjoyed citizens. During the brief time Casey was looking around, he seemed to notice a heavy set man wearing an old Michaels Inc. T-shirt. Instantaneously his eyes grow fixed on it with a bitter anger hidden in the back of his throat. He wanted to jump up and let everyone in this little eating hangout how much Michaels Inc. sucked as a team making any sort of impact to the known world. Casey grew sickened by the sight of it, thoroughly and most definitely treating the name as a failed experiment in which everyone got out of it as massive losers. Benny Ten There’s something about this place that just seems Twilight Zonish. ?C!Oh.C?! No kidding. Check out the big boy just a few seats behind us, he has a Michaels Inc. shirt on! What are the chances right? Benny Ten Calm yourself Case, it’s just a coincidence. ?C!Oh.C?! You know, I had planned to be just like the legend Brian Michaels and wow the entire roster. I wanted to do more than he had ever accomplished. Then he went and fucked up his career by throwing a potential group of talent under the bus and being unable to make up his mind whether he wants to stay in the MWE, or take his typical temporary leaves only to come back and expect a round of applause. He gave faces a bad name. I intend to rejuvenate the era of the good guys in MWE and make us something to cherish again. To rebuild a tarnished legacy like that in MWE, is my dream come true. Benny Ten It ain’t nothin Casey, you’ll get your chance to be a hit but give it time. ?C!Oh.C?! That’s all I ever hear anymore is time, time, time, time. I wish time could just fuck off sometimes and give us a break. The man in the Michaels Inc. shirt starts to giggle as he spies on Casey and Benny’s conversation with a snicker. The man that had laughed at their conversation is alone eating at a table full of empty plates while finishing off a tasty hash brown. Feeling disrespected, Casey walks over to the eavesdropping man during which Benny was about to intervene, except his strawberry shake had just arrived and he didn’t feel like stopping Casey at this particular moment. Without anyone to hold him back, Casey sits right next to the snooping gentleman and makes a disgusted look on his face. The man isn’t laughing anymore, he just contently smiles. His beard and glasses personify his face as Casey looks him dead in the eyes. He had a silly flare about him, but even though the man may look loveable, Casey was in no mood to be cool today. ?C!Oh.C?! What’s your problem? Snickering Man Oh nothing. Just how you denounced Michaels Inc. and everything it stands for! ?C!Oh.C?! So? I take it you were a big fan of the group or at least Brian Michaels himself? Snickering Man Well DUH! Despite what you say, the man went leaps and bounds in MWE to get the very foundation of a great company moving. If you really knew the guy’s motives you’d understand why he was changing his mind so frequently, there’s alotta pressure in the business, you know this. ?C!Oh.C?! Well seeing as I am IN the business, then of course I do. But I don’t appreciate guys like you not respecting other’s opinions. Snickering Man Who said anything about disrespect? I was merely finding your moronic rant on everything Brian has ever done a bit skeptic that’s all. You can’t just discredit the man for that one stunt. He was tired of the fans thinking of him as a pebble rather than a giant rock of fame. He wanted more for what he did, and I agree with him one hundred percent. His handprints in the cement and footsteps in the sands of time deserve to be graciously worshipped! He WAS….the MWE. ?C!Oh.C?! Do you realize that just before he left Brian genuinely disliked nearly everyone who ever loved what he brang to the table? It’s in the 2009 Hall of Fame speech he made, then he stayed gone. That should tell you something about how he feels towards MWE. And because of that, there needs to be someone else to fill in the shoes that were too big for him to walk in. If you really are a hardcore fan of Michaels then gimme your shirt. Snickering Man What? ?C!Oh.C?! Hand it over. If Michaels was such a great star, and Michaels Inc. was only an act, then why bother wearing it? If anything I’d guess the whole Michaels Inc. thing was a disgrace to a legend! Right? RIGHT? Snickering Man I guess. ?C!Oh.C?! Take it off. Snickering Man We’re kind of in a public place. ?C!Oh.C?! Here, take my jacket. I promise I have a moral behind this. The man frowns in embarrassment and takes his shirt off tossing it to Casey in exchange for a rather small hoodie that the man could barely even zip on. At this point the entire building has all their eyes on these two men with Benny only a table away enjoying his shake and gobbling down his recently served meal without caring much for anything that’s going on. With a stern look, Casey takes out a lighter and starts to burn the shirt, a couple of screams are heard around him but are quickly ignored while he tosses the burning shirt to the ground and stomps up and down on it like a maniac. He then gives the tattered shirt back to the now terrified man sitting next to him and scratches his eyebrow in thought. The man didn’t exactly know what to say, he could only stare and blink his eyes in a whimsical pattern. ?C!Oh.C?! What I did there, was decimate the very gruesome lie that Michaels has created. He killed the baby faces, and I intend to bring them back to life like a fucking thriller video. If you have ANY dignity left in you, you’ll agree with me, and put this mockery of a shirt back on just to show everyone how Michaels was still the shit even after what he did to stars like me trying to make it big. Snickering Man I’m…I’m sorry, I didn’t realize you had such a hatred to- ?C!Oh.C?! Hatred? No. Disgust, disappointment, disregard? Yes. Now I’m going to leave you to whatever food you haven’t consumed here, and we can pretend this never happened. Enjoy the rest of your day. Casey stands up fully aware of the scene he has created, but still keeping a confidence and a structure in his posture. He walks passed Benny still chewing his food and heads to the back of the restaurant getting a whiff of fresh air as the people inside are still very much startled at what occurred, luckily no cops were called. Casey leans against a garbage can and notches his head up to the clouds just to see if he can make any shapes out of them, but all he can see is himself, floating by like the years of his life. He often wishes for one day he doesn’t have to worry about his image or his lifestyle, that he can just go nuts and do the naughty things he was never told to do all his life, but he is obsessed with good morals and everlasting acceptance in the eyes of the fans. ?C!Oh.C?! There’s always struggles on the road to absolute stardom, especially as a member of a dying breed things can get shaky. I’ve waited to come into the business with gritting teeth so I can bring back the excitement and the it factor MWE has been lacking. We’re living in a time where bad guys get the girls, win the fights, AND steal the fans all in one quick sweep. That’s disgracing the sideliners like me in every aspect. WE’RE the ones that are supposed to be the favorites, everything is all fucked up now. I’ve been overhearing that the original lifeline of MWE is starting to collapse a little bit, the originals of MWE have slowly been falling off the radar and a NEW line of hopefuls have come to take the stage. Believe me when I say I have nothing but respect and congratulations to the Hall of Famers and the retiree’s, but just the same, it’s time to move on. Visions of his first match and his victory start to dance in his head. Jack Lupin may have been a nobody just like Casey, but it’s always that first decisive moment that defines a person in any career. It’s the first big step to a whole stairway of opportunities. If Casey is to catch the attention of every single active MWE roster member, he needs to stay on the path of a champion. No matter how long it may take, Charisma is bound by chains to unleash his best in every match in order to strike fear in the hearts of those who oppose him. There may be jokes, there may be times of hilarity along the way as well, but there is always a genuine desire to win and be the very essence of cool itself. ?C!Oh.C?! My reasoning behind that little incident inside was only to prove a point. And that point is that nobody, not even an MWE legend can kill the flowing epicness of a baby face. Once I get my label and I get thrown to the moon as an established name, then I’ve already done half of what I came here to do, and that’s put Mayhem Wrestling Entertainment on the map as the kingpin to the wrestling industry and home to deserving athletes like myself. Lupin and Evermore I’m sure are great guys, both came from tough pasts, and I wish them only shits and gigs from here on out, but they just aren’t on the same level of competition as I am. As a role model to cool fans everywhere, you can’t just be the nice guy, you have to be the BEST guy. You have to make the villains FEAR you. My two opponents are still stuck in their comfort zones, and I just can’t sit by and let two potential winners become overlooked nobodies. A simmering emotion slowly builds within the gullet of Casey’s voice. He clearly explains the issue with wrestling today, that not every superstar is about making people bleed and smashing tables, that some are much more composed and tons more complex than others. There are multiple personalities and varied individuals throughout the roster that seek more than just what a usual gutsy and balls out athlete would go beyond to accomplish, there are those that want to keep both a traditional stance and a professional demeanor for the audience rather than unhinged acts of craziness and testosterone. Casey is far from the extreme type, you can say he’s more of a chill yet dedicated man. He only gives out the best, and expects quality reactions in return. ?C!Oh.C?! There have been legends my friends that make companies from the ground up and bring in fans from all over the world to watch their amazing talent take action. But once they hang up their boots and move on, there’s always someone to take their position as even greater talent. Believe it or not, if Jack Lupin or Zack Evermore quit today there would be fifty other wrestlers just WAITING to be hired. Nobody would really care, which is why I’m trying to step out of my skin a little bit and make myself ONE OF A KIND. I already have a win under my belt, and I can feel the very earth beneath me rise one inch at a time until I’m carried by the gods of sweetness to become the chosen star out of all the fading comets. I’m only telling the truth! If you look around I’ve really been the only fresh draw into the company that has transgressed himself into being noticed and demanding attention. I’m not afraid to have all eyes on me. Neither should anyone else in my similar position. You don’t currently see me heavily talking down my opponents right now only because looking back on the past, that’s always been the common tactic to get a crowd pumped. I want to show all of you come MNM, that I don’t need words and a few opponents with their feelings hurt to win a three way dance. Then once I can prove myself in a match that doesn’t just involve one person, I think I can finally get some publicity and go back to my old school shenanigans. But Monday, it’s gonna be real talk. A spark of electricity starts to regulate in his eyes. He cups his hands around his nose and looks blankly at the ground below, like some overpowering wave has just crashed down on him. So many things on his mind, so many plans left to be fulfilled. There’s no destiny to a person who has nowhere to go, no plans to strive for. There’s no green pasture on the other side of the hill to those who aren’t willing to make the climb. Without a shadow of a doubt, Casey is willing to climb whatever the fuck he has to and achieve whatever dreams he has made to be the person he knows he can become in the MWE. There’s no telling what this man will do to live the life of possibly one of THE MOST important heroes that the MWE has ever had the pleasure of welcoming into their home. He’s going to make the deeds of the dirty be wiped clean with a hint of charisma to boot. He aims to bring laughter and parody to a dark and desolate wasteland. Can he do it? ?C!Oh.C?! I guess my hope is that whoever bothers to even listen to what I have to say understands what I’m trying to accomplish here. There’s a deathly fog around the MWE that desperately needs to be cleared. I wanna be the shining sword to this gravestone of destroyed ruins. The partied out rock star in Zack Evermore and the depressed ghost within Jack Lupin will always overshadow them and hinder their abilities. I’ve learned to overcome my past, I don’t let it follow me around like a love sick puppy anymore. I’m blessed to have a good life, and I’m ever so enjoying what I have now. So Zack…..Jack…..regardless of how this little event of ours turns out, I need you to know that whatever the result is, I’m going to stop at nothing to give guys like us a name again. We are the voices that aren’t heard guys, and that voice needs to be a lot louder. To the MWE fan base, I tell you, I PLEAD to you, to open your minds and stop falling victim to horrible gimmicks and terrible puns. We are the unseen and the unheard unity of what you people really need, the low card will one day be the starting grounds for a whole pool of exciting next generation athletes. You can quote me there. Let’s just keep our fingers crossed that the day an old generation finally dies, we’re no longer passed up as failed talent. So gentlemen and fans alike, if you can’t trust my judgment, and if you refuse to trust probably the only person with common sense IN this industry, then who CAN you trust? I’ll see you in the ring.
