Who Do You Trust

Apr 25 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PART I … A faint sound... A silent whisper... Who is that? It's getting closer... What could it be? Who could it be? *BANG* In a misty hazy, a dreamlike state creeps into focus, the nightmares of his father constantly haunt his subconscious. The cold sharp blade getting closer and closer to his mouth, the piercing pain of the metal separating skin and muscle from each other. His mothers screams of horror and emotional pain drown out his thoughts, his father's laugh echoes continuously through his brain as he struggles in pain. With a violent shake Jekyl wakes up from his nightmare. Sweat beating down his face, the blankets thrown askew from the bed, his breath heavy as if he just finished running a marathon. Jekyl: I really have to stop eating before I goto sleep. Standing up from his queen size bed Jekyl staggers over to a window, ripping open the curtains, standing in front of the window wearing nothing but his smile as a middle aged woman walks by with a small shiatsu dog. Stopping in her tracks she looks over to see Jekyl in all his glory staring at her with his in-humane smirk. Eyes wide and jaw dropped she picks up her dog, covers its eyes and rushes off, while Jekyl looks down, smiles and opens his window. Jekyl: It's usually bigger! Closing his window and curtains he walks over to the other end of the room to turn on the light as he stubs his foot on the leg of his bed. Jekyl: GOD DAMMIT! Stumbling towards the door Jekyl hops on one foot, limping to hit the lights. Flicking the switch the blinding light illuminates the room as Jekyl limps back towards his bed, closing his eyes struggling to erase the images from his nightmare and past from his head. Taking a moment to regain his composure, Jekyl stands up and walks to his closet to get some clothes. Opening the closet door he see there is a plastic bag on the floor, he kicks it violently to see if it makes a sound. CRASH The sound is glass clanking against one another, bottles from the night before. Jekyl: Whoah, I thought it was another body, but I guess the glass say otherwise... Jekyl grabs a shirt and throws it on while heading back towards his bed. Flicking on the T.V he flips through the channels when he sees the MWE logo flash over the screen, a coarse voice begins to emit from the speakers. “The latest ICW call ups are none other then Jekyl and Scotty McKnight, a duo that has dominated the ICW roster, but now they the dynamic team of Zak Warner and Kevin Hardaway. This will be both of these teams first matches together as a part of the official Mayhem roster. The question now is will Warner be able to bounce back after two big loses to the Vanguard, or will Famous and InFamous continue their reign? Jekyl is an MWE vetran, newly returning to the MWE scene...Scotty McKnight, fresh to the MWE roster... These two men begin their reign together for the first time on April 27th, can they capitalize on their ICW spree? Or will they fall victim to Uncultured Anarchy? Two men form opposite ends of the spectrum unite as one to take on the likes of Kevin Hardaway and Zak Warner.” The screen fades to another commercial as Jekyl turns off his television. Sitting on the edge of his bed he runs his fingers through his hair in a deep thought process. Jekyl: The commercial makes a point.... I have never really fought with this guy nor have I ever really gotten to know him... I think its time I went on an adventure to see if I can trust him... Jekyl finishes his ensemble and leaves his room, down the hallway and out the front door. Quickly he hops into his '81 Pontiac FireBird and drives off down the street, all thats left is the tire marks in the middle of the road, his taillights fade into the distance. Part 2 *Click* The door swings open and we see the notoriously known maniacal clown Jekyl carrying a body over his shoulders. All we see is the back end and limp legs of the body, assuming it’s either dead or unconscious we can only hope for the best. Jekyl: Why must they always put up a fight? Walking over to a secluded corner of the room Jekyl violently tosses the body onto the floor immediately causing the body to regain consciousness and start to move around. Jekyl pulls over a chair and sets it in front of the body which is wearing black denim jeans, a black shirt and white etnies shoes while completing the mystery with a cloth bag tied over his head. Jekyl: So, if I take this bag off your head, i need you to promise me that you will listen to me carefully and answer every question honestly... A moan of satisfaction creeps it’s way out of the cloth bag as Jekyl stands up and walks over to the person. Untying the bag we see the man is none other than the other half of Famous and Infamous, Scotty McKnight. With a groan and sudden movement to cover his eyes from the light he slowly realize who his kidnapper is. Scotty: What the fuck? WHY THE HELL DID YOU HIT ME?!?! Jekyl: Calm down sunshine, your fine aren’t you? Now just sit still and listen to me, we are going to have a little Q & A session OK? Scotty nods his head slightly as Jekyl Returns to his chair as he fixes his coat. Jekyl: The reason I am doing this is so I get to know you better and so I can see if i can trust you as a tag team partner... Scotty: You know a simple “Please” would of sufficed... Jekyl: I have a unique way of doing things... Scotty slides himself up and tries to get comfortable while Jekyl turns his chair around and leans in close. The lights still focused on Scotty, bright and nearly blinding he continues to try and obtain full consciousness. Jekyl: First I wanna know what your biggest secret. Scotty: If I told you, you would think I was more looped than you... Jekyl leans in closer, intrigued by Scotty’s reply. His eyes widen, the gears in his head begin to turn, ready to contemplate what Scotty is about to tell him. Jekyl: Continue... Scotty: Well I’ve never told anyone this but I am a wiccan, and as such there is a prophecy telling that in 2012 the world will come to a stand still and the earth will open up, the sky will open up and heaven and hell will be in a universal war, god will fall and Lucifer will attempt to take the throne to rule the universe. Jekyl for once thinks he has found someone a little more crazy than he is, still surprised about the story Scotty is telling him he intently listens to Scotty continue, the lights begin to flicker behind them, the room is near dead silent. All that can be heard other that Jekyl and Scotty is the eerie breeze whistling from beneath the door they came in through, Jekyl: So you're telling me that the world is going to end in 3 years and heaven and hell will fight in a war? Scotty nods in agreement. His head still throbbing from the blow to the head he continues his story. Scotty: There is a catch though, there are 20 spirits said to contain themselves in humans and are supposed to fight against Lucifer to prevent him from taking gods throne. 5 reapers, 10 prophets and 5 guardians. The Reapers take shelter in humans as guides, the prophets are split up into 2 groups... 5 for the reapers and 5 for the guardians, they take form as their spirit animals... the guardians can split into to forms, animals and humans. I'm still not sure why. Jekyl: I think I understand, but then again, this is the weirdest thing I have ever heard... Scotty smirks at Jekyl as he looks up, squinting from the light. Scotty: Kind of unorthodox to find someone crazier than you? Jekyl: Very.... Continue... Scotty: Right, well this is where you think I'm crazy... You see I myself carry one of the 5 reapers, who has asked me to refer to her by the name of Sky...She is the ONLY female reaper. She talks to me when I need advice, she appears to me in front of my very eyes if she needs to, she possesses me if need be, and I talk with her when I have questions or seek guidance, but no-one else can hear or see her, sometimes I make the mistake of talking out loud. Jekyl almost flies out of his chair at this point in time as he is completely speechless, unsure of what to say he sits back and tries to take all this information at once. Jekyl: So let me get this straight... The world will end with a universal war between god and Satan. Scotty: Yep Jekyl: There are 20 souls meant to fight Lucifer... Scotty: To protect the throne... yeah... Jekyl: And you yourself are carrying one of these 20 spirits which is called a reaper named Sky, and you have a guardian animal spirit? Scotty: So far so good... Jekyl: You can hear and see your spirit? Scotty: Bingo...How much of this are you believing? Jekyl: Next to none of it..... Do you believe it? Scotty: I live it, of course I do... Jekyl leans back and rubs his chin, his scars seem to pop because of the lighting from the ceiling, his eyes nearly seem hollow, his train of thought leaves him and Scotty silent. Their breathing takes the awkwardness out of the silence.....For now...... Jekyl: Well Mr. McKnight... I think you and I will get along famously...or infamously...... To Be Continued?
