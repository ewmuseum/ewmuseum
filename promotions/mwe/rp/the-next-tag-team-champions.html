The next Tag Team Champions; Triple Threat tag team RP

Nov 17 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The camera turns on and we see Wraith and Ringmaster sitting in an empty alley with traffic going by and people ignoring them as they walk by. The 2 men look up at the people but they are still ignored. They look at the camera and Wraith starts talking. The Wraith Our first match in MWE and it is for a chance to wrestle for the Tag Team titles. Some people may say that we don't deserve this, that we need to work our way up the ladder first. You know what I say fuck the ladder, I went through hell my entire life and I deserve everything I get. You see this scar running down the middle of my face? My mother gave me this scar with a razor blade when she shot up a bad batch of heroin. So to all the people who say we don't deserve this shot FUCK YOU. Wraith sits back as he gives the finger to the camera and The Ringmaster leans forward a little bit and starts taking. The Ringmaster Even though I don't have any scars on my body my life has been just as much hell as his was. My mother was a crack head and I was usually in the same room while she was whoring herself out for a couple crack rocks. I've seen my mother get beaten till she was almost dead and I've been beaten up by her jons more times than I can count. The 2 of us have had to fight just to survive, nothing was handed to us by anyone! Not like our opponents in this match who think they are entitled to everything and think it all should be handed to them. Wraith gets a look of fury as Ringmaster talks about the Hooligans and interrupts him in mid sentence. The Wraith Ryan & Devlin Sharpe The Hollywood Hooligans. You 2 are the worst kind of scum on the planet, you think just because you are good looking you can do whatever you want whenever you want and that pisses me off. Wraith starts shaking in anger and he is unable to talk. Ringmaster pushes him back and he takes over. The Ringmaster Hooligans you've been a horrible team ever since you came to MWE. Your 1st match was against The Bowens which you lost. Your 2nd was against Project Revolution which you lost. Even after those 2 loses you were put in the Tag Team Title Match at Turmoil which you lost. You them got a match for another shot at the Tag Team Titles against The Bowens which you lost. You most recent Tag Match was another #1 contenders match against Project Revolution and you got your asses handed to you By Jason Fear and American Animal. Wraith jumps in and interrupts Ringmaster as he is talking. The Wraith You 2 have lost every match you've been in as a team and yet you still have a match where if you win you get a shot at The Tag Champs. This is what I'm talking about, if the 2 of you didn't have movie star good looks you would have been fired a long time ago. i hate the 2 of you more than anyone on this planet and we're going to make sure this is your last match in MWE. As The Wraith shakes with rage Ringmaster starts to talk again. The Ringmaster I may not be as vocal about it as my friend here but I feel exactly the same way. When you 2 step into the ring with the Triple Threat there will be no escape for you. We will finish what Project Revolution started on Mayhem and when we are done you will never wrestle again. All of a sudden Wraith jumps up, grabs the camera and brings it so close to his face you can see the fury in his eyes as he talks in a choked whisper. The Wraith Hooligans when we get done with you your movie star good looks will be nonexsistant because the 2 of us will destroy you and make sure you leave in an ambulance. Wraith stops talking and He gets up as does The Ringmaster and they both walk down the alley as they are obscured by steam and the camera fades to black.
