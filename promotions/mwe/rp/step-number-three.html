Step Number Three

Sep 13 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
*James Sullivan is seen with Stephen Arthur in his Mansion's main lobby. They are simply talking, as MWE cameras walk in to interrupt them. They are both wearing suits, but as far as the camera can see there is no reason why. They walk down to the living room type area of the mansion, and take seats across the room from each other. They look at the cameras and smile in unison. Stephen says something quietly to Sullivan, and he nods in approval.* James: I am guessing you want some thoughts before my "big" match this Sunday against Sinclair. Well, no problem. You don't seem to ask any other time you barge into my house, so why start now? Eh, whatever. *Stephen laughs a little, moves back, and adjusts his seat. Sullivan fiddles with his gold watch and looks around. Finally, just as MWE cameras zoom in a little, Sullivan looks back at the cameras.* James: Well, this Sunday Sinclair Mercier gets to face me one on one for the Mayhem Wrestling Entertainment United States Championship. It should be...fun. Yes, fun, that is a perfect word. See, I already won the match. I don't even have to set foot in the ring and I know that the gold will be coming home with me. Anyone who compares me to Alex Bowen seriously has no idea what he is getting himself into. I do not ever want to be compared to that man ever again. We are more different than even you think Sinclair. Unlike him, I will beat you, and there is just one way I am different from him. Stephen: Yeah, plus Alex doesn't have a three time world champion as a mentor either. Sinc, to me you are just like Aiden Copane. You talk and talk and talk, but what do forget to do. You forget to remember your opponents. You underestimate your opponents, something that James learned not to do last week. You say you love being arrogant and cocky, well then you are in for a world of pain, because I guarantee that James will not take that as a compliment. James: Actually, you are wrong. I could care less that he doesn't want to worry about me. Just an easy win that I can get under my belt, my United States belt for that matter. Stephen: Well in that case, keep on talking that smack because it will just come back to bite you. James: Exactly, you know that I love making people eat their words. Sinclair being cocky just makes me one step closer to becoming the champion and proving to the locker room that I am not a fluke. Stephen: Honestly, if anyone still thinks that you are a fluke, they are a fool. James: Look at Sinclair, he still thinks that I am a fluke. He compares me to Alex Bowen and rips on me for losing to a sadistic, twisted, disgusting, evil man that is Kanto. I would like to see him do any better. I underestimated Kanto, as foolish as that may have been, but after last Monday, I can say that Kanto is confident...but over confident, no. Stephen: Honestly, what he should look at is not the fact that you lost, but more of the fact that you survived that brutal move and didn't even go to the hospital. That, more than anything, is a feat in itself. A feat, that Sinclair will never achieve, I might add. James: Sinclair Mercier thinks he is tough, well there is no way in hell that he would have been able to do what I did. I lost...in style. I didn't cry, I didn't whine, I didn't pout. In fact, I realized how far Kanto would actually go to win. That, to be honest, is probably the smartest thing I could have done. Stephen: So back to the cameras. Sinc Mercier. this Sunday you will feel what James felt. And that is pain, because as well as he recovered from the blow that Kanto delivered, it still hurt. You will feel the misery of being beaten by someone who you ignored and put down. You will be beaten by someone who has only been in the company for a month. You, Sinclair, will feel the pain of someone who thrives to please his fans and win his matches. And even more than that, you will be beaten by a man who rightfully deserves to be called Mayhem Wrestling Entertainment's United States Champion. James: Do you ever stop talking me up. *James Chuckles* But I can not disagree. I will make you wish that you could take back what you said. I will make you want to rewind your life so you can say, I need to lose to Alex Bowen so I don't get destroyed in the finals. Not to mention you would want to save that crazy bitch of an ex-wife you have. Listen, you have to face the facts. The odds are against you. Just like they were against Xavier Stone, my first step to the United States Championship. You are smaller, slower, weaker, and older. I have all the attributes a superstar could ask for. I don't plan on having them go to waste. Stephen: Well, you seem to have this well under control, so I am going to leave. Sinc, you will lose, believe that. *Stephen stands up and so does James. They both walk to the door, exchange a small little handshake, and Stephen steps out first. The door shuts, and James begins to walk back to his spot in front of the cameras. *CRACK* ... ... *THUD* James: What the fuck was that! Oh, fuck no. No! *James Sullivan, protege of three time world heavyweight champion Stephen Arthur walks to the door, rips it open, and he is reduced to his knees. There, in front of him, is his mentor, dead, in a pool of his own blood.* James: No, fucking...I can't....How...Why...Who! *A man in all black runs from the trees to the left of Sullivan's house to Sullivan. Sullivan turns around, just in time to see himself get kicked in the face. Instant knockout.* ???: Consider this a warning and a message. I have a license to kill, don't test me or you are next. *MWE cameras back off and fade to black as the disturbing scene is set.* -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- By far, my worst RP ever. It was another last minute thing, as I keep forgetting to RP. I now have the site bookmarked and won't forget again.
