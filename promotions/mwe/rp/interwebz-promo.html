Interwebz promo

Nov 20 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Patience... Time... Karma... All these things have have their purpose here in MWE... All these factors make up what it takes to reach gold. You had to take TIME off because of an injury, you are losing your Patience with getting a title that should have been yours... but KARMA will take its toll and you will eventually regain what is rightfully yours. The scene is pitch black, not a single element of light or any form of life can be seen, but the words that are spoken are as clear as a bell in a hollow room. Scotty: Patience is not my strongest virtue; Time is something I can buy; And karma is a bitch... Sky: Well you need to learn to control you patience and bide your time, things don't happen over night. Scotty: Yes, but when will karma come back in my favor? When will it be my turn to have good shit happen? For several months I have had nothing but shit handed to my life... First was Jekyl, then was my injury causing me to lose my title shot, THEN I was sent back to a low life stage called ICW... and now I have to start back from the beginning... I'm sick and tired of this bullshit being handed to me over and over, time and time again. Sky: Well if karma wasn't in your favor, then you wouldn't of eliminated Jekyl and ended his career; Your injury could have been worse, and ICW was supposed to be a practice run for you, therapy if you will. Starting from the beginning is a good thing. Scotty: How do you figure? Sky: Well it allows you to regain your focus, your composure, and hone your skills for the better, it will make you world class material! People will see that you were coming close to a title not once, but twice, and the next time you go for that gold, you will succeed. Scotty: Fair enough... I got stuff to do and my knees are starting to hurt... I will talk to you more later... The scene fades in from the dark to a dimly lit room. The only light emanates from several candles scattered randomly in the room, which we can now slightly make out. The furniture has been pushed against the walls and a shag rug lies in the middle of the floor, the light flickering off glass on the walls suggests that they are indeed picture frames or something similar. Scotty gets up and grabs a candle off the mantle while blowing the others out. Using that one candle as his only source of light Scotty easily maneuvers himself to a wall and turns a dimmer switch, the automatic blinds retract and allow light to slowly enter the room, a small gust of wind from no where blows out the remaining candle in Scotty's hand. Scotty: My luck has had it's change for the worst and now it's time for it to change for the better. This Monday, I will step into that ring with a mission... Destroy or be destroyed, I will not take no for an answer, that 3 count will not fall upon my shoulders. Scotty walks down a short corridor into a small office like room, a cherry wood desk sits in the back by the window as pictures rest on the corners, a land line telephone sits beside a computer monitor that rests in the middle of the desk, he flips on the power to the computer, the screen flashes once and turns on instantly. Scotty proceeds to sign into MSN messenger to check his e-mails and see who is online. The Virus has signed on Scotty McKnight: Yo Virus. The Virus: Hey Scotty! S’up dude? Scotty McKnight: Not a whole lot. You? The Virus: Oh you know…kickin’ ass like usual. Though there was this ex-mafia guy who I almost obliterated a little while back. Scotty McKnight: Really? The Virus: Yeah. Let me put it to you this way. Nobody, under any fucken circumstances, hospitalizes my wife. Scotty McKnight: Shit... I can understand why you say that. Mind if I ask what happened? The Virus: It’s a long story. A really long one. Scotty McKnight: Ah. Well when we spar later, tell me then. The Virus: Sure. Scotty McKnight: Things are fine on my end. Though I have to admit, These past few months have been a real bitch for me. The Virus: Oh…why? Scotty McKnight: Well it’s mainly because of that injury I went down with in August. The worst possible time. The Virus: Still pissed off about that eh? Scotty McKnight: You have no fucking idea. The Virus: You got me there. Scotty McKnight: What irritates me the most, was the fact, somebody who I defeated to earn that spot, swoops in and takes what was supposed to be mine. The Virus: Yeah I can understand your anger towards it. But it seems you’re not the only person who has issues with Zack Crash. Scotty McKnight: Why does that not surprise me? Seriously, this guy is just somebody who grinds my gears. But I know exactly who you’re talking about. I don’t care what his past is, I have first dibs. The Virus: Perhaps, but apparently, Oblivion’s beef with Crash is far worse then yours. Scotty McKnight: I still say fuck him. I earned my spot in MWE. When I do take that title, it will only be a forewarning for the rest of MWE, when I shoot to the top. The Virus: I know. but in all honesty, I think that guys like us, are being held down by MWE management. Scotty McKnight: Yeah. You’re the Devil’s reincarnate, and I’m a Wiccan. That being said everyone thinks I’m fucked up in the head because of Sky. The Virus:…HAHAHAHA! I’m sorry, but compared to me, you’re sane. Look, I’m not a religious guy, except for the fact I am what I am, but I know that your shit is real as well. Scotty McKnight: THANK YOU!!! The Virus: But that’s not exactly what I was talking about. Ever notice that a lot of the guys from the original MWE have always been pushed more then guys who came in during the 2nd incarnation. Some have managed to overcome that, but that’s probably because they know how to suck cock. Scotty McKnight: eh Heh…wouldn’t be surprised. POLITIKZ!!! The Virus: But that’s the thing. They fear us. They fear the future. I say, we should not only become that fear, but overwhelm MWE management’s fear of the future. Scotty McKnight: True. The Virus: I might be the US champion right now, but if I wasn’t told to ‘tone it down’, I would have destroyed most of the roster by now. I’m sick of holding back Scotty McKnight: Same here. You saying we should form a team? The Virus: More like a cause. A revolution. Anyone who wants to take control will join us. The rest, they will all stand in our way, acting all defiant. But when we come to them….WE SNAP THEIR NECKS!!! Scotty McKnight: HA! The Virus: And right now, it’s at the best time. See, MWE is already in a war, against GWC. I am proud to represent GWC. At least everyone earned their spots there. But…when the war is over, and MWE has lost, we can begin our revolution there, and destroy MWE, and reshape it in our image. Scotty McKnight: I love the sound of that. The Virus: In the mean time, We have other priorities to fulfill. Speaking of, what are your plans for this week? Scotty McKnight: My official return to Monday Night Mayhem will not be forgotten. I will go out there, and eliminate each and every single one of them. I will hunt them all down. When I defeat them all, they will be marked. Every time they are in pain, they shall all remember the one who thrashed them beyond recognition. And that name, is Scotty McKnight. Virus: Amen brotha... Scotty McKnight: Listen dude, I got some shit to do before we meet up... I'll see you in a couple hours down by the docks.. The Virus: Sure thing dude, cya there... Scotty closes the window to the conversation and shuts the computer down, not having the time to check his e-mails. Standing up from his desk he leaves the room and heads towards the front door, grabbing his leather jacket off the banister, muttering to himself as he quickly moves by. Scotty: Better believe me, MWE has something new coming to them... the old Scotty is dead, It's time for a new man that shows no mercy, shows no hold bard.... show no regret or remorse... and Monday, those other 5 stunt cunts will be used as an example.. .starting with Josh Parker! Without missing a step Scotty hops in the drivers seat of his 2010 challenger, taking off into the down the street and into the distance. ----------- ------------ ------------ ----------- ------------ ----------- ----------- -------------- ------------- ---------- 10 hours later ----------- ------------ ------------ ----------- ------------ ----------- ----------- -------------- ------------- ---------- The sun has begin to set past the water of the bay, the dock deserted except for a new SUV parked beside a warehouse. Scotty parks his car and steps out into the cold crisp air, his breath lingers. A man stands at the end of the dock, staring out into the lake, his breath releases an eerie fog. Scotty walks up behind him and begins looking out into the lake as well. Scotty: You ready? Virus: Let's do it... The scene fades as Scotty and virus take a quick look at each other with a smile, ready for the fight ahead. -FIN-
