Brian Michaels on Jimmy Kimmel Live!

Dec 3 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The camera focuses in on a large set with TV cameras, a live audience, and a raucous band playing. They are blasting their instruments as Jimmy Kimmel suddenly appears on the screen, in his traditional tuxedo and slacks. The audience rises in applause as Kimmel holds up a DVD of Wrestle-Wars II on his desk. There is now scattered applause as the band stops. Kimmel: Welcome back to Jimmy Kimmel Live. Our guest tonight is a professional wrestler for Mayhem Wrestling Entertainment. He is the current MWE Champion, ladies and gentlemen give it up for, Brian Michaels! They clap as the lights turn back on as Brian Michaels comes out of the backstage area wearing a black dress shirt, dark blue jeans, white Air Jordans and a pair of designer sunglasses. Brian walks over to Jimmy, who is standing by his desk and shakes his hand. They both take their seat as the music comes to a stop and the audience stops clapping and Theory of a Deadman go backstage. Kimmel: We got you set up huh? How about that Theory of a Deadman? That’s better than watching Sal in a hot dog eating contest right? The audience chuckles at the thought of Jimmy’s cousin Sal throwing down hot dogs as Michaels readjusts himself in the seat. Kimmel: Anyway, we have MWE Champion Brian Michaels here with us. Brian lightly claps as the crowd once again cheers for Michaels. He smiles at the camera as Kimmel pulls up the Wrestle-Wars II DVD to show to everyone. Kimmel: Now, this is Wrestle-Wars Two, right? Michaels: Yeah, that’s the second Wrestle Wars. We had one over a year ago, but we went out of business. Kimmel: What happened? Was it the economy? They took away my house too. The crowd and Michaels both chuckle at the joke about the recent downfall in the economy as Michaels again lays back in his seat. Michaels: No, we had some creative differences within management, and they couldn’t decide what direction to go really. Kimmel: Like republicans and democrats? Michaels: Yeah, I’ve actually been accused of using ‘’politics’’ to move up on the card, but that’s not true. Kimmel: Yet you’re the current MWE Champion? The fans again laugh at the latest Kimmel comment, as Michaels shows a bit of a grin as well. Michaels: Touche. Ha, ha. Kimmel: Alright, what about your match with…who is it? Jimmy looks down at his blue card as the audience laughs at his fumble. He now sees the name and puts the card down as if he didn’t make a mistake. Kimmel: The…World Heavyweight Champion, D.C. So it’ll be you vs D.C. at Adrenaline Rush, correct? Michaels: Yes, D.C. and I actually used to be in a stable by the name of Rated-X. Kimmel: Sounds familiar. Brian grins at the comment, with Kimmel referring to WWE’s D-Generation X. Michaels: Yeah, it’s our ripoff of that ‘’other stable’’. But yeah, this match is about one year in the making, and it’s for the MWE Undisputed Championship. It’s a history making night, because it’ll be the first time MWE has an Undisputed Champion. Kimmel: And how did this whole feud come about? Brian leans forward and takes a drink of his coffee, conveniently placed at the table in front of him. He takes a sip as Kimmel is talking. He puts the cup back down, thinking of how to answer the question. Michaels: Well basically, he ran me over…with a car. Jimmy looks at him with dead seriousness, his eyes wide open. Kimmel: …Was it an expensive car? The government took that from me too. Jimmy can’t resist but bursting out with laughter, as does the audience as Michaels gives a bit of a smirk. Michaels: It was big enough to spark a rivalry, apparently. So far we’ve had two draws, so this next match is going to be what MWE calls Three Stages of Hell, where we go through three crazy matches and whoever wins two first, wins the Title. It’ll definitely be interesting. Kimmel: I’m sure your back cannot WAIT to take those bumps. Brian chuckles as he again takes a sip of his coffee. Kimmel: And it appears that MWE isn't being affected by the whole economic breakdown, they're still going strong and are even filming their own movie, from what I've been told? Michaels: Yup, Bleeding Freedom is a movie that we're financing right now, it'll be a really gritty war movie, one like you truly haven't seen before. It stars Joey Dallas, Sinc Mercier and Jason Fear and those three are really great guys, so it'll definitely be exciting what those three young guys can do in their respective roles. Kimmel: And Bleeding Freedom is out March 6th, 2009, folks. Michaels: But yeah, I wish had some good stories for everyone, but I can’t lie…the real reason I’m here? The chance to watch Theory of a Deadman AND Kanye West perform live. I just couldn’t pass that up. Kimmel: No, that’s my plug, you’re the guest, you can’t take my plug. Not cool. Anyway, Adrenaline Rush is December 21st, only on Pay-Per-View! Thanks to Brian Michaels for being on the show, and up next, performing his brand new single, Love Lockdown, Kanye West! He comes on-stage and shakes Kanye’s hand as the camera fades back to Jimmy. Kimmel: Thank you for watching, good night everyone!
