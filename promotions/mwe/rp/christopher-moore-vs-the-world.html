Christopher Moore vs. The World; Crowning Glory IV RP

Aug 20 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I remember this girl that’s been floating in my head for the past couple days now. I can picture her like it was yesterday. She had long brunette and red hair, flowing in the wind like she was on the cover of some mushy romance novel with Fabio on the cover holding her embracefully. Her body curved like no other. If I’m not mistaken, her body was a 40-32-40. Yeah...yikes is correct. Very rarely do you see women like that. And I’m a guy who if he had to pick between a supermodel and somebody with a little meat on her bones, I’d pick the latter. Don’t mistake me though, it’s not like I’m into the FAT women, even though there’s more cushion for the pushin’ if you catch my drift, heh heh. I just like for my women to look...normal. Not like they puked up 15 pounds in an afternoon. They also feel normal. Not fake or small. Just right. You can take that sentence any way you see fit, mind you. Her legs were as sensual as any woman I’ve seen in my life has been. Nobody had legs that smooth, that slender, that...wow, I am really getting carried away with myself here. Stop thinking like that and maybe you’ll live to see tomorrow. But her...just, looking at her every day, every minute, every second...it sent shockwaves towards myself. When in your life do you ever get to be with somebody as radiant, as dominate as herself in her absolute prime. There just isn’t anything else to say about her right now, except for maybe one thing. Just one little, tiny thing about her. What a fucking bitch. Yes. I said it. What of it? A bitch. Nothing less, nothing more. She has the uncanny power to completely trick you and bastardize the ever-loving hell out of you. How do I know? She’s done it to me, countless times. I wouldn’t even be in these kinds of situations if it wasn’t for her. And yet...there she is, stalking me like I was on the cover of whatever these fucking tweens read and listen to these days. Dare I even mention names. Every single time though I would crawl and weasel my way out of a tight spot, there she would be again, waiting, hoping, dreaming that somehow, she’s by my side. It’s kind of bizarre though. Because back in the day, when I first laid my eyes on her, I really did believe that the term “love at first sight” was actually real, was actually a formality. It was a random day in Philadelphia, just hanging out with the guys, watching the Phillies kick some ass like they usually do, on their way to the World Series (this happened last year, lest we forget) and as I was going to get something to eat, I accidentally bumped into her. I can’t really explain whom or what I saw? Hell, she didn’t look how I recognized her just a moment ago, because you couldn’t tell. She was wearing normal guy clothes. Hat, t-shirt, jeans. She was just enjoying herself just like every other fan that was in the ball park on that day, yet somehow our two paths crossed because some idiot (*points to himself*) didn’t see where he was going and spilled his shit all over her. But then we laid our eyes on each other and BAM! You start picturing her naked, sitting on a clam. What? You haven’t seen a naked chick riding on a clam before? Get out of here! Anyways, you try and speak, apologize, do whatever, but you start fumbling your words and she starts giggling like your IQ is somewhere below 90. It’s the typical cliched intro to a love story that you see in the movies, in the books, on TV. And somehow, it’s happened in real life. Who would’ve thought it up? I get my thoughts together and I get my words together and I apologize to her as we both get our bearings across and get back up from the grimy concrete floor. Hey, I’m even a gentleman and help her up. Even feeling her hand across mine sends a shock of lightning straight through my heart. I know I’m sounding schlocky and cheesy, but who wouldn’t? Have you ever had that feeling when your with somebody and your entire body just starts to go numb and it locks up on you. Yep, that’s the feeling with me now. I start to walk away though, feeling that whatever happened, just happened and nothing else, when she’s fast to catch up with me as we head back out to the stadium. “Hey!” No way. “You’re heading to the same section as where I’m sitting.” Ah. Karma. My favorite piece of work known to mankind. “Really? Where are you sitting at?” “Section 215" “Nice. Who are you here with?” “Bunch of friends. They like this game a little more than I do. I just needed to get out of the house. Who are you here with?” “Ditto.” “Works for me.” We both share a laugh at that point. You know, one of those awkward yet interesting laughs that random people share when they meet each other for the first time. Worst comes to worst however as she gives me a wave and starts to head back to her seat. I figure that I have one shot anyway, might as well go for it. What’s the worst that could happen, that she has somebody. Least she can be honest about it and tell me straight up. That would cushion the blow somewhat, then I can drink myself to death here at the stadium. Win-win, baby. “Hey! I didn’t get your name.” She immediately turns around to face me. “It’s Jennifer.” Now she’s walking back towards me as she gets back near me and hands me a piece of paper. Did she know I was going to do that? Is she psychic? “But you can call me Jen.” She walks away again, back to her seat as I slowly open up the piece of paper she gave me. On it...her e-mail and phone number. Magic...it has it’s own way of creeping up on you and working it’s weird ass stuff onto you, or somebody else. I just happened to be it’s assistant for today, that’s all. After the waiting period one man has to wait to call or e-mail or whatever to a girl when she gives you a piece of paper like that, I would end up calling her (because e-mail and texting, screw that. When was it so hard to TALK to somebody) and we would end up making arraignments for whatever it is we were going to do, I can’t remember. It was so damn long ago. All I remember is that it was a little more fancy than some ball game and that it was the first moment that I saw her in her full...how can I describe this? I saw her in her prime. Of course, she doesn’t look half as perfect as she does now, but she still looked absolutely gorgeous and it’s a complete bewilderment on my part on how she didn’t bag some lucky guy already. Of course, after dinner, one thing led to another...I’ll leave you to fill in the details on the rest of that sentence. But this was the norm for me for the next week. We just couldn’t keep our eyes off one another...let alone our hands, haha. And then the question that plagued me and the one that I mentioned just a few moments ago just hit my head like a sack of bricks. I had to ask her, I had to know. And late one night, after ANOTHER night of not keeping our hands off of one another, I asked that question. “How does a completely beautiful girl like you not already have a lucky guy at your side?” Of course, she giggled and told me a completely predictable answer. “Because you’re the lucky guy, silly.” Hey. I tried. Oh well, I was enjoying the time I had with her and that’s all a guy could give, right. I mean...come on, you think it was all about the sex? Well, the sex was just the icing on an already fantastic cake. I mean, she was funny, she was adorable, and she was absolutely beautiful. But then...maybe a couple weeks later, I got the answer that I was looking for. On why she’s been single this entire time. No, it wasn’t like I had to defeat her seven evil ex-boyfriends to win her heart. No, it wasn’t like that. Although I could’ve kicked their asses cleanly, that’s for sure. But it wasn’t that. It was a couple of weeks later. Somehow everything just stopped dead in it’s tracks. The smiles, the humor, the sex. I mean, we tried to keep all of that, but something just didn’t seem right. She seemed a little off actually. I tried asking her what was the matter but she just ignored me. I passed it off, thinking that it was her “time of the month” even though she was about 10 years older than I was. And I’m 25. Don’t judge me, I had no clue either until about a few weeks after we met. I was just as clueless as you all were just now. She doesn’t look 35, believe me. Yet, she wasn’t taking all my compliments and words and I thought if our relationship was just a two-week fling. I thought about leaving, and I was about ready to sit her down and tell her the grim news. But it was the weirdest thing. It was like the second I was going to tell her, “We’re through”, she suddenly perked up again, like she was the same old Jen that I met at the Phillies game. So I chalked it up to “standard female problems” on my list and we moved on with our lives. Or once again, so I thought. That’s when she really started to get a little out of place. It was the little things as well. She would get bitchy if somebody overcooked her meal, or when there was no nail polish in the grocery store. Little things like that. It wasn’t like she completely went postal or anything. Hell, I’ve seen “Falling Down”. And she was no Michael Douglas. But it was still creepy. Then, “it” happened. Don’t worry, I won’t go into too much information. It was just like any random night. We were in bed, we were watching TV. I thought to myself that I was in the mood and I tried to get her attention that I was feeling...you know. She wasn’t buying it. With me, I want to make sure that she was feeling okay, so I tried playing around with her. No avail. It was then where she got really pissy. I wasn’t aware, so I kept tickling her and playing around with her. It was then, from the top of her lungs, she yelled out... “GET THE FUCK OFF OF ME!” WHAM!!! She landed a right jab directly to my temple as I fell to the floor. Yeah, that hurt like a motherfucker. Immediately I thought that all hell was going to break loose and that she was going to rip my head off and shit down my neck. When in fact...she didn’t. I KNOW! It was like she had a random mood swing. She just clicked back to normal ol’ Jen and went right towards me. “Oh my god, baby...are you alright?” “WHAT DO YOU THINK? YOU FUCKING PUNCHED ME!” “I didn’t mean to.” “Then what was for all the yelling? All you had to say was “Hey, not in the mood.” and yet I get fucking punched for my troubles.” “I’m sorry.” “I...I...I don’t know what to say.” “I said I was sorry.” Yeah. Right. A simple apology for getting punched in the temple. She walked up to me though and wrapped her arms around my waist, gently kissing the bump on my temple that was slowly bleeding down the bottom of my cheek. Then, another bizarrely weird thing happened. She started to lick the blood off the side of my face, like she was strangely turned on by it. Turned on by violence, by the fact that she punched me. Her kisses turned more fast and frequent, and the next thing you know, well...yeah. Hey, I was still in the mood somehow. But afterwards, I accepted her apology. Just this once. Just this one time. The only reason I accepted it was hey...she was quick to rebound. Maybe it was just a flash of impulse, we all get it right? Wow, I realize all the excuses I’m making up here, aren’t I? Anyways, our semi-dysfunctional relationship continued as it was. Then, it happened again. Where I was “accidentally” punched out by Jen. I told her that it was the only time I was going to deal with this and I lived up to my promise. I didn’t want to leave her, she was the perfect woman. But if she was going to keep having these random bursts of violence and bizarre behavior, I couldn’t handle that. She was just too mental for my problems. And I left. And that was the last I would ever hear from her. For about 3 months. See, she never left me. She was always there by my side, stalking me like I was her pray. And the problematic thing was...when I had no one else to go to, I went back to her. Like a damn abused woman. And this went on for about a year. Where everything would be fine for about a couple months, then she go off and do something completely out-of-whack, I’d leave and then a few months later, I’d go back and do the same fucking thing over and over again. I know, I’m ashamed of myself, really...but, how many chances can you get of going back to somebody like her? And this has been going on ever since. Cut to right now...I’m sitting in a jail cell with a few other people. Not even looking at them, I’m looking straight, front, and center towards the wall. I guess I need to tell you all what happened? Simple. I got beaten up. Not by Jen, don’t worry...but by somebody who was trying to go out with her. I haven’t seen her in over 5 months, but I knew what this guy was up to. All I was trying to do was warn this guy that I went down the same path and it ended out badly. I was warning him that Jen has a few screws loose and she loves using the advantage of her beauty to completely fuck with the heads of any guy she meets. This douche wasn’t buying any of it. I told him that she would randomly hit me whenever I tried getting her attention. All he did was just laugh and pick up his cell phone. He was going to call her and let her know what was going on. For some reason, I just snapped and nailed him across the face. The bastard would say it was an uppercut, but it was a right jab. I don’t uppercut you unless you really deserve it. This poor fuck didn’t. Next thing you know, we’re on the ground, completely laying into one another. That’s when some random bitch calls the cops and arrests the both of us. Once again, back to right now. I’m staring at the wall, he’s laying on the bed in the next cell. I can barely see straight. I think I got a black eye from this. I can feel the blood drying up on my lip. I look like I just won a UFC contest against Brock Lesnar. Either way, my train of thought gets completely blindsided by these five words. “Christopher Moore. You sprung bail.” Now who the hell would do that? I have no friends on the outside who have the kind of money to do that. Maybe a family member did that, but then again...who in my family would do something like this? My dad would leave me in the fucking cell. That’s when the doors opened and in she walked. You know exactly who I’m talking about. It was like the room turned into a slow-motion clip from a movie, complete with Jimi Hendrix’s cover of Bob Dylan’s classic, “All Along the Watchtower”. Her image was striking. Even more striking then when I was with her. Remember, I haven’t seen her in awhile. I was kind of enjoying the company without her. But seeing her now...it was seeing her for the first time all over again. Her flowing fiery hair contrasted her creamy white complexion, her amber eyes like those of a cat, the low neckline of her bodice emphasized her voluptuous figure. Okay, man...getting ahead of yourself again. Stop it now. You know how crazy she is. How she’s ca-ca for Cuckoo Puffs. But...dear god. The officer opens my cell door as I immediately walk out, passing by her, not even glancing a look at her as I sign my way out of this piss-soaked hellhole. Not a word goes by between us. Maybe because she’s doing this as a one-time offer, or because she doesn’t want to bother the person in the cell who she could’ve bailed instead of me. The person who was going to become the next “Me”. Instead, she went for the classic. The one that never got away. A bastard guardian angel of sorts. I’ll be honest, I can’t stand her. I know that she’s completely batshit crazy, I know that she wants to treat my life as a living spectacle. But as I get into her car, and slowly yet angrily embrace lips, I think I finally realized something... She’s the only thing I have. And it’s the thing that’s going to be the death of me. === Fast forward my clock to a few hours. Somehow, she decided to give me a ride from Philly all the way to New York City. I’m on a roll, huh? The ride down was probably more worthy of a death stare than anything else. We talked, but it was for only a few minutes. The rest were either me sleeping, or me listening to the radio trying to tune her out. Thankfully, we reach the N-Y-C and my destination of choice...Madison Square Garden. Home to many classic wrestling encounters, many classic wrestling moments. Hopefully it’s my turn to add onto the list in a few days. As we head to the back and I show my credentials that, yes...I’m allowed to be here, we find a nice parking space and we both get out. That’s when far beyond my eyes can see, I can see a tall, young blonde standing near the elevator, smoking a cigarette. Suddenly I get a pinch in my neck as Jen starts giggling to me. Wasn’t very funny, but she thought it was. (This is where the camera would turn on, mind you) “Don’t stare, it only hurts the heart.” “I wasn’t staring. That would be Amy. My manager.” “Oh. You do good, I may repay the favor for you. I won’t spoil the surprise though. I think you’re in need of some good in your life.” She hands me a piece of paper as she rolls up her window and immediately drives off, the sound of her tires skidding across the concrete road inside the garage. Remember the good old days where I got a piece of paper and it meant the beginning of a beautiful friendship? She’s actually a pretty cool cat if I say so myself and she does for me as well. Amy’s the type of girl who doesn’t take no crap from anybody. If she has a problem with it, she’ll let you know about it, first hand. Amy’s been around for longer than I have though, but took a recent sabbatical from the world of wrestling to take care of her daughter, Ellie. Her husband, Jay has taken over the job now as he’s enjoying his retirement as well. Recently he released his own autobiography and is enjoying the success out of that. The Christopher Moore I know and respect is the type of person who’s going to lay waste to whomever gets in your way. I’m not a hugger, especially towards somebody like Amy. I grab my bag and follow her into the elevator. “You’re fucking weird, you know that?” “Pretty much.” Says me, Amy. Says me.
