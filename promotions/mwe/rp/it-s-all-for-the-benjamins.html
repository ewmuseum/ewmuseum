It's all for the $BENJAMINS$; Money Money Money

Aug 13 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
*MWE Monday Night Mayhem is the setting in which fans are roaring of excitement and ready for the next to begin. But there’s an interruption as Jason Fear appears on the titantron having a microphone on hand. He smiles and the fans boo.* Fear: Hey guys what up in the cheap seat over there. *He looks up as if were look at the higher tier of seats (acting like he could actually see). The crowd cheers asshole.* Fear: You guys are hurtful (kitty voice) and sickening (evil voice) *He has a serious reaction and then laughs* Fear: You know I got everything I would ever want. I have a career I could base my life on. I get the money you idiots waist on this meaningless show, iI have my Escalade parked in the back of the arena and incase you didn't know I was gifted by yours truly Sara Patterson last night but I’m still missing something. I’m missing gold around my waist. *Fans cheered when Fear mentioned Sara’s name but then booed what he added to the sentence* Fear: X-Television Champion is my first goal to greatness and once I succeed it’s straight for the United States title. I could see no one defeating Fear so that would cause me to purposely forfeit the title and soon rein as your MWE World Heavyweight Champion and become the King of Kings and rule the MWE Nation. * Fear raises his hands up high for about twenty seconds as he taunts at the fans* Fear: I could just imagine it. I have the picture in my head. I stand tall in the center of the ring anxiously for my opponent. Aeron Beowulf comes out with his X-Television Title on hand and he enters the ring. Now we’re face to face in front thousands in attendance and of millions watching at home. I look into his eyes and I could tell he really does not want to be inside this ring with Fear in the other corner. I smile and strike first and that the conclusion because he just gives up and runs away. The face you are looking at in this moment will be your next X-TV Champion whether or not your happy about it. I am Jason Fear and you will regret not being in my side because assholes like J.K Valentino who try to be nice to you for the money. You fans should be smarter and make a change. Take the side of the king and you’ll be glad to always have a champion. * The crowd roars Bullshit!!(3 times)* Fear: You guys are so negative. Just like Mr. Covell. I enter his office suited up since he’s the boss. He says hi and I reply back dah dah dah. My question was “Have this imagine in your head Richard, Jason Fear X- TV champion, More money…. more fans. Richard you are a businessman and you’ve been one for many many years, you are smart enough to react when money is up to grabs so screw Aeron and anyone how comes your way with this issue just make the right choice and give me the chance to earn gold.” Well he thinks about it and looks at me and then says” Well Jason you are a real son of a bitch you know that. You care for no one and fans hate you. If you become champion “ he kept quiet and said………. *Richard Covell’s Music plays and he comes out with a microphone ready to trade some words with Fear.* Covell: Jason, Jason, Jason *Covell nods his head in disgrace* Fear: Richard, Richard, Richard. How are you feeling old man. Covell: Great. And you. Fear: Just fine Covell: So I see you were talking about me being unfair and not giving you a shot at gold but I was thinking it will make money if the fans see you get your ass whip by Aeron Beowulf but to make even more money how about a five man elimination match for the X-TV Championship title. *Fans Cheer as they’re excited for the event accruing tonight on Monday Night Mayhem* Covell: It’s going to be Aeron Beowulf, Jason Fear, Jeff Austin, J.K. Valentino and Gus. Now that what you call Mayhem. Fear: Wait A second Richard is that all you have against me? All those juniors? Thanks a lot Covell, you’re just making things simple. Covell: You’ll see, Ill show ya. Once you get in that ring you’ll regret accepting the challenge. Covell Gets out the ring and Fear has an angry face on as Covell walks away* Ryan Adams: You heard the boss David it’s gonna be a 5-man elimination for the X-TV champion ship title David Ferrera: Sure I did Ryan, and believe when the boss says it’s gonna be Mayhem.
