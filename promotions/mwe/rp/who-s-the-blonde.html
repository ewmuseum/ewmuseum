Who's the Blonde?; Eric Derf VS American Animal

Jan 19 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The scene opens on a large white house, huge green yard, and some fancy trees. It looks like it could have cost millions to build, something only a rich lawyer would live in. The shot is from the opposite side of the street, as the road can also been seen at the bottom of the frame. The sun is just starting to set, as the sky is a bright orange. It's dark enough to see that almost all of the windows in the house are lit up, and only to the people with the best vision, it can be seen that there are multiple people in each of the windows. The house is packed, and the hum of music can be heard all the way on the other side of the street. In this large mansion of a house, is a party. Suddenly, a cab pulls up to the side of the street. Only stopped for about 15 seconds as the passenger gets out and grabs his luggage out of the back, the cab pulls away. The passenger has three items of luggage. The first, is simply a gym bag. The 2nd and 3rd items, however, are far more meaningful. The X-TV Title, and the Havoc Title. Those two items alone reveal the identity of the man who just got out of a cab, Eric Derf. Literally holding his luggage with his hands wrapped around it all, he carefully sets it all down on the ground, minus the Havoc Title. He wraps that title belt around his waist, covering the top of his blue jeans, and the bottom of his Mecca T-shirt. He then picks up the 2nd belt, the X-TV title, and wraps it around his waist as well, hanging just above the Havoc Title. He then uses his feet to kick his gym bag up in the air, throwing his hand out to grab it in midair. At the moment he grabs it, he lets out his first vocal reaction so far. Lets party it up!!! Although no one is around to hear his speak, he quickly begins his short walk to the front door of the giant house. As he reaches the front door, the music, rave, can be heard allot louder, almost as loud as a live MWE event. He then realizes, he can't make a grand entrance to a house party with his gym bag, that's just not cool. He looks around, wondering is theres a place he can put his gym bag. He notices a small window near the bottom wall of the house, probably leading to the basement. He quickly jogs over, and stuffs his bag into the window, letting it drop with a "clunk" as it hits the ground inside. He then gets up, brushes himself off, and walks to the door. Alright Eric, time to party it up! He doesn't knock. No one knocks at a party. He throws open the door and steps inside. Instantly, people swarm him. Not being a celebrity party, most people are shocked to see a famous person at a social event like a house party. Chants of "Derf! Derf!" and "Oh My God, he's here!" begin to flow through the house, and Eric's just stepped in the doorway. Eric reaches behind his waist with both arms, and unattached the belts, he throws his arms up in the air, clinging to the belts, as people continue cheering. Your champion has arrived! He pulls the belts back down and tosses each one over his shoulders. As he slowly moves through the crowd, they slowly go back to dancing, although there are still a few clingers, people that are starstruck. Eric continues making his way through the crowd, grabbing an unopened beer from a cooler on the way. He enters a room with a pool table, and 4 other people playing pool. Eric notices a 5th person watching, tall, young looking. Shaggy hair, 5 o'clock shadow he looks like your typical jock wearing gym pants and a school jacket. He walks up to the jock. Hey Matt man, what's going ON! He talks in a sort of fake rapper voice, it's obvious he's exited. Hey man, nothing! Just chilling. Welcome to my party man! What's a Celeb like you doing here anyway? Oh man, I needed a place to party. I mean, I won the Havoc title last night, I HAD to do something tonight! I needa get drunk, I need get laid, I needa get FUCKED UP! Matt laughs as Eric takes a drink of his beer. Oh man, you came to the right place! Theres a cooler over there... Matt points to a cooler in a different room, barely noticeable by the traffic of people. ... Theres some bedrooms upstairs, for some "adult entertainment"... Matt points in the other direction to a stairwell. ... And in the basement, we have a room that's gonna be filled with smoke... If you know what I mean. He chuckles as Eric finishes chugging his first beer. Dude, you know I don't smoke that shit. I'm not a drug kinda guy, I can't do that stuff. I'd be looking at a fine with work if I got caught doing that shit! And I can't risk losing either of these babies for drugs. Eric shrugs both shoulders, lifting his title belts up a bit. He stares at his X-TV Title as if it's his firstborn child. By the way man, what's the deal with those titles? Ain't one of em going to be merged with the other or something? Or are you keeping em both and defending em both? Eric looks away from the X-TV title and down at the Havoc Title, with a slightly less interested look on his face. This title... It's not much of a title. I mean, how many champions have held this title? 2? I am glad I won this belt, obviously. But it's prestige level, I personally don't think it's worth much. Unlike the X-TV Title, this title has been held by guys most people know. JJ Executioner? Milenko? How many people know these guys? Now, THIS title... He holds up his X-TV Title, and shifts his eyes from one belt to the other. This title has been held by some of the best in this business. Our former Champion, DC, has held it. Paige has held it. Many men have held it, and I plan on being the longest running champion we have. I plan on bringing so much pride to MWE with my title run, that the X-TV title will be one of the greatest belts in not just MWE, but all of the fedding world. He kisses his belt as Matt looks on. So... Your retiring the Havoc Title? Eric, still looking at the X-TV Title after kissing it, hands the Havoc Title to Matt. Here, have a gift. Give it back in the morning, take some pics. You can be the last man to ever hold this title. Have a blast with it. Matt gets an uber exited look on his face, and is about to run off to show off the belt to people, when Eric grabs his shoulder. As Matt turns around to him, he see's Erics face blank, staring into space. Dude, what's wrong? You Aight? Eric points forward, and as Matt looks, he see's three very attractive looking ladies standing across the pool table. A blonde, a brunette, and a redhead, all wearing short black dresses. Those ladies... Are HOT! Dude, you gotta introduce me! Matt laughs as he looks at Eric, shocked that he just asked him to help pick up. Well man, I can introduce you, but I gotta tell you... Those three girls, they've got some confusing names. You'd best make sure you know em before you ask em, they get pissed off when people ask their names. Eric is still staring. He's so attracted to these girls, he's barely gripping his X-TV Title as the bottom of it rests on the ground. His mouth is almost hanging a foot from the ground, figuratively speaking. Well, their names.... Umm... Who's the Blonde, What's the brunette, I don't know's the Redhead... Eric interrupts him. Well that's why I'm asking you! I wanna know their names! Matt snaps back. I just told ya! Who's the Blonde, what's the Brunette, I don't Knows the Redhead- Do you not know their names? Yes. Well then who's the blonde? Yes. The blonde? Who? The blonde right there! Who? That hot blonde right there! Who is the blonde!! Well what are you asking me for! I'm not asking, I'm telling you! Who's the blonde! I'm asking YOU who's the blonde! Both men are getting frustrated at each other. That's the blondes name! That's who's name? Yes! Ugh!! Eric is growing more and more frustrated by the moment. All I want to know, is what is the name of the Blonde? No, no, no... what is the brunette. I'm NOT ASKING who the brunette! Who's the blonde! I don't Know! That's the Redhead. We're not talking about her. How the heck did the Redhead get into this? You mentioned her name! When did I say who's the Redhead? No! Who's the blonde! Oh my god man, all I want to know, is what is the name of the blonde. No, I already told you what's the brunette! I'm not asking who's the brunette! Who's the blonde? I don't know! She's the redhead! Eric just stops speaking and stares at Matt as the music stops. He's become quite irritated, and it's obvious by the crowd of people that have gathered to watch them. Eric, realizing he's got an audience, turns around, and simply waves. That one wave sets off a huge reaction of people, as everyone screams and cheers while the music comes back on. People eventually go about their business as Eric turns to Matt again, who seems embarrassed. He's now holding he Havoc title over his shoulder, similar to Eric holding the X-TV title over his own. Dude, I told you they got fucked up names... Eric just stares a blank stare. Dude, I'll just go find someone else. I gotta get around this place, make some noise, and party it up! Aight, have a good night, and get FUCKED UP! Will do! Eric walks away, reaching into a cooler for another beer, as people in a different room begin cheering. *** The next scene opens in a small bedroom, the morning sun shining in on everything. A nice bed with baby blue sheets right in the middle of the room, the blankets ruffled up. As the camera gets closer, we see two people in it. One is Eric Derf, topless. The other, a skinny Asian girl with short cut blonde hair. Both have the blankets pulled above their chests, both asleep, or at least they were. Slowly, Eric slowly begins to stir, and rolls out of the bed, wearing nothing but grey briefs. He walks towards a bathroom and closes the door. The usual bathroom noises can be heard, a flush, running water, and a silence, for at least a minute. When the door opens back up, he exits the bathroom wearing a pair of shorts, black, and the same shirt he'd been wearing the night before. He walks over to the bed, where the Asian girl continues to sleep. He reaches for her face, brushing his hand across her cheek, before reaching past it, and inbetween where he and her were sleeping, picks up the X-TV Championship. Still tired, he mearly holds the belt by the center, the ends dragging just above the floor, as he exits the room. The music from the night before can still be heard, but at a much quieter level. People are scattered all over the place, most asleep on chairs and couches, even on the 2nd floor. A few people, awake, wave at Eric as he walks down the hall towards the stairs. They say nothing, as it would break the nice morning silence. He reaches the stairs and slowly moves down them until he reaches the bottom. The first thing he notices, is his friend Matt passed out on a couch, the Blonde, Brunette, and Redhead all laying on top of him, and the Havoc Title sprawled across all four of them. Eric smiles as he walks over and quietly picks up the title, trying not to disturb them. By this time, he's a little more awake, so he tosses it over his shoulder, followed by the X-TV title on his other shoulder. He continues past his friend towards another door, the one earlier labeled the basement. He opens the door, and steps in, closing the door behind him. Now in the basement, Eric walks around. This is no regular basement, it looks like a regular apartment in some ways. Many doors cover the walls, a couch and an old TV in the middle of the room. Eric looks around, thinking for a moment, opening a door, the closest one to him on the right. As he opens it, a huge ball of smoke begins to pour out, causing him to close it right away with a "SLAM". He waves the smoke out of his face, coughing a bit, before moving along to the 2nd door. He reaches it, opens the door, an steps in. This is the exact room he was looking for. A Gym room. And more ironically, at the far wall, is an open window, the same window he'd dropped his Gym Bag in the night before. He takes his shirt off and walks towards a weight bench, with about three weights on each side, but it's unclear as to exactly how much weight is there. Eric don't mind, as he lays down, and begins lifting weights. Minutes go by, Eric continues lifting the weights. As he lifts, the door opens, and a young man walk in, dressed the same as Matt from the night before, with a school jacket, and pair of jeans. He enters the room seemingly unaware of who is in it, as he simply walks to a treadmill and begins running. Both men ignore each other for a little while, before the guy on the treadmill speaks up. So, your that Wrestler guy that was at the party last night, right? Eric don't look at him, he keeps lifting. Yeah. Eric Derf. MWE Superstar, X-TV Champion. And you are... The name's Glenn. And I'm a big fan of yours Mr Derf. Eric sets the weights down and looks at the kid. I thought you didn't know who I was? The guy stops running on the treadmill and walks over to Eric, who has sat up. He extends his hand for a handshake, which is returned. Man, I know who you are! Forgive me if I seemed shy. I'm a huge fan. Eric stops shaking as he lays back down to continue his workout. The guy, Glenn, sits on a nearby bench as he continues talking. Wow... Look man,I work for my school newspaper. Do you think I could get an interview? Eric once again stops lifting and sits up again. You got some questions for me? Glenn gets exited, clearly, as he begins to shift around the bench, to exited to sit still. Alright, well... What do you plan on doing as X-TV Champion and Havoc Champion? Eric looks at the two titles sitting on top of his gym bag as he thinks about the question just asked. Well, I can tell you right now, that you'll probably never see the Havoc Title again. Once I take it home, I'm hanging that baby over my fireplace, and leaving it there, as a reminder that I was the last man to ever hold that gold. Now, my other title... The X-TV Championship. I plan on taking that title, and defending it as much as possible. I don't want to be one of those guys that wins and loses the title right off the bat, I don't want to be another random stat in the titles history. I want to be the longest running X-TV Champion ever, and when I finally do have to lose it, my first goal is higher gold. And don't think I won't find higher gold. Look at my path so far. I made my debut in a tournament for gold. Then I won gold. My next week, I won more gold, and now my next match, I face yet another champion in "The American Animal" Josh Callaway. I'm like a Gold Magnet. Champions are attracted to me, they want a piece of me. And when I beat Animal, I will continue to face Champions until I have beaten em all. Eric stops talking, as he feels the question has been answered. He looks at Glenn, who has seemingly pulled a notepad out of nowhere. Anything else? Glenn stops writing and looks up. Good answer... Another question, what do you think of your opponent, American Animal? Eric stands up and walks towards a towel rack as he begins speaking again. Well I don't know much about Josh Callaway, or American Animal, as you all know him. I do know one thing about him though, and it's that when we face off, he won't be in his right state of mind. You see, before our match, his partner, Jason Fear, has his own match. A Last Chance Match. As you probably already know, when you lose a last chance match... Your gone, no matter what? Eric nods in agreement. Exactly. And if Jason Fear is fired, then he's stripped of his Tag Team Championship, which would, in return, cause American Animal to lose his half of the Tag Title. And I don't know about you, but I know if I went into a match knowing I'd just lost my gold, I wouldn't be in a right state of mind. I'd be a little pissed, I wouldn't care about winning a match, I'd just want to beat someone up. Well Animal might want to think about something. If Jason doesn't win, Animal loses a Tag partner. He becomes a singles star. So if he was wise, he would start expecting to lose his title right now, so that the blow was less hurting. So that he could prepare for his singles run. He's facing one of the hottest young superstars in myself, a champion. If he was wise, he'd make sure he was ready for me. Ready for singles gold. Ready to fight for himself, no backup, nothing. Because when Jason Fear don't show up, your all alone. You'd best get used to it early. Eric walks towards his Gymbag, lifting the X-TV Title up to his face. American Animal. I don't care about your past, I care about right now. I'm offering you right now, your first match as a singles superstar. Make it a good one. If you want to get places by yourself, I'm your first stepping stone. I'm your ladder to success. I'm the measuring stick you need to pass. I'm the X-TV Champion. If you want to get someplace, you need to beat someone. Eric stops talking as he smirks, and Glenn notices the pause and looks at him, excitement in his eyes. But then again, on the flip side... Don't think for a second I'm just gonna hand you a win. I didn't become Champ by letting people beat me. I go out there each and every week, and I give 100% of what I got. When I'm not in the ring, I'm building myself up, raising my 100% higher, and giving even more. If you want to compete at my level, you've got alot of work to do. And with myself working almost all the time to beat everyone at any time, your gonna have to use every waking second to train for me. Because if you want to make a name for yourself, you need to beat someone big. And that's what I plan on doing. As big as you may be, I plan on beating you. I plan on moving past even more gold, and I aim to become on of the best and brightest young superstars MWE has EVER seen! And you know what? Call me cocky, but there is no way you can beat me, once I think about it. You might be an American Animal, but I'm the Champion of Canucks. I'm above you, figuratively and literally. And come Monday Night Mayhem, after your forced to drop your Tag Team title, I'll offer you a chance a a singles career. And destroy you in the process. Eric picks up his Gym Bag and Havoc Title, and leaves the room as Glenn continues writing down the interview he just had, still star struck.
