Serenading A Dahlia.

Oct 25 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The soft familiar tune of a piano fills a darkened concert hall. With the acoustics in the room you could swear it was a quartet, but the cold, nimble fingers stroking the keys was only the doing of a single man lost deep in his gratuitous melody. The lighting is dim, created by studio lights high above the main stage of this giant music hall, only half of the man’s body is seen by this fading light and burden of cheap musical equipment. He is wearing a black jacket and black jeans, not necessarily the properly dressed type. His face is young and filled with life. The rest of his body is bobbing and melting into the darker part of the room as he adjusts his seat and slides his fingers across the piano like it was his favorite toy. This tune sounded alike to Beethoven’s Moonlight Sonata, except it seemed to be played with a harsher lower vibrato than the original score. The passion of the solo could be felt inside the room, shaking its very foundation and screaming to be heard, but there was only one person watching this deep performance. His name was Jackson Gabes, Jackie Black as he likes to call himself, and he was watching his pupil, friend, and colleague perform on stage, the one playing the piano is Tristiano Slater, or Tristan Slade as he has appropriately labeled himself, MWE’s newest asset. Once the performance was complete, Jackie applauded him with a cigarette in his mouth burning to its core. Tristan just kinda sat there, shrugging his shoulders and stretching his now tired fingers like it was just another hobby to add to his…collective…of talents. JB: The Malice of Mind are we? Moreso in this moment, The Talent of Mind huh? I don’t understand why you want others to hate you so much Tristan, your brilliant, you could be the wild card of the people, surprise them every time with your many creative ingenuities. Tristan: I will NEVER succumb to giving those dim witted followers of common moral conduct what they…WANT. I give them what they need, someone to hate and force them out of rooting for the kid friendly mascot of our terrible and wounded times. It will be like making the blind see, except not with hands or touches of light and compassion, but the dirtied and darkened palm of the troll under the bridge. I’m their wicked witch of the west JB, except I don’t fucking melt, and holy water isn’t gonna work on this apparition. Jackie puts out his cigarette smashing it into the arm of his seat as he sits surrounded by the stench of velvet and the structures of organized freshly cleaned theatrical chairs. Tristan is eyeing him down on stage, with that “I’m creative and your not” look on his dashing yet sinister face. JB at this point is just smiling doggishly letting Tristan think whatever he’d like. JB: What are you trying to say Tristan? You don’t want to be the center of attention? A necessary twinkle to the public eye? If you don’t want to sell yourself to the people to give you more of an opportunity to roll in bundles of cash, then you’ll never pay back your debt to the poor children of that foster home. Being the BAD guy is hardly the answer. Tristan: Screw them, they did nothing for me, they passed me off from family to family like a diseased pet. Think what you want, but I’m not going to be the hunchback of fucking Notre Dame here, I’m gonna be their worst nightmare, and maybe, JUST maybe, there may be one person out of that crowd of damnation that can see what I’m trying to pull off here, the world’s GREATEST catastrophe, Tristan Slade coming to the MWE, and giving off an explosion of reality’s poisonous air. JB: Oh, that’s rich, apparently you’re a Bat Man villain now. I give up. Tristan: That would be true except I am not a cliché comic book cutout. I’m more….philosophical than the average villain. More methodical than the Grinch who stole fucking Christmas, more tactical than a Metal Gear Solid vigilante, I’m…me. The piss stain on Dahlia‘s favorite pair of pants, his arch nemesis, his VICE. Take this piano for example, and how I can define it as more than an instrument, but a weapon of mental fortitude. Tristan slides his hands up and down the piano pressurizing the keys then carefully going from high to low tunes with a sudden need in his mind to explain what he is doing, to somehow make it a metaphor for JB and his opponent to understand as he goes about his actions in a sequential manner, to better pair with what he is about to say. Tristan: You see, the higher keys are louder, more aurally demanding, and much more upbeat, while the lower sounding keys seem almost forceful, dark, and eerie. Much like the higher pitched key itself, the upper class system of society is always more out there so to speak, constantly making themselves known through sights and sounds, media and big name companies to become rich and fill their spirits and hearts with gluttony as they snicker and snort over a bowl of fuckin Cheerios. All this while third world countries collapse in front of their disgusting muzzles, food dripping from their mouths out their asses. Filled with shit and a deep ignorance, they simply continue to treat themselves to society’s many illustrious gifts. JB: Well, I wouldn’t exactly call them gifts, they DID work for them you know. More like, agreements…. Tristan: Well these AGREEMENTS are a giant cock smashing the egos of the underclass. Now the lower keys, or for metaphor’s sake, the lower class, their not as, how to say this properly for those politically retarded, in-your-face as the louder more attractive, more wealthy bunch. They are rarely heard, they play no giant part in a tune filled with happiness or joy, no, their role belongs with the depressing songs of sorrow and banishment. The underclass are the low keys, dancing to the beat of a fat corporate ass hole’s drum, sucking off the teat of their revolting man boobs, suckling to stay alive under a neglecting parent’s rule. JB: Wait…now Tristan….would MWE be the tit or the one suckling it like a premature lion. Tristan smirks standing up from his seat and jumping off the stage leaning over a chair to shove his face in front of JB’s nose, front and center, to strike his point across completely in order for JB to understand his odd…euphemisms. Tristan: MWE…is the very…VERY thin line between the two. The middle note, the one wild card that can be swayed any which way. All we have to do, is make sure we aren’t part of the lower class working under a mighty fist, or, for that matter, the ass kissing higher ups, we simply need to make sure people see that WE…are right, and everything else, will be a bonus. We will be our own bosses, MWE, is our playground. JB: I don’t care much for playgrounds. Tristan claps his right hand into a stabbing gesture with the forefingers touching the thumb signaling JB to shut his mouth and let Tristan speak. Tristan: Listen to me, and listen well. In order to generate any sort of following or warning to the locker room or to anyone else that cares to pay attention to our doings, it is essential that I win this first match, ESSENTIAL JB. Dahlia is just a stepping stone, I need to get to the pearl stairwell and open the gates of MWE with a fucking bazooka and a shotgun to get the attention of the entire company AND everyone else watching. Tristan Slade and Jackie Black will become household names turned kings. But to get to that high point, we need to prune a few thorns on the way. And from what I’ve studied, Dahlia will be quite the thorn. JB: I do recall I’m not much of a florist either, your metaphors and philosophies are starting to irritate me Slade. Tristan clenches his teeth and sighs his head trying to prevent himself from losing his composure on his long time friend and mentor. After a moment of collecting his feelings Tristan straightens his jacket and brushes his bangs away from his face to leave towards the projector room upstairs seeming to want alone time away from JB. Jackie shakes his head and shouts at him as he walks away. JB: You know I’m right, you can’t be the rebel all the time Tristan, you need more to go off of. Tristan keeps walking. JB: Fine, be my guest, have your alone time, but remember who’s had experience in the business and who’s still the student here child. Come back to ol’ Jackie when your done moping. Tristan stops as if he was about to turn around before taking a detour towards the projector room, hesitant, he goes on, leaving JB’s words in the wind. As Tristan finally leaves the scene JB takes out another cigarette and lights it with his vintage Harley Davidson lighter and whispers to himself. JB: Why thank you Jackie you were right, having a positive image in a company IS a good thing….jesus what have I gotten myself into…. And with that, we join Tristan, sluggishly walking to the projector room in a narrowed ascending hall up some sketchy wooden stairs. He swings open the door to the room with a loud creak and slams it shut sitting on a small dusty sofa just behind the projector machine eyeing the empty stage from above not bothering to look down at JB enjoying his tobacco. Tristan gazes at the empty stage and thinks of the emptiness deep inside a man like Dahlia’s heart, a broken, desperate man. Tristan: A victim of bullying, a former police officer, blossoming fresh from a torn relationship with his darkened family. And the icing on the cake, of course, his one true love murdered just before marriage. Now pardon my language, but to me, this sounds like a cluster fuck and a bad remake of the Sixth Sense. If Bruce Willis and Charles Manson were in cahoots, and adopted a child, that child would have Dahlia’s background and a disastrous drive to be violent and potentially insane. Now, if you dare ask me why someone like Jason Dahlia would even be in the right mind to wrestle in a ring to begin with after all that’s happened to that poor delusional fool, I’d actually say….why not? Tristan giggles at the thought of Dahlia going toe to toe with him inside the MWE ring. It is hard for Tristan to picture without putting a straight jacket around Dahlia and shoving a handful of Ritalin down his throat. After all, Tristan doesn’t want to be accused of beating up a mentally handicapped individual now does he? But oh how much fun it’d be. Tristan: Is Dahlia crazy? Maybe, but I’m crazier. Like him I’ve had a very dark past. Now he may poke fun at me for growing up in a foster home, question my ethics and my morals as a parentless fiend, but really, it actually made me smarter, learn from my mistakes so to speak. I never needed any sort of parental guidance or comfort to forge the man I am today, and I’d like to think I came along quite nicely. I mean, I’m far more smarter than Dahlia or any of these other imbeciles in the MWE locker room, I understand how corrupt large businesses and corporations can be these days, and I know that as I speak, the MWE executives, the staff members, even the owner herself, realize that I, Tristiano Slater, am untouchable as long as I know their…unrighteous…set of rules and codes of conduct. You can bet your ass I’ll go as far on the edge as I can to tear out the heart of this business inside out until I get every single one of those doubting me to agree with my ideals. I will be the hand of god in this miserable waste land. I will give life to the dying, and teach the uneducated. Tristan stands up from the couch, his eyes sharp and focused, his body tense and unnerving. His mind is obviously in some other place, he’s not just in a theater or projector room anymore, he’s in his land, his sweet escape. Everything around him is his own imagination at this point, he has christened the room to his blessing, and the world is his own thoughts. He grabs a film reel from underneath the projector and inserts it into the slot of the machine clicking a mechanism and prepares to roll the reel. Tristan: I may be a troublemaker, I may have debts to pay and dues to go through with, but I’m no fucking pushover. Dahlia is going to be forced to see the type of force he is going to reckon with. He will become the leper I will cure and then castrate out of spite. There’s a reason why the last company he had partaken in shut down, because he is a rodent infested with the plague, forgotten to be executed before deciding to destroy the next federation he comes across. This time, it won’t be the federation that dies. I will break him down mentally and physically to a point of no return, he will thank me for ending his career before he has the time to ruin another promotion. I will be his new beginning, and his only end. Tristan presses a button on the projector and a light emits in a square shape onto the wall of the stage across from him. The projector slowly starts to load an image and it begins fading in to the wall in a display of dark shakiness. Tristan: Dahlia may have found a new girlfriend to fondle, a new outlook and a new chance to make his life mean something again after his twisted avenue of ins and outs in his past, but I’m still the fog in the way of his bright new horizon. I won’t let him leave that ring come Monday without wishing he never crossed paths with The Malice of Mind. A man who’s finishers are named after his own contradictions, genocide…seclusion…still lost in his demonic past when he wants to start new again. Still trapped but not willing to admit it. The only love and happiness dear Jason may find is that cloud of depression that will not stop hovering over his mindless little head. He needs that cloud just like plants need rain, to nourish him, to drive him to the next chapter in his life, making him hope and pray that someone will come along and pluck him right out from the ground to branch elsewhere. Well Jason, those branches are very weak, and if you honestly believe some new hot little blonde with a last name resembling a dragon is going to save you from your own shit storm, your sadly mistaken. This is why I made this for you Jason, to show you that Tristan Slade will forever be in your thoughts eating you alive until you finally snap and become your own bitter finale. The projector makes a loud buzzing noise all the sudden and the image flashes brightly and clearly on the wall. JB looks ahead slightly jumpy at the image as he sits from beneath Tristan in the projector room with an awe. JB, after the image fades away turns around and looks at Tristan in the projector room in worriment. Tristan is simply seen standing there in his continual mindset dark and withered. Focusing on Tristan’s facial expression he smiles and utters some words. Tristan: Jason, you best bring your crystal chalice, because in your mind, I AM the malice. The theater lights go out moments after he says this and turn back on. Jackie Black and Tristan are now nowhere to be found. The same image pops up on the wall again and now plays in a loop until finally the scene cuts to static. Happy Halloween.
