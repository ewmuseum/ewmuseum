~~August 17th, Monday Night Mayhem~~ Leon was in the back, after being blindsided by the Englands. He can honestly say that He didn’t see that one coming. Not by a long shot. He expected Chris England to beg for mercy. But this turn of events didn’t make the Embodiment of Sin mad at all. In fact, for everything to work, this just proved that Chris could stand on his own two feet, and that he could be worth the trouble afterall. Leon: Not surprise I lost tonight. Couldn’t count on my partners worth shit. His head was still reeling from being hit with that move that got him eliminated last. He didn’t stumble a lot though. He had since recovered. Enough by the time he challenged Chris England, it hardly looked as if he had fought earlier. He goes to his dressing room, after managing to get a bag of ice on the way there. A few days later, August 20th to be exact, Leon was currently working out in his gym at home. His insanely huge basement had many rooms, and one of them was both a wrestling ring, and fitness room. He was doing a type of weight training he hadn’t done in quite some time. You know how in some animies (yes, we’re going there), some of the heroes do s special weight training, by having a certain amount attached to their arms and legs. Well Leon is currently doing about a hundred each limb. It isn’t easy for him, but he’s progressing. Anybody who could see him now could clearly see, that this man was trying to exceed his limitations right now. This kind of training doesn’t focus on just building muscles. It builds speed as well. As fast as he could, Leon was punching his punching bag. But after a minute, he collapsed on it. Leon: Dammit. I really shouldn’t have let off this training as long as I have. I feel exhausted. Leon almost collapsed to the floor. Well he did, but it was slowly, so not to alert everyone. He tried to get back up, by pushing himself up from the ground. But his arms collapsed, and he fell asleep. He woke up a few minutes later, when he felt a small hand on his face. He slowly opened his eyes, to see the face of his son Keith. He knew it was Keith, as between the two boys, his hair was lighter, almost blond, like his grandfather’s hair used to be. Leon smiled. Leon: Hey buddy. I’d pick you up but I’m tied down right now. Keith smiled a bit. Leon tried to turn over, and he did. He was surprised to feel so light. He then noticed why. His weights had been removed. Keith then climbs on top of Leon’s chest, and snuggled up against his father, who happily wrapped an arm around his son. He looked up to see Demonica standing there, holding Courtney. Demonica: You know, even for you, this is a lot. Leon: Yeah sorry. But I was getting out of shape a bit, and I needed to get back to my old regimen. Guess I over did it today, eh? Demonica: Looks like it. Don’t get me wrong, it’s a good method, but if you put to much strain on your body, then you won’t even make it to Crowning Glory. Leon: Yeah that would suck, wouldn’t it? Demonica Yeah. Anyways, do you have the strength to get up. Leon: Let’s see. With his free arm, Leon tried to prop himself up. He managed to sit up, before he slowly stood up. He sighed, following his wife back upstairs. But from Leon’s point of view, oh he had a nice view alright, especially with Demonica wearing that skin tight miniskirt she wore. Later, Leon was in his private den. He was still hurting from earlier, and thus, was lying down, with the pull out bed all out. He had his laptop in his hands, while he also had a sketchpad in his hands. He was looking at various Youtube videos of Chris England. He had a bit of a smile on his face, as he saw Chris totally pulverize somebody, and make them bleed, and enjoy it. Leon: That’s the challenge I hope to see. Leon then began to sketch something, as the video ended. It was a very detailed sketch, that had all of his attention. He didn’t even notice Demonica coming in with tomato soup. Demonica: Honey? Leon jumped a slight bit at that. But he smiled when She handed him the soup. She had a look at his sketch. It was a closed roof cage. Demonica: What’s this? Leon: Oh. Well it’s an idea that’s been brewing around for some time. Something I would like to see happen. A new match, if you will. Demonica: Well a closed cage isn’t exactly new. Leon: No, but what I have planned. See, I’m going to add more onto it. Like…barbed wire walls, or even electric walls. The roof will contain various weaponry to use, as will the entire outside. Demonica: Okay. But why are you thinking that? Leon: Backup plan. In case if my match against Chris at Crowning Glory doesn’t do the trick. Demonica: Ah. But I’ve been wondering though. What’s your obsession with him? Leon began to eat some of the soup, blowing off an answer for Demonica. She knew he wasn’t going to answer, and this irritated her. She swore in her native language, which was Spanish. That made Leon smile, before, he lightly grabbed her arm, and began to place soft kisses up it. This made Demonica giggle. Leon: You spoke Spanish, my love. You know that a fourth of the reason I love you, is because of my fetish for Latin women. Demonica: Oh I know that all to well. Demonica leaned over, and kissed Leon for a second. She then pulled away, smiling. Demonica: Eat up, and get your strength back. Please. And next time, when you do that kind of training, don’t fuck yourself up like you did today. Leon: Alight alright. I know. Demonica smiled, before she turned around and walked away. Leon sighed. Leon: What’s happened to me? Have I really become so weak? I mean, a year ago, I was king. Have I really fallen so far that I’m just a shell of my former self? Leon’s hand began to shake. The spoon he had in his hand began to bend under the pressure of his hand. His eyes momentarily flashed red, as he clenched his teeth tightly. He quickly, but angrily, drank down his tomato soup, not caring how much it spilled all over him. He then threw the bowl across the room, effectively shattering it Leon: I’m….I’m not the monster that I claim to be. And yet, here I am, demanding that Chris England becomes something. I really am a hypocrite. I hate it. Well no more. I don’t care if it kills me, I will not be held back anymore. I can’t. I just…can’t allow myself to be this weak. Leon rose up from the bed. He felt reinvigorated. His energy had returned to him, but he still felt the strain of what he had done to himself before. Still, that didn’t stop him. He walked upstairs, to grab a leather jacket, and his keys. He then trned around, and went downstairs. Demonica intercepted him. Demonica: Where are you going? Leon: Out of my way. Now, you would think that Demonica would e used to Leon’s ever shifting behavior. But she was clearly shocked at what he said. She looked at him with anger on her face. Demonica: Excuse me? Leon: You heard me. Get the fuck out of my way. Demonica: You’re supposed to be resting, so you can be in good shape for Crowning glory. Leon smirked. He walked forward to Demonica, gently placing his hand on her shoulder. Leon: It’s not a matter whether you think I’m ready or not…. Leon then grabbed her, and tossed her to the side. She hit her head against the wall. She wasn’t out cold, but she could see the nasty smirk he had, along with the cold not-caring attitude that Leon had now. Leon: It’s a matter on how much I have to do in order to reclaim what was rightfully mine. Being on top. Leon turned away from her, leaving the house. He went into the garage, and got out his motorcycle. Firing it up, he left his property quickly, speeding along down the road. However, it wouldn’t take him long to reach the destination he wanted to. Which was, an abandoned house. He got off his motorcycle, and went inside, after taking his helmet off. He walked around for a few minutes, just looking at the worn down empty place. He smirked. Leon: They say I’m fake. I’m a phony. But….do they know me? Leon sighs, dropping his helmet on the floor. He walked around liking the noise of the broken glass he stepped on. Leon: I‘m not misunderstood. I simply am. My sole existence is to cause pain to others. That’s why I’m still alive, isn’t it? Leon nodded to himself as he turned around, to see the living room. This house had once been a whore house, although it wasn’t exactly considered a good one, as fights between the customers broke out, along with a lot of the women not exactly willing to do their work, but were subjected to do such acts anyways. Leon could still smell the amount of fornication that went on in this room alone, mixed in with the dried up alcohol all over the floor. Leon: Like this house, There is more to me then what people see. Yes, I am capable of emotions. Is that a good thing thought. Emotions have been making me weak lately. Making me soft. Not like how I was before. Leon sat down on the couch. He smirked again, relaxing against the torn fabric. Springs had popped out, and the couch was almost as hard as a rock. But oddly enough, Leon didn’t seem to mind it. Leon: What happened to me? What made me lose my focus? Lack of motivation, or what? I’ve just been…floating around MWE for quite some time. But I don’t like it. I should be out there, not giving any mercy. It’s odd. I stalked Chris England, so he could become a monster, like he once was. And yet here I am, in the same position. I’m not who I once claimed to be. And I hate it. Leon growled to himself, as he looked all over the room. He could still hear what had happened in this house, especially on the night it became abandoned. The owners of the business cared enough to make money. They never once stopped the raping if it ever happened. They were non to pleased when they heard gunshots from their clients, as the girls were murdered by a couple of cheap street thugs, who just wanted to get their rocks off without having to pay for a thing. Leon: But perhaps this will mark a turn around for me. Crowning Glory approaches fast. I know they want to see an event they won’t forget. Well I plan to deliver. I want them to see something that they will remember, whether they like it or not. Originally, I just wanted Chris to be the monster, eventually him thanking me for doing what I did, to reveal his true strength. He was a former ICW champion who showed he didn’t care what it took, as long as he showed he was dominant. Much like I was, except I never bothered with the ICW title. Maybe it was his own personal problems that caused him to want to enjoy life better. But he should consider himself lucky. Everyone alive should. Why should they….for the one fact is, they never had to go through what I did. I know what it’s like to die. Most people, they will never be able to say that. Some people die temporarily when they get transplants. Me. I was legally declared dead. Then, brought back to life, by a miracle. I call shenanigans on that, I know the truth. Lucifer brought me back, on his own free will, for one purpose, and one purpose only. To be his harbinger of chaos. It’s time I started to live up to it. Leon stopped talking, when he noticed a bottle of cheap wine there, half empty. He shrugged, deciding to try it. He got up, and took a gulp of it. But he spat it out, repulsed by the dusty and bitter taste of the cheap wine. He chucked it across the room, effectively shattering it into many pieces. Leon: Chris England. You can put up your bravado of wanting to be a role model, and wanting to fight for what’s right. But in this world, what truly is right? It’s all everyone’s point of view on how they look at it. My point of view, is that there is no true justice, because one person’s gain is another’s loss, no matter how right it seems. So I do what I do to get what I want. Unfortunately, I’ve slipped up. My goals become clouded. My thirst for violence, left unsatisfied, and ignored. But. Not anymore. Not now. Not ever again. Leon took off his jacket, before he picked up a large shard of glass. He jabbed it into his left palm, relishing the pain and the blood flowing from his hand. He knew he would have to get it fixed up, but that’s not what he cared about right now. Leon: England. There is a change of plans. You’re still essential to them. But, you’re not the monster I was looking for. I am that monster. You’re still essential due to you being whom I originally chose to face me. Why waste this opportunity just because my plans have been tweaked around. No. Maybe you’ll come back as a monster in the end, but I can guarantee, on the blood that flows through my veins, and onto this house of fornication, that you will be nothing more then a sacrifice. But it’s not a holy virgin type of sacrifice, it’s a blood and message sacrifice. Somebody has to be it, and you, are it. Walking away from Crowning Glory for you, is not an option, for you won’t be walking. And when you come to, to your senses, in the operating room, fixing up your extensive injuries, you will feel the hatred against me, for taking you away from something you love. I’m going to admit, I want you to hate me. I want them all to hate me. For my biggest mistake thus far, as the true devil on earth, is not taking things more seriously. I know, that in the heat of my takeover, people are going to look up to their heroes, and they will beg to be saved. But their heroes will either join my cause, or perish beneath my feet, with the cries of the innocent going ignored, or better yet….punished for their naivety. Much like this house. Only when it was to late, when everyone killed each other in this sacrilegious house, did the people outside of it recognize just how evil of a place this was. Just like how it’s to late for people to understand, that I’m not the merciful person they’ve seen lately. And Chris England, well…..instead of a monster, you’re going to become a number, a statistic…..just another fucken victim of my own chaotic wants and needs. Don’t go praying to god, because he won’t help you. Because if there is one person who could give less of a fuck then George W Bush, it’s the supposed creator of life that you spineless cunts love to worship. Leon winces in pain, as only now does he register the pain he is in from his self mutilation. But he smiles at it, as he sees his blood shine off the light of the moon through the window. With a newfound resolve, Leon looked even more forward to meeting with Chris England at Crowning Glory now. And it will be a lot of fun, for the Harbinger of Chaos.
