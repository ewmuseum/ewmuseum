A Dream Begins..

Jul 27 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A black scene is seen scaling across the screen as a sudden MWE logo flashes across the screen in a diagonal axis. The dark pitched black begins to fade into a bright white where the camera finally jolts into the actual visual perspective. A white blur comes over the screen as it starts to fade away into the actual seen-environment of a typical American suburban street with the same type of small box houses going up the entire block. The weather seems to be a dusk, cloudy day with recent rain known through the lenses of the camera with puddles in the gutters of the roads. The camera scales around continuously looking at the surrounding environment as cars proceed up and down the street to their destination. Not many people are seen on the streets more so due to the fact the weather is predicted to rain in near time. As the camera continues to scale the environment, suddenly, out of no where, MWE's newest roster addition, Jai Williams ducks under and in to the camera's visual perspective. He is wearing a baggy red jacket with the zipper un-done with a white sleeveless shirt on underneath, long baggy denim jeans down a bit revealing his Calvin Klein underwear label with the left leg held up at his knee with the use of a red bandana, Soulja Boy-like sun glasses, a green gangster hat tilted on a right angle and three long pieces of bling resting around his neck and a small chain on his right wrist. Jai Williams: So here it is.. My dream is here awaiting me prolifically, my dream to be apart of the number one wrestling federation in the world today has now become reality.. Through the ruins of mass cobwebs and life restraints I've made it. I've learnt a lot over my lifetime, and morals I now use everyday and will to the day I rise to the heavens above.. I've seen and witnessed things that have made life tougher than it should be, and it makes me happier now that I've made it, and that I've pushed problems aside and achieved my life goal. But I can't bask in the glee of making it to this level, because as tough and as gruelling the ride to get to this stage was, the real fight of strength for history begins here. Jai begins to walk upon the sidewalk of the suburban street as cars continue to pass him. Jai looks up and can see the clouds brew over him seemingly looking like it could bucket down in rain any second now. The camera comes to the side angle of Jai as Jai turns to the camera and looks at it in a happy yet serious mood. Jai Williams: For two years now, I've been tuning in each Monday to watch the chaotic and entertaining, and even sometimes unpredictable fallouts of Monday Night Mayhem.. With having no physical experience I should be unaware of what to expect come Monday when I make my MWE In-ring debut, but I feel as though I've already got the essentials to at least do some good.. I've watched enough matches, I've seen enough segments.. Heard enough stories from my half-brother Sonny Williams to know that life in MWE is tough, sometimes rewarding.. Sometimes disappointing.. But always, and I repeat, always a journey of hardship and perseverance.. Or at least that's what I expect from my career in this industry. Jai continues walking down the sidewalk of the suburban street as scattered clumps of rubbish brush on past him as he walks from the gentle, yet effective powers of the wind. Jai continues to walk until he finds a small bus-stop where he sits on the bench looking into the distance at the cars surpassing him. He has an expression on his face that showcases seriousness, yet not too much, mixed with nervous excitement bottling up inside. The camera scales in and continues to watch the progressions of Jai. Jai looks up at the camera ready to talk. Jai Williams: Most of the time, actually 95% of the time, I'm a down to earth guy.. I like to chill, have a good time and make the most of nights mixed with alcohol and smoking hot women. So in all honesty, a large percentage of my friends and family would see this footage of me now surprising.. But there's no need for them, or anyone else to be so. Just like Martin Luther King had a dream, I have one also.. And the seriousness rubbing off now is just a vague indication of my proposed dedication to the dream I'm now lucky enough to be living.. Jai looks down at the ground for a split second or two before raising his head and widening his mouth revealing his grillz to the camera. This indicates the more exciting and out-bursting side of what Jai Williams was well known for on the indy circuit before making his way into MWE. Jai Williams: But enough about fairytales, dreams and lifetime goals.. The seriousness is not about me making it into MWE, no, if that was the case I wouldn't have thrown a party.. I wouldn't have got completely hammered.. And I wouldn't have hammered about 2-3rds of the female party population.. Yet enough about parties and sexual activity also.. The whole y and x axis of me being here was to prepare, to build un-capitalized momentum heading into a bout where I will make my MWE debut. Jai suddenly uses both his hands and uses the force to push himself up slowly from the bus stop bench as he progresses to walk down the sidewalk of the suburban street. Jai brushes his baggy red jacket of the crinkles and possible dirt on it from sitting down in the bus stop bench, but also just as a look of confidence. He continues to walk down the sidewalk as various cars continue to surpass him on their way to a destination. Jai Williams: With wanted preparation and momentum, it brings me to my opponent, a man who is alike in set circumstances, yet so completely weird and different to the style and way of life I live by. The only form similarities are that the pair of us are making our MWE debut.. Everything outside of that small minute similarity is completely opposite.. I wear flash, straight up ballin' clothing labels.. He opts to wear potato sacks.. I wear hats.. While he wears masks.. Yet as strange and bizarre as this 'man' is, nothing about him makes the hair on my back rise, nothing about Oblivion makes me.. Scared. And you wanna know why I'm not threatened or mentally frightened to take the ring and wrestle this guy? It's because I've been watching wrestling since I was three years old, I've lived through wrestling and watched it expand over many eras, and in each and every era wrestling has been through there's been one guy who would come across creepy, who'd have a bizarre background and would formerly be known from the false-claim-of-attempted-fright, otherwise known as 'Parts Unknown'. Usually, the whole roster is scared to be placed in a ring with these stereotypical freaks, but not me, no. Jai comes to the end of this side of the suburban sidewalk as he comes to a street light and firmly presses the button awaiting the lights to turn red giving him the chance to cross the street to the other side. He stops and awaits a red light as the camera continues to follow Jai as he turns to it ready to speak more of his match. Jai has an expression on his face showcasing confidence and determination. Jai Williams: For the 22 years I've watched professional wrestling this sort of monster whom thrives on the pain of his opponents, have risen.. And then have fallen. It only takes one loss for any freak, monster or strange being to lose instant credibility as it showcases their weakness, it showcases that past those big, bold eyes.. The intimidating entrance music.. The creepy life background.. And the suspicious mask, that they're nothing different to any other wrestler you see lace up a pair of boots. The traffic light suddenly turns red as a buzzing noise is heard to alert Jai that it is now accessible to cross the road to the other side of the suburban street. Jai swaggers on to the other side of the street as the camera follows his every action. The green light lights up once again as cars begin to come from both directions once again. Jai turns as the camera is behind him, slowing down and letting it get to his pace as he walks up the sidewalk of this side of the street. Jai Williams: But the thing surrounding Oblivion and his stereotypical self, is that those freaks of which he is apart of, rise.. They rise to levels that have commentators asking themselves, 'Have we found the next big thing?'. People may think to themselves that due to that fact, things could not sway my way.. But history was made to be altered.. It was made for moments like the induction of Barrack Obama as the first black US President.. It was made for a guy like me. And I know Oblivion wants to make his own history, he knows who I am.. He knows my background.. He knows that I'm the brother of MWE Hall of Famer Sonny Williams, and he wants to be the first to use the crown of defeating a Hall of Famer's brother.. Yet I won't allow it. Like me, Oblivion wants to start his MWE journey in class, he wants to be undefeated.. He wants this, and he wants that.. Yet I won't allow it. As Jai continues to walk he turns a sudden corner on the sidewalk which takes him to a noticeably different side of the suburb. Big infrastructures with paled bricks and smoke coming out of pipes rising into the earths atmosphere. Jai makes a slight cough in instant notice of the change of environments from a child-loving neighbourhood, to a set of factories. He continues to walk and turns another corner as cars also continue to pass by him. Once turned the next corner, about four or five factories are seen lined up.. Most looking out of shape with cracked windows, and dodgy front gates. The new environment gives Jai a more sense of overwhelming solemn. Again, the camera follows Jai on a side-angle. Jai Williams: You see the reason why I'm so hell bent on disallowing Oblivion to win, is because unlike myself, Oblivion sought entry into professional wrestling to lessen his violence, he sought escape from his former job as an Assassin into wrestling just so he could harm people.. He's not like me. He doesn't have a right, little alone a chance to be in the same ring as myself.. My entire lifestyle has been eating, drinking, sleeping.. BREATHING, wrestling, while this freak chucked on a mask and did a few grunts to earn a contract. Jai takes a deep breathe as you can feel he is beginning to become enraged and completely vocal and out-going with his feelings and thoughts about his opponent, and about his debut match. Jai re-adjusts his green gangsta hat as he continues to walk on the sidewalk whilst the camera continues to capture the show casings of Jai's emotions. Jai Williams: But I will remain calm.. I won't enrage myself to lengths that has me looking through the same eyes of Oblivion, seeking harm, and not seeking victory, because all of the crap.. All of the stereotypical crap that surrounds Oblivion will fall before it ever gets the chance to rise. Every little thing he has said about me.. The ways he has tried to attack me through my brother has failed.. He's done things that have taken him no where but uncharted territory. Jai comes to a halt as he suddenly puts his hand in a pocket in his baggy jeans where he pulls out a crunched up and cluttered piece of paper. Jai begins to unfold it and pat it down into it's right readable self. He turns to the camera whom is on his side. Jai Williams: Look at this piece of paper.. More importantly, Oblivion.. Look at this piece of paper. This has been the words, the lyrics that have stuck in my head like glue and has given me inspiration, stimulation and most importantly, motivation leading into this match, but even more so.. My career. Because I know if I keep my attitude to something like this, I'm gonna find success, whether it not be today, or the next day, or the following day.. It will come. "If ya don't believe, you don't succeed And if you don't apply, you wont achieve You keep ya head up high Feet down low, Follow your heart Til you get to the door Bells and wheels and wheels away , Never trust a soul I'm here to say Stop dependin' on and be yourself And grab a hold, and don't look left So take a swing and here's your chance, You got nothing to lose so make a stance The clock is tickin' and time ain't waitin' All alone see that's what your facin" Jai brings the piece of paper down to his level as he looks down at it, recognising the words and their meaning. Suddenly small droplets of rain begin to come down hitting the piece of paper and smudging the ink. Jai looks up as it continues to sprinkle down on him. He gets a slow jog on as he finds a small shelter outside one of the factory gates. He brushes his jacket and clothing, getting rid of the wet rain and making his clothing dry and clean. Jai looks down at the piece of paper again as the ink smudges begin to come out in droplets and dribble across the paper as Jai drops it to the ground in ease. The camera is in front of him as Jai slowly looks towards it. Jai Williams: Oblivion.. As much as I have doubted you now, I won't be taking any chances in doing so come Monday. Yet as much as I have poised my soon-to-be efforts, I won't turn back on them.. I'm not going to take this match easily, and what I want from this match is for Oblivion to turn up not as some phony scare tactic hopeful, but as a threat to me and a challenge to the beginning of a new era.. Despite any of this happening, no matter how galliant the fight, no matter how determined Oblivion comes to be.. He will fall, and be my startling message. Jai remains to be looking straight-on into the camera as he doesn't make a slight reaction or blink which indicates his pure determination for his debut match in the MWE. The rain begins to increase, louder and louder, tougher and tougher. Jai Williams: So Oblivion get ready.. Go look yourself in the mirror.. Go put your mask on.. Go and do whatever the hell you want, but when it comes to Monday I want you coming at me at 110 miles with no sign of giving in. Give me something to believe in, give me something to question my appearance in MWE.. Tell me you'll beat me, but more importantly bring the real monster out to play, and beat me. I beg you, do it! .. But until then, chuck your journal away and get ready, cause a whole nother page in the history of MWE is about to begin! The scene begins to fade as we see Jai give a deep stare into the camera before slowly turning and looking out at the loud, thumping rain. A deep and dark black begins to overcome the screen as the promo comes to a halt.
