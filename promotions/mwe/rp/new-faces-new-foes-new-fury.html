New Faces, New Foes, New Fury; Alejandro Rodriguez RP

Jan 10 2009

Alejandro Rodriguez

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Manchester, New Hampshire Thursday 8th January, 2:32pm Verizon Wireless Arena. The scene opens inside the Verizon Wireless Arena, the camera pans around showing a living-room type area, with a number of couches and some big LCD televisions on the walls, there was a Pepsi machine in the corner and there were big windows and a few doors, letting the Manchester afternoon light shine in bright, illuminating the entire room. In the room is Darrel Jones, wearing a black suit and tie, he has a laptop on his lap and he is sitting on a sofa. The camera rotates so there is an over-the-shoulder view, Darrel can be seen typing an e-mail. The camera then pans in on the person sitting next to him, Billy Joe Barker. He is wearing his favourite black cowboy hat, a brown striped shirt and jeans, he seems on edge, he keeps looking out of the window and he twitches his thumbs continuously. Meanwhile, the camera focuses on the man opposite him, he is holding a newspaper which covers most of his torso and face. The camera sweeps around it and it shows Alejandro Rodriguez, with his trademark black mask, wearing a black “A-Rod” t-shirt, white sneakers and jeans with a few slashes in them. He decides that he is done with the newspaper, he puts it down and looks at Billy Joe, who is looking outside the window again, Alejandro follows his stare, but doesn’t see anything, he turns his head back around to Billy Joe. Alejandro: Why did we come here? Darrel looks up, but realises Alejandro is talking to Billy Joe, and he puts his head back down as Billy Joe looks toward Alejandro. Billy Joe shrugs his shoulders, Alejandro laughs and looks toward Darrel. Alejandro: What’re you doing? Darrel looks up to see Alejandro is talking to him, he looks back down and continues typing for a moment before he talks. Darrel: If you must know, I’m sending an e-mail… Darrel anticipates Alejandro’s next question before he opens his mouth. Darrel: …Richard Covell. And no, I’m not going to ask him why David Whitman is in your match with D.C. for the Undisputed Championship, this e-mail is strictly business. Alejandro snorts loudly. Alejandro: About ICW I suppose, taken over your life, that has. Darrel sighs loudly and looks up at Alejandro. Darrel: Mr. Covell has entrusted me with a very import- Alejandro waves him off. Alejandro: Spare me the lecture. Darrel seemed indignant about Alejandro’s reaction. Darrel: ICW is responsible for the up-coming generation in MWE. You shouldn’t be taking that tone, you’ll probably have to take a new protégé soon from there. Alejandro points at Billy Joe, who was looking out the window again. Alejandro: Excuse me Master Yoda, I’ve trained my padawan. Billy Joe: Obi-Wan hayd more than wuun Padawan. Darrel looks up in confusion. Darrel: He only had Anakin… Billy Joe: ‘e hayd Luke… Darrel: There was no Jedi Order at that time, there were no Padawans. Alejandro: Also… how can you say Obi-Wan Kenobi’s name correct and not mine? Billy Joe shrugs and looks back out of the window. Alejandro: Why do you keep looking out the window? Billy Joe thinks about an answer. Billy Joe: ’Am Bored… It’s a pretty bad lie and Darrel and Alejandro each smirk. Alejandro: If you’re so bored, why don’t you go and get me a Pepsi? Billy Joe looks around in shock and slight horror as if he must not leave the window. Alejandro: What are we waiting for? Billy Joe realises that there was no point lying. Billy Joe: ‘Am waitin’ faw sumone. Alejandro laughs for a moment and adjusts his mask. Alejandro: Seriously though… why are you looking outside? Billy Joe: ‘Am waitin’ faw sumwan. Alejandro realises he’s not clever enough to lie. Alejandro: Really? Who? Billy Joe looks down and twiddles his thumbs, he says it so quiet that the camera barely picks it up. Billy Joe: Childhood friend. Alejandro sits up more straight, apparently more interested in the conversation, he chuckles a bit. Alejandro: Should be interesting, eh Darrel? Darrel closes his laptop, stands up and puts it in his bag. Darrel: Sorry guys, I’d love to meet your friend, but Covell is counting on me I need some privacy. Alejandro waves him off again. Alejandro: You sound like Brian Michaels. Darrel: Goodbye. Darrel exit’s to the room and now both Alejandro and Billy Joe look out of the window, waiting for Billy’s friend. Alejandro: How long is your friend gonna be? Billy Joe looks toward Alejandro. Billy Joe: It’s not layk yu got better things ta do. Alejandro scowls and sits back down, he looks across to see Darrel left his laptop bag. Alejandro beckons Billy Joe over to it, they take it out and set it up and look through Darrel’s e-mails, they reach his last e-mail and read it. Alejandro: That little liar! Billy looks at Alejandro. Billy Joe: Bud it izz abou’ AY SEE DUBYA. Alejandro: No you idiot, look. Covell is talking about his third son coming into ICW, Randy Covell. Billy Joe and Alejandro both look quizzical. Billy Joe: Breed lyk rabbit’s, those Covell’s duu. Alejandro: That’s not the point, why didn’t Darrel tell me? Billy Joe: Ya didn’ need ta know. Alejandro: Whatever, I think your friend Is arriving. Billy Joe splutters in excitement, and runs out the door to see a black car parking in the private parking lot. Billy Joe runs toward the car and Alejandro walks along behind him, with one hand in his pocket, and Alejandro biting his fingernails on the other hand, showing no real interest whatsoever. Billy Joe is already at the car and Alejandro arrives some seconds later. Billy Joe opens the car of the sleek black saloon, and out of the car comes a tall and wide figure. With mid-length dirty-blonde hair, a muscled figure and a thick brown beard, wearing a distinctive brown cowboy hat, denim jacket and jeans, white shirt and huge cowboy boots. He smiles and him and Billy Joe share a bone-crushing hug. Billy Joe breaks out and turns to Alejandro, who is checking his reflection in the tinted windows. Billy Joe: Aleebansacko, I’d lyk ya ta meet, Bob Benson. Baab, this is Alehankano Podfreechez. Alejandro: It’s Alejandro Rodriguez. Alejandro holds out his hand and Bob takes it and shakes it. Bob: Ah, Meester Rodcheesemess, nice ta meet cha, A’ve hurd all abou’ chu, o‘ course. Alejandro corrects him. Alejandro: …Rodriguez. Bob: Yes Olliejambo, that’s what a’ said. Alejandro sighs and turns to Billy Joe. Alejandro: Well I’d love to stay and chat.. But I… have… an interview. Billy Joe looks at Alejandro with a puzzled look. But Alejandro runs off before he can say anything. Alejandro runs back into the lounge and returns to a walking pace, he picks up Darrel’s laptop and walks through the door and out of the room into a hallway, he walks past workers and the occasional jobber before reaching a door, he doesn‘t bother knocking, he just walks in. It is a vacated office, with a desk and two chairs, Darrel is sat in one of them, with a phone in his hand, he is talking to someone, he looks up to Alejandro. Darrel: Hold on a second, I’ll call you back. Darrel hangs up the phone. Alejandro: Who was that? Darrel: Never you mind… Alejandro holds up Darrel’s laptop. Alejandro: I have your laptop. Darrel: Thanks. Alejandro places the laptop on the desk and sits down opposite Darrel. Alejandro: Some interesting e-mails on there. Darrel scowls. Darrel: I thought you were with Billy Joe’s friend. Alejandro: Turns out, he’s more annoying than Billy… Darrel: Well, do you have something you want? I’m kinda busy here. Alejandro: An interview, or something? Darrel: Sorry Al, you’re going to have to do it yourself I’m afraid. It’s Alejandro’s turn to scowl. Alejandro: Fine, have fun being Covell’s pet. Darrel laughs and Alejandro gets up and walks out the room, he steps out in the corridor and walks round a corner, at the end he comes out of a tunnel, in sight is the huge interior of the arena, where the ring is being set up by workers. A camera approaches Alejandro as he sits down into a front row seat, watching the ring being set up. Alejandro looks at the camera. Alejandro: Following me, are you? That’s hardly a surprise… Alejandro pauses for a moment looking around at the arena. Alejandro: Look at this arena. It’s a sight to behold isn’t it? This is a stadium that has seen likes of Elton John, Led Zeppelin, Justin Timberlake, Nickelback, Coldplay and many others… Here, is where Barrack Obama and Oprah Winfrey drew 8,000 people to the biggest political event In New Hampshire, and this Monday it shall see the biggest event in wrestling history when Alejandro Rodriguez wins the MWE Undisputed Championship. The camera does a close-up of Alejandro’s face, the lights from the arena reflect off Alejandro’s mask making it glow a little in the camera’s lens. Alejandro: Now no-one can be sure what will happen this Monday. But I can be sure than I can deal with David Whitman and D.C., I have done with both of them before. I can be sure that nobody has stuck with MWE longer than I have, I can be sure no-body has messed with MWE and got away with it, I can be sure that no-one else has held every men’s title in the MWE. The camera follows Alejandro’s gaze around the huge arena and then it returns back to him. Alejandro: I can be sure, because I have been with the MWE for every single moment it has been there. From the very beginning, Crowning Glory, then the original MWE Days, my feuds with Sonny Williams, some of the best matches in the original MWE, then the WAR invasion, Hostile Grounds, and then my World Title victory at Highway to Hell. The demise of MWE. And then the EW era… And then start up of the new MWE. I was one of the main people involved with bringing MWE back, I was the one who pleaded with Covell to give it another chance! Alejandro pauses and glares at the camera with furious intensity. Alejandro: So when you ask a guy like me, what this title means to me. You have no idea… You have no idea what the MWE means to me, this IS MY LIFE! I have given everything to this company, and for them to put David Whitman in this match, when I slaughtered him last week, is an absolute disgrace! I made him bleed after what he did to my Stacy, I beat him down and humiliated him in front of millions of people, and the MWE have the nerve to put him in this match! After all I’ve given to them! After shortening my life in order to put on a show for fans worldwide, they shorten my odds at World Title gold by putting Whitman in this match!?! Alejandro takes a moment to pause and he regains his composure, he adjusts his mask a little bit and then continues talking. Alejandro: David Whitman… I hope that last week you learnt a very valuable lesson. My Stacy is still in hospital, thanks for asking. What you did to her, will affect her for the rest of her life, she’ll never be the same, she will change dramatically. And it was all because of you. But you, are not the man that interests me in this match, I already proved that I was better than you, that I was the better man last week. And you know it’s true… Alejandro’s expression does not change, but he puts his feet up on the chair in front, taking a more casual approach. Alejandro: You see, what your problem is Whitman, you bring too much emotion into your matches. You were so sorry about what happened to Stacy, you didn’t dare stand up to me and fight me like a man… You were weak… pathetic, an easy opponent. You brought your remorse into this match and you made yourself a sitting duck. You last that match for yourself, you may as well have come in and stuck a razor to your head, it would have saved you the beating that I gave you. Behind the mask a grin appears under his face. Alejandro: Your time has come and gone, you’ve held that title, that time has gone, it’s time for someone else, the only reason you’re in this match, is for a novelty, the current champion, versus the first champion, versus the next champion, the greatest champion there was in MWE, me. You never made a lasting impression Whitman, you hold the title for a couple of weeks, then loose to Brian Michaels, that’s not how a champion does things. I’ve beaten Brian Michaels, D.C. beat Brian Michaels last week, have you beaten Michaels, Whitman? Alejandro shakes his head into a “no” and laughs again. Alejandro: I’m not going to waste all my time talking about you Whitman, there’s another man in this battle, I think you’ll have noticed. And his name is D.C. probably one of the fiercest competitors the MWE has seen to date, probably one of the only men in the world who could make Brian Michaels say “I Quit”, the first ever MWE Undisputed Champion, probably the best X-TV Champion to date and the man who carried MWE through the era, after I stepped off the throne. Alejandro swivels his legs off the chair in front of him, giving the camera his full attention. Alejandro: I’m not going to lie, those are some fine attributes D.C., but I don’t care about you, I care about that title you have wrapped around your waist, you may have accomplished a lot of things D.C., but that means absolutely nothing to me. I know you won’t want to loose that title after one week, you won’t have a damn choice, I am going to take it from you whether you or David Whitman like it or not. I don’t give a damn about your legacy. I care about the prestige of the title and knowing that the company’s future is in safe hands. Alejandro smiles and makes a title gesture around his waist. Alejandro: I’m not going to sit here and watch your run as Undisputed Champion, D.C., I’m not going to sit here while you let your ego grow, mouthing off everyone from Mickey Mouse to me and beating jobbers every other week. I’m going to give you a real challenge. And I want you to run onto me head-on, because you can’t beat experience, and that’s something you don’t have when you have under-dog situations. So I’m not gonna wait while you get more and more power. Alejandro’s expression changes when he says power, he pauses a moment, looking as if trying to digest something in his mind. Alejandro: Power, is something that corrupts everything, people and other things. For example, just a few months ago, Darrel was an idiot living off Aiden Copane‘s success, I got his ass out of un-employment and now look at him, Head of ICW, the future of MWE is on his shoulders… and now, he won’t give me the time of day, the power that MWE uses to undermine me, when MWE wasn‘t running, they were great to me, after I helped them get off their asses, not look, they put people who I just beat last week into my match, the MWE is trying to keep me down. Anger is etched on Alejandro’s face as he continues his rant, the cameraman takes a step back as he is a little intimidated. Alejandro: The MWE has been screwing me from day one. I knew Tim Fitzgerald years and years before ANYONE at MWE knew him, he was like a brother to me , and I was given 30 seconds to pay my respects! I had to sit there and watch Brian Michaels and D.C. induct him! The fact that there were any other inductions last night was bullshit! No-one was in in the same league as Tim, NO-ONE! Especially not Whitman or Katherine Tenaud… I was told I was inducting Whitman weeks before the ceremony, the day before I was told I had to share that privilege with Sonny Williams, a man whom I hate! The anger in Alejandro’s face contrasts against the light shining off his mask. Alejandro: And then I watched as Katherine Tenaud was inducted, bull… shit! This was a woman, who came, won a title, stayed for a month, dropped her knickers for D.C. and pissed off, IT SHOULD HAVE BEEN ME GETTING INDUCTED! A man, who has stayed with MWE since the very beginning and beaten all there is to beat. A man who inspires every little Mexican boy who has a dream, a dream I once had, to be on the grandest stage of them all! A man who is the very reason MWE came back to life. Alejandro now stands up, and the cameraman nearly stumbles back at the look of fury on his face, a fury also told throughout his tensed body. Alejandro: And if you’re out there, watching this, and you’re questioning my heart, or my desire, or my integrity. Look into my eyes, the things I have accomplished, the things I have survived, are absolutely mammoth compared to the binge-drinking Whitman or the motor-mouth D.C., they’re walking into an absolute brick wall this Monday. D.C., David Whitman… in time, you will LEARN.. TO.. FLY! The camera fades to black as Alejandro breathes heavily in the background.
