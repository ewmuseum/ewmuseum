The Bitter Side of Brian Michaels

Oct 18 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
QUOTE Michaels’ yell turns into a bit of a sinister laugh as he begins shaking violently on the ground. Tera walks into the scene with a worried look on her face as the police officer goes to talk to her. Michaels: This time…Mason. There is no running…there is no D.C…there is nothing on God’s GREEN EARTH…that could prepare you for this match. Throw the lessons out. Throw the teaching out. This is the real FUCKING DEAL. Michaels…Resoan. One on one…this Monday, you’ll get yours Mason. You’ll…get…YOURS. The camera pans over to Tera, who kicks both officers in the balls as they bend over, grunting. She walks over to Michaels, helping him take off his handcuffs. Michaels: Know why, Mason?! I’ll face you ANYWHERE. I’ll face you in the ring, in jail, motherfucker, I will face you in the goddamn church! You’re going to feel the pain that I felt last year…and you…Mason Resoan…are going…to LOVE IT. Tera takes the handcuffs off of Michaels as Michaels punches the cameraman in a fit of rage, causing the camera to go to static. However, after the static goes off, the camera turns back on, revealing what happened following the promo. Four more police officers barge into the bathroom, taking Michaels down and Barbie with him. They lock them in handcuffs as the scene again goes to static. The flashback ends and the new scene opens up in a musty cagey jail cell. A man is seen holding handcuffs inside the cell, locked up wearing an orange jumpsuit with the entire room lighting up except for his exact spot. A policeman opens the cell door with his large key, squinting his eyes at the jailed man. Policeman: You got one phone call. The man leans forward in his seat, his deep green eyes looking at him with a pool of resentment and shakes his head. The policeman shakes his head in disapproval. Policeman: Suit yourself. The man is seen rocking back and forth on his rocky bed. The bed is without covers and is revealed to be very bare and empty. A light bulb is turned on as the man puts his hands in his eyes, trying to block out the light that is dangerously bothering his eyes. He takes his hands out of the way and reveals himself to be Brian Michaels. He has a five-o-clock shadow as he rubs his beard, trying to scratch his problems away, but he is handcuffed still. He sits on the bed and puts his hands on his head as the camera fades in. Michaels: One year ago…at this very event. Wrestle-Wars. One year ago today, I thought I was the best wrestler in the World. I thought I was the top guy of the top promotion. I had D.C. and Amber at my side and everything was going…great. I went into Wrestle-Wars as the MWE Champion, heading into my match with the number one contender ‘’Mr.Mayhem’’ Pala. Michaels bows his head, reminiscing of his Championship-carrying days. Michaels: The days…when everything came so easy. When I knew what I wanted to do…night in, night out. When I had such an ego, I really thought I was the hot shot around MWE. Michaels sighs, thinking about what came next for him and his career. Michaels: And then I lost my Title. But I didn’t lose…just a Title. I lost…my confidence. I lost…my smile. I lost the passion…the will to win. But most importantly…I lost my leg. Michaels shakes his head, as the flashback plays constantly in his mind. QUOTE It is Wrestle-Wars 1. Brian Michaels has just lost his match and is in the parking lot as Sara Patterson chases after the now-former MWE Champion to get his thoughts on his past loss. Sara Patterson: Brian! Brian! Can I get your thoughts on the match? Michaels: Um, look. Pala gave me a helluva match and I credit him for the victory. But you bet your ass I’m getting my rematch. Be on the lookout Pala. Sara Patterson: Any other comments? Michaels shakes his head in disapproval as he steps into the long white limousine waiting for him. He gives the camera a bitter look and slams the door behind him. The car accelerates and all of a sudden, another vroom is heard from the side, as it crashes the side of the limo. There is a large BOOM heard as a large fire erupts. The next thing seen is Michaels in a unstable state of being. He rises in a sweat, breathing impatiently. He scratches his hair as he begins to calm down, now speaking in a whisper. Michaels: Do you…remember, that night? Do you remember…the pain you caused me, D.C.? Of course you don’t. D.C. doesn’t feel pain. He doesn’t have…emotions. Michaels looks down on the floor, now thinking about what happened after the accident. Michaels: D.C., I only want one question answered from you. Do you know…what it’s like, to not know if you’re going to walk again? To not know…if you’re going to be able to do…what you love to do ever again? Do you know what it’s like when a doctor tells you he doesn’t think you can do it again? Do YOU…know…what it’s like to come from the bottom of all bottoms…to rise to the occasion, to look death in its eyes…and give it the ol- fuck you? Michaels continues to look directly at the floor, too exhausted to look at the camera. He takes a deep breath before a long silent period is heard. Michaels: What’s that…? You don’t know, do you D.C.? I didn’t think you did. I don’t think you know what it’s like to have that blade go on your leg…to have tubes up your nose…sitting there, knowing you’ll never be the same again. See, D.C., you made the choice to take me away in the prime of my career. Brian puts his hands on his nose, remembering the pain he went through. Michaels: I was so lonely at that hospital. That hospital bed was heartless,… cold…brutal. It took my mind, chewed me up and spit me out. Kind of like…how you chewed me up and spit me out. How you claimed to be my best friend…to be at my wedding…to take care of my kids when I needed it… Michaels’ gives an unsympathetic look and finally looks up at the camera, again showing a face full of resentment and hatred towards D.C. Michaels: D.C., you were an X-Television Champion. You were one of the biggest stars MWE had seen. When you held the X-TV Title, its prestige INSTANTLY went up. You were one of the bright young stars…but just like Mason, you fell and crumbled under the pressure. You were corrupt...just like everybody else. You turned on the guy who helped you get to where you were at for Titles. For MONEY. For all the SHIT…that you told me you didn’t want to stand for. He nods in disapproval again, blowing the air and messing with his hair in a playful yet still serious way. Michaels: See D…I’ve got a little friend. Maybe…you’ve heard of her. Because Barbie can be a very convincing…convincing…part of our match. You can call her a momentum changer. Michaels finally rises up from his cold rusty bed and looks at the camera directly with what appears to be his first legitimate smile all day. Michaels: Barbie…likes…ruthless aggression. She likes…blood. She likes…pain. Do you catch my drift yet, D.C.? Do ya? Michaels shakes his head, showing off the dusty old floor that looks as if it hasn’t been cleaned in years. Michaels: Before I answer who…or what…Barbie is…I want to give a little rant. Michaels inhales deeply and exhales before looking at the camera with a serious look and speaking with a serious tone. Michaels: I’m so sick of hobos, always begging for change. Brian sarcastically puts out two hands in exchange for what appears to be money. Michaels: I hate people who barely have to work, who just sit around and get paid. Brian’s face has a bright-red look to it, now speaking with a pissed off attitude. Michaels: I hate all of the people who can’t drive their cars… Michaels’ serious face turns into a playful one. Michaels: Get it? He then goes back to a serious mood, visible in his facial features. Michaels: Motherfucker, you better get out of the way before I fall apart. Michaels: I hate my ex-wives…they’re always on my ass. They always beg for brand-new things, but in this economy…I ain’t got cash! He reaches back into his jumpsuit, digging deep into the back pocket and takes out a wallet. He opens it to reveal zero cash, only a credit card and a Blockbuster membership card. Michaels: Nothing turns out right…there’s no…end in sight. My dad/boss is a dick, I don’t get paid nearly enough to put up with his shit… I fucking HATE…my life. Michaels’ serious look of depression turns into a look of sarcasm. Michaels: But not Sunday…not…Sunday. Sunday…I am going to enjoy putting D.C….out of his misery. See, because all the shit that I’ve been through my life…it’s never been worth it D.C., is different. D.C...Sunday, you’re not going to get the old Brian Michaels, or this Brian Michaels. I’m better off without D.C…I don’t care about your shit and gimmick anymore… Michaels begins to shake his head violently, grabbing the camera and shaking it with him before putting on a menacing tone. Michaels: NOT…Sunday. D.C., you wanna fuck around with my mind? You wanna play mind games? I’ll play your bullshit. I don’t pay attention to your swagger…to your calls of ‘’Fuck you!’’ If you want to get hardcore…then bitch, you’d better get the FUCK out of the way, because I’m not like anybody else .I’m not just gonna stand here and take it like everybody else because…it’s D.C. D.C. is D.C…and you can’t touch him? Michaels goes over to the wall and begins to rub it smoothly, before beating it like a drum in motion. Michaels: Oh, but you see D.C….it’s fine. I don’t HAVE to even touch you. That’s what Barbie’s for… Michaels bends down and begins to reach under his bed, taking out a rather large and shapely box. Michaels: Speaking of Barbie…here she is. Here is the Barbie…that everybody wants to see… Michaels reaches opens the box to reveal a large piece of barbed wire. Michaels salvates over the barbed wire, sticking out his tongue near the wire, dreaming of its possibilities. Michaels: …But nobody wants to touch… Michaels leans over, looking to tap the wire, before he stops mid-way there to look back at the camera. Michaels: Because if you touch it…D.C…you…die. Michaels’ serious expression turns playful again for a few seconds. Michaels: Not…DIE, DIE…you know…your career…is over. You won’t fuck…with me. Again…never, I never want to hear your shit again, bitch. He gives the camera a smug look, showing people the bitter and cold side of Brian Michaels. Michaels: I remember the day…when it hurt to breathe. When it hurt to live. When they snapped me up on that hospital bed…when they told me it was all over for Brian Michaels. But I lived. I found the passion that had escaped me twenty years ago, and I used it to get back up on my two feet. I will not fall, I will RISE…to the occasion once again, and I’ll be fucked over before I let D.C. pin me one…two…three. Michaels holds up his fingers in a one-two-three motion, causing it to stand for a pin. Michaels: And you know D.C…that’s ALSO…what Barbie’s for. It’s to ensure…that we don’t have a ‘’bad match’’. No D.C…we’re not gonna have a sloppy match… Michaels gives a ‘’No-no’’ gesture with his finger. Michaels: You know why? Because we are the two most combustible elements in MWE HISTORY. We’re going to give the fans…not just match of the night. Not just…match of the WEEK. Not JUST….match of the MONTH ….but the match of the MOTHERFUCKING DECADE…why? Because…we can. Michaels plays around with the barbed wire, accidentally cutting himself. Michaels opens his mouth in a sarcastic way, as the camera zooms in on the cut. Michaels oohs in aahs in anticipation as the blood begins to come down his finger in a swift motion. Michaels: Take a real close look at this D.C. Take a look at the blood, and notice…it’s not just blood. This stands for your career. This blood is slowly moving down and down my finger…just like your career is in a downward spiral. D.C., you don’t know what to do. You’ve never been on the ropes…anticipating the knockout. You’ve never been the underdog, and you’re not the underdog on Sunday. Michaels pulls his finger back, his face hardening with an evil expression. Michaels: I am. Michaels keeps his hardened look as the camera zooms in on him. Michaels: So D.C. this Sunday…when the pressure’s on, when the lights are on bright when everybody in the World is watching…can you get the job done? Can you do what you talk about on, and on, and on…and finally beat Brian Michaels in a match? He puts his hand on his chin, in a sarcastic thinking posture, looking at the camera with a D.C.-esque look. Michaels: I guess we’ll have to till Sunday for that answer…but Barbie…doesn’t want to wait. Barbie…wants to show MWE what it can do…right now. Michaels delicately reaches into the box, cutting his own hand, as he grimaces in pain as the barbed wire is injected into his palm. He grunts a bit, before taking out the foot-long barbed wire. He swings it across the cell, and it makes a large impact, heavily scratching the door of the cell. Michaels sees the dent and smirks. Michaels: See, because just like on Sunday…Barbie’s making the shit hit the fan…right now. Michaels takes another swing at the door, and sees it begin to budge. He drops Barbie, as the camera pans over to his hands to show a crimson red streak beginning to run down his whole palm. He takes a large step back, looking to see if any guards are watching. When he sees no one, he runs forward, knocking the cell door down with his impact. Michaels: Fucking hell, that hurt. Michaels holds his shoulder in pain as he walks outside of the cell, looking for a key to Barbie McQueen’s door. He sees a key on the desk in front of him and reaches for it with his uninjured shoulder. He smirks as he grabs it and walks over to Barbie McQueen’s cage, where she is also in a orange jumpsuit doing her nails. Barbie: It’s about time. Michaels: Well there was another Barbie involved. Barbie: Excuse me? There’s another girl? Michaels: I’ll explain later. For now, we gotta haul ass. Michaels opens the cell door with the key, before walking back to his cell to put ‘’barbie’’ back in its box. He grabs the box and begins to carry it as Barbie awaits outside of the cell. They run out of the jail, trying their hardest to be discreet. They finally make it outside to where there is a Black Lamborghini waiting for them. They hop in the car, revving the engine as soon as possible. The camera follows them and gets a closeup on the windshield, where Michaels again goes into a serious tone. Michaels: And D.C….you can play your little fuck-you rants…you can coin your catchphrases…but at the end of the day, it’ll be about who wants it more on Sunday night…primetime… Michaels’ revs the engine some more as Barbie McQueen also puts on a smirk. Michaels takes out the box again, which was previous left in his lap. Michaels: So don’t bother coming to Wrestle-Wars if you’re not willing to give me a hundred percent. Michaels gives a bit of a chuckle, looking down at barbie, which now has some of Michaels’ blood over it. He finally looks up after a few moments of eyeing the barbed wire. Michaels: And you know D…they say the past is the prologue to the future. But if at Wrestle-Wars 1, my blood was on your hands…then at Wrestle-Wars 2…your blood... Michaels again looks down at barbie in a sinister way. Michaels: is in barbie’s hands… Michaels speeds away with the Lamborghini at 150MPH speed as the camera goes to static. [/center]
