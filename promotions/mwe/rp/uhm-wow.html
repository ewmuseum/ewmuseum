uhm, WOW; Xavier Stone rp 1

Aug 6 2008

Xavier Stone

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The scene zooms by, the sound of a tuned engine echoes in a small area, other tuned cars fly by as Xavier pases them in his Nissan skyline. He is in a race, rushing in between cars at 180km/h, after passing a part of the highway where several road flares are set, Xavier hits the E-brake and drifts to a stop. Fans rush to his car as he steps out and a man hands him a wad of cash. Man: There you go bra, 10k in the bag...Nice race bro... and better yet, nice car. Xavier: Thanks bro... listen i gotta jet, ill catch yah later Jablez:. Jablez: Word, take it easy, and don't spend all that cash in one place... Xavier: No guarantees bro. Xavier laughs as he gets back in his car, the crowd parts as Xavier leaves the scene. After several hours of driving Xavier arrives at his house in Miami, the night sky is warm with the humidity, and a cool breezy passes over the area. Xavier locks his car and heads into his house, Laura and Troy are playing need for speed Most Wanted on the 56" HD LCD TV on the XboX 360. Xavier: Baby, I'm home. Troy: DAD! Troy pauses the game and drops the controller on the floor and rushes over and hugs Xavier. Xavier picks Xavier up in his arms as he is met halfway down the hallway by Laura, she embraces him and gives him a kiss. Xavier puts Troy down and pulls the cash out of his pocket and hands it to Laura, here babe, go buy yourself somethin nice. Laura looks at the money and smiles. Laura: Won the race? Xavier: Nope, this is last places prize, heh of course I won. Laura: Cool, so when are we gonna cut your promo? Xavier: Right now. Troy, come here bud. Xavier grabs the camcorder off the top of the fridge and tosses it to Troy. They walk into the living room and troy stands in front of the TV as Xavier and Laura cuddle on the couch. Troy rolls his eyes. Troy: Ok its running. Laura: Babe? Whats going on with your job now? Xavier: Well its simple... I quit AEW, for the simple reason because the bastard running it, doesn't know what the fuck hes doing, so I quit. Then I found out MWE was back in action so thats where I went, got my contract to be the third highest paid superstar on the MWE roster. The other day I got a call... I was begged to go back to AEW, because they needed me, they needed high end superstars that can put on a show, bring a crowd and make some money. Laura: Well? What did you say to him? Xavier: Simple, I told him to make me the highest paid superstar, along with giving me my rightfully deserved AEW US Championship. And because he needed me back so bad, I got what I wanted, I got my title, and I am now officially the highest paid wrestler on the AEW Roster. Goes to show the sadness of AEW, but enough about that, I Xavier Stone have brought the WOW to MWE, I will not only reign over AEW as the highest paid athlete, but I will Dominate the MWE roster and come crowning glory, that world title will be around my waist along with my AEW US title. Xavier stands up and walks over to a wall where he has a trophy case, and at the bottom is the AEW US title, above it are 3 more shelves with holders for more belts or trophies. Laura curls up on the couch and watches on. Xavier: In all honesty, this belt means nothing to me, other than something to hold up my pants... To win it fair in a match would give it meaning, I fought in that match, and a certain retard named T.H.K interfered costing me that title. Laura: That makes no sense, you wanted the title as compensation for going back to AEW, So why does it mean nothing to you? Xavier turns around to face Laura and sits on the couch beside her. Xavier: Babe, I wanted the title because it rightfully belongs to me, but the principal behind it gives it next to no purpose. Given I will give that title the proper reign it deserves, but until then, it has no meaning to me other than a payment and bribe. But mind you when I enter that ring at Crowning Glory, it will be for the big cheese title, and 9 other clowns will be striving for the same thing. Troy chuckles as he holds the camera. Laura sits up and hugs Xavier. Laura: I believe in you baby, that title will be yours. Xavier: Speaking of clowns, who the fuck does this Jekyl asshole think he is? Comparing himself to me...his daddy gave him a permanent smile and he gets traumatized for it, he grew up hurting people with no purpose or remorse. I grew up with a loving family until they were torn away from me, I grew up fighting for my life, surviving in the urban jungle, wondering where my next meal was coming from, stealing, hurting, only to survive because I lost out. Then when i was 16 a man named Ghost took me under his wing, he fed me, clothed me, taught me the ropes on how to survive on the streets. Granted it wasn't the right way, but it was my only way, I have killed people, but unlike you, I regret it, I regret ever hurting anyone, I did what I had to do, but at a cost. Don't ever compare me to you or anyone else you freak! Xavier's hands are trembling in fists of rage, the thoughts of his past flash before his eyes, from meeting ghost and living as a biker outlaw, to the worst memory of all, losing his parents. A tear drips down his face as Laura hugs him and kisses his cheek. Laura: Baby it's ok...Troy stop filming please, were done... Troy: Alright, you ok dad? Xavier: I'm fine, it's just the one thing in this world I can't stand is being compared to someone else, and being told what I can and cannot do. Laura: Were done filming, lets go for dinner, Xavier: Turn the camera back on, I have one more thing to say. Troy steps back and begins to film again. Laura lovingly holds onto Xavier as he finishes his promo. Xavier: I don't care who I step into that ring with, it could be god himself, but I guarantee NOTHING will stop me from reigning over MWE as the World Heavyweight Champion. Xavier looks and points to the top shelf of his trophy case as Troy zooms into that spot and a flash of the MWE World title appears just suddenly as Troy set the camera down facing the front door; he forgot to turn it off. As Laura grabs her purse and Troy grabs his PSP, they all head out the door as Xavier twirls the car keys around his finger. The camera's screen begins to flash low battery as it dies and fades to nothing. -Fin-
