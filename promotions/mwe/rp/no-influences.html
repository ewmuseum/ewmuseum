No Influences

Aug 20 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
October 19th, 2004 Exeter, Pennsylvania Craig Zachery Age: 17 *Craig is sitting on a beige leather couch staring at the television screen as his two friends played Super Smash Bros. against each other. He wasn't entirely sure who was who, but it didn't matter. He was honestly only out at Mike's house to see this girl Allison who was currently in the bathroom. He sighed as he listened to the mindless clicking of buttons on the Gamecube Controllers his friend's had in their hand. Across the room Craig's close friend Bryan sat talking to his friend Brett and Peter about something to do with pop culture or video gaming; Craig couldn't tell, as they were a little to far away to hear over the drowning sound of grunts, yells, and clicking of the Smash Bros. match. However, it didn't really matter what they were talking about because Allison had just returned from the bathroom. She walked over to the beige couch and sat opposite Zachery, laying with her head resting on one arm and her feet laid across Zachery's legs.* Allison: So this is pretty intense, huh? Craig: What's intense? *She then nodded towards the two guys playing Smash Bros. and Craig chuckled.* Craig: Yeah, you've been missing out on a whole lot of grunting and yelling. Allison: I'm sure it was pretty hardcore. *Out of nowhere Mike drops his controller and Dan capitalizes by finishing the game against the host himself, but not without a loud scream of victory. Dan: YEAH! SUCK IT MIKE GET FUCKING RAPED! *Allison kind of tucks her body in at the shock of it all as Zachery just laughs and Brett, Peter, and Bryan just turn to stare. Then from the background of it all, their friend Michelle who had been checking her facebook walked from the computer room and sat down in a chair, sighing.* Michelle: Finally you're done! Can we please doooo something now? Dan: Yeah like what, it's 10:30, what do you possibly want to do? *Michelle kind of ponders her head as Brett and Peter resume their conversation.* Michelle: I don't know, not play Smash Bros. Dan: You know you're a pain in the ass sometimes. Michelle: Yeah we'll you're boring. Seriously, you need to drink or something, cause seriously every time we come over, you just want to play games. *Almost like a lightbulb went off in his head, Dan spun around to look at the man he just beat in Smash Bros.* Dan: You heard the lady, you got anything? Mike: Any what? Dan: Beer or something? Mike: Yeah, I should have some in the back fridge, but don't touch any of the wine or hard alcohol. *And with the Dan walked through the door in the basement to the garage where two refrigerators sat. Dan rummaged through one finding nothing, but as he moved to the second fridge he was able to find the stuff he was looking for. Grabbing two green bottles he walked back in and sat down, opened one up with his mouth.* Dan: We'll I guess we'll see, now won't we. *And with that the drinking began and while hesitant at first, others joined in grabbing some of the alcohol out of the back fridge. It wasn't anything out of the ordinary and it wasn't something that they had never done before, so things carried on casually. However, majority of the time that they had drank they were in an empty house, so for their to be actual authority figures around, it kind of put a damper on their normal drinking fun. By 12:15 a few were tipsy and two in particular, Dan and Peter, was absolutely drunk. Craig and Allison were probably the two most sober, only enjoying maybe a drink or to each, as they continued to talk.* Craig: So I was thinking you should come down to the shore with us sometimes. It'd be great, the shore is awesome. Allison: But don't you think that's kind of awkward. Craig: Nah, I thought the same thing the first time I went with someone to the shore, but it turned about being awesome. Allison: We'll who'd be going? Craig: Well as of right now I'm having Bryan, Michelle, and Mike come down. I might invite some other people, but I wanted to get the people I really wanted to come down to get in first. *Allison ponders it, but stops short as she she's Peter grabbing a small exercise trampoline and sets it up near the middle of the room.* Allison: Hold on a sec, what is he doing. Craig: Ah he's blasted who the hell knows. *Craig laughs a bit at this, but Allison looks genuinely concerned at this point.* Peter: Alright, it's set up. Do it and see what happens. Dan: It's a ceiling fan I stop ceiling fans with my hand all the time and it doesn't hurt. My head will be fine. Peter: Then do it already dicklips. *Allison shakes her head and turns to Craig.* Allison: Can you please stop these two jackasses from hurting themselves. Craig: Huh? I mean, it's not a smart idea, but I doubt it will hurt him all that much. Allison: Well then just do it for me, not even for them. Craig: Well what do you want me to do, sit on him? Allison: I don't know, but c'mon, this can not end good. *However as Allison and Craig are talking Dan has flung himself into action taking a few steps before bouncing off the mini-trampoline. As he reached the blades he got intimidated and instead of trying to stop it with his head he went for a somewhat kind of flip. Hitting into the ceiling fan his force knocked it down and it crashed all over the floor. The sound was deafening and all sound was sucked from the room. Side conversations stopped, everyone put their drinks down, and only Craig slid over to Dan to see if he was alright. Luckily Dan's back and neck weren't to hurt, but the landing did cause some pain. However the worst was about to come as they all heard a door open and slam shut. All in shock, not really knowing what to do, they all just kind of froze up and stared at the giant mess of a broken ceiling fans, examining the bits of sheet-rock that fell from the ceiling, as well as the shattered glass the was spread around the room. Dan started to get from his feet holding his back as everyone jumped when they heard the sound of Mike's father from atop the stairs looking down.* Mike's Father: WHAT THE FUCK HAPPENED?! *The memory begins to fade...* August 19th, 2010 New York City, New York Craig Zachery Age: 23 *Craig Zachery was sitting in a very elegant hotel room by himself looking out the window. MWE only pays for a portion of the air fare and lodging for an MWE superstar, however Craig Zachery, his girlfriend Crystal, and his friend Bryan all pitched in for a very nice suite. Zachery insisted that they did not spend the money on it, but as all good friends wanted, Craig's friends wanted him to be in comfort for the nights he had to spend in New York before Crowning Glory. Sitting in grayish beige cargo shorts, a plain blue t-shirt and sunglasses on his head, Zachery looked somewhat relaxed but his friends were no where to be found. Checking his phone he saw that he had a text from his friend Bryan saying he was on his way. Crystal however was in the bathroom doing who knew what. He didn't care though, he was thinking about other things. Crowning Glory was around the corner, but that was a side thought. He was actually feeling depressed, but wasn't really showing it. It had been almost 5 years.* Crystal: Hey why's it so quiet out there? You got something on your mind babe? *Zachery looked over his shoulder at the doorway as Crystal stuck her head out from the bathroom.* Craig: Nah, I'm fine...just waiting for Bryan, that's all. Crystal: Alright, well, I'm almost finished up in here, so that's perfect. *Craig gave a faint smile and turned back to the window looking out. Bryan was as close of a friend as he had, and he had a bunch of friends. Bryan just so happened to be one of the few people who frequently stayed in touch and one of the few who actually came down to south Jersey to visit. Depressed as he may have been Zachery couldn't help but feel excited that he was going to be spending the night with his good friend again.* Crystal: So what do you have in mind of doing when Bryan gets here? Craig: I don't know, probably just gonna chill for a bit. It's been a few months so probably just catch up. Crystal: Well that sounds good and dandy...but we're in New York's heart babe. Let's go out tonight. *Craig pondered it in his head for a bit. He didn't really feel like going out, considering New York was a place he was somewhat familiar with anyway. But to keep peace, he swallowed took a breath and accepted that he was going out tonight.* Craig: Sure, I'm down. *Zachery looked down at his phone again and saw that it was already around 6:40, so it wouldn't be long until they went out. Looking out it was slightly dimming outside, so that meant around an hour of actual quality time with his best friend. Saddened Zachery hung his head a little, however a knock at the door electrified his spirits as he hopped up out of his chair and opened the door. There stood an skinny average looking man around 5'8" with brown hair and pale skin. This must have been Bryan.* Craig: Hey look who turned up Crystal! Bryan: Yo, sorry it took so long, but I can see it was worth it. This place is nice. *They walked to the middle lounge area of the suite and sat down each in chairs opposite of each other. Crystal walked out shortly after and took a seat on the couch, but not first before giving Bryan a hug.* Craig: So what's going on dude, it's been a few months. Bryan: A few months, man you really have been busy. It's been a little over a year now. Craig: No way, there's no way it's been a year. Bryan: Yeah dude, it's crazy how busy you've been. I'm pretty last time we saw each other was at Michelle's kid's birthday party last June. I would have seen you again this year, but... Craig: Yeah, I've been busy. Seriously, I wish I could have made it to some of these things, but my life has just been so crazy... *Bryan holds up a hand to pause his friend in mid-sentence. He gave a little chuckle as he shook his head.* Bryan: Listen, don't worry about it, it's cool. Everyone knows that you've been busy lately. But isn't that why I'm here anyway. Crystal: Yep yep, you get to see him kick some ass Sunday. *Crystal was all smiles, but it kind of injected the fact that Zachery was competing on Sunday back into his mind. He slouched back in his chair a bit as he waved her hype off.* Craig: Yeah I got a match, but that's for later...for now, we just chill. Like it used to be. Crystal: Yeah, speaking of chilling, it's getting darker out. We should probably leave in a bit. Bryan: Oh, we're going out? Crystal: Yeah, might as well...I mean, it is NYC. Bryan: Yeah I'm diggin' it. Definitely diggin' it. *The three of them kept talking for a few more minutes, but as night slowly crept up on them they began to disperse to their bags getting out nicer clothes as they walked out of the house. After a bit of changing and freshening up they all filed into a car and headed out to the heart of the city. As they drove, conversation sparked up here and there, mainly deciding what they wanted to do. None of them had eat, so they decided to stop at a restaurant for about an hour, eating and talking the whole time, with Crystal and Slack even ordering a few drinks. As they walked to the car it hit them that they once again had nothing to do.* Bryan: Man, excuse me for being blunt, but that was fucking tasty. *He let out a cheerful burp and patted his stomach as Crystal wiped her mouth. Craig nodded in agreement, not sure what to say.* Bryan: So now what? *Crystal looked Craig and stood still for a few seconds, obviously thinking of something.* Crystal: How about drinks. Bryan: Hmm, I had like two already, but I guess a few more wouldn't hurt. Crystal: Alright then, it's settled, let's find ourselves a place to drink. *Craig looked a tad worried for some reason, feeling uneasy about the whole situation. He offered an alternative.* Craig: Would you guys want to just get some from the store. I've got money. Bryan: Yeah, but where's the fun in that. I mean, like Crystal said earlier, we're in New York, a bar sounds like a grand idea! *Shuffling his feet and looking a bit nervous, Craig again was in a position he didn't want to be. He saw the happiness on his good friend's face and accepted reluctantly, taking over as driver now. After like ten minutes of driving Craig finally found a place parked, put a few coins in the meter, and along with his two passengers made his way into the bar. It was a decently respectable bar with nice stools and some pretty large televisions along some of the walls. The actual counter was pretty massive and the stools were long to accommodate. A bowl of peanuts sat at one end and a pool table was seen on the opposite side of the room past a sea of tables. Taking her seat at the bar itself, Crystal patted the two seats next to her signaling for Bryan and Craig to take a seat. The bartender walked over and she eyed the three of them down before taking their orders.* Bartender: Can I get you three something to drink? Bryan: Does a bear shit in the woods? Crystal: Yeah, let me get a jack and coke. Bryan: Make mine a Capt'n and Coke. Bartender: And you? *Craig looked up at her wondering what she wanted, before realizing that she was taking his order. He just kind of waved her off shaking his head slowly. She walked away to get the other's their drinks.* Bryan: How come you're not gettin' anything? Craig: Someone's got a drive. Crystal: Oh honey, don't worry about that, it's cool. Relax and get a drink. Craig: Nah, it's cool. Not thirsty right now anyway, maybe later... *Time passes, drinks are drunk, and slowly but surely Crystal and Bryan begin to loosen up, much like the rest of the bar. People are talking and mingling, but Craig is just boringly looking at the rows of beer and liquor on the wall. He didn't really want to drink, mainly because of his recent thoughts. It was making him feel uncomfortable. Now on his sixth drink of the night, Bryan ordered one more, as he laughed at a joke Crystal had just finished telling, when he noticed his friend aimlessly looking over the bar.* Bryan: Yo Craig, seriously, get a drink. You look so down right now. *Crystal turned to face Craig as well.* Craig: Dude, like I said, it's cool, I don't want to drink. Crystal: What's the deal? Seriously, we're at a fucking bar. Craig: What? I said I don't want to drink, drop it. *Bryan is too intrigued now, so he persists.* Bryan: Nah man, what're you thinking about man, it's cool. Craig: Holy shit. *Craig gets up and walks away from his two friends outside the bar and leans against the wall taking a breath. Soon out is Bryan and Crystal.* Crystal: Baby you need to calm down, we didn't mean... Craig: Crystal. Shut up. Bryan: Woah dude, c'mon we're just trying to be friends here. Craig: Listen, I don't care if you're trying to be friends, enemies, or fucking toasters. I just want some space. Bryan: You thinkin' about Mike? *This strikes a nerve in Craig. He suddenly becomes wide eyed and looks dead in the face of Bryan.* Craig: You're thinkin' about it too? Bryan: Yeah, I mean, it's been almost 5 years now. Craig: And you're still drinkin'? Bryan: Well I mean, I can't just deprive myself because... Craig: How the fuck are you still drinking when you know what happened. *The tension rises a little bit and Craig's depression turns to anger.* Bryan: Listen, I'm a grown man. I know what happened in my past and I don't plan on letting shit like that happen again. Craig: Some way of showing it. *Bryan puts his drink down on a ledge and starts to get a bit frustrated. Bryan: Listen, I was there too, don't try to make me feel bad, I know what I'm doing. Craig: I'm not making you feel bad, I'm just grasping it myself that you're drinking with that heavy of a topic on your head. Bryan: What are you saying I shouldn't be? Craig: No, I'm saying that you're fucking ridiculous. Bryan: Alright dude, I can see you that just can't get over it. Craig: I am over it, though. Bryan: Then have a fucking drink. *Craig pauses trying to find words. He can't and so he just walks away.* Crystal: Where are you going? Craig: I'm going where someone isn't going to try and force me to do shit I don't want to do...have fun fucking drinkin' with Bryan. *And with that Craig tossed the car keys to the feet of his girlfriend before walking away, angry and confused. He didn't really know what to say to either of his good friends, he could only talk to himself right now. As he walked down the streets of New York, he ignored anyone who saw him and just passed them by like they were ghosts. He made it back to his apartment about a half an hour later and the car was gone so that meant they were still out. He didn't care.* Craig: Fuck it. *Without thought he walked upstairs to his hotel room and entered his room and sat in the same chair as before, thinking about the same things as he was before, except now he was angry.* Craig: It's been almost 5 years since my friend Mike died and it's all because of fucking alcohol. How could he drink right now. *He shakes his head and lightly pounds the window still.* Craig: If we hadn't drank that night...if Dan hadn't broke that fan...nothing would happened. We were all supposed to be happy and friendly. It's not my fault Mike got in trouble for that fan and couldn't take the trip down to the shore. It wasn't. *Craig was visibly angry, his face pale and colorless.* Craig: How the fuck was I supposed to know that Mike was going to get depressed that we went without him...how could I have known that. I couldn't have. I regret ever getting into it with him that night after the fan incident. QUOTE Mike: So yeah, my dad won't let me go. Craig: That sucks dick dude. It sucks that you can't go, we were all expecting to have you down there with us. Mike: You're still going? Craig: Well yeah, we've had this planned for months. I already have the house rented. Mike: Fucking cool. Awesome. Craig: Well what do you want me to do? Mike: Nothing. Nothing at all. Have yourself a good time. Craig: Listen it isn't my fault your dad's fucking retarded and doesn't realize it wasn't your fault. Mike: And now you're calling my dad stupid. Ya know, why don't you just shut the fuck up. Craig: Alright then, why don't you call me back when you get your head straight. I don't talk to whiny emo bitches you twist words around. *Craig just stopped talking altogether. He was shaken thinking about it and how his friend completely disregarded the situation.* Craig: I shouldn't have called him emo...wait, fuck that. I didn't know. I had no clue that he was going to actually go emo. I didn't know he would get that depressed and actually fucking kill himself. I didn't... *Zachery stopped again. He didn't want to get upset. He knew himself and he knew getting upset would not help him.* Craig: All I know now is that I'm not gonna let anyone tell me what the fuck to do, especially not someone who was there. If Michelle didn't get Dan to get the alcohol in the first place, it would have never happened. *Craig pauses again taking a huge breath finally completely regaining himself.* Craig: And now I have this match...this huge match. The amount of pressure...especially me saying I'm not gonna get pinned...it's huge. But I guess it's worth it when a number one contender's spot is on the line. But the more I think about it the more I feel better because I know my head will be in the right place when I walk into the match. I know that I'll be focused on winning and proving myself for a second straight week. *Craig gives his chin a slight rub and feels some hair growing in as the stubble is prickly on his hands. He readjusts his seat and sits up straight.* Craig: I look at my opponents and I see three men all with problems, all with their minds focused elsewhere, with winning somewhere in the back of their mind. Evermore with problems about figuring out his place and spot in MWE. Moore with this stigma of wanting to show he people how great he is, so much that instead of winning, he's focused on showing people how he can out wrestle and out talk me, Evermore, and Charisma...and then that brings me to him. *Craig cracks his back and knuckles loosening his body up in the process. He licks his lips and gives a half chuckle half frown and sighs.* Craig: See I didn't come to MWE with a problem with anyone and just based off the first week I know who I like and who I don't. And I've decided that I don't like Casey Charisma. He's like that friend who always has to one up you. Or better yet, he's like a religious zealout trying to force his thoughts down your throat. *Craig swallows and has a pretty upset look on his face as he says this. He doesn't look any happier than before.* Craig: And if there's one thing I can't stand, it's when people try to force their thoughts on other people. It doesn't work, has never worked, and will never work. What I don't think Casey realizes is that while he may think his words are in good heart, they come off as dickish and rude, and it's evident the fans agree. See to try and force and idea into someone's thought pattern is to basically strip them of their free will. If you can get an idea pounded so far into someone's head, you just stripped them of probably the greatest quality a human has...and that is to think on their own. Look at any dictator in the world's history...they tried it, they failed eventually. It simply doesn't work. *Zachery stands up and begins to pace back in forth and just like before, the upset feeling in his stomach turns to angry feeling.* Craig: And especially with me, after the shit I've gone through in my life, that type of behavior won't fly. I'm not trying to fight for the people, because honestly, I still don't know if the fans are who I want to fight for. What I do know is that I'd sure as hell rather be on their side than your's Casey. Benny Ten may be a "cool" guy in your book, but what about if you just met him on the street. What if you just met him and he didn't have thousands of hours of your brainwashing under him. Would he still be cool? Would he still like you? I think Casey, you need to look into your mirror because to me, you're just as bad as Catholic priest walking around the room look for money and getting it because he tells people what is right and what is wrong. *Craig stops dead in his tracks.* Craig: And who in the fuck honestly gives them the right to say what is right and what is wrong? Who in the hell gave you the right to tell people what is cool and what isn't. The thing is, they didn't, and the fact that you walk around with your self-righteous smug attitude about you definitely rubs me the wrong way. And just like Charisma, Moore who in the fuck are you to tell people that you are the best? Who do you think YOU are that you can claim to be better than someone when you haven't even faced a match. You think you're so good and that everyone is below you, but to be frank, I think you should focus on actually winning first and bragging later. *Zachery had once again resumed pacing back and forth looking out the window as he did. The anger was coming out slowly, but he didn't want to explode and it was evident something was being held back.* Craig: Ya know, I almost feel like I'm facing the church and to be honest, I'm not that religious of a guy. You have Evermore who is the brainwashed Christian who is so unsure of himself and his spot in MWE that he just hopes and prays for a win and for some guidance. And then you have Charisma who spreads the bullshit into people like Evermore's head, convincing them that they are worthless and they aren't cool. Building up to Moore who might as well think he is God himself, proclaiming himself better than Charisma and Evermore and people just come to him hoping that he with his confidence can save them. *Zachery once again stops in pacing to put emphasis on his point.* Craig: Well I say fuck that. *Craig actually sits down altogether now and tries to cool off.* Craig: I say if I'm facing the church then I'll be MWE's household atheist. I don't buy into any of my opponent's bullshit stories and I never will. I'm focused on the match and trying to distract me only makes that focus stronger. Now I'm not going to be a hypocrite and tell my opponent's to start focusing, because to be honest, I don't care. If they wish to focus on shit that is insignificant and pointless, so be it, they'll figure out what is really their priority later on in their career, but for Sunday, let it be known that I'm going for a win and that is my focus. It's the only logical thing to do in a situation this big. *Craig slouches in his chair and relaxes.* Craig: I'm not looking for friends, I'm not looking for praise, but I'm looking for a reason to keep going. I'm looking to make sure that the last year hasn't been a waste of time and all my training, all my exclusion of friends and family was worth it, and the best way to do that is by winning on Sunday and that is what I'll do. I said at that press conference that I had something special planned and I still do. And with my focus you'll see it in the ring, but until then, you all just get to watch and wait as my opponents bury themselves before the bout even starts. *And with that Zachery had completely calmed down and like earlier in the day he sat staring out the window, thinking about events of the past.*
