It's Joseph; J-O-S-E-P-H

Apr 23 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The scene cuts to Triple G backstage warming up and jumping around, excitedly. Mr Markowitz walks up to G and pats him on the back. They wink at one another and their entrance music hits, Fake It by Seether. They start walking down the ramp, and slap hands with fans along the barricade. They look at each other once again, both holding a microphone. They start walking towards the ring. They get to the ring, and they both simultaneously slide in. Markowitz hoists his microphone up first. Markowitz: Sharpman, your little, group, with your buddies will fail worse then, Nathaniel Samsonite. You already, lost one of your members, in Paige. Hell I give you till June, until one of you turns on each other. Markowitz smiles and puts down his mic. He runs his fingers threw his hair as Triple G hoists his mic up to his mouth. Triple G: The same will happen, from before. You guys will turn on each other, team again, turn, team, turn, team, turn causing a never ending cycle between you guys. How can you even trust each other? You guys, especially you, Sharpman, are a disgrace to wrestling. Triple G looks at Markowitz and looks at the mic. Markowitz hoists his mic up again. Markowitz: Vanguard, you guys have a bad reputation, while Mayhem Mafia, doesn’t. We are known as the better stable. While you...you guys..are a waste of time. Markowitz looks up and at the titantron all the way in front of the arena. Markowitz: Also… Triple G: Hold it there Marko. I have something to say. Sharpman, I use to look up to you, in my early career. I think it was during our days in, Rev~Pro, was it? I’m pretty sure it was. Back then, you were something I thought I could never be. Boy, did I think wrong. Once I came to the great federation, Mayhem Wrestling Entertainment, I realized something, something big. Something huge. Something GIGANTIC. You want to know what that thing was? It was you. I realized something about you. You know what that one thing was? That thing is that you are a weak, spineless coward who hides behind people, to get what he wants. Triple G snickers and looks at Markowitz. Markowitz nods at him and lifts up the microphone to his mouth. Markowitz: You didn't change your name and team with Cannon for the both of you, you did it for yourself. Remember that time when you punched that girl in the face? You don’t care for anyone but yourself. You are a selfish, pathetic bastard, and you don't deserve to face us. Markowitz points at himself and then at Triple G. Triple G: Yeah, that’s right Marko. He is a pathetic bastard. Shellfish, spineless, loser. Hell, you use Cannon as a human shield. Why don’t you get out and fight like a man. If you think you’re so good, then forget Cannon for this one match, and face us for real. No human shields. Just a clean match. Let’s see who win’s. Let’s see. Triple G looks at Marko. They both smile at each other. Markowitz looks around and sees a cheering crowd. There are many “Sharpman Sucks” signs and chants. They see a mixture of: “It's spelled Joseph!”, “Sinister Suck-a-dick!”, Sharpman=Epic...Fail! ”, and many more like those. Markowitz looks at all of them. Reads them all, word for word. Triple G as well. They look at each other and smile. They look at the fans, and they smile again. The cheers and the “Sharpman Sucks” chants are heard loudly all around. Even the upper deck can hear it clearly. Markowitz picks up the microphone near to his face. He starts to talk but is interrupted by the cheers of the fans. Markowitz looks down and continues. Markowitz: Guys, Guys. I know how much you like Triple G and I. Just take it easy, on the Sharpman Sucks chants. I know it’s true, and I personally like it, but I have something to say to you all, and Sharpman. Sharpman, a little birdie told me, that you think this match was made because the MWE staff believes you are good enough to face the best tag team in MWE, one on two. That isn't the reason, Sharpman. The reason is, because you need to be shown, you are NOT what you make yourself out to be. You are not "Jesus", and you are not, "sinister". You’re a loser. Markowitz pauses, and takes a deep breath and turn to Triple G who nods. Triple G: Joesph, may I call you Joe? Anyway I don’t even understand how you can call yourself “sinister” or anything like that. Sorry to brake it to you buy you’re a loser. Just a plain loser. You think that you’re going to have an easy win against us, but you’re wrong. You’re wrong. We will make sure to give you hella pain. Hella. Pain. Blood Fuze is coming for you Sharpman. Coming for you. Marko, what do you say? Are we going to beat him or what!?!? Markowitz looks at Triple G and then at the fans.. He smiles and the fans cheer. Markowitz: Of course we are going to beat him. How can we not? The man can’t win without a human shield, like Cannon. He may think he can beat us, but he can’t. He’s daydreaming to tell you all the truth. He thinks’ he is better then everyone. He has a super ego. The thing is, he isn’t better than any of us, even though he thinks he is. Hell, it may be two on one, but I do NOT care. Your gonna get the beating of a lifetime. And there is nothing you can do about it. The fans break into loud cheers. The chant of: “Blood Fuze” is heard loudly and is echoing around the arena. There are a lot of new signs up in the hands of fans. They see: “Blood Fuze Rules”, “Triple G=TheBest”, “Markowitz…… MARKOWITZ”, “Blood Fuze IS Marko”, and many more. Markowitz points to many of the signs he see’s. Of course being the ones about him. Triple G just looks at him and points at the signs of him. Markowitz: Let’s settle down, let’s settle down. Now, my final word to Sharpman… Is, screw you. Markowitz quickly drops his microphone, and G does the same. Triple G and Markowitz both then simultaneously climb out the ring. They signal goodbye to the crowd and then turn around and walk away. After a minute or two of slowly walking back with the crowd cheering, Blood Fuze gets to the end of the entrance ramp, scream goodbye one more time and head to the back. The fans start to cheer dramatically, and quickly Mr Markowitz walks back in. Mr Markowitz: Oh, and it's spelled Joseph, not Joesph. J-O-S-E-P-H. Joseph. -End-
