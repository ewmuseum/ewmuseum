No Place Like Home; Supernova WW2 RP

Oct 25 2008

Supernova

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A black and silver Cadillac puled up to a driveway in Scarborough. It was a relatively quiet neighborhood compared to others in the region. Out stepped non other then the new MWE X-TV champion, ‘Supernova’ Vega Knight. He took his title which he had placed in the front seat, and carried it. He knocked on the door. After a minute, a smiling woman, with long brunette hair, answered the door. Vega: Hey mom. The woman was non other then Adrian Knight, the mother of Vega. The two embraced each other. Adrian: This is quite unexpected. Come in, come in. Adrian allowed Vega in through first, as Adrian closed the front door afterwards. The two sat down in the living room. Adrian: So, how is my baby boy doing these days? Vega blushed a bit, before shaking his head. Vega: Mom, how many times do I have to ask you not to call me that? Adrian: Not enough. Vega sighed, before he smiled. Vega: Well I’ve only wrestled a total of three matches so far. Lost only one of them, but the odds were heavily against me. I do know that everyone thinks highly of me there, and it shows. Adrian: Oh really? Vega: Yeah. Check this out. Vega then brings his title out into view. Adrian knew it was a championship belt. She was impressed. Adrian: Well done Vega. I knew that as long as you stayed true to who you were, you’d be going places. And you won it during your first three matches. That is impressive. Vega: Thanks mom. I had to beat a guy who was otherwise undefeated, and then a real thorn in my side. A man by the name of JJ Executioner. Adrian: Why do you say he’s a thorn in your side? Vega: I kicked the overgrown pussy’s ass in my first match, he was involved in the match I won this belt, and now, I have to face him again come this Sunday. I’m sick and tired of him spewing out crap that he was a mentally tortured kid who snapped and injured a few classmates. As far as I’m concerned, if he can’t deal with some kids just poking fun at him, how the hell is he supposed to succeed at life. Hell I know I pick some people, and some pick on me, but do you see me losing my cool over it? Adrian: The only thing you lost your ‘cool’ over, was that one incident. Vega: Oh that reminds me, I am going to visit that hospital that I admitted myself to, right after this. Adrian smirked, knowing the real reason. Adrian: You mean the psych ward that you were kicked out of for having done the horizontal grind with that receptionist. Vega ponders that for a second, then bursts out laughing. Vega: Yeah. That's the one. Adrian smiles, knowing that her son was in a good mood. Vega: But, as it seems, there is also another reason why I must go there. I got a shoot to do on my opponent. It's really starting to irritate me how they keep putting me against the same guy. I mean do they honestly expect that he will actually defeat me? Adrian: Well, I have yet to see you wrestle on TV so I wouldn't know. But if you keep defeating the same guy, over and over again, that does tell you he will have no chance of defeating you. Vega: Yeah. I mean, what is this guy really? Sure, Halloween is coming up soon and all, but he doesn't have to dress like a retarded Hannibal Lectar all the fucken time ma, what is he THINKING!!!!! Adrian giggles for a second, knowing it was purely Vega's sense of humor kicking in. Adrian: Didn't he say you picked a fight with a redneck hobo before you won the X-TV title? Vega: Yeah he did, but that's just his immaturity, and failure to see what really happened. See I was at the bar, conducting a funny promo for the show, with Matt and Jeff, when the Redneck decided to pick a fight with me. I just kicked his ass, because I don't like to lose a fight, no matter how big or small. Adrian smiled. She knew it was the truth. Vega had no hesitation in his voice, nor was he wound up when he said it. It was his usual, relaxed self, as he talked about it. Vega: Anyways ma, I better get going, I have another shoot to do. Very important shit. Adrian sighed, understanding. Adrian: Alright. But stay here until you need to go. You don't need all the extra stress from living life in the fast lane. Vega: I know. I've already lost my mind once, don't need to again. So I'll see you around. And thanks mom. I really appreciate your offer. Two hours later, Vega was now in the psych ward of the Toronto Memorial Hospital. He had brought in his camcorder, and switched it on, currently focussing on himself. Vega: Hello once again MWE, this is your master of disaster, 'Supernova' Vega Knight. Now this promo is different from others I've done. See I'm actually visiting a place that has a great deal of importance to me. See this place is where I self admitted myself for a total of six months of my life, due to the imfamous incident that I have often talked about. Now I come back here sometimes to still try to figure out how I can get over this. Vega sighed, almost content with himself. Vega: Now for your pleasure, I'm going to be conducting an interview with this man. Vega shifts the camera view to a gray haired, late fourties doctor. The man had the usual doctors clothings on, including the white coat. Vega: Meet Doctor Richard Meridian Doctor M: Thank you Vega, good to see you again. Vega: Let's sit down for this, shall we? Or are you to busy with other patients? Doctor M: Actually I'm not at this time. So yeah, let's do this. The view is a bit shifty as Vega sets up his camcorder on a tripod that he brought with him. Once that is done, both Vega and his doctor sit down in the lounge. Doctor M: Well first, I have to ask you Vega, how are you these days since your last visit. Vega sighs for a second, as he folds his hands. Vega: Well truth be honest be honest, I'm almost over it. It still will be something that bothers me, but in the end, I had to do it. I might have saved a lot of people's mental mind, or even lives when I stepped up and dealt with the man. Doctor M: That's good to know. But after seeing you're promos, it would seem you're using your own self anger and using it against your opposition. Vega was surprised by what the doctor said. Vega: You watch wrestling? Doctor M: Yeah. I see nothing wrong with it, plus it's entertaining. That being said, congratulations on winning the X-TV title. Vega: Thanks doc. But yeah, you're right. I am a believer that aggression does result in a far more focused and stronger person in the wrestling ring. So now my questions for you are, have you seen the recent promos done by JJ Executioner? Doctor M: as a matter of fact I have, and I have to say a few things. It would seem that he's trying to be somebody he's not. He could very well be one of the nicest of people in the world, but nobody truly knows, as they buy into his gimmick. I mean seriously Vega. Would they let somebody who supposedly sentenced to maximum security prison, for life, for doing what he did, out? Vega: No they wouldn't. There would have been no way for JJ to escape. Thus it's quite obvious that it's purely a gimmick, and a lame one as well. There are just far to many of those supposed psycho and monster gimmicks out there. Doctor M: Exactly. The novelty of such a gimmick has worn off, unless you can make it original. But I digress. Vega chuckles for a second, before composing himself. Vega: Now I have to ask you doc, if that story of JJ was actually true, what would you say about his mental stability? Doctor M: Well then I have to say that there must have been something wrong with him, not realizing that some kids enjoy picking on others. He could have turned the other cheek and ignored them. But then again, he was supposedly beat up, and retaliated. If that were me, I would have taken legal action to restrain the guys who supposedly tortured him. Vega: Well thank you doc, I'm glad you were able to give your review on JJ. Just goes to show you that my opponent at Wrestle Wars Two, is probably the least convincing person that is in wrestling at the current moment. So JJ, do yourself a favor after I kick your ass for a third time. Change your gimmick around, and actually try to sound convincing. Jeez, trying to listen to another one of your promos is worse then hearing Jillian Hall sing Toxic. Frightening to even think about. Both men burst out laughing at that statement. Vega: Now I better get going now. I have places to go and shit. It was great talking to you again. Doctor M: My pleasure Vega. Go out there, and defeat JJ Executioner. For the love of all things holy, please shut that guy up. Vega: I will be more then happy to do so. Vega got up and detached his camcorder from the tripod. He began to look around the hospital focusing on the receptionist. Vega: Well now, if this isn't a sight for sore eyes. This is a babe right here. The beautiful brunette receptionist, Miss Cloe Kytes. Cloe: Hello Vega. The camcorder was once again displaying Vega's face, as he shifted it to do so. Vega: for those of you wondering, I had what a lot of people like to call a 'good time' with her when I was in here. After that, the doctors thought because I had done that, I was more then mentally fit. I'm mentally fit enough to have a good time with this gal. Cloe: Thanks Vega. Say, how's about you show that JJ guy how to kiss a girl. Vega: Oh I like the sound of that. Vega put down his tripod for a second. He then shifted the camcorder to show the two kissing. Needless to say, it was a hot one. Once the kiss broke, Cloe sighed, with a more then satisfied smile on her face. Vega: Now that ladies and gentlemen, is how you do a kiss right. I'm Vega Knight, signing off, and I'll see you, the MWE fans at Wrestle Wars Two, where I will once again, destroy JJ Executioner, and expose him for the biggest, most fake piece of crap that the wrestling world has to endure. Vega switched his camcorder off, satisfied with his promo being done. Right afterwards, he left, heading back home, until he had to leave to attend Wrestle Wars Two
