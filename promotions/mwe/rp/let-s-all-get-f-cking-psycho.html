Let's all get "f*cking" psycho!

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
IMAGINE: All cuss words being bleeped out and imagine this as some cheesy Jerry Springer skit. it'll help understand it ALOT better. And I assure you all this was not rushed. It just flowed right off my hand after I saw a recent movie it gave me chills how funny this turned out. The scene fades in with Sinc sitting in front of a black background VIA Satellite wearing a zipped up brown coat and sunglasses are fitted on his hair as he smiles at the camera. He is filmed from the waist up while he sits intently. Sinc: As you all may or may not know by now Sonny said some very....harsh words. And it's tough to talk about because...becau- Sinc breaks down into fake tears as he rubs his eyes and lowers his head. Then raises his head back up in just seconds and in a passionate focus back at the camera. Sinc: BECAUSE I JUST DON'T GIVE A FLYING HORSE'S ASS WHAT YOU SAY ABOUT ME! You can be drunk, you can be sober, you can drop F bombs like Howard Stern on a Jerry Springer set hell I don't care! All I know is I am not going to let you say such false rocky mountain top of the line CRAP! Without me saying my OWN share. I am going to make this short and sweet. Obviously you had no idea that you were dealing with a man that will not just stand there and take it like a kangaroo in a boxing ring. Sinc shakes his head in pure disgust. Sinc: So I took the liberty to find the most similar ancestors of yours on this earth. Call it a great journey if you will but after many days of searching I think i've finally found...THE ONE! He is quite possibly the greatest predator alive today. Ladies and Gentlemen boys and girls, be prepared to go on an epic journey of ups and downs, and where F bombs and bad rap songs rule. We shall venture to....Prerogative Asylum In Alexander Valley, California! A male nurse in a white lab coat and white pants is seen standing in front of the door holding a clipboard. The door has a very small slit window on the top of it. The camera is only framed on the door, the nurse, and finally Sinc comes into view. He is seen wearing a white T shirt and tan cargo pants. Their lower bodies aren’t seen. Sinc: So tell me Mr. Man Nurse….what is beyond that door? Nurse: Well the name is Trace Cogon. CERTIFIED male nurse sir! Sinc: My my you’re a little jumpy today slow down there cowboy we don’t want to ride that horse JUST yet. Trace: Sorry I’m just PUMPED MAN! I’M PUMPED! Sinc: And why are you a nurse again? You seem to fit RIGHT in with the crazies here. Sinc makes a whistling sound and winds his finger up to make a gesture to the camera that Trace seemed a little crazy. Trace: I used to be a patient here but not anymore NO WAY MAN! NO WAY! Sorry I just always wanted to be on TV! THIS IS FAR OUT MAN! FARRR OUTTTTT!!!! Trace looks at the camera and giggles while waving hello. Sinc: Ok enough enough. Now tell me what you have behind this door so I can get on with my reasoning’s to the MERCIFUL Sinc fans out there and to get on with this EPIC adventure of sex and slander. Trace: Well we got a Class-A turrets patient behind those doors. He cusses almost every ten seconds. He can be feisty. Sinc: Open that bastard up Nurse guy I want to see this man for myself. Trace: You got it. But I am telling you, he’s a little loud. Sinc: Loud? COME ON! Trace…. Trace: Yes? Sinc leans closer to Trace and gets up to his face eagerly. Sinc: COME ON!!!!! Trace: Ok ok. Here you go. Trace grabs some keys from his back pants pocket and slowly unlocks to broad white door. When the door opens it reveals a man that looks very similar to Sonny Williams……if he just got mauled by a dog. The man is crouching in a corner and biting his nails. Crazy Patient: GET THE F*CK OUT F*CKING F*CKER F*CKKKKKKK!!!!!!!!!!!! YOU!!!!!!!! Sinc and Trace seem to almost be blown away by the cussing and yelling. Sinc: Are you SURE this isn’t an ancestor or relative of Sonny? Cause if I am not mistaken….that’s EXACTLY like Sonny. Sinc kneels down and looks at the patient as if he was a type of animal. Sinc: Hey there big guy….how about you and me have a little….talk huh? The patient is able to sustain his cussing problem long enough to walk over to Sinc and sit in front of him. Sinc then sits down with him. Sinc: Got any problems you wanna talk about there bud? Trust me I can relate. Crazy Patient: Sorry….I havn’t…F*CKERRRRRRR!!!!!!! I haven’t takin my meds yet. And when I don’t got my meds in me I get like this the name’s…F*CK F*CK F*CK! Sinc: That’s a VERY interesting and quite amusing name. Crazy Patient: No no…it’s Jones…ma name’s Jones mister. Sinc: Well Jones mister it’s nice to meet you, my name is Sinc Mercier! Jones: So why are you he-F*CKING COCK SUCKER! Here? Sinc: Woah there big man chill out for a second. I am just here to show the fans that Sonny has a few offspring waddling around this earth. The Nurse, Trace, is standing in front of the door way and watching intently as the camera remains on Sinc and Jones. Sinc: Your quite a character aren’t ya. Hey I got something for ya. Sinc takes something out of his cargo pants pocket and it appears to be some sort of CD album. Jones: What…what’s tha-F*CKIN F*CK!!!!!!!! That. Sinc: This is a home made CD of EVERY Sonny Williams rap ever recorded taken from live audio from previous Mayhem TV Broadcastings. And no, I am not a fan. Jones: Man that sounds really cool! F*CK IT ALL!!!!!! Sinc puts his right hand over his right ear and cups it as if trying to hear. He does this with sarcasm as he blinks his eyes out of humor. Sinc: What’s that you don’t want to listen to it? I don’t blame you. Jones: Hey I never said that…..come on now. lemme hear man. LEMME F*CKIN HEAR IT! Sinc: Now now we all know his raps are unbearable so why possibly take a listen to it. Jones: NO LEMME HEAR IT!!!!!!!F*CK! F*CK! F*CK! F*CK! F*CKKKKKKK!! Sinc: Alright if you insist. Sinc looks at Trace with a wide smile across his face. Sinc: So buddy, you got a CD player or boom box laying around somewhere? This is going to be great! Trace nods and signals Sinc to wait a moment. Jones: HURRY THE F*CK UP F*CKER! Sinc: Hey Trace, you might want to hurry because sooner or later he is going to start getting super angry and then probably end up thinking he’s unstoppable and oh god…I don’t even want to THINK about what would happen after that. No Way Jose Muchacho. No way. Trace comes back with a mini silver boom box and sets it in front of Sinc. Sinc plugs it into a nearby wall socket and inserts the CD. Sinc: You sure you wanna hear this? Jones: YES F*CKER! F*CKKKKKKKKKK! Sinc: You asked for it man. Sinc plays the CD and it starts off with Sonny’s odd poem to Jekyl. QUOTE My life continues to follow, As I pay the ultimate price. I am now the World Champion, But I remain rolling the dice. I've done something spectacular, Which for once is a change. I've completed a hard mission, And it took a long range. Jekyl is not a human, He is what I call superficial. His skills are of a low standard, But that's already official. Now I wait for a challenge, Will one happen to appear? That is the grand question, Someone the crowd will cheer? I'm pulling out a Kanto, Something pretty weird. He may be a freak, But I am more feared. To you Jekyl I leave on this, You are nothing to me, I am the Main Man, Of the MWE! [/center] Sinc: MY GOD! Sinc raises his eyebrows in fear. He couldn’t believe how horrible that was. Sinc: That is the SINGLE most ear piercing thing I’ve heard! I never heard that recording! Suddenly Jones looks like he is about to bust a nut and go crazy. Trace makes a worried look. Trace: Sinc you might want to leave….like….now. Jones face is now red. He now stands up after Sinc backs away still in his sitting position a bit confused as to what was about to happen. Jones: WHAT THE FLYING F*CK IS THIS F*CKING BULL SH*T MUTHA F*CKA’S TRYIN TO GET ME GOIN! F*CK THIS!!!!!!! F*CK THIS! F*CK IT!!!!!!!F*CK F*CK F*CK! Jones then begins smashing the boom box with his fists and kicking it at the same time until it was forced out of the wall socket and broken into chips of metal and wire. He then picks it up and chucks it at the wall in frustration and rage. Jones: WHY YOU SHOWIN ME THIS SH*T DUMB F*CK!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! F*CK! F*CKER! Sinc then stands up and rushes out of the room. Trace enters the room after grabbing a sharp needle injector tipped with liquid. Trace: It was nice meeting you Mr. Mercier and being on TV and all. Trace looks at the camera in a quick rush and waves bye. Trace: BYE MOM! AND DON'T WORRY, I'LL BE HOME IN TIME FOR MARTHA STEWART'S THANKSGIVING SPECIAL! Trace then closes the door as Sinc just stands there dumbstruck raising a brow. Moaning is heard as well as banging and screams. And small murmurs were heard and it appeared to be saying “Calm down calm down. It’s gonna be all better soon…” Sinc: I think him and Sonny deserve each other. Hell they already got the room and room service, so why don’t those two crazies move in. What a turn of events. To find Sonny’s long lost chum here in Alexander Valley. Sinc shrugs and walks away from the door also walking off frame and whistles "What A Wonderful World" as the camera fades to black. -------------------------------------------------------- The scene fades back into real time with Sinc VIA Satellite. Sinc then responds still in his Australian voice and full of laughter. Sinc: Well mates this makes me giggle and all that junk but I really got to get goin here mates. I'll be a WALLABY'S uncle if I am going into THAT dangerous territory again. Until next time folks this concludes another WILD adventure.....in MWE history. The camera shuts off right as Sinc winks and begins standing up to leave the room.
