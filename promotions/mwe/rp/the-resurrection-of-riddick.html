The Resurrection Of Riddick; Relive The Days Of Faith

Jul 31 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
-Every man can falls, and every man can rise. Every man can stare into the light, and every man can stare into the darkness. Every man can live, and every man can die. In this life, in this world we live in we tend to forget that no matter what contrasts we can find between our lives and the lives of others, they do not define whether or not we’re human. You cannot measure a man by the luck in his soul and the fire in his eyes. You cannot judge a man by the color of his eyes or the number of siblings dead or the way a man grins at the thought of revenge with his twisted thoughts. A man can fall a thousand times down to the very bottom of the abyss however a man only has to rise once to regain his confidence and pride. At the beginning of a new day, the sun will rise and it will shine its power throughout the world. Jay Riddick walks in the hallway of the Thompson Boiling Arena in Knoxville Tennessee. The long corridor with no one to be seen and only echoes of the soles of Riddick’s shoes are to be heard. But in Riddick’s head, it isn’t shoe soles he’s hearing, it’s his soul. His soul gives a scenario into the vision of his mind, his footsteps down the corridor of an arena in his mind transforms into himself in an orange jumpsuit, handcuffed by his wrists. Instead of walking down the corridor into the locker rooms, his mind is projecting himself walking down to death row. Imagine that, making your way down a cold, dark, damp hall. The door at the end is the proverbial light of death as you essentially are walking to your direct place of death. Memories of everything that went wrong in your life, parts you wish you could go back and fix, but in the end, you wait to hear the door being pushed open. SNAP! Back to reality, Riddick walks to the end of the corridor and goes in to the door on the right, into the locker room. Riddick is in the room alone just like the corridor. It’s as if the arena had not one living soul besides Riddick to be found within its perimeters. All is seen is clean white walls with no imperfections, no scratches, no dents, clean wooden benches and the shower room. Riddick sets his bag down beside him in the middle of the room. Riddick looks around the room, without blinking to be made, without a breath to be wasted. This may appear to be a standard locker room, but in the mind of Riddick, it’s an execution room. The electric chair being ready to be used, any man sitting in it has two different fates, the fate of being electrocuted in the chair and dying in indignity, or to refuse to believe there is no hope and fighting your way out alive. Riddick has two choices, to either condemn himself for letting his fans down after his first loss against Joseph Sharpman, to die off into the darkness, never to be seen by a living eye again. Or he can take the path of repent and brush off his loss by coming back twice as strong continuing to fight to earn his spot. Riddick stands alone in the center of the locker room, no judges, no jurors, and no witnesses. Nobody in the room to influence his decision based on pressure, or based on fear. The only person in the room is someone who has to make a decision that will affect not only his career but his life. Riddick begins to speak, as if he’s speaking to the spirits in the room, or imagining people were there because he has a feeling that there may not be anyone in the room but he feels there are people listening hard enough that they’ll hear him, his fans around the world.- Riddick: People in the streets, people in stores, people that walk by me ask me if all of this is real. They ask me if what I do is real, they ask me if all my feelings, they ask me if everything I do is genuine. And the sad fact is I don’t tell them anything. The people that believe in me, the ones that ask me those questions I cannot answer. To many the answer may be obvious, but I cannot bring myself to even attempt to tell them whether or not it’s real. I walk away and ponder to myself... How can I tell them it’s real when the pain that has torn my body apart, the agony of my mental state, the deterioration of my will is beyond belief? I ask myself the same question every day, is this all real? Can any single living being receive this much punishment? -Riddick has his hands placed on his hips, looking up at almighty almost as if he was looking for an answer, a revelation. His prayers to god brought him no answers because for him to make a prayer is to search within himself. An act he isn’t ready to process quite yet. To search within yourself is a true action of inspiration, a true act of believing that you can find the answers that nobody else can give you, answers that surpass your measure of intellect and intelligence. Almost as if his soul within is someone who holds more knowledge, more power than he himself has. The connection between himself and his soul is a strong bond, wherever he’s been, whatever’s he’s done his soul has been there aswell. Except for the fact that his soul can do one thing he cannot. It can communicate with those who cannot be seen, those who cannot be seen with our perceptive eyes.- There are times where I wish I could simply leave this profession, this life, this world. The days I’ve suffered pain that didn’t have to happen. My body has been torn to pieces; it’s a challenge to make it out of the arena. -Riddick wipes a bead of sweat rolling down his brow, a bead of sweat that glistens by the projection of the lights within the room. The muscles in his eyes and forehead begin to twitch as he utilizes his brain muscles to find exactly what he needs to say.- Fighting and wrestling is something I love to do. Whether or not the fans liked me I made my way through each and every match I’ve been featured in, coming in and coming out gaining respect for the sport of professional wrestling. However I’ve been in fights that have beaten me down physically and mentally, torturing me, telling me to surrender. Some moments I wished I had surrendered, I wish I could give the title away and walk out with my bags packed never to return to the ring ever again. -Riddick is looking down at the ground during those words, as he cannot bare to face anyone directly at eye level to admit of his willingness to surrender his championship and his profession as a professional wrestler. For Riddick to surrender his position, to surrender anything that relates to his new profession, would be to set himself free of pain, free of pressure, free of fear by no longer being able to let others down. To live in harmony and in peace.- But I don’t. You’ve seen me come back each and every time ready to fight, ready for a new challenge. And I do it because of the people that are supporting me in the ring, the people that are asking me for autographs, and the people who cheer my name. Zak, you can bring up how unimpressive you believe my win/loss record is, you can call me a soon to be transitional champion, and you can bring up on how I was undeservingly handed the X-TV Championship. But you know what Zak? Your mind games you’re trying to play with me, it’s a waste of your time. -Riddick takes a big deep breath to relax not only his words but to refresh his memory, to be prepared to be able to say what he is willing to say next to really make his opponent, Zak Warner ponder of his words he directed towards Riddick. Little do we know, it isn’t mistakes that affect our lives. It isn’t mistakes that change the path we must take. What changes our lives, what makes us change paths is whether or not we choose to condemn ourselves and punish our selves by forfeiting. Those who do not condemn themselves for their misdeeds brush themselves off and continue.- You see Zak, all my career I’ve been telling my mind to shut up. My mind is what is telling me to submit or to keep myself from kicking out, but I’ve overcome what my mind is telling me. I’ve made mistakes like any other person Zak, and deep down inside Zak, if you search within yourself, I am sure you’ll find at least one big mistake you’ve made recently. But what makes me a champion is that I can take my mistakes and turn them to work in my favour. I know the mistakes you’ve made Zak, and it’s all because you underestimate the knowledge others have, the resources and power people have, and don’t lie Zak, whether or not that intimidates you, it’s the truth. -The room continues to be filled with the empty hollow echoes of Riddick’s words bouncing off the walls. Silence that truly defines how his words to his opponents have to be delivered. And echoes that will be heard throughout the ages.- I may be the cause for your next mistake Zak, maybe this match is the one you won’t walk out of, I may be the man to stop you dead in your tracks, and I may be the one that you’ve feared throughout your career. And why? Because I’m the one you think won’t be able to, I’m the one you think you know, but little do you know, I live to defy people like you, and that’s why I’m the champion and that’s why I am “The Uprising” Jay Riddick. Zak, ignore your mind and I can tell you what you’re true belief is, and I do not question your true beliefs. Zak, I know what you’re thinking, and I agree, I am unbelievable. -Riddick then looks towards the camera; perception of the room is filled with ivory walls full of the residue and stains of Riddick’s words still echoing silently among the entire room. Even climbing up the mountain has its rough patches, its jagged edges, its obstacles.- I’ve heard the people talk, I’ve read the dirt sheets, I’ve overheard conversations, and I can sense it within the powers that be in MWE. The fact is people believe that my time is up, that the uprising is over. Because of my last two matches not ending the way I’ve intended them to. Well how can I deny that fact? I am not denying anything, Kraven was the better man at Independance Day and Sharpman capitalized on a ref’s temporary loss of perception to our match and took advantage of it. Then I was handed the championship by Kraven. Well let me tell you something, Kraven handed me that championship because he believed I could be a great champion. He was seeing something in me that you doubters used to believe. But then I lost my match, and all of a sudden alot of my believers gave up on me. So does that mean I’m going to lay down and die? A saviour once came into this world with the belief and power we could not believe. But when that saviour started to lose his believers he faced the punishment of being crucified. Then, when nobody believed in him anymore, when they all thought his soul was perished and when everybody thought he was done for good, he resurrected from the dead to send his message to the world. -Riddick soul searches within himself, that action, that process he needed to do within himself to find the answers he is looking for not only to answer to his fans, not just to himself but an answer everybody must hear. The saviour, the son of the lord once came into our world to project a message and so must Riddick for the sake of the people.- And the message is to believe again, to believe in the unbelievable, to believe in defiance, and to believe in those who demonstrate power that we cannot understand... Jesus Cruz, you’re one of those people that lost their faith in me. Even within your soul of arrogance and our cultural differences you believed in me. And you will have to worry no more, because this week is my resurrection. Cruz, your sense of judgement is blinded by your arrogance and sense of superiority. When I started here in MWE I was exactly like you, a minority in a world where you have to fight to gain every bit of respect. But what’s to stop you from suffering defeat by the hands of someone who’s more experienced than you? You’re a living shadow of me, living under my former characteristics, and one day, maybe even in our match, a revelation will strike you like nothing you’ve ever felt before. A revelation that will change your ways and open your eyes to who is real and who is not. Cruz, I am the one man you can assure is real. My abilities, my personality, my championship status, I am as real as belief can trust. This is your 2nd match Cruz, your beliefs have yet to develop, and you lack faith within those who truly earned it. You’re no different than each and every person that steps into that ring as a rookie, and those who survive are those who are able to put themselves in a situation where they can defy the beliefs of others. I’ve done it Cruz, I’ve been there and I’ve done that. I’ve earned this championship because I fought no matter who stood in front of me not sure if I’d come out victorious, but I gave everything I had each and every time and what I got I earned. -Even though it’s not seen by perception of others, it’s almost as if a light from above has shined onto Riddick, a light that can communicate a light that can speak and convey messages to Riddick. Riddick looks above. Above the ceiling, above the building, and above the sky as he truly becomes enlightened. The voices are speaking to him, and he understands, he understands the answers, he understands fate and he understands what will happen.- Cruz, my belief and my faith tells me that you’ll soon receive the message and revelations I’ve received, and whether you receive it after I beat you down or whether you decide to smarten up and remember who you’re talking to you will receive that revelation that you will need to be somebody in MWE. Believe me or don’t believe in me, I will not stop until I’ve proven to each and every person in the world that to believe in me is to believe in each and everyone of you. To believe you can fly, to believe you possess the power, to believe that we as people can amount to anything as long as we pay the price. Resurrect your beliefs, Resurrect your faith, Resurrect your powers, This message is the beginning of a new day, a new time, and a new era. This message is only the beginning of the Resurrection of Riddick. -Riddick still possesses the voices, still possesses the mental instability that he had when he first came into MWE and since he first came to become the person he is today, Jay Riddick. However those voices turned from being demonic and destructive to voices of enlightened and power. Perception has always been defined as the way living people see you as. Not only the things we see but the things we believe. And perception can either cause your success or cause your demise; however the way to overcome the belief that others do not perceive you as a real entity in this world is by silencing what your mind tells you. Your mind wants to direct your life, it wants to lead you. In temptation it’ll tell you to forfeit, in times of suffering it’ll tell you to simply give up. It doesn’t believe you can handle the pain, it doesn’t believe you can become a champion. Riddick has overcome the words his mind projects to him, he’s overcome the perception of others because he has a guidance within himself. His guidance is his belief and he leads his belief to conform to anything he wants. By witnessing Riddick these last couple of weeks, your mind may be telling you he’s nothing, telling you he’s not real, he’s not a champion, he’s all talk. Well it’s time to listen to Riddick by silencing that voice that is our minds and searching within ourselves to truly see how we perceive the champion, Jay Riddick.- Zak, Cruz, do what you must. Unleash everything you have, bring everything you possess and try and bring me down. Bring your lashes, bring your fire, bring you greatest weapons and I will rise to take it all, everything you’ve got. I tell you this because I am the champion and I will not lay down and die, if you two want to destroy me, if you want this to be my last match than I dare you to attempt so, because my faith tells me that no matter what you two bring, no matter what you two are capable of doing, I will come back stronger than anyone can expect and I will stand up with my scars and blood all over my body ready to fight another day! And when it’s all said and done, it won’t simply be you two that have changed your perception of me, it’ll be every single former believer of mine that will find faith within themselves to believe in my once again, and I will hold that up high in victory, a prize more valuable to me than my championship and I will wear the belief of all you people in honour. You will believe in me once again! -Riddick speaks to everyone witnessing him while he’s in a room all alone. However I believe his message will be conveyed as the message of his soul will reach to the hearts and souls of others. This match is beyond the point of glory or victory, it’s for something Riddick holds dear to himself, it’s for belief. However as Riddick stands in the room where the walls are no longer covered in the bleeding faith as his faith has been restored, where the lights have become brighter than ever, his cell starts to go off, and as he pulls it out of his left pocket to see what the message is, the message says... One day Riddick, one day. Alexa
