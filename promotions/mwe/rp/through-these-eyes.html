Through these eyes; A David Whitman RP

Nov 30 2008

David Whitman

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The camera slowly opens up on an atmospherically deprived and dark room, where a barely discernable figure stands alone in the middle of the enclosed space. The camera continues fading in, slowly. Just when one would expect the camera to stop fading in, it continues. The room gets brighter and brighter. A blinding flash envelops the entire screen, and now all that is visible is a dazzling shade of white. A familiar voice starts talking as the sound of what appears to be a heartbeat mixed with a heart monitor slowly starts accompanying it in the background. “The one man I could never defeat. The one man who took everything away from me. The one and only Brian Michaels. A picture of Brian Michaels flashes across the screen briefly. “The one thing that means the world to me. The one thing that separates me from some asylum. The MWE Title. The title flashes across the screen for a second. “This Monday night, I’ll be facing you yet again, Michaels. But this time, it will all be different.” The camera slowly fades in a bit and shows what appears to be David Whitman standing there with the belt hung over his shoulder, but the vision is blurry. The white background remains. “You can talk all the trash you want. You can change stipulations, you can cheat- but this time, the winner will be David Whitman. Bec- ok, look, this is bullsh*t.” CUT! The camera suddenly cuts to black. ------------------ Funniest thing I’ve ever been a part of? A David Whitman promo shoot. The man himself standing in front of a green screen, dressed in his signature long black pants and a white t-shirt with the MWE title slung over his right shoulder. Looking like a force to be reckoned with. ------------------ “I CAN’T DO THIS!” “But the contract- I mean i-” ------------------ His ideals- or whatever, his mindset, call it what you want, but something keeps holding him back from finishing the promo. He gets into it for a bit but then somehow it falls apart. And I can’t stop laughing. Don’t get me wrong, I was honored to be there. Even though there were a ton of people present- and I got no idea how they all even fit into that warehouse- I felt like I was witnessing something grand. Majestic even. Ok, that was overselling it. But the whole thing really DID feel- I dunno, special. Call me a hopeless fan, but I looked up to the guy. He was what got me into wrestling. Sure, I was just an amateur- but I’d wrestled. I knew what it was like in that ring. Some people even said I had what it takes to get far. My history was considered “amazing for a non-professional.” Dunno how I feel about that, though. I mean yeah, I’ve had my fair share of matches, but this guy… this guy is the king. The unwanted King in a land of predators, each one waiting to pounce and take away that which he has worked so hard for. Case in point; the upcoming MNM. Brian Michaels has a shot at the title. And the chances that he has at winning are… let’s just say they’re not nothing. But David Whitman’s chances are better than they would have been in the past. And I would know. I’ve been following MWE, as well as Top Rope Tavern for a while now. Heck, when I got word that I had won the contest and would get a chance to spend a week with the cast of the Top Rope Tavern during the pre-production stage of their final season one episode… I went nuts. I had finally gained an opportunity to A.) hang out with TV stars, B.) hang out with some of my favorite wrestlers, and C.) hang out with people that actually lived just like me; wrestle, drink, talk crap. It was a dream-come-true. The flight to Arizona was fantastic- FIRST CLASS! There was even a picture of David Whitman in there- along with several other superstars. I kept getting nervous ‘cause I kept thinking about the connections- David Whitman’s picture was in there, me winning the contest was special, David’s special move was the Final Flight- Yes, I was scared the plane was going to crash. Lame. I know. Anyway, MOVING ON! Upon my arrival at the airport I was greeted by none other than David Whitman- along with the entire TRT gang. Chants of “Hey kid!”, “Who’s the new guy?!”, “Is this even legal” and “Seriously, I can’t find my toothbrush” surrounded me as I was half-hugged, patted on the back and even playfully slapped. During the limo ride a lot of dialog took place, which I wasn’t really expecting- Whitman and his friends talk a lot. The subject frequently turned to “the boss’s son”, Brian Michaels. That was the first time I got a whiff of Whitman’s insecurity- and it caught me off-guard. Big time. The one sentence that started my doubts about him? “I don’t know if I can beat him.” I mean come on. An MWE champion should never think thoughts like this. But I digress. We drove all around New York, talking all the way. We talked until my ears hurt. I have to admit, it was pretty fun. ------------------ David: Dude, seriously. You have no idea what you’re talking about. David tosses a chip at a laughing Kate, who dodges it as she playfully tosses a chip back at him. Kate: I MEAN IT! Look, I’m just saying that when the boss’s son gets a title shot against you from out of nowhere, things can’t possibly end well! Kate starts laughing louder in her even girlier than usual laughing voice as James and Thomas hold David back. David’s expression turns to one of faux-seriousness as he turns to the chuckling 20-something year old in the corner of the seat across him. David: You think there’s foul play involved, kid? The kid, without missing a beat, answers David. The kid: You’re DAVID WHITMAN! You beat the best of the best including names like Williams, D.C., Rodriguez and all that! You’re gonna kick Michaels’ @$$! Everyone in the limo gets a serious look on their face as they turn to each other, one by one. James bursts into laughter. Eddie & Scott abruptly start chanting “FAN-BOY! FAN-BOY!” as Kate starts giggling loudly and David just smiles. James: What are you, kid; 10?! ------------------ I learned that David was excited to wrestle at the Times Union Center again, where he had only wrestled once before- and lost. I thought he was excited because he would get a chance to redeem himself, but apparently that was not the case, as “the winning isn’t as important as you’d think it is.” Another minus-point, as I slowly started to realize some things about the man I once looked up to. Upon arriving at the Times Union Center, we just got out of the limo and hung out right there in the parking lot. It was… odd, to say the least. ------------------ Eddie: You guys, this is like, the worst tailgate ever. Scott & Thomas: TAILGATE! Scott and Thomas both open their tall-boy cans of beers and try to down them as fast as humanly possible. James: Hey, David, it’s like 7 or something. David nods, takes a small black pouch from his pants pocket and drops a couple of pills from it into his hand. He strokes them lightly with his thumb as he concentrates on them. He then swallows them with some of his beer as the kid looks at him a bit surprised. All of a sudden, David starts shaking his head violently. He falls to his knees, still shaking, as the kid panics. The kid: SOMEBODY CALL 911! Abruptly, David drops completely to the ground as he begins laughing loudly. He is joined in by the others. Well, all of them except for the kid. James: YOU GOT PUNKED, KID! David gets up slowly, still chuckling. David: But seriously, though, that’s my medication. ------------------ I found out he takes SSRIs, and will continue doing so for at least a month. While I was still thinking about what it all meant, we made our way into the arena. After checking out almost every nook and cranny in the place, we hung out some more, this time in some of the seats surrounding the half-set up ring. Sitting there felt surreal- even though I felt as if I was changing. Mentally, I didn’t know if I could walk into Monday Night Mayhem and cheer David Whitman on. ------------------ David casually tosses his feet up and kicks back as Scott and Thomas get into the half-finished ring and start trying to set the ropes up in the most insane way they can think of. The kid, who is sitting behind Eddie, James and David, turns to Kate, who is sitting right next to him. The kid: Is he always like this? Kate: Wh- huh? Like what? Kate seems very confused. The kid: I don’t know… he seems… I dunno. Unconfident? Unfocused? Not ready. I guess what I’m getting at… is that I’m not sure I think he should be the champion. Kate now looks visibly distressed. Kate: Listen, I know it might not seem like it, but you trust me, that man has more going on in his mind right now than all of us here combined. The kid is taken aback. Meanwhile, Scott and Thomas, who have made an X in the ring with the ring ropes, get back to the others. Scott: We should leave. ------------------ After our little excursion at the arena, we went to the St. Regis hotel where we’d be staying for the week. We drank and chatted for a while in the main living room area of the giant suite before I excused myself to go check out my room. I sat in my separated bedroom, thinking for about an hour. Putting everything together. Coming up with a conclusion... Since I got into wrestling, I had always had the utmost respect for David Whitman. I thought he was strong. Courageous. untouchable even. When he lost his string of matches to Michaels in the first incarnation of MWE, I thought he was just having a stroke of bad luck. I mean, since his return he’s been on fire. Surely, he’d finally beat Michaels this time, right? Then I thought some more. About how insecure he is. His mental “issues”. Does he even care about his fans? He has to, I thought. He is the unstoppable force I’ve always known him to be… But now I know better. David Whitman is not the man I looked up to. He is a man who would let his principles get in the way of everything else. He is a man who would rather depend on pills for his “psychological problems” than be strong and break them. He is soft on the inside. David Whitman does not care about anything. ------------------ Hey man, what you writing there? Oh, uh, just finishing up this chapter of my book. David pats the would-be writer’s shoulder with a look of confidence and joy on his face. David: Awesome. Keep at it! David leaves the room as the jaded ex-fan sits silently in the wooden chair, staring at the paper in front of him disappointedly. His slightly angry face turns to one of contemplation as he slowly takes his pen, and scratches through the last few lines on the paper. All of a sudden, he crushes the paper up into a ball and tosses it in the direction of the trashcan, nearly missing it. He gets a slight smile on his face as he gets up and leaves the room.
