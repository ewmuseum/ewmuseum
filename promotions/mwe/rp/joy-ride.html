Joy Ride; RP 2

Sep 28 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
We continue our little adventure in the MWE limo Where Jekyl is enjoying himself to an extent, he has not been one so used to such luxuries, such hospitality, but when he can he will enjoy it. Jekyl sits in the back of the black limo...black, MWE logo on he side, chrome trim. The inside has white leather upholstery, a bar on the right side and a cigar rack along with many other accessories on the left. The chauffeur opens up the dividing window and leans back, he is a younger man about the age of 26, clean shaven, black hat and black suit with the MWE logo on the right breast pocket. Chauffeur: Where we going sir? Jekyl: Let’s just take a spin around the city, I want to see what I have to work with. Chauffeur: Yes sir. The limo pulls out from the parking lot and begins to drive around the town, the sky turns pink, the air is calm, the transition between day and night is almost at an end as the sunset just beyond the horizon...Jekyl’s favorite time of day... Jekyl: David, you have done great things in MWE, 2 time world champion, and in HQW you were the US Champion...These are great feats indeed, but alas, all results have reasoning, and you sir have a dark deep truth behind your reasoning. Looking into our biography and history, I learned of your motivation...I learned your weakness.... Jekyl kind of walks low towards the limo’s divider window which is still open. As he sticks his head through the driver notices and gets startled, sending him swerving on the road a bit, luckily there is no traffic. Jekyl looks around the hole and decides to climb through. The driver confused asks him what he is doing, his face frightened yet concerned at the same time. Chauffeur: What are you doing? Jekyl looks at the driver and smiles, the scenery behind him zooms by, the sun now set, streetlights in the distance flicker as the closer unseen trees hidden by the darkness flash by them causing this illusion. Jekyl: I feel uncomfortable back there, I much rather prefer it up here, it adds a certain... fly out the window feeling to it. Let me demonstrate what I mean... Jekyl sets the camera on the dashboard facing the passenger window, the rear view mirror shows there is no traffic directly behind them. A click is heard as the drivers seatbelt is undone, a shuffle is heard as the car begins to swerve all over the place, it’s a wonder why the camera hasn’t moved from its spot of placement. Chauffeur: WHAT ARE YOU DOING?!?!? ARE YOU INSANE?!?!......NOOOOOoooooooo!!!! Another click...Rushing wind is heard as the driver door is opened, a scream and a thud. In the side view mirror we see the man rolling on the road as the car drives on. Jekyl takes over the driver seat and begins to drive. Jekyl: Like I ever drove before... Jekyl makes a smacking sound with his mouth and starts to laugh maniacally Jekyl: I love my job....heh ha ha ha ha ha ha ha ha Jekyl leans out the window as he continues to drive, a smile on his face, wind blowing through his hair, he licks his lips as the blowing wind has dried his mouth, enjoying the drive he gets back in the car and begins to drive around until he comes up to a hill overlooking the Kanawha river, the limo stops near the edge of a cliff, the engine stops but the lights remain on. Jekyl steps out and goes to the back door in search of something. He exits the back with a cigar and a 40oz bottle of Jack Daniels. Jekyl: Now to continue my little “rant” about Whitman.... You have basically been living in your cousin’s shadow until you came to MWE, and here you did this to try and become better independently without the use of your brother’s name, yet in EVERY promo you have EVER done.... James has been the backbone, he has been your support, your comic relief if I may say...You can't do anything on your own, can you? Jekyl walks along the side the limo to the front where he hops on the front of the hood. The engine has kept it warm and comfortable to sit on but the night breeze is chilly and causes a tingle to creep up Jekyl’s spine. Jekyl: Lets look at James’ record shall we? Jekyl opens the bottle of Jack and begins to pour half a bottle out...then takes a swig. Jekyl: WcE Xtreme Heavyweight Champion, WcE Intercontinental Heavyweight Champion, WcE European Heavyweight Champion, and WcE World Heavyweight Champion. These are all the titles that your cousin has won in the past, you have only won 2 and held one of them for 2 separate months.....This is where your goals and dreams crash and burn. I am going to stop you from ever getting that title shot, keeping you in the shadow of your cousin where you belong. A sudden flash of light quickly illuminates Jekyl’s face s he lights the cigar. A splash is heard as he pours out another bit of Jack Daniels, finishing off the last of the Whiskey himself. Jekyl: This whiskey has represented your wrestling career, the bottle itself represents you, as every bit I poured has represented all the accomplishments your cousin has that you don’t, where he has succeeded and you haven’t. The portion that I drank represents your upcoming loss to me, disgracing the Whitman name as a wrestler, which leaves us with an empty bottle. This empty bottle represents you after I beat you this week, it represents that after this match you will degrade to nothing, you will wallow in the shadow of your cousin in the world renown gay bar, “Top Rope Tavern”. Jekyl lays back on the windshield of the limo as he stares up at the stars above, his breath lingers in the air as its turned to steam, the cool breeze brushes over him, the trees near and far rustle in the wind, the noise of a deserted night. Jekyl: I remember in your first promo this week that you were trying to disprove my theory of the “Top Rope Tavern” being a gay bar by bringing up old episodes from your web show.... Those women that you had on your show were probably lesbian and well payed to be there.... the joys of being an actor... Right David? Jekyl gets up and looks out on the Kanawha river where the water flows peacefully, city lights nearby reflect off the calm current. Jekyl chuckles to himself as he looks at the bottle of JD and without and form of hesitation grabs it by the neck and smashes it against the hood of the limo sending broken glass flying everywhere...Jekyl tosses the dismembered neck of the bottle... Jekyl: Just like that bottle, I will smash you into tiny pieces, I will send you crawling back to the shadows......Shit, I’m gonna need to get that fixed......Covell isn’t gonna like the fact that I pushed his driver out of a moving car, but a dent and scratches all over the hood? I have an idea.... but it will have to wait until tomorrow... Jekyl walks back around the limo and crawls in the back area. Jekyl: Nite! Ha ha ha ha ha........... *******************************Next Day********************************** It’s early morning outside a car garage as we see Jekyl waiting outside pacing around waiting for something. Wearing his usual purple outfit, Jekyl stops as a man dressed in a dark blueish grey work suit comes out of the main office, hat backwards, scruffy facial hair, purple paint around his face. Mechanic: Ready? Jekyl: Of course I am... I haven’t been waiting around to ask you the time....SHOW ME! The mechanic steps back shocked at Jekyl’s remarks, Jekyl looking at the mechanic with squinted eyes, thinking of the pain he could inflict on this guy while planning on something instead. The mechanic wastes no more time and opens up the garage to reveal the limo, except now its purple with deep purple flames on the side with a green pinstripe outline, chrome linings, and “Jekyl” written on the side near the rear passenger door in a gruesome font. Jekyl: Beautiful, you did a great job....Can you take a picture of me inside it with this disposable camera? Mechanic: Whatever.... Jekyl hands over a disposable Kodak camera, slips into the drivers seat as the mechanic steps back to take the picture. Jekyl: Thanks....Keep the camera! With an even more confused look the mechanic rolls up the winder for the camera ready to take the picture, then realization sets in. Before he gets a chance to stop him Jekyl speeds off into the distance. The mechanic now pissed off cause Jekyl only payed the first half of the money throws the camera at the ground causing it to shatter.... The camera shows Jekyl inside driving the limo as he glances at the camera with a twisted smile on his face. Jekyl: What, I knew he was gonna get mad so I gave him a disposable camera to break... besides, Covell should like the change I made to his limo.... HA HA HA HA haa heeeh hooo...... -FADE-
