The helicopter has come to rest in the middle of the intersection as D.C. is wheeled towards it. He opens his eyes, looking out the corner of them... not wanting to see as he spots the only familiar faces in the crowd. Eli Silver, Paige, Ace Cannon... and her... it's Lindsey. She looks like a woman defeated by the hands of mercy. He stares at the group. As if everyone beside them has all but disappeared as the move closer to the chopper. D.C. feels the gurney bang into the side, as it is being ready to be lifted. The medics begin their count in unison, up to three, to lift and remove D.C. from the scene. Lindsey turns her head with her hands upon her face. As Cannon, Paige, and Eli continue to watch, un-evaded by Lindsey's reaction. They glare, like Meerkats watching for trouble. D.C. watches them back, answering their questioning eyes with a gesture... it stands in the sun light as the blades pass shadows across it in rapid sessions. It's an ominous tower of hate and passion. A symbol that is unfamiliar in this kind of scene... It's a middle finger... and its directed at them... He shuts his eyes... cold... The mighty, hath fallen... and now resides in blackness.
