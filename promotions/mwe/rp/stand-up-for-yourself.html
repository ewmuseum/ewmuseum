Stand up for yourself

Oct 19 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Devlin Sharpe is about to walk in to his locker room suddenly he hears Ryan and Hayley Talking so he stops Ryan Sharpe: Oh you just know I am better than Devlin Hayley Williams: I didn't think you were before but now... Hayley pauses while in thought then with a tone of uncertainty Hayley Williams: Yeah I guess you are right... Devlin Sharpe suddenly bursts in to the room, glances at Hayley Williams and stares through Ryan Sharpe Devlin Sharpe: What the hell...bro He spits out every word, pauses and delivers the last word in a venomous tone Ryan Sharpe: Jealous? Ryan sharpe smirks at Devlin Sharpe Devlin Sharpe: You know that I like Hayley a lot and you try to steal her away from me? Ryan Sharpe: You are just pissed off because she pays more attention to me than she does to you Ryan sharpe says with a tone of arrogance that infuriates Devlin Sharpe, although Devlin is trying not to show it, it begins to crack through the surface which worries Hayley Hayley Williams: Dev... Devlin Sharpe: You keep quiet, I am dealing with Ryan first, I will deal with you later... Hayley tries to sound apologetic however Devlin cuts her off Devlin Sharpe: Well bro...first of all why did you do it...this better be a good excuse suddenly Ryan Sharpe's ego deflates as he becomes apologetic Ryan Sharpe: Man to be honest, I really liked her even before you but I couldnt bring myself to ask her out and when I finally managed to bring up the courage you had already asked her Devlin Sharpe looks like he genuinely cares for a second however his facial expression changes Devlin Sharpe: That is the...BIGGEST PILE OF BULLSHIT I HAVE EVER HEARD Ryan Sharpe: So you finally decide to stand up for yourself eh? Ryan gets up and approaches Devlin, then walks circles around him Ryan Sharpe: Ever since we were little you kept being our slave always trying to keep us happy Devlin becomes nervous Ryan Sharpe: Sure you worked the hardest and made the most money but you were always willing to give it to us because we said we would be nice to you... Devlin Sharpe: Please... Devlin begins to shake Ryan Sharpe: You always needed attention, our parents never cared for you so you wanted attention you would do anything for attention, get good grades, earn a lot of money, buy people stuff just so people would notice you, but in case you haven't noticed dear bro, people have been using you because of that... Devlin Sharpe: Stop please Tears begin to well up in Devlin's eyes Ryan Sharpe: Even joining the MWE, you only did it for the attention, you don't care if you win or lose you just want people to like you, here's the newsflash Devlin...no one does Ryan lowers his voice and spits out each word of the last phrase in to his face, Devlin flinches then suddenly loses control and knocks down Ryan with a clothesline which causes Hayley to yelp Devlin turns around not facing Ryan or Hayley Devlin Sharpe: You are right, I do want attention...I never get enough I WANT people to notice me, that is why I do what I do, that is what makes me what I am, these choices are what shape my life Ryan still on the floor, speaks as a wise older brother Ryan Sharpe: That's what i've been trying to teach you all this time and by god I think it's finally sunk in, stand up to people, if you think something is right, fight to prove it, do not just lay down or do whatever people tell you just because you want them happy, your decisions are your decisions and if they are unhappy fuck them, they shouldn't decide how you think, it should be entirely your choice Devlin Sharpe: Yeah... Devlin Sharpe thinks for a moment over everything his brother just told him Ryan Sharpe: Oh and if you want people to notice you, you might wanna wear some gold around your waist, I am talking TAG TEAM GOLD Ryan puts emphasis on the last 3 words Devlin Sharpe turns around, grabs ryans hand and pulls him up, but doesn't let go of his hand Devlin Sharpe: Sure thing...bro Devlin Sharpe begins his sentence seriously but softens up at the last word in contrast to the beginning of the conversation
