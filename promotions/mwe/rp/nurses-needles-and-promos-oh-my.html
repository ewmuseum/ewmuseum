Nurses, needles, and promos...OH MY!!!; Zack Crash PPV RP

Jul 1 2009

Zack Crash

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Morning. It’s probably the most dreaded part of any person’s day. And who can blame them. Let’s be honest, who in their right mind enjoys rolling out of bed, their comfortable, dreamy sleep disrupted by an annoying alarm clock, morning breath so bad it could wilt flowers, and grit covering your bloodshot eyes? Not a very inviting prospect at all. However, Zack Crash isn’t like other people. Our scene opens up in the early morning hours of Charleston, WV. The sun is just starting to rise, casting its orange-reddish rays over the clouds. While others are still dazed and confused, Zack Crash is already wide-awake, bursting with energy. He has just gotten out of a hot shower, a wet towel wrapped tightly around his waist (cause we don’t want to have an embarrassing situation while filming), flexing for himself in front of a full body mirror in a very Hulk Hoganish sorta way. Zack: Oh yeah, work it baby! Who is that sexy beast in the mirror? OH! It’s just me. He doesn’t seem to notice the camera filming him, and if he did, he probably wouldn’t care anyway. After a few more minutes of posing, there is a loud banging off screen at Zack’s front door. The sound vibrates down the hall like a gunshot, and Zack immediately knows who it is. Only one person he knows knocks like that. Rushing to the door, he wrenches it open to find his best friend Kent Bishop standing in the doorway. Zack throws his arms wide, inviting him in for a hug. But Kent just scowls at him. Kent: Put your rapid ass arms down! What the hell are you calling me so damn early in the morning for? Kent brushes past Zack, who closes the door behind him, unfazed by his friend’s reaction. Kent is a very average looking man. Average height, weight, looks, everything. In a crowd, you most likely wouldn’t even be able to point him out. He’s THAT average. The only noticeable thing about him is the hard expression lining his jaw, which seems forever etched into his face. Kent: Dude, it’s 7:30 in the frickin’ morning! You know I like to sleep in. Couldn’t you have called me at like, I don’t know, around 4:00 or something? Kent sits down at the kitchen table, accepting a hot, freshly brewed mug of coffee from Zack. Zack is still unfazed by Kent’s less then friendly attitude. The two men have known each other for many years, and while odd to some, this is just the nature of their relationship. Zack: Come on man, look outside. The sun is bright, the birds are singing. It’s a brand new day! How can you sleep at a time like this? Kent doesn’t answer. He is taking a long moment to take in Zack’s appearance, only to notice the only thing Zack is wearing is a wet bath towel. Kent: Will you PLEASE put some pants on! Zack looks down at his waist and laughs it off before walking down the hall and off screen to change. There is a long, awkward silence as the camera focuses in on Kent. He stares at the camera, an eyebrow raised and a puzzled expression. Kent: Uh, Zack…what’s with the camera crew? Zack: Oh don’t mind him. MWE sends them around every so often to follow us for the day. Zack calls from off screen. Kent’s eyebrow rises even higher; so high it nearly touches his hair. He looks as if it’s the most absurd idea he’s ever heard in his life. Kent: They send a cameraman to follow you? All day? Zack: Yeper. Zack answers simply, as though it means nothing. Kent takes a sip from his mug, looking exasperated. Bored, he begins sorting through a pile of mail on the table. He sorts through until one specific letter catches his attention. Looking at it intently, the camera closes in to see an MWE logo in the upper left corner. Zack: Anyway, I was thinking we could hang out today. They got that new skating rink over by the aquatic center finally finished. That looks pretty cool. I’ve been meaning to try that place out. Zack returns into the frame dressed in a pair of kaki shorts and a black muscle shirt. Kent: Hey man, you know you just got a letter from MWE right? From Zack’s reaction, it appears that this is news to him. He walks over and takes the letter from Kent, opening it up carefully and pulling a folded paper from inside. Unfolding it, he reads aloud. Zack: Dear Mr. Crash, First of all, we would like to congratulate you on your first victory in MWE. However, we regret to inform you that due to the rushed processing of your contract, we neglected to inform you that all new MWE wrestlers are required to go through a mandatory physical examination. This examination is to see that you are in healthy enough condition to wrestle, as well as to make sure that you have not violated our Drug Wellness Policy. It is essential to ensure both your physical health, and our legal protection. Before you can continue to wrestle for us, you need to have this examination as soon as possible. There is a MWE approved doctor in that area. Please see him and send us the appropriate paper work as soon as possible. His name and address are included in the envelope. We hope the above issue will soon be rectified. Signed, The MWE Board Of Health Administrators. Silence. So profound you can taste it. And it doesn’t taste very good. Zack’s face drops, the normal, happy expression replaced by one of grimness as he stares at the forbidden word. “Doctor” Kent: Well that doesn’t sound too bad. Zack looks at Kent with total indignation, as if he can’t believe what he just heard. Zack: NOT TOO BAD?! They want me to go to the doctors. THE DOCTORS!!! Kent rolls his eyes. Having known Zack for many years, he has gotten used to his many eccentricities. But for a man who enjoys jumping out of planes and off cliffs, he still finds it sad that the Ultimate Thrill Seeker’s biggest fear could possibly be something as childish as doctors. Kent: Oh don’t be such a baby. Just suck it up and go get it over with. Zack: But, the needles…and the blood…and the, uh- Zack looks torn, trying desperately to come up with any other options he might have. After a few seconds babbling, he sighs in surrender. Apparently, he has none. Zack: Fine, I’ll go. But you’re coming with me right? Kent: Sure I will…when my balls grow wings and fly away. Forget it! This is your problem man. As for me, I’m going back to bed. Kent makes for the door, but Zack, being as fast as he is, leaps in front of Kent and holds him by the arm. Zack: PLEASE? Pretty PLEASE? Pretty PLEASE with sugar on top, and a side of fries, and a free dental plan, and- Kent: ALRIGHT! Just shut up already! Geez, sometimes I wonder why I hang out with you. Zack: Because if you didn’t you know you’d be alone. Zack smiles triumphantly at Kent. He knows better then to take his words seriously. Despite how it may appear, the two of them are really close. Kent just looks over his shoulder as he walks past Zack out the door and to his car. Kent: Get in the car before I change my mind. Zack: Ok. But if they ask for a stool sample, I’m bolting. ....................................................................................................................................................................... Several minutes later, we reopen the scene and find the boys sitting in an examination room of a local hospital in downtown Charleston. It’s about the most uncomfortable room one can be in. It’s unnaturally cold inside, making Zack regret his decision to wear a muscle shirt and shorts as he shivers on the exam table. The room is so plain and dull it’s almost painful to look at, and there is a very heavy “doctor’s office” stench in the air. While Zack sits swinging his legs on the exam table, Kent sits off in a corner, bored as he randomly shifts through an old copy of People Magazine. After many agonizing minutes that feel like hours, and door swings open. Nurse: Hello. You must be Mr. Crash. To call this nurse hot would have been the ultimate of understatements. Her fine, shoulder length raven colored hair looks like it’s made of fine silk. Her skin looks soft and smooth. Her uniform’s so tight you can read the lining of her underwear. It reads: “Tuesday.” Funny thing is, it’s Thursday. Nurse: So, I’m here to check you in before the doctor sees you. So if you don’t mind I’ll just take your blood pressure. Her voice is like a chorus of angels as she straps the device to his lower arm and begins to pump. Zack is completely dumbfounded for words, mesmerized by her beauty. He chances a quick glance to his right and sees Kent starring at her with equal interest. The two of them met eye to eye, and Kent nods his head in silent agreement. Apparently, they had a winner. Zack: So… Zack began with a swagger in his tone, making his move. Zack: …what’s your name? Nurse: It’s Emily. –giggle– That giggle was so frickin’ adorable. It makes Zack want to just eat her up. Looking at Kent, who smiles at him and gives him the thumbs up in support. Zack decides it’s time to impress her. Zack: You know I actually wrestle for a living. Maybe you heard of me. I’m Zack Crash. You know, MWE? The Ultimate Thrill Seeker? Defeated Blight my first night? big match coming up this Sunday? Zack mentions of his few credentials, hoping to illicit a favorable reaction. Nurse Emily looks up at Zack a moment in mild interest, then turns her attention back to taking his blood pressure. Nurse Emily: Sorry, but I’m not really a wrestling fan. Zack is stunted into frozen silence. He doesn’t move for several moments, but finally is able to gather himself and try again with Plan B. Because when you’re trying to pick up chicks, you always need to have a Plan B. Zack: So…what kinda movies do you like? He seems to have struck gold. Nurse Emily looks up eagerly at him, enjoying the change of subject. Zack turns smugly toward Kent, who is nodding. Nurse Emily: Oh I love movies. My favorite movie is probably High School Musical. Kent snorts loudly, which he turns into a cough. Luckily, Nurse Emily doesn’t catch on. Zack just puts on a very forced smile, speaking through gritted teeth. Zack: Really? Me…too. Zack speaks this last part with great difficulty. Just those two words cause him a great deal of inner shame and pain; to go against his creed on the one film he has been adamantly against for so long. Kent: No you don’t. Just last time you said– Zack: Shut up you! Don’t listen to him. I just love, love, LOVE High School Musical. –mumbling– Especially if they have poor acting and aren’t even a musical. Nurse Emily: REALLY?! Oh that’s so cool. You know, most guys I meet say they hate the movie. But I don’t know, I just find it so cute. Hey, what’s your favorite song? Zack looks pleadingly at Kent, who just smiles and waves, clearly enjoying watching his friend squirm for his spoils. Zack: Um, you know…that one song. The song where they…uh… Nurse Emily: Oh, you mean the ending song. Zack: Yeah! Let’s go with that. Nurse Emily: I know. It’s a great song. It’s a very painful sight. Nurse Emily begins to talk nonstop about HSM with Zack, who is forced to agree with her on every positive statement she makes about the movie. Kent is in near tears, trying hard to keep his jaw tightly shut and his laughter contained. Having endured enough, Zack goes in for the kill. Zack: So, you wanna go out sometime? Nurse Emily: I’d love to. Zack punches the air in triumph. Nurse Emily: But I have a boyfriend. Sorry. Ouch! Denied! The words have all the effects of a dropping bomb, which are clearly readable on Zack’s blank expression. Finishing with his check-in, Nurse Emily leaves the room, muttering to herself about HSM. With her gone, Kent burst out in laughter, clutching his stomach. Kent: DUDE!!! THAT WAS SO –laughter– PATHETIC!!! Zack scowls at Kent for a moment before sighing deeply. He slaps himself on the cheek a few times, punishment for ever agreeing to like such a film, then smiles his usual smile. Zack: Ehh, she wasn’t my type anyway. Kent continues to laugh while Zack sits waiting for the doctor. In fact, Kent is only able to stop himself just as the doctor walks in. If there were to be a comparison between doctor and nurse in terms of looks, there would be no comparison. The doctor was a short, chubby man with a round face, baldhead, and a very obvious comb over. The sight of him made both men cringe. And when he spoke, it was in a small squeaky voice. Doctor: Good morning. I’m Doctor Jon C. Pecker. Both Zack and Kent snorted under their breath. Doctor Pecker: I understand you’re here for a physical you need for work. Zack confirms this with a nod, speaking with much difficulty trying to keep a straight face. But come on. Doctor Pecker? Anyone would find that funny. Zack: Yes sir. You see –snort– I’m a wrestler and I need –snort– this physical before I can go back to work. Doctor Pecker: Well I do believe we can help you out. First we’ll need a blood sample. Zack’s snorting stops immediately. He gulps a fist full of air. Zack: Blood sample? As in…needles? Doctor Pecker: Yes, but it’s quite painless. Zack looks around desperately for a way out. He tries glancing at his wrist, only to realize that he isn’t wearing a watch. Zack: Oh, look at my wrist. I just realized I have another appointment. In Canada! So I gotta go bye! Zack rushes the last part of his sentence and makes a break for the door. But Kent seems to have been expecting this kind of reaction, and jumps from his chair, catching Zack around the waist with one arm. Zack continues to try and thrash and kick his way out of Kent’s grip. In fact, he is struggling with so much strength that eventually 6 more doctors have to be called in to help hold him down. Zack: YOU GUYS ARE NAZIS! YOU HEAR ME! A BUNCH OF NAZIS! I HATE YOU KENT! I HATE YOU MAN! WHY WOULD YOU DO THIS TO YOUR – OWWWW! As the others hold him down, Doctor Pecker has snuck up behind Zack and given him a small prick with the needle, taking the blood sample he needs. Once he is done, the others release Zack, who is slumped over in a heap on the table. Doctor Pecker: There, not so bad was it. Now you just stay put right here. I’ll go get this to the lab for analyzing and should be right back. He and the others doctors leave the room, Zack sticking his tongue out at them as the go. “Not as bad as you think.” He wouldn’t be saying that if he were getting the needle. Kent frowns at Zack with a pitying expression. Kent: Dude, you are such a dork. Anyway, I’m going to go get something to eat downstairs. I’ll be right back. With that, Kent leaves the room. Now, Zack is alone. He sits up on the table again, kicking his legs in the air, gazing around and looking quite bored with himself. He is avoiding eye contact with the camera, though he chances a quick glance every now and then. After awhile, he sighs. Looks like he can’t avoid now. Zack: Ok, ok. Looks like its time to get this promo thing done with. I guess it’s a good thing I’m in a hospital. Because after my first match with Blight, woooo, I’m still feeling some of the stuff he did to me last week. Anyone remember how he caught me and dropped me on the barricade? God that hurt like a son of a b! Man, good times. Good times. Zack looks up at the ceiling, lost in a moment of reverie as if it’s being replayed for him on the ceiling, allowing him a brief moment to remember the “finer” moments of the match before looking back at the camera and continuing. I just may need them to take a look at some of the stuff. Because if first impressions are everything, then Blight left one hell of an impression on me I won’t be forgetting anytime soon. Zack points to his lower back and lifts up his shirt to reveal a small bruise, roughly the size of an apple. Most likely a gift of Blight’s from their first match. And he wears it like a badge of honor. Ain’t she a beauty. So kudos first off to Blight. I may have come of the better man last week, but it didn’t come easy. We definitely need to do this dance again sometime. And kudos secondly to MWE. If Blight is only the beginning of the level of competition here, then I can’t wait to see who’s waiting for me higher up the ladder. MWE is turning out to be exactly what I hoped for. And so for that, my hats off to ya…well, if I were wearing hat it’d be off to ya. Zack mimes tipping a hat off to those watching, then firmly places said invisible hat back on his head. Putting his shirt back down to cover his nasty bruise, he leans forward, smiling his “happy go lucky” smile. From what I hear, I’ve been making something of an impact here on Monday Nights. There’s been a lot of things said about me that I find interesting. Some have said I got moxy. Others are saying I’m a future champion. There are even a few who have even gone so far as to call me, and I’m quoting now, refreshing. And guess how that makes me feel. Makes me feel like I’m a new sports drink. Try new Crash Energy Drink! It’s got moxy and it’s refreshing! For the future champion in you! Zack points to the camera all Billy Mays (God rest his soul) style, an exaggerated smile on his face as he holds up an imaginary can of Crash Energy Drink. Zack holds the pose for a long moment, still looking into the camera. Unable to hold it any longer, he breaks it, and frowns at the camera. Why doesn’t anyone laugh at my jokes? Any who, people are saying my futures bright right now, but I wanna tell ya, it ain’t because of any possible championship prospects down the road. Although that would be quite nice, nudge nudge, hint hint, wink wink. My future is bright because of the fans that support me, and by every single person in the locker room. Because love me or hate me, just being able to wrestle against this level of competition has given me more thrills then I could have hoped for. And speaking of competition, let’s talk about the upcoming Independence Day. Zack reaches into his back pocket and pulls out a few note cards, holding them at arms length. In 1776, the Founding Fathers got together to take a stand against– oops, wrong Independence Day. Zack tosses the notes cards over his shoulder as they flutter randomly to the ground. I find it rather symbolic that my match at Independence Day is a Ladder Match. Because that’s exactly what my journey in MWE is. A long, arduous climb to the top of a tall ladder. And with my victory over Blight, I’ve stepped up one rung. It ain’t much, but it’s still something to be proud of. And with this match, I have another chance to go up one more rung, both figuratively and literally. I also find it symbolic that, like me, my opponents and partner are just a few other fresh face guys ready to burst through the glass ceiling. All three of these guys are immeasurable talents, and I can’t wait to see what they bring to the table. Again, Zack reaches into his back pocket to pull of a small folded piece of paper. He unfolds, revealing it to be a small picture of his partner for the match Chris England. First, there’s my partner, Mr. Chris England. I find it weird that an Englishmen is fighting at a pay-per-view called Independence day, but hey, who am I to judge. But all joking aside for a moment, I just wanna say I have a lot of respect for you Chris. We haven’t really spoken at all or even met since I’ve been here, but I’ve done some research on you, and I gotta say, I liked what I found. I’ve learned a lot about you. About how you fought on the mean streets of the UK, about how you wrestled on the Indies for the honor of your mentor, about your success in EWL, and everything in-between. And through my research, I think I know what you’re about man. You’re man of immense pride, who has nothing but patriotism and love for your country, who is never satisfied and always pushing yourself further. But more importantly…you’re all about theladies. Zack smiles perversely at the camera, licking his lips and nodding his head. And how can I NOT respect you for that. Now we may not know much about each other, but we’re more alike then you may think. Because I too am always striving to better myself, both in and out of the ring. They say actions speak louder than words, and I think your actions are going to speak so loudly it’ll make the audience go def. What else can I really say about you? I may not have had a mentor like yours, and I didn’t fight growing up, but either way I don’t think we’ll have any problems getting along. And so my good man, I salute you. Zack salutes the camera, neatly placing the picture aside, and pulling out another folded picture, which turns out this time to be Peter K. However, unlike England’s, Peter’s picture has many poor doodles drawn on it, such as a funny little mustache, a missing tooth from his smile, and a word bubble that says: “I’m a poopy head!” Next on our list of competitors is Peter K. Or as I like to call him, Peter Cliché. Hey, that sorta rhymes! Did I turn into Brian Michaels without knowing? Ok, where was I? Oh yeah, Peter Cliché. Why is he a cliché? Because it’s like, wow Peter, we’ve like, NEVER seen an arrogant persona here in MWE before. It’s like, TOTALLY original. Oh wait a minute, NO IT ISN’T! But I kid, I kid. What actually interests me about you is who trained you. A little guy by the name of Mick FRICKIN’ Foley. This man is the king of taking risks. Everyone remembers him being thrown of the top of a cell, and still managing to walk away. This man has left gallons of blood, pieces of skin, and broken teeth all over the world. So I wonder, what kind of sick, twisted, demented moves did he teach you? I think I’m looking forward to seeing that first hand more than anything. And from what I hear, you’re known yourself to take some pretty big risks. So it would seem that the Mickster taught you well young grasshopper. You and me, we’ll be like peas in a pod in this match. Zack tosses Peter’s picture aside haphazardly. He glares into the camera, his look suddenly serious. But I think I’m a better risk taker than you. And you wanna know why? Because you’re obsessed with one thing…winning. A true risk taker can’t be obsessed with winning, because that means, deep down, you’re afraid of losing. And if you’re afraid, then there are just some risks you won’t be willing to take. You’ll think, what if I lose because of this one move, and in the end, you won’t do it. You can’t be afraid Cliché. All it’ll do is hold you down to the ground. As for me, I’m not afraid of losing, and that means I won’t hesitate to take any risk I can. And I’ll fly circles above your pretty little head. So I fail to win, big deal. Winning isn’t everything. There are things in life more important then winning. Like they say, ‘it’s not whether you win or lose, but how you…something, something or other.’ I respect you for what you know, but if you really wanna be a high flier, then you got free yourself of all fears. Otherwise, you’ll only just Crash N’ Burn. Just some friendly advice. Zack reaches into his pocket one final time to pull out a picture of Scotty McKnight. Like Peter’s, Zack has decorated it with his own brand of art. Scotty is now donning an eye patch and a scar on his right cheek. And finally, last, but certainly not least, our final competitor. Rich, snobbish, stupid, and obsessed with money. But enough about Paris Hilton, let’s talk about Mr. Hyde. Get it? Because his partner is named Jekyl, and he’s Mr. Hyde? So it’s like Dr. Jekyl and Mr. Hyde…the book? Again, Zack receivers no laughter for his joke. In frustration, he throws his arms into the air. That’s it! I give up! But back to Scotty McDonald, or whatever his last name is. I’ve heard some interesting things about him. And surprisingly, none of them came from the voices in my head. That’s what caught my attention first when I heard about Scotty here. A guy who hears voices? Wow, this guy might be crazier than me, and that’s saying a lot. I mean, he hangs out with Sweet Tooth, he’s been trained by one of the Jackson 5, and even though he’s rich he wears wife beaters. Now I thought I was nuts, but THAT is truly insane in the membrane. But something I’ve learned from personal experience is to never, EVER underestimate the crazies. Cause they’ll do unpredictable things if you push the right buttons. Now most people avoid pushing these buttons. Luckily for me, I know which buttons to push, and I will push them. Why would I do something so stupid? Well, because first off, I already told you I’m nuts, and secondly, I wanna see what Scotty can do. Zack stands up off the table as he begins to pace the room. As he talks, rather then look at the camera, he gazes interested at the many doctor’s tools in the room, picking up and handling several of them. Now I’m sure many of you fans are thinking this can’t get any better. Well lucky for all of us, you’re wrong. But Zack, how could they possibly make this match any cooler? The answer is simple. Take two high fliers, a nut job, and a brawler, and give them a steel ladder to beat each other to hell and back with. And with a ladder, the possibilities are endless. Me and Peter could use it as a launching pad to jump from ten feet in the air. Or Scotty and Chris could use it to pummel someone’s brains out. Now I may not be good at math, but I think the equation goes something like this: Awesome match + killer opponents + cool partner = THRILLS GALORE!!! For me, this is like Christmas and my birthday all rolled up into one day. Now I may be in a little trouble after this promo, because I’m sure Scotty and Peter won’t like to hear what I just said. Hell, they may have their own choice of select words about me. But I don’t care. Say what you want. In the end, talk is cheap. And all that matters is the ring. But whether or not I end up eating my words, all I care about is one thing. And I think by now you all know what that is. I’m experiencing a bunch of firsts this week. It’s my first Tag Team Match, my first pay-per-view, and my first Ladder Match. The pieces are in place, the word is out, and I’m slowly crawling up the ladder of MWE. Where will it take me next? I don’t know. But wherever it is, I’m going to enjoy the ride. As the promo comes to a close, Doctor Pecker opens the door and peeks his fat little head inside. Doctor Pecker: Sorry to keep you waiting Mr. Crash, but we’re also going to need a stool sample from you as well. I’m just look for a container and then I’ll be right with you. He shuts the door behind as he goes to look for a little container. Zack just slowly turns to face the camera once more, his face sweaty and his expression graven. Zack: Oh snap! With all the swiftness of a cat, Zack bolts toward the door, wrenches it open, and jets down the hall at full speed. As is form grows smaller and smaller, we hear Doctor Pecker’s voice call from off screen. Doctor Pecker: HEY! He’s making an escape! Get him! –FADE–
