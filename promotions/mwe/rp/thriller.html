Thriller; Michaek Jackson Not Included

Sep 2 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Beautiful Woman: Oh Kent. I love you so much... Said the beautiful woman. She lay naked next to Kent Bishop, who was also naked. Together they lay sprawled underneath a thin white blanket. They turned to look lovingly into each others eyes. Kent: I love you too. Kent reached over and moved a small thread of the woman’s golden hair room her face. My God she was beautiful. She was perfect; almost like a goddess in her every detail. He leaned in and kissed her tenderly on the lips, and she returned the kiss in kind. Beautiful Woman: You were wonderful. Kent: Wanna go again? He whispered playfully into her ear. She giggled at the suggestion. Beautiful Woman: But we just did it two times already, and it’s not even mid-afternoon. Kent smiled devilishly at her sheepishness. He leaned closer and kissed her forehead. Then her cheeks…then her neck. Kent: I know. And she was his. Kent began kissing her all over her body. He could feel her melt beneath his lips like she was made of chocolate. The heat and passion between the two of them was so immense Kent could almost feel her heart beat within his own chest. Passionately, he continued to kiss her as she fell deeper into his love spell. ???: Hey Kent. Came a distant voice, barely noticeable. He had never had a woman like this before. So he ignored the voice. It was probably nothing anyway. ???: Hey Kent. The voice was louder this time, and more distinct. It only distracted Kent for a moment, then he returned to kissing the woman besides him. But something was different. The room, the woman, everything started to feel odd, and everything became distorted. The voice called again, and this time, it was as loud as if the person were right next to him. Zack: HEY KENT!!! WAKE UP!!! … …… ……… Zack stood over his friend Kent Bishop as he slept in his bed, watching role over this way and that, getting tangled in his bed sheet. Kent was mumbling something in his sleep as Zack poked and shook him, trying to wake him up. But Kent was in a deep sleep. Kent had always been a heavy sleeper. And judging from the smile pursing his lips, he must have been have a really good dream. Zack smiled deviously. He quickly ran into Kent’s kitchen, grabbed a cup of water and filled it to the brim. Then, carefully not to spill the water, or wake up Kent, he tiptoed back into his room. He positioned the cup right above Kent’s face and mentally started the countdown. One… Two… THREE!!! Zack: HEY KENT!!! WAKE UP!!! Zack poured the water onto Kent’s face, who immediately woke up, violently falling out of his bed and onto the floor with a thud. He grabbed the bed to lift himself up, looking all around for the source of his rude awakening. He spotted Zack smiling down at him and returned the look with an expression of shocked awe and mild disgust. Zack: Oh good, your up. Kent groaned audibly, making sure Zack could hear his annoyance at haven been woken up so rudely, and from such a pleasant dream. He sat on his bed and looked over at his bedside clock. It read 7:13 AM. Kent groaned again. Kent: What do you want Zack? Zack threw him a dry towel from the bathroom, which he used to dry himself off. Zack: Hurry up! Get up and get dressed! We have a lot to do today and a short amount of time to do it! Zack began darting in and out of rooms, apparently searching for God knows what. Kent wiped himself dry with the towel as Zack started to throw at him a random assortment of clothes. And Kent was looking forward to a quiet day. Kent: What exactly do we have to do today? Zack poked his head back inside the room and gave him a wide smile that threatened to break Zack’s jaw line. The look made Kent’s heart plummet into his gut. When Zack smiled like that, nothing good ever came of it. Especially for him. Zack: We’re going to do a thriller! Kent raised an eyebrow in apparent confusion. Kent: What does Michael Jackson have to do with this? Zack laughed at the poor joke, darting once again in and out of rooms as he answered back. Zack: Not THAT kind of thriller…a thriller. It’s an urban legend within the thrill seeking community. Another eyebrow raised in confusion. Kent: Thrill seekers have urban legends? Zack nodded vigorously. Kent: I know I’m going to regret asking, but…what the hell is a thriller? Zack: It’s when you do a bunch of thrill seeking activities at once. Either that, or all in one day. I’m not really sure what the legend is, but it sounds awesome and WE are going to do it. Kent rolled his eyes. Kent: It sounds stupid. You call THAT an urban legend? Zack appeared from around the corner, looking indignantly at his friend. Zack: Don’t knock the thriller my friend! Nobodies ever done it before. Kent: Probably because it’s a stupid urban legend. Zack: Just get dressed already! Kent: Why do I have to go? Zack: It’s the rules of the legend! You can’t do the thriller alone, you have to bring at least one other person with you. Now hurry up! Sighing in surrender, Kent got up and started to get dressed using clothes OTHER than the ones Zack threw at him, which were smelly and needed to be washed. After a few minutes, Kent was dressed and ready to go. Zack was still darting in and out of rooms as Kent watched in mounting irritation. Kent: Ok, I’ll bite. What are you doing now? Zack was currently in Kent’s office, rummaging through his file cabinet, through papers all around the room like snow. And of course, Kent thought to himself he would have to be the one later to clean this mess up. Zack: I’m looking for your passport. Kent: But I don’t have a passport. Just as the words left Kent’s lips, Zack’s hand shot up, producing a passport bearing Kent’s name and a very old picture from years back when he still had a mullet. Don’t ask. Kent look on at the embarrassing in total surprise as Zack brandished it in his face. Kent: But…I never… Zack: I had one made up for you a few years back in case I ever wanted to take you overseas for any thrill seeking. Never mind the fact that Zack went behind his back and got him a passport, let alone how that’s even possible to begin with, there was a more pressing, and in Kent’s mind, frightening question eating at him. Kent: Why do I need a passport? … …… ……… Kent: AHHHHHHHHHH!!!! The wind rushed past Kent like a torrent as he fell like a rock to the solid earth below. This was only the second time he’d been sky diving, and he’d liked it even less this time around. Immediately after finding his passport, which apparently he’d always had, Zack dragged him to the airport where a plane was waiting to take them to Queenstown, New Zealand, or as Zack called it… The Adventure Capital Of The World! The first they decided to do, or more appropriately Zack decided to do was go skydiving…again. And just like before, Zack had to literally throw…and I mean THROW Kent out of the plane. Kent screamed until his throat was raw, while beside him Zack did flips and twirls in the air. He always was a showoff. Kent: I’M GOING TO KILL YOU FOR THIS!!! Kent screamed to be heard over the rushing wind. But Zack either didn’t hear him, or was just ignoring him. Finally, after what felt like an endless fall, Kent had somehow survived, and with his jumpsuit still dry. He bent over a few feet away from the drop zone, gasping heavily for air, feeling as if he was about to cough up one of his lungs. Kent: How *gasp* do*gasp* you *gasp* do *gasp* this? Zack bounded over, still full of that annoying energy like a little kid. He barely looked like the events had fazed him. Zack then gave Kent a huge slap on the back, making Kent nearly double over. Now he felt like he was going to cough up BOTH his lungs. Kent: I think I liked the “emo” you better. Zack: Come on dude, the day is still young. Kent groans loudly. Kent: Now what? … …… ……… Kent: AHHHHHHHHHH!!!! Now our scene has drastically changed again. This time, Kent dangles upside down above a rocky terrain below. As the blood rushes to Kent’s head, he is slowly raised back up to the platform. Finally back on solid footing, Kent steps off to the side, once again breathing hard, as Zack is attached to the bungee cable. And despite himself, Kent cracked a small trace of a smile. Zack: WOOOOO BITCHES!!! Zack called out (his “ thrill cry” if you will) as he leapt off the platform. Zack hadn’t been himself the last couple of months. In fact he was almost an entirely different person. Always moody, losing interest in things he once loved, and lashing out at everything and everyone. And, although he’d deny it if asked, Kent had been worried. Kent never fully understood why Zack’s lifestyle meant so much to him, but he was still worried when Zack seemed to change. Kent had never appreciated Zack’s choice of lifestyle. In fact, he downright hated it. He not only found it irresponsible, but also incredibly stupid. But some of that may have come from his jealousy toward Zack. Zack was a go-getter who wasn’t afraid to try anything at least once. Zack lived the life of adventure everyone, including Kent, dreams about at least once in their life. Kent would be lying if he said that, even if he didn’t approve, he never felt Zack lived a fuller life than he himself did. But even with these conflicting emotions, he was worried about Zack when even thrill seeking seemed to have lost its edge to his friend. Now here he was, his same old self once again, annoying and energetic as ever, and Kent was very relieved. It was as if Zack hadn’t changed at all. So, despite the miserable day Zack was putting him through, Kent cracked a small. Then, as Zack dangled in the same position he had only moments ago, that smile turned into a small chuckle. It was good to have his best friend back. Definitely. Zack was pulled back onto the platform, where he stood in front of Kent, still filled with boundless energy. Zack: So, what do we do next? Kent’s jaw dropped as Zack pulled out a long list of what he dreadfully suspected were the activities Zack had planned for them today. Kent snuck a quick peek of the list. So far, it didn’t even look like they’d scratched the surface of what they were supposed to do today. He was almost afraid to ask. Kent: There’s more? … …… ……… Hours later, and many soar muscles later, Kent hangs off of one of Zack’s shoulders as he is nearly dragged into their hotel room. Kent groans and moans at nearly every step, until Zack plops Kent down onto the bed, where he lets out a sigh of relief. Zack: Sorry about that again dude. I seriously thought you were going fast enough to make that jump. Kent gave him a sour look, but said nothing to this comment. His body was too racked with aches to argue or complain. Kent: So…is that enough for your little thriller? Surprisingly, Zack flushed slightly at the question, and rubbed his hand behind his head. He looked like a little boy you’d been caught in some act of mischief. Kent ddin’t like the feeling this was giving him. Zack: Yeah, about that, the thriller thing…I kinda…made it up. Silence echoed in the hotel room like a hammer. If Kent’s neck weren’t feeling so stiff, he’d have done a double take. It was all he could manage to just scowl at Zack. Kent: What? Zack: I made it up. You were right before, there is no “thriller” urban legend. I just wanted to do a bunch of stuff to celebrate my CG win. But I didn’t want to do it alone, so I… Kent could feel his body shaking, but he wasn’t sure if that was from the pain or the anger, possibly a little bit of both. In that moment, Kent took back every nice thought he had had about Zack that day; about being glad he was back to normal. Now he was wishing Virus would have killed him at Crowning Glory. At least then he might have some peace and quiet in his life. Why couldn’t his friend have been an accountant? With all his effort, and the last bits of his strength, Kent sat upright just long enough to calmly say three simple words. Kent: I…hate…you. And with those loving last words, Kent Bishop finally passed out from exhaustion. Zack tip-toed out of the hotel room, closing the door quietly behind him. He smiled to himself. Kent was sure to give him a nagging by the time he woke up, and he supposed he would deserve it. He hadn’t been exactly easy to deal with the last few months, let alone today. He appreciated how much Kent put up with him, but today was different. He needed this today. He needed to do something to remind himself of what he was before he went all “emo.” He needed this so that he could finally feel like his old self again. To prove he could still “The Ultimate Thrill Seeker.” And most of all, he needed Kent to be with him for that. Zack: Thanks Kent. Zack muttered to himself. With the word “emo” still in his head, Zack checked his watch. It was still early. He thought he could still do THAT one final thing today. Something he’d always wanted to try but never had. Something he’d been saving for a “special” occasion. And if he was quick enough, he could be back before Kent even realized he left without him. After pulling out his One Day Pass plane ticket and checking it, he instantly pulled out his cell phone and dialed the number for the airport. Next stop…Sicily. This is the champion Zack Crash. The flight from Queenstown, New Zealand to Sicily, Italy had been a long one, but as Zack looked out over the sight before him, he knew it was well worth the time and money. Mount Etna is one of the few remaining active volcanoes in the world today. Normally, its blackened slopes are covered with ash. But, if you come at the right time of year, its face is covered instead with a thick white blanket of snow. Also, if you pay a pretty penny, you can come to the very top of the volcano and ski down its slopes. And that’s exactly what has paid to do here today. As he looks down over the expansive sea of white, and the plummeting slopes beneath him that are calling his name, Zack feels literally on top of the world. Appropriate for a MWE Champion…don’t you think? Zack: When you first become a World Champion in wrestling, you can hardly believe what just happened. You spend your time as champion in a half-dazed state of mind, wondering if any second you’ll wake up from some marvelous dream and find you were never champion to begin with. So that’s why I feel most of us can’t really remember our first World Championship reign. We’re just so caught up in the amazement of the moment that we don’t stop to really appreciate or examine it. It’s only once we reach that mountaintop a second does it truly mean something. Because it’s your second title reign that legitimizes your first. It proves beyond any shadow of a doubt that you ARE championship material, that your first victory was no fluke, that lightning can, and WILL strike twice. Zack looks off to the side. There, lying patiently in the snow is a single snowboard. Zack look at it longingly, then even more longingly down the downhill slope. He wanted to ride down it now, to conquer this force of nature like he was conquering MWE. But not now. Just like wrestling, thrill seeking was a very precise art form. Much like how a wrestler has to chose the precise move and moment to execute it, a thrill seeker must chose the right moment in time to launch themselves off the plan, down the mountain, and so on. Because, just like in wrestling, if you chose the wrong moment, you just might crash and burn. And lightning did strike twice. Because here I stand, on top of this mountain, a two time…count ‘em…two time World Champion. When I first became world champion in UUW, I heard what all the critics had to say. They said I only became champion because it was a watered down version of MWE and that there was no real competition there. They said I got lucky, and that if I were to go up against the glory days of MWE, I wouldn’t stand a chance at being MWE Champion. Now, I don’t hear those critics saying jack shit. Because now it’s real. Because now that it’s happened a second time, they’re forced to recognize the first. They’re forced to recognize me! Because I made the devil say…I quit! Zack thinks back to that night of August 22nd, 2010. One of the proudest days of his life. It was a day where he not only achieved his life’s dream, it was the day he silenced his demons. And Leon Roberts too hopefully. It was in that moment, standing atop the stage, holding what was now his MWE Championship that Zack truly felt worthy of all the praise he’d ever gotten. It was then that he truly felt like the main event draw people said he was. This championship is the validation of everything he’s ever done. It means that everything was worth it…that HE was worth it. But I still got some proving to do. Because winning the championship, that’s the easy part. The hard part is defending it, and that’s where the true measure if a champion lies. And this week, I get my first chance to prove my worth as your MWE Champion when I go up against the “Emo Warrior” and “God’s Unwanted Child… …Alexis Black! Off in the distance, there is a small rumbling of power as the volcano stirs from its sleep under Zack’s feet. The rumble is ironically reminiscent of the rumbling of anger and rage beneath Crash’s heart at even the mention of Alexis Black. Because Zack still remembers what Black tried to do at Crowing Glory. Crash still feels the pain from every one of those kendo shots. Crash still remembers how Black almost cost him the MWE Championship. Well those actions are going to cost Black so much more this week on MNM! A lot more than just a few bruises. You know we’ve been seeing a lot of each other recently Alexis. We had that one match before Crowning Glory, then you attack me at CG, and now we have another match this week on MNM. And I gotta say, despite our violent encounters, I feel like we’ve gotten closer these last few weeks, almost like brothers even. I’m serious dude, I feel a genuine bond with you brother. I really mean it. Because if you can’t trust a guy who beats your head in with a Japanese wooden sword…who can ya trust? If trying to kill a man and cost him his dream doesn’t make you as good as brothers…then I don’t know what does. Zack snickers at his own sarcasm. But then that smile turns deathly grim. Time to get serious. But in all seriousness Black, I thought you were better than that. You attacking me from behind, all in exchange for some “deal” you and Virus made? Let me guess, the deal was if he won, you’d get the first title opportunity right? Please tell me I’m right! You know, we’ve never seen eye to eye, but I at least thought you were a better man and a better wrestler than to try and politic your way to a championship match. You disappoint me Black. I honestly thought you were above that sorta thing. I got to say, I’ve never been more upset to be proven so wrong. Zack hangs his head in shame for “God’s Unwanted Child.” He never really liked Black, but at the very least Crash respected him. Although most wrestlers never admit it, they generally have at least some respect for those that manage to beat them in the ring. And Zack Crash was one such wrestler. Even if he doesn’t like you, if you can beat him, at the very least he can respect you. But there is NO respect for what Alexis Black tried to do. No way in hell! But that doesn’t matter anymore. Because it looks like your deal went right down the shitter. Because Leon Roberts isn’t the MWE Champion…I AM! And now you get nothing. It was all wasted effort. All you managed to do was waste your time and piss me off, which is something you don’t want to do Black. So now, this week on MNM, I plan on making an example of you. I plan on returning every kendo shot, kick, punch, and bruise you gave me at Crowning Glory back in kind. Because I’m an honorable man, and an honorable man believes in pay back what he’s owed. My title reign will be something not to be taken lightly, and I will prove that on MNM. Because a king’s reign is only as good as the respect he garners from his subjects. And if I can’t even beat the Hot Topic’s poster boy, then maybe I don’t deserve any respect. Another rumbling from the waking volcano. This one more powerful than the last. So much so that the earth shakes so violently Zack is nearly thrown off his feet. But he’s the Ultimate Thrill Seeker, and it’ll take much more than that to throw him off his feet. Just like it’ll take more than a eyeliner wearing, Robert Smith of The Cure wannabe to undermine his championship reign. Oh, but I bet your not all that worried are you. After all, you did beat me last time we met in the ring, so why should you take anything I say to heart? Well here’s the problem with that. The Zack Crash you faced before isn’t the Zack Crash you’ll be facing this week. Last time we faced off, I was even more emo than you, which is funny when you think about it. I did your gimmick better than you do, but I digress. The point is, I wasn’t in the right frame of mind back then. I was to concerned with looking toward the future, planning out my next move, and worrying about what will happen if I fail. In short, I had forgotten who I was. And just who exactly am I? I’M THE ULTIMATE THRILL SEEKER MOTHERFUCKERS!!! Zack lifts up his arms and shouts his nickname at the top of his lungs. As the words echo down the valley, the volcano rumbles more violently than ever before, almost as if the mountain is cheering Zack on. Who knew Mother Nature was a wrestling fan. Even better, it appears she’s a junior thrill seeker. I’m Zack Crash! And I don’t worry about the future or worry about whether or not I’m going to fail anymore. All that stuff is for wimps and accountants. Thrill seekers only live in the moment, and that’s where I plan to stay. In the moment. I take things as they come and face them head on. Any challenged I meet along the way I just welcome them as an unexpected treat. Just another obstacle for me to overcome. Just another thrill for the adrenaline junkie. That’s right, I’m a junkie. I get high off of bungee jumping, and sky diving, and snowboarding down volcanoes. Do you people honestly think calling me a junkie is supposed to upset me? Come on Black, I dare you to use that against me! Crash smirks and opens his arms wide, as if offering a free shot to the chest. Zack has heard the junkie remarks so many times that it actually makes him laugh. He finds the comments to be cute now actually. Just a bunch of wrestlers cycling through old promos for old ammo against him. Zack had heard the same stuff for so long, he was truthfully hoping for something new from Alexis Black. And what about you Black? What have you done with your career? What did you do after MWE closed the first time? While I was actually making good on what I learned in MWE, becoming champion, you were probably off in some corner of Hot Topic, making fun of all the preps coming in. You know I’m starting to think that all that eyeliner is making you blind. Blind to the simple fact that your career just isn’t as great as you thin kit is. And you still have the nerve to criticize my lifestyle? You still think you’re better than me? Dude, all I’m saying is compare our accomplishments and let them do the talking for us. Yes, you beat me once, but that’s precisely it…ONCE! One victory against an opponent doesn’t mean jack crap in this business. Beat me a second time, than come talk to me. Until then, sit down, shut up, and apply your “emo man” makeup a little more evenly, because dude, you just look like a REALLY ugly street whore. A final, ear-shattering rumble echoes from Etna’s peak. Zack looks back behind him with a gleam in his eyes like a fat child in a candy store. Zack straps himself onto the snowboard, and then carefully positions himself just right. He looks on with eager anticipation for the thrill soon to come. But he doesn’t launch himself down the mountainside. Not just yet. The timing still needs to be perfect. Right now Black, my reign as champion is like this volcano, ready to burst at any moment. And you’re only a small obstacle for me to maneuver around. In the end, you only have two options. You can either be a witness to it…or you can be a victim to it. So what will you do when your world comes crashing– Before Zack can even finish his catchphrase, the volcano behind him roars one last time as it breathes liquid fire out its head. From a distance, Zack can see the small, slow flow of lava as it moves inch by inch closer, turning the white snow now black, as a pillar of smoke rises into the sky like a flag. Zack’s smile widens as far as it will go. NOW is the time! DOWN!!! And with a small river of lava chasing after him, Zack leans forward just so slightly, and off he goes, speeding down the face of Mount Etna. And as Zack speeds faster and faster down the mountain, he doesn’t once look back. Because even though there is a liquid river of burning danger behind, Zack doesn’t care about what’s behind him. He doesn’t even care about what’s further down the mountain. He only cares about what is in front of him right here and now. That’s the attitude of a thrill seeker. That’s the attitude…of a champion!
