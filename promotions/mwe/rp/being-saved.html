Being Saved; Anthony Clark RP

Jun 19 2009

Anthony Clark

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The screen opens We see a rainy, cloudy, dark, miserable New York City day. You would see the rain coming down heavily, and the lightning flashing in the day light skies. You would see a big old stone church. The rain running down the painted up windows of biblical characters on the. The camera would follow the rain that’s pouring down, and you would see a set of stairs leading to the church doors. You would see a man with his head down sitting on the staircase. All you can see is the cigaret hanging from his mouth, and the burning ashes light up as he takes a drag from it. He’d take it out, and flick off his ashes. The man would slowly look up, and it would expose his face to show it’s none other then Anthony Clark. He has a bewildered look upon his face. He seems a bit rattled, and a bit shaken up as well. His eyes going back, and forth a bit showing a sense of panic, and uncertainty. Anthony Clark: Some say... It’s better to love, and lose... Then to never love at all. Well I gotta say the man who came up with that saying... Has never been in love. Cause the pain I’m feeling right now is one I have never felt in my lifetime as a pro wrestler... You see I earned my shot. I earned my chance at the XTV title... I had my love. It was in the grasp of my hands. It was there torturing me. All I had to do was grab it.... Yet somehow... Someway.... It slipped out of my grip. I lost my chance at that title. The title I felt so close to being mine. I blew it when it mattered the most to me. I let myself down. Anthony would flick his ashes off his cigaret again, after taking another drag off of it. He would stand up, and take off his soaked hood. You would see he has on a pair of baggy jeans, with a black Los Angeles Lakers 09 championship t-shirt showing off his pride for his hometown team, with a black unzipped hoodie on over it. He’d walk up the steps, and walk in threw the doors. He would walk into the main room, and see the amazing sculpture threw out the room. When he walks in he would take one knees, and touch spots on his body to make the shape of a cross. He’d stand, and start walking inside again. Anthony Clark: Some people are starting to question my speechless, my words, and my beliefs in myself. Word on the street is most fans that follow MWE are starting to say this once upon a time future all star in wrestling is turning into nothing more then a sham. He can take big but never follow up on it... They are saying I’m like Alex Rodriguez, or Lebron James. I can talk big, but when it comes to the big games, the championship style games... I’m a flop. I turn over on my back, when it means the most not to. Everyone is saying the XTV is the proving point. They are saying the once self proclaimed “Rookie Of The Year” is nothing more but the “Joke Of The Year”. Anthony Clark is not a joke. He would take a seat inside the church, and watch people walk over to the candles. There would be a few ladies in wet rain coats. They would be lighting candles, and making prayers. Anthony would watch on, and take a deep breath making sure if he is in the right place. Anthony Clark: The last time you people, you fans, you haters... The last time you saw me speaking I was rather angry with my trainer... But yet very confident in myself. I’m not an easily beaten person. I’m not a person who comes to others for help. And as we all know I don’t need anyone on my side. But I have come here... To this church... Looking for the answers. The answers I am desperately seeking. Some will say “Anthony isn’t this too drastic too soon?”. And to those people I say no... It’s not. Failure is not acceptable anytime in my mind. You see failure can make me go well insane. Now you see a lot of people in life have different definitions of the word insanity. You see my definition insanity is doing the same thing over, and over again expecting to get a different result... But you know it’s going to be the same one all the time. It’s like running into a concrete wall. You know you keep getting headaches from running into that wall... But you keep doing it. But for some odd reason you keep doing it, cause you think.... you believe there is a glimmer of hope you’re going to get threw that wall. You believe the headaches will go away. My insanity is thinking about the word failure will never haunt my damn mind again. Failure some how though just peaks it’s ugly little head into my life though. When I’m starting to get ahead of the game... Failure pokes it’s head out and decides to knock me back a few steps. Some call it a reality check, I call it a problem. And now I’m looking for my answers... And I think I have found em. But before we get to my solution... How about we discuss some... Well new topics for Anthony Clark. Anthony would slowly walk up to the candles then, and light one. He would look up at the cross, and whisper his prayer on his knees. He would get up, and nod his head to the cross. Anthony Clark: Now on Monday Night Mayhem... I wrestle two men. One familiar. And well the other not very much at all. First off I have the very familiar Jay Riddick... The man who is saying to be the number one, or a close number one in the new class of wrestlers here. I was always over shadowed by you. You know why? Cause you got one pin over me. In a match where I worked my ass off. I pinned two men prior to your win over me in that match. You got the best of me when I was tired out. Now I get to see you again but.... Here’s the catch. Yet again there is someone joining us in that ring. Chris England. For me Riddick... Anthony would shake his head, and take a deep breath thinking things threw. Anthony Clark: This match isn’t about title shots, or anything like that. This match is for me... Its so I can get my one on one shot at you. So I can get my hands on you, without anyone getting in my way one damn bit. And if it takes me this match, and get rid of Chris England so be it. This series of matches is like my minor league career. Once I top this series of matches I am big in the big leagues. I’m back where I rightfully deserve to be in life. In the tops. I’m tired of being over shadowed by you Riddick. I’m tired of you holding a damn victory over my head. I wanna put what we have against each other to rest. You are nothing to me but a thorn in my side. You pinned me once. So what Riddick? The sun has to shine on a dog’s ass once in a while. That day was just your day. Nothing more, nothing less. Anthony would walk all the way back the isle. He would get to the seat he was once sitting at. He would sit there, and look at his watch as if he was waiting for someone. Anthony Clark: Chris England... I never met you, I have never cared to meet you. I don’t really have much care for your Brits but that’s neither here, or there. You’re a big guy, and I’m not. But you seem to be fast, but son I bet you... Your not fast enough for Anthony Clark. You say you have been fighting since you were 9, and that you had this hard knocks life apparently. How bout the next time you tell people your past, how about you stop reading the 50 Cent autobiography ok buddy. Apparently half the wrestling world grew up on the “Streets”. Most of you guys in the back have no street cred anyways. I mean you guys have almost as much street cred then Kanye West does, and he’s from like the suburbs. Chris England it’s just what I said about Riddick. You are in my way England to getting what I want most. My one on one match with Riddick. You are in the middle of me proving to this world I am not the joke. That I’m not just a false myth. An urban legend. A fraud Anthony would he the entrance doors open, and he’d stand like he is expecting someone still. Anthony Clark: Chris England, Jay Riddick... MWE world... I once said I will do anything it takes to make sure I am not this joke everyone sees me as today. That’s why I am here at this church today. I know a lot of you are wondering why I am here. I know you are asking yourself “Has Anthony Clark been saved?”. An to answer that question... It’s yes. I have been saved. And not in the way of religion... I came here for my sins I will say make. For my sins I will have to make just to make myself who I once was. And I came here to make my prayer that everything goes to plan. My deal is done ladies, and gentleman. I am saved. It’s just not by what you think. Anthony would look over to the entrance to see Brian Michaels at the doorway. The word on the internet of Michaels recruiting a new young crew seems to be showing to be right as he is standing at the church doorway. Anthony would walk over to Michaels, and smiles. Michaels would extend his hand, and Anthony Clark would shake it showing the MWE the talks on the MWE forums were right about Clark going to Michaels Inc. The two would walk out of the church together, and having a conversation some as they do. The camera would turn to show the sculpture of Jesus hanging from the cross. The screen would slowly fade. And the words apear. Michaels Inc Production. Black screen. End.
