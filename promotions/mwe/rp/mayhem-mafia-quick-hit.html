Mayhem Mafia Quick Hit; Short but Sahweet!; Michaels/Whitman vs. Cannon/Resoan rp

Apr 24 2009

Brian Michaels & David Whitman

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The camera opens up slowly to reveal a location many haven’t seen in a while… the inside of the Top Rope Tavern. The camera moves along to showcase everything inside the bar… From the drunks at the bar area, to the guys at the pinball machine, to a couple at a booth, to… some film cameras with cameramen adjusting the lenses and cleaning the front of them. The view quickly shoots back to the drunks at the bar. By which I mean the JESTers. Eddie Sanchez, Scott Jones and Thomas Williams are standing there drinking beers and talking to each other. Clad in their JESTers shirts, they each have a “Bartender” nametag on. Eddie: Dude, I mean it. This is RIDICULOUS. We’ve been working on the last season one episode for months and trying to get him to finish the script, and now David just wants to use the Tavern for a stinkin’ PROMO?! Scott: I know. I’m not even sure I wanna go through with it. Thomas: Well, when he gets here, I’m gonna give him a piece of my mind. I mean, you know, not literally. Cuz that’s just gross. Scott: Or very thoughtful. …GET IT?! THOUGHT-FULL? A PIECE OF YOUR MIND?! Scott explodes in over-the-top laughter as Eddie and Thomas just shake their heads “no” at each other. All of a sudden, a bell is heard, signaling that someone has just entered the bar. The camera cuts to the Tavern entrance to show MWE’s own David Whitman, dressed in his signature baggy black pants and a Mayhem Mafia shirt, with a title on each shoulder. David: WAZZAPPENIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIN?! The camera cuts to Eddie, Scott and Thomas, who are now all three shaking their heads in unison. David walks over to them and slings both titles onto the bar. Eddie: Dude, how come sometimes you have one title, but at other times, you got two? Thomas: Continuity errors much? A rimshot is heard. Everyone looks to their right as Thomas yells out “ZACK!” The camera cuts to the small stage area, where Zack Evermore is playing with the DJ setup. He shrugs and gets a “Woops” expression on his face. The camera cuts back to Eddie, Scott, Thomas and David, who are now all four shaking their heads. Once the headshaking stops, Eddie opens up a beer and passes it to David. David: FINALLY! Now my day can begin. Scott: Dude, it’s like 6 PM. David: Yeah, but I’ve been training all day. Scott: For what, your promo? David: Huh? Oh, no, that’s not gonna happen. The JESTers: WHAT?! David: Yeah, no, I uh, decided against it. Eddie slams his own beer hard on the bar. Eddie: You have GOT to be kidding me! We called up the camera crew and everything! Scott: Yeah! And besides, if you HAVE to talk about your tag match anyway, why not do it right here at the Tavern? What the hell made you change your mind? David takes a big gulp of his drink. David: I don’t feel like talking much. He lets out a burp. David: Sooooo... EVERYONE, OUT! Jeers of “AWWWW”, “DANGIT”, “What a waste of my G#!d@mn time”, “this sucks” and “I never got to the last level of that last N64 Zelda game” are heard as the bar patrons and camera crew start leaving the bar, cameras and all (except for the one showing us all of this, as logic would suggest). Eddie: STOP! Everyone stops in their tracks. Eddie: Leave those cameras right there! Look, everyone go get a beer, on the house. We’ll let you all know if we’re still gonna be filming in a bit. Cheers of “YAY!”, “YES!”, and “I’m gonna need a ladder to get that beer if it’s on the house” are now heard as everyone who was leaving scatters toward the bar area. The JESTers and David barely make it out of the moshpit-like situation and dust themselves off. Scott: Buncha savages in this town. David: Now why the hell did you go and do THAT, Eddiver? Eddie: Come on, dude. I know you; you haven’t made your mind up yet about this whole filming here thing. It’s gonna take you at least another HOUR for you to make a decision. Also, don't call me Eddiver. Or Eddie Renshaw. It makes no sense to me. David: Dude, I told you. I don’t feel like filming today. We’re not filming today. The special edition of the Top Rope Tavern show is NOT happening. NO. FILMING. David’s serious glare stays fixated on the JESTers, but they slowly turns their eyes to the direction of the camera. They then turn their attention back to David, who is still looking at them, seriously. David: GOT IT? They all shrug. Once again, and almost as if on cue, the bell sounds. The camera moves to the entrance of the bar to show Triple G and Mr. Markowitz standing in the door, posing in their own Mayhem Mafia shirts. Cheering and clapping is heard as the camera cuts back to the JESTers and David, who are all looking around as if trying to locate the source of the sound. Scott finally succeeds in locating the source as he looks off-camera. Scott: Oh, for the love of- THEY FOUND THE GOOD WHISKEY! He runs off-camera towards the bar area as Eddie is left shaking his head in his hands. Thomas is just looking concerned, and Triple G and Markowitz walk into the scene and stand next to David. Triple G: So, what’s going on? David: Well, basically, we’re trying to figure out whether or not I should do a promo. Markowitz: Why WOULDN’T you? David: Well, I mean, Brian already did one, and you know, it was insane. I can’t top that. Triple G: You’re not SUPPOSED to. Wrestling isn’t about who’s got the best promo- Triple G, Markowitz, David, Eddie and Thomas all five turn to the camera very briefly before looking back at each other. Triple G: … It’s about how you do in the ring. Markowitz: Yeah. And besides, even if it WAS about who’s got the best promo, Brian’s on your team, so it doesn’t matter. Triple G: Nice one, cuz. Markowitz: YEAH! Blood Fuze shares an enthusiastic high five. A loud “NO! NO! NO!” is heard off-screen, and suddenly, Scott returns to the scene, shaking his finger violently at Blood Fuze. Scott: NO HIGH-FIVING! That’s OUR thing! Markowitz: Dude, Lighten UP! Triple G: Yeah man, we’re all factions! Can’t all the factions just get along? Triple G, Marko, Eddie, Scott, Thomas and David all six turn to the camera for a second before turning back to each other. David: Look, here’s the thing. The th- David is interrupted by loud cheering again, but this time everyone turns to look towards the entrance of the bar. The camera turns as well, to reveal a giant mob of fans (Get it? MOB?!) going crazy outside of the Tavern. The three JESTers as well as the three Mayhem Mafia’ers shrug, and the JESTers head towards the bar as the Mafia heads towards the Tavern entrance. The camera cuts to the outside as Blood Fuze and Whitman arrive to address the now insanely loud crowd. David tries his best, but the chants of “Ma-Fi-A!”, cheers of “WOOHOOOO” and the clapping all overpower his voice. He turns and goes back into the bar as the crowd seemingly turns on him and start going “AW, COME ON”, and “COME BAAAACK!”. After a minute or so, David reappears, with a megaphone in his right hand. The fans erupt once more as the three present Mayhem Mafia members make their way through the blob of people. David: I’m way too tired and I don’t really have much to say, but I gotta get a promo done, apparently. And since you people are a big part of what’s gonna make the Mayhem Mafia THE group to be reckoned with- The fans, as one would expect, erupt yet again. David: … I thought maybe some of you could say a couple of words. The crowd gets loud as many extend their hands, trying to grab the megaphone to say something. David hands it to one fan, and Triple G and Markowitz hoist the fan up on their shoulders. The slightly young-looking kid puts the Megaphone to his mouth… Fan: MAFIA FOR THE WIN! Vanguard is not a faction. Vanguard is… A VIRUS! AND THE MAYHEM MAFIA IS NORTON 360, BIAAAAAAAAAAAAAITCH! The crowd screams in joy as David just shakes his head. The fan tosses the megaphone to another guy in the crowd. Blood Fuze hoists the fan even higher, and he jumps off of them in a Final Flight-like manner as some other fans catch him and the crowd goes “OOOOOOOH!”, followed by a lot of clapping. The next fan to hold the megaphone is now also being hoisted. This time, it’s the prototypical token hot chick. She struggles with the megaphone a bit but eventually succeeds in putting it at her mouth (that’s wh-), and begins to speak enthusiastically. Chick: Mason Resoan… PSH. MASON??? More like “FACIN”! As in; FACIN the two BEST WRESTLERS IN MWE TODAY! The crowd lets out a simultaneous hissing sound (you know, the one used to convey “Ouchhh”) as some jeers of “that’s ridiculous” and other such insults are hurled. Chick: Gimme a break, you guys! I’m new at this! Okokokok, how about this one, how about; RESOAN? More like “GOIN’!” As in, “Goi- It is at this point that the girl is dropped from Blood Fuze’s shoulders. But don’t worry, some guys caught her. And molested her violently. NO, I’M JUST KIDDING! Could you imagine?! But seriously now. The megaphone is now passed to someone else, but he refuses to get hoisted up as he accepts the megaphone. Guy: I’m good. So… Ace Cannon and Mason Resoan. That’s like… A deadly combination. Some of the best in-ring ability mixed with some of the most brutal insanity… and it’s going to be hard to beat. Or, it WOULD be hard to beat. But Ace and Mason are like Oil and Water… Separately, they’re amazing. And if you mix them, in theory, the result should be even more amazing. But they are just immiscible. The camera begins swerving around to show different faces in the crowd, yelling loudly at the camera. Even so, tackling these two is going to be a challenge. HOWEVER, the Mayhem Mafia is no stranger to challenges. As individuals, they’ve been through hell and back; Michaels and Whitman both being in-ring monsters with monster records. Both have been with MWE from the beginning… Fans (chanting in a “GOOOLDBERG” style manner):“OLD SCHOOL! OLD SCHOOL! OLD SCHOOL!” If my history is correct, Cannon’s first contact with MWE was when he was part of WAR and they attacked MWE… On that night, he beat the living sh*t out of- Mason Resoan. Fitting. “SPLIT THAT SH*T! SPLIT THAT SH*T! SPLIT THAT SH*T!” Mason is an amazing athlete, and we all know he’s one of the best at what he does. But the problem is- he works alone. Exclusively. His ego is bigger than Cannon’s, impossible as it may seem, and he won’t allow himself to be helped by his tag partner. Which actually works well, because Cannon wouldn’t offer him a hand if Resoan were a flaming sack of crap on Cannon’s doorstep and Cannon had a big bowl of soup in his hands because he was just having dinner when the doorbell rang. “WOOOOOOOOHOOOOOOOO” YEAAAAAAAH!” “That’s just ridiculous.” And as insanely crazy and odd as that analogy sounds, it STILL makes more sense than the team of Cannon and Resoan beating the Mafia. “MAFIA! MAFIA!” It is simple. Cannon and Resoan will not function together. Either the team of Whitman and Michaels use teamwork to overpower them, or we’ll see Mason and Cannon blow up and attack each other, giving Mafia the win by default. “DEFAULT, NOT OUR FAULT! DEFAULT, NOT OUR FAULT!” Speaking of giving the Mafia the win, Blood Fuze taking Sharpman out should set the tone nicely for the tag match. Anyways, I digress… The point is that… “What’s the point, anyways?” *Clap, clap, clap clap clap* All of this is just a roundabout way of saying… The camera stops showing random cuts of the fans and moves to shot from above, showing the whole crowd. Monday Night Mayhem belongs to the Mafia. The crowd completely loses it as loud chants of “MAY-HEM, MAFIA!” erupt and the camera swings down to reveal David Whitman holding the megaphone up high as Triple G and Markowitz both throw up their hand signs. The camera slowly fades into a logo on a black screen.
