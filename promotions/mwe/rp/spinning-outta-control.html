Spinning Outta Control; Anthony Clark RP

Jul 16 2009

Anthony Clark

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
After commercials disperse, cameras slowly fade in to a diner, focusing on the glass door. As seen coming from the parking lot, dress in his finest is Anthony Clark. Clean, white bandana covering the top of his head, an Silver Star George St. Pierre series t-shirt and blue denim jeans to meet his clean white DC sneakers. He pushes the door with ease, his eyes seem distraught. Glancing over the tables, his eyes stop in one spot, a slight smirk creeps on his face. He begins to make his way towards a table, the camera following him. But like everyone else, Anthony s there’s no camera. Anthony Clark: Well look who it is. The camera snaps around to show a skinny fit woman with a warm grin on her face. Her hair is flowing, and it’s very dark in the shining light. Her skin looks like the sun took its aggressions out on him in the form of a dark tan. She stands up, reaching up to Anthony's chest. Anthony Clark: Oh Miss Michelle Jordan Michelle Jordan: Anthony Clark. Anthony’s hand extends outward, grasping Michelle’s waiting hand. After he takes her hand he’d kiss it gently. They both chuckle slightly before taking their seats. Clark scoots in his chair closer to the table. Clark: It feels like its been years since we last talked. Jordan: A couple weeks, I’d say. Clark: That long? Damn. Jordan: Time goes by too slow when you don’t see the one you wanna be with. Anthony nods as he would seem lost in the glimmering eyes of Michelle. He would sit there looking at her smiling, yet hiding behind that smile would be a feeling of guilt. A feeling of he is doing wrong. He thinks of what Jessica Richards is probably doing back at their home. Clark: No kidding. I mean it feels like I haven’t seen you in a decade. Jordan: Oh I agree with you. I mean it feels like it’s been over a century since we worked together in the old feds. Clark nods his head slowly as he’d think bout the old times, but the thoughts are soon interrupted of the thoughts of Jessica joining him at the ring currently. Clark: You should get back into the wrestling business. Jordan: You know I’m working on my modeling these days. A slight chuckle from Anthony as Michelle laughs about it as well. Jordan: So I saw you at the Pay Per View, that was a shame that you came up short like that. But hey you proved to Brian Michaels during the main event you had his back. Clark: That I did, that I did. It’s just a shame he well kinda left. I mean I thought I found my new mentor. Someone to teach me the ropes of what I was missing when it came to pro wrestling. Jordan: Well you still have you old trainers around with you there at the company. Anthony grabs his drink and pulls a gulp from the straw. Clark: Oh yeah, my two former trainers who have been destroying each other. Yeah there really isn’t much help coming from there at all. I’m pretty much a loner now these days. I haven’t heard from the Michaels Inc guys. Who knows if we are together anymore. I mean what’s a group when there isn’t even a leader to help you out. We are a bunch of young kids with no leadership. I mean don’t get me wrong. I could probably lead these kids. Michelle smirks and laughs slightly. Jordan: Still a cocky as always. You were always so sure of yourself. Even when we were kids on the playground. Clark smiles and lets out a laugh himself. Clark: No surprise there, right? Jordan: Not in the slightest bit babe. Clark: So how’s the modeling job? Michelle leans in close. Jordan: I’m gonna be real with you; it sucks. All I really am is some piece of ass for guys to look at in skimpy outfits all day long. Anthony smirks and shakes his head. Clark: You dug your grave babe, you should of finished college. Or you shouldn’t of left me back in the day to go on to pursuit modeling. Jordan: Yeah, yeah, fuck off. Even with the tone Michelle used, Clark can’t help but to still laugh. Michelle looks down at her drink, toying with the straw. Jordan: Do you remember the old days? You know, when I used to manage you. Clark: yeah, can’t forget ‘em. I mean those are back in the day when my career got its start. Jordan: Ain’t that the truth. Hey, remember that one match where I had that guy right in my grasp? Clark: Michelle, you never did shit that involved beating a guy. You usually just distracted them with your looks to be honest. The two would chuckle some as they think bout it. Jordan: Oh yeah, I was. Oh, hey, remember that match when I did that splash on... Clark: Michelle, you never did shit from the top rope either. Jordan: Hey, I used to kick some of the girls asses outside the ring! She slams her fist on the table, but then her face eases a grins. Jordan: I could slap some bitches pretty hard. She laughs as Anthony shakes his head. Jordan: Oh c’mon, don’t tell me you’re still holding that stupid grudge. Anthony sizes her with his eyes. Clark: Stupid… grudge? You mean, that “stupid grudge” where you told me you were going to go drinking with the girls because you had a party, then you went out and got totally fucking annihilated before one of my matches, and no showed on me? That stupid grudge, or the “stupid grudge” where you got my best friend drunk and fucked him. Tell me Michelle, what “stupid grudge” is it? Michelle’s taken back. Reality sheaths her eyes. She would say then, trying to break the tension with sarcasms. Jordan: Wow… I was gettin’ laid when I drank? That’s no where near what happens now when I drink. Clark: Oh fuck you, Jordan! That’s why it’s been years since I’ve seen your ass, and why I was scared to see you again. Jordan: Hey, fuck you! I drank because our relationship was falling apart, okay? Not all of us deal with stress through exercise and whipping our own asses like you. And as for your best friend, is it my fault he was a horny bastard? Don’t hold that to me, dick. Clark: So let me get this straight; you’re justifying your alcoholism with stress, which your alcoholism is what put you in the middle of your world which most people would refer to as “rock bottom”, and now my best friend is a “horny bastard” because you got him drunk when I was out of town working a show and you, perfectly sober for once, took advantage. Is that what you’re saying? Michelle goes to talk, but she cuts herself off. Calmly, she replies. Jordan: The past is the past, Anthony. Let that shit go, babe. What happened happened, and I’m a changed woman now. I’m not some wild chick anymore. I’m gonna be honest with you here… I called you because I want to make a return to wrestling. You know, get back together. I’ve been working on my skills, and you have an easy in for me. We can... Clark: You know what I have at risk if I do this. My entire world could be rocked from this. Jessica would find out bout all this. And you want to put all my money making on the line! Jordan: Whoa, YOUR world? Clark: Yeah, MY world! That ring is my life, this business is my sanctuary. I don’t know if I can risk that. I need some time to think about this Michelle. Jordan: Anthony, listen to me... Clark: No, YOU listen to ME! You disgraced the trust I had in you. You know how hard it was for me to see you last time. Even though I wanted to badly. I can’t let what you did to me happen again Michelle till I know I can fully trust you. You know how hard it is for me to let the past be the past with Xavier after you slept with him. Michelle shakes her head, sighing. Jordan: It was worth a shot, eh? Clark: I guess... Just give me some time...I got enough on my mind, okay? I mean this match coming up is a big match for me. There is history in this match, there is future at stake in this match. I mean I’m going up against an old locker room buddy, and well a man I have face before. Jordan: Yeah I saw you are going up against CSB this week. That’s gotta be tough for you, I mean it makes you think bout the old days. Clark: Yeah... Back in the old GWC days... The man has a lot of talent. He has a point bout how he made a revolution with that damn GWC Ultimate title. But what the man needs to realize is, I was facing the best in that division when I had that title. I was taking on the likes of Jamal Carter, The Emperor, Steve Carr, and Random McRally. Hell I defended that title against Zak Warner in probably one of the bloodiest matches in history there. The light tube scares still cover my body. The scares from the staples on my body. Yet I came out victorious. CSB isn’t a threat to me. Jordan: He won the titles when the company was at its down fall. Clark: Don’t get me wrong. I respect what he has done. The credentials are there. I can’t take that away from him. No one can. But the man is just like everyone else to me. He is someone who once had spot lights shining, and he needs it to shine on him again to prove he can still hang with the big boys. But I haven’t seen this man in the ring in a long time. Now he can be running fed to fed. But who knows. But all I really know is this man can be covered in ring rust. Yet again he is just like the others in the company. Jordan: What do you mean by that? Clark: His history. He grew up on the rough streets of London. Everyone is always from “Rough Streets”. I know I said the same thing bout Chris England, but it’s true. All these guys are either dark creepy dudes, or they had a rough living on the streets. Look I’m from the suburbs of Boston, now living in a plush area in Los Angeles. I admit it. I’m not some street thug who had to grow up tough. I had it easy as a kid. I’m tired of these guys trying to be intimidating. Quit trying to be a hard ass, and just use your fucking talent. Jordan: Are you even worried bout Devlin? Clark: I met him in the ring before. He really isn’t a threat to me. He wasn’t last time, and he won’t be this time. Nothing bout Dev will change for me. Aperently the kid had potential, and never lived up to it. The only thing I have to worry bout Dev is not becoming live Dev. I can’t blow away all my potential these people are saying I have. I need to keep striving for the top of this company. And I think I can finally do that. Jordan: I really think I can help you out, you just gotta trust me again. Clark would put his head down not sure what to say. Michelle keeps quiet. She then realizes the bridges she’s burned as she sees the fire in Anthony’s eyes. Jordan: I won’t keep you, you can go. Anthony looks at her, there is no answer in his eyes. He gets up from the table, walking towards the door with a certain confusion in his swagger and his eyes seem blank showing signs of even more confusion. The camera sits on Michelle, who looks back down to her drink, stirring it with her straw. This is when things begin to take a dive southward. From behind, Anthony is focused on the back of his head, staring a hole right through it. A waitress walks in front of him, Anthony’s eyes shift downward. He stops the waitress, stealing the empty platter from her hands. He clutches it tightly and with full force, swings for the ground, sending the food all over the floor. The stress of his life falling apart is starting to catch up on young Anthony. Clark fixates on the chaos as everyone gasps and makes commotion, rushing to help the waitress clean it all up. While their attention is diverted, Anthony back steps, slipping out the door with a tainted smile on his face. Michelle’s face is showing signs of worry. She knows Anthony is slowly spinning out of control with his life. His love status is a mess, his trainers are fighting one another, his third trainer he hasn’t heard from in years, and his newest mentor bails on him when he needed him the most. No one is sure on what will happen to Anthony Clark... The End.
