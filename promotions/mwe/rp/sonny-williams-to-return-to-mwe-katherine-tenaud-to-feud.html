Sonny Williams to return to MWE? Katherine Tenaud to feud with Paige?

Jun 19 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hey everyone, I'm Zack Kash with your bi-daily MWE Rumor Mill...here's what I'm being told is going on backstage at MWE. Sonny Williams making a possible return - Word going around is that Sonny Williams is expected to walk back into MWE at any point in time, and his Hall of Fame spot could be in jeopardy if he does, as the Front Office wants all the Hall-Of-Famers to be retired. If this does go down, expect a feud with Sinc Mercier heading into Crowning Glory, potentally for the MWE Championship. Also, anticipate them (MWE) to hype up the fact that Sinc and Sonny both climbed up the entire card. However, word going around the wrestling business is that Williams is making a return for ANOTHER company, and that could damage his chances of keeping his HOF spot as well. Katherine Tenaud to come back and feud with Paige - A feud that the MWE brass have wanted to see for a long time is one between former Women's Champions, Paige and Katherine Tenaud. KT is tight backstage with both D.C. and Brian Michaels, and both are lobbying for the company to make an aggressive push to sign her, as they too feel that Paige vs KT would be a big draw. Add that to the already seemingly-booming card for CG. With Jesse Anderson, David Whitman, and Alejandro Rodriguez all expected back at that time, things will certainly heat up in August for MWE. Brian Michaels to job his last five matches? - Word is, Brian Michaels has been humbled and wants to put any "mid-carders or rookies" over that he thinks deserve it before he goes. Hmm. Very unlike him. Some might say I have an agenda against Michaels, but I'm just going by his past. Anyway, thanks for reading the first ever MWE Rumor Mill. Stay tuned, as our pre-ID spoilers will be up here on saturday night.
