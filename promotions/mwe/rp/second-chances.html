Second Chances

Sep 6 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
*James Sullivan is seen wearing a hooded sweatshirt while riding a a exercise bicycle in front of a television screen. He is in his mansion's living room with Stephen sitting on the couch to the right of him. Mayhem Wrestling Entertainment cameras swing around to the front of the television, only to discover that the program Sullivan was watching was his opponent's promo from earlier in the week. Kanto must have just uttered something that had some meaning as Sullivan stopped riding his stationary bike. Sullivan: Quick, Stephen, pause the video. Great, now rewind until I stay stop. *The tape rewound until about 15 seconds earlier. Stephen held up a finger, signaling that he found the spot he wanted. Stephen pressed play, and Sullivan and Arthur watched with great curiosity.* Kanto: James Sullivan. You've felt my wrath once before. Having to suffer through it twice? That's a death sentence. Poor unfortunate soul. *Stephen handed over the remote control to Sullivan on command, and Sullivan played it back once more. He smiled with glee as he watched it the second time. Stephen was confused, as Sullivan saw, and proceeded to announce his thoughts.* Sullivan: Ok, so you may be wondering why I am so happy. Kanto is undefeated, scary, twisted, and overall...a badass. However, I smile because he says that I am an unfortunate soul. For two reasons, is that statement incorrect. Number One, I am going to compete in the United States Championship final at Turmoil. I beat Aiden Copane, like I said I would, and now I am in the my first championship match. How in the world I could be unfortunate, I have no idea. Actually Kanto, I know you reasoning. Which brings me to my next reason. Number Two, I get a chance to prove either how weak or strong I really am. And in more than one way. If I lose the will to fight, my spirit will be weak. If I am overpowered, than my strength attribute is weak, and if I am humiliated than I have weak charisma. However, if I win, I prove that my spirit is as strong as anyone's, my strength will still reign supreme, and on top of it all, my charisma will stay in tact. *Sullivan brushes off a few beads of sweat and takes off his workout clothes. He sprays himself once with some Axe, and lays his clothes on the floor. He moves the exercise bicycle out of the way, and puts back a chair that once sat in the very same spot. He quickly got himself a drink, and sat down.* Sullivan: Listen Kanto, you are creepy, and your promo gave me some chills. You are a force to be reckoned with, considering you haven't lost a match. And you are somewhat of a badass, not many people can or will do what you do. But what you have to realize, is that I am getting a second chance. It will not be a second beat down, or a second massacre and or a second match of misery. In fact, I will consider it fun and a challenge to step back into the ring with you again. Personally, I love competing and you are probably the biggest challenge I could ask for. *Sullivan searches around a nearby table, and finds his watch and cell phone, which he pockets. Slowly, he takes a sip of his drink, and gives a refreshing "ahh" sound, which shows that he is enjoying himself at the moment.* Sullivan: You see that noise can be taken in a couple of ways. One way, of course is the most obvious, in being I enjoyed my drink. A second way it can be taken is that I am being cocky, and only making those noises to brag in my promo. However, those are both incorrect. In fact, it is a third way, that I am sure you would have never guessed. See, I am just so happy to get another crack at you. Even if I get my ass handed to me...again, I will enjoy this match. See while you are creepy and do send chills down my neck, you don't scare me with your promos. Blood is just something that comes out of a cut. A broken bone is just something that happens when you get hurt. Something that can be fixed. It is also something that will make you stronger. And silence, well, silence isn't scary at all. In fact, sometimes silence is nice. No one, no sounds, just you and your thoughts. You get to thinking, whether it be a strategy for a match, your financial a simple joke to get some laughs. So while I watched your promo, it kind of pleased me. I see now that you are a man of very few words. *Stephen got up off the sofa and got himself a drink. He whispered something out the side of his mouth to Sullivan, and proceeded to walk upstairs away from Sullivan and the MWE cameras.* Sullivan: Now, are match this week, enough said, is going to be a good one. And you can keep talking about me and silence, because it might work, but I have to move on. And moving on is good. Well, I mentioned my match at Turmoil, our next Pay-Per-View. It will be my first Pay-Per-View match since signing with MWE and it will be for the Mayhem Wrestling Entertainment United States Championship. Who will I face though? It would have been nice to know last week, but I will live. Sinc Mercier or Alex Bowen. Who will it be to face me for the MWE US Title? Sinc, you rave and rant about how Alex Bowen is a freak, how he is nothing for you and he stands no chance. And Alex, you talk about how you will win the gold because Sinc Mercier is like a spider. What does this do for me? Why do I always take the backseat. What do I have to do for people to finally realize that I like to compete, and I like to win. Honestly, I will give you guys the same advice I gave Aiden Copane, I am not a weak superstar. I will hit hard and hit fast. Muhammad Ali once said, "Float like a butterfly, sting like a bee," and that applies to me as well. And hopefully, one of you two can live up to the challenge that Aiden gave me, because if not, consider your chances pretty much gone. Texas Hold Em' is a game of great skill and little luck. You have to look at the odds, and if the odds are against you fold. You look at football, you will never see the Miami Dolphins conquer the New England Patriots or the New York Giants, because the odds are against them. The better team will win, and if you guys can not come of with the same challenge Aiden gave me, then you will be the Dolphins and I will be the Giants. Turmoil is right around the corner, and I am ready. I am not scared of you, Alex Bowen or you Sinc Mercier. Same applies for you Kanto, I am not scared. I show no fear, and when you show no fear...you can't be scared! ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Last minute Role-Play, sorry Kanto. I would have loved to give you more time, but I lost track of time and date with school starting for me this week. So, again sorry, and good luck to you Sinc and you Alex.
