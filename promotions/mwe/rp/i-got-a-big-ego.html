It is sunrise, at around 6:00 in the morning. The camera pans from the beautiful, orange-filled sunrise, to the local folks who are doing the usual brisk early morning walk. Dressed in their black workout pants and matching upper-torso, they look like full-blown athletes. Meanwhile, the camera pans to a local wrestling school. Instead of being mats set up, there is a classroom, a typical one at that. The teacher’s name, Kendra Rellington, is written on the chalkboard in large letters. Immediately, the wrestlers recognize her as Sinc Mercier’s old girlfriend and Nate Rellington’s sister. The seats are filled with mainly high-school wrestlers, all looking to get their chance at the big leagues. They are dressed in the gear, as they anticipated being put into an actual match, not in a class. At the back of the room sits Brian Michaels, dressed like a rookie, wearing his in-ring attire. His hair is slicked back as the luscious teacher steps into the room, dressed in a business outfit. Michaels: Look guys, she’s already getting ready… After another chuckle, the teacher shakes her head. Trying to ignore the famous wrestler who obviously does not want to be there, she puts down the ruler and begins writing on the board, making a list of wrestling moves in one column. Popular names such as the Pedigree and the Killer Classic come up, before a ringer is heard playing in the background. Kendra: STOP TEXTING!
