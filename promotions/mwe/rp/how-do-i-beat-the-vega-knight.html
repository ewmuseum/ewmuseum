How do I beat the Vega Knight?; A JJ Executioner RP

Oct 19 2008

JJ Executioner

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
*Please remember, that in a previous match featuring JJ Executioner, we discovered his past. We found he had been bullied and as a result, took action on those who were persecuting him. This is just a little more backstory.* Clunk The hollow sound of thick metal chains resound on concrete. Two feet struggling awkwardly to walk forward in tandem, bound by a chain between ankles. Black shoes, black trousers. A booming voice is heard. It is serious and direct in tone, only interrupted with the rhythmic clunking of the chains against the concrete. Voice: “On five counts of serious assault with deadly intent, I find you guilty. *clunk* You are sentenced indefinitely to stay in the highest possible security prison possible due to being a dangerous risk to society. We will make a decision imminently.*clunk*In the meantime you will be granted no civil or social privileges *clunk*” The loud impact of metal slamming. A key turning, scraping, a rusty lock slides, grinds into place. Then silence. And darkness. Deathly quiet. We see a long corridor. There are narrow windows to the left and to the right; a number of horizontal and vertical metal bars lay across jail cells. Dull grey concrete embodies everything else and long shadows cover the length of the floor. A man appears out of a door, which he allows to slam behind him. The echo resonates deeply. He looks to be about fifty years old, with a slightly greying mop of thick dark brown hair. He is dressed in a sharp, grey pinstripe suit on top of a crisp white shirt and a royal blue tie. He walks without any impediment and stands upright with authority. He hastily makes his down the corridor towards the camera. He stops just in front of the camera and turns to the right, facing in towards the cell. He looks uncomfortable and apprehensive, as if a man of his stature should not be in a place like this. Voice: “What are you doing? A place like this could get you killed. Is that what you fucking want?” The voice growls. The camera moves forward ever so slightly. Man: “Cease your insubordination immediately. Your attitude to seniority is what put you in here” A pause. Man: “You serious assaulted five fellow pupils at your school. Two are on life support. Two have severe brain damage. And the other, well he won’t walk again. You are lucky to have received the punishment that you did, especially given the choice of victims. You had to choose the sons of the five most powerful families in the town didn’t you?” Another pause. Man: “You deserve nothing less than to spend your life behind bars for your acts, truly reprehensible, with no motive, no reasoning. For in this instance, justice has been served.” Voice: “Your self-congratulatory tone is riling and your arrogance is astounding and sickening.” Man: “Thank you.” A sly smile crosses the slightly wrinkled features. A thick wad of phlegm flies out from in between the bars. It lands square on the left cheek before dripping off slightly. The old man pulls a handkerchief from his inside pocket and wipes it away without hesitation. Voice: “That was not a compliment.” The camera moves forward. “Trust me. If I could, I would strangle the life out of you.” A pair and lips protrude out through the bars. The camera turns. Standing behind the barred structure is a young man. He is tall and quite muscular with pale white skin and black, greasy, shoulder length hair. He holds onto two bars with shovel-like hands and stands about six inches above the head of the man in front of him. Man: “You think that you deserve to get out for what you did. Your unprovoked assaults, complete denial of any blame and the serious injuries that you have inflicted means there was no alternative.” Prisoner: “If you expect me to say sorry......” Man: “I know that you’re incapable of meaning that!” Prisoner: “Then what do you fucking expect of me old man?” Man: “I expect you to finish the damn job next time!!!!” This heated exchange of words has brought the old man closer to the cell bars. He turns away and rearranges his tie. Man: “You have all the potential in the world but you will rot to your death in this place.” Prisoner: “Why do you care?!” The man turns around. Man: “I hate to see abilities like yours go to waste. You have a canny knack for causing extreme pain. So you are much better suited to prevention as opposed to pure revenge. I would like to recommend a career path, that is all.” Prisoner: “What? FBI?!” Man: “Nothing so official; more of a social cause. Thousands of people can boo you. But in your hands, a title belt is raised above your head; you have just become a champion.” The prisoner turns away. Man: “You are a remarkable physical specimen. You have all the tools you need.” Prisoner: “I am under no one’s control or influence. You will not earn my respect. I have none to give.” Man: “All I suggest is that we help to solve each other’s mutual problems. I can resolve yours by turning a key. You can help me but it will take longer, much longer. You are capable of building a legacy, you can be dominant. All I ask is that I get the opportunity to show what I have to offer.” The picture fades slowly. As it does, we hear a rusty lock grind itself open, followed by the high pitched screech of the bars sliding open. The picture comes back up again to show the giant MWE superstar JJ Executioner sitting in his locker room. There is a small amount of natural light coming in from outside but the room is generally very dark and JJ’s features are just about visible. JJ: “Every one of us has their own reasons for being here. I have been very clear with my motives. I stand you before as someone who is determined to destroy those who infect those in my company. I took out James Sync and James Sullivan with ease. They were simple creatures who posed little resistance.” JJ stands up by the lockers. JJ: “But now, four shows into my career in the MWE and I am faced with a problem so complex I cannot figure it out.....how do I destroy the Vega Knight?” JJ punches the locker in frustration. JJ: “His ego is unbefitting of someone of his ability, yet he has somehow managed to avoid the same fate as everyone else who has crossed me. And he holds the X TV title. It is surely only a matter of time before I beat him.” Another locker is punched. JJ: “Knight seems to be so proud of what he has accomplished in the past but in truth he has done naught but gain criminally overrated self belief. He is a man who so proudly admits to murder. Someone who claims to have had the best training around but goes out picks fights with drunken hobos in preparation for the biggest match of his career, which he fucking wins!” JJ punches the door again then rips it off the hinges, throwing it across the room. It hits the wall and then comes crashing to the floor. JJ: “Well I have had fucking enough of Vega Knight. I have had enough of his bitching about me and my life. Despite all of his personal privileges in life, he is a more miserable, depraved, filthy piece of shit than I could ever be! I don’t claim to be perfect Vega. I have made it clear where my origins lie. I have suffered in ways that I would not wish to bestow on my enemies. But now I consider it a personal pleasure, a duty from which I will not falter, to take you out of the MWE and give the X TV title a champion that it deserves.” JJ pulls a rope out of the locker. He begins to wrap it around his fist. JJ: “You claim to have been trained by Randy and Leon Roberts. Your actions harm their legacy through your recklessness. You are not fit to be in the MWE, let alone wear championship gold. You are a cancer, affecting everything and everyone around you to their downfall. It is simply not acceptable anymore. You know why.......you’re a joke Vega, a joke so poor and downright obnoxious that it is not funny. You are not funny, not to me......not to anyone.” “I have been unleashed on the MWE to make sure people like you do not last in this business. So I promise to live up to my original vow, I will be the one to take the Vega Knight out of the MWE. I will be the one to end your X TV title reign. This will be the beginning of my legacy” JJ steps towards the camera and pushes it out of the way. “Now get the fuck out of my way.”
