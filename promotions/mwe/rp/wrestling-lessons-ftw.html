Wrestling Lessons FTW!

Sep 7 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
*The scene opens up to show Christina Darby wearing pink workout pants with a black tank top driving her car. She looks at the mirror, admiring her facial features while a car nearly bumps her. The camera pans over to Brian Michaels’, whose hair is covering his eyes, seemingly sweating thinking Christina will crash. He runs his hands through his hair, pushing it back. He is wearing a necklace with an X on it, and a blue Brian Michaels shirt. Christina sighs in annoyance.* Christina: Why don’t you ever trust me? Jeez, I said I’d keep my eyes on the road after I did a quick facial check. Michaels (whispering): I’ll show you a facial… Christina: Excuse me? Michaels: I said nothing that could’ve been sexually used against me. Now, keep your eyes on the road and listen. *Christina shrugs and turns on to the intersection, swerving right next to a GMC Yukon Denali. Michaels puts his hands on his head and shakes it.* Michaels: Okay, so the first thing is, ALWAYS keep your eyes on your opponent. Like, I don’t care if Lindsey’s got the biggest Christina: Or fakest… Michaels: Tits in the World, you FOCUS on that bitch…and you attack. Attack her like you attacked me that one-time when I came home with lipstick on my shirt. Christina: Where were you that night again? Michaels: I told you, I was attacked by a bunch of drunk moms at a bar and they wouldn’t let me go. There’s only so much a wrestler can do, ya know? Anyway, you keep your eyes on Lindsey, and I’ll make sure D.C. doesn’t pull his shit, got it? *Christina gives Michaels a knowing look and Michaels points at her to look at the road.* Christina: What’s with you being all secretive about D.C.? I mean, are you scared? Michaels: Scared? Christina: Yeah, you jealous that D.C. can pack more heat than you can? Michaels: Listen, the Taco Bell dog’s BITCH….packs more heat than D.C. *Christina chuckles to herself as she turns into a Diary Queen. She looks at Michaels with a questioning look in her eyes, wondering why they’re there. Michaels puts his hands up in defense and unbuckles his seat belt, walking out of the car. Christina follows suit as they walk into the Burger King, looking around at the empty fast food restaurant. They see a black-haired man cleaning the tables. Michaels whistles at the man, who doesn’t budge. Michaels shrugs.* *The man looks back, suddenly stopping. Christina elbows Michaels in the abs, causing him to give a grunt. Christina inches toward the other man with a kind look on her face.* Christina: Um, excuse me, I was told by a friend that a certain Lindsey used to work here? *The man looks down, seemingly shameful. He then looks up and shows off his rather large mustache. He walks behind the register and puts on a sombrero.* Cashier: Welcome to Burger Kin, how may I hell you? *Christina and Brian slowly back away and take a look at each other.* Christina: We just want to know if Lindsey used to work here. Cashier: Oh-ho! Lindsee…Lindsee Lowhand?! *Michaels makes a cut-throat gesture and shakes his head at the cashier who looks clueless. Michaels hangs his head in disappointment as Christina raises her eyebrows in awe.* Christina: No…Lindsey, the one who became wrestler? Cashier: Oooh yes, she like peanuts. Michaels: The hell? Peanuts? *The cashier nods, beginning to walk to the kitchen. He gestures to Christina and Brian to go back there and takes out a framed picture. The camera doesn’t show the picture itself, but shows Brian and Christina’s facial expressions. Brian’s jaw drops and Christina rolls her eyes, scoffing in the process.* Michaels: How many can she fit in there?! Cashier: Uno, dos, tres! (One, two, three) *Michaels gestures at Lindsey in a sarcastic manner.* Michaels: Jeez, why can’t you do that? *Michaels chuckles and gives a sly grin as Christina shakes her head in disbelief. Lindsey walks up to the man in the sombrero and gives him a vicious slap across the face. She waddles off in a huff, beginning to walk back to their car.* Christina: Brian… *Michaels hangs his head low and walks out of the kitchen, moaning.* Michaels: Fine. *They walk back into the car and back out of the Burger King parking lot. They go back into the street and are waiting on a red light.* Michaels: So rule number two, no bitching towards the referee. This isn’t the mall, there’s no refunds in Pro Wrestling. *Christina nods as she begins to tune the radio, checking for stations. Michaels puts his hand over hers and shakes his head. She hangs her head.* Christina: So is that it? Two rules? Focus and no bitching? *Michaels puts a hand on his chin, pondering his answer.* Michaels: Pretty much. Christina: So, can I talk now? Michaels: Go ahead. *Christina runs her hand through her blonde hair in Brian Michaels-esque fashion and throws her gum out the window. She gets on a pissy face and looks at the camera menacingly.* Christina: Lindsey....I am so...sick...of people like you. You talk and talk...and you ain't done nothin' bitch! You call ME a whore? *Christina gives a little chuckle and leans forward a bit, slapping her face ever-so-slightly.* Christina: Let me guess...to Lindsey and D.C., I'm some little girl-next-door who can't do anything, right? Lindsey, I'm done with insults. When we get into the ring, I'm gonna show you what I can do, you emo BITCH! *Christina flips off the camera as the scene begins to fade away. Brian is heard laughing as the scene goes to static.* OOC: I know, not much talk on Lindsey
