Liked By Few Hated By Many Respected By All; Project Revolution

Sep 7 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Camera shows from far away a man sitting in a brown two man bench observing things around him camera get closer and closer towards him a Budweiser can is seen next to him lying. As the camera got close enough you noticed that man was American Animal. Animal stares at the camera with a much serious grim in his face and picks up his beer for a drink. Animal: You see this can right here it will always be my friend because it never answers back yet it has the power to still get something out of me. And I like that because it means it isn’t a fool it gets two bucks out of me every time I buy it. Animal then throws the can towards behind me without a care then looks to his side noticing a couple of twins. Animal: Richard Covell called me yesterday explaining to me a couple of things and announcing my next match against the Hollywood Hooligans with the debut of my new stable Project Revolution as I team up with fear. Animal with a hopeful look in his face stares at the camera again. Animal: The Hollywood Hooligans I cant believe I American Animal have to vs. the Hollywood Hooligans it could actually only be a fare match if it would be me vs. them too but ill take this week off with this match if you know what I mean me and fear are unstoppable and we are going up against a couple of twins who should have become only one to keep up with my kind of society. Animal angrily shoves the camera back the person holding it gets up from the floor from its own help. Animal: Last week was a challenge only because my opponent came with in a opponent with great power and great agility he is part of this great stable Project Revolution B.G. Mancini. Animal: what you didn’t see last week you will see this week in a better more fancier view. Animal: The Revolution Starts Now And Nobody Will Be Able To Stop It. Animal then punches the camera and turns it off pressing the switch. PS: Big Ace you know what happened as for others this RP was wrote in 10 minutes.
