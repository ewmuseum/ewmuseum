Borderline Crazy; Jekyl RP

May 2 2009

Jekyl

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Scotty and Jekyl are seen walking down an empty nearly abandoned road, the odd car is parked on either side of the street, the streetlights illuminate the road and parts of the sidewalk...it's warm. The alluring warm weather brings about an emotion of calm, but the awareness of danger in the darkness. Passing in and out from light to dark, Scotty and Jekyl continue up the street, walking in and out of the streetlight's dull glow, their voices become clearer... Jekyl: So what made you instantly tell me about your “situation”? That's not something you just spit out randomly to save your ass... Scotty looks over at Jekyl with a kind of embarrassed look. As the two men get closer we see that Scotty is wearing an open black with white trim jersey style shirt, the lettering on the left breast pocket reads “MWE” in red and white, he is wearing black denim jeans, and black and white Etnies shoes; Jekyl is wearing his standard outfit of purple velvet dress jacket, light blue dress shirt with green and purple vest, and purple dress pants. They turn and begin walking to this warehouse which looks completely abandoned... Scotty: Well Sky was the one who told me to tell you, and I figured if we were going to be partners, I should show my trust... Jekyl: Seriously? A voice in your head told you to trust me.....? Scotty: Yes....as a matter of fact she did, her reasoning was the same as mine... Jekyl: Whatever...this looks like a good enough place to shoot my next promo....lets go.. These 2 deranged men head towards a broken down warehouse when Scotty stops in his tracks, Jekyl turns around and sees that his partner has a blank expression on his face, perfectly still with clenched fists, unaware of what's going on inside Scotty's head. – – – – Sky: Something evil comes near... Scotty looks to his left and realizes he is no longer in plain reality, he is in the reality of his mind and standing in front of him is a body to the voice, a woman he has seen and spoken to many times before. Wearing a short black corset, fishnet stockings with a medium length black skirt, long white hair that reaches the mid of her back, and finally a blue sapphire pendant with gold trim dangles round her neck. Scotty: I hear it.... it is coming from behind us! What is it? Sky: Hell hounds..... they are after us, quickly, run for that building ahead.... – – – – Scotty: Get in the warehouse..... NOW!!! Scotty regains his state of mind and grabs Jekyl by the shoulders, a worried look on his face. Without any real questioning Jekyl follows Scotty into the warehouse, as the door swings violently shut, years of dust rises from the ground and into the air. Jekyl: What the hell man? Whats your issue? Scotty locks the doors and walks backwards, keeping a trained eye on the warehouse doors, waiting for an inevitable bang on the steel doors. – – – – Sky: We are safe, I have set up a barrier outside to the hounds may not enter for a while... Damien is trying to scare them off... Scotty: Good, so we can continue our promo in peace? Sky: Yes... I will leave you be for now... – – – – Jekyl Walks over to the nearest wall and finds a metal box which has had the label ripped off or eaten away by time. Without any form of worry, Jekyl opens up the box and pulls a big switch, the lights around the warehouse immediately illuminates in a glow that had not been seen in several years. Scotty: Go ahead man.. Do yer thing! Jekyl: Right... well to start off, next week I face off against one of the cockiest, ignorant, dumbest sons 'a' bitches I have ever heard of... this man Chris England thinks he can come into MY ring and think because hes Australian he is greater than a--- Scotty: British.... Jekyl: Wha?! Scotty: Chris England is British, he's from the UK... Jekyl: Oh....Either way... They can both lick my sweaty saggy balls....As I was saying, he thinks he is greater than a pure blooded red, white and blue American, he needs to look in a fucking history book and find out who saved the British in all the wars...Aside from the Canadians...To hell with them and their damn igloo's.... Scotty: They don't live in- Jekyl: STOP CORRECTING ME AND LET ME FINISH MY DAMN PROMO! Scotty gives Jekyl a look of disgust and begins to pace around the large empty warehouse. Jekyl looks around and spots a set of stairs leading to a second floor and without hesitation, he heads towards them. Dust kicks up with each step, the old metal creaks with each pound of pressure put down with each foot, the stairs begin to sway to the left and right with every step. Scotty follows with the curiosity of a 6 year old of what the second floor contains. Jekyl: You see Chris, I have been through a lot in my time, and you may have been the EWL US champ, and a four time EWL tag team champion, but to me, that means absolutely nothing. I don't know what EWL is, and I don't care to find out, You are in MWE and I am personally going to show you what MWE is all about. Your accomplishments in any other company mean nothing once you step in an MWE ring with me, cause once that moment becomes a reality, you become a rookie, a jobber with little experience. Scotty enters one of the side rooms, exploring to see what he can find of interest, or maybe following the voices in his head. Jekyl heads to a door with a corroded metal plate that says... "Head Office" Without hesitation he quickly turns the knob and opening the door. The room is about sixteen feet by 20 feet, a big office with a wooden desk covered with age, a padded chair, torn, tattered and covered in dust. Scotty pokes his head into the room and looks around and immediately sees a portrait on the far wall to his left, it has pictures, plaques and shelves arranged in a random display, but one picture stands out above the rest. Scotty: Oh my god! I knew I recognized this place! Jekyl: What do you mean? Scotty: That picture is my dad! Jekyl: Seriously? Your not yanking my chain? Scotty: I'm not kidding, by the looks of it, this was my dad's factory, but what was made here I haven't gotten the foggiest idea... Jekyl: Odd... anyways, back to business. Chris England, just around the beginning of March you were kicked out of Ireland and then bitched out some random dude to pick your poor, sorry ass up out back of a football stadium, then willingly got in the back inside a cargo hold? Trapped like a pathetic animal, no food, stuck in a box with little holes and the connecting you to the outside world is your cell phone. Scotty: I can't believe what a dump this place turned into... I wish i knew what they did here... Jekyl fires a grim look at Scotty, but then thinking about his question, it makes him begin to think... If This was McKnight Seniors old warehouse, what was held here... what did they do? The answer would soon be revealed. Jekyl: Scotty... Why don't you, those voices in your head, and whatever else you see or hear, find another room, AND COLLECT YOUR THOUGHTS THERE!!! I'm trying to make this god forsaken promo, and I can't do that with you constantly interrupting me all the time... Jesus fucking Christ in a hand basket.... Scotty: Excuse me? This is MY dad's warehouse, why don't YOU find yourself walking down those rickety stairs, and finishing your promo in the sanctity of the EMPTY FUCKING WAREHOUSE?!?! Jekyl kind of staggers back in his mind that Scotty has not only defended himself verbally, but has raised his voice and showed a disregard for any form of pain he might receive from his tag team partner. Jekyl then settles and walks out of the office and down the stairs into the empty space. He looks up from the floor and see's the window where the head office is. All of a sudden the window smashes as the padded chair flies through the window, sending glass flying everywhere, while the chair hits the ground with a hard bang, remaining unbroken. Scotty clears the rest of the glass and leans out the office window. Scotty: THERE, HAVE A FUCKING SEAT! Jekyl keeps his arms over his head while random pieces of glass continue to fall. The last piece hits the ground as Jekyl looks up with a look of satisfaction, anger, and surprise. Jekyl: Thanks! I was getting tired of standing on this dirty ground anyway........ you gypsy.... Picking up the chair, he sits on it and tilts his head backwards in deep thought. Jekyl: I remember that match you had... You tagged up with Eric Derf against Semi and Alex Bowen... And if i remember correctly, you got destroyed in that match, the only reason you won was because you got a face full of chair by Semi, and even then you were out cold. Also if i remember correctly, you said, and i quote "everyone at some point in their career gets a lucky break from time to time"... Jekyl Rubs his chin in satisfaction as a smile cracks across his pale white face, his teeth begin to show as his smile grows bigger by each second of thought. Jekyl: Every one at some point in their career gets a lucky break from time to time... and yours just ended. The stairs creak and moan as Scotty heads down the stairs in a hurry while Jekyl continues. Scotty stands close, waiting to talk to Jekyl, making sure not to rudely interrupt again and piss his partner off. Jekyl: Plain and simple Chris, I am going to rip out your gasteral intestinal tract, use it as a condom, AND FORNICATE WITH YOUR SKULL! Scotty: Your gona what to his skull? Jekyl shoots a look over to Scotty, his smile is sadistic and twisted, eyes almost wide with enraged excitement. Jekyl: I'M GOING TO SKULL FUCK HIM! Scotty: Interesting... Well uhm, I found something out about this place. Jekyl sighs, his temper drops a few notches, as he listens to what Scotty has discovered. Scotty lifts up a beige scroll and begins to smile. Jekyl: I'm assuming those are blueprints? Scotty: Yeah... but can you guess at what they are blueprints to? Jekyl: The Apollo 13? JUST TELL ME! Scotty unravels the roll of paper, careful not to tear or rip it, it reveals to be blueprints for some sort of nuclear bomb from the 1950's. Jekyl: Where did you find this? Scotty: It was in a drawer and it's German... you think this could be one of the blueprints Hitler claimed to have of his atomic bomb? Jekyl: Probably.... hold onto that, we will look at it later, I still have things to do here... Scotty: Ok, I'm gona look around some more, see what else I can find. Scotty begins searching around some more, while in the meantime Jekyl rocks back in forth in the tattered office chair, dust slowly falls from the newly opened crevices of his seat. Scotty rummages through some lockers looking for some more information about his father, or maybe even something about this building, nothing is certain. Jekyl: Chris, if you wanna toss around =your accomplishments in other companies than I will toss my accomplishments here in MWE... I may not have been a champion, or held gold, but I have defeated 2 of the biggest superstars here in this company, something you could never hope to accomplish... Scotty yells from across the building. Scotty: What? Who did you beat? Jekyl: I have beaten both Sinc Mercier and Sonny Williams, both are highly respected and tough people, both have held gold many times, and I single handedly beaten them both. You see Chris, I don't hold gold for one reason; I DON'T CARE! My goal here in MWE is as it was before I left, to enflict as much pain as I can and strike fear into the hearts of every member on this roster, if I get a spiffy golden belt for it, then so be it, a trophy to say that I accomplished my goals. All you have here in MWE is simple rookie wins, nothing to brag about, nothing to show your abilities. Scotty heads back running with something in his hand, what seems to be a small brown leather book. The air is so musky that Scotty easily looses his breath, wheezing and coughing, attempting to catch his breath. Jekyl stares at him blankly, waiting for him to explain his rush. Scotty: I just found this book, it's my dad's business records and notes... Jekyl: Good job... I'm almost done here, why don't you go off and talk to yourself plan out your next promo or something constructive... Scotty: Sky says your an asshole.... I agree.. Jekyl: That's nice, tell someone who cares, wait, on second thought, don't... If I think your nuts, I guarantee that other people will stick you in a comfy padded room with those nice spiffy white jackets... Scotty: Yeah, White isn't my color... Jekyl sighs in the sheer stupidity of Scotty's comments. Standing up from the chair he looks of at his tag team partner who once again has a blank look on his face. Rubbing his chin he walks around to wait for Scotty to snap out of his illusions. – – – – Scotty: We need outa' here, is it safe to leave yet? Sky: Yeah, Damien scared off the hounds so as long as hes around, they won't be back. Scotty: Perfect...LETS BLOW THIS POPSICLE STAND!! Sky: Never again... – – – – Scotty snaps back to reality and looks around for Jekyl, he is off in the back, poking around the lockers. Scotty: YOU DONE?!?! Jekyl: ALMOST, HOLD ON! The camera zooms into Jekyl as he slams one of the lockers shut, the sound echoes and breaks the silence. Jekyl: In my final words of this promo all I iwll tell you is a warning, my only warning... I am an animal, that ring is my cage and when I step foot in my cage I can't be stopped. The cage is my tranquility, it is my own personal reality where I do anything I want, however I want. Do yourself a favor and drop out before you get hurt. Jekyl walks back to the main doors and exits, Scotty turns off the lights and the 2 men head off into the distance, the street is silent, the only sound are the footsteps of the two most feared men in MWE, the last thing we see is the warehouse fading. -FADE-
