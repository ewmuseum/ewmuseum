At The Border; BLM H2H RP

Mar 28 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A custom black and white flame painted Cadillac pulled up to a driveway, to a nice house, on the outskirts of Toronto Canada. Of course, being that it's a nice day out, and it's not raining or anything, the owner of the said car had the hood down. The owner of the car stepped out. It was no surprise that the man who was now walking to the house, was 'Supernova' Vega Knight. As usual, he wore black face paint on the right side of his face. Unlike usual, he was not wearing his spiked hoodie. He went to knock on the door, when he heard the door being slammed against.....repeatedly. Vega: Fucken hell. Those two go at it like jackrabbits on Viagra, in heat. Knowing that he wouldn't be able to go in for at least a few minutes, Vega leaned up against the wall, and took out a cigarette. He placed the cigarette in his mouth, and lit it up. Vega: Fucken hell. All we decided to fucken do was be nice and see how the fucker is doing, and guess what we almost walk into. Them two going at it. Just our luck. It would take Vega five minutes to finish his cigarette. By that time, The loud banging noises had ceased. He sighed, knocking on the door. The person who answered it, was Demonica, who was clad in nothing but a really large T-shirt. Demonica: Oh Vega, didn't expect you to be here so soon. Vega smirks at Demonica. She could almost feel his eyes undressing her. It was in a way that even made her creeped out. Vega then quickly shook his head, to snap him out of his lust filled gaze. Vega: We've noticed. Demonica blushed and giggled at that, while leading Vega in. Leon has just finsihed putting his jeans on, much to Vega's relief. Leon turned around, and smirked. Leon: Yo dude. Enjoy the show? Vega scoffed at that comment. That caused Leon to briefly chuckle, before he hopped onto his couch. Vega pulled up a chair, sitting down. It would also be at that moment, when crying noises could b heard over the baby monitor that was in the living room. Demonica sighed, before she went upstairs to check on the triplets. Vega: With the way you two go at it, there might be another devil kid on the way. Leon: Ugh, don't remind me. Anyways, did you get the tickets? Vega: Yeah. Four tickets in total. Two heading to our first destination, then over to the United Kingdom, where we will rock the Highway To Hell. Leon: Awesome. Vega: How's the back? Leon: No pain. Did you honestly think Dallas hurt me as much as everyone thought? Vega: No not really. Leon: Then what do we have to worry about. Especially since we're going to be coming home as the MWE tag team champions. Vega: If we had a drink with us, we'd drink to that. Leon: Well why didn't you say so. I'll grab us both a beer. Vega: Thanks. Leon goes into the kitchen to grab the two beers. It only take him a minute to get them. He opens both as he sits down. He gives one of the beers to Vega, and they toast, to what Leon has said previously. Vega: So about what you said before....you know.....that whole 'firestorm' thing......is that all true? Leon: Most definitely. Why would I make that up. I'm sick and tired of being mocked for being who I am. I have to at last show the entire world, starting with the MWE, that they need to throw everything out the window. All that order, and balance, and all that shit. Makes me sick. Vega: Well, you argued your case against us before, and well, we've sort of embraced that lifestyle. Leon: Would you please stop talking in the second person? It's annoying. Vega looked at Leon with a smirk. At least, his right side did. With that in mind, Vega twisted his neck, so that the right side of the face was the one seen more to Leon. Dark Vega: Oh, but it's fun. I mean afterall, we can taunt the devil, and get away with it. You would never kill us. All of us know it Leon. Leon: Laugh it up jackoff, and I will scramble your brains. Dark Vega: Like I'm honestly going to believe that threat. My light side, maybe. But not me. I fear nothing. Not the so called 'god' that supposed to guide us, not death, and certainly not you. Leon sighed. He did his best to ignore the ranting of dark Vega. But despite that, he knew it was true. Leon had no desire to actually kill Vega. But like a lot of things in life, that could change in an instant. Vega: Do you regret taking that time off when you did, having to wind up in ICW for a little bit? Leon: Not one bit Vega. I got to see my three children come to life, and I wouldn't have had I decided to stay. What about you? Vega: Makes sense. But the thing is, Alejandro has been doing good work while we were in ICW. He had been facing a few well known names. Leon: So? Did he win every match? Nope. Fact is Vega, you have to understand, that he's trying to claim that he can beat us based on experience. I've already proven him badly wrong before. Vega: Yeah. I remember last April. Man that was one helluva match. We can't wait to shut him up. Billy Joe Barker........well shit, he isn't going to be doing anything worthwhile anyways, so lets make him......how do you say..........famous? Leon: I'll drink to that. The next day, Leon and Vega were actually down in Texas. That was part of their trip, as they had booked a flight down their. But they did it for one reason. It wasn't to go and attack one of their opponents in his home. No, they had another reason That reason, was of course, a promo. They had also found the perfect location for it as well. The Mexican border. Leon: I've always wondered what this place was like. Well, the security of it, that is. Considering how many Mexicans sneak across each year. Vega: Like Alejandro did? Leon: Yup. Vega sighed. He had set up the camera, so that it was directed at the two. He quickly switched in on, before standing beside Leon. Leon: Well despite a minor setback from the previous show, it seems as if the wheels have started moving for the Black List Mafia. Vega: This week, we prove that we are without a shadow of a doubt, that we are the best damn tag team in MWE, and that MWE will ever see. Despite that one blip back in ICW, we have been unstoppable. And that small blip, was only a fluke. Leon: But what does it matter though. Who's getting the tag team title shot. WE ARE!!!!! Both Vega and Leon chuckle at that statement. Leon then stopped chuckling, but Vega didn't. Once Vea noticed that Leon had stopped, and was looking at him with somewhat embarrassed look, the two faced warrior stopped, and blushed in embarrassment. Naturally, you could only see the unpainted side of his face begin to have a tint of red in it. Leon: But what about what I said last time. How MWE was going to be going through a change, that included a massive dose of Chaos and anarchy. Well It's already begun. Whether you people see it or not, my minions have been running rampant. The best part, they don't even know that they are dong my work for me. You see, in order to truly cause as much pain and destruction that I have planned, you need to......how do I say this...........break people, trick people, and corrupt people. Vega: Is that the reason why we're at the Mexican border? Leon: Not now. Vega sighed. He just more or less stepped out of view, letting Leon be the only one in focus. Vega knew that Leon has something important to say in regards to what was coming up. Leon: One of the many advantages of being the devil's incarnate, is that you have a natural understanding of how the human mind works. You could easily trick and corrupt people to do your bidding. If I wanted to, I could make even the most so called 'holiest' of men become raging psychopaths. Hell, you can ask ying-yang over- Leon suddenly noticed that Vega was no longer beside him. He let out a growl of frustration, looking at Vega. Leon: What, the fuck do you think you're doing, leaving the shot? Vega: It's your promo practically. You had something important to say. But if you're gonna talk about the face this soul is split, then yeah, we'll talk. Vega came back into the view, letting out a breath of frustration. If people hadn't noticed by now, while those two are like brothers, they do tend to piss each other off. Vega: As Leon here was saying, he can corrupt anyone. Look at what this soul used to be. A Happy go lucky, prank pulling, joker. Now, what you people see is a focused, split personality, vicious son of a bitch. All because this fucken bastard here kept saying that the darkness was strong within this soul. Reminding of all the shit that has been seen through this life, and what's been done, only fed it. This was the end result. A two faced warrior was born. Could have been total darkness, but chose not to. Vega then smirked at Leon, knowing full well that is exactly what Leon wanted him to say. At that, he then left again. Leon sighed. But he knew he had to continue, so he looked back and the camera, and started again. Leon: As Vega said, I reminded him of what he had to grow up around, and even what he's done. Also, everyone knows about me and my so called 'brooding'. Heh. It's just me reminding myself why I am what I am, and love each second of it. But enough of Vega and I. It's time I told of other examples. Well more importantly, the main example. I once pushed a man to his very mental limits, but only because he was a greedy little jackass, who simply wanted something I had. Gold. And a very valuable slab of it as well. I made him experience true pain. I put him through the ringer. I unleashed a rage within him that he didn't know existed, and now, he's no longer just concerned about winning. No, he's more concerned about making people suffer. An unforeseen sacrifice was made on my part, but it's all for the bigger picture. But what does this has to do with my opponents this week, in the form of Tex-Mex.....well in a lot of ways, my ability to see people's weaknesses, well I do know about their weaknesses, and I plan to exploit them. And with those two, it's pretty easy. Vega then moves back into view, and looked at Leon with a slight frustrated look. Leon was sort of surprised by this. Because what he saw was not just any frustrated glare, but a two faced frustrated glare, and that's never a good thing, no matter who you are. Leon: What? Vega: When the fuck are you going to get to why we are at the fucken Mexican border? Leon: Jeez dude, lighten up a bit. I'm getting to that........Eventually. Vega sighs at that remark. He balls one of his hands, making a fist, and sort of shakes it, as if he's twitching. He wants to punch Leon, but knows that it's not a good idea to. Leon: But if you must know, it's because I wanted to see the very place where that Mexican Jumping Bean tried to actually jump over this rather large wall. Just because he's a fucken Lucha Libre wrestler, doesn't mean that he can jump over a wall like this, at least not from the ground. Hell I'll go as far as saying that there were quite a few human shaped dents in that wall they had to repair. The fucker tried, but failed to do it. So he did the next best thing, and used all the other wannabe immigrants like a ladder, and finally got over this fucken wall, and into America. Vega burst out laughing. He totally agreed with what Leon had said, but the fact that it seemed something like Alejandro Rodriguez would do, and everyone knew it. Leon: but with that out of the way, It's time I prove why the Black List Mafia are going to become the tag team champions. Not only is it because we're hungrier, more chaotic then you both are, but we're also smarter. Because the Tex Mex team has two fatal weaknesses, each member having their own one weakness. These flaws I'm talking about, are Pride, and stupidity. Vega's dark side smirks for a second. It was a quick one, that could have easily have been missed if you didn't pay close attention. Leon: The Prideful one being Alejandro. I don't blame him for being full of pride, because he's had quite a few accomplishments. Former United States, X-TV and world champion. In fact, the ironic thing is, When he won the MWE world title, it was at the last highway to Hell Pay Per View. But now that I think about it, wasn't that like almost two years ago? Vega: Yeah it was. Damn. Back, then, George W Bush was still president. Hell back then, the economy didn't suck balls. Both friends burst out laughing at that statement. Afterall, such a fact was very much true. After a minute of their hyena like laughter, the two friends calm down, getting back to the task at hand. Leon: Yet, Alejandro still thinks he's actually one of the best. It's pathetic to think that he can jump around, doing the same fucken thing every single time, and win. Sure, I know the Mexican jumping bean still wins a lot, but he's nowhere near the level he need to be to be able to be worth mentioning. Sure, a few weeks ago, he somehow managed to defeat our MWE champion David Whitman, but beyond that, when was his last actual notable win? Vega: We don't know when it was either Leon. Sorry. Leon smirked at that. He kind of already knew the answer, but it was fun to hear Vega put his own input in. Leon: Exactly. Hell, one year ago, you and I participated in a world title tournament for EW. I was voted to be eliminated in the first round due to who I faced, and you, one of the more likely people to win the tournament. But neither happened. I not only beat Sonny Williams, a man you know all to well Alejandro, but I beat him twice, in two weeks, and he got pissed off because I was simply the better of the two. Then, when we faced off against each other for the world title, in that iron man match, sure you almost had me, but after what I did, I'm sure you're still picking out wood splinters from your back, after being slammed through that table, just before I would take the win, and the title. I proved on that day I was better then you Alejandro, and that fact will remain. I know it still burns that you thought a guy who had less then four months of experience could not only defeat you, but become the world champion. I held onto that belt the longest in EW. I was EW. You, what have you ever been? You weren't anything in particular. You never truly made your name synonymous with anything before, other then jumping around. It burns deep, doesn't it? Go ahead, talk about how you hate me for showing the world your true colors. Say that you will be the victor. Your deep hatred for me, your stubborn pride, is what will push you off the deep end. Make you even crazier then before. You want to believe you still have it, I'm going to prove to you, Alejandro, that you don't. At least not against the Black List Mafia. We are a team that in the indies, dominated. We know each other quite well. We've trained together, grew up together, and despite the fact you can say you're part of the better tag team, you know, you're worng on that part. Leon takes a deep breath after finishing his little speech. Stretching a bit, Vega steps forward, his unpainted side towards the camera, with a genuine smile on his face. Vega: All of that is true. But we don't exactly mean that you're not good, no no no. It simply means, that, we're better then you guys. But people are wondering as to why Texas's own Billy Joe Barker decided to join. Well we think we have the answer right for you. Closing his visible eye. Vega turns around, to display the black painted side of his face to the camera. That eye is fully open, and a sinister smirks appears on his side of the face. It's Dark Vega's turn to talk. Dark Vega: It matters not how you look at it, but the fact that the backwater hick that we also have to deal with has nothing but stupidity. Can't help but wonder if it's from him drinking all that alcohol like water that's screwing up his brain, but he's nothing. A nobody that is only attaching himself to a dying career, in some hopes of either trying to suck the life out of it at a faster rate, or just bask in what little glory is left. Billy Joe Barker. The only way you could actually help Alejandro Rodriguez against a team such as the Black List Mafia, is if your preparation for the upcoming match was to totally give up drinking until it is all said and done, because we guarantee, that we will not only out skill you, we will make you taste your blood, kick you around like a ragdoll, and cripple you. The only silver lining in that story is that you can wear the T-shit saying 'I got my ass kicked by the Black List Mafia'. Both Vega and Leon looked at each other for a second. Try as they might, that joke was far to funny for them to not burst out laughing. But that laughing fit they had even almost got them to roll on the floor. But after a minute, they two composed themselves, and Dark Vega was back in view again. Dark Vega: Billy. Your stupidity of you being you, and teaming up with Alejandro, will cost you. You'll see. You'll understand these prophetic words of wisdom. But to this two faced warrior, or the Devil's incarnate, it doesn't matter who we have to take out. We've made it very clear, that we simply live to make people bleed, to make them suffer for not embracing our ideals. Our ways of life. What will it take for you all to get off the bench and cause a never ending havoc everywhere? We will find that answer, but until then, those who are with us will enjoy the awesome bloodfest that we put on, week in and week out. But for those who don't, well, you'll just have to deal with it, because there is no way that anyone can make us stop. Alejandro and Billy Joe will find out at Highway to Hell, and it's not going to be the easy way, or the hard way. It's going to be, the Black List Mafia's way, and that is after the insane torture we put them through, they will go into shock, getting knocked right the fuck out. Vega then turns, so that we can see both sides of his face. Leon wraps his arm around Vega's shoulder, and we can see him smile, clearly impressed by Vega's words of wisdom. Of course, following that, Vega takes out a cigarette, places it in his mouth, and lights it up. Leon: I couldn't have said it better myself. But I can guarantee something great will happen. Whether it's me, raising one of our opponents up to heaven, before slamming them to hell, or if It's Vega hitting one of his high octane finishers. I can guarantee, that the impact, will make people watch in awe, the whole world chanting our name. Along with that, either the crowd, or some random ass douchebag with an annoying high voice, will scream out...........TOASTY!!!!!!!!!!!! Vega then stops any movement for a second, before slowly turning towards Leon looking at him with a confused expression. Before he talks, he blows a puff of smoke into Leon's face. Vega:.................WHAT THE FUCK WAS THAT?!?!?!?!?!?!?!?!?! Leon the shrugged, not exactly know, and not really caring. Vega sighed. Vega: Well regardless of the devil's really retarded randomness, we stick by what we said. Tex-Mex, you better get ready to protect yourselves from as much pain as you can, because BLM is on our way, and on the Highway to Hell, a road that this two faced warrior, and Leon often travel down, We will be taking what is ours, and that is....the MWE.....world.....Tag team....championship. Vega then takes out what looks to be a coin. He twirls it around between his fingers for about a minute. He then puts it in position, and flips it into the air. Catching it, and slapping it against his opposite hand, he begins to smile. Leon leans over, to see the result, and he to, smiled like a madman. Vega then takes the cigarette out of his mouth, and exhales a large puff of smoke at the camera, blinding the view just for a second. But the camera is shut off right after, signaling that Vega and Leon are finished, and are ready, to head to England, for their tag team match. END.
