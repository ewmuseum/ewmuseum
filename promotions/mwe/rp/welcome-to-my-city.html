Welcome to MY city!

Aug 6 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The sun was shining brightly as Sinc Mercier was enjoying a nice hot dog at a local hot dog stand in his neighborhood. He had just finished purchasing one when he looked behind him and noticed a camera man. He turned around and saw that he was wearing an MWE shirt, so he decided to say a few words knowing it wasn’t the USUAL press. The camera man zoomed in on Sinc. Sinc: “Well christ. I didn’t expect this. Lets have this little interview around the corner huh.” Said Sinc, hot dog in hand, as he walked around the corner of his city street, cars zooming by, sounds of horns and people chatting filled the air. Just around the corner was a group of druggy’s and hobos. Sinc stopped and decided to have the interview there next to them. Sinc: “Well hello there MWE fans and wrestlers alike! The names Sinc Mercier….and if you don’t like my name we got problems amigo, problems like Michael Jackson at an adoption center. Ok maybe not THAT big but you catch my drill. Anyway, welcome to BEAUTIFUL downtown San Francisco! Let me introduce you all to my group of friends here.” Sinc said pointing to the group of people behind him as the camera man zoomed on them keeping Sinc out of the frame. Sinc: “Friend number one, a dirty filthy old lady I call Jekyl. Shes the typical beggar on the streets not much of a looker but she knows how to give you a good time. She is also known as Mrs. Get Around Town because…well…she knows how to get around." The camera man zoomed on Sinc for a minute. Sinc: “Hey hey hey buddy. Im not done yet!” Sinc forcibly turned the camera back to the group of people. Sinc: “Now the man next to her, friend number two, is a crackhead named Aeron. Hes tall but also kinda jumpy know what I mean? If you get too close he will try to go all incredible hulk on you . Stay off the roids buddy.” Sinc lead the camera guy to a short man wearing adult diapers. Sinc: “Now this little bundle of joy…this little bastards name is Aiden. Hes a good guy when he wants to be, but he has some stomach problems. Stay away from the burritos there pal.” Sinc said patting him on the head. He then pointed out a frail old man who was def and dumb. Sinc: “Ah old man Sonny. He can never hear the sound of his own childs voice. Poor soul. Kind of like not ever hearing the sound of a ring bell. It truly sucks for this guy.” Sinc sighed. Finally he pointed to an average sized woman with little clothing on. She had a cigarette in her mouth and her makeup was messy. Sinc: “Ah my VERY good friend Brian. Shes a very CRABBY lady if you catch my drift. She tends to stand at the block trying to get some extra CASH. But one good thing about her is her funny personality. Aint that right Brian?” Sinc said entering the frame putting his arm around her as she then vomited. Sinc: “Well you all know what I am trying to say.” Sinc said then shoving the camera back on him. Sinc: “Just like the old days you can all come after me. I don’t care. I am here for one thing and one thing only. CHAMPIONSHIP GOLD! I promise you I will make my way to the main event, I will give the fans what they want, and I will be the most SHOCKING man in the MWE.” Suddenly the group of people walked up to Sinc and tapped him on the shoulder. Lady known as Brian: “Can we get our money now? Im late for my real job…” Said Brian as he took his wig off revealing short black hair. The camera then zoomed out to fit everyone on screen. Sinc: “Dammit I told you you’ll get paid later….Now all of you get outta here please!” Said Sinc as they all walked away as the adult diaper wearing man flipped him off on the way out. Sinc: “I SAW THAT BUDDY!” Sinc said pointing at him as he walked away and went back to the camera lens. The camera man zoomed back on Sinc’s face. Sinc: “As I was saying before I was rudely interrupted. You can think you have the upper hand…but in the end I will take that hand and break it into a million pieces before you dare raise it any higher. Take me on or take me out…because in the end….” Sinc took a breath. Sinc: “SINCCCCCCCC MERCIERRRRRRRRR! Will be VICTORIOUS! Welcome to San Francisco people…welcome to hell.” Sinc said as the camera faded and the sound slowly cut off leaving blackness and the fading noise of cars and honking horns.
