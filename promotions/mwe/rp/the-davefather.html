The Davefather; David Whitman RP

Sep 4 2008

David Whitman

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The scene opens up slowly on a dark, smoky room. We hear the Godfather theme playing lightly in the background. As everything becomes clearer, we can see David Whitman slowly swiveling in a chair behind a fancy desk. On the other side of the desk there is another man, but it’s still too dark to see who it is. David swivels and is now sitting so that the camera can see his right side. He is wearing a black suit, and his cheek seems swollen. His attention is directed towards the man on the other side of the table. David: I understand. You found paradise in America. You had a good trade, made a good living, the police protected you and there were courts of law and you didn't need a friend like me. But, now you come to me and you say "Don Whitman, give me justice." But you don't ask with respect. You don't offer friendship. You don't even think to call me Davefather. Instead, you come into my home on the day my daughter's to be married and you ask me to do murder for money. The camera stays focused on David for a moment before abruptly cutting to the other man, who is none other than his cousin James, dressed in his usual clothes. James: …what the F*CK are you talking about?! David: Huh?! Oh, sorry, I got into the moment. James: And will you can it with the f*cking jawbreaker already?! David spits out the jawbreaker. James: Real nice. On my new carpet. David: I can’t help but feel that this promo is going down the crapper. James: Astute observation. David turns to the camera. David: I mean, all I wanted to do was point out the irony that lies in the fact that BG Mancini is all mafia-ey and I’ve been in the Mayhem Mafia, and by the way I’ll be facing another Mayhem Mafia member at Turm- James: Ahem, Mancini? David: Right, right. So yeah, the irony in all that, plus the fact that we’re currently doing a mafia storyline on my cousin’s TRT show. So I wanted to encapsulate all of that, and then talk about Mancini, but let’s be honest, who really knows anything about this guy? James: Dude, you can’t talk like that about people. You know how your conscience gets afterwards. David: You’re right. I’m sorry. Wait, can we just start this over? The camera turns to static before opening back on the same room. David is walking around the room, gesturing wildly with his arms. He has a gun in one hand and a piece of paper in the other. David: Who’s the mezzafinook that set this match up?! This- this jamook! I’m so mad I could- He turns to James, who is sitting in the same place he was earlier. James looks surprised. David points the gun towards James’ face and pulls the trigger. A stream of liquid shoots out and onto James’ face. Suddenly, James gets a big smile on his face. James: BEER!!! The camera cuts to static, and then comes back into focus. We see everything is covered in what is probably beer as David and James are fighting for the squirt gun. The camera turns to static again and comes back, this time we see James standing in front of a picture of BG Mancini that is taped to the wall. James has a pointing stick in his hand, and David is sitting a couple of feet away. James (pointing to the head area): Here? David: … clothesline? James: I’ll allow it. James now points to an arm. James: Here? David: Uh… Armbar! James: I’ll allow it, and an extra point for using the word “Bar”! They high-five. James: I’m thirsty. Let’s go get a drink. They look at each other for a second, before both rushing out of the room. The camera fizzes out for a few seconds before slowly coming back into focus. It’s the same room, only this time, we see David sitting on the desk and facing the camera. He is now wearing his signature long black pants with a shirt that says “How can you describe what MWE is about in 3 words? Mayhem. Wrestling. Entertainment.” He has the MWE Heavyweight Title draped across his left shoulder. David: Hi. I’m MWE’s World Champion, David Whitman. We’ve had some fun here tonight, but I wanna get serious for a moment. This Sunday, at the Conseco Fieldhouse in Indianapolis, Indiana, I will be facing BG Mancini in a one-on-one match. Now, I’ve seen this guy wrestle, heck, I’ve gotten close to tangling with him in that battle royal. Remember that? Anyway, my point is, I’ve seen promise in this guy. If he tried his best he could easily win matches. But he’s getting sloppy. Spending less time training. Taking his matches less seriously. And that, my friends, is a couple of huge mistakes. Because once you go down that road, coming back is hard. Believe me. David stops and nods for a second, before taking a glance at the title. He then turns his attention back to the camera. David: I’ve worked hard to get to where I am today. I’ve taken the right things seriously and goofed off at the appropriate times. Well, you know, discarding my dating history. What I’m saying is; BG, you’ve got a chance to prove to everyone that you deserve to be here by giving me your best and making this a hell of a match. You and I, Mancini, we’re opening the show. Let’s set the bar a bit high, shall we? Let’s make everyone else work twice as hard. Because after all, that’s what MWE is all about, isn’t it? Shattering expectations and imaginations? Making fans explode with excitement? David stands up. David: All I know is, this Monday I’m not going to hold back. So Mr. Mancini, you’d better bring your A-game. David Whitman; Signing Out. The camera turns to static before turning to black, but we can still hear audio. James: SIGNING OUT?! AAAAAHAHAHAHA David: Shut the f*ck up! YOU told me to try some catchphrases! James: Not LAME ones! AHAHAHAHAHAHAHHHA We hear loud rustling and a very loud thud before the camera turns to static again, and then completely off.
