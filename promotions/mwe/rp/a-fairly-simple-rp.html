A fairly simple RP...; Derf VS Semi

Mar 27 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The scene starts at what looks to be the backstage area right after Tims tribute show. Superstars, backstage staff, medical personal, everyone simply walking around, doing what they do after every show. A few people gathered in a corner are having a conversation about Tim. Two guys dressed the exact same, yet have about 200 pounds difference between them, are eating at the buffet table. A doctor crosses the screen, one would assume he's going to treat one of the many injured superstars from earlier in the night. And then there is Eric Derf, still in wrestling gear, playing a Game Boy SP. Unusual, he doesn't seem to be a Game Boy kinda guy, but that's what's happening. The sound is turned up to a maximum, which is still nothing more than a few faint bleeps and other weird melodys. Suddenly, a shadow covers a small amount of Derf, which causes him to pause his game and look up to see Shawn Spitzer. He stares for a few moments with a silly dumb look on his face. Can I help you Spitzer? Well yes, I was just wondering how you feel to have just beaten one of the greatest men to ever fight here in MWE, DC? Derf, still grinning, looks down at his Game Boy and closes it over, sets it inside his gym bag, and stands up next to Spitzer. Well, to be honest, I feel- Derf is interrupted by giant metal claw grabbing him by the throat. Screams are heard all around the room, people scatter through the many doors, even Shawn Spitzer tries to run, but is grabbed by a second metal claw. A young woman, standing right in the middle of the room, is frozen in place due to fear. Oh my god, it's Doc Ock! He's got Derf and Spitzer! Help!! Doc Ock lifts both men high in the air. Derf uses both arms to try to create some space between his throat and the metal claws, and he actually succeeds. Hey Spitzer... Spitzer, who can't even move at the moment, and is too weak to try to break free, ignores Derf, and instead tries to wiggle his way free. Derf see's this, and assumes he's listening. I just... wanted to say... It felt awesome... to beat DC... one on one... I showed... the world... that I can... hang with... the big boys... and no matter... what anyone says... or what anyone... thinks of me... never count me out... I've always... got a trick... up my sleeve... Eric lets go of the claws, causing his entire bodyweight to pull on his neck as Doc Ock, who has remained quiet for some reason, uses another claw to rummage through many of the gym bags left around the room. Spitzer is almost unconscious by this time, barely moving, barely breathing. Can't... breathe... Getting weak... getting... angry... You Don't want... TO SEE SPITZER ANGRY!!! Shawn Spitzers face, which was as red as an apple, slowly begins to turn as green as... a green apple. His shirt sleeves begin to fill up with what could be air, or could have been air until the sleeves rip, revealing huge green muscles. His hair turns into a shaggy green color, his eyes green with rage. In mere seconds, Shawn Spitzer has become The Hulk! Using his superhuman strength, he grabs hold of the claw that's already grabbing hold of him, and crushes it like a banana, as if it were made of metal. Realizing what just happened to one of his claws, Doc Ock uses his other three claws to attack The Hulk, including the claw he was holding Derf with, but dropped him moments earlier, so he could attack The Hulk for crushing his claw, because Doc Ock was trying to... You get the picture... But now Derf is on the ground, rubbing his throat, watching as The Hulk (who is actually Shawn Spitzer) fights with Doc Ock. Shawn! Fighting DC was great! I knew I could beat him, and now I feel like I could beat anyone! Hell, I can even beat Doc Ock, with a little help from another friend... Saying this, Eric reaches into the crotch of his wrestling tights, and pulls out a ball. No, not that ball. A small red and white ball. A ball with a small circle in the middle that, when touched, causes the ball to grow from the size of a grape to the size of an orange. Holding the orange sized red and white ball, he tosses it at Doc Ock. Go Bulbasaur! Use Razer Leaf on Doc Ocks body! Help free Shawn Spitzer! The ball (which was obviously a pokeball) opens up with a bright flash of light, and out pops a Bulbasaur! Bulba! Bulba Saur! Saur! As soon as it lands on the ground, it immediately begins shooting razer sharp leaves out of its back, all in the direction of Doc Ock. Most miss him, until one leaf hits him right in the throat, slitting it and killing him. The claws go limp. The Hulk stops struggling with them, and once he realizes his fighting is done, he eventually shrinks back into much smaller, weaker looking Shawn Spitzer. Derf recalls the Bulbasaur, and shoves the Pokeball back into the crotch of his shorts. He then walks over to Shawn, who's laying on the ground in shock (and rags) and helps him up. As Shawn stands there, not sure what to believe what just happened, Derf walks away from him, towards Doc Ock. But he doesn't need anything from Doc Ock, he needs the microphone that his dead corpse lays near. He grabs it, and takes it back to Spitzer. ... What is this for? What the hell just happened?! Eric smiles his silly dumb smile again, as if nothing had just happened. We were doing an interview. I just finished telling you my thoughts on my match against DC, do you have any more questions for me? Or am I free to go? Spitzer is in complete shock, not at what just happened, but at the fact that Eric Derf is still so calm after it all. But alas, he lifts the microphone to ask another question. Umm... Ok, umm... Well, at Highway to Hell, umm, You Face Semi... So what do you think, umm... Of him taking the X-Havoc title tonight... Umm, after the TerrorDome match...? Spitzer is still shaking, he probably doesn't even realize he's the Hulk, he probably only remembers getting attacked by Doc Ock, then seeing him dead... or maybe he's just shivering cause all his clothes are ripped. Derf's still smiling his big silly dumb smile. Well Shawn, my opinion on Semi is- Before he can answer, both men are eaten alive by a T-Rex. Fin. Career Wins = 9 - 14 - 0 MWE Wins = 6 - 1 - 0 Former X-TV Champion Former Havoc Champion ... You didn't think my RP would end like that, did you? Anyway, Derf and Shawn had just gotten eaten, and are now inside the belly of a T-Rex. Sounds fun, it really isn't. It's actually quite cramped. Remember that scene in star Wars IV when the characters were in the trash compactor? Right about the time when C-3P0 reverses the compactor, that's about how much space Derf and Spitzer have, except theres no metal, it's all just gunk, stomach acid, stuff like that. Oh, and an entire live duck as well. But Derf notices Spitzer still has the microphone, so he decides to finish his earlier comments. As I was saying, I think it was bold of Semi to just take that title. He never earned it, he doesn't deserve it, hell, he don't even deserve to wrestle in MWE! He is a liability. He don't care about the rules, he just wants to hurt people. All he cares about is his streak, and if he thinks it'll end, he pussies out. He pussied out of the tag match by using a chair. He bitched his way into the terrordome match because he was scared his streak was going to be ended by a guy that I beat, and then once he was in the TerrorDome, he still bitched out by having an old friend help him win his match. Semi deserves to be in the unemployment line, hell, even that duck there deserves a place in MWE more then Jesse Anderson. The duck looks at Derf, as if it understood what he said. Really? You think I could- Shut up Duck... Back to what I was saying Shawn, Semi stole that belt. It isn't his, but if he thinks it is, then I have no problem facing him for it. I told people Bowen beating me for it was a fluke, and Bowen has proved that by not defending it since. Hell, since he won that title, has he even showed any effort to even compete? Bowen never deserved to win the title, but at least he won it fair and square. It might have been a fluke, but a fluke is better then a steal. So Semi, don't bother even taking that name plate off that belt. Don't bother to get them to make one with your name on it. I've got my name plate ready to go back on that belt, and I plan on- Shut up Derf! Shawn Spitzer screams this with almost perfect female vocal cords. Derf stops talking, but continues smiling his silly dumb grin. Spitzer seems pissed. Derf, we're in a T-Rex! We're inside the belly of a dinosaur that's been dead for like 10,000 years or something like that! How the hell are we going to get out of here? Got any bright ideas? Derf chuckles before Spitzer can even finish speaking, not enough to interrupt him, but as if to say "Stupid boy" or something like that. Stupid Boy... Listen, we're probably still in Texas right now. And if there is a T-Rex walking around Texas, I can think of at least one guy that can, and probably will help us get out of- Before he can even finish his sentence, a giant "RAWR" is heard, followed by alot of shaking, more "RAWRING", and finally, a huge BANG that leaves the small stomach of the T-Rex lying sideways. Before either man can figure out what the hell just happened, an arm reaches through the wall of the T-Rex's belly, followed by a 2nd arm. At first, the two arms don't do anything, but Derf looks closely and see's that there are letters tattooed on each knuckle. |L|A|W|O| |R|D|E|R| As Derf reads the tattoo on the knuckles, he immediately realizes whos knuckles they are, but before he can react, the two hands grab hold of Derf and Spitzer, and pull them right through the belly of the T-Rex and back into the real world. Everything is bright now, as opposed to being inside the belly of the beast. Derf and Shawn look around to see they are in a large open field with some cows in the distance, simply eating grass, pooping on the grass, and eating more grass. The sun's position makes it seem like it's probably about noon, which is odd, because when the dinosaur ate them, it was right after the show, and they'd only been in the belly as long as it took you to read that part of my RP. But the best looking site, was the man attached to the arms that saved them. Huge muscly arms, a beard that could shatter bones, and the worlds luckiest hat, this man is none other then Chuck Norris. Oh man, thanks again Chuck. You're the best! Derf's still got his silly dumb grin on, Spitzer is confused, he's got no idea what the hell is going on here. Chuck begins to speak, but before he does, Derf quickly covers Spitzers ears, and with Spitzer so confused, he just figures it's best not to argue, and leaves his ears to be covered. !!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!(The words of Chuck Norris are so powerful that if any normal man were to hear them, his head would explode and his penis would fall off as if it were mearly scotch taped into place.) I couldn't agree more Chuck. Thanks for your help. Have fun killing Ninjas! !!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! Chuck nods, and leaps into the air, as if he were Superman. He's not flying, he's mearly leaping over tall buildings looking for the underwear wearing superhero, he owes him $5. Back on the ground, Spitzer has sat down, and is leaning against the dead corpse of the T-Rex. Eric, who had been watching Chuck jump away, turns back to Spitzer, still with his silly dumb grin, and sits next to him. So yeah, those are my thoughts on Semi stealing the title belt... Any more questions? Spitzer doesn't even look at Derf, he's to busy staring into space, or maybe at a really pretty flower. Derf, every time I've asked you a question today, something really fucked up has happened. I was going to ask if you though you could beat Semi at Highway to Hell, but if I do ask, I'm sure something else really fucked up will happen. Derf's smile fades, as he now realizes Spitzers actually upset. Oh, I see... Well, I do think I can beat Semi at Highway to Hell, Because- No! As Shawn Spitzer shouts this to stop Derf from talking, Derf shuts up. At first, it seems as if he'd simply shut up, but as both men stare into space, it's clear that they ain't alone. A figure is walking towards them. A man with wavy blonde hair and a leather jacket. And a microphone in his hand, much different then the microphone that of an MWE interviewer would use, but one a singer would use. As the man gets closer, he says something into the microphone, or actually, he's not saying it, he's singing it. I'm a cowboy, on a steel horse I ride. I'm wanted dead or alive. Wanted dead or- Ah Fuck! It's Bon Jovi! Spitzer looks at Derf puzzled as to why he's so scared of Bon Jovi, but gets a scare himself as he see's Derf holding an AK47, aimed at Bon Jovi. He don't even properly aim, he just open fires on the guy, blowing him away. What the hell? You just killed Jon Bon Jovi! Derf drops the AK47 and looks into Spitzers eyes, which are scared, angry, and a little hungry. Derfs face is still his silly dumb grin. He was interrupting my promo, and besides, he sucks... Anyway, I don't think I can beat Semi, I know I can. Look at what we've been through today. We fought with Doc Ock, got eaten alive by a T-Rex, and survived at least 5 seconds of Bon Jovi singing live. I can handle just about anything, and no fat crybaby, chair swinging, truck driving, taco eating tub of crap is going to stop me. I'm going to face Jesse Anderson, and I'm going to take back the Championship I never should have lost, the championship he never earned. And when all this is said and done, you're not going to remember any T-Rex, any Doc Ock, and Incredible Freaking Hulk- Wait, when was the Hulk here? Derf finally lets down his silly dumb grin and just looks at Spitzer for a moment before bringing the trademark grin back. Like I was saying, you won't remember any of this after Highway to Hell, cause the image of me pinning Semi is going to be what is burned into your mind. Semi had better not even think about bringing a chair to that ring, because if he decides he wants to pussy out - again- and use it, I won't hesitate to get myself a little steel friend to even the odds. The two nod as two men, one young black male, and one older white male, both in black suits and black sunglasses, approach them. I'm agent K, this is Agent K. We'd like you to take a look at this.
