One; All it takes is one

Jul 3 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
-The camera shows Riddick stepping outside of the hotel building as he makes his way down a large set of cemented stairs. The people he walks by are drinking coffee, fiddling with their blackberries, and honeymooners going over their itinerary.- “The time has passed, the grains of sand have dissolved, the bell tolls opportunity. The day of reckoning has arrived. One thing people take for granted when they grow up is the true value of opportunity. And when they get older and it’s too late then we truly realize the value of opportunity. In life, there are three types of people... People who will pass by infinite opportunities at greatness, willing to pass one by because another one will be coming.” Riddick walks past a rich young college student in his sports car. Riding in a car he didn’t have to pay for, living a life paid by his loving mother and father. The base of the stereo rocks the car as it idles at the red light with Riddick walking past it on the cold hard sidewalk with cigar butts and gum to be found all over. As Riddick reaches the intersection, the light turns green and the young man slams the gas and drives off with his music blaring in the streets. Riddick waits for the walking signal. People who will get no shots at all in life, whether the cause be because of a choice they made in life or their poor luck and misfortune. While Riddick waits for the walking signal a man walking across the street wearing a torn jacket and a scruffy beard asks for change to be able to get something small to eat. As the pedestrians walk past ignoring the bum with their eyes glancing into other directions, the walking signal goes and Riddick starts crossing the street as the bum sits on the curb with a sense of disappointment for where his life now stands. But then you have the people like Riddick who will only come by maybe only one opportunity to be great in life. One single moment to make your life worth something, one single moment to change your life and one single moment to change the world. Very few people in life will live their life on one side of the spectrum of life and live long enough to take the opportunity and see the other side. The brighter side with their names in the lights and worldwide recognition. But it’s rare for one reason, life is a high road, and it’s either the highroad to heaven, or the highroad to hell. Either way, it’s a long way to the other side. Riddick suddenly feels vibrating and check his phone, an incoming call. No number is given or any information on who it is. However Riddick answers his cell.- Riddick: Hello? -Short pause as a light breath is heard over the speaker.- ????: Is this Jay? -Riddick ponders on who’s calling him.- Riddick: Is this some fan? Some telemarketer? ????: I assure you it’s not. Riddick: Well then who is it? -Long pause...- Riddick: Who is it!? -Long pause...- ????: It’s Alexa... -Silence occurs, Riddick is in shock.- Riddick: I can’t believe it’s you... Alexa: I can’t believe you’ve been talking to my son David. Riddick: He’s my son aswell. Alexa: You’re son?! Where were you for the last eleven years?! I spent his life making all these sacrifices to make sure he’s raised right and now you think you can suddenly barge in and take over? Riddick: It’s not what you think, he came to me several weeks ago on your business trip. Alexa: Oh that’s just like you Jay, that’s all you do, you were never able to take responsibility for your own mistakes so you blame others. Riddick: Why are you calling me? Alexa: I don’t want you to see my son anymore, Jay. You had a chance, you could’ve fixed your mistake for what you did to me at prom but you couldn’t admit it was your fault. You took my dignity, and you took a father away from what could’ve been your son. Riddick: You want to talk about taking things away? Well I wish you could look at me now Alexa, I wish you could look at where my heart used to be, because it’s no longer there! That was all I had and you took it all when you lied about loving me! Now all I have is two cold eyes that will never see the beauty in life ever again! Alexa: I actually did love you Jay, but I was confused during that time of my life because I felt sorry for you. And I found out that you were a nice person, but then you threw it all away. Riddick: I know you still love me! When I talked to David, he told me how you always talked about me, seeing me on TV, telling him that I was a decent person. I know you still love me Alexa. Alexa: I did, because I didn’t want my son thinking his father was a screw up, a failure in life, a quitter. I did what I had to do to get my son to believe that the world is good, I didn’t want him to end up like you. Riddick: I’m not a failure Alexa, I have a great opportunity, I have a title match this weekend at the MWE PPV. How does that make me a failure? Alexa: Jay, listen to me. You had an opportunity to be your son’s father, to be my husband if you would’ve stayed with me. I would’ve forgiven you, but if you can’t capitalize on that opportunity, what makes you think you can handle any opportunity? Riddick: I made a mistake, I screwed up. But I am back on my feet again. And I will not fail. I don’t just want to be champion, I want to be the father of my son. I want to see him grow up and learn. I want to be there for him. Alexa: Just stay away Jay, I don’t want to talk to you anymore. -Alexa hangs up. Riddick closes his phone without turning his eyes to glance. He is in utter shock and the expression on his face makes it nearly impossible to tell what his true feelings and emotions he now possesses are. He puts his phone in his pocket and as he glances ahead there’s a shining gleam in his eyes. His eyes start to water up. There maybe a heavy layer of clouds that is blocking the suns light, however now looking at Riddick there is nothing hidden. A tear rolls down his cheek and he simply walks along on the sidewalk. He can’t hold it in anymore, the pain of never being able to see David again, the fact that he really screwed up his life now hangs above his head. The true pain of mistakes isn’t making them, it’s the long term effects. Failing a test that now leaves you in a dead end job. Stealing a candy bar that puts a stain on your records. The long term effects of screwing up can place a burden on your soul but perhaps no screw up can compare to Riddick’s. He may never see the glowing eyes of his son, he may never see him grow up, he may never be a father ever again. Riddick rushes into an alley in between two rented properties. Trying to be hidden from public eyes as he can no longer handle the trauma. He sits against the brick wall of the establishment. With his head placed firmly in the palms of both his hands. Looking down in shame at what he’s become.- Riddick: Is it true, Jay? Is it true that you’ve screwed up? I wish I could go back, I wish I could alter the past and be in a better situation, any situation that’s not what it is now. But is Alexa right? Is this just going to be another opportunity I’m not going to capitalize on? Will this Sunday be a wasted moment? This isn’t even about impressing people anymore. This isn’t about getting the blood of others onto my cold hands, this is about me getting my blood off of the hands of others. I don’t want to live in the life where I can’t see my son grow up, and I have a feeling that if there’s even the slightest of chance of turning my life around, I’m going to have put this all behind me and seize this opportunity. -Riddick’s cell vibrates again as he promptly answers it hoping its Alexa or David or somebody that he can talk to and change what happened.- Riddick: Hello? Road Agent: Hey, you’re supposed to be at the Independance Day press conference right now. You’re up in 15 minutes! Riddick: I almost forgot about that, I’ll be down in a flash. Road Agent: Don’t worry, the limo’s looking for you now. Just call the driver and tell him where to pick you up. Riddick: Okay, I’ll be there. Road Agent: Hurry up! -Riddick hangs up and then calls the limo driver to indicate where to pick him up. Riddick awaits by the end of the street as the limo arrives and he steps inside. He’s checking his watch hoping he has enough time to get there before it’s too late. The limo goes to the back of the outdoor stage of the MWE Independance Day Press Conference as the road agent spots him coming out of the limo.- Press Conference Announcer: Next is the number one contender for the X-TV Havok Championship as he’ll step up against Anthony Clark and champion Nic Kraven this weekend at Independance Day, I introduce to you, “The Uprising” Jay Riddick! -Riddick hops up on the stage and goes around to the curtain to a loud cheer from the crowd. Usually he’d get a mixed reaction however because he turned down Michaels Inc. the crowd must’ve found respect for Riddick. Riddick steps to the center of the stage to the podium as he prepares to talk about his match at Independance Day.- Riddick: MWE Independance Day is finally here. And finally I get my chance to not only go 5 and 0 here in MWE. I have a chance to become your MWE X-TV Havok Champion! -Crowd cheers- But let me ask you people something, this is no secret. My two opponents are members of an illustrious group formed by Brian Michaels called Michaels Inc. -Loud boos are heard- This is Independance Day, so let me ask something to both Anthony Clark & Nic Kraven. Before your match when Brian Michaels comes to give you some advice about the match, telling you what’s best to do and what to avoid, have you ever considered what he’ll tell your opponent/partner. Will he counteract his advice, and in the end, whose back will he pat and whose will he ignore? Clark, you claim that by becoming the first Jay Riddick and not the thousandth Brian Michaels that I’ll become a loser? Well I apologize but this loser defeated you twice. The only reason you’re in this match is because you stayed out of my path of destruction and squeaked your way into the match. Clark, week in and week on I remind everyone that tunes in to listen that you’re arrogance is what makes you who you are, but that arrogance costed you losses against not only myself but also against Nic Kraven. So as the old saying goes Clark, three strikes and you’re out. It’ll be over, you will never be able to defeat me and you will never be worthy to stand in the ring with me. Now please answers me this, what do you believe makes you worth of a title match? What have you accomplished in your tenure here that gives you faith that you can stand in the same ring with a champion. You had your match, and blew it. You gave everything you had and it wasn’t enough. If I were you Clark, I’d save my dignity and not show up for the match. Pack up your bags and leave that locker room forever, because you are not fit to lace up a pair of boots, to step into the ring nor are you fit to be in this profession. Clark, you did the right thing. You signed that dotted line when you decided to join Michaels Inc. To follow under the guidance of the multi-time champion, Brian Michaels. He wanted to take upcoming stars to breed and form like putty in his hands to his perception of what a World Champion should appear to be. And then there’s Nic Kraven, the current MWE X-TV Havok Champion. The man who no matter what outcome of the match will drop his championship to either I or Anthony Clark according to the rules of the Highroad Match. Kraven, I don’t want it to come to you forfeiting the championship. I want to take that championship from you. I have all the respect in the world for you Kraven. We’re not so different you and I, we both have lived our lives in hurt filled environments, our perception is a major contrast to society and we fight despite what the critics advise we do. -Crowd cheers for the beloved profession of professional wrestling- But I don’t want to settle for seconds Kraven. We’re both on a massive uprising, but Kraven, I have to let you ponder a thought, have you ever stood in the ring against someone that may be your exact duplicate? You have fought people entirely different to who you are, but my question is have you ever went toe to toe with someone that completely understands your motives and your thoughts? Kraven, I want your championship. I want it for so many reasons, I want my winning record to be bigger, I want to be in a higher position, I want the glory, I want the happiness, I want the opportunity, I WANT IT ALL! Kraven, you cannot even begin to comprehend what winning this championship means to me. These veins in my body aren’t filled with blood, these veins that keep pumping and working every moment, these veins are filled with envy. -There’s a silent pause as Jay Riddick chokes up a little staring into the eyes of loyal MWE fans as he prepares for what he’ll say next.- None of you here or anyone will know, but my life, my current situation is all a revelation of the results of my past. And I’ve recently lost something that’s so dear to me, something that came into my life a little while ago that I am now without. My life has always been about blaming others for my mistakes and for the decisions I’ve made throughout my life. I’ve recently now realized this and I’ve recently realized that of all the opportunities I’ve received in life, never before have I been able to seize an opportunity that would make my life, the life of Jay Riddick a little brighter. But the days of running away from hope and opportunity is over, and the days of seizing the opportunities life gives me and making Jay Riddick truly define the word “Uprising”. This Saturday you can call it Independance Day if you want. After all, it’ll say Independance Day on your tickets, t-shirts, DVDs, and all other merchandise and apparel, but for me, Saturday July 5th won’t be Independance Day, it’ll be my Day of Reckoning. -You watch all of these movies, listen to these songs and read these books about people who were famous for seizing one single opportunity to become who they are today. Rising from the bottom of the preverbial abyss into the limelight life of success and fame. At Independance Day, the tides can turn in one second. The life of one can be changed in one second. One moment to seize one opportunity. One life can be changed. One man can have a new lease on life. All it takes is one man. One Title, One Moment, One Match, One Opportunity, One Life, One Chance, One Move, One.-
