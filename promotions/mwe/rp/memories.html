Memories...; A Nic Kraven RP...¬_¬

Apr 11 2009

Nic Kraven

“Prophet!' said I, `thing of evil! - prophet still, if bird or devil! - Whether tempter sent, or whether tempest tossed thee here ashore, Desolate yet all undaunted, on this desert land enchanted - On this home by horror haunted – tell me truly, I implore - Is there – is there balm in Gilead? - tell me – tell me, I implore!' Quote this forsaken Raven, forevermore... ------------------------------------------------ PROLOGUE ------------------------------------------------ Dominance... The only word that can describe “The Definition of Violence” Nic Kraven's performance these past few weeks. Out of sheer dominance, Nic has risen up the ranks here in Mayhem Wrestling Entertainment. Scotty McKnight...Devlin Sharpe, they mean nothing now except just a mere of the past. March 22nd, 2009 will mark “Sick” Nic's next step to achieving glory. Who dare stands in Nic's way now? None other than Mr Markowitz and Triple G, the team known as Blood Fuze. Will they succeed in stopping Nic from reaching his destiny? Or will Nic make Mr Markowitz and Triple G just another part of his past? What will become of this showdown? ------------------------------------------------ SCENE #1 ------------------------------------------------ [It is another dark and cold night. The scene fades to what seems like a bedroom. As the scene opens up more, it is revealed to be none other than the bedroom of Mayhem Wrestling Entertainment Superstar, Nic Kraven. The camera pans around the room as we see a closet, a king size bed, a brown wooden counter, and a window with light black curtains. The room is bare and white. The camera moves to the window. On the outside, the sun is barely reaching the horizon. The street is calm and silent with no activity in sight. The camera now moves back to the bedroom and to the bed of Nic Kraven. We see Nic sitting there fully dress with his head bowed. Hes wearing a leather jacket with a T-Shirt that reads Hell Hath Unleashed in a sloppy style of writing, a pair of dark blue jeans, and a pair of Jordan's. He sits there for a few seconds thinking. Thinking in nothing in particular, just thinking. He sits there a few more seconds before finally speaking.] “The Definition of Violence” Nic Kraven My past... My past is something I would never want to experience again. All of the pain and suffering in that hell hole. Its something that I just want to forget. However every time I am close to escaping it, it finds away back to me. It is the demon that haunts my dreams. It is the serpent that has coiled around me....squeezing until I suffocate. It is...a parasite that has infested my life. Every step I take, everywhere I go, its there like a malignant cancer. What do I do? What can I do? TELL ME?! [Just then, a voice what sounds like a women, rings out in the darkness] ??? Isn't it obvious Shawn? [Nic then looks up with an astonished look appearing on his face] “The Definition of Violence” Nic Kraven You're here again.. Standing in my room, spinning your conspiracies for me to consume. You have a plan now..don't you? ??? Relax...and all shall be revealed. [The voice was back yet again. In a moment of hopelessness, the voice had return like it always does.] “The Definition of Violence” Nic Kraven Why don't you leave me alone? Why do you insist to disturb me? The Voice I am nothing more than a guide to you. A salvation if you will. I came at a time of hopelessness. So I hear that your past has been bothering you again. “The Definition of Violence” Nic Kraven And if it has? What do you want from me? The Voice I only seek to help you... So I hear you want to forget about your past? “The Definition of Violence” Nic Kraven And if I do? Get to the point. The Voice Don't you get it Shawn? To silence the past, you must go back to where it all started. Back to where you suffered from the torment. You have to go back and face your past. [What seems like an eternity of suffering and pain from the place where he was forced to call home. The place that has made him what he is today. The place that was the cause of the scars all over his body. The place that gave him nightmares, and even now the nightmares are still there. He would no have to face the same place yet again. Nic begins to stand up slowly and speaks.] “The Definition of Violence” Nic Kraven I know what I must do now... [With those final words, Nic leaves the room and walks downstairs shrouded in darkness. The scene now slowly is engulf in darkness.] ------------------------------------------------ SCENE #2 ------------------------------------------------ [The scene opens up in the cold morning air. The camera pans around the streets of Downtown Fresno, California. The streets are silent and the sun has yet to reach the horizon. As the camera moves over a few inches, we see a shadow. A shadow of a tall athletically built man. As the camera comes a few inches closer, the man is revealed to be none other than “The Definition of Violence” Nic Kraven. He has on a black hoodie, dark blue jeans, and a dark blue with white stripes sweat pants. Nic has a disgusted look on his face. He looks around the street with hate in his eyes. He then walks down the sidewalk to the corner of North Maple Drive. He continues to walk down the sidewalk past old boarded up houses. In his mind, he thinks of how much he hated this city and its people. He now comes to a stop and looks at one house in particular. The windows are boarded up. The house is small, white, and old. The door is boarded up also. Nic glares at the house but looks disturbed. He quietly begins to speak.] “The Definition of Violence” Nic Kraven Alas, I am back to the place where it all started. I am back to the place where the man you see now, “Sick” Nic Raven, was born. I am back to the place where the boy known as Shawn Travis was murdered. This place..this place that I dreaded so much, it helped destroy the boy named Shawn Travis. If you haven't guessed...that boy is me. For the first 15 years of my miserable life, I was succumbed to beatings by my father. The man that had taken part in destroying my childhood. The man that had destroyed the life of Shawn Travis.. [Nic looks visibly disturbed bringing back the memories that he wanted to lock away forever. He slowly walks up the steps of the house, and begins to pull the boards that is shielding the door. He kicks and pulls harder as the first board comes off. He does this for a few minutes longer until all the boards have been pulled off. Nic now kicks the door in and slowly walks inside. At first glance, we see the living room on the left side. He takes a gasp of air and begins to speak again] “The Definition of Violence” Nic Kraven Theres nothing here anymore.. Its all gone. Right here..right here was where all my beatings took place. Nowhere else. Right here was where I was succumbed to punches after punches, kick after kick. There was no one to save me. Nobody was ever there to save me. My cries of help only brought more pain. And what about my mother? My mother..she just stood there. Staring as I was beaten to a bloody pulp. Why couldn't I be saved? Why was I suppose to be the one to succumb to these beatings? Why couldn't I defend my self? The fact of the matter was...there was no one. I was always alone. Even from the start. I was known as the misfit as school. I was shunned, and I was endured to bullying. They came at me like animals going after its prey. And like a frightened child, I ran. I ran like any frightened child would. I ran home, as if hoping someone would at least comfort me. My father was there, drinking his life away. He was clearly drunk, at one glance at me, he came after me looking for another beating. I ran again; but only a short distance before my father caught me. He brought me inside, took his belt off, and whipped me. As he whipped me, I lost hope. I lost hope of ever being saved from this hell hole... I knew right then, that I could only rely on myself and no other. [Nic looks visibly depressed as he relives the memories of his past. He now walks over to what looks like a blood stain on the carpet. Nic stares at it closely before speaking again] “The Definition of Violence” Nic Kraven At this spot, was where my mother would walk out of my life forever. It was just like any normal day. I was in the middle of another beating handed to me by my heartless father. While whipping me, he turned his attention to my mother. He stopped and went after my mother. He whipped her, and beat her to a bloody pulp. This was how this stain came to be. After that beating, my mother left. She had snuck out during the night and left with her possessions. This would change my childhood forever. You see, once my mother was out of the way, my father was free to do anything to me he wanted to. My mother...she was the one who stopped my father when the beatings had gotten to far. Once my mother left, the beatings became more violent. Every time my father came at me, fear would consume me. My eyes would go blind with tears. And then...I would feel pain beyond everything I have experienced before. Do you know how that feels? Do you?! [Nic strokes his hair back as he begins to walk out of the house. On the outside, the cold winter air blows hard. The sun has now risen as a Red with white stripes Hybrid drives by. Nic stands on the steps watching it go by with a hint of sadness.] “The Definition of Violence” Nic Raven At these steps, my life would change forever. I was 15 at the time. My father had just returned from the store and he had made me carry the groceries. I came into the house carrying the groceries but I accidentally dropped the milk carton. My father became outraged. He took me dragged me by my hair and started beating to an inch of my life. I begged..I begged for him to stop. He didn't. He beat me harder and harder. He stopped a few minutes later and went into the closet. He came out a second later with a baseball bat. He swung at me. He missed and at that point, I fought back. He swung at me again but I ducked and kicked him in the gut. He dropped the bat. I picked it up and swung. It hit him in the head sending him to the ground. I felt a rush of supremacy and I hit him again. He bled..and he begged. I laughed at how pathetic he was and swung again. When I had stopped, he was almost dead. I left at that point and never turned back until now. My parents..they despised me. They hated me. There was never a hint of love. [Nic showing a gleam of hatred in his eyes, sits down with his head bowed and continues to speak.] “The Definition of Violence” Nic Kraven Alas now, I am on my own. I am scarred, trapped in a cold cruel world. A heart filled with vengeance, and a soul of fury brought upon by unjust pain. When I unleash my pain upon my victims in the ring, while the world is watching, I picture my victims to be my father. It fuels me as I exact pain on them. This will not be different as I do the same thing to my opponent, Mr Markowitz and Triple G this upcoming MNM, as I have done many years past. Though this time, I have a partner in Peter Kaymakcian. Kaymakcian, the ICW Champion, a championship that I never did get get a chance for. But what does it matter now? I am now on MNM, where the strong will survive while the weak will suffer. Kaymakcian, when I look at you, I see hate inside of you. Theres no hiding it Kaymakcian. Like me, you grew up having to fight for most of your life. We are both more alike than you know. Yet your attitude speaks for itself. Kaymakcian, you have left broken bodies in your path on the way to the ICW Championship. This is what I like in a tag team partner. Yet, you seem hateful of me. Why is that? Perhaps, the pain and hatred in your past has caught up to you? Or does a man like me intimidates a man like you? Nonetheless, it does not matter now. The fact is, we are both partners whether we like it or not. Triple G...Mr Markowitz, when you face me in the ring, prepare to face The Age of Apocalypse. You see April 13, 2009, I promise you won't walk out without feeling my pain and my anguish I have stored for so long. They say when in adversity, it builds character, but in reality, adversity reveals it. Oh, we will see soon enough. When adversity comes knocking, will you face it like men? Or will you run like children? [Nic looks up showing an evil smile. He chuckles for a moment pondering what he will do to the team that call themselves Blood Fuze and then speaks again] “The Definition of Violence” Nic Kraven Blood Fuze...when I see you two, I see men that can't fare for themselves so they decide to bond together. Mr Markowitz, you arrogant piece of trash. Your nothing but a little punk. I hear what you call me. A freak...a twisted freak who talks to himself. Well Markowitz, I'm more of a twisted freak then you'll ever know. I've hurt people...I've made them cry and scream for mercy, I've even done the unthinkable and break body parts. You see, when you two step in the ring with me this upcoming Mayhem, you won't be facing a man. You'll be facing a demon. A demon from your dreams. A demon rising from beneath the earth destined to destroy anybody in its path. You two...you party like theres no tomorrow. You party like drunken idiots. This you see, will be your downfall. Instead of partying, you should be worrying what Kaymakcian and I will do to you two this upcoming week. Instead of partying, you should be praying. Praying that the fury we unleash on you won't be the end of your demise. [Nic smirks upon picturing Blood Fuze's demise. Slowly, he leans back against the wall of the house and speaks] “The Definition of Violence” Nic Kraven The way I see it, Markowitz you are a man incapable of success so you form a Tag Team known as Blood Fuze and hide behind your little cousin...Triple G. Triple G...he..he is the weakest link in this Tag Team. Face it, Triple G, you are insuperior to Markowitz. Like Jeff Hardy is to his brother Matt. Like Christian is to Edge. G, you will always be just a little behind. Doubt will always be there. Doubt will surround you every waking moment. Doubt if you will ever surpass your cousin. Doubt if you could even make it big on your own here. Even if you don't show it...there will always be doubts. You know deep down in your heart that this is the case. And you BOTH know deep down in your hearts that you can't beat Kaymakcian and I. Its no secret we don't get along, but its no secret either that both of you will turn on each other if you even had the slightest chance at the MWE Undisputed Championship. Tag Teams never last...and it might just be the end of Blood Fuze this upcoming Mayhem. Bring everything you have to this match, because you just might not walk out... [Nic chuckles as he says those last words. He begins to stand up slowly as people in the neighborhood wake up on this morning. Nic is now shown walking down the sidewalk as his figure fades the further he goes until we can not see him anymore. The scene now slowly fades into darkness.]
