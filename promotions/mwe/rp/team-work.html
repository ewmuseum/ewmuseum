Team Work

Nov 30 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Devlin Sharpe is in a car driving at 130 miles per hour towards the emirates stadium, one of the biggest stadiums in England, Devlin Sharpe: I cant believe the MWE is giving us a bunch of newbies do they honestly think they will come back after we break their bones and spirits? Devlin got out of the car as he arrived at the stadium, the camera pans to show the brightly lit stadium and Devlin Sharpe in comparison, he was tiny, then the camera panned to Devlin sharped once again, he shivered, he then reallised where he was, he was in england and as always, it was cold as fuck, the cold was bringing tears to his eyes and he had to squint Devlin Sharpe: You may ask me why I decided to bring you here...well I brought you here to show the MWE fans the true meaning of teamwork, you see the english have a very team based game, we call it football you guys call it soccer Devlin enters the lobby of the stadium and walks towards the stadium on the way there he passes many articles of history which describe the achievements of the club such as trophies and new articles and busts of famous players heads Devlin Sharpe: You see in football, good teamwork can lead to greatness as you can see around me, all this, has been achieved through teamwork Devlin Sharpe passes many portraits of former teams as he glances at them Devlin Sharpe: Many teams come and go, allies become enemies, enemies become allies, in the world of football which is not that different from the world of wrestling, teamwork is everything and the longer you work with a person the better you are as a team The Camera pans on the win loss record of the team based on years Devlin Sharpe: Me and my brother Ryan Have been tagging for a very long time now, we know each other very well, we know that we can dominate this tag team division Devlin Sharpe smirks as he stares at the F.A Cup Devlin Sharpe: Scotty McKnight and Chris Cassidy you have no experience...you don't know tag team... you dont even know wrestling Devlin Sharpe suddenly runs to the pitch, the wind blasting him in the face, tears well up in his eyes from the cold as he screams at the top of his voice Devlin Sharpe: WE WILL FUCKING DESTROY YOU It echoes around the whole stadium
