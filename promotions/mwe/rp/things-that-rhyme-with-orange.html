Things That Rhyme With Orange

Oct 23 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
QUOTE Yeah, it was rushed, I did what I could. Blue=Not Zak Orange=Zak Even after everything I’ve gone through in life, even after the way I’ve fucked up my life countless times, I never thought that my choices would lead me down the path I’ve taken. I’ve lost some of the people closest to me, I’ve seen my life spiral down the drain, yet I still found a way to rise above it all. Never once did I ask for help, not once did I look to someone else to show me the way. I preferred independence, and it helped make me who I am. I admit, I chastised Kevin for going to see a therapist. Had I known that he was trying to nail her though, I would have patted him on the back. She was a great lay, there is no doubt about that…and I find it funny that there is yet another example of how I succeeded where Kevin failed. The thing about Sarah though, was that she got me wondering. She had been someone to talk to, and even though it wasn’t an official therapy session, I have to admit that it helped. Who can deny it really? After talking with her, I was able to put on the best performance of my life, and survive one of the most brutal battles in MWE history. Now here I sit, a few months later, with yet another career defining match sitting before me, and it terrifies the living hell out of me. I need someone to talk to, but I can’t turn to Sarah. Apparently she is one of those girls who frowns upon sneaking out after sex. Who woulda thought that? Anyways, I made a couple calls, talked to some people, and I was able to find a decent therapist in St. Louis. Sitting in his office, waiting for my first session to start was quite the odd feeling. Here I was, a grown ass man, rich as hell, stoned off my ass, sitting in a room by myself as I waited for someone to tell me to describe my feelings. It wasn’t something I was used to, but I figured that if it helped me once, it would help me again. After a bit of a wait, his secretary stuck her pretty little face out of the doorway, telling me that he was ready to see me. She led me to his office, which was a bit different than I expected. Most people think of a therapists office as plain, boring, and just all around lame. This guy was different though, you could tell he was a special kind of guy. Sports posters were hung everywhere, with a flat-screen TV mounted on the wall and instead of the stereotypical therapists couch, there was a nice leather recliner in the middle of the room. His secretary told me to have a seat, and that he would be with me in a moment. A few minutes passed, and I heard the door open and there he was. My back was to him, and I didn’t feel like turning around but I heard him pull a chair up. I took a deep breath, and waited for him to speak. A minute passed without either of us talking, and I began to wonder what the problem was. I started to get out of my chair, which was when he finally spoke. “Stay seated please, there is no need for you to get up.” I was a bit confused by this dude. He was pretty damn rude for a therapist, and I wasn’t sure it was worth my time. Ain’t that a bitch? Two minutes in and I was already about to slap the taste out of this guys mouth. “Now tell me Mr. Warner, do you feel that your unbridled agression comes from a lack of love as a child?” “What are you talking abo..” “Is it because your mother didn’t hug you enough? Or was it because your father hugged you too much?” “What the hell are you talking about?” “You know what I mean, your father would sneak into your bedroom and night, and give you a little extra lovin. It’s ok, it isn’t your fault.” I had enough at that point. I damn near jumped out of my chair, and spun around ready to unload on this guy. As soon as I saw who it was though, my fist stopped. “You have got to be kidding me…what, all the KFC’s in the area were closed today?” Yeah, that’s right, it wasn’t a therapist that had been talking to me. It was everyone’s favorite stereotypical black man, the Ebony to my Ivory, DeAngelo Williams. You see, for months I have tried to get De into MWE, but apparently he is banned from the company. I think it had to do with impregnating someone’s daughter, but we aren’t really supposed to talk about it until after the lawsuit. “You know Zak, it’s that kind of talk that sets my people back more and more each day.” Zak: “No, it’s the way you constantly reinforce every negative stereotype that has ever been made.” “You are crazy man, I never do that kinda shit.” He had to be kidding me. DeAngelo was a walking, talking, breathing billboard for every racial stereotype that had ever been made. Ace Cannon would have a field day with him. “Ok, then answer me this….what are your three favorite foods?” “I’m not answering that. You already know, don’t make me say it out loud. I rest my case. As much as I rag on the man though, he has always been there for me through thick and thin. I reached into my coat, and pulled my handy dandy portable camera, and tossed it to him. “What do ya say, one more time for old times sake?” He didn’t even have to answer, he just smiled and pointed the camera. This is where I finally get to say what I’ve been wanting to say. For too long have I held back and played the role of a guy who actually gave a damn. For too long did I sit back and do what was asked, without any bitching. Well fuck that, I’m tired of it. That’s why there are no fancy lights, or slick settings. I’m not going to jump out of a plane, or play with a crystal ball. I’m not going to hold a board room hostage to get my point across. I’m just going to say what I have to say, and if you don’t want to listen then change the fucking channel. "After months and months of ups and downs, wins and losses, injuries and rehab…I finally get what I deserve. For too long have I sat around and watched as this company dicked around and treated deserving people like shit. People like Mason Resoan and Joe Sharpman kept getting pushed, and people like CSB and I got shit on. Yet look at this place now. It’s been a total changing of the guard, and I love it. Look at this Pay Per View for example. Normally, we would see the same old recycled bullshit, like Sinc versus Mason, D.C. versus Brian Michaels, and Paige versus some sort of testosterone." Yeah, I made a lesbian joke, so what? She is open about it so I feel NO shame, especially after she kissed me in the ring and never called me again. Wow, talk about the pot calling the kettle black right? Anyways, this is a side of me that most of you have never seen, and there is a reason for that. My temper is my greatest enemy, and I do what I can to hold it back. Sometimes though, it just can’t be helped. "Instead, we have CSB headlining, myself about to win yet ANOTHER title, and Zack Crash given the chance to show why he is the single most hyped superstar in the company. D.C. had a point before our match, when he said if anyone should be leading a revolution in this place, it should be me. Yet, just like everything else, it was just words. This so called “Revolution” that D.C. thought he was leading, ended up being nothing more than empty words and paintball guns. So let me go on the record right now, and say fuck D.C., fuck Paige, fuck Grand Mystique, fuck Sinc Mercer…fuck em all. Fuck M-28-6, fuck Vanguard, fuck Uncultured everything…that’s all the past. The future is now, and the REAL takeover starts at Turmoil. It must TERRIFY the suits up top that this pay per view could destroy everything they ever built. They must be shitting their pants knowing that when the final pyro goes off, and the final bell rings, that the gold will belong to the right people. Eric Derf has money in the bank and will beat Paige straight, Zack Crash will continue to elevate the X-TV title to new heights, and my brother from an Irish mother, Mr. CSfuckingB will be champion. Say what you want about how good our opponents are, but just know that we are that much better." You can hear my voice filling with emotion, and you know that I’m speaking from the heart. This isn’t scripted, this isn’t planned. Some might call this “shooting”, I call it being perfectly honest. DeAngelo holds the camera steady, keeping his usual cracks to himself. "MWE Management won’t tell you this, but they are absolutely terrified about what could happen if we ran shit. Why do you think they really suspended me when I “got caught with weed”. I’ve never hidden my lifestyle, and they knew what they were getting into when they signed me. The suspension was just another way of trying to quell the revolution before it could even start. I’m not stupid though, I knew what was up and I called them out on it. You wanna know what their brilliant solution was? They offered me a shot at GM on the last Mayhem. They thought that they could essentially BRIBE me as a way of admitting they fucked up. Most people would have jumped on that chance, but I said fuck it. Because I knew that no matter how hard I fought, I wouldn’t have won. I haven’t figured out what would have happened though, because they are some crafty fuckers in the back. Maybe D.C. would have came out and “tried to help me”, or maybe they would have paid some jobber like Oblivion to come and screw me over. Either way, I knew I wouldn’t have won so I didn’t want to waste my time." Is this honestly true? I still think so. There is no proof, and most people would call me paranoid but I can’t help it. Every company has favorites and it has always been that way. GWC had Kevin Hardaway and Joe Ragnal, MWE has D.C. and Sinc. Does saying what I think have the chance of backfiring? You bet your sweet ass it does. But I’ve already started, and the passion won’t let me stop. It’s got a vice grip on my emotions, and I can’t pry it off. "That pissed them off pretty bad, so that’s why I’m in the position I am today. I have to go up against one of the 2 people in this company that I consider a friend in Brad Kane, and a man who I have beaten more times than Bobby Brown beat Whitney. I call him Satan Lite, you all know him as the Virus. You see, I have NO doubt in my mind that I will be walking out of that building with the gold around my waist. There isn’t a single person in this company who wants to win more than I do. I don’t want to win for the fame, I don’t give a fuck about having my name on a piece of metal. I just want to stick it to the doubters, to the haters, to EVERYONE who has tried to hold me back. I want to show that there has been a transfusion of new blood in the place, and there isn’t a damn thing you can do to stop it." I sat back down in the recliner, but I don’t relax. Leaning forward, I glare into the camera with a calm yet intense stare. Think of Keanu Reeves in The Matrix, but with more talent and charisma. "Most people are quick to point out that I’ve never beaten Brad Kane, but they fail to realize I only faced him once. Hell, back then I knew I wasn’t going to beat him, I was just trying to keep myself from getting my head kicked in. Things have changed now though, he isn’t the same man he used to be. He loves this business, but it doesn’t love him. He has nothing left to accomplish in the industry. He has beaten everyone from me, to Kevin, to his own brother Spike. Granted, another title or two would look nice, but he knows it isn’t worth the risk. He has a family to take care of, he has a wife who loves him. It’s never easy to walk away from something you love, but I have a feeling this will be his last hurrah. Brad, you know I respect you and I look up to you. You know I care about you like you were my own brother, and for that we will always be connected. So I beg of you, I PLEAD to you, just stay home. People won’t think any less of you, they will still know you are one of the greatest to lace up a pair of boots. You know, deep down in your heart of hearts, that I’m right. Do you really want to take a bump one day, and never be able to lift little Lacey up? Do you want to land a move wrong, and never be able to hold Megan in your arms again? It’s just not worth it Brad. You had your Road of Reckless Jack…but that road ended. Ride off into the sunset and never look back." I don’t want to speak ill of Brad, and I won’t. The man taught me pretty much everything I know today, and if it wasn’t for him I would have quit back in GWC. He is one of the best, and deserves to go down in history as such. I don’t want to see him tarnish his name because he feels like he has something to prove. It isn’t him, it never will be. "Leon Leon Leon…how am I not surprised that it comes down to us? Time after time, our paths seem to cross. Yet no matter the company, no matter the match type…the end result is always the same. I’ve knocked you out, choked you out, and pinned you in the middle of the ring. I’ve beaten you one on one, two on two, and three on three. There has been chairs, bats, and table involved, and yet you have STILL never beaten me. Why is that Leon? You can sit there and make every excuse under the sun, but the fact of the matter remains that I am just better. Than. You. There isn’t a single thing about you that is superior. I am faster, stronger, better looking, have better friends who don’t fuck my girlfriend…you get the point. To quote the lyrical geinus known as Eminem…”Your style’s generic, mines authentic made.” You try so hard to be big and bad, when you are just like every other Joe, Frank, and Bill in the world." In case you couldn’t tell, I hate Leon Roberts. There isn’t a particle in my body that doesn’t wish to see his face caved in, his career ended. He is the lowest of the low, and doesn’t deserve to breath the same air as people like me. "Tell me something Leon…when you heard about this match, how hard did you try to get it changed? Did you run to Eli, begging to put it off for another week, another month? It’s bad enough that you had to face Brad Kane…but me as well? I hope you have enjoyed your title reign, because it ends at Turmoil. You see, you can go fuck your girlfriend as much as you want, beat up random hobos, but in the end you are nothing more than a two-bit smoke and mirrors act. Nobody is buying your little devil act man, it’s time to move on. It wasn’t scary in GWC, it wasn’t scary in APWC, so why would it be scary here? Speaking of acts…you want to sit there and talk about my drug use, and act like I have some sort of problem? At least I admit I like to do drugs…you sit there and act like you only do them when you get a lil bump on your head. I’ll give you 5-1 odds that if I walked into your room and opened up your bedside drawer, I’d find enough pills to make Ozzy fucking Osborne shudder. It’s people like you that give the hardliners like Kevin reason to hate drugs. The addicts like you, who hide behind prescriptions and doctors orders are worse than people like me." I can’t help but to chuckle, and for a moment so brief, the old Zak Warner is back. The seriousness vanishes, but I quickly regain my composure. There is a time for joking, and this isn’t it. "You are one of the most fake people I have ever met, and it absolutely sickens me. Ask anyone who knows me, anyone who has ever met me, and they will tell you that above all else, I hate fake people. I’m one of the realest motherfuckers you will EVER have the privilege of knowing, and I want the people around me to be the same way. Everything about you is fake Leon, from your friends to your girlfriends tits. No real friend would try to and then SUCCEED in fuckin his best friend’s woman. Even after everything that happened with me and Kev, not ONCE did I go for Angie. You know why? Cause I’m a REAL FUCKIN MAN." Yeah, I’m screaming, so what? Can’t a guy let his emotions get the best of him once in awhile? Isn’t that what makes us truly human, and differentiates us from the rest of the mouth breathers? I could sit here and be monotones and bore you all to death, but that isn’t me. "You all would think that I would have Leon’s back, seeing as how we were both in GWC. You would think that I would feel the same sort of loyalty to him as I do to CSB and Brad. Fuck that, I never have nor will I ever give a shit about Leon. He tried ending my career, as well as DeAngelo’s back in the day. I haven’t forgotten about that, and I’ve just been waiting for the right time. I’ve let the anger build up through the years, I’ve sat by and watched him blow his way to the top. I’ve been waiting for the right time to strike, and that time is Turmoil. Remember folks, this message isn’t endorsed by CSB, Zack Crash, MWE, or anyone else. The thoughts and views expressed in this are strictly of my own, and if you don’t like it then fuck off. I’m done caring about how people see me, and about if I get TV time or not. If management wants to put me as a curtain jerker, so be it. They will have to deal with the consequences when their stars end up with broken kneecaps. Consider this all to be a warning. To the people like D.C. and Sinc, who just don’t know when to quite. To the people like Paige and GM who hold onto the spotlight just a bit too long. Change is comin. Love it or hate it, there ain’t a DAMN thing you can do about. So shoot up my Addicts, the Revolution is starting." With a smile, DeAngelo clicks the camera off. We stand up, and embrace in a totally heterosexual way. "Good to have you back man.” “It’s good to be back.” Fade.
