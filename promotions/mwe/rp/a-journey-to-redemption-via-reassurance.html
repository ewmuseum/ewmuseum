A Journey to Redemption Via Reassurance; A Grand Mystique RP

Sep 21 2009

The Grand Mystique

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
From coming in so high as World Champ, the events at Monday Night Mayhem had humbled the Grand Mystique in a way that had never happened to him before. Defeats against Whitman, Resoan or DC had only managed to spur him on and he could say that each contributed to his career in their own little ways. But this was different. Though it appeared insignificant after it was shown that D.C. had in fact taken the legal pinfall, the outcome stung him. It was forcing him to look back at the journey that was his career to this point in MWE where his personal and professional life had changed so dramatically. We see the Grand Mystique in a room that is nondescript and practically empty. There is only enough light through a small gap in a window to make out the current World Champ who is taking the time out to reassess his current path, because if nothing else, there would be no other reason for him to be in a room that is so dingy and dull. He is pacing from right to left, clearly anxious. For once, the MWE World Championship is not on his person; it is discarded carelessly on the bed behind him. He picks a mobile phone out of his pocket and presses a phone number into the pad. He holds it to his ear. Ring, ring ring.....It goes to answer phone. Eli: “This is Eli Silver bitches! Leave a message, do whatever you have to do and I will consider getting back to you.” GM huffs out, frustrated that when he needs to speak with Eli and get some shit sorted, he can’t. He hangs up and tries again. Eli: “This is Eli Silver bitches! Lea-” He hangs up and slams his phone down out of annoyance. He knows he has to speak to Eli, even if he doesn’t particularly want to. Not after the events of Mayhem anyway. He picks up his phone and dials one more time. He listens to the tedious message again and then speaks. “Eli, its GM. Look.......I want this contract shit sorted once and for all. I’ll take the five million downside deal. I’m not happy but I just want it sorted. It’s not the priority right now.” He considers hanging up there and then but then considers the one more point that he wants to make. “By the way Eli, I really want to defend this belt at some point and before the pay-per-view. Any chance of making that happen?” He hangs up and chucks his phone carelessly on the floor. He sits down on the end of the tidily made single bed with his elbows on his knees and his head in his hands. He is pensively considering what he could do next. “It’s easy to be philosophical when the dream that you held for so long is falling on its’ ears around you. All the work that has gone into making it happen and it’s slowly falling down the pan.” He is unhappy and it’s clear from his tone that he is feels embarrassed and humble – GM’s least desired sentiments and something he hoped would pass eventually. He is looking down at something that he holds. Turning it monotonously in his hand, he traces its’ rough lines with his fingertips. “And I’m left wondering whether it was all worth it.” This is GM in a state of angst and emotion. It was rare to see GM in a mood that didn’t show him as intense or aggressively determined. This is him showing that he cares so, so much that it looks to be painful. “I’ve been described as underrated, perverse and sadistic. Only recently had “champion” been used to describe me. it thought it would suit me so effortlessly well.” “I guess I was......wrong. And Zack Crash will probably tell you this and explain his arguements in great detail.” The hesitation and tension in his voice is palpable. GM rarely admits to being wrong, let alone being concerned about being wrong. “Suddenly everything has come into perspective. The contract isn’t important. It never was. The only thing that was important was the belt being in my possession. I wouldn’t care if people didn’t think I was worthy but now everyone is chipping in saying I will drop the belt at the first chance.” “Maybe that’s what’s in my future? Zack may tell you that too.” The buzz of Crowning Glory has faded away to leave only flat paranoia that makes him feel like he is running on empty. “I work so hard to avoid burning out after Crowning Glory that it was inevitable I would do so. I was planning on riding the crest of a wave of personal euphoria that I didn’t see the iceberg.” “Sometimes I think that taking off the mask made me lose something. The intimidation I used to drive into their hearts. The drive that ground so many hopes and dream beneath my foot.” The words barely make it out of his mouth as he allows himself to be buried by fatigue. He exhales, trying to rid the stress from his system but he fails. He falls backward and collapses on the bed. He has run out of energy. His eyes fall shut and in seconds he is unconscious and looks vaguely relaxed. Or as relaxed as he can be. The scene changes. Fading slowly into white fuzz, there are voices and they begin to get louder. Even in his subconscious, the mind is racing. These voices are familiar and they pound his ears. Maxx: “You suck as a world champion” DC: “When Eric Derf can pin you then you should just give up, should drop the belt and leave MWE.” Brian Michaels: “You aren’t even the shining light at the head of this company; the leader that you promised to become is nothing but false dawns.” Negativity is bashing against GM’s skull like floodwater against storm-ravaged beach defences. Slowly, the pressure builds and the voices intensify. Sinc Mercier: “MWE is just too far and Crowning Glory was a fluke” David Whitman: “This belt should be on a future Hall of Famer, not a train wreck who clusterfucks his way through a career and gets lucky enough to pull down the brass ring.” Eric Derf: “Crash was right, you’re a fake and playing at being champion.” This last sentence reverberates, causing loud echoes. There is a series of soft clicking noises, the soft sound of a door clicking shut. A figure emerges from the left of the screen. It is immediately familiar and only takes a second at the face to identify it. The masked GM looks steely determined as we were so used to seeing him before his dramatic unmasking. “They are wrong. They are so very wrong.” He shakes his head; clearly unimpressed. “Hardaway and Sharpman let you down. And DC, that bitch may as well have left you to fight two on one for all the good that he did. No champion survives those odds with DC as their partner.” “It was practically three on one; it’s no wonder that you got pinned.” Bitter maybe, but this masked figure that looks and sounds like GM is making a lot of sense. “You have escaped with your title on your shoulder, which is more important than having your pride intact. It is more important that you live up to your ambitions, dream and aim to be the champ you expect to be.” “You can recover from this. No one is strong enough to stop you. Derf and Crash may be the future but you are the present, the reality. It’s their fault if they can’t get to grips with your version.” He is surprisingly calm. No big gestures, only stern but fair and encouraging words. “You will decide the future of this belt and it’s not going anywhere for a long time. It may be open season on you at the moment but the belt still proves you are the number one guy. You have the drive, the passion and the belt is your obsession. It won’t leave your side until you are ready to move on past it.” “The legacy you are creating is what you make of it. Defeat is always humbling even if it wasn’t your fault. But it needn’t have the catastrophic effects that you seem to think it will.” The screen flashes again. The masked GM disappears behind a dull screen of fuzz. It fades quickly to show a blue backdrop with red MWE logos falling diagonally across. It appears that GM is in a studio. He is restless and visibly unenthusiastic, which is unsurprising given recent events. He paces around in front of the camera, which holds it focus on the backdrop behind him. The MWE championship dangles from his right hand. “I’ve been misguided by my own ambition. The thrill of reaching the top of the mountain loses a little of its lustre. I guess what I am trying to say is that this title is not the most important in my life. And I do not have total control of the MWE. I could lose the belt next week, at Turmoil or even at WrestleWars but it will all be out of my hands.” “But as champion or otherwise I refuse to be anyone’s bitch. I’ve begged to prove my worth to the world. To show that I am worthy of respect that this belt affords but it seems I’m being deprived of the chance to defend this very prestigious title.” He is talking in a very slow and deliberate manner. Forced by the events of last week, GM knows that shit happens and very occasionally, life tries to throw it all down on you at once. When it happens, it is extremely humbling. He raises the belt. He casts a forlorn look at it before putting it on his shoulder. The setting seems to match his mood; quiet and uncomplicated. It certainly seems to help him form what he is trying to say. “I hate being defeated and I hate being out of control. Yet that is exactly what happened. Crash pinned DC and took the control away from me. For that, he deserves real credit.” “The XTV champ, the man behind the revitalisation of the entire division deserved his wins last week. He is on the path to stardom for sure. And he deserves to know that his efforts last week have forced me to reconsider some things.” “One fact above however is that I am not unbeatable.” While GM has tasted defeat as often as most and knows the belt provides no protection, it hurts a little to say that as GM is a proud man above all else. “But he talked some shit that I cannot allow to pass without consequence. There will be consequences; there has to be.” “People need to understand. Maybe they have forgotten about the small details. He hasn’t done his research to find out more about me has he? I hate that.” GM is able to have a small chuckle at himself, even during this moment of absolute seriousness. “I have to say that sharing my face with the world has taken away a little of the mystery. I’ve destroyed careers and made my own name by taking people out and I did it behind the image of this mask.” He pulls the ever-famous mask from his back pocket with his free hand. He puts the belt across his shoulder before paying attention to the famous disguise. “The world always wondered who was pulling the trigger. Maxx, Windy Bridges, Brian Michaels.....big names one and all and yet they were all clueless. I demolished them all at the peak of their careers. I surprised the world one superstar at a time and this was the symbol behind it all.” “Zack can talk about smoke and mirrors all he wants. He couldn’t be further from the truth. I’m far too direct for my own good.” This statement is so very true. Zack was not to know that GM could simply recall any of five or ten events to prove this fact. It would make him look more than a little foolish to assume a “mystical” name would lead to actions in the same vein. “It is very easy to be philosophical about the lack of surprise. It is now all too clear who is behind the smoking barrel. I never hid. I didn’t do sneak attacks and I always explained myself.” He is evidently feeling smug as his confidence flows back a little. He holds the mask on his hand. “I’ve never explained to anyone why I chose to remove it. But I guess the thrill of the chase got too much. There was a touch of ego about it but we are in a business where egos rule.” “Crowning Glory was the biggest high of my life. My career may never top that one most significant of moments. The dream that maybe one on ten of wrestlers here will actually achieve.” “But I did it by being myself, not some nameless image – this mask held me back as much as it got me over. People were scared by this mask but they were terrified by what came with it.” He looks at the mask. Slowly a perverse grin sneaks across his face. As he looks up, a revitalised energy reignites and it is burning through his eyes. “Crowning Glory was supposed to signify a new level of success. Last week was supposed to kick it off in grand style.” “I can’t say I am at all impressed by the events of last week but I get to make up for it now. Zack Crash can finally make the call to his friends and family. Last week wasn’t a fluke....he actually made it to the main event.” “And I’m banking on you continuing to fulfil your potential, because to establish yourself in the main event, you will need to survive crushing disappointment alongside the adrenaline-fuelled highs. Being the World Champion at this level means everything, everyone is waiting for their time, to take their shot, to have people aspiring to be the man that you are” “To live your life as their dream. And although I wish I could say that my title dream was different to that, but I can’t.....that’s exactly what happened. ” “And you know kiddo, I’m happy for you. The youth are the future – you, Derf, CSB. You may be misguided and foolish on occasions but soon this will be your ship. You and your peers will tread the path that I have and with that experience will come a newfound respect for me.” GM’s top lip curls with a vicious sneer to show how resentful he is that he didn’t have it in the first place. “I’m not expecting you to realise that for a very long time. But I hope you do because that is the only way to get the high that you truly crave deep down.” “Even then you need to realise that the buzz will always lead to a crash. No matter what you try, no matter who you become, the crash will bottom you out. I’ve learnt that the hard way.” “You are wrong about me though. I’m no magician. I actually think that you didn’t do your research. Maybe Derf needs to have a word” He leans in and tips his head quietly before whispering. “No magician or smoke and mirrors bullshit could do what I have done, as effectively as I have done it.” He backs away, turning his back to the camera to the camera before returning with trademark energy and bravado. “Ask anyone here, from Brian Michaels to DC to Whitman to Sinc, ask them what I do to get the job done. They may not like my style but I know I have their respect when it comes to being upfront and blatant. When I want something, I am as direct as they come about going about my business to get it.” He pauses, not out of hesitance but to compose himself again. “I questioned myself recently about sharing my face with the world. You may not know that I used to be the most feared name in the company simply because no one knew who I was. No one knew who pulled the trigger but they knew the name all too well and a stream of my victims’ blood followed me down the path.” “With or without this mask, that tale continues to ring true.” He raises the mask again before discarding it off-camera to his right. “That is why I am World Champion and that is why the MWE will be better with me at the helm. You may not believe that I deserve it but what makes you think I care what you think. Not everyone wants to risk their ass to please the fans. Shit, the belt is everything I want and need. I don’t care because whatever you say is what I have heard before and will hear again and again!” He shifts the belt from one shoulder to the other, with deliberate effort. “Your limits are barely enough to get me out of bed in the morning; your best will not even scratch the bottom of the glass ceiling that I stand on. What makes you think you are capable of reaching the stratosphere that is the Main event here? You think pinning DC is an achievement? DC is a shadow of a man. Our match last week was effectively a handicap match because DC can’t cut it anymore and I was crushed by the weight of his overinflated ego.” “But this isn’t about him and his time to suffer will wait for now.” GM hates DC with a passion. Yet he knows Crash deserves to receive a five star performance from his World Champion. That will require full focus on him. “All this name calling probably won’t faze you though, you’ll only think of getting higher and higher. But I can bring you crashing down to earth faster than you ever have experienced jumping out of a plane.” “Our match will be as close as you get to falling out of a plane without a ‘chute. This match is akin to your death wish; you’re just too hyper and buzzed out to care anymore.” GM’s eyes narrow to slits and his brow furrows. He seems to grow a little as he inhales. “That is not how you become someone in this business Zack. Allow me please to demonstrate that on Mayhem. You will find out why I am the World Champion and why I will fight with every sinew in my body for things to stay that way.” And with that, GM walked off. A man once again confident in his own ability; he had allowed his self doubt to pass by. As a result, he has emerged as a man, confident in his own ability to make Zack and the entire federation alert to his revitalised presence.
