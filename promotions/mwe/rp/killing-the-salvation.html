Killing the Salvation

Dec 17 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
** The camera opens and the first image we see is Ace Cannon standing in front of a bathroom mirror. He is not looking at his own reflection, but we can see his forehead is covered by a large bandage as a result of his post match brawl with Joey Dallas. As he continues to not look at himself in the mirror he places his hands on the sink and leans forward a little bit and sighs to himself rather loudly. He finally begins to talk.** Ace: Dallas, it has come to this… ** Ace reaches up toward his head a slowly pulls away the large bandage off his forehead. Once he pulls the bandage off we see the damage that was created at the hands of Joey Dallas. Upon pulling the bandage off Cannon finally looks up and in the mirror and we see the staples that were put in Cannon’s head to close up the wound. Upon Cannon seeing this his face turns to sheer anger.** Ace: Dallas, this right here is the preverbal straw that has broken the camels back. You see Dallas, me and you have a storied history together. A history that is full of deceit, screw jobs, title wins and violence. ** Cannon reaches down and picks up what appears to be a flat head screwdriver. Cannon examines it and laughs manically for a moment before bringing it up to his head and popping out on of the staples. As the staple comes out we hear it fall into the sink and Cannon once again laughs.** Ace: Our story starts out in MWE…. You took out my protégé James Young and ended his career. Then you proceeded to steal my beautiful girlfriend Paige and the both of you injured my knee and tore it to shreds and put me on the shelf indefinitely. As I sat on the sidelines I saw you rise to become one of the best wrestlers MWE has ever seen! I saw you become the United States champion but as I sat at home and rehabbed my knee I started to think about getting revenge on you. ** Cannon takes the screwdriver and pops out another staple and looks in the mirror with a cold blank stare. He breaks his gaze after what feels like a few tense moments.** Ace: Finally I came back to MWE but I couldn’t wrestle. My knee was still to fragile to step into that ring and kick your ass, but I got an offer that I couldn’t refuse. That offer was for me to come to MWE as commissioner where I could use my power to make your life hell. ** Cannon once again lifts the screwdriver up and pops out another staple. He takes a deep breath and anger begins to wash over his face.** Ace: As I started to make your life hell you disappeared. You vanished and no one knew what happened too you or where you went. Word was that you were scared of what I had in store for you and you went off and faked getting shot in some kind of botch robbery attempt and when I found this out I was upset. I was upset because I couldn’t make you pay for almost ending my career making my life a miserable living hell! ** Cannon once again repeats and removes another staple from his head and this time he winces in pain and this time rightfully so because blood starts to trickle down Cannon’s head. Upon seeing the blood drip down his forehead Cannon gives a slight smirk.** Ace: As time wore on I eventually got my life back together and rebounded and found myself in EW where I went on to win the world title and was atop the pro wrestling mountain. Eventually EW went out of business and I went to an upstart company called RevPro were I was there marketable star. I was put into there world title tournament against a young upstart named THK. As we wrestled our match I took him out and was about to pin him but before I could do that I was attacked…. Attacked by you and you ended up costing me the match! ** The blood continues to trickle down Cannon’s head as he takes the screwdriver and pops out another staple. This time his expression is blank as the stable comes out. As he sees the blood get closer to his mouth he sticks his tongue out and tastes his blood! He smirks once more at the taste of his blood.** Ace: You cost me this match and then we inked the contact for a huge grudge match to take place that was a year in the making. As the match rolled around I was getting nervous and once we got into a ring the butterflies were taken over by hate! All the things that had happen between us began to race through my mind and then the bell rang and I proceeded to give you the ass kicking that had been bottled up for a year and right as I was about to beat you DC stuck his nose where itbe didn’t belong and he cost me the match… ** Cannon moves to the wound on his head and plucks out the last staple and lets out a sigh of relief that all the staples are now out.** Ace: Needless to say I was pissed that you got away with a victory and as I tried to get revenge you hid behind that stupid canuck retard Windy Bridges and formed Brokeback Express where all you did was hog the spotlight and avoid me, but I let it go and went on about my business and then RevPro closed it’s doors. ** Cannon looks in the mirror and shakes his head..** Ace: Now after RevPro closed we both ended up here in MWE. When we arrived we instantly started feuding again. It started right as I was about to kick DC’s head off and become champion of Genesis you came to ring side and grabbed my foot and stopped me from doing so. As I reached down to pull you into the ring you spat in my face and from there the rest is history… DC capitalized and gained victory over me and I was more then pissed! Following that little altercation me and you were booked into a tag team title match ad one another’s partners.. ** Cannon stops and sighs.** Ace: As we became a reluctant team we gave one another zero respect and I kicked you in your damn melon and somehow we became champions… We then had a vicious brawl that left us both bloody and bruised and that leads us to now…. Present day. ** Cannon looks in the mirror one last time. He turns around and exit’s the bathroom and the camera follows him out the door. Cannon walks through his house and down hallway and out into a large foyer. He continues to walk and opens a sliding glass door and walks out into the bright sunlight. Cannon walks over to his right and standing in his yard is wooden cross. It is about seven feet tall and four foot wide. Cannon turns his back to the camera and looks the cross over and he nods approvingly at it’s presence. Cannon then turns back toward the camera as the cross casts a large shadow over him.** Ace: Now in present day all I hear about is how you are calling yourself the savior and how your going to save MWE from everything that is bad including Ace Cannon…. Well Dallas, it’s not going to happen. You can’t beat me, I am bound my hate to destroy you ! Dallas, you see you ruined my life once when you destroyed my knee and took my girlfriend and when that happened I had nothing…. And as sat at home by myself I had all these horrible ideas and thoughts enter my mind. **As soon as Cannon finishes that last sentence he quickly takes his eyes off the camera and looks down at the ground before continuing.** Ace: So one night as my life was at an all-time low I got in my car and drove out into the desert and just pulled over and sat there not knowing what to do with my life. As I sat there I opened my glove box and there it was… My thirty-eight special revolver. As I took it out I shoved the cold metal barrel in my mouth and sat there with my hand on the trigger with all intensions of pulling it. **Cannon stops and takes a very deep breath as he continues to look at the ground.** Ace: As I was about to pull the trigger my phone rang. I sat the gun down and took the call and it was MWE Offices offering me the commissioner position. As I accepted the job all the doubts and everything was washed away from me because I knew that with my new position your days were numbered and you then disappeared. ** Cannon looks up from the ground with hate and disdain written all over his face.** Ace: But this time there is no disappearing! There is no avoiding your date with me… There is no DC sitting at ringside to help you. It will be just you in me and a brutal match that will make one of us a clear cut winner. I have been anticipating this day ever since our last encounter and as the clock ticks closer and closer to our date the butterflies in my stomach get stronger and stronger but I know when the time comes and the bell rings those butterflies will turn into pure hate for you! ** Cannon takes a deep breath and continues.** Ace: All this stuff I have said today is one hundred and ten percent words that are driven by hate. My action will also be driven by hate and as I beat you down in that match I will turn your lights out and once I do there will be a surprise… ** Canon turns his back to the camera and looks up at the ever present wooden cross.** Ace: That surprise is this wooden cross. You see, you call yourself the savior… But I see through you. I know this is nothing but an act for you to line your pockets with the fans money so you can live the high life! Your simply a wolf draping yourself in sheep’s wool to fool everyone, but I know better and I know that deep down inside of you living this lie is eating at you and making you reconsider your stance as the Savior for all these stupid, fat and pathetic fans. ** Cannon continues to look at the cross and then into the air. As he looks into the air two black vultures circle overhead and land on either side of the cross.** Ace: Now this leads me to this cross standing here… So Dallas, since you call yourself the Savior I plan to give you the treatment of one! After I turn your lights off I will bring you to this cross and you will then see what it feels like to be a so called Savior! You will pay for your sins against me and the physical and mental anguish you caused me. It won’t be pretty! I can gurantee that the thousands in the arena and millions at home won’t want to see this and Dallas you won’t want any part of this…. But you have no choice, the warrant on your head has been signed and sealed and I will deliver! ** Cannon turns back toward the camera and looks at it with a far away and almost crazed look in his eye.** Ace: Dallas, I hope everyone you have saved shows up to help you because that the only way there so-called Savior will be spared and you can take that to the fucking bank! ** Cannon once again turns around and looks at the cross and after a few moments the camera slowly fades out. Into a video.**
