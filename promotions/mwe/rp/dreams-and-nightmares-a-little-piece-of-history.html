Dreams and Nightmares - A little piece of history; Alejandro Rodriguez RP #1

Aug 7 2008

Alejandro Rodriguez

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Some people dream of being an astronaut, flying to the moon and soaring the heavens above. Some people dream of being a celebrity, being admired worldwide for their talents or beauty. Some people dream of being rich, bathing in their own wealth and getting anything they want. But I am not one of those people, all I have ever wanted to be was a wrestler, even as a little boy, growing up in Mexico City, begging off the streets, working 12 hours a day since I was six years old, life was tough, but I never let it get to me, I always knew I would find a way out and become the man that I am today, the man I always wanted to be. My name is Alejandro Rodriguez. My mother was murdered when I was just a teenager, a bullet lodged in her brain by Bandits to whom she owed money. My mother warned they were coming, yelled up to my room, I packed my things as fast as I could, my mother walked into her own death to save me, see, they didn’t want to kill her. The Bandits decided that they would take me, sell me as a servant and that would be the end of the debt, my mother would have none of it, she threw herself in front of the stairs, they asked her to move and she didn’t, the seconds in which she struggled with the Bandits were the seconds which saved my life, but ended hers, all I heard was a gunshot, a sound I heard many times later, in my nightmares. And from there, I set off to America, I knew I had to go there, I knew that that was where I would find my fortune. It was the land of opportunity, especially for a guy like me. I set so much hope by America, it would be my true place to escape, the only way for me to survive. My mother had died to save me, and so I decided to live in a way that would truly honour her, I would be happy, I would be the thing she wanted for me to be most. And know I know that I haven’t let her down, because that would be the only thing that would destroy me, failing her. I never knew my father, he was with my mother for many years before me, he was a wrestler, something I have never told anyone for one simple reason. That reason was that you hear all these wrestlers that are second generation superstars, that their fathers were legends and they were legends in the making, I never wanted to be known as one of them, because I have no father, he wasn’t a father figure to me, I never knew him, he wasn’t there to support my family when we needed it most. Whatever he was, my mother adored him, but he went off to be a great Luchadore, touring America and Mexico immediately when my mother got pregnant, he never once visited us, but I had already decided I wanted to be a pro-wrestler. When I was in America, it was barely better than in Mexico, I was a homeless boy on the streets, people never looked at me twice, I travelled from place to place, sometimes sneaking onto the backs of trucks to take me from place to place, I was taking a complete chance, I didn’t know which place I was going to next, I didn’t know how long I would be travelling, or when my next meal would be. Every town I went to, people treated me just the same, with the same contempt, only some would see pity, they would give me a few dollars. Most treated me with disgust, some people tried to beat me up, I was treated with racial discrimination every single day of my life. And I watched average people, in their ordinary lives, I’ve seen them be sad, when they have everything to be happy about, I hated them, I resented them, I still do. So I begun training as a wrestler, Steven McCartney taught me, he ran a school in Virginia, its safe to say that this was my first home in America, I trained in the gym every day and I wrestled by night, at the end of the day, I convinced Steve to let me sleep on the canvas with my sleeping bag, he was my first true friend and without him I would never be what I was today, he took me in and trained me for free, it was all I had and he was the only one who was willing to offer it to me. I will never forget his kindness and because of him I became who I am and I found a place that I could call home. He fed me and put a roof over my head, he was the father I never had. Unfortunately Steve died later that same year, I was 18 and I had no home, the gym had been bought and it’s land was being demolished for more houses, I was devastated and I had to leave, I left Virginia and I went back to Mexico. I was took in by a young woman and I fell in love with her, her name was Annabelle, she was so kind and supportive. Then I went and did something stupid, I hunted after the men who had killed my mother, stripped her away from me when I was so young and so vulnerable, I found them, alright, I bought a gun and I set to kill them, when they saw me, they laughed, and that laughter touched a nerve, they both pulled out guns, I shot one of them in the leg and then I had to run for my life, I knew that if I stayed in Mexico I would be in constant danger, I had to leave Annabelle and go back to America. While I went back to America, Annabelle fell pregnant, my child. And I had just left her, done exactly the same thing that my father did to me, I was totally unaware of it, until Stephani was three years old, I had no idea she existed. I first met her when she was handed over to me, for me to look after her, Annabelle had died. I had infuriated the Bandits and when they couldn’t find me they turned to Annabelle, she died and somehow Stephani had gotten away, they had left her, they didn’t kill her. Just as they didn’t kill me. So after that she was mine, and I was so happy to be with her and so upset that Annabelle had to die just because I was so stupid. Blood was on my hands and I couldn’t cope with it. At this time I was still in the MWE, right towards the end it was. During the time that I wasn’t with MWE and I quit the AEW I went back to Mexico, and I tried to forget it but I couldn’t, she had died because I wanted revenge, and I wanted revenge even now, but I know that wouldn’t be what she wanted. I took my current girlfriend Stacy Adams and Stephani to Annabelle’s grave, I put them in danger and I couldn’t take that. I took them back home, I know that Mexico is my home, and I do my best to help the people there, I give the children money and I do volunteer work for charities, it’s my favourite place in the world, but so many memories are there, so many bad things have happened to me, but I’m not going to make excuses, I’ve got the biggest match of my life coming up… The ring has always been my escape, and at Crowning Glory I hope to win the MWE Championship, and I know that everyone has been saying that they will win, betting their life on it and already dedicating my victory to a loved one. I’ve learned the more you love, the more you have to lose. I loved my mother and I loved Annabelle, and I both lost them, I loved Steven McCartney, who took me in when no-one else would, and I lost him too. I’m not going to dedicate this match to anyone, not my mother, not Annabelle and not Steven, because if I let them down then that would kill me, but I hope I do them proud, because I am going to try my hardest to win the thing that I will hold dear. Some people want to soar the heavens… all I ever wanted to be was a wrestler.
