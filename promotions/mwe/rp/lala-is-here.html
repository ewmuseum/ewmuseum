LaLa Is Here!

Dec 9 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
*The scene opens with LaLa backstage in her locker room. She's going through her ring attires and pulls out a gold outfit, a pink outfit, a purple outfit and sits them all out. She then turns around realizing the camera is in the locker room* LaLa: "Oh I didn't realize you guys were in here. Sorry let me clean up the place a little." *She goes to put her outfits back in her bag and then stops.* "On second thought...let me not. Usually thats the stereotypical job of a woman. To clean up and keep everything in order. Take care of the house and kids, and pretty much wait on a guy hand and foot at every hour of the day. Well thats not me. As a matter of fact, I have a different job here on Genesis, and thats to kick the guys asses!" *She stops talking and looks at the camera* "Wait...I forgot to put on my makeup, I can't let these fans see me without makeup can I?" *She pulls out her lip gloss and eyeshadow and quickly puts it on.* "You see...yes, I'm a beautiful woman, but I can still hang with the big boys here on Genesis. I didn't mean to come off as arrogant a few seconds ago, but the fact is no other woman has ever been taken seriously as a threat when it comes to competing with the guys and I"m here to change that. I have to face Shane Perry, another newcomer to Genesis. Truth is I've did my homework and tried researching Perry but found nothing on him. Apparently he's a newcomer to the sport in general. Well I'd like to welcome him personally on the next show, and I'm sure he'll understand the phrase about a woman's scorn. He WILL be begging me for mercy, but I won't stop until my hand is raised in victory." *She asks the cameraman to shut off the camera and he does. When it comes back on, they are in a training facility and she has a spar partner in the ring with her.* "I know some people won't take me seriously by just listening to me talk, and they'd rather see me in action, so I'm ready to prove to everyone what I'm capable of in that ring and what I've been training non-stop to accomplish." *A ref rings a bell and LaLa runs at her sparring partner and hits a running dropkick sending the partner to the mat. She locks on a headlock and is keeping the partner down. She picks him up and whips him into the rope and hits a lightning quick armdrag and then goes to the top rope. When her partner stands up she hits a flipping senton right onto his chest knocking him down and then gets off him after the ref counts to 3.* *She smiles* "You see that move right there is called the LAlipop. NOBODY gets up from it as you just saw. So Shane Perry....welcome to Genesis and I want to thank you in advance for being the first person to go down to LaLa and for being the first brick on the wall and the stepping stone I need to make it to the top of this company. After I beat Shane Perry...everyone that was doubting will have to listen, and everyone thats supporting me, will have an even greater reason to stick behind me, and I will be the fastest rising superstar in MWE history. Thats all." *The camera's watch her exit the training facility and then cut off* END OF RP OOC: Feedback would be greatly appreciated.
