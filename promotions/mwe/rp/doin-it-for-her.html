Doin' It For Her...; Wait....wha?!

This I whispered, and an echo murmured back the word, 'Lenore!' Merely this and nothing more.” Prologue Defeat. Defeat is something Nic Kraven has been through most of his life. Defeat is something Kraven felt last week as he faced off against the former MWE Intercontinental Champion, Zak Warner. With this defeat, it ended Kraven's hot streak here in the MWE. But now, Nic has a chance to redeem himself just a week before Tribulation. Tribulation, where the stars shine. Where the bitter enemies D.C. And Kevin Hardaway battle it out. Where the young stars battle for a chance at gold. This Tribulation, Kraven faces Eric Derf for the X-TV Championship. Up until now, Derf has been in a coma. Now, he has awoken and will make his long awaited return this Tribulation. Can Kraven pull out an upset victory and take his rightful place in MWE? Or will Derf prove that even in a coma, hes still on top of his game? The answers lie ahead, live from Rome, Italy, Tribulation. When will it end When will it end When will it end When will it end [Tears streaming down his face, Shawn yells out again but to no avail. The scene now slowly gets engulf into the solid darkness. Its final site...a crying Shawn Travis.] #01 [The scene opens now in the same park but in the dusk of night. There are no laughter, fun, nothing. A silence so unbearing. The only thing that can be heard is the creaking of a swing. As the camera pans over to the swings, we see a figure swinging back and forth slowly. His head bowed with his long black hair covering his face. He has on a black shirt, leather jacket, and baggy jean shorts. As the camera moves closer, the figure is revealed as none other than The Definition of Violence, Nic Kraven. Kraven slowly stops swinging and looks up at the camera. He simply wears a blank expression. However though, with the gleam in his eyes, we can tell something is bothering him. He begins to speak out in a soft quiet voice.] “Pain and suffering. Two of the best words to describe my past. What is pain? Is it something you feel when something or someone has caused you harm? Is it something that hits you as an unpleasant feeling? Or is it just something you feel from an emotional experience. You see, people talk about pain as if it is something so horrible that they can not withstand it. But they don't realize that they have it easy. You don't know pain until you have felt my pain. The pain of growing up in such a broken home. Pain of always being alone. Nobody really cared whether I lived or died. I was simply just a burden to this world.” [Kraven strokes his hair back with his left hand. You can see some scars and bruises left from his battle against Zak Warner last week. A gleam of hatred can be seen in his eyes. But there was also something else there...sadness. Sadness of having to relive these memories once more. Kraven looks up at the night sky. Its a full moon out. A beautiful night. Kraven simply takes a breath and speaks again.] “You know, I wasn't always alone in this world. I wasn't a cold heartless demon as you people assume I am. I had someone I cared about. Someone I loved, cherished. Someone I wanted to protect against all odds. She was the only highlight of my childhood. But now...shes gone. Shes gone because I wasn't strong enough. I wasn't strong enough to save her. Its my fault...my fault. This memory haunts me every waking moments. Nobody...nobody knows how I feel.” [We can see a slight tear comes to his eyes. He bows his head down pulling his hair with his left hand. “My fault.” It WAS his fault. It was all his fault that shes gone. If only he could relive that moment again. Then, he could save her. But alas, we can't go back in time now, can we? Kraven slowly lifts his head up and looks at the camera. He continues staring until finally speaking.] “Cassidy. My sister. She was born when I was 5. She was born in the same house, under the same conditions. Like me, she was not loved by our parents. It was like...we were just there for their own sick enjoyment. I knew it was my job to look out for her. My job to protect her. She was so innocent. She was easily sucked in about what people said. Cassidy was gullible. See, if it wasn't my responsibility to protect her, who will? But thats it, nobody. Nobody was around when we were children. We were on our own.” [Its clear, Kraven was deeply saddened having to talk about this. His expression showed it all. It isn't the same expression hes usually wearing. A cold emotionless stare. This expression showed sadness, anger, fear] “We stayed with each other until thick and thin. We looked out for each other. She helped me, I helped her. Until that faithful day. That day changed my life forever. A man, an escaped convict kidnapped her right in front of me. And I couldn't do anything about. That split second, that one split second that day turned into a living nightmare. The police surrounded the whole park looking for him, but unfortunately, he had disappeared. It was all because of me. I was weak, to weak to save Cassidy. Now...shes gone forever. Just a fragment of my past that will haunt me forever.” [Kraven slowly stops speaking and looks over at the swing besides him. Its a bit nostalgic really since it was the swing Cassidy was on on that faithful day... Kraven then cracks his knuckles a bit before turning back to the camera.] “For those of you who have been through complete bullshit in life, you know that no matter how hard you try to just live a simple life, trouble is never far from behind. And then, there are other people who have barely ever been through emotional and physical. People who's lives who just seem so wonderous. People like...Eric Derf.” [Kraven slowly gets up from the swings and starts walking. Out of the park, yes. But not technically back to the city, but to the dark forest that lies behind the park.] “A title, a match, a Pay-Per-View, and a opponent. This Tribulation, I face you Derf for the MWE X-TV Title. Championship Gold... I ask myself this all the time. What exactly, does championships mean to me? Is it an extension of life? A meaning to keep wrestling? An award that earns you respect? Well...the answer is none of these things. Yes...championship god means nothing to me. I'm not in this business to win championships, to win respect from the fans and fellow wrestlers. I'm here for one thing, to hurt. Can't you see? The only thing I've ever been good at was hurting people...” [As he walks along, he passes trees...just endless trees. Just out of the corner of his eyes, he sees an owl. “Hooot!” “Hooot!” It goes. Kraven basically ignores this as he continues walking. An occasional breeze blows past him.] “So Derf, what does the gold mean to you? Is it a means to earn respect out of people? Hmm? I can see it in your eyes Derf, you crave for that X-TV title. You want to prove to everybody that even though you were in an a coma, you can still pull a victory out of our match. Thats why you want it don't you? Then by all means, prove it. Beat me Derf. I want to see the best out of you, for I thirst a challenge. I will be waiting...” [Kraven keeps walking and soon comes upon a pile of neatly stacked and chopped up fire wood. What the...? Now why would there be neatly chopped up wood here? There are no cabins around. Kraven bend down to examine. As he does, he continues speaking.] “Derf, how was your life so far? Was it great, grand? A life people would kill to have? You went from a lowly jobber in WZCW to a success here in MWE. You were an X-TV Champion, X-Havoc Champion, the Rookie of the Year. And with that, a kiss up and a crowd favorite.” “Its no secret Derf, that the fans love you. They see you as a god in their eyes. But really, what good are the fans to you? Hm? They can't help you when you're in trouble. They can't get your possessions from those crooks. They can't even pull you out from your coma and bring you back to the real world. You know Derf, you know it deep inside your heart that they would turn on you...[Kraven snaps his fingers]..just like that. They're hypocrites Derf, they're no use for anything. Michaels knew that, and now hes beyond what he was before.” [A slight smirk appears on Kraven's face as he continues examining the wood. He knows it. Derf knows it. What he just said was true. But would Derf eventually realize this?] “Tell me Derf, do you still struggle with your self-identity? You sure seemed like it...when you dressed up in that ridiculous outfit and mask. Mysterious G. Tell me, what caused you to do such a stupid thing? Was it just your poor act of trying to draw attention? Were you trying to be known by the fans? Or was it something more? Were you trying to be something that you just weren't? Were you just trying to be a super hero? I know now...you just don't seem to understand who you are. You struggled to accept your own reality then, and you still do now..” [Kraven shakes it off and stands up. He continues to move on through the forest. Crickets can be heard chirping in the distance. What a weird ass place he thinks. As he continues walking, he speaks.] “...Let me ask you something Derf. Why does man feel the need to constantly be at the center of attention? Is it not good enough to just be happy with what you have with life and just leave it as that? Derf...why do you feel the need to be the center of attention? When you beat D.C., it was all everybody could talk about. You were at the constant center of attention for the few weeks. Then it died down. Then, when you went into a coma, you became the center of attention once again. All anybody could talk about was, “Ooo..did you hear what happened to Derf?” or “Oh my god...Derf is in a coma, I hope he'll be ok.” Even when you were in your own world with no way out...you were still at the center of attention. And you...you refuse to acknowledge other people's ability. You refuse to acknowledge MY ability.” [You can see the anger in Kraven's expression, in his voice. To calm himself down, Nic stops walking and sits down at a nearby tree. He pauses, looks up at the moon, and continues to speak.] “You refuse to acknowledge my ability Derf. I know what you think of me. A man who uses his past for sympathy. A cheap knock off of Raven. A lunatic, a headcase. You just don't understand...do you? You don't understand what I'm capable of. You think I'm a lowly mid-carder destined to go nowhere. This will be your downfall. Honestly Derf, have you ever faced anybody like me? Think long and hard about this Derf, have you?The truth is...you haven't. Derf...haven't your mom ever taught you to not judge a book by its cover? Well...hasn't she?” [Kraven pauses for a moment and chuckles to himself. What exactly is so funny? Kraven continues to chuckle but stops a second later. He looks directly at the camera and speaks.] “Derf, you want that title don't you? You thirst for it. And you will do anything you can to get your hands on it. You want it because you want to prove you still got it. You want to prove that even when you were in a coma, you were still at the top of your game. Your game however...dropped when you lost to Alex Bowen. Derf...the cold hard truth is that you don't have it anymore. You lost your title to Semi and you continued losing. Where did it all began? How did one become so evil and bloodthirsty that they were banished and went into the underworld? How did one become so pure, and kind hearted? Do you see how God and the Devil have been at war for centuries? I think to myself...and I find that our battle to come is similar to that situation. You see, in your “fan's” eyes, you are God and I am the Devil. You are the hero and I am the villain. Well...so be it.” [As Kraven continues walking, we see an opening at the end of the forest. Hes almost there. Just a few more steps.] “Our upcoming battle will be just like the battle of God and the Devil. For I am, as the fans see it, a Devil and you are God. You want to humiliate me Derf? You want to put me in my place? Well do it, be ready, beat me as I stand in the ring.” [Kraven is nearly there... nearly out of the forest.] “Step up...” [Now at the opening, he pauses.] “Prove that you can beat me...” [Kraven takes a breath and looks at his surrounding. Just trees and a land full of grass. The city lie up ahead. Kraven continues to walk.] “Don't hold back...” [The scene now begins to fade, he looks back at the forest and speaks his last words.] “Fight me...” [With this, the scene fades into solid darkness. The last sight, Nic Kraven walking off into the distance with the moonlight shining down on him.]
