Russian Roulette (With Your Career); A Joesph Sharpman Production

Jul 17 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
My eyes open, finding my fingers wrapped loosely around the cold steel in my hands. Reading those words over and over again I am flooded by that all too familiar wave of familiar emotion. An emotion that I simply can't describe. Because written clearly along the side of the barrel, are three faithful words. 'COLT CAL .45' and as much as I may come to regret this decision, there is simply no other way for this to happen. So I snap the chamber closed, and with shaking hands spin it freely watching as everything comes down to a game of luck. With that, I close my eyes and look at the darkness in front of me as I take a long deep breath in, and with those same trembling hands, bring the barrel up, pressing it tightly against my temple. Finger pressed lightly against the trigger. My eyes open again, and I find myself staring forward. Into that familiar red light, the same camera that has followed my life in this company, and the same crew. Dressed like the conforming sheep that they are in their blue and grey uniforms. It makes me sick, that I am forced to sit here like a caged animal as these slobs watch my life unfold from behind the lens. But that brings me to today ... Slowly I exhale, feeling the breath escape from my lungs as I focus my eyes once again on that MWE camera lens and with my lips quivering I begin one of my famous "promos" "Life is truly a beautiful thing isn't it? ... But what happens when somebody takes your life away from you ... What happens when you become this mindless little puppet, strung along helplessly through the mud ... Trying desperately just to pull yourself back up from the grave that was dug for you by the very people that vowed to give you a life?" I force the cold steel of the .45 deeper into my temple, groaning from the sheer agony that I am putting myself through. I stare blankly into the camera that sits before me, a routine that has trapped me for the last two years, and I can tell that whoever is watching this tape can see what I know I am. A trapped man. "What happens ... Is the old Joesph Sharpman. What happens, is a tangled web of lies and false truths that you begin to conceive as reality. So much to the point that you don't know who you are anymore, and you become so trapped that your every move only seems to put you deeper and deeper into the lie." That camera, it's like a car crash. I hate it, I want to escape it ... But somehow, I just can't seem to tear my attention away. I have tried, I have failed, and all that's left is to face the harsh reality that my life is destined to be another Discovery Chanel documentary. Yet, as I sit here with my life finally in my own hands, I begin to realize something. I begin to realize, that my life ... My career .. My fate. It's always been in my hands. "So you see now, why I had to escape from the trap that this entire industry has created ... You see the reason why I had to leave ... Because I didn't know who I was, I didn't know where I wanted my destiny to lead ... However watching from the outside looking in ... as this sideshow of an industry claimed victim after victim, I began to understand what my purpose here truly is ..." Letting my lips twist into that familiar fashion, I can't help but let out a small chuckle as my thumb traces that now unforgettable feeling of steel. I lace my fingers tighter around that single bit of complete power that I have been striving for, yet as I sit here and contemplate the other side of reality ... I somehow start to know, that this complete power has been in my hands all along. "My purpose in this travesty of a company ... Was to become a part of that unremorseful and unforgiving trap that ensnared my life ... My destiny is to take control of what I ran from ... My fate, is to become the very thing that I feared ... My purpose, is to be the reason why people like the old Joesph Sharpman, couldn't escape." With that I let my tongue slip between my teeth and stare into the camera. My intent, methodical. It feels impossible not to burst into laughter at the mere thought of completing my destiny. However, I manage to hold back for now. Pushing the cold barrel into my flesh, my finger presses slowly on the trigger. ----------------------------------------- Our scene opens, where we are is unsure. However our surroundings are oddly familiar, as if we had seen them before. In front of us, empty benches line up and down a long, shined hard-wood floor. Each covered in a luxurious satin, burgundy in color. Stained-Glass windows are scattered around the immense room diluting the sunlight shining through them. As our view slowly circles around the room there is one thing that suddenly flicks that switch, bringing our memories of this faithful building back into perspective, because hanging proudly at the front of this room is a large wooden cross. A sculpture of our lord and savior Jesus Christ strung across it in the familiar position of his crucification. Now this picture alone should be enough to jog any fan's memory, yet there is one last thing that brings this entire scene into a undeniable Déjà vu. For dressed in that sleek black suit, the same one that has symbolized suffering for many on the MWE roster, stands Joesph Sharpman. This combined with the blond hair glistening against a single ray of sunlight, and that oh too familiar glare locked on his face bring back all the times we have spent in this chapel. Every moment, every memory, everything from the times of Joey Dallas, standing in this very church suddenly seems to burst back into our minds as Sharpman's lips slowly twist into a grin, his serpent like tongue lying ready with a full explanation of his recent actions, or so we hope. Joesph Sharpman: "Let me welcome you ... To my own personal hell." As we slowly and surely circle around this demon of a man, we see that his piercing blue eyes are locked in front of him. Stuck on the ceramic sculpture of the savior, and the one glint of anger in his eyes is enough to tell the story even before his words once again escape his mouth. Joesph Sharpman: "Not for the fact that this church is now everything that I stand against ... But because of the stinging memories that this brings back ... To when the fans of this company had me strung around their little fingers ... When I was nothing more than the voice of the hopeless, helpless and most importantly ... the damned." His eyes carefully move up and down the sculpture in front of him, his face showing the pure disgust that is boiling inside of him. His tone matching his expression to a tee. Joesph Sharpman: "The memories of when I claimed to be the very breath that this company needed to survive ... When I was the self proclaimed messiah of MWE ... They bring nothing but a feeling of remorse. A remorse, of wasting so much of my precious time trying to be a hero while I should have been focused on something much more important ..." For a few moments, there is silence. One that seems to last an eternity while a sick and demented smile slowly yet surely twists it's way onto the features of Joesph. Everything that he has done in his career, from his days as Joey Dallas to the creation of The Vangaurd Movement has all lead up to this point. And it is clear that all of this is mere child's play in the new mindset of Sharpman. Joesph Sharpman: "Twisting, corrupting ... and tormenting the stars that this company has created with my own brand of torture ... You see, I should have been focused on slowly perverting the pathetic lives of self proclaimed superstars from the inside out. I should have been focused on making them feel like the very people I swore to protect one year ago." That sick look of twisted pleasure stays locked on Sharpman's face as his eyes lock even more intently on that colorless statue before him. It is obvious that looking at the man that is supposed to be the savior of mankind, the very thing that this man has claimed to be, has sent him into a disturbed state of mind. Joesph Sharpman: "And all it took for me to realize that all of this ..." His eyes finally break away from the cross to gaze around the room, even if just to show the audience what he meant by "this" Joesph Sharpman: "Was the wrong path, the wrong destiny for me ... Was to feel the warmth of a so called legend's blood seep between my fingertips. All it took for my mindset to change to what has become my entire life, was to hold the fate of another man in hands ... And to drag his life through a series of events that would soon lead to his unfortunate downfall." His hands raise, and are soon resting gently in front of him as he stares down at them. In his eyes, they are stained red with blood ... In his mind, they are responsible for everything that has happened to lead to one man's life and career becoming a distant memory. As far as Joesph is concerned, he has been the man that has finally dethroned D.C ... Or is it D.C that he's talking about? Joesph Sharpman: "You see, there is a reason why Vanguard fell ... There is a reason why Ace Cannon has become nothing more than a common man, fearing for his livelihood ... And that's because of me. The rise and fall of Ace Cannon ... Was because of me." With this Sharpman finally turns around, the black of his suit seems to glisten against the faded light in the room as he begins to pace between the rows of church pews. That same look of disgust from just moments ago locked back on his face as he looks up and down the aisles. Joesph Sharpman: "And just as easily as I rose him up to the peak of his career ... To when he was on top of the world, lost in his arrogant little mind as the number one contender ..." He stops at the back of the chapel and slowly turns around to look up at the statue of Christ once again. A small smirk coming over his face as he brings his right hand up to a rest in front of him. His eyes becoming focused on his palm, as tho he held something precious in his hands. Joesph Sharpman: "I crushed him ..." On cue Sharpman clenches his hand into a fist, crushing that same precious object that he held so dearly just moments ago. The smirk growing, almost into a smile. A hint of a childlike glee shining through as he looks at his fist. Joesph Sharpman: "... I bastardized him ... I was the single reason behind the downfall of the world's greatest villain ... I got into his head, under his skin and ultimately forced him to play the hand that he did leading to his travesty of a career coming to an untimely end." Is it true? Was that his plan on all along? To finally end the career of Ace Cannon ... Or is this just another one of his devious lies to get into our heads? Perhaps we'll never know, all that we can do is guess, and all that we know is what we see. As Sharpman begins to strut back towards the front of the chapel, his cold stare looking around him in that familiar manner. Joesph Sharpman: "You see, I could never force myself to align with a man that had once ended my career, left me lying in a pool of my own blood ... No, I knew from the beginning what I had to do ... I had to end his career in this company in a way that he would never forget ... And now that all that I strove for is finished, but it's just not enough ... Which brings me right back to where I stand today ..." His timing is perfect as he brings himself to a stop, perfectly in position of stare back at the lifeless cross and messiah still hanging proudly at the front of this chapel. As if mocking him, trying to turn him back to his old ways. Joesph Sharpman: "To the day that I have to set a new course for my destiny ... One that will forever be recurring in my career .. And that course is to undo everything that I have made possible, which starts with undoing the work of Vanguard ... And you see Jay, that brings me to you ..." And with this Joesph turns towards the camera, his cold stare piercing through it. As though looking towards Riddick himself. Joesph Sharpman: "Because if it had not been for the movement that Vanguard started ... Your career would still be in an endless pitfall of wins and loses as you try to prove yourself, as you try to make your name the marquee on the buildings along with those that have been handed that opportunity ... You see Jay, had it not been for my involvement in Vanguard you would never have been handed that X-Television title." A bright ray of sunlight finally breaks through the window and shines down on Sharpman, lighting him up in an almost majestic way. However, this light only makes this twisted man look even more sinister. Joesph Sharpman: "Had it not been for myself ... Had it not been for the fact that I wanted to force Cannon into your position, you would still be jerking curtains for the true stars ... Which is exactly where I plan to put you back ... Because I have seen that look of agony in your eyes every time that you have been forced to open a show ... I have seen that desire in your heart just to claw up the ladder one more rung ... And by kicking you back down into a pile of your own failures .. I feel that one thing that has always eluded me ...." His tongue just slightly slips between his teeth, slowly running along the sharp edges of them in a diabolical manner. Joesph Sharpman: "Satisfaction" Sharpman chuckles that evil laugh of his as he continues to stare into the camera, his eyes, his lips, every feature of his face showing the sick pleasure that he is getting out of this. Joesph Sharpman: "Because making your nightmare a reality ... Dragging your kicking and screaming body down to your own personal hell ... Will give me that one little feeling of satisfaction ...." He slowly brings his hand towards his face. Running his fingertips gently over his cheek he lets that little smirk of his twist wider and wider. The look on his face is almost crazed, almost demonic. This is one of those things that had someone said that one year into his career, Joey Dallas would become this ... No one would have believed it. Joesph Sharpman: "That I have become addicted to ..." Another chuckle escapes his lungs as looks into the camera, into the eyes of Riddick. Joesph Sharpman: "So what I need you do Jay ... Is go home tonight, and sit down your precious little son David ... The one thing that you will have left in your pathetic life after you have been used to feed my sick addiction ... Because you need to look into your son's eyes Jay ... And tell him that his father has to go down the Highway to Hell, and when he comes back ... He will never, be the same again." With that Sharpman bursts into an evil laughter, the camera suddenly zooms backwards showing the entire scene of this evil, horrible man ... His demented pleasure, and the holy church that surrounds him. What Sharpman's plan is for this Monday is uncertain ... All that is certain .. Is that this is proof. That the devil ... Wears Prada. ----------------------------------------- Click .. Nothing. The demented Sharpman sits before us on a wooden chair. His hair in a scruff and his black tuxedo wrinkled. A sick .. Twisted smile on his face as he almost silently chuckles to himself. A Colt .45 pressed tightly against his temple. His finger pressed down on the trigger. Joesph Sharpman: "When your good ... You don't need luck. And my purpose in this company ... Is to prove that." He lets out a laugh and snaps the chamber of the cold gun in his hands open, pushing himself off the chair as he does so. With that he begins to walk towards the camera, dropping the gun as he does so. "The greatest trick the Devil ever pulled ..." The camera now zooms in on the gun, showing that the chamber of the gun was empty this entire time. And with that our scene slowly fades to black, putting and end to this .. Strange promo. "He who is not with me is against me" ~Jesus Christ OOC: I know it's short, But I've been busy the last few weeks and I barely managed to get the time I wrote this in. I hope you enjoyed the read, and let me know if the ring rust is there or not.
