Darkerside; Zack Crash RP

Aug 4 2010

Zack Crash

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
When Zack opened his eyes, he felt so disoriented he felt as if he was in complete darkness. Darkness so thick it seemed to suck away the air around him. For a brief moment, Zack’s senses were a blur of confusion. After a moment of his senses began to catch up, he finally realized that he was laying flat on his back. Slowly, he sat up, rubbing his head that felt as if it were splitting apart. Zack: Uggg, my head. Zack look from one side, then the next, trying to get a grasp of his surroundings. But he only saw an infinite darkness stretching out on all sides as far as anyone could see. Apparently, the darkness was from more than just disorientation. Cautiously, Zack then examined the ground in which he was lying on, only to discover that there was no ground there at all. The darkness stretched further on below and above him as well. Zack jumped to his feet, spinning around in a panic. Yet as he stood, his feet still felt as if they were on solid earth. What was this place? How did he get here? How could he be standing? He had no memory of any of these things. This dark dome was as mysterious as if was unsettling, but all he knew and cared about right now was finding a way out. Zack: Where the hell am I? Zack had said to himself. Zack was only thinking out loud, so he didn’t expect to hear a voice answer him back. ???: Where indeed? Zack wheeled instantly, readying himself for a possible fight. What he saw nearly made him stumble back. The person he saw before him bore features that very entirely all to familiar to him. But something was different somehow. This other person looked much more crude, much more rugged…much more sinister. It was as if he was looking into a full-life mirror. There before Zack stood an identical version of himself. Walking, talking, independent and with its own free will. But how could that be? HE was Zack Crash. How could there be another him? But as much as he couldn’t accept it, as much as he tried to deny it, Zack noticed too many little nuances that defined him as himself. The way he walked, the way he carried himself, all of it was there in the other Zack Crash. Without a doubt, the person he was looking at was indeed himself. But how? Zack: It’s impossible! Anit-Zack: No, I assure me it’s very possible. Zack composed himself, then readied himself again for a surprise assault. Zack: Who…or what are you? Zack demanded. The person standing across from him seemed to be finally and truly taking Zack in. He simply looked him over with a condescending smile, than burst out in a fit of uncontrollable laughter. Laughter that seemed even more condescending than his previous smile. Anit-Zack: Wow! I guess I’m not too smart after all huh? Zack: Excuses me? Zack responded, genuinely taken off guard by the way he (or whatever it was) said that. The other Zack merely threw up its arms as if in surrender, then smiled another mocking smile. Anti-Zack: I mean here I am, looking directly at myself and I can’t even come to terms with what I’m looking at? Man, people have called me stupid before, but I never believed I was this stupid. Zack was starting to lose patience. Not only was this person’s constant, insulting way of speaking starting to annoy Zack, but his way of speaking in general was confusing and incomprehensible, which only fueled Zack’s growing temper. Anti-Zack: I gotta say, I really disappoint myself. Zack: Stopping talking like that and tell me who or what the hell you are! The creatures smugness vanished, replaced by an expression of both exasperation and annoyance. Anti-Zack: I’m you ya little dipshit! The comment made Zack mentally reel back. Hearing and seeing himself speak this way was more than just a little disheartening. Sure, he could muster up the intensity when he needed it during promos, but even still, seeing himself speak that way was quite…disturbing. Never mind the fact that the statement made no sense at all. Anit-Zack: I’m sorry. Would it make more sense if I said you’re me? Hardly. Nothing this guy did or said was making one bit of sense. Zack was starting to get so irritated he wanted to scream out. Zack began pacing back in forth, trying to decompress. His heart was beating so fast he could almost feel it hitting against his ribs. Anti-Zack: Do you mind if we change locations? This place is just soooooo boring. Before Zack could even throw back a reply, Anti-Zack snapped his fingers, and less than an instant later, both he and Anti-Zack were standing on top of what appeared to be a mountain as wind and snow roared all around them. Zack stumbled back from the edge of the cliff he was just inches away from, falling back into a small pile of snow. Stunned, he picked up a small ball of snow in his hands and examined it. It felt cold, and he could see it melting in his touch. What the hell was going on? Anti-Zack: There, much better. Zack slowly stood on his feet and brushed himself off. He was now more confused then ever. He felt lost, dazed, and disoriented. He wonder if this was how Alice felt when she stumbled onto to Wonderland in the books. Walking blindly through a world where everything was opposite, guided by creatures that spoke in riddles that had no solutions. Finally, Zack came to the only logical explanation. Zack: This is a dream? Anti-Zack smiled and began applauding. Anti-Zack: I finally figured it out. Give me a prize! With that uplifting piece of news, Zack was finally able to calm down. Surely now he’d wake up in his bed any moment now. He tightly closed his eyes shut, hoping to accelerate the process. But when he opened them again, he was still on the mountain top, with Anti-Zack shaking his head at him. Anti-Zack: Sorry dumbass, but that shit ain’t gonna work in here. Zack: If this is a dream, why can’t I wake up! AND WHO ARE YOU?! Anti-Zack breathed in deeply, and when he spoke, he spoke slowly as if talking to someone with low intelligence. Anti-Zack: I’m the darker side of your personality. I’m the part of me that feels all those terrible feelings I try to deny myself. Zack: Will you PLEASE stop talking like that?! It’s annoying. Anti-Zack: I can’t talk any other way. I don’t have my own personality. After all, I am you. Zack shook his head, trying desperately to wake himself up, or to take some control over this nightmare. But despite his best efforts, he couldn’t get a handle on this place. If this was his dream, why couldn’t he control it? Zack: No, you’re not me! You’re just some figment of my imagination. Anti-Zack stepped closer, walking casually as he leaned with one hand placed restingly on his hip. Anti-Zack: First off, don’t try and change anything because it won’t work. I’m here for a reason, and I’m not leaving till I learn why. And second off…I am you whether I like it or not. Zack: No…you’re not! Anti-Zack stepped closer, and as he did, the wind seemed to howl around him like an invisible cape. Anti-Zack: See, this is exactly why I’m here in the first place. I try to deny everything I am but I know I can’t run from myself. Zack: Shut up! The gale winds started to pick up as the clouds above them began to darken. The other Zack stepped closer, his gate ominous and foreboding. He seemed to tower over the real Zack now, his gaze piercing like knives. Anti-Zack: I try to suppress these feelings I have, I try to pretend they don’t exist, but I know they’re real. The arrogance, the adoration, the sensation I get win I beat my opponent within an inch of their life. That’s my real adrenaline rush. And I’m so tired of putting on this “nice guy” act, but I still hide my true self out of some misplaced guilt. Zack: I said shut up! Zack closed his eyes, desperately trying to wake himself up, but the more he tried the more power the other Zack seemed to gain over him. The gale was a full on hurricane now that threatened to blow Zack off the mountain top as thunder and lightning flashed on all sides. All the while, Anti-Zack moved closer, slowly, like a predator, growing large with every step. Anti-Zack: But why should I be ashamed of that? So I’m arrogant, so what? I know I’m the best there is in MWE. Hell, they should be thanking me that I even considered coming back! I know that there is nobody more worthy of being MWE Champion than me. So I like violence, big deal! This is wrestling dammit, and if they can’t stand it, then they shouldn’t be here at all. So I like the adoration of the fans, what of it? Who else should they cheer for? Zack: Please stop it. Zack backed away until he felt his feet touch the edge of the mountain cliff. Below, he saw only an eternal darkness. One false move, and he would fall forever. Anti-Zack moved closer, and Zack tried to shut his eyes and banish him away, but still nothing. Finally, as Anti-Zack came face to face with him, the storm seemed to sudden die. Inside Anti-Zack’s eyes, he saw only flame. The same look he saw in Virus’s eyes. Anti-Zack: Maybe Virus was right about me. Maybe I should give in. So what if people don’t like me, I don’t care. As long as I win and get to hurt people I’ll be fine. Cause all I care about is me! As long as I get my rush, I’m perfectly content. Right? After that, all Zack felt was a hard shove that sent him flying over the edge, falling deeper into the abyss below. Zack awoke with a shutter, breathing hard, his body covered in his own sweat. He looked around, heaving a sigh of relief. He was back in his bed inside his downtown Charleston apartment. It really had been all a dream. But even now that the dream was over, he could see and hear the other Zack inside his head as clearly as if he were still on that stormy mountaintop. Zack: It wasn’t real. It was just a dream. He said repeatedly to himself, trying to make himself forget it. Zack fell backwards into his bed, staring up at the ceiling. Though his body felt tired, he knew there would be no further sleep tonight. Sleep might bring back that dream, and that dream might bring back a truth Zack did not want to face. With nothing left to do, Zack climbed out of his bed, dressed himself, and headed toward the living room. He had a particular method for making himself feel better. This is the rise of Zack Crash. Zack sits alone in the darkness of his apartment. Something about it makes him feel comfortable tonight. It makes him feel safe. Almost as if it hides him and nobody can touch him, not even the truth. As a child, he’d been somewhat afraid of the dark. Now, as an adult, he liked it. Because not only did it protect his truth, but it also hide the truth of others he dared not know. He sits casually in his favorite armchair, legs crossed, looking directly into the camera as it zooms in on his face. His expression looks to be one of forced calm. Zack: Well MWE certainly is off to an explosive new start isn’t it? Just last week we put on what has to be one of the best MNM’s we’ve put on in a long time. And not only that, I restarted my MWE career with an even bigger bang by defeating the “Image Of Perfection” himself in James Preston. And to top it all off, I secured myself the main event against The Virus for the MWE Championship at Crowning Glory. Not a bad first week back on the job if I do say so myself. But despite all that, I’ve been met with almost nothing but doubt from others. He feels a sense of bitterness as the memory comes back to his consciousness. Others doubting him? It was almost insulting. But he forced down those feelings. He didn’t want to feed those ideas any more than he might have already done. On the good side though, this gave him the chance to prove people wrong. And Zack oh so loved proving everyone wrong. Because everywhere I go people doubt my decision. They ask me why I didn’t let DC just hand me the MWE Championship in exchange for the UUW Championship. Even some fans have come up to ask me “why?” Well apparently I did make it clear enough before, so let me say it again. Zack Crash doesn’t need to be handed anything. I’ve gotten this far in my career by fighting for it, and even though I may have lost now and then, and even though I may lose at Crowing Glory, I’m not so weak as to get by in MWE on handouts and pity championships. Because if I had accepted DC’s first offer, that’s all it would’ve been. Pity that my first World Title came to such a tragic end. And if I ended up accepting that, I wouldn’t have earned any respect. Well if I plan on being the new top dog in town here in MWE, I need respect more than anything. You think David Whitman, Sinc Mercier, or even DC himself didn’t have the respect of the entire MWE locker room and Universe? Of course they did, and do you know why? Because they were fighters. They did ask for anything, and they didn’t take handouts. They wanted something and they worked for it. They wanted to be champion and they defeated a champion. And I plan on following the example they set. So that’s why I denied my being given the MWE Championship. Because if I want real respect, if I want to be consider a real main eventer, then I have to prove it. Zack tells himself to keep his composure. He can feel his heart racing and his heat rising, but he will maintain control. Last week, his promo had been vicious and downright cruel. Looking back, Zack was amazed to see that even in himself. And it scared him. Well this week, Zack told himself he was going to keep control this time. He was the number one face in MWE and he had an image to uphold. He had to represent all that was good in wrestling and MWE. For the fans. And he was the Ultimate Thrill Seeker. Should be easy enough. Right? And I plan on do that as I go into this week’s match against “God’s Unwanted Child” Alexis Black. And this isn’t the first time me and the human billboard for Hot Topic have clashed. I remember our first and only match very clearly Alexis, but do you want to know what I remember most clearly…it’s me pinning your ass to the mat as the ref counts one, two, three! So right there, I got the odds in my favor. And hell, even if I do lose to you it won’t hurt me or my reputation. Everyone seems to think I need this win in order to gain momentum for Crowing Glory, but I see it very differently. I say I’ve got nothing more to prove against you or MWE in regards to you. I’ve already beaten you once, so everyone already knows I can beat you again. But let’s say you do manage to win. What if you beat me? What does that prove? Nothing! I could’ve made a mistake, or it could’ve been a fluke. Either way, it doesn’t put you any higher on the ladder than me if you do manage to win. If you ask me, you’re the one who needs this victory. Cause if I can beat you twice in a row, then maybe that says something. Maybe it says that you can’t beat me no matter what you try. Maybe it says that I always was, and always will be better than you. Zack feels something crawling inside of him, nibbling away at his heart. A viciousness starts to rise to the top of his head and a confidence just shy of arrogance, both trying to escape through his words. Zack remembers the dream, and tries to force the words down. He doesn’t need to resort to that. But the words are just too powerful. But they also make him feel an intensity about himself, and that’s just too much to deny. So the words come spewing out. And speaking of memory, I also remember something else. I remember James Preston slamming your head against a concrete wall…just before I slammed his against the turnbuckle. I’m sorry man, but I don’t care who trained, if James Preston can take you to the limit, then you might want to consider getting your money back. Or maybe Sharpman should consider getting himself a new student. I remember watching some of the great matches Sharpman has had over the years. Guys like Cannon and DC. And he is no doubt one of the greatest in this business. Yet with a master like him training you, how does your career end up? Time in ICW, the lesser brand of MWE with lesser wrestlers, which amounted to nothing. And a career in MWE that amounted into your losing streak against Madison Seton. Remember her? The girl who took your spot on Team MWE in the war against GWC. Unlike you, I had no great legend to teach me. I was always on my own, and had to pull myself up on my own. Yet the difference between us is vastly different. I’ve gone onto to great success without anyone’s help, while you had someone like Sharpman behind you, and you’re still trying to make a name for yourself. If that isn’t a waste of resources, I don’t know what is. Zack tries to regain control. Why is he talking like this? He never used to speak like this, with such harsh words they bordered on the reckless. Zack may be a thrill seeker, but he certainly isn’t reckless…not like this. But for some reason, he can’t stop. He stands up from the chair in which he is sitting, moving toward the window till the moon light is illuminating him. But that’s not even the worst of it with you. The worst it this “God’s Unwanted Child” business, how you think it’s you against the world. Don’t flatter yourself! That’s nothing more than you stroking your own ego. You just want to feel special, so you convince yourself that your not wanted, and everyone’s against you. You simply seek discrimination where it doesn’t exist. Because hey, if everyone hates you, that must make you special. Like so many others in the world, you want to be hated because you think that makes you better than them. And THAT’s why you’re really “God’s Unwanted Child.” Because that’s exactly what you are, a child, and nobody buys this cheap shit your selling them. They all know you’re an arrogant ass! A whiny little child wanting attention, so he causes trouble just to get it. Well head back to the nursery if you want attention that way, cause in MWE, we get attention the old fashioned way…we beat each other to a bloody pulp. I never whined, cried, or pissed myself to get noticed. My skills were enough to do that. Zack is losing control. Even in the darkness of his apartment his face glows red with anger. He tries to pull himself back, but he just can’t stop. Even his body flails about, as if wanting to hit someone. And I know exactly what you’re going to say in retort. Hell, I’ve heard it all before. You’ll say something like I’m too undisciplined in the ring, that I’m inexperienced, that I don’t deserve my spot, or just as arrogant as anyone else, or the really original catchphrase, that I’ll “crash and burn” in our match. Well I challenge you or anyone else to prove I don’t deserve my spot. I’m so sick of being tested! Articles and interviews might suggest that others respect be, but I know better. If that were the case, I wouldn’t get matches against the likes of James Preston, or Alexis Black, or other glorified mid-carders. But I’ll play the game for now. Just wait till Crowning Glory comes and I make the self-professed devil himself shout “I QUIT” underneath my boot heels. With a lasting effort, Zack is finally able to pull himself back down to earth. He breathes deeply and calmly, and he slowly begins to feel his heart race steady toward a normal rate. He slumps back into his chair, feeling very awkward. What was that just now? Was that really him just now? Does he really feel that way? He buries his face in his palm. He can’t afford these thoughts right now. The words have been spoken nonetheless and the dice cast. He needs to finish what he started. Crowning Glory is just around the corner. This is the time when we either put up, or shut up. I can’t see the future, so I don’t know who will win this week on MNM. But I do know this much. When this week’s MNM comes, on my path to my Crowning Glory, I will turn a corner from which there’ll be no going back. Where that path takes me…is yet to be seen. And with that, it’s finally over. Zack wished he could feel proud of the intensity he showed, but instead all he felt was shame. Quickly, he rushed to the bathroom, throwing on the light, and splashing water in his face. The light was blinding as he examined himself in the mirror. But as he looked deep into his eyes, deep enough to almost see into his own soul, Zack saw something. Not the normal light and life he usually saw. What he saw was something else. What he saw was a twisted reflection of darkness, of viciousness… … of a virus.
