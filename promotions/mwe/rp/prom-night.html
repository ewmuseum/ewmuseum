Prom night; Jekyl RP #2

Aug 7 2008

Jekyl

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Voice: Here we see Jekyl wandering the downtown streets of Chicago. Illinois. Velvet purple suit, scraggy green hair, and a permanent smirk on his face, looks probably about the age of 19, a flashback glow surrounds the scene. Jekyl walks up the street and eyes a car parked on the side of the road, a black corvette. wit ha devious smirk on his face he breaks the window and opens the door, hot wires it and speeds off. Jekyl: Oh ha ha hee ha oh haaaa... perfect ride for my sweet date. And what the hell is that voice? Voice: I'm the narrator, don't you recognize the voice of Morgan Freeman? Just ignore me and do what you're doing. Jekyl Raises an eyebrow at this and continues on his way. Voice: He licks the tip of his hand and runs it through his hair as he drives in and out of traffic, heading onward to pickup his date for the high school prom. As he comes to a screeching halt he honks the horn several times as his date walks down the pathway and gets in the car. The female is very pretty, black silk dress, red rose lips, blond hair, black eyeliner, and a very nervous look. Jekyl: You know the deal sweetheart, we have a good time, and your family remains on the ground and in tact. Understand? Morgan: The girl nervously nods her head knowing that if she doesn't do what Jekyl says, her house will be blown to pieces by this lunatic. What Jekyl did prior to this night was hire someone to goto her house and plant explosives around her house. Continuing on, Jekyl and his date arrive at the Convention Center where the prom is behind held. Jekyl prances around the Corvette to her door and opens it with his hand extended. She ignores his hand and walks out toward the entrance. Jekyl slams the door and catches up to her and grabs her by the arm. Jekyl: Listen sweetheart, if this night doesn't go over well, you will no longer have a family, got it? Girl: Your pathetic and psychotic, lets just get this night done and over with. Morgan: The girl rips her arm out of his grip as she begins to storm toward the entrance, Jekyl catches up and take her arm in his as they enter the building. As the hours go by Jekyl has danced with the girl several times, as each time she becomes more and more irritable. As the final song is about to be played an older man walks on stage to announce who the King and queen are. He looks nervous but tries to keep an excited face. Principal: Ladies and gentlemen, it is now time to crown our king and Queen! Morgan: A drum roll echoes the room as the principal opens up a small purple envelope. Principal: Your queen is none other then... Ashley Buckingham! Morgan: Jekyl smiles as his date rushes to the stage as for once the entire night she is happy. little does she know, this is only momentary. The principal receives another envelope this time its green. Jekyl stands up as the principal looks more nervous this time around. Principal: L..Ladies and ge....gentleman...y...yo...your king is none o...other than............................................JEKYL? Morgan: The atmosphere in the room goes from cheerful to the likes of a funeral as the spotlight focuses on Jekyl as he gets a big grin on his face, the crowd goes silent as the odd comment can be heard throughout the crowd, "are you serious? this clown got king?". Jekyl makes his way to the stage as his date Ashley buries her head in her hands and begins to cry, the principal rushes off stage to a safe distance, mic still on he speaks from back stage. Principal: Now our king and queen will share the first dance. Morgan: The crowd parts as Jekyl leads the way with Ashley closely behind him still in tears. Stairway to heaven begins to play as Jekyl forces her close to him as they begin to dance, crowd still amazed at the decision. As the solo speeds up, the rest of the students now dancing too, Jekyl plants a kiss on Ashley. She pulls away violently once again. Ashley: EWW, LISTEN YOUR A PIG, YOUR UGLY AND NO-ONE LIKES YOU! YOUR RIGGED THIS AND YOU GOT WHAT YOU WANTED, NOW LEAVE ME ALONE!!! Morgan: Ashley rips herself from his grip and smacks him in the face as his face goes from a grin to an angry frown, he rushes after. He catches up with her outside and grabs her. Jekyl: Ok Darlin, it's time for a drive. Morgan: He throws he into the car and locks the door, he rushes over to his side. They take off speeding down the street, Ashley's face twinges with the fear of hitting something at the high speed, Jekyl looks at her with a smile for he knows she is in for a surprise. Ashley: STOP! YOU GONNA GET US KILLED! Jekyl: Don't you worry your pretty little head off sweetheart, this is the LEAST of your worries. Plus this voice I'm hearing says it's not going to be very pretty for you. Morgan: Leave me outa this, your playing the role I just tell how it goes. Anyways, Jekly comes to a screeching halt once again in front of Ashley's house, she struggles to unlock the door, but it's no use. Jekyl then pulls out what looks like a RC controller with a key in it. Jekyl: Do you know what this is? It's the remote to detonate the plastic explosives I've had someone place around your house for just an occasion. I warned you what would happen if things didn't go right, now its time for the fireworks, lets watch shall we? Ashley: NO! What do you want? I will do anything, just don't hurt my parents! Jekyl: Why so serious? Tonight was supposed to be about fun and you were all serious about it... But it's funny you asked, cause I expected this night to go off with a little more fun if you catch my drift. Morgan: With tears running down her face she begins to take her dress off and tosses it in the back, she is now in her underwear and bra which are black. Jekyl's smile becomes even bigger as she climbs on top of his lap. As she kisses him she makes an attempt to take the controller away. He then opens his door and throws her out of this car, he steps out with a tight grip on her arm and the controller in the other. He lets go as she runs toward her house screaming in her undergarments. Jekyl: In 3.........2................1..........and BOOM! Morgan: He then turns the Key as an explosion is heard further on down the street, a "For Sale" sign lands beside him. Jekyl: Damn he put them at 191 instead of 91. Well darling you got fortunate, you got to humiliate me in front of the school and you and your family got to live. On another note, I don't think that house I just blew up is going to get much in terms of money. Morgan: Jekyl gets back in the car and zooms off laughing maniacally. The scene then fades out into the present time, Jekyl is lying on his bed in a shitty small room of a small apartment staring at the ceiling. Jekyl: That night was, entertaining, humiliating, and awkward. I was embarrassed by Ashley, I blew up the wrong house, and I was hearing a voice that said it was Morgan Freeman that was narrating my life, which I'm not too sure of. But I have a plan, the humiliation i faced that night, I will use it at Crowning Glory as fuel to get that title and beat everyone else that gets in my way..... A knock at the door brings Jekyl back to reality. Voice: POLICE, OPEN UP!!! Jekyl gets a large smile on his face as he opens the back window.[/color] Jekyl: Oh, gotta run, I think they are here about that guy I stabbed the other night... WAAHEEE!!!! Jekyl jumps out of his bedroom window as the police break through the door. his laugh echoes down the alleyway back into the window. -Fade-
