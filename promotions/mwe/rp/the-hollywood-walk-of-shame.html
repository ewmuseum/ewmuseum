The Hollywood walk of SHAME

Aug 13 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
*Scene comes to play with a camera moving forward following Sinc Mercier on the Hollywood walk of fame. Sinc is followed by good friend and MWE staff member Shawn Spitzer. The sun was beaming as Spitzer and Sinc were both wearing sunglasses and proper street attire.* Shawn: I guess I should start off by asking why choose this place for an interview? *Sinc smiled knowingly because it was a very good question* Sinc: Well this is the birthplace of every piece of bad talent imaginable. And If I am going to talk about my tag match I am damned to talk about how untalented my opponents are right? Plus I was visiting a friend here for a few days anyway. Now ask your questions. *Shawn put a mic close to his mouth and began to speak* Shawn: Good point. So tell me Sinc, the man with the plan, The Arrogant Aviator himself. You have a tag match with Alex Bowen against B.G. Mancini and Devlin Sharpe. What are your thoughts on this matter? I mean come on, don’t get me wrong you’re a great guy, but these guys seem to be real pro’s. Sinc: Well I don’t even need to think about this one. The last tag match I was in in MWE was a bail. My partner bailed and left me to get the crap beat out of me. After that I quit the MWE making a very small and discrete return, drank a few beers and had lots of sex, now I am back on the block wanting more. So it was a tag match that was my fate a few years back. Now hopefully it will be my boost I need to get in the main event. Call me crazy for thinking I can be a main eventer, call me crazy for even wanting to compete in another tag match after last time, but if you do, I will have to call you crazy for thinking any less of me. *Shawn laughed a bit.* Shawn: Well do you think Mr. Bowen will bail? And if he does will you be leaving the MWE again? Sinc: Well lets get things straight here. I honestly don’t like guys who wear makeup and have an unnatural obsession with scorpions. Last time I encountered someone like that I had a hangover next to some chick who‘s face was full of piercings, and whos body was so hairy it was like seeping in a bowl of pubic hair, don‘t ask. But hey, that’s life right. As for leaving the MWE, I think I will hang in there. But I will stick to singles matches, and matches where I am a lone wolf. As they say, kill two birds with ONE stone. I am that stone my friend. *Sinc said stretching his arms flaunting himself for a second* Shawn: Your opponents. B.G. and The Dev. What are your thoughts on their skills in the ring, and against you no less? *Sinc put his hand on his forehead and began rubbing his head* Sinc: The mention of those names give me a BLISTERING headache. Please, I doubt their even worth my time. See the name below me? “Kermit The Frog” that’s who I compare those sorry saps to. Their like frogs with a bad outbreak of warts that I just want to stomp. *Shawn looked down and shrugged* Shawn: Well if it doesn’t ache too much mind answering my question bud? *Sinc sighed* Sinc: Well I might as well wrap your question up like a jelly roll and mold it so it makes sense to everyone watching this. All Devlin does is fly around the ring like some crazy squirrel on ecstasy and B.G. is an Italian with an appetite for a meatball supreme. Put them together and what do you got? An ass whooping of a lifetime involving a squirrel and some bad meat. With or without my partner I will make sure they get a beating worse than what their momma ever gave them. Unless their some pampered fruit loops. *Shawn nodded and made a serious look on his face* Shawn: Now you were telling me backstage at Crowning Glory that you used to be a comedian? Tell me more about that. *Sinc suddenly made a grin liking the topic* Sinc: Finally something I can really yap about. As a young lad I always stirred up some real trouble. Lighting bags of dog crap on fire, pissing on my neighbors lawn, the usual shenanigans. But I knew I wanted to be a comedian when my dad made me do stand-up at a local bar after wrestling practice. I was so scared I actually started to sing the ABC’s Peewee Herman style. Then my dad threw a bottle of liquor at my head and suddenly I started crackin jokes. And everyone seemed to like me. But wrestling was my real passion. A fire that wouldn’t go away. *The camera zooms a bit closer to the two as they slowed down their pace* Shawn: Very deep man. Did you ever think that you would go down the MWE route? *Sinc rubbed his chin* Sinc: I mean not really. But it was an opportunity I couldn’t pass up. I loved wrestling more than comedy. And seeing as how I can use comedy IN wrestling it truly balances things out. Kind of like Tyra Banks and Oprah in some kind of fight for supremacy. Or maybe just a fight for bad TV. *Shawn smiled and nodded* Shawn: I can see the relation. Heh heh. Now your slogan, take me on or take me out. Can you once again simplify that for us? *Sinc looked at Shawn in arrogance* Sinc: You mean you don’t know one of the greatest slogans in HISTORY? Simple Shawn. You either come at me full on or take me out like a backstabbing fairy. You can’t do both. Because I am too smart and too bold to let that happen. Only the weak and dick minded would stab me in the back and take me out the trashy way. But the strong and smart ones would meet me in a fair fight. But that’s their choice in the end and theirs alone. In my match at Monday Night Mayhem I will show the fans and everyone else watching what my slogan really means. Shawn: Alright Mr. Mercier, any finishing lines for us today? *Sinc put a finger up thinking for a moment* Sinc: Not really. Devlin equals flying squirrel….B.G. equals meatball packing ape….this interview equals kick ass….and me equals done. And if we were each one of Lindsay Lohan’s crabs we would be nothing more than a clench fest for UNICEF. *Shawn stopped as did Sinc* Shawn: Well thank you very much man. Well said. It was great to see you again hope to catch you later bud. *Sinc smiled shaking Shawn’s hand* Sinc: It was a pleasure. And you will definitely see me kickin ass in the ring next week. To you and all my fans, thank you from the bottom of my god forbidden freakin heart. *Shawn nodded with a smile and walked away as the camera faded on Sinc as Sinc suddenly, oddly, had a sinister look on his face, nothing but silence was left*
