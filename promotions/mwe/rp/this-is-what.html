This is what...; could have been an awesome RP

Jul 17 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
((OOC: Just a little note, Mason recently got a virus and was not able to finish his half of the RP which was supposed to be focused on kevin, so I am only posting my half on what could have been an amazing RP, I hope you all enjoy it, for what's in it that is)) THE KID IS NOT MY SON! Michael Jackson begins blasting in a very open backyard. There is a gigantic pool just feet away as daylight begins settling in straying away from the morning hour and into the early afternoon. Sinc is in his bathing suit while he rocks out to “Billie Jean” and busts some moves on the diving board of the pool. Sinc: YEA BABY! THAT’S THE STUFF! A neighbor trimming their bushes next door raises a righteous fist at Sinc with a squabbling yell. The neighbor is an old lady with a bright pink dress and a straw hat securely on her head with an assortment of flowers. The typical commercial old person you’d see on some TV ad. Lady: Turn that racket down! Sinc: I WILL NEVER TAKE MY TRUNKS OFF! What are you a pedophile? Lady: THAT’S NOT WHAT I SAID! Sinc: That’s not what I said! Lady: Oh why you- Sinc: NEVER! Sinc ignores the woman as she calms down and aims his body at the deep end of the pool with great concentration. He bounces in rhythm on the board ready to jump in with a timed arch, but before he can… ???: What the hell are you doing dude? He gets startled and falls right off the board landing awkwardly in the water. A splash of waves is the outcome while he flaps around and scoops at the water to maintain afloat. Sinc looks behind him and he sees Mason walking out of his house with a margarita and some very expensive looking leisure clothes. He looks like the kind of a guy that just threw something on after recently getting laid. Sinc: What’s with the expensive getup? Mason: What’s with you barging into my backyard and using my pool without my permission? Sinc: Oh….uhm…about that. Mason: Just get out of my pool and come over here so we can chat. Sinc swims to the edge of the pool and jumps up onto the cement with a plop. Mason fiddles with his drink and notices an odd shape in the center of the cup. He looks baffled and stunned staring down the liquid pattern. Sinc wraps a towel around his waist and walks over to Mason and the cup with a raise of the eyebrow. Sinc: Kinda looks like a dildo doesn’t it? Mason: What? Sinc: I’m saying that it’s shaped like a penis! Mason: I dunno…it kinda looks like jesus to me… Sinc: You are a sick man my friend. Mason just shakes his head and takes a seat under a bright blue umbrella with the casual table set up including a glass frame top and polished wooden chairs. He takes a graceful sip of his drink and looks at the calm waves of his pool with bliss. Now relaxed and focused, he begins to bring up their match. Mason: So…GM and Kevin….shall we consider them a challenge or just plain average? Sinc: Can’t we just play hot or not instead? Do we really have to do the typical promo questionnaire….that’s a little outdated if you ask me dude. Mason: Sinc, there’s something you must learn about the art of a question. There is no right or wrong answer, there is only a simple response. So please, express yourself. The thought of rice cakes and tea pots briefly settles in Sinc’s mind, but he figures that if he was to be truthful and honest about such an answer, he needs to have his mind completely cleared. There may have been a few sex scenes that just popped up but NOW it’s cleared. Wait…. NOW. No, NOW. Dammit at least he tried, so he is just going to blurt out some nonsense reply. Sinc: If you want an honest answer, I think Kevin is gonna whoop my ass crack and GM is gonna sew up my hole. Mason: Are you kidding? Sinc: Maybe….or maybe not? He pretends to do a Jedi mind trick on Mason with a twinkle of his fingers and a variation of strange eye movements. But ultimately he just ends up looking like a total retard on camera and Mason remains content unembarrassed by his colleague’s actions and knowing full well what Sinc Mercier is capable of in the ring. Mason: Can you at least tell me why you think we are basically going to get slaughtered out there? Sinc: Slaughtered? SLAUGHTERED!?!?! WHAT DOES HAVING MY ASSH*LE SEWN UP HAVE TO DO WITH GETTING SLAUGHTERED BUDDY? Mason: Ok…now your just being weird. Sinc: I am not trying to doubt us padawan, I am merely saying that after losing to Michaels while his former goons beat him to death, I am just a little on the iffy side so to speak. And to face such top brand competitors straight from a practical loss boggles me. I’m stuck, more stuck than the time I glued myself to my own toilet and then shouted the ABC’s twenty times. Sinc recalls the moment in time where he did such deeds and begins to giggle. Mason takes the lime from the margarita and chomps down on it moistening his lips. He makes a confident smirk enjoying the lime and sees good things coming by the time MNM hits. Mason: Not to worry partner, those two from what I understand love to underestimate people, and let me tell you something, they are not going to get away with underestimating us. We got things covered and on lock from coast to coast, they FEAR us, this I assure you. Sinc: I appreciate your assurance but I’ve been knocked off my game and who knows if I can recalibrate myself when we tussle with those dudes. A guy like Grand Mystique who unmasked himself to the world and destroyed all credit he had being a masked profit, is now a man who is two times better than what he was. He has the confidence, he has the motivation, and what do I have? Mason: You have the world title. You’re the one man everyone is most afraid of right now, which brings back the term fear. I’m a champ, you’re a champ, life is good. Don’t let one loss sway you. We can beat these guys, and if they don’t think we can, well then fu- Sinc: HEY HEY HEY! Don’t go there. Sinc puts a finger to his lips signaling Mason not to go that far with his point. He wants to make it clear to his tag team partner that they can’t exactly take it to such far levels anymore. Sinc: We are faces now, VOICES OF THE PEOPLE! We can’t be so hateful anymore. Now I know your still in the beginning stages of such a wonderful experience but you must know to just let the angry superstars of this company talk their garbage and we simply prove them wrong. Mason: But I mean come on, we’ve been doubted before. Sinc: Who cares, who cares what they think. Mystique is a talented athlete and he knows how to strike a nerve with me, but at the same time I can boast and rant that I am the world champ, the MWE world champ, not an EW has been, I have a belt he has NEVER won, and hopefully such a ruthless man like him won’t ever win it. I can say that he can threaten me, kick me in the balls, and hog tie me to a telephone booth but I am STILL…world champion. I am not going to shove it all in his face unless he really ticks me off, and right now Mason, I am feeling just fine. Mason: But you just kinda boasted right there….. Sinc stops himself and taps his chin. Mason was right, he did indeed just boast about GM with strong comparison. But in response he just shrugs in full knowledge that he was merely using an example. Sinc: Examples don’t count. Mason: Whatever. Is there ANYTHING you can say about GM then? Even a shred of something? Cause if not he’s gonna pretty much tear you apart. Sinc: Alright, I will say a few things. The guy in a blink of an eye can catch you off guard and shock a crowd, he can use such brutal vitality to prove something and make you feel it the next day. He is the definition of power and viciousness. But he lacks what you and I have buddy. He doesn’t know passion, he doesn’t grasp the truth or the facts of this sport. Mason nods in agreement enjoying what his partner has to say. Uncomfortable, Sinc etches to his feet and takes a seat at the edge of the pool dipping his toes in the water and calmly staring at the sun’s reflection on the blue blanket of liquid. Sinc: His words can strike like venom and gets under your skin, his emotion flows so well similar to water or a hypnotizing rain storm and I am truly mesmerized by his very presence. In the end though, he has only won the tag team title, with a man he massacred, literally destroyed. I can imagine that he must have some kind of disgust for the tag team division after that, he made it pretty clear he is better off solo. And now all he wants is to defeat me and become the dark cloud on an otherwise beautiful sunny day, he wants my title just like Michaels did, and it is clear to me that MWE has become a golden eyed frenzy where no one is just simply happy anymore. His toes dangle into the painted chlorine like ten little fishes being suffocated by a cleansing fog while his face is depicted into the glimmering of the water. He stares at himself with question, and wonders if he should be so focused on tending to himself, when there are so many others in need. Sinc: He doesn’t get the purpose of this company. We entertain people, he hates them. We bleed with sweat and tears, he’s known how that feels for a while. We’ve even won two titles he’s never had the opportunity to obtain and yet it doesn’t phase him. The man is an iron block that we cannot move or budge. He only needs himself, and his thirst for pain to get him by while we rely on the people and the atmosphere. He is not entertainment, he is the epitome of a maskless demonspawn. He’s the metaphor of ruin and darkness, a verb used only for the most denouncing thing imaginable, and I ask, how can we face such a monster? Mason seems somewhat stumped for an answer as his partner stares waiting in an impatient glisten for SOME kind of solution to a creature that interlopes with smoke machines and vanishing acts. A creature so focused on winning a championship that he could never be denied access. That creature is Grand Mystique. Mason: The solution is actually easier then I thought. We prove him and his philosophical bull crap wrong. We counter his wise lies and trickery with our own slice of mind games. We feed off the fans and give him and Kevin all that we have, they cannot even compare to what we have achieved, no matter what they say, we are the foundation of MWE, they are pre-MWE rejects. Sinc: That’s a pretty good solution there Mason. And your absolutely right, if we doubt ourselves and think those two are better than us then that’s what will really happen. I can’t continue to let things get to me, GM can rant to high hell about his greatness and his PAST achievements in other federations, but he cannot shatter my spirit. If Whitman can beat him with his head held high then so can I, GM’s days are numbered, I can’t put it any other way. Mason gets out of his chair and joins Sinc by his side sitting cross legged next to him not dipping his feet in the water due to wearing some very nice shoes. He pats Sinc on the back and grins menacingly. Mason: That’s what I like to hear. I may be just getting used to this good guy business, but I think I will adjust just fine. Your already showing me that though there are limits, we can still break through them with ease. You and I will do great things in the future ahead, I can tell. Sinc: We are no Rated-X, or CX, but we are fresher than that. Mystique has the potential to be an awesome champion, but I have the energy and the fire that will not die down, he’s the prey, and I’M the predator, I will not allow myself to be the lesser man in our little clash. MWE means too much to me to just let him do what he wants, and the sooner he realizes what I am capable of the better. I defeated Whitman, as much as I like the guy, I feel GM should recall that moment because it’s something he has yet achieved. And it was THAT moment, that I became the champion. The figure of the people, and the dude to beat. Mason gets to a kneeling position and splashes his hands in the water thinking a lot harder about their match. He lathers his hands together and splashes some water on his face, the intent and the concentration in his eyes cannot be questioned or compared to. He turns his head towards Sinc and nods. Mason: I think that if you can continue to think of yourself as a winner, rather than a loser, and add that to the amount of chemistry you and I both have, we can become two people worth remembering, people that fans can point at and idolize. That’s what you and I should aim for, that’s what we should revolve around. Sinc: I can’t argue with you there. Another thing GM doesn’t seem to understand is that I defeated not just Whitman, but his partner Hardaway too. Kevin was the new trend in the business and the man everyone loved to cheer for. And look what happened, he snapped and lost it JUST as I predicted and now he will become the man people love to hate. In my opinion, GM will be just as predictable. I am going to be the prophet for once and straight up say that his crummy return will get even worse, he will wish he wouldn’t have attacked D.C. the night he returned, he will realize that him returning, is possibly one of the biggest mistakes he can ever make. I respect him I really do, but I can’t let him stomp all over me, when I am backed into a corner with no where to go, I can only do one thing, and that is defend myself. And what I do know, is that his offense is getting weak, and it’s MY turn to strike. Sinc raises his fists in the air pumped now to face GM and Hardaway in his tag team matchup. Mason lets out a laugh sensing Sinc may be getting a little too carried away now. Mason: I like your spunk man, but let’s not treat it like it’s pokemon or something. Just bring your A game when the time comes and give em hell, that’s all I ask. And me? Well I’ll do my thing and just continue to be myself, you won’t have to worry about me. I suggest you leave Hardaway mainly to The Canadian Classic, you HAVE recently faced him before after all and I think you and GM are closer skill wise, it can be a really good strategy if we let it. Sound like a plan? Sinc: Sounds good to me. Mason: Good, now let’s go inside and see what the girls are up to. Sinc: Wait a second I’m not done yet. I am not the kind of person unlike GM, who needs break after break to recuperate on an injury or figure out my personal garbage. I left once, because I wanted to spend more time with Kendra. And now that she’s gone, I can be myself again and focus on what I love most, and that is wrestling. Mystique can NEVER take that away from me, this is what I do best, and if he can’t see that he might as well just lay down and get pinned because I have proven myself time and time again and he damn well knows it. Sinc molds his hands into circular curls to pretend like he has a bomb in his hands, or some other circle shaped object. He intends to make everyone see what kind of man GM really is. Sinc: Mystique is someone who is a ticking time bomb. He cannot be controlled, he is physically unable to maintain himself. Think of me as the guy who has to come in and defuse the dangerous object of violence and bring him to succumb to my knowledge of what I do best. If he was really a bomb it would be defusing it, but in this case, the knowledge of what I do best is wrestling, and he has only shown me he understands the fundamentals. Like I said, he doesn’t have that extra edge you or I do, he is unable to spark such a thing anymore, THAT’S what he has shown me from his antics. He ends his example of a time bomb and now begins to count on his fingers of what his opponent lacks all around. There is no being subtle, there is no being irrational, there is only truth and indifference. Sinc: He lacks charisma, he lacks understanding, he lacks a sense of reality, he lacks honesty, and he lacks intellect. He simply cannot grasp what I have already felt and tasted. I’ve gotten to know what it feels like to hold that US title Mason, I’ve gotten to know what it feels like to wear the world championship around my waist, and I most importantly have gotten to know what it TAKES to be a champ. Mystique lives off EW and hating everybody because they won’t cheer him on or respect him, well I respect him, he just fails to see that because gold has blinded him. Mason has clearly made a fatal error in getting Sinc very much into the idea of him being more confident in himself, and now full of flaming determination. Resoan just simply sighs and lets Sinc continue on. Sinc: Even if he doesn’t hide behind a mask anymore he is still hiding behind a barrel of lies. He thinks he’s so tough, he thinks he’s unbeatable even after losing he calls it a flaw. You know what I think? I think he’s not ready to confront what he can’t handle. He cannot handle people cheering for the nicer superstars, he cannot handle being in a slump right after a big return, he cannot take being looked at as a lesser man. And that will be his demise, no matter what he does, he will always be stuck in this mindset of hatred and distaste, for him I say good luck, because he will need it. Faces are world champs for a reason, do you know why Mason? Resoan just massages his head letting out a broadened breath to the motor mouthed Sinc Mercier. He is reluctant to reply to the question, but he feels obligated and does so. Mason: Jesus Christ Sinc, WHY? Sinc: Because WE HAVE WHAT IT TAKES! He can deny it all he wants, but then he’d just be lying again wouldn’t he? In conclusion I say that whatever he spews at us we will counter with ten fold. He is only halfway to world champ material, and you have renewed my spirit in the sense that I need to start taking more action. GM, this time there will be no buildings burning down, because I will be the pyro and you will become the rubble. If that message can’t be computed to you then I will make sure it is when we enter that ring. Thank you Mason, for awakening me again. People need a champion they can depend on, that champ is myself. Mason: NOW can we go? Sinc: Alright fine, but you better not trick me into drinking vodka again, I don’t even want to tell you what happened last time. Both men stand up and begin walking to the back door to enter Mason’s home. The camera zooms out in a slow merge and begins to gradually fade to a blackness. Picture one of the typical big time movie transitions with the fancy zoom outs and fades and you’ll get the image. ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- After some quiet time and heavy thinking the scene now shows dozens of energy drink cans on the floor. A mix of monster and rockstar cans are laid out everywhere. Christina is sitting on a recliner at the far end of a living room reading the latest issue of vogue while Sinc is in the kitchen just feet away layering a pile of waffles with syrup and Sapphire is sitting on a three seat leather sofa watching Gossip Girls on Mason’s plasma. And Mr. Resoan himself on the other hand, is in the dining room next to the kitchen area chucking plates at a plastic target on the wall in front of him. ((And it's done. Yea his half would go here.
