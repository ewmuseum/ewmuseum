Who wants to play CoD?

Sep 3 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Derf314 - Dude, you should have seen the kill I just got! I threw a sticky off a rooftop and killed four guys at once! It was epic! As the scene opens, we can see Eric Derf sitting on a couch. Scratch that, he's not sitting at all. He's literally sprawled across it, wearing nothing but loose fitting boxers, his hair a mess, empty pepsi cans all over the place. A Playstation 3 controller in hand, and a headset in his ear, he continues playing Modern Warfare 2, because after all, everybodys doing it. And for the sake of this RP, we're going to assume you can clearly hear the voice on the other end of the headset. Also, I'll use actual PSN names. Add em if ya want LMAO. XENA_4_LIFE - I've done that before. I killed 7 people with a single sniper bullet, no scope, while flash banged. Derf314 - That's bullshit man. Total Bullshit. Chee-sus - Yeah, you gotta stop lying man. You don't need to keep trying to impress us. Derf314 - Dude, watch out for the sniper in the back of the airplane. I hate fucking campers. XENA_4_LIFE - But that's a really good camping spot. Derf314 - Camping is gay. Oh, and I got Alpha covered, Cheesus, go get beta with the other dudes. Chee-sus - Is Beta "B"? XENA_4_LIFE - Haahahahaha You dummy. You're so handicapped! Chee-sus - Handi capable!!! Derf314 - Dudes, someone go get B! They're at - FUCK! Someone n00bt00bed me. With a few seconds to spare, Eric Derf quickly grabs a can of Pepsi sitting in the table in front of him, takes a quick chug, and continues playing CoD with his friends. XENA_4_LIFE - Hey Eric, don't you gotta train for a match against some noob in a few days? Derf314 - Who, Chris Moore? Psh, he's nothing. I can - FUCK! Someone kill that damn noob tuber! Chee-sus - Guys, I got an emergency airdrop. Want me to use it yet? XENA_4_LIFE - Go for it. Derf314 - Make sure you leave the airport first, or the crates will land on the roof. Chee-sus - Umm, too late. HAHAHA XENA_4_LIFE - Oh my god you dummy!! HAHA MGR_PWNage - PSHSHSHSHSHSHSHSHSHSHSHSHSHSHSHSHSHSHS!!! Derf314 - Fucking shitty mic dude! "muted" XENA_4_LIFE - Dudes, we got this in the bag anyway. We only need a few more points. We got this... NOO!!!! Chee-sus - NUKE!!! YES!!! Derf314 - OH FUCK YEAH!!! Derf sits up, this nuke is the most exciting thing to happen to him all day! As the game comes to an early end, Eric simply remains on the couch, underwear and all, and just continues to play video games.
