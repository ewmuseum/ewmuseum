one side to a 2 faced coin.; tournament round 1 rp JEKYL #1

Aug 13 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
3 PM, downtown Chicago the day after Crowning Glory. Jekyl makes his way upstairs of an unknown hotel’s room, as he passes people in the hall he intimidates them by making faces at them and yelling at them as they run off frightened. He then comes to room 608 and knocks on the door smiling at the peep hole. Voice*mumbling*: Oh it’s Jekyl… Jekyl: Why helloo there buuuddy… Jekyl snickers like an evil clown. Aeron Beowulf stands in the middle of the doorway looking at Jekyl Aeron: Nothin much. Jekyl: Lets go before we miss the bus. Aeron: Kay, thanks for waking me up. Jekyl nods as Aeron closes the door behind him. Jekyl and Aeron step in the elevator as Aeron presses the ground floor button. Jekyl seeing another patron in the elevator with them. The first feeling of it going down, weightless, almost made the two jump. Jekyl looks at the man that’s trembling and sweating in fear of him. Jekyl smiles and pulls out a knife and looks at the man. Jekyl: Tragic things those elevator murders eh? The man takes a large gulp of air. The elevator dings as it opens about two floors down as the door opens up, the man runs out panting and stumbling down the hall. Jekyl: RUN PIGGY RUN! Jekyl roars in maniacal laughter, Aeron nudges Jekyl’s arm. Aeron: Leave the poor guy alone, people are gunna think we're crazy. Jekyl raises his finger as he starts to open his mouth. Aeron: DON’T, remark on that. Jekyl chuckles as they reach the ground floor. They make their way to the front desk, Aeron dragging along his suitcase behind him, he check out and the 2 make their way to the bus terminal. Traffic zooms back and forth in front of them as they make their way to their bus, but in the blink of an eye it is gone and heading down the road. Aeron: God damn it, Jekyl smiles as his looks around and points to a car dealership. Jekyl: How bout we go take a car for a, "Test drive". Aeron shrugs. Aeron: Sure, this should be fun. The two men walk across the street, cars honking at them. Jekyl taunts at them dancing and flipping them off. Aeron grabs Jekyl’s arm and pulls him across the road. Jekyl with the look of a disappointed child on his face. Aeron and Jekyl are looking at the cars as a dealer runs towards them. Dealer: Can I interest you fellas in anything? Jekyl points to a small smart car. Aeron looks at Jekyl evilly. Aeron: I am not fitting into that clown car. Jekyl: No not that you idiot behind it. The three walk around the smart car slowly revealing Mustang GTO 2009. The dealer puts a large smile on his face. Dealer: Nice choice fellas, so will that be cash or check? Jekyl: Can we take it for a test drive first? Dealer: Suure! Jekyl hops in the front seat while the dealer hands him the keys. Aeron closes the door to the passenger’s seat after he buckles his seat belt. As The dealer attempts to open the back door Jekyl laughs and locks the door, he rolls down his window. Jekyl: That was the test, now watch me drive! The tires screech as Jekyl takes off. Aeron: Yee haw! The two cruise interstate 94 as Aeron’s stomach growls. Aeron: Hey you wanna grab something to eat? Jekyl: Suuuuuure just gotta pick up some money. Jekyl pulls onto the curb of the bank as they close the doors to the car, Jekyl and Aeron walk in. Jekyl stands in line as frightened people let him ahead. Aeron waited at the front door. Jekyl makes his way past frightened guests, who recoil in fear and resentment for his grotesque looks. As he approaches the teller, security guards keep a close eye on him, he looks from side to side to see people staring at him. Areon watches on laughing to himself at the remarks and looks Jekyl is getting. Teller: How may I help you today sir? Jekyl: I would like a withdrawal. Teller: If I could just get your bank card please? Jekyl reaches in his pocket and puts something on the counter. His butterfly knife lies underneath his hand. Jekyl: M’am, I would like you to take that bag behind you and fill it with large unmarked bills…If you do this for me, you will not die today, understood? The woman gulps in fear as a tear quickly runs down her face as she steps backwards and grabs the bag. She goes to a small window and hand I to the woman calmly. As she returns she attempts to keep a smile on her face as to not draw attention. Jekyl pockets his knife and takes the bag and runs out the doors, Aeron follows behind him. They get in the car and speed off once again down interstate 94 Aeron: Dude are you crazy! Robbing a bank in the middle of the day!? Jekyl: You should know me by now. Aeron shrugs as he furls his eyebrow. Aeron: Good point. After about half an hour of driving they pull into a small plaza by Battle Creek. They pull into a McDonalds drive through. Orderer: Welcome to McDonalds how may we help you. Jekyl: I'll have two McChicken’s extra McChicken sauce please. Aeron: And a double Big Mac. Orderer: Will that be all? Jekyl: Did I say that would be all? I two large colas. Orderer: Thank you, your order is $14.57 please pull up to the next window. Jekyl drives and lays a $20 dollar bill that he had taken from the bag of money he had stolen from the bank on the silver window sill. Orderer: Thank you, pull up to the next window to receive your order. Jekyl sighs as he pulls up. Jekyl: Why can’t they just make you pay and give you your food at the same damn window? Aeron shrugs as the lady hands Jekyl their order. Server: Thank you and have a nice day. Jekyl: Bite me. Aeron laughs as he grabs his big mac and starts eating it. Taking breaks in between bites to drink. Jekyl: Damn. They call me a pig. Aeron: What? I was hungry. Jekyl: I can tell. Jekyl laughs as Aeron continues eating. After about another hour of driving they finally make it to Detroit. Aeron: Hey, just drop me off at a hotel. Jekyl: Sure. Aeron finishes off his fries as Jekyl drops him off. Aeron waves good bye as he walks into the hotel. Jekyl takes off down the road as the scene fades for a moment. As the scene returns Jekyl is in his hotel room lying on his bed, the heat creeps in the windows from outside making Jekyl sweat as he ponders, so he gets up, shuts the windows and turns on the AC then lies back down. The sudden cool breeze washes over his face making him fall into a sudden sleep. ****************************************************************************************************************** Jekyl is seen as a boy about the age of 9, in his house at the point in time where his father was drunk and about to cut his mouth. Jekyl’s Father: Why So Serious? Let’s put a smile on this serious face. At this point his father has taken the kitchen knife and cut Jekyl’s mouth open giving him the permanent smile after trying to fight him back, he runs out the door screaming, his father runs behind him, but not after him…His father took off into the distance never to be seen again. As Jekyl is running down the road stumbling over the untied shoelaces he falls onto the road where a car comes to a screeching halt. A woman steps out as the boys face is illuminated in the headlights. She comes to his aid and brings him to the hospital, the police and nurses stand by as Jekyl tells them the story of what happened. The police put a warrant out for his arrest but he was never seen again. ****10 Years Later**** Jekyl is the age of 19 and his temper has gotten the best of him, he is standing nose to nose with a strange man in the corner of a bar, they both breathe heavy as if they just had a scrap, tables behind them overturned, broken glass all over the floor, a wide area is cleared behind them with shards of wood scattered all over the place. The bartender jumps over the bar and separates the two men. Bartender: What the hell are your problems? You attack one another for no reason disturbing my business? Jekyl gives the man a sharp look then hit the bartenders arm away from his chest. Jekyl: This asshole is my father… The bartender now confused looks at the man. Bartender: Is this true? Man: Yeah, I recognize that scar anywhere. That’s my son, Ryder… Jekyl: Wanna know how he knows theses scars? That son of a bitch gave ‘em to me… The barman even more confused asks the inevitable. Barman: How did he give you those scars? Jekyl: I was 9 and as you can see, this asshole is a drunk, and one night he came home drunk and started trashing the house in a rage, my mother grabbed a kitchen knife to protect herself and “daddy” over here didn’t like that, he goes over and grabs it from her hands. I was watching this the whole time when he came over to me with the blade. Now I am obviously frightened and he kneels down and asks me “Why So Serious?”. At this point in time he said “ let’s give this serious boy a smile, and that’s when he cut my mouth open. After that night I never saw him again until now. Do you understand now? The bartender’s mouth is gapping open as he cannot believe the words that just came out of Jekyl’s mouth. He gives the man a look to confirm its true as the man nods his head. Bartender: Well I don’t care what beef you two have here but I don’t want this fighting in my bar. Both of you out! Jekyl: Well “Dad” You look nervous, let’s give this serious man a smile! Jekyl now furious punches the bartender knocking him out cold as he pulls a knife out. The scene focuses on the man’s face as his eyes widen and blood drips from his mouth as he drops to his knees. Man: You…… I …love you son.... Jekyl: Don’t waste your breath asshole! The man falls to his side as the floor, the bartender unconscious in the ground beside his dead father. Jekyl folds up his knife and walks out of the bar, bystanders confused and scared do nothing to stop him. A laugh is heard as Jekyl exits the bar and the scene fades once again. ****************************************************************************************************************** Jekyl opens his eyes and sits up in shock, he looks at the clock as it says 4:27am. He flops back down and rubs his face and sits up again, the air conditioning still running cools his body and calms him down a bit. Jekyl: Where the fuck did that come from? Jekyl gets up and walks to the washroom turns the cold water on, he lets it run for a minute to make sure its cold. As he fills his hands with water, he looks at himself in the mirror and bends over to splash his face with water, as he stands upright and continues to look in the water, he dabs his face with a dry towel, as he lowers the towel a flash image of his father appears behind him in the mirror. He spins around, knife drawn, startled but nothing is there, as he walks out into the hotel bedroom he takes one last look in the bathroom as he sees something written in red on the mirror. “Ryder ‘Jekyl’ James MWE US Champion” Jekyl staggers back in disbelief and for once in his life since the incident, he is scared, he blinks a few times hoping that it was only a figment of his imagination, he rubs his face once more as he storms out of the room. 30 minutes later we see Jekyl sitting on the edge of a different bed, the door creeks open as Aeron walks into the room. Aeron: What the fuck? What are you doing here? How did you get in? Jekyl: Funny what you could do with a window. Aeron: Were 5 floors up! Jekyl: Well, you should have seen it to believe it! Aeron shakes his head for a second and continues speaking as he drops his bags by the dresser. Aeron: Well what are you doing here? Jekyl: We need to talk… and you’re going to listen. Aeron: Fine but you have to listen to what I have to say afterwards. Jekyl: I wasn’t negotiating, but I will let you go first. Aeron: After you dropped me off I got a nice visit by to officers just outside that door! Jekyl smirks for a moment. Jekyl: What did they ask you? And what did you tell them? Aeron: They asked about you, why I was with you, and asked about the robbery and the car theft. Jekyl: How did you reply sir? Aeron: They know I was with you so I answered them that, as for the car I admitted it was us, and for the bank, I told them I had no knowledge of your intensions or actions. Jekyl: Good boy, now It’s time for you to listen. Aeron pulls out a bottle of whiskey from the bag and a couple of shot glasses out of a cupboard, he pours the 2 a couple shots and sits down. Jekyl takes his shot instantly and grabs Aeron’s and downs it. Aeron: You just stole my drink! Jekyl: Fine price to pay for talking to the cops! Aeron: Touché Aeron pours himself another shot and sits on the edge of the couch. Jekyl: I had a flashback, one that I didn’t expect and one I have tried not to remember for a VERY long time now. Jekyl begins to explain his flashback and the writing on the mirror as the scene fades to black with Aeron having a disbelieving look. -FIN-
