This Secret is Better; Sonny Williams RP

Nov 7 2008

Sonny Williams

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A camera begins to fade into what is now a black and blant image. It takes time for the camera to fade and dissolve into a clear image, but it does so leaving it with a picture perfect image. It seems to be based in a trashed apartment. Food scraps are seen on the ground, alcohol beverages seen scattered amongst it all and the bed sheets completely torn off. The apartment is silent, with all but what makes noise; the sound of a television. The camera turns around, as you can see the television on, but there watching the television is former MWE World Heavyweight Champion Sonny Williams. Sonny is sitting up with no shirt on and baggy black jeans. He has a cigarette in one hand and a bottle of beer in the other. He takes a small drag of his cigarette before sculling down some beer. His hair is messed up and looks dirty, and his eyes are accompanied by huge bags under them caused by sleep deprivation. The camera turns back to the small television which has food scraps surrounding it. It is set on a channel that has a comedic show on. He takes another scull of his beer and press's down the cigarette in an ash tray. The television audience laugh is heard as Sonny snubs. Sonny Williams: That wasn't even funny. Sonny turns to the side and notices that their is a camera. He takes a quick scull of his beer and leans back on the couch, taking the remote by his hand to change channels. He surfs the television channels for a bit, as there is nothing good to watch on television. Sonny gets aggravated and quickly chucks the remote across the other side of the room, breaking it into two pieces. Sonny Williams: Fuck Cable Television, they give you 500 channels to choose from but there is never anything on. They can go screw themselves once, then again and then again, and then maybe they'll realize what fucking morons they are.. -Goes into a quiet mumble- Sonny looks to the camera and has a look of fierce anger and annoyance in his eyes. The camera angle doesn't move off him as moment by moment, the aggravation builds on. Sonny Williams: What the fuck are you looking at, get the fuck out of my apartment before I shove all the fucking fruit I got stashed in my fridge up your pinkie tight asses. Sonny looks on as he finally realizes something. Sonny Williams: ...Shit I ate all the fruit. The camera begins to forward out of the apartment as orders of Sonny Williams. Sonny stands up and yells out to stop the camera crew from leaving his apartment. The camera turns around to see Sonny speaking. Sonny Williams: You know what, you can stay. BUT.. if you ask any questions, you're out of here. If you laugh or make any sly comments, you're getting a fist to the face and if you go as far as to mention the name of the MWE World Heavyweight Champion, you're gonna have a lifetime of health bills to pay off. I hope I make myself clear.. The camera makes a nodding motion indicating that the camera crew agree with what Sonny had to say. They come closer to Sonny, who flips out a pack of cigarettes. He takes one out and picks up his lighter and lights the cigarette. He throws the lighter back onto the coffee table and takes a few drags of his cigarette. He sits down on the filthy couch and looks towards the camera. Sonny Williams: I'm guessing, just alike every media crew in this fucking country, you want some answers, you want something to sell magazines. You see I haven't left this apartment for two days, I haven't bathed for three days and I haven't had a good night sleep since the night before Wrestle-Wars. And before you fuckers ask me about Sinc, I'll get along to that DC impersonating son of a bitch soon but first there is another motherfucker whose name will not be spoken of who needs to listen up and hear my words of anger and aggravation. Sonny places his cigarette in a ash tray and picks his bottle of beer up, sculling it until finished. He throws the beer bottle across the room carelessly making it shatter into tiny pieces. He looks up and fiercely into the camera from his sitting position on the couch. Sonny Williams: You know who you are, but what you don't know is the heart ache, the hurt, the pain, the suffering and all the fucking shit I have felt since our last meet. What you took off me was like taking a child from it's parents, it is unbearable to cope with. Fuck because of you, you son of a bitch, I have started smoking again. That is the fucking grief and aggravation you have caused me. And while none of this is a laughing matter, what makes me laugh is the fact that you think that I'm just gonna fuck off. Well you're wrong you motherfucking disgraceful bitch. I've been through the darkest of hours, only to get back up on my two feets and raise my middle finger to all those fuckers who thought small of me. Sonny Williams: But you see I'm not a guy to mess with. People have seen the charismatic, joking and hope bringing Sonny Williams. People have seen the destroying, brutal and remorseless Sonny Williams. Hell, people have even seen the ho fucking, alcohol chucking and party animal Sonny Williams. But never before have they seen a man I am now, because if you throw all those attributes together you turn out with this son of a gun you're watching right this fucking moment. This didn't come of my own will, this came of a man whose name will forever so be silenced. This person I have evolved to, he doesn't like to be made a fool of, he doesn't like to be humiliated but what he does like is, REVENGE! Sonny has fire in his eyes shown from the anger and frustration he is showing. His veins on his neck are seen to be popping out also due to this. Sonny Williams: So to the same guy who took my most prized possession, hear this. I'm coming after you. Not this week, and maybe not the week after that but one of these days I'm gonna pop right back into your life and put it at risk, because you've caused too much grief to be let loose with a working bone structure. My pain will become your weakness, because like one motherfucker once said, revenge is sweet. Sonny takes a small drag of his cigarette before he gets up from the filthy couch he is sitting on. He walks over towards the fridge and opens it fully. Nothing is seen inside but about a case worth of beer bottles. Sonny picks out two beers. He looks at the camera whilst opening one of the two beers and heading back towards the filthy couch. Sonny Williams: Now this is where all the shit I've gone through over the past week can be unleashed on one motherfucker I could say, I happily dislike. You see when I discovered that my match for Monday Night Mayhem was against Sinc Mercier for the United States fucking Championship I was fucking furious. It's absolute fucking bullshit! One week ago I was known to be the MWE World Heavyweight Champion and now I'm contesting for a second-rate title, one of which I have held two fucking times? I've made that belt what is it today and now I've gotta pick up the fucking pieces? Sonny sits down on the couch, sculling down a few more sips of one of his two beers and taking a drag of his cigarette before pushing it out in a ash tray. He looks back into the camera with a look of frustration on his mind. Sonny Williams: It's fucking oblivious to say the least. What, is Richard Covell that fucking stupid to put me in a match where I could win with my eyes closed? But if that's how the cookie crumbles, then so be it I'll just have to put up with claiming another title to my collection. Which finally bring me the very man I call my opponent for this week, Sinc fucking Mercier. Sonny chuckles as he takes another scull of his beer. All of a sudden a loud thud is heard on the front door of the apartment. Sonny makes no movement in care as he continues to drink down his beer. The door hears to open as footsteps come walking down the hall way and into the room of where Sonny Williams is at. It appears to be Sonny's manager Mr.Cooper who as always is in a stylish Italian suit. He looks quite pissed off. Mr.Cooper: Where the hell have you been? Sonny shrugs and takes down some more beer. Sonny Williams: On this place we call Earth. Mr.Cooper: Seriously, where have you been? Sonny Williams: Here. Mr.Cooper looks a bit annoyed at Sonny. Mr.Cooper: Can you just tell me!? Sonny Williams: Jesus Christ, I've been in this exact fucking apartment. Wooptie fucking do! Mr.Cooper: .. You could have said that in the first place. Sonny takes another chug of his bottle of beer. Sonny Williams: Well, as you could imagine I am one pissed off motherfucker at the moment, don't make me even worse. Mr.Cooper: Okay then, but your match this we- Sonny interrupts Mr.Cooper. Sonny Williams: It's being taken care of. Mr.Cooper looks a tad confused at Sonny's response. Mr.Cooper: How so? Sonny Williams: Lights, Cameras.. And Sonny Williams fucking action! Sonny takes another sip of his beer as Mr.Cooper puts his hand on his forehead. Mr.Cooper: Oh dear lord.. Sonny Williams: What? Mr.Cooper: You're off your bloody face! Sonny chuckles and takes another dose of his beer. Sonny Williams: Nah, not yet. I'm not like you Cooper.. I can actually stand after three beers. Mr.Cooper: That was only one time! Sonny chuckles and sips his beer again as it is finished. He throws it over his head carelessly, as it smashes on the ground Mr.Cooper looks on. Sonny puts his hand out and reaches for his other beer on the coffee table and opens it before taking a few more sips of that beer. Sonny Williams: I was only joking. Mr.Cooper: Ohh.. Well so was I. Sonny chuckles once again. Sonny Williams: If you say so.. Now, could you push aside for a bit, I'm kinda in the middle of shooting a promo. Mr.Cooper nods and finds a seat to place himself in. Sonny Williams: So where was I again? Mr.Cooper puts his hand on his forehead in annoyance at Sonny. Mr.Cooper: Your match, Sinc Mercier. Sonny Williams: Oh yeah.. So this guy, Sinc, thinks he can be the next US Champion which to be completed he is gonna have to defeat a huge, monstrous structure so very powerful, and no I'm not talking about the Golden Gate Bridge, I'm talking about.. Me, Sonny Williams. But what this guy should really be concerned about rather than winning and growing his reputation, is growing a pair of fucking balls because lately he's been the guy around the block who shits out their mouth rather than their ass. Sonny takes down some more beer before wiping the remainders from his lips. Sonny Williams: But if that's who he is, I can't change that. And I can't change the fact that he likes to joke around and attempt to be DC version 2. But I'm sick of this motherfucker. I'm sick of the typical stereotype alike him who think they're fucking king when they've proven fuck all to back it up. I am a fucking king. I am a former two time United States Champion, a two time World Champion.. So if anyone around here is that fucking good, it's me. Sonny Williams: And Sinc, I know you're gonna watch this because your fucking scared that you won't be holding what has been your life for the past three months. But that's life, life can fuck you up from any angle but it's only the ones with true power that get back on their feet and say fuck off to the rest of the world.. Are you one of those people Sinc, are you? Fuck no, because all you've got is ego and ego means fucking shit in this industry if you ain't got wrestling skills under your buckle to combine with it. Sonny takes another sip of his bottle of beer as Mr.Cooper looks on at him. Mr.Cooper: How bout we stop with the alcohol for now? Sonny turns and looks at Mr.Cooper and takes another drink of his beer. Sonny Williams: I'm in the fucking mode, don't tarnish it. Mr.Cooper: Hmm.. Whatever you like.. Sonny smirks at Mr.Cooper as he has thought of something. Sonny Williams: Stacks on deck, Patron' on ice, and we can pop bottles all night.. Baby, you can have whatever you like, I said you can have whatever you like.. YEAH! Mr.Cooper looks a bit annoyed at Sonny for singing apart of a song, when he should be on his match. Mr.Cooper: Can we get back to more important things now? Sonny Williams: Okay, you can have whatever you like. Yeah, back onto that albino motherfucker whose obsession with cocks is more noticable than a lesbian outlook. The guy is one of the most obnoxious fucks I have ever come across, which is actually kind of awesome because thankfully he isn't a good wrestler so it gives me the chance to kick his ass from here to the middle east. So he's a former United States Champion, fuck so am I. Sonny takes another chug down of his beer as he scratches his chin also. Sonny Williams: But despite that fact, he's still just a.. What? A loudmouth? The fuck does that have to with wrestling? Nothing. Doesn't that make a clear fucking signal that this match is going to be more than less a landslide.. Let's not forget the fact that this isn't even my fucking leagues, this is one below what I should really be and whilst maybe the United States Championship may not be the correct title for me to be aiming for, I'm gonna take it and with a smile on my fucking face. Sonny Williams: And whether or not people have faith in me to hold it, I couldn't give a flying fuck because there's one thing I'd hate more to see than lose and that is to lose to see a reign of a bad champion. So Sinc, go ahead and make some jokes, fight the claims of what I said because at the end of the day I don't give a fuck what you think because until you've achieved what I have is the day when I will think of you with respect in one hand and thought in the other. Sonny picks up his bottle of beer and stands up and looks firmly into the camera. Sonny Williams: Sinc you can call me a disgrace to this company, but you know just as well as I do that I mean more to this industry than you will ever. So I will end on these few final fucking notes.. I'm not just taking the United States Championship home with me, I'm taking away all the hope and dignity you once had inside of you. You're one ungrateful motherfucker I'm gonna be wiping off my wind shield. I will beat you, and I will be the next MWE United States Champion no matter what! So this is how it ends.. Sonny Williams: CHA FUCKING CHING! Mr.Cooper begins to slowly clap as Sonny turns and looks at him. Mr.Cooper: Bravo! Sonny guzzles down his last drops of beer in the bottle. He throws the bottle over his head as it smashes on the ground. Sonny Williams: Someone go start the shower and get me another fucking beer. The scene begins to fade as Sonny sits back down on the filthy couch. Promo ends. OOC: I'd like to say sorry to Sinc, just for having this up a little to late. I was very busy these two weeks with work and this is it. I hope this gives you enough time to reply to it if you wish.. Please leave feedback.
