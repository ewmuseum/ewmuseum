Corruption at it's finest hour...; Jay Riddick's Debut RP

May 1 2009

Jay Riddick

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
-“Throughout the centuries there were men who took first steps down new roads armed with nothing but their own vision.” - Ayn Rand The mind is perhaps the greatest human utility to ever exist; it’s one of the most powerful forces that lie within us. It gives us the ability to think, to give us perspectives on what we see and will create new beliefs. It is an external communication that tells us things we don’t always think. Think of those moments, those moments in life where we can’t handle the pain, the pressure, the temptation and while we reside at a crossroad. Our minds begin to speak to us, they tell us either “Stop, you can’t handle this. Give up.” or it’ll tell you to hang on and encourage you to make it to the other side. The mind is what creates what we want to believe in, it retains them deep in our hearts and our memories. If perspective is what creates diversity, diversity creates unity and unity creates understanding, the mind is what creates perspective and it is the gateway to bigger and better things. Indeed, the mind is a powerful force of positivity and understanding, however what happens when one’s mind becomes corrupted? What happens when one’s mind is twisted? The mind isn’t simply our greatest resource of mental thoughts; it’s our weapon of mass destruction. It’s what can change our entire views of the world from positivity and purity into pure negativity and evil. The process of corruption to the mind can be extremely simple. It can either be caused by one big occurrence or simply even a build up of short shots to the heart can cause the slow demise of the mind. For a strong force, the mind can be very weak. Don’t fully understand? Can’t think about somebody who has a corrupt mind to relate to? Well here’s a story about a man called Jay Riddick. A man who has withstood all the physical and mental abuse for many years and has finally deteriorated his mind after all of it. But now Jay Riddick isn’t going to take it anymore, now is the day of “The Uprising”. Welcome to the Chronicles of Riddick! It may not seem like much, but as a child, Jay was neglected by his parents and had it done enough to the point where Jay had low self-confidence, at that point even when he was given constructive criticism at school or anywhere else he’d take it too personal as a shot towards him. Given that he developed a sensitivity problem at a very young age, he suddenly became everyone’s top target in the classroom for people to make fun of him. There’s still plenty of time to tell more of the story, but I’ll save that for another time as time moves along. At the moment, Jay is inside the house he grew up in. As the camera finds itself climbing a set of creaky, carpeted stairs, it lurks through the hallway on top of the stairs to the 3rd door on the right, even though no door is set there. It turns into the room only to see a room painted navy blue, and Jay Riddick leaning against his old bed with his forehead planted in his palms. Beads of sweat roll down from the top of his bald head and as he raises his head away from his palms and looks into the camera to show his red face from perspiration. He heaves his breath back and forth through the small spaces in between his teeth to show his frustration.- Jay Riddick: Every time I tell you, every single moment I keep myself within isolation of this obscenely demonic world, the only thing I can hear. The only thing that I have to listen to is the sound of ringing, I look for the source of the sound yet nothing is to be found. I then realize that the sound is nowhere but inside my head. That infernal ringing won’t cease, it has no definition to me, I cannot understand it. The only thing I do know is that it causes my impetuous emotions. It all starts off when I finally become happy, then the ringing drives me to frustration and then eventually to madness. But until now, this didn’t make sense. I couldn’t understand my feelings for the sound of madness that rung through my head, I couldn’t understand how something that could not be understood can be the inner drive, the motivation that lies within me. But one day, one sombre day, I had an enormous headache, I made my way down to the kitchen and emptied out a bottle of painkillers but the pain didn’t stop. This pain that lurked within my head lurked deeper within, this wasn’t a headache, this was a psychological revelation. You see, revelations are enlightening yet sometimes it hurts to learn a lesson. Sometimes when there are things to be won, there are things to be lost. And I learnt this the hard way; I learnt that the sounds in my head were subliminal messages. Messages that told me the pain would soon cease for me. That my days of suffering would be over. Nobody is going to be laughing at Jay Riddick anymore, those days will be over. A new day is upon us, a dawning of avenging. These days will be featuring Jay Riddick getting his revenge by taking all the pain that was given to me being returned on every single body that steps into the squared circle against me. My first taste of revenge will be the sweetest and tastiest. After years of receiving this antagonizing pain, after tasting its bitter taste of being made fun of and talked about, it’s all going to stop. This story already had its ending and it is done! It’s done! Finished! No more talk will come to this story! The day of the uprising is upon us. And no longer will the dark cloud of oppression stay above my head. No longer will the name Jay Riddick become a ridiculed name. On this day, the day of the uprising, Jay Riddick goes from being a national opprobrium to becoming something bigger than the values of life itself. Not even the brightest of days will be greater than my gift of the darkest hour. Because I make my debut this week in MWE against four other people who think they have a bright future. However when I step into the ring, their young lives with futures ahead of them will become a thing of the past. If anyone had anything to say after this match, I guarantee you everyone would say ““The Uprising” Jay Riddick”. Shhh...I hear footsteps, It’s coming, It’s finally coming..... It’s creeping in.... -Jay Riddick is sitting by his bedside, holding his head inside both his palms. He then rests his head back and dulls the pain that lies within by humming a familiar tune to himself, a beat from a poem of his own creation entitled “Visions”. It goes like this... Infinite visions bound to set sail, All but one are doomed to fail. The Anti-Christ deems religious genocide for solution, Out to set the revolution. Pain from heaven and hell collide, All is known is that the pain is inside. Lurking within mindsets and oceans of thought, You cannot buy my dignity but my soul can be bought. Corruption has been misdefined, A clueless state of mind. How can corruption be the one, That made me the animal I’ve become? Cease the mercy and cast the pain, Let it fall on me like the rain. I’m built to destroy and on one mission, To bring truth to the eyes of my vision. Written by the Chronicles of Jay Riddick.-
