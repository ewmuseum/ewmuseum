Boozing up to a Conclusion

Sep 26 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
An MWE camera begins to fade into picture as it takes time to blend and become a picture perfect image from the camera. The camera is situated in West Virginia at night in a cab driven by an Indian cab drive. The cab pulls over in front of an exotic club. The cab driver turns back to look at his passenger in the back seat. The passenger is in a ragged grey hoodie, with blue jeans with the left leg held up by a red bandana and the MWE World Championship resting on his shoulder. He also has a pair of Nike Air Force's on. He takes off his hoodie as it shows to be current MWE World Champion Sonny Williams. Indian Cab Driver: Okay sir, that comes to a total of 43 dollars and 35 cents. Sonny has a look of shock on his face as the Indian Cab Driver puts out his hand indicating for Sonny to pay up. Sonny Williams: How does 15 dollars and a nickel sound? Sonny puts his hand in his pocket and collects all the loose coins and few notes that are in his pocket. He gives it to the Indian cab driver and quickly exits the cab running off, holding his title into the mix of the crowd outside the club. The Indian cab driver gets out of his car and begins to try and find Sonny. Indian Cab Driver: Oh for Ganesa's sake. Mr. Main Man come out now, nobody gets away from Akmed Shulvi without paying up. The Indian cab driver continues searching for Sonny as Sonny is too far ahead in the mix of the crowd outside the club for the cab driver to find him. Sonny is near the front of a queue for entering the club. Sonny puts his hand in his pocket and takes out a piece of paper. He unfolds it and reads the message on it which reads "Meet at Club Central @ 8:00PM tonight". He looks up at the big sign above where he is standing which reads "CLUB CENTRAL" in exotic type. He throws the piece of paper on the ground and puts the MWE World Championship back on his shoulder. It takes a few minutes for him to get to the front of the line. He finally does. Two big bouncers are out the front of the line allowing and disallowing people coming in. Bouncer No.1: Well look who we got here, it's yo homeboy Sonny 'The Main Man' Williams. You know the most amazing freestyler turned poem writer. Dude, you're a pathetic joke. The bouncers begin laughing between one and other as Sonny walks a little closer to the bouncer who just criticised him. He looks deeply into his eyes and makes a low grunt. Sonny Williams: I wouldn't start pressing my buttons if I were you.. Now you're a bouncer, and I like to bounce things. One thing I'd like to bounce is your head against that brick wall over there, because you see, I'm not a moraless, thick headed piece of shit who still lives with his mother and collects action figures that are waaaaaay past it's date. But, you know, if that's how you roll it's fine by me. If that's what floats ya boat then all is cool, but don't ever try to insult me because I've been through a career where I have been paid to dish insults and cop them. The bouncers look fumbled as Sonny licks his lips in a sinister motion. Bouncer No.2: Let him in. The bouncer opens the front rope to the entrance of Club Central as Sonny walks in slowly eyeing off the bouncer who had harsh words to say to him. He makes a low grunt when passing and looking at him before entering the club. He enters through a small passage way which leads on to a club. There are exotic lights, many beautiful women dancing around and a DJ playing tunes. Sonny stops for a second and looks around at the club before continuing walking. He walks through the dancers as some stop and stare and notice his arrival. He forwards himself over to the bar and takes a seat. A stunning young lady comes beside Sonny ready to order a drink. Sonny notices her and looks back, checking her out. Sonny Williams: Hey, I heard they don't allow females to purchase drinks here, mind if I buy you a drink? The lady turns and looks at Sonny and his MWE World Title and smiles at him. Lady: Sure, that'd be good thanks. Sonny Williams: Hey you, in the outrageously embarrassing apron. Could I get this fine lady here a martini and me, could I get a shot of whisky? Waiter: Sure. Sonny turns back to the lady who is looking at Sonny with a gaping smile on her face. Sonny makes a slight smirk at her. Sonny Williams: So you from round here? The lady is still staring at Sonny and his title with a smile on her face. She snaps out of it realizing what Sonny had said. Lady: No, I'm, I'm originally from New York, just down here at the moment for family. Heard about this place somewhere so I decided to give it a look. It's impressive. Sonny Williams: Yeah it's not half bad. The waiter comes up to Sonny with a martini and a shot of Whisky. Sonny turns to him and gives him the waiter the money and takes each drink in hand. He turns back to the lady who is seen to be making out with another man. Sonny looks pissed off as he quickly chugs down the martini and the shot. The lady and the man stop making out as she looks to Sonny who looks quite annoyed. Lady: Jeez, these waiters are really slow. Sonny Williams: Sorry I was wrong, they actually allow female dogs to purchase drinks here, so looks as though you're set for the night ay. The lady looks quite ticked off at what Sonny had said as Sonny has a evil smirk on his face. The lady and her man exit from Sonny's presence as Sonny turns back to the bar sitting in a stool. He signals for the waiter to come down as he does. Sonny Williams: Another shot, when your ready. The waiter nods as he gets Sonny's shot glass and takes it over to fill up with whisky. All of a sudden a man begins to prod Sonny in the shoulder trying to get his attention. Sonny turns thinking it is the lady. Sonny Williams: NO, I would not like to have a threesome with yo.. Sonny sees it to be his part time manager, Mr.Cooper who is in a very delicate business suit. He has a grin on his face as Sonny looks surprised to see him here. Sonny Williams: .. Oh, Cooper. I thought, I thought you were someone else. Mr.Cooper: You know, I've had my fair share of threesomes in the day. But, that's not what I came here to talk to you about. Sonny looks puzzled. Sonny Williams: How did you know I was here? Mr.Cooper smirks. Mr.Cooper: I'm the one who sent that message, and also I knew you were here cause I found it on the ground out the front, see. Mr.Cooper pulls out the piece of paper and shows it to Sonny as Sonny blankly looks at it and back at Cooper. He turns back to the bar in his stool as the waiter hands him another shot of whisky. He guzzles the shot down in a swish as Cooper puts his hand on his forehead in disbelief. Mr.Cooper: Why are you taking down shots? You've got a big match this week, did you ever take into consideration what lies on your lap now may not be there next week? Sonny turns to Mr.Cooper careless and turns back and indicates to the waiter to get him another shot. Mr.Cooper: You can't keep guzzling those shots down. Sonny Williams: Watch me.. The waiter walks over again and hands Sonny another shot of Whisky as he takes it down quickly. He looks a bit dizzy but shakes it off. He turns to Cooper and puts his MWE World Championship over his shoulder. Mr.Cooper: I didn't come here to watch my client get pissed off his tits, I came here to talk to you about your match, about MWE. C'mon Sonny, you've got a big match this week against the Vega Knight. Sonny Williams: The Vega Kite? Mr.Cooper looks annoyed. Mr.Cooper: No Sonny, the Vega Knight. You know the guy you opened your newcomers Invitational to last week at Monday Night Mayhem.. He's quite the competitor for a newcomer too and if he's half the competitive wise of Alejandro, you're in for a match. Sonny slowly turns to Mr.Cooper with a look of disgust anger. Sonny Williams: I almost had him done. I was too caught up in my past victory that I thought he would be as easy also. He will pay. Mr.Cooper looks puzzled. Mr.Cooper: Whose 'he'? Sonny Williams: Alejandro.. Rodriguez. Mr.Cooper: Ohh c'mon Sonny, I know you wanted that win but you can't keep sorrowing over it. People don't want to see the depressed, emotion thriving Sonny Williams. They want to see the attitude, the integrity, the raw passion that you have locked up inside of you. Think of it like this, he may have the win over you, but you've got the World Championship. Sonny isn't looking at Cooper and staring off into the distant in his stool. He gets the attention of the waiter. Sonny Williams: One more shot thanks. Mr.Cooper puts his hand on his forehead in annoyance. Mr.Cooper: That's it, we're leaving. The waiter comes over with Sonny's shot of whisky as Sonny snatches it from him and lugs it down his stomach. He gets up out of the stool and opens his pockets. He is empty and has no cash as he looks over to Mr.Cooper who stops. He looks very annoyed as he grabs out his wallet and puts a 50 dollar note on the bench and looks at Sonny in annoyance. Mr.Cooper: .. Last time I ever meet with you at a club. Sonny Williams: What'd you say? Mr.Cooper: Nothing. Sonny and Mr.Cooper make their way through the dancers in the club nearing to the exit. They walk out through the front entrance as many people notice the pair to be from MWE and off television. They walk out the front and past the bouncers who Sonny eyes off with. Mr.Cooper pokes Sonny to stop and continue walking along. Sonny Williams: Where are we going? Mr.Cooper: Somewhere without alcohol, drugs and girls in tight jeans and tank tops. Sonny Williams: Why? Mr.Cooper stops walking as Sonny does so. Cooper looks at Sonny ready to respond. Mr.Cooper: Why Sonny, I'll tell you why. You were in that club guzzling down shot after shot when clearly you are aware of your match that is this week for the MWE World Championship. You must take this serious or mark my word, you will see the same fate you did last week. Sonny looks onto the distant knowing of what Mr.Cooper said. He continues walking and finds a path bench and sits on it as Mr.Cooper follows him in doing so. Sonny is hunched over with the MWE World Championship in his lap once again. Mr.Cooper is sitting beside Sonny. Mr.Cooper: I hope that has kicked some sense into you boy cause you better beli.. Sonny cuts Mr.Cooper off from speaking. Sonny Williams: SHH! It's 9:07PM. Mr.Cooper looks down at his watch and sees that it is 9:07PM and seems shocked in thought of what is going on. Mr.Cooper: Is, is a angel talking to you? (SILENCE) Mr.Cooper: Sonny, can you hear something!? Sonny cracks up laughing as Mr.Cooper looks shocked. Sonny Williams: I was just messing with you fool. Mr.Cooper looks disappointed at what Sonny is doing and not focusing on his match. Mr.Cooper: Sonny! Sonny Williams: I know, I know. I'm aware of my match, I'm aware of the fact that after the next Monday Night Mayhem I may not be holding this beauty in my hands. Now, I know you're a man who likes to live in present and forget past memories but, that lose last week hit home hard. I actually can't remember a time that I felt so disappointed in my effort. But, that's all said and done. It's time to move on and move on to bigger and better things; the Vega Knight, right? Mr.Cooper: Correct. Sonny Williams: This Vega Knight likes to run his mouth like a coyote, he thinks he can walk into the scene and say whatever the fuck he likes. Well, he doesn't have that fucking right just yet. He has beaten one man, a man who just alike him is a newcomer also and after that he thinks he shits all over everyone. You see, I remember the good old days when you had to work for your words. You couldn't come running in like a fucking moron and say you're fucking this and fucking that. Where has this gone? Sonny looks at Mr.Cooper who looks puzzled. Mr.Cooper: You tell me.. Sonny Williams: Down the fucking drain. It's men like him who are in this society and are fucking things up left, right and centre. Vega has angered me. I don't know who the fuck he is, I don't know where the fuck he was born and bred, but I don't care. I hate the way he walks, the way he reeks of a coward, I hate the way he feels as though he is fucking top dog. You see what I hold in my lap, the chance to come up for it comes around like Christmas for most superstars, once a year. I've succeeded in my attempt bitch, which makes me the top dog. So if Vega, and any other newcomer motherfucker thinks they're the fucking best, that they're better than me, or that they've got the biggest fucking dick in all of MWE they can walk right up to me, look me square in the eye and request what I have opened; The Sonny Williams Invitational. Sonny spits on the concrete path showing his anger for what he is saying. Sonny Williams: Vega is going to be the first of many fucking victims to this. I've been in this industry for a while now, and I know arrogant, pissy fucking newcomers much alike Vega like to walk along with a chip in their shoulder in thought that they can be fucking best immediately. Okay, lets be honest, I'm not the best fucking guy but I know had of I walked into the room when I came to MWE and acted like Vega that I would have been eating soup from a fucking straw for weeks. That's what Vega is, he is everything I am fucking not. He is a piece of living shit, he smells of shit, he speaks bullshit, lets just conclude in saying he is shit. Mr.Cooper: Wow, I never knew that alcohol was your switch button. Sonny looks at Mr.Cooper with a crude look on his face. Sonny Williams: Fuck the alcohol, this is it. This is Sonny fucking Williams for ya. I may be drunk, I may even be fucking angered but this is who the people of this earth grew to love. I'm throwing away all my past emotional crisis's, I'm throwing away those fucking loser poems.. I'm not back just yet but I'm fucking Sonny Williams. I'm the World Champion and I will be for the next fucking millennium. Vega has brought out a man he shouldn't have, and now he's going to fucking pay a price. I'm going to kick his fucking ass half way across the states and when I'm done I'm gonna fucking party, fucking drink and get fucking laid because it's coming baby! Mr.Cooper: You got yourself a deal. -FADE- Promo ends.
