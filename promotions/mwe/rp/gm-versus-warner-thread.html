A reminder of the rules - unlimited RPs, max word count = 1000. We cut to footage taken from the hard camera that was taping the latest MWE house show in Manchester, England. Fred Carry is in the ring, ready to introduce the next match. Carry: “The next match is the first half of your joint main event of the evening!” The crowd pop in expectation and it only increases as the lights cut out, as the crowd expect David Whitman or Joey Dallas. The eager buzz pours into boos as “Headstrong” by Trapt rips through the arena. Coloured spotlights dance over the heads of those in attendance before the main lights return to show the Grand Mystique perched on the middle rope in the corner with his arms outstretched. He continues to take in the negative reaction. He looks focussed and driven, unfazed and able to shrug off the atmosphere with ease. Fred Carry had scurried back to his ringside seat and GM picks up the discarded microphone. “I’m here in front of you, the great unwashed scum of Manchester, to talk about my match against Zak Warner at the next Monday Night Mayhem.” GM pauses his initially monotonous speech to enjoy the noise that makes any talking inaudible. It dies down, just for a second and he continues. “After all I have done and what I have been through, I am being thrown to into a lions’ den! A match with no rules, no structure – it makes me sick.” GM is clearly frustrated but fights it back as he paces around the ring, head down. “No doubt Zak Warner suggested it. Your Intercontinental champion is trying to drag me down to his level.” Mention of the champ draws chants of his name from a large section of the audience. Frsutrated, GM looks down at the mat. ”I am a wrestler. I should not be made to fight like a caged animal! The coward hasn’t even put his belt on the line!” GM looks up, with a smirk of evil genius realisation. “Zak Warner – as good as he may be at the moment has booked his own funeral. He wants to face me in a fight? A match with no discipline or even a semblance of rules?” GM tries to conceal it but he chuckles. “Maybe I should send him a copy of Friday Night OverDrive to show him what I can capable of in a fight!” GM stops pacing and invites one of the ringside cameramen inside the squared circle. He moves him to the centre of the ring and looks deep into the lens. “Zak.....You want brutal.....animalistic......barbaric?! Then you will get everything I have. The style of the match *GM shakes his head and wags a finger for extra effect* won’t be an issue, I am more than happy to fight Zak Warner” GM spits a venomous emphasis on the word fight and he raises a taped right fist, as if he is already living the encounter. He backs away slightly as he continues. “I could make you tap, I can make you see stars but I will knock you the fuck out!” GM smiles, a little more content, relaxed, settled. “And when we are done, when I stand over you, victorious, *he raises his right arm as if living the moment* the distant look in your eyes will tell everyone watching that the last brain cells that you had left turned the lights out on their way out!” GM lowers his arm to point at something, a random point in the distance. A blank look comes over his eyes and his mouth opens slightly. He lifts the microphone again to speak as his other arm falls to his side. “Zak, this is not the last that you will hear from me but enjoy your memories while you can. Your future is not in your own hands. I control your destiny. Get yourself familiar with that feeling of total helplessness. That......that is what fate has in store for you.” GM’s music resumes as the lights cut out. Ten seconds later they come back up and Fred Carry quickly enters the ring to continue with the show. “Ladies and gentlemen, now it really is time for the first half of your joint main event for the evening!” The crowd cheers this news, the first positive reaction in a few minutes. *Fade* Ten minutes later, GM is backstage. He is sat in his locker room, on his own. Rather peculiarly, he has his hands outstretched in front of him. He wiggles each finger voluntarily. Frustrated, he removes his mask, throwing to the floor via the wall opposite him and rubs the back of his neck. GM would hate anyone to know but he has been suffering with pins and needles for the longest time. But recently, this had developed into sharp pains, shooting down his arms right into his fingertips and GM was especially concerned. Being a naturally pessimistic guy, GM discovered that he was surprisingly reluctant to do anything about this for fear of the diagnosis. He knew that he had to get through Zak Warner, to make sure that he kept all the credibility that he had from beating Windy so convincingly. Even losing to Whitman had not affected his momentum too much. This match could be the toughest yet and GM refused to do anything but back himself. If Zak wanted to underestimate him in any form, then GM would be happy to punish him for it. It’s all about escalation. You hit me, I will hit you harder. You cut me, I will cut you deeper. Basking in the flames, Warner continues. We keep increasing the standard, oh mysterious one. We keep pushing our thresholds. Inevitably, one of us shall fall, likely for the last time. While I cannot say which of us shall be the first to take their last gasps, I can say quite assuredly that I know no fear. Pain and death hold no sway over my thoughts, save for the embrace of a welcome friend. When they come for me, I will be quite happy to face them. And while it may seem melodramatic, I feel that when we finally let loose upon one another, chances run high that we shall not survive the night. Therefore, I offer you one last consolidation of my respect for you as an equal. My funeral pyre to what we once were, lighting the way to what must be. In the flames, the Intercontinental Champion seems to disappear. Still, through the crackling of the fire comes laughter so piercing that it leaves a trail of fading insanity. In the darkness, his voice echoes. So consider this, as a fellow master of mind games and manipulation. Of all the faces I have shown you, which is true? Betrayer. Failure. Maniac. Weak. Strong. Killer. Savior. Darkness. Light. Do you even know anymore? Do you understand what you combat enough to actually stand as the victor? Or has your perception swallowed you, leaving only the facades behind for you to deal with? What is truth and what is the lie? MWE lives. EW is dead. That’s the truth. Your superiority over myself? That is a lie. The king is dead, long live the king. All that is left is the sword in your belly. But don’t worry. Traitors are the best at finding places for knives. So let us battle, one last clash to be remembered in eternity. Something for you to claim as a legacy, even as your greatest accomplishments lie forgotten, hidden in the sands of time. Blood will be spilled, undoubtedly, with no quarter asked for and none given. However, I do not think either of us would wish it any other way. This is the end of you, Mystique. The death of a legend, in spirit if not in body. We will never forget. Then the night goes silent, as the home of icons meets its final end. We open to an inky sunset, where orange, pink, orange and yellow all flow into each other, meshing together above the solid line of the horizon. In the very far distance, a cliff face dominates above the blurry mix of darkness and street lights of a seaside town. The camera is lowered and jerkily settled in place. The tide rushes in, foaming water as it hits the pebbles a couple of yards away. We see a pair of legs that bend at the knee, bringing a torso into view. Two hands grab the camera and refocus it, to show the Grand Mystique in front of this epic backdrop canvas. GM looks out to sea and to the west where the sun is making its’ final retreat for the day. “Beautiful, isn’t it? It truly shows that on any given day, the universe is capable of something beautifully epic when it is least expected.” An unusual start but GM felt it warranted to acknowledge the backdrop. He leant forward and tucked in his knees. “Zak Warner – to say that you have been impressive is an understatement. Billy Joe Barker, Sonny William, Paige Windy – that’s an impressive list of talent to have held your own against. You have made it clear that you want two things from the MWE” *GM holds up two fingers* and they are very similar to what I expect of myself.” “That is gold on your shoulder and respect in your eyes. I can respect the title that you hold but I refuse to respect you; you haven’t earnt that from me yet.” GM chuckles at this last statement. He was sure people would disagree with the sentiment but was willing to back it up. “I’m going to focus on two of your most recent opponents, Windy Bridges and Sonny Williams. Now Windy” *GM hesitates, almost uncertain of how he wants to word this comment* “is a shadow of the man he was. I beat him senseless at Friday Night OverDrive, which left you to face an exhausted corpse of a being! So take no credit in pinning him in your first meeting.” GM smirks again. He looks away at the horizon, before shaking himself back to reality. He returns a look in the general direction in the camera. “Of course, I daren’t forget about Sonny Williams. Now I know you won your Intercontinental title from him but if you really consider it, you didn’t beat the real Sonny did you?” GM turns and faces the camera directly for the first time. “The real Sonny Williams – the future Hall of Fame lock? The star pupil of the man who will be honoured on the 16th? Sonny, the man who you could not beat on your best day!” GM’s tone is harsh, deliberately so to drive home the point. “You beat the Sonny who had already lost all the passion that he had for the MWE; that man pissed away and surrendered the honour and prestige of the I C title a very long time ago. Long before you pinned him for it.” “Now I see you on a drive to elevate the I C title again and I can respect that. You have snapped up the opportunity that should have been mine!” GM chuckles and looks down at his hands, which are mindlessly playing with the pebbles. “So do what everyone expects you to do.” He throws a pebble into the water and then looks at the camera. In a typically GM fashion, he burns the lens with his stare. “Beat me! In our first EVER meeting, I fucking beg you to try it and let’s see where it will get you and your title!” GM speaks with a confidence, almost never seen from him before. He dared try it as he is almost certain that he is not expected to win. The last image on the increasingly dark sky is a glinting smile. GM reaches up and flicks the camera off. His face, still scarred and bruised from the Friday Night OverDrive, contorts into a sick stare of intensity that even Ace Cannon on his best day couldn’t match. He storms off.
