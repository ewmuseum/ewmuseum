Your Devils; Sharpman RP inspired by The 69 Eyes

Aug 14 2009

Joseph Sharpman

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"If you're gonna start the killing, you best start it right here. Make sure I'm all the way dead, because I'll come back and make you my bitch!" ~Captain J.T. Spaulding (The Devil's Rejects) "Face reality Sinclair ... I have become your obsession" ---------------------------- A single spotlight shines from the rafters as our cameras bring us to the empty Allstate Arena. It is a strange sight to see the usually buzzing scene of an MWE event completely cleared, quite and lifeless. The only thing breaking up this otherwise dull sight, is the man standing center stage. Spotlight flooding down on him, is Joesph Sharpman. His attire is the usual jet black suit, and dark red tie that seems to dance in the light. In his hand, is a rare black rose. His eyes are locked on it, staring intently at the pedals as they gently limp over forming the beautiful figure of the flower. Joesph Sharpman: "Do you remember what this flower symbolizes Sinclair? Do you remember what I promised would happen to you the last time that we met in this ring? ... Because I do. I remember what this rose symbolized. I remember that promise I made to you ... That our last match, would be the death of your career." He let's his thumb slowly move up and down the stem of the rose, feeling the smooth yet harsh surface under his skin as his piercing blue eyes remain on this flower, this symbol of death. As it makes a second appearance in his attack against Sinc. His feet stand still, and his breath remains calm. This has become a rare sight from Sharpman in the last few weeks as his insane laughter has usually taken over by now. Joesph Sharpman: "And despite your glorious return to action .. I know that I succeeded in killing your career that night. I know that when you saw my turn my back and walk up that ramp, as though you were nothing, it struck something in the back of your mind. I triggered a chain reaction that is still in the works to this very day .. And it is slowly dragging your career to hell." This is one of those famous Sharpman tirades, he has done the same thing to many opponents and every time it turns out the same way. He forces the fans to question what he's saying, and he slowly twists and turns reality around to benefit himself. Sharpman knows how to speak, and perhaps this time his knowledge has lead to overconfidence, because a smile. An actual smile of joy, crosses his lips. Joesph Sharpman: "Yet this starts much earlier then that faithful match Sinclair .. No, this starts when you fell into my hands as a lump of clay. You were mine to mold, and you took everything I threw at you willingly. And as time passed by, I carefully sculpted you to what I knew I could destroy ... And to this day you remain there, because when I welcomed you into New Found Sin, I crafted you into a mirrored image of Joey Dallas ..." As he gently presses his thumb against one of the many thorns of the rose, still clutched between his fingers. A maniacal chuckle, a sinister grin and an overpowering feeling of self-confidence all seem to lock themselves on the features of Sharpman in an instant. Joesph Sharpman: "I slowly worked on you, and I forced you to become a carbon copy of me ... And now struggling, you are trying to find that one small bit of originality you once possessed. Yet deep down in your heart of hearts you realize that you will aways be made in my image ..." With his thumb still pressed against the thorn of the rose he pushes in and slowly tears down causing a slow steam of red to pour down his hand. All the while that grin still stuck on his face. His self mutilation just driving the fact that this man is not the same person that joined MWE nearly two years ago. He has changed into a monster hell bent on destroying the company he once claimed to be the savior of. Joesph Sharpman: "And the fact remains that my blood ... The blood that created Joey Dallas, still pumps through your veins. It's what has driven you to become what you are today and is the single reason you can claim to be a world class champion. So you see, my blood ... This blood." Sharpman raises his hand above his head ever so slightly, letting the warm red liquid catch the light and glisten as it continues to drip down his hand and onto his arm. The demented shimmer in his eye tells us exactly what he has planned before he can even open his lips again Joesph Sharpman: "Is what has fueled you to become what I have always craved to be ... The World Heavyweight Champion, and the fact of the matter remains that you didn't want it, until I failed to win it. So come Monday ... I will take back what is rightfully mine." He drops his hand back to his side while simultaneously directing his gaze towards the camera. Cold blue eyes lock on the camera as his evil grin grows wider on his face. Sharpman let's his top row of teeth gently graze across his lip as he begins to open his mouth to continue this promo. Joesph Sharpman: "So march Sinclair ... March like a proud solider to the ring, because this time you will march as a lamb to the slaughter. For this Monday night I will to rewrite the definition of Mayhem, as I bleed you like a pig. You see, this isn't about wins or loses ... This is about setting things back to the way I want them to be .. And I want things to be..." A maniacal laugh and a look up is the only signal that the camera needs to pan up slowly. However as it does the world may wish that it didn't, because our eyes catch the glare off of five tons of steel. A structure that has never before been seen in an MWE arena hangs high above the ring. Hell In A Cell! Joesph Sharpman: "To be dragged down to the depths of hell with me. For you see, I crafted you into the image of a savior ... And that being so, there is only one thing that stops this company from spiraling to it's demise .. Is you Sinc." Camera still focused on the Hell in a Cell that hangs above the ring we watch, as the structure slowly begins to lower towards the ring. That same spotlight making the steel shine in a hideous way, the structure looks godly, yet terrifying. The cell continues to lower as the camera pans back down to Sharpman, whose eyes are already in place to stare towards us in the all too familiar way. Joesph Sharpman: "So the only way to make my dream a reality .. Is to crush you, in every possible way. To only way to fulfill my destiny is to break you. So prepare yourself Sinclair ... For today, you have a face to face with your Devils." The camera pans up to once again show the cell lowering from the rafters, it is nearing the ring as the spotlight suddenly cuts out. For a moment there is silence and complete darkness, but soon everything changes as another spotlight kicks in, this one lighting up the entrance ramp as our angle changes to show it. "Ghost" by The 69 Eyes begins to play slowly as four mysterious men begin to walk towards the ring. Each are dressed in long black robes with a different colored trim on each. One is red, another is dark purple, the next is green and the final is pitch black. They each hold in their hands a large candle in a Gothic style base made of bronze. Their steps are slow and monotonous as they pace towards the ring as Sharpman's voice cues from off camera. Joesph Sharpman: "Sinc ... Consider these men your voice, for each of them holds with them a part of your life that will forever haunt your nightmares. These men are my followers, and they are your Devils." As though on cue all four men come to a halt as Sharpman's voice cuts away, they each stop at a corner of the cell, which has now reached the ground, and turn towards the empty stands. The spotlight onces again flickers out, leaving the only light in the entire arena as the candles in each man's hands. The camera quickly cuts to show the first "Devil" of Sinc Mercier, the man in the green-trimmed robes, like all the others he has a large hood on that casts a shadow hiding his face. The glow from the candle adds a feeling of fear as he begins to talk. "Sinclair ... Standing before you, is your betrayal. For I represent the deception of a faction you may remember as the New Found Sin." His voice is deep, vile and malicious. Yet oddly soothing, as it seems to draw us in to his every word. Making even the smallest of phrases seem of dire importance. Every muscle in this man's body appears to stand still as the words flow in a casual manner. "I am yet one Devil that lives with you through your life, I am but one memory that refuses to be cast aside. Sinc, I am the memory of your endless efforts to control the things that were just too far outside of your grasp. For when things seemed to play out differently than what you had expected, you felt cheated, deceived and ultimately powerless to do what you had done so well in the past." The man's head slowly lowers, his hood wrinkling as he begins to gaze at the candlelight as it dances in front of him. If it had not been for the grand entrance of the others we would be unable to tell if he were alone, because other then his direct surroundings the arena is pitch black. "I am the remorse you felt as you watched Joey Dallas turn his back on you and walk away, the intense sorrow that shot through you as realized that New Found Sin was just another failure added to an already hefty pile in your life ... Sinclair .. I am a memory, that will never die." He raises his head and peers towards the rafters as a mysterious wind blows through, ruffling his hood in the breeze and at the same time blowing the flame of the candle out, leaving this man in darkness as the camera angle switches again. This time in front of the man in the purple garments. His stance mimics the one of the last "Devil" Eyes forward, hands grasped tightly to the candle as the flame dances and gives an eerie glow to this scene. "Sinclair ... I stand here as the Devil of fear! I am the unrelenting nightmare you faced when you walked out on your fans and destiny." This voice is a drastic change from the one we last heard. This man's voice is stern, harsh and unforgiving. It is the type of voice you would expect to hear from an abusive father right before you hits you. The words coming from his mouth are important as well, however in a much different way. "I am that haunting feeling you remember when you decided to tuck your tail between your legs and run away from your problems. I am that reoccurring dream where you finally escape your cage and flee. Because I am that memory of relinquishing the United States championship, I am that feeling of terror that overpowers you every time your name is mentioned in a defense. But I am just one of your Devils, I am just one of the memories you can never escape." Just like the last "Devil" this man lowers his head to watch the candlelight flicker between his hands. A repetitive scene, yet one that seems to have some sort of importance with these men as his stern voice once again breaks through the silence. "I am the trembling feeling of horror that once drove you out of this company, never to heard from again. The fear of once again being forgotten once the one that made you a somebody was torn away from you ... Sinclair ... I am a memory that will never die!" Keeping the rest of his body completely still this man raises his head in the same fashion as the last, that unexplainable breeze once again blowing through to inexplicably remove the light from this man's hands. Once again our view shifts, this time to the man in red. Who stands in the same statuesque position as the last two men. His eyes on the camera, his hands clenching the candle before him. "Sinc .. I stand here today as your lies, your deception. To the people, and to yourself. You see, I am what you have become Sinc. I am the fear of being yourself." This time the change in this man's voice isn't as drastic, however the change in his tone is. This "Devil" is not stern, or harsh. No, he sounds as though he is concerned about Sinc. Once again it is a voice you would expect a father to have, but this time it reminds us of a loving and caring father. "I am that personal concern, I am that unsure feeling about your life and career. You see Sinc, I am the Devil that you stepped on to get to the top. I am the claims of being a fighting champion, yet the fear to defend a champion you had already won. I am the broken promises, to the people you claim to care about. Yet most of all, I am the lie to yourself. I am the voice in your head that tells you that you've done nothing wrong even though you know damn well you have ... But I'm just one of your many Devils Sinc. Just one more memory that's trapped inside the empty crevasse between your ears." Following suit to the two before him, this man drops his head. Forcing his hood to slowly wrinkle over his head, hiding his face even more from our view. The importance of this is still unknown to us, but it appears to be some sort of ritual for these "Devils" and we're sure that the scene next time will be exactly the same, however this time around is not yet finished. "Sinc I am that feeling of joy you get every time you get away with deceiving another man. I am that fear of being caught red handed, the fear to find yourself in an endless web of lies and ultimately the realization that you already have .... Sinclair ... I am a memory that will never die!" I'd like to tell you that things happened differently this time around, but that would be a lie. Because just like the two men before him, this one looks towards the heavens, letting that strange breeze once again blow through. Sending the flame of the candle in his hands into a flurried dance before finally blowing out leaving this man in darkness. The angle switches yet again putting us in front of the final "Devil" the man in those pure black robes and instantly a chill seems to shoot up our spine. Something about this man is just, different. It's not his stance, or the candle in his hands but something just seems ... Strange. "Sinclair ... I represent the personal Devil which has yet to come. For I am the Devil, of your death." That's it, that's what that chill was about ... It was an instinct of terrible things and what this man has to say could very well save Sinc's career, or drive it into the deepest depths of hell. His voice is how you would imagine the Grim Reapers to be, a rough, elderly voice. Although hard to hear, it is impossible to not listen. "I am your greatest personal Devil, and a fear which will haunt you until you draw your final breath ... I represent your realization that everything you have come to know and love, will one day all fade away, leaving you a broken and destroyed man. I am the recognition that you will one day be forgotten, and I am your refusal to stay dormant in peoples' minds. Sinclair I am much more than a fear, I am the inevitable." For the final time we watch as this man dips his head down to watch the candlelight. This time the pause seems longer as his breath is slow and rough, almost a gasp as he fights to keep breathing. However, for some reason the audience cannot wait for this particular "Devil" to continue. "I am that foreboding moment of self acceptance before your last breath ... The looming feeling of fear that is inescapable however, most importantly Sinclair ... I am a realization .... That will soon be met." With one last gasping breath he raises this final man raises his head, obviously having much more trouble with this task then the previous "Devils" before him. Yet just like every time before that wind gusts through the arena and puts an end to the life of the flame in his hands. His last few words seem to hang in the air in this darkness, what did he mean "That will soon be met"? Well my friends, that is for me to know, and you to think over in your heads, but this scene must go on. Almost suddenly four spotlights shine down, each lighting up one of these men, standing ceremoniously in front of the cell. Quickly the light travels from them towards the center of the ring where Joesph Sharpman still stands, proudly looking towards the camera in a small pool of his own blood as the liquid continues to drip from his hand. The evil grin, the unforgiving stare and the mere feeling of terror that he takes with him anywhere seems so much more at this point. Joesph Sharpman: "Sinclair ... My mission, my destiny this week is to drag you down. To break you, to bleed you and when you are at your weakest to strike you down and put an end to this miserable relationship that we have shared for all too long. For after I am finished with you, I move on to bigger challenges ..." Sharpman raises his hand in front of him, holding his arm straight out as he begins to let the blood from his hand drip down to the mat a few feet in front of him as he watches. He watches each and every drop as though it were the last, the grin on his face growing wider and wider each time one splatters against the canvas. Joesph Sharpman: "Because we are just one week away ... From what will be the final stand of a legend. You see, come the end of our match this week Sinclair, I will be prepared for facing a man you have idolized your entire career ... For D.C.'s hell will finally come to a bitter end." He lets his eyes once again drift back to the camera, taking them away from the sickening sight of his own blood and putting them back on the audience. Specifically D.C. and Sinc. The grin has faded away, but the intent is still the same. Joesph Sharpman: "The challenge has been answered ... And at Crowing Glory it will be the swan song of a man that will forever be remembered as the nagging thorn this company could just never get rid of. I will drag him kicking and screaming through hellfire and brimstone as I once again feel his warm blood seep between my fingers. Only this time .. I will finish what I started!" Still holding his arm straight Sharpman brings it around and holds it towards the side. His stare has become the most threatening we have ever seen it as he looks towards the camera, all signs of joy have vanished. Joesph Sharpman: "This time, I will paint the world red ... This time I will finally be the man to end the travesty that has become D.C. and I will do it, inside my playground ... Because August thirtieth there will be Hell in a Cell!" With this his smile returns, and an uncontrollable laughter bursts out from his lungs leaving the final thing we see a frightening scene of a man standing in a pool of his own blood. Laughing with a malicious intent ... All inside the most dangerous structure this industry has to offer. FIN?
