The build-up; A Grand Mystique RP

Aug 9 2009

The Grand Mystique

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GM Damien Thorpe Samuel Steel Chloe Cornette It is glorious summers’ day with a kind of natural warmth that guarantees a coating of sweat like a second skin. Most people would, if they could help it, find a way of spending some time in the outdoors to enjoy the sultry weather. As we begin however, we see the gentle ripples of an indoor swimming pool. The chlorine-tempered water laps at the tiled edges and drips into the plastic drains. The large pool appears to be empty until a hulking shadow rises out. The smooth motion that propels it almost silently pierces the surface with athletic precision, determination and rhythm. The man shows as he nears the edge. He allows his spent energy to push himself closer. His arms grip the edge and his muscular arms waste no time in dragging his drenched body out of the water. It’s soon clear that only a loose dripping speedo is protecting his modesty. He pulls down a clean white towel that was hanging on a heated rail and drapes it unceremoniously over his head. He rubs his face and lets it fall down to his chest. He carelessly allows the towel to fall to his midriff. Without paying too much care and attention, he dabs himself up and down until he is tolerably dry. He then throws the towel down back onto the rail and picks up another fresh one. He pulls this one around his waist. He pushes through a pair of pine double doors to a room that is filled with state of the art exercise equipment. He rubs his wet brown locks dry as he walks behind a screen that blocks off part of the room. When he emerges a minute later, he is still topless but he is now wearing royal blue knee length short and clean white trainers. He wipes the towel across his abs one last time before draping it over the screen. His re-emergence shows the Grand Mystique to the camera in full for the first time. “Every day for the last fortnight.....fifty lengths.” He points off screen to the right. “And five miles.” He taps the tread mill that he is standing next to. “But it is now simply not enough. I need to go even further, even faster.” With vibrant energy, he strides over to another piece of equipment, a cycling machine. “I mean, I feel like I just started. And if Crowning Glory was tomorrow, it would be perfect. For me anyway....maybe not Sinc Mercier.” GM flicks a pair of switches and precisely turns a dial before he hops on and begins to pedal relentlessly. “But nevertheless, the sun has risen on another day – Crowning Glory is still sometime away and the road, my journey to the single most important and significant match of my career has another twist before it delivers me to my destination. The destiny which will allow me to take over the MWE.” GM has now sat back in the seat; his palms ride his thighs as they drive powerfully up and down. “But distractions have been cast aside and I can say that I have never been on focussed. Crowning Glory and Sinc’s title are going to be dealt with in their own time. At the moment, I cannot think past Mason Resoan.” A beep breaks the monotony of his concentrated verbose. GM continues to pedal but as he does, he pulls an earpiece out and slots it around his earlobe and presses a button to activate it. “What?!” An extremely aggressive and disparaging tone that would rid any unexpected guests. “GM....It’s Damien.” GM sighs and wipes his perspiring brow. “That today?” “Yeah. Saturday 10.30m.” “Shit!” GM hops off the bike, noticing that he had clocked up three of his planned five mile ride in what seems to be a couple of minutes. Pleasantly surprised, GM picks up the towel that he had earlier discarded and presses a green button on the wall. It buzzes briefly. GM quickly swipes the towel across himself. He then picks up a white polo neck T-shirt. He slides his arms through and just as he pulls the neck over, he is greeted by the surprise look of two men in smart suits, agawp as they have just walked in on something they shouldn’t have. “Jeez Damien, what the hell is up?” He pulls his head through and straightens himself out before taking a long gulp of water from a plastic bottle. He smirks at Damien, his former colleague in EW and pretty much ignores the bulkily built man standing alongside him. “Sorry....erm, I guess haven’t met up in a long time.” “I don’t get it – mask off.” GM pulls the shirt over his head. “Mask on.” “OK, OK. Hilarious.” Damien plays along a little to change the subject. “GM, I want to introduce to you to MWE’s newest employee – this is Samuel Steele.” The ebony to Damien’s ivory offers a wide enthusiastic handshake and an enterprising grin. GM looks at him sarcastically and judges him with instant disdain. “Listen brotha.” Samuel raises an eyebrow but smartly decides against interrupting. “I don’t like interviews – this wasn’t my idea. I can’t do simple Q & A sessions anymore – I am not that simple or dense.” Even Damien seems taken aback by this. Sure, GM had established himself as a main event calibre performer but since when had he become this egotistical? “I know this wasn’t your idea.” “It never is.” “But Mr Covell and Mr Silver gave us strict instructions. They also confirmed to us that this interview is part of the deal to have you main event Crowning Glory, so you are going to give a great interview about a great superstar like Mason.” GM couldn't quite believe the audacity and blatant bravado of Samuel Steele yet part of him could appreciate the attitude and desire to make an impact and become someone significant. He points at Damien and focuses a withering glance of condescension on the pair. “Consider this a privilege.....You are talking to the next MWE World Champion. I wouldn’t even consider doing this by choice.....and I certainly can’t say spending my time with a former resident of No Fixed Abode is any of my to-do lists.” GM takes another long gulp. Samuel is careful not to react to any of GM’s comments. He initially expected this kind of reaction from some. He takes a deep breath to renew his patience and is about to speak when Damien interrupts the awkward silence. “We will cooperate with you if you play ball with us.” “Sure man, the ball is in your court.” GM rubs his palm across his stubbly chin. “OK......this is what we are going to do.” *** This is the first entry in the brand new Samuel Steele Shoots...... series! Every month, this former wrestler – turned interviewer will dig deep in the lives of our favourite superstars. And the first one is a cracking first entry to the series. This man is a former champion in three other federations, on top of his many other awards and accolades. He has feuded with DC, Maxxamilion Mustafa and Windy Bridges and now he has his sights set on Sinc Mercier and his treasured MWE World Heavyweight Championship. At Crowning Glory, Sinc will put his prized title on the line against the Grand Mystique. I caught up with GM in the midst of his intensive training regime in a state of the art facility in a facility just outside of Miami, Florida. We discussed many things but top of his agenda was his upcoming match with the best friend of his Crowning Glory opponent. There is no doubt that by speaking with GM about Mason, that he has real respect for the man who is a former World and Tag champ as well as a multi time champ of the belt he currently holds, the US title. “I remember hearing about this guy back in EW and there was nothing but constant positives; young, great attitude, talented.” Big words indeed and he was effusive enough to continue. “This is a guy who had the ability to get it done in and out of the ring, against anyone. This match is something I have been looking forward for some time as I knew we would have to clash sooner rather than later.” I couldn’t resist calling GM out on his use of the past tense. He is too smart to use it without it out of context. “There is no doubt that Mason has had an incredible ambition to strive for perfection, respect and the money and power that comes hand in hand with it. But he has over reached and became desperate in the process. This has hurt him, distracted him from realistic goals and he is in danger of losing everything that he worked so hard for. He is now more like just any other name on the roster. Flawed. Damaged.” I can’t quite decide whether GM is right on this or not. Sure, Mason is enduring a losing streak of quite legendary proportions, a curse that is placing a clear burden on his personal and professional life. But Resoan is a determined competitor, carved in the image of his former friend Aiden Copane. He is a classy in ring performer who will never voluntarily quit. I wondered if GM would see this. “I see Mason as an imperfect diamond. Cut so close to perfection yet improbably flawed. He is in position to ruin his own legend in record time. That is some achievement.” It seemed to me that GM was condemning Mason based on recent poor luck and if he was dismissed in the same way then he would be pretty annoyed. He seemed to see Mason as simply an insignificant road block on his way to Crowning Glory, rather than a serious opponent who would need total focus. I believe Mason will soon benefit from the little bit of luck that he needs to turn this losing streak into an impressive run of victories. GM didn’t seem so convinced. “Resoan is like a faithful puppy that has been sent away, to be shot in the back of the head by his master.” And he continued. “If this was a United States title match, people would be expecting to me to be victorious. There would be little doubt. I mean, what good is a champion who cannot draw and loses his non title matches?” “That belt deserves to be worn and defended regularly by someone who is fit to honour the prestigious history of that championship.” It is very clear that speaking to him face to face, that GM feels that he is in the right position to say this as a former US champ himself. “Not only does Mason not have the skill to be a great champion, he tries too hard to give the fans what they want. Sinc does this too and it really makes me sick.” It was at this time that GM’s attitude really began to noticeably change. In his eyes a distinctive fire began to bubble and his tone was intense. “Mason Resoan is now desperate. He craves the love and respect of the fans. He is willing to graft, cry and shed blood for the fans. He wants the fans to invest in his emotional journey and reap the rewards with him. But the fans will never demonstrate unconditional love and adulation for him in the same way that they have done for Sinc or Whitman.” “It is almost sad to say that he has become a Mickey Mouse shadow of his former self. He is not perfect, he never was. He creates no interest or controversy. Mason Resoan is second rate.” But he doesn’t seem to be sad to me. There is no remorse or disappointment in his voice. There is no doubt to me that GM is looking forward to his match with Mason. At another time, this could be easily headline a MWE pay-per-view. Whilst he is focussed on Sinc and most importantly, the MWE World title in his possession, he will not let Mason take advantage of this. “If Mason believes on any level that I am not going to be fully prepared then he really is very stupid. I know this is practically a dream match. Regardless of Mason’s current fortunes in the MWE, whether you think he is in a rut or selling himself short and suffering for it, he is still a wrestler that people want to see and that this is a match that will draw, create interest and get people talking.” I had to think twice about what he said next as he said in a very quiet voice. The Grand Mystique at his most menacing. “This will be the match where people will begin to care for Mason Resoan once more.” I tried to raise the tone by talking of his upcoming match at Crowning Glory. This is a man who has had criminally few championship opportunities in his career; underrated for so long, now lauded for his work ethic, demand for quality and his insistence that standards are raised and surpassed. I asked him another important question – Is the Grand Mystique ready to try and become the MWE World Heavyweight champion? “Beating Mason would be the ideal way to continue my preparations. They are so similar in ring that I am essentially scouting my opponent. I don’t want to comment on their out-of-ring relationship. And I won’t talk about Mercier for the moment so I can think about Mayhem and Resoan.” GM ended the interview pretty abruptly just after this, but not without some final, determined words from the Number One Contender. “No matter what match I go into, I never doubt my ability to win. I am at the point in my career where I can go toe to toe with anyone...a veteran, a motormouth.....I can make any match worth watching.” “The man who was impressed by your legend in EW is dead and buried, and this version, fresh, confident, is better than you can ever comprehend. I have grown beyond the constraints and absolute success has followed.” “Be ready to fall victim to me.” *** GM gets back on the exercise bike and gets back into a comfortable rhythm. He allows himself to drift off, focussing on the events of the past months and what could happen in that period of the time in the future. He dreams of what could be. The roar of the crowd. The ring of the bell. Three slaps of skin to canvas. And the smooth feel of the brass ring. He appreciates how it has felt in the past to be acknowledged as the top of a group of wrestlers. But this opportunity to be the single best manages to set his pulse racing. He was excited and it confused him a little at first. But this was a good excited. He could feel it. He was salivating at the prospect. He could feel the endorphins being emphatically pumped around and flooding his bloodstream. ` He opens his eyes to find his legs beneath him pedalling faster than he can believe. They feel like jelly, practically disconnected from his hips. Unconsciously they begin to slow down. His leg muscles begin to shake a little, causing his feet to shake on the pedals. He sits up and directly in front of him is Chloe Cornette. GM was relieved to see she is very much real in form. Given recent events, he needed to have his mind the right place. Crowning Glory was going to be mentally exhausting but he needed to be in the right frame of mind for the Monday Night Mayhem on the way there. He knew the show would be tough and that a win would be damn near essential if him to feel good about his prospects at CG. He knew that he was thinking too much and had this stunning woman in front. A woman who he hadn’t seen (in real life) for months. He then realised that if he didn’t talk soon, then she may as well slap a dunce hat on his sweaty ass. Holy Christ, is it possible that she got even more attractive. “Hi” *long pause* “Hi” GM gets off the bike and tries to hide his surprise and discomfort beneath a towel to hide his sweaty mug. “How are you?” “Great, thanks.” GM’s mind races with ways to break this tense conversation yet all seem unnecessary. “You have not caught me at the best time.” “It’s OK. I understand, I know you are a man in demand.” She seems similarly shy and in the cutest gesture possible, she pushes a stray lock of hair off her cheek. "I probably should have called." God damn, get a grip and grow a set. GM props the towel over his right shoulder and it falls down to his perspiring pec. He closes his eyes, takes a deep breath and walks up to her with a revised confidence and a fiery look of burning desire in his eyes. “I think we need to talk” She leans in towards him ever so slightly. “Where do you want to go?” *FADE* Within a second, the camera shows a dark room. A large bed is outlined by a stream of silvery moonlight. On it a white quilt is messily tangled amongst a pair of legs. We see GM’s slightly hairy and muscular torso lying flat next to Chloe’s curvy top half. She is curled up, with her legs sprawled out next to his. He seems to be uncomfortable and directly opposite her serenely sleeping shape, he is a tortured yin to her peaceful yang. He turns away and swings himself out of bed. He looks back at her for a second.......Did he just......Yes he did. He most certainly was not dreaming. Not tonight. GM gets up and walks to the basin in the middle of the small bathroom. He flicks the cold tap and puts his hands beneath the onrushing water. He allows to run it ice cold before pooling it in this hands and flushing it over his face. He does this twice, taking time to rinse his face and then his hair. His sodden black locks fall down to his neck and he stands there as the water drips slowly down his back and on the tiled flooring. He looks into the oval mirror in front and is concerned at the pale face that returns his gaze. At this time, weeks removed from the single biggest event in his career, he does not expect to look and feel the way he does. The pit of his stomach feels like it has collapsed beneath him, giving him a tidal wave of butterfly –like nerves in his gut. He tries to put it past him, down to poor food earlier in the evening. No matter how crazy it might sound, anything is better than the prospect of what may be the reality. And then he hears a hollow knock. Instinctively he turns to the door and then realises how foolish it would be for him to be interrupted at this time. Yet he feels compelled, almost dragged to the door as if guided by a spirit. He turns his head away, looking at the angel in his bed. A thing of outstanding beauty. He wants to hold her, protect her from him and the foreboding prospects that shadow his very being. At this moment, MNM seems to be a distant prospect. He places his hand on the cold bronze plated door handle and twists then pulls it. There is nothing there, nothing but fresh air and the cold artificial light of the corridor. Left a little embarrassed by his negativity, he quietly pushes the door shut and turns to head for bed again. Then he sees him. It. He can’t be real but the very image that his subconscious has chosen to show before him this time scares him. He remembers “seeing” Chloe a few weeks back and it did not faze him in the slightest. This was a new development. Something that would have an unknown purpose and relevant direction. But for the shock of the moment, GM cannot see this direction. Standing before him is the image of this man.
