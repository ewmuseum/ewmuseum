Refocused and ready to go!; A James Preston Rp

Aug 20 2010

James Preston

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(OOC= i have really been strugling with personal shit as of late i know this is far from my best and could of done with being much longer, but its an effort i just hope some of you will enjoy reading it.) Every now and then in life we lose our direction, we head down paths we don’t want to go down and we don’t even notice we are doing it until were already half way down that road! For James Preston it is one of them moments. He is well and truly on the road to consistent failure, he has been relying too much on his belief that he is perfect and has completely forgotten all the hard work that is required to achieve perfection in the first place, all of the hours of constant work out in the gym, the hours of in ring training he endured on a daily basis to get him to that level of perfection. He stopped putting in the work and the outcome was only ever going to be a negative one. You can’t compete at the highest level in any physical industry unless you put in the hard work and James Preston had stopped doing that and by the time he realised he was already on the road to career suicide. Having fully acknowledged this problem James has thrown himself into an intensive training schedule. It starts at Six Am every morning with a five mile run. After a healthy breakfast and a twenty minute rest to let his food settle he embarks on a two hour intensive work out in the gym. Weights machines, treadmill, cross trainer the whole lot. After an hours lunch break he would then embark on a three hour in ring sparring session with the best local semi-pro he could find, Quentin ‘Razor’ Sharpe, a young rookie who has been setting the indie circuit alight since his debut six months ago. Then it’s time for a quick protein only meal, a quick rest then he finishes the day off with yet another five mile run. It’s certainly a very physically demanding routine, but Preston knows it is one hundred per cent necessary if he is to once again become the embodiment of perfection. Its four pm on Friday the twentieth of August two-thousand-ten. We join James Preston in a local wrestling gym. He has just finished his daily training with Quentin Sharpe, Sharp dressed in black wrestling tights with barb wire patterning wrapping round the leg on each side, Preston is in a pair of black wrestling trunks and a black t-shirt with the word Perfection written on the front in bold white letters. Both men are at opposite corners of the ring, both clearly exhausted from the intense training session. They both make their way to the centre of the ring where they embrace in a manly hug as a show of respect for each other’s efforts, after the embrace Preston turns and goes to walk away. **Sharpe** Hey man before you go can I have a quick word. Preston turns back, He is clearly shattered but over the course of the week he has grown very fond of young Quentin, least he can do is give him a minute or two to talk. **Preston** Sure kid, shoot. **Sharpe** Well you know the last couple of weeks have been by far the best experience I’ve had in a wrestling ring. You’re freakin insane man, you’re a freak of nature, and you’re like the mother fuckin Duracell bunny. It’s been a pleasure sharing a ring with you man. Preston’s not use to people being nice to him, albeit usually he has done something to make people not like him. He has been so use to bad mouthing everyone for years, never letting his guard down so to speak. The last couple of weeks he has been way too focused on getting himself in perfect shape for his big match at Crowning Glory that he didn’t have time to put the wall up. He has grown close to this kid, he has massive respect for the kid too, not many can endure three hour training sessions every day and whilst he has struggled at times Quentin has managed to keep that pace. **Preston** Likewise kid, you’ve got a lot of heart and I like that about you. No matter what I have thrown at you, ya just keep coming back for more. I got a lot of respect for you kid and truthfully I think you’re going to be a star in this business in the future. **Sharpe** Thanks man, coming from a guy who from my stand point will someday somewhere will be a world champion that means a lot to me. **Preston** Hey look kid, are you doing anything right now, because I need to shoot a promo for my big match up this weekend, think you could spare me a few minutes to film it for me. Sharpe agrees and now a few minutes later is stood in the ring pointing the camera at Preston who is stood in the centre of the ring, a towel draped over his shoulder and beads of sweat still running down his forehead from the intense work out. Sharpe gives Preston the thumbs up to let him know the camera is rolling. I would like to welcome the entire MWE Universe to the place I have been calling home for the last few weeks. (The camera pans around the gym) See for the first time ever I had lost my way in life. I was relying on my own belief that I was good enough to not have to put in the hard work to stay at the top of my game. I thought ide done all that and I was very wrong, I had to go back to my roots I had to once again put myself through the intense, gruelling work outs that got me so far in the business to begin with. The last four months of my career have been absolute hell for me and now I have realised what I must do to rectify my mistakes that is all going to change and it’s all going to begin this Sunday at the granddaddy of them all Crowning Glory, an event where legends are born, a night that can make or break your career and if I have anything to do with it I will be leaving Crowning Glory a changed man, I will be leaving with a huge W in the win loss column and my opponent Alexis Black will forever have to deal with the fact that he once again came up short against me! With a huge raised smirk Preston arrogantly and confidently stares down the camera lens, sending out the message that he is more than ready for the task at hand. His whole body language is that of a man who knows he is at the peak of his own abilities. He steady’s himself and begins talking once more. Alexis ever since my return to MWE you have been a thorn in my side. You have been trying to provoke me since day one of the rebirth; you have got yourself one hundred per cent convinced that I am the missing piece of the jigsaw puzzle that will elevate you to super stardom. You even went to the extreme lengths of kidnaping my long-time friend and mentor Chris England just so I would agree to face you. I will admit kid you got ball’s, you’ve seen something that will elevate you to fame and you have gone after it with all you’re might. Unfortunately for you it’s not going to be your Crowning Glory, it’s going to be mine. Preston stares down the camera lens with a cold determined look in his eyes. He means business he has never looked as pumped up and serious in his entire life as he does tonight. Whatever the outcome this Sunday it is clear to see that James Preston is going to make this anything but easy for Alexis Black. I would like to thank you really, I mean if you hadn’t gone to all the trouble just to get this match up you might have actually stood a chance. See I was stagnating, I had completely lost my way and if it wasn’t for your efforts to cease upon the opportunity I might never have even realised it was happening! I had let the big league’s go completely to my head. I believed ide done all the painstaking hard work just getting here and now it was time to enjoy the moment. I couldn’t have been more wrong, see the one thing that has always set me apart from the rest is my pure determination and commitment to being the best, not only being the best but being bloody PERFECT at everything I do. For years I achieved perfection through years and years of hard work and now I am once more BACK at that level. There’s a huge sense of pride in Preston as he really does feel he has done everything humanly possible to get back to his best, He believes every word of what he is saying and that pure belief is definatly showing in his demeanour and manner in the ring, it’s as if a huge weight has suddenly been lifted from his shoulders now he is back in shape. I have trained long and hard for this match Alexis, I have put in superhuman like amounts of training, and I feel more ready for this match up than any I have ever went into. I mean look at me I’m in the best shape of my life, you’re not exactly the perfect physical specimen though are you Alexis. Do you really think a man of your build and stature has the stamina, will and determination to beat me in a falls count anywhere street fight? An environment where I can pretty much do anything to you I please and get away with it scot free. This Sunday I truly believe you’re in for the shock of your life, when that bell rings you’re going to realise you have bitten off waaaaaaay more than you can chew and by that point all you’re going to be able to do is try and defend yourself, try and hit me with everything you got and fail miserably. You don’t belong in a ring with me Alexis, you’re skills are good but they are no match for in ring excellence and don’t ever delude yourself that they are! Because I am THEEEEEEEEEEE IMMMMMMMMAGE OVVVVVVVV PERRRRRRRRRRFECTION and you can’t get better than perfection! Preston grins widely running his finger over the word perfection on his t-shirt. He is just radiating with confidence and belief, at this moment and time he is so confident in his own ability’s his ego is roughly the size of France!! This Sunday when the bell rings and the match begins I am going to summon up every memory of the sweat, adrenaline and pure will and determination I have expelled in this very gym and I am going to use it to spur me forward. Every time you think you’ve got me beaten I am going to dig down deep inside and remember the pain and torture I endured to be ready for this contest and I am going to break through the barriers. Alexis Black you better be prepared because this Sunday I am declaring war, I will not quit, I will not give in, I will never EVER go down without a full scale battle and if you’re not ready for that you will once again taste the bitter taste of defeat at the hands of The Image Of Perfection! Preston stands grinning widely down the camera for a few seconds before giving Sharpe the signal to hit the stop button. The two men shake hands as the scene comes to a close with Preston and Sharpe making their way to the changing room.
