Flying High; Za(c)K Crash RP

Oct 8 2009

Zack Crash

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The weather has an odd way of affecting us as humans. On those bright, sunny days we often feel energized and renewed. But conversely, those gray, gloomy days often suck the energy and spirit right out of us like the void of space. And today, was one such day. The clouds in the sky were such a deep shade of gray everything seemed stuck somewhere between day and night. A small rain sprinkled the landscape, giving off an even more depressing essence. Many say that when it rains, it is the angels crying in heaven. The gust of wind kept the temperature just cold enough to make anyone curse themselves if they forgot their hoody. Overall, it was just another lazy Sunday when you didn’t feel like doing much of anything. Which was why Kent was currently on his way to spend an ordinary afternoon with his friend Zack Crash. Although little did he know, the day would turn out to be anything but ordinary. Driving his beat up Chevrolet, Kent Bishop rode down the rain soaked highway toward Zack Crash’s apartment. He drove neither faster, nor slower than the speed limit. The radio was turned off and his attention focused on the road ahead. Kent had always been something of stickler for the rules, which might account for his stiff demeanor. He grumbled to himself as he watched a couple of cars pull around him on the highway. It always irritated him when other drivers did this. Kent: Geez people! I’m going the exact speed limit! Kent shouted to no one. Unlike Zack, and half of America for that matter, Kent prided himself on being a safe driver. There was no hurry. He wouldn’t get to Zack’s any faster whether he went 65 or 1000 mph. Well…maybe 1000 mph. Would get him there faster, but you get my point. Besides, people always underestimated how dangerous the roads were when they were wet. So, despite annoying cars honking their horns or giving him rude hand gestures, Kent bit his lip and ignored them. If they wanted to get in a wreck, fine, what did he care? He continued driving, using his normal route, for about another twenty minutes or so until he finally reached Zack’s apartment. The rain had turned from a sprinkle to a waterfall now, so Kent had to rush from his car to the entrance, trying to keep himself as dry as possible. By the time Kent had reached the entrance he was soaked. Pulling out his key (Zack gave all his friends spare keys to his apartment) and let himself in. Kent’s feet squeak under his steps, both from loose boards and from his wet feet, as he climbed to the third floor. Kent: God! When is Zack going to move out of this dump. The place really wasn’t a dump, but Kent knew Zack could afford better now with how much MWE paid him. But Zack was stubborn and refused to leave. It didn’t matter to Kent really. He guessed it was better this way because it meant a shorter drive for him. Reaching Zack’s apartment, #317, he knocked loudly on the door and called inside. Kent: Yo! Let me in stupid, I’m soaked to the bone. Kent called, using his favorite nickname for Zack. Nothing. Kent arched an eyebrow in confusion. It didn’t usually take Crash this long to answer his door. He tried knocking louder. Kent: HEY! You in there? Again…silence. Shrugging, Kent pulled out his key ring again, found the one for Zack’s door and stuck the key into the lock. He tried to turn the deadbolt, but it was slack. Already unlocked? That must’ve meant Zack was home. But if he was home, then why didn’t he answer? Slowly, Kent pushed open the door and poked his head inside. Kent: Hel- WHAT THE HELL?!?!?! The room was filled with a thick, yet somehow thin smoky haze that filled the room from wall to wall. Kent’s first instinct was that there was a fire, but then he realized he didn’t smell a fire. Instead, he smelled something else. Something…funky. It was a bad choice of description, but he didn’t know how else to describe it. Kent let himself in, shutting the door behind him as he felt a rising knot grow in his stomach. He had a feeling this couldn’t be good. Almost as if on cue, he heard a loud voice to his left, so loud in made him jump back a few steps. Zack: TEN DOLLARS! I SAID I BID TEN DOLLARS! DAMMIT, LISTEN TO ME!!! And there he was. Zack Crash, sitting on the sofa in his regular clothes, apparently shouting at the TV with all his strength. After Kent’s blood pressure went down, he turned to see what Zack could possibly be shouting at and saw that the TV wasn’t even turned on. He turned back to look at Zack, starring at the TV intently. Ooooooook, this was different. Kent: Uhhhh…what in the holy hell are you doing? Zack Crash turned to face Kent with a confused, almost dumb look on his face. For a moment it didn’t look like he even recognized Kent. Kent wasn’t even sure if Zack could see him through the thickness of the smoke. But soon he smiled broadly and spoke slow, slurred words. Zack: Ohhhh, hey man. I’m just watching one of those bidding channels. I’m trying to buy these awesome kitchen knives, but they just won’t listen to me. It’s very rude. Kent turned to look at the TV, just to make absolutely, doubly sure it was in fact turned off. And indeed it was. Kent didn’t know what to make of this. This was odd, even for Zack’s standards. Kent: Are you ok man? Zack tried to answer, but his response was muffled out in a random, almost frightening spit of furious laughter. Zack: HA HA HA HA!!! You said “man!” Kent: So? What’s so funny about that? Zack: It’s the –laughter– way you said it! It was so –more laughter– serious! Now Kent KNEW something was wrong. Zack was acting weirder than usual. Looking around at the fog inside the apartment, he thought he knew what had to do with it. Kent: Alright, what’s wrong? And what’s the deal with this smoke? Zack stood up from his chair, stumbling a bit, holding up a hand to gesture Kent to follow him. Zack: Wait till you see it. You’re gonna think it’s great. If by great you mean totally the opposite of great, then yes, Kent would agree. Zack lead him into the back room, where the smoke was the thickest, and the funkiest. Zack: Wayne gave me some of this sweet incense he made. He told me it would relax me, and man was he right. I almost don’t feel anything. I just feel…so…mellow. Kent stared down in open mouth horror at what he was seeing. Sitting in what was an incense burner was most certainly NOT incense. What should have been incense was wrapped up in a think white piece of paper, while out of the end fell tiny bits of black ash. Kent may not have been a user, but he recognized a joint when he saw one. How could Zack be so stupid? Kent: THIS ISN’T INCENSE YOU DUMBASS! IT’S A BLUNT! YOU’VE BEEN FILLING YOUR APARTMEN WITH– Zack doesn’t look like he’s heard a thing. He just smiles dimly and walks away, looking almost as if he is floating. Kent bites down on his teeth so hard he can almost hear them cracking under the strain. Kent: I swear to God, next time I see Wayne I’m gonna fucking kill him! He says through gritted teeth. Kent looks to see where Crash as run off, afraid that he might do something dumb (well, dumber) in his current stupor. Around the corner, Crash starts to climb the kitchen table and looking as if he’s…oh no!. Kent rushes down the hall, arms failing to stop him. Kent: NO NO NO NO NO NO! What are you doing? Zack: Watch this man. I’m going to do a Shooting Star Press off the table. Kent: You can’t do that! Zack: Sure I can. I do it in the ring all the time. Kent looks from the table to the ceiling, not sure there was enough room for Zack to even do the move in such an enclosed space. Kent: Yeah, but not in your apartment. And usually when you do you have someone… Kent stops mid-sentence as Zack grins down at him, bending his legs, preparing to jump. With grim clarity, the truth of what Zack was thinking hits him like a…well…a human body. Kent: Don’t you even think about it! Don’t you – But Crash did think about it, and he did do it. Almost. In Zack’s inebriated state, all he could manage was a clumsy leap off the table and to the floor. Luckily he wasn’t hurt. Because Kent was there to break his fall. As he lay on top of Kent, Zack started yet another fit of annoying giggles. Right now, Kent was really hating Zack, Wayne…and gravity. Zack: Dude, that was awesome! Kent: Yeah, it’s a laugh a frickin’ minute. Now how about getting off? Zack lazily rolls to the side, allowing Kent to get to his feet and check himself over. Nothing appeared broken. Thank God. Kent creaked his back to help with the pain shooting up his spine though as Zack just lay on his back, starring up at the roof above him. Zack: Dude, I just had an interesting thought. Kent: You thinking? It’s a Christmas miracle. Zack: What if, like, this isn’t reality. Like in The Matrix or something. Like maybe, we’re all living in a giant…snow-globe. And that’s why it’s always snowing. Kent turned to look outside. It was still raining. Though the weather had started to cool, it was still far away from snow weather. Kent: What snow? Zack reached up to grab at something invisible in the air in front of him. Zack: Right here. In the apartment. Kent sighed, exasperated. Insanity. That was the only way to describe it. Complete and total insanity. Kent: Look man, are you– Zack: AHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHH!!! Kent jumped back several feet, his heart pounding hard like a drum. Zack had sat up straight in a flash and just let out the most blood-curdling scream you’d ever heard. Then, he looked up at Kent as if nothing had happened. Zack: I’m getting hungry. Zack stated randomly, and then sat up to walk into the kitchen. Kent, if he were not close to losing his mind, might have found the whole thing funny. After all, someone high was getting the “munchies.” Who’d have thunk it? Besides, Kent was getting hungry himself. He followed Zack into the kitchen to find him dipping layers of an onion in…peanut butter? Then…eating it? Suddenly, Kent didn’t feel so hungry anymore. Zack: Ths iz vury gud. Zack grumbled with a mouth full of food. The smell of onion, mixed with the smell of the smoke was not a pleasant combination to the senses. Kent: Dude, that’s disgusting! Before Zack could respond, the phone rang. But instead of answering it, Zack dived behind a chair, hunching low to the floor. Kent threw up his arms, needing to externalize his exasperation. He didn’t know how much more of this he could take. Kent: I’m afraid to ask, but what are you doing now? Zack threw up a finger to his lips and hushed Kent with a “shhhhh,” giggling while he spoke. Zack: I’m playing hide and seek. Kent: With the phone? Zack began laughing louder, covering his mouth in fear of revealing his hiding spot. Soon the phone finally stopped ringing. But Zack did not emerge from his hiding. And then, when Kent should have felt stretched to his limits, he suddenly felt calm. Very calm in fact. His body felt weightless, like the smoke around him, and his mind is blank. He even snickered a bit at the sight of Crash hiding behind a chair. And with sharp realization, Kent understands what’s happening to him. Kent: Oh fuck! 1 hour later… Kent: Dude, this stuff really does mellow you out. Kent and Zack both lay on the couch now, their backs low against the cushions, an empty box of Twinkies tossed randomly to the floor. After breathing in the smoke for so long, Kent had finally succumbed to its wonders. In fact, he seems to have succumbed to it a lot quicker than it took Crash too. And it’s funny to see the straight-laced Kent break out a little. He sits on the couch, his shirt unbuttoned down to his chest, his belt undone, and his normally scolding eyes falling in and out of focus. Even his normally stiff tone of a voice is quiet and calm. Perhaps this is the real Kent Bishop we never see. Or perhaps it’s only a side effect of the smoke. Who knows? Zack: I know. Kent: It’s like nothing matters anymore. I feel so…free. Zack turns to face Kent with dizzy eyes. Zack: Did you just say something? Kent lifts an eyebrow, as if he isn’t sure himself. Kent: I don’t know, did I? I can’t remember. For a while, the two of them just sit in mellow silence. Every now and then, one of them would make a grunting noise, or try to start a conversation, only to lose the words. Finally, after about fifteen minutes of silence, Kent spoke up with a thought that had just passed through his head. Kent: Hey dude? Zack: Uh huh? Kent: Can I ask you something? Zack: Sure. Kent: What’s the deal with that Oblivion guy? Why did he attack you? In an instant, the once calm, relaxed feeling is sucked away, leaving behind only a grim tension as cold as frost. Even the high that Zack is feeling right now isn’t enough to make him forget about that. Nor is it enough to make him spill his guts. It’s a history lesson Zack does not particularly want to get into. Not ever if he can help it. So he turns away from Kent, not trusting his expression to hide his secret. Zack: I don’t know. The lie is obvious. Even if Kent can’t see his face, he can tell just from his voice that Zack is not being truthful. Kent frowned and leaned in closer. Zack, however, keeps his back to him. Kent: Come on. We’re friends aren’t we? Maybe I can help somehow. For a brief moment, Zack considers it. It’s been such a burden this past week for him to carry that he considers telling Kent simply in the hope that it might help lighten his load. But Zack soon realizes that is only wishful thinking. The gesture is nice, but Kent can’t help even if he wants too. He’d worked far too long to force that terrible memory into the lowest recesses of his mind, and he wasn’t about to bring it back so soon. Doing so would make it real again. Zack knew on some level that the truth was inevitably going to come out, but he planned on avoiding it as long as possible. Zack: I honestly don’t know what he’s talking about. Zack lied again. Kent only sighed, sinking back deeper into the chair. Kent: Whatever dude. But if you change your mind, I’ll still be willing to help and listen. Within minutes, the smoke had overtaken Kent and he was soon fast asleep, snoring loudly. Zack looks down at Kent, appreciating his words and offer for help, but Zack knew this was something he’d have to face alone. It was his sin to bear. And he knew Oblivion would be the one to pass punishment on him. But the thing he was afraid of most, more so than Oblivion’s dark vengeance, was how his friends might react. That he might lose them. Because, once they found out what he did, he wasn’t so sure they’d be on his side anymore. Zack wouldn’t be. A few hours pass as the scene slowly dissolves and changes. We find Zack leaning against the wall of his apartment, the smoke much thinner now than earlier, and Kent’s snoring still playing in the background. Judging from the bloodshot of his eyes and the way he carries himself we can assume the effects of the smoke are finally starting to die away. Zack looks a little worst for wear, but he knows now what he has to do. Though his head is splitting, there’s something that needs to be said. Zack: First off, let me state that for those of you expecting me to talk at all about the Oblivion thing, well I’m sorry to tell you you’re going to be sorely disappointed. I’m not going to cry about how he may have cheapened my victory over GM, I’m not going to bitch and complain about his assault on me, and I’m not going to even acknowledge Oblivion at all. I’m going to deal with this problem like I’ve dealt with it in the past…by just walking away. Call it cowardly if you want, but I honestly think that’s the best way to deal with this situation. Because no good can come for this. It’ll just end badly like last time. Sorry if you don’t like it, but that’s all I have to say about that. Zack is just avoiding the truth again. It’s as clear crystal. Zack face scrunches up in pain, and Zack rubs the palm of his hand against his forehead as he feels a deep burning inside his skull like fire. Whether this is from the intoxicating smoke or the bleak memories, we cannot say. Uhhhhhh, my head hurts. Honestly, I don’t know how anyone can stand doing this sorta thing day in and day out. But this week, I go up against a man who does this thing day in and day out, and not only does it but enjoys it too. How ironic. And if what I'm feeling is any indication, then he has to be one of the toughest SOBs in MWE. That's right boys and girls. This week, the Ultimate Thrill Seeker goes up against Mr. Ivory himself...Zak Warner. Zak Warner. A name that is well known to Crash, and not just because he happens to be friends with CSB. For anyone in this business, Zak Warner is a name that many fear. Simply put, Zak Warner is a legend. Countless victories, several accolades, and feuds that still exist in the memories of fans. He’s not just a legend within a few federations. He’s a legend in the business as a whole…period. Zack smiles at the camera, trying to lighten the mood a little. Speaking of irony, I feel like I’ve fallen into a parallel universe or something. I mean, two guys named Zack facing each other in a wrestling ring? That’s just madness I say…MADNESS! But in all seriousness, when I look at you Warner, I feel like we have a lot in common. We’re both two different kinds of junkies, looking for a high. Only you get yours by smoking while I get mine by jumping out of airplanes. You get a high figuratively, while I get mine literally. Now before I continue, let me assure you that, unlike everyone else, I’m not going to attack you for smoking weed for whatever it is you do. To be honest, I don’t care what you or anyone does with their free time. That ain’t none of my business. And for me to criticize someone for being a junkie would be kinda like the pot and the kettle. So you can dispel any thought that this will be the same old generic “Warner’s A Loser Junkie” promos that K-Hard (or as I call him, A-Hole) does, or anyone else for that matter. Zack is not a person given to the generic or the predictable. It’s boring as far as he’s concerned. That’s why he’s the Ultimate Thrill Seeker. He lives for the different, the exciting, the goofy, and the unpredictable. Being ordinary and dull was about as lame as they came. Zack looks into the camera, an impressed smile crossing his lips. What I am going to talk about, is your legacy. And what a legacy it is. In GWC, you were the first ever this, the first ever that, and the first ever something or other. You were the first person to do so many things there it’s literally mind-boggling. Not to mention you were GWC World Champ, two time US Champ, two time TV Champ, one time Tag Champ, and 2008 Hall Of Fame Inductee. Put simply, you were one of the top dogs in GWC. That’s a very impressive resume. You see that’s another reason why we’re very much alike. Not only do we get a thrill out of life, but I see you as a model of what I could become if I keep working as hard as I am. Zack looks at the camera, the smile and brightness he was just exuberating suddenly vanishes, replaced by something else. It could be due to him feeling the latter effects of the drugs wearing off, but that is unlikely. It’s most likely coming from the unexpected twist and edge hidden beneath his words of praise. But there’s another BIG difference between you and me. See, I want you to listen back to all your accomplishments I just mentioned, and tell me if you here a pattern. Anything? I make constant use of the word ‘were’…meaning past tense. Meaning all this happened a while ago. Now what have you done since then? Don’t answer that, cause I already know the answer. Nothing! Zip! Zilch! But instead of doing something to fix it, you do exactly what you’ve done since you’ve been here…nothing! And that’s what the main difference is. Not in our accomplishments, but more in our motivation. I have all the drive in the world, were you don’t seem to have any anymore. Crash lifts a hand to his head, a sharp pain coursing through his brain. He grips hard at his head, as if he might be able to catch the pain between his fingers. But the attempt is in vain. It’s the intensity of his words causing him such sudden pain, but Zack pushes through them. Because, to him, he feels these words might be the most important he’s ever spoken. And I just don’t get it. I mean, you used to be so great, but now you come here and it’s like you don’t care anymore. You’ve gone from being THE Zak Warner, to just being “that guy on the roster.” Just another mid-carder who is neither ignored nor the focus of anyone’s attention. And that’s what we call indifference. And I don’t know about you, but that’s the worst possible thing you could be in the wrestling world. A wrestler who people are indifferent to. Where did all your motivation go? Zack asks the question with the utmost seriousness. He honestly wants to know. Crash would be lying if he said he didn’t idealize Warner a bit and found it to be the mark of his career to be facing him. But something in the way Warner has remained stagnant bothers him. Crash removes the palm from his face, examines it for a moment before squeezing it tightly, releasing all the tension inside him will a single fist. Did it just vanish along with GWC? After all that success you had in GWC, are you just dried up? Are you fine with where your career is? Are you ok with just being a mid-carder the rest of your days here? All these questions and their possible answers irritate the hell out of me. It makes no sense that a person so talented could actually be fine with where you are. I mean, all you’ve done here is the IC Title. And where is that now? You pretty much wrestled that belt into retirement. Now I don’t mean to be disrespectful, and maybe I’m reading the whole thing wrong, but this is the vibe I’m getting from you bro, and it honestly worries me. Kent grunts in the background, releasing a giant snore. But the momentary distraction does nothing to deter Crash from his path. He is focused beyond all measure of the word. His environment no longer exist around him. Right now in his mind, there is only Zack Crash, and there is only Zak Warner. The rest of the world is empty. Well you might be fine with where you are, but I’m not. I’ve said it before, and I’ll say it as many times as I have too. Once I reach one height, I seek another to climb too. Because the reaching the top, will never be good enough for me. I’m currently the hottest thing in MWE today. Hotter than Derf, hotter than GM, hotter even than my good buddy CSB. And I’m not even the World Champion. I’m the man who is single handedly making the X-TV Championship the most sought after belt in MWE right now. Think about it. Which belt are the lower people asking for? The US Belt? Nobodies done anything with that since Sinc. The World Title? I beat the World Champ last week, so that’s all I need to say about that. And this is only the beginning. If you think I’m hot now, well, you ain’t seen nothing yet. Just watch me, and I’ll awe and amaze you with reckless abandon like you’ve never seen. I’ll show you moves some of you only WISH you could do. I plan on shattering that glass ceiling before me and raising to the main event. The main event. The ultimate feather in the cap of any pro-wrestler. It’s what they all strive for. More than championships and more the fanbase, what they desire is the spot at the top. The pedestal for which all others below you are measured. And Crash wants this spot like anyone else, although not for the same reasons as others. While most want this spot merely so they can stroke their own egos, Crash just wants to enjoy the view from the top of the mountain. And if I have to blast through you to get there, then so be it. If you’re just going to sit on your hands the rest of your career, then you don’t deserve the spot you have in this federation. Spots like the ones we have, true stardom, should only belong to those who have the motivation to achieve it. But I don’t want it to be like this Warner. I know you’re better than this. You’re better than training some MMA reject. I know you have a drive in you that exceed possibly any other superstar’s drive. We all saw it at Crowning Glory. And I want to see it this week on MNM. Crash moves away from the wall he’s been leaning on and begins pacing around the room. He walks toward a window, opening it to help filter out the rest of the smoke, which is still causing him a headache. The cold night air rushes in, and it feels good on Crash’s face as he gazes out at the star filled night. And he speaks to the night. When we walk into that ring this week, I want to see the side of you everyone from GWC talks about. That way, I can truly consider this the turning point of my career. If I can defeat a World Champ one week, then a GWC legend the next, then there’s nothing anyone can deny me here. I’ll shine brighter than anyone. So much so that even the stars will seem pale compared to me. And a 2 Out Of 3 Falls Match is the perfect match to find out exactly who is the better man. Crash turns again to face the camera, his face the image of focus and unshakeable determination. Say what you want about me. Chances are it won’t be any different from what anyone else says. Or maybe you’ll flaunt those GWC accomplishments I mentioned earlier. Well, while I did compliment you on them, let me also say that this is MWE and they don’t matter now. All that’s in the past. All that matters is who you are now. Those accomplishments may mean you have more experience than me, but they don’t mean your better than me. But go ahead, flaunt them anyway. Call me a junkie. Say my lifestyle will crash (oh, and that’s really clever by the way) around my ears one day. I don’t care. All I care about this week is seeing the old Zak Warner. The Zak Warner that actually mattered. Not this stale shadow of a former legend. Zack closes the window shut and the roar of the gale stops. Crash quickly walks over, placing a blanket over the still passed out Kent on the couch before turning and walking down the long hall. Crash stops at the door at the end of the hall, his hand holding firmly to the knob. He looks back, the elongated hall framing him oddly for a moment before he speaks one last time. Have any of you ever wondered the meaning behind my theme song? If you listen closely enough, you’ll hear the one lyric, “before I crash through you.” And that’s exactly what I’ll do this week, and ever week following. Zak Warner…this week…I will crash through you! And with that, Crash walks over the threshold and shuts the door with a reverberating finality, leaving us with nothing but the echoing sound of his words ringing in our ears.
