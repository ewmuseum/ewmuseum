The remaining moments of 2008 are a warning...; For the year to follow. Derf vs Milenko

Jan 2 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The scene opens in a room, white walls, fully furnished with a leather couch and chair, end tables next to each, a 42" Flatscreen TV covers a wall. On one wall, an open window reveals the outside, dozens of tall buildings, most with windows lit up. The building in the very middle of them all has a giant red digital clock on it, counting down the remaining minutes of 2008. 11:53:00 A man walks into the room, dressed in a very flashy blue and white suit with a bottle of "Prince Igor" vodka. The man, pouring himself a glass of the vodka, is Eric Derf. With the glass half full, he stops pouring, puts the cover back on the bottle, and sets it next to the freshly poured glass. 11:53:11 Ah, the new year approaches... And what a year it is going to be. Good things are going to happen for good people, evil will fall, but I'll get to that in a few moments. Right now, I feel I owe an explanation... As to my actions lately. An explanation... for this. 11:53:30 He reaches towards the glass of Vodka, but instead reaches past it, and picks up a much more familiar sight: The mask of The Mysterious G. He puts his hand in it and holds it to his face, looking into its eyes as if it were looking back at him. When I first heard about MWE from a friend, I immediately checked it out, and the first thing I saw were the people that had left my former company, Rev Pro, and let it die without them. Those men, now a huge part of MWE, had turned their backs to their former company, and left a small handful of men to pick up the scraps, myself being one of those men. With his other hand, he picks up the glass and takes a small drink, cringing his face at the taste of it, before continuing. 11:53:56 The second thing I saw were men that were kicked out of my other company, WZCW. Men that got in the faces of the wrong people, and were punished for their actions. Men that though they were the greatest thing since sliced bread. Men that though they were wrestling gods. With all the hate in these companys at the time, I didn't know how much of an impact I would have by going to what my other companys considered "The Enemy." I mean, I wasn't a spy or anything, I wasn't stabbing anyone in the back. I just wanted the work, the chance to face off against newer, fresher opponents. Something new. 11:54:20 Still holding the mask, just as before, as if he were talking right to it. But I also though about how much of an impact Eric Derf could make. I though of what people would think of me being here. An when I really though about it, the answer... was a painful "Not Much". I knew I wasn't a huge name, and for me to have joined at the time, I'd have been pushed aside and lost in the shuffle of all the other men, and I guess women, coming into the doors of MWE at the time... Enter, The Mysterious G. He sets down the glass of Vodka, runs his hands through his hair, and using both hands, carefully pulls the mask over his face. He then looks into the camera. It's Mysterious G all right. 11:54:48 There were alot of free agents at the time, alot of big men had just walked into MWE, and were instantly made famous. I wanted to be one of those men. And then I realized, I could make myself just as big as them. It was simple really. Just toss on a mask, any old wresting mask, and walk right into MWE without a care in the world. No one would know it was me, and they'd even think "Hey, maybe that's Johnny What's-his-name, former world champion of "Whatever-the-fuck wrestling" or something. I added the thing that would make people look at me. Mystery. 11:55:09 And I KNEW it would turn heads. People turned when I walked by, whispers of "Do YOU know who he is yet?" and "What a freak!" were heard everywhere I went. But I knew it was worth every second of it. I knew with every whisper, every head turned, that's one more person watching my every move. One more person that would be watching when I did remove my mask. One more person that would be exited to see Eric Derf. 11:55:29 So last week, Monday Night Mayhem. Richard Covell, being the only man to know my true identity, and therefor, my true skill, put me in a tournament for the vacated X-TV title. He knew I could handle it, so he put me in there with Joe Roberts, an MWE vet. I went to the ring, I beat him, and moved on. Simple. 11:55:43 And then, it came time to finally fight for a championship. I've fought for titles before, and lost. But I knew, this time was going to be different. It HAD to be different. Because if I lost, my identity would be let out, and I wasn't ready for that. Yet. Eric reaches behind his head, and removes the mask, carelessly tossing it across the room, as it lands on the floor with a soft smack on the ground. But now I am ready. 11:56:00 Before my match against Samuel Steele, I talked to a friend. A fellow MWE Superstar. We talked about politics. We talked about other wrestlers. We talked about Masks... And it made me realize something. I was in a great company, without the politics. With great superstars. Why wouldn't I want the world to know? Why should the world not know Eric Derf was the man entertaining them? Call me greedy, but I wanted the fame. I wanted the added respect. So I promised myself "I'm going to win this match. I'm going to win the title, and then I'm going to show the world exactly who just won that title." 11:56:35 He reaches for the glass of Vodka again, but once again, goes past it for something else. Something much larger then a glass, it's Championship Gold. The X-TV Title. He slaps it over his shoulder, holding onto it with his left hand, while his right hand finds its way into the pocket of his jacket. And I did just that. At the expense of Samuel Steele, I might add, but that's not my fault. Like The Mysterious G said, I respect the man for cleaning himself up, but he lost that respect by falling to old habits. Maybe he'll use this spare time he's got to clean himself up. I personally hope he does. 11:56:49 Eric turns around and looks at the giant red clock outside his window. He walks over to the window, and with his spare hand, opens it. A cool breeze can be heard blowing into the room, as a shiver can also be heard from Eric Derf as the cold air runs throughout the apartment. But another sound overpowers the breeze, and that's the sound of an audience. Much like the fans at MWE shows, these men and women were eagerly awaiting 2009 to arrive. The new year is almost here, so I've only got a few moments to discuss the reason I'm here right now. And that reason is... Milenko. 11:56:57 At Monday Night Mayhem, one night after winning the X-TV title... Wow, these days are confusing! New Years taking place inbetween shows that are one night after... Anyway, back on track... On night later, I get an opportunity to add more gold around my waist... If you can call it gold, that is. The Havoc Title, what's it even worth? 11:57:06 Anyway! I'm not trying to trash talk a title, because all titles have value, even that one. I'm here to talk about a man. Milenko. His first ever championship, he beat me for it, and I've never forgotten. He claims he's better then me, and that night, he proved it. And I hated myself for it. So I quit the company I was in, I was ashamed to show my face there after such an embarrassing loss. But I knew somehow, I knew deep down I'd be facing Milenko somewhere down the road. I just knew it. 11:57:31 So I trained, and I studied him. Not like my other opponents, I really studied Milenko, as he was the man that cost me the most. I've had title matches before, but not many people consider a Tag Title much of an accomplishment. Milenko robbed me of my first singles title, and I'll never forget that. So I studied. I learned all about his childhood, how he ran away from home to avoid his older brothers Alex and David. I learned about his issues with his drunken abusive father, that he felt unloved by his mother because he was a rape baby, I watched first hand as he and Alex ripped each other apart in WZCW, and every other place he followed Alex to. 11:57:57 He walks over to his glass of Vodka, and takes another small drink, once again cringing at the taste, as if he's never drank it before. I also noticed... You can't fight alone. Everywhere you go, you've got cronies. You got your buddies. In WZCW, you've got Dynasty, which was actually after two other guys, guys that were barely around long enough for anyone to remember their names. And then theres your new Halloween buddies, The Wraith and The Ringmaster. I mean, come on Milenko! You'd think all that abuse from your father would have toughened you up, made you able to fight on your own. You seem so good at making friends, how the hell are you so messed up?! Your Mr Popular! 11:58:20 And now, me and you have a match. You'll likely have your buddies there to watch your back. Don't worry. Worst case scenario, I've got my back covered too. But I'll not get to that quite yet. Because I'm guessing your watching this, thinking to yourself "Eric Derf will never beat me, he's not man enough" or something messed up. Eric walks over to the 42" TV and turns it on manually, then walks towards an end table that holds a DVD player, as well as other odds and ends. As he reaches for the DVD remote, the TV slowly gets brighter, showing an image already showing, it's of Milenko in a wrestling ring. A large "PAUSE" is in the corner of the screen. 11:58:34 This right here... Is a Milenko match. Most fans might not remember it, as it was taken at a House Show, and I doubt most fans follow house shows. But I personally like this match... You see, it took place not to long ago, on the night of Dec 3rd. And there is a reason I like this match. It's because... of who he faced that night... Eric aims the DVD remote at the TV, and presses a button causing the video to play. As we watch, Milenko is standing in the ring alone, arms raised as if he'd just knocked someone out of the ring, however being a home video, it's unclear as to who it is, being on the other side and all. As the top parts of The Wraith and The Ringmaster can be seen on the other side of the ring walking towards the fallen man, he suddenly jumps back into he ring and attacks Milenko. The man is The Mysterious G. The man is Eric Derf in a mask. Suddenly, the screen begins moving in fast forward. 11:59:11 I fought Milenko that night. He had no idea who I was, so I took full advantage, and went all out, using all the studying against him. I took every piece of anger, every piece of lust for the gold he took from me, and I took that man out.. Oh, here comes my favorite part. 11:59:23 He stops the fast forward a mear second before Milenko receives some Sweet Shin Music at the hands of The Mysterious G. The moment Milenko hits the ground, the video rewinds again, only to go back to normal just in time to watch Milenko get a 2nd helping of Sweet Shin Music. At that moment, the TV shuts off, revealing a blank black screen. Lights out. That's what you saw that night. And you had no idea. 11:59:35 And now, I know I can beat you. You know I can beat you. So what are you going to do about it? Because when I show up to fight you in 2009, I'm going to be a new man. To hell with my losing streaks in other companies, that's all in 2008. That's all in the past. 11:59:50 Suddenly, the countdown can be heard from all directions. Outside, through walls, everywhere. Eric ignores it as he's staring right into the camera, dead serious on getting his point across. No more 10 Mr Nice Guy 9 ... 8 No More 7 Friendly Eric 6 ... 5 2008 is 4 Almost over 3 ... 2 2009 is 1 My time. HAPPY NEW YEARS!!! ... This is MY time! Eric downs the last of the Vodka, once again making a cringing face, before walking off camera.
