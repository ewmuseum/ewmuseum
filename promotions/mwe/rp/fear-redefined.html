Fear Redefined; Za(c)k Crash RP

Oct 18 2009

Zack Crash

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

I know that the admins don't really like it when people use the * * * to transition, but I tried something different in this RP, and giving what I, I honestly couldn't think of any other way to transition. Plus, I've had problems transitioning anyway. So, whoever ends up grading this, I hope you'll forgive me for doing that. Anyway, on to the actual RP. Hope you enjoy. This is how it feels to be Zack Crash right now. Though the room is filled with light, you sit alone in the darkness. Back hunched, elbows on your knees as you gaze down at a large championship belt that is so important, words fail you to describe it. The apartment that once felt so safe, now feels utterly foreign. Unwanted memories creep into your consciousness and play across your vision. You shut your eyes tightly, trying to banish them from your mind. But you can’t. You never could. And you most likely never will. Despite your best efforts, those memories haunt and torment you like a phantom. You hear your heart pounding, and your breath is raspy. All these things and more add up to mean only one thing. You are afraid. You feel a fear as cold and unforgiving as fire. And it burns away at you until you are nothing but ash. But this fear is not the fear of pain, nor is it the fear of possible defeat. It is something much worse. This fear…is the fear of guilt. Guilt for a wrong you know is yours. And that fear has a face. A face that hides an even more horrible one behind a mask. The face of Oblivion. A name that rings both literal and metaphorical foreboding in your head. The prospect of your match with him looms over your head like the axe about to fall. And the most brutal revelation is that, somewhere deep inside you, you feel as if you deserve every ounce of punishment he has promised to give you. And you are afraid. Kent: Come on man! Let me in! Please? A voice calls out from a distant. The voice is familiar. A friend perhaps? A loud, echoing bang reverberates in your ears. It sounds close, yet far away. Kent Bishop is knocking at your door, but you do not answer it. You simply stay where you are. You choose to ignore the sound, and instead decide to sit there. With nothing more than the bitter memories, and the cold darkness as your companions. This is how it feels to be Zack Crash…right now. * * * * * * Kent: You can’t stay in there forever! Kent Bishop pleaded with Crash as he pounded louder at the door, drawing several eyes out from their own apartments. But Kent paid them no mind. He had far more important things to worry about. His friend Zack Crash had shut himself up in his apartment ever since the last MNM, ever since the announcement of his match with Oblivion at Turmoil came down. No one had seen or heard from him since. And Kent Bishop was starting to get worried. Kent: Dude seriously, let me in! Lets talk about this. Nothing. Kent tapped his foot irritably as he waited several minutes for Zack to come let him in. Kent: You know I have a key, so I can let myself in whenever I want. Kent informed him, hoping to coax Zack into letting him in of his own real will, although he did mean it. Everyone had agreed to leave Zack alone and let him come to terms at his own pace. But Kent thought differently. Zack needed to face this, and he needed to face it now. The time for running away had passed. Kent waited a few more seconds before shrugging. Kent: Fine! Have it your way. I’m coming in. Kent rooted in his pocket for his key ring before pulling it out and unlocking the deadbolt with a loud creek and letting himself in. The apartment was not as he expected. He thought he’d find the place in shambles. But it was as it always was. A sort of…ordered chaos. Not really messy, but not neat either. Everything was still in its designated place. But this only made Kent worry more. He wondered down the hall toward Zack’s room, and there he found him, sitting in the dark in a bedside chair, gazing down at the X-TV Belt as it lay on his bed sheets. He didn’t even look up as Kent entered the room. Kent: Hey. Kent greeted him. Zack slowly turned his head to finally look at Kent. Even in the darkness of the room, Kent could see the blankness in Zack’s face. Zack: Hey. Zack responded weakly, almost in a whisper. The two of them held their stares for a long while, neither one speaking. The room seemed to build up with unspoken tension until it was unbearable. Zack finally broke the silence, still speaking as softly as if death were coming tonight. Zack: You…want something to drink. Kent: Yeah, sure. Thanks. Zack rose to his feet, brushing past Kent and walking back toward the kitchen. He opened up the refrigerator and pulled out two bottled waters. He handed one to Kent and they both sat down at the kitchen table. They unscrewed their caps and began drinking. Neither of them was thirsty, but they just wanted something to do. Perhaps that would help get the conversation going. Kent took a few large gulps, draining his bottle halfway before setting it down, folding his hands, and looking at Zack very seriously. Kent: Look, I understand that this history you have with Oblivion is a touchy subject. And I know this must be hard for you. But I want to help. And if I’m going to do that, I need to you tell me why this bothers you so much. Zack took a small sip of water, as if looking for some excuse not to reply. Kent went on despite his friend’s silence. Kent: You shouldn’t keep this kind of stuff bottled up inside. It isn’t healthy. If you don’t let it out then– Zack: I don’t want to talk about it. Zack interrupted Kent in mid-sentence. He spoke quietly, but firmly. But Kent did not deter. Kent: I just don’t get why you won’t let anyone help you. I’m your friend. Just let me try to– Zack: I don’t want to talk about it. Zack repeated, louder and more forceful. But Kent continued on. Kent: Please? Just tell me what it is. If you don’t eventually you’ll just– Zack: I SAID I DON’T WANT TO TALK ABOUT IT!!! Zack rose to his feet so quickly he knocked over his chair. Kent followed suit, his temper matching Zack’s as the two friends shared a fiery stare down that would’ve rivaled any in MWE history. Kent: What is your problem? Zack: My problem?! What’s YOUR problem? You’re the one that won’t leave me alone! Kent: Only because you refuse to talk about it! Zack: Well let’s see. Hmmmm. Maybe that’s because I don’t want to talk about it! Haven’t you been listening to me? Kent: Yeah, I gathered that! But you NEED to talk about it, whether you want to or not! Zack but down his water bottle, throwing up a hand in a gesture that said, “I don’t need this shit” and began walking away back toward his room. But Kent bounded for him, grabbing Zack’s shoulder to keep him from going any further. Zack: Let go of me! Zack demanded. Kent: No! Not until we talk about this. Zack was biting down on his lower jaw so hard he could he his teeth cracking. But Kent was being so stubborn it was infuriating. With a strong push of the shake of the shoulder, Zack broke free of Kent’s grip and walk back to the safety of his room. Kent himself was feeling such rage he was half tempted to say, “fuck it” and leave. But then…he got an idea. Kent: Sorry. I didn’t know you were such a pussy. The comment had the desired effect. Zack looked from around the corner of his darkened room, a mixture of shock and anger on his face. Bingo! Zack: What did you just say? Kent smirked, deciding to milk this for all it was worth. He stalked forward, like a lion hunting its prey. Kent: You heard me. I said I didn’t know you were such a fucking pussy. Zack marched forward out of his room to meet Kent halfway down the hall. Together, they stood nose to nose, Zack glaring at Kent, looking damn near ready to slug him. But Kent stood his ground, smirking, inwardly proud of himself. Zack: I’d shut up if I were you! Kent: And I wouldn’t be running away from my problems like a scarred little girl if I were you. Zack: I said shut up! You don't know what you're talking about! Kent: Man, what a let down. I thought you were tougher than this. Zack grabbed Kent by the shirt collar, throwing him hard against the wall with a echoing thud. Zack: I AM TOUGH! Kent: Oh really? Then why are you so scarred of Oblivion? Zack: I’m not scarred of him! He’s nothing! I’m the Ultimate Thrill Seeker! I’m the brightest star in MWE today! I eat fear for breakfast! And compared to fear…Oblivion is nothing. Slowly, the smile on Kent’s face softened from arrogant to proud. Kent: Exactly. Zack looked back at Kent, confused for a moment as he cocked his head to the side. But then, as Kent started to chuckle lightly under his voice, Zack’s face widened in surprise. As realization hit Zack, his grip slackened and Kent fell calmly upright once more. Zack: You just played me didn’t you? You did that on purpose? Kent smiles at Zack. Kent: Wasn’t that hard. Zack gently lets go of Kent, who pats himself off. Zack simply stands still for a moment looking confused and awe struck at Kent’s boldness. Suddenly, he chuckles weakly to himself and finally, for the first time since Kent had arrived, smiles. Kent: So…we feeling better? Zack stops laughing; now breathing heavily. He only nods his confirmation. Kent: Then shall we try this again? Kent suggest as he gestures back towards the kitchen table. Again, Zack only nods a yes. Kent leads the way back into the kitchen and they both sit back down in the same chairs. Zack leans forward in his chair, back hunched and hands clasped on the table. Kent gives him a meaningful, allowing him a moment to gather himself. He knew how hard this must have been. October being Breast Cancer Awareness Month always made him think of his mother who died of breast cancer. But having to deal with also must have been nearly unbearable. Kent: Alright, now please, just tell me why this upsets you so much. What history do you and Oblivion share? Zack doesn’t answer right away. His hands clasp tighter around each other as the silence between the two grows heavier. Kent: Don’t rush, just take your time. Zack: So you’re a therapist now? Zack joked with a small laugh. Kent returned the smile, glad to see even a sliver of Zack’s old humor returning. Kent: Well I ain’t no Dr. Phil, but I’ll try. Zack: Dr. Phil isn’t a real therapist either. Kent: Ok, bad example. The two men share an all too quick laugh before the humor dies away and silence returns like a sickness. Zack looks down at the table, as if ashamed about what he’s about to confess. Zack: Back when I was in the indy circuit, me and Oblivion knew each other. Kent nodded, quietly urging Zack on. Zack: He didn’t go by Oblivion back then. He went by another ring name. Anyway, we used to train and wrestle together. Practice moves off of each other and stuff. But one day there was…an accident. Kent: An accident? The statement was more from shock than anything. Kent: That’s why he’s been harassing you? All because of some stupid accident from when you two were in the indy circuit together? Zack: You don’t know what you’re talking about. It wasn’t just some run of the mill accident. It was more…serious than that. And…there’s more. One other thing I did to him. Kent: Ok. You want to talk about that? Kent said kindly, looking curious again. But Zack said no more. His face was wrenched and tight, his hands clasp so tightly together they look liked they might meld into one. Kent: What about who he really is? Can you tell me about that? Zack shook his head. Kent sighed, but relinquished. Zack had talked at least some about what was bothering him, and that was a start. No sense in making him go too far if he didn’t want to. He could deal with the rest later. Kent: It’s ok. I guess we don’t need to talk about everything now. At least you made a start. And I bet you feel better now don’t ya? Kent stood up from his chair, ready to leave. Zack looked up and him smiling down on him, and in that moment, he could honestly say he’d never been more grateful for the man’s friendship. He tried to think of something profound to say to express his gratitude, but Zack had never been too good with words. Zack: Thanks. Was all he could think of. Short and simple, but he felt it was enough. Kent turned to leave, but Zack stood up to call him back for a moment. Kent: See, told ya you'd feel better. You should listen to me more often. Anyway, gotta get going. Zack: Hey, why don’t you stick around? I still need to shoot a promo. You wanna hang out and watch? Kent looked over his shoulder as he threw his jacket over his shoulder, his usual scowl returning to his face. Kent: Pass. Sounds pretty lame to me. Besides, I have better things to do. Call ya later stupid. Kent said has he walked out the door, closing it behind him. Zack laughed as he went. He knew better than to take what he’d just said seriously. It was just Kent’s way of saying ‘your welcome’ to Zack. Or maybe it was his way of putting things back to normal for them. Either way, Zack thought at that precise moment that he had the best friend anyone could ask for. Zack: Thanks buddy. Thanks. * * * * * * This is how it feels to be Zack Crash…redefined. You find yourself alone again in your apartment, but it no longer feels foreign to you. Everything, from the chair you now sit in, to the empty water bottle in your hand, all of it seems the same again. Refreshed. Renewed. And you feel like your old self again. Your mind is focused and set. You have things that need to be said, and they will be said. You look deeply into the camera and speak. Zack: We all make mistakes in our lives. No matter how hard we try to live the picture perfect little existence, we all screw up somewhere down the line. Some screw-ups are bigger or worse than others, but it’s all part of human life. It’s how we learn. We make mistakes, we learn from them, and then we move forward. But sometimes, these mistakes come back to bite you in the ass, and you can’t get away. They just keep hounding you, and hounding you, and they just won’t leave you alone. Now, there are two ways to deal with these kinds of problems. 1.) Is to simply ignore it and hope it goes away on its own, which usually it won’t. And 2.) Is to stand up against the problem, and fight it head on. To look it straight in the face and spit in it’s eye. Well this is the kind of problem I find myself in currently. I’m facing a past mistake that won’t leave me alone. And I went with option #1 for sometime now. I tried to ignore it and act like it never happened, and that may have been cowardly to some, but it was all I could do. But now that ain’t good enough anymore. Now, all that’s left for me is option #2. And personally, I like the sounds of option #2 better than #1. So I will stop running, and stop ignoring, and face a mistake from my past. You begin to pace around the apartment, gazing absently at your various furniture and other items as the camera follows your every move. You do it mainly to keep your mind busy. Because truthfully, you’re still a little scared. Though the fear no longer has its death grip on your throat, you can’t still feel it breathing down your spine, making the hairs on your arm stand up. So you move so as to keep your mind from wondering to darker places. The darkness will come soon enough. And you WILL face it, and you WILL defeat it. And that mistake from my past is Oblivion. You know Oblivion, I tried to keep this thing between us private. I really did. And I tried for a lot of reasons. It wasn’t really anyone’s business but ours; I didn’t want to drag the fans or the roster into this messy history, and all kinds of other reasons. But you just keep pushing, and pushing, and now it’s out there in the open for everyone to comment on. So go have yourself a nice victory jerk-off because we’re finally going to settle this once and for all just like you wanted. Little baby Oblivion finally got his way. You know, I find this whole situation with us laughable, I really do. Why? Because you act like you’ve been wronged, you act like I did you an injustice and as if the world is against you. Yet how do you prove yourself to be the…“victim” as you might say? You beat up a helpless woman and sell her for sex as if you were the pimp from Hell. Yeah, you’re such a victim. Bet you feel real tough when you’re beating on someone who can’t fight back huh? For someone who claims to be a bad ass assassin, that seems kind of…what’s the word I’m looking for…oh yeah, pathetic! Well get it out of your system now, because come Turmoil, you’re going up against someone who won’t be tied up, who won’t have any electroshock collars, who will fight back, and who WON’T be forgiving you. The rage in you begins to build, slowly causing the heat in your face to rise. You can feel your muscles tighten and your hands form into a closed fist. You think only of Oblivion. The evil in your opponent cannot be denied. And neither can the fact that he must be stopped. The good must always triumph over the evil. It is simply the way of the world. You pause a moment, searching for the right words in the labyrinth of your mind. Because you’re the one who was wrong this time. It’s you who screwed up. You’re not a victim in this game you’re a participant. Only you’ve done something far beyond unforgivable. Your mistake is way worse than mine. Not only have you provoked me (which is something you don’t want to do) but you’ve also attacked a woman. I don’t care what she did to you; NOTHING justifies beating up a woman. As far as I’m concerned, you and K-Hard can go fuck each other since you both seem to get off picking on women who can’t defend themselves. But I guess that’s just the kind of person you are. Blaming all your inadequacies and problems on someone else. Never your fault and always the victim. You wanna know whose fault all this really is? I’ll give you a clue, all you have to do is take off your mask, then look in a mirror at that ugly face of yours. Because you just couldn’t let the past go. It’s sad really. A few bad things happen to you, and all of a sudden your Mr. Poor Pitiful Me. You say I’m the weak one when it’s really you who’s weak. You’re the one letting the past control you. Instead of being a man and moving on, you sit in a dark corner for years, crying like a pussy until now you’ve gotten your chance for revenge. Well get ready to crawl back into that corner for another cry-fest, because I’m going to finish this. You allow yourself a quick smirk of a grin at the camera. You can just imagine the look on Oblivion’s face as he watches this. You can practically hear and feel the words piercing him the moment you speak them. You can even feel the heat of his glare cutting through the camera lens, boiling into you. Opening the door, you step out onto the deck of your apartment into the coldness of the night. A night that seems oddly reflective of Oblivion somehow. At Turmoil, I’m going to do what I should’ve done years ago. Instead of running away from this, I should have ended it then. But at Turmoil, we’ll finish what we started. No! Screw that! I’LL finish what YOU started! The mistake I’ll be correcting isn’t what I did, but it’s you. You’re the mistake Oblivion. You should never have been allowed to disgrace the MWE locker room with your emoness. The moment you set foot in an MWE ring I should’ve run you out. Back in the good ol’ days when this all started, I should have confronted you then and ended it then. But unfortunately I didn’t. But I’m going to fix that. I’m going into that ring and erase you not just from my history, but also from everybody’s history. I’m going to cripple you in that ring so badly that even your grandchildren will have whiplash. Once our match starts, I’m going to look you dead in the face, give you one large slap across the face and ask you one thing: Yeah, I did you wrong. But what are you going to do about it? The cold whips at your skin as the wind picks up. You rub your arms in an attempt to warm yourself, but to no avail. So you walk back inside and close the sliding door behind you. But the cold does not leave you. You can still feel it under your skin, crawling slowly into your heart. And you understand this is a different type of cold. It’s the frozen feeling of the fear that still lingers inside you. However small, it’s still there. But I will admit one thing, I’m still afraid of you somewhat and what you might do. I still fear that maybe I deserve to be punished for what I did. And maybe you are as fearless as you claim to be. But you’re wrong if you’re thinking that fear makes me weak. On the contrary, fear is what makes a person strong. It gives them something to overcome, whereas those who are fearless have nothing to fight for and nothing to prove. Fears lets a person understand what they are and aren’t capable of and work around those problems, while those who are fearless make stupid mistakes. So you see, my fear isn’t a weakness. Fear is only a weakness if you let it consume you. And I will not let my fear eat me from the inside. I will overcome my fear of punishment, of my guilt, and turn it into the biggest rush of my life. I’m the Ultimate Thrill Seeker dammit. I live off of fear. Fear is my adrenaline rush. I jump out of planes, climb rock walls with my bare hands, jump into caverns with only a cord as my safety, I’ve looked death and danger in the face more times than you ever will Oblivion. The Grim Reaper’s on my side buddy boy, so you better watch out. But now, our match is about more than just revenge and correcting past mistakes. From behind on the table, you pull out the X-TV Championship, holding it up for the camera to see. The belt feels slightly heavy in your grip. You gaze at it long moment, realizing now in the shadow of your first defense of this belt, how much it really means to you. Although it’s no World Championship, it doesn’t matter. To you, it might as well be. This belt represents many things to you. It represents your pride for your accomplishments. It represents the reward for the long road you took to get to where you are now. And to you, it represents the greatest glory you’ve so far achieved in your wrestling career. To you, it proves you belong here in the MWE. To you, it proves you’re one of the best. It’s also about this. The X-TV Championship. The belt I’ve spent the last few months building into something better. And against you, I’ll defend it for the first time. But if you think I’m going to let you get your ugly hands on this belt, you are sadly…sadly mistaken. Because the moment this belt touches your waist is the moment all the work I did to build this championship up is undone. And I won’t let that happen. Because you can’t carry this belt the way I have. Nobody can. I’m going down in history as the greatest X-TV Champ ever. You’re setting your sights a little too high Oblivion buddy. Why not start a little lower on the scale? Why not try facing someone your own league, like Milenko, and if you beat him then maybe you’ll be ready…maybe. I’ve taken a second-hand belt nobody cared about and made people take notice of it. I’m sure the MWE universe is probably tired of hearing me flaunt that by now, but hey, I need something to stroke my ego right? But what have you done since your arrival here to “stroke your ego?” Oh, one victory against the US Champion. Big flippin’ whoop. I beat the current World Champ. I’m currently on one of the biggest winning streaks in MWE history. So suck on that! While you, on the other hand, have lost Money In The Bank, lost to Eric Derf last week and the week before in tag team action, and you’re going to lose to me at Turmoil. And let me make it perfectly clear when I say that it isn’t arrogance, and it isn’t an ego when I claim that. It’s a fucking fact! It’s truth! You WILL lose at Turmoil…no discussion and no debate. Your body heat continues to rise as adrenaline motivates your every word. Big beads of sweat caress your cheeks as you breath hard and harsh. Your throat feels dry and pleads for a drink of water. After indulging in its request, you usher the camera out into the hall outside your apartment door. There is no more you can think to say. But just as the camera is about to turn and leave, you reach out a hand to halt it. With one final statement to say, you glare into the camera with absolute conviction in your voice. You think you know what fear is Oblivion? You think you can control the fears of others? Maybe you can, but not for long. Because at Turmoil, I plan to redefine what fear is. And when I’m done, there won’t be such a thing as fear. The word won’t even exist. Then it’ll just be you…me…and the good old Flash N’ Crash. You say you’ll cripple me? You say you shall have your revenge? Well then, there’s really only one thing I think to say to that…COME AND FUCKING GET ME!!! And with that, you slowly shut the door, keeping your stare hard and fix on the camera, looking past it, silently daring Oblivion to do his worse with nothing but a glance. Finally, the door quietly shuts, you lock the deadbolt, and you are alone once again. But this time, fear and darkness do not control you. This time, it is you, who controls and darkness and the fear. This is your fear redefined. This is how it feels to be Zack Crash…redefined.
