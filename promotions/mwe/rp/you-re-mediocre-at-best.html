You're Mediocre at Best; A more Serious RP

Sep 20 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Vega Knight is currently in a local Columbus gym. He's getting himself ready for his debut against JJ executioner He's currently at the punching back, and each time he strikes the bag, it's a shot that would normally hurt somebody. He then stops briefly. Vega: The fucker says I can't take wrestling serious. I'll show that wannabe. Vega then gives one last shot to the punching back, and the thing is now swinging violently. The look in Vega's eyes is obviously enraged. Vega: I wasn't trained by one of the most hardass coaches for nothing. I'm the last person who he wants to face. Because in the end, those homicidal gimmicks are all just for show. Vega takes a towel and wipes his face. He then heads to the locker room, to get changed. A few minutes later, he comes out in his street clothes, as he calls a cab to take him to his hotel. Once's he's at his hotel, he gets changed into a nice dress shirt, and a decent pair of black pants, and he then pulls out a video camera, and switches it on. Before he sits down. Vega: Hello once again MWE. The future of fun has arrived, in 'Supernova' Vega Knight. He used both thumbs to point to himself. He then focusses back on the task at hand. Vega: Now I'm sure you guys watching this, just watched the two bits done by JJ Executioner, and the interview I had with Shawn Spitzer. You know, the one where I had to shield my face from that spitter. Vega briefly chuckles at that, before becoming focussed. Vega: But a lot of people are saying I can't take a damn thing seriously. Well I don't know if you guys did pay attention beyond my jokes, but I very much am serious here. See, this guy, JJ, there is one thing that I can say about him. My opponent, is a poser. Me, well what you see is what you get. I mean seriously. I said it before that I just can't seem to take the so called 'Homicidal Darkside murderer' gimmicks seriously, because after the first five to ten guys who've done it, it just becomes boring, and non believable. Vega sighs, shaking his head. Vega: Oh sure, the guy certainly looks the part, and had his noose for Sullivan the other day, but come on, Halloween isn't for a month and a half. Why dress up like a homicidal maniac on the three hundred and sixty four days where you'll be criticized and ridiculed, to just the one single fucken day where people will praise you, especially with that wig that looks like a collection of a bunch of guy's pubic hair, doing that one prank from Jackass Number Two . Well enough with the Halloween reference. I don't know where you grew up, and I certainly don't give a shit, but it's probably better where I grew up. Hell the only other guy on the fucken MWE roster that knows what kind of life I had to live before, is Sonny Williams. However, he's to stuck in the past. I try not to think about it. I once lived next door to a real menace to society. You try waking up one day to find out that your next door neighbor is a sex offender, and you had no idea. Try seeing a friend......no......training buddy, somebody who you felt was almost like a brother, get shot down with fourteen bullets, and is the single most luckiest son of a bitch alive because he's wrestling and is taking more names then anyone could imagine. Vega smirks, despite probably pouring his heart out in that last bit. Vega: Point is JJ, the gimmick that you have, and I blatantly call it a gimmick, does not scare me. In fact, it makes me roll my eyes every single time I fucken see it, because once you live through the shit I've been through, it takes a lot more then just some 'OOOh look at me, I'm a homicidal maniac. Oh look, I got a noose and I will use it, but I won't kill anybody. FEAR ME!!!' routine to scare me. So you got beaten up when you were a kid. Who hasn't? I know I did, and I'm not using some Homicidal gimmick, now am I? Now as for your wrestling skills JJ, I'm not gonna say anything bad about them. You did pretty good on Turmoil the other day, but to be honest, you'll only be mediocre at best. James Sullivan was already leaving, so he basically gave you the match. As for what you did afterwards, man I just shook my head at the utter ridiculousness of it. Vega sighed. He got up for a second, to go into his mini fridge to pull out a bottle of Molson Canadian Beer. He opens the bottle up, and takes a bit from it. Vega: Now unfortunately, you never did tell anyone about who trained you, but I don't think you got the best deal out of it. If you want the best damn deal out of it, you'll go to somebody who's been trained in the Dungeon. That's right, the legendary training camp of Stu Hart. Now I didn't train in the dungeon itself, but I know what the feeling is like, as I was trained by somebody who was trained there. Believe me, if we got a move wrong, he'd stretch our limbs out until we were screaming like we took a ninety volt shot of electricity to the balls. I bled to become a wrestler. I got stretched, torqued, and beat up, just to be here and now. Hell I got one better. Near the end of the training for me, He said that he'd give me his triple piledriver every damn day I couldn't invent a unique finisher. I had a whole weeks straight of being dropped, on my head and neck, in various positions, three times per day, as per the move. That's a total of twenty one piledrivers, and believe me, it fucken hurts like a bitch. Vega pauses to catch his breath, taking a big gulp of his beer, before he would continue. Vega: My point being all this time JJ, is that if you want to intimidate somebody with your gimmick, well you got the wrong guy. That being said, I'm afraid you've already lost this match, for you are one of those guys who try to play mid games with other people's heads. Now that you know I can't be fooled, you know that you have to rely on skills, against somebody who was trained in all aspects of wrestling. So the last question is, how will I beat you. Will it be by the Supernova, or by the devastating Burst Limit. I guess it depends. Now if you'll excuse me, I got better things to do then to keep talking about how futile it will be for you JJ, to defeat me. I'm sure you already know by now. Vega gets up, and cuts the feed. He finishes the rest of his beer. He grabs his jacket, and leaves. A few minutes later, he was at a bar, having a few drinks. He had more then enough time to train some more, so he figured, now was a good time to just relax. Vega: Bartender. Jack and coke please. Vega is given a Jack and coke. He doesn't pay for it, knowing full well he plans to have another. When he begins to drink it, he notices that the bartender is a really gorgeous brunette. Vega: Whoa. Now I know this isn't the booze talking, but I think god just put a sample of heaven in front of me The Brunette blushed, and smiled. Brunette: I get compliments a lot, but certainly not quite so unique like that. Well Vega, I think you're cute. Vega: I get alo-wait a second. How do you know my name. Vega drinks a bit of his drink, as the bartender giggles at that. Brunette: I just so happen to be a fan of wrestling, and I happen to be watching MWE, plus checking out the site for news and stuff. Saw the video interview you did. Must say, you're a real one of a kind in MWE. It needs somebody like you there. Vega is pleasantly surprised. He takes another gulp of his drink. Vega: So miss.....- Brunette: Mila. Vega: Mila. That's a good name. I'm going to be honest. I love women, and I love having fun, if you know what I mean. Mila giggles at that, as Vega finishes his drink. He signals for another one, which Mila gladly makes. Vega: So I'm going to be honest. While I might not be a guy for a relationship, and despite we know each other's names, the honest to god truth, is that I want to be with you for one night. A night full of fun, and hot sex, anywhere you want. Call me a pervert, but I am a honest guy when it comes to things like this. So you and me, tonight, for a night for us to have a whole lotta fun. Mila smirks. At that. She leans over, so that Vega gets a nice view of her cleavage. Mila: Tell you what. Since you're being so kindly honest...... Mila leans into his ear, and whispers something. Mila: I'm done in one hour. I hope you can keep up. Later, Vega had opened up his hotel room, already with Mila embracing and snogging him. Once he was in, he and Mila were up against the well. They stopping their insent kissing for a minute, to both catch their breaths. Mila: I gotta ask you. Do you smoke after sex? Vega: I don't know. I never bothered to check. Both of them burst out laughing at that one. That is when Vega leaned in, and whispered something to her. Vega: But, when it's all said and done, you'll be the one who's smokin'. Mila: Mmmm sounds hot. The began to kiss again, as Mila wrapped her legs round Vega's waist, as he led them to the bed. END
