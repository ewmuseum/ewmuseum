Stating My Business

Aug 13 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
*MWE Cameras are viewing the outside of MWE Headquarters as a 2008 Pontiac GT, with leather interior and a copper paint job, pulls up. Slowly, James Sullivan gets out of the car and walks to the row of revolving doors that is at the front of the building. Sullivan walks through, and proceeds to walk down a finely carpeted hallway, all the while people admire his fine clothing and fantastic build. He continues to walk, and he checks the time on his gold watch. He smiles as he is right on time. James walks down several more hallways before finding a large door with a shiny plate that read "studios" in a chiseled black font. He pushed the doors open and walked into a gigantic room with pictures of Richard Covell and the latest MWE Hall of Famers, Katherine Tenaud, MWE World Heavyweight Champion David Whitman, and Tim Fitzgerald. Sullivan fixed the cuffs on his t-shirt and proceeded to a section of the room where there were bright lights, and cameras. There were two booths, and Shawn Spitzer was sitting in one of them.* Shawn Spitzer: Right on time Mr. Sullivan, please take a seat. James Sullivan: Thank you Spitz, and before we get this interview started, I would like to thank you for inviting me to this interview today. Spitzer: Well, now let's get down to it. This week on Mayhem, you have your very first match since signing with Mayhem Wrestling Entertainment. As you already know, it is one of the many first round matches in the tourney for the MWE United States Championship. What is going through your head as you prepare for your first match? Sullivan: Well Spitz, there are a number of things that have been racing through my head. First and foremost I am thinking about the win. I will never ever disappoint my fans, and for me to lose in a first round match would kill all my fans. They would be so upset, and I keep that in mind ever time I train, every time I speak about my match, and every time I look at the Monday Night Mayhem card. I stated that I was setting my eyes on a prize, and the United States championship is my prize. I take care of business, always have and always will. Until Monday Night Mayhem was announced to have me face Xavier Stone, he was not my business. However, it is a much different story now. *Sullivan cracks his neck and checks his watch. He takes a breath and goes over what he just said in his head. Shawn Spitzer looked at him, and proceeded.* Spitzer: Well, Xavier Stone has experience on you, so how do you plan to counteract his experience? I mean, he has already faced some of Mayhem Wrestling Entertainment's top stars. Sullivan: Well, I will give Xavier that. Experience does count, and he is definitely much more experienced than I am, but at the same time I can say that experience is not everything. I mean, I have youth on my side. Also, I have strength and speed, as well as endurance. And most importantly, I have the fans. You could call the fans my "secret weapon", because I know they respect me and will chant me on until I win my match against Xavier Stone. See, it wouldn't matter if Xavier Stone grew 2 feet and gain 250 pounds, I would still challenge him. No way will I ever back down to any challenge. No one intimidates me and not once will anyone try to scare me with their advantages. Believe me when I say this, I will please my fans and I will go through this whole tourney to win my prize...my business. I don't care who gets in my way, whether it be Brian Michaels or Aiden Copane, no one will ever stop me! Spitzer: Powerful words, but how do you plan to back them up? I mean, no one has seen you ever wrestle before, and you are practically unheard of. I will be completely honest when I say that I had never heard of you until you signed with Mayhem Wrestling Entertainment. *Sullivan frowns in disappointment at Shawn Spitzer. He shows both anger and passion when he hears these words.* Sullivan: So be it, if you haven't heard of me, and if MWE hasn't heard of me, they will hear me now. I plan to win this match and send a message to anyone who thinks I am just another new guy talking out my ass, because that is far from where I am. I will use my strength, my speed, and my youth to show everyone that I am going to be the next MWE United States champion, and frankly I don't think anyone can stop me. I mean, honestly I am a big dude, and I will use that to my advantage. Like I said before, I am not scared like some people, and if I can, I will just intimidate Xavier Stone to the point where he will be easy pickings. And if, for some strange reason, I am not successful, I will simply connect with the J.S.B and it will be over. See, my fans love the J.S.B. I love the J.S.B. Xavier Stone, I guarantee you will not like the J.S.B, and you want to know why. Because it will end any chance you have to beat me. I am not cocky, I just simply tell the truth. Trust me, and trust the fans on Monday, because they know and I know that the J.S.B is the single most devastating move in all of Mayhem Wrestling Entertainment. Sullivan: Oh, and as for not knowing me is concerned, I should inform you that people are well aware of who I am. You'll see, on Monday Night Mayhem look into the crowd and you will notice all the J.S.B signs, and all the James Sullivan chants that will be chanted throughout my match.[/COLOR] Spitzer: Moving on, what do you think about David Whitman winning his second MWE World Heavyweight Championship? Sullivan: I have to say that David Whitman winning again was not a surprise. Whitman is one hell of an athlete, and with credentials such as his, I respect the fact that he rightfully one the match. He put on a fantastic performance, and came out with the win. His match for the MWE World Heavyweight championship on Monday Night Mayhem is sure to be a match that will steal the show. I have to say, I would love to go one on one with him one day, because I have great respect for the man. I mean, he outlasted 9 other men to win the gold, and that is no easy feat. Brian Michaels, Sonny Williams, Alejandro Rodriguez, and my opponent this week Xavier Stone all fell victim to Whitman, and that is why he deserves to me the champion. Honestly, if I ever become World Champion I will look back to when Whitman won the gold and push myself to make performances like that ever week. Spitzer: So, do you have anything before we cut the interview short? Sullivan: As a matter of fact, yes I do. Xavier Stone this week you get to face me in my debut match. I am telling you right here right now that I will take anything you throw at me and shove it back in your face. I will go punch for punch and kick for kick with anyone, especially you. You are just the first person in my way, because the championship belt will be around my waist, and when it is, my fans will be pleased. I am James Sullivan, the champ of the fans, the champ of the MWE, and the champ in everyone's eyes. This tourney is mine, and you want to know why? It's because when you show no fear...You can't be scared! *James Sullivan made a symbol with his hands in front of the camera, and stood up. He shook Shawn Spitzer's hand and walked away, as MWE cameras followed him to the parking lot. Sullivan got to his car, got in and drove away as the video faded to black.* -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Yeah, I know it was short, but it was just kind of a test of my new gimmick. Just for future reference, I am a face, but at times can be a tweener.
