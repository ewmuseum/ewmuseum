It’s like being stuck. Between an instant and eternity. Like déjà vu, and jamais vu at the same time- trying to remember something you’ve never known, trying to forget everything that hasn’t happened. And the light? Yeah. It’s real. It’s taken many different forms for many different people… Light at the end of a tunnel, a glowing house, a blinding horizon… Me? Death. It wasn’t so much a cinematic experience; I didn’t actually “see” anything… it was more of a knowing state. Like a story being told through emotions and thoughts. It’s odd though, how everything around him jolts rather than himself, but he thinks nothing of it. He surveys the area as he slowly climbs off his white hospital bed, ripping off the various tubes attached to him with relative ease. The ground… the dirt. It’s damp. He takes a couple of steps, trying to figure out where he is… The orange sun seems kind of dim. The trees all have dark red bark and golden leaves. And as he walks, he cannot help but get the feeling he’s been here before. He reaches down and takes a bit of the dirt in his hands… It’s very close to fluid, yet it’s not. It’s sand, very very white sand. He knows something is wrong, but he cannot put his finger on it. As he stands up, he looks in front of him. In the distance he sees a silhouette of a man. The world jolts again. He falls to one knee, but quickly gets up again. He feels tired for some reason, as he begins walking at a steady pace towards the man. The sound of wind through the air becomes intense; it almost sounds like words being whispered, loudly. Dee Fib… AVP… Coo ik- He now tries to run as he is getting closer to the man. His chest feels tight. His throat is unbelievably dry. And hunger hits him in a strong way. He falls to his knees as his surroundings jolt violently once more, and the wind turns to loud murmuring, then slightly understandable words, then loud discussion. The world around him is getting brighter, but he struggles and tries to get his head up to look at the man. He can’t muster up enough strength to move too much, and begins to feel like his heart is going to explode. As he squints in pain, he drops his head sideways, and notices an open hand, slightly smaller than his own. It seems to beckon him.
