And So My Journey Begins...; How will the story unfold?

Sep 3 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
When you look into the darkness yet again, you see nothing. A problem with the T.V. again? No, that isn’t the case. Behind this screen, there’s a man with a truth, but the viewers can’t see that. He isn’t seen, but there’s a sound, it’s faint, but if listened to carefully it’s clear. It was a humming sound, like an electric vibrating sound. Soon the scene fades slowly to the masked man that revealed himself last Sunday. El Sainto, his current name among the listeners. He sat slouched on his altar. No one knew what thoughts when around in his head, no one tried to know. But they were curious, just like anyone normal human being. They wanted to know who was behind this match. Who was this man who sat there on that altar? What drives him? Question like this couldn’t be answered at this time. However, he could answer questions about his motives. Why does he wrestle? What makes him so enraged when talking about it? What grudge does this man have against the people who’ve morphed it? This hate for the sport was starting to decay inside of El Sainto. His eyes wandered along the ground, the camera zoomed away. There the masked man sat, in his hall of masks. His head slowly looked up towards the camera. His eyes showing some signs of confidence after his win at Crowning Glory. Surely he had made a statement, and surely the powers that be took into account of purpose. He sighs out, ready to let the world know what went on in his conflicted mind. El Sainto: Last Sunday, I let the world know that I’m the definition of true wrestling. I gave my heart and soul in that ring. It seemed like my opponent didn’t. I could see it in his eyes, that confidence he had, just oozed to me, it OOZED...that he thought I was a fraud. To him I was from some backwater country, a no-name from Mexico. The thing is...I proved him wrong. I made him pay for his arrogance towards his superior, and he ended up giving himself to me for an easy victory. The darkness in the background begins to fade to pictures of the downed Lohm Cassidy. El Sainto turns his head towards them. A smirk of happiness appears on his partially covered face. He turns back, his eyes glowing with this sense of pride. El Sainto: See Lohm, he was just a taste; he was just a little preview of what happens to people who take a piss on MY sport. No one...no one...is forgiven for what they’ve done to it. Not even other rookies. The pictures of the fallen Lohm phase away and soon a picture of a bloody Peter Kaymackian, then Chace Magnum appears, unconscious. El Sainto slowly gets up, turning himself to the pictures above his altar. He looks at them, his back turned to the camera. He takes his left hand and wipes his hand through his hair, glaring at his work. El Sainto: ...The future of wrestling, does not lay inside of these men. People think they’re the future. They believe they can have a second chance after giving these men the spotlight. All I ask is why? Why give THEM the torch? Why give these men--the MEN, who disgrace what I’m fighting for the opportunity, knowing they’ll screw it up...? He looked at the downed men in these pictures. What did they have? What made them so special for them to be the light of the everyone’s eye? Nothing, that’s what, nothing made them special. They were just puppies in a pack full of wolves, and El Sainto...he was one of those wolves. He turns slightly to his right and peers through the corner of his eye, glaring some at the camera. El Sainto: That torch belongs to me and only me. Why? Because I deserve it, and those two know it. So I took it upon myself to TAKE, that spotlight from them. Honestly, I did them both a favor. They didn’t want to put in the hard work, they didn’t want to wrestle, they didn’t want it as much as I do. I...deserve...their spotlight. But...someone doesn’t agree with me...someone I thought I took care of...Peter Kaymackian... Sainto sits on his throne, peering off into space. He rubs his chin, Peter Kaymackian didn’t hold a candle to him, and Sainto knew this. El Sainto darts his eyes towards the camera, his crystal lens piercing the lens. El Sainto: Peter, you’re one of the fresh faces of MWE. You are...one of the few who have potential, from what I’ve heard at least. You...Peter Kaymackian, are one of the bright stars that should be able to shine light on the future. Yet here you are, mocking what a wrestler should truly be like. In my eyes you should already be a star, but see something holds you back, and that’s your conscious. Deep down inside Peter, you know you aren’t ready. When those lights hit you, you break into a cold sweat. Your spine begins to tingle, and you start to feel sick, like you want to puke up what you had for lunch, and Peter...that is what separates YOU...from ME...! He slowly moves his hands on his mask, letting out this conflicted sigh. He closed his cold eyes, recollecting his thoughts. Something about Peter got under his skin. It was like he was going after some personal vendetta. Was it something that happened in the past? Did they know each other? If they did, why did Sainto detest Peter so much? El Sainto: Peter in a way you’re a fraud, and you’re a liar. You led MWE to believe you could be the future of this company. In some spectators eyes, you could have been a great name. Honestly, you could have been a future champion. But then...I came along, and now I’ve taken your spotlight. So what are you going to do now? Will you manifest into a coward, or will you face me like a true man? El Sainto slowly relaxes in his seat. El Sainto: Make up your mind Peter, and fast. Xalapa, Veracruz, Mexico A Week After The Incident Things are looking up for the better... Finally, after trying so hard to get out of this place, I free. No more burning in the hot sun with horrid shacks being my only shade. I didn't have to drink dirty water, and I didn't have to stoop to new lows to get money to survive. Though it was harsh, though the conditions were terrible, I learned something. Wrestling was becoming corrupt. It was beginning to collapse and it felt like I was on the only person who give a shit about it. The thoughts gave me an empty feeling. It felt like the more and more I fought the more and more I would fail. No one cares about wrestling anymore, except for those who can distort it in their own view. Thinking about that made my chest burn with this hatred. Back then there was some type of class. Now it's all about jumping off of something high, or being mutilated to no repair. It makes me sick! But that one thought of getting the chance to wrestle in the U.S.A. again, that gave me some hope. Change was starting to happen, that was good right? Someone was looking out for me up there, and for that I wanted to think them. This hellhole was starting to make me antsy. I felt bitter; angry that I couldn't do anything without the money to leave. I let out a sigh, brushing my hair back. I looked at my mask, the one part of me that I had pride in. I picked it up, looking over it's red base and the black and white designs on it. I place my hands on the cross near the forehead. I felt the black lining and looked over it's white color with small red diamonds at the ends of it. El Sainto: One my chance. I muttered to myself, unlacing the back of the mask and placing it on my face. Afterward I put my silver contacts in and began to pack, readying myself to leave this God forsaken hole in the Earth. I slowly made my way out, my right foot hitting the dirt. I glared around and saw some people standing around, some talking about the incident. It was like they were surprised, like I was happy with how things were going for me. They didn't know a damn thing. They just paid money to see some circus act. These people were lost, and I wasn't going to lead them to a better understanding of what a true wrestler should be. All of them would stay ignorant, and lay in this fucking hole and rot for the rest of their lives. I made my way out of the shack and slowly made my way to the police station. See you couldn't just cross the border of Mexico and NOT be caught. Whoever told some of these idiots who tried to get to the U.S. this would be punched in the face. In order for me to get across the border and not be sent back, I'd have to go see my peace officer. His name was Deseo Otorgante (Dee-See-O O-Tor-Gan-Tee) The man had kept me out of trouble while I stayed here. Before I was homeless with little to my name. Deseo granted me a second life, that idiot at that wrestling promotion was full of shit. Before I ever went to that place Deseo was the one who gave me a home. His nephew had moves and went to America to become a teacher. So his shack was up for rent, Deseo gave me the shack, but I would have to go find work on my own. I got in some small altercations with some of the local drunks, but Deseo was there to make sure I didn't get killed. Though I could handle myself, myself, I was a man; I could handle my own fights. It was strange; it was like he treated me as one of his children. Deseo never talked about them, all he mention was he had one little girl and a son. After that I didn't ask anything else, getting into another man's personal life seems a bit nosey. I wasn't one to snoop around when it dealt with that kind of conversation. I walked down this dirt path towards the station. People stared, but I ignored them. Their opinions didn't matter to me. I was leaving and they would stay here. That was already a victory for me. Slowly I down this path, glaring at all of these eyes from time to time who thought of me as some type of maniac. I made a left turn, walking pass the wrestling building. There my former boss stood, a cigarette in his mouth and a glare of disgust in his eyes. Something in my head told me, "Keep moving, you have nothing else to prove to him." But my body stopped. Soon I glared at him, eye to eye with his staff behind him along with that meathead wrestler he tried to screw me over with by his side. He blew the smoke from his nose and then opened his mouth, pointing his cigarette in my direction. Former Boss:Tú ... no son nada ... [You...are nothing...] I glared at him, he had this smug smirk. He threw the cigarette down at my feet. Former Boss: Vienes a mi país, mi patria agujero pobre cabrón y cree que puede orinar en él y salir? [You come into my country, my poor little shit hole country and think you can piss on it and leave?] He had this fake amused expression, forcing a chuckling from his gruff vocal cords. Former Boss: USTED ... NO SON MEJORES QUE YO! [YOU...ARE NOT BETTER THAN ME!!] He spat near me, his brown skin turning a bright cinnamon. Former Boss: DO YOU HEAR ME?! YOU ARE NO ONE! I MADE YOU SOMEONE YOU LITTLE PIECE OF TRASH! [¿Me oyes?! ERES NADIE! Te hice un ser pequeño pedazo de basura!] I just smirked at him, yet another victory for me. I walked towards him, keeping my distance. The smell of his cigarettes made me sick. I gave him this smile and looked him up and down, like he was some peasant who was begging for change. El Sainto: You are a sad...and worthless piece of scum. You didn't do anything for me but try to screw me out of paychecks. You're a crook promoter who shouldn't even be in the wrestling business. Usted pertenece a la calle a barrer la mierda de los perros que pasan. His eyes widened, and his mouth foamed with spit. He shouts out curse words at me and tries to chase after me. His crew holds him back, trying to calm him down. I turned away from him, walking down that path towards the station. This was a sweet victory, that bastard didn't win. He didn't get what he wanted from me, and now he had my words to haunt him at night. I couldn't help, but smile my entire walk. That was just priceless! I could see him now, his vein popping out of his head, almost nearing a heart attack. God, I wish I could've stayed longer to watch him. But I have bigger goals and bigger places to be, and watching that man suffer--though it makes me happy--wouldn't help me gain those goals. The police station was beat down, but the brick walls had a smooth look to them. There were benches set up outside. On top near the door read "Policía" simple, but for those who couldn't read a lick of Spanish it was suited for them. I walked inside and looked around. It was quiet, not a lot of action seemed to be going around. I walked around, looking inside of the station. Deseo should be here, he was probably out. One way to find out, check with the front desk. I walked around until I found someone with a uniform on. Soon enough I looked up and saw a desk. Then there were faint chuckles. The laughter got louder when I approached. Someone was sitting down, but I couldn't make out there face. Mostly due to the fact they were turned around in their chair. This small T.V. was set up behind them on a file cabinet. When some dialogue was heard the man laughed. ? ? ?: Ha ha ¿De dónde vienen para arriba con esta mierda? ...HA HA HA HA! He sure was a jolly guy, nearly tipped over in his chair from laughing so hard. I noticed his police uniform, so he was the right person to talk to. Great. El Sainto: Disculpe. [Excuse me.] ... No answer, I blink and put my stuff down on the ground. El Sainto: Perdone, ¿sabe usted dónde Oficial Otorgante es en este momento? Realmente necesito hablar con él es importante. [Excuse me, do you know where Officer Otorgante is right now? I really need to talk with him it's important.] ? ? ?: ...HAHAHAHAHA!!! Ahhh, dios mío... I lick my teeth and then walk towards his desk. I cock my right fist back and slam it on the table. El Sainto: HEY! The guy turned around, he held a cigar in his mouth, chewing the end of it. The thing wasn't lit, that was fucking disgusting. He chewed and looked at me with an unimpressed face. El Sainto: Ahora que tengo tu atención de mierda, necesito hablar con el agente Otorgante! Me preguntaba si tenía alguna idea de dónde estaba. [Now that I have your fucking attention, I need to speak with Officer Otorgante! I was wondering if you had any idea where he was.] He eyed me for a second, like he was trying to make me out. He takes the cigar from his mouth and spits out a wad of saliva and tobacco into this cup on his desk. I close my eyes, trying to stop myself from throwing up at the sight of that nasty shit. He places it back in his mouth and leans back in his chair which makes this annoying creaking sound. ? ? ?: Yo te conozco, tú eres ese chico que causó que las perturbaciones en ese espectáculo de lucha libre. [I know you, you're that kid who caused that disturbance at that wrestling show.] It was like he was trying to give me a reminder of what I did. I strain my hair through my fingers in a calm manner. El Sainto: Sólo necesito hablar con Deseo. [I just need to speak with Deseo.] ? ? ?: Sabes que me ha costado mucho dinero. Por lo que he oído que ni siquiera se supone que debe ganar esa noche. Parece como si uno llega rouge y no le gustaba lo que su jefe estaba haciendo ¿eh?[You know you cost me a lot of money. From what I heard you weren't even suppose to win that night. Seems like you went rouge and didn't like what your boss was doing huh?] Something told me he wasn't going to make this easy. I lose my eyes, my patience was wearing thin. He was delaying me my freedom from this place! ? ? ?: Me debes ochenta pesos, ¿está usted aquí para pagarme?[You owe me eighty dollars, are you here to pay me?] This shit was getting ridiculous!! El Sainto: ESCUCHE arrugada que la mierda, no tengo puta tiempo para ocuparse de esto y seguro como el infierno se le paga por usted aspira en el juego. ¿Dónde está...Deseo oficial?! [LISTEN you wrinkly fuck, I don't have fucking time to deal with this and I sure as Hell will pay you because you suck at gambling. Now where...is...Officer...Deseo?!] Deseo: Right here Sainto. I turned and there he was, Deseo with a plain expression. He walks up to the desk and sighs. Deseo: ¿Cómo es que cada vez que algo no va a tu manera te culpo a la persona que camina en esta lista? Vamos que estamos a la ley, no podemos mantener venganzas personales, ya que atornilla la vuelta y perdió toda su cheque de pago del juego. [How come everytime something doesn't go your way you blame the one person who walks in here? Come on we're the law, we can't hold personal vendettas because you screwed around and lost your entire paycheck from gambling.] He turned back to me with a friendly smile. He placed a hand on my shoulder and looks me over. Deseo: Do you have everything? His accent was thick, but his English was definitely on point. I sigh out and rub my eyes through my mask. El Sainto: I just can't seem to get a break. Deseo: Don't worry about him, he's still bitter he has to work day and night shift to make up for losing all his money from gambling. Which should be a lesson to him to not gamble while on duty. It disgraces our uniform. Deseo turned back at the grumpy officer who scrunched his face up and jawed away at his cigar. Deseo: Do you have everything you'll need...? I nod my head. Deseo: Good, my truck is parked outside. We shall head off then. He pat my shoulder and heads off, I glare at the old deputy. He makes a face and I just walk off. In the car we didn't say much, there wasn't really much of a "what's going on with yoU" type feel. We just wanted the same thing at the moment, to make sure I make it across that line in one piece. I lean my head against the window, looking at the scenery. The drive would be long, that's why we were going to stop in the Federal District. Inside of my locker there was a plane ticket. The plane was set up for me, they just needed to know I was alive. Deseo: It's great to know that you're going to make something of your life. I turned my head partially and smirked. El Sainto: Anything is better than staying there. I honestly thought I'd go insane. Deseo: Crazy? No. no, you're probably grow bitter over a time, but not insane. Those people just don't understand your situation is all. You cannot blame them, at first when you arrived you weren't a very friendly person. He had a point, but he had to understand. Everything was taken from me, I had nothing...how was I suppose to react? Smile and say "Hi" to everyone? Deseo: All I'm saying is that you shouldn't look back at this experience as a bad omen. Think of it as...a miracle. All humans are not perfect my friend. Look at you, after being in Xalapa for three days you got in five fights. Most of those fights didn't even have to start, but they did. I've had to bail you Sainto, you have to understand how people are... El Sainto: ... Deseo sighs. Deseo: I know, I know...you do not like the lectures...sorry. El Sainto: It's fine, really don't worry about it. Well it was good someone thought about someone else before thinking about themselves. Deseo was a good man, and he was a great law enforcer. I rubbed my chin, thinking, I was returning to the states. I didn't know what to expect, I didn't know who still knew me. All I knew was that I would bring a message with me, others failed to bring. It's time they knew the true reality of wrestling. El Sainto: Thank you, for everything. Deseo didn't say anything to me. He just smiled and stared at the road. I smirk and look off to the side. Maybe it was a miracle...at least part of me thinks that now. Scene comes back to Sainto, sitting at the steps of his altar. The walls of millions of masks surround him; barricade him from the world that doesn't understand what he's trying to do. There ignorant, just like .Peter Kaymackian. He slowly glares up at the black ceiling, checking for some sort of sign that one day people will listen to him. El Sainto: It's kind of like an itch you can't get rid of. You try...and you try...and you try, but it returns. I thought somehow...me winning my match would open some people's eyes. But no, no, no they looked at the Chace Magnum vs Peter Kaymackian match, and their little eyes gleamed in awe. FOR WHAT?! They aren't true athletes! They aren't like me!! His clear hues quivered in this anger. The camera zooms in on him, his hair glistening from water. He start to take a breath in... El Sainto: And yet, they still cheer, for sub-par entertainment...when they could cheer for extravagance. It goes to show what type of people there are in the world. Now Peter, be honest with yourself: Do you really, really, really believe you can win tonight? Is that what's going to keep your drive alive? He gives a spiteful scoff and leans forward towards the lens. El Sainto: If so you've got a world of reality coming straight at you. If Crowning Glory didn't teach you anything then you're a lost cause. You shouldn't be in this match with me, because honestly you can't keep up Peter. I'm way above your regular level. I'm not some video game you can try and beat, no I AM TRUE TALENT! And true talent Peter, doesn't lose to mediocrity. I'll be damned if I lose to you. His eyes steadied. El Sainto: If I lose to you, it shows that I'm a liar and my misguided friend, I am NO liar when I say that I will beat you within an inch of your life, until you realize who your superior is. Your losing streak is going to continue Peter. Don't try to fight the already foreseen future. Don't pull away from your fate. I was destined to make an example as I did to Chace, and as I did to Lohm. He leans back into the darkness, beginning to disappear. El Sainto: And now you will be on that list.... And with that, he was gone, his room of masks; of his personality were only seen. The camera slowly zooms out, revealing the dark room fully. Suddenly the screen fades, and the watchers of this go back to their dull lives of ignorance.
