a glimpse of the future?

Dec 4 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
An orange glow lights a small clearing in the woods. The light is the glow of a warm fire crackling away under the canopy of dozens of trees. The fire glows, dances, and crackles as Blight sits in front of it deep in thought. His long black hair pulled back tight into a ponytail. He is wearing an Iron Maiden tee with a pair of ripped blue jeans and his motorcycle boots. The glows of the fire illuminating his face making him look angrier than normal. Blight shuffles his body around on the large tree stump he is sitting on, although under him the stump isn’t looking that big. He reaches down to his left side and grabs a bottle of Sothern Comfort and starts to drink slowly from it. He lowers the bottle from his face and looks at the fire; he takes the bottle and pours a tiny bit into the flame causing a flare up. Blight picks up a large stick on the other side of him and pokes it around the fire a bit before staring back into the flames that dance in the gleam of his eyes. Blight in his own head: Is this really what I want to do with my life? Do I really want to bare the pain day in and day out? I couldn’t pick a normal job….nah not my style huh. Instead for the last six years I have done nothing but abuse my body, and damn near kill opponents in a ring. Why wrestling? Why do I get such…such…such a rush for violence? I would like to think I am not a freak, not some kind of monster. Blight looks down at his arms and around some of his tattoos before taking another swig of his SoCo. Blight thinking: Each scar I wear tells another story. Each scar on my flesh gives me my legacy in my own right. Maybe I am not human, not one damn person could have survived the things I have if they were truly human. Ladders, chairs, tables, barbed wire, fire, shit I have even been electrocuted. And I don’t regret a damn moment of it. The fire dancing and crackling away Blight just stares as if something may pop out of the fire and greet him. Blight although plagued by his thoughts, looks content in the quiet surroundings. He runs his fingers through his hair and stands up to stretch a moment. Looking down in the fire he kicks around a rock in front of him slowly. Blight thinking: For everything I have gained I have lost so much more. Maybe I have gone too far. Did I try to re-live a dream that was better off dead? Did coming back to wrestling cost me the one and only thing I give a shit about? I should use it to fuel my own fire, to go and be more vicious and violent. Go and be the monster everyone sees. But something is holding me back. All I do is want it all back. I just want my life back. Blight walks around the fire over to where he has a tent set up. Outside of the tent is a duffle bag, he reaches inside and pulls out his mask and stares at it. He walks back over to his stump and sits on the edge of it holding the mask down by his knees. Blight thinking: I hide behind a piece of leather…that’s all she thinks it is. She doesn’t understand, this is a part of me, the part that allows me to control my anger, my anguish….my fears. If she could only see the world the way I see it though these eyes. Now it’s all just a blur. Now I’m losing control and there is nothing no one can do about it. Something is not going to end well…I just hope she isn’t in front of this bullet. Blight stands up and takes one last swig of his bottle and drops it next to the fire along with his mask. He crawls inside his tent and goes to sleep. The next morning… The sound of chirping birds fills the early morning air as Blight rolls over and sits up holding his buzzing head. The smell of burnt wood is still slowly drifting away from the fire the night before. Blight crawls out of the tent and stretches like a grizzly out of hibernation. Chipmunks are heard scurrying through the fallen tree leaves. Blight looks up at the sky for a moment soaking in the morning sun when he hears something rustle around behind him. Something larger than any chipmunk or squirrel. Blight: Who’s there? Hello? Blight who is still wearing his jeans and boots from the night before slowly reaches down to his left boot. He pulls out a hunting knife. The six inch blade gleaming in the light. He slowly walks towards the sound with knife in hand ready for anything he might have to do. The rustling gets louder and louder still with no response to Blights calls. Suddenly out of the clearing pops Jezibel who gives a small scream once she realizes the point of Blights blade is pointed straight at her face. Jez: Holy shit! Babe it’s me! Oh my god! Blight looking more shocked than anything lowers the blade. Blight: How did you know I was here? Never mind that why did you come find me? Jez: When you didn’t answer your cell or house phone and your motorcycle was gone I figured you had needed to get away for a bit. I came to apologize to you…for the fight. I was wrong I never should have questioned you last week. I just hope that you saw things in my perspective. Blight not wanting to admit he was wrong and apologize gives a little smirk in his “victory”. He walks over to Jezibel and gives her a big hug. They release the embrace and Blight checks his watch. Blight: let me just pack up my clothes and stuff, we need to get out of here. Got a match at the pay per view. Josh Parker I believe his name is. I say after the match we go and celebrate, it won’t take that long anyway. In the process Blight grabs his bags and starts throwing clothes in. Jezibel starts cleaning up around the tent throwing garbage into a bag that Blight handed her. Jez: Babe…you know it’s a barbed wire rope match. This is your element… She pauses for a second as she ties the garbage bag and sets it down. She walks over to Blight who is still packing clothes into the bag and kneels down next to him giving him a hand. Jez: I’m not trying to start a fight but I have a really bad feeling about this match. The danger factor is so high it’s scary. For me at least. Blight just looks at her out of the corner of his eye as he zips up the clothes bag and starts busting down his tent. Jez: I had a dream the other night which is why I had to come find you. Just to see if you were ok….There was this room. The walls filthy with mud and dirt, it was dark the one light bulb in there was so dingy it barely lit anything. In the middle of this room is a table. It was stained with just stuff, almost like dry blood or something. And there you were…lying there strapped down not able to move or anything. Blight looks at her as she is fighting back tears at just remembering the dream and how scared she was. Jez: There was this man; he was kinda tall and decently built. He was wearing a black robe but I couldn’t make out his face no matter what I did. He would walk over to you and just look down as if studying you like some type of experiment. She starts to cry a little harder now. Blight is kneeling next to her listening. The tent and clothing all packed away. Jez: He would take these weird looking tools and do all sorts of things. I would try to run up to him and stop him but every time I got close I wouldn’t be able to get in front of him. Something kept forcing me to stay behind him. I couldn’t see much but all I heard were your screams. The more I screamed and yelled for him to stop to more painful your screams became…until you just fell limp….that’s when I woke up. She buries her face in her hands and sobs heavily. Blight puts his arms around her to console her. Blight: It was just a dream. Everything is ok; I’m still here and see no weird tool marks in me. Don’t think about it nightmares come and go but they aren’t real. Jez: No you don’t understand. I have a feeling that dream meant something. That the man in the robe was Parker. And this match might not end so well. Blight you don’t know what he is capable of, I mean you never know what you’re getting into when you step in the ring…I don’t know this is just different. Blight: Babe it was a dream. I understand you’re afraid, but this isn’t my first walk in the park with this type of matchup. Parker yes might come out unpredictable…but it’s ok. I might not know what to expect from him but it’s ok. I know what I’m doing out there. I honestly don’t think this will be a big problem for me…his nickname is The Big Cheese. Jez smiles a bit at hearing the dorky as hell name for someone who is supposed to be this big strong bad ass. Blight: the only thing we have in common…we have no parents. He was a foster child…me my father was just a murderous prick. See his parents knew from the start he was going to be a failure at life so they dumped him off somewhere…too bad wolves didn’t raise him he might have been better off. This kid wants to make a name for himself against me but that’s not going to happen. Jez stands up and wipes the dirt from her pants as Blight is talking. She just looks at him still unsure she wants him to go through with the match. They both gaze at each other for a few moments until they feel rain start to fall from between the trees. Blight quickly grabs his stuff and loads up. He puts his hand out to Jez and helps her onto his bike. Blight: ill ride you down to your car and follow you back. You ok now? Jez: ok…and no I’m not but I can’t stop you. I can’t tell you how to live your life. Babe good luck, I’ll still be in your corner. Just please be careful. Blight: don’t make me throw you off the bike on the way down please ha-ha I’m playing with you calm down, don’t get all worked up. But yes I will try to make this a fast one…I got a hot date to get to afterwards. Blight starts up the bike; Jez quickly jumps off and runs over to where the fire was. She bends down and picks up Blights mask that was laying there. He looks at her and nods as she jumps back on the bike clutching Blight and his mask tightly as they drive off.
