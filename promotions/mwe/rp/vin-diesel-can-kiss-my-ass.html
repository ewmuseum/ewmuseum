Vin Diesel Can Kiss My Ass; Zack Crash RP

Jul 30 2009

Zack Crash

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Nighttime. It’s a most interesting time of the day with many associations that go with it. Many may associate it with rest. The end of the day when we’ve earned a good sleep after a hard days work. Others may associate it with mystery; even danger as the shadows it cast obscure many things. And some associate the night with a sort of freedom. A sort of release from the obligations of the day, as if the masking shadows allow us to be our true selves. There are many other countless associations, but this is the one that we’ll examine here today. It’s our opening scene, and we find ourselves in the back of what appears to be a car driving along the deserted streets of Charleston, WV. From the outside, we see city sidewalks void of any life as tall buildings stretch up high like giant monoliths. The only light we can see comes from the streetlamps, which break the darkness in small patches. The whole scene has a kind of reverence to an Alfred Hitchcook or M. Night Shyamalan film. Turning our attention back to the inside, in the front, we see a familiar face sitting in the passengers seat, arms crossed and stiff as usual. It’s Kent Bishop. And seated in the drivers chair is…guess who. It’s not Sinc Mercier. It’s not Brian Michaels. It’s not even Zak Warner, though you are getting warmer. It’s Zack Crash. If you couldn’t guess that, you’re an idiot. Seriously, who else would it be? Kent: Man, that movie sucked major ass! Why did you drag me to see that thing anyway? Zack: Because it was my turn to pick the movie. Zack answers cheerfully, able to divert his attention between keeping his eyes on the road and talking with perfection. A disgruntles looking Kent heaves a loud yawn which –author yawns– is contagious. Kent: Well your taste in movies sucks. Zack just lightly laughs the comment off, waving a dismissive hand and not taking it personally. Zack: Oh come on, it wasn’t that bad. It did have some awesome action scenes. Kent: Yeah. And that’s about ALL it had going for it. There was no story, or at least no good one. The whole thing was nothing but mindless action and cool special effects. Zack: And that’s all you need in my book. Kent: I think your book needs some editing. The debate drops here. Kent and Zack have always known they have very conflicting ideas on what makes and doesn’t make a good movie. While Kent appreciates the more subtle things like story and musical score, Zack likes flashier things like explosions and action. And the two of them have known each other long enough to think different about trying to convince the other differently. Thankfully, their differences in opinion doesn’t mean they can’t still enjoy a movie outing together. Or at least it does for one of them. Kent: Well couldn’t we have at least gone out a bit earlier? The camera, taking its cue, focuses in on the currently turned off radio to check the time. The digital dials read: 2:33 AM. Kent: You know I hate staying out this late. Zack shakes his head disapprovingly. Zack: Man, you need to live more. Kent shakes HIS head disapprovingly. Kent: Nooooo. What I need is eight hours of sleep. Zack shakes his head as he speeds up to catch a changing light as it goes from yellow to red just as he passes under it. He always thought of Kent to be something of a…“stick in the mud” to use an old expression. He’d tried several times to get him to loosen up a bit, but everything seemed futile. But Zack wasn’t one to give up. He takes a small left turn, going down a long patch of road. He swifts his gaze ever so slightly to put Kent just out of the corner of his eye. When he speaks, he does so with an almost overly poetic tone, waving his hand passionately through the air. Zack: My dear friend, you are young and strong. You need to take advantage of the time life has given you. Nighttime is the time when we, as the young, can truly know what it means to live with freedom. Carpe Diem...or whatever it is they say. After his long and very eloquent monologue, Kent arches an eyebrow upward. Kent: You’re a dork. For a while now, the trip passes in total silence. Kent props his elbow against the window, using his palm to hold up his chin. He yawns again as he gazes out the window at the scenery failing to keep his interest. Rubbing some grit out of his eyes, he looks very tired and like he might fall asleep where he sits. Zack, on the other hand, appears to be wide-awake. He stares wide-eyed out the windshield, tapping his hands on the steering wheel to a silent tune inside his head. A few more turns later, they come to a red light, and Zack slowly brakes into a halt. They sit there for a moment when, as if out of nowhere, another car pulls up beside them. Now this narrator isn’t very good with car know-how, but just know it’s a very fast looking car that wouldn’t be out of place in one of those racing movies. Yeah, one of those cars. Zack looks somewhat surprised to see another person out at this hour. Regardless, he and the other driver meet gazes and he offers a friendly wave. The other driver, rather rudely I might say, does not return the gesture. Kent: What’s his problem? A bitter-toned Kent says as he looks between the two of them. As if waiting for a reply, Zack still has his hand raised and his smile on. The other driver has an odd expression somewhere between sinister and mischievous. Later, his engine roars loudly. Zack doesn’t respond the way he wants, but rather cocks his head in mild confusion. Another revving of engine is enough to dawn realization on Zack’s face like a sunrise. And he smiles. Kent: Hey, what’s going on here? Kent asks, worry visible in his tone as he studies Zack’s growing smirk. Zack revs his own engine, and the other driver finally returns the smile and waves. Both drivers grip their steering wheels and face forward. Kent looks on helplessly as Zack’s painted expression is becoming more twisted. Zack: It would appear our friend over there wants to challenge us to a drag race. Kent: WHAT!?!?! You’re not going to accept are you? Kent should know better than that. The only answer he receives is the continued shouts of the engines as they let the night hear their contained fury waiting to be unleashed. Not exactly the answer he was hoping for. Kent: Dude, you can’t race him! I mean, look at what he’s got! He’ll murder you! Again, another fruitless comment and Kent knows it. After all, since when was Zack one to care about the odds being stacked against him. I thought we’d established that by now. The light is taking an unnaturally long time to change. It’s almost like the light itself is helping to build the tension between the two impromptu racers. Kent: Come on man! Don’t do this! Zack turns to see Kent’s face gaunt with fear as a single bead of sweat rolls over his brow. For a brief moment, a frown can be seen on his face. He doesn’t wish to harm Kent, nor does he want to cause him discomfort on any level. But he can’t help it. IT is taking over him now. And by IT, I mean the urge to seek any and all thrills. It’s like a drug to him. It’s not a matter of wanting to; it’s a matter of needing to. Needing to indulge that drug. Just as quickly as it appeared, the frown turns upward back into a grin. Sorry Kent. Zack leans forward, gazing at the light as he silently begs it to change. The engines roar again, and all time seems to stop for an instant. Then… GREEN!!! Zack: LET’S ROCK AND ROLL BABY!!! That is Zack’s battle cry as he pushes the acceleration pedal down, shooting his car forward like a bat out of hell. The odometer shots up quickly, yet is still too slow to keep up with the car’s real speed. Kent: AHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHH!!! Kent screams in adjacent fear as he desperately struggles to tighten his seat belt as the force of the car pushes him back in his seat. And on that note kiddies, DO NOT try this at home. Go to a friend’s house first and try it there. The cars are neck-in-neck, each not giving an inch. For having a much less flashy car, Zack is doing alright for himself. The other driver, noticing this, looks through the glass to give Zack a most obscene gesture. He then punches up the speed, and slowly begins to crawl into the lead. Zack: Oh no you don’t! Zack does the same, laying the pedal flat on the floor. Zack starts to gain ground, but the superior might of the other engine is too much. Slowly, it begins to pull ahead. They’re about to reach the first turn at the end of the block. Twisting their steering wheels, they both turn sharply, nearly scrapping their sides together. The camera begins to shake. Whether due to the cameraman’s own fear or due to the force of the driving is unknown, but does it really matter? A small wailing can be heard in the distance. It’s undistinguishable at first, but some becomes clearer. Now it’s unmistakable. And with the mingled screaming of the siren and flashing of blue lights comes a falling pit in your stomach. It’s the police. Zack: Oh crap-cicles. Both drivers face backwards to see a deadly fate following closely. The other driver smirks, then, saluting Zack and Kent, turns down a random road and disappears into the night. Unfortunately, the cop seems to find Zack’s car a far more interesting target. Kent: Shit! What are we gonna do? There’s nothing they can do really. All Zack does is press the brake, pulling the car to the side of the road where it comes to a smooth halt. The police cruiser stops behind them, and the sirens cease. Zack: Just let me handle this. Kent looks at Zack with an expression that says he’d like to do anything BUT that. However, he’s not going to stick his neck out either. He is still shaking from the less than comfortable ride after all. The cop arrives at the driver side and gives the window a tap, signaling for Zack to roll down the window, which he does. The man is the exact stereotype of a cop. Wide brim hat, stony expression, complete with a set of dark sunglasses obscuring his eyes. Wait! Sunglasses…at night? Perhaps he thought they made him look cool. It certainly was having an intimidating effect on Kent. The officer scowls at the two men, shining a flashlight in their faces. Cop: Do you know why I pulled you over? Zack: Depends on how long you were following me? Kent slaps his palm against his forehead. Did Zack really just try to make a joke with a police officer? Kent can already hear the sound of metal bars shutting them in as they spend the rest of the night in a cold cell with some fat guy named Bubba. Ugggggh! Not a pretty thought. Cop: Funny. The cop says, not looking at all amused. He lifts a pad of paper and starts scribbling. Cop: Did you know I clocked you going near 125 mph. in a 75 mph. zone? Zack: Really? It felt faster than that? Kent clapped his hands together and began praying madly for some kind of intervention on Zack’s stupidity. The cop scribbles some more, then looks down on Zack with furrowing eyebrows. Cop: You want to tell me what the hell you were thinking? Please don’t say what I think you’ll say! Please don’t say what I think you’ll say! Please don’t say what I think you’ll say. Zack: Well Officer, we were at this traffic light when some guy came up beside us. He challenged me to a drag race, and hell I couldn’t say no to that. So we raced around the block a bit before you pulled us over. Pretty simple really. Sounds cool doesn’t it? Son of a bitch! The officer stops scribbling. Cop: Step out of the car please sir. The officer begins to walk back to his car and Zack turns to give Kent the thumbs up. Kent just buries his face in his palms. He begins to wonder how bad a criminal record would look on a resume. He turns to watch outside the rear windshield as the two men talk near the hood of the cruiser, all the while, Kent expecting him to handcuff Zack at any moment. But wait? After a moment, the cops face suddenly turns jubilant. He can’t tell much of what they’re saying, but the conversation seems to have done a 180. Soon, the officer hands Zack a sheet of paper, which he signs, the two shake hands, the cop tips his hat, and returns to his vehicle. As the cop drives away, Zack returns to the driver’s seat of his own car where a gapping mouthed Kent waits completely dumbfounded. Kent: What the…what just…how did you…huh? Zack only laughs. Zack: Turns out the guy was a fan of mine. He said he’d forget about the whole thing if I just gave him an autograph and promised to send him a t-shirt. He said personally, a drag race did sound pretty cool. And he said it was ok since it was so late and we didn’t cause any property damage or anything. The two take off once again, driving back down the road at the normal speed limit. Zack turns to Kent, who has been shocked into motionlessness. Zack: Told you I could handle it. The rest of the trip goes by in silence until they finally reach the home of Kent Bishop some twenty-three minutes later. Kent pulls himself off the seat with all the strength he has left. It’s funny; though his body feels tired, his mind is now wide-awake. Zack: Sleep tight. Zack calls with a wave. Kent offers him a less than friendly return gaze along with his own variation of the obscene gesture given to him by the driver earlier. He doubts he’ll get any sleep tonight. And just as he’s about to close the door, he turns back to Zack and calls, Kent: Next time, I’M driving. The door shuts behind him, and a few moments later, the lights go out, and Kent’s home belongs to the darkness. Zack sits there a moment, just starring, not looking at anything exceptional. Then, he turns toward the back seat, looking straight into the camera and directly out at those watching right now. People like you. Zack: Well, that certainly was an interesting night. Zack says brightly as he turns to face the camera. He opens the door the steps out into the grass wet with due. He doesn’t particularly need to get out. It’s just that doing these promos is so much more easily done when one isn’t confided to such enclosed space. Out now in the open, he feels free to express himself. He paces around the car, running his hands along its smooth surface. Sometimes in life, we find ourselves to be exceedingly lucky. And I consider myself a lucky person for a number of reasons. I consider myself lucky that I was able to talk my way out of some serious trouble a moment ago. I consider myself lucky that with my hobbies, I haven’t hurt or killed myself yet. And I consider myself lucky for the career I have in wrestling. I may not be a champion, or have any other accomplishments for that matter. But either way, I’m still thankful for it. In every match, I have a great time, and I always go home with my blood pumping. If there’s a god of wrestling he must be smiling down on me. Zack stops at the back of the car, using it to lean up against it in a sitting position. Every week, MWE proves to me why it’s the ultimate thrill spot. Let me tell you, after being here for a few weeks, mayhem doesn’t even begin to describe Monday nights. And speaking of Monday, this week I’m once again in tag team action. It seems to be a pattern since half my matches now have been tag team. Only this time, I’m going up against The Virus and Scotty McNugget with my partner, the newly arrived CSB. Needles to say, I gotta a lot to talk about, so let’s get right into it. It’s time to get down to business. You know the drill by now. Talk smack about your opponent. Build up the partner. But Zack’s promos have always been a little different. So even though you know what’s to come, you still listen, and read, and watch. Why? Because whether you love him or hate him, you can’t deny that Zack is…interesting. First, let’s talk about my partner CSB. Now I’ve heard many things about this guy. And I’m sad to say a lot of it wasn’t too flattering. From what I heard, only one word can truly sum up CSB. Unfortunately, I don’t think the censors will allow us to air it. But I will tell you it rhymes with the word luck. And that’s an example of what we call irony. Get it? Because I was just talking about luck, and the word that describes CSB is– oh never mind. In truth, Zack knows full well that the censors would have no problem with the word in question. The reason he refrains from saying it is out of his own personal convictions. He never was one to use extreme profanity. Though he doesn’t mind it, he himself just doesn’t like using the words. They just don’t seem…cool. He continues, adopting a mock expression of disgust. They tell me CSB is a crude and vulgar man. They say he likes to drink beer and smoke weed, as well as use other illicit substances. He’s immature and crazy and violent. There have even been some implications of homosexuality with his friend Wayne Prez. I’ve heard things about him so horrible, that if I were to repeat them, you would flee in horror. HORROR I SAY!!! And after hearing all these things, you wanna know what I think of the guy? A question. A very serious one in which the answer could have several implications, both negative and positive. Like the equation goes, “every action as an equal and opposing reaction.” An answer. I think I’m going to like the guy. Now the reason I like the guy isn’t just because he knows how to fight or his many accomplishments. Though I will admit, those are reasons why I’m glad to be fighting with him and not against him. The reason I like him goes deeper than that and is based on something far more fundamental. The guy knows how to take a joke. It’s not a particularly admirable quality, nor is it something rare. Many people out there know how to take jokes. But even still, it’s what Zack likes most about the guy. Perhaps it’s the fact that he sees something of himself in the man. Or maybe it’s something different. Regardless, Zack is very happy about his partner both from a professional and a personal standpoint. Now most guys in the back take themselves WAAAAAAY too seriously. They run around, crying and complaining every time something doesn’t go their way. CSB is different. He actually knows how to have fun. And what’s life if not just a game where the person who has the most fun before its over wins. CSB understands that, and I understand that. So I think we’ll get along fine. I may not like his personal choices in life, but hey, that’s none of my business anyway. I mean, I don’t tell people that- (the rest of this statement has been edited out) His face falls. Did he just admit to that live? Oops. He’ll have to make a point to edit that part out later. Remind me to edit that part out later. Anyway, I respect a man who can take the hardships of his life by the horns, give them a swift kick in the nutshack and say, “Screw you!” And CSB has done just that. Where most men would have used their hardships as an excuse, CSB instead used it as a means to push himself. CSB was the last ever GWC Champion. He redefined the GWC Ultimate division. And although people may say, “this isn’t GWC” I don’t think he’ll have any trouble fitting in. Just remember one thing CSB. I’m the Ultimate Thrill Seeker. And I’ll do anything for the thrill. And if in the end that causes us the match…sorry. Zack shrugs. Oh, and as far as he and Wayne are concerned, all I say is, “don’t ask, don’t tell.” Now, on the other hand, one such person who does take themselves too seriously is Scotty McBigMac. Zack stifles a small chuckle. The joke isn’t all that funny. In fact, almost none of Zack’s jokes are funny to anyone but him. But it’s still a small way to get inside the head of his opponent. And even better, it gets a laugh out Zack. If it just happens to have an affect on Scotty’s performance, than that’s just icing on the wrestling cake. Now Scotty McSupersize Ego has been walking around with a big chip on his shoulder every since winning the #1 Contendership for the X-TV title. This worries me. It shows he’s suffering from a severe case of “Open Ass Insert Head” syndrome. This illness is caused when one is so full of themselves they can’t see anything else. Symptoms may include overconfidence, shortness of breath, a prominent losing streak, and diarrhea mouth. For those of you who don’t know, diarrhea mouth is when you spew a bunch of crap from your mouth. Scotty, I congratulate you on becoming #1 Contender for the X-TV Championship. I really do. But you’re making two mistakes here. One, your not concentrating on this week. You’ve got your attention too divided buddy. You’re looking so far toward Crowning Glory, looking so far past the present you can’t see what’s right in front of you. And what’s in front of you is me and CSB. So don’t worry about Riddick. He comes later. Right now, you’d better worry about me and CSB, because if I do crash n’ burn as my name suggest, I’ll take you down with me if you’re not careful. That’s my promise to everyone. I may literally crash sometimes, but I will take ALL of you down with me. And second, your just not having any fun. Don’t lie and say you are because I know you’re not. I can tell in the way you move and the way you talk. You’re too stiff and rigid. And THAT’S why your gonna lose not just to me and CSB, but to Riddick as well. Because you’re going to be thinking about every little thing. And that is when you foul up and make a mistake. You need to be like me. Loosen up. Have fun a little. Don’t worry so much about winning. Let the match just flow at it’s own pace. Let stuff happen as it happens. When you do, believe me, you get better, wrestling becomes a lot more fun, and you can take losing much easier. And trust me, you’ll be needing that quality really soon. It’s rather odd to see Zack give into arrogance. But then again, he’s not really arrogant. He isn’t stupid enough to assume victory before the match begins, especially with his tendencies in a match. It’s again, another mind game. Now my other opponent, and the one I’m even more worried about is The Virus. Now, I don’t know about CSB or anyone else to have wrestled this guy, but I have a very weak constitution. I get sick very easily. So if I have to wrestle this guy and get his Virus germs, I may get sick. I mean like REALLY sick. Like “Oh, I just finished watching High School Musical” sick. And trust me, that ain’t a pretty sight to behold. But now that I think about it, it’s rather ironic that he’s called The Virus. Because whenever the fans see him, that’s exactly what happens. They all get sick and puke their brains out. OOOOH SNAP!!! NO I DIDN’T!!! –finger snap– Other may not get his namesake, but I do. I know why he calls himself The Virus. You see a virus is an organism that seeks a host and soaks up all of its resources until its dry. Then, when the host is most likely dead, it moves onto to the next host. That’s his logic I believe, and I think it fits him. It’s kinda what happened with APWC. He shows up, bled the small fed dry till it was dead, and now has jumped to his new host called MWE. He fancies himself a being someone who goes from one opponent to the next, beating them until their senseless, then moving onto the next. Now doctors say there is no cure for a virus, that they just go dormant, but this is different. This Virus has a cure. And that cure…is me. I am the cure. No, not THAT cure you idiot! I’m the MWE’s cure from you Virus. You and Scotty McFrosty. I’m the cure to all your arrogance that the MWE fans have been waiting for. Because when I step into the ring, the fans know they’re getting a show from a wrestler who actually cares about them. And you can beat me senseless all you want. You’ll tire out before you deplete me of my energy reserve. Remember, I’m like the Road Runner on Red Bull, and your Willy Coyote. You’ll never be able to catch me. Time is running out. He’s about to reach the limit. So Zack decides it’s time to wrap things up in a nice little package. Sorry my friends, but it looks like my time is up. The hour is late and now your Ultimate Thrill Seeker must ride off into the sunset…or sunrise, or moonlight, or whatever. Soon it will be a new dawn. Where will that new dawn lead me? Maybe to a championship? Or maybe to Crowning Glory? I don’t know. But I have a feeling it wherever it takes me, it’ll be one hell of a ride. With the promo done and everything needing to be said told, Zack climbs back into the car. Oh, and just as a little side note. For one final time, Zack lets his engine scream. The night is almost done, but Zack wishes to let it know he is not done with it. Not by a long shot. VIN DIESEL CAN KISS MY ASS! AHHHH HAAAA HAAAA HAAAA!!! Rubber squealing, Zack pulls out of the driveway, spins around and drives off with the skill of a professional driver. As he does, a small stint of clouds part, letting forth a single ray of moonlight and stars shine down on Zack, guiding his path up the road and toward the new dawn slowly crawling over the horizon. –FADE– ...................................................................................................................................................................................................... Obey the rules of the road...OR ELSE!!! Also, leave a lot of praise for this RP. The guy who writes this has a pretty fragile ego. It's kinda lame, but help him out. Anyway, see ya next time.
