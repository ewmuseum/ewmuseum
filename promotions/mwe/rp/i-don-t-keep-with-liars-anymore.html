I Don't Keep With Liars Anymore

Dec 4 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
QUOTE Zak CSB Anna-Lee Douchbag Doctor As I stepped out of the shower after the best fucking tag team match I’d ever put on, I was approached by a member of the MWE Board of Directors. I’d tell you his name, but to be honest they all look the fucking same to me. A suit, tie, and shit-eating grin doesn’t differentiate you from the rest of the paper-pushers. So here I was, basking in the glory of my win, and this guy makes an amazing night even better. He tells me that I have the match I’ve been wanting for months. He tells me that I finally get a one-on-one match with The Virus, at the pay-per-view, for the US title. He also proceeds to tell me that there is some lame ass gimmick involving glass and tables, but I could give a fuck less. All that matters is I get a chance to shine, to have the gold around my waist. At this point, nothing can bring me down. I’m on cloud nine and I haven’t even hit the blunt. CSB walked into the room at that point, grinning even though he had lost. It was a great sight to see though, and a great feeling to have. The fact that we can go out there, talk so much shit about each other, beat the daylights out of each other and in the end still have this kind of bond…its rare. ”See dude…I told you that the first match was just luck, and that I let you win so you wouldn’t look like such a bitch in front of your son.” “Yeah yeah, the more you talk, the less I listen, haven’t you realized that yet?” This was what I liked about CSB. I didn’t have to be someone I wasn’t, I could be myself and he was all that different. ”Hey, I know it’s kinda late, but I was thinking about catching a quick flight to Boston, partying it up a bit to celebrate my return to glory. Since that’s your neck of the woods, I figured you could recommend somewhere, ya know? Maybe even come out and grab a drink.” ”If it were anyone else, I’d probably make some excuse…but yeah, sounds good. There’s this place called The Fire, everyone around town knows it and its pretty close to the airport. I figure we have a 3 hour flight, gimme time to shower and I’ll meet you there around 1?” ”Three hours? Still flyin commercial I see….maybe you can borrow my G5 sometime so you can see what flying is really like.” The slap that he gave was expected, and thus didn’t hurt like I thought. He grabbed his shit and left, and I did the same. I swear, if I didn’t have my own plane I probably wouldn’t be in the business. Flying commercial is horrible and I wouldn’t wish that on anyone. ANYWAYS, there I was, fresh off my win, flying high in more ways than one. What is there to do on a plane ride really, other than talk shit about the man who’s title I would be taking come Wrestle Wars? I had my trusty camera, and nothing but time so I figured why the hell not. People were used to my little single-camera productions by now, and while they weren’t on the production levels of say Brad Kane or CSB, they still got the job done. “So it looks like management finally wised up, and gave me the match I so rightfully deserve, and that the fans want to see. Fuck GWC vs. MWE, fuck Sinc retiring, fuck Paige and her carpet munching. Ever since I came here to MWE I have been waiting for a one on one match with Leon Roberts, and I finally have it. For months, he has gotten out of the match and it was starting to piss me off. When I finally did get my hands on him, it was either in a tag team match, or a three way. Not once did he face me by himself, and that’s why the fans have been deprived. There have been some great rivalries, but I don’t know how many can match the pure and utter brutality you will see when we step in the ring. I know most of you are going to tune in, expecting some sort of comedy. You wanna hear weed jokes, see me smoke a blunt, and damage some sort of property. Well I hate to disappoint the fans but this isn’t like that. This is serious business and I need to prove that life isn’t just a giant joke. You would think that I proved it with the matches with Kevin, or ending Sonny’s career, but there are still those who see me as nothing more than a two-bit stoner who likes to beat the shit out of people." Yeah, i can be serious once in a while. People think that All I'm capable of doing is being the funnyman, and that is where I catch them off guard. I don't need fancy lights to be serious, I don't need to be sitting in an empty arena to get my point across. All I need are my words, my thoughts, and a fucking camera. "The hatred between Leon and I is no joke. We might have been able to co-exist in the ring last week, but that doesn’t mean we like each other. Some people already know the history between me and him, spanning a number of places. The only two that really matter though are MWE and GWC though. APWC was a joke and I was pretty much hated by the owners there so I tend to forget about that place as much as possible. What people may not realize though, is that when it comes to one on one matches, I fucking own him. I have knocked him out, made him tap, and pinned his shoulders to the mat. I have won titles from him, and kept him from holding gold on a number of occasions. He hasn't beaten me in over a year and he won't beat me at Wrestle Wars. Now the casual fan might sit here and go “Wait a minute Mr. Warner, didn’t he beat you at the last Pay-Per-View”? And at first glance, you would think that the person would be accurate with that statement but they are actually quite wrong. Yes, it is true that Leon got the win, but he didn’t BEAT me. He caught a belt as it was falling through the air, thanks to Brad Kane. Did he pin my shoulders to the mat for the three count? No. Did he make me tap out? Fuck no, I don’t tap to bitches. Did he knock me out? HA you must be higher than me to think that it could happen like that. I’ve been knocked out ONCE in my career and it was because Brad Kane decided he wanted to kick my fucking head in." It's a damn shame that Brad isn't with the company anymore, but this isn't about that. It's slightly amusing to me though..My words are venomous, yet i say them with a smile. You would read the words I speak and expect my voice to be full of anger and fury, but they remain calm and even. "So don’t delude yourself Leon. You might have won the match but you didn’t beat me. Is that why you have avoided this match for so long? Have you known deep down inside that you can’t beat me, and you just haven’t admitted it? Is it because you know I don’t fear you like the others do? You know I don’t believe your little façade, and it scares the shit out of you. You know that in order to be feared, people have to believe your little devil act and I haven’t from the day I met you. Neal Donald Walsch one said “FEAR is an acronym in the English language for False Evidence Appearing Real” and you are the personification of that. There isn't a single thing about you that is real. Explain one little thing to me Leon…how am I supposed to fear that which I don’t believe. I have never believed in a heaven nor a hell, no god or devil. In my mind, those do not exist so how should I fear that which isn’t? You can use all the little tricks you want, and have all the friends stick up for you false image, but it doesn’t bother me. You are just like me Leon. You think, you breathe the same air I do, you need food to live, and you bleed just like any other mortal. You hold no power over me, and you never have. You didn’t back in GWC when I knocked you out and won the Underground Title. You were powerless to stop from tapping out when I took the TV title right out from under your greedy little hyper-masculine paws." I couldn't help but to grin into the camera as I spoke. No man likes his masculinity to be called into question, let alone a professional athlete. I needed to hit him where it hurt though, because he needed the reality dose. "What is it that makes you feel like you have to parade around as some sort of super-male? Why do you feel the need to brag about your sex life with your girl like people actually care? It’s as if you are trying to convince people that you are more than you seem to be. I’m sure people like D.C. and GM get bitches any time they want, but they don’t walk around bragging about it. Hell, I could have three different bitches on my dick right now but it wouldn’t prove anything. All it does is make you see like there is one of two problems with yourself. Either you like to swallow the man-meat and just won’t admit it, or you have an abnormally small wang and don’t want it to get out. Personally, I would peg you as the man-meat lover, but I won’t judge you but I will say that you shouldn’t have to hide who you are behind a curtain of lies." I would like to take this time to point out that I have absolutely nothing against the homosexual community. I support any lifestyle choice that a man or woman makes, and I fully believe that love is love, regardless of gender. That doesn't mean that I won't crack a joke or two. Hell, I made fun of DeAngelo daily for his race and it was all in jest. "You walk around the locker room like you have some sort of divine power, and you have a swagger about you as if we should all fear you in some way. Yet I have always been a firm believer that Power isn’t made or taken, it is given. In order for you to have power over someone, they must grant it to you. It’s why Democracy exists in the country. We elect a leader, thus giving him the power to dictate how we live. So tell me Leon, who here has granted you power? Is it because you hold that piece of gold and leather around your waist, that you have deluded yourself into thinking you actually matter? Sorry to burst your bubble Leon, but that title lost it’s value as soon as a hack like you called yourself champ. You got that belt because there was nobody else available at the time, and you still have it based on pure luck. Who have you beat since you had that title? Who has there been that has actually been able to provide a legitimate challenge? It’s no secret that MWE is going through a tough time, but it doesn’t help when people like you are holding a title. Can you blame fans for not wanting to tune in each week? How can you when they are forced to watch your half-toned skills go up against people like Blight?" Now my voice was raised. Now the emotion was flowing. The one thing I hated above all else, was anything that made a mockery of the business and I couldn't help that I thought that Leon was the epitome of everything negative in the business. "The future of MWE has already been decided. It was decided the moment Zack Crash, CSB, and myself signed our names on the contract. The sad thing is, you aren’t part of those plans. You are becoming nothing more than a joke in the industry, and while your little act might have gone over well in backyard companies like APWC, you need to sit back and accept the fact that MWE isn’t the place for you. When you go crashing through that glass, as the shards pierce your skin and you feel the blood rushing out of your body, you will know that I’m right. I still haven’t figured out why you accepted this match. Maybe you want the fans to care about a match of yours for once. Maybe you think that just like so many others, that I will make you famous. If that’s the case, then you are quite right. I plan on making the name Leon Roberts synonymous with failure, defeat, and embarrassment. I plan on pulling back your curtain, and exposing you for the fraud you are. It’s time for the illusion to dissipate and the man known as Leon to appear. It will be like the time you found out that it wasn’t Santa putting the presents under your tree. Your façade will be dismantled, and I will take pride in being the one to do it." As sick and twisted as it sounded, I meant every word I said. There was pure hatred in my voice and I wasn't going to sit here and mask it. I hate the man, I hate the lies he speaks, and I wanted to end him. I leaned forward lowering my voice to just above a whisper. "Now don’t get it twisted, I want “The Virus” to show up to Wrestle Wars. I want the so-called “Demon Prince” to step into the ring, so I can watch a broken and battered man be carried out on a stretcher. I want this to be your final dance, as we waltz under the pale light of the moon as shards of broken glass, blood, and tears rain down around us. It won’t be enough for me to beat you Leon. I want to hurt you, I want you to BEG for it to end. I want to make you tap out, knock you out, and drive you through every single breakable surface that the arena has to offer. When I’m done with you Leon, death will seem like a luxury, and you can finally reside in the hell that you so dream of. You won’t be bothered by mere mortality any longer, and you can once more feel like you actually matter. There has been talk that this might be the last card MWE ever puts on. If that is the case, then I plan on going out with the biggest bang possible. If this is the end, then so be it. If this is the last time that Zak Warner steps inside an MWE ring, I promise you all that I will be walking out as the last US champion. At Wrestle Wars, it will be one last fix, one more syringe of Ivory. So be ready to shoot up my addicts, and I promise you that it will be the best high of your life.” I reached over and click off the camera, having just recorded what I feel is the single best promo I’ve ever given. It didn’t have the pure, raw emotion that the ones for Kevin did, but there was just something about this that pleased me. I had a bit of time to kill, so I reclined my seat, kicked up my feet, and dozed off into a blissful sleep with a grin on my face as wide as Demonica’s baby maker. Now, I could sit here and describe the mindfuck known as my dreams, but I don’t really remember most of them so I’ll just fast-forward a couple hours. I was jerked awake by the feeling of the plane landing on the runway, and it took me a moment to remember what I was doing. I stumbled groggily out of the plane, where I had a driver waiting for me. Yeah, I have my own driver, so what wanna fight? ANYWAYS, I hopped in and told him to get me to The Fire, and off I went. It was around 1 o clock at this time, at there was still a line outside the club. One of the perks of being me though, was that there was no such thing as a line. I walked right past everyone, getting cursed at and glared at but I didn’t care. The lights were dim, the girls were hot, and my drinks were strong. It was going to be a good night. Surprisingly enough, not many people were approaching me for autographs which was a nice change of pace. Yeah, I had to sign a few but it was nothing like a regular night. One fan stood out in particular and I’m pretty sure it had to do with her fat ass, double D’s, and lips that looked like they could suck a basketball through a garden hose. She introduced herself as Anna-Lee, and asked if I wanted to dance. I accepted her offer, begrudgingly of course, and for the next half hour our bodies were closer than CSB and Wayne. It didn’t matter if the song was fast, slow, or a Lil Wayne beat, our bodies were connected. I’d go into details, but then I would be no better than Leon. All I will say is that at one point, we were in a bathroom stall, her legs were over my head, and I found out what it was like to hear a girl scream in Romanian. Yeah, I fucked a Romanian chick, be jealous. We walked out of the bathroom, her makeup just a bit smudged and my hair just a bit tussled. It was at that point that some dude came up to me looking a bit pissed off. “Anna-Lee, who the fuck is this? I’ve been looking for you for the past hour and a half. I thought you had gone outside for a smoke or something, but you didn’t answer your phone.” “Oh I’m sorry baby, I got talking to some friends, and then I saw Zak here and I just had to say hi.” “Who the fuck is Zak, and why is he anywhere near you?” It’s probably vital that I mention that this dude was 6’6”, and had to have about a hundred or so pounds on me. It was like Mark fucking McGwire’s steroids had taken steroids. It takes a lot for me to be intimidated, but I had to admit I was slightly worried at this point. “Baby, relax…it’s Zak Warner, you know the wrestler from MWE?” ”I told you I don’t like you watching that fake ass shit, let alone talking about it around me. And that still doesn’t answer my question. What the fuck were you doing with my girl?” Obviously that last part was directed at me, but I didn’t care. This man was disrespecting my life, and his size no longer mattered. Call me a stoner all you want, make fun of my drug use till you turn blue in the face, but never disrespect the industry I work in. Yes, it’s not as real as MMA or Basketball but we still put our bodies on the line each and every night we step inside the ring. ”First off, I’d appreciate it if you refrained from calling what I do fake. And secondly, I was simply signing an autograph for this lovely young lady.” ”I’ll call whatever I want fake. You don't even act all that well when you do wrestle so I don't see how people like you.” This dude was obviously drunk, but so was I. That’s the problem with fucking random girls in bathrooms, they always seem to have boyfriends. Now at this point, the smart thing to do would be to sign a napkin, smile, and walk away. But really folks, when have I ever been smart? ”Listen dude, I don’t come to your corner, slap the dick out of your mouth and tell you that I could do your job better because I’m just not that delusional. So walk away and this can all be over." It wasn’t the right time to quote Mr. Show, but it seemed like it at that moment. What can I say, I had a belly full of liquid courage, I had just nailed his girlfriend, and I was in a fighting mood. Unfortunately for me, I had decided to pick a fight with a guy would could clearly crush me. At this point, I felt his hands on my shoulder, and out a window I went. Ironic, isn’t it? A couple weeks before I’m in a glass table match, and here I am getting thrown through a window. Had I not been absolutely hammered, it probably would have hurt a hell of a lot more. The next thing I know, here comes Mr. Needle in his Ass out the door with a look of murder on his face. He goes for a swing, and instinct kicks in for me as I roll to the side. Even drunk, I have quite a bit of agility. The man had size on me, but I had experience and speed on my side. I was able to dodge his punches with ease, but my hits didn’t even faze the guy. Ever seen that Rocky movie where Apollo fights that big ass Russian dude? It was pretty much like that except I wasn’t dead. I was starting to tire though, and it wasn’t good. His punches started to land, and I started to bleed quite a bit. By now, quite a crowd had assembled and was cheering on. Some were for him, some were for me, and some were just enjoying the violence. Each time I got hit, I would stumble into someone who would just push me right back. It was like a mosh pit from hell but there wasn’t any music. There were just fists, blood, and a shitload of pain. This fight needed to end, and it needed to end SOON. I knew it wasn’t going to end well if it continued, so I did the first thing that came to mind. I grabbed some guys bottle, and swung it as hard as a could and watched it shatter. His eyes rolled into the back of his head, and he dropped to the ground. The fight was over, and I now owed somebody a beer. It would have to wait though, because I wasn’t in the mood to do anything but sleep for days. I started to stumble through the crowd, when out of the blue I felt a sharp fucking pain in my lower back. It was like Kimbo Slice had punched me in the kidneys, but worse. I felt something warm start dripping down my leg and my hand instinctively reached back to the pain and whem I drew my hand back there was nothing but blood. Some fucker had shanked me, square in the kidney. Now, if you know anything about basic human function, you know that when you drink, it thins out your blood and you tend to bleed quite profusely. I was once so drunk that a paper cut bled for what seemed like the entire night, so you can imagine what this was like. I couldn’t even make it back to the door before I dropped to my knees as my vision started to go. I don’t really remember much after that, but what I do remember is that the first thing I saw when I looked up was nothing other than Mr. CSB, your faithful MWE Champ. The look of pure, unadulterated terror on his face worried me more than anything, and I knew it had to be pretty fucking bad. I was in the backseat of his car, and he was driving like a bat out of hell. Even in times like this though, I couldn’t resist being typical Zak. "Damn dude…first you fly commercial, then you drive yourself to the bar? You really need to ask for a raise.” ”Nows not really the time for jokes man, you need to get to the hospital and I need to focus on driving, so for both our sakes please shut the fuck up.” I couldn’t argue, didn’t really have the strength. All I could do was lean back, and try my best not to pass back out. ”Sorry bout the blood man, I hear it’s a bitch to get out of the seats.” ”Yeah, well you can pay for the bill once this is all done…” That was all I heard, because at that point the blissful darkness that I had grown to love had once again washed over me. I kept fading in and out, and I remember the bright hospital lights, some ugly ass nurses, and all sorts of machines. I couldn’t hear much, but what I did hear was something that I could have done without. ”So, a couple stitches and he is good to go, right doc?” ”I’m afraid not Mr. Blaise. The puncture wound is quite deep, and has actually caused some irreparable damage to his kidney. He either needs a new one, or he is going to live the rest of his life without it. Unfortunately, there is quite a list of people waiting, and we can’t justify bumping him up on the list, especially with his lifestyle choices.” So there you have it folks. To keep it short and simple, I was fucked. I wasn’t going to die, but I wasn’t exactly in the best situation. Now a normal man could live a full and healthy life with one kidney but I wasn’t exactly a normal man. A normal man didn’t drink or do the number of drugs I did. A normal man didn’t put his body through what I did. It was going to take a miracle sent down by the holy hand of god himself to save me, and considering what I said about her earlier I wasn’t too confident. All I could do was to close my eyes, and for the first time in my sinful, deviant life…I prayed. Fade.
