The inferior follow up; A Grands Mystique RP

Jan 17 2009

The Grand Mystique

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Venue: Madison Square Garden, New York City - before January 12th broadcast of Monday Night Mayhem. Damien: “Did you hear me GM? About Windy?” GM has the same look of total and utter disbelief on his face. He blinks and turns away, unable to quite comprehend the words that Damien had uttered. Damien, the consummate journalist, allowed his eyes to betray the clear intention of getting an exclusive comment of some sort. Damien: “What do you think about it?” GM: “I.....I’m not ready to talk to you about it Damien. Not yet.” GM walks up the steps towards the exit. Damien catches him up but GM makes the first move, poking his finger sternly into his sternum. “Damien, please......” His tone is serious, concerned. ”I’m not prepared to talk about Windy Bridges yet.” And with that, GM jogs up the last few stairs and makes his way out of the legendary arena. * Venue: Chicago, IL. MWE HQ - one week later The floor that MWE owner Richard Covell used as his office suite was fairly busy. Visitors milled, in and out, the elevator pinged indiscriminately to signal arrivals and exit with equal vigour. This was the Grand Mystique’s first visit and he was reluctantly impressed. He knew that Richard Covell enjoyed his wealth and the luxurious design of this floor showed it. From the marble flooring to the leather chairs and sofas, it simply screamed “I’m rich! Whooooooooo!!!” As GM stepped out, he allowed some office staff to bustle past him. He knew from word of mouth that Covell’s secretary had an office at the end of the corridor. What GM noticed about this hallway more than anything was the gold framed pictures that lined one side at head height. These displayed the champions and their titles after each title change in MWE history. From Crowning Glory to Adrenaline Rush, these pictures captured the epic nature of the history and emotion of the moment. GM saw a pair of maintenance staff attaching the latest picture of David Whitman’s title win from Monday Night Mayhem. As he walked past them, GM became ever more determined to get his picture up there someday. A translucent glass divider signals the office that belongs to the secretary of MWE owner Richard Covell. Its’ occupant, a perfectly primmed, middle aged blonde, beavers away, tapping at the keyboard with speed and regularity. GM walks in with complete disregard. The fact that he was masked didn’t surprise her but the fact that he didn’t acknowledge her presence was a significant annoyance that she was reluctantly used to. “Excuse me, Mr Covell is otherwise occupied.” GM places his hand on the door. “Yes, he is going to be talking to me. It’s an urgent matter and I am sure you understand.” “But! I m-” GM ignores the high pitched shrieks of the secretary and walks casually through the double doors. She follows him through. “I, I am so sorry sir. I couldn’t stop him – security is on their way.” From his deep office chair on the other side of the desk, Richard Covell raises his hands in a comforting manner. “It’s fine my dear.” He gets up and gives a quick squeeze of appreciation, of reassurance. “You go and cancel that call to security and then go for a quick cuppa – divert all my calls to Marketing OK?” “Thank you Mr Covell.” She shuffles off meekly, still a little annoyed. Covell allows the thud of the door to reach his eyes heading back to his desk. “I’m not here to get comfy boss.....so don’t ask me to take a seat.” Richard swings his chair around and has a bemused look on his face. “What can I do for you Mr Mystique?” “Next Monday Night Mayhem, I want that wanksta punk, 50 Cent wannabe Sonny Williams in the ring, one on one, with his belt on the line.” Covell’s bemusement turns to a firm gaze. “You can’t do that. You can’t interrupt me and demand matches with who you want. You put two men in hospital in the last two weeks. One of them isn’t even a contracted talent and they are both being looked after on my tab!” Increasingly agitated, Covell becomes a little flush in the face. “Why should I do anything for you except dump your ass in ICW?” GM takes a step closer. “You want me on your show Ricky. You need me on Mayhem. If you aren’t giving me an Intercontinental title shot, make me a better offer.” “Why should I book you at all?” “If you don’t then someone else will go on your tab.” “Don’t threaten me Mr Mystique. I don’t take that at all well.” “No threats, just promises.” GM returns Covells’ icy glare right back at him before turning around and going to leave. After a few seconds of his mind whirring thoughts around his mind, Covell pipes up before GM leaves the room. “Whitman.......Non title.” GM stops and turns around to face his employer. He rubs his stubbly chin and has a contemplative stare that emanates from those dark, intense eyes. “You could have given me anyone, in any type of match Richard. Why choose Whitman – your World Champion of all people?” “Opportunity. You get what you want. A chance to prove yourself, to the fans, to me and to the best wrestler in the business – someone you have never faced. Whitman gets to face a potential challenger to his title and a good workout for his match at Friday Night Overdrive.” “So you get an idea where I stand?” “Exactly.” Covell smiles at the hint of a more relaxed conversation with GM. “And you get to see your World Champion have a half decent match with someone who hasn’t already held the belt and got comfortable in their spot. That will be a nice change.” Deliberately sarcastic, GM smiles insincerely at his boss. “Don’t be cocky boy; otherwise Whitman will take you out quicker than you thought possible.” GM considered what he knew of Whitman. An MWE Hall of Famer, a five time, reigning and defending World Champion and they were just his most recent achievements. He is the definition of Main Event in the MWE. If the match against Antonio DeMarco was important for GM, this may just top it. And then it dawned on him. “What will I get if I win?” “That’s funny.” “Why?” “Most people don’t ask that until after they get a win against someone like David Whitman. He is kind of a big deal and tends not to lose that often.” “I know about Whitman. Answer the question.” “We will deal with it if and when it needs to be dealt with. I don’t like what if’s in my business dealings.” GM takes another step closer to Covell’s desk. “I had Antonio DeMarco beat last Monday until that bitch Sonny Williams got jealous to of seeing me do what he couldn’t to him. That cost me a clean win. But I am still unbeaten Richard and I know that riles you. It must sit very uncomfortably. But sending Whitman to do what you are incapable of won’t help.” “As good as Whitman is, don’t expect that he will get anything less than all out war from me.” “If you beat him, you will get what you deserve.” “That’s a little cliché but I will take it.” GM allows a small smile to pass. Covell’s firm stare doesn’t break despite GM’s attempt at humour. “Enjoy your match Mr Mystique.” Covell gets up as GM turns to leave. * This whole time GM had been thinking so fast that he didn’t quite know what to do next. As he walked slowly towards the the door of this decadent office that Richard Covell had made for himself, GM considered the real reasons why he had been given this chance to face the current World Champion. Surely it must be personal; maybe Covell hoped Whitman would teach GM a lesson. Perhaps Covell hoped that GM would prove himself and go toe to toe with a main eventer, the MWE Legend, the man whose understated approach often made him less obvious than D.C. or Ace Cannon, yet his versatile in-ring skill were second to none and often the difference in his matches. GM knew that his performance at the last Monday Night Mayhem broadcast was already being described as one that was typical of Whitman. In a match with the World title on the line, David Whitman stepped up and won yet again. The fact that was particularly not lost on GM was that in Alejandro Rodriguez and D.C., Whitman had overcome two men that GM had faced, yet not beaten, at the same time. David Whitman got opportunities that GM yearned for and more often than not, he won. The difference between Whitman and the rest of the roster is that while they wanted and hoped, often in vain, for title shots, David Whitman often expected them and seemed to consider the title as more of a right, a given, a possession that would always find its’ way back to him if it was taken. It was a quality that GM had to admire and hate at the same time. * Just before GM reached the door, he turned around. Covell still had both eyes firmly on GM and seemingly refused to relax until he was out of his sight. “I don’t know what your intentions are, Richard but I am going to give Whitman the match of his life at Mayhem. He is going to be grateful that his precious title isn’t on the line. He will want to have D.C. and Rodriguez after him. He will learn to know my name and know that I am the next credible threat to him.” GM pokes a finger in Covell’s direction. “You are looking at your next main eventer. You just don’t want to believe it yet but I plan on making it impossible for you to miss me.” Covell’s tension dissipates from his face. “I wish you luck Mr Mystique. I am glad that you appreciate the challenge. David Whitman is MWE to the core – he represents us wholeheartedly in the ring and he is the best of a truly great group.” “I’m sure; great choice boss.” GM walks out of the room and passes the snide gaze of the secretary, just as the picture of Whitman has been put up. Whitman’s beaming smile shines down on him. He is clutching the belt and the crowd celebrates with him in the background. GM cannot help but think about one day getting the opportunity that he craved - a shot at the World title. He walks out of the busy hallway and decides against taking the lift, instead opting to take the stairs. * As he opens the door and the cold air greets him, he remembers his match in EW against MWE champion D.C. He recalls the match being extremely close and totally natural it felt to main event the show with the biggest name on the roster; its’ most popular character at the top of his game. The crowd totally against you, baying for blood and retribution. Most of all, GM remembered loving it. He begins to jog down the narrow stairwell and he is grateful that it is empty. About halfway down, he pauses, resting on the handrail to catch his breath and pondered – was this destiny? Did he have a guiding eye over his shoulder, pushing him onwards, up in the right direction? Since the falling of GM, which had shocked GM and thrown him, hard off course, away from a guaranteed title shot, he had felt a little out of control. Adrenaline Rush seemed to end that – GM certainly felt like he was back behind the wheel again and was delighted to have a go at David Whitman of all people. GM reached the bottom and placed his hand on the cold, smooth plastic of the door handle that was the exit of the stairwell. On contact, he instantly got the feeling it was truly symbolic. GM had reached the link between his EW past and his MWE future. David Whitman stood between it. While GM felt certain that he was entering a new chapter of his career, he wanted to make a statement while doing so. He knew instantly, that to do it, he had to beat David Whitman. GM entered through the door and stepped into the frenetic activity of the lobby. People walked to and fro, across in all directions. Phones rang, were answered, transferred and picked up again. But GM didn’t hear it at all. Instead he was in the ring at Monday Night Mayhem. His left arm raised by the official. David Whitman is flat on his back, conscious and looking up at the Grand Mystique. The crowd was booing. Almost deafening volume. Yet the rush was more rewarding than adrenaline and it coursed through GM’s veins and arteries, flooding his system more effectively than any possible intoxicant. It was so, so real and yet it was a cold, fresh winter breeze that caused GM to realise he was blinded by the low winter sun outside the building. * Dazed for a second, GM regained his composure and knew that he was in the mood to cut his promo. If he delayed it now, then the impact of what he wants to say may be lost. GM walked away from the busy building and hailed a taxi. He gave the driver his destination and sat back. He tried to relax and focus on something else instead of the faint yet foul stench of sweat that insisted on wafting up his nasal passage. Whitman. He had to think about Whitman above all else. With many sure to believe that GM was going to Mayhem as anything but the overwhelming underdog, he could appreciate the fact that he was not the one expected to win. Having been the sensible pick in pretty much every match since he faced in DC in EW, he felt surprisingly OK about it. He wasn’t about to let it faze him. He was certain that Whitman would not be fazed by the favourites tag. “He probably won’t worry about me at all.” GM allowed himself to wonder if Whitman had even heard of him before. After all, they had only been in the company for a matter of weeks and had never crossed paths. GM knew more about Whitman through word of mouth than anything else. He and his mates, the JESTers had their own stoner comedy internet show, the Top Rope Tavern, which had great reviews and a popular following. GM didn’t think it was that great. Yet while Whitman often played the clown, he approached and participated in his matches with unparalleled respect and professionalism. His style, unique in the MWE was a combination of explosive high flying and solid technical skills, with a set of balls that GM had to admit, matched anyone in MWE. His all around style was a credit to the company that he embodied. GM was determined to give it his all, win or lose. This was a defining point and GM had to leave his mark. GM allows his train of thought to wander off into the future. To Whitman. “I have built him up to be invincible, when he is far from it. He lost to Michaels and Rodriguez in quick succession.” “Sure he has won the belt five times, but that means he lost it four times. He is beatable.” While GM felt it had to be said, there was an element of self doubt that meant GM needed to say it to believe that he had a chance. Then, one last thought crossed his mind, in a flash, gone as quick as it had arrived. David Whitman is a star but....... * GM arrived in the ICW building, just as Koala Typhoon was doing rope running drills. Covered with sweat and his torso, slightly overhanging his trousers, glistens unattractively in the artificial light that fills the room. As a new ally, Koala had been instrumental in the events of the last Mayhem. Koala had come to GM to put that into action and it had worked a treat. And now, GM wanted Koala’s assistance in a plan much less complicated. * “GM!” Koala yells over. He could have been standing there for a while, hidden in the shadows. Koala hops out the ring with surprising agility. “What you doin’ here buddy?” His Canadian drawl and breathlessness made him sound drunk and GM remembered what annoyed him most about Windy Bridges. “I need a favour......I need to cut a promo. Where are the facilities in this place?” Koala shows him to an anonymous blue door on the far side of the room, behind the bleachers. GM steps through and sure enough is exactly what he needs. A television camera. GM has a look around before moving to adjust the lights before picking up a stool. He sets the stool in front of a white and blue background that has small ICW logos diagonally across it, before checking the lens of the camera is properly focussed. GM moves into shot as Koala checks it is OK. He gives a quick thumbs up to GM, which he acknowledges and leaves the room. GM puts his hand to his mouth, coughs a little and takes a deep breath. “David Whitman. Mr Hall of Famer, World Champion of the past, present and future. You are the man; certainly to Richard Covell. You see, I find it a little difficult to respect you as champion when you drop it so often.” “The MWE Undisputed Championship, in its’ various forms and guises, currently in your possession, has been passed around between you and the men that you have faced one hundred times before. D.C., Alejandro Rodriguez, Brian Michaels, Sonny Williams. It’s like a hot potato between you lot.” GM makes an almost playful juggling motion. “And you, the darling of the MWE, Mr Dependable, loved by fans, for being whiter than white, with your never say die attitude. You can’t do anything wrong.” “You don’t dip your toe in the cold water of reality unless it has something to do with the main event or the JESTers. And you know, that makes me kind of sick.” “But now, I have my shot at you. Not your title, just you. This is something I have been waiting for ever since I turned up in MWE. You don’t know me but you know what I am capable of. I want to see your best because I know that as this is non title, you may not want to strain yourself ahead of your big title match at Friday Night Overdrive.” “But I want you to. I want you to come at full tilt, both barrels blazing and leaving nothing to chance. I need to see David Whitman at his best. When we wake up in the morning, sore, bruised and unable to move all that much, I want to know that you left nothing behind and lost fair and square.” GM scratches his nose quickly. There is a glint of clean, white teeth in a small, half hidden smile. “Yes I said lost. You see David, just because you are the man in this company that I am going to lie down for you?” GM pokes his finger and waves it side to side, shaking his head at the same time. “No, no, no. That is not how I do things. You will get all that I have. All may be well in your world now that you have your belt back but my world has been turned upside down.” ”So facing you is an opportunity that I am not about to pass up. This is going to help me get Windy Bridges out of my mind. You have got my full attention. No distractions or interruptions. This match is going to help me clear my mind and make everything right.” GM clenches his fist and pounds it into his palm. ”Your famous “unbreakable spirit” is about to get a journey through a torturous hell at my hands. You will find out exactly what your body can endure and when you think it is going to stop, and your heart tells you to give up, you will allow the Hand of Prophecy to come and end the agony.” A flash of steely determination becomes visible in GM’s eyes. “You aren’t in control of your destiny David, I am. When you look in the stars and see the infinite and absolute power of the galaxy, sparkling back down at you, you will know what the Grand Mystique has brought down to face you at Mayhem.” “The reason for my confidence is simple. You are a star Whitman. Of that there is no doubt at all. But there is something that all stars have in common.” GM stretches his arms outwards and shrugs his shoulders as if the answer is simple. “Death.” GM chuckles. “All stars die, quickly and spectacularly. They are replaced by one bigger and brighter. The Grand Mystique is bringing the Big Bang to Mayhem and it is coming to extinguish the star, the legend, the MWE Undisputed Champion that is David Whitman.” GM hesitates for a second. He considers saying more but doesn't. Instead he steps out of shot. The ICW background is the only thing that can be seen for a few seconds before it is turned off.
