Tick, tock.; Not so much a threat akshully.

Sep 5 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The scene opens to Aeron standing in between two abandoned cobblestone buildings, an eerie green moss stuck on the walls. The area was damp and dingy and gave off an aura of antiquity. Grass had grown from the floors but without much sun being where they were died quickly. The sound of the wind howling through the windows and the grass is all that can be heard of course besides that of Aerons own unintelligible muttering. The cobblestone building to the left was all but a humble store. The old driftwood sign still covered in moss was somewhat readable, of course if you can read Gaelic that is. Inside a metronome that wasn't taken with whoever it was that was there before. Tick, Tock, Tick, The other building was a simple souvenir shop. The door was made out of what seemed to be dead tree branches tied together and attached with a hinge made of what seemed to be a rusted cut-in-half can of soup or something alike. Whatever it was, it was rusted, metal and probably loaded with tetanus. Another sign but not made out of driftwood, this was made out of what Aeron had presumed to be one of the cut down trees. Still, it too was in Gaelic. Aeron is seen walking up to the souvenir shop. He stopped and looked around before kicking the door in. Shelf's, old stupid things like, straw dolls, what seemed to be a more premature version of an ant farm. He walked up to what seemed to be a locked chest, Aeron grabbed it and pried it open with his bare hands. Coins clattered to the ground as Aeron sighed and started picking some up. He walked back out. Tick, Tock, Tick, The metronome continued on as Aeron walked away. The green plains rolled up and down the hills, sheep baaing gleefully everywhere. Aeron looked around and stop and sat in the grass. Aeron: So lets recap on my opponents.We got, James Sync, and the air-headed Aiden Copaine. I think I'll start with, Mr.Moneybags. Yeah, Aiden Copaine. Do you sir actually think that flashing your cash is actually going to do anything? Showing us "your" luxury mansion and all "your" cars and that suit you "bought" for that guy a few weeks ago. You know, I did like your house, but I also liked it when I seen it on MTV Cribs with..who was it again? Aeron contemplates for a second as he lays back in the grass, the wind still blowing as if it was following them. The sheep still baaing in the background. He points his finger in the air. Aeron: Fiddy Cent, that was it. "Fifty Cent"s luxury mansion. Everybody does realize those are just sets they let you use for promotional purposes. OH and that suit, that Armande or what ever, did anyone see any of that shmutz on the collar? It looks like it was rented beforehand. I think Im just about done with you, oh and before I move on...BRUSH YO TEEF. I know mine are bad. Aeron smiles a sadistic and twisted smile revealing large black and yellow chipped teeth. Aeron: But at least mine are fake... Aeron chuckles to himself as he takes off the mouth-guard type of fixture in his mouth with the fake teeth that overlapped his other teeth. Aeron: But seriously, you had some spinach in your teeth last week. Aeron scratches his teeth as if he was attempting to pick something out of it. He leans back and laughs heartily as he looks back at the camera. Aeron: Back to seriousness, James Sync. The "Resident Rock star". There isn't much to say about you really, but I'll tell you one thing, you come into MWE, your coming into the house of pain. This wont be an easy feat for you good sir. Cause on stage is your turf, where you rule but here in MWE, wrestling...the X-Television championship, that is MY turf..My belt is staying around MY waist whether you like it or not..Simple as that. Aeron got back up and put his hand into his pocket fidgeting trying to pull something out. His cellphone, he opened it up and phoned one of his friends staying at a local pub. Aeron taps his foot on the ground impatiently. ???: Y'ello. Random Voice:Aeron was calling Dusty, an old friend he was visiting before Monday night Mayhem. You guys remember him right? The local drunk? Yeah... Him. Aeron: Hey, Dusty Dusty: Yeah man? Aeron: Send me a cab to...oh, fuck where am I.... Aeron stops for a second and, heard from Dusty's phone is Aeron cussing under his breath. Aeron: The two old buildings with uhh....Damnit Aeron stops again and footsteps are heard as he walks closer to the buildings. Aeron: The old Gaelic shops. Dusty: Sure thing bro. Aeron: Thanks, Aerons' phone beeps as he closes it. He sits on an old log that was placed right beside the road. He sighs with his head in his hands as he waits for the cab.A few minutes later a car beep is heard. Cab driver: Allo, are you the one I was sent here for? I had a hard time finding you, there arnt any Gaelic shops anymore, but there were a few here loong ago. Aeron raised his eyebrow as he look back and shrugged. Aeron: I hope so. Cab driver: Good, where to? Aeron: Uhh... Earls Teck Pub. Cab driver: Okay. Aeron opens the passenger side door and steps in and closes it. The cameraman gets forgotten. The sound of wind is heard as the car drives off with the sun reflecting off a large body of water, the metronome still heard. Tick, Tock, Tick, The wind howls as the camera mans to where the two buildings were before. Engulfed in a dense fog, the sound of the metronome gradually slows down. Tick... Tock... Tick.... Nothing is heard, the sound has stopped, the wind still howling, the two buildings that were engulfed in fog are no longer there, all that stood in their places were two old trees, each with what seemed to be nooses. The camera pans closer, each noose had a peice of wood with scrawlings on them. The first one had said "Aiden Copaine, wipes his ass with gold TP" the second had said "James Sync, Rockstar failure, forgot to turn on his amp". A peice of paper blows by as it gets stuck to the camera, FIN.
