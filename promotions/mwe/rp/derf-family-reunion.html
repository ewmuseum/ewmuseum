Derf Family Reunion; ...Or is it? Derf VS Micheals

May 28 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
As the scene opens, all that can be seen from a birds eye view is yellow. Long, yellow fields of straw stretch out for miles as far as the eye can see. The only thing not covered in yellow is a long dirt road, and an old wooden water tower in the middle of a field. With the sun out full force, and a slight breeze in the air, the straw waves back and forth lightly, but it all moves quickly as a bright red convertible drives by, causing dirt and gravel to fly into the air behind it. Inside the car, we see Eric Derf in the drivers seat, one hand on the wheel, the other at his side, possibly changing gears, or maybe trying to find something. Who knows. He's dressed in a light blue flannel shirt under a pair of jean over-alls, with a pair of aviator sunglasses blocking the bright sun from his eyes. The radio is playing "On The Road Again" as Derf spits out the window and continues drive down the dirt road. It looks like a scene out of "The life of a Hick" Eventually, as Derfs driving, he begins slowing down, as a large wooden house can be seen getting closer and closer, until the car is right in front of the driveway. He parks on the side of the road because the driveway is already filled with multiple cars, ranging from pick up trucks to Lamborghini, and even one tractor. As he steps out of the car, he stretches his arms out and yawns, before spitting into the field as he lowers his arms. Yeeeeeeeeeeeeeeaaaaaaaaaahhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhh........ it's good to be home... He reaches into the backseat of the car and pulls out a large blue cooler. Without a cover, the contents - beer- can be seen as plain as day.He begins walking at a fast pace towards the house. As he gets closer, multiple different voices can be heard coming from inside, most of them quite loud and rowdy. He takes a step onto the front porch, which makes a loud "Creek" sound with each step. Finally at the door, he reaches out to knock, but slowly lowers his hand towards the doorknob, thinking that he's expected anyway. He twists the knobs and swings the door open... Everything has gone blank. No noise, no movement, nothing. Pure Darkness. Still nothing... Suddenly, voices can be heard. Multiple voices can be heard, but they're all whispers, not loud enough to make out what they're saying. Also, while the scene is still dark, there can be some shadows made out, however, it's still to dark to tell what's going on, other then we're now in an area that has at least three or four people around. Then out of nowhere, a huge flash of light engulfs what is revealed as a large auditorium in the form of a giant video screen. While the large white screen at the front of the room is the main focus in the room, it can now be seen where the voices were coming from. The three large sections of seats all face a stage at the front of the room. As far as the camera can see, every seat in the room is filled, most of the people filling them look like they all just came from a business meeting. All the men wearing their suits and ties, all the woman with their hair up, long skirts, and dressy blouses. It's like a giant office meeting. The whispers become quieter as the screen lights up, but they continue at a low mutter. Until... As the video plays, Eric Derf appears from behind a curtain, dressed in a blue suit and a red tie, he's just as proper looking as the men in the seats. He walks towards the middle of the stage with a microphone in his hand, and slows down to a complete stop when he gets there. The look on his face is a rarely seen one, the look of seriousness. This is clearly not good news for the usually upbeat superstar. The music has now stopped playing, with a plain image of the MWE logo now taking up the screen. The room is now just as quiet as it was before Derf came out, with only a few whispers making their way through the crowd of people. Finally, after scanning the crowd, Derf finally speaks, his voice in a plain, boring tone. First of all, I'd like to make an apology. To the superstars in the back, to the fans of MWE, and to all of you in this crowd. What you just saw was the beginning of the promo I'd been planning on using against Brian Micheals for our match. It was going to be a comical promo, in which I would have myself dressed at several other people, playing my family members. It would have given the fans a good laugh, which is usually my main goal here. Some of the boys backstage were really looking forward to that promo, so I'd like to say I'm especially sorry to them. However... The anger I hold towards Brian Micheals is too much for me to handle. I can't cut a comedy promo against a man that has done so much wrong. I can't try to smile while talking about the man that broke into my home and attacked me from behind. I can't even begin to think about having fun knowing he's out there, making other people miserable like he has done to me and so many others. Eric Derf paces back and forth on the stage, one hand holding the microphone up, while the second hand rests comfortably looking in his pants pocket. He continues speaking, still in a serious tone. All of you people out there, consider yourselves extremely lucky. You don't know who Brian Micheals is. Hell, you don't even know who I am, or for that matter, any professional wrestlers. I have gathered you individuals here together to prove a point to the world. None of you watch pro wrestling, you all watch other TV shows. Or no TV at all, but the main point is, you don't know me, or Brian Micheals. And what I'm here to do tonight, is inform some of you about the kind of man Brian Micheals is. I want to inform the non wrestling public just how bad this man is, hoping that some of you will see the wrong he has caused and not let your children watch our product while a man like Brian Micheals is a part of it. Brian Micheals attacked me in my own home, putting me in a coma for a week. We'd never even spoken more then two words to each other, yet he attacked me. He also attacked two of his students, injuring them so bad they were forced into retirement, both of them not even at their primes. He also has a tendency to sexually harass women, telling them to "suck his cock" on multiple occasions. Even last week, he sexually harassed the former wife of a fellow superstar, kept her trapped inside a room with him, and then knocked her on her face when she tried to escape. If that wasn't bad enough, he'd also just revealed she was pregnant to a small group of her students. And even before all of that happened, he took a mentally handicapped female out of a classroom, and locked her in a locker, with future plans of having sex with her. I mean seriously. Sex with a mentally handicapped girl? She wasn't even legal age! What kind of a man assaults a pregnant woman? His friends? His co-workers? I'll tell you what kind of man does that... No man does that. He stops walking when he says that, standing near the left side edge of the stage. He's looking strait down into the eyes of someone in the front row as he speaks, as if speaking to Micheals himself. The man simply stares back, not sure what to think. No one can do any of that stuff and call themselves a man afterwards. It's not just right. Hitting woman alone is the most pathetic thing a human being can do, and the fact that he knocked a pregnant woman on her face makes him a worm, not a man. Sir, would you ever hit a woman? He stands at the edge of the stage and holds the microphone out towards the man he was looking at while still staring at him. The man stalls for a moment before replying. Umm, well no... Eric quickly stands back up and walks a few feet towards the middle of the stage before holding the mic to another guy. And you sir? No, I'd never hit a woman. And you? No, that's just wrong. This man seems like he should be in jail! Eric nods in agreement with the man before he stands back up and walks towards the middle of the stage, satisfied with the answers he got, although still obviously upset. No man would ever hit a woman, or do any of the things he does to woman... But those woman aren't the only people he hurt. In addition to harassing multiple other woman, he's also attacked some of his co-workers, attempting to end their careers. I was one of those men... I was lucky though. My injury only kept me sidelined for a week. However, not everyone was so lucky. Two of our younger bright talents had the world in front of them. Known as Mr. Markowitz and Triple G, they were taken in by Micheals and shown the ropes of the business. He taught them well, but as soon as he realized there was a chance someday they might surpass him, he stopped them. He beat them down, and he knocked them out. He severely injured two innocent young men, simply because he was afraid of them. Afraid that these two men, who were no wheres near as talented in the ring as he was, that they might someday surpass him. But he never completely finished the job... Derf pauses for a moment, looking around the crowd as the people continue watching. Most of them don't appear to even be interested in what's going on, themselves not being wrestling fans. Recently, I had a chance to speak to Mr. Markowitz about how he's been doing since he was permanently taken off the roster. I spoke to him about his life, his injuries, all kinds of stuff. And I want all of you to hear about his story. So ladies and gentleman, please welcome Mr Markowitz! Eric motions towards the left side of the stage as the former MWE Superstar, Mr Markowitz, makes his way towards Eric. He's dressed in jeans and a button up white stylish shirt, quite different then the rest of the room. His hair is pack in a pony tail. The two meet up in the middle of the stage and shake hands, even giving each other the half hug as they do so. The two separate, as Mr Markowitz stands with his thumb tucked into his pocket, and a large scar on his forehead, probably the result of Brian Micheals attack. Mr Markowitz, it's nice to see you. How are you feeling? I'm feeling pretty depressed actually. You see, a bunch of things have been going bad in my life lately, and to top it all off...I get injured by someone who I thought was a friend. Well I think I can speak for everyone backstage that no one saw it coming, and that we all wish you were still around to compete. It's a damn shame your career was cut short. By the way, some of us were wondering, what exactly happened anyway? What set off Micheals to attack you two the way he did? Well Eric...I believe he attacked me out of fear. He believed that Triple G and I, were a threat to him. What he did to me....he kicked me. He punched me. He elbowed me, He kneed me, He even hit the Ace of Spades on me. He....so many things. I could barely walk for 2 weeks! Eric nods in agreement, with a disappointed look on his face. That's awful, just awful. What about Triple G? What did he do to him? Pretty much the same. Except....except he took a bit of pity on G. I mean.....he is much weaker than I. Yeah, that's true... I just have one more question. If you had the chance to look Brian Micheals in the face, what would you say to him? Well Eric... I wouldn't say anything. I would point up, distracting him, then knee him in the nuts. A few people in the crowd chuckle at this, the most sound they've made the whole time so far. I couldn't agree more. Thanks for coming out tonight and telling these people your story. Thanks for having me.... Oh and one more thing. Kick... the living crap... out of Brian Micheals. Derf and Markotiwz exchange another handshake/hug before Markowitz exits the stage without even waiving towards the people. A few claps can be heard from the people in the seats, but are quickly cut off when the people clapping realize they're alone doing so. Eric notices the clapping and turns around with a small smirk on his face, the first sign of happiness he's shown since appearing on the stage. This has clearly made him feel like his cause might be worth fighting for. Well, it's nice to know some of you are taking an interest in my point tonight. But theres more to the problem that is Brian Micheals. It's not just the attacks on innocent people that make Brian Micheals the worm he is, it's the- He stops mid sentence as he can hear coughing in the audience. At any normal event, people cough, and it usually goes unnoticed. Derf notices however, and looks across the sea of faces, trying to eye the person that did it. After looking around for a moment, he walks towards the edge of the stage, and hops off. As he hits the ground below, a few people begin whispering in confusion, trying to figure out what's going on. Derf walks past several rows of people, looking back and forth, trying to find someone. Finally, he stops walking next to a well build man in a fine white suit. Excuse me sir, was that you that interrupted me? The man, looks around, as if there was a possibility that Derf was looking at someone else, before answering an unexplainably annoyed Derf. Umm, No, I nev- Don't interrupt me, jackass! Derf's interruption came quite abrupt, which caused a few people to gasp. One person not gasping however is the man in white, who gets out of his seat with an ugly scowl on his face and stands eye to eye with Derf. Technically he actually towers over the superstar. It's quite clear this isn't the kind of guy you'd like to mess with, so one has to wonder what the hell just happened to Derf. Yeah, I called you a jackass. What are you going to do about it, pussy? You won't hit me, you pa- Before Derf can finish his unexplained insult, the man pushes Derf backwards, causing him to spill over the seat behind him, ass over head, and into a crowd of other people. As Derf slowly gets up, security officers rush towards the large man in white, latching onto his arms when they reach him. The man puts up a small amount of resistance, but doesn't fight it. Stop! Let him go. The security officers stop struggling with the large, now confused man, who pulls his arm out of their grasp in a quick, forceful motion. He gives them a "fuck off" look before turning around and seeing Eric Derf standing in the isle, acting as cool and calm as can be. Just standing there, acting like nothing had happened, like he didn't freak out, like he never got knocked onto his ass. Sir, you may take your seat, and I thank you. The man flashes Derf a confused face, but ultimately takes his seat as Derf makes his way back to the stage. About 10 feet from it, he gets a bit of a run going before hopping off one foot, and landing on the stage with his other, not even using his hands. He walks back to the center of the stage and looks into the crowd. The large man is now whispering something towards the man behind him, probably something along the lines of "WTF?" Sir, I would like to say I am sorry for that outburst, and I do not mean any of what I just said. I do not even know you, and I should not have done that. I hope you can accept my apology. Derf holds the microphone down to his side as he stares towards where the large man is standing. At first, the large man looks around at the people surrounding him, but he then looks towards the stage, and gives a simple "nod" of approval, signaling he accepts the apology. Now, I'd like to explain my outburst there. You see, Brian Micheals tends to do that alot. But the thing is, he usually gets away scotch free for it. Wether it's the man he pretended was his father to get places in his career, his fellow employees, or just some random people he meets, Brian Micheals is a dick. He don't care who you are, he will flat out rip you apart if you insult him in the slightest way. If it were Brian Micheals up here when someone sneezed, he'd go out there, and probably attempted to insult the person responsible. Simply, because he is a dickhead. I did what I just did in the hopes that he will watch this tape, see what happens when you attack someone bigger then you, and think of the consequences. I doubt he will though. He's a rude, selfish, ego-maniac. He doesn't care about anyone but himself really, and he has no problem attacking other people physically or mentally just to get his way. He's got so much extra ego, he's even telling people his promo this week will be the best ever. As if anyone would even care to read the promo of a man who thinks himself then Jesus himself... He doesn't care about family values, his friends, anyone. The man even went to the funeral of his own mother, and used that upsetting occasion to cut a promo on his own brother. A day meant to remember a loved one, and he verbally attacked his brother and father, only to reveal several days later he wasn't even related. He was merely using the man to fulfill his selfish desires. He lied about his whole life, just to get places in his career. He lied about his life, and caused pain and suffering to those involved in it, just to get places in his career. And he would gladly take out each and every one of you, just to- Derf is interrupted once again, only this time it's not by a cough, but by a young man in the second row standing up and shouting something at Derf, shouting loud enough so the room can hear him. Excuse me... What exactly are you telling us all this for? None of us watch wrestling, none of us know who this Brian Micheals is, or even you for that matter. Eric looks at the man as if he'd just asked why the sun was green, which is a weird look considering the sun is clearly purple. (Had to toss in something random) I'm telling you this as a warning. People like Brian Micheals need to be stopped. Whether is be a professional wrestler, any other famous athlete, or even the guy that works in the cubicle next door, people like this are getting away with to much these days, and these men need to be put in jail. I'm telling you people all of this in the hopes that one of you will follow this situation enough to see what's going on, and do something about it. No die hard wrestling fan will do anything because this man- I mean, this worm of a man, is actually one of the greatest when he's wrestling. So, I need to spread the message to others, and hope to god that one of you will be able to help stop people like this. The camera positions itself at the very back of the room, with Derf in the very center and a sea of multi colored heads below it. Slowly, the camera begins to zoom in on Derf as he continues to speak, one hand in his pocket, and the other one holding the mic. Brian Micheals is a pathetic worm, who will attack anyone if it makes him look stronger. He will say the lamest insults, if it will get a pop out of the younger, less mature fans. Micheals doesn't care about anyone, or anything. He only cares about himself. His ego. An ego so large, it has tarnished not only MWE, but the wrestling world as a whole. An ego so self inflated, he actually thinks he's the best thing since sliced bread. Hell, if you asked him, he'd probably tell you he invented sliced bread. The camera has now reached Derf as a very close level, as he looks right into the camera, as if to speak to Brian Micheals himself. Brian. I am not egotistical. I am not pathetic. I am a man, something you have absolutely no right to claim to be. You can go out to that ring each and every week, and wrestle your ass off, and you can do a great job of it. Hell, you do such a good job, you've earned a spot in the Hall of Fame. But know this. When you're not inbetween those ropes, when you're not wrestling between the bells, you, Brian Micheals, are NOTHING. He shouts that last word, clearly getting himself worked up just talking about Brian Micheals. With the camera so close now, the beads of sweat on his forehead have become as clear as a river from the view on a bridge. And when it finally comes time for me and you to step into that ring, keep in mind that no person you attack, no woman you harass, no threat you make, nothing, will make a damn difference. Because when it comes down to me and you... the rest of this world will not exist. As he says that last line, he gets an almost evil looking grin on his face as the camera slowly begins zooming back out over the auditorium. But something is different this time, as the seats are all empty. Not a sign of life is left withen the walls, other then Derf himself. There will be no fans to insult. There will be no woman to harass. When that bell rings, as far as you and I are concerned, there will only be one other person in the world. One other person to insult, harass, attack. But your insults, as petty as they are, will not pierce my skin. Your harassment, as pathetic as it is, can not make you any more of a man, just as it will make me any less of a man. And your attacks? They won't have nearly the same effect this time when you are forced to attack me with my guard up. Brian Micheals. Nothing you say is going to help you in that ring. When we step inbetween those ropes, I am going to give you absolute hell. I am out for blood. And don't think for a second I'm only looking to cause pain. Oh no. I WILL cause pain, I will humiliate you, and then I will defeat you. I will pin you, Brian Micheals. I need to pin you, I THRIVE on the thought of pinning you, Brian Micheals. And you know why? Eric stares deep into the camera, his eyes filled with an emotion rarely seen by him... only to be quickly replaced with the goofy little grin he usually has. What a nice change, eh? Because, Brian Micheals, you are nothing but a perverted, egotistical, self loving, piece of crap, fudge packing sexist, pussy-ass, waffle eating, cock sucking, man juice drinking, little shithead, piece of crap asshole, know it all prick, moronic Fascist, potential rapist, little dicked, fat-headed, shit headed, dick headed, piss headed, fuckhead! (PWN'ed) I'll see you in the ring Brian Micheals. Bring your best. PWN me. "I asked for it." Derf does the "bunny ears" thing on his last line before he drops the microphone with his sly, silly grin as he walks back behind the stage curtains, as the MWE logo on the screen slowly fades to black.
