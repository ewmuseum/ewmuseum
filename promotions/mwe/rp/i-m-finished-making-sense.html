I'm Finished Making Sense!; Sonny Williams RP

Jan 9 2009

Sonny Williams

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
QUOTE The article is suddenly scrunched up tightly into a ball as a camera zooms out to see that it is none other than MWE Intercontinental Champion Sonny Williams whom has scrunched the article into a tight and firm ball. Sonny is sitting on grass a few meters away from a scenic lake. Sonny is wearing long jeans and a white Sonny Williams shirt which is tight on his abdomen showing his muscles. He has the MWE Intercontinental Championship placed beside him and a small band aid on his forehead from the attack by Antonio DeMarco at Adrenaline Rush. The camera zooms out again and sees that the environment Sonny is located in is a natural residing. There is not a sole person in sight as the surrounding trees stand tall with the humming of birds heard repetitive. The sun is seen to be slowly lowering indicating it is in the afternoon. A beautiful lake is set in this natural environment as the camera pans back to Sonny who has the scrunched article in his right hand and his other hand resting on his knee. Sonny suddenly throws the scrunched article into the river, making it ripple a bit whilst watching the article float out to sea. Sonny looks down at his MWE Intercontinental Championship and then turns to the side to see the camera. Sonny Williams: Back in the days, back in what was the old MWE this is where I would come to refresh my mind, this is where I would come to get away from everything. You see here, there's no reception, there's no technology, there's no Richard Covell nagging me in one ear and the media in the other.. Everything here is so peaceful, and calm. Sonny Williams: All I can hear is the birds humming, and the sound of relaxation run through my body. This isn't like Detroit, this isn't like MWE, this isn't even like the world I wake up to every day.. It's better. Sonny pushes himself off the grass he is sitting on abruptly. He picks up his MWE Intercontinental Championship which was placed next to him on the ground and holds it firmly by one of it's straps in his right hand. Sonny walks a few steps forward to the lakes water, as it is still and clear. Sonny can see his reflection in the water and notices the band aid placed on his forehead. Sonny Williams: But let's cut the crap.. Lets cut the scenes of blossoming plants, the crystal clear water, the positive vibe of the natural environment.. Let's just cut all the nice things in life and talk about what has me here, trying to calm myself down and refresh my mind. Sonny puts his left hand on the band aid and rubs it softly, but suddenly he rips the band aid off and throws it into the water, making ripples once again. Sonny looks down at his reflection and the three stitches in his head and turns back to the camera with an angered expression. Sonny Williams: At Adrenaline Rush, at a Pay-Per-View designed for MWE Superstars to suffer pain, to leave in agony, TO LOSE WILL, I had what the whole course was made for come gunning straight at me; Adrenaline. It was a big event, and despite the cliché the adrenaline came in a rush, but I beckoned.. I advanced and respectfully, and successfully defended what means so much to me; the MWE Intercontinental Championship. Sonny Williams: I took chair shot after chair shot, ladder smash after ladder smash and the pain those objects, those weapons had on my bone structure played a toll, but at the end of the day I was able to pin not one, but two men to retain my title.. Sonny turns back to the water as it seems to have eased down concerning the ripples rather than before. Sonny brings the MWE Intercontinental Championship in his right hand and puts it over his left shoulder. Sonny Williams: I was sweating, I was hurting, I was BLEEDING, but despite that I was over the moon, over the moon that I was able to beat two men in one.. I was gonna party, I was gonna celebrate.. Until.. A man.. No, a coward, a cheap coward otherwise known as Antonio DeMarco marched out to the ring and attacked me, AMBUSHED ME, when I was already battered and bruised. Sonny swiftly turns away from the water and to the camera as the first thing you notice is the passion and fire in the eyes of the MWE Intercontinental Champion. Sonny is seen to be breathing deeply with the emotion and anger running through his body. Sonny Williams: He picked my lifeless body up and started throwing closed fists in every direction.. He made me bleed, he made me hurt, and he left me angry. He is why I'm here now, and not preparing for what will be a brutal battle this Monday in Manchester.. Sonny Williams: He left me in shock, because I thought, I THOUGHT, a man who was recognised for being a veteran, a legend, a man the younger guys would look up to for inspiration would have acted more formally. Sonny brings his left hand to his forehead where the band aid was and begins to softly rub it, remembering how it happened. He re-adjusts his MWE Intercontinental Championship on his left shoulder as he still looks to be in anger. Sonny Williams: You see I respect that guys are gonna want a shot at me, and I understand.. I consider myself open to challenges and open to finding someone for an encore. But I don't respect that a so called 'legend' of his calibre had to assault me when I was at my weakest to send a message, that he wants my gold. Sonny Williams: But I got a message of my own, Antonio.. I remember you, I admired you, I thought the world of you in the old MWE.. You had a reputation for winning, and a reputation for winning titles. You had the same aura Kanto had in the new MWE, but as soon as you lost, as SOON AS YOU LOST, you left.. You took off and gave everyone, EVERYONE, the idea that you are and always were a bi-polar son of a.. Sonny makes a slight smirk as he takes one deep breath whilst re-adjusting the MWE Intercontinental Championship resting on his left shoulder. Sonny turns a bit and begins to walk close to the lake up a dirt path about three metres or so away from the water of the beautiful gazing lake. The camera follows Sonny as he turns and looks into it, a little calmer than he was before. Sonny Williams: Now that I'm through with expressing, with explaining my feelings, my thoughts, MY CONFESSIONS, I need to talk about two other superstars who didn't attack or assault me but still deserve the same treatment, the same dose of pain Antonio DeMarco will receive the very day his bi-polar eases, and his balls drop.. Sonny stops walking on the dirt path and leans over and picks up a rounded rock. He brings the rock to his eyes as he looks at it as if it was evidence on a crime scene. Sonny turns to the lake which is open to him from the dirt path. Sonny turns back to the camera and plays around with the rock in his right hand. Sonny Williams: Let me move on, move on to Monday Night Mayhem.. to January 12th.. TO 2009! A new show marks a new challenge, a new year marks new heights and the first challenge is set to a height, a standard, a level of battle between three superstars fighting it out to claim the satisfying tag known as the winner.. of the match. Sonny Williams: Last week, I was able to go into war against two other superstars with a 66% chance of losing and a 33% chance of winning, but I was able to come out with my hand held high, with my title on my shoulder and the fact that I was able to break the odds and advance. Sonny suddenly throws the rock across the lakes water as it begins to skid across the waters and bounces up six times. Sonny turns back to the camera as you can feel the change in mood and the near change in tone. Sonny Williams: But that means nothing now.. That was last week, that was a different day, that was a whole different year. I could go into Monday Night Mayhem with the same motive and the same attitude, but I know, Richard Covell knows, hell little baby Jesus sitting up in the clouds knows that this week is going to be harder, and that this week I'm gonna have to work for it that inch more. Sonny Williams: Because this week, I go into battle, I go into a fight for respect and a fight to prove that I am the deserving and pride-bringing MWE Intercontinental Champion.. Cause this week I face against a sadistic, psychotic pulveriser in Paige, and an arrogant, self-centered newcomer in Zak Warner.. Sonny brings his right hand to his chin and rubs his chin and gives an indication that he is thinking about the concerns of his match this week. He continues to rub his chin as he looks into the camera with the same passion as he did before. He looks down at his MWE Intercontinental Championship on his shoulder as he stops rubbing his chin. He turns back to the camera once again. Sonny Williams: I look at my past, I look at my history, I look at it, I GLIMPSE at it, to take me into this match at Monday Night Mayhem. But the problem is, the problem is that I haven't got much history with either of these two.. Sonny begins to continue walking up the jagged and dirt filled path nearby the lake situated in the middle of this natural environment. Sonny re-adjusts his MWE Intercontinental Championship as the camera follows Sonny walking. Sonny Williams: That can either be seen as a problem or a new era of challenge.. You see I'm not the type of guy, the type of opponent who runs to the computer and begins to research his opponents to find dirt on them for my promo, because that's lame, that's not the way a TRUE wrestler does and sets out his promo.. And yes I understand that I have barely exchanged blows with either of these two superstars, but I've seen enough to be aware.. Sonny continues walking down the dirt path as he kicks the dirt as he walks, step-by-step up it turning to look at the sunset and back to the camera to express and talk about issues in his career at this present time. Sonny Williams: My mother, she would always used to serve my Aunty first at the dinner table, and would say "ladies first", so I thought I'd go back to my roots and begin with Paige.. Sonny comes to a halt as he bends down and picks up a rusted old Wonder-Woman action figure on the ground. Sonny bends back up into a standing position and brings the action figure closer to his face as he uses his spare hand to wipe all the dirt off it. Sonny turns to the camera and shows it the action figure. Sonny Williams: Funny I found this really, because it's most probably the closest, most similar comparison for someone like my opponent, like Paige.. Except there is one thing, Wonder-Woman takes sausage over donut! Sonny makes a slight chuckle at his comments and does the smallest of slight jumps to re-adjusts his MWE Intercontinental Championship resting on his left shoulder rather than him moving it with his hand. Sonny turns to the side and scratches his head using the head of the Wonder-Woman action figure. Sonny turns back and looks into the camera with a more serious look on his face. Sonny Williams: But that's not my point.. You see, if you took Wonder-Woman out of her costume, she looks just like any other girl you would see on the side walk of the chaotic, social environment of New York City. She looks beautiful, she looks innocent, she looks like she wouldn't harm a fly.. But like Paige, looks deceive her, because deep down, DEEP DOWN past those fake boobs and that raw flesh, she's incredibly powerful, and incredibly.. Perseverant. Sonny Williams: And Paige is a lot like that, she's aggressive, she's determined to leave a mark on her opponents, and she just can't take no for an answer. Go ask Virus.. Ask him what Paige did to him, how she made him bleed, and suffer. Virus, he underestimated Paige.. He thought, HE JUMPED TO CONCLUSION, and thought that because Paige was a girl that it was going to be simple, and that it was going to be like taking candy from a baby. Sonny lowers his head for a split second and smirks at the ground and at what he had just said. He raises his head and turns it to the side once again and scratches the tip of his head using the Wonder-Woman action figure. He looks back into the camera again and re-adjusts the MWE Intercontinental Championship. Sonny Williams: But Virus, he learnt the hard way.. He learnt not to underestimate Paige, and not to be so sexist, and assume because he is a male that he had the win in the bag. So regardless, male or female, athlete or freak of nature, I'm not going to underestimate or go easy on Paige just because she is a girl, because I've seen the pain, the fury she is capable of producing. Sonny suddenly drops the Wonder-Woman action figure on the dirt once again as if it were really Paige and begins to continue walking down the dirt path as the camera also continues to follow Sonny's progress. Sonny Williams: Just like me, Paige has conquered things in her career that can't be unjustified or disrespected. She's held the MWE X-Television Championship.. She's been a MWE Women's Champion, there's no doubting her ability, but can she answer this.. Through being buried alive, the lesbian sex with Lindsey and the WAR Invasion has she ever, EVER, been able to sit down and pre-write the chapter in her Auto-Biography where she won a World Championship? Sonny Williams: Has she ever been the top dog? Has she ever been the leader of the pack? HAS SHE!? No.. She hasn't, but I have. I've been the guy fans would look up to and say to themselves, I want to be just like that one day.. I want to be a World Champion! And maybe, MAYBE, Paige hasn't had the justified chance in her career to become a World Champion, but it's something to this very day, to this very moment that she has not been able to include on her illustrious record.. Sonny is still walking as he walks off the dirt path and walks towards a tree stump to sit on. He makes his way to the stump and avidly sits on it looking into the distant and at the sun beginning to dismiss from the presence of the forest-like environment. Sonny turns back to the camera which is situated in front of him. He looks to have a serious expression printed on his face. Sonny Williams: You know, I've seen enough promos from Paige to know that she is going to be high on confidence, and high on the fact that she just decimated a man, a former World Champion, and a current failure.. And I know enough to be aware that she is going to come out with something against me.. But maybe considering she's a chick and all, I might be lucky and see some originality.. Something, I, Sonny Williams do not see so often. Sonny Williams: You see, every week I hear the same jokes and every week they're getting more stale than Madonna. So surprise me Paige.. I don't want to hear how I'm not up to your ghetto standards, or how you're better than me.. Because hear this, there's a reason why you haven't done as much as me in this business, and that's because you haven't earnt it.. Sonny looks dead on into the camera with a angered and determined look in his face, showing how serious he is taking this match this week. The trees in the background are seen to be swaying to and fro, almost in a rhythmic pattern. Sonny Williams: You don't get it Paige.. Yes, you're a girl, but that doesn't mean I'm not gonna hold back from the infurity, and destruction I already plan to lay down. I do respect women.. But I don't respect the shit you say. How your so crazy, how you are the real destructionist in this company and how you think you're so damn great.. You're like the slim Rossie O'Donnell of this business! Sonny Williams: And Paige.. Know this.. There's a reason why people doubt you, I've done so many higher and prolific things in my career and people still doubt me! So while you continue to tell yourself that you're the future of this business, and how you are scaling up the ladder so fast, SO QUICK, why don't you get ready, why don't you get ready and prepare for me.. For Monday Night Mayhem.. Sonny is still looking directly into the camera as he looks to be on a very serious note, which could also be noticed by his lack of blinking. Sonny suddenly re-adjusts his MWE Intercontinental Championship without making a notice to it. Sonny Williams: Go.. Crack those eggs into a cup, and drink it down.. Pull out black beauty, and use her more.. Cross off the days til MNM, til the day my expectations and fulfilments will come into effect.. Sonny suddenly stands up from the log in a swift motion indicating his intensity and fierce, vigorous emotions concerning this match. He looks straight on, dead on into the camera like he has been doing for the past few moments. Sonny Williams: Because at Monday Night Mayhem I plan to do something no one else has been able to do so far.. I plan to turn the next page in the career of Paige.. We've all seen the aggressive, ruthless and malicious Paige.. We've seen the determined, courageous and bountiful Paige.. But I plan to make my own page in her career, I plan to give you, Paige a dose of your own medicine, I plan to make you look to the heavens above and ask for help, because we haven't seen the struggling, and all-for given up Paige. Sonny Williams: So although I haven't ever been in a ring at the same time as you, Paige, I've seen enough, heard enough, read enough, WITNESSED enough to know that this is going to be something different.. Perseverance meets perseverance.. And the rest will go down as history. Sonny re-adjusts his MWE Intercontinental Championship and looks down at it and rubs the face plate of it for a few seconds. He suddenly stops and slowly, very slowly, turns back to the camera in front of him. He rolls his neck as a small cracking noise is heard. Sonny begins to make his way back onto the dirt track to continue his walk, to wherever he is destined to go. Sonny Williams: So now that I'm done with Wonder-Woman, and her concerns with me, and our match this week, I now have to do exactly what I did in last weeks promo, and change from this to that, from her to him, and talk about my other opponent, the arrogant and full of himself, Zak Warner. Sonny continues walking down the dirty path as he looks to the sunset and the beautiful reflection off the lakes water. He continues walking but suddenly notices something just offshore of the lake. Sonny looks down and sees a decently sized fish with a hook placed right in between the centre of him mouth. Sonny stops and looks around the natural environment, looking for someone else who may come across the fish and unhook it. He sees no one as he takes a deep breathe knowing that the right thing to do is to unhook the fish. Sonny walks three metres or so and carefully picks up the jumpy fish who is obviously in shock. Sonny turns back to the camera and raises the fish for the camera to see it clearly as he makes a slight smirk. Sonny Williams: As I think about, Zak Warner is a lot like this fish.. He's only just entered MWE and is looking to break free, but like most of the newcomers here in MWE it takes time for them to completely break the barrier, and in this fish's case, break free of the hook. Zak feels uncomfortable.. He feels like he's alone in this chaotic environment that is MWE. Sonny Williams: And you know what? He is. And Zak, he's afraid because for the first time in his career he hasn't got two big men on either side of him, for protection, for REASSURANCE. He's alone, and he's scared.. He doesn't know how to break through the barrier.. Alone. That's why he's been bringing back old, bad memories, and why he's even contemplated suicide.. Sonny changes his focus to the fish as he brings his spare hand up to the hook of the fish and slowly, and as gently as possible begins to tug on the hook to break free and let the fish swim around in peace, like it should. Sonny finally pulls the hook out as a small gash in the fish's cheek is seen. Sonny brings his face away from it in disgust and gently lowers the fish back into the water as it rushes away beneath the water. Sonny Williams: Zak, he doesn't like the fact he's alone.. He doubts himself and knows that alone, without anyone's help, that he is doomed to see positive results in his near future in the land of mayhem. You see it's been days, weeks, MONTHS since Zak has been in form, since he had 'that' touch which saw him named victorious, and announced a champion. Sonny wipes his hands on the thighs of his jeans to get the fish smell off his hands. He walks through a few small plants near the brink of the lake as he makes his way back onto the dirt track as he, Sonny, continues to walk down the path-way as the camera continues to follow. Sonny Williams: But you see, Zak is completely aware about his faults, he knows that he barely, BARELY, scrapped past Billy Joe Barker last week, and that this week is going to twice the competition.. And that's why he continues to bring up his past mentor, and friend, Jason Cross who literally, killed himself. Jason couldn't handle that he lost, he had no one to help him.. He was alone in a world filled with guilt and misery. Sonny Williams: Doesn't that situation, that criteria of suicide and reasons being ring bells with Zak Warner? It does, but I'm not here to discuss Zak Warner's possible depression selections.. I've seen my fair share of Zak Warner, and truth be told, I've actually wrestled the guy too.. Sonny continues walking as the sun is seen to be setting faster as it seems to be getting dark, and turning into night. Sonny looks around at his presence and sees a deer gallop away into the midst of the wildness. Sonny scratches his head and turns back to the camera. Sonny Williams: Back in the days where Enigmar Wrestling was alive and well, there was a confrontation, an elite confrontation between myself and Zak Warner to declare who would be the Havoc Champion. I won, I beckoned and added another title to my lifetime's career. I remember Earl Sampson coming up to me after the match and congratulating me on such a victory.. Sonny Williams: You see it was a big thing back then, because Zak, he had people behind him then.. He wasn't alone, he was recognised, announced, and credited as a big name.. He had it all, in EW and GWC.. The fame, the fortune, and for me to beat him with people he could trust to pound on me if I did anything wrong, was a little surprising actually. And now, here he is.. Turned upside down and shaken up, no friends, no allies.. Alone and he expects to beat me this time round. Sonny makes a slight chuckle as he is still walking along the dirty pathway he has been walking trots during the time of the promo. Sonny scratches his head once again and re-adjusts the MWE Intercontinental Championship resting on his left shoulder, as he usually does. Sonny Williams: Zak expects to beat me, even when he claims, HE ANNOUNCED, that he is at a low in his career and that he has ring rust.. And without anyone on his side. Well, Zak you need to learn that since EW I've done a lot of improving.. And I mean A LOT. While you've been out for months sitting on your couch with a packet of chips in one hand and a bottle of soft drink in the other, I've been working my ass off.. I've been improving, rapidly whilst you have been dropping down the ranks, rapidly. Sonny comes to an all-out abrupt halt once again and stares down into the camera with that same look he had when talking to and about Paige earlier. Sonny Williams: But I know what it feels like to have people think you're prime is up.. Ever since my demotion to the Intercontinental Division, people have been saying the word on the street is that my career, my highest points in my career are complete.. They're finished, and that I'm no longer Main Event material. But you know what, they're wrong.. PAIGE.. is wrong. Sonny Williams: I assume you too, Zak, feel angered.. And feel like you have something to prove, to the media, to the fans, to those half-assed superstars who haven't done nearly as much as you have but continue to doubt your abilities.. Because that's how I feel. But Zak, this is your time to prove the doubters wrong, to turn the heads of media crews.. And make me a believer, that you have a future in MWE. Sonny continues to look down into the camera as the sun is almost completely out of the sky as you can tell by the change in lighting around the natural environment. Sonny Williams: Because Zak, I saw your previous promo.. I saw how you told me, how you told Richard Covell, and the entire face of this universe that you can guarantee to be the next MWE Intercontinental Champion, and this is just the first step up that ladder to your shot at my title. You need to prove something, you need to showcase something why you, YOU, ZAK WARNER, deserve a chance over Paige or Antonio DeMarco, you need to win.. You're gonna have to pin.. Me. Sonny begins to walk down that same dirt path again as the camera turns to see where Sonny is walking to, and it is seen to be a car-park-like area where only one car is faintly seen from the distance the camera is in. Sonny turns to the camera with the same fierce and serious expression he had before. Sonny Williams: At Monday Night Mayhem, Zak, I want to see the real you.. I want to see that legacy, that man who captured gold left, right and centre.. The man people recognised to be something big, and something to really prepare for before battle.. But now you've changed, you've doubted yourself and considered life after death.. GET A GRIPE! I want you at MNM at your best, because if I am to walk out a winner, if I am to have my arm raised I've gotta do it in style, I've gotta do it with respect and with a proven point. Turn up cause you want to, not because you've GOT to.. Sonny takes a good look into the camera and begins to continue walking the final meters towards the car park where it is presumed that his car is parked. It takes time as the camera follows the Main Man and his walk to his car. They finally arrive to the scene as it is seen to be a 1987 Black Cadillac parked in the parking lot. Sonny looks at the camera and makes a very quick and barely noticable smile in response to the camera seeing his stylish, old school car. Sonny walks towards the car door as he re-adjusts his MWE Intercontinental Championship resting on his left shoulder once again. He brings his right hand to the back of his jeans and pulls out the key of the car and unlocks the car. The camera comes up close to the car as Sonny sits in the drivers seat and closes the door firmly shut behind him. Sonny places the MWE Intercontinental Championship on the side seat as he starts the engine and rolls down the window for the camera to hear his final words. Sonny turns on the radio as none other than the Monday Night Mayhem theme song is heard. Sonny puts his arm out the window with the other arm resting on the steering wheel. Sonny Williams: January 12th, in another exciting edition of Monday Night Mayhem, I'm gonna bring the ball to the ball game, I'm gonna do everything in my absolute power to come out victorious and to come out.. With a point proven, to the doubters. I want to win, I NEED to win.. I want to win more than Suge Knight wanted Tupac dead. Paige.. Zak, I'm ready for battle, I'm ready to give it my all and I'm ready to face the facts if it isn't me who is declared winner. But before I go, take a close listen to the lyrics of this song.. The song you're gonna hear just before I plan to beat the both of you, and show why I really am the rightful MWE Intercontinental Champion. "I'm finished making sense Done pleading ignorance That whole defence Spinning infinity Boy, the wheel is spinning me It's never ending, never ending Same old story" Sonny Williams: Once I'm done with the both of you, my arm will be high above the world and your self esteem will be lower than ever. Trust me, like the lyrics read, it'll be the same old story.. Sonny..Prevails! Sonny looks deeply into the camera from inside his car as if he is about to scream out his trademark catchphrase but doesn't. He puts his head back into his Black Cadillac and skids out of the parking space he was in. The camera looks at him in the car as he suddenly stops.. Sonny pops out of the side of the window and with the MWE Intercontinental Championship held up high in his left hand. Sonny Williams: CHA CHING! Sonny remains there for a few moments with his MWE Intercontinental Championship raised high, with a look caught in between serious and imitating to smirkful. He, Sonny, pops his head and his title back into the car as he vrooms off from the scene as dirt flies out from his departure. The camera begins to fade as the promo ends.
