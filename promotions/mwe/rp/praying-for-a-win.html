Praying for a win; An Eric Derf/J.P. Smith joint RP.

Aug 6 2010

Eric Derf & J.P. Smith

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
As the scene opens, we're in a bedroom. The sheets on the bed are not even on the bed though, the only thing on the bed is J.P. Smith, dressed in nothing but briefs. He's completely passed out, even though the morning light is shining right on his face. The only noise in the room is J.P.s black cat, who just walked through an open doorway. He hops onto the bed next to J.P. and starts sniffing him up and down, J.P. barely moving. Then as if a hooker being paid good money, the cat starts to lick J.P.s chest. Over and over, stands there licking. J.P. moves a bit from the tickling feeling, when suddenly he awakens instantly, throwing his arm towards the cat, which launches him across the room. J.P. lays in bed panting loud, as if he had the crap scared out of him somehow. (J.P.) Fucking Supernova... He reaches for his head, holding it in both hands. By now it should be obvious he had just been dreaming. Probably about Supernova, probably about his last match, about his loss. (J.P.) You think what you say means something to me? You think I'm not good enough because I don't walk like you, or talk like you? Who died and made you King Shit of the world? Some people don't live in your fucking fantasy world where everything goes so awesome all the time. A world where you get to party and have threesomes every night. Well I live in a world called REALITY! He sorta yells that last part, clearly he's awake, and Supernovas promo is obviously bugging him quite a bit. (J.P.) You claim you could be having threesomes, but we all know your favorite threesome is with beef jerky and gay Internet porn. At least when you have that threesome shit don't go wrong for ya. J.P. snickers at his little comment there, but soon wipes the smile off his face. (J.P.) And you wanna bring up my family? Yeah, they might reject me, but I don't use that as an excuse, I embrace it. It makes me stronger, it adds fuel to my fire, and it makes me a better person all around. You may not think it, but life outside the ring does effect your performance in the ring. So instead of taking your advice and being what you claim I should be, you can just go suck off your buddy Leon Roberts. Because I'm going to be in MWE as myself, for a VERY long time to come! J.P. starts to pant, he's breathing heavy, he's tired from pouring his heart out. He gets an extra jump of energy when he hears a quiet knock at his bedroom door. With no idea who or what could be on the other side of the door, J.P. plays it safe. He grabs a baseball bat laying next to his bed, and gets in position to swing with one hand as he reaches for the door with the other. Just as he begins to turn it, very slowly... (Eric) Dude, just open the damn door already! J.P. recognizes the voice of his best friend Eric Derf, so he tosses the baseball bat down besides his bed, which lands with a loud CLUNK sound. He opens the door to see Eric standing on the other side, his hand raised in preparation to knock again. (Eric) It's about time! Put some clothes on first next time! J.P. turns towards his closet without saying anything as Eric enters the door and casually plops down on J.P.s bed. (Eric) So... Who were you just talking to anyway? Your phone is in the other room... J.P. is silent for a few minutes more as he pulls up a pair of shorts. As he reaches for a t-shirt, he breaks his silence. (J.P.) I had a dream last night, or more or less a memory. I can't get what Supernova said out of my head, it's been bugging- (Eric) Dude, you have GOT to get wrestling out of your head 24/7!!! J.P. pulls the shirt over his head and gives Eric a WTF kinda look but doesn't say anything. (Eric) I know your still excited you got signed to MWE, and I know we both have wrestling in our veins, but you NEED to do something for yourself! If all you ever think about is wrestling and what your opponents say to you, your gonna go insane. You need to just chillaxe, not let a simple loss get to you. (J.P.) But what about you? You've gotten pissed about losing before, I've seen you furious because you got beat or something. (Eric) Yeah, you mean when I got attacked in my own home, or the time I had my dead child verbally attacked? Sometimes, yeah, emotions get the better of ya. But it's not like Supernova said anything you didn't already know. He's just trying to push your buttons. I wouldn't worry about him, I already beat him three times last summer. But right now, lets not get into this. We need to get you someplace to clear your mind. Any ideas? J.P. begins to leave the room as he speaks, Eric quickly gets up to follow. (J.P.) Not really, this town is boring as shit. There's never anything to do. (Eric) That's not true, we could go go-karting, or mini putting, or we could go on a road trip and catch some strippers! J.P. stops in place, thinking of his options. While he thinks, Eric looks out a window and whistles at something, probably a good looking girl. (J.P.) What are you whistling at? (Eric) Just checking out my new car. She's a beauty. You'll see in a few moments. Wanna just go for a drive? Gives me a chance to drive my baby some more! J.P. casually nods Yes as he and Eric exit the apartment and head down the stairs. When they reach the front door Eric gets to it first and grabs the handle before J.P. is even close. (Eric) Allow me to introduce you to my new baby! Treat her well! NO FOOD! Eric swings the door open to reveal his car, the only one in the entire parking lot. And while Eric is uber excited to see his new baby, J.P. nearly shits a brick at the sight of the new car. The car Eric is driving is the exact same Red Honda that was following J.P. a few weeks back. This is his stalkers car. (J.P.) Dude, where the fuck did you get this car! Eric is completely oblivious to the fact that J.P. even has a stalker, let alone the fact that he's driving his car, so his response is pretty casual. (Eric) I found it cheap at a police auction. Someone got stabbed in it or something. Don't bother me. (J.P.) STABBED?! (Eric) Or arrested, I don't know. Don't care either, I got a nice new car for $2000! That's a nice deal no matter how you slice it! J.P. doesn't know what to say. On one hand, he's about to get into the car that apparently belongs to his stalker. On the other, it would seem his stalker is somehow in custody, or maybe worse. But he hasn't talked to Eric about this stalker yet, and he's not about to either. But still oblivious to what's happening, Eric is already in the car. He turns it on and revs the engine a few times, showing it off. (Eric) HEY J.P.!! Get your ass in here! LET'S GO!!!!! J.P. is a little freaked out, but he keeps his cool and gets in the car, and almost as soon as the door is closed, Eric hits the gas and they're off! As Eric drives down the road, J.P. starts checking out the inside of the car. Maybe he can find some sort of clue as to who his stalker is. He turns around and checks out the back seat, nothing. he checks the pockets on the door, nothing. He checks the glove compartment, still nothing. He continues to check the inside of the car out when Eric takes notice of what he assumes is rummaging. (Eric) J.P. man, you looking for something? J.P. doesn't know what to say. Eric doesn't know he has a stalker yet, and probably wouldn't believe him, so in the heat of the moment, he blurts out the first thing that comes to his mind. (J.P.) SEX! (Eric) Wha?! What are you talking about! Eric is all smiles now at J.P., oblivious to what's really going on. (J.P.) Umm... I haven't had sex in a while, so I was just checking out the size of your car. Just needed to see if it's big enough for me to have sex in. When you gonna let me borrow it to find out? Eric knows J.P. is fucking with him now, but he goes along with the joke anyway, since it's the only thing he can do right now that makes any sence. (Eric) Yeah, don't hold your breath. No one is having any sex in this car. Ever. Not a single person. Except me. But not you. No sex for you. Now I don't even wanna let you borrow the car. Your grounded for even suggesting it! (J.P.) Oh no, anything but that! The sarcasm between the two has reached a top point as they both burst into laughter. But the laughter quickly fades as J.P. remembers the car he is in. He needs to know what is going on, so he decides to indulge a little further on. (J.P.) So, what exactly do you know about this car anyway? What happened to make it so dirt cheap? Eric sighs a little bit before replying. (Eric) If I tell ya, you gotta promise not to bail on me, aight? J.P. nods in agreement, prompting Eric to continue. (Eric) Well, what I heard through the grapevine is the owner of this car was stabbed a few times. Some guy was waiting at a payphone, and when the car pulled up, the payphone guy pulled him out of the car, stabbed him a few times, and stole the car. A few days later, the murdered was caught. He was speeding up and down the roads, cops saw him as a speeding pedestrian, and they pulled him over. The guy tried to play it cool till they found out the car was stolen, he was arrested, and the other puzzle peices fell into place. (J.P.) Oh man, that's intense... Why would some idiot be speeding in a stolen car anyway? (Eric) They think he was following someone. Witness' said they saw him pass the "Gentlemans Club" Restaurant a couple of times, the last few times he was following someone in a truck. They still don't know who he was chasing. J.P. is now sure this is the car his stalker was driving. But if what Eric is saying is true, that means his stalker is behind bars now for murder. If this is the case, J.P. doesn't need to worry about the stalker anymore. And while this weight is lifted from his shoulders, he still seems nervous. He needs to get this off his chest and talk to someone. (J.P.) Eric, I have a confession to- (Eric) BSHISHISHIOSHISHOISHISHISHISHSIHSIHSISHIOSHSISHISHISHIOSH! J.P. nearly jumps out of his seat at the ungodly sound just made, as he looks over to see Eric holding a Vuvuzela out the window, blowing it at random people he passes. (J.P.) What the fuck are you doing! (Eric) Breaking the rules! DC don't want Vuvuzelas, so I'm annoying him! BSHISHISHIOSHISHOISHISHISHISHSIHSIHSISHIOSHSISHISHISHIOSH! Eric continues to blow the horn at pedestrians as J.P. simply laughs at his reckless rule breaking of stupid rules, just to piss off the boss.And then he realizes something. (J.P.) Eric, we should cut our promo. Like right now. We can drive around till we find a decent spot, and just rip into Virus and Supernova, what do ya say? Eric drops the Vuvuzela out the car window and looks at J.P. as opposed to the road ahead. His face dead serious, no jokes whatsoever. (Eric) Know what, that's a good idea. And while we're ripping on the same people, I can even give ya some pointers. You know, the student- (J.P.) -and the retarded teacher? (Eric) Hey, watch it tubby! The two share a couple of laughs as they drive up the road. Several minutes pass as the car drives around looking for a location to cut a promo on the Black List Mafia. Finally, the car can be seen from a distance stopping in front of, what do you know, it's a church. Eric and J.P. get out of the car and slam the doors shut behind em. (Eric) You sure you wanna do this in a church? You say the wrong word and you're going to hell for sure! J.P. smirks a bit as he looks back to his friend and teacher. (J.P.) I'll save ya a seat next to the fire. That being said, J.P. turns towards the church, determined. Eric casually follows his student inside, curious to see the kind of promo J.P. can cut against a former World Champion and a man that just beat him the week before. As they enter the church, J.P. dips his fingers in the holy water and blesses himself. Eric does the same thing himself before following J.P. about halfway up the isle. Eric moves into one of the pews and sits down, lowering his head, as J.P. walks right to the front. He drops to his knees and looks towards the ceiling, a statue of Jesus Christ looking back at him. He nods at the statue before lowering his head to speak, but the words that come out are not directed towards Jesus. (J.P.) Leon Roberts... How dare you talk down to my Lord and Savior. If he really let those murderers kill him just for the fame, why wouldn't he have stuck around to enjoy that fame longer? Can you even imagine the fame he could have had when he came back to life? What you said was a disgrace to our Lord and Savior. But it's alright. I forgive you. Yes, you heard me right, I forgive your misguided comments. I see that the darkside has clouded your mind. I can see past this image you put up about yourself, and behind that image in a poor confused child. I see that child. J.P. still has his head down, almost as if he is praying. (J.P.) I see you as a young child going to school, and getting picked on by bullies. Not having any friends. Being alone for your whole life. I can see the virus start to grow inside you. I can see how you grew older and that virus warped your mind, body and soul. I can see what happened in your life, but you don't need to be afraid anymore. I have the power of Jesus Christ on my side. And I have a plan to save your soul and show you the light. I'll show you that you don't need to be as miserable as you are. I feel you, ANYONE, can have a little joy in their life. What is my plan? That is simple. You are going to be my sacrifice to the Lord Almighty Himself! He will take you into his arms, he will embrace you like one of his many children, and he will look you in the eyes, and tell you everything is alright. And without knowing it, he will have killed off the virus that has been inside you for so long, and he will send it down to the depths of hell! I will give you a second chance to live a good life, to start over and enjoy yourself for once. When I finish with you in the ring, you can consider yourself... saved. As J.P. is talking about Leon Roberts, he realizes there will be another man in the match. His expression completely changes from calm, compassionate, to anger, frustration. He gets to his feet and looks down to the floor, making sure to avert his eyes from the Jesus statue as he feels the frustrations pouring out towards Supernova. (J.P.) Supernova, I do not feel the same sympathy for you. God may forgive you, but I do not. There will be no saving of your soul. When we meet in the ring you will look into my eyes and you will see that I mean business. My gift to you is not happiness, but of destruction. There will be no interference from your fucking sluts. My friend, teacher, brother will be there to make sure of that. He turns to point to Eric sitting down, only to notice Eric has disappeared. He doesn't worry about it, he's sure he's doing his own thing. He instead focus back on his attack on Supernova. (J.P.) I will rain down a storm of pain and misery on you, and just when I feel I have hurt you enough, I will condemn you to an eternal damnation in the ugly pits of Hell! Just saying that right there is enough to condemn myself to that same sentence, and believe me, I will gladly meet you there just to apply more suffering for the remainder of your existence. But right now, I have something to say to someone more important than you, or Leon Roberts, or even myself. I need a moment to speak to Jesus himself. J.P. drops back to his knees again and lowers his head, hands together, and as he slowly, quietly begins to speak to himself, the scene fades into a different one. This scene is outside the church, where Eric Derf is leaning against the church wall, gazing into the bright morning sky. He's puffing on a smoke, which is why he left the church. However, looking closer, it's not a smoke at all, it's a Popeye candy stick. He eats the stick without using his hands before speaking towards the sky. (Eric) J.P. might be using church to gain an edge over you two clowns, but I don't need some fancy setting to prove my points. Supernova, I beat you in Money in the Bank last year. I beat you a second time in a tag match when you pussied out and left. As far as the books go, you got beat by me and a girl. And then as the icing on the cake, I beat you a third time in a one on one match. So honestly, I am not worried about what you're bringing to the table. You beating J.P. last week was a simple fluke, you've got a better chance catching aids from a toilet seat then you do beating us this week, but you know all about aids, you've probably got a whole collection of STDs with you and your kidnapped psycho rape twins. Eric reaches into his pocket and pulls out another Popeye stick. He sticks it in his mouth and stands there, looking like he's smoking, because everyone knows smoking makes you look cool. (Eric) Supernova, your skills in the ring are below average at best. Your mind games are mediocre. You talk a big game, act like your superior to so many people, when we both know you're not worth the dog shit on the bottom of my shoes. I will destroy you for a forth time this week, and hopefully never have to look at your crazy rapist face ever... again. But one man I would love to see again is Leon Roberts. A man I have NEVER faced. And while I might not think much of his life outside the ring, at least Leon Roberts has earned my respect for his in ring work. You're a former World Champion, no thanks to my early retirement, and you've beaten some of the best in the business. But you never beat me, and I'm not about to let you either. I've also beaten some of the best. DC, Brian Micheals, Grand Mystique, and even last week Ace Cannon, I've beat some of the best men to ever step into MWE, and next week Leon "The Virus" Roberts will be added to my list of names, list of the people to fall at the feet of Eric Derfs climb to the top. But unlike Supernova, I don't want next week to be the last time we face off. Because come Crowning Glory, you have a one in two chance of walking out the champion, and nothing against Zack Crash, but I really, REALLY hope you win. Hell, I'm at a church, I PRAY you win. Because it would be my careers top moment to beat YOU for the MWE World Title. Because I am making that championship my top priority this time around in MWE. I will be Champion, regardless of who holds the title after Crowning Glory. Supernova. Leon Roberts. You will both fall at the hands of me and my student. You will be nothing but a lesson for his studys, and a day in the office for me. We will make an example of you two. Former tag champions or not, nothing is stronger then the bond J.P. and I share together, and come Monday Night Mayhem, we will prove that blood is thicker then whatever bonds you two might share. Eric sucks the Popeye stick into his mouth and gets off the wall. He walks back through the church doors and down the isle. J.P. is still on his knees, silent. Eric puts his hand on J.P.s shoulder, who looks up at him and smiles. Eric nods back before droping to his knees as well. The two are kneeling down in front of the alter when J.P. begins to speak. (J.P.)- Dear God. Please forgive me for all the hateful things I have said today. I do try to follow your teaching, it's just that sometimes my anger gets the best of me. I hope that you bless me and my friend Eric as we head into our match this Monday. I pray that you can watch over us and make sure nothing bad will happen to us. Last but not least, I want to thank you for letting me and my friend Eric have the power and courage to be ourselves and not feel pressured into being anything but what you want us to be. In the name of the Father, the Son, and the Holy Spirit, I thank you God. Amen. Before either man could get off their knees, a nearby priest who has over heard J.P. walks over. He places his hand on both mens shoulders. (Priest)- You have been forgiven my sons. J.P. and Eric look at the priest with a look of appreciation. (J.P.)- Thank you Father. (Eric) Yeah, you're a cool dude. The priest smiles as Eric and J.P. get off their knees. They walk towards the door to exit, but not before blessing themselves one last time with the Holy Water. Eric looks at J.P. as they reach the door. (Eric) Dude, we got this match. Don't worry about it. (J.P.) Would you say Bank on it? (Eric) Umm... Naw, that got old. Lol. (J.P.) Don't say Lol. It's annoying. The two continue to playfully bicker as they exit the house of God, their thoughts clear, and their conscience clear and ready for the Black List Mafia on Monday.
