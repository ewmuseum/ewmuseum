Desert Heat; Sinc Mercier RP

Sep 4 2008

Sinc Mercier

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The camera pans in with a bright shot of the golden sun, then it zooms out into a large, dry desert filled with sand and cactuses. There is a town that comes into view, a small town but it is far away in the camera background. The camera then zooms out a little more and Sinc is seen with a black buttoned light cotton shirt and black jeans. He is wearing brown leather boots and silver aviator sunglasses. The camera zooms on his face as dust blows around. He takes his glasses off and looks at the camera, his eyes squinted, his face emotionless. He looks at the ground for a second in anger then at the camera putting his hands at his hips in thought. Sinc: My loss…..I might as well start with that. Get it off my chest and out of the way. A discrete sound of wind slicing through the air is heard as well as the rustling of dry brush and crab grass. Sinc: I have nothing to say really…..other than it was a fluke. I knew that Kanto couldn’t tap me out even if he tried. So he had to make sure I was beaten, then throw me out of the ring. Alejandro Rodriguez, being as weak as he is, was the one to tap. I guess it just shows who the stronger one really is. Kanto is still just a freak hiding in a barrel of sharks to me. Sinc spits on the ground. And grabs a water bottle of his and begins to chug it. Sinc: I will not let one loss get me down and away from my goal on what truly matters. And that’s the MWE United States Title. You see…I have a plan for Alex…and a plan for the MWE….to offer a change…a change that will signify a new era in wrestling entertainment. Which I will release to you all at a MUCH later time. Right now I want to focus on a continuing problem….Alex Bowen. Sinc puts his hands together and clasps them around his nose thinking a moment. Sinc: Alex….why do you continue to think your greatness….it makes me sick. Without your brother at your side you seem weak….in fact…so weak that after SHOULDER surgery 3 different times, you were fired from some no life no rep wrestling company. Bowen are you seriously kidding me right now. You couldn’t even make it in such a crappy career, buddy, what makes you think you can make it in THIS one. Sinc drinks his water again. Sinc: With a mute brother who has no intelligence of his own whatsoever and who resembles the guy from Texas Chainsaw Massacre you are DEFINITELY kidding yourself. And together you think you can call yourselves strong? I think I can call you two idiots with no life or no idea what your thinking. You got your brother who would resemble Elmer Fudd, and you who would resemble Bugs Bunny, wait a second, lets be real, more like Bugs Bunny when he dresses up as a female to trick the dumbass hunter. That is more like the two of you. Because your tricking yourselves….your tricking yourselves into thinking you can make it in this business….you can’t and you never will. So might as well pack your little purses and walk. Sinc makes a gesture of goodbye with his right hand in an arrogant way. Sinc: Because after our match, I never want to see your sorry ass in my presence again. I have a common goal with you yes, to gain gold and aim for that US title shot. But the difference between you and I is I have more of a chance than you do. You have no chance, the only chance you have is if the title shot involved the whole cast of Miss Saigon and some no good actor from the movie RENT. Two HORRIBLE plays and two HORRIBLE movies. Hell lets be real, we all know you can’t really wrestle, so I am pretty sure you can’t act either, even in lame ass plays. In the end you’d probably end up being an extra in something as a hobo who does blow jobs and pretties himself up with some BenGAY and black eyeliner. Sinc begins walking to the left. The camera follows him to a pair of boots with scorpion symbols on them lying on top of a boulder. He grins and rubs his hands together. The sun rays seem to intensify on the lens a bit. Creating somewhat of a glare. Sinc: You see these boots? Do they resemble someone you know? Sinc circles around the boots, he is eying the camera in sarcasm. He stops in front of them looking at the camera with focus. Sinc: I know they resemble a guy in jeopardy of failing as an MWE superstar. You see I am not afraid of you Bowen. No matter how much you try to scare me you can’t. It is not ever going to happen. You see Sinc Mercier dives into danger, and grabs it by the balls. Sinc reaches in the left boot and flinches a bit, but grabs a live scorpion by the tail. Sinc: You see how easy danger is to face? Too easy. I have faced danger my ENTIRE life Bowen. All I did was throw myself in harms way day by day night by night. Something you would never know. And if you did know something about it, you’d know I am the most dangerous there has ever been mother fucker. I am one unforgiving SOB. Sinc drops the scorpion on the ground and stomps his foot. The camera stays in place and does not move to his feet. Sinc: I believe I have just shattered your obsession and your very being with one stomp. Just like how I can injure you with one small move. And all that will be said is oops…it was an accident. And there would be NOTHING you can do about it Bowen. Nothing at all. Sinc backs away from the boots a few steps and stops. The camera zooms out a bit more. Sinc puts on his glasses and claps his hands twice. The boots begin to shake and a long formation of scorpions came pouring out of both boots and into the desert rushing away. After a few moments they crawl off the scene and the camera zooms on Sinc as he walks back to the boots. He picks them up by their laces, the laces are tied together. He walks to a tall dead tree a few feet away from him as the camera follows. He ties them to one of the branches of the tree. And leaves them there. Sinc: Time to hang up your boots Bowen. Lets go ahead and roll the dice already. Because knowing you, you have no luck, you just have you and your brother. Together the best you two could get is a snake eyes. And those are very bad in the game I loooove to play. And that’s a fact. Sinc says walking to a black limo where a large Caucasian man in a black suit is standing. He has a bald head and broad shoulders completed with large black sunglasses. The man is holding a black suitcase. Sinc grabs it and looks at it with great interest. Then at the camera, and grins. Sinc: Yea….I will definitely change the MWE alright…and I will make sure of it. Sinc whispers to himself winking at the camera quickly as he then enters the back seat of the limo and the large man closes the door and enters the driver’s seat. The limo trails off onto the dusty desert road as the camera fades with the sound of vultures jabbering and the tires of the limo screeching.
