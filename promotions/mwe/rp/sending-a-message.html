Sending a Message; Alejandro Rodriguez RP

Aug 16 2008

Alejandro Rodriguez

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Alejandro Rodriguez is in Chicago, Detroit. He is driving his car down a street, he is accompanied by Stacy Adams, who is wearing a white “A-Rod” t-shirt cut to make a blouse, sunglasses and jeans. Stephani is in the back seat, watching kid TV programs on the built in television. Alejandro makes a left turn and parks up on the sidewalk. Stacy undoes her seatbelt and checks her reflection in the mirror. Alejandro: When should I pick you up? Stacy: I’ll ring you. Alejandro: Ok, have a good time. Stacy gets out. Alejandro: Ser bueno para Stacy, ok? (Be good for Stacy, ok?) Stephani: Ok, papá. Hasta luego. (Ok, Daddy. See you later.) Stacy opens the door and lifts Stephani out. Alejandro smiles as the two walk off into the shopping street. Alejandro turns on the ignition and drives away. A couple of minutes later he pulls into the arena which will play host to Monday Night Mayhem on Monday. Alejandro parks in the backstage parking lot and he gets out of his car. He walks into the arena and comes face to face with Shawn Spitzer and his camera man. Alejandro: Hey Spitz! Interview, now. Spitzer: I’m going to interview.. Alejandro: I don’t give a shit, get your camera rolling. Spitzer: I.. uh.. Ok. Shawn talks with his camera-man and they begin the interview. Spitzer: Good day, ladies and gentlemen, Shawn Spitzer *he spits and Alejandro cringes* here and here along side me now is Alejandro Rodriguez.. Alejandro holds up a hand as Spitzer spits again. Alejandro: Ok, this isn’t working Spitty.. Spitzer stares as Alejandro storms into the parking lot. Spitzer: Where are you going? Alejandro: I’m taking matters into my own hands. Alejandro gets into his car. There is a handheld camera on Alejandro’s dashboard, he turns it on and speaks into it. Alejandro: In my life, the only thing that keeps my alive is my two beautiful girls and my job. Well today, I’m going to give a man back his job, which he lost quite a while ago. And I believe I can help him launch his career back, in return he will help me too. I’m on the way to where he is staying currently and he will not be expecting my visit. Lets go. Alejandro turns on the ignition and begins to drive, within five minutes he pulls up outside a huge mansion. Alejandro pulls up onto gravel and looks at the mansion. It is big and beautiful, ivy occasionally falling into place in the right areas. A big swimming pool with a diving board lays beside it. Alejandro drives up the large iron gates and presses a button beside a screen. For a few moments the screen remains blank but then Aiden Copane’s face appears on the screen. Aiden: Alejandro? The midget convention is next-door, what do you want? Alejandro: Haha, very funny asshole, I’m here to see Darrel. Aiden: Darrel? I never knew you swung that way, Al? Alejandro: Let me in prick. Aiden laughs and presses a button. Aiden: Whatever.. Come in. The gate opens and Alejandro drives up the gravel drive, he see’s a couple of women in bikini’s playing around by the pool. The drive is very long and when he finally reaches the end he parks up by Aiden’s Lamborghini. He gets out of his car and Aiden opens the front door. He is in a blue open neck shirt and shorts that wouldn’t look out of place on a surfer, he is wearing sunglasses and is holding a beer in his hands. Aiden: Alejandro! Long time no see amigo! Alejandro: Where’s Darrel? Aiden: Jerking off, I expect.. Hold on.. DARREL! Darrel appears at the top of the stairs after a few seconds. Aiden: You’ve got a visitor… Darrel: Who? Oh.. Hey.. Alejandro? Alejandro: Hey. Darrel: Uh.. Why do you want to see me? Aiden turns on Alejandro. Aiden: That’s a good question. Why do you want to see him? Alejandro: Because I’m sick of being spat in the face while I’m trying to have a interview. Aiden: Oh, I get it, you don’t like that Spitzer guy. Alejandro nods his head. Alejandro: Anyway, if you would like Darrel, I want you to do my interviews from now on. You’ll get paid, you’ll have a job and publicity and I’ll have a clean mask every time I go into the ring. Darrel turns to Aiden. Darrel: Are you ok with this? Aiden: Well it’s your life, you can do what you want, just know this, if you take that job, you will not have a home here anymore. Darrel looks like he’s about to cry. Darrel: Wha.. Why? Aiden looks on sternly and then smiles. Aiden: I’m just fucking with you man, go ahead, I don’t mind. Darrel: Really? Awesome. Alejandro: You’ve still got camera’s and shit right. Darrel: Yeah one minute.. Twenty minutes later Darrel has all the camera equipment set up outside Aiden’s mansion. Alejandro is sat in a chair as is Darrel who is slightly adjacent to Alejandro. Aiden has summoned up Hailey, a attractive girl in a bikini to man the cameras. Darrel signals for her to record and she does. Darrel: Good day ladies and gentlemen, some of you may know me, some of you may not, I am Darrel Jones, former MWE interviewer… As most of you know this Monday will mark the return of MWE’s week-night show Monday Night Mayhem. And alongside me today is a person who was in Monday Night Mayhem’s first ever main event, a match that is about to repeat itself, I’m talking of course about former MWE World Champion, Alejandro Rodriguez, how are you doing Al? Alejandro: I’m doing great thanks… Darrel: Now last Sunday at Crowning Glory you came so close to winning the World Heavyweight Championship in a 10 Man Over-The-Top Battle Royal.. How do you feel about coming so close to the title? Alejandro: We’ll it’s happened before Darrel and I can say that it feels just as terrible as it did last time. Coming so close and working so hard to fall at the final hurdle, I would have rather come last than second, but at the time it was a risk I was willing to take, the more you risk, the more you have to loose, but you will never know how much you can achieve if you don’t try. And besides, it’s not like it’ll be my final chance ever, I do have a championship match against David Whitman this Monday. Darrel: Indeed you do Al, do you feel confident going into this match with a Hall of Famer? Alejandro: Hall of famer or not, even David Whitman has his weaknesses, and I know them better than anyone else. I inducted him into the Hall of Fame, I have a ton of respect for the guy, he is a great champion. But that title means more to me than any respect or friendship. That thing is what I strive for, I’ve worked so hard and this title shall be my reward, I’ve held that gold before, and that reign was cut short by the unfortunate downfall of MWE, I should have been declared the champ as soon as MWE was re-instated. But I’m all in favour of competition, and we all know what Covell cares for, ratings. Darrel: Wha.. Do you have something against Covell? Alejandro: Let me ask you a question Darrel.. Richard Covell is the man who fired you, replaced you with a guy who can’t say one sentence without spitting all over the goddam place, because a guy who spits on wrestlers faces, getting them pissed off, means more to him than having a likeable guy give honest and spit-free interviews. Now, knowing all this, do you have something against Covell? Darrel: I guess.. But it’s all for business.. Ok, another question.. He clears his throat. Darrel: Brian Michaels has said some pretty rough things about some of the roster lately, I just wanted to know what your opinions are of him. Alejandro: Well to be quite honest, even though his lame attempts at humour please even the dumbest of crowds, he is seriously loosing his touch. For a man who used to hold the MWE Championship he really has lost his nerve. I mean, come on! Being eliminated, by a clown? Are you for real Brian Michaels? Now that’s funny, for a man to think he was and still is bigger and better than everyone else to be eliminated by a sideshow freak? That’s pure tragedy. Darrel: Ok, thank you for the interview Alejandro, any last things you’d like to say. Alejandro: Oh yes, this is directly to David Whitman. You better train hard, you better bring your all, because you can bet that I will. All my respect and friendship for you will go out of the window on Monday, because I will not loose my chance for that title, this is war. No, not the shitty kind that produced Windy Bridges, I mean an all-out, chaotic warfare. Because if you think I’m leaving without a fight, if you think I’m leaving without that title, you can think again my friend. In time David Whitman, you will.. LEARN.. TO.. FLY! The scene fades to black. ATTENTION! OOC: Okay this is not a Sonny Williams RP meaning I still have one RP up my sleeve against Brian Michaels.
