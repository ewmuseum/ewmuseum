But for now he was perfectly content with the position he was in now, being favorably noticed as a powerful asset to the MWE locker room yet also not quite regarded as a top ranking power in the main event. He loved being that seesaw on the rubber ball of MWE's card, because he could fuck with people....and fucking with people is one of his all time favorite pass times. ?C!Oh.C?!
