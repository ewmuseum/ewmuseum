Alex and David Bowen are setting together on a large brown old couch. Alex has a dvd remote in his hands and David is watching the dvd closely. They are both out of wrestiling gear in street clothes. Alex looks as if he hasn't slept in a few days. His hair is a mess and his there are bags under his eyes. The camera turns as alex is rewinding the dvd, its there match from turmoil. Alex- How the hell did we lose?! David- Calm down Alex, were getting a title shot this week again. Alex- No David this is horseshit, we diserved thoes titles, we haven't lost a tag match since Grapler and Polo allmost 3 months ago. The Devlins didn't even show up, let alone wrestle. And we lost to Project revolution? David- Calm down please, and you really need some sleep you've been working to hard lately. Alex- I'm just confused David, I worked my ass off for that match, and we get upset by THEM! We were the 3 to 1 favorite on Mwe.com. The whole fucking world hates us, but still they voted for us. Alex puts his head in his hands and starts to shake his nee's. He jerks his head forward as a loud ringing sounds from his pocket. David- Who the fuck is that? We have a secure line? Alex shrugs his sholders and reaches into his pocket pulling out a cellphone and answers it? Alex- Who the hell is this and how did you get this number? Voice from phone- This is your boss, Richard Covell. IS that any way to talk to the person who pays you every week? Alex- Well no dick, Whats up? Richard- First off never call me dick again, and I need to talk to you in my office right now! I know your in town so don't try and lie. You have fourty-five minutes to get here, you and David both. Alex closes the phone with a slap. Alex- This is fucking bullshit. Alex shakes his head and laughs. David- Who was that? Alex- Our boss Richard Covell. David- Well what the hell did he want? Alex- We need to be in his office in fourty-five. Alex checks the watch on his wrist. Alex- Sorry now fourty four minutes! David- Well what the fuck did we do? Alex turns to his brother and puts a hand up to his head and starts to rub it then laughs. Alex- Well you remember how pissed I was right? David shakes his head. Alex- Well I have a feeling thats why he wants us there. So comon lets go I don't wanna get late. Thirty and some odd minutes later we see Alex Bowen in a building chatting with a rather sexy secretary. Alex- So hey uhh... hows it going?? Secretary- Mr. Bowen, Mr Covell will see you in a minute. Alex- I'm not worried about ol' Dick right now babe, I'm worried about thoes sweater puppies popin out. Alex looks down at the womans chest. Her said "sweater puppies" are rather large and pushing aganst her shirt. Secretary- Mr. Bowen I would like you to stop staring at my chest. Alex- Aww comon love, maybe we could go back to my place and give em a breather. Just then a door opens and Richard Covell comes out and points at Alex and David. Richard- Bowens... In my office NOW. Alex- Allright Dick no need to get your shit in a tangle. Richard- What did I tell you about that name Alex? Alex mumbles under his breath and calls over David who is staring mouth open at the secretary's chest, Covell hurries them into his office and they set in two rather large fluffy office chairs. Richard moves over to his desk and grabs a dvd and tv remote and pulls his chair back and turns on the t.v. Richard- Now boys this is why i've called you to my office today. I have some footage of the train wreck you call calming down. I will bill you for the damages after the dvd is over. Richard turns on the dvd player and it starts. A hidden camera shows Alex walking down the hall with a few camera men following him. Alex- Get thoes fucking cameras away from me I've told you the last fucking time. The camera men continue to follow Alex and he turns around and grabs one of there cameras and throws it up aganst a wall putting it out of action and grabs the man behind the camera. Alex- WHEN I FUCKING SAY TO STOP I MEAN IT! Camera man- I'm just doing my job, were going to put it up on mwe.com. Alex- YOU THINK I GIVE A FUCK!? Alex throws the camera man into a large black box of crew equipment. Richard cuts the dvd out. Richard- WHAT THE HELL WAS THAT?! Alex- Well by the looks of it I possibly broke one of his ribs. Richard- You broke one and brused his sturnum, he's threatining to sue us! And that fucking camera was 11 thousand dollers. There not cheap you idoit. Richard grabs a few papers on his desk and A pen and hands them to Alex. Alex- What the hell is this? Richard- I'm suspending this weeks pay check and a fourth of next weeks. That comes to 10, 500 dollers. And youe twenty precent bump from your merch will also be suspended for 6 weeks for workman's comp for the injured camera man.
