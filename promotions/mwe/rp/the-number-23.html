The Number 23; NOT STARRING JIM CARREY

May 2 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Instead of this scene starting with the typical “MWE cameras fade…” we’re going to do things a little differently. Okay, close your eyes. Just kidding, obviously, you gotta read this, LULZ. Let’s say you live in an apartment, and while you’re walking to your room, you see a yellow slip on the door. You squint your eyes to see what it says; We regret to inform you that you have been kicked out of room 23-A. What the hell, right? So now you take your little ass on down to the landlord’s room to state your complaint. You knock on the door, he opens it. He tells you that since you haven’t been in the room in 21 days, and have failed to pay the rent since the last time you were there, he has the right to lock, and kick you out, by Florida law. You try and debate it, naturally. He’s got a smirk on his face, his white hair making him seem older than he really is. Not only are you irritated at being kicked out, but this old bitch’s smile is making the situation worse. You just want to lay him out at this point. You start to grit your teeth, fully pissed off now. Michaels: I told you I’d pay it when I got back from France…you damn well know I had business to attend to over there and that I wouldn’t be able to pay you with a check. He doesn’t care, period. Landlord: You know the law, son. Now you walk on out of here before I call the police. Pack your bags, then head on out. He tells you that without a care in the world, as if you have the money to sleep anywhere you want. He closes the door abruptly, and leaves you out in the hallway. You sit down in the hallway, letting the maroon carpet rub against your pants as you ponder your future. Where am I going to sleep? What am I going to eat? You thought you’d never hit rock-bottom. You were wrong. As the promo resumes, the camera shows MWE’s Brian Michaels, in that very position. He is wearing the same jeans from the last time we saw him; in fact, he is wearing the same exact clothing. Instead of moving on like a strong, determined person-of-character would, Michaels just sits there. Michaels: Am I the most hated man on the planet? What have I done, to deserve this? My own family didn’t even want me. The wrestlers backstage don’t respect me…what do I have? His head in his hands, Michaels looks like a broken man; again. At this point in time, Michaels is the kind of guy who would be the butt of the “Brian Michaels is so poor…” jokes. He’s looking for something, someone, to guide him past these troubled times. He looks down the hallway at the door, waiting for someone, anyone, to walk through the door. But there is no one. It’s almost like God is telling Michaels to figure it out on his own. But instead of thinking of what to do next, he is fixated on something. Michaels: 23-A…23-A. 23. He remains fixated on the number, not necessarily the letter. The number twenty-three is a number of significance in his mind. His eyes wander around until finding the door of his room. The numbers are there in bold, staring at him like death stares at drug addicts. Michaels: 23. It’s the number…of Michael Jordan, the best basketball player ever. Michaels: Not just that…the 23rd letter in the alphabet, is W. White supremacists and skinheads, use it in tattoos to represent Whites… The power of this number is significant. Michaels had been doing research on this (while he was twittering) and found out that there are a group of 23rdians. The 23rdians claim to see the number 23 in multiple combinations throughout the day, and claim the number leads to enormous success and fruitfulness. Michaels: 23 is the perfect number. It’s the number that creates greatness. I want to be great again. With that, he rises. Slowly but surely, he begins to regain his poise, and walks out of the apartment a brand new man with a purpose in life. As he walks outside, the once cloudy day disappears, as the sun comes out shining brightly above the horizon. He is cheered up by this, and walks with a smile. The second he steps outside the dark and gloomy apartment, he sees the cheeriness of the Floridians around him. Women in mini-skirts allow him to take a quick glance now that he is single, and even the rough, rocky sidewalk doesn’t seem to affect him at this point. His eyes now fixated on the house of his ex-girlfriend, Christina Darby. Michaels: I’m about to go to Christina’s house and smack that bitch so hard her fake tits WILL pop out…just kidding America. I wouldn’t do that. Michaels: I don’t want to be like Chris Brown; or Ace Cannon for that matter... Michaels gives his traditional grin before walking onwards down the sidewalk. As he does, a long white limousine pulls up right next to the sidewalk with loud, obnoxious hip-hop music blasting. Michaels looks on with a questioning look as right when he reaches the Sawgrass Cinema that has TWENTY-THREE THEATRES, a man wearing all-white; suit, slacks, shoes; opens the door for himself and steps out of the back seat. He gives Michaels the impression that is he a big-shot before slamming the door and giving a bit of a chuckle. Agent: Brian Michaels, huh? He has seen this face before; it’s the face of LeBron James’ agent. James wears the number 23. Leon Rose is his name. Rose: Used to be a big-shot wrestler.. Quickly feeling that his ego is about to be hit, Michaels throws his hand out as If to tell Leon to stop speaking. Michaels: Used to be? What’s that supposed to mean? Michaels cocks his eyebrow, wondering where this conversation is leading. Leon quickly throws his hands up in defense of his comment, knowing it might’ve bit Michaels a little too hard. Rose: What I meant was…you never settled for second-best. You always wanted to be the top guy, right? Well…I represent a lot of top-tier basketball players and I get them some deals they usually wouldn’t dream of getting…do you really love MWE enough to want to work for them for free? In the past month, Michaels had almost forgotten that the MWE no longer had him on the payroll. He was, in essence, giving the promotion free marketing, all for a shot at one match at Crowning Glory? This guy was right; how much DO I love MWE? What the heck was I on that made me take this deal? Michaels is easily swayed by this, as it seems like the agent is about to propose a lucrative offer. Michaels: Keep going… The agent gives a smirk, knowing that he is luring Michaels into leaving MWE for a potential better job. Rose: You’re Brian Michaels. You don’t have to sit around and watch guys like Ace Cannon get title shots over you! You’d be World Champion in any other promotion, and let me tell you, as long as Richard Covell is running the show backstage, the closest thing to a Title shot you’re getting is being Whitman’s tag partner… Oh, how he craves that Gold. To be able to call himself the best in the World, the top dog, once more…would be a blessing. Michaels’ ego is seemingly taking over the humble side that Whitman kept preaching to him. Michaels: Y’know…you might be on to something. I mean, I slave myself traveling across the world every other week for those bastards, and they can’t even pay me minimum wage? How messed up is that? Not just that, but I can’t even get a Title SHOT? Rose nods, recognizing that Michaels is just being wasted in nu-MWE. His left hand is extended as he continues to entice Michaels to join him so he can find a better deal. Rose: This is your life, kid…you really want to stay here and be with… With that said, a loud screech along with a beer bottle is heard across the street.. D.C.: THANK GOD YOU’RE HERE! D.C. is seen half-running across the street, wearing a black Rated-X hoodie and matching tights. His eyes are drowsy and it looks like he hasn’t slept in hours. He is apparently hangovers still as the cars honk at him as they narrowly avoid hitting his hungover ass. He flips them off before turning to Michaels and Rose. D.C.: THIS FAT BITCH JUST TRIED TO RAPE ME! Rose points to D.C. as if to use him as an example of where his life is headed; cold, confused, and claiming that a fat bitch raped him. Michaels: What are you talking about? D.C. shrugs it off, before almost falling into the limo. D.C.: Who’s this limped-dick fucker? Rose smirks, recognizing the immature ways of MWE’s D.C. He shakes his head in disapproval, and not even acknowledging D.C. Rose: So Michaels, you gonna take my offer or what? Don’t keep me waiting. D.C.: What’s he talking about? D.C. turns to Michaels, who is scratching his head now. He doesn’t know how to possibly break the news that he could be leaving MWE. He turns red before asking Leon for a second alone with D.C. He pulls D.C. away, only for D.C. to take a long, deep sigh, exhaling the huge amounts of alcohol in his breath. Michaels: Jesus Christ, I almost got a buzz off your breath…but anyway…look, I don’t know how to break this to you…but I’m leaving. I’m done with MWE. Because D.C. is still slightly tipsy, he doesn’t understand that Michaels isn’t joking. With the serious face Michaels just gave him, D.C. breaks down laughing and playfully punches Michaels shoulder. D.C.: Nice one, dude. Leon rolls his eyes, and Michaels looks on almost embarrassed for D.C. He grabs him by the shoulders and proceeds to shake the shit out of him. Michaels: I. AM. LEAVING. MWE. There’s a split second of doubt, but that is erased as Michaels puts his head down, knowing he is about to leave the promotion that made him who he is today. D.C.’s chuckling stops, as he slowly grabs Michaels’ hands and takes him off his shoulders. D.C.: You told me…that I could leave MWE, and you’d still be here. When I got run over, where were you? You were at MWE. When I got drafted to Genesis, where were you? You were here. When I won the MWE Championship, where were you? He asks this question almost in a non-question form because Michaels already knows the answer. D.C: You were here. You told me you were committed to MWE and now you want to leave over this? Over a World Title? Over some bullshit agent wanting to give you more money? Michaels: It’s not that simple…I just got kicked out…I need cash, pronto...man, it’s always complicated with you…why can’t I do my own thing for once? Jesus Christ, you don’t control my life D… The two face-off with an intensity that MWE has not seen since D.C vs Michaels III. Bones chilling and vertebras trying to stand straight, D.C. speaks in a muffled yet. The men know they are at a cross-roads and something has got to give. D.C’s eyes gleam at Michaels, while Michaels doesn’t even flinch, showing that he is no rookie at the old-school showdown either. D.C.: Go. Leave. We don’t need you here and we’ll move on without you…believe that. Michaels: This isn’t something I wanted to do… D.C.: Sure it’s not. Walk out, just always Brian…just like always. Michaels dreads the comment, knowing full well how many times he’s busted his ass off for MWE. These words send Michaels into a frenzy, but he doesn’t want to let it show, wanting to be the bigger man. He walks past D.C., bumping his shoulder, before opening the door to the limo. Leon smirks again and walks right in, leaning so as to not hit his head. Michaels looks in, knowing that the limo is the future…in there, await all the World Championships, women and money he could want. But as he takes one final look at D.C., who is leaning on the wall of a busted down grocery store, Michaels realizes that the grocery store is his life. The torn up and messed up walls represented his knees; the foundation that once had been built so sturdy, but after so many years of wear-and-tear have finally gotten to them. The door; his eyes. Opening and closing, searching for the next opportunity. As he looks at the grocery store, he couldn’t leave it like it was any other store. This wasn’t a decision he could just rehearse until he got it right. Michaels: I’m staying. D.C. in his mind takes credit for getting Michaels to stay knowing damn well Michaels couldn’t walk away from MWE without a fight. Leon meanwhile, looks away angrily, and slams the door shut. As the limo drives away, Michaels knows he has just shut the door on any potential future World Title shots. His mind is confused, but his heart feels like he made the right choice. Michaels: Ah, ya bastard. D.C.: I KNEW you wouldn’t leave. Now come on, let’s take your old ass to a club. Michaels sighs, throwing both of his hands in the air as if to say no more. Michaels: I’m good man, I gotta find a place to crash and I still need to hit up Christina’s house. D.C.: Alright, I’ll talk to you later. The two MWE veterans shake hands before going their separate ways; D.C., off to confront his demons in his own way; Michaels the opposite. Michaels begins to stroll down the sidewalk once more, before taking a left and finally arriving at the intersection of the street he likes to call “Fuck” and “My Life”. As he walks right in front of the dark-red apartment, he yells as loud as he can, hoping to wake up a probably hungover Christina. Michaels: HEY! GET UP! As he looks into her window, he sees a pair of finely-manicured hands snap it shut. She flips him off, before waddling off in a huff, her blonde hair being the last thing he sees. Wanting to mess around, Michaels reaches down and picks up a small rock, shaped like a baseball, and rears back, throwing the rock at her window trying to get her attention once more. He can hear her scream in disgust, before walking towards the window and opening it again. Christina: IF YOU DON’T STOP ANNOYING ME, I’M GOING TO CUT YOUR NUTS OFF! Michaels: BITCH, GIMME BACK MY COUCH! A crowd begins to gather to watch the ex-boyfriend and girlfriend argue in their own way. Some of the teenagers chuckle, and the adults try to hastily walk away, not wanting their smaller children, dressed like they’re about to go to pre-school, hear the nonsense and cussing. Christina: I WOULD IF MY BOYFRIEND WASN’T SATISFYING ME ON IT EVERY NIGHT, UNLIKE YOU, JACKOFF! He smirks, wanting to continue the insult tirade. Michaels: SHOW ME YOUR JANET JACKSONS! She leans over the window, and is about to flash Michaels before throwing up two middle fingers. He knows he just walked into that, and sticks his tounge out. Michaels: SLUT! Christina: MANWHORE! Michaels: GOLDDIGGER! Christina: LITTLEDICK! Michaels: HAVINGAPERIODWHILEWE’REHAVINGSEX! YEAH, I WENT THERE! NOW GIMME BACK MY COUCH! Christina’s eyes give Michaels the same look that she gave him when she kicked him out, and so he walks up the steps and knocks on the door aggressively. She opens it about a minute later, her hair wet from the shower, wearing a large pink towel. She reluctantly lets him into the room, and he doesn’t bother looking at his old apartment. He makes a beeline for his love-making couch and hoists it over his shoulder, carrying it out. Michaels: I DON’T NEED YOUR SEX, I’LL MASTRAUBATE. These are the last words Brian Michaels says to Christina, laughing while he does it. She immediately slams the door shut and again screams in disgust at her ex. The disgust however, turns into a sinister grin as we see she is devising some kind of plan. The camera pans back to Michaels, who has the couch from his old-school college days laying on his shoulder. He drops it, feeling this is the right place. Right in the middle of the street. Immediately, cars begin honking and the stressed-out businessmen slam their doors angrily and attempt to pick Michaels up, but he plays like a crazy hobo in the middle of the streets as they try to wake him up. Michaels: YOU YOUNG WHIPPER-SNAPPERS GET OFF MY LAWN! He holds his back, as if he couldn’t walk straight. The people who were focused on getting him out of the way just throw their hands in the air, pissed off already, and get back into their cars. The honks continue, before Michaels sprawls his body out and spreads his legs across the couch, just chilling. Michaels: I’ve never given a rant in the middle of the street…but there’s a first time for everything…from anal sex to being a good guy after having a bigger ego than Joseph Sharpman… Michaels smiles, reminicising the Joey Dallas days. Michaels: They said 23 is the number that brings you fortune. Michaels: I have 23 wins in my career. I’ve slept with 23 women. My little brother is 23 years old. He shrugs, thinking it might just be a coincidence, before changing his mindset and looking up at the sky, admiring the cumulus clouds much like he did in his last promo. Michaels: 23…I’ve been in two matches with the Virus….three with Mason. Irony enough? Michaels: Good…because yall are about to hear a rumbling you haven’t heard, ever before. He cups his hands together and puts them near his mouth as if he were about to let the world in on a deep, dark secret. He even speaks in a whisper. Michaels: Honestly, modesty was never in my policy…so if David Whitman wants to hear me truthful and speak from the heart…here goes… He smiles, knowing the rant that is about to hit both Blacklist Mafia and Mason Resoan. Michaels: Vega Knight is Two-Face. Literally, you’re not even a glorified Two-Face. You’re like, a less-known version of Two-Face. Your “dark” side is so cheesy, Cheetos is getting rid of their tiger and putting you on the cover. No joke. Michaels: Not only is your dark side cheesy, but your light side contains about as much cheese as the city of Milwaukee! I mean, shit bro, you cannot get girls, period…I mean that whole looking-the-other-way and pretending not to look at a girl? You call that spitting game? I call that scrimmaging. Michaels shakes his head in disgust, knowing that Vega would probably only be able to pull half the girls he does. Michaels: But enough about YOU, let’s talk about ME! Let’s feed MY ego for a second here…Vega Knight has one IC Championship under his belt. Vega was IC Champion before it was prestigious, which was about as prestigious as the EW Championship. Michaels: Vega, I’m about as hard as the richter scale. You can go around and go to your clubs and screw your cum-guzzling bitches but at the end of the day…you will just be another one of those dudes who writes up one of those FML’s. He sarcastically acts as if he is typing on the computer, pretending to wipe his tears at the same time. He puts on a scene that is damn near Oscar-worthy, if the Oscars weren’t rigged to give the Indian millionaire all the glory. Michaels: Today, I went to the club. I met a girl and she gave me AIDS because I told her she was so hot she’d make the devil sweat. FML. Michaels grabs a pretend knife and fakes slitting his wrists. Michaels: Here’s mine, Vega; today, I took a dump. It felt good and when I flushed the toilet, I coulda sworn I heard your voice in those fake sex scenes… Come Monday, your life will be screwed Vega, because this ain’t your pappy’s rasslin’ promotion where you can book yourself into a win, NO, NO, NO! Vega…your ass has officially been chopped and screwed…chopped…and….screwed. Michaels: The Virus? HOLY BEJESUS, I could go on and on about the Virus. He was big-time at GWC. The pioneer of the promotion according to everyone…er, I mean Zak Warner and Kevin Hardaway. He rubs his chin, wondering who else really respects The Virus at MWE; D.C.? No. Sinc? Maybe. Derf? Who cares? Michaels: He just about ran EW into the ground because he couldn’t stand the sight of seeing someone holding a World Champion and now he’s in a tag team… He throws his hands up in the air, wondering how The Virus, one of the most respected and popular wrestlers at EW, could bring himself down to being in a tag team and needing the guidance of Vega Knight to help him win. Michaels: Also, lemme ask you, how realistic is it that Virus walks around bopping models and popping bottles, with no Demonica to say anything about it? Michaels: Honestly, I’m kinda scratching my head there. Tell Demonica to get off my nuts because she’s starting to sprout ALGAE, SON! She gives me so much head, I could’ve sworn I went to college… He winks at the camera, hitting the MWE fans with another inside joke. Michaels: Speakin’ of college, gee Virus, you sure are a dumbass. Again, modesty was never in my policy, so I can say what I want DAMNIT! Virus, did you drop out AFTER they asked you 1+2 or when you went to nappy time? Michaels puts both hands together and acts as if he is about to put Virus to sleep. Michaels: Probably nap time…Virus, I’m putting your career to sleep. I hit the strip club, girls turn extra hype…YOU hit the strip club? Girls turn extra DYKE! After this match, I swear to GOD you won’t want to show your face at MWE again…so you know what, you walk around and you try to relive your wet dreams from the GOOD OL’ APWC AND GWC days because that’s about as CLOSE as it’s gonna get from a dude who’s biggest claim to fame was telling Maxxamillion Mustafa that he WAS HIS FUCKIN’ BRO! With the climax of the promo nearing, Michaels stands up like an aged rock star and takes his bow. Michaels: Then you got Mason. Perfect. Perfect, perfect, perfect. He makes no mistakes and in his mind, he didn’t lose last week, Cannon did. I bet you all this couch that Mason will even say that…if he manages to actually say something this week… Michaels: See last week, I was waiting for Mason to say something to me, a comeback if you will…but nothing. Mason hid behind his rent-a-slut and then blamed Cannon for his loss. Michaels sits back down before wagging his finger in the air, like Santa telling a boy that he was acting naughty on Christmas. Michaels: That’s supposed to be perfect? Mason Resoan, you’re about as perfect as a straight circle. Michaels: Which means you’re not. Time and time again, I see myself in you, and you’re making the same mistakes I made. You’re walking around with too much swagger for your own good and I’m telling you now, it’s gonna bite you in the ass like a dog biting Kim Kardashian. Michaels: You know what’s wrong with people like Mason? They have no depth. There’s nothing exciting about Mason Resoan unless you count kicking TV’s and hot plates… He rises from the couch and stands in the middle of the street, kicking at a rock as if they were the hot plates Mason Resoan kicked a long time ago. Michaels: So you can go and talk about your watches…your houses…give us the typical Mason Resoan promo, because that’s what I want to see. Give us the typical Mason Resoan match. Predictable as usual. His finger goes up and down as if Mason Resoan’s life were a chorus and it just played over, and over, and over like a song that would never end. Michaels: Mason Resoan thinks he’s so fly, he’s on autopilot. There, that’s the promo. Hit me with the politics and dick-sucking and ass-kissing…I know you will… Michaels: Mason…TLC…Pay-Per-View…your time will come. But Monday? Six Man Tag? Mayhem Mafia? The future of MWE and Brian Michaels? You really think you’ll win this week, if you couldn’t do it last week? No. Just, no. Blood Fuze is done fuckin’ around…they will TAKE IT to Blacklist Mafia and Mason Resoan…welcome to the big leagues, again. As Michaels winks at the camera, he feels something under his couch. He reaches an iPod that he was lost a long time ago, and puts it in, playing the last song played. Mr.Carter by Lil Wayne and Jay-Z blares loudly, becoming the only thing the viewer can hear. I got summer hatin’ on me because I’m hotter than the sun I got spring hatin’ on me because I ain’t never sprung Winter hatin’ on me because I’m colder than yall And I will never, I will never, I will never fall. I’m being hated by the seasons So fuck yall who are hatin’ for no reason. One of the headphones falls out of his ear and as Michaels reaches under the couch and his eyes hazes he realizes what else lies under it. 23 dollars. Woah.
