MWE 24/7 — 08-09 PPV PR Archives

Balanced Perfection

Mar 28 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
*Mason Resoan is standing in the middle of a large room, wearing a suit at the head a grand black marble table. There are several seats around the table with no one sitting in them. As of right now, the camera can only see Mason Resoan, all proper and ready for what looks like a big meeting. However, as the camera pans out, Sapphire Garvin, Mason Resoan's girlfriend, can be seen sitting in a small wheeled computer chair. She has her hand gently against her face, as she sighs and stares out the window. Mason isn't looking at her, but rather, the center of the table, in which a single black folder sits. He was told not to open it, so as he waits for the answer, key, or call...or whatever it may be, he just waits. Sapphire sighs again, takes a quick spin, and turns to face Mason.* Sapphire: I'm bored...what are we waiting for? Mason: To be honest...I can really put my finger on it. I think some sort of sign or or phone call...or something. I am not allowed to touch the folder until I get that signal though. Sapphire: This is dumb, what could really be so important about this folder that you aren't allowed to touch...wait a second. They never said anything about me taking a peak. *Sapphire rolled her chair over a few feet, jumped out, and tried to jog to the folder. Mason stepped in front of her though, preventing her from going through.* Sapphire: What's the deal? Mason: If they don't want me to touch the folder...do you honestly think that they want you to either? No, and that is why we are waiting. This has to be important...at least somewhat. *Sapphire just crossed her arms, much like a little child, and stomped back to her rolling chair. She often acted like a child, and Mason somewhat liked it, but now was not the time to joke around...not when he didn't know what was going on. He then took a seat, playing with a pen that he found inside his suit pocket. Click...snap...crack...smash. These four sounds were made with the pen and Mason's hand to make a improv drum beat, which he bobbed his head to. This went on for minutes, at least five. He finally got bored of the drumming and put the pen away, right as several men walked through a big door to his left. All were in suits, ranging from brown to grey to black. They all take seats around the table, all but one, who is the only one wearing a black suit. He walks up to Mason, whispers something in his ear, and takes a seat at the other end of the table. Mason, just after he recieved whatever the man told him, smiled and sighed in relief. As the man sat down, he reached across the table, grabbed the thin black folder, and opened it up. Inside, a few papers could be seen, but not what was on them. All that could be seen was the disappointed look on the face of Mason, and Sapphire saw this immediately. She walked over to the table, grabbed Mason's left shoulder and looked over, down, and at the papers. Her face immediately became the same expression, but worse.* Sapphire: Are you guys kidding me? I waited this entire time her for this...for a few pictures and some stats? *Mason waved his arm at his girlfriend as the man in the black suit stood up. He was a tall man, burly in the shoulders, with some scruff on his face. His eyes were intimidating, and he felt as though he had been disrespected. The last thing Mason wanted was trouble before his big match.* Man: Nice to meet you to. I am Lance Vercetti, I was the man that you spoke to over the phone. Listen, I know it doesn't look like much, but we will explain. Mason: Please do...Otherwise, I am going to leave. Man #2: Listen, it is simply what you asked us to assist you in. You wanted for us to help you...and this is what we saw best for you, especially on a professional level. *Mason looked at the second man with a blank stare.* Mason: I paid you guys $1000 to get me valuable information on him. I told you specifically to get me information that would help me focus on his strengths and weaknesses...and what do you do? You go to f**king wikipedia! Worthless! Lance: Listen, Tom, Bryan, Ricardo...leave, I will deal with this man myself. *Without questioning, the three men to Lance's left get up and leave, completely abandoing the room, leavin Sapphire, Mason, and Lance himself. Lance, who had stood up, now switched positions with a furious Mason, who sat down. He simply sighed and leaned back in the comfortable leather head chair.* Lance: Where to begin... Mason: I'll tell you were you can begin...start by explaining why in the world you guys went to wikipedia after I paid you $1000 to find valuable...and I repeat...VALUABLE information? Lance: Why? Isn't it obvious.....so you can focus on him. *Lance took a pause, waiting for a witty or insulting rebutle from Mason, however, Mason stood still, with his arms crossed and a sour look smacked across his face. Mr. Vercetti took this oppertunity to take a deep breath, in which Mason did as well.* Lance: Some of the highest ranked officials in any type of crime investigation or police force will tell you that the best place to hide...is in plain sight. You don't hide...you stand in the open, around other people, like you normally would and you "hide" in plain sight. Well, same thing applies here. Mason: How so? Lance: Think about the information we have supplied to you. His name, birth date, height, weight, signature moves, title history, moveset, and we even gave you his record history. We supplied you with all the information that you could normally get from a search engine, such as Wikipedia. Now, my point is this...the infomation you needed was right in front of your face...you just needed to open your eyes. You see, by taking a look at this information, you are able to tell yourself what he perfoms best in. Main events, Monday Night Mayhem matches, house shows, ect. It will tell you how much he weighs and how tall he is, so you can gather information about how to attack him. Will you chop him down, or throw him up? And we also gave you the information of what his move set was. Now you can determine wether he is a high-flyer or a brawler. A striker or a submission artist. It all serves a purpose. *Lance Vercetti takes another deep breath, taking in some air before he continues his rant. Mason, at this point, has lowered his arms to his legs and is now sitting, Sapphire leaning on the back of the chair. They both seem intrigued with the man's logic.* Lance: I own my own company, and I own it for a reason. I can get into the craziest physcopath's head or the craziest loose cannon's mind. I can make the most emotional strong and impowered man or woman to cry and I can transform Satan himself into an angel of good deeds. Our business is a very fine one, that only some of the elite are apart of. We all know exactly what we are doing, and the resumes hat we compile together and turn down are phenomenal. Some would call us crazy for doing such things. However, it does not, for a second, change the fact that we are in full control all the time. It does not change the fact that we know what we are doing and it doesn't change the fact that we can do with the most perfect execution you have ever seen. We searched you up...we have watched your so called promos and we know how cocky you are. We also know how good you are, but in the sense of perfection, in my profession no one is better. We appreciate the generous $1000 dollar payment, but you could have done it yourself, if you took the time to hop onto a computer. You want to say you are perfect, well will tell you a little something right now. You are not perfect in the sense of getting into people's heads. Normally you would have ignored this, but you are intirgued because of the way I am speaking. I am speaking like you in one of your rants, and that is why I have you on the edge of your seat. You your skills right, and Highway to Hell will be a good night for you...have fun with David Whitman, Mason Resoan. *The big man, Lance Vercetti, stood up and walked out of the room, fixing his overcoat as he went. He seemed very pleased with himself, as he had a certain stride in his step. Mason just looked at the table, baffled by what he just saw. He could not believe that he was just shut down with what he did to other people. He could not believe that he had just been embarrassed in front of his girlfriend. And he could also not believe that the man was compeltely right. He looked at Sara, and she walked away, obviously seeing that Mason needed some time to reflect.* Mason: If I have ever been so verbally shut down in my life...I would like to know when that was. *Mason simply put his elbow on the table, opened up his hand, and placed it on his forehead. He looked pretty embarrassed, something that is rare for him.* Mason: And you know what is the worst part about the entire rant that he just finished with...it is all true. Yes, maybe I am perfect in some categories...but maybe not that one. And it seems as though he hit every part of the MWE locker room that he could, which is something that I have recently don... *Mason places his head back into his open hand, realizing how good this Vercetti guy was. He looked at the stack of papers and frowned, as he did not want to be wrong.* Mason: David Whitman...I may not be perfect, in the sense into getting into people's mind, but I am damn sure close. *Mason lifts his head out of his hands to look into the camera. There is some sort of weird passion in his eyes as he now stands up and begins to circle the circular table. He takes a stop, giving a little chuckle as he does so, probably thinking about Lance Vercetti.* Mason: Lance made some good points to me, and he used my own technique against me...but even though that worked so well, I am not going to use false comedy to try and get myself over. I am not going to try and shield my problems from others just so I can claim to be called "marketable" and "wholesome", because I get over without those things in my title description. *Mason begins to quicken his pace a little, as he makes his fourth circle around the table. His shirt is becoming a little ruffled.* Mason: You see, my name is Mason Resoan...not Lance Vercetti nor David Whitman. I am not a man to copy others and I am not going to pretend to be someone I am not. Being yourself, as some people will say, is key to a successful career...and me, well I already know that I am a perfect wrestler. You, David Whitman are not. Not only do you not have the right mind frame to be a perfect wrestler, but you also don't have the experience of being at the bottom. In Mayhem Wrestling Entertainment, you started at the top and stayed there. On the other hand, people lik D.C and myself fought our way to the very top of the MWE roster, eventually capturing a title or two, before getting our first world championship. We know what it is like to look at a card and see people above us. We know what it is like to be in a tag team, wishing we could be out so we could go after the ultimate prize, and you don't know that feeling. *Mason is now in the state of power-walking around he table, but as he reaches around his fifteenth circle around the black marble table, he stops.* Mason: And you call yourself balanced? *Mason shakes his head in disgust. He begins to fix his shirt, before undoing his tie. He places the tie on the table and begins his circling around the table once more.* Mason: I don't claim to be things I am not, because I am not a liar, nor am I a hypocrite. Well, you say everything in life should be balanced, yet your whole career is one-sided. Your whole life is one-sided, and you will sit here and preach about being one-sided. I suggest that you step back and look a little closer at your life. On the other hand, I claim to be perfect...because that is what I am in the ring. I am a promoter's dream. I am good on the mic, great in the ring, and I can do whatever I need to do. You couldn't become the bad-guy, could you? You couldn't let down your fans, could you? Or could you? That, is the million dollar question. Would you, to keep your word on being balanced. Appeal to the fans, yet hate them and gain for yourself? It all depends if the fans will buy it...or if you will buy it yourself. *Mason, like last time, begins to quicken his pace around the fourth circle around, forming some sort of pattern. He continues to circle, not breaking a sweat, or coming close to it.* Mason: I doubt you could buy it and I don't think you will ever try to buy it, so I will let you be a hypocrite all you want, but I can't wait for it to start eating you alive, because it will. It will begin to catch up to you, eventually engulfing you until you can't take it anymore. Meanwhile, I will laugh and watch as you admit I was right. You will admit that I am perfect and that you were wrong. It just depends on how long it takes you to realize that I am right and that I am perfect. I mean, I don't see why it should take all that long for the latter of the two, considering my record is damn-near immaculate, with wins over Paige and Windy Bridges, Dallas and Sinc, and Michaels...Michaels. *Mason is again near his fifteenth circle around the table and this time, he takes on the nice black overcoat, leaving him with another nice white dress shirt. He places the shirt on the table, neatly against the tie.* Mason: Michaels is a man that you have never beaten...something that I don't think really lives up to the claim of being balanced. I mean...0-3 against a man that I am 2-0 on? Seriously, is Michaels your kryptonite or something...because if so, then I will be to you, like Warner Music Group to YouTube. You have no idea what balance is...and it sickens me to know that you are a liar of a champion. "Marketable"...since when have liars been marketable. When was the last time you saw a liar be promoted and exposed, yet praised? To me, this is a first, and this is quite upsetting, to be honest. However, I am glad that I get to change all of this, come Sunday night. *Mason once again shakes his head and goes straight back into his routine, starting out with a brisk walk around this table, making his rounds (no pun intended).* Mason: Honestly, you have probably been in MWE the longest out of any superstar...but, I don't get where you get off telling the public, the locker room, Richard Covell, or even yourself that you are balanced, because you are far from that my friend. In fact, if I could spot one person's career who was less balanced than myself, it would be you. If I am perfect, then you are right underneath, so I don't want to hear any of this ridiculous nonsense about how being perfect is impossible...because it is so very possible. *Mason thinks back to what Lance Vercetti said.* Mason: The ability is always there, and it is in fact, right in front of every single solitary wrestler's face...you all have to just open your eyes and realize your true potential. However, as you realize you true power and skill...I only advance that much more. I only become more successful, I only make myself more famous, and I only capture more and more championships. I will throw down a vow right now, that after I win the title Sunday, I will become the most decorated champion in history. Whether I have the most controversial championship run, or the longest title run, or the most exciting run...something extraordinary will happen during my reign, and only one thing steps in my way. And that is where you walk in Whitman. *Mason makes the trip all the way around, and this time undresses the white dress shirt, showing his bare chest. He six-pack bulges out of his chest, and his pecs show like a piece of art. He truly is in perfect condition for wrestling.* Mason: The way I see it Whitman, if you are a bird, I am an archery. I will shoot you out of the sky. If you are a deer, I am a hunter, and I will shoot you down. If you are a boxer, I will knock you out. And if you are trap, I will trip you, capture you, and take you out with a trap of my own, because that is what I do. If you want to know what it feels like to lose, Sunday will be your opportunity, because while you have height and weight on me, like most do, I will always have one thing more than you. *Mason taps his head a few times, pointing to his brain.* Mason: Intelligence. I will always be one step ahead, both academically and psychologically. *Mason slips off his nice dress pants, revealing himself to be in his normal ring attire. His purple Canadian Classic wrestling tights shine in the light.* Mason: And they say, especially when you are a little wrestler, that you don't have to be the sharpest knife in the draw to wrestle...but they were wrong. You do...well, at least if you want to be successful...and that is why I am here, getting my Mayhem Wrestling Entertainment World Heavyweight Championship shot. Not because I am the biggest...not because I am the baddest...but because I am the best and I use everything I can to propel me to great heights. I am smart, because I come to business meetings, ready to wrestle at all times. I train in this, I sleep in this, I eat in this, and most of all...I wrestle in this. And why does that make me smart, why does that make me the best...it is because I know what I need to do...and I actually do it. *Mason is rejoined by his lovely girlfriend, Sapphire Garvin, and she clings onto him instantly. He leaves the clothes on the table and they walk out of the room, cameras following them as the walk down the stairs. Mason is leading, holding Sapphire by the hand as they walk down four flights of ten steps, to the ground floor. Outside is a parking lot, in which they cross. They reach a balcony on a beach, just as the sun is setting.* Mason: I say all the time that I get the best because I work for it. That is 100% true. People tell you that you can be whatever you want or get whatever you want, as long you work for it, and that is also 100% true. People will also say things, such as if you can't take the heat stay out of the kitchen, or if you can't take the burn, stay out of the sun. Well, I can take the heat and I can feel the burn. I do work my hardest and it pays off, and because of that, I get rewarded with perfects things, such as skybox views or beautiful sunsets. *Mason soaks in the final part of the beautiful sunset.* Mason: I am going to enjoy this sunset until it goes down, because this is going to be the last peaceful, calm, or nice thing I will see until at least Tuesday. Sunday, David Whitman and I will wage war over the prestigious Mayhem Wrestling Entertainment Championship, and whether it be balanced or perfect...it will be be one thing...memorable. *The last bit of sun was barely visible, and Mason slapped on one of the cockiest grins he had ever produced. As the sun finally set, he turned to walk towards his car with Sapphire Garvin.* Mason: Now that...was perfect...and if ya didn't know...now ya know!
