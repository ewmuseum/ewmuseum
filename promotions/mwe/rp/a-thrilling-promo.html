A Thrilling Promo; Zack Crash RP

Jun 12 2009

Zack Crash

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The camera opens up with a long view of an endless horizon that stretches out to both sides. The atmosphere shines with a majestic, crystal blue as even patches of cotton clouds decorate the sky. The sun beats down, breaking through the clouds with its radiant rays, warming the Earth, which is a field of green and brown. From this high up, it is as if we are looking at the planet through the eyes of God himself. And everything is perfect. Magnificent isn’t it? Calls a voice from off screen. We slowly pull back and turn to find Zack Crash seated on a small chair. Steel walls surround him, and there are a few wooden carts stacked here and there in the background. From the simple appearance of his surroundings, it is safe to assume he is inside a small plane. He smiles faintly at the camera before continuing with what he is doing. He stands up and pulls a red jumper suit over his clothes and zips it up the center. He puts on goggles neatly over his eyes, straps on a pair of gloves, and throws a small brown backpack over his shoulders and tightens it. He offers us no explanation as to what he is preparing for. After he is finished with whatever it is he’s doing, he heads for the side of the plane and stares out the small window. A serene, peaceful expression paints its way across his lips as he looks out at the expansive view. No matter how many times I come up here, it still gets me every single time. When I’m this high up, all the troubles of life seem like they’ve been left behind. Looking out at the world like this, I’m convinced God must have been an artist. Because how else could he make something so beautiful. Zack continues to look out the window a long moment before laughing out loud to himself and turning to face the camera, a grin so wide on his face we can see the shine of his pearly whites. Sorry about all that. Got a little poetic there for a moment. I didn’t bring you all up here to talk about stuff like that. I brought you up here so I could introduce myself. Because mamma always said first impressions are everything. I hope you don’t mind the surroundings. Introductions have always made me a little nervous, so I like to get into my comfort zone. Zack gestures to the area around him. In truth, it is not the area itself that calms Zack, but rather what he is about to do. This action, as well as several like it, are his comfort zone. When attempting these actions, which when viewed in certain minds appears daft, Zack feels alive. Every molecule in his body screams with life and he notices things. Like how delicate the hairs on his arms are when they stand up. The soothing pounding of his heart as it beats across his ribcage. Details that normally are taken for granted, and Zack knows what it means to be truly alive. So now, onto the introduction. As you may, or may not know, my name is Zack Crash. I’m a Scorpio who has a lot of energy, who is eager to please. I feel my most attractive quality is my eyes. My turn ons include long walks on the beach and cuddling. My turn offs include the color pink and High School Musical…JUST KIDDING! Zack throws back his head, laughing madly as he slaps his knee. He stops abruptly, looking into the camera with a raised eyebrow, almost as if he is expecting a reaction. After a moment’s awkward silence, and without a response, Zack simply shrugs. Well I thought it was funny. Although I really do hate High School Musical. I mean, seriously! What was that about? But I digress. In all seriousness folks, I’m all about one thing…thrills. I’ve done just about every crazy, extreme sporting activity you can think of. Rock climbing, bungee jumping, spelunking, anything that gives me a good ol’ adrenaline rush. And after I’m done with one, I look for the next one. Zack body tenses up for a moment. Inside his head, his memory replays several moments in the past of his countless adventures. They flash in front of his conscious, mixing together in a blur. He has committed each one to detailed memory, wanting to forever capture the rare emotion of each. To Zack, no two moments are the same. Each is separate and unique. Each is a moment one will never experience again and worthy of individual remembrance. But after doing the same old tricks over and over, they started to get a little boring. But then, once when I was in Japan, I saw this wrestling match. I was bored and I’d never watched wrestling before, so I thought ‘what they hell?’ and bought a ticket. And that day changed my life forever. I sat on the edge of my seat in that arena with its sticky floors and people shouting out incoherently in a language I didn’t understand, watching as these men who flew through the air as if they were hawks gliding on the wind. They looked so sleek and graceful, like dancers. And in that moment, I knew man could fly, that man had invisible wings, and I wanted to fly. So I trained, and practiced, bleed, sweat, and cried, and here I am. Which brings me to why I’ve come to MWE. It’s quite simple really. I’m here to give the MWE faithful the best damn show their bottom dollar can buy. But more importantly than that, I’m here for the thrill of it all. I want to throw my body through the air, with complete disregard for my safety or the safety of my opponent, just to hear the fans scream “HOLY $H!T!” I want to face opponents twice my size and strength, just for the danger of it. I want to be in the most punishing, grueling matches ever devised. Sweat begins to slowly drip down the contours of Zack’s face. He is growing more intense as he goes. Many people, when faced with challenges deemed difficult, they will, and with good reason, search for an easier path. But such a path is not a path Zack wishes to choose. If every provided the choice, he will always pick the more difficult road. Why? Because it’s simply more fun that way. And speaking of opponents twice my size and strength, that brings to me my very first opponent in MWE. He’s a little 326 lbs. monster who goes by the name of Blight. Now I’m not here to say how much better I am then he is, or how I’ll win, or blah blah blahbity blah. First off, that just ain’t my style. Secondly, I’m sure you’ve heard enough to the generic “I’m the wrestling bad guy” stuff to make your ears bleed. And thirdly, I may be many things but I’m not stupid. I saw Mayhem and I saw what he did to Sharpe. Pretty nasty stuff if you ask me. And after seeing what this guy is capable of, I have to say…I couldn’t be happier about who my opponent is. Think about it. BLIGHT!!! I mean look at the guy. He makes all those old horror movie monsters look like something out of Disney. But make no mistake, because I’m not afraid of him. All he is to me is another mountain to climb. He’s the next thrill I’ve been looking for. And I couldn’t ask for a better challenge on my first night. The bigger the challenge, the better the fun I always say. Now you may hear this and say to yourself, “Zack are you insane?” And the answer is…yes, indeed I am. I’m nutty, loopy, wacko, I got a screw loose, I’m coco for Coca Puffs. But I’ve always said sanity is overrated anyway. Blight’s bigger than me, stronger than me, maybe even faster than me. My chances of winning are slim at best, and I rather like those odds. In retrospect, I may be in over my head here. Screw that, I AM in over my head and that’s the way I like it. Because despite everything I just said to Blight’s credit, I myself have a drive inside me I would advise none of you to underestimate. And be assured that I will do things that you’ve never seen before in an attempt to gain victory. And if I go down, I won’t have gone down easy. The camera closes in on Zack’s face, his eyes narrowed. He looks deeply into the camera, speaking this next part to Blight directly, not caring if anyone else hears. So Blight, if you’re watching, and I’m pretty sure you are, I want you to give me everything you got. Come at me, and try to break my bones, bruise my body, destroy my very soul. Otherwise, it just wouldn’t be as much fun. And likewise, I’ll give you everything I got. This way, everyone goes home happy. The fans get a good show, one of us gets a nice mark in the victory column, and I get another thrill. Because, win or lose, I always go home happy…because I always get my thrills. Zack begins to pace again, randomly tightening his gloves and fixing his equipment just so his hands can have something to do. But slowly, Zack’s expression begins to fall. That once gleeful, exuberant smile is now replaced with a scowl of determination, as he looks harder into the camera, looking past it now to all those watching. This next part means a lot to Zack. It is the creed for which he has modeled not only his whole career, but also his life. He has adopted it after many experiences that stick out in his life. Some good, some bad. But that’s another story. I don’t expect this match to be easy. But then again, nothing in life that’s worth it is. And those who aren’t willing to take risks now and then, they don’t succeed. In the end, they just stay drowning in a pool of mediocrity. But I’m not satisfied with that. I want to reach the highest point there is, and then when I’m no longer happy with that, I’ll go even higher. Let’s look at some real life examples. Do you think Sinc Mercier played it safe to finally win the MWE Championship that he knew he deserved…HELL NO! Do you believe David Whitman took the easy road to be a success…NO FRICKIN’ WAY! What about DC, or Brian Michaels, Ace Cannon, GM, Alejandro Rodriguez, or anyone of the big names in this business? They got to where they are today because they weren’t afraid to put it all on the line for just a single chance. Whether in the ring, or in real life, they took chances. Zack points a stiff finger toward the camera, indicating all the seniors of the MWE locker room watching. Zack then turns the finger round on himself, poking himself hard in the chest. And that’s what I’m all about. I love to take chances. Taking risks is what makes life so exciting. The feeling you get of not knowing what the outcome will be. The sensation that one move could be the difference between victory and a humiliating defeat. The other side is a safe and secure life, but that’s for wimps and losers who just can’t take it. Their just not strong enough for failure. It doesn’t always pay off, but you can’t be afraid of failure. Otherwise you will fail, and you’ll never get up. And no matter how many times I fail, no matter how may bones I break, no matter how many matches I lose, I will always get back up. Because I’m not afraid to lose, I’m not afraid to fail, and I’m not afraid to try and fly. Zack is nearly out of breath now. It’s been a while since he’s felt so passionate about anything. He hunches over, his breathing hoarse as he slowly calms back down. He sits back down in his chair, crossing his legs, curling his fingers on the side of his cheek. But I’m not just here to have a good time. So you can bet I have big plans for MWE. I’m not going to come here and demand title shots and main event matches and all that. I know how the game is played, about pecking orders, and if you want something, you have to earn it. Besides, I’d much rather work for it anyway. Zack places his hand underneath his chin, adopting a mock look of hard concentration. So I was thinking I’d start off with a few victories under my belt. Then after that, maybe I’ll start a feud with someone. Build a name for myself after that, then maybe go for the US or X-TV Title, lose it, maybe a few more feuds, and then reach for that ultimate prize. You may have to climb from the bottom up, but that’s half the fun right there. I’m not about taking the easy road cause when you work for something and you finally get it that just makes it all the more sweet. Zack leans forward, placing his elbows on his knees. The expression on his face is unreadable. If one were to guess, it would be placed somewhere between thoughtfulness and resolution. Zack has spoken every word with as much truth as he can. Though he’s never thought of himself as being particularly articulate, his one goal in this promo is for his opponents, whether they be friend or foe, to understand him. If achieves at least that, then he’ll consider this a successful introduction. You may hear all this and think I’m nothing. That I’m just another crazy high-flyer who has a big mouth with little or no skill to back it up. That I’m a small boy playing a big man’s game. And you know what…you may be right. But there’s so much more to me. I’m… One minute to drop! Calls the voice of the pilot from off screen. Zack looks over the camera and nods, checking his equipment one final time. Zack heads to the hatch and wrenches it open, allowing a rush of wind inside. Zack gazes down at the land awaiting him below, breathing in deeply as the wind licks at his face. He turns to the camera, shouting so as to be heard over the rushing gale. I’M THE ULTIMATE THRILL SEEKER, ZACK CRASH! AND THE REAL THRILL RIDE…IS ABOUT TO BEGIN! With that, Zack salutes the camera, turns his attention to the outside, and with a single motion, leaps out into the infinite possibilities of the horizon. *Fade*
