Dinner

Jul 23 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
J.P. - UUW is dead... It's time to start over, time for a clean slate. Time to erase the past and create the future. J.P. Smith is in his locker room, packing his stuff for the very last time at a UUW show. For those that don't yet know, UUW has gone out of business. Alone, J.P. finds himself talking to himself out loud. J.P. - My future starts now. While I lost one opportunity, I gain a bigger, better opportunity. MWE has reopened, and I plan on making this my time. And to anyone that tries to get in my way, I'll literally boot fuck'em. J.P. wraps a towel around himself and walks towards the shower room. Once in, he removes the towel and begins his shower, all while still talking to himself. J.P. - Ever since I was a kid, I knew I wanted to be the best in the world. This, here in MWE, is where it all comes together. I can feel it deep inside, right to my bones, that my dreams will come true in MWE. I won't let it be any other way. I'll prove to everyone here in the locker room, just like in UUW, that I belong here, and in a matter of time, there will be gold with J.P. Smiths name on it. J.P. finishes up a very quick shower, dries himself off, and returns to the locker room. Oddly quiet now, he gets dressed in a fine navy blue suit, finishes packing his stuff, and exits the locker room, and eventually, the building itself. He wastes little time finding his truck and getting the hell out of Dodge, he's got plans tonight. He has a VERY long drive, all for a dinner date back home. And last time he had a date with Celeste, the girl he's trying to romance, he bailed. So he can't afford to be late. These thoughts are in mind as he speeds away into the night. Seemingly hours later, J.P. has made it back to his hometown for his dinner date. He's till a few blocks away, currently waiting for a red light to turn green. As he waits, he notices a Red Honda behind him. This same car has been behind him for a little while now. J.P. is a little worried, he's had a bit of a stalker for the past few weeks, and assuming this is said stalker, he tries to make out the driver, but the windows are tinted. Instead of waiting to see if this car is indeed his stalker, J.P. quickly cuts to the right and speeds down the street, hoping to lose the Honda. However, as predicted, the Honda makes the same turn and continues to follow J.P., who continues to make some speedy dashes and sharp turns, all in the hopes of avoiding whoever this psycho is. He can't just go to his date with this stalker around, who knows who it is, or what their intentions are. And in avoiding this person, he actually makes a dash right past the restaurant he's supposed to be eating at any moment now. Eventually, this chase gets boring. J.P. drives past a small alleyway, and takes note of it. Circling around the block, his next pass at the alleyway, he get's his car right down deep in there, shut's his car off, lights and all, and waits. Silently, he waits for the Honda to drive past. And sure enough, it does. About three minutes after it passes, J.P. decides the coast might be clear, so he pulls out of the alleyway and finally makes his way to the restaurant to meet Celeste for dinner. He get's there, still no sign of the Honda. After parking his truck, he gets out of the vehicle, straitens his suit a bit, and makes his way inside, where a finely dressed host awaits him. Host - Good evening Sir. How may I help you this evening? J.P. - Yes, good evening. Umm, dinner reservation for two. Smith. The host looks down at the reservation book, searching for the name. Host - Here we are. Smith. Your female friend is already waiting for you. I'll show to your table right away. J.P. - Ok great. Thanks. The Host leads J.P. to his table. When he arrives, J.P. finds Celeste sitting there, wearing a nice little black dress. Waiter - Here we are Sir. A Waiter will be back with your menus in a few moments. J.P. - Thank you. The host walks away as Celeste stands up and gives J.P. a hug. After the warm felt embrace they sit down at their table. Celeste - So? How have you been? It feels like it's been forever! I'm so glad you could make it this time. I didn't want to interrupt your busy day. So I saw you won your match against that fruity guy who likes to look at himself in the mirror all the time! Celeste has a big smile on her face which causes J.P. to crack a chuckle. J.P. - Yeah, I sure did... Speaking of which, I got some good news, and bad news... Celeste - Oh? Do I even want to hear this? What did you get yourself into this time? J.P. again begins to lightly laugh as a waiter approaches them. He's tall, skinny, and maybe a little flaming. Waiter - Hello this evening! Here are your menus, and I will be back in a few moments to take your order! J.P. - Can we actually have some water to start please? Waiter - Of course! I'll be RIGHT back! The waiter walks away at a fast pace to receive the water, once again leaving J.P. and Celeste by themselves. Celeste - So, you were talking about good news and bad news? Are you trying to scare me half to death? J.P. - Yep! Hahahaha... Anyway, what first? Good or bad? Celeste - Well... Gimme the bad news to start I guess. J.P. - Alright, bad news... I lost my job. Ultimate Underground Wrestling has, well, gone under, if you will. Celestes is shocked by this news. Celeste - Wait, what? How are you going to support yourself now? What are you going to do? J.P. - Well that's the good news. While UUW may have closed, another company, a bigger company, MWE, has reopened, and all UUW talents have free contracts in, myself included. J.P. waits for a few moments to see how Celeste reacts to this news. Celeste - MWE? What's that? Now J.P. is the one with the shocked face. J.P. - Please tell me that you're not serious. You're joshing with me. Mayhem Wrestling Entertainment is one of the top wrestling companys ever. This is the place where all my dreams will come true. This is where I will become a bonafide megastar! J.P. slightly raises his voice a bit in excitement just as the waiter returns with the water. Waiter - Oh, are we excited over here! Anyway, have you decided on a meal yet folks? J.P. - No meal yet, but if you don't mind, we'll take some of your finest complementary bread sticks while we look at the menu again. Waiter - Oh, of course... I'll be right back... The waiter seemed slightly less excited as before as he leaves for the breadsticks. Once again, J.P. and Celeste are alone again, so J.P. continues his thoughts on MWE. J.P. - Anyway, the first show is in a couple of days, and I'm going to be fighting on it. I've got a match against some weirdo named Supernova Vega Knight, and I'm pretty sure I got this in the bag. This guy don't know if he's coming or going, he don't know if he should love you or hate you. Celeste - He sounds kinda creepy. Do I even wanna see him? J.P. - He might be creepy, but he's nothing to be afraid of. I mean, take a look at his name for example. "Supernova" sounds like such a sissy name. No way are a bunch of little stars going "KABOOM" going to scare me. All he is to me is a punk in a mask. Celeste - Punk in a mask? J.P. - Oh yeah, his mask. He wears a fruity black and white mask when he makes his way to the ring. It makes him look like a fairy or something. As Celeste chuckles at the fairy remark, the waiter approaches, looking almost a little insulted. Without speaking, he places the breadsticks in the center of the table before reaching for his notepad and a pen. Waiter - How about now? May I take your order now? J.P. and Celeste takes a quick flip through their menus, then flash a quick glance at each other, then back at their menus, then each other, then the waiter. J.P. - You know what sir? We are just not ready to order yet, we can't decide on what we would like to eat. As soon as we make up our minds, we will be in touch with you somehow. The waiter is visually very annoyed, but he stays professional. Waiter - Ok that would been fine sir. Just let me know when you two need me. The waiter clearly rolls his eyes as he walks away again as J.P. is starting to find annoying the waiter fun. But he was talking about his wrestling, so he get's back to that. J.P. - But yeah, this Supernova guy ain't no big deal in my opinion. In fact, the only thing he has on me is that he's always got some hot babes leading him to the ring... Hmm, I wonder if I beat him, if these babes wouldn't mind rubbing their asses on me... Celeste - You PIG! J.P. - What did I say? Celeste - I don't want to hear about you and what you want to do with those two fucking whores. I can't believe you just told me all that shit. Is this why you brought me here? To tell me about these two sluts? J.P. starts to smile and chuckle a bit, despite Celeste being furious. Celeste - What the fuck is so funny? J.P. - I was kidding! Calm down! If I was talking about a guy you thought was cute, you'd have done the same thing. But anyway, back on topic... SuperFairyNova is nothing but a chicken shit. Last time anyone saw him, he hopped the security barrier to attack Zack Crash from behind. If that's not a pussy move I don't know what is. Celeste - Zack Crash? Now THERE is a hottie! J.P. - See my point? As they chuckle, they finally take a good few moments to look at their menus. They go over them a few times before Celeste breaks the silence. Celeste - So... This place kinda sucks. You wanna just bail? J.P. - You read my mind! The two get up from their table and quickly made a speedy walk towards the door. As they reach it, J.P. just happens to look behind him to see the waiter standing by the now empty table, furious with his last two customers. As the two reach the parking lot, laughing at the flaming waiter, J.P. begins to look around the lot. He wants to make sure the Red Honda from earlier isn't around, and to his delight, he doesn't see it, or any cars for that matter. He's starting to get paranoid about the whole situation, this stalker could be dangerous, could be harmless, but who knows who they are, what they want, why- Celeste - So where to now? Being interrupted in thought, J.P. comes back down to this world again as he chuckles. The two are now at the truck as they continue talking while they drive into the night sky.
