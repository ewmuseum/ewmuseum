The Static,; You'll learn to fear it.

Sep 7 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The scene opens to Earl's Teck Pub somewhere in the UK, Aeron and Dusty are seen sitting on a wooden oak chair. In front of them, a table with two coffee cups placed lazily on a pair of coasters. The pubs citizens shuffle by with large pints in their hands. The bartender cleaning a glass with a dish cloth. In the corner, two amps, a microphone and a pedestal. A large neon sign floating behind the pedestal, "Karaoke". It was a classic sunday night at almost any bar, the air filled with a dense but translucent blanket of fog. draped just under the roof. Aeron takes a sip of coffee before closing his eyes and leaning back in his chair with his hands behind his head. Aeron: Barkeep. clicker please. The bartender sighed as he tossed the small remote to Aeron, who hands it to Dusty. Aeron: Change it to MWE, I wanna see if either of the opponents have decided to even throw in a small promo, Im starting to think that I'm scaring them off. Dusty nods sleepily before hitting the buttons, the channels changing. Dusty stops on the channel that shows the MWE Promos and etc. They watch, rolling their eyes on constant occasions. Unsatisfied Aeron speaks. Aeron: Aiden, Aiden, Aiden....you do realize that the fans dont tune in for losers like you, Fear or Sync. They tune in for the real excitement, the real deal, the stuff they come and pay good money to watch, like Me, Kanto, Sonny, Michaels, Sinc. We are the reason they tune in. Your just the stepping stone that builds the excitement for the higher-ups. I know its kind of ironic saying that while most of the time Im either opening or the next match after, but you know what, Im alright with that, you start with a bang you end with a bang. Thats how things work 'round here. The only bang your ending with, is my fist breaking your jaw like a twig. Aeron takes another sip of his coffee before taping the half asleep Dusty on the shoulder. Aeron: Dusty, go set up the next shot we talked about. Dusty jerked away, nodded slowly and got up before stumbling out of the bar. The bell above the door dings as the door closes behind Dusty. Aeron: Aiden, I don't need a congratulations from the likes of you, thats like asking someone with no arms for a high five. Its just rude and stupid. As for that "Wall of Aiden", I'll take care of that when I get the okay from Dusty. You dont think 'Im going to go any further? Are you kidding? I am a fucking bulldozer in a playground. Aeron sighs loudly before taking the last bit of his coffee in his mouth and swishes it around before swallowing it, his phone beeps. Dusty had text him. Aeron: Ah, its ready, come, lets walk and talk. Aeron stood up and stretched his back before exiting the bar without payment. Aeron: You attempt to make a point clear? Bro your as clear as toast, and just as bright and dull as a pencil. I am the epitome of evil, of destruction, you cant touch me. That belt, the one that's around my waist every week. That's staying that way. You say there's going to be one body? Lets make something clear, there are going to be TWO bodies lifeless, or at least, unconscious on or off the squared circle. I will be standing in the middle, with my hands in the air, one gripping MY gold. Aeron turned a street corner, to see a Dusty waving a sledge hammer at them. Aeron: ALRIGHT ALRIGHT, God damn it, were coming. Aeron picks up his pace and starts jogging. He stops and looks at an old abandoned building, with spray paint saying "The Wall Of Aiden" written in a bright blue. Aeron: See Aiden, This is your so called "Wall of Aiden" lets see what happens to it when I walk into it shall we? Dusty hands Aeron the sledge hammer. Dusty than shoos off the Cameraman setting the camera down. Aeron who than grips the sledge hammer in his hands happily as he starts to walk up to it. He takes a baseball stance and readies the sledge hammer. He swings it taking out a whole chunk of the wall that starts to crumble. He moves over and after about two more swings, most of the wall is in peices on the ground. A triplette of bricks stands infront of Aeron, who pushes it over with his hands. Aeron lets the sledge hammer fall to the ground before clapping the dust off his hands. Aeron: And there we have it folks, thats what happens, I tear it down, and tonight, when I come face-to-face-to-face with you low lifes, I'm going to rip your bodies to shreds. Your going to wish you hadn't come across me. And as for that facepaint comment, I'm going to let it slid, because I'll show you why someone does whatever the fuck I want in the real world. Aeron starts walking off with Dusty with the wall in ruins behind him. Aeron: Do you think that flashing your eye candy is actually going to win you anything? Dude, if that was the case, I could go to a strip club and I'd be the most popular person in MWE. But that isnt the case, its how we fight, how long we can go in a fight, how hard we can beat the hell out of the other. There are a few that can take that title. You arn't one of them, I'm going to end your segmant of this show with one line. Aeron stops for a second before looking into the sky chuckling. Aeron: You got manhandled by a ballet teacher. Dusty bursts out in laughter, Aeron looks over at him with a wierd look. Aeron: Dude...? National TV... Dusty: Shit, really? Aeron sighs loudly before slapping the back of Dustys' head. Aeron: Now lets move on to James, he hasnt even shown up. Hes the Brock Lesnar of MWE, he joined a band to quit it to join another band...just to quit that one too, DAMN. He didnt show up to his last match, and I dont think hes going to show for this one, hes a minimal threat. Oh, Aiden, your ugly mug scares the TV shows, thats why it fades to static. All thats left in the fuzzy static mess is my face outline, cause you dont scare me, and by tonight, when your carried out in a stretcher, you'll learn to fear me. Aeron picks up a decent size rock that he had found off the streets, chucks it at the still in place camera, it shatters the lens. Fade to static with the outlining of Aerons face. --- Short, sweet, to the point. And yes we have the MWE channel, but I r hiding it :3
