The Life of the Rich and Famous

Aug 15 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
10 PM / Aiden Copane’s Private Estate MWE cameras turn on in front of a shady mansion surrounded by waves of endless pine trees. As the camera approaches the gates at the entrance of the estate you can hear the sound of chains rustling against the iron gates, soon after the gates inch forward a bit, and then creak open. The cameras approach the huge doors of the mansion and once they do you begin to hear the noise of the lock turning in the dead silence. The door cracks open slowly and the camera makes its way into a dark looking entrance chamber. It appears creepy only because of the silence and darkness but otherwise it appears to be properly maintained. The light from the lantern hanging outside the door begins to dim down as the door behind the camera now closes with a loud thump. The camera seems to take this as a cue to go up the stairs. The camera winds up along the staircase and reaches the second floor of the mansion. It goes along the railing and makes a right down a corridor. The camera suddenly turns to the right again to a door. Dull light is seen to come out from underneath the door; the door suddenly begins to open. Behind the door and into the low light you can see that the person that opened the door was a woman, a woman with brown hair going all the way to her shoulders. She is cupping her breasts with her hands as she doesn’t possess a bra. She turns around and walks further into the room; the camera follows her into the room and turns to see where she’s going. It’s a large room with a lot of furniture in it, in fact, most people would be happy to have an apartment in a room as finely furnished as this one. The main attraction of the room is the huge king size bed at the end of it. The woman who opened the door heads to the bed to join several other women who are missing several pieces of their clothing. Lying in the bed is none other than Aiden Copane. He is stripped down to nothing but his boxers and the women around him are catering to his every need. Aiden Copane: Ladies and gentlemen, welcome, to my humble abode. I hope you’ve had a look around, I would offer to give you a tour of the house but I sorta have my hands busy here. There’s that and there’s also the fact that a tour of this mansion would probably take me a few hours. But I know that isn’t why you people are here, you’re here for me, you’re here for a show of what I’m going to display on Monday, you people want to see if my bite is as big as my bark. Monday is a display of my ability, tonight, is my ability to talk shit, which I’m good at so, until Monday night, all I can say is, my bark is pretty big. Aiden begins to lift himself off of his bed and puts his legs over the side as one of the women who was pampering him climbs on the bed around the other side and slides her fingers through Aiden’s long blonde hair. Aiden closes his eyes through this and climbs up off the side of the bed. He walks across the room and onto his tiger carpet and gets a black shirt off of a fancy chair, he walks back over to the bed and the women again begin to serenade him and pass their hands across his chest and hair. He then takes the shirt and whips it softly at all three of them, sort of giving them the cue to leave. They all get dressed and leave the room leaving Aiden alone; Aiden goes over to the wall and turns the light switch all the way until the room is brightly illuminated. Then he turns toward the camera. Aiden Copane: As all of you know very well, I’m not a newcomer to this game. I’ve been here before, I’ve been in a tournament, a few as a matter of fact, and I’ve pulled off some pretty sick promos. Just because I haven’t done so in awhile doesn’t give Jekyl the victory. I watched his promo, and, it was mediocre to be honest with you. I almost fell asleep as I watched you trot along aimlessly. I mean, how are you going to attract fans when you put your fans to sleep? Literally. So, what I’ve decided to do is give you a few pointers to make your stay in MWE a little more enjoyable, I mean, you want a smooth ride don’t you? Isn’t that why you’re a clown, I mean, while we all work hard to make a living you walk around in your ridiculous costume and cut people’s mouths open. You haven’t worked hard a day in your life. My sarcasm seems to be coming back to me, every word I say is getting me back into the groove of things Jekyl, something you probably don’t want to see happen. For your sake… But, anyways, let’s get back to the point, Jekyl, and…I knew this moment would come, this is why I’m always prepared. Aiden goes over to his walk in closet and goes all the way to the back, he gets on his tippy toes and grabs a pair of jeans hanging overhead. He reaches into the back pocket and pulls out a small crumpled up piece of paper. He opens it up and squints to read… Aiden Copane: Aeron Beowulf. What has the world come to, this is a wrestling federation, not a freak show. But I guess you got me there Jekyl, what’s more entertaining than to see a clown and the “God of War” walking across a highway. Oh, but Jekyl, the next time you need money for the bus, just stop by and I’ll lend you one of my cars, you can pick between the Ferrari F430 Spider and the Lamborghini Murcielago LP460. Sorry, I don’t have any clever pictures but you get my point you low class fuck. Do you want some more constructive criticism or have you had enough? Of course you haven’t… Just as Aiden begins to open his mouth again there’s a knock on the door. Aiden Copane: Who is it? Nikky? Mindy? Veronica? Sarah? Mataya? Vanessa? Hailey? Kim Kash: (From outside the door) Aiden? Who are those girls? Darrell says the jet is fueled and ready for flight, did you pack my bags for me sweety? Aiden Copane: Baby, I knew that was you, I was being sarcastic, and yeah, of course I packed your bags baby, it’s right here, hold on a second baby, tell Darrell we’ll be there soon, I just need to fix up my hair a second. Aiden begins to look around the room rapidly and finds her empty luggage laying on the floor in the closet, he grabs it and puts it on top of the bed and goes to her dresser and opens it up, he starts throwing random pieces of clothing into the bag and stops when he gets to a red thong. Aiden Copane: (With thong in hand) How come she never wore this for me? I’ve never seen her in these in my life, for fuck sakes. It doesn’t matter; let’s hurry it up here Aiden. Kim Kash (From outside): So other than talking to yourself are you doing anything constructive in there Aiden? Aiden shakes his head and ignores her, he closes the piece of luggage and zips it up, he places it on the floor and goes to the bathroom and looks at his face and hair in the mirror and fixes himself up, then he walks over to the door and unlocks it letting Kim into the room. Kim Kash: Give me my luggage, are you sure you packed things that match into here? I need to look good in Detroit. Aiden Copane: (Rolling his eyes) You need to look good for who? Anyone you’re trying to look good for in Detroit other than me? Kim Kash: Yeah Aiden, I have a squeeze in every city we go to for shows. By the way, if the schedule changes for any reason, tell me! I don’t want to annoy any of my boy toys. Aiden Copane: Oh, aren’t you a fucking comic? … How long did Darrell say its gunna take us to get to from Toronto to Detroit, this gel I put in my hair earlier can’t be at high altitudes too long, it said so on the bottle. Kim Kash: I’m sure you’ll be fine Aiden, Darrel said something like 45 minutes so it shouldn’t be too bad for your precious hair, I swear, you’re needier and spoiled than me, and that’s saying something. Aiden takes the high road and avoids confrontation, he just walks out of the room with his own luggage and takes the stairs down to the front door. He puts his hand on the shiny door knob and turns it and walks onto his front yard, he then goes over to a small box shaped entranceway in his lawn, he steps in and holds the door open for Kim and presses a button on the side. The doors close and the elevator moves down slowly, once it reaches its destination a bell chimes and the doors open once again. Aiden takes a step outside the elevator and looks down the runway he built under his mansion and the jet that he would be on in a seconds at the right end. Aiden Copane: It doesn’t matter how many times I glance at it, every time I’m down here I just have to applaud myself. Where did you guys think all the money from the old MWE went? That’s right baby, right here. This is where every single cent I made in MWE is invested in, an underground runway. No, Jekyl, maybe not as cool as Batman’s Bat Cave, but it fits my needs. Just as Aiden finishes this comment the camera focuses on the entrance of the jet, all of a sudden everything goes into slow motion as the jet hatch slides open and out comes Darrell, in an Armani suit and sunglasses, the shot continues in slow motion until Darrell gets to the last step of the ramp. Darrell: Aiden, nice to see you arriving so late, as usual. You ready to get on board? Detroit is waiting for Aiden Copane. Aiden Copane: Yeah, I think I’m about set to leave, did you remember to bring the liquor on board like I told you too? Darrell: Yep, the pilot is enjoying it as we speak but I did separate some and put it in your luggage when you were with all the ladi…when you were taking a shower. Aiden Copane: Yeah, that’s what I thought Darrell, and what about the pilot? Exactly how much is he drinking? The camera stays back and watches Darrell, Kim, and Aiden board the jet, after a few seconds the jet approaches the runway and does a final check before an enormous hatch covering the size of a football field miles on the complete opposite of the jet opens up and lets sunlight in, after a few seconds the hatch is big enough to allow the jet clearance and so it speeds up and goes into the air and out the hatch, the hatch then closes back into position and the lights in the underground runway flicker off. The scene shifts back onto the jet where Darrell is pouring three glasses of champagne. Aiden and Kim look each other in the eyes give a toast. Darrell then goes to the back of the jet and into the pilot’s cabin leaving Kim and Aiden alone. Aiden quickly scooches over behind Kim and begins to kiss her on the cheek. Kim’s head goes up with pleasure and Aiden pulls it further back as he tugs on her hair lightly. Kim then turns around and they begin to make out. Kim pushes Aiden back and climbs on top of him and kisses him, he starts to take off his shirt and right as this is going on Darrell walks in on them. Darrell: Oh for god sakes, we’re on a plane! Aiden, Kim, can’t you control yourselves until we land, Jesus Christ, Aiden, haven’t you joined the mile hi…forget it, never mind. Kim Kash: What were you about to say Darrell? Something about the mile what? Pilot: (Via intercom) We might be getting into a bit of turbulence lady and gentlemen so if you could fasten your seatbelts that would be great. Darrell: Actually, I think I should check on the chicken parmegian I was microwaving back there, go fasten your seat belts it might get a little bumpy. Darrell goes to the back and gets a plate out of the microwave as Aiden and Kim fasten their seatbelts, Darrell gets back and does the same. Aiden Copane: Well now that the wind has decided that I’m not gunna get laid on board this jet I might as well continue to talk shit about my opponent on Monday night. I forgot where I was actually… Oh yeah, I was saying how boring watching you and freakshow do absolutely nothing. I mean, I thought it was pretty boring, but when I had to watch as you guys cruised along the highway to Detroit I thought I was gunna slit my wrists. Have you ever heard of editing? Or at the least turning off the damn camera, since you’re probably too poor to afford a good editing job, I mean, if you have to rob banks, honestly, how poor are you? I mean, riding the bus? That’s filthy Jekyl. And right when I thought it was over, and the snoozefest was finally coming to a close, what do you do? You forget to turn the camera off as you sleep! You literally made it a snoozefest, and man do… Aiden looks at what Darrell is doing. He’s eating his chicken parmegian. Aiden Copane: Hey Darrell, is that the Armani suit I bought you to look nice in front of everyone in Detroit? Darrell: Why yes Aiden, it is, it’s nice of you to notice, I look pretty fly huh? Aiden Copane: Yeah, I had to notice, you know what would make it look even better though? If you didn’t spill fucking tomato sauce on it you jackass… for fuck sakes forget it, you just pissed me off, I mean, all you do is waste my fucking time and money and now I give you a present and you get shit all over it? Forget it Darrell…and as for you Jekyl, you snore too much, so use some of that money you stole on an editor that’ll make your promos acceptable so I don't have to watch you drive, or sleep, as for me, I won’t bore you into watching the plane fly, this is where I say good bye. See you later fuckers, we’ll see on Monday who bites harder. Static.
