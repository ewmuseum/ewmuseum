A Picture is worth 1000 Words.

Sep 11 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
*The MWE cameras show a scene that appears to be Brian Michaels’ home. At first, it shows his home from the outside, highlighting the balcony especially. The camera then zooms inside where Brian Michaels is sitting in a chair at a table with a finely-sequined red robe. He has reddened eyes, taking out an envelope with ‘’Rated-X’’ written over it. He carefully opens the envelope and sees a bucket-load of Rated-X pictures come falling out. Michaels puts a hand on his face, sighing deeply. While looking at the pictures spread out over the table, Michaels uses his other hand to grab his coffee. He is noticeably trembling as the coffee almost spills from his shaking. He takes a sip of his cup and puts it back down. He sighs again and then grabs the first picture, feeling the nostalgia. It is a picture of Rated-X’s first appearance in MWE. He sees him in the S.S. Rated-X, hiding in the background. Michaels can’t help but give a chuckle as an overview is heard.* D.C…what a whirlwind, to say the least. This is the very first picture of you in MWE I have…the night Rated-X debuted. You were there, Spiral was there…so was Amber, my ex-girlfriend. It was a great moment in history…a true turning point. See, I knew you’d excel D.C…it was just a matter of being patient. It was just a matter of taking one for the team once in awhile. But…you refused. You wouldn’t do it…but at this point in time, things were looking good. You had D.C., you had Downward Spiral, you had Brian Michaels…it all looked good. *Michaels shakes his head, before putting that picture to the side. He carefully grasps the next picture in the stack, taking a minute to admire the features. It is a picture of D.C. kneeling in front of Alejandro Rodriguez’s car. Michaels gives a sly smile.* This is the last picture I have of the real D.C. The D.C. who didn’t backstab the guy who showed him the ropes…no, this D.C. put a smile on everyone’s face. His ridiculous antics were hilarious and a spark that MWE desperately needed. That night, I bailed him out of jail. In the car, on the way to arena, we talked. We talked…about life. We talked about all the shit we both went through growing up and how together…we’d raise hell for everyone. It was a dream we had…and for awhile, I think it’s fair to say that we lived the motherfuckin’ dream. But then… *Michaels looks down at his right leg and takes off a bit off the robe to reveal several scars all over his leg. He admires them for a few seconds before the camera looks back at him. He sighs and grabs the next picture. It is a picture of D.C. holding the X-Television Championship backstage with Brian Michaels looking like a proud father figure. He grabs a napkin, putting it over his eye to hide some tears. He shakes it off before twiddling his nose and looking at the picture again.* This…was the turning point. The turning point of not just your career, but mine. See, you won this X-Television Championship at Wrestle-Wars. Devlin Sharpe won his match at Wrestle-Wars. I lost. I lost the MWE World Heavyweight Championship…and, I was pissed off. I was pissed off, at myself. But, at the same time…I was happy for D.C. I was proud…he had an accomplishment to his name. Although that night, I went off into my limo. I thought about the loss for a second…and the next thing I knew…this happened. *Michaels points at his leg again, closing his eyes in pain, thinking about the fateful night where he was run over. He covers his leg again and puts it down, grabbing the next picture. The picture is a close-up on D.C.’s maniacal face, driving his car into Michaels’ limo. Michaels shudders at the image and slides it next to the others. He slowly rises from his chair, still in a somewhat crouched position. He breathes heavily, before his thoughts go away and Michaels begins speaking as himself.* Michaels: D.C…we’re men. You used to be respected…held in high regard…and even sometimes feared, by your peers. D.C., you give off an aura that nobody else in MWE can say they give off. You’ve got the ‘’it’’ factor…D. You are a man that a lot people want to be like. *Michaels scratches his head and chuckles a bit, still looking directly at the camera.* Michaels: At least…they used to want to be like you. They used to want to be…D.C. The MWE World Champion, the X-TV Champion, one of the leaders of Rated-X…the ass-kicker, the loud-mouth, the shit-talker. But now D.C. you’ve lost your aura. You lost your mystique and you panicked…so what did you do? You took me out. You took me out not because I was being selfish, no, no….YOU…are the selfish one here D.C. *Brian walks around his living room, admiring the paintings on display. He touches them, studying their features.* Michaels: Patience was a virtue and D.C., you had none of it. You felt that I was in your way. So instead of asking me to give you your shot, you took matters into your own hands. You cruel, son of a bitch… *Michaels’ whispering begins to rise into a deep and booming voice that is spread throughout his entire house.* Michaels: You think…I forgot about what you did? You think…running me over is something I can just shake off?! You’re wrong. You’ve been wrong your entire life. From living in L.A…to the indy matches…you’re wrong. *Brian shakes his head in disapproval as he starts to get fired up from all the emotion.* Michaels: D.C….LIFE…is too short to spend it on sex, lies, and drugs. But none of that matters anymore. You’re in the match of your FUCKIN’ life…don’t give me the typical D.C. rant about how you’re underrated and hated because D.C., I am sick to DEATH about your bullshit complaining about how D.C. is greater than MWE. *Michaels licks his lips, and starts to twitch violently.* Michaels: D.C. didn’t create MWE…MWE created D.C., you ungrateful motherfucker! I fight for three things in my life. The honor of my career. The honor of my girlfriend, and the honor of this, motherfucking promotion. I will be damned and fucked in the ASS…before I let someone stomp all over it like you have done for the past few weeks. D.C., I told you that I would do ANYTHING…to get a match. I told you I’d break the goddamn limit to make it happen and it did…I’m not worried about the mental part. Mentally, you’re not here right now. Which brings me to my argument. D.C…has never, EVER…given it his all in a wrestling match. *Michaels tilts his head and ponders why he said that. He puts a hand on his chin and puts on a thinking look. He then takes it off and ‘’aahs’’ to himself.* Michaels: He’s beaten the best, there’s no doubt. From Alejandro Rodriguez, to Sonny Williams, to Aiden Copane, to David Whitman…he’s done it all. You can’t make the argument that D.C. isn’t one of the greatest of all-time…but I don’t think D.C. has given it his all ever. You can say you’ve beaten them all, you’ve held more Titles, you’ve got a better-looking girlfriend, but at the end of the day D.C…none of it matters. What does matter? Funny you ask… *Brian chuckles a bit and runs his hands through his hair, smirking a bit and showing off some of the old Brian Michaels off.* Michaels: Because D.C. relies on people falling down and staying down when he hits them. The difference between Brian Michaels and everybody else…is that I’m going to stand up. *Michaels lays down on the floor and slowly rises to his feet.* Michaels: See you may knock me down, you may knock me out, but I won’t stay down. Why? Because I’m not scared of D.C. I will get back up on my feet, square up, and punch you dead in the eyes. You knock me out, I make you bleed. You hit me with a chair, I hit you with barbed wire. You lock me in a cage, and I swear I will break that shit open and give you a MOTHERFUCKING concussion. *Michaels continues twitching violently and looks at the camera with a dead heat in his eyes.* Michaels: D.C. if you are what you say are…underrated and hated…then prove it. If you’re better than me like you say you are…prove it. *Brian walks around the room, violently slapping himself, his face turning a bright red from the vicious slaps.* Michaels: You can hit me all you want, I will keep getting up. I will do it for MWE. I will do it for the right to say that I…made D.C…shut…the fuck…UP! D.C...the shit is on. The time is noww, you've avoided me for a year, I outright REFUSE to take your shit anymore. MWE is back, it's ALIVE and I'm not going to stand for people like YOU...killing it. I've said it once, and damnit I'll repeat it 'till my fuckin' TOUNGE comes off! MWE made D.C. what he is today and if you think otherwise then you're a biased prick living off the name and hype he made off of MWE. We spilt blood, sweat, and tears for the FANS...not so D.C. could run off, a year later, talking about how HE made MWE. *Michaels shakes his head in disapproval.* Michaels: You didn't make MWE. You were a tiny piece of puzzle. A worker on the extremely long list of guys who busted their asses for paychecks! And you have the nerve to say MWE wouldn't MWE without D.C? *Michaels chuckles to himself as he puts his hands on his reddened face. He sits in his comfortable chair again and gives off deep grunts, almost snarling directly at the camera’s direction.* Michaels: Until you have the balls…to look me in the eye and tell me that you’re better than me, I won’t believe it. Until you’re able to beat Brian Michaels one-on-one in a match, I won’t believe it. D.C., you have overcome the odds again and again, but you used me. You used me to get to the top and now D.C….I’m sending you back to the bottom. I’m gonna send you to the one place where your ego can’t stand. I can’t make a guarantee that I’m gonna win this match…but I know and everybody in MWE knows…that this is a war that I can’t risk losing. I am fighting for my company and I am fighting for my LIFE against this sadistic bastard I used to call my friend. Michaels: Times have changed D.C. The times of Rated-X are no more. The times of degenerate plotting…is no more. The laughing is gone, the smiles, the joking, all irrelevant now. They don’t mean a goddamn thing to me anymore, and neither do any of these pictures. And that’s why…I’m gonna let these burn. I’m going to let them burn one-by-one…I’m going to burn, not just the pictures…but the memories. D.C. and Brian Michaels is no more. We are alone, D.C., there is no more running, there is no more hiding…this….is the real shit. D.C….vs. Brian Michaels. MWE Champion vs. MWE Champion, the real FUCKIN’ DEAL! *Michaels breathes heavily from all the angry yelling but manages to calm down after awhile. He speaks in a raspy voice, as the camera gets a close-up on his deep green eyes, full of emotion, mainly anger. Michaels quietly leans over to reach the table. He grabs the picture of Rated-X debuting on the S.S. Rated-X and throws it on the ground. He takes a deep breath and promptly finds a cigar and lights a match, throwing it on the ground near the picture. The picture begins to burn as Michaels steps in front of the fire.* Michaels: Because a wise man by the name of Tim Fitzgerald once told me that to be the man in Professional Wrestling…you’ve got to pay a price… *Brian leans over towards the table again and takes the picture of D.C. holding the banana near Alejandro Rodriguez’s car and flips it right into the fire, causing an even bigger set of flames. Brian runs his hands through the air as the smoke alarm comes on in his house. He ignores it and begins to speak even louder, standing ahead of the now-building flames.* Michaels: You’ve got to bleed….you’ve got to sweat… *Brian again leans over towards the table, standing over on the side. He grabs the picture of D.C. proudly holding the coveted X-Television Championship in his hands and absently throws it behind him.* Michaels: You’ve got to cry…you’ve got to SUFFER D.C.… *Brian grabs the last picture of D.C. with the maniacal look in his eyes. Michaels twitches feverishly with emotion and anticipation. He looks up at the roof of his house and hears an ambulance siren in the distance. He looks around, seeing the once-small fire turn into a large set of flames. He angrily tosses the last picture into the flames, causing the flames to widespread. Michaels tears up and speaks in a voice loaded with passion.* Michaels: I’m not afraid to tear the roof off my house…or the Quicken Loans Arena. But until then…you’re just another…walkin’ *Michaels holds up five fingers.* Michaels: Talkin’… *Michaels brings down his pinky finger.* Michaels: Piece… *Michaels brings down his finger.* Michaels: Of… *Michaels brings down his thumb.* Michaels: SHIT! *Michaels brings down his index finger, leaving him with his middle finger directed exactly at the camera and the entire scene suddenly engulfed in flames.* OOC: At the beginning with my words in italic, it's Michaels' ''thoughts''
