Hell Has Arrived

Sep 16 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Handler's note: Sorry if this first bit is a bit dry and doesn't seem to make much sense, but I wanted to give a bit of a back story with my first RP. I'm only doing it this once so you can see where this character comes from, so you won't have to worry about this happening again. :)] I tell you this story, because where this tale ends, my own begins... Jace Gryphon was born into a rough blue-collar family in central Pennsylvania. He grew up a rough life, with an alcoholic father and a mother who died when he was still young. The closest thing he had to a real family that he could count on was his brother, Michael. However, even they weren't that close, especially after Michael turned to Christianity while searching for answers to why his life had turned out this way. Jace didn't see things the same way and became an agnostic himself, leading to several heated debates and arguments with his brother. But I'm getting off track. Jace, much like his father, turned to alcohol at an early age. Though, he resented his father for being an alcoholic, he vowed that he himself would never become an alcoholic. He just used it as a vice. He was forever getting into fights at the bars he frequented, which led him to a few brief stays in the county jail. He got quite a reputation for this, which ultimately led to him being framed for killing a co-worker. It was during this stay in prison when Jace met a man that would change his life. He was sharing a cell with a professional wrestler from some unknown federation. After sharing their stories, his cellmate gave him a name and number to contact when, or if, he got out. He thought Jace had the potential to make it far as a professional wrestler. I'll try to speed this up a bit now and get on to how this ties into Becky's story. Jace bounced around from fed to fed for a while, due to fed closings, or arguments with owners. It took him quite some time until he finally found what he could call a home in the Viking Wrestling Federation. It was while he was the World and Tag Team Champion of the VWF that his path crossed Becky's. He knew that he would be asked to defend both titles at the same pay-per-view, and the World Title was set to be defended in one of those most grueling matches the VWF staff had ever devised. After some persuading, he finally talked the General Manager of the VWF into allowing him to find a replacement for his Tag Team match. Jace had been watching Becky in the Indy federations for some time, and felt that she would be a perfect fit to team with his fiancee, Kirsta Lewis. Just then, another voice interrupts the narration Voice: "If only he'd known better..." The voice trails into an evil bit of laughter as the scene finally come to light. It's the backstage area at an uncertain MWE event. Becky Hellian is standing in front of a MWE logo sign. She's wearing a black corset top with dark violet lace at the top and bottom, a matching black mini-skirt with violet lace around the bottom, black knee-high boots covered in several buckles, and fishnet stockings. Her black hair falls in loose ringlets to the middle of her back and her lips are stained black and twisted into a devilish smirk. Becky: "They say the best way to make it to the top in this business is to make your presence known early, and do it in a big way. That's exactly what I did in the VWF. I used Jace to make my way in the door, and the first thing I did was screw him over. Before my match even happened, I took his poor little girlfriend and left her in a bloody heap in a locker room closet. Then, I went out to the ring without a partner, and handed the Tag Team belts right away. The top dog in the game trusts me to take over his title reign, and I hand it all away without a fight. I'd say that's quite the way to make an impact." Becky pauses and turns to look at the MWE logo poster behind her and smirks. Becky: "I can't say I have the luxury of being hand-fed such an opportunity here. I don't have any connections on the inside to screw over. I'm not being handed a championship title on my way in through the door. I truly am starting at the bottom of the ladder here, aren't I?" The camera pans to a side where a ladder is set up. Becky walks over to it and sits on the bottom rung. Becky: "So here I sit, the new face in this federation. The bottom rung of the ladder. But don't let that fool you. I'm a top-rung competitor, and I know it. And you will all soon come to know it as well. It all starts at Monday Night Mayhem when I take on Blight." "Now, I don't really have any clue of what to expect in this match. I've never met the man before, and I can't say I was exactly interested in the MWE enough before signing a contract here to watch every match close enough to study every potential opponent I might face. What I do know, however, is what the critics are saying. There are those that are saying I'm going to lose the match, just on the principle that I'm the new face here, and Blight's been here for some time. Then there are those that are saying I'm going to win, not based on what they know of my wrestling prowess, but based simply on the fact that 'Blight is Blight', whatever that means." Becky shakes her head and stares off into the distance in silent thought for a moment. Another shake of her head snaps her back to reality, as though she forgot what she was doing here for a moment. Becky: "Well the critics can say what they want to say, it doesn't change reality a bit. Just because no one here at MWE knows what I'm capable of, that doesn't mean that I immediately don't stand a chance. All the different, it means I stand that much more of a chance, due to the unpredictability of my very nature. No one knows a damn thing of what to expect out of me, the least of all Blight. When Mayhem comes around, that's exactly what I plan on bringing... Mayhem. And when the final bell rings, and I stand victorious over the broken body of Blight, then I'll take my step up the ladder towards the top." As Becky ends the last sentence, she stands from her seat on the bottom rung of the ladder, and steps up to sit on the next rung up. She looks around the empty backstage area with a satisfied smile playing on her features. Becky: "Such a fitting name for you... Blight. Funny that you would choose such a name for yourself. A name synonymous with decay and death. A miserable state of affairs. Perhaps it's why you wear that mask. Did you get a blight on your face, maybe a bad case of acne, and now you wear that mask to hide your shame? Regardless, maybe the critics have a point with this catchy line, 'Blight is Blight'. You're a blight on this federation, and not only that, on this business as a whole. And who better to help with the transition from decay to death than the daughter of Satan herself?" "I don't care what kind of a dismal life you claim to have lived. I grew up in the shadowy halls of Hell. Any story you can come up with, I can tell you a story ten times just as dreadful, and it probably happened to me, not just someone I know. When you step into that ring with me, Blight, bring your best. There's no amount of punishment you can deal out to me that compares to anything I've seen before. So when our match is over, you can be thankful that I let you live another day, rather than drag you down to my father's domain. And when I'm through with you, it's onto my next victim, and another step up that ladder." Becky stood again, and took another step up the ladder, now sitting down upon the third rung from the bottom. She put her elbows on her thighs and brought her chin down to rest in her palms as she smiled off into the nothingness in front of her. Becky: "So what will that next step be? Well, no one really knows what the next step will be at this point. That waits to be seen. Until after I defeat Blight in my debut. After I make my presence known here in the MWE. After management posts the next card, that's when we'll know what my next step is. And that's how it will go on until the end of time, I suppose. One show to the next, and we wouldn't have it any other way, would we? Or would we? I can't say what step two, three, or four will be. But..." Becky stands on the ladder again and a devilish smirk plays over her face as she climbs up the ladder until she's standing on the top of it. Not just the highest rung, but the one above that labeled clearly "THIS IS NOT A STEP!" She holds her arms out to the side, almost like that scene from The Titanic and gazes up into the lights above her head. Becky: "Obviously, my ultimate goal is the same as everyone else's ultimate goal. The World title. Everyone wants it, don't they? And just because I'm immortal, it doesn't make me any different than the rest of the mortals here in that regard. Of course I want to hold the world title. But not yet...that time will come. For now, I'll settle to set my sights a little lower." Becky laughs and takes a couple of steps back down the ladder, taking a seat on a rung near the middle. Becky: "I've got to be honest, I think I'd like the way the X-Television Championship belt would look around my waist. So consider yourself put on notice, Zack Crash. This week, I've got a curtain jerker known as Blight to contend with. Next week, who knows who will be put in my way? But in time, like any other mortal, your time will come Zack. My eyes are on you now, and in time, that X-Television Championship will be mine. But for now, at Monday Night Mayhem... Blight, and the rest of MWE, I hope you're prepared. Because Hell has truly come to the MWE." Becky stands on the rung of the ladder where her feet had been resting once more, and this time jumps off of the ladder to the floor. She looks at the MWE logo on the wall again and lets out another laugh before walking off camera as the scene fades to black.
