The shortest RP in MWE history.; vs J.P. Smith

Aug 21 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The backdrop is solid white. Nothing around, nothing to hear, nothing at all. That being said, there is also not alot to describe for a few moments untill Eric Derf walks into view. He's dressed in a fine blue suit, white shirt and striped tie. Clean shaven, hair neatly cut, very professional looking. He comes in and looks right into the camera. You all know who I am. I am Eric Derf, one of the most exciting superstars in MWE today. I have beaten some of the best, I have had some of the most memorible moments in MWE history, and at Crowning Glory, I reach yet another of my goals. J.P. Smith. For our whole life we have been best friends. And we swore whoever made it big first would come back to bring the other up with them. And not one to break a promise, I did just that. I have taught you everything I know, and now your almost ready to go out and fight without my teachings. Eric stops speaking for a moment, for no reason other then silence for a moment. I'm not gonna go into detail on what I can do to you. You know what to expect from me, and I know what I'm expecting from you. So all I can say is good luck, you will need it. Because I have beaten the best of the best, and you, my friend, will fall like the rest. After quite possibly the shortest promo ever, Eric Derf simply walks off camera, which slowly fades out. OOC: Sorry bout this being so small AND late, I dropped the ball, but I figure this is better then a no show.
