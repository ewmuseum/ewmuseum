Colin's Thoughts #6 - Can England get over the hump?

Jun 18 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
On Monday Night Mayhem, Chris England has a shot to insert himself into MWE's first ever High Road match, in which he'd receive a Title shot. For England, it's a chance to redeem himself. It's a chance for him to get over his own personal hump. Ever since England has been in ICW, he's awlays been overshadowed by someone. At first, it was Jesse "The Semi" Anderson, who deserved his hype with an ICW Championship under his belt and a slot Main-Eventing Tribulation. Now, you could argue that he's being passed by Nic Kraven, another fast-riser. With Kraven moving up a division and Anderson on break, this is England's time. With only Jay Riddick and Anthony Clark in his way, all England has to do this Monday is NOT get pinned, and he's onto an X-Television Championship match at Independence Day. He knows this, and is fully hyped for the match. I spoke to him the other day, and he knows this is his chance to stand out and make a name for himself. In fact, most polls on MWE.com have the fans voting for him to advance to the High Road match, and even most wrestlers feel that England is now ready to embrace a Title shot. England is still fresh from his break, yet he came back firing last week, overcoming one of Brian Michaels' proteges, Peter Kaymakcian in a match that some expected him to lose. When I think about it now, I don't think the old Chris England would've been able to overcome ring rust. The new and fresh attitude that England's showing could be changing his life in and out of the ring now. Some might ask me if I think that Chris can finally come into his own and win an X-TV Championship at Independence Day. They might ask me if I think that his new attitude is fresh enough to carry him to heights he's never been before. You know what I might say? Yes.
