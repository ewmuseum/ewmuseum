The Protest Of The Main Attraction; Victim 1, VLR

Sep 12 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
OOC: Now, to those that are not a part of the Main Event match, I will only say that most of Staff knows what is going on as well as Derf and Crash (to an extent), and what this is meant to actually intend. This was not soley to my credit. The answers will come for all to know this Monday Night Mayhem. This is late, but it leads to MNM. The scene opens up slowly to show D.C. standing in front of an old house. He holds a mic in his hands as he cracks his neck and points behind him with his right thumb at the house. Just as he begins to speak, the sound crackles loudly. The scene scrambles, as if someone had recorded over the tape. The footage changes instantly into that of a spacious, dark room. In the middle of the spacious room there is a pillar. In a sitting position, head down, at this pillar, is a beautiful girl, her hair covering her hung face. And in front of her there is a man, dressed in black shoes, black pants, and a black hoodie with black gloves. He is wearing a skull mask that covers his mouth and nose, and the rest of his face is covered by a black bandana and sunglasses over it. He looks straight at the camera, presumably, as he talks with a forceful voice. Mayhem Wrestling Entertainment. That’s all it is. Entertainment. I mean, just ask the people behind it. The board, the directors. The masterminds. The geniuses who plot and write and make sure that every Monday without fail, the masses have an enjoyable product, packaged and ready for the world to devour. The fact that there are literally hundreds of men and women, who have fought and broken their bodies and minds and families for a chance to be recognized for their abilities, for a chance to maybe earn some money doing what they love, the fact that all these people get tossed around like names in a hat, like pawns on a chess board, like protagonists and antagonists in an amateur novelist’s bullsh*t debut… that’s entertainment also. Maybe not to you, but it is. To THEM. To the Top. He walks over to the girl, who raises her head, moving her long blond hair slightly out of the way to reveal her face. It’s Lindsey. Here. He takes a piece of paper out of his pocket, and tosses it in her lap. She tries to adjust it with her arm before he screams at her. HEY! You’re supposed to be tied up! Lindsey: RIGHT RIGHT She puts her arm back behind her as she sniffs loudly. She forces herself to position the paper on her lap and begins reading with a quaking voice. Lindsey: Dear Board of Directors. We hereby want you to know that your time has come. Every evil gets a day to pay and yours is approaching at breakneck speed. There is no use in running for cover. There is no use in hiding behind rules. It is over. You lose. The man picks up the paper, props it up and tosses it at the camera. All of a sudden, from behind the camera a loud creaking noise is heard, and as Lindsey and the masked man both turn their attention towards it the camera does so as well to reveal D.C. coming in through the door with and angry and confused look on his face. D.C.: WHAT THE FU- The camera cuts to static.
