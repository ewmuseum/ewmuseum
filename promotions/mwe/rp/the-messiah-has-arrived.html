The messiah has arrived; debut match rp

May 1 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The scene starts off with Sin inside of his house in an unknown area. Sin is shown sitting in a chair dressed in all black," A detailed shirt that reads nobodys sin's more then the Messiah himself" and black pants. Sin is watching tv gangland on the history channel to be exact when his phone rings. Sin: Who could this be...... (The man on the other end of the line is revealed as an old friend from a former federation Sin was apart of) Vin: Hey Sin nice to see your on to bigger things since the last time we spoke. Xcw closed down due to my absence and things but i gotta ask you.. What is your main goal in MNM? (Sin takes a deep breath before responding) Sin: My goal.. (snarles) that wouldn't be much of a gold if i told you now would it? I like to let my goals be seen and not heard you get me? Vin: You haven't changed a bit, I just wanted to check on you and wish you luck. Sin: Me? luck? You forget who im vin (Sin hangs up and goes to his fridge for a cold beer) Sin: So another debut another match against Blight and 3 nobody's trying to make an impact. Blight you always "try" but fail everyime you know it and i know it. Back in CCW i was the grasshopper and you were the teacher.. My friend have the tides changed. Im more powerful then you, more sadistic and more creative then you will ever be. And you know as well as i do it doesn't matter about Riddick,Clark,or Dexter because we have unfinished business. So while they pretend to be hardcore you and i will tear each other limb from limb and you know as well as i do that i don't obey rules this match might be for one fall. But to me this match and every match is a hardcore match. Dexter think's he has some vampire ability he claims to be like vampiro a 3rd rate piece of crap wrestler from wcw. Clark just a pretty face from the real world, and Riddick is just an old washed up steriod using gold medalist. (Sin downs his beer and walks outside his house. Its a nice day the birds are chirping, people talking amoungest themselfs and heading towards the beach. Sin just glances on when a young boy walks up to him) Young Boy- Hello Mr Stephens Sin: Good afternoon Young Boy- I can't wait to see your match this week. Ive followed your career for awhile now and i hope to see some big things from you. Sin- Such as what? Young boy- I wanna see you become Champion and rule the ring. Put fear into your opponents be a man of more actions then words. BE..... MY IDOL! (Sin looks at boy with a weird look. As the young boys eyes turn red) Sin- You wanna idolize me? Young boy- Yeah. Your amazing at what you do and you don't care about anybody but yourself. (Sin paces back and fourth while the boy just stares in amazement) Sin- no no you see what i do as a living and who i really am are two different things. Nobody will ever undrestand that. Hey kid head on home i got things to do. (The boy leaves as Sin scratches his head) Sin- This kid is just like how all of you were growing up. You all wanted to be something and your looks prove it. The differnce between me and all of you is that im a man of my word. If i say im gonna make you bleed i wil, if i say im gonn break your limbs i do. This match is my match and i will continue to raise hell and claim this company as my wrecking ground and nobody will stand in my way. (Sin grabs the camera man and knocks him in the head with the cam. He looks into the camera while on the ground and says.... "AFTER TONIGHT YOU WILL ALL FOREVER BE A MEMORY"
