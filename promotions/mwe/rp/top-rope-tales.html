Top Rope Tales; Semi RP

May 21 2009

Jesse Anderson

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The hot sun beats down on a visibly hot afternoon. As the scene turns downwards from the blistering sun, we find a red pickup truck, worn from time, experience, and rust. The exhausted truck sits in a parking lot that is scattered with few other cars outside of a small establishment. We move now into the cab of the truck where Jesse, The Semi, Anderson, clad in jeans and a leather jacket over top of a black shirt, sits in the driver seat beside his personal assistant, Dewey Smith, who is wearing an orange shirt and short tan slacks. Semi takes the keys from the truck ignition and squeezes them into his side jeans pocket. He speaks up, softly. Semi: We're here. Dewey takes a long look around to see a whole lot of nothing. Nothing but desert stretches in almost every direction under the bright sun. Dewey's expression is quite puzzled as he stammers. Dewey: Um... so are you going to tell me why we're in Arizona right now? When you said to prepare for a long trip, I thought you meant like to Italy for Tribulation... or something. Semi rolls his eyes upward then glances over at Dewey and speaks sharply. Semi: Nope. We're leaving for Italy tomorrow. Today I needed to come to Arizona. Semi quickly nods his head, signifying that he's finished dishing out information and he grabs the handle of his truck door. He pushes the door but it goes nowhere. He then thumps it hard with his broad shoulder and shoves it open with a squeal. Semi steps down and slams the door shut again. Dewey follows suit and gets out of the truck after removing his seatbelt. Dewey steps to the end of the truck bed where Semi is waiting and shields from the sun as he stares at the small building. You can see for the first time that he is wearing long white socks that stretch high up his calf. Dewey: What is this place? Dewey stops beside Semi and looks up to him, still with shielded eyes. Semi replies with little enthusiasm. Semi: It's a bar. Dewey replies loudly, shocked. Dewey: A bar?! Semi's smug face becomes angry and he clenches his teeth as he barks at Dewey. Semi: Yeah, twirp! A bar! Dewey's head sinks in between his shoulders and he flinches back a little as Semi shouts. He replies meekly. Dewey: I can't go in a bar; I'm only nineteen. Semi turns a glare towards the bar and slowly begins stepping towards it. Semi: Don't worry; you're with me. Dewey scurries to catch up with Semi. Dewey: Why are we coming all the way to Arizona for a bar? This place better have the best beer... Dewey trails off as Semi cracks a grin and chuckles. Semi's look and tone then become very serious all of a sudden. Semi: I'm looking for someone. Semi steps up to a tinted glass door with a red handle. He grabs the handle and tugs the door open. He steps through it and Dewey scurries in behind him before the door hits him in the back. The scene fades out to get the first good shot of the small brick bar with a large sign on the top, above the doors. Semi and Dewey slowly make their way through the cozy bar towards the counter while Semi scopes the place out. The air is dimly lit and scattered with smoke as Rock and Roll music plays lightly over-head. There are large lights, cameras, and stage props filling out part of the middle of the bar, in front of the bar counter. There are pin ball and arcade machines against one wall and the opposite side of the bar is taken up by seating booths. Semi makes his way around the props in the middle of the room and stops, feet behind a man in a green shirt sitting at the bar with his back turned to Semi. Feet in front of him is the bar tender, in a red shirt, with his back also turned as he appears to be wiping out a bar glass. The camera angle turns to behind the counter where you can see the grin on Semi's face. Semi is standing behind Thomas Williams, who is currently listening to the bar tender, James Whitman, speak from behind the bar. These men are also known as JESTers. James is furiously scrubbing at a bar glass that is half stained brown. James: Dude, how did you even manage to do this? What is this anyway? Thomas: You seriously don't want to know. Trust me. Thomas laughs at James and takes a sip from a beer in front of him. James tosses the glass in a trash can off to the side and it's heard shattering in the bottom of the can. James then grabs another bar glass from a line of them, this time, not covered in brown, and begins wiping it out. James: So anyway, after that, I ran through the woods for a good long time until I found a road and hitch hiked a ride home. I'll tell you, it's not that easy to hitch a ride when you're naked and half covered in blue paint. Thomas: Wow, I'll bet. It's just a good thing that ostrich layed that egg in time. James: I know. I don't want to imagine where I would be if... James cuts his sentence short as he has turned around to face Thomas but first sees The Semi. Thomas notices the surprised look on James' face and turns around as well. Thomas: Whoa! I never thought you would be very good at sneaking around! Thomas lets out a laugh and sips his beer again as Semi gives him a sneer and moves beside him to the bar counter. Dewey just stands in the background, next to the camera equipment. Semi's smug eyes meet James' confused eyes and James sets down the glass with the rag in it and Thomas turns back around in his seat. James: So... can I help you with something, Semi? Thomas: You need an oil change? Thomas bursts out laughing again. Semi wrinkles his forehead in frustration and keeps his attention focused on James. Semi: Actually, I was looking for your cousin, David. He wouldn't happen to be around here, would he? James shoots an uneasy look to Thomas who has his tongue shoved far down into his beer bottle. James returns his look to Semi and shakes his head. James: No, David doesn't come around here any more. I haven't spoken with him in... well, a while. Semi wrinkles his chin and nods his head in thoughtful understanding. He looks only slightly dissapointed. The scene is then interrupted by a choking sound. Semi and James turn their heads toward Thomas, who has apparently gotten his tongue stuck in the bottle. He flails his head about, trying to get the bottle unstuck, but it just flaps around with his tongue. James puts his palm to his face, looking embarrassed to even be there. Semi watches on, looking unamused. He reaches out and grabs the bottle. He promptly yanks it off Thomas' tongue with a wet popping sound and sets the bottle down in front of Thomas. Thomas winces and draws in a quick gasp of air. Thomas: ...Thankths Semi then turns his attention back towards James. Semi: Well, if David is halfway across the world, I guess I might as well stay for a beer. Make it four, on the double. Semi flashes James a quick but ugly smile and James takes a moment to respond. James: Um... is he with you? James points passed Semi towards where Dewey is standing. Semi: Yeah. He's legal. Don't worry about it. James scowls and sighs deeply, but he gets Semi his four beers and sets them in front of him on the bar. Semi: By the way, what's all this camera crap here? James: We're shooting the next episode of Top Rope Tavern soon. Semi: Oh... good luck with that sinking ship. Semi, with a dirty grin on his face, takes two beers between his knuckles, two in each hand, and turns from James immediately after, not saying another word. He walks passed Dewey and still says nothing. Dewey turns and jogs to catch up with Semi, who heads towards a booth far from the counter. Semi motions with his head for Dewey to follow him and he does after Dewey and James meet uneasy looks. Semi sits down at the corner booth with his back towards the wall and Dewey slides into the seat across from him. Semi sets down his beers and grabs one. He takes a long look at it then mutters, sounding upset. Semi: No good little worms... Semi then tilts the beer to the sky and lets the entire thing empty into his throat. Dewey watches on with wide eyes. Semi finishes the beer and sets it down hard on the table with a loud thud. He then wipes his mouth with the back of his hand and lets out a long belch. He glances towards the bar with an angry look and growls. Semi: I hate those damn clowns. Dewey glances as well. Dewey: I think they seem fun. Semi raises his eyebrows to Dewey and replies loudly and with anger in his voice. Semi: Fun? You think those morons seem fun? You don't know fun. You want some real fun? Semi grabs one of the remaining three bottles of beer and slides it towards Dewey. Dewey looks around and stammers, not knowing what to say at first. Dewey: I uh... The... Um... I can't. I'm not old enough. Semi: Like I said, you don't have anything to worry about; you're with me. Try it. Dewey looks at the beer in front of him and leans forward. He sniffs it and winces. Dewey: I don't think I want to. Semi snaps back loudly. Semi: Try it! Dewey slowly reaches out and grabs the beer. His eyes are full of worry and his bottom lip is trembling. Slowly, he puts the bottle to his lips and tilts it back a little. Again, Dewey winces, much harder this time, and sets the beer back down. Dewey replies with squinted eyes and perced lips. Dewey: It tastes like pee. Semi laughs loudly for a long moment and his big gut bounces. Semi grabs one of two remaining full beers and takes a big, long swig of it before setting it back down. Semi: Well, drink up. I'm certainly not touching that after your filth has been all over it. Dewey looks offended. Semi takes another drink. Dewey does the same, again puckering his lips and squinting his eyes afterwards. Semi: Now this is fun. Those guys (Semi points a thumb towards the JESTers) don't have a clue what fun is. Dewey nods and looks around the bar nervously. Semi: I mean these guys are always doing something ignorant for attention. There was this time back in WHQ where James supposedly fake raped David in his sleep, then after David spent half the day sitting on the roof, James got him down by letting him slap him like a little girl for a month! Dewey chuckles and takes another begrudged sip of beer. Semi: I mean, how stupid is that? If a man even tried to get into my bed, whether I was in it at the time or not, not only would I run his ass down, but I would feed him to my dog! Dewey: You have a dog? Semi: Shut up, that's not the point! The point is, these JESTer freaks are fruitier than Fruity Pebbles... and dumber too. Semi finishes the second half of his second beer in one long swig. Dewey takes another small sip from his. Semi: This other time, David said he needed to clear his mind, so those lunatics at the bar threw him into a car blindfolded and drove him out to the middle of nowhere, where they dropped him off and drove away. Semi takes a big swig of his third beer and both Semi and Dewey laugh. Semi: Actually, that was pretty funny, but still, these guys are fools. Semi belches again and shakes his head back and forth quickly. Semi: Not bad, but I need more beer. Semi stands up and walks away, leaving Dewey sitting alone. Dewey takes a long sip of his beer and looks around, looking increasingly nervous. Dewey's eyes grow wide and a little red as he looks around the room. There is a young bald headed guy playing pinball and a middle aged couple now sitting at the bar. He now clings to the bottle with both hands like he's holding himself down to the table. He picks the bottle up and sips from it some more. Then he tilts it back higher and starts gulping. Dewey chugs the rest of his beer and tries to shake the few remaining suds from the bottle to his mouth. At this time, Semi reappears with four more beers. He speaks loudly and for once, rather friendly. Semi: Slow down there, slugger! You don't have enough meat on your bones to be drinking too fast. Dewey looks up at Semi with a big, goofy grin and nods in agreement. He then releases a quick and loud hic-up. Semi laughs a big belly laugh and sits back down. Again, he scoots one of his four beers over to Dewey who immediately grabs it and takes a long sip before putting it back down. Semi shakes his head. Semi: I hope I didn't create a monster. Semi then grabs a beer and drinks most of it straight down as Dewey consistently sips his. Semi puts his beer down with authority and begins talking loudly and with frustration. Semi: Damn, I'm really getting tired of those pipsqueaks up there at the bar! That jerk asked me AGAIN if you were legal! How many times do I have to say something for people to take note?! Dewey shakes his head, signaling he doesn't know. Semi continues on, beginning to talk quickly. Semi: This whole lot of Whitmans and JESTers and all these other butt buddies around here are really getting under my skin. Semi finishes his first beer of the new round and begins the second. He puts it down and begins talking, quieter this time, but with more anger and frustration in his voice. Semi: Take David for example, that no good waste of air. He's pretty much the ring leader of this freak show. The guy spends most of his time hanging out with these losers sending chat messages, playing in the pool, getting into slap fights, and drinking too much beer! Semi takes a swig of his beer and Dewey does the same. Semi: Yet some how, some freakin' way, that screw up became MWE cshampion? I don't get it! Semi downs the rest of his current beer as Dewey takes another big swig. By this time, both of them are beginning to look a little drunk. Semi's eyes are glossy and a little red and Dewey's are very red. Semi's speech is becoming lower and more grumbly with the occasional slurring. Dewey just listens on, looking fascinated, as he slowly sips his beer away. Semi: Let me tell you shomthing! As long as I live, I may never understand how clown boy Whitman became an amazing sensashtion and the MWE champion by playing games of grab-ass with his drinking buddies and making some crappy TV show while the Semi is asked to give up his X Havoc TV HD DVD what-the-hell-ever title and all I do is bust my ass and more importantly, bust everyone else's! Semi has began talking quickly again and is now fuming with irritation. He clenches his teeth and fumes before gulping down some beer. Dewey has set his beer down and is leaning forward towards Semi, still listening very intently like Semi is telling him the meaning of life. Dewey: That's r..r..right! You're damn right! Abso-positively-lute..lutely! Semi looks at Dewey with a confused but still angry face and shakes his head with disappointment. Semi: You keep up with WHQ don't you? Dewey: Helllllllll yeeeeeeeeaaah. Dude, WHQ rockzorz! Semi: What? Dewey: Rockzorz! Semi: I think you've had enough to drink. Dewey: I'll tell you when I've had enough! Dewey drinks the rest of his second beer and puts it down on the table. He then begins laughing hysterically. His face turns red and his laughter echoes through the entire bar. Tears roll down his face and he pounds on the table in front of him, making the several beer bottles jump and slide. Semi watches on, looking extremely annoyed. Finally, Dewey gets hold of himself and releases a few last laughs before exhaling deeply and smiling wide at Semi. Dewey: I've had enough. What...what about WHQ? Semi's bottom jaw drops to respond but nothing comes out. Semi's eyes shift back and forth a couple times and he re-locks them on Dewey after a swig of beer. He begins loudly and soon falls back into more of an angry growl as he speaks, dripping with confidence. Semi: Oh... yeah; you've seen WHQ. You've seen how I man-handled his happy ass all around that ring. I beat him from pillar to post twice! Dewey: But not three times! Dewey explodes into another fit of laughter. Semi clinches his fists and his teeth hard. His face turns red from anger. Semi cuts Dewey's laughter short as he loudly barks at him viciously. Semi: You shut your mouth! Dewey quickly stops laughing but still has to restrain himself from snickering. Semi talks loudly again, radiating contempt. Semi: You know what, yeah, David Whitman did pin me in WHQ. In fact, he's the only person to pin me ever. But that was because that was the most incompetent referee in the history of this sport! I hit Whitman with The Collision and pinned him, 1-2-3; the bell rang and everything. Then the referee changes his decision and Whitman rolls me up from behind while I'm trying to figure out exactly what in the hell is going on. Semi snarls before drinking half of his sixth beer straight down. Dewey giggles to himself, but continues listening to the drunk and pissed off Semi. Semi: So what does that little coward do after that? They offer him a title shot rematch against me and he runs away. He quits and gives the shot to one of his damn JESTer asshole friends, Zack Evermore, who, by the way, I WILL be running down next week at MY pay per view, The Collision! Dewey: Dude, that match is gunna be awesome! Semi: Didn't I say shut it?! Dewey turns a hurt gaze to the bottle in front of him, but quickly begins chuckling to himself again. Semi: AS I WAS SAYING... David Whitman decided to focus all of his strength on the MWE title so he left WHQ. He knew... KNEW... he couldn't beat the Semi for the WHQ title, so he concentrated on MWE and won their's. Semi stops and the corner of a smile peaks on his angered face. He speaks in a sick tone of voice. Semi: But guess what... I've come after him. I have come to MWE to catch that no good dip shit and I have. Or at least I will, Sunday at Tribulation. And I don't care if they want to strip me of my title to receive a non-title shot! I'm not going into Tribulation hoping to skyrocket my career. I'm going into Tribulation looking for blood. David Whitman's blood! Semi fumes as droplets of spit spray from between his clinched teeth. Suddenly, Dewey, who has been still listening idly becomes wide eyed and clamps his hand to his mouth. He jumps up and runs away, toward the men's room. Semi doesn't even watch him leave. Instead he downs the rest of his beer and tosses the bottle to the table. It skips and rolls to the floor where it shatters. Semi's eyes become wild and full of rage. His face turns beat red and he begins visibly shaking. Semi stands up out of the booth and makes his way quickly and aggressively towards the bar counter. Semi stops behind the camera for the Top Rope Tavern shoot and pushes a couple buttons. The camera angle changes to the still camera Semi has turned on, showing Thomas still sitting in the bar seat at the right side of the shot, the young couple to the left, and James pouring a shot with his back turned. Semi then appears behind Thomas and forcefully grabs his head and slams it down into the bar counter. The woman to the left lets out a shriek and both her and her date jump down from the stools and hurry away. Thomas barely lets out a sound and falls limply to the floor. Another scream is heard in the back of the bar and you hear the door slam shut soon after. James spins around to see Semi just across the counter. James: Hey, what the... Semi clamps his hand around James' throat and James drops the drink in his hand. Semi pulls James across the counter with his one hand as James desperately grabs and claws at Semi's hand as his face begins turning blue. Semi turns around, holding James by the throat towards the camera. He then grabs the back of James' knee and flips him up and over Semi's head, Collision style. James slams back first into shelves of alcohol, smashing some of the bottle, and he crashes down to the floor behind the counter. Broken glass shimmers in the light as falls of alcohol pour from the shelves as James' unseen but clearly broken body lies behind the bar counter. Semi turns around to face the camera. His eyes are bugged out and wild. They are full of anger and hatred. His nostrils flare and droplets of spit dot the sides of Semi's face. He steps close to the camera and seemingly picks it up as the angle raises to point slightly down on him. Semi stares into it for a few moments before he begins yelling loudly, particularly the beginning. Semi: DAVID WHITMAN! MWE! Do I have your attention now?! Is this the splash you were expecting?! This isn't the first time we'll meet and it damn sure isn't the last, Whitman! I beat you two out of three times while I've been at the top of WHQ! You ran, but I found you Whitman! And even though you're at the top looking down around here, the result is going to be the same in MWE! THE SEMI IS GOING TO RUN YOU DOWN! Semi sets the camera down and takes a step back, with violent and malicious eyes. Dewey then trips onto the scene. Dewey: I thought I heard... Dewey looks to see what he has tripped on, and it turns out to be Thomas Willam's lifeless body. About this time, sirens can be faintly heard in the distance. Dewey's eyes grow wide and his voice squeaks out desperately. Dewey: What did you do?! Semi barks in response, now with an air of desperation in his voice. Semi: Let's go! We're going to Italy; now! Semi hustles by the camera then the angle suddenly becomes the floor that is quickly getting closer. The camera crashes into the floor and the scene cuts out to fuzz white noise. OOC: All of the crazy antics of the JESTers that Semi was talking about happened in WHQ. I tried to give some background to Semi and Whitman's history there. I hope everyone enjoys.
