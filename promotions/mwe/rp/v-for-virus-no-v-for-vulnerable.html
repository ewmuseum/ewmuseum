He knows when he needs to focus, when he needs to train, and when he needs to make sure that he has all his wits about him, because the next match of his life could be the biggest of his career. I know, for a fact, that he knows that. And Independence Day, there aren't any breaks...there is no slacking...and there is no pushing off important things. Independence Day is the ONLY important thing for a wrestler. And that goes for any Pay-Per-View. *Mason sighs and runs his hands through his hair and again. He is now slouched against the wall, his legs evenly spread apart, with an arm resting on each.* Mason: Wrestlers take breaks because they need them, not because they want them. And they take them, when they can. When it comes time for a match, you can believe that we are ready for action. You can believe that we have done our homework, studied up on some old tapes, and made sure we knew what we were walking into. And I can only hope that Virus has done that for me, because I have for him. And even if he didn't, it doesn't really matter. I know, either way, he is going to give me his all, and nothing less. Because, when a championship is on the line, you don't give 100%. You give more than that...you give 200% or 300% of your best effort. You try harder than you would in a normal match. You try harder than you would in a number one contender's match. You try your hardest and you pack your biggest punch. You unload, you explode, and you try to dominate in any way you can. Most of all, you try your hardest...to win. *Mason is now leaned forward, looking deeply into the eyes of the four people listening. He has brought his hands up in a "v" like formation, holding his hands together, able to control his two pointer fingers which are no longer locked.* Mason: And trying your hardest is something that Virus, just might not be able to do. You might be thinking, "Mason, he won last week, what are you talking about." Well, just look at the facts. In the last few months, he has been tag teaming with Vega Knight. They've been the MWE Tag Team Championships, and they have been much more. Friends outside of the ring, companions, helping each other through tough times, and making sure that both was healthy, fit, and in great condition. They are a team...a formation, if you will. *Mason takes a breath, pausing to let people understand what he is saying. Pacing himself so people don't fall behind.* Mason: But you know what, I learned something about formations the other day. Sapphire and I, a couple of weeks ago went to her nephew's 6th grade graduation and we listened to all these boring speeches. Finally, we came to one...that actually struck my interest. It actually, got me thinking, and was the only thing worth listening to the entire time. This man talked about birds...and their formations. Why they form a "V" formation, and what its purpose was. Sitting there, at first, I thought to myself, "What is this old fool talking about?" but he kept talking, and it came together. His point wound up being how should form a formation for life, but that isn't where I am going... Birds, as I would find out, keep themselves in a "V" formation so that the bird right behind it can boost itself and keep it going, off of the effort exerted by the bird in front of it. They do this to, I think the guy said, to preserve their energy. And the birds way in the back, will honk or make that weird goose noise, to encourage the birds in the front to keep on going. They make sure that they are well-oiled machine, always in perfect formation so they can make the best time, while using the least amount of energy possible. *Mason's fans are looking at him, trying to see where he is going, but they don't have a clue right now. He continues to talk anyway.* Mason: And you know what...that is how a tag team works. You work together to win in the quickest time possible, using the least amount of energy, while at the same time, working off each other, and encouarging the other to keep on going. But, there is one...final thing. *Now the fans understand where he was going, and are interesting again, wondering what the final thing is. A few actually lean forward, as if it was a secret or something.* Mason: The thing about this formation, is that when when one bird falls out of the group...it stumbles, and will usually have a difficult time getting back into the group. And if it can't get into the group again, it becomes slow, using much more energy, and becoming less efficient. It goes from being well-oiled and in-groove with it's flock, to being alone, confused, and vulnerable... *People lean back, grinning, knowing exactly what Mason's point was now.*' Mason: This...is what will happen to Virus. He will drop out of his formation, stumble, and be vulnerable. To anything and everything. He may be confident, he may tell you otherwise, but I can gaurentee you, that is what will happen. It always is. *Mason smiles a little bit, as Sapphire is sitting up watching to. It seems she couldn't not watch what Mason was doing, and he chuckled at that.* Mason: You see, Virus, is going to be out of his element at Independece Day. He may have Demonica...but he won't have Vega, and he is who really matters. See, Virus trains and prepares himself, with Vega, for Vega, and to Vega. If he needed a spot, Vega was there. If he needed advice, Vega was there. If he needed to show-boat to someone, Vega was there. No matter the situation, no matter what the cause, Vega has been in Virus' corner for months now. On the other hand, I have been by myself, training by myself, with no one, for no one. And that will be the difference. *Mason rubs his hands together and stands up, before leaning against the wall. He looks at the baseball game at hand and looks at the stadium, which didn't sell very many tickets. There was still an entire empty section, over by right field, and then there was a completely packed and crowded section behind the catcher. Mason pointed to both.* Mason: Ya see, football is a team sport, where you have to be united. In football, the inches mean everything......everything. In wrestling, for the most part, you're on your own. And in wrestling...you don't have inches...you have seconds. You have seconds to perform either the right move...or the wrong. You have few seconds, to decide to attack or defend. It is a game of time and reaction...to be honest, and you have to prepare yourself. This isn't, as much as people will say it is, a showy sport. It is about winning...about beating your opponet, and pinning them. And if you train with mind frame of being in front of people, then when it comes time for action, you'll be to focused on showing off, that you won't see the task at hand. But if you train, with the mind frame of being in front of no one, it will be completely different. You will focus...yes you will, but you will crack. Crack under the pressure of the people. And I can assure you that. But, when you train, not caring who is there, then you will succeed. If it doesn't matter if you are wrestling in front of your mom, or the f**king president, you WILL win. And that is the difference between Virus and I. He looks across this baseball field, and he makes a choice. He decides if he should go to the empty side or the crowded side. Me...I'll let someone else choose, because I just don't care. My objective is gonna be the same, either way. *Mason smiles as the fans look at him in awe, as if that was a moving speech or something. He just likes to think it as, logic.* Mason: At Independence Day, Virus can try his hardest to break me down. He can play mind games, he can hit me with weapons, he can make me bleed, but he just needs to realize something. Walking into Independence Day, I have nothing to lose. I'm already on a four match losing streak. I've already lost to the X-TV champion. I've been hit in the balls by a former mentor, and sat in pain, as he made his way out of the cage. I been embarrased, one way or another, by these people...and there is not taking that back. But Virus needs to know that. This Sunday, I come with so much to gain and nothing to lose. It is has been said before, but I really can't lose anything, but another match of course. Tables or not...points or not...I don't give a s**t. I really don't to be quite frank. I know I am going to walk in there, and have my focus set. And if I get beaten...like I said, I'm not losing anything. All I know, is that when then the strongest of us get taken down... Sapphire: We become stronger. *Mason looks quickly to the side at Sapphire. He shifts over to her, puts his arm around her, and slouches back.* Mason: Yes...yes, we do. *And the scene fades to black, as Mason and Sapphire sit together, with their fans, relaxing for the last time before Sunday.*
