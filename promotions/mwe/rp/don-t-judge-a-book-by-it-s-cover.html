Supernova

*I've done this RP in Blog format. I know it's not exactly original, but I've been busy, and I needed to do something more straightforward. Enjoy* I have been anxiously awaiting this one match for a while. The one match, where I can avenge my only one on one loss. Yet I can't help but feel as if nothing will be accomplished. Because when I look at the shell of what used to be something worth talking about, is now just something that can easily break. Break underneath any form of pressure That man I'm talking about, is Sonny Williams. A Man who people used to fear being in the same ring, because he was capable of pure and unadulterated mayhem. Of course, this was during the original run of MWE, so things do change, but normally not that much. I mean seriously, at what point in this man's career, did he drop his balls, and they kept rolling farther and farther away from him, until they were to far away for him to ever pick up? Was it during his failed run in Enigmar Wrestling, because he was unable to defeat some of the newer individuals to wrestling, or was it when MWE had returned? To be quite honest, from the look of things, It was both. I mean, it wouldn't surprise me if they dropped off one at a time. Though to be honest, I don't think they were just rolled away. I think that a certain individual had stepped on them, and Sonny felt it. Of course, I'm talking about me when he began his lame and pathetic Rookie Invitational when he was the champ for the second time. While he did defeat me, He failed to break me, and make me look pathetic. No. It was the other way around. He had to resort to a blatant illegal move to blindside me, in order to win. But that proved that if he could not soundly defeat somebody with only one professional win under his belt, then he was royally screwed. Would have been worse if he would have been a good boy, and not blindsided me, because he knows, deep down, Sonny Williams can't defeat me without doing what he did. Now after reading his recent blog, you would think that I would have been intimidated. That's not the case. It takes a shit load more to intimidate me. All I saw was some dude who did the same thing he normally does, and that's whine, bitch and complain about every fucken damn thing that's wrong in his life. To be honest, it reminds me of certain scenes I used to see occasionally while growing up in Scarborough. I used to see some gangs around, just chilling, while I was minding my own business. Sometimes, it looked as if a guy was complaining. One of three things happened. One, you were made fun of due to it. Two, you got your ass kicked. Or three, and believe me, I wish this n very few people, I saw a guy suddenly get his brains shot onto the nearby wall. Not a pretty sight. Makes it worse if some of them were actually your friends. Now I have to admit that you did say something that totally makes sense, and I agree with you. Confidence, acceptance, and perseverance. In case if people have forgotten, that is what I am all about. I reinvented confidence. I gave it's worth back. I am the guy who proudly talks about all of his accomplishments, stories, and even personal demons, hardly ever hesitating, and my ring work shows I am very much comfortable with not only my skills, but my ability to adapt to any situation. But what of acceptance and perseverance? I have those, but from what I hear, nobody believes I do. Well then I guess I have no choice but to spell it out. The definition of Acceptance is basically to acknowledge and allow things to happen, or certain aspects about people, even situations, whether you like it or not. I accept that maybe, I am a bit of a dick, but that's how I was brought up. See my view of things is that if you are easily pushed over, you won't get anywhere, and believe me, that is something I know, that nobody wants to accept. I know I don't. Finally, we have perseverance. The definition of that one strong and powerful word, is to keep pushing forward, no matter what. Which is what I do on a daily basis, not just because I'm not the biggest guy in the ring, but because of my personal demons. Hence the jokes and pranks, and the consistent sex. Without them, I would no longer be human. I would be a machine, getting by, day by day, until My usefulness is up. Kind of like....Sonny Williams. Actually, to be honest, I take that back. Any machine would still be much more exciting then Sonny Williams. Because unlike Sonny Williams, Machines can actually do stuff that matters, while Sonny just still hangs out in some dark corner where nobody knows where it fucken is, crying about how he keeps losing, yet keeps trying to bring himself back up by saying the same exact crap, over and over again, saying that he knows he can do better. It's gotten to the point, where after I hear each and every single one of his motherfucken promos, I ask myself 'Wheres a nearby buzzer, so that I can show him how wrong he is?' Before, I half paid attention to them , just because I was bored. This week I have. Now as I said before, nobody in their right mind would accept being a pushover. I certainly am not. Sure, I admit, that I haven't always faced a decent caliber of wrestlers in my professional career, but I did what I do best to everybody who faces me, and make them famous. Hell that's why people actually gave a damn about JJ executioner for a little while. Now, nobody gives a shit about him, exactly the way it should be. But let me ask you this? Did YOU defeat a certain individual known as Aaron Beowulf for the X-TV title? Naw...nobody was able to, not even the guy who owned you last week (like that was a surprise, on more then just one level), except for one person. I forgot who his name was. Oh wait, I remember.....it was me. Then I went to defend the title at Wrestle Wars Two, where you lost your title, and I kept mine. Then we know all about the brand split.....waste of time that it was, as we're merging back after Adrenaline Rush. Yeah, I was stripped of my X-TV title, but we all know that if I wasn't, then I would have been the longest reigning. But I was handed the IC title by a sleazeball, named Dick-in-his-ass Malibu. Did I want to be handed the title we're going to be fighting for? Hell no. I would have rather done it the old fashioned way, in a tournament, against people who actually mattered, and I don't think that you Sonny Williams, count. See, I would rather face off against some real talent. Talent, like Brian Michaels, Joey Dallas, DC, David Whitman, Alejandro Rodriguez, my brother from another mother, Leon 'The Virus' Roberts, and the two guys who I made my 'bitches' on the last show, in Project Revolution You all forgot about them eh? How I stunned the world and eliminated the biggest guy in the entire match, in American Animal, and how I outperformed Jason Fear just 2 hours earlier? Yeah I thought so. See Sonny, there is more to me then meets the eye. Like any other guy in the locker room, I love gold, I want to win gold, and defend it proudly. It would be a fucken shame if I did actually lose to you, because the short history that the IC title will have at the moment, will be forever tarnished by you. The life of the IC title will remain short when I defeat you, as I plan to merge it with the X-TV title or United States title, whether anybody likes it or not. Why keep around extra baggage when you don't need it, because after Adrenaline Rush, that's all it will be. Now I'm considering myself lucky to be able to write this blog properly as my eyes are rolling to the back of my head due to the girl of the night is giving me the heaven of all blowjobs a while before I started writing this (high stamina level, for the win). But now that I mention it, maybe that's the whole problem with Sonny Williams afterall. Because as much of a so called pimp he calls himself (more like a whore in my opinion), there is one massive problem, which could explain why Sonny sucks so much. Simply put, he can't get laid. It makes sense when you think about it, for he complains that I'm more interested in what he calls 'getting my genitals wet' instead of focusing on my match. The reason he's hating on it so much is because he's jealous of the fact that I can get laid any time I want to, and like the player I am, I always look to satisfy the better sex. Yes, I said it. Women are better then men, and I say is fucken proudly. So I say this to you, Sonny Williams,and anyone else who think the same way he does. Do not judge a book by it's cover. Everyone who has judged me that way has paid for it dearly, in some way or form. Especially you Sonny. You should know better that despite the 'Happy-go-lucky' and 'Sex for dinner' attitude I have, I am more then capable of whooping you ass from here to the moon, all around the world, and back, and barely even be winded. Does it sound like I talk a big game? Hell fucken yes I do. But that's because I am more then capable of of backing what I claim up. If you recall that American Pie series, I'm like Stiffler. In that sense, I am confident, always able to get laid, and without question, I am the life of the party. Sonny is....well he's like that guy Sherman. He talks an even bigger game then I do (Sherman is easier to pay attention to), but when it comes down to game time, he pisses his pants. Ultimately, because of all that shit, he's nothing more then an embarassment. Sonny, I'm going to go out of my way, and tell you what you need to do. Instead of being the bipolar bitch that you are, look for the old sonny Williams. Look for the guy who did make people's head turn by just doing his thing. Because without it, you'll never advance any further up. You'll keep sliding down that mountain, getting all scratched up, and bleeding while you fall. Hell I'll go as far as saying, that if you don't find the Sonny Williams of the old MWE, then you'll stop rolling down the mountain, as I will stop you, only to toss you off a nearby cliff, in a more dramatic fashion then that one guy in Revenge Of The Sith, and watch you splatter on the ground like that kid in Final Destination Two who got flattened by a huge pane of glass. If that ain't a graphic image, I don't know what is. Now If you people will excuse me, I got a lady to satisfy. She won't be walking right for at least a day afterwards, just like the rest of them. So I'll see you, my loyal fans, and even all the damn haters, this Friday, on Genesis, where I will soundly kick Sonny Williams ass into the last millenium 'Supernova' Vega Knight PS: For those paying attention, LEARN TO SAY MY FUCKEN NAME RIGHT!!!!!! Jeez, how many times do I heard the sequence of my name misread. I mean seriously, it goes 'Supernova' Vega Knight. Not 'The Vega Knight' or Vega Knight 'Supernova'. Seriously, that's fucked up.
