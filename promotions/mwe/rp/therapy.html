Therapy?; Hardaway RP...

Jul 13 2009

Kevin Hardaway

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
QUOTE OOC: I'm going to say this flat out...it sucks, I know. It's terrible, I know. Don't blame me for it, i've been really swamped and i'm trying to figure out a way out of it. Next show should be better, so I apologize for those of you who were expecting a masterpiece. And for those of you that don't accept it...bite me, worm. Do not adjust your television sets, ladies and gentlemen. This is not a test or any other crap from the Emergency Broadcasting System. You’re still watching the MWE, you’re still looking at MWE.com...and this is still Kevin Hardaway. I am here for a few reasons and I’m not going to sugarcoat it like some douchebag would do. I’m going to say this as honest to God as I even can. No frills, no lies, no bullshit necessary. You see...I’ve apparently done something wrong in this business. I made a mistake and guess what? I’m out on the streets, people are yelling at me, calling me terms that I’ve heard before. “Sell out” “Controversial asshole” “Egomaniacal” “Lil’ Hitler” “MARTYR!” Names that I’ve heard before when I apparently caused all of the mayhem at the GWC. Where I was forced out by people who, to my opinion, shouldn’t even be in this business. The first times those terms were yelled out at me, I threw it into a little ball in my stomach and just passed it to the side, I walked to the side of the fan where the shit wasn’t going to be thrown at me. But, as time went on, my steps got closer and closer and closer to the middle of the fan. That little ball inside of me was growing and growing until it was about the size of a tumor, it was that bad. And then one day...it all just popped. The shit hit the fan. I couldn’t take it anymore. And why did all this happen? Because I made a mistake or two that couldn’t happen in a wrestling promotion. It all happened though by mistake. I wanted to go to the front offices and tell everybody that I made a mistake. “Shit happens when you’re fucked up!”, that quote seems to be my life story. Well guess what? I couldn’t. Not for the life of me. Everything just exploded and I couldn’t even get looked at without somebody giving me the evil eye. Everybody stabbed me in the back, even people who I thought of, as some of the best friends I could have in a business like this, even though I was told as a very young kid back home, that “human beings are the worst type of animals” because you can get close to them and than they gut you like some sort of sick fisherman. They looked at me like I was the bad guy, when all I was trying to do was say that I was sorry. Nothing else, nothing more. At that point, I was sitting at home and I had only one wish at that time. And that was I hoped that somebody on that damn roster, could come up to me, and accept my apology. I couldn’t confide in Angie. It’s not what I wanted. I wanted somebody off the roster of the Global Wrestling Coalition to say “Dude, I accept your apology and I don’t think any wrong of you.” A couple weeks later, somebody up above, gave me that wish. Zachary Warner. He shot me a text and asked me what was going on. Not in the sense of what happened, but in general. I thought he was one of the people who hated me. Turned out I got a gift when he stepped up to the plate and became that said person I was talking to you about. For the first time in about a month, a smile came across my face. Now, I’m going to end up skipping the next part, because time and time again, this story has been told by me, about how he showed me the MWE, about how he’s been my companion for the past couple years, all of that. Skipped. Done. Gone. Fast forward now, to a couple months ago. May 26th, 2009. From the MWE.com website itself. It reads. QUOTE Tonight's #1 Contender match, against the winner of the U.S./IC Scavenger Hunt, has been forfeited by Zak Warner. A few days ago, Zak Warner tested positive for THC. Due to the drug policy Mr. Warner will be suspended for a to-be-determined amount of time. And then it read a couple days later by his spokesperson... QUOTE At this time, Mr. Warner has decided to volunteer check himself into this treatment center for an addiction to anti-depressants. This will coincide with his recent suspension from MWE, and hopefully when he is released he will have a second shot. Now if you recall, me and him fronted the group known as Uncultured Society (and to a lesser extent, Uncultured Anarchy). But if you also may remember, that group wasn’t just some random stable thrown together because people wanted to see me and a bunch of people together raising hell. No, the reason for that was because not only me...but Zak Warner...and once again to a lesser extent, Anthony Clark, our creedo was that “Straight edge means straight edge.” We were tired and sick of people ruining their lives because of addiction. Because they couldn’t get their heads on straight, and had to go to the local bar to drown their feelings away. Whomever did that was on our “Hit List”. But, right now...IT SICKENS ME! Where are they now? They’re both right on top of the damn list. Anthony Clark: Doing god knows what...partying, having sex with random bitches off the street, drinking, the whole nine yards. When he signed his contract here, me and Zak wanted him to follow us, be a honorary member of the group. And what does he do? He abandons us, like we’re no good to him anymore. And then he goes off and does whatever the hell he wants. Remember Anthony, when you were with us. Look at what you did at GWC. You became one of the greatest Tag Team Champions ever. You became one of the greatest Ultimate Champions ever. Speaking of Zak, you two went out there and had one of the most gruesome straight-up hardcore matches that I’ve ever seen. Straight-up, no goofy shit involving weed-whackers and light tubes. Just straight up standard stuff. You two made me proud. Now, you’re just nothing more than a parasite, looking for blood off of some whore’s leg. Maybe that’s why you’re still stuck in the low-cards. Because you’re paying more attention to the strip club and not inside the ring. The Anthony Clark I knew is dead and gone. But, I ran off from the main story here, so let me get back to it. You see, I don’t think I’ve felt a more devastating pit in my stomach than when I found out the news that you were suspended for drug paraphernalia. How come? Just like Clark in his prime, Zak was the same way as well. But instead of wanting to kick him to the curb for the same reasons...I just couldn’t. You want to know why? Because of what he did for me. He went past all of the bullshit, all of the supposed lies, and he still believed in me, he still had hope for me. I somehow knew that deep down inside the shell of a drug addict was the same Zak Warner that helped me out and got my feet back in the ring. That’s why I didn’t throw him away, because I wanted to make sure he would’ve gotten back in this ring alive and well. It didn’t happen. You all saw how when I visited him in rehab, he was still a mess. Still that shell outside of him, rotting away like a dead corpse. He still felt the need to take drugs even though he was there to get help. The room he was in reminded me of what kind of room Sarah Connor was in during Terminator 2. White, cold, soulless. And then the bastard just laughed at me, cracked jokes, he was trying to be a comedian when he shouldn’t have been. He didn’t care. He didn’t care about how I was trying to help, or about making jokes about my family. He just didn’t care. So I just left, but still...inside and out. He was behind that shell, and he was going to break out. That’s why I made the match against him at Independence Day. But as the days came closer, I tried checking up on him via the phone. This is part of the story that you don’t know. The doctor said that he wasn’t progressing one bit. This was 2 weeks before Independence Day. And as it came closer, no progression. But somehow, someway...he was able to escape from it. Either by actually trying, or by bribing off the staff, who the hell knows. But at Independence Day, during our match...the fans may have been into it, the audience at home may have been into it, the announcers, the staff, everybody was into it. They said it was the best match on the card that night, which is saying a lot. But one person wasn’t into it... ME! I knew flat-out that Zak wasn’t even trying, he was just pulling luck out of his asshole. And I had to make sure he wasn’t trying to pull off anything stupid. At that point...for some odd reason, it just popped. The ball in my stomach exploding, the shit hitting the fan, you name it. Everything just collapsed. And at that point in time, I realized that nobody could help this man out. Nobody. No one. NOTHING! All hope was lost. And at that point, I realized that “hope” did die on that day. To me, hope never died, it was my heart, it was my system...NOT ANYMORE! GONE! Hope dies. Dreams die. Everything dies. Zak Warner had none of those things left in his body. So I did the only thing that was necessary. I repaid him the favor. That favor he gave to me when I was down on my luck. I repaid the favor, by letting him go. Letting him rot away in whatever box he’ll live in ‘til he dies. I’m done hoping for a better tomorrow from this guy. See, now I can go on and on and on about this worthless waste of a person, but you know what? I’m not gonna. I have bigger things on my plate to worry about. Because the two biggest champions in MWE right now are teaming up together. OH NO! Their opponents are so doomed, I tell you. DOOMED! Obviously, sarcasm is punishment for shoplifting in some countries. Their opponents, I’m sure they know of them. Everybody knows of them. Everybody SHOULD know of them. Because, right now...me and Grand Mystique aren’t the happiest of campers. GM had to suffer the fate of that...asshole, D.C. at Independence Day. Believe me, I know the feeling about that one. And well, I’m a douche, but I’m not that big enough of a douche to tell you the whole story again on my front. The simple thing is this. We’re up against two people that shouldn’t even be HOLDING gold in the MWE. Mason Resoan could very well be the luckiest man in the history of the world right now. A title gets vacated and he jumps on the bandwagon train all the way. He’s lucky Sinc Mercier vacated the damn belt so he could have it. Not only that, but he’s very lucky that my match with Zak had to end in a draw, because he would’ve been facing...and losing to...me at Crowning Glory for the United States Championship. I had my meal ticket punched for that show and some stupid “domino effect” had to come and completely fuck it up. And the cream of the crop. I wasn’t forgetting about you, Sinc. That would be stupid of me, wouldn’t it? Yeah, the “glorified midcarder” finally broke through the ceiling, he finally has his moment in the spotlight. Good for you...good for you. You know....NO! It isn’t good one single bit. Like Mason, you have that horseshoe all shined up, don’t you? Ever since you won that belt. You don’t think I remember the last time we were in that very ring? I’m still scrubbing the mental scars from my body in the shower with that. For three measly seconds, you took advantage of me not being in the ring and you got the biggest win of your career, hands down. It’s all bullshit if you ask me. If I wasn’t thrown out like yesterday’s news, I would’ve kicked your god damn skull so far in, you have to wonder if Christina was seeing Sinc Mercier, or Rocky Dennis. See, this Monday...it’s all about revenge. It’s about redemption. It’s about not giving a fuck what anybody does. Because I, Kevin Hardaway, doesn’t care. It’s time to realize that hopes and dreams are nothing else but that. Stop living in your fancy little bubble...the Tooth Fairy ain’t real, Santa Claus ain’t real, life isn’t a bunch of unicorns, rainbows, and happy times. We live in a Great Depression where stock markets are crumbling, auto makers are closing up shop, and a world where I swear to god, our very own President of the United States himself is on a fucking vacation instead of trying to help. Open your eyes and see what’s fallen before you. Just make sure I’m in the picture...seconds before I cripple you and take whatever piece of pussy is yours. Alright. We good? Thought so. What the hell ever.[ Open your eyes now. Therapy. I don’t need it, yet I’m the one in here talking to a quack. Angie’s in the other room getting her head brainwashed by somebody that wants money. Did you know that “therapists” are the definition of “thieves” as well. Go look that up. I dare you, double dog dare you. God, I have a splitting headache from this. Why do I need to hear the fifth degree from some professional when I can just go to one of my friends and have them tell me the following... “Yeah, your wife is a bitch. Leave her ass, take your kid, and go the fuck home.” See, I can get that advice for free. But no. The wife has to make me pay an arm and a leg so we can save our marriage. Save it for the 504th time. Yeah, I fuckin’ said it. Got a problem with that? She needs to stop worrying all the damn time. Is she done worrying yet? Now that I mention it, the therapist is kind of hot. Yeah, she’s a woman. Problem again? Hmm...maybe I should... “Mr. Hardaway?” Umm...was she yelling the WHOLE time? “Mr. Hardaway, can you hear me?” Better pay attention to that one now. Sigh. “Yeah, sorry...I zoned out. Was thinking of something I said the other day.” Please don’t say what it was...please don’t say what it was...please don’t say what it was. “Ok. So tell me, what makes you think you and your wife need counseling for?” Really now? Oh boy, this could take awhile...here it goes. “Ok...” TO BE CONTINUED...
