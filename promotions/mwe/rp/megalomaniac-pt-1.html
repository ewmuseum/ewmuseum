Megalomaniac Pt 1

Nov 18 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
OOC: This is a 2 part RP. I will put the other part up either tomorrow or thursday. ** The camera opens during mid-afternoon on a busy street. The sun is shining and cars are driving by and people are walking up and down the street. The camera turns a focuses up the street and a black Audi A4 comes into the picture and comes racing up the street at a good clip. As the car comes up the street toward the camera it begins slowing down until it comes to a complete stop near the building. Finally the car stops and the engine cuts off and the driver side door swings open and after a few seconds we see a very disheveled looking Ace Cannon emerge. An Ace Cannon that is supporting a few days of facial hair growth and his clothes appear to be wrinkled and dirty. Ace walks around the front of the car and toward the building when someone yells at him “NO PARKIN THERE, BUD!” Cannon just flips him the bird and keeps walking toward the office. As Ace passes the camera it begins to follow him and we notice the we are about to enter MWE OFFICES! Ace swings the door open and the camera continues to follow him as he enters the bank of elevators. He hits the up arrow and impatiently awaits the elevator. After a few seconds and elevator finally arrives to the bottom floor and the door slides open and Cannon enters but the camera does not. The camera opens up again this time facing an elevator door. We see the top of the elevator light up and then the elevator door opens and Ace Cannon emerges. He gets out of the elevator and pushes past the camera man ad walks up to a huge desk were a young, beautiful brunette receptionists sits. She is talking on the phone and doesn’t pay Ace any attention for a good couple of seconds until…** Ace: Get off the fucking phone you bimbo! ** The receptionist hangs up the phone and looks up with a very sheepish look on her face.** Receptionist: C….C…Can I help you? Ace: You sure can. Is my dad in? Receptionist: He is currently in a meeting. ** Ace doesn’t reply. He simply starts to walk down the hall as the receptionist yells he cannot go back there, but Ace ignores her. He continues to walk until he reaches the big wooden door at the end of the hall. He opens the door and sees his father Richard Covell sitting there with Chole Cornett. Both break conversation and give shocked looks toward Cannon who is standing at the door.** Richard: Hey Ace, take a seat. ** Covell motions to the couch. Ace walks over and sits down next to his father and Chole is sitting in a chair to the right of Ace.** Richard: This is Chole Cornett, asstiant GM on your show Genesis. ** Ace looks at the beautiful women sitting in the chair before he even says anything. After looking her over he begins to speak.** Ace: If they trashbag whore is assistant general manager I would LOVE to see who the general manager is. Chole: Excuse me? ** Richard Covell buries his face in his hands as the proverbal fireworks are about to explode.** Ace: Did I offend you when I called you a trashbag whore? Chole: Yes, yes you did. Ace: Well, I call them like I see them there sweetheart. Chole: I am not a whore. **Ace looks her over before he speaks.** Ace: Really? You sure could have fooled me. I have nailed so many chicks like you that it’s not even funny. Hell, the only reason you have this damn job is because you sucked the old man’s cock. ** Richard lifts his face from his head and looks at Ace.** Richard: Chole, could you please leave so I can have a word with Ace? Chole: Not a problem. I hope you can get this asshole of a son that you have in line. ** Chole gets up and begins to walks out but before she reaches the door Ace pipes up.** Ace: Oh he Chole, don’t break Maxx’s little heart. The whole locker room knows he has a massive hard-on for you. **Chole looks at Ace and shakes her head in disgust as she leaves. As the door shuts Richard gets up and walks over to his wet bar and makes a drink. He then sits down in the chair Chole was just in and looks at Ace for a long moment before speaking.** Richard: Son, what in the hell is your problem? Ace: Why didn’t you offer me anything to drink there dad? Richard: Ace, don’t avoid the question. What is your problem? ** Ace sits there and stares at his dad for a long hard moment before speaking.** Ace: This entire fucking situation. Richard: What would the situation be? Ace: This little world title tournament. Not only am I pissed that I wasn’t named champion but I have to wrestle two matches for me to even become champion! Someone of my caliber shouldn’t have to go through that. Richard: Son, it’s more then fair. Everyone will have to win two matches to become champion and not only that you have one of the easier roads to the finals. Not to take anything from American Animal but he isn’t exactly a Sonny Williams, Joey Dallas or DC. Ace: I know that. But this is still horseshit! You don’t understand dad. If my name was Brian I would have had the title given too me or I would have already been asserted into the finals. **Richard shakes his head and takes a drink for the liquor filled glass.** Richard: Ace, that isn’t true and you know that. Ever since the revelation was made last year that you were my son I have been nothing but fair too you. Ace: I beg to differ on that pops. It is quiet evident that Brian is the golden child. Richard: So is this why were here? So you can beg for attention from me and possibly get a world championship handed too you? **Ace doesn’t answer right away. He ponders the question for a moment or two before answering.** Ace: Actually dad, that’s not a bad idea. **Richard Covell shakes his head.** Richard: It’s not happening. Work for it son… Work for it. Because at the end of the day you feel more accomplished then if it was just given too you. ** Ace stands up from the couch and looks at Richard Covell before he answers.** Ace: Fuck that dad… You know why I look like a slob right now? All that is on my mind is getting my hand on this damn title. That’s all that I see when I close my eyes is this title! I can’t function! I need this title like Chole needs cock or like a crack head needs and eight ball or like how you need to wake your drunk ass up in the morning and have a scotch! This title is my drug and I need it. Richard: Well, it still isn’t happening. As a matter of fact get out of my office. You are making me sick and you are ruining the family name. **Ace reaches down and flips over the coffee table. Richard Covell just sits there and watches Ace throw his temper tantrum. As Ace walks out of the office he slams the door behind him and head back to the bank of elevators. He gets there just as the elevator door is opening. He enters and once again the camera doesn’t follow. The camera opens up again this time we see Damien Thrope standing in front of the camera with a microphone in his hand.** Damien: I am Damien Thrope and I am about to get an MWE.COM exclusive from Ace Cannon. Word has it he was just upstairs in his father, Richard Covell’s office having some type of meeting. He should be coming out of one of these elevators any second now. **Damien sidesteps the camera so it can get a clear show at the bank of elevators. As the camera focuses in on the elevators one finally opens and we see Ace Cannon emerge and begin to walk right past the camera until Damien Thrope pops out.** Damien: Ace, can I get a word with you? **Ace stops and stares at the camera with an angry look covering his face.** Ace: Yea, why not. Damien : Word has it you were just in your father office having an important meeting can you tell us what is was about? Ace: Well, first off it wasn’t a meeting. I went in there and I demanded him to give me the Genesis world title. He didn’t like what I had to say and told me no. I then trashed his office a little bit and left. Damien: Why did you demand to get the title? Ace: Do you think a man of my wrestling and athletic caliber should have to wrestle a bunch of inferior people to prove my worth? Damien: That is a good question Ace... Ace: Are you going to answer it or are you coming to be the spineless jellyfish you are and not answer it? Damien: I don’t want to ruin my journalistic integrity by answering such a question. **Ace shakes his head at Damien.** Damien: What are your though on your first opponent in the tournament American Animal? Ace: I don’t have a whole lot to say about American Animal. First off, who is he? Has accomplished anything here in MWE? Damien : He is a founding member of Project Revolution and… **Ace holds up his hand and stops Damien.** Ace: He is a part of that group of rejects? That’s funny because when word got out that I signed here in MWE I had bunches of e-mails rolling in from websites wanting me to confirm the rumor and stuff like that but I also had an interesting e-mail. As I opened it I began to read it and it was from Project Revolution begging for the services of Ace Cannon. I read the e-mail and laughed because they actually thought that a legend such as myself would join there little group but I never responded for the simple fact that they have nothing to offer me and I have everything to offer them and it would have been a waste of my time. So, besides that has American Animal done anything else? Damien: He was wrestler of the week with BG Mancini and his tag partner Jason Fear. Ace: Wrestler of the week? That isn’t impressive. Hell, if THK came here he could at least win a wrestler of the week or two and for that matter Xavier Stone probably could as well. So has this guy done anything notable here? Because everything you have told thus far is far from impressive. Damien: He has held the MWE Tag Team titles. Ace: That doesn’t impress me either. The MWE tag titles are so overrated and that is a fact. There have been complete goobers who have held them! I mean people like Damien Rose and Jose “HoHo” Dejesus. That was the worst tag team in history but they held the MWE title. So once again American Animal hold those is laughable just like every other quote un-quote accomplishment. Damien: Well since you have done nothing but knock American Animal and ruined my questions by doing that is there anything else you would like to say to end this exclusive? Ace: American Animal.. You are my first step to immortality here in MWE. I have never wanted anything more then to hold a world title in this company. Ever since my first go around in this company it was something that I dreamed about. It was something that constantly eluded me! Now that I am back in MWE it is within grasp my dreams are at my fingertips but you stand in my way. **Cannon pauses and scratches the scruff on his face before he continues.** Ace: You see Animal, when gold becomes involved I become crazy and get obsessed. I mean I have been known to lose my mind when gold is around and let just give you a couple examples of what I have done in the past… First off, I went to a bar and almost killed a man in a bar fight in my quest for gold. When I got my hands on that man and had him down on the floor all I could think about was a world championship that was almost within my grasps. As I slammed that mans head into the floor over and over and over again I could sense that title was almost mine…. But then I was pulled off that man and snapped back into reality and realized this wasn’t for a title but once I saw this man on the floor unconscious and pouring blood I felt almost happy and I realized I enjoyed what I just did all in the name for gold. **Ace pauses again and thinks of the second moment and cracks a slight crazed smile on his face.** Ace: Then the second time after I won a number one contenders match my opponents daughter climbed into the ring to check on her father and as I saw this the feelings of of gold came over me again and when this happened I ran across the ring and….BOOM! I kicked that child right in the head! Why? Because I wanted to make a statement that nothing means more to me then gold. I will do anything to prove that I want it. You see Animal this is my drug and I crave it and I want it and you being in my way will not stop me! If I have to murder you in that ring to beat you then so beat it. I have gold on my mind and there is no stopping me. So get your insurance policy in order and before our match make sure you contact all your friends and family and tell them you love them because it could very possibly be the last time you have ever see them. **Cannon pushes the mic away from his face and walks out of the lobby and back outdoors. The camera follows him and as it does we see his car Audi A4 is in the process of getting towed. Cannon runs up to the tow truck driver and begins yelling.** Ace: WHAT THE FUCK IS THIS? Driver: You parked in a no parking zone. We got to take it away. Ace: THAT IS SOME BULLSHIT! Driver: Well, read the signs next time bud and this wouldn’t happen. **Ace looks at the guy and hauls back and begins to throw a punch…. But the camera fades too black.**
