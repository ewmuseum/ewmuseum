Pinball Wizard

Mar 14 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
In walks Mr Markowitz, he is wearing a grey shirt with a picture of Homer Simpson on it and the text "i iz smrt" in green, he is also wearing blue, baggy jeans. Markowitz walks over to the light brown cabinets and pulls out a white bowl and a silver spoon. He then goes to his fridge, opens it and pulls out the milk. He puts them both on the counter and then walks back to the cabinet, and takes out his favorite breakfast cereal, honey nut cheerios. He sits down one of the tall baje chairs and starts to make his breakfast by pouring the cereal in the bowl, and then the milk. His dad walks in in white shorts and a blue T-Shirt that says Florida State. He also walks in and sits on the chair to the right of Markowitz. Outside the square shaped white windows we see a bright blue sky filled with plain white clouds and a glowing yellow sun, meaning it is morning. Markowitz: Morning pop! Markowitz takes a spoonful of cereal and pops it into his mouth. He smiles at his dad and then takes another spoonful of cereal. Dad: Morning son, go turn on the radio will ya? Markowitz walks over to the radio sitting on the small round table in the living room. He turns it on and leaves it channel, 104.3. The song Pinball Wizard by The Who is playing. Ever since I was a young boy, I've played the silver ball. From Soho down to Brighton I must have played them all. but I ain't seen nothing like him In any amusement hall. That deaf, dumb and blind kid Sure plays a mean pinball! Dad: Two words: Good, Choice. He stands like a statue, Becomes part of the machine. Feeling all the bumpers Always playing clean. plays by intuition, The digit counters fall That deaf, dumb and blind kid Sure plays a mean pinball! Dad: One of my favorite songs. Markowitz: Agreed. He's a pinball wizard There has to be a twist. A pin ball wizard, S'got such a supple wrist. How do you think he does it? I don't know! What makes him so good? ain't got no distractions Can't hear no buzz and bells, Don't see no lights a flashin' Plays by sense of smell. Always gets the replay, Never seen him fall, That deaf, dumb and blind kid Sure plays a mean pinball. I thought I was The Bally table king. But I just handed My pinball crown to him. Even on my favorite table He can beat my best. His disciples lead him in And he just does the rest. He's got crazy flipper fingers Never seen him fall. That deaf, dumb and blind kid Sure plays a mean pinball! The whole time Markowitz and his dad were singing along and joking around. Markowitz forgets about his cereal and leaves it on the counter. Markowitz and his dad sit down on the black, wrinkly, old couch in the living room. Dad: That was some performance out there from the both of us. Markowitz dad is breathing heavily twitching a bit. Markowitz: Yeah, dad what's wrong? Dad: Nothing, I'm just a little, ow... uhh.. My stomach.. OW.. son.. I cant breath.. I don't feel so.. Markowitz dad gets up before falling to the ground with a very loud "thump" Markowitz: Dad! No. DAD? Dad get up! No.. NOT another..... Please dad.. Markowitz runs over to the kitchen and takes the phone out of the receiver. He dials 911 as fast as he can. The phone is ringing and then a woman picks up. 911: Emergency call. Markowitz: Yes, my dad he.. he.. he fell... I don't.. I think he may have had a *gulp* heart..attack... he.. we were singing this song.. on the radio.. and... he said his stomach hurt.. he.. he then fell.. he is still on the ground.. 911: We'll be there any minute. Don't worry. Markowitz: Thank you.. I'm scared..What should I do? 911: Just stay calm. Our ambulance is on it's way. Markowitz now bursts into tears. He is uncontrollably crying. He sits were he is, sobbing. Minutes later the ambulance arrives and the paramedics come in and put Markowitz dad on a stretcher and carry him out. About 7 minutes later they arrive at the hospital, they quickly run Markowitz dad into a room. They get him in there and Markowitz tries to get in but they tell him to wait in the waiting room. He walks over there and once again bursts into tears. He walks over to the waiting room and sits down on a blue chair at the end of the row and just does nothing. He waits and waits as he is crying. Suddenly a man walks over to him. He quickly realizes who Markowitz is. Doctor: Your Mr Markowitz. From MWE. Markowitz *still crying*: Yes, I.. I am.. Doctor: Your facing your partner right? Mr Markowitz now looks up and smiles for a second. Markowitz *calmed*: Yea. Doctor: Well, I am the assistant of the head doctor here at Dallas Hospital. I know what happened to your father and I wanted to speak to you about something Irrelevant about this.. like MWE so you can take you mind off of this whole thing. Markowitz: I am facing Triple G. And all I want to say is that at this time, I need him more than I ever did. He is my cousin, my family, my own blood and I need him to help me in this rough time. Doctor: Very good.. Markowitz: When I face you, all I want you to know is that I love you, and no matter who wins.. we will still always be Blood Fuze. Win or lose, I still want you to be my partner and continue our stride to the Tag Championships. I also want you to NOT have any mercy for me in the match because there is no way I am going to do that for you. I want you to hurt me and try to defeat me, which we both know won't happen. But always remember what I told you. Never give up. Never back down. And never have mercy for anyone. Always try and win and always. Doctor: Very, very good. It always helps to just talk about something else when something bad happens. All of a sudden a woman is a nurses outfit walks into the picture. She walks over to Markowitz. She has a smile on her face and puts her arm around him. Nurse: Good news. Your dad is recovering from his stroke. He wont be able to walk for a bit but in about a months time he will be back in your house and living the same life he lived before. Markowitz has a big smile on his face and then gives a big hug to the nurse and the doctor. The scene fades with Markowitz smiling and tears running down his eyes.
