Simply...Perfect!

Feb 28 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
*Mason Resoan is sitting in a projection room, minding his own business, simply staring down at the floor. Mason stares at the floor, as a video rolls on in the background. The light from the screen is the only light in the room, and it makes Mason only somewhat visible. As evident from Friday Night Overdrive, his Mayhem Wrestling Entertainment United States Championship is missing, as Sinc Mercier now has it. Mason looks up at the film and watches as Sinc Mercier clutches the MWE United States Championship in his hand as he pulls it down. He shakes his head, gives a small chuckle, and applies a nasty smirk to his mouth, almost proud of his loss.* Mason: Sinc Mercier...I am almost proud of you. You actually, for once in your career, stepped up to the plate. You have defeated me in a ladder match...and for that, I congratulate you. Accomplishments are nice to be able to boast about, and majority of them are spread about your career, but in one single night you compiled quite a list. For starters, you ended my undefeated streak here in this new MWE. Secondly, you captured your second MWE United States Championship. However, I have to say that the greatest feat your reached on Friday, was handing me my first ladder match loss. *Mason takes a deep breath, relaxes his shoulders, and slumps back into his seat. Really, only his head and legs are seen, as those are the only two parts of his body that are visible. In the darkness hides a black collared shirt, as well as black jean shorts and black basketball shoes. His gold watch rests on his hand, like always, and he is missing his shades, due to him being inside. Mason takes another breath and looks at the screen and once again, shakes his head.* Mason: Now, I have lost battle royals, cage matches, and even Iron-Man matches, but never a ladder match. I have had over 10 ladder match wins, some for money, some for championships, and some for contracts. And in every single one, whether I rose from the ashes or I dominated the whole time, I won. So, kudos to you for being able to...make me humble. And, while I could make several different excuses, such as the fact that I was shocked by some news before the match, I won't. Truth be told, you beat me and b**ching won't change a damn thing. However, I will say this...win, lose, or draw, I am finished with you. I am now on to bigger and better things...things that will catapult me farther in my career than a mid-card belt. *Mason picks himself up, out of his slump into an upright position. He stands up, quickly spins, and walks over to the wall. He flicks the light switch and in floods light, illuminating Mason so that his wardrobe could be seen. Shortly after, he walked to the projector and pressed a few buttons, and slowly the image of his previous match faded away. Up came a slide-show, and it read "Facts and Stats."* Mason: Sinc, before I say goodbye to you, I would just like to show you a little something. Just to refresh your memory a little bit. Maybe then you will focus, before you start your celebration on becoming the new MWE US Champion. *Mason clicks a button on the projector and the slide changes. It was obviously made in PowerPoint, as there was a simple black background with white text. It said,"Stat #1" on it. Mason set his hand down and looked at the screen.* Mason: Now, let me...let us examine this first slide. It shows you our records. Both our match records against each other and against others. First, our records. As you can see, made evident by the white text, you can see that the slide says Mason Resoan = 2 Wins and 1 Loss and Sinc = 1 Win and 2 Losses. Now, by looking at that, you can see that I am obviously superior. Not only did I beat you, but I beat you for the title that you now hold. Clearly shooting me into the advantage. *Mason reaches toward the big projector again and the slide slowly fades out, revealing a new one. This one has more records, and Mason takes a good long look at this one. It is becoming more evident that this was made with PowerPoint, as the text is even simpler, with a strict blue background with orange lettering.* Mason: This slide is possibly my favorite, as it truly shows how much better than you I really am. I mean, look at this slide. Sinclair, you have eight wins and six losses. Right there, you have a winning record of two more wins than losses. On the other hand, I have a winning record of six wins, with a total record of seven wins and one loss. You would have thought that by your tenth match you would have started winning, but instead, you just kept on losing. Pitiful...really and I don't feel a single bit of remorse for you. Let's move on now, to the final slide. *Mason clicks the button and up pops a third and final slide. On this slide is a picture, meaning this is slightly more advanced, of Joey Dallas. Mason chuckles as he looks at it, and then turns away from the projector.* Mason: Now, it may not be my favorite slide, but it is definitely the most important of all. You see, while we don't face off against each other this week, we do, however, have individual matches. This one, is by far my favorite match of Monday Night Mayhem this week. Sinc Mercier v.s. Joey Dallas. This will show if last night was a fluke or if it was the real thing. If you didn't simply keep in your blind rage for one battle, and then let it loose the next. If you can beat Joey Dallas Monday tonight, then I will have a little more respect for you, but not much. You see, I have already beaten Joey. I beat him and then you came down...and beat me. You tried to gain the trust of Joey Dallas...you tried to get his approval. And you thought that it worked...and then it crashed. Joey turned on you and left you to defend yourself. He fed you to the wolves...and the wolves were hungry. That day, you got a wake-up call...I hope you realize that. *Mason takes a little breath in his rant, trying to regain his composure.* Mason: Now, as sick and twisted as that may have been, in the eyes of some, it was only necessary. I see it as a favor Joey tried to do for you, to show you what it is like to be back stabbed. It showed you that no matter how close to someone you get, you have to keep yourself closer, because if you don't...that happens. I hope you understand that despite our match, you are still not what you need to be to actually be considered as consistent. You, unless you can beat Dallas, will never be taken seriously. Not once in your entire career have you ever been granted a world title match, while we both have. If you can beat both of us, then maybe you will have showed someone something, but for now, you are still minor leagues. And if you don't believe me...look at where you still are. Reflect. *Mason sighs as he presses a final button on the slide and watches it as the screen dies down. He walks over to the other side of the room and opens the black door. He begins to walk down a hallway, soft, cushiony rug beneath his feat. He places and hand on the wall to his right as he walks down the hallway. He looks, almost like a grade school kid walking around the school, trying to skip a boring class. He continues to walk rather aimlessly as he comes to another door. He turns, opens it up, and it turns out that he is in a stadium. Inside this room, there are many leisure items, such as a mini-fridge and a pool table, reminding him of his own home. He walks over to the gigantic bay window, and looks down into the empty stadium. The sight from the sky box is quite amazing.* Mason: Sinc, I will not deny the fact that I like the nicer things in life. I won't doubt that I, am in fact, a little greedy, but it is because I deserve it. I have done enough, in my four years as a wrestler, to qualify me as a serious contender. When I go to these places, when I see these things, I do reflect. I realize what I have done to get to this spot, and to be honest, it feels good. If only you could experience what I am looking at right now, you would certainly be in a time of peace and self-control, so you could reflect. Reflect on your first U.S title win, then your second, and now your third. You would be able to reflect on your little rants of "insanity" and your lack of emotionally stability at times...you will be able to see how far you have come. That's what I do...and that is why I am 7 and 1. *Mason takes a short breath again, and runs his hands through his head. He walks over to the glass on the window, turns to the side, and leans against the glass, his forehead pressed against it ever so gently.* Mason: And with these reflections in mind, I am able to plan for the future. I am able to see what lies ahead for me...and my girlfriend. Which brings me to my next topic at hand. Sara Whitaker, for a while, was the one person I opened up to and allowed in my life. She was, what some people would call, my pride and joy. She brought me happiness and pleasure, as well as stability and care. She was a loving person, to those who she thought to be competent and deserving...and you took that away from me Sinc. Well, up until Friday Night Overdrive, I had no idea who had done it...and like you were, at times, I was in a state of confusion. Well, here is an update...Sara Whitaker is coming back this Monday night on Monday Night Mayhem. We will both be in the ring, sometime before our match, and we will address what needs to be addressed. You, your ex-girlfriend or wife, or whatever, and anything else. And believe me, it will be important, so you must pay attention. Yes, I did say that I was finished with you, and that is true, but that doesn't mean that Sara is. So, be aware. *Mason lifts his head off the glass, and there is an evident mark that he is there, as there is a big smudge on the bay window. Mason walks over to the pool table, lifts one of the sticks, and chalks it. He takes all the pool balls out of the respective slots, places them in a pyramid, enclosed by a black triangle piece of plastic, and forms them perfectly. The eight ball rests in the middle of them all. He lifts the piece of plastic, places the stick on the table, and walks to the end of the billiards table.* Mason: Well now...it is time to get down to business...my match this week on Monday Night Mayhem. Against the head b**ch in charge, the only woman wrestler in MWE, and one hell of an athlete. Ace Cannon's girlfriend...a decision in which I question. Ace Cannon...of all people? Now, I wasn't in Enigmar Wrestling or Revolution Pro, so I don't know your history, but I can say this much...that was a bad decisions...and I know bad choices when I see them. Look at Sinc, I tried to tell him, and he didn't listen. Ha, so you better hope that this is your biggest one. *Mason picks up the pool stick, slides in through his fingers, and stretches back his leg. He grips the fine burgundy wood that the stick is made of, gets the cue ball in position, and thrusts the stick forward. Almost as if it was a Rube Goldberg machine, the balls begin to fly all over the place. Some, immediately after the break, go into various sockets, but others linger around, only for Mason's magic to work. Two balls bounce back and forth off the bumpers of the table, rapidly flying this way and that, before they each knock themselves, as well as another ball into other sockets. The cue ball spins, majestically in place, as all the other balls spin around it, except the eight ball, which knocks the cue ball into several others. This begins a chain reaction, sending other balls flying into their sockets, as well. After all the chaos, on the billiards table, only two balls remain. The cue, and the eight ball, which align in a perfect position, directly across from each other. Mason reels back again, gets in stance, snaps his billiard stick forward, and with one last movement, sinks the eight ball in.* Mason: I may not be perfect, Paige, but you can be damn sure that I am good at everything I do. *Mason takes his stick and places it back on the rack in which he grabbed it from. He returned to the bay window as the pool balls rolled back into their places and Mason had a smirk on his face, ear to ear.* Mason: Paige, when it comes to women, I like to stay respectable. To be honest, I never once hit a woman or even picked on one. I like to stay classy, because your name is a very precious thing. However, when it comes to wrestling...I could care less about my rules. I could care less about my morals, and I could care less about my opponent...so, if that happens to be a women, that so be it. Now, I am sure you have heard many different variations of this speech before, but it never makes it any less true. I want to get this out of the way now, because if, even once, I get in trouble for doing anything to you that could be potentially dangerous, well things might get a bit more feisty...but that is a what if question. What isn't, "if", but rather fact, is the knowledge that we have never faced each other. I don't know if you know me, but I can say that I certainly know you...and I can't say that you are all that bad. *Mason turns around, from facing the bay window, and his grin has turned into his smug cocky grin.* Mason: I mean, you can see that D.C is nothing more than hype...so we already have something in common! *Mason gives a slight chuckle to his less than intelligent remark, and walks over to the pool table. He rubs the felt of it, smooths out a spot, and hoists himself up. As he sits, he stares around the room a couple of times before focusing again.* Mason: D.C...the man without a plan...and even if he had a plan, it wouldn't be any good, now would it? Now, I am not going to go around pointing fingers, as I don't think that you did it or anything, but I just think that you must be extremely happy about his misfortune. I mean, I don't have to much wrong with him, but I certainly have my issues...but you... *Mason's grin expands a little bit as he gives a big smirk.* Mason: You seem like you want him dead sometimes! I mean, why shouldn't you? Lindsey, the girl, as it seems, of your dreams, is currently dating him. D.C is the only one preventing you from truly being happy with her and not only that, but it seems as though neither of you have any control of Eli Silver. I mean, he caters, or at least he did, cater to you both. So, not only does he interfere with the girl of your dreams, but he also messes with Eli Silver, and not only that...but he has beaten up your boyfriend a couple of times too. *Mason puts his hands to his mouth and makes a little "tehe" type of sound.* Mason: Did I go to far? Oh, well, I can go as far as I want, because Monday isn't going to be D.C v.s. Ace Cannon. It won't be Ace Cannon v.s. Mason Resoan and it won't be Paige v.s. D.C. No, it will Mason Resoan v.s. Paige for the first time ever, and you know that every time Mason Resoan is in a match, that it will be big...it just so happens that two of the top contenders in MWE today are paired together. *Mason swings his legs off the pool table and hops down and walks over to a chair on the other side of the room. He sits down in the comfortable seat and rubs the side of it. He smiles a big smile and continues to rub.* Mason: I remember, from around the 80's...I can't remember his name, but he is very similar to my matches. I don't remember his name, all I know was that he was perfect, just like my matches...just like this chair...and this arena, and a lot of different things in my life. He was one of the only guys I could say I respected when I grew up watching wrestling. His excellence in everything he did has inspired me and ever since then, I have striven to be just like him. Well, now I know longer strive to be him, because I am what I want to be...and if it happens to look as though we are similar, than so be it. As far as you Paige, I would say you are on the border of perfect and normal...almost like what a demi-god is. You are imperfect, but with the right choices and the right decisions, that could change. *Mason has since stopped rubbing the chair, and has rather kicked back and relaxed a little more. With this last statement, there is a certain aurora in the air that makes him seem like the most cocky man in the world. He continues to smirk and laugh though, as he sits a little straighter up. He leans forward, with his hands on his knees and his head bent past his legs.* Mason: Paige, I have beaten the best of the best because of my decisions. With my intelligence, I have been able to determine, in the blink of an eye, what the best outcome would be. I know that with my decision making skills, that I will be able to go far. That I will be able to keep my word whenever I claim to do something, and I know that I am not wrong. Not once did I ever guarantee a win over Sinclair Mercier, and in fact, I was expecting to lose. I knew, for whatever reason, that he might beat me at Friday Night Overdrive, and I was right. In fact, I encouraged him to do so...not to make me humble, but rather to prove me right...and guess what? He did exactly that. So, when I say that if you continue down your path of bad choices, such as associating yourself with a man like Ace Cannon, then bad things could happen...especially if it starts to involve me. *Mason stands up, always having to move, and begins to pace around the room in a very organized manner. Three steps forward, turn, three steps forward, turn, and then three more steps forward. He is looking at the ground while he does this, and he places his hands in his pockets.*' Mason: Now, despite my banter on decision making and my constant trying to tell people that I am right, I am not forcing anything upon you. I am no Joey Dallas. I don't force people to do anything, I let them be, but simply voice my opinion. It is your choice if you want to listen to me, and that is what I told Sinclair last match. Well, two times previous, I told him the same thing, and he had not listened. Well, finally on our third match, he listened, and what do you know he beat me. I guess it goes to show that you can't beat yourself, and with the aid of Sinc, my own advice beat me. So, I will give you that same opportunity, and say the same thing I told Sinc. Focus. Ignore Cannon, ignore Lindsey, ignore D.C's condition, and just focus. *Mason switches up his pattern of pacing and begins to walk about the room, almost in circles. He is no longer looking at the ground, either. Instead, he is looking straight in front of him, and his path seems very peculiar.* Mason: And the reason I say focus, is because I am not a fan of easy wins. I like competition, and I like to test myself. I know that you are not a very easy to beat superstar, and that is why I have to train myself to make these situations harder. In the hard times of war, the strong prevailed. Well, I wish to be one of those people that will be remembered for being strong. For being powerful, for being attentive, and for being...perfect. *Mason stops his pacing and walks over to a closet. He opens it up, reaches inside, and pulls out a sweatshirt. It is black, with white lettering, and says Adidas on the front. He takes his hands out of his pockets and places them in his sweatshirt's pouch.* Mason: Perfection is a state of mind, it is a form of person, a collection of items, and a position of well-being. Well, like I said in my interview after Friday Night Overdrive, I am missing one thing out of those three criteria. I have the form of a person with awesome strength and breath-taking quickness. I am in a state of mind of complete and total domination, with nothing but good thoughts. I, despite things that have happened recently, am a happy man with my priorities set. I am in a position of well-being like no other. Great wealth, awesome physical condition, and friends by the thousands. Not only that, but I still have a larger fan-base than most of the other mid-card chumps, despite my action. However, I am missing one key element, and that is a collection of items. I have won the United States Championship, the Tag-team championship, and once before the World Heavyweight Championship. However, once before will not cut it. I need it once again...I need that belt around my shoulder soon...because until them, I am not truly...perfect. *Mason sighs as he continues his pacing around the room. He cracks his neck and stops and hears a small ring. He takes out a phone from his right pocket, the Dare by Verizon, and answer the call. His face lights up as he hears the voice of Sara Whitaker* Sara: Hey, so, am I still coming to Monday Night Mayhem tomorrow night? Mason: Of course you are coming tomorrow, baby, not worry. I have some bad news though. Sara: What is it? Nothing between us, is it? Mason: No, no, it's just...I lost the MWE United States Championship. Sinc Mercier beat me. Sara: ARE YOU KIDDING ME! Sinc f**king Mercier. Why..When...How? *There is an obvious amount of disbelief in Sara Whitaker's voice. Mason's smile is dimmed a little, but he keeps his cool.* Mason: Chill, it is cool, I am going for bigger and better things. More specifically, winning the MWE World Title again. Sara: Well, you better hope that you win it. Is it a definite yet, or is it another one of those "wants?" *Mason's smile is almost completely gone now, but he is trying to contain himself as much as he can.* Mason: Listen, it is not definite now, but I will talk to you tomorrow night. I will have a clearer head and we will be able to discuss the future. For now, just rest until tomorrow...there might be some stuff going down tomorrow night. Sara: Alright, baby, I'll talk to you tomorrow. Love you, goodnight. *Before Mason has a chance to reply back, she hung up. He took the phone away from his face and frowned. He dialed another number, and as it rang, he placed it back to his head.* Mason: Hey, so, are you sure of what is going on tomorrow? No confusion, right? ???: No confusion, baby, it is fine. So, when should I come out? Mason: Believe me, you will know when to come out. Don't worry about dressing up, too much...things might get messy. ???: I thought that you were going to do it? Mason: You deserve to have a little fun...don't worry about it. It is yours. ???: Well, thank you, baby. See you tomorrow! Mason: No problem, see you tomorrow! *Mason hangs his phone up, places the phone is his pocket, and stretches his arms up into the air. He takes a big breath and sighs, relaxing. His loving smile instantly turns into his cocky smile. He turns to look at the bay window one more time, and smiles even wider as he walks back to the sky box door. He turns to the camera, opens the door, and smiles as big as he can.* Mason:See Paige, that was simply...perfect! And if you didn't know...now ya know! *Mason then opened the door all the way, took his Zune out of his pocket, plugged it in, and left, closing the sky box door behind him.*
