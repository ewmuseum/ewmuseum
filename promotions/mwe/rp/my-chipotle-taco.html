My Chipotle Taco; Zack Crash RP

Jul 15 2009

Zack Crash

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Opening scene. It is early afternoon as the sun hangs high in the crystal blue sky. Just outside of downtown Charleston, we find ourselves near a long stretch of city-street, surrounded on both sides by mini-malls. The whole street is covered by a veritable cornucopia of businesses, including a Hollywood Video, Hobby Lobby, 5/3 Bank, as well as several others. Many people step in and out of the various places, going about their daily lives without much incident. However, near the end of the road, there sits a certain place reeking of total awesomeness, among other things. Chipotle And it is this place that is the object of our interest. As we close in, we find the inside to be packed, which is not surprising considering it’s lunchtime. The place is loud with noise, both from several intermingled conversations, as well as the employees doing their work, shouting orders to each other. The camera pans down the line to find, near the end, two familiar men. The first man, the one looking very disgruntled with his arms crossed, is Kent Bishop. And predictably, the man to his right is everyone’s favorite thrill seeker, Zack Crash. Kent: God this line is taking forever! Kent says, visibly irritated. Oddly enough, despite the noise, the camera picks up every word the two of them is saying clearly. Zack: Calm down man. See, the lines moving. The camera focuses on the front of the line, which seems to have moved all of two centimeters. Kent turns a scowling look at Zack, who just smiles back, unaware of Kent’s inner desires to strangle him. Kent: Why do we have to eat here anyway? I wanted to get some Chinese anyway. We could’ve gone to that new buffet. Zack: No way dude. I love this place! This place is the best! Kent can’t argue with that. Chipotle is a great place to eat. But looking at the length of the line, he also can’t argue that the buffet is looking pretty good right about now. Kent: Well at least there we wouldn’t have to wait in line. Zack: You care too much about instant gratification. Don’t you ever wanna just sit back, relax, and just take everything in. Kent: No! It’s lunch, I’m hunger, and I wanna eat…NOW! Zack just shrugs. It’s pointless to argue the point any further. The next several moments are pretty dull, as Zack and Kent wait in silence as the line moves at a snails pace. So I guess I’ll skip the details. Fast Forward. Now we’re at the moment where Zack and Kent are next in line. Kent looks up at the menu above, stroking his chin as a young Caucasian girl waits to take their order. What? Did you think everyone who works a Chipotle had to be a Mexican? Forshame! Kent: Let’s see. I guess I’ll have a Chicken Burrito with pinto beans, rice, mild salsa and that’s it. And to drink, I’ll just have water. Chipotle Employee: Right away sir. In a flash, the team of highly skilled fast food, minimum wage workers have Kent’s order already prepared and on a tray. Now, it’s Zack’s turn. He mirror’s Kent from before, stroking his chin as he looks at the menu hanging above their heads. His face is deep in concentration as he scans all the possibilities in his head. Zack: Hmmmmm… Several minutes go by. Zack still hasn’t begun his order. Zack: Hmmmmm… He opens his mouth to order, but closes it quickly. More time goes by. Zack: Hmmmmm… Kent begins to tap his foot in irritation as the line behind Zack starts to build. Zack: Hmm- Kent: WOULD YOU HURRY UP AND ORDER!!! Zack just chuckles as he pats Kent on the back. We get the distinct feeling that Zack was stalling simply to annoy Kent for the fun of it. Zack leans in one the window, peers down into the array of goodies ready for his consumption, looks at the employee and begins. Zack: Give me a steak burrito. Black beans, rice, onions and green peppers, spicy salsa, and some guacamole. And to drink…I’ll have a uh…root beer. The employee nods in confirmation, and just as quickly as before, Zack’s meal is ready in well-rehearsed time. The employee puts the burrito on a tray and hands it to Zack, who offers her a suave smile. Zack: Thanks sweetheart. Say, you’re pretty cute, you know that? How’d you like to go out sometime? The employee blushes furiously as Kent rolls his eyes. Kent: Let’s just go eat Romeo. Kent says, paying for the food and pulling Zack away by the arm. Zack looks back one final time, putting up his hand in the universal sign for a telephone and mouths: “Call me.” Kent: Man, do you ever quit? Zack: Nope. Zack says with a grin. Kent and Zack end up taking a small table for two over in a deserted corner of the restaurant by the windows, overlooking the outside eating area. They both sit down, go off to get their drinks, and return. Eagerly they both unwrap their lunch and take them in hand, but before biting into his, Zack looks down at his own with a curious expression. Kent: Waa iz it? (Translation without a mouth full of food: “What is it?”) Zack: You ever hear that one joke by comedian Jim Gaffigan? Kent swallows his food and shakes his head. Zack: He did this one bit about how Mexican food is just the same stuff just renamed. It went like, ‘What is a Taco?’ Zack does his best to reenact the joke he is speaking of, raising the pitch of his voice to match the voice used by Jim Gaffigan during the joke. Zack: ‘It’s tortilla with cheese, meat, and vegetables. Well what is a burrito? Tortilla with cheese, meat, and vegetables. Well what is a toast tata? Tortilla with cheese, meat, and vegetables? Well what is- Look it’s all the same! Just say a Spanish word and I’ll bring you something.’ You should check him out. He’s really funny. Kent: Uh-uhh. Kent is busy ravaging his food and doesn’t appear to have heard a thing Zack just said. Zack, as usual, either didn’t notice or doesn’t care. He opens his mouth to begin eating, but again, closes his mouth before taking a bite, looking down at his burrito with yet another curious look, as if he’s never seen such a thing before. Kent: What now? Zack: Have you ever heard those Chipotle commercials on the radio? Kent: No. What about them? Zack: Well they have people come on and talk about their own custom burrito or taco combinations. Then they all talk about how they’ve given it a sorta nickname, like The Repeater or something like that. Kent takes another large bite of his burrito, chewing it vigorously and washing it down with a swig of his water. He looks at Zack with a sidelong look, wondering where he’s going with this. Kent: Sooooooo? Zack: Sooooooo I was thinking… Zack levels the burritos with his eyes, examining it intensely. Zack: …what kinda name do you think I should give my burrito? Kent, who was busying taking another bite, hunched over and coughed madly. He looked to be near choking on his food, but a few quick slaps to the chest and he’s fine. He took a therapeutic gulp of water before looking at Zack with an exasperated look. Kent: Excuse me? Zack: My burrito. What do you think I should call it? Kent: Why on God’s green earth would you want to name your burrito? Zack: Because it’s cool. Zack spoke as if this settled the matter once and for all. He took thoughtfully at his food, twisting and turning it in his hand while Kent buried his face in his palms and shook disappointedly. Zack’s face brightened. Zack: I know how about…El Grande? Kent: Why that? Zack: Well it sounds Spanishy for one thing. Besides, since my burrito is kinda spicy with the salsa, I thought it might sound appropriate. Kent: It doesn’t “sound” Spanishy, it is Spanish you idiot. Kent starts to eat slower now. Knowing Zack, this train of thought will occupy his mind for a while, and he’ll try to involve him in some way. This way, he has something that he can use to distract himself from being apart of this asinine topic. Kent decides to propose his own idea, hoping it’ll end the conversation. Kent: Why not just call it the Thrill Seeker? That’ll fit your wrestling nickname. Zack shakes his head, rubbing his chin. Zack: No. They’ll be expecting that. I have to think of something more original. Kent: Who’s they? Finally, Zack begins to eat his food. He takes one large bite, chews it for a moment, and then swallows. He looks at his food with a rather disappointed look on his face. Zack: Well that’s no good. My food’s cold. Kent: Well not $hit Sherlock! You’ve been sitting there for about five minutes just starring at your food. Zack finally begins eating in a steady stream, and Kent heaves a sigh of relief. He feels as if Zack may finally no longer be interested in continuing the conversation and he can finish eating in peace. But there’s no such luck for Mr. Kent Bishop today. Zack: I got it! How about…The Hot Rod? Yeah, I like it. Got a nice ring to it. The Hot Rod! Kent just groans. Before Zack can go on there is a small, weak tug of his shirt. He looks down, Kent following his gaze, to find a young boy looking up expectantly at him. He has a short crop of blonde hair, and from the looks of him, he can’t be much older than six or seven at the oldest. Kid: Are you Zack Cwash? Zack looks down at the kid, turning in his chair to give the kid a friendly smile. He always did like kids. Especially the ones who are his fans. Although Zack appreciates all his fans, it’s the kids that give him the most pleasure. To him, they were the truest wrestling fans out there. Zack Crash: Why yes I am. The kids face lighted up, and Zack could see the magic forming behind his blue eyes. He began jumping up and down, treating the whole situation as if he were meeting his hero. Kid: I wike you a whole wot. You’re my favowite westler. Zack chuckled. He found the kid’s small lisp to be cute. He got off his chair and kneeled so he could be face to face with the young boy. Looking the boy in the face, Zack feels as if he radiates nothing but pure joy and is sharing it with him. Zack: Why thank you very much. What’s your name kid? Kid: Billy. Billy flushes a light pink and fidgets with his shirt. He looks very nervous, almost like the kid at a spelling bee who’s just been ask to spell a difficult word he has only just forgotten. Zack smiles, getting an idea. Zack: Hey Billy, would you like a brand new Zack Crash t-shirt? Billy: OH YES, YES, YES!!! Young Billy jumps up and down, flailing his arms excitedly as if Christmas had come early. Zack holds up a finger to calm the child down, then rushes out to the car, grabs one of the newly printed Zack Crash t-shirts hot off the presses, then returns. He hands it to Billy, who looks near tears of joy. Zack feels a surge of warm pride for having just made the kids day. Billy: Thank you soooo much! Zack: No problem kid. You take good care of that. It’s going to be worth a lot one day. Zack says with a wink. Billy just nods his head. Zack holds out his hand for a high-five, which Billy energetically returns before he is ushered away by his mother, who thanks Zack humbly. Zack watches as they walk away, still smiling. Kent: That was really nice of you. Zack: I didn’t do much really. Just gave the kid a shirt. It isn’t that hard. Kent: But still, you made the kids day. Zack: I guess. When it comes to the fans, it’s the little things that count dude. Zack returns to his meal, eating slowly. Kent remains still and silent, briefly stunned. This is a side of Zack he’s never seen before, and the shock of witnessing it hasn’t entirely left him yet. His eyes flash as he just realizes something. Kent: Hey Zack, I’ve been meaning to ask you. Why have you started carrying those new shirts of yours everywhere you go? Zack: Oh, just in case I run into a situation like this? I always try to make time for the fans and I at least wanna give my something. Even if it’s just a t-shirt. Zack speaks plainly and casually like he was describing the weather. For the first time, we see Kent break a small smile, proud of Zack and his charity. It’s amazing how one man could speak of such kindness as if it were nothing. Zack: Now, back to business. I was thinking Fiesta might be a good name. Kent groans deeply. It’s also amazing how Zack can totally kill the mood in one fell swoop. Perhaps he thought to soon. Kent: Dude, you totally just killed the mood, you know that? Zack: I got it! The perfect name! I’ll call it…the Thrill Seeker! It’s great! And it matches my nickname. Kent grits his teeth and starts twitching in mounting irritation. He looks like he’s about to lose it. Zack begins talking to himself for many more minutes, tossing out other names. He’s in his own world now and doesn’t notice that Kent as gotten up from his seat, and as several minutes pass, does not return to his seat. Another tug at his shirt, and Zack turns to find two more kids by his side, these two slightly older than Billy. Kid 1: Hey, you’re Zack Crash aren’t you? Kid 2: We watched you just the other day on pay-per-view! You were totally cool. Zack can hardly contain himself. It seems he’s rather popular today. He thinks to himself that he should’ve brought more shirts with him. But all the same, he spins in his chair and pats both boys on the shoulders. Zack: Why thank you. I’m glad you enjoyed the show. So, what can I do for you two? Kid 1: We just wanna ask you something? Zack leans in closer to hear as the boys work up their courage and daring to ask. Kid 1: Why didn’t you grab the briefcase when you had the chance at Independence Day? Kid 2: Yeah. I mean, jumping off the ladder was cool and everything, but why didn’t you go for the win? Zack is silent for a moment, taken aback by their questions. Kids can be so precocious these days. Zack looks at the camera for a moment, then back at the kids. Camera, kids, camera, kids. He smiles. It seems the kids have presented him with the perfect transition into his promo. Another reason why he loves kids. They can be helpful when you least expect it. Zack: I don’t know how many times I’ve been asked that this week in one fashion or another. Whenever I’ve gone outside, it’s been nothing but people coming up to me to ask: “Zack, why didn’t you grab the briefcase?” “Zack, why do something so stupid?” “Zack, why are you so damn sexy?” Ok I made up that last one, but you get my point. Nothing but people all wanting to know one thing…why? So finally, I’m going to answer that question. The reason is, because there are certain things in this life more important than winning. Sometimes, you have to give up getting the victory in pursuit of something deeper. So yes, I may not have won, but I gained something far more important. Respect. Zack turns away from the boys and faces the camera. It’s at this point he is no longer speaking to the young boys, but rather to all those of the MWE watching right now, speaking freely and forgetting about his surroundings. Not from the wrestlers. I still have a ways to go to get that. I gained the respect of the most important people in this business…the fans. Because it’s them, whether your goal is to make them cheer or jeer, that drives us. Some wrestlers will deny it, but it’s the truth. And now they know what I’m willing to do to my body just to give them a show. They know that their desire is my desire. Their adrenaline is my adrenaline. We get high off each other, and it feeds me in every move I perform in the ring. Now naturally, not everybody is going to agree with my decision, let alone be happy with it. Namely…Chris England. His faces falls slightly, and Zack looks somewhat mournful, maybe even upset. But he quickly washes it away, replacing with that usual grin of his. He speaks again, unaware that as he goes he is drawing more uncomfortable stares from others. Now Chris, I know you’re upset with me and you haven’t been answering any of my calls. And I get why you’re upset. You have every right to be. You’ve been here for months now, just carving for a title shot, ANY title shot. And when you were finally givin’ a shot, with it was just inches away from your thick tea drinkin, crumpet chewing fingers, I jump off the top of the ladder and crash (pun intended) into you and Scotty, giving Peter K. the chance he needed to win. So essentially, it’s all my fault. And I’m sorry dude. I’m sorry that I basically gave the X-TV Title shot to Peter Cliché and Scotty McNugget, a shot you rightfully deserve more. Now I’m not going to say they don’t deserve it, because they do. They’re great talents. All I can say is that I hope you can forgive me. He bows to the camera, looking genuinely apologetic. But as Zack raises his head, he has a difficult time hiding his grin as he recalls the spot of the match. But you weren’t there man. You weren’t on the top of that ladder, listening to the crowd scream, begging for you to just pull of one daring, insane move. And I stood there, bathed in their cheers as they readied their cameras for the moment they hoped would come. And as I looked out at the thousands in attendance, imagining the millions watching at home, it was just so…tempting. I couldn’t help it; I had to give it to them. Like they say ‘the customer is always right.’ And besides… Zack turns his head slightly to the left, arching his neck back and pointing a finger into the skin of his cheek. We can almost see the imprint of a certain size12 boot on his face. Zack hasn’t forgotten. How could you forget having your own teammate kick you in the jaw? …you see that? That’s where I remember taking a nasty big boot to the face for no reason whatsoever. Ok, so I missed a Spinning Heel Kick and accidentally hit you instead. What can I say? Zack shrugs. I f’ed up. So in all fairness, I say that makes us even. But I still don’t get why you’re so upset. You’re acting like it’s the last title opportunity you’ll ever get. Don’t worry man, there’ll be better championships. That championship wouldn’t have appreciated you anyway. She’d probably be minxing around with some other wrestler while your at home with the kids. My point being that with your talent, how can they not give you another championship match sometime down the road. If anything, that move hurt me more than you. Zack picks up his tray, unaware that his every step is drawing every eye. He walks over to the garbage bin and deposits his food, placing the tray with the others on top of the bin. Both kids follow him in his wake, despite the fact that he’s apparently forgotten about them. I mean let me tell you; jumping from 12 foot ladders and gravity do not mix well my friend. But even still, it was totally worth it. Now I was hoping we could make up afterwards, because I gotta say, I still think you’re a cool guy and wouldn’t be against us teaming up again. But this time, we’re opponents. And honestly, I think this’ll be good for both of us. I mean, you get to blow off some steam toward me, and I get another week of fun by having my ass handed to me in a doggie bag. Because if that boot to the face told me anything, it was that Brixton are the real “mean streets” of the world. But before we meet in the ring again Chris, let me just say that you better bring more than that to the match. You better bring everything you got and then some. Otherwise, I’ll be sorely disappointed. The two young boys, who have been hanging on every word he has said, stare blankly at each other. Clearly, the Zack Crash they see on TV is not what they expected him to be in person. He’s a little bit…odder than expected. Kid 1: Well what about your other opponent? Kid 2: Yeah. What are you going to do about Vega Knight? Briefly, Zack turns his attention back to the boys. He looks at them with bulging eyes as if surprised to find them there beside him. But then he comes to the realization. Vega Knight. He’d completely forgotten about him. Which isn’t hard considering the guy hasn’t been seen in months. Again, he turns his attention back towards the camera. Zack: Yes of course, we have another factor to consider in Vega Knight. Now when I first heard of this guy, you wanna know what my first was, ‘Vega? Man his parents must’ve really hated him.’ I mean, who names their kid Vega? Sounds more like a STD than a name. Zack adopts a mock expression of pain as he reaches down to scratch his crotch. Many of the mothers shield their children’s eyes as they watch dumbly, blissfully unaware of what an STD is. “Oh man! My girlfriend gave me vega last night.” Zack chuckles lightly, throwing up his hands and waving them at the camera. I’m kidding, I’m kidding. But there are a few issues about what Vega has said that I’d like to address at this moment. First off, I don’t think you know me very well Vega. Which is ok considering you haven’t been around lately. But let me tell you one thing. I DO NOT blame other people for my losses. Unlike certain others in this business who will remain nameless. My and England’s loss at Independence Day was my fault, and MY fault alone. I accept that and take full responsibility. And guess what, I don’t care. I’m not looking to bounce off any defeat. Because wins and championships aren’t on my radar right now. All I want to do is have as much fun as I can. So for now, I just live from match to match, moment to moment, hoping the next match will be better than the previous and that the next moment will be more thrilling. That’s issue number one covered. The camera zooms in on Zack’s face as he holds up his right hand. He extends his pointer finger a moment, leaving it there alone before the middle finger joins to give it some company. Issue # second, you say I haven’t faced anyone like you before. Now you’re right about that, but you’re wrong if you think that scares me. Because it doesn’t. Quite the opposite in fact. It excites me. I love facing new challenges. Why would anyone want to face the same challenge over and over again, especially if they know they could win every time? I spell that with a capital B – OR – ING. You also said something about me being fearless. Don’t be stupid, of course I have other fears than just needles. Sure, if you did that syringe thing you talk about, I might actually drop one in my pants. All I ever said was that I wasn’t afraid to look my fears dead in the eye and give them a rounding, stiff middle finger. That is what a true warrior is all about, facing their fears. The only thing I do NOT fear, is failing. Which is why no matter whom I’m up against, whether it be one, two, three, or twenty superstars. Whether they big and burly, or crazy and unpredictable, I will always meet it head. Issued number two, covered. Zack raises his third finger, as the total number of finger now reaches…(let me see, one plus two equals…) three? Yeah, three. Issue # third, you talked about who is hungrier, and you THINK it’s you. Notice the emphasis I placed on THINK. If anyone in this match is hungry, it’s me. Not to say neither you or England aren’t, but what else is there for you two to prove? You Vega, are a former Tag Team Champion, and Chris has been enjoying a comfy seat in the MWE Power Ten for some time now, not to mention his success in ICW. So what left is there to be prove, to be hungry for? We all know you’re tough guys. I, on the other hand, still need to show the locker room what I can do. So I won against Blight, big deal. I could’ve been lucky. So I had an impressive showing at ID, so what. Anyone can jump off a ladder onto someone else. But this third match, will be the deciding factor. If I win, it will disprove anyone who might be thinking I’m just a Crash N’ Flash in the pan or just a passing sensation. Like they say, “third times the charm”, and this match will show I’m here to stay and intend to make an impact. Zack looks intently at the camera. In his mind for a moment, he is pictures England, Vega and no one else, sitting past he lens in front of him. And when he speaks, he speaks directly to them. Now that we’re nearing the end of the promo, this is usually the part where I say I’m going to win, how my opponents have no hope, or some other generic crap-olla. But like I said before, that ain’t my style. But I will say this much. Chris, Vega, this may not be a match for championship, or #1 contendership, or even against a champion. But that’s no excuse for all three of us to go out and give each other hell in a hand basket. So bring me all that you got, and I’ll return what you give in kind. Because every moment is a chance for us to shine. So lets go out there and show the MWE faithful what the next generation is all about. Zack finishes as silence so thick its palpable sets in on him. He looks around to find all eyes on him. Everything is quiet. Nobody is moving as they eye him awkwardly, as if he were insane. Zack smiles an embarrassed smile before slowly slinking out the door. Kid 1: He’s really weird. Kid 2: Totally. Outside, he goes for where Kent has parked his car, only to stop short and find the space filled with nothing but air. He had completely forgotten about Kent, and it appears Kent has left him behind. Zack rubs his head, thinking hard. He’s in a real pickle now, but he doesn’t look too concerned. Zack: Looks like I’m hitchhiking my way home? Stepping to the curb, looking at the endless line of on coming traffic, Zack sticks a thumb high in the air. Almost at once, as if on cue, a shiny red Corvette pulls over. Inside, sitting in the driver’s and passenger’s chair, are a pair of two fine looking ladies. They say nothing, but eye Zack with longing. Zack also says nothing, but smirks at the camera with a thumbs up. He hops in the back, and the last image we see is a red blur as the car speeds off into the distance. –FADE–
