Is it cool to work at Burger King?

Apr 8 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
MWE cameras open on a scene similar that the Virus’ promo are used to starting at. A bar. It’s not your average strip bar either; the girls are actually not bad-looking. Then again, this is a bar that MWE’s The Virus frequently travels to. The camera pans over to a pair of slacks of someone who is sitting on a chair, with something in his pants standing at attention. The white slacks he is wearing cannot cover it up, so he attempts to put his hands over it. The camera slowly rises and shows a full-on white suit with black sunglasses covering the familiar green eyes of one Brian Michaels. The camera closes in on him, with his body sprawled out on the chair, and shows off his long, athletic figure. His arms are almost popping out of the designer white suit, as a stripper in a black thong, heels and a double D lacey black bra. Her blonde hair smoothly sways back and forth with her body, as the luscious beat of the music fuels her adrenaline and her attractiveness at the same time. As she continues to give Michaels a lap dance, he coughs abruptly, turning her off and almost throwing her off her regular routine. She puts her hands on her hips, giving a “DOUBLE U TEE EFF” kind of look as Michaels takes his iPhone 3G out of his pocket to read a newly-received text message. He presses the main screen button at the bottom of the phone, and reads the text. QUOTE Recieved Mar.19th – 6:07 PM by Mike hey…this bitch is crazyyyy, and shes pregnant…what do I do…hit me up man -D.C. – Y0UR G1RL 15 MY B1TCH. 345 4 LyFe. Michaels: Why am I JUST getting this now? For…fuc---goodness’ sake. Michaels hits the green “reply” button as the virtual keyboard pulls up with all the letters, and he begins to feverishly text back. QUOTE Its like the song says…when it comes to condoms…put two on LOL. Sucks for u… Sent – April 5th – 11:31 – by Brian He presses the lock button on the top of the phone, and puts it in his pocket with a smirk. The stripper looks back at him with an angry face, jealous that Michaels chose texting over her lap dance. She speaks in a Russian accent. Stripper: Ju…haz cell over me? Michaels leans back in his chair, pondering how to respond to the confused Russian. He puts his right index finger on his lips, before nodding his head slowly. He now speaks with a mocking Russian accent. Michaels: JES! The stripper waddles off in a huff, obviously annoyed at the Mayhem Mafia wrestler’s actions. Her stripper heels click again the hard platform beneath her, as she slowly leaves the camera’s view. It pans back to Michaels, who now has thrown a dollar bill on the floor. What he doesn’t realize, however, is that it’s his last dollar bill. Not figuratively speaking, literally. People may say, well Michaels is a wrestler, how could he be broke? Well young padawans, what you DON’T know, is that Michaels is about as broke as Mike Tyson, to use that analogy. All he has left are his personal belongings. MWE no longer pays the charismatic son of a gun himself, so he now must find a job. As we reflect back to the scene, Michaels is no longer happy-go-lucky as he was at the beginning of the promo. He takes a deep sigh, before getting out of his chair and slowly walks out of the strip bar, passing by several drunk, bearded men with blonde and brunette golddiggers rubbing their crotches. He ignores the young 18 year old getting his first (or second) sight of some tig ol' bitties, He is now outside, standing out with his white suit. As he turns to his left, he sees a group of teenagers wearing all-black, making his white suit REALLY standout. To his right, he sees a group of three old men, exchanging their CASH MONAY! as Wilmer Valderamma would say. Michaels gets the sense that these men are very, very rich, so he approaches them in a non-chalant manner. Michaels: I see you gentlemen have a load of cash… The seniors laugh out loud, chuckling off the thought that the younger, yet athletic man next to them could have a business proposal. His baby-faced nature makes him seem even younger around the older men, but he shrugs off their chuckles. Michaels: Look, I have no money right now…I’m looking to sell my stuff. Michaels bows his head down a bit, almost embarrassed, as the men look over his white suit, which should be worth a very large sum. One of the men turns around fully to look at Michaels in the face, obviously intrigued. Senior #1: What stuff? He points at his custom-made Mayhem Mafia suit, which he has always wanted to wear. It pains him, but he reaches into his pocket and takes out his slick iPhone 3G. The man’s furry eyebrows perk up, astonished at the items Michaels is attempting to sell. Michaels holds out the iPhone in his right hand, and extends his left hand for the money. There is a stiff tension on the sidewalk, with the five men giving each other a gleaming look. Senior #1: How much? Michaels licks his lips in a sly motion, and instantly the men know that they are going to have to pay a very high price for it. Michaels: 200 for the phone…150 for the suit…and if you want…300K for the car. A fancy-ass silver car pulls up to the sidewalk; it’s a Mercedes-Benz. The Benz’ rims spinning and the reflection of the street are bouncing off the car. They walk near the car, admiring its slick features. They realize that they are getting a sell. The leader of the group rubs his chin, before nodding his head slowly. Senior #1: We’ll take it. Michaels gives one last glance at his car, realizing that he is not just giving an ordinary car, but a piece of his heart. The car had been with him for years; from losing his virginity, to being the car that was there when D.C. ran him over. This car was a part of him, and it was hard for him to swallow his pride and let it go. But he had to; for his well-being. The man hands him a suitcase, and Michaels opens it; the green money immediately hits his green, eyes. He doesn’t even bother to check to see if all $300,350 are in it; he simply takes off his suit, leaving him naked aside from a pair of boxers. A driver inside the car throws Michaels the keys. He stares at them, knowing that they held the key to his future, and tosses them at the leader of the group. He then reaches into the white suit he took off, and reaches into the slacks; he takes out the prized iPhone and doesn’t even bother looking at it, simply handing it to the man. He now drops the slacks and suit he held in his other arm and begins to walk away, almost as if to walk away from his past. As he walks on the sidewalk, the thudding of his Air Yeezy’s (one of the only things he hasn’t taken off) almost blocks all his thinking like a cock-blocker. What do I do now, he tries to ask himself. He now rounds a corner, the bitter April weather beating at his body like a boxers jabs, a Burger King lies directly in front of his face. He gives a =/ kind of look, wondering if this was a sign from God that he was destined to work at Burger King. He shakes his head, incredulously, and continues to walk regardless. He tries to stop, as he passes by the other local chains, but cannot. He palms his face and shrugs his shoulders, walking into the Burger King. The bell rings as he looks around, with not one seat taken. There is a red-haired, pimple-faced young man wearing the BK uniform working the counter, and a slightly older, seemingly complacent man working the kitchen. The pimple-faced teen is obviously lollygagging; he is texting feverishly to his “friends”. The other man is just working the Drive-Thru now, which is about as empty and washed-up as Lindsey’s pussy. Michael s walks up to the counter, trying to act as confident as possible, yet can only mutter. Michaels: I want a job here… It pains him to say this, as the redhead slowly takes the time out of his texting spree to look up at Michaels. Michaels, not wanting to let his eyes or the camera get a view of the suitcase with money, puts it behind his back. The redhead ignores the sudden movement, and speaks in a pre-mature voice. Red-Head: What was that? In his head, Michaels thinks “goddamnit”, but doesn’t let the weaker man know about his humility and desire to work anywhere in the world but Burger King. He shakes it off, regardless, and somehow manages to keep his composure. Michaels: I said, I want a job application to work here. With that, the redhead nods his head and puts his cellular device on the counter, which appears to be a G1 with a green cover. He is seen walking towards the kitchen, before disappearing completely from the camera’s view. After a second, he reappears with a short, stubby old man wearing a suit, or what is an attempt of a suit. The perception is that he is the manager of the BK, and he takes his greasy, hairy hand out of his pocket to shake with Michaels. Michaels shakes his head, culpepper’ing his potential new boss. Boss: You uh, you lookin’ for a job? Michaels leans to his right side and hawks a loogey. He raises his right hand and rubs the back of his head in a rough manner, before the camera pans back to the boss’ point of view. Michaels: Yeah… The manager wastes no time in going back to his office, bringing back the BK uniform, complete with black slacks, a black hat with the Burger King logo on the front, and a maroon polo shirt with the letters BK on the right patch. He throws it at Michaels, only for Michaels to side-step it in disgust. Michaels: I ain’t wearin’ that. He stands defiantly, insisting on never degrading himself to the point where he’d have to put on the cheap uniform, but the manager looks at him with a disapproving grin. Boss: Well, I guess you don’t really need this job then… The man walks to the front of the counter and bends over to pick up the uniform, only for Michaels to stomp on the uniform with his left foot. He reaches down and picks it up. He speaks in an exasperated voice. Michaels: Fine. Michaels now walks to the bathroom, feeling degraded and humiliated. The camera does not follow him to the bathroom, but stays in the same vicinity, with the manager looking excited. Boss: Talk about the PR we’re going to get from this…I can see it now…BRIAN MICHAELS WORKS AT BURGER KING! I wonder how he’s going to take orders…”Welcome to BK, my name is Brian Michaels!” Golly, this should be fun! The giddy boss walks back to his office with his hands folded together, thinking of the possible media attention brought to a local Burger King. The camera fades out for a few seconds, before panning back to Michaels, who has now put on the tight BK uniform. He finishes off the outfit by putting on the black hat. The pimple-faced man moves from working behind the register to in the kitchen with the other man, and Michaels takes his place with a mop in his hand. He drags the bucket of water with him over to the corner near the side door. The bell rings once again and in walks D.C., on his crutches, with a D.C. shirt and blue jeans. Also with D.C. is Tera, wearing a black skirt, a white tank top and black boots. She is immediately intrigued in the menu and skips across the mopped area over to the counter. D.C., however, is not as lucky as one of his crutches slips and he falls to the ground on his back. He uses the counter where the drinks are to try and help him stand back up, not wanting to let the MWE fans see him in pain. He gets back up with a smirk, as he holds his back with his left hand and leans on his left crutch. D.C.: Whitman texted me, and I just had to see this for myself. Both men exchange a smirk as the camera pans back to the register to show Tera looking at the menu in an odd way. She begins to jump up and down suddenly, pointing at a specific thing on the menu. Tera: I like, want, like, a vanilla milkshake with like, pickles. The camera fades over back to D.C. and Michaels, as D.C. rolls his eyes. Michaels: So, you joining Mafia then? His former Rated-X friends gives an obnoxious laugh for a second before realizing that Michaels is indeed serious. He tries to clean it up. D.C.: What, the Mickie Mouse Club? Nah, sorry to break your heart Donald Duck. Michaels gives him a grin as D.C. reaches into his pocket, putting one of his crutches on the counter and chuckling again. D.C.: So you know I can sue you for me falling right? Michaels: Good luck... In a showdown of egos, Michaels prevails. They both laugh it off for a second before D.C. finds a way to change the subject. D.C.: Anyway, apparently Cherry came by, and now your life is the pits. So, take my phone, I don’t even know how to work it. He takes the iPhone out of his pocket and hands it to Michaels. Michaels, swallowing his self-pride, grimaces while accepting the gift from his former friend. Tera finally receives her milkshake, and there is an awkward silence. They realize, despite their timid jokes, that just four months ago, they were beating each others’ brains in. After that, D.C. promptly whistles and throws a crutch up as if to call Tera over. D.C.: HEY B****, LET’S GO! They shake hands, as D.C. leaves with Tera. Michaels puts the mop away and begins to work on the cash register. With almost perfect timing, the familiar bell rings and the door is opened to reveal an old man with black hair, on his phone in an obnoxious way. He laughs out loud every three seconds, before finally making it to the counter and not even acknowledging Michaels’ presence. Michaels: …Welcome to Burger King, Home of the Whopper, can I take your- The man interrupts, not caring to hear the introduction. He puts the phone on his shoulder. Customer: Yeah, lemme have a Whopper with cheese, pronto kid. Michaels: MOTHERFU- At this instant, an instrumental version of “New Workout Plan” blasts throughout the Burger King, and Michaels reaches into his pocket and takes out the iPhone that D.C. just gave him. He shrugs, and despite not knowing who is calling and turns it on. Michaels: Hello? There is an almost a deadly, yet funny tone coming from the other side of the phone. Whitman: Remember what we talked about. NO CUSSING, SON! Michaels scratches his head, wondering just how the heck Whitman knew his number. Regardless, he turns the black cell phone off, and stores it away in his right pocket. He looks back at the customer with a grimacing face before entering in the order. Michaels: HEY! COOK THIS MAN A BURGER! The camera pans to the back, where the men are now feverishly at work, cooking the burger and dipping the fries. Meanwhile, Michaels has given the man his drink, and he has filled it with Sprite. After an awkward silence, the food is finished, and is put on a red plate. Michaels goes to hand it to the customer, but the plate slips and the food falls to the ground in a towering mess, enraging the customer. In an instant, he puts his cell phone on the table and threateningly sticks his finger right at Michaels’ face. Customer: YOU DIRTY MOTHER****ER! PICK UP MY FOOD RIGHT NOW, OR I WILL HAVE THE BOSS ON YOUR ASS LIKE HOTCAKES! With that, Michaels is about to explode right back, however the boss hears all the commotion and rushes right out to try and solve the problem. Boss: Hey, hey, hey! What’s goin’ on around here? The customer points at the ground and shows the double-stack of meat slopped around, the ketchup is splattered, and the drink has spilled all the way to the seats. The boss looks on angry, and points to the kitchen. Boss: MICHAELS! GO WORK THE DRIVE-THRU! For god’s sake… Michaels, not sure on whether to feel disappointed in himself for failing to pass the tray to the customer, or the fact that he can’t even succeed at Burger King, of all places. He sighs, and begins to take the long stride to the booth where the ordering takes place. He opens the door and walks to the room, quickly putting on his headset and sitting down. As he waits around, a car finally pulls up to the ordering station and mouths into the speaker. Michaels, wanting to play an elaborate prank, puts on his half-mexican accent. Woman #1: Um, hello? The speakerbox has some static for a second, before a Mexican voice travels outside of It and into the woman’s ear. Michaels: WELCOME TO BURKER KIN! MAY I TAKE YOUR ODER?! Woman #1: Yes, I’d like- The speakerbox interrupts her, and it is apparent that the prank is over. The voice now coming out is angry, and speaks in a rude tone. Michaels: YEAH?! WELL I’D LIKE SOME PUS**! BUT GUESS WHAT? I CAN’T SAY PUS** BECAUSE IT’S NOT THE “BABYFACE” THING TO DO! ISN’T THAT KIND OF ****ED UP?! Ironically enough, Whitman’s one of my best friends now. Anyway, that’s beside the point. The point is, the second I got to the top of Ace Mountain, the “smarks” came out and criticized my every move. I mean, I was always too over-the-top, too emo, or too comedic for their taste after I won the Title. It’s like what the hell, you know? They believe in themselves…they know that while some of MWE’s Main Eventers laugh at them now…in a few years, hell, months…they can be right up there with them, fighting for the gold. THIS PROMO WAS FIRE! FIX THAT SPEAKERBOX! After awhile, the static goes away, and the power is restored. The fans look on with happy faces, as Michaels continues to talk. I want The Virus...not your fake tag team bull**** where you need Vega to hold your hand in every match because you know what Virus...maybe you ARE washed up. You thought you were too good to wrestle guys like me...and now that I've OUTSHONE your spotlight...and now that my ACCOMPLISHMENTS are worth more than yours...you want me in a match? We'll see who's talking s*** when I kick your F***ING TEETH DOWN YOUR THROAT, WE'LL SEE WHO'S TALKING S*** THEN, BOY! Michaels: So, Virus...I only have one question; do you want fries with that? FADE. [/color]
