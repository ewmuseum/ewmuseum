Birth Of The Thrill; Zack Crash RP

Sep 8 2009

Zack Crash

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Many years ago… Within all of us, there are certain moments in our memories that stand out. We recall them with crystal clarity and they can vary in size and meaning, ranging from the monumental to the trivial, or from the joyous to the miserable. It is these experiences that ultimately shape what we eventually call our personalities. Their impact on us cannot be argued, transforming us into something solid and definable, someone with ideals and opinions. As a wise man once said, “without a past, we have no future.” And it is one such moment that we find ourselves in examining today. Many you I’m sure would assume that the last moment we saw of Zack Crash’s past would be the most impactful. While it is a key memory in his life, that is not true. It is the moment you are about to witness that will be the deciding factor to change him forever. For days after being told the truth about his friend Kyle (he only used the word “friend” for lack of a better term), Zack walked around as if in a daze. Although he went through his normal routine, in truth he wasn’t really there. He was just going through the motions of living. He felt much like a blind man who has suddenly regained sight, but the world is no longer how he remembers it. He’d even skipped out on his sessions with Mr. DeMarco, who was understanding enough not to press himself upon Zack until he was ready. And he was far from ready. Today was no different. He was walking down the street after school had finally let out, making certain to take the long way home. He wasn’t in much of a mood to deal with anyone for any reason, so whenever he could avoid people he did. He even avoided thought. Slowly he walked down the street, his face down and his head empty. It was funny really. He didn’t feel broken up inside or numb as you might expect. He didn’t feel much of anything, which actually, if it makes any sense, felt worse to him than anything. Finally, motivated by some urge, Zack felt like lifting his head. And when he did, he was met with a sight that sucked all air from his lungs like a vacuum. There, leaning against a fence some few feet away…was Kyle. For a moment, Zack stood frozen as Kyle smiled back at him, raising his hand in a friendly greeting. Kyle: Hey dude. Zack hadn’t the faintest clue how to react to this. Should he wave back? Or perhaps he should just press on and pretend not to have notice? Considering Kyle wasn’t real to begin with, Zack picked the later. He fixed his eyes forward, straining not to glance sideways as he passed. From behind, he heard Kyle’s feet slapping against the concrete as he followed. Kyle: Hey, what’s up with the cold shoulder dude? I haven’t been in school a few days and this is how you say hi? Harsh dude, really harsh. So, how’ve you been? Kyle replied in a sarcastically hurt tone. Was he serious? Didn’t he know he was fake? So many questions were buzzing around inside Zack’s mind. To make the situation even more bizarre, Zack actually found himself responding to Kyle. Zack: So where you been? Kyle: Oh I just wasn’t feeling good…or at least that’s what the school thinks. I just felt like taking a few days off. So I decided to play some hooky. You up for doing something to day? Zack glanced over his shoulder briefly to see Kyle in sync with his own steps. Heel to toe. Heel to toe. Kyle’s footsteps (if there were any) echoed off of Zack’s. The sound of his feet only made Zack doubt himself further, particularly his sanity. Seeing him following so closely on his heels reminded Zack of a life-sized shadow, which Zack found to be grimly humorous. Eyeing him suspiciously, Zack decided it was time to confront Kyle…this thing…or whatever he was. Zack: How come you didn’t invite me? Kyle: I figured you wouldn’t be up for that right now. I mean you are kinda a wimp. –snicker- But hey, next time it’ll be you and me all the way ok? Zack nodded quietly in confirmation. Passively questioning him wasn’t as easy as it sounded, but he didn’t want to outright confront him either. If only he could come up with some question he couldn’t answer and get him to admit it at least. That would make things much easier. Thinking of something, Zack tried a different angle. Zack: How come you didn’t meet me at Devil’s Backbone the other night? Zack smiled inside his mind. Backed into a corner. There was no way he’d get out of this one. Kyle: Oh, about that. Sorry if I left you hanging, I just forgot about it, that’s all. But hey, tell me about it. Was it any fun? Crap! Guess that strategy just went down the drain. Zack: I just watched. Zack said sardonically as he considered different attacks inside his head. Kyle: JUST WATCHED?!?! You mean you didn’t do anything? What the hell for? Man you really missed out. You’re never gonna experience anything if you don’t take risks every once and awhile. Ok. This was getting too absurd, even for Zack. He was not going to stand here, walking home, talking to an imaginary person, while at the same time being lectured by that imaginary being about how he lives his life. Without any more cares or concerns, Zack spun on his heels so quickly he made himself dizzy. Kyle: Whoa dude! What the hell? Zack: I know what you are? Zack said defiantly, as if Kyle were his mortal enemy. He starred so directly into Kyle’s eyes he could see his reflection in them. Soon, his body began to shiver with a nonexistent cold. He was summoning all his courage just to face Kyle, and it was starting to falter. But he was going to win, no matter what. Kyle only tilted his head and raised an eyebrow in humble curiosity. Kyle: Excuse me, but I don’t know what you mean by that. Zack: You’re not real! You’re just a figment of my imagination! Part of my…subconscious or something. You hear me. You’re a fake! YOU DON’T EXIST!!! Zack shouted those last words, hoping that they would overpower Kyle and he would vanish into nothing. But they didn’t. Kyle just stood there, smiling back at Zack as if he found the scene funny. Then, suddenly, Kyle burst out into a loud fit of laughter. Maybe he did find the whole thing funny. Zack stepped back a few feet, growing increasingly weary. Once he finally calmed down, Kyle whipped away a tear from his eye, a leaned to one side with his hand on his hip. He looked cocky and smug. Kyle: So, how long have you known? Well…this certainly was something Zack didn’t expect. Sure he was aiming to get Kyle to admit it, but he never considered how HE’D react. His facial features bulged in shocked and he stumbled back so far he tripped over his own two feet and fell flat on his butt. He looked up, starring at Kyle as if he were a ghost. And for all Zack knew, maybe he was. Kyle just stood there, hands on his hips, gleaming down at Zack with a triumphant smirk. Seeing him like this made him appear to be a completely different person than before. Zack: You…you knew? Kyle: Of course I knew. I’m not an idiot you know. But then again, I am part of you, so maybe I am an idiot. I don’t know, the whole thing is rather confusing. Zack: Then why the hell didn’t you tell me? Kyle rolled his eyes, as if some ignorant, pestering child were irritating him. The gesture made Zack feel a surge of irritation himself. Slowly, he stood up back on both legs and dusted himself off. Kyle: Well I can’t exactly tell YOU that now can I? Zack: Oh! And why the hell not? Kyle: Because I’m a part of you ya ding bat. Remember? I can’t recognize it unless YOU recognize it first. Understand? Zack didn’t think he could understand even if he tried. The whole thing sounded like nonsense. First he was told he was near schizo days earlier, but now that “schizo being” was telling him he could recognize he was a fake unless Zack did? Zack didn’t know which was more absurd, having an illusion admit to being an illusion, or the fact that he was talking to said illusion. Zack thought it best to end this now before he lost his mind completely. With one strong motion, Zack turned and marched away. Zack: No,I don’t understand. It doesn’t make any sense. And I’m not having this conversation with you. You’re not real. From the sound of feet behind him, he guessed that Kyle wasn’t going to go away so easily. Kyle: Don’t blame me, I don’t make the rules. And I am real man. Just not in the way you think of real. Zack stopped in mid-step, turning to face Kyle with an icy glare. Kyle stopped instantly, standing perfectly still and perfectly straight, the smile still ever present on his lips. Zack: Who are you, Gandhi now? What the hell does that mean? Kyle: It means I’m part of your personality. So when I say “me” I technically mean you. Zack just shook his head in disbelief and stomped away again. Zack: You stay away from me, you understand? Whatever you are, just stay away from me! Zack turned his back and began power walking. But Kyle kept in tune with his steps. Heel to toe. Heel to toe. Zack tried to ignore it as best he could, but not even five minutes later he turned to face Kyle, his face red and his features stretched tight. Zack: I TOLD YOU TO LEAVE ME ALONE! Kyle: Dude, you’re being a real baby about all this. You know the solution to this is real simple. Zack: And what might that be, pre-tell? Kyle’s smirked widened and it was clear to Zack that Kyle was enjoying stringing him along like this. Kyle: Remember when Mr. DeMarco told you that you were scarred of the freedom you had now that your mother was dead? Well, that’s the whole problem. You are scarred. You’re scarred of expressing what you really want. All those “dangerous” things your dear mommy wouldn’t let you do…you want to do them. But you’re too afraid to go for it. But you really…really…REALLY want to. And so, your tiny little mind created me as a sorta outlet for your desires. In Zack’s heart, he knew what Kyle said was true. He remembered a few days before at Devil’s Backbone. He had come to that hill with the courage to ride his bike down its steep slopes, but lost it as soon as he had arrived, only to walk his bike home. Kyle had not been there that day as they promised. And suddenly, it all was as clear as a finely cut crystal. In his heart, he knew the truth. But Zack’s mind wasn’t ready to accept it. Without a word, Zack turned and calmly walked away. Behind his back, he could see or hear the tiny chuckle under Kyle’s breath as he followed after him. Kyle: You’re not going to get rid of me that easily. This proved truer than Zack would have liked. Over the next several days, Kyle was with him everywhere. And when I say everywhere, I mean EVEYWHERE. Whether it was morning or night, Kyle was there. As faithful as the sunrise each morning. Zack did his best to just pretend he wasn’t there, but the truth of our eyes can sometimes be difficult to overcome. And the constant ringing of his voice was even more irksome. He would always interject, usually when someone was already talking to Zack, with a: Kyle: When are you going to accept it? or sometimes: Kyle: You can’t run forever. Perhaps not, but Zack was more than willing to try. But about after a week of this, Zack was starting to feel drawn thin. He hadn’t had a decent night sleep in days, and the skin under his eyes was starting to shag. Kyle: You’re making this harder for yourself than it needs to be. Kyle said one day while twiddling his thumbs, walking by Zack’s side as he walked home from school. Zack was starting to think at this point he’d never be rid of Kyle. So Zack had resigned to letting Kyle speak as much as he wanted, neither ignoring nor acknowledging him. He was something that was just…there. As common and insignificant as a pebble on the road. Or at least, this is what Zack tried to convince himself of. A job which he was doing very poorly. But then, suddenly, he had a thought. It came to him so suddenly it made him physically stop in his tracks. It must have showed on his face, because Kyle looked him a long while, tilted his head and said, Kyle: Are you ok? Zack didn’t answer. His thoughts were racing too fast for his mind to keep up, let alone form into words and sentences. It was all so simple. How could he have not seen it? Zack almost felt like kicking himself for not noticing the solution sooner. But there was no time for that. Regaining himself, Zack went off at a marched that surprised Kyle and forced him to jog to keep up. Kyle: Hey! What’s with the sudden burst on energy all of a sudden. Zack smiled a mirthful smile. Zack: You wanna ride down Devil’s Backbone so bad? Well ok then! Guess what we’re doing tonight? Zack was walking so fast, he failed to see Kyle’s smile from behind, full with just as much mirth, perhaps more so, than his own. Several hours later… It was well into the late night hours before Zack set out on his bike with Kyle sitting behind him. It wasn’t really necessary to do this at night, but for some reason Zack wanted the cover of darkness, and he had a feeling by the end of the night, he’d be grateful for it. The dirt path through the woods was bumpy and uncomfortable, made even worse by Kyle’s constant groaning every time he hit a particularly nasty patch of dirt. Kyle: OWWWW! Watch where you’re going will you! Kyle said irritatingly after one such pothole. Zack ignored, wondering if Kyle was an illusion of his mind, then how in the hell could he feel pain? So Zack continued to pedal, doing his best to avoid the rougher parts of the dirt road. About ten minutes later, they finally arrived. For a long while, Zack stood at the top of the hill, staring down its long, narrow path, surrounded by skeletal like trees. The darkness obscured most of the hill, which seemed to make the hill seem even steeper. A portal to an endless abyss. Zack felt a gust of cold wind, and it whispered thoughts of doubt and fear into his head. What if something happened? What if he got hurt? Who would find him this late at night? He might even die. And for the first time that night, Zack was starting to regret his decision. Klye: So, you wanna tell me what you’re up to? Kyle asked as he stood next to Zack, arms crossed. Zack: Remember when you said I was afraid to do those “dangerous” things I wanted, afraid to express my freedom? Well maybe I am. And if I’m actually going to be free, I need to confront that. So I figured, maybe if I do something risky, something I’ve always wanted to do, maybe you’ll finally get out of my head. Kyle: And why, may I ask, would you wanna ride your bike down a steep slope like this? Zack: I don’t know it just always looked like fun to me. It looks…thrilling I guess. Zack made to move, but the doubts in his head were speaking louder, and with a great deal of logic. This did seem dangerous after all? Why should he put himself at risk for this? Maybe there was a safer way? Slowly, Zack began to back away from the edge. Kyle: You know you can always turn back if you’re too scarred. Zack turned to scowl at Kyle, although he was surprised at his expression. It wasn’t arrogantly or smirkish as it had been recently. It looked calm and blank. Almost guiding. He stiffened his face and snorted at Kyle. Zack: I’m not scarred! Zack said in shaky defiance. Kyle lips curled into a pleasurable, proud smile. Kyle: Then prove it. Was his simple rebuttal. Well…it looked like there was no turning back for Zack anymore. Slowly and deliberately, Zack hooked his legs over the sides of his bike and walked himself just to the edge of the hill. Zack looked down at the hill for a minute, or maybe it was hours. The hill was both calling and foreboding at the same time. Several gruesome outcomes ran through his consciousness, but he took in a deep breath of air, and exhaled them all away. And with a small push, he rolled his bike over the edge. It lasted maybe all of five seconds. Zack gave a loud scream as the tough terrain made the handles wobble in his grip. The ride was proving much harder to control than he expected. Soon, Zack struck something. Maybe it was a rock or tree root. Whatever it was, it launched Zack clear off his seat and down the hillside. Zack struck the ground hard and rolled on his side a few times before lying flat on his stomach as he “flew” like Superman down the hill. Branches and rock scratched his skin and ripped his clothes until, finally, his momentum began to slow, and he came to a halt at the bottom of the hill. Zack didn’t move for a moment. Inside he wondered if he had died, but then realized if he were dead, his body wouldn’t hurt this much. Slowly he got to his feet and checked himself over. He had a few cuts and bruises here and there, but luckily, he didn’t seem to have any broken bones. He looked around until his eyes adjusted to the darkness and found his bike a few feet away on its side. Zack: Well, that could’ve gone better. He said jokingly to himself, patting himself down. And in that moment, as soon as the joke escaped him, he discovered something. He had fallen who knows how far down a steep hillside; hurt himself in doing so, nearly getting himself killed. And Zack was not afraid of those facts. In fact he believed he was having fun. His heart was beating as fast as a drum against his ribs and his blood felt so hot it was making him sweat. The danger he felt, the pumping of his heart, fast racing of blood in his veins. It all felt so…addictive. Zack looked up in realization, and saw that the spot where Kyle had been standing was empty. But Zack didn’t feel Kyle was gone. In fact, he felt closer to him than ever before. He wasn’t gone. He had just gone home. And he understood what Kyle meant when he said, “you can’t get rid of me that easily.” Zack: Thanks man. He said quietly to the darkness. Then, gathering up his bike and heading home, he made a decision. The next chance he got, he was going to do something his mother had never permitted, no matter how much he begged. It was the one thing he’d always wanted that she denied him. So much so as that he almost disobeyed her a few times to get it…almost. Now he had both the freedom, and the courage to grab it. So Zack headed home bruised and battered. And later that week, he would watch his very first episode… …of wrestling. And the rest, as they say, is history. Let us now return to the present. Time passes us by as the years vanish like seconds. A whirlwind of time and space clouds our senses, and we have no distinction of where we are. Soon, the long fast-forward stops, and we are starring straight at the adult form of Zack Crash. We see only from his lower torso up. And draped over his shoulder, is the X-TV Championship belt, gleaming in the sunlight. His hair flaps in the strong breeze and behind him, we see the backdrop of a clear, diamond blue sky. For a moment, Zack gazes at the belt draped over him, and when he speaks, it’s soft and quietly, almost to himself. Zack: I read somewhere once, “Success is when dreams and ambitions meet hard work and determination.” At first it was so simple I thought I understood it right away. But now I know it goes deeper than that. You see after my match at Crowning Glory, after achieving my first great success in MWE, I went to my hotel room, let my bloodied body collapse on the bed, and thought long and hard about that saying. It seemed like I was lying there for hours until it finally hit me like a chair shot to the head. It’s really about the evolution of success. First, we begin with a dream, something lofty and that can’t realistically be obtained. Then, our dreams become ambitions, still out reach, but now obtainable. It’s a reality. The way we achieve this reality is through hard work. But that alone isn’t enough. Many work all their lives, but still die in poverty. But when your hard work becomes determination, when it transform into a desire that pushes you through thick and thin, then it becomes a reality. Because the nice thing about dreams are that they are fantasies. And if you succeed, then it never was a dream to begin with. Slowly, the camera pulls back to encompass Zack’s entire form. He stands firm and proud on what looks to be a slab of mysterious rock beneath him. It’s barely noticeable, but underneath his clothes, we can see the traces of some bandages. Most likely the final remnants of his match with Jay Riddick. Nasty reminders of how hardcore Riddick can be. Another gust of wind whips his hair across his features. So we naturally assume Zack is up high. Maybe he’s been rock climbing or something. But if that’s so, then where is his gear? But that’s not important right now. What is important is his dream. A dream that was very important to him. His dream to make an impact here in MWE. He’s dream to one day, become a champion. His dream to be a success in the wrestling business and to be able to stand next to such legendary figures. But now, it no longer is a dream. Standing here, belt draped over him like a cape, it’s now a reality. And what a sweet reality it is. Ever since I first stepped into an MWE ring, I’ve been following these steps rigorously, slowly turning dream into ambition, and hard work into determination. And at Crowning Glory, all the variables came together to equal the final answer…success. I overcame a great athlete, and a worthy champion to become your new X-TV Champion. I overcame a man who finally conquered his own demons and returned to his former glory, but even still he couldn’t conquer me. My desire, and my resolve were stronger than his. So here I stand still bruised, but not broken. And this is only the beginning. Many of you may look at this belt and may not care one way or the other about who has it. But with me as the champion, I’m going to make you care. This belt may mean nothing to you now, you may look at it as the lowest belt in MWE, but that’s all in the past. Now, when you turn your head away from the X-TV division, I’m gonna force you to look back. When you scoff at the belt, I’m gonna make you recognize it. This belt will no longer be the hand-off belt, the charity championship given to low carders who “might” have potential. When I’m done with this belt, it’ll be the workhorse title. It’ll be the belt you give to future World Champions. Just like my buddy CSB did for the GWC Ultimate Championship, I’m gonna revolutionize this championship, take it to a new level of prestige. And who better to do that than me, the Ultimate Thrill Seeker, the man who isn’t afraid to fly. Not to sound arrogant, but who else than the most exciting wrestler on the roster today could do that? I’m the next big thing, and the belt is only the first of many other successes. And if nothing else, I promise you this much. That I will do my best to be a champion you can be proud of. That I will represent the X-TV Championship to the best of my abilities. That will do my best to go down in history as the greatest X-TV Champion in MWE…ever! Taking the belt from his shoulder, Zack grabs the leather and lifts it high above his head. Another, stronger gust of wind blows past, and for a moment, Zack looks picturesque. He almost resembles a Norse god, holding his weapon aloft in triumphant victory against his defeated adversary. The wind dies away, and Zack returns the belt to his shoulder. But Crowning Glory wasn’t just my night. Oh no. Many of us in the locker room were able to walk out that night with our hands held high. Two such men, were Eric Derf and DC. Why do I mention them specifically you ask? Well it’s because, once again, MWE bookers have placed me in a tag team match, and these two our today’s lucky winners in the “Whose Going To Be Crash’s Next Tag Team Partner” contest. One person who will carry the banner and pull everyone’s weight. But even with GM, I look at this team, and I gotta wonder if they’re really up for this match. While GM is the new World Champion, and thus suggest him being the best, all three men are of equal talent, and equal viciousness. So Zack decides to just do it randomly. Besides, that suits his style much better anyway. “The Devil In Disguise.” “The Antichrist Superstar” “The Counterfeit Christ” Since when was the Church Of Latter Day Saints sponsoring wrestlers? He still remembers how K-Hard locked in that Dragon Sleeper and forced him to tap out the last time they met in the ring. Not exactly a pleasant recollection. But Zack forces it away and smiled. Nothing but smoke and mirrors and a creepy entrance. And once you know that, it all becomes clear. Everything he does, these mind games, which are pathetic at best, his weird entrance, which is lame, all of it is just to cover up the fact that he can’t get by on his own merit as a wrestler. He’s the man behind the curtain…the Wizard of Oz. Even his name is a joke. The Grand Mystique? There’s nothing mystique about you. You’re as easy to read as an open book. Let me break it down for you with names you’ll understand. When you compare us, if I’m Harry Houdini, you’re Bonko The Clown. I mean seriously, the darkest side of the cosmos? This ain’t Star Wars Chewie. The only time you were interesting was back when you had your mask. At least then you actually had some mystery to ya, and I didn’t have to look at your ugly mug shot of a face. When it gets right down to it, your nothing but a gimmick. Which is why you don’t deserve that championship. Mercier was twice the champion you’ll ever be. I am so sick of hearing the same carbon copy crap you all spew about him. He was immature, he vacated the US Championship and blah blah blah. Maybe all that was true, but at least the guy didn’t need no fancy tricks to get by. And chances are, you and the rest of your loser team will shot out the same crap I’ve heard hundred times already about me. Talk all you want, I don’t care what you say about me. Call me a junkie; say I’ve reached my limit, none of your words matter. Because your words can’t hurt me. Sticks and stones and all that jazz. The only approval I need is from the fans. And you don’t know what my limits are any better than I do. So keep on talking, because that’s about all your good at. So take your tarot cards, your prophecies, and your supernatural bull and get back to the circus where you belong because we don’t have enough room on this roster to entertain sideshow rejects. Zack stops pacing for a moment and breathes heavily. A few beads of sweat crawl down his face, his passion starting to wear him down. But he is the Ultimate Thrill Seeker. He has a much higher endurance level then this. Brushing the sweat away, he continues. Now, my partners on the other hand, they’re entirely different stories. First off, we got Derf, a man who at one time held this belt of mine. Now I’m not concerned about what he’ll do in this match at all. If anything, he’s the strong man on our team. He probably had the greatest victory of anyone at CG, even me. He overcame seven; count ‘em, SEVEN other guys to win Money In The Bank. Now that’s pretty damn impressive. So I’m gonna say that wrestling three guys this week, the over seven at CG, should prove to be a cakewalk for him. Especially considering that two out of those three came out of CG with their tails between their legs. And besides, he doesn’t need stupid nicknames, or a sob excuses, or fancy tricks. He’s just good because he’s good. He doesn’t need any gimmicks. He just needs to be himself. No strings attached. So I’d watch my back in this match if I were you GM. Now that you’re the World Champ, you got a target right on you’re back. And with that briefcase in hand, Derf’s the one holding the rifle. I don’t think you can handle that kinda pressure. Just one slip up, and BAM! We’ll be seeing a new World Champion. Because with that briefcase, I see Derf going to new heights in this business. So take what’s yours Derf. You deserve it much more than GM ever will. You’re proof that you don’t need to be a prick to get somewhere in this business, that there are still a few of us left out there who wrestler for pride, honor, and for the most important thing of all…the fans. Zack pauses a brief moment, gathering his thoughts before going on. And then there’s DC. Now, DC has had some troubles in the past, and he may be a bit of a loudmouth at times. But there’s a huge difference between him and our opponents. He has guts. Unlike the other team, DC has actually instead of just bitching and moaning, actually makes challenges to the whole roster. Not just one or two people, but the WHOLE frickin’ roster. Now that takes guts. And he isn’t the type to back out of a challenge once somebody steps up. He’ll actually get in that ring and kick your ass this way and that way. So yeah, he may be outspoken, but at least he isn’t all talk like the rest of you heels on the show. Sharpman tried to make his life a living hell, but he couldn’t keep him down. All he managed to do was throw fuel on an already blazing inferno. And in the end, Sharpman got his ass handed to him in Hell In A Cell. So if you’re the devil Sharpman, then I guess that makes him God right? I don’t know if DC will like having us as partners or not, and frankly I don’t care. All I know is that I’m glad I’m not the one facing him, and am going to enjoy watching him kick your ass again this week Sharpman. Zack believes that would enough of a promo to satisfy. He has blasted his opponents, build his partners, and everything you’d expect. So really, what more do you want? Stopping his pacing, Zack stands firm and proud, legs spread apart in front of the camera, wearing a confident smile that rather then say “I hope to win” says “I will win.” When you combine the brashness of DC, the attitude of Derf, my daredevil mentality and mix it with our collective wrestling skills, all it can equal is success. We’ll be soaring so high above the other guy’s heads they won’t be able to touch us. So before we close, I just have one more thing I wanna ask our opponents. A challenge if you will. Sharpman, K-Hard, GM… …how high can you fly? Zack takes one final, dramatic pose with the championship belt as the camera pulls farther and farther away, faster and faster. Zack’s form is quickly diminishing as the rocks around him get bigger. In the open sunlight, they appear to have a whitish gleam now that we see more of them. Suddenly, the face of the mountain takes on a defined shape. It’s odd though, because the carvings appear to be manmade. They are too finely cut to be creations of nature. It’s funny to call it the “face” of the mountain, because as we pull back far enough now that Zack is nothing more than a black speck on the horizon, we see carved into the mountainside exactly that…faces. Four faces of four of the greatest men in American history. And come this MNM, three of the greatest faces in MWE history will step into that ring. And marks my words, they will be going to war against the enemy.
