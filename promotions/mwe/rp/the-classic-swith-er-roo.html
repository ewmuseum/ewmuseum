The classic swith-er-roo

Jul 30 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A car pulls out of a dirt driveway and onto a four lane highway. Not many cars are inhabiting the roadway, but the driveway on the other hand is filled with about 7 to 10 vehicles. One being the all too familiar black and white Harley Davidson rode by Blight. At the end of the driveway is a small set of steps that lead into Daisey's Roadside Diner. Inside is a bit of a rundown old diner with 1970's retro wallpaper and the classic diner counter with stools. On the right side of the diner sits Blight inside a booth all by himself reading a menu and sipping a cup of coffee. The waitress who has just got done bringing several plates of food to the family in the booth in front of him walks over with her pad and pen ready. The middle aged grumpy waitress stands with one hand on her hip looking at Blight. Waitress: So what will it be slim? Blight: (just gazing over his menu at the waitress) I'll take the buttermilk pancakes with bacon and homefries...extra butter and a refill on the coffee too. Waitress: Yes your highness, anything else your kingship? ( as she waves her hand in a bowing motion) Blight just puts down the menu and glares at her angrily due to her sarcasm. The waitress picks up the menu gives a litle snarl as she grabs it off the table and rushes away. Blight sits in the booth listening to dishes clank around as the bus boy cleans up, he listens to the discussions of the different patrons around him complaining about the rude waitress and how difficult their lives are. Blight snorts in disgust at hearing the same conversations but into different words by everyone around him reaches into his pocket and pulls out a quarter and slips it into the slot of the mini wall jukebox. He quickly scrolls through the selection before finding Hotel California by The Eagles. The song slowly starts to play as the volume raises just a bit so Blight can hear it in his booth. He sits back and relaxes a bit. The waitress walks over and puts his food down infront of him and pours another cup of coffee before walking away. Blight picks up his coffee and brings it to his lips before his cell phone starts to ring. Blight: Hello? FreakShow: Dude did you hear? Blight: Huh? Wait who is this? FreakShow: Its FreakShow man stop playing games, did you hear what happened? Blight: What the fuck are you talking about? An older gentleman is walking past Blight when he hears him curse and stares at him. Blight turns and stares at him angrily the man quickly walks away but keeps turning back to look at Blight. FreakShow: Yo your there bro? Hello? [center/Blight: Yeah sorry so what happened now? Is everyone ok? FreakShow: Well everyone here is ok but your tag partner for this week isnt. BLIGHT: What do you mean he isn't? Are you drunk? FreakShow: Dude take your Blackberry and go to MWE.com and check it out, then call me back. FreakShow quickly hangs up the phone. Blight looks at the phone and shrugs it off and begins to eat. More than halfway through his meal and a variety of songs played by other patrons later Blight sits back to think for a moment. He picks up his Blackberry and goes to MWE.com where he finally figures out what FreakShow was talking about. QUOTE Devlin Sharpe was attacked in a parking lot this morning as he was entering his vehicle, Devlin is suffering from 4 broken ribs, 2 broken arms and 2 broken legs, Devlin Sharpe's Girlfriend Julia Hudson has signed his resignation notice on his behalf citing the fact that Devlin Sharpe has suffered a life and career threatening injury due to his involvement in MWE, Devlin Sharpe's assailant was identified to be the London-Born David Renshaw, MWE Owner Richard Covell and MWE General Manager Eli Silver had scheduled a meeting with him to discuss the situation and in a shocking move, Richard Covell and Eli Silver have announced that after a very interesting discussion, that they have decided to hire David Renshaw, MWE welcomes its new acquisition and wish him success in his career, no doubt with the impact he has already caused, rumors are flying rampant [/color] Blight reads the article over and over for a few minutes. He puts his phone down and just shakes his head, he begins to eat again when the phone rings. Blight: Yeah. FreakShow: Is that all you have to say is yeah? What about the match you will have to face the two of them by yourself. I mean its Peter K, and Anthony Clark. Dude Eli gave the rest of us the week off and its too late for us to get a flight to the arena. Blight: Don't worry about it, it isn't gonna be a big deal. You and the other three just chill out at home have fun. Take Jezibel to a movie and dinner or something. Go meditate with Martyr and just keep on eye on my little brother Talon. I'll handle business over here. FreakShow: Ok did Cruz's Flying Knee knock your brains loose last week? Dude there is something wrong with you if you think you are going against both of them. Blight: Ok first off my head is still spinning from that damn knee, but eigther way Peter K. is my biggest problem. I don't have to worry about Clark. FreakShow: How in the hell do you figure that one...dude those two are no joke, listen management has given you the option of not going through with the match without it affecting your record so you can----- Blight: ( now yelling into the phone) Fuck that! If I got a match I got a match. I don't give a shit who I'm facing. Was it my fault Dev got his ass kicked and had to run home to mommy with his boo boos. Fuck no...is it my fault that I now have to go against two metrosexuals and just hope that they don't try to make a move on me in that ring...fuck no. So why would I not go through with it. Anthony Clark is nothing. He is a joke, he is a fluke. I have been on a downward spiral as of late but this dude has fallen straight through the toilet. See I get over looked because of my record. Meanwhile my first two matches were with Clark and I didn't lose eigther one of those matches. Jay Riddick took me out by chance the first time, and Clark pinned Sin the second time. So it is now my duty to expose him for the joke he is. FreakShow: What about Peter K. He kinda has been on a downward spiral as well...hold on Jez is showing me something...uh huh, really...thats odd, ok I will tell him. Dude it looks like you don't have to go into it alone after all the dude who took out Dev is your partner now! The other side of the phone is quiet until FreakShow hears Blight mutter "Fuck" before hangin up. Blight slams down his phone as every customer in the diner is looking at him he quickly gets up and throws his money down and storms out leaving the people in the diner confused as to what just happened.
