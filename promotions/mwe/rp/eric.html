Eric.

May 28 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
MWE cameras open up with a scene in Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic. The camera highlights the ghetto areas of the capital, where kids use sewed cloth as clothes for their feeble little bodies, that being the only thing protecting them from the sun and its vicious rays. Their dilated eyes stare at the camera in pure hunger, wanting food desperately. There is a general sadness in the area, which makes the cameraman question that, if we go hungry in our own country, imagine what things are like here. With the scenes of poverty, abortion and sadness fresh in mind, a silver Mercedes-Benz is seen driving right past the area, revving its engine. One thinks, just who the hell could drive a fancy car right in front of a bunch of ghetto children? Brian Michaels. The children agonizingly astonished at the fancy car, which is now stopped at a red light, and see that it is one that they only see on their black and white television screens. The adults, who walk the streets with teeth needing replacements, plead for anything from the drivers to feed their children. An elderly lady, her face seemingly deformed, walks with her two bare feet and an evening gown towards the window of the car driven by Brian Michaels. She knocks on it repeatedly, only for Michaels to continue looking forward. The light pauses at red, almost like a sign from God that Michaels should help this young woman out. Instead, he turns to his left slowly, Terminator-style, and raises his middle finger with dead seriousness. The elder woman sobs, and puts her head in her hands, walking away with shock. Her fragile bones shake with each step she takes, and walks back to her family with her head still down, who are cursing Michaels out in Spanish. Michaels throws his hands in the air before revving the engine and driving off to his house, which is near the market. A black screen overtakes the picture, and moments later, Michaels is seen in the living room of his brand new house in the Dominican Republic. He has French toast halfway in his mouth, and is blasting “Way I Am” by Eminem on his Surround Sound speakers. He is wearing his pajamas, and is walking around in his black socks. His waiter Hector, of Dominican descent, walks towards Michaels with a note and a club sandwich on a plate. Michaels stares at the letter with curiousity, wondering just who the hell knows his address. He licks his lips, puts the sandwich aside, and opens the letter. Quote: May 9th, 2009 Dear Brian or Mr.Michaels, or however you’d like to be called. I just wanted to let you know that I’m was your biggest fan. Michaels pauses for a second, not really wanting to read anymore. He rolls his eyes, before reading further again. Quote: I’ve watched every single one of your promos. From your Crowning Glory I promo to ‘I got a big ego’, I have them all archived in my video library. I posted all your matches on YouTube so I could show all my friends who the best wrestler in the world was. Heck, I even have that shit you pulled outta your ass in WZCW. I hate those guys, don’t you? I really would like an autograph, Brian. I know you probably get tons of these letters every day, but please, please, my little sister loves you and everything that you do for MWE. I know that you’re a bad guy now, and that’s your job, but I will always be a Brian Michaels fan. By the way, I’m 17 years old, and I’ve been watching MWE since it started. I even started my freshman year in high school wearing a Brian Michaels backpack. I got called gay and a lot of other stuff, but I didn’t really care. I just wanted to talk to you. Man, you’re my inspiration and motivation. I don’t think I could go a day without a Brian Michaels promo. You’re the best dude, I really hope you kick Derf’s ass for all your loyal fans. When you went emotional, I did too. I stopped talking to all my friends and it became me against the world, just like you Brian. Now you have an ego, so do I. My girlfriend dumped me because I told her to shut the fuck up, just like you said. Life’s a bitch, who needs more of them, right Brian? Anyway, I sent you a mug that I bought from MWE.com a long time ago, and it’d be awesome if you could sign it. I’m seriously starstruck right now…please, please sign it and reply back. - Your biggest fan, Eric Michaels was fucking creeped out, and paid no attention to the obvious emotion and effort that Eric put into the letter. Michaels: The fuck? How does this guy know my address? Hector: To tell you the truth sir, it was pretty sad. It seems that he is a very big fan of you and- Michaels: No one asked for your opinion. Are there more of these letters? Hector gives him no response, instead walking towards the large kitchen and grabbing a stack of letters. He reads through them quickly and picks out the ones from Eric, hands those to Michaels, and throws the others aside. Along with the letters are a pair of “Michaels 1’s” which are the very first version of Nike shoes custom-made for Brian. There is also a ticket stub, but the camera cannot make out what is on the stub. There is also a large poster with the words “ADVERSITY” on it. Michaels: Hey, you’re a waiter. Pick that shit up. Hector tends to the rest of the mail, as Michaels picks the letter that was sent directly after the first. Quote: May 11th, 2009 Hey again. It’s Eric. I don’t know if you got my last letter, but it’s felt like forever since I sent it. I just wanted to know what’s going on with ya right now. I’m at the hospital, bored out of my mind and watching some game tape on Derf for you. From what I’ve seen, he taps out real easily, and got pretty lucky in beating D.C. a while back. I honestly think you can take him, no problem. Still though, I never got that signed mug. I guess it’s okay if you don’t reply to my letters, I just hope that you read them, and know that even though everyone said you were too emotional, or that you used politics, I was always with you. From day one, I told everyone you’d be a World Champ and you totally proved me right! From Amber Von Dresch to Christina, all women are bitches who end up cheating on you. That’s what you’re all about, right man? Forget the rest of the roster, if it wasn’t for you, MWE wouldn’t be alive today. They got racial pricks like Cannon and religious zeagots like Sharpman (I LEARNED A NEW WORD!) Not to mention, I saw what you did to Markowitz and Triple G, HOLY SHIT BRO! I totally marked out for that, it’s about time someone put them in their place. I’m really liking the old-school Brian Michaels, dude. I hope you continue, keep up the good work, and please reply to this, I really want to hear from you. Also, I got a ticket stub from Wrestle-Wars II. I live in Milwaukee, so it was a huge deal to be there. I got seated in general admission but I fought my way to the front row just to see you fight D.C. (YOU SHOULD’VE WON!) Security kicked me out and I ended up sitting the last few matches outside in the rain. I waited for you until like 3 AM but they said you went back to your hotel room. Oh well, I just want you to sign the ticket and know that I’m still your biggest fan. I’m so greedy. - Your biggest fan, Eric D. For a second, he looks at the word ‘hospital’ in particular. He wonders what he’s in the hospital for. His eyes then blow up in amazement, as he sees another two letters on the counter. He takes a step back before opening the next letter. Quote: May 13th, 2009 Hey Brian, Are you gonna talk to me? I can understand if you’re busy and stuff, but the least you could do is at least send me a basic mail. You know, like one of those that the President sends out?? I’m sorry if I’m getting a little overanxious, but it just feels like you’re starting to ignore your true fans. I’m not a bandwagoner, I’ve been faithful since day one, from the WZCW days to the emotional stages, I’ve been right here rooting for you. I even flew out to London to the SAME wall you were at and found the Adversity poster that you used in your Adversity promo. Can you sign that too? I’m sorry again, I keep asking for too much. But really, am I? What does it take to get a response out of you, Brian? I just want to be friends, man. I just want to be friends. I do want you to read this, because I’m starting to think you’re ignoring me, and if you are…I don’t know what I’d do… - Your biggest fan, Eric De The last letter puzzles Michaels, who doesn’t know if he is too late to respond or even if he should respond. He is in a dilemma, and doesn’t understand whether to stay true to his gimmick and ignore this overly-obsessed teenager, or if he should talk to him, maybe get to know him? Michaels: What the hell is wrong with this kid… Finally, he reaches for the last letter. He looks at the date of it, before opening it. Quote: May 24th, 2009 Hey Brian, It’s midnight and I have to go down to the hospital again tomorrow, but I really wanted to let you know something important: I finally uploaded the Brian Michaels vs Mason Resoan match from earlier! I know you’re probably exhausted, or you’re at a club, so it’s okay if you don’t respond to this one, but YouTube was being a total bitch and MWE kept taking it off. But you know me by now, I was hell-bent on getting that thing up, and I finally made a new account and got it up. I’d love to walk in your shoes for day, too. I’d love to see what wrestlers do every day (work out I bet!) and just be Brian Michaels. But I know that couldn't happen. I had a bad day today. The nurses really roughed me up for bringing the “See You In My Nightmares” promo to the hospital. They told me to bring in some entertainment for myself, and that's what I brought. They turned it after the third fuck, haha. I thought I’d let you know that I spent about five hours in line at Foot Action to get your "Michaels’ 1’s" on opening day. It was packed and it was raining again, but I got it. I was so happy, I wore them at my house the next day, but someone stole them during my stay. I’m so sorry that it happened, Brian. It shouldn’t have, I completely blame myself for it, and it’ll never happen again. Those shoes cost a lot and my parents were pretty mad, but I got some money from my dad and made enough to buy a new pair! I sent them to you in the package, it’d be great if you could write “From Brian, to Eric”. I’d literally jizz my pants, haha. Hope you get this one, it’s been like two weeks since I sent the first letter. Just remember that, like you, I use my arrogance as the steam to power my dreams. Rated-X forever, and remember to kick Derf’s ass for me! - Your biggest fan, Eric Der Michaels: That’s it….I’m writing this sonova bitch back… Leaning over the counter, he finds a notepad and pen next to each other. He reaches for them and walks over to the table in the living room. He puts the pencil on his ear and ponders the situation as he stares directly at the blank notepad. Michaels: Eric Derf, did you see how that kid was all over me? He PRAISES the ground I walk on..why can’t you be more like him? He shakes his head, but keeps his eyes focused on the notepad. Michaels: I mean, shit…I know people love me, but that kid was straight up, OBSESSED with me! The focus on the notepad is gone now, as Michaels takes a second to lean back and relax on his couch, stretching his muscular arms out. Michaels: Damn, Derf, don’t you know who I am? I ask that, because I know exactly who you are… Michaels: You’re that guy who tells all MWE fans to believe in their flyness, and to conquer their shyness He smirks at the thought of a wrestler telling fans to believe. He knows that wrestlers themselves couldn’t believe if there was no back-biting backstage. Michaels: Here’s MY saying…if everyone thinks it’s right…you’re doing something wrong. Michaels: Derf, I know you got people backing you up big-time in this match, telling you that you CAN win. They’re telling you that you CAN do it. They’re on YOUR bandwagon, kid. He compares Derf to a young D.C., Sinc Mercier or even a current Kevin Hardaway. He is one of those young “babyfaces” who all the fans just love and can relate to. Michaels HATES that kind of guy. Michaels: Get used to getting used by all these “fans”…Hell, it’s NECESSARY that they use you…because if they can’t use you, you’re useless…get it? Michaels: In all my years of wrestling, there’s ONE thing I’ve learned…it’s to not let people think that you owe ‘em something. Why’s that? Because then you’re in a weak spot. Michaels reminisces to when he was in the same position Derf was in; upcoming guy who thought he owed the fans something. He was wrong. Michaels: Derf…THE DIFFERENCE, BETWEEN THE TWO OF US, IS THAT I’D RATHER BE HATED FOR WHO I AM, THAN LOVED FOR WHO I’M NOT… Michaels: And I am NOT…a PUSSY who drinks for fun and wants to laugh with all his fans…trying to live the “good life” with my friends from high school. Know what I say to my high school friends? FUCK EM! Each, and every ONE of them… The camera can feel Michaels’ passion for winning shining through. He truly, does not give a hoot, about all those in this past. Michaels: So go…talk. It’s your time. Keep in mind…I’m not D.C. I’m not gonna go out there Monday…and let you walk all over me for the “future” of MWE. Michaels: FUCK THE FUTURE, I, LIVE, NOW! Who’s better…right now…Brian Michaels or Eric Derf…? He asks this with a serious tone, his eyes giving the camera, and seemingly Eric Derf and his fans the question. Michaels: ANSWER ME, ANSWER ME, ANSWER ME… Michaels: I want every single MWE fan…to answer that question, in their minds…because that little thought in the back of your head…telling you that Derf can beat Michaels… Michaels: …is just a thought. That’s all it’ll ever be…see, ‘cause while D.C. is great and all…he’s not the greatest superstar in MWE history. Get this…because I ain’t talkin’ outta my ass…I am the greatest in MWE history. Hundreds of wrestlers, have WALKED, THAT, AISLE…and none…can say they’re better than me. Not David Whitman…not D.C…not Alejandro Rodriguez…and sure as FUCK…not Eric Derf. Michaels says this last sentence with a bit of a Ric Flair accent, half-yelling at this point. Michaels: You will not beat Brian Michaels. I’m giving you credit. You’ve got an X-Television Championship, and more people are on your dick than Hugh Hefner… Michaels: As for anyone who says you’re SUPPOSED to beat me, because you beat D.C…why don’t we look at your past? He leans back and chuckles, wanting to remind all the “believers” of all the times that Eric Derf has come up short. Michaels: I mean, shit…weren’t you supposed to beat Alex Bowen? You failed at that. Michaels: Shit…weren’t you supposed to beat Nic Kraven? You failed at that, too. Michaels: So why the fuck, do you think you’re going to beat me? Once again, the question is thrown right at Eric Derf. It is apparent that he is speaking to Derf and Derf only. Michaels: I JUST PUT YOU IN A FUCKING COMA, and you’re gonna come at me with the bullshit inspirational “I’m going to kick your butt!” story? Go get a movie made out of your comeback…but cut out the final scene…’cause, there won’t be no happy ending… Michaels: But, Derf, you want to be a Champion, don't you... The word champion can mean many things. One might think that a champion may settle for a moral victory, but not Michaels. Not Michaels, indeed. Michaels: YOU WANT TO BE A CHAMPION?! I will FUCKING SHOW YOU, WHAT IT TAKES…to be the Champion. Derf…look at the MWE Roster. There are Eric Derf’s…everywhere. You, can be replaced…MWE has Whitman…MWE has Sinc….MWE has Hardaway. Where are you on the totem pole? You’re on my balls…that’s where you are. Michaels: So if you can be replaced, what does that make me? I’m a fossil fuel, motherfucker. I’m NON-RENEWABLE, I am ONE, OF, A, KIND…you will never see another Brian Michaels on God’s green earth… Michaels: Facts, are facts. I have been here before…you haven’t. You have potential. I have experience. Who wins out? Michaels looks down, and that sentence triggers an idea in his mind. He puts the pen to the notepad and begins scribbling down the letter. Michaels: You wanna talk shit, but you have YET to back it up in the ring... Michaels: But come Monday…all that shit’ll be thrown out the window...because at that point…it’ll be put up or shut up, for you… Monday night is the moment of truth. As Michaels continues to write his letter to Eric, he realizes the magnitude of this match. He knows that he is the favorite, and that he cannot allow himself to lose this match. Michaels: Damnit Derf…you’re just about as unreliable as the economy. You lose to Alex Bowen, beat D.C., then lose to Nic Kraven. Michaels: I mean, if everyone wants to label you, the next top guy…then fucking prove it to me. It is a challenge, and fuck the world if Derf doesn’t have the balls to respond. Michaels is looking for more blood than George W Bush at this point. That is, if blood meant oil. Michaels: Make this bigger than the MWE Title match, make this bigger than life…and most of all, try and make this bigger than my dick…which is just about impossible. Was that too insensitive for your fans, Eric? Bite me. Michaels: I’ve wrestled in the Dominican Republic. They treat wrestlers they love like Gods, and the ones they hate like shit…straight up. They’ve had riots and people have brought guns to their arenas…looking to shoot down the motherfucker who beat David Whitman that one time… He says one time, but puts three fingers up as If to signify that he has beaten Whitman three times, whereas Derf doesn’t even have the respect of the MWE brass to go against Whitman. Michaels: They would fuckin’ stone me there, and they’d compare you to Jack Veneno…greatest Dominican wrestler of all time… Michaels: Don’t hate me, because I’m a prick…don’t hate me, because I don’t sing praises, and because I have the BALLS to stand up and SPEAK MY MIND, in a society where people are dying to tell you “no, it’s wrong”. Michaels: In a society dying to tell Brian Michaels that I should go away because MWE won’t play my promos on TV more than ONCE... He looks down at the paper and is seemingly content with it, putting the finishing touches on it. He rears his head back up, looking directly at the camera. Michaels: I was sick of being like you, Derf. I was sick of telling stories and making everyone smile, because it made me fucking SICK to my stomach. Michaels: I was sick, of all the bullshit hype over the “good guys”…this is MY TIME. This is THE BEST OF BRIAN MICHAELS, AND YOU ARE GOING TO GET EVERY OUNCE OF ME, UNTIL I CAN’T FUCKING BREATHE… He grabs his throat in a violent way, frightening Hector, who is watching from a distance. He is about to walk over to him, but decides against it, not wanting to disturb his boss. Michaels: Come Monday…I’m going to take your little head…and I’m going to beat it…to a pulp. I will win on Monday night. Anything less…is a failure. Period. Michaels: I have, just one question…and it’s the same one I asked Devlin Sharpe, all too long ago...HAVE YOU TAKEN YOUR FUCKING PILLS TODAY?! He reaches over his maroon couch to the desk next to it and takes a few Valiums and throws them at the camera, telling Derf to “take them”. Michaels: If not…go get your binoculars, and meet Mr.Popular…I shit better wrestlers than you Derf. Michaels: Because Derf… just like D.C…this is the new See You In My Nightmares… Michaels: This time though…there is no one to make my jaw drop… Michaels: I’ll be in your nightmares for the rest, of your life...bank on it. With that, Michaels is done scribbling on the notepad. He looks at it, knowing that a fan named Eric in Milwaukee would be willing to kill whoever to get his hands on it. He walks over to the kitchen and reaches over the large brown dinner table, and grabs an envelope. He rips out the paper and is about to put it in the envelope, but takes one long look at it once more. Quote: Dear Eric, What can I say man, I’m humbled. It’s great that you think that of me, and I’m your idol, but get this kid, I’m a lot of people’s fucking idol. Yeah, I’m being completely serious right now. You’re lucky I’m writing to you right now, too. I was friggin’ bored since every time I go out, people want money. It’s their fucking fault they’re poor you know? Who said I have to give them money? Fuck giving a hand to ‘em, they put themselves there, not me. Anyway, enough about me… Well…nah. You bet your ass I’m going to annihilate Derf. Know what, I’m bringing you out to here to DR. You deserve it, son. I’m sending the MWE private Jet to your address, and you’re going to be in the front row at the show. I’ll also sign the stuff you gave me when you get here. - Peace, Brian Michaels Hector, meanwhile, was glancing over Michaels’ shoulder to view the letter, and is shocked that Michaels would propose such a thing. Hector: Are you really gonna take this kid to the show? He looks at Hector as if he were on crack, and bursts out laughing. He almost drops the letter, and has to hold onto the counter to keep from falling. Michaels: Fuck that! You seriously think that I’ma take time off from my LIFE, to give this kid what he wants? No, fuck him. I’m leaving his ass at the airport, and he can find another dick to suck there. Hector scratches his head for a second, not believing that Michaels would stoop this low. He takes the letter remorsefully, and walks outside, driving off to the post office. Michaels shakes his head, still laughing at the suggestion of letting a kid walk around with him. ::: SEVEN DAYS LATER ::: MWE cameras focus on Hector, who is wearing his traditional butler suit, with black bow-tie, slacks and all. His face is disconcertingly mournful as he walks to the living room, where Michaels’ body is sprawled out on the couch, watching a DVR’ed episode of Scrubs. Hector tightens his lips and looks down at the ground, not wanting to make eye contact with him. Hector: Brian… Michaels looks up at Hector with a puzzled face. Hector knows that Michaels wants to know what’s up, but instead… Michaels: It’s Mr.Michaels to you, I TOLD YOU ALREADY! Hector takes a step back, not anticipating that kind of reaction yet. Hector: Um...sir, Eric is dead. Immediately, Michaels felt a lump in his throat. You know that lump you get when you’re like friggin’ shocked? Michaels: What are you talking about, how? How…did it happen? Hector bows his head, not knowing how to break it exactly to Michaels. He ponders it for a second, before just diving right into it. Hector: Well, since you never went to go pick him up at the airport…well…it turns out, he had cancer and… Hector: He was one of the Make-A-Wish Foundation children, and it was his last day to live. Dagger. Michaels leans back, putting his hands on his face and rubbing his eyes in disbelief.He simply can’t believe it. He’s dead. The thought repeats in his mind dozens of times, and he continues to shake his head, not wanting to believe it. Hector: Also, the funeral will be downtown at Los Tres Ojos (The Three Eyes). I’d advise you not to… But in a flash, Michaels rises from his position on the couch and goes out the door into his car, almost in a dead-sprint to get to the funeral in time. ::: 45 MINUTES LATER ::: Michaels’ car stops at the church, and he begins running again. Still in a dead sprint, he plasters through the large and bright church doors, almost knocking them down in the process. The large get-together of family and friends all turn their heads to look at Michaels, who begins coming down the red-carpet aisle, down to the casket. He is intimidated, a feeling he’s not used to having. He grabs a microphone at the front of the church, standing right before the portrait of Jesus and begins his very own eulogy. I hate the world. I hate people in the world, everywhere…but this kid…this kid had something, that reminded me, of me. I think that’s why, I have to live…with the guilt…of knowing, that I killed someone…who would’ve killed, to see me live… The bottomline is, it’s my fault Eric DeRosa isn’t here. Ya know, Eric’s parents told me I shouldn’t have come here… They told me, I should keep my mouth shut… It’s funny…because Eric and I always complained about people who tell us to be quiet, and to shut up… And we would just flip them off, and ride off in the sunset, not giving a damn who cared. Eric was a loner. His “friends” always picked on him for being a wrestling fan. But he told them to screw off…that it was real… He, in his heart…believed, that I could win every match. He flew hundreds of miles, to watch D.C. vs Michaels all three times…because it was his dream match. In one of his last days, on Earth…he uploaded Resoan vs Michaels within minutes…because he wanted to be the first to upload it. Eric made me fan pages…and even pretended to be me sometimes…because he knew he’d never be a wrestler. It’s weird, that in a country where gays are still scrutinized…where whites threaten to shoot a president, because he’s black… In a promotion, where Ace Cannon mocks Hispanics…where Scavenger Hunts happen… That one kid…told the World…to F off…because he loved wrestling, and no one could take that from him. He sent me letters…asking me, to not throw his name out in a promo…he just wanted to be acknowledged… The kid stood in line for hours, waiting to get the new Brian Michaels sneakers, because he would be walking in my shoes for once… Maybe it’s true…maybe everyone goes away in the end…but I know this… Eric DeRosa, you are a greater man than I could ever be. For that…I grant you, your last wish. Your last wish…for me to kick Eric Derf’s ass…on Monday night. Know this…that without a shadow of a doubt…I will… Win. The promo is about to end, but… Michaels: HA! ALL YOU MOTHERFUCKERS THOUGHT I GAVE A SHIT?! AH-AH,WRONG-O! Michaels: COMING SOON TO A THEATRE NEAR YOU, STARRING ERIC DEROSA…RANDY COVELL! At the very second Michaels names off Covell, the camera pans to the black, beat-down casket, and up rises Randy Covell, Undertaker-style. He flips off the camera with both hands before hopping out of the casket and breaking down in laughter. Michaels: This…was meant to show everyone…how FUCKING gullible, emotion really is…this was to show everyone…how PATHETIC, you all are…every single one of you, BELIEVED that Eric was going to meet me…hell, you believed he was a real person…but he’s not… Michaels: THERE IS NO ERIC FUCKING DEROSA. I made ALL this up...so I could show you a lesson. The lesson being...that these, fans...are fickle. These FANS...that CLAIM to love you...will drop you, at the sight of a PENNY on the ground... One by one, the people in black who were there to pray for “Eric” begin to laugh out loud alongside Randy Covell. Michaels: This was all a ruse…and ALL of you fell for it…and that’s why you love Eric Derf. Michaels: HE TELLS YOU…what you want…to hear...I don’t. The sinister grin comes across Michaels’ face, spread from ear-to-ear. He throws a middle finger up at the camera, and laughs. Michaels: MWE…go fuck yourselves… Michaels: Derf…while what I just did was fake…what I said…about winning…was real. Mark my words…I will do what I live for…I will... He makes the same determined face he gave during See You In My Nightmares, and snarls at the camera. Michaels: Win.
