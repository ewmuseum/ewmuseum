Exclusive Interview ; A Grand Mystique RP

Dec 11 2008

The Grand Mystique

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Grand Mystique walks away. He feels satisfied with how well his practise promo went down in front of the ring crew. They were certainly enthusiastic, though GM wonders if that had something to do with how his audience had been treated by Joey Dallas and Ace Cannon in the past. It was a well known joke backstage that Ace Cannon shares none of his father’s qualities when it comes to relating with his fellow workers. His tendency to rub his million dollar lifestyle in the face of everyone he comes into contact with is not an attractive personality trait and it tends to rub most up the wrong way. Maybe GM had chosen the wrong target audience as a sounding board. GM: (internal thought) “That lot find DC funny; of course they’re going to love me ripping on Cannon and Dallas.” He rubbishes that self doubt from his mind quickly, convinced that the promo will come good. As he continues walking, he meets a pretty blond in the room where the MWE’s media facilities had been set up. For the first time, GM meets Sara Patterson. GM:”Miss Paterson.....your legend and your beauty precedes....It’s an honour to meet you at last.” He offers her hand. Sara: “Mr Mystique, the pleasure is all mine.” GM: (internal thought)“You ......No I can’t even think it.” Sara: “How come you are here so late?” GM: “I’m afraid that I’m a workaholic. They share a laugh. “I was just jibbing with the ring crew, had a sudden urge to practice my upcoming promo. ” “Was it well received?” “Yeah they seemed to like it.” “Wait, so you know who you’re facing at Genesis?” “Miss Paterson, your nose for an exclusive is impeccable.” She blushes. “Can I at least offer to take you somewhere more comfortable to conduct this?” "Sure, let me get my things. OK to take a dictaphone?” “Perfect.” *One hour later* GM and Sara are sat in an Italian diner. It is intimately lit and they are in a booth by the window. Between them are two empty plates that look like they had some sort of a red sauce on them as well as a couple of extra empty plates between them. Sara’s dictaphone is stood upright on the table but is not turned on. They are both relaxed. “So you make some very interesting comments. It will make a great read.” “I’m sure it will.” “But are you sure that you want to stand by what you say.” “I’m a man of my word Sara.” *One week later* The newest edition of the MWE edition has come out. An interview written by Sara Patterson featuring the Grand Mystique is the cover feature. QUOTE Exclusive: Interview with Tag Champ, the Grand Mystique. An incomparable enigma, the Grand Mystique has been a revelation in the MWE. His talents, famously precise and well rounded are unique on the roster and he has added to remarkable title record by winning the Tag Team titles on his in ring debut with the King of the Universe, Maxx. In this intimate interview with Sara Patterson, The Grand Mystique gives us a frank insight to his world as well as revealing the amazing match for Genesis for the tag champs – an encounter which has all the potential to be a classic. The Grand Mystique seems to have it all – youth, charisma, skill plus the gold on his shoulders to back it up. This young star is the consummate professional in and out of the ring and his gracious approach has reaped much success in every company that he has been in. But who are the next opponents for GM and his tag team partner, Maxximillion Mustafa? Confirmed by the office of Dick Malibu, we can exclusively reveal that Genesis will play host to a non title tag team showcase featuring the champions against…..Joey Dallas and Ace Cannon! GM seemed to be looking forward to the encounter. “I came to the MWE to face this kind of talent. Cannon and Dallas are in the upper echelons of the company. This sort of match could main event Adrenaline Rush, instead of just playing warm up to it. It’s impossible not get excited about it.” Despite the less than polite comments emerging from the Cannon camp about Maxx, GM was almost entirely complimentary about their opponents, who fight on pay-per-view at Adrenaline Rush in a Lights Out match. “Their match could be one for the ages. Both men can go at it 110%, to leave everything hanging in the ring because they have the skill and desire to reach their goals, by any means necessary.” GM continued enthusiastically. “It could be argued that their accomplishments don’t match their skill level. That comes down to a lot of factors – luck and destiny certainly play their part. On more than one occasion these men have fallen short, by fair means or foul.” “But to their credit, they are two men who have their egos bruised, yet are strong enough to come back stronger still. That alone makes them dangerous to each other as well as Maxx and I.” When GM was asked about Maxx, he spoke with similar fervor. “Maxx is determined to see us walk out of Adrenaline Rush with our unbeaten streak intact. That means doing whatever is necessary to flatten whatever is put in front of us, whether it is a non title contest or with gold on the line.” But then something changed, in his tone. Having spent a great deal of time complimenting his next opponents, GM let rip with some damning criticisms. These are comments that will surely sting Cannon and Dallas and get them buzzing about getting their hands on GM. “Cannon and Dallas are more similar than they will probably ever appreciate. They are constant underachievers who have never stayed around long enough to see if their efforts will come to fruition in the long run. They expect instant results but lack the bottle and guts to put in the effort beyond the short term.” GM was not finished. In fact, it was far from it. “I firmly believe Ace and Joey need a serious look at themselves if they are to seriously challenge for the MWE title in the future. I’m talking about a reality check because the whole locker room sees these as a joke. They are still a poor man’s’ version of the men whose shadows they have been desperate to escape from. A complete failure to unleash their potential.” “And there are plenty who wanted to see them become the superstars that they knew they could be. But Ace Cannon will always be known as the guy that has been the face of Gucci, dangling a napkin in front of his package and stuffing socks down his pants to make up for it. Cannon is the guy who hangs around on street corners in Beverley Hills. “ Past “He can shout as much he likes about what happened in EW but he knows the truth. He knows that he was far from the best there at any point. And he knew that I was more than just an ordinary mid card guy and he daren’t continue to ignore me. He knew that he got an EW title reign out of sympathy, because his daddy pulled some strings.” Limelight “But Ace is annoyed and frustrated because ALL of his achievements in wrestling are overshadowed by his most famous role as the bastard brother of MWE legend Briand Michaels!” “And Dallas has not fared any better. This is a guy who followed others like a meek little puppy for his whole career; he came back from the dead and carried on where here left off in Rev Pro, only for that bullshit to fail miserably as well!” “But on his return to MWE, he suddenly becomes the Salvation? That makes me sick. I think he spent too much time in the shadow of that redneck bitch Windy Bridges.” “I refuse to be in ANYONE’S shadow.” And there may not be anybody better positioned than the Grand Mystique to talk about former MWE star Windy Bridges. These two had a bloody feud that spanned two federations and begun over a dispute involving the EW version of MWE's United States title. The Grand Mystique emerged the other side defeated but as an improved talent. GM places that feud as good as any he has had and crucial to his development, despite the outcome. "Windy Bridges took me to my limit and then way past it. Joey Dallas can only hope to get close to that level. He dreams of saving the MWE, on his way to winning the World title, are far-fetched." He also credits that feud for his continued success, including his great run in the MWE affiliate company, the UWA where he is a dominant member of the roster and Heavyweight champion. "Enigmar Wrestling allowed the Grand Mystique to become reborn. The UWA has benefitted and will continue to do so. But the MWE has yet to feel the impact that I will make in the future. Project Revolution and the Triple Threat will feel what I am capable of at Adrenaline Rush. But we don't think Ace Cannon and Joey Dallas need to be left out. Nobody needs to be left out" It is fair to say that emotions will be running high at Genesis. GM believes that Ace bringing up what happened in EW between himself and Maxx will only harm him and Dallas. "To say Maxx is fired up to get in the ring with Ace Cannon would be an understatement. He feels that he owes Ace a dedicated beating. Who am I to stop him? I can’t blame him." "But in all seriousness, Maxx still feels the downfall on EW more than anyone. I only wish that Ace felt a pang of guilt over the way that he took his ball and ran away, screaming like the immature little bitch that he is. “ GM is a man who speaks with total certainty and self belief. And whether it is about his career or someone else’s, he is someone who demands attention and commands respect. “The Grand Mystique refuses to be a stepping stone any more. I refuse to be the one who lies down with no complaints because some egos can’t handle it. I am the one who should be fighting for number one contendership. I should be the one main eventing pay-per-views, with titles on the line.” Champion “I am a champion. Ace, Joey and you’re not. I know this match is non title. I know it’s a showcase match to get ratings. And it will involve two men who have less than no respect for each other against two men who have done enough to lay a trail of success behind them.” “Joey and Ace can and inevitably will talk all the smack that they want to us but as individuals, we hold more success in our past than either of them. For THEM to be taken seriously by myself and Maxx, they need to begin to back up their verbals with gold around their waists.” “But don’t allow yourself to relax for one second in my presence because I will not. This match will showcase the Grand Mystique at his best – running rings around both of you simply because I can.” And what is next is store for the Grand Mystique? “The next two shows, Genesis and Adrenaline Rush will offer a glimpse of the future. Ace Cannon, Joey Dallas, Project Revolution and the Triple Threat will all begin to realise what I am truly capable of. They will see why Maxx called me to help him out.” “That is why, after everything that happened in EW, everything that SHOULD HAVE happened in Rev Pro, Maxx decided that the Grand Mystique could make his ambition become reality. Maxx knew that we could become Tag Team champions. Maxx knew that we could face people of main event calibre. But Maxx knew that he needed me to make it all become reality. But I am the one that has made it happen.” “Beyond Adrenaline Rush, everything that should have happened happens. Pain, regret, impact….It WILL ALL come to the fore!” The Grand Mystique is typically a man of few words. But he has shown this reporter all the sides of his personality. His quiet yet intense demeanour protects someone who strives for perfection, who drives for success every time he gets in the ring and even someone who will consider going to any means to get the job done. His recent successes in the MWE and UWA should be the first of many for this man, who many predict will be a future World Champion. But GM can pinpoint all of his successes, past present or future down to one thing. “When the Big Bang cometh, nothing will stop me. Nothing will prevent me from continuing to be the type of champion that that the MWE needs right now.”
