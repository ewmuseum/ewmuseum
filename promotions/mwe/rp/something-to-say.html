Something to say.; A JJ Executioner RP

Oct 3 2008

JJ Executioner

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Drip. Drip. Drip. Is the only thing that can be heard. Nothing can be seen. Silence. A faint draught is felt as a whistling gale blows by. Then more silence. A hood is removed. Still darkness. Then blinding light. There is massive glare as the view adapts to the surroundings. A rat crawls across from a ledge nearby and pokes its head into view before it is shaken off onto the floor, where it scurries away. There is no natural light, only bright, harsh artificial lighting providing by television studio-style lighting. It is impossible to look directly at them without flinching and shunning away. The room itself is not extraordinary in any way but wholly unwelcoming at the same time. Walled on four sides by large, rough stone bricks, there are very thin, narrow gaps in the wall. This allows for the wind to squeeze in from outside. The room is bitterly cold. The door is on the left and is a thick and heavy oak. There are two wooden chairs placed near the door. Behind the lights is a table with a blanket over the top. “Shawn!!” The camera judders with shock, before moving away to the left. It swings back around to reveal Shawn Spitzer, hung up like a joint of meat with his arms tied up above his head. He is gagged and appears to be unconscious. His suit is scuffed badly and absolutely filthy. Splash. A bucket of lukewarm water is dropped over him. Steam fills up the room briefly before it is sucked out of the room by the stinging freezer-like temperatures of the room. Needless to say this wakes up Spitzer in a hurry. He is soon shivering. Click. Spitzer crashes to the floor as a mechanism releases the grip on his hands. Instinctively he tries to get to his feet and removes his bounds, which is no more than thick rope. He does not see a large figure looming over him, having just moved one of the lights away. “Hunter and hunted, take cautious breath. One wrong move, badly timed step Will lead to painful death.....” “‘Neath shrouded head Lies heavy chopping block. Death Scythe raised Like cock’d pistol, Glock.” Shawn turns but freezes as he sees this dark shape looming over him, the source of the voice. SS: “You.....you’re....” A giant pair of hands grabs him by the torn collar of his suit jacket and drags him onto the one of the chairs. S: “I’m s-, s....-” *saliva flies everywhere from Spitzer’s mouth* “If you continue to do that, I will remove your tongue and saliva glands.” The shadow reaches over and forcefully shoves an operating mask over Shawn’s face. He then drags the chairs into the middle of the room before he moves the lights. The light becomes immediately bearable as the stands which hold them are adjusted. Spitzer rubs his eyes as everything becomes slightly clearer. The shadowy giant is revealed to be JJ Executioner. He stands in front of the lights, which profile his entire figure and makes him looking even bigger than his 377lbs, six foot eleven frame. He has a black jacket that is almost as longer as he is. Beyond that he is wearing his normal wrestling attire; thick steel toe capped boots, long black trousers and a grey vest top. JJ: “Shawn, you give interviewers a bad name. I brought you here to make you aware of your actions. And I-” SS: “Whaf haf i d-” JJ: “Interrupt me again and it will be the last thing that you do.” Shawn’s eyes widen with shock. JJ: “Despite your obvious inadequacies, I believe that you have interviewed every MWE superstar in the course of your MWE career....everyone except me. That was not right, so I have taken it on myself to .....ha ha ha....remedy the injustice. So if you think you think you can do this without showering me in the excrement of your mouth, I would like you to conduct an interview with me.” Spitzer looks aghast. JJ: “No conditions, no restrictions. Any question you want.” JJ pulls another chair so it is opposite Spitzer. SS: “How long have I been here?” Spitzer readjusts the mask so his voice is clearer. JJ sits down JJ: “five, ten days....I don’t know I lose track of time....”JJ smiles menacingly. “Seven days Shawn. The MWE are concerned for your well being. Covell has hired a bunch of rag tag personal investigators and everything. Someone must really like you. Maybe I should have taken his idiotic son and really given him something to worry about.” SS: “So what has happened in the MWE? I missed Mayhem haven’t I?” JJ ignores the question. JJ: “What nobody seems to understand is how serious I am about being in the MWE. Nobody is safe, everybody should be on standby and yet, I get laughed at, ignored and fed insignificant nobodies who are not even fit to be in the company.” JJ removes a card from an inside pocket and hands it to Spitzer. JJ: “This is the results of the latest Mayhem episode, the most recent MWE house show and the forthcoming Mayhem card. Happy?” Spitzer scans it. He considers mentioning the outcome of the Rated X match against Project Revolution or the forthcoming Pick Your Poison encounters, but chooses to overlook it in favour of his personal safety. SS: “So you beat James Sullivan, that is a great result. He is a real up and comer.” JJ: “Hardly. And you need to past tense when speaking of Sullivan. He was doomed when he came up against me. Mason Resoan wanted him out of the MWE and after I was done with him, he was no position to stop anyone, let alone an MWE legend like Resoan.” SS: “And Vega Knight? He got a shot at Sonny Williams, th-” JJ: “Don’t finish that sentence, lest you want to lose some teeth.” JJ pulls a pair of pliers out from his belt, just in case Shawn had any doubt that JJ was bluffing. “Vega Knight is many things but worthy of holding MWE is not a quality he possesses. He simply....how can I say it, pissed off the right people to get that title opportunity and he squandered it.” JJ calmly rubs his right fist into his left palm as Spitzer looks on concerned. “You see Shawn, the MWE is made for me. Legends have been created here. Lives have been torn apart, bodies broken and devastated beyond compare. I have come here to serve justice on those simply not worthy of being in this company. I alone am Judge and Jury on the talent who want to be worthy of a place in MWE history.” SS: “Erm, how are you planning on doing that?” JJ: “Making a name for myself Shawn. I plan on making an impact.” Spitzer looks at the card again. SS: “You have an X TV title shot against Aeron Beowulf and Vega Knight, that’s good right?” Shawn is showing more confidence, to challenge JJ. JJ: “Shawn, don’t talk down to me....I don’t need any motivation for this match......there are too many positives for me not to be ready for this.” JJ stands and walks over to the covered table. He whips off the sheet and throws it to the right. On the thick wooden table top is a range of metal objects which glint and catch the light. JJ begins to play with a couple of the objects as he talks. He examines a hammer and tests a small electric drill, pointed menacingly in Spitzer’s direction. JJ: “You see Shawn. I have been forced to show my hand, to demonstrate exactly how serious I am about being in the MWE and being in a match for the X TV title.” SS: “Erm, you must be happy to get another shot at Vega Knight, especially with some MWE gold on the line?” Shawn tries to be diplomatic but JJ does not take too kindly to Vega’s name being mentioned. JJ: “A title shot at any time is...satisfying. Like the feeling of a metal cleaver through fresh meat.” JJ sneers and Shawn instinctively recoils from. “I will drag Vega Knight and Aeron Beowulf down to the very depths of hell and while I am there, I will claim a spot in MWE history as the next MWE X TV champion.” “I don’t expect either Beowulf or Knight to pose a serious threat. Vega Knight has a World title shot but was more concerned about Sonny Williams’ record in another company to be a serious threat! In the end, Knight was simply unprepared. He daren’t make that mistake twice and be more focussed on being ready” “And Beowulf is a cartoon character who looks like he is coming out of the wrong end of a paint factory. He is not going to be at 100%, not by a long shot and I am not the kind of person that someone in his state will want to be the vicinity of.” “I am the only once truly focussed on this match. That is why I will be leaving the cesspit of the Mellon Arena in Pittsburgh as the true MWE X TV champion.” JJ now has a pair of gardening shears, which he is admiring. SS: “What do you mean, true champion?” Spitzer has a wary eye on each blade of the shears. JJ places down the shears and bends down. Placing both arms underneath the table, he moves something around and pulls out the MWE X TV championship belt. SS: “How did you get that? Beowulf has had that for weeks, he would not willingly let that go.” JJ: “Who said I do anything willingly? I took this in anticipation of being the one ending the reign of Aeron Beowulf. The man who beat J.K. Valentino to win this very belt. The man who beat Aiden Copane and DC in recent weeks. But Aeron, we both know that I have taken your very reason for being in the MWE. I have taken the very essence of your being, your soul. At Monday Night Mayhem, I will destroy your body.” Spitzer has become increasingly nervously as JJ speaks. He is shivering due to a combination of the cold and terrifying fear. JJ moves the belt so it sits proudly on his right shoulder. JJ: “You look scared Shawn. I see the fear in your eyes. I have observed a similar look in the eyes of Vega Knight and Aeron Beowulf. Take heed and be aware. Those two men who are so concerned with their own egos that they won’t notice the noose dropping before them and tightening around your necks. Which it will. It is simply....” A church bell tolls. It is so loud and resonant that it must be very close by. JJ: “...a matter of time before the Judge, Jury and Executioner becomes the next MWE X TV champion., Supernova and Aeron Beowulf will be found guilty and be sentenced to execution.” “And Shawn, it’s time to put you in prime position to witness the destruction that will be wrought.” And with that, JJ sprints up to Spitzer and knocks him out with a stiff strike to the side of the head. The camera cuts out instantly.
