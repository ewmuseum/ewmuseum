Times Have Changed

Oct 8 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Mason: I am back. Oh, yes I am back and better than before. Mayhem Wrestling Entertainment, all I have to say is it took you long enough to send out the invitation...either that or the postal system really sucks...either way I am back and already making an impact. *Mason is talking about his obliteration of James Sullivan last week on Monday Night Mayhem. He laughs at it a little bit and his golden watch jingles a little bit. He is wearing one of his old "Mason Resoan" T-Shirts and a pair of black jeans. Mason is traditionally in his Mansion, which now has experienced parties, death, sex, and drama. He is smiling as he stands walks around his living room.* Mason: Poor Sullivan, so stupid...it's actually kind of pathetic. It was honestly like beating up a little kid who was looking around for his glasses. Sullivan, I am glad that you quit, frankly it was only a matter of time. Losing four times in a row, pitiful. You do not deserve to be a superstar with four consecutive losses. Especially...especially when one is for a championship belt. Did you just not care? Were you just too tired of pleasing people you called "fans"? Bullshit, no excuses. Honestly, I am a little disappointed in this company. They hired you...but didn't hire me? How stupid can one group of people get. *Mason lets out a little chuckle and looks at the time on his watch. He looks at his cable box, and proceeds to fix the time on his watch, quickly.* Mason: Well, times have indeed changed, both on my watch and in Mayhem Wrestling Entertainment. Sonny Williams is the MWE World Heavyweight champion...Sinc Mercier is the United States Champion...and who in the hell...Aeron Beowulf...What the fuck. Wasn't Beowulf a movie or some shit? I sure you have endured enough of those jokes in one lifetime so I will save my breath. I have to say that going 6-0 to start off your career is impressive, but then you lose to Sinc Mercier...I mean come on. If you can't beat him, you better hope you never face me. Speaking of Sinc Mercier, how did you get to be the MWE United States champion? Oh wait, you beat that dumb bitch James Sullivan...that explains it. Well listen up, your title is in danger...seeing as it took MWE so long to send me the invite to come back, I would only guess they forgot who I am. So, they are probably going to put me against some chumps to start. Well that is ok, because I won't mind relieving you of the United States title, but I was definitely right about one thing. MWE is putting me against chumps to start. *Mason takes a breath and gives a small sigh. He paces back and forth a couple of times.* Mason: Brian Michaels...my old mentor...It will be fun showing everyone that the student is indeed better than the master. *Mason thinks for a second, and then something hits him.* Mason: Thinking about it, you were probably the worst teacher I have ever had. Honestly, you did not teach me anything. You were to busy with your little Rated X group to notice me, most of my training was done with Stephen Arthur...It's a shame that I had to put the hit on him. However, I do have my reasons. But you were honestly the worst teacher in the history of teachers. You taught me only one thing, and that was how to break people down and analyze them. I am happy to inform you, that since you taught me that little bit of information, I have since mastered the technique. It will be a pleasure using it on you. Actually, if I think about it, I have already shown you before. *Mason looks at the camera man, who seems a little puzzled, and Mason simply chuckles.* Mason: Of course an imbecile like yourself wouldn't understand. I mean, look at you, you push a camera around for a living. That is probably worse than pumping gas. At least you are making yourself useful though. Anyway, I will remind you...it looks like you need it. Before Mayhem Wrestling Entertainment went under, I destroyed, single-handedly the greatest faction MWE had witnessed. Rated X was unstoppable, they won titles, they broke rules, and most of all they had extreme talent. However, Brian Michaels, the leader, decided to take a more civilized side project in me. That was the first mistake. *Mason cracks his neck and pulls up a cushioned bar stool, with a leather seat. Mason sat down and relaxed a little as he continued on with his story.* Mason: You see, while training me, as little as Michael's did, he told me that he would always be there if I needed to learn something new outside our lessons. In total, I had two lessons, all focused on analyzing the opponent and the surroundings. Besides these two lessons, I got nothing else. I always asked and bugged when no one was around, yet Brian would still ignore me. So, the next time we scheduled a lesson, I never showed up. The day after Brian told me that there would be no more sessions, and I was fine with that. Yet, it lingered inside of me for a little bit as I proceeded through CX. Aiden Copane, a filthy man indeed, aligned himself with me as I carried our team slowly to the MWE Tag Team Championships. The whole time, I was practicing the technique of analyzing my surroundings. It helped me tremendously, and that is how when CX imploded, I walked out on top. After pursuing a tag team career, and abandoning my gold, I took a shot at the Mayhem Wrestling Entertainment X-Television Championship belt. However, there was a distraction in Greg Cherry. *Mason Resoan shakes his head a few times and sighs.* Mason: Yes, Greg Cherry was tough and he beat me for the title, but upon fighting him I realized that I had mastered the technique, the only technique Brian taught me, and was ready for bigger and better things. Specifically, the Mayhem Wrestling Entertainment World Heavyweight Championship. Aiden Copane, that filthy human, beat me to winning the gold, but it was a fluke. He was simply a transitional champion as I used by abilities and beat both him and Pala for the gold. It was a great day...and then Brian Michaels had to poke his head back into the picture. So, I did what I had to do...break him down and take him out...and that is exactly what I did. I used my surroundings against him. Step one was finding the ultimate partner, in D.C. who I convinced and persuaded to go against his leader. That step was easy, but to get him to agree to take my former mentor out...well that was a little hard. I had to show him I meant business, and that is why I took out Devlin Sharpe. Not only that, but it weakened Michael's spirit, and destroyed a third of Rated X. So, now with Sharpe out of the picture, it was only fitting for someone else to do the dirty work for me and that was D.C.'s role in my plan. He drove the car and took Michaels out of action for over 6 weeks. It was brilliant and ironic all at once, and that was what made it so much more sweet. Not only had I mastered the technique, I had gone beyond mastering it. I controlled it and it almost felt as though I was the man who created it. It felt good to be me. *Mason took another breath as he finished out the reminder. He was smiling, almost proud to recognize his talents and what he accomplished with them. From the camera's point of view, the grin was evil...almost as evil as it can get.* Mason: Well, now we are in present days and I get to show Michaels in the ring what I am capable of. He will feel the wrath of a rejected student, a master of the art, and the power of an animal. Brian Michaels, you are in for a world of hurt come this Monday. And how fitting for it to be D.C, of all people, to be the person to set this match up. I am sorry D.C., but after I am done with Michaels, I don't think you will have as much fun with Brian as you would of. And that is a fact, through and through. *Mason hops off the bar stool, rubs his own back, and puts the stool away. He cracks his neck once more.* Mason: How fitting that this is a student v.s. teacher match, as well. Kind of a sloppy gimmick, but it will make the humiliation I bring to so much more funny. Putting your head through a blackboard will probably make my month. Brian, you need to realize that I have everything that you have, except I don't have all your flaws. Instead of being cocky, like you are, I am knowledgeable and intelligent. I know that when I speak that I can...and will back it up. You like to talk, and yet you have nothing to show for it. Two world title reigns is nothing. Back when you won the gold the first time, I wasn't even allowed to participate in a match past the second commercial. It's different now, and like I said before, the times are changing. This is the present, and it is time for you to realize that you can not and will not beat me. My re-debut is this Monday, and it will be as impactful as ever. Havoc will not describe it. Either will destruction. However, devastation and domination will describe our match. Be prepare to get struck down by the move that won my World Title and that ended James Sullivan's pathetic career, and that is...the Resoan Bomb. And if you didn't know...Now...ya know! *Mason walks away from the scene and the screen fades to black. Mason Resoan is back in Mayhem Wrestling Entertainment.*
