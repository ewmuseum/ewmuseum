Doing Something

Apr 25 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The scene opens in a hotel room. The camera pans around showing a grand setting, with a modern theme. The hotel room is like a little flat in itself, with a bathroom, kitchen and a bedroom. The camera focuses on the bed, where Billy Joe Barker, wearing cowboy boots, cowboy hat, denim jeans and jacket, is lounging around flicking through channels on the TV. He sighs and throws the remote to the side. Billy: Alleeeehanbo! There ain’ nothin’ on ‘ere but French stuff! Alejandro walks into the scene, wearing a white t-shirt, jeans and a black mask. He is followed in the room by Darrel Jones, who is wearing a pale blue shirt and trousers, looking rather busy. Alejandro: We’re in Paris dumbass, what do you expect? Darrel: You don’t want to sit around here all day watching TV. Paris is a great place, there’s lots of things to see! Alejandro yawns and Stacey Adams walks into view, looking gorgeous in a black dress which complements her assets, she is carrying some bags across the room, and one of them splits. She bends over to pick up the contents and the camera-man zooms in on her cleavage. Alejandro notices. Alejandro: Lens up, pal! The cameraman fumbles around with the camera and apologises as Alejandro laughs to himself. Stacy picks up her stuff and puts it on the kitchen side. Billy Joe gets off the bed yawning, he turns off the television and stands beside Billy. Darrel: So we’re in agreement, then… We’re going to see the sights today? The other three, Alejandro, Billy and Stacy, mumble agreement together in un-enthusiasm. Darrel claps his hands together. Darrel: Ok, shall we go to the Louvre first? We’ll bring Mr. Cameraman too. An Hour Later… The scene opens inside the Louvre, the gang are standing in front of some extra-ordinary paintings. The camera pans around the room showing hundreds of paintings plastering the walls. Alejandro and Stacy are walking along the room looking at all the paintings. Billy is looking thoroughly bored as Darrel explains the history about one of the big paintings. Darrel: Ah yes, The Raft of the Medusa, painted by Theodore Gericault in the 18th Century, it-… The camera focuses in on Alejandro and Stacy. They take a seat on a bench in the middle of the room, Alejandro laughs to himself. Alejandro: Shame Stephani couldn’t have come herself, she would have loved this… Stacy: Well my folks were happy to look after her for the week. Alejandro: Well, at least I get to spend more time with you. Stacy smiles at Alejandro as Billy Joe and Darrel walk over to them. Alejandro looks at Darrel. Alejandro: Why did we come here, I hate paintings and all that… Darrel: Well the Mona Lisa is just a little further down, we’ll see that and get out of here… They get up and walk down a hallway, where a large number of people are crowding round and queuing up for a look at the worlds most famous painting. It is surrounded in bullet-proof glass with armed guards standing either side of it, the frame is solid pure gold and there is lots of flashing as people take pictures from all angles. A security guard walks up to the camera, talks to the cameraman and he shoves it away. The camera is then turned off and the scene fades out. The scene opens again outside the Louvre, next to the giant glass pyramid which it is famous for, the camera-man waves his hand in front of the camera to check it is working. The camera focuses in. Stacy is taking pictures of the surroundings, Alejandro, Darrel and Billy-Joe are talking to the security guard that threw them out, Darrel shakes his hand and the guard walks away. They walk back toward the camera. Alejandro: Nice going, George, we got thrown out. Billy: Awhh… don’ be mean, he didn’ see the “no video cam’ras” sign. The cameraman mutters an apology. Darrel: Well, at least we’re not spending the night in jail. The cameraman focuses the camera on Stacy, and steadily he zooms in on her ass. Alejandro: What have I told you about doing that, George? The camera ruffles about again as George apologizes again. Darrel calls Stacy back over to them and they all gather round. Stacy: So, where to now? Darrel checks his watch and pulls a map of France out of his pocket, he looks at it for a moment. Darrel: Well, it’s still early in the day, how about we go to the Arc de Triomph? Billy: Ohw kay, lets get a cab. The group walk toward the main road, where a number of cabs are waiting for people to come out of the Louvre, the five (including George) get into a larger cab. They pull out of the bay and head off down the road. The cab is kind of cramped, George the cameraman keeps shuffling next to Stacy, much to the amusement of Alejandro. Darrel looks excited to be in Paris, his face pressed against the window looking outside at the sights and Billy Joe seems amazed at how the belt buckles work in France. Darrel turns round to Alejandro. Darrel: So… Black List Mafia, huh? Alejandro sort of laughs and sighs at the same time. He turns to Billy. Alejandro: Can’t even take one day without talking about our match. Darrel: Sorry I brought it up. Darrel turns back to his window as Billy Joe begins drawing faces in his fogged-up window. It was not a long drive, and was fairly silent for the rest of the way, apart from Darrel bringing up a few boring monuments. It became more interesting toward the end, and the Arc de Triomph was in sight, with just a roundabout separating them. Darrel: There it is, the Arc de Triomph, you know, this roundabout is one of the most dangerous ones in France. Alejandro: Hmm… thanks for that. The driver takes a terrifying swerve to avoid another car, and he is as calm as ever as Billy Joe does an unexpected high shriek. Billy: Mah apologee’s. They pull over at the side, Darrel pays the driver and they all get out. They walk over to the eternal flame, which burns in the centre of the monument. The stone is carved with names who served in the great war. Alejandro and the camera take one look around the place. A small French man jumps in front of them. FrenchMan: Vous êtes l'homme que la lutte au large de la télévision. Alejandro looks around to make sure he is the one being spoke to, the French man takes out a pen and paper. Alejandro: Umm… Oui? FrenchMan: Puis-je avoir votre autographe, s'il vous plaît? Alejandro panics and looks to Darrel for assistance, Darrel shrugs… Alejandro: Oui? Stacy laughs and walks forward, she takes the pen and paper and gives it to Alejandro. Stacy: Sign that. Alejandro looks confused, he signs the paper and gives it back to Stacy. Stacy turns to the French man, she gives the French man the paper. Stacy: Ici, vous allez, monsieur, avoir une bonne journée. FrenchMan: Merci beaucoup, mademoiselle. Au revoir. The French Man walks away looking delighter. Stacy turns around to an open-mouthed Alejandro, Darrel and Billy. Alejandro: What the hell was that? Stacy: Didn’t I tell you I can speak fluent French? Yeah, half my family is from Quebec. Alejandro: Oooh la la! The group can hear a trickle as the camera focuses in on a poodle taking a pee on Alejandro’s leg. Alejandro: God damn! Alejandro rounds on the woman beside the dog. Alejandro: Lady! Your damn dog just pissed on me! The lady does not look concerned and walks off. Darrel is laughing with tears in his eyes. Darrel: Good dog. Alejandro wipes his leg on a stone, looking extremely disgruntled. As the laughing stops a little. Alejandro: Why the hell did we come here? Darrel: Alejandro, this is a place of great historical importa- Alejandro: Save it, Einstein… I’m bored. Billy: An’ you smell like Uncle Bilius’ ole boots… Alejandro hits Billy on the arm, which shuts him up. Stacy: Well, why don’t we go to the Eiffel Tower. The group un-enthusiastically nod. Alejandro: Fine, but we’re taking the metro, I’m not killing myself in one of those taxi’s. The scene fades out as they head towards the underpass. The scene opens again as the train pulls into the underground, Alejandro, Billy, Stacy, Darrel (and George) get onto the train. Stacy and Billy sit down as the rest just stand up around them. A tramp suddenly walks onto the tube, with a hat full of change, he puts the hat on the floor and he begins singing in French. The singing keeps on going, it doesn’t affect the rest of the carriage, but Darrel, Stacy and Billy are chuckling slightly to themselves and Alejandro is just looking at the singer incredulously. Alejandro: What the hell is this guy doing? Alejandro begins chuckling too, and the train begins slowing down for their stop the man keeps singing louder and louder. The train stops, Stacy and Billy put some change in his hat, Alejandro walks straight past. Darrel walks to him and gives him a 50 euro note. Darrel: Keep trying man, you’ll make it one day. The tramp smiles and nods at Darrel, Darrel smiles back and walks off the train with the others. They walk through the station and up into the streets, where they are right at the foot of the Eiffel tower. Darrel: There it is, the most ce- Alejandro: Save it, Darrel, we don’t care. They walk to the entrance of the tower, Alejandro stops just before that. Alejandro: You know what guys, I’m gunna sit this one out with George. Billy: ‘ow come? Alejandro: I’m not a big fan of extreme heights. Stacy: Ok baby, you guys have fun. See you later. Alejandro waves them off, as they walk to the entrance. Alejandro walks back over to George, who is sitting on a bench. Alejandro: You know what George, I think it will be good for me to talk about my match. You watch wrestling right. George nods the camera. Alejandro: Well my opponents this week, you’ve probably heard of them… They’re the Black List Mafia, Leon “Virus” Roberts, and SuperNova Vega Knight. Some would say they’re crazy, some might say they’re down to earth, mostly people say they’re mentally retarded… but I think, they’re just normal guys, who want normal things, and just act crazy about it. Alejandro walks over and sits down beside George. Alejandro: And maybe they are retarded, well… not in a Billy-Joe way, in a psycho way… You know, they’re not fit to be stable members of society. But whatever you say, they’re smart, and they’re tough. They’ve beaten me twice already, but they’ve let that get into their heads, their thick, stupid heads. Twice they beat me, twice it was a big match, it was for a title. The first, with Virus, was for the EW Championship, a belt about as prestigious as “Employee of the Month” at McDonalds… and then it was for the MWE Tag Team Championships, held by myself and Billy-Joe. Alejandro thinks it over in his mind for a few seconds. Alejandro: But, I would call it unfair to say we cannot beat them, me and Billy Joe are a great team, speed and strength combined. There is a first time for everything after all, like there was a first time to ride a bike, a first time to wrestle, a first time to win a World Heavyweight Championship, and for Virus, the first time to fuck his boyfriend Dominic. There is the first time, that Tex-Mex will beat the Black List Mafia, and it’s coming. Alejandro pauses as he see’s Stacy’s lift going up the Eiffel tower, he turns back toward the camera after a few seconds. Alejandro: So what could you say about Black List Mafia? They have Virus, a great young talent, moderately successful, slightly arrogant, loves to have sex a lot… But that won’t help him in the ring, because instead of being fucked, he’ll be getting fucked up. My experience speaks for itself, we’ve seen all the young hot prospects cross my path before, and each of them has fallen. Alejandro gets out of his seat again and he begins patrolling the floor. Alejandro: They also have Vega Knight, the stupid man with half a mask who pretends his has two souls in him? I mean, that’s verging on crazy, but to also speak in different sides of him? It’s completely pathetic, a stupid mind-game, supposed to scare, but instead entertains people. Alejandro laughs to himself and bends over to be nearer the camera, his facial features barely visible beneath the mask. Alejandro: So they think, that they, being who they are, and what they are, this they can beat us. They got another thing coming… The camera turns around and zooms in on two French women kissing in a café… Alejandro: GEORGE! The scene fades to black as the camera fumbles again.
