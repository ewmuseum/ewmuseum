Change On The Horizon

Aug 30 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
8 AM / St. Vincent Indianapolis Hospital The camera fades into a hospital room in Indiana. The room itself is shiny and white and full of doctor supplies and complicated looking machines. The walls are all adorned with plaques, celebrating specific doctor’s achievements and diplomas. In the middle of the room against a wall and hooked up to many needles and tubes lies Kim Kash. She is sedated and cannot speak at the moment. On the opposite wall in a corner there is a chair; Aiden Copane is sitting in that chair, slouching, with his head back, and snoring. After a few seconds the sliding doors to the room open up and a doctor in a white lab coat and a clipboard walks over to Kim and begins to examine her condition. Aiden wakes up to notice this. The doctor, when concluded, turns around and begins to head out before something on the clip board catches his eye. He looks at it, turns around to Aiden and says… Doctor Chase: Mr. Copane is it? Hello sir, how exactly are you related to the patient Mr. Copane… Aiden Copane: The patient is my girlfriend, if it makes any difference I was about to propose and I can tell you right now she was going to say yes so you can just write down in your little clipboard that I’m her fiancé. Doctor Chase: (Chuckles) you sure she was gunna accept Mr. Copane? Because this piece of paper says that when we took her off her sedations medications, the first thing she said was that she didn’t want you in her room anymore, something about not being welcome into her life, so Mr. Copane, I’m going to have to ask you to leave immediately. Aiden Copane: What? Are you kidding me? What part of I’m her fiancé didn’t you understand, I’m not going to leave her room, you screwed up or something gave her the wrong meds and she was confused when she said that, she couldn’t have been thinking straight, you dosed her… Doctor Chase: Look, this is as awkward for you as it is for me, do you think I enjoy asking you to leave? The only thing I hope at this point is that you don’t make a scene and I don’t have to call security, so please Mr. Copane can you exit her room. After a few seconds of asking this question Aiden Copane looks blankly into the doctor’s face and then just goes back to his chair and sits down. The doctor gives off a disappointed look and leaves the room for a few minutes and comes back with two large men in uniform with yellow letters reading on their chest’s “SECURITY”. The doctor points at Aiden and Aiden looks up. The men grab Aiden by the arms and begin to drag him out of the building and Aiden doesn’t fight back, he just continues to be dragged all the way to the entrance of the hospital where he is deposited. He picks himself up and walks over to the black stretch limousine parked out in front of the hospital and opens the back door. He dives into the limousine and turns over to look up. Aiden Copane: James Sullivan, I would hate to be you right now, you might wanna be me, even if you joke about it because you think you make people laugh, but, I would absolutely hate to be you right now. You’re pretty much right about everything though. My life is down the toilet, how am I ever going to bounce back from this, when you made your segment you didn’t see what just happened to me, it just adds to the list of things. But the reason I would hate to be you is not because you’re ugly, it’s not because you smell like a horse’s ass, no, none of that. It’s because I would hate to be whoever’s gunna get the aftermath of all this. I would hate to be the person who gets all my fury unleashed upon them. It’s gunna be hell for you James, and I’m going to apologize right now for any damage I cause your body in this match we’re gunna have in advance, because there’s no saying in what condition I’m going to be in on Monday. Aiden gets a look of blind rage in his eye as he takes his hands and courses them through his hair to comb it back. Aiden Copane: Driver, please do what your job entails and drive… somewhere, nowhere in particular just drive the damn car. I’m about to tell you guys something you probably won’t hear every day; starting next week, there’s going to be a new Aiden Copane. Yeah, that’s right, I don’t know how long its gunna last or if there’s any real motivation for it but I’m going to change, after my match with James Sullivan it’s going to be different because this Aiden used to work for me, but it isn’t working anymore, not here in the new MWE it’s not. I need something new. I don’t know what the change is gunna be but… I need something new. I’ll figure it out eventually, don’t worry. For the first time in my life I actually have nothing to do, there’s nothing on my mind, and I’m not really thinking straight but who’s to say that’s bad? I don’t even know where I’m going right now, doesn’t really matter. Since I have this time off I might as well comment on some of the things James Sullivan had to say to me, sort of like a true and false. True, I’m arrogant, cocky, and trust me, that’s not something that’s going to change, it’s who I am and I would be wrong if I tried to change that, but, illiterate? C’mon James, how else could I write your mother those monthly child support checks? Now that we have that out of the way, we should move along shall we? What else did you say, hmm, oh, that’s right, I didn’t mention you enough to your standards, well there is a reason I mentioned D.C., Brian Michaels, Kanto, and hell even Xavier Stone, over you, because their legitimate threats… What the fuck are you? Oh, that’s right, not a legitimate threat. Aiden starts to pass his hand across his forehead and looks at the sweat on it. Aiden Copane: Can you turn on the AC back here, I’m sweating bullets. Driver: Sorry but the AC is broken, the last time miss Kim was in the car she attempted to put ice cubes into the vents to make the air colder… it doesn’t work anymore. Aiden Copane: For fuck sakes… Aiden opens up the moon roof and the camera follows until it’s on the roof. Aiden Copane: (Air in face, semi-yelling) okay where was I? Oh yeah, you’re lucky I even mentioned you in my entire promo Sullivan, because honestly you weren’t worth it, I mean, you act like I lost the match, if you actually participated in the match maybe we would of won, it was obvious who was doing more of the work and which one of us just wanted to go home. So James, let me just sum it all up for you, you’re not going to walk out of that ring Monday the winner, I won’t let it happen, mark my words. And now it’s time for my favorite part of the show, Brian Michaels vs. D.C., this part of the show always gets my heart racing. D.C. has put 500,000 dollars on the head of Brian Michaels, he wants to see who’s loyal to the company, or who’s just here to get paid and wow, am I excited. Because me and another MWE superstar who’ll remain anonymous, unless he decides to speak up are going to take these motherfuckers down because it’s not even about Brian Michaels anymore. This isn’t about any one person anymore, this is about the company, and do you know what D.C. wants to see happen? He wants to see us rot in hell, and I won’t let it happen, if you decide to accept D.C.’s invitation to take down Brian Michaels, you’re going to have to go through me and another superstar to get to him. So, be my guest, accept his challenge and make my day, I can’t wait to step in the ring with a traitor, it’ll be fun. Aiden drops back down into the limo and the camera looks down the moon roof down upon Aiden. Aiden Copane: Oh, and one more thing, am I the only one who thinks James Sullivan and that Stephen guy are totally gay for each other? Well…maybe I am… anyways that’s today’s show, I’ll see you next week my “fans”. Aiden lifts up his boot and kicks the camera the camera is now unstable and begins to fall back and drops off of the limo and all you can see is Static.
