I Will Choke You Out With a Peace Sign!

Mar 14 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
*Mason Resoan is sitting, very neatly, on a bench against the wall of a very large building. He is wearing a white undershirt with a plaid, yet stylish, sports jacket on. He is fiddling with his pocket and looking aimlessly in the air, as the last few children leave the area, leaving Mason completely by himself. He snickers at them, as they walk past, but they don't notice Mason's disgusted looks of disapproval. They simply walk along, minding their own business, without a care in the world. Mason, with no one else around, stands up and walks straight through the soft green grass to the middle statue. A pitcher, and by the looks of it, a good one at that. Good enough to get a monument at Cooperstown Baseball Hall of Fame, at least. He places a hand gently on the large, life-size, bronze statue and sighs. He takes a second look at the departing children, and is relieved to be alone, at last.* Mason: Cooperstown Baseball Hall of Fame is a sanctuary of sorts. Baseball fans all over the world come to this very spot, right here, and admire the sights. They acknowledge the greats and respect the nominees for the current year. They pay homage to their favorites, and if not their favorites, then to people they can respect. This is a sight of tourism and envy, however, there are no flaws. Every man that has been inducted into this prestigious Hall of Fame did something so spectacular in their career that they deserve it. They are truly amazing and they have all been come icons, either of small or wide popularity. *Mason rubs his hand over the statues head, paces back and forth a little bit, and walks down the stone path to the catcher, waiting for the ball to be thrown by the pitcher. He leans against the memorial and sighs once again.* Mason: Very few people have got into this Hall of Fame...and thus, the elite are shown. When you see a name or a plaque on the wall here, you know that they got what the deserved. Much like Mayhem Wrestling Entertainment's Hall of Fame, only some of the elite have ever gotten in. David Whitman and Katherine Tenaud, to prestigious champions. Both wrestlers, both fantastic athletes, and overall...well, at least to the majority, great people. Wholesome and marketable, some would say. And if you take a look, the MWE Hall of Fame is much like the Cooperstown Baseball Hall of Fame. *Mason places his hands out, almost as though it were a balance, and kept looking from right to left and moving his hands up and down. He rolled his eyes, looking at the camera, and continued.* Mason: First, look at the MWE Hall of Fame. Katherine Tenaud was the very first MWE Women's Champion and David Whitman was the very first MWE Champion. Well, Babe Ruth has been on the winning team of the World Series, and thus captured a championship himself. See the similarities? *Mason balances his hands out again, turns them, and pushes himself away from the bronze pitcher statue, going back to the Cooperstown Baseball Hall of Fame wall. He ran his hand against the brick, as he walked through the grass, looking straight down at the ground. As he approached the road, he turned right and walked to a tree, under which he stood still. The camera focused in a little bit, and Mason kept on.* Mason: When people are placed in the Hall of Fame, they must have endured some physical and mental tests. They must have made it through some vigorous judging, and possibly faced some cruel cuts before making it in. However, if you have done enough...at least to get recognized, then of course you will make it in one day. Take myself for example, I will one day make it into the MWE Hall of Fame, but if I don't make it now, I will someday. Well, before people start to think that I am full of myself, you must look at some of the important things I have done. *Mason takes his hands off the tree, squeezes them into tight fists, and gets ready to count. He holds out one finger.* Mason: Well, for one, I was undefeated in the nu-MWE for nearly five months. *Mason stretches out a second finger, this one being his middle finger.* Mason: Second, I was one of MWE's very first stables with Aiden Copane, in which we had many incredible accomplishments, which include becoming the very first MWE Tag Team Champions! *Mason holds out his ring finger, making three, and then drops his hands.* Mason: And while I could go on and on, I will say that I was once MWE Champion. A feat. that not many people can say they have done...but I can, and proudly too. You see, you need to be able to think about a person in Mayhem Wrestling Entertainment, past or present, and immediately think of three or more accomplishments that they have done...that are worthy of being called MWE Hall of Fame material. Credible material means credible accomplishments, and thinks can weigh that down. So, shall we begin? *Mason takes a few steps away from the tree, back over the stone path, and then sits on a bench near the wall. He puts his elbows on his knees, leans forward, and takes a deep breath.* Mason: Let's start with the ever controversial, but unforgettable Ace Cannon. This is a man, who was, as of last year, by far the most overrated guy in MWE. He was hyped up, placed in the main event...but kept failing terribly, always falling short of the goals in front of him. He kicked a little child over half a year ago, and is honestly sick in the head. And I know what you are saying," But Mason, those are negatives!" Well, I told you it would weigh you down. I mean, what does kicking a child do for your character or your reputation? Yes, you are sick. Yes, you are twisted. Yes, you will do whatever it takes to get the job done, but then you take a step back. You just kicked a defenseless child. A human being of incredible small stature and extreme innocence, and you call yourself a tough man. All that shows me is that you are a coward. It shows me that unless you have a way at getting into your opponent's head, you will lose...and I think I am right. *Mason slowly shakes his head back and forth before sitting up in his seat, his back placed firmly against the wood of the bench. He cracks his back and then his neck, followed by a small stretch, after which, he returned to his slouched position.* Mason: Ace Cannon, you are a man of great talent. You have excellent ability on the microphone and some great in-ring talent, but a mid-card championship and one moment where you completely violated any moral value you had will not get you into the MWE Hall of Fame...at least in my books. You are imperfect! *Mason sniffles a little as he rubs his noses and shoots a snot rocket at the floor, almost as he is showing disrespect to Cannon's career. He gives a small evil laugh and continues.* Mason: Well, Ace Cannon is only one man. He is only one person, and I guess everyone can not be perfect, so let us move right along. Next, we have A-Rod, Alejandro Rodriguez...a 5'6" man who has done some amazing things in his life. He was one of the first Triple Crowns...he is a man who has beaten the almighty David Whitman, much like Ace Cannon...but sorry A-Rod, that will not cut it. Really, you are remembered for having some great battles with our current MWE Champion. You were said to be one of the best and you always lived up to that. You won a few world titles, a few mid-card titles, the X-Television title, and now the MWE Tag Team Championships...but unlike Cannon, you don't have anything people will remember for besides your matches with Whitman. You are not controversial, you are not memorable, and you wear a mask. You never had a Michaels v.s. D.C or Dallas v.s. Cannon style feud. You never had a catchphrase like Aiden Copane or myself. You never had a long title run like D.C. You have...nothing, but title wins to your name. And there was a very intelligent man who once said that the titles don't make the superstar...the superstar makes the title...and that could never be more true for you. Yes, you have had a good career...on paper, but in all reality you are no more a coward than you are a man. Ace Cannon is a coward that punt's children in the skull and you are a coward that hides behind a mask! *Mason counts two on his fingers as he takes a look at the ever changing atmosphere. Some more people have piled into the area to look at the monuments, so Mason stood up, politely walking past people left and right, and entered the Cooperstown Baseball Hall of Fame, the cameras on his tale. It was quiet inside, and Mason made as little noise as possible as he made his way up to the second floor of the building. He entered an empty room with a few desks scattered about, and he took a seat at one. And with one final movement, adjusted himself to a more comfortable position.* Mason: Well, it looks like a classroom...so we might as well go back to our grammar school days and keep this run-down in alphabetical order...so...Alex Bowen, you are next! This is a man who has recently come back to MWE fresh, new, and full of surprises...as evident when he won the X-Havok Championship, once again able to call himself the "King of Hardcore!" Alex, I have to tell you something...just because you think it is cool to stick thumbtacks in yourself doesn't mean that you are going to be recognized as anyone other than flash in the pan. You can go around calling yourself hardcore and extreme all you want, but it boils down to looking at your career and asking yourself..."Have I done all I can do?" Listen, I know that winning this championship meant a lot to you...but what does it mean when you lose the next week to the man you beat for the title and a English Patriot? It means nothing...that is what, and unless you can defend your gold with pride, there will be no way in hell that you can be called a serious threat to anyone...but yourself. *Mason stands up, walks to a blackboard sitting on the while and looked for a piece of chalk. He found none so he fiddled with some cabinets and searched through a draw or two before he found a long piece of blue chalk in a desk to his left. He walked to the board and writes 'Ace Cannon, A-Rod, and Alex Bowen' in a list, numbering them as he went. He placed a four on the board, held his own chin in thought, and then wrote, Billy Joe Barker. He thought for a second, shook his head, as if he was disgusted, and wrote the number five right after. He then wrote a familiar name...Brian Michaels. He stopped, placed the chalk in the holder, and walked over to the desk, in which he sat atop.* Mason: I was waiting for this one...a man who so many people love and adore...my former mentor...or so he likes to think...Brian Michaels! He is the career stopper and a man who has memories as long as you can count. D.C v.s. Michaels I, II, and III. Michaels v.s. Whitman I, II, and III. Classic matches with the so-called greats, such as Sonny Williams and Aiden Copane...he even clashed a few times with Pala and Alejandro Rodriguez...and he beat them all. BRIAN MICHAELS WAS INVINCIBLE.....and then he met me! Michaels was the man to beat, along with Whitman, for the longest time. If you could beat Brian Michaels, you had made it. If you could get the upset that everyone didn't want, you really broke in...and I did just that. I, after a near 9 month break, came back and destroyed Brian Michaels in the ring. I smashed him in the face with as many things as I could find...many could actually be found right here in this classroom. Rulers, chalkboards, desks, chairs, you name the school supply, I smashed him with it. I came back, after ending the career of one James Sullivan, and began on my journey of a near 5 month undefeated streak, and I started with Brian Michaels. *Mason hopped off the table and walked over to the board. He, on the opposite side of the board, wrote Brian Michaels name again. He then wrote a factor tree of all the people he beat. A-Rod, Sonny Williams, Ace Cannon, Joey Dallas, D.C, David Whitman...and many more. The tree was very large, but Mason stopped and thought a second. He then wrote a line above Brian's name and wrote his own. He placed the chalk down and walked back to his seat.* Mason: As you can see, that is a tree of all the men Brian has beat in recent memory. It is quite a list and very prestigious...if you ask majority of people. However, I believe on person had this equation to say: Person A > Person B, Person C > Person A, thus Person C > B Well, based off of that logic...I guess I am untouchable right? You see, I did what not many other people could do. I beat Brian Michaels first try with no problems. A-Rod and Ace Cannon, Whitman and D.C all had problems. They all had difficulties doing what I did so easily, so obviously this equation is right...right? Brian Michaels, I am the man who stuffed you and shut you down. I am the man who beat you without effort...and if I can not make it into the MWE Hall of Fame right now, three MWE Championship's under your belt or not, you are not making it either. You are imperfect! *Mason gets up once again, walks to the board, and writes the number six, followed by Chris England's name. He stays standing for this one.* Mason: Chris England...I will make this quick...you are new...you have potential...and you have passion...hell, very few people will literally ignore an entire government just to do a show. That is true dedication, if you ask me...however, unless you pull a rabbit, a couple of world title wins, or some memorable moments out of your a**, then you are no where near MWE Hall of Fame...you are imperfect! *He writes a number seven and keeps on his role. He then writes the name of D.C on the board. He turns, smiles, and does exactly what he did for Brian Michaels. He sits atop a desk and takes a deep breath, getting ready for another speech of sorts.* Mason: D.C is a man that by now, everyone who watches MWE knows. When you think about outrageous...you think D.C. When you think of talented...you think D.C. When you think of memorable...you can not think of anyone else but D.C. However...you are no more worthy than me and you are no more worthy than Brian Michaels. You, much like myself, were someone who had to build themselves up from scratch in this company. You went from an under hyped guy who swore to much, to a mega superstar who won the MWE World Heavyweight Championship, much like myself. However, what is the difference, between you and I? Is it the fact that I have a winning record? Is it the fact that I have beaten more big names than you? No...it is because I am the guy on the attack...and you are the man who lets people walk over him, left and right. *Mason steps off the desk and starts to pace back and forth a little bit. He raises a finger in the air and shakes it a few times, before turning back to the MWE cameras.* Mason: You never once, or at least not that I can remember, ever challenged anyone. You never called anyone out and you never took that extra step that you should have taken. On the other hand, I have...in fact, I took it towards you. In fact, I am still calling you out...yet you never once gave me an answer. Do you not want to get revenge on your lost to me from the old MWE? Are you trying to ignore me or something? Please, do explain because I would love to know. You never once, as long as I have known you, were afraid to say anything publicly. You stated over and over again that you "Didn't care!", but not look at where we are. Why no answer? Do you really not care? I mean, if I didn't care, I would have given you an answer, so once you stop being a hypocrite and grow a pair of testicles to answer me, then I am finished with you. Until you can prove to me that you are better...that you have what it takes to beat me...then you, despite being the longest reigning X-Television champion, are never going to make it to the MWE Hall of Fame. You, just like the people before you, are imperfect! *Mason walks to the board and writes the number eight on the board, and smiles delightfully as he writes Eric Derf's name on the board. He makes a face of slight confusion, but puts the chalk down slowly.* Mason: You know...I don't know what to say about you. You go on this huge winning streak...you lose the title to Alex Bowen...you say you might be gay or something, and now you are in a big time match with D.C...what an odd career. Now, odd is good, I will admit. Odd can ever be called great...but for you...I don't think so. You have won the X-Havok title once and lost it to Alex Bowen...and not like you want to be in the MWE Hall of Fame, but don't even think about it. Odd is good, that is true...but in your case...odd is bad. *Mason looks as the board, grabs an eraser, and erases the equation on the board to his right. He then writes the number 9 and continues with his shakedown of the MWE Roster.* Mason: Another mask...another coward...another fool...Grand Mystique...you should think about what you do...because you might not want to follow in the footsteps of Alejandro Rodriguez...I mean...it isn't exactly the best career...now is it. I have to credit you though...you have worked hard, finally beating Windy Bridges, just like I will do this week. I would ask how it felt, but I shall know soon enough. What I will ask, however, is how does it feel to know that the highlight of your career is being a redneck? I am sure the minute you got your hand raised...you shouted in glee, because you thought you made it. Well, newsflash you masked man of cowardice...you need more than just win. You need titles, you need impressive streaks, you need memorable moments...you need to attain household stardom...you need to have a reputation of being...perfect! You, just off of you mask, makes you imperfect. So...I am sorry, but you just aren't MWE Hall of Fame material. *Mason places the chalk back into the draw and walks out of the room with the cameras following him as he went. Half walking, half jogging, Mason made his way down a flight steps, through big double doors, and across the Cooperstown Baseball Hall of Fame courtyard to a statue of Babe Ruth. It was an extremely large statue, most like 15 feet high, and as Mason looked up at it, he laughed when he thought of who was next in his list. He patted his leg, as he brushed off some lint, but primarily fixed his position next to the statue. He cracked his knuckles very quickly, and continued.* Mason: Babe Ruth is an icon...he has a candy bar...a fifteen foot high statue...memorials and plaques of all sorts all over the world. He, at one point or another, had tremendous feats. in Baseball and life, and to many he is more than an icon...he is a god. He is a baseball god...and he could be related to as if he was the Michael Jordan of Basketball or the Martin Broduer of goal tending in hockey. He is the ultimate...the best of the best...the one that people aspire to be when they get in the major leagues. Well, Joey Dallas, you might believe that you are the Babe Ruth of Mayhem Wrestling Entertainment...but what have you done? You beat Ace Cannon a couple of times...you lost to David Whitman and myself...and you turned on Sinclair Mercier. Babe Ruth won championships...held records...and had respect. What do you have? You have a win over Ace Cannon...a brutal win over Ace Cannon and that is all people will remember for...because that is all you have done. You have not won a single title in nu-MWE. You have not been apart of a great stable. Hell, I don't think you have even been in a high-quality memorable main event match yet. You were always below the main event...and there is a reason for that. There is a reason for everything...no divine power can tell me any different...and the reason you are not ever going to get into the MWE Hall of Fame is because until you do something outstanding...you will be remembered as the man who beat Ace Cannon, well think back to that equation. You beat Ace Cannon, as did Sinc Mercier. I beat both you and Sinc Mericer. Thus I must be able to beat Cannon as well...so that victory doesn't do anything, but over hype yourself. You think you are a god...well gods are supposed to be immaculate...untouchable. You are certainly neither...you are imperfect...you are no god! *Mason walks away from the statue, but pays a bit of respect to it, by giving it a baseball salute, of sorts, as he continued to walk away. He turned and walked away from Cooperstown, walking down the street. The cameras followed and Mason turned to look at the building one last time, getting and outside view of the building.* *It was a beautiful sight, but Mason had to ignore as he walked down the hot asphalt. It was getting towards Spring time and everything was blooming. The trees were getting color, the grass was getting more green, and everything was fresh and healthy. He enjoyed the sight very much, but like always...someone or something ruined it. He picked up his phone, and a familiar voice brightened up his face, as he smiled. It was Sapphire.* Sapphire: Hey, baby, where are you? Mason: I am coming, don't worry. I should be there in about a fifteen minutes to and hour. Sapphire: Alright, baby, I'll see you when you get here. Bye. Mason: See ya later! *Mason closed the phone and kept walking down the street and everything seemed to get nicer by the step. Mason placed the phone back into his pocket and stopped short, as he hopped over a fence and began to walk down a park pathway. Everything instantly felt safer and more controlled as he walked down the path until he reached a bench. He took a seat and focused on the cameras.* Mason: Only three left...and up next in the woman I beat last week...Paige. You see, when you first see Paige, your eyes light up, because she is quite beautiful. However, then you talk to her and realize she actually is a b**ch. She could take this scene of delight and beauty and turn it into something horrible. She is the kind of woman that makes you want to run, because all happiness is drained from. Paige, I can tell you right now...you won't make it into the MWE Hall of Fame with that persona glowing off of you like a bad smell on a fat person. Forget accomplishments right now...forget records...and forget moments...and focus on personalities. Katherine Tenaud was a sweet woman...wholesome and marketable. You are quite the opposite...and the Hall of Fame committee will definitely take that in effect. Imperfect much? *Mason counts down with two fingers, looking at them with glee.* Mason: Zak Warner and Windy Bridges! *He lowers his hand and thinks in his head. He quickly does an inie-mini-miny-moe on his hands and he lands on his left hand.* Mason: Well, it seems as though class is definitely out of session now...we are going with Zak over Windy first! *He folds his hands in his lap, as he leans forward. His elbows on his knees again, he looks around at the trees and he bats at one, showing complete disinterest in Zak Warner.* Mason: Zak...what can I say, there are some qualities that you definitely need to get close to Hall of Fames. You need to be charismatic...intelligent...clever...and of course, a good entertainer. Well, it seems as though you generally disagree with these requirements...as you are constantly ignoring them. You just drone on and on about your life...and yes, you may be the Intercontinental Champion and you could be a good wrestler in the GWC, or whatever fed it was that you were in...but that does nothing for me. For all I know, GWC could have been filled with great wrestlers...or could have been a s**tty little company in Maine or something. Now, you have been making quite the name for yourself, here in the new MWE...but it boils down to experience. You don't have the experience in MWE that I, Brian Michaels, David Whitman, or D.C have. You don't have the edge that Alejandro Rodriguez might have or that memorable moment that someone like Aiden Copane had. You just don't have it...and it is because you are imperfect! *Mason holds up one finger high in the air, looking at it with great intent.* Mason: How funny this is...the final person...and my opponent for this week. Windy Bridges, the Canadian redneck! *Mason takes a small piece of cloth out of his left coat pocket and holds it straight in front of the camera, so it is the only visible thing.* Mason: This is the Canadian flag...this is what represents our country...and it is what symbolizes us. With this flag, we can proudly call ourselves Canadians...as I do every time I think of my heritage. You, as I have noticed, also believe that being Canadian is great. Rightfully so...but how ironic is it that you let some masked punk burn it. Grand Mystique burned the Canadian flag...and how did you respond? You lost the match...your first lost to him, if I remember correctly. That is bad irony. *Mason takes another object out of his pocket. It was a silver peace sign, on a silver chain. Instead of putting it in his hand, he just lobbed it up and down a little in hand.[/color] Mason: Irony is a fun, when you know how to use it properly. Well, this is a peace sign. You and I have a match this week. I am going to do everything in my power to make sure you lose for what happened to our great flag. In short I am saying... *Mason lobs the peace sign high in the air and catches it with the other hand.* Mason: I WILL CHOKE YOU OUT WITH A PEACE SIGN! *Mason places the peace necklace back into his pocket, along with the Canadian flag. He stands up and begins to walk and talk, as the cameras follow him.* Mason: I will do whatever I need to do to defeat you. If it means beating you over the head with a pipe...I will. If it means choking you out with a peace sign, I will. You are imperfect, and you know it! *Mason kept walking and looked to his right and hopped back over the wood fence and made his way across the road to a black car. He knocked on the window, and Sapphire Garvin rolled the windows down. He told her that he would be ready in a second.* Mason: My shakedown of the MWE Roster is factual, accurate, and meaningful. You all need to realize that you are imperfect. You need to realize that there is only one real perfect man in MWE's history...and that man is...m..... *Mason then got a text message. He flipped open the phone, read the text, and closed his phone very softly. He placed it in his pocket and stared blankly at the sky for a second.[/color] Mason: That man is Tim Fitzgerald! *Mason's hands begin to shake a little bit, as he sways in place a little.* Mason: That man...is the Babe Ruth of MWE. He is who Joey Dallas wants to emulate...he wants to be like Tim...who some people could consider a wrestling god. Eric Derf wants to have his odd little things, like Tim had his. Brian Michaels wants to be the icon that Tim was. D.C wants to be the one remembered for making controversial decisions...like Tim...and I want to be perfect...like Tim.... *Mason took a few steps back and thought for a second.* Mason: Tim is the man that everyone wants to be. He was the man that through it all, kept on the good side of everyone. With great power...comes great responsibility...and Tim knew that. He led this federation with an iron fist and he was the man who knew what to do. If anyone ever deserved respect...it was this man. He wasn't a very strict man, but he knew that when business had to get done...it had to get done. He had charisma...he was intelligent...he had pride...he was exciting...he was Tim. And that is all there is to say. *Mason takes a few steps around the car and opened the door.* Mason: Tim was truly...perfect. And if ya didn't know...now ya know! *And with that, Mason closed the door to his Black Ferrari F430 and took off down the street.*
