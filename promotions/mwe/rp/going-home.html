Going Home; Dedicated to Tim Fitzgerald

Mar 14 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Tim made it all happen, and everything said in this RP about Tim, is damn true... This is still his fed in my eyes, and we are still his pupils... He was the greatest, and always will be. And this is my way of honouring him, in a way that will never be enough. R.I.P Tim. _____________________________________ Dallas, Texas Thursday 12th March 2009 7:32pm Yaddleworth Avenue The scene opens in Dallas, Texas. The camera shows a virtually empty street, with big houses on either side, and fancy cars in the driveway. The camera pans around 180 degree’s and it focuses on Alejandro Rodriguez, who is staring down the street. He is on his own, wearing a white tank top, black shorts, black mask and sneakers. In one hand, Alejandro is carrying a huge bouquet of flowers. There is a beeping noise, coming from Alejandro’s shorts, Alejandro pulls out his cell phone and answers it. Alejandro: Hello… Darrel, what’s up? There is a slight pause as Alejandro listens. Alejandro: No, honestly I’m fine on my own… There is a loud revving noise as Darrel pulls round the corner of the street in his silver Aston Martin DB9 Coupé. He pulls up beside Alejandro, who looks shocked and hangs up his phone. Darrel: Are you sure about that? Darrel gets out of the Aston Martin and walks over toward Alejandro, he is wearing a blue button-up shirt and black trousers. Darrel looks calm, but Alejandro looks worried. Alejandro: Where are Stacy and Stephani? Darrel: Booking into a hotel as we speak… Alejandro looks a little angry. Alejandro: And you left them alone? Darrel: Calm down, Al. Billy Joe is with them. Alejandro sighs and walks over to the edge of the road, Alejandro sits down on the edge of the curb and Darrel does the same beside him. They are both silent for a minute, not bothered that the camera is in both their faces. Alejandro: I thought I was travelling to Dallas alone, to prepare for my match in peace, so I could concentrate. Going against Whitman ain’t no walk in the park, I have to be focused, and I can‘t when everyone is with me. Darrel: We thought that this would be a very emotional week for you, and that you need some company, otherwise you’ll never get through your match. If you’re alone, you’re just going to dwell on your bad thoughts, you need your family Alejandro, and you need your friends. Alejandro sighs and nods, Alejandro looks at his roses and both he and Darrel stand up again. Darrel smirks and looks at the roses. Darrel: So what, are those roses for me? Alejandro: They’re for Tim… I’m going the lay them out in front of his house. Darrel: He lived here? Alejandro and Darrel look out toward the beautiful houses. Alejandro shakes his head. Alejandro: No… Come on, I’ll show you… Alejandro and Darrel walk down the road, with the cameraman stumbling behind them, they walk for a minute or two before the come to a halt. Alejandro: Here… The camera focuses on another house, it looked like a nice house, but nowhere near as big or beautiful as the houses on the other roads. The house looked inviting and friendly. Alejandro knelt to the ground, and placed his flowers at the gate. He stood up again, and Darrel put a firm hand on Alejandro’s shoulder as he wiped away a single tear, Alejandro looks at the camera. Alejandro: You see, despite Tim Fitzgerald making MWE, he never got the six-figure pay checks that we do, he never lived the life of luxury. He never had the flash cars or the limousines taking him to work, or the free flights and health insurance. But he was happy, and that’s what mattered, that’s why he didn’t care, he loved MWE so much, he gave us so much, and yet, he asked for very little in return. Alejandro takes a few steps back from the house with Darrel and he just stands there to admire it. Alejandro: He was the first man I ever trusted… My dad had left me, my mother had died, everyone else shunned me. But not Tim, he cared about wrestling and he helped me achieve my dream… Nobody was a lost cause to Tim, nobody was rubbish, just someone who hasn’t achieved their potential yet, but will get there, in time. He was patient and funny and friendly and enthusiastic. Alejandro’s words have some toll on Darrel too, and soon, Darrel wipes away a tear from his eye., but never letting go of Alejandro’s shoulder. Alejandro: I can’t begin to name all of the people Tim has inspired. Antonio DeMarco, James Whitman, Eddie Sanchez, Eddie Freeze, Scott Jones, Jimmy Tuno, Thomas Williams, Nero Fayng, Ryan Teal, Dan Stown, Natasha Stone, Nemesis, Xtreme, Samson, Rocky Stevens, Sasaki, Raoul Duke, Zack Evermore, Mark Marvin… The WcE bunch, his most beloved federation, his home… That’s not even half of the guys from WcE. And then WHQ, he helped set that up, Jack Savage, Jesse Anderson, Dylan Duncan and more, he helped all of them. Alejandro inclines his head that they should go, and they walk down the street at a slow pace, taking in the evening warmth, Alejandro stares back at the camera. Alejandro: And think of all the things, that wouldn’t have happened if it weren’t for Tim. In WcE, there would be no JESTers, no Terrordrome, no DeMarco, no eight time Xtreme champion Mark Marvin, no longest reigning tag team champions Florida’s Finest, there would be no WcE. In WHQ, there would be no undefeated Jesse Anderson, no Jack Savage comeback. In MWE, no WAR invasion, no Crowning Glory, no David Whitman, no Brian Michaels, no D.C., no Alejandro Rodriguez World Title reign… There wouldn’t be an MWE without Tim. Alejandro slows down some more to take another glimpse at the bigger houses. Darrel: I’ll go get the car ready… Alejandro looks at the camera, which closes up to him as he turns his gaze to the houses and then back at the camera. Alejandro: As far as I’m concerned, everything that is and was MWE, is all because of that one man, that one legend, who will hold a place dear in my heart, for the rest of my life. This still is his federation, and we are still his pupils. And me and David Whitman… We were his first pupils here in MWE… His first Main Eventers… So how ironic, I look at the card this week, to see my name next to his. Alejandro takes another pause and he walks toward the nearest house, he doesn’t even stop to look at the house, as he knows he will just find sadness in comparison to Tim’s house, he sighs and looks back toward the camera. Alejandro: Whenever I see my name against his on the card, my insides just bubble up, I love going against David Whitman. Me and him have had so many classic matches, I can’t even count them off my fingers. He is a monumental athlete and a person I greatly respect. And despite what happened a few months ago when he crushed my Stacy damn near in half, he has since been forgiven, and I know we will put on a great match. Alejandro puts his hands in his pockets and crosses the road, the sun shines through the houses and onto him, the camera follows him across. Alejandro: There is a reason this match was chosen to Main Event the show… Even though D.C. vs. Brian Michaels and Joey Dallas vs. Ace Cannon, will be considered as some of the great feuds of the 21st Century… neither of them compare, to what me and Whitman can do when we’re in that ring together. D.C. and Michaels battle off on ego’s, and Dallas and Cannon battle off on hatred… Me and Whitman, we battle for pride, we battle for respect, we battle for our love of wrestling, and that is what this show is all about. Alejandro smiles and tilts his head up, taking in some more fresh air, he lingers looking around at his surroundings a little more. Alejandro: I’ve faced Whitman more times than I’ve faced anyone in the MWE… And the last time we met, I came off better, and Whitman came off bleeding. I was so fuelled with anger, hatred and revenge, that I forgot that I do care about Whitman, and all he has done for the MWE and me. Whitman and I are more evenly matched than twins… And instead of the hatred and anger, this Monday it will be respect and emotion, we’re fighting for Tim, and that must not be forgotten. Alejandro looks over and see’s that Darrel has the car ready for them to leave, Alejandro signals he will be there in a minute. Alejandro: And Whitman, he knows and loves Tim just as much as I do… And he knows what this show is all about. I will go into this match, I will go into the ring, and we shall shake hands. And not because we’re friends, not because we respect the hell out of each other, because it’s the right thing to do, and it’s what Tim would have wanted. The last time we faced off in the ring, it was because I wanted to make him bleed, which I successfully did… But this time, it’s because I want to put on the greatest show in memory of the greatest mentor I’ve ever had, and trust me when I say, we will successfully achieve that. The scene fades to black as Alejandro turns around and walks toward Darrel’s car.
