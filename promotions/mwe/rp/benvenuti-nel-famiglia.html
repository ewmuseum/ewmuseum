Benvenuti nel Famiglia; Mancini RP #1

Aug 8 2008

B.G. Mancini

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The camera turns on and we see B.G. and some of his associates are sitting around a table playing cards in his office located in the back of his club when his Lawyer/Manager Mike Esposito walks in and drops a stack of folders on B.G.'s desk. Mike What are you doing, you have a World Title match to get ready for. What I have here is info on all your opponents. weaknesses, title history everything you're going to need to walk out of Crowning Glory as the MWE World Champion. B.G. puts his cards down and walks over to his desk and sits down before he starts talking in a real thick NY Italian accent. B.G. OK what do you have on these guys that will help me walk out as champion. Mike picks up the top folder and opens it up. He leafs through it before he starts talking. Mike The first person we have is a man called Alex Bowen. He has been in a company AEW where he was there Havoc Champion 2 times and a company called APWC where he was one half of the Tag Team Champions with his Brother David until David got injured and then he switched partners. The one thing you can exploit is his shoulder he has a metal plate in it and he has had 3 surgeries in the last 4 years. B.G. smiles at the thought of inflicting pain on his opponent as he waits for Mike to get to the next person. Mike OK the next person is Xavier Stone. He used to be the leader of a Bike Gang called Anarchist Wandering before they kicked him out. He wandered around Canada and the U.S. looking for his True Love. After a while he joined APWC and became their World champion. Then he wentto AEW and became a top contender there although I'm not sure if he won their World title. B.G. So this guy knows how to get down and dirty if he needs to. You think he would work with us? Mike You didn't let me finish. When he joined up with AEW he found the woman he was looking for but she died in a car accident shortly there after. She left him a son though so if you need to get him to cooperate I'd go through the son if I were you. B.G. So that everything i need to know about this guy or is there something else you need to tell me. Mike looks through the packet and stops to read something. Mike] It says here that a friend of his recently got back in touch with him, a Laura Banting. They seem to be pretty close so if you get a hold of both his son and this Laura woman there is nothing he wouldn't do if you told him to. B.G. Good I don't like having only one option if I need to get someones cooperation. Who's next? Mike puts down the file on Stone and picks up the next one. He starts reading it and he gets a confused look on his face. Mike We don't have much on this guy. He goes by the name Jekyl, He's never wrestled before and noone knows who trained him. The only thing we found out for sure is that this guy is completely insane. Stay away from this guy and let other people deal with him. He will kill anyone who gets in his way. B.G. gets a thoughtful look on his face and then he looks at the file. B.G. Do we have anything he wants? If this guy worked with us no one would mess with the Mancini Family.[/COLOR Mike thinks about it for a little before he shakes his head. Mike] No that wouldn't be a good idea. This guy is bat shit crazy, if he wants something he will take it and Kill anyone who gets in his way. B.G. OK who else you got there is 6 other people in this match. Mike puts the folder he is holding in the pile of ones he's already gone through and picks up the next one on the pile. Mike The next one is David Bowen. He's the brother of Alex Bowen so you know his title history. He's 7 feet tall so you need to be quicker than he his. You're smaller than he is so that shouldn't be a problem. You may want to get someone to help you throw this guy out of the ring. All of a sudden Mike's cell phone rings ans he stops to answer it. HE talks for a couple minutes before he hangs up and looks at B.G. Mike That was your Uncle, he wants to see us now. We can get to the other 5 people later. B.G. and Mike grab their coats and leave as the camera fades to black.
