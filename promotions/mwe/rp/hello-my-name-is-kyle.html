Hello…My Name Is Kyle

Aug 25 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Many Years Ago… Zack Crash: You don’t know what you’re talking about! Kyle cares about me! He’s my friend! He was more than just a little angry. Zack’s youthful features, betraying that he couldn’t be much older then when we last saw his younger self, were contorted in extreme rage. The room felt cramped enough as it is. But his fury seemed to tighten the rooms grip on him. Across from him sat Mr. DeMarco, the school’s counselor. This man was the object of his anger…and he was definitely more than angry. He was down right resentful. How dare this man tell him who he ought and ought not hang out with! Who did he think he was?! Mr. DeMarco: Please just listen to me Zack. I’m just worri– Zack Crash: You know Kyle was right about you! You’re nothing but a flake! Just some loser who couldn’t make it as a real therapist! You don’t know anything! Under normal circumstances, such adults would be outraged at such blatant disrespect from a student. But Mr.DeMarco was a kind and understanding man and only sighed, shaking his head in pity. He knew the boys circumstances, and thought they were truly unfortunate. Losing a mother to breast cancer was something no child should experience. This whole situation with the boy named Kyle, only made things more complicated. Removing his glasses to wipe away some sweat, he reached into his file cabinet and pulled out a manila folder. Reaching inside, he pulled out a small photo taken from an instant camera and handed it to Zack. Mr. DeMarco: I want you to see something, and it might upset you. I took this a few days ago while you were in the lunch from talking to Kyle. Zack glared at the photo for a moment before swiping it violently from Mr. DeMarco’s hand so badly he crumpled it a bit. Zack looked at the photo, examining it intently. The more he stared at it, the more his furrow quickly turned into a gap of utter, horrified revelation. There was a pause as Mr. DeMarco let the reality sink in before continuing. Mr. DeMarco: I’m afraid to tell you that Kyle isn’t your friend Zack. When Mr. DeMarco spoke, it was with sincere sympathy and genuine condolences. But Zack heard none of these in his tone. His mind was too heavy to process with anything else. He felt like all the air had been sucked from the room as a cold hand gripped at his throat. No! It’s not true! It can’t be! The image dissolves like rippling water as years pass like seconds, and we soon find ourselves back in the present. The camera re-opens again, only to find our view…obstructed? Something seems to be blocking the whole vision of the camera to both sides of the screen. From the brown squares we can see, it can only be guessed that we are looking into a pile of boxes. In the center of the pile, a tiny box begins to wriggle to life, and is soon removed to create a small viewing area. And whose head should pop out of this viewing area? Why none other then Zack Crash. Zack Crash: Oh, hey boys and girls. He says in his usual, friendly tone. He takes down the rest of the boxes as his apartment slowly fills the whole screen now. But this isn’t much of an improvement. His apartment seems to be littered with a sort of, controlled mess. Stacks of boxes cover the floor, many filled with various amounts of stuff. Closet doors hang open, clothes lay out neatly on the couch, and even the kitchen plates have been emptied from the cabinets. Zack looks back at the apartment that looks more like a warehouse and blushes in embarrassment. You’ll have to forgive the shape of my apartment. I haven’t gone through my things and done some serious clearing out in awhile now. So I decided it’s time to do a bit of spring-cleaning. And yes, I know spring-cleaning in the middle of summer doesn’t make much sense, but it’s my apartment so get off my back. Zack takes a few of the boxes, grunting and groaning under their weight, and stacks them into a pile out of view from the camera. Wiping some sweat from his brow, Zack begins to slowly make his way between the mazes of boxes on the floor. He goes into the hallway, and the camera goes to follow. It takes the cameraman a moment to guide himself through all the chaos, and the camera shakes violently as he carefully tries to balance himself while walking without stepping on anything. Finally, he manages to have followed Zack into his bedroom, which looks every bit a mess as the living room and the rest of the apartment. We find Zack kneeling on the floor next to a box, pulling out items, examining them, then either tossing them aside or placing them back into the box. Anyway, my messy place isn’t the reason you’re here right now is it? I think I know what you’re really here for. If I could just find the damn thing. Now where did I put that thing? No…no…not it either. Ah! Here we are. Zack pulls out what looks to be a long piece of shining paper. But one he turns it around so the camera can see the front, it can be seen clearly as the Crowning Glory poster. On it, posing very extravagantly, we see the images of many MWE superstars like Sinc Mercier, Nic Kraven, A-Rod, Zak Warner, Joseph Sharpman, and many others. I assume you’re here because of this. Our next pay-per-view is the big one. The super show. The ultimate. El grande pay-per-view. If I missed any other synonyms, then insert the rest here. That’s right, you guessed it boys and girls; it’s that time once again for Crowning Glory. And I am fortunate enough to be competing in my very first Crowning Glory ever. If that weren’t awesome enough, I’ll be competing in a Hardcore Match for the X-TV Championship. Now before I get to the actual match and my actual opponent, I want to turn your attention to something I find to be actually kinda funny. Many of you should remember how last month at Independence Day, me, England, McKnight, and Peter K. were in a Tag Team Match to determine which team would face off for the rights to become #1 Contender for the X-TV Title. Now K. and McKnight ultimately won, and Scotty would later end up winning the right. Now, has anyone discovered what’s so funny yet? Zack looks at the camera, arms shrugging with an eyebrow raised as if awaiting a response of some kind. He folds his arms and begins stroking his goatee, putting on a mock look of intense concentration as he too tries to come up with the answer he already knows. Anyone? You can buy and vowel. Or how about using one of your Life Lines? So what’s it going to be…deal or no deal? He waits again, and after a long moment with nothing but silence between him and the audience beyond the camera, Zack sighs and answers for them. Scotty should be the rightful person competing in this match, yet I’M the one they book. Seriously, how funny is that? Now I don’t mean to brag, or say I’m ungrateful, but this is the funniest thing I’ve ever heard of. Look at it like this, Scotty wins a match, but nobody cares. I come off a two match losing streak, and they reward me with HIS championship match. All these other rookies win their matches, and nobody so much as coughs in their direction. I lose matches and people still call me the “Next Big Thing.” If ever there were a self-esteem booster, that’d be it. But don’t misinterpret this as arrogance or me getting an ego. Far from it. This isn’t arrogance. Arrogance is when you have your head so far up your own butt, you could eat for lunch what you had for breakfast. No, this isn’t arrogance, it’s confidence. Confidence in my own skills and my ability to win, but no so stupid as to underestimate my opponent. Which brings us finally to what has now become my championship match. Zack reaches into another box behind him and begins throwing its contents haphazardly about the room. Briefly, we see flying a few DVDs, books, and other odds and ends. We even see what might be the glimpses of a priceless glass chest set, which we hear shattering off screen. Finally, he pulls out a miniature dry erase board and a blue marker. Removing the cap, he begins marking furiously on the board, writing while he talks. There are three important key factors in this match. The first is the type of match…a Hardcore Match. The second is the opponent, Jay Riddick. And third are the stakes, the X-TV Championship. So we’ll start with the match type factor. Now I believe this match type favors me much more than my opponent. How so, you ask? Well it all comes back down to one thing. I’m a thrill seeker, a daredevil, an adrenaline junkie, spin it any way you want. And when you think of these things there are many words that come to mind to describe such people. Stupid…perhaps. Crazy…for sure. But there’s that none of you can deny…hardcore. How else would you describe someone who jumps out of a plane thousand of feet in the air with nothing more than a piece of thin fabric to save them? What else could a person be who climbs rock walls with their bare hands? And what is a person who leaps into giant ravines with nothing more than a glorified rubber band to rely on for their safety? I’ll tell you what they are. They’re hardcore! I’m hardcore! There is an audible squeak as Zack touches the board with the tip of the marker, and a blue tail follows as he starts to write. On the giant erase board, Zack writes HARDCORE in giant letters big enough for all to read and underlines it three times. Clearly, he’s trying to stress something. This kind of match hinges on the participants being willing to sacrifice their blood and body to get the win. So naturally, it favors me greatly. I mean if I’m jumping out of planes for God’s sake, then clearly my own personal safety isn’t too high on my priority list. I haven’t hurt myself or broken any bones yet in my thrill seeking, but hey, there’s a first time for everything. So you can believe I will go to any lengths, use any weapon, jump off any ladder, and bleed as much blood as needed to win this match. It won’t be easy, or at least it won’t if my opponent gets his act straight, which brings us to factor number two. Zack quickly erases the board and draws a rather crude sketch of Jay Riddick. It isn’t a particularly flattering drawing either. Not only is the likeness very poor, but Riddick is shown to have a sad expression with a single tear pouring down his face, as Zack draws a tiny glint top Riddick’s bald head. Jay Riddick. Really, right now there’s only one thing I wanna ask you. What happened to you? Seriously dude! I mean, when I first got here, I remember how you were the talk of the locker room. All I heard was “Riddick will be big one day” or “Riddick’s one of the best lower card guys” and “Riddick’s the next big thing” just nothing but Riddick this and Riddick that. They used to call you “The Uprising” but you wanna know who they talk about now? Do you wanna know who they call the “next big thing?” I’ll give you a clue, it certainly isn’t Scotty McKnight. It’s me! Now it’s not to say I came and stole your thunder. No, you fell from glory all by yourself. Ever since Kraven gave you that title, all you’ve done is proven why you DON’T deserve it. All you’ve done is run around with your sob story and pity party about how hard your past was. I am so sick of people in this business using their past and how “hard” it was as an excuse for their failures. They spout their sad story to anyone unfortunate enough to hear, and eventually it becomes a crutch. Zack takes the marker and violently draws a giant X over his epitome of Riddick. The board is so crowded now it appears to be nothing but scribbles and chicken scratches. All you’ve done since you got that belt is lose match after match, then run to the locker room and sit and pout and feel sorry for yourself. I don’t mean to sound like a dick, but this isn’t how you prove you’re a champion. Losing matches, then crying about your backstory isn’t the behavior of a champion. It’s the behavior of a spoiled child. You know what you are? You’re not a bad person or wrestler by any stretch of the word. You’re just a lost soul man. Plan and simple. You have no direction in your life right now. You’re not even really here. You’re just going through the motions of living and wrestling when your heart isn’t in it anymore. And because of that’ you’re disgracing the belt. And sorry to say this man, put those excuses of yours have made you weak and calling you third rate would be a compliment. If that’s the Jay Riddick you’re going to bring into our match, then you might was well hand me the belt and not waste my time because I’ll rip you to shreds like a tiger on fresh meat. But that’s not the way I want it. This is my first Crowning Glory and my first title shot, so I want to earn it. I’m a thrill seeker so it’s only natural for me to want a challenge. So you need to reach down deep inside yourself and find the Riddick from before. The Riddick inside of you I know still exist. The Riddick that was once the biggest rookie in the locker room. The Riddick that everyone once believed was a future World Champion. The Riddick that knew how to take names and kick ass. Bring him back, revive him, and remember just how good you can be. Focus your mind, and together, we’ll show MWE what hardcore really means. Otherwise, this match will be nothing but a major disappointment to me and the fans. And lastly, we arrive at factor three…the stakes. With an eraser, Zack wipes the board away clean, leaving a pure white canvas. Then, slowly and deliberately, he writes three simple letters…X…T…V. When he speaks, it’s with a firm, cold finality. When we step into that ring, we’ll both be fighting for the X-TV Championship. Now to some, this may not seem like much, and why should it? Compared to the US or World belts it’s the lowest on the prestigious radar. Certainly this belt doesn’t matter right? Well, that’s where they’d be wrong. Dead wrong! Look at this belt and its history. Look at all the legends that have held this belt before us Riddick. Do you know what this belt represents, what it REALLY represents? It represents the beginning of a legacy. Many of the people who held this belt have gone on to become World Champs, or are on their way to becoming World Champ. Some have even become Hall of Famers. Essentially, they become immortals in this industry of ours. Look at those already building their legacy off that belt. Names like Nic Kraven and Eric Derf. Legend or rising star, their legacy’s all began with one belt…the X-TV Championship. It’s the first step. It’s the moment when the training wheels finally come off. It’s a step up to the next level. Of course, all that doesn’t matter now. You see, a belt’s only as good as the champion wearing it. And as I said before, you’re disgracing that belt right now. People talk about how the US belt and how it’s been disgraced, but I say look at the X-TV belt. Look at it, handed off, not won, to a guy whose more emo than Green Day and Linkin Park combined. A guy who lets his past rule him, instead of overcoming it to make himself stronger. So I highly doubt Riddick will use this belt to elevate himself to the next level. Whereas me on the other hand, I’m the most talked about rookie in the locker room today. With his pride clearly visible now like it were a physical object, a small smirk crosses Zack’s lips as he stares into the camera so deeply the barrier between wrestler and audience seems to vanish like smoke in the wind. I don’t like to brag much about myself, but it’s the truth. I’m not going to say I deserve the belt more than you, because that’s not what it’s about. It’s about who is ready to move on, about who’s ready for better things. It’s about who is ready to truly become the next big thing. And between the two of us Riddick, you’re not ready, whereas I am. I’ve proven myself ready by basking in the glory of my wins, taking the shots of my defeats, paying my dues, and showing this company all that I got. And while I may not be the best, I’m definitely one of the most exciting. So with this belt, and this win at the biggest pay-per-view on the MWE calendar, I will take one step closer to my ultimate goal, my ultimate dream…my ultimate thrill. But what is my ultimate thrill? To be one of the greatest MWE wrestlers who ever lived. To be a future World Champion. And then to know what true immortality is when I become a Hall Of Famer. There is a loud knock at the door, which temporarily distracts Zack. Regaining himself, he stores away the dry erase board and with great care guides himself through all the boxes and items scattered on the floor. The camera follows him with much shaking until they’re finally at the door. With one hand gripping the knob, Zack turns to face the camera one more time with a very thoughtful look. We both have something to prove in this match Jay. You have to prove way you’re still worth anything, and I have to prove why I’m worth everything. So whose legacy will begin at Crowning Glory? Only time will tell. There is another knock at the door, which somehow sounds a bit more annoyed than the first. Turning the cold, golden knob, Zack opens the door to find Kent Bishop standing in the frame. He taps his foot in rapid secession on the carpet, arms crossed and scanning Zack with a disgruntled look in his hazel eyes. Kent: What the hell did you keep me waiting for? And who the hell were you talking to just now? Zack: Oh, nobody. Zack replied in an innocent voice, turning to face the camera and giving a small thumbs up. Kent just rolls his eyes. Kent: Whatever. Anyway, I just thought I’d come over to ask if – ahhhhh! Without waiting for an invitation, Kent takes brisk steps and stumbles into the apartment. Without watching where he was going, his foot strikes a box, which makes him jump in surprise. Finally, he looks around to see the mountain of boxes, taking the new details of his surroundings with the utmost interest and confusion. His eyes are bulging so wide he looks as if he feels he might have wandered into the wrong apartment by mistake. He turns to face Zack. Kent: Ummmm, did I miss something. Zack brushes past him quickly, returning to his rather “different” method of organizing. The camera peeks inside the box Zack is currently attending to, and it looks as if things have been thrown inside of it at random with no regard to it’s neatness or contents. Zack: Just doing some cleaning out of my old stuff. Zack says simply. He ignores Kent for a long while, continuing to shuffle threw various boxes, examining some objects before putting them back and moving others to different boxes. Although we, and Kent observe him, there seems to be no rhyme or reason to what he’s doing. Kent: Ooooook, why exactly are you doing that? Zack: Just so I can get rid of some stuff I don’t use anymore. Kent: Do you want any help? Zack: Sure, thanks. Just start sorting through some of those boxes over there. Zack says as he indicates a random direction without even looking up. Kent looks in the direction Zack is pointing and sets to work on a random box. Kneeling down beside it, he looks inside to find the contents just as chaotic as the others. None of the boxes are even labeled. Over his shoulder, he gives Zack a disapproving look at his inability to coordinate things properly and the hard work he’ll have to suffer through now because of it. Kent: Have I ever told you you’re one of the messiest, most disorganized people I’ve ever met in my life? Zack: Uhh, you say something? Kent just sighs deeply. Apparently Zack wasn’t listening to a word he just said. Kent: Never mind. The two work in silence for a long while as both men move from box to box, examining the contents of each. Zack moves along rather quickly, picking objects up to looking at them for a few seconds before either placing them back inside, or randomly throwing them into another box. Kent, on the other hand, moves much slower with an almost permanent look of confusion. He examines many items for a long time, eventually having to turn to Zack to ask whether it’s junk or not and to guide him on which box to toss them in. About a half hour later, Kent comes across something interesting. Kent: Hey, what’s this? Out from the bottom of a box he pulls out a large, black-leathered book. It looks old and worn. The black leather has started to discolor a bit and has even begun to peel slightly. The writing on the front, done in a shiny sliver, has been chipped away by the years and is no longer readable. Zack looks over and smiles brightly at the object. Zack: That’s one of my old school albums. I almost forgot I even still had that thing. With mild interest, Kent opens to a random page and starts flipping through them, paying attention to any possible signs of a younger Zack Crash. After turning one page, a small piece of something flutters to the floor from between the pages. Bishop goes to pick it up and realizes it’s a small photograph taken from an instant camera. Looking at it, he now sees the younger version of Zack he’d been searching for. The picture was of a young Zack sitting at a lunch table, immersed in deep conversation with someone. Kent: What’s this about? Kent said in confusion, holding up the photo for Zack to see. He turns to look at it, and instantly, Zack’s face falls several degrees. Zack: That is a very long and complicated story. Zack replies, sounding almost melancholic. Zack was hoping this would deter Kent from pursuing the subject, but if anything, it only made him more interested. His face brightening, he nudges closer to Zack. Kent: I’ve got time. A long silence passes between the two where Zack refuses to look Kent in the eye. The picture had a very long, personal, and even traumatic story accompanied to it, and it wasn’t one he enjoyed telling. Hence why he’d kept it a secret for so long. But from the look he saw in Kent’s eyes when he eventually looked up, he knew there was no avoiding it. And who knows, maybe finally telling someone about this might actually prove therapeutic. Zack: Well, it all started back when I was a kid… The room was uncomfortable and felt cramped. Sitting in a comfortable leather chair (the rooms only redeeming feature), Zack Crash looked around for something to hold his attention. But Zack had been in here enough to know nothing could hold him for more than a few seconds. The walls were mounted with a number of motivational posters that were cheesy at best. The filing cabinet and desk in front of him had every bit of their flat surfaces covered with various knickknacks. He supposed that all this was an attempt to make the room feel more relaxing, but he thought the attempt was a little too obvious. Mr. DeMarco: Zack, are you listening? Zack turns his attention back toward the man sitting at the desk before him. Mr. Lucas DeMarco. The school’s guidance counselor. Ever since his mother finally died of her breast cancer a little over a month ago, he’d been ordered to attend sessions with Mr. DeMarco to help him “deal” with the situation. Mr.DeMarco was nice enough, but Zack still found him more annoying than helpful. Zack: Of course. Zack lied. Mr. DeMarco seemed to know this, but only sighed and pressed on. Mr. DeMarco: I was just talking about what we discussed last time. Zack groans under his breathe in irritation. They been over this subject more than he cared to experience and it was starting to get on his last nerves. But Zack did his best to hide his frustrations. Letting it out would prove to be more troublesome than the discussion itself. More trouble than it was worth anyway. Zack: What about it? Mr. DeMarco: Well how has that been going? What they were talking about was, in their past sessions, Mr. DeMarco and the school had expressed an interest in Zack to try new things. His mother’s death had been a terrible loss, but they also viewed this as an opportunity. Under his mother’s protective wing, Zack had become somewhat inclusive. So the school thought Zack expressing his new freedom and exploring new things would be a good way to help deal with the lose as well as grow as an individual. Unfortunately, it did not take as well as hoped. Zack: Ok I guess. Zack answered in a bored tone. In truth, it hadn’t been going at all. Mr. DeMarco: Well, tell me about it. This pressure made Zack sweat a bit, but he was quick to come up with what he thought was a suitable excuse. Zack: Well, I’ve been hanging out with that Kyle kid a lot lately. He’s pretty cool. Mr. DeMarco frowned and shifted uncomfortably in his chair. Apparently this wasn’t what he wanted to hear. But Zack didn’t see what was wrong with this excuse. He’d been encouraged to make new friends and “explore” as they put it, and Kyle was much like Zack. Something of an outcast among the students, Kyle was practically ignored by everyone including teachers. In fact, Zack didn’t first notice him till some time ago. He just seemed to appear out of nowhere. Mr. DeMarco: Well I think we also talked last time about how he can be a negative influence. Zack: How’s that? He’s like the coolest guy in school. Mr. DeMacro: Maybe, but cool doesn’t always equal good Zack. Zack turned away and roll his eyes. How predictable. He should have guessed this reaction. It wasn’t the first time they objected to this friendship. But as far as he knew, Kyle’s only smudge on record was getting expelled from school, or so Kyle told him at least. This is why Zack assumed the teachers pretended he wasn’t there. Mr. DeMarco: Well anyway, it looks its time for you to go to lunch now. So we’ll put this off until next time. Ok? Zack nodded, though he was only half listening. Eager to get out and get food in his stomach, which was grumbling in protest, Zack stood up from the chair so fast his knees bumped the desk. Righting himself, and turning his back on Mr. DeMarco with only the briefest nod, Zack headed out the door and toward the lunch room, where he was certain his friend was waiting for him. Kent: I never knew your mother died of cancer! The scene returns to the present day, and we now find Kent and Zack sitting at the coffee table sharing a drink. All the boxes and plates that stacked the table before have now been moved into a new location off screen. Kent looks in awe at Zack and almost offended. Zack merely bows his head in shame. Zack: It’s just…kinda hard to talk about, you know? Zack says weakly, looking expectantly at Kent. Kent leans back and groans, irritated, but sympathetic. Kent: Come on dude. We’re better buds than that. Sure you may get on my nerves every now and then and I say stuff, but you all know that none of that is serious right? Zack gave a gentle smile, truly appreciative for the kind words. Although he did know this, it was always nice to actually hear it once in a while. Thinking of nothing else to do to show his gratitude, Zack lifted an arm and gave Kent a small squeeze on the shoulder. Zack: Thanks man. Zack said simply. Taking a quick sip from his cup, Kent’s smile widened in anticipation as he leaned in closer and nudged the story forward to its next chapter. Kent: So you’re mom died and you went into counseling, and they didn’t like your new friend. That still doesn’t explain this photo I found. Zack: I’m getting to that. Haven’t you ever heard of dramatic tension and building. It’s part of any story. Anyway, me and Kyle were pretty tight and I always thought him to be a pretty cool dude. Or at least, that’s how I used to think… As Zack walks in to the cafeteria, he finds it to be the usual jumble of noise. Several conversations going on at once mix together in one giant, incoherent mess. Slowly, like a predator seeking its prey, Zack scans the faces at the tables, looking for any familiar features. Then, sitting off alone at a table on the far end of the room is Kyle, waving Zack down excitedly. Kyle looks just like any other kid. Average height, a little on the skinny side, short brown hair, and blue eyes that have a mischievous gleam in them. Seeing him, Zack walks over and sits down, placing his food tray of meatloaf down in front of him. Kyle had no tray. He never ate at lunchtime. Kyle: Yo dude. How’d the session with Dr. Whack Job go? Zack: It went ok I guess. Zack says with a faint sign of a chuckle and beginning to devour his meal. Kyle: So what did he tell you this time? Zack hesitated for a moment. He was uncertain how to react to this. He’d told Kyle all of the things Mr. DeMarco had said before and always enjoyed listening to him make fun of the man. But he’d never told him about how he viewed their friendship out of fear of upsetting him. But today, for some reason, he felt compelled by something. An urge inside of him with no direction or reason, told him he ought to tell Kyle. Zack: He uhhh…said you’re…kinda…a bad influence. Much to Zack’s surprise, and relief, Kyle’s smile broadened, as if he already expected this answer. Kyle: Well duh. Man, are you letting that guy get inside your head, don’t let him man. Guys like him don’t know what they’re talking about. They just wanna find some excuse to say something’s wrong with you just so they can keep their jobs. Trust me man, nothing is wrong with you. You’re fine just the way you are…in fact…you’re great! Zack: Thanks. It was a simple reply that couldn’t really express what those words meant to Zack, but he supposed it was the best he could do. Kyle: Hey man, I heard some of the guys are taking their new BMX bikes and are going to ride them down Devil’s Backbone tonight. You up for it? Devil’s Backbone was the name of a particularly steep hill in the neighborhood. Many of the “cooler” kids would take their bikes and ride them down the hillside. It was dangerous, and off limits, but that didn’t stop most kids. The kids in question that Kyle mentioned weren’t a group Zack actually hung out with, but they were deemed the “cool kids” of the school. Zack: Sure I guess. Kyle: Sweet! The scene quickly dissolves back to the present, and Kent is leaning back in his chair, fiddling with his cup between his fingers. He eyes Zack with a somewhat disappointed expression, expecting something of a more thrilling story from the self-proclaimed “Ultimate Thrill Seeker.” Kent: So what’s the deal with this Kyle kid? He seems like he was a cool enough guy. What, was HE the one who got you into thrill seeking an everything? Zack took a moment to gather his thoughts more clearly. The story was reaching its climax, and it was a very difficult climax to face. Breathing deeply and steeling himself, he continued. Zack: Not exactly. Well I went to the hill and waited, but Kyle never showed up. After that, I didn’t feel like going down the hill with the others. So I just watched for a few hours and left. Then the next day… The moment Zack stepped into the counselor’s office he knew it was going to be bad news. The air was rank with a grim foreboding. The air felt thick and hot, making him sweat slightly. And the grievous look on Mr. DeMarco’s face didn’t make him feel any better either. Mr. DeMarco gestured to the chair in front of the desk, and Zack sat in it. Mr. DeMarco: I want to talk to you about something very important Zack. Oh boy. Here it comes. Mr. DeMarco: I’ll get right to the point. You need to stop seeing you’re friend Kyle. Silence. It falls between them like a silent death. Zack’s jaw slackens open in shock. This was something he hadn’t expected. Zack: Wh…what? Mr. DeMarco: Look Zack, I know he may seem like a friend to you, but he isn’t. You have to understand why you hang out with him. Zack: And why’s that? Zack felt the heat inside him rising like a storm. His silent fury echoed like the raging of thunder within his mind, and he glared at Mr. DeMarco, hoping he felt every burning fiber of his anger. Mr. DeMarco: Because you’re scarred. I know about what happened at Devil’s backbone. Don’t ask me why just listen. When your mother was alive, she was very protective and didn’t allow you to do much. But now that you have more freedom, you’re afraid to use it. So, you seek out someone who can push you to do those things you really want. In a sense, you want Kyle to make those decisions for you. Zack Crash: You don’t know what you’re talking about! Kyle cares about me! He’s my friend! He was more than just a little angry. Zack’s youthful features, betraying that he couldn’t be much older then when we last saw his younger self, were contorted in extreme rage. The room felt cramped enough as it is. But his fury seemed to tighten the rooms strangle on him. Across from him sat Mr. DeMarco, the school’s counselor. This man was the object of his anger…and he was definitely more than angry. He was down right resentful. How dare this man tell him who he ought and ought not hang out with! Who did he think he was?! Mr. DeMarco: Please just listen to me Zack. I’m just worri– Zack Crash: You know Kyle was right about you! You’re nothing but a flake! Just some loser who couldn’t make it as a real therapist! You don’t know anything! Under normal circumstances, such adults would be outraged at such blatant disrespect from a student. But Mr.DeMarco was a kind and understanding man and only sighed, shaking his head in pity. He knew the boys circumstances, and thought they were truly unfortunate. Losing a mother to breast cancer was something no child should experience. This whole situation with the boy named Kyle, only made things more complicated. Removing his glasses to wipe away some sweat, he reached into his file cabinet and pulled out a manila folder. Reaching inside, he pulled out a small photo taken from an instant camera and handed it to Zack. Mr. DeMarco: I didn’t want to do this. I want you to see something, and it might upset you. I took this a few days ago while you were in the lunch from talking to Kyle. Zack glared at the photo for a moment before swiping it violently from Mr. DeMarco’s hand so badly he crumpled it a bit. Zack looked at the photo, examining it intently. The more he stared at it, the more his furrow quickly turned into a gap of utter, horrified revelation. There was a pause as Mr. DeMarco let the reality sink in before continuing. The moment lasted both for an eternity and mere seconds. Mr. DeMarco: I’m afraid to tell you that Kyle isn’t your friend Zack. When Mr. DeMarco spoke, it was with sincere sympathy and genuine condolences. But Zack heard none of these in his tone. His mind was too heavy to process with anything else. He felt like all the air had been sucked from the room as a cold hand gripped at his throat. No! It’s not true! It can’t be! Mr. DeMarco: Because Kyle doesn’t exist. And there, Zack Crash sat for a long while, stunned into cold silence as he starred in blind disbelief at a photograph of himself, sitting in the lunch room…talking joyfully to an empty seat beside him.
