Bryant, Jackson, Jordan, Alejandro - Legends

Feb 12 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The scene opens inside a well-lit room. The room has grey walls and a white carpeted floor. Spreading across the room are a number of leather couches with a coffee table in front of them, on the wall opposite to the couches is a large widescreen television. The camera sweeps around to the room and focuses on a man, Alejandro Rodriguez. He is wearing a black mask, a black t-shirt, jeans and white trainers. He is standing at a window, which takes up one of the four walls in the room, it seems the room is high up inside a skyscraper, and Alejandro watches the streets of New York, New York. As traffic and pedestrians pile through the streets on their way to work. Alejandro glances toward the camera, and just gazes back at the street for a moment, before turning his back on the window and looking straight into the camera. Alejandro: Every day, people crowd these streets on their way to work. They travel like that because they need to make a living. they need to provide for their families. The food isn’t going to put itself on the table, and some people go through hell to make sure food is provided for their families. And then you hear people complaining how they’ve got it tough, it really gets on my nerves, wrestlers moaning how they bust their ass every week and travel miles for their job. Wrestlers that get paid six figure sums every year to do what they “love”… Alejandro makes the quotation marks with his fingers and takes a look back outside. Alejandro: When I was a young boy, I worked twice as hard as I do now, and I wasn’t making six-hundred thousand dollars then, I was making six. And do you know why I did it? I did it to survive, I have a survival instinct like non-other in the MWE. A skill which has seen me into matches with the likes of Brian Michaels, D.C., David Whitman and Sonny Williams, and it has seen me win. Paige has never defeated Michaels, David Whitman or Sonny Williams. And despite what she thinks, she has never defeated D.C. Alejandro walks over to the leather couch, he sits down on it and surveys the camera as it comes closer. Alejandro: They are, and I am, the cream of the crop of MWE, and you desperately want to be up their. So you claim a cheap victory… well that just won’t cut it will it, because we all have the vital things that you don’t have… we have balls. And we have respect… I respect everyone in the MWE, even you, but you… you have no respect for anyone, you think you’re the best the MWE has to offer? Well I’m afraid it’s time for you to step away from people like Virus and come into the major leagues, and see who shuts you up then. No-one is going to stand for your crap anymore. Alejandro feels he cannot stay in one place too long, he gets up from his feet again and begins walking back and forth across the room, the camera following him as he goes. Alejandro: I mean, did you even think how Lindsey felt? D.C. was the love of her life for years! He was half-dead, and you came out and claimed you beat him? I’m no friend of D.C.’s, but I know that he has sold out more shows and had more sucess than you could ever hope to achieve, he is better than you! And there is no way, absolutely no way, that you could have defeated him, when even full-grown macho men struggle to on a weekly basis. Your confidence is your worst enemy, and you shall realise that this Friday. Alejandro reaches the window yet again and he presses his hand to the glass as he looks down at the world below. He then looks at the camera. Alejandro: But for all your confidence, what have you achieved in your career that’s so great? Dominating a three-woman women’s division? Winning a few lower-midcard titles? Holding your own with a few minor names? I have battled legends, defeated legends… X-TV Title, I’ve held it. US Title, I’ve held it, twice! MWE World Heavyweight Championship, I’ve held it. You are nothing compared to me! What right have you to run your mouth to a guy like D.C., what right do you have to run your mouth to me? Alejandro takes away his hand from the window, revealing a large hand-print still left there on the window. Alejandro wipes it away with his hand and backs away from the glass, he then looks directly back into the camera. Alejandro: This Friday, you’ll be facing someone of a higher calibre than your usual Virus or Zak Warner. We will be wrestling at the greatest arena in the world, at the Madison Square Garden, a place where Kobe Bryant scored 61 points against the Knicks, a place where the Jackson 5 made their first television appearance, a place where Michael Jordan played his first game as a pro and was given a standing-ovation as he left, they are legends Paige. And at Friday Night Over-drive, you’ll be facing a true legend, a veteran of the MWE, you wont be able to cope. You won‘t be able to breathe, you won’t be able to scream, you wont be able to escape. Alejandro smiles at this thought, and the camera rotates as Alejandro paces up and down the room again, Alejandro looks down toward his hands, and he clenches them into tight fists, making his knuckles turn white. Alejandro: And if you want proof, just ask David Whitman… David Whitman, who in the past month alone has taken out D.C. and Grand Mystique, but not me! Not me, who made him bleed for what he did to my beautiful Stacy, I defeated him… I still have his blood on my hands… And I know, that he still fears me, he is not willing to fight me, he feels guilt, bringing emotion into a match, makes you loose the match. And Paige, that includes confidence… His fists loosen into their original place, and he looks at them once more, as if checking for the blood on his hands, the camera temporarily zooms the camera in on his hands but they zoom out as Alejandro walks past the television, looking at the camera. Alejandro: So Paige, do you think you can step it up? I’ve beaten people with no respect before, I’ve taken on guys like Jekyl, I beat him, and no-one has seen him since. I’m the only one to defeat the “untouchable” David Whitman in a long, long time. I’m in the best form of my life Paige, and nothing you can do or say to me will change that. Time for you to earn your respect Paige, and with that respect I will teach you to be respectful. Time to step it up… See you Friday. Alejandro begins to walk backwards and the scene fades to black.
