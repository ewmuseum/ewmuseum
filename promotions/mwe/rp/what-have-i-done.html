What Have I Done...

Aug 23 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
August 24 / 11:00 PM MWE Cameras fade into a busy street in Detroit at night. Cars passing by the cameras make a hissing noise as they go past but the sound of a club and the line outside of it are louder. The cameras get closer to show a bouncer arguing with a heavy man at the door as several onlookers yell “FIGHT”. At this distance the music coming from inside the club is prominent. The cameras move by the argument occurring at the door and passes through a hallway with mirrors on everywhere except the floor. Once clearing that passage the neon lights begin to flood the lens of the camera and the people near the entrance dancing back away to let the camera through. The deeper the camera goes it begins to encounter drug use in the form of ecstasy tablets being popped. The camera gets to an entrance being guarded by two more bouncers, they move out of the way for the camera. The camera gets to a dimly lit room where Aiden Copane and Kim Kash are laying on a sofa laughing and sipping on champagne. Aiden Copane: Sorry baby, now it’s time for real serious business. Aiden turns toward the camera. Aiden Copane:Welcome, ladies and gentlemen to the Club Carnage…and newly casino, down this passage and your second right, you can’t miss it. Club Carnage is owned exclusively by me. This establishment is just another example of the power of money. Well, honestly, who wouldn’t wanna be me at this point in my life, I’m on my way to capturing the only MWE title I’ve never had, and once I do that, I’ll be up with the big dogs once again. Let’s not get ahead of ourselves though because Covell’s amazing booking has me in a match with…no, not James Sullivan, not even one man at all. At least it’s not a handicap match like Whitman, but no, I’m in a tag team match against Xavier Stone and, Kanto? Who the fuck? When I read that off I had to go onto MWE.com and check who it was. Then when I saw his picture on the roster page I couldn’t help myself, I put my head down and just shook my head. Another one? When is this circus show going to end? First Jekyl, then Aeron, now Kanto? Honestly, I thought he was a joke, until I saw his promo. For a second I actually felt a little something I don’t feel often, fear. It didn’t last long though because then I remembered who I was. Aiden Fucking Copane, I WILL MASSACRE YOU, I WILL FUCK YOU UP…*Cough*…Anyways, as part of Covell’s famous booking abilities, instead of facing James Sullivan and getting these idiots out of the way of the real gold, I have to partner up with him against those other two idiots. Covell can delay the inevitable all he wants but I will win this tournament, just like I will walk out of the arena tomorrow on my own two feet. Kim Kash: Baby, I’ll be right back, I’m gunna go get something I forgot in the car. Kim stands up, grabs her purse and walks out into the club full of people. Aiden Copane: Well, what was I saying; actually, enough of that anyway, let’s get onto more important matters. Anything other, than my silly little tag match, and since I’ll be main eventing any day now I might as well talk about the hot issue, D.C. and Brian Michaels. I remember when you guys got along, back in the good ol’ days, and now, Chemi-Kill to Brian Michaels? What happened to the good ol days? Now it’s taken a turn for the worst. And I think it’s time I picked a side on this matter and my fans would probably already have me figured out and decide that D.C. is the ultimate bad ass and came out on top so I should be in his favor. Well you think you know me? Try again. D.C. has seriously crossed a line when he did that to Michaels, and for once, I find myself defending another enemy in MWE. So, after I finish smacking up all these chumps, I offer my assistance to Brian Michaels, and I hope he takes me up on the offer. Because D.C. you’re very wrong… Brian isn’t the only man to shut you up in MWE, because I’ve beaten you not once, but twice. Kim walks back into the room sniffing up her nose to try and get her nasal passages clear. Then she goes into her purse and pulls out small little white tablets and puts them in her mouth. She then closes her purse back up and sits down next to Aiden who is now more than curious. Aiden Copane: What the fuck was that? Did you just pop pills? And why were you snorting on the way in? Kim Kash: Oh, Aiden, aiden, aiden, it was just a little ecstasy. And when I walked in I was just snorting up the last bit of cocaine I had in my nose. I don’t have any more cocaine, that’s some expensive shit, but, if you want I have a few more tablets. Do you want some? Without Aiden responding she begins to fiddle through her purse again and pulls out a small bag with a few ecstasy tablets in them. She takes them out of the bag and grabs one and pushes it between Aiden’s lips and Aiden backs up and spits it out. Aiden Copane: What the fuck? Are you crazy? Since when do you even do drugs? You better make sure that ecstasy doesn’t show up on my MWE Drug Tests you fucking bitch. Kim begins to cry and her makeup begins to run down her cheeks, she grabs her bag and begins to storm out to the door but Aiden grabs onto her wrist and pulls her back. After a bit of fighting like this Aiden begins to tug hard on her wrist and pulls hard one last time. Kim falls to the ground and begins to grab her wrist. Kim Kash: What the fuck Aiden, Oww! What the fuck did you do that for? Aiden Copane: Baby! I didn’t mean to, I’m trying to help you, you have drugs in your system and you’re not thinking straight, I don’t want you to be a druggie baby, please, I didn’t mean to hurt you, please baby! Kim Kash: Fuck you Aiden Copane, FUCK YOU! Kim picks herself up off the ground clutching her wrist and opens the door of the room and walks out of the back entrance of the club. Aiden decides not to follow her and sits back down on the couch. He looks up into the air and begins to think about various things. Maybe drugs aren’t all that bad…Maybe I can win her back if I try some too. No, that won’t work you idiot. What should I do to get her back? I mean, for fuck sakes I broke her damn wrist…Fuck this… Aiden has a change of heart and decides to go after her, he walks out of their room and exits under the illuminated exit sign out to the back of Club Carnage. He turns left and then right and then spots her, her and two other men dressed in black leather jackets, she starts to scream as they start to surround her. Aiden begins to run as fast as he can at their direction. One of them looks his way and moves out of the way but the other is met with a volley of punches to the forehead, blood begins to pour out of the man’s forehead as Kim screams in horror. Aiden looks over to see the other man grabbing her and trying to take her away. Aiden jumps up and charges at the man and goes for a spear…it all happens so fast but the man pushes Kim into the way and Kim takes the full blow of the vicious spear. Aiden stands up and backs up into a concrete wall as he looks at what he did. He puts his hand to his mouth in horror. The other man runs into the street and escapes leaving his companion in a pool of his own blood but the damage is done. Kim begins to cough up blood and she whispers in a threatening voice… Kim Kash: Fuck….you…Aiden. She starts to cough up more blood as Aiden looks on with a face of terror, unsure of what to do…Frozen. He turns around and punches the concrete wall as hard as he can and lets the blood drip from his hand. He then passes his hand up at his forehead and through his hair as he continues to think. What have I done… Blood drips down from Aiden’s forehead onto the concrete… STATIC. ---------- Not my best rp ever, but I think the end showed some emotion.
