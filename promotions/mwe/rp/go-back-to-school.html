Go Back to School

Oct 25 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Mason: This is disgusting, vulgar, and pretty pathetic if you ask me. *The cameras zoom around and see Mason watching Sinc Mercier's promo. He has a nasty look on his face and even his gold watch can't make him look good at the moment.* Mason: I can not believe that you would go so low to say that you actually give a crap about this company. Sinc, you are a fool...more of a fool than I had anticipated and it will be fun showing you how to wrestle, this Sunday See, when I am called out, I don't back off and I don't turn down the challenge. *Mason calms down a little bit as a woman walks into the room. She is moderate height, about five foot seven, and has dark hair. Her eyes are brown and her legs are slender. She is extremely beautiful and she walks over to where Mason is.* Mason: Can you believe this garbage? ???: What is it? Not Sinc again? Mason: It is, look at the clip from his promo. QUOTE Sinc: The things on my mind at this very moment can not even begin to describe the constant CRAP I hear spilling out of everyone’s mouths. My vacation is over, and my game face is back on. I am here stating the fact of the matter right now, MWE is in my blood and my blood is in MWE, no matter how cocky I sound, how arrogant, how uncivil, how foolish. You can look into my eyes and see my truth. Call me whatever THE hell you WANT. But my feelings for this company and my passion will always remain the same. They will never EVER die. Sinc straightens his title as his voice echoes through the empty arena. Shadows flood the ring with only a single light streaming down from above glowing beside Sinc as he stands halfway into its brightness. Sinc: I can go on and on and on how a guy with a mask made a united states title a Mexican title just to show he can. Or how a Canadian plate smasher can talk so much about his opponents it’ll make your head spin. But there is the sheer facts that their time has come and gone. Where mine is just beginning. They have yet to realize that! Sinc begins walking around the ring and contemplating with himself a few moments then steps back into the light and smiles at the camera. *The woman shakes her head along side of Mason.* Mason: Am I right or am I wrong, Sara? Sara: Sinc makes me sick. Mason: Thank you, you can go do what you want now. Sara: Alright, see you later babe. *The woman stands up and walks out of the room as Mason is left with the television on. He stands up, and the screen goes black.* ***Scene Change*** *The cameras come back into view and Mason is in an empty club. He looking around at the elaborate patterns on the wall. Colors of pink, purple, magenta, and blue are implemented in all the titles and in all the lights.* Mason: So, I have been called a lot of things as of late. Harsh, ruthless, and even forgettable. However, one thing I have not been called is a liar, and that is for good reason. I may deceive people and they may think that a situation differs from what it actually is, but I do not LIE. No, I do not lie, so when I say that at Wrestle Wars II I am going to walk out as the new MWE United States champion, you know that I am telling the truth. Unlike my opponent, Sinc Mercier, I do not lie to anyone...not even myself. You see, Sinc, you may not know it yet, but know one cares about this company. All we care about, when it comes down to it, is winning and losing. All that you need to do now is accept it. And that is how Mayhem Wrestling Entertainment is divided. The people who will except their fate and their true opinions...and then those who believe lies and things that make no sense. *Mason takes a little bit and walks to one of the clubs tables. He brushes off the table of all that was on it, walks to the bar, pours himself a drink, and sets in on the table. He takes a swig and lets out a little burp. He is drinking out of a chilled mug and he puts it down. Mason:From day one, I was able to tell who is a fraud and who is real. Alejandro Rodriguez, as much as I hate to say it, is one of those people. He knows the truth and he knows how things operate in this company. He knows that being nice will get you absolutely nowhere and that is what you lack. Your time has just begun, that is true, but has my time come to end...no. I am not even in my prime yet and I know that I will still be able to teach you a few things. *Mason takes another drink of his beverage and lights up a cigarette, but does not put it into his mouth.* Mason: Sinc, you believe that my career is like this cigarette. Where after a few drags it will be out. I will show you that you are sadly mistaken. My career is not like a cigarette...no I am more like a cigarette. They say that ever time you smoke a cigarette you will lose seven minutes off your life. Well, in the same respect, when you walk into the ring with me this Sunday, I will cut a year off of your career. Like I said before, I don't care about this company...and really all I care about is winning or losing, so when we get into the ring, you will see the true meaning of that. That belt, that now rests on your shoulder...IT WILL BE MINE! *Mason lets off a sadistic laugh as he takes another drink of his beverage, whatever it may be.* Mason: And Alejandro, if you think that you are going to have any say in the matter, well...that is just as laughable as Sinc Mercier's feeling for Mayhem Wrestling Entertainment. *Mason lets off another little chuckle, less sadistic, however. He finishes off the small glass of whatever beverage and slides the mug away from himself. He snatches a chair and brings it to the middle of the dance floor as the cameras follow.* Mason: I always found it hard to take people seriously when they only come up to my penis. So, sorry, Alejandro but you are a joke. You brag about beating Sonny Williams? Why...why would you ever embarrass yourself by boasting about beating a man who couldn't wrestle his way out of a wet paper bag. I have already beaten Mr. Williams and I could do it again, so what is the difference between you and me. Oh yeah, about two feet of height and hell of a lot more charisma. Go back to school, because being able to fly won't do you any good this Sunday. *Mason looks at a big screen on the wall and a picture of Alejandro pops up. Mason walks to the screen with a pool stick in hand. He taps the screen.* Mason: To help you understand a little better, I have come up with a visual aid. Exhibit A is a picture of yourself Alejandro. *Mason taps the screen* This is Exhibit B. It is a picture of a school. See if you put the pictures together it will show you what you will have to do to have chance at beating Sinc, let alone myself. Go back to school before Sunday, or this Sunday I will take you to school myself. It is sad to know that one of the only other competitors in the business that knows the key to winning is so dumb and such a poor wrestler. It will be a shame to have to compete against such an incompetent fool. *Mason lets off another laugh that shocked the camera man a little bit. Mason then laughed again and walked back to his seat.* Mason: You see, I don't need to have an advantage to win this Sunday. I don't need to be as good as a wrestler as I am. I don't need a beautiful girlfriend and I don't need any luck. I will win this Sunday. I piss excellence and shit greatness! This Sunday I am Ying and Yang. I am Omega and Alpha. I am your god this Sunday and you will feel my wrath, my fury, and my drive to win. *Mason puts the chair back and decides to walk over to the bar again. He hops up top and sits looking at the camera ever more intently.* Mason: This Sunday, has more than just my match though. Brian Michaels v.s. D.C. happens and it will be a match to remember. However, I think it will be over quickly. Actually, I am almost sure that it will be over quickly. D.C., you better make quick work of Michaels, because if you don't, I will have to make quick work of both of you. This should have been over and done with many months ago, but that is what I get for being lazy and not doing the work myself. D.C., I softened him up for him, deliver the final blow, and end it. Your feud with that imbecile will be over this Sunday one way or another and if it isn't...well I may have to do something about it. *Mason looks around and then at his watch. He sighs and puts his sleeve back over his wrist.* Mason: Alejandro Rodriguez v.s. Sinc Mercier v.s. Mason Resoan for the Mayhem Wrestling Entertainment United States Championship this Sunday at Wrestle Wars II will be nothing short of a decimation. Sinc be prepared to lose the gold that James Sullivan failed to win from you in the first time, and Alejandro bring your pencils and papers and take some notes, because you are going back to school this Sunday so I can teach you what is really is like to have some skill. And if you didn't know...No Ya Know! *Mason laughs one more time, signals that the belt will be his, turns and runs out of the building after looking at his watch a final time!*[/center]
