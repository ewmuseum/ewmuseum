Extinguishing the Fire

Nov 30 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
*Mason Resoan is sitting with his back against the wall of a stone building, waiting...apparently...for something. There is lots of noise on the other side of the wall, but what it is, the cameras can not see. They swing around and look at the building's entrance. It looks as if it is mall of sorts, but the cameras are still unsure. Suddenly, the music and the noise in the background stop and only one man is heard.* Man: Ladies and gentlemen, it is now time for our special guest speaker to arrive on the stage. He is currently the number one contender for Mayhem Wrestling Entertainment's United States Championship. He is from this very fine city of Toronto and he is a classic. Not any classic, though, he is the Canadian Classic...Mason Resoan! *Mason, on que, walks from around the building and walks through the triple glass doors that happen to be the side entrance to the building. He is very a thin black shirt, with some sort of red fire pattern on the outside. There is clearly a shirt underneath this one, but it is not visible enough to read. Mason proceeds to walk up a miniature staircase to an elevated platform above a jam-packed building. He looks and sees some evident fans, but there are some that he has to really strain to see. After he finishes surveying the crowd...and the crowd settles down, he takes the microphone from the big man with the barbell mustache and places it on the stand.* Mason: Monday Night Mayhem...folks, it is only a night away. Only one night before I prove to Sinc Mercier that he is nothing more than a pushover and a nobody. Someone, who can be easily forgotten, who has not made a single impact thus far in MWE. Sure, he has won the MWE United States title twice, but is it really impressive? Especially if you look who he beat on both occasions...I mean, James Sullivan...really? If I was him, I wouldn't be boasting. And Sonny Williams...what is prestigious about a man who dropped from Champion to mid-card in less than a night? Don't answer that, because I already know. And that answer is nothing. there is nothing special about you Sinc Mercier...you don't deserve to be champion right now, and that is why I can not wait until Adrenaline rush. Oh, how sweet it will be to finally relieve you of a strap that you should have never touched in the first place...and if you believe that you will go up in the ranks...then I will laugh even harder than I did in my other promo. Beat a former MWE Champion besides Sonny Williams and then talk to me, until then, shut your mouth. *Mason takes a minute to pause and upon doing so, thinks hard for a second. He thinks and takes a little longer, but it finally hits him as he taps his chin.* Mason: Sonny Williams is a two-time MWE Champion, and yes, you have beaten him. But what is that supposed to do against me. I have beaten the best of the best...I have beaten them all. D.C, Brian Michaels, David Whitman, Sonny Williams, Alejandro Rodriguez, Aiden Copane, James Sullivan, Pala, you name em', I've beat them. You are the last one in my list that I have to beat...and then, I will have everyone beat. No one, not a single person worth wrestling will have walked out of a match with me saying they have a perfect record against me...and I will continue that tonight. Sinc, prepare yourself... *The crowd inside this mysterious building are very impressed and attentive, listening to Mason's every word. While Mason takes his pause, the camera zooms around the building, to reveal a setting of metal and tile...everywhere. Metal pipes and air vents, tile walls and floors, and even metal desks, benches and chairs. All the people in the crowd have some sort of MWE Merchandise on...and this must have been a conference of some sort, that Mason is starring in.* Mason: I seem to have noticed that you don't like to shut your mouth...and because of that, you call yourself a loud-mouth. I mean, I guess it makes sense. You annoying and obnoxiously loud...all the time, but I believe you refer to yourself in a different context. The kind that means that you are willing to state your opinion no matter what...the kind that means you don't care what people think of you...but how much sense does that make. You are always playing to the crowd and cheering them up when they feel uneasy...yet, you proceed to say you don't care about your fans right now. Pick a f***ing side and stick with it, you insecure b**ch. *Mason chuckles a little and takes a breath. He begins to sway back and forth.-* Mason: See, I have always picked my side...I never switch, I never let people down. I tell them the truth...flat out...every single time I speak to them. I let them know that I could care less about them. I let them know that when I have to do business, they are second on my priority list...and everyone else should follow that too. However, then we have people like you, a grown man, who acts like a 15 year old girl with her first boyfriend. One day you are sad and depressed, the next you are perky and spunky. So, when you lie, when you gain people's trust by letting them know you perform for them, you should stick with that. Not tell them, even though they deserve it, that you don't care about them. See, when I let people know that I hated them, after my split from CX, I was hated...by everyone. Now, people applaud me, praise me, and some even throw themselves at me, because they know that my actions are right and true. They know that when I say something, I mean it. I told them that I would not lose my match at Wrestle Wars II. You know what, I didn't lose the match. I walked out of the match the indifferent man. I didn't win...yet, I didn't lose. On the other hand, you walked in to Wrestle Wars II assuring everyone that you would obliterate me and the midget. Well, you were dead wrong. You decided to say something that you couldn't guarantee and when you lost, you resumed your 15 year-old girl persona. Don't lie to yourself this time around, admit defeat and it will be over. *When Mason pauses this time, the crowd goes wild for the mini-speech that Mason threw together on the spot. They whooped, cheered, and hollered for their hometown hero.* Mason: Sinc Mercier, you scream, you rant, and you make promises that you can't fulfill. So how does that make you look, respect wise? How are the guys in the back supposed to talk about you in a positive way. They may like you, at least now, but they still know that you are a liar and a cheat, with nothing going for yourself. When you step into the ring to me, you better take some caution, because if you don't I will rip you to pieces, walk away the victor, and once again, make you a liar. Then what? Are you going to get drunk on the side of the road again? Are you going to scream at your brother's gay employee? What will you do? You will retreat. You will reside, like a hermit crab, in your home trying to console yourself until Adrenaline Rush. And when you pop out of your shell, I will be waiting for you, because that is what I do. I wait, I pick my spots, and I win. I don't make statements by talking, I make statements by winning. By beating the hell out of people...and then when the dust clears, I let my track record do the talking. I have beaten the best of the best...so I have this to ask you. How can you beat me? How Sinc, how will you do it? You won't...that's all. I can break you down, shatter you, put you back together, and break you again...because you are a shell of what you were in the original MWE. In the old MWE, you had something going for you. You were starting to have people notice you, and then MWE went under. No we are back...and what happened? Lose your flare...your passion.....your real passion. *The crowd make many confused faces, with the occasional "huh?" heard by Mason. He smiles and laughs.* Mason: I still have my flare and my intensity, but you do not. You have this false sense of security that comes out as your so called "passion." However, I see right through it. I have my stuff, Whitman has his, and as much as I hate to say it, D.C still has it too. But what happened? Ring rust? Family problems? Sure, you have been winning, but how long will it last? How long can you keep it up? You need to get back the fire you once had, because on Monday, I am putting out this fake passion that you have been displaying as of late. I am the extinguisher to this fire, and I hope, that by Adrenaline Rush, you get your old fire back. I have said it before, but i will say it again, Adrenaline Rush is the real deal and there is no playing around. *The crowd is still semi-confused, but they understand what Mason is talking about a little better now.* Mason: Yes, I have been kind of nice so far, but don't let this warning blind you. I am not finished yet...oh no, not by a long shot. You need to fully realize that what I am capable of, is real. You make fun of me, because I participated in some indie fed during MWE's downfall, well what did you do? I watched EW, and I didn't see anything out of you. Nothing major, at least. I, on the other hand, went on to beat over 40 men in one night, something you will probably never get close to doing. And then you have the audacity to make fun of me...for losing the title match. Well, get your facts straight before you talk s*** next time, because I would have loved to see you even make it to the big dance that I was in, let alone win it past all of the bulls**t that was involved. And it amazes me so, that you make fun of me, while you act retarded, as usual. Make fun of Canadians and make fun of me in particular. How much sense does it make to make fun of my intelligence as you burn down a f***ing field? Save me...I saved myself, so you can save your own damn-self. I'll give you warnings all day long, but save you? Save you from what? Your own stupidity? I am sorry, but that can not be done... *Mason thinks for a second.* Mason: Especially when you talk about p***y and pancakes in the same sentence. *Mason shakes his head in disgust.* Mason: I would now like to prove how much of a retard you actually are. If everyone would pay attention, not that you need to because it is so blatantly obvious. Either way... *Mason points to a screen to his right, as a clip from Sinc's promo pops up.* QUOTE Sinc: Let me just start by saying that you need to get off Covell’s dick for a second and actually think about what your saying. Beating Sonny Williams is never that hard to anyone. All you need is a fake record label to throw at him and tell him sweet dreams every now and then and he is perfectly harmless, like a new born dumbass he’ll walk away thinking he is the greatest thing to ever NOT be main event material. So let me tell you this, I didn’t worry about Sonny Williams, or Alex Bowen, or James Sullivan. Why should I worry about someone who obviously can’t even worry about himself. You see….your too busy playing pin the syphilis on the Sara while I am worrying about more important things. *Mason laughs once more, but stops his swaying.* Mason: You claim that you are worrying about more important things...I would love to hear them. I mean, you completely contradict yourself all the time...it is starting to sound like you do it on purpose, to make yourself look like an idiot. Honestly, what is more important than your match. Your the United States Champion for crying out loud and you are not focusing on keeping the gold. Well, then this will make the victory even more easy than I was expecting. Monday night, I can assure you, will be hell, because based off of that clip alone, I am going to take you to school. No interference with any Mexicans either. I am taking you to school and I am going to show you why I am going to be the next MWE United States Champion. *Mason is looking throughout the crowd and is seeing loads of nods, majority of them are in approval. He smirks his evil smirk and takes a look at the screen again, as the clip continues to play over and over again. He stops the screen and up pops a picture of the MWE United States Championship.* Mason: This, for us, is what it is all about. Personally issues aside...and beyond anything else, this should be our top priority. Nothing should stand in the way of a great match at Adrenaline Rush. So, throw all your little bitch problems away, like you brother, your ex-wife, and your problem with getting it up in bed...and focus. Focus as hard as you can. Take a good look at me, my strategy, and my life, because you're going to need all the help you can get. Let Devlin Sharpe be an example of what I'll do. I don't care if your back is turned or if it is an impromptu match, you should be ready at all times to be in a brawl. It's not like it hasn't happened to me before, so don't complain if I take advantage when you decide to scream your name or when you decide to stand on the top-rope and pander to your fans...that you care about...but you don't care about now...but you would do anything for...like I said, pick a side and stick with it. *Mason takes a deep breath, backs up, and steps forward once more.* Mason: And if ya didn't know...now ya know! --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- OOC: Told you Sinc, I knew I would contradict myself, ;).
