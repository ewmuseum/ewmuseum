Time to rewrite history, without a pen… MWE cameras open up on the skyline of Miami, Florida, showing South Florida’s exotic sights and sounds. Being that it’s Michaels’ hometown, he is driving around town, just chilling, until he sees his favorite movie theatre, Sawgrass Mills 23. As he pulls his Mercedes into the parking lot, he notices that there is a huge line of people, most of them teenagers looking to sneak in to watch the R-Rated movie, The Hangover. As they are devising their plans to buy tickets for Land of the Lost, Michaels walks out of his Mercedes with a major swagger. Aviators cover his light green eyes, full of mystery and curiosity. He is wearing khaki cargo shorts, white and red Nikes, and a red MICHAELS INC. shirt with an X in the middle. The camera begins to follow Michaels, who is angrily stomping towards the front of the line, and also pans to show some of the younger members in the line who immediately stare at Michaels in disbelief, not knowing he lived in Miami. Michaels: I AIN’T MAKING THAT LINE! I’M RICH, MOTHERFUCKER! In typical Brian Michaels fashion, he barges through to the front of line, and pushes a young blonde lady to the side and leans into the speaker to ask for tickets. The 18 year old brunette working the counter, begins to rapidly chew her gum as she recognizes Michaels’ face. Michaels: HEY BITCH! GIMME TICKETS TO THE HANGOVER! The teenage girl doesn’t hesitate to ask for money, intimidated with Michaels’ presence. The red-and-blue tickets are handed right to Michaels, and he snags them, walking off with a huge swagger. Michaels: Swallow that gum too…it’ll help you in the future. Trust me. He gives her a smirk before opening the large theatre doors and walking in. The first thing that catches his eye is a fellow that looks STRANGELY like Seth Rogan, who is grabbing the tickets and telling people where to go. He is dressed in the official movie uniform, and the same that the brunette was dressed in, a maroon polo shirt with an “SM” for Sawgrass Mills at the top in black. He reaches for Michaels’ ticket, but Michaels pulls back and gives the young, bearded overweight fellow a grunt. Michaels: Fuck off, I know where it is. With that, he begins his journey to the food line. He stands there for a second, obviously irritated, before a random blonde kid, blue eyes, with buckteeth, looking about 10 years old, comes and kicks Michaels in the shin. He proceeds to run away laughing, and Michaels begins to degrade him right away. Michaels: YOU LITTLE NAZI! Get the fuck back here… He throws his hands in the air, deciding that the kid is not worth his time. A few people look back at him, wondering what the Nazi comment was all about. Suddenly, all the Jewish people in the line begin to run amuck, screaming and ripping their hair out. Jews: NAZIS?! WHERE?! A brief cameo from Rev-Pro's The High Kid is seen. He too, is running amuck. Michaels: CALM DOWN. The kid was blonde and had blue eyes. It was a joke…JEEZ. He silently mutters under his breath, before finally walking into the cinema and trying to pick his seat through the nearly-packed audience. He sees one near the front, and steps over a drunk brunette to do so. He chugs on his soda, drinking damn near the whole thing, before he comes to a realization. He needs to go to the bathroom. Shit. Right when the opening credits are starting too. He looks down, almost as if to ask his bladder if it can make it another hour and a half. Uh, no. He kicks her again for good measure, and grins before she mutters a “What the hell?” Michaels: My bad… She rolls her eyes, and Michaels goes down the alley (the thing you use to exit the cinema and go to the bathroom). After walking back outside, he encounters the two teenagers with the skateboard and sees them leaning against the wall, and smirks to himself for a second, before running and grabbing their skateboard from them, which was leaning next to them. He runs off grinning towards a trash can, and the boys go to chase him, irritated.
