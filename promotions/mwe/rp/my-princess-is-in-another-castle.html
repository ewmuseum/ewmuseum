My Princess is in Another Castle; Derf/Seton VS Oblivion/Milenko

Sep 24 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
OOC: In my last Role Play, Eric Derf got a letter from a woman named "Katie", saying to meet him someplace. No other explanation was given as to who this woman was, or what she wanted. But after thinking about it, Eric decided to agree to the terms in the letter, and met the woman. When he finally met her, it was revealed she was a slightly overweight middle aged woman, who seemed excited to meet Eric. And after a few moments of Eric wondering who this woman truly was, it was revealed that she was the woman he'd hooked up with at DisneyVille, also known as "Snow White", which brings us to... Katie stares deep into Erics eyes, still holding his hands, the look you'd expect a guy to give a girl right before a proposal, not the other way around. And then she drops the bombshell. Eric... I'm Snow White! Eric stares at her for a moment. The look on his face says alot of things. One is, he thinks she is lying. Another, is saying "maybe I was really that drunk". But it's what his voice says that matters. ... Really? It didn't sound cheesy, or confused. It was more or less excited sounding, which couldn't be worse, as Katie takes this as a good sign, and wraps her arms around him in excitement. She seems like she just had her lifetime wish granted. It's hard to tell what Eric is thinking. Wow, this is great! I was really hoping I could find you, that night was so much fun! Yeah, it was a hoot!... I had so much fun., I can barely remember! Eric gets these words out of his mouth while somehow holding in the vomit, he clearly made a mistake. Children, this is why you shouldn't drink much. Oh my god, it was great! I mean, to think that I just met a professional wrestler, and damn, you're one hell of a wrestler! When we got back to my hotel room, you- Whoa! No need to remind me, I think I remember how it went down! Of course he don't remember what happened. Nor does he want to be reminded. He's just trying to think of a way to get out of this awful uncomfortable situation without looking like a dickhead. But Katie is to oblivious to notice how weird Eric is acting towards her. Oh, you were great... This is nice... She's still hugging him, so lucky for Eric that she can't see his face, it looks like he's seen a ghost. How would you feel if you just found out you slept with a woman that looked like her! And then one of the greatest sounds of all happens. A small beep. Just, a plain "beep". Oh! Text message! Eric goes to reach between himself and Katie to get to his pocket, which causes her to let go of the hug. But she still stands there, one arm on Erics side, as he nervously checks his phone to see what the message was. "Hey Eric, Josh here. Can I eat the last piece of chicken in your fridge?" Eric looks at the phone, then at Katie. His lips start to quiver, his eyes start to water, and suddenly, he bursts into tears. Katie is shocked at this, and quickly grabs a chair and sets it behind Eric so he can sit down. She don't know why he's upset. Oh my god baby, what's wrong! Eric looks down at his phone, the text message Josh just sent him still on the screen. My mom just texted me... My dog, 14 years old, just got hit by a car. They don't think he's going to make it... I gotta go! What a lie! But it seems to have worked. Without giving her a chance to say anything to him, Eric quickly gets up off the seat and runs out of the restaurant, throwing both doors open as he goes. Katie is left standing there, now partially in tears too, still under the impression Erics dog is dying. After running for a few moments, Eric reaches the front door of the building he lives in. He wipes the fake tears from his eyes and makes his way to the elevator. While he stands there waiting for the door to close, his phone beeps again. Another text message. "Omg Eric, how's your dog? I'm so upset about him! I hope he makes it. Let me know if there is anything I can do to help you feel better. <3" Obviously, this text message is from Katie. How the hell did she get his number? This woman seems a little nuts, if you ask me. As Eric stares at his phone a moment longer, the elevator door opens, and he makes the short trek to his apartment door. He goes to open it, it's not locked, so he just goes on in. A voice on the inside shouts something out. Hey Eric, that you? The voice sounds muffles, as if something was covering the persons mouth. Eric ignores the odd sounding voice. Yeah, it's me. Wasn't gone to long, was I? The source of the voice, Erics friend Josh, walks through a doorway with a chicken leg in his hand, his jaw moving with a chuck of chicken inside it. He talks with his mouth full, once again making it seems muffled. No, but I can understand you getting back if your dog was dying. Eric nods for a moment and goes to sit down. His ass literally hits the cushion, and as if their was a pin on it, he leaps back up. Wait, my dog is dying? What are you talking about? Eric only made that up to Katie as a quick escape route, so why would Josh be thinking the same thing. Oh, I got a text message from someone, saying your dog was hit by a car. They said they wanted to know if you were ok. Eric gets up and walks towards Josh, and without even asking him anything, reaches into his front pocket and pulls out his cellphone. Josh, caught off guard, tries to take a step backwards. Whoa man! Hands away from the goods! He ignores this and flips open Joshs phone, while opening his own phone at the same time. He hits a few buttons on both phones before looking at Josh, the look on his face one that a detective would make right after figuring out the final clue to a huge mystery. She's got both our numbers. How the hell did she get both our numbers?! Who? Fuckin' Katie! Josh laughs with the chicken in his mouth, he'd completely forgotten that's what Eric was doing all this time. Oh yeah! How is she? She a looker? Did you stick it to her? What did she want? Eric looks at Josh, a cold damp stare. Dude, not even funny. She's like fucking 45 or something. And she's got the body of a giant water balloon full of pudding. Eww. What did she want? Josh didn't sound to grossed out, his expression gave off the impression he didn't care about her looks anymore. She wanted... me. She must have thought that night at DisneyVille meant more then it was, so she- Wait, what? She's the girl you were talking about? Just in case someone is out of the loop, a while back Eric slept with a "hot chick" at an amusement park, but was really drunk when it happened. He though she was hot, now, he knows otherwise. And for a guy that regrets almost nothing, Eric sure is thinking of quitting drinking right about now. Dude, lets just drop it. I got drunk, made a mistake, and now I got some cougar on my ass thinking I'm Mr. Right. I'm 21 years old, she's like twice my age! Eric sits back down. Josh is still standing, still eating the chicken leg. Ya know, if ya marry someone older, and they die before you, you get all their money... Dude, not even funny. I think I have a stalker on my hands. What am I gonna do about it? There is a silence in the room for a few moments. Finally, Josh chucks the bone from the chicken leg into a nearby garbage can, looks at Eric, and speaks with a dead serious tone. Wanna play Battleship? Yeah sure, whatever gets me in a better mood... Fuck I can't wait till Monday, big ol Tag team match... Keep in mind, what's going on right now took place BEFORE Monday Night Mayhem and the events that unfolded throughout the night. As of right now, Eric is still excited about a 6 man tag match, and as Josh sets the Battleship box on the coffee table in front of them, they begin chatting about that match, trying to get their minds off the elephant in the room. --- Fast forward a few days. It's now the night of Monday Night Mayhem, or should we say, the next day. It's past midnight, and the show literally just ended. The cameras just went off the air, but Eric Derf and Zack Crash, both fresh off their victory over DC and the World Champion Grand Mystique, are walking around the ring, high fiving the fans that paid so much money to come and see them. DC and GM have both already made their way backstage, and as Eric and Zack reach the top of the ramp, they strike a pose one last time, holding up the MITB Briefcase and XTV Title, respectively, before finally retreating behind the curtain as Harrys thanks everyone for coming out. As soon as their backstage, they part ways, and go about the usual backstage business. Eric makes his way down a long corridor, still sweating from his match. He grabs a towel off a table he passes and begins to wipe himself down with one hand, his other hand continues to hold MITB. After a short walk down a long hall, he reaches the door he wanted to reach. He knocks on the door a few times, but no response. He puts his ear to the door, and can clearly hear at least one voice on the inside, so he knocks a again. This time, he can clearly hear what's being said. "Oh can you hold for a moment?... yeah... WHAT THE FUCK DO YA WANT?" Eric thinks if this as "Please come in", so he opens the door to see Eli Silver sitting in his chair, holding a cell phone in his hand, but looking at Eric. The phone is on hold, so he's not setting it down, he probably expects this to be quick. Oh, it's You, Mr Money in the Bank, Eric Derf! He responds in a sarcastic voice, still wondering what the hell he wants. Let me guess. You want a raise right? I gave one to DC, I've got the World Champion down my back for more money, and now, I bet you feel you deserve more cash as well, am I right? I want a match, against DC, next week. Eli looks at Derf for a moment, his eyes and expression telling one story, not likely the one Eric wants to hear. He hits a button on his phone and brings it back to his ear. Yeah, I got something to take care of. I'll give ya a shout back later. Both Eric and Eli are starting into each others eyes as Eli closes his phone with one hand. As soon as it clicks shut, Eric speaks. I want a match against DC, next- I heard ya the first fuckin' time. You want a match? I want a solid gold toilet. Eric doesn't respond to this, which in turn allows Eli to continue speaking. Look kid, you're hot stuff here. You got a bright future, plenty of years ahead of ya, if ya want. Tell me, why would you want to throw that away by pissing me off? I'm not hear to upset you, or threaten you, but I feel I deserve a match against DC. He's going around saying he wants a challenge from a younger guy. I'm a younger guy looking for a challenge myself. Yes, and? Eli sounds like he couldn't care less, so Eric has got to sell this pretty well. Look Eli, I know you're all about the money. And you know I'm one of the fastest rising guys you got here. And DC, he's always in big money matches. So imagine, the two of us, next week. Think of the money that would make. Eli looks like he's thinking about it as he gets out of his seat. Well, I guess it sucks for you then, because I already got next weeks card set up, and DC is already booked against Zak Warner, a man that has never faced him before. And besides, you have already faced DC, and you beat my man there. Why do you got your panties in such a bunch about a re-match? Eric sets the briefcase on the table, not to make a point, just for the sake of setting it down. Last time we fought, it was a tribute show for Tim Fitzgerald. He was emotional, and I took advantage, and now, whenever that match is brought up, people take away from it by saying "He let me win" or "he wasn't all there" or something. I wanna prove it wasn't a fluke. I want a one on one shot at DC to prove I'm in his league. Well, you'll get your match, someday. But if you don't stop being so impatient, you'll get nothing. Eric can't help but snicker at this as he looks away for a moment, he can't look Eli in the eyes after such a petty thing has been said. Eli, Eli, Eli... Me, impatient? Clearly, you know I'm not impatient. If I was impatient, I would have just come in here and demanded a match. If I was impatient... He looks down at the table, at his guaranteed title shot sitting on the desk. If I was impatient, I would be standing here with the World Heavyweight Championship. I wouldn't have left that ring moments ago without picking the scraps of the World Champion that I just pinned. If I was impatient, it would be the best thing to happen to me at this point in my career. Eli looks annoyed. (Theres some old school Dev description for ya) The fuck is your point? I'm not impatient. I want a match against DC, and as long as you can guarantee I'll get that match, whenever, wherever. I'll be happy. Deal? Eli looks at Eric, who's got his hand out for a handshake. All Eric really wants is a match against DC at some point in time. I can't make that deal. You want a match against DC? Go talk to him about it, I don't wanna hear another word from either of you bitching and moaning about one another. Now, you gotta get your ass ready for the next show. I've got you booked in another tag team match. Eric is annoyed he didn't get what he wanted, but he's not gonna make a fit about it. He knows if you want something, you gotta earn it. And if you wanna prove your the best, just defeat everyone that tries to stand in your way. Who's in the match? Well, I managed to scrape together a few guys you're a bit familiar with. One of your opponents is Oblivion, you two met in MITB. The little freak almost beat you. And? Eli sits back down in his seat and kicks his legs onto his desk, with a smug smile on his face. I just signed a new contract for, lets say, and old friend of yours. A "Cory Bowen", if the name rings a bell. Eric knows exactly who Cory Bowen is. The Great Milenko. You resigned Milenko? Good. We never got our rubber match. Well I'm glad your happy. Because I also just signed your partner, and for your sake she's as good as her hype. Eric nods in agreement. The fact that Eli said "SHE'S as good as HER hype" gives Eric the impression his partner is Madison Seton, the new female on the roster. But that part don't faze Eric much. Alright, sounds fun... I'll be back after I win next week to see if you got a match for me and DC in the works. You do that, and you're match will never happen. Until you two figure it out among yourselves, I don't wanna- Eli is cut off by his cell phone ringing. He holds up his finger to Eric, implying for him to hold on a moment. Hello?... What, Who?.......... Don't ever call this line again, this ain't a fucking hotline, and I don't care if your sons phone is dead, get ahold of him some other way! God dammit! Eli snaps his phone shut and stares at Eric, a slight smirk on his face. Eric, boy, if ANYONE ever calls me looking for you again, you'll be jobbing to a leprechaun for a month! Erics face is puzzled, what is Eli talking about? But not before long, it clues in. That fucking Katie chick is still trying to get ahold of him, and now she's calling Eli to get to him. Look Eli, she's don't represent me. She calls again, say what you want to her, I couldn't care less... And one last thing. Eli looks at Eric as he picks up his briefcase and holds it into the air for a moment. When DC and GM face off, if DC happens to win, I will use this to get my rematch, even if it means taking the title off of him right away. You can call it impatient, I call it killing two birds with one stone. A bad example of wordplay, but Eric don't care, he turns away from Eli and exits the room, Eli's face looks like he couldn't give a shit. Now in the hallway, Eric begins walking towards the dressing room. He walks past the table where he earlier grabbed a towel, and tosses his used towel on top of the bunch, just to fuck with whoever comes by after him. As he continues down the hall, a voice calls out to him. Yo boy, you got a minute or two? Confused, Eric turns around to see new MWE Interviewer Samuel Steele approaching him from behind, mic in hand. Eric see's this and smiles, he could go for an interview right now, after all, he just had the biggest win of his career. Yo Sam, good to see you back again. Yeah, I got a few minutes for an old homeboy! Eric was talking in a very fake gangster ascent, trying to play up to Sam, who chuckled at it. Yo dog, I was just wondering if I could get a few words about your tag match next week? I mean, you're teaming up with a chick, fighting a couple of masked freaks, what you gonna do bout it? Erics smile slowly fades, he wanted to brag about his recent win, not what he's going to do in his tag match, which he just found out about moments earlier. But, not one to disappoint, Eric answers the question. Well Sam, like you said, I got a debuting female as a partner, which could be good, could be bad. Females in MWE are always hit and miss, I don't think we've ever had a middle ground. And now, Madison Seton is coming into MWE. An athletic girl, just starting collage. Plays sports, has been wrestling for a little while, you're average girl, ya know? Umm... No dog, I like my girls in mini skirts and big ol' butts. Eric laughs at this, gotta love an interviewer that can crack a joke. After a moment, he continues. Yeah, I suppose. But she seem's cool to me. Can't really talk her up much, I haven't seen her wrestle yet. But I can tell you this. Her debut match won't soon be forgotten. Not because of her though, but because this match is going to be another stepping stone for my quick climb to the top of MWE. Because, like Madison Seton, I am in it, to win it. And I am going to win this match, and eventually... He holds up his Briefcase for the camera to see, and Samuel Steele takes a good look at it as well. I am going to become the MWE Heavyweight Champion. He pauses for a moment to let the comment sink in, before continuing. He talked up his partner, now, time to talk down his opponents. So, I guess you want to know my thoughts on the guys I'm facing eh? Well, we got the Bong Smokin', Thong Stealing, Man with a Shlong like King Kong, The masked freak of MWE himself, Captain Obvious! Yo man, I think it's "Oblivion", not "Obvious". No man, I think it's obvious. I think it's obvious that he's got a problem with the world. He had a tough break before, and he's letting it take over his life. He hates the world because he's jealous of what everyone else has. It's obvious to me though, that if he wants to get someplace in life, he shouldn't be taking handmedowns. He should get off his ass and win dammit! A few weeks ago, he had a golden opportunity, he got to fight for the Money in the Bank briefcase. But, of course, he lost. Not his fault though, it's just cause there was someone better to beat him there. He pauses for a moment, almost as if he wanted Samuel Steele to ask "Who do you mean?" But both men were smarter then that, so he continued. But joking aside, Oblivion has a problem. Even when he gets what he wants, he still won't be happy. Because this is a man that always needs a thrill in his life. Wether it be from kidnapping his ex girlfriend, to fucking her sister, and all the shit he snorts up his nose inbetween, he needs a high. He needs the thrill of doing something dangerous to himself and others. Well, he sure picked the right career, but next week, he's stuck with the wrong oppo- Eric is cut off immediately by Samuels cell phone ringing. Samuel reaches into his pocket and pulls out one of those old school blocky cellphone things, the kind of thing only used now as a paperweight in the middle east. Yo... What? Ho, stop calling me, call the cracka yo'self! He rudely hangs up his cell phone before shoving it back into his pocket, barely, and looks towards Derf. Yo dawg, you gotta get that bitch of yours on a tighter leash, she's been calling all o' us all night, trying to get to you. Get a damn cell phone or somt'in Uh, yeah, that bitch is crazy as shit, I don't even know her... Anyway, back to the interview? Ya man, you was talking some shit about Oblivion? Yeah, Oblivion. Well, I don't really know what else to say. Next week, when he and I face off, I can already guess how things are going to end. The same way they ended at Crowning Glory. Him lying on his back, with me standing tall above him, prize in hand. But then again, that won't be all his fault if he loses. I mean, look at his partner! Eric is of course talking about Milenko, the only man that Eric has ever faced TWICE in singles action. Milenko has returned! Oh thank the loud, the Hardcore Juggalo has returned! He speaks in a mocking tone, like he's preaching or something. It would be funnier if you could actually see it in person. Ladies and Gentlemen, for those of you that haven't seen this man before, gather round for a spectacle like no other! Come next show, for... probably, one night only, witness the power of a man that has specialized in weapons, a man with a troubled past, who uses it as a crutch to keep himself going today. Witness the return, of THE GREAT MILENKO!... Well, I suppose he was never that great... Samuel Steele lets out a single "HA" as Eric continues. Seriously, he and I have fought twice. The first time, he beat me. I won't make no excuse. He got lucky. The second time however, with both of our titles on the line in a unification match... He completely dropped the ball. I don't mean he just lost the match, but he literally gave it up! He tossed the match, dropped his title, and disappeared, ashamed of what he had done. And now, upon returning, he gets another match against me, this time in a tag team match. But that won't make the outcome that much different. Eric turns around for a moment, eyeing the buffet table a few feet away. He quickly makes a short dash for the table, grabs what looks like a muffin, and comes back to Sam Steele, one would assume to continue the interview, only now he's got food in his mouth while he talks. Ya know, it's kinda hard to take Milenko serious... (get it?) The man is a joke. (get it again?) He comes out here, asking for understanding one day because of his troubled past, then tries to brutally beat down his opponents the next day. Dude, no one cares about what happened in your past. I had a bad past, my father is dead, but do you hear me out here every single week using that as an excuse to want to win a match? NO! When your past deals you a shitty hand, learn to fold it and get some new cards. Don't go all in and try to bluff, cause you will be burned. And all of this was said while Eric had a chuck of muffin in his mouth. How could you have taken HIM seriously? But now the muffin has been swallowed, he looks into the camera and wipes the crumbs from his mouth. BTW, incase you think him grabbing a muffin was random... everyone has to eat, and he just had a match. Don't hold it against him. Besides muffins are good. Just make sure you don't only eat the tops and toss the bottoms, that's bad luck... Anyway, Eric just started looking into the camera, so you know this is where he tries to get serious again. Milenko, Oblivion, in two weeks, you two will form one of the oddest tag teams in MWE history. And unfortunately for you, the makeshift tag team across from you consists of a girl who is "In it, to win it", and the man that will soon become your NEW World Heavyweight Champion! He holds the briefcase high above his head. Just like tonight, when I pinned Grand Mystique in that ring, you two will fall, I will stand tall, and that that is all. Samuel, thanks for the time. Yo dog, no problem. Eric goes to turn away, but stops for a moment before turning to look back at Samuel Steele one last time, a slightly evil look on his face. And when Eric gets an evil look on his face, nothing bad ever happens, so... By the way Samuel, did you ever get revenge on the guy that got you thrown out of the company? Naw dog, he wore a mask, I don't know who he is, but if he could beat me, damn, man ought to be good. Yeah, I didn't think you would remember him... and he is pretty damn impressive in the ring... Eric turns around and starts his walk towards the dressing room. He's smiling now at the recent PWNage on Samuel Steele, ya know, since it was Eric Derf himself that sent him packing the first time around. And among that smile, is a mouth now filled with more muffin, remember, eat the bottoms too! Finally, after about 15 seconds or so, he reaches the locker room. Most of the guys have already cleared out by now, a few refs linger around. Eric walks towards his locker, and as he reaches for it, a single beep is heard. Holy fuck Eric, answer your damn phone! Eric looks to his right to see ring announcer Fred Carry, now dressed in jeans and a t-shirt. Dude, every single time I come back here, that damn cell phone of yours is ringing, and even before your match, I got a phone call from some woman asking for you. I don't know who that chick is, but she ought to learn to respect other peoples lives, and stop calling us to get to you! Eric ignores the message altogether and replies to something else instead. Fred man, you sound alot nicer out there, more professional if you will. Dude, just answer your phone. Yeah alright... I don't know how this woman keeps finding out everyones numbers, I don't even know how she got MY number! Eric nods and laughs as he finally opens his locker up. Just because of all the calls, he decides to check his phone before anything. And it ain't pretty what he finds. Holy shit! I got 47 Phone calls since the start of the show, and 25 text messages! He goes to check the calls, see who any of them were. Should be obvious by now though... Hmm... Katie, Katie, Katie... They're all Katie! He tinkers around with his cell phone for a few moments, until he gets to the auto delete of all calls and text messages with that number, and instantly, all but one call is gone. Eric recognizes the one remaining phone number and decides to call it. He takes a seat on a bench next to his locker as he waits for the other end of the line to answer. Finally, a voice replies. Hello? Hey Matt? It's Eric. You called? Yeah man, first off, congrats on pinning the champ tonight. You should have cashed in, it would have been awesome. Yeah, thanks man. I could have, but I got plans on when I want to do that. Anything else going on man? I gotta get changed and get out of here ya know. Yeah, one other thing... Get that damn cougar of yours to stop calling me! And then Matt hangs up, only to further his pretend anger at Eric. Eric knows he's not super pissed, so he just laughs as he tosses his phone lightly into his locker and begins changing. This week is over, now he's gotta get through life until the next MNM. And mark my words, he won't disappoint. Bank on it.
