A David Whitman RP.; A Description Sandwich with Dialog Buns

Mar 1 2009

David Whitman

I require Grapeseed. Not in oil-form. A singular grapeseed. This is the most ridiculous conversation ever. And you, sir, are the most ridiculous person ever. … You still there? Yeah, it’s just… It never ends, does it? Du’nt seem that way, no. D.C. and Alejandro, Grand Mystique, Joey Dallas… now Ace Cannon. Well you ARE the champion. You represent the company. Thing is, we have all these unbelievable athletes- it’s like we don’t have divisions. You know what I mean? Everyone can beat everyone. And when every one of those everyone-beaters is thrown at you in rapid succession- Well, I’m just saying it’s a lot to handle. Well yeah. I don’t doubt that. But we all have faith in you. And, more importantly- you have faith in yourself. Yeah. I do. Mm. And hey, if you beat Ace Cannon you’ll at least have a win over Michaels’ BROTHER. Oh, HA-HA. Don’t you have someplace to go or sumn? Heheh-Oh right, I gotta get myself washed up for the audition. Yeah. Good luck. Sorry about the whole TRT thing, it’s just that- with me and James not on speaking terms, an- Hey, you don’t have to explain anything to me. Look, I’ll call you later tonight, and we can talk longer, kay? Alright alright. Get the hell out of here. Chaha. Click. David Whitman, clad in his fresh, new, and as the kids would say, "pimpin'" black “Checklist” shirt and signature baggy black pants, closes his phone. Sitting on the bed in the room in the Sylvan Hill House B&B in Limerick in Ireland in Europe on earth, he takes a solemn, content sigh. With Monday Night Mayhem but a day away, he is taking a bit of time to relax and unwind. The cozy B&B, nestled closely to the Mourne and Dromara mountains, offers the perfect feel for David’s purposes. And now I’m gonna try to not sound so much like a catalog with the description, by talking about his feelings. His feelings… what of them? Nothing else but your average mixture of happiness, sorrow, confusion, anger… Happiness, knowing how well he has been doing lately. Sorrow, which comes with missing close friends and family. The confusion about which path to take, life-wise as well as literally speaking, since he’s in Ireland. And of course, anger… angry at having to perform so hard, week in, week out, against the best wrestlers in the world. And at himself for not knowing exactly how his next match will go. It is an internal struggle that is beautifully and inexplicably brought to balance by external peace. Ah, Balance. Such is the way of the world. Of course, if he could have it his way, he would much rather turn it all around. Trade the inner battle and external tranquility for internal serenity and outer conflict. Oh, but don’t worry. He will. As he and many others often do. In the ring. At MNM. Where all your troubles melt away, and your mind focuses on just one thing, the matter at hand; getting a win. Many of them don’t even realize it, but that is one of the big reasons why they do what they do. Grasping at some semblance of control over their life, wrestling becomes like a drug; a chance to spend some time lost in another world. Still sitting there, quietly, it is this exact thought that the MWE Champion is thinking. About his opponent, Ace Cannon. And how he is. Or rather, WHAT he is. A sick f*ck. Ace Cannon had said it best himself in his latest promo, and it had gotten David to thinking. Is that why Cannon wrestles? Because David has come to the conclusion that it has a lot to do with why HE wrestles. And Cannon, having had the… interesting life he had… it would make perfect sense. He wrestles for control. Even though he might not know it. So we could add that striking similarity to the fact that Cannon and Whitman are quite evenly matched, at the same height and roughly the same weight, and technically speaking as well. Though Ace Cannon prefers a brutal and powerful ground attack, David Whitman has come to prefer high-risk a bit more. But, again, balance. Of course, the funny thing, that did not escape David, was the timing. While Cannon’s psychological issues are at an all-time high and strength, David is just coming from that place. That dark place, where you don’t know whether to stab someone in the heart with a rusty knife or use that same knife to slit your own neck wide open. The difference being that while Ace Cannon has gotten to act out most of his nightmares on unsuspecting, as well as less so, victims, David had to struggle to overcome his own problems. Most might say that that is what would give David the edge here. But this is what he’s trying to figure out right now. Sure, most therapists and whatnot would tell you that overcoming a problem instead of giving in to it is the healthy thing to do, but… Does Ace Cannon have the upper hand? Does he, in acting out his innermost sick situations, free himself every time? And is he really going into this match wanting to hurt David, just because he’s the one in there? As the sun slowly sets, David, still sitting on the bed, lets out another sigh. His thoughts are coming dangerously close to doubt; Doubt about the outcome of the match and how he will perform in it in general. But this is of no consequence. After all, soon enough he will be brimming with confidence as he steps into the ring to face another of MWE’s monsters. And while the odds might not be as in his favor as one would want them to be, he knows he can disarm the Cannon. Yet, with all of these thoughts, another stray one enters David’s mind, and he jumps up off the bed. His one promo a month minimum clause. Despite still not being big on promos, he wants to get March’s one out of the way. And what better time to use this opportunity? -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- About two hours later, at a very generic house in Northern Ireland. A young MWE fan, sitting in his room, turns on the radio. Between slight static, a driving rock song can be heard, and the boy jumps up and down, in that markout fashion, and not for the song. But, because he knows what is coming up next. As the song comes to a close, a man’s voice stumbles through the slight static. And that was a classic by Saliva, you know how we do, kickin’ it old school! And speaking of kicking, I promised you all a minute or two of a special guest star here tonight, and he’s right here next to me. How does that have anything to do with kicking? Well, he’s facing off against the King of Kicks, Ace Cannon. That was horrible, dude. I know HA but still, anyways, listeners, I give you… David Whitman! No MWE champion? No Hall of Famer? You’re not gonna build me up? Just get on with it. Haha… all right. Well… a minute or two. Let’s see… What should I say? I thought you were gonna rant on Cannon? Well, RANT. I mean I COULD, but what would I say about him? I mean, I COULD mention how he claims I get more money than Lucas to produce promos while he’s sitting on a private jet and out buying, and I quote, “Huge Mansions in the Caribbean”, how he scares Lindsey witless which is proven by how her talking becomes so strained when she’s with him, how apparently he saw fear in my eyes when he attacked Dallas even though his attention was solely ON Dallas and I was pretty out of it after beating the guy who kicked his @$$ at AR2, to name one time... I mean I COULD say that? We- Or maybe I could talk about how he says I am now the hunter instead of the hunted, even though I’ve pretty much BEEN the hunted since D.C./Rodriguez/Whitman, and how I won every one of those matches? You know what, inflicting pain is what drives you, right, Cannon? Then I’m gonna do you a favor. I want you to come into this match and unleash everything you have on me. Have a ball. You’ll be in heaven. But remember that apparently, RECEIVING pain is what drives me, cuz the harder my matches get, the more I keep on winning. And maybe the gold doesn’t mean anything to you, but you forget what that gold means. It means I beat Dallas. It means I beat countless other monsters. So once I take you out as well, the title won’t be of much relevance. Heck, it isn’t even on the line. But when I look at it after our match, I’ll smile, and sigh contently, as I often do, and remember back to the time I defeated Ace Cannon in that ring. Thank you, and BAH BAH! Uh, hello? Mr. Whitman? Well I guess that’s it for us, uh… I urge you all to tune in next time, and remember to catch Monday Night Mayhem!
