Lesser Of Two Evils; Zack Crash CG RP

Aug 18 2010

Zack Crash

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
“All evils are equal, when they are taken to the extreme.” Thug Leader: I ain’t gonna ask ya again! Hand over all yer money…right…now! Zack stood surrounded on all sides in a circle of thugs. As he looked around, hoping to find an escape that wasn’t there, he saw as men brandished their weapons of lead pipes, baseball bats, steel chains, or good old-fashioned bare knuckles. Zack’s mind came up with several different scenarios as to how to get out of this predicament with his skin intact, but each was as hopeless as the last. It seemed the “Ultimate Thrill Seeker” had finally bitten off more than he could chew. As his situation grew bleaker by the second, Zack wondered of how he got into this situation in the first place. == + == + == == + == + == Night had finally fallen, and the great city of New York was slowly being shrouded in a blanket of black. Lights in the tall buildings lining the skyline turned on one at a time as the city began to shift from day life to nightlife. Zack wondered the city, turning and walking down random streets and sidewalks. He had no real destination he was heading towards, which he felt was right now sadly similar to his life. Zack had come to the city a few days early to prepare for his match at Crowning Glory IV, but now that match felt more like an axe over his head than a blessing. Zack had never before had an identity crisis before, but now he wasn’t even sure he knew himself anymore. It almost seemed like everyone knew more about who Zack Crash was than Zack Crash himself did. For weeks now, Zack had been questioning himself and his motives, wondering if maybe Virus was right about him. And his lose on MNM to Alexis Black had sent his doubt into overdrive. Now Zack wasn’t sure of anything. All he knew was that he was afraid. Afraid of Virus, afraid of losing, and worst of all... ...afraid of himself. So Zack walked on, with his head bowed and his hands in his pocket. He looked as if he was half-awake and half-asleep, which I guess is what made him seem like an easy target. ???: Yo man. Zack had walked down a rather decrepit alleyway on New York’s lower east side. The place stank of mildew and rotten trash, with the sides of buildings painted in vulgar graffiti, ready to topple over. Out from a shadowy area of the alley crawled a group of three men. The men look like your typical street thugs. Piercings on nearly every surface of their face; tattoos of various macho and scary images painted up and down their arms. And a walk that clearly told everyone in the area that these men were NOT to be taken lightly. They were the perfect stereotype of bad news. Zack said nothing, but looked on in annoyance and disinterest. Thug Leader: Didn’t ya hear me, I said yo. Don’t ya know it’s rude not to answer someone when they say hello? Thug 1: Maybe he’s too scarred to say anything. Thug 2: Yeah! I’ll bet he’s just pissed himself. The three of them started laughing condescendingly. The sound of their snickering only irritated Zack. So disgusting these vagrants. So vulgar and undisciplined, believing themselves to be top shit. How much Zack wanted nothing more than to split their heads open. But he quickly calmed himself down. He figured it was best to try and avoid trouble. Zack: What do you want? The leader stepped closer until he was barely an arms length from Crash. His breath stank of alcohol and drugs, while his body looked and smelled as if he hadn’t taken a bath in days. It was an assault on Zack’s senses just to even look at him, with his ‘tough guy’ bravado. Thug Leader: Here’s da thing. This here be our turf, ya understand? And we be sorta this neighborhood’s unofficial protection. We protect these people here from the really bad guys, like guardian angels and stuff. And all we ask is that people gives us a little…compensation for our kindheartedness. Either that, or we got a…friendly alternative. Ya get my meaning? Oh yeah, Zack got their meaning alright. If it wasn’t already made clear by the man’s tone, or Zack looking for the man’s shoulder to see his cronies gesturing the ‘friendly alternative’. Zack groaned to himself. This was just what he needed right now. If it wasn’t stressful enough facing Virus in mere days for the MWE Championship, how about a good old fashioned mugging on the side…just to spice things up a little. Zack: I don’t have time for this. Zack made an attempt to walk away, but was swiftly met with a hard left hook to the cheek. The blow sent Zack stumbling back a few feet into the opposite wall. Zack was stunned for a moment or two as the man looked angrily down at him. Zack reached up to rub his lip and felt a small trail of blood oozing from his lips. Thug Leader: Maybe I wasn’t exactly clear, I said hand over all yer money. Or I won’t go so easy on you next time. Zack stood back up to his full height, spitting out some of the blood in his mouth. As he looked into the faces of the three men moving closer toward him, Zack was starting to feel legitimately worried. The last thing he wanted, or needed tonight, was more trouble than he was already in. Zack: I don’t have any money ok. The leader grabbed Zack by the shirt collar and ran his back into the wall, nearly lifting him off his feet. Thug Leader: Don’t lie to me! Who the hell do you think you are? One of the man’s goons looked inquisitively over his shoulder at Zack, as if in attempt to answer the question. Zack prayed he wouldn’t recognize him, but looked on in dread as the man squinted briefly, then open his eyes wide in realization. This certainly wasn’t going to help matters at all. Thug 2: Hey, I know this guy! He’s one of them wrestlers I seen on TV. He’s here for some kinda pay-per-view or something. The leader looked over at his man before looking back at Crash, a wide, proud smirk twisting his face. He looked like he’d heard exactly what he wanted to hear. Zack’s heart began to sink into his gut. The man lowered Zack back down onto his feet, then leaned in so close their faces were practically touching. Thug Leader: Well, well, well. Ain’t that interesting news. So then you must be pretty rich huh? I guess then you can afford to make a rather large contribution to our organization…right? The man spoke barely above a whisper, yet it sounded almost as menacing as anything anyone in MWE had ever threatened him with. Zack was starting to get scarred. He held up his hands in a gesture of peace, trying desperately to get the man to back off. A plan he didn’t have much confidence in. Zack: Look, I don’t want any trouble. I– Another left hook connected with Zack’s face, strong enough this time to send Crash face first into a dirty puddle. As Zack stood on his knees, nearly choking on dirty water, the thug leader sent Zack back down with a hard kick to the stomach as his thug friends laughed in amusement behind him. Using the brick wall, Zack was finally able to get back to his feet. Thug Leader: Just give us the money. Zack: I told you, I don’t have any money on me. The man grabbed Zack by the shirt and threw him into the alley street, ripping Zack’s favorite shirt down the middle. Zack tried to quickly get up and run, but somehow, a new group of thugs had emerged secretly from the other parts of the alley, blocking all the possible exits. Zack looked all around in desperation, but there was no escape, and nobody nearby to call for help. Zack Crash was trapped. Thug Leader: I ain’t gonna ask ya again! Hand over all yer money…right…now! Many of the thugs were now brandishing their weapons to stress their point. Lead pipes, steel chains, bare knuckles, and even a few switchblades for the truly ambitious. And in that exact moment, where Zack felt a complete lost of hope, Zack gave in. He decided he didn’t care anymore. About what others said about him, about his inner demons, or about anything. And everything became insignificant. And for the first time, Zack Crash truly, and consciously tapped into his inner darkness. Zack: I got a better idea. How about you get out of my way, then you all go home to fuck yourselves. How’s that sound? There was an audible silence in the circle now, as the leader doubled back a few inches, a mixture of both shock and anger painting his face. From his reaction, it was clear he was the kind of person used to bullying people to get what he wanted. It was also clear that nobody had ever dared to speak to him like that before. Zack looked around at the awe-struck expressions of the thugs surrounding him…and he wasn’t afraid anymore. In fact, the sight of any of them made him ill. It made him wanted to bash their brains against the pavement until their brain fluids drained into the sewers. Zack really liked the image. Thug Leader: You better watch your mouth ya little shit! I don’t care if you’re some big shot wrestler, I’m– Zack: Well I don’t care who you are either you little cock sucker! Zack reached out and shoved the man backwards with all his force, nearly sending him tumbling to the ground. As the man looked back at him in stunned silence, Zack merely glared menacingly at him. He wanted the man to attack him, any of them. Zack bent his knees, ready for an assault, daring them silently to come for him. Zack: If you want my money so badly, then why don’t you try and take it from my cold dead hands? The thug leader smiled disturbingly at Zack’s challenge. Thug Leader: Very poor choice of words motherfucker! Get ‘im! At his command, two of the men charged for Zack. Without thinking or hesitating, Zack ran in, clotheslining one as he raised his steel pipe high for a strike. The man instantly fell to the ground, the pipe hitting the ground with a thunderous clatter. The other attacker stood frozen, long enough for Zack to hit him with a boot to the face before he realized what was happening. As the two men lay groaning in the street, the rest of the circle looked on in quiet amazement at having seen two of their friends taken out so easily. Filled with arrogance and bloodlust, Zack began bouncing on both feet like a pro-boxer. Zack: Is that all? Come on guys keep it coming. I like the training program in this part of town. It’s a good workout. The thug leader’s eyes boiled with anger so intense it can hardly be put into words. Throwing up his hands, he pointed an accusatory finger and Zack and shouted a mighty war cry. Thug Leader: KILL ‘IM!!! The next few moments past in a blur for Zack Crash. He didn’t really remember picking up the steel pipe off the street and swinging wildly as the circle collapsed into a frenzy. He didn’t remember breaking legs, and arms, blackening eyes, or bloodying faces. Even now, he doesn’t fully remember and images. But he remembers how it felt. The sensation in his hands that would shoot up his arms as the pipe would impact the body. He remembers how it sounded. The screams of pain from the weak men who tried to rob him, but ended up biting off more than they could chew. The sounds of breaking bones and pierced flesh that was like a symphony to his ears. He remembers the adrenaline. And he remembers how much he enjoyed it. Zack’s senses finally fully return, and all around him are broken and wounded men as they crawl and run away down various parts of the alley, none killed, but hurt enough to know better than to mess with any strangers again any time soon. All except one. The thug leader’s leg is badly broken as he tries to crawl away to safety, his moan’s echoing down the alley. Zack spots him, and slowly stalks toward him like a lion chasing down its wounded prey. Thug Leader: No! Stay away! Closer Zack came, dragging the tip of the pipe on the floor. The pipe hummed with hunger. Zack now stood over the man, grabbing his shirt collar and bringing the man up to face level. He smiled as he looked at the man. His fear practically had its own taste to it. And he liked it’s flavor against his lips. Zack: Not so funny now is it motherfucker? Thug Leader: Please, I’m sorry! Just let me go! Zack: No you’re not…but you will be. With that, Zack raised the pipe, ready to strike the final blow as the man clenched his eyes shut. Zack could feel the man shuttering underneath his death grip. And in that moment of waiting, when Zack took in the man’s fear and let it into himself…he had an epiphany. Though the man’s eyes were shut, he could still see in them enough to see his reflection. But what he saw wasn’t his own reflection. It was something else, something much worse. It was… And in that moment, Zack knew that the Virus had been right about him. That Zack Crash truly was as twisted and cruel as his ultimate nemesis. That darkness truly was within us all, even where the brightest light shown. Virus had always been right. Zack looked around at the mayhem he had caused. Thugs looking on as he held their leader at his mercy, while others simply moan and groaned, too hurt to even stand up. And Zack recalled the sick twisted pleasure he had just taken in hurting this people. Thugs and criminals though they were, but still in the end…people. And for the first time in a long while, Zack felt ashamed of himself. What had he let himself become? Had Virus’s taunting really made him fall that far? And in this new moment of doubt as shame, Zack had a new epiphany, one more important than the last. Zack leaned in close to the man he held and whispered into his ear. Zack: Get out of my sight. And Zack threw the man down to the pavement and watched as two of the thug’s crew, who had been watching nervously from a distance, came to lift him up and carry him off while shouting curses back at Zack. But he didn’t hear any of them. He merely stood within the center of the alley, within it’s darkness recesses. And as he stood there, absorbing the darkness into himself, Zack finally knew what he needed to know to fight not only his darkness, but the darkness of The Virus. Because there was one thing Virus still didn’t understand that Zack now did. Looking up, Zack smiled at the irony of it all. He was standing in the shadow of probably the most famous building in New York City. A building that stood higher and more dominant than any building in the city. What better place for the “Ultimate Thrill Seeker” to send his final message. This is the rise of Zack Crash. The city falls deeper into night as Zack Crash stands atop the Empire State Building, looking out over the city. Zack looks down at the blackened, shadowed streets below. The height and distance sends a swell of adrenaline through his veins and a smile to his lips. Right now, this building, and this city, almost feel like monuments to Zack Crash and the lifestyle of the thrill seeker. A lifestyle that can overcome anything…even yourself. Zack looks at the camera with an intensity not seen since his debut. Zack: Have you ever had a moment of true clarity, a moment in which everything around you suddenly makes sense? It’s a very overwhelming sensation. All the doubts and questions that are bothering you and obscuring your path just vanish like that, and your path is once again visible. And this time, your path is so clear that you wonder why you ever lost your way in the first place. Well that’s the moment driving me right now. It’s no secret to anyone that over these past several months I haven’t been myself. I’ve doubted myself, my motives, and been transforming into something that I wasn’t entirely proud of for something I didn’t even understand. Even in UUW I was starting to crack. Perhaps it was the pressure of finally becoming World Champion. Or perhaps it was just my own insecurities. Whatever it was, it doesn’t matter now. Because I’m not lost anymore. My questions have been answered, my doubts gone, and I’ve transformed back into the thrill seeker you all know and love. In other words… I’M BACK!!! Zack turns on that old, zany, adrenaline-seeking smile that was the signature of his debut in MWE. How long as it been since we’ve seen Zack smile an honest smile. Zack doesn’t even entirely know. He feels so warm inside, like he’s been separated from his own body for too long. But now is not the time for sentiments. Now is the time for business. And I gotta say it wasn’t a moment too soon. Because MWE has its biggest show coming up on August 22nd, 2010. Crowning Glory IV. The one-day of the MWE calendar when all eyes are on us. The place where rivalries come to a bitter and violent end. Where you either put up, or shut up. And those lucky enough to win on this day are destined to become legends to this federation, while the others simply fade away. It is simply put, the “be all, end all” of the MWE. Everyone in the locker works hard year round, and some still only dream of heading to Crowning Glory. Crowning Glory. Zack can practically hear the hype and discussion from all the way up here. The city is rank with CG fever. Zack moves closer, clutching at the chain fence separating him from the edge of the building. He wants to stand on the edge so badly. He wants to climb over this fence towards the precipice. He wants to feel the danger of being within just inches of falling toward a bloody doom. He wants to make physical the dilemma that’s been plaguing his mind and heart. So that he can prove he is truly no longer afraid. Looking ahead to this years Crowning Glory, I find myself looking back at my career since arriving in MWE. Last year’s CG was my first ever in this federation, and I know the phrase is cliché, but what a difference a year makes. Just last year, I was fresh and new, going into Crowning Glory III to face Jay Riddick for the X-TV Championship. Now, one year later, I head into the main event of the reborn MWE to face Virus in an “I Quit” Match. And when the dust has settled, the one with the most endurance will stand over the other as the new MWE Champion. Now I know what it’s like to touch that gold ring we call World Championship. I’ve tasted it once before, and I’m hungry to taste it again. Because I never lost my first World Championship. Unfortunately, UUW collapsed beneath itself before I could even prove myself properly as a champion. I don’t how any of you would take that, but I’m really pissed, so I feel I got something more to prove this time. So this time around, I plan on making this pay-per-view my “crowning glory”…pardon the bad pun. I plan on becoming what people always said I’d be. A champion. But before all that, I have one…HUGE…obstacle in my way. Zack begins pacing along the walkway of the viewing tower. Looking out at the city through the fence, he feels like a caged animal, just waiting to be released from its restraints. The City That Never Sleeps looks so quiet and peaceful from this high up. Quite the contradiction from the thieves and squatters plaguing the night streets. Quite the contradiction from the war that will be waged in Madison Square Garden in only a few days. But as Zack has learned tonight, any evil can be faced and defeated. All it takes is the right weapon. Or in this particular case…the right words. Leon ‘The Virus’ Roberts. You and I have had quite the feud over these past months. From UUW to MWE, from Hell In A Cell to the “I Quit” Match we’ll have at CG, our war for greatness has certainly been physical to say the least. But the true scope of our feud isn’t in the physical, but rather the mental battle of our feud. You have tried to prove I’m just as twisted as you are, while on the other hand have made the promise to save you from your demons. And I gotta be honest with ya, while we be equal as far as the physical aspect goes, mentally… …I think you’ve been kicking my ass. The words are hard for Zack to admit, as it would be for any warrior. It’s never easy for anyone to admit his or her own weakness. It is said that it takes great courage to stand up to your enemies, but much more to stand up to yourself. That is what Zack’s battle has been. A battle of his inner-self. But that battle is done. And now the true battle begins. As I said before, it’s not a mystery that I’ve doubted myself as of late. And I think it’s even less mysterious that YOU were the one causing me to doubt myself. And you did a good job of it, no arguing that. You are the first and only person to have ever made me question myself Virus, and for that I have to salute you. Oh I may have acted like you weren’t in my head, but honestly, everyday when I went home, I’d hear your words, and your speeches, and your taunts in my head. Every time I looked into the mirror, I’d see you smiling your cruel smile at me. And it was torture. If things had continued that way, I’m not entirely sure I could have beaten you at CG. But I have a bit of bad news for you Mr. Roberts. You ain’t in my head anymore. I finally figured it out. The one thing you and I were both forgetting. And the answer to it all was so simple, I feel embarrassed that neither one of use noticed it. Zack can’t help but smile in spite of himself. It’s often been said that sometimes the most difficult problem has the simplest solution. And this was one such occasion. The answer was so simple it was right in front of Zack the whole time. But Zack had been so concerned about why Virus might be wrong he didn’t even consider how much Virus might be right. And that held the key to his victory. Virus’s own truth would lead to his own downfall. Oh the irony. You see, you were only half right. Yes, we all have a darker side of ourselves. You, myself, even Eric Derf. We are all comprised of both light and darkness, and not one person on this earth can escape that. It’s not a matter of good and evil, that’s simply what being human is all about. And yet despite all that, there is a HUGE difference between you and me. Do you want to know what it is? Control. Yes, I have darkness in me. Yes I am addicted to adrenaline, and yes I love to fight and wrestle inside the squared circle. Does that make me a bad person…NO! Because I now know something that you still don’t understand. I can control my darkness, whereas you can’t. I am a man, whereas you are a slave. And that’s where the difference between us lies. Your darkness controls you Virus. You let it make your decisions for you. You’re a slave to it. But I’m the “Ultimate Thrill Seeker” and I refuse to let anything control me. I make my own decisions. And my decision is to be different from you Virus. I have darkness inside me, that much is clear, but I control my darkness rather than let it control me. And only when I need it do I let just a tiny sliver of it out, and then when I’m done, put it back into its mental container. Because I’m not afraid to use the darkness in me when needed. Not anymore. Because fear is what gives the darkness control over you. Our greatness power is not how much pain we can take, or how high we can jump, or how much punishment we can dish out. A man’s greatest ability lies within himself. His free will and ability to chose right over wrong is what makes us strong. Zack moves closer to the fence, gripping a portion of it tightly as he lays his face against its links. He can’t help but feel a tiny bit silly. All this talk of darkness and light, and about how strong our will to decide sounds a tad bit…corny, even to a man like Zack. But he doesn’t care. He’s learned not to care. Because he is simply Zack Crash. Not the “Ultimate Thrill Seeker”, or former UUW Champion. He is not Co-Number 1 Contender for the MWE Championship, and he is certainly not what Virus says he is. None of these things properly define him. He is simply Zack Crash. And that’s all he’ll ever need to know. Look down below. This building is obviously very high up. Now, my insides, the thrill seeker part of me, is telling me to jump. But I don’t. Because I’m not dumb enough to forget that, while I would get a major rush, I’d still in the end become a sidewalk pancake. I have control over my adrenaline, whether anyone believes it or not. I CHOSE not to indulge it. But you Leon, your darkness has no boundaries. You simply chose to hurt and torture without limit. Now you may think that makes you strong, but instead it only makes you weaker. Because in reality, it’s you who fears the darkness. And you’re violent persona is simply your way of running from facing that darkness. But boundaries and limits give us measures of how far we can take ourselves without destroying our bodies. Without them, we just push, and push…and push until we end up destroying ourselves. Leon, you pretend that you have no limit. That you can take more punishment than anyone in MWE. Well believe me when I say that line of thinking is one day going to get you killed if you don’t change now. If you don’t truly face your darkness, it will consume you. And soon, you’ll be beyond saving. Save Leon Roberts. That mission feels so old, Zack can hardly remember when or why it started. But that’s trivial information. What matters now is that now he CAN save him. Now Zack sees that he and Virus are more alike than they appear, even to Virus. For while they both are twisted, Zack also knows that there must be fear in Virus, like there was inside of him. And that fear can become Leon’s light, like it did for him. The stars in the night sky start to twinkle out one at a time, sending the city into an even heavier night. The night is darkest just before dawn. But I plan on showing you your own limits, because our match is all about making an opponent reach their limit. The ever-infamous “I Quit” Match. Virus, you said recently that this match was specifically marketed toward a person like yourself. And maybe you’re right. But what other match could we have at Crowning Glory. Now that I’ve come to terms with myself, I can finally truly save you from yourself. But in order to do that, I need a certain something. I need you to make the decision yourself, aware and perfectly conscious. If I pinned you or KO’d you like in any other match, you wouldn’t be making the decision for yourself, and my goal to save you would be wasted. But an “I Quit” Match. There is something just poetic about that match type. In this match, you beat your opponent so badly that they willingly, and consciously…give up. And THAT’S why I picked this match. In order for you to see past your demons, I need you to willingly give that up. I need to you consciously see that this Devil Incarnate persona is only that…a persona. The Virus isn’t the real Leon Roberts. We’ve never seen the real Leon Roberts. But we will soon. Because the Devil truly has no limits. And if that’s so, then how could an ordinary man, like Zack Crash, ever make him shout “I Quit”? When you say those words, it’ll prove that you’re just human, and not really a devil at all. And when you are faced with that undeniable truth, you will finally…be saved. A small shimmer of orange, yellow, and red blossoms in the horizon. The darkness of the night starts to crack, as small portions on the coming day filters into the city streets. As Zack watches, he wonders if this moment is a sign of things to come. If maybe he will be the first to shine the light into the darkened spirit that is Leon Roberts. Even in his newfound confidence, Zack does not know. But he does know that he will try. He has to. For Leon. For himself And for the MWE. We each hold victories over the other. We’ve been in so many confrontations we know each other’s styles as well as our own. So there’s no point in arguing who’s better and who’s not. The MWE Championship is only a minor thing in our battle now. Our battle has become something much larger Leon. Our battle has evolved into the essential battle of the universe. Night vs. Day Black vs. White Good vs. Evil Our two different lifestyles reflect this. And while you may have tried to make me question myself and whose side I was on, now I know that come this Sunday, I step into that ring for more than just myself or the fans. I step into that ring for everyone who ever fought for the light. We may both be sick and twisted men Leon, but between the two of us, I’m the lesser of two evils. And I plan on showing the MWE Universe that from the darkest shadow comes the brightest light. As he looks out over the city one final time, Zack reminisces about the lesson he has learned tonight. The power of an individual’s decision and free will. It is the simple truth that broke through his darkness. It is the power that saved him from becoming a monster. It is the sun that slowly rises over the skyline of NYC, flooding the city with a blinding light. In that pristine moment, there is only one thing Zack Crash needs to do… …decide. So he does. He decides to win. “A man’s heart is comprised of two conflicting selves, one light and the other dark. The one that wins is ultimately the one we feed.” End
