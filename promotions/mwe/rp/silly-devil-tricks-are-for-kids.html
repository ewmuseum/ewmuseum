SIlly Devil, Tricks are for Kids; Jesus is a Heel & The Devil is a Face?

Mar 15 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
OOC: I know that this RP has very little to do with this being a tribute show to Tim. But the thing is, I never met him so it's quite hard for me to pay my respect to the guy. I've heard the great stories and traits of Tim. But the fact is, I never KNEW him. So this RP, although not about him, is still my way to honor him. Thank you Tim, for making such a wonderful e-fed and some great roleplayers. I wish I knew you. Also .. Sorry this is so late. I was out of town from Thursday until Saturday afternoon from surgery so I lost a few days of writing time. It would have been un earlier today, but I've been drowsy off my meds and the last like 1000 words took me all day to write. ---------------------- Our scene opens in beautiful Dallas, Texas. More specifically, in the gorgeous St. Peter's church. As pews upon pews lined with red velvet cover the majority of the scene. Men, women and children all alike are dressed in their Sunday clothes, forming a sea of black suits and colorful dresses against the otherwise dull and lifeless surroundings. An almost silent chatter coming from them as some talk amongst themselves, and others bow their heads to offer a calm prayer. The children running up and down the aisle, chasing each other in play as they wait for the service to start. At the front of the chapel we find, a short stage, risen just high enough above the rest of the church goers that it is the center focus of everyone in the room as an elderly man walks forward. Taking his place at the microphone placed at a small wooden podium. "Dear brothers and sisters, we are gathered here on this holiest of days to honor our heavenly father." His raspy voice booming in an oddly calming way through the church, gaining the attention of everyone in attendance. The smooth black robes, and salt and pepper hair of this man only add to the soothing effect he has in this environment. Under his arm is tucked a large leather bound bible that he silently places in front of him, flipping through pages as he lets his voice once again echo through the room. "At this time I would like to ask each of you to bow your heads in silence as I offer a prayer." This time as his voice fades away into the calm silence of the church he lets his neck lean forward, bowing his head and placing his hands together atop of the bible in front of him. The congregation following suit quickly, heads bobbing down all around the room as the raspy voice of the priest begins to pray. "Dear heavenly father, we pray to you to keep us on your holy path of enlightenment. To break the evil grasps of Satan that attempt to pull was away from your divine plan and into the fiery depths of hell." His calming voice becomes stern as he prays, almost in a way that warns us of the harsh reality that life is. Trying to tell us to be this holy entity, free of any type of sin or else we'll be thrown down to hell. Just one of the many, frightening traits that all members of the Catholic priesthood possess. Every ear in the building seems to be turned towards this man as he continues to ask favors of God, all the while pumping a genuine emotion of fear into the peoples hearts. "We ask thee to nurture our lives, keep us from the evils of sin as we continue to live by your doctrines! We ask thee to guide us through our hardships and times of judgement without straying from your words of hope and love. Let us not fall into the hands of the Devil, let us not fall victim to his serpent like tongue as he attempts to sway us from the right way of man." The old mans breath is shallow and wavering as he pauses from his prayer. Giving his words time to sink into the heads of his congregation, you see, a prayer is rarely ever to ask the lord something, it is usually a deep message for either yourself, or the people praying with you. In this case, the priest's message is quite clear. "We ask this in the name of your holy son, Jesus Christ. Amen" Finishing the opening prayer the people in attendance all chime in with an "Amen" of there own in an almost monotonous tone. Letting there eyes open, arms drop and heads lift back up to look back towards our priest as he looks down at the scripture before him. Running his wrinkled finger run across the smooth surface as he lips his frail lips part, his voice once again in that calm rasp of just minutes ago. "Today's lesson is from Matthew twenty-three. Verses twenty-nine to thirty-three .." The priest clears his throat as the almost silent sound of people reaching for their Scriptures and turning to the page that is being read hums over the chapel. Waiting just a few seconds for everyone to find the verse, the priest begins to read the words of wisdom. "Woe unto you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! Because ye build the tombs of the prophets, and garnish the sepulchres of the righteous, and say, if we had been in the days of our fathers, we would not have been partakers with them in the blood of the prophets. Fill ye up then the measure of your fathers. Ye serpents, ye generation of vipers, how can ye escape the damnation of hell?" That stern sort of tone once again overpowering his soothing voice as he reads, people listening carefully as they either follow along in their own bibles or watch the priest unwaveringly. The words of the true saviour with the meaning of the world can not be explained by any man, but what would a priest be if he didn't try? Pulling his eyes away from the scripture the elderly man peers over the congregation before him, a meaningful little twinkle in his eye as his stern voice once again crackles over the microphone "This scripture refers to our generation! A generation of people that continuously look past what this book tells us to do. This book, is the word of the lord, yet this fresh, young generation look at it like nothing more then one of their Harry Potter novels ... Something that they can just throw aside after reading and not take the message that this great book has to offer!" With his last sentence the man slams his lose fist against the book, causing a loud booming thud to echo through the church, forcing the full attention of the people to turn towards him. If only for a moment before dozing back off into their day dreams. Quite weird isn't it? How these "great" people of religion feel that they have the right to judge everyone else. "This generation is nothing but sinners and hypocrites! This generation has fallen right into the hands of the Devil, and the saddest part is that we just let him grab a hold of us by the arm and lead us ... And unless we do something about it now ... Then we will all be failures in the eyes of the heavenly father and cast into hell to suffer for our sins in eternity!" In a way, this is one of truest things that has ever been said. This generation is really one of the worst the world has ever seen. Everyone is too concerned with their own pleasure over trying to improve the world. So if that means sinning every damn day of your life, then no one really cares anymore. Yet the way this man let's his words ring out, it is quite apparent that he's referring to everyone but himself with the phrase "this generation" Although he were this perfect man that could do no wrong. "Think of our forefathers! .." Think of our ancestors ... WON'T SOMEBODY PLEASE THINK OF OUR DEAD ANCESTORS!? Is this man seriously telling us to think of men and women that died hundreds of years ago? For what!? "What would they think, if they could see us all today! ... What are they thinking now, as they look down at us from heaven, only to see that we are all destined to spend the rest of eternity .. burning in the fires ... of HELL!" ONOZ! Our long lost dead relatives are gonna be disappointed in us. Whatever will we do? This little guilt trip that the priest is trying to lay onto his congregation really seems to have no affect on them. They aren't really listening, most of them have drifted off into thoughts of what they're going to do later today while others chat amongst themselves just quietly enough that the priest cannot hear them. All the while he is swinging his arms around violently to emphasize his words. "I want each and every one of you, to look at your own lives ... And see that although no one is perfect ... That this generation of sinners and evildoers is the worst that it has ever bee-" CRACK Heads spin around as the large wooden doors at the back of the chapel swing open. Hitting the walls with a crack almost like that of lightning. The ominous light of the early morning sun shining through. As a tall, dark figure begins walking towards us. "You people are truly pathetic." The man, dressed in an oh too familiar pin-stripped suit emerges from the light. His bleach blond hair and disgusted stare are enough to tell us the entire story of this man. He is ... Joey Dallas. As he slowly paces up the carpeted walk way between the pews he peers back and forth at the many people, all staring at him as he walks. The children's jaws dropped in shock at this man. Who is taking his time to look at almost every set of eyes upon him. Joey Dallas: "You all waste your lives .. Following the rules, of a man you can't see ... When you have the perfect example of a saviour standing right here! Now, the only question ... is why?" Stopping beside one of the young girls standing close to the isle Dallas crouches, slowly running his hand down her cheek looking at her although she were the Devil himself. An older woman snatches her away as she looks on at Dallas in shock and fear. Joey Dallas: "Well the answer is because you're all afraid ..." Dallas pushes himself back to a vertical base. Taking a few seconds to look at the people, still staring towards him before continuing along his path towards the front of the church, walking very slowly as he does so. Joey Dallas: "You're afraid of some sort of punishment for your actions ... So you sit here every single week, because you think that coming to church and listening to some idiot preach week in and week out ... Will save you .. You seem to think that when your God is looking over the list of people to send to Hell, he'll just look past your life of bad deeds. Well here's the news flash of the century ..." Finding himself directly in front of the priest, just below the stage, Dallas turns around to face the crowd of people that still sit in awe of him. The same look of utter disgust still plastered across his face as he continues his own rant. Joey Dallas: "That's just not how this works ... You cannot beg your savior for forgiveness, only to go off and continue doing the same things that you were forced to repent for in the first place .. That is why I have given up, on people like each and every one of … you.” Pointing a cold, hard finger towards random individuals every time a new word popped into place in his last sentence, Dallas pauses for just a short second between words. He is talking down to these people, much like he does to our fans each and every week, but I suppose he does have a right to. After the way they have booed him for the last six months, all because he has fallen onto the bad side of their heroes. Dallas keeps his finger pointed as he turns around to face the dumbfounded priest who attempts to act like he has been agreeing with everything Dallas has said to this point. Joey shakes his head in disappointment at this man of faith as he lets his hand drop casually down to his side. Joey Dallas: “But you … Are the most pathetic of all” He once again begins to move, taking his time as he struts towards the short set of stairs that reach onto the stage, refusing to take his eyes off of the shocked priest as he does so. Joey Dallas: “You try to convince these people that they can change ... That they can be the people that you’re precious God wants them to be. When you … are the most indecent person in this building.” The old wooden steps of the stage crack beneath his feet as Dallas puts his weight onto them, slowly pushing himself closer and closer towards the priest who finds himself utterly speechless at this man's straight up attack on his congregation and now himself. Joey Dallas: "Because you continuously try to force them ..." Joey motions towards the still staring crowd of people as he stares into the elderly eyes of the priest. Joey Dallas: "To be better people ... To be the kind of people that deserve to be saved! When you commit sins that even the Devil himself wouldn't try ... You try to persuade this people to believe that you are the good and the right, while trying to pawn it off as a way to put them into heaven!" The now common strut of Dallas takes over again as he walks towards the priest, causing him to squirm away slowly, afraid of what could possibly happen next. Joey Dallas: "All because you want to have the glory once you reach the pearly gates for yourself ... So I want you to take a long hard look at yourself in the mirror, and actually try to convince yourself that YOU'RE going to heaven, and stop worrying about them!" Finding himself just inches away from the priest Dallas finds it impossible to constrain himself any longer and throws his hand up, slapping the priest across the face with a loud smack! The impact and shock of the action sends the man to the floor like a ton of bricks. Joey Dallas has just SLAPPED A PRIEST! But the question remains .. What is Dallas doing here? Why has he decided to disrupt a common church service with his unique brand of insults? I have a funny feeling, we're about to find out. Dallas turns around and begins to slowly strut away as gasps of complete shock escape from the congregation. A grim smirk across his lips as he silently chuckles to himself feeling that he had done his job. "May the lord have mercy on your soul" Hand pressed lightly against his cheek as a large hand print becomes visible the priest looks up at our self-proclaimed savior in a strange look of fear and anger. Fumbling with his words he manages to speak that one, meaningful sentence. The sound of the frail voice causes Dallas' neck to spin around violently, cocking his eyes like a gun onto the downed priest. Joey Dallas: "What did you just say?" Letting the rest of his body align with his neck he stalks back towards the priest, who seems to have gotten a sudden burst of courage. Not backing down (Not like he can get much further anyway) the priest locks eyes with Dallas. "I said may the lord have mercy on your putri-" Joey Dallas: "I heard what you said!" Something seems to catch Dallas' attention from the corner of his eye as he speaks. He let's his neck slowly turn in the same direction, eyes finally coming to rest on that cold, black, leather-bound bible. Running his fingers aimlessly over the smooth page he let's his words once again escape from his mouth. Joey Dallas: "You want to live your life through this, but this thing is outdated. As times change ... So do the rules." Dallas pulls his fingers off the page and wraps them around the cover before slamming the large book shut with a thud. Joey Dallas: "These rules were made thousands of years ago .. Since then we have seen the world evolve. People have changed ... And to account for that, a savior has to make himself known. To change the rules with the times ... So as far as I'm concerned ..." Tightly grasping the thick leather book as well as possible in his hand Dallas pulls it off the wooden surface and with one fluid motion turns his body, throwing the book so it lands just beside the elderly man that has already seen his religion degraded by this man. Dallas is truly giving a new meaning to the old saying "Adding insult to injury" Joey Dallas: "That book, is just about as useless as you are." He could sit there for hours and completely tear apart this man, physically and verbally, but Dallas seems to feel like he has already wasted enough of his time on this cowardly man and starts to walk to the opposite side of the stage, stepping over the priest as he does so. Dallas doesn't look back, but I think he knows that as he leaves this man behind he has changed him, in some small insignificant way perhaps but he is never going to be quite the same after that encounter. Joey Dallas: "As little as you people deserve it .. You all need someone to show you right from wrong. You need someone to lead you ... You need, a Savior. Now unfortunately for myself, that Savior is me." Reaching the steps at the end of the stage Dallas carefully steps down them, a very small clunk is heard every time his foot hits the wood. He lets his eyes drift around the room, looking at the stunned crowd as he once again begins to strut at the front of the room. Joey Dallas: "Now call me a false profit if you want ... But to be a profit, I would have to claim to talk to God." Head turning up, Dallas looks towards the ceiling, towards the heavens if you will. A small smirk coming over his face as he does so. Joey Dallas: "Simply put, I don't need to take directions from him! Because unlike him .. I have seen what you people do first hand. I've seen how you degrade yourselves to pretend to feel guilt from your wrong-doings ... All the while you insects continue to take this planet, and you constantly shit all over it!" Eyes once again drifting over the many people that still stand watching him, Dallas locks his eyes on a random woman as he strides down the long isle between the pews again. Children have began crying, men tighten fists by their sides and Joey Dallas just keeps being his usual self. Joey Dallas: "If there is anyone that actually knows what I have to put up with from you people ... Then it's Leon Roberts ..." What on earth is he talking about? Doesn't Virus stand for everything that Dallas is against? Isn't the whole battle supposed to be good versus evil? Here I was thinking the self-proclaimed Savior was supposed to be you know, SAVING US, from the Devil. Dallas pulls his eyes away from the woman, swinging his head around to look at someone else in the crowd as he turns to walk behind the long rows of pews. Barely anyone is sitting anymore as they watch Joey pace up and down the rows. Joey Dallas: "Leon knows what you people are willing to do just for that little bit of happiness ... He knows that you will all throw away your lives, just for one minute of fun. The only difference is that Leon wants you do those things." Makes sense I suppose. A savior must know his people so that they can be helped, and the devil must know the people so he can pull them away. Dallas is smarter then he looks, and right now he is still pace around the pews. It seems that he is looking for someone in the crowd. Joey Dallas: "You see, Leon Roberts, likes to claim that he's the Devil's Incarnate ... All because of one little recurring nightmare that I have heard about many, many times ... So naturally, Leon feels that by doing these bad deeds it makes him, what he's supposed to be ... But he needs you all to follow him down that road, so that he can feel better about doing those things himself." He finally finds the person he has been searching for and takes his time to squeeze between the two pews that they are down until he finds himself just inches from a shorter man, dressed very similarly to Dallas in a dark suit and tie. This man takes a defensive stance, tensing his muscles and cocking his fist as Dallas stares him down. Joey simply shakes his head as he looks around himself. Joey Dallas: "Let's take this man as our example today, it is quite obvious that you have been paying very little attention to this churches teachings in the first place .. These people want you to believe that violence is never the answer, so put your damn fist down." Remaining tense the man slowly lowers his fist back down to his side, refusing to let himself get out of his defensive instinct. Joey Dallas: "That is really just the first of your problems ... Just from looking at you I know that you are nothing more then some punk kid who gets his kicks out of doing stupid things as long as it gets a laugh ... So if that means you have to hurt someone else, if it means you have to take something from someone, then you're perfectly fine with that .. That doesn't make you evil, that just makes you an inconsiderate idiot." This man's expression changes drastically, from an intense stare down on Dallas to a look of confusion and just the slightest bit of anger. Our savior pushes past him with a small push with his arm, eyes once again looking through the room to find someone else. His words still focused on the young man he has just left in his path. Joey Dallas: "But it's all okay, because you've seen it on television, right? Just because someone else does these stupid things .. I makes you feel better about doing it yourself." As he searches, he once again finds his subject for verbal attack. This time, a younger couple, with a baby resting quietly on the woman's shoulder. As Dallas draws closer the man of the couple takes a very similar approach as the last one, getting face to face with Dallas, only to be pushed backwards casually. Dallas takes the man's hand, looking at it for a split second before dropping it. "WHO THE HELL DO YOU THINK YOU ARE!?" The man yells out as Dallas tries to open his mouth, finally someone in this place has the guts to stick up for these people and the religion. Joey Dallas: "That isn't the type of language a good little church family should be using is it? Especially one that has already broken your precious God's rules ... Premarital sex, and you end up with a child. Now .. Let me ask you .. Does that mean this entire little family of yours is going to hell?" Dallas looks intently towards this man as he waits for an answer, the man stands still, in his aggressive stance as he stares right back at him. An awkward silence has seem to have fallen over the church. Joey Dallas: "Does that make you .. A bad person?" "I don't know!" Joey Dallas: "NO! It just makes you an immature little boy that can't control his urges ... It makes her your average teenage girl .. Just because you can't control your sexual urges does not make you a sinner!" Dallas takes off, leaving the man standing alone as his wife slowly reaches up to him, taking him gently by the hand as Dallas struts back up the isle for what seems like the hundredth time. Joey Dallas: "It does however make you nothing more then the immature nation of people that this world has become accustom to .. This kind of thing happens all the time, so you feel better about yourselves." Finding himself once again at the front of the church Dallas turns to look over the congregation, for the most part they are feeling comfortable with him at this point. That small bit of tension from his earlier actions still looming in the air however. Yet it doesn't seem to effect the message Dallas is trying to send to these people. Joey Dallas: "Does everyone understand what I'm trying to tell you?" Silence as the man looks around. It sickened him to realize that as much as he hated these people, he was now trying to help them. All to prove a point. Heads shake, some nod and Dallas was going to explain himself anyway. Joey Dallas: "You see, it doesn't matter if you cause minor harm to yourself or others. It doesn't matter if you give into your urges and partake in premarital sex. It doesn't make you the Devil's advocate ... Or the Devil himself, all it makes you ... Is an idiot." The silence remains as the crowd of people listen carefully to his words, obviously talking about how The Virus claims to be the Devil. As he let's his last words sink in Dallas once again begins to pace back and forth at the front of the large church. Joey Dallas: "This Monday the MWE honors the man that made everything possible ... Tim Fitzgerald. The creative genius that built this company from a no name brand into a contender to be the greatest federation the wrestling world has ever seen ... So we honor him, with competition, and what greater competition then a Savior ... And a man who pretends he's the Devil. This Monday ... The man, that is single handily responsible for forcing Ace Cannon into a life of hatred and deceit. Joey Dallas ... Goes one on one, with the man that claims to be the Devil's incarnate, wishing that he could see the type of success that people like I have seen. 'The Virus' Leon Roberts" Dallas stops dead in his tracks, looking all around the chapel. The beautiful colors and cool tones of his surroundings are unusually calming for him. The crosses upon the walls, remind him of the day that he fought Ace Cannon in the most brutal match of his career, and the people, remind him of his pitiful performance against David Whitman. But that is all in the past, everything he has needs to be focused on Virus. Joey Dallas: "A man that uses flashy magic tricks and camera effects, to try to convince everyone that he is what he claims to be ... Only to fall miserably on his face, because his so called, evil deeds are out-shined by mush more putrid human beings." It's the truth, Virus' has a lot of competition for being the most vile man in MWE. There are men that kick children. There are men that bleed others out like a pig. There are men that will slaughter you in a drunken rage ... As a matter of fact, it is all one man. Ace Cannon, a vile man, and a man that has truly "Out Devil'ed the Devil" Joey Dallas: "Leon Roberts, is just like all of you ... He makes the wrong choice, at the wrong time ... Yet instead of just looking at the media and seeing other people do the same things as him .. He tries to pawn himself off, as this all powerful being .. I say, let him pretend, because just like this sham of a priest." Dallas lifts his right hand, staring at it intently as he does so. Giving everyone a moment to remember just minutes ago, when he struck down the priest with a horrendous slap across the face, Dallas chuckles to himself before continuing along. Joey Dallas: "He will be struck down by the hand of the true Savior." With that Dallas drops his hand back to his side and begins walking towards the back of the church. Heads turn as he passes by the congregation without so much as a second look, heading strait towards the door that he entered from as he is engulfed by that same, bright light from the entrance before the camera, cuts to black.
