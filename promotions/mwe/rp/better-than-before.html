Better Than Before

Jan 10 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
*Mason Resoan is seen sitting in a bedroom that is not his. It looks as though it is a hotel room, as the size is not that of Mason's normal standards, but it is close. There is a mini-fridge sitting in the back left corner, white, and when opened is very cold. There is also a giant bay window in which Mason is staring out of. He is standing, looking down, in front of one of two beds in the room, both of queen size value. It seems as though he is in awe of something, but the cameras do not know what. Instead, they continue to survey the room, looking in nooks and crannies finding all sorts of interesting things. A pair of black athletic socks, several wrestling magazines, a Mason Resoan T-Shirt, a couple of promotional posters, a microphone, and a PSP. However, one thing that is not seen is the Mayhem Wrestling Entertainment United States Championship. The cameras look back at Mason and see that he is actually holding it on his right shoulder as he continues to look out into the space of the bay window. His trance hangs in the air like a wonderful smell, as there is no concentration, yet...there is. The cameras continue to parade around his room finding more and more interesting little trinkets and even a remote or two for the wonderful High Definition Television that is mounted upon the wall to Mason's left. Mason, at the moment, is wearing his casual clothes of black pants, accompanied by a white button up T-Shirt and his gold watch. He is also sporting, as aforementioned, the United States Championship and his black sunglasses rest gently in his golden-brown hair. It has been close to ten minutes and Mason is still staring out of the window, when the camera man slams the bathroom door shut to get his attention.* Mason: I thought, for a while, that winning was the most important thing in the world. I figured that there was nothing better than winning and that cementing my legacy into this business' history would really make a difference. Technically, it already has, but it really doesn't make a difference anymore. I realized, that the reason winning meant so much, is because other people, or another person, in my case, could share that joy with me. After I won my United States Championship, I never got to experience that joy with that person, because of rage and no self-control. Someone took it upon themselves to try and get personal with me. Well...it worked. *Mason turns full way to the cameras now and the United States Championship is in full view. They zoom into the nameplate which reads "Mason Resoan" and then they zoom out to see a happier Mason Resoan.* Mason: You see, I have no idea what these people actually intended to do. If they tried to relay a message that they sent business, if they tried getting revenge...to be honest, I could care less what they were trying to send to me. All I know, is what I got. What I got, was nothing more than a wake-up call, telling me that there is more to life than winning...and by doing that, they opened up rage I have never felt before...rage I can not express, but when the time is right, I will release it. *Mason's smile fades away and his mellow looking face comes back on as he takes a seat on the queen sized bed nearest his bay window. He places the newly won MWE U.S title on the bed besides the one he was sitting on and cracked his neck.* Mason: By attacking Sara Whitaker, it really opened up a door-way to a new level of competition. It made me think more and more about how much I want to win, so hopefully, Sara can enjoy the victories, even if I can not. It opened up a door-way to a new level of skill, as I am now able to focus on my opponents better than ever before. And it also opened a door-way to a new level of talent. Talent, which allows me to break my opponents down even faster and more precise than before. Talent, which allows me to see not one move ahead, but three. A talent that allows my reflexes to go like clockwork, so that no matter what is thrown at me, I can dodge and counter. Talent, which will help me beat Joey Dallas. *Mason, true to his normal character, put on his evil smirk, which for some reason, has an even more cocky look to it than usual.* Mason: Joey Dallas, when you told Sinc Mercier that you would take care of me, I pondered what you meant. I thought long and hard about what you could have meant and when you said that we had a match, it made me smile to think that you thought you could beat me. I mean, maybe, just maybe in my previous state, you could have beaten. When I was less talented, less skillful, and less intelligent than I am now...but that whas then and this is now. Now, through that attack on Sara, I have new direction and I have a new motivation. Winning is now second priority, first...is finding who assaulted my girlfriend...who did nothing...to anyone. *Mason has a little anger in his tone, but is holding it back and keeping his calm. The cameras notice that one of Mason's hands, his right, was clenched into a fist.* Mason: I am now faster, stronger, smarter and more talented. Almost as if I evolved overnight. To think that you can still beat me would just mean that you need to save yourself. Save yourself, Joey, from a beating you aren't expecting. Make sure that you realize what I have become, listen to me...listen to what I have to say and learn. Make Monday better for yourself and shape up, because starting Monday, I am going to have an intensity I have never...ever had before! *Mason quickly stands to his feet and looks into the camera. He picks up his United States Championship and holds it up to the camera.* Mason: This is not for the title...this isn't for a contender's spot, this isn't even for pride...this is just a normal old match...but I am still going to bring everything I have because until I find out who attacked my girlfriend...my loved one...my motivation, then no one will have an easy time. Not now...never. You might have ideas of saving others, but believe me, I don't care how many times I have said it...I will continue to tell you that I need no saving. Sinc Mercier can join forces with you...you can let him come under your wing and try to shape him up for me, but he will soon learn that he isn't the only one who can't beat me. Especially not now. *Mason drops the title to his side and clenches both his fists in anger again as he walks back to the bay window and releases some stress. He continues to talk, but refuses to look back at the camera.* Mason: You have no idea, Dallas, how angry it actually makes me to know that I don't know who attacked my girlfriend. My anger can be controlled, but Monday is another story. You see, we have never faced each other before, but I have seen you wrestle. I saw the Lights Out match, I have seen you win titles and I have seen you try and "Save" Sinc Mercier already and I know you. I know how you wrestle, I know how you think in the ring and I know what motivates you. Beating Sinc gets to you...knowing that the person you took under your wing already lost. Losing to Cannon would make you break down, as well. Losing...it makes you... lose it...It angers you, doesn't it? It makes you want to crawl up into a ball and hide away from everyone else in the world. It makes you wish that you could rewind the tape and change history. Losing titles, losing main events, losing anything drives you insane. Winning, just like me, motivates you to keep going. You like success, you like to "Save" people...which I have now taken as a sign that you are training people. You want people to be successful, you want to be successful and if that doesn't go through you train harder and harder, right? Well, I am almost the same, so this match-up is good, right? Wrong! *Mason is seen shaking his head as he gives a slight chuckle. He turns, glances at the the United States Championship, and looks deep into the cameras.* Mason: That, right there, that title signifies that I am one of the best in the business. You, what do you have, a match victory over Ace Cannon. Look where he is now, facing Sinc Mercier, a man that I have beaten twice. If you, for one second, pride yourself over Sinc Mercier improving or beating Ace Cannon..well than I have some news for you. I can beat Ace Cannon and I could help Sinc Mercier improve faster and more than you could in half the time. You have nothing...you are a failure...yes, nothing but a failure. *Mason lets those words sink in. They linger in the air like a bad smell as Mason tries to further get into the head of Joey Dallas. He continues to let the words sink in as he puts his cocky and evil grin back on his face. He grabs the United States Championship and puts in on as he looks back out of the big bay window.* Mason: How does it make you feel to know that I have accomplished more than you have? I have won the MWE Championship...I have won the MWE Tag Team Championship...I have won the United States Championship and even more than that, I am currently undefeated, if you haven't noticed. Just something for you to think about. Oh yeah and did I mention that I have beaten both Brian Michaels and D.C? Well, if you didn't know...well, I am not finished quite yet. *Mason motions the camera to come over and look and for the first time the cameras can see what Mason Resoan has been gandering at the entire time he has been by the window. It is a beautiful sight of a gigantic stadium and by the looks of it, the crew and Mason have to be at least 60 stories high.* Mason: Magnificent..isn't it? And despite what I have been saying...this is what our match will be like this week, Joey. Even if I beat you horribly, our match will be nothing short of magnificent. It will certainly be better than Mercier v.s. Cannon, that...we can both agree on. Joey Dallas v.s. Mason Resoan will be better than Dallas v.s. Cannon, Resoan v.s. Mercier, Michaels v.s. D.C, or anything else that could be put together. We both strive to win...we both hate to lose...we both want success and unlike what people may say, this will be match of the night. D.C, Alejandro, and Whitman have the actual main event, but they won't be able to top us. How can a main event be better than a match in which both superstars in the match have beaten every superstar in the main event? The can't and that is my point. Dallas v.s. Resoan is Stallone v.s. Schwarzenegger, it is the Giants v.s. Patriots, it is U.S v.s. Russia...no, it is better...it is Dallas v.s. Resoan...and that is all it is. *Mason walks away from the giant bay window and instead goes to a closet right near the bed farthest away from the bay window. He looks around a little bit until he finds an object, which he pulls out. It is a weight and he drops it on the bed. It bounces up and down for a second, but eventually it comes to a stop.[/color] Mason: If you look at that weight, you can see how it relates to our match. It will go back and forth...back and forth until eventually it will stop. It will be a match to remember with spots and the like allowing us to capture match of the night, but like I said it will have to end, with me beating you. But regardless of the result, it will be a match to remember and it will be unlike Cannon v.s. Mercier. *Mason picks the weight back up off the bed and simply tosses it onto the floor. It does not bounce, nor does it even move, it simply plops on the cold floor.[/color] Mason: And if you see the weight that time, you didn't see it move. that is because Cannon and Mercier are boring. They don't sell seats...they don't sell tickets, because they are below us. We will show them what a true match is like, what a true performance actually is and we will bring the house down, because we are the future of this company. We will take it places not even Brian Michaels could have. And we will start Monday! *Mason picks up his weight from the floor and places it back into the closet, which after he sorted through it, had become messy. He then returns to a vertical base and walks back over to the big bay window and takes the belt from his waist to his left shoulder.* Mason: Monday will mark, without a doubt, a new era of Mason Resoan. With this championship on my shoulder, I will continue to win...I will continue to prove to everyone why I am a triple crown. Yes, a triple crown...something few people have ever accomplished before. D.C has not, Brian Michaels has not, hell, even Whitman hasn't accomplished what I have with this most recent win. And I deserve it, I deserve it because I don't need to be saved, because I can stand on my own two feet. I can carry myself and I could carry you...carry you to heights that you couldn't dream of bringing Sinc Mercier. But, considering we are on the opposite side of the ring...you won't be seeing new heights...no...you will only be seeing the mat, as I slam you on the mat. As I kick you...as I punch you...as I destroy you...as we put on a match so great that you will be displeased you can't remember it. Dallas, right now, I am a captain of MWE. I am leader, a general, a front man and I won't disappoint. I won this title because it needed to be in the hands of someone... someone worthy enough to hold it. And if you think, after I did all this work, that I would let you beat me and keep me from showing all these fans that I am not a liar, than you have got another thing coming. *Mason continues to stare through the window and the camera can clearly depict the awe in his eyes as he looks at the magnificent sight once again.*' Mason: Monday, when I prove that I am indeed a captain of wrestling...I will show you that there is no escape from my new found intensity. I will show you and the rest of the locker that I can become a monster just like Kanto was...I can show that I want to see others in pain. I can be just as sadistic as you or anyone else in the company. You wear a crown of thorns and what am I supposed to think? That you don't care about your body and that you mean business? Ace Cannon can remove staples from his head and what am I supposed to think? That he is willing to put himself through pain to get what he wants? Well, I would go beyond that. I would go far beyond that to show you that you can't win...because winning is what we both strive for...and I wouldn't have it any other way. Plus, how would it look if I win the title and Sara sees me lose the next match? That would not be good...not...one...bit. So, I won't let it happen...oh no, this will be a match never forgotten...but it will also be a match that I refuse to lose it. *Mason shifts the title and cracks his neck again. He checks his watch and shakes his head as he continues to stare out of the giant bay window in front of him.* Mason: Dallas, we will have the whole locker room watching as we go into our match. We will have the eyes of millions and millions...we will have the eyes of D.C, David Whitman, and Alejandro Rodriguez...we will have the eyes of Richard Covell on us...we will have the whole world watching us and when we finish the match, we both will have more respect from the human race than we had twenty minutes prior. So, just in case there was anyone thinking about not watching this match, you better...because you will see what it is like to face me. You will see what my new intensity is like and you will see what is like right after you try and get personal with the champ...with Mason Resoan...the Canadian Classic! *Mason turns to face the camera and there is a fire in his eyes never seen before. His grin is gone and it looks like he is serious.* Mason: Before Adrenaline Rush, I stated that history would repeat itself and it did. I said that this belt would soon be mine and I also said that it was a stepping stone. This...like I called, would all come true...because after Dallas...I am coming to claim what I haven't held in almost a year...the MWE Championship. Which brings me to this main event... *Mason holds up three fingers, each representing one of the participants in the main event's triple threat match.* Mason: David Whitman...a month ago, we stepped into the same ring for the very first time...but if you recall, we never actually got to have a physical encounter. Instead, I tossed around your partners while you got the closest seat in the house. You witnessed what I could do and you saw what I had then. My team beat your team and we put on a show...no doubt about that...but I warned you that you should stay out of my path...and like a good boy you listened. Monday, you have the chance to win a title that I have my eyes set on. I won't tell you to stop and not win the title...but think long and hard. Watch the tape and try to remember what I did to your partners and think about you being in that spot. Think about what would happen if you won the gold...it wouldn't be a surprise if you started seeing a little more of me...and that is all I will say to you. *Mason puts down his ring finger leaving only his index and middle finger remaining.* Mason: Alejandro...a man two countries down...yet one card spot up...a man I have also never faced one on one in the ring. I've never liked you...really, I haven't. Back when you feuded with Aiden Copane was the first time we met and that wasn't a pleasant encounter. It was a showdown indeed, but not one that I think you would like to see again...especially not in the ring. Intensity is something I had, but was restrained the last time we were even within 50-feet of each other. It is true that we faced off for the United States Championship close to three months ago and you came out on top, but let us not bring up dead topics. I was different last time we met and even then, I almost mowed you down with a car. Canada and Mexico do not mix and we have proved that time and time again, so if you were to win the title, think what would happen? I mean, my new path, my new intensity, my ideas and intelligence...coming after you, as champion vs. champion...with me having every advantage possible...it wouldn't be good...at least not for your sake. *Mason lowers one finger, leaving only his middle finger up flipping off the camera.* Mason: Ha..ha! *Mason continues to laugh for a bit as the camera looks on with a puzzled and confused face.* Mason: It would just so happen that this would be the last finger up...this hand gesture that D.C has given cameras world-wide for close to two years. A gesture that pisses people off...a gesture of rebellion...a gesture of independence...a gesture that can start wars. It is funny that D.C, who made this infamous gesture in MWE would now be on the receiving end of it. Well, there is only one thing that can be said when this gesture is around. *Mason looks around his hotel suite, adjusts his championship belt, and then snaps back into the camera.* Mason: F*** YOU! *Mason lowers his finger and puts his arm at his side as he agains shifts is title. He looks at his watch again, then back out of the bay window, finally ending up looking straight back into the camera's lenses again.* Mason: D.C, I say f*** you...because that is all that really needs to be said. Before Adrenaline Rush, I told you that without Lindsey you would be nothing...I said that you would crumble and be nothing...and what do you do...you break up with her. I say f*** you, because I figured of all people, you would have finally realized that I am never wrong. I told you that turning your back on Michael's would relieve you of his pressure...I told you that you could be more and it all came true. So, what is the difference now? Nothing...because I am always right and there is nothing that can change that. F*** you, because you just said good-bye to your MWE career...I can already tell that something will happen to you, something not good, something that will once again prove me right and you wrong. You want to be rebellious...go ahead, be my guest, but when the s*** hits the fan, don't come back to me. Don't come to me asking for advice, because all I will tell you is...I told you so. D.C, you may be MWE Champion, but your chances of winning are slim to none...without Lindsey you will not be the same...you will not focus...you won't be the same. So, D.C.......F*** YOU! *Mason raises his wrist and looks at his watch again. His eyes widen slightly as he looks around and sees his room is semi-messy. He leaves the bay window and finds a duffel bag, in which he begins to throw clothes in. It takes a few minutes, but Mason has cleaned up majority of his hotel suite and has his clothes and belongings in several duffle bags stationed on his bed. The only thing still in the room is the MWE United States Championship which continues to lay on Mason's shoulder.* Mason: Dallas, you should learn from D.C and listen to me...you should learn from Sinc Mercier and listen to me...you should learn from Brian Michaels and listen to me...listen to me and you can "Save" yourself. *Mason does the Dr. Evil style finger parentheses.* Mason: If you do this one thing...then maybe...just maybe you can beat me, but lately, everyone is being ignorant so I don't expect anything more from you. Monday will be a match...one hell of match...that is for sure, but it is up to you to decide whether or not you want to make it just that..."that" much better. Be prepared, because going against a triple crown and the MWE United States Champion will not be as easy as you will think...and if you think it will be easy...then you have got another thing coming. *Mason reaches into his pocket and takes out is Microsoft Zune device and clicks a few buttons. He then raises it to the screen and a music video for the song, "You've Got Another Thing Coming" by Judas Priest is playing.* Mason: And if ya didn't know...now ya know! *There is a knock on the door and several hotel employees walk into the room and grab Mason's bag as he carries his Zune and Mayhem Wrestling Entertainment Championship out of the room with him. The door is shut by an employee and the camera man goes back over to the giant bay window. The magnificent view is shown as the promo fades to black...*
