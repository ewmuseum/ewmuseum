MWE 24/7 — 08-09 PPV PR Archives

Destiny

Mar 25 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
**We see Ace Cannon walking the dark streets of London. The weather is a cool but seasonal and we see Cannon dressed simply in a black Affliction thermal shirt and a pair of jeans. He continues walking down the street not paying attention to anything other then his steps. He pulls a pack of cigarettes out of his pants pocket and extracts one from the pack, he puts it to his lips and pulls a lighter out of his other pocket and lights it. With cigarette lit he continues walking down the street taking puffs of it here and there and still not really paying attention to his surroundings. Cannon eventually comes to a building that makes him stop and take a double take. As Cannon stops and the sign reads ST.ANNE’S CHURCH. Cannon just stands there and stares at it as he finishes his cigarette. He walks up a set of concrete stairs that take him to a set of big wooden doors that cause him to hesitate for a moment. Nevertheless he enters the building. As Cannon enters the building we see rows upon rows of pews. We see a few people toward the front on there knees at the alter praying. Cannon just stands at the back of the church not really doing anything other then taking in his surroundings. He makes his way to a pew and sits down, he looks at Jesus hanging from a cross over the pulpit. Cannon stares at the statue of Jesus for a long time. His gaze is transfixed on the on the slender man wearing the crown of thorns hanging on a cross. As Cannon continues to look he begins to laugh. The laughing continues and it is loud a boisterous. People who are praying turn around and look at Cannon with disgust and contempt on there eyes and as this goes on they all begin to leave the church one by one. Finally the church is empty and Cannon is alone and still sitting there with a smirk on his face. After a few moments an elderly man who is a priest steps in front of Cannon.** Priest: Excuse me sir, do you find something funny? **Cannon looks the man with that wide smirk still written across his face.** Cannon: Yea.. Religion is a joke. That man on the cross is a joke. ** The man just looks at Cannon and shakes his head. The man is obviously a priest.** Priest: My son, I don’t want you hear you say that again. I will be in the confessional and if you want to confess your sins you know where I am. ** Cannon looks at the priest as he disappears. He sits there for a few moments pondering the offer that was just presented to him. After a few moments Cannon gets up and walks down the aisle and makes a turn toward the confessionals. Cannon enters the darkened room and sits down.** Cannon: I don’t know how this shit work. I am not catholic so I don’t know if this is going to work for me or not. ** The priest who is on the other side of the screen begins to talk.** Priest: My son you may not be catholic but confessing your sins is always a good thing. **A smile pierces Cannon’s lips.** Cannon: If I were to confess my sins we would be here until hell freezes over. ** The priest on the other side of the screen nods.** Priest: That is understandable my son… So why don’t we start with your apparent vendetta against Jesus Christ. **Cannon rubs his chin before answering.** Cannon: My vendetta isn’t exactly against Jesus Christ. ** The priest doesn’t answer right away.** Priest: Then why were you laughing at the Savior hanging from the cross? ** Cannon strokes his chin before answering.** Cannon: You see that statue in there represents to me the pain and suffering I want to cause to someone. ** The priest just sits there and ponders what Cannon just side.** Priest: Jesus should represent love to you and forgiveness. ** Cannon nods his head.** Cannon: To most people he does.. But no me. You see, I am a sick fuck. I have beat up children, put my hands on women, gotten completely piss drunk, lusted after women and many other things. I have no real feelings. The only feeling I have is the one of violence and when I look at Jesus on that cross it bubbles to the surface. ** The priest has heard a lot of things in his day but this leaves him mildly shocked.** Priest: If that statue makes you feel violence then why were you laughing? **Cannon lets out a bit of a chuckle.** Cannon: It was sick laugher… It is the only thing that stopped me from getting out of that pew and ripping down that statue and defecating all over it. ** The priest is once again a little shocked at this.** Priest: So what brings on this deep seeded violence? ** Cannon closes his eyes and leans his head back on the wall. He takes a deep breath and slowly exhales before he begins to speak.** Cannon: It is a man by the name of Joey Dallas. He has drove me to this… ** The priest scratches his head on the other side of the screen.** Priest: Why has this man drove you to this point ? ** Cannon with his eyes still closed and head resting against the wall begins to answer.** Cannon: You see he claims to be the savior of people, yet he is a thorn in my side. He has tried to ruin my life at every corner. He has tried to help hold me down! He makes me look at Jesus Christ and feel the hatred of a thousand armies! But this Sunday I shall get my revenge… It will be glorious and bloody. ** The priest just sits there before answering.** Priest: My son, revenge is not the answer. ** Cannon sits up and opens his eyes and laughs.** Cannon: The fuck it isn’t. It is the answer! I don’t care what the bible tells you… This is the only viable answer in this war we have and the last shot will be fired this Sunday. ** The priest openly sighs.** Priest: I am telling you it is not the answer. Revenge only begets revenge and that is never good my son. It only creates for more problems. ** Cannon takes a deep breath.** Cannon: Then what in the hell is my answer? ** The priest sits there for a moment…** Priest: Destiny… ** Cannon shakes his head.** Cannon: That is fucking bullshit…. Destiny isn’t the answer… Revenge is and this conversation is over. **Cannon gets up and walks out of the confessional. The priest is left there..** Priest: He is a poor lost soul… ** The camera then cuts to Cannon who is standing in front of Jesus on the cross. Cannon spits on the statue. He then continues to stare at it and after a few seconds he begins clawing at it and ripping it off the wall. Cannon grabs a chair that sits on the stage and begins banging it against the statue until the chair breaks. He stands there for a moment once again laughing at the statue and after a few tense moments he walks out of the church and we focus back on the priest who is now standing outside of the confessional.** Priest: Him and his enemy will be a part of destiny... A destiny that no one will see coming.. ** The camera fades out on the priest who watches Cannon as he storms out into the London night.**
