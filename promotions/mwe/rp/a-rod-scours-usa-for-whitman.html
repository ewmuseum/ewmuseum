A-Rod scours USA for Whitman.

Aug 9 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
August 9th 2009 by David Guether MWE is happy to announce that former MWE World Champion Alejandro Rodriguez has returned to MWE again, after searching every single place where David Whitman has lived at some point in his life, from his hometown in Phoenix, Arizona, to the remote island of Aruba[*] where Whitman is rumoured to have lived before. This news came after an seemingly agitated General Manager Eli Silver announced to MWE reporters that Alejandro was back, and "ready to work as soon as possible..." Alejandro has been unavailiable for comment and progress on his search for David Whitman is unknown. * - Scientists are still speculating whether Aruba is an island, or just a large rock.
