Title #2; MilenkovsJJ Executioner Havok Title RP

Dec 19 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The camera turns on and we see Milenko sitting in the corner of an MWE ring, The only light on is a Red spot light shining directly on Milenko and an unknown object he has on his shoulder. He looks up at the cameraand the object falls off his shulder and in to his lap. He moves it to where the camera can't see it and he looks up at the camera and starts talking. 'The Great' Milenko At Adreneline Rush I face JJ Executioner for the MWE Havoc Championship and the match is an Inferno match. Most people don't know this but I'm going into this match already a Champion. Milenko picks up what fell and reveals it to be the WZCW Mayhem Championship. 'The Great' Milenko This title means that I cause more mayhem than anyone in this business. I can brawl with the best and spill more blood than anyone and there is nothing anyone can do about it. Milenko stops talking for a second as he puts his title on his shoulder as his mind goes to the backstage fight him and JJ had last month. QUOTE We see the scene begin through the lens of a shuddering camera, which is rushing towards an incident backstage. We see black shirted security running ahead and bumping into the cameraman. As he makes his way around the corner we see JJ Executioner and The Necromaster Milenko brawling furiously backstage. Both men are visibly bloodied and have gone through catering tables and doors in the backstage areas. The bulky security guards try and break up the two but they refuse to be separated and break through them. They continue to connect with punch after punch to each other face, smearing blood onto their fists and all over the floor. Security gets back between the two and somehow manages to get between the two. Eventually they give up trying. JJ gives Milenko an evil grin, gesturing to his eyebrow where Milenko is bleeding. JJ: “You interfering fucker.......I’m gonna take you apart you prick!” Milenko doesn’t respond. He just smiles and allows himself to be pushed away. MWE owner Richard Covell appears from around the corner. He is shocked at the damage and destruction and immediately walks up to JJ. Covell: “I knew you would be behind this. This is coming out of your wages you giant piece of scum!” JJ pushes past the security and goes nose to nose with Covell. Well, not nose to nose, as JJ is significantly taller. JJ: “I want that fucker, one on one, at the earliest opportunity.” JJ’s tone is aggressive and demanding. Milenko That brawl was nothing compared to what will happen during our Inferno match. I will make you regret asking Covell for a 1-on-1 match with me. The fans of MWE have voted us into an Inferno match and the only way to win is ot set your opponent on fire. Milenko smiles a sadistic smile at the thought of setting JJ on fire, the red light giving his face an eerie tint as he starts talking again, this time to Richard Covell. 'The Great' Milenko Covell what I do to JJ Executioner will be on your hands. You wanted ot make money and to see your ratings go up so you decided to put us in the ring against each other for the amusement of others. I hope you're ready for what I'm going to do because it's all your fault. Milenko stops talking to Covell and returns his attention to JJ. Milenko JJ I know you hate me with a passion and would love nothing more than to set me on fire and possibly end my career bu that isn't going to happen. I am more sadistic and cold hearted than you ever were or will be and I have no problem setting another human being on fire. In fact I look forward to hearing your flesh sear and watching it crisp and blacken. I'll see you at Adreneline Rush. Milenko stops talking and smiles another sadistic smile as he gets up and starts to get out of the ring. The red light shining on him makes it see mlike blood is dripping off his title and his face. The last thing seen is a man with his entire body on fire.
