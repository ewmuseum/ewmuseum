The whole week, he stayed at Scotty’s. Right now, he was dressing a wound that he had inflicted on his own ankle. More like carved. After fully dressing his wound and putting his sock and shoe back on, he limped out of the washroom. And that is when he saw her. Demonica was actually there. She wore a beautiful black, strapless dress, that more then complimented her figure. He smiled, as he limped towards the table. Demonica saw him, and weakly smiled. Leon: Thank you so much for coming Demonica. You don’t know how much I appreciate this. Demonica: Yeah, I understand. But you got to understand that you really wounded me. I was this close to actually getting divorce papers. Leon: Shit. I know I would have deserved it as well. Demonica sighed. She took a sip of her water, while having this distant look in her eyes. The physical pain had already passed, but she was still dwelling on what Leon had said. Demonica: You went into an area you shouldn’t have. The punching, the choking…I struggled, yes, but they didn’t hurt me that badly. Well, the punch did. You almost knocked me out there. But yeah. It’s what you said, that felt like you drove a knife into my heart. Leon: I know. Mad or not, I shouldn’t have said that. And believe me, there is not a damn second that goes by that I wish I could go back in time and stop myself from saying that. You aren’t one. In fact, no matter what anyone else says about you, I will always consider you to be the single, most beautiful creature that has ever existed. Those other fools who say otherwise, are either jealous, or ignorant to the fact that you are an angel. Demonica: Well….thanks. But you can quit with the brown nosing though. Leon: Who said I was? I certainly haven’t. I mean it. I guess……I over reacted. I mean, yeah I’m upset that you cheated on me, with one of my best friends, and somebody who tried to rape you before. But that’s irrelevant compared to how I feel. Demonica scoffed at Leon. He sighed, knowing that he was telling her the truth. Demonica took another swig of her water, before putting the glass down. She looked directly into Leon’s eyes. She saw the hurt in them. She also saw the one thing only she was able to see behind those eyes. Love. She was the only person, that could find the love behind the eyes of the one man who often claimed to be the devil’s incarnate. She nodded. Demonica: I believe you Leon. I truly do. When you messaged me last week, I knew that you meant it. You still mean it. That’s why I didn’t go for a divorce. Because I know we can work our way through this. Leon instantly smiled. He then gently took her hand, brought it up to his lips, and kissed it. It made her blush a bit, as he gave her hand back. Demonica: I suppose that I should explain my side of the story as well. Leon: Well, I am curious to know. But if you don’t want to tell me now, I don’t mind. I can wait. Demonica: No. I’m going to tell you. Demonica sighed for a bit. She then adjusted her dress a bit. Leon saw this and chuckled. When she noticed this, she chuckled as well. When she was done. She relaxed herself. She had to, in order for her to explain. Demonica: When I heard about your concussion, I was so distraught. This was much worse then what you had went through in that derby. I saw my one pillar of strength, crumbled down before me. I lost my mind. I was upset. It started the vicious circle for me. Leon: Vicious cir…….Oh. Right. Demonica: Yeah. When I’m really upset, a drink heavily. And when I drink heavily, I become extremely horny, and I need to get laid. Vega had been drinking with me, and thus, he didn’t stop me from jumping on him. Not that he would have if he was sober, knowing him. Leon: Yeah. I know. Hence why I kicked his ass like I did. That, I don’t regret. Demonica: Fair enough. But trust me. Neither one of us intended it to happen. It just….did. And yes…..It does feel as if I’ve betrayed you. A lone tear sprang from Demonica’s left eye. She didn’t bother to wipe it away. But Leon, couldn’t stand a single tear of sadness from his angel, so he reached over, and gently wiped it away. She smiled at him. Demonica: There’s the man I fell in love with. The man who cares. The strong, yet gentle demon that my heart yearns for. Leon: Yeah. Thank you. Hopefully, we can put this behind us for good. Demonica: We will be. But you are very lucky Leon. No other woman is strong enough to put up with you. Leon: Isn’t that the truth. They both chuckled at that. After a minute, they both looked into each other’s eyes. They both leaned over. Slowly, the gap between the two closed, as their lips met in a soft, passionate kiss. Something both had missed out in some time. When they separated, both had a big smile on their faces. Leon: So…..how was your week? Demonica: It was fine. Slow at first. But then something amazing happened. Courtney began to crawl yesterday. Leon: Really? Wow. That’s amazing. Damn. Demonica: I’m sure the boys will be following very soon. But please. Come back. Move back in. Leon: I plan to, but there are a few things I need to do first. I’ll be back tomorrow. Demonica: Alright honey. But yeah. I’m glad we had this talk. Leon: Me to sug. Me to. ~Scotty’s Day~ Scotty was having a bit of a rough day. Board meeting at his father’s company really irritated him to no end. Not to mention the hangover he had. But at least there was some sort of silver lining. He did manage to run into one of the newest MWE wrestlers, in Tali Preston, as she was visiting the city. Currently right now, they were at a local coffee shop, just talking, trying to get to know each other. Tali: Wow. This coffee is really good. Scotty: Yeah. My buddy, Leon recommended this place to me. Tali: Your tag partner? Scotty: Yeah. Tali smiled, just before she took a sip of her coffee. After she took a sip she briefly licked her lips. Scotty continued to smile. He definitely liked what he saw. Tali: Well Mr. McKnight, I am curious about the whole…wiccan thing Scotty: Please…call me Scotty. As for my religion, well what do you want to know? Tali: Well, I’m curious about this voice you claim to have inside your head, due to being wiccan. Scotty: Oh Sky. Well, that’s kind of…hard to explain, but I’ll try. Scotty nervously rubbed the back of his neck, before he would take a sip of his own coffee. He sighed, trying to think of how to explain why he was who he was. Scotty: Well…..I don’t remember when I started hearing Sky’s voice. But she told me about everything she was. About Wiccan. Apparently, I am one of the people around the world, who were born with this….type of guardian. It’s like, leading towards a certain day in the future, where the fate of our world will be decided. Apparently, My spirit guide is the incarnate of the one who will decide. But…as for having the voices in my head…yeah it makes you wonder sometimes at first. But, I trust their instincts. Besides….it’s good company. Tali: Wow. That’s interesting to know. Tali took another sip of her coffee. That was when Scotty’s cell phone went off. He checked it. It was a text from Leon. He smiled. Tali: What was that? Scotty: A text from my partner. He just got back together with his wife. They had a huge argument last week. Tali: Ah. Well that’s good. Anyways Scotty, I have to get going. Thank you for the coffee, and I’ll see you on Monday at the show. Scotty: Yeah. Don’t worry about the bill here. I got it. Tali: Thanks. See you later. Tali got up from her seat, and left. Scotty sighed, as he watched her leave Sky: That is a fine piece of ass isn’t it. Scotty (thoughts): Yes It is Sky... Yes it is. I’m hoping that I become…more then friends with her. Just as he was about to get into his car, he got another text from Leon. This one mentioned they should meet at Scotty’s for one more promo. Scotty sighed. He was out of his trance.
