Turning Heads by Prospering; Sonny Williams PPV RP

Sonny Williams

QUOTE "Is it time to wake up? I see nothing but blank darkness, so I guess not. But it is time for something else.. Something that has been beckoning and tampering for a long, long time. The thought of what is coming, it brings a chill up my spine. The thought I have is like gold. It's precious, it's powerful, it's what a man desires. But now.. .. Here and now. I can not believe it is here and ready to be announced. DAVID WHITMAN vs. SONNY WILLIAMS This is what I've been thinking, those nights I was laying my head back staring at the ceiling. Honest to god, I never thought this day would come. But it has. I keep asking myself the same question, am I ready? The question is yet to be answered. Ahh, yes, I can picture it now.. Can't be Touched is blasting the arena as I walk out to a curious and anticipation packed crowd. You can smell the excitement in the air. The day I've dreamed, the day is here. From the days of Mayhem Mafia, to this, the biggest showdown I could imagine. Period. This isn't a fight for pleasure of succeeding.. No, this is more than just that. This is a fight to declare who is the better man.. This is a fight for the biggest prize in the industry. THE MWE WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP! And to answer the question.. HELL YEAH!" My eyes widened open, as I raised my body up in excitement of what I was dreaming. I knew it was looming to come, but it all felt surreal. Every step of the way, it feels like each bit is like a scene from a movie.. But it's not. This is happening to me, not George Clooney, or Sylvester Stallone staring in the latest Rocky film. I looked to the window. It was still pitch black outside as I swiftly turned to look at the beaming red digital clock. 4:48AM. Fuck. I lay my head back on the pillow, staring at the ceiling with a million thoughts running through my head. But only one stood out of all; My Match at Wrestle-Wars. I remember the days, one whole year ago, when Brian Michaels could actually get a root, when Alejandro Rodriguez was trying to disclaim himself of the name 'Mighty Mouse' and when Aiden Copane actually turned up to shows. But I think the most remembering moment was when David Whitman lost his title after two weeks of holding it. Ahh yes, I can remember every little move that was made in that pinical point in MWE's history. I'm not David Whitman though, you see when I say I'll do something, I do it. If I knew I was going to lose at Wrestle-Wars, there is no way in hell that I'd be this.. Confident. But I also remember one year back, when I was nothing but a match filler, the guy the boss would call up if one of his wrestlers had issues to tend to instead. You see, there's a lot differencing between me and Whitman. He started at the top of the leagues, he doesn't know what it feels like to be told by the owner of the promotion that they might not be able to fit you in to their salary. I had to work for my position, I wasn't handed success like he was. I had to defeat men just to keep my career alive. I've defeated guys who alike me started from scratch to build a legacy of their name, but you know what, I have built myself a legacy in that period of time, a strong legacy unlike Whitman. But what really is the legacy of David Whitman? Pure motherfucking luck. Thomas Jefferson once said that the harder you work, the more luck you will have, and he was a strong believer in that quote. That doesn't make him a worthy Hall of Famer. I went from fucking rags to riches and claimed the biggest prize in Sports Entertainment. Doesn't that make me a more worthy option? Anyway, we all do know that luck gradually runs out, right? Don't get me wrong, I like that in him, because you see I want to become the best wrestler I can become and the most difficult part of attaining perfection is finding something to do for an encore. I'm as close as it gets to perfect, and if Whitman is as lucky and good as everyone makes him out to be, then he'll step up to the plate and be that very encore. But what motivates me most of all, is how I go into this match as the Under-dog, like time after time. People doubt my talent, but haven't I proven them e-fucking-nough? It's like this whole match is about David Whitman going for his third MWE World Heavyweight Championship, not Sonny Williams' title defence. But do I care anymore? No. I don't care anymore because it doesn't matter. It doesn't matter if you sell the most merchandise in the company, it doesn't even matter if you're the boss's son. What matters if you are holding the MWE World Heavyweight Championship, which I am. My thoughts, my heart, my sweat, my blood made this what it is today and that is what really matters in this industry. People have asked me the question, what kind of Sonny Williams will we see at Wrestle-Wars? You're not going to get the old Sonny Williams, or the one you've come to know.. You're going to see a man who is motivated on succeeding.. A man who wants nothing more than to prove to the whole world that he is better than David Whitman and the best this promotion has to offer. I'm better than Whitman, I've surpassed his talent and did that a very long time ago. I have got the ideal mix of attributes to take me to a win, that Whitman hasn't. You see, physical strength can be acquired in a gym, wisdom through experience, and knowledge between the covers of books. But true power is only obtained when the balance of these three assets is ideal for the tasks which we seek to accomplish. What is physical strength without knowledge of correct application? We cannot expect to gain wisdom before conquering our mental barriers. And physical strength which lacks the appropriate avenues to be applied is exertion in vain. How can we expect to progress to greatness without first tasting defeat. This is the wisdom which is essential in success. I'm not going into Sunday to entertain people, I'm going out there to prove everyone wrong. To show them that I am the best there is in this industry. And to retain what will be mine for the next fucking millennium, THE MWE WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP! I look back over to the red beaming digital clock which read the time. 5:35AM WOW!
