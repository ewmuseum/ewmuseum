The cameraman was holding his camera with his hat backwards and his frame glasses. Fear taps his shoulder. He turns around and Fear splashes water at his face. He grins as the camera men wipes off the water from his face.* Fear: Hey what’s your name? Cameraman: Greg? (freaked out voice) Fear: Nice name nerd bomb why so afraid huh. Set this shit up fast. *The Cameraman quickly looks for red switch then press it as it lights up* the red switch to turn it on* Fear: Hey ladies and gentlemen. It’s Fear here! Im not very popular amongst you losers called fans but im getting up there.. *He grins at the camera and looks to the side where the cameraman is stand* Fear: How about a round of applause for our cameraman Gary for his work I mean look at my face on HD. Cameraman: It Greg Sir. *Fear grabs the cameraman’s shirt fiercely as the cameraman looks right into his eyes with fear* Fear: See I am use to that look on peoples faces. Get out of my face chump. Your lucky I’m good mood. *Fear Laughs at the cameraman as he runs away* Fear: Thanks for your help but I’m keeping the camera. *Fear picks up the camera and stares at it* Fear: Look at him run. See that an exact example of MWE fans. When I go out the fans boo me and put me down but they can’t touch me. They’re just too afraid but I love you guys because, because you guys pay me and I live the good life while ya work your asses off. *He laughs then waves hi at the camera* Fear: As you can see I’m clearly victorious. Look at this smile.
