An Arrested Development; Virus ID RP

Jun 29 2009

Leon Roberts

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A police officer is seen walking through the Toronto MCU. It was a busy week for them, as they had to catch one really crazy man who used a police car to go on a massive joyride the other day. Despite the property damage, everything was taken care of. The man actually paid for it, and then was let go after posting a hefty bail. Oh and the best part about this incident, was the fact a lot of people knew this man. It was Leon ‘The Virus’ Roberts. Anyways, the police officer actually was there for a reason. To release him. They approached the cell of the deranged wrestler. He was kept in with other inmates. A lot of them being some meth heads, crack heads, and gang-bangers. But Leon had his sinister smile on him. He fed off of people’s negativity. The strange thing was, those men had backed away from him, after seeing what he could do. The thing that happened was, one of them had began to taunt Leon for winding up in there with them. Basic stuff those guys do. The guy was a big muscular guy, having a bald head, a big red beard, and sported a leather jacket. He had gotten a little close to Leon for his tastes, so Leon had managed to wrap the man’s arm around a couple of bars, before breaking it. The man cried in pain. Leon knelt down, and looked at the man, as he began to talk. Leon: Not so tough now, aren’t you? But I gotta admit this. Your arm was good practice for what I plan to do to my next victim inside the wrestling ring. Leon rose up, and looked at the other prisoners. They were at the opposite side of the cell. One of the officers tapped onto one of the bars a couple times, with his baton. Officer: Okay Leon, your bail has been paid for, and the charges dropped. I seriously don’t know how you do it. Leon looked at the officer. He tilted his head to the left a bit, still with that smirk on his face. A faint chuckle could be heard from Leon. The officer was not intimidated immediately, but after a couple seconds, it looked like he was. He then looked over and saw the injured man. Officer: Your doing? Leon: Naturally. It’s only fitting that I broke his arm in the way I did. Afterall, nobody takes advantage of me. But I admit, I can feel the sins these low lives have caused. Some regret, some don’t. But regardless. I wouldn’t have touched him had he not given me reason to. The officer sighed. He unlocked the cell. At that, the others tried to run towards Leon, trying to escape. But Leon turned around, and punched the nearest one in the face, right at the nose, effectively breaking it. This sent the group of prisoners back a bit, while Leon quickly got out. Leon: Just remember this. What they say about me, is true. Officer: Yeah yeah, let’s get you out of here. And here’s your clothes. The officer gave Leon a bag, which contained all his clothes. Leon smiled, as he went to the nearest change room. He would emerge from there in a black T shirt, a bandanna under his hair, his spiked wristbands, and black jeans just two minutes later. After that, He left the MCU, and got into a car, which was driven by Vega. They took off. While driving, Vega sighed. He just didn’t understand what made Leon want to actually do what he did. Leon: Awkward silence, much? Vega just ignored Leon. Leon clearly could see his friend’s look of anger. It made him smile, knowing that just by talking, he had gotten under his friend’s skin. But after a few minutes of driving, Vega stopped the car at a mall parking lot. Neither men got out of the car. Vega: You do realize that your wife isn’t going to be to happy. A couple weeks ago was bad enough when you needed those sedatives to numb the shock, but this shit? Why did you have to steal a fucken police car, fucken drive around Toronto like a maniac, and do some property damage. I mean, this isn’t like you man. Leon: On the contrary Vega. This is exactly me. You know how I am. You know that I do things on a whim. Vega: Yeah, but you should think about the consequences once in a while. Leon: There is no time to do that, ever. Consequences are a hindrance to my fun, and I refuse to fucken acknowledge them. Vega: Hmpf. That’s what I used to believe in as well. But I learned. At that, Leon bursts out laughing at that statement. Vega was shocked at his friend’s response. Leon would not stop laughing. Vega sighed. His patience had worn thin. He went to give Leon a hard slap across the face, when Leon caught it. Vega was surprised by this. Leon had stopped laughing, staring into Vega’s eyes, with a slight bit of malice behind them. Leon: I’ve already broken a guy’s forearm and bent it into a position it shouldn’t be in today, Don’t make me do the same to you. Just because you’re my best friend, doesn’t mean I wouldn’t do it. Vega growled for a second. He withdrew his arm from Leon’s grasp, and snarled at Leon. Leon still chuckled at Vega, before he stopped. Leon: But can I ask you something? Have you truly changed? I know that your personalities have become one, but you do have that mean streak in you. I know you better then you know yourself Vega. I know which buttons to push to send you back down to being a two faced bastard, and I won’t hesitate to do so if need be. Because as much as you don’t like being it, all you are besides that shit, is nothing more then a prank pulling, color blind piece of crap. Leon could see Vega’s nostrils flair. Vega then sighed. Vega: Perhaps your right. But damn dude. You have an extremely important match coming up at Independence Day, and you can’t afford to be doing any shit that you just did. Leon: Indeed I do. Mason Resoan. A fellow Canadian. Fighting for the United States Championship. A great opportunity, especially since I will be legally allowed to break stuff, over his soon to be dead body. Vega: Well at least you have the right mind for this match. But please, tell me what happened. Knowing you, it should be an interesting story. Leon: Well it all happened a week ago, after I kicked Kaymackian’s ass...... ~~FLASHBACK~~ Leon was at the coffee shop he liked to visit. He had about five cups of coffee already. Naturally, he was feeling quite hyper. He noticed a single police officer was having a donut and coffee. Leon growled Leon (thoughts): what the fuck do those fuckers think they are anyways. They think they have the right to control the world. Trying to work for the higher ups to keep the so called public offenders off the streets. They’re all lazy ass donut munchers, who can’t do shit. I hate them so fucking much. Hell, I’ll bet half the time they bust the crackheads, they sniff their evidence. And that overweight fuck, I bet he’s had a couple sniffs himself, just because he is part of the so called law. Well if only you knew. Knew all the corruption that happens. Like how maybe sometimes, those fucken times when a police officer dies in the line of duty, isn’t accidental or a result of a gang banger getting a good shot. Somebody should teach those fucks a lesson. Leon then smirked, seeing the semi fat and balding officer munch down yet another donut. The gears in Leon’s twisted brain kicked into overdrive. He had thought of something that he should do, and the idea to him, he liked it. Leon (Thoughts): This city needs an enema. Don’t care of the size, but it needs one. Besides, I got so much pent up energy that it could last me a few hours worth of iron man table matches. Damn this coffee is good. Leon immediately got up from his seat and left. He had already paid for his bill. He walked out of there normally, to avoid some suspicion. He was going to steal a car, and go for a ride. That was his plan. But he noticed the police car was parked in the nearby alley. Naturally, the perverse and chaotic thoughts took over, with a total disregard for the rules. Taking off his flame bandanna, Leon wrapped it around his right hand, and punched the driver seat window in. Leon was surprised that there was no alarm going off, but didn’t hesitate to unlock the door and step in. Leon was also surprised to see the keys dangling from the visor. He slimed, snatching them, and placed them in the ignition, and fired the car up. Leon: Stupid idiot cop. Now. It’s time for a.........JOYRIDE!!!!!!!!! Leon shifts to drive, and slams his foot against the gas pedal, speeding off from there. He also knocks over a fire hydrant, which sprays the car and a lot of people Leon: Well, it is a pretty hot day out there, HAHAHAHAHA!!!!!!!!! Leon still keeps going, and getting faster and faster. Surprisingly, he doesn’t hit anybody. It’s not long before he’s being chased by other cops. He smirks, turning on the radio Leon: Fuckers. But they’re only doing their job. Dammit.....can’t waste them like I plan to waste Mason at Independence Day. Leon still drove at a face pace, even as he saw a large pane of glass being wheeled across the street. The workers there knew that the car Leon was driving would not stop, so they ran. Leon smirked, seeing a small ramp off to the side. He sped forward, and jumped off it, through the glass. There were a few cracks in the windshield due to that, but the glass pane had been shattered. Leon’s police car landed, creating a couple of sparks at the bottom hit the ground Leon: WHOO! This is what I’m talkin’ about now bitches. The chase continued, as Leon dodged oncoming traffic pedestrians, and also bashed a few more fire hydrants. He then got out onto the Gardner expressway, when he overheard the cops plan to spike him. He then picked up the radio communicator. He didn’t care if they recognized his voice at this point in time. Leon:-Pshh-You guys realize I can hear everything, so whatever you are planning isn’t going to work. Officer:-Pshh-Citizen, stop this vehicle right now! Leon:-Pshh- And get arrested? HA! You guys must be joking. I’m having the time of my life here. Officer:-Pshh-If you stop the vehicle now, you may not face to many charges. You’re putting people’s lives at stake here. Leon:-Pshh-Yeah well obesity and smoking will do the jobs for me, if not he guns or old age. Face it. This is one guy who lives to raise a little hell. That being said, this whole chase...I’m surprised it didn’t start sooner. So I just have one thing to say to that. Leon sighed, and he went off the next ramp, which was Spadina avenue. He chuckled, because he always wanted to say something like he was about to say. Leon: -Pshh- You been hit by....you been struck by....a SMOOTH CRIMINAL! HA! Leon tossed the communicator piece to the side as he began laughing. But he didn’t pay attention for a second, and clipped a guy. Leon did see that, and smirked to himself. Leon: Well they can add Hit and Run to the shit I’ve done today. Leon still kept going, and the police cars were still trying to catch him. Leon the noticed the tank was running on empty. He mentally cursed, forgetting the one thing that could ruin his day. And it did. He sighed, knowing his fun was over. He immediately turned into a nearby garage building, breaking the stop arm on the way in. He kept going up and up, the police cars still chasing him, until finally, at the fifteenths floor of the garage building, he stopped. He sighed, and stepped out, with his hands up in the air. Quite a few officers surrounded him. One pushed him against the car, and patted him down. Leon: Careful there, otherwise I’d think you’d want me. Leon laughed at his little, but overused joke, while another officer read him his rights. Leon rolled his eyes at that, knowing them full well. ~~END FLASHBACK~~ Vega was stunned after Leon described the entire event. His jaw was literally hanging, threatening to fall off. Leon saw this, and snapped his fingers twice, which snapped Vega out of his daze. Vega: Whoa man. That actually did sound like fun. But yeah, I remember that chase, it was all over the news. Man. I’m still disappointed in you though. Leon: I don’t care Vega, all I care about is breaking things, which include laws, rules, tables, trash cans, glass, and bones of my opposition, no matter who it be. I’m always on a war path. Vega: Yeah. But dude..I can’t believe you actually taunted those cops with Smooth Criminal. That must have been like...a bad omen or something. Leon looked at Vega skeptically, even raising an eyebrow at this. Seeing that Vega wasn’t responding, Leon scoffed at what Vega had just said. Leon: The fuck do you mean man. It couldn’t have been like he passed away or something. Vega: Actually.......... Leon stared at Vega for a second. That was something he did not see coming. He felt kinda bad for saying that. Leon sighed, hanging his head in shame. Leon: Seriously? Damn. Now I really feel bad. How did it happen? Vega: Cardiac Arrest. Found at his home. Tried to revive him but........didn’t happen. Leon: Wow. Well this is a surprise, but we can’t dwell on this. I got me a match to win, and I need some props. Vega: Whatever. Three hours later, Vega and Leon were back at Leon’s house. He smirked as he entered into his house. He knew that Demonica was non to pleased with him, especially the look she was giving him, followed by a sound smack across the face. But Leon still smirked after receiving that. Leon: Not surprised at all by your reaction Sug. I mean it’s been what....a week since you last got laid. Demonica’s jaw dropped, as she went to punch Leon across the face, for that remark. Leon grabbed her arm, and pulled her in, forcing a powerful kiss onto her. She tried to resist, but Leon didn’t let her. She eventually did enjoy the kiss, but Leon quickly broke the kiss, releasing her arm. Leon: You know sug, those lips of yours almost made me regret what I did. But not enough. No. I had so much fun doing that, and showed just how incompetent those police officers really were. It’s not like I killed anyone. Demonica: But you easily could have. That’s what I’m so worried about. Leon: Relax Demonica. I knew that I had all my bases covered. I mean, I know what’s on the line coming up at Independence Day. I know that this could be the next big break that I need. If I knew I couldn’t get away with what I did, then I wouldn’t have done it. See, there is a reason why money works, and so does skeletons. Demonca: So, you paid off the right people to get your ass out? Leon: Well I knew you couldn’t wait to stare at my ass again, so I posted the bail, paid for the damages, and of course......Got the one judge that I wanted. Demonica *sigh*: You’re impossible sometimes, you know that. Leon: I know. But it’s who I am. I am meant to do the things that everyone else can only dream about doing. I am the one person, who is able to live out everyone’s sick and twisted dreams, and urges. I can tell a priest to go and suck my nuts, then kick his balls off, then burn the church down, followed by talking to all my friends about it. I am the embodiment of Sin, and sometimes, I need to do stuff like this to remind people about that. Demonica: Good point. But still......you ain’t getting laid tonight. Until Indepedence Day, you’re sleeping on the fucken couch. Leon was stunned, Vega had heard the whole conversation, and smirked to himself, before he burst out laughing like a madman. Leon angrily shot a glare at him, but that did not shut Vega up. Vega: Duuude. Even the devil himself goes in the doghouse. Leon: Just set the fucken tables up. One hour later, Leon had set everything up in his basement. A few buffet tables, a couple of DVDs, the sixty inch TV, and a weed whacker. The camcorder was recording now. Even in his foul mood, Leon still smirked, as he began his promo. Leon: I’m not sure if anyone knows this, but in case if some of you haven’t, I was arrested last week. I admit, it was my own doing, as I decided to steal a cop car and go joyriding. Do I take it back? Fuck no. That’s not the kind of person I am. I wanted to have some fun, and I did so. I mean, I needed to just blow off some stress, and Demonica wasn’t around at the time, so I couldn’t get laid. Well, I could have with some random chick, but it would have sucked compared to how good Demonica truly is. But Enough about that. As you are all definitely aware, I had made a big announcement on the last Monday Night Mayhem, and needless to say, the wrestling world has been talking about it non stop. I made the announcement, that I was going to face non other, then former MWE champion Mason Resoan, who by the way, won the title two years ago, almost to the day, if I recall. And people, particularly over the IWC, have been betting that Mason will defeat me. Well as good as Mason Resoan is, has he ever come to face somebody remotely like me? I don’t recall so. Here is a guy who’s finally just taking it easy, being a clam, cool, relaxed person, instead of the hyper and sarcastic son of a bitch he used to be. That my fellow anarchists, is a mistake that will cost him dearly. Leon stretches out his arms, and leans a back a bit, letting the camera get a good view of his scarred eight pack. In fact, a lot of the scars that Leon has accumulated is actually visible, including the bullet wounds he had received when he was 15. Leon finishes stretching, and looks back at the camera. Leon: However, it’s not my business whether or not if Mason wants to be himself or not, but I believe it is a mistake. See, what I’ve done, what I do, what I’m capable of doing, Mason will be getting all of that. I’m the kind of guy who likes to hurt people. It’s not just a personal bloodlust, but just the way I see the world. I see it for what it is, and needless to say, peace, especially at this point, is truly a foreign concept. Or maybe it’s because I’ve chucked my compassion for others out the door when I was almost killed, I don’t know. What I do know, is that I don’t go around everywhere, making childish insults, screaming my name out loud, or just swear constantly. I am not just a talker, I am a man of action. Maybe, that’s what’s going to make the 30 Minute Iron Man tables match between me and Mason oh so much more interesting to watch, because here is a match between two people who want to win. Both who want to strive back up to the top. And yet, everyone is saying that my chances aren’t looking to good, mainly because I’ve been dwelling in the tag team circuit for to long. Well maybe. But maybe, you should remember what I have done in my singles career as well. That will be enough reason to believe I aam capable of winning this important match. Leon moves out of view for just one minute. There is some noise heard, as it seems like Leon is trying to take something down. When he comes back into view, there are two glass cases on the table that he had set up in front of him. Leon then went to grab another. He placed that one on top of the two. He then turned all three of them around. They were championship belts. Leon: In case if people have forgotten, I’ve held a few good championships during my time as a wrestler. The first belt you see, the one with the Red Strap and gold plate, that was the GWC Ultimate championship. I won that title when I had a whole roster trying to get that title as well. Yet I was more hardcore then anyone else there, and I won it. But the beautiful thing is, I can honestly claim I’ve never lost it, as when GWC folded, I was the last person to hold this title. But the belt beside it, is another GWC title. The Underground title. I can also claim I never lost this belt, as It was vacated in a title sweep. But it’s not those details, it’s about HOW I won this baby. The champion hit me a few times, but all I needed was just one single heart punch, and the bitch was down and out. But...but...the one on top is my most recognizable title. A title people knew I was destined to carry. Leon opened the case, and took the title out. He showed it up close to the camera for a minute. Obviously, this was the one title that meant the world to Leon the most. I twas, of course, the EW World Championship. Leon: This title is the EW world title. The very first title I ever won. I had to beat guys like Sonny Williams....Twice. I had to go through a fatal four way elimination match. I even defeated Alejandro Rodriguez for this title, in a fucken 60 minute Iron Man match. But what is the point of me showing all of this. The point is, despite my tag team success with my best friend, Supernova, I am a very much accomplished singles wrestler. I took an extended break from being a singles wrestler, to let me refocus myself for when the time came for me to take over the wrestling world. In case if people have forgotten, I’m also the one person who also blew Paige up...or at least tried to, but the girl managed to escape. I am also the one who can officially say drove Ace Cannon into madness. He wanted to pick a fight with me, and made him a disturbed, mentally scarred, and violently compulsive individual who eventually got his ass kicked out of MWE for good.
