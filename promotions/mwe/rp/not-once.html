Not Once; A David Whitman RP

Jan 25 2009

David Whitman

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The scene opens up to an unbelievably bright light… THE brightest one around. The sun. The beautiful, beautiful, sun. It shines, as is its proclivity, on this incredible day. The clichéd birds are chirping clichédly, the prototypical morning people are out, about, and whistling, and heck, even the good ol’ life-affirming air is as crisp and fresh as it should be on this, the epitome of perfect days. And ON this wonderful day, we can see the camera move around a bit and show us a park, in all its generically yet admittedly not unpretty glory. The aforementioned birds come into view as we see the three feathered siblings chirping along merrily on a tree branch. The camera moves along a bit more, focusing out a bit. We see the park pathway from afar, and the token park bench. On it, we can just about make out a male figure, with a belt-shaped object on each shoulder. As the camera is still having trouble focusing and/or zooming in, what appear to be a couple of birds, pigeons most probably, decide to land around our hero in a very Disney-like manner, making the scene that much more pleasant- even if slightly blurry. All of a sudden, our supposed hero begins swatting at the birds, scaring them away instantly as the cry of a boy nearby; “Mommy! The poor pigeys!”, decimates what was otherwise a very touching scene. A second passes as the camera gets in focus just enough for us to make out the angry man’s face and realize it is that of ICW’s Semi staring into the camera- And then it abruptly cuts to black. The camera abruptly cuts to a run-down neighborhood. Not quite the worst ghetto ever, but run-down in every sense of the word. Dead trees, litter everywhere, potholes… the camera moves along a bit to the driveway- if you can call it that- of a dirty-white, slightly beat up old house. Some sort of rap song is faintly heard as the camera gets closer to the gray 2008 Porsche 911 GT2. I hate car names, by the way. They sound ridiculous. I’m always like, “What is this, a serial code?” But I digress. As the camera cuts to the inside of the car we can now clearly hear the song proclaiming “F*CK THE POLICE F*CK’EM!” loudly and proudly as we see David Whitman and a cute little blonde girl who appears to be around 12 years old or so headbanging and singing along. David abruptly turns off the radio as he turns to the kid. David: You know, I really shouldn’t let you listen to this sort of stuff. Girl: Oh come ON, Unca D! You even said it yourself; I’m way smart and mature for my age! David pats her on the head while nodding. David: Yeah, you’re a regular Eli Silver. Girl: Oh, Ha-ha. They share a laugh. As it becomes quiet, David looks outside at the unkempt yard, the dirty house and the general unclean feeling of the whole area. He sighs and looks at his niece. David: Nikki… this isn’t right. You shouldn’t be living in a place like this. I’m making a good living, your uncle James still has a ton of money left… The little girl interrupts him. Nikki: Hey, come on now, Unc. I understand how life works. The statement prompts a chuckle out of David, and his niece follows (as kids tend to do). He lets out another sigh before turning to the camera, and then back to Nikki. David: You know, I’m usually not that big on little kids, but you’re pretty awesome, dude. Nikki: Gee, I’m sure your fanbase will love hearing that. David starts laughing loudly. David: Come on now! You know what I freakin' mean! He feigns punching her in the arm and she fakes being hurt really bad as the overacting nearly sets the MWE camera on fire due to excessive lameness. But hey, sometimes lame is funny.* *see above David: You’re an idiot. Nikki: The mirror’s over THERE, Unc! David: Oh HA-HA. The two share a laugh, and promptly follow with a little hug. David: Anyway, enough of this SAPFEST! Nikki: YEAH! David: Let’s go on in. Nikki: Well we can’t stay out here all day, that’s for sure. They share yet another laugh. The camera cuts to outside of the car to see David stepping out as Nikki grabs a big bright red gym bag and steps out as well. They both take a few steps and are now standing next to each other in front of the house as they take some time to look at it. The camera is behind them as we can see their backs and some of the house. David has a grey shirt on that says “Whitman’s Win-Loss Record” and the green bar under the Win is pretty small as the red bar under the Loss extends to over the word Loss itself. The camera cuts to the front of them and on the front of David’s shirt there is the image of what appears to be a paper cutout replica of the old MWE title hugging the waist area. Nikki is wearing the MWE three words T-Shirt (You know where to get it) and a C.R.E.A.M. hoodie over it. She probably shouldn’t really be wearing that, but by now we’ve established that David is by far THE worst uncle on the face of the earth. And besides, Paige FTW. David and Nikki both start walking towards the house and enter with a loud creak from the old, old door. The camera cuts to inside the home as we see a battered and scratched up old sofa, an old table- just think of that house you’ve been to that just makes you feel kind of bad for the people living there- well this one is maybe just a slight step above that. Real lower mid-class to upper low-class. Sitting on the aforementioned couch is a not ugly but slightly unkempt dirty-blonde woman. Her green eyes are fixated on the television screen, which has its volume turned down. The woman is clad in faded pink and purple pajamas and has a cigarette in one hand and a half-empty glass of water in the other. (See what I did there?) David and his niece walk into the scene and the woman instantaneously puts out the cigarette in the ashtray on the table as the beaming young girl runs up to her. Nikki: MOM! Unca D got me a ton of great stuff! The woman smiles as she hugs her daughter. She coughs a bit before answering. Woman: O really? Nikki places her gym bag on the table, opens it up, and takes out an electronic turntable. Woman: Ah. Nikki punches her mom in the arm. Nikki: Not “ah”, “WHOA!” And check THESE out! She takes out a bunch of LPs and giddily shows her mom them as David just smiles. Nikki: Grand Funk, Mystic Lion, some Foghat, the Blues Brothers album- David: BRIEFCASE OF BLUES, BIAAAA! Nikki turns to David and they share an ecstatic high-five. Nikki’s mom lets out a laugh, followed by a quick sigh. Woman: Calm down, you! Why dontcha run on to your room and give those a listen and let me and your uncle talk for a bit, eh? Nikki rolls her eyes as she packs up her stuff back into the gym bag. She turns to her uncle, who shrugs. She runs up to him and gives him a hug before leaving to her room. The atmosphere changes immediately as absence of the full-of-life ball of energy starts to sink in and blends with the depressing feel of the general area. The woman on the couch relights her cigarette. David is leaning on the wall and looking around the so-called “living” room. David: So… Nicolette’s turning out quite great. Woman: Yeah… David: And uh… your… wallpaper is uh… fun. David points to a particularly ravaged-looking section of the wall. The woman lets out a giggle. Woman: You’re an idiot, you know that? David smiles at her. David: Yup. She points at his shirt as she chuckles lightly. Woman: Nice tee. Trying to build up your confidence for your next match? David: You know how I roll, wild and out of control. They share a chuckle. The room turns silent again for a bit before the woman turns her attention to the camera. Woman: And this? As I recall, this sort of thing hasn’t ever really been your style. David: I take it you missed my last match and the life swap? She scoffs. Woman: You know I don’t watch wrestling anymore. David walks over to the couch and takes a seat next to her. He pats her on the shoulder. David: But you should, Jess. You really should. They share another chuckle. Jess: What’s it been, five years? David: Well, unless you’re counting the last family gathering, which you really, REALLY shouldn’t- then about… eight years, give or take. Jess: Whoa. David: Yup. David takes a deep breath, but coughs a bit as he isn’t really used to such smoky environments. Jess: What made you change your mind about us? David: You know it was never about that. James has always been there for me, and it wasn’t like I had to choose him over you, it just- you know. I needed him in my life as a guide, you know? He always showed me the way, and… Look, I’m really sorry. The woman stares at the lit end of her cigarette. Jess: It’s ok. He’s MY brother, and it’s our problem, not yours. HE’S the one who doesn’t want me in his life. He shouldn’t have dragged you into it. It’s messed up that he made you choose in the first place. David: He DIDN’T… well, I mean, he just didn’t want me talking to you. She scoffs. Jess: Or your niece. David: Or- yeah, that. Bu- Jess: Look, whatever. This isn’t something we should discuss right now. David: You’re right. The atmosphere seems a bit more relaxed now as David slumps back in the sofa and puts his feet up on the table. Jess is now blowing smoke rings, which makes David laugh. She offers him a cigarette without really expecting an answer, and he ignores her. Jess: So then, ok, what changed? Why are you here now? David: Well… ok, you haven’t been following MWE, but you know about my problems, right? Well, recently, some big changes happened. I’ve taken the steps necessary to change my path. I’m going to fix everything. I’m turning my life around. Jess: Sweet. David: Sweet indeed. The moment I decided to change my life, I wasn’t really sure it would work out- but then I became the MWE Undisputed champ. So uh, that’s good. They chuckle yet again, as Whitmans apparently do all the time. Jess: Nice, congrats. David: I’m far from out of the woods, though. Covell, in his usual villain of the year manner, decided it would be a good idea to have me fight the man who just leaped into main event territory with an amazing match last Mayhem. Never mind that I had to go through hell and back to pull out the win against A-Rod and D.C., ‘cause hey, a technical wrestling success story might just be the thing to ease me into my new championship phase huh? Jess: Calm down! David: I AM calm. I’m just… tired. David lets out a big sigh as Jess puts out her cigarette in the ashtray. Jess: You can do it. I believe in you. As David turns to look at her, she reaches for her pack of cigarettes, takes another one out of it, lights it, and starts smoking it. David looks at her dumbfounded. David: That’s IT? I can do it, you believe in me? THAT’S your big pep-talk? I have a big fight against the British Alejandro Rodriguez and THAT’S what you wanna support me with? Jess chuckles nervously as David rubs his face with his right hand, while I myself laugh out loud at that British Alejandro crack. David looks serious as he turns to her again. David: This guy overcomes obstacles for a living. All I can be sure of right now is that coming off that big win, he’s coming into this match cocky, and I might be able to use that. Other than that, it seems ol’ Davey Whitman isn’t getting his resting period. Jess: Still a drama queen then, eh? David gets a look of disbelief on his face. David: Seriously, I don’t know what’s WITH you people. Is everybody expecting me to be superhuman? Couple of weeks ago I had to bring everything I had to the ring, and between the past ten house shows, public appearances, meetings and interviews, I’ve barely gotten any time to myself to recover. Hopefully I’m gonna be able to give Misty my all, but… David stops talking. He sits there in a pensive state for a bit as Jess begins looking worried. Jess: …You ok? David suddenly gets a smile on his face as he rises up from the couch. David: Never better. Without looking back, he starts walking. He enters a room as the camera follows him and then cuts to within the room as he walks over to his little niece who is sitting next to her new record player on a bean bag chair listening to an LP. She stops it as she looks up at her uncle. David: Hey, you. Nikki: Hey. You guys done with your grown-up talk? David chuckles as he sits down next to her on another bean bag. He looks up at the various wrestling posters on her wall… Alex Bowen, Thomas Williams, D.C., and a whole slew of other wrestlers. David: Pretty much. I almost fell back into my old self again there for a minute. Nikki: Fell back into yourself? David: Meh, you know what I mean. Nikki: Sure. You just might wanna word things better. David laughs and promptly fake-strangles his little niece. She slaps him upside the head as he reacts exaggeratedly. After they both calm down, David starts sifting through the LPs. David: How you liking your presents? Nikki: They’re ok, but I like the bigger picture. Her uncle shoots her a look of utter confusion. David: Huh? Nikki: I like that they’re a small part that represents a new chapter. David: … in your life? Nikki: In all our lives. They share a content smile. Nikki: So Unc, you ready to face the Gra- David interrupts her and slams his right fist hard into his left hand. David: Heynow! Let me tell you something, I’ve never been more ready. Well, a couple of times, but that’s not the point. The point is, that come Monday, I’m going to be fit and in shape. I’m going to be expecting a man willing to do anything to prove himself to the world. I know when I get into that ring it’s gonna be a fight to the finish… and it’s gonna with pure exhaustion. When the Big Bang meets the Burning Hammer. Good… Evil. Ground onslaught versus aerial assault. Nikki looks captivated by the impromptu improv talk session. David: I hear he’s coming into this with the intention of giving it his all, and the thought that that will be enough to take me out. Well, since I’m bringing my all as well, we’ll just have to see about that, won’t we? Nikki does her uncle’s pose. David: That’s right! They both start laughing loudly, but are interrupted by a yell from the living room. Nikki rolls her eyes. Nikki: B R B. David shakes his head jokingly. David: Kids these days. He pats her on the head as she leaves the room laughing. David gets up off the bean bag chair and stands there silently for a bit before cracking his neck and knuckles. He then grins at the camera. David: Wanna see a magic trick? He passes his hands slowly over the paper belt design on his shirt. Nothing happens. David looks at the design and feigns confusion. David: Whaaat… That’s odd. It usually disappears right away. He turns back to the camera and grins again. He proceeds to pass his hands over the belt numerous times, increasing in pace each time, but nothing works. The design stays there. David: I guess all the magic in the world can’t make the belt disappear. He shrugs, and then looks at the camera very seriously. David: You know, a wise man once said that all stars die quickly and spectacularly. For that man’s sake, I hope he’s right. Because I know a star that’s changing. A star that’s undergoing a brand new process. A star that has proven to be unlike any other. And unless this particular star dies before next Monday… things aren’t looking so bright for that wise man. He lets out another small mischievous grin, but quickly gets serious again. David: Because this wise man didn’t choose to follow the star, as other, apparently wiser wise men have done. He didn’t look at the star and admire it. Heck, he didn’t even acknowledge the star’s… well, power. Its ability to shine. He looked at the star’s once-shortcomings and thought; “Oh well. Nothing special.” He puts his hand to his chin and feigns thinking. David: This wise man underestimated the star. And the funny thing about underestimation- He turns to the camera, grinning yet again. David: You’ll always get much more than you expect. The camera abruptly cuts to black.
