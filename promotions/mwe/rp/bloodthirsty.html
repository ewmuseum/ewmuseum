Bloodthirsty; Virus MNM RP #1

May 31 2009

Leon Roberts

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It was night time at the house of Leon 'The Virus' Roberts. Tonight, was a bit different then most other nights. Some crazy shit was going to be going down. However, one wouldn't think that right now. Two PM in the morning, and the usual sounds of Leon and Demonica having sex could be heard. Well, for those who were actually up. Which in this case, Leon's eldest daughter, Liberty, she was up. See, she was thirsty, and wanted some water. She didn't turn on any lights though, as she had really good vision in the dark of night. As she got her water though, she heard a window being shattered. This had alerted Liberty. Putting down her glass, she immediately hid, knowing what most likely happened. She looked around the corner, and sure enough, somebody had broken in. Liberty (whisper): Shit. The guy looks armed. I can't do nothing. But dad.......he'll be pissed if I do alert him. But he'll probably be even more pissed if I don't. Well...........Sorry guys. Liberty knew what she had to do before the burglar could actually steal anything. Taking a deep breath, she let out a really high pitched scream. Thanks to her bat and vampire traits, the sound of her screams were enhanced enough to break a few glasses. The burglar was in pain from her scream. Naturally, everyone else was woken up by Liberty's screaming. She had stopped, to not only hear her sibling crying, but seeing the lights being turns on, and the burglar flailing about in pain. Leon was indeed there, dressed only in a pair of knee length boxers. His hair, ruffled all over the place, and a scowl on his face. Leon: The fuck is going on? Leon slowly made his way down the stairs. The burglar had recovered just as Leon stepped infront of him. The burlglar knew he had been caught, but was prepared to take some hostages. The burglar pulled out a gun. Burglar: O-O-O-N Y-Y-YOUR KNEES!!!!!!! Leon's scowl became worse, as he swiftly grabbed the gun, and crushed it underneath his hand. The burglar was really panicking now, as he tried to escape. But Leon grabbed him, and but him in a choke hold, until the burglar had passed out. Leon dropped him on the floor. Liberty then came around the corner. Leon glanced at her. Liberty: Umm....sorry dad about.....disturbing you and mom. Leon: Lib. You did the right thing. There is no telling what could have been done had you not screamed. Demonica will be a bit mad, but once she sees everything, she'll understand. But I have to ask.....why were you up. Liberty: Well for one thing you and mom were keeping me up, AGAIN. Leon scoffs at that. He knew that it was partially his fault that Liberty hardly got any sleep during the night, but he was at least glad she had vampire genetics and traits, as it immensely helped her with that problem. But, Leon knew he wasn't going to stop just so his eldest daughter could get some sleep. Heck he was standing there still hard, ready to burst. Luckily, Liberty really didn't see that. If she did, she really wasn't paying attention. Liberty then walked forward, examining the burglar. She knew the man still had a pulse. She smirked, very happy that she could easily do what she wanted. Liberty: And two, I was thirsty. But........it would seem that keeping this douchebag alive was a good thing, because I quite frankly, am now craving some blood. Liberty looked at Leon, with her fangs bared. Leon smiled, knowing that it was mostly this sick and twisted child's mind that suddenly made her a little bloodthirsty. In some ways, it could be worse then Leon's own bloodlust, because Leon just admired the blood flow, while Liberty actually drinks it. Quickly looking at the knocked out burglar again, an even bigger smile then before. Liberty saw what kind of smile it was, and she could almost match that massive, sick and twisted smile that Leon currently wore. Leon: Well I just got a really great idea. You will have this bastards blood, but you'll have to wait a few minutes. See, what's going to be done, is this fucker....will be used as.....an example. Liberty tilted her head to the side for a second, not sure what Leon had meant by that. But after a minute, she gets it. She sighs, knowing that Leon had yet to actually do a promo, and wanted to use this guy. She couldn't blame her father, it was a very logical route to go. But she was thirsty, and when she was thirsty, it was not a good idea to deny her drink. But, for the sake of helping her father, she knew she could wait. Liberty: Okay dad. Do your promo. Just make sure you get me a ticket to Mayhem. I wouldn't mind tasting either Riddick's or McKnight's blood. Leon: I doubt that will be a problem. Leon hoists the burglar onto his shoulders, and then proceeds to the basement. The basement is a huge place, with three rooms. An entertainment room, a miniature gym, and one of Leon's favorite rooms, an interrogation room. Leon put the burglar in the interrogation room, and onto a chair. Being that he always had rope handy, he tied the burglar up, and really tight. Leon smiled, before he quickly looked down, and sighed. Leon: Lib....can u keep an eye on this guy for me? There is..... a problem I have to quickly look after. Leon immediately bolted upstairs. Liberty rolled her eyes at that, knowing exactly that her father wasn't exactly done with Demonica. So Liberty just patiently waited for Leon to return. But while she did that, she went behind the interrogation room 2 way mirror. She flipped a switch, to lower the mirror. She took a chair, dragged it over to the camera, and turned it on. About ten minutes later, Leon came back down with his ring attire pants on, and a bag. He also had a really large smile on his face, both for what just happened, and what's about to happen. Leon looked at Liberty. He was ready to shoot his promo for MWE. He nods, a signal for her to start recording. She presses the recording button, and then sits on the chair she was standing on. Leon: Well Well. Welcome back. It would seem it's been a while. But regardless of that, Monday Night Mayhem, just fresh off a hot pay-per-view called Tribulation, where we saw a three hundred pound man try to act like superman, but would up imitating a massive boulder dropped from the sky, a scavenger hunt which..........was entertaining, just not in the right environment. Then, we saw two men who by tragedy, wound up going to war against each other, and one stood tall. But there was one more match. One more, which saw a mafia prove their dominance. Vega and I retained the tag titles over a Wiccan worshiper, and a bonafide poser. Which means, they both have to mark out for the Black List Mafia as long as we're the tag team champions. Well boys, just make sure you can mark out for us for a long time, because it will be a long time before the titles will change hands. But, now onto Mayhem, many things will happen, that could very well top Tribulation. Two titles will be on the line, including the MWE world title. It's to bad I'm not in that match. But the fans will be guaranteed to see some blood flowing, as I am involved in a triple threat first blood match against the undefeated and ultimately over-hyped Jay Riddick, and that Wiccan lover, Scotty McKnight. Leon then moves out of the way of the camera for a second. The burglar is seen full view, all tied up. The black face mask, is still on. Leon then comes back into view. Leon: Now, I'm sure you are wondering why there is some dude here that is...dressed up like he wanted to rob me. Well turns out, this guy did try to rob me. That, was one of the worst ideas anyone could ever do. I'm fine, even disarming and knocking this bitch out. But why is he here, and not being apprehended by the police. Well I'm the goddamn devil's incarnate, and I do whatever the fuck I want, and everyone knows that, and there ain't a goddamn thing anyone can do about it. And I feel like using this bitch, as an example. Leon then walks around the burglar. It was a slow, methodical or of..stalk. Ones that interrogators are professionals at using. He rips off the mask, to show a black haired man, around the same age as Leon. Leon then hits a hard, backhand slap to the burglar, which promptly woke him up. Burglar: OW! What the fuck was that for. Leon: SHUT UP!!!!! Do you have any idea who I am? Burglar: Y-y-y-es I do. Y-y-y-you're Leon Roberts. The Devil's Incarnate. A very powerful person, who can destroy anything in his path. I-I-I-I was trying to ind out what gave you that power, and take it for myself.....a-a-and sell it on the black market.....it's worth billions. Leon smirked at the guy. Leon turned around to face the camera, still having his smirk plastered on his face. This was something new to him, and he was going to milk it. Leon: You hear that. I'm worth billions. I say that because there is no jewel or anything like tat, this is the source of my 'powers'. I am all of them. Leon then turned his attention back to the burglar. The burglar was clearly scared. But Leon could see in his eyes, that there was a hint of admiration for the devil's incarnate. Leon still slowly circled around the burglar, as if Leon was a tiger, stalking his prey. But Leon also had a look of small respect for the man he had captured. Leon: I can tell that you, like me and one of my opponents, is a reject of society. Someone who's been outcasted and dealt a bad hand. I admire the fact that you wanted to gain a lot of money quickly to live the rest of your existence comfortably. But, just because I admire that, doesn't mean you're off the hook for breaking into my house. No. See, I simply just can't allow that. I'm not known as the most vicious man on the planet for letting assmunchers like you, go away without a scratch. Leon then looked at the table, where he was placed his bag. He grabbed it, and unzipped it, before emptying it's contents onto the table. It was various pieces of broken glass. Leon sifted through them, until he came across a big piece. One that his entire hand could not fit. It was nice and sharp as well. The burglar was shaking in his boots. Leon then circled the burglar again. Leon: See, it's men like you, who understand this world better. Who understand my message of war and anarchy. Because you live it. You survive it. Rich people, and religious people, they don't understand our pain. They don't understand what it's like, to have to whore yourself to do dirty and disgusting jobs just to survive. They don't know the reasons why people do those 'get rich quick' schemes. They don't understand, that we also try to bring the rest of this world down to our level. It's because of two things. One, they do have money. They don't need to worry about anything. And two, they worship a dictator and his zombie son. They give their free will up, believing that God will keep them secure for the rest of their pathetic existence. Then, we have other types of followers, who believe they are guardians for God, against me. People like Scotty McKnight. He's the prime example of being a rich, religious, self righteous snob. He has the audacity, to believe that he alone can determine whether I rule this world, or not. Well then, I ask you this. Do you really believe that? Leon then looked at the burglar right in the eyes. The Burglar didn't know what t say. He had assumed that Leon was just talking to the camera. Leon: ANSWER ME!!!!!!!! That outburst really shook the burglar to his core. Leon could tell when he sniffed the air. He smirked, knowing exactly that the burglar had pissed himself. Burglar: Um.......I...I.......I don't think he can. Leon: Is that your honest answer, or are you just telling me what I want to hear? Burglar: I swear......it's the honest answer. Leon looks at the burglar again, sort of, playing with the glass shard. Surprisingly, Leon isn't getting cut up. Leon smirks, nods his head, knowing that the burglar was telling the truth. Leon: Good. Now, we also have a man who believes in Karma, and luck. Jay Riddick. A vicious person, like me, because life and society, crapped on him the day he was born, and kept crapping on him. Almost like me. He understood that in order to truly live, he must make a name for himself. I ave as well, but tell me this? Did you have to sell drugs when you were ten years old just so you could pay for your next meal because of a money hogging mother? Did you know you had a lust for blood the moment you got your first papercut? Did you even come back from the dead? The answers are no. See, Riddick, you might have similar qualities to me, but you are far from being me. No. You believe that after all the crap you've been dealt with, you will get your personal revenge. You believe, that luck if finally on your side. That is what makes you weak. Even this bitch right here, all tied up and pissing his pants, knew he had to fuck the rules to attempt to get a billion bucks. Despite his predicament, he understood all the risks involved. Burglar: Uhhhh....T-t-t-thanks....I guess. Leon smirked. He pulled up a chair, and sat on it, with the spine of the chair pressing against his chest. He looked at the camera for a minute, before letting out a sigh, before turning back to the burglar. Leon: See Riddick, you can keep saying all the things you want, about how it's 'your time'. Well I have a piece of advice for you. It's never 'your time' unless you step up, and make it so. My time, could already be here if I wanted it to be, but I must grow stronger first. I am slowly enjoying my time where I'm at right now, perfecting my craft. One day, I will step up and become simply the greatest goddamn wrestler to ever exist. The face, that permanently represents the sport of kings. When that day comes a war that has been raging for two thousand years, away from the public view, will at last, be known. The war between Heaven and Hell will finally come to the surface. People will choose sides, whether they want a world without rules, or being dictated around for the rest of their pathetic lives. No so called guardians will stand in my way, from freeing the people, and them embracing me as their leader of this world. I say it so, because I can guarantee you all this. It's only a matter of time before the true savage nature of humans will come to the surface. Because of this, I will grow stronger, by each and every passing second. Heaven will fall, Hell will become Earth, and I alone.......will rule.......EVERYTHING!!!!!!!!!!!! Leon then gets up from where he was sitting. The burglar was somewhat smiling from Leon's ranting. Leon moved behind the burglar, and with his empty hand, he held the burglar's head in place, lifting up the bangs a bit. Then, with the hand that held the glass, he moved it back, and quickly slashed the burglar's head with the glass. It was a deep wound as well, as the burglar screamed in agony, as the blood began to flow. Liberty: About damn time dad. I'm parched. Liberty then placed a goblet on the table. She had gotten one just before the recording started, and just kept a hold of it. Leon grabbed it, then placed it on the table, right infront of the burglar and Leon. Leon tilted the burglar's head forward, right over the goblet, and began to squeeze the burglar's head, like trying to pop a basketball with both hands, as a lot of blood went into the goblet. When it was full, Leon tiled the man's head back. Liberty went and got the goblet. She was in the camera's view, as she drank the thick crimson liquid that was blood. Some oven spilled over her mouth, leaving a little bit of a stream going down her chin. She finished up, and had a satisfied grin on her face. Liberty: That....was delicious. This man has some good blood. I want more. Leon smirked, as he repeated the process. The burglar was screaming in agony again, as he was barely able to see his own blood pour into the goblet. Once it was full, Liberty drank it all down. Liberty: Thanks dad. I'm gonna go get some sleep. Liberty left, and went upstairs. Leon smirked again. Leon: That's my daughter alright. But I have a warning for Scotty and Jay. What you saw, this man, having his blood consumed by my daughter....well, it will be one of you guys, when I make you bleed. It could be both, but either way, if you two think that with just who I am, and what I can, and probably will do.....do you honestly think that you tow have a chance of taking me on? I don't think so. So my last message is this. Prepare yourselves for the upcoming battle. It could be where you simply become casualties of war. People will mourn you, because they saw you die. But that will be the only reason to mourn. As for you, my bounty seeking friend. Well since I'm in such, a nice mood, I'll patch you up. Burglar: R-r-really? Leon: Yeah. Problem is, I won't be using any anesthetics to numb the pain. Leon looked at the burglar with a massive, sinister filled grin. There was a slight, throaty chuckle that could be heard from Leon as well. The burglar gulped, knowing full well that Leon was not lying. In fact the burglar even knew that Leon would enjoy his pain. At that moment, the burglar knew that he sympathized for Jay Riddick and Scotty McKnight, for what's going to happen to them, will be a lot worse then what Leon did to him. That's how it was, when dealing with the devil.
