A camera clicks. Birds flutter. Whistle young little things. Whistle and hum. Sing and breath. Samuel Steele will be watching at where? A park. Sitting at a park relaxing…chilling…just plain…AT ease. What can stop his current state of zen? Samuel is not strange, he is UNIQUE. People seem to be sitting around him but not paying much attention to poor Samuel. What is wrong? What is wrong with this guy? What is wrong with…HIM….the OTHER guy…or maybe what is wrong with THEM? NO! NO MORE QUESTIONS! But wait! He smiles for some reason. But he looks at nothing but the pond placed in front of him in this very…BROAD…park. He loves big and broad spaces because his mind thinks that of a sharp, heart splitting, blade. He is ready. Yes I think…he is ready to talk. Let us observe. Samuel: Come here ya damn camera fool! I ain’t no jive turkey. I’M A MAN DAMMIT! I’M A HUMAN MUTHA F*CKIN BEING! The camera? It shakes just a little. Samuel is very angry right now. Wonder why. He hollers and hoots like an owl he does. But he manages after lighting his favorite poison. A cigarette…a flick…a getup…a torque…he looks to the pond. That poor man’s eyes…saggy and drooped…the things he has been through…the things he has seen. If he was blind what then? Happiness. Samuel: So who do I got who do I got…a dude that looks as if…as if he was a big, red, herpy, on my dick! Mason suckin dick in the shade….oh? MASON BLADE! DAMN! I’ll be damned, a HERPY on my dick named Mason Blade! LET’S CELEBRATE! He ruffles though a rucksack he had brought with him to the park. He feels around with a determinedness. Oh the determinedness he has right now. It is unbreakable. He manages to find a fine bottle of Captain Morgan, could it be his getaway? Or his key to the netherworld? He swigs the sweet poison down his parched throat one bottle of beer on the wall….two…three….seems to never end. He can’t compute. This is…an issue. Samuel: Ok enough wit dat….next guy….HEY! The next guy looks like a bad churro I ate last night…mama was not happy with my stomach problems after dat….James go to hell? OH DAMN SON! TREVEL! Wouldn’t ever remember that! BUT HEY! He could also be the kid who gave my dog a damned blowjob in my backyard yesterday…it was someone…don’t remember all of it…coulda been him? LET’S CELEBRATE! More swigs. Sigh he shall. But not to fret, he can still be victorious….he is a gladiator…he is Samuel Steele! Or maybe….drunk and confused…maybe mislead….he looks at the birds again. No more young ones but more so adult ones scratching around for food. He looks at his beer. Maybe thinking about throwing it. But no. He keeps it and takes yet another guzzle. How unfortunate. The camera zooms it does. Samuel: Next man is…HOLY MUTHA F*CKIN SHIT! That guy…that guy looks like my sisters dildo! LOOK AT THAT B*TCH’S HEAD! Steve Cowman? OH SH*T MY BAD! Cowan. CHRIST MAN YOU LET A GUY LIKE THIS IN WRESTLIN! You people crazy…you guys crazy man….I ain’t fightin no dildo head man….F*ck that stupid sh*t. But what da hell. LET’S CELEBRATE! One more swig of beer. But yet…it is now gone. He looks at the empty bottle….could it lead to frustration…more anger maybe…or maybe something else. But only Samuel knows that. The bottle flies from Samuel’s hand into the pond. Ducks quack and wiggle and fly away they do. Splashes splashes splashes. The pond is filled with waves of water splashes. But alas poor Samuel….still ignored…no one says a thing. Samuel: WHAT THE F*CK YA’LL LOOKIN AT! THIS AIN’T A CRIME, A MAN LIKE ME BEIN FREE! COME ON NOW! Or maybe…not. People do look at him. A misconception maybe. He gets himself together. His mind narrow and set on his goal to be a great wrestler. Nothing can interfere. Or maybe a theory more than anything. Let’s watch he seems to be putting out his cigarette again. He has named all his opponents as if he is god crossing out a list of people he has condemned. But why? His eyes are fixed on the camera. Interesting. His cigarette falls from his mouth and into the murky pond. But elegant in a way it seems. Samuel: Now I know ya’ll think I’ma bit harsh at times but come on man! DON’T HATE! That’s just me people. A free individual to say my piece dawgs. That’s all I am. Wrestlin became my thing and I’m stickin to it! I ain’t goin back to my garden hoe. Nuh uh no way! I am goin big time a**holes! BIG TIME BABY YEAH! THAT’S RIGHT DAMMIT! BIG TIME! A bird flies gracefully over Samuel’s head. It seems to have laid a present on his grey golf cap. A white blotch engulfs the center of it. How rude it is. He stops and looks above him. His face quite red. But oh no, the bird has already flown away. Again…poor poor Samuel. But his pain seems to not be felt by anyone but him. He looks ready to scream. Shall we take cover? It seems he is almost flying out of his blue jeans and black sneakers with anger. And his white T-shirt, if it had hair, it would be standing still in fear. Samuel: GOD DAMN MUTHA F*CKIN BIRDS! F*CK YOU GOD DAMN BIRDS! F*CK YOU! Samuel is on a raging rant right now. He is not happy. No no, not at all. He is rushing to people one by one at this once quiet park. Screaming and yelling at them loudly. Not good. Samuel: AND F*CK YOU! AND YOU! AND YOU! Wait… He sees a friend of his with what seems to be a female a few yards down the park. He approaches them. He doesn’t seem to recognize them at first. His eyes squint, his head flutters with thoughts. But he knows them. Yes. Most definitely. Samuel: DAMN! Orlando? THAT YOU BROTHA? SH*T MAN! Almost blew up on you man. Hold on a second brotha. He leaves Orlando for a moment and continues letting out his righteous fury on others in the park. Samuel: AND F*CK YOU! AND YOU! AND YOU TOO FATA**! F*CK ALL YA’LL B*TCHES! AND I AM THROUGH! He walks back to his friend known as “Orlando” with wide eyes. The camera zooms out I suppose to show his friend and his girl better. But instead it seemed to be two white crane birds sipping at the pond’s edge. It seems these are his friends….perhaps both known as “Orlando” how are they addressed? Orlando one and Orlando two? The world may never know. Samuel: Sorry man….I shouldn’t bug ya with your girl bro….I should probably get goin man…gotta scoot out ya know dawg? I’ll be back…strollin and rollin back here…you just wait and see….I’ll cya lata man. It appears he is leaving his friend Orlando. Poor Samuel. Now he is in front of the camera. Not nervous, not stricken. But bold….and determined. His determinedness seems endless. Always a good thing. Samuel: The last man seems to be me folks. I named em’ all but me. Ain’t NUTTIN to say about me people. Cept I am proud of who I am and who I AIN’T! My opponents are all dicks and pussies waitin to be f*cked. Just like my ma every Sunday at church…waitin to be swept off her god damn feet by some f*ckin butt ugly , old ass coffee stained b*tch. But hey. If ya’ll don’t know me….YOU AIN’T LIVIN! Now gimme that cam man! I’ma make some money off the damn thing. A camera is grabbed. It is taken away from the previous human being behind it. Samuel runs off with his new toy ready to play. He needs to make that money. He needs that illegal satisfaction. That risky side of life. Spicy yet cold Samuel is the next HATED thing in the industry. Goodbye for now my friends.
