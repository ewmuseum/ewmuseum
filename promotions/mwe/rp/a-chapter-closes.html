A Chapter Closes

Mar 1 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
OOC: I know this Roleplay sucks and is late, but to be perfectly honest I have been lacking motivation and time lately. So this is the effort that I managed to come up with, and if it seems forced and rushed, it's because it is. -------------------------- Respect is not something that you are simply given, it has to be earned, it is an emotion that is given in the highest regard to the men and women that prove to me that they can find themselves in a position of power. Sinclair ... You have yet to earn that respect from me. Dark. Silent. Words that can be used to describe the sight before us. The pitch black of a room, not a light to be seen anywhere. The only noise is the calm, intent-full voice of a man, that was broken. A man, who's soul mission in life, was failed. It is the voice of a saviour. It is the voice of Joey. Dallas. His cold tone pierces through the black of our scene once again. Joey Dallas: You may think that I lied to you, and deceived you into believing that I truly wanted you to succeed, only for me to stab you in the back, and leech off your taste of success. His words seem to sound even more planned out and cunning than they ever had. We are unsure if it's because he is smarter after suffering the greatest lose of his career, or if it was simply because of his cunning way to begin this promo. Joey Dallas: But Sinclair, that is not the way I remember it at all. For I took you under my win, much like a mothering bird does with her young ... Where I nurtured you ... Taught you the skills you would need to soar above the rest of the pathetic men that call themselves superstars. Was it really the truth, that Dallas' intent had been to teach Sinc? To actually make him the type of wrestler that he saw fit to hold the world title. To put an entire company on his shoulders, and carry it through the darkest of times that he seems to think we are in as long as Whitman carries the belt. Joey Dallas: It was never a lie Sinc, I saw the potential in you to become one of the very few men in this world, that are enlightened. One of the people, that see what this entire thing is all about ... But you were unwilling to learn. I tried to teach you, but you would rather continue with your childish little ways. You wanted to keep running down that trail, of being the fan favorite. You decided somewhere ... In that unworthy little mind of yours ... That those people cheering for you, are worth more than your own success. That's a well and good, but why is this man still sitting the dark like some little emo pussy? Yeah, that's right, Mr. Tex just made a joke in his RP, and you'll all be like "ZOMG WTF" Why am I writing this? Because you know, I'm bored, and it makes my RP look bigger even tho it's not. THAT'S WHY! But again, in all seriousness, that seems to be the question on everyone's mind. Sitting here in the pitch black, it is not the usual type of scene when we find ourselves listening to that sly little voice of Dallas. We are use to this almost omnipotent sort of sight with him, not this low, self-pitying darkness that sits in front of us. Joey Dallas: That was exactly the kind of reckless disregard for yourself I was trying to avoid, because while you were supposed to be breaking those vultures' prying grip on your life ... I, was trying to find the man behind the vail of sarcastic jokes. I was trying to open your eyes ... With that a blinding white light shoots through the entire scene, leaving us without sight for a few brief moments before we finally begin to see what we have been looking for this entire time. A clean room. Everything in order. Bed neatly pressed against the wall, made up tight enough that you could bounce a quarter off the sheets. The usual makes of what a hotel room would appear to be. The only thing that is off today is that everything ... is upside down. Bright light shining brightly from the roof by our feet as Joey Dallas walks into the scene. His body covered in the usual dark pin-stripped suit that has become somewhat a trademark of this man. The dark jacket is covering the white shirt, and a cool blue tie. Finishing off the ensemble is a single, black rose in his breast pocket. However, what is most noticeable about his appearance is not his outfit, or even his usual dank emotion. Instead it is his hair. Still stained red with the blood from a brutal and unthinkable attack from Ace Cannon. The bleach blonde of his hair barely making it's way through the clumped together blood. Joey Dallas: I was trying to show you the inevitable fate that the fans will lead you towards in the end. You see Sinclair, these men ... These, parasites will build you up, take all your success, and pin it as a direct result of themselves. They are not building YOU a legacy ... They are building themselves a legacy. Dallas reaches towards his head, letting his fingertips rest gingerly atop of the stitches from last Friday. He runs his fingers up and down along them, feeling the dried blood and thread. Almost putting him into a rage the likes we have never seen from Dallas. Instead, he pulls his fingers away, now letting them pull through his styled hair, forcing flakes of dried blood to litter his face as it becomes calm. His breath slowing escaping from his body as he allows his lips to part again. Joey Dallas: You try to give them everything they want, as if every time they ask you for that little thing that would give them pleasure, it was their dying wish ... Then you, being the blind man that you are, give it to them, because you THINK that it's what they want .... for YOU. But it's not, it's all their selfish little wants. Strange isn't it? How this man can still be so down on the fans, even after losing the biggest match of his career to David Whitman. The man that the crowd loves more than life itself. Usually we would expect Dallas to be bitching and whining about that, but it just isn't the case here. The look in his eyes shows that he is obviously preoccupied by something, much more deep than any lose could be. Letting his head tilt up (or would that technically be down in this scenario?) Dallas looks towards the ceiling. Blood managing to seep through the tight stitches in his forehead that bound together his injuries from that barbed-wire crown as his fingers travel along the edges of his injuries. Joey Dallas: They don't want you to be a legend. They want you ... to be their puppet. They want to string you along, run your life, see what it is like to be a wrestler. Through YOUR pain .. Through YOUR and suffering. All I was trying to do ... Was open your eyes to all of it. He let's his eyelids gently close, relaxing as much as he possibly can in his current position. Fingers still running gently over the gash that is bound to become a scar, letting his arm drop beside himself. His eyes open slowly, as his chest pushes out with air. Joey Dallas: Yet, you're still as blind as the day I tried to lead you towards the light. I know now, that I should have taken a tighter grip on your arm, to keep you along the right path ... But instead you wandered right back down the trail that has taken you to nothing more than living on the strings of the broken dreams and wasted hopes of those people. Chest slowly moving back towards him as the air escapes from his lungs, Dallas let's his eyes open slowly. Camera still upside down to represent the night he was bloodied after his last match. It symbolizes a shattered dream of a man that could have seen himself finally at the top of the world. Joey Dallas: Sinc, you decided to run down the path of .. failure. Our scene spins around, a sudden feeling of self pity overcomes Dallas as he hangs his head. An emotional truck had just hit him, about his own career, his own life. How every time he had found himself just inches from the top, he was thrown down from the rafters to an untimely end to everything. Joey Dallas: You know, something has just occurred to me. You see, Sinc. Every time I have been in a position to certify myself in a position of unmatched power in this industry, someone has stepped into my way .. Someone has been there, to knock me down off my pedestal. Reaching his hand towards his hair, still crunched together in dried blood. Running his hand threw it to get as much of the memory of that night away from himself, knowing it is far too much for himself to bare at this moment. Yet, not willing to admit it to the people. Joey Dallas: One man, has always been there, to see me in my time of self righteous glory. To steal the spotlight for themselves .. Take all of my hard work and success, for themselves. There has always been ONE MAN! Dallas rips a sleek black phone off of the bedside table, ripping the cord out of the wall. With one fluent movement he throws it at an empty wall, shattering the plastic coating on impact. Only for it all to fall towards the floor, landing. Broken. Shattered. Much like a certain person standing here today. Swinging his head violently back towards the camera, emotion running rampant over him. For the first time since he has returned Dallas is actually showing an emotion other than anger. Well, that's not entirely true, Dallas is filled with rage, but somewhere, deep down inside, there is something ... else there. Joey Dallas: Ace Cannon! Has been there since the day I have walked into his industry, to rip my dream out from under me! He has always been in my way to the top of the ladder, only for him to push my back to the bottom ... Only for him to take the momentum from knocking to top man off ... To chase after the brass ring himself. Beginning to stare down at the shattered remains of the telephone, Dallas hangs his head in remorse. Refusing to let his emotions control him, yet failing miserably at it. He is a man of layers, and his hard exterior is finally starting to crack. Joey Dallas: Cannon has always been there, when I'm at my lowest point .. To drive that last little bit of hatred straight through my skull. Well, if by hate Dallas meant his foot then that was exactly what has happened to him. Time and time again. Throwing his hands down on the wooden night stand before him, finally moving his eyes away from the phone to the cool surface in front of him. Taking a deep breath in, causing his shoulders to move up. Dallas pulls his eyes towards the camera again, once again back to that emotional stare. Joey Dallas: But there is an old saying, that a man cannot save his people. If he cannot save himself, and Sinc, while I don't have the desire to take those miserable people to any form of salvation I suppose that it can apply to my situation as well. He lets his hands run across the surface, letting them fall off of the night stand. Fingers wrapping around the handle of the drawer firmly, pulling it slowly open before reaching in. His hands emerging wrapped around a small, leather bound bible. Placing it spine first on the wooden surface, letting the pages fan open. Joey Dallas: You see Sinc, although this book speaks nothing but lies about what it takes to save this world. It still speaks words of unmatchable wisdom. For there is one verse, that has been the basics of my renewal of life. Flipping through the bible before him until he reaches a page with a long, strand of red velvet lying carefully over the words. Pressing his index finger against the smooth page, running it down until he finds the words that he is looking for. Joey Dallas: Second Corinthians, ten three four, states ... For though we live in the world, we do not wage war as the world does. The weapons we fight with are not the weapons of the world. On the contrary, they have divine power... Dallas let's his neck tilt back up, looking once again towards the camera. The words speak wonders for any other man, but this is not the kind of thing that we would expect to represent Dallas in any way. His wars are more than usually about the pitiful things that people around him do. Joey Dallas: My wars are waged through a much deeper, meaningful type of way. For when I am forced to declare war on another man, it is not for trivial reasons of these pitiful little men that seem to have taken a stranglehold over the world. Choking the life out of it until it crumbles at their feet. Dallas holds the sides of the night stand tightly. Peering down at the meaningful literature in front of him before letting his eyes once again drift back to the camera, his stare full of falling emotion, finally managing to calm them down to a point where he can hide them behind his cool, harsh exterior. Joey Dallas: Much like the war that waged on between you and Mason for months. Not a war of words, not an emotional war, but a physical battle to prove who is superior. Yet, in the end neither one of you have gained anything except for the scars that will constantly remind you of a useless battle. Tapping his fingers casually against the wood of as he again turns his head towards the bible. His layers once again rebuilding, but the damage has already been done. It is quite apparent already, that our Saviour is just as human as the rest of us. Joey Dallas: So, in the end. You may have the United States championship, but do you feel any more accomplished for beating this man? Do you feel like you have added to this little legacy you have been building? ... Do you feel that beating this man in a physical battle makes you more of a man? Well, let me be the first to tell you. That simply, beating a man is not enough to call yourself a better human being .. You never managed to outwit, outplay or even in the end ... outlast your opponent. You see, Mason Resoan is still a much better man than you, because it is never simply a physical battle for him. Dallas pushes himself away from the night stand, taking a moment to himself to think about this, but it is simply not the time for that. Peering once again down at the bible below him he let's his lips break apart, his words fluently escaping from his mouth. Joey Dallas: Resoan, knows that a man needs to become not only physically involved, but spiritually, emotionally, and mentally involved into a war ... He did not need to win every match, because he has already won in the long run .. He has proven to be the superior, simply by being his OWN man. Turning away from the night stand, Dallas readjusts his suit, flakes of dried blood falling from his shoulders as he faces the camera. An emotionless stare through the camera and into the eyes .. of Sinc. Joey Dallas: He did not need to STEAL, the swagger and unmatchable intelligence and make a mockery out of it. Like you did to me ... Throwing around words, that you have no idea the meanings to. Trying to make you appear to be something you are not ... Dallas extends his index finger, bringing it towards the blood stained hair on his head. Tapping himself sternly a few times, as he looks into the camera. It is quite obvious what he means, but it just wouldn't be Dallas if he didn't explain it to the Sinc'ers. Joey Dallas: .. Smart. Sinc, I don't need to prove anything to you. I don't need to lie, and deceive in order to beat you. I just need to play MY game. A game of wits, intelligence and strategy .. So even, if you do manage to beat me, with your childish taunts and ploys .. Just like in your fued with Resoan, I will be the better man. He reaches calmly towards his pocket, taking the slim green stem of the rose in hand. Taking the flower gently into his hand, holding it intently in front of himself. It is a symbol. Of what? We're not sure, but we are sure that there is a single man in this room, that is bound to tell us. Joey Dallas: You see, I am like this black rose. I am ... unique. Surrounded by a world, of red roses that represent nothing more than the fake emotions and needs of people. All the while I am standing alone, beautiful, special. You see, those red roses, are the ones that pretend. Pretend to be, something special to the people, and they love it. Because they are nothing more, than the puppet masters of people like you Sinc. The black rose represents .. Death. The death, of your career. Dallas takes a long hard look at the rose in hand, twisting and turning it around. It represents too much in life. As much as Dallas would like to say, and more. Pulling his gaze away from the camera, towards the bible that still lays open on the night stand. He places the rose on the open pages of the book. The camera focuses on the black pedals of the flower, resting gently on the words. As we fade to black.
