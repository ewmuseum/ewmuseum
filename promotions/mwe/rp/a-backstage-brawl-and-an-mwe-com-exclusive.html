A Backstage brawl and an MWE.com Exclusive; A JJ Executionrt RP

Nov 15 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
We see the scene begin through the lens of a shuddering camera, which is rushing towards an incident backstage. We see black shirted security running ahead and bumping into the cameraman. As he makes his way around the corner we see JJ Executioner and The Necromaster Milenko brawling furiously backstage. Both men are visibly bloodied and have gone through catering tables and doors in the backstage areas. The bulky security guards try and break up the two but they refuse to be separated and break through them. They continue to connect with punch after punch to each other face, smearing blood onto their fists and all over the floor. Security gets back between the two and somehow manages to get between the two. Eventually they give up trying. JJ gives Milenko an evil grin, gesturing to his eyebrow where Milenko is bleeding. JJ: “You interfering fucker.......I’m gonna take you apart you prick!” Milenko doesn’t respond. He just smiles and allows himself to be pushed away. MWE owner Richard Covell appears from around the corner. He is shocked at the damage and destruction and immediately walks up to JJ. Covell: “I knew you would be behind this. This is coming out of your wages you giant piece of scum!” JJ pushes past the security and goes nose to nose with Covell. Well, not nose to nose, as JJ is significantly taller. JJ: “I want that fucker, one on one, at the earliest opportunity.” JJ’s tone is aggressive and demanding. Covell: “Why should I do that for you? Great job by the fucking way but your match finished five minutes ago.” JJ is about to respond when cheers and whoops are heard across the room as the victorious Texan, Billy Joe Barker enters, surrounded by James, Eddie, Scott and Thomas. They have the big man on his shoulders. BJB has a huge grin on his face. Alejandro Rodriguez emerges from a locker room. JEST let BJB fall to the floor. A Rod offers his hand to the big man. But BJB in response picks up the former champion and carries him off to great whoops of laughter from JEST. Covell was looking at this and grinning. But JJ has not taken his laser-like stare off of Covell and is annoyed at the blatant disregard of his presence. Covell turns back around. The boss quickly regains his composure and addresses the angry superstar in front of him. Covell: “You will have your match with Milenko when I say so.” Covell, assertive as ever stands up to JJ. JJ presses himself forward and bumps into Covell deliberately. He stumbles back but quickly regains composure. Stepping forward to JJ, he decides to take direct action. Covell: “Look you oversized jackass, you threaten me or any of my staff one more time and you will be fired. I refuse to be dictated to in my company. I will not be threatened like this and neither will anyone in my employment!” JJ looks unfazed. “You got your rematch with Milenko at the next show.” JJ: “Good choice…see you next Monday.” Covell: “No, I said the next show. You have a triple threat match with Milenko on MWE Genesis to determine one of the competitors to fight it out in the cage match for the new MWE Championship later in the same show! And your second opponent? Joey Dallas!” JJ flashes an evil smile at Covell. JJ: “You have got to be kidding me?” Covell: “I don’t joke about the MWE. Have a good week.” Covell turns to go but JJ places a hand on his shoulder and stops him. JJ: “So I get to beat up that crazy clown and your number two draft dick at the same time? And then you are willing to put me in a steel cage with three of your other picks for a World title? You have just made my day Boss.” JJ turns to leave but this time Covell stops him. Covell: “By the way you have not had too much luck here recently.” JJ grimaces at being reminded of his poor recent record. “If you don’t win a match in between now and the next pay-per-view, Adrenaline Rush, then you are FIRED!” JJ’s facial expression barely changes but his eyes narrow out of pure determination. JJ: “By the time I am done with those two, it won’t be me that you want to give the future endeavours to. I damn sure don’t intend on giving you that pleasure. You can sit back while I send your second pick to hell and back with Milenko riding shotgun.” Covell: “Oh trust me when I say Joey Dallas can handle himself. Milenko as well. Though you just found that out.” JJ turns to leave again. “Oh and another thing, *JJ stops* you have an interview next week with Sara Patterson and Shawn Spitzer. Don’t miss it….that’s an order.” Covell turns and walks through the parting sea of security surrounding the two. As he strides away from JJ, the giant glints a hint of a smile and rubs a blood smeared fist into his palm. Part 2 – One week later Spitzer: “But sir.....” Silence Spitzer: “I don’t think I’m ....” More silence. Spitzer: “Sir...yes sir” Despondence. More silence. A phone slams shut. “Shit!” Sara Patterson breezes in the room. Sara: “Hey Shaw-, oh my god you’re deathly pale. What’s wrong?” Spitzer: “JJ Executioner. He’s been told to turn up for an interview. In fifteen minutes. With us.” Sara: “Who ordered that?” Spitzer: “Mr Covell.” Sara: “Oh.” Spitzer: “He said there will be security. But......” Sara: “Are you scared?” Spitzer: “No!” Instinctive reaction. Sara: “It’s OK.....” Reassuring. Or at least trying to be; it doesn’t work. Spitzer: “I know. Let’s go get ready.” Fifteen minutes later. Shawn and Sara are sat in the chairs in front of the camera set-up that will be used to film this interview as part of a special podcast based around the first episode of MWE Genesis. The larger chair opposite them both is unoccupied. Behind both chairs and the camera are two security guards dressed in red shirts and black trousers. The room itself has been light particularly bright at Shawn’s request. The lights flicker. Shawn jumps. Dressed in a thick black jacket, JJ Executioner storms into the room. He removes his jacket and drops it onto the floor. He then barges between two of the six men stood behind the camera and sits down. JJ: “Shawn. Sara.” JJ tries a courteous greeting but it is clear that the comment is dripping with sarcasm and “I don’t want to be here” attitude. Director: “Let’s get this going.” A gravelly voice pipes up from the wall of red shirt security. The camera moves slightly to focus on JJ. It picks up his swollen lip despite the mask and greasy hair that obscures most of his face. “Action!” Sara: “I’m Sara Patterson and tonight I’m here with MWE colleague Shawn Spitzer to have a very special podcast interview with just one of the contenders for the new MWE championship going towards the first ever broadcast of MWE Genesis. This is JJ Executioner.” Shawn is unable to speak. His eyes are agape at JJ. He knows that he is supposed to speak and ask the first question but can’t. Sara quickly realises and does it herself, only after she jabs her colleague in the ribs. Sara: “JJ, what are your thoughts on the MWE owner Mr Richard Covell making your match for Genesis?” JJ: “Covell is an insipid character who has ridden others to get to his position. He can try all that he would like to get in my head by he simply won’t affect me.” JJ’s tone is monotonous. He fails to try and hide his boredom. Sara: “You face Joey Dallas and Milenko in arguably the toughest match of the title tournament. What are your thoughts on your opponents?” JJ: “Joey Dallas is a whiny little bitch who builds himself to be the biggest disappointment in wrestling history. His ego, his self-belief is so large and over rated that his head won’t be able to fit inside the fucking arena! He was the number two draft pick because somebody bought into his bullshit. Well, it will be a pleasure to make that choice seem as stupid as it was reckless.” JJ’s tone picks up as he talks about the match. It is clear that he enjoys talking about inflicting pain. JJ: “I hold no worries about Joey Dallas. He can be as agile as he wants but I am bigger and stronger. Every little trick that he has in his little bag of tricks will see him and his million dollar looks get pummelled beyond recognition. By the end of the night, Joey Dallas won’t be in a fit state to add to his US title win in the MWE. Joey Dallas is a lot of things but he will be remembered as just another punk, flattened and left in my wake.” JJ is getting in the spirit of the interview. He even sits a little forward as his enthusiasm is clear. Still, while he speaks, he has a look of total disrespect on his face, matched only by the very deliberate and serious tone. JJ: “MIlenko is an ICP wannabe but even Shawn has bigger balls than that punk. This is someone who destroyed his brother at WrestleWars II but stood back and hid while I threw him off the stage to hell and back. Milenko should be grateful to me for taking Bowen out of his life for good.” “But no, instead he tries to put me through tables and attempts to throw me down some stairs backstage. Ultimately my single minded determination blinded me and saw the match end without my involvement while I was taking care of Milenko.” “What Milenko needs to know is that he will find out what exactly I am capable of at Genesis because he suffers from guilt; a personality trait that I detest. He is guilty over his brother and he so desperately wanted to be the one who ended his time in MWE. It was a timely honour that fell to me and now I repay the favour by sending him down the same dark path that Alex Bowen is treading right fucking now.” “Joey Dallas and Milenko are living on borrowed time. They are an endangered species; confused and bewildered by the lack of any instinct or awareness that they are being hunted. They will be the first casualties of my war on Genesis and it will be a pleasure to see them scream and bleed and buckle and fade.” Sara goes to answer the next question but pokes Shawn and makes him do it. She sits back and Shawn squirms around in his seat. He is nervous enough being next to Sara but petrified that the man who abducted him so recently is only two yards away. With a deep breath, Shawn speaks. But no words come out. His lips move like a fishes’ fighting for breath. Then after what seems like a lifetime to everyone in the room, the salvation of sound. Spitzer: “The winner of your match is one of four men to fight inside a steel cage. The man who survives will walk away with the belt and be known as the first MWE Champion on Genesis. Are you looking forward to getting that far?” JJ is tempted to tease Shawn. But at the back of his mind, a quiet, deep voice reminds him that a World title is at stake. JJ thinks about this and decides to stay on topic and refocus his energies. JJ: “A steel cage match. An environment that I am more than suited to. I know not to guarantee to get that far but everyone on Genesis needs to understand that if I am trapped in that structure with them, then the repercussions will be felt beyond the brand of Genesis.” JJ’s tone changes when discussing the cage. It is solemn but extremely serious. JJ: “Because the man who survives; he who has just enough blood and strength to lift his hands above his head with the MWE title held proud, will be a man who not only will be feared on Genesis but throughout the whole company as he carries it on bigger and better things.” “Not only will he be able to stand tall as a champion but he will be more powerful in the knowledge that he went through the best of the roster to do it.” “So to answer your question Shawn, I am very much looking forward to it. To looking into the eyes of four men and seeing fear bounce back at me. To see the moment of realisation that there is no escape and that they are trapped with me, in that environment. To see all that as MWE champion will be the ultimate moment of true satisfaction” JJ’s tone has changed again. It is now full of clear determination. Shawn: “You feel that a cage match is to your advantage?” JJ: “A place where there are no rules to safeguard you against the reach of the Hangmans’ Noose. Somewhere that has non prejudice or preference as to who it pains. For it to be decided inside a steel cage, suits me just fine. To say that a steel cage with me in is an unforgiving environment would be an understatement. It is a place that I feel completely at home and the MWE World title will be only ample reward for my survival over three helpless souls who have been condemned.” Both Shawn and Sara sense that they are getting a really great interview and begin to relax slightly. The slightest glance is exchanged between them, causing Shawn to have a brief, childish grin on his relaxed face. Sara: “Do you have any preferences over who you would like to face in the cage match?” JJ: “I don’t care who I have to face in that environment. In fact I would happily face ANYONE on the roster of either brand because I will scrape the flesh off their face, watch blood drip slowly down until it covers them and then subject them to the Death Scythe!” JJ is speaking quicker all the time, as he clearly believes what he is saying. JJ: “I am the one that no sane person would want to be in that cage with because everyone knows that I will use the cage as a tool of exploiting their weaknesses and systematically destroy them as competition on the way to my success.” “And whether it is DC, Sonny fucking Williams, Project Revolution, Ace Cannon; hell it could even be that insipid Vega Knight, they will suffer physically and mentally at my hands. Joey Dallas and Milenko are already destined to tread down the lonely path to painful execution. It doesn’t matter who joins them.” The lights cut out. The sounds of people milling around trying to figure out what has gone on. Suddenly, JJ can be heard laughing. The lights come back though they are much dimmer than they were before. JJ’s chair is still the focus of the camera and it is empty. The camera moves to Sara, who is remarkably composed alongside Shawn who has reverted to being pale and scared stiff. Sara: “That man, JJ Executioner, is sure to be a contender when MWE Genesis decides its’ first World Champion. This has been the first podcast for MWE Genesis. I’m Sara Patterson alongside Shawn Spitzer for MWEWrestling.com. Thanks for tuning in and stay logged on to get more in the future!”
