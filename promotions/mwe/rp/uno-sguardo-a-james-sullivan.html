Uno sguardo a James Sullivan; B.G. Mancini Vs James Sullivan RP #2

Sep 21 2008

B.G. Mancini

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(*OOC*All quotes in the following RP are credited to eXo_Arthur.) The camera turns on again and we see B.G. sitting in his uncles office again, but this time his manager Mike Esposito is sitting next to him looking a bit confused as to why he's there. 'Don' Vito Prima le cose prima, Mike Ho bisogno di fare qualcosa per me. Mio figlio fuori a LA ha ottenuto lo stesso nei guai con l'INS. Qualcosa su un anello utilizzando la prostituzione illegale degli immigrati cinesi. Voglio che tu vai lì e farlo uscire da questo pasticcio. Mike gets a surprised look on his face, but he knows better than to argue. Mike Sure I'll go to LA and take care of your son, but what about B.G. you hired me to keep him out of trouble and to manage his wrestling career. 'Don' Vito Sì e dove guardare che ha lui. Ha fatto perdere nulla, ma da quando è iniziato ragazzi. Penso che il momento per il mio nipote di avere un nuovo mangiatoia, uno che sa come arrivare al top e rimanere lì. Il suo nuovo mangiatoia sarà di me. Mike voglio che tu vada a ottenere è pronto per lasciare LA la mattina. Both Mike and B.G. looked surprised at that. As Mike gets up and leaves the room B.G. looks at his uncle and starts talking. B.G. Are you sure that's such a good idea? What I mean is if you come up and down the road with me who's gonna be here in the city and take care of business. Also the Cities we're gonna be in are posted on-line weeks in advance so if one of the other Families were to put out a hit on you then all they would have to do is pick a city we're gonna be in down the road and set it up. You know as well as I do that the other Families would love to take you out. 'Don' Vito smiles as his nephew voices his concern for his well being. 'Don' Vito Non devi preoccuparti per me o la Famiglia. Paulie sta per prendersi cura delle cose qui in città, mentre io sono andato. Come qualcuno per mettere un colpo su di me non vi è alcun modo le altre famiglie hanno le palle. Io non diventati 'don' della famiglia più potente da seduto sul mio culo sapete. Ora siamo stupirà fare un paio di cose diverso rispetto Mike ha fatto, ho avuto ogni promo tuo avversario James Sullivan ha MWE voglio che tu a guardare tutti e costruire su di lui prima di lui bash verso il basso. I'll assicurarsi che ottiene una copia di questo nastro, così come parlare di lui in camera, ho avuto modo di passare un paio di cose raddrizzò con Paulie, prima di lasciare. Before 'Don' Vito leaves the room he puts a stack of tapes on the desk with the words 'Sullivan Promos' written on the top one for B.G. to go through and study. B.G. takes the first one and pops it in the VCR. QUOTE Sullivan: Well Spitz, there are a number of things that have been racing through my head. First and foremost I am thinking about the win. I will never ever disappoint my fans, and for me to lose in a first round match would kill all my fans. They would be so upset, and I keep that in mind ever time I train, every time I speak about my match, and every time I look at the Monday Night Mayhem card. I stated that I was setting my eyes on a prize, and the United States championship is my prize. I take care of business, always have and always will. B.G. pauses the tape a look at the camera with a serious look in his eyes. B.G. Sullivan you talk about your fans like they actually matter. News flash, the fans don't matter, they don't sign our paychecks or make sure we stay healthy. Basically what I'm saying is the fan don't matter and the fact that you worry about what they think about you makes you patetico. B.G. plays the tape and he pauses it again fairly soon and he looks at the camera with a slight smile on his face. QUOTE Sullivan: So be it, if you haven't heard of me, and if MWE hasn't heard of me, they will hear me now. I plan to win this match and send a message to anyone who thinks I am just another new guy talking out my ass, because that is far from where I am. I will use my strength, my speed, and my youth to show everyone that I am going to be the next MWE United States champion, and frankly I don't think anyone can stop me. I mean, honestly I am a big dude, and I will use that to my advantage. Like I said before, I am not scared like some people, and if I can, I will just intimidate Xavier Stone to the point where he will be easy pickings. And if, for some strange reason, I am not successful, I will simply connect with the J.S.B and it will be over. See, my fans love the J.S.B. I love the J.S.B. Xavier Stone, I guarantee you will not like the J.S.B, and you want to know why. Because it will end any chance you have to beat me. I am not cocky, I just simply tell the truth. Trust me, and trust the fans on Monday, because they know and I know that the J.S.B is the single most devastating move in all of Mayhem Wrestling Entertainment. B.G. Before I got signed up for this match I never really never paid that much attention to you either, Yeah you were in the tournament to crown a United States Champion but I never thought you would get as far as you did. you're not facing Xavier Stone this time and I'm not easily intimidated so you can try to please your fans by hitting your lame excuse for a finisher, but don't be surprised when you miss and I hit you with The Mob Hit and the The Clean Up and there is no way you'll be getting up after that. He plays the rest of the tape but doesn't find anything of interest on it. He takes it out and puts in the next one. he pauses it about 5 minutes in and turns around to look at the camera. QUOTE J.S: Not training, eating the wrong foods, and not getting enough sleep. No, this is not a Pay-Per-View, no this match is not for a title, but it is my debut, and I will not let my mentor down. Stephen Arthur is a great man, and out of 400+ people, he chose me to be his next "project." He has been teaching me many useful tricks, and in return, I have been slacking off. Now I realize how stupid my actions have been, and I have to apologize to everyone. In my mind, if I disrespect my mentor, I am slapping my fans in the face, and that is not my intentions in the MWE. B.G. You can train all you want for your matches but I'm 6'2 265 LBS. You may be 1 inch taller than me but I out weigh by 20LBS of pure muscle. You can train and lift weights until your arms fall off, you won't be able to beat me. As for your trainer he's nothing, I was trained by The Big Guido & Little Guido out of ECW. That means I am a powerhouse and I can move around to keep you from getting your hands on me. Anything you've done I have done better. Again with the fans, you need to realize that they don't matter, if you ignored them you might be the US champion going into this match. B.G. plays the tape some more and he takes out the tape when it ends and he puts in the next one he pauses it after only 30 seconds. QUOTE Sullivan: Man, I told you. MWE, I told you. Xavier Stone, I told you to. And guess what, I won my match this week. I debuted and I won. I am moving on farther into this United States Championship tourney. And even with this victory, I am not pleased. See, I was not pleased with my victory, because frankly, the crowd just didn't give me the reaction I wanted. That makes me believe that my fans were not into the match, and that just won't work. I dedicated my whole match last week, solely to my mentor, my master, my trainer, and my friend...Stephen Arthur. And now that I have done that, it is all about bigger and better things. My fans will respect the fact that I keep to my word, and I like that. And frankly, if anyone doesn't like the fact that I praise my fans so much, well they can take it up with me. Because just like I did to Xavier Stone, I will hit them with the J.S.B, and pin them, and pick up another victory. B.G. So you beat Xavier Stone, congratulations James. I wouldn't be pleased either, you managed to beat someone that's not even in this company, he's off making movies. Again you're worried about the fans, This seems to be a pattern so I'm gonna let you in on a little secret. The fans don't actually care about you at all, If you were to get injured and taken out of action your 'fans' would forget about you and move on to the next flavor of the month. They always do, they have the attention span of a flea and if your not shoved down their throat every week they forget about you, just wait and see I'm positive it will happen to you. B.G. plays the rest of the tape and when he doesn't find anything he likes he takes it out of the VCR and plays the 4th one he pauses it after a couple minutes. QUOTE James: It sickens me actually. You are so cocky, so arrogant, and so illiterate. Can you tell right from left, apparently not. Seriously, you ignore your fans. You ignore your opponents. And if that wasn't bad enough, you spend your time and money opening up Casino's. Continue with that, and you will be in for a rude awakening. See unlike you, I don't go around ignoring things. I focus on the things that matter. The fans, winning, and learning. I am sure you have done your research on me...right? If not, I sure do hope you get in as much as you can within the next three days, as I pretty know you back to front at this point. B.G. I ignore the fans as well, so you have a problem with me? I'm also cocky and arrogant only I can back it up. I'm going to prove to you on Mayhem that you don't need the fans to win. You're gonna be to busy giving them what they want and I'm gonna be busy talking you apart piece by piece and getting the win. B.G. turns his back to the camera and plays the tape again. After only about 15 seconds he pauses it again and turns around to face the camera. QUOTE James: When I signed with MWE, I didn't come in prepared to lose. In fact, I try damn hard to win all of my matches. I try to keep on nice grounds with everybody...but with you, I don't know if that will happen. You seem to believe that the only real wrestler in this company is you. You align yourself with the people in power and stay away from any real challenges. Well this week, I will show you that your cockiness will be a major flaw. You say that you deserve to be main-eventing...well prove it. This week, for both of us, will be our time to shine. And if you can beat me, I will keep my mouth shut, but I am serious man. Dead serious, and I am not scared of you or your ego. Unlike some, I am not an ass, and I look at all my opponents as equal. B.G. I also came here to win, lately I haven't exactly done that but with my Uncle in my corner there is no way I'm going to lose anymore. Another thing that puts this match in my favor is that unlike you I don't care about anyone I step into that ring against, all I wan to do is hurt them and get the win and move on to bigger and better opponents. You need to realize that I'm better than you and there is no way you can beat me. B.G. watches the rest of the tape without seeing anything good and he takes it out and puts in the next one. He watches that one as well but still doesn't see anything he can use so he takes that one out and puts in the last tape. He watches it with an irritated look on his face as he doesn't see anything on that one either. Then h e turns off the TV and tunrs toward the camera. B.G. Enough with the tapes . Sullivan it me and you 1-on-1 and I am going to beat you. I'm the only member of Project Revolution who doesn't have gold and that is going to change very soon. When I beat you I will move up the ladder closer to a title shot. I don't care if it'd Aeron Beowulf and the X-TV title, The man that beat you Sinc Mercier and the US title or even Sonny Williams and the MWE World title, I will face one of those 3 men for their title and I will bring one more piece of gold to the Revolution. B.G. gets up and leaves the room to go find his Uncle as the camera focuses and the James Sullivan Promos sitting on the desk as it goes to black.
