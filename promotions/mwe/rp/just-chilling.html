The Grand Mystique

OOC note to begin with……OK this starts in the middle of an RP done by DJ a few weeks back (http://z4.invisionfree.com/MWEntertainment/index.php?showtopic=559&hl=). When GM’s dialogue is in italics, it is internal dialogue. I hope it all makes sense October 30th 2008, 16:57, Golds Gym ,Detroit QUOTE *The montage ends as Maxx and GM are sparing in the middle of the ring, chain wrestling commences as Maxx and GM match each other counter for counter.* Maxx is placed in an arm lock but reverses taking GM down to the mat. GM: “Now we are champs, everyone is going to want a piece of us.” GM counters with a head scissor takedown as Maxx nips up and floats over for a front face lock. GM: “We have giant targets on us.” GM attempts to once again kick out. GM: “People don’t want us to succeed in MWE and he’s more concerned with that slut Chloe!” But Maxx locks the hold into the Maximum Impact! GM: “OWWWWW!!!!!” GM suddenly grabs the bottom rope with his left foot and Maxx relinquishes the hold leaving his tag team partner angry. GM: WHAT THE HELL WAS THAT! Maxx looks on puzzled by his tag team partner’s actions as GM looks on in rage. GM: “What the f-!!! *yells* WHY DID YOU LOCK ME IN THE CROSSFACE YOU BIG PIECE OF….. Maxx turns aggressive and shoves GM, they both lock up once more this time GM breaks it up and slaps Maxx across the face with his left palm Maxx: YOU SON OF….. GM, now even more angry, runs the ropes and attempts a cross body but Maxx powers out and lifts him into a fireman’s carry, but Maxx hesitates as he suddenly remembers who the man he’s fighting with is……. QUOTE Maxx: (Internal dialogue) what the hell am I doing? [/color] GM: “Shit….he’s gonna do it, the little…..” GM suddenly leaps down and kicks Maxx in his right ankle, he positions himself and locks in an ankle lock submission with a sadistic smile. GM: “Well I’m gona prove them wrong!” He grabs Maxx’s ankle but suddenly stops as Maxx looks on in horror GM: “Oh shit…..” GM: "Looks like my instincts got the better of me, for that, my apologies" QUOTE GM holds out his right hand as Maxx looks on remembering the look he saw as his own tag team partner attempted to rip his ankle into shreds, he however reluctantly accepts as both men depart, GM hits the showers as Maxx sits alone and looks on at his ankle thinking what could have happened We see GM in the shower, head leaning on the wall, relaxing as the hot water gushing over him. GM: “I could have destroyed him, taken his ankle apart and left him lying. Is he suspicious of my motives for joining him in the MWE? No…..I can’t think like that…..we will have great challenges lying in wait for us. Our title reign will be long and prosperous, no matter who is put in front of us.” Scene end ***************************************************************** November 21st, 10pm We now see the backstage area immediately following the first episode of Genesis. It is a hustle-bustle of activity and many wrestlers are milling about in the area. JJ Executioner has stormed off without hesitation. Ace Cannon has just come in, kicked a couple of objects and slammed the door behind him as he was ushered into his locker room by security. The camera turns around to show the Grand Mystique and Maxx, sitting down by the entrance to the area. GM is holding both his MWE World Tag Team Championship and the UWA Heavyweight Championship across his lap. GM: “I still haven’t had it fully explained to me; why weren’t we booked on the show again?” Maxx: “Look GM, I’m sure Dick has his reasons.” Chloe Cornett enters the room, looking ridiculously hot in a white vest top and jeans, which cling to her figure and accentuates her curves. Maxx’s eyes nearly pop out the socket when he sees her. GM: *whispers* “Seriously, stop thinking about her naked already!” Maxx: “What? Look it’s not like that!” GM: “What....she just tripped fell and landed on your d...?” GM: “Just get your head in the game mate. Seriously....we got a tag title match at Adrenaline Rush coming up but don’t think for a second that Malibu won’t hesitate in taking us out of that match if we lose the belts in between now and then.” Maxx: “Relax pal.” GM: “Don’t tell me to relax Maxx.” Chloe comes over to where the tag team champions are sitting. Chloe: “Hey stud.....you wana go get some food. I’m almost done here.” Maxx: “Sure hun, let me get your coat.” Maxx, with a huge smile on his face races off around the corner. GM and Chloe sit together for a tense few seconds. Cue uncomfortable silence. Her phone rings from inside her handbag. Chloe: “Hello?.....Hello Mr Covell!......A meeting at Mayhem, sure I can do that......yes I can get there no problem......Can I ask what it’s about?.......OK, no problem.....Bye sir.” She ends the call and writes a text message. Cue more uncomfortable silence, only broken by the celebrations of the new World Champion, DC, his girlfriend Lindsay and her whooping manager Eli Silver. Maxx returns, lumbering with a thick fur coat and a beaming smile. Maxx: “Let’s go babe.” GM and Maxx shake hands. Maxx offers Chloe his arm and she accepts as they walk away. GM gets up, props a belt on each shoulder and proudly walks across the room, which has gradually emptied. He walks up to a door with a sign on it that says “Dick Malibu, Genesis General Manager Esq.” GM pauses to knock and then strolls right in. Malibu is sat at a large oak desk, typing on a laptop, which he shuts as GM enters the office. Malibu: “Well Mr Tag Champ, sorry Mr Dual Champ.......what is so important that you come into my office uninvited?” GM: “I want to know what the hell went down here tonight? Why were we not on the card?” Malibu: “Is it that important to you?” GM: “I’m not in the habit of wasting my breath and asking unimportant questions.” Malibu: “Well.....” GM: “Look Dick. If it isn’t already obvious I hate not being booked, especially for no good reason.” Malibu: “You have gotta be kidding me!” GM: “No Dick, I don’t kid about this shit. I take my job seriously and it’s about time the MWE started to take me a little more seriously.” Malibu: “Look my mask wearing friend; you don’t need worry about why you did not wrestle tonight.” GM: “Well I am and I don’t expect it to be repeated.” Malibu: “Duly noted Mr Mystique.” GM turns to leave. “Do you want to know who you are facing next?” GM: “Consider me intrigued.” Malibu pauses as if to extend the drama. GM turns to leave again. GM: “I’ll get it from MWE.com if it’s that good.” Malibu: “OK, OK....you and Maxx face Joey Dallas and Ace Cannon at the next episode of Genesis.” GM walks around to the side of Malibu’s desk. He offers his hand. Dick shakes it but then GM pulls him in sharply. GM: “I have two weeks of pent up frustration to unleash on someone and you want your number two and three draft pick to be in the firing line? You do realise that when we beat them -” Malibu: “If you beat them” GM: “After we beat them....and retain our tag team titles......it will not only prove that we are the superior tag team in the whole company, but it will discredit both Cannon and Dallas, your draft picks Dick, as nothing more than a waste, a pair of egos who achieved nothing of any note without whining and begging for it first!” Malibu: “So you are happy about my choice then?” GM: “Ecstatic.” And with that comment, dry as ice, GM leaves the room. He strolls down a corridor and out onto the stage in the arena, where less than an hours earlier, thousands had been watching the first episode of MWE Genesis. He sits down on the edge, rests his legs over the side and looks down at his title belts. GM: “On the way to the biggest MWE pay-per-view yet, possibly the biggest ever, certainly my biggest yet, and they want us to warm up against a pair of number one contenders? Normally I would say this is a challenge to be relished and await with anticipation. But in this case, it’s different....these two contenders.....are the biggest pair of egos in the company! But if ego is the way that this company wants to go, then maybe the right champions and challengers are in play!” “But let’s look at this objectively......neither Dallas or Cannon got the job done when given ample opportunity and they have the gall to blame each other for it even though they have never actually faced each other.” “So I don’t understand why Cannon and Dallas have the egos when they have achieved barely anything to back it up. If titles defined ego then *he chuckles* I would have been able to get my head through that curtain every night, let alone get in the ring to do my job!” “So Ace, Joey, you need to be aware that no titles, not mine nor any other. And unlike you two, myself and Maxx are main event quality stars who can work together. We are the tag team champions but we have barely even begun to construct our legacy in the MWE. We are not about to let that be disgraced before it is even under way.” “Myself and Maxx are ready to steal the show at Adrenaline Rush. But taking you two no-talent ego-pushers apart before you go to war on pay-per-view will be exactly the preparation I want, going into Adrenaline Rush.” GM drops down to the arena floor, about seven feet and begins to walk towards the ring. A couple of technicians are beginning the long process of dismantling the ring. GM casually rolls into the ring. He gestures to one of the technicians. He throws GM a mic. GM pats it gently, until he gets some feedback off the sound system. GM: “You hear me fellas.” He leans on the ropes, facing the stage where he just came from. Some more technicians come out and upon seeing GM in the ring, they sit down. GM: “Lazy fucks!” Said with a cheeky grin. GM: “OK let’s go over this.” GM begins to pace. “Joey fucking Dallas!!!!! It’s time to get this off my chest. I’m sick of your holier than thou attitude! You think you’re deserving of a number one contenders spot. You could not even beat a pair of jobber freaks to get a shot at the belt. You were given ample chance and you blew it. Just like Barbie McQueen.” GM tries to hide the chuckle but fails as roars of laughter echo around the empty arena. “You got your match at the pay-per-view thanks to some Texas sized ass kissing. Yeah I said it. I’m sure your mama Dallas is proud.” More laughter but GM is more serious. GM: “One last thing.......the MWE doesn’t need a salvation.......the Grand Mystique is already here!” That is greeted by a ripple of applause.GM continues, unabated, determined. “But in comparison to Ace Cannon, Joey Dallas doesn’t have that much of an ego. But the problem with Ace fucking Cannon, Mr Madness….well I’ll be honest I think Mr Madness has gone a little la-la. I heard he seems to think he is in a triple threat match against Dallas and Maxx. Well that little prick has underestimated the Grand Mystique for the final time.” GM is really fired up and his small audience respect his intensity with hushed silence and engaged looks. “You see for those of you with good memories will remember that I had to pair up with Ace Cannon in EW. Thanks to that punk I got pinned, because his wrestling mind went AWOL and his ego took over. He was too busy focusing on DC to use his stupid little brain and come and help me. There’s only so much that the best training money can buy will teach you and Ace, you little bitch, I found out that day that you were useless in any environment where you have to look out for anyone’s interest but your own.” Another small ripple of applause. “Joey Dallas will figure this out for himself. Cannon can be misconstrued as unfocused and unprofessional, someone capable of blowing hot air directly up himself. But make no mistake; Ace Cannon is a totally different beast when he has nothing to lose and everything to gain.” GM stops pacing and has a thoughtful look on his face. “But Ace," *GM moves his belts so that they are clearly shown* "these belts show that I am good enough to beat you one on one and in a tag team match. No ego, that’s simple fact. When we see you at Genesis, make sure next time you back your shit up when you get in my ring.” GM thinks about continuing but then stops. “What do you think?” GM is greeted by whooping cheers and rapturous applause. Satisfied, he throws the mic out of the ring and steps out. He walks up the ramps, shakes a couple of hands and tells them he will see them next week. As he steps through to enter the backstage area, GM chuckles to himself. GM: “Next week is going to be seriously interesting.” With that he readjusts his belts and walks away.
