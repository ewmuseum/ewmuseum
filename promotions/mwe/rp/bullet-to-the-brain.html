BULLET TO THE BRAIN!

Mar 7 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
** We see various people dressed in casual clothes moving about it what seems to be a museum. Upon further inspection it is a museum dedicated too The Kennedy Assassination in Dallas, Texas. As the camera pans around we see Ace Cannon. He is dressed in a black Affliction t-shirt and jeans, he is casually walking around the museum looking at plaques and artifacts from that faithful day on November 22nd, 1963. He moves into a little theatre where they are showing footage of Kennedy being shot… As the video plays Cannon begins to laugh uncontrollably. People in the showing turn around and give him a horrific looks but he continues his gut busting laughter. After a few moments of continual laughter a usher finally comes and whispers something in Cannon’s ear. Cannon looks up at the usher with tears from laughter pouring down his face and the usher says something to him and then exit’s the theater and after a few seconds Cannon does the same. Cannon walks to an elevator still laughing at the video of the assassination. He enters the elevator and still continues his gut busting laughter all the way down to the ground floor. He exit’s the elevator still laughing and tears running down his face. He finally succumbs to the laughter and sits on the steps of the museum.** Cannon: Man… **Cannon keeps laughing but it finally comes an end.** Cannon: Don’t get me wrong about this laughter it isn’t about Kennedy getting his head blown up like a watermelon but it reminded me of something… It reminded me of my brother, Brian. ** Cannon stares out into the street.. The exact same street where Kennedy was shot down.** Cannon: See the comparison of my brother and JFK is all too eerie in my opinion. There both charismatic men who everyone loves, they both can politick with the best of them and know one can see the wrong in what they do. ** Cannon continues staring at the street where the most beloved President in United States history took a bullet right to the brain.** Cannon: But there is more.. You see, I believe that what Kennedy was set-up. If you watch the video then you can see his Secret Service agents standing down and that is something they never did. By doing that it became way to dangerous and gave a potential shooter a better shot at the Presidents head. ** Cannon continues looking at the street where Kennedy had his brains splattered.** Cannon: Now like I said Kennedy was set-up I believe that Brian is being set-up as well. I know a lot of you are saying who could possibly be setting up the beloved Brian Michaels? Well, it is none other then our dear old father. ** Cannon continues his unbreakable gaze on the street.** Cannon: Now I know a lot of you are saying this sounds as crazy as Kennedy being hit by two shooters.. Or does it? Let’s think about this for a moment… Why would our father in his right mind pit his favorite son against the crazy son. The son who has kicked children in the head, the son who kicked a crippled, and the son who has ended more careers then this current economy. I mean it really makes no sense unless my dad is tired of Brian and all of his bullshit games and politicking that he does oh so well. ** Cannon’s gaze on the street is stronger then it has been in the previous moments.** Cannon: Weather my father is setting Brian up or not is beyond me, but I do know my role in all this… I am the shooter… I am Lee Harvey Oswald… I am the man gets to deliver the death blow to the legend of Brian Michaels! ** Cannon finally brakes his hypnotic gaze off the street and looks directly into the camera with eyes that are glowing with anger.** Cannon: I saw your little retort to me Brian and I know it is a faux pa around here to respond to another’s promo… But I say fuck it… ** Cannon continues looking directly too the camera.** Cannon: Brian in your little promo I got the instinct your rattled and that I am in your head. Sure you came across as a badass and all tough but you know how I found that I got into your head? ** Cannon pauses and continues looking into the camera.** Cannon: You were getting fact wrong and that is a sure sign that your nervous and rattled about the impending ass whipping your about to receive. I mean you sat around boasting that you and Dallas both banged Paige before I go to her…. Well, your wrong Brian and no pun intended but your DEAD wrong.. Let me jog your memory here. Paige and myself were a couple when we left WZCW and were that way until she left me for Joey Dallas and then a year later in EW you finally got a piece of Paige. So that was pussy that had been fucked twice before you even got to it…. That’s a shame and when you got it Windy was also fucking her at the same time and my question is how does that make you feel? ** Cannon winks at the camera.** Cannon: Now Brian, I never won a title in RevPro and sure that was a two bit company where I should have been champion but I did something you have never done in your career…. I jobbed to a younger talent to give him a shot at the number one in a company. God forbid Brian Michaels does that! I mean he wouldn’t even put his own little brother Randy over if he was on his deathbed with life support hooked up too him. ** Cannon stands up on the steps and the camera pans up with him.** Cannon: You know how else I got into your head Brian? You came out with a promo that wasn’t even yours. You came out with a promo that DC would do and that’s funny seeing as how he ran your ass over with a car. I mean your really scrapping the bottom of the barrel when you have to cut a promo like a man who tried to kill you! I mean what’s next? Are you going to start kicking kids and cripples like your dear old brother to get recognized? ** Cannon stops and a sarcastic grin comes across his face.** Cannon: Brian, I know when you see this your going to be filled with anger, hate and violence toward me and that doesn’t bother me… That’s my game and that hate, anger and violence I have toward you is second to none! It has been building in the pit of my stomach toward you for a very long time and now I get to finally unleash it! It will better then any sexual gratification I have ever felt. ** Cannon walks down the last couple of steps and walks into the street and stands into the exact same spot where Kennedy was shot.** Cannon: This spot right here was where Kennedy was shot . This is where he took his last breath and met his demise… And the wrestling ring is going to be similar for you Brian.. It won’t be a bullet to the brain like it was for Kennedy, but instead it will be my boot upside your head… ** The camera pans down to a shot of Cannon’s foot and then pans back up to Ace Cannon grinning into the camera.** Cannon: You have met your maker Brian…. And he’s your brother… He is me… ACE CANNON! ** Cannon makes the shape of a gun with his forefinger and thumb and point it as the camera. He begins laughing and pretends to shoot the camera as we fade to black.**
