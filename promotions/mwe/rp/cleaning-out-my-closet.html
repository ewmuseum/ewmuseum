Cleaning out my Closet; Eric Derf VS Alex Bowen

Feb 4 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The scene opens in a room, plain white walls. A few pictures hanging around, most unclear of what they are due to distance. A TV on one side of the room, 37" Flatscreen, and a Leather Couch opposite it. A coffee table inbetween the two holds nothing but a small pile of Magazines, probably wrestling related. In the corner, a small propane fireplace lights up the otherwise dark room, and hanging right above it, is the Havoc Championship, glowing brightly in the firelight. The room suddenly becomes alot brighter as a light is turned on. The glow of the fire now barely makes an impact, but what does make the room seem different is the man walking into it, Eric Derf. Dressed as casual as one comes, Jeans and a t-shirt, he walks into the room, ignoring his surroundings. He walk right to the fireplace, and stares at the Havoc Title hanging there for a moment. After a few seconds, he takes a huge deep breath, and turns around to look into the camera, his face filled with exitment. Ladies and gentleman, welcome to the road to Friday Night OverDrive! That night, stars will be made! Regular man will become legends, and legends will become even greater. Some of MWEs best are stepping into that ring, and believe me, we will ALL tear that house down! Eric is cutting into this promo with an intensity like we've never seen. It's not hard to tell he's exited about something. Friday night, your going to be treated. We've got one guy with a familiar face and another guy, who's an older face, fighting for a championship being held by the guest referee, a man who's deemed as always being a fresh face, probably because it's a different one he presents every week. We've got two new guys, supposedly huge names from developmental, facing off against our Tag Team Champions. Two guys that have never tagged against two guys that haven't actually worked as a solid team in quite some time, both with something to prove. He makes fists, and hold them by his chest, almost as if he was posing. We've got a psycho bitch facing a masked midget, another masked midget facing off against a fellow Canadian, and another Canadian holding US gold, facing a US citizen in an always exiting ladder match! He raises his hand, pointing to the sky as if he were reaching for the gold above the ladder. And then, after all of that has been said and done, we've got a Hall of Famer, a Champion, facing off against a man who thinks he's a god among men. Two huge egos colliding, either way it goes, it's going to be bigger then the real 2nd coming of Jesus Himself! He brings his hand back down, back into a fist, posing like he was earlier. He's still looking strait up. But before any of that happens... He looks right into the camera, still exited, but also now with a look of seriousness. The show will be stolen... Because before any of those matches happen, you will be blessed with the return of a great MWE superstar, a hardcore icon, Alex Bowen. And facing him, is one of the brightest, youngest superstars on the roster, the current X-TV Champion, Ladies and gentleman, ERIC DERF! He yells the last part, clearing trying to hype his match against Alex Bowen as higher then the rest of the card. Alex Bowen, one of the great wrestlers of our time, is returning at Friday Night Overdrive. He's looking to have his return match be one that's remembered for a long time. He wants to win the Havoc Title... Too Bad! Eric lets out a "huh" laugh, looks back at the title hanging above his fireplace, the glow from the fire not really adding much to it anymore. You see Bowen, I may not think of this title as very worthy, but damn, it looks so good there, don't it? But that's besides the point. Because your not going to win it back. I hate to ruin your return, but when you found out you were facing me, you should have known you'd be overshadowed by my continuing winning streak. Eric walks away from the fireplace, and into another room. As the camera changes rooms as well, we're now in a kitchen. This room doesn't look nearly as nice as the previous room. The fridge and oven both look quite old, there is no kitchen table. There is a counter with stools though, and on that counter, is an opened laptop. That is what Eric walks towards when he enters. You see, this is the part where I would go to my computer, type in the name of my upcoming opponent, in this case, Alex Bowen, but that's not the case this time. I know enough about Bowen from WZCW, from studying his brother, from the history books, I know enough about him to take him on. This is one match I don't have to break out the books on, but then again, it'd never hurt... But I'll save that for another time. Because right now, I've got more important thing to address. He stops staring at the laptop, and closes it without actually typing anything. He then reaches for a nearby water bottle and takes a quick drink before setting it back on the counter, without putting the cap back on it. If I know Bowen, I know he's going to be looking for anything he can to beat me. We all know he's going to go out to the ring and use whatever he can to beat the crap out of me, you know, the whole "Hardcore" thing, but I know Bowen, and I know he's not going into that match without digging up some dirt on me first. We all do it, dig up some dirt, rag on our opponents with it, and hope we get into their heads enough to beat them. Well Bowen, if that's your plan, you just hit a bump in the road. Eric pulls one of the stools out from under the counter, and hops on, both feet just barely dangling above the ground. I have a long, storied history. Some of you know it, some of you don't. But I'm willing to bet that in an attempted to get into my head, Bowen is going to try to spread some of that dirt around. Make me look bad in front of everyone. Point out some of the negatives of my past... Like my sexuality. Eric crosses one of his legs over the other one, clearly trying to get comfortable. It seems an old interview I did about three years ago has resurfaced. One I'd personally hoped wouldn't, but it did, and theres nothing I can do about it. For those of you that didn't hear the news, the rumors, it seems as if I am gay, or that's how they made me sound, anyway. Well, let me confess something right now, coming from my own mouth, right here, right now. I'm not gay, I am open minded. Yeah, I was only 17 when I did that interview, but that fact still stands. If I met my solemate, and it was a guy, I'd be happy with that. I'm no flaming ass clown, as some of you have since labeled me, I'm probably one of the most respected gentlemen most of you know, you just let pop culture brainwash you. Eric uncrosses his legs and hops off the stool, and stands with his arms crossed, resting on the counter. The reason I bring this up right now, is because this is the kind of thing that Alex Bowen would try to use against me. He'd probably go out, make fun of me, call me degrading names in reference to being gay, and hope I'd get upset at the insults. Well, he's wrong. He can call me gay, but it would be an empty insult. It would have no meaning. Because when I did that interview, I said I was looking for a solemate. And guess what people? I found one. And... SHE... is the love of my life. Eric lifts his arms off the counter, and reaches for the laptop. He lifts the screen back up, and turns it around so that the camera can see it. It's his desktop showing, with an interesting pic as the backdrop. After a few moments of showing the pic, he closes the laptop again, and his face isn't angry, it's rather cocky looking. So that's one thing the world can stop talking about. Eric Derf isn't dating men. Eric Derf has never kissed a man. Eric Derf is actually dating a female right now, and a damn good female at that. Bowen can bring up that little three year old interview, but all it is is a piece of my past, something that now has little to no meaning for me. But, as I say that, I can't help but think that Bowen would stop at that. No, no, no, no, no. Eric shakes his head as he walks around the counter, basically just to stand on the other side of it, his whole body now showing. Alex Bowen won't just call me gay and call it a day. No, he'll keep looking for dirt on me, anything he can use against me. Something he can tell the MWE fans that will get them riled up, get under my skin... Something like my losing streak in WZCW, for example. Eric crosses his arms and continues staring into the camera. Yeah, it's true. The undefeated MWE X-TV Champion was once the biggest joke in a different fed. I would go into every match as the guy the fans wanted to win, the guy the fellow wrestlers would want to win, hell, even Bowen himself wanted me to win. And yet I would be booked to lose every time. I'd go out there, give it my all, and lose. But I would also leave the ring, every single time, to a standing ovation. The fans knew who the real winner should have been, and they'd never let me, or anyone forget that. And now, much like my little "Gay Secret", that's in my past. I've moved on to bigger, better things. Bowen can go and tell the world of how I went over 6 months never winning a match, but we're not in the same place that happened. He can brag he won more matches then me, but that'll only make him look foolish when I beat him at OverDrive. The cocky expression on Erics face slowly begins to fade as he continues, hands now in his pockets, still fairly casual. But then again, give credit where credit is due. Alex Bowen will definitely be my greatest challenge to date, and my Championship, whichever one he wants, has never been this close to being lost. So, I suppose I shouldn't take the guy too lightly. I mean, I know he won't stop at a gay joke and a reminder that I was a loser. No, he's going to really dig deep, and he's going to try to hit me with something harsh... something... Erics face suddenly goes pale, as if he'd seen a ghost. His hands drop to his sides, he stops talking. This goes on for about 6 or 7 seconds before Eric looks into the camera. Alex Bowen, there is something else I want to show you, show the world. Something about my past, I need to get out of the way myself before you have the chance to... But I can't do it here... I need to go... back there... Eric slowly walks out of camera view as the camera slowly fades to black, leaving those watching in a state of confusion... *** The room is dark. Sunlight fades through ripped and tattered blue curtains. Along the walls, there are multiple blank spots, spots where the white paint is clearer then other spots, obviously there had been pictures hanging there. The only furniture in this room is an ugly old red couch, the left side of it with a grove in the seat, someone had spent alot of time sitting in that very spot. The floors look like they haven't' been swept in years, as the dust is as visible as the sunlight through the curtains. The room is almost silent, except for a near unhearable clatter in the ceiling, possibly from mice or some other rodents. And even though you can't tell on camera, there is an eerie feeling in this room that makes it known that the stench is almost unbearable. A door on the far side of the room opens, as Eric enters, dressed as he was earlier. The first thing he does, almost instinctively, is pull his shirt above his nose to reduce the smell of the room. Ugh, that's awful! He walks across the room and sits on the couch, next to the grove. In front of him is a coffee table with a garbage bag on it. He reaches in, and pulls out a pile of old photographs. Not saying anything, he slowly flips through them all for a bit. Eventually, he stops on one picture and just stares at it for a few seconds before replying in a monotone voice. I haven't seen a picture of my father in years. I forgot what he looked like, and I think I might have preferred it that way. His sick smile, the bottle always in his pictures, it makes me sick! He tosses the photo in front of him without showing the camera. About a foot out of his reach, the picture slowly flutters to the ground. He sits in silence for a few moments before breaking the silence. ... I hate that man... Since I was a boy, I have hated him. My siblings hated him, my mother even hated him. He was an asshole... But he provided us with money, something my mother could never do. He'd come home every weekend with a paycheck and some liquor, and the same fight would happen, every damn weekend. He'd bitch at my mother for being lazy, she'd take his money anyway, and eventually, us kids would get drug into it against our will. Eric takes a deep breath as he slowly pulls his shirt down from over his nose, not even worrying about the stench anymore. The bastard even broke my little brothers arm. I was with him when he went back to school that monday, he had to lie to his teachers just to avoid getting his father in trouble, cause we both knew it would only result in more beatings. Eric stands up, and walks towards the camera, looking right into it, anger flaring in his eyes. Alex Bowen knows of my past, hell, everyone in WZCW knows of my past. I have already told this story once, and I've since let it pass me. But I'll be damned is I'll let Bowen use it against me... I killed my father... Eric sits back down, this time on the coffee table. As he lands on it, his behind brushes some of the photos to the floor, enough of them to make a fairly loud "clud" on the floor. It was about three years ago. I was wrestling at an indy show, booked to win my first ever title... My family was there. I wanted them there for the big moment... But I didn't invite my father. It was Friday night, he would have been drunk by then... But he knew, and he showed up anyway... He found my family, he harassed them, he humiliated them... I saw it, and I knew right there that, after all the beatings he'd given us, I was finally strong enough, brave enough, to shut him up. I was in the moment... Eric lets his head drop towards the ground, still speaking in an angry tone, only now with a raspy tone to his voice. It all happened so fast... I grabbed him, pulled him away from my mother, and I punched him... And he never got up after that... The doctors said he died of Alcohol Poisoning, that I mearly knocked him unconscious before he died... As much as I hated that man, and I still do... I never wanted him to die. And when he did, a little part of him was past onto me. The little bit of good that was in him, ended in me. Eric gets up from the coffee table and walks to one of the blank spots on the wall. A large picture had once hung here, but has since been taken down. This is where my mother hung my graduation picture. She was so proud of me. After what happened to my father, she left. She abandoned her house, this house, and left everything behind. Friends, family, she couldn't deal with the pressure... Although his face can't be seen at the moment, he whipes his face, probably wiping a tear away. And now, three years later. I face one of the men to know of this history... Alex Bowen. He turns around again, and although he's clearly upset at past memory remembered, he's forcing his cocky face as he addresses his opponent. Honestly, I don't know if Bowen even remembers this. I don't know if he would have brought it up. But he should remember it. Because he grew up in a similar lifestyle. An abusive, alcoholic father, a neglectful mother. Hell, things in your house were so bad, your baby brother had to leave home. I'm sure your parents took that well, didn't they? The forced cockiness quickly disappears, and a look of guilt quickly takes over. Look man... I don't know about you, but I'm not about to rip into your childhood. We grew up in the same boat, and we both came out better men because of it. We've both escaped our pasts, and made our own futures. And at Friday Night OverDrive, we finally have a chance to meet, one on one. We can see who's benefited the most from such an awful childhood. But remember... I'm expecting you to bring your "A" game. Don't try to get into my mind with gay jokes. Don't try to get into my mind with calling me a loser. And I know you won't try to bring up my childhood... Just bring your "A" game, and I'll bring mine. And to the winner, goes the spoils. Eric looks towards the ground, slightly upset... only to lift his head, exited, overjoyed, or so it seems. As if he wasn't still standing in his parents abandoned house, but in front of a live crowd of screaming fans. X-Havoc Title. Friday Night OverDrive. Eric Derf. Alex Bowen. Ladies and Gentlemen, prepare to have the show stolen when two of the brightest superstars in MWE duke it out. Forget the midgets, the bitches, the ladders, the savior, because our match will steal the show. Eric takes a step back, looks right into the camera, and points. Alex Bowen... See you Friday. We're stealing that damn show. Eric opens his hand and reaches towards the camera, covering it in blackness as the near dead silence completely dies.
