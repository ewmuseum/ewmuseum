A Sharp Man.; A David Whitman RP.

May 8 2009

David Whitman

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
QUOTE (It happened on...) February 13th 2009. That was the day. The day I was saved. And on March 22nd, 2009, was the day my savior died. After a long, hard battle with what many would call personal demons, I had finally begun making some headway and started going down the positive path. But upon hearing of my match with Dallas, these issues had come back, and with a vengeance. If it wasn’t for that match… I might not be here now. Mentally or even physically speaking. So when Dallas basically went over the edge… I felt… I don’t know what I felt. Responsible, maybe? Like, it should have been ME? Had I helped create this monster? No matter. What matters is now. And what’s happening now is that I have a match against the… new incarnation of that man. I have a chance to do for Dallas, what he did for me. Or… do I? He saved me… But how can I return the favor, if it’s too late? The questions never stop… Though for now, I have facts to focus on. On May 11th, it will have been 87 days since I was saved by Joey Dallas. On May 11th, it will have been 50 days since Joey Dallas died. A sense of gloom hangs low and steady throughout the atmosphere, clinching tightly on to the cloudy skies as the dark symbolism of the situation escapes all but those who pause to ponder it very carefully. Birds, carving invisible trails through the air as they fly, not for pleasure; but to escape to a safer haven. Knowing rain, or possibly worse, is afoot. Noise, most of it distinctly identifiable as murmuring; a sea of scattered sentences and worried words tumble and thrash against and through each other, melting together into a mass of unintelligible faux-communication. Air; soft and moist, yet still slightly crisp… and gentle. But somehow intuitively indicative of a harsher, louder, more frantic future. And… A war-worn David Whitman. From all his cuts, nicks and bruises; the wounds on his knuckles would seem most recent. The shades ranging from purple to red to pinkish, they convey a back-story of hours and hours of punching- at first glance, probably not a punching bag, but something more along the lines of a wall- and they communicate aggression, and they express madness. Anger, and a hint of sorrow. And they convey all of this within a split second of looking at them. His face, patched with a few thin white bandages, does not immediately give off any conceivable expression, as he seems focused on nothing but the moment at hand. His hair giving the impression that he hasn’t combed it in forever, and the forming of a light mustache and beard show he might have been skipping some morning steps recently. He is there. He is just there. A war-worn David Whitman stands. He stands straight up. His black, red and white Mayhem Mafia shirt slightly ragged, a title on each shoulder. Two symbols that should impart the notion of a proud man, are instead hanging and seeming more like bookends; all that makes the difference between a mass of wisdom, history and promise and a disappointing, messy pile. His signature, unmistakable baggy black pants differ from usual only in that they seem more frayed near the foot area, as if incessant kicking had been taking place not too long ago, and red/black stains near the calf areas that ironically enough compliment the tattered t-shirt. He stands intently, and he stands at attention… A warn-worn David Whitman stands in front of a Palace. Not in Paris. Not in France. But in Romania. He is standing in front of the Palace of the Parliament in Bucharest. The impressive monument is illuminated by inner and outer lights that, combined with the darkness bestowed upon the area by Mother Nature in the form of a seemingly brewing storm, radiate an unsettling vibe. He stands. Behind a podium is where he stands. A small, feeble, frail wooden podium. Its apparent home-made aura gives it its own little charm, especially when highlighted by the surrounding ambiance of restlessness. The Podunk podium has obviously been set up merely minutes earlier, maybe less. And as is the proclivity with nearly anything even remotely connected to Mayhem Wrestling Entertainment, it had taken about that exact same time for a crowd to begin forming around David Whitman, and his little podium. It is an untelevised event, if it could even be called an event, and that is the kind that piques the attention and interests of the masses the most. The feeling of belonging to a relatively small group of strangers, brought together by a common cause or reason. Feeling separated from the majority, experiencing the sense of wonder. The sensation that you are witnessing something special. The usual chanting and cheers of fans are absent among the whispering and mumbling; held back during these moments by chains of confusion and anxiety, unknowing and perplexed passersby stand to witness something they know nothing about, and journalists and the like try to squeeze in and get closer as cameras flash with irony in trying to capture an intimate moment, only to share with the world. David Whitman, standing at the top of the steps behind the podium, and with no microphone or any voice amplifier of any kind, waits a couple of moments for it to become quiet. He is looking down, holding the ends of the podium with each hand. Slowly, he raises his head to look at the crowd. His motion has the effect of a dimmer switch on the voices, and once he is looking directly at the crowd, it is quiet. Very silent, apart from the odd camera click and faint, low rumbling in the distant background. David Whitman takes a solemn deep breath. He speaks. I stand here before you for one sole reason… and that reason is that I will not set foot in France. He pauses. Intently. For now. The wind begins to pick up as murmuring amongst the people begins again, this time slowly and quietly. I leave it… to them. A raised hand, finger pointing towards the distance. I will leave France to Vanguard. I will leave it to the Black List Mafia. The crowd gets slightly louder, now that the reason for this event has seemingly come to the surface. I want their evil, their sickness, their foul dementedness to cling onto every molecule in that place. The crowd grows quieter now. I will give them the chance to taint it, taint it all as much as they can. And quieter. I want their presence to linger, to infect and corrupt that arena as much as possible. I want them to be… in their element. Befuddlement creeps among the witnesses as they try to anticipate where this is going. I will not interfere, or try to catch them off guard. All eyes seem focused on him now, as he speaks. I want them to feel like the demons they are in their own little personal firepit, and I want them to anticipate our arrival. He looks down at his hands; from the left fist slowly to the right one. I will… I want… His stare moves to the crowd. It is as if he is looking at all of them, simultaneously. But most of all… I wait. Silence. I wait, for the moment I set foot in that ring to face Sharpman. A breach in silence, as many a person of the mass of people expresses their opinions to one another. I want you all to understand… what I feel. And what I feel is that this has been enough. His fists clench tighter as he takes a deep breath before continuing. We have stood by, and watched mouth agape as a horrific group of… people… commit unspeakable acts of gratuitous violence and wanton hatred. We have done wrong. We have done wrong by not fixing what’s wrong, and I stand here to TELL you, we are going to begin fixing. And we will begin this Monday. A contemplative breath, as he reaches for the past. He continues. Last Mayhem the Mafia dominated, and showed we were a force to be reckoned with. Unstoppable, we physically explained to Sharpman, Cannon and Resoan that the Mayhem Mafia is on a mission. But the thing is… the mission is far from over. Last Monday was a triumphant beginning. And now… we focus on the future. We will invade France, and we will thrust our bare hands into the bodies that are evil, and pull out the bloody, still beating heart that is good. Black List Mafia needs to be stopped. Resoan needs to be put in his place, once again. And there is no one better to do that than the Mayhem Mafia. By now, more cameras are flashing as the tone in his voice becomes slightly more determined. So I stand here to tell you that I believe we can fix this situation, and we will. But like I said, in the meantime, I am waiting. A pause, for dramatic effect. For Sharpman. Nailed it. February 13th marked a huge leap in my recovery. I was in the middle of the process of rising from the ashes, when the old David tossed me back down, and kicked dirt into my face… I had no idea what happened, until I got a chance to process it… It was fear. I feared change. The old me wanted to lose… and if I let that happen, worse would have been certain to follow. He stops again as a face in the crowd catches his attention. Undeterred, he nods and quickly gets back to the matter at hand. It was on Friday Night Overdrive that I faced Joey Dallas in the ring, one-on-one, and it all changed. After that night, I haven’t had to fall back down again. Sure, I take a step or two back every once in a while, but it’s been mostly an upward climb. He stops once more. He cannot convey a proper emotion for what he is about to say, nor can he feign one. He decides to press ahead. I owe a lot of that to Dallas. But… when he decided to join forces with Cannon… When that Vicious attack on D.C. took place… I knew what had happened. Dallas was truly gone. It was not some gimmick, a joke, something to say to make waves or headlines. Dallas was gone. One look at that man in that ring and I understood… that for him, there was no going back. What many people fail to realize is that all the events that surrounded that night… they all just add fuel to us. And that is not a good thing… for people like Cannon, and… He stops briefly. Not a pause, but more of a moment of hesitance. Joseph. A droplet of rain splats onto his left fist, which is clenching hard onto the left side of the podium. Sharpman. Devoid of any emotion, David Whitman stops. A couple of moments pass, as more and more droplets of rain plop onto the podium, the steps, the sidewalk. Almost as if he is waiting for anyone to leave the crowd, he looks at the people, seemingly one by one. Nobody moves. The familiar David is oddly absent, and everybody wants to understand all the intentions of the David who IS here. He rubs the flat top surface of the podium with his right hand; he stops dead in his tracks all of a sudden. He pulls his hand up to his face to see… A splinter. A sharp, sharp splinter. He gets an unclassifiable smile on his face as he watches the tiny crimson tear flow down his pointer finger, to his palm… He places his left thumb on his right wrist, stopping the blood drop in its tracks and pressing it hard for a moment. Shaking his head, he turns his attention back to the crowd. Refocused, he begins talking again. Joseph Sharpman. I will tell you what he is… though what I say about him doesn’t really matter. Because nobody can say something about someone, better than that person themselves… And everything Sharpman says about himself is true. The most important fact… to me… is that he did kill Dallas. Most of us have our future in our hands. And for some of us, the distinction between our past and future selves become so… sharply contrasting, that it feels like two completely different people, vying for control. I have never supported or condoned anything Dallas did. As a matter of fact, I could not stand him. And as I have said, I stand against everything he was. But the simple truth is, he DID save me. And I DO owe him. Sharpman is NOT Dallas. Anyone who still doubts that, take my word for it. And while they do share similarities… Somehow, it feels like Sharpman might be… wiser. Yes. I don’t know how, I don’t understand it. But I’ve seen him. The way he talks, his voice... his actions. He is a wise man. He is a smart man. But most of all… David stops. A sigh. He is a shadow of his former self. It took my all, but I defeated his former self. How much harder can a shadow be? But this is not about that at all. He stops talking again, and this time stands a bit straighter as the crowd unknowingly seems to mirror his actions at this point. On May 11th, it will have been 87 days since I was saved by Joey Dallas. On May 11th, it will have been 50 days since Joey Dallas died. And on May 11th… He pushes himself off of the podium, as if he is done. But he utters his final words to the crowd before leaving. I plan to avenge Dallas. Shoving himself off the podium, he does not waste any time as he heads right down the steps. The crowd disperses easily for him as he navigates through the murmurs and flashes. Within moments... He is gone.
