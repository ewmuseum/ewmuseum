Speechless

May 23 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
-Riddick is walking backstage in the venue as he makes his way past locker rooms, training rooms and many other doors as he is making his way to the filming room to film his promo for the MWE Tribulation Pay-Per View. With his rolling suitcase being dragged behind him making smooth wheel sounds which is the only sound heard as he makes his way down the hall. The memory of Riddick’s first win is still fresh in his mind as the visualisation of choking out his final opponent and his music being blared declaring his victory was something he’s dreamed of. Not of simply winning, but for once he can leave knowing nobody can criticize him, they were all there to witness a shining moment of Riddick’s glory. One thing you learn in life is that negative people are still human, not all of them are egotistical, they can admit they made mistakes, however it’s a process of choosing your path in life from then on. You can stay who you are or you can attempt to change, however it won’t happen overnight, at least at most times. Riddick makes his last left turn as he’s heading into the filming room, he meets up with the director and leaves his luggage at the door.- Director: Okay, we just need you to shoot a short promo for your match at Tribulation. Keep it short and sweet. -Riddick says nothing, what can he say? Sometimes it’s hard to say what we want unless the timing is perfect because it can only be said honestly and genuinely once. So he makes his way to the filming area and positions himself in front center of the camera view. - Riddick: There are plenty of unorthodox traditions, concepts that make sense of occurrences that we cannot explain. Concepts such as karma and luck are examples of things we tend to put blame on repercussions because we as people must have some sort of justification for every action and reaction. I step into the ring with four different debuting professional wrestlers. One by one they stand and one by one they fall. All goes according to plan, which means I’m happy. I am undefeated in Mayhem Wrestling Entertainment. So with that being said, before my match I claimed a promise to me, a promise to those watching on TV that I would become the most dominant force in pro wrestling. However it’s a lie I told to you, it’s a lie I told to myself. Last week wasn’t a wrestling match, it was a battle. The match itself consisted of me using next to no wrestling holds, dropping people on their heads and choking them out. I don’t keep promises; I am not one to always speak the truth. I lie; I do things that I could not repent to god. Things I’ve done that would shatter and part the dirt ground as I march my way down a one way fate of being hell bound. Call me crazy, but I do believe in karma, I believe in luck, I believe in second chances. Karma, what goes around comes around. So with that being said, does that mean everyone will get their revenge on me? No chance, I’ve lived my life always taking everybody else’s shit, what everyone would have to do to get anything I’d have to do twice as hard. If anything everyone who’s grown up with me will suffer the greatest of consequences for the way I was treated. Luck, the immortal tradition to explain why things happen the way it does and we have no explanation for it. Underdogs winning, the odds losing, and the inexplicable happening are all examples of luck. Luck, I do believe. It’s luck that all four participants in my last match are still alive. Luck is that I’m not yet champion when it is inevitable. Luck is what the Saintz Of Los Angeles will need when they walk into the squared circle against me. Who the hell do you posers think you are? You think just because you’ve smacked your head off the curb driving in California receiving brain damage and spending your allowance of 10$ a week on temporary tattoos that you believe you stand a chance against me? The sad fact is, I spent some of my valuable time looking for tapes to see why you two have a feeling that I will be a stepping stone to both of your lifelong dreams of finally getting needles to prevent your future cases of hepatitis. You two goofballs have done absolutely nothing in MWE! The reason I was put in a match against you two is because nobody wants you here in MWE. You’re taking up the payroll and I was chosen to take you two to execution. However before your execution occurs, I’d like to know one thing, what imposing threat do you intend on leaving on anyone including me? All I see is two surfboarding, pizza eating, dolphin riding, OC watching, punk Californians. You mock me and you mock my life. It’s people like you that I tried giving a second chance to during my life. I told myself people like you would once grow up and realize how idiotic you really are. However maturity isn’t an attribute you’re likely to attain. As if that wasn’t bad enough, you criticize me and my observer Peter K because we were never a tag team and claiming we cannot work as a cohesive unit. Well I believe you’re mistaken. MWE Tribulation Match = True Riddick & K vs. SoLA = True Tag Match = True I have to give a damn and tag Peter in = False It’s not a rule that I have to give a damn and tag that egotistical mouthpiece in. I don’t need him, I don’t need anybody for help to defeat you two pieces of shit or anybody for that matter. I never needed help my entire life, no one at my side, no one to catch me if I fell and I’ll be damned if I start to. Peter K, you think because you’re hanging out with rich people, living the high life that you’re more talented, more powerful than I? Well it’s sad to say that even my partner is pitiful, a disgrace and overall useless. So do yourself and everyone else a huge favour, when you walk into that ring. The bell rings and you stand outside in the corner. Please, learn and see how I dominate the so-called Saintz Of Los Angeles. Don’t even consider being tagged in because I don’t need you. And when I inevitably win the match and become 2-0 here in MWE. I’m going to take the path of dominance down to even greater things, destroying any and everyone in my way. So Saintz Of Los Angeles, you’ll have great pride for being known as my 2nd set of victims for my win here in MWE. After tonight the suffering will be over for you people. You can lay in the hospital beds knowing that you’ll never have to step into the ring again as I destroy every single being in my path. The end of ignorance is upon us, The prolonging of pain continues, The Uprising begins... -Riddick then instantly walks off the filming area as the director quickly speaks before Riddick exits the room.- Director: Great one Riddick, that’ll do just fine. Thanks for your time. -Riddick once again walks off without a word. Much like Riddick to only speak when he absolutely needs to. You have to realize somebody who lives his type of lifestyle doesn’t like speaking because words are what hurt them in the first place. Sometimes people like them don’t want to wake up. They don’t want to be seen because they know people will look at them negatively. Riddick walks past many doors as he with his rolling case in hand makes his way to the parking lot. He walks past a couple dozen cars trying to spot where he parked his. As he sees his car he walks over to it as somebody was waiting by his car the whole time by the driver’s door. Riddick walks over to his car trying to keep his eyes away from what appears to be a child, roughly 10 years old in appearance. However it’s no use as the kid begins to speak.- Kid: Are you Jay Riddick? Riddick: Yeah, now can you move out of the way? I need to get back to my hotel. Kid: But you don’t get it. Riddick: I get it alright, you want an autograph, a picture, you want to tell me how I’m a good fighter? How old are you? Shouldn’t you not be hanging around alone? Kid: That’s the point, I need to tell you something. Riddick: What are you talking about? Go home to your parents. Kid: But that’s the problem. Riddick: What’s your name? Where do you live? Kid: I live in Niagara Falls, my name is David Girard, I’m 10 years old. Riddick: Wait a minute!? Who’s your mother? Kid: Her name is Alexa, I know who you are... Riddick: What!? Wait a minute! Kid: You’re my father... Riddick: No! This can’t be! -A flashback plays in Riddick’s mind. It plays a timeline in his head. Alexa meets Jay in 12th Grade, asks him out to the prom. They hang out together, simply talking about life and it’s obstacles. They go to the prom. Riddick and Alexa dance a slow dance, only for Alexa to start crying and runs off. Riddick finds Alexa crying in a motel room. Alexa admits she asked Riddick out due to pity, she admits their love isn’t real. The flashback pauses as the kid speaks.- Kid: How come I’ve never seen you? Mom almost never mentions your name. Were you and mommy in love when I was born? -The flashback continues, Riddick gets mad and strikes Alexa out cold after false pity love is uncovered.- Kid: Why doesn’t mom ever talk about you? -The flashback shows that Alexa is raped in anger by Riddick. The flashback is done playing through Riddick’s mind, he suddenly opens his eyes drastically and realizes that this story of his life is not over.-
