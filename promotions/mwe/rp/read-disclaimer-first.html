Read Disclaimer First; A Grand Mystique RP

Feb 3 2009

The Grand Mystique

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
QUOTE **Disclaimer** I have included this disclaimer because in this RP against Prax’s character Windy Bridges, my character Grand Mystique (GM) has done something that may be considered pretty extreme that involved the Canadian flag. It is something that may be against the law over there however I have staff permission to do this and Prax is aware of it. If you feel that this is something that may offend you, do not worry about reading it. I hope you enjoy it. I enjoyed writing it. **End** The Grand Mystique didn’t want to hurt Windy at first. It was the memory of Collision Course and Starstruck that caused emotion to boil over. I remembered how it felt to have the MWE US title – a title that I was trying to bring some credibility to in EW – stolen by this upstart, who said he was the one who made this title mean something. I had never heard of him, until he invaded my company and insulted my title. That in my mind justifies what I did to Windy Bridges at Mayhem. But it is not over. Not yet. This is going to end the epic wars between the Grand Mystique and Windy Bridges that have dominated the wrestling world for the last five months. Non sanctioned at Friday Night Overdrive will be the conclusion. The Grand Mystique didn’t want to hurt Windy at first. That was a lie. * Koala: “What a way to end it though. Penultimate match at Friday Night Overdrive – non sanctioned; you must be excited.” Koala gulps down another mouthful of beer. GM: “I should be, even for me, it’s difficult to believe that son of a bitch had the gall to try and steal my limelight at Mayhem.” GM’s tone is solemn, almost regretful. Almost. Koala: “I know......only Windy would demand show time to announce something that the whole world had assumed.” GM gulps down the last of his fizzy water and gets up to go. GM: “Good luck in ICW this week.” He puts on his jacket. Koala gets up and shakes his hand. Koala: “Cheers for everything GM.” GM nods and takes his leave from Koala Typhoon and the bar, which had got a little smoky and crowded for GM’s liking. * When the Grand Mystique arrived back in his hotel room, his eyes felt heavy and his muscles were tense. He had a quick cleansing shower which helped relieve the cramped tiredness that ran up and down from his neck to his toes. As he lay down on the cheap, bumpy mattress, he tried to get some rest. As he did, Windy Bridges crept into his mind and blood began to boil. GM refused to try and sleep angry so refused to remember the negatives and recalled the positives; the one positive. “Windy Bridges turned me into the most feared and hated man in the MWE. He did it in EW and Rev Pro too.” GM could be grateful for that much at least. The Grand Mystique vs Windy Bridges was the most anticipated match at Friday Night OverDrive. That had a lot to do with what had happened in other companies. But it was going to be finished once and for all in the MWE. It felt right too. With that knowledge comfortably in his mind, GM drifted off into a peaceful slumber, snoring loudly and turning often. * He wakes with a start, sitting bolt upright, with perspiration on his forehead that glistens in the street light that drifts in through the thin net curtains. His chiselled yet imperfect features revealed for the first time to the shadows. He gets out of bed and walks to the bathroom. He washes water over his face, and then takes a gulp to drown the nauseous taste that lingered at the back of his throat. He spits it out and leans over the sink, allowing the drop to fall without prejudice into the basin. GM contemplates going to bed again, knowing he could do with the rest but he ignores his instincts. So when he emerges from the bathroom he turns right and sits in the uncomfortable chair that is placed besides the desk/dressing table that many cheap places like this have. He knows he has to relax and yet, at the same time, he doesn’t want to. He knew what awoke him and wasn’t ready to confront it again in his subconscious. GM leaned back. He rests his head against the poorly decorated plasterboard and shuts his eyes, to rest them for a second, as you do. But then, it came back. Emotion, stress, passion, frustration, an explosive concoction of feelings and thoughts all boiled over. GM’s eyes were wide open; angry, almost glowing in the dull light of the room. He randomly lashes out to the right and smashes an empty glass vase to thousands of pieces with his hand. He gets up, pacing across the room, frustrated and agitated, worse than a caged tiger, kept hostage at the zoo – the one that can remember the feeling of unrestricted freedom. Then he stops. Lifting up the palm of his right hand to the light, he rubs it. It feels wet and moist with a thick oil-like texture, shimmering like a dark puddle. Then the pain hits. Unimaginable, torturous; GM grabs his wrist in the vain hope that it stops but it is futile. The liquid agony courses up and down his arm from fingertips to elbow, setting his veins on fire; his muscles feel like they are on the verge of dropping off. A volcano poised to explode. He feels light headed; the room dances around him. Stumbling over the end of the bed, he heads to the far side of the room, certain that a light switch is somewhere by the door on that side, in the dark that seems more like a coffin with every time. He reaches desperately at the wall and gets nothing but thin air. Becoming increasingly dizzy and faint from the pain, his legs buckle under his movement and he collapses like a tower of cards. Then blinding light, like every star in the universe points directly at his corneas from a metre away. Eyes scrunch up so tight but to no effect. Blinded sight, blinding pain. And then just nothing. * My eyes open to a normal room, with normal light. Grateful relief floods my system for a second until the pain returns. It throbs.....but it is surprisingly bearable. At last, I can see straight. My left hand is still wrapped around my right wrist. But the wound is really severe, so deep that it feels like it goes through my palm. This should hurt a lot more than it does. As I poke it randomly, I am sure that I can feel a bone, embedded among sticky blood and stringy ligaments. But the pain, why doesn’t it hurt? This should hurt worse than anything. I drag myself to my feet and go to the bathroom. I place my cold and limp hand onto the basin and push the tap on full blast to try and clean it. But the blood has coagulated into a thick sludge. I grab a hand towel and try and scrub it away but......it doesn’t shift. Instead the flesh around the wound comes away ever so slightly. But as I scrub harder, the more flesh that is scraped away. But no pain. It should be intolerable, totally unmanageable but is dissipates down to a little less than nothing. I look at the wound closely. It is difficult to believe that it is even still attached as there is nothing but numbness. If I didn’t see it, with my fingers poking up at me and this wound gaping wide on my palm, then I would not believe it. I look up and see my face at the mirror. My eyes wide open; stare back at me with a vacant paleness. My skin is whiter than a cadaver and I feel like all the life has been drained from me. Then darkness. * GM awakes to find the lights in the room are on. Surprised and a little confused, GM moves to get up. His left arm feels a little stiff, almost like it has been bathed in a Plaster of Paris mixture. Still more than half asleep, he rubs and scratches at it innocuously. He thought it was just a dream; a manifestation of his daily physical pain. But then if that was the case, then why was his arm covered in crusted, dried blood? And why was there a large dark red patch on the side of the bedding? Still very pale but now more awake, he washes the crusty blood, checking every millimetre of his skin for a potential wound, only to find nothing of the sort. He comes out the bathroom and get dressed quickly, made nervous by the whole episode; he goes to pick up his jacket and notices the vase still in one piece. Then he saw it. The mirror on the far side of the room had a message scribed in untidy writing. GM recognised instantly that it was written in blood. The way it had set and forming hard lines made it eerily haunting even though the message was clear enough that GM had played a part in its’ creation. The events of the last few hours had caused an unnecessary distraction. GM packed his things, intending to get out quickly. While he agreed with the intent of what he had seen, GM didn’t like what had happened in the build up to it; the intensity of his dream, the reality of the nightmare, the very real outcome and the state of mind that would lead to him writing this message. It was a disturbing and haunting development yet GM knew that only he could be the one to end them. He had control over that situation. But in his sleep, the same sequences had been repeating with increasing regularity in the past month before the Friday Night OverDrive event, but never with these consequences. GM zipped up his travel bag and left the bloodstained room quickly. He leaves the room without looking back, hoping that he could kill off the root cause soon enough and put the whole episode behind him. All he could do at the moment was leave. Staying in this environment was doing him no good. The camera doesn’t follow GM. It stays behind and turns towards the mirror. The root of GM’s shock and dismay. The message is short, clear and terrifying. GM ends WB at FNOD * Despite the shock, GM felt OK as he headed to the place that he to be at before he headed to New York for Friday Night OverDrive. He had a promo to finalize and was stubbornly focussing on that. It had to suitably emotional and hard hitting – the single best piece of his career. The match meant so much in so many ways that GM had to match his high expectations and then surpass them a thousand times over. He felt sure it could happen. But he was certain of one thing. The Smitty Johnson programme that detailed the feud between Windy Bridges and the Grand Mystique had broadcasted early in the night before. It had revived a lot of uncomfortable old issues and feelings that GM had managed to bury. They were now alive, fresh and hurt so much on their revival that GM knew that a supply of emotion would not be hard to come by. GM got out of his red rental Ford KA and looked up at the old building in front of him. It is an old church. It looks at least a couple of hundred years old with really faded brickwork, with crumbling gravestones and disintegrating tombs that were just as old. The poor upkeep of the area only lent to the haunting presence. The setting was perfect. GM took in the atmosphere with a deep breath and felt immersed in history. He stepped up to the thick oak door, pressing his palm to it, to feel the weather-beaten dents and cracks that covered the surface. He turned around to face the camera head on. He looks as if he was going to speak but hesitates, almost as if nerves get the better of him, for just a couple of seconds. “After God knows how long, words almost fail me.” As he begins to speak, it is with the tiniest quiver in his lip. “But even God wouldn’t have thought of this. From EW to Rev Pro to the MWE, this has been the biggest story and it is coming to an end, the final chapter. More than six months in the making, the non sanctioned match at OverDrive will finish the Grand Mystique / Windy Bridges saga.” “This match sells the event – this is the match that people would pay to see and tune in to watch; the real main event. They – the viewing public, the whole fucking universe – wants to see the Grand Mystique kick your gurney riding chops out of the company, out of the business for good. Finally, it is going to happen and I couldn’t be any happier.” He doesn’t look any happier though he is noticeably focused as a firm grimace is visible. “Despite the blood, the sweat and the tear that I have shed for you, for us, nothing has changed. What began as a war of respect will end as a crusade of an epic nature under the same banner. And I still don’t respect you Windy.” GM looks down at the floor. He shakes his head and then looks up. “How can I when all you have done recently is put your feet up and relax, be an actor and live off Windy O’s when I have been around the world, doing what I do best for the fans that you abandoned out of pure laziness.” GM turns and uses his shoulder to heave the thick door open. He steps in and allows the door to slam shut with a dull thud after the camera follows him. The camera moves past GM and begins to take in the building. Despite the age on the exterior, inside it is laid like a modern church. Clean white walls, newly-varnished wooden pews, a raised parapet in the corner with tapestries, ornaments and other regular religious paraphernalia that line the walls and shelves. Higher up on all four walls are stained glass windows which let in a lot of natural light. At the front is the reason GM chose to come here. Hung on the far wall with a lazy droop in the middle, is the Canadian flag, the Maple Leaf. The iconic symbol of a proud nation. GM steps in front of the camera but doesn’t face it. Instead his position allows an over the shoulder perspective as GM stares up at the flag. His silence speaks volumes. GM is unable to feel anything but disdain for the flag as its’ very image is a clear reminder of Windy Bridges – a symbol of him, his presence and what has happened before now. GM moves up to the table and turns around to face the camera which begins to close in slightly now on his face. “Mr. Canada: Windy Bridges……So haven’t I killed you already? *GM allows himself to chuckle at the thought* Oh well, I’m here for your funeral though. I’m wearing my Sunday best and everything!” The camera pulls back to show GM wearing his newest MWEshop.com t-shirt and a pair of ripped black jeans as well as his blue mask. “I’ve threatened to do it for the longest time but at Friday Night Overdrive, I will finally end the sorry career of Winston Montgomery Bridges III.” GM moves again. This time he goes to the raised parapet to his left. The camera stays in place as GM prepares himself. Coughing quietly into his hand, he stands tall and proper, like a vicar or priest may address a congregation. “The world famous spirit of Windy Bridges is finally going to get smashed at my hands. And this time I know that you may never die but you will feel like you want to give up and leave sickly sweet baby face act behind.” GM puts his right palm across his chest. “Because I don’t care how beloved you are in Canada, America or Timbuk-fucking-tu – the act got stale a year ago.” GM places both hands back on the sides of the stand. ”And now, the Grand Mystique is everything that you want to be. I can go toe to toe with the best that this company has to offer. But finally, it’s come back to you.” “The unresolved business – the wrongs of 2008 – is going to be corrected in 2009.” GM looks up at the flag. “Wait....something is wrong.” He shakes his head as he steps down the podium to underneath the Maple Leaf. He reaches up and pulls the flag down with ease. “I’ve just realised something.” He turns the flag upside down and then flips it to show the camera. “I think the Maple Leaf was put on the flag by someone with a phallus complex. I always figured Canadians were dicks!” He chuckles briefly before it turns into an angry grimace “Shit, what a crap joke. This isn’t a Rated X audition!” GM dumps the flag on the table unceremoniously. He turns away from the camera and puts his hands up to his temples, grabbing angrily on the scruff of the mask. GM turns around. Suddenly the nerves have been replaced by an emphatic stare of intensity and determination. It is fair to surmise that a more familiar look has returned to GM. “Fuck you Windy Bridges! I’ve had enough of you, of this. You chase me from company to company, desperate to prove your apparent superiority over me one more time. But 2009 belongs to me and finally the purpose, the destiny that you were supposed to serve becomes reality – the Grand Mystique is elevated at your expense – on way to becoming the MWE Main Eventer that I know I am ready to be!” GM goes back to the parapet. “There was a clear difference between us in EW. Personality, experience, a young champion and a jealous veteran. But now, I’m better. I have evolved and matured in a veteran in my own right, someone who doesn’t need your retired, beaten up old face to help him anymore. I am what you could never be – a threat to everyone on the roster – champion or otherwise. I have become a more well-rounded character than I ever was in EW. I should be the one next in to face the Undisputed Champion but instead I have to deal with your overpaid, ego inflated ass that I have nearly crippled three times already” GM scratches his wrist. He pauses and sees no blood. He goes on. ”You made a grave mistake coming to Mayhem. To announce your retirement of all things; the one message that you could have put in the obituary section of the local rag because it would have made a bigger impact! Most of the world had assumed you were dead or dying anyway. Being chucked off a balcony will do that to you. “ “But instead of retiring your shit quietly, you had to do it on my show – in public – and that makes me sick. Windy Bridges is at last exposed as an attention whore and that fact alone can justify what I did at Mayhem!” “You see, all my achievements seemed to be overshadowed by you. I should be known as a great Internet champ from my time in EW, but the stupid fans will only remember me as the guy who beat you up in the worst way” GM lifts up his fingers, holding two to the screen.”Twice, but couldn’t get my hand raised over the US title.” “We damn near destroyed each other’s careers in those two matches but that is all I am known for – being a loser and I now refuse to take it any longer.” GM rubs the back of his neck, which still hurts a little from the effects of the Whitman match. “Even the win in Rev Pro wasn’t good enough, Maxx nearly cost me the match by not tagging me in and then that cocky son-of-a-bitch DC erased any memory of a memorable win by chucking you of a balcony. He practically ended your career, your chances of making it any more in a big company and he nearly ended any chance of this match happening.” “But” GM beckons the camera closer. He now fills the screen with his presence. He leans forward and speaks in a slightly hushed tone. “I’m really glad he didn’t.” GM stands back up again and points at the camera. “Because that is my fucking job!” GM is visibly seething. His eyes catch the light. His intensity and naturally aggressive nature is coming through clear as day. The reincarnation of the feud with Bridges had dragged up a lot of old feeling and memories back to the surface. Old scars were ripped open, left stinging and freshly painful. “I tried to get it done in EW and in Rev Pro. Both times, fortune favoured you and it haunts me even now. I wasn’t good enough.” GM’s tone is anger tinged with regret. “But that is not a problem anymore......In recent weeks I have taken Antonio DeMarco and David Whitman to their limits. These aren’t APWC rejects like Xavier Stone or a UWA pleb like Derek Wellings. Those two aren’t good enough to jerk the curtain for me now.” “DeMarco and Whitman are legends of this industry and I gave them their biggest tests. I was this close” GM pinches his finger to show a small gap “to making Whitman tag in the middle of the ring last week. I am a significantly more powerful entity in the MWE than I was anywhere else.” “So, am I afraid of facing the real Windy Bridges?” GM pauses and pensively strokes his chin under the edge of his mask. “Hell no – if it takes dropping you on your head to get you to bring your best then I was wasting my time in EW wasn’t I?” “Windy, you are the definition of useless hot air. You can threaten all you want but you will not deliver! But don’t believe that I will underestimate you again, no, no, no,” GM wags his index finger from side to side, disparaging the very notion of the idea. “I know you will bring it as much as your body allows and I know that even in your state, with your forehead split open like a melon, that you could beat pretty much anyone. But I’m not just anyone Bridges, we both know that.” “It may have taken six months but I am prepared for you Windy and if I have to go further than ever before to put you away and end that torturous record that I have against you then I will, by any means necessary.” GM pauses, to allow those words to sink in but also to consider his next words. “I won’t hesitate to throw you off the stage or crush you again. And I’m not going to think twice about giving you more scars on that f-ugly little head of yours. That forehead alone has a giant bull’s eye on it!” “At my hands you will bleed; we will hurt so much that nobody can quite understand how much it will affect us, for the rest of our careers. This will change everything.” Those last four words come out slow, deliberate and clear. GM steps down from the parapet and makes his way to the table that is placed in the middle of the room. The flag has been dumped in a heap in the middle of it. GM looks back at it before looking up to the camera again. “Putting you though hell on the 4th July didn’t work. Neither did crushing you and your truck! But I am not going to let the past haunt me. I was almost out of ideas but this non sanctioned match, the first in MWE history, gives me plenty of chances to get revenge and secure the victory that I deserve.” “I will not allow your victories to haunt me any longer. After Overdrive, the star that burns brightest will be mine.” As he utters the word “burn”, the entire room lights up. Candles dance in the windows, the chairs and even on the table. They bathe GM in a warm, yellow light that swallows the entire area. He walks around to the other side of the table which has a single gold candlestick on it. “And everyone burns before me!” He picks up the candlestick and lets a drop of the red wax candle to drip a solitary drop on to the floor. “The first victim, casualty, martyr – call him what you wish – but the first of many in 2009 is you Windy Bridges.” GM eyes are encapsulated by the side to side motion of the flame but he continues to speak regardless. “You came into MY world, you interfered with my destiny and you brought everything upon yourself. The match at Friday Night Overdrive is the end, a natural conclusion. No run-ins, no interference – two men and there will be one winner. And just like the start of this story, it will end as I stand over your bloody, broken shell of a body!” GM pauses briefly to consider everything that he has said and takes a deep breath before he continues. “And Richard Covell is the one that you can thank for it being non-sanctioned. He is terrified of what I am capable of. He doesn’t want it to happen, the MWE do not want it to happen. But it will happen to a finish, one, two, three.....nothing less will satisfy. You could be unconscious and it will carry. It would continue.....*GM uses his finger to play with the flame* even if you were on fire.” GM picks up the flag and lifts the candlestick up to it. He allows it to lick the material until it bites and take hold of the material. It burns quickly and he allows the flaming carcass of the Maple Leaf to be held in full view of the camera. The smoke billows but GM seems unperturbed. His eyes reflect only the flickering flame; no emotion, not even a cold hearted glimmer of regret. But as GM looks at what he has done, without a drop of regretful sentiment, he considers the reaction of his move. For all that GM had done to Windy, from the beer bottle to the face in England to the way he ruined Windy’s retirement announcement two shows ago, GM felt that he could justify that this was just another logical step. By now the flames had engulfed most of the flag. GM dropped it and with a barely audible rustle, the charred remains hit the floor. He stands over it, like he imagined a trucker might regard a fresh piece of roadkill. GM kneels down. He pushes some burnt strands out of the way, the charred black material that used to form the Canadian flag. What he finds is a perfectly intact crystal ball, sitting on the dead carcass of a dead image, the lifeless symbol of a nation that will never feel a fresh new gust of hope and optimism again. A pang of something that can only be described as humanity flashes across his face. It doesn’t truly befit him. His eyes betray the true reflections of his thoughts as he recalls what has happened. The camera draws a little closer and reveals more detail – the stress and the strains of being the man, the one to carry all the anger and intensity, the burden of dragging these memories around with him, like a lame horse pulling a cart; it was taking its toll. Yet the relief that washed over him just for knowing that there was a chance that it could all be over. It gave him energy, a buzz that had abandoned him all too often during his time in the UWA or was non-existent in WZCW, where GM lacked a rivalry that even approached what he had with Windy Bridges. GM picks up the crystal ball. He uses his sleeve to rub small black Maple leaf dust of its’ circular perfection. He looks up at the camera, solemn yet unwavering in his single-minded drive to what he feels is his date with destiny. “So from Birmingham’s NEC Arena to the Brawl at the Mall Tag Team Match in Rev Pro. From a United States Title match in EW to Madison Square Garden for Friday Night Overdrive, after the bloodshed and the pain – it will end with a match not sanctioned by the MWE. A match that is happening because we agreed that it HAS to happen. And it will happen.” GM readjusts his mask slightly. “Never have I been so focussed on achieving something. Bridges, we have driven each other to the point of insanity but I couldn’t be more excited” GM chuckles, it sounds almost a little perverted, given what he is talking about. “I know what is going to happen, how this all ends and what I am going to achieve." *GM grabs the camera and brings it closer to him, so his face fills the screen* "The Grand Mystique will stand tall over you, with my hands raised and finally I will move on to bigger and better things.” ”This match will be the one to decide who can hold their head high, the one who will after the bruises fade and the muscles heal can write the history books that they had their hand raised after the first non sanctioned match in MWE history.” He pauses, ever so briefly. ”And after everything, when the dust falls over the annals of time and history, Friday Night Overdrive will be known for the demise of Windy Bridges at the hand of the Grand Mystique and the impending Big Bang.” GM shoves the camera down and quickly walks past and out the building. The camera is left looking at the burnt remains of the Canadian flag as the scene ends.
