Old Attitude?

Apr 11 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
*Mason Resoan is walking about his house, really pointlessly, not thinking about much. In fact, you can see in his face that he doesn't even know why he is awake right now. It is around noon and he is fully dressed...with nothing to do. No promotion to go to. No fan meet and greet to attend. No autograph signings to particpate in and house shows had been canceled for the week. Mason then just stopped short, sat on the couch, and put his hands on his face, looking like a bored 8th grader while a science teacher rambled on about something unimportant. He couldn't stand having nothing to do, but all he could do is sit in house. Sapphire was out driving, doing god knows what, and Mason simply sat there looking at a wall. Then, it clicked. He had something to think about. Not something he wanted to think about, but something that had been lurking in the back of his head for the last two weeks. His loss to David Whitman.* Mason: David Whitman is a man known for taking people to their limits, stretching their athletic abilities farther than they can imagine, and showing people that wrestling isn't easy. And he did not do that for me, what so ever. In fact, my match against him was mediocre at best, not living up to the true expectations that I had made before the match. Not only that, but I walked in very confident in my abilites. So, how did I lose? *Mason sighs, switches hands, and views the entire room a few times with a small motion of his neck.* Mason: I watched that match over and over, as I do every match to maximize what I can do in my next match. To see where I went wrong...or what I did right. Well, I didn't do anything wrong on Sunday...or at least not in my mind. I kept a steady pace, inflicted some pain into David, and caused him to try. I had him in the perfect position...about to end the match in good fashion, and out of no where, he comes out of no where with his Burning Hammer move. Needless to say, I was not suspecting that move coming, and he caught me off guard. He only, once in the whole match, used those abilities that are revered once. And that was to finish me off. *Mason takes his hands off his face and his elbows off the arm of his chair. He slumps back a little, rubs the white leather, getting somewhat comfortable, yet stressed at the same time. He took a small breath and checked the time on his watch. Five to noon, and he still was doing nothing but talk about his second loss in nu-Mayhem Wrestling Entertainment.* Mason: Maybe it was intelligent of Mr. Whitman to draw me in, thinking I had him beat, and then kick in the nitro. I won't deny that it was clever and very crafty, but it proved to me that if we were ever to meet again, he would not be able to use those tricks on me again. And without tricks, am I beatable? *Mason stands up, feeling a little more stressed than comfortable now. Agrivated almost, Mason begins to sway in place, standing right above the chair.* Mason: I mean, look at all the people I have beaten. All these supposed "Everyone Beaters." Windy Bridges, Brian Michaels, Joey Dal..excuse me, Joesph Sharpman, Sinc Mercier, and Paige among others. All these people did not stand a chance against me, and every single I beat cleanly. Every single person fell to me, because they didn't have any advantages. They had no help, no foreign objects, and no tricky cheap shots. Sinc Mercier beat me once, yes, but after two previous and failed attempts. No one else beat me though, and it goes to show that unless there is some trick involved, I can not be beaten. Whitman, if you had not kicked in your adrenaline in the end like that, after not using it at all in the match, there is no doubt in my mind that you would have lost...and that is a fact. *Mason has been talking with his hands a little throughout this little rant. He realizes it and stops, as well, he leaves the living room, feeling as though it was closing in on him. With a little stride in his step, Mason went over to his large stair case, walked two steps up, spun and had a seat. Without even thinking he placed his right hand on the rail and his other on the wall, making a barricade of sorts without intention. An odd position, yet Mason was starting to feel a little less stressed, getting this heavy loss of his chest.* Mason: David Whitman, I respect you for the fact that your resume is almost perfect. I mean, who else has more MWE Championship wins? Who else can beat D.C for the Undisputed Championship, as well as Alejandro Rodriguez? Not many people...not many at all. However, despite all those accomplishments, I can not respect your in-ring talent until I see it myself. Sunday, you didn't try your hardest. Instead, you resorted in tricks and a small bit of intelligence to beat me. I was excited to face you for the first time and you let me down. Next time we face, it will be perfect...and we will have a match actually WORTH remembering. Not some mediocre joke like High Way to Hell was. *Mason sighs again, wishing to get back in the ring as soon as possible, but instead, gets a small beating sound in the back pocket of his pants. Someone was calling him, and as he grabbed the vibrating phone, he looked to see that it was his girlfriend calling him. He picked up, hopefully expecting good news, but was disappointed.* Mason: Hey baby, where are you? Sapphire: I am at the f**ing police station. This prick of a cop pulled me over for something I didn't do and when I tried to leave, he arrested me. Mason: What the f**k! I'll be right down. *Mason clicked his phone to silence, placed it on alerts only, and trotted up stairs. He grabbed a pair of keys off a beside table in his giant bedroom and slowly jogged to his gargage where he started up his favorite 67' GT Black Camaro. With a screech of his tires, Mason flew off, riding towards his local police station. Mason looked around his section of Toronto as he accelerated to speeds of almost 70 miles per hour. A cop sitting on the side of the road saw him fly by at this ridiculous speed, put on his sirens, and Mason simply drove faster. They were going to the same place either way. Mason figured that it couldn't get worse if the cop did do something to him at the police station, and turned on his Sirus radio. Whiplash by Metallica came on, a fast paced song, and Mason laughed as he drove faster. At speeds of nearly 85 miles per hour, Mason was exhilirated, turning his boring day into one of extreme fun and excitement. In less than no time, Mason was already at the police station. In Ace Ventura like fashion, he performed a tailspin into a parking spot. Liking his driving, he got out of the car, and began to walk towards the buliding. The cop arrived shortly after, did a sloppy parking job, and hopped out of his car, confused, but screamed stop anyway. Mason then laughed even harder as he swung open the double doors to the police station.* *The police man charges after Mason, barreling through the doors as he watches Mason turn a corner. Mason reaches a small reception desk, checks a book on the table, and locates where his girlfriend is. He takes a right down a hallway, followed by a left and then finally into the room in which his girlfriend was being held. The policemen, fortunately, lost track of where Mason had gone. Mason looked at Sapphire as she stood up and ran to Mason as two policemen stood up and apprehended her. Mason took the defensive, grabbed Sapphire by the shoulders, and moved her behind him. The policemen, who were of smaller stature than Mason, stopped.* Mason: Who the hell do you think you are trying to put your hands on my girlfriend like that! Cop #1: Sir, can you please step aside so Miss Garvin can take her seat. Mason: See, I could, if you answered my question... Cop #2: SIR...can you please step back. *Mason chuckled and shook his head back and forth, looking is disgust. He eyed the second cop as Sapphire slowly began to gain her confidence back. She leaned over his shoulder, looking dead in the second cop's face.* Sapphire: Yeah, shut the f**k up! *The second cop lunged forward towards Sapphire and as the first cop tried to restrain his partner, it was to late. Mason immediately went into defense mode, pushed Sapphire back, and kneed the cop in the gut. The gut of the cop sucked in and as he lost his breath, Mason pushed him to a waiting chair.* Mason: Listen...do you know who I am or what I do. I am Mason Resoan, former triple crown at Mayhem Wrestling Entertainment...I am the definition of perfect and what you tried to pull just now was far from perfect...in fact, it was the opposite. I'll give you the bill for whatever the f**k Sapphire did, but if you ever try to put your hands or me or my girlfriend again...I am going to have my lawers all over your a** before you can even react! *The second cop just kept holding his gut as Mason stopped yelling at him. He then turned to the other cop who was in disgust of his partner's failure to contain himself.* Mason: What did she do anyway? Cop #1: This young lady violated several traffic laws...not enough to put her in jail or anything, but it is a hefty fine. Most likely around 2,000 dollars or so. *Mason spun to look at Sapphire...and then the cop...and the back to his girlfriend again.* Mason: That's it...I could have just got put in jail for assualting a cop...over traffic violations...what the f**k! Here, take the money, Sapphire, we are gone! *Mason takes a wad of bills out of his wallet and throws it at the second cop, who grunts when it comes in contact with him. Mason grabs his girlfriend gently by the hand and begins to walk out of the police station. They get to the car, Mason opens the door for her, and lets her inside first. He swings around and begins to drive west. The car is silent for a bit, with Sapphire simply looking out the window, almost zoning out. Mason, however, breaks the silence.* Mason: Why didn't you just pay them...why did I have to come down? *Sapphire takes a breath and snaps back into full reality, looking at Mason after she shifted in her chair. Mason couldn't look at her, but he knew she was a giving a "What the f**k does it matter to you face" right now. Mason didn't break direction or confidence though, eventually breaking Sapphire's attempt to ignore the question.* Sapphire: Because I didn't break a law...I didn't do anything wrong. *Sapphire tried to mask the lie in her teenager innocence style voice. Mason sees right through this and takes his eyes off the road to look straight into her eyes. She gets a little neverous, slumps into her seat, and looks straight out of the windshield.* Sapphire: Why should I have to pay for anything! *Mason stops at a red light, shifts to the left, and looks dead in the eyes of Sapphire, completely disgusted.* Mason: Are you joking me! Why do I have to pay...why do I have to wrestle? Why don't I just get a world title? I'll tell you why, because you have to work sometimes. *Mason looks over again, right before the light turns green and sees that Sapphire is not very happy, even agitated that she is getting yelled at.* Sapphire: You know...I am not twelve. Mason: Yeah, you might now be twelve, but you certainly are acting like one. I am waiting for you to stick you fingers in your ears and start going la-la-la-la-la! Honestly, no one likes getting yelled at, and you are only scolded by me, but you need it. It is all for your benefit. You need to be perfect...like me. *The rest of the car ride was very silent, without much chater.* ...ABOUT AN HOUR LATER... *Mason Resoan had been driving for nearly fifty minutes since he left the police station, and since then Sapphire Garvin had resumed her position looking out the window aimlessly. Mason swung around a corner, kicked the speed up a little bit, and in a little less than five minutes, all the driving was solved. It was now almost 2 P.M now, and the sun was shining extremely bright. Eagar to release her energy, Sapphire spots something in the distance and immediately snaps into attention.* Sapphire: Take a left...it is right over there. *Mason smiled as he spotted a huge building in the western part of Toronto. It had to be eight stories tall and it was a plain white building with a gigantic sign. It was red in big letters and read: He whips around some traffic, makes it into the parking lot, and parks the car. He steps outside, takes in the fresh air, and walks around his own car. Sapphire Garvin had zoned out to the point where she didn't know that she was stopped. Her face was agains the glass and Mason laughed as he tapped the glass. Startled, she flew back in her seat and looked to Mason, who simply chuckled as she opened the door in anger. They began to walk inside of the dojo of sorts and Mason looked around as he saw some Mixed Martial Arts training was happening. Mason had reserved a room for the two of them in this place, a week before, so he could get some training in the last two days before his match against Ace Cannon. He took Sapphire by the hand to the bottom of a stairwell and told her to go up to room 143. She did as she was told and soon, Mason was left by himself, on the stairwell, with nothing but the faint sound of training in the background.* Mason: You know...on the way to this Mixed Martial Arts training facility...I thought a lot about dicipline...and how much of it I would need. I thought of my girlfriend and how she needs disicipline...and how one of the keys to being as perfect as I am, is knowing my limits and where they go. I know how far I will go before I am taking out of my perfect state...and I know that those limits are extremely extremely hard to get to. However, I do not need to know about myself...but rather about my opponent's limits. I need to know what I have to do to beat them and I need to know how to get them to their strongest point. For David Whitman...I did not know that I had to know this information. *Mason places a single hand on the side of the stairwell, rubbing the black metal back and forth as he contemeplates what he just said.* Mason: My match with Ace Cannon has been hyped up, by both of us, as neither wants to lose. I need to redeem a loss that should not have occured and Ace Cannon wants to prove that he is the best. I know that much about Ace Cannon...but how far will Ace Cannon go against me...just to get a win? *Mason begins to think a bit harder as he takes a seat half-way up the gritty metal steps. He beige cargo pants dangling through some of the slips between the stairs.* Mason: Well...he kicked a child in the head. But I have no child. *Mason thinks a little harder, placing his palm flat out on his knee, looking at the center, as if it will help him remember.* Mason: Well, He tried to kill a baby...but I have no baby. *Mason keeps thinking, leaving his mind no room to rest, trying to think of the little history that he has seen from Ace Cannon.* Mason: He has tried to punt people in the skull...over the smallest thing and he will attack close ones to try and get inside your head. *Mason then thinks to Sapphire Garvin, who very well might be at Mason's match on Monday.* Mason: Sapphire Garvin is a woman I care very much about...and it seems as though Ace Cannon does not care about anyone but himself. He is a lone wolf, per say...yet he teams with Joesph Sharpman and dates Paige. How does that work? It doesn't...because a man as "ruthless" as him wouldn't have enough care for anyone to form a relationship, buisness or not, with anyone. So, Cannon, how does that work. How are you supposed to be revered as a man who cares about no one, when clearly, that is not true? *Mason stands up, walking a few more steps up to the very first square platform in the stairwell. He leans against a wall and looks around the white white walls.* Mason: Ace Cannon...there is a reason for everything...and I mean EVERYTHING I do. I keep myself concealed for majority of two weeks. I hold out from press and people in general, and that shuts you out as well. No ammo...nothing to use against me. Nothing to help you with your attack against me or my mind. On the other hand, you leave yourself to mind games at all times. You let yourself in the public eye...like an open book...so easy to read...so easy to understand...and so easy to study. You like to play mind games...you like to get people worried about their saftey, and their friend's safety. You would like for me to care about Sapphire to the point that I will forget about you...and frankly, it won't work. Playing mind games with someone who revolves around sneaking under the skin of others is not a very smart tactic to go about. Smarten up. *Mason gives a small chuckle, cracks his neck, and walks up the second flight of stairs, about halfway, and then sits down again, ever so intently focusing on a new topic in his head.* Mason: Cannon, I don't like you...for a multitude of reasons. For one, you are cocky when you shouldn't...wait, not when you shouldn't, but when you haven't earned the privelage to be cocky. You think you are tough or something. You think you are bada**, but in all reality, your still another newcomer in MWE trying to make his name in this company. Teaming with Joesph Sharpman may have seemed like a good idea, but it doesn't give you the right to prance about like you're a veteran. Hell...I'm not even a veteran, but I have been here a hell of a lot longer than you have. *Mason takes another breath, fixing his cargo pants before the get ripped. His glasses rest on top of his head, ever so gently, but he fixes them so they don't fall off. Mason just shakes his head.* Mason: You know, another reason I don't like you would probably be your stupidity. Now, on the outside looking in, people believe that you are meticulous and methodically, very percise, never to leave a trace of something you do wrong. However, being a fellow competitor, I have come to see how sloppy...and moreover, how stupid you really are. You don't boss around D.C's girlfriend like I see you do in public appearences on Mayhem. She is always in the back of the group, very quiet...and you know, it is not going to fly. Eventually D.C, or Lindsey herself will get you back, one way or another. Not only that, but you really need to make sure that when you take someone out, you do it right next time. You are just like your brother, you know. So sloppy when you go for a goal. Michaels tried to take D.C out too, you know, and look at what happened. He didn't do it when he had the chance a D.C came back and beat the living hell out of him, knocking him back into his place. You tried to take out D.C with Joesph Sharpman, a man who tried to take me out, and see how that worked. What next...you going to get Sinclair Mercier to help you. You want to be bada** and take people out...make a name for yourself...do it right next time you stupid f**king pathetic fool! *Mason's expression has turned from that of angry and disgust into near hatred, really expressing the passion in his tone and choice of words. His face is red with anger and he has nothing but a nasty snarl on his face.* Mason: And while were at it...I can't stand the way you walk around acting like you are the best athlete in our company. What have you done that is so special, huh? You beat David Whitman once, in a fluke win. I came in kicked the guy in the balls and you pinned him. You want to take credit for that. If it wasn't for me, you wouldn't have the arguably biggest win in your ever so mediocre career. I can't stand the fact that you are rated so high, when in fact, you lost to Sinc Mercier and Joesph Sharpman twice, two men that I beat on my first try. You aren't a great athlete or a great wrestler...your a fluke and probably the most overrared Wrestler of our company in 2008. Yet, you beat David Whitman once, with assisted help, and you get your d**k sucked by all the writers in the back! You are only successful in a world of hypocrites, liars, and retards...because you can outsmart people with IQ's lower than their age! Maybe that is why you avoided the old MWE, because you knew that people would see right through your bulls**t! *Mason flies to his feet shaking his head as he jogs up several steps to the second platform. He tries to cool off by leaning against a wall, but it doesn't work as he continues his rant.* Mason: And while I am at it...I hate you for the fact that Vanguard is being looked at as though it is going to be the most devestating force in Mayhem Wrestling Entertainment's history. That is laughable, considering back when I was with Aiden Copane, we could have each single handedly beat your entire group without thinking twice. And as for Joesph Sharpman...how long is it before he turns his back on you, as he did Sinclair Mercier? How long before he is tired of hiding behind you...before he wants and MWE Championship oppertunity...before he wants to have the women and the glory...how long? *Mason looks at his hand and begins counting on his fingers, before he stops suddenly and answers his own question.* Mason: It is not going to be very long...I can tell you that much. *Mason takes a small break from his rant and pushes himself off the wall like ring ropes. He begins to pace back and forth, thinking hard about his opponent for this next coming Monday. He jogs straight up the stairs to the next platform, not stopping on the middle stair this time.* Mason: And you want to something else. I hate Joesph and you for another reason, something you probably didn't think about before you started b**ching to your dad. You started off, both of you, in the main event. You see, my career was much different. I came into MWE at the bottom of the ladder...or in this case, the staircase. *Mason looks at the floor and smiles as he is proud of his ability to pay attention.* Mason: In a few short weeks, after beating others in my attempt to rise in the ranks, I formed Canadian eXchange with Aiden Copane. We became the first ever MWE Tag Team Champions and together we made history. Not once did I stop from that point...I kept going...kept getting better...and you know what, eventually I made it to step number two, Singles Competion! I turned on Aiden Copane and took him out of action for over a month. I got him out of the way, and the next thing I knew I was in title contention. I kept destroying people and started to get recognized. Eventually...I was at a crossroads! *Mason made an "X" with his arms to signify what he was talking about and he kept on telling his MWE career.* Mason: Now, here is what is different about all the other times that I tell my MWE career to someone. I could have won the MWE X-Television champion...could have beat D.C and Greg Cherry in a triple threat...because I am just that perfect...or, I could have gone higher than even D.C had gone at that point. The main event. It took over five months to get my first MWE World Heavyweight Championship, and you know what...I earned it. I worked my a** off to become champion, and I held it for near two months. I made it past everyone in my way to get my shot...you and Sharpman came in and were given the main event to you on a silver platter...and for what. While I had to prove MWE writers and staff wrong...once again. So, before you b**ch and complain anymore, just think about that! *Mason took a long break, jogged up a few more flights of stairs, stopping three platforms up. He then began to sway in place, passion still in his voice and eyes.* Mason: You know the saying, if at first you don't succeed, try again. Well, I am going to do that, without a doubt. And my new mindframe is like my mindframe of old...if you get in my way, I am going to shut you, down, take you out, and ship you back to wherever the f**k you came from. I am perfect, yes, but the old Mason Resoan is back and better than every before. I took people down before my title shot, but it wasn't nearly how it should have been. It should have been quicker...it should have been more perfect...it should have hurt my opponents more. It has been over a year and a half since I have had this mentality, and now that it is back, you get to be my guinea pig. Ace Cannon, you want to play mind games, well bring them on. I WILL SHUT YOU DOWN! *Mason laughs as he jogs up a few more flights of stairs. Without any thought, he keeps on his roll, even more passionate then he had been.* Mason: Ace Cannon, we have been on the same side, but never against either other, so Monday will be fun. You should know that I don't joke when I say that I am the closest thing to wrestling perfection. Sharpman should know all about the devine, and if he knows, then you should to. Well, throw your bibles and religious papers away, because this Monday, I am not your savior, but I will shit on your worthless wrestling talent from such a height, you might as well have thought that god himself shit on it. I piss gloriness and shit excellence, because you won't beat me come this Monday. Ace Cannon...that was perfect...you are not! *Mason jogs up the last couple of platforms before he stops at a door, most like to the hallway in which his room lies. There is a small room, but Mason's head is blocking it. Mason opens the door, slightly, still blocking view from the inside.* Mason: And if you didn't know...now ya know! *And as Mason swings the door open, we see a room completely out of expectations. It is a training room...almost like a dojo and Mason begins his training in MMA for Monday.*
