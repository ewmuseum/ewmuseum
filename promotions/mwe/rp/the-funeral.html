The Funeral

Jul 16 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The sun glares in the bright blue open sky as we hear the familiar roar of Blight's Harley rumble past. He rides up the winding road about a mile and a half more before stopping along the side of the road. The desert heat beating down on his already sunburned face and neck. He dismounts his bike, takes off his sunglasses and hangs them on his shirt. Strapped to the back of his bike is a black wreath that reads " In Memory Of". He unstraps it and starts to walk out over the desert sands to a whole in the ground shaped like a rectangle. Blight walks over and rests the wreath on the pile of sand that has been unearthed. Along side of the hole are a number of seats only four of them filled all by Dark Karnival members FreakShow, Jezibel, Talon, and Martyr. All holding black roses and wearing black suits and dresses. Blight in his t-shirt and jeans walks over to each of them shakes their hands and hugs them saying " I'm sorry for your loss". He turns his back to them before speaking. Blight: Now lets begin...Today we gather here to remember a loved one. Some one who was a brother to many of us, maybe something more. None the less they were a part of our family. He was a provider for his kids, a man for his wife, he was an all around man's man. Now our dearly departed will be sent to rest peacefully with his creator. Would anybody else like to try to say some nice words. Jezibel? Jezibel wearing a black veil over her face stands from her seat walks over to Blight who gives her a comforting hug before sitting down himself. She walks over to the make shift grave and stands before it dropping her rose into the hole. She raises her veil and trys to speak. Jezibel ( in a cracked teary voice): To say...he will be missed...is so much of an understatement. There was so much... She stops to wipe tears from her face. Jezibel: I'm sorry. There was so much more he could have lived for...Such a young... She starts crying hysterically as Martyr and Talong jump up to go comfort her. Talon puts his arm around her ushering her back to her seat while Martyr tries wiping the tears from her eyes. FreakShow stands up and walks past them over to the grave dropping his rose in as well. He wipes the sweat from the desert heat from his face before speaking. FreakShow: It's ok Jez we all miss him. As Jezibel was trying to say he was so young, he had so much more to live for. He was a very talented man...I truly am at a loss for words. You will be missed my friend. FreakShow gazes at the grave a moment before heading back to the chairs when Blight stands up again and walks back to the grave. Blight: Talented indeed...If stupidity was something to be famous for. The other four look up at Blight, Jezibel no longer crying but is smiling. FreakShow starts to laugh a bit as Martyr and Talon grin. Blight:Why do we mourn this pathetic waste of skin? What has he done for us lately? Nothing! But as of Monday he will be the reason why I am being handed a fat check. So maybe I should be thankful or maybe I should be thanked. Thanked for putting you out of your misery. Blight walks over to the wreath he placed down on the sand pile earlier picks it up and unravells the rest of the banner. It reads " In Memory Of Jesus Cruz". Blight takes the wreath and places it as the head of the grave pushing the small prongs of a stand it is on into the golden sands. Blight: Jesus Cruz I have found it fitting to bring what is left of you back here to your home town of Xico, Mexico. Just because the thought of having your remains in the United States sickens me. I am doing you a favor Cruz, I'm going to give you a way out. Just leave, go back to your litle MMA buddies go and tell them how you slain a monster. It's ok I don't mind being a myth in a story. Every fairy tale has its demon and your story has ended before the first chapter. Cruz if you step into that ring with me this demon will cause you to swim the river Styxx to find your maker. When the ref needs to stop that match because you are physically unable to respond to anything I will drag your body here and lay you to rest. I dare you to knock me out, I dare you to submit me, choke me out. Try whatever makes you happy, pull every mixed martial art out of your bag of tricks and it still will not work. You cannot slay this demon. Everybody please rise. The Dark Karnival all rise to their feet and stand heads bowed behind Blight facing the grave. Blight: Today we mourn the death of the hopes and dreams of Jesus "El Peligro" Cruz. Monday we mourn the demise of his being. Jesus Cruz you will forever be a memory...if you were worth one They all walk off as the camera focuses on the wreath and its banner. END SCENE.
