A long, deep and rather dirty and dusty hallway is seen in the 10 metre distance of the camera's radius. At the end of the long stretch known as the hallway is seen to be a swing-open door with two rectangular windows on each door. Through the windows it seems to look dim, and dark but that could also be the filth and dirt which is gapped on the edges of the window. A closed off light on the ceiling of the hallway is seen to be twitching, and in need of desperate replacing. The white paint on the walls of the hallway are seen to be pealing off as the grey wall material is seen to stand out during the twitches of the light going on and off, and almost in a fast rate flashing motion. A loud noise is heard to be coming from behind the camera, roughly ten to fifteen metres away from the camera itself. THUD ~ THUD ~ THUD The noise begins to increase as the thudding noise is heard to be the sound of a foot within a shoe hitting the cold concreted ground of the hallway's environment. The thuds continue to become louder, and louder as the sound of the unknown figure's shoes are proven to have walked strongly and slowly, down the endorsed filth of the hallway and closer to the camera. Suddenly.. The same figure whom was making the thudding noise on their way into the visibility of the camera and towards the end of the enclosed hallway, ended with a swing-open door. The figure has their back to the camera's sight of recording and is only a few inches away from the camera itself as all is seen is their upper clothing appearance. The figure is wearing a dark grey jumper with a more so darken hood located on the back of the jumper for extra head warmth. That is all that can be seen as of now due to the unreliable close span between the camera and the figure. The back of the neck of the figure is slightly seen, just above the hood which restricts visions to the most part of the neck. The skin looks to be a darker, more toned colour which indicates that the figure is African-American. The figure once again begins to tread it's feet forward, in a slow decisive motion towards the swing-open door. The camera jolts a bit and jerks around before finding a position to make the environment and type of presence feel right. The camera is slightly tilted and is just above the shoulder of the figure, to see their path of treading footsteps towards the swing-open door. Step.. By.. Step.. Thud.. By.. Thud.. The figure comes closer and closer to the door.. The twitching of the ceiling light continues to play up as the flashes of the light make the room that little bit more trauma filled. As the figure continues his path to the door the camera spots a small hole on the curved base of the ceiling, which is leaking some sort of liquid substance. It drips in a pattern and hit's the surface of the concrete ground making a sudden pitched noise in it's melody. Slam! The swing-open door collides open as the camera and figure walk in to the next environment in what appears to be a building of some sort. The doors swing in and out, as the air of the swing makes it's notice on the hooded jumper of the figure. This environment is similar of sorts, but strangely more creepy and spooky. The similarities are slim yet to a rather large extent of existence. The dust and bacteria that covers it all is beyond revolting, and yet unlike the hallway there is not a light to keep this place easily viewable, as all is seen to be dim and dark through the lens of the camera. The figure stands in a tall halt as the camera drastically moves from it's position to view the surrounding environment. To the left, and the right and all down the corridor until the next swing-open door is seen to be dirty, empty and lifeless prison cells. Torn mattresses with a feathered, yet tattered pillow resting on the filth head of the bed, a small bin and a dusted and mouldy sink with a tap that runs dirty water and a cracked, and jagged mirror which is seen to be equipped into each of the cells the camera scuffles past. All of a sudden, the figure steps into the visual course of the camera and puts their hand up against the cell bars, and gently sways their hand back and fourth amongst the cells bars making a sound as the dust falls to the ground due to the pressure. The figure stops, and slowly yet drastically turns its body towards the camera, as the camera zooms out to get a better full-on image. SONNY WILLIAMS! The figure completely turns a one hundred and eighty degrees angle to prove the mysterious fact that the figure walking the concrete surface of what now appears to be a prison is none other than MWE Intercontinental Champion Sonny Williams. Sonny has a calm yet stricken to-be fierce expression on his face as his breathing can indicate that at this point in time. Other than his grey warmth bringing hooded jumper he has on, he also has a clean sparkling t-shirt under the jumper, and long scornful looking deep blue denim jeans on, with his left leg of the jeans held up with a red bandana, representing and expressing visually of his Detroit days. The MWE Intercontinental Championship is resting on the opposite shoulder of what the camera had a opted visual perspective of. The golden plate shimmers slightly which is easily seen in the ore darkness. Sonny blinks slowly as he glances at the camera in a intimidating expression as he turns his body back to look into the cell. The camera changes it's visual angle yet again and goes to a position where we can see the brittle on the face of Sonny from a side angle. Sonny has his two hands firmly gripped around two bars and his head resting gently on one of the dirty, and rusted cell bars in the middle of the two he had gripped onto ever so tightly with the palm of both his hands. Sonny takes a deep breath as he closes his eyes as they begin squint hard trying to despair the thoughts running through his head. The camera screen begins to fade into a bright, pale white as the scene changes to a flashback. QUOTE It was January 5th, 1998 on a late and cold, negative degree's winter's night in the city of Detroit outside of the local, yet usually deserted gas station. The street lights just outside the gas station are seen to be awfully dull and in need of repairing as they give little brightness for any drivers passing by. A figure walks into the visual perspective just outside the gas station. The angle of the scene is turned to see the figure being young Sonny Williams. Sonny is wearing a dark and dull hooded jumper with the hood placed over his head and dark grey denim jeans with the left leg held up at his knee via the use of a red bandana. Sonny's breathing pattern seems to be heavy as he looks to be in a nervous and scared state. Sonny puts his hand in one of the two pockets in his denim jeans and pulls out a black bandana. He looks at it a for a few moments, letting the many thoughts in his head wash away. Sonny takes one last deep breath and puts the black bandana over the bottom half of his face, disallowing anyone to get identification of whom he is. Sonny looks towards the gas station still wary of what will come across in the following moments of happening. He rubs his hands together firmly to bring extra warmth in this incredibly cold state of a night. Sonny looks down and pulls his jumper up slightly as a nine millimetre gun is seen sticking out of his pants. It shimmers in the darkness as he checks to see if it is fully loaded.. It is. Sonny lowers his jumper again and begins to slowly trot his way towards the gas station opening. An Indian man is seen through the clear glass of the gas station surrounding at the cash register. Sonny Williams: I've got to do this.. I will do this.. I need to. Sonny begins to make a slow jog towards the gas station, getting ready to do what he must to further himself in life and to progress towards his dream of becoming a professional wrestler. He gets closer, and closer as he finally reaches the door of the gas station and slams it open, letting the gas station worker aware of his arrival and his needs. The Indian man jumps in fright as Sonny pulls out his nine millimetre from his pants, holding up to the head of the worker as he walks towards the front counter in a fast and hurried motion. Sonny Williams: GIVE ME ALL THE MONEY OR I WON'T HESITATE TO BLOW YOU'RE BRAINS OUT! HURRY, GIVE ME ALL OF IT! Indian Worker: Ok, O-Ok. Sonny Williams: ..NOW! The voice of Sonny yelling at the Indian man is heard to muffle due to the bandana over his face as the Indian worker holds his head in fright as he begins to open the cash register and gets all of the money out and places it on the basic counter of the front counter. Sonny grabs the money as it comes and places it in his jumper as he holds the gun up to the head of the worker still as a threat if he doesn't give the money. Sonny looks out the gas station window swiftly to see if anyone as secretly the Indian worker clicks a button under the counter which calls the local police station to the scene of the gas station. The cash keeps flowing onto the counter as Sonny continues to stash it all in his enclosed jumper he is wearing. This takes a few more moments as the Indian worker gets all the money out of the cash register in a hurried and scared state, not wanting to be shot by Sonny. Sonny Williams: Come on.. HURRY! The Indian Worker looks up at Sonny in fright as he continues to stash all the cash register's money onto the front counter for Sonny. Sonny also continues to put it all into his jumper as he frantically looks around for anyone nearby the gas station, unaware that the worker has the police coming his way immediately. The Indian Worker puts the last of the money on the counter as he looks up at Sonny in a scared state of mind. Indian Worker: That's, that's all there is. Please, don't shoot me, I've got a family I need to provide for. Sonny bends over the front counter to see if he is telling the truth concerning the issue of there being no more money. Sonny continues to hold the gun in a firm and correct position as he looks at the cash register. It's empty. Sonny stashes the last of the money into his jumper as he looks at the Indian Worker and begins to run out of the gas station with the gun still aimed at him. Sonny is now finally exited from the gas station as he begins to run off, whilst putting the nine millimetre back into his pants to run faster and more freely. Suddenly, police sirens are heard as Sonny stops and nervously hears the ring of the sirens and the red and blue lights shinning from down the end of the road. Sonny turns and begins to run faster than he ever has in his entire life, trying to frantically get away from being caught on the scene from the Detroit Police Squad. Sonny turns back whilst still running 100 miles an hour to escape the possibility of being caught and in legal trouble. He see's three police cars vrooming down the road with their sirens bleeping brightly and piercing noise. Sonny turns a corner and begins to run, faster and faster as he tries not to lose much energy by breathing in and out to keep him agile and fresh. The sound of the sirens, and the brightness of it's glare becomes louder and bigger indicating it coming closer, and closer. Out of no where, a load of the stolen gas station money falls from Sonny's jumper onto the hard and cold concrete ground. Sonny Williams: Fuck.. This can not be happening! Sonny stops and hesitates to go pick it back up with the chance of being caught increasing, but he turns and picks it up, knowing that it is too much cash to just leave behind if he were to get away cleanly. The three zooming police cars turn the corner and spot Sonny as Sonny drops some of the money he was picking up and begins to run, faster and quicker than ever now. Another police car turns from the road ahead as it seems that Sonny is absolutely screwed with near to no where to run to get away from being caught. Sonny turns to his right and left and sees no roads, no fences.. Nothing but enclosed houses, next to each other in a formerly odd pattern. Sonny turns around and see's the three police cars coming closer to him and his time in the real world, coming to a near end. Sonny tries to run around the one car ahead but is tackled to the cold concrete ground in a awe by a policeman whom got out of the car. Sonny Williams: Get.. Off.. Me! Police-Man: Sir, you're under arrest for the holding up of a store. Anything you do or say may be used against you in the court of law. The police man pulls out a pair of handcuffs out and puts them on either hands of Sonny. The camera begins to fade into that same bright and pale white colour once again as the flashback seems to come to an abrupt end. The scene scales back to Sonny whom has his head pressed up against the cell bars, taking in past experiences with his eyes widely closed. Sonny's eyes squint as he tries to forget those memories this place reincarnates to his mind. A few moments pass as nothing but silence is surrounded by the intense and emotional state of environment the MWE Intercontinental Champion is based at. Sonny Williams: This cell, this prison.. This hostile environment is what I called home for 10 months. I didn't like it, it was cruel, vicious and beyond anything I could imagine.. But I deserved it, and looking back at the time of anguish I spent in here, I am thankful, because it made me think about life.. And how I wasting it, day-by-day.. Moment-by-moment. I used to try and convince myself that on this insane, corrupted world we live on that sometimes good people have to do bad things to get along in life. Because what I did, wasn't in the act of crime.. It was in the act of desperate measures. But despite everything.. I considered myself a bad person then. Sonny's brim of his eyelids open wide as he frantically and swiftly turns to the side with his head still up against the cell bars and his hands both gripped nice and firmly to the bars also. Sonny looks into the camera to show the emotion in bringing back past encounters and experiences. Sonny blinks in a slow motion as his pupil in his eye pearls sensitively, which indicates the emotion. Sonny Williams: I came back here, to re-live past moments, to wipe off the dust and prepare for an event that has me in the same position I was 10 years ago when I was forced to come here, and pay for my consequences.. I came here because I'm in the same state I once was, I'm desperate.. Desperate to win my match this coming week, because I'm not the type of guy who has the persona, the skill.. the ability to comprehend with losing.. You see, losing is not an option, it's something I have to force from even becoming a possibility.. Because if I lose, my pride, my dignity and worst of all my MWE Intercontinental Championship reign goes swirling down the drain, and for it to occur.. I won't handle with care. Sonny brings his head off the old, rusted and awfully filthy cell bars and then pushes his hands off and takes one small step backwards from the cause of the push. Sonny brings his left and right hands back to the dirty, thin, rounded cell bars and grabs a tight and firm hold of one. He takes a deep breathe and jilts the MWE Intercontinental Championship on his left shoulder thus helping it not fall down to the dust filled ground. Sonny begins to firmly pull at the cell bar, trying to use his strength and muscles to pull at it and open it up allowing access into the cell itself. This takes a few noted moments for the process to end it's progress as a loud and sudden screeching noise is heard, indicating that the cell has been open to access. Sonny brushes his hands together to remove any excess dust or dirt that was extracted from the cell bars onto both his hands. The camera begins to move around to a more appropriate position as Sonny halts with removing bacteria from his hands as he slowly and maliciously trots into the prison cell. The camera turns more to see the visual perspective of Sonny. Sonny looks at the torn mattress and wipes his finger across it as it draws a large amount of dust to it. He brings his finger to his face and blows it as the dust floats down to the ground in a very slow motion. I was confident I could pull through, confident even though realising the task at hand was something incredibly challenging. My confidence didn't pay off, nor did either of my opponents.. We put our bodies at risk, threw our health around like a football, and refused to say no, but at the end of the day it was declared a three-way draw, and it made me wake up and realize that none of this is going to be an easy breeze. Sonny turns abruptly and sits on the patch which he brushed away to prevent dust from extracting onto his denim jeans. Sonny leans his arms onto his knees in front of him and slowly turns to look into the angle of the camera's visual imagery. Sonny Williams: It made me realize that this week, this match, this intense battle for glory is going to be exactly that.. Here and now, it's my turn to take the test, to test my skills, my pure pride in this championship against a man, who despite our fresh dislike for each other, has enough experience and knowledge to bring the ball to the ball game. All is still dull as the camera turns it's angle to see Sonny's face in jagged positions on the mirror. Sonny Williams: Zak Warner.. Sonny Williams: You see last week I realized, I discovered that Zak Warner can not be doubted, that he shall never be considered to a low standard, from me at least. I learnt that this man, this championship craving man, is everything he says he is. He's a hall of famer, he's the first ever triple crown champion in GWC.. He hasn't got the determination, the perseverance, the attitude, the mindset, the intensity to ever, EVER, be in my shoes and announced as the MWE Intercontinental Champion. His cockiness is inevitable, and as much pleasure as he gets from considering himself as a god now, the worse will come back to bite him. Sonny looks into the more clear mirror now as the camera changes angles to over his other shoulder. The expression shown on Sonny's face is a fierce, intense look indicating his thoughts and feelings concerning the fresh topic. Sonny Williams: Through the fades of shown dislike for my opponent, Zak Warner, I still stand towards my words, those words of truthful wisdom when it comes down to doubting my opponent, to thinking low of him. The glass of the mirror smashes down into the sink as the right hand of Sonny begins to pour of blood. Sonny looks at his right fist and breaths heavily as he uses his left shoulder muscle to jilt the MWE Intercontinental Championship from falling into the pool of blood in the sink. Sonny turns frantically and looks into the camera with a fierce and intense look on his face as he holds his right hand up to help stop bleeding. Sonny Williams: Zak, look into my eyes.. Look at them deeply and take note at what you see. Do you see the determination, the heat, the fire .. Can you feel it!? You've done this, you've hit a gong you shouldn't have and now, now you're going to have to pay a price with no limit .. You're going to have to suffer pain, injury and anguish. Sonny Williams: You've told me, Richard Covell and the face of this universe that you can beat me time and time again but when it comes to that occasion, when the bright light is looking you down in your tearing eyes, when that stupid smirk is removed from your face, and when it's seems like it's all over, are you going to get up off your hind feet and look me in the eyes, and continue or are you gonna lay there waiting to be pinned?
