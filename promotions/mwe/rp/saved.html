Saved.; A David Whitman RP

Feb 12 2009

David Whitman

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Do you ever wonder? The big questions? Life, where it came from, what its purpose is, etcetera, etcetera…? Okay, let’s take it down a notch. Do you ever wonder about yourself? About your problems, and how, if viewed from a certain angle, you, and you alone, are to blame for each and every one of them? And how, when viewed from another angle, your troubles all seem influenced by other forces at play? What can be done? How can one know how to view which problems? How can you fix it all? How can you be saved? The wrestler known as David Whitman is awoken by the rough sound of an alarm clock. He struggles to move. A surge of pain disguised as numbness envelops him as he stretches out and hits the snooze button. Once again in silence, he just lies there and enjoys the detraction of one more annoyance. However, as the invisible spikes pierce him again, thoughts forcibly thrust themselves into his mind and he focuses on them with an out-of-place frown on his face. About how his back hurts. About the two red kneeprints on his stomach. And about the slight, yet sharp little pain that accompanies each and every deep breath he takes. All courtesy of Grand Mystique. David still has a frown on his face as he forces himself into a sitting position. And then- a miracle. Like a pristine, cleansing wave flowing over dirty sand; other, happier recent memories fill his head. His win over Alejandro and D.C., his becoming Undisputed Champion, his eventual defeating of GM… The pain becomes an afterthought; a mere background accompaniment to these brighter truths. They wash his frown away, and carry him out of bed. Minutes later, David is dressed in his usual casual garb and sitting at a raggedy old kitchen table with an empty bowl in front of him. Sitting across from him is his little niece Nicolette, also dressed in long black pants, a wrestling shirt and a hoodie. Jessica, David’s cousin and therefore Nicolette’s mom, clad in her customary pajamas, is bringing milk and cereal to the table. As she places the breakfast items in the middle of the table, David gets a “what the sh*tf*ck” look on his face. David: … Windy-O’s? Are you freakin’ kidding me? Nikki: Yeah mom, did someone sh*t in the Cheerio’s or something? Jess: HEY! David holds back his laughter as Nikki is chuckling loudly. Jessica slams her fist hard on the table to assert her maternal authority. Jess: You watch your mouth, young lady! And look, I just bought this because I thought, you know, it might be nice, having a little wrestling theme going on. David tries to console her by patting her on the back as Nikki grabs the box and looks at it from side to side. David: That’s real nice of you, cuz. It’s the thought that counts. They share a hug and David raises his eyebrow at Nikki, who giggles silently. As they all settle down and start eating, the bonding vibe is broken by David’s cell phone ring, which Nikki half-hums/half-sings along to. Nikki: Your own personal Jesus… David waves at her to stop and stay quiet (as people do) while he places the phone to his ear. David: Fyo. Yeah, what’s goo- Oh. Okay. Sure. Jefferson Street, Tim’s Cafe. Ok. He ends the call and looks at Nikki, then her mom. David: I gotta go. He gets up and gestures at his niece in a Fonz-like manner. David: Let’s roll, soul! Nikki gets a huge smile as she kisses her mom goodbye and follows David out. Minutes later, at Tim’s Café on Jefferson Street (What else were you expecting?) The tiny Whitman and the bigger, more wrestler-ey one are both sitting at a very generic-looking booth, along with David’s old friend and mentor Thomas Williams. Nikki has a stack o' pancakes in front of her, which she is going to town on. Thomas and David each have a glass of orange juice in front of them. Thomas: It’s pretty bad. I mean, I wish we could’ve met a bit later so you’d at least have some beer to soften the blows, but I just had to come to you as soon as possible. David chuckles lightly as he takes a small sip of his OJ. David: You make it sound like the world is ending. Come on then, spill it, Chicken Little! Thomas looks down and takes a deep breath. David notices this and is suddenly thrust back mentally into his match with GM for a second, before his thoughts are abruptly brought back to all that remains from that match; the pain that accompanies his own deep breaths. He shakes it off, as he has involuntarily become accustomed to doing. Thomas turns to David, with a concerned look on his face, obviously taking what he is going to say very seriously. Thomas: James… is still pretty pissed. He said… he said he thought he could trust yo- Thomas is interrupted by David’s nodding towards the pancake-devouring Nikki, as if to convey a nonverbal “Not in front of the kid” to his old pal. Thomas: Right, uh. But yeah, he went to Manhattan to chill with Zack at THEIR Pay per View. He’s probably not going to be at OverDrive. David: He’ll come around. Thomas: I doubt it. David: Look, just- whatever. Is that it? Thomas: We-ell- no. James gave me your match info before he left. David looks at Thomas seriously for a moment, anticipating the next words to come out of his mouth. Thomas: Joey Dallas. A moment passes. An expressionless moment. It is a quiet, unmoving couple of seconds, almost as if the entire world had decided to stand still and process this news. David then takes a deep breath. David: I can dig it. Thomas: …That’s it? David: I’m not gonna make a big deal out of this, Thomas. Dallas is a contender, for sure, but I refuse to start going downhill again. I know I can beat him. I KNOW I can. Thomas: Huh. David: Now if you’ll excuse me, I’ve got a little niece to take out shopping and to a show. David smiles at a confused Thomas as he gets up and leaves, followed by a full and visibly satisfied Nikki. . - - - Later, or, to be precise, after seven hours of batting cages, miniature golf, laser tag, a shopping spree and just about everything else imaginable, David and Nikki are driving home along a quiet street. A content Nikki is fiddling around with her new iPhone as she turns to her uncle. Nikki: I didn’t know so many people were rooting for Dallas. David turns to her, surprised at the seemingly random remark. David: What? Where’d you hear that? Nikki: On this forum, I mean, almost everyone’s saying he’s gonna win. David: Let me see that. David gently nudges his niece aside, and looks at the iPhone screen. David: Huh. Nikki: You’re gonna prove’m all wrong, right Unca D? David just shrugs as he turns his attention back to the bumpy road ahead of him. = = = = Brep. Brep. Brep. Bre- The jolting, ear-drum scraping noise comes to an abrupt halt as David slams his left hand onto the alarm clock buzzer. He hesitatingly turns his head and opens his eyes to check the time. Hours: 09. Minutes: 13. Seconds: 02. Huh. How odd, he thinks. He can’t quite put his finger on it but… As he rubs his eyes with his right hand, he brings himself to a sitting position on the side of the bed. The physical pains are still there, though more subdued. David lets out an extensive yawn while he clumsily searches for his cell phone on the night stand. He passes his hand over smooth, wooden surface as h- wait, wasn’t this surface completely smooth? He shakes his head, as if to fully awaken himself, before turning to his hand, which is still rubbing the nightstand. He removes his hand, slowly, almost as if in fear of what it might reveal… … Of course. How utterly delightful. He shakes his head again, and then, still not convinced he’s fully awake, slaps himself thrice on the right side of his face. He checks once more, but the roughly etched message still remains ingrained in the wood of the nightstand. He sighs loudly as he gets out of bed. No time to figure this one out. The first stretch of the drive to New York will be a tedious and soul-consuming one, and unless he leaves in twenty minutes, it will be even worse. There’s gonna be enough time to think about all of this later on. =========== A cold shower and quick breakfast later, the MWE original is ready for his trip. Despite the fact that Nikki and Jess are both waiting for him at his car, he has apparently decided to take a couple of minutes to say goodbye to the guest bedroom, which he will leave behind the worst of his aches and pains in. After some rummaging in his backpack, he takes out a couple of first class plane tickets. He double checks each one carefully before he lays them neatly on the pillow of the bed and leaves. As he walks out of the house trying to remember exactly what the team of Cannon and Resoan did right, the brisk air hits him immediately. Slightly chilly… but nothing compared to almost everywhere else. Yet he can feel the cold ahead…He shakes it off (as people do) and walks towards his car, where the Whit-Women, if you will, are standing and forcefully smiling. I know, I know. Whit-women? Really? Seriously? The Undisputed title is on the line, and I’m throwin' out puns like “Whit-Women?” Good grief. But I digress. Reaching ever closer to the car, David checks his pockets for his keys, trying to keep the always-uncomfortable “goodbye” process to a minimum. David: Oh, I think I forgot m- Nikki: Here they are! I got them for ya! David: Tha- David analyzes the keys as Nikki hands them to him. His car key is slightly dirty under the edges, with- He rubs his thumb over the usually shiny grooves. Yep, it’s wood shavings. Well, there’s a piece of the puzzle. Of course, it’s a blue piece, and the puzzle consists of ocean and beach- But hey, a piece’s a piece. He jams the key into the lock as he turns back and smiles at his little niece with a newfound sincerity. She runs up to him and half-hugs him at the waist, a slight air of concern in her voice as she says… Nikki: Be careful. He is still smiling as he answers her, trying to put her young mind at ease. David: You know me. Jessica now also turns to David, with a bittersweet expression accompanied by a fitting smile. Jess: Be safe. David lets out a barely audible scoff as he pops a half-hearted cocky grin. David: See above, b*tch. He gets into his car and drives off as Nikki waves wildly. = = = I'm sorry. David moves uncomfortably as he walks over to the closed white curtains. It... It's taken me way too long to say it. It has. He opens the curtains up a bit, letting a few warm beams of light into the otherwise dimly lit room. After what happened... I knew it wasn't right, but we didn't get a chance to talk... and even if we could have, I don’t… know how that would have gone. You know what I mean? He moves away from the curtains now, almost as if discontent with his own choice of opening them up in the first place. The point is, I know you didn't like it, and rightfully so. I mean, I don't regret it. In a way, it had to happen, and I'm glad it did. But at the same time, I know it had to hurt you... a lot... and I'm deeply sorry. As he walks around the room in a worrying manner, slowly drowning in the silence for a bit, he keeps his eyes to the floor. …So yeah. There’s nothing else I can say except for that. I’m sorry. Heh. … He finally stops moving around and looks into the direction of the curtains, peering out into the light-filled world outside. Well on a brighter note though, I guess… I also want to thank you. For... being my friend. Even though you might deny it, you know it's true. I'm truly grateful for everything you've done for me... And the fact of the matter is, that if it wasn't for you, I wouldn't be on this path, and I… really… owe you my life. His stare shifts to the ground once more as his body seemingly follows his eyes and he squats down, still looking at the carpeted floor underneath his feet. When I face Dallas... it’s gonna be a hard match. I’m going in there, never having faced the guy, except for if you count that three-on-four handicap tag match, which I don’t. I’m going in there on the 13th having to give my all and hope I win, not knowing what’s gonna happen, against a guy who believes beyond a shadow of a doubt that he will walk away from OverDrive as the new Undisputed Champ. Still uncertain of whether or not he should look up, David starts fiddling with his thumbs nervously. And, you know, if it were the old David Whitman, I wouldn’t have stood a chance. Cockiness costs you in wrestling, unless you can back it up, in which case there’s a chance it’ll actually HELP you in a match. Joey Dallas would have obliterated me back when I was still… struggling. But now, now that I’m getting better mentally, and putting all that bad stuff behind me, his cockiness becomes just another weakness. Another target on the range. Now that you’ve helped me… evolve… I can take him down. David finally looks up and in front of him. A couple of seconds pass as the silence engulfs the room yet again. And suddenly, from out of nowhere, David chuckles. Yeah, yeah, the Steelers won. Big whoop. Congrats. He gets an honest-to-goodness smile on his face. At least it wasn't the Cowboys beating the Cardinals. Now THAT would have been an omen. Heh… Erhm, anyway, I uh, I think it’s getting to be about that time. David stands back up and cracks his neck uncomfortably. He takes a look in front of him again. Man… You know, I feel pretty bad for you… I mean, not just because you’re all, you know, half-dead and whatnot… but… I mean… you lost a sweet car. He chuckles lightly. Come on now! I gotta get SOME cheap shots in. When you get better, things’ll be… I dunno... Whatever. I gotta get going. He raises his hand in the “peace” motion. Peace Out. David turns around and walks to the door. As he takes the first step into the sterile-looking hallway outside the room, he stops... as if hearing a voice inside his head. He lowers his head in a slight chuckle. Yeah yeah... screw you too... He says it sarcastically with a twinkle in his eye as he leaves the room. = = = QUOTE (Play Tape) A blinding white flash zips across the pitch-black screen quickly, disappearing as abruptly as it had appeared. Suddenly, the silhouette of a man becomes visible after a second flash, and now slowly but surely the man begins to come into focus. It is David Whitman, standing in front of the blackness. He is wearing a black shirt with white outline, and similar pants. The fact that he is barefoot would probably escape you, thanks to the shining of the newly designed MWE title that is draped over his right shoulder. A moment passes as he closes his eyes and takes a deep breath. He opens his eyes again and stares intently at the camera for a second. Then, the seriousness of the moment is suddenly broken; David chuckles. With feeling. With real, unabashed feeling. Almost as if he were expressing something that words can’t describe. As if everyone around him, everybody watching, nay, everyone in the UNIVERSE, is in on the joke. He lazily stops chuckling and turns his attention completely to the camera again as he is now begins to speak slowly, like a teacher trying to explain an important point to a classroom full of students. David: I have overcome obstacle after obstacle, climbed to the top of countless mountains, and endured the toughest tests thrown at me. With every word, his gaze slowly rises upward. David: Dedicated my body and health to this company, defeated some of the best MWE and wrestling in general has to offer, and captured the highest regarded title four times… He is by now looking straight above himself. His stare begins moving downward, as if he were watching something falling, something way behind the camera. A pebble off a cliff; an atom bomb onto a helpless city. He stops dead in his tracks as he is now presumably looking beneath him, his body still suspended in the blackness. David: …And people are raking my name through the mud. Calling me a paper champion, worthless, a couple-hits wonder who doesn’t deserve to hold the gold. So in other words, I’m the underdog who gets to the top, only to still be considered the underdog. He chuckles again, turning his attention straight at the camera. David: Funny, how ever since the Life Swap, I’ve been turning more and more into D.C., eh? A smile begins forming on his face, but it fades away quicker than it started. David: BUT… I digress. Let’s get to the point of this. He turns around and takes a couple of steps, seemingly walking on nothing. The only visible image in a completely dark space. Slow, but deliberate. He now turns around to face the camera again. David: Joey Dallas. He holds his open right hand out in front of him and stares at it, as if examining an invisible miniature Dallas. David: High school athlete, former soldier, impressive history in various feds… the usual story. He cracks a “Riiiight” kinda smile. David: I am, of course, kidding. We all know Dallas is no chumplet. He’s smart, he’s proven it. The man is a walking, talking Wikipedia. And if there’s one thing he definitely ISN’T, it’s a terrible wrestler. Deceitful, yes. Cynical? Most definitely. Hate-filled? WELL SADDLE ME UP AND CALL ME PEGASUS! David does the cheesiest two-thumbs-up you’ve ever seen, and winks at the camera before lowering his hands. David: YET... no one can deny that he is an amazing wrestler. A look of seriousness grasps David once again. David: BUT... The question is... Is he as amazing as he believes he is? His right eyebrow slowly raises itself, as David conveys a feeling of intrigue. The camera pans out a bit as David starts to talk again. David: Dallas walks through life… as if controlled. Haunted. Possessed. With the idea of… and everything surrounding… being the proverbial Savior. Redeemer. Deliverer. He can… David conjures up some air-quotes. David: “take us all out of our misery”. And HERE'S THE KICKER! Understand that phrase any way you want to, and he probably thinks it. David lets out a scoff. He now points right at the camera, full of intent. His motives appear crystal-clear as his no-nonsense expression communicates an honest need to explain an important truth. David: Let me tell you something. I have never been one to rant on my opponents, always one to show respect. Well, Joey Dallas has done the unthinkable. He has SUCCEEDED, in getting into my head. He takes a deep breath, slightly flinching a bit, as he lets his hand drop slowly, and now gets a slight quarter-smile on his face. David: Now, he probably thinks that might give him the upper hand, but last time I focused this much on an opponent, I pinned one of the best champions this company has ever seen in Sonny Williams, with the title hanging on the line. He pats the gold belt on his shoulder, ostensibly without realizing it. David: The thing of it is, though, that the REASON he has gotten me this focused on him and our match is not because I am going into Overdrive thinking this will be a huge challenge. It’s not because of the impressive people he beat in amazing matches right in the middle of the ring. And it’s NOT because I... believe he can... save me. David flinches slightly, shakes it off, and continues. David: Joey Dallas makes me irate because he is everything I stand against- He shrugs nonchalantly. David: Nothing. A light nod accompanies his now pensive expression before he begins talking again. David: Dallas... is nothing that he says he is. From his name, to his claims of grandeur and how he will save everyone and everything in his path, to his records. Yes, undefeated in two federations. But at a couple of matches a fed, should I REALLY waste a quarter on calling Guinness World Records? David raises his head, taking another deep breath. This time, he does not flinch at all. David: The man who promised to save Rev Pro, Sinc Mercier, and God knows what else- Is this who I have to face? A faint look of disgust begins forming but David fights it off as he continues. David: I WILL give him this, however… Dallas is conniving. He is a double-tongued snake, and he is proud of it. He is proud… in general, and thinks he will defeat me no problem. But I… will NOT let him win. David’s determination is certain as his words become stronger with each passing sentence. David: This “paper champion” walks into every show on time and ready, this “weak clown” knows his place in the universe. David Whitman won’t make claims he can’t back up, and I am claiming, right here and right now, that I will not let you walk away from Overdrive victorious, Dallas. And I am not just talking about the title here. Visibly worked up, David now looks slightly off-camera. He lets out a half-chuckle before becoming completely serious. He focuses his attention back at the camera, fuming and fighting for his self-control. David: At Friday Night Overdrive, I will take on the Machiavelli of Mayhem; the Messiah of MWE. THIS MAN WILL SAVE US ALL, DONCHA KNOW? Well Joe, I guess there’s really only one thing left to say about that… If Jesus was a heel, the Romans were the faces. I’m the Emperor, and I’ve got a Burning Hammer with your name on it, Sharpmen. Not even a second passes after his last word before the camera abruptly cuts to black. David stares at the blank screen in front of him for a few seconds. Editor: You just said you were going to kill Jesus. David turns to the editor, who is sitting next to him. David: Yeah, I’m thinking we might wanna cut that last bit. ============== David: I mean, it started right after I found out I was facing him. The morning after that, the whole self-message thing happened… I knew something was obviously wrong… but then, at the promo shoot I just completely lost it. We had to re-shoot eight times, and each time I kept getting angrier and angrier! David Whitman is at a private gym. After using up the last of his road money to rent the gym for 24 hours, he is exhausted from a full day of working out. This is his five minute break, which he is spending doing sit-ups. Well, that, and talking on the phone with a… well, I guess you can call it an almost friend? Ok, it’s Lindsey. Lindsey: Well that's human. It's… understandable. David: Yeah, but I can't let the fans see that. I gotta be the good guy. Lindsey: Right, right. I get that. So, whadidya do then? David: We took a break, I got drunk, and we did another promo where I rag on him for never managing to save anything. Lindsey: Ah. David: Yeah, we're not using that one either. I guess the gist of it is I'm going up against Dallas without a promo. Lindsey: That's risky. Some fans might not like it. Management sure as hell won't. David: Meh. Details, details. Lindsey: Heh. Why don’t you try changing your approach? David: Well… Yeah… I don’t know. I don’t- WAIT! David stops exercising and gets up quickly as an epiphany appears to him in his thoughts. Lindsey: What?! What is it? David: THAT’S IT! I’m not scared of Dallas… I’m scared of changing! Lindsey: …Wait, what? David: That’s what this whole thing was about! I’m scared! Well, I mean, I WAS- Look, the old me was afraid of change… and stuck in all that bad sh*t… and now that I’m setting everything right and moving on and fixing my life, the old me wants to stop that! Lindsey: ...Are you schizo or something? David: No, not other me as in other personality- I mean my subconscious. You know? Look, I was always stuck in those bad feelings, and ever since the life swap, I’ve been changing for the better. And all these emotions I forgot how to feel started coming loose… and THAT’S why I snapped at the promo shoot! Lindsey: O…k… David: The old me WANTS to lose. The old me wants a reason to start slipping back downhill... That’s what the “Save Me” meant; “Save me by not winning this match, David”. You know?! Lindsey: I- David: But it wasn't the old me that beat DC and Alejandro, It wasn't the old David that made GM tap out. And it damn sure isn't gonna be the old me that pins Joey Dallas at Overdrive, because I'm not gonna let that happen. I win this one, and it's over. I will be cured. Lindsey: I don't know if that's uh, how the mind works. David: Exactly. You don't know. I do. I can feel it. Lindsey: Ok then. You obviously don’t need me for this conversation, heh. David is only still holding the phone up to his ear by habit, as he is now completely gone and focused on only one thing. David: I know how to fix this. This match… is it. Joey Dallas will be my savior. But not in the way that he might expect. =====================================
