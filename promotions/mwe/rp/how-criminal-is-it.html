How criminal is it?

Sep 20 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The scene fades in showing Sinc screeching across the pavement at a right corner as the traffic light hits green. It is evening and the sun is just beginning to set. The city of San Francisco is quite calm. Not many cars are jamming the streets, that is why Sinc enjoys the evening so much. His car is a Mazda RX8, silver with black and red tribal. His windows untinted and his car’s back end equipped with a smooth tail. He finishes his turn and swiftly glides down seventeenth street as the lowering hill gives him speed. When he reaches the bottom of the long, stretched street Sinc begins to apply his brakes to make a quick turn to his home. Before he is able to make the turn a man rushes in front of his car with a white, dirty clown type mask on. The man stops in front of Sinc’s car as Sinc is able to prevent running him over and hit’s the brakes hard. Sinc steps out of his vehicle and opens his car door with a rather rude expression. Sinc: Hey man. What the hell? What the hell do you think your doing? Sinc is wearing a black suit with a white dress shirt under it. It is completed with black jeans and brown boots. Sinc is also wearing black, silver rimmed sunglasses and a rather expensive gold Rolex around his right wrist. Masked Man: Me? What are YOU doin. I am trying to run my ass as far away from here as possible. The masked man is also carrying some sort of brown bag filled with bulky items. He is wearing a black hoodie and blue jeans with white sneakers. Sinc: May I ask WHY the hell your doing this? Sinc then closes his car door and nears the man with anticipation. The camera follows Sinc with the man. Masked Man: Look man just stay away and let me go on with my business…. As Sinc inches closer he takes off his sunglasses and winces his eyes as the glaring evening sun beams down. The masked man seems to recognize him. Masked Man: Hey I know you…your that one wrestler on TV I have seen around before…Sink Mercy or something like that… Sinc: Sinc MerCIER. Dammit people need to start getting that right. Anyway, I had no idea a guy like you even watches MWE. I mean…look at you…a masked man wearing a sack on his back…how much more awkward can it get? Masked Man: Hey can you do that thing where you yell out your voice real loud and it echoes? Sinc: Uhm….well….again this situation is strange to me. What the hell are we talking about anyway? You still have not explained to me what it is exactly that you are doing. The masked man sweats a little and looks very worried. Sinc continues wincing at him and puts his hands on his hips. The camera zooms out a little. Masked Man: Well….you see….I am kind of running away from the cops…and that’s just being honest. Sinc: Your kidding right? How did those cops not find you by now…committing a robbery is a pretty big thing in San Fran. Masked Man: Does it look like I am kidding? And technically it wasn’t that big. I stole from a blind china shop owner. Sinc: Wow that must have been REAL hard. And take off that mask and I can tell if your kidding or not. Masked Man: I would if I could but I would really rather not. I just got fired from my last job so I am trying to make a quick living. Blind owner or not. Sinc: I am surprised you have not pulled a knife or a gun on me yet. Guess I will never be like Tupac or Fifty Cent. Masked Man: Well I don't see a reason to. I was an associate for some crazy ass psycho. Man did I learn a thing or two about that. He always wore makeup to cover cuts of his. And he always wore purple. To be real, I always thought that was kind of gay…but that’s just me. Sinc looks shocked. His eyes widen as he hears this. No sirens or cops are heard at all. Just the usual honking of horns and the sound of tires turning across hot cement. Sinc: This dude would not happen to go by the name of Jekyl would he? Masked Man: Yup…I am pretty sure he is on that MWE show too. I could be wrong since he did not really explain that to me. He always liked filming things constantly. An obsession maybe. Usually its based around violence or some shit. Sinc at this point is convinced and nods in agreement. Sinc: Yup that’s Definitely him. I am facing him this week. I want to kick that psycho’s ass so hard he will see the implant of my foot on him the next day. The Masked Man laughs a bit. Masked Man: He is as crazy as you can get man. I used to be one of his little henchmen so to speak. But I didn’t get my share of the cut and was displeased. So he basically beat my ass and left me nothing. Sinc: Haven’t you learned anything about clowns by working for one? They can’t be trusted. One minute your sitting in the audience politely and the next you get a god damn pie in the face! Remembering things like this and then seeing Jekyl’s face it makes me want to beat him to a pulp. A melody is heard, it is the sound of a ringtone. Sinc checks his cell phone but it isn’t his. The man feels around in his pockets and grabs his phone, he receives a text message and checks it. QUOTE Sent TODAY at 6:53 PM by XxloLmastaxX yo g0t the g00dz? [/center] The camera zooms on the man’s received message. The man begins dialing away words to reply. Sinc is unaware of what this message says. Sinc: Who you talkin to? Masked Man: Just a minute… Sinc: Who you- Masked Man: One Sec… Sinc: WHO YOU- Masked Man: Almost done… Sinc: I just want to- Masked Man: There. The man finishes his message and sends it. QUOTE Sent TODAY at 6:54 PM by LilKillA Yea man no worreez [/center] The man clicks his phone off and tucks it in his pocket. The camera zooms out on the two again. Sinc: Are you at least going to tell me your name? An autograph maybe? Shit I don’t know I am sure this is strange for both of us…I mean I would offer you a ride but you being a freakin convict right now and all…but if you can say anything about Jekyl tell me I would like to know. Masked Man: Just call me killa. I’ll try and watch your show man…and boy…this is awkward as well isn’t it? I can’t say much about my old boss other than the typical stuff. Crazy violent lunatic you know same old same old. He gets kinda touchy on his past though. Sinc: So if I said something along the lines of….hey Jekyl why didn’t you take up being the next Ronald McDonald he would be upset? Killa: Yes…. Sinc: Or hey Jekyl being as you had amnesia, you should be like K-Fed when he banged Brittany Spears, always forgetting your protection? Killa: I guess…. Sinc: Well Then I GUESS that is all I really need to know. So I won’t tell any cops I saw you. Don’t worry about it. Unless they bribed me or something…but that’s only unless their orange haired fruit loops like Donald Trump. I guess I will see you later. Sinc was then making his way back to his car when sirens are heard booming around the corner. Sinc: Damn. Go ahead and see if you can fit in my trunk real quick! Sinc quickly pops his trunk open and Killa jumps into it with his bag barely fitting. Sinc closes his trunk and the cops screech to a stop in front of his car. Sinc is casually leaning against his car trunk. The cops get out of their cards and confront Sinc. The camera fit’s the cops and Sinc into frame. The 4 cops that were there in two vehicles surrounded Sinc in a circle. They were all Caucasian wearing the usual blue shirt black pants cop outfit. Each of their uniforms said “SFPD” on the right arm sleeves. Two of the cops were overweight while the other two were extremely skinny. Sinc: Well now I am officially scared. I feel like a power puff girl. One of the fatter cops approaches Sinc. Fat Cop: Did you happen to see a man around here with a mask on and a large brown bag? Sinc: Uhm…no? Fat Cop: May I ask why your car is parked at a turn and a no parking zone? Sinc: Oh yea…it uh…broke down. Hey if you need a doughnut or something I think I may still have a box from this morning. Fat Cop: I find that hilarious…haha….don’t be a smartass. How exactly did it break down? Sinc: Uhm…I ran over a nail? Sinc shrugs as the other three cops begin searching around Sinc’s Mazda. Sinc: You know this is really unnecessary…I feel violated! One of the searching cops replies to Sinc. Fat Cop Two: It’s the usual procedure. If we suspect something we look around and investigate. Chill out, Sinc looks at him in disgust. Sinc: What, you need a doughnut too? Because Crispy Kreme is half off today pal. The cop doesn’t reply to Sinc and continues searching around. Killa remains quiet in the trunk. Fat Cop: Find anything boys? The fat cop walks to them to see if they found anything with his own eyes. At that moment while all the cops are distracted searching in every place but the trunk, Killa opens it with the bag and proceeds to move UNDER the car slowly not making noise. He is well hidden from the cops sites as he slides under the Mazda. Skinny Cop One: Nope nothing. Fat Cop Two: Nothing in the back seats or the passenger or driver’s seats. Not even under them. Skinny Cop Three: Nothing in the glove box either. The fat cop makes a thinking gesture and moves toward the trunk. Sinc: Shouldn’t you guys have a warrant for this type of shit? Fat Cop: Well when there’s a convicted felon on the loose especially around here we technically don’t need one. Mind if I pop this trunk open? Sinc: Well I TECHNICALLY think that’s BS, but to show I got nothing to hide, go for it big boy. Watch out though there might be a bomb in there. The cop looks at Sinc with discontent. AKA gives Sinc the evil eye. Sinc rolls his eyes as the cop searches the trunk. Nothing but empty oil cans, dirty grease rags, and a roll of paper towels. The fat cop looks upset. Fat Cop: Ok your clear…but if you see him you know what to do… Sinc: Run in fear? Fat Cop: Call the police! Now get your car fixed and get goin buddy! The cops got back in their cars as they slam their doors and take off. Their sirens blaring back down the road they came from. The camera zooms out to get a shot of them driving away, then switches back to Sinc in a zoom. Once the dust from the back of the cars settle Sinc takes a sigh of relief. Sinc mocks the cops. Sinc: Call the police…bleh bleh bleh…now get your car fixed…bleh bleh… Sinc then dips his head under his car and signals Killa to come out. He does and stands up near Sinc. Killa: Thanks man I owe you one. Sinc: Save it. Just watch the show this week pal. I will be sure to remember what you said about Jekyl. It’s not like I have amnesia or anything. Killa: That wasn’t all that funny. Sinc: SINC MERCIER…makes it funny. Killa: Ok well, I’ll be sure to let my buddy know about the show. Later. Killa runs off down the road and into an alley disappearing from Sinc’s sights. Sinc gets back in his car and turns on the radio. “Don't Stop Believin” By Journey is playing. He blasts it up as he starts his car back up. He sings to it. The camera is now focused inside Sinc’s car zooming in on Sinc. Sinc: JUST A SMALL TOWN GIRL….LIVIN IN A LONELY WORLD…..SHE TOOK THE MIDNIGHT TRAIN GOIN ANY..WHERE! A man opens his window in an apartment across the street. He smiles and begins singing with Sinc. The camera gets a quick shot of the man. Man: HE’S JUST A CITY BOY…BORN AND RAISED IN SOUTH DETROIT….HE TOOK THE SMALL TOWN TRAIN GOIN ANY…WHERE! Soon other people open their windows and began singing. The camera zooms out. It was then followed moments later by a whole block of people singing. It was in a harmonic chorus now. Sinc then looks around totally in awe. Sinc: What….in…the…living…HELL! Sinc now feels embarrassed and turns off his radio. Everyone stops and shuts their windows. One voice screams “Asshole..” And ignorantly Sinc just drives away trailing down the road on his way to his house trying not to think of his ex-wife. Sinc: Last time I sing Journey…that’s for damn sure… The camera frame then switches to a sky shot of Sinc’s car going down a long road. The scene fades with a beat orange sky.
