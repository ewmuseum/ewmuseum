Blood Eye; What do you see?

May 6 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
-Jay Riddick is sitting in an old-fashioned desk/chair found in an abandoned classroom. He sits in the direct center of the classroom with at least 30 other uncomfortable chair/desk combinations. He writes on the chipped, old, stained wood that was deteriorated after years and years of mistreatment. Mr. Riddick looks out the window in curiosity due to the moonlight striking and penetrating its light through the window as the window mulligans made the lights on the floor into divided squares. He takes a stand from the desk and walks over to the window, looking at the moon in amazement. He swipes his tongue on both his lips as the dryness perishes. He stands there motionless, not even blinking. Suddenly, Jay Riddick blinks. He then looks towards the camera, striking the lens with his vision. He stands there speechless, looking back towards the moon, he finally begins to speak.- Jay Riddick: July 1979.....I came into this world... I wish I hadn’t. Its day has brought a light of darkness upon my eyes. I saw the world for what it was. Born without bias, my first thoughts were of how cruel the world truly was. I came out bloody; the blood ran down my eyebrow as it seeped through the sweat on my eyebrow as it slid its way into my eye blinding me into a red vision of evil. I then recall being wiped by the nurses’ finger removing the blood, I opened my eyes and caught first glimpse of the battlegrounds known as planet earth. I was there wrapped up in a towel to cover the nude. Because one simple rule of society was that the nude is sinful, unpleasant, and disgraceful. However that rule of society’s creation was disguised as a subliminal message. A message of hiding who you truly are. See, society isn’t what it is in plain sight. Society is a religion of redefined morals and standards. We obey it because to us it seems simple and somewhat obvious. However we never bother to question it. Well that is who I will never become... -Riddick walks away from the window and strolls his legs around the corner of the room as the camera’s peripheral vision follows. He takes long, steady, calculated strides. Walking across the line of darkness until only the left side of his body is visible as he turns towards the camera. He stares down at the front desk, the teacher’s desk. He grabs a stack of papers that was probably on the desk for dozens of years.- April 25th, 1990. This was my 5th grade classroom. My teacher was Ms. Jarris. She told us everyday how the future is important, the future is coming, that we must be ready for the future. Our careers, our lifestyle, our personality will be decided in these years of our youth. She claimed that our work hard to be done at our best, that we had to do our best and expect the best from ourselves. -Jay Riddick speaks calmly as his words flow smoothly from his throat as he flicks through the stack of papers revealing to be his past school work...- Maybe it was just me; maybe I had too much hope for myself... -Riddick passes through one particular piece of his schoolwork and then picks it out to observe it himself many years later. Flashbacks as it were. Riddick stares at the paper. His eyebrows angle like it was two collapsing overhanging streets. His light breaths became heavy heaths of oxygen.- I tried my best each god damn time! But she always wanted more! She would always tell me that she wanted more, and more! Nothing I did was enough for her! My best wasn’t as good as everyone else! So you know what I did!? I stood up to her and told her to rot in hell! I stormed out of that room and I never came back till this very day! Now, as of this moment, I’m in this classroom alone. This school is no more, it’s abandoned. Nobody wanted this school to be alive. Ms. Jarris is hopefully dead or at least her corpse is rotting into old age. Everybody gave up on her in the end. And I was the pioneer, I was the only one with the guts to stand up to her and made the way for others to do so. I will never be a sheep; I will never step down or step aside. When there is no one else to hope for, when there is nobody that will stand by my side, I embark on my black stallion and I become the one man army. I will be the one man rebellion, the solo insurgency. -Riddick after slamming the pages down sat in the teacher’s chair. Forcing out all the pent up anger accumulating within the thin narrow passages of his veins were blood was supposed to flow. His face was red, his eyes were so starting to moisturize as a thick coat of tears made itself into a barrier in front of his eye.- I didn’t give up on anybody, how am I the quitter after my best wasn’t enough. I’ve lived my life with people who were never satisfied with anything about me. I wasn’t enough of this; I wasn’t living up to everybody’s standards. That’s why I’m stepping into the ring this week for the first time in my life. I’ve spent years training for this moment. And my best doesn’t have to live up to everybody’s standards. Because me beating every person and deteriorating their health is not based on opinions. My strength, my abilities, my potential is all fact. However I would like to take a moment and address those who will suffer during that short time I will be found in the ring with them. Anthony Clark, you seem to believe that you are the true uprising that I claim to be. However you are simply ridiculing yourself. Your arrogance is an attempt to hide your uncertainty. You live the life of lust to assure yourself reliance in your own abilities by making confidence in your abilities of your definition of love. But I ponder something about you, what fact really makes you believe you can battle against me and believe you’ll walk out victorious? What makes you better than me? Please take these last couple of days to ponder and hopefully find some false hope in between the legs of some woman you’ll meet the night before our meeting in the ring. Sin, a name that I personally find to be bigger than you can keep. You seem to ride along the roads of arrogance trying to convince everyone that you are sin. But why must you believe that you live up to your name? What have you done that truly lives up to sin? Have you ever had a moment where the line that went beyond evil was not an obstacle to get through? You’re stepping into my fields, fields that I walked on and falsely claiming you live by. Blight, you step into this ring and expecting to live up to your dead father expectations. You recently lost him and hope to make him proud by beating me in that ring. But before he died you made him proud, but let me ask you something. Do you really think he loves you? Now that you satisfy him suddenly he holds compassion for you. You’re finding yourself in a false sense of approval. Your father died because he gave up hope on you. He is tired of giving you chances, he is tired of you. He doesn’t love you, nobody loves you. It’s time you received this revelation. You didn’t abide by society and now you’re like me, unloved. I will not show you any compassion either, you will learn what I had to learn so painfully, and it’s up to you to decide your fate if I hadn’t yet made the decision to conceal your fate. Sinister Dexter, I will make sure that you’re experience with me in that ring will be much less enjoyable, and much more suffering. Suffering is the only way you’ll learn not to step into the ring during my reign of dominance. -Jay Riddick stands up off the chair and walks in front of the teacher’s desk. On all four tables in front is featuring the name of each one of his opponents in the 5-man battle royal.- Being in a classroom, I’ve reserved all four of you front row seats in my classroom, the wrestling ring. I will teach you a hands-on lesson on how the best way to learn and receive revelations is the same way every self-proclaimed saviour has learned by, pain, suffering and torturing. An important lesson will be learnt on May 8th, 2009. A lesson that my teacher should’ve learnt, a lesson many people have learnt, a lesson about the next man to cause the worldwide revolution, the uprising, Jay Riddick. I have dreamt of this day since my first day on this earth, a baby born with bloody eyes seeing the truth of the world. Reality was defined. I plan on doing that to my opponents, getting blood in their eyes and seeing the reality that I saw when I was born. The day has arrived, once the sun rises, all hope descends to the lowest of extremities. Everyone that has ever criticized me, telling me I wasn’t enough, that I wasn’t capable will no longer have the capability of speech nor the capability of movement. And once my blood from my own body falls into my eyes as I hold myself up in victory, I will see the world for what it was meant to be, bloody, corrupted and usurped under my control. That baby that laid in the hospital room with blood in his eyes was me, and I would do anything to get that back. Beg me to repent my sins, beg me to forgive those I’ve hurt. However I’m not done. I have sinned more than I have spoken and I have yet to cease. A myriad of sins over the duration of my existence, it brings relief to myself as hell for me is nothing. Hell has always been feared as the dreaded aftermath. Everybody would follow religion to assure themselves amnesty from its deathly grasp. The fire and brimstone that brings any set of eyes to tears overlooking possible fears that may occur. If that’s what you people will fear, you’re in luck. Because once I seize control and abolish society to create my own set of rules, I will make hell seem no more than a deep breath at midnight. Refreshing and addicting. My world will be deeper than definition. Defiance will be impossible. An insurgency upon my throne will be denied. Possibilities will become a myth. Nobody will stop me. Not one damned person on this earth will be able to stop my uprising. Do you know why? Because you people being civilians that lived under the wings of society, obeying it’s morals and rules will not be able to comprehend my ways, you will not be able to stop me because you will not know how. I’m not an absolute negative man, I have love and compassion in me. I’ve always loved my walks at midnight, breathing the night air that nobody would take from me. They would all be sleeping and the world was mine. I was leaving fresh foot prints where nobody else would ruin. That darkness was a drug to my lips. I would pray for darkness because it heightened my senses. It gave me more power; those noises in my ears would finally be that of silence. No messages or obscene noises would occur during the sweet air of the duration of the moist star. However the one thing I regret in my match is that the ringing messages in my ears will return, telling me not to stop, telling me to continue. I would do anything for that noise in my head to stop, I’d do anything to satisfy its crucial hunger. May 8th, 2009. The vision of Jay Riddick begins, not as a dream, not as a possibility, but as a deadly truth, a definite reality. I want every man in MWE to not lie in fear, as I want them to listen carefully to a quote of my own pondering I take to heart. “All those who revolutionized and changed the world once had to stand against it.”
