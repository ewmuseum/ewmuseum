MWE 24/7 — 08-09 PPV PR Archives

YESM!; Billy Joe Barker RP

Dec 27 2008

Billy Joe Barker

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
-------------_ The scene slowly opens up in a fancy restaurant, and as soon as the entire scene is visible in all its crisp glory (MWEHD, B*TCH!), the camera starts panning around to show some couples having a nice dinner, one big table with what appears to be a family having a nice meal, and so on. The atmosphere is quite joyful as assorted tasteful Christmas decorations lend a cheery, almost jolly feel to the otherwise just about excessively fancy place. All of a sudden, the camera pans past Billy Joe Barker and Alejandro Rodriguez. The camera nonchalantly keeps moving, but then stops abruptly and moves back to the tag team. Alejandro is wearing a black suit and tie, and has his white mask on. Billy Joe is wearing a light-blue tux and tie and has his big ol’ cowboy hat on. As you can imagine, quite a few people are staring at the two, but they both ignore the odd stares. Billy Joe Barker: Ollie-Han-Throw, can yew remind me agin why wur in this fancy place? Alejandro lets out a sigh. Alejandro: For the LAST TIME- Hey, are you even listening? The camera turns to Billy Joe, who is obsessed with his fork. BJ: I never seen ‘um so shiny before. The frustrated Alejandro puts his face in his hands. Alejandro: Godda- ok, ok. He takes a deep, calming breath. Alejandro: We’re here because we got a call regarding your match against Zak W- Billy Joe interrupts his tag partner. BJ: HEY! WHURS MA SPOON? DID YOU TAKE MAH SPOON? I- Alejandro angrily slaps the fork out of Billy Joe’s hand. Alejandro: THERE IS NO SPOON! As the fork still bounces on the table, Billy Joe slowly shifts his stare from the angry Alejandro to the camera as he points to his Mexican buddy. BJ: Izzee mad? Alejandro starts banging his head on the table. As Billy Joe is laughing at Alejandro, a waiter comes up to them. Waiter: Sir- Billy Joe: THUR IS NO SPOON! Waiter: Ehrm, quite. The waiter turns his attention to Alejandro, who is now slightly calmer. Waiter: Mister Rodriguez, there is a gentleman on the telephone who wishes to speak to you urgently. Alejandro turns to the waiter. Alejandro: I HAVE a f*cking cell phone. The waiter goes to say something but Alejandro cuts him off as he gets up. He gestures to the waiter to lead the way, and before he follows him he snaps his fingers at Billy Joe, who now turns to him. Alejandro: You’d better behave yourself, you hear me? Billy Joe gets a wide, stupid grin on his face. BJ: Yes, maw. Alejandro scoffs as he follows the waiter. Billy Joe starts laughing loudly at his own “joke”. After a few seconds he seems to get bored as he picks his fork up off the ground, takes his knife as well, and starts playing with them as if they were action figures. He is mumbling something that’s barely audible; the camera zooms in a bit on his face to understand what he is saying better. BJ: Can’t just do that… I’m Big Ol’ Billy Joe… en a’m gonna get you, Jak Horner- Billy Joe is cut off abruptly as we see a black bag cover his face quickly. The camera starts swinging around wildly as the loud screams from the terrified restaurant patrons cause loud feedback. The camera suddenly turns to static. A few seconds pass before the camera turns back on, and as it opens up slowly we can see a nice pool area, which is seemingly a couple of stories downward. You guys, I got the camera to work! Will you stop messing with that thing? Tex here’s waking up! The camera turns around quickly to reveal it is actually inside a hotel room. As it finally comes into focus, you can see a very groggy Billy Joe Barker waking up on a couch, and next to him James Whitman, Thomas Williams and Eddie Sanchez are standing and looking at him. James turns to the camera angrily. James: Scott, are you deaf?! Stop f*cking around with that thing already! Scott (from behind the camera): Dude, you know I love being a cameraman. Besides, we need to document th- James: Shut the F*CK up! James sighs and turns his attention back to Billy Joe, who is now almost fully awake. Eddie: Thank GOD! I thought we killed him. BJ: Kill who? Eddie: Shut up and listen, hick! Billy Joe chuckles. BJ: Yuw sound jes like Alee- Eddie: I SAID SHUT UP! Eddie raises his hand to slap Billy Joe, but BJ flinches and Eddie rethinks his next move. James moves Eddie out of the way and stands in front of BJ. James: Listen, BWOAY, we have a serious reason for setting that whole thing up and kidnapping you. The thing is… we need to talk to you about this whole Alejandro thing. You’re the only one he might listen to, and there’s some things we need to know. Thomas: WHOA WHOA WHOA! Everyone turns to Thomas. Thomas: I thought we were just gonna take him out drinking! James slaps Thomas. James: Get the F*CK out of here! Thomas looks genuinely annoyed as he raises his arms up in the air in an “I don’t want anything to do with this” manner as he leaves the scene. Everyone turns their attention to Billy Joe once again. James: So yeah, tell us right now why Alejandro got all evil bad guy on David at MNM. Billy Joe gets a confused look on his face. BJ: Naw, naw. He din- Alleymandrove’s a gud pursin. He’z bin trainin’ me fur mah match with Jak Horne- James interrupts Billy Joe. James: ZAK. WARNER. Billy Joe tilts his head a bit. BJ: Naw, A’m Billy Joe. James slaps his own face and rubs between his closed eyes and nose with the pointer finger and thumb of his right hand. James: DAMN, you’re stupid. Billy Joe gets up and amicably puts his arm around James. BJ: Thur, now, Jimmy, dun’ be suw hard on yerself. Thankfully, just before James loses it, Thomas comes running into the scene. Thomas: GUYS! Come out here quick! James: What IS it, Tho- Thomas: The manager- I think it’s serious. Eddie, James, and Billy Joe follow Thomas to outside of the room as the camera follows them. They meet the manager of the hotel right outside the door of their room. The camera moves a bit to the side and is now viewing the hotel manager from behind James and Eddie. James: What is it? The hotel manager seems to be at a loss for words. Manager: Uhm, I just received a call… James: …AND?! The camera slowly starts moving back a bit, now moving James and Eddie’s backs more into the center of the screen. Manager: Your contract with the MWE wrestling federation has been terminated, I’m afraid. The camera slowly moves further as James and Eddie turn to each other, confused. They turn back to face the manager. James: Wait, what? We’re not employed by MWE. I mean, we have a deal with them about the whole TRT thing, but we’re not… I mean… what else did they say? Manager: Not much, I’m afraid. They just called up and told me to deliver the news in person. Eddie: Wh- huh? This makes no sense whatsoever. James: Yeah, w- They turn to each other as if they just had an epiphany, then they simultaneously turn around and look straight at the camera, which is now quite a bit away from them. James & Eddie: THOMAS!!!! The camera starts swerving around wildly before showing the floor of the hallway moving fast, then voices sounding loudly and- all of a sudden, it cuts to static. The camera opens up again after a few seconds, this time showing Scott Jones’ face. Scott: Alright, I got it; it’s back on. The camera turns around to reveal Thomas Williams is sitting next to him, and they are apparently in a car. The camera turns to between them to show Billy Joe Barker sitting in the backseat with a huge grin on his face. BJ: That wuz fun. The camera turns to a smiling and/or driving Thomas. Thomas: You bet your @$$ it was, Blueberry. And that’s the way it should be. These guys want to get you involved in something you shouldn’t be a part of. Thomas turns to Billy Joe now. Thomas: You listen to me and you listen to me good, Huckleberry, don’t EVER let ANYONE take advantage of ya. If David’s got a problem with Alejandro, or Alejandro’s got a problem with David, or James has a problem with Alejandro having a problem with David, or- The camera pans out a bit so now you can kind of see both Thomas and Billy Joe. Billy Joe starts rubbing his head. BJ: Mah head hertz. A loud honk from an oncoming car sends their car swerving and turns Thomas’ attention back to the road as he quickly maneuvers. Once the car is back on the road and driving decently, he turns his attention back to Billy Joe, this time looking in the mirror. Thomas: Sorry. But you know what I mean. It’s not your problem. You just got to worry about your upcoming match, and having fun. Oh, and you think your head hurts NOW? Just WAIT till tomorrow morning. Scott turns the camera so that we can now view his huge smile. Scott: Ohhhh, YEAH! DRINKIN’ TIME, BIAAAA! The camera turns once again as Thomas hits the brakes hard. The car comes to a screeching halt as Thomas maneuvers the car into a parking space. They stop after a hard thump. They all get out of the big black Navigator and walk in the sudden rainstorm for a few seconds before the rain abruptly stops. As they cross the street Scott turns the camera to view the car. Scott (from behind the camera: James is gonna be maaaaad! The camera turns back around and we see Thomas and Billy Joe walking. Billy Joe is staring at Thomas with a stupefied look on his face. BJ: I tawt thet wuz yur car! Thomas: You TAWT wrong, ya half-@ssed Tweety! Now help me find the place, it’s called Bar 89. It should be on this street. As they walk, they pass a street sign and the camera stops moving. The sign says “Mercer Street”. The camera turns, and now we see Scott’s face. He moves the camera a bit further away from his face with one hand as he puts an imaginary mic to his mouth with the other. Scott: MERCERRRRRRRRRRRR… street. He giddily smiles at the camera before realizing the others have continued walking, and thus he runs to catch up. The camera now focuses on Thomas and Billy Joe again, who are having a conversation of sorts. Thomas: So he’s like 25, has won many football state championships, and knows various Martial Arts. He kickboxed, was in K1 when he was 18, was an enforcer in the mafia, and went to jail for 2 years… He’s been all over. BJ: Yism. Thomas: You even listenin’ to me, boy? BJ: HUR! They stop as Thomas looks up, and the camera follows his stare. They have arrived at Bar 89. Thomas: C’MAAAAAAAAAN! They enter quickly, and find a place to sit at the bar. Scott stays standing so as to get both Billy Joe and Thomas in the scene. Thomas: Barkeep! Three beers! Thomas turns to the camera. Thomas: Hey uh, Scott, if you wanna keep filming, I got no problem, but uh… We’re gonna drink for a bit, so maybe you wanna… I dunno, conserve the battery? Scott: Sure. Just as Thomas turns to the barkeeper to receive his drink, the camera cuts to static for about a second; the camera then turns back on and we see a very similar scene; the only difference being numerous empty beer bottles next to Thomas. He turns to the camera. Thomas: It back on? Scott: Yup. Thomas: Alright, cuz we need this talk so that if he forgets anything he can just look at the tape. Thomas turns to Billy Joe, who has an almost untouched bottle of beer in front of him. Thomas: So, you ready for your big match at Adrenaline Rush, Billy J? BJ: Is mah furs Paper View match evur! Thomas turns to the camera, then back to Billy Joe. Thomas: Did… Did you say “paper view?” ANYWAY, look, never mind. Covell made the Warner/Barker match the first match of the night, after sending the others to ICW. The only reason he let Warner get a match against you and forego the ICW thing is probably because of Warner’s history. Billy Joe tries to take a sip of his beer but he doesn’t seem to like the taste. Thomas ignores this as he continues talking. Thomas: Covell probably thinks Warner is gonna massacre you. You may have been in MWE for a few more weeks than he has, but Warner’s been fighting for at least six or seven years. BJ: But ah’v bin dream’n o’- Thomas: Dreaming of being a wrestler is a lot different from being one. You DO know that, don’t you? Billy Joe slowly nods. Thomas: On the bright side… there are eleven matches on this card. It’s the biggest, longest one I’ve ever seen. Scott (from behind the camera): That’s what she said. Thomas: Shut up, Scott! Anyway… Billy, your match is first. Meaning you don’t have to wait through all those other matches. You get to go in fresh. As a matter of fact, it’s all on you to set the tone of this Pay-Per-View. Billy Joe gets a surprised look. BJ: ME?! Thomas: YESSIR, YOU! I mean think about it, we all know Zak Warner is gonna make the match a success… He HAS a history. You… you’re relatively new. I mean, these people have seen you wrestle, sure, but this is your first big time match… You have GOT to prove that you deserve to be here. You have to prove to everyone that you’re MWE material! BJ picks his bottle up and slams it onto the bar, hard. BJ: YESM! Thomas: Yeah? BJ: A’m gunna make this match one fer the hist’ry books! Thomas pats him on the back. Thomas: Warner doesn’t stand a CHANCE! You’re gonna beat him up good, arencha? BJ: But I like th’ Big Tymers! Thomas: What?! BJ: Nufn. Thomas lets out a big sigh before getting a very serious look on his face. Thomas: Look. This guy? This Warner? He’s got a history of raising hell. And you? You’re just a no-name hick. A cowboy stereotype. You didn’t even win your first tag match. Billy Joe gets a sad look on his face. BJ: But ah won mah first singles ma- Thomas grabs him by the shoulder and shakes him violently. Thomas: EXACTLY! EX-AC-TLYYYYYY! BJ looks very confused. BJ: Wh- Thomas: You’ve got to beat Warner. Listen, this guy is all serious, all about trying to get into his opponent’s mind. He uses big words and intimidation. Lucky for you, you’ve GOT no mind, and you prolly don’t understand half of the words he’s saying! Billy Joe shrugs. BJ: Troo. Thomas: Look, you beat Zak Warner, and you’re guaranteed to get another shot at being a tag team. But you start getting into a losing streak, and you might get demoted down to ICW. You don’t want that, do you? BJ: NAW! Thomas: Exactly. Now look, this guy- he’s good. He’s been a TV champ- BJ: I LIKE TV! Thomas: A US Champ- BJ: I LIKE THE U.S.! Thomas: A Tag Champ- BJ: I LIKE TAGS! I GOT ONE ON MAH MATRESS! Thomas chuckles. Thomas: You idiot. Look, he’s won a sh*tload of titles, you gonna keep interrupting me? BJ takes a while to think. Thomas: Never mind. I doubt it’s impressing you or changing your mind on him anyway. Billy Joe shrugs as he nods “yes” and takes a sour sip of his beer. Thomas smiles at him. Thomas: You know, for an uneducated country bumpkin you’re smarter than most of us when it comes to SOME things. BJ smiles. BJ: RULLY? Thomas: Yeah, ya half-@ssed Scooby-Doo. Now hey, look, Warner’s held some deathmatch title and the havoc title too, so that means he knows how to get down and dirty as well. Which means he’s got you beat in pretty much every aspect, except a couple. One is heart- which don’t mean SHYAT! BJ chuckles. Thomas: And two; Height and weight which means every chance you get to get a power move in you need to completely obliterate him with ALL your strength- BJ: Yes’m. Thomas: You need to pulverize him into a fine powder. He can’t be fast if he can’t move, right? BJ: Yizm. Thomas: And last… he likes to be in charge. He’s arrogant, and confident, and likes to think he’s the man… so what you do is, you let him think it. For a bit. Then, you KICK HIM IN THE FACE! Thomas grabs Billy Joe by the collar. Thomas: YOU KNOCK HIM DOWN! YOU TAKE AWAY THE POWER! You teach him that this ain’t “Who wants to be an MWE star”! This is ADRENALINE RUSH! BJ: ADRENALINE RUSHHHHHHHHAH! Thomas: AND IT MAY BE CLOSE TO HIS HOMETOWN, BUT IT’S CLOSER TO YOUR HOMETOWN! And that’s the cozy, relatively unknown little town named BILLY JOE BARKER, FUTURE MWE MEGASTAR! Billy Joe slams his fist hard on the bar as he starts waving his right arm around. BJ: uYEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEHARW! He settles down after a couple of seconds, and looks at Thomas: BJ: I dun get it. Thomas: That’s prolly cuz I’m drunk. BJ: Ah. MOTHERF*CKER! The camera turns towards where the loud yell came from; it’s James Whitman and Eddie Sanchez, both fuming. Holy sh*t The floor of the bar zooms in at an alarming pace before the screen turns to static.
