revenge is a dish best served cold; Milenko Vs Alex Bowen

Oct 25 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
the camera turns on and we see Milenko sitting in a dark corner in the backstage are of the Wachovia Center in Philadelphia, PA. He notices the camera and starts taking in a hushed voice. Milenko Alex after i choked the breath from your lungs at Kingdom Come I thought that we would be finished. I beat you to within an inch of your life and I won the biggest match of both our careers. You have no idea how good it felt to wrap that chain around your neck and feel you try to draw another breath just to find out that you couldn't. Milenko has a sadistic smile on his face as he looks away from the camera and recalls the match. When he looks back at the camera he has fire in his eyes and he starts talking in a choked whisper. Milenko That's the past though Alex and here in the present we have a Hardcore match at Wrestle-Wars II. I read what you put up on MWE.com about us not facing each other here in MWE and I have to say I'm glad you're wrong. Our Kingdom Come match was only the beginning of my revenge. You are the only one I can still get my hands on and the pain I plan on inflicting on you will make you wish you were dead. The smile on Milenko's face gets wider as he thinks about what he plans on doing to his half-brother. Milenko Alex I've been thinking about it and I'm going to tell you what I have planned for you that way you will live in fear of your younger brother. Everything thing that happened to me when we were growing up I will do to you. I was choked and beaten and I did that to you ten-fold at Kingdom Come. I was constantly beaten so bad I wasn't able to move for weeks, At WW-II I will beat you so bad in our Hardcore match you won't be able to move for months. The fire in Milenko's eyes gets brighter as he talks about what he plans on doing to Alex. The sick smile stays on his face and he talks in a normal voice. Milenko If you make it out if WW-II I have other plans for you but I'm going to leave those to myself. The biggest fear is the fear of the unknown, so I'm going to take great pleasure in he fact that you will wake up at nights next to your wife in a cold sweat thinking about what I'm may or may not to do to you. You know what I went through when we were growing up and it must horrify you knowing that the same things are going to happen to you. Milenko leans his head out of the shadows and it can be seen that he is shaking with rage thinking about the pain and humiliation he went through at the hands of his step-father growing up. Milenko they say revenge is a dish best served cold, and I have to agree. Alex the Ice cold hand of revenge is coming for you and when it wraps it's cold dead fingers around your heart you'll wish you were never born. Milenko gets up and walks into the shadows as the camera fades to black.
