The Darkest Alley.; RP #1 vs Marko.

Mar 9 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The scene cuts to a dark and lonely alley. We can hear cars passing by. He sees, an Acura, Daytona, Accord, Concord and many more. All of them seem to be just the color black. Everything seems black. But, it is a dark alley, in New York. The alley is very quiet. All you can hear is cars and crickets making noises. A figure walks out into the open, and is revealed as Triple G. Triple G walks a bit down the street and sees a nice and happy family in a restaurant called “Indian Food Emporium”. Scene 1: Triple G starts to stare at the family. They have 3 kids; one is a little 2 year old baby. He is playing with a ball. Then there is a 5 year old. He is playing with what seems to be his 12 year old sister. There is also a Mom and a Dad. The dad is just watching the kids play while the mom is helping the baby eat his food. Triple G continues to stare until the parents realize. They stare at him with a furious look and shout at him the words: “Get Away”. They put down the curtains and Triple G can no longer see the happy family. He starts to walk away, as the mom had said. Scene 2: Triple G walks down the street. He sees a man with a bag of money. There are police chasing him. The robber hands Triple G the bag of money. The robber runs away. Triple G gets startled by the police. They take the bag from him and run after the robber. They catch him at 6th street. Scene 3: Triple G starts walking back towards the restaurant he was at earlier. He sees the family walking out. He sees how happy they are. This time they are all laughing. What a nice and happy family they are, he thinks to himself. They stare at him for a moment but then walk away. What a happy family, too bad I’m not happy. Triple G starts to walk down to the dark alley again. He sits down next to the wall. He looks around and sees 3 dogs eating out of the garbage. He says Hello to them. They just stare at him and bark. They then continue to eat of the garbage. Triple G: Man. I can’t believe I lost last week. Dammit. How could I lose to Supernova? Marko probably thinks I am not fit for Blood Fuze. But, if that’s what he thinks, he’s wrong. I will prove everyone wrong that I am no joke. Yes, Supernova beat me. But, the losing ends now, today, Forever. No more losing. Losing will not be tolerated in Blood Fuze. Triple G gives a mean stare at the dogs. The dogs whimper and run to another garbage. They stare at Triple G again with a scared expression and eat out of the garbage. Only one of the dogs stayed there. He just kept eating out of the garbage. He didn’t seem to be scared of Triple G. Triple G stares at the dog. The dog stares back. Then continues to eat. Triple G: Marko, you’re my cousin. We are very close to each other. You know that too. Let’s not let this match get too out of hand. We are just here to fight each other and forget about it after. This is just for fun, and I know you know that. I am not going to try to put you out of action. I will just get my finisher and win. That will be it. We won’t have to talk about it again. That’s all. We will just go back to being who we are. Blood Fuze. Remember, Blood Fuze, Forever. Triple G takes a look at the garbage. The dogs are all gone. He now sees a cat. Just standing there. Just Doing nothing. Just staring at Triple G. Triple G smiles at it. The cat then gets scared and walks away. Quickly. Triple G: Geez, what’s with animals these days? Well, whatever. Now is not the time for, for, animals. I got to worry about my match. Against, Markowitz. My cousin, my idol, my friend, my best friend, my mentor. Marko, man, you have always been there for me. Always. Whenever I needed your help you were right there waiting to help. Even when you broke my ankle when wrestling once, you still, stopped and helped me. I never forgot that day. I really appreciated that. I really appreciate you. I love you. You are my cousin, so why wouldn’t I? I hope you realize, that if I hurt you in any way in this match, I don’t mean it. I am only wrestling in this match just to prove who is better. Even if I win, that you are still better than I ever will be. You have taught me every single thing I know today. You are the reason why I love wrestling. The reason why I am even in MNM. So, I thank you for all the things that you’ve done for me. You are the reason why I am who I am today. Thank you. Triple G smiles and thinks back to the “good old days”. He remembers breaking his ankle. The first time he wrestled Markowitz. He looks at the garbage again and sees the dog is back there. Eating again. Triple G is still sitting against the alley wall. It is colder and darker now. Triple G shivers and goes back to talking. Triple G: As I was saying, Marko, you have always been there for me. You were even there for me when my mom.. Died. Those were tough times for me. Also for you. I know you were very close to her. You helped me get through the tough times. Man, I really thank you for that too. It’s because of you that I didn’t decide to just give up on life. It was you. I would never forget these things. Never. Triple G looks at the garbage and now there are 5 dogs. It seems to him that this is the dog alley for eating. Triple G looks up in the sky; he sees a picture of his mom. He shakes his head and it is gone. Triple G: You also helped me when my dad mysteriously left my mom when I was 14. It was hard but you made it easier. I had no friends. You were my only one. I looked forward to seeing you every time. You were the only person I had to talk to. My childhood wasn’t the one everyone wanted. You helped make it better. I will never ever forget what you did. Triple G: Man, I am not too happy to face you. I don’t really want to hurt you. But, it seems that you want to do this. So, I will do it for you. I will do anything for you. You are my favorite person to be with. My best friend. The only one I have in my life. Marko, good luck. Good Luck to you. I know you can do many things. But, this is one thing you won’t do, I promise I will beat you and show that I am good. Show you that I am no joke. Thanks for everything. Oh and, I accept your challenge for the captain of Blood Fuze, Forever. Triple G smiles and the scene fades away as we see 12 dogs eating out of the garbage. END RP OOC: NONE OF THIS RP IS TRUE IN REAL LIFE. (Thank You For Reading)
