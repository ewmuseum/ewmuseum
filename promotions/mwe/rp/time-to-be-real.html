Time to be real; I think this is personally my best RP ever written

Aug 5 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The scene opens in Peter’s room. He’s fast a sleep, lying on his stomach, with his right cheek on the pillow with half of his left leg and entire upper body outside of the blanket. It is seven forty-five am when his alarm clock goes off. He wakes up with his eyes barely open, he lifts himself up slowly, and he just shakes his head back and forth and doesn’t know what is going on. Suddenly Jason enters the room and we get to see what a mess it is. There is a half filled coke cola bottle, two liters, on his radiator, his clothes are all over the floor as if his closet and dresser threw up all over his floor, there are cobwebs under his tv stand with dust bunnies and just visible dirt, and finally his garbage is completely over flowing with some of it just completely rolling off and onto the floor below it. Jason looks at Peter with a face full of disbelief and utter confusion. He doesn’t know what to think, does a man live here or an animal? How did he ever get a women with a room like this? Am I getting fat? Finally Jason decides to speak in a voice in which he tries to mask his anger, but it ultimately gets through. Peter remains oblivious to the situation and treats his conversation with Jason as if it were a joke. Jason: So, um, I see you decided to, um, choose a festive way to, um, DECORATE your room. Peter: Yeah its a little messy, but hey a little dirt never killed anyone. Peter laughs at his little joke, as Jason stands there dumb founded. He looks at Jason funny wondering why he isn’t laughing along. Jason is to busy scanning the entire room over and over and over again to laugh at anything. Suddenly his eyes meet with a confused Peter’s. He begins to speak in a frantic voice. However Peter remains laid back and sarcastic. Jason: Please tell me this clusterfuck of a room is joke. Peter: It could be if I brought in a clown. Jason: Enough with the jokes. Tomorrow you and I are going to be doing a whole lot of cleaning. Peter: Why not today? Jason: Because today we are apparently going to see your room, the zoo. Peter: Have fun with that one. I’m going to remain right here, while you watch animal planet live. Jason: No you're coming with us. Peter: Whose us? Are you experiencing a case of dissociative identity disorder? Jason: No, and stop using your psychology degree on me. By us I meant you, me, and Natasha. Peter leaps out of his bed and lands towards the end of it, his blanket falls onto the floor and onto some ants. The of seeing Natasha again not only excites him, bit gives him a chance to rectify their last encounter. He places his hands, that are closed to make a fist, on his hips. He looks like a super hero staring into the night, all he is missing is the cape. He looks at Jason confidently with his eyes burning with love’s passion. Jason just cracks up and begins to speak with his words being interrupted by laughter. Jason: Okay.... Super.... Peter. Just...... don’t forget your cape. He burst into laughter and falls on the floor. He begins to punch and the kick the floor as a little tear falls from his eye from laughing so hard. Peter just stares at him angrily with a look of disgust on his face. He is wearing his trademark angry scowl that he got when he was working at Biggies Clam Bar. He suddenly gets that evil doctor Cox smile on him from Scrubs. His smile just says that he has something bad in mind and know that this will hurt his friend. A deranged smile that when you see it, you know something bad is coming. He speaks in a playful, yet serious tone. Peter: You know your on my floor. Peter chuckles obnoxiously as Jason jumps off the floor and shrieks like a little girl. His eyes bulge out of his head as he frantically spins in circles wiping himself off. He begins to scream in a frantic voice. Jason: FOR THE LOVE OF GOD I NEED A SHOWER! SOMEONE START ME A SHOWER! OH MY GOD I’M FILTHY! Peter just walks by the trunk sitting at the end of his bed. There is a label on it titled useless junk. He walks by the still spinning Jason and makes his way towards the kitchen. He walks down the white hallway and enters the kitchen. He looks around it is spotless. The kitchen is painted in all different colors, actually its just a bunch of random colors splattered all over the walls. He walks straight towards the fridge and opens it. He looks and sees a barren and desolate fridge. He mutters to himself. Peter: The guy finds time to make this crappy house sparkle, but not enough time to pick up some God damn milk? Priorities need to be checked. He heads back to his room and looks upon the floor. He seems to be looking for an outfit to wear as he has his eyes set on the pile of shirts on the floor. He just shakes his head as he checks out all of his shirts lying on the floor. Suddenly he sees a plain black shirt hanging in his closet. His eyes widen as he walks towards the shirt. This is the shirt he will wear to the zoo and the shirt that Natasha will remove from him, while they are in her room. Now all he needs to do is find some pants and socks. He searches around frantically. He looks underneath his bed, nothing, he looks in his dressers drawers, nothing, and then he looks in his closet, nothing. Finally he sees some pants on the floor and picks them up. He sniffs and right after makes a cringed face like someone just farted right in his face. He throws on the bed and grabs a can of axe body spray. He unloads half of the can onto the pants and then proceeds to smell them again. There not bad, but they reek of axe fumes. So he runs off into the living room. He leaps over the couch and searches around the room for a particular item. He finally sees that its right next to him on the coffee table. He picks up a can of frebreze. He hops over the couch again and runs into his room. He sprays some frebreze onto the pants and smells them one last time. He comes back up with a smile on his face and a thumbs up. Now all he needs to complete his wardrobe is a pair of socks. Instead of looking around his room he just walks across the hall and into Jason’s room. He makes a quick left as soon as he enters the room and opens up the top drawer and takes Jason’s last remaining socks. He leaves the room with a devious smile on his face. He sits on the bed and gets himself all dressed up and completes the wardrobe with black high top nikes. He walks back into the living and turns on sportscenter. He gets the results of the various games that happened yesterday and some of the latest sports news. The times is now eight fifteen when Jason exits the shower and goes into his room. Jason finally comes out of his room at eight twenty and asks Peter in an inquisitive tone. Peter remains stone cold in his innocence. Jason: Dude have you seen my last pair of socks? Peter: Yeah because I do your laundry. Jason is now angry. He is livid at the fact that he knows Peter is lying to him about stealing his last pair of socks. He takes a deep breath and again begins to speak in a voice that shows he is masking his anger. Peter remains sarcastic in a hope that Jason will believe him. Jason: Well can you please explain to me how you got an outfit together when all of your clothes are on the floor? Peter: Your right I just had to steal your clothes because I couldn’t have just easily picked up some of my clothes from the floor, sprayed axe and frebreze on them, and play them off as clean. Jason finally relents, knowing that Peter had a valid argument, and goes back into his room to pick up his flip flops. He walks out of his room and in a defeated tone speaks to Peter. Peter just responds in a laid back fashion, masking his inner desires to see Natasha again. Jason: Alright lets get on with this little adventure. Peter: Lets go then. Both exit the home and walk towards Peter’s piece of shit car. Its a 1998 Pontiac Sunfire that hasn’t been washed in months, has a rejected sticker from the DMV, whose passenger door is bent in, and the inside is just as messy as his room. Peter tosses Jason the keys. He catches the keys and looks down at them and then looks up at Peter. He speaks in a confused tone. Jason: You want me to drive? Peter: Yep. Jason: You never let anyone drive your “baby”. Peter: Well I feel I can trust you Jason. Be gentle, oh so gentle. Jason: Alright man. In reality Peter just didn’t want to pull up to Natasha’s house driving that piece of crap. He was already in bad shape with her and if he pulled up in that piece of crap he’d have no shot with her. Jason starts the car as Peter sits in the passenger seat and they both buckle up. Jason drives down three houses and pulls into Natasha’s drive way. Jason honks the horn as the sexy latina makes her way out of her home. Peter’s jaw drops at the sight of her. She is wearing a low cut tank top and short jeans and is looking extremely sexy. He quickly exits the car to open the door for her and pulls up the seat to let her in the back. Natasha is shocked to see the type of car that Jason is driving, thinking it belongs to him. She looks around and sees what a mess it is on the inside. Her jaw is slightly open, but she decides to say nothing. At least Jason has a car. So they get a half an hour into the driving when Justin Bieber comes on the radio. Peter quickly shuts off the radio. Both Natasha and Jason look at him furiously and both say at the same time in angry voice. Jason/Natasha: HEY! I was listening to that. Even though he wants to be the man that Natasha wants, Peter could not hide his hatred for Justin Bieber. He says back in a smug tone, with a little sarcasm and comedy thrown in their. Peter: One that kid makes me want to buy a sniper rifle, buy tickets to his next concert, sit in the back row, and make every teenage boys dream come true. Two Jason I find it a little weird that you listen to Justin Bieber. Is there something you need to tell me because I have nothing against guys like you. Three- Jason quickly cuts Peter off with a snarky remark. Jason: Wait this coming from the guy who is obsessed with Taylor Swift’s songs? Your taking about me? Natasha cracks up in the back seat. She finds it hilarious that a grown man loves songs meant for sixteen year old girls. She can't take it. She has to ask Peter a question about it. Natasha: You’re obsessed with Taylor Swift? Do you want me to plan your sweet sixteen party? Peter: Obsessed is a strong word. Jason begins to sing just to prove Peter’s obsession true. Peter now regrets letting Jason know his secret about Taylor Swift. He knows however that best friends will do stuff like this sometimes. That is why he hasn't told Jason about his interest in Natasha. Jason: You’re on the phone with your girlfriend she’s upset, she’s going off about something that you said, she doesn’t get you’re humor like I do. Peter can’t resist that song and Jason knows it. You Belong To Me is one of Peter’s all-time favorite Taylor Swift songs. He knows all the lyrics and if someone starts to sing it he has to join in or finish it. He begins to sing loud and proud. Peter: I’m in my room it's a typical tuesday night, I’m listening to the music she doesn’t like, she’ll never get your story like I do...... After singing the entire song both Natasha and Jason begin cracking up in the car. Peter sinks a little in his seat as Natasha and Jason continue to laugh. Jason swerves a little into the other lane as he can barely control the wheel from laughing so hard. Natasha is stomping her feet on the ground uncontrollably from laughing so hard. She begins to speak while still laughing. Natasha: I got a sure fire way that you can win your match this week. Sing that song and you’ll have your opponent tapping from laughing so hard. Peter’s face glows bright red as he is humiliated in front of the girl who he has such strong feelings for. Probably the strongest set of feelings he’s ever had for a girl. For the rest of the half hour trip he is ridiculed by his love interest and best friend for his love of Taylor Swift. It was the most embarrassing moment of his entire life. However he has a plan to turn it around. All he needed to do was score some one on one time with Natasha. All he needs to do is get Jason away from them for at the very least ten minutes. As he is walking into the zoo he sees his ace in the hole, a spitting camel. As Jason and Natasha walk into the zoo, Peter secretly pulls over one of the zoo staff. He whispers in the staff man’s ear. Peter: Look I need a HUGE favor from you and I’m willing to PAY handsomely. Staff Man: Alright what do you need? Peter: I need you to get that camel to spit on that guy. He points to Jason. Staff Man: Alright, but it will cost you five hundred dollars. Peter: Alright here. He pulls out his wallet and reluctantly hands the man five one hundred dollar bills. It leaves his wallet barren and empty. However to him its a small price to pay for Natasha. He leaves the staff man’s side. The staff man begins to speak in a showy tone as he tries to get Jason and Natasha’s attention and it works as they step up closer to him. Staff Man: Come one, come all to see the Incredible Spitty Magoo. Peter slaps his head that he paid that man five hundred bones for that. Still he hopes to God it works or else he is screwed. Staff Man: You sir step closer and see the marvels of nature. Jason obliges to the request and steps forward. Suddenly without warning the camel spits on Jason. Staff Man: The wonders of nature. Peter: That should buy me at least twenty minutes alone with Natasha. I can clear up the Taylor Swift mess and climb up out of that hole I’m in. A random man hears Peter and begins laughing. Man: You like Taylor Swift? He walks away as Peter screams defiantly at him. Even though his obsession with Taylor Swift landed him in hot water in the first place. He just had to defend her. Peter: She’s a wonderful singer whose lyrics speak to me. Peter turns around quickly to see Jason has already left Natasha’s side and she is standing all alone. He quickly makes his move. He comes up from behind and grabs her side startling her. When she turns around she is relived to she it was Peter. She speaks in a humorous tone. Natasha: I thought you left us to jam out to some more Taylor Swift. He doesn’t let it phase him because he already has the perfect lie in store. That will definitely work because it has a hint of truth to it. He responds in his normal confident tone. Peter: You actually believed that? Girl you were getting played. You see I bet Jason fifty dollars that if I sang most of Taylor Swift’s You Belong to Me that you wouldn’t laugh. I said that you would find it sensitive and would sing right along with me. Peter already owed Jason fifty bucks so pretending that he lost a bet would work perfect since he owed Jason for a previous bet. So no matter how Natasha worded her question to Jason he would remain bullet proof. However she saw right through the lie and figured he was behind the camel spitting on Jason. So she decided to give him a chance since he went through all of the trouble just to get a chance to be alone with her. She decides to ask him a question about a subject he’d be comfortable with and could be honest with her about. Natasha: So who are you facing this week? Peter laughs and simply points to a piece of elephant shit on the ground. Natasha looks at him with disgust and begins to walk away. Peter chases after her and begins to speak in a confused tone. He doesn’t know what Natasha wants from him. His face seems confused and frustrated at the same time. Peter: What? I told you who I was facing, Ace Cannon. Natasha seems like she is done pussy footing around the situation. She goes directly at Peter’s heart. She speaks in an angry and frustrated tone. Her face is full of disgust and she seems to be sickened at the sight of him. Natasha: You know after you made the effort for the Taylor Swift lie and to get Jason away from me, I thought maybe this guy will stop putting up a front and be honest with me. Instead you decided to be the same douche bag who I met last week. Here’s some advice for you asshole a girl like me wants honesty. Not this game you try and spit. He looks at her with both anger and sadness in his eyes. Natasha reluctantly decides to give him one more chance. She asks him another heart jerking question, however this one is filled with sarcasm and a snarky attitude. Natasha: So what made you want to become a wrestler, the money? The women? What was the egotistical thing that made Peter Kaymakcian decide to make a show of himself? He looks at her because no girl had ever even gave two craps where the money was coming from. Let alone what made him decide to chase the money. He never expected Natasha to actually care about Peter Kaymakcian the person. All everyone ever cared about was Peter Kaymakcian the wrestler, the persona, the in-ring gimmick. He responds in a forth coming tone. He finally decides to maybe delve into his actual emotions instead of putting up a front. Peter: No I didn’t come into wrestling for either of those reasons. To be honest it was the same reason my mentor Brian Micheals became a wrestler. Its the joy we got from beating scum like Ace Cannon. I don’t know how many times Micheals Inc. would celebrate the defeat of someone like Ace Cannon. Probably the same number of times Micheals beat Cannon. I mean the joy we got from pinning scum like Cannon one, two, three was unimaginable. Its sort of like the feeling a child gets when opening up his gifts on christmas. There is a certain magic about it. I guess its because guys like Hollywood(Ace Cannon) walk around like their shit don’t stink even though haven’t done shit. Cannon faced off against Micheals a dozen times and beat him zero times. Yet after ever match the cocky son of bitch acted like he won. He begins to mimic Ace Cannon to Natasha’s delight. His voice sounds like that of a mix between a spoiled sixteen year old girl and a guy who is pretending to act tough. However his face shows that of someone who is finally breaking down the wall between himself and his emotions. Even while keeping his calm, cool, and laid back style of acting his emotions finally seem to be coming to life. Peter: Well I might have lost to Ace Cannon over and over and over again, but at least I’m surrounded by girls who want nothing to do with me. I mean, yeah, I’m so cool for never ever ever beating Brian Micheals, while he’s beaten me several times. Micheals wishes he was this cool, but all he has to his name is multiple titles and an undefeated record against me. I’m so much better then Micheals. I deserve to be better then Micheals. I deserve to win ONCE!!!!!!!! Peter begins to pretend to cry as Natasha continues to laugh. After a few moments he decides to speak as an actor. Peter: And end scene. She finally seems to be getting the real Peter Kaymakcian and not the one shown on MWE or the one who tried to impress her last time they met. She seems to genuinely be able to get the Peter Kaymakcian she wants and decides to dig a little deeper into this Peter, not the suave, cool, All-Star Peter, not rehab Peter, not Hollywood Peter, or the beer drinkin’, pill poppin’, authority defyin’ son of gun Peter, but the real Peter. He seems to genuinely want to show her this guy. The man he has been so desperate to hide from everyone including himself. She continues pressing forward with questions. Her eyes show that she is really liking this open and honest Peter Kaymakcian. Natasha: Wow so you must really hate this guy. Why? He answers openly and honestly. His voice is filled with passion and a fire that he seemed to be missing for a while now. His raw emotions is making his rant against his opponent not only honest, but actually hitting the point. He no longer seems to rely on simple jokes and pointing out the obvious for his promos. He now uses the real world for his promos and his real emotions which makes them so much more meaningful and truthful. Peter: Well as I’ve said before, Cannon is a cocky son of bitch who has accomplished nothing in his entire career. He’s challenged the elite of the elite and has lost every single time. Micheals, lost repeatedly to. Sinc, lost repeatedly to. Derf, lost repeatedly to. His greatest stake to fame is that he let some loser by the name of Joseph Sharpman or whoever he is push him to his limit. His in-ring resume is pitiful. I mean the only reason he is still with MWE is because of how “controversial” he is out of the ring. She sees the hate he has for men like Cannon and decides to push on it. She knows that by pushing on it she’ll get to see more of the man that she wants. She’ll get to see Peter Kaymakcian raw, full of emotions and life. Not that robot presented to MWE fans that gets a script reads it and hopes for a crowd reaction. She speaks as a person who is really curious as to what Cannon did. Natasha: What did he do? Peter is shocked that she had to ask. I mean it should’ve been all over the news when it happened. It wasn’t that long ago, only about a year or a year and a half at most. However he relents and continues on his tear of Cannon. However this time his face full of disgust and rage at the type of man that Cannon is. His face is full of emotions. Anger at Cannon’s actions and word. Confusion as to why or how somebody could be like this. His emotions are finally being set free as he speaks. Peter: Kick a pregnant lady and her baby. Hey Cannon, Snitsky called he wants his gimmick back. I bet you all the money in the world that he’ll make a comment about it. It will probably be along the lines of, “Peter I kicked a pregnant woman and just guess to do you.” Well what that moron fails to realize is that I’m not a pregnant woman. I’m not a helpless woman who can’t defend herself. I’m not a helpless and lifeless fetus who won’t fight back. I’m a man on a mission who wants nothing more then to make Cannon suffer for his sins. The guy deserves to suffer for his sins because while I got fired for my personal demons all he got was a slap on the wrist for his racists comments. Now he gets his job again, while I have to jump through hoops for MWE. I mean I got help, while he is still that racists who thinks its cool and original to degrade an entire ethnic group of people. I can’t wait to face this asshole and show him why Micheals and I couldn’t stand him. I’m going to beat his ass from one side of the ring to the other and then back again and then once more for good luck. The man is a nobody who doesn’t even deserve his roster spot. That is why guys like me and Micheals hate Cannon. What Derf, Sinc, and Micheals all did to Cannon is what I plan to do to him. I plan to pin him one, two, three and then after the bell rings make him tap out. In will rid MWE of Cannon as I will beat him so badly either management will fire him or he’ll leave on his own because of how pitiful I made him look. Natasha seems impressed by the fire Peter is presenting. She sees the true man who lies dormant and who needs to be unleashed if Peter ever wants to truly compete in MWE like he did before and like he did in every other promotion he has ever been in. Natasha has a devilish little smile as she knows that she is helping Peter finally see the fire that will make him a great competitor in MWE. Natasha: You sound a little cocky for someone who just lost last week. He laughs at her last statement. He speaks in a confident tone. He isn’t arrogant or over looking Cannon. He genuinely believes there is no way he can possibly lose this week. His face, his body, everything fiber of his being is showing the passion, the fire, and intensity that led Brian Micheals to hand pick him for Micheals Inc. and the future of MWE. His voice rings out through the zoo like an alpha wolf’s howl through the cold wintery forest. He is now showing signs of being an alpha wolf, rather then just a pack wolf. Peter: Look I’m not cocky, I’m confident for good reason. Lets look at our respective title histories. Cannon is a one time tag team champion in which his partner carried him to gold. I on the other hand am a five time world heavyweight champion, a nine time tag team champion, a Canadian champion, an extreme champion, a two time international champion, and the last ever ICW National Champion. Is it just me or are our championship histories a little loopsided? Not to mention my mentor, Brian Micheals, Cannon could never beat. So if he couldn’t beat the man who taught me all of my tricks, then why should he be able to beat me? I know all of the tricks Micheals used against Cannon and those tricks beat him numerous times. She keeps pushing Peter onward. She wants him to see the man he was, the man he is, and the man he is capable of being. She is using every sentence as a tool to motivate Peter to greatness. To get him so psyched up for his match against Cannon that there is no way in hell that he could lose. She keeps trying to free Peter from the chains that prevent him from being a huge success and future hall of famer in MWE. She speaks in a sly and uncaring tone to mask the point of her trying to motivate Peter. She wants him to think that he got himself psyched up. Natasha: You got a point there. He hears the way she said that last sentence as if she was unimpressed by his resume. She sounded as if all of the gold he has and Micheals’ teachings aren’t enough for him to beat Cannon. He speaks with passion and fury to drive home the point to everyone who has been doubting him that he is ready. That he is better then Ace Cannon. He knows he has made some mistakes, but he starts rectifying those mistakes NOW! Peter: Plus Brian Michels, an MWE legend, hand picked me to be the future of MWE and I’ve been letting him down for a while now. So starting with Cannon I plan to rectify that situation. I know Micheals saw great talent in me and now its time to show the entire world and everyone in MWE exactly why. I know that everyone doubts me and expects me to lose to Cannon this week, but I don’t care what they think. I know I can win and I know I will win. They don’t think I hear them in the halls talking about me and how they pick every other guy I go against, but I do. They think that I am going to just sit their and give up and prove them right. And they are right to think so because I never gave them a reason not to. That stops now though as I make each and every MWE superstar who said that I wasn’t good enough to beat Cannon eat their words. As far as I’m concerned they can take their predictions and shove it straight up their ASS! No longer will I squander my talent and be the whipping for guys, no for losers like Ace Cannon. No longer will I be the laughing stock of MWE and the resident jobber for anyone who wants to move up a level. No longer will I be this MWE’s Devlin Sharpe! Natasha so filled with joy to see the real Peter Kaymakcian stops him at that sentence with a passionate and love filled kiss. He doesn’t fight the kiss and begins to embrace her as the scene fades to black.
