Better then nothing; Derf vs Paige

Oct 23 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The scene opens in a dark room. The small ammount of light that pours through an open window reveals a fully furnished room. A 45" Flatscreen TV hangs on the wall next to the window, a red leather couch and chair are placed in the center of the large room with a coffee table in the middle of them, and a nice rug under it all. But most noticable is Eric Derf, standing next to the window, just barely escaping the light. His face, although barely noticable, isn't his usual overt excited face. Rather, it's a dim, semi serious face. These past few days have been quite fucked up for me. People go through problems in life, and this is no different. My best friend just got signed to MWE, which is awesome. But now his ego has become huge, and he'ss never around. While he used to help me with my promos and all my training, now he's become seft centered around himself, I can't get that help anymore. He's still my best friend, but he's useless to me when it comes to MWE, simply because he is now one of us. Eric takes a drink out of a glass we couldn't see he was holding. The contents of the glass can't be seen due to the dark, but the clink of ice cubes can be heard as he literally takes a sip. One would assume he's drinking some kind of alcohol, which could explain his emo-ness. And then there is that crazy ass bitch, Katie. I blew her off a few weeks ago, told her how it was. And it actually worked. I havn't heard a word from her since. No phonecalls, no text messages, nothing. It's as if she actually disapeared from my life, just like I requested. But yet, I feel lonely. That's it, Eric has GOT to be drunk now. His words arn't slurring, but then again, not all drunk people slur words. I might not have liked it, but Katie quickly became one of the closest people to me. I have fans out there, but none of them have been able to make me feel as good about myself as she did. Sure, she came off as obsessive, and yeah she was old enough to be my mother, but in the end, she cared about me, and I basically treated her like crap. In trying to be a better person, I've taken the two most important people out of my life. And now, a third person is also walking away, that man being DC. It was announced last week that he was being forced to take time off, which sucks for me. All I want to do is prove myself against one of the best, and he refused me time and time again. I'm sure I'd have gotten that match eventually, but he had to go do something fucking stupid, get himself sent home. And now, I stand in my apartment, just after the sun has set, too depressed to turn on a light, drinking this fucked up mix of the little bits of alcohol I could find in my fridge, with only one thing in mind. He takes another sip. Yeah, there's a pause in speach, but not for dramatic effect, but because Eric lets out a quiet, slurred burp. In my career, I've done so much. I won the XTV Title before ANYONE even knew Eric Derf was in MWE. By the time fans figured it out, I already held the title. I merged that very title with the Havoc Title a mear two weeks later. I was the man that created what we now call the X-Havoc Championship, a title currently worn my a great man, Zack Crash. I defeated DC once before, in his first match in months. I took the momentium train he was riding, kicked him off, and hopped into the drivers seat. And for anyone that don't remember, I ASKED for that match after to so called "Semi" jesse Anderson backked out because he was scared. But I wasn't scared, I'm fearless. Devlin Sharpe once tried to attack me from behind, he even put me in the hospital. But not even that could stop me from coming back and facing the man face to face. He tried to make a name off of me, and now where is he? Exactly. And for those of you that have yet to put two and two together, I was THEE very last man to pin the legend that is Brian Michaels. I made a promise to myself that I would beat him, I made a promise to the fans, to my fans, that I would prove I'm main event material, and then I went out there and proved it. Eric goes to take another drink from his glass, only to realize it's empty. And he already said once today that he just drank the last of his liquor. So now with nothing to wet his whistle, he playfully tosses the glass out his apartment window, a moment later the glass can be barely heard hitting the ground, shatering into shards. Where was I... Oh yeah. After I beat Brian Michaels, I went into a rut, I disapeared for a few months. But who doesn't? Everyone has their hard times, that was mine. And you know what I did when those hard times were over? I won Money in the Bank. Eric points over in the distance, probably at the breifcase. However, the camera don't move, it's set in position. Eric is just to drunk to notice. I beat seven other guys, and won a guarenteed title match at any point in the future, I went through hell to do that, and people STILL doubt me as a main eventer. The fans continued to cheer me each and every week, but the higher ups still don't see my potential. The very next show, in a tag team match, I pinned the World Champion, only for him to hide his embarrassing loss behind a technicality. True, neither of us were the legal men, but to those that ain't blind, I think I proved a point. I pinned the current World Champion, and legal or not, that should show someone that I'm main event material. Yet the people that matter have yet to see it. The people I need to impress are to busy giving pay raises to people that don't deserve them, too busy taking care of their own reputations. Eli Silver, for example, won't even listen to me anymore when I make mention of his "buddy" DC. He's too busy filling DCs wallet and ego with undeserved praise. So here I stand, trying to continue to make a name for myself. Suddenly, Eric stumbles backwards, the alcohol finally taking a real effect on him. yet he continues on his promo, or more or less right now, just an angry drunken rant. And now we come to Turmoil. And I get a match against my first ever Hall of Famer, Paige. A good friend of DC, but obviously a better competitor if she's in the Hall of Fame and he's not. DC might claim he turned ddown the offer because he'd have to retire, but look at what we have here now. A Hall of Famer coming out of retirement. And to be honest, I'd have to say now that I got a match against Paige, I don't need DC anyways. If I can beat a Hall of Famer, then surely I'll have proven myself worthy of the Main Event. So enough about DC, I've got to focus on Paige more... Eric stops talking for a moment as he stands in place. It might not look like it to the camera, or the average fan, but the room is spinnning round and round and round... Ugh... Paige... My head hurts... Paige is an ass kicking, tough ass, son of a bitch, wait what? Eric is clearly now out of it, he can't even cut a proper promo on his opponent. Paige thinks she can beat me? Psh, she's a gir- Wait, no, I can't say that... It's pretty obvious what Eric was about to say, and pretty obvious it's not a very smart attack on an opponent like this. Ok, I'll admit it. I've never hit a girl in my life. There, I said it. I'm a terrible person for never hitting a girl, and I'm even more terrible for not wanting to hit one now. I got myself into a match that I never really wanted, and now either I have to hit a girl or get hit by a girl and I don't wanna hit a girl but I don't have a choice but to hit a girl or I'll get hit by a girl and then- Eric inturupts himself with a sound no one wants to hear, as well as a sight no one wants to see. That would be vomit. Not alot of it, but enough to gross someone out. It hits the ground, making a wet squishy sound. Lovely. Oh man, I'm so not ready for this... but I gotta do this. I need to straiten myself up, get to training, go out there on Sunday, and just fucking kick her ass, pin her, and get it over with. Wow, maybe this is why so many people get beat by her, because they're to a-scared to hit a girl. I doubt it though, most guys here are cavemen, they're not gentlemen like I am. I'm a nice guy, people love me. Will people still love me after this? Oh god, what am I doing... Eric shakes his head a bit, trying to clear his mind and think strait for a moment. It's not likely going to work though. Ok, so this is how it is. Paige. I respect you in that ring. You're an awesome wrestler, and you're just flat out tough to boot. And I'd not likely admit this so easily if I was sober-er, but it's going to be a great honor to fight you in that ring. But once I sober up, I might not like it, but I will hit you if I have to, I will give it my all, I will do everything in my power to get that win. I have so much more to prove then you'd even think possible. To face ANY Hall of Famer, it's a privlidge, and I'm honored to face you. But once Sunday comes, may the better competitor win. And I think we all know what I mean by that. Because I have no plans whatsoever to lose. Bank on it. Eric stands in his darkend window for a moment staring into the camera before collapsing backwards. He must have drank more before the promo lol.
