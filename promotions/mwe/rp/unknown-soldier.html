Hell, even I went home to get some sleep. I figured that there was nothing I could've done, so instead of sleeping by myself in a cold hospital, I went home and slept. You were fine, don't worry." Kevin breathes another sigh of relief as he lays back...and immediately shoots up again, grunting again over the pain in his body as he lies back. Hayley seems confused and bewildered at what just happened. "What's wrong?" "I forgot...Angie. How is she doing? Is she still okay?" She gives off a brief smile and looks at the curtain. She grabs it gently and looks at Kevin. "Umm...why don't you ask her for yourself?" She pulls back the curtain to reveal who was Kevin's roommate for the time being. He sees this and smiles. Just like in his dream. The most perfect smile, as he looks at the beauty laying next to him. She turns her head over...awake and alive. Like in the dream, she also gives the perfect smile. She slowly moves her hand up and waves gently, mouthing off the words... "Hey, love." Angie. Alive and well.
