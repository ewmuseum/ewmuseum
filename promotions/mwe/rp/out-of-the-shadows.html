Out Of The Shadows; Debut RP of Oblivion

Jul 21 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It was a cold and rainy day in Chicago. People‘s mood were obviously down. A common side effect of such a day. One such person was not in a good mood. A completely masked man, wearing a beige trench coat, was running down the streets, trying to avoid everyone. Plus he was running a bit late. He was heading to a costume shop, but it was about to close in ten minutes. Luckily for the man, he got there in time. The Shopkeeper noticed him, and smiled. Shopkeeper: Welcome to Rick’s costume shop. We have- Masked man: -I already know. I have an order here for some wrestling masks. Shopkeeper: Okay then. I just need your name for the order. Mr….. Masked Man: Oblivion. Shopkeeper: Right. The shopkeeper typed in some words. The man adjusted his glasses, smiling when the order had been correct. He wrote down a number, before heading to the back. In the meantime, the masked man, now known as Oblivion, looked around the store. He didn’t have to wait long, before the shopkeeper came back with a medium sized, 2 feet by three feet box. Oblivion took out a Swiss army knife. This startled the shopkeeper. Shopkeeper: Sir….please don’t do anything stupid. Oblivion: Keep acting the way you are and I might just. Oblivion cut open the top of the box to look in. He saw the amount of masks he ordered, and in the exact size, and with some lightning designs on it. He nodded. Oblivion: Good. Everything is in order here. Be glad that it is, punk. Shopkeeper: W-w-will that be everything? Oblivion: Yes. Shopkeeper: Th-th-that will be $151.87 in total. Oblivion grunted, as he took out his wallet. He pulled out five twenties, a fifty, and a ten. He gave the shopkeeper the money, and the shopkeeper gave him some change. Oblivion then took out some tape, to re-tape the slash he had made. He then put the box into a garbage back, which he had take out form his pocket, so as to not get wet by the rain. One hour later, the sky had started to become night. The rain had slowed down, much to Oblivion’s relief. He had also arrived at a small, empty warehouse lot which he occupied. His safe house. He had converted most of it into a makeshift apartment, which included a private room for himself, and a shower. He put his bag down, and stripped off his jacket, and hung it on a coat rack, just as he turned on the light for his place. The spacious living quarters lit up. Oblivion then went to his table, which he had right smack dab in the middle of the place. He looked over the mail he had gotten earlier. One was a package. He ripped it open, to see something that made him smile underneath his scarf. It was his official MWE contract. He looked it over very carefully. He saw nothing wrong with it. He immediately found a pen, and signed the papers, opting to return the contract tomorrow to their headquarters. He then noticed that there was a plane ticket and a reservation for him, when he arrived. Oblivion: I guess they like to keep their employees happy. Good. Sick and tired of hiding like this. Eventually, I’ll just get a nicer place. Easier to relax. Although, this is a nice enough place as it is. Oblivion then looked at his watch. He sighed. The ting was, Oblivion, towards helping his skills as a wrestler, often competed in free for all events, at the local gyms and such as an event he had signed up for today. The event started at 8pm. An hour and a half away. He went over to the nearest shelf, and grabbed a can of pasta. His supper for the night. Thirty minutes later, Oblivion had stepped out of his room after having a shower. He wore one of his new masks, as he slipped on a different jacket. He grabbed one of his wrestling outfits, some knee and shoulder pads, a pair of gloves, and stuffed it into a duffel bag. He promptly left his place with his stuff, and went to the nearby street, where an empty cab was sitting there. Oblivion smiled, knowing that he could get this one. He ran up to it, and knocked on the window. The Taxi driver rolled the window down. Taxi driver: Need to go somewhere sir? Oblivion: Yeah. Take me to the Lincoln Park High School. Taxi driver: Okay sir. Oblivion immediately got into the back of the cab. Once the door closed, the taxi driver began to drive. Luckily it only took the Cab only 10 minutes to get to the school, as it was a lot closer then Oblivion thought it would be Oblivion (thoughts): Hm… Next time I have a match here, I’ll just walk. Yeah. Changes venues often enough. Oh well. Taxi driver: That will be $13.50 sir. Oblivion grunted, as he reached for his wallet. He got out the exact change for the fare, and handed it to the driver. He then left the cab. He walked into the school, where he saw the receptionist there. She smiled, as she greeted him. Receptionist: Hello Sir. You here for the wrestling event? Oblivion: Yeah. I’m one of them. Receptionist: Ah. Your name? Oblivion: Oblivion. Receptionist: Right. The gym is located in the back of the school, and you’ll see the assigned locker rooms there. Oblivion: Yeah…..Thanks. Oblivion immediately began walking to the back of the school, to get ready for his match. An hour and forty five minutes later, Oblivion was in the ring, as his match was about to start. The crowd was booing him, like normal. He just smiled, taking in all the heat. He then requested a mic. Oblivion: Do you people honestly think that I give a fuck what you’re saying? It’s like this each and every single motherfucken time. You people don’t like me, because You know that I don’t mince my fucken words. When I say I’m going to do something, I do it, without any regard of human life. He then heard a woman shout out ‘There are children in the crowd, stop swearing’. Oblivion managed to locate who said it. It was a rather attractive looking brunette mother in the front row, with what looked like, her nine year old son. Oblivion continued to smirk, looking right at the woman Oblivion: Who asked your opinion, grade A slut. The small crowd that was there immediately hushed in surprise. Oblivion chuckled to himself, seeing the reaction of everyone. Oblivion: Oh yeah. Say kid. Do you know how kiddies like you are made? Well it all starts, when a man fucks a woman. The kid’s face was even in more shock then anyone else’s. Everyone started to boo him badly. Oblivion just continued. He was loving this. It was so easy for him to get into other people’s heads, as he had been doing this, all his life. Oblivion: Oh yeah. Ask your mom. And tell her to not hold back any details. Not every single time it’s successful, but then there are those who actually try to make little cockblocks such as yourself. Oblivion’s already present smirk grew as he saw the kid starting to cry. People were starting to throw their garbage at Oblivion, who effortlessly dodged most of it. Oblivion: Easy easy there people. You’re not supposed to start throwing garbage at me until after I whip this little assfuck’s ass six ways until Sunday. Jeez, learn some fucken patience. Oblivion was then shoved by his opponent, who was a 5’10, 200 pound, short blond haired wrestler, who’s apparent gimmick was a face army guy. Oblivion then turned around, staring at the guy, with pure malice in his eyes. Oblivion: You shouldn’t have done that. I wasn’t done with these asswipes. If you’re so desperate to get your sorry ass pinned, then lay down. Oblivion turned around, totally ignoring his opponent. He then smirked again, once again focusing on the audience. He looked at the kid again Oblivion: Kid. Do me a favor. Grow the fuck up, because all I’m doing is telling the truth. I might be one with the shadows, but I hold nothing back. Not my words, not my skill, and certainly, not my total disregard for human life. The life of my opponents that is. More heat came Oblivions way. He just soaked it all in, loving the fact that he was easily able to make everyone hate him. Oblivion: But let me ask you something. Have any of you just signed a big contract? The answer, is no. Not the MILF in the front row. Not her pussy of a son. Not the fat man in the back, and certainly not the guy who just whipped off his shirt. Dude, do me a fucken favor, and introduce the words soap and deodorant into your vocabulary, because I can smell you all the way over here. The booing continued. The guy who had taken his shirt off even threw it at Oblivion. Oblivion caught it, and faked a vomit motion, before he tossed it back. He then looked at his opponent. Oblivion: And certainly not you. See, I just signed onto MWE. One of the absolute best Wrestling companies in the world, that all started, right here in Chicago. Now that announcement actually got people to cheer for him. This made Oblivion laugh to himself. He was so amused on how quickly everyone had went from booing him, to cheering him. Oblivion: I know you people love MWE. But I’m sure each and every one of you, along with the rest of the inbred population of America, hell most of the world, will still be booing me, the day I solidify myself as top dog, wearing the MWE world championship. And when I do, you’ll have no choice but to get used to that sight, because I’m going to be the man for a far longer time then anyone else will be. But enough of this. Time that this dolt finds out that being in the army means shit in the real world. Oblivion handed the mic back to the timekeeper. The ref rang the bell, and the match had officially begun. About five minutes later, Oblivion jumped off the rope, doing his signature move, the ‘Shadow Splash’. It was successful. He then got the clean pin. The crowd erupted because it was such a good match. Oblivion was announced the winner, as he went to the back. A few of the other wrestlers there all gathered around him, and poured some champagne over him. It was their way of congratulating him on not only his win, but his recent contract signing. The wrestler who he just beat, even congratulated him. Army wrestler: Dude. Awesome way to get the crowd all pissed off. Been a while since anyone has done it as good as you. Oblivion: Thanks. Army Wrestler: Hey boys, what do you say, we hit the bar, and we celebrate with this guy? The whole locker room exploded in cheers. Even Oblivion was happy. Mostly because everyone in the back had accepted him. Oblivion: Yeah Yeah. Ought to be good. Anyways, I’m gonna watch the rest of the fights now. The guys in the locker room agreed, as Oblivion got his stuff, and left. He changed back into his normal attire in the nearest washroom. After that, he found a decent spot underneath the bleachers, and took out a pen and his journal. He began to write in it. Oblivion’s Journal. July 22nd, 2009. Do some days go from bad to good? I believe in that. Today was a bad day for the weather, and the buses were running late. Barely was able to get all that I needed done. But once I actually got to my wrestling event, everything turned out better. No, that’s a lie. Everything was better when I got home. My contract for MWE has been finalized. It was a great deal. Very few that you find this day in age. Even for a guy like me. An ex bounty hunter and assassin. Anyways. I got to the event, and I did my part, like I always have done. I’m a natural at getting into people’s heads, and make them hate me. It’s what I’ve just about done my whole life, so yeah, it’s second nature. Regardless. I am very pleased, that in a world going to hell on a bullet train, opportunity for something like this does exist. I don’t know what I’ve done that I deserve this, but why the fuck should I worry. It’s probably just karma rewarding me after being dragged away from a normal life. I’m also up against a second generation wrestler. Well, more like pseudo second generation. Jai Williams. Brother to legendary MWE wrestler, Sonny Williams. A man, who went from absolute bottom, and did it all. How nice. Ugh. I read everything about the two of them. How they lost a brother due to street violence, and apparently, Sonny is never able to forgive himself. Makes me sick on how not only it shows how much of a pussy the dude ism but he kept talking about it, on and on, making him seem like an emo. He should learn to move on. But I guess Sonny, much like most people in this world, don’t have thick skin like I do. I’ve been ending people’s livelihoods since I was at least twelve. It’s part of who I am. But Enough about him, and onto Jai. All I see in Jai, is a guy who has a bigger shadow over him, then anyone else. I know this for a fact, because of Sonny Williams. No doubt, Jai will do his best to make his own name. Maybe he’ll be successful, but probably not. All he’ll be is the brother of Sonny Willaims. Nothing more. Nothing less. I guess that’s how it is with second and third generation talent. They use their forefathers (or in Jai’s case, as mentioned before, his brother) legacy to elevate them to even bigger things, with far less skill. That’s the main difference between me and Jai. I’m sure He could take the lazy way, but something tells me he won’t. I will give him respect for that. But I have no choice but to forge my own legacy. I wouldn’t have it any other way of course. Nevertheless, I stand by what I say. Jai Williams will forever be in his brother’s shadow. And I can tell that he hates it. Well that works to my advantage. For when we will fight, I will whisper ‘you’ll never be better then Sonny’. I will taunt him. Make him go crazy. To the point that when he thinks of him being in his brother’s shadow, he’ll remember my words. He will a very hard time focusing, and will become reckless. Making more opportunities to strike. Before you know it, he’ll be at my mercy. He’ll look up at me, at my face, begging for me to show him some mercy. But I will not. Why should I? Why should I have to show mercy to anyone, when mercy has never been given to me? Everyone is a fool who thinks otherwise. That is the game plan at least. Knowing my opponents strengths and weaknesses are key. All my years of what I’ve done, It’s only natural that I apply my skills. Efficiency is key in a lot of elements, and there is just something about men squirming in fright when they think of me, and what I’m capable of, that gives a different kind of high. I like it still. I new chapter in my life has opened up at this point. No longer having to travel the world and not being able to enjoy everything, because you had to work for people who used you as a means to an end in shady deals. It was money. Good money. But this new direction isn’t about the money. It’s good pay, but it’s not about it. It’s all about pain, aggression, and even in some sort of way, redemption. Don’t know why I’m thinking that, but it is. As I write this, I write it as Oblivion, a man trying to escape the true horrors of this world. By the end of this journey, I want to me writing as a man who has conquered the world. Starting with Jai Williams, that is what will happen. I am officially stepping out of the shadows of the underworld, because for to long, have I been in the shadows of this world. END
