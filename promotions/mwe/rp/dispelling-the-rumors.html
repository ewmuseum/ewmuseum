Dispelling the Rumors.

Dec 10 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
** The camera opens up down a long corridor. The corridor is just like your average one, the walls are made of cinderblock and painted an off white color, overhead are fluorescent lights that burn bright and white, and the floor is you average white tile with some specs of red and blue in it. As the camera moves along the hallway we see trunks labeled MWE along the wheel and me wearing MWE Staff t-shirts walks around packing things up and putting them in the trunks. The camera continues down the hall and we go past a few local jobbers who were at tonight’s house show and they all smiles sharing the best experience of there young career so far. As the camera continues down the corridor it finally comes to a stop at a specific dressing room. The camera looks at the door and the name plate reads Ace Cannon. An arm extends from behind the camera and knocks on the door… There is no answer. The hand once again reaches out and knocks again and finally after a few seconds we hear a gruff voice come from the other side giving his blessing for them to enter. As the door swings open we see Ace Cannon sitting on a black couch and his boots are halfway un-laced and he is in his wrestling trunks. He sits back on the couch with a smile on his face. As the camera stays transfixed on him he lets out a slight audiable laugh before talking.** Ace: Now this interview is unlike any other I have given in MWE thus far and that is because I have requested this camera come down here because there are a few locker room mumbles that I need to clear up. ** Cannon smiles again and shakes his head.** Ace: Now the first urban legend of the MWE locker room is that I am afraid of Grand Mystique. This dirty little legend started when I cut a promo a week ago and really didn’t talk about GM and I just covered Maxx. So after that promo people were saying I avoided Mystique because he is better then me and that I’m scared of him… ** Ace pauses and his face turns to a sheer look of aggravation ** Ace: Let me just say right now that rumor is pure bullshit… As a matter of fact I made a it a point not to really talk about Grand Mystique. Now, why would I do that against one of my opponents? **Ace lets the question hang in the air some people can infer there own answers** Ace: It is a very simple answer. It’s an answer that has it’s roots in EW… Now when I was in EW I was the best wrestler there and as I stood atop of my throne and saw people trying there claw my way to me I heard about this guy Grand Mystique. I started taking notice of him and saw he had potential to eventually be where I was in my career but as I watched him more and more I saw a man who kept failing. ** Cannon stops and a grin comes back to his face as he looks at the camera.** Ace: When I saw him coming up on the short end of the stick every time I quickly dismissed Grand Mystique as nothing more then a mid-card title threat. I knew that when the big match presented itself he would never come out on top and that is because he is a professionally choke artist and sure he may have the tag titles with Maxx but that is nothing! For a guy who has been around as long as Mystique he should be past building his career on tag titles and if he thinks because he is a tag champion that he can beat me then he needs to think again because I am the best wrestler in the world and it will take more them some little midcard ass clown with a tag title to beat me! **Cannon runs his hand through his short buzzed head.** Ace: So Mystique, don’t get things twisted because you will see why I am better then you and will always be better then you! **Cannon sits up from the couch and is looking directly into the camera with the look of pure scorn across his face.** Ace: Now the next thing I am about to talk about isn’t for the faint of heart. So if words hurt you then you don’t want to hear this because it is about to get ugly and when I say ugly it means it will be even worse then the night when Brian Michaels beat David Whitman. **Cannon continues to sit on the edge of the couch. He itches the side of his cheek before he continues.** Ace: So this second bit of mumbling going around the locker room is I am taking personal shots at Maxx… Well, if I was taking personal shots at Maxx they would be a lot more hard hitting then what I did say! If I was going to get personal I could have attacked your family or something along those lines but I didn’t. I didn’t do that because yes, I might be crazy but I know where to draw the line and for you to go to the powers that be and complain I was too hard on you is a bitch move.. Plain and simple. **Cannon shakes his head in a disapproving manner before he continues.** Ace: So Maxx, lets get real.. If you want to cast stones saying someone is getting personal then I can do that. I mean I could talk about how Maxx was the real head of EW and how he booked himself into the Undisputed title. I could go on and on for days about you and the lies and deceitful ways that you sold to everyone but like I said before I won’t get personal because in all reality I don’t want to catch heat from the brass. I got better things to do besides deal with them. ** Cannon motions for the camera to come closer. As it does we get a tight shot of just Cannon’s face as he once again begins to talk** Ace: So Maxx, with all your bitching and whining you prove my point of everything I said before about you in my last promo but I do know that all that bitching and whining can’t stop me from whipping your ass. So bring everything you have to the table at Genesis because you will need it and if you call me out one more time for being too hard on you or bringing personal matters into things then I will find that trash bag whore Chole and I will kick her fucking skull in and that is a promise! Because If I have done it to a child I will not hesitate to kick in the skull of a common street whore! ** Cannon turns his head to the side and begins laughing manically.** Ace: So Maxx, heed the warnings because there real.. and the ass kicking you will receive will also be nothing more than six months anger that you have just made worse by slinging bullshit at me. So let me wish you goodluck, not because I am a gracious oppoent but because you will need once I get my ahdns on you. ** Cannon pushes the camera away from him and gets up from the couch and walks out the door as the scene fades out.**
