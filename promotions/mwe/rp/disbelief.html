Disbelief

Jul 28 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Quote: OOC- Hey guys, this was a bit rushed seeing as my 21st bday is thursday. I hope you all like it. Zak Midget TV Reporter Our current setting is a lovely, luxurious hotel room. It is fully furnished, tricked out to the max. On the wall is a nice 52 inch Samsung TV, with an XBOX 360 hooked up. A movie seems to be playing on the TV, and it is none other than a bootleg copy of Transformers two. It’s hard to tell whose room this is. It could belong to Sinc Mercier, although I doubt he would be watching an awesome movie like Transformers. It could belong to CSB, but there is a lack of a passed out Wayne on the floor. If you scan along the room, right in the middle of the coffee table is a 4 foot tall, handblown glass bong. That means there is only one MWE star that could be living here…Zak Warner. From the other room, a voice can be heard softly speaking, as if carrying on a conversation with himself. Warner walked into the living room, sitting down on his couch as he begins to pack the bong. He has on a brand new Minnesota Timberwolves jersey, with Jonny Flynn’s name and number on the back. He has on a matching fitted, and his phone is tucked gently between his neck and his ear. “Listen Angie, I’m not sure what’s going on with Kevin, but I know you must be as confused as I am…No, I’m glad you called me, you know you can anytime…sure, I can meet you, just name the time and place…yeah, I know where that is, I’ll see you after your therapy session.” The phone is shut off and tossed aside, and it seems as though Zak is just dumbfounded. It would be easier to comprehend if we knew the extent of the conversation, but unfortunately we don’t have that luxury. Shaking his head, he took a nice, huge hit off the bong, letting the sweet smoke fill his lungs before exhaling. He sat back, watching a bit of the movie as the lovely Megan Fox graced the screen. He grinned, as his phone alarm went off. He flipped it open, realizing that he had an autograph signing down in the hotel lobby. He took another hit, before spraying a bit of Axe on himself to cover the scent of weed. Pocketing his phone and adjusting his hat, he walked into the hallway and towards the elevator. As soon as he reaches the lobby, he noticed a long line of fans, waiting patiently for the MWE stars to arrive. Thankfully, it wasn’t just Zak at the signing today, it would be him, and Sinc Mercier autographing stacks of pictures, toys, shirts, and whatever else the fans brought. Zak was honestly surprised that there were so many people, considering his recent suspension for drugs. Normally, that would turn most people away, but apparently his fans didn’t care. The line seemed never ending, and Warner quickly lost track of time. He barely noticed when Sinc got up and left, even though there was still a decent amount of people left in line. As the line FINALLY began to dwindle, Zak took a look at his watch and noticed that it was getting close to his meeting with Angie. As the last fan, a female walked up to him with a picture ready to be signed, Zak exhaled a slight sigh of relief. “Wow, I can’t believe I finally get to meet you!” she said, her voice bubbling with enthusiasm. “I’ve waited for days to see you!” “Well then I’ll make this a special day for ya.” he said, as he smiled. The best part of the job to him, was seeing the kids happy. “What’s your name? I’ll personalize this for you.” “Brittany….Brittany Summers” He signed the picture “To Brittany. For a lovely little girl, from the future EPW Champ.” He handed it back to her with a smile. As she read what he had written, a look of fury came over her. “EXCUSE ME? Did you just call me a little girl?” “Is there something wrong?” he asked, shocked by the sudden outburst. “Just because I am 4’5, doesn’t mean I am a little girl. I will let you know, that I am a 25 year old, card carrying member of the American Dwarfism Association.” she screamed, as her veins began to pop. “I’m sorry, I had no idea.”said Zak, as he quickly backed away. He bolted towards the door, the midget still screaming. He was sure that he would get a phone call later from his lawyer at some point, but he didn’t care. He had other priorities right now. Thankfully, the diner they had agreed to meet at was only a couple blocks away from the both of them, so Warner could walk with no difficulty. It was a decently warm evening, the sun setting off in the distance. The sky was an interesting mixture of pink and orange, and it was slightly relaxing for Zak. He reached the diner with a few minutes to spare, so he sat in an open booth and ordered a cup of coffee. He wasn’t too hungry, which was surprising because he normally got the munchies after smoking. Maybe the dwarf had taken away his appetite. Time slowly ticked away, as Angie was nowhere to be found. Zak tried calling her a few times, getting only her voicemail. He was worried, but there wasn’t much he could do about it. He had a bad feeling about it though, because in the few years that he had known her, Angie was never late without at least calling first. A few more minutes ticked by, and he finally got up to leave. As he exited the diner, he heard sirens off in the distance and he chuckled to himself. “Heh, I wonder if those cops are after Kevin…poor bastard might have finally snapped…” That would be too ironic though, and he quickly pushed those thoughts out of his head. He wandered the streets of Baltimore, wondering what there was to do on a night like this. Suddenly, a brilliant idea came to him and he pulled his nice little portable video camera out of his coat pocket. It was a brilliant little thing, coming in handy when he needed to voice his thoughts. He turned the camera so it faced him, and he pressed record. “So I know this is a bit different than my normal promos, as I am lacking DeAngelo, weed, or any of my typical surroundings. Seeing as my life has been anything but normal as of late though, I figured this might be a bit more appropriate. You see, the past couple months haven’t exactly been the best for me, as everyone seems to enjoy reminding me. I can’t go a day without some damn fan walking up to me and asking how I feel about what Kevin did to me. What all of you marks, and naive sonofabitches seem unable to comprehend, is that this had nothing to do with MWE. This wasn’t storyline related, or anything like that. Kevin turned his back on me when he cheap shotted me like that, and the damage that it caused is irreparable. I didn’t just lose a match that night, I lost a friend…I lost a brother.” His voice is filled with emotion, and it is obvious that he is hurt even thinking about what happened. His voice wavers, as he tries to maintain his composure. “This week though, I can’t focus on that. I can’t focus on Kevin even though we have a match looming at the next Pay Per View. Instead, I have to focus all my anger, all of my animosity at my two opponents for the week. Jay Riddick and Jesus Cruz, I feel bad for you. I feel bad that MWE management chose you two to stand across from me this week. You see, I actually like you two. You are two of the guys that have the most potential to rise to the top of MWE, at any given moment. Yet for whatever reason, management decided that instead of utilizing you to your potential, they would throw you to me. They know the state of mind that I am in, they know that I am hell bent on destroying anyone and everyone that gets in my way. So, before I continue this little promo any longer, I want to take a second to apologize to both Jay, and Jesus. Guys, I am truly sorry that you will have to endure the pain I plan on inflicting on the both of you. For this, I will give a moment of silence.” He bows his head and closes his eyes, as if he were mourning the passing of someone close to him. A clap of thunder snaps him out of his daze, as a light drizzle began to fall around him. He continues his walk, his eyes focused not on the camera, but gazing just past it. “First, let me talk a bit about Mr. Cruz. You see Jesus, you remind me an awful lot of myself when I first started in this business. I too started out in the world of Mixed Martial Arts, in the company known as UFC. I had a promising career, winning my first six matches by KO or Submission. I had a title shot lined up, life was going great, then I fucked up. I got into a bar brawl, took out UFC’s top draw, and was pretty much blacklisted from every major MMA association. That’s not too different from what happened to you, now is it Jesus? You snapped, took out a referee, and there went your MMA career. I know what it is like to have your entire life snatched out from under you Jesus, I know what you went through because I had to suffer the same thing. Just like me, you entered the world of professional wrestling, and already you have high hopes, and lofty goals. You push your body to the limit every day, and train like there is no tomorrow. Yet unfortunately that won’t make any difference when you step inside the ring because there is one major flaw that you have yet to correct. You think you are invincible. Oh trust me, I know what it is like to be the new kid in town, with a lot to prove. People doubt you because of your MMA background. They think you are one-dimensional, and you have to fight tooth and nail to prove them wrong. Yet you also think that because you have knocked people out, and almost broken their limbs, that it somehow makes you superior to the rest. Major fucking mistake Senor Cruz.” The rain has intensified a bit, and brief flashes of lightning can be seen in the sky. Water is dripping down Warner’s face, but he barely seems to notice. “You see, my MMA background has helped me everywhere I went. In EPW, I got paid to knock people the fuck out inside a steel cage. I was on my way to claiming the Ultimate Edge title, but a loaded glove ended that chance. In GWC, I was the first, and longest reigning Underground champion. I made the best of the best tap out, or watched them crumble after taking a strike from me. So you can sit around, and boast about how superior your striking might be, or how much better you are at making people tap…but until you make ME tap, or knock ME the fuck out, your words carry as much weight as those spoken by Kevin Hardaway. Hell, after I take care of Kevin, I would be more than glad to meet you in a match where only KO or submissions win. I would show you what it really means to be able to adapt to your surroundings. You fight for money, for titles, for glory…I fight for respect. I don’t want it handed to me, I want to earn it when I feel your jawbone crumble from my fist. I want to earn it when I see your elbow popping out of its socket as you scream for the referee to stop the match. In this company, nothing is given to you Senor Cruz…you have to fucking earn it.” The rain has reached a borderline downpour, which forces Warner to duck under a nearby awning. He sits down on the driest patch of concrete, propping the camera up on his knee. He takes a moment to wipe the rain off of his face, before continuing to speak. “Now Mr. Riddick…as much potential as you have, and as impressive as you were at first…I can’t help but to be a bit disappointed as of late. I find it hard to take a champion seriously, when he didn’t even win the belt that he carries around. You were handed the X-TV title even though you didn’t earn it, and I have to say it makes you look even less credible as a champion. Not to mention, you had a chance to take out one of the biggest names in the company, to succeed where even I have failed…yet once again you couldn’t live up to expectations. Is that how you want your career to be defined? Do you want to be known as the man who was never able to reach his full potential? Do you want your name to be synonymous with ‘Transitional Champion’? Because if you do, I will be more than glad to assist you in that endeavor. You see Jay, as good as you COULD be, you aren’t on my level yet. You couldn’t beat Nic Kraven…you couldn’t beat Joey Dalla…I mean Mr. Sharpman. What makes you think you will be able to beat me? Honestly Jay, what have you done here in MWE that would make me sit here and think ‘wow, this guy might actually beat me.’?” A small chuckle escapes his lips, as he gets back to his feet. The rain seems to have stopped suddenly, and now a slight fog has rolled in. Warner steps back out onto the sidewalk, continuing his journey. “Let us take a look at who you have beaten here in MWE, shall we? You had a 5-way battle royal that you won, only after letting my boy Anthony Clarke eliminate two of the other guys. You pinned Blight, which in MWE seems to be no big accomplishment. Then, after Clarke had done all the hard work, you sneak out a win by getting a lucky submission. You have beaten Virus and Scotty McKnight, which I would be impressed with, except for the fact that I’ve done that multiple times. If you are honestly proud about beating them, then you need to sit down, take a look in the mirror, and determine if you need to get your head checked. So it seems Jay, that you can beat the easy people, but as soon as a challenge is thrown in front of you, you curl up into a little ball and can’t win to save your life. Nic Kraven, a man I DESTROYED in front of everyone, was able to beat you without hesitation. He even felt so bad for embarrassing you, that he handed you his title as a consolation prize. You had the opportunity to ride your winning streak right to the top, and instead you stumbled to the bottom of the barrel. The sad part is, this week you will just continue on that downward spiral, because there is no way that I am going to let you win. I have too much to accomplish, and I will be DAMNED if I let a guy like you, a man who can’t even EARN his title.” It is obvious that Warner is getting a bit hyped up during this promo, as is evident by his voice. It had remained quite calm until this last little bit, and he has to take a moment to calm himself. He has reached his destination, which is actually the hotel we started at. When he starts to speak, his voice is calm once again. “This week, I will go out to that ring and prove to everyone, myself and Kevin included, that I am still the champion I once was. People still doubt that I have what it takes to reach the top, and all that does is make me want to prove it even more. So shoot up my addicts, because this week, the flag of Warner will be flying high once again.” With his signature line, the camera clicks off as he made his way back into the hotel lobby. He scanned around, making sure the psycho midget wasn’t still lurking, before dashing to the elevator. It was a quick ride to the top, and he walked into his room where the menu to Transformers was stuck on an endless loop. He plopped down onto the couch, lighting what was left in the bong as he flipped the TV to the news. As he inhaled, what he saw on the TV made him choke and he started coughing furiously. He exhaled, catching his breath as he turned up the volume as a reporter spoke. “…MWE Superstar Kevin Hardaway was arrested today on domestic abuse charges. Cops arrived outside of Dr. Sarah Myers office to find Kevin’s wife, Angelina in tears with numerous bruises and lacerations. Mr. Hardaway posted his $50,000 bail, and is currently awaiting his court date. As soon as any more information is released, we will be the first to tell you. Reporting for WJZ news, this is Weijia Jiang.” Warner sat on the couch, dumbfounded at what he had just watched. He knew that Kevin had issues, but to go as far as hitting Angie…well this wasn’t the Kevin that Zak knew. He never thought it would go this far. He picked up his phone, dialing Angie’s number once more. Of course, straight to voicemail. Instead of hanging up this time, he waits till the beep before speaking. “Angie, It’s Zak…I just saw the news, and I wanted to tell you that if you need a place to stay, or you need someone to talk to, I’ll be in my hotel room all night. You know where it is, seeing as you got me this room. I’m here if you need me Angie, just remember that.” He hung up the phone, holding it in his hand as he stared blankly at the ground. “Damn Kevin…what the fuck is wrong with you…”
