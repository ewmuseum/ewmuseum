MWE 24/7 — 08-09 PPV PR Archives

Twisted Days.

Aug 27 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
OOC: You've all heard the phrase shit happens, right? Well, there you have it. So much shit happened this week, I barely got around to doing this RP. I feel bad.. Surely nowhere near my other stuff. Sorry to Money for this poor RP.. Well, without further ado, here ya go. QUOTE Nic "Shawn" Kraven Cassidy Mother The battlefield is a scene of constant chaos. The winner will be the one who controls that chaos, both his own and the enemies. - Napoleon Bonaparte PROLOGUE 1...2......3! Three seconds. Just three seconds, before his return was tarnished. Now, a lost is normal for the man known as Nic Kraven. Hes experienced it, everybody has experienced it once in a while in their life. And anybody who says otherwise...is only bullshitting themselves. But this particular lost however, has really got him thinking. Now he faced D.C that week. The former world champion, D.C Facing a former MWE World Champion isn't anything new for him. Hes faced Mason Resoan and beat him cleanly, but D.C on the other hand, he lost to. And this has him thinking, was that victory against Resoan just a mere fluke? … … No. He can't think like this. It wasn't it a fluke and hes going to prove it. He has another chance, another chance at glory. Against that very man. But the thing is, Resoan is now the U.S. Champion. Hes on top of the freaking world in his own mind. And it's his job, it's Kraven's job to knock him off his very own throne. Crowning Glory...it WILL be his time. But we'll find out if he can now, won't we? Only a few short days, we'll find out if it really is...his time to shine. [A room. A pretty small room at that. Its pretty quiet except for an occasional silent breathing. A small narrow window is on the right side of the room with light barely shining through it. The camera pans around the deserted room until finally stopping at the center. A chair is there a long with a person on it. A quite large figure with a black shirt, and a pair of ripped jean shorts. Slowly, the camera approaches the figure coming around him. Its none other, than Nic Kraven.] “I admit it, I'm intimidated right now. Last time I faced you Resoan, you were broken, beatened. Riding a losing streak and no hope in sight. And now...now you're once again a U.S. Champion. It shocks me that in such short time, you managed to get off your feet and win that title once again. Oh, I feel intimidated right now. The biggest stage in MWE, Crowning Glory. Legends were born here. And I'm facing you, one of those legends and current champion. You're on a roll right now, while I have just come off a lost against D.C.” “But then I think, how can I be intimidated of a man I beat right in the middle of that ring? How can I be intimidated of a man I humiliated not so long ago? I beat you Resoan! In my first try, I beat you! 1..2...3. And everything I said, everything I said I would do, it came true. But I give you props Mason, you managed to somehow come back and that US Title.” [Quietly, Kraven strokes his hair back as he bows his head.] “But wait, what in the hell did you do to deserve that title shot? What did you do? You lost to me for christ sake and immediately get a U.S. Title shot after that? It's funny really. Was this some kind of joke? Or did you pull some strings in the back to get what you want? Its not the first time from you. Back then when you were with Michaels, you two were going crazy with the bullshit backstage politics. But lets get back to now. Tell me Resoan, who did you beat? The Virus? A man who thinks hes some sort of Devil? Hes a no name now Resoan. A no name, nobody gives a damn about the man known as The Virus. I don't see how you can be proud of this.” “But long story short, you beat him. And still, you continue to prove how you're not even close to deserving that U.S. Title. I don't give a damn, if you were a former World Champion. I don't give a damn if you're considered some sort of legend by these people. Its about here and now, and currently, you're NOT U.S. Title material. A few weeks ago, you faced Kevin Hardaway. And there were the same results in your match against me. You lost. You lost Resoan! And then again the next week, you faced GM and won. But at what cost? You had help from your buddy in Sinc Mercier! Correct me if I'm wrong but doesn't a true champion wins on his own?[Kraven looks up just enough to see the camera.] Its a joke to even call you champion. I, however can be called champion. I defended that X-TV title with all I had. High Road match, singles match, fatal 4 ways. You name it. I made the X-TV title seem at least important compared to the “top” titles. I did what I could. But all you've proven so far...is that the U.S. Title shouldn't even be around your waist. You can't even act like a champion, let alone be one.” “You can deny it all you want. Hell, you can take all the shots you want against me. Call me unoriginal. Call me some wannabe “dark wrestler.” But its just talk, you're a joke now. What you were, the guy who everyone looked up to. The guy who carried the company, who wore the coveted MWE World Championship, who oozed of confidence every step he took. You're not him. Who you think you are now compared to what you were years ago, you're nowhere near that level. You're not a so called god anymore. You're not some perfectionist. Your past is clouding you're mind. You still think you're that man...when you're way past that time. I'm surprised your little girlfriend is still hanging around with you.” “So go, call me whatever you want. Rant on me. “Destroy” me from the inside out. But know this, you're past your prime. And with this rising talents all around MWE, you're gonna be damn lucky to hold on to that even if you beat me. Listen Resoan, I've always wanted to ask you this question. Why the f*ck are you still in the spotlight? Why? I mean, you're still the talk of the day. You're still being focused on. Was 3 years of being in the spotlight not enough for you? Can you not just be satisfied with what you have and step back? I guess not. But I think I know why. I think I've figured out the very reason why. You're afraid. You're afraid of being forgotten. You're afraid of people surpassing you on every obstacle. But don't worry, no. You'll be remembered...for a while.” “When people call you “the champ,” they're lying to you Resoan. They feel sorry for you. When you walk through those curtains with your head held high, with that pathetic smirk of yours, and those little children mark for you like there's no tomorrow, they're just feeling sorry for you. Look at you. You're a walking shell of your former self. You're walking down the last path of your career. And when you lose that pretty little title around your waist, you'll finally realize, that it just might be that point in your career to just stand back, and walk away.” “See Resoan, Crowning Glory, there won't be any excuses when I take the U.S. Title away from you. There won't be any doubts. There won't be any, “Kraven's win was just a mere fluke.” No, there won't be any of that. I know what you've been doing Resoan. Watching our match over and over again. Looking for flaws. Just figuring out a way to beat me. Come Crowning Glory, I'll finally expose you for the fool you really are. Do what you can, bring who you can. It doesn't matter. Bring the whole damn American Mafia for all I care. It doesn't f*cking matter. I am NOT leaving Crowning Glory without that gold. And for all of you people who doesn't believe that I can do this. For all of you who doubted me until now. I'll prove to all of you that a new era has arisen. It's my f*cking time. The clock is ticking. See you August 30th. [And with this, it all ends. Kraven sits back against the chair sighing. He can feel it, everybody can feel it. Hes more ready than hes ever been. And recently suffering the lost against D.C., he'll prove he has what it takes. The camera backs out of the room, shutting the door behind him. Walking down the hall, and away from the room. The scene fades, the camera cuts. Static.] [Our scene opens to the very same place we had left off last time. A house. Cassidy's house. It's actually quite surprising that she got a house in such a short time. A beautiful house at that. The camera makes it's way into the living room. Nic Kraven is seen sitting at a circle shaped wooden table with a big white sheet covering it. Kraven is no longer wearing the hoodie but has on his black “Welcome to the End” T. Other clothing includes a slightly tattered, dark blue baggy jean shorts. He leans back on his chair a bit with an annoyed look on his face. He occasionally glances over at Cassidy who is seen sitting on her leather couch. Shes slouched a bit with a pink pillow behind her, eating a bag of Doritos with the radio almost turned on at full blast, and staring at 45' Plasma TV. Cassidy has on a pink spaghetti strap and a pair of blue shorts with white stripes on the sides. Kraven is seen glancing at her once more but this time, Cassidy notices. A smirk appears off the corner of her mouth, biting down on the chips in her mouth.] A look of displeasure comes from Kraven as he strokes his hair back with his left hand. He sighs a bit as he considers his option. Maybe he should talk to her again. Hell, its been about an hour since arriving at her doorstep. .. .. More silence. Alright, the silence has gone on for long enough. He stands up and shoves his hands in his pocket as he walks over to the couch. Cassidy is still enjoying her bag of Doritos, watching TV with a deep voice coming out of the radio speaking some random nonsense.] “Alright, c'mon Cass, talk to me. I said I was sor-” “Don't even say it.” [She has her arm out pointed directly at Kraven like “Talk to the hand because I don't even want to hear it.” And her look, well the look says it all. One of the most annoying look you can get from a women. Cassidy rolls her eyes back with her arms out, with a “Bitch, please” look on her face.] Spoiler: click to toggle [Uggh. Kraven has his head down, teeth clenched, steaming a bit. He then shifts his feet into another direction, deciding just to wonder around the house for a minute. And before even walking, a song comes on on the radio. It's just one of those songs that will make you rage even more.] .. [Poker Face by Lady GaGa.] Mum mum mum mah.. Mum mum mum mah.. [Kraven simply just facepalms sliding his hand down his slightly scarred face. If there was ever an inappropriate time for the song to come on, it'd be now. He stares at Cassidy, now nodding her head to the beat of the song.] “Oh god. Will you please turn this shit off? It's giving me a damn headache.” [Oh, she did hear what he said, but just decides to give Kraven the silent treatment. She also turns the radio on full blast just to annoy Kraven a bit more.] LoveGame intuition play the cards with spades to start And after he's been hooked I'll play the one that's on his heart Oh, oh, oh, oh, ohhhh, ohh-oh-e-ohh-oh-oh [He glares at her with hatred in his crystal blue eyes. Not really wanting to put up with this anymore, he grabs the TV remote and points it at the TV. Click. It's off. He then walks over to where the radio lies and turns it off also. Cassidy then looks up at her older brother with a “WTF” look.] “Hey! What the hell? I was listening to that.” “Oh, now you finally talk to me. So talk, why are you so pissed at me? I said sorry you know. And did you not catch my recent promo? I explained all of that.” “Yeah, yeah. But I didn't know where you were Shawn. You could've been dead for all I knew! You just got up and left one day for a few weeks without saying a damn thing to anyone! [Kraven sighs and just collapses on the couch. He glances over at Cass and begins to speak.] “I know. But I needed to get away from here, there was just too many distractions.” “So that's what I am? A distraction?! Shawn, we've only been reunited for a month and you just get up and walk off. I thought I lost you again..” “I didn't mean it like that, I just needed some place to think without having to worry about anyone ok? I'm sorry.” [Cass looks at Kraven for a bit without saying anything. She finally stops and looks down, kinda rubbing her forehead.] “Yeah, next time you have one of your little moments, just tell me. [Kraven cracks a bit of a smile showing his bright teeth. He then just slouches against the couch and looks up at the ceiling.] “Alright. Deal.” “Yeah. Whatever.” [Cass reaches over to Kraven's side and snatches the remote away from him and turns the TV back on. She changes the channel for a while before landing on ABC Family. Just a shrug, and she puts the remote down. Just then, a little T-Mobile ringtone rings out of Cassidy's shorts pocket. She takes the black Iphone out of her pocket's and quickly glances at the number. She sighs a bit before pressing the “talk” button.] “Hello? Oh, hey Uncle Trent! Yeah, I settled into the house fine. Yeah? Oh, Shawn is here. Wait...what?! [Suddenly, she sits straight up. Her eyes just filled with shock and her mouth hanging open. Kraven notices this and raises an eyebrow. He then mouths the words “What happened?”] “Oh my god... Really?! Oh, wow. Alright, I'll tell Shawn right now. Yeah, bye.” “Tell Shawn what?” [She turns back to Kraven with a mix of fear and shock. Judging by Cassidy's expression, shes hesitant to tell Kraven. She opens her mouth to speak, but the only words coming out were..] “Umm...” “What? What the hell is it?! “Uncle Trent found Mom...” [Everything around Kraven seems to stop moving. Everything is still. Kraven's eyes go wide, he sits up straight but then bows his head down. He grabs his hair with both of his hands, staring down at the ground. His mind is swirling with thoughts...way to many thoughts. After all these years? Why now?] “Wh-where?” “Oh, shes in Downtown Los Angeles. It'd be a 5-6 hour drive..” “We're going.” “Woah, are you sure? I mean, we haven't seen her in so long..” [Kraven loosens his grip on his hair and slowly let his arms fall. He now looks up and straight at Cassidy. His eyes, focused. And somewhere deep in side him, he feels excited.] “Yeah, I'm sure. We have to..” “Alright. Let me just get my coat and then we'll go.” [Kraven slightly nods as he heads toward the door. Focused, and intent on confronting his mother. Hundreds of questions just runs through his mind. This is going to be one awkward meeting.] [--------HOURS LATER-------] [Hours past. The scene now opens in Downtown Los Angeles, around 5:30 in the afternoon. The streets are practically filled with people from shoppers to the occasional worker. A 2009 Bright Blue Nissan Sentra is seen cruising down the street. Inside, we see Kraven in the passenger seat with an earphone on, occasionally glancing out at the shops, malls, people, you name it. In the driver seat, we see Cassidy with the same outfit as before except now, shes wearing plain black hoodie with a pair of sun glasses on. They continue on for a little while longer before pulling into an apartment building. A bright building, a bit run down, but still looks decent. Cassidy parks on the other side of the street as Kraven gets out of the car followed by Cassidy. They hesitantly enter the apartment building, nervous about the meeting. Kraven's heart thumping like a metronome, while Cassidy follows behind him looking down. They enter an elevator as Kraven presses a little button that will be taking them up. Seconds past, and the doors finally open. They slowly walk down the narrow hall before finally stopping at one particular door. On the front, the door reads “32A.” Kraven reaches his arms out...and gently knocks on the door. Cassidy gasps a bit as Kraven glares at the door, hoping his mother would answer.] [His wish came true, not a few seconds later. The door knob slowly turns and the door is open revealing a blonded hair women, around the age of 40-44 or so, wearing a raggy grey T, and a pair of blue sweatpants. She looks at the two in wonder. Obviously, she doesn't know who the hell they are and why they're knocking on her door, late in the afternoon. She scratches her head a bit before speaking.] “Who the hell are you two and why are you here? You aren't cops, are ya?” “Umm, no...we- [Kraven cuts Cass of in mid sentence, glaring at the women with emotionless eyes.] “We're Cassidy and Shawn, your kids. The same kids you left years ago, abandoning both of us and leaving us to be raised by a fool of a man.” [The women's dull look turns into a look of fear and shock. Her eyes wide, she backs away from the door a bit, never thinking that she would see her children again today. She runs a hand through her hair looking around blinking rapidly.] “No... No, I don't have any children.” “Deny it all you want, but we both know you remember us clearly. So it's been a while...mom.” [She cringes as Kraven says mom. Not knowing what to do, she just falls on the couch behind her.] “So can we come in, or what?” “Yeah...yeah, come in. [Kraven walks in still glaring at hid mother while Cassidy hesitantly enters and closes the door behind them, along with the camera.. Nowhere to go now, the camera man walks away and with this the camera is shut off.]
