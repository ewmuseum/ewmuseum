SAW (Southern Alliance, 2002) — Roleplay

Speak Ya Mind #7 (part 1 of 2)

4 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
SAW presents "Speak Ya Mind" on April 4th, 2002 You will hear from the Following SAW superstars ************************* 1 - SAW Commitee 2 - Antoine Bradley 3 - Risley Brothers 4 - Reaper 5 - Bungle 6 - Tommy Flames 7 -Frankie Cash 8 - Vern Walters 9 - Rob Fabulous ******************** [ Fade In ] [ We fade into a view of the SAW commitee in a meeting of some sort.Around a large, circular table with the SAW logo in the middle of it, we see all of the
SAW top gun's dressed in nice suits. From left to right we have President Mike
Johnson, Commish Mike Pace, Vice President Shawn Cross, Fever announcer Fred
Hart, Interviewer Johnny Jackson, Head of Security Jay Trisler, Head Referee
Sasaki Hirokama, Second Referee Wayne West, EMT Jeremy "Lifeline" Rivers and
The legendary announcer Frankie Tha Pimp who works SAW supercards...We listen in as they discuss.]
Mike Johnson : " The supercard is the 28th guys...We need to make sure this is
_BIG_. I don't want to be one of those federations where the supercards bring out as much desire and excitement as your weekly shows. I want this to be something people will anticpate and sit on the edge of there seats for."
Mike Pace : " I think with the current talent roster of the SAW we can make that happen Mr Johnson. Without a shadow of a doubt in my mind, I think that if the SAW wants to put out a truly super show..We can."
Frankie Tha Pimp : << Laughing >> Being the Commish, Talent Scout and director of shows you kinda have to say that Huh Mike ? Or else it would seem like your not doing your job."
Mike Pace : " Are you trying to say we don't have any Talent..?" Frankie Tha Pimp : " Not saying that, I mean I am here, Frankie Cash is here, and there's a couple other guys, But if you want to make an impact << Pauses >>
You need some sort of Impact player, Or at least an impact match..A match that will have the world in awe, and be fun for me to announce at the same time."
Mike Pace : " Such as ? " Frankie Tha Pimp : " I guess I do have to do EVERYTHING around here...You should give the fans what they want to see.People want to see titles on the line first and formost, and they want to see grudges settled.Best way to do that is make sure the match stays One On One right ?. What better way to settle your scores and put your male ego to the limits ? "
Mike Johnson : " That would be a nice goal to have...I like matches which stay between the wrestlers in the ring, instead of the one's outside of it. Lately things have went a little crazy out there."
Frankie Tha Pimp : " Thats why I say..Put EVERY SAW title on the line at Down on the Bayou and Make the Southern Title match a Lumberjack match.Take some of the SAW wrestlers, Stick them outside, and let's see what happens. There might be a couple fights outside the ring, but that should mean the actual title contenders remain In the ring."
Mike Johnson : " Is everyone alright with the SOUTHERN TITLE match at Down on
The Bayou being a LUMBERJACK Match ? "
[ Everybody nod's in agrement, There is a slight buzz around the table for a moment, before we continue on.]
Mike Johnson : " Then it's settled...Lumberjack match it is, The winner's of this week's main event, will meet next week in a One on One match to see who gets one of the spots at Down on the Bayou in the match up..That's alrighty been noted, but how should we get the other half ? "
Sasaki Hirokama : " With all due respect Sir, I assume it will play itself out, and we need not worry ourselves."
Mike Johnson : " Good Point...<< Looks to Mike Pace >> How's the situation with
Antoine Bradley..? "
Mike Pace : " We have a lead on someone else possibly being involved on the attack, but as of now.It is still Antoine Bradley we have in lock down. That may change soon though."
Fred Hart : " So Antoine MAY be innocent ? " Mike Pace : " Possibly." Fred Hart : " Oh Samuel Smith is gonna love this. An Innocent black man framed and put in jail already here in the SAW."
Frankie Tha Pimp : " It is the dirty south you know...We can be rascist as hell." Johnny Jackson : << Coughs >> Frankie Tha Pimp : " Oh...Sorry my African American friend. Didn't see you in here." Johnny Jackson : " Are you trying to say something about my dark skin ? " Frankie Tha Pimp : " << Chuckling >> No man...Relax " Mike Johnson : " Keep Antoine on your possible list for the supercard gentleman.To change subject a bit, We should have a Southern Regional Champion by the end of the week. So that leaves two titles we will need to crown champions or do something with if we want to put them on the line the 28th. The
Lightweight and Tag scene need's some direction."
Shawn Cross : " Could I direct those titles ? Be in charge of there very existance ? " Frankie Tha Pimp : " If your asking if Rob Fabulous can be handed both titles without even wrestling for them, The answer is _NADA_.."
[ Shawn Stares at Frankie coldly, as Frankie just smiles back in his direction.] Mike Johnson : " That is another issue Frankie, One to be discussed in private with myself and Shawn..But I will have to say No Shawn. I like having joint meetings to discuss thing, instead of a dictator type role of one man in charge."
Shawn Cross : " Understandable << Glances at Frankie One More time >> " Jeremy Rivers : " Before we get into serious discussion about those titles, I would like to inform you that the NEW lightweight you sent me papers on. Did pass his physical and is cleared to wrestle."
Mike Johnson : " Good " Jeremy Rivers : " Also on the injury scene...Nobody is seriously hurt, but Matt
James is suffering from lower back pains, Reaper had a Head CT which showed up negative the other day, I requested Vern Walters have a RIB X ray done, and
Chris Page has way more than your average bumps and bruises currently. I am sure SOMETHING is broken or seperated in his body, but he refuses X ray everytime I ask."
Mike Johnson : " Just do what you can Jeremy.." Jay Trisler : " a couple security guards got hurt last week during the six man tag match...Rud Groz and Simon Starr did a number on my men."
Mike Johnson : " Jeremy, Take a look at Jay's men to will ya..When you get the chance." Jeremy Rivers : " Yes sir." [ The group discusses slight Injury's for a little longer, then changes it's view point back on the Lightweight and Tag Title pictures.]
Mike Johnson : " The Tag Team matches this week look very good gentleman. Bounty Hunters and Risley Brothers both come highly regarded, and The Business
Men's work out session the other day went well."
Fred Hart : " Do the Indy team have a chance again the Business Men Sunday ? " Mike Johnson : " Prolly not...Which means we have three solid tag teams currently." Mike Pace : " I am in talks with two or three more teams Sir." Mike Johnson : " Good...Get them signed by The 28th Mike, I want to have a tag team Elimination match of some sort at the Supercard, but I need a couple more teams first. Next week I am signing a FOUR WAY tag match in which the winner will be the last team in the elimination match, Thus having the best chance of winning the titles at Down on the Bayou."
Mike Pace : " Whose gonna be the Fourth Team.." Mike Johnson : " It's covered Mike << Smiles >> Lightweight title guys ? " Fred Hart : " I don't really think we should have another tourney...The Southern Regional Title was enough for that." Wayne West : " And A rumble wouldn't be fair to the small guys quickness, speed and mobility."
Shawn Cross : " How bout we have a eight man tag match next week involving the lightweights...The winner's will then branch off to tag teams to face each other, With the winner's going One on One at the supercard in a Best two out of three falls match..."
[ Silence ] Frankie Tha Pimp : " For once you have a good idea...." Mike Johnson : " Are we all in aggreement on the Tag and Lightweight scene ? " [ Nods across the table ] Mike Johnson : " Then there you have it....And for the record the weight limit is 235 for the Lightweights.."
Mike Pace : " How did you get the Century tell center in Shreveport Mike ? It's gonna be hard to sell that out, even with these great matches.."
Mike Johnson : " Don't worry about that Mike, Just put together a good show..Use guys like Chris Page, C K Walker, Tommy Flames, Vern Walters, Eddie
James and so on to feel the seats as best you can. I will handle the rest. We head out to leave LA for the fist time Saturday...Vicksburg is only an Hour away, but none the less let's call it a night so we can prepare..Meeting
Adjourned..." [ Fade Out ] ************************************************************************** ************************ ANTOINE BRADLEY IN JAIL--DAY FOUR [The camera opens on the same, tiny jail cell inhabited by "The Real Deal" Antoine Bradley and
Cedric. Cedric appears to be dozing off to sleep, while Antoine is watching the latest edition of Speak
Ya Mind on the small, black-and-white TV provided to him. The screen is showing Tommy Taylor and Seth
Baptist discussing things.] A: Wonder what the gift Tommy Taylor is planning to send me is? [Cedric is not responding.] A: Ced? [Cedric starts.] C: Wha? It's not shower time, is it? A: No, no, we won't get another shower 'til Saturday night. Tommy Taylor said that he's going to send me some gift.
[Cedric rubs his eyes.] C: What does Tommy Taylor want with us? And why are you so worried about that? A: I don't know. There's nothing really to do besides watch this. You know what else I've noticed?
C: I hope you've noticed something. You've watched that tape about thirty times in the last twenty-four hours.
A: C.K. Walker is a jerk. This guy comes in here and thinks he's the greatest. Why doesn't Mike Pace hate on him? I may be black, but at least I don't look ridiculous like this guy. It's painful to see that guy on my TV.
C: Probably. Now, what about Tommy Taylor? A: Never mind Taylor. Let's think about Mike Pace. [Camera cuts to black as Cedric and Antoine begin to talk a little business that apparently they don't want us to hear.]
************************************************************************** ************************ V/O: It's time boys, it's time... [Fade in.] [We open in an average sized room containing typical gym equipment including a treadmill, bench press and an incredibly thick blue gym mat. As the camera pans around, we notice three men standing in the doorway. In the middle of the three is an older man with long flowing brown hair and a moustache. On the outside are a set of young identical twins no older than
18, both approximately 5'10", 170 with short spiky black hair and searing black eyes. They look to be of Spanish descent with their looks and their deep tan.]
[The twins are dressed in matching black tights with the word "NEW" written on the left leg in green writing and the word "BREED" written on the right leg in the same green font. Meanwhile, the older man, perhaps their father, is dressed in a pair of old denim jeans and a red and black long sleeved plaid shirt. The twins step out of the camera's view as the older man smiles at the camera and starts speaking.]
MAN: The name's Janusz, Janusz Risley... and these are two o' mah boys, Marshall and Orlando. [Janusz Risley beams proudly as we hear the treadmill start pumping in the background. He continues...]
JR: This is where mah Cesar trained durin' his younger days an' look where he is now, in the big time kicking ass with the bes' of 'em. These two young men standin' before ya righ' now are gonna follow in their brother's footsteps... their debut is fas' approachin' an' in two nights time in
Vicksburg, Mississippah, the Bounty Hunters are gonna be the firs' of _many_ victims of the Risley Brothers.
It's gonna be a night ta' remember. [The camera spins around briefly to see Orlando practicing his landings on the gym mat and Marshall running on the treadmill. We then go back to
Janusz, who's still got the grin on his face, excited by the fact that his twins are soon to debut in SAW.]
JR: Ya see, Marshall and Orlando may not be the bigges' of men, but they're fas', furious and will have ya down for the count before ya even know it!
Lobo, Hawke, my two sons have nuthin' to lose... _nuthin'_. They're not wrasslin' for the paychecks nor the fame or fortune. They're wrasslin'
simply ta' _HURT!_ The New Breed is coming ta' ya town and don't ya forget that! [Fade.] ************************************************************************** *********************** [Fade In] [The scene comes into the back parking lot of the SAW arena in which a House show is about to take place. We see fans lined up around security rails, next to the wrestler's entrance. Several cars drive up, and wrestlers get out. The wrestlers naturally play to the crowd. Regardless of who it is, the crowd cheers for them simply because the SAW is bringing some much needed fun into their dark, dreary lives. As the wrestlers continue to flock in, the crowd continues to cheer. The camera pans, and in a back corner of the parking lot we see The Reaper sitting in a folding metal chair. The Reaper is attired in a pair of black, baggy pants and black boots. We see the scars on his upper body that serve as reminders of his past. He is sitting, watching the wrestlers flock into the arena.]
The Reaper: It's rather funny, don't you think? How one simple little thing such as a wrestler stepping out of a car can make people so happy that they cheer uncontrollably. It's almost as if they are robots. Commanded to do things by the SAW. Do you think this interaction with the wrestlers is just a chance occurrence? [Chuckles] All that happens in this sport is planned out.
The suits in the SAW require certain things out of their wrestlers as they arrive. Things to whip the crowd into a frenzy so the show goes over better when at last it begins. To me, it's foolish really. All these wrestlers stepping out of their cars, legitimately happy that someone in the world knows who they are enough to cheer for them. But, do you think any of these pathetic bastards are thinking about what is going to happen later tonight? For in just a few hours, the show will begin. Regardless of the fun, and glam that wrestling brings along with it....things are very dangerous. Not necessarily because of the things that we do to each other.....but simply because hatred can form in the wrong people. Hatred is a very powerful emotion to feel.
Hatred is something that can drive one to do things that there are simply no cause for to be honest......hatred is what drives me through my every day life!
[The Reaper pauses, brushing his hair back out of his face he sighs] The Reaper: By and large the world today is oblivious to ramifications of someone hating you until it is far too late and the damage has been done. This is a story as old as time. Everything is going along as well as could be expected.....then suddenly a bomb is dropped......a plane is flown into a building.....a border is breached.....then suddenly the world is all about you.
One thing you do in an aggressive manner damage control begins and the people will not stop until you have been taken out. But I wonder.....what would come of us if someone that we didn't have the answer for attacked? What would come of you all if one day, unprovoked you were beaten within an inch of your life?
Sure, there will be a brief period of recovery. Afterwards, you would come back for revenge. But what happens when you simply cannot do anything? When you try to attack, yet somehow you end up on the bottom yet again. What do you do then? Many of you I'm sure are still feeding your ego, saying that it can't happen to you. [Chuckles] Ah, I'm sure that is simply another one of the stories the world tells themselves to help get through their day without screaming too much. It's a hard thought to ponder, isn't it? Well.....I give you this piece of advice......it might not be a bad idea to begin thinking about it. For I promise you, that day is fast approaching!
[The crowd erupts yet again, as The Reaper turns we see "Tough Stuff" Rob
Fabulous getting out of his car. Fabulous plays to the crowd, then quickly walks inside.]
The Reaper: Do I sense a bit of anticipation on Mister Fabulous? He seems as though he was in quite a hurry to just be heading into a run of the mill show. Perhaps it is not anticipation. Perhaps......perhaps it is fear.
Fear of what is surely coming his way tonight. Rob, you and I have yet to cross paths, yet we have known of the other for well over three years now. It seems rather odd that after all these years you and I are finally going to be going head-to-head, ay? Ah, to be quite honest it is a day that I have been hoping for Rob. After all the times of seeing me wrestle, actually seeing me develop in this sport....some might feel as though you have the inside track.
Perhaps you have some inside information that no one else has...[Chuckles] Ah, the wishes of the weak. Sure, you may know as much about me as anyone in this sport.....yet, the phrase "knowledge is power" is simple rhetoric. Tonight
Rob, our saga begins. I feel almost certain that it won't end here......but tonight will be where we look to a few months from now when all is said and done, and you will have your regrets!
[The Reaper pauses, and stands up out of the chair, he begins walking towards a back entrance to the arena. We see a large bruise on his temple, obviously from the brass knuck shot last week.]
The Reaper: Which brings me to Mister Walker. This week I've heard you spewing your bullshit about being a superstar. Ah, well....no since in making excuses CK. Last week, you got me. That much I will admit. However, that was the first battle in what is sure to develop into a war. You see CK, it's not all about one match. Otherwise, I would have been a defeated man at many times in my career. It's bout the long term CK. Who can out last the other? So go ahead.....talk about how old The Reaper is, make your claims that
I am washed up. I want you to feel good about yourself. I want you to walk through the SAW thinking that you don't have a care in the world. For that is going to make it that much better when I get my hands on you....over and over.
I'm going to put you through hell CK. For that's what hell is.....repitition! I hope you have prepared yourself for what you've gotten into. Although, after listening to your words this week, I have no choice but to think that you are completely lost. That is fine CK, if that is the way you want to perceive your current situation, that is your right. However, I warn you.....lying to yourself won't change the facts. You're a hunted man! Fuck armageddon......THIS IS HELL!!!
[Reaper chuckles, and turns walking inside the back door of the arena. The door slams behind him.]
[Fade Out] ************************************************************************** *********************** <<The scene opens with Bungle standing, behind him is a bus with the SAW logo on it. Bungle is standing in a strong pose while watching the camera.>>
[Bungle]: "I don't wear much jewellery, but sometimes I do like a bit of
Gold. And the time I like Gold is when it comes on belts....I have seen my fair share of belts, some with boring black leather a few with white, but they are all the same in one way and that is they have gold on. Now I think
SAW could have got a better belt for their Regional Championship, maybe one with a nice pink leather strap, or even break the model of professional wrestling and start having thongs instead of belts, a nice pink thong on the front a big gold badge and a hole in the back so it can be worn for fun in the other ring."
<<Bungle steps onto the bus and motions for the camera to follow him, Bungle makes his way to the back of the bus where he sits down on the large seat.>>
[Bungle]: "Tommy Flames, you have it all made, you aren't the one that has to get up and put on a bear suit and run around after children for a living, although I don't actually have to do it, I like to. But anyway, I'm just saying that winning the SAW regional title will do wonders for me, at the moment I only get one or two children's birthday party bookings and hopefully when I get the title at he weekend I will get more bookings. I know that most children are frightened of me, but that really has more to do with me touching them up than what I look like. I want to be a role model to these children and I think that winning this belt is going to do that."
<<Bungle lifts up a plastic bag from the floor and places it onto the seat next to him, from it he takes a party hat and places it on his head.>>
[Bungle]: "I've beat a businessman, a hotshot and now it is time for you. Are you ready to see what this Teddy bear can do?" <<He then picks up a balloon and starts to blow it up, after several attempts he finally manages to tie a knot in the end of it.>>
[Bungle]: "Go on tell me what you want me to make." [Cameraman]:"A dog..." <<Bungle starts to twist the balloon around and tie it in knots before it pops.>> [Bungle]: "Looks like the poor little dog didn't last for long...Anyway Tommy Flames, this coming weekend when the two of us meet in the Co Main event, redeemer one thing and that is I am the only gay bear in this sport,
I am the only gay bear in the world and I am the only gay bear that can bend you over and as soon as you start tapping on that canvas I can lift up that title and become the best champion SAW will ever have, because your only gay if you take it, and I"m going to make you gay."
