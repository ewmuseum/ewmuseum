[ Fade back into the announcer's booth.] Fred Hart : " Well fans, you just witnessed a tough match between two tough individuals." Samuel Smith : " Tough? You ain't seen tough yet.Get a load of the two guys we're about to see. Now THEY are tough. These are the guys I predict will make you smile as they pound those lil Risley Brothers."

Bountyhunters. Let's hear what these two masked hombres have to say before they get down here to wrestle."

Lobo: " Ever wonder what it's like ta face an opponent an' _know_..." [ Camera pulls back to reveal Lobo holding the burning magazine. Beside him stands his partner, Hawke.]
Lobo: " There ain't no way in Hell you can beat'em?" [ Lobo's diabolical grin can be seen through the mouth hole in his black mask. Both men are dressed in their ring gear of black singlets, black full-length trunks and boots, black masks and black cowboy hats.]
Lobo: " Well Risleys, yer about ta experience that particular feelin' tonight when you step into that ring against The Bountyhunters. All our careers we've done exactly what we've wanted to. Despite the critics who said we'd never make it, we've beaten the top talent in this business an' earned the right ta call ourselves the best in this sport. Now we're in the SAW an' what do we hear? "
" The gaddam Risleys are gonna be tag champs? " " Not if me an' Hawke have anythin' ta say about it. Tell'em big man." [Lobo drops the burning magazine to the floor as Hawke takes the forefront.] Hawke: " RISLEYS! I don't know who in the hell you boys think you are but you better lissen an' lissen up good! We didn't come to the SAW ta take a backseat ta ANYBODY, boys! We came here ta handout generous portions of asskickin' an'
melon-swellin'. You two stiffs jus happen ta be the first victims on the list. The classic case of wrong place, wrong time. Tonight yer gonna find out why it ain't healthy ta put yer name on the dotted line with the Bounty hunters, my friends. An' when we get our hands on you tonight we're gonna snap them stacks o'dimes you call necks! The 'Hunters are a-comin' boys...
An' Hell's ridin' shotgun." [Lobo steps back to the front.] Lobo: " Tonight we're gonna show the SAW, the fans an' especially you, Risley Brothers, why we ARE..the most feared tag team in the sport today. Get ready, Risleys...
Yer fifteen minutes are jus about up." [ Cut back to ringside to Fred Hart and Samuel Smith.] Fred Hart: " Wow. Talk about intensity. The Bounty hunters appear to really be ready for their matchup tonight against The Risley Brothers."
Samuel Smith: " They are the personification of the word tough, Freddy. I predict good things for them in their future here."
Fred Hart: " Yes well.. were about to see if your prediction's reign true or not..The Bounty Hunters vs The Ris.....New Breed is next." Samuel Smith : " Ya...Remember, There the NEW BREED.." Stacy Normand : " Our next match up for the evening put's Two of the hottest tag teams not just in the SAW, But on the wrestling scene in general against one another. We will have the privledge of witnessing The New Breed versus The
Bounty Hunters.."
["Blood Brothers" by Papa Roach hits the PA system bringing a heel pop from the crowd.] Stacy Normand : " Introducing First, at a total combined weight of three hundred and thirty eight pounds, coming out of Birmingham, Alabama... they are accompanied to
the ring by Janusz Risley... Marshall and Orlando Risley... THE NEW BREED!!! [The father of Marshall and Orlando, Janusz Risley, is the first one to come out from behind the curtain. With long brown hair and a moustache, Janusz is dressed in a pair of faded denim jeans and a red and black plaid shirt. He stands at the top of the ramp, arms open as his identical twins Orlando and
Marshall step into the entranceway, grins on both of their faces. The two look exactly the same, with short spiky black hair and deep dark brown eyes.
Both are dressed in black tights with the word "NEW" on the the left leg in crazy green writing and the word "BREED" written on the right leg in the same green font. Tape is wrapped around their wrists and around their right bicep. The only miniature noticeable difference between the two is that
Orlando is _slightly_ taller than Marshall. The two boys are focused on the task at hand as they walk down the ramp with their father Janusz Risley.
Stopping at the bottom of the ramp, Marshall and Orlando charge at the ring and slide under the bottom rope, taunting the crowd as their father applauds their actions on the outside.]
Stacy Normand : " And the men looking to ruin the New Breed's in ring debut...Weighing in at a total combined weight of Five Hundred and Forty
Pounds. Hawke and Lobo...THE BOUNTY HUNTERS...." [ "Death By Hanging" by World Of Khaoz (live version from the "Hangovers from
Eden" album) begins playing over the speaker system. Out walks the Bounty hunters as the crowd boos severely. They give the "hook'em horns" hand gesture to the crowd and make their way down and into the ring. They enter the ring, and the Risley brothers slide out of the ring in respect of the big men. Both brothers talk to there father, as Stacy exits the ring.]

He wrenches down on it sharply, Putting a painful expression on Lobo's face, before Lobo breaks the hold by reversing it with pure power. Marshall quickly counters Lobo's poor arm bar though by rolling to the ground, and reversing it into once again an arm bar of his own. Marshall then smiles as he did this great counter, And pulls back on the arm some more..Marshall then lands a few kicks to the ribs while holding onto the arm bar bending the big Lobo over,
Marshall then tucks Lobo's arm behind his head, and jumps up landing a rocker dropped on Lobo, While also puting the elbow/arm in a weird position. Marshall while still laying on the mat, Looks around at the crowd almost in AWE as they pop cause of the brilliant wrestling...]

Marshall then drops down and applies a side headlock to Lobo trying to keep the big man off of his feet. Lobo then lifts him up again and this time Atomic Drops him. Orlando let's go of the side head lock and jumps up into the air in pain towards his corner. He tags in His Brother, Who jumps over the top rope and runs towards Lobo trying to stop him while he remains in the middle of the ring dazed. Marshall is caught though with a Tilt o whirl back breaker by the big man. Lobo then slowly drags Marshall over to the corner by his hair, and tags in his partner Hawke..Hawke automatically begins to stomp away on the wounded Marshall Risley.]
Samuel Smith : " Both Risley brothers are related to Ceaser Risley, a Young but talented wrestler in such federations as NEO and IWF/WOW...The Risley family know's how to wrestle, but also knows how to show us the unexpected...Don't look for Hawke and Lobo to squash them now that they have the advantage."
Fred Hart : " I am sure the SAW booked this for good reason....I don't think for one second it will be a squash."

Risley instantly sending him down to the mat face first.Hawke follows this up with an elbow to the back of the head, Then picks Marshall's head up and pounds it into the mat several times face first. Hawke get's up, almost laughing at

Risley.]
