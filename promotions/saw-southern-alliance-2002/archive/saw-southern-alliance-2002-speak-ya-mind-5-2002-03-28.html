Speak Ya Mind #5

28 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
SAW presents "Speak Ya Mind" on March 29TH, 2002 You will hear from the Following SAW superstars ************************* 1 - Tommy Flames 2 - Vern Walters 3 - "Tough Stuff" Rob Fabulous 4 - "The American Rage" Chris Page 5 - Antoine Bradley 6 - "The Universial Chump" Chris Payne ************************* << Fade In >> << We fade into a view of Tommy Flames sitting on the tailgate of his Green
1999 Ford Ranger late at night. The star's are out, and Flames seems to be gazing up at them.>>
[Tommy Flames] : " Amazing how So much detail, and creativity went into one idea." << Tommy leans back a bit, Looking at another group of stars.>> [Tommy Flames] : " In Highschool I didn't pay enough attention to the marvelous work that's put into these things, but Boy I wish I would have now."
<< Tommy looks straight ahead towards the camera, with that Sincere look on his face.>> [Tommy Flames] : " The detail that God put into these stars is more than I could ever put into a wrestling match, but as God went into detail on his creations, I try to go into detail on all my matches."
<< Tommy hops up off of the back of the tailgate, and dust off the back of his blue jeans. He is wearing a Tight muscle T shirt, and some dark blue jeans as we can now see in full.>>
[Tommy Flames] : " I am nowhere near the area of God, or some of the other
Brilliant people who we have had on this earth, but I do consider myself a smart man. I always go into detail preparing for every match, Ranging from working out, Running, Praying, Eating right, and studying video tapes. I am always prepared and always ready almost everything possible. These stars amaze me, but I have been known to amaze people in that ring.The next man I must amaze is Vern Walters, It appears to be that he doubts me. That's alright though, People have doubted people since the start of the time, and ALWAYS a couple people go against the doubt and prove the others wrong..I hope to be one of those people."
<< Flames lift his tailgate up and slams it shut.He leans up against it, Once again looking up at the beautiful stars.>>
[Tommy Flames] : " I made it here to New Orleans late last night.Me and my girlfriend drove down here non stop. Took about Seven hours, As we missed our turn once or twice. Since I have been here the lady has been laying out by the pool and enjoying some drinks, but I have been studying match tapes and studying the earth trying to get some inner peace, and I have found that Inner peace."
" A bunch of the words Vern said would of made your average man mad, but Not me. I blocked him out and have decided to go on with my own personal business.
Which is do my best, and don't worry about anybody else. We are both on the same quest, Which is to be the Southern Regional Champion..I came at you with respect, and you came at me with a way different view point. Thats fine, We have different looks on life I guess, but Vern..Did you really mean some of that stuff you said ? I sure hope not, Cause if so..I have already beat you and the match hasn't even started yet. That's how confused you sound Vern to be honest with you."
<< Tommy looks back towards the camera, and stands straight up taking his back off of the tailgate.>>
[Tommy Flames] : " You seem to think your the only one who has a "right" to the title, and Hell Vern, I think myself, Bungle and Matt James all have just as much right. That belt doesn't have anybody's name on it yet, and that belt hasn't been awarded to any of us quite yet. I have dreams and hopes just like you Vern, You need to learn to respect that.. You might not respect that now, but I hope to make you respect that by the end of the night Saturday. I am gonna be willing to put it all on the line when we meet in that ring. I made that promise to myself, to My father,To my girlfriend and the SAW fans before I stepped into the ring last week with Eddie James. I will not be a push over, I wasn't for Eddie James, and I won't be for you. If I start off slow, I will end up on fire, If I start out on fire, I will keep the flames coming, and Vern.."
<< Tommy does a long drawn out pause >> [Tommy Flames] : " The flames are coming Saturday Night man..This may be my only chance for along time to get my name out there and to win a title. I will not go down without a fight. New Orleans has been good to me since I have been here, and I do plan on being good right back to it."
<< Tommy walks around to the door of his truck, and opens it up. Before getting in he looks to the camera and says a final group of sincere words.>>
[Tommy Flames] : " Win, Lose or Draw Vern, All I want when the match is over for you to respect me, for the SAW to respect me, and for the fans to respect me. Please give me that chance, Please.."
<< Tommy enters the truck and shuts the door. The engine doesn't start, but as we peak in courtesy of the back window we can tell it seem's as if he is trying to get a nap out here in the middle of nowhere.>>

Vern. He is wearing a wife beater shirt, jeans, his old cowboy boots with duct tape on them, and a Sterling Marlin Nascar baseball hat. We can see his ribs taped because of the outline under the shirt. He has his left arm down as if to be protecting his ribs.>>

You're a pesky som' bitch. Yea I watched you and that wigger jackass Eddie

I'm still the winner, and I'm still going to whip your ass. You're pretty much a revolving loser. <<Short pause.>> You're a loser any way you look at it."
<<Vern reaches into his back pocket and pulls out a pouch of chew. It appears to be Red Man Golden Blend. He takes in a modest size and stuffs it into his mouth.>>
[Vern Walters] : "Tommy, you tell the world you are going to bring your best. And win, lose, or draw you claim to be bringing the heat with ya. Bring your best Tommy. Last week a damn monster brought his best and he couldn't get the job done. Can you get the job done? Can you? C'mon Tommy, this is for a step closer to championship. Don't sit back and get you're ass whipped. You know last week when I saw a fellow Texan getting his ass whipped so bad, you made me ashamed to call myself a Texan. At the Alamo the ratio of Texans to Mexicans killed was 1 to 8. Every Texan took out 8 Mexicans. And then you like some wanna be gangster dumbass whip your tail. You oughtta be ashamed of yourself.
<<VW turns his head to the left and spits to tabacco juice over the side of the pick up.>> Tommy, you bring shame to yourself and your home state!"
<<Vern looks down and shakes his head.>> [Vern Walters] : "I seen you running around with your girlfriend and you're new
Ford. You should be thinking about more important things, Tommy. If I got my ass whipped like you did I sure as hell wouldn't be runnin' around with no woman. Ain't you got a brain? It appears to me to be a lot of empty space between your ears, Tommy. Tommy you say your gonna be hot? Hot? You Jackass, it sure is gonna be hot when I tear ya down. Its gonna be hot when I take your damn head off. Yea Tommy Flames you're on fire. And you're gonna burn to the ground. You're gonna burn... <<VW smirks.>> Tommy, you're the bull's eye to my gun and your ass is the bull's eye for my boots."
<<With that the music fades out and so does the screen...>> ************************************************************************** ********* << *CLICK* >> [Unidentified Voice]: Not the best way to start, but hey << deep breath >>...I did what I had to do.
<< *CLICK* The visual fades in showing the rough faced Rob Fabulous sitting on a metal folding chair in his wrestling gear, attention directed towards a nameless man sitting across from him. We really can't see the man sitting accross from Rob. The only defining factors we can make out are that he is
African American, and is wearing a black and grey "SAW" t-shirt. The unidentified man motions towards Rob, and speaks. >>
[UM (unidentified man)]: Right. ...But still << begins tapping pencil on the upper edge of his clip board >> [UM]: A loss on the opening event can't make things much easier for you this week. << Rob shakes his head in disbelief, all the while smiling >> ["Tough Stuff" Rob Fabulous]: Look man, whatever. I wasn't even the guy that lost, it isn't important who it was anyway, but my point is... <<thumbs into chest>> Rob Fabulous wasn't held down for the three count.
<< Rob nods his head approvingly as the unidentified man speaks again >> [UM]: That is definately true Rob. << crosses his legs >> That event asside, a lot of people want to know what the check you recieved from Shawn
Cross was for? << Rob smirks, and shoots a wink to the man >> ["TS"RF]: Look, if I wanted to hear gossip, I would've went out with a couple of ladies instead of sitting here with you. << raises his eyebrows >>
<< The interviewer, sensing Rob's impatience, speaks >>
[Interviewer]: Fair enough, fair enough. ...What about this week's encounter you will have with Chris Page? All rumors aside, the fans nor the company can deny that they feel you have set your sights on The American Rage.
<< Rob folds his arms and leans back onto the chair as he speaks. >> ["TS"RF]: What can I say, you've got me by the balls on that one. <> The fact is, I know he's not what everyone says he is. And I sure as hell know he's not what he says he is. He's become my primary target for one reason, and one reason only; he's standing in my way. I'm not going to deny the guy his talent. Fabulous? << shakes his head >> Chris Page wishes on his BEST day to be as good as me. And even then, he's far from measuring up. << The interviewer sits motionless as he speaks >> [I]: Some people say that this early in your career, that you have made some outlandish accusation before and...<< interrupted >> ["TS"RF]: Look man, I know you have no idea who I am. ...But that all ends once we step into the ring. It'll be there that I shine, and show you just how easily a champion can be defeated.
<< The interviewer begins to speak, but is again interrupted by Rob >> ["TS"RF]: Speaking of Champions... << smirks >> Ask The American Rage is he remembers his stint in a promotion ran by Damian Knight...All-Pro Wrestling
I believe it was called. Ask him if he remembers trying to duplicate my good friend and mentor, Chris Champion. Ask him who pulled his card when he walked down the isle with an actor protraying "The Lousville Lip" Max Money. You ask him who ran him out of All-Pro Wrestling and chances are...he'll be speechless.
<< The interviewer begins writing something down while speaking >> [I]: ...Wow. I know who Champion is and I know who Max Money is. And I know the amount of respect surrounding those guys, so if it is true...that would put a serious dent in the credibility of The American Rage.
<< Rob holds his arms up at his sides and shrugs his shoulders as the visual slowly fades to black >>
************************************************************************** ********* [We fade slowly to black.] [A gentle yet artificial and cold tap, tap, tap of metal on metal.] [Slowly the sound of a key turning in a door. A thrust as the lock is drawn back and a slow, medical slice of noise as the door is, we assume, pushed open.]
[Click.] [Click.] [Click.] [A triangle of light creeps around the corner of a long drawn out corridor. A figure stands, or rather rests up against the clinical, medical grey coloured wall. Tiled, clean, sterile.]
"All my life I've been fighting." [The voice is deep, tinged with an accent. It comes at us slowly and most disturbing, completely calm.]
[The figure casts a glance up, still shrouded in relative darkness. One side of his face is illuminated far beyond what we can see; the other is pitch black.
He is powerful, that much is obvious. Broad shoulders mean he is forced to stand at a more acute angle than perhaps an ordinary man could. His hands are held down at his waist, by the movement in his shoulders it's obvious he is working something around and around. Our only clue is a few specks of round light.]
"All my life I have been battling to simply survive." [Still the door creeks open, with it the figures shadow grows. It is getting closer and closer with each second, elongated and distorted.]
"And I got lucky because I made it here." [SAW reads his T-shirt, the white jagged typeface is recognisable. His head is bowed, still in relative darkness as he looks at his hands passing a chain of
Rosary beads from one to the other.]
"But YOU had to stand in my way again." [A static flash of light from behind the figure as he looks up.] "Why did you do that? Why did you have to get involved?" [Almost strobing, it's eclectic. Too much for the eye to take. To much information to comprehend. Focus on the figure? The light? It's source? The surroundings?]
"This was a chance for me, this was an opportunity to show all the doubters that I could be somebody, that I could make it alone. You think I'm going to let you stand in my way. Do you really think I am going to let you take that from me...I'd rather see you dead than give this up. I would rather watch you bleed your way out of this world than let you snatch it out of my grasp."
[One man somewhere is having this very nightmare, his name? Rob Fabulous. All he can see is this man, all he can think about is just what is going to happen when they meet. All that runs through his mind is panic, crisis, fear as he meets this man face to face. Uncontrolled, unrestricted, unrivalled...this man, the man who stands before us now...
...'The American Rage' Chris Page.] "And now it looks like I have my wish at last." [A brief and unexpectedly sustained flash of light comes from behind, Page is blessed with a surprising halo as a room behind him lights up. His reflection and that of the camera faintly distort its contents, a long, long silver basin and a chair. But this is no ordinary chair, this is the chair used to take away life, heavy duty leather straps and shining silver buckles rest on the arms. A thick head guard juts out, the wood is worn, the ground underneath stained. The only blemish in an otherwise clinically sterile environment.]
"It's time we end it Fabulous." [Now we see, we see the face, the man that truly owns you. It has been a long build up Fabulous, but now you see, now you know that there really is NOTHING you can do to avoid this.]
"Rob, I have been waiting for this for a very long time. I have no doubt that you have been as well, but just how far are you prepared to push yourself? How far are you willing to take things when we meet?"
"How much do you want this Rob? How many nights sleep have you missed thinking about exactly what this means? What do you have planned inside that head of yours for me? I'd love to know Rob because if you have half the venom inside as
I do...well, one of us won't be leaving that ring by our own means. I've dreamt about Saturday for endless nights Rob, I have been thinking about it every waking moment of every day and each time I do, each time I think about what I am going to do to you I can't help but smile. You just don't realise Rob, you cannot possibly imagine what you have walked in to this time."
"Every time I close my eyes I see something new, a new threat to your life. Another way to end this. So many things Rob, I'm plagued by it, haunted by all the ways I could put you away. For good. It's bloody Rob, its cruel, it's painful. It's everything this nightmare should be and it's coming true,
Saturday March 30th Rob. No cowards, no attacks, no excuses. Just you, me and whatever cards we deal out to each other."
[Page takes a step forwards, a powerful, definite step in our direction. His eyes quite simply burn with desire, the green colour jumps out. His face is completely straight, angled and chiselled all the way; he is there, in front of us. Hands by his side, each one straining as he clasps the Rosary beads. Every vein is pronounced and jumping with adrenaline, every muscle is taut, rigid with utter hatred. How can you not fear this?]
"Would you stop? Or would you just keep on going until you had beaten me until
I couldn't bleed any more. That's what this means to me Rob, this doesn't have anything to do with the SAW Titles. This has nothing to do with Reaper or even
CK Walker. Forget The Reaper, forget CK Walker, forget everything you know."
[Page takes a long, deep breath. His head cocks back as he sucks in the emptiness of the corridor, his nose comes alive with the smell of the sterile walls, the disinfectant, the unnatural calm. It makes his hands pulse and his chest bulge but somehow he continues.]
"Because you have NEVER experienced anything like Chris Page, I swear to God
Fabulous, you are going to wish that you had never been born because I am not going to stop at the bell, I am not going to stop when you beg me to. I am going to make sure you NEVER, EVER get into the ring with me again. I don't care if I cripple you, I don't care if I kill you. I just don't care Fabulous because I...
...I F[censored]king hate you." [SNAP, Page's hands tear apart the Rosary beads, they fall in an dramatic fit of motion, tearing away his physical and mental shackles. His left hand is sent crashing into the wall. He doesn't even recoil as his knuckles smash through the tiling, a cloud of dust spills up. His forearm is an instant mess of blood, dust and pumping veins.]
"You see Rob, it is that simple. I want to make you suffer, I want to make you hurt like never before. I need to hear you wheeze, watch you bleed and see you beg in front of me. I need that power Rob, I need to take away everything that you have ever worked for. I want to be the one who ruined your life."
[Page draws his left hand up and rubs it across his face, a thin veil of crimson settles over his forehead. Slowly he clenches his fists together, a slow grimace creeps over him.]
"Ever since the beginning you have been the thorn in my side for so, so long
Fabulous. That constant knowledge that you, you, YOU DON'T RESPECT ME.
HOW DARE YOU FABULOUS... I deserve that Fabulous, I damn well OWN you and yet you keep opening your mouth and getting in my way. That's enough to make me want to see you bleed
Fabulous. You seriously think this is a game, that you can keep on playing without getting hurt? Fabulous, I just raised the stakes. It's all in so lets see your hand Rob. Let's see what you got. This is your chance Rob, this is your big opportunity to prove to me that you aren't just some nobody.
Can you do that Rob?" [And then, all of a sudden back to relative calm. Page's grip loosens and his arms flail down beside him. His neck relaxes and his head is free to scan around. A quick glimpse at the broken tiles, the spilled beads and then back up to camera.]
"It seems everywhere we turn, this business deals us a "joker", seemingly pushing the inevitable back, further, and further in time. The 'inevitable', fitting, because it is Rob, it is inevitable. Every time we enter the same company, it seems that this business deals one of us a crooked hand, and one of us must fold, and come back when the time is right. This time I have the winning hand, because quite simply, I am better.
I hold two cards in my hand, intensity and desire. I cannot, will not LET you beat me Rob and I will do whatever it takes to ensure that I am the only one left standing come Saturday, whether I have blood streaming down my face or not is up to you. But I guarantee that Saturday March 30th will be the end for you, one way or another, you are OVER."
[Page turns and looks into the back lit room, there it stands. He stands silent, motionless for a few seconds.]
"We end it now. Not because I can, Not because I want to, Simply because, I... AM... BETTER!" [His voice trails off...] [Click.] [Click.] [Click.] [And we are plunged back into silent darkness.] ************************************************************************** ********* <<The camera opens on a rather deserted playground.
