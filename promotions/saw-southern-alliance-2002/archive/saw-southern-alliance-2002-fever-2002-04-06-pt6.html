SAW (Southern Alliance, 2002) — Card

Saturday Night Fever, 6 April 2002 (part 6 of 6)

6 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[ The scene fades back into Johnny Jackson standing in the middle of a ring with a microphone in hand.]
Johnny Jackson : " Before we end our show with a main event to put most federation's main event's to shame...Lemme be the first to introduce you all to one of the best lightweight's in the wrestling world..Sort of unheard of, but by those who have heard the name an instant smile comes to there face. He is
"Dangerous" Devin Downs...."
[ "Dangerous" By Busta Rhymes begins to play and from the back comes a small, very well toned man wearing windpants. He slowly walks to the ring soaking in the mixed reaction. He slides under the bottom rope, and stands next to the ex bouncer now interviewer..With his hand on his shoulder.]
Devin Downs : " Hello there Johnny....Nice to be here. << Removes his hands from Johnny's shoulder, and shakes the man's hand.>> What's on ya mind my man.
What you want to talk about with Devin Downs infront of Vicksburg, Mississippi and it's great wrestling fans..."
Johnny Jackson : " The Lightweight title was announced last week to the public,
Is that what brought you to the SAW."
Devin Downs : " I'll admit it did. I know, I should of come for the competition and the great fans, but it took the dragging in of a lightweight title to drag me in here...I am happy to be here, but the gold is an extra bonus."
Johnny Jackson : " Fair enough.... Any plans on who to go after first, Or who to alliance yourself with."
Devin Downs : " No real plans yet. I just want to have a chance at winning the title, Other than that all is fair game..People like Bungle, Lenny, Taylor
Threat and Johnny Danger better be on there toes now that I am here...I saw
Bungle earlier, and he is impressive, but I am _DANGEROUS_..."
Johnny Jackson : " That you are...You competing anywhere besides SAW ? " Devin Downs : " I was in GCW, But my contract expired and I felt holding the
Cruserweight title for almost a year was enough for me there. Thus I came here,
Looking for new challenges and setting some higher goals for myself. Which starts with winning the lightweight title, and continues with myself winning the Southern Title.."
Johnny Jackson : " Big goals there Devin, Good luck in your quest to acomplish that, but while I got you here....What you think about the guys about to come down to the ring and fight."
Devin Downs : " All our cocky, All our confident, but most importantly all are talented. Those four men are DANGEROUS just like myself.It would be an honor to step into the ring with any of them, One day soon, but for now....I'll take what is tosses my way and run with it...."
Johnny Jackson : " Thanks for coming out here Devin..Let's get out of here, and become fans in the back while the stars take center stage."
[ Devin nods, as "Dangerous" begins to play again. Both men walk to the back together, Chit chatting along the way. Once they reach the back, we fade back to the announcer's booth for the final preperations before the main event.]
Samuel Smith : " Johnny was right...The stars are about to take center stage." Fred Hart : " Another star is about to join us...Frankie Cash is about to come out here and Join us Samuel."
Samuel Smith : " I can't wait << Chuckles >> Honestly tonight has been great..I don't even think Frankie Cash being out here with us could spoil this night. We crowned a champion, and Antoine got out of jail.."
[ "Greed" by Godsmack blares over the PA. A few seconds tick by and then through the entrance curtain comes Frankie Cash. He is wearing his shiny black
"pleather" tights with green dollar signs on each leg, black boots, black elbowpads, black wrist tape and his ever present green wraparound shades.
Cash's long black hair is wet and bulled back from his face. He smiles cockily and strides to the aisle. The fans boo long and loud as Cash steps in the direction of the announcer's booth. He sit's down beside Samuel, and applies a pair of head sets.]
Frankie Cash : " Hello guys....It's definitely not an honor for me to be out here with you both, but I am sure it is for you guys."
Samuel Smith : " Oh yes....The highlight of my announcing life << Chuckles >>
Just call the match Frankie, and I think we will all be alright.."
Fred Hart : " Agreed..." Frankie Cash : " Fuggedaboutit!!!!!!! I am here to do more than call a match. I am here to showcase a match, To hype a match, To spotlight a match.."
Samuel Smith : " Which basically means call a match.." Fred Hart : " Agreed." Frankie Cash : " Well thank you dumb and dumber....Don't we got a match to
"call" as you guys say.."
Stacy Normand : " It's now time for our main event." [Crowd Pop] Stacy Normand : " Introducing first...The team of Tough Stuff Rob Fabulous and the Super duper Mega star C K Walker...."
'[ My Way' by Limp Bizkit begins to blare as the lights dim. C.K Walker struts out wearing his usual attire and a C.K Walker black t-shirts. Walker looks around at the crowd, smirks then walks down to the ring, making sure none of the fans can touch him on the way through and picking on some as he does.
Walker climbs the steps, faces the crowd, shakes his head then dips through the ropes. He takes off his shirt and throws it to the outside. As the music fades
Walker jaws at the crowd, Until sharply Rob Fabulous music begins to play...From the back then struts Fabulous, who very cocky like walks down to the ring.He slides in under the bottom rope, and shakes hands with his partner for the night C K Walker.]
Frankie Cash : " GREAT TAG TEAM RIGHT THERE......BEST TAG TEAM IN THE HISTORY OF WRESTLING BESIDES MYSELF AND STEVE GREEDY." Fred Hart : " They have never tagged before anywhere, at anytime Frankie..." Frankie Cash : " Call it a gut feeling.." Stacy Normand : " And there opposition...." [ Before Stacy can announce anybody else Chris Page runs from the back and slides into the ring...Fabulous exits the ring on his own, as Walker is tossed out by his hair threw the middle ropes.]
Frankie Cash : :" Damn cheater.." [ Stacy exits the ring and slowly from the back stalks the Reaper. He stands on the ring apron awaiting the tag in from Page.]
************************************************************************** *********************** Main Event No Time Limit Tag Team War C K Walker and Rob Fabulous vs Reaper and Chris Page Referee - Wayne West ************************************************************************** *********************** [ Fabulous helps his partner up and back onto the apron. Fabulous then rolls into the ring to start the match off against Chris Page.]
Fred Hart : " No love loss between these two guys.." Samuel Smith : " That's an under statement.." [ Page stalks over towards Fabulous and trys to lock up with him, but Fabulous side steps just sending Page into the ropes, Using his anger against him.
Fabulous then tries a kick, but his foot is grabbed by Page who pushes him down to the mat easily since he was off balanced. Page tries to drop an elbow, but
Fabulous rolls out of the way. Page gets up quickly and trys another elbow, but
Fabulous rolls again...This time Page is slower to get up, and he quickly get's caught with a swift kick to the head by Fabulous as he tries to get up..Fabulous follows this up with a with a leg drop setting an early slow paced tone for this match.]
Frankie Cash : " That was Money.." Samuel Smith : " Just a leg drop." Fred Hart : " Agreed." Frankie Cash : " There yall go again." [ Fabulous picks Page up by the hair, and head butt's him. Fabulous staggers back, and lunges in for another...Page falls to the mat, and Fabulous almost does. Fabulous dazed tags in C K Walker...Walker and Fabulous then do a nice double suplex on Page.]
Frankie Cash : << Standing up clapping >> Well done...Very well done.." [ Fabulous doesn't exit the ring though, and continues to stomp on Page with the help of his partner Walker.]
Samuel Smith: "I can guarantee that Wayne West will have his hands full with this one." Fred Hart : "I second that motion." Frankie Cash : " At least you didn't say Agreed this time.." Samuel Smith : " Agreed..." Frankie Cash : " AHHHHH....You to ? " [ As Fabulous finally exits the ring, Walker looks to the crowd briefly, Giving
Page some daylight. Page get's to his knees and Nails Walker with a hard right hand. Walker rolls out of the ring, Holding his jaw.]
Frankie Cash : " Illegal move..." Samuel Smith : " True, but not one to be dq'd for, Least not in any wrestling federation I have ever seen in action."
Frankie Cash : " Don't you say agreed you lil twerp << Points at Fred Hart >> " Fred Hart : " Okay...Relax man.." [ Fabulous and Walker regroup on the outside as Fabulous whispers something into Walkers ear. Inside the ring, West finally orders Walker back into the ring. Finally, Walker climbs back into the ring, while Fabulous hops to the ring apron.]
Samuel Smith : " C K and Chris trying to slow down the match up a bit, Might work out good with the high intensity level of Page here tonight and the obvious strength of the huge Reaper."
Frankie Cash : " Of course it will work well...C K and Rob are brilliant in the ring.." [ Page and Walker lock up...Page pushes Walker back into Walker's corner, Not being a very vetrean tag wrestler, Stays in the corner landing back hand chops..Fabulous then reaches out and pulls Page down by his hair. Walker like a hungry dog stays on top of Page with several kicks.]
Frankie Cash : " I told you all..." Samuel Smith : " The match is young Frankie..Sit back and enjoy.." Fred Hart : " Yes please, Just sit back..Don't even worry about announcing." Frankie Cash : " I hope for your sake that didn't mean anything." [ Page crawls back to his feet blocking out the pain of the swift kicks, and
Walker jumps on his back with a sleeper hold type move. Page stumbles briefly with Walker hanging on tightly to Page with the maneuver, but then Walker is caught with a huge jaw breaker sending him to his back...Page gets back to his feet and stomps away on C K Walker.]
Fred Hart : " Prolly should of made a tag there, but didn't...Page is new to the tag team scene, and has let his anger get the bst of him.."
Samuel Smith : " Can you blame him ? Fabulous, Walker and the man sitting beside me has made his life hell in the SAW...Literally Hell.."
Frankie Cash : " Agreed << Laughs >> " [ Page picks Walker up slowly and short clotheslines him down. Page then runs at Rob Fabulous who quickly dives off of the apron. Page then slowly walks over to his corner and tags in Reaper. Walker crawls to his corner as Reaper moves towards him, and beg's for a timeout. Fabulous stands behind him also asking for a timeout...Reaper smiles, and moves in closer to the enemies corner. He nails Fabulous knocking him off of the apron, but leaves himself open to C K
Walker who lands a kick to the lower area..West warns Walker, but let's the match continue. The small C K Walker then get's up and begins to stomp away on
Reaper. Fabulous gets back up onto the apron and reaches out. Walker tags him in, and both men stomp away on Reaper before picking him up and sending him into the ropes only to execute a double clothesline.]
Frankie Cash : " More good team work.." Samuel Smith : " They are doing better than I thought they would..Much better." [ Page stomps his boot on the ring apron, trying to get the fans to rally behind Them. They of course do so and begin a "We want Page" chant...Reaper sits straight up, Looking in the eyes of Walker and Fabulous who look in shock.
Walker exits the ring, and Fabulous back peddles as Reaper stands up tall.] Fred Hart : " HERE WE GO......DARKSIDE TIME..." Samuel Smith : " Reaper and Simon Starr both got some weird powers..." [ Reaper moves towards Fabulous who trys to hit him with right hands, but
Reaper shakes them off. Reaper then lands a punch of his own sending Fabulous into the ropes. Reaper then runs at Fabulous clotheslining him over the top rope. Fabulous lands on his feet though, and pulls Reaper to the outside.Where the two once again begin to trade punches. C K Walker then walks over to the brawl and begins to help Fabulous...Page runs over nailing Walker with a running elbow, then stops infront of the announcer's table. Page then taunts
Frankie Cash who stands up quickly.]
Fred Hart : " Way to close for me.....Go away guys.." [ Cash sits down his headset, and steps over infront of the table to go eye to eye with Page. Page kicks him in the gut, and puts him in a powerbomb position..He points to the table, and the crowd pops.Page lands a few double ax handle blows, then prepares for the Powerbomb.]
Samuel Smith : " Cash is going threw a table, Our damn table......" [ Page as he is about to lift Cash up, gets hit from behind by Fabulous with a forearm.Fabulous is then caught from behind by a clothesline from Reaper sending him face first to the floor...Cash staggers back to his seat at the announcer's table breathing heavily.]
Fred Hart : " Nice to have ya back Frankie..." Frankie Cash : " SHUT UP....I can't beleive he tried to put me threw a table. Just cause I did it to him, Doesn't mean he can do it to the Cash - Man..." [ Reaper rolls Fabulous back into the ring, and helps his Partner up to his feet.Walker has managed to get back to his corner as well...]
Fred Hart : " Control starting to come back here...." Samuel Smith : " Might get to see a good ending after all." [ Reaper enters the ring and lands a chokeslam on Fabulous..He then tags in
Page, who runs in and begins to pound Fabulous, while mounting him. Page picks
Fabulous up and hits the ropes..He comes off hitting the dazed Fabulous with a
HUGE clothesline.]
Fred Hart : " Has there been any pin attempts in this match ? " Samuel Smith : " Nope..That's why it was booked as a tag team war.." [ Page picks Fabulous up once again, and tries for a piledriver, but is back body dropped to the mat..Fabulous crawls to his corner and tags in C K Walker.]
Frankie Cash : " Fabulous instead of dropping to his knee's after that move like most men do, Stays on his feet and makes the tag..GREAT MOVE..."
Fred Hart : " He did show good determination there.." [ Walker enters the ring and begins to stomp away on Page showing a high intensity level. Walker picks Page up and whips him into the ropes, As he comes off Walker lands a huge spine buster, with his head going to the groin area of
Page at the same time.]
Frankie Cash : " HA HA HA " Samuel Smith : " Ouch.....I am sure even for a white man that hurts." [ Walker then hits the ropes and somersauls off landing on top of Page with a splash.He then hooks the legs and the tights going for a cover.]
[ONE] [TWO] [Kickout by Page..] [ Walker grabs Page by his hair pulling him towards his corner. Walker tags in
Fabulous and both men stomp on Page in there corner. Walker exits the ring, and
Fabulous continues the attack in the corner by himself until the ref finally pushes him back. Fabulous picks Page up and begin's to nail him with right hands. Fabulous shoots Page out of the corner and into one of the other corners. Fabulous charges in,but Page moves out the way in desperation..Both men lay on the mat.]
Frankie Cash : " Lucky move by Page..." [ Page crawls to his corner and reaches for the tag but Reaper Pulls away...Reaper hops off the apron and glances back into the ring before walking to the back.]
Samuel Smith : " Tell me he just didn't.." Frankie Cash : " Oh I think he did..." Fred Hart : " It's two on One now..This doesn't look good at all for Chris
Page, espically when it comes to his quest for revenge."
[ Page uses the ropes to get to his feet and catches Walker running his way who also didn't make a tag..Walker falls and in comes Fabulous who is caught with a hard right hand as well sending him to the mat. Page executes a powerslam on
Fabulous, Then hops up to catch Walker coming at him with a huge clothesline turning him inside out.]
Frankie Cash : " One sec guys...." [ Cash sensing the momentum changing gets up and climbs to the apron.Page of course goes after him throwing a punch, but the plan was well thought out, and
Cash saw it coming. He blocks it, then grabs the head of Page, while dropping down to the floor. Page's throat goes over the top ring ropes and he sling shots off..Landing in the arms of Rob Fabulous who lands A Death Valley
Driver.,..Fabulous puts Walker's arm over Page being he is the legal man and the ref hesistantly counts.]
[ONE] [TWO] Samuel Smith : " I hate to see it go down like this.." [THREE] Fred Hart : " NO.........PLEASE DON'T LET THIS HAPPEN.." [ Cash rolls into the ring, and helps Fabulous stomp away on Page..Both men then pick Page up and sits him on the top rope..Fabulous climbs the rope and lands a huge superplex. Cash then goes to the top rope and lands the money maker.]
Fred Hart : " THEY ARE PURE EVIL.....REAPER, WALKER, CASH, FABULOUS...ALL OF THEM.." [ Walker finally gets to his feet and stands over Page yelling at him.] Fred Hart : " Fans...We are out of time...Page has been screwed by Reaper and
Cash..Then beaten by Three men..."
[ All three men stomp away on Chris Page as the scene fades to black, With the fans in the arena throwing ice and beer's into the ring at the three men stomping away on one of there favorite's Chris Page.]
