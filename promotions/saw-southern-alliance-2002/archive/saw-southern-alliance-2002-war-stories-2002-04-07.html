Sunday Night War Stories, 7 April 2002 (part 1 of 2)

7 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
************************************************************************** *********************** APRIL 7TH, 2002 _SUNDAY NIGHT_ WAR STORIES From West Monroe, Louisiana The MAIN EVENT for the evening put's Frankie Cash Vs Patrick Robbinfield Also in ACTION. Dark Tower vs Damian Rock Johnny Danger vs Simon Starr Business Men vs Bayou Bandits Making appearances Antoine Bradley Cedric Mike Pace Mike Johnson Chris Page Reaper Rob Fabulous Tommy Flames C K Walker ************************************************************************** *********************** [ Fade In ] [ We fade into the SAW arena in West Monroe, Louisiana for our third straight
War Stories from this place in a row. The arena is pretty closed to packed, and would be considered a sold out arena technically, even though a few seats remain. As we pan across the crowd we see such signs as " I want to team with
Chris Page" , " Flames = Champion ", and " Reaper is God" with horns coming out of the word God.]
Voice : " Welcome to SUNDAY NIGHT WAR STORIES !!!!! " [ The camera now goes to the announcer's booth where we see two men beaming with excitement. One is the young man we only see on War Stories, Joey Howard, and the other is Long time announcer Fred Hart. Both men are dressed nice, and have papers in front of them for side notes. Fred continues where he left off,
As we focus in on the announcer's table he continues.]
Fred Hart : " I am Fred Hart, and beside me is Joey Howard. We got an exciting card lined up for you all here tonight. Coming off of our most exciting and controversial Saturday Night Fever yet."
Joey Howard : " Tonight we will be discussing that controversial card from top to bottom, Plus getting you up to date for the Fever coming up next week."
Fred Hart : " That's right Joey...Last night was so action packed, I don't know where to start."
Joey Howard : " Then start at the front Fred..Lenny and Threat need to have a rematch somewhere down the road."
Fred Hart : " That's for sure...I am not sure how that match would of turned out if Arthur Dexter Bradley's manager would of stayed out the way and left
Taylor Threat alone. I know Threat helped the man a couple weeks back, but that's just outta respect for ADB...Not for him. Patrick Robbinfield needs to realize that."
Joey Howard : " Robbinfield is a very persistant person, and he showed all of us that last night. Robbinfield will be in our main event tonight, as he steps into the ring for the second time in the SAW. This time with Frankie Cash.."
Fred Hart : " Cash showed us last night and the week before what he is here for..To make sure Chris Page life is hell, and so far he is doing a good job. I have a feeling he is also gonna make sure Robbinfield's life is hell here tonight."
[ The camera cuts to the back where we see Frankie Cash entering the building with that trademark smirk on his face.]
Joey Howard : " There he is....One of the biggest stars in the SAW.." ******WHAM********* Fred Hart : " OH MY GOD AND THERE HE GOES !!!! " [ Frankie Cash instantly goes flying up against the wall and slowly slides down the wall to his backside, With his body leaned against the wall. We see Chris
Page standing over him, who caught him with a running elbow which busted him open. A blank look of intensity is on Page's face, as He looks down at Cash and his blood.]
Chris Page : " You messed up Cash....Putting me threw a table was okay, Coming to the SAW wanting a match with me is okay, Talking a lil noise about me is okay, but costing me a chance at the Southern Title....Is not okay.."
[ Page kicks Cash in the head at the point in which he is bleeding. Cash tries to cover up, but Page keep's kicking letting out grunts with every fierce kick.]
Chris Page : " Tonight your gonna see why I told the SAW I am a madman. Tonight your gonna see why I am the "American Rage" Chris Page.."
[ Page picks Cash up and tosses him over his shoulder. The two then head into the building together, With Cash begging for Page to leave him alone.]
[ Pan back to a shocked Announcer's booth.] Fred Hart : " I can't say that it suprises me Chris Page is doing this. He has been put threw two tables, Sneak attacked, Jumped in the back with his Knee being taken out, and left to fight a tag match all alone...In the last couple weeks. He has alot of built up frustration inside of him."
Joey Howard : " Yes indeed, and he just let some of it out on Frankie Cash. I wander if Cash will be able to be in our main event tonight ? "
Fred Hart : " I am not sure, I guess it depends on how long Page messes with him, or what all Page does to him."
Joey Howard : " I am scared to imagine what Page is gonna do to him here tonight..Frankie Cash might want to start praying, Just incase Page goes a little over board."
Fred Hart : " Praying Joey ? " Joey Howard : " Ya cause he might be meeting his end here soon.." Fred Hart : " Let's not talk like that okay....I hope Chris Page don't take it that far. << Shivers >> On a good note....Tommy Flames has won the SAW Southern
Regional Title and he is here tonight to tell us all about it."
Joey Howard : " I wish we knew what his girlfriend's name was.There always together, but we have no clue what her name is."
Fred Hart : " We will have to ask Tommy that one day. He is a real class act guy in and out of the ring Joey."
Joey Howard : " Yes he is....I had the privledge of talking to him in the back before we went onto the air, and he is just such a nice person. You can't help but go for him."
Fred Hart : " Two men who are gonna be going for him soon is Vern Walters and
Eddie James..The match has been signed for Down on the Bayou..Triple Threat
Style..."
Joey Howard : " Not to mention..HARDCORE RULES...That match is gonna bring us some bone cringing maneuver's I am sure. I am GLAD I don't have to call that match, That way when I turn my head away from the action, nobody will laugh.."
Fred Hart : " I am sure some people are laughing now though...Got a weak heart eh Joey ? " Joey Howard : " When it comes to stuff like that..Yes I do..." Fred Hart : " Well, Let's get to our first match and see how well you handle it..Interesting lil saga we have going here....The wrestler's hate each other, and so do there managers...We have there managers in the ring here tonight.."
Joey Howard : " Both our former wrestler's though, So it should be a good match up." ************************************************************************** *********************** Opening Bout Dark Tower vs Damian Rock Ten Minute Time Limit Referee : John Mercer ************************************************************************** *********************** Both managers enter the ring, and left there wrestling friends in the back. Both men our former champions in other federations. They each had there own style and presence. Dark Tower being your huge powerful wrestler, While Rock being your crafty vetrean, With lots of suttle quickness and athletic talent.
They didn't do your typical lock up to start the match up, Both men went eye to eye and blamed the other for there wrestler's slump at the start of the
SAW...Rock tried to shrug off the words, And walk away to his corner, but was
Grabbed By Dark Tower. The two then went eye to eye again, and Rock slapped the huge Dark Tower. Tower smiled and nailed Rock with a right hand staggering him back. Tower tried for another right hand, but Rock ducked under and hit the ropes behind Tower.Tower turned around in time to catch a running clothesline from Rock, Which had lil to no effect on Tower...Rock's eye's then got big, and as Tower went to throw another punch..Rock dropped to the mat and rolled out of the ring. Tower followed though, as He wants revenge badly.
Soon as Tower drops to the outside Rock catches him with a dropkick to the knee knocking him to his knees. Rock then lands a couple haymakers to the top of the head of Tower, And throws his head into the ring apron..Tower using the apron stays on his feet, but has a dazed look in his eyes...Rock then uses his vertical to jump up onto the apron, and lands a sweet kick to Tower pushing him away from the apron. Rock then dives off the Apron with a half Plancha and catches Tower..Both men fall back into the guard rail and the crowd pops.
Rock get's up first of course, and kicks Tower in the head...Which makes the back of his head go into the guard rail fiercly. Rock then rolls Tower into the ring, and slides under the bottom rope himself. Rock lands a couple swift leg drops, then goes for a cover.
[ONE] [TWO] [T...Kickout] Rock slaps the mat once as he is suprised from the kick out,But does stay on the attack. He lands a couple rib shots with his feet and hands, but then is met by Tower with a knee to the midsection..Rock bent down and Tower put him in the piledriver position, But instead of a piledriver..Tower jumps up and comes down on the top of the head.Sending Rock to the ground. Tower then picks Rock up by his hair, and hip tosses him back to the ground easily showing huge power.
This lead to Johnny Danger who has a match later coming out...He stood in
Rock's corner and began to root him on. Tower blocked this out and kept on the offensive end. Tower applied a huge headlock on Rock....And wrenched down on it good. Rock tried to fight out of it, but couldn't...It appeared Rock was gonna have to submit from a vicious headlock, but before he tapped...Danger jumped up onto the apron. Tower broke the hold and walks over to Danger.
From the back TVR ran out and pulled Danger off the ropes. The two had verbal wars, which led to nothing, but both men rooting on there partner...
Tower went back to the attack and landed some high impact moves...Before Finally messing up when he went for a back body drop, Rock hit him with a rocker dropper type move. Both men laid on the ground...Rock got up first, and walks towards Tower, but was caught with a punch to the midsection.Tower then threw Rock right threw the middle ropes to the floor...Trying to give himself a breather.
On the Outside TVR ran over and caught him with a running knee....Danger saw this and ran over to TVR hitting him with a huge clothesline, sending the bigger man down. Danger then rolled TVR back into the ring. Danger enters as well.
Tower back peddles and watches TVR and Danger trade punches in the middle of the ring. Damian Rock also slid back into the ring, and began to help Danger.
Of course Tower comes over, and all four men begin to trade punches with one another. The referee trie's to get control, but seemingly can not..He calls for the bell, but this war continues...Danger lands a superkick on Tower's chin from the side where he couldn't see it coming and sends him over the top rope. TVR lands a huge clothesline which makes Rock roll out of the ring..In the ring TVR and Danger once again begin to talk about what's been going on in the SAW in a heated manner.
The TWO come close to going back at it with each other, but right before they can security comes out and breaks the two up...Slowly all the men are lead to the back, With much heat being drawn.
Winner - No Contest Time - Six Minutes and Thirty Five seconds Finisher - None ************************************************************************** *********************** Fred Hart : " Nothing solved whatsoever there.....Danger has a match later tonight, He might want to watch his back."
Joey Howard : " I don't know which one I am rooting for there. There both cocky and evil. Both have managers and both have there styles...The only difference is there obvious height and weight difference."
Fred Hart : " I agree with you there.." [ Suddenly we cut to the back where we see a Dark room lit by a couple candles. In the room we can see fairly well Chris Page standing up with his back turned to us.] Chris Page : " You want to screw me over ? You want to screw me over ? <<
Begins to raise his voice >> YOU WANT TO SCREW ME OVER ? << Whispers >> Well so be it.This is what you get when you do so..."
[ Page steps to the side a bit, and we now see Frankie Cash tied up in a chair. A look of fear is on Frankie's face.] Frankie Cash : " Man...I got a match later tonight, Please let me go. I have only wrestled one match so far, and that was when I << Pause >> Beat you....I need to get the rest of the ring rust off."
Chris Page : " Oh you will wrestle lil Frankie...I won't make you miss your match tonight."
[ Page begin's to slap Cash on the top of the head.] Frankie Cash : " Chris...Please stop..." [ Page laughs and continues to slap Frankie on the top of the head. Page then bends down and goes eye to eye With Cash.]
Chris Page : " I will be back in a moment...It's time to go get revenge on the other guys who have screwed me over here in the SAW."
[ Page rubs Cash on the top of the head like he was a dog....Then walks off. Cash screams a couple times " What about me"....But Page keep's going.] [ Instead of cutting back to the announcer's booth, We fade to a nice office somewhere in the building where we see Mike Pace, Antoine Bradley and Cedric all sitting down.]
Mike Pace : " I am SOOOOO Sorry about what happened. I am gonna make it up to you Antoine. Antoine Bradley : " We will see." Cedric : " I am sure you will Mike....<< Hits Antoine on the shoulder >> Mike Pace : " Are you sure you don't want to press charges ? If you don't want your name involved, I'll do it..."
Antoine Bradley : " Nah, Just give him to me in the ring, and make sure a weapon is legal. I want to show him how it is in the joint..."
Mike Pace : " I will see what I can do..." [ Antoine and Cedric get up out of there chairs.] Antoine Bradley : " Don't see what you can do, Do it man..You owe me." [ Antoine walks out of the room.] Cedric : " Thanks for the chance to be here Mike." [ Cedric walks out.] Mike Pace : " I can't beleive I thought it was Antoine...Tommy Taylor is gonna pay, And pay next week. What can I do though to make it up to Antoine ? << Rubs
Chin >> "
[Cut back to the announcer's table.] Fred Hart : " We got Page running around in the back like a madman, and the Guy whose supposed to stop madmen running around trying to make one of the wrestler's happy. Sure, He owe's Antoine a bit, but he need's to focus on his job as well."
Joey Howard : " I think Page need's to get a lil revenge..Maybe it's good Pace is side tracked."
Fred Hart : " Possibly so...What do you think about the current tag situation
Joey." Joey Howard : " I think both tag teams we saw the other night were very impressive. I also think the team we see tonight in the Business men will be good...Not good guys, but definitely good wrestlers."
Fred Hart : " I would agree...The Bounty Hunters won the match the other night, but the Risley Brothers reall left an impression."
Joey Howard : " You mean the new breed don't you..? " Fred Hart : " Oh yes..I forgot about what they did to Jeremy Rivers. One of those moments last night I tried to block out."
Joey Howard : " Understandable, It was kinda _SICKENING_.." Fred Hart : " That wasn't the only thing sickening last night...Reaper walking out on Page causing him to go crazy here tonight,and Rud Groz suplex exhibition on Chris Payne were also both sickening.."
Joey Howard : " I am sure if we looked deeper we could come up with more, but there was also good things going on..Flames winning the title, Vern Walters coming to show his support, and lots of wrestlers showing there true talents."
Fred Hart : " Yes, the SAW does have lot's of fine wrestlers...I have seen major talent in guys like Rob Fabulous, C K Walker, Lenny, Johnny Danger and of course the current Regional Champion Tommy Flames among others.."
Joey Howard : " I am telling you to watch out for the Risley Brothers....They looked nervous in there debut against the Bounty Hunters, but showed us moment's of being great."
Fred Hart : " I think both of them will be fine SAW wrestler's as well.." [ Cut backstage to Chris Page standing outside C K Walker's locker room with a plate. He is holding it in the typical Butler or waiter position. The plate has
Donut's on it, Jelly and some Coffee.]
*****Knock Knock***** C K Walker : << From inside the room >> " Who is it....? " Chris Page : <<From outside the room >> " Room service." C K Walker : " I didn't order any..You do know this is the Super Duper Mega
Star C K Walker's room right ? "
Chris Page : " It's compliments of the SAW....Your favorite dish has been prepared." C K Walker : " THEY FIXED MY FAVORITE DISH ? How did they know what it was..Oh my Gosh....I love these guys."
[ Walker answers the door, and Page catches him with his free hand.He then hits him with the plate knocking him down. Page get's down on a knee right over him, and begin's to speak.]
Chris Page : " So you want to play with the madman ? " [ Page dumps the Coffee on Walker..Then shoves the donut's in his mouth.] Chris Page : " Well the madman doesn't want to play with you." [ He pours the Jelly on Walker, who is now kicking and screaming trying to get away from the present situation. Page just laughs and uses the plate in which all the items were on to Pound Walker in the head. He does this about five time's in anger, Busting the top of the forhead of Walker open.]
Chris Page : " I will get you everytime you mess with me Walker..What you did last night was your last time to ever put Chris Page down on the mat. With help or without help....Doesn't matter..Tell your friends Reaper and Fabulous I am coming for them...Just as I did you."
[ Page hits Walker with the plate again, and walks out of the room as if nothing happened. Walker remains on the ground for the time being, and we fade back to the announcers.]
Fred Hart : " ....... " Joey Howard : " Page is making sure his presence is felt....I got a feeling were gonna see him alot more tonight, but before we see him any more, or take a commercial break..Let's go to the ring for our second match."
************************************************************************** *********************** Second Bout Ten Minute Time Limit Business Men vs The Bayou Bandits Referee : Aaron Kent ************************************************************************** *********************** Tommy Taylor started it out for the Business Men, as Bayou Bandit Joe Williams started it off for there team..Both Tommy and Joe have about the same build and body look. Tommy though thought he was in much better shape, and asked Joe to step back so he could pose.Tommy then posed for the crowd which Boo'd quickly.
Joe then took center stage and poses for the crowd which let off a huge face pop. This sent Taylor into a temper tantrum..And once again he posed. A different pose, but the same result from the crowd. Smiling Joe then poses, but was attacked from behind by Taylor with a running elbow. Tommy now smiling as well, Followed the attack up with a few double ax handle blows pounding Joe to the mat.
Taylor picked Joe up by the hair, and tossed him in the direction of there corner. The Huge Seth Baptist held onto Joe, and Taylor ran in landing a body splash. Seth let go of Joe, and the ref began to warn him. The team of the
Business Men didn't seem to take it seriously though. Tommy stomped Joe down in the corner, then tagged in Seth Baptist. This led to a fairly good size heel pop.
Seth picked Joe up by his throat, and held him in the air laughing at him. Seth then dropped him right back down thinking he would let on his rear end, but Joe landed on his feet..He threw a couple hands to the face of Seth Baptist, but
Seth shook them off and pushed Joe back into the corner. Seth followed in with a knee, then a snapmare putting him out of the corner. Seth then kicked Joe in the back of the head as he was setting up, Knocking him down to the mat.Seth followed this up with a lazy cover.
[ONE] [Kickout] Seth chuckled, and tossed Joe in the direction of his tag partner Frank Bradford. Frank was hesitant to tag in, but did tag in. He circled Seth Baptist, before trying to lock up with him. As he locked up, Seth instantly pushed him down to the mat showing his huge power. Frank got back up, but this time went in landing a kick to the midsection. Frank then put Seth in a side headlock, but was pushed off and shoved into the ropes. Frank came off, and was hit with a huge shoulder block.Frank hopped back to his feet, Hit the ropes again and once again was caught with a shoulder block. This time Frank stays on the mat.
Seth grabs the leg of Frank Bradford and pulls him over towards there corner. Seth Tags in Taylor and drops an elbow on the leg of Bradford, keeping his weight on the leg making it impossible for Bradford to move and causing some pain.Taylor then comes in and drops a beautiful knee to the face of Bradford landing square on the temple. Seth rolls out of the ring, and Taylor looks as if he is going for a cover, but instead does a blatant choke hold. The ref makes him break the hold with his cout, and afterwards warns Taylor who just smiles.
Taylor then lands a snap suplex, but hangs on to it..Working his way back to his feet, he lands another snap suplex, and this time bridges into a fisherman's pin.
[ONE] [TWO] [Save by Joe Williams...] Joe stayed in the ring to talk trash to Taylor which was a mistake, as he was caught with a running boot to the head by the big Seth Baptist. Seth then tossed Joe over the top rope, and picked up Bradford...The ref tried to get him out of the ring, but Seth wouldn't listen....Seth then Held Bradford up in the
Powerbomb position, and told Tommy to go to the top rope.Tommy did so and
Caught Bradford with a huge leg drop helping Seth drop him down to the mat with a legdrop/powerbomb combo.Taylor then went for the cover, with Seth standing over him.
[ONE] [TWO] [THREE] Both men stand tall in the middle of the ring with there hands raised. The crowd boo's, and The two men just walk out of the ring smiling, Leaving there impression on us for the evening.
Winner - The Business Men Time - Six Minutes and Twenty One seconds Finisher - Takin' care of Business [ Legdrop/Powerbomb combo] ************************************************************************** *********************** Fred Hart : " Sadly, but truthfully The Business Men show us there a good tag team..We will be right back, we got to take a commercial break."
[The scene fades from the announcers booth to a view of the offical SAW shirt. It is just like the one Joey is wearing on tonight's show..It has SAW in big black letters, On a white background.The shirt then flips over to the other side, and you see " The south will rise again"....The shirt flips back over to the front, and onto the screen comes an image of Rob Fabulous.]
Rob Fabulous : " Tough Stuff << Points his thumbs at his chest >> Rob Fabulous wears this shirt, So I know you can..."
[ The image of Fabulous fades off of the Screen, and we now see an image of the
Reaper.] Reaper : " I WILL TORMENT YOU IF YOU DO NOT WEAR THIS DAMN SHIRT.." [ Reaper laughs a bit evily, and then his image fades from the shirt...Now we see an Image of Tommy Flames come up onto the screen.]
Tommy Flames : " This shirt reminds me of what it is I am working so hard for...To be apart of the SAW.."
[ Flames winks to the camera and his image fades...Voices are then heard mumbling, until finally you hear "Enough"....An image of Mike Johnson then pops up onto the screen...He points towards the camera.]
Mike Johnson : " Simply put...Support those who want to support you..Buy the offical SAW shirt.."
[ We slowly fade to your typical TV commercials.]
