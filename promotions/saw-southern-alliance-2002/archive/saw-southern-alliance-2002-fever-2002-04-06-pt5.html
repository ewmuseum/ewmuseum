SAW (Southern Alliance, 2002) — Card

Saturday Night Fever, 6 April 2002 (part 5 of 6)

6 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[ As we fade back in Bungle and Flames are already in the ring ready to fight for the Southern Regional Title..]
Samuel Smith : " The night continues....and I am glad it does. I am in a good mood now that my boy Antoine is out of jail..."
Fred Hart : " And I am glad that Vern Walters beat Eddie James, and that were about to crown our first SAW champion.."
************************************************************************** *********************** Fifth Bout Co Main event No time Limit Southern Regional Title Match Tommy Flames vs Bungle Referee - Sasaki Hirokama ************************************************************************** *********************** [Tommy Flames and Bungle start to circle each other in the middle of the ring before quickly locking up! Bungle takes Tommy over with a quick armdrag, Flames gets back up quickly and turns around right into the brown bear who takes him over again and applies a stiff arm bar. Flames rolls backwards on the mat taking away the pressure and twists Da Brown Bear's arm in a top wrist lock!
Bungle drops to one knee and flips forward once again removing the pressure, with his free hand he reaches out grabbing Flames' leg and taking him down with a single leg trip! Both men get back up quickly but Tommy catches the incomming
Bungle with a flying headscissors sending Bungle out of the ring to the floor!]
Samuel Smith : " Both good Wrestlers, Both showing Vicksburg and the SAW just that..Just what you expect in a championship match.."
Fred Hart : " I agree Samuel.." [Bungle gets back up as Tommy rushes to the ropes and leaps up, Bungle ducks out of the way as Tommy springboards up to the top rope and catches his balance leaping back into the ring and getting ready for his bizzare opponent to come back into the match. Bungle climbs up onto the apron and starts to thrust his pelvis in the direction of Flames before gyrating his hips! Tommy quickly charges over only to catch a shoulder to the midsection from Bungle doubling him over! Bungle slingshots over the top and rolls across Flames' back before turning around and thrusting his groin into Flames' backside and rolling him back into a pin! The gay icon slaps Flames' ass for good measure]
[One..] [Tw--shoulder up!] Fred Hart : " Interesting.....Very Interesting." Samuel Smith : " If you say so.." [Flames gets back up to his feet quickly as Bungle turns around towards him,and catches a stiff punch to the side of the bear head sending him stumbling back into the ropes! Tommy drives a stiff kick into Bungle's midsection and spins around with a back kick to the stomach keeping Bungle doubled over. WIth his opponent close to the ropes Flames springboards off the middle ropes and twists in midair hitting a rocker drop! Tommy quickly covers Bungles for the pin hooking his leg as all good wrestlers try to do.]
[One...] [Two...] [TH--Foot on the bottom rope] Fred Hart : " Foot on the rope there by Bungle...Good ring smarts from the weird wrestler."
Samuel Smith : " Bungle wants to make the belt pink.I hope he doesn't win.You saw the belt earlier on Shawn Cross shoulder..It's perfect how it is..."
Fred Hart : " I bet Bungle wouldn't really do that..Well he might not." [Flames pulls Bungle back up to his feet and whips him into the ropes catching him on the rebound with a leaping spin wheel kick! Bungle lays out on the mat as Flames heads up to the top rope, measuring his opponent Flames leaps off with a twisting Senton hitting nothing but the mat as Bungle rolls away! Tommy gets up to his knees as the Bear graps his arm and rolls through with a La
Mahistrol cradle in the middle of the ring!]
[One...] [Tw--shoulder up!] Samuel Smith : " Close to having a pink belt, and a bear as our first champion.." Fred Hart : " Come on Tommy...." [Bungle catches Flames on the rise with a knee to the midsection stopping him in his tracks, Bungle lifts him up into the air and falls back with a vertical suplex! Bungles gets back up to his feet and leaps forward in a front flip connecting with a furry leg drop! Bungle kneels and sits down on Flames' face and gyrates his hips as he covers him for the pin!]
[One...] [Two...] [Th-Reversal!] [Tommy rolls back on his shoulders and hooks his legs around Bungle's arms pulling him over in a sunset flip!]
[One...] [Two...] [Kickout!] Fred Hart : " WOW...." Samuel Smith : " Agreed....This is why both men made it this far in the tourney.They have the talent to make you say "wow"..."
[Bungle rolls up to his feet as Tommy gets up quickly only to be taken back down by dropkick that was on the money! Bungle rose back up to his feet and began to skip around the ring until he stopped in a corner, Flames slowly started to rise giving the bear the opening he needed to rush forward and plant both hands on the mat doing a handstand and carring his legs over him and wrapping them around Flames' head taking him over in a modifed head scissors and keeping it locked on! The ref checks on Tommy to see if he wants to give up as Bungles keeps the pressure on!]
Fred Hart : " Once again close to having a bear champion...Bungle is taking the fight to Tommy Flames here tonight.."
Samuel Smith : " I must admit I am suprised..." Fred Hart : " I can't picture Flames giving up here though..That's not his style, To much hard work and dedication."
[Flames struggles to get free kicking his legs out as Bungle waves his arms around in the air waving to the fans. Flames is able to slip free and kip ups back to his feet, Bungle scrambles to get up but gets caught with a spin kick that takes him back down to the mat with Tommy quickly following up for the pin!]
[One...] [Two...] [Thre-shoulder up!] Samuel Smith : " Like a damn seasaw this match is..." Fred Hart : " Just what we expected, Espically after seeing a match up like we just saw with Eddie James and Vern Walters."
[Bungle escapes at the last second as Tommy pulls him back up to his feet and catches him with a nice european uppercut knocking him back into the ropes!
Tommy whips Bungle into the ropes and leapfrogs over him on the rebound and comes off the ropes himself! As the 2 men head for each other Tommy leaps into the air flipping over looking for a clothesline that never hits as Bungle baseball slides out of the way! Tommy hits the mat with the wind knocked out of him, Bungle spins around and grabs Tommy's leg and lacing it, reaching forward he locks in an STF as Vern Walters starts to make his way down the aisle his arms folded across his chest as he watches the match. The ref checks to see if
Flames wants to give up in the mean time.]
Samuel Smith : " Here comes Vern...He is lucky to be standing after the match he just had."
Fred Hart : " I guess he wants to see what's going on in the tourney he thought he should of won with a close up view.."
[Flames shakes his head no as Bungle pulls back on his chin more, after a few moments he finally breaks the hold. Bungle lifts Flames up and drops him across his knee with a backbreaker laying him out in the middle of the ring. Bungle quickly climbs up to the top and never hesitates as he leaps off with a shooting star press! Flames instinctivly raises his knees as Bungle comes down hard grabbing his midsection and kicking away on the mat! Vern show's his approval and claps for the fellow texan]
Fred Hart : " Great sportsmanship there....These men did share a beer last week after the show."
Samuel Smith : " Sportsmanship is over rated for the record Fred.." [Flames gets back up to his feet slowly as Bungle staggers up and turns around into a stiff side kick from Flames! Tommy hoists Bungle halfway for a powerbomb and drops down in a sit out Japanesse powerbomb as the ref slides into position to count the pin!]
[One...] [Two...] [Th--kickout!] Fred Hart : " How did he kick out.....The punishment and the weight of that
Bear suit should of been to much."
Samuel Smith : " When a title is on the line...This happens my man.." [Tommy drags Bungle up to his feet and whips him into the corner! The Brown
Bear staggers out as Flames goes for the Flame Kick but Bungle caught his foot,
Bungle twists his ankle over before tripping his other foor out from underneath him and keeps the ankle lock sinched in! Flames screams out as he rolls into the ropes after a few seconds causing Bungle to break the hold!]
Samuel Smith : " Back to the seasaw routine we go...." Fred Hart : " Somebody has got to win soon...They CANT go on much longer out here." [Bungle release it and waists no time pulling Tommy up to his feet and waist lock from behind before gyrating his hips with the Bungle Bonker! Bungle hosits
Tommy up tossing him back over his shoulder for a Release German Suplex but
Tommy twists around and lands on his feet in the middle of the ring! From the back Eddie James comes sprinting down the aisle and cracks Vern Walters across the back with a stiff chair shout before taunting him and stomping across his back!]
Samuel Smith : " EDDIE JAMES SEEKING REVENGE FOR THE LOSS HE JUST HAD.....ACTION IN AND OUT OF THE RING.." Fred Hart : " WHAT ELSE WOULD YOU EXPECT IN THE FIRST EVER SAW TITLE MATCH..." [Bungle turns around and runs straight into the Flames Kick! Tommy rolls through with the kick and covers Bungle for the pin! James pulls Vern up to his knees and slaps him acorss the face shouting down at him]
[One...] [Two...] [Three!] *DING DING DING* [Tommy raises back up to his feet and raises his arms in the air before diving out of the ring as he sees the brawl on the outside and catches James with a right to the jaw followed up by a stiff shot sending him straggering into the guardrail! Flames slams the palm of his hand into James' chin as Walters gets back up to his feet slowly picking the chair up with him! Flames grabs James from behind as Walter glares at Eddie and hauls off with the chair bringing it down across the head of Flames as James ducks out of the way!]
Fred Hart : " OUR NEW CHAMPION JUST GOT DRILLED...." Samuel Smith : " Complete chaos...It was an accident, but it led to complete
F'n Chaos..." [Eddie smirks as he slips away to the back as Vern blinks in disbelief that he hit Flames! Walters leans down to check on Flames quickly trying to help him up, Flames shakes the cobwebs out before shoving Walters hard, Vern shakes his head and holds up his hand saying it was a mistake and motioning towards the back to James as Tommy shakes his head and rolls his eyes before turning away.
Vern grabs his arm and pleads his case as Tommy nods and waves it off excepting an extended hand of Walters as the two head back to the locker room. Before they reach the locker room though referee Sasaki Hirokama runs up from behind and hands Tommy Flames his newly won Southern Regional Title..He holds it high into the air with Vern by his side, and the two texan's head to the back followed by a huge crowd pop.]
Fred Hart : " WHAT A MATCH......Flames showing sportsmanship forgiving Vern for the chair shot.."
Samuel Smith : " He forgave him, but did he forget the chair shot...That I doubt..When we come back we have the main event, and Johnny Jackson is set to interview a NEW SAW SUPERSTAR.....Stay tuned.."
Fred Hart : " I am pretty sure they will....DID YOU SEE THAT MATCH.." [ As the camera fades from the announcer's booth, we fade to the back where we see Reaper and Page in a locker room. Page is taping up his fist.]
Chris Page : " I want revenge on them both.....LAST WEEK THEY PUT ME THREW A TABLE REAPER..." Reaper : " They did didn't they..." Chris Page : " Yes they did....Tonight we have a chance for revenge and a chance to fight for the Southern Title...Let's handle our business man.I don't want to lose this one, To these damn guys."
Reaper : " It will be like taking candy from a baby man..." [ Reaper smiles evily as Page continues to wrap his fist with tape we fade to commercials.]
