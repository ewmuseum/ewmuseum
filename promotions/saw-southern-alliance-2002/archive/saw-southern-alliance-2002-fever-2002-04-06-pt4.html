Saturday Night Fever, 6 April 2002 (part 4 of 6)

6 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[ Fade into the arena ] Fred Hart : " Well fans were once again back with you from Vicksburg, Mississippi..So far tonight has been a lil bit of everything." Samuel Smith : " Suplex machine Rud Groz went CRAZY on Chris Payne, C K Walker did his usual and made me want to gag by his comments, We had a hell of a tag war, and we have seen an EMT member get attacked."
Fred Hart : " The night is only half way done to might I mention...Were about to see Vern Walters and Eddie James get it on with each other, Later we have A title match, and a tag team war...."
Samuel Smith : " Plus we are being told after this next match, were going live to the jail cell in which Bradley is in....Mike Pace supposedly brought a camera crew with him out there in New Orleans. I can't wait to see what happens to my boy out there."
Fred Hart : " Let's not forget also Johnny Jackson will be interviewing a new
SAW wrestler....As he mentioned earlier when talking to C K Walker in the back."
Samuel Smith : " Basically what were saying...WE GOT A LOT LEFT FOR YOU PEOPLE AT HOME..." Fred Hart : " Yes we do....Yes we do..." Samuel Smith : " Let's hurry up and get Stacy in the ring so we can get this match over with. No offence to ethier of these guys, but I want to see what happens to Antoine Bradley."
Stacy Normand : " Our next bout puts Enemy against Enemy..Put's style against style...We have introducing first....From Compton, He is the "White Gangsta"
Eddie James....." [ Some hardcore rap music begins to play and from the back walks Eddie James with a crowbard in hand..The same weapon he jipped Walters with last week..James slides into the ring and raises his hands high leading to a loud amount of boo's.]
Stacy Normand : " And his opposition.....From Texas, standing at Six Foot Five inches tall and weighing Two Hundred and Sixty Six pounds....He is VERN WALTERS
!!!!!! " [ "County Boy Can Survive" by Hank Williams Jr., through the arena. "VW" Vern
Walters begins walking towards the ring in his blue jeans, vest, and cowboy hat. He struts out to the beat of the song. He stops about 5 feet from the ring and takes off his hat and vest and gives them to the time keeper. He slides in under the ropes and runs from one side of the ring bounces off the ropes and to the other. Then he stops and gets to the center of the ring and slowly raises his arms in the air for a couple of seconds then he walks off to a corner ready to begin...]
************************************************************************** *********************** Fourth Bout Grudge Match Twenty Minute Time Limit Vern Walters Vs Eddie James Referee - John Mercer ************************************************************************** *********************** [As the bell rings James and Walters step up, eye to eye. They exchange a few words before Eddie James gives Walters a stiff shove.]
Fred Hart: "This one is going to get ugly folks, I can guarantee that one." Samuel Smith: "Yeah, I mean you can feel it in the air." [Walters, looks at the crowd in disbelief before he unleashes on James with a series of right hands. Walters backs James to the ropes and sends him to the other side with an irish whip. James comes off the ropes and ducks Walter's clothesline attempt. James then comes off the ropes from the other side and connects with a clothesline of his own.]
Samuel Smith: "Nice reversal by the "White Gangster" Eddie James. Fred Hart: "What kind of name is that anyways, the "White Gansgter?" Samuel Smith: "It's a lot better than Freddy Hart if you ask me << Smiles >>
Just playing Fred."
[Walters gets back up and meets a boot to the mid-section. James now setting up
Walters for a DDT. He motions to the crowd and...]
[Walters counters the DDT and sends James over his head. Walters now mounts
James and delivers some hard right hands to the face of Eddie James.]
Fred Hart: "This one looks to be personal." Samuel Smith: "Where is the referee, those are closed fists, Vern Walters should be disqualified right now."
[Walters now brings Eddie James to his feet and sends him into the ropes. Walters catches James with a devestating neckbreaker.] Fred Hart: "Textbook neckbreaker performed there by Vern Walters." [Walters now looking at the crowd before laying three hard kicks to the back of
Eddie James. Walters backs off and James is slow to get to his feet. But as
James is trying to get to his feet Walters is waiting on the other side in a three point stance. Walters starts running and...]
[James moved out of the way and Walters went through the middle ropes onto the outside attempting a spear. James catches his breathe in the ring as Walters lies half-dead.]
Samuel Smith: "OH MY! Vern Walters just went soaring through those ropes like
Superman but fell like Christopher Reeves."
Fred Hart: "I guess you could say that, but either way, Vern Walters looks to be out of this match."
[James now coming to the outside where Walters is laying. James lifts Walters up and immediatley throws him in between his legs. He is attempting his finishing move the piledriver and...]
[Walters once again reverses James' move and send James hard to the thin mat surrounding the ring. Walters is laying on the mat as so is Eddie James.]
Samuel Smith: "That one looks like it took a lot out of Vern Walters as well. He looks to be hurt." Fred Hart: "But I think James' is feeling the worse end of the stick. He just went 6-7 feet over Walter's head and slammed into the thin matting out here."
[Walters is the first one to his feet and rolls inside the ring to break the count. Just as he breaks the count he rolls back outside to inflict more punishment on Eddie James.]
Samuel Smith: "Now why isn't the ref going for the bell, I mean Walters is making it obvious that he does not care if he wins this match, he just wants to hurt Eddie James, and that's just not right, and I'm not even a big fan of
Eddie James, But after what I seen done to Antoine...I got to watch out for the fellow rap lovers out there."
[James is slow to get to his feet but when he does he is met by a knife-edge chop, and another... and another.]
Fred Hart: "A tri-fector of knife-edge chops, that's gotta leave a red mark." Samuel Smith: "Yeah and quite a sore." [Walters now sends James towards the barricades surrounding the ring... but
Eddie reverses it and send Walters hard into the steel barricades. Walters is lying against the rails as James follows in with a big time clothesline, sending Vern Walters over the barricades and into the first row.]
Crowd: "SAW... SAW... SAW..." Fred Hart: "How's that for in your face entertainment?" [Eddie James gets back in the ring to once again break up the ten-count. Only this time, he catches his breathe as the fans help Walters back over the barricades. Walters enters the ring, tired and hurt.]
Samuel Smith: "Walters looks to be in no shape to continue this match. I mean he was just steamrolled over those barricades into the first row. The ref might have to call this match, as the one before did the Groz and Payne match..."
Fred Hart: "But look...he is still getting into that ring." [The two men lock up and it is James sending Walters into the ropes with an irish whip. Eddie James now going for a huge lariat... but Walters ducks underneath and hits the other ropes. He comes off and...]
*WHAAAAAACK* [Walters nails the referee with a huge forearm as Eddie James ducked and the referee was in the wrong place at the wrong time. The referee is out cold.]
Fred Hart: "Now what are we gonna do, with no referee how are we going to find a winner?" Samuel Smith: "Good that ref deserved it for his job in this match..." [The two men get up and go at each other with a succesion of rights and lefts. Eddie James comes out on top hitting thrr consecutive right hands in the face of Vern Walters before giving him a kick to the mid-section and...]
[James hits a huge DDT on Vern Walters in the middle of the ring, but the referee is still out cold. Eddie James gets up and notices that the ref is still out and makes his way to the outside again.]
Fred Hart: "Now where's he goin'?" Samuel Smith: "You think he's had enough and he's takin' off?" [James goes under the ring apron and pulls out his crowbar. The crowd starts booing very loudly as James enters the ring with the crowbar.]
Samuel Smith: "He's not leaving, he's just gonna finish this Vern Walters off for good." Fred Hart: "That's just plain cheating." Samuel Smith: "You gotta do what you gotta do." [Walters now getting back to his feet. James is waiting on him with the crowbar raised high.... he swings, and.... Walters moves out of the way. Vern Walters now hits a HUGE reverse neckbreaker on Eddie James who is caught off guard. The crowbar comes loose of James' hands and hits the ring mat.]
Fred Hart: "There goes the crowbar." [Walters now sees the crowbar and picks it up. He lifts Eddie James up as weel and sends him to the other side of the ropes with an Irish whip. James comes bouncing off the ropes and...]
*CRAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAACK!!!!* [Vern Walters absolutely NAILS Eddie James in the head with that crowbar laying
James out flat. Vern Walters throws the crowbar out of the ring and covers
Eddie James.]
Fred Hart: "Here comes the referee, he makes a slow count." ONE.... . . . . . . . . .TWO . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .THREEEEEEEE!!!! Samuel Smith: "Now that is just plain wrong he used a foreign object to get that win. I am protesting this win."
Fred Hart: "It was the same crowbar that James brought into the ring if I do remember correctly."
Samuel Smith: "Whatever." Stacy Normand : "The winner of the match.... .... VERN WAAAAALTERS!!!" [Vern stands as the referee raises his hand. Walters makes his way out of the ring as the camera cuts to the shot of Eddie James laying in the center of the ring now bleeding from the shot to the head.]
[Fade to New Orleans, Louisiana.] [ A uniformed cop comes into the scene as he hands Cedric and Antoine Bradley a small package who are both in orange uniforms behind bars.]
Cedric : " Thanks officer.." Antoine Bradley : " Open it up Ced.." Cedric : " Alright...." [ Cedric opens up the package and it is a bar of soap..] Cedric : " How nice...I guess " Antoine Bradley : " Do you want to read the card or do you want me to tell you what it says..."
Cedric : " I'll read it..." Don't drop the soap guys, Sincerly Tommy Taylor"..That's nice of him...He must know how dirty it is here in the Jail cell.." Antoine Bradley : " C'mon Ced...It was a gag gift, Taylor is trying to play games with us man."
[ Suddenly into view comes Mike Pace with a couple security guards.They unlock the door to the cell, and Pace stands there blushing.]
Mike Pace : " Your free guys....We have taped phone conversations from Seth
Baptist and Taylor saying they did it.I decided to trace them after the comment's they made to you guys, and the sending of that package.Taylor was behind it the whole time. If you want to press charges on them, or want me to I will..."
Antoine Bradley : " Nah.....Forget that bull shit. Just give me the white man in the ring at the supercard...Make sure you hook us up real good..."
Mike Pace : " I can do that, but you sure you don't want him to serve any jail time." Antoine Bradley : " Nah...Not nessecary, I will make sure to give him a HARD
TIME of my own."
[ Antoine walks off...] Cedric : " Thanks for not giving up on us Mike...I am glad you kept trying to get us out." Mike Pace : " I am just glad you aren't the guys who attacked me.." [ Cedric and Mike shake hands, as we fade to a commercial promoting federations such as RSP, NEO, IWF/WOW, and IGA..]
