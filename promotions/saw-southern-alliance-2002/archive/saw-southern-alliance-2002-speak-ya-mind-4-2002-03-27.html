SAW presents "Speak Ya Mind" on March 27th, 2002 You will hear from the Following SAW superstars ************************* 1 - "The Devil's Child" Simon Starr 2 - C K Walker 3 - Tommy Flames 4 - Vern Walters ************************* << Darkness.>> [VOICE] : " You can Hate me now " << Deep breathing >> [VOICE] : " I repeat, You can hate me now. " << The darkness slowly comes to light as we now see a room lit by candles. Several candles burn lighting the large room. We see a shadow, Which appears to be a figure, but as the shadow is in the back portion of the room we can still not make out who it is, or what it is.>>
[VOICE] : " Soon you will be able to hate me more though." << From the shadow's steps a huge 6"11, 345 pound man. That man of course being the largest man in the SAW, "The Devil's Child" Simon Starr. A sadistic look is on the man's face and in the man's eyes, as he looks into the camera.>>
[SIMON STARR] : " What you saw on Fever, was the work of the Devil's Child..It was just foreshadowing though, Vern Walters was not the soul we were set out to capture. My father, Did not send me here to bring down Vern Walters. We have much bigger plans than Mr Walters."
<< Simon moves his neck gently back and forth cracking it with every movement.Finally, He stops this motion and reaches out towards the camera.
Coming just shy of it.>> [SIMON STARR] : " SAW, I am trying to reach out to you with every word I say." << He pulls his hand away from the camera.>> [SIMON STARR] : " But I can't quite seem to reach you all." << Chuckle >> [SIMON STARR] : " So now your tormentation shall not be on my hands, For I have tried to warn you. It shall be on your own hands, Your own soul, and espically your own body. You saw some of the power that I used on Mr Walters, but next week I might use more of the power. The following week, I might use more. You just never know, and that means you should be scared. Very scared. I told you all that I am evil, and you didn't seem to believe me. I told you all I am the son of The Lord of Flies, Yet you laugh at me. Now, I am the one laughing, but not only laughing. I am the one spitting on you, Kicking you, Hurting you, and even hitting you with Steel items in the case of last week."
" This week, More of the same. I don't care what you small men say to me, I don't care what you pathetic mortals do to me. For I know, You can Not hurt me.
To be hurt, You must have something to hurt, and SAW I have nothing. I have no heart, No soul, No weakness.."
<< Hangs his dead down towards the ground.>> [SIMON STARR] : " I have ABSOLUTELY fucking nothing." << Lifts his head back up towards the camera, and you can see his eyes are rolled up in the top of his head.>>
[SIMON STARR] : " But soon I will have one your pathetic souls.." << Points to the camera >> [SIMON STARR] : " It just might be yours. " << Lowers his eyes back down to normal vision and lowers his hands.>> [SIMON STARR] : " Five men will be in the ring with me or around me this weekend. Five men much watch there souls. Two of the five are on my so called team, But I have no team, except for the one's in the firey flames known as
HELL. Other than that, I hope to get rid of you all."
" Chris Payne, Rud Groz, Taylor Threat, Johnny Danger and Todd Van Rooyen are all just names and faces to me. Nothing more, Nothing less, but soon if my
Father ask of it. They could be souls I seek to possess. I could become one of there stalkers, Hell I could become all of there stalkers."
<< Smiles >> [SIMON STARR] : " We will just have to see." << He exits into the back portion of Darkness in the room once again.You can hear him breathing deeply as we fade out.>>
************************************************************************** ********* <<The scene opens to the BEAUTIFUL Samantha Owens. The blonde bombshell is decked out in a navy blue skirt with a white blouse and black knee high leather boots. She seems to be sitting in a living room like area that is decked out with leather couch's [Which she is sitting on btw.] a few leather chairs and a
HUGE entertainment unit in the corner. The room is also decked out with other
'miscellaneous stuff and big sliding doors leading outside to a balcony that let's the sun shine through. The young diva smiles into the camera.>>
[Owens]: Hello SAW faithful! I'm Samantha Owens here with you once again. Today
I'm gonna get a few words from the man who raised a hand at me after the Sunday show and acted like a total jerk. BUT [smiles] he apologized for his actions and has PROMISED to answer ANY question I ask him. So without further waiting.
Today my guest is C.K WALKER! <<The camera turns to see a arrogant C.K Walker sitting in a leather chair with one leg resting over the other in a cocky manner. The young Walker is decked out in black leather pants, black leather boots and black half sleeved shirt with white flames coming up from the bottom and on the sleeves. A gold watch covers his right wrist while a thick gold wrist chain covers the other. His dyed blonde, almost white hair looks wet and his in a messy spike like style.
He rubs his Elvis like sideburns then starts to tap his upper lip with his index finger while looking at the beautiful blonde.>>
[Owens]: Thank you for joining me here to day Mr. Walker. [Walker]: No, no Miss Owens. THANK YOU for allowing me to be in your presence today. And please [smiles cockily] call me C.K.
<C.K smiles at the blonde then folds his arms on his chest looking to be a smooth cat. Samantha smiles and continues.>
[Owens]: Okay... umm... C.K. My first question is why were you so mad about having to face Reaper in a NO DQ, Falls Count Anywhere match-up? Mad even so enough. To threaten to hit me?
[Walker]: Whoa, whoa little lady. Now C.K Walker would _NEVER_ hit a beautiful creature like you. I was simply going to swat the fly from your hair when I raised my hand. Nothing more. When Chris Page showed up on the scene trying to look like a hero.. [smirks] We'll talk more about him later I'm sure.
[Smiles] But to answer your question. You can bet your last pair of bobby socks that C.K
Walker was hot under the collar when he heard the news. I'm a _WRESTLER_ Miss
Owens. I'm a Mega star. In fact, I'm a Super Duper Mega Star. I came to this federation to _wrestle_. Not to sit in the ring with a overrated lug to trade punches with and throw him through tables. I came here to become a _wrestling_ champion. Not to lower myself to one of Reaper's low levels.
[Owens]: What do you mean by Reaper's level? [Walker]: Pllleeeease Miss Owens. Reaper is a dud! That is all that walking freak is good at. Punching, kicking and smelling funny! This man doesn't know one thing about wrestling. He's a damn freak! Just because I came into the ring during that little three way and stopped what would of been the biggest embarrassment to this company [sneers] I'm getting punished for it. This company should be patting me on the back, not throwing me in _RIDICULOUS_ matches like this.
[Owens]: Wait... I'm confused. What do you mean you stopped what would of been the biggest embarrassment to the SAW. Do you mean by Reaper one second away from becoming champion?
[Walker]: BINGO! Not one second away Owens. It would of been the blackest mark on SAW wrestling if that walking dead fool won the belt. This company would of been looked at as a damn joke. [deep voice] "Hey look. There's SAW on
Television. Let's skip the channel though cause they got some wannabe dead freak with no skill as champion.' [normal tone] See what I mean. I single handedly saved this federation. This is a _WRESTLING_ federation. Not a freak show for untalented stool pigeons like Reaper. If Reaper wants to become a star... go see if Buffy The Vampire Slayer is looking for cast members. Leave
SAW to the TRUE talented guys... like C.K Walker!
[Owens]: You smashed Reaper with a chair not once, but twice. Now you have to go into a match with no rules. You have to be a little nervous. I mean no matter what you say. Reaper is one of the toughest men in wrestling today.
[Walker]: Is C.K Walker nervous [scoffs] Ha! Yeah right. I fear no man. I already showed the World what I can do to Reaper. I already proved to everyone that the dead man doesn't scare C.K Walker in the least. I laid that freak out.
I busted the [mocking voice] 'invincible' [normal tone] Reaper out! This time won't be any different. The SAW thinks their punishing C.K Walker [smirks]
Hell, their not punishing me. Their punishing Reaper cause this time I will finish the job I started at the last show.
[Owens]: Why don't you think Reaper would be a good champion. He was the favorite to become champion and even I think he would be a great if not unbeatable champion. Why do you have so much venom for him?
[Walker]: The reason he wouldn't make a good champion is because he's not a good wrestler [shrugs] Plain and simple. He wears that big black hooded cape, doesn't wash his hair and talks like a freak and all of sudden he's the toughest guy on the planet. HA! Well it takes more then bad clothing and lack of washing to scare C.K Walker. Let this guy hang around with ghosts and goblins. Let him brag he met Casper The Friendly Ghost and point at tombstones saying that's where he will bury me. Let him talk till the sun goes down because he will never, NEVER scare C.K Walker cause I'm the man HE FEARS!
[Owens]: Why would Reaper be afraid of you? [Walker]: Cause I got the skills baby! He felt what I could do to him. I took that aura of invincibility [smacks hand together] and crushed it. I showed everyone just how vulnerable that walking freak show is. I'm a mega star. The true star of SAW. Reaper knows this now and I bet he scared to death of what
I'm going to do to him in this match-up. [smiles] Which he damn well should be.
I _WILL_ be the man that runs Reaper out of the SAW for good. [Owens]: You threw a huge wrench in the proceedings of crowning a new champion. As it stands right now the championship is still vacant. [Walker]: That's because there is only _ONE_ true champion of SAW and that's
C.K Walker. The bonafide, Super duper Mega star! I couldn't bare to think of any of those clowns walking around with _MY_ title. I will be the first and
ONLY SAW Heavyweight champion. Once I win that title nobody will come close to defeating C.K Walker... cause nobody in this joint has the talent.
[Owens]: There are alot of great wrestlers. Chris Page comes to my mind. [Walker]: Chris Page. [smirks] Another reject. This guy is all hype, no substance. Once again Johnson thinks he has punished Walker by putting him in the ring with him one night after Reaper, but he is wrong. C.K Walker is the present and FUTURE of SAW. I'll put Page in his place just as I will Reaper.
[Owens]: How can you be so sure? [Walker]: Cause I'm the walking dream baby! Hitting Reaper with that chair was a dream come true. But hitting that moron Chris Page was almost as satisfying.
I took out two idiots with one stone. I put my name on the map. They want to try and be superstars? No problem. They want to be famous, then step on up cause I'll make them famous for being the first two pretenders C.K Walker sent packing!
[Owens]: Well thank you for your time and I guess good luck in your matches against Reaper and Page. I think you'll need it.
[Walker]: I don't need luck hon buns. C.K Walker is the real deal. First I'll bury the Reeker. Then I'll hand Chris Page is overrated ass. Nuff said. [taps chin] Since I answered all your questions. Now do you mind if I ask you one?
[Owens]: Well.... not at all. [Walker]: Do you have any doubles of your nude photo shoots? I would love to see what is under that clothing!
[Owens]: What! Excuse me Mr. Walker but I think this... [Walker]: Oh come on, don't deny it. EVERYONE in the land knows you dropped the linen in front of the camera. Maybe when I'm done destroying Reaper and Page.
You can come on over to my house and C.K Walker will make you star as well! <<C.K Walker begins to laugh as the lovely, but embarrassed and angry Samantha grabs her suit jacket and walks off.>>
<STATIC> ************************************************************************** ********* << Fade in >> << We zoom into a view of Tommy Flames and His girlfriend washing Tommy's Green
1999 Ford Ranger. Tommy isn't wearing a shirt, and has on some blue jeans. His girlfriend is wearing a silver Bikini. Both are smiling while washing the vehicle.>>
[Tommy Flames] : " I miss Dallas, But I can picture West Monroe becoming like a second home to me."
[Girlfriend] : " I don't think I would mind that." << Tommy grabs the water hose and rinses some of the soap off of the truck. He then sprays his girlfriend with it, Who screams and begin's to run around the truck laughing. Tommy chases her, Still spraying her, and finally tosses down the hose. He grabs her, wrapping her up completly in his arms, and begins to kiss her gently.>>
[Girlfriend] : " Oh Tommy." << His girlfriend points towards the camera, and Tommy looks up. His smile goes away briefly, but then he regains it. He let's his girlfriend go, and the two hold hands infront of the camera.>>
[Tommy Flames] : " Well hello there, I didn't see ya there, or didn't know ya was coming." [Girlfriend] : " I'll let you boys talk. I'll be inside baby, Come get me when you want to finish what we started."
[Tommy Flames] : " The trucks pretty much done baby, Just needs to dry." [Girlfriend] : " I didn't mean the truck Tommy." << Smiling Tommy's Girlfriend reaches up and gives him a kiss. She waves to the camera, then goes inside. Tommy scratches his head, and speaks.>>
[Tommy Flames] : " What a tease eh guys ? " " Still not used to the camera's following you around all the time, but It will come to me slowly. Last week the match came to me slowly, I had to find my groove. Eddie James is a tough man in that ring, and For a while there everybody thought I was out classed being in there with him, Even myself, But I came back strong. I started off slow, but I did what everyone wants to do in life, and that's finish good."
<< Tommy shakes his head >> [Tommy Flames] : " I am not sure if the situation with James is over or not, He jumped me after the match briefly, but I can't think about that right now. What
I must think about now is Vern Walters. My second round opposition in the
Regional Title tourney."
<< Tommy flexes a bit out of instinct at the mention of having a chance to gain a title.>> [Tommy Flames] : " He is a big boy from Texas, And will be a hand full. The good thing is though, Last week physically and mentally he got _BEAT DOWN_.
Hopefully, I can use that to my advantage. I think I can, but then again who knows right ? "
<< Tommy backs up and leans against his truck.>> [Tommy Flames] : " All I can honestly tell you all is I am gonna try my hardest, Give it my best and come to preform. I want to please the fans, Later tonight were taking my truck here, and driving down to New Orleans. Haven't been to the big easy in along time, and this should be interesting. With my girlfriend by my side, I think it will be real interesting. New Orleans is gonna be fun in and out of the ring I do believe. The French Quarters, Hand
Grenades, and Bourbon Street out of the ring. Whle in the ring a tough challenge, Screaming fans and my girlfriend watching from ringside rooting me on from start to finish. Can't get much better than that."
" I am gonna try my hardest to make it better though. I am gonna try to improve from last week, and instead of making the crowd go crazy a couple times. This week, I hope to make them do it several times.Which is something I feel I can do. I wrestled okay against Eddie James,and came out with a tough, Hard fought win, But I hope to wrestle damn near perfect against you Vern. Which is something I have done in the ring before, and if you can do it once. You can prolly do it twice, Right ? "
<< Tommy chuckles >> [Tommy Flames] : " Win, Lose or Draw,Tommy Flames is bringing the Heat Vern. I got a good feeling that this weekends Saturday Night Fever is definitely gonna be _ H O T_ "
<< Tommy Waves "goodbye" to the camera, and goes back inside to his loving girlfriend.>> ************************************************************************** ********* <<We fade into the inside of a house or a trailer we cannot really tell. Inside their is shag carpeting and various pictures on the wall such as an autographed photo of Hank Williams, Jr. We see Vern Walters sitting on an old sofa. The sofa has some duct tape on it to keep the shit inside from falling out. Vern is sitting there with no shirt on, just tape on his rips and a pair of blue jeans. In his right hand he holds an unopened bottle of Shiner Bock.
Shiner Bock is Texas Best beer, brewed in Shiner, Texas. He has pain in his eyes and he has his left arm he has across his ribs.>>
[Vern Walters] : "Last Saturday... Well, what can I say. That damn coward had to use a steel chair on me. That big man with his tail between his legs realized he couldn't put me down so he got a damn chair. Looking back I had a chance myself of using a chair on big Simon. Why? I could whip that dumbass any day of the week. He just had to pull some steel out his little bag of shit. Fine. <<Short Pause.>> But, you Simon are a goddamn stupidest person.
You know this is the tournament for the SAW Regional Title. But dq-in' yourself you basic stuck a thumb up your ass. I guess you get a big thumbs up for being a jackass."
<<Vern twists the top off his Shiner Bock with a look of pain on his face. As soon as he is done his left arm goes right back on his ribs. He is in some pain.>>
[Vern Walters] : "I got to admit to you Simon... you swing a hell of a chair. You got me a banged up a bit. But if you look at it close you can see that you purt near got somebody's hopes up. I go on for the Regional belt and the man I face next has got to be happy about these odds. A Mr. Tommy Flames has got to be the happiest S. O. B. around. Think about it, if someone would have already took out the man I was facin' I would be 'bout as happy as a 'coon in a trash bin. Yea, Tommy, you might believe you got your self some kind of easy match.
But you know right, that this sport is a lot physical, but its mostly right here... <<Vern uses the hand with the beer bottle to point at his heart.>>
...in your heart."
<<VW takes a swig of the beer.>> [Vern Walters] : "Tommy I might have a few broken rib. I might have a few bruised rib. But it won't matter. A man can fight through pain. A man can fight through the bad times. Any man can swing a chair. But can a man come back from some battered ribs? I guess we're bout to find out. Its kinda funny how fate deals us our hands kinda like poker. Some guy might get a pair but the guy next to him might get a full house right off the bat. I guess it just pretty much depends on what you draw ain't it? Well hell, I drew a hell of a hand. I tell ya. I drew some chair shots to the ribs. But I also drew a chance to be the Regional champion. The good in with the bad. Tommy maybe you don't get it. Maybe you just can't get it through that thick skull of yours.
Tommy can you stand between a man and his dreams? <<Short pause.>> Can you stop a man from getting what he wants out of the this world? <<Short pause.>>
Tommy you cannot stop a me."
<<VW throws the beer back and takes the rest of it down. He takes the bottle and chunks it. The camera follows the bottle as it goes out a window. The camera quickly goes back to Vern.>>
[Vern Walters] : "Tommy, a wiser man than myself once said, 'Sometimes you're the man for your time and place.' Tommy I am the man for this time and this place, the SAW. You can't stop me. Hell son, you got a fight on your hands.
I'm gonna come down on you like Texans came down on the Mexicans at San Jacento!" <<Fades to black...>> ************************************************************************** ********* There yall go, That's all the flashes for Tuesdays Packet =)....Yall keep up the good work, and get ya flashes in before 11 central time Thursday for the last packet before the show.
