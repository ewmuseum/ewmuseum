SAW (Southern Alliance, 2002) — Roleplay

Speak Ya Mind #6

2 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
SAW presents "Speak Ya Mind" on April 2nd, 2002 You will hear from the Following SAW superstars ************************* 1 - Tommy Flames 2 - Antoine Bradley 3 - The Bounty Hunters 4 - C K Walker 5 - The Business Men ******************** [ Pan In ] [ We see Tommy Flames and his beautiful girlfriend standing up talking to each other in the kitchen of there small rental home in Monroe, Louisiana. Both seem to be in a good mood by the tone of there voices and the looks on there faces.]
Tommy Flames : " Another win baby, Not really how I wanted it, but definitely another win."
Girlfriend : " Eddie James is trying to mess everything up Isn't he.." Tommy Flames : " That's alright, If he wants some more of the flames, He knows where to find me."
[ The two give each other a hug as they smile after that comment, and Tommy's
Girlfriend exits the kitchen.]
[ Re Focus ] [ The camera now pans in close to Tommy Flames as we see he has a bruise on his shoulder, and a small black eye under his right eye from the match with Vern
Walters. He sits down at the kitchen table and folds his hands.]
Tommy Flames : " Vern is a good ol boy, I hate to get a cheap win over him like that. I will be more than happy to give him a title shot though, If I beat
Bungle this week in the finals of the Southern Regional Tourney."
[Unfolds his hands, and put's them down on the kitchen table. Just like your mom told you not to do as a kid.]
Tommy Flames : " Bungle on the other hand is a unique challenge..Never seen or met anybody that is quite like this guy. Loves the fans, Loves kid, But I don't think he loves himself. He treats himself like he is the bottom of the totem pole. I don't think he realizes he actually is a talented wrestler. When he beat Tommy Taylor two weeks ago, I SAW that he has talent in that ring. The counters and moves he did.._AMAZING_."
" Just not sure why he is how he is.." [ Tommy shakes his head for a moment, then re folds his hands again in the typical thinking Position.]
Tommy Flames : " To each there own though I suppose, and I will respect that from Bungle. I will go in the same as I always go in. Open minded, Prepared and
Focused. His unique talents will not throw me off my gameplan. << Raises voice
>> THEY CAN NOT << Back to normal >> If I let him throw me off, then he will win, and that's something I can't let happen. Everybody know's this is my big break, and I can't let this turn out to be my big blown break.I would eat myself up on the inside till I got a chance to redeem myself, if that happened.
I am not sure how I would react. I must handle him as I handled Eddie James and
Vern Walters. Slowly, Cautiously and carefully."
[ Tommy gets up from the table slowly and looks around the kitchen.] Tommy Flames : " What am I doing in here ? I can't be eating my baby's mexican food, I need to be training. I must be in training. Mike Johnson might not give me this chance again, and I really think I can beat Bungle in a One on One match. I really do."
[ Tommy looks over towards the stove which has some food on it, with steam just barely coming from the top of it.]
Tommy Flames : " Self control.." [ Tommy frowns, Then walks out of the kitchen slowly with his head down.] [ Pan Out ] ************************************************************************** ************************ [The camera opens inside a jail cell. "The Real Deal" Antoine Bradley and Cedric sit inside the gloomy-looking cell, which has drab gray bricks on the walls and a small, barred window that lets in a few rays of sunlight that show Cedric's face. Antoine is seated on the bed on the opposite side of the cell, but he is less visible in the very dim light. Cedric is picking at what appears to be a slice of bread.]
C: Man, we've been stuck inside this cell for two days and already I'm gettin' sick of it. I hate stale bread, cold showers, and sitting around all day.
A: It's all got to do with that [expletive], Mike Pace. I told you last week. Mike Pace gets his white ass pasted with a lead pipe and who does he blame? He blames THE BLACK MAN. Never mind that everybody saw me get my lead pipe taken away from me by the security guards.
C: Damn, bro, you said all this last week. We already know what you think about all this. It didn't convince
Mike Pace last week and it sure isn't going to convince him now. [Antoine turns his head to look at Cedric.] C: Pace is going to think you're guilty until he finds someone else to blame his mishap on. He's gonna have to find some hard evidence that someone else did it before he lets you off.
A: Yeah, well, we can't do anything about it now. There's not much we can do from a jail cell. And as long as Pace thinks I assaulted him, we're going to be stuck in here.
C: Surely we'll get out of here soon... how long can they hold us in county lockup like this?
A: Look, I placed my one phone call to the SAW front office. They told me they couldn't get me out as long as there was no hard evidence against anyone else. So
I guess Pace has gotten all his cronies in the front office convinced that I'm guilty as well.
C: Damn, that sucks! I guess we're gonna be stuck here for a while. [Cut to darkness.] ************************************************************************** ************************ <<Fade in to a shot of the words "HANG 'EM HIGH" written in red letters sandwiching an emblem of Texas.>>
[VOICE]: "Two things are about to happen to the SAW." <<Pause.>> [VOICE]: "Two very _big_ things." <<Pause.>> [VOICE]: "Two very _violent_ things." <<The camera pulls back to reveal that the words are written on the back of a black leather vest worn by one of two men standing with their backs to the camera. They're dressed in identical black singlets and long trunks. They wear identical black cowboy hats. And they both are wearing black masks with red trimming.>>
[MAN 1]: "An' those two things are men you see before ya. We are The Bountyhunters." <<Both men now turn around to face the camera.>> [MAN 1]: "For those of ya who don't know us, m'name's Lobo an' this six feet-three inches of rawhide leather is my partner, Hawke. Grab a pen.
Write'em down. Get used ta hearin' them names, folks... Cuz we're the new measurin' stick for tagteams in the SAW. We ain't here ta prove nuthin' ta anybody. We've already done that but cuttin' a path over every team that dared step 'tween then ropes with Hawke an' m'self. We've been coast ta coast an' border ta border an' we ain't met the team yet who can stand toe-ta-toe with the toughest bastards topside of Hell.
An' now we're here in the SAW ta do what we do best. Draw money an' crimson. An' it don't matter which we get first. Tell'em, Hawke." <<Lobo slaps his partner hard on the chest as if tagging him into this interview. Hawke speaks in a very loud, almost yelling manner.>>
[HAWKE]: It's pretty damn simple! When we come ta town two things happen. One is we make money an' two... people get hurt. An' if anybody...an' I mean
_any_BODY_.. thinks we're out here yappin' jus ta hear our own voices... they better get outta Dodge right now. We're dead serious when we tell ya ... we didn't come here for the weather.
We came to show the SAW jus what _real_ tagteam champions look like. Well take a good goddam look at yer tv screen, people. What yer lookin' at is
_the_ most feared tagteam in this business. Don't believe us?
Drag yer ass into our shadow an' we'll _make_ a believer outta ya. It's like
Lobo said... we're here ta draw money an' blood... an' we don't give a damn which one we get first."
<<Lobo steps back into the forefront now.>> [LOBO]: "Make no mistake about it, we ain't nice guys. We ain't gonna make no TV commercials... sign no autographs or be on the cover of GQ. We don't shake hands... we don't take pictures with the idiot fans an' we don't kiss babies.
We beat people up an' put 'em down for the count. That's our job an' we do it better than anybody else walkin' this earth. We ain't in this sport for fame an' we damn sure ain't in it for some popularity contest.
We're in it for the money. It's jus that simple. We got mortgages to pay. We got families to feed. We got bills ta pay an' they don't get paid by wearin' a white hat an' wavin' ta the fans. Winnin'
matches...beatin' our opponents so bad they pray fer unconsciousness jus to stop the pain... an' then gettin' that one-two- three... _that's_ what pays the bills, boys. It's all about the green an' gold, brother. Ya see whoever wears them shiny gold straps around their waists takes home the biggest checks.
An' yer lookin' at the two men who will stop at nuthin' ta get those shiny gold belts with the big paychecks, people. Get in our way an' it won't jus be yer
_toughest_ fight.
It may be yer _last_." <<Lobo gives the hook'em horns handgesture to the camera as it fades to black...>> ************************************************************************** ************************ Voice: I told ya I would do it, and I did! [The scene opens up to a beaming C.K Walker standing in the middle of a vacant ring. A huge white bandage covers the right side of his forehead and the youngster is decked out in a normal pair of blue jeans and a black Nike sweater. Gripped in his right hand is a pair of brass knuckles, possibly the same knucks that sent Reaper to sleep. Walker continues to smile as he continues.]
Walker: [mockingly] Ewww the big bad Reeker. C.K Walker doesn't stand a chance. [scoffs] Well how is everyone feeling today? [smirks] I told the WORLD I would defeat the Reeker and I did just that. You see [taps temple] Reaper thought the
Super Duper Mega Star would come right at him and play _HIS_ game. Everyone thought that Reaper would grab poor little me by the neck and do whatever he wanted to do to C.K Walker. Well I proved two things to not only Reeker.
[shakes head] I proved something to _EVERYONE_ everywhere. The first thing I proved is quite frankly [beaming smile once again.]
Reaper is without a _DOUBT_ the biggest moron walking on two legs! [Huge smile again.] I mean what did you think Reaper. Did you really think C.K Walker would walk down to that ring and go toe to toe with a guy twice his size who has made his living off punching and kicking? [smiles] Did you really think that C.K Walker would fall for such an idiotic plan? [nods head] Yes, I believe you did Reeker.
But I showed the World just how non-intelligent you really are. I set you up big man. I sent the bait out and you chewed it up like a hungry fish. You played _MY_ game Reaper. You thought you were so smart. you thought this match was going to be just another cake walk for the walking dead freak but I only got one thing to say to you Reaper. [smiles broadly again!] I told ya so! I
_TOLD_ you I would destroy you. I _TOLD_ you that even though I would be out of my element [smiles again] I would come out on top and BURY you. That's exactly what I did little trooper! I took you to the school of C.K Walker, [smiles] and you failed miserably. I knew you didn't have the IQ of a peanut. I told the masses of people that. [shrugs] But of course being the inbred, no teeth, old rusty pick up driving inbreeds they are, of course they didn't. If you want to get right down to the facts Reaper, I played in your backyard big man...
[smiles again. That ear to ear annoying grin.] And I _TOTALLY_ kicked your fruity ass! [C.K Walker leans against the turnbuckle. He twirls the turnbuckle tie-up string with his fingers as he continues.]
Walker: The second thing I proved is that without a doubt, C.K Walker SAVED this company from the biggest _JOKE_ in wrestling history. Everyone is mad that
C.K Walker ruined the title match. But, [smirks] Like I said before I saved this company from the biggest travesty in not only this federation, but the whole Wrestling world as a whole.
I mean let's face it. Reaper is _OLD_ news. Sure once upon a bad dream the
Reaper _MAY_ have been dangerous but that was some thirty, sixty, hell maybe even before Fright Night was made [smiles] and everyone knows that movie was made a _LONG_ time ago [smirks] catch my drift? If you don't [shrugs] it's simple. The Reaper you see today is a washed up _HAS-BEEN_ who can't acknowledge the _FACT_ his best days are behind him. I mean back in his prime some hundred years ago, would anyone beat him in a 'Anything Goes Match'?
[shakes head] I know what your saying, HELL NO! Would anyone have beat him so
_EASILY_ who's name _ISN'T_ C.K Walker? BINGO! HELL NO! But it did happen just a few short days ago. Reaper was a pathetic shadow of his former self. The man is walking on borrowed time and it's only a matter of time before [puts hands in Prayer and looks up to the rafters] God willing, that the dead freak will bury himself and _NEVER_ be heard from again.
[Walker smiles] The point is SAW would have not only had the biggest joke in the World holding the SAW's most important title. But it would also have had a washed up has-been. Think about it people [taps temple once again] Think about C.K
Walker's FACTS then the next time I see you. [huge annoying grin] you can kiss my boots and tell me how sorry you are for doubting that Walker _SAVED_ this company from being the biggest joke in the World!
[Walker begins to pace around the ring. Not a intense pace. But more like a cocky strut that leaves little doubt he is one of the most arrogant self centered people on the planet.]
Walker: It seems as thought Mr. Johnson hasn't gotten it through his head that
Reaper is NOT on my level. I destroyed him to save the company and giving that freak the belt. [smiles] And I destroyed that punk in a match that was in his turf. When are you going to get it through your head Mr. Johnson. Reaper is old news pal! He will _NEVER_ defeat C.K Walker. He will never be has talented or has popular as your one and only Super Duper Mega Star! Now your throwing me and the great Rob Fabulous into the ring with the walking has-been freak Reaper and the all hype not substance Chris Page?
What in the hell are you thinking? I mean by all rights it should be the two most talented wrestlers in SAW fighting for the titles. One being [points at himself] your looking at him. And the other of course, Rob Fabulous! But no. Instead you give those two losers another chance to make your company into a joke. Another chance at vying for the big belt of SAW. The stipulation is whatever man wins, get to move onto face Reaper I suppose [makes a nasty face then rolls his eyes] for the chance... no check that.. the easy match to strap that big ole piece of gold around his waist. [smiles] Well that's right up my alley Southern Hick! C.K
Walker was destined to become SAW Heavyweight champion and if I have to rip
Reaper a new one along with Chris Page [smiles and shrugs] Then so be it. The
FACT is I have ALREADY defeated BOTH of them! I left them lying in the middle of the damn ring and so had Rob Fabulous. So what do you think is gonna change
Mr. Johnson? If we have already dominated these morons in singles [smirks then scoffs] Then what in the hell makes you think those two have ANY chance in hell of beating the BOTH of us at the same damn time?
Are _YOU_ simple? [winks] But if you wish for me and Fabulous to RID the federation of those two once and for all [shrugs] then so be it. I've got the time and the skills [winks again]
As you all know. It will be my pleasure amigo to destroy those two geeks in the center of the ring then move on and show the World just who truly deserves that piece of gold around his waist.
[Smiles and thrusts a thumb to his chest] ME! [Does a thumbs up] Then that's exactly what I will do amigo! Cause whether anyone likes it or not
C.K Walker, the TRUE Super Duper Mega Star of SAW WILL be the Heavyweight champion when it's all said and done. Bring those two pieces of CRAP down to that ring at FEVER and me and Rob Fabulous [smiles wide] we'll bring the pooper scoopers! See you LOSERS at Fever boys. Once again C.K Walker will prove he is tha man, while Reaper... [laughs] Well he will prove once again that he is nothing but gum on the bottom of my three hundred dollar shoes.
[Smiles] Oh and Page [winks] When Fabulous is done whipping your carcass around like a rag doll, maybe I will show the world just how big of a sissy you are when I break that scrawny neck with the 'Sissy Smasher'. Fever is gonna be hot baby!
WOO-HOOOOO!! [Static-] ************************************************************************** ************************ << Fade into a view of Tommy Taylor and Seth Baptist sitting behind a small round wooden table. Both men are in rolling chairs laid back, with there feet kicked up onto the table very relaxed. The table has a phone, A speakerphone, Computer, and some pieces of paper with some sort of information on it. Both men are dressed well, as there suits just sing the words "MONEY". The two act as if the camera is not even there, and begin to "shoot" off there mouths bout the SAW.>>
[Tommy Taylor] : " Bungle one Night, Matt James the next, Whose next Seth ? " [Seth Baptist] : " I don't know, But whatever BUSINESS you want _HANDLED_ Just let me know." << Both men chuckle, and Tommy sits up removing his feet from the table. He grabs the pieces of paper, and hands it to his big friend Seth Baptist.>>
[Tommy Taylor] : " You will notice three things on the pieces of paper infront of you there Seth. First being We fight the Bayou Bandits this week on War
Stories, Don't really worry about that one. I got that covered, If you know what
I am saying << Rubs his fingers together implicating the use of money >> Secondly you will notice the name Rud Groz. He is anti american. I want you to take
_HIM_ The F[censor] Out...Can you do that for me ? "
[Seth Baptist] : " << Smiling >> Sure can, But why Boss ? " [Tommy Taylor] : " I am alot of things, but among that list of alot of things I am American. I love this country, It gives me the chance to grow and become rich. People like Rud Groz, Want to take that oppurtunity away, So being a good
Business man, I need to protect my business. Thus I need to take him out before he grows into any sort of a problem. Shouldn't be to hard, He seems kinda dumb, and I personally don't think he's ever been laid. With the cunning use of Ladies and brains we should be able to whipe him out completly."
[Seth Baptist] : " If that's how you want to do it boss.I don't mind pounding him into submission ethier if you like."
[Tommy Taylor] : << Chuckles >> " If it comes to that, I don't mind ethier, But let's try to use our brains before we use our brawn shall we ? "
<< Seth nods in agreement, and sits up in his chair as well, Taking his feet off of the table. He looks real hard at the pieces of paper, then back up at
Tommy.>>
[Seth Baptist] : " Why is Antoine Bradley on the list ? He's in jail huh..." [Tommy Taylor] : " Yes he is...Just make sure our friends send him a little gift of some sort."
[Seth Baptist] : << Bewildered look on face >> " Sure Boss.." [Tommy Taylor] : " Handle that now if you will.It's kinda important to me
Seth." << Seth still surpised Nods, and reaches over to the phone. While Seth works the phone, Tommy finally looks over acknowledging the camera for the first time. He has a tricky little smirk on his face, and speaks to us slowly so we are sure to understand.>>
[Tommy Taylor] : " I am a business man, Together me and Seth are Business Men. Do you know how dangerous that makes us ? I don't think any of you do quite yet, but soon you will. << Winks >> Very soon."
<< Tommy looks away from the Camera and props his feet back up on the table,
Once again relaxed. Seth hangs up the phone and looks to Tommy.>>
[Seth Baptist] : " It's done Boss.." [Tommy Taylor] : " Good....Very good Seth." << We pan out with Tommy laughing and Seth looking very confused at his mentors
Actions.>> ************************************************************************** ************************ That's it guys...Next packet Thursday GET IN SOME SAW COMMERCIALS =) Thanks, Josh AKA - Steve Greedy
