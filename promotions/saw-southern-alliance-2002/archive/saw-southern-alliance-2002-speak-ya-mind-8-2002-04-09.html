SAW (Southern Alliance, 2002) — Roleplay

Speak Ya Mind #8 (part 1 of 2)

9 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
SAW presents "Speak Ya Mind" on April 9th, 2002 You will hear from the Following SAW superstars ************************* 1 - Tommy Flames 2 - Antoine Bradley 3 - C K Walker 4 - Bungle 5 - Rud Groz 6 - The Business Men - Tommy Taylor and Seth Baptist 7 - The Bounty Hunters - Hawke and Lobo 8 - Kyp and Miko 9 - Devin Downs ******************** [ Pan In ] [ As we come into scene we see Tommy Flames sitting in a chair, at the same motel we once saw him at before. Tommy has one noteable difference though, This time he is wearing the Southern Regional Title belt across his shoulder. Tommy has his face burried in his hands, looking to the floor of the motel. Near by on the bed, reaching out is his lovely girlfriend. She has her hand on his shoulder, a smile is on her face the size of Tommy Flames home state of Texas.]
Girlfriend : " You did it Tommy, you really did it." [ Tommy looks up and lowers his hands. On his face you can see a couple tears, and his eyes are red from where he has been whiping them consistantly.]
Tommy Flames : " I did do it didn't I ? " [ His girlfriend nod's happily and the two instantly hug. Playfully Tommy tackles her on the bed sending his title belt to the far end of the bed, and while laying there cuddled up Tommy looks up peaking at the camera.]
Tommy Flames : " I must say thank you to everybody in the SAW. I came in here a young gun with alot of hope, heart and happiness. If it wasn't for yall putting up with me, I wouldn't of had this chance. I also got to thank my girlfriend though, Without her...There would truly be know me. There would be know Tommy
Flames Southern Regional Champion, There would just be simply Tommy Flames,
Another name on the roster."
[ His girlfriend reaches up and kisses him on the cheek. The two lock eyes for a moment smiling, and Tommy uncuddles himself from his beautiful girlfriend. He sits up on the corner of the bed, and shakes his head back and forth.]
Tommy Flames : " I still can't beleive it...But I really did do it. I really did win the tourney for the Southern Regional Title.I beat the rough and tough
Eddie James, I beat fellow texan Vern Walters, and I beat a very competive
Bungle. Don't let his attire and mindgames full you. Bungle is a TOUGH son of a bitch.."
[ Tommy reaches over to the end of the bed and grabs his title. He put's it back on his shoulder and proudly continues.]
Tommy Flames : " I did it all so I could get this title, but as much as I love being the champion. I did it for more than just that. I did it so I could look myself in the mirror, I did it so I could hold my girlfriends hand proudly in public, I did it so my home state of Texas would be proud to say in the diner every morning " Ya that Tommy Flames is from here, About an hour down the road"...I did it for all those things, and to prove to myself I can compete with anybody. Something in which I think I done."
[ Tommy gets up off the bed and takes a couple steps to the near by dresser. He then slowly, carefully lays the belt on the dresser, and walks back to his bed.
He sits back on the corner of the bed, and continues.] Tommy Flames : " I think she will be safe there till in the morning << Smile >>
It's hard for me to put that baby down sometimes. It's like when you have your first kid, You just want to hold it all the time, Or so I hear..I currently haven't had a lil Flames yet, but hopefully I will one day."
Girlfriend : " Soon I hope." [ Tommy glances at her and nod's cheerfully, before looking back up to the camera and continuing.]
Tommy Flames : " This week I get to face another man in which I have respect for. Seem's the SAW loves for me to fight the good guys, Which makes it so much harder in there. It's easy to throw the FLAMES kick at a guy who has no respect for himself or the fans. I want to kick that guy in the head, but a guy who respects the fans, respects me, and respects himself..I hate to try and knock him out, Or at least long enough to get a three count. That is very hard to do, but I have learned it's something you _GOT_ to do once that bell rings."
" So with that said..Antoine Bradley, I know it's a non title match, and I know you probally have your mind on Tommy Taylor, but This weekend I am gonna give it One Hundred percent against you. As I do everytime in that ring. I hope and expect you will do the same. Our match could be a good one, Definitely a good tune up for my Down on the bayou match with James and Walters."
[ Tommy lays back in the bed getting close to his girlfriend again.] Tommy Flames : " Not saying I am gonna beat you, Just saying this should tune me up for any possible thing that James and Walters tosses my direction on the
28th. Don't get me wrong, I am not trying to be cocky by calling this a tune up. I have not been around long enough to do that, Nor beat enough people to do that. I am just really excited about what this match could bring up. You should be as well. Tommy Taylor was in the same tournament I was for the Southern
Regional Title, and we see he didn't end up with the belt for whatever reason.
So maybe I can get you tuned up for him as well. I guess we will just have to see what happens."
" No matter what happens though, Let's shake hands before the match and after. Hopefully we will be shaking hands AFTER I land the Flames kick, but even if were shaking hands after you land the South Memphis special let's make sure our attitude's stay positive and we learn something while we are in there with each other this Saturday on Fever."
[ Tommy get's under the cover's and snuggles close to his girlfriend.] [ Pan Out ] ************************************************************************** ************************ [The camera opens on a seafood restaurant somewhere in Louisiana. "The Real Deal" Antoine Bradley and Cedric are seated in front of a large window. Outside the window is a large lake. Both men have plates of shrimp in front of them. Antoine is wearing a black t-shirt, a gold necklace, and baggy blue jeans; Cedric is wearing the same, minus the necklace. They are discussing something quietly. The camera zooms in on the two men in an effort to better hear what they are saying.]
C: ...I don't see why you didn't press charges. AB: What's there for me to press charges for? Tommy Taylor didn't do anything to me. It was Mike Pace's problem for thinking we did it. It's not Taylor's fault that we both had to spend a week in jail.
C: Are you saying you're perfectly fine with Taylor? You just want him to get off? AB: I don't think you heard what I said. There's no reason to press charges against Taylor. There IS a reason to beat the crap out of him.
C: But I thought you said... AB: Look, Taylor didn't do anything to me THAT WE CAN PRESS CHARGES FOR. The only man who can press charges against Tommy Taylor is Mike Pace, and do I look like Mike Pace? C: No... AB: Listen up, I can't press charges against Tommy Taylor because he attacked Mike Pace. And I can't press charges against Taylor because
Mike Pace thought I did it. If I could press charges against anyone it'd be Pace. He's the one who made me spend a week in jail. But let's leave Pace alone. Right now, it's Tommy Taylor and Seth Baptist that
I'm after. C: Okay. I still don't understand why you want to get Taylor if he didn't have anything to do with it.
AB: YES, he had something to do with it. I'm not saying that it's the same lead pipe I had on me, but it's a little bit suspicious that he used a lead pipe. I'll bet you anything that Taylor did some business with those two guards to check everybody for weapons and frame somebody.
C: I don't like the SAW security guards, but why would they do that for Taylor? AB: Money. Now, that may not be the case, but the fact that Taylor had the audacity to frame me for attacking the Vice President is something that I can't forgive. That's why I want Taylor.
C: You've got Tommy Flames this Saturday, you know. AB: Yes, I know that, and I don't care about Tommy Flames. C: Why not? Tommy Flames is the Southern Regional Champ. If you can beat him, you're guaranteed to get some respect around these parts.
AB: Respect is a good thing to have. But revenge is even better. I can't allow Tommy Taylor and Seth Baptist to go around thinking that they can do whatever they want to. Framing people, attacking the Vice
President, hell, attacking everyone, sending us messages reminding us not to drop the soap... it's all got to stop. I'm telling you, April
28, when I get in the ring with Taylor, he's gonna wish he never put me in jail. Taylor, you picked the wrong guy to mess around with. Because once you go black, you ain't never goin' back.
[Darkness.] ************************************************************************** ************************ The scene opens in the motel room of one C.K Walker. The flamboyant and self proclaimed 'Super Duper Mega Star' sits on a double bed decked out in a 'C.K
Walker' t-shirt, black Adidas warm-up pants and black Nike's. His forehead is heavily bandaged from the recent attacks and a somewhat smirk is etched across his face. On the television is last 'Fever's' Main Event. Walker smiles as a crawling Page outstretches his arm.....
Walker: This is my favorite part. [beaming smile] [The camera pans back to the television. Page out stretches his hand to the massive Reaper who looks at it, then at Page before jumping of the apron and heading to the back. Walker pauses the tape and looks at the camera.]
Walker: Without a doubt one of the _GREATEST_ moments in SAW history right there folks. To see that big moron Reeker turn on Page like that and leave him the ring to meet his doom what for sure, a Kodiak moment!
[Huge smile] But more then that it showed the World another thing. It showed that Reeker
_KNEW_ him and that punk cherry Page didn't have a _CHANCE_ in hell of beating us. Reeker saw just how badly we were outclassing those two punks and decided to get out while the gettin was good instead of being on the losing end of yet another match. To tell you the truth [looks at camera] Reeker I thought you saw the light so to speak amigo! I thought you finally learned that you are no match for me or Rob Fabulous. I thought for the first time in your life you saw that you were out gunned and decided to join the winning team.
[smirks] But no Reeker. Instead you want to be on your own. Is that what you truly want big fella? I mean me and Rob came out there and patted you on the back. Giving you encouragement to destroy that punk Page and to show that we forgave you for being such a _MORON_. And how do you repay our gratitude. By GRABBING US BY THE
DAMN NECKS AND CHOKESLAMMING US ON THE CONCRETE! [Walker calms down] Well
Reeker you just opened yet another chapter in the 'Fabulous and Walker book of how to whip Reaper's and Page's ass easily'. You could of just _walked_ away dead freak. You could of just destroyed Page and did whatever the hell you wanted to do to him without the worry of me and Fabulous making you look like a fool. But no. [smiles] But now you have shown your stupidity once again by not only pissing off me and Fabulous even more. But by getting on Page's bad side as well. Well that boy scout doesn't scare me in the least. But seeing as your an over the hill wind bag Reaper [smiles and winks] I think you did BITE of way more then you can ever hope to chew. You better grow eyes in that back off that dented up head of yours Reeker, cause you can bet soon rather then later C.K
Walker is gonna add a few more dents to that skull of yours before your career is through.
[Walker rewinds the tape. He smiles broadly into the camera as a small 'click' sound is heard of the tape finished rewinding. Walker hits 'Play' and talks as the Main Event match starts all over again.]
Walker: As for you Page. you see this [points at bandaged head] This isn't the work of maniac. No, no, no bucka-roo. This is the work of man driven by jealousy. Sure everyone and their mother can say you went over the edge but the fact is, you ended up with the short end of the stick and couldn't take the fact that me and Rob were able to overcome your average skills and lack of talent. So instead of being a real man, seeing as you knew you couldn't defeat us where it counts. you decided to sneak attack three of the best in SAW to even the score. [Walker smiles huge] Well I have to say is do what you will
Page.. [pauses] and smiles] But the good ole 'Super Duper Mega Star' is _STILL_ going into a match to determine who will fight for the Southern Heavyweight title baby! That's right puke. You can bust my head open, kidnap Cash and beat up Fabulous. But the bottom line is. All your actions are out of the fact that you _KNOW_ you can't hang with us. Trust in due time C.K Walker will get his revenge for this mishap. In due time amigo, you will regret the day you ever put your hands on me. [winks] and that Page is a Walker guarantee. But until that time I will be looking forward to my chance at putting the _SOUTHERN
HEAVYWEIGHT TITLE_ around my waist. What will you be doing Page [smirks and shrugs] Well that's easy bucka-roo, you'll be parking my car like the bus boy of SAW you should of been when you first put your pathetic signature on the dotted line.
[Walker pauses the match again where a pain filled Chris Page is on the mat out stretching his arm to Reaper.]
Walker: See that Page. You imbed that in your small little head their chump cause the next time you meet C.K Walker. That's _EXACTLY_ the same pose you will be doing. On your knees _BEGGING_ for help as the Super Duper Mega Star kicks your pathetic chump ass all over the damn ring!
[Walker turns off the television and leans back on the pile of pillows behind him. He smiles once again.]
Walker: It seems something in SAW is final;ly going to go down right. Since I
_SAVED_ SAW from a HUGE and pathetic mistake, I have said all along that the two _BIGGEST_ and most _TALENTED_ superstars should be fighting for the
Southern title and those two are of course Rob Fabulous and yep [thrusts a thumb at his chest with a huge grin.] C.K Walker. Well Johnson you got it half right. But the fact that me and Fabulous have to meet each other in a match to see who will go on to face whoever for the title is, well [shrugs and smirks]
Downright crappin stupid! Everyone and their grandmother knows whoever wins between me and Fabulous will have a _CAKE WALK_ in beating whoever the chump might be for the biggest title here in SAW. Johnson what you did was take the hugest buy rate PPV match in history and instead of making it a title match
[shakes head] you put it on Fever to determine who will face a low life chump for that honor... what is up with that? I'll never quite figure how you think amigo. It truly amazes me how you could over look such facts. Rob Fabulous v.s
C.K Walker, [smiles] think about it. Two of the three biggest stars in SAW vying for a shot at the Southern title, I'm tellin ya Johnson you screwed up big time. Instead of making _LOADS_ of cash off this _HISTORICAL_ match.
[shakes head] you give it to a bunch of lowly red necks in some barn. You should be ashamed of yourself.
[Walker smiles] Walker: Now rob Fabulous. There is no doubt that C.K Walker has all the respect in the world for you. The way I see I see it. you and me think alike Amigo! We see diseases like Reeker and Page contaminating SAW and we decided to take the trash out. We _KNOW_ were the two greatest athletes on this planet and prove night in and night out by beating chump after chump without breaking a sweat.
But now it's the time of truth Robbie. The question running through everyone's mind will finally be answered. ' Just which one is the best?'. Well i know we both have a different answer to that Fabulous. On one hand you think your the best [holds up a palm.] While on the other [holds up his other palm] I know I'm the best. There is no need to diss your accomplishments amigo. you have destroyed Page week over week and shown that he is a chump. [claps] Bravo my friend. But [holds up index finger] I _SAVED_ SAW from a terrible and near company killing tragedy. I left the Reaper lying in a pool of his own blood.
Not only that, C.K Walker fought the 'Big Bad' Reeker on his own turf and whipped that dead man's fruity ass like _NOBODY_ before me has ever done. I went into the fire of Reaper and came out unscathed. So yeah Fabulous, while your accomplishments have been good... mine have been great. We both have the same goal in mind but are taking different paths. you chose to get rid of Page,
I did get _RID_ of Reaper. I made him bail on Chris Page cause he _KNEW_ he was on the losing team. He didn't want his shoulders to once again get pinned by
C.K Walker. That is why on this upcoming Fever, I will walk out the winner
Fabulous. It will be a true test. Yes, C.K Walker will have to use all his talents but in the end, C.K Walker will prove he can rise to the occasion. The bets are piling up and right about now were even, and with good cause. But when the match is all over and done with. [Smiles] The men and women] who put down their bets on the 'Super Duper Mega Star' will walking out a fistful of dollar richer. I know you feel the same way Fabulous. [smiles] That's what makes us great. But there can only be one winner to move on to wrap that Heavyweight title around his waist and that guy _WILL_ be C.K Walker. You better bring your
'A' game to the party Fabulous cause I don't want any excuses when I kick your ass all over that ring. Till Fever bucka-roo [pauses and smiles] I'll see you on the flip side.
[Camera Fades.] ************************************************************************** ************************ <<The scene opens in a Mexican resturant, Bungle is sitting down at table wearing his normal bear costume. On his head he is wearing a large Mexican hat.
In front of him on the table is a tequilla slammer.>> [Bungle]: "You can't get better than an Italian, but when you can't afford a
Pizza there is always room for a Mexican as far as I'm concerned."
<<Bungle lifts the slammer up and downs it in one.>> [Bungle]: "Four men in one tag team match, damn this is going to be crazy. Mexican rules? I didn't think anyone in Mexico had any rules, I better not say that to loud. You see Tommy Flames did me the pleasure of getting me out of that damn Southern Regional Title division into one that I have succeded at in the past so many times, you see there is nothing anyone can do better than this bear and that is fly round the ring. Lightweight, Cruiserweight or Junior
Heavyweight it doesn't matter what you call it, it doesn't matter how many people you put in the ring with me or how many stupid foriegn rules there are, it will not affect me"
<<Bungle gets to his feet and walks over to the bar where he is handed a beer.>> [Bungle]: "You see no one in the wrestling industry has ever listened to me, for years I have been asking for a Gay division. No one cares about the
Cruisers, Heavyweights or Tag Team titles anymore. This is the year 2002 and people want Gay Vs. Gay with some nice Gold at the end of it. Come on can you imagine the two best Gay wrestlers in this sport going head to head. I've had a fair few matches against Taylor ThreaT in the past but that would be amazing with the Gay Title on the line."
<<Bungle grabs the Mexican behind the bar and kisses him on the lips.>> [Bungle]: "So remmember guys, your only gay if you take it...and I'm going to make you all gay."
<<scene fades to black>> ************************************************************************** ************************ [The camera opens up in the locker room of Rud Groz, the russian suplex machine stands against the wall with the russian flag in both hands draped behind him.
Rud's head is lowered and his eyes closed, the sweat from his match with Payne dripping down his face as he speaks lowly, his Russian accent marking every word]
Groz: Last week..I proved why this Chris Payne is indeed a chump. He was just another large american who thought he can enter a real man's sport and excel.
The only thing he was able to excel in was loosing.... [Groz slowly looks back up and tilts his head back looking up at the celling] Groz: I proved why Rud Groz is number one...I proved why Mother Russia is number one..and this week...I shall do it once again this week one one Todd Van
Rooyen...
[Groz draps the flag around his neck and shoulders as he looks up to the camera] Groz: I am not some character...pretending to be dead....a homoerotic fool running around in a bear costume...White Gangster...comic book characters...religious zelots, or scrawny mexican luchardo...I am Rud
Groz...the greatest pure wrestler in the SAW and this filthy capitalist country! [Groz's lips take on a sadistic grin as he glares into the camera] Groz: Chris Payne did not give up...nor did I pin him...he wasn't able to continue because like in all sports...Russia had once again shown their dominance over the US and the world....Todd Van Rooyen, if you did not take note of what occured in that ring...your fate will match that of your American brothern...only no one will be able to stop me...from ending your career. Make sure you are ready to wrestle me this week...or you may never wrestle again...
[Groz looks down and spits onto the ground shaking his head in disgust] Groz: American swine... [Rud walks away as the camera pans downward showing the ground where the American Flag lies in a crumpled heap on the ground with a wad of spit visibly staining it as the camera fades to black]
