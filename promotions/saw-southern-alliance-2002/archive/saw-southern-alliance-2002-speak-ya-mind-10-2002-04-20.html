SAW presents "Speak Ya Mind" on April 20th, 2002 You will hear from the Following SAW superstars ************************* 1 - Daniel Trenton 2 - C K Walker 3 - Juan Sanchez 4 - Rud Groz 5 - Estrago 6 - Antoine Bradley 7 - Bungle 8 - Tommy Flames ******************** Sometime in April. The air would move fast between his golden highlighted hairs that were roughly pulled back into a makeshift ponytail for the time being. He would go whizzing down the interstate at a high rate of speed. He always loved to drive fast cars. It was that one factor of danger that he just loved so much. He had always loved to do things his way, the way he was deprived of as a child. As a wrestling superstar, he had everything in front of him. A job that he enjoyed, getting paid for doing something he loved. He was achieving what few could ever do in their lifetime.
Privilege by Incubus was blaring fairly loudly over his expensive sound system. With his base rattling subs pushing out all of that air that it could. His head was nodding slightly as he would move faster and faster. Then he heard the phone ring from the drivers side of the car. He could remember the last time he talked on the phone while driving was to MISSLE back when the two were teaming in the Fans Wrestling Federation for a brief stint in their battle against mutual enemies. He would pick the phone and place it close to his ear as he would hear the voice of a man he never thought he would hear. Hey, This Daniel
Trenton? , the voice reeked of a cheesy busniss man accent.
Not wanting to lie, he would contemplate for a second, maybe two. Should he answer? Knowing the Fans Wrestling Federation it could be their World Champion,
Nathanial Rezno Glore playing some kind of mind games with Daniel after he had left the fed. With a deep gulp in his throat he would swallow any self doubt and answer back towards the man. Yea, who would this be? he questioned.
This be Steve Greedy, I was wondering are you signed to a federation right now? Because the SAW would be very interested in getting you to join the talent roster, if you are up for it.. The man would say.
Daniel would say, Well .. I dont know, I was looking forward to taking some time off from this whole wrestling thing. Yea, that whole wrestling thing, the thing that he had spent his last four years or so doing. The thing he had wanted to do ever since he was a child. The THING that made him the man he is today.
Hey man, dont think of it as wrestling. Think of it more as a chance to become the superstar that FWF wouldnt make you. Steve would try to bribe Trenton with his own ego. It was true, the Fans Wrestling Federation didnt serve him right.
They had used his talent time after time with out him getting one title shot, without him getting the star treatment that everyone else got. Hell, it was partly the reason of why he left. but we want you here in the SAW, Southern
Alliance Wrestling, and I want you here for my own reasons.
Maybe we will talk after all, Ive got to get off the phone right now though. Im slightly busy. He would tell the SAW owner. His thoughts and memories of some of his most memorable matches would fill his head in those few seconds. His first title shot against Jimmy in hopes to get the FWF International title. His match against Terminal Frost to win his first title in the big mouth title. His last match, that forever will go down in the history books against Brandon
Louden, the pit of hell match. Surly if he could have survived all of those matches, he could survive in the Southern Alliance Wrestling.
A week, maybe two weeks later it was time for Daniel to go to the SAW studios. It was time for Daniel to speak his mind for the first time since he had left
FWF. He was wearing his normal attire. He didnt need to dress-up like the other wrestlers, he never needed to. All of those guys were fake on the outside and hollow on the inside. He nodded as he looked forward, and with that a red light would flicker on and he would speak his mind.
The next couple of weeks might just be some of the hardest weeks of my life. Im starting on a journey, that very well could lead me to the end of my career.
Some kind of ride, eh? I sit and think about all of the time I spent in my life trying to please the billions of people in my life, but Im left to ask my self one thing, have you ever tried to please your self?, now that was the question
I should have been asking when I was fighting Ranma Saotome, or when I was looking for the acceptance of the fans.
Breath in, breath out. Thought turned into words. "I think about all the greats that I faced in the Fans Wrestling Federation, and then take a look at the Southern Alliance Wrestling. I think to myself, new names, fresh faces. And maybe that is just what I need in my life, a new, fresh start."
The tape stopped rolling, and the red light turned off. For one more day, he got to be the hero he wasnt. ************************************************************************** ************************ The scene opens in the locker room of C.K Walker who is lived. Against anyone else besides yours truly I would pick you as the winner. But against me your the under dog Robbie. your the guy who is looking up and thinking, can I beat that stud muffin?!
