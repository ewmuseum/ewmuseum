SOUTHERN ALLIANCE WRESTLING "Time to go down south" Update # 2 March 24th, 2002 Table of Contents 1 - Out of Character Notes 2 - News and Rumors 3 - The Roster 4 - Records 5 - Rankings 6 - Deadline and Dates 7 - Booked Shows 8 - The Ending *************** 1 - OOC Notes *************** Well guys, Week One is in the books, and I think it went pretty smoothly..We got some angles developing whether you know it yet or not...The flashes were pretty consistant and solid..I must say things went as good or better than I expected to begin with. With that being said though we must now move on and prove to be consistant...Like in sports, One good game don't make you good, and for us to become a GOOD fed we got to keep the ball rolling..So Thanks in advance for yalls help on keeping this ball rolling Thanks, Josh *************** 2 - News and Rumors *************** 1 - The SAW roster may be getting a couple more new faces besides that of C K Walker, Lenny, Antoine Bradley, Rud Groz and "The Disciple" Seth Baptist...Be on the look out for that 2 - Mike Johnson has released the name of the first SAW supercard...It will be called "Down on the Bayou"...An arena has not yet been booked, Nor a date set, but Mike said the line up should start taking form very soon, along with the rest of the details. 3 - A big tag team is currently looking over the contract's SAW sent them, and is possibly close to signing them..Commish of the SAW Mike Pace is huge into tag team wrestling and is trying his hardest to get them to sign. 4 - Arthur Dexter Bradley is questionable for showing at next week's Fever. Word is Johnson was highly upset with him sending his manager to fight in his place at Fever last week.. 5 - For the first ever Supercard of the SAW Mike Johnson is trying to get ahold of the Century Tell arena in Bossier City, Louisiana which holds just over 10,000 people...The deal is still a long shot though to be honest... 6 - The SAW is in talks with an all japan federation in possibly having some inter fed matches on some of the Japanesse federations cards to help promote both feds and give good action to the wrestling fans...Rumored names to be competing over there are Tommy Flames, Vern Walters, Simon Starr, Bungle, Tommy Taylor and leading the possible group would be Chris Page...The SAW wrestlers would be working for a real low price if the deal does go down. 7 - Mike Pace is investigating the attack on him that occured on Fever, and said "Somebody will pay next week"....We will continue to follow this as it unfolds. 8 - C K Walker is being forced to compete in the first ever hardcore match in the SAW cause of his attack during the championship match at Fever as punishment from the boss.... *************** 3 -
