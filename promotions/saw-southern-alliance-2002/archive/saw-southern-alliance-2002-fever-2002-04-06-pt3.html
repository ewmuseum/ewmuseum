Saturday Night Fever, 6 April 2002 (part 3 of 6)

6 April 2002

[ Fade back into inside the locker room of Todd Van Rooyen. We see Samantha
Owens sitting on the couch in between TVR and his manager Dark Tower. All our smiling, and the interview starts.]
Samantha Owens : " My first week in SAW I got to interview Taylor Threat, and C
K Walker...My next week ADB no showed me, and I had to slap Robbinfield..This week I got Todd Van Rooyen...and I am in his locker room."
TVR : " A place I bet your used to being...<< Winks >> " Samantha Owens : " I personally don't like doing interview's in someone's locker room, but I do what I have to do."
TVR : " So I hear..." [ Tower and Todd chuckle as Samantha gets a lil red in the face.] Samantha Owens : " Why have you been so silent, yet so deadily so far in the
SAW ? Usually your a man of lots of words and actions....Now your just a man of lots of actions."
TVR : " Don't actions speak louder than words sweetie ? " Samantha Owens : " My name is Samantha.." TVR : " Sam, Whore, Slut, Bitch, Dick sucker...It's all the same to me sweetie.." Samantha Owens : " WHY I NEVER......" [ Samantha gets up off of the couch and storms out of the room, as she opens the door to the locker room though. Johnny Danger and Damian Rock bust in trapping her inside as well.]
Johnny Danger : " Looks like the heatseeker found some heat going on up in here..Two guys on the couch and the girl trying to leave the room...Todd,
Didn't I tell you once before girls arent into the whole thing like we are. We like two girls, they don't like two guys..."
[ Todd and Tower get up off of the couch angrily..Balling there fist tightly. Danger side steps the door and pushes Samantha out.] Johnny Danger : " I am about sick of You two...Both of ya....I don't know what your deal is Guys, but soon you will both be out of my hair. Rock is gonna take on Tower tomorrow , and show why he is a former EULW champion,and soon....<<
Raises voice >> VERY SOON....I will get my chance to beat you down Todd...One
On One.."
TVR : " Why don't you do it now big man....I am right here..." Johnny Danger : " Your not worth it..." [ Danger and Rock laugh then exit the locker room...An upset TVR begins to throw stuff in the locker room as Tower tries to calm him down.]
Fred Hart : " I can't wait to see those two get it on in the middle of the ring, It will be just like old times in the EULW."
Samuel Smith : " Didn't follow that federation...All you there Fred." Fred Hart : " It was a great federation, Really was.....I hope they can carry it to that level."
Samuel Smith : " Forget that level...I just hope they can carry it to the level in which our first two matches have went to..They have been great.."
Fred Hart : " Talk about great matches Samuel, and this next one might not be the first one that comes to mind, but it should be!"
Samuel Smith : "I couldn't agree with ya' more Fred. We're gonna' have the six foot, nine inch, Chris Payne stepping into the ring against Rud Groz. Even though I don't really like Rud Groz and the anti-American stance he has, I sure can't knock his wrestling ability. That kid can flat out wrestle, and if you don't watch out, he will throw you all around the ring with suplexes that you haven't even heard of. "
Fred Hart : "And don't forget that you've got to be watching out for that
Russian Neck Drop all along! That thing could not only send you home for the evening, but it might put a damper on your chances of making it to the next
S.A.W. show! "
["My name is Kiss" By Jadakiss starts to blare over the arena P.A. while the lights begin the flicker.]
Fred Hart : "Of course, we can't forget about Chris Payne. Another man who you might not always agree with his actions, but you can't knock his in ring style!
"The Ultimate Chump" is not a wrestler who you are going to want to mess with, and tonight he might just knock off Rud Groz in what would be considered an upset."!
[Chris Payne makes his way out to the ring with a few poses, getting alot of boos. Payne gets inside the ring, walks around talking to himself, ignoring the chants, and with the infamous cocky smirk on his face, he plops down to the ring and sits against the turnbuckle.]
Samuel Smith : "I don't know why people have a problem with Payne! Sure, he might get you with a cheap shot here and there, but look at that confidence.
You really can't knock a wrestler who has that much confidence on his side. He rolled up Johnny Danger for a pin last week...Just last week him and Groz were on the same six man team....What a difference, a Week makes."
Fred Hart : "You thought the crowd wasn't receptive to Chris Payne, listen to the ovation that Rud Groz is getting! The man doesn't even have music and it is probably better that way, because with all the booing it would just get drowned out anyways "
[Rud Groz stands in the entrance way. He stops at the top of the ramp and puts his hands on his hips. His tights and his boots are both matching red, with the
Soviet Union's hammer and sickle on both of them. People are booing this man like none other as he walks down to the ring and Groz begins to jaw at some who seem to be heckling him especially hard. He looks like he is going to jump the barrier and go after one of them, but just does the throat slitting motion as he keeps walking to the ring. He rubs his bald head as he steps up the ring steps and walks to his corner and begins to stretch.]
************************************************************************** *********************** Third Bout Fifteen Minute Time Limit Rud Groz vs Chris Payne Referee - Aaron Kent ************************************************************************** *********************** Fred Hart : "Russians are not really welcome here in the south, and when you start going after the fans, you have to think that they might start to like
Payne a little bit more, maybe even get behind him."
Samuel Smith : "I don't know if you can go that far, but the two men are just staring each other down from the opposite corners, and you can already feel the heat between these two fellas. This is going to be a helluva' match man!"
[Rud Groz and Chris Payne both walk into the center of the ring. The two men go face to face...or attempt to. Groz is quite a deal shorter and is still pressuring Payne to hit him first. Groz's chin is barely up to Payne's pecks.
Finally, after having enough of the Russians lip, Payne rears back and nails
Groz right in the temple.]
Samuel Smith : " That knocked Rud all the way back to his corner and Groz looks happy that he got hit! He is rubbing his temple with a smile on his face and is enjoying it!
Fred Hart : "I knew the man was a little different but we might want to get him checked out for some sort of chemical embalance! Not many people like getting hit by "The Ultimate Chump" because he is no small man, but Rud doesn't mind at all!
[Rud comes back into the center of the ring and Payne is still sitting there. Payne is the one talking now and it looks like Rud is just ignoring him. Rud seems to be shrugging off the fact that he just got punched in the face and is asking for it again. Payne obliges and hits Groz square in the nose!]
Samuel Smith : "Did we just see a replay? Groz basically asked for another shot like that and it didn't seem like he cared this time either. He is actually chuckling as he rubs the sting out of his face. I think you are right Hart, this man might have something wrong with him upstairs. He IS a Ruskie after all."
Fred Hart : "What's that supposed to mean? I have Russian herritage after all! Groz is going back again. The six foot, one inch Rud Groz is going back to the center of the ring. He wants one more!
[When Groz goes back to the middle of the ring this time, Payne doesn't wait for him to talk trash. Payne gives Groz a huge boot to the gut and Payne locks him up. Payne starts using some knees right to the mid section of Groz. The crowd starts chanting and counting the knees! One! Two! Three! Four! FI---Groz grabs the knee on the fifth attempt and flips Payne right over his back into a modified Norther Lights Suplex! Right into a pinning combination.]
Fred Hart: "What a counter by the Human Suplex Machine of the S.A.W...Least that's what I call him after seeing him in action last week and at a recent house show."
[One!...] [Tw--Payne Kicks out, and Groz shakes his head in disgust.] Samuel Smith : "What? Did Groz actually think he was going to get a pin this early in the match? Hell, did he think he was the one inflicting all the pain from those blows to the face he was taking earlier? What a tough skull this man has.."
Fred Hart : "You'll never have short matches here in the S.A.W.! If you want a quicky, go see our competition. I hear JCW has plenty of them! Payne is back up and is a little groggy after that wonderful suplex by Groz."
[Groz grabs the groggy Payne and locks him up. He lifts him over with a quick snap suplex attempt. Groz back to his feet, he grabs Payne again and goes for the same manuver! Rolling Snap Suplexes by the Soviet Suplexer! The second one has him up and over, and without breaking stride Groz is going for a third. He has Payne up, and over! Three snap suplexes! Payne grabs his back on the mat, and Groz goes over to the ropes where the fan was talking to him earlier as he was coming in! Payne is hurting, but Groz doesn't capitalize.]
Fred Hart : "The young Soviet maybe making a mistake by not going for the quick pin attempt and instead talking to the fans. This is givining the big man plenty of time to recover from those vicious snap suplexes he took just moments ago."
Samuel Smith : "It isn't that hard to throw a kid around from behind with the rolling German Suplexes that you see some wrestlers try and do. Try and do that while you are squared up on the wrestler and lifting him right up and over your head three times in the row, when you are giving up eight inches and about twenty five pounds! That is a suplex monster!
Fred Hart : "His strength might be unmatched, but his in ring psychology has a little left to be desired! Payne is back to his feet and is just waiting for
Groz to turn around!"
[Payne is back to his feet and is rubbing the back of his neck. He squats down and rests his elbows on his knees. He is just waiting for Groz to turn around.
Rud, done jawwing with the crowd, turns his body around but still has his head on a swivel. Payne takes off from his squatted position and clotheslines Groz up and over the top rope. Payne's momentum carries him over the top rope as well, and both men taking there time to get to their feet. The ref begins the standing ten count.]
Samuel Smith : "That shot from The Chump damn near decapitated Rud, but carried him over the top rope and he smacked his head pretty hard on the ground!"
Fred Hart: "You're right, I'm actually not to sure who got the worse end of that, and by the looks of things, and with the ref already at four, it looks as though Payne is making it to his feet, but barely. He uses the bottom rope to get some support and slides under the bottom rope, breaking the count.Smart move by Payne..."
[Payne is laying in the middle of the ring, and as the count is started over,
Groz is getting back to his feet. He shakes some of the cob webs free and rolls back into the ring, very slowly. Payne picks him up off the ground, and delivers an irish whip. He has Groz going off the ropes, Payne goes for the big boot, but Groz ducks under. Groz spins the big man around, grabs one arm, HEAD
AND ARM SUPLEX! Groz just lifted Payne up and over his head with a head and arm
Suplex.]
Samuel Smith : "OH MY GOODNESS! That was a huge manuver! " Fred Hart : "Groz is looking good after that move, and all be it he is a little tired, he is picking the big man back up!
[Groz lifts Payne up off the mat. He has Payne off of his left hip, like he was going to go for a Side Salto slam. Rud picks him up and throws him right back down on his face! The Russian Dominator! Groz isn't done, and wants to finish this man off the embarassing way. He picks the man up and hoists him up for a brain buster! He drops it flawlessly. Payne isn't responding after that move, and Groz is just standing over the big man laughing at him. The ref is calling the match over and is trying to push Rud away, but Rud grabs the ref and gives him a snap suplex.]
Samuel Smith : "Seems The ref hs thrown this match out, calling it a victroy for Groz! The ref deemed that Payne was not able to continue, but it looks like the Soviet wants blood!
Fred Hart : "This has gotten out of hand folks! The ref was just taken out by that snap suplex, and Groz has now positioned the debilitated Payne in the corner, facing the crowd.
[Groz is right behind Payne who is propped up by the turnbuckle. Groz puts
Payne on the top rope and climbs to the second rope himself. He starts yelling at some of the fans, points to the sickle and hammer logo on his boots and then grabs Payne in an inverted DDT position. Groz lifts Payne up and down with an inverted suplex from the top rope! Payne is shaking now and holding his face and Groz points at Payne, and then rolls out of the ring.]
Fred Hart : "Groz is a sick individual! It looked like he was trying to kill
Payne!" Samuel Smith : "Well, at least we can see Payne moving now! That means he is alive, but we don't know in what condition..While we get him out of the ring,
Let's go to the back..I hear C K Walker is back there with Johnny Jackson."
Fred Hart : " I would rather stay here and check on Payne, than listen to
Walker.." Samuel Smith : " Same here, but we got an outline to follow..." [ The scene opens to SAW interviewer Johnny Jackson. The big ex-bouncer is decked out in black dress pants, black dress shoes, white shirt with the top button undone and a black, stylish suit jacket over top. He stands in front of the black SAW logo that is splashed over a white wall. Jackson looks at his watch then at the camera.]
Johnny Jackson : " He told me to meet him here. Where in the he....." [Before he can finish a voice makes him jump.] Voice: " Double J is in the house!! Raise the roof! Raise the roof! " [Jackson rolls his eyes as C.K Walker walks up on the opposite side. The youngster is decked out in white wrestling pants with gold barbedwire entwined around the legs and waist, black boots with black shinguards over-top and a black SAW t-shirt. Walker smiles broadly at the camera then at Jackson.]
Johnny Jackson : "You scared the crap out of me Walker. Now why did you want this interview? "
[Walker smiles broadly.] Walker: " People told me you were a hard ass Double J [thumbs up!] and they were right. A 'Right down to business' sort of guy. I dig that amigo! "
[Walker smiles again and does another thumbs up towards Jackson. Johnny shakes his head.] Walker: " The reason I sent for you is because you were the right man for the job to listen to the super duper Mega Star C.K Walker. That Samantha is one loose cannon, WOO-HOO, but right now I need a serious guy for a serious look on some facts!."
[Walker smiles again then pops a piece of gum [Sugarless Clorets gum by the way:)] in his mouth. He motions the pack at Jackson who shakes his head. Walker shrugs.]
Walker: " Fine [sticks gum back in his pocket.] Suit yourself. If you want to have teeth like all these Southern fools to feel more at home, then go ahead. I like ALL my pearly whites in my mouth, not sitting in a jar at home with Uncle
Coot like their some kind of trophy."
[Walker begins to chew his gum loud. Jackson, who is already annoyed seems to be getting even angrier.]
Johnny Jackson : " Well what do you wanna talk about. I got other things to do. Later I am interviewing a NEW SAW wrestler..." Walker: [hands out in front of him] Whoa! Whoa! Whoa! Settle down there Double
J! You just relax there karate kid and take a good ole chill pill!
[Walker smiles as Jackson who rolls his eyes.] Walker: " Now as everyone knows in just a few short moments me and Rob Fabulous will be taking on the dysfunctional team of Chris Page and Reeker. Now this was no big news to anyone, but I have something to tell the World that they MAY not know. "
Johnny Jackson : " And that would be? " Walker: " That Page and Reeker are deadly SCARED of this upcoming match! " Johnny Jackson : " I hardly think so Walker." Walker: No? Then why is it that nobody has heard from PAGE or Reeker since the match was signed. Why is it that Reeker hasn't said a damn word since I destroyed him and cost him the title. Wanna know why Jackson.
Johnny Jackson : " Actually he spoke on the last 'Speak Ya Mind." Walker: He did? [taps chin] Must of fell asleep. But besides that you wanna know why Page and him have kept their mouths pretty much shut?
Johnny Jackson :" Sure.... why not." Walker: Cause they damn well know C.K Walker has their number that's why. Page knows that Fabulous and myself are going to take those two pretenders and ship them out of the SAW. They are mad as hell that Mr. Johnson is making them face the two of us because we have proven time and time again that their not even
CLOSE to being as talented as us. The fact is Jackson. Their mad cause they
KNOW their gonna get embarrassed by the best damn talent SAW has. Hell, [blows a bubble] I'd be mad to if two more talented guys were gonna make me look like a fool.
[Walker pops the bubble, chews loud then blows another bubble. He pops it again then chews in a cocky, yet annoying manner.]
Walker: To tell you the truth I can't wait for this match. C.K Walker and Rob
Fabulous in the ring against two of the biggest stinkers in wrestling history.
The chance for us to prove our dominance over these fools has not been some sort of lucky winning streak. But a week or more of PROOF that these boys aren't what everyone cut's them out to be. I mean come on people! Look at
Reeker! I, little old C.K Walker DESTROYED this man in a Hardcore match! Rob
Fabulous DESTROYED big bad Page like he was NOTHING in the middle of the ring week in and week out. Yes [smiles] This Fever is gonna be something. I can't wait to FINALLY knock those two out of SAW then wrap that big piece of gold around my waist. To finally take my rightful spot on top of SAW and be the most dominant champion this place has ever seen.
Johnny Jackson : " You know your just adding fuel to a fire that is burning for revenge in both Reaper and Page don't you? "
Walker: [waves his hands in 'Shoo' like manner] Big frickin deal Double J. I heard all their crap. These guys prance around like their some kind of icon....
[points at himself] C.K Walker is the real icon! They blab about what their gonna do to people then go out and get their asses kicked! You think I care what 'fire' is burning inside of them? Hell no! Heck I already walked into
Reaper's fire and came out unscathed. These guys talk and talk but when it comes time to put up or shut up [winks] We both saw what happens. They get their fruity asses handed to 'em. This match will be the final cut on these guys careers. I'm sick and tired of hearing their names. C.K Walker and Rob
Fabulous will prove without a doubt that when it comes to the two biggest stars in SAW, it's us!
Johnny Jackson : " Well....." Walker: Well nothing Double J. Don't make excuses for those losers. Those two amigo's have NOTHING against me and Fabulous. Their running scared but me and
Rob will catch him and when we do [smiles] Were gonna kick them out of OUR federation for good. Now if you'll excuse me, I have an appointment to prepare for.
[Walker walks off as the camera goes to a commercial promoting future SAW house shows just recently released to the public..]
