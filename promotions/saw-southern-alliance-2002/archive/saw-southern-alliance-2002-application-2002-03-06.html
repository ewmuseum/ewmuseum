Application: Time to Go Down South

The actual Application ---------------------------------- 1. The Purpose of SAW ---------------------------------- The SAW like many federations before it is going to be ran in the Southern parts of the United States. The Arkansas, Mississippi ,and Louisiana area's will be used frequently, and possibly for Supercards we might hit up the Texas or Tennessee area..Small cities like Vicksburg,Crossett,Monroe, Alexandria and
Shrevport will be used as our main stops among others.I myself am a Southern man and love the South with all my heart and soul. I am not gonna lie or try to manipulate you and say this will be like NOOOOO other federation before....That's not true, but What we are gonna do is try to build a fun,
Satisfying, Humble home for role player's such as yourself..A place where angle idea's will be used, and show's will be enjoyable to say the least...I have ran federations before, Many as a matter of fact...And some of you are prolly saying "Here he goes again", But this time I want to keep it small and instead of my tradtional AOL type fed...This will be a net fed...I am sending the application to mostly net role players so It's not a problem, but for the few of you who mainly AOL role play...Any questions and I will answer...You can catch me online late pretty much every night.
I am not saying the federation won't have it's hardcore moments, but my goal is to keep it realistic...Supercards we might go a lil out of our way, but for the majority I want to have a realistic vibe and flow to every result which ever comes out with SAW on the top of it...I want you to feel like you are really in touch with the show and the characters...Kinda that feeling you feel when your at your home, sleeping in your bed, Instead of that feeling you feel when you are at a friends staying the night....I know you all know that feeling, and that's not the one we are going for this time around...Storylines, Angles, and of course fun is our main goals, but don't let me fool you. I still want to be successful...There is different ways of being that of course, and the way I hope to be successful is by making this a "HOME" for some of you....That would be the way I look at us being successful or non successful...Cause that's what it is all about..
---------------------------- 2. Office Members of SAW ---------------------------- Owner/President : Mike Johnson...Formerly known as Mike "The Always Right" Johnson, The old president of the KAWF now looks to try his luck again, Except this time he is following his heart and soul...KAWF was a big time promotion ran with alot of money and Flashy items, people and Ideas..This time Mike is going back home, and doing what he always wanted to do..Which is build a wrestling dynasty in the southern part of the United States, His hometown...He has alot of money put back to pay the wrestlers, get the arena's and make the merchandise, but he hopes to not have to use it..This time Mike wants the merchandise to sell itself, and hopefully it will...Mike has always been a cocky man, With an edge, but with those negatives we must list his positives...Which is being able to do the best with what he has, and getting the best out of any certain person.
Head Booker/ Commish : Mike Pace. Mike has announced for many federations in his past.Some ran by Mike Johnson and Some not, but this time Mike's roll has become just a lil bit more important...Mike is in charge of booking, designing, outlining and running all the show's in the SAW. Mike Johnson has the final say of course, but this man right here Has the first, Middle and next to last say..Which could lead to him being the most important man in the SAW as Mike
Johnson wants to kinda play a hands off role as his motto is gonna be for the SAW " Let the Merchandise sell itself.." Pace is a nice guy, A very big fan of Tag team wrestling when done fairly and loves to see clean finishes..So hopefully we will see our share of them in the SAW..
Vice President/ Public Relations Specialist : Shawn Cross. With all the on TV stuff, There is just as much off TV stuff to be done, and tons of media, fans, reporters, and so on to deal with...University of Louisiana Monroe graduate
Shawn Cross is the lucky man picked to deal with these situations...If there is a press conference look for him to be there, if there is a crisis or an honorable mention for something well done look for him to be there...Shawn is gonna run the political side of the SAW, and hopefully he will run it well..
Little bit bout me....My name is [name withheld] as most of you already know, and I have handled a couple E wrestlers...Mainly being The Outlaw, Steve Greedy and
Horo Atashi....I have ran many federations in my seven years of doing this...Some of em worked, and some of em didn't...I am leaning towards this one working, Just trust me if you could....I am 19 years of age and got a full time job....So my online schedule might be a lil haywire, But I do get online a decent amount...Enough to run a federation and role play in IWF/WOW, SCW, and
Soon SAWA plus NOP...I promise ya....By the way I reccomend joining SAWA and NOP both seem to me by talking with the presidents and looking at the basic outline to have poiental to be good federations...
-------------------------------------- 3. Tradtional Rules and Regulations -------------------------------------- 1. The SAW will be an all Fake email federation of course, So the only person you will send things to for shows or when it comes to flashing is yours truly, But I do highly encourage the getting together of handlers to make things come out on air a lil bit better than they would have without the actual handlers getting in touch with each other. So anytime you want a role players Email ADDY you lemme know and I'll see what I can do
2. We are here to have fun...So please express all ideas, opinions or even what you may consider facts. I am pretty level headed and easy to get along with. Part of this is also though, When you lose....cause it will happen....Everybody loses sometime or another..Please don't complain, Just take it like a man and regroup for the next go around..
3. No More than Two Flashes a week per character, and Please no Logo's, colors or cute lil fonts [ I put that in there for the AOL guys : p ]..... --------------------- 4. SAW Titles --------------------- The SAW is gonna start out with Three titles, and More may be added later when we see it nessecary or beneficial to the federation, but for now the following are the only titles you can win, Which makes em that more special to have them..
1 - The Southern Championship - This is the impact player of the federation, He is the man currently holding the key to the success of the federation. Our top belt, Held by our top guy at that current time in the federation..
2 - The Southern Regional Championship : This is the man sitting by the throne, Being the backbone of the Federation..He represents the South and this company the best he can..He will preform a little more often than our champion, As all three office members like to make title fights special, So the belts won't be defended every week, But since there is only two single titles...This man will be hunted by many and prolly stalked by some.
3 - The Southern Tag team Championship - Office Member Mike Pace loves tag team action to the fullest extent. Most federations have a weak Tag division, but
Pace stated in a news article involving the SAW's opening " He will do whatever, It takes to make the tag division Prosper"...So we will just have to see what he means by that...
Possible belts for the Future 1 - The Southern Lightweight championship - For the little guys, who make sure we don't blink during there matches and make us go blind with there speed
2 - The Southern Television championship - The man best for marketing and the man who will prolly be the busiest wrestler in the company if you know what I am saying.
3 - The Southern Streetlife Championship - This is for those chair swinging, Blood dripping, Snot nosed tough guys out there....The guy's who don't want rules and won't folllow them anyway fit in right here...
----------------------------------- 5. Schedule and Shows ----------------------------------- The SAW will have A review/ House show type show Every Sunday which is called War Stories, A live interview session called "Speak your Mind" on Tuesday's and Thursday's, Along with a weekly show on Saturday's called "Saturday Night Fever"...Occasionaly a house show might appear, and once a month or maybe every two months the SAW will have a supercard in a bigger arena than we normally hold our shows in...This will be whenever Mike Johnson feel's is a right time for doing so...
Tuesday and Thursday = Flash Packet days Saturday = Our Main show Sunday = A review of the past, A Preview of the future and some House show type matches for the present..
Deadline's and dates will be given later when the federation technically "Starts"... ----------------------------------------- 6. Roleplaying and Strats ----------------------------------------- Roleplays will be sent out on "Speak Your Mind" On Tuesday's and Thursdays...Don't get these confused with In ring's or Interview's though..They will of course be posted on the results or the review show depending on what day they are meant for..
When it comes to role playing I Just hope we can see some serious realistic stuff, along with some witty humor...I love creativity, but remember we are striving for realism here in the SAW...
Strats, These are meant to help develop your characters, Help the match writers, and get story lines over in good detail...I highly encourage these,
Tell me some good spots for your characters, Against your characters, Give me there mindset, attitude, feelings...Nothing to fancy, but lemme know what you want done and I'll do the best to try and make you happy...I'm not perfect, but
I can try to be eh ? --------------------- 7. Character choices ---------------------- The SAW wants you to be creative when you decide your character for the federation....I love Gothic, I love cowboys, the Rich cocky guy we love to hate, Thugs, Hitmen....Whatever, but remember we want to keep it realistic, and we want to cut back on out rageous gimmicks...Maybe one or Two, but I want the fed to bring back an old feeling...Which puts excitement in the in rings, announcing and action instead of on the piss your guy left in all of the SAW locker rooms =)....
----------------------------------------- 8. SAW Announe Team ----------------------------------------- Fever Announcers Fred Hart - Fred has had a tough time most of his annoucing life..He has been fired, Re hired, Put on the spot and beat up, but he thinks this is finally a place for him to settle down as he has a wife and kids now...He lives in the
Arkansas area, So he felt this was the perfect oppurtunity for himself, and hopefully it is..Fred calls the action as he see's it...No forshadowing or
Covering up anything with this man...He is without a doubt a straight shooter Samuel Smith - One of the first black men to announce in the south..Samuel is not a gangster or anything like that for the stereotypical people out there..He grew up in a semi hard life, but ended up graduating from Stanford with honors...He is a very educated man, with some very educated and extreme points on life, wrestling and of course religion....
War Stories Announcers Fred Hart - Fred will be doing War Stories along with Fever...See above for background information
Joey Howard - Joey is a young man, At this age you would think he would be wrestling instead of announcing, and at his size...You would definitely think so as he is a rather large man, But he had knee problems which stopped his dream, So now he is trying to carry out his second dream...Of being a pro wrestling announcer...Joey is young and unexperenced, but with Fred helping him along the way we hope he comes along alright....I am sure there will be downfalls with this young man's energy and attitude, but hopefully more bright spots than dull one's.
Supercard Announcers Fred Hart - He is a busy man.....What can I say about him ? He is the man who has the mic in his hand for the most part in the SAW..
Samuel Smith - Once again these two guys work together to hopefully bring you a quality show
Frankie Tha Pimp - The supercards will be a three man booth, as what is a Mike Johnson fed without Frankie Tha Pimp..Long time NMW AND KAWF announcer Frankie Tha Pimp has been with Mike Johnson since there youth....Don't let the name fool you, He knows this sport like the back of his hand, but Frankie is one of the most controversial announcers in the history of the sport and will only be working supercards as Mike couldn't work out a financial deal to get him on tv anymore than that...We hope Frankie does his homework in his off time....Or else the Supercard idea of three in the box might be a short used idea.
Interviewers Johnny Jackson - Once again another Black worker for the SAW, as we try to show you the south isn't as rascist as it seems to the outside world....Johnny will ask the questions most won't, and will not be scared to do so as he is an ex bouncer / Martial Artist trainer....None of the talent will push this man around,and hopefully he can do some pushing and shoving to get us the info we all want...
Samantha Owens - This Beautiful Blond bombshell was told she couldn't wrestle anywhere in the south which had standards, cause of an incident of her posing nude in her younger days...So instead of wrestling in some trashy wrestling federation, Or going into hardcore wrestling, this lovely 21 year old lady will not wrestle in the SAW, But will be given a chance to show what she is made of as a mic will be in her hand here in the SAW....
Ring Announcer Stacey Normand - One of the few red hands you will ever see with a good tan, Will keep you posted with the height's, Weights, Locations and so on of your favorite SAW wrestlers as she will be the one bringing them to the ring night after night here in the SAW...
Head of Security Jay Trisler - One of Mike Johnson's old buddy's, Has signed on to try and keep things under tradtional rules and regulations..With the help of Mike Pace,
Hopefully he can...He is an older man, with a lil bit of a beer gut, but he is a former Army Ranger who is willing to do whatever is nessecary if put in a situation...Hopefully the situations will not come up, but if they do so...We are confident Jay can stop them for us...
