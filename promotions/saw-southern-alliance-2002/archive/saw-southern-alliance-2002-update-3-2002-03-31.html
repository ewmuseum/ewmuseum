SOUTHERN ALLIANCE WRESTLING "Time to go down south" Update # 3 March 31st, 2002 Table of Contents 1 - Out of Character Notes 2 - News and Rumors 3 - The Roster 4 - Records 5 - Rankings 6 - Deadline and Dates 7 - Booked Shows 8 - The Ending *************** 1 - OOC Notes *************** A little late this week, Unlike last when we were a little early,but I think all in all we had another good week. Considering I moved, and It was a holiday weekend.. This next week should be a good one as well, Since story's and all our starting to take place. As well as a tag team scene and soon a cruserweight scene. This means though, That I am gonna have to book some matches for War Stories, and start making them a lil more competitive. So instead of Joe Blow versus Reaper...It's gonna be TWO SAW stars like Reaper vs Rob Fabulous to help keep everybody involved. There will be some matches in which you might get a cheap win, but usually you will be facing another SAW person. Which means you have to start flashing for War Stories now.I might do random house shows every now and then as well...Just trying to add another dimension to the SAW. Also, Don't get unfocused or let down if you lose a match. Losses will happen, Just keep up the character development, That is the main key anyway to having fun and producing a quality show. Thanks, Josh *************** 2 - News and Rumors *************** 1 - Antoine Bradley is still in jail just outside of New Orleans for Assault and Battery of Mike Pace. 2 - Frankie Cash has signed an offical contract with the SAW. 3 - Cruserweight and Tag titles are being made currently for the SAW at a gold shop in Bastrop, Louisiana. They should be made by next week's Fever. 4 - Reaper suffered a minor concussion from the cheap shot by C K Walker with the brass knuckles on Fever 5 - April 28TH, 2002 The SAW will have it's first supercard "Down on the Bayou" which will be from the Century Tell Center in Shreveport, Louisiana. Most people don't think the small time SAW can sell out this arena. 6 - Mike Johnson and ADB are having a meeting later in the week to hopefully work out an appearance shortly for the superstar on SAW tv. 7 - The SAW said in a recent press conference they were very pleased with the work of Rud Groz, Chris Payne, Lenny and Bungle last week on Fever. Look for Some sorta merchandise to be made for these guys in the near future. 8 - The SAW is still in talks with an all japan federation in possibly having some inter fed matches on some of the Japanesse federations cards to help promote both feds and give good action to the wrestling fans...Rumored names to be competing over there are Tommy Flames, Vern Walters, Simon Starr, Bungle, Tommy Taylor and leading the possible group would be Chris Page...The SAW wrestlers would be working for a real low price if the deal does go down. *************** 3 -
