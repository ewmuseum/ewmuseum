Calling All Tag Teams

24 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Sup Gentleman of the RSPWF ? I said I would keep you posted on the SAW, and I beleive I did that by sending out like 1,499 mails to you just a second ago...= )
But putting that behind us, Hopefully some of you enjoyed the show, and like what you saw from the SAW.
Feel free to email me ya thoughts, I would love to hear it, as I know I am still rusty...Trust me, I will get the match writing vibe back soon enough, At least before the supercard. I felt Fever was pretty good, but could of been better, but then again I will probally always feel like that being the kinda person I am
If you want on the list to view SAW and everything which goes down email me, If you have a good angle in mind and a singles wrestler..EMAIL ME..We will se whats up, but what I really sent this email to the public for was to call to the tag teams...I LOVE TAG TEAM WRESTLING, AND WANT A TAG TEAM DIVISION, BUT I
NEED...TAG TEAMS..Go figure right...
So, Please, any interested tag teams write me back...I would love to have some of yall out there..MACH II, BOYS NEXT DOOR, OUTER CHOICE, SEXUAL ENERGY, FIRE
AND ICE, SEX AND VIOLENCE, THE FAT OUTLAWS, DOMINATION, UNTOUCHABLES, AND THE
LIST GOES ON....Feel free to give me a chance, for without yall..THE SAW would be missing something very important to me..A solid tag team division =(
Much Respect, Josh AKA - Steve Greedy
