Sunday Night War Stories, 7 April 2002 (part 2 of 2)

7 April 2002

[ As the scene fades back in we see C K Walker and Rob Fabulous standing together in C K's now messed up locker room. C K has a bandage on the top of his head, Nursing the wound he suffered earlier.]
Rob Fabulous : " Nice lil Decorations you got going here..Show's real flare." C K Walker : " Shut man..Just shut up. I just called you in here to warn you
Page is coming for you here tonight."
Rob Fabulous : " Chris Page is coming for me ? Doesn't he know I am Tough Stuff
<< Thumbs to chest >> Ro..."
C K Walker : " Rob Fabulous....Ya Ya.....Look I was just warning you man." Rob Fabulous : " Thanks, but you didn't have to....He can't get me, He can't even come close to me."
C K Walker : " Sure Rob << Rolls eyes >> Whatever you say.." [ Fabulous nods and walks out of the room.] C K Walker : " Page is gonna get him good << Shakes his head >> I just hope he doesn't hurt him to bad, I want to beat Rob next weekend while he is in good shape. So nobody can say anything when I tell the world I am the only SUPER
DUPER MEGA SUPERSTAR in the SAW.."
[ Walker then sadly begins to clean up his locker room, as we fade out.] [ "God Bless Texas" Begins to play over the PA system.] Fred Hart : " Here comes the Regional Champion...He defeated Bungle last night in one helluv'a match."
Joey Howard : " He's gonna be a good one." [ From the back wearing dress clothes comes Tommy Flames with the Regional
Title over his shoulder. Flames slaps some fans hands on his way down to the ring, and receives a huge face pop. He takes the steps into the ring, and climbs each turnbuckle playing to the crowd raising his hands.]
Fred Hart : " They love Tommy Flames here in West Monroe, Louisiana don't they
Joey.." Joey Howard : " Yes they do Fred...." [ Flames then walks to the center of the ring, and pulls a mic out from his leather jacket. With a beautiful smile on his face, Flames begins to speak.]
Tommy Flames : " Cut my music please guys.." [ "God Bless Texas" fades out, and the fans continue to pop in enjoyment of
Flames being here..]
Tommy Flames : " Thank ya much....Now how we doing here tonight West Monroe,
Louisiana ? " [ Cheap Face Pop.] Tommy Flames : " Good to hear..The Flames is doing pretty good today as well. The path to the championship had some bumps and still has a few bumps on the outskirts currently, but none the less I am still the Southern Regional
Champion." [ Tommy holds the belt up high into the air as the crowd pops, Tommy then sets the back down on his shoulder and continues.]
Tommy Flames : " My Good buddy Vern caught me with a chair last night, I knew it wasn't on purpose, and so did Vern...For a few moments there we were upset with each other of course, but in the end we shook hands, and did what Texans do..Went and had a beer. Eddie James is the reason for all the little bumps when it comes to my Southern Regional Title Reign. Hopefully at Down on the
Bayou the bump will be permantly removed."
" That's a lil ways away though.So let's focus on tonight..We have seen why
Chris Page is a madman, We have seen Frankie Cash almost cry, We saw the
Business Men handle business, and we have seen former wrestlers, now manager's do battle. What else do we want to see tonight guys ? "
[ Tommy pauses ] Tommy Flames : " How bout Tommy Flames defending his title here tonight ? " [ Crowd Pops ] Tommy Flames : " Sounds good to me to. I want to be a fighting champion, and what better way than to fight the night after winning the title ? "
[ From the back comes Mike Pace..He enters the ring, and shakes hands with the champion.] Mike Pace : " Let me be the first to say congratulations.." [ Both men nod and smile.] Mike Pace : " I know you want to fight here tonight, but you might want to wait a bit. I know you thought you wouldn't be booked next week, but I am gonna book you against one of our best young talents Antoine Bradley in a non title match.."
Tommy Flames : " No problem, but why Antoine...? " Mike Pace : " I feel he deserves the match, and a chance to prove himself.." Tommy Flames : " Okay I see where your coming from Boss. Antoine is here tonight right ? " Mike Pace : " Yes." Tommy Flames : " Then how bout Tommy Flames vs Antoine Bradley here tonight.." [ Crowd Pop ] Mike Pace : " No, Next week on Fever Tommy.We have a schedule to keep, You need to rest, and Antoine needs to re focus after the tragedy which happened to him injustly here in the SAW. So please head to the back, and get ready for the match next week..."
[ Tommy shakes his head, but heads to the back listening to his boss faithfully..Pace follows, and the crowd let's off a mixed reaction.]
Fred Hart : " I thought we had a good one on our hands there, but Pace managed to keep it from happening."
Joey Howard : " We do have a schedule Fred.." Fred Hart : " I know...But still..Hold on, I am getting something in my headset, Let's go to the back where there is a possible Chris Page attack.."
[ We see Rob Fabulous in the break room sipping on some bottle Water.] Voice : " Hey Rob." [ Rob turns around sharply and balls his fist up dropping his water. When he turns around he see's it is Vice President Shawn Cross..Rob sighs, and lowers his hand.]
Rob Fabulous : " Don't sneak up on me like that.." Shawn Cross : " Sorry, I didn't know I did sneak up on you.." Rob Fabulous : " Well you did, What do you want ? " Shawn Cross : " I was just gonna say you can keep the money, but the deal with
Page is off. He is to crazy to mess with like this. I picked a bad target to take advantage of.."
Rob Fabulous : " Long as I can keep the money thats fine..." Shawn Cross : " It's all yours, and maybe in the future we can do business again." [ Shawn walks off, and Rob turns back around.He picks up his bottle water and once again takes a sip. A hand is placed on his shoulder..]
Rob Fabulous : " What now Shawn.." [ Rob turns around and is now eye to eye with Chris Page. Fabulous back peddles, and Page catches him with a hard right hand. Page then pushes Fabulous over one of the tables in the break room, and Fabulous goes to the ground. Page get's on top of him quickly and pounds Fabulous with several intense right hands.]
Fred Hart : " Here we go again...." [ Page picks Fabulous up, and slams him into the water bottles, which all fall on top of Fabulous. Page then picks Fabulous up and takes him outside the break room.Page begins to Yell at him as he guides him down the hall way by his hair.]
Chris Page : " I am gonna show you how much I hate you...I am gonna show you how much I despise you."
[ Page suddenly stops as we are infront of one of the Glass Window's used on the entrance set up at Fever. Page smiles and tosses Fabulous into the glass plate. Fabulous flys threw it, and Page begins to laugh.]
Chris Page : " I told you I needed the storm to exist....And now you are all seeing _THE STORM_....Three down, One more to get.."
[ Fade back to the announcer's table.] Fred Hart : " Somebody better check on Rob Fabulous." Joey Howard : " Ya...Going threw a glass window can't feel good." Fred Hart : " Page said he has one more to get, and I am assuming that is
Reaper." Joey Howard : " I wander how Cash is doing ? He has been tied up somewhere in the building bleeding pretty much the whole show."
Fred Hart : " Hopefully we will see soon as Cash is supposed to fight in our main event tonight."
Joey Howard : " If Page let's him..." Fred Hart : " I heard them two are gonna have a match at Down on the Bayou, I don't know if I reccomend that. Something about that worry's me."
Joey Howard : " It would be a sight to see, thats for sure..." Fred Hart : " An epic battle like that would draw a crowd." Joey Howard : " Look for that to be signed, but also look for more tag teams and lightweights to be signed to the SAW."
Fred Hart : " Both those title's will be on the line at the supercard, and they get to start jocking for position next week..In the SAW meeting we had, we discussed both titles...Big plans were made and will be carried out shortly."
Joey Howard : " I really like what I heard from Devin Downs last night, and I like what I hear about the signing of Estrago and Prankster as well when it comes to the lightweights...And as far as the tag teams..I would put my money on the New Breed, and the Bounty Hunters against ALMOST any tag team I have ever seen."
[ The scene cuts to the back where Mike Johnson is sitting down on a Sofa,
Talking on his cell phone.]
Mike Johnson : " Yes I have signed two new teams...They will be debuting soon. One next week, and One at the supercard...I promise to sell out your arena. The
Century tell center will be PACKED..Trust me."
[ He begins to listen, to whomever is on the other end of the phone.] Mike Johnson : " Understood....Just trust me.." [Hangs up the cell phone and gets up off of the sofa exiting the room.] Fred Hart : " Interesting..." Joey Howard : " what you expect ? This is the SAW..." Fred Hart : " So we got two new teams, and Mike has made a promise to sell out the huge Century Tell Center for the supercard."
Joey Howard : " Huge for us...Not the big feds..The Century Tell Center isn't
THAT big...."
Fred Hart : " True...But still." Joey Howard : " I know what your saying Fred." Fred Hart : " It's time to see the monster Simon Starr taking on Johnny Danger...Classic battle of Goliath vs David.." ************************************************************************** *********************** Third Bout Ten Minute Time Limit Simon Starr vs Johnny Danger Referee - Wayne West ************************************************************************** *********************** The biggest man in the SAW Simon Starr who hurt some security members last week on Fever made his way down to the ring, And One of the smallest men in the federation in Johnny Danger with his manager Damian Rock also made there way down to the ring. Both with boo's from the crowd.
Danger tried to play to the crowd, But Starr attacked him quickly, and Attacked
Rock sending him out of the ring and to the floor. Starr then began to kick
Danger around the ring, and stalked him as Danger continued to try and get away from the big man. Finally Danger got enough space from the big man to dodge one of his kick attempts, and leg sweep him down to the mat. Danger then wrapped his leg's around the leg of Starr, and did a modifeid figure four leg lock on
Starr applying a nice amount of pressure.
Starr ethier not feeling the pain, or blocking it out set up and reached out to
Danger, but couldn't quite reach him. Starr then uses his hands to pick himself up, and lunges at Danger out powering the hold of Danger and landing on top of him in a mounting position....Starr then releases a fury of combo's on the head of Danger, Until Ref Wayne West talks him out of the use of the fist. Starr gets up and chases West around the ring once, but just laughs as the ref runs from him. Starr then re focuses on Danger.
Rock on the outside hits the mat a couple times to try and encourage Danger, but it doesn't help much. Starr continues his attack by landing a hanging vertical suplex,and follows it up with a nicely done Butterfly powerbomb...Starr goes for a cover, but Rock jumps up onto the apron to distract the ref. An Angry Starr walks over and throws a punch at Rock, who ducks back down to the floor. Starr turns around and is caught by a superkick which sends him over the top rope, but Starr landed on his feet.
Starr grabs Rock who is in the spot right beside him, and Choke slams him on the floor...Rock screams in pain as his back bounces off of that hard cement...On the top of the ramp way you see TVR and Dark Tower standing there smiling. TVR has a microphone in hand..
Todd Van Rooyen : " Hey Danger, Rock...I said Hey Rock...Oh nevermind..Don't look like yall are doing to good out there. What seem's to be the problem ? "
Danger walks over to the edge of the ring and begins to shout at Todd...From behind Starr rolls into the ring with a chair and hits Danger in the back of the head, pushing him up against the ropes.The ref calls for the bell, but
Starr keeps hitting Danger in the back, Until finally the pressure of the shots send him over the top rope and to the mat. Starr then tosses the chair down and rolls his eyes into the back of his head.
Todd Van Rooyen : " Ouch.....Seriously guys, You might want to get that checked out by the SAW medical team.."
Starr exits the ring and heads up the ramp with his eyes locked on TVR and Dark
Tower. Todd Van Rooyen : " Talk to you guys later, See ya..." TVR and Tower quickly exit....Starr then also exits our viewing sight. Slowly some medical team members, none being Jeremy Rivers make there way down and check on Danger and Rock..Both men get up, but need help getting to the back.
Winner - Johnny Danger Time - Six Minutes and Thirty Three seconds Finisher - DQ win ************************************************************************** *********************** Fred Hart : " Simon Starr continues to be a monster, but also continues to get himself dq'd. I guess he doesn't care about winning."
Joey Howard : " It does seem that way....If Simon would get his mind straight,
He could be a HUGE force in the SAW."
Fred Hart : " Yes he could..We will have to see how that develops in the SAW future..Oh god....To the back again we go....A CHRIS PAGE SIGHTING..."
[ We fade to the parking lot area, but the back of the parking lot area. Where no car's, people or anything is. We see Reaper sitting in a folding chair with his arms crossed.]
Voice : " I figure'd you would be here.Just like you were at the house show earlier in the week..What you did to me last night was messed up."
[ Reaper gets up out of his chair and turns around calmly. We now see Reaper and Chris Page going eye to eye.]
Chris Page : " You turned on me...It could of been Me and You going One on One for the title next week, Instead it's Rob Fabulous and C K Walker..For that,
You WILL pay."
Reaper : " Now is not the time Page..I am not saying I am better than you by any means, but now is not the time to attack me. For your own sake you better wait, This is the darkness, This is my element. You will be hurt badly if you come at me here, Now."
Chris Page : " Looks like we might have to just find out eh Big man.." Reaper : " Frankie Cash is about to wrestle, You better get back to him if you want to keep your promise of lefting him wrestle. There will be another time and a better place for us to do battle Chris..I promise, I am not Fabulous, Nor
Walker..I have a brain, Here......YOU WOULD LOSE TO ME.....Somewhere else, You have a chance at least."
Chris Page : " Another time then Reaper....I do need to get back to Frankie." [ The two nod and Page walks off..Smiling Reaper turns back around and sits in his chair as the guardian of the parking lot.]
Fred Hart : " No attack there....Page decided against it." Joey Howard : " Smart move by Page..A parking lot would not be a good place to fight the Reaper."
Fred Hart : " I think Page has got enough payback for the time being..Just ask the knot on Walker's head, and Fabulous wounded body."
Joey Howard : " I don't think the tied up Cash would disagree with the fact
Page has seeked and got REVENGE here tonight."
[ In the back we see Chris Page running...] Fred Hart : " Look's like he is going to find Frankie..." [ Page takes a couple sharp lefts, then a right and enters a room. We see Cash still in the chair, with the candles lighting the room. Cash is still bloody and seem's to be asleep..Page walks in and slaps Cash who instantly wakes up.]
Chris Page : " There About ready for you to fight Cash....I am gonna clean you up, and your gonna go out there and win another match."
[ Page uses a wash rag to clean the face of Cash a bit, then dumps a small bowl of water all over him..Cash kicks in the direction of Page, who just laughs.
Page then unties Frankie and pushes him out of the chair to the ground.] Chris Page : " Well go big boy..Make somebody ' Money '..." [ Cash scrambles to his feet and exits the room quickly. We fade with the laughter of Chris Page being heard.]
Fred Hart : " Page has lost it, but I got to admit payback is truly a Bi...." Joey Howard : " Watch yourself Fred..." Fred Hart : " Well payback is cold and harsh then. Page is really enjoying himself here tonight, and really so am I for once...."
Joey Howard : " Ya tonight has been interesting and fun Fred...I know you haven't enjoyed alot of the shows cause of the evil actions and so on, but tonight has went pretty smooth."
Fred Hart : " So far, So good.....Let's get to the ring to see if Cash can shake out the cob webs, and take it to Patrick Robbinfield."
************************************************************************** *********************** Main Event Twenty Minute Time Limit Frankie Cash Vs Patrick Robbinfield Referee - John Mercer ************************************************************************** *********************** Robbinfield came to the ring first, once again without his good friend Arthur
Dexter Bradley who has yet to debut in the SAW...Cash then followed with a chorus of laughs and boo's being heard as he entered.Cash blocked them out of his mind, and still came out cockily..He slid under the bottom rope, and Dared
Patrick to step towards him, but of course Robbinfield didn't.
The bell sounded, And the two locked up..For a rare moment Cash out power'd somebody and pushed Robbinfield back into one of the corners.Cash acted as if he was gonna do a clean break, but then caught Robbinfield with a back elbow to the head. Cash then lands a few chops to the chest, and whips Robbinfield out of the corner hard and into the other corner.He hits the corner, and flips up over the turnbuckle cause of the momentum and lands on his feet outside the ring [Allah Flair]...Robbinfield tries to walk down the apron, but is caught with a drop kick to the head sending him to the floor..Cash pops back up to his feet smiling, and bow's to the crowd.
Robbinfield slowly rolls back into the ring, and Cash continues the attack.Cash executes a beautiful side slam on Robbinfield, then hops up to his feet and quickly lands a beautiful leg drop across the throat of Robbinfield.
Fred Hart : " I wander where ADB is..You would think he would care about this being done to his manager."
Joey Howard : " Well we saw Robbinfield in a neck brace last week cause of what
Fabulous did to him.I think ADB would of showed then, if he actually cared..."
Fred Hart : " True....Taylor Threat helped Robbinfield once, but I don't think he cares about Robbinfield ethier...Robbinfield kinda cost Threat his match last night, so don't expect Threat to save him here tonight."
Cash executes a beautiful standing drop kick on the small Patrick Robbinfield.Then goes for a cover. [ONE] [TWO] [Cash lifts him up] The ref warns Cash, but Cash just smiles...He picks Robbinfield up and lands a few small cocky like jabs to the face. Then position's his hands as if he was looking threw a window frame pausing the action and playing to the crowd.He then lands another Dropkick to the head of Robbinfield. Cash smiling for the first time all night, Then executes a beautiful neckbreaker on Robbinfield setting up another cover.
[ONE] [TWO] [Once again Cash lifts him up] The ref really barks at Cash this time....but Cash acts as if he wasn't there and lands a beautiful DDT...Cash then drags Robbinfield towards the center of the ring, and goes to the top rope..He Jumps off and flys out all the way across the ring landing the Money Maker....One of the most vicious frog splashes in the sport, espically since he uses his knee's when he comes down instead of the body...Cash goes for another cover.
[ONE] [TWO] [THREE] Cash hops up to his feet after his victory, but is instantly speared by Chris
Page who came from nowhere..Page then picks Cash up and lands the Red, White and Blue....Page continues the attack as he kicks Cash in the head several times after landing his finisher...Page then picks Cash up again and does the
Red, White and Blue again to him...
From the back both bandaged walks Rob Fabulous and C K Walker....They stand side by side talking trash to Page in the ring.
Chris Page : " GET IN TH RING...C'MON GET IN THE RING YOU BASTARDS..." C K jumps up onto the apron, but is knocked off by a charging Page with a shoulder block. Fabulous catches Walker so he doesn't fall....Both men continue to talk trash, Until the lights flicker in the arena.Both Walker and Fabulous smile.
The lights stop flickering and from the back walks Reaper..He slowly walks down, While Fabulous and Walker seem happy..Reaper reaches the steps and looks into the ring where Page is. Walker comes over and pats Reaper on the back,
While Fabulous comes over and says to Reaper "Get him"...Reaper starts up the steps, but then stops..He grabs Both Fabulous and Walker by the throat on each side of him, then choke slams them down with only one hand.
Reaper : " Just cause I don't like Him, Doesn't Mean I like you..." Reaper looks to the ring where Page seems eager to fight.Reaper smirks, and walks to the back. Page remains in the ring, and let's out some more frustration on Cash with some kicks to the rib cages..He then goes to the top and lands a Money Maker of his own....Not quite as pretty, but definitely effective. Page then rolls out of the ring, and we fade back to the announcers.
Winner - Frankie Cash Time - Five Minutes and Twenty Three seconds Finisher - Moneymaker ************************************************************************** *********************** Fred Hart : " Tonight War Stories was all about Chris Page...He got a little revenge for last night and all the night's before..."
Joey Howard : " Oh boy did he..." Fred Hart : " We are all out of time, but it will be interesting to see what happens next week when all these guys get together one more time.."
