SOUTHERN ALLIANCE WRESTLING "Time to go down south" Update # 1 March 11th, 2002 Table of Contents 1 - Out of Character Notes 2 - News and Rumors 3 - The Roster 4 - Records 5 - Rankings 6 - Deadline and Dates 7 - The first ever LINEUP for Fever 8 - The Ending *************** 1 - OOC Notes *************** First off, I would like to say thank you to you all...I have ran many different styles of federations in my E dub career, but this is the first time I have ever gave one quite like this ago...I am sure we will have up's and downs along the way as there is some aol style role players here, and I myself still lean towards the AOL style of play...But just like a good coach at halftime, I am sure we will make modifications and come out with a winning record / effort in the end with your help...Thank you all for giving me a chance to put out an enjoyable show.
I would also like to say thanks to people such as Ceaser Risley, Matthew Reason, James Morgan, Maverick, Lestat, Jon Talon, and The Boys Next Door for being on the list, Even though it is just to view...I thank you for your eyes and ears here in the SAW..I will ask for your opinions, value them and view them even though your not on the active roster [ Least not yet ].....Once again
I say thanks... Enough of that though right ?, Time for business to get going...I am currently role playing in two federations, and soon to be three...I enjoy all three of them, and will continue to participate in them at a high level, but I hope all of you realize SAW will be my priority when it comes to E wrestling...I will strive to put out enjoyable shows, On time cards and get you all involved...If any of you see me slipping, Slap me in the face, and if any of you think im being unfair...Speak up right away and if any of you want to help me in recruiting...Feel free to do so...
On a final note here...Make sure you read the whole thing, As lineups are posted, rosters and a few dates and deadlines which could be very important to yall..
Thanks, Josh *************** 2 - News and Rumors *************** 1 - The SAW'S First show will be Saturday Night Fever on March 23rd live from The Monroe Civic Center in Monroe, Louisiana..Followed by a special War Stories on The 24th from the newly Built SAW arena in West Monroe, Louisiana...
2 - A former SCW title holder is in talks with the SAW currently and could very well join the federation by the time the first show makes it's way to the arena.
3 - A big tag team is currently looking over the contract's SAW sent them, and is possibly close to signing them..Commish of the SAW Mike Pace is huge into tag team wrestling and is trying his hardest to get them to sign.
4 - Rumor is Mike Johnson has already came up with a way to decide who is gonna be the first ever SAW Champion...The offical idea has not yet been released to the public, but alot of speculation is going around the Southern wrestling scene.
5 - For the first ever Supercard of the SAW Mike Johnson is trying to get ahold of the Century Tell arena in Bossier City, Louisiana which holds just over
