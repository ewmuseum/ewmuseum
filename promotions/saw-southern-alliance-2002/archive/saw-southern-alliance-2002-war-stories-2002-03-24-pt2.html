Sunday Night War Stories, 24 March 2002 (part 2 of 2)

24 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
<< We fade in from commercial, But instead of being back at the Announcers booth to announce the main event's for next weeks Fever, We are in the backstage area where we see The lovely Samantha Owens standing by Vice
President of the SAW Shawn Cross. >> [Samantha Owens] : " On Fever you were seen giving Rob Fabulous a check, and it was before he even wrestled so it wasn't for his match that night..Later that night he attacked Chris Page, did you set that up Mr Cross.."
[Shawn Cross] : " Of course not Samantha...I am a Southern Gentleman, and Mike
Johnson wants his office to stay out of the spotlight, drama and cameras..I was just simply paying Rob for cutting my grass...Which he did this morning might I add."
[Samantha Owens] : " You had to write a check for that ? " [Shawn Cross] : " I didn't have a chance to go to the ATM, What can I say..." [Samantha Owens] : " So you promise before this SAW crowd that you didn't pay
Rob Fabulous to Attack Chris Page."
[Shawn Cross] : " I << Pauses >> Promise....Now if you will execuse me Samantha, I got to have a meeting with Mike Pace. I am trying to help him find out who attacked him with that lead pipe.."
<< The scene fades back to the announcer's booth, Where Fred picks the show back up.>> [Fred Hart] : " I am not sure about that Shawn Cross, I think Mike Johnson and
Mike Pace both mean well, but Something just doesn't cut it when it comes to Mr
Cross..
[Joey Howard] : " I got the same bad feeling about him that you do Fred, But he is one of our bosses so we might want to keep that to ourselves."
[Fred Hart] : " Your learning quick Joey....Before we had to take a commercial break we had announced next week's fever card, except for the two main events..."
[Joey Howard] : " Which are good ones..." [Fred Hart] : " So without leaving yall in the dark any longer..We have two main events...One puts Reaper up against C K Walker in the first ever NO DQ match in the SAW....Mike Johnson signed this as a way of trying to get back at
C K for ruining the title match last week..."
[Joey Howard] : " And the other one put's Chris Page in the ring with Rob
Fabulous..A chance for Page to get some revenge for Fabulous attacking him in the back stage area last week and busting him open in the main event.."
[Fred Hart] : " Rumor is Mike Johnson is gonna come out at some point during the night and speak on the Title situation, SAW's first show and more...It will be interesting if Fabulous, Page, Walker and Reaper are all sorta fighting for chances at the Southern Title.."
[Joey Howard] : " Which very well could be true, as there all top notch talents...Also on War Stories next week we will get to see, TVR, Johnny Danger,
Eddie James and our main event will be C K Walker versus Chris Page...So just one night after Walker going toe to toe with Reaper in a hardcore match...Chris
Page will get a chance for some revenge on Walker as well here on War
Stories...Next week should be exciting Fred.." [Fred Hart] : " Yes it should be Joey, but let's worry about this weeks action...Southern Indy legend Don Lowry Versus big Vern Walters..
************************************************************************** ************ Second Bout Ten Minute Time Limit Vern Walters Versus Don Lowry Referee - Aaron Kent ************************************************************************** ************ When being introduced both men got good pops from the crowd since there both good ol southern boys who love to drink beer, hunt, fish and fight...
Once the bell rang though, Lowry was no longer a good ol boy...He ran right at
Vern Walters landing a knee to the midsection knocking the breathe out of Vern who defeated Simon Starr last night, but not without getting some bruised ribs..
Lowry then stayed on the attack as he landed various moves which put pressure on or hurt the ribs...Lowry went for a pin after landing a tilt o whirl slam, but only got a two count...Lowry tried to keep Vern on the ground with a front headlock, but Vern stomped his feet getting the crowd into the match..The small arena meant the crowd could be heard very well...Lowry's eyes got big as Vern begin to move around trying to get to his feet, Lowry threw some shots to the hurt ribs, but they didn't seem to help...Vern powered back to his feet, and picked Lowry up who held onto the front headlock....Vern then grabbed Lowry's side and spinebustered him down to the mat hard, causing the crowd to pop...Vern shook off the effect of the headlock and the rib shots...Then layed the boots to Don Lowry.
Lowry tried to fight back to his feet several times, but Vern kept knocking him down with his incredible power...Finally Lowry got back to his feet, but
Walters didn't let him stay long...Vern executed a huge vertical suplex in which he held Lowry in the air for a good ten seconds..He went for a cover, but barely got a two count...Vern smiling picked Lowry back up, and held his head down with his left hand, while landing vicious ax handle type blows with his right to the back area of Don Lowry...He did this three times, all three times sending Lowry to a knee with his power, but as Vern tried a fourth, Lowry caught the hand coming down with his two hands, and popped up landing a back elbow to the lip of Walters which busted him open..
Lowry followed with a clothesline which knocked Walters down....Lowry then dropped to his knee's and began to choke Walters.To get a rest himself and to make it hard on Walters to breathe...Referee Aaron Kent quickly made him stop though..Lowry argued with the ref briefly, but then went back to the attack on
Walters landing rights and lefts to the top and side of the head..
Just when you thought Lowry was gonna finish Vern off....Vern reverses a whip into the ropes, Lowry ducks Vern's big boot attempt and hits the other side, but as he comes off Vern Walters catches him with a HUGE running clothesline which turns him inside out...The crowd pops and Walters covers for the three count...
Vern exits the ring with his hands held high...Moments later Referee Aaron Kent and Don Lowry leave the ring with Kent somewhat helping Lowry to the back..
Winner : Vern Walters Time : Eight Minutes and Twenty seconds Finish : Walters landed the Texas clothesline to pin Don Lowry ************************************************************************** ************ [Joey Howard] : " That was a pretty good match there Fred..I would have to say that was an upset..Vern Walters looks like he might be something special."
[Fred Hart] : " Hopefully so Joey, as he has signed a long term deal with the
SAW..We have two more matches on tonight's show, but let's go back once again and look at this title situation real quick before we continue.."
[Joey Howard] : " I personally think Mike Johnson should reward Reaper the belt, who did land his DEATHSPIKE on Robbinfield at the same time in which
Walker came out with the damn chair...I also think Johnson should fine ADB for not showing up, and forcing us to watch Robbinfield try to wrestle.."
[Fred Hart] : " I don't like seeing anybody being given a belt Joey..Seen it to much in my time in pro wrestling, and I don't think Johnson is the kind to do that.Which is part of the reason I work for him..Johnson is using a small budget for the Southern area only federation we are in, but Don't be mistaken
Johnson is rich...Don't be suprised if we see a cage, scaffold, ladder or something unique come into play when it comes to finding us a champion..He is a fan of good ol fashion wrestling, but he is also a fan of giving the fans an occasional thrill by putting the wrestlers outside there normal limits...Johnson is a unique and powerful man Joey, It will be very interesting to see who gets the title.."
[Joey Howard] : " Johnson isn't the only powerful man in SAW...Mike Pace is also powerful as is Shawn Cross....Cross seems to be a lil bit dirty, Pace got attacked by two men with a lead pipe...I think Johnson might want to increase security if he starts playing with these main eventers and there title hopes...."
[Fred Hart] : " Title shots our a very dangerous thing to mess around with...I am sure Johnson will try to treat everybody fairly, even that equal oppurtunity pisser offer C K Walker..."
[Joey Howard] : " He might want to for his own safety, but I am sure Walker will be upset about this hardcore match with the mammoth Reaper, then coming back the next night fighting another guy he hit with a chair in Chris Page...So he might already be on C K Walker's bad side..."
[Fred Hart] : " Speaking of bad sides...Our next match put's one of the baddest men in the SAW in a match against a mexican cruserweight who has made his way to Monroe and West Monroe to work indy matches...El Tigre' is the mexican and
Eddie James is the bad man I am talking about...In a great match last night
James lost to Flames, but he did get some revenge in after the match...James was intense earlier backstage when I saw him, and for some reason I want to say
El Tigre' is in a load of trouble here tonight.."
************************************************************************** ************ Third Bout Ten Minute Time Limit Eddie James Versus El Tigre' Referee - Wayne West ************************************************************************** ************ El Tigre' got announced and didn't get much of a reaction, but the same can't be said of Eddie James...When Eddie got announced most the crowd started to
Boo, and the rest of the crowd begin to chant "Tommy Flames" which is the man who beat him last night on Fever...Eddie covered his ears and cussed at the crowd, but they didn't shut up of course...When the bell rang though, Eddie seemingly blocked them out and locked in on the Mexican Cruserweight El Tigre'
Eddie tried to lock up with El Tigre', but he was to quick...Tigre' got behind
Eddie and put him in a wrist lock...Eddie acted as if he was going to elbow
Tigre' to get out of the move, and the ref warned him...Eddie then put his elbow back down and screamed out in pain as Tigre' squeezed on the wrist hard...Finally Eddie Reversed the wrist lock and Put Tigre' in one of his own...Tigre almost automatically though, Rolled frontwards to get away from the arm bar, and then caught Eddie coming back at him with an arm drag...Eddie jumps back up to his knee's staring at Tigre' angrily, While EL Tigre' stands in the middle of the ring with his hands raised high playing to the crowd.
An angry James gets back up, and trys to punch El Tigre' with his right hand, but Tigre blocks it then strikes back with an open palm strike, then another, followed by another which pushed James into the ropes...Tigre' whipped James across the ring, and tried for a back body drop, but put his head down to early against the aggresive Eddie James who caught him with a big punting type kick to the head which sent Tigre' upright, Followed by a hard right hand to the face putting Tigre' on his back..James then stomped away harshly at Tigre' over and over until the ref pushed James back....James acted as if he was gonna hit the ref, but didn't
Minutes Later James still in control of the match, Now has Tigre' in a position ready to be piledrived...Tigre' back body drops James over though, and both men are down..The ref begins his ten count, but only reaches six as both men are up...They trade licks, until Tigre' takes the advantage with his open palm strikes...Tigre then lands a standing drop kick right on the chin and goes for a cover...He gets a long two count, and the crowd AWWWW's as they thought James was beat...Tigre' then gets on top of James and begins to throw awseome palm strikes to the head..The crowd counts with every one which lands..He lands
Nine, and then pauses before the tenth...He looks around to the crowd, and this moment of in-descion gives James the chance to reach up and poke Tigre' in the eye..
Tigre rolls off of James, who then gets up shaking the cob webs out from all those palm strikes...James lands a kick to the midsection, followed by a DDT, and goes for a cover...He as well gets a long two count from referee Wayne
West...Who for the record is the second highest ranking referee behind Sasaki
Hirokama...
James then picks El Tigre back up, and pushes him back in the turnbuckles...He lands a solid combo of lefts and rights with the ref warning him the whole time...Then puts El Tigre up on the top rope...James goes up as well, Tigre trys to fight him off with body shots, but instead James lands a HUGE
SUPERPLEX, and floats over for the cover....And the three count.....James then hops to his feet and begins to stomp the mexican cruserweight...James then picks him up and tosses him out of the ring by his mask which draws a huge heel pop...James holler's at the crowd who does it right back, and finally leaves the ring....Referee West helps Tigre to his feet, who gets a good pop from the crowd...Without help from the ref, Tigre' and west both head to the back with the crowd clapping..
Winner : Eddie James Time : Nine Minutes and twelve seconds Finish : Superplex by Eddie James ************************************************************************** ************ [Fred Hart] : " Eddie James is a mean man on a mission...Him, Simon Starr and
Reaper would be the men I am most scared of in the SAW."
[Joey Howard] : " They are very dangerous men...Samuel Smith made a comment on
Fever last night about the first supercard of the SAW...Is it true Down on the bayou is gonna be the name of the first supercard Fred ? "
[Fred Hart] : " Yes that is true Joey....No date or place is set yet, but the
SAW staff is leaning towards maybe late April or early may...The Supercards of the SAW are gonna be aired all around the United states...It will be anxious to see who chimes in and who doesnt.."
[Joey Howard] : " If the supercards sell good, SAW should air all in the states instead of just the southern half.."
[Fred Hart] : " I am not sure if that's what Mike Johnson wants...He is even still in debate if the supercards should be aired all over the states or just in the South...I do know though with dangerous men like Starr, James and Reaper here the SAW better make sure the rest of the US is ready for us before we go hitting up the air waves.."
[Joey Howard] : " You know Fred...We got one more match here tonight and it's an interesting one.."
[Fred Hart] : " Johnny Danger Versus Todd Van Rooyen...The interesting story here is more between there managers than the actual wrestlers...Todd and Johnny have known each other for a very long time...Had good times, bad times and so on, but there managers have never met, until last night on Fever."
[Joey Howard] : " It is safe to say that Damian Rock and Dark Tower cost there team the match....There mess up and confusion on the outside led to Danger getting rolled up and there team losing...Which led to the wrestlers having words....Now the two get a chance to go One on One....But the action on the outside is prolly gonna be as good as the action on the inside if you ask me...Damian Rock is a former champion in retirement and Dark Tower is a big ol boy.."
[Fred Hart] : " The saga of TVR AND DANGER won't stop here though...Next week they have to be in that six man tag match together...along with Taylor
Threat.." [Joey Howard] : " I am not sure if Danger or Todd is the favorite in this match, but it is a contrast of style's thats for sure...Danger is slick, tricky and quick, While Todd is aggressive, powerful and crisp with all his moves..."
[Fred Hart] : " It's time for Todd and Danger to take out there frustration's from the night before....Let's just hope this one stays in the ring and the two managers stay out of the ring."
************************************************************************** ************ Main event Twenty Minute Time Limit Todd Van Rooyen With Dark Tower Versus Johnny Danger with Damian Rock Referee : John Mercer ************************************************************************** ************ Danger came to the ring first with The legendary Damian Rock at his side...Both
Student and Teacher ignored the fans,and entered the ring very focused...Then out came Todd Van Rooyen with his mammoth bodyguard/manager Dark Tower...They also ignored the fans and entered the ring focused...Once the music cut,
Referee John Mercer tried to get the managers out of the ring, but had no so luck...As TVR AND TOWER went eye to eye with DANGER AND ROCK...
Eventually the stare down ended and Tower exited the ring...Rock and Danger poked a couple jokes at Todd and the now outside the ring Dark Tower which made
Tower hop up onto the apron....As soon as he did so though Danger ran at him and clubbed him with a right hand knocking him off the apron...Todd Then clotheslined Rock in rebuddle to Danger's attack, and Rock rolled out of the ring..Todd and Danger began to circle each other sizing each other up.
They locked up, and the obvious strength advantage went to TVR..Who pushed
Danger all the way back to a turnbuckle..TVR then flexed a bit, and Danger ran right back out trying it again, but got the same result...An angry Danger began to jump up and down..He then acted as if he was going to try it again, but stopped right before and strutted away from TVR trying to draw some cheap heat...an angry TVR chased Danger all around the ring, but coudln't catch the quicker, smaller man..Danger rolls outside the ring, and stands by Rock...Both men look in the ring smiling..An Impatient TVR begs Danger to get back in the ring.
Finally Danger does get back in the ring, and ask for a test of strength...Todd laughs, and agrees..He lifts his hands up, but at the same time is caught with a front kick to the stomach by Danger, then a spinning heel kick which knocks
Todd down to the mat. Danger being the student of the game he is, tries to keep the bigger man down on the mat by grabbing the leg and putting Todd Van Rooyen in a standing figure four type hold...Danger applies pressure, and Todd tries to reach up and get him, but Danger is just out of reach...Danger for the next couple minutes stomps, twist, and pulls on the leg trying to wear the big man down..
Rock on the outside begins to hit the apron rooting on his pupil Johnny Danger, this Causes Dark Tower to walk over there closer to Rock, but still doesn't act apon or say anything to Rock..
Finally Danger lets the now limping Todd Van Rooyen up, but he let him up for a reason...Danger lands a kick to the gut, then goes for a POWERBOMB, but the 160 pound Danger can't lift the 300 pound TVR up....TVR easily back drops Danger off of him, and drops to a knee....He shakes his knee trying to get feeling back in it, and Danger gets to his feet...He runs at TVR, But TVR catches him with a fist from his knee's....Danger back peddles, then comes in again, but
TVR grabs him by his throat...TVR stands up and choke slams Danger down...Todd limps around the ring trying to get his leg's back to how they were at the start of the match up
Dark Tower on the outside begins to rub it in on Damian Rock...The two walk towards each other and get in each other's face..The ref notices it and jumps to the outside trying to seperate the two managers...
Meanwhile in the ring...Van Rooyen gorrila press slams Danger much to the crowd's delight...Then drops an elbow on the chest area, He trys to cover
Danger, but the ref is still outside....Todd notices this finally and walks over to that side of the ring to complain...The ref finally seperates the two managers, and slides back into the ring...At the same time Danger rolls TVR up from behind and grabs the tights.
He only gets a two count though, and TVR is quickly up to stomp on the head of
Johnny Danger..TVR then lifts Danger up by his "Got Heat" T shirt, and pushes him back in the turnbuckles...TVR runs in landing a splash, then drives elbow's into the face of Danger...TVR Backs off, and Danger falls out of the corner head first, but is caught by TVR before falling completly...TVR then does a belly to belly suplex, but hangs on...He gets back to his feet and this time does a huge overhand release on this belly to belly suplex, getting a pop from the crowd, and having Danger hold his back in pain after hitting the mat...TVR smiling walks towards the now wounded prey in Johnny Danger.
Before TVR can continue the attack though...Damian Rock jumps up onto the apron, and begins to bicker at TVR.TVR Looks at him bewildered, as from behind
Dark Tower pulls rock off of the apron...The two begin to trade punches, TVR watches as the two managers trade right and lefts, but then once again is caught from behind by Danger with a roll up....
Once again we get a two count, except this time both men are up to there feet at the same time...TVR tries to land a clothesline, but Danger ducks, and catches TVR with a dangerkick to the head.Danger quickly goes for a cover, but once again gets a two...He begins to realize he is gonna have to hit his finisher on TVR to finish him off,and starts to set up for it..As he waits for
TVR to get up though...Dark Tower and Rock roll into the ring continuing there fight....Danger looks on in a confused manner...As if he was saying " So whose wrestling here ? "....Tower lands a powerbomb on Damian Rock which leads to
Danger kicking Tower in the head, which leads to referee John Mercer calling for the bell...
Just cause the bell is rung though doesn't mean the fight is over.....Danger got rolled up on Fever last night costing TVR his chance for a title...Danger is also upset though as he feels he wouldn't of got rolled up if it wasn't for
TVR's manager Dark Tower...So of course the heat rages on...
TVR Grabs Danger from behind and lifts him up by his spiked hair, then slams him down...then does it one more time before letting go....TVR Then helps Dark
Tower up, and the two begin to kick away at Johnny Danger...
When from the back comes TAYLOR THREAT..... Threat rolls into the ring, and happens to have a mic in hand... [Taylor Threat] : " I ain't saying I like yall two, or that I need yall two, but next week myself, Johnny Danger and TVR we got to team up to face three other chumps...I want to win, I don't want to lose...I don't want to have to worry about you two messing up my chances, I can prolly do it without you, but it would be easier with you...So at least for now, stop the madness, and let's get the job done next Saturday on Fever."
Threat looks at TVR and Dark Tower who are looking down at Danger and Rock....Both Danger and Rock get back to there feet...They act as if there gonna throw a punch at TVR AND Tower who go to block it, but Danger and Rock don't actually throw a punch...They chuckle and exit the ring...TVR and Dark
Tower then followed with angry looks on there faces...Threat then threw up his punk sign one more time for the night and exit'd the ring..
Winner : None Time : Fourteen Minutes and Nine seconds Finish : Double DQ ************************************************************************** ************ [Fred Hart] : " Well that was an interesting finish...I don't know if what
Threat said will work or not, but I guess we will have to wait and see."
[Joey Howard] : " I can't wait to see how this one unfolds...All three of those guys got major talent, and I am also anxious to see how this Taylor Threat and
ADB situation plays out...It would help if ADB would actually show up to the arena, I am sure.."
[Fred Hart] : " Very true....It was fun working with you here Tonight Joey, and
I am looking forward to next week ,but for now....For Joey Howard, This is Fred
Hart...Keep the SAW in your heart..."

[C K Walker] : " LISTEN TO ME YOU STUPID SLUT I AM NOT A HARDCORE WRESTLER, I DON'T WANT TO FIGHT REAPER IN A HARDCORE MATCH." [Samantha Owens] : " I am sorry C K...But I can't do nothing about it...I am just an interviewer..Comaplain to the higher ups.."
[C K Walker] : " I DON'T FEEL LIKE COMPLAINING TO THEM, I AM COMPLAINING TO YOU....DO SOMETHING ABOUT THIS OR ELSE.." [Samantha Owens] : " << Sniffling >> There is nothing I can do..Honest " [C K Walker] : "ALRIGHT THEN BITCH, YOU LEAVE ME NO CHOICE...." << C K Rares back as if he was gonna hit Samantha, but before he can swing his hand is grabbed...He turns around, and his eyes get big...As in the picture is none other than Chris Page who will be facing C K Next week here on War
Stories..>> [C K Walker] : " Hey look man...I wasn't gonna hit her, and I didn't mean to hit you with that chair on Fever.."
<< Page pushes Walker back, and stands infront of Samantha Owens...Walker rubs his chin, then just back peddles slowly while smiling at Page, until he is out of sight, and we fade out to the SAW Logo.>>
