SAW (Southern Alliance, 2002) — Roleplay

Speak Ya Mind #8 (part 2 of 2)

9 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[ We once again come into view of Tommy Taylor and Seth Baptist in there nice office in which they do business from. Tommy Taylor is sitting behind the desk, with his feet propped up as usual. Seth Baptist is sitting on the corner of the desk, Tossing a roll of tape up into the air time after time.]
[Tommy Taylor] : " Show them what they need to do with that Tape Seth.." [ Seth stops tossing the tape, and pulls a piece of it off. He sits the rest of the tape down on the huge desk, and puts the piece of tape over his mouth.]
[Tommy Taylor] : " That's EXACTLY right..Frankie Cash, Eddie James, All the tag teams and more importantly Antoine Bradley...Just put some damn tape over your mouths and keep em shut. I don't want to hear it and nethier does anybody else."
[ Seth removes the tape from over his mouth, and get's up off the corner of the desk. He walks back behind the desk and sit's in a rolling chair of his own.
The desk is huge, so Both men remain behind the desk with much room to spare.] [Tommy Taylor] : " I know everybody is saying, Why did we do this, Why did we do that and what are we gonna do next. << Pauses >> Thats fine, Yall can keep saying all that, but don't expect any answers from us. A good business men never tell's his secrets or goals until EVERYTHING is done. Some business men even take all there information to the grave. So don't push us, or you might never find the answers that your looking for. I know we attacked Bungle, I know we attacked Matt James, I know we set Antoine Bradley up..You all don't have to remind me of these things, They were done for a very precise reason, In which one day if your lucky you will all find out."
[ Tommy points behind Seth Baptist ] [Tommy Taylor] : " Hand me that grey book on the shelf behind you Seth.." [ Seth looks behind him to a small bookcase, and pull's out a huge grey book. He hands the book to Tommy, Who smiles and opens it up.] [Tommy Taylor] : " Mike Pace, I hear you might be wanting to press charges on me still. I hope you realize I went to law school, I hope you know I am highly educated and very rich. This book I have in my hand has a loop hole to almost every rule in America. << Opens the book and looks threw it briefly >> And right here...Incase you Antoine are thinking bout pressing charges for me not standing up and taking the blame for something I did, Letting an innocent man sit in there..Right here it is a perfect loop hole out of that one. So yall decide if you got the firepower and money to bring me to court and press charges. << Slams the book shut >> I hope yall make the right descion and keep this as a wrestling thing, and not a business thing. We all know on the business end I will out smart yall EVERY TIME. Not once, Or twice, but
EVERYTIME." [ Tommy tosses the book over to Seth who put's it back on the shelf where it came from. Tommy then continues.]
[Tommy Taylor] : " If you guys keep this a wrestling thing, At least you guys have a chance. A slim chance yes, but a chance at least. I have said since day one wrestling is my second gig, but being as talented as I am..Even at my second gig I am pretty damn good. << Sighs >> Last week we had it easy with the
Bayou Bandits, but this week they put a busy schedule on our plate. They must not realize how busy of a man I truly am, That's fine though. I will save the pathetic company and wrestle twice this week, and twice the week of the supercard. So as you can tell, When I said busy, I meant it. This week does have something in common with last weeks though. Both are EASY weeks, This one a touch more busy, but both definitely easy."
" On the "The Business Man" Tommy Taylor's plate this week is gonna be listening to Antoine Bradley complain and ask "Why me..Why me", A one on one match with Frankie Cash, and also a tag match with my good friend Seth standing in my corner. So many things to do, and so little time to do it I say...That's alright though..We will take this one step at a time, as all great busines men do.Not letting anything rattle them, or shake them one bit."
[ Tommy kicks his feet up into the air sharply and removes them from the desk, planting them on the floor. He put's his hands on the desk, Folding them casually.]
[Tommy Taylor] : " Antoine, We can put you off till the 28th, I might have some more suprises for you before then, but truthfully your not an issue until the
28th, So by the looks of my calander, I don't have anything to say to you..How bout you Seth, Anything towards Bradley, Or you want to put him out of our minds for the time being ? "
[Seth Baptist] : " I just want to know if he did drop the soap while he was in
Jail..That's all I want to know Tommy."
[Tommy Taylor] : << Chuckling >> " Let's hope not Seth, We already have one gay guy in Bungle around here, We don't need another one. Moving on, The tag team division get's going, Sorta a number one contender's match..."
[ Tommy unfolds his hands and raises them up behind his head while somewhat sighing..] [Tommy Taylor] : " Me oh my. So many loser's in this one I don't even want to think how non profitable these guys were before there wrestling lives. Put the other six guys together and you got about one brain...MAYBE. The Bounty Hunters are your basic dumb rednecks, The New Breed is your basic bible duo. You know of two brothers working together to do something, and will eventually realize nethier one of em is good enough to do anything and kill one another, And then two guys who can't be english by the looks of there names, and prolly won't understand a word of this I am saying. So to help them understand what I think about them..Help me here Seth."
[ Both men instantly raise there middle fingers up while smiling, and hold them there for a moment before lowering them back down.]
[Tommy Taylor] : " Once again moving on....Frankie Cash, The Famous Frankie
Cash << Yawns >> Iv'e heard alot about you, To much really. You don't seem to be all your cracked up to be by the looks of what I see. Big deal, Your fighting Chris Page at Down on the Bayou. Look at Page's past, Who hasn't beat the chump. Not a real special list your gonna add your name to there
Frankie...Get a life, Move on, TRY and step up in competition. Try beating someone with some actual talent << Whispers >> Try beating someone like myself
<< Shakes his head >> Note the word try though Frankie. Your gonna have to get lucky in there with me to win one, and after War Stories I bet your not feeling to damn lucky now are ya ?.."
" No matter, Try to keep that ugly head of yours up long enough for me to try and knock it off. When I beat you Frankie, I hope you realize it's nothing personal, It's just _BUSINESS_"
[ Tommy looks over to Seth ] [Tommy Taylor] : " You got a match beside's our lil tag match this week don't cha Seth ol buddy ? "
[Seth Baptist] : " Why yes I do Tommy.." [Tommy Taylor] : " Why don't you tell the SAW what your gonna do in our tag match, and in that other lil match of yours."
[Seth Baptist] : " No problem " [ Seth clears his throat, and cracks his knuckles trying to intimidate a touch.] [Seth Baptist] : " I am no genius Like Tommy over there, but like he said were fighting complete morons in the tag match. Guys in which make me look like a rocket scientist.. They want to play with chairs, Fire and get mad about everything. Thats fine, We will take a DQ win..A WIN is a win no matter how you look at it. Me and Tommy want to be in the ring with those losers as short of amount of time as possible. Let the Risley's and the Bounty Hunter's have there petty arguments..Just let the Business Men have the "w" in the win column."
[ Tommy claps as Seth finishes up at that sentance.] [Tommy Taylor] : " Well put my man..Well put." [ Seth smirks and continues ] [Seth Baptist] : " Then we have Eddie James, Who has his mind on the paper champion Tommy Flames. Beating him will be like taking candy from a baby I am sure. I wouldn't be suprised even with my massive frame if he calls me Tommy
Flames while in there with me. Which is an obvious insult to myself, and will lead to him getting chokeslammed on the mat like he has never been before."
[ Seth gets up from his chair and walks back around the desk sitting on the edge again. He picks up the tape and tosses it in the direction of the camera.]
[Seth Baptist] : " Use some of that and keep your mouth shut like Tommy said earlier and maybe I will only choke slam you once Eddie.."
[ Tommy stands up from his chair as well, but remains behind the desk.] [Tommy Taylor] : " Very well said my big friend. Just make sure you back up those words and everything will be fine and dandy."
" SAW, We will be seeing alot of each other this week, so for the time being lemme leave you with this question. Is the SAW a safe place for any of you to do your business, as long as the Business Men are here ? "
[ Tommy shakes his head "No" answering the question for us, but we still fade out to black.]
************************************************************************** ************************ [Fade in to a shot of the dented seat of a folding metal chair.] VOICE: "What yer all lookin' at... is a mistake." [The camera pulls back to a wider shot, revealing the chair being held up by one half of the tag team known as The Bountyhunters....the man called Lobo. He stands there beside his partner, Hawke, both still dressed in their wrestling gear.]
LOBO: " A mistake made by three people who have sorely over-estimated their abilities... and their career longevity. It's a mistake that those three people should've avoided. It's a mistake that won't go unpunished. An' it's a mistake...
That YOU, Risleys, will pay fer in blood." [Lobo tosses aside the chair with a loud clang.] LOBO: " Last Saturday night at Fever you boys showed us exactly what ya really are. Cowards. Ya proved that cuz the minute you punks realized you couldn't handle The 'hunters, ya up an' got yerselves disqualified. I guess we know what color's on yer family crest now don't we, boys?
Yer as yella as a doubleline road stripe. Ain't that right, Hawke..." [The larger of the two masked men now looks into the camera with a crazed stare.] HAWKE: " RISLEYS...NEW BREED... whateverthehell yer callin' yerselves this week... you boys done made THE biggest mistake o'yer lives! Did ya really think a chair across my partner's back was gonna get the job done?! Didya think we'd let ya get away with a cheapshot like that without gettin' a receipt?!
Well boys, what was started last Saturday night by you punks is gonna be finished THIS Saturday night... BY US! At Fever we're fixin' ta dust it up with you an' two other teams o'dreamers in a four way tag match. That means we'll get the chance ta change yer yella colorin' ta black-n-blue. An' believe me, boys... what we're gonna do ta you... it ain't gonna be a wrestlin' match.
It's gonna be a homocide." [Lobo now takes the forefront again. Once again he has the metal chair in his grasp.] LOBO: "There's a storm comin' ta Manshack, Louisiana, fellas. It's a storm like the wrestlin' world ain't never seen. Saturday night it's gonna blow inta Fever an' leave a path o'pain an' destruction in it's wake. That storm is called The
Bountyhunters an' make NO mistake about this... the mistake you boys made last week? It wasn't jus the WORST mistake you ever made in this sport.
It was yer LAST." [Lobo swings the chair at the camera and the screen abruptly goes to static...] ************************************************************************** ************************ {The scene opens up to the inside of a Grayhound bus. The bus is packed with people traveling to places across the US. Two young twenty something men are sitting in a seat together. One is asleep, the smaller of the two men is reading a book and listening to a CD player.}
Smaller man: Wow, I cant believe we are actually doing this. {The bigger man wakes up.} We have spent the past four years wrestling in San Fransisco. We have been busting our asses for so long for this chance. To think, seven years ago, we barely even knew each other. We were only on a high school wrestling team together. Thats it. Then we go out and audition for a wrestling organization and put us together as a team. What are the odds of that? We were so green when we started. After all that work, and taking the gymnastics classes, Fear Factor 2002 was born. Who ever thought, Kyp Durron and Miko
Reglia would be traveling across the US to Louisiana? Who ever thought we would have the chance to wrestle for the SAW tag team titles. What an experience this is gonna be.
Bigger man: I know Kyp. I still cant believe we are doing it myself. When
John brought us in the office and Mr. Johnson came to us with the contract off,
I was in shock. Us, two kids from San Fransisco who took on pro wrestling as a job, and now it has become life. Something that we are. We are Fear Factor.
We will do what it takes to win and entertain. Kyp Durron: Your right, Miko. We are going to Louisiana to wrestle against 3 others teams to have a shot at the titles. What a way to begin our career away from Frisco. Win the SAW Tag Titles. Well, here comes a stop buddy, you want something to drink?
Miko Reglia: Yeah, grab me a one liter of Code Red. Kyp: Yeah, that sounds good, I will grab me one. {The bus stops and Kyp gets out while Miko grabs his bag down from the overhead and pulls out a book. the title is Star Wars: New Jedi Order. The scene fades.}
************************************************************************** ************************ [ Fade In ] [ We fade into an old baseball field which has some roots growing out of the grass, A half way torn down fence in right field, and some dirt which hasn't been raked for what look's like years as rocks remain on the infield, and the dirt goes in many different directions. Two very important things remain though, Pitchers mound is marked clearly, and there is a homeplate with a backstop behind it.]
[ From the distance we see two men walking in the direction of the baseball field. Both men look to be in fairly good shape, and have some baseball equipment with them. There to far away to hear what there saying, but discussion is being made and laughing is happening as well. Both men get closer and closer until finally they reach the baseball field. They sit there equipment down on the side, Or at least most of it.]
[ We now see one of the men is Devin Downs. Devin with a baseball glove on his right hand, Meaning he is left handed makes his way out to the pitcher's mound with a smile on his face. Apon reaching the mound, He turns towards homeplate and begin's to shadow pitch towards home acting as if he was pitching in a game of some sort.]
[ The other man smiling yells out ] " Nice Pitch Devin, But let's see if you can do it with a real ball." [ Both men chuckle and the other man begin's to reach into the bag of equipment and pull out the gear for a catcher. He applies all the needed equipment, including a cup and catchers mit before walking towards home plate. He removes his mask and sit's it on the ground beside him. While standing the young man prolly in his early twenty's, pulls a ball from his catchers mit and throws it out to Devin who catches it accordingly. Both men then begin to play toss for a bit warming up so to speak.]
" So Devin, You still got the arm you had while we were in highschool ya think
? " "Dangerous" DD - " I don't know for sure Mark, but why don't you get down behind the plate and we will see "
[ Devin holds the ball for a moment, and let's his friend Mark get down in the catcher's position. He Put's his mask on carefully and pats his mit a couple times while encouraging Devin to throw the ball. Devin pauses for a moment, then goes into the windup. He tosses the ball hard towards homeplate, but the ball sails widly over Mark's head and hits the top of the backstop. Mark removes his mask and yells out towards Devin jokingly.]
" Same ol arm...Fast as can be, but the same control a drunk driver has behind the wheel of a car. Remember when coach used to say that to you man ? "
[ Devin nods and motions for Mark to get back down. Devin then tosses another ball in Mark's direction, but it bounces up to him hitting him in the chest gear. Mark quickly tosses the ball back to Devin. Who once again pauses before pitching again.]
"Dangerous" DD - " They say lightweights can't make it in the big time these days... I say there wrong. I don't know to many people in the world who can consistantly beat me in a wrestling match. You let me fight a guy five times, I will at least beat him once, Prolly two or three times. I think that should be considered making it.
If the guy is One Hundred pounds, or Three hundred pounds, you let me fight him five times..I wil figure him out, and _BEAT_ Him somehow, someway in that ring."
[ Devin throws another pitch which once again goes wildly away from homeplate. Mark get's up from the catchers position, and throws both balls out to Devin, who drops one on the ground beside him and keeps the other in his glove preparing it to be thrown again.]
"Dangerous" DD - " It's not that I am THAT good. Sure, I have been blessed with some talent by God almighty in which some people don't have, but seriously there is plenty of people better than me in this sport. It's just that I am willing to work hard, and learn. I am willing to practice, until indeed practice does make perfect. I am what they call a student of the game."
[ Devin tosses another pitch and it once again goes badly. Devin shakes his head and reaches down picking up the other ball. He throws it hard, and it bounces up to the plate as well. Mark managed to snag this one out of the dirt, and tosses it right back to Devin. Who looks down and begin's to kick the dirt back and forth on the mound trying to "fix" it.]
" It's not the dirt Devin, It's the man throwing the ball." [ Devin looks up towards his friend laughing, and tells him to shut up. Devin then looks back down, continuing to play with the dirt.]
"Dangerous" DD - " I like to think I learn quick, and that I can learn to do things on the run if needed.
In the ring I think this helps me alot. If you can learn how to do something quick, Or even learn how to counter something quickly, maybe after only seeing it one time. You have a huge advantage in that ring. Most people can't seem to do that. I don't know if the other people in this lightweight contendership match can do that yet or not, but soon they will find out.
_I CAN_ " [ Devin looks back up and winds up once more. This time he throws the ball right down the middle of the plate. He chuckles as he does so, Throwing the ball back Mark makes a comment.]
" That a boy Devin.." [ Devin catches the ball and prepares to throw it once more.] "Dangerous" DD : - " Yep...I definitely adjust quick to anything and everything
I do. From the in ring stuf, to out of the ring stuff."
[ Devin tosses the ball again right down the middle of the plate at a very high speed making the mit pop loudly.]
"Dangerous" DD - " Which is what makes me _DANGEROUS_ " [ Devin smiling walks towards homeplate, where his friend Mark has his glove off and is shaking his hand back and forth as the last pitch kinda stung his hand.]
"Dangerous" DD - " What's a matter Mark..Can't handle it ? " " Ever since you were a little boy I was the only one that can handle you,
Don't give m that crap.."
[ Devin reaches homeplate and pats his friend on the back respectfully, Before continuing with what he was saying.]
"Dangerous" DD - " Just like my good friend said, Not many people can _HANDLE_
It...Soon we will see if any in the SAW can _HANDLE_ it.I am almost sure there is some who think they can, I am almost sure there is some who can, but I am definitely sure there is some who CAN'T. It will be interesting to see if any of the one's in this match Saturday CAN.
Iv'e heard the name Taylor Threat Before, and the name Lenny. Can't say I have
Johnny Danger or El Tigre'. I know they think they can handle it, but can they
? "
[ Devin looks around the baseball field for a moment pausing in the middle of his thought.]
" Well can they Devin ? " [ Devin looks down at Mark smiling.] "Dangerous" DD - " Well I can't answer that Mark..Only they can, And Saturday they will get there chance. Don't be fooled by anybody else telling you otherwise, I am _DANGEROUS_. Nice and loving outside the ring, but chaotic and ruthless in the ring. Outside the ring I will be your best friend, Inside the ring I will be your worst enemy.
Which is what makes me _DANGEROUS_ " [ Interrupting Mark speaks ] " Ya you are pretty dangerous." [ Shaking his hand trying to get the last of the sting out he puts his hand on
Devin's shoulder, who just smiles and returns the favor.]
