Saturday Night Fever, 6 April 2002 (part 1 of 6)

************************************************************************* ************************ April 6th, 2002 The SAW Presents SATURDAY NIGHT FEVER From The Jim Black Recreational Center in Vicksburg, Mississippi In action Taylor Threat vs Lenny Risley Brothers vs Bounty Hunters Rud Groz vs Chris Payne Vern Walters vs Eddie James Co Main event - Southern regional title match - Bungle vs Tommy Flames Co Main event - Reaper and Chris Page vs Rob Fabulous and C K Walker Also in the house President Mike Johnson Antoine Bradley Cedric Mike Pace Shawn Cross Jay Trisler Jeremy Rivers TVR Johnny Danger Frankie Cash Hosted by Fred Hart Samuel Smith ************************************************************************** *********************** [ The scene fades from black to show a logo of red letters outlined by white lines. The logo reads "SAW." It begins to pulsate in and out. Then it suddenly stops. Again the screen is black. No picture, no sound, nothing. Then a faint chant of "SAW.. SAW.. SAW" begins to be heard in the background. The blackness is then filled with a shot of the jam-packed Jim Black Recreational Center In
Vicksburg, Mississippi. The fans are on their feet chanting the letters,
"S-A-W." The faint cheer that once was, is now a deafening chant in the small arena in downtown Vicksburg.]
[ The camera then cuts to a shot of the squared circle. The wrestling ring has the same logo that was in the beginning of the show, only now it reads Southern
Alliance Wrestling in a smaller lettering. The ropes are black and the ring, a light blue color. The camera then shows a shot of the announcers table, sitting behind the wooden table with monitors is none other than Fred Hart and Samuel smith. Both men are looking towards the camera smiling. As normal Fred Hart starts the show off.]
Fred Hart : " Welcome everybody to SATURDAY NIGHT FEVER !!!! I am as usual Fred
Hart, and beside me is None other than Samuel Smith."
Samuel Smith : " Much appreciated there Fred..We got an interesting show lined up tonight. Tag action, A title match, and a Tag Team war which puts some of the bigger SAW names up against each other."
Fred Hart : " Let's not forget a grudge match between a cowboy and a Gangster..The ultimate contrast in style's there.." Samuel Smith : " If you want to look deeper than there personality's and background you can even say there complete opposites in size..One man is fairly small, while the other is one of the larger men in the federation."
Fred Hart : " Later on tonight for that Tag Team War you mentioned we will be visited by Frankie Cash, Who will be helping us announce that one."
Samuel Smith : " I am still shocked Cash is in the SAW, But one of Mike Johnson's good friends Steve Greedy is a friend of Cash's and Page is in the
SAW. So I guess the shock shouldn't be there at all, but still it's hard to believe the Cash- Man is in the SAW."
Fred Hart : " I agree completly Samuel...It's time for the SAW office members to come down here I do believe. They are scheduled to start the show off here tonight."
[ "Blood Brothers" by Papa Roach hits the PA system bringing a heel pop from the crowd. After a few seconds of music, Janusz Risley steps out from behind the curtain. With long brown hair and a moustache, Janusz is dressed in a pair of faded denim jeans and a red and black plaid shirt. He stands at the top of the ramp, arms open as his identical twins Orlando and Marshall step into the entranceway, grins on both of their faces. The two look exactly the same, with short spiky black hair and deep dark brown eyes. Both are dressed in black tights with the word "NEW" on the the left leg in crazy green writing and the word "BREED" written on the right leg in the same green font. Tape is wrapped around their wrists and around their right bicep. The only miniature noticeable difference between the two is that
Orlando is _slightly_ taller than Marshall. The two boys are focused on the task at hand as they walk down the ramp with their father Janusz Risley.
Stopping at the bottom of the ramp, Marshall and Orlando charge at the ring and slide under the bottom rope while their father takes the more common method, going up the steel steps. He asks for a microphone from the ring announcer, raising it to his lips.]
Fred Hart : " This isn't the SAW Office..." Samuel Smith : " Good call Fred << Chuckles >> There a little shorter than most the office guys."
Janusz Risley : " Vicksburg, Mississipah..." [CHEAP HOMETOWN POP!] Janusz Risley : " ...Y'all suck! " [HUGE HEEL HEAT!] Janusz Risley : " Now I didn't come out 'ere with the nex' tag team champions for nuthin', I came out 'ere for a reason and that reason is ta' get some damn answers. "
[ Janusz looks at his boys, continuing...] Janusz Risley : " I wanna know why mah boys were referred ta' as the Risley
Brothers rather than wha' they're actually s'posed ta' be called... the New
Breed. So if one of ya fat motherf[BLEEP!]ers can get of ya ass backstage to come out and explain the situation, I'd appreciate it. For God's sake, how coul' ya be stupid 'nough to confuse the two names..."
[ As Janusz Risley rambles, a figure appears at the top of the ramp. We pan around to reveal the SAW doctor, Jeremy Rivers standing at the top of the ramp. Tentatively he approaches the ring, a microphone in hand.]
Janusz Risley : " Come on, come on... we don't have all day! " [ This speeds up Rivers a little bit, stumbling his way through the ropes and into the ring.]
Janusz Risley [in a stern tone]: " Now tell mah, why--" Jeremy Rivers [interrupting]: " Hey, you're new here, why don't you chill a little bit and let me just answer the question on behalf of--"
[ Before Rivers can even finish, Janusz Risley whispers some words to his sons Marshall and Orlando, The New Breed, who suddenly charge at the EMT...]
"THUUUUUUUUDDD!" [HUGE HEEL POP!] Fred Hart : " OH MY GOD!! MARSHALL AND ORLANDO JUST TOOK OUT JEREMY RIVERS WITH A DOUBLE SPINE BOMB SLAM!!! Samuel Smith : " And they're not done yet... I usually like attitude, but on an EMT might be pushing it..."
[ Orlando picks a motionless Rivers up off the canvas and puts him in a Cross-Knee Backbreaker Hold as Marshall Risley hits a monster Catapult Somersault Senton, bringing some of the biggest boos out of the arena the SAW has ever heard. Janusz Risley then spits on the fallen Jeremy Rivers, raising the microphone to his lips one last time...]
Janusz Risley : " Don't interrupt me again, ya get that? Good. Now understand that The New Breed are now 'ere in Southern Alliance Wrasslin' and they're here ta' HURT! "
[ As the three Risley family members leave the ring, the rest of the EMT crew rushes out to attend to their injured leader, still not moving. Janusz,
Marshall and Orlando taunt the crowd on their way up the ramp as we switch back to the announcer's desk at ringside.]
Fred Hart : " NOT A GOOD WAY TO START OFF THE SHOW << Puts his head down on the table >> " Samuel Smith : " Don't do this to me again already Fred..You got to block these bad things out of your head, and keep your head up high for when the good stuff happens."
Fred Hart : " That was an EMT for christ sake Samuel..How can I block that out
? What would stop them from attacking one of us one day ? "
Samuel Smith : " Just remember later on those two lil guys got to go up against the Bounty Hunters. That should put a smile on your face."
Fred Hart : " Ya...Those guys are big." Samuel Smith : " Now your thinking positive." [ "We love this stuff" By Monroe Rap Artist John Brodnex and Keith Powell begins to play.] Fred Hart : " And here comes the SAW top guns that I was expecting to hear from a second ago at the start of the show."
Samuel Smith : " The boys with all the power are about to come out." [ From the back steps Mike Johnson, Shawn Cross, Jay Trisler and Frakie Tha
Pimp. Each man has at least one title belt on there shoulder, as Jay Trisler has two on his. Both being the Tag Titles we assume since they look exactly alike. The men slowly walk down the asile, each with there own attitude's coming out..They enter the ring and invade the middle area. President Mike
Johnson begins to speak as he brought a microphone with him to the ring.] Mike Johnson : " Hello Vicksburg, Mississippi.." [ Cheap Face Pop.] Mike Johnson : " As you all know this is the _FIRST_ Time we have been out of
Louisiana in the brief history of the SAW. Louisiana treated us good, but let's see how good Vicksburg treats us here tonight."
[ More cheer's from the crowd.] Mike Johnson : " On each of our shoulders is a title belt we had made special in Bastrop, Louisiana. The one on Shawn Cross shoulder is the Southern Regional
Title which will be on the line later in the night. The One on Frankie's shoulder is The Lightweight title which will start it's progress towards crowning a champion next week. The TWO on Jay's shoulder is of course the Tag
Team Titles, which starts it's process tonight, and the one on my shoulder...Is the SOUTHERN TITLE...The big championship here in the SAW. In which I tried to crown a champion once before, but thanks to C K Walker that didn't happen. So now on April 28th the champion will be crowned."
[ All four men hold the titles into the air as the crowd pops.Once the pop faints a bit, Each man put's there title back on there shoulder. Then Mike continues.]
Mike Johnson : " Mike Pace couldn't be here tonight, He is in New Orleans trying to follow a lead he has on the Antoine Bradley situation, but he did say to tell you all that he wishes he could of been here and he will be on Fever next week."
" Every man up here now has an important part in the SAW, And they all have something to say to you all here tonight. So let's start with Vice President
Shawn Cross."
[ Mike hands the mic to Shawn Cross, and the fan's boo. Shawn looks on in shock, but then continues on.]
Shawn Cross : " I am Vice President and head of public relations in the SAW. It is my job to keep as many people happy as possible. Tonight I hope we make you happy Vicksburg, and I hope these titles we have make you happy in the future.
As I am sure our wrestler's will go all out for them." " Stuff that the Risley Boys did earlier tonight, Stuff like what Rob Fabulous has been doing, and the evil attack on Mike Pace will not be tolerated in the
SAW. Yes, I am just one man, but with the help of this man...Head of Security
Jay Trisler, I do beleive we can make most of you happy most of the time. Tell em Jay.."
[ Shawn hands the mic to Jay Trisler and the crowd pop's a bit.] Jay Trisler : " Some of my guys got beat up last week, and sometimes we have lost control here in the SAW. I can't promise everything to go right, but I can promise we will try."
" The belt's on my shoulder represents teamwork, More than one person making it happen, and strength in numbers. Which is what the SAW is gonna try to be about in general. I hope all of you in the SAW respect that."
[ Jay hands the mic to Frankie Tha Pimp, as the fans let off a huge crowd pop.] Fred Hart : " Oh god...Listen to them chant for this mad man.." Samuel Smith : " He's like a cult leader everywhere he goes Fred. Very intelligent, cunning and smart, but also very disrespectful and definitely a bit egotistical."
Frankie Tha Pimp : " HELLOOOOOOOOO JACKSON, MISSISSIPPI !!!!!! " [ Crowd Boo's ] Frankie Tha Pimp : " Uh....What happened to the cheap hometown pop ? " [ A "Vicksburg" chant begins to reign over the small arena.] Frankie Tha Pimp : " I don't have any Vicks Vaper Rub....I am sorry." [ Jay Leans over and whispers something to Frankie.] Frankie Tha Pimp : " Just playing yall...What I _MEANT_ To say was HELLOOO VICKSBURG, MISSISSIPPI..." Fred Hart : " Oh Brother..." [ Crowd Pops ] Frankie Tha Pimp : " I am Frankie Tha Pimp, One of the Match designers in the
SAW, And also a supercard announcer..What I have on my shoulder is the belt for the little guys in the SAW, And well.....I don't think little wrestlers are any good.You put Reaper against Taylor Threat, I put my money on REAPER every damn time, but oh well right....This is only for little guys, and should lead to some decent matches."
" The SAW is starting a buzz around the Southern United States, which I am sure some of you have heard.I mean you are here, Aren't you ? We Hope to continue this buzz, but even if we don't..We promise to let you listen to Fred "Crybaby"
Hart, and Samuel " I hate the white man" Smith every week....No matter what,
Thank me later.."
[ Frankie Sarcasticly bows, and tosses the mic in the direction of the President, who bobbles it, but manages to keep it from hitting the ground.] Mike Johnson : " Interesting Frankie as always, Fans....Without keeping you occupied any longer....Let's get the beautiful Stacy Normand out here, and get ready for our opening Bout."
[ The crowd pops as "We love this stuff" Once again begins to play. All four men exit the ring, and the lovely Stacy Normand enters the ring with a microphone in hand. Once the office members get to the back, the music cuts off, and Stacy offically start's the show.]
Stacy Normand : " In our first match we put two Lightweight's in the ring with one another. A prelude to all the great Lightweight matches we will be having here in the SAW. Introducing first, We have from San Antonio, Texas coming to the SAW from the great IGA....Here is LENNY !!!!!!! "
[ The strum of guitars as Shannon Harris rocked the arena with the vocals words of "Oh My God" almost in an orgasmic tone, before the explosion of "Sucking
Spikes" ripped through the arena. Lenny slipped a hand up and between the velvet curtain and pulled them apart. As he stepped out the crowd was a mixture of boo's and cheers, Lenny glanced at them from under the brim of his hat and rolled his shoulders, before heading down towards the ring. Lenny moved steady to avoid the out reach of fans' hands, and rolled into the ring under the bottom cable. Rising he stalked his way towards the furthest ringpost. As he raised a leg to catch the middle turnbuckle, his hands grasped the top rope and he jerked himself to the top. He stood there for a moment watching the crowd, before up to grasp the brim of his hat and toss it down for the ring handler.
His shirt was next to be grasped, ripped up and over his chest he tossed it down to the corner of the ring. A backwards jump and he turned in stride to watch the entrance way.]
Stacy Normand : " And His Opponent coming to the SAW all the way from California. He is one of the most extreme and crazy men in the business. Here is TAYLOR THREAT !!!!! "
[ Pan Backstage ] [ Taylor Threat is trying to make his way towards the entrance, but is stopped by Patrick Robbinfield who doesn't have on his neck brace this week.]
Patrick Robbinfield : " I just wanted to say thanks for helping me a couple weeks ago. ADB would of appreciated that."
Taylor Threat : " Ya, Whatever..." Patrick Robbinfield : " Do you want me to be your manager until ADB joins the SAW." Taylor Threat : " Nah....Not nessecary." Patrick Robbinfield : " Well at least let me come to ringside with you tonight, to show my support for you."
Taylor Threat : " Just stay here and chill man. I helped you out of respect for
ADB. That simple..."
[ Taylor gently shoves Robbinfield back, and heads out towards the ring. Once stepping out from behind the curtains, Threat's music picks up in volume and
Threat begins to hop around on top of the ramp way warming up. Threat then runs down to the ring, and slides in under the bottom rope. Threat throws up the punk sign to the crowd, In which a few of them respond. His music then fades out, and Ring announcer Stacy Normand exits the ring.]

Stacy said both men are lightweights, but nethier man usually competes in the lightweight division or admits to being a lightweight. I don't know how there gonna like the sound of that."
Fred Hart : " The SAW is in need of Lightweight's right now, so for the time being they may HAVE to be used as lightweights."
Samuel Smith : " True...I am sure they won't mind, espically with the talent the small guys got around here."
[ Threat slaps Lenny and the match offically get's underway as Lenny fires back with a hard right hand knocking Threat back. Lenny then pushes Threat back into the ropes and shoots him off. As Threat comes off, Lenny Leapfrogs him, and
Threat slides right out the ring to the floor. Threat then tells Lenny to come outside.Lenny runs towards the ropes and also slides out of the ring. Threat catches him with a hard right hand, but Lenny fires back with one of his own.]
Fred Hart : " We all know how strict Hirokama is...He's not gonna let them stay on the outside to long."
[ The two men continue to trade licks, Until Threat lands a knee to the midsection.He then jumps up onto the apron, and then jumps back off landing a
Whirlwind DDT on Lenny who had his head down from the Knee given earlier.
Threat pops up throwing the punk sign up once again and rolls into the ring. Hirokama begins his count.] [ONE] [TWO] [THREE] [FOUR] [FIVE] [SIX] [SEVEN] [ Breaking the count Threat rolls back outside and picks Lenny up by his hair. Threat tries to whip Lenny into the guard rail, but it's reversed by Lenny who sends Threat back first into the guard rail. As Threat bounces off the guard rail, Lenny runs in his direction until jumping up onto the side guard rail,
Then walking it with cat like agility before landing a Whirlwind DDT of his own. Lenny then gets back to his feet, and rolls into the ring..Hirokama once again starts his count.]
Samuel Smith : " Lil bit of copycat being played by Lenny here..." [ONE] [TWO] [THREE] [FOUR] [FIVE] [SIX] [SEVEN] [ Lenny rolls out of the ring..] Fred Hart : " Here we go again..." [ Lenny Picks Threat up by his hair and lands a couple backhand chops to the chest. Lenny then pushes Threat up against the steel apron, and tries to chop him again, but Threat moves making Lenny hit his hand on the rail..Lenny shakes his hand back and forth in pain, and Threat nails him with a side kick to the side of the head knocking him down. Hirokama in the ring screams for both men to get back into the ring. Threat acknowledges him, and Rolls Lenny back into the ring. Threat jumps up onto the apron, Then slingshots himself over landing a leg drop on Lenny. He hooks the leg with his hands, and remains on top of
Lenny with his leg going for a cover.]
[ONE] [TWO] [Th..Kickout] Fred Hart : " Close but no cigar there by Threat." Samuel Smith : " Both men showing why they are lightweight's, but don't really have to stay in the lightweight division to win. Great talent, minds and heart."
[ Threat rolls to his feet,and stomps away on the head of Lenny before Hirokama pushes him back. The two men talk for a brief moment, Then Threat goes back to work.]
Fred Hart : " Hirokama laying down the rules here...Just like it should be." [ Threat goes to pick Lenny up, but the slight delay in action gave him the window of oppurtunity to poke Threat in the eye's. Hirokama warns Lenny, but a cocky hate filled Lenny blows him off, and continues to go towards Threat.
Lenny then lands a couple punches to the side of the head, before whipping Threat into the ropes, As he comes off Lenny catches him with a flying back elbow testing the jaw of Threat.]
Samuel Smith : " Lenny landed a stiff blow there...Follow that one up and Might just get himself his first SAW win here tonight."
Fred Hart : " Last week he lost a tough match to your man Antoine Bradley." Samuel Smith : " Ya, and we all know where Antoine is this week. Not on the card, but in the slammer..."
Fred Hart : " Hopefully not for long...We should hear more on that later." [ Lenny follows the elbow up, with a spinning russian leg sweep on Threat, Which has the same effect as a normal leg sweep, but add's a bit more show. Lenny gets to his feet and hits the ropes, As he comes off he try's to land a huge splash with lots of air underneath it, but Threat get's a knee up in the air, and Lenny catches it solidly in the stomach. Both men lay on the mat..Lenny rolling in pain, As Threat is on his back trying to re - gather his composure.]
Samuel Smith : " Back to square one. Even field here, as if the match just started." Fred Hart : " It looks that way, but nethier man feels as if this match is just starting. There both feeling it.."
[ Threat Uses his legs and jumps back to his feet causing the crowd to pop. Threat then picks Lenny up holding his head down towards the ground, and slams him down with a Face Buster to the mat, He then rolls him over hooking his arm's with his legs, and reaching down with his arms to hook the legs to have an ackward pin position on Lenny here.]
[ONE] [TWO] [ Lenny kicks out of the move, but courtesy of the weird pinning position Threat had, When Lenny kicked out, Lenny was able to roll Threat into a pinning combo with a bridge.]

[ONE] [TWO] [THR....Last second Lenny kicks out.] [The crowd pop's as both men show great wrestling knowledge. Lenny gets up on top of
Threat and begin's to land hard right hands.The crowd counts with each hand landed to the top of Threat's head.]
[ONE] [TWO] [THREE] [FOUR] [FIVE] [SIX] [SEVEN] [EIGHT] [NINE] [TEN] [ Lenny looks around pausing for a moment, and then continues to nail Threat with hard punches as the count continues.]
