Sunday Night War Stories, 24 March 2002 (part 1 of 2)

24 March 2002

*********************************************** MARCH 24TH, 2002 _SUNDAY NIGHT_ WAR STORIES From West Monroe, Louisiana The MAIN EVENT for the evening put's Todd Van Rooyen Versus Johnny Danger Also in ACTION. Eddie James Vern Walters Taylor Threat Making appearances Mike Pace Shawn Cross Samantha Owens Antoine Bradley C K Walker Chris Page *********************************************** << The scene fades into a small arena which has SAW all over it..On the wrestling mat, outside the ring, Banners in the back of the arena, All the camera's, and the announcers table all say SAW...We pan across the arena once or twice looking at the area, and all the people gathered..The place isn't sold out, but there is a good bit of seats filled..The camera stops panning across the arena, and we are now focused on the announcers table which is at ring side. Two men are seen looking down shuffling papers..One is a now common face to us in Fred Hart..He is dressed in a business suit just as he was last night on Fever, and the other man is rather large. He has on some nice pants, but is wearing a T shirt...The shirt says "SAW" on it in big black letters against the white background of the shirt.This is the only shirt the SAW has made so far....So we assume The man is trying to promote it. The two men look up at the camera's about the same time, and Fred starts the show off.>>
[Fred Hart] : " Welcome everybody to Sunday Night War Stories, I am of course
Fred Hart and the man sitting beside me is Joey Howard."
[Joey Howard] : " I'm new to all this Fred, So take it easy on me here tonight." [Fred Hart] : " Don't worry about a thing Joey,Just be yourself..For those of you who don't know Joey was a top prospect here in the southern area, Until he hurt his knee in a six man tag match over in Japan. What he was doing in Japan,
I will never know, but I am glad to have him sitting beside me here tonight, and for the many nights to come."
[Joey Howard] : " Thanks Fred, Our job tonight is to review what happened last night, See a couple matches ourselves, and Preview the next Fever."
[Fred Hart] : " Thats right Southern Wrestling fans, We have the new line up's for fever sitting right infront of us..Before we get to that though, Let's cover the basics.."
<< Fred looks down at his papers, Moves a couple back and forth then looks back up to the camera smiling.>>
[Fred Hart] : " I think the first thing we should cover is Mike Johnson, and the opening of the SAW.."
[Joey Howard] : " Seems important to me Fred, So why not.." [Fred Hart] : " Mike started the SAW off in a very classic, respectful way.I was proud of the once power driven Mike Johnson. The speech he made to start the show had the fans excited about the good ol fashion action they were about to see. He then helped call the show with myself and Samuel Smith, but what
Mike did at the end of Fever shocked me.."
[Joey Howard] : " Same here...He made a TRIPLE THREAT MATCH for the Southern
Championship on the first night of the card.."
[Fred Hart] : " Mike promised the winner of the six man tag match would get rewarded, and boy did they...They had a chance to fight for the Biggest title in the SAW, and soon the biggest title in the Southern Parts of the United
States.."
[Joey Howard] : " It wasn't all good news though Fred...Patrick Robbinfield was in the triple threat match instead of ADB, and was used as a punching bag..ADB refused to show up for the show, so he sent his manager to take his place..How un classy was that..The match was like a One on One match basically between
Chris Page and the Reaper...With both men trying to pin the weak Robbinfield so they could walk out of the opening show of the SAW with the title..."
[Fred Hart] : " It went back and forth...Both men were tired from there previous match, but just when you thought Reaper was gonna win the title...Out came C K Walker with a steel chair.."
[Joey Howard] : " I am not sure where C K Walker came from, or really who he even is...But he made an impression on the SAW Like no other has done quite yet. I am sure someone who piss people off more in the future of the SAW, But for now...C K Walker is the Ultimate heel of the SAW."
[Fred Hart] : " C K did make the fans mad, Reaper mad, Page Mad and the President Mike Johnson mad by ruining the title match, but just as dirty was
Tommy Taylor...He had his friend Seth Baptist destroy Bungle, and then later in the show had Seth take all the autographs Tommy did from the little kids..Tommy said it was just "Business", But I tell you Joey...That made me sick to my stomach. He took autographs from the kids man."
[Joey Howard] : " That is pretty sad..I still think C K Walker made the most people mad though on Fever.."
[Fred Hart] : " You can go with ethier one of them..They both showed poor class in my eyes. I do think though C K Walker made the wrong man mad...Not talking bout Reaper or Chris Page by hitting them with chairs. I am talking about Mike
Johnson..When we get to the new line ups later in the show you will see what I mean, but I feel sorry for C K Walker, Let's put it that way.."
[Joey Howard] : " Me to...I noticed that as well Fred." << The two men kinda chuckle as they know something we don't know..Fred Hart then begins to clap, and continues to speak while clapping lightly.>>
[Fred Hart] : " I would like to congratulate Bungle, Tommy Flames, Matt James and Vern Walters for moving on in the Southern Regional Ttitle tourney...They all put on a helluv'a show..<< Fred stops clapping >> Sure Bungle got beat up by the man he fought's friend, Flames got attacked after his match from behind,
Matt James flipped off the crowd, and Vern Walters wrestled a beast of a man who pretty much snapped in the ring, but none the less they are all moving on in the tourney which will continue on this coming weeks Fever.."
<< Just then over the PA system in the small arena the SAW built in West Monroe, Louisiana you hear the song "B.O.B" By Outkast begin to play and from the back wearing all FUBU attire is Antoine Bradley and his friend Cedric...The two men slowly walk down to the ring slapping the hands of fans along the way.
They reach the ring, and ask for a microphone from our ringside crew, who gladly hands them one...The music cuts out and Antoine begins to speak for the first time in an SAW ring.>>
[Antoine Bradley] : " Sorry I couldn't make the impact I wanted to on Fever last night..I told you all on one of the SAW shows that I would be down in the dirty south looking to raise some hell, but when me and Cedric got to the show..You all saw what the SAW security did to me, and it just pissed me off...We decided to just watch the show, instead of showing our faces...I guarantee you though, Next week we will be doing it right up in this federation."
<< From the back runs four security guards..They slide into the ring and surround Antoine and Cedric.>>
[Antoine Bradley] : " Hey now, Yall already searched me and my boy in the back...What's the deal here ? "
<< Slowly from the back comes Commish and head booker of the SAW Mike Pace..He enters the ring with a mic already in his hand.>>
[Mike Pace] : " Antoine, I saw what the security did to you, and maybe you were treated a little misjust. I am not saying it's cause your Black, I am just saying they were suspicious of you for obvious reasons..As you know I was attacked by two men with a lead pipe on the show...I am not sure why someone would attack me, I just book shows and try to produce quality shows...But you were seen on tape saying you would Raise hell in Monroe...You never did....You weren't seen accept at the start of the show trying to get into the arena. In which security took a lead pipe from you....Your friend Cedric was with you...You also mentioned a complaint to my staff about not being booked, I am the booker..I don't know if your capable of attacking me Antoine, You seemed like a good guy when I hired you, but I will investigate this thing and currently all evidence leads to you my friend.."
[Antoine Bradley] : " Are you coming down on me again because I am black Mr
Pace ? I don't see you accusing anybody else on TV...Sure we only come on in the southern states, but I was born in Memphis...I love the south, People know me here and I don't need you giving me a bad name..I wouldn't do anything like that man...I didn't show my face again cause of your security team making me feel like a prisoner instead of a wrestler.."
[Mike Pace] : " We will see Antoine, I hope your telling the truth, Really I do, but my eye is on you.."
<< Security and Mike Pace leaves the ring...A stunned Antoine Bradley then tosses his mic to the ground..Bradley and Cedric then leave the ring in disbelief..The camera pans back to the announcers booth where we see Fred Hart and Joey Howard both with serious looks on there faces..>>
[Fred Hart] : " That was the next topic of conversation on my list...Mike Pace getting attacked by a lead pipe is something we can not have here in the
SAW..We want to be based on pure wrestling, and good morals..With people like whomever attacked Pace with a lead pipe, Tommy Taylor, Simon Starr and Matt
James in the SAW...We won't achieve are goal..So we might as well start now, by getting the bad seeds out of the soil..If Antoine Bradley and Cedric did attack
Pace, then they need to be kicked out of the SAW...If they didn't, an apology needs to be made..We will just have to wait and see."
[Joey Howard] : " All evidence points to Bradley, and he is a lil thuggish...It fits his background."
[Fred Hart] : " Antoine doesn't come off as a thug to me, Just cause he's black
Joey don't mean he's a thug, and the baggy clothes don't mean anything ethier.
Don't let Samuel Smith here you say stuff like that Joey...He is sensitive on the whole race issue."
[Joey Howard] : " Ya, Race and Religion are sensitive subjects. I guess I should drop that for now."
[Fred Hart] : " Good call Joey...Let's get to the ring for our first match tonight as we try to fade away from that subject..A man we heard alot from last night is gonna make his in ring debut here on War Stories...Taylor Threat is gonna take on James Malibu..."
[Joey Howard] : " Malibu is a big man from Monroe, Louisiana, but doesn't have to much ring experence...I can't see Malibu upsetting our SAW superstar here
Fred."
[Fred Hart] : " Me nethier Joey, but this is The south...Anything can happen down here..Just look at Mardi Gras every year down in New Orleans."
************************************************************************** ************ Opening bout of War Stories Ten Minute Time Limit Taylor Threat vs James Malibu Referee - Wayne West ************************************************************************** ************ The match started with James Malibu playing to the hometown crowd a little bit.Trying to enjoy the moment as long as he could...He tried to run from the smaller Threat for a little bit, Just trying to stay in the ring as long as possible, Or so it seemed..
Finally the quicker Threat caught him with a drop kick to the knee, stopping him in his tracks. Threat then stomped away at the fingers of James Malibu who was trying to use them to get back up..Threat landed a baseball drop kick to the head of Malibu which got a pop from a few of the real Threat fans in the audience..Out of 759 people in the arena, Maybe 75 were going for Threat in this match up..
Threat kept the match simple for the next couple minutes in the match up with your basic arm bars, Snapmares and kicks...Threat then went to the top rope and wow'd the crowd with a flying head scissors take down on the struggling to get up James Malibu. Threat very cocky like then went to the turnbuckles and propped up against them acting as if he was yawning which brought out some heat from the West Monroe crowd. Threat smiling then ran over and dropped a knee across the throat before going for a cover..He got a two count, but didn't seem to mind one bit. Threat quickly got back up and begin to stomp away on the left arm of James Malibu...Malibu fought back to his feet, and Taylor put him in an arm bar to try and keep the pressure on...Malibu back elbow'd his way out of the arm bar, but instead of hitting he far ropes, He turned to face Threat...He nails Threat with a couple right hands to the face, but then trys to swing that
Left hand of his which is hurting from the attacks by Threat..The punch was thrown to slow, and Threat blocks it..He then drops down, Lands a drop down toe hold...Climbs over the back of Malibu and slaps him in the totalimmortal....Malibu couldn't escape this version of the rings of Saturn and quickly tapped out..
Threat quickly released the hold, jumped up to his feet and pointed to his small group of Threat fans which were mostly seated together..He did the "Punk sign" in there direction then left the ring to a mixed reaction from the crowd, who he somewhat won over with his in ring talent...
Winner : Taylor Threat Time : Four Minutes and Forty-five seconds Finish - Malibu tapped to the Totalimmortal ************************************************************************** ************ [Fred Hart] : " Threat very impressive in his in ring debut for the SAW...I hope we can get him on War Stories often,He really knows how to work the crowd.."
[Joey Howard] : " I agree with ya there Fred....Threat had an interview on last nights Fever and he really told the world what he felt..I kinda like this guy, but whether you like him or you don't, you got to respect his in ring skills, as we just saw.."
[Fred Hart] : " Yes you do...Next to compete tonight is Vern Walters, Vern won by DQ last night just across the bridge in Monroe...I am shocked he is willing to compete here tonight after the cruel attack Simon Starr did on him, but Vern is a rough tough Texan and wanted to compete tonight against Don Lowry...A southern Indy ledgend...Who not to long ago was a world champion in a federation based out of New Orleans..
[Joey Howard] : " This will be a match up of power, but before we get to that...Let's look at one thing Fred if you don't mind.."
[Fred Hart] : " What's that Joey.." [Joey Howard] : " Next week's line up...." << Joey holds up a piece of paper, which we assume is the line up for next weeks SAW shows..>>
[Joey Howard] : " Some of these matches look great !!! Both these main events got me INTERESTED !!!!..."
<< Joey puts the paper back down on the table, Fred chuckles a bit at the excitement of Howard, then speaks.>>
[Fred Hart] : " Glad to see you got good spirit...Sure we can go over next week's card real quick before Vern Walters and Don Lowry meet up..."
<< Fred glances down at the line up, clears his throat and continues to speak.>> [Fred Hart] : " Our opening bout on Fever next week will put two SAW newbies in the ring with each other making there debuts...The man who we saw earlier on the show, Who is a supect on the attack of Commish Mike Pace...Antoine Bradley will go against Lenny.. Lenny comes to us from IGA...An International wrestling federation which competes all over the world..I am not sure if Lenny is accustomed to working here in the south, or used to dealing with our fans, but
I am sure he will help Antoine put on a good opening bout next week...Both wrestlers will want to win there debuts of course."
[Joey Howard] : " I have seen Lenny work, Fever will be a small crowd for him, and this will be less televised than he is used to, but he is a fine worker..He will give it all he has in that ring against Antoine.."
[Fred Hart] : " And I am sure Antoine will give it all he has against Lenny...Next is a second round match up in the regional title tourney putting
Bungle up against Matt James...Bungle the crowd loves, Matt James the crowd hates...These two men do know each other from past federations, and this should be fun.."
[Joey Howard] : " Both men are coming off of a hard fought victory, but James had it a little easier than Bungle who got beat down by Seth Baptist after
Bungle beat his friend Tommy Taylor...Bungle might be a little sore from that attack."
[Fred Hart] : " I am sure Bungle will be ready to go when it comes down to it...Were also supposed to hear from ADB...But ADB was supposed to wrestle last week and sent his manager out there instead into the lions den...So who knows if we will see ADB or Patrick Robbinfield...But hopefully Arthur Dexter Bradley will make his SAW debut next Saturday Night as Samantha Owens is gonna try to interview him...."
<< Fred Pauses >> [Fred Hart] : " This next match....Is going to be something to watch...Todd Van
Rooyen and Johnny Danger will team up once again whether they like it or not..Along with Taylor Threat who we saw earlier to go up against Chris Payne,
The Devil's Child Simon Starr and Rud Groz who will be making his SAW debut.."
[Joey Howard] : " Later tonight we will get to see Danger take on Todd Van
Rooyen...Those two have a weird love and hate type relationship..It will be interesting to see them team up once again....Also, on the otherside of the coin..I have seen tapes of Rud Groz...He is a suplex machine...I repeat he is a suplex machine Fred..."
[Fred Hart] : " So what we have here is crazy Taylor Threat, The legend Todd
Van Rooyen and the student of the game Johnny Danger taking on The demonic
Simon Starr, The man coming off of a hard fought loss and will want revenge
Chris Payne and a suplex machine making his debut to the SAW...Should be fun to watch there Joey..."
[Joey Howard] : " The next match is going to be fun to..Less bodies, but prolly just as much action...Big Vern Walters versus Hard working Tommy Flames.."
[Fred Hart] : " Flames and Eddie James put on an awseome match last week, and
Hopefully Flames and Walters can put on this way..Different styles, but hopefully they can make it work.."
[Joey Howard] : " The winner of that will go on to face the winner of the earlier mentioned Bungle vs Matt James for the Southern Regional title at some point or another..."
[Fred Hart] : " We are hoping Eddie James don't come out during this match up as he prolly wants revenge on Tommy Flames...If James doesn't come out though,
This one could possibly steal the show..."
[Joey Howard] : " Then we get to hear from Tommy Taylor and Seth Baptist which should be interesting to say the least."
[Fred Hart] : " There both sick and twisted...I am hoping there mic's get cut off in the middle of there interview..There not what the SAW wants or needs right now or ever for that matter.."
[Joey Howard] : " Before we get to our two co main events for next week's
Fever, and before we mention who will be on our show next week...Let's take a commercial break, Stay tuned though as later tonight you will get to see Todd
Van Rooyen versus Johnny Danger.."
<< The scene fades from the announcers booth to a view of the offical SAW shirt. It is just like the one Joey is wearing on tonight's show..It has SAW in big black letters, On a white background...The shirt then flips over to the other side, and you see " The south will rise again"....The shirt flips back over to the front, and onto the screen comes an image of Rob Fabulous...>>
Rob Fabulous : " Tough Stuff << Points his thumbs at his chest >> Rob Fabulous wears this shirt, So I know you can..."
<< The image of Fabulous fades off of the Screen, and we now see an image of the Reaper..>>
Reaper : " I WILL TORMENT YOU IF YOU DO NOT WEAR THIS DAMN SHIRT.." << Reaper laughs a bit evily, and then his image fades from the shirt...Now we see an Image of Tommy Flames come up onto the screen.>>
Tommy Flames : " This shirt reminds me of what it is I am working so hard for...To be apart of the SAW.."
<< Flames winks to the camera and his image fades...Voices are then heard mumbling, until finally you hear "Enough"....An image of Mike Johnson then pops up onto the screen...He points towards the camera..>>
Mike Johnson : " Simply put...Support those who want to support you..Buy the offical SAW shirt.."
<< We slowly fade to your typical TV commercials...>>
