Just the kinda week SAW needed after the one we had last week. Much love to you all for your help on making SAW fun.. A reminder - You might want to read the SAW commitee flash on the last RP packet if you haven't already.It has some insight on some of the matches this week, Incase you are a clueless as to why some of these matches were booked. I don't like putting that many people in the same match usually, but this week it was needed to help story lines. Also - Wrestlebowl IV is starting to pick up, If your not on the list, You might want to get on quick...Some great names our apart of that Like Maverick, Ceaser Risley, Chris Page, Alex Martinez, Vince Viper, Maxime St Jean, Fang,and one of my personal fav's Brody Thunder among others Thanks, Josh *************** 2 - News and Rumors *************** 1 - Tommy Flames was crowned the FIRST ever SAW title holder last week as he defeated Bungle. 2 - Two NEW tag teams are in the process of looking at the SAW contracts sent to them, One new team signed with the federation this past week, and with these two signing the SAW tag scene should be ready to get going strong. 3 - Mike Johnson and ADB have agreed to a "debut" date for ADB...The date has not been released to the public though. 4 - Bungle has been asked to appear at Mike Johnson's Kids birthday party in a couple weeks 5 - Chris Page got in a bar fight after his preformance on War Stories Sunday, and is rumored to be "cooling down" this week Thanks to SAW commitee not booking him,Giving him some time to get his head back on straight. 6 - After deep thought Mike Pace released the comment to the SAW website saying he still might press charges on Tommy Taylor for the attack. 7 - Vern Walters offically has One Broke Rib and Two cracked one's from his wrestling so far in the SAW. Most think from the match with Simon Star the opening week of the SAW. 8 - The SAW is still in talks with an all japan federation in possibly having some inter fed matches on some of the Japanesse federations cards to help promote both feds and give good action to the wrestling fans...Rumored names to be competing over there are Tommy Flames, Vern Walters, Simon Starr, Bungle, Tommy Taylor and leading the possible group would be Chris Page...The SAW wrestlers would be working for a real low price if the deal does go down. *************** 3 -
