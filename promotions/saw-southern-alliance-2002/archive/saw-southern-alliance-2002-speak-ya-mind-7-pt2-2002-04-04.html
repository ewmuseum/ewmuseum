SAW (Southern Alliance, 2002) — Roleplay

Speak Ya Mind #7 (part 2 of 2)

4 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
************************************************************************** *********************** [ Pan In ] [ We see Tommy Flames in a small hotel doing push up's on the ground near the bed, As his lovely girlfriend lays on the bed tilted towards him to watch. A smile is on her face the whole time she stares at Tommy. Tommy is in nothing, but his boxers which could be why she is smiling. Tommy's very tone and tan body is obvious to our camera, and I am sure to the ladies at Home.]
Girlfriend : " You work so Hard Tommy." Tommy Flames : " You got to do this, To make it in this sport." Girlfriend : " Your gonna make it in this sport Tommy, I just know you will." Tommy Flames : " I sure do hope so." [ Tommy finishes his set of push up's, and flips over to his back to continue his workout routine with crunches. His lower stomach bulges with every crunch, as it is obvious Tommy is in great shape.]
Tommy Flames : " I wander if Bungle is working out right now ? " Girlfriend : " I doubt it baby, He didn't even talk about you any Tuesday." Tommy Flames : " Silence is a deadily weapon baby, He could be setting a trap or in deep training. This is a title match, and Bungle know's that. He isn't a dumb man,His whole mirage he has on TV with the bear suit, dancing and so on is just a fake image to distract. He is really a good talent.. This is without a doubt the biggest match I have ever had in any federation."
Girlfriend : " You deserve this match Baby." Tommy Flames : " We will soon see." [ Tommy stops doing sit ups, and pops up off the ground. He gets in the bed gently moving his girlfriend aside. Both lay under the covers wrapped up against each other.Tommy looks at her.]
Tommy Flames : " It is late..Get some sleep." Girlfriend : " Okay baby, I'll wake you up at Seven so we can head on out to
Vicksburg, Mississippi."
[ Tommy nod's and kisses his girlfriend on the top of the head. She closes her eyes, and Tommy looks to the top of the hotel room with a blank look on his face. He speaks in a low tone.]
Tommy Flames : " All I have to do is stay calm, Cool and Collected " [ Pauses and says over ] Tommy Flames : " Calm, Cool and Collected. I can do that, I know I can do this. I could walk out of the arena tomorrow night being the first ever SAW champion. << Gulps >> Everytime Bungle dances, I must stay focused, Everytime Bungle wrenches back on my arm in an Arm Bar or form of one I must stay calm,
Everytime Bungle looks at me in that funny old bearsuit of his, I must stop myself from laughing and land one of my best kicks..."
[ Tommy looks down from the ceiling and in the direction of the beautiful girl laying next to him.]
Tommy Flames : " I deserve to be a champion, She deserves to have a champion in her life, and the fans deserve to see a true champion as there first SAW champion. I am willing to go out night after night and be a champion."
[ Sinks down in the bed getting comfortable.] Tommy Flames : " The Flames are definitely COMING Saturday night on Fever." [ Closes his eyes ] [ Pan Out ] ************************************************************************** *********************** <<Open on a clip from last weeks Sunday Night War Stories. Rob Fabulous and
C.K. Walker have Chris Page stretched out across a table as Frankie Cash climbs to the top rope. Cash leaps off putting Page through the table with his
MoneyMaker Frog Splash...Fade out to a plain black backdrop. A voice is heard.>> [Voice]: "Man...I tell you. That...Was...MONEY!" <<Entering the screen from camera right is Frankie Cash. Cash wears his ring gear long black shiny "pleather" tights with dark green dollar signs on the side, black boots, a black t-shirt with a picture of a smiling Cash on the front with the words "FUDDGEDABOUTIT" in green letters and green wrap-around shades. Cash smiles and runs his hands through his long jet black hair.>>
[CASH]: "Yes, yes...The Cash-man himself has come to the little ol' SAW. The hottest free agent in wrestling and the 'Past, Present, and Future of
Pro-wrestling'...in other words...Me." <<Cash spins around as if displaying himself to all the fans of the SAW. He stops facing the camera once again and nods.>>
[CASH]: "Why am I here you pathetic hilljacks might be asking yourselves? Well besides the fat paychecks...After all the Cash-man gets paid the big green...it's for one reason. One man. That's right you all guessed it. That freakin' mook Chris Page."
<<Cash's smile fades away and his body visable tenses up.>> [CASH]: "See quick history lesson for youse who may not know...Page and I hate each other. It goes back to the GRW and the EWA, but all that matters is that I want nothing more than to see Page's career ended here in the SAW. Page, these past few months you know what you've been? Huh? A friggin' itch. That's right an itch. An itch I couldn't scratch because you've been runnin' from me. Yeah, you been duckin' and runnin' all the while I've got this itch and it's drivin'
me f'n crazy. FINALLY...Finally Page I got to scratch that itch and how sweet it was. Looking down at you..."
<<Cash glances off camera seemingly re-living the moment from War Stories.>>
[CASH]: "Looking down at you on that table as my boys C.K. and Rob Fabulous-o had you stretched out...It was just perfect. I had waited for this moment for so long and then BAM!"
<<Cash rolls head back and an evil smile spreads across his face.>> [CASH]: "I keep playin' it back in my head over and over, it was that good
Page. I would give up all my McBubblies to have you in that position again
Page. The chance to come off and drive you down again...and again...Yeah, it's personal Page and this time. This time it is going to come to an end. It is finally time for you to get made...like...money."
<<Cash makes a quick cutting gesture across his throat and the screen goes immediately to black.>>
************************************************************************** *********************** [We fade in on an interesting scene. First off we have Vern Walters decked out in his normal attire of blue jeans, cowboy boots, and this time a Mark Martin baseball cap. He is in some woods somewhere in south Texas. He is sitting down leaning against a trunk of a big tree. There is lots of brush around and what have you. Looks like a place to meet some rattle snakes.]
VW: "It sure as hell been an interesting week, ain't it?" [Vern smiles and shakes his head in disbelief.] VW: "First its starts off, Tommy Flames and me knocking each other's block off and then some jackass appears and thinks he has a right to just mess with me and Tommy. Thats the funniest damn thing I ever heard of."
[VW smirks.] VW: "Eddie James you som' bitch. You ain't worth the two cents you daddy paid that little whore who had you. You know that. You're a worthless sack of shit. Just thinking some rap listening white boy thinks he is tough shit, that just pisses me right on off. You think you could pull some shit like you did and not have a damn thing coming. You thought you were smooth as baby's ass.
Well, boy, you got some lessons comin'. But out of you're stupidity something did come into play. I found a friend here in SAW. Tommy Flames is a good ol'
boy. And I now know first hand that Eddie James found out what a couple of pissed off Texans can do."
[Vern Walters pushes his cap up a bit with his hand.] VW: "Wigger James, you ain't seen a damn thing yet. I'm coming to take you're head off. You screwed me out of a chance at the Regional title and now its your turn to pay. Hell, you try to screw Tommy but you screwed it up and hit me. That is one mistake you won't live down. You see Tommy you have started the wheels. You started pushin' the goddamn truck down the hill, and its gonna run your ass over."
[VW looks down with almost a sadness in his eye.] VW: "Its a pity these days. You hear those African American guys on tv talking about the black on black crime is hurting America... Its like when me and you face each other 'cept its gonna be white on white crime, but getting rid of you will be good for America. One less wannabe out there. But then as far as
Rappers go, Vanilla Ice was the best damn rapper and he came straight outta
Dallas. And Eddie I'm sending you to hell where all Wiggers belong !!!"
[Fade Out.] ************************************************************************** *********************** [Our once blank monitors become vital as a SAW logo appears on the lower left hand corner of the screen. The visual aspect begins to fade in to the sound of
"Dig", by Mudvayne.]
"....." [A heavy breath is heard as we find the camera focussed on a sweating Rob
Fabulous, pushing up whatever rep he is at on what appears to be a leg press.
Ten plates at each side, which may seem like a lot but loses it's luster as we here Rob exhale the word "twelve." After a few more reps he stops, and stands up from the machine. He desperately pulls a large bottle of water up for a drink and gulps it's entire content down as if it was his first drink after ages of no fluid. He then sets the bottle down and moves to a stationary bike machine and gets on it. He begins to pedal until he notices the camera. He greets it with a smile and gets off of the bike and walks towards a radio and turns down the volume of the music we've been hearing since the initial fade in.]
["Tough Stuff" Rob Fabulous]: Perfect timing... [Rob grabs a towel off of a bench and rubs it over his face. His sweat pants dont really show signs of prespiration, but his sleeveless shirt does. His hair is tied back into a loose pony tail and his face is red. He wraps the towel around the back of his neck and grabs hold of the ends with each hand and speaks.]
["TS"RF]: Hello Mis'ippi. << cracks a smile >> Take a seat. ...Relax. [Rob takes a pause with a cause, then continues...] ["TS"RF]: Lets see. << sighs >> Who is it this time? Hmm... << raises eyebrows with a sarcastic smile >> Well gee, Chris Page! [Rob laughs for a second and shakes his head] ["TS"RF]: Haven't I beat this guys head in enough over the past couple of weeks? Hasn't it been enough...me, humiliating him every week? << shrugs >>
I guess not. But hey, it's all cool with me. I mean, just another main event, just another pay check. << smirks >> All bringing me one step closer to a title, and one step closer to finally getting rid of "The American Rage." That aside though, I'd like to applaud Mike Johnson for schedualing another great match for me.
[Rob stands loose and claps his hands together slowly, all the while with a face filled with a bit of disgust and sarcasm.]
["TS"RF]: Seriously though, I'm getting a little bored with playing to the scripts with these guys. It's hard enough to have to waste my talents against them let alone have to listen to them for another week. << shakes his head >>
I mean, let's be honest here Chris. << double thumb point >> I've got your number. I've played you like a cheap fiddle over the past couple of weeks;
real fast and real hard. And chances are, I'm still going walk out of this event a winner. While you may have Reaper on your side, I've got CK Walker on mine. And while you may have your side of the story, I have the truth.
...You're going to lose. Simply put. And I'm gonna be the guy who makes sure of that. [Rob takes the towel off and rubs his face one more time, and sets the towel down.] ["TS"RF]: Much like I've done for the past couple of weeks, I'm going to out smart the both of ya. I'm going to perform better in the ring, and I'm going to connect when it counts. So I don't want to hear any bs from the two of you about how I "have no chance." You might be good, but I'm always going to be better than you. Not because I'm bigger, but because when it comes to wrestling I know my elbow from my asshole unlike the two of you. And that's pretty sad considering you both have a good amount of experience on your side.
But hey, I wont hold it against you once I'm running you guys into the ground. Maybe on a different day, of a different week would you stand a chance. ...But don't get your hopes up.
[nods his head and smiles] ["TS"RF]: We're still going to win. [Shoots a wink to the camera] ["TS"RF]: And you can bank on that. [Rubs the back of his neck] ["TS"RF]: I had more to say... [Smirks] ["TS"RF]: But why waste my time? [Fade to black.] ************************************************************************** ************************ Deadline is up, Another solid week for the SAW in my personal Opinion. The SAW is picking up as I have said before, but also the SAW is getting noticed more.
I have had alot of people Im'ing me and emailing me about the SAW. I do believe that's all thanks to you guys. Pat yourselves on the back.
Anyways, You know my Addy and Im if you got any questions or want to discuss. I should be on for a while, I got to think of some story lines for Greedy and
Horo Atashi. I won't mind chit chatting if any of you all got something on your minds.
