SAW (Southern Alliance, 2002) — Roleplay

Speak Ya Mind #9

12 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
SAW presents "Speak Ya Mind" on April 11th, 2002 You will hear from the Following SAW superstars ************************* 1 - Little Blue Super Jew 2 - Estrago 3 - Frankie Cash 4 - Tommy Flames ******************** LBSJ: "The things I do for a paycheck." (Lightning Crashes, an old mother dies. Wait, that's a Live song. And not a very good one, either. But despite that, the storm does in fact rage outside the window pane from which the camera view pans in from, raindrops streaking the glass and slowly dripping down to await death by evaporation to the rising sun. With a barely visible trace of blue curtains seen on the outskirts of the image feed, the camera pulls away from the window, and once again lightning fills the room. The flash is the kind which illuminates all in a blinding haze, and for a moment we are without our vision. It clears quickly, coming back to us with a roll of thunder in the distance. Just like that, the lights come on in the room, dispelling the darkness which had encased it. We are in a small study, bookcases reaching from floor to cieling lining the walls, books fastidiously arranged within them. A fire burns, and above it is a mantle bearing a series of belts and trophies, as well as pictures of the owner of those recognitions of achievement. The camera continues slowly panning through the room, blue carpeting, blue wallpaper, and, hung by the window through which the wind whipping at the trees is visible, an Israeli flag. A creak, and the camera turns. The owner of the voice which began the whole viewing process we currently enjoy is revealed.)
(He sits with his elbows on the desk before him, fingers in a pyramid in front of his face. Long, dark blue hair is tied back behind his head tightly, and cobolt eyes stare forward towards the camera. Massive forearms and biceps, thick slabs of muscle wrenched tight against bone and held beneath skin, come from out of two huge shoulders, connected to what appears to be an even bigger chest. Concealed, however, is the rest, both by a shirt, plain blue in color, and the desk which hides our host's legs. A blue pair of eyebrows knit momentarily, and then relax. Fingers unclench, and hands come to rest, revealing the rest of the patron's face. A nose, larger than average, held above thin, humorless lips and covered with olive skin. He gives the camera a brief regard, then slowly leans back in his leather-padded office chair, the wheels making no noise against the thin layer of carpet on the floor. Finally, after holding us in suspense, he speaks.)
LBSJ: "Look who's here. Before I get to that, let me just say a few words. The past few months for me have been terrible. I've been up and down this country and all around this world sticking with this sport, and I don't think
I've ever had as bad a time as I did in the company I just came from. I don't even think a name is worth mentioning. I picked up a new set of scars, showed some people who the boss was, and then high-tailed it out of Dodge when the time came to either take the fall for some head-bashing or protect myself. So you lead one group of excessively violent rebels in an attempt to destroy the corporate order. Is that suddenly a crime?"
(Lightning is seen, highlighting the plants hung from the cieling and casting an ominous shadow against the face of our speaker. He takes a breath, but then continues.)
LBSJ: "I should apologize. You didn't deserve that rant. I guess I just didn't expect to see you here is all, took me a minute to gather my thoughts.
It's not everyday the cameramen of Southern Alliance Wrestling show up inside the home of Little Blue Super Jew."
(The thunder which follows up the lightning rolls in right on cue.) LBSJ: "I'll take by your presence that my interest in your federation was recieved and returned by the guys in charge. Well, whoop-de-do, I get to spend another year or so breaking my bones, sweating out my strength, bleeding 'till the last drop is gone, all in an effort to reach that next big payoff, that next time when the fans drive in like sheep to hand over money that I can use to continue living like I do. I appreciate the fans. They let me keep on with the comfortable life I enjoy. I appreciate my competition. The shiksa's seem to dig it when I grind their faces in, and I, personally, like how I look on
T.V. when I make someone tap out. So you see, I'm not a bad guy. I'm just starting to question as to whether or not it's all worth it."
(The camera moves in closer, and we see what Little Blue Super Jew has got spread out on his desk. A map, of the entire world, several dozen areas marked off with Stars of David. They are placed over Canada, Japan, Mexico, Puerto
Rico, Texas, New England, the South, California, several African countries,
Malaysia, some Eastern European countries, various parts of South America, and some others. Even Australia seems to be marked, and come on, who goes to
Australia these days?)
LBSJ: "My conquests. My successes and defeats, all carefully marked up and drawn out, charted, recorded, down on paper and in the books. I've fertilized plenty of soil with my vital fluids throughout the years. I've been through it all, and then back again. Sometimes for the fans. Sometimes for the glory.
Sometimes just for me. The world has been my playground in Professional Wrestling. And now SAW has decided to open up its roster to me. My question to you is - after such a long and harrowing career, after countless scars that will adorn my skin forever, after the surgeries, the hospital visits, the sprains, the breaks, the tears, do I really want to be down South? Do I want to risk everything that I've gotten so far, everything I have built up, for one more pay-off? Just for that last bit of security? I could retire right now, before my thirtieth birthday, and be secure for the rest of my life. Wrestle a few indy shows to get presents for the nephews come Hanukkah. But ---"
(Little Blue Super Jew, this new presence in our midst, pauses in his diatribe. His eyes squint, and he looks into the camera, slowly drumming his fingers against his desk. This continues for a moment, and then silence. Only the steady beat of the rain on the window sill punctuates this obtuse lack of audio. Finally, LBSJ leans forward again, and finishes what he began to say before.)
LBSJ: "But nothing. That's not for me. Kicking back like some lazy schmuck resting on imagined laurels for the rest of my life? Forget it, jackholes, you ain't that lucky. Little Blue Super Jew's made up his mind, and if Southern
Alliance Wrestling wants to pay me to come in and break up their little band of sissy girly-men like I did in every other place I've been, then it's their money. Mark my words here and now; I'm joining up, and I'm not going to work my way up some fershluggener ladder or wait to break some damn glass cieling.
You want to work your way up from the bottom, then kindly allow me to throw you down to it with everyone else who wants to waste their time. I came here to earn the big money. I came for the big payoffs. And there's nothing that can keep me back. Why?"
(Once again, bolts of celestial judgment rain from the heavens. Somewhere on a hilltop, a tree sets ablaze. Thunder crashes into the house louder than ever before, and the lights flicker. Though only for a moment, the temporary darkness provides us the haunting image of Little Blue Super Jew's bright blue eyes, still visible even through complete inky black. Or is it just a trick of the little remaining light?)
LBSJ: "Because I want to humiliate people in front of thousands of fans while getting paid for it. That's as good a reason as any --- right?"
"See you soon, SAW. See you soon." (The camera begins to fade out, as thunder rolls forward again, and we fade to blue...) ************************************************************************** ************************ (Scene opens in a crowded conference room. Many people derssed in business suits as well as camera crews came be seen busily moving around the room. A hush falls over the packed room as a small man in a business suit approaches a microphone. He clears his throat before addressing the crowd.)
Man:"It is with great honor that I introduce one of the newest signees to the
SAW. He is man who has earned many accolades in numerous federations across the globe. He is noted as one of the best cruierweights in the game. It is my privelege to honor .....ESTRAGO!"
(Applause go around as a man enters the right side of the stage. He is dressed in a black suit. What's awkward about the man is the fact that his face is completly covered in black mask, the right side of which is metallic with net mesh over the eye socket, while the lef side is made of black lycra. The man smiles at the crowd through the cut out mouth piece.)
Estrago:" A few weeks ago, I recieved a call from Mr.Johnson, he told me of the
SAW and was wondering if I'd like a spot on the roster. I sat back a few weeks and checked out the competition and it is off the hook. So I grabbed my tights, signed a contract and here I am. I'm now goign to open the floor to any questions you guys my have out there."
(A man in the front row jumps up.) Man:" Mike Jakowski, New Wrestling Times. I've been followinf your career for along time. All the way from the IPWA to IGA and now here. Do you plan on bringing your brand of high -flying, high risk wrestling to the SAW."
Estrago:" Of course. I'm going to take whatever measures I need to in order to wow the fans. And you better believe I'm goign to take it to my opponents in the process. Inside the ring, outside the ring, I'll be all over the place."
( A woman with a clipboard in her hand bolts up and addresses Estrago.) Woman:" Susan Ungerland, Wrestling Observer Weekley. Your debut match is a 6 man tag match with Mexican rules. Do you feel this works to your advantage?"
Estrago:" Let me think... Which has a better advantage in the water? A shark or a tiger? Throughout my career I've been in numerous Mexican rules matches andf you better believe that it is going to work to my advantage. Next?"
(An extremely obese man waves to Estrago.) Man:" The SAW does not currently have a cruiserweight title holder. Do you feel your in contention for the title?"
Estrago:"You know this! Mike called me cuz I'm guessing he wanted a specialist. And don't worry, I'm goign to show all of you what I can do when it comes time for the six man tag team match. It's going to be crazy! I hope the SAW is ready."
(Finally, an extremely gorgeous women stands up and looks to Estrago.) Woman:"What are you doing later?" (Fade to black.) ************************************************************************** ************************ <<The camera opens on what appears to be a backstage area of an arena. In the foreground are heavy equipment boxes with "SAW" stamped on them. The camera pans to the right past a couple of tables heaped with the remnants of deli trays. A voice is heard as the camera keeps panning.>>
[VOICE]: "Did you freakin' see it Steve-O? He had me tied to a friggin' chair." <<The camera settles on a man standing in a side hail. He is dressed in black dress pants, leather shoes, and a dark green dress shirt. His long jet black hair is wet down and swept back over his shoulders. He is clearly speaking into a cellphone.>>
[MAN]: "Was a scared. Yeah, I was scared. The guy is a maniac. I almost pis..." <<The man turns revealing the trademark green wraparound shades of Frankie
Cash. Cash suddenly stops speaking as he sees the camera.>>
[CASH]: "What the frig?! Uh....So anyway Page had me tied to a chair, but was I scared... FUGGEDABOUTIT!! That's right I had him right where I wanted him. Look I gotta go." <<Cash hangs up and approaches the camera.>> [CASH]: "First of all...Don't sneak up on the Cash-Man every again jag-offs. Now onto business. Chris Page..." <<Cash shakes his head and pockets his cellphone.>> [CASH]: "You think you are big and bad...You jump me from behind and then tie me up...That makes you big? That just makes you a coward and a punk. You think tying me up and slapping me around is going to intimidate me? Please...I told you once I was going to make your life a living hell and it looks like I get my chance at Down on the Bayou...Last Man Standing. Yeah, that's right."
<<Cash pulls his shades off and eyes the camera with a building intensity.>>
[CASH]: "Count the days Page. It's the final chapter in our little history. You thought getting put through a table by the Money Maker was tough...You ain't seen nuthin' yet chump change. It's time to get made like money for the last time Page. Bank on it."
<<Cash slides back on his shades and a cocky smile breaks across his face.>>
[CASH]: "Tommy Taylor...I haven't forgotten about you. Well the Cash-Man has always taken care of business...Business Man. You bring your best and you will get Made Like Money...Just like the rest."
<<Cash makes a cutting gesture across his throat and the scene fades to black.>> ************************************************************************** ************************ [ Pan In ] [ As we come into scene we see Tommy Flames girlfriend standing infront of us,
Looking beautiful as always. Flames is not in sight, as Only his girlfriend is in view. From the scenery behind her we can tell we are in some kind of storage room, Somewhere in a house. With a sad look on her face, Tommy's girlfriend begins to speak.]
Girlfriend : " Tommy has done anything anybody has ever wanted him to do his whole wrestling life. He has wrestled infront of crowds of twenty, Took bumps from on top of a scaffold, Lost to champions past there prime in federations run by politics to help keep them on top."
[ She looks away from the camera in disgust.] Girlfriend : " Tommy has done so much for this business, and this business has done nothing back in return."
[ She looks back to the camera, Now smiling much to our disbelief.] Girlfriend : " Until now that is. Tommy is the current SAW Southen Regional
Champion, The FIRST man to hold gold in this young federation. He is finally main eventing, Finally getting to showcase his moves, and finally going in the right direction."
[ Her face just beams off excitement, as suddenly we hear a door open.] Girlfriend : " Hey Tommy...We were just talking about you." [ Tommy Flames walks into the picture wearing some shorts, and A white muscle T shirt. Sweat pours off his body as he has been working out. He wraps his arms around his lovely girlfriend, Then speaks.]
Tommy Flames : " So what we were talking about in here huh ? " Girlfriend : " Just how great you are." [ Tommy smiles and while having his arms still wrapped around her, picks her up and put's her back down before giving her a kiss on the cheek. Tommy unwraps his arms from her, and put's his arm around her pushing her up against him, as he looks to the camera for the first time.]
Tommy Flames : " Well thank ya sweetie, But soon we will see if I am truly that great. Two days from now Me and Antoine Bradley enter the ring in a non title match. A match which puts two of the younger, brighter stars in the ring with one another at the same time..."
" This DEFINITELY should be a good one...Whether I'm hot landing moves that you couldn't imagine anyone ever landing or I'm cold in the ring not being able to hit you no matter what distance you are from me, Always be looking for the possible flames Antoine, Cause I am gonna try my damndest to catch you with the
_FLAMES KICK_ "
