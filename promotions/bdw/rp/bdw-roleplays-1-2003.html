BDW Roleplays #1
Date: test
Location: test

The worst night of my life has occurred, and what do I have now after Sunday? Nothing! My title was taken from me, from some guy with a dream. That's right Chezina lived a dream this Sunday, but don't worry Underground King is going to put him through a nightmare on Sanitarium. This isn't over, as a matter of fact this is far from over. Chezina, it's only a matter of time before I get my title back, so don't get to attached.

BEEP!

'Hey, Revelation...this is Jeffery. None of us has seen you since Sunday night. Look man we know you lost, it's all right. You'll get your title back soon enough. There is no need to mope around the house all day and night, hit me back on my cell man, later,' Co-Owner Jeffery says.

Revelation sits on his couch peering at the answering machine. The light blinks and the number 13 changes to 14 as another message has been saved onto the machine. Revelation has one hand around his shirtless body, bandages wrapped around his waist. Revelation stands up, slowly holding his waist in pain. He limps over towards the table where is cordless phone sits. He picks it up and leans on the arm of the couch. He reaches down sliding open the drawer of the end table.

'What in the hell did I do with Ashley's card?' Revelation thinks to himself out loud.

Revelation digs through the drawer tossing out small pieces of paper, and little sticky notes. Finally, he pulls out his hand with a small white card and he lays it on the table.

'Should I call her or not?' Revelation thinks to himself out loud again.

Revelation stares down at the card, he ponders on whether to call or not.

'Maybe you should try calling me, or Jeffery, or hell even King back?' A voice very familiar from behind says.

Revelation turns around to see Apocalypse behind him in his den doorway. He leans against the frame of the door, with his black sleeveless trench coat and his black denim jeans with leather boots.

'Man, you scared me,' Revelation says to Apocalypse.

Apocalypse slowly walks down the two small steps onto the hardwood floor. His footsteps echoing through the quiet home of Revelation. Apocalypse walks around and puts and arm on the shoulder of Revelation.

'I've told you time and time again, a title isn't worth anything. It's for looks, I mean I have a gold watch, does that make me a champion? No, yea some people think it makes you the best, but hell if you know you are the best you don't need no damn belt to show it,' Apocalypse says.

Revelation peers at Apocalypse with an odd look on his face.

'If all that were true, then why the hell even wrestle?' Revelation asks.

Apocalypse shakes his head, as if Revelation hasn't listened to a word he has ever told him.

'For the love of the sport, you don't need a title to justify your abilities to perform in the ring. Do I have a belt? No, and many people fear me that is what keeps me going. People's fear and hatred, that is what I love...not no damn title,' Apocalypse tries to explain to Revelation.

Revelation turns his shoulder and stands up holding his waist. He backs up a few steps and looks up and then down at Apocalypse.

'What in the hell is wrong with you? Underground King must have hit you pretty damn hard, he knocked the hell out of your ass. You came back all 'proper' or something. You used to come out thinking you were the man and you were unstoppable, something has happened to you,' Revelation says staring at Apocalypse with what looks like hatred now.

Apocalypse stunned at Revelation's actions.

'Something wrong with me? If there is anything wrong with anyone it should be you. Chezina leaping of a the tron landing straight on your ass, its a wonder how you are still alive and breathing. You are the one who has changed, we used to be tight. You'd listen to me and take my advice, now since you took that title and I have been out of the picture you think you have surpassed me. I got news for you, you aren't half the man I am,' Apocalypse says yelling at the top of his lungs.

Revelation getting furious stands with his hands clinched tight together. Knuckles turning white and his face turning red with anger. Apocalypse stands calm, but a little out of breath after his speech.

'Go ahead Revelation, take your best shot. I see those fists, I know the power your packing behind them. You think your ready, but your about to see just how ready you think you are. I been lying in that bed for weeks and weeks, you think I'm not 110%? I been waiting to get back in the ring and get my hands on someone for so long, do you really wanna be the first for me to get my hands on?' Apocalypse asks Revelation.

Revelation still stands with his fists ready to explode at any second. As it looks he gets second thought's his hands slowly come apart. He moves them back and forth relieving the stress and then crosses his arms across his chest.

'Sorry Apoc, it's just I lost my damn title Sunday and I am so vulnerable right now. The smallest thing could set me off, we are a team we don't need this,' Revelation says extending his hand.

Apocalypse reaches out and shakes the hand of his partner.

'I know how that is man, and if that title means so much to you then I'll help you get it back just tell me when. We are a team and this is what I am here for, but you can't let one lose get you down like this. You got a match next Thursday, you gonna stay at home the whole time?' Apocalypse asks.

Revelation shakes his head.

'No, one lose isn't gonna get me down! I am going to bust my ass, and that rookie I have to face is going to wish he never signed with BreakDown Wrestling!' Revelation says with confidence.

'Aight man get dressed,' Apocalypse tells Revelaiton.
