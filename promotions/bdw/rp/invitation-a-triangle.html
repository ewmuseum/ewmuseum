BDW Roleplay — #10

Invitation: A Triangle

24 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Confrontation: A Triangle Brock RockHard BDW Roleplay #10 Date: 5/24/04 Location: “Son, listen to me please.” “SON? You’re not my father! Mother get this man out of here!” “Listen Brock, I know I’m not your father, but I was his closest friend and he told me what he wanted for you and this is not it.” [Brock Rockhard, one of BDW’s newest signees stares back at the man. His intense blue eyes aimed like a shotgun directly across the table where another large man sits next to an older lady. The man has his hand on the ladies shoulder as she tries to fight back tears to no avail. The tears flow from her eyes and down her cheeks, but Brock doesn’t advert his glare from the man.] “Brock, we’re getting your mother worked up, let’s go for a walk.” Brock: You should go for a walk Bill and never return to tell me what to do with my life. [Brock, his father’s friend Bill and his mother all sit around a small round kitchen table in the home of Brock and his mother. Brock’s massive frame engulfs the chair he sits in. The chair is turned around backwards and Brock’s forearms rest on the back of the chair as he impatiently taps his foot. His father’s friend Bill is an imposing figure himself, an older man with graying hair and crows feet. His gray eyes shift frantically back and forth between Brock’s sobbing mother and his best friends’ son.] Bill: Your father knew that one day you would be a great wrestler Brock, but that day isn’t today and it’s not tomorrow either. This sport is rough on the body and it’s rough on the heart so if you would just listen and wait another year into your training and then go to a more small scale place than BDW. Brock: I’m not the kid you used to pat on the head and suplex for fun anymore Bill. [Brock hesitates momentarily and looks up and smiles arrogantly at Bill.] Brock: In fact I’d like to see you suplex me now. [Bill tries to get a word in, but Brock speaks before he can and eyes him as though he dares him to interrupt.] Brock: My father was the greatest teacher in the world Bill, you know that. I’ve been blessed with this frame and with quickness and regardless of what you think or think he might think, I’m ready, I’m prepared for whatever challenge awaits me. [Bill sighs.] Bill: I could never question your talent or your desire Brock, but you’re not going to a tiny independent federation Brock, you’re going to a PTC federation. There’s not a whole lot of places featuring better talent than the PTC and that’s a big jump for someone who’s never wrestled for the big money. You…. [Brock growing frustrated interrupts him.] Brock: Now you listen here…. [Brock is promptly interrupted by his mother who has wiped her tears and shouts angrily at her son.] Brock’s mother: Brock, please listen to Bill, he knows of what he speaks. [She may be small and frail, but she’s still his mother and the only remaining person on the earth he’s scared of since the passing of his father. His shoulders slump as he obediently turns his attention back to Bill.] Bill: There’s no doubt Brock, no doubt in my mind that one of these days you will become one of the greatest wrestlers ever to grace the squared circle, but right now you’re a twenty one year old kid with plenty of time to make that happen. Let’s go to a small independent fed and let me manage you to a few titles before we jump the gun and climb aboard BDW. [Brock looks at the man, this his mother then back at Bill. In the back of his head he knows Bill’s right, but the blood that flows within his veins is racing, his passion for the sport ready to abrupt and it needs a bigger scene than some lowly independent, it needs Break Down Wrestling.] Brock: No. Bill: Brock please… Brock: I’ve already signed with BDW Bill, and I already have my first match this upcoming Sunday. My first win! Bill: Please tell me your joking. [Bill can see the kid’s expression, he knows that he’s not.] Brock: My decision has been made Bill and while I respect you, there’s nothing you can do to change my mind. [Brock’s mother is a very strong woman, but with the loss of her husband and now this she can’t control herself. She begins sobbing heavily, almost to the point of hyperventilation and gets up from the wooden chair and exits the room. Brock looks on sadly as his mother departs.] Brock: It’s time for you to leave now Bill, I must watch tape on Jeremy Page from last weeks static and begin preparing. [Bill is really angry now, angry that Brock won’t listen and angry that they’ve upset his mother so.] Bill: You’ll be sorry you didn’t listen to me Brock. Jeremy Page may not make you realize that you’re wrong, but somebody will whether it be JJ Jarone, McClancy or another PTC superstar like Hessian, Chris Storm, or Chryste. [Bill smiles.] Bill: When a female beats you, you’ll understand. [Brock stands up from the chair and stares down at Bill who returns the stare, his cold, gray, meticulous eyes aren’t afraid of the young boy.] Brock: I’m tired of all this nonsense. You could fill up a hot air balloon with all that crap Bill. You know what I’m capable of and now it’s time for BDW, and PTC to know. Bill: To know that you are an inexperienced rookie getting in way over your head. Brock: Bill, as I said a moment ago, it’s time for you to leave. [Frustrated, angry Bill spins on his heel and walks away from Brock. He looks back over his shoulder to see Brock’s intense blue eyes staring a hole in his back.] [The youngster feels he has to justify himself, most likely because Bill’s words got him thinking, is he really ready for the big time? Sure he is he reassures himself in his head and then aloud.] Brock: You’ll see Bill, you’ll see! I’m ready for this! I’m ready to unleash the heart of a lion onto BDW! You’ll see. [Bill just shakes his head in frustration as the door shuts behind him.]
