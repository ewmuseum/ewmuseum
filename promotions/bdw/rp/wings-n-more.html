Wings-N-More

29 May 2004

Wings-N-More Brock RockHard BDW Roleplay #13 Date: 5/29/04 Location: “Wings-N-More it is then. [CLICK] [The voice belongs to BDW wrestler, Brock Rockhard.] ********************************** [30 Minutes Later] [Brock’s Toyota Four-Runner glides into a parking spot in the Wings-N-More parking lot. The door swings open and Rockhard’s massive frame emerges from the vehicle. With a quick swipe he removes his shades from his eyes and tosses them into the driver’s seat. With another quick motion he shuts the door and strides towards the front of the restaurant. As he walks that way he catches the eyes of two women walking out the restaurant. His muscles bulge out of his tight black knit shirt and he looks sharp in his starched khakis, with his blonde tips shimmering in the light produced simultaneously by the moon and the parking lot overheads.] “Brock, Brock!” [Rockhard looks up and sees an averaged sized guy lingering near the doorway of the restaurant. The guy is definition of metrosexual. He’s wearing a black pinstriped suit; his hair tossed about his head and he’s still wearing some glasses that resemble Ray-bans. Brock nods to the man and tosses a hand up to acknowledge his old friend. As he approach him they smile at each other and do some elaborate handshake like a fraternity might do.] Brock: How have ya been Wade? How’d school treat you this semester? Wade: College is fun, but the whole school part still sucks man. We all envy you, getting paid to do what you love and not having to worry about making grades. Brock: Yeah, but I miss you guys and feel like I don’t hardly ever get to see y’all since I’m always working out or sparring or on the road. Are the other guys already here? [Wade shrugs and takes off his glasses. He hangs them from his shirt near his chest.] Wade: Probably. You know Oates and Neil are always punctual. [The two share a laugh and Brock slings open the double doors as the two walk in the restaurant, Wings-N-More. As they enter a hostess with a head set on approaches them tentatively. She’s a cute girl, Hispanic, brown hair pulled back in a pony tail, white blouse and tight black pants.] Hostess: How many for y’all tonight? [Wade smiles at the hostess and winks.] Wade: Actually we’re just meet….. [Brock quickly cuts him off as he looks of the shoulders of some waiting customers and two men, obviously the aforementioned Oates and Neil are waving at them. Brock hits Wade on the shoulder and points to a guy nearly his size and a wiry guy with dark curly hair and freckles camped out at a tall table near the bar.] Brock: There they are! [Brock begins walking towards his old friends as Wade lingers behind trying to make small talk with the hostess before she shies away when her attention is diverted by an elderly couple entering the restaurant. Wade catches up to Brock and the two slide perch themselves atop tall bar stools at the table with the other two men.] [The one whose been called Oates thus far speaks. He’s a good looking guy, broad shoulders and seemingly athletic. He doesn’t have a hair out of place and his babyface is well groomed.] Oates: Brock, it’s good to see you man. Wade: HEY! How about me? Oates: Wade, we’ve been trying to get rid of you since highschool. [The group laughs.] Brock: It’s good to see all you guys, I’m glad we could do this before I fly up for the pay per view in the morning. How’s baseball Oates? Oates: Aw, you know man, having a good season. Brock: And football Neil? [The wiry, curly haired guy speaks.] Neil: In division three football those linebackers hit me about like you get hit I would assume. [Neil pulls the collar of his shirt down a little to reveal a large black bruise atop his collar bone. Oates and Brock wince. Wade is too busy eyeing the waitress. Brock notices.] Brock: Hey Wade, snap out of it will you. The wife will get mad at you. [Wade keeps looking for a minute before turning around.] Wade: Stop saying shit like that man, I’m never getting married. Oates: You really wouldn’t cheat on Brittany would you? Wade: No, but I’ll look til the day I die man, no harm in looking. [The three guys can see the wheels turning in Brock’s head as a waitress approaches.] Waitress: Can I get you guys something to drink? [She proceeds to take everyone’s orders but Brock who is staring off into space.] Waitress: And something for you sir? [Brock still hasn’t heard her until Oates kicks Brock under the table.] Brock: WHAT? Oh um, I’m sorry, I’ll have a water please. Neil: So what’s on your mind? Brock: No, it’s nothing man. Neil: It’s got to be something. Brock: I told myself I wasn’t going to talk about wrestling tonight. It’s my short night off before I have to get my mindset right for tomorrow. Oates: An opponent huh? [Brock thinks. He nods.] Brock: It’s just that you mentioning cheating to Wade and I was thinking about this Rob Zone guy from NGW and how he’s with a women named Rachel who seems like a borderline fiance if not a wife and yet just the other day he went home with some woman of a guy whose ass he beat in the street. What a slime ball. I really want to beat this prick. Wade: Winning has never been your problem, have a few beers relax. [Neil and Oates simultaneously hit Wade.] Oates: He doesn’t drink idiot. Wade: Oh yeah, all that nonsense about subjecting your body to toxins. Beer is good for the heart. [Wade receives a stern look from Brock.] Wade: Well it is! Neil: So what are you worried about? Brock: I think this Zone guy could actually pose a decent challenge unlike the other schmucks in this match. Granted he is so dumb he keeps contradicting himself something fierce, but he does have A LOT more experience than me in real, televised matches anyhow. It was only a few days ago he said he had the upper advantage and would beat us all then wasn’t sure later in his promo and now he’s claiming it MIGHT be in his destiny to actually lose. Wade: Seems like a tool, don’t sweat it! Brock: I just hope he doesn’t go through with killing the king pin of New York, as if there actually is one because then he’ll either be dead or arrested and never make it to the match. It’s not plausible to think he could commit murder and get away with it until Sunday night. This is the real world not fantasy land that Methid lives in where you get out of rehab in three days. Neil: Or where kingpins who worked their way all the way to the top by murdering person after person as the compassion to raise a kid and a wife will give in and say that she will be the kingpins to avoid death with merely one measly minute of dialogue. These guys HAVE to be kidding, they HAVE to be. Brock: So you saw? Oates: We all keep up with you Brock. You’re making us all proud man. Brock: Yeah, but my mother wishes I would go back to school, get a degree have something to fall back upon, what should I tell her? Neil: The truth. Brock: And what’s that? Oates: That you are making all of us proud, and more importantly you are making your father proud. [Brock goes silent. He looks at his old high school friends, the wheels in his head turning at rapid pace, not unlike the way he plans for the match to go come Sunday night. His friends are all staring back at him beaming with pride. He can sense their pride, and he feeds off of the positive energy from their beaming eyes and their smiles. Rob Zone and the others will prove to be a daunting task, but he will prevail, he has to prevail, it’s that notion that keeps him plugging away week in and week out.] [Fade Out]
