Acension

Acension Brock RockHard BDW Roleplay #14 Date: 5/29/04 Location: [A blur bounces around in an old constructed ring. The blur bounces off the ropes and propels itself towards the center of the ring where it ducks absolutely nothing, turns around and promptly suplexes the air. As the blur gets up from the old stained canvas we can recognize that it’s Brock Rockhard. Rockhard quickly gets to his feet and continues to perform a complicated and various array of high impact wrestling moves. He’s dressed in mesh shorts and no shirt. The top of his sky blue shorts are dark blue from the sweat dripping down from his chest and back. After performing his finisher on the air he comes to a halt in the center of the ring.] “I’m ready” [States Rockhard as he makes his way to the corner closest to the feed and climbs up on the turnbuckle. He stares down at the camera. His words come smooth and easy for someone who just kicked the living crap out of the air for the last 8-10 minutes.] Brock: Oh yeah, I’m ready. [A quick swipe of his forearm to his forehead as Brock wipes away some of the sweat. His hair is scattered everywhere, his veins exposed, and his muscles still pounding away in unison as though he was the perfect machine.] Brock: I’ve said a lot about my opponents thus far this week, but I’ve yet to say much about myself. You see I’m a firm believer that your opponents don’t really lose the match, you have to go out and win it. And to many of you that may sound absurd or corny or whatever; however, I assure you it’s very true. This is a hard business and I’m aware of that. I know nothing is handed to be on a silver platter and I damn sure know that these guys aren’t going to go down without one hell of a fight come tomorrow night at the Pay Per View when I contend in the biggest match of my short career. I’m confident, I think I will prevail. My father always said you had to think you were going to win otherwise you’d defeat yourself before you ever started. All my life I have been preparing for a moment like this. A moment in which I could shine on the national stage and come tomorrow night, the epitome of all my dreams may very well come true if I do what I’m capable of doing. All the tools are there, it’s just a matter of obtaining the right mindset and putting God’s gifts to use. [Brock climbs down from the turnbuckle. His ragged tennis shoes are about as far of a cry from his wrestling boots as his family’s old basement facility is to Madison Square Gardens. He steps through the ropes and hops down directly in front of the camera. His upper lip is perspiring as he begins to speak again.] Brock: These guys are going to be surprised when I reveal my speed for a big man. They’ll be surprised when they see how technically sound I am and how calculated and precise each attack is, but most of all they’ll be surprised when I kick out every single time. These opponents are worthy opponents, there’s no doubt. Each of them have their own attributes and I’m certain that each is better than me at some aspects of the sport here and there, but I can assure you that none of them have the heart and desire that I will bring to the table come Sunday night. Heed my warning, this is only the beginning. Brock Rockhard is here to stay and he shall climb the BDW proverbial totem pole to the top. Nobody, I mean nobody is going to stand in his way until he obtains not only the heavyweight title, but the utmost respect from each and every roster and staff member of Breakdown Wrestling. The time has come boys and girls. What of it? [With that Brock reaches up and gently taps the camera to turn it sideways. It pans around his old training facility as Brock grabs a towel and a water bottle and heads up the stairs ascending into his mother’s living room.] [Fade Out]
