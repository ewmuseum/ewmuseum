Reassurance Brock RockHard BDW Roleplay #16 Date: 6/5/04 Location: “Your mother is just being a mother bro. It’s the whole bird in the coop thing?” “You mean bird in the nest syndrome or whatever it is?” “Umm yeah, something like that. She just wants her baby boy to stay around and take care of her and she hasn’t been weaned from it like our mothers’ have when we go off to college because you never went to college.” I was staying with my best friend Brian Oates, most commonly called Oates, and he had a good point. I wasn’t sure if I had gone to college for a semester or not. That is, I couldn’t remember for the life of me what I put on the history part of my bio page. Bah, it didn’t really matter. Oates and I were sitting on an old couch in his apartment. It was fairly comfortable living for a college kid. He was majoring in political science at a small private school in San Antonio and was playing baseball for them. He was good, but wouldn’t ever make the show. He was the type that wouldn’t be satisfied unless he had the money though. In that respect I suppose he was like me yearning for respect in the wrestling world. “What about her taking a dig at BDW though? That was below the waist, was it not?” I asked Brian. He adverted his attention from the television to me. I couldn’t tell which he was paying more attention to, but he had always been a good friend and I valued his advice so I listened regardless. “I suppose so Ryan, but she is your mom and she does know the wrestling business.” As I pondered his words he turned back to the television, but obviously something was still on his mind. Without looking back at me he stated something else. “All the guys are proud of you man, but BDW just doesn’t seem that great. Perhaps you should consider somewhere where you could obtain some quality respect.” I was absolutely flustered. I slapped my thighs with my hands and looked at him as put out as anyone I could look I would imagine, though I certainly couldn’t see myself nor really guage how put out someone could look. “You too?” I whined. “Look bro, I know you’re happy with your belt, but think back. Was it really that hard to come by? And I’ve been watching all week and I haven’t hardly seen any promos from anyone in BDW.” I too couldn’t think of many guys cutting promos this week, but there were only a few matches on the card to hype up. If I wanted I could probably step out of character and note that I was pretty sure that the Dervante versus McClancy match was angled; however, despite the fact that it’s still bad not to cut a promo regardless, it would be bad form to step out of character. I knew better. I thought about my previous matches and come to think of it, with the exception of the battle this weeks opponent Rob Zone gave me on the pay per view, none of my matches had been all that challenging. As I thought about that, an idea popped into my head. I immediately wanted feedback so I posed the idea to Oates. “Hey Oates. I have an idea,” I stated excitedly. “Oh yeah. What’s that?” he calmly replied. Oates was always a cool, calm, collective sort of guy. “I know the fans love me and would probably back me on this so what if I asked to be tossed into the mix for the Heavyweight title?” Oates thought for just a second before responding. “But you hold the Platinum title and Dervante will soon be the number one contender,” he explained. “Right, but with a win over Zone for the second week in a row I think I will have proved that I am main event caliber and the fans would love a triple threat match between myself, Dervante and McClancy. The match would at least make my mother see that there is some solid competition within this federation.” Oates shrugged which frustrated me because I thought my idea was a solid one. “It would be a good match Brock, but you three can’t fight each other every single week. That’s not going to increase ratings. I hear y’all got some new super stud too, but still. I’m sorry bro, but I’m still with your mom on this one.” I sighed. They were trying to implant ideas in my head, but I had faith in Jeffery to get this keep this ship afloat and make the mast a mighty one and I was certain my main event challenge would be supported by the stands. I nodded to myself. My idea was a splendid one. “I’m tired man, I’m hitting the hay,” I announced to Oates. “Cool Bro,” was his response. We were really comfortable with each other in the sense that that’s all it took. I took two steps and curled up on a futon my and an arm-chair. I was too tired to think who has a futon and a couch so I merely pulled a blanket over me, ignored the sounds of Sports Center and drifted off to sleep, dreaming of my splendid idea.
