Moving Out Brock RockHard BDW Roleplay #15 Date: 6/5/04 Location: “Mother, I know you think I’m crazy, but I think this move is inevitable.” I loomed over my mother’s tiny frail frame. The glow in her skin was long gone. Her dress was old because she hadn’t bought anything new since my father had passed. She had tears welling up in her eyes and it killed me to hurt her so, but I had convinced myself that it was time to find an apartment. “Brock, you’re only twenty one. That’s not old enough to just move out,” my mother stated. I had thought all of her possible retorts out. I remained patient with her, but on the inside I was getting frustrated. I was a professional wrestler now, and I wanted my own place. “I need to move to New York or California or somewhere where BDW usually hosts their shows,” I told her. My mother shook her head no, seemingly more angry than sad at this point. “I don’t know who BDW thinks they’re kidding,” she exclaimed. I was shocked to hear her bellow out the words from her tiny frame. She continued. “BDW has zilch for shows and they pretend like they’re WWE having shows at Madison Square Garden and the Staples Center. It’s absolutely absurd. I watched your father through various independent federations and those types of federations don’t make the money to have shows like that.” I pondered what she was saying as she ranted on. “The fed has zilch for talent with the exception of about four guys. More than half the roster doesn’t cut promos. They’re not getting good ratings. They can’t possibly be making money Brock. Get out while you can. Paying for the venues they are paying for as an independent federation and not even filling the seats, there’s no way they can pay you enough to move out on your own, especially where the cost of living is so high like in California or New York.” She took a deep breath and spoke up again before I could get a word in. “You may think your old mother knows very much, but I can assure you that I know about wrestling!” she exclaims firmly. I was staring down at my black “what of it?” shirt too scared to look my mother in the eye. “You’re against me, Bill’s against me. Can’t you all just be proud of what I’m accomplishing? I’m content with where I am at right now and BDW will come around, you’ll see,” I pleaded BDW’s case to my mother who remained unsatisfied. “Brock, they have to get a talent pool and they have to settle down to one region of the country and start hosting shows in college gymnasiums and the like or they won’t obtain any money. They’re not the WWE and they’re not the fWo, they’re a small, growing fed and they should act like. I got news for you, with the talent pool they have surrounding you the Platinum title holds about as much value as most feds lowest tier belts,” she explained. She was really pushing all my buttons now. I had battled hard for my Platinum championship and I was going to be damned if she was going to sit there and belittle my federation and my status in said federation. I had had enough, nearing boiling point for sure. I took a deep breath to prevent going off on my mother. “Listen mother. I got to fly to California first thing in the morning. I’ll stay with my friends tonight and we’ll talk about this again when I return. I refuse to sit here and let you take potshots at your own son trying to make his father proud of him,” I said in a rather tense tone, holding back the rage boiling within. I watched as my mother slid back in her chair and stood up. She glared up at me. I could see she had worked up a retort, but it never came. Perhaps she thought anything she said would be unfair, perhaps she thought I wouldn’t listen anyhow and either way she was probably right as far as I was concerned. She turned around and left the kitchen giving him one quick glance on the way out. Angry and frustrated ran a hand over my face, but it didn’t wipe away the discontent I felt at this moment. I proceeded to my room where I would back my duffel bag. Brian would let me stay with him for the evening and tomorrow I would catch the flight to California where I would attempt to defend my hard earned Platinum Title against Rob Zone.
