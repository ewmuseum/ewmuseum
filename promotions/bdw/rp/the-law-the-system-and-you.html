Above the Law, the System and You... Emilio Dervante BDW Roleplay #9 Date: 5.26.04 Location: Somewhere Who wants it more? Three men step in the ring at BDW's first pay per view a triumphant return. This man will overcome a brutal assault and hell of a match. This man will also be immortalized forever in BDW history. The honor that comes with the belt is more than anyone can image. Few realize that World Champion is more than just a belt. Emilio Dervante is one of them. He will step over, crush, beat any man or even have sex with an ugly... No... An OK looking chick to get it. Emilio Dervante is shallow, cold hearted, arrogant and worst of all... no longer a cruiserweight. An emerald green turf and magnificently bright blue sky off sets it all. The sun sits in the sky and has a uncanny resemblance to a spark in the eye of an inspired artist. It was a day that even the dorkiest of geeks would pack up their XBOX and bring it outside. One of those days were people throw caution to the wind and purposely start fire and dance around them naked... It was just one of those days. Anyone in his or her right mind would be outside but someone wasn't. The entire population was most likely outside, but not Emilio Dervante. He was in his stupid apartment with bigger problems. He had just returned home from the police station... what and why he was charged is all in the story. Emilio Dervante was coming off his Static victory and wanted a well-deserved celebration, and that is what he got. SUNDAY NIGHT / EARLY MONDAY MORNING... 3AM Emilio Dervante had just defeated the Underground King and even participated in the farewell party for him. But as King's girlfriend ran out after him, she caught Emilio's eye. Emilio followed and managed to get a little closer to her, eventually taking her to the same apartment he sat in pondering it all, but they were just talking. 'It's late... I'd better get home,' The young woman said breaking the silence at the beginning of the transcript. 'No, c'mon stay a bit longer,' Emilio met the woman as she stood up and grabbed her coat. 'I really ought to be getting home. He will suspect something... even if we didn't do anything.' She stuck by her guns and began to open the door. 'Exactly, we haven't done anything... yet' Emilio slowly shut the door in front of her and as she back stepped he menacingly stalked her. She screamed but no one heard a thing. Downstairs was WAH MEE's Laundry mat and between the sound of the packed place and Emilio's muffling... no one heard a thing. He shoved her down to the bed and ripped off her shirt and the skimpy skirt still followed. She struggled, throwing kicks and punches but as she was given a gap, Emilio nailed her with a right, that not even the toughest person could've withstood. She stopped moving and everything went black. Emilio is seen from the awakening eyes of the woman, pulling up a pair of dress khakis. Emilio looks down and grins, but then sends a backhand across the face of the woman and again nothing but black. The same slowly awakening scene is opening again, some random man now opens the seen on top of her, but a slam of the door and Emilio Dervante rushes in and pulls the man off the woman. 'Bastard. Why the hell are you in my apartment?' Emilio roars as he nails the Asian-looking male and sends him to the floor. Emilio rushes to the woman on the bed and sits her up, holding her close and rocking her back and forth... the same woman he slapped and probably raped. 'Don't worry. It's all over now, no one is going to hurt you.' Emilio comforts the woman in his hands as he continues to rock back and forth. Emilio pulls out a cell phone and after a few minutes of talking and maybe a quarter of an hour of waiting the police arrive. They take the Asian man into custody and decide to call a rescue unit to take the young woman to the hospital. As the cops and EMTs leave the apartment Emilio plops on the bed and puts his head in his hands. He looks up and his remorseful face soon turns into an evil, sinister face. 'Sometimes, the best things in life are free...' MONDAY AFTERNOON, 1:21 PM Emilio Dervante had recieved a call by the hospital authorities and was asked to come down. While the BDW superstar arrived, officers cuffed him. 'Emilio Dervante, you are under arrest for the rape of Ms. Samantha Lynch.' The officer stated and then continued to speak and give Emilio his Miranda rights. Meanwhile a nurse explained the situation to BDW staff. 'Yes, there was semen matching Emilio Dervante's DNA, that we had on file, as well as pubic hair from another male.' She talked straigh tinto the camera, while Emilio was being dragged away by the cops. MONDAY EVENING, 7:04 PM Emilio Dervante was now sitting in a room with his lawyer. Emilio is sporting a pinstripe dress shirt complete with shirt cutoffs and suspenders. He actually looks like this should be filmed in black and white with a copyright of 1951. Since this is America, court systems are very speedy and a court date as well as the judge have been picked for the job, I wish. An officer walks into the room and Emilio stands to greet the cop. 'You did good man... She is hot!' The officer commends Emilio on his victim's looks. 'Anyway you got Judge McGrady so there is nothing but smooth sailing,' Emilio shakes his head and nods towards the table where a rectangular box sits, recording the whole conversation. '...for her that is. McGrady is one of the strict judges of them all. She is very hot as in mad and your type of convict disgusts me. If it wasn't for this being watched I'd kick your ass right here.' Emilio gives a thumbs up and then the door opens again and another cop pokes his head into the room. 'Mr. Dervante, your time is up.' Emilio gets up and walks out of the room as he slaps the previous officer's hand and acts as though it slapped him in the face. Emilio is walked out of the room with the same arrogant girn he walked in with and the cop recognizes the grin. 'I hate it when celebrities think they are above the law. We'll see how above the law you are when you can't wrestle at The Return One.' The officer says almost like a 2nd grader who stole a cookie from another classmate. 'You're a rookie huh?' Emilio asks as he is being almost dragged down the hal lby the fast paced cop. 'Yeah, I just got outta school a few weeks ago... What's it to you?' The same tone is used as the officer answers Emilio. 'Trust me, I'll be wrestling Sunday, and afterwards I am going to have a night out... At your expense rook.' Emilio says as he pulls away from the officer and heads out of the station with a very, I'm better than you, grin. As Emilio steps into a black short limo that was waiting outside for him the scene slowly fades black, ending on the vanity license plate labeled 'HIGHLFE'.
