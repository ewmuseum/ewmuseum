Finding A New Life Jackson Kable BDW Roleplay #17 Date: June 12 Location: Some Guys Building What should I do to my life? Should, I kill myself? Should I commit sucide. No, I needed to do something more than those two things put together. I needed help, I needed help to see where my future was going in BDW, and in all of my life. I needed help from someone that could tell me, if my future was a good one, or if it was a pile of trash. My mom, to talk to? no, she wouldn't give a shit about me. My dad? no, he died, and he wouldn't give a shit either. The priest? nah, that guy is the saddest sack on the planet. The only reason he became a priest, was to actually have something to do with his life. He had threatened the town to kill himself, but the cops talked him out of it. I needed someone, to hold onto and say, I'm not afraid to be by you, and I'm not afraid to do stuff, with you. Who should I talk to? where was I going to find this guy? Who knows, maybe the drug store? maybe downtown theres a guy, that could help me, beome a better person than what I am. Look at me, I have hardly any clothes, which all of them are ripped. I was sitting on my sofa, in the middle of my living room, talking to my self. i didn't know what to do, or anything. I just sat there. It was pouring rain outside, with thunder booming acros the sky. It was late in the morning, maybe 5:00 in the morning. I didn't sleep at all last night, nor did I want to. I didn't want to do anything but lay down in a blanket and die. But that did not happen. I have taught myself mayn things, one being not to give up on my life. Because, if I did, I would just waste it all away, doing nothing. I got up off the ripped sofa, that was in the middle of my living room, and walked to the kitchen. I opened a drawer beside the fridge, and grabbed a phonebook. I needed someone to help me, and that person would be somewhere in this phone book. I opened the book and started flipping the pages. I stopped, and looked very closely to what the number was. I grabbed the phone off the wall and dialed thenumber that was in the phone book. Ring Ring Ring A guy answered. He had a very low voice, I could hardly hear because of the static on the phone. The phone book, layed on the counter, as I started to speak to the man on the other end of the phone. 'Hello' I said. I listened to the man speak. And then said bye. The man had told me where to go, and what to do to become a new person. I set the phone down on the counter and ran out of the kitchen. The drawer was still open, but I didn't care, I was going to become a new person. I opened the door, and ran out into the pouring rain. I started running down the street already soaking wet, water dripping from my hair. I turned the corner of the street and kept running. *10 Minutes Later* I was breathing really heavy as I walked into the front door of a little building. There was nothing inside, but a few couches, and a few people walked around with stuff in there hands. I started walking. I walked up some steps, and came to a door that read the name, 'Jack McCall' on it. I turned the door knob slowly as the scene started to fade.
