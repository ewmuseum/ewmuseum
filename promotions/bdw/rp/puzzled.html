Puzzled

Puzzled Brock RockHard BDW Roleplay #11 Date: 5/25/04 Location: [We find ourselves in a small white room. In the center of the room is a small wooden stool. Perched atop the school is BDW’s newest phenom, Brock Rockhard. Rockhard is donning baggy polo jeans and a white cut off T-shirt with the words “What of it?” in black block letters across his barrel like chest. Behind Rockhard is massive red BDW 3D wooden letters. Rockhard turns and looks at the letters calling attention to them with his thumb.] Rockhard: Do you like my simple little set up? [He pauses to allows everyone to see the arrangement.] Rockhard: First of all let me just tell my newfound fans out there to bare with me while I cut this promo. For one, this is the first promo I’ve ever actually cut with the exception of a trial promo I sent into BDW when I was trying to get a contract. On a side note, I’m all about what happens in the ring. I’ll be the first to admit I want the fanfare, but cutting promos comes a distant second to actually strutting my stuff in the squared circle. [Brock runs his hand through his brown locks with frosted blonde tips.] Rockhard: The One. I still can’t believe you’ve labeled yourself so unwisely Mr. Page. I mean with a name like The One what happens if you get crushed in a few matches. Do you change your name to the second one because quite frankly I don’t think you can name yourself the one unless you are truly the best wrestler or are destined to be the best wrestler and let’s be honest with yourself Jeremy, you’re neither. [Rockhard pauses and takes a deep breath. He smirks at the camera.] Rockhard: There’s some damn fine wrestlers in BDW Jeremy; JJ Jarone, Sean McClancy are the first two that come to mind, but you Jeremy you don’t come to mind. You may argue that you’re young and that you just broke into the scene like I did and you have plenty of time to grow, but again Page, I just don’t see it. You expect me to believe that you were just watching the tube in prison and all of a sudden decided to be a wrestler when you’re whole life at been about rock n roll? To each their own my father always used say, but I find it hard to believe that you’ll be able to keep up with me in the ring with all that smoke in your lungs. I’ll just take whatever beating you wish to throw my way and just outlast you Page. I can picture your frustrations now and I just kick out over and over again and you get tired and start coughing up a lung. [Rockhard’s goofy smirk turns to an evil snarl and his intense blue eyes stare harder into the camera.] Rockhard: It was easy for you to make fun of me Jeremy, but moderately classless to bring my mother into it. That’s okay though because most of us, unlike you and that Jackson Kable dude, live in the real world. I had to stare reality in the face when my father passed away and I had to take care of my mother and younger sister. You unlike so many before you [the sarcasm is dry] have had a drug problem and miraculously overcame in, turned your life around and became a professional wrestler. Where’s your probation officer Page? I’m certain you’d have one if you’re only 19 and you’ve spent 6 months in prison on a possession of cocaine charge. [Brock’s momentary anger turns to confusion as he puts both hands on the stool and rotates his torso, you can hear his back crack. He adverts his attention back to the camera and continues.] Rockhard: There’s something else that’s puzzling me Jeremy. You said you may not ever get to the top, but that you will be the great f**cking wrestler ever. That’s perhaps one of the most inane comments I’ve ever heard someone make. The great f**cking wrestler ever would sure be considered at the top would he not? The great f**cking wrestler would surely be a multi-time heavyweight title holder. I sincerely hope you cut another promo before our match Jeremy, because I’d like for you to clear that up for me. While you’re at it come up with something against me other than I suck because the only thing that’s going to be sucking after our match is said and done will be you due to all those cigarettes you smoke. [With that Brock stands up from the stool issues a ‘thanks guys’ to the BDW camera members that filmed him and spun around on his heel and leaves the shot.]
