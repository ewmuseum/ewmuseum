A House For Many

A Suprise For Many Co-Owner Jeffery BDW Roleplay #18 Date: 01/06/2005 Location: Shreveport, LA The birds are already chirping as the light barely gazes over the horizon. A few cars occupy the highway next to BreakDown Wrestling's thirteen story building. It's pretty early in the morning and the tempature is chilly enough to have a jacket on. At a nearby redlight you can hear an engine at high rpm's. If you take a look over you can see a Mitsubishi Eclipse alongside a Mustang GT. The engine's are roaring as the light is still red. Suddenly the light turns green and both cars are off. The Mustang sits in place for a moment waiting for his tires to catch traction. The Eclipse is already off the line and hauling down the highway. The Mustang is quick on his tail, but the race can't be finished due to traffic. 'Ha Ha!' you hear right beside your head. The camera jerks around and Owner Jeffery is standing staring at the two illegal street racing cars. He has a smile on his face as he stands with his arms crossed. 'That was a nice race, I would have liked to catch the whole thing. My guess is about fourth gear the Eclipse would have tore apart the Stang. Who knows though,' Owner Jeffery says as he pulls the keys out of his pocket. He turns to the two glass double doors and inserts the key off his key ring. He opens the door and makes his way into the building. The building his empty and very dark. Jeffery flips on a few switches and the lights have a very dim glow coming from them. After a few seconds they finally flicker on as the place illuminates. Jeffery leans over the secretary's desk to check for any messages. 'Two? That's all?' he says to himself. He taps the button and the messages begin to play. 'Hey Jeff! What's goin on man? I guess your not here yet, but give me a call as soon as possible. I'll give you a hint, Judgement Day is coming!' you hear a click as the phone hangs up. It then goes to the next message, 'Call me back, it's Apoc.' 'Yeah, he ain't changed,' Jeffery says erasing both messages. Jeffery then walks to the elevator doors and presses the call button. A ding is heard and Jeffery enters the elevator. He presses floor 11 and the elevator makes that real quick jolt and starts taking him up. Another real loud ding is heard and the doors open, he turns around the corner and flips on a few more lights. He grabs his keys once more and begins to insert them in the keyhole. 'What tha....' Jeffery says. Jeffery notices the office door is already cracked open with the lights on. He slowly pushes the door open and pokes his head in. He looks around and sees nothing. Everything is in it's normal position and nothing is out of the ordinary. Jeffery shrugs his shoulder and makes his way to the desk. He grabs the chair and goes to spin it around, but before he can it spins around. 'The King' Steve Owens sits in the chair with one leg resting on top of the other. His fingertips pressed together and his usual arrogant smile. His large physique bulges through his black muscle shirt. He stands up and removes his sun shades and extends a hand to Jeffery. 'How's it been boss?' 'The King' Steve Owens says to Jeffery. Jeffery shakes his hand, 'Not bad.' 'The King' moves aside and points to Jeffery's chair for Jeffery to take a seat. Jeffery takes a seat and sort of leans back staring at 'The King.' 'Well what do you want, I know you and you're not here to socialize?' Jeffery asks. 'The King' leans against the desk and crosses one leg over the other and puts his shades back on. 'Yeah, you're right. Just wanted to talk about the last time BreakDown Wrestling was here. The fans, the smiling faces, the...title belts. You know I beat Judgement and that title should be around my waist. I earned my shot and was screwed out of it. It's back again and I want my shot.' 'The King' demands. Jeffery spins around in his chair and leans onto the desk. He slightly turns his head. 'OK, you get your shot on the first Sunday Night Static,' Jeffery says leaning back with a sigh. 'The King' Steve Owens leaps to his feet with a look of joy gleaming off of his face. He reaches down and shakes Jeffery's hand. Jeffery then nodes and points to the door. 'I have work to do, man,' Jeffery says. 'The King' acknowledges and makes his way to the door. He opens the door and closes it behind him. The camera then cuts back to Jeffery who is now facing his computer. 'He just doesn't know against who, or how many,' the camera then slowly fades.
