What this is A roster card from BDW’s own 2004 site, as it stood in the operator’s 8 June 2004 site backup. The card’s closing out-of-character Handler Information block is left out.

Andrew Irving Age: 20 Birth Date: July 11, 1983  Hometown: Miami, Florida Height: 6'0"  Weight: 245 lbs Affiliation: Heel  Theme Music: "Princes of the Universe" by Queen Wrestling Style: Technical Set-up Move: The Pre-Irvingizer Set-up Move Description: Kick to the midsection, followed by a DDT Finishing Move: The Irvingizer Finishing Move Description: Moonsault off the top rope Entrance: "Princes of the Universe" by Queen plays, and blue and white pyro shoots off at each side of the ramp. Irving comes out with his arms raised in the air.  He struts down to the ring and climbs the steps. He brushes the dirt off his suit and straightens his tie as he raises his arms and screams at the top of his lungs.
Biography: Andrew Irving made his fortune off commercials as a young boy. Constantly made fun of for these commercials, he lies and says that he was a stunt man.  Irving divorced his parents when he was 18 and moved into a grand mansion. He decided to buy his way into wrestling school. When his methods were called unorthodox, Irving simply bought the school and graduated himself. Now he is in BDW in order to show the world that his unorthodox mix of high flying moves and technical moves will win him a place in the high ranks of the wrestling world.
