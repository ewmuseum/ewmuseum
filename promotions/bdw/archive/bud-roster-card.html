What this is A roster card from BDW’s own 2004 site, as it stood in the operator’s 8 June 2004 site backup. The card’s closing out-of-character Handler Information block is left out.

Brock Usher Daniels Age: 21 Birth Date: August 14, 1983  Hometown: South Beach, Miami, Florida Height: 6'1" Weight: 210 lbs Affiliation: Neutral Theme Music: "Ride Of Your Life" by Neurotica Wrestling Style: All-Rounder Set-up Move: Blunt Out Set-up Move Description: Acid Drop Finishing Move: Blunt Suicide Finishing Move Description: A back flip into a 5 star frog splash. Entrance: The lights in the arena start to flick and a loud bang is heard as a women's voice is heard over the PA."Welcome to High Time Industries sponsored by the BUD" You better be ready to get burnt out by the future and to go for the ride of your life" A picture of BUD shows up on the titontron and his video starts to play as "Ride Of Your Life" by Neurotica plays. Smoke fills the entrance way as the fans start to boo the man known simply as the future BUD. The music gets louder as sparks start to fly out of the ramp turning into a fire work of green and black. BUD finally walks out from the smoke and has a joint in one hand and a glass ash tray in the other. He takes a few puffs and  puts the joint in the ash tray as he blows smoke rings. He enters the ring. He puts his joint and glass ash tray on the corner of the ring. He grabs a mic and starts to talk as the highness of his eyes are seen." I am ready! Get this chump, that is my opponent out here so he can get embarrassed!"
Biography: Originally from Jamaica, B-U-D had an unusual habit at a young age. This was buying, smoking, and selling pot. He moved to Florida when he was fifteen years old and the habit continued. He was a very good high school wrestler. He got a partial scholarship at Florida State University to become a wrestler. He needed money to pay for the rest of his college, so he joined Schmidtknecht Wrestling Academy to make a few bucks here and there. He was learning a different style of wrestling now. He kicked his addiction to drugs for his college days which did not last too long. Only one year when in the ACC wrestling finals he broke a kids neck. He snapped and did a pile driver. He was kicked out of NCAA wrestling forever. Therefore his scholarship at Florida State was revoked. He went back to his addiction, and needs money today to buy. He now wrestles from fed to fed looking for a place to call home. He then ended up in 360 wrestling where he held the cruiserweight title for 3 months until he hurt his back doing the first ever blunt suicide off of a 25 foot cell.
