Sunday Night Static Airs Live For The Fith Consecutive Time! Posted by May 16, 2004 by Co-Owner Shawn Veto on May 18, 2004 Sunday Night Static hit the air live again for the fifth consecutive time, and what a show. The ratings shot up this Sunday Night making this weeks Static incredibly great. The first rounds of the BDW World Heavyweight Championship tournament is now in the books. Pretty Boy Evans and JJ Jarone finaly get to meet eachother in the ring, did Evans tear Jarone apart like he said he could? The Rebel took on Jackson Kable in what is said to be one of the greatest performances in a long time. Also Brock Rockhard makes his debut targeting 'The One' Jeremy Page. New Co-Owner Assigned to BDW Posted by May 15, 2004 by Co-Owner Shawn Veto on May 18, 2004 Earlier this week BreakDown Wrestling founder and now co-owner Jeffery sat down in an interview with Shawn Veto to discuss a possible spot at co-ownership. Not too long after the interview media released the news that Jeffery signed Shawn Veto new co-owner. Veto a 36 year old Massachusetts native is sure to have alot to bring to BDW on its rise to the top. Static Aired on time fourth week in a row, Static Card posted! Posted by May 09, 2004 by Co-Owner Jeffery on May 18, 2004 Sunday Night Static aired on time for the fourth week in a row. The show was a huge success as usual, with lots of action. Co-Owner Jeffery had a huge talk with Underground King. Apocalypse went crazy on Pretty Boy Evans, and more!! The Sunday Night Static card is also up and on it's way to next week. Sunday Night Static Aired and Sunday Night Static Card posted! Posted by May 02, 2004 by Lacey Phillips on May 18, 2004 A huge Sunday Night Static with major things happening. It isn't a show you want to miss. Four great matches and a night full of champions. Sunday Night Static was a huge success and next week will be even better as the next card has been posted.
