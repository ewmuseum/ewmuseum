BreakDown Wrestling Launches New Site!

14 October 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A BDW news item from the fed’s first run, dated 14 October 2003, kept in the operator’s 8 June 2004 site backup.
BreakDown Wrestling Launches New Site! Posted by Lacey Phillips, on October 14, 2003 Only moments ago, Co-Owner Jeffery announces the launch of his new website for BreakDown Wrestling. Several weeks before rumors rose about the new site, but no one was for sure. Suddenly, Jeffery announced the launch and was very pleased with how it turned out. The old site has been saved, and could at anytime go back up, if Jeffery isn't satisfied with the new site. In a press conference concerning this issue, Jeffery had this to say: "Currently, I am more than pleased with the outcome of the site. I wasn't expecting the outcome of the site, but after many hours of planning and creating, I finally finished," Co-Owner Jeffery stated.
Jeffery single handidly created the site, with his spare time and did an amazing job. He says things are more navigatable, and a lot better looking than the before site. Things could change, but for now I am Lacey Phillips signing out.
