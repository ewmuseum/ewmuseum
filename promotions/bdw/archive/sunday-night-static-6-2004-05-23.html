Sunday Night Static (6th)

23 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is BDW’s own results page for the sixth Sunday Night Static, the World Title tournament’s second round, booked for 23 May 2004; the fed’s own news of 25 May says it aired late, on the Monday night.

The show started with the usual pyros and introductions of the announcers. The announcers talked about tonight's show, before they were interuppted by Co-Owner Jeffery. Jeffery hyped the pay-per-view and told everyone about the World Title match and the other title matches. Co-Owner Jeffery left the stage and the camera went to the first match.

Singles Match Flawless Vs Andrew Irving

Flawless and Andrew Irving entered the ring and Andrew Irving managed to get the upperhand. They went on a power struggle most of the match and Andrew Irving ended up winning. He tossed Flawless around the ring before going for the first pin.

The first pin was unsuccessful and Flawless tried his hand on offense. He battled with fight and passion, but it didn't matter Irving got right back on the attack and sent Flawless out of the ring. The referee almost counted out Flawless, but he managed to get himself back into the ring.

Andrew Irviing taunted a bit giving Flawless a chance to get the upperhand. He took Irving down with some technical moves, before locking in a single leg Boston crab. Irving was close to tapping out before he reached the bottom rope and the referee forced him to break the hold.

Irving gets back to his feet, but limping and Flawless tries to take advantage. He runs at Irving, but Andrew Irving catches him with a shot to the midsection followed by a DDT. He then makes his way to the top ropes and leaps off with a moonsault, that he calls The Irvingizer. That ended the match, one, two, three!

Four Way Over the Top Battle Royal Broken Championship #1 Contendership Julio Vs Methid Vs Adam White Vs Revelation

All four guys made it to the ring, but Revelation charged at Methid trying to get him out quick to make him lose his first match. Adam White ignores Revelation and Methid and attacks Julio. Julio doesn't let it happen and he lands lefts and rights all over the place. Meanwhile, Revelation has Methid on the mat stomping away onto his chest.

Adam White is stcuk in the turnbuckle catching hands, and boots. Revelation sees what is going on and lands a forearm to the back of Julio hanging him over the top rope. Revelation grabs the legs of Julio and tries to throw him over the top rope, but Adam White leaps into the air with a flying bulldog, but Revelation catches him and drops him over the top rope to be eliminated.

Julio is now back in action and starts wrestling with Revelation, but before he can get the upperhand Methid goes to help. They double vertical suplex Revelation, but Julio doesn't like help, so he attacks Methid knocking him into the ropes. Julio spins around for a diving clothesline, but Methid ducks and sends him over the top rope, eliminatiing him.

Now all that is left in the ring is Revelation and Methid. Revelation is finally getting to his feet, before Methid realizes that Revelation is there. Methid then runs at Revelation and gets on the attack. He whips him into the ropes and knocks him to the mat with a shoulder block, but it goes the other way and Methid falls. Revelation gets to the top rope for his finisher, but Methid runs up the turnbuckle and dropkicks Revelation off the top rope sending him sailing into the air eliminating Revelation for the win.

Singles Match  Brock Rockhard Vs "The One" Jeremy Page

Brock Rockhard was trying to make his way to the ring, but Jeremy Page attacked him before he could make it to the ring. Jeremy Page really laid it down onto Rockhard slinging him into and steel object he could find.

Brock was pretty wore out before they finally made it to the ring and the match started, but Jeremy Page stayed on the attack. He slamed Brock into the turnbuckles and hit him with a few clotheslines.

Brock then cames back with a burst of energy and dropped both me to the mat with a ddt. They both laid on the mat lifeless as the refree tried counting them out. Brock suprisingly was the first to his feet, but Jeremy Page wasn't far behind. Jeremy Page sent Brock into the referee knocking him to the mat.

Out of the crowd with a chair Co-Owner Jeffery climbed in and told Jeremy Page to pick Brock Rochard up. As he did Co-Owner Jeffery to Page to move. When he did Co-Owner Jeffery send a loud crack throughout the arena onto the skull of Jeremy Page. The fans booed as he ticked Jeremy Page. Co-Owner Jeffery then left the ring and Brock piked up the win.

Singles Match Apocalypse Vs Pretty Boy Evans

Pretty Boy Evans Wins

Singles Match World Title Tournament Match 2nd Round Underground King Vs Emilio Dervante

Emilio Dervante Wins

After the match Co-Owner Jeffery brought UnderGround King to his office and gave him his pink slip. UnderGround King then was escorted to the exit, but not before Co-Owner Jeffery punched UnderGround King to the concrete outside the arena.

Singles Match World Title Tournament Match 2nd Round JJ Jarone Vs Arik Caine

Draw

The camera quickly cuts to the back as we find Annie Leanan, the valet of Sean McClancy, standing in front of a mirror. She's wearing a long sleeved shirt with the ends of the sleeves billowing out with a lace cuff. The top ties at the front, allowing a bit of her cleavage and the black lace of her barely there bra to show, a black pvc miniskirt, stockings and just over the knee boots complete her ensemble.

She's just finishing up adjusting her 'assets' in the mirror as there is a knock on the door.

Annie - Yes?

Sean McClancy's muffled voice from the other side of the door responds.

McClancy: Are ye decent?

Annie smiles and turns her head to the door.

Annie- As decent as I need t' be.

The door opens, and standing there is Sean McClancy wearing a black T-shirt with a sylized celtic knot dragon on the front of it in red, with the words "No Raísôn Dêtré" in white over it. He looks in and Annie turns to join him walking down to the ring.

She joins him and they both turn to head down out to the arena.

McClancy: Ye'd better damn well remember. Ye don'na touch Kable 'er th' Ref until after th' match. Ye don'na do it durin' the match.

Annie: I understand.

McClancy: Ye really goin' t' wear tha' down there?

Annie: Damn straight.

McClancy: Whatever ye be wantin'.
