What this is BDW's own rules page — a roleplay-quality-over-quantity policy and its own stated 75% roleplay / 25% angle split.
Below are a list of rules made and set up by the BreakDown Wrestling Staff and you are expected to follow these rules at all time. Failure to comply with the following rules below will result in the following: 1. One Week Suspension 2. One Month Suspension 3. Fired and Added to Black List BreakDown Wrestling runs like and e-federation should be because BreakDown Wrestling enforces its rules and guidelines, so that BreakDown Wrestling can be enjoyed by the staff, employers, and fans. Below you will find the list of rules that BreakDown Wrestling employers are required to follow. 1. BreakDown Wrestling judges its roleplays by quality, not quantity. When the group of staff members judges each match quality, not quantity is considered in deciding the ending factor of each match. 2. If you are not satisfied with the outcome of your previous match, contact a BreakDown Wrestling Staff member either through e-mail or other means of contact, but NEVER on the BreakDown Wrestling Boards. If the complaint is legitimate then a re-match will be in order, if complaining becomes a problem one of the above three actions will be taken. Also, BreakDown Wrestling is 75% Rp and 25% angle, so keep that in mind after the outcome of a match. 3. You may never use another e-wrestler or any head staff member without permission from the character owner themselves. To contact them use e-mail or any other means available. If this happens, depending on what you do, you will either be warned, suspended, or even as far as being fired. 4. Absolutely no racial or gender discrimination at anytime while here in BreakDown Wrestling. It will immediately be erased and be considered in applicable. Sexual slurs are allowed, but to an extent, you know your limits and do not get carried away or you will either be suspended or fired. 5. All vulgar language is allowed at all times during roleplay. Everyone hears cuss words and you do not have to bleep out your curse words, so to speak. It is up to you whether you want to bleep them out, but it is not required. 6. To be accepted in BreakDown Wrestling you must come up with your own character name. Everything involving your character must be totally original. You can not use celebrity e-wrestler names or celebrity story lines, BreakDown is a totally original e-fed. 7. There will absolutely not fighting on the boards what so ever. If any arguments are started then it will result in immediate termination of your contract. If there is any problem of any sort, handle it through e-mail or some other form, but not our boards. If the above rules are followed, then you will have a great time here in BreakDown Wrestling. If there is violation of the rules your time here in BreakDown Wrestling will either be horrible, or very very short. Its up to you to follow the rules all we do is enforce them.
