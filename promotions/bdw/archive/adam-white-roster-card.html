“Mr. Essential” Adam White — BDW roster card

What this is A roster card from BDW’s own 2004 site, as it stood in the operator’s 8 June 2004 site backup. The card’s closing out-of-character Handler Information block is left out.

"Mr. Essential" Adam White Age: 27   Birth Date: March 20, 1977  Hometown: Boston, Massachusetts  Height: 6'2" Weight: 245 lbs Affiliation: Heel Theme Music: "Sic Transit Gloria...Glory Fades" by Brand New Wrestling Style: All-Rounder Set-up Move: Must Have Always Set-up Move Description: Double Arm DDT Finishing Move: The Essential Finishing Move Description: Picks up opponent over his shoulders, like a death valley driver, but he tucks his opponent's head in as he drops him down to the mat, dropping him completely on the back of his head and neck, instead of the back.  The neck takes the biggest impact, stunning the opponent momentarily.
Entrance: Light brown hair down to his shoulders, very muscular for a man of his size.  He wears a wrestling singlet that is a lime green color with black trim.  On the front is a black outline of Adam's face and upper torso and on the back it reads, "Yours, Mine, The Essential" in black lettering.  He has black wrestling boots that read Adam White on them.  "Adam" being on the outside of each boot and "White" being on the inside.  The boot lettering is in the same lime green color as the singlet.  He waits a few seconds after his music starts to walk out from the back.  As he walks out, the word ESSENTIAL flashes on the video screen.  He puts his arms in air and does a complete turn while yelling at the crowd.  He constantly insults the crowd on his way to the ring.  He walks up the ring steps and climbs through the second and third ropes and waits for his opponent. 
Biography: Adam White grew up in a very rich part of Boston, MA.  He pretty much was given everything as a child, not having to lift a finger for anything.  He was the so-called jock of his high school and dated the head cheerleader.  Being the typical high school kid, he picked on the nerds and the geeks, then went off to college, The University of Texas, on a baseball scholarship, and things changed.  He thought he could rule the school, but was in for quite a culture shock.  UT wasn't the little preppy private school Adam was used to and quickly was taught the way of life in the south.  He all of a sudden wasn't better than everyone and even the hottest girls were passing him up.  Then on a drunken night at a frat party he opened his mouth up for the last time.  A few two many drinks and mouthful of insults later, Adam found himself in a bloody mess in some hospital, puking his guts out.  He was beaten up at the party.  Of course, this enraged Adam.  Still not believing what was happening to him, he went back to college, but as word of the fight grew, he lost his scholarship.  The first time something in his life had been taken from him.  Adam left school and returned home to Boston.  His parents, finally realizing exactly what Adam needed in life, gladly accepted his homecoming and even bought him a very expensive condo in the Back Bay of Boston, but that would be the last time Adam would get anything from his parents.  He had to make his own money, and survive on his own for the first time in his life, but his little rich boy attitude hadn't left him and it led him to the world of wrestling.
