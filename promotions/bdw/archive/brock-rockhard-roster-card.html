Brock Rockhard Age: 21 Birth Date: August 18, 1982 Hometown: Austin, Texas Height: 6'4" Weight: 260 lbs Affiliation: Face Theme Music: "Burn" Finch Wrestling Style: All-Arounder Set-up Move: Falling Inverted DDT Set-up Move Description: Falling Inverted DDT Finishing Move: Boston Crab Finishing Move Description: Boston Crab Entrance: ["Burn" by Finch blares throughout the PA system as the curtains part and "The Prototype" Brock Rockhard steps out. He thrusts his hands upwards towards the heavens and roars intensely before promptly making his way to the ring slapping a few hands on the way. When he reaches the ring he slides in and makes his way to his corner.] Biography: Brock is THE prototype for a wrestler. His father was a successful indy wrestler who passed away at a very early age. His proudest moments were watching his son inside the squared circle and while Brock inevitably has a long road ahead of him he insists it is his destiny to become the best wrestler ever and thus he has left his home and his widowed mother behind in order to endure the tests that both life and wrestling have for him. His power, speed, and knowledge of wrestling are unrivaled for someone his age and his body type and there's no doubt that his heart is unparalled by any man; however, Brock does not know what cards life will lay before him...One lucky federation will get to watch as the youngster develops and grows into a man and possibly one of the greatest wrestlers ever; however, that federation may also be an end before he even begins, because it's certain he's a ticking timebomb waiting to explode.
