BreakDown Wrestling News, April–June 2004

4 April – 4 June 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is BDW’s own news page as it stood in the operator’s 8 June 2004 site backup: ten dated items, newest first, from the roster-building of April 2004 to the signing of Chris Rock on 4 June.

Returning Legend Signs to BDW! June, 04, 2004 by Lacey Phillips BreakDown Wrestling administrators have just released breaking news to the media that they have signed a wrestling legend coming off the injury list. 32 year old Chris Rock is knows around the underground wrestling promotions as a rising star waiting to get his shot at the pro's. Chris Rock famous in JJ Jarones older promotion the United Wrestling Association, he held multiple championships, and is a two time World Champion. One underground promotion reporter quoted "Chris Rock reminds me of JJ Jarone, both have the fight to keep strong in the ring, and both know they won't go down until they have no more fight left in the. Chris Rock deserves to be in the spot light, he fought hard to train, and heal the spinal injury at the same time.".
Chris Rock injured his spine during a three way bout for the UwA World Heavyweight Championship. One opponent lifted Chris Rock and landed a devastating pulling piledriver form the second rope which had fatal results. Rock was sentenced to three years down time, and his career was just about over. One fan quoted, "Chris Rock fought hard to be the best, and his goal was to make it to the top. One injury just like that kept him out of the ring, and pulled him away from grabbing his goal.".
We caught up with Chris Rock earlier this week but he had nothing to say to the camera. Off camera Chris Rock said, "I'm returning to the ring, my home, my job, my life.", then rushed away with agents and security in
The First Pay-Per-View, The Return I aired! June, 01, 2004 by Co-Owner Jeffery After many hours unaired due to technical difficulties, I, Co-Owner Jeffery forked over a lot of money and time to get the show aired live. Many disappointed people, but after the show went up it was a huge sucess. The fans loved it as well as the other superstars. There is a lot of action, so be sure and catch the re-cap. Also, the Static Card was announced, so be sure and check it out to see who wrestles who this Sunday.
Static Aired and The Return I Card sent out! May 25, 2004 by Lacey Phillips The show aired late Monday night due to some technical difficulties. After more than half of the roster gone this week, apparently, the show wasn't to great. For the first time the show was late, but The Return is still scheduled for this Sunday live in none other than Madision Square Garden. The titles are on the line, and even some people's jobs.
Static Aired on time fourth week in a row, Static Card posted! May 09, 2004 by Co-Owner Jeffery Sunday Night Static aired on time for the fourth week in a row. The show was a huge success as usual, with lots of action. Co-Owner Jeffery had a huge talk with Underground King. Apocalypse went crazy on Pretty Boy Evans, and more!! The Sunday Night Static card is also up and on it's way to next week.
Sunday Night Static Aired and Sunday Night Static Card posted! May 02, 2004 by Lacey Phillips A huge Sunday Night Static with major things happening. It isn't a show you want to miss. Four great matches and a night full of champions. Sunday Night Static was a huge success and next week will be even better as the next card has been posted.
The Second Live Static Aired! Owen Steel Injured! Static Card Posted! April 26, 2004 by Co-Owner Jeffery The second Sunday Night Static aired on schedule and the fans enjoyed it very much. Lots of carnage was created in the BreakDown Wrestling ring. Heads were turned, and heads were bleeding, but the fans yelled at every second. 
Owen Steel sustained a small injury and isn't sure when he may be able to return. BreakDown Wrestling wishes him the best of luck in his recovery and hopes he can return soon.
The third Sunday Night Static is ready to be aired as the card quickly goes up after Sunday Night Static. This week is expected to be the best yet.
 Second Static Card Posted! April 19, 2004 by Lacey Phillips Next Sunday won't have a big show, but big things will happen. Only having three matches this week here in BreakDown Wrestling, Jeffery is still confident that BreakDown Wrestling will continue to impress the public. He expects more applications soon, if not Co-Owner Jeffery said he can work with the amazing staff he has.
First Show Aired, Lost Superstar, & Re-Grouping! April 18, 2004 by Co-Owner Jeffery As scheduled Sunday Night Static was aired at 7:00 p.m. central time. The show was a huge success with huge suprises. One man loses his job, but who? A former BreakDown Wrestling group re-united live early tonight, but which one? Catch the recap, of the Sunday Night Static results. BreakDown Wrestling also bought more air time on their HBO network to have another show. The show is scheduled to open Wednesday and is entitled, "Sanctiuary." The show will have an interview with a BreakDown Wrestling superstar, a re-cap of the show, and a top 5 list. The show will be weekly, and aired at 6:00 p.m. central time, and 12:00 p.m. central time for an encore presentation. The Last Contract Was Signed, The First Card Was Announced! April 6, 2004 by Co-Owner Jeffery Mid-afternoon today Apocalypse signed the last remaining contract needed for BreakDown Wrestling to announce their first card. Now that Jeffery has his last application to have the show, he soon after posted the card. The show is scheduled to be the 18th of April giving everyone to two weeks to get ready for their matches before the show aired. Jeffery wishes all of you the best of luck, break the house down.
With The Roster Count At 6, BDW Gets Closer To First Show! April 4, 2004 by Lacey Phillips Jeffery signed his sixth superstar mid afternoon on Saturday. With six superstars occupying the lockeroom, we are just two superstars shy of having enough talent to have our first Sunday Night Static. It was reported that the last two superstars are in the negoatiating stage of signing with BreakDown Wrestling. If everything goes as planned, we are just two weeks away from the first Sunday Night Static.
