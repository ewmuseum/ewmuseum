What this is A roster card from BDW’s own 2004 site, as it stood in the operator’s 8 June 2004 site backup. The card’s closing out-of-character Handler Information block is left out.

Pretty Boy Evans Age: 25 Birth Date: 03/21/1979   Hometown: Beverly Hills, CA Height: 6'3" Weight: 275 lbs Affiliation: Heel  Theme Music: "Porno Creep" by KoRn Wrestling Style: Technical Set-up Move: Knee Breaker Set-up Move Description: To soften up the knee for his finisher, the figure four leglock, Pretty Boy hoists the opponents into the air and sends him crashing down on his knee.
Finishing Move: Figure Four Leglock  Finishing Move Description: Pretty Boy Evans grabs the opponents leg and drags him to the center of the ring.  He then proceeds to twist the opponent's legs around and locks in the figure four leglock!  He applies the pressure, watching the opponent scream, and occasionally grabbing the ring ropes for leverage.
  Entrance: The lights cut out and the arena goes black.  "Porno Creep" by Korn begins to play as a soft purple glow comes over the ringside area and smoke pours from the stage.  Pretty Boy Evans strolls through the curtains and stands on the stage.  The crowd roars with boos as the Pretty Boy blows kisses at the fans.  He prances down the ramp and climbs up the steps.  Pretty Boy steps through the ropes and twirls around in circles like a sissy.
Biography: Hailing from Beverly Hills, Pretty Boy Evans was born into wealth.  Spoiled beyond belief with cars, houses, and women, he believes himself to be the pretty man alive.  He is disgusted by ugly things and ugly people.  Thus, he joined BDW to hurt those he sees as below him.
