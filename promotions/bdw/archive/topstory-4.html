Second Static Card Posted! Posted by April 19, 2004 by Lacey Phillips on May 18, 2004 Next Sunday won't have a big show, but big things will happen. Only having three matches this week here in BreakDown Wrestling, Jeffery is still confident that BreakDown Wrestling will continue to impress the public. He expects more applications soon, if not Co-Owner Jeffery said he can work with the amazing staff he has. First Show Aired, Lost Superstar, & Re-Grouping! Posted by April 18, 2004 by Co-Owner Jeffery on May 18, 2004 As scheduled Sunday Night Static was aired at 7:00 p.m. central time. The show was a huge success with huge suprises. One man loses his job, but who? A former BreakDown Wrestling group re-united live early tonight, but which one? Catch the recap, of the Sunday Night Static results. BreakDown Wrestling also bought more air time on their HBO network to have another show. The show is scheduled to open Wednesday and is entitled, 'Sanctiuary.' The show will have an interview with a BreakDown Wrestling superstar, a re-cap of the show, and a top 5 list. The show will be weekly, and aired at 6:00 p.m. central time, and 12:00 p.m. central time for an encore presentation. The Last Contract Was Signed, The First Card Was Announced! Posted by April 6, 2004 by Co-Owner Jeffery on May 18, 2004 Mid-afternoon today Apocalypse signed the last remaining contract needed for BreakDown Wrestling to announce their first card. Now that Jeffery has his last application to have the show, he soon after posted the card. The show is scheduled to be the 18th of April giving everyone to two weeks to get ready for their matches before the show aired. Jeffery wishes all of you the best of luck, break the house down.
