"The One" Jeremy Page Age: 19 Birth Date: July 21, 1984 Hometown: San Francisco, California Height: 6'1"  Weight: 230 lbs Affiliation: Heel Theme Music: "Ambush" by Sepultura Wrestling Style: Technical  Set-up Move: Almost There Set-up Move Description: Out of nowhere Superkick Finishing Move: The Last Page  Finishing Move Description:  Elevated Texas Cloverleaf, while putting his knee on his opponents head
Entrance: The fans are quiet inside the arena, just then you hear a voice over... When You Go Down, When You Go Down, MotherFucker When You Go Down You Go Down Fighting! When You Go Down, When You Go Down, MotherFucker When You Go Down You Go Down Fighting! Fireworks begin to explode around the curtain area as 'Ambush' by Sepultura pumps throughout the arena.  The fans begin to boo violently as 'The One' Jeremy Page steps out from behind the curtain area.  He stops at the top of the ramp, some strands of his long black hair dangle over his face as he smiles and looks over crowd.  He's sporting his typical wrestling gear, black cut off jean shorts, black boots, and his vintage Montana Jersey.  He makes his way down to the ring slowly as he gawks with the crowd a bit, fingers fly as he rolls into the ring underneath the bottom rope.  He stands up and holds his arms and puts his head down as Fireworks explode around the ring.  After the smoke clears he takes off his jersey and stands waiting for his opponent. 
Biography: Born July 21, 1984 to Pat and Michelle Page, Jeremy was a healthy eight pound baby.
His parents were wealthy so as a child Jeremy was givin' pretty much everything he asked for.  He didn't feel spoiled but his friends always thought he was, but hey they had the money to spend on him.
At the age of about six Jeremy was at the local mall when he saw a CD called Pantera : Cowboys From Hell.  He didn't really have a music taste at such a young age but he asked his mom for it anyway, he thought it had a cool name.  When he got home he popped it into his CD player, and from then on he wanted to grow his hair long, and be a musician.  Sex, Drugs and Rock N' Roll was in his blood.
His parents got him a Guitar and for hours he'd play his favorite music, ranging from Pantera, to AC/DC to Sepultura.
Now fourteen Jeremy was showing promising musical talent, he and his buddies put together a little band and would do little shows for a little bit of cash, but the money wasn't what he cared about, he just enjoyed the music.
As time wore on he became a little to obsessed with it and his buddies didn't like the way he was trying to run the entire group, so they left him, and went to venture on their own.  Jeremy was very disappointed as his future as a musician was very low now.
For a few months after his drug abuse got bigger and worse.  He'd come home a total mess, he'd forget most of his past as the drugs altered his brain.
His parents weren't to happy about his sudden drug abuse problem so they tried to help, but you always know that you can't tell someone NOT to do something.  Jeremy rebelled against his parents and did even more drugs, until one day he woke up, inside a cell at the Police Station.
The Police were charging him with Posession of Marijuana and Cocaine.  This is not what he needed at the moment, but he did it to himself.
While serving his six month jail sentence Jeremy was watching the Tv when he noticed something that struck his interest, it was Wrestling.  He watched more of the amateur wrestling then the Entertainment aspect but he liked them both.  He began to work out a little harder, and then as he was released from Jail he instantly went to a local wrestling gym where he hoped to learn the basics.
His trianer Jeff Costal saw a lot of potential in Jeremy from day one, almost like he was a natural.
A few years later Jeremy has broken apart from the little local shows and now is trying his luck in the big times...Sex, Drugs and Rock N' Roll turned into Blood, Sweat and Tears...
