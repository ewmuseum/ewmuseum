"IFWF Weekend Warfare results (announcement)"

17 March 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
These are the results of the very first IFWF card, Weekend Warfare for March 16th 1996. Although I have no idea how many lines it will encode into, I do know it is quite long. So decide now if you want to download it or not. You have been warned! The IFWF is a sub-federation of the GWF Global Wrestling Federation. This weekend the GWF officially activates the many 'sub federations' it has created to enable it handle the 100+ wrestlers in a more enjoyable to all concerned fashion. The IFWF is new, and run by me - I am also new. Any creative criticism or suggestion is very definately welcomed, so if you read it, find it crap and have suggestions for improvements, please tell me. If on the other hand, you just read it, find it crap, and just want to tell me that, please keep mum. If you are interested in the GWF, or any of the sub-federations of the GWF, please refer to their web page at the following URL: http://www.geocities.com/Colosseum/3311/ Thank you, and a very pleasant evening/day/whenever to you!
