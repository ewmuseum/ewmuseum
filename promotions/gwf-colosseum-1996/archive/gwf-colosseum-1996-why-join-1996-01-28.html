"JOIN THE GWF!!!!!!!!!!!!!!"

28 January 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
There are tons of fantasy wrestling leagues. Lots will tell you how "super," how "hardcore" or how whatever they are -- many fold, and even more suck. So what makes the GWF the league to join? Why should you sign up? Here are a few things to consider: Unlimited action! The GWF hosts two weekly cards and one monthly blockbuster event / pay-per-view. Things are run on a timely basis and the fed always makes an effort to get everything done on time. In addition, you receive updates, news, polls and interview mailings all week long, making sure you're always in the know. Member participation! The GWF members are some of the best, giving lots of interviews and coming up with great story angles. The great majority of monthly card matches are put together by players, and the fed heads are always willing to play out even our most complicated and diabolical stipulations for matches. Heh heh! And, since all GWF members are allowed up to two singles and one tag team, there is never a shortage of competition in the GWF! Great variety! The GWF has the greatest variety in e-wrestling. No type of wrestler dominates and all styles are given equal time so the fans get the most out of it. On a single card, you can see a superb, nail-biting technical duel, a wild steel cage encounter and a hardcore bloodbath -- and that's just how well the GWF's variety works. Brawlers, technical wrestlers, good, bad, neutral, flashy guys, high fliers, hardworkers, the bizarre, crazy managers and lots of different others make the GWF a great place to grapple. Fantastic title competition and tag team scene! With two singles titles (World Heavyweight and Intercontinental championship) and a tag team award (GWF World Tag Team belts) and tons of great talent, rest assured that many wrestlers are in the hunt for gold. While the tag scene in some leagues lacks, the team grappling competition in the GWF rules! Regular title defenses are required and, rest assured, you'll see high-quality championship bouts -- either one-on-one or specialty match. The GWF demands the highest quality! No screwjobs or prebooked matches! One of the best things about the GWF is that a match is really a match and you won't have to worry about someone interfering and ruining your fun. Oh, run-ins and foreign object attacks still occur, but you won't see them determining your match before the action starts in the GWF. You're also assured of getting a match, instead of some before-match attack which destroys your wrestler's chances. This is pretty important, for some leagues will allow attacks before matches or screwjob beginnings, trashing your wrestler's record and the excitement of seeing two wrestlers going at it. The GWF heads are committed to making sure everyone has lots of fun while still keeping the unpredictability that makes this league so special. A great web page! GWF Central is an essential component for GWF news, updates, cards, signup and plenty more. Put bookmarks on the GWF page, because this league really does knock out all frauds! And it is absolutely free! To join, point your web browser to GWF Central at: " http://www.geocities.com/Colosseum/3311 " or email [detail withheld]
