Global Wrestling Federation — News

"Help wanted!"

20 February 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Are you considering starting a fed, but lack the wrestlers or support? Do you have a small fed that is struggling and looking for backup? email [detail withheld] With over 130 original e-wrestlers, the GWF is e-wrestling's largest organization. However, it's slowed things enough that the GWF is looking to create a federation in the style of the old NWA, where member feds cooperate as a group, but with their own autonomy. Basically, GWF members would voluntariy join your fed, which would be a part of the GWF; however, they would wrestle exclusively in your fed and on the GWF monthly show. You are your own fed, which is allied with an alliance of feds. We ask that member feds be no larger than 45 wrestlers. think about it... members already involved with a fed and experience to join your organization, an alliance of feds which make up e-wrestling's only true federation, and the support and people to help you keep your fed alive even as others fold every day. experience is preferable, but if you want to learn and help, the GWF can connect you with others in that process too. The four GWF "member feds" would have their own titles (though no World or IC belts, for consistency of having a true federation of groups) and autonomy (your own weekly events, PPVs, etc.), but would be part of the GWF and its monthly Blockbuster Events. The federation-wide titleholders would wrestle on your cards as well as the wrestlers in your fed. some questions, if you are interested, which you should answer and email to [detail withheld] are: - do you know what you want to name your member fed? - how many titles and which ones? (remember: GWF still has World and IC belts) - what is your membership limit? - how many cards per week/month? - what help would you need (example: writing cards, sending out interviews, etc.)? - what fed help would you need? - when would you be ready to start? - would you be able to have a monthly meeting (on AOL or IRC) with the rest of the GWF heads to discuss ideas and needs? there is no right or wrong answer. it's just for info.
