"February Special Challenge matches: GWF stars face the pros!"

28 January 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Mark and Jim, GWF commissioners, have been negotiating for several weeks on and have been involved in extensive talks with many athletes to achieve a spectacular series. it's one way of spicing up GWF action and to boost already great ratings for Sunday Slam and Friday Frenzy during ratings sweeps month. Without delay, here we go:::::::: FEBRUARY SPECIAL CHALLENGE MATCHES! GWF STARS FACE THE PROS!!!!!!!!!! that's right -- the GWF has contracted Special Challenge matches with some of wrestling's most recognized faces in non-sanctioned contests against GWF stars!!! these matches will not count for or against your wrestlers' records -- they're strictly for fun. 9 legends have been contracted, and, at minimum, you'll be seeing a Special Challenge match at all eight weekly cards in February, but may see a lot more than that. to sign up, you must be a GWF member -- first come, first served. these matches will be run with lots of details (the moves of the pros here are carbon copies of their favorite moves in the ring) and you'll read a blow-by-blow summary of how these matches will go down! sooooooo, without further delay:::::: THE PROS -- TAG TEAMS!!!! THE ROAD WARRIORS / LEGION OF DOOM -- Hawk and Animal revolutionized the way we look at tag team wrestling with a style that is rough yet skillful. What hasn't been said about this team? Former champions in the UWF, AWA, WCW/NWA and WWF, The RWs are as good as it gets, and their reunion at the recent Clash of the Champions means we're in for more great action. After signing the open contract in the GWF, both guys have guaranteed they'll crush whoever their opponents are in the GWF Special Challenge. This one will be a must see! LEX LUGER & STING -- Two singles stars in their own right, the Total Package and the Stinger have had some tension between them since winning the WCW Tag Team titles, but they've inked the GWF deal with confidence. Not only are these guys physically powerful, but their wrestling skills promise to make a great match. Any contest with them will be a real test of greatness for any team! THE PUBLIC ENEMY (FLYBOY ROCCO ROCK & JOHNNY GRUNGE) -- TPE gained their greatest fame in Extreme Championship Wrestling, where they were five-time tag champions. Rocco and Johnny put the "extreme" in ECW with numerous bloody encounters. Their fearlessness in executing high-risk manuevers -- including Rocco's near-legendary dive onto opponents from atop a steel cage -- makes TPE a team to beat.. Now in the WCW, the masters of the table splash have issued a warning to all GWF tag teams for the Special Challenge! Any tag team squaring off against TPE is in for a total brawl!!! SINGLES -- GOOD GUYS / FACES!!!!! "THE HEARTBREAK KID" SHAWN MICHAELS -- Right off a big win in the WWF's Royal Rumble, The Heartbreak Kid left GWF negotiations supremely confident he'll beat a GWFer -- like the RWs, guaranteeing he will win. Michaels is an excellent wrestler and the GWFer who steps up to the Special Challenge with him should be ready for a real battle. HULK HOGAN -- The sport's best-known athlete is still going strong. Loved by many and hated by many more, the Hulkster says he's ready to run wild all over the GWF, though he has expressed concern about the often uncontrolled nature of GWF events. Still, he says he's ready to take on any GWFer who comes his way. So whatcha gonna do? This one will really be a match to watch. THE UNDERTAKER -- The man from the dark side has made the WWF exciting with his brand of athletic wreestling. He says he'll make sure one GWF star rests in peace! This will be another great matchup! SINGLES -- BAD GUYS / HEELS!!!!!! "NATURE BOY" RIC FLAIR -- Whooooooo! The Nature Boy is 13-time WCW/NWA champion, a former WWF champ and legend in his own time. What GWFer wouldn't give it all for the honor of facing Flair? Some say he's still the best this sport has, and like it or not, he's still the dirtiest player in the game. What GWFer has the guts to face the Nature Boy? We'll see! VADER -- Who's the man??? Vader! 12-time WCW champion and one of the sport's most intimidating athletes, Vader has made a reputation of destroying opponents here and overseas. Despite rumors of surgery, Vader has signed to meet a GWF star in a Special Challenge. Following agreements with the GWF, he's said he's heard a lot about GWF competition and is ready to crush any rising star who got in his way. This one will be a classic! "THE RINGMASTER" / STUNNING STEVE AUSTIN -- They don't call Steve Austin the Ringmaster for nothing. His great tehnical skills, as well as his ability to mix it up, make Austin a truly formidable opponent in the ring. His long career has included championships and feuds, but his current job is the WWF's Million Dollar Champion. Austin warns that GWF stars better be ready for the wrestling lesson of their lives! This match will definitely be a technical showcase! there you have it -- the February Special Challenge!! again, you must be a GWF member -- first come, first served. details and matches for the Special Challenge to be announced this week!!! to join the GWF, point your browsers to " http://www , geocities.com/Colosseum/3311 "
