"GWF WEB PAGE UPDATE!!!!!"

3 February 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GWF Central at " http://www.geocities.com/Colosseum/3311 " has been updated (a bit late, so to include the Rumble results) with some really exciting stuff -- if you haven't been by, check it out! in its first seven days, GWF Central logged 125 visitors and about 20 new wrestlers have joined the ranks! Check out the GWF roster on the web page (more info below) and see some of the new stars -- and make them feel welcome by offering to kick their butts! the new wrestlers should also take a shot at any of the GWFers who've been around and make your reputations at their expense! the new things on the page: You can also find GWF Central via Yahoo, Infoseek, the Whole Internet Catalog and Lycos web searchers! FOR THE RECORD: this is a (to today, friday afternoon) complete roster of every GWF wrestler and his record. PLEASE read it over and, if i have gotten your wrestler's name, position (face/heel/tween), stable or whatever incorrect, or if i have left out a wrestler, DO email me. you'll notice there are quite a few wrestlers with 0-0-0 records -- these are the new guys and you will see them in action soon. hopefully, the roster will give everyone the chance to size up the competition and issue some challenges based on the field of talent. FRONT OFFICE in response to one member's request, the Front Office page has opened the Challenge Center. say you want to just issue a challenge -- no interview, no a "let's go" statement to a wrestler. go to the Challenge Center, email your challenge to the fed and it's on the streets and likely in the next Interviews packet. NEWS ROOM Rumor of the week: What challenge do the Masters of Motion have for the top GWF tag teams? What does Hogan have to say to Serge? Why are there two wrestlers named Hammer and two named Sonic Boom in the GWF and how will the name fight be settled? Also, submit your news and rumors via email right from the News Room. OVER THE TOP Check out the best quote this week, courtesy of a brand new star, the God of Destruction, Thor, as well as this week's interview from the Terminator stable. IN THE CARDS Up-to-the-minute update on matches for the upcoming Gladiator School card as well as results from the Royal Rumble. Don't miss the great photo of The Public Enemy's Rocco Rock readying for a dive off a cage! JOIN THE GWF!!!!! " http://www.geocities.com/Colosseum/3311 "
