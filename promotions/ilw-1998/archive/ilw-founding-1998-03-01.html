An Introduction to ILW ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ ILW is a federation that originated from it`s bellegerent and hard headed creator, Lance Avery. Lance has been a wrestler for six years and a wrestling fan all of his life. About a year ago, he decided to begin his journey on starting a wrestling federation. It took him a few months but he finally found a company that could support his idea.
PaCific Coast Productions took Lance`s word and gave him most of the money he`d need to get things started. They told him that if ILW got off to a good enough begining, he`d see more money roll in.
With all the dough he was given, Lance went on a one man quest. For 10 months he searched, wandered and looked everywhere for the whole shebang. He found rings, belts, staff and he even purchased an official ILW Arena in
Seattle, Washington. (It took a little begging for the suits at PaCific Coast to give in). The man worked twelve hours a day, seven days a week, five weeks a month. With alot of blood, sweat and tears poured into the operation, Lance is ready to present ILW to the public...
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ 2. What You Can Expect From ILW ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ If and hopefully when you join ILW, you can expect alot of things. First of all, you can expect fun. ILW provides you with on time, fully written and detailed cards. You can expect entertainment. Me and my staff can think of the best angles to entertain you and you don`t even have to be involved in the angle to like it. (Unlike other federations.) Next, you can expect the unexpected. I know, I know, its a worn down cliche but here in ILW it`s true! Wait a sec... I`m sure you`ve heard that line too... OH WELL! ILW will give ya surprises around every corner. There`s no telling of exactly whats gonna happen.
And last but certainly not least, you can expect good rough competition with plenty of big names that will help elavate ILW to a household name...Well, maybe at least an... E-wrestling fan`s household name. You can also expect a jobber free federation. If you can`t live up to the standards then we`ll through you out on the street.
Simple as dat. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ 3. Role-Playing ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Here in ILW and like all the other _GOOD_ feds, ya gotta Role-play. I`m not going to go on and on about how you should Role-play because I know that everyone is different, but, instead, I`ll give you a few tips you can take under consideration;
1. Try and give people a full description of the scenery, the clothing being worn and other things that can paint a picture in the readers mind. _BUT_ don`t go overboard. Remember, the most important thing is to keep it intresting so no one loses intrest.
2. Try and be original. There`s so many DX wanna bes out there that I quit most of my feds cuz of them! Say some stuff that can be a little genuine because too many "Monday Night Quotes" can get boring too.
3. Say what you need to say in an intresting way. Plot your RP out before you do it. List what you want to say (maybe you can write it down), how you might say it and anything else you could put into your RP. Like small storylines for example.
In ILW, I want you to at least RP twice a week. The more you RP, howevever, the more involved you`ll get and the more likely you`ll win. In the begining of International League Wrestling, I want to make it a close system
RPing. Then, probably about once everyday, I`ll send out the RPs in a Newsletter like packet. This packet won`t be like those regular ones you see with just a table of contents and then the RPs. These are cool and I haven`t seen any other federation do them like this!
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ 4. ILW`s Championships! ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ ILW offers 4 different titles for it`s members. Each with it`s different status and responsibilty. Can you make it to the promised land? :)
International League Wrestling World Title- This title is for the _BEST_ wrestler in all of wrestling. Winning this could be the hallmark of your entire
e-wrestling career... Win it and become immortal. Oh yeah, It has to be defended once a month at least! International League Wrestling International Title- This title is here for those who are not quite up to the World Title challenge but will be someday.
This is a perfect stepping stone in the careers of those who win it. Be the International champion and you`ll have to defend it once every three weeks at least! International League Wrestling North American Title- BE THE BEST ON YOUR CONTINENT! WIN IT! This title is for the working man of ILW. You`ll have to defend this once every two weeks at least!
International League Wrestling Tag-Team Titles- This title is for the best two man tandem in all the land. _ONLY THE BEST_ Win this. The champs must defend it once a a month at the very least!
I might be adding a lightweight title if we get get enough of `em. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ 5. ILW`s Cards ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Since I`m all by myself :( I`ll be offering one card a week and a PPV once every two months. There will be more cards As soon as I get a staff WHICH I
WILL! I`m also sure that you know why I`m having PPVs once every two months but I`m gonna blabber on about it anyway.
PPV`s are meant to be special! One every month wears things down and the PPVs aren`t that great. Once every two months gives everyone and everything time to progress and lets angles and fueds grow alot more. I also get alot more time to brainstorm cool things for the PPVs.
ILW Sunday Night Survival is ILW`s main wrestling card. It always has 5-10 action packed matches. Each card has a a spectacular Main event and usually a few stipulation matches are thrown in there. It`s hosted by "Lethal"
Larry Cobalt and Marcus Tate. ILW`s second card is on Thursday. It`s known as ILW Thursday Ringside Chat. There`s three or four interviews including a run down on the house show results from around the country. (Remember, House Show Matches do count but don`t take them too seriously!) This show is hosted by your very own President
Lance Avery. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ 6. The Rankings ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ I want to make this as real as possible. So just like in real professional wrestling, the better you are THE MORE MONEY YOU MAKE! (It`s not real money:) )
Here`s how it all works: Win: By Pinfall or Submission: $30,000 By Countout or DQ: $30,000 Speciality Matches have different amounts of Prize money. Lose: By Pinfall or Submission: $ 5,000 By Countout or DQ: $ 5,000 Specaility Matches: $ 5,000 You don`t gain anything if you lose in any type of Battle Royales. Not only is it by Money, but it`s also by the more traditional Win/Loss record. Everything has it`s say when we decide things. Money can be used to buy air-time on cards, make advertisements for your wrestler(s), and if you get to the big plateu you`ll be able to buy your own one-time shows. Wether they be award shows or a wrestling card. Your money can get you alot of things!
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ 7. Say Hello to the Staff! ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Owner- Dave Simmons Dave Simmons doesn`t really have any power in ILW. Dave is the owner of PaCific Coast and he doesn`t really have any time for ILW. He just gives the money to Lance and puts his name on it. Dave, however, is an avid wrestling fans and tunes into Sunday Night Survival each week. When needed, he is more of a tiebreaker and will make any big decisions that Avery can`t.
President- Lance Avery You know him! (If you don`t, see 1. An Introduction to ILW somewhere near the top.) Commisioner- James Madison Lance knew what he wanted AND HE GOT IT! Lance and James have been pals for awhile and when Lance brought up his idea James was the first one to jump on top of it. James Madison use to be a heel wrestler back in his glory days but has gained a few lbs since then. James Madison was the one who taught Lance how to wrestle. Madison has alot of experience and a great deal of power.
Sunday Night Survivial Broadcaster- Marcus Tate Before his entrance to ILW, Marcus was an announcer for many Indy federations and college sports in the Washington/Oregon area. Marcus applied for the announcing job and was by far the most impressive. He gave it his all and Lance loved it. Marcus Tate usually favors the "good guys" when he announces. He and "Lethal" Larry Cobalt have been known to get their disagreements every once and awhile.
Sunday Night Survivial Broadcaster- "Lethal" Larry Cobalt Larry Cobalt is a man somewhere around his early 40s and has long since retired from wrestling with a back injury he suffered in the 80s. Larry perfers the heel wrestlers since he use to be one himself. He never backs down from a challenge, even now in his later years.
Ring Announcer- Arnie Myers Arnie is an old guy, somewhere in his fifties. He often gets teased about his balding hair and small stature but he is known as one of the greatest ring announcers in all of wrestling!
Timekeeper- Craig Hawley Craig Hawley is a fun loving guy but is rather boring and can get on your nerves very easily. Craig Hawley was one of the major candidates for "Lethal"
Larry`s announce position but was lowered down to Timekeeper because he can get kind of irratating. He has a secret grudge with Larry Cobalt that he keeps down inside. Some say he has some mental problems...
Head of Security- Jim Johnston Jim Johnston has been a wrestling fan all of his life but a torn achilles tendon kept him out of the ring. The next best thing is a Head of Security job at ILW. He`s always afficient and hard working.
