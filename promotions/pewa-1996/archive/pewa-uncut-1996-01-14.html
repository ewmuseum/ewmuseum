CHALLANGES------------------------------------------------------------------------
Jamie Cutlass has just signed an open contract! Anyone can face him!
NEWS------------------------------------------------------------------------------
We're getting a new card! That's right. Starting this upcoming Wednesday, there will be a new weekly card, Crash n' Burn. It will only have 5 matches, and will be summary style. The lineup will be released tomorrow.
----------------
The next tournament, and 8-man tag team tournament for the International Championship, will be held this week on Weekend Wargames. The wrestlers in the tournament were chosen COMPLETELY out of random. Here is the first round of the tournament.
The Indefatigable vs. "Sexy" Stevie Vincent
SSgt David M. Plantem vs. "Latin Sensation" Quelle Mondo
The Frenchman vs. Bodybag Jones
Executioner vs. Ultamite Fighting Machine
-----------------

Just in case you didn't know, the winner of the first tournament and new World Champion is
Brian Maloney.
