[JWL-Unsanctioned] Saturday Fever!

10 February 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The show opens with a loud drum beating in the background and the camera fades in on Eric Love and Dave Drater at the announcer's booth.] Eric Love: With the stockpile of money I had, I've opened the JWL back up, and now we're about to see the future of the JWL roar into effect! Ladies and Gentlemen, I give you... JWL Saturday Fever!!! [A huge disco ball drops down and "Disco Fever" starts to play. The letter JWL Saturday Fever appear on the screen and flash wildly. The camera comes back to Dave Drater and Eric Love.] Dave: Wow Eric, we have a great card tonight. First off is Jimmy Cougar and Matt Meyers, AKA The Glamour Boys taking on the Destructors. This promises to be a wild, wild match. And here come the combatants. Let's go to the action in the ring as the Destructors have just charged the ring! ****************** MATCH 1: The Glamour Boys vs. The Destructors ****************** Drater: The fists are flying as Monster #1, or is that #2 pounds on Matt Meyers in the corner. Jimmy Cougar has just dropkicked Monster #2 to the floor! But Matt is being pummeled in the corner! Cougar positioned in the opposite corner, head of steam and, WHOA!! A Splash on Monster in the corner! Oh god, Matt got crunched in between! He looks like he's out! Love: Hey, look at Monster #2, he stole a crutch from a fan! He's heading up behind Cougar who is attending to Meyers! Drater: DEAR LORD! #2 just Craaacked that crutch against Cougar's skull! Cougar is down and out. Unbelievable. Love: Heh, Monster #1 rolls out of the ring while #2 is positioning Cougar and Meyers on the mat. There goes #2 to the middle rope! Drater: I think if he hits this, it's all over for the Glamour Boys. Bounces up...and DOWN INTO BOTH MEN'S KNEES! He took to long and is paying for that one! Monster #1 has grabbed a table and is putting it in the ring! Cougar running into the ropes as #1 climbs onto the apron and...HOLY COW! Sunset flips him onto the floor! Both men are out of it now! Love: But Monster #2 is setting up the table! Kicks Meyers in the solar plexus a couple of times. Grabs him and...CHOKESLAM through the table! Wicked!!! Meyers is NOT getting up from this! All he needs to do is cover him. Drater: And he does. 1...2..NO! Meyers put his foot on the rope! Great example of ring awareness being performed by Meyers. He's in a bad position though! Monster #2 picks him up and suplexes him on the remains of the table! Now he's stomping Meyers in frustration! Monster looks a bit angry at Matt for kicking out! Love: We've got a brawl going on the floor! Cougar grabbed a chair and is just nailing Monster #1 with it! Where's the ref? This match should have been stopped! This isn't ECW for God's sakes!!! Drater: I don't think he cared. He hasn't had a chance in hell of controlling this one from the word go! This is crazy! Monster #1 isn't even PHASED by the chair though! He punches Cougar and bodyslams him on the floor! Who's the legal man? Or men even! Love: Monster #2, throws Matt into the corner and...BIG AVALANCHE! Matt goes down and Monster #2 climbs to the top! Kinda teetering, dives and...MISSES! Matt rolled out of the way of the splash! Come on you dumb Monster! Can't you do anything right? Drater: Well, Monsters aren't known for being smart Eric! Anyways, Cougar and Monster #1 are still going at it! Cougar charges and a spinning wheel kick knocks Monster #1 against the guard rail! Monster #1 falls into the crowd, I think he hit his head! It looks like Matt is actually doing something now! He's grabs on to Monster #2 and a reverse neckbreaker! He covers, 1...2..kicked out. Matt runs into the ropes and comes at Monster. Monster stands up and...BACKDROPS MEYERS ONTO THE FLOOR! Meyers HAS to be out! Love: Here comes Cougar into the ring from behind. Taps Monster #2 on the shoulder, Monster turns around and is kicked! DDT! DDT! Wow! Cougar leaps up on the top rope and comes down with a flying legdrop! Covers, 1...2...Throws him off! Cougar stumbles out of the ring. Drater: Monster #1 is back up though and BASHES Cougar in the head with a chair! Meyers crawls back into the ring which is a big mistake in his condition! Monster #2 grabs him and chokeslams him! Picks up Meyers and whips him into the corner! Avalanche! Stands him back up! Tosses him into the other corner! Another Avalanche! Meyers can't take much more punishment like this! WHips him into the other corner and AVALANCHES him yet again! Love: Monster #2 knows what he's doing. Meyers is finished. He can't even fight back at this point. Drater: And once again! Here comes the Aval...HE MISSED! Monster #2 is down and Meyers just fell backwards onto him! 1...2...3!!!!! Good golly Miss Molly! Meyers unknowingly wins the match! How? How? ********************* Winners: The Glamour Boys ********************* Love: Monster #2 forgot one thing... EVERYTHING! Hahahahaha! Drater: He was more interested in injuring Meyers than winning. COugar and Meyers are completely out still! The Destructors self destruct once again! They walk out of the area. Where was their manager? Love: Who? Demon Dave? I told him to go to pick me up some beer before the match. Drater: When was this? Love: Ten minutes before it started. Drater: Heh. Here he comes now. Demon Dave: What happened??? Love: Missed your match. Hey, did you get my beer? Demon: Did they win? Love: Yep, sure did. I'm surprised at the Glamour Boys. Demon: That's a relief. I was worried they lost! Love: Who? Demon: The Destructors! Love: Oh, yeah. They lost. Demon: WHAT??? Love you JERK! Love: Yep, love you too. [Demon Dave stomps off.] Love: What's this? He didn't even give me my beer! Demon Dork is more like it! Oh well. Drater: Um, ok. Well, looks like we've got a commercial break coming up. When we come back, Ron Silver faces Mr. Johnson. Should be a good match. Love: Yeah right, Mr. Johnson is about as good as Mr. Hughes. Drater: Anyways, that coming up after THIS T.V. timeout! [Commercial break] Drater: We're back and Johnson and Silver are in the ring! **************** MATCH 2: Mr. Johnson vs. Ron Silver **************** Love: DUD for sure! Drater: Eric, will you quit it already? Now into the ring for the action. Love: Or lack of it. Drater: Eric! Ok, a lock-up, and Silver fireman carries Johnson onto the mat. Johnson clutches at his back and backs off. Silver waits for him to stand up and Silver shoots at Johnson's leg! Trips him down and applies a hard leglock. A japanese type of leglock I would say. Alot of pressure being applied to the knee, but Johnson is too close to the rope. Ref calls for the break and Silver slinks back. Love: Seems like Silver has the perfect game plan. Ground the big boy and keep him from hitting his power moves. Silver isn't the greatest wrestler, but he's smart. Drater: Johnson stands back up as Silver shoots in, but this time Johnson drops down and elbows Silver in the head! Silver is down. Love: He telegraphed that one a mile away! Drater: He certainly did. He picks up Silver and punches him in the head. Just wailing on him now! Silver trying to defend his head, but to little avail. Johnson is just too powerful! Johnson grabs Silver and sets him up and PILEDRIVER! Love: Ouch! That's not good! Silver didn't want him hitting stuff like that this early! Silver screwed up big time! Drater: Johnson parading around the ring now like he just hit the big one! Too bad he doesn't cover Silver, he could have the victory! Silver rolls out of the ring and looks like he's taking a break. Smart move there. Love: And Johnson just stands there waiting. Johnson could do some major damage right now if he would just go out of the ring and attack! Drater: Well, Silver standing back up and walks around the ring. Gets on the apron and motions Johnson back. Steps in and dives at Johnson! Silver hammering away at Johnson! He has him on his back and Silver has just SNAPPED! Silver is punching and punching Johnson! The ref is pulling Silver off now, but Silver decks the referee!!! Love: Well, Silver had better cool off a little! He isn't going to win this way. Drater: Silver lands and elbow drop. Goes to the second turnbuckle and drops another elbow. Covers him but the ref is out still! So Silver gets up and climbs out of the ring. He tosses in a chair and rushes into the ring. Love: Look at him! Silver grabs the chair and wacks it against Johnson's leg! Bashes it again and again! This is smart! Very smart! Keep working on his leg! Wacks it again for good measures and tosses the chair out of the ring. Drater: He sets him up and a figure four! Alot of pressure being applied now, and the ref is still out! Silver grabs the ropes for leverage and now Johnson is flailing and thrashing in pain! He's gonna pop that knee right out! The ref has to stop this! Love: The ref finally coming to now. Drater: He sees Silver hold the ropes and makes him break it up. Silver kicks away at the knee and locks him in a spinning toehold! Love: Nothing like a good old spinning toehold. You get the knee up high and cause all that blood to rush back in as you cut off blood to not only the foot and lower leg area, but you apply a great amount of pressure on his knee. The best he can hope for is to have his leg fall asleep. Drater: But now Johnson is in great pain and is kicking at Silver. He keeps kicking and...LOW BLOW! Johnson kicks him in the lower bread basket! Love: What school of announcing did you graduate from? Drater: Gorilla Monsoon's I believe. Love: Figures. Drater: Johnson picks him up and signals for something. Lifts Silver up and over, into a torture rack! Johnson locking it in and...Johnson's leg just gave way! Silver gets up, locks him in the Sharpshooter! The ref asking and...JOHNSON GIVES UP! **************** Winner: Ron Silver **************** Love: Ha! Old intelligence wins again! It's a classic tale ain't it? Drater: Well, intelligence can beat power. Like they say, the pen is mightier than the sword, and that holds true here. Silver walks out slowly and silently while Johnson attempts to stand up in the ring. Silver grins all the way back. Johnson slowly stumbles out of the ring, his knee is injured pretty badly. Love: A couple of nights sleep and he'll never remember it happened. Drater: Yeah, but his paycheck will sure remind him! A good match I thought though. Love: Eh, not bad for two stiffs like those. I've seen much better. Like any Jim Goodyear matches. Drater: Well, anyways, time for another commercial break. And when we come back, Mark Magic takes on the Moldy Grape in a stretcher match! Only on JWL Saturday Fever! [Commercial break] Drater: Welcome back to JWL Saturday Fever. We've got a stretcher at ringside for this one! Love: Why Mark wastes him time on this damned Moldy Grape, I've no idea! Drater: Well, I guess he just wants to have fun. Not like the Grape has shown ANY skill in his matches yet. Maybe the trend will continue tonight. Remember in this stretcher match, anything goes! And the bell is rung! ********************* MATCH 3: Mark Magic vs. The Moldy Grape (Stretcher Match) ********************* Drater: Magic walks over and slaps Grape in the face. Moldy looks mad now! He charges at Magic who takes Grape down with a leg trip. Mark stands up and kicks Grape in the back of the head repeatedly! He climbs to the middle rope now and waits. Grape getting to his feet and... The Magic Montage! The Grape falls and flops to the outside of the ring. Here comes Lucifer Overdark from the back! He's got a baseball bat! Love: This is great already! Drater: He's wailing on The Grape and the Grape has been busted open! Magic grabs a chair and smashes away as well! They keep attacking! This has got to stop already! The Grape is defenseless! This poor, pathetic Moldy Grape is being killed! Magic grabs a table, puts Grape on it and DDTS him through the table!!!! Blood is all over now! Man, we're gonna lose our sponsors for this one! And wait one damned second! Lucifer is unsheathing his knife! DEAR LORD NO! [Assaf Levavy runs out and grabs the knife from Lucifer.] Love: What the HELL! [Assaf Levavy walks into the ring and grabs the mic.] Levavy: Don't forget Love, this kinda [BLEEP] doesn't belong in the JWL! I'm the Vice-President and I tell YOU what to do! [Love laughs, stands up and brushes himself off. He walks into the ring and stands face-to-face with Levavy.] Love: I don't give a [BLEEP] who you are, because you're no VP here. I brought this federation back from the muck it was in. Me! Not you, me! You catch my drift? Levavy: This kind of sick crap doesn't belong in wrestling Love! They were gonna kill him! Love: Well, under your era, I'm sure he woulda just come back to life anyways. Now listen here Assface, these wrestlers I've transferred their contracts from the JWL of old unto my era. They answer to me, and I DON'T recall signing you! I'll tell you what though. You leave, right here, right now, and you, and the Moldy [BLEEP] can live! Because of you the old JWL doesn't exist anyways, so you have your last chance to leave and never come back. [Levavy glares at Love, looks around and leaves the ring.] Levavy: This isn't over between us Love. Love: Ask me if I care! And by the way, Grape, you miserable piece of lard, you're FIRED! Get out of the JWL now! Boys, haul his miserable carcass out of here. I'm sick of this. [Love throws the microphone down with a thud and walks back to the locker room.] *************** Winner: Mark Magic *************** Drater: Well, uh... hm. Anyways, when we come back, our main event! The Natural Born Killaz vs. The Hawaiian Islanders in what promises to be a VERY exciting match! Back in a minute. [Commercial Break] Drater: I'm back, and with me is Mark Lewis, Mark nice to see you. Lewis: Well Dave, you seem to have taken my spot, but best of luck to you. Drater: Well thanks Mr. Lewis. And now off to the ring where the Maui Wowee and Surfer Kyle are now posing for the fans. What is this? They've got a picture of someone. Is that the Molly they've been talking about? And now here somes S.O.B. and the Cape! Storming the ring and the bell rings! ********************** MATCH 4: The Hawaiian Islanders vs. The Natural Born Killaz ********************** Lewis: The Cape locks up with Kyle and hip tosses him to the mat. Beck runs at the Maui Wowee and is met with a backdrop! Maui starts dancing and spinning? What the hell is wrong with him? Drater: I wish I knew. The Cape picks up Kyle and suplexes him hard. The ref puts Wowee and Syrus out of the ring. Meanwhile, the Cape clamps on an armbar on the Surfer Kyle. Surfer climbs to one knee and slowly stands up. Cape backs him into the ropes, whips him and the Cape rolls forward, into a clothesline! Awesome move by the Cape! Very Konnanesque. Lewis: The roll forward into a clothesline is about the only move Konnan does well. Drater: The Cape grabs Kyle's arm and plants a short arm scissors on him. As he clamps it on, a reminder that the winner of this match will get to face the team of Mark Watson and Chris Johnson for the titles at a later date. All of these men hate each other and we've got a heated tag team situation. And now we have the Glamour Boys and the Minions of Magic creeping back into the picture, we've got a very complicated scene going on! Lewis: No doubt. The best wrestlers are right here in the JWL. Even if it is unsanctioned and Love used his own money to bring it back. [name withheld] still owns the rights to the name, which Klootzer doesn't. Klootzer owned the fed, not the name. Drater: Back to the action, the Cape working on Kyle's arm. Drops and elbow on it and locks on a stiff front armbar. Kyle bars his other arm up against the Cape's face and lands a knee into the Cape's back. Cape lets go of the hold. Both men stand up, Kyle dropkicks the Cape in the knee and falls onto the mat. Kyle gives a couple quick kicks and picks the Cape up. Tosses him into the ropes and a crossbody press! 1...2..kickout. Kyle gets up, runs into the ropes, sunset flip, 1..2..kickout. Kyle gets up and picks the Cape up and a backbreaker. Cape planted on the mat and Kyle ascends to the top rope. Lewis: He jumps and does a somersault splash! I had no IDEA Kyle had that in him! Unbelievable! Drater: Nor did I! Kyle only gets a 2 count though. A spin kick by Kyle and tries to pin him. Only a one count this time. Lewis: Kyle grabs the Cape and tags to Wowee, Wowee climbs to the top and hammers down on the Cape's arm as Kyle leaves the ring. Wowee doing some more dancing. He lands some quick kicks in and starts acting like a ninja! The Cape stands up and THRUST KICK by the Maui Wowee! Maui goes and punches Beck, who is now trying to enter the ring. The ref cuts him off and is distracted. Meanwhile a double DDT! Maui and Kyle are putting the boot to the Cape! Drater: Now the ref turns around and demands Kyle leave the ring. The Cape crawling to his corner. He makes the tag! The ref didn't see it! The ref didn't see it! Beck was about to lay into Maui and he's stopped! Now the Maui Wowee is pointing and laughing at Beck. Beck charges into the ring but the ref trying to get him out. Kyle and Wowee grab the Cape and a BRAINBUSTER! Wow! What a nasty Brainbuster! Beck just distracting the ref more as the Cape gets a spiked piledriver delivered to him! Lewis: Beck is doing more harm than good, he needs to step back! Drater: Maui climbs to the top and lands a moonsault! Maui dancing around the ring like a moron! Cover him! Lewis: Wait a second! Who's that? Drater: From behind! It's Watson and Johnson! The double clotheslined Wowee! They double punch Beck! They double punch Kyle who ran into the ring! They're cleaning house now! We've got chaos! Watson and Johnson have cleared the ring! Let's get the referee's decision! Announcer: Ladies and Gentlemen, the referee has declared this bout, a DOUBLE DISQUALIFICATION! ************** Winners: DOUBLE DQ (No Winners) ************** Drater: There's a stare down in the ring and we're running out of time! There's only one way to solve this dispute! A triangle match will have to be scheduled! This feud has got to stop! Ladies and Gentlemen, that's all the time we have. From the JWL, we'll see you NEXT WEEK!!! [The camera fades to black with the JWL logo.]
