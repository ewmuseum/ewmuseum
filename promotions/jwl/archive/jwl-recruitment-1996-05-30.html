JWL is accepting new wrestlers!!!

30 May 1996

I'm [name withheld] and I run the JWL, I'm looking for 12 new wrestlers to join up on the league. You are allowed up to three wrestlers. We are using TNM 6.2a7. Currently, I havn't gotten the homepage up but it will be just as soon as Angelfire is no longer down. Here is the application for, first 12 wrestlers will be accepted! Name: E-Mail address: Wrestler's Name: Manager(Optional): Weight: Finisher: Setup for finisher: What is the finisher similar to? Maximum weight for finisher? Heel/Face: Select one of the following: 1.Amateur Wrestler 2.Top-Notch Brawler 3.All-Rounder 4.Stiff Martial Artist/grappler 5.Basic High Flyer 6.Basic Brawler 7.Luchador 8.Submission Wrestler 9.High Flyer/Good Technician 10.US Standerd Worker 11.Brawler/Arielly Skilled 12.Technically Skilled Brawler 13.Technician/Brawler 14.Daredevil Flyer Distribute 150 points between the following: How good of a worker is your wrestler? How much stamina does your wrestler have? What foreign object does your wrestler use? What move will lead to a pin more easily than others? Will he use chairs? Will he do a move after a cross-corner whip? Does your opponent have to move out of the way? What move? Will he give a sign for his finisher? Is he cocky? Will he juice(bleed extremely)? Will he work the crowd? What will he do to work the crowd?(If answered yes to above question) Does he have a despiration move? What is it? Will he have a special combo? What is the combo?Setup move: Follow-up move: Will he do moves while opponent is in the corner? Will he do his finisher outside the ring? Will he be a hardcore wrestler? Will he do moves through tables? Will he do his finisher through a table? Does he fall to his knees and beg? Will he walk out of his match from time to time? Choose yes for two of the following: Will he be someone that doesn't submit? Will he sit up after taking a move? Will he be hard to pin? Will he have a Hulk-up?
