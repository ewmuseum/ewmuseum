The EOW tours venues globally, as the name suggests. We are a booked league, meaning that match results are mainly decided through roleplaying, strategies and angles, plus other factors such as effort. Matches are written in play-by-play style, meaning we will utilize commentary on our shows, providing more detail, thus making the cards more enjoyable to read. The major card is Saturday Night ShockValue, while War of Words every Friday gives the wrestlers the opportunity to voice their opinions,War on words is a newsletter and chance for wrestlers to let people know how they feel. Our aim is to make the federation as exciting as possible for our handlers, providing them with entertaining storylines and angles for the ultimate eWrestling experience. ##################### The Creation ##################### Planning of the Extreme Online Wrestling began in mid-2000 when business tycoon Lenny Low got in touch with wrestling star Ben Kit Karson. They began planning every intricate detail of the league throughout the months leading to the grand opening, which was March 1st, 2001. Only two weeks later, the administration signed a deal with AWP and IWA to run a talent exchange deal. With Karsons experience in the industry and Fox's financial backing, EOW is destined for success. ##################### Weekly Schedule ##################### Sunday: Weekly Update Monday: Off Day Tuesday: Off Day Wednesday: Off Day Thursday: War of Words Friday: RP Deadline at Midnight Saturday: Saturday Night Fury =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= RULES AND REGULATIONS =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= ##################### Applications ##################### With applications, all characters must be of original creation. This means that the EOW will not be accepting wrestlers with names such as "Stone Wall" Jim Austin, The Block, Scott Reiner or anything similar. Characters _may_ have the same finisher as a real wrestler, but it must have a different name. Another thing that is stressed is the use of original music. Note, all fields MUST be filled out on the apps unless marked as optional and the sample roleplay section may be left blank if you were invited to the league by one of the executive committee, otherwise it must be included. Applications will undergo a review process and you will be contacted on the status of your application within 2-3 days after being submitted. ##################### Roleplaying ##################### The EOW limits 2 Roleplays each week for ShockValue and one in-ring Interview for War on Words. Please try to keep things realistic in Roleplays, so don't have your wrestler act like a superhero and fly, or run as fast as a locomotive. Although our characters are indeed fictional like Superman, we're aiming to be as realistic as possible. One of the major rules is to stay IN-CHARACTER during your interviews. This means to talk as your wrestler the entire time and not the man/woman behind him/her. Also, do not break kayfabe. Kayfabe is described in the RSPW FAQ "to mean not exposing that the business is worked." Therefore, do not call an opponent a "jobber" or refer to the "booker". These are terms rarely heard of televised wrestling to keep the illusion that wrestling is real. If you lose a match, do not blame officials Its voted on by 3 people. Finally, DO NOT under any circumstances include other wrestlers in your roleplays without the written permission of the respective handlers. EOW Officials reserve the right to refuse any inappropriate material that may be submitted to them. This includes prejudice towards race, gender, intellectual disabilities and sexuality,some will be allowed but keep it to a minimum. We have the final say on things and if you don't agree with the rules, please refrain from applying. ##################### Sneak Attacks ##################### Sneak attacks are allowed in moderation, but are only included if they are used to further an angle between two or more wrestlers. Normally, this is the case. Attacks that cause injury to other wrestlers, whether it be temporary of permanent, will be rejected unless there is permission from the victim's handler. All handlers are expected to "sell" attacks in their flashes and strats. Sneak attacks will never be used to affect the outcome of a match, unless it's part of the angle or the person being attacked is already booked to lose. If you have any complaints with the way your character was portrayed in an attack, please eMail the EOW administration to discuss the matter further. ##################### Other Rules ##################### If you lose a match, please don't go complaining to the administration with a head full of fire. A calm eMail to the executive committee will be sufficient. We take this rule very seriously and if it's not abided by, the offending handler will be released from the company immediately. If you have any doubts about out-roleplaying your opponent, you are probably right. If you lost, it's because administration believed you were beaten in flashes and strats. More rules will come here if we believe they are needed. =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= STAFF =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= ##################### Real Staff ##################### The Global Wrestling Connection is owned and operated by [name withheld], who serves as a solitary fedhead. However, we're always looking for more people to give us a hand and there are positions open for a Co-President and match writers, so if you're interested, drop us a line. [name withheld] eMail: [detail withheld] Major Characters: "Kandid" Kyle Kannon ##################### Administration ##################### Lenny Low: LOW is a multi-millionaire and an owner of a major worldwide grocery chain. He noticed the potential revenue that pro-wrestling could generate after attending an event and decided that with a bit of help, he could create a company that could dominate the industry. Only time will tell if his prediction turns out to be correct. Kit Karson: All dedicated wrestling fans would have heard of Karson, who has featured in such promotions as AGW, AWP & CSW. He's also won several awards during his short career and is looking to try the administration side of things for a change of scene. He's looking forward to the challenge and hopes to reward the faith Lenny Low has in him by producing good, quality shows. ##################### Announce Team ##################### Stewart Nash: The leading man on the announce team, Stewart Nash is a former journalist and a good friend of EOW Owner, Low. This is his first shot at wrestling commentary and he has been doing his research leading up to the first EOW show. "Malicious" Mel Jergin: A veteran wrestler known for his malicious tactics, Mel Jergin loves the sight of seeing someone being busted open in the ring. Mel is the color commentator for Saturday Night ShockValue and he generally favors the heels. He has worked as a commentator in the past for Midwest Championship Wrestling (MCW). ##################### Referees ##################### Danny Madden: A veteran wrestler and referee, Danny Graham knows the rules inside out. His vast knowledge and experience earned him the spot of EOW Head Referee. He prefers old-school wrestling and only officiates for normal matches. He is not afraid to call for a DQ and is the best referee in the Extreme Online Wrestling and handles most of the main events. Jim Burley: Burley takes his job very seriously and is extremely strict with the rules. He is not intimidated by the wrestlers while doing his work and has the potential to become of the EOW's top referees. Zach Lewis: Zach loves the new wave of hardcore wrestling and enjoys standing back and watching wrestlers make each other bleed. He'll never call for a disqualification, even during a standard match and encourages the use of foreign objects. Ray McKeon: Ray is a newcomer to the refereeing scene and gets scared very easily. He's easily intimidated and fearful for his safety in the ring. However, he can referee any type of match and enjoys his job, somewhat. Ryan Bower: A former indy fed sensation, Bower quit wrestling due to some extreme circumstances. He's taking up the job of a referee in the EOW and loves to see wrestling moves being executed to perfection. ##################### Misc. Staff ##################### Jimmy Evans Jr: A former boxing ring announcer, Evans Jr. made the switch to wrestling after being sacked from his previous job. He's in his mid-thirties and can handle the bumps he occasionally takes while sitting at ringside. Robbie Starr: Starr is an experienced in the interview department, working as a courtside announcer at college basketball games for over 4 years. He's easily intimidated by the wrestlers of the EOW, but executes his job professionally at all times. Starr is also the official spokesperson of the EOW. Jenny Winston: Jenny is the female half of the interview/backstage team and has had past experience as part of the North American Wrestling Council (NAWC). She knows how to take care of herself and is not afraid to confront any of the EOW Superstars. Gary Johnson: GWC's Timekeeper. =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= TITLES =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= ##################### World Heavyweight ##################### The best and most prestigious belt the EOW has to offer. The holder of this belt is usually the top wrestler in the federation at that point in time. It is to be defended at least once a month. All title matches are with no time limit and top contenders are decided via rankings. PAST HOLDERS: None ##################### United States ##################### The second best title in the EOW. The United States Title signifies you are a top class wrestler in the league, but aren't quite ready to take the next step. Most wrestlers use this belt as a stepping-stone to greater things. It is to be defended at least twice a month. All title matches are with a 20-minute time limit unless specified and top contenders are decided via rankings. PAST HOLDERS: None =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= Lightweight =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= Tag Team =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= ##################### Saturday ShockValue ##################### Held on Saturday Nights between 8:30 PM and 10:30 PM,SV is the EOW's weekly card. Hosted by Stewart Nash and "Malicious" Mel Jergin, SV usually contains anywhere up to 8 matches. If this is the case, an hour prior to the show containing dark matches written in summary style is used. Otherwise, all other matches are written in play-by-play style. ##################### War of Words ##################### War of Words is shown on Friday Nights and the main purpose of the show is to air all the Ideasmade by the wrestlers of the EOW ##################### Pay Per Views ##################### The EOW holds six pay per view events each year, with one being held within each two-month cycle. The league's signature PPV event is SuperSlaM, which is held in July January/February: Survival March/April: Quake of Fire May/June: Fatal Bonds July/August: SuperSlam September/October: Annihilation [2001] November/December: EOW Invitational Cup =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= SPECIAL EVENTS =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= ##################### EOW Invitational Cup ##################### Held in November, it is the biggest event on the EOW calendar. The EOW Invitational Cup is a 32 man invitational tournament, with one half of the draw consisting of EOW wrestlers and the other half being made up of outsiders from other leagues wishing to taste some EOW action. The major purpose of the EOW Invitational Cup is to gain exposure for the league as well as attract some big names in hope of signing them to contracts.
