The match begins without the officials. Morgasm had already picked up a chair and slammed it over X-Pac's head. He slides outside and picks up the ladder. He sets it up in the center of the ring and sets up a table. He lifts X-Pac up to his feet and puts him through the table with the G-Spot Jackhammer. He climbs the ladder and grabs the title. "No chance! Yeah that's what ya got!" Smitty and HBK step out on the ramp. Smitty has a mic in hand.
"Morgan, put the title back. The match hasn't officially started, so therefore, it can't end yet.

X-Pac moves, causing Morgasm to hit the mat. Morgasm staggers to his feet, and gets hit by a spinning heel kick. He falls into the corner and on his ass. X-Pac runs to the opposite corner of the ring, and crotch chops the audience. He then turns around and runs toward Morgasm, hitting him with the
Bronco Buster. X-Pac climbs up the ladder. In a move of desperation, Morgasm tackles the ladder, causing it to fall over, and X-Pac to fall outside of the ring. Both men are out. HBK walks over to the corner and stomps his foot.
Smitty steps in front of him and shakes his head, motioning for him to stop. HBK moves as Smitty takes the corner and stomps his foot. The Cutthroats run into the ring and get hit by a double Sweet Chin Music. The remaining member of the Cutthroats gets hit with another one by HBK. HBK and Smitty slap each other a high five. Morgasm gets up and picks up a chair. He slams it on Smitty's head. HBK turns around and is hit in the stomach with the chair.
Kevin: "No! That's not fair!" Ethan: "It's fair enough!" Hulk: "As much as I hate to admit it, Kev, I agree with Ethan." HBK falls to the floor Morgasm sets up the table. X-Pac runs in from nowhere and puts Morgasm through the table with the X-Factor. The crowd goes wild.
X-Pac sets up the ladder and climbs it to the top Morgasm is still out. X-Pac gives him two crotch chops, then he grabs the Hardkore title.
