AWA Saturday Night Slaughter

12 September 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The opening moments are devoted to still images from last 2 week's card. RAW is live from Hong Kong Polytechnic University, Commentator Stephen
Yang brings Lawrence Shamrock to the ring. The man Lawrence said before, he renewed his contract and will soon back to action. He will focus on the Hardcore competition. However, Mr. Hardcore shows up, Josh says there will be no celebration about Lawrence's return and claims that he will feel what hardcore is actually is. Lawrence Shamrock threatens to end his career if Josh continues to talk trash. The associates stop Josh from doing further. Lawrence Shamrock takes his Nun-Chuks and goes backstage. Cut to commercial.
Dream King and Big Shot vs Jagpreet Ghuman and Hitkid: (Tag Title) Jagpreet tries some of his fancy moves but is overpowered by the Big
Shot. He tags in Dream King but has less success. Hitkid gets in and double team Dream King and keeps the advantage until the Big Shot comes back in. Big Shot then tags Dream King back in. They are tagging well and now Hitkid is starting to show signs of fatigue. The tag champs are effectively isolating Hitkid who is clearly running out of gas. Dream
King is inside with Hitkid, Bob Peil turns around and posing for the crowd. Jagpreet Ghuman calls some of the Knightmare's fans to shut up.
Hitkid isn't too happy when he goes for a tag and Ghuman isn't ready. Cut to commercial. We're back and the Champs are still in charge. Shortly there after
Hitkid finally gets lose and tags in his partner. Ghuman cleans house then gets a pin on Big Shot, but his foot is on the rope. Big Shot recovers quickly and comes back with a sidewalk slam. Both men (Big Shot and Dream King) exchange tags. The Knightmare dominates again and tags
Big shot back in - who continues the assault. Ghuman escapes and tags in
Hitkid while Big shot tags Dream King back in. Dream King taking the advantage. He whips Hitkid to the opposite corner -
Jagpreet Ghuman comes out and drops an elbow on Dream King's back. Hitkid takes advantage and goes to the top and flys but Dream King catches him and suplexes him over his head then pins him! It's a Perfect
Teamwork show. Commercial time.
Backstage Lawrence Shamrock is looking for a Mr. Harcore, Tom Chan starts to interview him but Mr. Hardcord Josh shows up and attacks
Lawrence Shamrock.
Back to the ring,Jack comes out and says that he is going to give a chance to Kerry as he is his brother, and says that he will forgive everything if he get down to his knees and apologize for what he did, he says that Kerry can think well and give his answer whetever he thinks it is good to him. Cut to commercial.
Jack Morris vs. Terminal Frost: Jack Morris is in the ring, waiting for Terminal Frost. Terminal Frost music hits, but Kerry Morris comes out instead, with a kindly smile.
Kerry says Jack wants the answer, he got it. Terminal Frost music hits again and runs out Terminal Frost. He attacks Kerry Morris and puts him down - he is pulled off and Jack Morris goes after Terminal
Frost.Terminal Frost gets his shots in before he is dragged off. Outside the ring Terminal Frost gets into it with Kerry again. Finally the match starts. Jack Morris dominates the early going, then turns his back on
Terminal Frost and gets clobbered from behind. Terminal Frost bulls Jack
Morris into the corner and pummels him. Now its all Terminal Frost. He pounds his opponent into the mat then goes up for a Dropkick. Changes his mind and goes all the way up for a Moonsault - but he's taking too much time and gets caught by the recovered Morris. And the ref Aki counts 1,2,3..The match is over. After the match, Kerry takes a mic and he seems get a final answer. Suddenly, Kerry spits on Jack Morris's face and says that he no longer consider Jack as a brother and tries to leave, but Jack nails him on the back and Kerry starts to take a beating from his brother. Terminal Frost attacks Jack Morris from behind they tumble to the outside where they continue to brawl. The referee Aki gets creamed in the ensuing melee. A second ref Jacky Chan runs out to declare the contest over as we go to commercial.
The lost Soul vs Mr.Hardcore Josh: Mr.Hardcore and Josh still continue to brawl in backstage. Finally
Lawrence got separated by a few officers. Mr. Hardcore walks out. Before he can step into the ring. The lost soul tries to execute a flying moonsault. He is largely successful. Mr.Hardcore can't seem to get anything together at first. He finally breaks lose and turns the tables.
A couple of flashy moves later he goes to the top but gets distracted by
Lawrence Shamrock at ringside. Referee Jacky Chan stops it. Then
Mr.Hardcore goes back up to do the a flying dropping, OH...nobody home...The lost soul rolls him over and pins him.
The Enforcer vs Justin Bismark (Asian Title),Steel is the referee, anyone wins in this match will face Steel: Scorpion accompanies The Enforcer to the ring. This match is a warm up for the Title contest coming up at the next PPV. Enforcer shoves Justin off twice then pauses to wipe Justin grime off of himself. Enforcer uses his power advantage well throughout this match. Justin is fighting back.
Justin is working on Enforcer's knee as we return. He pounds on the guy repeatedly but can't get the pin. The Enforcer finally picks Justin up and holds him for a looong time in a verticle suplex. Justin is getting frustrated. He distracts the referee and hits Johnson with the title belt. Enforcer is up again, but a little bit dizzy. Justin hits him the second time. The belt rings! Ref Honman sees that! The winner is The Enforcer! Screen fades to black.
Scorpion vs Canadian Thunder (Non-Title): There's an interview with Canadian Thunder at early time. Thunder says its personal between him and Canadian Thunder now. Scorpion shows up and tosses a drink in Canadian Thunder's eyes then goes to work on him in the ring. Gives him a big DDT. Queen Robin comes in and slaps
Scorpion. A strange fans suddenly runs in and grabs Robin in a waist lock and starts shaking her. Security breaks it up and hauls the stranger away. Canadian Thunder carries Marlena away from the ring.
Jagpreet Ghuman, Hitkid vs Mytik, The Hot Shot and I love win: The Hot Shot seems have problems with his partners Mytik and I love win. Finally the match turn to be another handicap team workL Ghuman,Hitkid and The Hot Shot vs Mytik and I love win, Obviously Ghuman and Hitkid dominate the action until they try to double team Ghuman. Hitkid puts them both down then The Hot Shot runs in for the The Venom Drop and gets an easy 3 pins. What an easy match for these superstars.
Special Match: Queen Robin vs Steel w/ Lawrence Shamrock be the special referee, to determine whether Queen Robin can get a LHW title shot next week! Scorpion joins commentator Tom Chan. Queen Robin goes on the offensive immediately, before the match is officially started (No special ref yet). Some officers search for the special ref Lawrence Shamrock while the match is going. Queen Robin jumps right on Steel's back but Steel throws her off to the floor. Steel trys to clothesline her on the floor but she counters it a back drop! At this time, Mr. Hardcore runs out, behind him
is Lawrence Shamrock. The crowds gets exciting. Lawrence and Mr. Hardcore are exchanging chair shots in the ring! Queen Robin and Steel back to the ring. Scorpion continues to babble at ringside. Lawrence knocks Mr.Hardcore into Steel and Mr.Hardcore hits Lawrence with a double clothesline! 3 men down in the ring! Queen Robin covers all 3 men and the crowds shout -1-2-3!!
