AWA(aWA) Asia Wrestling Association, I'm the owner of AWA and we would like to employ new talents forever. http://hk.interspeed.net/awa OR http://www.geocities.com/TimesSquare/Ring/7028 -- Introduction I've been in the wrestling business since May of '97. I've ran it more than 1 year! **This fed is based on original wrestlers.** -- Cards, Titles, Pay Per Views etc... We've 2 cards per week. Thunder Challenge on Wed and RAW on every weekend, ie SAT. We will have one PPV for each month. Besides HW title, IC title, Asian Champ, Tag Title, we've hardcore title and also, Women division.
-- Roleplay We don't like 3 lines roleplay, we like creative roleplay. Quality roleplay rules. We roleplay in guestbook[guestworld]. Roleplaying decides whether you win a match or you lose a match. You must roleplay for every match you have or you are guaranteed to job.
You can also use interviews to help you win your match. Well thanks for reading all this. If you are a responsible matchwriter or know of any, let me know too.
