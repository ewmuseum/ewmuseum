Everyone, Happy Chinese New Year. This PPV takes place in Hong Kong. Screen fades in the wrestlers' rest room, Sid Shady talks with the lady Li'l
Cheetah, the reporter guess they are talking about the women Battle Royal, they seem talk happily. Sid Shady once again said he've retired.
Asian Title: Canadian Thunder vs Big Shot Bob Peil Bob Peil pinned Canadian
Thunder after a power bomb in 0:08:29. Canadian Thunder holds this title for
3 months, now Bob Peil takes the title. It's a great match
Light Heavy Weight Title: -Steel vs Attitude,Inc Steel challenged Attitude,Inc to an arm wrestling match, during the match, Tammy Lynn Sytch hits Steel a low blow and Attitude,Inc execute him with a TKO. Then the match starts and Attitude takes advantage. But Steel get an opportunity, an opportunity to pay back the low blow, Steel had Attitude,Inc in the corner for the low blow kick Crowd:OH! (Attitude,Inc falls to the ring.) After the match, Steel gave Tammy a red pocket money, inside the red pocket is not money, it's...a condom!! Tammy and Attitude are angry! Later during the Men
Battle Royal, Steel gives some real red pocket money to officers, each pocket have $10. Attitude calls Big Poppa Pump joins their fight,while that time Big
Poppa Pump should be participate in Battle Royal. Attitude,BPP and Tammy attack Steel from behind.. Winner:Attitude,Inc by DQ.
I-QUIT Match: Lawrence Shamrock vs Shane Jeckins & Ironbreaker, all people are banned from ringside; if Lawrence Shamrock,Shane Jeckins or Ironbreaker lose but he is the winner of the Battle Royal.He will stay in AWA
Ironbreaker has no shows. Match starts off fast. Lawrence Shamrock goes down,
Shane pins, and he gets a two count. Shane picks him up and gives Shamrock a big suplex, he goes for the cover and gets a two count again. Shamrock does a knee lift on Shane. Shamrock kicks Shane in the face. The match slows down a bit now. Shamrock is dominating the match up. Lawrence kicks Shane and goes for the pin, 1 -2... kick out by Shane Jeckins. Shane takes Shamrock down face first into the mat, and is slow to get up. He pins Shamrock and gets another 2 count. Shamrock is slammed into the turnbuckle. Shane climbs up and starts punching Shamrock. Both men are tired...A side slam by Shamrock.
Shamrock is setting up Shane for the Hell on Earth, and he tries to put it on, but Shane scrambles to the ropes. Shamrock attacks the arm. Shamrock goes after the arm again. Shane fights back and accidentally hits the ref with a clothesline, Shamrock dropkicks Shane from behind, then both men go down.
Ironbreaker runs in!! Hits Lawrence Shamrock with a chair!! The ref didn't see it... Shane covers, and the ref is slow to get there, 1-2- NO!!!Shamrock kicks out. Shamrock side kicks the ref accidentally. Then Shane and Lawrence hit each other by double clothesline, they go down again. Wait a min! A referee runs in the ring, and locks Shane Jeckins into an ankle lock!!
Lawrence Shamrock recovers and snaps! He execute a Hell on Earth on the referee!! The crowds get mad! The owner Chen Zhen and some associates quickly stop them, and call them back to lockeroom. Later we know the "ref" is AWA new signed wrestler: Peter Lowry. This match has no winner, no one quits.
World Title: Dream King Guy Gagne vs Jack Morris Dream King Guy Gagne made Jack Morris submit to a sleeperhold in 0:11:26. Hardcore Title: Steve Walker vs Marvelous Mike w/HBK - Marvelous Mike defeated Steve Walker via Pinfall, to become the new Hardcore Champion.Marvelous Mike is the new Hardcore Champion, since Steve Walker suffered a major injury caused by an attack by Canadian Thunder the night on
Raw Saturday Night. Steve Walker still holds the IC Title. In the fighting between Walker and Mike, both men took it to the backstage and outside area, where they used several objects to their advantage. The majority of the match took place on the outside, in a restaurant outside the AWA arena. They both tossed each other into the fishing pool which is filled with water and fish, and continued to brawl. Marvelous Mike chokes Steve Walker into the water,
Steve can't breathe and he's out cold! Marvelous Mike came back into the arena to obtain his Hardcore belt. The crowd gave him a huge ovation. An
Exciting match! After the match, a 7' hooded man opens the restaurant's door and saves Steve Walker, the 7' hooded masked man bad mouths Marvelous Mike. A cook comes out from the kitchen and askes what happan, suddenly he hits the masked man a superkick, hey the cook is HBK!! Marvelous Mike unmask the 7'
hooded man, obviously, he's Sid Shady!! 1999 Battle Royal Women division-The order they enter as follows: Ryoko, Ruby Lam, Sonia Mann, Queen Robin, Tammy Lynn Sytch and Li'l Cheetah, lady who win win get a shot of the Women Champ, if the current Champion wins, she can choose anyone to defend.
Li'l Cheetah won a 6-man Royal Rumble: Simple description: The first women Battle Royal in AWA, Ryoko and Ruby started out after Ryoko sang a song. Ruby hits her from behind. Sonia Mann entered the ring at 0:02:00, they double team Ryoko, after 2 minutes of beating, Queen Robin hits the ring at 0:04:00, this time,Sonia Mann eliminates Ryoko after a big back drop in 0:04:52, Tammy Lynn Sytch entered at 0:06:00, Queen Robin throws out Ruby after a side step in 0:07:31. The last fighter Li'l Cheetah came down at 0:08, 4 girls fight in the ring and battle out for a few minutes. Ruby Lam trys to enter the ring again and kicks
Li'l Cheetah, Li'l Cheetah nails her a schoolgirl pin, Sonia Mann trys to save her but Li'l Cheetah moves away and Sonia hits Ruby, they are nearly eliminated, Tammy Lynn Sytch double clothesline them off the rope so she elimiated Sonia Mann in 0:11:31, Li'l Cheetah throws out Queen Robin after a hair pull in 0:15:14, Tammy Lynn Sytch low blows Li'l Cheetah but she doesn't care, and the crowds really chants behind them, Li'l Cheetah nails her two heart punch and throws out Tammy in 0:19:45. Li'l Cheetah gets 4 points;
Tammy and Queen gets 2 points. The crowd is so loud, but Tammy and Attitude,Inc hit her down, Tammy is going to take off her clothes!! A man quickly runs into the ring and protects Li'l Cheetah, Tammy and Attitude,Inc continue kick Sid Shady until some referee stop them!
1999 Battle Royal: Order of Entrance: Shane Jeckins, Steve Walker, Dark Knight, Bob Peil, Canadian Thunder, Marvelous Mike, Big Poppa Pump, Ironbreaker, I love win, Lawrence Shamrock, Dream King Guy Gagne, Jack Morris; man who win win get a shot of the World Champ, if the current Champion wins, he can choose anyone to defend. - Details: Shane Jeckins and Steve Walker started out. Shane is in tremendous shape. Dark Knight enters the ring in 0:02, but is thrown out by Shane Jeckins in 0:03:21. Meanwhile, Bob Peil enters at 0:04. Big Poppa Pump was supposed to come in as the fourth participant, but he misses the change since he's beating Steel along with Attitude,Inc, after injuring Steel, Steel was shown backstage in the wrestler's restroom, laying in the floor. A stretcher was brought into the restroom,and Steel was hauled outside of the arena into an ambulance. Meanwhile, the action continues in the ring. Steve Walker eliminates several superstars Shane Jeckins in 0:05; Bob Peil in 0:06, After
Bob Peil is eliminated, Canadian Thunder made his way down, then Marvelous
Mike entered at 0:08, action continues. Ironbreaker entered at 0:10, they brawl each others. As the action continued in the ring, Canadian throws
Marvelous Mike, the new Hardcore Champ, after an atomic drop in 0:11:01; I love win entered the ring at 0:12, Marvelous Mike came down to the ring,
Steve Walker eliminates himself and runs behind Marvelous Mike, in 0:12:01.
Lawrence Shamrock entered at 0:14. He quickly throws out Ironbreaker in 0:14:29. Region member Dream King entered at 0:16:00. Meanwhile, Canadian
Thunder throws out I love win after a moonsault bodyblock in 0:17:39. Jack
Morris entered at 0:20:00, Thunder clothesline Jack Morris but throws out
Lawrence Shamrock accidentally, in 0:22:07, Dream King argue with Thunder;
Jack Morris went after Dream King and throws out Dream King in 0:23:30, Only
2 wrestlers in the ring, Jack Morris and Canadian Thunder, they fight several minutes, Canadian hits Jack Morris with Thunder From Above, and he climbs to the top turnbuckle, but Jack Morris hits him a running dropkick, Canadian
Thunder fell off the top turnbuckle, Jack Morris eliminated Thunder in
0:28:49, and becomes the winner of the Battle Royal!!!!! Big Shot Bob Peil comes down to celebrate.
