WWCW Chaotic Colomn, dated 7th July 2001

July 12, 2001, 12:00:00 AM

Chaotic Column - 07-07-01 Edition by Red Dragon I guess the first thing to say would be 'hello'. The second thing would be 'welcome'. And then...see, then I begin to get stuck. The obvious thing to do would be to start from the very beginning. Most of you - if not all of you - know me as Red Dragon, the fun-lovin' greatest
(and only) WWCW Interstate Champion of all time, who has an unhealthily close relation with his buddies Maverick and Kinky John Fowler. I'll be taking over the reins of the WWCW magazine / column / article from now on.
Aren't you lucky people? If you're going to be reading my mindless babble for the foreseeable future, I suppose it would be best if you knew a little more about me. So here goes... My real name's [name withheld]. Don't ask me what it means, because I don't know. At the time of writing, I'm 16 years, 5 months, 11 days, 14 hours and
24 minutes old, and I've been living in Bangor, North Wales all my life. Never heard of the place? Don't worry. Neither has anyone else. I'm interested in wrestling obviously, but I'm also a big fan of rock music, comedy movies, video gaming and The Simpsons, as well as an interesting variety of pizzas.
I haven't been in e-fedding very long...in fact, some of you may just remember my very first RP, posted on the old WWCW Roleplay Board at 8:41 pm on the 27th of October, 2000. It seems like so long ago now - I got into the federation by the way of a complete fluke, as I happened to be in the
WWF.com chatroom at the same time as Jonny C (then known as Joey M, of course). Many of you are much more experienced and talented than me, and yet it's yours truly that gets my own column. That's life for you. Get used to it. Other than my glittering WWCW career, I'm also actively involved with
Jonny C's promotion, RSW, both as a wrestler and as a staff member. And it is I who'll be writing these kerrazy columns whenever I can be bothered, taking over the task from the capable hands of LBB (come to think about it, does he even know about this?!). In each column I'll be going over a number of things - what's right and wrong with the fed, short event recaps, what's caught my eye recently, and how we can make this a better fed for all of us, interspersed of course with my own unique brand of wacky humour. To start off then, let's have a quick recap of the Tuesday Night
Massacre show that was held on the 2nd of July. Event Recap - Tuesday Night Massacre We start the show with the usual shot of the crowd displaying their witty signs before cutting to Robert and Jerry, who offer their thoughts on the rumour that Dizz may be leaving the WWCW for pastures new. Jay Hunter is standing by with Julia Parker, and it seems that old Jay might be turning into a fan favourite right before our eyes...we'll see. It may be just for tonight, as he will be taking on a bona fide heel in Dark Venom.
After Jay's spiel is over, we're ready to go to our first match of the evening, which is Chris Stiller against the returning Mr. OOC. The match begins explosively with an Olympic Slam, and is dominated throughout by power moves from both men. After DDTs, DVDs and suplexes are traded between the two, it's OOC who picks up the win on his comeback match, forcing
Stiller to tap out of the Steiner recliner. An exciting opening match that left us in doubt as to who would emerge victorious.
Another backstage segment follows, as we overhear Dizz talking on the phone. He says that 'this WWCW is beginning to get beyond me', and that he's ready to move on. A hint of things to come? We can only wonder. Who should come into the office after the phone is put down but The ICON and everyone's favourite lizard, Red Dragon (yay). He tells them to be on special lookout for Ruin tonight, and to take him out at the first sighting.
Lars vs Flood is next, a rematch for the hardcore title from last week's show that planted seeds of doubt in Flood's mind about the mysterious blackout man. Once the two men are in the ring they start trading quick, wild blows that leave them both breathless. After a chair shot, a sharpshooter and several suplexes inside the ring, the two men return to the locker room area, Lars is blinded with a blast from a fire extinguisher, before Flood takes the match back into the ring. Just as the DownPour is to be hit, the blackout strikes again, leaving Flood an unconscious heap in the ring, and giving Lars a chance to make the cover for the win. A good continuation of last week's match, keeping us in suspense over the identity of Darkman. Cool name, huh? Whaddya mean, it's already been taken?
A much-hyped match follows as Jay Hunter takes on Dark Venom for the right to take him on at Reckoning Day for the IC Title. We start with a lot of striking moves, and after some more offence by both men, Jay botches a lionsault that begins the ten-count. Both men soon get up though, and Venom begins an offensive streak, 'stomping a mudhole' into his opponent - a reminder of his rattlesnake days there, methinks. But Jay soon fights his way back with a Hollycaust, an Aztec Suplex and his finisher - the Texas
Cloverleaf hold known as 4 play, that gives him the win, and gives us the second submission victory of the night. This match did a good job of putting the returning Jay Hunter over as a serious contender, and I for one am looking forward to the PPV match against Venom.
Up next, BDM takes on another returning superstar in the form of Mad Dawg. Before the match starts, Big Doyle Man gets on the mic and actually chastises Team LBB and all it stands for, even getting a face pop in the process! Did you ever notice that Big Doyle Man isn't actually that big? I just find that to be odd. Anyway, the match starts similarly to the last one with a good few striking moves being used by both men. An early and futile cover is made by BDM, but he follows up with two Benoit-style German suplexes for a two-count. After fighting his way out of a reverse chin lock,
Mad Dawg throws BDM to ringside and after some heavy offence, narrowly misses hitting a powerbomb on his opponent through the announce table, putting the ref out with an elbow in the process. Dawg nearly has his head knocked off with a chairshot, but BDM makes up for the mistake with a fierce shot with the ring bell.
The two men get back in the ring, but Mad Dawg doesn't stay in for long as he's thrown out and lands smack bang on top of the announce table! Just as
Mad Dawg has regained the advantage and is about to go for his top-rope finisher, who should storm in but the shady characters of Team LBB, who go to town on The Dawg. But their plans are soon scuppered by the arrival of
The Boss, ICON and Red Dragon (yay), who engage LBB's troops in a fierce brawl, with BDM and Mad Dawg stuck in the middle! As we go to commercial, bodies are strewn around the ring, a testament to this white-hot feud. Great match, and a pretty insane ending. Nice!
With the chaos over, main event time looms, pitting The Boss against the WWCW World Champ, Justin Vincible. But the match never took place - Boss revealed to the crowd that he wasn't ready to fight a man like Vincible, and so introduced his replacement - Red Dragon (yay)! It was a high-paced match, with Red being portrayed very well as an athletic, high-flying God among men. Well, not quite, but you catch my drift. After a succession of neckbreakers, spinning elbows, hurracanranas and shooting star presses, it looks like Red is going to get the victory with the Flight Of The Dragon.
But who should storm in but both members of The Brig, Jay and OOC, who pull the ref out of the ring just as he is about to make the three-count.
DAMN YOU TO HELL!!!!! Ahem. Sorry. Jay hits the Zero Signal on Red, prompting Team Dizz to storm down the ramp and engage the members of The Brig, which obviously anger Team
LBB, who also make their appearance and cause the second double DQ of the night. And I suppose I have to mention Red getting hit with Vincible's Final
Chapter. Sigh. As the show comes to end, all three factions size each other up, which only serves to hint at the fireworks there will be between them over the coming weeks.
This was undoubtedly the best card WWCW has put out in recent times. It wasn't simmed for one thing, which made it a Hell of a lot easier to read.
Angle progression was good, and we got to see some brutal moments - Mad Dawg getting thrown out of the ring through the announce table, anyone? I think we'll all be seeing some drastic improvements in the way the fed is run in the near future, which will plainly be seen both in the cards, and in our own RPs.
I can honestly say that I can't remember a time when the entire WWCW roster just seemed to gel as much as it does now. Absolutely everyone is involved in some way with this 3-way stable feud, and it has to be said that everyone plays their part well. Judge and Vincible are instantly hateable heels, and
Dark Venom is getting there, too. Jay Hunter and OOC are funnier than I ever remember them being, ICON is as great as he ever was, and The Boss has been a pleasant surprise - I wasn't aware of his IWA and CWF background.
And even though we've got some great talent on board and have got rid of the dead wood that used to clog up our roster pages (anyone remember Juice? The
Mur? Jordan Suicide? Thought not.), it can still be said that we're in need of some solid main-eventers. I know that everyone in this fed is worthy of a good spot on the roster, but if we were to push everyone, we wouldn't have anyone to fight for the lower titles. This is a plea to all members of the
WWCW roster - if you're involved with another fed, or if you know anyone that RPs well from a fed you used to be involved with, get in touch with them and tell them about us. Dizz and I have been trying to get a hold of some fresh talent, and by gum, it's hard.
RPs that caught my eye this week... Mr. OOC - 'Judge, Venom, Red Dragon, Lars...listen up...' The Judge - 'Visiting the bailiff in the local mental institution' And on the cool quotes side of things, we have... 'How many licks does it take to get to the tootsie roll center of a tootsie pop?' - Bailiff 'Shitbuzz.' - Brad Vickers 'What a guy!' - Bruce Jones, referring to Mr. OOC Now then, just like LBB used to do, I'm going to end my little article thing with a news & rumours section. Some are false, others are true. I'm going to need you, the good folk of the WWCW roster, to email me some juicy tidbits that you'd like to reveal to the World. They don't have to be true at all.
In fact, they can be as false as Trish's breasts, it's up to you. You can get in touch with me at either
[detail withheld] or [detail withheld] . If I start a rumour about you, don't go blabbing all over the place that it's a big, stinking lie. It only takes the fun away. And now, the juicy stuff...
It's been whispered in the locker room that Dizz is thinking of jumping ship to another federation, as the pressures of running WWCW are getting to be a strain on him. If he did abandon us, that would put LBB in charge. But with some doubts hanging over his future here as well, it's anyone's guess as to what's going to transpire...
Lars was sighted speaking on the phone backstage with someone he referred to as a 'small fry', and with Lars being only 5'11'', what could this possibly mean?
Many people are wondering about the status of a mysterious masked man that has been prowling the backstage area of the WWCW arena. He is described as having a 'prominent mean streak', and a strange taste in clothing...
With the popularity of RSW picking up, it's been hinted that an alliance might form between both federations. But how will this work out if the rumours that RSW's Oroku Saki and our very own President Dizz have their differences are to be believed?
The mysterious blackouts that have been hitting WWCW shows have generated a lot of interest as to who has been causing them. Could it be a new wrestler, an old star, or simply a current member of the roster who wishes to make a bigger name for themselves? I have a feeling we'll get to know pretty soon.
Finally, rumours are flying that several former WWCW greats have been sickened by the current state of the roster and are heading back here to reclaim their place at the top. Names being mentioned include Wilburman,
Urchin, C4, Topp Dogg, Inferno and even Purity. There are other rumours flying that VP Tazz is in the wings, waiting to make his grand return.
Wouldn't you know it, I think I'm done. If you have any comments about this first effort from me (good or bad, I can handle it), be sure to send them my way. Those magical addresses once again are - EITHER
[detail withheld] OR [detail withheld] . Also, be sure to send in those rumours. You get a big feeling of power messing with people's minds like this, believe me.
Until the next time folks, be excellent to each other. And... ...party on, dudes.
