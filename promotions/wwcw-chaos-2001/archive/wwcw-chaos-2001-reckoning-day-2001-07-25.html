WWCW Reckoning Day part 1

July 25, 2001, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Live from the Staples Center Los Angeles, Chaos TV proudly presents, WWCW's Reconing Day!! Brought to you in full surround sound and Technicolor. Any unauthorized use WILL result in legal action from our highly trained army of lawyers. They may be midgets, but we believe in equal opportunity, and they will take your ass to court. You have been warned.
(As the scene fades in, we find ourselves in the sold out Staples Center, Los Angeles, California, USA, Earth, The Milky Way, yad a yada a yada. The crowd is going wild as a massive set of Pyro's explode, a wall of fire forming in front of the Dizzytron, before they fire up several fireworks that explode into an array of colors. The crowd again go wild, before the Dizzytron cuts to scenes of LBB and Dizz argueing. We see images of ICON power bombing LBB from the rampway, as well as Ruin laying out Dizz.)
(The Dizzytron then explodes into scenes of color as we see various members of Team Dizz and Team LBB throwing punches, a large bang coming through the
PA with each landed punch, before the music fades down to scenes of Dizz speaking with the CWF, and Wilburman storming from Dizz's office, before we cut back to the fans.)
(As with before, the fns are going wild, and showing support for their wrestlers. The camera zooms into on very noticeable sign which reads "FOR
YOUR INFORMATION, RED DRAGON IS BETTER THAN KANYON!!" The sign is shown upon the Dizzytron to which the crowd go wild. There are various drawings of wrestlers, and the fans support of ICON through the banners is made evident.)
RG: WELCOME FOLKS TO THE SOLD OUT STAPLES CENTRE!! Tonight we will have many questions answered for you, the most important of which is Who Will Be our President following tonights Pay-Per-View??
JS: I'll tell you who. LBB, my future boss. He wont be yours, as reliable sources tell me this is your last evening with us, and after tonight, LBB is going to be firing your ass for a much more talented, much younger, and much more attractive naked woman. Oh how I cant wait. The fun that will be had as I commentate and she, well, sucks, making me the commentator..if you get my drift.
RG: Oh shut up!! Tonight is billed to be a historic night, and not for the reasons our friend Jerry here portrays. Tonight, the saga between Lars and
Flood comes to a head in a match up for the hardcore title!! We will also see BDM take on Mad Dawg in a circle of cars match!
JS: On top of that, we have Maverick vs Boss, as well as Mr.OOC settling their differences both in, and out of ring differences in a match for the
European Belt!! RG: And that's not all! Jay will be meeting a mystery opponent for the Intercontinental belt, as well as the big title match. ICON takes on Red Dragon and Justin Vincible in a triple threat Hell in a Cell match for the world belt. JS: I have no doubts that Justin will remain the World champion after a victorious night for him, and LBB. Tonight, Dizz and all that he stands for shall be wiped out, and it shall be LBB who stands victorious. LBB. Prez
LBB in fact. That holds a certain ring to it! RG: Maybe so, but up first we have our Hardcore Title rematch pitting Lars against Flood, and these two are far from strangers to each other, Jerry.
JS: Damn right - they've fought many times in the past, and they've been interrupted by that blackout attacker on many occasions. Let's hope it doesn't happen again tonight.
(Just then, the lights in the arena are cut off; images of thunder & lightning flash on the Dizzytron and mist floods from backstage, covering the stage. Through the mist, purple laser lights are shot up and as the house lights are turned on again, the Deftones' 'Shut Up And Drive' is played over the PA System and Flood makes his way from backstage, complete with a shopping cart full of hardcore weapons. He yells loudly before making his way down the ramp, and once at ringside he throws the trolley over to the top rope into the ring before climbing in himself.)
RG: Would you look at those weapons?! I tell you, blood is going to be spilled here tonight!
JS: There's a few 2x4s in there, some pipes...is that a cactus? (Operation Ivy's 'Yellin' In My Ear' hits the speakers and Lars walks out onto the stage, also getting a good response for the crowd. He walks nonchalantly down the ramp, shaking his head in disgust at the sight of
Flood's cavalcade of weaponry. He climbs into the ring and paces it, not once taking his eyes off his opponent.)
RG: The bell rings, and we're off! Flood gets the first offence in with an elbow to the side of the head, but Lars fights back with a few stiff elbows of his own. Folks, this match will not be pretty. Lars is whipped into the turnbuckle...
JS: Flood's going for a clothesline here...but Lars moves out of the way just in time, and Flood just went crashing into the turnbuckle with his chest! Ooh, and Lars just followed up with a bulldog, smashing Flood's forehead into the top of the turnbuckle!
(As Flood lies motionless on the mat, Lars delivers an angry kick to the trolley of weaponry, sending its contents flying across the ring. Flood gets up, but Lars is waiting with a belly-to-belly suplex that smashes Flood's head against the trolley! Lars gets up to an almighty pop, and glances bitterly at the weapons strewn about the ring before making an early cover.)
RG: There's the pin! One...two...and a kick out!! JS: Flood did well to kick out after that last encounter with the trolley, and he's on the attack! Flood with a barrage of rights and lefts, knocking
Lars into the ropes! RG: Wait a second - what's Flood doing now? He's got a lead pipe in his hands...someone stop him! He's going to attack Lars with that pipe!
JS: Lighten up Rob, it's a hardcore match for God's sake! Woah! There's a killer strike, and Lars got sent over the top rope!
(Flood picks up his trolley of goodies and flings it to ringside, narrowly missing Lars' head. He climbs out of the ring and drags his opponent up off the floor before whipping him into the steel steps, following up with an incredible spear! He returns to his trolley and picks out a large piece of wood almost entirely wrapped in barbed wire before moving towards his opponent, a sick smile on his face. He swings, but Lars moves out of the way and Flood strikes the ring post, sending shockwaves throughout his entire body.)
RG: Lars grabs Flood by the arm and whips him halfway up the entrance ramp now - folks, let me remind you that in this match, falls count anywhere in the building, and indeed out of it.
JS: Lars is following Flood like a predator about to pounce on his prey, but look out! Flood still has that barbed wire 2x4 in his hand, and he just smashed Lars across the face with it, splitting him open!
(Seeing his own blood, Lars gets enraged and starts hammering Flood with blow after blow, sending the weapon flying out of his hand and driving him up the ramp towards the backstage area. When the two men are on the stage,
Flood is knocked to the floor and Lars capitalizes with an ankle lock on his opponent, prompting Flood to scream out in pain. But the Dallas native manages to break out of the hold by spinning onto his stomach and nailing
Lars with a deadly kick with his free leg. Both men get up, Flood getting the upper hand with a double-arm DDT onto the cold steel of the stage.)
RG: There's absolutely no give whatsoever on that stage - it's solid steel! Lars must be in a whole world of hurt right about now! JS: Jesus, that cliché's older than your mother. RG: I beg your pardon?! (Flood leaves Lars on the stage and heads behind the curtain. After a while, Lars painfully gets to his feet and bitterly walks into the backstage area, searching for his opponent. He passes through a busy storage area, and officials direct him to the locker-room section of the arena. Treading softly, Lars listens at the door of one of the locker rooms, but is ambushed from behind by Flood, emerging from another one of the small rooms! Lars is sent head-first into the wall before being thrown through a door that reads
'Official WWCW Referees'.) JS: Who's that in the room?! It's Martyn Clarke, David Ellery and Earl Robinson! Hee hee! Look at 'em scatter! RG: Flood certainly displaying a mean streak here as he rams the head of Lars into that wall once, twice, three times!! But Lars is fighting back! He just kicked Flood in the...
JS: Googlies! RG: ...and now it's Flood's turn to be smashed into the wall! (After smashing Flood's head into the wall five times, Lars rips a spare referee's shirt off a hook and wraps it around Flood's neck, choking him.
Gasping for air, Flood collapses to the floor, but is to be put in even more pain when Lars climbs up on the referees' bench and leaps off with a leg drop! Flood moans in pain as Lars hooks the leg...)
RG: That's ONE... JS: And TWO... RG: And a kick out! (Lars curses loudly and gets up before spotting a sink on the wall. Showing his immense strength, he rips the sink out which makes water spurt all over the room, drenching both competitors. Lars hoists the sink high above his head, intending to hit Flood's head with it. The sink is dropped, but Flood rolls out of the way just as it crashes down on the floor, breaking into hundreds of pieces. Again Lars curses before delivering a sharp kick to his opponent's head and picking him up off the floor. Flood is then thrown out of the doorway, landing in a heap in the corridor beyond it. Lars follows, closes the door behind him, grabs Flood by the hair and leads him back into the large storage area.)
RG: Should Lars be doing this? There are innocent people here...they could be hurt. JS: Lighten up Rob, it's a hardcore match for God's sake! RG: Will you stop saying that? (Lars carelessly flings Flood onto the concrete floor and heads over to a large group of crates in the middle of the room. He rips the top off one of the crates and looks inside to find a huge collection of steel folding chairs, before tipping the contents of the crate on the floor of the storage area. By now, Flood has risen and heads over to his opponent, taking him by surprise with a back body drop onto the mound of chairs that has piled up on the floor. He gets up and takes two of the chairs in his hands before clashing them together and waiting for Lars to get to his feet. The punk soon gets up, only to be sent back down again with a double chair shot to the head.)
RG: Lars looks to be in serious pain here...he's clutching his head, and referee Vinny Fraser has gone to check on him. But Flood tosses Fraser aside and starts choking Lars!
JS: That'll teach Fraser! No-one talks about my sister like that! RG: What did he say about her, then? JS: Tell you later. (Vinny Fraser tries to split the two apart, but to no avail, as Lars continues to be choked. Flood gives up of his own accord soon enough, but doesn't count on a supremely pissed-off Lars getting up immediately and giving his opponent a pump handle suplex right through another wooden crate, spilling its contents - dozens of steel scaffolds - out onto the floor.
Flood lies motionless among the scaffolds as Lars picks one up and nails a stinging blow right across Flood's chest!)
JS: Holy crappola! I think I heard something crack right there! (Lars heads for a wide entryway on the far side of the storage area and waits for Flood to get up as various WWCW personnel scurry away. Before long, the Texan gets to his feet and picks up one of the scaffolds before engaging Lars in a stare down from opposite sides of the room. The staring continues as Flood advances on his opponent, and only stops when Lars elbows his opponent in the chin.)
RG: A good, solid strike there from Lars, but Flood is there with the scaffold, and smashes Lars in the face! Lars is sent flying into that wide corridor beyond the entryway, and skids along the floor.
JS: Where does that corridor lead, anyway? What's through that big, black curtain on the far side? And why does it slope up so much?
RG: How devilishly mysterious. (Flood pads after his opponent, scaffold in hand, but Lars kicks the weapon out of his grasp and flips to his feet before nailing a neck breaker, then heading over to a drinks cart on the side of the corridor. He grabs the cart, and as Flood gets to his feet, rams it into his stomach at full-speed, sending Flood flying right back to the entryway. Lars pushes the cart to the very top of the sloping corridor, and in a suicidal move, climbs on top of it as it begins to roll forward!)
JS: Holy...! RG: Lars is insane! That cart's going 20, 25, 30 miles an hour down that ramp!! He's going to run Flood over!!
JS: NO! NO!! FLOOD ROLLED OUT OF THE WAY...and Lars just crashed into another crate at an incredible speed!
(Flood drags himself to his feet and begins laughing at Lars, who is sprawled on the concrete floor of the storage area. But his laughs quickly die out when the punk emerges behind a crate and climbs over it, making a beeline for his opponent. Flood is taken off his feet with a spear, but Lars seems to be injured as he clutches his ribs and leans against the wall of the corridor, wincing in pain. Before long Flood hoists himself up on his feet and takes advantage of a weakened Lars, kneeing him in the face and lifting him up for a power bomb!)
RG: A powerful move there from Flood...and there's the cover! JS: One!! Two!!! WHAT?!!! RG: Umm...the lights in the backstage area just got turned off, and we're in total darkness here at ringside as well. You don't think...?
JS: What are those sounds coming from backstage? Someone's getting beaten up good - d'you think it's that blackout attacker again?
RG: I sincerely hope not. And we have light once again...oh, dear Lord. Lars and Flood lie bloodied and beaten on the floor of that corridor...Lars has a huge rip in that white shirt of his...this doesn't look good for either competitor. Damn these blackouts!
JS: Geez, you'd think we'd be able to afford a better electrician. RG: This is no time for jokes, Jerry. I sincerely doubt that these men can continue this match...and...and we're being told over our headsets that this match will NOT be stopped. Hardcore rules apply, and this match will continue!
(Both men lie motionless for a long while as their fans in the crowd chant their names. Lars gets a huge cheer as he is the first to get to his feet, but is closely followed by Flood. They stagger blindly up the corridor towards the curtain before Lars turns around and nails a right hook that sends Flood skidding down the corridor, giving Lars a chance to recuperate briefly by leaning against the wall. Flood soon gets to his feet however, and takes Lars on in a frenzied fistfight that sends both men through the curtain and onto...)
RG: THE DIZZYTRON!! THE DIZZYTRON!! MY GOD, BOTH MEN ARE ENGAGED IN A SCRAP RIGHT ON TOP OF THE DIZZYTRON! JS: This don't look good... RG: And Flood is knocked to the floor! Lars is trying to push Flood over the side of the Dizzytron! My God, he's going to be killed! Flood resists his opponent and gets to his feet...
JS: DEATH VALLEY DRIVER! RG: Lars just nailed a Death Valley Driver on Flood, and he's certain to be pushed over this time...oh goodness, I can't watch...!
JS: Wait a second...who's that?! RH: Huh? JS: A hooded figure just emerged from behind the curtain...and he's taking on Lars! Wait...that's not a man! PUPPIES!!
RH: This...person...is taking on Lars in a fistfight, which is not...OH MY GOD!!! LARS JUST GOT KNOCKED OFF THE DIZZYTRON! LARS JUST GOT KNOCKED OFF THE DIZZYTRON! JS: Jesus Christ, who is that?!! RG: And now Flood is getting up...HOLY S***! HE JUST GOT THROWN OFF AS WELL!! HE LANDED ON TOP OF LARS!! JS: Wait...what's this? There's Vinny Fraser on the stage where those two bodies are. What's he doing? He's...he's counting the three! That's it!!
RG: What?! JS: Look - Flood inadvertently pinned Lars' shoulders when he fell on him...Flood is the new Hardcore Champion!
(As 'Shut Up And Drive' is played over the speakers, a throng of medics rush onto the stage and pick both men up, putting them on stretchers. As they are carried backstage, the entire crowd rises, giving them a standing ovation.)
RG: Truly incredible, and the question still hangs in the air - who was that mysterious person in the hood? Perhaps we'll get to know soon enough. Well folks, time now for an eagerly-anticipated match...
JS: Wait Rob, I'm getting word that there's a disturbance backstage. Let's take a look-see.
RG: What the Hell is that?! Lars has got up off his stretcher, and is heading towards Flood! What's he doing? He's got a steel chair!
JS: NO!! He's smashing Flood in the face with that chair! Again and again!! My God, he's lost it! RG: Lars has snapped! What the Hell is wrong with him! He drops the chair, leaving Flood a bloody mess!
(Lars gives the camera a one-finger salute, and has a few choice words to say...) Lars: F*** you! F*** all of you!! RG: My Lord, Lars has lost it completely, but Flood is the new Hardcore Champion! What a twist in the proceedings!! And hang on a second.. We have Red Dragon, Lars's former colleague in the back, wanting to say something!! Lets throw this one over! (We cut to the backstage area of the Staples Centre, where Red Dragon and Maverick are relaxing in front of a TV screen with all five members of the Cherry-Flavoured Antacids - singer [name withheld], guitarists David Jones and
Conor Martin, bassist Hawys Tomos and drummer Huw Ap Dewi.)
RD: What the...that was pretty mean of Lars, don't you think? What the heck is he up to? I'll have to have a word with him... anyway, how are you guys enjoying WWCW hospitality?
Huw Ap Dewi: It's great. Did I ever tell you about the time... David Jones: Yeah, shut up now. Where's that servant bloke with our drinks? M: Yeah, where is he? RD: Hang on, I'll get him. KINK!!! (Kinky John comes rushing onto the scene wearing a pink frilly apron and carrying a tray piled high with glasses of beer.)
KJ: Your drinks, ladies and gentlemen. [name withheld]: Damn right. (Conor takes a beer from the tray, sloshes it about in his mouth for a while, and spits it out onto Kinky John's new apron.)
Conor Martin: This is Carling! I wanted Foster's! FOSTER'S!! RD: Kink, get the man his Foster's, for goodness sake. Honestly, you'd think we aren't even paying you for this.
Hawys Tomos: Are you paying him? RD: Good point. M: Anyway, I'd better get ready for my match. See you guys later, alright? (Everyone says goodbye to Maverick, who exits stage right. There is a brief pause in the conversation.)
Huw Ap Dewi: Did I ever tell you about the time... RD: Yeah, shut up now. Let's watch the rest of the show. RG: Well, interesting words from Red Dragon there!! But I believe we are now ready to go outside where we have Mad Dawg and BDM about to fight in a circle of cars match!! This should be a mightily bloody match, and no doubt of great entertainment value to the sadists who follow us.
(Mad Dawg And The Big Doyle Man Begin To Circle each other within the cars, Bdm, The more Technical of the two makes jumps to MD's legs, but Mad Dawg carefully avoids. The two stand again and start a collar and arm tie up, before MD powers BDM into one of the bonnets. However, the ref pulls him off, so agreeing MD walks away but turns back with consecutive forearms sending BDM sprawling. BDM falls down and rolls away from a stomp as the ref once again separates the two. BDM gets up and dusts himself down as the two circle once more)
RG: Mad Dawg rushing in again but OH GOD! A devastating drop toe hold slamming Mad Dawgs face into the car bonnet from BDM there. MD rolls away from the bonnet, and there, BDM's drawn first blood, Mad Dawg just spat blood. He busted up his lip from the impact there!
JS: And he's rolling away now for security, but BDM is stomping him back down, there's a elbow, and another! But the third didn't connect there, and instead, BDM gets nothing but the concrete of the parking lot.
RG: Mad Dawg stumbles back to his feet, and Mad Dawg is now stomping away on him! This is getting brutal! Mad Dawg follows up with a falling fist drop, and another, wow, those are particularly brutal, BDM trying to crawl away. But Mad Dawgs on top now, and bringing some brutal front smashes onto
BDMs face, really hitting him hard. (Mad Dawg lets go of BDM and lets him slump to the ground, pointing out the brass knuckles on his hand and leaping onto a bonnet, he measures another fist drop onto BDM with the knucks before picking up a now bleeding BDM and attempting a pile driver, aiming to drill BDM's head into the concrete, but as he tries a lift BDM manages to block it. Mad Dawg goes for it again, to no avail, and so he tries a third time but BDM counters to a back body drop onto one of the bonnets. BDM hammers away with lefts and rights, making sure that MD is incapacitated before positioning him to lean across two of the bonnets)
RG: I don't like the looks of this, Mad Dawg is hanging loosely between the two bonnets now, his head and neck on one, his legs on the other and BDM is climbing to the top of the car.
JS: GOOD GOD!! GUILOTENE LEG DROP!! MAD DAWG SNAPPED IN TWO BY THE BIG DOYLE MAN!! (MD rolls away wriggling in the dust, but barely able to move, as BDM once again climbs to the top of the car, and raises his arms before nailing a frog splash, he covers but only gets a two count, BDM gets up and starts to harass the ref over a slow count whilst MD gets onto his hands and knees.
BDM sees this, and stomps on Mad Dawg some more and pulls him up by the hair but MD throws a handful of dust into BDM's eyes, blinding him.)
RG: MD now with a comeback has grabbed BDM from behind. Good God! A Inverted DDT onto the Dusty floor! BDM now on the floor. And Mad Dawg with a running elbow drop, straight to the heart!
(MD stomps BDM down heavily for a few seconds before hitting a standing splash and covering, only getting a two he pushes the ref away and picks BDM up, nailing him with consecutive blows the already bloodied face of BDM. MD now whips BDM in to the car bonnets and BDM springs over, cracking the glass. MD jumps onto the car and attempts a suplex, But BDM blocks, MD attempts another but once again BDM blocks and counters with a falling arm bar.)
JS: WOW!! The falling arms bar there!! The snapping downward motion sent MD' s head straight through the glass.. The Referees asking MD if he submits- but MD saying no, he's reaching out for something..
RG: But there are no ropes in a ring of cars ma-GOOD GOD!! MD JUST GRABBED A PIECE OF GLASS AND STABBED BDM WITH IT!! BDM BLEEDING HEAVILY FROM THE ARM!! (BDM stumbles up onto the roof of the car and painfully removes the piece of glass, tossing it aside, MD stumbles up after him and the sloppily exchange punches before the Dawg nails a kick to the midsection, following with a face buster onto the roof.)
JS: Good God, That's more there then your standard man could take in 3 weeks- kids, don't try this at home (Mad Dawg pulls BDM up and slams him between his legs calling for a power bomb) RG: well it looks like a power bomb on the bonnet is in store for the Big Doyle Man. NO!! JS: Hee Hee, A Falling pile driver through the sunroof there, you called that one wrong- looser
RG: In my 25 years of commentating- I havn't seen anything quite like that since our last Pay Per View!
JS: And They Don't Call Us Chaos Wrestling For Nothing Folks!! (BDM, dangling half in the car and half out slumps down into the car whilst MD jumps down he heads to the door and opens it up, only to receive a vicious blow from a steering wheel lock straight to the head- the dull thud of the steel being echoes by fans cheers. Mad Dawg stumbles back and takes another into the chest, MD collapses as doyle drags him back to the car.
Placing his prone head between the door and car.) RG: Oh God No, It doesn't take a fool to figure out what's next- Big Doyle Man repetitively slamming Mad Dawgs head within the door now! Oh, and now a cover 1-2-late kick out by the dawg
(BDM picks up Mad Dawg and slaps him once, MD slumps back and dares BDM to "fire another" as true wrestling style Mad Dawg looks up and BDM brings back his arm, Mad Dawg moves away last second, having played possum, BDM slams his fist straight into the back window, gripping his loosely bleeding fist and stepping back into Mad Dawg who rams him head first through the window before pulling him out and onto a bonnet with a scoop slam. MD nails a quick leg drop before picking BDM.)
RG: Jackhammer countered there by BDM who slides down his back and nails a clubbing forearm to the back of Mad Dawg...OH MERCY A GERMAN SUPLEX OFF THE
BACK OF THE CAR, OVERHEAD RELEASE GERMAN SUPLEX OFF THE BONNET!! MAD DAWGS NECK MUST BE IN TWO! (BDM jumps down and covers Mad Dawg but with amazing resiliency MD kicks out. BDM covers again but once more he barely gets out. BDM pulls Mad Dawg up and begins to beat him about; BDM nails a snap mare, knocking the wind out of MD and then hits a sharp blow to the spine. BDM picks up the out of action MD and raises his hand in preparation for the Doyle Bottom)
JS: BDM setting MD up for the ride.. Looks like this ones over. NO!! Mad Dawg! Mad Dawg counters with a inverted Russian leg sweep! And he's getting up! Mad Dawg Slowly getting to his feet.. He's walking away from BDM.. He's
Got a pipe. and Mad Dawg looking to end this now! RG: OH GOD!! WHAT JUST HAPPENED? WHO THE HELL? SOMEONE IN A CAR HAS JUST KNOCKED MAD DAWG DOWN!! SOMEONES KNOCKED MAD DAWG DOWN!! (The picture cuts to replay of Mad Dawg above BDM, he raises the pipe when from the side one of the "disused cars shunts him from the side- MD flies off and the car slows down- the camera shows this twice more from separate angles before cutting back to the car. The door opens and too a huge pop..)
RG: That's Jeff Johnson- Former GWA superstar Jeff Johnson looks to have just retired Mad Dawg with that hit.But Why? And what's he doing at our PPV?
JS: BDM is back up- another former GWA superstar..he's looking confused.. DOYLE BOTTOM!! Jeff Johnson just nailed BDM With his own move! The humiliation!! He's pulling Mad Dawg over him now..1! 2! 3!
RG: Mad Dawg picks up the victory- but what are Jeffs motives? And what about Mad Dawg? This is carnage!! What the hell is going on here??
JS: I don't know, but I like it!! But someone I like even more is LBB, and we've got him backstage!! Wooohoooo!!
