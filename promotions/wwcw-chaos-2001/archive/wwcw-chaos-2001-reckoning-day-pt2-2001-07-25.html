WWCW Reckoning Day Part 2

July 25, 2001, 12:00:00 AM

Back stage in the offices for the powers to be. Where one LBB gets ready for his match. All decked out in his tights, no shirt, all sweaty from the water be poured over himself. His head band that he wears around his forehead is soaked as well. He is stretching when Ruin walks in.
LBB has been quite, quite as of late, he has a lot on his mind. Like running the WWCW and kicking Dizz's ass later tonight. Ruin walks up to him.
"Right! We do this shit right tonight! We'll own this fucking joint!" Ruin says all gun hoe.
LBB stops and stares at him. "We?" he asks. "No where during all of this shit that was going down, we the word we brought up anywhere. In fact Ruin. YOU, have no part in this match tonight! It will simply be me and Dizz...in a match in which I kick Dizz's ass and once and for all run this hell hole. Dizz will be gone. Justin V will still be the champ, and you will, well, how can I say this......You'll be looking for a job else where."
Ruin looks at him all puzzled. "What's a matter? Cat got your tongue. Can't understand the words that are coming out of my mouth? Speak no English do you? Let me break it down right here. In a way that your mind will understand, YOUR FIRED!!!!" LBB says with anger in his voice, unleashing 1,000 knifes and leaving 10,00 scars on the young man's body.
"I only brought you in for one reason and one reason only. To fire people. And I did that cause I didn't total control...But after tonight. After I kick that wanker's ass all over this arena tonight. After I taste his blood, and force him to leave with his dick between his legs....The future of the
WWCW will change....forever. I will be the big dog in the house. I will be the man with the plan. I will be the one who calls the shots. And more importantly, I won't need you to do any more firing...cause I will have the power now. And some firing I will do. Icon, gone...you can bet on that. And that also means you too Ruin. So go get your shit and hit the highway. Your no longer needed here."
Ruin is mad, and sad. But he knows what is going on. He mumbles under his breathe as he turns and leaves...LBB watches him leave..."AND DON'T LET THE
DOOR HIT YOU IN THE ASS ON THE WAY OUT!" He exclaims.
"But, Dizz...I'll make sure the door hits your ass on the way out.....you can count on it."
RG: Strong words there from LBB!! No doubt he means every word he says though!! JS: You'd better believe it!! Tonight is the end of Dizz. He doesn't stand a hope in hell of beating LBB tonight, and when its all over, LBB shall be the new CEO and President of the WWCW!! And then, the real fun will begin!!
RG : Well folks, with that out of the way, we have the european championship on the line! It's The Brig vs Dark Justice! OOC vs Judge! It's next! The camera cuts to backstage, to outside The Brig's locker room. All that is heard is "Do you want that title?!" "Yes." (Jay slaps Mr. OOC) "DO YOU WANT THAT TITLE?!" "Yes!" (Jay slaps him again, harder) "I SAID DO YOU WANT THAT F*CKING TITLE?!" "YES!" (Jay pushes OOC roughly, psyching him up) "SO GO AND GET IT!!" (OOC socks Jay in the mouth. and storms out of the locker-room, in a demonic state of mind)
As OOC walks off, Jay leans back on the doorframe, smirking and nodding, and holding his jaw.
The scene cuts to Dark Justice's locker-room, where The Judge is pacing back and forth, head down, with his European title on his shoulder. Dark Venom sits on the bench, without a shirt, showing bandages on his ribs.
DV : I can't believe I'm getting forced to relinquish my Intercontinental title...I can wrestle tonight...as Jay himself would say, "I'm good to go".
(The Judge stops and looks down at him.) Judge : Listen up, Venom, there's only one belt that's on tonight that matters to me, and that's MY belt, the European title! It's the only thing on my mind, and I'll be damned if Mr.OOC and his band of lovers steal this belt from The Justice!
(DV looks up at him, not believing) (The Judge throws his hands up in anger) Judge : Oh forget it! I'm out. (The Judge turns and storms out of the room) The camera cuts from the commentator's table, to the ring, where Billy
Sheppard stands, holding the mic, shaking a little, looking up into the sky.
The spotlight finds him, as the lights go down in the area. RG : What do you make of that, Jerry? A little descention within the ranks of Dark Justice!
JS : That's right - I haven't seen The Judge take that sort of stand before - It's exactly what he needs before going into the biggest match of his life.
RG : He doesn't need this bad blood seemly brewing between the two, they are *still* the tag team champions! JS : That's right...I believe Brad Vickers is going to issue about that sometime tonight! RG : But before all that ladies and gentlemen, we have one of the Brig's founding fathers - Mr. OOC, and one half of Dark Justice - up next!
BS : The following contest...scheduled for one fall....is a LADDER MATCH...(the crowd roar!)...for the European Championship! The sound of the striking of a high-hat hits three times. *"I Disappear" hits the arena, to a huge pop!* (The crowd roar in disbelief) RG : Oh god...oh god no... JS : This couldn't be...the Wolverine... (The music stops, and *With You* by Linkin Park hits!) BS : About to enter the arena, weighing in at an even 300lbs, from The Brig,
Dublin, Ireland, Mr. OOC!
As smoke fills the entranceway, a green and red light finds it's way up the ramp, settling on the top of the ramp.
A darkened figure enters the arena, and the spotlight finds him - It's Mr. OOC! He stands, head bowed down, motionless. He then looks up, and into the crowds. Dressed in his usual attire of black combats, black boots, with a ripped OOC t-shirt. He goes to one side of the ramp, raising his hands, to a raunchous pop, and then makes his way to the ring, circling it before going up the stairs and getting in. He alights a turnbuckle, and points to his
t-shirt, with two thumbs and a smug grin. Jumping down off the middle turnbuckle, he turns and leans his back against it, waiting for his opponent.
"With You" dies down, and the crowd bustle and go quiet. "By Myself" by Linkin Park hits! he crowd pop! As it continues, the Judge comes out, clad in a Judge's robe. He stands at the top of the ramp, and takes off the robe, revealing a wrestling singlet.
Clad in electric blue pants, he shows off his European belt, rubbing it, worn around his waist.
He smirks and then walks calmly to the ring, never taking his eyes off OOC, who slides out of the ring, to the opposite side. The Judge jumps straight up, catching the top rope, and enters the ring. The lights turn down and a blue strobe hits the ring as we see a Ladder has been placed in the middle.
The Judge takes off the belt, and holds it in his right hand, and then starts climbing the ladder. When he reaches the top, he sits in the middle, standing on the second top rungs, and holds up the belt! A mass of flashes are seen, then The Judge fixes the belt, and attaches it to the cord, which gets hoisted.
The Judge gets down off the ladder, and the referee compacts the ladder, and puts it outside.
Mr. OOC slides back into the ring, and the two size up. Mr. OOC, four inches taller, looks down at The Judge and smiles. The two nudge each other with their bodies until the referee breaks them up.
The bell sounds and the match is on it's way! The two square up again, with referee Martyn Clarke telling them to go ahead. Both talk smack to each other, as the camera pans in right to their faces. Mr. OOC pushes Judge in the face, he turns and squares up to him again. The trash-talking continues. Mr. OOC pushes Judge once again, and he turns and charges at OOC! He rushes
OOC straight into the corner, and starts pummeling him with rights! The crowd boo!
Mr. OOC stops a punch and quickly grabs Judge's head, and exchanges positions! OOC starts punching Judge right in the face! He then starts kicking him in the mid-section, forcing Judge to drop to the bottom corner.
The last stomp is especially hard, to the face! Mr. OOC turns to the crowd, raising his hands, to some heat, and some pop! He turns and rushes towards Judge, be he rolls out of the way before OOC can get to him. The Judge stands there, catching his breath, and the referee restrains OOC. Judge looks at the entranceramp, and starts walking. OOC yells at Judge, who stops and turns around.
OOC, majorly pissed off, sits on the ropes, bekoning The Judge to come back. The Judge signals no, so OOC falls down and rolls out of the ring! The Judge rushes him, and starts beating him in the head, and throws him into the stairs! The Judge quickly kicks him in the stomach, and runs to the other side of the ring, looking under it. He pulls up the valence, and gets out a black and steel ladder!
He lifts it up and slides it under the bottom rope, and follows. Setting up the ladder as quick as he can, he runs up the ladder. RG : He's gonna steal it at the start! OOC gets into the ring and starts climbing the same side as Judge, and grabs his foot! The Judge uses his other foot to kick off OOC in the face! And again!
The Judge climbs louder, but OOC is able to catch him again! He grabs Judge's leg, and then OOC gets up the ladder some more! He grabs Judge's back, and then starts punching it! He starts tearing slabs out of him with some chops!
Judge, drowsy, gets pulled off the ladder! OOC climbs the rungs! He's near the top! Judge receives an inadvertant hot shot off the ropes, while OOC is touching the belt! But Judge rebounds from the ropes into the ladder - pushing it over!
OOC has no choice but to stop the fall between his legs and gets hung up in the ropes! RG : Oooh! The jewels! Mr. OOC won't be making any little Wolverine's for a while! Judge leans against the ropes, then charges at OOC, knocking him to the outside. OOC falls gingerly, hitting his lower back on the ring apron. He then sets up the ladder between the first and second rope, pointing outwards.
RG : It looks like The Judge has evil intentions in mind! The Judge rolls out of the ring, and and gives OOC a few stiff punches to the face. He props him up on the top of the ladder, face and chest leaning down on the ladder.
The Judge rolls back into the ring, and gets on the second turnbuckle, he jumps, and delivers a leg drop to the part of ladder in the ring! The part outside shoots up, smacking OOC in the face!
RG : He's out! He's out! JS : Do it, Judge! Get that title! The crowd clap in appreciation of the move, but Judge has hurt himself with the move. He slowly gets up, and looks at the belt, and then looks at OOC.
JS : He's thinking Robbie! RG : What the hell is there to think about?! Get the title!! The Judge looks at both options once more, and gets the ladder, but pushes it outside! He's going for OOC!
JS : He's going to punish OOC! He'd rather punish OOC! RG : Remember, Jerry, it was Mr. OOC who cost Dark Venom his livelihood! -
He put Dark Venom on the shelf indefinitely!
The Judge gets on the outside and picks up a prone OOC. He shakes his head, and headbutts OOC down to the floor, narrowly missing the stairs. The Judge grabs the ladder, and props it up on the stairs and the black security wall.
He picks up OOC, and lifts him up by his knee, and drops him on the ladder - knee first! The crowd pop, as OOC screams in agony! RG : That certainly woke him up! The Judge picks him up once again, but OOC can't stand. As a last resort,
OOC uses his good leg to kick The Judge between the legs! OOC stands up and leans on the apron, shaking his leg, trying to get some blood pumping back into it.
OOC pulls The Judge up to his feet, punching him squarely in the jaw, and sets him up for a suplex. Judge blocks! He tries again! Judge blocks! Judge goes for a suplex! OOC blocks! OOC knees him quickly in the stomach - snap
suplex! - onto the ladder!! The Judge bounces off the ladder, and down, holding his kidney in agony! OOC gets up slowly, and gets the ladder. He points it down at Judge stomach, and rams it in him! And again!
OOC throws the ladder into the ring! He sees the Judge, and picks him up, and gets ready for a powerbomb! OOC picks him up - he wants to slam him on the security wall! Judge quickly punches him in the face, and OOC has to let go - Judge falls to his feet, and delivers an overhead suplex! OOC gets strung out on the wall! He bounces off it and out into the crowd!
RG : Both men are down! Both want that title! Who wants it more?! Both men lay down, trying to recover, as the referee administers the 10 count! 1!....2!.....3!.....4!....5! (Judge gets up to one knee)....6! (gets up to both knees)....7! (falls back down to one)...8! (gets back up to two)....9!...(catapults himself into the ring)
RG : You know, I don't know why the ref is counting! It's a damn ladder match! JS : Don't tell him that! The crowd pop! But what from! JS : GOLDBRIDGE, in the crowd! Look! RG : OH what the HELL is this! (Running down the stairs and towards the ring - it's Brad Brittania! He's leading a group!! What THE HELL!
JS : WHO ARE THESE PEOPLE! RG : It looks like...that's Jeff Johnson! Taylor Jamison! BFJ!! HE'S BACK! WHAT THE F- JS : HEY! These guys are all from the defunct GWA! Look at those shirts! Get them outta here!
(The wrestlers jump over the barriers) RG : My GOD! Get these men out of here! Iit's the best of the GWA! Here in WWCW! RG : Brad Brittania must've masterminded this! Damn him! I gave him a start in this business - and this is how he's turned out!
The crowd pop unbelieveably for the new stars, but then it turns to heat, as they take chunks out of Mr. OOC and The Judge! It's FOUR on TWO! What can we do?!
The lights go out! The crowd roar! SAY WHAT SAY WHAT? BOOM! "All In The Family" hits! RG : IT'S THE BRIG! THE BRIG! JS : IT'S JAY HUNTER! WITH A CHAIR! WHO'S THAT WITH HIM?! IT'S ICE! IT'S
ICE! HE'S BACK!! AND BDM! THE BIG DOYLE MAN AND ICE ARE WITH JAY!
The GWA stars are rushing out of the ring, content with their mauling!! Brad Brittania kicks ICE in the stomach -- Powerbomb! But then Brad is met by the Zero Signal from Jay!!
RG : It's only Brad Brittania in the ring - BDM and ICE attend to OOC, but
Jay Hunter wields a chair! The crowd roar! He swings!
But Taylor pulls Brad out of the way, and out of the ring! The crowd boo! The GWA make their getaway from where they came, as Jay Hunter, ICE and BDM check up on OOC, looking at the GWA, they get out of the ring, and charge after them!
Judge and OOC are left motionless outside the ring! (The camera shows replays of the GWA attack) After another few minutes, the two start to move! (The scene cuts to outside, where there is a black stretch limozine waiting, as they make their getaway cheering!)
Bruce Jones runs to them, trying to get an interview : "Brad! Brad! Why are you here?! What are you doing?!"
Brad : It's here! The GWA! And we're gonna take over this fed - and there's nothing you, or anyone can do about it!!
(Brad gets in the limo and it drives off) *The scene cuts back to the ring) The two slowly get up, but Judge gets up quicker, and nails OOC in the head! The Judge slowly gets to the other side of the ring, and looks under the ring - he throws in another ladder! The crowd pop!
JS : Wait...he's still looking under the ring!! (We can see OOC stirring) (The camera cuts to behind The Judge) RG : It's a table! A table! The Judge puts the table in the ring, under the bottom rope. JS : What is he up to?! He still looks under the ring, and produces a beige bag! He holds it up! He goes into the ring, as OOC gets into the ring, and the two exchange rights! OOC gets the better of him and he knocks the bag out of his hand!
JS : IT'S TACKS! IT'S TACKS IN THE BAG! The bag lays slumped in the corner, as OOC ducks a clothesline, and delivers a german suplex! OOC picks him up and....he sets him up for a suplex, moving
The Judge back - he picks him up! He slams his legs down on the top rope - and catapult him back! A springboard suplex! The crowd roar!
OOC kicks Judge to the corner of the ring, and then turns his attention to the table! OOC sets up at the table, and then sees the tacks! OOC gets the bag, holds it up to the crowd, and then spreads it all over the table!! The crowd go wild!
RG : Oh...MY...God...this is AWFULLY....pain...ful... The Judge gets up, and hits OOC in the back of the head, making him drop the bag. Now OOC is between the table and The Judge! Judge punches him in the face, and once more, and again! OOC is teetering! Judge kicks him in the stomach - he goes for a powerbomb! OOC hits him! He lets himself drop down, and the Judge turns it into an elevated boston crab! Then the Judge walks back, and slings OOC to the ropes! OOC gets tangled - up and out!
JS : Judge sees the opportunity! Will he go for it? The Judge gets the ladder, and sets it up a foot away from the tackked-table! He positions it under the belt, and starts to climb!
Meanwhile, outside, OOC gets to his feet, and slowly gets into the ring! OOC sees Judge, and rushes towards him, missing the table! He pulls The Judge down, dropping him a few steps, and grabs his head, slamming it against the ladder! OOC runs to the other side, and starts climbing! The crowd go wild!
Both start punching each other as the both go to the top of the ladder!! The fans drip with anticipation!! OOC punches Judge! And again! He's gonna drop!!
RG : He's hanging on by one hand! OOC strikes, but Judge ducks, and replies with some punches of his own! He puts OOC in a headlock -
JS : OH my GOD is he gonna suplex him! RG : Good Lord! That would be right onto the tacks! Judge lifts OOC up and over -- but OOC turns around in mid air - landing on the rungs below Judge! What dexterity! What athleticism!
OOC lays into the back of Judge, and tries to pull him off! He hits his back again, and gets into a waist lock!!
RG : GERMAN SUPLEX!! FROM THE TOP OF THE LADDER! JUDGE AND OOC GO THROUGH THE THUMB-TACK-TABLE! THESE SICK, SADISTIC FANS GO NUTS! JS : AAAAH! RG : They're gone Jerry, this may be the last match of both these competitors... (Replays of the suplex are shown, including one from the belt's point of view!) Both lay on the mat, motionless, breathing heavily. (More replays are shown of the two going through the table, with The Judge landing squarely on his neck)
RG : Now back to a live shot, ladies and gents, these two do need medical attention! OOC sits up! The crowd pop! He sees Judge, who turned over, now resting on his stomach. He looks around, and then puts him in the Pain! He locks the Pain in! Judge screams! He wrenches back! Judge screams louder! He taps!! He taps!!
RG : He's tapping!! But it's a ladder match!! OOC releases the hold, and slowly gets up! He sees the ladder! He turns around and starts climbing!
RG : He's gonna do it! We'll have a new champion! OOC goes up, and slowly reaches up...just a few more rungs... The Judge starts moving, and starts kicking at the ladder - with him still being horizontal! He rocks the ladder, and OOC must come down! OOC gets back onto the mat, with Judge trying to pull himself up - he delivers a low blow, but it's blocked! OOC knocks him down, and positions the ladder at either side of his shoulders - The Judge can't move!
OOC climbs the ladder one more time - he looks at the belt, then at Judge, who is fighting...but fighting to stay conscious!
OOC turns around, and....SPLASH OFF THE LADDER! The crowd go mental! Judge is out! OOC gets back up, and starts climbing the rungs...up one...and another...can he do it?! OOC reaches for the belt - he's still too low! He gets up another couple of rungs, he's touching off the belt... RG : Can he...? Will he..?... The crowd are so loud as they urge OOC on! OOC gets a firm grasp of the belt, and tugs it! It won't come down! He tugs again! It comes off! The crowd go nuts! *With You* hits the airwaves! BS : The winner of this match....and NEW EUROPEAN CHAMPION.....MR. OOC! RG : What a night! What a match! These individuals have given it their all -
Here for you tonight!
OOC hugs the belt, and holds it up for all to see! Jay Hunter, ICE and BDM come down to the ring, clapping and smiling! All three get in the ring, and start climbing the ladder! Jay hugs OOC and they all raise their hands! The crowd cheer!
After the pictures and flashes, ICE, BDM and Jay Hunter get down, and OOC fumbles down the stairs and falls on the mat, gasping for breath. The Judge still lays unconsious on the floor!
The three pivk up OOC and get him out of the ring, raising his hand! RG : What a match! After interference from members of the defunct GWA, and the save from the New Brig, we have a New European Champion! And it's Mr.
OOC! We see the four disappear behind the curtain, as the camera cuts to the ring, with a bloodied Judge, barely moving.
JS : An awesome desplay of athleticism by both competitors! RG : Uhh...I think...no, I know that Judge needs some serious medical attention... JS : Here come the paramedics... (The Judge gets slowly put onto a stretcher and gets wheeled away) RG : Both men put it al on the line, folks, it's a shame that one had to go away the loser!
JS : But we've not heard the last of The Brig and Dark Justice! Dark Venom is going to relinquish the intercontinental title! That's coming up!
RG : Damn them...Jerry...they came here with no agenda but to ruin our Pay-Per-View...to ruin WWCW! JS : Don't worry - this one's not over - not over by a long shot! The camera cuts to the backstage area, showing a name-plate on the desk, that reads : Brad Vickers! The crowd cheer!
As the camera pans out, we see Brad Vickers, dressed in a nice dark grey three-piece suit, with the Intercontinental title on his shoulder. He hugs the title and looks at it, holding the belt at arms length from him. Looking into the main peice, he sees his reflection, and starts fixing his hair with his free hand. He is childishly amused with himself, laughing at his reflection in the newly-polished title.
The cameraman goes to take a close look, as it comes around the corner of the desk, perching behind Brad. Brad, on seeing the cameraman, quickly grabs the belt and holds it against his chest, intending the cameraman to go away.
The cameraman back off a little, and Brad regains his improper relationship with the Intercontinental title.
The camera goes closer, but this time Brad turns his back on the camera, and holds is at arms length. The camera zooms in on the camera.
As Brad moves the title, he sees another man's reflection in it - Dark
Venom's! Brad quickly swivvles around. "Well, hello Mr. Venom, how goes it today?" (Dark Venom stares silently at Brad, hugging Dark Venom's title) (Brad looks around, then finally notices the belt) "...Lovely weather..." (Dark grabs the belt forcefully while shouting "Gimme that belt!") Brad, looking at his hands, to where the belt used to be, pouts like a wanton child. DV : Listen up, Brad, this is STILL my Intercontinental Championship belt! I am STILL the IC champ! And I'll be damned if some prissy, lacey
under-panty, girly cup-scouty B*TCH like you is gonna drool all over it! (The crowd pop and laugh) Brad : Bu- DV : Shut it! I've worked damn hard to get where I am today, and not to be put on the shelf by some cock called OOC and some chicken with a mask and a baseball bat!

(Dark Venom storms out of the room) Brad : But...oh nevermind...let's just say he'll get a suprise in his locker-room... *The camera cuts to the commentator's desk* RG : What did he mean by that, Jerry?
