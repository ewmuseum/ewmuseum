Is anyone interested in becoming a staff member or handler for WWCW?

July 16, 2001, 12:00:00 AM

Hi all, I'm Dizz, the president of WWCW. We've been going for over a year and a half now, and once again it is time to redisign the staff. There are now only 2 of us, and I am looking for 1 or 2 others to come in and help us with booking, match writing, designing storylines and help with some roster recruitment.
We are also looking for high quality RPers to come in and fight at the top. We have several mid-low card workers, and a few top card workers, however we need more. A sample RP is required, and if accepted, we will work you into some angles asap.
If you are interested, check us out at http://www.geocities.com/wwcwrules/index.html or drop me an email at [detail withheld] Cheers, Dizz
