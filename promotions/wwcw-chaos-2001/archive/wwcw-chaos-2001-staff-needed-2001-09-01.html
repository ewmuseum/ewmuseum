[WWCW] Staff needed

September 1, 2001, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hi guys, I run a fed called the WWCW. We are one of those obscure feds, that most of you have probably never heard of.
We have now been going since late November of 1999, with our first card being held on January 1st 2000. We now find ourselves at the point where we are in desperate need for staff.
I want some guys who can come in with fresh ideas on angles, how to manage and run the fed, and general new approaches. I want the fed to grow, to eventually become one of the greats, but at the moment, with only 2 staff, this aim is doubtful, for now my main objective is survival.
Now i know many of you out there are pondering starting a new fed of your own, but lets be honest, there are far too many feds out there as it is, so why not come on board in a fed which is already well established with an active roster, and some real talent, as well as potential talent in its roster.
So please, if any of you guys are interested in joining our staff, or know any one who might be, please get in contact.
For me this is a desperate situation, and I really dont want to close. I am still very much interested in the fed, and have recently joined the AWP as a roster member in order to improve with both RP's and cards, and am very intent on continuing into the future, and growing into the fed I know we can be.
http://www.geocities.com/wwcwrules/index.html Thanks for your time, Robin
