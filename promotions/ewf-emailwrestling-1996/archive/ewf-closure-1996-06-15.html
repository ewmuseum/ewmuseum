SUBJECT: Back UP! But a problem...
-------------------------
Dear EWF Wrestlers,
I hope I have not pissed off too many of you over the past few weeks.
I've been having MAJOR problems with my account, e-mail wise. They seem to be OK now, but my accociate VP, [name withheld], is now the one plaqued with problems.
ISP's are really starting to piss me off!! Too many god-damn newbies!!
In newer NEWS, I have recently attained a newer version of TNM6.2. In this version wrestler have the option to also "bring a table to ringside with them everytime" (ex. Public Enemy) and also have female managers.
The "tables" ting is the HOT news.
But in SAD NEWS... After installing "TNM6.2 BETA BUILD IV" I lost all of our wrestlers data when I encountered a bug in the software. But being the resourceful guy I am, I had an export file containing all of our wrestlers data...
But it didn't accept it.
I should have know better when I decided to start an e-fed with an
ALPHA, now a BETA version of the key element, SOFTWARE.
I'm sorry to say this, but until I [name withheld] (creator of TNM) comes ot with the FINAL VERSION of TNM6.2, the EWF will have to CLOSE.
This really pisses me off! I'm very sorry to have to do this to all of you! You all were very good about the league rules, etc. I want you all to know that I appreciate your efforts very much. And if anyone is pissed about me not responding in the past few weeks, I AM VERY SORRY!
The situation was out of my hands, and I hope this does not affect your opinion of the EWF.
I will be re-opening the league the very instant TNM6.2 is released!!
STAY POSTED TO THE "REC.SPORTS.PRO-WRESTLING.FANTASY" NEWSGROUP FOR
DETAILS ON THE RE-OPENING OF THE EWF!!
Thanks,
[name withheld],
President of EWF
