SUBJECT: New e-Wrestling Federation
-----------------------------------------
Dear R.S.P-W.F readers,
My name is [name withheld] and I have a HUGE announcement for all of you fantasy wrestlers out there who are tired of "One-hit-Wonder" fantasy
e-wrestling leagues...
I've started a new league called the EWF (E-mail Wrestling
Federation). I know that new leagues pop up every day and are posting messages here at the R.S.P-W.F newsgroup, but then slowly and surely crumble under the pressure.
I'm NOT GONNA TRY and talk you into anything. All I ask is for you to visit the EWF Homepage and see for yourself how serious I am about
e-wrestling.
Here are some quick specs on the EWF :
- Run under TNM6.2al8
- Roster of a MAXIMUM of 25
- ON-LINE wrestler creation form
- "Tuesday Night SLAM!" weekly event
- PPV is scheduled 2 preceeding the initial TNS
Check it out at
http://www.geocities.com/TimesSquare/4489
Thanks,
[name withheld]
President of the EWF
