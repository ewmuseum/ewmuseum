EWF (E-Mail Wrestling Federation, 1996)

"Had to make an example of this gentleman... / EWF.....DON'T JOIN"

27-28 May 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Dear EWF superstars,
I recently had to fire a gentleman by the name of Stuart out of the
EWF.
His un-cooperation and unwillingness to just read the rules was the cause of it.
I assure you that I will enforce the policies of the EWF.
Below is a transcript of the message I sent to Stuart that addressed his attitude toward the EWF.
---
Dear Stuart,
I'm sorry to tell you this, but I will not allow you to join this federation.
Obviously you did not even look at the WWW site. If you would have, you would know who is in the federation and THAT WE ARE BRAND-NEW.
I have no tolerance for "role-players" like yourself and want no part of you in this federation.
---
I assure you that the EWF does have patience, but will not tolerate
IGNORENCE.
Thank you for your time,
[name withheld]
AS YOU MAY HAVE READ,I WAS KICKED OUT OF THE EWF.....BUT FOR WHAT REASON.
THE LETTER HE GAVE YOU WAS UNTRUE.ALL I DID WAS CHALLENGE ANOTHER
WRESTLER.I GET KICKED OUT FOR THIS.
TAKE MY ADVICE AND DON'T JOIN
