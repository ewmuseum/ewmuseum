A website e-fed that ran three weekly shows in 2001, Sunday Slaughter, Wednesday Showdown and Friday Bloodbath, with a pay-per-view, called itself the 2000 and 2001 Fed of the Year as voted by the E-fed Hall of Fame, and posted its yearly awards shows from 2000 to 2003. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

All Star Wrestling, ASW on its own pages, kept a site on GeoCities with a front office of staff, rules and forms to join, to enter a tag team, to attack and to give an in-ring interview, a members section with a roster, tag teams, champions and contenders, a title history and members' info pages, and cards and results for each show. In June 2001 it ran Sunday Slaughter, Wednesday Showdown and Friday Bloodbath beside a pay-per-view, with a card deadline of midnight Eastern on the night before each show, and kept boards for roleplay, backup roleplay, out-of-character talk and challenges. Jake Blood held its World Heavyweight and International titles and Jake Turbulence its US and Canadian Heavyweight titles.

By July 2004 the front page called ASW the 2000 and 2001 Fed of the Year as voted by the E-fed Hall of Fame and ran Sunday Slaughter, Wednesday Showdown and a pay-per-view. Its updates tell of awards shows posted years late: on 10 August 2003 the owner restored the show for 2000 and finished the show for 2001, and on 1 December 2003 the show for 2002, its third year, was done. Trent Kiel was World Heavyweight Champion and Jessica Daly Women's World Champion, and its list of top wrestlers, dated 04-27-04 thru 05-11-04, began with Trent Kiel, Jonathan Storm, Gavin Payne and Mikey Austin.

The copy recovered in October 2009 carries updates from 27 November 2005 to 23 August 2008: the awards show for 2003, a new year under the president on 1 January 2006, a pay-per-view card of 5 June 2007 that promised some “Canadian Justice”, a fourth edition of ASW Magazine in May 2008, the pay-per-view Island Massacre, carded on 30 May 2008 with every title on the line and resulted on 22 June 2008, and a Wednesday Showdown in August 2008 with the Women's World Title at stake. Dominator held the World Heavyweight title, Goldeneye the North American title, Jonathan Sledge the Canadian Heavyweight title, Tavix Blood the US Heavyweight title and Rose Has It the Women's World title, and the Stable title stood vacant.
