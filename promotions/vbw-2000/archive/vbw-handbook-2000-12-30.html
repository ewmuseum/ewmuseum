VBW - Very Brutal Wrestling VBW Handbook Version 1.0 ========= Contents: ========= Section A: Introduction 1: League Type 2: Roster Size 3: Cards 4: Pay-Per-Views 5: Titles 6: Weekly Schedule of Events 7. Sample Card Structure for Sunday Night Spinebuster Section B: League Rules 1: VBW Golden Rule 2: Maturity Rule 3: "Play Your Part" Rule 4: Racism Rule Section C: Federation Management Section D: Your Participation 1: Role-playing 2: Mailing List 3: Application ============ Section A: ~Introduction~ ============ Thank you for taking the time to review VBW, a league that we hope will become synonymous with having fun. VBW will be a very small, structured league, that will provide a system so that all handlers will know what they need to do to be successful in this federation. Every aspect of this league will be covered in this handbook including league rules, federation management, and your participation when it comes to role-playing. This handbook in essence will become our "bible" of sorts, we will live and die by this handbook.
1: League Type VBW is a "booked" league meaning we base who wins and losses primarily on your role-playing for the week. We also take into consideration your stature in the league, how other people respond to your role-plays, and any angles you are in.
(Your opponent could possible send in a better role-play than you did as far as quality goes but if your role-play caused several other member to role-play in response to your role-play we will take that into consideration. Role-playing is NOT just about how you get your character over, it's also how you go about getting others to respond to your character.)
2: Roster Size Our goal in VBW is to make sure everyone involved with this league has fun and stays involved in angles/storylines. To do this we feel a smaller roster is best and it will also help us GUARANTEE that every wrestler/tag-team gets at least one match a week as well as a chance to SHOWCASE themselves. We will also have talent levels in VBW much like the "real" federations do. Every wrestler that joins the VBW will have to "pay their dues" so to speak. During ONLY their first month with the league they will be considered an "Undercard Wrestler" and their job will be to put over the upper tier wrestlers. (This does NOT mean that you will never win a match during your first month.) This is your way of showing that you are dedicated to the league itself and not in it to just pad your e-wrestling resume. It is during this time that you also establish yourself as a great role-player, show us you are dedicated to the league and then during that following month you will be rewarded with a push. Once you have "paid your dues" for that first month, you automatically become a
"Mid-Card Wrestler" and will receive better matches as well as trying to work your way upward into the better angles. The top tier of our roster is our Main
Event wrestlers, the cards storylines will ALWAYS focus mainly around the Main
Event wrestlers. Mid-Card wrestlers angles/storylines will be secondary and will take place mainly during their individual matches, whereas, the Main Event wrestlers angles/storylines will happen at the beginnings and ends of the cards. (Special Note* To be eligible for Main Event status you must be in the league at least 3 months) Listed below is a break down of how the roster will look.
Total Wrestlers on VBW Roster = 16 12 Singles Wrestlers 2 Tag-Teams (4 wrestlers) Main Event Wrestlers: 4 Singles wrestlers and both tag-teams (4 wrestlers) Mid-Card & Undercard Wrestlers: 8 singles wrestlers 3: Cards There will be 2 cards a week, one on Wednesday's and one on Saturdays. **Wednesday Night Warriors - This card will go out on Wednesday nights and every wrestler on the VBW roster is GUARANTEED to have a match! This will be a
"results only" card running down who won what match and any other major happenings that take place.
**Sunday Night Spinebuster - This will be a full commentary card booked on the fly. No one will ever know who is competing on it and it will feature those members who we feel role-played well and are trying to improve their stature in the league. To get on this show you have to EARN it!
4: Pay-Per-Views There will be one ppv every month. Four times a year there will be a huge ppv with lots of hype, the other ppv's will be more like glorified cards. The big ppv's will take place in January, April, July and October.
January: New Year's Bash (Huge Battle Royal) April: WrestleWar (Our equivalent to the WWF's WrestleMania) July: Summer Sizzler (8 Man Tournament similar to the King of the Ring) October: The Lottery (??? Surprise PPV???) 5: Titles There are 3 titles available in VBW. VBW World title - VBW International title - VBW World Tag-Team titles. 6: Weekly Schedule of Events Sunday - Sunday Night Spinebuster (Flagship Show) Monday - Tuesday - Wednesday - Wednesday Night Warriors (Results Only Show) Thursday - Friday - Saturday - 7: Sample Card Structure for Sunday Night Spinebuster Sunday Night Spinebuster *Opening Angle (reserved for Main Event Wrestler) *Opening Commentary from Announcers *Second Angle (reserved for tag-teams) *Third Angle (reserved for Main Event Wrestler) *Opening Match (reserved for Mid-Carder Wrestlers - Mid-Card Secondary angles pushed during the match) *Second Installment of Opening Angle *Second Match (reserved for Mid-Card Wrestlers - Mid-Card Secondary angles pushed during the match) *Second Installments of Second and Third Angles for tag-team and the other Main Event Wrestler *Third Match (Reserved for Main Event Wrestler) *Fourth Match (Reserved for tag-team) *Fifth Match (Reserved for Main Event Wrestler) *Card ending angle ============ Section B: ~League Rules~ ============ These are the rules that everyone must follow, they will help assure that the league runs smoothly and efficiently. They are not flexible but can and probably will be altered from time to time.
1: VBW Golden Rule Singles wrestlers will team up with one another to face off against tag-teams. In those tag-team matches the singles wrestlers WILL put over the tag-teams. The favor will be returned however when the tag-team wrestlers compete in singles matches, because they WILL put over the singles wrestlers.
2: Maturity Rule In the VBW we require that everyone act in a mature manner. This is e-wrestling, a hobby and we expect everyone to treat it in that way. None of us are actually "real" wrestlers and winning a match does not mean you are the best, losing a match also does not mean you are a bad e-wrestler. Everyone will win a few matches and everyone will lose a few matches, so live with it. This is a hobby and it's all about having fun so if you are going to get upset about losing a match then please, don't join this league! The VBW will not tolerate people who treat this as more than a hobby and we will not hesitate to kick someone out if they complain or show that they have an ego problem. Don't take that to mean you can't ask why you lost a match though. As long as you ask in a way that does not come off as confrontational then we have no problem with that.
3: "Play Your Part" Rule In the VBW we require that when you are role-playing as your character that you
STAY in character. If you are a heel then you MUST make sure that you do not have the fans cheering for you. You also do NOT use words such as role-playing, babyface, heel or JOB. (Instead of using the word role-play in your role-play, if you need to refer to something someone said then use the word interview or promo.) Also we require that you conduct yourself in a respectful manner meaning if you need to swear in your role-plays you keep it to a minimum, do not use ANY profanity that you normally would not hear on television.
(Examples: Hell, Ass, Bitch are acceptable but should be used sparingly - F*ck,
Sh*t are examples of what is NOT acceptable)
4: Racism Rule The VBW will NOT allow any type of racism. This includes all ethnic slurs, as well as sexual preference insults. Not only is it rude and unacceptable but it shows ignorance and immaturity. You don't hear wrestlers on TV calling other wrestlers gay or saying anything about their race, so you will not hear it here in this league. (Yes, I know there probably are a few of you that can point out that an angle has happened or a phrase was said but I'm still not going to allow it here in VBW.) If you break the rule once you will be warned, if you break it twice you will be fired. I AM NOT FLEXIBLE ON THIS AS I HAVE SEEN
LEAGUES FOLD BECAUSE OF SUCH INSENSITIVE REMARKS. =================== Section C: ~Federation Management~ =================== I will keep this section short because there isn't a whole lot to say. In the past I have run leagues using a committee or had people help out, and there was also a time when I ran things pretty much by myself. I have found over time that it is much easier running things by myself and that is what I will do with this league. I will make all decisions so address all questions to me. People who know me know that I am a perfectionist and I don't tolerate late cards, so running things by myself means that I don't have to depend or wait on anyone else to help with anything. I want this league to be fun and I want it to cater to people who have a life outside of e-wrestling. This is just a quick note about how I will decide winners. When it comes to deciding who wins matches I take several things into consideration. First I pick out ONE role-play that I feel best represents your wrestler and I then take into consideration any angles you are in and how other handlers respond to your wrestler. If you do a great role-play but no other handlers find it interesting enough to respond to then that will factor in to the outcome. (I said this somewhere else in this handbook. Role-playing is not only about getting your wrestler "over," it's about getting other handlers to respond to your role-play.)
(Just a personal note about myself (a tip about how to succeed in this fed) as
I'm sure you have probably seen it listed in several places in this handbook. I am one of those guys that does NOT like those really long role-plays! I can't stress this enough. I prefer shorter role-plays that are straight to the point and focus on your actual match. I know a lot of people try to be very descriptive in their role-plays, they do this so it helps to make their role-plays look like they spent a lot of time on them and makes them look really long. I don't want you spending a lot of time on them and making them long! This is supposed to be fun, a hobby, so only make them as long as what feels comfortable to you. If you start making them really long, to me it seems more like a homework assignment rather than a hobby. If I wanted to read a novel I would read War and Peace! I don't want to read a novel so keep them short!)
=============== Section D: ~Your Participation~ =============== 1: Role-playing Every wrestler is expected to send out at least one role-play every week. If you do not role-play you have no chance of getting on the VBW flagship show
Sunday Night Spinebuster! Also if you go several weeks and don't role-play or go on an excessive number of "vacations" or (LOA) "leave of absences" then you can and will be replaced. Also just to stress this again, QUALITY OVER
QUANTITY. (This means I'd rather see one good role-play rather than 10 five line ones. It also means I would rather see a smaller easier to read role-play rather than a long novel length role-play.)
