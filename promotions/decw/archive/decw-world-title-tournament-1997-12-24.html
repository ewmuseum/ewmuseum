DECW World Title Tournament

24 December 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
<Camera opens on Slayer and Slither standing in the middle of the ring.> Slayer- Welcome everyone to the almost sold out DECW The Crown Arena! We have more than 7,000 fans here to witness the DECW World Title tournament, they think it will be quite a show. I think they are right! Slither- We are already finished with the first three rounds. We will give you a brief run down and start the fourth round matches. Remember, the finals will be at DECW New Year's Bash 98. Shadow pinned Michael Beck after the Shadow Death Drop [Inverted DDT], Slash defeated the Masked Freak by god only knows since we were all asleep in this MOST boring match, Night Wolf over his arch rival Scotland Brawler with a tombstone piledriver, and the Red Panther DESTROYED "American Made" Lex Gold finally pinning him with the Pantherbomb. Slayer- Da Iceman forced ThunderStyke into submission with Da Ice Death Grip, Sycho Path went a little Sycho and pounded Aroma Man and pinned him after he punched him in the head really hard and knocked him out, Madness over Black Blaze with a small package, Jose El Santo over Stevie Spice after a Powerslam. Slither- Sturm Brightblade over Assassin after his Superbomb powerbomb from the top rope, Michael Bagwell over Shorty with his Interrogater Brain Buster move, Carolina Bandit over T-Bone after a Jacknife. Slayer- Death Toll over Hardcore Stone Cold with the Tombstone Piledriver, Lightning Lad over Hitman Jones in another snoozer, Disgusting Dan over White Blaze with his Gas Chamber maneuver. Slither- Ivan "Thor" Thunder over The Amazing Tuth Pic Boy when TPB tried a top rope move and Ivan grabbed him and threw him off and pinned him, and "The Franchise" Alex Gowan stomped the Rock who was filling in for Jack Mac in his abscense with the Primetimebomb. Slayer- In the second round it was Shadow forcing Slash to submit with the Shadowkiller, The Red Panther over Night Wolf when the dWo helped Red Panther after Night Wolf Tombstone Piledrove him, Da Iceman forced yet another victim to submission with Da Ice Death Grip and his victim was Sycho Path. Slither- Jose El Santo over Madness with a powerslam, Sturm Brightblade over Michael Bagwell with an inverted powerbomb, Death Toll over Carolina Bandit with the Tombstone Piledriver, Disgusting Dan over Lightning Lad with the Gas Chamber, and in the best match of the evening so far, "The Franchise" Alex Gowan defeated Ivan "Thor" Thunder with the Primetimebomb. Yet another victim falls to the Primetimebomb. Slayer- In third round competition, The Red Panther defeated Shadow after the Pantherbomb, Da Iceman forced Jose El Santo into submission with Da Ice Death Grip, thats three victims in three matches for Da Iceman. Slither- Sturm Brightblade over Death Toll when the dWo came down with chairs and beat his head in and Sturm did the Superbomb and pinned him. "The Franchise" Alex Gowan over Disgusting Dan with the Primetimebomb. Slayer- That brings us to where we are right now, getting ready for Da Iceman vs The Red Panther and after that Sturm Brightblade vs "The Franchise" Alex Gowan. Lets go to the Ring Announcer to announce the next match. Ring Announcer- The following match is scheduled for one fall, to my left, The Red Panther! Introducing now, a member of the Dark World Order, Da Iceman! <A.D.I.D.A.S by Korn blasts over the loud speaker and Da Iceman works his way to ringside.> Slayer- I just cant help wonder if either the Pantherbomb or Da Ice Death Grip will prevail here Slither. Slither- Neither can I. Panther claims no one ever gets up from the Pantherbomb and no one has escaped Da Ice Death Grip in Da Iceman's matches so far when he has executed it. This should be a good match. Slayer- Da Iceman charges at panther to go for a running clothesline but Panther grabs his arm and twists it so Iceman flips. He slowly pulls Iceman hits his suplex DDT and Iceman is laid out. He goes to the top for a 360 Splash but Iceman gets up and throws him off. Iceman goes over and starts to work on his legs and back areas. He picks him up and Suplexes him. He walks over, picks him up and chokeslams him. Iceman goes for Da Ice Death Grip but Panther kicks out. Panther slides out of the ring and grabs a chair. He yelled a challenge at Da Iceman. Slither- Oh no. dWo is running to ringside. Panther turns around and smashes each's head with a chair. Iceman jumps off the ring apron and nails Panther. He slides Panther back into the ring and works on those legs some more. He picks Panther up and does a piledriver. It looks as if he is gonna go for that Ice Drop move. He sets Panther on the top rope. Yes I think he is. Slayer- Ow that had to hurt! Iceman did that Ice Drop tombstone from the top rope move. He goes for the pin 1 - - - - 2 - - Kick out at 2 and a half. Iceman executes the Tiger Driver on Panther and goes for Da Ice Death Grip. Panther taps out! Its all over. 4 victims to Da Ice Death Grip. Ring Announcer- The winner of this match, as a result of a submission, Da Iceman! <A.D.I.D.A.S by Korn plays over the PA and the dWo make their way back to the lockerroom.> Ring Announcer- The following match is scheduled for one fall. Introducing first, Sturm Brightblade! Slayer- Sturm comes out with a big ass steel pipe and positions himself at the entrance. He must be waiting for Alex Gowan! Ring Announcer- Introducing now, led to the ring by Amber Gowan, he is the Franchise of DECW, he is Alex Gowan! <Trust by Megadeath blares over the PA system as the crowd goes wild! They desperatley want to see this match!> Slither- Alex isnt coming out. Wait there he is! He is working his way through the crowd with a steel chair. He sneaks up behind Sturm and WHAM! He busted him up side the head with that one. He picks Sturm up and pulls him towards the ring. He rolls him into the ring and grabs the timekeeper's bell. He brings it in the ring and bashes Sturm in the head with it. He picks Sturm up and puts him in the full nelson. But they are to near the ropes. Slayer- Sturm grabs the ropes and Alex lets go. Sturm slips out of the ring and grabs Alex's foot and yanks him out. He Powerbombs him on the floor. He pulls up the padding and picks Alex up and powerbombs him on the exposed concrete! He sets his throat across the guard rail and gets on the ring apron. Double Axe handle. He sets him back up on the ring apron and Sturm climbs to the top turnbuckle. He goes for a legdrop but Alex moves! Sturm just racked himself upon the guardrail. That had to hurt. Alex drop kicks him over the guardrail and jumps over. He picks Sturm up and they battle all the way up to the Nose bleed seats. They go into the exit and our camera's will keep following. They are battling each other and Alex goes head first into a bathroom door. Sturm pulls him over to the exit of the arena and they battle to the outside. Sturm pulls Alex down several flights of stairs leading to the street and they are still battling. Alex grabs Sturm's head and rams it into an RC Cola machine. He sticks 35 cents in and gets an RC. He opens it, drinks part and smashes the rest all over Sturm's head. Slither- Alex drags him across the street to where all of our trucks are at. He smashes his head into the back of a semi. They drag each other to the doors to enter the arena backstage and pull each other towards the parking garage. Sturm smashes Alex into a wall and then powerbombs him onto the concrete. He drags Alex over to the cars and finds a car with an open door and smashed his head in the door. Slayer- He goes over to a truck and opens the toolbox. He grabs a big ass wrench and nails Alex with it. Alex is out cold. Whats this? Amber runs over to Sturm and smacks him. He shoves her out of the way and goes back to Alex. She climbs up onto the truck and grabs a hammer and jumps from the roof of the truck and nails Sturm with the hammer! She gives Alex the hammer and leaves. Alex drags Sturm over to Sturm's car and smashes his head through the driver side window. Sturm is cut pretty badly after that. Slither- Sturm finds a sudden burst of energry and smashes Alex's head into the back window of his car. He picks Alex up and drags him towards the entrance to the locker rooms. He drags him into Alex's lockerroom and smashes his head into a chair. He drags him into the bathroom and dunks his head into the toilet. Slayer- He pulls him out and sticks his head in a locker and smashes it in there. He drags him out of that locker room and into Amber's locker room! Thank goodness Amber wasnt changing in here! He takes him over to the locker and grabs Ambers clothes and dries the pop Alex smashed onto him off. Alex is furious at this and nails him with a low blow. He drags him to his feet and does the Primetimebomb onto a chair. Sturm and Alex are both hurt. I think this match may be a no contest. But no! Alex gets up and drags Sturm out of Amber's room and locks the door on the way out. He smashes Sturm's head into the door and drags him back to ringside. He throws him into the ring and climbs in himself. Alex executes the Primetimebomb and goes for the pin. 1 - - - - 2 - - - - 3! "The Franchise Alex Gowan wins! This is the bloodiest match in DECW history. This is definately a match of the century candidate. Ring Announcer- The winner of this match, The Franchise of DECW, Alex Gowan! Slither- Amber runs down and helps Alex to his feet and they exit through the crowd since the dWo is on their way down. We have to go folks. It will be Da Iceman of the dWo vs "The Franchise" Alex Gowan at DECW New Year's Bash 98! Until Friday, see ya!
