DECW Monday Night War Hour #1

<Scene opens on Slayer and Slither standing in the center of the DECW Ring. The giant video screen behind them is playing clips of past DECW interviews. The fans are screaming. Slayer and Slither each grab a mic.> Slayer- Welcome everyone to the almost sold out DECW Crown arena! This is the DECW Monday Night War. We are live on national television and this is our first show. Tonight on the card we have Sturm Brightblade vs "The Franchise" Alex Gowan. It is taped from yesterday down there and a match before that is Jack Mac vs Jose El Santo also taped from yesterday in the DECW Sunshine Arena. Slither- Oh man I can't wait for the DECW New Year's Bash 98 on Janurary 1st. It is definately gonna be extreme! Also, the title tournament finals will be there and it will be a live telecast from each arena, DECW Arena in East Palestine Ohio, DECW Sunshine Arena in Fort Lauderdale Florida, and DECW The Crown Arena in Cincinnati Ohio. Now for todays matches, Carolina Bandit and Scottland Brawler vs Death Toll and Nightwolf in a Scottish Street Fight headlines the card. Another match will be Shadow vs Slash. We will have these matches and more to fill this 2 hour long card. Now lets go to the tape from DECW Sunshine Arena in Fort Lauderdale Florida from yesterday. <Tape rolls.> Joey Styles: HELLO, AND WELCOME TO DANGEROUS EXTREME CHAMPIONSHIP WRESTLING!!! I'm Joey Styles and we are here at the Sunshine Arena, formerly the home of Barbed Wire, Blood, and Broken Tables. This is our first big card here in this arena since November to Remember, and we would like to thank you the fans for tuning in tonight. Boy do we have a treat for you. Right here in this building tonight, you will see the now defunct BBB World Title holder, "The Franchise" Alex Gowan along with his fiancé Amber and he will be taking on a newcomer to this Arena...Sturm Brightblade. And of course along with me at ringside doing color commentary will be DECW's Florida state promoter Terry "The Destroyer" Brunk. Terry Brunk: Like always Joey, I'm just glad to be here. For the first time in almost a month now, we will be seeing "The Franchise" Alex Gowan returning to this very Arena, since winning the BBB World Title at November to Remember `97 when he defeated Rob Van Dam. And of course he will be taking on Sturm Brightblade. Brightblade is a lot bigger, and a lot stronger then Gowan, but Gowan has the speed on his side, and of course I can't leave out the beautiful Amber Gowan. [The D-Generation-X theme music blasts over the PA system at the DECW Sunshine Arena] Ring Announcer: Ladies and gentlemen our first match of the evening is scheduled for 1 fall with a 15 minute time limit...First making his way to the ring as this time, standing 5 feet 7 inches tall and weighing in at 230 pounds...HERE IS...JACK MAC!!! [Fans: Boooooo!!!] [Mase Bad Boy blasts over the PA system at the DECW Sunshine Arena] Ring Announcer: And his opponent...standing 5 feet 9 inches and weighing in at 235 pounds...HE IS...JOSE EL SANTO!!! [Fans: Booooo!!!] DING-DING-DING Joey Styles: There's the bell...Both men lock up in the center of the ring...Jose takes Jack Mac down with an arm-drag takeover, into a hammer-lock. Jack Mac rolls out of it, and gets to his feet...Jack Mac with the flurry of punches to the head of Jose El Santo. Jose whips Mac into the ropes and follows up with a drop kick right on the money. Jose goes for the quick win... 1 - - - - 2 - - - - Jack kicks out!! Jose hooks Jack into a front face lock...Jack Mac reverses it now into a key-lock...Jose reaches for the ropes. Jack Mac releases the hold. Both men circle one another again...Both men seem to be very cautious of each other... [Fans: BORING!!! BORING!!! BORING!!! BORING!!!] Joey Styles: The fans seem to be really getting on the case of Jack Mac & Jose El Santo... Sturm Brightblade: I don't know about you all but I'm sick of having to sit back here and watch these two jobbers in the ring, doing their little fancy moves, and putting the fans to sleep...Are you guys ready to see me beat the living hell out of The Franchise Alex Gowan? [Fans: YEAHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHH!!!!] Sturm Brightblade: Well I'm sorry to announce this, but you two jobbronies that are in the ring right now, need to get the hell out, because it's now my turn to show the world what I'm capable of... Joey Styles: Brightblade just threw down the mic. and is heading to the ring...Brightblade just stepped over the top rope and nailed Jack Mac with a crushing forearm...Jose El Santo jumping on the back of Brightblade just got sandwiched in the corner...Brightblade has Santo by the throat...CHOKESLAM!!! ["Trust" by Megadeath blasts over the PA system at the DECW Sunshine Arena] Joey Styles: Here comes "The Franchise" making his way to the ring, as his valet Amber leads the way. "The Franchise" looks like he's on a mission, and that mission is to none other then humiliate his competitor here tonight. Gowan jumps up onto the ring apron and opens the ropes for Amber. [Amber walks across to the other side of the ring and blows kisses to some of the fans in attendance] Ring Announcer: Ladies and gentlemen, at this time may I direct your attention to the first lady of the DECW, the beautiful and vivacious...AMBER GOWAN!!! [The crowd cheers] Ring Announcer: The first competitor of the evening, to my left, standing 6 foot 3 inches tall and weighing in tonight at 242 pounds...HE IS THE FRANCHISE....ALEX GOWAN!!! Terry Brunk: Gowan looks ready for a fight... Ring Announcer: And his opponent, to my right, standing 6 feet 8 inches tall and weighing in at 315 pounds...HERE IS...STURM BRIGHTBLADE!!! Joey Styles: Brightblade rolls into the ring and immediately "The Franchise" goes to work. DING-DING-DING Joey Styles: Gowan stomping away at his massive opponent. He picks Brightblade up and whips him into the ropes, Brightblade bounces off, and gets nailed with a flying clothesline sending him over the top rope and to the floor. Gowan steps out of the ring, and climbs up to the top turnbuckle. He's measuring Brightblade, attempting a flying ax-handle...Gowan dives off the top rope, but is caught in mid-air by the extremely large, Sturm Brightblade. Brightblade with a bear hug now, BOOM, and crushes Gowan's back into the steel ring post. Brightblade is asking for a chair, and gets it. Gowan is trying to make it to his feet, but is knocked down with a chair shot courtesy of Sturm Brightblade. Brightblade picks him up, and Irish whips him into the guardrail. Brightblade charges, and clotheslines Gowan over the guardrail and into the crowd. Brightblade picks up another chair, and whacks Gowan over the head with it...A fan hands Gowan a cheese grader, and OH MY GOD!!! I don't think Brightblade will be capable of having children after that one. Both men are down now, one of the fans is trying to help the Franchise to his feet. Brightblade makes it to his feet, and nails Gowan with a boot to the abdomen. Brightblade sets him up, and, OH NO...He just powerbombed The Franchise on the concrete floor!!! [Fans Chant: DECW...DECW...DECW...DECW...DECW...DECW...DECW] Terry Brunk: This one has turned into a war... Joey Styles: Brightblade lifts Gowan to his feet and the two are heading towards the Arena doors. Brightblade lifts Gowan up over his head, and drops him face first across the hood of a car. Gowan is on the ground now clutching his face. Brightblade asks for a weapon...Some hands him a snow shovel...A SNOW SHOVEL?...IN FLORIDA? Brightblade measures him, but his nailed with a low blow. Gowan gets up and hammers the big man with the shovel. Gowan has been busted open, and most likely before this match is over, Brightblade will be also. Gowan jumps up onto the hood of the car, and hits him with a flying clothesline. The Franchise, now hammering away at the head of this nearly 7 foot monster. Gowan whips him into the side of the car, and then throws him on top of the hood. Gowan climbs up on top as well, and nails him with several European uppercuts. Franchise picks him up on his shoulder...[SMASH]....DEATH VALLEY DRIVER THRU THE FRONT WINDSHIELD OF THE CAR!!!! Oh my, this truly is EXTREME folks...Gowan says, to hell with ya, and leaves Brightblade on top of all that broken glass. Alex is making his way back into the arena now, and these fans are giving him a standing ovation. Alex rolls back inside the ring and is laying down in the corner waiting for Brightblade to make his way back to the ring. HERE COMES SHADOW!!! Shadow pulls Alex out of the ring and goes to work on the lower back. Shadow with a chair, repeatedly hitting Gown in the back. What is Amber doing? She is up on the ring apron...AND SHE JUST JUMPED ON THE BACK OF SHADOW!!! She is scratching and clawing away at Shadow, as "The Franchise" is down on the concrete. Shadow throws Amber off, and grabs her by her hair...Don't tell me he's gonna hit her!! Gowan is up, and nails Shadow with a boot to the abdomen, and nails him with a belly-to-belly Suplex over the guardrail. Sturm Brightblade has made with way back into the Arena now, and his face is just covered with blood. Sturm also seems to be clutching at his shoulder, he may have separated it. Gowan tells Amber to go back to the locker room, and she does. "Franchise" now rolls back into the ring and is calling out Brightblade. Brightblade jumps back over the guardrail, and rolls into the ring. Gowan picks up a chair and hits him on his injured shoulder, but then takes a knee to the ribs by Brightblade himself. Alex is down on the mat, somehow Brightblade is on his feet. Sturm pulls Alex back to his feet, and grabs him by his throat...[SMASH]...Sturm just chokeslammed Gowan over the top rope and thru our announce table here. Brightblade with a big smile on his face, steps over the top rope and to the outside. Alex Gowan seems to be out of it. Terry Brunk: Gowan, just stay down...Just lay there, I'll go and get the doctors...This has gone far enough... Joey Styles: Gowan just took a swing at Brunk, he doesn't want anyone to stop this. Brightblade throws him back inside the ring...Sturms whips Gowan into the turnbuckle, but Gowan reverses it, AND THE REFEREE JUST GOT SANDWICHED IN THE CORNER!!! Sturm picks "Franchise" up and nails him with a tombstone pile-driver...THE COVER!! But wait, the referee is knocked out...1---2---3...1---2---3...But it's not official unless the referee is able to count the shoulders. Brightblade lifts up Gowan once again and sets him up on the top rope...Sturm might be going for his patent "Superbomb" off the top rope...Sturm climbs up the ropes as well...Sturm lifts him up...BUT WAIT...NO!!! GOWAN REVERSED IT INTO A HURNACARATA!!! Gowan goes for the cover!! The ref. counts!!! 1 - - - - 2 - - - - 3!!!! NO, Sturm kicked out!!! I thought he had him. [Fans chant: FUCK'EM UP FRANCHISE...FUCK'EM UP..FUCK'EM UP FRANCHISE...FUCK'EM UP] Joey Styles: Gowan stumbles to his feet...He kicks Brightblade in the stomach, and NAILS THE PRIMETIMEBOMB [Fisherman's Suplex into a powerbomb]...Gowan goes for the cover once again!!! 1 - - - - 2 - - - - - 3!!!!! HE GOT'EM!!! [Fans: YEAHHHHHH!!! FRANCHISE...FRANCHISE...FRANCHISE...FRANCHISE] Ring Announcer: Ladies and gentlemen, the time of the fall...18 minutes 27 seconds...YOUR WINNER..."THE FRANCHISE" ALEX GOWAN!!! Gowan defeats Sturm Bightblade here tonight, but something tells me, we've haven't heard the last of Mr. Bightblade...We'll ladies and gentlemen we are just about out of time...I would like to thank you all for joining us here tonight for DECW Monday Night WAR...AND UNTIL NEXT TIME...OH MY GOODDD!!! [Fans chant: DECW...DECW...DECW...DECW...DECW...DECW...DECW...DECW] <Tape ends. Slayer and Slither are sitting at the announcers table ringisde.> Slither- We're back. Man, that Gowan/Brightblade mach was a good one. I'll have to agree with Joey Styles on that one. I dont think we've heard the last of Brightblade. We have to take a commercial break and when we come back we will be ready for Shadow vs Slash. <Cuts to commercial break and comes back after some cheesy commercials and an add for DECW New Year's Bash 98. It is scheduled for one fall, introducing first. <Degeneration-X music starts playing.> Ring Announcer- at 6'5" weighing 240 pounds, Slash of the Degeneration-X! Shadow picks him up and OWW! That had to hurt. Nailed him with a dropkick that sent him out of the ring and he hit a steel chair! Shadow climbs to the top rope and does a legdrop across Slash's throat which was laying on the chair. Slayer- Shadow is one impressive athelete. But so is Alex Gowan. Slither- Slash nails Shadow in the back of the head with the chair and repeatedly hits him over the back. Alex Gowan gets up and staggers around. He finally finds his strength and picks Shadow up and drags him backstage. Slash is asking what the hell. He climbs into the ring and sets his chair up and waits for Shadow to come back. Slayer- Our camera man follows them backstage and see's Alex Gowan get nailed by Sturm Brightblade who has his shoulder that Alex messed up yesterday in a sling. Sturm hit Alex with a broom stick apparantly over the head and pulled Shadow to his feet. They start pounding on Gowan together. They pull Gowan towards the parking lot. Oh no. Can we get some security back there? Slither- I think, yes, they are gonna put Alex Gowan through the windshielf of his brand new car! I love this! Only can this happen two days in a row in the DECW! The double powerbomb him through the windshield! They pull him out and Brightblade chokeslams him onto the hood. Security comes running and they leave. <Slash still sitting in the ring calls for a mic.> Slash- Im not leaving the damn ring until Shadow makes his way out here and gets beat by me. <Cuts to a commercial break. Camera re-opens on Shadow making his way to ringside.> Slayer- I guess Slash gets his wish right here. Shadow climbs onto the ring apron and gets in. Slash grabs the chair and goes to nail Shadow with it but Sturm runs through the crowd jumps the gaurdrail and climbs into the ring. Shadow ducks and Slash's chair bounces harmlessly off the ropes.
