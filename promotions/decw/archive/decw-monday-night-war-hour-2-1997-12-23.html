DECW Monday Night War Hour #2

23 December 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Slayer- Welcome back everyone to hour number 2 of DECW Monday Night War! Earlier we saw the taped match of Jose El Santo vs Jack Mac go to a no contest. Alex Gowan defeated Sturm Brightblade. Shadow defeated Slash. And the Blazerz lost to The Amazing Tuth Pic Boy and Da Iceman. We have two more feature matches for you and an interview with the dWo. So stay tuned. The next match is Carolina Bandit and Scottland Brawler vs Nightwolf and Death Toll in a street fight. The other match is T-Bone vs Sycho Path. Ring Announcer- Introducing first in this Street Fight match, in the ring now, Carolina Bandit and Scottland Brawler of the dWo. Introducing second, their opponents, making their way to the ring, Death Toll and Nightwolf. Slayer- These two teams beating each others heads in with everything they can get there hands on. They leave the ring immediately and grab chairs, cords, mics, tables whatever they can get! Bandit put Death Toll through a table and him and Brawler are double teaming Nightwolf. But Death Toll grabs a table and throws it at all three! He pulls Nightwolf out from under the table and they stomp on it with Bandit and Brawler underneath. Tuthpicboy and Da Iceman come out looking for a fight with Nightwolf and Death Toll and get two chairs over the head for their troubles! Oh my god! All hell has broken loose! Fans are coming over the guardrail! Deliboy- Get some security! I am gonna help the wrestlers. Slither- I will stay right here and let Deli get beat up. The locker room has cleared and they are taking on the fans in attendence! It is almost a full scale riot. Somewhere around 6,000 fans vs 33 wrestlers and about 20 security guards. We have to get some order. Someone in the back call the cops. This match will continue with 6000 fans in it! Slayer- We have to take a break. When we come back maybe we can get some of the fans out of here. <Commercial break and when they come back some fans are being handcuffed and taken out. Some other fans scramble back to their seats to avoid arest. Finally most of the fans are under control.> Slayer- Ladies and gentlemen watching on TV around the US. We will have to cancel the T-Bone vs Sycho Path match since we are running out of time. The dWo interview will be taped and shown at the begining of Wednesday's show. This match will continue. Slither- I see Deliboy laying under a table. Let me go help him. Slayer- Well, I guess I have to call this match by myself. All 4 wrestlers are injured due to the fans rioting, but Death Toll and Nightwolf take control of the match and are beating Carolina Bandit and the Brawler with steel chairs. This has been one action packed show. Tune in Wednesday when we have tournaments for the World Title and US title. Bandit and Brawler are hurt and are now being choked by camera cords. Shadow and Sturm are running to the ring with baseball bats in their hands! They nail Nightwolf and Death Toll with the bats and pick them up and put them in the ring. They grab Carolina Bandit and Scottland Brawler and carry them into the ring and put them on top of Death Toll and Nightwolf. 1 - - - - 2 - - - - 3! dWo wins! With a little outside help from Shadow and Sturm. We have to go! We will keep filming to be shown on Wednesday. Thank you for tuning in! Copyright 1997 DECW.
