Bryan Willett Comments on Web War

2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Bryan Willett Comments on Web War Source: www.thesaintsonline.net "The Psycho" Bryan Willett took a break from his training to comment on the apparent war of words between the representatives of Dollar and the presumed injured Santino Romano. "This is what's great about the internet," Willett said. "Dollar and Santino are probably going to have one hell of a physical war once it gets to that point. Despite the mental games both camps are playing, I hope they think of the money both men could make together. Best of luck to both of them." Willett also commented on what type of match Dollar might have in mind for Outback Thunder when "The Psycho" takes on EAW Champion John "Mad Dog" Murphy in a non-title contest. "I can only hope it'll be a match with little to no rules. I prefer that atmosphere."
