Thursday July 3rd Newcastle Entertainment Centre Newcastle, NSW White writing forms across a black screen as EAW's latest Outback Thunder broadcast begins. A voice accompanies the message on the screen. "The following is a presentation of Eastern Australian Wrestling. The competitors in this program are trained professionals. Please do not try this at home." The words fade from the screen, before some more writing fades in. TWO WEEKS AGO A video plays from the last Outback Thunder, showing a shot of Australian National Heavyweight Champion John "Mad Dog" Murphy walking backstage, heading towards the Gorilla position. Darren: "And coming up next, we're going to hear from the champ, John Murphy!" Before either man could blink, we see Dollar coming into the picture with a run from behind. At the last second, he lets out a howling yell as he clubs Mad Dog from behind with a double sledge, causing the champion to fall forward, dropping the title belt slung over his shoulder. A screeching sound can be heard as Murphy's skin slides along the concrete. In an attempt to stand up, Murphy finds himself the receipiant of a hard kick to the ribs, causing the champion to roll further down the hallway towards the Gorilla position. Dollar: "You're never gonna make it to Collision!" Dollar pulls Murphy up to his feet before cocking his fist back and smashing it into the jaw of the champion. Murphy stumbles back from the blow and is now against a table. Dollar grabs a nearby chair and swings it at Murphy who ducks under the chair and lands a kick to the midsection of Dollar. An attempt to pull the chair out of Dollar's hands results in Murphy getting the blunt end of the chair in the gut before having Dollar bring the chair down across the champion's back. Johnno: "Someone back there stop this!" Dragging Murphy along by the back of his shirt, Dollar and the champ move closer to the Gorilla position. With a grunt, Dollar launches Murphy in the air causing him to land only inches from the curtain seperating the backstage area from the ringside area. Walking with a purpose towards Murphy, Dollar stands over the Australian National Champion and as he is about to pull Murphy back to his feet, he's met with a low blow from the champion causing the #1 Contender at Collision to double over. Murphy: "My turn, mate." The camera angle cuts to the ringside area where we see Dollar flying through the curtain followed by John Murphy who is holding his side. As Dollar brings himself to his knees, Murphy is right on top of him dropping the challenger face first into the entry ramp with a DDT. The Wollongong crowd is on their feet as the Australian National Champion looks around for a weapon. Being close to the ring, Murphy looks under the ring for something to hit Dollar with. What he comes out with is a Singapore cane and a trash can lid. Wielding the cane, Murphy brings it down across the back of Dollar causing the big man to let out a painful yell, and a whelt to form. Murphy: "Get up ya sonofabitch!" Murphy brings down a second shot across the back of Dollar before grabbing the larger man by the hair and tossing him into the ring under the bottom rope. Tossing the trash can lid into the ring, Murphy quickly follows Dollar under the bottom rope where he grabs the trash can lid and brings it down in the face of Dollar. At this point, security and backstage officials begin flowing out towards the ring. Murphy has enough time to land a second lid shot to the head before three men dive at him, pushing him into a nearby corner. The remaining men surround Dollar in an attempt to keep the two brawlers apart. Darren: "It's about time somebody gets out here." The men trying to break up the brawl find they're quickly overmatched as Dollar breaks through them, blood coming down his face from a wound opened by one of the trash can lid shots. Murphy likewise breaks through and takes Dollar down with a double leg takedown. Before Murphy can land more than two blows on Dollar (who is covering up quite well), the backstage officials force the champion off the challenger. The scene suddenly goes black as a voice is heard. Scott Carmichael: "Stop this! Stop this now!" Johnno: "It's the boss!" The scene cuts back in, focused on the owner of Eastern Australian Wrestling. Scott: "You two seem to want to fight so badly? In two weeks, you'll both get your chances, but not against each other. Dollar, you're going to face one half of the Ruff Ryders in Shawn Kelley." Dollar, shoving a couple of officials off of him nods his head and lets a sadistic smile cross his face. Johnno: "I think he likes that idea." Scott: "Mad Dog, you're going to face in a non-title contest a member of The Saints in "The Psycho" Bryan Willett." The announcement of Mad Dog Murphy's opponent brings a surprised look to his face, but he too nods in approval. Scott: "Oh, one more thing. Don't think I made these matches without placing a catch for the two of you. Not only will you two be in matches, but Mad Dog, you will choose what type of match Dollar faces Shawn Kelley in, while Dollar will choose what type of match you face Bryan Willett in. I expect you both to choose your opponent's poison in the next fourty-eight hours, otherwise I'll choose the type of match, and it won't be something you'll like. Good evening, gentlemen. And I use the term gentle very loosely." Dollar and Murphy are shown in the ring, trading insults as the scene fades to black. AND NOW...
