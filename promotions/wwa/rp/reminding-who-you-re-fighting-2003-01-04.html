"Reminding who you're fighting."

4 Jan 2003

By Jimmi Zion

Item 3 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 4 Jan 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
*[Fade In]... *[Cut to the interior of a hotel room. We see, sitting on a couch, "Sky Flyin" Jimmi Zion. He is wearing; black baggy Fubu jean shorts, black Nike sandels with no socks, a shiny Gazooks rock star shirt that is unbuttoned exposing a firm ripped upper body. He is watching the most recent promo of Chance who is talking to his best friend Jarod Crow and talking about Enigma. While he is watching the footage, Jimmi is finishing the final touches of individually braiding his long black flowing strands of dreadlocks. It's almost like Jimmi is measuring Chance through his hair. While he is watching, he's beautiful wife and manager, Mrs. Mikalia Rush-Zinn walks into the hotel room from cutting her own promo earlier. The small, petite, and elegant Mikalia walks up behind Jimmi sliding down on the sofa behind him and starts giving him a massage. Jimmi is still braiding his dreadlocks while he watches the promo. The promo ends and Jimmi turns the TV off with the remote, but not disturbing Mikalia while she is massaging his shoulders. They are talking amoungst each other, let's listen in.]... Jimmi Zion: "Damnit, I can't bloody believe that people actually thought for a moment that I was Enigma...or...Ekosyde, or whatever the hell he calls himself now. Excuse me, but last time I checked, I'm black, I'm english, I don't wear a mask... Mikalia Rush-Zinn: "Like you really need one my love." Zion: "Not to mention I'm way more good looking than that gothic reject is. To top it all off, Enigma better plug his ears on this one because this is a real mouthful for him to swallow...I have tons more talent than his sorry arss." Mikalia: "How he is the holder of two titles is totally beyond any civilized mind can comprehend. You are far more extreme than he could ever hope to be. He probably can't even fathom the depths of how HIGH you'll go to win." Zion: "No he could imagine it, the truth is he doesn't want to imagine the possibilities. That there is someone more superior than he is. I'm not Chance. I will not go down without a fight. He will have to literally kill me in order to beat me, but lets face it I don't see that happening Monday. If anything, it's him who should not show up to wrestle. He should just come down, forfeit the titles and let Chance and I wrestle to unify the titles. Then after he forfeits the titles, he should join his house of pain mates and take part in some suicide ritual just so they can save them the TRUE embarrassment of losing to Mister Millenium in the flesh." Mikalia: "What about Chance darling?" Zion: "Zion: "What about Chance? Baby, the boy got lucky in our match. Enigma was the answer to his prayers. (folds his hands together in a mock prayer) God please save me from Mister Jimmi Zion, he's gonna beat me like he does everybody else. (Normal voice) What did God do? Talk about working in mysterious ways. He sent him the most unlikely of miricles. He sent him that b[...]h Enigma to save the day. Talk about irony. Chance and Enigma hate each other, and yet Enigma saved him from me." Mikalia: "But I thought Enigma beat Chance before didn't he?" Zion: "Yeah I guess he did, but how did it happen? Chance must of knocked himself out and Enigma must of tripped and fell right on top of him. Enigma has never faught someone like me before. I'm better than Chance, better than him, and better than these punks in the WWA. If I hadn't of left, I could of been the WWA world heavyweight champion right now. Oh well s[..]t happens. Life goes on." Mikalia: "Yeah, but baby if you hadn't left, you and I wouldn't had gotten married." Jimmi: (He finishes the final braid to his hair and pushes it back, then turns his head and faces Mikalia, looking into her beautiful brown eyes) "You're right baby, leave it to you to put me back down on earth again. You are the best thing that ever happened to me. You are far more important than any title reign. That's why I haven't been really sweatin Enigma or Chance. You are the reason why I perform my best week after week. It's all because of you. Even if I do lose, you stick by me love. Just like it'll be because of you why I take Enigma and Chance out and become the 2 time X-treme Cruiserweight champion." Mikalia: "Jimmi, you've only been the Cruiserweight champion." Jimmi: (Turns 180 degrees to face her all the way now.) "Mikalia, even you said it yourself, I revolutionized the cruiserweight division when I was champion. It was an X-treme cruiserweight division. I did it then and I will do it again. Monday night it's gonna be two for the price of one. I get to go X-treme on two oponants. Zion time is working overtime. We will prevail and dominate them hands down. They won't know what hurt them. I plan on finishing what I started against Chance, and Enigma is gonna find out the true meaning to, 'messed with the wrong man'. I will take his best and shove it straight up his arss." Mikalia: "Then after you win, we can celebrate...if you know what I mean." Jimmi: "Why wait until after I win?" (Jimmi stands up, picks Mikalia off the couch in a honeymoon style cradle and starts walking toward the bedroom. A gleefully surprised Mikalia mockingly slaps Jimmi to put her back down.) Mikalia: "Jimmi, put me down, you have to train for your match." Jimmi: "First off, we're married now, we can do this anytime we want. Second, we can do a whole lot more of this after the match on Monday. Thirdly, who's to say that I'm not training. You know what they say that sex is the all around best exercise." Mikalia: "Well since you put it that way, take me away baby. Enigma and Chance don't stand a chance with you in...top form." Jimmi: "God I love my life." (Jimmi carries Mikalia into the bedroom, shutting the door with his foot behind him. The camera fades out to black.) *FADE TO BLACK*
