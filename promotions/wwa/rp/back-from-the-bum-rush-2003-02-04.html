Back from the Bum Rush

4 Feb 2003

By Venny LeTrav

Item 6 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 4 Feb 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Scene opens to New Orleans’ infamous Bourbon Street. Daytime partiers do their thing as they make small parades up and down the sidewalks. Slowly, the front window of an extensive bar comes into view. It’s green and gold awning covers a decent bit of the sidewalk and slowly the door comes into view. On the glass pane of the door reads in crazy script, “Tipitana’s”. The camera pans into the bar, showing off a rather lively party scene with a local band playing at the front of the bar and bar patrons dancing madly. The camera pans around the bar and eventually comes to a solitary booth, set into the bar wall at a semi-circle shape. There sits “The Ragin Cajun” Venny LeTrav. He sips slowly from a bottle of Miller Lite, brushing some of his wild blonde hair our of his face and looking at the camera. He motions with one hand for the cameraman to sit down and the view shifts to that of someone sitting across from him. Venny doesn’t look very depressed, considering he got his ass royally kicked at Rage In The Cage, but he does look tired. He gulps down the last of the bottle and signals for another.) Venny: Tipitana’s, one of the biggest and best bars in New Orleans. When I get off work, I come here to unwind and relax. I like a party atmosphere, although I won’t say no to a quiet evening at home with the wife. (Venny laughs just a bit at his comment just as a waitress with sleek black hair and a rather decent form sets two beers on the tabletop. Venny smiles at her and nods, and she walks away, looking back and smiling. Obviously, Venny has a bit of a fan here. As she goes back to the bar, Venny twists off the cap with his bare hand and takes a pull off the bottle.) Venny: Considering how bad I got my ass kicked at RITC, I loved it. The atmosphere, the ring, the cage, the high flying, that’s what I’m in this business for. Me and Joe did the best we could against those guys, and even though we lost, I had a great time. Do I want to get back at Havoc? Yeah, sure. But not with Joe’s help. That kid’s one too many crawdads short of a bowl of gumbo if you know what I mean. But despite him not being the brightest light in the chandelier, he impressed me. He’s tougher than a two-dollar steak and about as clever as one to boot. He’s brave though, I’ll give him that. Mighty Joe is brave. He’s mighty delusional, but he’s brave. I mean, I once knew this guy in college that always stayed in his dorm and word got out that he was smuggling women’s underwear into his dorm and he had this thing where he brushed his teeth too hard, like 12 times a day… (Venny kind of gives a half shudder and shakes his head. He takes a pull off his beer and then sets it back on the tabletop, heaving out a sigh of relief. He rubs his eyes and forehead slowly. He really does look tired. Even though he should be home healing up after the beating he got, he’s still at the bar having a beer and listening to a Rock band play the bar circuit.) Venny: My wife called the hospital I was at last night… she was hysterical. I need to get her some stuff to take it off her mind you know? Probably some new clothes from “House of Lounge”, or some flowers from “Perino’s”. She likes that kind of stuff. I wasn’t too badly beat up from the cage match, but who’d have thought, you know? I’m glad I was able to walk after that 20 foot drop from the cage. Oh well. Now I don’t have the tag team titles. J-Bo’s gone, Joe’s a shmoe, and the American Luchadores are no more. (The conversation pauses as Venny takes to the bottle again and downs the entire thing. He sets the empty bottle down with a relieved sigh and suprisingly a grin curves his lips.) Venny: I wish I cared! Sure, the tag team gig was fun, but the solo gig is the best. Don’t have to watch anyone’s back, don’t have to look out for anyone except Number One. It was fun while it lasted, but the fun’s all over. Too bad it was over so soon, huh? Oh well. What goes around, comes around. Havoc, you haven’t seen the last of me. Not by a long shot. (Venny twists the cap off of his next beer and drinks it slowly, enjoying it. He leans back in his booth.) Venny: Charlie, I guess the interview’s over. I guess this is where we… fade to black. (Fades to black as Venny says the last three words. Suddenly, words appear against the blank black screen.) THE SON OF A GUN IS BACK
