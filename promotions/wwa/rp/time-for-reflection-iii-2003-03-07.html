Time For Reflection III

7 Mar 2003

By Havoc

Item 3 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 7 Mar 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The chains hung silently from the ceiling, emptied of their captor. A dusky-skinned hand reached out, caressing the iron links. Deimos stared at the manacles hanging at the end of the chains, taking in the beauty of their unyielding strength. “These chains once held him.” Deimos stated wistfully. Behind him, Tiffani stepped forward. She brushed a few stray strands of her blond hair from her face. “I liked hearing him squeal like a little piggy, too.” She agreed. “But, it couldn’t last forever.” Deimos smirked, lowering his eyes from the chains. He could smell the leather corset that she wore, an unmistakable scent. Her soft, yet dangerous voice excited him. “We had such plans.” He continued. “After shattering his worthless partner’s spine, we were going to finish off Harley Trapper. It was all so perfect, flawless, and final. The Veteranz would have been finished. Forever.” Tiffani bit her lip. “I know.” She ventured. “Then someone interfered in the plan.” “Vik Ventressa.” He spat, his words slow and seething. “His interference ruined everything. In one mere moment, as he released Trapper from our clutches, he took away our final step in the destruction of the Veteranz. Because of him, Trapper came to the ring, interrupting our decimation of Johnny Shrapnel. Because of him, we can’t finish the crippling of Harley Trapper.” Deimos lashed out, striking the hanging chains and causing them to swing sharply into the marble ceiling. The sound of the clashing iron with the indomitable marble of the ceiling echoed throughout the room, giving a thunderous response to Deimos’ statement of rage. “Because of Vik Ventressa, the Veteranz still exist!” Deimos turned at the sound of the heavy oak door creaking on its hinges, his chest heaving with barely-contained frenzy. His passionate eyes surveyed the form that filled the door, standing over seven feet tall with long, black hair. Phobos stood in the doorframe, shafts of light from the room beyond slicing past him. “I’m not surprised to find you here.” Phobos stated. “They ruined everything!” Deimos exclaimed, staring directly into Phobos’ dark, calculating eyes. Phobos chuckled slightly as he stepped into the room. He glanced at the swaying manacles that endured the brunt of Deimos’ fury. “No, by brother.” He said, his voice calm and deep. “They have only staved off the inevitable for a short time. Ventressa made a mistake, one that shall cost him dearly. By placing himself in a match against the House of Pain, he has signed his own death warrant. On Monday night, he will stand against three men who have suffered from his meddling. He has become a source of irritation to the most dangerous, most feared man in the WWA, Jackson Kraven. That alone would be enough to seal his fate, but he’s also irritated the most dominant tag team in WWA history, us. And he has only the weakness and fumbling of the Veteranz to protect him.” Deimos’ breathing slowed as he listened to his brother’s assurances. “We have only lost one avenue to our goal.” Phobos continued. “Because of Ventressa, we must destroy the Veteranz in the ring, rather than outside of it. Perhaps it is for the best, anyway. We can add public humiliation to the pain that they shall suffer. On Monday, along with our House of Pain brother, Kraven, we will teach them all to be afraid. Be very afraid.” Deimos smiled with a malicious glee and glanced toward his lover, Tiffani. Her bright, blue eyes gleamed with malevolent anticipation. “Then let it begin.” Deimos stated. “CRY HAVOC...!” End.
