One Monster versus another

23 May 2003

By Venny LeTrav

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Scene opens to a church, empty, cold, and silent. A lone church bell rings as the camera angle is probably ankle high, moving up towards the altar of Jesus Christ at the back of the sanctuary. From this view, the altar is devoid of a preacher, the pulpit empty of it's speaker, and then the wooden panels of the altar take up the camera's entire point of view. Faint whispering is heard.) Notre père, qui l'art dans le ciel... Sacré est votre nom. Ton le royaume est venu, ton serez fait.. Sur la terre pour ainsi dire dans le ciel. (The camera pans up, and a man with medium length, tangly blonde hair. His face is hidden in the darkness, and his hands are clasped as his elbows rest on the altar. Venny LeTrav, is speaking the Lord's Prayer in French.) Nous donner ce jour, notre pain quotidien. Et pardonner nos empiéter.. Comme nous pardonnons ceux-là qui empiéte contre nous. Premier nous pas à la tentation.. Et nous livrer de Diabolique. Au nom du Seigneur et au nom le Sauveur, Jésus Christ, nous prions. Amen. (Venny lowers his hands, but his face is still hidden by the darkness in the church. Only the outline of his face and his mouth when he speaks is fainly visible via the soft candlelight in the sanctuary. He speaks in a low, soft voice.) Venny: I didn't always have a problem with you, Se7en. I didn't always want to beat someone within an inch of their life in front of a hundred-thousand people. I used to think of this job as something I could do and be happy with my life, with my family. Congratulate yourself, Drake. You've pissed me off. You've made yourself the enemy of a man you know nothing about. You've beaten me once, Se7en. And last time we were in the ring... I recall that I made you bleed. I recall that I swung a bat at your face and shattered it. As will I shatter the... creature that is Se7en. Be prepared to see the side of me that you have never seen before. Meltdown was just a taste of how much I want to end you, to end your career, and end your short-lived stint as a WWA Superstar. I have but one thing to say to you now, Drake. Me pardonner le père, pour je pécherai. ...it's French. It means... Forgive me father... for I.. will sin. (Venny leans forward, his eyes are now visible. His iris' are yellow with red rimming and slits in the middle, just like a cat's. He's smiling widely, his eyes staring into the camera.) Venny: I told you... if you stick around long enough, you'll find that I'm a person with whom not to f*ck, Drake. You'd better pray to God, or whatever mindless deity you worship that I never make it to the PPV, or the next Meltdown. Because if I do... you'll be wearing that mask for a catheter, and your own head for a suppository. (Venny grins and leans back into the darkness as we...) Fade to Black.
