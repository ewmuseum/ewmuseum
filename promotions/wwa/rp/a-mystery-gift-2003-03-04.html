A Mystery Gift

4 Mar 2003

By James Curzon

Item 1 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 4 Mar 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(James Curzon is sat in a darkened room. The blinds are down although a few shafts of light come into the room. He is sat on a wooden chair with his back to the wall. The room also contains piles of boxes and several pieces of furniture covered in white sheets. Curzon is looking around nervously, he holds a cricket bat tightly in his hands.) Curzon: Must stay awake, must not sleep! They might get me then! (Suddenly the door swings open. Curzon jumps to his feet but trips over his feet before he can get to the entrance. Silas Fagin enters carrying a large box. He sets the box down on the floor and helps Curzon back up.) Curzon: What do you want? I'm trying to stay concealed but you keep coming in! What do you want? Fagin: A parcel arrived, it's from Mr Showtime. Curzon: You idiot! You brought a large box inside the house? Who knows what could be inside! It's probably a bomb or a something worse. The Conspiracy are cunning they have a multitude of ways to get at you, this is bound to be one and you fell for it! You idiot! (Fagin shrugs.) Fagin: Shall I take it away then? Curzon: Yes, take it outside and dispose of it via a controlled explosion. (Fagin picks up the box, he frowns.) Fagin: That's strange, I'm sure it wasn't this light when I came in. (He turns the box over to reveal a ripped hole on one side. Curzon looks horrified!) Curzon: Whatever was in that box, is now loose in this room. You have endangered my life once again! Thank-you very much! (Suddenly something scuttles across the bottom of the screen.) Curzon: What was that? It's over there! (Curzon runs across the room and smashes a box with his cricket bat. The box falls on the ground with the sound of breaking china.) Fagin: That was the fine china tea service! Curzon: I know but saving my life is more important than my mothers tea service! (The things speeds pas Fagin.) Fagin: It's here now! (He spins round but isn't fast enough to see what it is.) Fagin: What do you think it is? Curzon: It's probably some kind of badger assassin. Fagin: A badger assassin? Curzon: Yes, I have developed this theory that The Conspiracy have trained badgers in shooting and marital arts, then they send these highly trained operatives out to take-down all those who oppose them. I mean who would suspect a badger? Fagin: Not me. Curzon: Exactly! (Suddenly something moves beneath the sheet over a sofa.) Curzon: GET IT! (He swings his bat and brings it down hard onto the sheet. There is a short whiny noise and a red stain spreads over the white sheet.) Curzon: Got it! That badger will kill no more freedom fighters. (Fagin goes over and lifts up the sheet. He shakes his head.) Fagin: That wasn't actually a badger assassin. Curzon: What? Fagin: You remember Cousin Betram? Curzon: Of course, a fine fellow. Fagin: You remember he is currently touring europe on horseback? Curzon: I do but he's going to be gone for several more months yet, I hardly see how this is relevant. Fagin: Do you recall what he left in our care for those six months? Curzon: Yes, his beloved poodle, Mopsy. He loves that dog more than life itself! If anything should happen to that cute, little doggy... Fagin: I don't think Cousin Bertram will be too happy. (Curzon lifts up the sheet and peers down.) Curzon: Well, at least we won't need an open coffin! (Suddenly their is a flurry of movement behind the two men. They spin round and see a small man stood on a box. He is dressed identically to James Curzon) Curzon: What is that? (Curzon looks absolutely terrified.) Fagin: That must be the gift from your new team-mate, Mr Showtime. I think it's a Mini-Curzon. (Mini Curzon grins broadly and starts to sing.) Mini C: I love you, you love me, we're a happy family... Curzon: AAAAAAAAAARRRRRRRRGGGGGGGHHHHHHH! (Curzon runs of down the corridor, Mini-Curzon jumps down and follows him still singing.) Fagin: Well, that was odd. Still I suppose that's what you get for joining up with Showtime! (Behind him a Mini Fagin dressed as a referee appears. Fagin turns and sees it.) Fagin: This is getting out of control! FADE TO BLACK
