True Competition

16 Jan 2003

By Nicholas Adams

Item 2 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 16 Jan 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
FADE IN (Nicholas Adams is shown sitting down in a coffee shop, presumably in Houston, Texas. Even though it's winter, the sun still seems to be shining brightly. Of course, that's just because that's how it usually is in Texas. Besides the fact that it was taken away from Mexico, very few can truly understand why people would live there. It would have been described as the worst place in the world, if people hadn't already discovered the Sahara. Anyway, Nick is just looking at a letter. There's some clothing accross from him, though it seems that it was mailed to him. The coffee cup next to him looks as if it has barely been touched. Adams is truly concentrating on the contents of the mailed piece of paper. It doesn't look like an official contract, so it probably isn't. It also does not hold the appearance of a WWA memo, so it probably isn't that, either. In fact, it just looks like a letter that somebody felt like typing. It has the name "Nicholas Adams" on it, but it's quite obvious that Nick did not write a letter to himself. Suddenly, Juan Guevesos walks in to the coffee shop and sits down accross from Nick. He looks at the apparel in surprise, and decides to break the silence.) Juan: So...what did you do, go to JC Penny's and find some of the stuff that they had on sale? Nick: No, Juan, I did not go shopping. I don't really need to do so. Juan: Then where'd this come from? (Nick shakes his head, looks up from the letter, and smiles. He then looks from the paper to the clothes, leans back, and tosses the letter onto the table. Guevesos looks at Adams with curiousity, since Juan obviously isn't in on whatever Nick is smiling about. Juan makes a motion as if to ask, "What?") Nick: Read it. You'd be surprised what kind of coincidences you run in to these days. (Juan lifts if up slowly, and Nick motions for him to go on. Nick then starts to drink his coffee. As Guevesos pores through the letter, he gets even more surprised. Finally, at the very end, he sets down the letter and starts laughing. Actually, it's more of a chuckle. Juan is only doing that so that the entire shop can't hear them.) Juan: Lemme get this straight. You met another guy named Nicholas Adams in an indy circuit... Nick: Yeah. He said that it had happened before, but the other guy was a momma's boy, though he tried to play tough. Juan: Your words, or his? Nick: His, to be quite honest. Anyway, he said that we might as well see where our careers take us. Juan: And since he decided to leave the ring and settle down, he gave you his wrestling attire? (Nick nods, then shakes his head.) Nick: Leather jacket, cowboy gear. Only things he didn't include were the gun, the gun holster, and the hat. Juan: He called himself the Colorado Cowboy? Now that's something that most would laugh at. Nick: Not as much as that Mister Cool guy I saw last week. Juan: Don't diss on the rookies, man. You have to admit, he got the fans riled up. Nick: Yeah, but so did I, after a while, and all I had to do was wrestle well. (Juan shakes his head, then looks out the window.) Juan: Sometimes, you're just way too confident. I think that you really need to learn the difference between confidence and arrogance. Nick: Look, Juan, I know what I'm doing. Shaman is a definite competitor. But unlike Payne and Mack Tenn, he actually wrestles fair. He actually does what we were hired to do. He doesn't mess with anybody, and he doesn't have a grudge against me. It's just him and me in the ring. We're not out there to win anything but a match. I already respect him, and he has said that I earned his respect. Juan: Yeah, but you... Nick: No buts, Juan. I know what I'm up against. He's huge in comparison to me. But he isn't nasty or anything. He's just a guy that wants to wrestle for a living. He has a grudge against Payne, just like I do. And he has no reason to beat me up. If he wins, he wins. If I win, I win. It's a main event, and I'm going to do my damndest to make it one of the best main events that I have done. Juan: That's gonna be hard to do, considering that match you had against Shane Dorian a while back... Nick: Juan, that involved weapons. I'm going to do pure wrestling because, unlike Shane Dorian, unlike Mack Tenn, unlike Christyan Payne, I respect Shaman. Please don't question my motives. I know that you want to, but don't. I'm not in the mood right now. Maybe an interview, you know? (Juan shrugs at Nick. Adams stands up, puts the clothing in the box on the table, tosses in the letter, and puts on the lid.) Nick: See you later, Juan. Juan: Yeah, sure...see you later. (Nick walks out of the cafe calmly without saying another word.) Juan: Man, he sure can be touchy. FADE OUT
