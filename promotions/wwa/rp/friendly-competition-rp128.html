Friendly Competition?

11 Jun 2003

By Enigma

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
{Darkness} “There is nothing wrong with a little friendly competition Grunge. If you, Nicholas and David want to have a nice little friendly match maybe you should go to a baseball field and play a little ball away from where it can get rough, tough and painful. Grunge, you and your two friends want to have a little fun then stay the hell out of the ring on Monday because there is no way you can beat me in the ring with that kind of attitude.” {The smoke enters the scene and we are once again in Enigma’s lair} “It’s funny how you believe that I have reached my peak. It is very funny considering this coming from a man who has done nothing to know one. Oh sure you have beaten Nicholas Adams and hold the United States Title, but that is meaningless to me. You see Kenneth, you have reached your peak. I have not. Your victory over Nicholas Adams will be the highlight of your wretched life. You have peaked whereas I have not even come close to my destiny.” {Enigma smirks} “You see Grunge my destiny and my legacy reaches far above you. It goes higher and higher then you will ever be. You may not see it now. You may not see it in three months, but you will realize that this day I spoke the truth. Grunge, you can’t be expected to see that now because you are blinded by your own ego. You are blinded by the light. You are much like Chance, unable to see the truth.” “Monday Night you, Nicholas and David will try and take me out together. That is fine. And to be honest that is smart. For you see Grunge, you cannot take me out by yourself. Nicholas Adams couldn’t take you out, so there is no chance that he can take me out. Dave Harley could not take out a cheap whore to a back alley, so there isn’t a chance in the world of any of you beating me one on one.” “Don’t believe me? Try it. You will fail. You will end up like Chance, a man who has fallen from grace. A man who has lost his way and joined up with the biggest joke in the history of the World Wrestling Alliance. Grunge, right now you may be a champion, but you are nothing to me and come Monday I will show you that for good.” NOTHING CAN STOP ME NOW {Fade to ?}
