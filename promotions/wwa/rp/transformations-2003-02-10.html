Transformations

10 Feb 2003

By Tiffani

Item 2 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 10 Feb 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The sharp clack of heels on marble echoed through the darkness. After several steps, the staccato rhythm ended abruptly. Small, indiscernible sounds fumbled through the darkness, as if reaching out to find their way. A moment of silence passed, unabated, before the sharp scratch. With the scratching, tearing sound, flame erupted. There, in the light of a single, burning match, the smooth, alabaster face of Tiffani smiled. The flame moved to the unshaded oil lamp. The licking flame of the match took joy in procreation, creating its offspring there upon the lamp. As its progeny engulfed the wick, burning brightly, proudly, the match shuddered, and died. Tiffani tossed the extinguished match aside, still smiling. “I have so many things to say, so many people to address, so much to do. Let me start with the obvious. Rick, you may be wondering how this happened. You’re probably wondering where things went wrong that caused me to leave you. What could have driven me to the arms of another man? If you were to sober up for a moment, you might discover the reason on your own, Rick. But then, I don’t really expect that you can manage that. For a long time, the bottle has been your best friend, Rick, your only friend, even your lover.” At that last statement, Tiffani’s smile faded, her eyes hardening. “A girl doesn’t like competition, Rick. I didn’t want to compete with Johnny Walker, Jack Daniels, and St. Pauli. I expected more from you, Rick. I needed more. “When you couldn’t give me what I needed, I began to realize something. I realized that you had never been able to satisfy me. Don’t take that personal, Rick. No man has ever been able to satisfy my needs. There was always something missing, something that ate at my heart every day of my life, calling me to something else. Now, thanks to Deimos, I know what that was. Throughout my life, I learned to be quiet, demure, and supportive of my man. Never, not once, did anyone ask what was truly in my heart. No one wanted to know what burned in my soul. It was always about everyone else. But, I found the lusts and desires that were missing from my life.” Tiffani’s eyes softened as her tongue snaked from her mouth, drawing a trail of moisture across her full lips. She giggled conspiratorially in the shadows. “Now, I’ve found out who I really am. ”I love to feel the sharp sting of leather against another’s skin. I love to lose myself in the screams of agony mixed with pleasure. I love the taste of fear. The smell of leather and vinyl excites me. It makes my heart race to hear the clanking of the chains that holds my prey, my naughty little victims.” Tiffani’s eyes drifted closed, accompanied by a deep, lustful moan. “Mmm. When the whip breaks their skin, I like to move in close, making their skin crawl with my warm breath, and slowly lick the trail of blood from their tight, fear-ridden flesh. And I love to hear a woman’s screams. Mmm.” Tiffani’s eyes reopened as a single corner of her mouth curled up into a coy grin. “This is my time of transformation. This is my new exploration. This is my exploration of my own truths, my own desires, my own needs, and perversions.” She giggled, realizing that most would probably find her latest forays disturbing. Somehow, that thought excited her, too. “And who better to explore the darkness with, than a god?” Full laughter erupted from her. Her shining blue eyes glittered in the wavering lamp light, as she regained her composure. “Grunge, you’ve already stated that you’ve washed your hands of me. That’s a good choice. You couldn’t handle the new me. Julie, I couldn’t understand before why you always annoyed me. There was something about you that just pissed me off. Now, I understand it. It wasn’t you, it was me. I hated that reflection of myself that I saw in you, the good girl, the sweet, innocent thing. So, for that, I apologize. It was unfair to you. With that said, both of you would do well to stay out of Havoc’s way. Otherwise,” Again, her glistening lips curled in conspiracy. “You’ll be nothing more than my new play toys.” A giggle, a dark, vicious giggle escaped her. She lowered her head, still staring forward. “There’s another person that I would like to address, someone that I feel I should express my gratitude toward. Lady Stephanie, several months ago, you opened my eyes. Your words struck home, ringing of truth about the unfortunate position that I had put myself in by tying myself to the two men that I managed at that time. Well, you were right. I moved on, finding better material to manage. Hell, I found the best. After all, why burden oneself with a losing commitment? Thank you, Lady Stephanie. Thank you for pointing out what should have been obvious to me, all along.” Her face suddenly changed, her eyes turning to steel, her mouth expressionless, and her jaw set sternly. “But, you’re still a bitch.” The sole surviving flame, burning so proudly, for so long, finally, inevitably, as all things do, succumbed to darkness. End.
