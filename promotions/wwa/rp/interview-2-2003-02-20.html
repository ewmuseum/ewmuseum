Interview #2

20 Feb 2003

By Joey K

Item 6 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 20 Feb 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
We open outside the same door as the last episode of insider. A sign saying 'Interviews here' hangs lazily from the door, which suddenly opens and out walks Joey K. The follows him, and as it pans round we see we are standing in a looby area. Chairs are placed in a line along the wall. Joey walks over to the first man in the line. JOEY: You next pal? The guy jumps to his feet and is about a 5 inches shorter than Joey. His has short blonde hair in a quiff and wears a pink shirt and blue pants. He grins at Joe and holds out his hand. Joey ignores it totally and points towards the office he has just come out of. JOEY: Through there. MAN: Sure! The to men walk into the ofice. The camera follows them and they both take a seat. Joey takes out a notepad and a pen. JOEY: Name? MAN: Rupert Fudgepacker. Joey stifles his laughter and looks at the guy. He has a grin on his face, but looks totally innocent. Joe decides to leave it. JOEY: Uh, ok. So, erm, Mr F-...........Rupert, why dyu wanna become my manager? Its a big job, especially for someone as talented and famous as me. Hehehe The guy shifts in his seat, before answering. RUPERT: Well, I've allways liked to manage a guy......like you. The wrestling world has allways interested me, especially with all those..ripling muscles...... JOEY: What? RUPERT:Well, all those men........in TIGHT shorts.......its fantastic! Joe takes an uneasy glance at the camera, as if silently asking the crew how is he gonna get out of this. He shifts in his seat and looks at Rupert. JOEY: I think you've got the wrong idea buddy, i haven't got anything against your............lot, but erm, i'm not really looking for that. RUPERT: What exactly dyu mean, my lot? JOEY: Well, imagine if LeTrav came down to ruin one of my matches again, I need someone to kick the crap outta him, or at least warn me, i don't erm, want him gettin banged in the butt.....*ahem* sorry. Rupert stands up shocked, and his chair flies backwards. Joey leans back in his chair. RUPERT: How dare you?! I am NOT gay! JOEY: Dude, ya coulda fooled me! RUPERT: WEll Mr K, from what I've seen lately, its not exactly hard to make a fool out of you! Joey freezes and looks at his hands. JOEY: WHAT, did you just say? His eyes are wide, as he slowly looks up at Rupert, who has gone silent. JOEY: The whole reason i beat the CRAP outta Venny LeTrav, was because of being made a fool out of! I was NEVER taken seriously, and that is all gonna change when people see what i can do! Starting with YOU! Joey lunges forward and socks the guy straight in the mouth. Rupert stumbles back into the wall, where, upon impact, a painting falls to the floor. Joe runs into him, and throws him to the floor. With the guy out cold on the floor, the camera zooms in one Joeys face. Eyes wide, breathing like a maniac, he utters the words through clenched teeth. 'Make way.......for Joey.....K!'
