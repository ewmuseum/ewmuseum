The first opportunity!

20 Feb 2003

By Ender

Item 1 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 20 Feb 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
****The scene opens with Steve and John setting off fireworks. The sky is full with smoke and the camera is very blurry**** Ender: Steve, you know I got to thinking, and well, I realize my match for last week was cancled!. I was so ready. I think maybe I need to do something to get noticed… like take out the bottle rocket wrap! Steve: Bottle Rocket Wrap? Ender: Yeah it's a new Idea I have, I took bubble wrap right and I put a bunch of bottle rockets in the middle, but they are filled with a acid, so when you pop the bubbles it will spill the acid onto the wick of the rocket thus shooting the rocket off, but the rocket won't go anywhere cause it's glued down. So If I slam someone on it...hundreds of bottle rockets will explode causing many bruises and burns. Sure it will stop stinging after a week or so but I'm pretty sure it will knock em out. Steve: Sweet, damn that would suck if they filled you over onto it. I mean think about, you burnt a hole I your shoe once when you stepped on a bottle rocket, their gonna be on it with no rubber in between them. Ender: I guess that's the risk they take facing me. But I;m not in a hardcore match, so who cares...I'll just have to win a regular match. I mean it's not like too many people in this company are much of a challenge. Right? Steve: Yeah, you can beat everyone here hands down. I mean the best talent here is a pushover. Ender: Yeah. We don’t need a partner…yet…I can win matches on my own but I don’t know if I can steal their souls alone. I need some help with that, but I will win. I will conquer the WWA. We will win…sorry. Steve: That;s cool I knew what you meant. I can’t wait to take him on too. I also can’t wait to see the bottle rocket wrap. That’s your craziest idea yet. Ender: I believe you’ve forgotten the cafeteria(Steve: oh yeah). I blew up half the serving desk. Good times, but those times are over, times of trouble have begun, but the lucky few will be saved. Steve: All I can say is I wish you were in a match on Thursday then the havoc could begin. But until then we must wait. Ender: but our time will come. We will seek and destroy every wrestler in the WIA. We will break the firm and the molds. We will kill corporate America as it is known. The time of Rebellion is now…the time for corporate America is over. We will be dominant we will kill we will pounce on every chance we get. The molds will vanish and “they” will come…many will be confused but we will understand. We will lead the select few we deem worthy, we will be rulers of all that is necessary and those who are not necessary will be cast aside like a damaged good. For the true damaged goods are the ones that we are, they are the ones looked down upon by the “Man” they are the ones who wear black or bright colors or other things not “normal” to the human eye. But is there really a normal, is there really a right or wrong. Is it true you can’t be successful unless you wear a three-piece suit and a tie? No, and I will prove to all just how untrue that statement truly is. I will take corporate America by the wrist just like a 8 year old kid crossing the street and I will shake him when he sees the dog and goes the other way…I will shake him until he learns who to follow and who to stay away from. I will shake him until he cries if I have to. He will loose all his brain cells from my vicious shaking if that’s what it takes to knock down the foundation of corporate America. If this is the only way to defeat the true enemy…then pass me a 8 year old boy. Steve: That’s cool, or you could get Michael Jackson and give him the 8 year ol… Ender: Steve I don’t need to here that! (Note: The 8 Year old boy represents Damien Ryan)
