Lounge Lizards

15 Jan 2003

By Extreme Corporation

Item 6 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 15 Jan 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Scene – The back of a television set. In the background sits the members of the Extreme Corporation, Dave Harley and Mal Somers. They are pointing at the screen and laughing, all the while eating from some bowls of popcorn.) Somers – “Check this bit out...it’s the coolest!” (The scene switches to what they are viewing on the television set. Mal Somers, dressed in a pinstriped suit, clubs a man in a chicken suit repeatedly with a steel chair, and then stands over the fallen figure, raising his chair high in the air.) Harley – (with a mouth full of popcorn) “Ahhhhh...good times. I remember when you did that, back in the EWA. Paying some enhancement talent to dress up in a chicken suit and then you beat the STUFFING out of him. You were trying to prove a point to Lockdown, depicting him as a chicken. Priceless! POULTRY in motion!” Somers – “Don’t patronise me Harley! It seemed like a good idea, and it was. It ticked Lockdown off, which is what I wanted. Last week on Insider, Lockdown kept going on and on about all the hijinks we used to get up to in the EWA and it got me a little nostalgic for the old days. Back when I could beat up some shmuck in a chicken suit or throw a midget over the top rope in order to tick Lockdown off.” (turns off the television with the remote) “Nowadays, its all about ripping ears off and ramming them down peoples throats, using tasers and slegehammers, all that sort of stuff.” Harley – “Oh yeah...NOW you’re talking! (gobbles another handful of popcorn) You know, it’s a real shame neither of us are getting signed up for any matches lately. I hope this “sit – in” idea works at Fiesta on Saturday!” (Somers looks at his feet, avoiding Harley) Harley – “Is there something you aren’t telling me, Chairboy?” Somers – “Ummmm..” Harley – “You surely don’t have a match at Fiesta? I looked at the card and neither of us are on it.” (awkward silence) (Harley sees a sheet of paper on the coffee table inder his feet, entitled “Meltdown Card”. He looks at Somers who smiles uncomfortably and leans forward to read it.) Harley – “Somers versus Maloy in a freaking ROOKIE TITLE match! Oh that’s just great! I thought we were a team! I thought we were going to do a sit in! What the hells going on you backstabber!” Somers – “Oh... I...was going to tell you...I just ...ummm...had to find the right time. I’m sorry Dave, we are still doing the sit in at Fiesta ‘cos Cruz wont give us any matches on Fiesta. I just got a call to be at Monday Meltdown to defend my belt. Im really sorry man.” (Dave struggles to hold back his laughter but can’t. He coughs up half his popcorn and keeps laughing) Harley – “You idiot! You gotta take a chill pill man! You are WAY too serious. I think its great, we’ll get to show those who watch Meltdown what the Extreme Corporation is all about...even though I am TOO HOT FOR MONDAY NIGHT!” Somers – (sighs) (Harley grabs the remote control from Somers and holds it like a microphone. He does his best Wallace Coleman impersonation) Harley - "With me I have the second best wrestler in the WWA, Mal Somers. Dave "The Extreme Machine" Harley couldnt be with us, so we got the next best thing. So Mal, are you excited about your return to Meltdown?" (points the remote at Somers' face) Somers - (reluctantly) "Well I'll tell you what, Dave..." (Harley shakes his head) Somers "...errrr, Wallace, I must say I am very excited about going back to Meltdown and defending my Rookie Title in front of all the MALcoholics out there. Even better than that, I get to do it against that wily Texan, Mac "Truck" Maloy. Our hatred for each other is no secret whatsoever, and I dont think thats going to change come Monday." Harley - "I can just hear it now...Somers goes to the top rope, leaps...CRAXXT! He hits the Somers Assault! Well any final thoughts for the fans out there?" Somers - "Mac...you know how I feel about you. Since our last match, you have reached rock bottom...and have started to dig! You are not so much a "has been", but more of a definite "wont be". Do you think differently? Lets see what you can do at Meltdown!" (Fade Out)
