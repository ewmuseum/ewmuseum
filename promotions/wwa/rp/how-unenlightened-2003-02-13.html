How unenlightened

13 Feb 2003

By Grunge

Item 1 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 13 Feb 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
FADE IN (Grunge is sitting at his desk typing something on his laptop computer. He is listening to his stereo while busy at work. The sounds of The Flaming Lips “Fight Test” can be heard.) I thought I was smart, I thought I was right. I thought it better not to fight. I thought there was always virtue in always being cool so when it came time to fight I thought I'll just step aside and that time would prove you wrong and that you would be the fool. I don't know where the sunbeams end and the starlight begins - It's all a mystery... Oh to fight is to defend. If it's not now then tell me when you would stand up and be a man. (As the music plays the camera changes view to peer over Grunge’s shoulder. He seems to be writing in an online journal. So far only a few lines have been written.) “Things to do today: Take over the world or learn French.” (Grunge looks over his should to see the cameraman and then shuts the laptop. Julie enters the study and Grunge looks up.) Grunge: You remember the first year we met? Julie: Of course. Grunge: Remember that time after class, I stopped by Subway for a quick lunch before going to work. I stopped by the restroom to wash my hands before eating and as I turned to leave I noticed that above the door, scrawled in blood red something a phrase was written: "Abandon all hope those who enter here!” Julie: College boys with too much time on they’re hands and somehow know how to quote Dante. What made you think of that? Grunge: Seems to fit the wrestling world. Seems to fit my next encounter with Nick Adams. Nick you should heed those words. Now I’m not going to hurt you Nick, well there will be some hurting on both sides of the fence but see your hopes rest on regaining this TV title. You should abandon that hope; it will make this a lot easier. Julie: Maybe he’s just bitter. Grunge: Nick you should know that your title run had to come to an end, much as mine will. Nothing is permanent. Buddhism shows that there is no permanence. We experience this life and then move on. I like that idea. However, I don’t have any intentions of letting this end anytime soon. I’m going to have my run with this for a very long time. Julie: Time will tell on that. Grunge: That's just so very unenlightened. Julie: Your Methodist so cut it out. Grunge: This much I must say...the music is fading out. FADE OUT
