Payne Barrier

8 Jan 2003

By Grunge

Item 3 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 8 Jan 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
FADE IN (Grunge is running not far from his home. He's on the University of Oklahoma wearing street clothes. He passes many of the students there who call his name. He waves politely and smiles. Somebody is running up behind him yelling to stop.) Wallace Coleman: Hey...Grunge....wait up. (Grunge stops in his tracks and turns around. WWA interviewer Wallace Coleman is running behind him trying to catch up. He finally does out of breath.) Coleman: (out of breath.) When you asked me to come running with you today I didn't think it would be this far. (Grunge grabs the towel off his shoulder and offers it to Coleman. Coleman declines.) Grunge: Hey no pain no gain. Coleman: Is that a crack towards Christyan Payne. (Grunge dabs the towel on his forehead taking off some of the sweat.) Grunge: Well no not an intentional one. I think your just fishing for one so we can get to this interview. Coleman: Well we do have to get to it. On Meltdown you have the chance to win the Television Title. Of course you do have to go through Payne to get it. Grunge: Of course. (Coleman stands there for a moment, silence between the two. Coleman starts to tap his foot and check his watch.) Coleman: Well....Do you have a gameplan? Grunge: Of course but it wouldn't be very wise to give it away on nation television would it Mike? (Grunge is comment is directed towards the cameraman. A voice is coming from off screen.) Mike: Probably not. (Grunge turns back to Coleman.) Grunge: See. Mike agrees with me. Wallace: Well you have to give me something! Grunge: I took you running and I paid for your lunch. Wallace: That's not what I meant. Grunge: Christyan Payne is a solid athlete. A little bit on the nutty side but still good. Although I can't see his reasoning for wanting Mack Tenn as lackies. Wallace: So you don't care for them that much. Mike: Duh. (Wallace gives a glare right at the camera obviously at Mike.) Grunge: I don't like their methods. That goes for Payne as well. I've never cared for cheating. And that's well known. Wallace: Still you would like to have that TV Championship. Grunge: Well of course. It would be another title to add to the old resume. Many former champions have gone on to greater things. Craven Moore, Jackson Kraven..... Mike: Spike. Grunge: Well yeah I guess every champion can't be that impressive. Still it is a stepping stone. Wallace: Still I'm sure Payne won't give it up without a fight. Grunge: This is true. He's a good wrestler. And a former rookie champion but as the old saying goes "anything you can do I can do better." Mike: (singing) I can do anything better than you. (Grunge points towards the camera.) Grunge: Where did you dig this guy up Wally? He's funny. I think he'll be our regular camera man from now on. (Wallace hangs his head wanting this to be over.) Wallace: Well anything else to say about Payne? Grunge: Yeah he needs a new catch phrase. Anything used off a video game as crappy as that one was shouldn't be reminded of. Oh and a haircut. Wallace: Well thank you Grunge. Grunge: No prob. Wallace: Aren't you forgetting something? Grunge: Oh yeah the catchpharse. United we stand, divided you fall. Hey Mike want to go get a sandwhich. Mike: Sure Grunge: All right. Common Wally. (Grunge and Wallace begin to job off as Mike turns off the camera to join them.) FADE OUT
