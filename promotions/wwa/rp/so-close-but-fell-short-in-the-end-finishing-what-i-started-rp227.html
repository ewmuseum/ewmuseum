"So close, but fell short in the end. (Finishing what I started, the IDR will truly set me free.)"

3 Jul 2003

By Jimmi Zion

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
*{Fade In}... *{New Orleans, Louisianna}... *{Somewhere on Bourbon Street}... *{Tuesday, July 01, 2003. Sometime after midnight following the WWA Televised Live broadcast, the Meltdown Bourbon Street Brawl.}... *{The scene opens up in one of the numerous sites struck by the WWA BSB, inside what's left of a bar. The bar is a mess, some of the bouncers and employees have gathered together to help chean up the establishment. The owner helps supervise the cleaning up process.} Bar Owner: "Maybe I'll think twice before allowing the WWA to use my bar in their annual playground." Bartender: "Yeah but you have to admit boss, at least it's good business for the bar. People will flock over here knowing that this bar was part of the great Bourbon Street Brawl. You'll see." Owner: "But it sure is a hassel to clean up after all of these things." Bartender: "Yeah but doesn't the WWA reinburst us for any damages rendered in return for the use of the bar?" Owner: "Yeah they do." {He pauses as he looks off over at the bar at a dark complected gentlemen sitting there alone with a drink in one hand, and holding an icepack on his knee and on his head in the other one, sitting in quiet contemplation.} Owner: "Say, speaking of the WWA, isn't that one of them right now?" Bartender: "Yeah it is. That's none other than 'Sky Flyin' Jimmi Zion. He almost one the Bourbon Street Brawl. He was in the within the final four people that was eliminated tonight. He and that other guy Leviathan I think it was...or was it Lockdown, I always get those two mixed up, got eliminated at the same time by the winner of the brawl...Chance." Owner: "Point being, why is he here right now? I thought that we closed." Bartender: "Sorry sir, but that was my call, after the brawl was over, he came back here to relax and everything, so I let him in for a little bit to help he clear his mind after his loss. I didn't mean to go over your head, but I thought that it would be good for business." Owner: "I guess he could stay for a little bit, but I want him out of here when he finish cleaning up." Bartender: "Yes sir." {The owner walks off back to his office in the back. The bartender walks back over to Jimmi Zion sitting at the bar nursing his drink in his hand.} Bartender: "Hey it's almost closing time, can I get you one more for the road." "Sky Flyin" Jimmi Zion: "Yeah, Long Island Ice Tea and make it a double on the rocks." {The bartender takes Zion's glass and begins filling it up, then mixes it up and stirs it up and passes it back to Zion.} Jimmi Zion: "How much do I owe you for this?" Bartender: "Don't bother, we'll just put it on the WWA's tab." Zion: "Thanks." Bartender: "Say you did really awesome tonight." Zion: "Thanks but I still lost." Bartender: "Yeah, but you lasted as long as you did, and who knows what you will do in the Independance Day Rumble next week." Zion: "What makes you think that I'll be in it?" Bartender: "Trust me Mr. Zion, you're money in the bank. The WWA would be fools to leave you off in the rumble. You were so close to winning it tonight, but that goes without saying. If you more than held your own tonight in the brawl. I can only imagine what would happen when you face off in the Rumble next week. If you wreste as half as good as you did tonight, they don't stand a chance against you, and you have the rumble in the bag. You almost won it, now imagine how well you will do if all you have to do is toss some guys over the top rope to eliminate them. That's easy money, I'm telling you Mr. Zion, you have this rumble won." Zion: "I appreciate the vode of confidence, but it's gonna take a lot more than just kind, motivating words to help me win the rumble." Bartender: "You're right, it's gonna take something supernatural phenomenal for you to win. Which is nothing short for you. You pull out the supernatural night after night after night. I'm a huge fan of your's Mr. Zion. I've watched you since the very beginning of the PWF, and I've heard all of the heat on you, and that doesn't matter. You are the future of the WWA. I know you can win it next week. Just take where you went wrong tonight, learn and go from here. I guarantee a victory for Sky Flyin Jimmi Zion at the Independance Day Rumble next week." Zion: {He stands up off the bar stool, slams the drink down and addresses the bartender with a muc different attitude than before. "Thank you for the words, I will do whatever I can not to let you nor the rest of my fans down. I guess it's what I needed. Thanks for the drink...it's good. Now if you excuse me, where would I find your washroom so I can let the lizard loose, and then I will take my leave of your fine establishment?" Bartender: "Oh it's just down that middle hallway and it's the last room on the right, passed the ladies room." Zion: "Thank you very much." {Zion walks off down the hall to the restroom and opens the door. The camera follows him inside. The camera watches as Zion steps behind the stall, closing it behind him. You can hear the sound of a loud resounding watering tinkle echoing throughout the bathroom. Zion lets out a nice relieved "Awwwwwwwwwwwwww" as he takes care of his nature business. After a moment or two, the toilet flushes and Jimmi Zion steps out from behind the stall, he walks over to the sink to wash his hands and then dries his hands off with a paper towel. After he is done, he glances over at the camera for the first time to address the views in question.} Jimmi Zion: "Tonight, I went toe to toe with the WWA in the annual Meltdown Bourbon Street Brawl, and almost won it. However, I ended up falling short in the final four combatants. I've said it once and I'll say it again. It doesn't matter if I win by a squash or a screw job...winning's still winning. The same can be said about losing. Winning and losing is all part of the game. Well I lost tonight, but I will not dwell in the past and wallow in self-pity, I will press on for the goal, and that's to win the Independance Day Rumble. It's funny how the Rumble is named or takes place on the American Independance Day. It's more than fitting for me." "This coming Sunday, My independance will be unleashed. I will make my impact. I will break away. I will BE SET FREE. I almost was set free once and for all from the confided of a mediocre midcard status. No more having to worry about paying my dues and earn my shots, I will have already made it to my top." "I know that there alot of potential WWA superstars who COULD win this Rumble, including tonights Bourbon Street Brawl winner, Chance. He could win two in a row, but I will not stand to lose to Chance or anybody again. I plan on finishing what I started. I almost finished the rumble, but I fell short. I failed basically...but no matter. I will not fail again. I will complete and fullfill my destiny, I will become one step closer to becoming the new WWA World Heavyweight champion. I will make my mark, and normally I say that even if I lose, I will still make everybody know and respect me. Again not this time. Losing is the farthest from my mind. I will stop at NOTHING until I am the last man standing. Until I eliminate the final participant of the Rumble and get proclaimed as the 2003 Independance Day Rumble winner." "Let all of the 29 other WWA superstars hear my words. I will not settle for second best. Losing is NOT an option. I will walk into the rumble with one clear goal in mind and that is TO WIN. I don't just do it for me, but on my wife and my unborn child on the way. I do this entirely for them. I dedicate my win entirely to them. They will be the reason why I succeed this weekend." "And to all of my enemies, my doubters, my adversaries, you will know exactly who I am and will respect me. So before you all place your bets and count me out, you better rethink EVERYTHING because I'm aiming high and aiming for the greatest prize of them all... IMMORTALITY... Through the persuit of the WWA title. Let the hunt begin." "Then to by biggest adversary right now... NICK ADAMS... Tonight in the Bourbon Street Brawl, I eliminated you, and you said that students weren't supposed to teach...teachers, but I showed you a thing or two didn't I when I pinned you in the brawl. I showed you that I could go the distance. If you do indeed choose to persue this little war between us any further, then by all means step up because I have plenty more where the brawl came from. I'm just getting started. In the rumble, I plan on showing exactly, that I will do more than just go the distance, I will personally win the whole bloody thing." "And to everybody else in the WWA, hear my words. What I say to Adams goes to all of you. I will do exactly that. So get ready because The one most high will not fall. He will rise above you and claim his godhood. He will claim his Immortality. He will claim his independance and thus becoming the next WWA World Heavyweight Champion." {Jimmi Zion brushes passed the camera man and walks out of the bathroom where he is greeted by a WWA "punching bag"...lackey/errand boy Antonio Cruz .} Antonio Cruz: "Sky Flyin Jimmi Zion I presume mang?" Zion: {Remembering all of the trouble that Cruz used to cause Zion when Cruz was WWA commissioner and alligned with The Cell} "Yes, what the bloody hell do you want...MANG?" Cruz: "Hey don't hate the playa mang, I'm just here to deliver you your opportunity to draw for the Independance Day Rumble." Zion: "Yeah go on." Cruz: {Pulls a handful of unmarkedenvelopes in front of Zion like a deck of cards} "Here pick an envelope, inside has a number, what you choose will determine order you will enter in the rumble mang." Zion: "Is this somekind of joke Cruz, you better not be pulling anything." Cruz: "Hey I'm just a lowly errand boy mang, what I can do?" Zion: "That's true, well in that case what do I choose?" Cruz: "Yeah but choose wisely, or else ye be fawked mang." Zion: "I get the idea, just shut up and let me choose." Cruz: "Okay here it goes." {Cruz shuffles the envelope almost like a deck of cards and then fans them out for Zion to choose from.} "Here you go mang...choose." {Zion takes his time as he draws the envelope. After a few moments, he pulls out one and then opens it up to reveal the number. As he meditates on the number, you see a huge smile appear on his face as he puts the envelope back into his pocket and then brushes off of Cruz as he walks off out of the bar.} Zion: "Thanks Cruz, I'll see you this weekend." Cruz: {Trying to run off after him.} "Wait a minute mang, Jimmi, what was the number you drew. Tell me, I won't tell a single soul, not even Lockdown or Leviathian. Come on Jimmi, is this about that thing that I did to you as commish? Oh come on no hard feelings. Forgive and forget mang. Jimmi please." {As Cruz follows Zion out of the bar, just as he walks out, he is immediately punched in the face by none other than Jimmi Zion. Cruz falls to the ground, all of the envelopes fly up in the air and all over the place. Cruz, lays on the ground rubbing his face trying to come to. Zion calmly massages his fists as he stands over the fallen Cruz grinning ear to ear.} Zion: "Oh yeah, thanks for reminding me Cruz. I've been iching to do that to you ever since you got demoted. I guess you lucked out up until this point. Well now you finally got your's in the end. Mikalia sends her regards as well." "You know Cruz, everybody's right, you really are a punching bag. I feel much better now. Now if you really wanna know about what number I drew in the Rumble. Well, you'll just have to wait and see until this weekend won't you. Let's just say that my chances of winning the rumble just got better. See you this Sunday Cruz, give the Cell my regards and tell them that their next." {Zion walks off down the sidewalk leaving Cruz on the ground trying to gather the envelopes as the camera fades out.} *{Fade Out}
