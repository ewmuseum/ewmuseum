Rumblings

30 Jan 2006

By Tracer Bullet

Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene opens inside Denrol's House of Pizza in Toronto. The place is quite busy, with all the TV screens showing the pay-per-view of a certain other federation that is occurring tonight. One section of the balcony is roped off and alone at their table, watching the show and finishing an extra large Victory Pizza, are our heroes Denrol & Tracer Bullet. They are joined by Bullet's wife Missy and Denrol's wife Alexandra. The foursome seem to be trying to enjoy the matches, but there certainly is a dark cloud noticeable over them.) Alexandra: What time is your flight? Denrol: 8:30 in the morning. Bullet: 8:28, actually. I'll never understand why they do that. Denrol: To try and pretend they get flights off on time? Bullet: But then they have ten- or fifteen-minute delays, so it's really an exercise in futility. (Tracer points at the screen.) Bullet: Sort of like that guy thinking he'll win a title shot. Alexandra: Maybe. But he's cute. (Denrol and Tracer turn to look at Alex.) Alexandra: What? I like short people. Bullet: Which I guess would explain why you hate basketball so much. And Randy Newman, for that matter. Denrol: Let's not mock Randy Newman. Bullet: I never intended to mock Randy Newman. But if she likes short people and he doesn't, they're just not going to see eye to eye. Alexandra: Why must everything come down to an obscure pop culture reference? Bullet: Well, look what I named myself after. It doesn't really say why, but it should give you some idea as to my mindset. (Denrol chews on a slice and then speaks with some food stuffed in his mouth.) Denrol: Which reminds me. What do you think of that P.I. Muppet Man hired? Bullet: To be honest, she kind of slipped under my radar. Which means she's either absolutely horrible, or way too damn good. Denrol: By the way, I'm going to need another fedora whenever we get a new match. Kinda threw my only one into the crowd. Bullet: If you're looking for something that makes you look foolish, you've got a perfectly good giant foam cowboy hat at home. Denrol: Now why did you have to go down that road? Just asking where you get yours. Bullet: I special order them, and I don't think you'll be able to get one before tomorrow night. Not that we're scheduled to do anything, but... Denrol: We're not. I called Vik to make sure. Alexandra: Four times. (Tracer looks up hopefully.) Bullet: So we've got the night off? Denrol: Well Vik offered one, but I turned it down. Bullet: One what? A match? With who? Please don't tell me we'd have had to lower ourselves to fighting the J.O.B. Squad this time. Denrol: No, Vik offered us the day off and to stay home. (Tracer tilts his head in confusion.) Bullet: And... you turned it down? (Missy just looks at Alexandra, and they both shake their heads. They've been here before on numerous occasions.) Bullet: What the hell is wrong with you? Denrol: Trent, if we just start taking shows off, we're going to get forgotten and we'll never get up the tag ladder. Bullet: Dude, all we have to do is win... which I know we can. Against anyone they could throw at us. Denrol: And we'll get a bad rep for skipping shows. (Tracer looks at the screen again. The show seems to be drawing to a close.) Bullet: That guy disappears for months at a time, and he gets himself put into the title picture. Maybe if we can just shake the right hand we can get this all over with quickly. Denrol: Shake the right hand? Bullet: I'm certainly not doing the alternative. Missy: But that would hurt your career anyway. Bullet: I don't know... there has to be some reason Zbyszko still has a job. Denrol: He's a legend. Bullet: So is Bigfoot, but you don't see it crushing cars any more. (There are a few moments of silence between our heroes as loud noises can be heard below while the fans begin to leave.) Voice: Man, that was some bulls**t. Just because the guy gets a little blood on him, he thinks he can waltz right back in and be a star again. It doesn't bother paramedics! Other Voice: That's not the point at all. It's a disclosure thing. That still wouldn't excuse it if he gets another title run at someone else's expense, but I think he deserves SOMETHING. Voice: They should give him a gift basket full of meat and Vaseline and call it a day. (There is another pause.) Other Voice: Meat and Vaseline? Voice: Don't question my logic! (We return back with our heroes.) Denrol: So you going to pay up tonight or later? Bullet: Pay what? Denrol: A certain person won a certain belt off a certain canuck. Bullet: Yeah, and? Denrol: Now he pretends to forget our bet. Missy: What bet? Denrol: We just placed some money on that match. Bullet: How can you even think about bets at a time like this? Denrol: What do you mean? I've won money. That's always important. (Tracer looks into Denrol's eyes.) Bullet: Look, bud... it's after hours. No one else is here but us and the wives. You can go ahead and let it all out. (Denrol sighs.) Denrol: I'd rather not. Bullet: If there's one thing I've learned over the course of my life, it's that you can't keep grief to yourself. Why do you think I bitch so much now? Denrol: Stuff happens. It's how this business works. Guys come and they go. If you let it get to you, you're going nowhere. (Tracer puts a hand on Denrol's shoulder from across the table.) Bullet: You knew him a lot better than I did. Despite all your problems, I know you had kind of a grudging respect for him at the very least. You can't pretend this isn't affecting you in some way. (Alexandra clears her throat.) Alexandra: I think the problem is the numbers and the ages. It seems in this business we have an unusual number of deaths in proportion to the people in it. And it tends to be young ones. Bullet: But this is the first time it's hit someone we knew personally. You can't say that Ian doesn't hurt a lot more than Eddie. And boy, Eddie hurt bad. Alexandra: It may be closer to home, but it still holds. Gordon feels if you let the tragedy get to you, you're going to just give up. Bullet: So you're just going to let him run away? He wouldn't let me do that, why should I let him? Denrol: I'm not running away. And this has completely nothing to do with this tag team. Bullet: Look, you're going to have to do something about this sometime. Eventually, you're just not going to be able to take it any more, and you're going to break down. And if this tag team thing is going to happen, I'd much rather it be now than in the middle of the ring, right? Denrol: You don't worry about that, it won't happen in the ring. (The former champ throws up his hands.) Bullet: All right, but don't come crying to me when you come... crying... to... me. Okay, that didn't come out quite right. (Denrol suddenly stands up.) Denrol: I got some paperwork to fill out. I'll meet you guys at the car in ten minutes. (Denrol turns and leaves, taking a door to the back offices. Tracer turns to Alex.) Bullet: Should we leave him alone, or do you want to go after him? Alexandra: Best if we leave him be. He doesn't work well with company in these moments. Missy: So how are you taking it? Alexandra: I didn't get to know him as well as Gordon did. Difference between doing an interview or two and doing matches. But it's tough anyway. Bullet: I seem to remember having all of one singles match with him, and that was after his return. So obviously I didn't know him altogether well, but just the fact that someone you'd seen kicking around backstage so recently will never be there again... you just feel a kind of emptiness. Alexandra: His character was supposed to be a mean guy, but that's not who he was. There are assholes in this business, even in the WWA. He wasn't one of them. Bullet: Yeah. Freaking men, though. Always trying to put on a brave face. We try to act like nothing bothers us. But isn't it true that it takes more balls for a man to cry than not to? (The women look at Tracer, then at each other.) Alexandra: I'm not touching that one. He's yours, you deal with it. (Alexandra finishes her soda.) Alexandra: Maybe we should get going. (Tracer stands up and stretches, grabbing the last slice of Victory Pizza as he does. He takes a bite and chews thoughfully.) Bullet: Maybe I should really come up with some other golden object to put on these things. This is good and all, but it's a Victory Pizza for a reason. Anyway, you're probably right. (The detective takes another bite of his very own culinary creation as the other people at the table rise.) Missy: Is Gordon going to be all right? Alexandra: He will. The man's parents died in an airplane crash. This cuts close to home, but he'll get through it. (Tracer and Missy nod. As a way to cut the tension without causing any disrespect, Tracer offers either of the women a bite of the slice he's been eating. They both dismiss the offer with a wave of their hands. The scene fades to black as the threesome collect their stuff to leave.) Designed By [name withheld] | © World Wrestling Alliance 1997 - 2005
