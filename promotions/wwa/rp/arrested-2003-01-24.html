Arrested!!

24 Jan 2003

By Shaman

Item 8 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 24 Jan 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
** The camera focuses on a jail somewhere outside Phoenix Arizona as three officers obviously battered and beaten are speaking with one another as they look at each other and the first one speaks.** Pete: Joe who the hell is that guy took twelve of us to bring him down and he was out to hurt each and everyone of us!! Joe: He that wrestler called Shaman! He is one mean hombre!! Pete: yeah no crap!! The guy nearly tore my head from my shoulders!! ** Pete rubs his neck as another man walks up!!** Joe: Hey Steve whats the scoop?? Steve: Well we gotta let him go. Seems we broke procedure and we have to release him completely no strings attached!! Pete: No way!! Joe: What the hell you talking about the guy beat the holy hell out of twelve of us and we are supposed to let him go!! Steve: yes and the command came from the higher ups!! Seems that Native Americans and especially this one where you do not read him his rights and gang rush him is kind of illegal in mosts states including ours!! Pete: Errr yeah you do have us there!! Steve: Unfortunately Shaman pointed that out to the Captain as he came in and began questioning him! Joe: So the man is free to go?? Steve: yep!! ** Suddenly the doors swing open as Shaman walks out with a torn shirt on and ripped blue jeans and sneakers looking at the three men as the three men back away as he stops and looks at them.** [Shaman] " Played with fire today and got burnt didn't you!! Not a big deal just avoid me and you won't hurt nearly as much as you would think. I thank you for the warm up gentlemen. I mean its not everyday that i get to kick the holy crap out of Phoenix's finest!! Besides the target im after will be in the Cage at the PPV. You need to move before i get angry gentlemen. You have pisses me off enough today!! ** Shaman pushes through the officers as each quickly back away from him as he walks over to a taxi and gets in as it drives off as the scene pans back to the officers.** Joe: Man the nerve of that guy!! Steve: yeah the only guy lately that has ever held us in check!! Pete: No the only man ever to beat up the swat team!! Joe: Im not swat i just pulled him over for a speeding violation!! Steve: yeah i gaurantee next time you see his truck you won't be pulling him over will you! Joe: Your damn right there! I'll just let him go!! Pete: Lets hope the newspapers won't hear about this! They'll have a field day. The mayors already in enough trouble!! Steve: Pete lets justget back inside and speak with the Chief im sure he will tear our heads off about this!! Joe: yeah lets go face the music! Pete: Ugghhhh i don't wanna do this!! Steve: Theres no choice!! ** As the three men walk back inside the precinct the taxi cab pulls back up with Shaman inside looking through the window smiling very sadistically as he rolls down the window and speaks.** [Shaman] " Told them when they tried to arrest me i wouldn't go easily and they needed to make the charges stick!! Guess thats a lesson for them to learn! Oh well Rage in the Cage is coming soon. Payne my revenge is at hand!! Adams and Grunge no disrespect to you, But im coming in this with a dangerous temprement. Avoid me at all costs if you can. This is not going to be a walk in the park ladies!!" ** Shaman rolls up the window and taps the driver on the shoulder as they drive off in another direction as the camera fades to black leaving these words.** GOT BLOOD!!
