Hero?

16 May 2003

By myZery

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The scene opens in a huge junkyard. There are rottweilers all over the place; barking and growling at anything they see. The moon reflects off the hundreds of car bonnets. A light wind whistles through the rusted doors. The scene is almost perfect; the only thing missing is the sound of the car’s crushing. Then out of nowhere the sound of the crushing machine that turns big piles of metal rubbish into little boxes begins to destroy an old Ford Fiesta. Then a wolf’s howl breaks the crushing noise and a dark figure arises from behind one of the burning cars. He is wearing a long black coat and black trousers; his long hair covers his face. He slowly emerges from the burning wreckage and approaches the camera. The man gets in a certain distance to the camera is revealed. It is myZery. He walks up to the camera looking quite depressed as usual. His fists are tightly clenched and stay steady by his side. He stops in front of the camera, his head facing the ground. a crane picks up a car in the background and leaves it hanging from the hook. myZery stares into the camera and begins to speak. “Saturday is a huge chance for me. A true test. I have been in two matches so far and haven’t come out bad after them; I think this is a good sign. I have made an impact here at the WWA and that is what I joined for. I didn’t join to be a loser; I didn’t join to destroy my career. I am not going to let this opportunity slip away. I don’t get many chances to become what I want to, but now I have the chance to win my first title at WWA. Showtime, it makes me sick to the stomach to ponder about you being a champion in the WWA. It is not for losers and dropouts like you, it is for true champions and talented wrestlers. So what the hell are you doing here? It gives me a headache to try and realise what the hell you are doing with a title. How the hell could you be a champion? You were kicked out of a federation for being the worst ever wrestler in it, how embarrassing can you get? Dude, that is pathetic. You sound like some untrained rookie. Maybe that’s what you are, but I don’t know how the hell you ever made it in WWA! I am going to enter Fiesta as a WWA superstar and leave as a WWA champion. If you have the stupidity to think that you can stop me your even dumber then I thought. You cannot survive Traumatic Cerebral Stress, you cannot outstand depression and you definitely cannot slow me down. So Showtime, pack your bags because after Fiesta when I brutalise your lousy carcass in front of the thousands of WWA fans, you aint ever going to want to show your face again. I am going to kick your ass from one turnbuckle to the other; I will leave you lying motionless in a pool of your own blood. Just remember that before you enter the ring on Saturday.” myZery slowly begins to take steps back and gradually disappears into the shadows. Scene fades. The scene opens again and I am sitting in my five star hotel suite. I am so tired from all the work, and stress I have been under lately and therefore decide to give myself a rest. I lie across the coach, legs spread out wide. As my eyes begin to slowly close, Hailey tiptoes into the room in a very short pink dressing gown. She is looking as fine as ever. I wonder to myself, ‘Where’s Jane?’ Then Jane scuttles into the room with nothing but a towel just covering herself up. Hailey: Hey honey. Are you ok? Jane: Yeh, you look a bit down. I think about my match on Saturday and how much it could take to actually beat Showtime. Is he going to be a challenge at all or is he all talk? I think are all these stories just a trick so that I let my defences down and give him the win on Fiesta? I don’t think Showtime would be that smart to go to all the trouble of thinking that out. Hailey: myZery? myZery: Yeh sexy. Hailey: Are you ok? myZery: Yeh I’m ok. But you could make me feel a hell of a lot better. I smirk at Hailey as she licks her lips. She begins to run her finger up my leg. Hailey: Now how could I do that? Jane: I want to make you feel better too. Jane smirked at me with an almost devilish look in her eyes. I had to be the luckiest man in the world. Hailey kisses me and then bends over in front of me to reveal a pink thong. My little smirk turned into a huge grin within seconds. Hailey got my hand and then Jane grabbed my other and they dragged me into the bathroom where they had a bubbling Jacuzzi set up and ready. There were sented candles all around the Jacuzzi and life couldn’t be better. Jane dropped her towel and then Hailey undone her gown, I shut the door and had one hell of a Jacuzzi, believe me. Scene fades. Hours later I emerged from the steamy bathroom. The two girls were exhausted and scrambled into bed. I on the other hand had things to do. I couldn’t stop thinking about how important this title shot was to me and what it could do for my career. It could end up to me one of the most important matches I have ever been in. I had to get my head clear so I got my tracksuit on and left my room to go for a jog. I paced down the corridor as fast as I could, as I didn’t want to bump into anyone. I got to the stairs and raced down them at the highest speed possible. As soon as I got outside the hotel I sighed in relief and began to jog down the street. I ran a few blocks as buckets of sweats poured from my scorching forehead. I suddenly hear a cry from a young woman. She screamed ‘help’ . I rushed over to the scene as fast as I could. “What’s wrong? Why are you screaming?” “HE STOLE MY HANDBAG!” The lady pointed to a man sprinting off into the shadows. In pure instinct I ran after him trying to pursue the young lady’s bag. I ran as hard as I could and finally caught up with the thief. As soon as I got beside him I speared him to the ground. I grabbed the bag off him and hit him one right-handed punch. I left it at that and tried to walk away but being the jackass that he is, the man charged at me with a knife. “I AM SICK OF THIS SHIT!” I grabbed the mans wrist and twisted it until he dropped the knife. The thief screamed in pain, as I held tight of his wrist. I hit the man with a light knee to the face knocking him to the ground. I looked at the man in disgrace. “PIECE OF CRAP” I grabbed the bag and walked back over to the woman. as I approached her I could see the delight in her eyes. “Here ya go.” “Thank you, thank you.” The woman began to check the bag to see if anything was missing and I took that as the perfect chance to do another disappearing act. I faded back into the shadows and continued to walk down the dangerous streets. Design and Graphics © World Wrestling Alliance 2002
