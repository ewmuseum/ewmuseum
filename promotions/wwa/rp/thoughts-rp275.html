Thoughts

22 Jul 2003

By Matt Keith

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
EXT. PARK, LOS ANGELES- DAY [Once again it’s a nice day in the city of Los Angeles, the city of angels. The sun is out and the temperature is a cool 80%, there is a perfect breeze as well. Even the birds seem happy as they fly past overhead. On the path several people ride past on bikes while others jog or skate past on inline’s.] [The WWA cameraman pans right and as you can see there is Cameron and Matt Keith sitting on a park bench holding hands, Cameron’s resting her head is on Matt’s shoulder. Propped up against the bench is Matt’s skateboard, it’s a Dragon Ball Z board with the lead character Goku on it, needless to say it’s a masterpiece of anime art. On Matt’s forehead is a bandage where he received several stitches from the wars at the pay per view] Matt Keith: What are you thinking about? [Cameron smiles affectionately] Cameron: I was thinking it would be nice if we could have an outside wedding. The weather seems almost perfect. Matt Keith: It is nice. Cameron: Where do you want to go after we leave here? Matt Keith: It’s up to you babe, have any ideas? Cameron: Hmmm, how about we go somewhere and have a nice dinner just the two of us. Matt Keith: That sounds nice. [Cameron sits up] Cameron: You’re not still hurting from that ladder match are you? Matt Keith: Truthfully I’m soar as hell. [Cameron is rubbing Matt chest in a slow and soothing motion] Cameron: [Sexy tone] Well we can always go home and I can massage the hurt away. [She kisses Matt on the lips and stands up] Cameron: Lets get some ice cream from the stand. [Out of the blue here come WWA interviewer Wallace Coleman, he must was off camera walking with the cameraman] Matt Keith: You go ahead I’ll catch up. Cameron: All right, don’t be long. [She walks off and the cameraman has the camera fixed on her rear end, getting a perfect shot of her sexy walk in those tight jeans, Coleman is damn near burning a hole in Cameron’s jeans] Matt Keith: Hey numb nuts the parties this way. [The cameraman pans back around so fast that he almost drops the camera] Cameraman: Sorry. Matt Keith: Do it again jerky and you’ll end up being the first WWA cameraman and interviewer that gets their ass whipped by yours truly. [He’s grins arrogantly and crosses his arms] Matt Keith: [Mock friendly tone] Wallace Coleman, how have you been dude? Coleman: Fine, thanks for asking. Matt Keith: Man you’re still as funny looking as ever. Coleman: Excuse me? Matt Keith: Sorry I forgot you’re still sensitive about those gapped teeth and weird shaped ears. I’m telling you man you look like Mr. Spock on crack; I bet you’re still a virgin. Coleman: I’ll have you know that I’m married to a very lovely woman. Matt Keith: You call that four hundred pound baboon I saw you with last week a lovely woman? Damn bro I thought that was the missing link. Coleman: She’s not fat she’s just big boned. [Wallace stops himself after he just realized that he’s never met Matt Keith before today, and Matt has never seen his fat wife.] Coleman: I see the stories are true, you are and asshole. Matt Keith: Maybe so but you’re still one ugly S.O.B, I mean planet of the apes ugly. [The anger in Wallace seems to be swelling up] Matt Keith: [Looking at his watch] Enough of the insults for now just ask the question Dr. Zaus. [Wallace holds back his anger and asks the questions] Coleman: [Mock sincerity] First and foremost let me be the first one to say welcome to the WWA. Matt Keith: [Doubtful tone] Right, that sounded real sincere monkey boy. [Wallace ignores Matt] Coleman: It has to be intimidating to be in the very same federation as such greats as Tracer Bullet, and the World Champion Kash. Do you feel pressure that you may not be able to perform on that level? [Matt smirks arrogantly] Matt Keith: If this was maybe a few months ago I probably would feel some pressure, but at this point and time in my career I have no reason to feel like I can’t perform on the level of the top stars. To be perfectly honest I’ve surpassed the vets in the WWA because I’m young, I’m innovative, and I’m the future of the business. On many occasions I’ve been labeled as the Icon Killer. Coleman: Well Matt Monday will be your chance to prove if there is iron in you words when in your debut match you’re facing another EWA graduate in Alex Knight. Matt Keith: You sure have a way with words don’t you Wallace? [Coleman nods] Matt Keith: Yes Alex Knight was one of three that was given a WWA contract by Joe Stock, however Alex is no way of form on the same level as me, the proof is in the pudding and Alex hasn’t impressed me. Coleman: He’s impressed someone since he’s on the WWA Fiesta roster. [Matt stares at Coleman] Matt Keith: Let me guess Coleman, you’re an Alex Knight fan. Coleman: Um… Matt Keith: Yeah just like I thought. [Cameron is now back with two vanilla ice cream cones, she hands Matt one and sits down next to him. Watching Coleman she licks the cone sensually, Wallace gets a knot in his throat watching her.] Cameron: Problem? Coleman: Oh, um. [Matt’s eyeballing Coleman, then finally finish talking] Matt Keith: As I was saying, Knight hasn’t impressed me. Did you take a look at his pay per view match? I almost went to sleep watching it, no charisma, no athleticism; it was just him getting his ass kicked by a two-bit curtain jerker by the name of Adrena Lynn. Coleman: He did get the win. Matt Keith: [Sarcastically] Wow he beat Adrena Lynn, what a feat. Please Coleman if you want to kiss his ass I’ll give you his phone number. Now he may have beaten Lynn with that hacky move the Kyrptoknight but the fact of the matter is this my chimpanzee friend, he won’t beat me. [Matt takes a lick of his ice cream] Matt Keith: Now if you don’t mind my fiancé and I are enjoying this nice day, so why don’t you go swinging from a tree or whatever you like to do. [Matt’s grinning at the angry Wallace Coleman] FADE TO BLACK
