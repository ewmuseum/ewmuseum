Land Of The Forsaken

9 Jun 2003

By Se7en

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The scene opens up outside the local WWA arena. Theres a huge amount of fans gathering outside for some special event, which is unknown. Suddenly a husky and deep voice from the PA begins to speak. "LADIES AND GENTLEMAN.. TONIGHT, AND TONIGHT ONLY.. A SPECIAL AUTOGRAPH SESSION.. A SPECIAL Q&A CROWD INVOLVMENT.. WITH THE RAJIN' CAJUN'.." The crowd roar with a huge delight, but something fishy is going on. The PA man sounds very much like Se7en. And seconds later from the back entrance, Se7en steps out holding his black cane. The crowd show there dissaproval straight away with boo's and abusive language. Se7en begins speaking in a loud and powerfull voice. Se7en: "So this is how gullable WWA fans are.. Weak.. pathetic.. It almost makes me laugh.. You poor souls make me sick" Se7en stands strong amongst the boo's and loud roars from the fans. Se7en: "One of your heroes has drowned in his sarrow.. Like a legless dog and a headless chicken, this federation is slowly becoming useless. Land of The Forsaken Souls.. " Se7en starts to walk towards the fans who are secured behind the barriers by the security gaurds. Se7en: "Fiesta will soon arrive, and Jimmi Zion will be your next fallen hero. A man who flies, like he has wings. A fly also has wings.. wings that i will poison. Wings that will be rotten to the core, along with his heart and souls... Rotted to the core" Abuse and booing are still being thrown at Se7en from every which way, but Se7en doesn't mind, he continues. Se7en: "I have been leaving a trail of injured and sore bodies behind me on my search for my internal justice. What is my internal Justice?.. The pain and suffering of a thousand men, A chance to see grown men look into my eyes and see what i have seen, known what i have known. I want each and every WWA wrestler to stand eye-to-eye with Se7en and try and stand while i feed him my torment" Se7en walks over towards the barriers, even closer than before. He stands still and looks into nowhere, puts his head down and looks at the ground. Seconds later he puts his head back up and theres blood dripping from the mouth piece of his mask. The fans goes silent. FADE OUT.
