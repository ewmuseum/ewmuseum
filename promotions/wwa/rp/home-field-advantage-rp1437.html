Home Field Advantage

4 Jun 2004

By Tracer Bullet

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(We see a busy city street at dusk. The shadows of the night are slowly creeping in as the sun sinks into the west, when a voice rings out.) The time: 7:38 PM The place: Downtown New Orleans The reason: Is a song by Hoobastank The person: Heh... well... (Someone steps out of a dark alley. He's wearing a black trench coat and a wide-brimmed hat covering his face. Ordinarily, this would look mysterious. However, this guy wears that outfit all the time. It's Tracer Bullet.) TB: The person is me. Tracer Bullet. Private eye. Three-time World Champion, dammit. Legendary lover. Tyler: What was that last one? TB: World champion. But most importantly, I am a New Orleans native. The Bourbon Street Brawl is more than just my brainchild... I almost feel as if it was my reason for getting into this business in the first place. It is the legacy I shall leave behind. Long after I have retired, people will still be talking about this annual event. And with good reason. So many WWA stars all duking it out in a no-holds-barred literal street fight in one of the sleaziest cities in the country. It's really quite a wonder to behold. But this year... this year, there are going to be some showcase matches, as well. Kash vs. Matt Warr. Denrol vs. Hardcore Kal. And of course... my loser leaves town title defense against Nick Adams. (Tracer smiles faintly.) TB: Which is why I'm out here tonight. When darkness creeps across this city, all of the disease and vermin come crawling out from the shadows. (Tracer points off-camera. Tyler turns the lens toward where he is pointing to reveal a drunkard passed out on the curb.) TB: That gentleman right there is merely the first of many disgusting pieces of trash that the curbs of this street will see tonight. And Monday night, those same curbs will be formally introduced to the skulls of many a superstar. Not the least of which is going to be my own opponent. You see, Nick... this city is a lot more raw and amoral than Seattle. Do you recall the opening to the musical version of A Streetcar Named Desire that was presented on The Simpsons? It really isn't that far from the truth... (Tracer clears his throat and begins singing.) TB: Long before the SuperDome, Where the Saints of football play, Lived a city that the damned called home, Hear their hellish rondalay... New Or-le-ans... Home of pirates, drunks, and whores! New Or-le-ans... Tacky, overpriced, souvenir stores! If you want to go to Hell, you should make that trip To the Sodom and Gomorrah on the Mississipp'! New Or-le-ans... Stinking, rotten, vomiting, vile! New Or-le-ans... Putrid, brackish, maggoty, foul! New Or-le-ans... Crummy, lousy, rancid, and rank! New Or-le-aaans! (The drunk vomits into the gutter.) TB: Hmph. Critics. If I wanted a review, I would have asked for one. Either way, despite all the filth and human misery in this town... or maybe because of it... I love living here. And that's going to give me one hell of a home field advantage when the time rolls around. You know, I almost feel sorry for any of the other WWA-ites who may get caught out here after dark. (He looks into the camera with a sardonic smile on his face.) TB: But then I remember that they're not me, so I don't care about them. But one way or another, I am going to come out on top in my match. And why is that, you ask? Because it's loser leaves town... (Tracer's eyes narrow, making him appear much more serious.) TB: ... and there is NO WAY IN HELL that I'll ever leave THIS town. (Fade to black.)
