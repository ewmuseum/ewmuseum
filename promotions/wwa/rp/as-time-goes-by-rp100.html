As Time Goes By

8 Jun 2003

By Dave Harley

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[A lone figure sits staring out of a window at the garden below. He sits not in a chair, but in a wheelchair. His loose clothing disguises the muscles which have turned to fat since he was last able to run the ropes. Despite all of this, his short brown hair and goatee have not changed since then. A small reminder of the man he once was. The door creaks open behind him and Dave Harley pops his head through.] Harley: Hey Jonny... [The figure in the wheelchair turns to face Dave as he enters. A slight smile touches his lips.] Carnage: Hey Dave. How did it go? Harley: It all went great. The plan worked perfectly. Mal got Rookie back and X Corp gained a few friends and allies. Carnage: How about the match? Harley: Well...we lost the Tag Titles. But that doesn't matter. We've done all that we set out to do. And more. We beat The Cell, we kept hold of the Titles until Revolution and we put up one hell of a fight in the King of The Hill. Carnage: So what do you guys have planned now? Harley: [smiles] Oh, we've got a few ideas. Carnage: [Laughs] Hell, I know that look. That's the look that used to get us into trouble when we were touring in Japan...good times. Harley: Absolutely...Listen, i just wanted to come and say...Thank You. Thank you for helping us out, for coming to Meltdown and playing along. Carnage: You know what the funny thing is? Sitting there at ringside on Meltdown and talking to some of the guys backstage before the show...I remembered how much this business means to me. I was reminded of the adrenaline, the rush of playing in front of the crowd...I guess I never thought that it would be you that gave that back to me...the love of this business. Harley: Well...thanks for all your help Jonny... [Dave slowly walks back towards the door. Jonny Carnage turns back towards the window. dave stops with his hand on the doorknob and turns back to Jonny.] Harley: Jonny...you do know...that if I could take it back... Carnage: [Smiles reassuringly] I know Dave. Thanks for stopping by. [Harley opens the door and makes to leave. He stops halfway through the door and turns back. A big grin crosses his face.] Harley: Hey Jonny... Carnage: Yeah? Harley: Wanna go get a beer? [Jonny Carnage laughs and turns his wheelchair towards the door. Dave holds the door open for him as he exits and the scene fades to black.]
