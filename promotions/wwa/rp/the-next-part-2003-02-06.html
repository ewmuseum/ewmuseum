The next part!!

6 Feb 2003

By Shaman

Item 2 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 6 Feb 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
** Camera zooms in on Shaman who is sitting in a down pour of rain as he sits there looking in no general direction as he smiles sadistically and looks up as he holds the mask of War from the Horsemen. He smiles as he crushes the mask in his hands snapping the ceramic like it was merely brittle paper. he then grabs the Famine mask and crushes it as well as he slowly raises and speaks.** [Shaman] " I have single handedly ended the Horsemen by ending the Magicians career. I have single handedly finished Troy Walcott. Now i have destroyed Chrystian Payne and ended his worthless career. The man may be dead for all i care. In every case i do not!! Now Matt Warr places another block in front of me!! Yes it is Nicholas Adams!!" ** Shaman rubns his hands together rather irritated it seems. Turning he looks upwards towards the sky as the rain falls and the lightning flashes in his eyes and illuminates his face as he stares at the camera again with his eyes appearing jet black and seemingly looking through the camera.** [Shaman] " Yes Adams you seem to have gotten yourself out of one jam and right into another. I am not going to take it easy upon you. Yes easy is to simple. I have plans Mr. Adams. You shall understand why i have changed or the matter of fact become what i should've been all along. No Mr. Adams you did not ring!! I have my eyes set upon a goal and you are the first stepping stone. You nor that worthless title Grunge has means anything to me, But a victory at all costs does. I get to beat the living hell out of an opponent and enjoy every damn minute of it!!" ** Shaman stops and smiles as he narrows his eyes.** [Shaman] " I even get to bleed and bleed some more. The loving pain of it all. I get to hurt and feel invigorated with each and every step of the way. No i think i will begin my rampage across the ranks of the WWA. Hurt the newest and brightest and make my way to the top. Denrol had done something right for once he changed. So have i games afoot Adams are you prepared to play??" ** Shaman smiles as he walks over to the camera moving in very close to it.** [Shaman] " Hey momma Adams can Nicholas come out to play?? I promise i won't hurt him too badly!! I will only make him scream and writh in agony!! I know he doesn't have many toys so i will bring a few of my own. I will show him how to use each and everyone of them!! Is it ok or does he want time to cry to you about how the big bad crazy man is going to get him!!" ** Shaman stops and sniffs the air in a sickening snortling sound.** [Shaman] " Can you smell that??" ** Shaman sniffs again.** [Shaman] " Can you guess what it is?? Its a beautiful aroma!!" ** Shaman turns and looks at the camera smiling.** [Shaman] " Its blood Adams im drawn to it like a moth to flame!! Yes i am going to watch people bleed again and again. The bloody path began at Rage in the Cage and it will not stop there!! Hmmm i wonder in which ways i can hurt the??" ** Shaman turns and reaches down picking up a flower and begins pulling petals off of it.** [Shaman] " I will hurt him, I will hurt him not, I will hurt him, I will hurt him not......" ** This goes on for a few minutes as Shaman seemingly has grabbed the biggest flower in the field. Not the best course of action for a man in his state of mind.** [Shaman] " Shut up....while i hink and contemplate a life heartening decision. I lost my count...AAAAAAArrrrrgggghhhhh!!!" ** Shaman turns and dashes directly at the camera snatching it from the camera man and slams it down upon the ground as he kneels down and picks up the mud and rain splattered camera and hands it back to the camera man.** [Shaman] " Hold on to it this time!! It won't work if you keep dropping it!!" ** Shaman seems completely oblivious of what he just did as he looks at the camera man smirking as the cameraman suddenly realises Shaman knows what he is doing all along.** [Shaman] " Ahhh i see the look of realization in your eyes. Good i was hoping you'd pick up on the fact that i am in complete control!! No more games!! No more running from anyone especially me Mr. Adams!! You want the Tv Title prepare to fight for your life to get it! This will be literally the most important match of your life if you leave standing up!! Good night Mr. Adams sleep well. Tell your lovely wife Morticia i said hello!! The most Sadistic individual to ever walk the WWA is coming over for dinner!!" ** Shaman stops as the camera man is seemingly backing away from him.** [Shaman] " No need to run little camera man!! I have only one other man to address. Thats the TV Champion Grunge!! Grunge where do you get off thinking that i am crazy?? Im as sane as anyone else!! I have my thoughts my wants and needs! I have my fears,,,Uh ...NO maybe not fears!! No Grunge you should fear the fact that this match may get way out of your control!! I will not take it easy on Nicholas Adams and do not even think of cheating me in the match because i have things well in hand if you do!! Everything other than that damn title you hold dear will be threatened got it old boy!! I sure hope so see you Monday it will be a good day in paradise!! ** Camera fades as Shaman stomps through the mud away from the camera as Shaman slams his feet down causing water to splash upwards and around him as he seems to not give a damn what is going on as these final words appear on screen.** GOT BLOOD!!
