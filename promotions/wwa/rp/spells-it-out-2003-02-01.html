Spells it out

1 Feb 2003

By Mal Somers

Item 2 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 1 Feb 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Scene – A modest hotel room somewhere in Calgary, Alberta, Canada. This year, home to Rage in the Cage VI. In the room, a travel bag is thrown on the bed, and the WWA Rookie Title Belt lays proudly on top of it. The room is quiet, except for the dull background noise of the internal heater.) Voice – “Hey, over here!” (The camera responds by panning to the left, revealing Mal Somers, sitting alone, playing Scrabble. He looks up at the camera.) Somers – “Greeting sports fans! This is the last chance I’ll get to chat to you all before Rage in The Cage. If you are putting any bets on, I’ve just worked something out which may spell out who will win! Check this out!” (Somers has arranged some Scrabble tiles on the board. Not realy sitting exactly on the squares, but nevertheless, spelling various words. Two of them in fact.) Somers – “Now lookie here. I’ve spelt “LEVIATHAN” and also “LOCKDOWN”. Two of the scariest S.O.B’s in the business today. Now just exactly who is the man in that relationship? Hmmm. Probably neither, but I digress. I’ve been toying with these pieces for a good...oh...five seconds, and if you rearrange the letters it spells...” (Somers moves a few pieces around, forming four words.) Somers – “NAÏVE CLOWN TALK, DOH!” (looks at camera semi seriously) Somers – “Man, am I a genius or what?! Don’t let Harley tell you any different. The Scrabble gods have come to me and have SPOKEN! Now onto Rage in the...” (The cameraman mumbles something to Somers) Somers – “What? What about our names? Well Ive never thought of that! What would the Scrabble gods have to say about us?” (Somers pulls out a few more letters and puts away the ones no longer needed, and spells “DAVE HARLEY” and “MAL SOMERS”) Somers – (rearranging tiles) “Hmmm. It spells “WINNERS OF THEIR MATCH VS THE CELL!” (pouts) “Not really, it actually spells “EMRD YAASEV LOMSH LRAE” (pouts again) “Hold on, I’ve got it... "DAVE, MS, RARELY LOSE!" The Scrabble gods have spoken again! Its official – Leviathan and Lockdown are naïve clowns and Dave and rarely lose!” (The cameraman mumbles again.) Somers – “Whats that?” Cameraman – “Actually Mal, you left out the letters “A”, “M” and “H”. So its not quite right. What would be correct would be “Dave, M.S., rarely lose ham.” Somers – (squinting in frustration) “We rarely lose ham? What the HELL does that mean? Are you crazy?" Cameraman – “Well I’m not the one trying to predict the outcome of a match using a FREAKIN Scrabble board!” Somers – (furious) “A famous person once told me that brains aren’t everything. In your case, they’re nothing! If I didn’t have to hype up my match at Rage in the Cage, Id kick your ass!” Camerman – “Sorry sir. Lets wrap this up.” Somers - (composing himself and looking into the camera) “ * ahem * It may come as no surprise to you viewers that I am a big fan of Scrabble. I realise it is only a game, but it is just too damn fun. But the problem with you Thomas, the problem with you, Lockdown... (smirks) ...if that is your real name. The problem with Miss Wise, the problem with Commissioner Penelope Cruz is that you all see this as a big game. (furrows his brow) You couldn’t be more wrong. You see this Sunday is only a matter of days away. Dave Harley and I, we may know how to joke around and have fun, but once you step into our office, the confines of the steel cage, the Extreme Corporation is all business! Lockdown, do you even remember who beat you into a pulp for the EWA EXTREME TITLE? In case you forgot, it was me! (muttering under his breath) And Dave then went on to beat me. That’s how EXTREME the X Corps are. (face starts getting red as the intensity builds) And you Leviathan. You stole my woman. You stole my chair. You stole a fair amount of my pride. Heck...you probably steal a few kisses from Lockdown and Cruz from time to time. Not that theres anything wrong with that WATER BOY! You are just like a calendar Thomas. Your days are numbered. Come Rage in the Cage, Dave and I have been given a mission, from none other than WWA President Matt Warr. To put you two in an ambulance. Heck, the Extreme Corporation may even go one better and put you in a hearse!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! (Fade Out)
