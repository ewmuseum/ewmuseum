Your Generation

1 Mar 2003

By Denrol

Item 6 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 1 Mar 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene opens with Denrol standing by an old, beaten up little car. Denrol himself is dressed in a very thick gray winter jacket, along with black jeans and winter boots. By the steam his breath is making, it must be quite cold. The car is an early 90s Mercury Tracer and it's clearly been in a number of accidents. The whole thing right now is covered with the white crap that old snow leaves behind. Denrol stares at the car, than slowly turns towards us and the camera.) Denrol: Take a long look Chance. This is a symbol. Just like your coin is a symbol of your luck, this car is a symbol that represents the dues I've paid. See, the WWA wasn't always this global organization. There was time when it was a small regional fed called Northeast Championship Wrestling. I joined the federation at that time. We didn't have first class flights. It was up to everyone to get to the shows on his own. I took this piece of junk car all over the Northeast. It's racked up plenty of miles. I slept in crappy little motels, where there was more cockroaches than people. When I was training, I wrestled at indy shows in front of maybe a couple dozen people. It wasn't fun. But I did it and I didn't complain. You're welcome to think whatever you like about me Chance. I couldn't care less. See, you yourself are a symbol. You're a symbol of everything that is wrong with the WWA today. That is why I picked you. Your own fellow wrestlers have voted you the next big star of the WWA. That's why I decided you needed to learn something about respect. Everything gets handed to your generation and none of you appreciate it. No, instead your kind just whines for more. (Denrol pats the hood of the car, for a moment thinking to himself.) Denrol: I struck a real blow to your generation on Monday I defeated two of your greatest representatives. First I pinned Wes Sanders, the man who returned by pretending to be a great legend, but proved out to be a nobody. Ian Tense and he fought in the most boring 60 minutes of wrestling I ever saw, at Rage in the Cage. So of course I beat him in less than 60 seconds. I think the irony is there is just wonderful. The "old guard" didn't even break a sweat, defeating the new "superstar". Wes Sanders is and always shall be, a nobody to me. Than there was Leviathan. One half of the Tag Team Champions. He also was defeated by the Greatest of All Earth Time. He didn't lose because he lacked his partner. He lost because no matter how big people push this generation, this generation is one of the worst the WWA has ever seen. This generation doesn't know a wristlock from a wristwatch. This generation couldn't wrestle themselves out of a paper bag. This generation is the biggest bunch of losers I have seen. I proved that at Global Warfare. I proved by defeating these two men. (Denrol cocks his head and grins.) Denrol: That leaves you Chance. Yes, you did manage, through a sneaky quick count, to pin me. Don't let that get to your head. Things will be different this Monday. For the first time ever, the WWA Franchise Players will tag up, to face 3 of the 2003 WWA generation of rejects. Your partners prove how low this generation has fallen. Jason Wainwright. A man who can't read or wrestle. A man who thinks because he had one decent match, which wasn't even a wrestling match, that makes him a legend. He calls his feud with Shane Dorian the "Feud of the Decade". More like the Feud of the Toilet. He makes his big return at Rage in the Cage, yet no one cares. What does he do when he comes back? Jack sh*t. Because of course, that is what he is. Enigamania. Or Nigrma. I never can't remember. Doesn't matter. Another nobody. He even wasted five minutes out of my life to do a stupid funeral for his character, who never amounted to anything. Yeah, that feud with Se7en really was out of this world. A former member of HOP, through nowadays, it seems almost everyone gets a chance to join that stable. Damien Thorne seems to run the place like a McDonalds. Come in for a week, leave before your first paycheck. And you Chance. It all comes down to you. The Breakout Star of 2003. The man who wants to earn his place among the superstars. The man who wants his own legacy. Here is your legacy Chance. You will be remembered as the little cockroach I crushed because I didn't like the way you looked at me. A nothing who for a short time, was a somebody only because a real somebody glanced his way. Their going to talk about our match at March Madness. It's going to be discussed by wrestling fans all over the globe. What they're going to be talking about is just how badly you got beaten. See I'm not going to wrestle you Chance. I'm not going to play your game and fly around like this is a damn circus with acrobats. I'm going to beat you up. I mentioned before what Bob Holly did to Matt on Tough Enough III. This is going to be worse. Matt only was in there a few minutes and than he tagged out. I'm going to give you a lot more. At March Madness I will pound you for a good 15 minutes, because that is what's expected of a pay-per-view match. I'm going to bloody your nose. I'm going to crack your ribs. I'm going to pull your hair out and toss it to the crowd. Than, when you're lying there, begging for mercy, I'm going to lock on the Meat Grinder and I'm going to listen to you scream. (Denrol chuckles.) Denrol: I'm going to love it. I'm also going to love this Monday, when the WWA Franchise Players show off what some real wrestlers can do. Chance, I'm going to be keeping an eye on you. I want you to pay close attention, to what we can do. I want you to know when you step into the ring with me at March Madness, you won't get to tag out. Back to that Meat Grinder. When you tap out and the bell rings, I'll let you go. I'll show you mercy. But I will get in your face and I will ask you one simple question. (Denrol holds up his index finger and points it at the camera.) Denrol: So you wanna be a wrestler? (Scene fades to black.)
