Time For Reflection II

28 Feb 2003

By Havoc

Item 2 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 28 Feb 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The deep, red liquid struck the white, plaster bowl messily. In the dim, dancing candlelight, the liquid nearly looked black. Only an occasional glimpse of reflected crimson gave away its true nature. Another drop struck the contrasting white bowl, splattering against the clean, smooth surface. Echoing in the flickering light, a titter of maniacal glee burst forth. “So precious, it is. Wouldn’t you agree? Of course you would, if you could.” Another drop of the crimson fluid strikes the bowl, melding with the previous drops. “Your partner interfered this past Monday, you know. He came out when the House of Pain was about to take over Global Warfare, establish themselves as a force to take on all other forces. He attacked Phobos and I during the House of Pain’s moment. He ruined it, just like the two of you ruined our latest tag title reign by making us lose to the Cell.” Two more drops of crimson join those already in the bowl, splattering and leaving little mini-droplets on the inner sides of the bowl. The near-black liquid mars the shiny beauty of the plaster, like tar on snow. “You may as well have stolen those belts yourself. You and your partner have caused us no end of trouble. You should never have resurrected the Veteranz.” The blood dripped from his victim’s toes, suspended above the white bowl. He laughed, not jovially, but gloomily. “But, by resurrecting the Veteranz, at least you’ve given us something to do. My brother and I, with Tiffani by our side, will kill the newly resurrected Veteranz. First, we’ll punish you for your weakness when the Dogs of War called upon you. Then, we’ll punish Shrapnel for returning by ending his career. Then the Veteranz will be no more.” He hung helplessly, his hands bound overhead. Deimos smiled at the mat of bruises, and the criss-crossing, angry, red lines that covered his victim’s body. “Like Prometheus, you are doomed to eternal torture, and agony. You’re my bitch, little Trapper.” Deimos felt the soft warmth caress his hand as more blood spilt forth, more precious life escaping from this heavy, bloated vessel. It ran down Trapper’s stomach, joining previously drawn lines along Trapper’s leg, until it reached his feet, where it dripped from his toes. It fell through the air, free from the confines of humanity, until it struck the growing pool of crimson life that slowly died in the shiny, white bowl. “All you need to know, Trapper, is that the Veteranz are finished. The Fates have already chosen us, Havoc, to be the greatest tag team in WWA history, not you. You were just keeping the place warm for us, like the Cell is keeping the belts warm for us. All will come to us in due time.” Deimos knelt, cupping the bowl in both hands. He rose with a smile, staring deep into Trapper’s one good eye. Blood and bruises nearly made him unrecognizable; his left eye had swollen shut as testament to the torment he had endured. In surveying the swath of destruction, Deimos smiled proudly. From behind him, her smooth, warm hand slid over his shoulder. He turned to face her. Her blond hair cascaded across her shoulders like a waterfall, framing his icy blue eyes, and full, deviously curled lips. The black, leather and chain halter teddy clung to the delicious curves of her body, along with the garter straps that held tightly to her black, fishnet stockings. The black leather and chain choker, with elbow length, fingerless gloves added to the mystique of her ensemble, rounded out by her ever-present black riding crop. “I like it when he squeals, like a little piggy.” Deimos stood over her, towering, massive, as though he could engulf her at any moment, smiling at her words. “You like the pain, love? You like hearing the whip crack? Does it excite you to hear and see the suffering of others?” Her breath quickened as she lunged forward, clenching her teeth onto Deimos’ pectoral muscle. A small moan of joy escaped her as she recalled the cries of agony. Deimos watched her, enjoying the feel of her mouth on his flesh with a lopsided, arrogant grin. She suckled at his dusky skin, before he reached around her, taking a length of her luxurious hair in hand. He pulled, extricating her from his flesh, holding her head tilted back. He brought the white vessel close, the red stain now coating the sides of the bowl. “Complete your journey into darkness, my sweet. Drink of the life of another, devour this little part of your enemy’s soul.” Her lips quivered in the dancing glow, her breath came in sharp, excited gasps. The bowl tilted, allowing the deep, red liquid to touch her lips, staining them for eternity. Feeling the coppery warmth, her tongue slipped from between her soft lips, delving into the recesses of the crimson liquor. Her lips reached out, grasping the bowl, tilting it a bit more to allow the warm vitae to gush into her mouth, exploding within her. Rivulets of blood, rivers of crimson, escaped her greed by flowing down the sides of her mouth, coating her chin and neck, running deeply through her cleavage and disappearing within the depths of her black, leather halter. Taking the bowl from her greedily slurping mouth, Deimos leaned over, pressing his tongue, broad and flat, against her chest to drink from the excess. Trapper’s one good eye closed tightly, recoiling in disgust. Nonetheless, the sounds of their horrific ritual assaulted him, mounting his revulsion. Tiffani whispered sharply to Deimos, her voice quivering with ecstasy. “Yes, my dark Prince. I am yours to mold, to take, and to...” Her voice trailed off in a groan of numbing pleasure. Trapper felt cold, sharp steel against his cheek. He knew the sensation. As the blade bit his flesh, Deimos’ voice echoed from the marble walls, filling his senses to the exclusion of all else. “Cry Havoc...!” End.
