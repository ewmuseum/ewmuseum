"Reflections of Beam and ME!"

7 Mar 2003

By Jimmi Zion

Item 6 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 7 Mar 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
*[Fade In] *[Camera cuts to the hallway of the hospital where we see WWA X-Treme and Cruiserweight champion, "Sky Flyin" Jimmi Zion walking down the hallway toward his wife's hospital room. He is still wearing his wrestling gear from the match the other night on Fiesta, but he is carrying the black duffle bag that Mikalia's father, Maximillion Rush brought in for him that he left at the arena earlier in the night. As he walks down the hallway, he seems like he is in deep thought reflecting on many things in retrospect. We hear an audio voice over of Jimmi Zion playing the background talking in a first person while he thinks back on everything with what he may of heard as of late.] *(Reflecting on James Beam, there is a TV, with VCR above it, as small black leather lounge chair and a very intent man sitting in said chair. He is ooking at the TV with a remote control in his hand. As the TV pans away from the TV, you see it is Jimmi Zion in a recent match.) James: "Awh... I've got it. This is a very interesting match Jimmi. You use the same move in this situation. In fact (beam looks at the screen) you seem to use alot of the same moves." V/O of Jimmi Zion: "That's just one of many many moves that I have. I have more than one. You think that I don't hit a move better than you. Trust me, I've seen a lot of your matches thus far since entering the WWA. I can say the same exact thing as you. There's just one difference between you and me, the fans say differently than what you think. I think that they would tell you that my move looks so much better. But don't take my word for it, just wait until this weekend when it will all fall into place and you will find out exactly just why I am the TRUE WWA X-Treme/Cruiserweight champion." *(James pauses the VCR and leans back in his chair, putting his hands behind his head.) James: I've been in this room, studying you Zion. Studying your tendancies, your weaknesses. (laughs) Trust me, my friend, there are MANY of them. But, this match is something I really look forward too. It will be my first for a few things.... a) It will be my first Extreme match. b) It will be my first Title match and Finally.... It will be my first title reign. (James smiles at his arrogance. Then dismisses it with a wave of his hand.) "You're right Beam, I have weaknesses, but then again who doesn't. I may have weaknesses, but ALL of my strengths outweigh the weaknesses. Just keep in mind, for every FEW weaknesses that you have found out in. You have twice as many. In fact, I can honestly say, based off of my own personal research on you, that you are ONE BIG GIANT WEAKNESS. Even with all of my weaknesses, I do not lean toward them. I focus on the strengths which I have much much more of. I am NOT weak. I am strong. YOU ARE THE WEAK! Smile at your dillusion of grandure all you want. Revel in your arrogance, in the long run it will be your undoing." *James: Seriously though... Jimmi, buddy... You and I are not that much different. Except for the fact that I am a rising star and that you are a fading light falling from the sky. I mean, eventually you and I will be at the same level as you fall right past me. I figure I will pass you on my ascent somewhere in the entrances and introductions Saturday night. I mean you and I use the same move to finish our opponents. Something has to be said for that... right? Granted you do it wrong... But I think that me being superiour to you has been established. (James smiles at his jest only to get more serious.) "You are SO way off. You couldn't be any more way off James. You are a rising star. I will give you that much credit. You are the man next in line to TRY and take my title. I'll give you that. I will even give you the benefit of the doubt that you COULD defeat me this weekend. The thing is where you are wrong is that my so-called light WAS fading and falling from the sky. That was up until I proved everybody wrong at Rage In A Cage 6. That is when I defied all of the doubters and proved that I am still the champion that I once was. I can still tear the house down, and I did exactly that. My light isn't fading. Contrary to what you believe, it is shining brighter than ever before. Even greater than the first time. So don't tell me that I am fading. This weekend, I will show that I am still the champion and living out in my immortality while you will fall like the star to the earth. I hate to do it to you, but in this business it's all about survival of the fittest, and I will survive and move on unscathed to the next pissant who thinks they can take my title. It won't be you." "Contrary to what you believe, we may have the same finisher, but with a different name, but I have other ways to defeat you than just that one move. I will be remember not for one single pretty finisher move, but for an entire plathoria of moves. When describing me in the history books, the next generation will not remember me by one single highlights, but through any of them that nobody will not even know where to count." *James: So... Zion, this Saturday... Me and You... Man to Mouse, respectively of course. (laughs lightening up) So, I've gotta wonder how your wife and my kid are doing? I hope she is feeling better, as she needs to be ready for my big party Saturday night. I am sure you won't mind is she is in attendance, heh I know she won't. Make sure she wears that cute little number with the flowers on it. I love that outfit. (James gets up from his chair and stretches. Then looks back to the camera.) James: I will probably chat with you again before Saturday Zion. But in case I have more important matters, like washing my @$$, I will see you Saturday. (Fade) "Man to mouse? You only wish. Trust me, after this Saturday, you will know the truth that I am far from a mere mouse." "As for my wife and unborn child. You can find out for yourself Beam because I'm about to go see them for myself. I'll give them my regards for you, like it will mean anything. Yes it's a bloody shame that Mikalia won't be at ringside to cheer me on, but hey she will be safe back in London taking care of my child on the way. It's for the best. As for me, I think that I will wrestle my best because I won't have to worry about her saftey, I will be able to concentrate solely on you in that match. That means I will fight you with clear mind and a clear focus. What does that mean to you James? It means you don't stand a chance in holy hell of beating me in the center of the ring this Saturday on Fiesta. You don't stand a chance at taking my titles. Most of all...*(Pause)*...What the hell are you talking about? Little outfit with the flowers on it? What have you been taking? That makes no sense you dumb arss. Little outfit with the flowers on it? Mikalia only wears business suits or evening gowns. She certainly isn't going to be wearing any dresses with flowers on them. [He pauses for a moment, and then comes to a quick realization.] "Unless if she wears something like that as some sort of maternity dress...AROUND THE BLOODY HOUSE, because she wouldn't be caught dead wearing anything like that in public. Oh never mind, here I am at her hospital room door. Well here I am signing off." [Zion approaches the hospital room door where his wife is staying. He knocks on the door and then slowly walks in. Inside we see his beautiful wife, Mikalia Rush-Zion laying down on her back peacefully sleeping like a little angel. She has on a hospital evening gown, and one hand placed on her stomach. The television is playing softly above her here showing some kind of overnight programing like Jerry Springer or something. Zion walks over next to her. Picks up the remote and turns off the tv, and then gives her a kiss on the lips and then sits down on the bed with her. Mikalia awakens and stretches to see her husband starring back at her.] Zion: "How are you feeling love?" Mikalia: "Better now that you're here." Zion: "You're father is outside in the waiting room, he can't wait to see you." Mikalia: "Daddy, oh no, he saw what happened, now mom and him are worried about me and the baby." Zion: "We all are. Max and I felt that it is best that after you are dismissed from the hospital that you return back to England with him." Mikalia: "But what about you?" Zion: "I'm more concerned about you and the baby. The doctor said we got lucky tonight, but what about if something happens the next night and I can't protect you. I don't think I could forgive myself if something were to happen to you or the baby. I think that this is the best. You need to go back, for the three of us." Mikalia: "I know you're right, but I have been by your side since the beginning, I don't think that I can leave it now. How will you get by without me." Zion: "I'll miss you, but I know that I will manage. At least with you in England with Max, I can focus on keeping my title and still take care of the Cell, Jason Williams, Chance, and for right now James Beam. I have more than a full plate right now, and it would make matters worse if I have to protect you as well." Mikalia: "But Jimmi..." Zion: "No buts please. They'll only make this harder than it is. I dont' want to do this any more than you do, but it has to be done. Max and I talked it over and your going back to England, that's an order. Do it for me, and do it for the baby." Mikalia: "Okay I will. I'll be cheering for you." Zion: "I'll be listening." Mikalia: "I'll be back before you know it." Zion: "I'll be waiting. Now I'm sure that Max will want to talk to you to, and then after that you finish getting your sleep. I think you will be able to leave first thing in the morning." Mikalia: "What about you Jimmi?" Zion: "I've got a plane to catch. I have to be in Atlanta, Georgia ready to fight James Beam with my titles on the line." Mikalia: "Then I need to be with you." Zion: "No you need to be here and then with your father on the Concord back to London." Mikalia: "Okay." Zion: "I'll call you every chance I get and I will keep in touch, and whenever chance I get I will come in and see you, but I gotta go, I have a plane to catch and a man to defeat...if I can still call him that after Saturday." Mikalia: "What brought that on?" Zion: "Oh nothing. I'll call you. I gotta get going. I love you." Mikalia: "I love you to Jimmi." [Zion gives Mikalia a kiss and then a hug, and then one more kiss before picking up his bags and starting to walk out of the door. Before he leaves, he takes one final look at her before walking out waving bye to her. Zion walks out to find his father-in-law and his two bodyguards standing outside waiting for him.] Maximillion Rush: "Well did you tell her everything?" Zion: "Yeah I did." Maximillion: "Excellent. Now in case if you forgot, this is Bruno, he will escort you back to the airport. I already arranged a flight for you, first class, so you will beable to arrive in Atlanta on time for Fiesta. You will not miss your match in the least. Now go out there and shine. This is your time and you don't need Mikalia by your side to do it. Go and show them why you are the champion." Zion: "I will Max." Rush: "Of course you will. Bruno, take him to the airport and make sure he doesn't miss his flight." [One of the big bodyguards nods as he walks up to Zion and then the two of them walk down the hallway toward the exit of the hospital room. Maximillion and the other bodygaurd walk into the hospital room. The camera fades out.] *[Fade Out]*
