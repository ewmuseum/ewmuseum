Musical Taste

6 Mar 2003

By Grunge

Item 4 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 6 Mar 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
FADE IN (Grunge is listening to music in his black room.) Jimmy Stewart follows Kim to where your portrait hangs on the wall You're such a haunting vision; he forgets his partner's fall. Jump into the San Francisco Bay... I'll follow you in; I know you can't swim when you've been dead 100 years...Carlotta (He hits stop on the CD player and addresses the camera.) Grunge-Harvey Danger. Fun guy even though he looks like Drew Carey. This week I'm really digging Karma's Payment by Modest Mouse. I've been listening to Modest Mouse more lately. For a couple of months last year I went though a cycle of only listening to them, this followed similar cycles of Radiohead and Soul Coughing. (Grunge opens the CD player and begins to switch CDs.) Grunge-Personally, Lonesome, Crowded West and The Moon and Antarctica are two of the finest albums I've ever purchased. There are so many great and beautiful songs on those two discs. At least I'll have some good music running through my head as I waste time with somebody like Joey K. (Grunge looks at a Stevie Ray Vaughn album, thinking about putting that one in, but puts it back in its case instead.) Grunge-I caught your little interview. Anger management problems their Joey? I told you to stay out of my way. I thought I should just prove it. I’m going to warn you one more time, stay in the back Monday. Don’t come down to that ring cause if I don’t break that idiot skull of yours I’m sure Venny would be happy to. You must have had mental breakdowns when you were younger, where one little thing would trigger this onslaught of screaming and crying and rolling on the floor curled up in a ball and your parents would just yell at you for being a little brat. (Grunge finally settles on an Eminem CD.) Grunge-But I’m not concerned about you. I’m more concerned about your former partner Venny LeTrav. He’s solid albeit a little nuts. But still he’s not the one I want to pin. (He puts it in and hits play but turns the volume down low.) Grunge-Hey Nick. Do you like Primus? Would you like me to stick nine inch nails through my eyelids? Don’t think I haven’t forgot about you. Don’t think Monday I’m not going to take what matters most to you. I’m going to take the TV Title and you know why? (Grunge pulls in right into the camera and almost whispers.) Grunge-Just because I can. (Grunge pulls out from the camera and turns the volume up a bit.) Grunge-See the three of you Monday, especially you Nicky. The music is fading out. FADE OUT
