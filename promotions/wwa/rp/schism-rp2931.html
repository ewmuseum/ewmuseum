Schism

1 Feb 2006

By Tracer Bullet

Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I didn't want it to have to come to this. But then again, I didn't want to stick around and keep wrestling, either. Nikodemous's development is carrying on at a fantastic clip, and I don't want to miss a moment of it. So I'm going to be fighting my friend... for my son. Someone shouldn't have to make that kind of decision. Though to be honest, it wasn't even my decision. Gordon is the one who suggested that stipulation for our match, which proves one thing if nothing else... he pays attention. With all of the complaining I'd been doing on the road, it's no wonder that it would begin to grate on him after awhile, but I am not one to suffer in silence. As I've pointed out already, that's a good way to end up with some severe emotional problems. I'm just hoping this match doesn't go too far. There's an awful lot riding on the outcome... 13 things, to be exact... and it would be far too easy to get caught up in it. I don't want to see either of us get hurt, because I'd never be able to forgive myself if I could have prevented the whole thing from ever happening. Of course, the only reason I agreed to have this match in the first place was the concept of it being my last one. The other stipulations... well, they just kept coming. There's only the one truly important one, and it's what's going to keep me fighting Monday night. To be honest, though, I can't think of anyone I'd rather have my last match with than Denrol. We've had some classics in the past, and I doubt that this is going to be any different, even with all the emotional ties involved. At the end of the day, though, I just hope we'll still be able to be friends. I seem to have really gotten on his last nerve. I could very easily claim that this was all a carefully calculated plot to arrive at this end, but I'm no evil genius... ... one only need look at the ninja angle to see that. All in all, I'm looking to put a final seal on this career of mine. I'm looking to make this my last match. And this time, you all get a warning.
