When does the mind snap IV

22 May 2003

By Wes Sanders

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The doctor quickly runs into the small prison like room from where the commotion was bellowing out. A Nurse stood at the edge of the doorway holding her head in her hands. The doctor approached her with Wallace Coleman running behind.) "What happened here" (The Doctor spoke with haste and urgency as the Nurse looked up at the Doctor, she gave off a faint smile and hugged him, clinging on with her hands grasping his back.) "I... I... I was just delivering his fan mail when... when..." (Her voice broken by laughter coming from within the room, she quickly releases her grip on the Doctor and holds her head in her hands once again. Wallace Coleman tries to look into the room, but the doctor holds him back.) "No! It is not the right time, we will have to plan this carefully" (Wallace looked confused but knew that the Doctors words were correct, he nodded in a nonculant tone as the laughter in room stopped.) "What's happened?" (Wallace spoke as quiet as a mouse as the Doctor edged ever closer to the door, he peered round to see Wes sitting crossed legged reading a piece of over and over again. He has one hand over his mouth like a little girl sniggering.) "Wesley are you ok" (The Doctor tried to share a concerned tone of voice with Wes, Wes just looked up at the Doctor and smiled.) "They are taking me back..." (Wes' tone was soft, gentle yet somehow very spooky he looked down at the paper, and then back up at the doctor once again.) "Did you hear me Doc, they are taking me back... The people who put me here in the first place are taking me back... Can you believe it?" (The Doctor is about to speak but Wes interrupts.) "I mean I couldn't believe it either, Matthew Warr, Ian Tense, Tracer Bullet and Jackson Kraven. All of them I will see again. And do you know what I'm going to do to them once I see them again?" (The Doctor shakes his head. And Wes laughs rocking back and forth on the bed.) "They put me here Doc, they put me here! DO YOU HEAR ME!" (The Doctor nodded in a "Yes" fashion and has a concerned look on his face.) "I have someone to see you Wes" (The Doctor motions his hand trying to bring Wallace in, Wes looks at the door tilting his head in anticipation.) "Hello Wallace, have you had fun watching me? Did the Doctor take you on the grand tour. This place is brilliant don't you think?" (Wallace steps into the room, covered from wall to wall are pictures of WWA superstars torn in half. A pile of fan mail ash lay still in the corner. As Wes locks eyes with Wallace, with a stare that could burn any man's eyes.) "Wes" Wallace nods. "Wallace" Wes too nods (Wallace takes a seat next to Wes' bed and sits down. Wes holds out the piece of paper and Wallace begins to read it.) "It seems the WWA want you back then Wes, that's got to be a good thing don't you think" "You don't think Wallace, you just get told to do, same as myself, but not anyone I am going to think, I am going to control my destiny in the WWA, no more will I be a puppet for Matthew Warr. No longer will I be a punch bag for Jackson Kraven and Ian... Ia.." (Wes' voice trailed off and he begins to laugh again. Rocking back and forth Wes falls back on the bed holding his hands up high in the air.) "Once I see Ian again, I'm going to shake his hand for holding onto the belt for such a long time and then.... SQUASH!" (Wes claps his hands together, Wallace looks up at the ceiling to see a half torn Ian Tense face. Wes had obviously been looking at that.) "But it's not going to stop there Wallace, oh no, Ian Tense is just the first of many on my list. You want to see someone make an entrance in the WWA. You want to see THE entrance in the WWA" (Wes pauses and chuckles) "I will battle through anyone to achieve my goal, to achieve my status as legend in the WWA, you think anyone can stop me? Do you?" (Wallace is about to speak but is interrupted) "For too long I have been held back, for too long I have been another cog in the WWA machine, its time to be my own machine, it's time to be "The Rage" Wes Sanders" (Wes snatches the piece of paper from Wallace and clenches it to his chest as he sits back up and rocks back and forth once again.) "Now get out of here I don't want to see you again until after the Revolution" "Wes, one question... Will you be at Revolution?" (Wes stops rocking back and forth and thinks for a moment.) "We will have to see about that, depends on whether I think that is a good idea." (Wallace gets off of his chair and looks around the room one further time. He has a very concerned look on his face as he walks out of the room and down the white plain hallways. His footsteps clip clop down the hall as the Doctor catches up with Wallace. And leads him to the main door.) "That's not Wes Sanders, what the hell have you done to him in here?" (The Doctor places a hand on Wallace's shoulder.) "We haven't done anything here, he is doing it to himself, I recommend that your company keeps him here longer. For further analysis" "You can't keep him here locked like a caged Animal!!" (The Doctor removed his hand away from Wallace's shoulder.) "Well I'm sorry you feel that way but you unleashing hell onto your company if you take him back on. We have methods to cure him you know." (Wallace starts to walk out the front door, he doesn't look back but he speaks in a serious stern tone of voice) "You've taken away a good human being there and replaced him with a monster. He came here in good faith for you to cure him of his thoughts and you've just let them develop. I hope you happy with yourself" (Wallace walks out of the building as the gentle pitter patter of rain flicks down onto his head. Wallace walks down as the camera slowly pans away from him it stops on a sign) "SALT LAKE CITY, MENTAL INSTITUTE" (It holds on that sign for a small amount of time slowly fades to black.)
