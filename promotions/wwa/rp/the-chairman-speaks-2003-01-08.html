The Chairman Speaks

8 Jan 2003

By Mal Somers

Item 4 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 8 Jan 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Scene - a flickering television set in a darkened room. The screen flickers and distorts as the camera zooms in. As the image fills the screen, the rate of flickering increases, then suddenly stops and the screen is black.) You four haven't learned have you? (Images from several weeks ago when Leviathan debuted flash across the screen, finishing with him lifting Mal Somers up and dropping him with the Hellenic Slam) Leviathan. You keep trying to keep me down but I just keep getting back up. Each time you hurt me I get stronger. When will you ever learn? (Images of Mal Somers' former P.A., Miss Penny Wise flash across the screen, distracting Mal Somers in his matches, telling him that he has been "demoted".) Miss Wise. You are now nothing but a memory to me. Im sure you will keep the members of "THE CELL" weel entertained, you piece of jailbait! When will you ever learn? (Images from last weeks Fiesta of Lockdown powerbombing Mal Somers and locking him in the Prison Walls) Lockdown. You sick freak. This used to be between Leviathan, Miss Wise and I. Now you've stuck your brown nose into my business. When will you ever learn? (Images of Commissioner Cruz flash across the screen, from his original scuffle with Somers backstage several weeks ago on Fiesta to him instructing THE CELL to inflict damage on Somers and Harley.) You stupid Gringo. You couldnt take me on one on one so you had to send for help. You've made a BIG mistake. When will you ever learn? (Images of Somers laying in hospital, being bandaged up, having stitches, walking on crutches are shown. The final image is of Somers being picked up from Hospital by his partner, Dave Harley.) You've all tried to put me out before and failed. You've tried again and I am again on the road to recovery. Will any of you ever learn? I DONT THINK SO. When the time's right, the EXTREME CORPORATION is going to teach all of you a lesson you will never forget. (Images from the recent Insider of Leviathan burning Somers' prized chair in the fire. The camera zooms into the flames until the screen turns white.) So, Leviathan... you've KILLED the Chairman? You've BURNED his chair? Big deal. Mal Somers is still alive...and very soon he'll be kicking. See the problem is...you've chosen to mess with the two most EXTREME wrestlers in the WWA. We were here first. And now you are here, well...you are now nothing but a very small FISH. In a very large pond. And while Miss Wise may be gone...the CHAIR may be gone, and the CHAIRMAN may be gone...there is one thing that I have...one thing that you will never be able to get your slimy hands on. And thats the WWA ROOKIE TITLE. I dont plan on losing that any time soon. You see, in ten years time you will probably be still TRYING to put the EXTREME CORP. out of business, and FAILING. And all the while I will be a WWA veteran, still defending MY ROOKIE BELT. Funny isn't it? So all you CELL members, I want to make one thing abundantly clear. There is an old daying that says that "The thrill is in the chase." Well you boys are in for the thrill of a LIFETIME because thats all you will be doing, and that is..chasing MY title and chasing the EXTREME CORP. And you better get used to it! (Fade Out)
