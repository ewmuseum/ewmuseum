Some real help!!

22 Jan 2003

By Shaman

Item 5 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 22 Jan 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
** A Doctor walks into an offcie sits down as he looks at the next patients chart and looks on in abject fear as he places the chart down and reaches into a cabinet and pulls out some demerol and places it down on the table and then turns back into the cabinet and pulls out some tylox as he turns and sits back down again. Shaman walks into the room looking at the medication and looks at the doctor.** [Shaman] " Is that for me doctor??" [Doctor] " No its for me im going have a headache when i through talking with you!!" [Shaman] " I get that alot lately!!!" [Doctor] " No doubts here! " ** Shaman looks at the Doctor and looks serious as he thinks and begins speaking.** [Shaman] " Well since the last time you had seen me i haven't had any major injuries or well just plain simply no risks. I know that im about to embark on a four way match in a cage soon!! Just thought to warn you and give you a heads up!! All though i have been feeling a bit different these last few weeks. Not looking at things the way they should be looked at. I care differently about stuff as well. Stuff that used to be important to me doesn't seem as important as it used to be. Am i losing it or is it change that has come faster than i expected Doc??" [Doctor] " The change is normal and in your case extremely good. Even though i do not believe a four way in a cage is healthy i think its healthy for you so you can vent and relieve all that pent up anger!!" ** Shaman looks at the Doctor who doesn't normally say such things begins to speak again.** [Shaman] " So im perfectly normal and ok?? If this is true then why the hell am i paying you money to give me a check up!! Ive given you enough money in these past few years to afford a military for a small country!! Damn it man can you not see that you have pissed me off!! I oughtta bust you in the head right here for making me pay for this. Yeah i have another question where the hell does therapy come undder my bill. I never took therapy and why was i billed for it??" [Doctor] " That was for me!! After every visit i believe i need therapy and im sure as hell not going to pay for it!! You on the other hand can afford it without effort!!" [Shaman] " I am paying for your therapy?? Do you not know how F#^%$@%^ dangerous it is to be taking money from my pocket??" [Doctor] " Wasn't the only thing i have billed you for!! I billed you for alcohol rehab i needed after you met with me and i needed recovery time!! I charged you lost wages and nurses pay for their therapy as well!!" [Shaman] " You know you offer a service i pay for it!! Now im offering you a service listen real good doctor!!" [Doctor] " Yeah what is it leaving my staff alone and me as well! So i do not have to see you again!! You made me rish beyond belief!!" ** Shaman reaches over and grabs a roll of medical tape and smiles as he looks at the doctor.** [Shaman] " Im going to let you live today on the conditions that all the money you stole from me returns to my account within 24 hours!! Im going to leave you with a small reminder are you listening!!" [Doctor] " You ain't getting jack pal!!" ** Shaman stretches forward and clocks the doctor knocking him senseless and begins tying the doctor up in the medical tape and gags the doctor as he turns and reaches for the needles.** [Shaman] "You had your chance Doctor so im going to give you a little taste of whats to come because you have messeed with me!! This will be the one chance you have to comply got it!!" ** Shaman grabs one of the fingers of the doctor and jams the needle up under the finger nail and grins as the doctor cries while being muffles as Shaman grins sadistically and then grabs the bottle of alcohol as he tears the finger nail upwards revealing the meaty underside and pours alcohol onto it directly and then grins and pushes the fingernail back down taping it back onto the finger as the Doctor looks on in horror as his eyes have gotten blood shot and his face beat red as blood pours from his finger from under the taped finger nail. Shaman smailes and turns to the cabinet and places all the itmes away as he turns to the Doctor and speaks.** [Shaman] " We have come to a conclusion i believe!! Now if you'll excuse me i must address the three other Combatants for the Four way TV Title dance!!" ** Shaman walks out of the Doctors room and makes his way down the corridor to the door and outside he heads. Stopping near his truck.** [Shaman] " Chrystian Payne, Nicholas Adams, and Grunge two of you i care very little about in this match up simply because i want to hurt Chrystian Payne and all in all your just more meat to the slaughter!! Get in my way and trust me you will hurt in more ways imagineable. See what happened to the Doctor in there. Heed that as a warning. Im not the same man who entered Fiesta last weekend. No im a man bent on hurting someone and the first people come at Rage in the Cage!! No this time i am going to do what i need to do!! If a thank you is needed for the warning than you are most welcome!!" ** Shaman opens the door and then stops as he turns and looks at the camera again.** [Shaman] " Leviathan and Lockdown you have pissed off the wrong guy this time!! You will get yours i promises this!! No you think i will walk away silently!! Nope you got it wrong!! Day after day, Night after night you will begin to get paranoid wondering where Shaman will exact his revenge upon you!! No little men of Cruz do not fear me. Just simply know that i am coming for you and will exact my revenge ten fold upon you. Your lives are now forfeit!! I will make sure you regret the decision to join the WWA!!" ** Shaman gets into his truck and shuits the door and speeds away down the street as the camera fades to black as these words appear on the screen.** GOT BLOOD!!!
