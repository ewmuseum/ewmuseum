7-11

16 Jul 2003

By Grunge

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
FADE IN (We seem to be traveling in a car, watching the cars and light changes at super speed. It is as if the entire scene is being shot from the car’s point of view. Over the scene is an lyric-less techno song. It doesn’t matter which, whatever is popular with the club rats at the time. Rather quickly the car pulls into a parking lot and as soon as it stops the camera switches gears to the inside were we see Grunge sitting at the wheel and Julie in the passenger side. She has a magazine out, folded, and a pen.) Grunge: I never have been able to figure out why you like this crap. Techno is the ugly step-child of the music world. No soul in there. (Grunge reaches over and turns off the radio as Julie doodles, blissfully unaware. Grunge looks around to see that they are in the parking lot of a 7-11 Quickstop. He then turns back to Julie.) Julie: So which do you prefer, the beach or the mountains? Grunge: Beach. (Julie marks it down as Grunge turns around to face her. For the rest of the dialogue we switch back from one to the other depending on whose talking.) Julie: Would you consider yourself extremely rational or extremely generous? Grunge: Extremely generous. (Julie gives Grunge a looks as if to say “yeah right.” Grunge shrugs his shoulders back defensively.) Grunge: Well I’m hardly rational. (Julie shrugs and checks some boxes.) Julie: Well that’s an extremely kind for the both of us. So when you go to a park do you see the forest or the trees? Grunge: Not all parks have forests. Julie: Well for the sake of this quiz they do. Grunge: Well then how many trees? Julie: I don’t know why? Grunge: Well because there’s this Chinese symbol and it’s a little tree and it stands for tree but when you have more than one tree it stands for forest. So by that rational two trees would be a forest. (Julie sighs.) Julie: So put you down for forest huh? Grunge: No the trees. (Grunge grabs the door handle and begins to walk out. He turns and leans back inside the car and kisses his wife’s forehead.) Grunge: Don’t put me down for trees. (Grunge then walks inside the store where we see the night-clerk watching a recap show of the WWA product. What’s being shown is the last few minutes of the Independence Day Rumble. The store clerk, who looks a lot like the Inn Keeper in the movie Identity, cringes as Grunge gets tossed out of the screen.) Clerk: That looks like that hurt. (It’s obvious now that the clerk speaks with a strong southern drawl. Grunge just can’t resist and after picking up a toothpick walks over the clerk and leans in right behind him.) Grunge: Yeah it kinda did. By the way do you have any canned whip cream? (The man turns around startled and quickly realizes who it is.) Clerk: You’re Grunge. Grunge: So that’s a no on the whipped cream? Clerk: I love your matches. You sure did good in the rumble. Too bad you came in second. (Grunge smiles and decides to humor the nice guy.) Grunge: Well Nick deserves it. Besides the United States Title gets me a World Title shot sometime down the line, well at least according to Tracer Bullet. Anyway I’ve got other things on my plate right now. Clerk: You mean that Leviathan guy and the Devil’s Own? They’re pretty dangerous. I mean just what they did to Jimmi Zion and Mal Somers. Grunge: I’m aware of that. The Devil’s Own is just a giant snake and what do you do to kill a snake? Clerk: Cut it’s head off with a shovel? (Grunge chuckles.) Grunge: Well I was just going to say sever the head which right now is old Levi. And on Monday he will pay for what he did to Dusk. But away since no whipped cream I guess I’ll just get going. (Grunge is almost out the door when the guy calls out.) Clerk: Can you say it? (Grunge smiles.) Grunge: Rock and roll. (Grunge heads out and walks to the door of the car and enters. He starts up the car and Julie looks over.) Julie: No whipped cream? Grunge: Nope. (Julie just shrugs her shoulders as Grunge looks back and begins to back out. Julie goes back to her work.) Julie: So when set next to a guy on a plane do you prefer to read a book or strike up a conversation? (At as Grunge backs the car out and leaves the parking lot we begin to…) FADE OUT
