The Evolution for Revolution 1 (The rules)

24 May 2003

By Leviathan

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
**Leviathan sits at his large desk. In front of him is an envelope and what appears to be a contract. He leans back and studies the contract** Leviathan: Lucky Lockdown decided to take a rode. Now to finish the King of the Hill rules, Mr Warr thinks he can save X-corp from us. Matthew that is not possible. Tommy Delucci is lucky we had a job to do. Show Havoc they are not immortal. Not Invincible. **Leviathan starts to flick through the document.** Leviathan: Now they have a force that thrives on Chaos against them. A force, more destructive than they can imagine. Now Mr Warr ask the Cell to lay down the rules for the King of the Hill match at the Revolution PPV. This is quite ironic, considering the Cell does not follow any. Thus the rules will mean little in this match and the cards should fall where we want them. **Leviathan places the document on the table.** Leviathan: X-corp, my personal friends Malcolm and David. David has a known back problem, curtesy of the Cell. Malcolm has questions on when David will decide the limelight is needed. Thus sending Malcolm to the Johnny Carnage day care centre. Throw in Chance, who believes in his cause. Protecting Malcolm from David, how very droll. X-corp knows the only reason they have those belts is because of the complete inadequacy of Commissioner Raine, who commissioned the Cell at March Madness. Borrowed time gentlemen, is what you have. The Cell are the true tag team champions of the WWA and at Revolution, we get our belts back. **Leviathan sits upright and pours himself a brandy.** Leviathan: Ahhh, delicious as was the trashing of Tommy DeLucci. Can the brothers be ready in time or is fear going to take them over. Not of the godlings but a repeat dose of chaos from the Cell. Pretend you are ready, pretend you have what it takes but the Cell knows the truth. It is amicable you enter with all good intentions, but stupid to continue. **Leviathan stands and walks towards a large open window and stares skywards.** Leviathan: Now the Fear and Panic, the godlings. Or are you the moons that surround your father. Maybe even the pet crows of Hino Rei, Sailor Mars. It really doesn’t matter. Can you travel to the chaos, can you fight the darkness. The place maybe even you two fear, Hades, pass Cerebus. That is what is needed at Revolution. If it means that at Revolution that Havoc will need to join their ailing father for the Cell to be victorious then that is what we will do. A Herculean effort maybe but worth it as the Cell has no problem in being god killers. (fade to black)
