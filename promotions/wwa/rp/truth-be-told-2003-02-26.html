Truth Be Told

26 Feb 2003

By Ian

Item 4 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 26 Feb 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Do you remember? Of course you do. After all, it is what you’ve bragged about for so long. You beat Ian Tense. Good job. Really. My God, has time really shaded that memory that much? Have you forgotten what REALLY happened that night? Are you THAT punch-drunk? Let me help you remember. Just sit back and watch… (Damien bites the bullet and slides in. Ian immediately starts stomping away. Damien pulls the referee between him and Ian. Ian shoves the ref out of the way and starts the attack again. Jimmy: Ian had better be careful, that might earn him a DQ. Fat Tony: DO you think he cares? This isn't a title match. Jimmy: Ian has been here a while now, I think we all know how he feels about losing, title match or not. Ian grabs Damien by the pony tail, drags him to his feet, and Irish whips him into the ropes Jimmy: Back drop by the US champ! Fat Tony: Did you see the air he got on that? I think Thorne's foot hit the lighting. Jimmy: I doubt that, but it was impressive.) I systematically took you apart, Damien. Inch by inch. Piece by piece… (Ian watches as Thorne still writhes in pain from the earlier attack. Ian walks over to the prone Damien, steps on his face, then spins around. Thorne crawls to the ropes and tries to use them for support as he crawls to his feet. Ian kicks him in the back of the head, and then uses the ropes to choke Thorne. The referee starts the warning count, and Ian lets go at four. Ian looks at the ref, then starts choking Damien again. The ref starts the warning count again. Ian lets go at four again. Ian shoves the ref and semi-chases him around the ring.) And all you could do was sit there and take it like the little bitch you are… (Damien pulls himself to his feet. Ian comes in for the attack again, but Damien drops to his knees and gives Ian an uppercut to the groin. Ian doubles over, and Damien lays into him with a European uppercut that sends Ian stumbling backwards. Damien gets to his feet, grabs the microphone, and clobbers Ian in the head with it. Ian drops. Damien grabs Ian's feet and goes for the figure four, but Ian thumbs him in the eyes as he bent down to grab his feet. Ian slings him into the ropes and delivers a huge clothesline that sends Damein spinning in air before allowing him to crash to the canvas. Fat Tony: DID YOU SEE THAT! Thorne looked like a tornado spinning around like that!) But THIS. THIS is the match that you drag about. THIS is the victory that you so readily waste everyone’s time reminding them of… (Ian slaps Damien across the face, and then locks in a bearhug. Ian squeezes tightly for a minute, then tosses him over his head with a release German suplex. Ian taunts the crowd to an enormous eruption of boos. Damein works his way to his knees. Ian turns and sees Damien making a "come on" motion. Ian walks over, pulls him up by the hair again and drops him with a butterfly suplex. The crowd boos.) Brag away, you little mother *beep*ing piece of sh*beep*it. Brag away… (Ian pulls his way to his feet and props himself up in the corner. Damien charges, but Ian moves and Damien crashes head first into the exposed steel turnbuckle. Damien lay in the middle of the ring as Ian tries to walk off the pain of the figure four. Damien struggles to his feet, and Ian sneaks up behind him. Jimmy: Russian Leg sweep!) Because it is the ONLY thing you have to drag about… (Ian doesn't let go of the hold, rolls over, pulling both men to their feet, and delivers another Russian leg sweep. Ian makes his way to his feet and stands over Damien. Damien tries to use Ian to pull himself up, but gets a big piledriver for his troubles. As Damien crumbles to the mat, Ian grads his feet, and sinks in a Sharpshooter.) You are and ALWAYS will be one step behind me, Damien… (The ref looks up and see Ian holding the ropes and calls for the break. Ian releases the hold at four. Ian semi-struts around the ring. Damien works his way back to his knees, and makes another "come on" motion to Ian. Ian shakes his head, plants a boot to the side of Damien's head, and pulls him to his feet again. Ian puts Damien's jaw on top of his jaw and drops to the mat. Damien flies backwards) I’ve been ahead of you EVERY *beep*ing second, and you know it… (Ian walks over and rakes Damien in the eyes, pulls him to his feet again, sends him into the rope, and catches him with a pendulum back breaker. Ian pulls him to his feet again and starts to go for the Ian-Tensifier. Suddenly the crowd diverts their attention to the stage area. Jimmy: Walcott is here! Fat Tony: See! Walcott is here to give Thorne a beatdown too. This guy doesn't have too many friends. Jimmy: Walcott has no business being here. What are these two up to? Was everything that happened between them earlier another setup? Ian releases the hold and confusedly looks at Walcott.) And at night, even the proud Damien Thorne goes to bed knowing THIS is the ONLY reason he was ‘the better man’ that night… (Damien works his way to his feet as Ian still has his attention turned to Walcott.) Even the mighty House of Pain leader realizes that all his bragging, all his bitching, all his moaning mean nothing… (Damien starts sneaking up behind Ian as Walcott starts down the ramp. Ian applaudes Walcotts ascension to the ring, but as Walcott gets midway down the ramp, Damien grabs Ian from behind, and slings him into the ropes Jimmy: UNION JACKED!! UNION JACKED) You can work the mic better? You’re a better planner? You’re Sh*beep*it. You’ve ALWAYS been sh*beep*it. A distant second, not even in the running. You don’t believe me? You’re God-awful pride won’t allow you to swallow the truth? Well don’t take it from me that you’re a bitch, listen to yourself… (6/25/02 - DT: Wrong! Wrong, Wrong, Wrong! Ian Tense lost his concentration in that match, and I exploited the oppertunity he presented to me. I mean look at the state of my face Cameron!!! Ian whipped my arse, there is no doubt about it.) listen to yourself, Damien. You’re *beep*ing pathetic! (7/17/02 – DT: Remember my match with Ian Tense? That guy f**ked me up!) And more importantly than all that, remember how I used you as an insignificant little pawn… (Damian" Hello? Oh, it's you...yes, I'm, in for Monday") Did you tell Shane Dorian how I mind *beep*ed you into attacking him before our US title match? Probably not. You wouldn’t have the *beep*ing balls for something like that, now would you? (5/20/02 - From off shot, a familiar voice calls out Voice "What are you? The WWA's answer to Tyler Durdan? You would be the Brad Pitt character, right?" Damian is standing alone. The floor and walls are clean of blood, and the knife stands in front of him, half embedded into the flaking plaster. He looks at his hands, which are also clean, the looks towards the direction of the voice Damian "I was just er....just finishing up something" Voice "So I see. What did that wall ever do to you? Anyways, enough. Just calling by to pick you up as requested. By the way, thanks for that job the other night. Sweet, brother." Damian "I asked you to pick me up?" Voice "Oh right, that would be the Edward Norton you then? Enough Big D, lets just roll, ok?" Damian "Yeah sure" Damian heads towards the door. As the camera follows, we catch a glimpse of the back of the mystery speaker....shaven head, thick neck and broad shoulders. Damian follows him out and can be seen standing in the doorway, visably groaning at the car in which his charge is waiting) Let the truth be known… I’ve owned you since day *beep*ing one, Damien. (Fade)
