Fernando the Forgotten Fiestacon

22 Jul 2003

By Alex Knight

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Post-Meltdown: Backstage I am here… Alex Knight has arrived, i am latest and greatest addition to the Fiesta roster, i am what you heared only whispers about. The hot prospect maybe? Or perhaps maybe just the one who will insure that WWA continues to develop and enhance it’s reputation. Whatever you have or haven’t heard, it matters not, for i am here. No longer shall rumours dictact how i am percieved, for now my actions shall determine my stature. My performances will speak far louder, of my talents, than even i will. First i come for you… So i am dealt the sacrificial lamb. Matt Keith;the poor soul how has to be humlilated on his debut. But fear not Matt, you shall not be the last. You may feel a little low, beaten down and dejected for while, but it will pass. In time you will be a name amongst many, defeated at the hands of the new future, the new youth and the new hope. I do not look past you Matt, for i already am strides ahead. I shall not dismiss you because to me you do not exist. To me you are nothing. Just another nobody. …and then come the rest! “Hey, Tracer.” (Alex waves furiously as Tracer Bullet simply walks past him, not even acknowledging him, as he mumbles something about a Viper on his way back from the parking lot) “Huh,” Alex shakes his head in disgust, “Detect this [crotch grab] MOFO!” (As Alex releases crotch and spins round; he spots Dave Harley, with GenX title in tow) “Hey, Dave.” (This time Dave walks by, not even a flinch as Alex waves his hands frantically, trying to grab his attention) “Ugh.” (Before Alex can retort he spots Lockdown, newly crowned TV champion, on his way to his lockeroom. Alex rushes over to him, backtracking in his path, making sure he cannot go unnoticed) “Hey there. Can I just…” but to no avail. (Lockdown just side steps him like he wasn’t even there, continuing on his motherf**king way, leaving Alex frozen to the spot. Ignored by two and shunned by a third, the pride that he holds so-so dear has been hurt so-so bad.) . o 0(Could it be that WWA isn’t aware of that Alex Knight exists? Could Alex truly be a small fish dropped into an ocean? Has Alex finally jumped in at the deep end without a rubber ring? Could it be that Alex Knight has finally become just another name, amongst a melee of nobodies? Pssh. Not on your nelly!) “Sod this. It’s time Alex Knight got some well-earned attention…” (Alex pulls his cellphone from the back of his jeans pocket, flicks it open, makes a call and waits for ‘him’ to pickup…) *RING.RING* *RING.RING* *CLICK* “What’s happening Big Al? Super Al! Al my pal! Al---” Alex jumps-in. “Tim.” Alex sounds a little peeved as he interrupts. “It’s much easier to say ‘hello!’ or ‘hello?’ or if your feeling continental ‘hallo’. So please spare me the bloody infuriating Alisms.” (Tim Davies, number one addict, sounds a little taken back by Alex’s outburst.) “Bu-limey. What's the matter with you?” He replies. “Uh, sorry. Just had a bad start to life in WWA, that’s all.” Alex says, with a more softened tone. “Oh yeah. Why’s that then? Piles?” “NO!” “Haemorrhoids?!” Tim jokes. (Alex chuckles a little before replying.) “No. None of these idiots even realise who they are in the presence of. They have no idea that under the same banner as they work, a man with prestige of an immeasurable amount, also works. Just now, Tim, i tried to ask one my fellow WWAer’s directions to the cafe.” “And?” “Nothing. I was SHUNNED. Sure i could put it down to lack of exposure and down to *BANNED*’s overexposure but that would be to easy. Reasons and excuses are for people who want to deliberate, question why. You know me Tim, i’m one who wants to think how? How can i change things? How can i make it so that everyone who sees me, recognises me, gives me a nod and tells me which way the cafe is?” “…” “Tim.” “What?” “How can i change things?” “Oh.” Tim’s brain kicks into gear. “Sorry thought it was one of those rhetorical things. Umm, well you’ve got Matt Keith this Monday, that would be a start.” “People know less about Matt Keith than they do me. Dropping this unworthy graduate on his head faster than he can say ‘Divorce papers’, ain't gonna do me no good. I mean sure, it’ll make me feel better putting the coat tail rider in his place but it want do s**t for my PR. What I need are idea’s Tim…” “…” “Tim.” “Yeah…umm…right, ideas. What about if you change your name?” “To what?” Alex replies, with a distinct lack of enthusiasm. “I dunno, anything. Just something so that people can see your evolving, moving with the times. ‘Alex Knight’ says to me; ordinary, my neighbour---” Cut-off. “What?!” Alex interrupts. “Your neighbour is Sammy the laundrette cowboy. You know, YE-HA and waving around ladies pantyhose; trying to lasso a washer. Beside that, i am not changing my name. It’s my name for christ's sake. I’m not gonna start masquerading as ‘PUMA’ or ‘Fernando Loveeto”. That’s story book crap…” “Hmm. Fernando Loveeto.” Tim starts to contemplate. “I said no.” “What then?” Tim replies, fresh out of ideas. “Forget it then. Just get me an interview. It’s maybe as unoriginal as the rest of these mundane folks round here, but it’ll be a damn sight more effective than…” “FERNANDO LOVEETO!” Tim chuckles. “Shut up and get me an interview.” “Ok. Whatever you say Fer----” “Goodbye Tim.” “Laters.” *CLICK* (He flicks the phone back together, sliding it back into the pocket where it once rest. Making his way back to the parking lot, he spots a cleaning lady mopping up some blood from the concrete paving, all dressed up in her best-washing gear i.e scraggy clothes with a do-rag. Alex goes over to ask her directions.) “Excuse me mam.” He politely engages the lady. “WHAT?!” Comes the butch response. “I was wondering whether you could point me towards the cafe.” “There AIN’T no ca-FE.” Avoiding all eye contact with Alex. “Ha. Wadda you know.” He chuckles to himself, “Starbucks, the evil corporation, i guess it is then--Wait. Or do i mean Barcardi? Nah. I just love the taste of that tainted mocalocachino.” (As Alex makes his way to his car, this weird color fills the screen…i think it’s black. But it’s not filling, its more like its fading in, as we…) - Fade out
