A United States Champion

19 May 2006

By Jonathon Winters

Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
As I’m slammed into the brick wall by the monster with his massive arms firmly clamped around my waist, and the back of my head impacts upon the decidedly unforgiving surface, I already know it’s all over. My body and mind have begun to relinquish their grasp on the battle. I’ve fighting for so long now, I know this is the end. I can’t go on any longer. I’m still conscious as my spent body slumps to the floor painfully, but am given no respite by the unmerciful beast. A wise tactic to utilise under the circumstances. Not particular news for me. He hauls me up into the air with all his might and I think I black out for a few seconds, but I quickly wake to find my arms outstretched. You don’t have to be a rocket scientist to figure out precisely where this is heading. The pain of the impact as he hurls toward the ground is so intense that I don’t even remain conscious long enough to feel any of the consequent pain. I bolt upright in my bed, my face and my bed sheets soaked with an indescribable and definitely unnatural sweat. Once I realise that same damn nightmare again, I grit my teeth just hold back a primal scream of pure anger that could conceivably wake up the entire neighbourhood. Third time this week and I’m getting pretty damned sick of it now. Nobody has ever managed to under my skin during my time within the World Wrestling Alliance quite as much as Proteus has during the Bourbon Street Brawl. I think it’s a matter of resentment on my part. I never knew I could be so petty, but I resent him for winning the damned Bourbon Street Brawl main event so soon. I have been here for the better part of the year and he’s been here for five minutes. I have accomplished a lot during my time here, I’ll admit that much, but he’s already claimed number one contendership for the WWA World Heavyweight without having to experience half the hardship that I’ve been confronted with. Perhaps deep down that is a very small section of the problem, I doubt it. I’m not nearly that petty and other people’s careers mean very little to me, no matter what they’ve achieved in comparison to me. It’s much more than that. It’s me and my inability to take my game to the next level. I’d won twelve matches in a row before gaining my World Title shot and what did I do when I finally received it? I choked. I drew with Ember. I couldn’t follow through the ability I knew I possessed. When I think about it, the exact same thing happened at the Bourbon Street Brawl. I gave up when there still a battle to fight. My body and mind collapsed under the pressure long before I lost consciousness for good and Proteus was able to eliminate me. A true champion cannot do that. A true champion must fight for all he’s worth and never give up before it’s all over regardless of the costs. Looking back at my failure within the Bourbon Street Brawl main event, it’s pretty damned obvious that I am not quite ready to be the WWA World Champion yet. That may be the case, but it sure as hell doesn’t mean Proteus deserves the title shot any more than me. He just got lucky and he hadn’t been through as much or lasted as long during the competition than me. He can tell himself he was the biggest, the baddest and deserving of the title shot all he wants to, but that don’t change a thing. This coming Monday I’ll Proteus precisely what it means to be a true champion. A United States Champion. Designed By [name withheld] | © World Wrestling Alliance 1997 - 2005
