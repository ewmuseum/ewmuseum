To be a god

12 Feb 2003

By The Cell

Item 2 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 12 Feb 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(An opulent house, decorated by various Greek and Roman artifacts is shown. A doorbell ring is heard. Into the frame comes Miss Wise, dress in a power suit, unusually long for her. She opens the door to bring into view for the WWA audience, Lockdown.) Lockdown: Penny, How ya doin' ,you hot little kitten? MW: Thomas is in the Study, and it’s Miss Wise to you. Lockdown: I thought by now we were friends. MW: HMMPH! Lockdown: (whisper) No wonder Somers dropped you. (Miss Wise leads Lockdown to the study where “Leviathan” Thomas Connor is waiting. He is staring at a large picture of an ancient battle scene, where an ominous figure stands above the battlefield. The title is “God of War”.) Lockdown: Doing some research? (Leviathan seems lost in the picture and fails to answer Lockdown.) Lockdown: Yo, Levi is you here? Leviathan: I heard you, just finishing a thought. As for your question, I am fully aware of what the God of War can do. Lockdown: You really into this Greek bull$hit? Leviathan: History can display a lot of answers, if you know what you are looking for. Lesson’s learnt from the past protect the future. Lockdown: History? These are two loonies acting like Greek gods. I’ll give it to them by being monsters, but gods come on. Who are they kidding? Leviathan: What if they are gods? Are you a god killer? Lockdown: I will tell you, if I need to be I will be. Your freaking out about these guys, aren’t you? Leviathan: No I am evaluating them, trying to understand them. Lockdown: Sympathizing more like it. Leviathan: Sympathizing yes. I sense what they can do. Kindred of sorts, brothers in arms. The sheer destruction they are capable of. The pleasure in waylaying all before them but that is all. They have something I want, we want. That is what it is all about. The WWA Tag Team titles are ours for the taking but the battle is calling. Lockdown: Look, loved the pep talk but we won’t be alone. I just know, one of the Handicap Corporation will try something. Leviathan: Cruz has them under control. He has everything in place. Lockdown: I hope so, for his sake. You know I am starting to like that Mexican as well. And if we, Fiesta will be the home of the WWA Tag team titles, so the stakes are high. Leviathan: If? More like when. To be a GOD you have to beat a GOD. Lockdown: It’s time for the Cell to raise Havoc back home.
