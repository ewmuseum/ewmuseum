An Introduction to the Intellectual Badass

22 Feb 2003

By Guido

Item 7 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 22 Feb 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene opens to the backstage area of a WWA house show. Glancing into the main floor of the arena, it appears that the show ended hours ago -- the place is almost completely empty. The camera pans around the backstage area, then zooms in on Wallace Coleman, who has a worried expression on his face. He greets the camera man, and begins to speak with a hint of anxiety in his voice.) Coleman: Hi. I'm Wallace Coleman, and tonight, we're going to catch an interview with one of the newest WWA applicants, a man who calls himself Guido the Poet. Follow me. (Coleman walks further into the backstage area, the camera following closely behind, until he reaches a generic red door with the words "Dressing Room" labeled in white text near the top. Coleman exhales sharply and proceeds to enter the room.) (In the middle of the room, seated on a plain wooden bench, is a man who appears to be Guido himself. He is hunched over, writing on a small pad of paper, his tangled mess of greasy dark-brown hair hanging down over his face. The camera zooms in on his acid-washed, ghetto-psychedelic jeans, which are marred by numerous holes. The camera repositions itself, and Coleman approaches the man nervously.) Coleman: Hello, I'm Wallace Coleman, the main interviewer for the World Wrestling Alliance. Can I ask you a few questions? (Guido looks up nonchalantly and squints at Coleman, then nods "yes".) Coleman: Okay. So, Guido -- (Guido twitches and opens his eyes wide upon hearing his name.) Coleman: -- although you have based your career primarily in independent federations, you recently applied to the WWA. May I ask what motivated this decision? Guido: Dear Wally, how dare you underestimate the power of the Norse gods! I have always been in the WWA, as have you. My so-called "application" to the WWA is merely the return of Odin to Valhalla. Do you catch my drift, Wally? (Coleman furrows his eyebrows confusedly, as if to say "What the ****?) Coleman: Ah, yes, of course. Now, Guido, if accepted to the WWA, what do you plan to accomplish in your first few months? Guido: Nature is a beautiful thing. Coleman: Pardon? Guido: Actually, I plan to eventually capture the Cruiserweight title. Not right away, of course. First, I will defeat gravity. Coleman: Gravity? Guido: Unless I still love the planet. (Coleman, visibly perplexed by Guido's insanity, is beginning to sweat like a pig. A single drop of sweat runs down the arc of his nose and hangs off at the end, which catches Guido's attention.) Guido: Wally, do I frighten you? Does my intellectual badassry intimidate you, Wally? Well, it should. (turns to the camera) Guido: And you, superstars of the WWA, you should be intimidated as well. Never before have you dealt with a man as incredibly talented and as wildly unpredictable as I! I will be the rabbit in your sockhole, the pilgrim in your veins, the liquid flower-sword of your most hideous nightmares! (pause) Guido: I will end you. Yes, all of you, collectively. For years, I have strived for perfection in body, mind, and spirit, and sadly, the WWA wrestlers will be the first to witness and feel the results. And in the ring, gravity will be my ally, especially when I bogglify your brains with the Impetus DDT. Yes, yes, yes. When you hit the ground, the Impetus DDT will bring it all back to you. That all-too-familiar feeling in the pit of your stomach, rising, rising, rising up, choking your soul, your heart, and your will to survive. That feeling is -- (Guido looks over at Coleman expectantly.) Guido: What is it, Wally? Come on, Wally, tell me what it is? Coleman: I don't know! Guido: Damn it, Wally! You failed me again! The feeling is defeat, Wally! My opponents will feel the suffocating hold of defeat! (Coleman hunches over in a corner and starts to cry.) Guido: Yes, and time will skip a frame as you, the opponent, stares up the ceiling, wondering what in the hell you did to deserve the horrific wrath of Guido, the Poetic Vigilante, the Cerebral Juggernaut, the Laughing Prophet of Doom, the -- (Guido is interrupted by a series of bizarre snorting noises coming from the other corner of the room. The camera pans over to reveal a homeless man sitting on the floor, talking to himself.) Guido: Ah, yes. I almost forgot. This is Funk. The Destroyer. My manager, valet, and soulmate, all rolled into one schizophrenic mess. He's a recovering crackhead, that's why he twitches so damn much. I found him in the bathroom of some ratty singles bar in Detroit, scrawling a haiku poem on the wall in lipstick. Just having completed my Haiku Ph. D at the University of God, I knew true talent when I saw it. In fact, I have the haiku right here. (Guido pulls out a tattered piece of paper and begins to read from it.) weed, guns, and chitlins watch me funkify your jazz I, the Destroyer (Guido sheds a tear. What a sensitive guy.) Guido: Genius. Pure poetic beauty. I knew at that moment we were destined to be soulmates, and that my spirit would finally be complete. This is why I am ready for the WWA. I've got poetry, Funk, and an impulse to destroy. Who gives a s*** about anything else? (Scene fades out. Coleman, scarred for life, scrambles out of the room hurriedly. The camera fades to a black void of eternal nothingness, but a voice, apparently Guido's, emerges from the darkness.) "WWA -- I'm coming."
