Caged

11 Jan 2003

By Jackson Krave

Item 2 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 11 Jan 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(A cage stands in darkness, illuminated by only a single spotlight, a single red spotlight.) (That single hateful red eye stares down at the cage as slow-motion footage of great caged WWA battles play in the darkness around it- Thorne and Cross the most recent, fading slowly into Denrol and Bullet, fading into the EndGame match between Bullet, Nelson and Mantis, fading into the apocalyptic Extreme Title match between O'Hara and Mystique which cost both men the best days of their careers...) (Eventually the footage fades into darkness, and leaves us with that hellish red eye, staring down at the ring. We slowly approach the monstrous construction and stare at it, stare at the emptiness within it...) (No, that isn't right.) (The *almost* emptiness within it.) (Things lie within the cage.) (Some of them are recognisable from a distance.) (One is a kendo stick, wrapped in barbed wire.) (One is a shattered piece of broom handle, ragged strips of black masking tape hanging from it.) (One is a steel chair, broken in pieces.) (But the last... all it is is a piece of paper. we cannot read it, for we are at the wrong angle.) (At least, we *were* at the wrong angle. As we look at it, the camera slowly heads up over the wall of the cage and down the other side, so we are staring straight at it.) (The handwriting on the paper is cramped and jagged, but eminently legible. The words are eminently obvious, even in the strange eerie red light.) v KEVIN SORRY IT TOOK SO LONG I'M A LITTLE BUSY RIGHT NOW I'LL GET BACK TO YOU THAT'S A PROMISE KRAVEN (As we watch, the light turns from blazig red to freezing blue, and the blue ink the note was written in seems to vanish before our very eyes, until all the screen is a chilling shade of blue.) (A shade we recognise as we pull back and see that we have been focused on Kraven's iris.) (The Tomorrow Man grins sadistically out at us.) Don't worry, Wes. I haven't forgotten you. But like I said to "Kevin", I'll get back to you. (Kraven winks.) How does the main event of Meltdown suit you? (Kraven chuckles, and the darkness swallows him whole.)
