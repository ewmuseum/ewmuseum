Round 1!!

15 Jan 2003

By Shaman

Item 2 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 15 Jan 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
** Shaman stands in an empty ring staring at the ring around himself and then looks around once again looking towards the lights and then stands straight in the center of the ring and begins to speak as he spreads his arms outwards and motions around the ring with them.** [Shaman] " Nick Adams you knew eventually we would come down to meeting in this ring! This is where heroes and villains are made!! You and i are neither. I have never really been a villain nor a hero. Ive been myself. Yet once again we have the opportunity to handle our disgressions between ourselves out and on the table. Im sure you will give me one helluva match. I mean look at you!! You have no quit in your body we will see who wins this one. I have alot of respect for you Adams. Especially where you didn't attack me from behind. that right there earns alot of respect from me. I may respect you , But in the ring everything goes out the window come saturday on Fiesta! You and i will dance the dance Adams. We will beat the living hell out of ya and im sure it will be a match the crowd has never seen before!! What do i have planned oh i have a whole evening of festivities planned. First i invite you to meet me before Fiesta to discuss a few things!! Nothing concerning the match. just a way to make sure no undesireables come along and attempt to spoil our match where we just want to face one another!! ** Shaman stops turns and looks at the camera and grins sadistically as he speaks.** [Shaman] " Directly threatening me Lockdown shortens your life extremely. You are not the only man to ever grace the halls and cages of prison. No i have been there in my youth and guess what i hurt people who messed with me. You are nothing and even less in the eyes of myself. You wanna cross paths with me i will gaurantee you will get hurt in more ways than even your befuddled mind and concieve!! No Lockdown your claim to hurt and threaten has caused you to be forewarned. I will get to you when i deem it necessary as for now you are less than a knat an annoyance to ,yself and others!! You should be more concerned with whats going to happen to you in the near future. I do not play fairly. mess with me your dealing with your own funeral!!" ** Shaman turns and hops out of the ring walking to a chair and sits down in it reversing the chair so his arms rest upon the back support.** [Shaman] " Chrystian Payne you do not go unforgotten in this at all!! I face Adams this week as we both gun for you!! He wants to hurt you for stealing his title and i want to hurt you for betraying The Four Horsemen and causing us to depart and disolve!! You run at every oppoertunity you get. then you claim you have achieved great victorie over me. All in all you won by another mans mistake. Your pathetic skills wouldn't have defeated me in the ring and in the end i will hurt you and make sure you never see what it was like to walk away from Rage in the Cage!! Many men have been crippled there and you will be next. Your nightmare comes soon Payne! Very soon in the form of the one man you are going to fear for the rest of your natural life!!" ** Shaman gets up and looks over his shoulders as he walks away speaking.** [Shaman] " The arena will be teeming with thousands of screaming and rabid fans. All going for the one moment to see the two men battle it out in the main event. Nick Adams vs Shaman!! The rookie vs The Veteran!! It is a sight to behold new vs the old!! No Adams im sure we will fight it out to the ultimate end. The WWA's Fiesta will never be the same in everyones eyes. Sham,an and Nick Adams beat the living hell out of one another and set the stage for Rage in the Cage!! Im sure you and i both would like to make a visit to Mr. payne on Meltdown Monday. I'll tell ya what your my guest come meltdown what do you say to that. We'll sit ringside and watch Mr. Payne fight for his life. When its all said and done all three of us will stand in the ring and look at each other and see what the real measure of each of us is. Will Payne cower or will he hold his ground!!" ** Shaman stops and looks up at the unlit jumbotron as it lights up showing last weeks match against Mack Tenn.** [Shaman] " We made a good team Adams and i thank you for the opportunity to get some level of revenge against mack Tenn. Now its time to make sure nobody screws up on Saturday. Ive got your back top be sure you and i are fresh for our match. One wrong move by anyone and i will gaurantee nightmares for the rest of there damn natural lives!!" ** Shaman turns and walks away as the camera fades to black leaving these words.** GOT BLOOD!!
