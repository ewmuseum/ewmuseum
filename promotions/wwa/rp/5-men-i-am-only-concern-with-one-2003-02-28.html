5 Men? I am only concern with one

28 Feb 2003

By Enigma

Item 5 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 28 Feb 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
{Darkness} It appears that the match for Monday Night will go on. It appears that I will be involved in a little six man tag team match. What is in the word Franchise? What does it mean? {Enigma is shown} Denrol claims to be the WWA Franchise. Why? Because he has been around for five years? Time is the essence of becoming a Franchise? So because you have been along for what it seems like forever is the reason you are the Franchise? That boggles my mind Denrol. It seems like the criteria for becoming the Franchise is just time. Now Denrol, I don’t really have any quarrel with you. I mean you seem to have an udder distain for Chance, and I mean who doesn’t, he’s nothing more than an annoying little bug that flies around your head and just can’t seem to kill it. I hope you finally squash that bug. {Enigma smiles} As for Shaman and Jason Wainwright, I do not care about either of you two. You can destroy each other for all I care. Now, then there is Tracer Bullet. The only reason I am in this match is because of you Tracer. I don’t care about all this talk about Franchise Players, Youth or any of that crap. I only care about one thing and that is getting my hands on you. Tracer this is the opportunity I have been waiting for since Rage in the Cage. In the six man tag team match, I have no partners, I only have one opponent and that is you. The other four men are not a factor or a concern to me. Tracer, on Meltdown, I will take it to you. NOTHING CAN STOP ME NOW
