"Chump verses REAL CHAMP!"

20 Feb 2003

By Jimmi Zion

Item 3 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 20 Feb 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
*[Fade In] *[It's about early/mid afternoon. The camera cuts to the interior of a indoor parking lot garage that is part of a hotel. The camera shows WWA couple, "Sky Flyin" Jimmi Zion and his beautiful wife and manager, Mrs. Mikalia Rush-Zion. Zion is carrying a black duffle bag, and wearing a black with white trim full Addidias nylon windbreaker jumpsuit with Fube tennis shoes, and his long braided dreadlocks individually braided. Mikalia is wearing; black ladies slacks, black stilletto high heel pumps, and a white button up blouse with a black long leather coat over it. They walk together holding hands as they approach a dark navy blue 2003 Humvee. Zion unlocks the passenger car door and opens it up for his wife to get in.] Jimmi Zion: "You feeling a little bit better now love?" Mikalia: "Yeah I think I'm feeling a bit better Jimmi." Zion: "Great, watch your fingers babe." [Mikalia looks like she was about to say something, but Zion closes the car door. She pulls her hand back so he doesn't shut her fingers in the car door. Mikalia just waits patiently slightly massaging her stomach while she waits for Zion jogging around to the drivers side and opens up. Throwing his duffle bag in the back, he slides into the seat behind him shutting the car up behind him. He Starts the car up and starts to drive off from the parking spot. The couple begins talking while Zion is driving.] Zion: "Oh yeah what were you gonna ask me before?" Mikalia: "Oh yeah I was wondering if we could stop off at the doctor's office, I made an appointment to get checked out." Zion: "You sure nothing's wrong baby?" Mikalia: "Oh yeah nothing's wrong. I just want to make sure it's not just some flu that I got. I'm sure it's nothing." Zion: "Yeah what time is your appointment?" Mikalia: "I scheduled it for 3:00 this afternoon." Zion: "Yeah I'll getcha there baby. Are you sure your alright?" Mikalia: "I'm fine Jimmi. Oh I was thinking if maybe I could talk to you later on sometime. It's kind of important." Zion: "Yeah anytime when your ready I'm here listening. Say what do you think about listening to the radio for a little bit?" Mikalia: "Yeah I think that'll be alright." [Zion turns on the radio and channel surfs around until he arrives onto the frequency, 110.7 FM WTF "ALL REQUEST ALL THE TIME". The DJ on air just fades out "Back In Black" by AC/DC and cuts back to the studio wit the DJ answering a incoming caller. Zion stops when he catches a much familier voice come over the radio.] Caller- "Hey there my name is Mal Somers." Zion: "Hey wouldn't you know it. Think of the devil and she...er I mean he shall appear. It's my oponant for this Saturday, Mal Somers. The current WWA Rookie Champion." Mikalia: "Hey lets listen to what she...er I mean he has to say. [She leans into the radio almost like she is trying to speak into it jokingly.] Sorry Somers, we were just kidding, well at least I was. [looking back at Zion with a smirk] as if you could hear me Mal." [She leans back in her seat smiling all innocently at Zion while he just sarcastically smirks back at her.] DJ Danny Mason – “Hey Mal, old buddy...Totally awesome! And what are your two requests?” Somers – “Yeah can I have “Rivers of Babylon” by Boney M and “Two Out of Three Aint Bad” by Meatloaf?” DJ Danny Mason – “Hmmm Ooookayy..not the ususal hip stuff we like to play on WTF 110.7 Radio – we play ALL REQUESTS ALL THE TIME...but for the reigining WWA Rookie Champion, anything!” Zion: "Interesting taste in music Mikalia this Somers has. Funny I would of never pictured him as the kind of guy who likes the classics." Mikalia: "What did you expect baby? Did you think he would be the type of guy who would like stuff like N-Skank and the backdoor boyz, or masterbating to the likes of Brittney Queers?" Zion: [Thinks for a moment and then smiles with a sharp witty response.] "Well...yeah." Mikalia: "James Alexander Zion, that's your oponant for this Sunday's Fiesta in the main event. You know the best 2 out of 3 falls non title match. Possibly the hardest match of your entire career up to this point, you better not be taking this match lightly." Zion: "Sheesh woman, what's gotten into you this morning? You are like all fussy today. I'm just stating my opinions about the guy. Of course I'm going to take him seriously. However, I can have some preconceived notions about him that's what makes it more fun." Mikalia: "You're right baby, sorry, let's just listen to the radio some more and hear what else he has to say. I think the DJ's gonna put him on the spot for an impromtu interview." Zion: "How do you know?" Mikalia: "Just a hunch, stuff like this always happens when a celebrity calls into a radio station." Somers – “Actually Danny, can you lower the volume on the song so we can talk over the song to all the listeners out there?” DJ Danny Mason - “Sure thing.” (DJ Danny Mason adjusts the controls) Mikalia: "See told ya." DJ Danny Mason – “Alright, we are back on the air and we have a few questions for Mal Somers. Firstly, why the odd choice of songs?” Somers – “Good question Danny. I chose “Two out of three aint bad” because of my upcoming match at this Saturdays Fiesta. This Saturday... the main event...non title match...Champion versus Champion...Jimmi Zion versus Mal Somers...for the first time ever...in a two out of three falls match!” Zion: "You were right Mikalia, sounds like a pre match hype to me. He's right on the money so far." DJ Danny Mason – “Wow! That sure sounds like an early match of the year candidate!” Zion: "DJ Danny doesn't even know when to begin to comprehend the s**t that's instore for his buddy Mal and me on Fiesta this Saturday. Please match of the year honors. That won't even be the half of it." Somers – “Damn straight Danny. I can say “damn” on the radio, can’t I Danny?” DJ Danny Mason – “Uh well you just did Mal, twice.” Zion: "Ah technically Danny, according to the FCC, you can say 'Damn' on the radio...just not the other choice four letter words." Mikalia: "I think he's about to fly off the deep end with the curse words very shortly." Zion: "How do you know?" Mikalia: "Oh just a hunch, give it a minute. It's radio for crying out loud." Zion: "Oh yeah that's right." Somers – “Yes it is sure going to be a great match that’s going to have a lot of people taking notice.” Zion: [In a mock game show host voice] "Ding...ding...ding...tell the man what's he's won Mikalia!" Makalia: "He's won...a cookie." Zion: "Is it chocolate chip?" Makalia: "Nope oatmeal raisen." Zion: "It should be chocolate chip." (The song in the background nears the chorus) Somers – “And to paraphrase Meatloaf...” (sings along with the song) Somers – “Jimmi, don’t be sad, because two out of three aint bad...” (chuckles) Zion: "Thanks for the tip sunshine boy. Only I plan on WINNING two out of three times this Saturday." Somers – “That’s right Jimmi, this Saturday, Mal Somers is going to win two out of the three falls. I saw you on Insider. And I'll grant you a few things; First, we both share a common enemy in The Cell Zion: "You can say that bloody again Mal." Second, you are indeed a talented individual. Zion: "Some of us more talented than others, *COUGH* "ME!" *COUGH!* but thanks for the reminder eh Somers." Lastly. That Mikalia is one HOTTIE. Zion: [Looking visibly upset as he yells back at the radio.] "Hey that's bloody wife you're talking about! Watch it Somers." But despite all that, I have got to say, lucky my TV isn't a SMELLavision because that interview STANK! Whats this about beating my bloody a$$? I’ll save that for you and Lockdown when you “lock up in the ring!” Mikalia: "Ok Jimmi what did I miss earlier. What did you say to get him so pissed off at you?" [Completely speechless and smirkly slightly, Zion just shrugs his shoulders as he tries to continue listening to the radio attempting not to laugh at Somer's sudden change of emotions.] DJ Danny Mason – “Excuse me Mal, this is a PG-13 rated show. Tone it down a little, buddy. Zion: "Yeah you tell'em mister DJ. Tell that biotch to chill out man." Somers – “Sorry Danny, Im just f**kiing pissed off! I was expecting this match to be a bit of a “friendly”. Put on a great match for all the MALcoholics out there. But then Jimmi appears on Insider, going on about how great he is, about how nothing can stop him from winning the match. Heck, he even compared himself to Helen Keller! Jimmi...Helen Keller may have been blind and deaf but unlike you, she wasn’t deaf blind and DUMB!!” Mikalia: "Thank goodness that Danny boy has one hand on the censor button because that could get his radio station in serious trouble with the FCC. Jimmi you really know how to make a person mad at you." Zion: "I'll say, and I wasn't even trying. It must of been something I said. The thing I don't know is how could Somers put himself down to Helen Keller. That's not a very nice thing to say about himself. He needs to be a little more positive. Maybe he should go talk to a professional." Mikalia: "Actually Zion I think that Hellen Keller comment was directed at you." Zion: "No I think that was directed subconsciously at him. I think Mal has some major issues he needs to deal out. [Yells at radio] "Hey Mal, just look yourself in the mirror, recite some Stuart Smally Affirmation reminding you that you're not dumb. You should feel much better after that. [Looking back at Mikalia] See Mikalia, I feel better already. I feel like I helped a fellow human being." Mikalia: "Why do I even both with you sometimes?" Zion: "Because you love me too much." Mikalia: "Oh yeah, you got that right love." “Listen to me Jimmi “Sky Flyin, death defying, toe tappin’, knee slappin’ jumpin and jiving, chumpion of CHUMPIONS, Daddy –O” Zion...you are nothing but the MORTAL one. Zion: "Oh...oh...oh...Chumpion of chumpions? And he has no room to talk. What is he? Oh my gawd, he's the WWA Rookie Champion. That's like saying 'welcome to the WWA, here's your free rookie title belt.' Get it right, I'm not MORTAL. I'm IMMORTAL. With all the s**t that I've done in this league so far and what I still know I can do...no goddamn human being can remotely top the crap I've pulled off match after match after match." A highlight reel of bloopers! Zion: "What the hell is this man rambling about. Highlight reel of bloopers? As if he has no room to talk? What has he done lately to keep the fans in their seats? Somers, you could only dream for a small fraction of the talent that I have in my little finger. Just remember that I'm duel champion...what are you? Oh yeah I almost forgot, he's the WWA Rookie Champion." The only thing you have encoded in your DNA is a few less chromosomes! Zion: "Okay...and who are you all of a sudden...GOD? Do you know what was put into the finest peice of work the human race has ever laid eyes on? Obviously no on all counts." You run your mouth about being X-Treme incarnate? Hell, Im the mother f**king co-founder of the EXTREME CORPORATION! You’d be nothing but a janitor there!” Zion: "Co-founder of Extreme Corp? I'm just a janiter? First off my WWA Extreme and Cruiserweight titles tell me differently. Second, how can you be a corporation? There are only TWO of you. A corporation is a collective group of individuals that are more than TWO. You are no corporation. You founded it, but you are just two guys who think that you're extreme...I know I am extreme. If you don't believe me then take a look at my history and it will tell you the same thing." DJ Danny Mason – “Whoa settle down Mal! I’ll get fired if you keep going off like that. And you’re listening to WTF 110.7 Radio – we play ALL REQUESTS ALL THE TIME...that was Meatloaf with “Two out of three aint bad”. Next up we have “Rivers of Babylon” by Boney M. Mikalia: "Seriously Jimmi, I think you and Mal both need to heed the good Dj's advice. You need to settle down and chill out. Don't let your emotions get all out of wack before your big match this weekend." Zion: "You're right baby. Like I said you are my better half. You bring out the best in me." Mikalia: "I know." (the funky vibes of Rivers of Babylon start playing) DJ Danny Mason – "Mal, I’m know I'm going to regret asking you this...but whats the reason behind requesting this song?” Somers – “Nothing to worry about Danny. I’m sorry. I just got worked up. Im sure Jimmi is a nice guy. I mean he hates The Cell too, so he’s not all bad. I chose this classic for two reasons. Firstly, Jimmi annoyed me because he decided to “babble – on” about our match. Heh I crack myself up sometimes.” Zion: "Hey now that you think about it Mal, you crack me up to. You're a funny guy, and it's gonna rock either way like those two choice songs you requested." “But secondly, hold on...I’ll get to that in a second. I just want to say to Jimmi that I’m ready for our match. I’ve never been more ready. I’ve been forced off Fiesta by Cruz and Ive been champing at the bit to get back in and show the world what the Extreme Corporation is all about.” Zion: "I'm glad your ready because I'm more than ready for out match to fight you and beat you 2 out of three times this weekend. You're wanting to prove that the Extreme Corp is all about. Well I'm going to show you and everybody what I'm all about, and why I am the the WWA dual champions." DJ Danny Mason – “OK Mal, we are going to have to wrap it up. Before we do...whats the other reason you chose this song? I’m curious.” Somers – “Oh yeah. I wouldn’t even have a clue where the Rivers of Babylon are...but I’m going to find out. And then on Sunday, the day after Fiesta, I want all the MALcoholics out there to come with me. Because... (clears his throat) Somers – (singing) “By the rivers of Babylon... there we sat down... ye-eah we wept... when we remembered Zion... “By the rivers of Babylon.. there we sat down... ye-eah we wept... when we remembered Zion...” “Heck, lets make Sunday the 23rd Jimmi’s Remembrance Day...because when all is said and done, and Ive won the match...Jimmi, you will be nothing but a distant memory!” DJ Danny Mason – Lets go to commercial... Zion: "Man Somers, that sounds pretty bad. That's almost like Will Shatner trying to sing. If I were you I stick to wrestling. See you on this weekend on Fiesta. I will win big two out of three times. End of story." Mikalia: "Hey Jimmi, I don't mean to interupt your little rant, but I think we're here at the gym." Zion: "Oh cool, well then it's time to get crackin, fade out, it's pre showtime." [Zion pulls into the parking lot of a local gymnasium and finds a parking space where he pulls into. The camera fades out.] *[Fade Out]*
