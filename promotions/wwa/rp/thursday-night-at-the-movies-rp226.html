Thursday Night At The Movies

3 Jul 2003

By Dave Harley

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The screen opens on a crackly old black and white caption that reads 'How To Win An Independence Day Rumble'. As the presentation starts, we see Dave Harley stood in an empty ring in an empty arena. He has his hair tied back and is wearing a tuxedo. He has a curly fake moustache glued onto his face. He smiles into the camera as a stereotypical sounding English accent begins to speak.] V/O: This Sunday the good chaps at the World Wrestling Alliance will host the Independence Day Rumble. A jolly old bash which will decide the number one contender for the World Title. Thirty lucky fellows will be participating in this extravaganza. Will you be supporting your favourite? [The scene cuts to a kitchen. A woman stands in a kitchen that is pretty reminiscent of the Fifties. She is wearing an apron and is sporting a ridiculous grin.] Woman: [Towards the camera] But what is an Independence Day Rumble? V/O: You silly woman! I suppose you'll need to brush up on the rules before Sunday night, but as a treat for all of you Ladies here are the basics of an Independence Day Rumble... [The crackly, black and white picture cuts back to the wrestling ring. Dave Harley now stands with an inflatable dummy in one hand. The voiceover continues...] V/O: Here is what your husbands will be expecting to see at the Gentlemens Club this Sunday; each man will enter one at a time and attempt to eliminate his fellow competitors by knocking them over the top rope to the floor outside. [On cue, the Fifties Dave Harley tosses the dummy out of the ring.] V/O: It's as simple as that. [The camera cuts back to the Housewife.] V/O: So remember, if your husband is not home in time for tea on Sunday Night... Woman: He's watching the Independence Day Rumble? V/O: That's right. Simply leave it on the stove until he comes home. [The woman looks rather pleased with herself as the movie fades out to an elaborate and crackly fanfare. The camera zooms out as the lights go up in the cinema that was playing the 'infomercial'. Dave Harley is sat in the middle of an empty row.] Harley: Wow, that film has cleared a lot of things up about the Independence Day Rumble! That guy in the ring looked kind of familiar though. Maybe one of my ancestors was a Rumble winner. Well no worries there. I'll make them proud this year. I mean, think back to the Bourbon Street Brawl. Hell, if it hadn't been for Rookies twisted logic I would never have been eliminated and probably would have become the winner. But I'm happy for Chance. He deserved a Title shot and he managed to beat EVD to it on Monday. This Sunday I have to take on twenty nine other guys. But if you think about it it's not so tough. It's not like a Bourbon street Brawl or anything. On Monday, X Corp made it into the final five. This Sunday, we aim to be in the final three. And then what? Well...we're workin' on a plan. [Harley stands up to leave, but turns back.] Harley: Oh, one last thing before I go. Joey K, you thought it was humiliating the way I eliminated you at the Bourbon Street Brawl? Well you're gonna have to get used to humiliation my friend, because on Sunday you'll not only be leaving the Rumble without a Title shot, you'll be leaving without a Title! [As Harley finishes an Usher walks in.] Usher: Sir, I'm afraid I'm going to have to ask you to leave. we need to clean this place up for the Hulk premiere and you've got popcorn all over the floor. Harley: I'll leave if you give me free tickets. Usher: .......Security! Harley: OK, time to fly! [Harley darts out as the scene fades out.]
