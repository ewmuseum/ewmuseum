You can run, but you can't hide *BSB*

4 Jun 2004

By Odium

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Drip. The scene fades open into a raining night underneath a bridge. A man stands underneath the bridge, leaning against the inside of it with his left foot knelt against the wall, his right foot straight. Below his eyes which are covered with black shades, his jaw is going up and down, his teeth chattering, he is chewing gum by the looks of it. Drip. The camera focuses on a small drip falling from the top of the bridge to the floor. It lands with a small splash, spreading around in a centimetre circle. The camera moves right again, and closes in on the man leaning against the inside of the bridge. We see he is wearing a thick black coat, blue jeans and black shoes. It's not a drugged up teenager out for a smoke, it's just Odium. The camera moves in until it is about 5 feet away from Odium, just enough so that you can see his whole body on the screen. "Where did water come from?" This was said with such a tone of curiosity in his voice that it seemed so tempting to answer smartly, but there really isn't a scientific answer to this question. "I'm serious. A few will just say that it came from chemicals. In that case, which chemicals? Others will say god made it. Well god doesn't exist in my opinion. He's just a fictional character who is an excuse to start a group hobby, needless to say it got big." Odium turns to face towards the camera, and gives a sarcastic wink and smile. "You should be aware of something. If I get a little cranky, you should know that rain is not exactly my all time favourite weather. In fact, you should know that I despise rain in every way. It's water, after all." Water. The cause of so many problems. Flooding, Tsunamis. But it is also a lifesaver in somer ways, such as that it can be used sometimes to put out fires. "Why is it that oceans are considered a ferocious danger? There not really that dangerous after all, nor are they particularly ferocious for that matter. Well, that is except for the flooding, the tsunamis and the whirlwinds in the water. And the fact that it is the habitat for dangerous animals such as the shark. But besides that, you have nothing to worry about." He flicks his glasses off from his eyes and they rest on top of his head. The left half of his mouth flicks up to be level with a nose, and suddenly the most sarcastic grin as humanely possible appears on his face. "The oceans are underestimated for just how dangerous they can be. One day you're swimming in them, riding on a banana boat in them, but the next day you're either on the top of the tallest building in the city, hiding from a flood, or you're running right out of the city away from a tsunami. Either way, you're gonna die sooner or later no matter what is happening. You can run, but you can never hide from it." The other side of Odium's mouth lifts just as high as the other side, and amazingly, while this happens, Odium manages to keep his face sarcastic all the while. He sighs. "I am the ocean. Philosophically speaking that is. No really, take a few seconds to strain your little minds and think about it. I am underestimated for just how dangerous I am. One minute you may well be on the floor, punching an unconscious me, the next minute it is the other way round. With me, it doesn't matter what you do, you're going to die, because you can run, but you can never hide from me." Odium breathes in and out rapidly after not breathing once while explaining that, and soon after you can tell the difference between him breathing normally and his sigh of satisfaction. "You see, the thing-" This sentence is cut short when Odium notices a man, rather a spotty teenager, with his head ducked low and a thick black overcoat, just standing in front of Odium, and then Odium breaks the rather random silence. "May I help you?" The danger of this situation only became apparent when the boy's gruff voice spoke rather harshly. "Listen, asshole, give me every penny you have in your pockets and nobody here will get hurt." "Ok" was the reply that came from Odium. He reached into his pocket, and pulled out three 1p coins. The boys gruff voice spoke once again. "Listen, punk, I know you have more money than that, give it to me!" "But...you only asked for the pennies?" With this sentence spoken by Odium, came a sarcastic grin right to the face of the boy. The boy's face went rather red, and he spoke, this time much more angrily. "I'm warning you, you won't like me when I'm angry." Odium's face suddenly lit up when the boy spoke this sentence, and he instantly replied. "From the looks of it I doubt I'd even like you when you're happy." Within a millisecond of Odium stopping this sentence being spoken, the boy in the overcoat runs at Odium with a knife in his right hand. Odium ducks the knife shot, and knocks the boy unconscious by slamming his head into the concrete wall he is leaning on. Above Odium, a train pulls in on the bridge, and Odium climbs the hill next to the bridge, running to the train station. "That's my train. Bourbon Street Brawl, here I come, it's game over!"
