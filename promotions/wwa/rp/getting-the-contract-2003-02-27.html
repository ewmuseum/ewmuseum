Getting the Contract

27 Feb 2003

By DeLucci Brothers

Item 4 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 27 Feb 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
10:47am ..::The thick smoke from the cars pour out in thier plentyful as we see Tony DeLucci standing back against a wall outside the WWA headquarters. His long thick grey trenchcoat droops against his slack shoulders as he stands waiting. His hands rest in his pockets and Tony seems very content looking up at the grey spring sky::.. ..::Busy people walking everywhere, a sea of brown, black, grey and white suits everywhere. Walking in unison, muttering with thier business chatter, Tony takes his hands out of his pocket and rubs on his rough forehead, this contents him as his brother walks up to him and clips him round the ear::.. Tommy: 'Ey's I's thought's I's told you to be in.. incon.. inconspic.. ..::Tommy splutters his wording::.. Tony: Inconspicuous? Tommy: 'Ey shut'up ya face, didn't mother tell you not to swear? ..::Tony looks confused for a moment then shakes his head, his brother was always right, Tommy prods Tony and both men go through the revolving doors of the main headquarters. Both men walk through the shiney mable flooring seems far too clean. Thank god Tony was wearing his old shoes as he leaves a trail of polish against the grey flooring::.. ..::Tommy heads towards the main reception desk. He points to some seating near the desk and clips Tony in the general direction of it, Tommy confidently strides up to the reception desk where a couple of very pretty girls a sitting::.. Tommy: 'Ey doll face, do me and my brother a favour and get him some juice he's runnin out of vitamins and needs a top up Receptionist: Er excuse me sir? Tommy: You heard now scram! Receptionist: I'm not going anywhere unless you tell me who you two are? Tommy: Were the DeLucci brothers, come on doll face don't tell's me you's never heard of the DeLucci brothers ..::The receptionist shakes her head in a "no" fashion very slowly. Tommy gives a why expression and gestures by gently slapping with the back of his hand under his chin. He calls his brother over who trundles over very slowly head facing the ground::.. Tommy: Look's doll face, we's got's some very important business with your boss upstairs to take care of, so if you's don't mind you's just call him and tell's the DeLucci boy's are here ..::The receptionist looks disgusted as she turns away to call her "boss" Tommy taps a small tune out on the modern desk. Tommy smiles at Tony and points to himself gesturing that he's done everything needed. Just about at that time a big security guard comes up::.. Tommy: You's here to take us to your's boss? Security guard: Do I look like I am? ..:: Tommy shrugs his shoulders and points his arm outstretched to a point behind the security guard with a look of horror on his face. The guard just smiles and nods his head also in a slow "no" fashion. Just about at that time the security guard grab's Tommy's shoulder and escorts him to the door with a friendly push on the way::.. Tommy: Yeah, well, same to you's too! ..::Tommy then walks away from the building and around the corner before pulling out a mobile phone, he presses a few numbers also lighting a cigar in the same motion. The smoke exhales from his body in a smooth stream before a voice is heard on the other end of the phone::.. Voice: Hello? Tommy: What the hell's do you's mean hello? It's your brother stupid. Did you sneak past the security guard? Tony's voice: Yeah I did's. Tommy: Good now go to the elevator and onto the fifth floor, that's where our contact told's us where the contracts would's be Tony: Ok's I'll call you's back in a moment then Tommy Tommy: Ok, don't screw this up now Tony DeLucci otherwise I'm gonna ..::The phone by this time has already hung up, Tommy is forced to look around at the world, the endless tooting of car horns and taxi cabs, Tommy walks down the street weaving in and out of the hustle and bustle, trying very hard to avoid contact with anyone. He stops at a shop window and peers around::.. Voice from the crowd: Pervert! ..::Tommy whips round faster than a greyhound to see the voice dissapear in the sea of suits, he looks back round to what he was looking at first and has only just realised that he was looking at La Senza "Bra and Panties" store. He turns back rests on the wall and smokes his cigar in the street. He gets some funny looks but no one stops him::.. ..::Rats the lot of them all of them scurrying around looking for food and a bigger piece of the pie, but not Tommy he had plans. He smiled to himself as he drifts off into a day dream, but that dream is quickly shattered as he feels a vibration in his coat pocket. He picks up the small phone::.. Tommy: You's best have some good news Tony: Ok's I's followed your plans Tommy and I's in the room where all the paper work is Tommy: Ok's you's know what were looking for it should have the word "Contract" in big bold letters. You's gots it? Tony: I think's so Tommy. Tommy: Well does it say contract on it's? Tony: No Tommy: Well, that's not it then you's idiot. ..::They say when time is precious it moves quickly, this pause however lasted almost an eternity::.. Tony: Oh, I's thinks I's gots it Tommy Tommy: Good's now just get the hell out's of there, anyone ask's you's just used the bathroom Tony: Ok's Tommy I'll be down in a minute ..::Tommy hangs up the phone and places it back in his suit pocket, he waits looking round the corner of the street for Tony's presence, the seconds tick past and a drip of sweat breaks on Tommy's forehead. He wipes it quickly, his hands twitching, people look worried for him as he sweats more and more. Tommy needed to calm his nerves he put his cigar in his mouth and inhaled deep::.. ..::Tony however was upping his pace as he clapsed the pieces of paper in his hand tightly. He looked at no-one and more suprisingly no-one looked at him, he kept his head down keeping a straight line. He could see daylight::.. ..::The noise was intense as Tony broke out of the building he had been in, he twisted left and right looking for a famliar, he could see the cigar smoke seeping out of mouth fifty paces down the road, he walked in that direction. The wind had started to pick up, and Tony's suit coat danced in the wind. He reached Tommy and handed over the notes::.. Tommy: Good's, the DeLucci boys are heading to the WWA! ..::Tommy glanced at his watch before walking off. Not bad for a mornings work::.. 11:03
