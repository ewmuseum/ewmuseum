Stare Into The Eyes Of The Genie Of Destruction And Despair...

24 Jan 2003

By Jackson Kraven

Item 10 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 24 Jan 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(A pair of eyes stares out at us, maddened with rage, ice-blue and deadly...) KEVIN! YOU F*CKRAG! I KNOW YOU'RE F*CKING OUT THERE, YOU F*CKER! (The parts of the face we can see around the eyes look quite animated, but the eys themselves merely stare emotionlessly out at us.) YOU WANNA COME DOWN TO THE RING, YOU WANNA RUIN MY F*CKING MATCH, YOU WANNA MAKE ME LOSE AGAINST IAN F*CKING TENSE? GO AHEAD! YOU JUST SIGNED YOUR OWN F*CKING DEATH WARRANT! (Still the eyes stare out.) You know Kevin, I listened to your entire interview on Insider. Hell, I even read the entire f*cking transcript so that I could make sure I didn't get a single bit of wrong. You think I ruined your f*cking life? You have no f*cking idea. If you hadn't run away from the f*cking WWA first time I apparently kicked your f*cking ass, you'd have seen just how bad your life could get. Alexandra Korth? F*ck her... ...Hell, most of the WWA have anyway... ...I don't give a f*ck about what I did to her. Hell, I probably did Denrol a favour. I probably did the f*cking world a favour! Saved it from seeing the world's first half-human half-German Shepherd mongrel. I've heard a lot of things about Denrol's little bitch, and one of those things is she isn't partial about who or what she f*cks. Just like I'm not partial to whose career I f*ck. (There is a pause, a pause which seems like an eternity as those ice-blue eyes stare deep into our souls, allowing the horrible, hateful words to sink in.) Kevin, stay away from me. Stay... Away... Otherwise I won't be responsible for my actions. Just like I won't be responsible for my actions at Rage In The Cage VI. Denrol has devised the ultimate expression of his hate and rage, and I will take great delight in humiliating him in it before utterly destroying him in it and bringing it crashing down on his f*cking head. Denrol, Rage In the Cage grows ever closer. I can feel ice-water in my veins already. How about you? (The shot begins to fade out. The last image we see is that of Kraven's still-unblinking eyes...)
