Relaxing??

30 Dec 2002

By Shaman

Item 3 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 30 Dec 2002, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Shaman is sitting in front of a large screen tv playing Spec Ops on the Play Station 2 as Suzy walks over to him and whispers in his ear as he looks up and notices the WWA camera and smirks and then pauses the game.. [Shaman] You know i thought you guys would've been here later. Oh well anyways i had a few things i wanted to say before all the Jack asses in the WWA get a bug up their $#@!@#$ A$$. You few at the top think your ever so invincible. I mean look you got jackson Kraven spouting off at the mouth like bad disease, Damien Thorne claiming that he isn't huiding behind the House of Pain rejects, And then you have Ian Tense the never defending my title champion!! Look a ranking on paper looks good. Yet again all of you have been only fighting each other. I on the other hand never turn down a challenge and face anyone and everyone unlike most of the top guys like Jackson Kraven, Damien Thorne, Ian Tense...The list isn't much longer than those names. Shaman gets up and walks over to the Tv and places his hands upon it palms down and turns towards the camera slightly. [Shaman] Not many would think Shaman has plans. I retired an idiot just a few weeks ago and i do have future plans. Jackson Kraven from what i saw at Seasons Beatings you have your hands full with Denrol. Unlike anyone else House of Pain will not be able to help you there. Trust me on this!! Their reign of dominance will soon come to an end when people decide to use all of you as punching bags!! Shaman stops and walks a bit back to his chair and sitting back down in it. [Shaman] Now back to the more important matter at hand. Chrystian Payne whom thinks i don't have things planned for him. Running like a scared rabbit isn't part of the plan Payne. Your hell is just beginning and i intend on handling it very well. Only you can recieve the utter defeat at my hands that is deserving of a man who flees the WWA abandoning his partners!! You know Payne Darken told me to let it go at the time, But he isn't around anymore. You, Mack Tenn are just going to have to keep running because i will get my revenge in the end. namely against every other WWA super star that has wronged me before or bad mouthed me!! I am through with playing games and i will get results. I'll make this short and sweet today since im playing a video game. Go ahead and look over your shoulder because i'll nail you from any direction i feel appropriate!! Shaman sits back down unpausing the video game and begins playing it again.
