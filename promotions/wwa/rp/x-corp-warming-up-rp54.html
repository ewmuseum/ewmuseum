X Corp: Warming Up

27 May 2003

By Dave Harley

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Scene - A large wooden door bursts open and Dave Harley and Mal Somers enter the room in their gym gear. Both look quite exhausted after several hours training for the upcoming King of The Hill Pay Per View Match. The dump their gym bags on the floor and slump on individual recliners in front of the television at Dave Harley's Chicago home.) Harley: Whoa! Was that a workout and a half or what? Somers: You're telling me Dave, but we know it will be worth it in the end. Just over a week now til the Pay Per View and we will have to be on the top of our game for the King of The Hill match. Harley: Which we will if we keep going at this rate! (Both men recline in their chairs and stare at the ceiling for a short while.) Harley: So, Mal, we're cool...aren't we? Somers: Oh hell yeah! I mean we wear pretty stylish threads, and have our fair share of fans. I'd say we are WAY COOL! Harley: (laughs) Nah man, what I meant was, is everything OK between us, after all thats being happening lately? Somers: Oh I see. (Moves to an upright position) Sure Dave, everythings cool. I mean, I'll never get over what you did in the past, but thats really got nothing to do with me. I trust you. What concerns me more if what Chance has been up to. I hope it doesnt prove costly to us. But us, we're cool. Harley: Thats great man, because we are going to have to trust each other in the big match if we are going to retain the WWA Tag Team Championship. Somers: Exactly. No worries there. We have survived the worst that the WWA has thrown us over the past few months...Rage in the Cage, the Hell in a Cell... Harley: Lockdowns B.O... Somers: We will just have to continue along the path we have chosen, and that is to make X-Corp the fightingest Tag Champs in WWA History! (Both men nod as Harley grabs the TV control and turns the T.V. on) Harley: Whattya say Mal, how about this evening we just "tune out." No talking about the PPV, our "issues", no board games, no karaoke. (Somers frowns) Harley: We'll just sit here and watch television for a few hours and chill out! (Somers smiles) Somers: Good idea man. It'd be good not to think about the losers we are facing at the PPV, even if it is only just for a couple of hours Whats on? Harley: Oh goody! The new episode of the Sopranos has just started! Somers: (winces) Ah, Dave? Isn't that just a little remeniscent of the DeLuccis? I thought we were "tuning out"? Harley: Oh yeah! Yuck! (flick the channel over.) There ya go, COPS! (starts singing) "Bad Boys, bad Boys, whatchya gonna do, whatchya gonna do when..) Somers: Hey Dave? Doesnt that guy there being hauled in the back of the police car look a little like Lockdown? Harley: Oh yeah, its probably just his outfit, but anyways..(turns the channel) thats a shame, I love that show. But as I said, tonight is going to be a WWA-Free zone! Ah this'll do. The Discovery Channel. Nothing on here could... (David Attenborough narrates a documentary on the mating habits of mackerel.) X-Corp: Doh! Leviathan! (They change to the next channel and start watching Hercules.) Harley: I think I'm going to cry..Havoc. (turns off the T.V.) Somers: Damn it! I guess our competiton is going to stick around like a bad smell. Harley: The only way to solve that is going to be by giving them some work experience at the Extreme Corporation at the Pay Per View! Somers: Damn straight. I guess Havoc are going to have to rent two plots next to their dying Dad! Harley: (laughs) Wanna hit the gym? (Somers nods as both men get back up, grab their bags and leave through the wooden door. It slams shut as we fade to black.)
