Chapter 3: The principle of the surprise

Chapter 3: The principle of the surprise “All warfare is based on deception.” – Sun Tzu Deception is key to all warfare. The ability to deceive the forces that you are going to fight against is a key component. Chance, I have deceived you before. Chance, deception is not just what you perceive to see, but what you perceive to think. That will be extremely key. “Hold out baits to entice the enemy. Feign disorder, and crush him.” – Sun Tzu Chance, I do not believe that any bait is necessary in this battle. For you see, you are already baited. You already want to destroy me. Much like I want to destroy you. Bait id unnecessary as you have been hooked. I shall crush you Chance. I am going to do everything that I say. I am going to punish you worse than you were punished in the Bourbon Street Brawl. And unlike the Bourbon Street Brawl, I will come out on top. “The spot where we intend to fight must not be made known; for then the enemy will have to prepare against a possible attack at several different points; and his forces being thus distributed in many directions, the numbers we shall have to face at any given point will be proportionately few.” – Sun Tzu Chance, there are points on every man’s body that can be exploited to cause extreme pain. For you see Chance, there are three specific focal points that I am going to focus on. Now two of them you know very well what I am capable of. Remember what I did to your knee and leg? Remember the rehab you had to go through because of me? That was not the only place I attacked. I attacked your heart and I attacked your back. Remember all the back pain I caused you? I know a lot about back injuries and I know that they do not heal…ever. Chance you can expect me to focus on those two areas, because that is not a secret. That is a fact that everyone knows. The secret lies in the third and most important focal point. This is one that you will have to wait and fell first hand. Chance, you are going to suffer, I am sure of it. “Rapidity is the essence of war: take advantage of the enemy's unreadiness, make your way by unexpected routes, and attack unguarded spots.” – Sun Tzu Quick hits, quick attacks, hit and run. Don’t let your opponent have a breath. That’s the way I am focusing on this match. You are going to be hit and continuously hit. You will not be able to stop my attack.
