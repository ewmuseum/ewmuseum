*BSB* Lazy Gazing

3 Jun 2004

By Mal Somers

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It was a perfect early summers day. A light breeze flew about aimlessly, tickling the long strands of emerald green grass that rolled as far as the eye could see. In contrast to this vista, the black X-Mobile lay parked atop a small hill, overlooking the undulating valleys below. A figure could be seen laying atop the roof of the vehicle, spreadeagled. Like a lizard warming its blood, Mal Somers was stretched out, basking in the suns rays. He looked up at the baby blue sky, interspersed with soft white clouds. Somers had been lazing here all morning, watching the clouds go by. They seemed to be static, but by gazing at them for a duration of time, he could track their movements. He grinned as he realised he would have to employ this strategy for the Bourbon Street Brawl. Mal had been studying tapes of all the expected competitors, and even some possible surprises. By following their mannerisms and peculiarities, he hoped to understand more about them. After a morning of gazing, Mal had stopped focusing on particular clouds, but rather, looking at the sky as a whole. Its funny that by losing focus, sometimes things can become a lot clearer. By now, the clouds had begun to take on various shapes. The twisted grin on the mask of the Muppet Man, the icy stare of SEAL and the iron fist of Leviathan. At that moment, the clouds passed the sun and Mal’s surrounds went a few shades darker, an all encompassing shadow. The temperature seemed to instantly drop three or four degrees. Mal closed his eyes to refresh them and collect his thoughts. He was hoping that was a coincidence but who knows? Somers reopened his eyes and all was bright and mild again. The clouds had passed the sun and Somers looked up at them again. One cloud seemed to be very white. It seemed to be growing in size. It seemed to… SPLAT! Somers yelped and sat upright, wiping birdshit from near his eye. Typical, just when you think the darkness has passed it comes back to shit on you. “What’s wrong bud?” Enjoying the serenity with Mal was his long time tag partner and best friend Dave Harley. A recent mishap at the hands of another Devil’s Own member, Quietus had Harley confined to a wheelchair, and as such, Harley sat in the sidecar of the X-Mobile, shades on, eating a Happy Meal or three. “A bird crapped in my eye!” replied Mal, removing the last of it with a handkerchief. “That’s what you get for sleeping on the roof.” Mal shook his head as he sat atop the vehicle. “I wasn’t asleep bud, I was thinking, thinking about the Bourbon Street Brawl.” ”You and me both! It’s a shame I’m not cleared to wrestle.” “Sure is” said Somers, “that way we could play paper rock scissors and I could pin you again!” (Harley stuck his tongue out as a third voice chimed in.) “That was soooo funny, but a dumbass strategy!”. (Somers banged on the roof of the X-Mobile, underneath in the drivers seat was last years winner of the Brawl, Chance, feet on the dashboard, reading Lord of the Rings with a smirk on his face.) “Yeah thanks for the advice” joked Harley “If we hadn’t done that who knows who would have won?” Chance pondered for a brief moment “Hmmm I think I still would have.” Somers and Harley shook their head at Chance’s good natured humour. This was the perfect X-Corp Annual General Meeting, as a Corporation they are required to have these. They enjoy them so much they have them four times a year, which is rather unique for an Annual General Meeting, but that’s X-Corp for you. “Damn Chance, can you turn the air-con off,” yelled Somers, “you’ll drain all our power!” Chance pressed the off button as Somers tried to remember why they installed air conditioning in an open air vehicle such as the X-Mobile. Dave downed his last burger and started talking. “So Mal, the Brawl, what were you thinking about?” Somers lay back down again, arms behind his head. “Well, Chance is right. This year people are expecting a lot from X-Corp, I don’t think we should concern ourselves with silly games half way through the match. We have to work as a team, strength in numbers.” “Good point Mal, but what we are the last two left?” pondered Chance. “Then most definitely paper rock scissors” said Dave Harley. All three nodded in unison, as solid as a signed contract. Somers looked skyward once more, looking for something. Although he knew X-Corp would have to be in full force because it was now expected of them, he did fear that another group would also be gathered. The Devil’s Own. Miss Wise had recorded the recent Insiders for Mal and sent them to him to view in his New Orleans hotel room. Muppet Man and Leviathan had been very vocal as of late, and even non-entrants such as Quietus were staking their claims. He would be a fool to think that the Devil’s Own would not be trying to make a big impact at the Brawl, using their numbers to help each other to victory. He recalled Leviathan assisting Damian Thorne to grab the WWA World Title at the 2003 Birthday Bash as one example of how effective this could be. Mal saw a rabid dog in the clouds, more particularly its underbelly. He knew he was the underdog, the dark horse. He knew if he was going to win, he would have to be cognisant of the safety that numbers could provide. He also knew X-Corp would have to work as a team to take on the Devil’s Own as well as the many other entrants. He knew that he would have to withstand the darkness, and if it passed, acknowledge that he would be standing in the eye of the storm. Nothing would shit on his parade. Not this time. BOURBON STREET BRAWL JUNE 7 2004 (Fade Out)
