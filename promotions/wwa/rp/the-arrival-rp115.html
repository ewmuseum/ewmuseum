The Arrival

10 Jun 2003

By "The Psycho" Steven Murphy

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The room is empty, showing now life around. The room is quite small, with only a table and chair in it. But in the middle of the room, there was a TV set, which had a fuzzy screen. Suddenly, it came to life, and shown a black screen.) TV: The new era has arrived…. (On the TV screen, P-S-Y-C-H-O came onto the screen one by one, all in dark crimson red.) TV: He has arrived, and now the WWA should be weary, or you could become…. (On the TV, Just Another Victim appears, then pictures of Chuckster bleeding, Jarod Crow in pain, and finally, Psycho winning the European Title.) TV: The Pain is coming…. (There is a dark shadow of a tall man covering the TV) Psycho: Christopher Vice? Tony Caesar? They are just pawns in Psychos game. And this Fiesta, I will show WWA what game I play. Vice, Tony, you are the next Victims, then who knows? (Psycho steps forward from the camera, and looks menacing in his speaking.) Psycho: Are you ready to Play the Game? (Psycho laughs as he walks back behind the camera, and the TV returns to its fuzzy screen.) (Scene Ends)
