Signs some autographs

6 Feb 2003

By James Beam

Item 7 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 6 Feb 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene opens to a mall-like setting. People are milling about looking in store windows and the like. The camera fans the thousands of faces and then stops on a large gathering of people, most are waiting in line and there are numerous flash bulbs going off. Once the camera begins moving towards the gathering, you see a group of teens talking.) Teen Boy1: Dude! Wes Sanders is over there! Teen Boy2: No way bra! He is the S to the H Iznit! Teen Boy1: Straight! Teen Boy3: We gotta see dat! (The boys run off towards the gathering. As they and the camera near, it is indeed Wes Sanders signing autographs. The three teens all give each other high fives and get in line. Sanders finishes an autograph and stands up with his hands out, as the fans grow restless. ) Sanders: Don't worry Fans... We got another WWA (pauses) Superstar coming out here. I have to fly out for Meltdown in Ontario. (The crowd is noticably upset at Wes Sanders having to leave. Then from behind the makshift curtain, James Beam steps out and moves in to shake Wes Sanders hand.) Wes Sanders (shouting out to the fans): You guys are why this sport is all the RAGE!! Peace out! (The crowd responds with a big pop, as Beam and Sanders shake hands. The flash bulbs errupt as the two wrestlers shake. Wes leans in to say something to only James Beam.) Wes Sanders (leaning in): Good luck following THAT, kid. (Wes chuckles as James Beam grimmances from the daunting task of following one of the biggest names in the WWA. The crowd quiets down and James waves to everyone.) James: How is everyone doing here in Detroit?! (The crowd is even more silent at this entrance from James Beam. More than half the crowd leaves with some muttering under their breath. But still some kids and teens are lined up for an autograph. James is notacably disappointed at people leaving, but brightens up when he begins talking to people in line and signing autographs.) (This goes on for about 5 minutes. People come and people go. Finally the teens that started this scene out are next in line. Teen Boy 1 steps up with a bummed look on his face.) James Beam: Who can I make this out too...? Teen Boy1: Dude! I didn't even know you were still in the WWA...?! James Beam (unsure): Uh... Yeah... I wrestled against Christyan Payne in a non-title match at the Meltdown before the PPV. Teen Boy 1 (not impressed): Oh, I saw you dude... You are lucky Payne didn't tear your damn arm off and beat you with it. (The teens behind him snicker, James Beam's face turns red and he forces a smile.) James: Look kid... I am trying here... Do you want an autograph or not. Teen Boy 1: Sure... (reaches over and grabs a picture of Wes Sanders) Could you get Wes to sign this for me?! James (trying to remain clam): Sure (through cleaned teeth). No problem. (Now that it is clear they are just harassing Beam, the other two boys step up.) Teen Boy 2: So... You see where Curzon is calling you out bra? James: Yeah... I saw that... I look forward to the match. It should be a good test of our skills. Teen Boy 3 (a look of disbelief): Did you really just say that? I mean comon' are you a man or does that hot box Rebecca have your d- James (interupting): Enough! (settles down) You guys need to get out of here. This is not working out for me. You guys are obvisouly not fans of mine, so make like a tree.... (The teens shake their head at the annoying 80's saying.) Teen Boy 1: Dude! Curzon is gonna whoops your ass on Fiesta! James: Perhaps... But that is why they play the game! You just never know. (The teens moves out of earshot and James regains his smile.) James: Next.... (Motions with his hand, for the next person in line. A young kid comes up to James with a complete look of awe.) Boy 1: Mr Beam... I con't believe you are actually here. You are my favorite wrestler ever! James: Well... I try my hardest... What is your name kid? Boy 1: Wesly... (rimshot!) (James's smiles at the irony and signs the autograph. The next person in line steps up, as the scene fades.)
