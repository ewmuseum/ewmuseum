Pain is abroad!!

20 May 2003

By Shaman

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
** Shaman is seen sitting in the center of the room as Terry Valentine walks into the room and sits in front of Shaman under the single light bublb which is swinging above him in a disturbing fashion as the walls are obviously straight concrete with a few cracks and water staine dstreaks upon them and Shaman raises his head looking at Terry Valentine.** Shaman: " Hello terry what brings you today?" ** Terry nervously looks about the room and speaks.** Terry: Shaman what happened on Meltdown? You could have won the match and yet you settled for destruction instead! Shaman: **grinning sadistically** " What happened was control. Zion had finally found a way to defeat me or so he thought. I goaded him into false reality and then beat the living hell out of him. Jimmy did you not see that it was i who controlled the match? You had a chance to get ahead and yet i countered with counter after counter. So ask yourself Jimmy who made who hurt?" ** Terry looked at Shaman and then spoke.** Terry: You have become even more brutal than every before and yet you seem to rise above the others in this category. Dare i say it you are quickly becoming as brutal as ever, Maybe even Jackson Kraven! ** Shaman looks at Terry very serious ** Shaman: " Jackson Kraven has his style and i have mine. Thats as simple as that. When im angered and annoyed with something or someone i simply destroy it. I care very little about what Jimmy Zion goes through. he started this fight and i will damn well finish it!!" ** Terry shakes his head and speaks again ** Terry: Shaman you have changed in the five years you have been here! Why the sudden change and sudden violent tendancies? Shaman: " There is no reason beyond the fact im going to climb back to the top one way or another. I've had all the games i can take. No bosses or ignorant presidents are going to stop me anymore. Matthew Warr has stayed out of my way and let me do my thing. Nathaniel Raine has my respect for trudging forward with his disability and hard understanding towards me. Jimmy Zion didn't give the respect and did not understand the fact that messing with me was going to get him severely hurt. No Jimmy Zion was blinded by the fact that i had changed. he paid very little attention to what i am capable of. I hurt him last night and i began my reign of blood. Your knee will look like a mangled mess today. Good i don't care. your aerial abilities are going to be useless in this future of yours. Do not play with what you have no understanding Jimmy boy. I am going to continue to hurt you. Do you think im scared. Do you Jimmy you are nothing but a braggart to me claiming to be someone he is not!" ** Shaman turns to terry and looks at him questioningly ** Shaman: " Let me ask you Terry why so concerned?" ** Terry looks at Shaman a bit taken aback ** Terry: I uh uh wanted to understand what was going on with you! Shaman: " Concern for me Terry that is almost laughable. I need no concern from anyone least of all anyone who has it for me. I do not need any sympathy save it for my opponents. No pity Jimmy Zion because he is on the run for an end. I will cripple him before his days are through with me. Remember awake the giant Jimmy get squished with his wrath!!" ** Shaman turns and walks away from Terry disappearing through the darkness as footfalls on wet concrete fades to nothing. **
