The Dawn of Havoc

24 Jan 2003

By Havoc

Item 6 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 24 Jan 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
He stood, immovable, against the buffeting winds of the Canadian Rockies. His long, black hair and trenchcoat flailed behind him. Among men, his giant frame elicited fear and terror. Yet, against the backdrop of the vast glory of nature, his frame appeared small. The ancient trees that surrounded him dwarfed him, as he dwarfed most men. His dark eyes scanned the horizon, drinking deeply of the pastel-washed clouds that herald the rise of day. His eyes narrowed, staring to the east, as though he could stare into the heart of Calgary, and even into the Saddledome itself. His face remained placid, inscrutable. From behind, his brother approached, carrying a steaming mug in one hand and a cellular phone in the other. Despite the bitter cold of morning in the Rockies, only a pair of jeans cloaked his body from the elements. "I’ll see you there, girl." he spoke into the phone. His short, dark hair waved slightly in the biting wind as he closed the small phone and placed it in the pocket of his jeans. He stood next to his larger brother, following the giant’s gaze. For several moments, they stood in silence, only the howling of the wind and the creaking of antediluvian branches accompanying their thoughts. "Can you feel it, Deimos?" the larger asked, never releasing his gaze from the sunrise. Deimos looked around for a moment, before shrugging. "Feel what, Phobos?" he asked with one eyebrow raised. "The earth is trembling." Phobos began. "Even Gaia herself recognizes that a new age is approaching, a new era. Rage In The Cage is approaching swiftly, each day burning itself out until the dawn of a new day for Havoc. She feels the terrible wrath within my heart. She knows that decimation and bloodshed will follow me into that cage. With the utter destruction of the Texas Longhorns, Havoc showed the world that our age of domination has returned. There is no hope for the American Luchadores. Their day as the Champions ends in Calgary, where they shall be plunged into darkness." "What about this mystery team?" Deimos asked as he raised the mug of coffee to his lips. "They don’t matter." Phobos answered matter-of-factly. "We will prepare for any eventuality. We are the sons of Ares, God of War. In the cage, we will show the world, and this hidden threat, what that truly means as we rain carnage down upon them all. Whether we are seeing the return of Amazingly Simple, Random Acts of Violence, or even the Overlords of the Dark themselves, we will walk away from Calgary having proven that the teachings of Ares are superior. We will walk away with our third WWA Tag Team Title reign." Deimos sipped from his coffee. Together they stood, facing east, heedless of the forces of nature that assaulted them. A strange, sick, little smile crept its way across Deimos’ lips. "CRY HAVOC...!" Sudden activity burst around them as small animals clambered to safety, and a flock of crows took to the sky, angrily cursing the intrusion. Phobos glanced over to Deimos. Deimos couldn't suppress his chuckle. "So much for the peace and quiet." End.
