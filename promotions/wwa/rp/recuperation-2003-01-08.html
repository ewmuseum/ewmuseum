Recuperation

8 Jan 2003

By TXL

Item 6 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 8 Jan 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene is a hospital ward. Two double beds take up most of the room. Two huge men take up most of the beds. They are like beached whales, like huge piles of flabby meat, they are the Texas Longhorns. They are also in a lot of pain due to the beating they took at the hands of Havoc on Meltdown.) Ace: Ow. Floyd: I hear that, brother. I hear that. Ace: I am in a lot of pain. I think they broke my ass. Floyd: And that is a lot of ass to break! Ace: True, true. (They both groan and try to find a comfortable place to lie.) Floyd: I'm hungry. Ace: I thought you were in pain! Floyd: I am but food would help to ease the pain. A nice cheeseburger or a couple of hot dogs. Mmm, tasty! Ace: Food is good, pain is bad. Damn but Havoc are tough mofos! Floyd: They may be the toughest dudes we've ever fought! (Ace muses over this comment.) Ace: I'm not sure, I mean the Patagonian Mecha-Giants were damn tough. They were even bigger than Havoc! Floyd: Yeah but we beat them easily, you remember at Summer Slaughterhouse, back in 1978. That was in Denver and I took a piss on the halfway line of Mile High Stadium. Ace: The Blood Butcherers were tough. Floyd: They were! They were damn tough. That was back in the day meant more than just being able to fight, when it meant sleeping on a bed of nails and eating only dry bread. Ace: Those were the days. In those days we were a force to be reckoned with, the greatesr team on the face of the earth. I mean, we'd beaten everyone else, no-one could stand up to us. Floyd: And now look at us. Injured by a pair of Greek gods, this really sucks, brother. (The two men lie still and contemplate.) Ace: We are getting too old for this s**t. Floyd: I hear that, brother, I hear that. (Fade to black.)
