Champ vs Champ

19 Feb 2003

By Mal Somers

Item 4 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 19 Feb 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
DJ Danny Mason – “Alright you’ve just been listening to a sixty minute music marathon on WTF 110.7 Radio – we play ALL REQUESTS ALL THE TIME. We just ended that set with “Back In Black” by AC/DC. Lets go to our next caller...Hi you’re on WTF 110.7 Radio – we play ALL REQUESTS ALL THE TIME! Whats your name caller?” Caller – “Hey there my name is Mal Somers!” DJ Danny Mason – “Hey Mal, old buddy...Totally awesome! And what are your two requests?” Somers – “Yeah can I have “Rivers of Babylon” by Boney M and “Two Out of Three Aint Bad” by Meatloaf?” DJ Danny Mason – “Hmmm Ooookayy..not the ususal hip stuff we like to play on WTF 110.7 Radio – we play ALL REQUESTS ALL THE TIME...but for the reigining WWA Rookie Champion, anything!” (The dulcet tones of Meatloaf play begin to play over the airwaves. Mal and his old buddy DJ Danny Mason talk about old times and then Danny asks Mal why did he request this odd coupling of songs.) Somers – “Actually Danny, can you lower the volume on the song so we can talk over the song to all the listeners out there?” DJ Danny Mason - “Sure thing.” (DJ Danny Mason adjusts the controls) DJ Danny Mason – “Alright, we are back on the air and we have a few questions for Mal Somers. Firstly, why the odd choice of songs?” Somers – “Good question Danny. I chose “Two out of three aint bad” because of my upcoming match at this Saturdays Fiesta. This Saturday... the main event...non title match...Champion versus Champion...Jimmi Zion versus Mal Somers...for the first time ever...in a two out of three falls match!” DJ Danny Mason – “Wow! That sure sounds like an early match of the year candidate!” Somers – “Damn straight Danny. I can say “damn” on the radio, can’t I Danny?” DJ Danny Mason – “Uh well you just did Mal, twice.” Somers – “Yes it is sure going to be a great match that’s going to have a lot of people taking notice.” (The song in the background nears the chorus) Somers – “And to paraphrase Meatloaf...” (sings along with the song) Somers – “Jimmi, don’t be sad, because two out of three aint bad...” (chuckles) Somers – “That’s right Jimmi, this Saturday, Mal Somers is going to win two out of the three falls. I saw you on Insider. And I'll grant you a few things; First, we both share a common enemy in The Cell. Second, you are indeed a talented individual. Lastly. That Mikalia is one HOTTIE. But despite all that, I have got to say, lucky my TV isn't a SMELLavision because that interview STANK! Whats this about beating my bloody a$$? I’ll save that for you and Lockdown when you “lock up in the ring!” DJ Danny Mason – “Excuse me Mal, this is a PG-13 rated show. Tone it down a little, buddy.” Somers – “Sorry Danny, Im just f**kiing pissed off! I was expecting this match to be a bit of a “friendly”. Put on a great match for all the MALcoholics out there. But then Jimmi appears on Insider, going on about how great he is, about how nothing can stop him from winning the match. Heck, he even compared himself to Helen Keller! Jimmi...Helen Keller may have been blind and deaf but unlike you, she wasn’t deaf blind and DUMB!!” “Listen to me Jimmi “Sky Flyin, death defying, toe tappin’, knee slappin’ jumpin and jiving, chumpion of CHUMPIONS, Daddy –O” Zion...you are nothing but the MORTAL one. A highlight reel of bloopers! The only thing you have encoded in your DNA is a few less chromosomes! You run your mouth about being X-Treme incarnate? Hell, Im the mother f**king co-founder of the EXTREME CORPORATION! You’d be nothing but a janitor there!” DJ Danny Mason – “Whoa settle down Mal! I’ll get fired if you keep going off like that. And you’re listening to WTF 110.7 Radio – we play ALL REQUESTS ALL THE TIME...that was Meatloaf with “Two out of three aint bad”. Next up we have “Rivers of Babylon” by Boney M. (the funky vibes of Rivers of Babylon start playing) DJ Danny Mason – "Mal, I’m know I'm going to regret asking you this...but whats the reason behind requesting this song?” Somers – “Nothing to worry about Danny. I’m sorry. I just got worked up. Im sure Jimmi is a nice guy. I mean he hates The Cell too, so he’s not all bad. I chose this classic for two reasons. Firstly, Jimmi annoyed me because he decided to “babble – on” about our match. Heh I crack myself up sometimes.” “But secondly, hold on...I’ll get to that in a second. I just want to say to Jimmi that I’m ready for our match. I’ve never been more ready. I’ve been forced off Fiesta by Cruz and Ive been champing at the bit to get back in and show the world what the Extreme Corporation is all about.” DJ Danny Mason – “OK Mal, we are going to have to wrap it up. Before we do...whats the other reason you chose this song? I’m curious.” Somers – “Oh yeah. I wouldn’t even have a clue where the Rivers of Babylon are...but I’m going to find out. And then on Sunday, the day after Fiesta, I want all the MALcoholics out there to come with me. Because... (clears his throat) Somers – (singing) “By the rivers of Babylon... there we sat down... ye-eah we wept... when we remembered Zion... “By the rivers of Babylon.. there we sat down... ye-eah we wept... when we remembered Zion...” “Heck, lets make Sunday the 23rd Jimmi’s Remembrance Day...because when all is said and done, and Ive won the match...Jimmi, you will be nothing but a distant memory!” DJ Danny Mason – Lets go to commercial... (Fade out)
