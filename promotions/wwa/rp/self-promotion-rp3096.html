Self Promotion

4 May 2006

By Tommy Starr

Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Fade in) V/O: It is almost midnight, and Tommy Starr, the WWA's self proclaimed Matinee Idol sits alone in his bedroom. Starr: Hey, it's a blue moon. Normally my bodacious boduoir would be full of Starr Struck hotties! V/O: Before Tommy Starr retires for the day, he likes to unwind, by sitting in front of his mirror and brushing his short cropped bleach blond hair. Starr: What's wrong with that? My follicles need fondling! V/O: Okay. So what has Tommy Starr been up to today? Movie shoots? Photo opportunities? Jetsetting? Starr: Well, lets see it was pretty busy. I have been preparing for the Bourbon Street Brawl after all. I woke up early, around ten thirty and got out of bed around a quarter past eleven. V/O: Right.... Starr: I had a croissant for breakfast and then a pedicure, that took me through til half twelve. Then a manicure, that took me through til just after one. V/O: Then an intensive six hour training session? Starr: I'm getting to it bucko. I then went to the tanning salon for an hour and had a light lunch with a couple of Starr Gazers. They paid the bill and then I went rollerblading in the park with Belial Kahn. V/O: Well I guess that counts as physical activity... Starr: Sure, I tied a rope around Kahn and let him drag me around for a while. It looked pretty strenuous, but I guess that's why he is The Beast and I am The Beauty! V/O: Tommy, excuse me but the WWA has employed me to provide a menacing voice over, showing the world how tough you are and how much butt you are going to kick this Sunday in the Bourbon Street Brawl. Starr: Well, I don't need you for that! I mean look at me, I am freaking Tommy Starr! The man that single handedly beat Sergei Jamusellson, the man that single handedly answered the call of the S.O.S and kicked them both a new girlfriend. I don't need a kooky voice over or arty farty segment to show the world how tough I am, I don't need the smoke and mirrors that comes with that. V/O: I was just... Starr: Shut it jerky! I mean you are so ugly you are consigned to doing voice overs for the rest of your days. If you were ever to stand in front of the camera the lens would break! V/O: Well I never! Starr: ...get any booty? With a face like that I wouldn't be surprised. Me on the other hand, I'm the guy the girls want to see and the men want to be. This Sunday on the Streets of New Orleans, Tommy Starr is going to show the WWA exactly what he is all about...and that is, making a scene. And think about this for a second, all you peons at home. Even though I am THAT good and THAT awesome, Tracer Bullet has seen fit to include my partner in crime Belial Kahn in the match. We are the only tag team in the Brawl, and that has got to have a lot of people donning the brown underpants this Sunday. You know what they say Shaman? You know what they say Komodo Dragon? You know what they say Proteus? Divided we stand...United we BRAWL! V/O: That's pretty clever! Starr: Pity your parents weren't...if they were you wouldn't be alive! V/O: ... Starr: That's about the best thing you've said all night, keep it up. This Sunday, I am going to give Armando the TommyHawk... I'm going to get it on with Bracer Toilet's wife... I'm going to Denrol on Denrol... I'm going to unmask the Estinkma... Gay-Led is going to get dropped on his ugly head... I'm going to give Hack Griffiths the Stage Fright Texas Cloverleaf... I'm going to take Dave Harley-Ever-Wins on a Walk of Fame... I'm going to make Mick Vein bleed... I'm going to shove my hand up Muppet's o-ring, Jim Henson style... I'm going to go psycho all over Stephanie Murphy... I'm going to rain punches on Winters... I'm going to turn Fanny Vista into a Sista... and Kylie James is going to get the Shooting Starr Press... V/O: ...and then what, Tommy, tell us! Starr: I'm going to let Damian Thorne know what it is like to be STARR STRUCK!!! V/O: You ARE the man Tommy! Starr: You don't need to tell me that. V/O: And so as another action packed day ends for Tommy Starr, the other WWA Superstars must endure another sleepless night, as they come one day closer to defeat at the hands of Tommy Starr at the Bourbon Street Brawl.
