Animal Instincts

24 Jan 2003

By Enigma

Item 5 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 24 Jan 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
{We open up and all we can see is darkness. A light illuminates the area and we can vaguely see that there is a wrestling ring set up and we are high up in the rafters. At least the camera is. The light that has turned on has shown us that there is a steel cage being set up in the ring. There are men working on putting up the side panels. The camera continues to look at the men setting up the ring.} These are the men that are setting up my element. These men are working diligently to create the one area of the human existence where there are no rules. There are no guidelines. There are no laws in the steel cage. There is nothing short of anarchy in the steel cage. There is nothing that anyone can do to stop the onslaught in the steel cage. No man has ever been the same after stepping out of the steel cage. This is an animal’s element. This is where men become animals. You need to be a hungry animal in the steel cage. If you are not prepared to let the animal out inside you then you might as well not show up because you will be eaten alive. Rage in the Cage will be the event where I become an animal. The match at Rage in the Cage to Unify the WWA Cruiserweight and Extreme Titles is going to be a blood bath. This match is going to be like throwing one small piece of meat to three starving wolves. These three men are all hungry for the titles. The only question that needs to be answered is which one of us is the hungriest. Which one of us is willing to do anything it takes to win the title. Which one of us is willing to die in the ring to get the coveted gold. Chance, are you willing to die in that ring? I don’t think you are willing to die for these titles. We will see though. Zion are you willing to die in the ring? Are you willing to give up your life with your wife for the chance to win these titles? I am willing to give it all up in the cage. I am willing to lay everything on the line in the ring. I am willing do what it takes to get back my titles. I am the one that will do it all. I am willing to die in the cage to get my titles. I am willing to become an animal to gain back my titles. I am willing to spill my blood all over the ring. I don’t care what happens afterwards, I just care about those twenty to thirty minutes in the ring where everything will be decided once and for all. Who will walk out of the ring the champion? Who will be victorious? I plan on being that man. {The camera turns around and we see Enigma sitting in the rafters surveying the floor. He is wearing a black trench coat with a black bandana and black raybands. He is just sitting there} Zion, I have heard that you have agreed with me that you are not a champion. I am glad you feel this way but the way I see it, it does not matter who holds the titles walking into the cage with the titles. The only thing that I am concerned about is who walks out of the ring with the titles. You and Chance are not full champions and I know that both of you feel that you were shortchanged. Both of you feel you deserve the titles. That’s good because you two will be hungry in the ring in Calgary. The way I see it is this match will come down to the man with more desire. Zion, you think you will finally stop me? Whether or not I win the match, you WILL NEVER STOP ME! I cannot be stopped. Haven’t you realized that? Everytime something happens that may in fact stop me, I make my way through it. I am a survivor. I will not be stopped. Especially at Rage in the Cage. There is no way that you are going to stop me on the night where I regain my glory. I will become the Extreme and Cruiserweight Champion for a second time. I am glad that you are willing to go to hell and back to get these titles. Because the simple fact is that is exactly what your are going to have to do. I am the one that will fight you through hell, heaven, anywhere. This is what I live for. I live to inflict pain on men like you Zion. This is why I am in the House of Pain. We are a group of men that love to punish everyone. I am a going to be a bloodthirsty lion at Rage in the Cage. You are going to be my prey. I am going to tear you limb from limb. I am going to selectively tear you apart. I am going to methodical. I am going to take no prisoners. Zion, you might think I have been ruthless and diabolic before, I don’t know, but one thing is for sure. What I have done so far is nothing compared to what I am going to do at Rage in the Cage. You will wish that you were back getting beat by Lockdown and Leviathan after I finish you off. You will be lucky to walk out on your own accord. {Enigma pauses for a moment and looks down at the workers who appear to have stopped for the moment and have two sides of the cage already up} Soon the construction will be finished. It appears that they have made it strong. Let me move onto my other opponent in the match tonight, the man that has experienced first hand the wrath of myself. You have seen what I can accomplish. You are the one that most understands what you two are getting yourselves into. Because Chance, unlike Jimmi Zion, you have been on the receiving end of a ton of punishment. I am the reason that you limp the majority of the time. I am the one that made you a weaker man. I am the one that has slowed your high flying, high impact moves. I have done all this. At Rage in the Cage there will be more of the same action. Chance, at Rage in the Cage I am going to look to finish you off. Chance, I plan on ending this in a cage. {The scene cuts black. After a few seconds or so, we are down in the arena and Enigma is standing at the top of the ramp and begins to walk towards the ring. Enigma slides underneath the ropes on the side where the cage has not been set up. The workers just walk. Enigma goes to the corner of the two pieces that are up. He begins to climb} There is only one way to win the match. There is only one way that you can be deemed the winner. That way is to climb over the top rope and out of the cage. The way to win is by incapacitating your opponent. I will not allow any of the other two men to keep me down long enough to be able to climb out of the ring. The only way that will happen is if you kill me in the ring. The only way I will not stop you guys is if you kill me. Like I said earlier, this match will come down who is willing to die in the ring. I am willing to die in the ring. You guys will see what I am going to do. I am going to climb to the top. I am going to hit you two with the Fall from Grace and I am going to ascend to greatness. At Rage in the Cage I will beat you two and take what is rightfully mine. The titles. NOTHING CAN STOP ME NOW {Enigma is at the top of the cage and folds his arms over as we fade to ?}
