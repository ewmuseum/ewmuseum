Fiesta

13 Jan 2003

By Enigma

Item 5 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 13 Jan 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
{Darkness} Oh what a night. Mid January in 03 I rocked Fiesta. {Enigma enters into our picture and we see him with a smug look on his face.} First, James Curzon, nothing personal buddy I wanted to show you that you do not mess with my partner. You do not mess with my partner. I don’t like Zion but if we are going to team together on Monday I want him in great shape. However it appears that Lockdown and Leviathan don’t seem to realize that they should not have went after my partner. I promise you this that if you try that crap again, you will not be spared. Just a warning. {Enigma pauses} Monday Night, we have the tag team match of the year….well maybe not, but Jarod Crow will step into the ring. How’s your head? Jarod? How’s your knee? Chance? Jarod, you stated that you could protect Chance. You said that nothing was going to happen at Fiesta. YOU WERE WRONG I happened at Fiesta. I took you both out. Nothing is going to change. On Monday, there will be no mercy. There will be no regrets. Chance, you will be shot down like so many Falcons have been before. You can’t fly. I have grounded you. DONE! You are not as strong as me. Crow is weak. Chance you are weak. I am strong. Zion will do his job and you will lose. Chance you and Crow are going down. NOTHING CAN STOP ME NOW!! {fade out}
