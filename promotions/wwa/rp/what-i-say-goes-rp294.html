What I say, goes.

25 Jul 2003

By Joey K

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I spent most of this week with, in my eyes, the WWA's best referee Larry Crosier. I'm special referee for Harley's match on Fiesta. He's got alot coming his way. I haven't decided who I will let win the match yet, I'm not fond of either of them. But a Harley with no title, is a Harley with no point. If there isn't anything to gain, why should I bother fighting him? Anyways, Larry taught me the basics. i've only ever done one guest ref. match, I was 19 and I got it all wrong, the crowd laughed, the guys backstage in the fed i was in laughed. I thought, 'F*ck them!' And look at me now, in the best fed around, and the most talented here. As I sit in the airport boarding lounge, waiting to be called, I'm going through saturdays match in my mind. What I say, goes, i can shape the match how I want, get involved as much as I like. Harley won't know whats hit him. Literally. I also have to organise the next installment of K Moments. Heh, the plan worked just fine last week, I came on, gave the crowd some excitmeent, and Harley lost the title, and all because of me. ABC, easy as one, two, three. 1, 2, 3. ONE TWO THREE The more I think about it, the more enjoyable it becomes, and the more I want to put the three count on Dave Harley, having him lose the match andleaving me free to kick his ass even more. DAVE Vs ME ONE TWO THREE DON'T MESS WITH JOEY.
