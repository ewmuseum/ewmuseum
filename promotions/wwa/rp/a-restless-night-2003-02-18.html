A Restless Night

18 Feb 2003

By Tracer Bullet & Denrol

Item 3 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 18 Feb 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
DAY 5 2:10 AM (The scene opens inside the leBeaux house. It's the middle of the night, yet Alexandra Korth is up and sitting ina chair, watching the TV with the volume down low. On her lap is Extreme and by her feet is US. The other cats must be elsewhere around the house.) US: I still want my catnip sonny. (Extreme seems very content to just sit and be petted. But he still finds time to disagree with US.) Extreme: My catnip, you mean. Daddy's alive, you lost the bet. Didn't happen before three days was up, I lost the bet. We both lost, nobody gets anything. Piss off. US: The bet wasn't if Tracer would succeed, only if Tracer will try. He did, we all saw it happen. You're not going to screw me just because you're cute. Extreme: Wouldn't dream of it. And you notice he never touched Daddy, either. Looked like he really wanted to, but he never attempted anything. Do or do not, there is no try. US: That's the last time I make a bet with you, cheater. And I will find that stash. Extreme: Good damn luck. You're more than welcome to try, but even I can't find it half the time. Granted, the times I can't find it is when I've been using the stuff just prior, but still. US: A-Hole. (Footsteps can be heard on the staircase and a somewhat tired looking Tracer Bullet enters the living room.) TB: God, I really wish I hadn't... (He notices Alexandra sitting up with Extreme.) TB: What are you doing up? Alexandra: Couldn't sleep. Decide to check out the lastest infomercials. You? TB: Rolled over in bed, landed on a wet hairball. (Extreme glares at US as if to say, "Now I know damn well I didn't leave it THERE." US grins sardonically back at him.) Alexandra: Ah. Welcome to the family. TB: Something on your mind? You don't quite seem your usual abrasive self. Alexandra: I'm sorry. Welcome to the family dickhead. (Tracer chuckles.) TB: That's better... (He sits down in another chair a few feet away.) Alexandra: You know, you guys have been real accepting of our presenses here. TB: Well, Gordon is a close friend, regardless of his recent leanings. (US walks up to Tracer's feet. Tracer picks him up onto his lap and begins scratching his ears.) TB: Not only that, but who can say no to these cats? But of course... there's a much bigger reason behind this. Alexandra: That being? TB: Jackson Kraven. Alexandra: You've lost me. (Tracer looks directly at Alexandra.) TB: Somehow, I don't believe I have. Just think about it a minute. Alexandra: Because you think Kraven may have pushed Gordon off the edge and you want to see if you could bring him back? (Tracer sighs and continues petting the cat on his lap.) TB: That's not it... that's not it at all. (He looks back at Alex with a very soulful expression on his face.) TB: It was really more what he did to YOU, Alex. Alexandra: Then you have me officially lost. TB: I can't really say I blame you for blocking that out. And I don't especially want to go into too much detail, it's painful for both of us. Rather, let me try it this way... the baby's still healthy, right? Alexandra: Yes, I remember the incident you're refering to, but I fail to see how it involve you. And yes the baby or as some in this country say, "fetus" is fine. TB: Of course you don't see how it involves me. I don't feel personally responsible. But you see, there's no way I'd turn someone away from my door that had that happen to them. (Alexandra stands up, causing Extreme to leap to the floor.) Alexandra: I don't need or want the likes of you feeling sorry for me. (Tracer doesn't rise. Instead he casts his head down to look at the cat on his lap as he keeps petting it.) TB: Doesn't matter. Whether you want it or not, whether I want it or not... that's what's happening. How could I not feel terrible after witnessing something like that? Alexandra: Fine. But I still don't want your pity. I have had enough of you wrestlers as it is. I would ratehr not exist at all in your eyes. (Tracer puts the cat on the floor and slowly stands up to meet Alexandra's gaze.) TB: Well, that's your choice. Just like it was your choice to get involved in the business in the first place. You know just as well as I do that you could have gone on living quietly without having anything to do with us aside from being married to Gordon. You did it for a couple years as it was. Tell me... what made you get involved in the first place? Alexandra: I did it so I could be with Gordon. So I wouldn't have to sit at home, worried about what he was doing and who he was doing it with. He always told me what a great bunch of people his fellow wrestlers are. I was dumb enough to believe him. TB: I wish I knew what to tell you. We've been quite antagonistic to each other in the past, so I can't argue your point of view for you. But let me ask you something... what is it you had against us in the first place? Alexandra: Us as in wrestlers or Us as in you and Missy? TB: Either. But particularly the latter. After all, I've got problems with quite a few other wrestlers as is. Alexandra: I don't have a problem with Missy, even if she did sleep with my husband. But I do have a huge issue with you, because you're one of the reasons Gordon won't leave. (Tracer winces visibly.) TB: I'd prefer not to be reminded of that, thank you. But I'm one of the reasons he won't leave... you don't think his thirst for revenge has a little something to do with it? Alexandra: He didn't come back to just get Kraven. He came back because he wanted to tag with you. TB: Really kind of touching, I think. But I suppose I can see your point. You want him out, don't you? You want the two of you to just stay home and live out the rest of your lives in peace, don't you? Alexandra: Not stay home. But I'm sick fo watching matches like Cell in a Frozen Hell and wondering how long until he gets crippled. (Tracer nods grimly.) TB: I understand. That match was absolutely brutal. But when you hate someone that much, I suppose it's easy to forget about your own well-being in an attempt to rob them of theirs. Alexandra: the other side is that you're no better than the rest. (Alexandra sits back down. Extreme takes a moment, but jumps back on her lap to get petted. Tracer remains standing, his back to her.) TB: What do you mean by that? Alexandra: You decked a man for calling YOUR wife a whore. But I'm suppose to sit there and listen to the comments about me without doing anything. Comments like "the village bicycle" or as you so nicely put it at Labor Day Brawl, just a "bitch". (Tracer sighs, still with his back turned to Alex, and crosses his arms, his elbows resting in his hands. He tilts his head up slightly.) TB: You know well enough that you've carried on just as much. And you did have a real attitude about yourself. But if it means anything at all now, months after the fact... (He turns around, his eyes closed in such a way that you can almost see the angst flowing forth from him at the moment.) TB: I'm sorry. Alexandra: (sighs) Thank you. Now what is wrong with you? I'm the one who suppose to be having the mood swings. TB: I already told you... it all goes back to that incident. I'm hoping to have children of my own someday, and I just couldn't take seeing something like that. Even done to someone I used to have so much antipathy for. And I know you don't want my pity, but like it or not, you're getting it. Alexandra: So you're telling me that the whole point of letting us into your house was what Kraven did to me last month? TB: Not the whole point, but yeah, a good lot of it. Gordon's a friend and all, but I still wouldn't feel right letting you guys stay for a whole week if you hadn't been having some extraordinarily bad fortune lately. Alexandra: Gee, thanks. As I mentioned at the door, it wasn't my idea and frankly I would rather not be here. TB: Don't get me wrong. You guys are good people. But it's not as if we had a guest room all made up before you came knocking on our door last week. Alexandra: So we agree the only person completely happy about this is Gordon? TB: Yes, ma'am. Despite how much Missy and I like cats, well... we're used to a few things we really can't do with visitors staying around so long. Gah, it's been almost a week, for God's sake. A man can only take so much. Alexandra: (chuckles) I don't think Gordon would even notice, he's a deep sleeper. TB: Clearly you're not, though. Alexandra: I can't be because of my job, no. But those types of sounds don't disturb me. TB: Umm... no comment... (Tracer rolls his eyes innocently.) TB: Anyway... how have you been holding up this week? I know how it is to be plucked from where you live and plopped down in an entirely unfamiliar location. Alexandra: I'm fine. I'm used to it. The cats aren't, but they seem to be adjusting. (Tracer sits back down in the chair he vacated a few minutes ago.) TB: Some of them seem to be adjusting a little too much, though. Seems like the one in your lap is the only entirely well-behaved one of the bunch. Alexandra: Yeah, he's quite the suck-up, isn't he? (glances at TV) Oooo, the Ultimate Juicer. Sounds useful. TB: Isn't that Steve Corino's nickname? Alexandra: While I'm sure my husband would have found that funny, the reference is over my head. TB: Oh, sorry. Natural instinct to make a wrestling joke when set up so well. Alexandra: So it's true you were once a private eye? TB: Absolutely. Not really as good a one as I was made out to be on my arrival here, but I made good money. I know it was said that not a one of my cases went unsolved, but most of those involved tailing the husband of some paranoid woman. Usually the same one, frankly. Alexandra: And your markmanship? TB: That's 100% true. Not a complete natural like I may have claimed in the past, though. I worked hard to get so good at that. Alexandra: (chuckles) What do you carry? TB: Name it. If it's concealable, I own one. Alexandra: Than what's your personal favorite? TB: Wait a minute... you're not trying to get information on me that can be used against me in court, are you? (Alexandra laughs, startling Extreme, who leaps off her lap as a reflex.) Alexandra: Don't we have quite an ego? No, I'm asking out of curiousity, as a fellow law enforcer. TB: Well, quite frankly, I do have a couple that were obtained under less-than-ideal circumstances. But since you're just asking, I have to say that my favorite is my Desert Eagle. Can't be a gun collector and a Trigun fan without one. Alexandra: Nice, through a bit too Hollywood for me. TB: Hollywood? Trigun is pure Tokyo, baby! Alexandra: I meant it's the gun used in all the movies. TB: Is it now? My specific one is more an AGL Arms .45 Long Colt. EF Auto Ejecting, Double Action. Kind of a custom job, really. I wanted as close to Vash's gun as I could get. Alexandra: Vash? TB: Vash the Stampede. Main character of Trigun. Guy I got the idea to wear that red leather trench coat from back last year, before I picked my old gimmick back up again. Alexandra: Ahh. Not a bad gun. While I have to use the Glock 23 .40 for work, I prefer to use Dirty Harry's S&W Model 29 .44 Magnum. TB: Nice. And you said and mine was too Hollywood. *smirk* Alexandra: Got to love something that does that much damage. And I'm sure I'm a better shot than you are. TB: Try me sometime. Alexandra: How about right now? I can get us into the New Orleans FBI Office's firing range. (Tracer checks his watch.) TB: At 3 a.m.? Though I do really like that Matchbox 20 song, I don't think a tired, depressed, former alcoholic waving a gun around in the middle of the night is going to be viewed very favorably. Alexandra: You'd be surprised how busy the place gets. Anyway, it's easier to get time in the middle of the night. TB: Well, all right. I just have to get a few things first... (Tracer walks out of the room and comes back seconds later with 8x10 glossy photographs of Shane Dorian and Enigma.) TB: Right, I'm ready now. Alexandra: Let me get fully dressed and I'll see you at the Dart. TB: Got it. And Alex? Alexandra: Yes? (Tracer stands up and walks over to her. He hugs her tenderly.) TB: You're free to drop by any time you need someone to talk to. (He lets go.) TB: You'd be surprised how sympathetic I can be in the right situation. Alexandra: I think I'm scared now. (A few moments later the two leave. Denrol and Missy enter the living room.) Denrol: Those two make such a cute little couple, don't they? Missy: I'm actually quite glad they made up. You know how hard it was on him to be at such odds with his best friend's wife? Denrol: Well things could have been worse. Missy: How's that? Denrol: Well he could have been at odds with his best friend's wife while his best friend at one time slept his wife while being married to the woman he can't get along with. Oh wait... Missy: Yeah, so I guess it couldn't be much worse then, could it? And I'll thank you and Alexandra to stop bringing that up, despite the fact that it was before he and I were even dating. Denrol: What can I say? I'm an asshole. Missy: Just as long as you don't start singing it, let's leave that at "Yeah." (Denrol shrugs as the scene fades to black.)
