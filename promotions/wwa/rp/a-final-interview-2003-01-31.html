A Final Interview

31 Jan 2003

By Denrol

Item 4 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 31 Jan 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Scene opens with Wallace Coleman and Denrol sitting across from each other. They are inside the wrestling ring setup in the Saddledome in Calgary. Crew members are running around outside the ring, setting up equipment for the pay-per-view on Sunday. The two men are sitting on folding chairs. Coleman is wearing a suit, holding a clipboard with notes on it. Denrol is dressed in grey sweats, including a WWA sweatshirt.) Coleman: I am here with Denrol, less than 24 hours before the fateful Cell in a Frozen Hell Match that will shown to a worldwide audience on Sunday at Rage in the Cage. Thank you for this interview Denrol. Denrol: You're welcome of course. Coleman: You seem very calm for someone who is about to go into what can only be described as a violent, sick match. Denrol: I am calm because I need to be. I can't let my emotions overtake me at this moment. I will show my anger on Saturday. Coleman: But you are angry. Denrol: How can I not be? Look at what Kraven did. Attacks on me are understandable. This is wrestling and guys get beat up all the time. But Kraven didn't just go after me. He assaulted my wife and the unborn child that lies within her womb. He went after my family. My anger is immeasurable. Coleman: Your anger is why you designed this match? Denrol: Yes. At the time it was anger about how he tried to end my career. Now my anger is tenfold and this match will help me punish him. Coleman: Do you expect to win? Denrol: I don't care. This isn't about winning or losing. It's about hurting Kraven. If my hands get raised, fine. If I lose, that's fine too. I just want to make him pay for his sins. Coleman: Denrol, how is Alexandra? Denrol: She is okay. She's been released from the hospital and she will be there to see the match live. The child is fine, no signs of permanent damage. Thanks for asking. Coleman: Of course. You recently had some words with Kevin Straves. Any new thoughts now that you have time to reflect? Denrol: No. Kevin is nothing but a child, that whines for attention. I decided to be generous and give him some. He knows he is a nothing, so he thought he would steal some of my limelight by sticking his nose where it doesn't belong. It's not accident he chose Rage in the Cage to return. It's the biggest show of the year and there's nothing Kevin doesn't like more than be in the biggest spotlight there is. Whether or not he deserves it. Coleman: Why don't you see him an ally? He hates Kraven just like you do. Denrol: With friends like that, who needs enemies? Kevin is a parasite and one that is lethal. He destroyed a partner of his, he put himself over multiple men in a pointless match, and he even wrecked a federation, just because he thought it would make him look good. I could join with him yes, but I don't need to. I know what my goal is and I know I will accomplish it. Kevin will have nothing left to deal with once I'm finished with Kraven. Coleman: So I guess the most obvious final question is... are you ready for tomorrow? Denrol: Yes. I'm not sure I'll be able to sleep. Tomorrow I settle all debts. I will go out there, step into that cage, with one mission. To destroy Jackson Kraven. The wait is almost over. Coleman: Last thoughts? (Denrol turns to the camera.) Denrol: I hate you Kraven. Tomorrow my hate will destroy you. (Scene fades to black.)
