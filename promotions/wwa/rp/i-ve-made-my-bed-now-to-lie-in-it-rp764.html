I've made my bed...Now To Lie In It...

27 Nov 2003

By Dave Harley

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[We open in a bar somewhere in New York. Sat at the table is Dave Harley and Mal Somers. Each man has a beer in hand. Sitting in between both men is Rookie.] Harley: WOO! Birthday Bash is gonna rock! X Corp’s going in with more force than ever! Somers: Funny though, I haven’t heard from our new team mate Venny in a few days. Must be busy I guess… Harley: Of course he is, Chairboy! He’s getting ready to take part in the greatest main event in WWA history! A main event in which he will defend his GenX Title! Somers: And where I’ll get the IC Title! Harley: What?! C’mon bud, you’re not still talkin’ about that are ya? And besides, I think the World Title will look much better on you! Don’t you think so, Rookie? [Mal stares at the silent but violent one, then turns and grimaces at Dave.] Somers: You hear that Harley? Rookie’s right! Harley: Huh? Somers: Like it or not Dave, I’m leaving Birthday Bash with the IC Title! Harley: Stop listening to that WWA Title dropout! Somers: He’s not a dropout! He retired! Harley: Alright, alright, let’s just calm down. We’re buds remember? Somers: [sighs] You’re right, I’m sorry…You want another beer? Harley: Sure thing amigo! [Mal walks over to the bar, leaving Dave sat staring at the macaroni and wool facial features of Rookie.] Harley: …So what exactly is your problem? Huh? Don’t think I didn’t hear those comments you made on ‘Countdown’. Just what exactly are you trying to pull? Me and Mal were buds long before you came along my friend and friends we’re going to stay! This Sunday I’m defending my IC Title against half the freakin’ roster and the guy I’m most worried about is my own partner! It shouldn’t be that way! [Dave turns and sees Mal glance over from the bar. He smiles and gives Mal a thumbs up before turning back to Rookie.] Harley: [sighs] What the hell am I doing? You’re a freakin’ belt! You can’t talk! It’s my fault isn’t it? If I hadn’t have challenged Enigma I’d have never got in this situation to start with! Yep, I guess I’ve made my bed…now it’s time to lie in it… [Mal returns with two beers. He hands one to his compadre.] Somers: What were you guys talking about? Harley: Oh, nothing much bud. Nothing at all. [Fade out.]
