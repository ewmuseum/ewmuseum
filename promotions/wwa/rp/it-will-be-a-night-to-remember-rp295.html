It will be a night to remember

25 Jul 2003

By Enigma

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
“What makes an event historical? An everlasting moment in the eyes of everyone who was fortunate to witness it? Is it the fact that it can not be duplicated? Or the fact that no one will forget the moment? One of the key elements appears to be a lot of pain and hardship caused. Most of the memorable moments in life have happened with pain and suffering. September 11th, Pearl Harbour, Normandy. All of these events are everlasting and no one, past or present or future will forget it.” {We hear these words being spoken from the darkness. Out of the darkness comes the man who lives and desires the darkness, that being Enigma. He stands staring directly at the camera.} “Those moments are some that will never be forgotten. This brings me to the first ever Fiesta after the roster split. The Forsaken Souls are intact. This coming Fiesta will be a night that will not be forgotten. It is not only because Wes Sanders and I are going to show Chance and Mal Somers who the best are. It’s not because Joey K is going to unveil another installment of ‘K Moments’, it is because we are going to dominate.” “The Forsaken Souls are going to prove to the world that we are the strongest and most formidable force in the World Wrestling Alliance. We will prove that fact on Fiesta.” Monday will be a night that some will remember…. For others… It will be a night that they will never forget… NOTHING CAN STOP ME NOW You shall all Fall From Grace… {fade}
