Rookie Ramblings

18 Feb 2003

By Mal Somers

Item 2 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 18 Feb 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Scene – The back of an opened laptop computer. The metallic silver of the machine shimmers briefly in the sunlight. The camera zooms out to reveal Mal Somers, WWA Rookie Champion and co-founder of Extreme Corporation looking frustrated. He is sitting at a large desk which is bare except for the laptop and a half glass of water. The camera moves around behind his shoulder. He is surfing the internet. His large, fingers, some calloused from years of wrestling are having difficulty manoevering on the tiny touchpad. Somers is currently on www.wwa-online.com in the forums section. A poll has been posted: “Which is the worst tag team of all time” KroniK – 67% Men on a Mission – 18% Island Boys 8% Other 7% (Mal smirks as he clicks “Other”. He types and the following appears on the screen.) “I think The Cell are the worst tag team of all time. The only thing Brutal about them is their ringwork. I reckon the Xcorp should kick their a$$es! Oh and get a title shot real soon.” Signed A.N.onymous (Mal smiles again and clicks “Send Message”. He smiles until his message is posted. Underneath “A.N.onymous” his saved signature appears; “MAL SOMERS WWA Rookie Champion Member of Extreme Corporation.” Somers – “F%@king computers!!” (Somers picks up the laptop and throws it against the wall. He stomps on it repeatedly until it is in many little pieces. He takes a deep breath and drinks from his glass of water.) Somers – “Hey there sports fans. I wont talk too loudly, Im still feeling the effects of my celebrations earlier in the week. Strictly water for me all week. I’m NEVER drinking alcohol again!” (Sits in his chair) Somers – “As most of you would be aware, Dave and I have got our issues with The Cell. More on that later in the week! However, earlier in the week, it dawned on me that I am the longest serving WWA Rookie Champion of all time. Over THREE MONTHS now, since BIRTHDAY BASH in fact.” (smiles) “Now that is a real honour and a privilege for me. Guess which other WWA superstar has held their belt since then? That’s right. IAN freaking TENSE! I must be doing something right! And now that I am allowed to actually DEFEND the belt on Fiesta, things are getting interesting.” (chuckles) “Ender...Damian Ryan. This Saturday, on Fiesta, you two will be competing in an “I QUIT” match. The last time I was in one of those, I was watching Leviathan wrestle in the EWA and I HAD TO QUIT watching! And I was just backstage waiting for my match! But seriously, I will also be watching your match with great interest. To see if you are in my league.” “You see, as I say time and time again...it is my goal to be here in five years time and STILL hold the Rookie Belt. Plus whatever else comes my way. That’s how much it means to me. I want to be known as the veteran who has the rookie belt. That way, all the MALcoholics out there who come along to those MALcoholics Anonymous meetings that you like to call WWA Events can keep seeing me do my thing.” (puts the belt out flat on the table.) “Understand? 1,000,000 MALcoholics can’t be wrong!” (Fade Out)
