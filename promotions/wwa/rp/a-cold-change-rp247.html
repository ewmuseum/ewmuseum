A cold change

16 Jul 2003

By Leviathan

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Leviathan stands in front of the WWA logo, he is wearing white designer pants and a white t-shirt with the words, DEVIL’S OWN! DEAL WITH IT! in red writing. Beside him is Wallace Coleman looking rather anxious, somewhat scared.) Wallace: I’m Wallace Coleman for WWA Insider. Standing with me is Leviathan. Leviathan can I get your thoughts on the IDR PPV? How do you feel about what happen? (Leviathan raises a hand to his chin and rubs it thoughtfully.) Leviathan: To be honest Wallace, I would say my emotions are mixed. First I achieve what I want of Kenneth, a US title shot but then eliminated in a cowardly way by Malcolm in the actual rumble. The winner, Nicholas has earned my respect, as has Kenneth with his show at Bourbon Street. This means only that they are worthy of my attention and that of the Devil’s Own. As for their ally, Malcolm, it shows what I have believed all along. Destroying his existence will only make the WWA stronger. Sure those mindless plebs believe he is a good guy. But lets put what he did at IDR in perspective. When Tommy and I were removing that zealot “Father Denrol” from the rumble, Malcolm attacked us from behind, a coward’s act by a true coward. This war between us Malcolm is far from over. (Wallace squirms as Leviathan’s face contorts with anger.) Wallace: What about Meltdown? The upcoming roster split. Leviathan: Wallace, it doesn’t matter whatever the powers that be decide. It doesn’t matter to the Devil’s Own which roster we end up on. It doesn’t matter who we have to face. The Devil’s Own are as my associate Lockdown put it, are taking control. Conquering each level as we see fit. When I look at my cadre called the Devil’s Own, I see brutal chaos and it is a sight to behold. Lockdown, “Psycho” Steven Murphy, Tommy Starr and Imperceptus are a formidable council of war. The fate of the WWA is sealed with our victim’s blood. Obstacles may arise but they shall be overcome by force. (Wallace takes a large gulp and dares to interrupt.) Wallace: I guess Grunge and the US title is one such obstacle? (Leviathan snatches the microphone and glares at Wallace.) Leviathan: How nice of you to remind me of Kenneth, Wallace. Kenneth, it is ironic that you are now allied with the man who was amongst the first who dared to speak up against the Cell. For he should be able to explain to you the danger you now face. Nicholas has tasted our wrath and his survival was only due to our concern with destroying X-corp. And this coming Meltdown, Kenneth you will realize two things when facing Leviathan. Fear and respect of the green-eyed monster. Fear, that each time you step inside the ring you may pay dearly for your bravery. Respect of the damage and brutality, Leviathan is capable of. Respect that your uncontrollable comedic routine is stopped and you must focus on the task at hand. A cold change is coming to the WWA and it will not be stopped. DEVIL’S OWN! DEAL WITH IT!
