Who are you?

11 Feb 2003

By Damian Thorne

Item 5 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 11 Feb 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Who is it that you think you are? You think you're everything I want to be? A representation of my hard work? Well, you're wrong..... You sit there, staring at me, reminding me over and over what it took to get you You constantly tear the scab from the wound on my soul, you drive it home over and over I'm a monster.. So you keep reminding me I'm fuelled by my hatred... So you keep reminding me I'm evil... Me? I'm evil? No, you're evil I ended up with you, a blight on my soul, because of the bad things I did. I hate you You disgust me You make my flesh crawl What should I call you.......? My ..SLUT? No My Whore? No... MY BITCH....*quietly* yeah, thats it, my bitch A bitch, that is what you are after all. You hang around with anyone willing to pay a high enough price to be seen with you. But, unlike them, there is one difference with me I don't need you, I choose to be with you. I chose you, you didn't choose me. I'm not like Dorian, or Tracer, or Kash. I don't have to have you by my side. *the scene fades in, to the WWA US title sitting on a dining chair, it's plate glinting in the firelight* But now that I have you, MY BITCH, you'll have to fight to be free of me. I'm in control now...ME. You belong to me, not the other way around. *a hand reaches out and grabs the belt, throwing it across the floor, it's plate skittering on the hardwood floor* Get out of my sight!
