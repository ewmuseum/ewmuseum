Sympathy for the Devil Part 1

24 Jul 2003

By Leviathan

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Leviathan stands in his opulent formal area next to sound system that would make Grunge drool with envy. He turns to his cohorts of criminality. Lockdown relaxes with his feet place on a stool and beer in hand. “Psycho” Steven Murphy enters the room with beers in hand and tosses one to Lockdown, then humorously motions for Imperceptus to have one.) Psycho: As if! F*ck’n mute. (Although not visible seen, by body motion alone, you can tell Imperceptus is not amused by Psycho.) Lockdown: Good one Steve, but next get the ones at the f*ck’n back this one is not cold enough. And you could have got Levi’s imported sh*t, he pays enough for so we should drink the f*cker. Psycho: Alright, f*ck get it yourself next time. Where is that little f*cker Starr? Leviathan: I sent him on an errand. And Steven, please do not disrespect Imperceptus again or you will answer to me. Psycho: F*ck just chill Levi, just having a joke. What are you up to? Lockdown: He’s putting some music on, I hope. Leviathan: I am, in honour of Kenneth. He seems to gain inspiration from music, so I thought I might try the same. Some Stones maybe good to hear. (Psycho looks at Lockdown and Lockdown motions for Psycho to zip it.) Leviathan: Hear we are. (The crisp sound reverberates through out the room. The music seems loud but does not drown out conversation.) **Please allow me to introduce myself** **I’m a man of wealth and taste** Leviathan: So very true. **I’ve been around for a long, long year** **Stole many a man’s soul and faith** Lockdown: I F*ck’n hear that! Leviathan: Indeed we have Lockdown. **And I was ’round when jesus Christ** **Had his moment of doubt and pain Leviathan: Substitute the preacher’s son, Kenneth. **Made damn sure that pilate** **Washed his hands and sealed his fate** Leviathan: As does anybody that dares to disrespect the Devil’s Own. Lockdown: Amen to that Levi. Psycho: Damn F*ck’n straight! **Pleased to meet you** **Hope you guess my name** Leviathan: Kenneth won’t be forgetting it. Lockdown: And this What’s a F*cking name! Psycho: Same goes for Chucky. **But what’s puzzling you** **Is the nature of my game** Leviathan: No more games, Kenneth. DEVIL’S OWN DEAL WITH IT
