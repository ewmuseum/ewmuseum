Post-Meltdown

4 Mar 2003

By Venny LeTrav

Item 7 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 4 Mar 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Words appear at the bottom of the black screen..) After Meltdown… (Venny LeTrav is untaping his wrists and is wearing a T-shirt and bluejeans when his cellphone rings. He picks it up) Venny: Hello? (The voice of Venny’s wife, Karine is audibly heard on the other line. He smiles widely as she speaks, letting his wrists go partially taped for now.) Karine: (VO) Hey, baby. Venny: How are you, K? Karine: I’m alright… just watched you on Meltdown. You okay? Venny: Yeah, I’m fine baby… you okay? Karine: Yeah, I’m fine… when you coming back to the hotel? Venny: I should be there in 45-50. I got some stuff to finish up here. Karine: You better not crash, or anything. Last thing I want to tell our daughter is that her daddy had to be squeegee’d off the road. (Venny rubs his face slowly, groaning a bit.) Venny: That is the last thing I need to hear… Karine: (laughing) Just making sure you stay on your toes, boy. (Venny smiles softly, looking as if he hadn’t done so in days.) Venny: Yeah, Yeah. I should be home in about 40 or 50, okay? Karine: Alright, alright. I love you, baby. Venny: I love you, wife. (Karine giggles a bit into the phone and makes a kissing sound. Venny makes one back and he smiles, folding the phone back up. He finishes untaping his wrists and rubs them roughly. He winces just a bit, then gets up, stuffing his remaining attire and other possessions into his black and cyan duffel bag and is about to leave when Wallace Coleman comes in. Venny looks a bit annoyed, but pastes on a smile anyway.) Wallace: Hey, Venny… could I get a word? Venny: Oooh, Wallace… I’m kinda in a hurry. Wallace: This’ll only take a second, I promise. Venny: (sighs) Alright, you have 2 minutes. Make it good. (Wallace makes a motion for Venny to sit down and he does, but reluctantly. Wallace takes out a tape recorder and smiles, turning it on.) Wallace: (into the recorder) Venny LeTrav, Post Meltdown. (to Venny) Well, Venny… how did you like the match tonight? Venny: It was great. Adams did a kickass job, it was really fun tagging with him. Wallace: What do you feel about losing to your former tag partner? Venny: It’s a pain in the ass, but you can’t win everything. Neither will he. Wallace: Do you think you’ll be tagging with Nick Adams again in the future. Venny: I dunno. (Venny looks at his watch, he is ready to leave, obviously.) Wallace: Alright, one more question. Venny: Look, fishbelly, I have my wife waiting in a hotel room, okay? I need to go. Wallace: Just one more! Venny: No, Wallace. Good Night. (Venny gets up and storms out of the room and down the hall. Wallace just shakes his head as we fade to black…)
