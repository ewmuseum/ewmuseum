The Searcher (part 1)

8 Jan 2003

Author not credited in the source

Item 5 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 8 Jan 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The camera approaches a frosted glass door. On the door something is written: Dirk Flint Private Investigator The door is pushed open and we see a man, presumably Mr Flint, talking on the phone. He is unshaven with short, dark hair. He is handsome in a rugged kind of a way and is smoking a cigar.) Flint: So basically all you want is for to find this guy and kill him? (An virtually inaudible voice can be heard from the phone.) Flint: Not kill him? Pity. How long do I got? 2 weeks! That’s crazy talk, how am I meant to find him in 2 weeks. Jesus H Christ! 2 weeks, I tell you, buddy, You’d better be paying well or this job’ll really stink. (A broad grin crosses Flint’s face.) Flint: How much? You got yourself a deal, buddy. You’re sure you want him alive? Ah well, what the hell, I’ll find him, now where was the last time he was seen? (The picture fades out and is replaced with a Starbucks. The manager is sat in his office. Suddenly the door flies open and Flint strides in.) Flint: Hey Joe, what do you know? Manager: My name is Carl Edwards, not Joe. What can I do for you Mr…? Flint: Flint, the names Flint. I’m a Private Investigator. I’m looking for information on this guy. (Flint shows a photo to the manager.) Edwards: I don’t recognise him, should I? Flint: Perhaps, back in June he wrecked your shop. He got in a brawl in the street and the fight spilled into your shop. Edwards: Wait, yes, I do remember him. He’s one of those wrestlers, isn’t he? Flint: That’s the guy. Do you know what happened to him after that fight? Edwards: No idea. He got up and left, never saw them again. Flint: So who paid for the damage? Edwards: The WWA, they paid for all the breakages but I don’t think he has anything to do with them anymore. Flint: No, he doesn’t, that’s why I’m trying to find him. They want him back! Edwards: Well, I can’t really help you. The only thing I can say for sure is that he’s not in New Orleans anymore! Flint: Thank-you for your time, Mr Edwards. Just one more thing, did he leave alone or was someone with him? Edwards: There was someone with him a short, South American guy. Flint: Really? Thank-you, you have been very helpful. The trail leads to Bolivia! (Fade to black.)
