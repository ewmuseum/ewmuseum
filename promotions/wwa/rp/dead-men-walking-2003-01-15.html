Dead Men Walking

15 Jan 2003

By Lockdown

Item 4 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 15 Jan 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Lockdown hands his Driver’s license to a uniformed corrections officer who looks at it, then at lockdown, and says Officer: You must like it here. You always come back. Even now when you don’t HAVE to be here. I thought I was through messin with you. Lockdown: just coming to see an old friend, that’s all. But you probably WILL have to mess with me again. Them folks have me on paper for 8 more years. I can’t do that paper time, you know. Officer: Huh! YEAH, I know! Lockdown: so who’s the champ of the Prison federation now? Officer: Nobody. There is no more fed. After Governor Ryan left office, the new guy shut it down. Lockdown: What an a**hole! Officer: Watch it! That’s my boss you’re badmouthing! Anyway, you’re clear. I don’t suppose you have ever been in through this door before. Go through this door, and then it’s the door on the left. Lockdown: Thanks. See ya soon. (Lockdown follows the guard’s instructions and enters an enormous room filled with long picnic style tables. The room, the visiting facility at Joliet State Prison, is abuzz with activity and chatter. If you look closely you may be able to notice wives and girlfriends giving various forms of sexual release to their men, or prisoners heading to the bathroom to conceal in their asses the drugs, money, or other contraband their visitors have smuggled in. Lockdown looks around and sees the man he is looking for, alone at a table waiting for him. Lockdown walks over and takes a seat The man across the table is a quite rotund and covered in tats.) Lockdown: How’s general population treating ya, Fat Cat? Fat Cat: Man, its great! 2 hours a day of sunlight in the yard, when my commissary runs out, I can hustle for more, TV, and punks to f*ck in the a$$! You don’t get that on death row. Lockdown: When I saw on the news that Gov. Ryan commuted all the death row sentences to life, I thought I’d come see you. How are you holding up? How do you think you’ll do once you get used to all the space? Fat Cat: Better than those two squares I saw whooping your ass on TV Saturday. I guess they don’t know who they are f*cking with! Lockdown: You saw that huh? Yeah well they think they really pulled something off. Yeah they whooped my ass, but it aint like it’s never happened before. Hell I can think of a few people in this building that got one on me. But look what happened to them. Fat Cat: They’re all in the assisted living wing now. Gotta have nurses push them around in wheelchairs and change their colostomy bags. What do ya have planned for the guys who ran ya over with that golf cart? Lockdown: Just watch and see, but I will tell ya this much: all those punks in special needs are gonna be much better off than Harley and Somers. Fat Cat: Oh yeah, I meant to tell ya, that dude you were with on TV, Connors? Well my cousin, Juicy Mouth, did some time with him. He’s a SICK motherf*cker, man. I heard when he loses his cool, everybody bleeds! Lockdown: Just the kind of man I need to have my back. MMMM-MMM. The Extreme Corporation are dead men walking. (Lockdown reaches down the back of his pants and winces. He pulls his hand back out and hands it to Fat Cat. What it is we cannot tell, as Lockdown keeps his fist closed over it. Fat Cat accepts it and his hand disappears under the table) Lockdown: Just a little something to get you back on your feet. Make me proud. (Both men stand, and Lockdown heads for the exit. Fat Cat turns and heads off to the bathroom.)
