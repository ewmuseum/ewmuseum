Revelations

18 Jan 2003

By Jackson Kraven

Item 2 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 18 Jan 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
WOE TO YOU O EARTH AND SEA (We see nothing but half the face of Jackson Kraven, illuminated by a flickering blue light, his eyes hidden by wraparound sunglasses and his face as stern and solemn as it would be were it craved from granite.) FOR THE DEVIL SENDS THE BEAST WITH WRATH BECAUSE HE KNOWS THE TIME IS SHORT (Red begins to bleed into the blue, and in the darkness behind Kraven we see a huge steel cage.) LET HIM WHO HATH UNDERSTANDING RECKON THE NUMBER OF THE BEAST (The single robotic voice reciting from The Book of Revelations becomes as thousands, and is accompanied by rising strings.) FOR IT IS A HUMAN NUMBER ITS NUMBER... (Kraven grins evilly as the blue is replaced entirely by red.) IS SIX... SIX... SIX... SIX... SIX... SIX... (The screen suddenly fills with tiny white sixes, each one glowing with a bright, white light.) SIX... SIX... SIX... SIX... SIX... SIX... (We pull back, and see that the sixes actually form a black-and-white image of Jackson Kraven's face. His face is twisted into a sadistic grin.) Ian Tense. On Monday Night Meltdown you step into the ring with the genie of destruction. On Monday Night Meltdown you have No More Tomorrows. That's all I have to say. (Red begins to bleed into the white.) Denrol. At Rage In The Cage VI you step into the cage with the man who broke your neck. At Rage In The Cage VI you have No More Tomorrows. That's all I have to say. (The white is entirely gone from the screen, replaced entirely by red.) WWA. 2002 was your fifth year. 2003, you have No More Tomorrows. That's all I have to say. (The red cuts out to black.)
