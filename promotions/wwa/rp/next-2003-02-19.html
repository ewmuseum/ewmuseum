19 Feb 2003

By Joey K

Item 9 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 19 Feb 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The scene opens upon a door. A sign is taped on it, saying 'Interviews today'. The camera pans round, and we seem to be in some kind of cheap waiting room. Chairs are lined up against the wall, nearly all are empty. Suddenly the door opens, and out steps Joey K, dressed smartly in a blue shirt and pants. He pulls another man out of the room and throws him against the opposite wall. The man collapses to the ground, and Joey dusts himself off. JOEY: Ok, people. That guy failed the interview. Now, who's next? Afew people stand up and quickly leave, all except one. A short, middle aged man slouching in the chair. He's wearing a black jacket, and an old cowboy type hat. He stands at walks towards Joey. MAN: I geuss that would be me. The older and overwieght man take of his hat, revealing a balding head of hair. JOEY: Heh, i geuss it would, come this way. Both men walk into the interview room, and the camera follows them. The old guy takes a seat and Joey goes to the opposite side of a large oak desk before taking one himself. JOEY: Ok, down to business. Whats your name buddy? MAN: Frankie Vallio. JOEY: And, uh, why dyu wanna be my manager? FRANKIE: Joey, you have it all! Charisma, youth, talent and most of all, a bright future. JOEY: Bro, I'm startin to like you already. FRANKIE: BUT, there's on thing you don't have... Joey frowns and looks as if he is about to stand. FRANKIE: Wait, wait wait. That thing is.....me! You need me as much as i need you! And together, we can rule the WWA! JOEY: Frank, your starting to sound like Darth Vader. FRANKIE: I'm sorry Joey. But, you know I'm right, you've got everything, you could be the next World Champion! Joey sits back in his chair and stretches. He sits forward again and looks at Vallio. JOEY: Frank, I like you, I like you alot. But, there's one thing i need to know, how do you plan on stoppping guys like Venny 'chicken lover' LeTrav on coming in and ruining my matches? FRANKIE: Well, does a eught degree black belt in Karate sound good to you? JOEY: It sounds pretty damn sweet! And lets hope you can shout loud too, i need you to warn me quickly! FRANKIE: Trust me Joey, I'm can shout. Joey stands and offers out a hand. Vallio accepts and the two men shake. JOEY: I'll get back to you soon man, you'll understand there are other poeple to talk to. FRANKIE: Yeah, certainly. JOEY: Ok, well, take care. Vallio leaves the room, and leaves Joey standing on his own. He walks towards the window and looks out at the city skyline. The screen fades with Joey, with his hands behind his back looking out into the sunset.
