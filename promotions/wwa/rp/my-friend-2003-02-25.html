My Friend

25 Feb 2003

By Mal Somers

Item 5 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 25 Feb 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Scene - A darkened room. Mal Somers is sitting down at a large dusty workbench, talking to someone. But we cant tell who. He is drawing at the same time with a large black marker.) As long as I have known you, you have always been there for me. You've seen me though the good times. And you've been there for me during the bad. (Mal makes a few last scribbles with the pen and puts it down. He picks up a tube of super glue and applies it to some uncooked macaroni.) You've stood by me when Leviathan stole my woman. You were there for me when Commissioner Cruz tried to screw me over. (Mal gets some pieces of black wool and cuts them into short lengths) You inspired me to battle on against the Cell at Rage in the Cage, against the odds. You are the last thing I think about before I fall asleep. The first when I awake. And everything in between. (Mal paints the macaroni red.) You cheered me up even when Jackson Kraven pinned me at Global Warfare. My own team mate. Jerk. You'll get yours. (Mal stops his handiwork and looks down at the desk. He smiles.) You dont talk as much as my tag partner Dave Harley, either. You deserve this. (Mal picks up the object he was working on. It is his WWA Rookie Title. But now, instead of the shiny gold surface of the title, Somers has attached black woolen hair, goggly eyes, a drawn on nose and ears, and a mouth made of macaroni. It smiles, eerily.) Somers - Hey there sports fans. Meet my new friend. His name is Rookie. You see, it has occured to me that I've been around here for almost six months. I'm well beyond being a rookie, but I am still the reigning WWA Rookie Champion, for over three months now. The longest ever in fact. Its been so long that the belt seems to have taken on a life of his own. (holds belt up to his ear) Somers - Whats that? Oh yeah. Rookie says "Hi". He wanted to say that everyone out there should probably get used to us two hanging together for a long time to come. This in no way affects the Extreme Corporation. I mean Dave has Jill, I have Rookie. It all makes perect sense. Well thats it for me... (holds belt up to his ear again) Somers - Rookie wants to give a big shout out to Leviathan and Lockdown. You both suck a$$!! Mal and Dave are coming for your tag belts, my brothers and sisters. Please let them return home safely! (Fade Out)
