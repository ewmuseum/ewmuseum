More questions than answers

22 May 2003

By Grunge

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
FADE IN Grunge: You, Joey K, are a failure. (Grunge sits in a chair in a dark room with spotlighted chairs arranged in a circle. Grunge sits in the chair at the end of the circle, the only things visible is the bright white suit he wears and his bright red hair. His features seem to be disguised by the darkness.) Grunge: (screams) I tolerate no failures! (The camera tilts down earning the disdain of the TV Champion.) Grunge: Mike I told you to hold that up. It’s hard to find good help. (The camera quickly tilts back to its original position earning Grunge’s approval or so we guess as he face is still not entirely visible. However it’s clear in his body language that he is at ease in the surroundings.) Grunge: I have been testing those for quite some time. To see if they are worthy to compete among the best. You have failed in every opportunity that has been given to you. All I have for you is disdain. (Grunge’s fist slams down onto the arm of his chair.) Grunge: Yet you are given the opportunity time and time again. You’re like David Lee Roth. You no longer have any talent but you keep coming back again and again and again. You just can’t accept the fact that for you this is over. (Grunge says this with grudging pride rubbing his chin.) Grunge: While I respect tenacity there is such a thing as a lost cause. I know you have the smarts as the guys who wrote “Dude Where’s My Car” so I’ll make it more simple for you. You should simply give up. (Grunge begins to laugh.) Grunge: You’re an annoying housecat who believes he is a lion. (Grunge leans in and finally we can see his face, that of weathered determination.) Grunge: So Monday it will be time to put this kitty to sleep. Still I may be conned into giving you a parting gift before I am done with you. (Grunge leans back into the darkness.) Grunge: Mainly because there is another. (A beautiful woman in red attire whisks in. It’s Julie, Grunge’s wife.) Julie: Sorry I’m late; I had business to attend to. (Grunge waves her off as she takes the seat next to her.) Grunge: Do not worry love. The others haven’t arrived yet. This business deals with Monday night. (He reaches over with his gloved hand and brushes her cheek. He then returns to his attention to the camera.) Grunge: The other’s personal path and mine have become intersected and perhaps he is what I have been searching for. I’m sure your confused Joey but trust me when I say that everything will be clear Monday night. Isn’t that right Nick Adams? (He poses the question to the camera if expecting an answer.) Grunge: The road to Revolution has begun. Rock and Roll baby. (At Grunge’s last words, people begin to enter the room but as soon as they do the camera begins to slowly…) FADE OUT
