Kash and Dart... Two Abandoned Sh*tboxes

1 Aug 2003

By Tracer Bullet

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The camera view is one that we're not normally used to seeing... that of the dashboard of a brand new car being driven by what is revelealed to be, as the camera turns left, Tracer Bullet. He seems to be rather upset, as you can easily hear him muttering to himself at a pretty good clip.) TB: Little arrogant bastard, take MY car... oh, the things I'm going to do when I get ahold of him... his nightmares will be nothing compared to... Tyler: So, Tracer, you wanna get on with relevant things? (Tracer jerks his head toward the camera, fire flashing in his eyes.) TB: That car IS relevant, jackass. I've had it for years. I've pumped cash, time, and lots of damn work into that car. It is MINE. And it is, for the moment, gone. Tyler: Well, you do have this nice new Mercury, Tracer. TB: Ha ha, very funny. Everybody's a comedian. Particularly you and that smug little punk over at Avis. Yeah, he just thought it would be a brilliant spot of comedy to rent me a Tracer when I told him I'd take whatever they had available. I'm just glad I'm not going to have this forever. Tyler: So anyway, after what happened on Meltdown last night, what are you planning to do about the theft of that belt? (As the Detached Narrator Thingy (TM), I only feel that it is my duty to mention the fact that while this was, indeed, taped the morning after Meltdown, it is being seen on Insider a week and a half later due to Tyler's remarkable ineptitude and sloth. Thank you.) TB: Do what detectives do best, Tyler... ask questions. Investigate. Find some suspects, and get all the information I can out of them. Hell, I've already got a pretty good idea of how may have been under that hood. Tyler: You do? TB: Absolutely. Just think about it, Tyler. That ninja was rather on the short side. So how many people could have fit into the outfit? Tyler: Quite a few. There are short people all over this country, you know. TB: On the roster, Tyler, on the roster! Tyler: Oh. Not as many, then. But still probably too many to have any definitive... TB: And had access to a set of my car keys? Tyler: You m... ohhhh! TB: Oh, indeed. Honestly, you'd think he would have been a little smarter than to... (Suddenly, Tracer stops talking and stares out the driver side window. His head turns as he continues driving. He is apparently looking at something stationary that is on the other side of the road.) TB: Hold on a second, Tyler, I'm going to do something really dangerous here... Tyler: Then just let me get my seatbelt... WHOA!!! (Before Tyler can even get the words out of his mouth, Tracer jerks the wheel to the left and drives over the median strip, then directly across the busy highway as several speeding cars just barely miss him. You can just hear some colorful language as they zoom by, but the driver seems to pay it no need as he pulls onto the shoulder of the road and parks, looking ahead and slightly to the right.) TB: Yeah... that's what I thought... (Tracer then opens the door of his rental car and can be seen running down into a ditch by the side of the road. Tyler is quick to follow, the camera jostling as he runs after his charge. Tracer runs quickly along the small ravine until he reaches what he was after. A split second later, it comes into the camera view... Tracer's 1978 Dodge Dart.) Tyler: Ah, so your car's been abandoned. TB: It surely has. Tyler: Speaking of abandoning... how do you explain... TB: *interrupting* Kash is a big boy. He handled himself fine... There'd better not be any damage... because if there is... (Tracer begins walking around the car, making a close inspection.) Tyler: He's going to be upset, you know. TB: No he won't... not when he gets the explanation... Tyler: Why not give it now? (Tracer leans down and checks the tires and under the wheel wells.) TB: Let him ask for it. (He then opens the driver side door and finds three items... one, his spare car keys still sitting in the ignition. Two, a black ninja hood. And three, a short note addressed to him.) Tyler: He's been here. (Tracer looks at Tyler and shakes his head sadly.) Tyler: All right, smart guy, you tell something else about him from this. TB: Gladly. The car couldn't have been abandoned for more than an hour or so, because if it had been, the thing would have been defaced, towed, or stolen by now... more than likely stolen, since the keys are in the ignition. (Tracer puts the back of his hand on the hood.) TB: Cold. The engine was turned off long before the thief left. As there's nothing but a broad expanse of grass off the side of this road, he couldn't have run off alone. He was waiting for a ride. Probably a very specific one, as I'm sure someone would have been pretty wary of a hitchhiker in a ninja suit. Tyler: But the hood is still in the car. TB: But the rest of the outfit isn't. There must be an accomplice who came by in another vehicle and picked him up. The hood was left behind just to taunt me. Tyler: Well surely somebody would have seen... TB: I seriously doubt it. I'm almost sure the hood was removed before he left the parking lot last night, and that he never got out of the car until his ride got here. Besides, good luck trying to track down someone who quickly passed by this car on an interstate to ask if they saw anyone around it. The mask was left just to taunt me, Tyler. Tyler: And the note? (Tracer picks up the note and reads it aloud.) TB: "Your choice in music sucks. I figured you wouldn't mind if I changed it to something more my style." The little bastard messed with my presets... (Tracer turns the key in the ignition and turns on the radio to preset one only to be welcomed by the sounds of Soundgarden.) TB: Let's see... actually, that one's no different... I always listen to WBTB when I'm in this part of the country. Let's try number two... (Tracer pushes two on the console and is met with heavy metal. Disturbed, to be precise.) TB: 91.3. WCCN. (He pushes the third button and gets easy listening.) TB: Argh... WUSS, the gentle sounds of "The Stream." Michael Bolton... whoever did this is a real sadist... or possibly a masochist, if they were actually listening to this station... let's try the last one... (Tracer pushes four and is hit with the classical rock stylings of The Eagles.) TB: That's more my speed. WTKG, classic rock. Tyler: So what have you been told by this? TB: That only one of those stations is going to tell me who the culprit is. Tyler: What? TB: Come on, Tyler, think. Each of these stations puts you in mind of at least one different member of the roster, and they can't have ALL done it. Tyler: So he's trying to throw you off the track by leading you to other guys. TB: Exactly. Tyler: Then it's possible that none of them are good clues. TB: Possible, but unlikely. Whoever was under that mask is rather brash, Tyler, and is going to get off on thinking that he can't be caught no matter how many little clues like these he may leave. It's what I like to call a "Riddler High." However, brash though he may be, this person has a slight lack of confidence, so he decided to throw in three red herrings. But rest assured, while all of those settings were there deliberately, only one of them implicates the real mastermind behind this. Tyler: Then is there a reason they left your one original preset? TB: I'm sure there is. What it could possibly be... well, I'll let that come straight from the horse's mouth once I catch up to him. In the meantime... (Tracer sits in the driver's seat and closes the door before kicking the car into gear.) TB: You can take the Avis clerk's pathetic attempt at a joke back to him. I'll meet you there. (Tracer eases his foot onto the gas pedal and slowly pulls up out of the ditch. He waves at the camera as he drives down the road. Tyler sighs audibly.) Tyler: There's nothing worse than a smartass who's actually smart... (Tyler climbs into the driver's seat of the Mercury Tracer and fades to black as he puts it in gear, slowly pulling out of the ditch after the Dart.)
