X Corp: A Celebration

8 Jun 2003

By Dave Harley

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The scene opens in a McDonalds somewhere in America. Seated at a table in the window are Dave Harley, Chance and errm...Rookie. Mal Somers approaches the table with a tray full of food. He puts the tray down, beaming as he seats himself next to Dave.] Somers: Happy Meals all around! Harley: Thanks Chairboy! Chance: Umm...Mal, there are four Happy Meals here. Somers: Yup. Chance: But there are only three... [Harley digs Chance under the table.] Chance: ...umm...only three of us hungry! You're not hungry are you Dave? Harley: [With a Big Mac hanging out of his mouth] Huh? Somers: Are you kidding? You should have heard his stomach on the way over here! Harley: [Quickly changing the subject] Well, all's well that ends well, huh guys? Somers: Yup, Rookie is home safe. Harley: That has to be one of the most painful plans I've ever been involved in! Somers: Hell yeah! That Somers Assault you took the other week? Chance: Oh, sorry about that Dave, I just figured that the 'Extreme Machine' could withstand a higher threshold of pain. Harley: Of course I can! Somers: C'mon Harley, you've had tougher training sessions with Jack than that! Anyway, I just want to thank you guys for helping me get Rookie back. Harley: No problemo Chairboy! Chance: But aren't you guys disappointed that you lost the Tag Titles to Havoc? Somers: Hell Yeah! Look at the pain Dave's going through after that dive from the top of the scaffold. [Both men stare at Dave who is shovelling French Fries down his throat at light speed.] Harley: Huh? Somers: The important thing is that we have Rookie back and that we have a new member of X Corp. We'll get the Tag Titles back. After all we are the most fightingest team in the WWA! Harley: Amen Chairboy! Chance: So did you guys see Enigma on Insider the other day? Harley: Pfft! That loser thinks he has the best trio in wrestling! He has got to be kidding. Somers: But technically we're a foursome. Harley: What? Oh yeah, Rookie. But he doesn't actually wrestle, does he Mal? Somers: Heh. I guess you're right. Chance: I don't think Enigma, Joey or Se7en will be a problem for us. Somers: [To Rookie] So did Uncle Eddie look after you? Harley: [To Chance] Don't worry Dude. You'll get used to this. [Dave turns to Rookie] You finished with that Happy Meal? [Chance shakes his head in bewilderment as the scene fades to black.]
