A Little Too Coincidental for Comfort.

24 Jul 2003

By Grunge

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
FADE IN (We open to a scene unfamiliar by WWA standards. In the middle of the scene stands a pulpit with a large white flag bearing the cross of Jesus behind it. We quickly change to a shot of WWA United States Champion Grunge.) Grunge: Ok I’m turning this. TV Minister: Wait, don’t touch that dial friend. (We turn back to the TV screen to see that a minister has entered the screen. He looks a lot like Alec Baldwin with a beard and a really thick southern accent.) TV Minister: Is there something wrong in your life? (We switch back to Grunge who has a rather large scowl on his face.) Grunge: Yeah and his name is Thomas Conner. (The Reverend is nodding his head, almost as if he is listening to Grunge. He opens out his hands and poses this question.) TV Minister: And does this problem happen to be a man, a man who uses the name of a biblical creature as a nomenclature? (Grunge begins to laugh nervously.) Grunge: There is no way he could possibly be talking about Leviathan. And what the heck is nomenclature? (The Reverend seems to be upset as he leans on the podium, almost as if he’s listening to everything Grunge is saying.) TV Minister: If so why don’t you stop learning all the word to “Kashmir” by Led Zeppelin, get off your butt, forgive me Lord, and do something! Oh and nomenclature is name. (Grunge scratches his head in confusion. In fact this is the first time you’ve seen his hand as he’s been shown the entire time from the neck up.) Grunge: It’s not like I haven’t been rocking and rolling with Leviathan. I mean it’s not like he’s beaten me yet, just had to go to the EMTs, which he had to go to as well I might add. I did bust him open. Besides I have a tag match against him on Monday with Nick and I’m going to teach him a little lesson about who’s the champ around here. And yes I know all the words to “Kashmir” but…why am I arguing with a TV? (We switch back to the Reverend, who it seems has been looking at his wristwatch when the camera comes back on him. He quickly jumps and turns, flashing that used-car salesman smile.) TV Minister: So the real question is what are you going to do about your problems. Are you going to act or are you just going to keep sitting there, on a beanbag, in your boxer shorts, eating Cheetos? (The camera comes to Grunge and pulls out and sure enough Grunge is sitting in a red beanbag chair, in red boxers, eating Cheetos. He looks surprised to say the least.) Grunge: OK, this is getting a bit creepy (The Reverend smiles as if he’s just reeled in a big fish. He then opens out his arms and closes his eyes.) TV Minister: Well then fear not because I can help you. All you need to do is send me a check with your…donation to… (The camera quickly switches back to Grunge who holds the remote to the TV.) Grunge: Now see you almost got me Rev, but I prefer to give in the collection plate. Now yawl say goodbye. And don’t make fun of Led Zeppelin. (And we switch back to the TV where it seems the Reverend has something else to say but a loud click is heard and the TV goes to black just as the camera…) FADE OUT
