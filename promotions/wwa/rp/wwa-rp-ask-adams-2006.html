Ask Adams (IC & OOC)

6 March 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
6 March 2006 Any questions you have, feel free to ask. Unless, of course, they're something relatively unanswerable along the lines of, "What is the average airspeed of an unladen swallow?" If that is the case, my answer will either be "3" or "42", and those will always be symbols for the correct answer, regardless of whether the real answer is actually one of those numbers, "Afghanistan", or "Key Lime Pie". So, let the questions begin. (Just specify whether it's to the character Nicholas Adams, or to me, Adams' handler, Nick V...or as somebody once called me, Nick Somethingorother.) ---- 8 March 2006 Shaman: Why do you choke at the big game man? ---- 9 March 2006 Adams: It's the pie. I eat too much during the game, and then I...oh, wrong big game. Well, to answer that, I don't choke. I do well, it's just that the other guy does better. You look at every single one of the chances that I've had, I fought my hardest, and was merely outclassed at the time. Choke, I did not. Any other choice inquiries? ---- 9 March 2006 Ember: Adams, please don't be mistaken here, I still don't actually like you, but I have to admit that you have the right attitude there. Everyone will lose at some point, it is how we deal with, and learn from, those losees that matter. There will always be someone better, somewhere down the line - as you found out when you faced me.
