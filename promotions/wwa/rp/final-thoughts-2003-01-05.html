Final Thoughts

5 Jan 2003

By Enigma

Item 1 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 5 Jan 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
{We open the scene and we see a picture, a painting at that, of Sky Flyin Jimmi Zion on the wall. Right next to it is a picture of Chance, former Rookie Champion and Extreme Champion} My two opponents for Monday night. Two men who have similar goals. Two men who are gunning for one thing. ME {Enigma walks into the scene wearing a black trenchcoat and his two WWA titles in his hands} Zion, you may not be on the same side as Chance, you two may not be able to work together even though you are on the same team. I realize this fact there Jimmi. I realize that you want to do this all yourself. I know you want to be the one that pins me in the center of the ring for the three count. I know you want glory. What could be more glorious? In fact there is nothing more glorious in my estimation than for you to beat the current Extreme and Cruiserweight Champion. I can see you are hungry to regain your so-called glory. You are a glory hound. You cannot fathom the fact that you are not on top anymore. While you still may be young, you had your chance. You had your time. It is time for you to realize that you are not going to be able to beat me and you are not going to be the new champion. In fact there is not going to be a new champion because I am going to retain my titles, just like I have been saying all along. You call me an imitation of you? How drole Jimmi. I am not trying to imitate you. God forbid anyone would want to be like you. There’s a reason that cloning is not legal and why people are trying to get it banned because could you imagine two Jimmi Zion’s running around. I mean my god, no one would want to see that. People would be jumping off of buildings just to get away from the horror of two Jimmi Zions. This division is not yours. It is not going back to you. You are right, you did leave this division and it was a sad division while you were in charge of it. Now I am the man of the Extreme and Cruiser divisions. I am the one that people recognize when they think of the WWA and this division. Not Grunge, not EVD, not Chance and certainly not you Zion. Zion, you should understand another thing about my rage. My anger fuels my fire. My anger gives me the right to inflict pain on other individuals. It gives me the right to beat the hell out of men like you. Beat the hell out of Chance. My hate and distaste for you and others is what makes me the best. Anger is a powerful weapon Zion. Hate and anger fuels the world. It influences the decision we live by. It influences everyone. Don’t act like you are any different. George Bush uses his hate of Saddam Hussein as his fuel to attack Iraq. It is by controlling and channeling our rage onto the people that are the cause it, is where the power lies. This is why I am more powerful than you Jimmi. This is why I plan on destroying you. I hate you Zion. I hate all my opponents. You will find that out the hard way on Monday. Greatness? You call yourself greatness? Please there are a few men who have achieved greatness. You are not one of these men. You want to talk about time. You think it is Zion time all the time? It’s not true. You are delusional about that. This is not your time. I will prove that on Monday. You think you are extreme? I plan on showing you what Extreme is all about. I will teach you a lesson that you will not soon forget. I will teach you what Extreme wrestling is all about. This phenomenon that you speak of will not run me over. I will not be defeated by it. It cannot stop me, nothing can stop me. You want to call my Captain Oblivious? Go right ahead, I don’t need to give you the little nursery rhymes about name callings. The only thing that is oblivious is you. You are oblivious to the fact that I am going to beat you limb from limb. You say I have not been tested? Maybe you are right. In reality, who can test me? I have been in the same match with Chance three times and twice I walked out with the Extreme Title. Chance was not a challenge and you will not be a challenge. Another fact, you are teaming with Chance. You may not think of him as your partner but it is a handicap match. You may not want Chance as your partner. I can understand that. I would not want Chance as my partner. But the fact remains you two Are partners. It does not make a difference to me. You two are the ones at a disadvantage. Not me. Scared? Of you? I don’t think so Zion. There is nothing in this world that I am afraid of and especially you. Zion, little kids and most women may be scared of you because you are a funny looking weirdo, but I am not afraid of you. I find you humorous. You tell these tall tales about your greatness and such. I cannot wait until Monday night. On Monday I will bring down to earth. Your done. {Enigma pauses for a second} Moving along to my other opponent, maybe a forgotten man by me. Chance, don’t worry kid, I did not forget about you. To answer your question am I happy that I almost crippled you? The answer is simple, no. I am sorry that I did not finish the job. I am mad that I did not eliminate you completely. It makes me upset that I did not finish the job. That was my mistake and possibly the one time I showed maybe a little bit of weakness. Chance, I let you survive, I will not make that mistake again. That’s not a threat, but a promise. I am glad to see that you will be flying high. It will be great to see me throwing you through the air. Watching you crash and burn. Maybe that should be yours and Zion’s team name. Crash and burn, because that is what is going to happen. You need to work out. You need to get ready. You know what happened the last time we were in the same ring. I took your ass out. Pity? You pity me? Save your pity for the starving in Africa. Save your pity for people who need it. Don’t pity the people who don’t want or need your pity. Chance, don’t pity me. I hope you tell your doctor to save you a bed in the hospital because I am going to send you back there. Chance, you will have to be lucky to win this match. You will have to be lucky to be able to beat me. I do not doubt the fact that you have been lucky in your life, but your luck is going to run out. You guys are not going to beat me. NOTHING CAN STOP ME NOW Especially Chance and Zion. {scene fades}
