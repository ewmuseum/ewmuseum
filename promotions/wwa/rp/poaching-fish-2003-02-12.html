Poaching Fish

12 Feb 2003

By Mal Somers

Item 1 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 12 Feb 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Scene – In what appears to be a hotel room in Ottawa, home of this weeks Fiesta. Co-Founder of the Extreme Corporation, and WWA Rookie Champion Mal Somers is cooking away in the small kitchen. Aromatic smells of tarragon and lemon fill the room. Mal is wearing a chefs hat and EXTREME CORPORATION apron as he cooks.) Somers - "Hi there sports fans and welcome to WWA Insider. Just fixing myself a bite to eat before heading off to the gym.” (sniffs the air) “Man that smells great! Lemon poached snapper. You guys don’t mind if I eat while I talk do you?” (the camera moves from left to right.) Somers – “Great.” (With that Mal pulls a snapper from the pan, puts it on the plate and using his knife and fork cuts it into little bitty pieces. He takes a big bite and chews away.) Somers – “Leviathan. The man, the myth, the moron. Your pea sized brain probably does not comprehend what I am doing here, so I will explain it to you. What this represents is an analogy. You see, the FISH here is you. Caught. Poached. Dead. Chopped into tiny little bitty pieces. In about 24 hours it will be flushed down the toilet never to be seen again. Unless you are like your tag partner Lockdown, but that’s another story. This cutlery. Represents weapons. Whether they be chairs, tables, bats, the Extreme Mobile, or my own bare hands, harm will come to you. That much is guaranteed. The only question is when that will be. This Saturday at Fiesta? Maybe.” (puts his hand on his chest.) Somers – “Actually, I think this fish is a bit overdone. Sort of like you Leviathan. See how I used one of your own kind to prove a point? I’m clever like that.” (slides the fish off his plate into the bin) “But I suppose the Cell think they are clever too. You put on a good match at Rage in The Cage. But nothing was resolved. We didn’t all embrace at the end and say what a great match that was. We aren’t like Tense and Sanders and go on about respect. I don’t respect you Leviathan. I think I respect you less and hate you even more as each day goes by. You stole my woman. Can't respect a guy who does that. I mean you guys didn’t even pin me. The Cell had me all to themselves and you still couldn’t get the job done. Word in the lockerroom is that Miss Wise has been saying that about you in the bedroom as well Thomas. Cant get the job done.” (chuckles) “And what else? Wrangling your way into a number one contenders match for the WWA Tag Team Titles. And then winning the damn match! Well done. Leviathan and Lockdown vs. Deimos and Phobos, this Saturday on Fiesta. A match for the ages.” (chuckles again) “Or not. Four of the most despicable wrestlers ever to enter the ring, slugging it out. Hopefully you all pummel each other into oblivion. That would be great.” (Camera zooms in so that just Somers’ face and upper torso fill the screen.) Somers - “Cell, The Xcorp come hell or high water will be at Fiesta. Whether we have to pay to get tickets, or disguise ourselves as road agents, or whatever we will be there. Just thought you might like to know that. The Xcorp have only just begun.” (Fade Out)
