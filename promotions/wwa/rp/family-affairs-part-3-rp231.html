Family Affairs Part 3

4 Jul 2003

By Wes Sanders

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene opens to the desolate gym, Wes Sanders is on his feet gently hitting several setup boards with his feet. He is also bouncing just off the ground to keep the momentum and blood flowing. He wears nothing but a pair of shorts and a sweat band on his wrist. Wallace Coleman walks up to Wes and looks at him.) “Wes, you’ve been training almost non stop for the last four days, don’t you want to take a break.” “If I’m going to get far in this rumble, I need to be on my best form. There are so many good competitors out there and I must destroy them all. They need to know that the name of Wes Sanders, is not a name to be taken lightly” (Wallace shrugs his shoulders.) “Look Wes, I’ve got to run this promo tape down to the WWA offices in Salt Lake City, you going to be alright if I leave you alone for a few minutes?” “Did I ever ask you to be here in the first place?” “Good point” Wallace says bemused. (Wallace turns away leaving a very focused Wes to turn his attention to the boards. As Wallace walks out of the gym building he bumps into another figure. The other figures just nods and Wallace walks on. The figure stands watching Wes attack the boards. Wes does a few short sharp low kicks, before doing a solid roundhouse kick.) (The dust from the board settles from the kick, and Wes remains on one knee as he looks up through his thick black hair towards the character. The character remains very still and silent both men just stare towards each other. Wes speaks up.) “I was wondering when you would find me.” (Wes stands and looks at the slightly smaller man. The smaller figure takes a step forward and we recognize him as the younger Sanders brother Chivo. Wes is about to speak but Chivo interrupts.) “It’s been a long time Wes, too long. Too many sunrises and sunsets have passed.” “What are you doing here?” Wes speaks softly almost humanly. “I have come to help you win the Independence Day Rumble, the time for differences need to be put aside you only have a couple of days left to train. I can see that you have the raw ambition but there is no drive.” “Look I don’t need your help… I can handle this myself.” “Ah yes, a saying I have heard before, look you always knew that you needed my help in a time of need and to me this looks like a time of need. After your injury, your slow and sluggish movements make me laugh. You need to be quick as you once were.” (Chivo pauses.) “In that ring on Sunday you have the likes of Nick Adams, Jimmi Zion and Chance, all of them are quicker in and around the ring than you. I’ve seen their movements and studied the battles that they have fought and lost.” (Wes looks confused and begins punching the boards in front of him. Chivo continues.) “Each time there has been a minor mistake by that competitor. You my brother cannot make that mistake. One mistake in this rumble could cost you the whole entire match. Do you know what number you have drawn yet?” (Wes nods in a no like fashion) “Ok it doesn’t matter anyway, sometimes its better if you stay in the ring for a little longer. The earlier you partake in the rumble you get a feel for how things are flowing. You get into a good rhythm. And of course there is always the advantage of coming in later. Half your opponents have already been thrown out.” (Chivo pauses and ponders for a moment rubbing his gruff filled chin.) “Look I got the perfect routine for you to try it’ll help take out the bigger guys and keep you quick and nimble for the smaller faster men. But you have to do everything I say.” (Wes nods.) “Weights are good for keeping the strength up but you need some relaxation, being calm before any match Wes will make you perform and feel a lot better. Tell me who do you fear most going into the IDR” (Wes and Chivo walk and sit down together on the floor Wes picks up a dry towel and wipes off the sweat on his face.) “I haven’t really thought about it, I wouldn’t think of that normally.” “Well do… Think of the only person in that rumble that has the ability to throw you out.” “Well I guess they all do…” Wes shrugs his shoulders “Precisely!” Says Chivo excitedly “Everyone who is anyone can eliminate you. I’ve watched WWA television, I’ve seen the recent promos cut by wrestlers, they are all just caught up in each other. Aiming for one particular person is not what you want to achieve. It is not a good game plan, everyone is your enemy Wes. Remember that.” (Wes repeats slowly.) “Everyone… is my enemy…?” “Yes, and they would all love to see you get thrown over that top rope and eliminated. It doesn’t matter how big or small of a superstar they are. From Chuckster to Chance they all want to see you gone.” (Chivo stands up.) “So who do you fear now Wes?” “I fear everyone who gets in that ring on Sunday.” “And who do you trust?” “I trust no-one” Wes talks slow. (Chivo claps his hands together.) “Good, your learning already now I suggest we go take a walk an assess each individual in the ring on Sunday. It’s going to be a long and painful task but it just has to be done.” “But what about training…” “Wes, trust me, I’m your brother!” (Wes stays seated on the floor looking up at his younger brother. A million thoughts are passing through his mind currently but he sees no reason why he shouldn’t trust his own flesh and blood.) “Why have you come back?” Wes asks the important question on his mind “To help you of course…” “My brother hates me, he would never want to help me” Wes snaps back “I don’t hate you… I’ve even brought a friend to help you through the rumble.” (Chivo points to the side of the room and within the shadows another figure comes out. This is a very familiar figure. Wes has a sheer look of fright on his face when he sees him. The figure walks closer and closer, we can instantly see that it is former WWA world Champion Ian Tense. He stands next to Chivo. As both men slowly walk closer towards Wes, the fear increases inside. Wes tries to battle away and crawl away but he is stuck in a corner.) ”What’s a matter Wes, you look like you’ve seen a ghost” Ian smiles as he speaks. (Wes hunches up in a ball as Ian and Wes’ supposed brother Chivo continue to throw insults towards the nervous wreck… Almost with a boom the scene switches.) (The scene opens to almost total blackness. Wes face can be etched out in the darkness and heard breathing heavy, his eyes blink quickly. As he looks around the empty room he realizes that he has just had a nightmare.) “…” (Wes lays back eyes wide open as he just blankly stares at the ceiling. He then starts to whisper the same words over and over again.) “Trust no-one, Trust no-one…” (And with this the screen fades to black.)
