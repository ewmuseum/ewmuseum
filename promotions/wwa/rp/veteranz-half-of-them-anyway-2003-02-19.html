WWA (1997–2019) Roleplays

Veteranz, half of them anyway.

19 Feb 2003

By Johnny Shrapnel

Item 8 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 19 Feb 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(It is a cold and windy night. Johnny Shrapnel is walking down a busy New York street. Everyone is hurrying to get out of the horrible weather.) JS: Have you seen Harley anywhere? (Shrapnel asks each passer-by. He stumbles on through the rain and wind. He walks past a McDonalds and a Starbucks before turning into a house. By that I don't mean he became a building, that would be stupid. I mean he went inside a house. Inside this house there is a nice log fire burning and lots of comfy chairs. A family sit around a table playing a board game happily together.) JS: Have you seen Harley anywhere? (The family turn towards Johnny Shrapnel. The father picks up a shotgun.) Father: How many times! Get out of my house! (Johnny leaves and stumbles on down the street. A magazine is blown into his face. He takes it and looks at it. It is WWA Weekly from several months ago. Harley Trapper is interviewed inside.) JS: Where are you Harley? Where are you? (A copy of USA today blows into his face. An article catches his eye.) JS: "Grease: SOLD OUT" Grease! That's where Havoc come from! (The wind gusts hard again and knocks Johnny onto the sidwalk. An old copy of Rolling Stone flys into his face. It is open on a page about Rap.) JS: "Jump around", damn it, House Of Pain kidnapped my buddy! (Johnny gets up and another piece of papr flys into his face. It is Time magazine. It is open on a page about retired soldiers and what they do after leaving the army.) JS: Well, that's totally irrelevant! (The wind blows hard and dislodges a bucket from a couple of stories up. Alice in Wonderland I think. It falls down and hist Johnny on the head. He spins round twice and collapses in a pile of trash.) JS: Have you seen Harley.... (Fade to black in Johnny's head, fade to black on the screen.)
