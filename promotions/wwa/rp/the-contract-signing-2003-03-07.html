The Contract Signing

7 Mar 2003

By X Corp

Item 5 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 7 Mar 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The scene opens on the front door of a very spacious looking home. The door is labelled ‘X Corp Headquarters: Chicago Branch’. As we enter, we hear the sounds of three men talking. The camera crosses the extravagant hallway, past the large marble staircase and into the lounge, as the voices grow gradually louder.] Voice 1 - I don’t really like to sign contracts. They always remind me of that episode of The Outer Limits when they cut out that guy’s tongue and put it in a jar and it wouldn’t die, it just grew and pulsated and gave birth to baby tongues. Voice 2 - That wasn’t The Outer Limits, Harley that was The Twilight Zone! Voice 1 - Oh yeah. It was still pretty cool. Voice 2 - Yeah, but how about the one with the gremlin on the side of the bus? Voice 1 - That was a plane, stupid! Remember? The one with William Shatner? Voice 2 - Hey, you’re right. [Doing his best Shatner impression] There’s…something on the wing…Some…Thing! [The camera peers into the lounge and reveals both members of The Extreme Corporation - Mal Somers and Dave Harley - sat in recliner chairs, each holding a beer. Mal holds Rookie in his free hand. The third person sits with his back to the camera on a large sofa. His identity cannot be affirmed.] Voice 3 - Guys, if we can get down to business. There’s no way you can turn down an offer like this. It’s payback for the hurt you’ve suffered over the past few weeks. Dave, look at what The Cell have done to your back. Mal, they've driven you to believe that your Rookie Title is a real person... Somers - [Gasps] Don't listen to him Rookie! He doesn't know what he's saying! Voice 3 - You see? This is your opportunity to end all of this. [The camera finally gets close enough to determine the third figure in the room. It is Commissioner Raine. Dave leans forward in his chair.] Harley - When would it be? Raine - March Madness. That gives you plenty of time to rest up and for your back to recover. Somers - Dave, are you sure your back is going to have recovered in time for March Madness? Harley - Relax Chairboy, You're talking to the Extreme Recuperating Machine. There ain't nothing that could hold me back from an opportunity like this. Somers - Maybe you're right. You've got yourself a deal Commissioner. Mr. Harley, pass me my contract-signing hat. [Harley reaches down and picks up a dirty old Sherlock Holmes-style hat from the floor and places it on Mal's head. Mal picks up the contract and signs it. He passes it over to Dave Harley, who signs his name below Mal's before passing it back to Commissioner Raine.] Raine - So that's it. The contract is signed. At March Madness, The Extreme Corporation will take on The Cell for the WWA Tag Team Titles! [As Raine finishes, there is a thumping at the door.] Harley - What the hell is that? Mal you'll have to answer it. If it's any Har-lettes... Somers - I know, I know. The pictures are at the door. [Mal gets up to answer the door as the scene fades out.]
