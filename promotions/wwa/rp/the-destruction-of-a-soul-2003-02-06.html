The Destruction of a Soul

6 Feb 2003

By Deimos

Item 4 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 6 Feb 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Before the darkness can renounce its hold on the scene, a voice pierces through. “How does it feel, Ricky?” Shadows move across the wall of the room, across the peeling layers of paint, and the yellow stains that mar what paint remains. “How does it feel to lose everything?” The single light bulb, hanging precariously from a cable, sways slightly. The corners of the empty room dance, swaying to and fro with the shadows. “I’ve taken your love.” The unseen owner of the voice giggles. Not the giggle of uncontrollable mirth, but deeper, darker, more dangerous. “I’ve taken your pride.” The sight of Tiffani shattering the bottle of Jim Beam over Venom’s head at Rage In The Cage intrudes upon the screen for the briefest of moments. When the sight departs, the dark, passionate eyes of Deimos remain. Shadows move across his face with the single swinging light behind him, evoking grim and terrifying images within his malice-ridden grin. “On Monday night, in Montreal, I’ll take what’s left of your soul and your mind. Your vengeance will fail feebly amid your broken dreams and your broken heart. And, I’ll finally take your career, the last thing that you hold dear.” A scene from Labor Day Brawl 2002 assaults the senses. As Venom uses the ropes to get to his feet, Deimos delivers a crushing blow right to the temple with the singapore cane before sliding back into the ring. We return again to the room, but this time the shadows are different. They dance more excitedly. The darkly grinning face of Deimos is absent. The scene moves, as if casting its eyes toward the floor. There, flames lick their way across the Texas state flag. “Montreal is a long way from Texas, Ricky; a long way from home. If you know what’s best for you, you’ll stay home, drowning your sorrows in the bottle that consumes you. Isn’t it ironic? The bottle that you crave so desperately is destroying you...” Again, Tiffani shatters the bottle of Jim Beam over Venom’s head. “...in more ways than one.” As the flames consume the symbol of Texan pride, it all ends.
