What is V-day?

22 Jan 2003

Author not credited in the source

Item 4 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 22 Jan 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(One of the most intriguing storylines going into Rage In The Cage 6 is the mysterious V-Day. What is it? What does it mean? Who is behind it? The only thing we know for sure is that all will be revealed at the PPV. We sent Wallace Coleman out on the streets of Calgary to find out what the Canadian people think.) Coleman: Excuse me, excuse me! (Wallace runs up to a teenage boy dressed in a "No More Tomorrows" T-Shirt.) Coleman: I can see you are a WWA Fan. Can I ask you a few questions? Boy: Yeah, whatever. Coleman: Are you exicted about Rage In The Cage? Boy: Yeah, I guess so. Coleman: Are you going? Boy: Yeah. Coleman: Who do you think is behind V-Day? Boy: Dunno. Probably someone. Coleman: Right, anything else you'd like to add? Boy: No. (The boy walks off down the street and Wallace accosts an attractive young lady. She looks starstruck and stare bemusedly at the camera.) Coleman: Hi there, I'm Wallace Coleman and you are Live to 20 Million people on WWA Insider so are you lookinh forward to the big PPV? Lady: !!!!!!! (She stares at the camera in a daze.) Coleman: Everyone is talking about V-Day, what do you think it is? Lady: !!!!!!!! (She looks at Wallace and then back to the camera. Coleman is starting to get a little desperate about this interview.) Coleman: Do you have a favourite WWA Wrestler? Lady: !!!!!! Coleman: Do you have anything to say? Lady: !!!! Am I on TV? (Coleman gives her a nasty look and turns to his final interviewee. A hugely, fat guy in a nasty Hawaiian shirt and a pasty complexion.) Coleman: So are you looking forward to the Pay-Per-View? Guy: I am, I'm hoping it'll be fantastic. Of course wrestling these days is nothing compared to what it once was! I remember the great day of Mantis and Shawn Cage, those were top quality grapplers. They were comitted to the cause, not like these day now it's all about commercialism and money and as for joining the PTC... Coleman: OK! OK! You are entitled to your opinion. Who do you think is behind V-Day? Guy: Probably Triple H, he's always behind everything. However I think even the WWA has more sense than that! I hope it will be Mantis or Shawn Cage, now they were top quality grapplers but, more than likely, it will be the JOB Squad or Kade Williams. Coleman: Thanks for your opinions. Should just stress those are words from the public and not endorsed in any way by the WWA. (Coleman walks away from the crowd that has gathered and stands in front of the Saddledome.) Coleman: Behind me is the site of the WWA's next super-pay-per-view. Tickets for this event sold out months ago and as we have seen, err, sort of, the anticipation in Calgary couldn't be greater! V-day remains a mystery so tune in to Channel 9 on Sunday night to find out! Prepare yourselves for Rage In the Cage 6!
