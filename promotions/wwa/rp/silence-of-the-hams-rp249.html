Silence of the hams!!

16 Jul 2003

By Shaman

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
** The camera turns in a broad full circle to reveal Shamans home in the Nevejo Desert. He is cooking over a bbq pit as several of his friends are there watching this. His brother is seen walking around behind him looking at his new wife and baby boy as he just cooks over the grill as Suzy comes walking up and hugs him and hands him a cold beer and Shaman smiles and drinks his beer watching everyone else as he turns to the camera and speaks.** Shaman: " My time, My place, My damn interview get over it! Now im going to have to look things over. IDR i fought everyone there had one helluva brawl with Wattsoner. A good ol fashioned street fight and i loved it. Now onto the rumble itself. Nick Adams i want to say congratulations on the win. As for everyone else you do realise that i was in that rumble for one helluva longtime. Now onto everyone else get the hel over your loss. As for Devils Own im not through with any of you yet. Come hell or high water its going to get F***ing messy real soon!!" ** Shamans phone rings and he turns his head and reaches down and picks it up answering. ** Shaman: " Yeah....Be at Meltdown on Monday. Im not scheduled for the show. Ok i agree yeah its needed. Ok i'll see you there then. Catch ya later!!" ** Shaman hangs up the phone and turns back towards the bbq pit as he flips over the bbq ribs and the steaks with the tongs and he then turns to the camera slightly.** Shaman: " Who controls what is the ultimate question in the WWA. Who has the ultimate desire to end it all on top. Question the motives where willi end up. I don't really care anymore anyone and everyone at the top are gonna know theres something hunting them. Well guess who!! Wake up and smell the coffee WWA talent. I don;t care who you are or how bad you think you are. Im coming and if your in my way im going to flatten you. So shut the F*** up and prepare for some real action!!" ** Shaman clears the bbq pit of all the food cooking and grabs the tray of food and sets it on the table and he speaks.** Shaman: " Foods ready time to eat!!" ** Everyone walks over and begins to place food on their plates and then walks away and Shaman turns and grabs some food for himself and walks away as the camera turns onto the plateaus and begins to fade as two words flash into bold view with seemingly new meaning.** GOT BLOOD!!
