What A Week

4 Mar 2003

By Vik Ventressa

Item 5 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 4 Mar 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
“What a week” (We open to see Vik Ventressa sat in a comfortable armchair. Black leather, reclining…. To cut a long story short, were talking about a Laz-E-Boy Chair. Yep, like Chandler and Joey had in Friends…. Picture it? Good…) “I got pinned….. By Mr. Cool……” (It’s a fairly wide shot. We can see plenty of memorabilia about the place. Not only pictures of Vik wrestling with the businesses greats, but shaking hands with Steven Spielberg, the caption says “In preparation for Indy 4” – bet you didn’t know he was investing in it huh?) “First, my surprise partner was Spike. The man that humiliated me by winning my TV Title, the man, who with The Victor, nearly made a joke of my career before I even started. Spike. One man I could quite easily live without. So I had to TEAM with the guy, against Jackson Kraven? It may have well been a handicap match. Sure, I had a ‘Team’, and Jack had Mr. Cool, but at least Mr. Cool has a fan club! Spike has nothing. I might as well have been out there on my own” (We see some more of the room…. We pan round and oh! The fireplace! Many a good promo has been shot with that thing on. But now it’s off. As we pan further round, the sunlight streams through the French doors, causing an unbearable amount of glare in the camera. It quickly pans through) “At least I get my chance at Revenge. Jack didn’t pin me. That’s the main thing. As long as I have that, I still have a little edge. In preparation for our March Madness match….. Which reminds me…. He still hasn’t accepted the match yet…..” (We pan back to Vik. He has lit a cigarette and is blowing smoke rings) “And this time, some decent partners. I have the biggest Tag Team in WWA History, with the most tag team championships than ANYONE in the storied legend of the WWA. So how did I come to be aligned with the Veteranz? Let’s have a look. This was recorded during one of the Meltdown matches…..” (We cut to see the MCI Arena. Backstage towards the locker room, Vik Ventressa walks down the corridor. He passes a door. He stops. A faint ‘Hmmf-hmphphph’ can be heard. He presses his ear to the door as his eyebrows raise) Vik: Man, are there people having SEX in there? Cameraman: I don’t know….. Maybe you should just move on….. Vik: Are you kidding? Cameraman: Sir, I am under instruction to make sure you are ready for your match….. If your not, I could get f- Vik: (Interrupting) Come on, this could be Tiffani and Deimos on the job, and you can be damn sure I’m not going to miss that…… That’s definite insult material…… make sure that camera is on, we can sell this later on….. (The camera nods as Vik breathes deeply, and then with a stiff kick, kicks the door in.) Vik: Aw man, what a let down….. It’s just Trapper….. (Thinks twice) Vik: Wait a minute…. (Looks at the camera) Didn’t Trapper get kidnapped by Havoc? Cameraman: Shit… yeah! You found Trapper! (Vik quickly gets on one knee and starts untying the rough looking Trapper. He undoes the hanky that is taut around his mouth and neck) Trapper: Thanks (Out of breath) I really owe you one….. Vik: Don’t mention it….. I- Trapper: (Interrupting) Sorry, love to chat, but Johnny’s about to go in a handicap match with Havoc – He’ll get maimed. I gotta save him….. (And in a Mighty Joe style, he dashes off to save his partner….) Vik: What the f-….. (He yells down the corridor) I’LL SEE YOU IN YOUR LOCKER ROOM AFTER THE MATCH Cameraman: Wow, what a find! But watch your back, HoP will be PISSED. ESPECIALLY Havoc Vik: Fu*k HoP……. And f*ck Havoc “Apologies for the colourful language. But that’s how it came about. Of course, the rest is history.” (We pan to a square on shot of Vik, cigarette toked, he blows out the smoke, which we can see disseminating from his mouth as the powerful light illuminates the room.) “And so we are here. 6 days away from a match, destined to be a bloodbath. Trapper and Shrapnel hate Havoc. And I hate Jack. On Monday Night Meltdown, I get to feel the “Pain” of HoP.” (We zoom to a close up of his face) “Well I got news, Jack…… V-Day might have heralded the return of the Veteranz, and you boys may think that Monday is HoP day. Well you’re wrong. It’ll be VM Day. Vik Ventressa. Veteranz. Victory on Meltdown.” (We zoom even closer to just his eyes) “Try and stop us….” (The words echo to nothing as we fade to black)
