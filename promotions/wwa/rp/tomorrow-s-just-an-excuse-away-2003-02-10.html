Tomorrow's just an excuse away...

10 Feb 2003

By Grunge

Item 3 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 10 Feb 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
FADE IN (Grunge is sitting in his living room. He holds his head in his hands upset. He's wearing his headphones but throws them off.) Grunge-(yells) MY TWILIGHT TO STARLIGHT DISC BEGINS TO SKIP AT THE END OF THROUGH THE EYES OF RUBY. I'M VERY PISSED NOW. AND I WILL HAVE TO BUY ANOTHER COPY OF THIS ALBUM. (Grunge eyes the camera and looks out. Julie comes up behind him and rubs his shoulders.) Grunge-Ah...but there is such a story behind this copy! The day it came out...I skipped school. I woke up at six thirty and walked to Wal-Mart. I walked through the doors as they opened for buisness, walked straight to the electronics department, picked up my copy of the album, paid and walked home. My reciept said 7:01 am. The first possible minute in which I could have bought the album. (Grunge sighs.) Grunge-I was always proud of that. Now, I'm reduced to listening to a skip loop during the fade in fade out fade in part of Through the Eyes of Ruby. Sad indeed. Julie-I don't think that's what your upset about. Your avoiding whats really upseting you. Grunge-Rick is my best friend. His neck is in severe shape. The doctors did what they could but know when he's going to get back and on top of this I have to wrestle Nick Adams, TWICE! Julie-You can beat him. Grunge-I don't have time to grieve. I have to put on a show. I have to defend that belt on Meltdown and Fiesta against a man whose obsessed with it. Julie-(sings) Common and cheer up, put on a happy face. Grunge-(laughs) You know how I feel about Wal-Mart. Still I am upset about that CD. And I'm upset about Rick but I'm not really upset about Nick Adams. He'll get his shot. Two shots really and I'm going to have fun playing with him. I'm going to show the fans what happens when I cut lose and Nick's finally going to see that I deserved to win that title. Julie-Don't get cocky. Grunge-Well that is the album Adam's theme came from. Seems appropriate. Oh and Deimos your on my list you little p*****t. Julie-You just need some sleep before you face Adams on Fiesta and then Metldown. Grunge-I don't think so. My dream last night consisted of driving around town, racing Ryan "Don't call me Bryan!" Adams. I in a Neon, he in a Gremlin. I don't know what it means. FADE OUT
