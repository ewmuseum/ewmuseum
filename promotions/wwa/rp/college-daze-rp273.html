College Daze

22 Jul 2003

By Alex Knight

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
“If you like a lot of chocolate on your biscuit…” (Alex hits his head, as he leans back and cracks it on the headboard. It becomes apparent, by the poor carpentry, that this is Tim Davies headboard and we are in his guest room. The guest room belonging to a wider structure known as Tim’s newly acquired US apartment, said apartment located in downtown Georgia. Alex scratches the back of his head and sits upright on the single bed. He is wearing a pair of torn Levis and a blue Animal House-esque ‘College’ sweatshirt. A white WWA towel is around his neck.) Alex: Whilst in the shower this morning, Tim asked me which drink i would prefer. He offered me a glass of a 1984 Chambolle-Musigny or any of the spirits he had lying around . Spirit was my reply, Southern Comfort on the rocks to be precise. As i got out of the shower and picked up the glass from the bedside cabinet, Tim approached me, with a half-empty wine glass in hand. “You know it is more appropriate to have a glass of red wine in the morning. Spirits are best saved for the dinner parties and night caps,” he said. (Alex leans over to the stereo, drops a copy of The Beach Boys ‘Pet Sounds’ into the cd deck and hits random play as he continues.) Alex: If i think about it, my college years have a lot to answer for…Step back in time we are looking at Alex Knight circa 1998…fresher year. It was the stage in every college man’s life where the peak is hit; playing sports, staying out late getting drunk, strolling back to someone’s dingy room with a kebab in one hand and a traffic cone in the other. Letting the good times to roll. (Alex scoffs.) Alex: My best mate back then was Adam Robinson. One time when we were both involved in a few party drinking games, i had to take a penalty because i lifted a pinky from my glass. They all jeered as i stared down the barrel of death by alcohol. Adam said, “You need to make a choice buddy…You can have one glass of red wine or you can chug down a bottle Tequila for five seconds.” I decided it was best to opt for the wine, little did i realise it was laced with vodka. As i finished the glass and wrenched like a trooper, Adam gave me some comforting words “everyone has to drink a spirit one way or another.” (On those last few words Tim walks into the room and flicks on the lamp. He is carrying a schoolboy’s outfit that no-one had the misfortune of seeing.) Tim: It could have been worse, they could have laced it with Tequila. Oh the irony. (Alex seems a little miffed as to why Tim would enter his guest room, flick on the lamp and be carrying a schoolboy outfit, all the time interrupting Alex mid-promo. He gives Tim a “What the?!” glare as “Here today” kicks in on the stereo. Tim perks up and jigs around like mexican jigging bean, flinging the outfit over his shoulder in an all too frantic manner.) Alex: Hey! I am attempting to make a profound statement. Tim: Using one drunken college night as a point of reference? What profoundness could possible come from such nostalgia---Wait it’s not some hidden sub-reference to infamous Last Call match is it? Alex: You're an idiot! Tim: I joke. But seemingly you are not…joking…that is. Perhaps that's where you and i differ. You are someone who feels the mood, where as i am someone who… Alex: …is a rude interrupting, yet amazingly welcoming, host. Tim: Least you forget that sonny. (Alex chuckles at Tim’s feeble attempts to sound authoritative. As Tim enters the on-suite bathroom, Tim’s real reason for entering, Alex picks up where he left off. i.e nowhere of any note) Alex: So there i was, completely wasted, my mates laughing as i wrench more and more…and i am forced to puke up the remnants of the wine all over the floor. Now you may not think anything constructive can come of this and back in ‘99 you'd be right, however this is ‘03 and i have moved on, grown up. I don’t chose to drink Spirits in the morning because one drunken night has put me off wine for good. I chose to drink spirits in the morning because it’s what i like to drink. (Alex’s smirk soon straightens out.) Alex: I follow nobodies lead. I am no wino, neither am i some idealistic t-total hippy. I cannot be pigeonholed as my dearest friend Matt Keith so valiantly tries to. I certainly do no look to attain some title of ‘cool’ especially if Matt Keith is the benchmark for said coolness. I am my own man, no need for any frills, for my mind and my wrestling abilities shall elevate me. I have no desire to spout meaningless catchphrases or cliché bad mouthing. I don’t even require a crowd of mindless sheep to make me feel secure. I am above all that, i’ve been there, done that and it’s merely child's play. I am above you Matt Keith, for you will be merely child’s play and more so than anything i am certainly above any ‘level’ that you see as high. Tim: Maybe you should call the match of then, if you’re just gonna beat him that easy?! (Alex turns the stereo up to the max, as both men leap up and engage in some spontaneous Karaoke as The Beach Boys blare out. Alex takes the lead…) Alex: “I keep looking for a place to fit in.....where I can speak my MINDDDD! And I've been trying hard, so hard, to find the people that I won't leave behind… (Tim jumps in as the two duet, resulting in a weird wave of high and low pitched noises…neither hitting the right key. And as the two sing along, we fade out to a few fleeting words…) “…join our club!”
