Advantages

4 Jun 2004

By Mr. Darkness

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
After a six hour flight the fatigued duo of Mr. Darkness and his ever loyal Butler wait in the parlour of the airport searching for a “Darkness-BSB” sign while ignoring the crowd which gathers to show their hatred to Mr. Darkness. Eventually the Butler spots the sign and points it out to his master, the two walk towards the sign at a steady pace, not wanting to slow down to much for the fans pleasure nor speed up to look as if they are frightened of the vicious mob. Upon reaching the sign the chauffer takes both suitcases from the Butler and walks down the celebrity departure corridor, away from the public. Mr. Darkness examines the walls of the corridor, staring at the photos of famous people who have achieved immortality and knowing that each one had an advantage. Behind every famous celebrity there are a thousand better who live a much more difficult life due to the simple fact they never got the advantage of being spotted, and that is what life was all about. Advantages, if you have one exploit it. The driver turned to face the rich Mr. Darkness and gazed at the grey and white pinstriped jacket, the white shirt which even after the extremely long flight still remains unwrinkled, even the black and white pinstriped trousers looked expensive. The chauffer then thought about his own life, he struggled to stay afloat despite him working hard ever day and had lost his family due to his lack of wealth and deep down inside he hated Mr. Darkness as he hates all rich people who have never worked in a factory or done any other form of manual labour. Yet unbeknownst to both men, they agreed with each other, advantages must be exploited and are what sets the rich and famous apart from the poor. The trio eventually reached the end of the corridor and worked out into a deserted car park, the VIP parking lot. A single vehicle sat on the road, the vehicle being a black stretched limo which had not been rented by Mr. Darkness but actually brought especially to transport him to the Bourbon Street Brawl, after that the limo would probably never be used again. The chauffer opened the back door for Mr. Darkness to climb into followed as always be his Butler and then closed the door and slid into the driver’s seat. He looked at the left wing mirror and could see Mr. Darkness looking at a gold Rolex checking time, the chauffeur’s grip on the steering wheel tensed yet being ever the professional he is he awaits for Mr. Darkness to signal he can pull out of the car park and towards the destination of one of the finest hotels in New Orleans. Eventually the signal comes and the limo gracefully pulls out of the parking lot and into the highway. “Do you believe in advantages Butler?” Mr. Darkness suddenly enquires knocking the Butler of guard. He turns to face his master and hesitates for a few seconds, he knew a pleased master was always better then an angered one. “Of course master, after all you have given me a great advantage in life,” answers the Butler in a genuinely sincere voice. Then again the Butler’s previous master had hardly been as generous, and that master was his own father. “So who do you personally think has the advantage at the Bourbon Street Brawl?” asks Mr. Darkness once again catching the Butler off guard, it was unusual for Mr. Darkness to even chat to the Butler let alone ask for his opinion twice. The Butler hesitated once again, after all many of the competitors in this years Bourbon Street Brawl have an advantage. Eventually the Butler sums up an answer. “Well, you have the advantage due to your intelligence sir. While others will take reckless risks in a match life this one you take calculated risks,” this didn’t seem to have the effect the Butler was looking for. Mr. Darkness did not smile or look pleased instead he just sat there as if accepting the Butler’s answer yet knowing it was not genuine. “Yet many of the other competitors have advantages do they not Butler? Dave Harley being confined to a wheelchair is not a disadvantage for him like he would have everyone think, he knows full well that many will not look to take the match to him because they will either feel sorry for him or merely overlook him. Mal Somers? He has the advantage of the predictions, he is one of the superstars that has been placed quite highly in the poll of predictions. Quietus is another guy who has the advantage, despite the fact he is hated by most many still predict he will win the Bourbon Street Brawl and there is no doubt that has done wonders to his self confidence. The Northern Wrestling Conference competitors have the advantage of unity, this is there show and there is no way they will back down and allow anyone from LDA or SCWF to take their title shot without one of the toughest fights that it is humanly possible to bring. The Devils Own? Perhaps they have one of the largest advantages over everyone else, the advantage of belonging to the devil. They will not utter a witty remark just for the viewers but they will go out there and get the job done, particularly the Muppet Man who is also somewhat of an underdog yet one of the most likely to win competitors in the match? Then there is the man with two advantages, Chance. He has not only lady fortune on his side but also the experience, he is the only one in the match to have won the Bourbon Street Brawl before and that will no doubt prove to be a massive advantage.” The car halts at a red light at the Chauffer stares back at the passengers interested in the topic of conversation. Mr. Darkness shakes his head in disgust and leans forward pressing a red button which blackens out the window separation between the chauffer and the passengers. “Now compare that to the advantage common people have, even this ignorant driver here has an advantage. The advantage of the disadvantage.” “The advantage of the disadvantage?” The Butler interrupts his master completely baffled by the sudden turn of conversation and the ridiculous phrase, not that he’d tell his master that. “Yes, the advantage of the disadvantage. These low common people who never have to struggle the same life like a rich person take money from rich people. In fact, at the Bourbon Street Brawl this is the one advantage I have. You see my intelligence will not be an advantage in a match as idiotic as this one, yet my disadvantage will. I a terrible win record and have very little experience of no rules wrestling apart fro the ladder/at your service match from EWA Agony Inc. Therefore I have a massive disadvantage, and it may be that disadvantage that proves to be the strongest advantage in the Bourbon Street come Monday,” replies Mr. Darkness baffling the Butler even more by the philosophy. Will the advantage of the disadvantage pay off? That question will be answered this Monday at the Bourbon Street Brawl…
