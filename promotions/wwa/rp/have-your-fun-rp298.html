WWA (1997–2019) Roleplays

Have your fun.

25 Jul 2003

By Matt Keith

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
FADE IN: INT. TOTAL DARKNESS- ??? [It is completely pitch black, no sight, no sounds, just eerie darkness. Suddenly a voice is heard, a voice that sounds a lot like Matt Keith, it echo’s] Matt Keith: Hypocrite? [Laughing] that’s the best you can do Knight? Label me a hypocrite and think it’s over? [Slowly the lights fade on, getting brighter and brighter, until it’s almost too bright. We see Matt Keith sitting on a single steel chair in an empty all white room. His head is down and his hair is dangling in his face, this is not like the Matt Keith we all know.] Matt Keith: Good job Knight you have a very analytical mind seeing as how you can analyze those words I had for you and attempt to discredit me, disrespect me, and disenfranchise me. [Claps] well-done jerky. Nonetheless in the end all you are doing is flapping your gums. Look at me Knight, look into my eyes, do you see fear? Yeah you have your way with words, you can talk one hell of a match but you’re not worth two dead fly’s in my or anyone else eyes. I was going to let you have you’re little fun and talk, get the last word but I thought about something, something hit me…and that was if I let you have the last word I’ll only feed that huge ego of yours. Am I a hypocrite Knight? That remains to be seen my mentally challenged friend. [Matt looks into the camera; his hair is still in his face] Matt Keith: You Think that you don't need to look up my history because that's the past, and this is now. Well Knight, if you choose to ignore history that's fine. But I believe the saying is “History repeats itself.” And you can be sure that this Monday I'll put you away just like I've put away every other punk-ass bastard who's tried to talk tough with me. [The intense gaze of Matt seems the be burning right through the TV’s of the viewers at home] Matt Keith: [laughs] They always say ignorance is bliss, so in that case I guess you must be in heaven right now Knight. The reason I say that is because you don’t know me junior and yet you make assumptions, I may be a rookie but you’ve just made the biggest rookie mistake of your career. You can quote me until you’re blue in the face but that will never make you a better wrestler than me. And personally you’re starting to make me sick, first you’re in the EWA looking like a f*cking cartoon cutout, then when I think maybe, just maybe I won’t have to see that b*tch boy face of yours here you are in the WWA…telling me that you’re better than me. What has the world come to? [He laughs to himself] Matt Keith: You know? I think I’m really starting to understand you, and why you seem to be too dense to understand the slightest things. Do you think that racing and acting a fool is what I’m only about? Do you think playing pranks on people is the same Matt Keith that will be at Fiesta? You dumb chump stain! I think it’s time for you to really understand who I am. Monday you’re getting into the ring with the icon killer Mr. Alex Knight. You are going toe to toe with the man that puts legends on the shelf, grant you, you’re nowhere near being a legend and never will, but you will do just fine. You fill your promos with empty threats and empty promises. You promise me pain, suffering, humiliation...everything you can think of that will take the shame off of yourself and move it to me. But please remember Knight that I have accomplished much in this business, in a very short time. I am fully aware of every move I make and the consequences that comes with them. You disrespected me and made it personal, and I will promise you this, at Fiesta I will do anything and everything I can to make you look like the fool you are, I will out wrestle you and out shine you every step of the way. And when it’s all said and done you will know first hand what it feels like to be hit with the Deadly Device. [Matt takes his hand and flips his hair back out of his face] Matt Keith: Having said that let me ask you again, are you ready for Fiesta? Are you ready to step into the ring with the biggest challenge you have ever faced? Are you truly ready to back up those empty words of yours? Like I’ve been saying all week, I’m ready. But as for you it is extremely obvious that you’re writing checks that your ass can’t cash. [Again Matt laughs] I have seen many men like you before Alex Knight. It is impossible to compete in this sport without running into a hundred of you. You run your mouth, claiming to be the best there is, the best there was, and the best there ever will be, or some other sort of bullshit that sounds catchy. You surround yourself with yes-men and expect everyone to bow before you like some sort of prissy little king. And some do, until someone like me comes around and rocks your little world so hard your tiny crown falls off your tiny head, and you go whining and crying until someone will gingerly put it back on. It's sad, really. What are you going to do when I do beat your ass beyond recognition Monday? I’ll tell you what you’re going to do, you’re going to cry and b*tch. [Winy tone] Oh he cheated, I wasn’t at one hundred percent, my back was hurting, he hit me with a chair. It’s going to be something, it always is. [Matt stands up] Matt Keith: Now, I know you have the attention span of a three year old, so I'm sure I need to wrap this up for you Knight. Go ahead and delude yourself with thoughts of what will happen at Fiesta. Claim that you’re going to beat me within an inch of my life...that you're going to one-up me, and that you KNOW you're the next hot stuff in the WWA. But you, me, and everyone else out there knows that you haven't got a Godd*mned thing. You're dead weight in this federation. And if you know anything about dead weight Alex, you know that it gets dropped as soon as possible. [He pushes his hair back again] Matt Keith: Oh and, if I were you I would keep looking over my shoulder Monday. No telling what might happen. [WOOOOOOM! Just when Matt was feeling the intensity the almost invisible door swings open. Danny stick his head in peaking around the corner like an idiot] Danny: What are you doing in my stage room? Matt Keith: Damn it dude can’t you see I had an atmosphere going? Danny: Oops. [Danny’s in the doorway with a stupid look on his face] Matt Keith: Man I need some new friends. [Matt stands there shaking his head] FADE TO BLACK
