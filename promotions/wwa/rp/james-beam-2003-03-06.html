WWA (1997–2019) Roleplays

James Beam

6 Mar 2003

By Jimmi Zion

Item 6 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 6 Mar 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
*[Fade In] *[Cut to the interior of the hospital waiting room where we left "Sky Flyin" Jimmi Zion at as he awaits for any news on his wife and unborn child's new progress. About two hours have passes since we last heard from Zion. He is still in his wrestling attire and has had no opportunity to go back and change. He still paces back and forth waiting for any further news when he will see his wife. With each passing moment, he seems to grow even more angry than before. While he is pacing, the sliding glass main doors in the front of the main entrance opens up.] [Soon we see an dark skinned complexioned bald man who appears like he is of passed middle aged. He does appear a little younger, but no younger than 50. He is completely bald on the top of his head, but has a nicely trimmed gotee. He is wearing a very nice black Armani dress suit with a thick overcoat and white scarf over his suit. He almost bares a small striking resemblense to actor Avery Brooks. He walks in with two very large, buffed white suited gentlemen behind him. They appear like their bodygaurds for the man. The gentlemen is also carrying a black duffle bag.] [Soon the gentleman finishes his search and looks over at Zion pacing and walks over to him as if he knows him. Zion stops his pacing as he sees these familer faces approach him. Zion meets him have half to greet him, let's listen in.] Dark bald gentleman: "Jimmi, how is my daughter?" Jimmi Zion: "She's fine Max. Believe it or not, both she and the baby are still safe." Maximillion Rush: "I got here as soon as I could." Zion: "How'd did you get here so fast Max?" Rush: "I took my Concord jet. You'd be surprised how fast that jet like that can fly, and had to be here for my daughter, what did the doctor say about her condition?" Zion: "He said that both she and the child are lucky to be alive. I call it all God's favor, but that's just me. He said that he wanted to keep her in observation a little longer and would come and get us when we can see her." Rush: "Excellent, thank God. In the mean time that gives us plenty of time to talk." Zion: "Sure what about?" Rush: "Come lets sit down and I'll tell you what's on my mind." Zion: "Okay." [Zion and Rush walks over to one of the couches and sit down to talk to one another man to man.] Zion: "I'm SO sorry that I let your daughter down. I take full responsiblity for putting Mikalia and my child in this position." Rush: "Jimmi, don't beat yourself up. Mikalia new of the consequences before siding with you. Although this was a huge gamble given your already voltile situation with this Cell, still she new the risks. She also didn't expect that this group of men would stoop this low. Mistakes were made, just count our blessings and move on." Zion: "So you know about The Cell?" Rush: "I know everything that involves you and my Mikalia. I've watched this situation very closely and I had a feeling something like this would of happened sooner-or-later. Well it happened now we're at where do we go from here?" Zion: "Where do we go from here Max?" Rush: "I'm glad you asked because I thought this over a great deal on the way over here from London. I think that when Mikalia is cleared that she needs to come back to with me to England." Zion: [Standing up suddenly, visibly upset] "What? That's bloody bull sh*t. I can take care of her just fine." Rush: "Jimmi, you can't and tonight showed it. Sooner or later you need to ask for help. It's not like I am offering my bodyguards to help you...not that you would accept them anyway. I asking you to let Mikalia come back with me." Zion: "You got that right I don't need your body guards. I need Mikalia in my corner. She is my everything. She brings out the best in me." Rush: [Trying to calm and console his son-in-law's anger.] "Remember that there are two sides to every coin. She also brings out your weaknesses. I know that you love your wife and soon you will be a father, well I love my daughter. More than you will ever know. I gave your permission to court and later marry my daughter. I'm even the one who told you to quit beating around the bush and tell her how you felt. Now I am telling you again to let her go. It's not like she's leaving you, she will just be more safe in London. Besides her mother and I miss her. We haven't seen her since the honeymoon, and after you returned from that, you flew back to the states to resume your career with the WWA, so we need to be with our daughter." [Zion tries to respond by brushing him off, but Maximillion keeps a hold of him to finish his speech.] Rush: "Jimmi, my lad, I have always thought of you like a son to me. Ever since your father died, I met him and told him that I would be a good father figure for you. I will not back out of my promise to him. You said it yourself that she was your strength. You have been depending too much on Mikalia." [A look of shock appears in Zion's face as he glares back at Maximillion looks him in the eye and tell him to let him finish.] Rush: "Let me finish please lad. Mikalia has become a crutch for you and I believe that it's time for you to shine without her in your corner. I know that you can make it in the WWA, and you don't have to rely on Mikalia to do it. She loves you regardless of whether or not you win or lose, but I know you are a winner, you will win, but you do not need Mikalia in your corner. She will be safe with us in London, and you can focus solely on you and defending your titles. You are the WWA X-treme/Cruiserweight champion, it's time for YOU to shine once and for all. I have faith in you." "Look at me, deep down you know I am right and this is the only thing to do. Do it for Mikalia, do it for us, do it for your child on the way. The sky is NOT even the limit to what you can do in this world my boy, and I know that you can defeat this James Beam next week on Fiesta. Think of it this way. God was on your side, he showed mercy upon you. He spared your wife and child. Now you can go out and fight like your were truly meant to fight. You will beable to fight... Without restrictions... without limitations... without any hinderances... Now takes these words to heart and then walk into that match and defeat James Beam. He thinks that he can beat you. He will try and exploit this situation to his advantage in order to defeat you. You have to take this situation and let it drive your to succeed against him. You are the champion and until he pins you, you will remain the champion. He will not be an easy oponant, he could very well be the hardest man you've ever faught, but I know you can do it lad." [Maximillion takes the black duffle bag and hands it to Zion.] Rush: "Here are you things from the arena, I even included a little extra money should you need anything else. You do your thing and let me do my thing. When Mikalia comes to and we can see her. Go visit her, and then get on the plane and get where this match will be so you can defeat Beam. Be focus, be at peace, be ready to win because I know you can." Zion: [Takes the black duffle bag, trying to mentally take the wisdom of his father-in-law to heart.] "I will Max, I won't let you down and I will shine on Fiesta against James Beam. He will learn first hand that my 15 minutes aren't ending, but my immortality is just beginning. He will be a difficult oponant, but I know that I can take him. Thank you, I will talk to Mikalia, and then I will head out of here for Fiesta." Rush: "Now that's what I wanted to here. Show them who the real champion is." Zion: "I will." [Maximillion gives Zion a quick hug before Dr. Newworthy walks out and over to them.] Dr. Newworthy: "Mr. Zinn, you're wife has come to, and she requests to see you. You may see her whenever you're ready." Zion: "Thank you doctor." Rush: "You better go see her Jimmi, you don't wanna keep her waiting, especially when she ask for you. Patience is not one of her virtues." Zion: "Tell me about it." Rush: "I'll be out here when your done. We'll make sure you'll get to the next town for Fiesta." [Zion nods to Maximillion as he walks off toward the room where Mikalia is staying at. The camera catches a quick conversation between Maximillion and his two bodygaurds as the camera fades out.] *[Fade Out]
