A Brave New World

26 Apr 2006

By Kindred

Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The ocean. It is serene. Calming. Tranquil. Some of history's best thinking has come staring over the oceans. Some of the world's finest literature has been penned looking into the vast depths. Some of the finest minds have looked to it for inspiration, clarity, and peace-of-mind. This is no different. Actually it is quite the same. Standing perched upon a high cliff staring down upon the ocean is Proteus. Dressed all in black, his dark shades cover his eyes. He is, for all intensive purposes, emotionless. "..." But his mind. His mind is running a million miles a minute. Before him stands a near impossible goal of not only competing in the Bourbon Street Brawl, but WINNING it. 18 competitors. One goal. To win. To beat everyone else. To SURVIVE . "..." Survive? That would be the easy part. But winning, winning is a matter of perspective. Before Proteus stood the fate of not only himself, but of his brothers, Legion and Kharon. Before him stood the decision of winning the Brawl, or gunning for either Tracer Bullet or Denrol. The World Tag Team Champions, as it were. A singular goal? Or the good of the group? He knew he could win the brawl. He knew it was well within his abilities to set the WWA on its ear bu upsetting everyone and walking away as the victor. The sole survivor. "..." But it wasn't that easy. Also, he knew that it would be just as easy to take out either member of The Franchise Players and set himself and his brothers ahead of the learning curve, so-to-speak, as WWA Tag Team Champions. Decisions. Decisions. The soothing ocean breeze gently touches His face. That always made him smile. A little reminder by nature that She could destroy us at any point in time that she wanted to. "..." Nature. Kindred wasn't unlike Nature in that fact. Beside him fought two equally lethal individuals. All three dedicated to the greater good of the family. Of the Kindred. That was a fact. That was undeniable. And all three of them would die to ensure that it was as well known to everyone else as it was to them. "..." To them. 'Them'. A collective group. 'Them'. He smiled. Two shadows snuck up behind him. He knew who it was. He knew why they were here. He could almost know exactly what it was that they were thinking. Brothers have that kind of luxoury. Although their voices were identical, he had no problem identifying which was which, and it was Kharon that spoke first. "What are you doing?" It was a simple question, and a million different answers came to mind but only one escaped his lips. "Thinking." "About Bourbon Street Brawl? Or about the Tag match?" Legion. His interogative nature preceded him. He was always the one with the question. Kharon always spoke deliberatively. With purpose. "Both." He turned to look at his brothers. They were like mirrors staring back at him. In their eyes, he could see tons of different questions pending. He knew of their uncertainty. He knew of their cautiousness. They were slightly aware of Proteus' struggle. They wanted to know if he would fight for the greater good or for himself in the brawl. "You know where I stand. You needn't question it." And just like that, their doubts disappeared. All three exchanged acknowledging glances. "What is your plan?" Again it was Legion with the question. Kharon rarely spoke when Legion was around because he knew his brother's inquisative nature would answer anything he wanted to know. "I have a plan that will have all of us come out triumphantly." He could tell Legion wanted to whip another question towards his brother, but Proteus halted him with a quick motion, answering his question before it even reached his lips. "I have forseen it."
