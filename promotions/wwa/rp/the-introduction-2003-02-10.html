The introduction

10 Feb 2003

By Ender

Item 4 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 10 Feb 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
***The Scene opens up with Ender sitting in an armchair twiddling his thumbs*** Ender: Hello, most of you don’t know me, so allow me to introduce myself. My name is Ender. My real name is irrelevant; you have no need to know that. It’s long forgotten and shall never be spoken again. As I sit here, there are many questions, some of you think I am gothic, well I can reassure you, and I certainly am. ***He takes in a few breaths*** Ender: But I unlike most stereotypical Goths do care for politics. I hate corporate America. They are the ones who “mold” you into something you are not, they are the ones who try and taint your ways and taint your thoughts. They try and make you what they want you to be, the perfect bouncy haired little bitch. ***There is a knock at the door and a man in black comes in, he is wearing an Atticus T-shirt. He pulls up a metal chair and sits down next to Ender and also starts to tilt his head in thought.*** Ender: I’d like to show you my good friend, Steve. You will be seeing a lot of Steve, seeing as well he is kind of my manager. Anyway, Steve can’t talk very well unless he really needs to because he pierced his mouth shut. Try and say a few words to these good people. Steve: He’ muh up? Ender: OK, well now that you know Steve, well now back to the point. Seeing as I am done introducing myself, I will now talk about my match on Fiesta. As you know, I am facing, “Hein Bloeck” he is a man who I already know. We both just came here from the pitiful wasteland of the WIA. There Hein Bloeck, was at the top, which is understandable seeing as he had been there for a longer time than I. But here, we are on equal footing. Here I am right along with you. Here I can take my shot at you, make you my first stepping-stone, my first mold to break. Steve: ‘ELL EM! Ender: Oh, ok well for those of you who didn’t pick it up yourselves, a mold is just basically the perfect image. The one that everyone expects you to look like. You know what I mean, blonde hair, nice face, slut, shiny lips, and not to forget the shallow personality. So look around at yourself, are you blonde? Are you shallow? Do you fit these other qualities? If so, then are you proud of the fact that you aren’t living a life of your own? I know what you must be thinking… this man… who is he? Why is he so mean, yet he does not know me? I can answer those questions, because as a child you shunned me, you made me feel an outcast. So now I am here to take these idols that you love, and turn them into outcasts just like me. I hope they are your idols too, because then you will follow them, and I will reign supreme, I will in my hands hold the power to change and shape the young generation, into exactly what I want! Sure you may say, “Isn’t that just a mold all over again, but in another form?” NO! It is not, because I want chaos, chaos is never the same you never see the same thing again. You always see blurs, little shadows of the real action. I want you to live my life! I want you to feel the way I feel. I want you to realize that you will never see a true form of life with your eyes again! You will only see parts of what you need, parts of what you hope for. Then you can say, “Well anyway, what does this have to do with your match ahead again?” I will tell you what it has to do with the match ahead of me; it has all to do with it! When I face Hein Bloeck, I will show you an example of what will happen to your precious “Idols.” I will show you my true power! He will be the first to be changed the first to be punished. He will see blurs and only thin lines of life, he will hold on by a thread. I can’t wait to see what your world says when you feel the way I feel! ***Ender rips the microphone off his shirt and slams it down with a thud and taps Steve on the shoulder and they open the door and walk out, the scene fades to black***
