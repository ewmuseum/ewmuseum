Mastermind Sucks!

10 Jun 2003

By Mal Somers

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Scene – Mal Somers and Dave Harley, two thirds of X-Corp are seated at either side of a fold out card table. Each stare intently down at the table. Harley wears his customary sunglasses and Somers has Rookie strapped around his neck like a necklace. After a few moments, Harley grins and looks up.) Harley: Red, blue, white, yellow! (Somers flips the card table over in disgust.) Somers: This game absolutely sucks a$$! You keep guessing the colours right off the bat! Harley: I gotta agree with you there Chairboy, Mastermind is a crap game. Speaking of (makes inverted comma gestures with his fingers) “Masterminds”, have you seen Enigma running his mouth on Insider lately? Somers: Oh yeah, the self proclaimed Mastermind of the WWA. He sucks more than this game he named himself after! He may think he is the master mind, I think he is the master of putting worms on hooks when he goes fishing! Harley: Uh Mal, usually I get your out there comments but you’ve lost me on that one. Somers: Sorry Dave, I confuse myself sometimes. Whats a word for someone who puts the worms on hooks when you go fishing? Harley: Baiter. Somers: Cool. So I was saying that Enigma is a master... Harley: (interrupting) Oh I get it!! (Both men chuckle as they pick up the table and all the little pieces of the game.) Harley: So this guy, he sits there, doing what he does, in his little lair. I wonder if he thinks he is Dr. Evil, sitting in his underground lair? Somers: His domain. Harley: No, I don’t think he is the master of his domain! (The two chuckle again.) Somers: So, what would that make Joey Spray? Fat Ba**ard? Harley: And Se7en? Mr Bigglesworth? Somers: Well, whatever way you look at it, they are proving to be a thorn in X-Corps side. I’m facing Joey for the TV Title at Fiesta this Saturday, and then you are in a four way with Enigma involved this Monday at Meltdown. Harley: Plus, Leviathan has accepted your challenge and will be facing you on Meltdown too. Who’s a busy boy then? Somers: Looks like after our tag run, we are straight out of the frying pan and into the fire in terms of singles action. I can't wait to lock up with Joey and knock that sneer right off his face. And now he is talking about putting his ugly mug all over WWA Programming. I can’t let that happen. Hopefully this Saturday I can remove him from our sights...FOR GOOD! Harley: You tell ‘em Mal! And in terms of my match, it’s a weird one. I’ll be gunning for Dr Evil and don’t think for a second that our new “partner”, Grunge won't want to join in for that matter! Somers: Just watch your back, Grunge and Adams will want the win as much as you. Harley: But will they want to kick Dr Evils a$$ as much as I will? Somers: About as much as I want to get my hands on Leviathan at Meltdown. Harley: If you make it past Joey K! Somers: Thanks for the vote of confidence bud. Harley: Sorry dude. I’m sure you’ll be fine. Anyways, nice stipulations...one point for every hit with a foreign object and five points if you put him through a table, first to sixteen wins! Somers: Well it was Rookie’s idea! He is nuts! But I figure if I put the Kingfish through two tables stacked on top of the announcers table and then shove a chair up his butt, that’ll be my sixteen points right there! Harley: Be careful, he might think you’re Lockdown. Somers: Huh? By putting him through tables? Harley: No, with what you had planned with the chair! (Both men sit at the table again and set up the Mastermind board. Harley looks at Rookie and grins.) Harley: Hmmm. Blue, red... Somers: Hold on a sec..(looks down at Rookie and then at Harley.) You can see the colour of the pegs in Rookie’s reflection! You’ve been doing this the whole time! (To Rookie) How much did he pay you to help out! Grrrrr! (Somers gets up and storms off in a huff.) Harley: Heh Heh! (looks to the camera) Just goes to show you, there is always a way around every mastermind!!! (Fade to black.)
