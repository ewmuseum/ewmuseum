Old School Baby!

21 Feb 2003

By Mal Somers

Item 1 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 21 Feb 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Scene – A darkened room. Illuminated by the radiation of a computer monitor's light against the wall. WWA Rookie Champion Mal Somers is tapping away at a keyboard. Noise can be heard eminating from the monitor. BLOP BLOP BLOP Somers – Hey there sports fans. Its been a busy week for yours truly. And not a week I am 100% proud of. My hot temper has resulted in me destroying my brand new laptop computer, and, by the looks of last nights Insider, managed to rile up young Mr Zion. Sorry Jimmi, but you needed a kick up the bum! Dave has smashed his computer too so I cant even borrow that! So now I have got the freight company to courier across to BC my other computer. My old Commodore 64 Tape Drive Computer. Ive been playing classics like Wizzard of Wor, Ghosts and Goblins, California Games and Impossible Mission. Ahhhh...good times. Yeah so sorry Jimmi, I didn’t mean to make you mad...you just caught me on a bad day. I guess you’re hoping on Saturday I don’t wake up on the wrong side of the bed because you’ll be in for a world of hurt. That’s not to say you didn’t get upset too listening to my interview on the radio! Which brings me to my favourite C64 game. Mr Angry. This little guy runs up and down stairs in a hotel trying to open and close doors. This guy reminds me a lot of you in fact. In fact this whole system reminds me of you. You are the tape drive to my DVD! See you on Saturday, Jimothy! (turns computer off and turns on the light which illuminates the hotel room) Somers – Which brings me onto Global Warfare. James Curzon goes on about being the victim of a conspiracy...well what about me? Who’s on MY team? Leviathan. Jackson “bleeping” Kraven. A Mystery Guy. Whats going on? Who have I got to watch my back? I’ll look one way and have my arch enemy, turn the other and it’s the Tomorrow Man. I swear, if this fourth person is Lockdown, I’m gonna sue! (pours himself a glass of scotch from the decanter and swallows the drink whole) Somers – But I am a proud East Coaster so Im going to do whatever I can to get our team to win. Risk life and limb like I do everyday. Make all the MALcoholics out there proud. But what am I going to do with my teammates? I mean with teammates like this, who needs enemies!” (pours another drink as the scene fades to black)
