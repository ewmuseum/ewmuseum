Desperate favors

5 Mar 2003

By Ian Tense

Item 3 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 5 Mar 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(A poster. More importantly, a poster of Wes Sanders planting some poor bastard with his Flaming DDT. Boy, the kids must've ate that up at the Wal-Marts and souvenier stands.) Wes Sanders. Now you know. Now you know EXACTLY what this is all about. (And in front of this poster, staring blankly at it as if it were a living, breathing person, is the WWA World Champion, The Prototype Ian Tense) Did the EMTs treat you okay? Were the nurses nice? How was the hospital food? (There is a certain sulleness to his face. Something that might belong to someone that, oh I don't know, just tapped out for the first time in his career...) I told you since day one, Wes. I told you at every turn, every corner, every stop. But time and time again, you wouldn't listen. And look where it got you. (Someone trying to deal with the bittersweetness of seemingly eliminating one nemisis, while creating two more...) How did it feel? How did the pain flash up and down your spine as I ended you? Did your pathetic life flash in fornt of your eyes? What was it like? (Or someone with the sudden realization that he is on a sinking ship) Can you even put it into words? Do try, Wes. Try real hard. (But more importanly than all that, a desperate man) Let others learn from your mistake. Do your boyscout duty. HELP others learn your lesson before it's too late. Damien Thorne...Kash...they both need your help Wes. Desperately. I hope they watched. I hope they saw. But they couldnt've. No one is stupid enough to do what they did. (The thought of both Damien Thorne and Kash both trying to tear him into bits suddenly ignites a mixed look of fear, and pure, uncaged fury) No one. (but it passes...) Now I know they aren't brain surgeons Wes. I know you'll have to talk real slow, but you tell them. They have to know. (Only to recapture him again. His calm voice turns suddenly diabolical as he looks at his clenched fist) They have to know. (But then he notices the poster again, and the blank stare engulfs his twisted face again) They have to know what it feels like to have my hands at their throats, Wes. How it feels to have to check around every corner, look in every door, sleep with one eye open. Goddamn it, they have to know. I want them to know, in the worst way. I want you to save them Wes. (Ian pokes the poster with his finger, accentuating the anxiety of every word that is about to come out of his mouth) You have that power, because on Monday Meltdown you can SAVE Kash. You can save him boyscout. He's your friend, right? Do him a favor. Warn him. Tell him not to come to the ring. FORDIB him if that is what it takes. Dear God, Wes, you have to. (God? What does HE have to do with anything? That must've been the thought that crossed his mind because that sullen, fearful/angry looks twists his face, like live, open nerves were just ignited...) You HAVE to because nothing I've done to you, NOTHING, will compare to what I'm bringing on Meltdown, Wes. (And anger starts to build) Tell him, Wes. Tell him if he and his alter ego decide to show up, that will be the last ANYONE ever sees of them. Show him your neck brace, Wes. Tell him how the doctors looked at you with that 'would you look at this poor bastard' look in their eyes. (and build) How the children ran away crying as they wheeled you into the emergency room. (and build) Tell him how your friends and family looked at you like it was your funeral while they visited. How it smelled like death all around you. What it felt like to not be able to move for 24 hours. (until it boils over enough to force him out of his chair, leaving him standing and screaming at the poster...) Because if you don't, Wes. I swear to God if you don't... (Until he realizes it and regains his composure) Well, we don't even want to THINK about what will happen to him. (Fade)
