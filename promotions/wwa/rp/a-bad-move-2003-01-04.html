A bad move....

4 Jan 2003

By Damian Thorne

Item 2 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 4 Jan 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Manhattan night.... A sky like black ice, peppered with shards of crystal. Thousands of coloured lights wind their way through the streets below...the lights of cars, taxis, limos and the like. I whole other story unfolding as far as the eye can see. All taken in by a pair of musing eyes. The eyes of Damian Thorne....as he sits, possibly on the roof of his appartment building, watching the world go by. Contemplating??? As the night is cold, he is dressed warm, and true to character, all in black. As he breaths, his breath frosts in the air, like clouds of captured conversation. He speaks quietly...in a measured tone. These words have been waiting to be spoken for quite some time. "You've made a very bad move Matthew...very bad indeed. I'll take nothing away from Kash, HE IS a rising star in the WWA. But, he's no Damian Thorne. And for you to place him, one on one against me....? That's one of two things.. Plain dumb... Or just plain sick. Kash is alot of things in the WWA. Bright Talented Weird climbing the ranks... ...but he's no US champ. He carries that belt on a technicality. He knows it, you know it, hell, even Edmunds could have become US champ that way! (pauses) But most of all Matthew... He's not ready for me. How do you get ready for me Kash? You can't. You can't prepare for war with an unknown foe. You can't stop what can't be stopped, hunt what can't be killed. Bring whatever game you need to Kash, because come meltdown, you're in for one hell of a shock." Damian stands from his perch and strolls to the edge of the safety cage surrounding the top of the building, casting his eyes out over the night. "Picture this Kash. You're like one of the lights down there on the street....miles below me. Sure, you have skill and tenacity, but so did EVD, and so did Randy Cross. Forget any other fool I've beaten in my career and what I may say about Eddie and Randy...those guys were the best at what they did. That is, until they met me. I've silence my critics time and time again. I couldn't beat the new innovator of extreme? Bullsh*t.... Not only did I beat him, I beat him in the match he wanted. I beat him at HIS GAME. Randy Cross? He still had his A game, no matter what people said. They didn't wrestle him, I did. I know what he's capable of, I FELT, what he was capable of. And where is he now? Beaten..at his own game. Retired, gone, finished. The point of this all Kash is simple. I have the talent, the power and the balls to do what I want, when I want. I can reach out and blink you out of existance as quick as I can click my fingers. Hell son, I'm Damian Thorne...I can do whatever the hell I like. But monday coming Kash, you have to face the consequences of the ultra lopsided booking of Matt Warr. You have to be thrown to the lion as it where. Do you even realise what's waiting for you on monday? a man who is capable of dispicable acts of violence.. Remorseless Fearless Relentless Ahead of his game.... And way ahead of yours Kash." Thorne reaches into his pocket and fishes out a silver dollar. He begins to idly flick it in his gloved hand, rolling it from finger to finger, then finally flicking it into the air. "As quick as that Kash, as quick as the flick of this coin, it can all change. This monday, will be the true measure of you Kash. Can you show me, and the rest of the WWA that you have what it takes to hold that strap and run with us big dogs? Or are you just another belt shiner, keeping that strap warm for deserving people like me? Show me Kash.. Show the world... Show me that you're worthy of standing in my shadow Kash. Because if you don't...I'll f**king bury you Kash. What I did to EVD and Randy Cross combined won't even come close to what I rain on you. So bring what you got Kash, bring it all And pray.....that it's enough" Damian flicks the coin one last time, sending it careening over then edge of the saftey cage and out into the night
