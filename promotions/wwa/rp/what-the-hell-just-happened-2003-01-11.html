"What the hell just happened?"

11 Jan 2003

By Jimmi Zion

Item 1 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 11 Jan 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
*[Fade In] *[Salt Lake City, Utah] *[Cut to the interior of a hotel highrise penthouse suite in downtown Salt Lake City Utah. It's a one person honeymoon/newleywed suite. At this time there appears to be no signs of human life as it looks like it is empty. After a few moments of scanning and waiting, the front door opens up and two people walk in. The camera turns around to identify the two individuals as "Sky Flyin" Jimmi Zion and his beautiful wife and manager, Mrs Mikalia Rush-Zinn. Mikalia is wearing a white ladies Armani business suit. Zion is wearing; black baggy Fubu jeans, white Nike tennis shoes, and a red shiney/glittery/ Gazooks rockstar shirt, and his long dreadlocks are neatly braided. Upon entering the room, he immedaitely drops the luggage and slams the door. He walks over to the bar and pours himself a drink of Jim Beam, then takes it out onto the balcony and slowly begins nursing it down. It's obvious that there is something that has Zion all emotional and agree. His wife's eyes follows Jimmi with much concern on her face. She follows him out to the balcony where he is leaning on the balcony rail looking down sipping his Jim Beam. She puts her arms around to hold him kissing his neck. The camera moves into position, let's listen in.] Mikalia Rush-Zinn: "Jimmi baby, it's just us lets talk about about it. What's bothering you?" "Sky Flyin" Jimmi Zion: "F[..]kin co-champion!? You have got to be kidding me. The nerve of that goddamn ref to make me a co-champion. I was expecting something much more like I get first crack at Chance for the titles, so I can finish this s[..]t between us once and for all. So I can prove once and for all that I am the superior wrestler, not him. I needed to prove to everyone that I am the true WWA X-Treme/Cruiserweight champion. Who in the hell does that ref think he is? A co-champion? My f[..]kin god! These WWA people get dumber each week. What did Matt Warr do, make all the personel sign a waver that says you much forsake all forms of common sense and intelligence at the door?" Mikalia: "Well Jimmi, I think you need to bite the bullet and share in someone's glory for once. I know you like to work alone, but there are somethings that even you need to share...if only for a season. This is only temporary. Soon enough you will have your opportunity to take Chance on and unify the title once and for all. Not to mention you have more than enough time to educate Enigma in that time. Afterall, he is not a true believer in you yet. This is far from over and you have a true opportunity to kick Enigma's ass once and for all. Then you can move onto Chance and take his half of the title for yourself. This is a very interesting position for you but like usual, you can get yourself out of it. I know you can." Zion: "I know I can Mikalia. You're right, but still it utterly pisses me off. This was not what I had in mind. I wanted the title more than anything if I needed to kick Chance's ass all over the arena in order to get it then I would of done exactly that. I actually felt like I lost last week on Meltdown." [Jimmi downs the rest of the glass and then chucks it off the building watching crash to the ground below. Luckily it didn't hit any passersby below. It shatters into peices on the sidewalk below.] Mikalia: "Of course, you hate to lose baby. It's understandable, and you view this as a loss to say the least, but you always bounce back. This is no different. Enigma definately hasn't seen the last of you and Chance will bare witness to the true WWA Extreme/Cruiserweight champion." Zion: "Enigma despises me, well the feeling's mutual and I plan on proving to him what I have been saying all week long. He said that he was gonna kill me in order to beat me. Well the way I see it he failed just as much as I did. He didn't even come close to killing me. I'm still here and I dare him to try and take the breath from my body. I dare him to do his worst to me. He tells me not to get in his way, maybe I outta get in his way just to see how serious he really is. What Enigma needs to realize is Chance is not his. He is all mine. I am the co-champion. I deserve to be the whole champion. I am the next in line to unify the championship. There are no such thing as two champions. There can be only one. I WILL BE THE ONLY ONE! Fine I am a pompous prick, but I am a pompous prick who knows without a shadow of a doubt that I am right. Enigma will find out one way or the other." [Zion escorts Mikalia by the hand and walks back into the suite inside. The camera follows him over to the large sofa where they sit down together.] Zion: "Chance. Here my words. I want the other half. I need the other half. I did not win. You did most of the work and it pisses me pff more than anything. How one man with a messed up knee and back could riddle a fresh man on his back is totally beyond me. Obviously I wasn't fighting to my true abilities. I looked like s[..]t in that match. I will agree with Enigma one one thing. However I will not stay that way. I want...no I need to issue out my right to a match against you. We need to fight each other onca and for all. We need to fight to unify the WWA X-Treme/Cruiserweight title. We need only one champion and I will be that one champion or I will die. There will be only one champion and you're looking at him Chance. You name the time and the place and I will be there ready to take back what belongs to me. Most importantly I'll be ready to finish what I started with you Chance several weeks ago. You will know X-Treme incarnate. You will know who the supernova blasting...supernatural...phenemononal...Mister Millenium in the flesh before your eyes. I wil set the highlight reels on fire for years. They will never forget you Chance. Most importantly they will never forget me. Just accept my challenge and meet me there ready to hand your half of the titles over to me. You have no choice. I am using my right to a rematch." "Then after I am done with Chance, I will finish with Enigma and show him just who the real X-treme cruiserweight really is. It isn't you. It's the man who has X-Treme literally encoded and embedded into his DNA. It's the man who eat, drinks, and sleeps supernatural greatness. I am the man who will say what I mean and I will put you where you belong. I will show just who the real champion is and it isn't Chance. It certainly isn't you. After I am done with Chance, I will finish you." "Now lets talk about my oponant for this week right here in Salt Lake City on Fiesta. Let's talk about James Curzon. I don't know much about you, but then again the feeling is mutual. You probably don't know much about me either. That's okay because I plan on telling you everything you need to know about me. I plan on introducing you to true supernatural greatness. I plan on showing you why I am Mister Millenium. I am the living breathing highlight reel. Your gonna be famous, but not as famous as what I will be. You will not be forgotten, but you will also be the man who got whooped by X-Treme incarnate. You will lose big time to Sky Flyin Jimmi Zion. You say that I don't stand a chance. How wrong...er I mean how stupid can you be? So toast all the champaign and wine away with your friend Mr. Fagin. Drink yourself plumb rotten, and then watch your hopes and dreams fade before your very eyes as you lay face up looking up at the lights. Hell bring in weapons if you like, like they will do you any good against me." [He pauses for a moments as he re-thinks everything to himself.] Zion: "Come to think about it, I think that I need to change some of what I said. Normally I go for highlight reels of the year matches, but instead I am just gonna have to go for short quick matches. The sooner I get passed you Curzon. The quicker I fight and unify the titles from Chance. The sooner I settle the score with Enigma. Sorry to do this to you but I have bigger fish to fry than your ass, so why don't you make like a good little jobber, put on a good show but then know when to fall when the time comes. There are more important people for me to fight than you. It's nothing personal but I have plenty on my plate right now. Care to add anything else baby." [He looks at Mikalia who shakes her head "No".] Zion: "Then on that note, this is me signing off. See you Curzon on Fiesta. I'll will definately be ready for you. As for everyone else, Chance and Enigma better be ready for me because I will finish what I started with them. Now stick a fork in me, I'm done. Fade to black." [Jimmi gives Mikalia a passionate, sensual, subtle kiss on the lips as he waves off the camera making the cut motion to fade out. The camera complies and cuts out on his signal.] *FADE TO BLACK*
