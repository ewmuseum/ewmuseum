Drowning out the b*tching with music

1 Apr 2004

By Matt Keith

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
FADE IN: EXT. STREET, LOS ANGELES- MIDNIGHT [We open in downtown Los Angeles in the warehouse district; it’s pretty much quiet out with a few cars here and there but not anything you wouldn’t expect this late at night. The cameraman stands on the corner in front of a stop sign. Suddenly he pans and there we see Matt Keith, he walks right past the camera as if he doesn’t see it and makes a right down the block, on this side of the street we see a lot of very expensive cars, not to mention this block looks a bit familiar. Finally Matt comes up to a big rusty steel door and knocks, the viewer window slides open and we see a pair of eyes looking out. After confirming who it is the locks on the door clicks and the door is open. Once opened music blast out of the now apparent nightclub built into an old warehouse.] INT. WAREHOUSE NIGHTCLUB- MIDNIGHT [From the looks of this place we all remember being here around this time last year, the place looks about the same as it did then, it’s still a haven for LA’s street racers community. Matt continues to walk, slapping five with random people he passes. Matt finally comes up to Danny who is sitting in the dark area on an oddly colored couch, with him is another one of Matt’s friends he hasn’t seen in a while, Dante] Matt Keith: [Slaps five with Dante] What’s up dude, it’s been a long time. Dante: Sup man, I knew if I hung around long enough I would catch up with you here. Matt Keith: Where have you been man? One day you just vanished into thin air. Dante: Well bro I got a little home sick and decided to go back to New York. Matt Keith: [Laughingly] Riiiight, you home sick? [Laughs] Tell me another one. Dante: Okay, okay, I went back because of a grudge. Danny: Somebody down in New York was bad mouthing his racing skills. Matt Keith: [Laughs] Oh now you talk Dee. Danny: Why cut in where you two were already talking. [Dante laughs a slaps five with Danny] Dante: To make a long story short I took care of business and now I’m back. [Matt sits down on the couch between both men and leans back with his hands behind his head. Before Matt can say anything two hot chicks walks over to the trio.] Girl #1: [Sexy tone] Hi boys how would you feel about buying us a drink? Girl #2: [Sexy tone] Yeah we’re all out of cash. [Danny and Dante hop to their feet like lightning] Danny: Well ladies we have lots of money to spend. [The two walk off leaving Matt sitting there alone] Matt Keith: Damn you two are just gona leave me hangin like that? Dante: Sorry man but we’re not married like you [Laughs] [Matt laughs to himself as he watch his friends walk off with the two girls] [However the laughing stops as Matt remembers the WWA cameraman pointing the camera right at him, even the thoughts of what Zion said is churning in his diabolical head. Matt smirks and looks into the camera] Matt Keith: I would say don’t get your panties in a twist Jimbo but I’d be a day late and a dollar short. Here you are Zion being as predictable as ever with your pissing and your moaning, crying about our match at Rage. I mean I never expected you to take the loss and just disappear into the night, no not you. What you rather do is make an ass out of yourself and complain about if the match was one on one it would have been a different story. [Laughs] Yeah you’re probably right Jimbo; maybe you would have lost even faster huh Jimmi? The fact of the matter is Jimbo, beating you is almost as easy as scoring with your wife; the difference is your wife likes my Deadly Device. [Matt chuckles to himself, obviously pleased with his vulgar statement] Matt Keith: Your biggest problem Zion is that you never know when you’re beat. You can call the match at Rage a fluke if that’s what gets you to sleep at night, but Jimbo we all know it wasn’t a fluke, I beat you, I beat Joey, and I walked out with the title that made your career. [Smirks] Well if that’s what you call a career that is. [Matt sits up] Matt Keith: You’re a sad little man Jimbo, look at yourself. You’re trying everything to wash away the stench of failure even cowering behind the old concussion excuse. [Laughs again] You said that I would have never beaten you if you still weren’t suffering from the effects of a concussion. Tell me this Zion, how long do you plan on making excuses? There are guys that have won matches with far worse injuries than a punk ass concussion. Enigma wrestling and won matches even though he was damn near burned alive, I’ve wrestling with a cracked rib after falling from the stage with Chance. So why is it that you’re the only one making excuses? I’ll tell you why Jimmi, it’s because you’re what I like to call a one trick pony. You ride one accomplishment and when you suddenly find yourself in a two-month losing streak you start blaming injuries. What’s next Jimmi? Maybe your legs are bothering you, how about telling us you have a bad case of explosive diarrhea. For someone that clam to be so tough you sure sound like a b*tch. [Matt seems totally disgusted at Zion right now] Matt Keith: Oh but you weren’t done there Zion, you had the nerve to call me a flavor of the month. [Shakes his head] What is the definition of a “flavor of the month” Jimmi? Let me straiten you out right now junior, you are not looking at some “flavor of the month” you are looking a wrestling general that has been going strong in the WWA since last year, you are looking at a man that has made more of an impact that you have ever or ever will make, you are looking at the man that has beaten or wrestled against more big game players in the WWA than you have ever dreamed of. [He crosses his arm and focuses his green eyes into the camera] Matt Keith: But since we’re on the subject of flavors of the month I think all eyes should be on you. Here you are with your dog and pony act every week, you have a ring entrance that’s longer than all three Lord Of The Ring movies combined, and you have a chick that’s goes down faster than a fat man that dropped his last slice of pizza. And what’s it all for Jimbo? Has it won you any matches? The last time you won a match New Star and Star Station X was still in the WWA. I told you once Zion you think you’re a bad ass but you’re staring a true bad ass right in the eyes. You may have dreams of gold but all they are is dreams. [Keith leans back and listens to the music with a smile of his face] FADE TO BLACK
