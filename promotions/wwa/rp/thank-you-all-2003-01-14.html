Thank You All

14 Jan 2003

By Lockdown

Item 3 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 14 Jan 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Police photos of beaten and murdered men, women and children flash on the screen one by one. The subjects of the photos have been savagely brutalized. As each photo is displayed the words, “The Cell” are briefly superimposed before fading to reveal a clear shot of each picture.) Lockdown: Thank you all (More photos) Thank you Zion (More photos) Thank you Enigma (More photos) Thank you Shaman (More photos) Thank you Somers (More photos) Thank you Harley (More photos) Thank you all, you dumb bastards! (Photos continue to appear on the screen as Lockdown speaks) Thank you for making my job so much fun. Zion, do you tremble with fear when you think of our match next week? Do your knees go weak? Can you sleep at night? Do you realize just how easy you got off last week? You bent a steel chair over my head last Saturday and it didn’t stop me. It didn’t save you. Nothing can save you, Jimmy. Nothing. Not your wife, not a chair, not even Enigma. Say your goodbyes. Your fate is sealed. Enigma, nothing can stop you? You were gonna make sure Chance never walks again? You were gonna make sure chance didn’t make it to Meltdown? What Happened? I saw meltdown and you barely squeaked one out. All that work was for nothing, Enigma. You interfered in one of Cruz’s matches and it didn’t help a damn bit. Now you must pay the price. You like to try and end careers? If you make it to a recovery room in time to catch the rest of next week’s Fiesta, watch my match with Zion and see how it’s done. Stay in your yard til you’re full grown, boy! Shaman, you thought you could handle Cruz’s problem? Did you think I wouldn’t understand you? You think you can put your hands on Cruz’s broadcasters with impunity? What was Cruz’s problem now is yours. My Spanish is bad but not as bad as yours. Why didn’t you stop and think that I just might have learned a little Spanish in the joint with so many Latin Kings around? Cruz told me to let you go, but Last Saturday when you put Mikal through the announce table you gave me the excuse I was looking for. When I’m through with you, you’ll wish you never left the reservation, and no “great bear” B.S. can help you. Harley and Somers, you think you’re cute? You think you little matchbox car impresses us? I have a car too. Mine has a lot more trunk space. I’ll have to take you for a ride in it. I know a great spot out in the desert. Don’t you know when to admit you’re beaten? You must be getting tired what with all the stunts you pull. The time is drawing ever closer for you two to take a nice long nap. Leviathan and I are gonna make examples of you. We’re gonna show the rest of the WWA what happens when you cross The Cell. Get your final affairs in order boys. ( As Lockdown finishes speaking, one last picture appears and stays on the screen. The victim’s eyes have been removed, and the nose and ears have been cut off and stuffed in the victim’s mouth. Words fade onto the screen. They read: The Cell. Ungodly Brutality, The New Reality) Fade to black
