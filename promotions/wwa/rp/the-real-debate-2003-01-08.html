The real debate!!

8 Jan 2003

By Shaman

Item 1 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 8 Jan 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
** Shaman stands near a ring in his gym at his home staring down at the floor thinking and obviously contemplating something. He seems bothered by whatever he is thinking about and looks forward not looking at the camera.** [Shaman] " You know Adams i went through the stage of what i wanted to do to you and have come to a simple conclusion. You and i will meet somewhere down the road in the ring and get stuff off of our chests and set the record straight once and for all. Right now though my concentration is on Mack Tenn!!" ** Shaman turns and looks at the camera and leans forward a bit towards it. ** [Shaman] " On to Mack Tenn the lap dogs for Chrystian Payne the whining little coward who runs with shame from everyone. Who gets a win over me by a quick count and yet during the whole match gets uried by me. Show up at Fiesta and i'll have a world of hurt waiting for you. I have only begun to get angry and when im through you will be hurting far more than expected!!" ** Shaman turns and walks up the stairs and into the ring looking at the ring ropes.** [Shaman] " This is where all dreams come true in wrestling. I came into the WWA as a rookie Adams. I earned everything i have ever had. Faced the greats of the WWA and had several great feuds with new up and coming stars. I worked hard and yet still was here as they departed or retired!!! What makes me so special?? Its here in the heart folks. yeah thats right i have the heart and the drive to continue whether you like it or not. Lately its been the fact in the biggest pain in everyones backside since Wild Thing Chris Rogers, But he was a pain in a different way. Anyways none of that matters as i will continue walking into the ring and defeating my opponents. Starting this saturday with Mack tenn. My partner Nick Adams i sure hope is a man of his word, because i would surely hate to have to get even right then and there. I have a goal and thats to beat the living hell out of that coward Chrystian payne and his whipping boys Mack Tenn. What am i thinking most would ask. Simple actually. To be the most dangerous individual i know i am!!" ** Shaman walks across the ring and grabs the ropes and shakes them a bit then leaning against them he smiles.** [Shaman] " Such a perfect place to cause pain and miscontent and demolish my opponents. Mack tenn you will be the first of a long line of victims at Shamans hands. Well maybe more statistics because there won't be much left of you to be victims will you!" ** Shaman just simply turns and hops out of the ring and walks away as the camera fades to black with these words appearing to be seen last** GOT BLOOD!!
