Peace at Last

8 Jun 2003

By "N.A.I." Nicholas Adams

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(A completely black screen. It's ominous, except for the fact that there's no music, just a voice-over that is just a normal voice.) V/O: Well, I definitely screwed up. I could have won...I could have been victorious. Instead, I fell. I crashed and burned. (The voice continues, and light begins to eat at the edges of the darkness...and stops when the voice resumes.) V/O: I tried to do my best. The fact is, my best wasn't enough. I was extreme. I was insane. I picked the damn match. And I still lost. (The light suddenly just burst onto the screen, revealing Nicholas Adams...in a business suit...standing in front of white dry-erase board.) Adams: Let me see how I failed, step-by-step. (Adams grabs a dry-erase marker.) Adams: Step One - I started slow. Too slow. Meanwhile, Grunge went at me full force. Maybe I should use some basic chemistry. (Adams removes the marker lid and begins to draw two graphs of equal scale. Both have three slants and two flat areas, but one has its flat areas lower on the graph than the other. Adams then writes his name over the graph whose slants are longer, and Grunge's name over the graph whose slants are shorter. In comparison to the numbers, Adams' higher flat line is at 100, while Grunge's upper flat line is around 80.) Adams: Okay...these are heating curves. This is a relation of temperature to time. I used the basic heating curve of water for myself, and the basic heating curve of alcohol for Grunge. Temperature is the vertical axis, time is the horizontal axis. (Adams waits for a couple of seconds.) Adams: Okay, now let me get the inverses of both of these graphs. (Adams draws the lines, but they seem to be on a mirrored slant, compared to the original. In fact, now that these new lines are drawn, we can see a cast difference. Adams' line is lower than Grunge's...if only by a bit.) Adams: Basically, I'm saying that in the match at Revolution, Grunge got to his top potential just a bit earlier than I did, allowing him to take more out of me sooner. Sure, I was insane. But I decided that if he wanted to win so much, he could have it. So I tapped. (Nick shakes his head.) Adams: Well, he did save me, though not in the way that he thought. He destroyed my misconception that I needed a title to be great. Now I realize that the fans will cheer me if I just do my best, win or lose. (Adams goes to the dry-erase board and wipes off the charts.) Adams: Now for some random algebra, or, to call it by its original, al-jabr. (Adams writes up an equation, then reveals it, showing it to be S=D x P x E x H.) Adams: To convert to English, strength equals drive times perseverence times endurance times heart. I have a lot of each, which makes me very strong. Nobody can go as far as I will go. And you want to know why? (Adams takes off the jacket and button-up shirt, revealing a white tee-shirt with the words "I am the Rainmaker". He then rips off the dress pants, revealing some denim shorts.) Adams: You can't stop the rain. And I am the Rainmaker. (Fade out.)
