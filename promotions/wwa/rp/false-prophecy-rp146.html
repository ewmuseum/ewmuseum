False Prophecy

14 Jun 2003

By "N.A.I." Nicholas Adams

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene fades in to a scene on the street. Nicholas Adams is walking down it. It seems to be a ghetto. Spray paint graffiti runs along some of the walls. Nick is wearing some black clothing, obviously gang-neutral. He looks around, and suddenly, "Ain't No Sunshine" by DMX plays in the background, but it's obviously added in from the WWA production trailer. Nick is just walking around with a purpose. He suddenly puts a hood above his head and keeps going.) It's dark and hell is hot (Nick goes and leans against a wall, obviously reflecting on the scene around him.) Ain't no sunshine when it's on Only darkness every day Ain't no sunshine when it's on Cuz when its on, ya suckaz gonna be gone Every time cuz we don't play (Adams stands and begins to walk, seemingly to the beat of the song.) "Who turned out the lights?" Is what suckaz be sayin' (Adams walks past a flower shop with cracked windows and bullet imprints in the walls.) Now you don't wanna fight But ya suckaz be playin' Thinkin' its alright keep playin with that road And you gonna know the night When you layin in that cold (Nick passes past some houses with kids hiding behind the windows, which have been partially blocked with boards. He then walks past a small church, and stops and stares at it.) Dirt gettin' tossed in your grave Now it's all over Preacher said "You was brave" But now it's all over You just one of the many, plenty I done gave it to (A shot fires off in the song, and Nick continues past the church. He walks up to an intersection with some commotion.) Ain't no savin 'you No matter how many tears your moms' cried Ain't gon bring your life back Plus in hell, you gon fry (Nick passes by some medics putting a body onto a stretcher while a man standing next to a car shows his grief over hitting that person. Police are interviewing him, but he's saying nothing due to his shock.) Why? But now the only 2 releative questions Is "Do we bury him or burn? Any suggestions?" Either way, you up out of here for good Now when suckaz mention you name They knockin' on wood (Nick shakes his head and walks away from the scene, continuing down the road.) Did I, get my, point across? Another body on the shotte Another joint I toss Ain't no sunshine when it's on Only darkness every day Ain't no sunshine when it's on Cuz when its on, ya suckaz gonna be gone Every time cuz we don't play Every time cuz we don't play Every time cuz we don't play (The music fades, and Adams leans against a chain-link fence and looks into the camera.) Adams: It's sort of strange to think about how priveledged life I have led. I've been to a ghetto before to meet the family of this kid that was a fan of mine before he was caught in the middle of a gang shoot-out. But now, looking around here, I have to realize that my petty fights with people like Enigma are nothing. (Adams reaches up and pulls off his hood, then sags back into the chain-link fence.) Adams: Just to tell you, I'm not in Tennessee anymore. I'd rather not say where I am, but it's enough to assume that I'm in a slum. Anyway, I walked past a church a while back, and it got me thinking about Enigma's most recent message to the fans and employees of the WWA. He said I was young, and that I was choke artist. I am neither. I'm older than you, punk. That's right, Enigma, I'm talking to you. And I am no choke artist. You want a choke artist? Look in the mirror. Because you will be stopped. (Adams reaches into one of his hoodie's pockets and pulls out a picture. He looks at it, then puts it back into his sweater.) Adams: You see, he used the image of three crosses. But there's one slight problem. None of us are Jesus. And Grunge, Dave, and myself are criminals. And finally, you're not even Barabbas. You want a Bible analogy, though, so I'll give you one. You, Enigma, are Judah-bar-Simon, also known as Judas Iscariot. You have betrayed many, and now you will learn to regret that fact. You have made your own execution, Enigma. By your words, you have committed suicide. (Nick stands.) Adams: But unlike some of the attacks made in the ghetto, the attack that I make upon you will have reason behind it. The attack that I make upon you will be purposeful and completely calculated. It will not be a random strike just to see whether you have money or not. It will be... (Suddenly, a man in a mask with a knife comes up behind Nick and puts it up to his throat. Nick looks down.) Mugger: Well, well, well...you sure ain't from this neighborhood. C'mon, rich boy, give me your cash or I slit your throat. And you, camera boy, don't try anything funny... Cameraman: Fine, fine. We're just shooting a news video. It's cool. Mugger: Good. Now give me your money. Adams: Fine. Just let me get out my wallet. (Obviously, the promo has taken a turn for the worst. Adams probably anticipated this possibility, but he's being as stoic as possible. In fact, he pulls out his wallet, and then tosses it on the ground about three feet away.) Adams: There. Now just go away. Mugger: Good...I knew a loser white boy like you would just give in. Now don't try anything funny. Adams: I won't. Just take the money and leave. (The mugger takes the knife away from Nick's throat and walks over to the wallet. Nick immediately goes up behind the man and kicks him directly in the back. The man falls in pain, and the cameraman apparently tosses one of his spare cables at Nick. Nick catches it, and before the mugger can raise the knife, Adams kicks it away. He then kicks the would-be thief and ties his hands behind his back.) Adams: Don't ever mug somebody again, you hear me? If you do, you'll eventually get yourself killed. [To cameraman] Get those cops back at the car wreck and tell them what happened. [Mumbles] This could be one hell of a video for "Cops" if it were in LA... (The camera begins to go away from Adams, and as if it were an afterthought, the camera fades out.)
