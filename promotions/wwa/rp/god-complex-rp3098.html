God Complex

4 May 2006

By The Almighty Damian Thorne

Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Narcissism ? A God complex? Why not? God is very important, all important. Others exist for his sake, not their own. That is, they are to fulfill his desires, not their own. Their conduct is to glorify him, not themselves. They are here to serve him, not their own interests. He doesn't pay them for their services: they owe him everything, including any sacrifice. Notice that, in any other context, his rights with respect to them amount to property rights. They are rights of ownership. Just like I own you Of course, the owner of property is also the judge of its worth. He has a right to take a sledge hammer to it if he wants. By virtue of his ownership of others, a god is therefore the judge of their worth. He too has a right to destroy his property if he wants. In fact the ancient words for "god" and "idol" were synonymous with "king" and "master" and meant "judge." He is to ultimately judge us as worthy of keeping, or not. If not, he condemns us like a worthless building and smashes us into oblivion. But he himself is never to be judged. To fault him in any way is unthinkable. He is righteous by virtue of who he is, not what he does. Do you understand Ember? Do you see who I am now? I don’t hide behind a mask. Everything I am, I show you. No half truths No lies No masks In other words, a god has the right to do anything. Whatever he does is right because he did it. This is, of course, a double standard. So, a god needn't abide by the standards he sets for others. Nobody holds a god answerable for anything. What would be wrong for others to do is okay for a god to do. And so, it's no wonder being The Almighty, the world champion in the WWA is very important; all important. YOU, Ember, exist for MY sake, not your own. You are to fulfill my desires, not your own. Your conduct is to glorify ME, not yourself. You are here to serve my needs, not your own. I don't pay you for your services: you owe me for everything I give you. Everything. Every punch Every kick You are my property now Ember, and by my godlike virtue, I am judge of the worth of my property. Worthless Broken Empty Cold and forgotten, you will lay there, alone. Thinking about how unwanted you feel, how useless. It will seem peace never existed for you when I tear you apart. When I rip that thin veil of secrets from your face, all you were, all you had become. Your unusual past, and your dark future, all gone, leaving nothing but a blackened and broken present Can you feel the hate in me, the fear that surrounds me like a shroud, like a pestillence on my shoulder. All you will remember, from that point on Ember My eyes turned to frozen blue ice, burning into your soul. Your screams cannot be heard, will not be heard, because nobody gives a f**k about you Ember. Once your mask goes, who are you? Do you remember? No, you don’t, but what you will remember, for as long as you draw breath Crumpling in pain Sleeping in pain Living in pain Your hope all but gone This world will have beaten you to the ground So run Ember, run as fast as your little lycra clad legs will carry you. Paint me some more literary pictures if you think it will help. Run RUN Faster Run all you like, but realise the one thing you can’t outrun Is your fear I can’t help you now, you’ve gone too far On your knees Ember, and pray you survive Designed By [name withheld] | © World Wrestling Alliance 1997 - 2005
