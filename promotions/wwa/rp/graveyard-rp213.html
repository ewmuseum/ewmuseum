Graveyard

30 Jun 2003

By Grunge

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
FADE IN (A cemetery, the resting ground that we will all be layed to rest some day. Well unless cremated. Over the peaceful scene “Pretty Baby” by Vanessa Carlton begins to play. Kind of a strange choice considering were we are.) You light me up and then I fall for you You lay me down and then I call for you Stumbling on reasons that are far and few I'd let it all come down and then some for you Pretty baby, don't you leave me I have been saving smiles for you Pretty baby, why can't you see You're the one that I belong to? I'll be the embrace that keeps you warm For you're the sun that breaks the storm I'll be alright and I'll sleep sound As long as you keep coming 'round Oh pretty baby (The cameraman leads until we reach Grunge sitting on a bench in rather Forest Gump style of way near the cemetery path.) Grunge: We all have are problems. (We focus in the see that Grunge is even eating out of a box of chocolates. He offers one to the cameraman and we see a hand reach on screen and take one.) Grunge: So I’m here in a graveyard. I do not say this to get appreciation from "goths". There is a lovely graveyard by the coast that is tranquil and secluded. All of the gravestones are from 1912 or earlier. You can read the stones and get a feel for the time, and place the person lived in. It strikes me that you can be spotted speaking openly to a tombstone and nobody thinks of you as a "crazy person". No, they'd think it sweet, and leave you alone. Besides, I'm tired of telling everything to my pillow, trapped in doors. Some nice, fresh air, and sunshine would do me good. (Grunge shrugs.) Grunge: Maybe this is a bit morbid. Of course those like Se7en would find this funny. But like I said this Sunday is the Independence Day Rumble and we all want to win, for whatever reason there exists. (Grunge gets off the bench but before doing so places his things down on the bench to the left of him.) Grunge: My poor cat has heard all of my confessions already. I do appreciate her gazing eyes, and carefully placed yawns…. (Grunge begins to laugh.) Grunge: Heh, my cat. I’m sure Leviathan doesn’t really care for cats after Monday but I digress. (Grunge begins to walk South in the graveyard.) Grunge: Some of you have taken a whole different route; you've hired someone to listen to your inner conflicts, and therefore you're rewarded to being bored silly while trying to force words to a stranger you only slightly hope will like you. Hey, at least its air conditioned. (He reaches the crypt were some people choose to stay.) Grunge: And now thirty men get to play out their problems in the squared circle. And now we shall all fight to win the prize. And of course I will, I want to win. (Grunge stares at the building, almost out of awe and he begins to smile and points his head up skyward.) Grunge: Rock and roll. (And at that we begins to…) FADE OUT
