The WWA Contract Signing (Part 1 of 2)

21 Jul 2003

By The Chuckster

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Start Part 1: Chuckster’s WWA Contract Signing] (The scene opens up with a large wooden podium in the middle of the screen. The letters WWA can be seen on it. Suddenly the EWA logo appears on the bottom right corner of the screen, while the WWA logo appears on the bottom left corner. You see lights flashing all about, and a small fat man standing at the podium speaking. However you can not hear a word that is coming out. The camera moves to the right, and you can barely see the former WWA European Champion himself. The Chuckster is sitting in a chair, as the man’s words become audible.) Man: The WWA talent committee took session again this last week. (The camera moves in and starts to focus on the Chuckster. The camera shot reveals a nice pair of black dress shoes, originating from what appears to be alligator. Man: And from the committee a decision was made. (He is wearing white khaki slacks perfectly creased down the middle. At the top of the pants a leather belt plated several times over with metal links, and spikes embellishes his pants.) Man: The decision was… that three EWA wrestlers… have been issued a WWA contract. (You can also see that he is wearing a red and black unbuttoned silk flowery Hawaiian shirt. His abs and chest aren’t overly developed nor under developed either.) Man: One of them being the man sitting right here… next to me… (points over at the Chuckster) (As usual the black Oakley sunglasses hide the Chuckster’s eyes. His dark hair is combed back, slightly raised and dripping wet.) Man: And here he is… the former WWA European Champion himself… Theeeee… Chuccckkkssssstttttteeeeeeerrrrrrrr! (The room busts with applause and bright snap shots as the Chuckster makes his way to the podium. He shakes the man’s head and nods, as the man says something that can not be picked up by the cameras. The Chuckster then positions himself in front of the microphone, and begins to speak as lights all around flash.) Chuckster: They said that... the people want their champion! They said that... the people want their idol! They said that... the people want their god! And then… HE CAME… To the WWA that is. (The press people laugh at the Chuckster’s slight twist of his now famous line.) Chuckster: That’s right folks… it is now official… The EWA Franchise is now a major player in the WWA! (The lights continue to flash as he finishes speaking.) Chuckster: WWA… I want you to welcome the best… that the EWA has to offer! Because boys… lower your guard… AND WE WILL… pass you by! (He looks around after saying this, and begins to speak again.) Chuckster: Now… that I have said, what I needed to say… are there any questions? (The floor becomes alive with noise, rustling and shouting.) [End Part 1: Chuckster’s WWA Contract Signing]
