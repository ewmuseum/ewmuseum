Getting To Know You

21 Feb 2003

By Dave Harley

Item 2 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 21 Feb 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[We see a close up on the face of Mac Maloy. His expression is intense and still. The camera zooms out revealing Maloy’s face to be a cutout picture that has been taped onto a balloon. The balloon has been stuck on top of a make-shift body, which is sat at a table. As the camera zooms out further, we can see that a Scrabble board is set up on the table with a rack of tiles in front of the Mac Maloy effigy. A door behind ‘Mac’ opens and The Extreme Machine Dave Harley steps out with a can of Budweiser in his hand. He places the can in front of ‘Mac’ and sits opposite him.] Harley: Here you go Mac old buddy. Thanks for joining me for a game of Scrabble. Sorry Mal couldn’t be here to join in but he’s preparing for his big Main Event on Fiesta and the Global Warfare match. But I guess this gives us time to get to know each other before our match on Saturday Night. You want me to go first? OK…errrr, let me see…[Harley examines his tiles.] Oh, I’ve got one…E-W-A. Remember the EWA? Y’know, when you were a superstar? …What d’ya mean I can’t have abbreviations?!! OK, OK…L-O-S-E-R. A word you’ve become very familiar with since you joined the WWA. Your turn. [The Mac Maloy effigy just sits there and glares at Dave.] Harley: You don’t want to play? …C’mon Truckster, I invite you round to my house for a friendly game of Scrabble and you won’t even play? Let’s hope you’re in a better mood for games this weekend when I CRAXXT your ass from one side of the ring to the other. But you’re not in the mood to wrestle anymore are you Maccy-Boy? I’ve watched you on Insider, sat in that stinkin’ little bar drinking all night. The WWA isn’t for alcoholics Mac…well maybe there’s room for a little MALcohol, but only a drop. What I’m trying to tell you Mac, is that since you arrived here in the WWA you’ve dropped the ball. You had your shot and you lost it. So what d’you have to say to that, huh? [The Maloy effigy’s head droops and slides down slightly in the chair.] Harley: Where you drinking before you came round here? You were weren’t you! You thought you could come round here and get beer money from me?!! You thought wrong you drunken asshole! [Harley picks up the can of Budweiser and throws it at Maloy’s head. As it strikes, the balloon head bursts and the rest of the body (made up of various household items) falls to the floor.] Harley: Hey, that was easy! [The phone rings. Harley clambers over the remains of Mac Maloy to answer it.] Harley: Hello? Hey Mal, my hardcore amigo, what’s up? …No, I’ve just been playing Scrabble…with Mac Maloy…No I won by default, his head exploded…OK, see you later. [Harley hangs up and reaches into his pocket, pulling out his wallet.] Harley: Listen Mac, I gotta go pick Mal up in the Extreme Mobile. Here’s a few dollars for bus fare. Don’t spend it all on drink, I wouldn’t want you hungover for our match on Saturday. [Harley throws five dollars down onto the homemade Mac Maloy before grabbing his jacket and walking out.]
