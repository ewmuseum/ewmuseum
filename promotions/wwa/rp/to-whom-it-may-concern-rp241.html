To whom it may concern!!

5 Jul 2003

By Shaman

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
** Shaman stops and grins as he slowly walks over to a chalk board and begins writing names down upon it and he smiles as he finishes off the writing. He then looks at the camera and grins as he begins to speak. seemingly and very sadistically his tone takes a nasty turn.** Shaman: " Eddie Van Dorn. You seem to understand me very little. I take great insult as you put me in the same category as Devils Own aka the Cell. My size does make a big difference in the Indy rummble. Yet i do agree on one thing. It is survival of the fittest. You may lose. You may win, But in the end you will come to the same conclusion you are merely second best! Now now before you go get your panties in a bunch let me explain. There is a level of competitors here. Everyone is going to bring the best they have. Right now Kash is the best and we are all second best until we defeat him!!" ** Shaman casually slips his hand to his chin and wipes it and rubs his forehead and then drops his arm back down to his side.** Shaman: " X Corp/Youth United i have no problem with you gentlemen. My goal is the end and if my goal crosses paths with Devils Own i will make them suffer greatly. I am far from finished and my anger demands to be satisfied! have you really asked yourself what Shamans capable of or are you being morons and thinking i will be a cake walk?? Answer that question before you continue!!" ** Shaman turns and looks at the list again and smiles.** Shaman: " David Wattsoner this should be a very interesting piece my friend. Two will fight again after a match where we beat the living hell out of one another. Yet i will truly forgive men that will cross us. I think we will hurt them in more ways than one. I should hope that Devils Own would get squarely involved, Because they have a world of pain coming for them!!" ** Shaman turns and crosses off Stephen Murphy, Tommy Starr and Se7en.** Shaman: " You three will be at a learning curve. I am not threatened by either of you three. Tommy Starr and Stephen Murphy have recieved beatings at my hands several times. Tommy Starr had just recently been beat up by Fuel after trying to beat down another man. Oh the shame Tommy Starr oh the shame to be beat down by a guy who seems to be not gaining much momentum. Stephen Murphy the beatings i have given you seem to adle your mind. No matter Mr. Murphy. You will learn again and again. Se7en you and i have tagged in the past and believe it or not i have no qualms with you. Just be warned when it comes down to you and me at anytime there will be no quarter given and i expect none!" ** Shaman turns and watches the board and smiles as if it seems to take on a life of its own to him.** Shaman: " Six years gentlemen i have seen it come and go. Six years i have seen the best come and go from it. Six long years and you may call me old and out of his time. Guess what it is i that has always been and here. When you are gone i shall still be here!! Never ever underestimate the fact that you should confuse longevity with age!!" ** Shaman pushes the black board away from him and turns as he crashes against the wall in the darkness making alot of noise and he smiles and speaks.** Shaman: " gentlemen the game has begun. No more time for crying and whining. Its time all is said and done. Its is time for you to begin the suffering that is to come. The next four months shall be the uprising and the past shall repeat itself. The wheels have come full circle and guess what. Shaman is coming for anyone and everyone who has dared to cross his path. Welcome to hell boys. Its only starting to look gloomy for all of you isn't it. Don't take me seriously when i say this. Then after you are angry that Shaman has hurt you all or defeated you in a match. Don't be angry at the man who has done it. Be angry with the person who hadn't believed it!!" ** Shaman turns and walks away as the camera turns and focuses on the wall.** GOT BLOOD!! BRING THE PAIN!!
