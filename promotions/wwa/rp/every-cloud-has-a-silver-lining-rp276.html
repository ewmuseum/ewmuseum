WWA (1997–2019) Roleplays

Every cloud has a silver lining.

23 Jul 2003

By Mal Somers

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(We open at Mal Somers' apartment in Long Island New York. We know this as it is in its usual messy state. Light shines in through the thin curtaining, and in the foreground, three figures can be seen sitting around Mal’s dining table, drinking beer and jotting down notes. Central to the table is a small plastic box. As the camera adjusts to the light, we can see that the three men are of course Dave Harley, Chance and Mal Somers from X-Corp, unwinding after a long week on the road. Chance in particular after his hard fought no – contest versus the WWA Champion Kash at EWA Truth or Consequences, followed by a narrow loss with Nick Adams versus Kash and Tracer Bullet at WWA Meltdown. He is bruised and aching, but enjoying the quiet time with his closest comrades. Dave Harley after his TV Title loss but subsequent GenX Title gain versus his old foe Lockdown also at Meltdown. He seems to be relishing the time with him buddies also. As we saw at Meltdown, Dave has tapped back into his “Extreme Machine” roots via the GenX Title win, grinning maniacally at his two stable members. And Mal Somers, after being pummelled from pillar to post, with some sporadic comebacks versus his current nemesis Wes Sanders and his Forsaken Souls team mate Enigma at Meltdown, looking quite the worse for wear, with some bandaging to his bruised face courtesy of a Fall from Grace by Enigma and a Third Time Strike from Sanders. He seems comforted by the fact that he picked up the unlikely win thanks to some timely assistance by his beloved Rookie.) Harley: “POOMBAH!” Chance: “HOOP!” Somers: “BUM!” Harley: “HAY!” Chance: “POO!” Somers: “BOY!” Harley: Hmmm and I’m all out! How about you guys? Chance and Somers: Yep. (The camera zooms in to see they have been playing Boggle. Chance gets up to get some more frosty brewskies from the bar fridge, ever so slightly limping. There is a knock at the door, so Chance gets it.) Somers: (yawning) Man, I better get some shut eye, this Boggle Marathon is mind boggling! Harley: How lame are you? Just because Chance and I have been whipping your... (Harley stops as Chance walks in carrying a large box, marked “Attn: Mal Somers”. He dumps it on the table in front of Mal.) Somers: Whats this? Chance: Beats me, it was just left at the door. Harley: Oh, maybe its that bronzed Hungry Hungry Hippos set I ordered with our pay per view bonus payments! (Chance and Somers turn and look at Harley.) Chance and Somers: Cooooooool! (Somers hastily opens the box and inside is a large hessian bag, marked “Fan Mail”. Somers beams from ear to ear.) Harley: Dude, I have NEVER seen that much mail! Your stock is really rising. Chance: Need some help sorting though it? Somers: Sure. (The trio tip the bag over and scores and scores of letters flood out, covering the table and falling to the floor. Harley picks up the first one. He looks at it and smirks, then picks up another, then another. Chance does the same.) Harley and Chance: Errrr, Mal? Somers: Yeah guys, you haven’t got paper cuts yet have ya? Harley: Not quite… Somers: Whats wrong then? Chance: These are all addressed to Rookie! Somers: Wha~? (Harley and Chance start cracking up at Mal as he rummages through all the mail, trying to find one with his name on it.) Somers: Oh this is just great! First he gets the pin on Wes at Meltdown, and everyone is ranting about him on the fan pages, and now this! Harley: Rookie is more popular than you! Hahahaahhahahah! Chance: That’s just GOLD! Somers: Shut up! (puts his hands over his ears) I’m not putting up with this! I need some fresh air! (Somers gets up and goes out the front door, slamming it shut as Dave and Chance keep laughing away.) Chance: Hmmm..Hope he comes back before Fiesta on Monday, we’ve got a big tag match against Sanders and Enigma. Harley: Nah, bud, don’t stress, if he doesn’t come back, you could just substitute him with Rookie! (Chance and Harley clink their beers together as we fade to black.)
