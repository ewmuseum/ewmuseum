It's All About The Ratings

23 Jun 2003

By Dave Harley

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Dave Harley stands in an empty WWA boardroom. On the wall are huge graphs which show the Nielsen ratings for Saturday's edition of Fiesta. Harley stands studying them. He scratches his head and turns to the camera. He is wearing an 'Extreme Corporation' T Shirt and a pair of leather trousers. his hair has been tied back and his usual sunglasses are missing from his youthful face.] Boy, that was some segment on Fiesta Joey. You see this? [Harley points to the chart.] This chart represents the viewing figures for Fiesta. Now I'm no scientitian but it looks to me that ratings went through the roof as X Corp's music hit. But then something really weird happened. The ratings plummeted. And I mean plummeted. Now at first I thought it might have to do with Mal's inherent ugliness (don't tell him I said that!) but I decided to review the tape just to be sure. Take a look at this... [Harley moves across the room to a TV. He pushes play and Fiesta flashes onto the screen. the grainy picture shows Dave Harley and Mal Somers stood in the centre of the ring. Suddenly blue lights shine out from the stage and a voice announces 'Make Way...For Joey...K!' As Joey steps out onto the stage, Harley freezes the frame and turns his attention back to the camera.] You see that Joey? That's you! The presenter of 'K Moments'. And guess what? You're the one responsible for the ratings drop on Fiesta. looks good for you doesn't it Joey? This Monday you debut a brand new talk show. A talk show which will have people flicking on over to the competition. And that's bad. Y'know why? Because those people might not tune in to see 'Not So Late Night With Dave and Mal'. Those people may not see our exclusive interview with Nick Adams. So you've got to make a decision Joey. You can either cancel 'K Moments' or you can go head to head with 'Not So Late Night...' in the ratings. But let's be honest here, you don't stand a chance really, do you? Not that it bothers me. It just means that the Extreme Machine moves a step closer to taking that TV Title from around your waist. So go ahead Joey. Have as much fun with 'K Moments' as you want. It will only prove one thing though, and that is that 'Not So Late Night...' is Too Hot For Monday Night! [The camera turns away from Dave Harley and focuses in on the TV screen. It zooms in on Joey K's TV Title as the scene fades out.]
