WWA (1997–2019) Roleplays

Chillin

28 Feb 2003

By The Cell

Item 1 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 28 Feb 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(An indiscriminate place known as “O’Callahan’s” is viewed by WWA fans. It shows a seedy bar with four barfly’s are perched at the bar. Their faces show signs of not fully destroyed by alcohol.) Barfly 1: Did they come in last night? Bartender: No big show last night, Global Warfare they called it. Only the angry one did, and it was late. Barfly 2: Fitting for these days. BF3: GW should nuke Saddam. BF2: That’s your answer for everything. If Bush nuked everyone you wanted him to, the planet would be one giant golf ball. Potholes everywhere. Last night you wanted him to nuke Canada. BF3: Well Canadians piss me off. BF4: Everyone does. BF1: Peaceful night then? Bartender: Yeah had some college kids come in but like I said the angry one came in late and scared them off. BF3: He thinks we should nuke Saddam. BF4: Yeah and he thinks stabbing a man for spilling his drink should be legal. BF3: That’s why I like him, real sensible. BF2: They’re both menacing and scare some people away. BF1: But there is not as much fighting any more. Bartender: Good and Bad BF4: At least one of them pays. BF2: Big wuss, he’s bigger and scarier but real polite. Now that’s odd. BF1: Those green eyes make me pi$$ myself when he signals you out. (As the Barfly’s continue their conversation, the door opens and a familiar rattle of a bell sounds. They all turn around to see who has entered, then stop looking as soon as they realize who has entered. The men they have been talking about, Leviathan and Lockdown, The Cell.) Bartender: Sh#beep#! Lockdown: That’s twice in two matches. Leviathan: Don’t worry, Shaman is of no concern to us. Raine will realize why when he finally looks at our contracts. The less we talk about him the more sense we will make. Lockdown: He reminds of the #beep#ing Chief in “One flew over the Cuckoo’s Nest”, only that guy when he talked. Leviathan: But he didn’t. Lockdown: That’s my point, a blind mute is more coherent than him. (The two laugh as they walk to their table, the bartender can be seen wiping it down and helping a young couple to another table.) Lockdown: What about Zion? Leviathan: No need to worry. He sneak attacks only have short shelf life and when he finally meets eye to eye. He will just be a bug on the windscreen. Lockdown: And he has a tasty wife, who is a liability. I hope Penny isn’t a liability to us. Leviathan: Leviathan has no liabilities. Lockdown: Good, because I don’t want to be left #beep#ing stranded in the ring while you attend to manly duties. Leviathan: That will not happen. Lockdown: Now what about those maggots X-corp. We’ve beaten and battered them but they still disrespect us. Now they #beep#ing calling for a title shot! It’s time we #beep# them over permanently. Leviathan: If David tries once more to be a hero, I will personally break his back. Lockdown: That’s the #beep#ing way. Now I’ll get us a drink, my shout. Hey Bartender the usual. (The bartender grabs two bottles of Jack Daniels but only one glass and takes them to Leviathan.) Bartender: Here you go, and who’s paying? Lockdown: No one so #beep# off. (Bartender leaves the Cell at their table. Leviathan grabs a bottle and pours himself a drink as Lockdown just opens the bottle and drinks straight from it.) Lockdown: How can you drink out of that, you never know which #beep# has been drinking out of it before you. Leviathan: I trust the man to clean it properly. Lockdown: One of these days, your trust is going to #beep# you over.
