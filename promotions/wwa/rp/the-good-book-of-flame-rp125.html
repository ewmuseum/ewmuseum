The good book of Flame!!

11 Jun 2003

By Fuel

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
** The camera pans around a burning house as Fuel walks around. Suddenly Fuel is seen standing and holding a burning book. Obviously a religious book of sorts and then he turns and looks at the camera with some sort of devious expression upon his face as he turns and begins singing a nursery rhyme of sorts.** Fuel: " One two buckle your shoes!! " ** Fuel reaches down and pulls a tight strap across his boots.** Fuel: "Three four shut the door!!" ** Fuel shuts the door after squirting some lighter fluid into a room with no fire. ** Fuel: " Five six pick up some sticks!!" ** Fuel grabs a bunch of popsicles sticks and tosses them into the room and turns again.** Fuel: " Seven eight don't be late!!" ** Fuel tosses a lit bunch of matches into the room and grins as the room bursts into flames. ** Fuel: " Nine ten im gonna start a fire again!!" ** Fuel turns and opens a door and walks out and smiles as a sudden back rush of air causes the lightly burning room to huge flames as Fuel does a little dance and turns walking away from the room out into the backyard and hops a fence and then stops as the camera catches up and he lights a match and speaks.** Fuel: " Prometheus a self proclaimed god. Now i must say that in greek mythology there were alot of things that fire signified. The beginning of the end. The coming of storms or wars. Mars was the god of war if i remember right. Yet you see in Norse mythology Surtur was the lord and master of the fire giants and when he and Odin the All Father would fight for the last time it would signal the end aka Ragnarok. All kinds of Gods would die etc etc. Yet for the moment fire will simply signify your end!!" ** Fuel holds up a bible and smiles as it is one of the ones Reverend Tweak carries to the ring and simply lights it on fire as he speaks.** Fuel: " Tweak your surmon has ended. Your goal to get converts has been defeated. You have played on the wrong side of the fence this time. Must i explain to you the many ways that you can be burned by fire. Fire it can be so trivial at times. It won't necessarily be harmful to burn you. You can be burned by your best friend. You can be burning mad. Yet i do not need a match to get to you Tweak. I already have studied you and i know what makes you how can i say tweak. No Tweak you have playedwith the one thing you know nothing about and i know everything about. Maybe i'll shgow you many ways to be burned. In fact i think you and i will have to be prepared. I don't think your good book here reacts to fire very well. Ice Cream will not help you either it will simply melt. You may consider yourself a man of thwe cloth, But im am your living hell. With the fire and brimstone and explosions and the pyrotechnic and the maniacs. I am at home Tweak. Oh and only one thing reamins. See you soon Tweak you may never know where i will be showing up!!" ** Fuel tosses the burning book on top of a pile of dried leaves and walks away as the leaves begin to flame up and burn brightly as fire trucks and police cars can be heard in the distance.**
