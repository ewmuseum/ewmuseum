WWA (1997–2019) Roleplays

Self Debate

7 Jan 2003

By Nicholas Adams

Item 1 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 7 Jan 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
FADE IN (Nicholas Adams is shown sitting inside a hotel in Pittsburg, Pennsylvania. He's just looking around, and reading a book titled "World Wrestling Alliance: A Story Five Years in the Making". He's engrossed in it, and when he comes to a certain page, he freezes and shakes his head.) Adams: Dedication...well, toughing it out for five years really is dedication. (He continues scanning the page, the shakes his head.) Adams: Former TV champ...former Extreme Champ...former US champ...even a former World Champion. This is the kind of person they relegate back to Fiesta? What the hell is the WWA thinking? Sure, the guy has been out of the limelight for a while, but he's stayed together throughout it all. I can't believe he's taking this. But you know something? (Adams closes the book and stands up.) Adams: I'm damn glad he's my partner on Fiesta. (Adams walks towards the elevators, and the camera begins to follow.) Adams: Sure, he clotheslined me to the mat, and I did make him lose the match last Saturday. But I think that he would rather have a win on his record by cooperating with me than having a loss to Mack Tenn. (Adams arrives at the elevator and presses the button.) Adams: Well, at least I don't have to travel with him. I'd hate to see what he's like after a ref counts fast and costs him the match. (The elevator opens with a ding, and Adams steps in. The camera yet again follows, and nobody else joins them.) Adams: Of course, the real problem is trying to get him to listen. He wouldn't listen when I smacked Simon Mackinaw out of the ring. He got angry when I said that Christyan kicked out, even though it was completely legal. I was surprised that he didn't try to destroy me. But all's fair when you step in the ring...especially when it's no DQ. (The elevator stops with a ding, the doors open, and Nick walks out. The camera moves closely behind, with Nick still talking to either the camera guy or himself...it's not really apparent.) Adams: Well, at least it's going to be even further revenge upon Mackinaw and Tennyson. Just like in that falls count anywhere match at Season's Beatings, they won't know what hit them. (At this point, Nick has stopped at a room door, opened it, and just as he finished speaking, the door shuts.) FADE OUT
