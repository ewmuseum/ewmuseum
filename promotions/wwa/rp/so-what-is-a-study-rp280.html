So what is a Study?

23 Jul 2003

By Grunge

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
FADE IN "Sleep will not come, to this tired body now... peace will not come to this lonely heart there are some things i'll live without but i want you ton know that i need you right now I need you tonight." (Grunge is lying on his bed with headphones on. Yet for some strange reason we can hear what Grunge is listening to. Grunge looks up at the camera and begins to speak.) Grunge: Did I listen to pop music because I was sad or was I sad because I listened to pop music? (Grunge begins to laugh as he takes off his headphones and turns to the CD player off. He rolls over on his side and the camera follows him.) Grunge: Sorry about that. I just couldn’t help myself. And that was the Smashing Pumpkins. Remember them? I certainly do. Why is it that I feel that "in the arms of sleep" is the best song that Corgan wrote? (Grunge begins to smile.) Grunge: This song is what most and I myself would call pathetic sad music. To be honest this is the type of music that I like the best at times like this. When I’m angry, say at a certain fish type fellow, this helps me calm down. (Grunge’s smile turns to his frown as his opponent from Meltdown’s name was mentioned.) Grunge: So thanks to Leviathan I need some big time “comfort music.” I’m getting a five disc boxed set soundtrack to ken burn's PBS documentary "JAZZ". There are some really great old jazz songs spread on this set. Stuff that I will lose myself in, once I get another decent pair of headphones. (Grunge laughs.) Grunge: Unfortunately I had to put the item on layaway so no one would attempt to buy it. Yes layaway. I recently bought a new house for my wife as you can plainly see. As such I’m not made of money. (Grunge listens and we can hear a hammering sound in the distance.) Grunge: Speaking of my wife, I wonder what she’s up to. (Grunge gets up and exits the bedroom and walks through the house to were he reaches what look like a study. Here Julie is hanging up a shadowbox with a Kimono inside.) Grunge: What do you do in your room? (Julie stops what she’s doing and gets off her little step-ladder. She gives her husband a very perplexed look.) Julie: What do I do in my room???? Grunge: Yeah. Well I bought the house because you wanted more room but you really haven’t done anything with this. Julie: I’m still decorating. (Julie begins to climb back up the step-ladder when Grunge stops her with another comment.) Grunge: You don't have to study, do you? Julie: What? Grunge: Well this is a study. Generally you do paperwork or, oh I don’t know, study in a STUDY! (Julie just begins to laugh and approaches her husband. She kisses him on the cheek and the slaps him on the back of the head.) Julie: Don’t worry about what I do with MY study. Worry about your match with Thorne and Leviathan on Monday. (At the slap on the head even Grunge can’t help but start laughing. What he does next is put Julie up on her shoulders for a boast and walks over to the box on the wall. He kicks the step ladder out of the way.) Grunge: Thanks to me and Nick Leviathan and Thorne’s momentum will be crushed. But in the meantime why don’t you let me help. (Julie begins to hammer as both of them laugh. Of course sometime between the action we hear a soft statement from Grunge.) Grunge: (whispers) Rock and roll. (And at the sight of the two working and having fun, we begin to…) FADE OUT
