Revalations

19 Feb 2003

By Truck

Item 5 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 19 Feb 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
( The camera fades in on a cold steel door painted grey. The paint chipped here and there with dents scattered between the revealed rust. The camera begins to pan back when an exit signs appears into the view. Without worning the door swings open and a bluster of cold wind and rain enter the room along with a shadowy figure. As the figure steps into the light of the hallway we see that the man is none other than Truck Maloy. The wind slams the steel door shut behind Mac as he begins down the corridor. The camera pans back as it treks with him. Mac walks past a couple standing in the corridor to his right, kissing. Mac pays them no mind and walks past. The man releases his wet liplock on the girl as he catches a glimpse of Mac over his shoulder. He turns his head to face the camera presenting his familiar face. It's the man Mac had recently made aquantances with a week before. The Boyfriend. His face is riddles with cuts and scraps with a beak immobilizer, stabilizing his broken nose. The boyfriend quickly grabs the wrist of the girl he was kissing, who is none other than that "ungrateful bitch". They quickly scatter down the hallway and out the exit into the cold storm. Mac walks on never giving them any attention and finds a place at the bar. The bald headed bartender finishes washing a pint glass and makes his way over to Truck.) Bartender: Usual? Mac: Yep? The bartender turns and makes his way down to the other end of the bar. He reaches down and flips back the sliding door of a cooler and reaches in, retrieving a yellow labeled Shiner Boch. The bartender reaches into his back pocket and pulls out his favorite tool and pops off the cap. He returns and places the bottle in front of Mac with a coster beneath it. Bartender: You know you've been here an awful lot lately? Ain't you got somewhere else to be? I thought all you wrassler's was supposed to be on the road all the time. Mac: Well that's usually true but I ain't had the best of luck as of late. I had to take a couple weeks off to go visit Aunt Tillie down in Huntsville. You remember Tillie. Bartender: I think so. Wasn't she the one who came in here looking for ole Joe Turner that night and caught him with that sassy blonde girl for the Coffe Cup Cafe. Mac: That'd be her! Bartender: When she get out? Mac: September. Mac pauses for a second to drink his beer as the bartender attends to another patron at the corner of the bar. The bartender tends to his duties then returns his curious ear to Mac. Bartender: Anyways you were sayin'. Mac: Awwww I suppose it ain't that bad, it's just I've noticed some things since I started working in the WWA! Bartender: Like what? Mac: Well. You know how it was back in the EWA. I went up there and whooped some ass and became champion. And I was a good champion. I stood up to the bullies, kept my nose clean, went to charity drives and all that stuff. The kind of stuff a champion should do. A role model I'd guess you say. Bartender: Yeah, yeah, that's true. Mac: Then I went to the WWA and all that stuff just don't seem to make a difference, to anybody. All they want is hardcore matches, blood, all that high flyin' nonsense. Nobody appreciates the wrestler's anymore. It's like that lady the other night. I thought that ole boy was gonna start smackin her around so I stepped in and tried to difuse the situation, right? Bartender: Yeah. Mac: And what'd it get me? A slap in the damn face! It's the same way in the WWA! No matter how hard I try to be a nice guy all I get is a slap in the face! Bartender: Don't worry bout it Mac. The fans will come around, they always do! Sooner or later your gonna get your shot at being noticed and when you do it'll all be straight! Mac: It ain't just the fans though. I'm talking bout back stage, or better yet, I'm talking bout the head office. It seems that the only matches I'm ever in are against guys they know couldn't beat me in a fair fight. So they bring in chairs, or have a buddy sittin' at ringside, or attack me from behind comin' out of the crowd. It's a hardcore match, or a handicap match, or a brawl for all. It's like somebody has it in for me. And the fans, hell the fans they love it. That'll dumbass Mal Somers is a damn poster boy for All - American to these people. A guy who should have lost that damn rookie title to me three times but cheated to keep the gold is looked up to like a golden god now. Something's wrong with people today. Bartender: Well, I don't really know what to say about the fans. But as for someone whose got it out for ya! Sometimes the only way to catch a snake is to go into its den! I think I'd fi..... A ring from under the bar interrupts the bartender. Mac reaches down into his back pocket and pulls out a silver cell phone. He flips down one end. Mac: Yeeelllloooo!!!........ Is that right..... A harcore match,huh?....... Mac turns toward the bartender. He raises his hand and points to the phone as if saying to the bartender "see, I told you." Mac: Just tell me who the hell its against..... Harley huh?...... Yeah, I'll be there but I got a stipulation..... Well, we'll talk about it when I get there.... Yeah, see ya then!!! Mac hangs up the phone and places it back into his back pocket. He turns his face back toward the bartender and picks up the Shiner Boch. Both men begin to shake their head in disguist as the camera fades to black.
