Four's Company

27 Feb 2003

By X Corp

Item 2 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 27 Feb 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[We here the murmur and mumblings of The Extreme Machine Dave Harley as he sits hunched over a table with a lamp illuminating his work.] Harley: …stupid Mal…he thinks he can outvote me with that stupid belt…I’ll show him… [In walks Harley’s Personal Assistant Jill. She takes a look over Dave’s shoulder and starts toward the door.] Harley: Oh, hey Jill. Didn’t see you there! Damn this back injury! I could have gone to the gym with Mal…instead he took Rookie. [Jill hears the contempt in his voice as he talks about Mal’s golden companion.] Harley: That damn title belt is making Mal side against me! Well I’ve come up with a way to even the score…[He lifts his creation from the table. It is a Scrabble board with a crudely drawn smiley face on it.] Meet Scrabby! [Jill stares at Dave in disbelief for a few seconds.] Jill: …I’ll be in the bar. [As she walks out the door, Mal walks in. Under one arm he carries a sports bag. In the other he carries Rookie.] Mal: Hey, what’s up with her? Y’know she could at least be polite to Rookie…what’s that?!! Harley: This is a friend of mine. His name’s Scrabby. Mal: It’s a freakin’ Scrabble Board! It’s not real! Harley: It’s just as real as Rookie! Mal: Right, that’s it! There’s only one way to settle this! [Five Minutes Later: Mal and Dave lay on the floor. Between them, Rookie and Scrabby have been propped up in a toy-wrestling ring. Both members of X Corp simply lay there watching, waiting for their friends to duke it out.] Harley: Ummm…is anything gonna happen here? Somers: Yeah, this match is almost as slow as one of The Cell’s Tag Title defences. [Suddenly, through the open window, a freak gust of wind blows through and knocks Scrabby out of the ring.] Mal: WOOHOO! IN YOUR FACE HARLEY! Harley: DAMMIT!! [Harley picks up Scrabby and snaps him in half.] Mal: Harley you stupid ass! That was our only Scrabble board! Harley: Oh yeah…um, sorry. Mal: Well what are we gonna do now? Harley: Don’t worry my friend; I’ll take care of it. [picks up his mobile phone] Jill, I need you to pick us up a new board. Jill? Can you hear me? JILL!! Damn phones! [Tosses it out the window.] Umm…you know how to play Snap? [Fade out]
