Hanging Out

27 Feb 2003

By X-Corp

Item 1 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 27 Feb 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Scene – Mal Somers, WWA Rookie Champion and one half of the Extreme Corporation is sitting at home on his sofa, watching television. A bottle of Budweiser sits half consumed beside him. On the other side of the two seater sofa lies his Rookie Title, with a full bottle of beer next to it.) Somers – C’mon Rookie, you aren't thirsty today? Drink up! (Somers takes another swig and the camera briefly catches what he, uh...they are watching, highlights from Global Warfare. Mal is being laid out on the announcers table by Grunge and Eddie Van Dorn. Meanwhile, the door opens and in steps Dave “The Extreme Machine” Harley. He walks across to the sofa, holding his sore back with one arm.) Harley – Hey Mal, whats up? (Harley picks up the Rookie Title and briefly examines the wool and macaroni that adorns its face. He smirks and tosses it on the bean bag to his left, slowly sits next to Mal and takes a swig of the full bottle of beer.) Harley – Ahhh. That hit the spot. You must’ve known I was coming over. Somers – You really should show Rookie more respect. That’s his beer you are drinking too! Harley – (shrugging) Uh whatever Mal. Somers (pointing at screen and turning the volume up) Hey check this out! (Somers flails on the announcers desk as Eddie Van Dorn leaps high in the air for one of his patented high risk, death defying maneuvers. At the last moment, Somers rolls out of the way, off the table as van Dorn crashes through the table.) Somers – THAT was close. Harley – You’re only offensive move of the match! Somers – Shut up. At least I showed up. Harley – Touche. Hey check THIS out! (Back to the TV screen, as Jackson Kraven gets a pin on his own teammate.) Harley – That’s my favourite bit. HA! Lets see that again! Somers – (turning the screen off with his remote) No! Oh dear, it looks like we have had a blackout. Harley – Yeah right. The lights are still on. Somers – Doh! OK then lets put it to a vote. Harley – You idiot. There are only two of us. Somers – I beg to differ. Rookie and I vote in favour of leaving the television off. HA! Harley – That’s just a belt! Hold on. (Harley presses a button on his cellular phone, which speed dials a familiar voice) Jill – Hello, Jill speaking. Harley – Hi Jill, can you just say “YES” Jill – Pardon? Harley – Just say the word “YES” Jill – Oh I don’t know Dave, this kind of thing ends up getting me in trouble in the end. Harley – PLEASE! Jill – Hmmmm. OK I’ll say... (The phone switches off as the battery runs flat.) Harley – Jill? Jill? Are you there? Oh DAMN IT!! (Harley throws the phone to the ground and stamps on it with his big boot.) Harley – Grrrrr! First my computer, now this! They don’t build em like they used to. Somers – Yeah that’s what your mom said when you were born. Harley – Hardy har har. See this? (points to his waist) See? That’s my side splitting with laughter. Somers – Hmmmph. Oh well Rookie and I won the vote. What do you want to do now? Play scrabble? Harley – Hmm maybe later. How about we talk about something that on my mind. Somers – Oh this’ll be quick. Harley – Jimmi Zion. Last week you are abusing each other and this week you are teaming with him? What about X-Corp man? Somers – I saw that today on the booking sheets. I assume they paired me with him because you are in no condition to wrestle with your back and all. Harley – Yeah fair enough, but what about just giving you a singles match? Or a big make out session with you and “Rookie”. Somers – Yeah right. Well I suppose we will have time to talk Jimmi with turkey, oh I mean talk turkey with Jimmi on Saturday. Maybe we can all come up with a comprimise. Harley – (grinning) Yeah I’ve got a few ideas. Somers (sipping his beer) – Hmmm this beer tastes great. No where near as good as MALcohol though. Heh heh. Harley – Hmmm. I’ll take your word for that and stick to the real deal. Is it having all that MALcohol whats making you stack on the pounds? Somers – Of now THAT’S funny. Not. Speaking of fat people though, how about that Cell hey? Harley – Oh yeah. We are their biggest fans. Last week on Fiesta, locking you in the change rooms, ganging up on me, what to they think they are? High school bullies? Somers – And then to top it off, I almost had the victory over Jimmi in our two out of three falls match, the referee was counting, and they had to mess that up for me too! Rookie said to me this morning if you hadn’t made it back in the ambulance I’d have been done for. Harley – Well Rookie is a very clever...what the hell am I saying? He, uh IT’S a belt! (Somers gets up and goes to the fridge, grabbing three bottles of cold beer, handing one to Harley and one to Rookie and the other for himself.) Somers – Something has to be done. Lets talk some strategy about how we are going to rid the WWA of the Cell and get us some Tag Team Titles! Harley – Cool! I’ll get the scrabble board! (Fade Out)
