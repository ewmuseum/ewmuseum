James and Becca in the Park

14 Feb 2003

By James Beam

Item 5 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 14 Feb 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene opens to a nice park-like scene. You see two people sitting together on a small bench. A couple people riding by on bikes and a few walk by paying no mind to the couple on the bench. As the camera moves closer to the couple, you notice it is James Beam and his lady friend, Rebecca Lynn Schafer. She has her head on his chest with her eyes closed, with a smile on her face. James on the other hand, has a grim look on his face, as if he is thinking or perhaps worrying about something.) Becca: Thanks for this Jim... I know you are training hard for your return to Fiesta and all that... But this is nice to take your time out for us and to give me another wonderful Valentine's day. (James's face melt into a smile as he kisses her on the head. At this she sits up straight and stand, laying a huge kiss on her man.) Becca: I know you need to do your thing here (motions to the camera with her head). So I will leave you to it. (looks at her watch) It is 3 now... How about we meet back at the hotel at 6 and get ready for dinner. James (smiling): That sounds wonderful.... See you then. (With that, Becca leaves the scene and James's face returns to what it was when the scene opened. James still seated begins to speak directly at the camera.) James: Well... It is time to get back onto the WWA-Express. Time to start winning matches again and time to gain back the respect and the love of the fans. (James gets up and begins walking, with the camera to the right front and to the side.) James: Ever since the autograph signing in Detroit, it has opened my eyes a little. Those kids were actually mocking me. They were actually making light of how I wrestle and how I perform in the spot light. They are worshipping people like Wes Sanders and Kash. People that break the rules and simply win at any cost, under the guise of fan favorite. (James stops walking, facing the camera directly.) James: Is this what the fans want?! Do they want me, James Beam, to break the rules and through my morals out the window and become the thing that I hate? To become the ego-maniacal buffoon that cater to nobody but themselves. (James shrugs and begins walking again. He shrugs then forces a smile.) James: I am not sure I can do that. I am not sure I can throw the rulebook out the window. I am not about that, nor have I ever been. So to Mr Curzon.... You and I seem to share a name and the similarities end there. You choose to cheat and bend the rules to your whim, whereas I choose to abide and follow them. One thing I do know James, is I am going to give you the best match you have ever had. One thing the fans expect from me is 150 percent. I will run you from pillar to post and you will know and REMEMBEr the name James Beam. That is a promise, Curzon. (James smiles at a young woman pushing a baby carriage. Then gets serious again.) James: James Curzon... You are a trouble young soul, this is for sure. You and I are going to have an excellent match and I look forward to it. Two young lions dueling for the next win that could propel us towards stardom. With light of what I said before, Curzon, expect a slightly different, if not more edgy, James Beam. Let me apologize to you right now for the change in style. I hope that you and I can still give the fans a good match. I hope you can keep up with me.... (James smiles to the camera. Then stops.) James: Life is too short to always give it your all. Keep chasing your dreams... Because we ALL DIE YOUNG!! (Fade)
