An Interview

21 Feb 2003

By The Cell

Item 4 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 21 Feb 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(A WWA studio with Wallace Coleman sitting in a black swivel chair in full view, the camera pans back to show he is not alone but with Leviathan and Lockdown, The Cell. Leviathan is smartly dressed in a white suit with a small blue trident on his jacket pocket. Lockdown is casually dressed with a Cell t-shirt “BRUTALLY TRUTHFUL” in bold red letters centred in a pool of red liquid.) Wallace Coleman: Welcome WWA fans for the first official interview with the new WWA Tag Team Champions, The Cell. These two men have risen rapidly since joining forces under former Commissioner Cruz… Leviathan: Wallace we were never under Cruz, merely contracted to enforce his wishes. At no time were we his team. If you checked the tapes, you will see who actually proposed the alliance. Lockdown: Don’t you #beep#ing forget it, or I will make what I did to Shaman look like a #beep#ing picnic. Wallace: Sorry, as I was saying. These two men have achieved a lot in the short timeframe they have been in the WWA. Victories and now the WWA Tag Team Championship. My first question is what do you put it down to? Leviathan: Hard work and our ability. Lockdown: And wanting to destroy any #beep# that gets in our way. Levi here is power personified and me, well I’m #beep#ing ruthless and dangerous. Any team knows once they get in the ring. It ain’t going to be pretty. Leviathan: Essentially we are poles apart but together, we are a brutal force. Lockdown: We both enjoy #beep#ing hurting people. Wallace: And what do the WWA Tag titles mean to you? Leviathan: Respect. Acknowledgement to what we are doing. Lockdown: And #beep#ing good times. Women, beer and #beep#ing money. Wallace: Will you be victorious in you first title defence? Leviathan: Yes, and you can be sure of that. Lockdown: Brutally sure. Cruz held us back last time, but this time no #beep#ing strings are attached. The body bags are ready. Wallace: What about the new Commissioner? Lockdown: What about him? We don’t fear that mutha. We have the belts. We will do as we #beep#ing well please. And if he doesn’t catch on, he will be #beep#ing dealt with permanently. The Cell is in control and he better learn fast. Leviathan: Agreed. Wallace: X-corp thinks they can change that and I believe they want a shot. Lockdown: Who won at Rage in the Cage? The Cell, end of story. Wallace: But most people think they deserve a rematch. Lockdown: Do you? Wallace: Yes. Lockdown: Wrong answer. (Lockdown stands and moves towards Wallace Coleman but Leviathan catches him and asks him to sit back down.) Leviathan: Wallace, you have been warned. As for X-corp, why do they deserve it, who have they beat? And I doubt Dave Harley has what it takes anymore. His back will take the punishment. Wallace: Do you feel any remorse for what you did to Dave Harley at rage in the Cage? Leviathan: Only that it wasn’t higher. Lockdown: (laughs) Good one. They knew what the #beep# they were getting into and that’s it. They don’t deserve anything from us. We have been smacking them all over the WWA. For a title shot you have to be number one contenders. Are they? Wallace: Not that I know of. Lockdown: Case closed moron. Any way we have to BEAT Shaman and his mystery man. Leviathan: BEAT is the word. Wallace: What about Global Warfare? You and Mal Somers are on the same team. Leviathan: For one match only. To me winning is important for personal pride. Somers will be a distraction but first victory is paramount. Lockdown: Then you can kick the #beep# out of him. Wallace: Can you co-exist? Leviathan: If it means victory, yes. It’s an honour if we achieve this especially with Mr Kraven. Wallace: What are your thoughts on Kraven? Who also is the leader of the East Coast team. Leviathan: I respect his methods. I respect his ability and most importantly I respect the man. That is why he is the leader. Wallace: Well that’s it we’re out of time, thanks for you time. Lockdown: It’s been your pleasure.
