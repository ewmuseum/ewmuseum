Two's Company, Three's a Crowd... but Four is Going to be Unbearable

12 Feb 2003

By Tracer Bullet

Item 3 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 12 Feb 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene opens outside Tracer Bullet and Missy LeBeaux's house. You cna tell it's their house because the name "LeBeaux" is on the mailbox and that's not exactly a common last name, even around here. By the lighting, the sun is just rising. The camera zooms in on the door as a figure walks up to it and rings the doorbell. A voice can barely be heard coming through the door.) Voice: Speak friend and enter. (A familiar female voice responds.) Female Voice: He's even a dork about answering the door. (The door opens and there stands Tracer Bullet himself. The crowd cheers.) TB: Oh, sorry... I was just listening to Live at Budokan... (He reaches off camera and turns the CD player off. The crowd stops cheering.) TB: And what's the matter, don't you appreciate Lord of the Rings? (The camera zooms out a little and we can see Tracer is talking to a sleepy and pissed off looking Alexandra Korth.) Alexandra: I want you to know this wasn't my idea. TB: Fairly obvious, as you never have ideas as brilliant as visiting me. But of course I kid. Do come in. (Before Alexandra can respond, a weird beeping noise, like that of a truck backing up can be heard. After a moment, Denrol walks into view, carrying about 3 large boxes. Denrol is the one making the noise.) Denrol: Better hold that door open, don't want to drop these on anyone's feet. TB: Moving in? Denrol: Ahh, a great wrestler and smart too. (Alexandra steps back to let her husband through, she rolls her eyes in disgust. Tracer's eyes goggle momentarily before regains his composure.) TB: Um... I was JOKING, Gordon... (Missy walks down the hall from the main room.) ML: What's going on? (Denrol slips past Tracer and puts down his boxes.) Denrol: Hey there Missy. Good morning to you. (turns to Tracer) I had a vision yesterday when flying home. If we are to tag together, we need to know each other inside out. We have to be like brothers. So what's the best way to get to know each other in a short span? That's right, live together. (Tracer quirks his eyebrow quizzically.) Denrol: What? TB: It's just that... well... Alexandra: Again, not my idea. Denrol: Well that and the fact that my place is getting painted and we needed a place to crash for the week. ML: I suppose you're welcome to stay for a few... (Tracer cups a hand over his wife's mouth.) TB: Heh heh... surely a superstar the likes of you, a FRANCHISE PLAYER, can afford to stay in a hotel? Denrol: Well sure yeah. But then I would have to put the championships in a kennel. TB: Um... say who? You travel with your replica titles? Denrol: No, no. Those are in storage. Wait, I'll get them from the truck. You'll love them. Through Extreme isn't quite house broken yet. (Denrol leaves the house.) TB: Alex? What in the hell is he trying to p... oh wait, wait... let me guess... Alexandra: (sighs) He was more insane in college, believe me. ML: What, babe? What is he doing? Why is he insane THIS time? TB: Just wait, I'm sure you'll hear them coming in a second... (All of a sudden five cats march their way into the house, all shapes and sizes. They quickly scatter throughout the house.) ML: Aww, how CUTE! TB: Oh, thank God. I thought it was dogs... Alexandra: He's a cat person. (Denrol follows them, carrying more boxes.) Denrol: They're cute, aren't they? World, US, Light Heavyweight, Extreme, and TV. (This time, even Missy looks at Denrol as if he's insane.) ML: You named them after the belts you've won? Denrol: Yep, got one each time I won a belt. TV is the oldest and Extreme is still a kitten. (Missy and Tracer continue to look at Denrol like he's grown an extra head.) TB: Right... ML: I... suppose that's kind of cute... maybe... (There is a long awkward pause.) TB: But anyway, why HERE? Denrol: You're my partner man. Remember, Meltdown? The WWA Franchise Players, together for the first time ever. I called Shaman, he might be joining us tomorrow or sometime during the week. (Tracer puts his face in his hands in an exasperated fashion.) TB: Great, this is the last thing I need. My house is not some kind of hostel that the entire roster can just wander around at will, you know... Denrol: Not the entire roster man. Calm down. I'll chip in some cash for food and stuff. You won't even know we're here. Though you're right about Shaman. I wouldn't want that nut in my home either. (Tracer suddenly looks up at Denrol.) TB: Wait a minute... isn't the WWA doing a Canadian tour right now? Denrol: Yeah, but I'm still recovering from my match with Kraven. The docs don't want me to wrestle till Monday. So I'm off the house show circuit. TB: So then, I guess my next question would seem highly logical... WHAT THE HELL ARE YOU DOING IN NEW ORLEANS? Denrol: I told you, I had a vision yesterday. We got home, I packed our stuff and we chartered a flight down here. (Alexandra sighs and sits down on a box, clearly very tired.) TB: Excuse me a minute... (Tracer exits and comes back seconds later with a cushioned chair.) TB: Try this instead. I'm sure it's a lot more comfortable than that box, and you look like you've had a bitch of a day... as I'm sure I'll have several of in the next week as well. (Alexandra nods and sits in the chair.) Alexandra: You may be a dork, but you are a gentleman. TB: Say what you will about me, but I'm never discourteous in my own home. Now then... (Tracer turns to Denrol.) TB: Since it's obvious you're here, and my subtle hints aren't working, and I'm too polite to just up and say "Leave," it's clear that you're going to be here awhile. That being said, shall we at least discuss some business? Denrol: Sure. Let me just get our luggage. (Denrol leaves the house. Melissa tugs on Tracer's shoulder.) ML: Can I talk to you privately for a minute? (Tracer looks at Alexandra.) TB: Would you excuse us a moment? Alexandra: Yeah sure. I'll just wait for my insane husband to get back. (Tracer and Missy walk a short distance off from their home's entrance.) ML: I don't like it... TB: *sarcastically* Gee, what's not to like? ML: Seriously. He's been awfully off-kilter lately. You saw what he did to Kraven... to Chance... he's changed... TB: Actually, he hasn't. He's always been kind of a prick. But a fun prick. ML: You know what I mean. What if he goes off and does the same thing to you before Monday night gets here? TB: Nah, he wouldn't do that. ML: Despite the fact that he said he'd have no qualms about crippling you if you had met in the finals at Labor Day Brawl? TB: Ah, that's different. Apparently Craven Moore had no qualms about lynching me, and he somehow pulled out of 2002 as the Most Popular Wrestler of the Year. Go figure. I say we let them stay, at least for a few days. But they'll find themselves on the lawn if they try anything funny. (Tracer looks down at the floor.) TB: And besides... (He reaches down and picks up Extreme.) TB: Kitties! Extreme: Mew? ML: *giggle* All right. For the kitties... (They walk back over to the entrance, Tracer petting Extreme.) Denrol: There they are. Aren't they cute honey? (Alexandra has fallen asleep on the chair. Denrol has finished bringing in luggage, there is now about 7 boxes and 4 suitcases.) TB: Hmm... that kind of begs another question... did you really need so much stuff for one week? Denrol: Well the suitcases are our clothes and stuff. You know how much stuff women pack. But the boxes are my wrestling tapes. (Tracer, still petting Extreme, looks back and forth at all the boxes.) TB: Sleep much? Denrol: I tape every match I have and even some of my practice sessions. The Television matches I get mutiple tapes of, the feed from each camera, so I can study it from every angle. TB: Sometimes I think you overanalyze things. Seriously. Maybe if you learned to go with the flow of a match, rather than looking at your own mistakes over and over, you'd have held the World Title more than three days. (Tracer notices that Denrol is giving him a dirty look.) TB: Of course, I haven't done anything worth a damn for almost two years now, who am I to talk? Denrol: I also have a few tapes of Chance here and some guys I think he might pick as partners. You wanted to talk business, right? TB: Well, YEAH, but... Denrol: But what, partner? TB: But... all this? Denrol: Well I had to take them. These are priceless artifacts. I couldn't leave them where they could be stolen. (Denrol opens a box and takes out a tape.) Denrol: This is my first match ever, in my wrestling school. You know how much this would go for on eBay? TB: Dunno... buck and a half, with shipping? Denrol: Very funny. Anyway, when's breakfast? I'm starving. TB: We just had it, actually. You're welcome to lick the plates, though. Failing that, you could always pull a nice blueberry bagel out of the pantry. Denrol: Blueberry bagel? You got no taste in bagels. I think- (Denrol gets cut off by the honk of a horn outside.) Denrol: (looks at his watch) A bit early, but not bad. TB: First of all, I've got more than just the blueberry. Wide selection of bagels. But no cream cheese, I take them plain. Secondly, what the hell just pulled up in my driveway? Denrol: The wrestling ring. I had a couple left over from the GLWF and I keep them in storage around the country just in case I ever start my own promotion. (Denrol opens the door and leans out. He then yells at the guys driving it.) Denrol: Just leave the stuff in the back, we'll put it together later! (The men nod and start unloading the truck and carrying stuff into the backyard.) TB: *to Missy* It's always so nice when we go to visit friends. And we're in luck, they're having a new conversation piece installed today! Denrol: The entrance and rampway will be here in the afternoon. TB: I'd throw my hands up in exasperation, but I'd drop the precious little kitty. Denrol: Well, I'll go get myself breakfast. You two love birds have fun. Don't worry, Alex will handle the litters when she wakes up. (Denrol smiles and leaves.) TB: Boy, this is going to be an interesting week... (Tracer continues petting the kitty, then raises his pinky to his mouth.) TB: Isn't it, Mister Powers? ML: That was horrible. TB: I know... (In the background the sound of something fragile being shattered by an errant cat can be heard.) ML: Much as I like cats, that had better the HELL have been an ashtray... (Missy and Tracer run off down the hallway. The camera slowly pans over to Alexandra, who seems asleep, but isn't. She mumbles to herself.) Alexandra: Welcome to Hell Trent and Missy. (The scene fades to black.)
