Untitled

9 Jun 2003

By Tommy Starr

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(We begin by looking at the tanned and toned back of the WWA's resident Super "Starr", Tommy Starr. He is in a bathroom, and is standing very close to a mirror. The camera moves closer so that we move alongside Tommy Starr. Both of his hands are on his face. He is squeezing a zit.) Starr: Why do you always have to bother me in my most intimate moments? Can't get enought of the NEXT next big thing, I guess. Its very rare that my perfect complexion would ever be blemished by a zit. Honestly! The secret to squeezing a zit is you have to get it all out. Otherwise it stays under your skin and comes back to haunt you later on. (Starr pauses for a moment as he squeezes away.) Starr: Almost there. Anyway, this zit reminds me of someone. Thats right, you know I'm talking about you Reverend Tweak! I mean, what kind of surname is that? Is that what you like doing to your nipples or something? Anyways Tweak, you are nothing but a zit to me. A minor blemish on an otherwise picture perfect canvas. A minor annoyance, but you give it a bit of a push, and a prod...and it goes away. (Suddenly Starr's work is done as the residue deposits itself on the mirror. Starr wipes it away with some waste paper.) Starr: And just like that...gone away, never to be thought of again! Although that pus probably put up a better fight than you ever would...GEEK! (Starr throws the paper in the trash as we zoom in on it. Beside the trash is Reverend Tweaks Bible, with a page torn from it. We fade to black.)
