The Lull Before the Storm

11 Jun 2003

By "The Psycho" Steven Murphy

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The rain pounds down hard over the River Mersey, as the wind howls in the cold summer night. The moon shrouded by the clouds gathering above, with the trees swirling from the harsh gales from the sky. The sloppy mud off the banks of the river seeps into the water. At the Side of the banks, the streetlights flickers on and off every few minutes, as the rain continue to pound down onto the Liverpool streets. The drops of rain slowly rolled down the fence that aligned the polluted river. In the dead silence of the night, loud, slow footsteps are heard. The camera quickly turns, and shows in the distance a tall man, wearing a long leather coat, soaking wet from the torrential rain. With his hands in his pockets, and him trying to keep warm in the bitter cold, the footsteps get louder. As he gets closer, we can see the distinctive scar on his right eye. It is WWA’s own Steven “The Psycho” Murphy.) Psycho: Lovely weather, aint it?! (Psycho continues to walk down the street, closer and closer to the camera. As he gets about a metre away from the camera, the streetlights go out, leaving the whole street in darkness. You can only see the outline of Murphy, who begins to speak even with the blackout.) Psycho: It seems that my opponent does not get me with my warning to him. (Laugh) I have seen your type to many times before Chris. Every federation I have been in, there is always one. One person who really does think he is “the man”. Vice, get your ego in check first before you come and trash talk a very dangerous person. (The lights flicker back on, and the Psycho is not in front of the camera, but now behind. The camera spins around, and shows Psycho laughing. He is now on the other side of the fence, on the banks of the River Mersey.) Psycho: You see Vice, I have beaten people like you before time and time again. You come and go, making a little fuss, and get your ass kicked to give you a reality check. In my mind, people like you Vice remind me off this river. It’s dirty, no-body likes it, but its there. My aim? To make you not be here after our match. (Psycho turns his back on the camera, as the rain slows down. The wind suddenly comes to a halt. Psycho is looking into the now calm river.) Psycho: The lull before the storm. Silence, calm, everything seems peaceful. Then BOOM, lightning strikes. Vice, Caesar, this is the lull before the storm, and you better be ready for that storm, because if you are not….well, lets say injury is a nice word to use. (The Psycho turns to face the camera once more, as the wind starts to pick up once more.) Psycho: Why do you think im a skitzo Vice? Do I act like one? Do I sound like one? I may be a vicious son of a bitch, but im not a skitzo. Skitzo’s belong in Looney bins, not the WWA. And why do you mock the WWA? Is it because you’re a asshole? Maybe. But it is really one reason. You are scared Vice, you are scared of me. Your scared of the damage I will cause on Saturday. You try to divert attention with your behaviour, but it can’t fool me. And you cannot fake it on Saturday. Because on Fiesta, it wont be a match, it will be a presentation. A presentation on how I will make you Just Another Victim of The Psycho. (Psycho turns his back on the camera again, as the rain slowly drops from the heavens, as Psycho utters some final words.) Psycho: The Storm is coming… (Scene Fades.)
