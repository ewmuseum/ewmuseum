"Aiming high (No I'm not joining the Air Force)"

29 Nov 2003

By Jimmi Zion

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
*{Fade In} *{The camera opens ipside a large sports bar and grill in New York City. The bar is busy with people playing pool over in one area, a group of people doing Karoke over on another side of the bar, and then some people drinking and watching sports games on a wall of televisions. The camera moves around searching for signs of familer life and then it comes across the section where there is nothing but dart board machines. At this time, there only apparently appears to be one person playing darts. The camera moves into position to identify the familier face as none other than "Sky Flyin" Jimmi Zion. He is quiet and keeping to himself as he throws darts against the target. The camera looks on the target to reveal that there are pictures taped to them. The camera zooms into their position to reveal the identies of this pics as Jimmi's oponants for The Cross Roster fatal 4 way elimination tag team match for the IC/U.S. titles match this weekend.} {In the target are the following} Chuckster and Fuel... Sniper and Kaled... Akiro and Polaris... {Then down on the outside of the target is Zion's Meltdown tag team partner for the event} Desolation... {Zion looks out of the corner of his eye to see the camera looking at him and his darts game. Zion gives off a subtle smile at the camera while still sparatically throating darts. He begins talking to the camera while still throwing his darts at his targets. Let's listen in.} "Sky Flyin" Jimmi Zion: "You know, I like this game of darts. Such a precise little game. It requires lots of concentration and skill, but at the same time there is a certain amount of luck that goes into the playing of this game. It's kind of like wrestling. Yeah it's kind of like wrestling in many ways. You can have all of the skills, the physiqe, the determination to win, but there is also the certain X-factor of luck that gets involved into the match, and using the circumstances to your advantage." {Zion walks over to gather his darts and then returns to the line to continue speaking and throwing again} "I know that I have had people do that to me, but in turn that has caused me to use these same factors againsts other people. Sometimes it works and sometimes it makes me fall on my face. All in all what does this boil down to, as agreesive and insense as I am in the ring, I have come to one FINAL conclusion... "I have been very weak. I have shown too much mercy on all of my WWA brotherhood. I have held back on them for too long. All in all where has it gotten me? Nothing...well except for the fact that it has made me lose everything that I have ever worked for." {Zion gathers his darts from the boards.} "However that I must remember that I am no more weaker than the WWA. If anything they are just as opportunitistic as I have been in the past. Always being in the right place at the right time. Well I've had enough of this its time for me to shine even at the expense of the entire WWA." "This Cross Roster Fatal Fourway elimination match that we are having is an excellent example. 8 competitors all vying for their shot at greatness. Their early or late birthday present if you wanna call it that. I mean our present to the WWA is kicking people's asses and putting on an awesome show for the fans and the WWA, increasing PPV buyrates, and generating revenue. That's our gift to the WWA. We've...no I take that back...I've done everything for the WWA. When is it time they they do something for me. When will it be time that they give me my birthday present? When it will be time that they give me MY time to shine?" {Zion walks over to the board to gather his darts.} "You know, some people play Scrabble to focus, to me, my game of choice for unwinding and focusing on the tasks at hand is darts. My mind is so clear...now where was I oh yeah the match this weekend." {Starts throwing his darts again.} "I'm not playing anymore. I'm tired going by the rules and hoping that I be at the right place at the right time. It's time that I make things happen. It's time that do things for myself. Helping people maybe all good and well, but you still have to look out for number 1, and that's me. I have to take care of me first, and I plan on doing that this weekend at the Birthday Bash." "I plan on returning back to my roots. Here let me explain for all your feeble minds out there in TV land. I see it just like this so pay close attention because I'm not saying this twice." {He walks over to gather his darts then returns to the line and starts throwing them into the boards again.} "8 Competitors, and I'm fighting 6 of them. That means if your not with me you're against me. When you sign your name on that contract, and I know that you all have. You probably failed to read the find black bold print at the bottom. Well since you all didn't allow me to re-read it to you... {He throws a dart, it hits dead center inside a picture on the board} Chuckster... {Throws another dart hitting a picture} Sniper... {Throws a dart hitting a picture.} Fuel... {Throws another dart hitting a picture} Polaris... {Throws another dart hitting a picture} Khaled... {Throws another dart hitting a picture} Akiro... "That means that all six of you are marked. Marked by me because I'm not holding back like I may of done before...Akiro. You will know exactly what I can and will do. I don't care what happens to you. I know this, if your in that match that means you are putting your lives in my hands. You are at risk of losing more than just a title. You could lose alot more than just a title shot. Your careers are on the line in this match against me. I don't care what happens to you. This is the way that I see it. You wanna win, I wanna win much much more than you do even at the cost of all of your personal saftey, I will win and get my shot at the WWA Intercontinential title. Something that I should of had a long time ago. I should of been the very first Intercontinential champion...not LeTrav. Well I will be the man who takes it after the Birthday Bash." {Zion walks over to gather his darts and returns to the line to begin throwing them again.} "I don't care if you are a friend or a foe. If your a member one of my CPE stablemates it doesn't matter. I'm not holding back." {Zion stops for a moment as he looks directly at the camera for the very first time} "I've been told that I'm reckless. Justin Bourne found this out the hard way and it cost him as he was put on the shelf. Shaman pointed this out to me and I tore his ass up at May Revolution. Nick Adams tried to think he was all that and a sack of apples, and I showed him who's the real teacher during the Bourbon Street Brawl. Haven't seen Mal Somers, but he tried to voice his opinion....er I mean talk his s*** on me, and then couldn't back it up. You know what I finally realize about all this? You're all right, I'm reckless. I am very reckless...in fact... ...I'm the MOST RECKLESS MAN ON THE PLANET.... Here's the thing, then I wasn't even trying, and I was being reckless. What happens if I just did it with no regards or care in the world for my actions? It means I will go alot further if I do this. I probably won't get brownie points in the friends department but so what. I'm not in this business to make friends. I'm here to win. I'm here to look out for number one...MEEEEEEEEEEEE! "All you ball lickers are in for night because in that ring this weekend in the birthday bash, I'm not holding back, and you will find out that I am the most reckless man on the planent." "And as for my Meltdown tag team partner Desolation, all you have to do is step aside, do your thing and stay out of my way and I'll give you your U.S. Title shot. You just know your roll and stay out of my way. Can you do that Des?" "This is my time to shine and it's time the WWA give me some birthday presents. If it wasn't for me you wouldn't have a GenX division or a title. Hell you wouldn't have a WWA if it wasn't for me. As for the CPE, I have one thing for all of you... ...ALL BETS ARE OFF. "Now if you excuse me I bave other things to tend to for now. See you on the Birthday Bash." {Puts his hands together in a mock prayer like fashion} "Bring me lots of presents. Oh please make my night. Show me your best, and I'll show you the meaning of reckless endangerment." {Zion turns back around and walks over to the dart board, picks his darts up then returns to the line where he starts throwing them at the board again hitting the pictures and enjoying himself. The camera zooms in on the holed pictures of Chuckster, Sniper, Khaled, Polaris, Fuel, Akiro, and even the ocasional holes inside Desolation's picture. The camera fades out.} {Fade Out}
