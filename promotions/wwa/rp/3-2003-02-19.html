By Damian Thorne

Item 11 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 19 Feb 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Is that the floor? one, two, three...three floors? Jesus my head is killing me. (the image you see swims, three identical images, each one slighty jarred from the others. three floors, three tables, three windows, all taken in at a glance, swimming, spinning around each other like some sort of bizzare kaleidascope) What the f**k is wrong with me? I've had worse, much worse, but never suffered like this. Jesus, this spinning is making me feel sick (You stagger a liitle to the left, shooting your hand out to one of the three chairs you see, hoping to at least reach the middle one. You steady yourself for a second, but the images continue to dance about. Your knees buckle slightly, then you retch, not enough to vomit, but enoughto tear your eyes up, making your vision worse) What the hell is this, a tumor? Am I dying here? It's like there is a small child trying to escape from my head. Damn eyes just won't focus, my balance is all off key.... (You take a sharp intake of breath as a searing pain stabs into your head. You shoot a hand to your temple, pressing with all your might for some relief...but it doesn't come. Your vision swims more, your grip on the chair weakens, your knees buckle further. The sofa is only ten, maybe twelve steps away....) Can I make it? If I fall between here and the sofa, I'm gonna bite italian marble floor. I just just stand here, withering like an old man......f**k it (You push off from the chair, almost toppling it over. Your legs feel like lead, making it seem almost impossible to walk. Your knees are weak, and you can't straighten them. Your vison swims faster and you have to shut your eyes to supress the gag reflex. Four more steps..... You stumble, once, twice, three times, then fall to your knees, right by the arm of the sofa. The pain is so intense that you can hear your heart screamin its beat in your ears. Your vision hazes, begins to white out as your body gives up) No, not like this..... (Your now feeble arms stretch up and grasp the arm of the sofa weakly. You flop your head from side to side, trying to clear it. One instant of clarity befalls you, and you cling to it with all your remaining strength, and with what feels like a herculean effort, your darg yourself onto the sofa. The pain pounds at your head, and your vison slowly fades from white to black. Sounds become echos as you fall, deeper and deeper.....until a faint noise stops you. In an instant, your vison clears, and the pain relents it's torture for two, maybe three seconds, just long enough to hear..) "Damian...I'm home honey!" (you use the breif seconds to roll onto your back and gaze up at the three identical faces of the one angel who just brought you back to reality) "Hey princess" "Damian...I'm over here. Look at you, your eyes are all over the place. You ok hun?" "Fine. I just have a headache" (fade)
