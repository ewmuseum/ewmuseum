Bow down

11 Feb 2003

By Lockdown

Item 4 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 11 Feb 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Scene opens and we find Lockdown sitting on a dirty bed of a cheap hotel, wearing the Cell’s “Brutally Truthful” t shirt which features a police photo of a savagely murdered young woman. He holds a remote in his hand watching a tape of recent WWA shows Meltdown and a few WWA insiders.) Lockdown: Well I guess that was alright but, I still think that get well card should have had high explosives in it. Cruz never lets me have any fun. Oh well. The money is worth it. (Turns toward camera) Dave Harley, you say you keep coming back for more. That’s fine by me; I really enjoy kicking your ass. I’m glad to see you give me opportunity after opportunity. You’re such a good sport. I mean even in the hospital bed all you can think of is letting me beat your sorry ass again. I don’t know what I have done to deserve such kindness, but thanks buddy. (Turns back toward the television and watches some more. This time it is a recent Jimmi Zion segment from insider. Lockdown bursts into almost uncontrollable laughter. Lockdown is holding his ribs as he regains his composure and turns back toward the camera.) What’s that, Jimmi? You handle your pipe better? Must be all the practice you get when Mikalia out working the streets. (In a mocking tone) “Hi, I’m Jimmi Zion. I am a sh*tty wrestler, but I shore can handle my pipe! Yessiree bob, I am a grade A monkey spanker. I could win a blue ribbon for punching the clown. I ain’t no good in the ring, but I love to go a few rounds with the bald headed champion.” (Lockdown again laughs loudly. Suddenly his demeanor changes and the laughter ceases. His jolly countenance becomes abruptly menacing) But seriously, Jimmi, I hope for your sake… and your little road whore’s… you heed our warning. If you think that by attacking us at RITC you’ve accomplished something, think again. You got us after we had just finished one of the WWA’s most brutal cage matches ever. One in which the Cell came out on top, I might add. You think you beat us up? Hell we were already beat up. All you did was piss in the pool. Then you go on insider and talk about beating me when you’re at your best? Jimmi, I am gonna say this once, just in case we weren’t clear enough on Meltdown. If you keep f*cking with the Cell…( Points to the victim on his shirt) … Mikalia will be on our next t shirt. (Turns back toward the television and lights a cigarette. He is now watching the aftermath of the tag title number one contender’s match. Havoc is pounding the Veteranz.) Now I don’t understand this. Havoc, you attacked the wrong guys. The Cell is the team that’s gonna take your titles. But it must be hard to be gods. I bet you work up quite an appetite up there on Olympus. Well the good mortal, Lockdown is here to help you out. I have some food of the gods for you to munch on right here.(Grabs his crotch) You can get on YOUR knees and bow to that, you fake ass, dumb gimmick having, about to be FORMER tag team champion, sons of b*tches. This Saturday on Fiesta The Cell is gonna commit Deicide and walk out with the tag titles. (There is a knock at the door. Lockdown opens it and standing before him are two attractive, yet completely trashy young women.) Lockdown: What do you two sluts want? Whore 1: Some Mexican guy called the agency and had us come here. Whore 2: He said to show you a good time. It’s all paid for. Lockdown: (Turning toward camera) I guess Cruz lets me have some fun after all. Fade out
