Penthouse Suite

14 Oct 2003

By The Mysterious GAROU

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(We open on The Mysterious GAROU sitting cross-legged on the floor of a posh hotel suite. Dressed in black jeans and a red t-shirt, GAROU emits a sense of serenity. Behind his trademark black and red mask, we can see his calm, soothing eyes staring directly into the camera. As GAROU sits perfectly still, we begin to notice that the room around him looks curiously untouched. All the chairs are pushed in neatly. A folded card with the world “Welcome” scrawled on the front in elegant script sits undisturbed on a nearby table. Two small mints, wrapped in shiny foil, lay upon the fluffy pillows of the perfectly made bed. GAROU waits a moment longer before speaking in a reserved, soothing tone.) GAROU: It has been a little over a week since I received word of my promotion to the WWA. In that time, quite a bit has changed. For instance, I find myself moving from a squalid one-room apartment in the city of London, England to this exquisite hotel in New York City. The WWA has graciously agreed to pay for my stay here until I am able to arrange more permanent living arrangements. (GAROU looks around at the pristine room.) GAROU: While I do appreciate the generosity of my new employers, I cannot help but feel a bit out of place here. You see, the life of penthouses and deluxe suites is something I once coveted. In fact, it was my initial motivation for pursuing a career in the world of professional wrestling. Soon enough, I learned that such motivations are shallow and lead down the path to ruin. It was a harsh lesson, and I nearly crumbled under the weight of it. Being here today serves as a reminder of everything I have vowed to shun in favor of a life of righteous combat. Still, the complementary fruit platters and jet bubble bathtubs are indeed wondrous. (GAROU, still sitting, gives us a coy smile.) GAROU: But the heart of a warrior is not rightly forged amid silk sheets and lavish wall-to-wall carpeting. The past few days have been a refreshing break from my usual routine of intense physical training and meditation. Even so, my body yearns for a return to this regiment. Before long, it will be time for me to make my WWA debut. When the time finally comes for me to embark on the road to destiny, I want to be ready. I want to go out and give my all for the WWA fans and show my appreciation for their support. I want to deliver a message to the superstars of the WWA, to make them see that, while I may be young, I am not to be taken lightly. If I am to accomplish these things, I will need to be in the best shape of my life. I will need to pay a penance for the past few days of soft living. (GAROU quickly rises from the floor. The camera shakily pans up to focus on his upper body. His eyes, once so quiet, are now roaring with intensity. GAROU shakes his masked head back and forth and stretches his arms as though awaking from a long slumber.) GAROU: Yes, I’ve wasted enough time lying dormant in the lap of luxury. It is time to get moving. My warrior’s spirit demands it! I cannot rest until I have earned the right to rest. I must let nothing distract me from the task at hand. (GAROU looks to the sky for a brief moment before rushing out of the shot. We are left with the sight of a perfectly organized room. After a brief moment, GAROU walks back into the frame and plucks a banana from the bowl of fresh fruit sitting on the table.) GAROU: Perhaps just one more. (Once again, GAROU strides off, filled with determination. The shot quickly fades to black. After a moment, silver letters reading, “Join the G Generation” appear. We are given a moment to take in this message before the screen goes completely dark.)
