Self Diagnosis

2 Feb 2006

By Mal Somers

Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
When Mal Somers awoke from his slumber, whether it be morning, afternoon or night time, he could always count on waking in one of three places. His bedroom, a hotel room or a hospital. This morning it was the latter. He shrugged his shoulders, and winced as the pain seared up to the base of his neck. Mal had just been enjoying a very deep sleep, and within that comforting slumber he had been dreaming a very happy and enjoyable dream. It was the closing moments of Rage in the Cage 2006 and Somers had managed to reverse Ember’s “From the Ashes” finishing manoeuvre into the “MALfunction” screwdriver tombstone. The crowd roared and counted to three and as Somers was handed the World Wrestling Alliance World Championship Title for the second time, he finally felt complete. He looked down at the fallen Ember and knelt beside him, feeling the will of the MALcoholics urging him to do what no-one else had done before. He grasped the base of the mask with the tips of his fingers and started to pull on the elastic. That’s when Mal woke up. Another day, another pain in the neck. Mal thought to himself as he blinked his eyes and adjusted to his new surroundings. And it wasn’t the darting pain in his neck that he was alluding to either. It was Khaled. After Birthday Bash, Mal had planned to mark the days off to Rage in the Cage one by one, and get stronger, fitter and mentally prepared for his second Title shot in his second Rage Main Event. However nothing ever went that smoothly, and ever since Birthday Bash, Khaled had been the proverbial thorn in the side of Mal Somers, making his life a living hell, worse than his final weeks with Penny Wise. The last straw had come at Meltdown, when Khaled had attacked him from behind. Mal touched the back of his head and felt the pain pulsate throughout his skull. He held his breath, letting the pain naturally subside, and after a few moments Mal observed the dark gray dust on his fingertips. “A cinder block, how original” he muttered under his breath. Mal thought back over his career in but a few moments. Feuding with Tracer Bullet over a deep dark mystery from the past, and battling Dave Harley over the wounds that form when a strong friendship goes bad. He thought about his wars with Bonafide and Eddie van Dorn over the theft of his beloved Rookie and his feuds against The Cell over who was the most extreme team in the World Wrestling Alliance. He thought about those feuds, and drew absolutely no comfort from them. He drew no solace as even though each of those battles were brutal, exhausting and career defining, none of them felt as real as what he had been through this past month with Khaled, let alone what he was about to go through in his No Holds Barred match at Total Meltdown. There were no jokes and none of the standard practical jokes to prove his point as the feud reached a crescendo. Instead, it was just two men who hated each other, two men who wanted to step into the squared circle on Wednesday and take each other apart until there was nothing left to give. How can I dream about fighting Ember when I may not even make it there in one piece? sighed Mal to himself. At that moment, a nurse made her way into the room and settled at the base of Somers’ bed, looking over his chart. She ticked a couple of boxes with the blue pen that she procured from behind her ear and then began to open a small jar that she was carrying. “What’s in that? asked Somers. “Just some standard painkillers, love, something to take away the pain.” Mal shook his head, “No thanks, I’ll be fine as I am.” The nurse frowned with surprise and then went on her way, leaving Mal all alone once again. He put his right hand behind his head and felt the spot where Khaled had assailed him the night before. As he ran his finger along the seeping wound, Mal knew it wasn’t going to be good to be Khaled for much longer. That was the only medication he needed to dull away the pain.
