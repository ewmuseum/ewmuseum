The Long Road

11 Feb 2003

By Xcorp

Item 3 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 11 Feb 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Scene – Outside Calgary General Hospital. Dave “The Extreme Machine” Harley stands at the kerb, waiting for something. He is healing quite well from the brutal Xcorp vs. Cell match at Rage In The Cage VI. He is holding a travel bag in one hand and in the other, he is talking on his cell phone.) Harley – “Jill! What time did you say Mal would be here to pick me up? Yeah that’s right, 9.30 am. Well its quarter to ten and he is not here yet! It’s BLOODY COLD out here!” (Several ambulance sirens can be heard in the background. Soon after, the strains of “Damage Inc.” by Metallica can be heard as well.) Harley – “Yes I have been taking my vitamins. YES I have been eating properl...Hold on Jill. I think that’s him now. I’ll see you tomorrow in Ottowa. Bye." (Harley presses the red button on his phone to end the call. He looks to his right and sees an unusual sight. The black Extreme Mobile has pulled up the long driveway to the hospital doing around 8 miles an hour, and a convoy of five ambulances are trailing behind it, sirens wailing. After what seems like an eternity the Mobile pulls into the parking bay next to Harley as the ambulances roar past to the emergency section honking horns and screaming abuse. Mal turns down the radio.) Somers – “Whats up Harley? Man, there was heaps of traffic on the roads today, sorry I’m a bit late to pick you up. Everytime I looked behind me it was backed up for miles.” (Harley rolls his eyes and eases himself into the passenger side, throwing his travel bag into the custom dumpster afixed to the vehicle.) Harley – “Well better late than never I guess. Nice of you to pick me up in style but how are we getting to Ottawa for Fiesta?” Somers – (reversing out) “You’re sitting in it my man! That way we can discuss strategy, sing songs and play “I-spy”. We’ve got a lot of bonding to do if we are going to start functioning as a team properly.” Harley – “MAL! That’s over 1700 miles! You idiot!” Somers – (driving down street) “No way! Isn’t Canada smaller than New York City?” (Harley redials Jill on his cell phone and organises a flight to Ottawa and transportation for the Extreme Mobile while Somers struggles to comprehend the size of Canada.) Harley – (hanging up) “All sorted. We are off to Fiesta! WOOHOO! Heaps to do as well. We have to show that Cruz who’s boss and start laying the CRAXXT down on those Cell morons.” Somers – “I cant believe Ottawa is that far away. I honestly thought Calgary and Ottawa were adjoning suburbs. You learn something new everyday.” Harley – (laughing) “Don’t let Damian Ryan hear you talk like that...” Somers – “Damian Who? Isnt there a wrestler called Damian 666?” Harley – “Damian Ryan. He is one of the four new Rookies who will be competing against each other at Fiesta, sort of a Rookie Showcase. Theres also the Fish man Hein Bloeck, Ender and That Other Guy” Somers - "Which other guy?" Harley - "Focus on the road Mal." Somers – (smiling) “Four new Rookies?! I imagine they will all want to make an impact. Actually I remember one of my first matches here in the WWA where I 450 splashed you off a ladder through a table wrapped in barbed wire and got the pin!” Harley – (sarcastically) “I thought you got half way up and realised you were scared of heights and fell off?” Somers – “Uhhhhh...NO! I did not fall off! Hmmmm this talk about Rookies has got me thinking. How are they going to mix with the WWA Rookie Champ when Cruz wont even let us wrestle on Fiesta?” Harley – “Beats me. Thats why we are going there, to kick a$$ and take names.” Somers – “Pass me the phone buddy, I’m going to call in a favour.” (The ExtremeMobile trundles down the left lane of the highway, on the way to the airport as the scene fades to black)
