One Last Chance

22 Jan 2003

By Denrol

Item 2 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 22 Jan 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Scene opens in a hospital. Denrol is sitting alone in a waiting room. He seems to be staring off into space and doesn't see the camera at first. He is dressed in a gray sweat suit that looks like he's been wearing it two days or more. His hair is loose and a complete mess. He looks over to the camera.) Denrol: What do you want? (The cameraman does not respond. Really, why would a cameraman talk? How stupid is that?) Denrol: You're here to get my reaction to what happened on Meltdown, right? I figured. Well I'll give you my reaction. (Denrol stands up and glares into the camera.) Denrol: I've seen a lot of low moves in wrestling. Jackson Kraven, you crossed a line on Monday. That line is one that you cannot walk back across. But I will give you a final chance. I know what you did; you did in the heat of the moment. You did not think of what pain it would cause you. So I have a deal for you. I am willing to step back from the edge. Before Meltdown, I was ready to forgive you Kraven. I was willing to forget about my hatred for you. What you did was way out of line, but I am still willing to give you one last chance to save yourself. All you have to do Kraven is apologize. You just have to say you are sorry. I don't care if you're sorry for what you did to me; I want to know you are sorry for what you did to Alexandra, an innocent woman. Do this and I will forgive you. Our match at Rage in the Cage will just be under normal rules, in a normal cage match. I won't cross a line of my own. If you don't Kraven, than may God have mercy on your soul. Actually he won't, because you will be damned to Hell. Not just any Hell but my own personal version of hell. If you don't apologize than I will enforce my right to decide the stipulations as outlined in the contract. Our match will be the first ever Cell in a Frozen Hell Match. And I will fulfill my promise Kraven. (Denrol grins evilly.) Denrol: I hate you Kraven. My hate will destroy you. (Denrol starts laughing as the scenes fades to black.)
