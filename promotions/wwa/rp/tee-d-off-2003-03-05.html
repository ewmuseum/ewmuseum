Tee'd off

5 Mar 2003

By X Corp

Item 1 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 5 Mar 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The scene opens on a golf course. Walking towards us in the distance are two men. Both are dressed in all the golf gear, including hats and gloves. Each carries a golf bag over his shoulder. One seems to be walking stiffly. He is wearing sunglasses and his long brown hair is tied back beneath his hat. In his free hand he holds a club and is swinging it violently in front of him in practice mode. This is The Extreme Machine Dave Harley. The man with him – X Corp team-mate Mal Somers – ducks each time the club is swung his way.] Somers – C’mon Harley, watch where you’re waving that thing! You’re gonna take my eye out! Harley – Relax Chairboy. I’m just practicing my swingin’ arm! Somers – Well don’t! Geez, I came golfing to relax, not be attacked by my own tag team partner! Harley – Think of it as training for your match with Leviathan this week. Think fast! [Harley swings his club and smacks Mal in the gut with it.] Somers – OWW! What the hell are you doing?!! That really hurt! Mal takes his bag off his shoulder, pulls out a sand-wedge and clubs Dave in the back with it.] Harley – AAAAAAAARRRGGHHHH!!! Somers – Oh yeah…um, sorry bout that…So you gonna be at Fiesta with me this week? After all, where there’s Leviathan, Lockdown is never too far away. Harley – I dunno Chairboy. It all depends on how the scans look at the Doctors. And clubbing me with golf sticks aren’t gonna help that! Somers – Well it stopped you swinging that club around didn’t it? Harley – Oh yeah, you’re right. Good work buddy! [Dave and Mal have almost reached the camera now. They stop and start setting up for the shot. Mal sets up first. He places his ball on the tee and selects a club.] Harley – So how’s Jack’s training sessions going? Somers – The guy’s out of his mind! It’s okay for you, lying at home with those two blondes of his! Harley – Hey, it’s hard work being the Extreme Machine! [Harley’s mobile phone rings. He drops his clubs and reaches into his pocket, pulls it out and answers it.] Harley – Hello? Hey Jill, where are you? What? I can’t hear you you’re breaking up. You’re where? Hello? Damn technology! Somers – Maybe you should change your name to ‘the Extremely Against Machines’ Dave Harley! Harley – Just shut up and take your shot. [Mal swings and his golf ball soars through the air. The camera follows it as it makes its way down towards the green…and the figure walking over it.] Somers – It’s Jill! [The ball bounces off Jill’s head and lands in the rough. Jill collapses to the floor.] Harley – Unlucky Chairboy! Somers – Maybe we should go and get help. Harley – Are you kidding? And let me miss my shot? Ten bucks says I can get it in her mouth in one. Somers - …you’re on. [Fade out.]
