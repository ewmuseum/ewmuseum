Latter To Jackson, Part 3 "Kevin"

8 Jan 2003

By A.N.Onymous

Item 2 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 8 Jan 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Dear Jackson, Still no responses. I should have guessed. Too busy beating up innocent children, and harmless women to worry about some pathetic loner who has to make his point in the form of writing a letter. But at least I can write a letter, you stupid f**k. You can claim I'm a pussy. I can claim that only cowards become bullies. You can claim I could hide beind these letters forever. I can claim you could hide behind your "Reputation" forever. You can claim a lot of things. You can claim you ended my career. But you claim you ended a lot of careers. One thing you cant claim though.... Is satisfaction. The Dictionary term for "Satisfaction states this. sat·is·fac·tion ( P ) Pronunciation Key (sts-fkshn) n. The fulfillment or gratification of a desire, need, or appetite. Tell me what you have fulfilled? Your lifelong dream is to at least equal Randy Crosses four time WWA champion record. Your one purpose all throughout your career in the WWA has been that. That is your desire. That is your appetite. That is your NEED. But you got sloppy Kraven. Dumb, and sloppy. You let your passion, hatred and fear get in the way of your dream. Some people say it was your destiny to win a fourth title reign. Your hatred towards others has cost you the World Title limelight. Its banished you away from the scene, and only now is it coming back into light. A number 1 contenders match on Meltdown. Your chance. Your shot. Your opportunity. Against Wes Sanders. A man I can relate to. Pushed quickly to the top by a great technique and a niche with a mic like no other. A man with skill. A man you hate. You may not know who I am yet Kraven, but you will soon enough. But for now, you can call me "Kevin". Sincerely, A.N. Onymous. "Kevin"
