Maybe he needs a dream therapist

25 Feb 2003

By Grunge

Item 3 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 25 Feb 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
FADE IN (Grunge is sitting in his chair listening to music.) All my life I’ve been slow and senseless not struck dumb, I’m just dumb, that's all I can give you the constellations lay down here and we'll count them all Madeline Madeline Madeline call me back when the war is over call me back when your boyfriend's gone I’m aware of your oscillations don't believe I’m the only one slave to the inside light my world is burning on eternally for the fire I lack this flame is feeling fine Madeline Madeline Madeline (Grunge throws down the headphones and looks at the camera. He looks a bit upset.) Grunge- Madeline by Mike Doughty. I just woke up from a strange dream. I still lived in the apartments that I currently live in. however; Joey K was a maintenance man here. He was busy cleaning and repainting my foyer. As he did this, I walked around my apartment carrying a dog around like a baby. (Grunge pauses for dramatic effect and then continues.) Grunge-There was a flash back sequence of me carrying the dog around like a baby when she was small enough to fit into my hand. (Grunge scratches his head, even he thinks this is strange.) Grunge-Julie called me and told me that I had better enjoy my vacuum while I still had it, then hung up the phone...which was quite menacing, as I remember. I sat my dog down in the foyer and Joey, who was working with his own vacuum, looked up at me. He kicked the vacuum to the side and asked, "What's the radio code for retreating from enemy fire?" Grunge-I tried to remember. As I was thinking, I noticed that I heard gunfire from an apartment building just northeast of this one. The parking lot was made of Oklahoma red clay. No pavement, no curbs, no grass or shrubbery. Just the red clay. As I listened to the firefight the doors of the apartment complex swung open and people began walking out, as well as jumping out of the windows. (Another dramatic pause.) Grunge-It was an Asian street gang. (Grunge begins to look around nervously.) Grunge-The Asian street gang consisted mainly of females except for two being Venny Letrav and Nick Adams. A few of which were dressed in shining lavender robes. The rest were dressed in lavender business dresses...they were dressed like Mary Kay saleswomen. As they walked across the red clay ground, they lit cherry bombs and firecrackers and started throwing them everywhere. Grunge-They threw some into my foyer, which I promptly threw back. The Asian Mary Kay street gang continued their onslaught as they passed. Adams and LeTrav from the gang stopped in front of my building. Adams lit a cherry bomb and decided to throw it. LeTrav knocked it out of his hand, stomped on it and said; "no!" he then walked into my foyer to pick up a random m-80, which had been tossed toward my Joey K and I. (Grunge sighs before continuing.) Grunge-As he left, I said, "you should really stop this nonsense." to which he, replied, "You know, I should really hit you!" I asked, "Why don't you?" and he said, "Because I don’t feel like it." then he turned to walk away as my alarm went off, drawing me out of this dream. (Grunge rubs his chin.) Grunge-Maybe its about Meltdown. Maybe I need to go see a therapist. The music is fading out. FADE OUT
