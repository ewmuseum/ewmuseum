WTF

7 Mar 2003

By Ian Tense

Item 4 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 7 Mar 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"Can you feel it...?" "That coldness, that terror?" "That moment of clarity you suddenly experience when you realise it's about to come to an end?" (I guess there should be some description of what is going on.) What the *beep* is this? (But has Ian ever been one to play by the rules? Just *beep*ing pay attention to what he is saying) Damien, you'll never cease to amaze me. Never. Your 'winning streak' over me is just as 'paper' as you might want everyone to think I am. (I mean, that is what is REALLY important, right?) Think about it. First it took a distraction to keep me from giving you permenant brain damage. Next it took TWO of you to make me tap...AND it took me being in the Kashmission before you'd even THOUGHT you had enough balls to try something like that. Yet you still brag. My, my, my. Do tell. I'll tell YOU this one last time. Listen carefully, because it TRULY is something that everyone else already knows. You are NOT and never will BE in my league. DO you seriously believe you CAN or WILL take this title away from me? Is your head THAT far up your ass? Of all the people that could take this away from me, do you REALLY think it is going to be you? There is no chance in HELL! First, you're the pure symbol of EVERYTHING wrong with this business...self-promoting, egotistical, and delusional. You've built yourself up to this...this..'thing', this 'person', this 'character' that you want so desperately to be, yet time and time again you forget, in your most private moments, you forget. You lose grip with what you really are. It's funny if you really think about it... You are EXACTLY what you claim I am. A coward. Why else would you surround yourself with the likes of Jackson Kraven and Havoc? Randy Cross and the Minnesota Wrecking Crew? Dorian? Enigma? Why? Because deep down you KNOW you can NEVER carry the load by yourself. And deep down you know the ONLY way to keep yourself on top is to keep THOSE 'partners' of yours underneath your thumb. You know you DO NOT have what it takes to be tops in the business. You NEVER have, and you NEVER will. You're a cancer, Damien. An ugly malingant cancer. So continue to inflate your over-grown ego. Keep believing EVERYTHING you're telling yourself. Keep thinking you 'own' me. It'll only be THAT much more fun when I shatter EVERYTHING you've ever believed in. It'll be THAT much more fun when you realize EXACTLY how far away from the top you actually are. It'll be that much more fun when you break. I won't have to 'kill' you Damien. It won't be anything like that. I'll walk away from you, and you'll LET me. You'll let me because after it all ends, you WILL know that everything I've EVER said about being one step in front of you is 100%, completly TRUE. You'll understand that as 'bad' as you're inflated ego might want you to believe you are, and as 'paper' as you claim me to be, I'll ALWAYS be RIGHT where YOU want to be. You want to prove you belong in the same ring as me? You want to prove you belong on the same portion of the *beep*ing card as me? You want to prove you have what it takes be the WWA World Heavyweight Champion? You'll have you're chance, "mongo." You'll have you're chance, and I hope and prey you make the most out of it because there WON'T be a 2nd chance, Damien. Not because I'm going to duck you as you are going to bitch and moan about, oh no, but because when you step in the ring with me when it is all on the line... You're going to find out EXACTLY how good I am. You're going to find out that behind all the little charades, and all the little acts, gimmicks, and scripts, that I am EVERYTHING you wish you were. So get your 'whiney-bitch' face ready. You'll be needing it. (DIE to black)
