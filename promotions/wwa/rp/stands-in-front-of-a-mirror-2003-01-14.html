Stands in front of a Mirror

14 Jan 2003

By Leviathan

Item 2 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 14 Jan 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Leviathan stands in front of a mirror. Rage is simmering in his eyes. The scars from Mal Somers and Dave Harley’s attack from Fiesta show signs of healing. Stitches can be seen on his forehead. The tap can be heard running.) Leviathan : Malcolm, I thank both David and yourself. As does my associate, Lockdown. As your actions at Fiesta have shown that the stakes need to be raised. You have shown yourself to be a worthy opponent for the Beast. But Malcolm, can you run with the Beast. Can you trade blow for blow with the Beast. Because Malcolm, the next blow may be the last. (Leviathan scoops water and rinses his face. He repeats the action again but the rage still burns in his eyes. He grabs a towel and pads his face. Then stares directly into the mirror.) Leviathan : Water to most people Malcolm is used as a cleanser. Cleanse wounds, douse fires and even wash away their sins. Myself, it is a fuel. As look to the drain and see my own blood merging with the water, a reminder of your attack. A reminder, that there is a job to do. (Leviathan stands and the mirror now shows his massive frame.) Leviathan : Those actions are the last comfort you shall feel. The Beast is ready. Ready to be unleashed. And Malcolm, there is nothing in yours or David’s power to stop it. Nowhere to run, nowhere to hide and no one to help. Come to Fiesta, stage your sit it and see what will happen. The plan is coming along nicely. (A knock at the door.) Voice : You ready, Cruz wants a talk. (Leviathan puts on his shirt, on the back is the words “YOU’RE IN TOO DEEP”. Leviathan opens the door and outside stands his partner in “THE CELL”, Lockdown.) Lockdown : Seems, things are heating up. (Lockdown takes a long inhale of his cigar) Leviathan : How so? Lockdown : Somers and Harley are threatening a sit in. And the Riddler has given us a warning. Leviathan : Riddler?? Lockdown : Enigma. Leviathan : One problem at a time. First, it’s time we put Somers and Harley on blocks like the “Extreme Mobile”. Lockdown : That is what I thinking.
