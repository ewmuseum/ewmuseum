5 March 2006 OK, so I stole it from other forums. That doesn't mean I'm not a WWA Innovator! So step up, peons. Dave Harley will enlighten you. ---- 5 March 2006 My pet rabbit was called Harley, are you guys related? If he was I'm sorry, your cousin died of 'natural causes' while I was on a school trip in 1985, my dad never liked my rabbit... :( ---- 5 March 2006 You have been with the WWA for quite a while now, and have seen many changes, so can you answer a few things for me? 1/ What are some of the most memorable things that you have seen? 2/ What was it like for you to overcome the odds and become WWA champ? 3/ What is it that keeps you there, why haven't you branched out into the PTC further? 4/ How does the WWA of today compare to when you first started there? - Rick James (former PWW press officer) ---- 6 March 2006 Who is the toughest opponent you've ever faced? My pet rabbit was called Harley, are you guys related? If he was I'm sorry, your cousin died of 'natural causes' while I was on a school trip in 1985, my dad never liked my rabbit... :(
No, we are not related. Although I was once quite 'friendly' with your Mom.
1/ What are some of the most memorable things that you have seen? 2/ What was it like for you to overcome the odds and become WWA champ? 3/ What is it that keeps you there, why haven't you branched out into the PTC further? 4/ How does the WWA of today compare to when you first started there? - Rick James (former PWW press officer)
1) My most memorable moment was sending Ian Tense packing, making me new Devil's Own leader and then that very next moment beating the living hell out of my old partner Mal Somers. He'd had that coming for a long time. I was X Corp and he sought to replace me. And where is he now? ...The Main Event of Rage In The Cage?! Damnit! 2) I didn't have to overcome the odds, just overcome second-rate Champion Mal Somers. That was a walk in the park. 3) PTC provide the Extreme Machine with very little in terms of competition. I know I could beat you all with one hand tied behind my back! And besides, would you really want my new business partner Damian Thorne involved in PTC? I don't think so... 4) The problem is not with the WWA. The problem is with the WWA fans. I gave them everything I had to offer. And where did I end up? In a rehab clinic, under constant surveillance to prevent me from ending my life. But they wanted me back in the WWA. Why? To cheer for me? To make me feel wanted again? Hell no. They're bloodsuckers. I hate the WWA fans. In the coming weeks, they'll learn to respect a very different Dave Harley.
Who is the toughest opponent you've ever faced? Who is your most hated and bitter rival? Who is the one person you respect more than anyone else since you've been part of WWA? Outside of yourself who do you feel is the greatest WWA superstar of all time?
My toughest opponent was Leviathan. He ended up becoming my bodyguard. We wrestled a 60 minute Extreme Iron Man Match which was brutal. Not only that, but my most notorious match was the Burning Hell In The Cell. Leviathan was one of my opponents there, too. My most hated rival? Mal Somers. After feuding in the EWA, we developed a mutual respect which turned to friendship in the WWA. We formed the greatest tag team anyone had ever seen - X Corp. But when my back was turned, Mal replaced me and ended up winning the World Title. But I took it from him, putting a restraining order on him preventing him from touching me. When the restraining order expired, we squared off in a Cage. I almost crippled him that night. It's a shame I didn't try harder. The one person I respect? Damian Thorne. He brought me out of my recent bout of depression and taught me to be me again. Together we'll turn this company on its head. The greatest WWA superstar of all time? Ian Tense. He was the architect of Devil's Own. But it took true greatness to use devil's Own to gain control of the WWA. That was me. ---- 6 March 2006
