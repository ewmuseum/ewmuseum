Finding your way

4 May 2006

By "The Almighty" Damian Thorne

Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Why do you hide? To the watching public, the fans, even most of the WWA locker room, you’re just some idiot in a mask. I know different. I’ve told you before, I understand who you are. It wasn’t difficult to work out. Your mask lets you do things you couldn’t normally do. People who know you, would talk, would judge. The mask takes all of that away, right? For instance, an individual in clown makeup, is allowed in most societies to behave in ways that would otherwise be deemed inappropriate or offensive. This is certainly entertaining to the viewers, but it also highlights and presumably conveys something about the boundaries of what is appropriate in that particular society. We all know, masks are a form of disguise. It is an object that is frequently worn over or in front of the face to hide the identity of a person and by its own features to establish another being. This essential characteristic of hiding and revealing personalities or moods is common to all masks. Many say that it also a way of a person to live with ill feelings, such as guilt, or grief. False faces Untruths Deceit Lies At the brawl, EVERYONE gets to understand you, like I do Ember. They will all see, the truths, the reality. They all see that behind the mask, behind the character, is just a scared little orphan boy that nobody wanted. A sad little freak, hiding his insecurities, his guilt, his grief, his LIES, behind some painted lycra. Mommy didn’t love you? Well boo f**king hoo Of course, the mask, in studies, is nothing without the costume, is it Ember? With the costume, you complete the new identity. Surprised? Don’t be. If you even bothered to do any digging about me, you’d know I’m a well read man. I know all about the symbolism of masks, as vessels to house spirits, to allow or imbue the wearer with new powers. They say that when the mask is worn, it is a channel to the spirit that inhabits it, thus protecting the wearer from harm But let me shatter your comfortable little image Ember. That lycra you wear on your face, only protects your pride. It won’t protect you from me. Nothing, will protect you from me. I know you’ve lost your way Ember, so let me show you I know you’ve worn that mask so long that you can’t remember who you are, so let me show you I know you think you’re a god, some kind of transient being, but the truth is plain. The wwa viewers are right. The other wrestlers are right. You’re just an idiot in a mask. So either come to the ring as Ember, the spirit of the mask. Or come as (censored by Mr Ember’s legal team), the man under the mask. I don’t care. Just come prepared to be unveiled, to be excorcised of your demon. Come prepared to have both of your personnas torn apart, at the hands of The Almighty You tried to bring chaos to the WWA? Well here I am Every bad thing, that you ever thought you were, is here, in me. Dress up time is over, Ember. It’s time to come back to reality As with all things that have no answers in this life Ember, turn to the Lord, turn to me. The Almighty, will set you free Designed By [name withheld] | © World Wrestling Alliance 1997 - 2005
