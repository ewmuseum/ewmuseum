The Beginning

31 Jan 2006

By Dave Harley

Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
BRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRR … BRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRR He's not there… BRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRR He must have moved on. BRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRR Damnit, I knew this was a dumb idea, shoulda spend the dime calling my family. BRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRR One more ring, then I'm out… BRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRR *CLICK* He picked up. He picked up. Voice: (thick with sleep or drunkenness) He...[cough] Hello? Harley: Hey. It's, um...Dave. Dave Harley Voice: Dave Who? Listen Mister, I don't want whatever it is you're selling.....wait...did you say, Dave Harley? Like THE Dave Harley? Harley: I'm in this hospital. Well, it's rehab, actually. I didn't know who else to call… Voice: Last time I checked, I wasn't your mother. Well, should I feel honoured that the big cheese of WWA has called on little old "cast out in the cold and left to rot" me? Well forgive me if I'm not shooting sparks outta my ass right now. Whoop-dee-f**kin sh*t, you're in rehab. You always were a drunk....just like me [chuckles] Harley: Shut up and listen, OK? Cos I had to steal this quarter off a guy who thinks he's Elvis and he's too good an impersonator to steal from! Voice: Uhuh-huh. Harley: I may have been the 'big cheese' of the WWA, but that was months ago. I lost my control, I lost my belt and Devil's Own walked out. I had nothing. I...tried to take my own life. That's how far off the f*cking rails I've gone and I need help. I need to get out of this damn hospital and back on the WWA roster, before it's too late. Voice: And this has what exactly to do with me? And why the hell should I help you? You ran out on me after running me into the ground, you and your cronies. A lot of what has happened to me since then could be attributed to you and your kin. WHY THE HELL SHOULD I HELP YOU???? You got 10 seconds before I slam this phone down and forgot I ever heard your name. One. Two. Three. Harley: WAIT! Voice: You better make it good Harley. Harley: Listen, I've got nobody to turn to. You know I burnt my bridges with Mal and Chance. Who else am I supposed to call? I need to get some motivation back and the only person who I can think of to help me is...well...your guy. I know it sounds crazy, but where is he? Even if you won't help me, at least give me his number, or something. Voice: .......you don't want any of that, Dave. Harley: Don't tell me what I want! I want to be out of here and anybody who can help me do that is alright in my book! Voice: Listen, you're in a dark place right now, and you possibly think you can't get any lower. But Dave...what you're asking is.....insane. You’re asking me to give you keys to the underworld, man. You think its dark now? You go after this, it gets darker. So dark you won't be able to see anymore. So dark you won't be able to tell right from wrong, friend from foe. Even if I could get you the info you’re asking of me, in your present state you’re not ready, plus, you'd have to somehow get out of the hospital. What you got, some big dumb Indian who's gonna throw a washbasin island through the window for you? Harley: I'll get out of here, don't worry about that. You think a guy who cheated his way to a seven month World Title reign can't get out of a hospital ward? Tell me where he is. I need this. Voice: Listen Dave, I've tracked him where I can. Some of the threads lead nowhere; others lead to places he might have been. Always there was no-one to find when I got there. It's like he's vanished, become some kind of urban legend. In every town was a different story and a different description. In every town, evidence of his work. From what I can gather, there is quite an underground fighting movement going on all over the states........and he's like their poster boy. He's the hushed name on their lips, he's the story parents frighten their kids with at night to make em behave and play nice. He's like Keyser f**king Soze! You still want this? Harley: Where does the trail end? Voice: That’s just it Dave, it doesn't end. I often feel like the watched and not the watcher, so I try to not pry too much. Up to a certain point, we were still "affiliated". You know the deal, you saw what happened. Since then, there is nothing of that man left. Tread carefully Dave, don't make too much noise or you could end up back in the hospital...or the morgue. I know he might pop his head out for Ian Tense's funeral, so my best guess would be to start searching in whatever town Tense's funeral was held in. Trust me, you'll find a trail. Harley: OK, thanks. I guess I'll either find him or he'll find me. Either way, I'm on my way back to the WWA. Voice: No Dave, you're on your way to hell my friend. And you're on your own. *CLICK* Designed By [name withheld] | © World Wrestling Alliance 1997 - 2005
