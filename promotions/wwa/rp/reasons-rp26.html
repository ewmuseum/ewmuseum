Reasons

18 May 2003

By Chance

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"Why? Why is it the human condition to always search for reasons?" I ask this to myself as I stare into a camera, in a darkened room. A darkened hotel room, devoid of light, life, and more importantly ... devoid of Lucy. There's a reason for this, although I doubt even the person watching the video will need to know. Atmosphere is always important in these promos, any guy on the roster should be able to work that out. "Why do people keep asking me, don't they understand? Don't you understand?" I feel a kind of bemusement, that no one understands my actions. Some think they do, but none really do. The smart ones are the ones who don't pretend or think they understand anyone. Especially women, no one should pretend they understand that, even if women say there isn't much to understand. I feel pangs of nervousness in the pit of my stomach at the thought of how people will react to me. The new me, the person people are just beginning to see. "Sometimes I think it would be easier to be like Joey or Enigma. Hatred is easy to understand, as is insanity really, something so opposed to the confused mess of reality and humanity is always easy to comprehend, simply because it defies comprehension. When a person is pure evil, it's easy to know why they act as they do. And if I were like them, you would all understand why I act like I do. How hard is it to understand this equation? Enigma hates me. Enigma is dark and evil. Enigma wishes to destroy me and all that I stand form, so he gets his 'friend' Joey K (who is also dark and evil) to try and run me down in a truck, and thereby end my career." Hatred bubbles up inside me, sweet and bitter at the same time, I feel a loathing of Enigma and Joey, and for the first time I embrace the hatred, letting the oily avalanche of liquid, icy fire rain down over me, surrounding me, drowning me, infecting and corrupting me. The hatred bubbles further and grows, encompassing Dave Harley, who is just as dark as Enigma, even if he doesn't realise it, even if I'm the only one who realises it. "So why don't people understand my actions towards Dave and Mal? Because they're too human. When actions, even morally reprehensible actions, are motivated by compassion, by a desire to help, no one will really understand. But what you have to understand is this, I'm doing this to help Mal, I want you all to understand that." I know that I need to understand this too, I need to keep it in my mind, because it will justify what I do, what I have to do. It will make it less destestable when I have to resort to lower means to acomplish my goal. Or goals, Enigma and Joey are just as important as Mal and Dave. "So when you think about me, think about my actions and my reasons, remember this ... I'm the good guy" I have to believe that, because if I don't, I'm no better than Enigma, no better than Joey, no better than Dave Harley, the shadow in disguise.
