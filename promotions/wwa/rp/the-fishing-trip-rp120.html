The Fishing Trip

10 Jun 2003

By Leviathan

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The blue horizon is pierced by a white beacon. A luxurious 50ft single hull yacht, anchored in the calm waters. The closer the approach the name becomes clearer, S.S.INCARNATE. Sitting on the back deck is two muscular figures, one relaxed in a deck chair, who is surveying the ocean but always watching his fishing reel to his right. The other, a more angry man, stands ready with a shotgun to unload on his prey. They are Lockdown and Leviathan, members of the Devil’s Own.) Leviathan: You do realize, if you use that we won’t be able to eat the fish. Lockdown: Who gives a F***. I hate fish, present company accepted (Lockdown laughs to himself.) Lockdown: Now remind me, why the F*** are we here. I mean out in the middle of F***ing nowhere. Lucky you brought all the comforts or I would have to use this on you. (Lockdown taps the shotgun.) Leviathan: I don’t doubt that. As for why we are here. Serenity, I felt we needed the rest and looking at you the recuperation. Plus, I think better in the calmness of the sea. Lockdown: Okay, but did you have to bring him along. Leviathan: Tommy fits into our plans. And he will spend most of his time working on his tan or the girls. (The fishing reel starts to move slowly. A few tugs then it speeds through the rhythmic rotation of the reel. Leviathan swiftly grabs it and fights for control.) Leviathan: Lockdown get the net ready and hook. Lockdown: F*** the hook I have this. Leviathan: Don’t shoot the fin, it’s a delicacy Lockdown: Okay, Sh*t you told me that already! (Leviathan fights the fish and his biceps tighten as he gains control of the line inch by inch. Until, finally he dominates the fish on the line. A head lashes out of the sea, teeth gnashing and tail thrashing.) Lockdown: F*** his BIG! Leviathan: Ready! (Lockdown steadies himself when an object flies past him, spearing the shark in the head. Standing on the top deck is Ian Tense holding a spear gun.) Lockdown: F***! Don’t do that a F***ing AGAIN! Leviathan: Nice to see you up Ian. I hope the girls stoke the fires. (Ian smiles at Leviathan.) Ian: Let’s Talk. Fade to black…..
