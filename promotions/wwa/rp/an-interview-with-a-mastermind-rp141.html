An Interview with a Mastermind

13 Jun 2003

By Enigma

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
{The scene opens in the WWA studios. There are two chairs set up that face each other with a coffee table in the middle. There are two coffee cups on each end near the tables. In the background there is a WWA Banner. Sitting in one chair is none other than WWA Interviewer Chris Crenshaw.} Crenshaw: “Hello everyone and welcome to a rare opportunity for myself as here on Insider we will have the first ever sit down interview with the man known as Enigma and the current head of a trio of himself, Joey K and Se7en.” {With that through the door on the ring walks in Enigma. He is wearing a nice black suit. He is wearing a black skin tight shirt underneath his jacket. He has his glasses on and takes a seat across from Crenshaw. He takes his glasses off and looks at Crenshaw with that icy glare} Crenshaw: “It is a pleasure to here with you Enigma…” {Enigma puts his hand up in a stop motion.} Enigma: “Get to your point.” Crenshaw: “Okay, the first question I have is that you have been talking about a tormented one in your recent interviews. A sort of surprise on Monday, would you like to clarify that whole situation?” Enigma: “Interesting query there Christopher. Let me tell you one thing, for you see I am the Mastermind. That means that I will and have been outsmarting people all my life. The real question is, have I been truthful this entire time. It is quite possible that this could all be a ruse. A sort of red herring if you will, to throw everyone off their game and to give me another advantage. But then again Christopher I could be telling the truth and just giving everyone a warning. A warning against the fact that the power shift in the WWA will be complete and towards myself, Se7en and Joey K.” Crenshaw: “Okay, well this brings me to my next question, why did you choose Joey K and Se7en to be apart of your trio?” Enigma: “I will answer that question on Monday.” Crenshaw: “Okay, well what about Monday Night Meltdown. It looks to be a big night for yourself as you face Nick Adams, Dave Harley and the WWA United States Champion Grunge.” Enigma: “Ah yes, my match on Meltdown. It shall be an intriguing contest. You have myself, the Mastermind, the US Champion, a joke and a man who always chokes at the worst times. And interesting group, no? Kenneth, has spouted off long and hard about his intentions and how he thinks all three men will gang up on poor little ole me. But that does not matter to me because I have an Ace in the hole, no pun intended Chance, and there is no way I am going to lose that match. David is nothing more then a putz. He is on Meltdown purely to do something childish or stupid. Nothing more. Nicholas is an intriguing fellow. He knows some karate and such and actually may be able to make some noise in this match, but he will not beat me.” Crenshaw: “Speaking of Monday Night, the man you defeated, with help from Joey K, in a Casino Match at Revolution, Chance will be taking on a returning Wes Sanders in the main event. Do you have any thoughts on that match?” Enigma: “Chance? Sanders? These men do not concern me. Like I have said before I am done with him. He is nothing to me anymore. And why would I care about Wes Sanders, he has not been around in awhile. He is a loser, another man who could never get the job done. Both men are irrelevant to me.” Crenshaw: “Fair enough, now what about this coming Fiesta! Where Joey K will defend his WWA Television Title against Mal Somers and Se7en will take on the former GenX Champion and the man that beat you at Rage in the Cage, Jimmi Zion.” {Enigma seems a little annoyed at Crenshaw’s mention of Zion’s victory at Rage in the Cage} Enigma: “First of all, Zion did not beat me. I made sure Chance did not win the match, I let him have that match, but that is all in the past. At Fiesta, Zion will meet his match in the monster known as Se7en. Zion may be able to fly and I will give him credit for that, but Se7en will use him as a javelin and make him fly further than ever. As for Mal Somers, he actually thinks he can take the WWA Television Title from Joey K? I do not think so. Mal may have been a former Tag Team Champion, but he cannot beat K. He will be the champion after the night is over.” Crenshaw: “Alright, do you have any final thoughts?” Enigma: “Nothing Christopher except remember.” NOTHING CAN STOP ME NOW {Fade to ?}
