The Barriers are Down

12 Jan 2003

By Chance

Item 1 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 12 Jan 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
-There are too sides to everything. To every situation, every opinion, every little thing that is said, and every little thing that is heard. Just as there are two sides to what we are seeing now. No, I mean literally the image is split down the middle into two sides. The first of the brightest, most blinding, white light. The other of the blackest, most impenetrable darkness. The finest of lines down the centre separates the white from the black, the light from the darkness. For a few moments nothing happens, nothing seems to change. But suddenly, yet slowly, two shapes come into being, one within the light, the other within the dark. The light is not darker, nor the dark more light, but nevertheless the figures have faded into view. The man who stands in darkness we can discern nothing about, but the man in the light, has one recognisable feature. A silver coin, that glitters in the brilliant light. Suddenly the man in the light speaks out, and we recognise his voice as that of Chance, joint Cruiserweight and Extreme Champion- Chance: “I walk in the light, which hides my sins from all, for no one suspects that one who walks in the light such as I do, could have any sins to hide.” -Another voice rings out, almost in answer to the first, we just recognise this as the voice of Jarod Crow- Jarod: “Whereas I was born, was raised, and have lived most of my life in the darkness, and all who see me believe me to hide my sins, and shroud them in darkness. Even though I carry the sins of the world on my shoulders this hurts me as all they see is the initial darkness.” -There is a brief pause, and the darkness and light seem to fluctuate slightly, before Chance carries on- Chance: “I seem to all to be pure, unsoiled, and almost purely good, incapable of almost any evil. For they do not believe the environment of light that I live in could have allowed that to happen.” Jarod: “But in my world of darkness no one is pure, no one is good, and everyone is evil to some degree. This is a world of deception and trickery, a world that slowly poisons your soul and your mind. A world of corruption. This is the world that bullies come from, and this is where I fight them.” Chance: “And that is often the difference between two people who may seem similar. Where they choose to fight their battles, whether land or sea, plains or mountains, or darkness or light, the battleground they fight on defines the kind of loser and winner that they become.” -Again the border between the two sides ebbs and flows, changing second by second, before once more settling down- Jarod: “We are both one of the few, one of the chosen. He chosen by Luck, and I chosen by destiny. He to be favoured by Fortune, I to be cursed by the Victims. What caused this change? One simple thing:” Both: “We live in different worlds, of light and dark, shadow and brightness.” Chance: “So come Monday, both I, chosen by Fortune,” Jarod: “And I chosen by the Victims” Chance: “Shall rush to join the battle.” Jarod: “Because the barriers are weakening,” Chance: “And the bullies rush out into the world.” Jarod: “So when the time comes and battle is joined against Enigma and Jimmi Zion,” Chance: “I will fight Enigma for Fortune.” Jarod: “I will fight Enigma for the Victims.” Chance: “I will fight Zion for my pride.” Jarod: “I will fight Zion for it must be done.” Chance: “And so on Meltdown come Monday night …” Both: “The barriers will fall …” -The line between light and dark blurs and is gone, and the light and darkness rush into each other, struggling for supremacy, and for a moment we see Jarod Crow and Chance for the first time, both in wrestling gear- Both: “… And the Battle shall be joined.”
