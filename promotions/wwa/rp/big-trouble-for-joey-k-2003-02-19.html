Big Trouble for Joey K

19 Feb 2003

By Venny LeTrav

Item 1 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 19 Feb 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Scene opens to a solitary stool under an unseen light and illuminates a small black room. The angle of the camera lowers down to the pegs of the stool, where a pair of feet slowly walks into view. The bottom of a pair of black, yellow and orange tear-away pants sweep around the expensive white shoes and soon, the legs step around the stool and the stool shifts, as if someone’s sitting atop it.) Venny: You’re in big trouble, Joey K… I trusted you and you betrayed my trust. Why couldn’t you just be a man and deal with your sorry wrestling skills in a more productive way? When I lost the match, I went to the hospital, got fixed up, and then me and my wife watched Sleepless in Seattle… But no… you didn’t want to deal with your problems like a man… you had to attack. (The camera slowly starts to move upwards, very slowly as Venny speaks.) Venny: I’m sorry you didn’t do better in your match against Jimmi… I really wish that I‘d been in the helping mood… too bad I just wanted to get you back. You attacked me in front of my fans. You put me on the mat, destroying our trust as friends and a former tag team. Well, this time, I’m gonna let you really so who the f*ck you’re dealing with… (The camera reaches his knees, where his hands are clasped.) Venny: After the match, after dropping you and plastering you to the mat… it was good to feel like a man and not just half of a team. The American Luchadores are dead, but I’m not. I won’t be beaten. I can’t be beaten. I’m one mean son of a gun… (Camera reaches his chest) Venny: And for you, Joe, I’m gonna make this special. I’m gonna make you see how much sh*t you’ve just tracked in your door. The attacks aren’t going to stop, the interferences will get worse… until you and I have it out. Finish it off. Because when I get you in that ring, Joey K… (The camera comes to Venny’s face. He’s wearing his yellow, orange and silver facepaint and his red and yellow cat’s eye contacts, smiling like a child at Christmas.) Venny: It’s gonna be Big Trouble for Joey K!! (He begins to laugh softly, then as the camera fades out, the laughing gets intense… maniacal.)
