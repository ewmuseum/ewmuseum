Gettin the files

8 Mar 2003

By Brothers DeLucci

Item 1 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 8 Mar 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
..::The scene opens up to Tommy DeLucci looking and snooping around WWA headquarters. Even though some people recognize him and allow him access to the WWA wrestler files, he still hides under his sunglasses and hat to cover his “known” identity. He opens up various filing cabinets and pulls out files looking through each individual one::.. Tommy: Come’s on there’s got to be something’s about these guy’s ..::He then looks at a particular file and his eyes widen, he grins with delight like a schoolboy getting a grade A, he pulls out the file slowly which have the words Showtime written upon them. He beings flicking through the papers::.. Tommy: Let’s see what we got’s here ..::He looks through each paper carefully reading every possible document his eyes lower as he notices a few things::.. Tommy: Huh? These document’s are doctored! Nothing is known about Showtime before his WWA days? No notes, no previous employers, nothing. All we’s gots is a mysterious fire and there pops out Showtime. Something’s not right here! ..::Tommy scratches his chin again as he continues looking through the documents he turns the page carefully looking at training regimes and documents. Nothing seems to be of use though as the crisp pieces of paper give no more information than Tommy already knew::.. Tommy: What’s the hell is up’s with this! First nothing’s is known about this guy and he’s got no training schedule what is this guy a nobody? ..::Tommy places the file back in the filing cabinet with no regard for care. He just shoves it in and continues searching through some more documents he then finds a James Curzon file. He places the file on top of the cabinet and continues to read through::.. Tommy: Englishmen. Bah’s I hate’s the Englishmen! Why doesn’t he’s goes back to his own land and fight’s there. ..::At that moment Tommy felt a vibration in his pocket, he pulls out his mobile phone and answers it::.. Tommy: DeLucci here’s, uh huh? ..::Tommy pauses as the other voice talks he continues to flick through the papers as he does so he stop::.. Tommy: Boss’s package? But’s we delivered that the other week? I personally gave it to him’s myself. ..::Tommy begins to walk off whilst holding onto the Curzon file::.. Tommy: Look’s I’ll go to the lair now and I’s sort this problem out ok? Right *Click* Tommy: Right’s I got’s the file I need on James Curzon, Tommy DeLucci is ready for Fiesta ..::With this comment Tommy leaves the WWA building still under his shades and hat and looks on as he breaks through the doors and into the sea of passing bodies::..
