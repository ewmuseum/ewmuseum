Family Affairs Part 1

30 Jun 2003

By Wes Sanders

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene opens up to a darkened gymnasium, a single light shines down on a sweating Wes Sanders who is doing some sit ups. The wooden flooring on which Wes lays looks warm and inviting. Yet the sparse lack of equipment make the room look bigger and colder. He skin glistens in the light as he pulls his body up. His hair has a certain wet look to it and hangs over his eyes. All of a sudden Wes lays flat on the floor.) “It’s only a matter of time now Wallace” (Wallace Coleman squats next to Wes and hands him a towel. Wes sits up and looks back up at Wallace.) “Training is the key to victory, a well trained body chiseled to perfection, that is only fifty percent of the problem solved. The other fifty percent comes from the mind set you take upon entering the ring.” (Wes stands up on his fee whilst Wallace remains silent as he sets up the next equipment. Wes dries his hair a little.) “When I approach the ring this Sunday my mindset will be clear. Eliminate and protect, eliminate the rookies who will try and fail. And protect my fellow stable friends from elimination. But this Sunday there is a little more personal pleasure I can take.” (Wallace moves out of shot and comes back with some hefty weights placing them next to the seating.) “This Sunday marks the one year anniversary of myself winning the Fiesta Bowl from my fellow comrade Se7en. It also marks the birth of the new era, the birth of “The Rage” Many watched that day as I beat Barrett Sinclair in a highflying match full of speed and agility. Secondly I defeated Se7en in a hard fought match which made me think and calculate every move.” (Wes lies back on the bench as he picks up the weight, his arms shake slightly as the weight moves up and down in a steady motion. The light begins to swing slightly, and Wes drops the weight down after a rep of ten.) “What is it Wes, are you ok?” (Wallace spoke with a soft tone, as Wes just lay motionless on the bench looking up at the light. Wes blinks after a couple of seconds. Then sits up holding his head.) “You ok dude? You don’t look so good, you look like you’ve just seen a ghost” (Wallace looks concerned and places an hand on Wes’ shoulder. Wes just shrugs it off and shakes his head in a no like fashion.) “I just had an image, just like you said a ghost from the past. He’s looking for me, and he’s close.” “Who? Who is the ghost from the past?” (Wes gets off his ass and begins pacing up and down the wooden flooring. Wes then starts to speak in a strong local dialect.) “Cómo él me encontró?” (Wallace looks on in amazing as Wes just stands there. Deep in his thoughts.) “Who are we talking about here Wes? Is it someone I know?” (Wes remains silent like a statue his thoughts rush through his head.) “Is it Ian Tense? I bet its Ian Tense he’s always been in the way, he used to get on my nerves when I interviewed him. I was never sure what to say to him.” (Wes looks above him and sees a bar hanging above his head. Wes jumps up reaching for the bar and begins to do some chin up’s.) “Plus it couldn’t be a worse time with the rumble coming up. How do you feel your going to proceed if this presence you felt grows stronger during the rumble?” (Wes seemingly ignores Wallace’s’ question still mumbling to himself in Spanish.) “I wouldn’t know what to do if I was in that position, I think I’d be a wreck of emotions, first tension, and suspense. Probably leading on to Anger and Surprise. Who is this ghost again Wes?” “Mi Hermano” (Wes continues to show no signs of stopping as he continues to pull his body up and down on the bar. Gaining more and more speed as he goes.) “What did you say Wes? Me what now?” “Mi... pequeño... hermano” “My what.... Sorry my Spanish is not what it once was” (Wes drops down looking directly into the eyes of Wallace, sweat drips down onto the floor and Wes breathes deeply in and out. He threads his hands through his hair not blinking for a moment.) “Mi... pequeño... hermano” (Wes pauses just for a second just to take another breath in.) “My... Little... Brother...” (With this the screen fades.)
