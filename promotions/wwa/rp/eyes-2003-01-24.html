Eyes

24 Jan 2003

By Ian Tense

Item 4 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 24 Jan 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I have nothing. (Hate rolls of these words like slow death) No wife. No family. No friends. Nothing. (It's so thick you could cut it) And with that nothingness comes more nothings that have been among the most helpful characteristics in my career. (But it's calm, and it's devilishly soothing) No remorse. No concious. Nothing to slow or retard a drive to hurt, maim, and destroy that is second to none. (It raises the hair on the back of your neck) Nothing. (Gives you goose bumps) And I want revenge because of that nothing. But not for the usual things. Oh no. (Leaves you wanting more, yet knowing you've already heard too much) I want revenge for being born. (Born. Born. Born. The words echo like a gunshot. Far enough to hear in the distance, yet close enough to be alarmed) People like me are gifted with a big black hole through their soul. I call it a gift because that hole allows us to do things that normal people simply can't. (And now those gunshot's speaker can be seen.) Kraven kicked a pregnant woman in the stomach, would a Wes Sanders, a Herman Denrol, or a Tracer Bullet be able to do something like that? (He is the former WWA Television champion) Absolutely not. (The former 2-time WWA United States Heavyweight Champion) But the Shane Dorians, Damien Thornes, and Jackson Kravens can. (And the current WWA World Heavyweight Champion) And as I watched Alexandra Korth double over and writh in pain as her unborne child felt the first of many sins of the father, I laughed, then shed tears of sheer joy and appreciation. (He is Ian Tense) It was absolutely beautiful. (His face is calm and even, but his eyes...) Because I UNDERSTOOD what it was that Kraven was doing, and I KNEW where it was coming from. (His eyes are as close to hollow as a human being can come, occassionally shifting as his mouth contorts just enough to let the words slide from his clenched teeth) A big black hole. (His lips tightly purse together immeditely after slipping those four word out. But those eyes...) It be from that very same black hole that will allow me the freedom to tear Wes Sanders to pieces for one full hour in front of wives and children, mothers and fathers, brothers and sisters. (My God, there has never been anything so unsettling) And I will ENJOY doing it. (His lips move, his mouth contorts, his eyebrows furrow and lower, all without notice. Those God-awful eyes continue to distract and steal attention away from every single other action and movement there is) Every second. Every minute. Every punch. Every kick. Every slam, suplex, submission. (They are ALMOST normal. Almost. The errieness is subtle, just enough to let you know something bad is inside. Like the slightly opened door in an empty house.) v All the blood. All the sweat. All the pain. (And calm, they are so goddamned calm.It's not a soothing calm as it first appears to be. No. It's the disturbing calm before the storm moments before the death verdict of an f-5 tornado or a level 5 Hurricane is handed out on network TV to a whole city, region, or state...) EVERY *beep*ing second of it. (When you just know. You just know.) Know what you are getting yourself into, Wes. (Finally there is a sparkle. A slight twinge in the darkness of the eyes) Hell. (SOMETHING that lets you know that those eyes do indeed belong to something human...) 60 minutes of Hell inside a steel cage with a cold, calculating monster. 60 minutes. Nothing more, nothing less. Every single second of it. (Although the words might point otherwise) You've been warned. So live your life Wes. Live it as full as you possibly can between now and then. Enjoy the simple pleasures. The small things. (And those same eyes widen. It's almost like they are excited. Sure, the rest of his face is moving, twisting, smiling, frowning, whatever. But his eyes...) The love of a woman, the laughter of children, and the company of good friends, because after Rage in the Cage, AFTER 60 minutes of hell disquised in an Ironman match... (they don't belong to a human after all. They belong to something MUCH worse) All you'll hear are their shrieks of horror when the force themselves to look at you. (The belong to the closest thing Earth will ever see of teh biblical Devil. A madman. A psychopath.) Know what you're getting yourself into Wes. Know every single horrifying second of it. (Fade)
