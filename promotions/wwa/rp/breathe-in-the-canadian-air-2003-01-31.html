Breathe in the Canadian Air

31 Jan 2003

By Damien Ryan

Item 5 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 31 Jan 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The screen fades into a negative image of former FAW and AW superstar Damien Ryan, a Canadian athlete. It seems to be after a match, for he is breathing heavily and is sweating. The film has been slowed down as he looks across the ring. His long, black curly hair falls down around his head and face. Then screen then slowly fades into Ryan executing different moves to other wrestlers, showing him nailing a moonsault off the top turnbuckle. Then fades him into giving somebody is submission finisher, The Wings of Fire. The screen then fades into Ryan running down the ramp and jumping onto a zamboni and tossing a wrestler who was driving it onto the arena floor. It then shows Ryan standing on the top turnbuckle with his hands up, raising a title belt. We can see his ring attire, blue wrestling pants with two red maple leaves on eachside, red knee pads and boots. Over that image the Damien Ryan, copyrighted logo shows up then the screen fades out.) (The screen opens up in a nice park on the water. It's a beautiful but cold day, the ground is covered with a dust of white snow, and the bare trees shake when a breeze comes along, blowing some snow off the brown branches. There's a sidewalk along the edge of the water and a barrier. We can see a man walking down the sidewalk, dressed heavily to stay warm on the cold day. He's wearing jeans, and a black fleece jacket; also a black winter hat and his long black hair pulled back under it. As the camera zooms in closer, we can see its actually the man from the short promo earlier, Damien Ryan. He stops and faces the water leaning on the barrier. We can see the steam coming from his mouth and nostrils from the coldness in the air. The camera comes in close towards Ryan's left side, and he begins to speak as he stares at the horizon.) Damien Ryan: Sun should be going down sometime soon, probably in the next hour or so. The sunset is such a beautiful thing, especially around this area. How the sun changes the color of the sky, and drops on the water as if its going under to sleep for the night. Turns the sky colors of orange, red, and pruples. Definetly a wonder of the world, like a sacrimental experience or something of the sort. Yet, what most of all I like about the sunset, is it could be used metaphorically if used correctly. For instant, the sun drops, so do wrestlers to the mat. The sun rises, like talent. The sun is a star, like us, we are all superstars. But there's nothing like a Canadian sunset, how you could just sit here and be so zoned out by the brightness of such a gigantic mass. It's mesmerizing. Reminds me of when I step into the ring for matches, how the feeling gets to you performing in front of thousands of people, your music playing, the fans cheering...yet again, it's mesmerizing. (He leans back off the barrier and begins walking down the sidewalk again. The camera moves along with him, with Ryan's fontside face the camera, as if the cameraman was walking backwards.) Damien Ryan: Wrestling is life to some people, and some people it means more. You say, well it can't be more, you live for something, that's it. Well for me, yes, wrestling is life, but there more to the wrestling then just stepping into a ring and fighting somebody. There more to the game then just hitting the 1,2,3; you walk to down a rampway with thousands of people chanting your name, or even telling you that you suck, whatever, its the reaction you want to recieve. For me, I want people to realize that this is not drama. Wrestling isn't a fight over a couple because someone slept with their best friend. This is a sport that you take seriously. (the screens fades out, then fades into a gym. It has a light color to it, kind of dark but with some sunshine peeking through the ceiling windows. In the gym is some weight lifting equipment, a wrestling ring in the middle, and mirrors all over the place. It fades in and out of Damien Ryan to some training and you hear his voice in the background) Damien Ryan: I have gone through countless training, different federations, from indies to top promotions. Defeated many, lost some. Wrestling is not a game of which you can put your money on the line, or where you set high stipulations of stupid acts for the future. No, it's about setting goals, and creating a master character that you can show in front of millions of viewers, that you will start from the bottom of the list and work your way to number one. From starting at a low paying job to becoming a man of success, one of the most famous men in the world because you have achieved the highest prize in the business. (the screen then shows Damien Ryan putting moves on trainers inside the ring in that gym, and him getting some tips from the trainers. The trainers were actual wrestlers themselves who retired from the game, and now are training new, young, talent. Still Ryan's voice in the background) Damien Ryan: Hours and hours of endless training, because there's no slacking. This job is your life, and your life is 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. When your not performing in front of people, your working hard to make yourself better to improve what you did wrong that night before. (the screen then fades into an arena full of thousands of people at some wrestling event in Montreal, Canada, and shows Damien Ryan at the top of the ramp after his match waving the Canadian flag and fans are going absolutly crazy) Damien Ryan: I owe my country a great debt of gratitue for where I am now. I have been a champion in other organization, and have made my neame clearly known around the wrestling world. Yet, I wouldn't have all this if I didn't come from the greatest country on the face of this planet. I'm waving the Candian flag because it is a symbol of my life, my talent, and my personality. It is a warning to people, that when they step the line to me, that they are crossing the border of Canada. I will represent Canada is all ways. Damien Ryan is Canada. (the screen fades back to Damien Ryan walking along the shore on that sidewalk and he looks at the camera) Damien Ryan: I'm ready to embark into WWA, its the question of is WWA ready to embark into Damien Ryan. (the screen fades out and shows the Damien Ryan logo)
