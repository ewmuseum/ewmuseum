Down Home Cookin’

28 Feb 2003

By Venny LeTrav

Item 3 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 28 Feb 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Once again, we find ourselves at the doorsteps of the LeTrav family. The doors open slowly and the camera stalks into the large and comfy-looking house. Voices are heard from the kitchen as the camera gets closer and we see a visibly pregnant Karine Malbrough, with her husband Venny with his arms around her stomach, kissing her cheek. Karine is chopping carrots and celery as a pot of boiling water is on the oven.) Karine: Baby… would you let me cook already? Venny: Nope. Karine: Do you wanna eat or not, Remy? Venny: Depends on what you mean. (Karine grins and turns her head, kissing her husband sweetly on the lips. She whispers something to him and he grins at her, kissing her lips again. As he turns around, letting go of his wife, he walks to the fridge and gets a soda out of it, popping it open, he sips from it deeply. Karine keeps chopping veggies as Venny sits on the bartop of the pleasant kitchen.) Karine: Baby, don’t you have a match Monday? Venny: Do I? Karine: Yeah, against Joey K and Grunge. Venny: Oh, that match. Yeah, I’m tagging with Nick Adams. Karine: Who? Venny: Nick Adams. Great guy from what I hear. Karine: What’s he think of you? Venny: Damned if I know. Probably thinks I’ve got a screw loose like everyone else. Karine: Well, you DO act like Martin Riggs on speed. Venny: Who? Karine: You know, that guy that Mel Gibson plays in Lethal Weapon. Venny: Oh, him. Yeah, you would know that. Karine: Mel… how I love thee, let me count the ways. Venny: Yeah, yeah, yeah. Women… (Karine turns around, giving him a defiant look. She points the large knife threateningly at him.) Karine: And all the times I’ve seen you drooling over those old Baywatch shows you had taped? Jesus, boy. Grow the hell up. (Venny looks a little hurt, but he sips his soda all the same. Karine goes back to chopping and the silence is thick for a second, but not for long.) Karine: Are you coming home after the next few shows? Venny: I’m gonna try to. Karine: Don’t give me that, Remy. Yes, or no? Venny: Yes… Karine: …good. (Venny walks up behind his wife and wraps his arms around her, resting his head on her shoulder and closing his eyes softly. The once pleasant demeanor of the couple now seems diminished.) Venny: You worried about our kid? Karine: Yeah… Venny: Don’t worry about him- - Karine: Her. Venny:…her. If he…she’s got our blood in her, she’s a tough cookie to crack. Karine: …I’m still a little scared. I want her poppa here. (She turns and looks Venny directly in the eyes. They stay fixed on each other for a solid second before she presses her forehead to his and kisses him gently. Venny closes his eyes and kisses back slowly) Venny: I love you, wife. Karine: I love you, husband. (Karine starts to sniffle a bit, then lays her head on her husband’s shoulder. She drops the knife to the floor and wraps her arms around Venny’s neck. He holds her close and the camera pans around. Karine may be trembling in her loving husband’s arms, but Venny’s eyes stare straight-ahead. His gaze is troubled, angry even. He closes his eyes slowly, then opens them again.) Venny: Nothin’s taking my child away from me. Nothin. (FADE TO BLACK)
