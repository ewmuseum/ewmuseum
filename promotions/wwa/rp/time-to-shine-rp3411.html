WWA (1997–2019) Roleplays

Time To Shine

22 Dec 2006

By "The Deity of Wrestling" Fatality

Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Fear… (The scene opens up once again to Fatality sitting in a dark, dingy room. He’s sitting in a chair, holding his head in his hands. He looks up into the camera and has that “I’m better than you” look on his face.) They say fear is the greatest form of intimidation. Fear, the feeling or condition of being afraid compels all of us. In different forms, in different ways, we all fear something. Some of us believe that the only thing to fear is fear itself. Others feel that fear is your only God. As far as I go, fear is for the futile. Fear is for men who are weak enough to accept it. Come Birthday Bash, all of my opponents should be filled with fear. You see, they all have to fear the man who has nothing to lose. I have nothing left to lose. There is nothing left in my life except for wrestling. The one thing in my life which makes me feel invincible. That’s why come this Sunday; I will be prepared to take any steps necessary to win. Now, it pertains to vision that most of my Bash opponents have been making candid comments on WWA-Online.com about me, and the other combatants. So, why don’t I stop sitting here and taking the insults and instead give them a piece of my own intelligent mind. (Fatality takes out a clipboard, reading off the names of his opponents in the Birthday Brawl) Rawhead – Well, if you took your partners name and your name, you’d get the Boneheads. I think that’s quite fitting, don’t you? Apologies, I forgot about your condition, you glorified caveman. Let me slow it down for you. Rawhead…be…stupid. I think he understood that. But, how could you possibly be concentrated on the Birthday Brawl? Your partner is competing in the Fatal Four Way for the Tag Team Championships. And, if you guys are anything like I think you are, you’ve probably got some kind of interference planned for that match. With that being said, I will not consider you a threat in the Birthday Brawl. Jevon White – Yeah, I forgot the Mr. Wait, I know what you’re thinking. Who in the sweet blueberry hell am I? To be blunt, I’m your worst nightmare. I’m the guy that’s going to destroy you in the Birthday Brawl. And much like the Boneheads, you couldn’t possibly be able to concentrate on the Birthday Brawl. Not if you want to retain your Tag Team Championships anyway. Ry Ballard – Ah yes, the self-proclaimed One Man Show. Well, come Christmas Eve, I’m about to steal the show. And whether it’s Tarin, Ty or even Tinkerbell Ballard in the Fatal Four Way, it won’t have any effect on the Birthday Brawl. For the outcome is inevitable and that is another victory for The Deity of Wrestling. All it takes is one…and it sure as hell isn’t you. Pyro – Pyro, Pyro, Pyro. The World’s Tallest Midget. Honestly, I could take this little simpleton down with a sneeze. Personally, I would’ve preferred if your partner Dragon was in the Brawl, for I take great pride in being able to piss people off. This Sunday Pyro, you won’t be able to beat me and you will not be able to set me on fire. The only thing in flames come Christmas Eve will be your career. Armaan – The current World Champion. The man who overcame all of the odds at Day of The Dead inside Hell in a Cell. Kudos and I suggest you enjoy that World Title reign because it simply won’t be a very long one. The World Championship has always changed hands in the Birthday Brawl and this year will be no different. You see, I think Hell in a Cell did some permanent damage to your already inferior brain. You haven’t been heard from ever since you won the World Title. And after what I do to you at the Birthday Bash, you’ll never be heard from again. Richard Cranium – You got one thing right. Come Sunday, it will be time to fight. And from the looks of things, it seems that you are going for the home run, looking for every title up for grabs. After all, who isn’t? I love to be the bearer of bad news, so let me be the one to tell you that your dreams will be shattered. You will not be physically able to win any titles or contenderships in the Brawl. Mentally, you aren’t even there either. Your mind is somewhere else, thinking about weddings. You simply won’t be ready, and that will be your downfall. Jamal Diggs – Where do I start? Do I criticize his wrestling ability, his credibility or the fact that he is currently rotting in a jail cell, and he’s the God damn Television Champion? See, this is the exact reason why many of our fans are so pro-violence. They see you representing the company and figure that if it’s OK for you to go to jail, then it’s OK for them to be so bloodthirsty! Hell, you might not even make it to the Bash. Either way, I’m going to destroy you. I’m either going to tear you limb from limb in the Brawl or make you watch as I tear your own brother limb from limb in the Brawl. One way or the other, I’m going to make you suffer. Then, I’m going to take your TV Title. Crush – Ah, Crush. Wait a minute, Crush? Who the hell is he? Well, whoever you are, whatever you do, you better hope to God that New York’s Paramedics are on the top of their game come Sunday. AJ Locke – The future Golden Boy of WWA. I can see it now. You are going to be the guy who will be the face of this company in a few years down the line. Not if I have anything to do with it. Not if I am the one opposite you in the ring at the Birthday Brawl. If you had any ideas of becoming World Champion, then you will find out why I am called The Deity of Wrestling. Christian Heights – If there ever was a man who was literally “Homicidal, Genocidal and Suicidal”, then it’s you. And I don’t mean your wrestling ability. And if you thought me vs. Pyro was going to be David vs. Goliath, take a look at Christian Heights. I’m gonna squash you like the bug you are. This Sunday, I’m going to finish the job that you started. Nemesis – Finally, I can pick on someone my own size! I was beginning to wonder if I was cursed with having to fight dwarfs’ everyday. It’s a shame that one of the only big men has next to no ability. You talk about how you owe people your life etc. Well, you owe me something too. You owe me my time back. For, I’ve just spent the past minute ridiculing you. Give me that time back at the Brawl by lying down for me, how does that sound? Kash – The three year veteran in WWA. This Sunday, I’m going to do something that no-one else has ever done before. I’m going to eliminate Kash. Not just from the Brawl, but quite possibly from the WWA. You see, Kash has never faced a force like I before. Kash has never been in the ring with a man like me. Kash has never experienced…ultraviolence. He can have his squabble with Armaan but when it comes down to it, he will have to be worrying about me, not Armaan. Kade Williams – The man everyone has been talking about. You’re right, Williams, you don’t know me. And maybe that is for the best. You don’t want to know me. You’ve got a whole lot of hype surrounding you. Don’t believe the hype, Williams. Don’t fall into the trap. Because the minute that you start to think that you are the greatest thing that’s happened to this company, BAM! Reality just slapped you in the face. Personally, I hope I do meet you up on the WWATron, because if I do, the only impact you’ll be making will be against the steel ramp 25ft below. Tommy Starr – There’s a name I haven’t heard much of in a while. And after Birthday Bash, don’t expect to hear much more of. This one goes out to all of the Starr Gazers out there. I’m gonna take your man Starr, and I’m going to make sure that he never gets the opportunity to wear the World Title. Nigel Dawkins – The Boy Wonder himself. Want to know the difference between you and me? You’re still just a boy, whereas I have become a Deity. I’ll show you up in front of the whole world, and by the way, it isn’t just girls who wear make-up. Clearly – Now here’s one of the only guys in this business that I respect. And, if the opportunity presents itself, I will form an alliance with him. But, when it comes down to the two of us, I will not hesitate to break his neck. Sure, he’s my friend but this is wrestling. We both understand that we have to do what we have to do. It’s just business, nothing personal. We know each other inside and out, so I wouldn’t be surprised if it came down to us two again. But I want to speak to Clearly here, because I know you’re watching. Remember when you hit me with the best move you could dish out, and I kicked out? Well, just wanted to make sure you remembered. Jack Griffiths – I got a fair bit of backlash from the WWA Fans and Kade Williams for calling Griffiths a has-been. I said this because that’s how I viewed Griffiths. But, after looking closer I’ve come to see what he really is. Jack Griffiths is a…washed-up, good for nothing has-been who never managed to win the big one. That’s right, I said it. In fact, I don’t know if I can call him a has-been when in fact, he never was! Chris MacBeth – Just when I thought they couldn’t get any smaller! I’ve eaten bigger steaks than you, kid. But, I’m being a little hasty because Chris MacBeth is the possessor of one of the most destructive finishers in the business. The Sunset Flip. I mean, if he hits me with that I know for a fact that it’s ove- (Fatality bursts out laughing, wiping tears from his eyes) I’m sorry, I’m sorry. I just couldn’t make the Sunset Flip sound like a move I’m supposed to be scared of. If I were you, son, I’d use my size to my advantage. That is, to run like hell! Belial Khan – Ah, from one side of the spectrum to the other. The biggest current competitor on the WWA Roster. He lacks one thing though. Desire. He lacks the passion to become World Champion. That’s one of the things that have made me so successful. The sheer drive to push myself to another limit. I’ll use that same drive to come out victorious in the Brawl. Maximus Victorious – OK, this is where I draw the line. Romans? Seriously, come on. From what I hear, Maximus refuses to use a weapon. He refuses to do anything high-risk and he refuses to go outside the ring. That’s all fine and dandy in a regular contest, but this isn’t any ordinary match. This is the Birthday Brawl. And to win the World Title, you have to go outside the ring, climb the WWATron, then climb a ladder on top of the WWATron and retrieve the World Title. I don’t like Max’s chances in this one. Danny Vista – Ah, the former Television Champion. He’s coming into the Birthday Brawl looking to gain that championship back among other things. Well, I’d be fine with someone looking for the Television Championship if it wasn’t for one thing. He vacated the championship in the first place! Honestly, give some other people a chance! Zachariel – The New Age Messiah, leader of the New Age Army. A fine person, if somewhat a bit delusional. That and the fact that it will take a whole army to slow me down on Sunday Night. At least he has his fans… James Whitefield – Speaking of the New Age Army. Well, he may be good looking, charming, confident and intelligent, but you’re also a cocky son of a b****. I can’t trust you as far as I can throw you. Unsurprisingly, I’m expecting you and Zachariel to form a team, trying to eliminate as many people as possible. Try anything funny on me, and I swear to God you will feel the Fatal Enigma. The Rainmaker – What can I say? Clockwork Orange, great read. Anyway, as I said earlier with Jack Griffiths, I have been on the receiving end of a lot of hate mail from the WWA Fans on calling the Rainmaker washed up. Hell, I may as well say “See Jack Griffiths” The Butler – Oh boy, am I going to enjoy this one. After years of searching, Butler will be reunited with Mr. Darkness, and what does he get? Dismantled in front of his master! In front of millions for viewers live on Pay-Per-View! God, I can’t wait. Santa Claus – Just when I thought that destroying the Butler wasn’t going to be fun enough, along comes Santa. As a kid living on the Streets of New York, I never got any presents at Christmas. Years later, I get the opportunity to take out all the anger, all the fury I had every morning of December 25th. I’ll force Madison Square Garden to watch as I make Santa spill copious amounts of blood, to match his coat. Goody! Mr. Darkness – And so we reach the bottom of the barrel. While, it may be an early Christmas present for the WWA fans…and the Butler to see Mr. Darkness return to the ring, I will make sure to rain on that parade. In fact, I’ll make sure that everyone watching will have a Black Christmas. Unleash the ultraviolence. (Fatality stares a hole through the camera, having made his point clear…) Designed By [name withheld] | © World Wrestling Alliance 1997 - 2005
