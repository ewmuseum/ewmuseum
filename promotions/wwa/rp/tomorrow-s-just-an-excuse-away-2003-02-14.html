Tomorrow's just an excuse away...

14 Feb 2003

By Grunge

Item 1 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 14 Feb 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
FADE IN I could be your stumbleine, I could be your super-queen. (Grunge is sitting at home in his large armchair. He invites the camerman to come closer as he hits stop on his CD player.) Grunge: Smashing Pumpkins Thirty-Three. So what does this growing boy eat for lunch? (Grunge pulls out a cracker box and fiddles around in it. He pulls some out and pops a few in his mouth.) Grunge: Why, PB&J with Wheat Thins, of course. I had a very decent night last night. I also concocted a plan to go to Vegas, Baby. Julie and I would then go, crash several weddings and start our own buisness; a drive-thru wedding chapel with an all you can eat wedding cake buffet. That's truly America! (Grunge chuckles to himself thinking its fairly funny.) Grunge: Except that in a handful of days I have to face Nick Adams for the right to wear my TV title. So no go. Instead Julie and I stayed home. We had quite a good evening. We played guitar, and I heard some lovely songs that she had composed. I'm quite jealous of her talent, but also quite intrigued by it. A nice balance which is the key to a soild marrige I'm told. (Grunge hits play on the CD player again.) Grunge: All in all a good way to unwind before a match. See ya fans and you Nick. (The Smashing Pumpkins begins to play again.) i've journeyed here and there and back again but in the same old haunts i still find my friends mysteries not ready to reveal sympathies not ready to return i'll make the effort, love can last forever graceful swans of never topple to the earth FADE OUT
