That's it?

6 Feb 2003

By Nicholas Adams

Item 9 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 6 Feb 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
FADE IN (Nicholas Adams is shown in his basement office. Usually, wrestlers don't have offices in the WWA Towers...but it was sort of done as a prank to Mary Beckham. Luckily for Nick, nobody really needed the office, and they already mailed him security passes, so he has his own office. And luckily for him, so few people actually come down to the basement unless he calls them down that he loses very little heat. At the moment, he's hanging up some pictures in his office. One is of the most recent collective WWA picture, which includes Nick. It has Barrett Sinclair circled with the word "Gone" above it. Christyan Payne's face is crossed out in red ink. Another picture is actually a dart board with Commissioner Cruz's face on it. We then see him hang up the final picture, which is below a plaque that says "Current Target". Oddly enough, it has a picture of the WWA Television title. Nick then turns towards the camera.) Adams: You know, I originally thought that it was a temporary office for me, and then I would just get rid of it. Instead, Matt Warr said that he was cool with me having an office if I was. So, here I am, two months later, and I still have Mary Beckham's old office...obviously renovated, of course. But that isn't what I called you down for. I called you down to have a little speech...more like a...actually, I won't rant. (Nick goes back to his desk and sits down. He then leans back and places his feet on the desk.) Adams: Grunge, thanks for giving me my proper respect. Still, Shaman is a tough and twisted man. I think even you should recognize that. As for Christyan Payne, the loser is gone. As for Shaman...see you at Meltdown. (Nick then moves his legs off and then puts his hands on his desk.) Adams: Shaman, I'm coming for you. You can't stop the weather...and you can't stop...The Rainmaker. (Lightening bolt effects flash across the screen, and then the scene does a...) FADE OUT
