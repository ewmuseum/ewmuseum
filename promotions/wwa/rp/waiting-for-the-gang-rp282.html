Waiting for the gang

23 Jul 2003

By Matt Keith

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
INT. DINER, LOS ANGELES- DAY [Chuck’s diner, not to be confused with the Chuckster, it’s your average everyday eatery however some how it looks a lot like the diner on that hit show about nothing we all use to watch in the nineties, Seinfield. It’s pretty much pact at this point and time of day, the cameraman pans and gets a shot of the clock; it’s 12:05am, lunchtime. Panning back to the entrance we see WWA superstar Matt Keith enter, alone, he walks past several tables until he reaches a booth. Some clown tries to beat Matt to the seat and sit down but he gets pushed to the side for his trouble. Seeing Matt have a seat an extremely hot waitress walks up to the table to take his order.] Waitress: What will it be? [Matt looks up her and smirks] Matt Keith: Coffee. [She writes the order on her tablet] Waitress: That all for you? Matt Keith: Yeah. [She put her pencil behind here ear and walks off. Matt Keith: [Thinking out loud] Where are these jokers? [He sticks his hand into his pocket and takes his cell phone out, then lays it down on the table. The camera zooms in as Matt passes the time by speaking to a certain WWA wrestler, Alex Knight.] Matt Keith: So it's become a situation now, has it? Alex Knight likes to talk big, he tries everything he can, attacks every possible point he can think of to try and get me riled up. What's going to anger him even more than usual is the fact that it's not working. Why? Because Alex Knight lacks intimidation. I sat and watched your promo Alex and I decided that the easiest way to show you exactly how wrong you are about...well...everything is to just go to Fiesta and a cave that mistake of nature you call a face in. [Matt glances down at his phone then looks back into the camera] Matt Keith: Yes Alex, I came into The World Wrestling Alliance with a "holier-than-thou" attitude. Why? Because I can afford to, because I the future of the industry. I did more in my first year of wrestling than most wrestlers can dream of doing in an entire lifetime. I’ve went to federations and gone strait to getting high profile title matches in only a few weeks time because I was unstoppable. I defeated some of the biggest names in wrestling history, I defied the odds and spat in the fans face while doing it, I came out on top, I became a star. If you want to sit there and get ideas in your head that you’re the next big thing you better surpass me first Knight because I’m the measuring stick around here jerky, I’m the rookie that other rookies wish they could be like. [The waitress brings Matt’s coffee and sits it down in front of him, Matt continues speak] Matt Keith: But what really gets to me is that after I make my big splash in the EWA and now WWA by knocking a few heads together and reminding the fans what real entertainment is, I get put into a match against some poor fool. That’s all you are Knight, a poor fool crying out for attention, screaming please look at me. Pathetic! I’ll give you all the credit in the world Knight you did get past Lynn at Truth or Consequences, but what happened in the Elimination Chamber? Don’t think I didn’t see, you ran around that ring avoiding any confrontation like a winy little b*tch. Is that the way a man that seems to have such a high opinion of his abilities act? Look at yourself, even when you were cutting your little train wreck called a promo you were shaking like a wet dog. Again, that doesn’t seem like a guy that’s ready to be in the ring with Matt Keith. [Matt reaches over and puts some sugar and cream into his coffee] Matt Keith: Now, now, now, don’t take it the wrong way it’s not like I’m not looking forward to Fiesta, I am. I’m looking forward to stepping into that ring; stand face to face with you, and in front of all your pea brain fans beat you until you bleed. Monday that’s my joy, that’s how I’m going to get my quick fix for the night. [He blows his coffee and takes several sips of it.] Matt Keith: The point of the matter Alex is that no matter what you have in your mind when you enter that ring, I will defeated you one, two, three, it’s as simple as that. If you do not have the mental capacity to deal with the facts Knight then maybe you should go back to kindergarten where you got your insults. The WWA is not the place where you win matches by sheer hope and empty talk, this is the big show junior. The magic elf of destiny isn’t going to use his magic wand to help you win the match, truth be told, when you step foot into the ring you belong to me. The talks going to be over Mr. Knight, it’s just going to be me, you, and the moron fans screaming their lungs out for you to pull out a miracle victory, it will become put up or shut up time. Can you handle it? I know I can. [He takes a few more sips of his coffee] Matt Keith: And you know, thinking about it I think the entire WWA Fiesta roster needs to be put on notice. Boys I want you to take a long hard look at me, this is the face of the rookie legend, the real show around here, I’m the center of attention, I am the whole damn show. And guess what? It seems that my arrival into the WWA is just in time, there has been a roster split and with this split has come a new title, the Intercontinental title. Needless to say my sights are on this title and I want everyone to keep in mind, I always get what I want. [Here they come, in walks Danny and Dantes Morgan, late. They see Matt and walks over to his booth, then sits down.] Matt Keith: Took you long enough. Dantes: We’re late because of that Paul Davis guy was at the garage starting all kinds of s*it with his friend. Danny: He was looking for you, it looks like he’s still pissed from that ass whipping we gave him. Matt Keith: So he just left? That doesn’t sound like Paul. Danny: Nope, we had to gang up on those motherf**kers. They backed down when they seen it was so many of us. Matt Keith: This is getting old. Dantes: Hell yeah! Matt Keith: He starts it and when I finished it he wants to start some more crap with me. The only other person more dense is Alex Knight. [Laughs] Danny: What do you have in mind? Matt Keith: Nothing yet, my minds on more important things, mainly the WWA. But I’ll think something up. As for now I want to stay focused on Monday. Dantes: That reminds me; didn’t you tell us to come up here? Something about Monday night Fiesta. Matt Keith: Yeah, you see I have this job I want you two to take care of Monday. And by take care of I mean I want someone educated if you get what I’m saying. Danny: Who? [Before Matt can say a word his phone rings, he picks it up] Matt Keith: Yeah. [A devious smirk forms on Matt’s face] Matt Keith: It’s ready…okay…no, no…I’ll pick it up. Okay thank you. [He closes the phone] Danny: What was that about? Matt Keith: Lets just call it entertainment. [Danny and Dantes and looking at each other as if saying “What the hell”?] FADE TO BLACK
