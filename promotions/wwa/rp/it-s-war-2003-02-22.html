It's War

22 Feb 2003

By EVD

Item 1 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 22 Feb 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The camera opens up to reveal two loose piles of playing cards, and two neat stacks of the same. A hand from the opposite side of the table tosses out a seven of clubs. What is apparently the cameraman's own hand tosses out a five of diamonds.) Voice: Mine... (The hand from across the table reaches out and picks the cards up. As it pulls back, the camera pulls up to reveal that he is playing this game with Eddie van Dorn.) EVD: Hi everyone. Perhaps you've been wondering where I've been lately. Quite frankly, I can't remember much of the past couple months. Took Lily home to Christmas dinner at my parents' house, and we had a good time. Then came New Year's Eve... and that's where it gets quite a bit hazy. Last I remember, Lily and I were racing our car across time zones so we could celebrate the New Year over and over again. We ended up somewhere over the Mexican border, found a guy selling... (Eddie appears to think better of mentioning what this guy was selling.) EVD: ... well anyway, long story short, I kind of lost track of time after that. Ended up coming back very recently, and it wasn't long after that that Wes Sanders walked up to me and said, "Hey, how'd you like to fight a bunch of people at once?" Or something like that. Sometimes it's hard to remember what Wes has been talking about. Well of course, he was talking about the Global Warfare match coming up Monday night. I naturally accepted... I wanted to get back into the swing of things as soon as possible after my extended absence, and what better way than something like this? Cameraman: You have had a match since your last one in the WWA, though. (The cameraman tosses out a knave of spades.) EVD: Yes, but I'd really rather not talk about that one. I'd prefer focusing on the future than dwelling on the past. And this Monday night, it's all about... (Eddie turns over a knave of hearts and puts it next to the cameraman's card.) EVD: ... war. (The cameraman and Eddie lay three cards face down and Eddie motions for him to wait before turning the next card face up.) EVD: Wait up. I have to talk first. You can probably already see where I'm going with this, but I don't particularly care, it's going to be said anyway. You see, this setup is exactly like the one I'm facing Monday night. (Eddie holds up the next card, its back to the camera.) EVD: Four cards to a side in a war. Four men to a team in Global Warfare. Yet in both setups, one card can make all the difference... (Eddie tosses the card, a four of spades, onto the three face-down cards.) EVD: And despite what it may look like... (The cameraman turns over a three of clubs.) EVD: ... it may be one of the smallest cards in the deck that does it. (Eddie and the cameraman reveal their overturned cards. The cameraman's hidden cards were an ace of hearts, a king of spades, and a king of diamonds.) EVD: One card, no matter how small, may be able to take out some of the biggest cards in the deck just like that... by proper placement and strategy. And in this match, I just happen to be one of the smallest cards in the deck. And quite frankly... (Eddie looks right into the camera as he gathers up his booty. They seem to have been playing quite a while by now, as the cameraman has but two cards left to his hand.) EVD: ... I do indeed plan on taking out some rather big cards. And some of the biggest ones in the industry are going to be there... Jackson Kraven, Havoc... I'm not going to back down from any of them. For the pride of the southern region, and most importantly, for my own personal pride... (Eddie tosses a queen of hearts onto the table.) EVD: ... I plan on letting those cards fall one by one. (The cameraman lays down a ten of clubs and Eddie puts the two cards into his pile.) EVD: It's as simple as that. Just placement, and strategy. (The cameraman places a king of clubs onto the table. Eddie looks at the next card to come up from his stack and grins.) EVD: And no matter how big the kings of that ring are... (He tosses the card in a spinning arc through the air, landing next to the king. An ace of spades.) EVD: ... they will always be beaten by a flying ace. (Eddie grins mischieviously as the cameraman begins fading to black.) Cameraman: That was a long way to go for such a horrible joke, you know. EVD: I do. (Fade to black.)
