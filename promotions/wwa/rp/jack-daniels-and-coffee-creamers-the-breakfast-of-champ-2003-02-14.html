Jack Daniels and Coffee Creamers... the Breakfast of Champions

14 Feb 2003

By Tracer & Denrol

Item 7 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 14 Feb 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
DAY 2: THURSDAY, 4:00 PM (The scene opens inside the LeBeaux household. The soon-to-set sun can be seen from a window inside the living room. The cat named Extreme is playing with a ball of yarn as US is bathing himself on a chair. Again, we hear them speak with subtitles.) US: So did you hear the news? Light Heavyweight crapped on Tracer and Missy's bed this morning. (Extreme holds the ball of yarn between his four paws and looks over at US.) Extreme: Pff. That's nothing. Daddy threw up in their laundry hamper this morning. Claimed to be having sympathy morning sickness for mommy. But since it happened at 3 a.m., I'm willing to bet it was that bottle of Jack combined with the twelve packets of coffee creamer he swiped out of Tracer's pantry. US: Hmm, I may regret our little bet. I thought I saw Tracer's eye twitching eariler today. Extreme: You don't think that has anything to do with the way the four of your guys are ransacking the house? You think it's all daddy? US: Like you are innocent. I saw you nearly trip Missy while chasing that damn ball. (Extreme pitches the yarn ball away with his feet, rolls over onto his stomach, and sits up.) Extreme: At least that was unintentional. Your breaking that Kurt Angle plate yesterday, Light Heavyweight dropping a load on their bed, World ironically scuffing Tracer's World title replica with his claws, and TV... wait a minute, where's TV been lately, anyway? US: I think- TV: Oh yeah, it's the KING baby! (Both US and Extreme look up and see that TV has managed to perch himself on the highest shelf of a bookcase.) Extreme: What's he on about? I've been up there, I didn't notice a copy of that breast-happy maniac's biography... US: Like you could read. TV: This is it. It's time for the biggest leap in all of cat history. Watch as I propel myself from this shelf right to that table. (The table in question has a large and somewhat unsteady lamp on it.) US: Uh oh.... Extreme: The biggest leap in all of cat history? He think he's Tony Schiavone or something? US: I think you've been hanging around daddy and Tracer too long, personally... making all these horrible wrestling jokes... TV: Shut up, you fools! Watch as I place myself into the history books. Drum roll please. (Extreme and US shake their heads, but do start slapping their tails against the floor. TV gets ready, swinging his butt back and forth.) TV: WOOOOOOO! (TV leaps off the shelf, nearly knocking it over. He makes it onto the table, but slides right into the lamp, sending it flying off and to the ground where it of course breaks with a loud bang.) US: What an idiot. Extreme: Well, it looks like I'm the only one who hasn't willfully and wantonly destroyed our host's home. I'm proud of that. US: Footsteps. Humans. PLAY DUMB! (The three cats start sniffing the ground, licking themselves, and generally acting like normal cats.) Tracer Bullet (from hallway): Really, is there ANY reason why you thought that JD and coffee creamer would be a good combination? Denrol: I think a better question is why you have coffee creamer packets in your home. You're not suppose to steal them... (The two enter the room.) Denrol: ...you know. TB: I didn't steal them, I actually buy some of my packaged foods from a restaurant supply catalog, and... (Tracer stops short as he sees the broken lamp on the floor, a limping TV walking away from it. He speaks to the cat for some reason.) TB: You know, you're the oldest, you should really know better. Denrol: Ahh, this isn't good. Well listen, cats take awhile to get used to new places you know. TB: I'm not worried about the lamp, actually. Picked that one up for about a buck at a yard sale last year. Been keeping it in the basement. Never used it until you and the cats moved in and I decided I needed to keep the good stuff out of their reach. Never know what possessed me to put it that close to the edge of the table, though. Denrol: Bad decroating design. I know a good girl who can help you with the place. Well how about we clean up this mess and than get to ddoing some real work. Not that assembling that ring wasn't real work you know. But since the girls are out shopping, I think we can get some stuff done. TB: Sure thing... (Flash forward about half an hour. The broken lamp has been cleaned up from the floor, and the floor lamp has been turned on.) TB: Right. Now we were going to do some planning or something. Denrol: Well we have a bad situation here. Chance has not reveled his partners yet. I think we could get the match canceled because of that, but I don't think you want that. So we have to guess who we'll be facing on Monday. (Denrol slides one of his tapes into the VCR. The screen shows the three-way match between Chance, Enigma, and Jimmi Zion from Rage in the Cage.) TB: Right. And since Chance will have decided his partners the same way that villain from Batman chooses everything, we have just as much chance of choosing right as we could possibly have. Denrol: That villain from Batman? TB: Two-Face. Guy flips a coin for everything. Denrol: Ah. Not a Batman fan. More Superman. TB: And interestingly enough, we've got a guy in Showtime that's just as annoying as Mxyzptlk. Do you think if we get Showtime to say his name backwards, he'll go away? Denrol: Emitwohs doesn't sound very deadly to me. So if we really can't guess his partners, what do we do, Mr. Private Dick? (Tracer visibly falls deep in thought.) TB: I honestly don't know. But since judging what we know about Chance leads us to believe that he'll be suggesting his partners at random through that silly coin thing he does, and knowing that fate likes to be cruel to me quite often when it comes to this sort of thing, I'm willing to guess that at least Shane Dorian or Enigma will be in there. Maybe both. Denrol: Possible. Neither man have a match that night. So yes we have to cinsider them possibles. But them both being in the ring wrecks their advantage. TB: Yeah, that's another thing... you wouldn't think anyone would be damn stupid enough to already have a match scheduled that night, and then STILL want to face us and Shaman, would you? Denrol: I don't see anyone on the lineup who hates any of the three of us THAT much. Well Vik Ventressa is booked to be there. So is Kraven. TB: Hmmm... I'm quite frankly not worried about either of them. Vik's been gone for like a year... not like anyone missed him... so he shouldn't have his stride back just yet. And Jackson... well, I'VE never lost to him. And you were able to get through him at Labor Day Brawl. Then again, just imagine if Chance gets both Vik and Jackson... it's like he's forcing a handicap on himself. Denrol: Vik never had a stride. Kraven brings back a topic I've been thinking of talkling to you about. Trent, you're my best friend, but you have really made a mistake. Tracer: *under breath* Letting you stay here for a week? Denrol: Taking on the entire House of Pain was not a good idea. You've been a good guy for awhile, but I've never seen you take on an entire stable of guys. Believe me, I've done it before, and it's an impossible task. At least without backup. TB: Allow me to rephrase the same thing I said on Meltdown... I am not taking on an entire stable. Not all at once. Thorne is the mastermind behind the attack on my wife, and he will pay. But not before the two people who were actually willing to do the harm themselves. I'll deal with Shane and Enigma... and I'll go for Thorne himself. I was never intending to go after everyone at once. Besides, it could be worse... I could be trying to take on two heel stables while still a heel myself like Jim Black did last summer. Denrol: What makes you think Thorne and Kraven will leave you alone? The whole point of a stable is when you go after one, you go after them all. I had to make special rules in my match with Kraven to keep his buddies from sticking their noses in it. They will go after you as a whole. TB: Well, quite frankly, my main target is still Dorian. We need to finish what we started. And Damian hates Dorian. He may be working with a HOP member, but that doesn't mean that Thorne's going to be helping. Denrol: Thorne will help. I don't buy this "Dorian Isn't HOP" crap. Shane Dorian is still part of that crew. And the second you think you got Dorian and Enigma handled, the rest of HOP will get you from behind. They don't fight fair. TB: And quite frankly, after Rage in the Cage, I've about had it with fighting fair myself. I've got my plan. It'll work. Trust me. Denrol: Alright. I'm just giving you some advice. I would help you out, but I got enough on my plate and don't need to deal with HOP too. TB: I quite understand, but I don't need your help. Only reason I'm going along with this tag match is because you're a friend. I mean, if you think the three of us are "the franchise players" here, you're welcome to do so. But I can't make teaming up with you and Shaman a recurring thing. After all, you seem to have gone quite off the deep end lately, and Shaman... well, Shaman's just a big psychopath, and he kinda scares me. Denrol: Shaman scares me too. I had to include him as a member of the Franchise because of his time in the WWA, but he really is the oddball here. But Trent, I don't know what you mean by going off the deep end. I don't think I have been thinking this clearly since... well it's been a long time. TB: What you're failing to remember, though, is that we were in the same boat once upon a time. Me more than the two of you, but you have to see my point here. Denrol: You're talking in metahphors and it's giving me a headache. I have stopped plugging my ears and blinding my eyes. These newcomers don't respect us or the WWA. They think they know it all and they are the greatest things since sliced bread. TB: What the hell do you mean, metaphor? What I'm talking about is that at one time, years ago, WE were the new guys coming in and trying to establish ourselves as someone that the fans could get into, love us or hate us. And we succeeded. And why? Because we were able to show that we were talented and deserving. You just have to give these new fellas a chance, seriously. Where would we be if we only let the old, well-established guys make a run of the place? Where WCW was and where WWE's RAW brand is now. Denrol: There is something that we had that these rooks don't have. TB: A half-filled roster to step into? Denrol: No. Respect. Respect for those who came before us. All three us dealt with bullsh*t. We had to pay our dues and work for everything we got. Did we complain? No. Did we whine and bitch? No. We kept our mouths shut and we showed our talent. This generation is one of people who don't want to pay their dues. They expect everything handled to them on a silver platter and that idiot Warr is giving it to them. (Tracer looks like he is about to say something, but then scratches his chin thoughtfully.) TB: You know, you've got a point there... Denrol: All I did to Chance was what he deserved. he wnats to be the best in the WWA, he has to take his bumps and bruises. Bob Holly did it to Matt on Tough Enough and I'm going to do it to Chance as much as needed. TB: Still, though, you can't deny that these other guys are the future of the WWA. And of course, here lately, it looks like people can just meander in here and get things laid out for them a lot quicker than we ever had. But that seems to be the way they're all going these days... what with Brock being the Undisputed Champion basically four months after his television debut. Denrol: I don't see the future. Only the cream of the crop rises to the top. Shaman did it. I did it. You did it. 3 guys lasted, out of hundreds. I look at these kids and I see the lastest batch of rejects, to be thrown away once it gets too tough for them. Now the girls should be home soon and I want to get something wrapped for Valentine's Day, so let's finish watching this tape. But there is one last thing I wnat you to think about. TB: And what's that? Denrol: Three WWA legends will team up for the first time ever on Monday. Yet we are the midcard match. The main event will include a man who won the World Title by falling on it in a match that has nothing to do with wrestling. Than he got to keep it for more than three months, with only two title defenses against a glorifed jobber. That man has no clue what hardship is. The three of us do. Why should we sit back and just let that happen? TB: Duly noted. VERY duly noted. But there's one last thing I want YOU to think about, too. Denrol: That is? TB: What you're going to do about that cat sh*t on my bed. (Denrol nods, sits down, and turns his attention back to the television. Fade to black.)
