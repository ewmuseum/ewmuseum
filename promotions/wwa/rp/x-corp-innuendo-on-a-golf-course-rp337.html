X Corp: Innuendo On a Golf Course

6 Aug 2003

By Dave Harley

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[We see an open field. The position of the Sun indicates that it is sometime after midday. We hear the birds singing in the trees. Suddenly three figures emerge from over a nearby hill. Two of them are strutting along with big grins on their faces. The third figure appears to be a woman in her late fifties. She is wearing a suit and a pair of glasses. she has two golf bags, one slung over each shoulder. She appears to be having some difficulty carrying it all. This is Dave Harley's ever suffering Personal Assistant Jill. We hear her voice as the three figures draw closer.] Jill: Are we nearly finished yet? I'm getting rather tired. Harley: Yeah, that's what they said! WOOO! Somers: Will you stop that Harley! If I hear another gay innuendo I'm gonna kick your ass! Harley: I bet that's not the only thing you'd do to it! WOOO! Somers: For crying out loud! Harley: I bet you were! WOOO! Somers: Shut up! Harley: Relax Chairboy! I'm just preparing you for what you might expect on Monday when we face Homo/Phobic for the PTC Tag Team Titles. You wouldn't want to go in to a match unprepared would you? Somers: I guess you're right [As the trio draw closer to the camera, we can see that Dave Harley and Mal Somers are decked out in golf gear from head to foot. The insignia on their shirts reads 'X Corp'. It is spelt out in Scrabble tiles. Their hair has been tied back underneath their caps. Mal stops and takes a look around.] Somers: OK, this must be the spot. You want to go first or should I? Harley: Maybe you should go first because you'll be a lot quicker! WOOO! [Mal shakes his head and sets up for the shot. With a mighty swing he hits the ball and watches as it sails off into the distance.] Harley: Wow Mal, I didn't know you 'swung' that way! WOOO! Somers: Will you hurry up already? I left Rookie in the Extreme Mobile without leaving a window open! Harley: He'll be fine! There's plenty to do in there. He's got my GenX Title to keep him company. Somers: Pfft. You want Rookie to sit and talk to a belt?! Now that would be really dumb! Harley: [Gasps] Somers: ...what? Harley: I've just had a thought! Somers: .........did it hurt? Harley: Do you reckon Homo/Phobic will get 'homophobic' when they get to the Gaylord Entertainment Centre this Monday? Somers: ...You think of that one all by yourself? That was almost to clever for you! [Harley takes his swing. The ball swerves to the right, landing in the rough.] Somers: Jeez, that was pretty poor that one Davey! Harley: I'd like to see you do any better! Somers: Stand back my GenX Champion compadre. [He places his golf ball on the tee.] Somers: This shot will be as perfect as the ass kickin' Homo/Phobic will recieve on Monday. Harley: It's not the ass kickin' that worries me! WOOO! [Mal swings and his ball shoots at breakneck speed towards the horizon. Suddenly a figure sticks his head up in the distance. It would appear he has been camouflaged in the middle of the field the whole time.] Somers: What the ~ [The golf ball lands squarely on top of the figures head.] Harley & Somers: OOH! [The figure then picks the golf ball off the floor and starts racing towards Mal and Dave.] Harley: Bwaaaahahaha! You're in for it now Chairboy! Somers: Holy crap! It's... [As the figure gets closer, we notice it's Jack Knight aka Bridge, X Corp's personal trainer and ex EWA Television Champion! As he accelerates towards his trainees he hurls the ball. It zooms through the air hitting Mal Somers right between the eyes and knocking him out cold.] Harley: AAAAAHAHAHAHAAAAAA! Bridge: THAT was for missing last nights training! You think you had better things to do or something? Harley: I bet he had better things to do! WOOO! Bridge: No more of this messing around! You two have got to prepare for a PTC Tag Team Title match next week. It's time to start acting like contenders! Mal get up! Somers: Wha~? Bridge: I trained you boys to be WWA Tag Team Champions. At March Madness you brought home the gold and I was proud of you. But on Monday, you're not bringing home the gold for yourselves. You're bringing home the gold for the WWA and all the guys in the back who believe in you. So it's time to get up and get to work. i'm gonna work you boys hard! Harley: Work us hard huh? WOOO! Somers: WOOO! [Bridge just glares back at them.] Harley: wooo? Somers: RUN! [Harley and Somers bolt across the grass as Bridge gives chase, a golf club in each hand. The scene fades out.]
