I'm Still Alive

4 Feb 2003

By Jackson Kraven

Item 2 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 4 Feb 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
DRIP DRIP DRIP (It's blood, of course.) (Was it ever going to be anything else?) (Was anything else ever so thick, so red, so human?) DRIP DRIP DRIP (It drips slowly into a white porcelain sink, the type you'd find in a bathroom anywhere in the world, staining the pristine white a deep crimson.) DRIP DRIP DRIP (We pan up and see where the blood is coming from- the shattered face of the Tomorrow Man, Jackson Kraven. The face-paint he wore in the Cell still clings to his features, and the wounds he suffered are altogether far too obvious.) DRIP DRIP DRIP (That huge gash down his entire face is where most of the blood is coming from. The flow is sluggish, showing that some time has passed since the Tomorrow Man tore open his own face, but that not much time has passed at all, oh no. No, this is obviously from that horrible winter night in Calgary one night before Rage In The Cage...) DRIP DRIP DRIP (Kraven smiles at his own mutilated face in the mirror above the sink.) I'm still alive, Denrol. Twenty-four shots with that barbed-wire Kendo stick. Fourty-one brass-knuckle-assisted punches. I'm still alive. All your hate couldn't stop me. (Kraven drives his own fist into the mirror, shattering it, bringing fresh blood to his shredded knuckles.) I'm still alive. You wanna play the hard-ass? Fine. You wanna go beat on some punk who has trouble fighting Fiesta wrestlers? Fine. But you *beep*ed up. I'm still alive. (Kraven drives his fist into the mirror again. We see glass embedded in his knuckles.) All debts are paid? Revenge is served? *beep* that. You're gonna have to keep one eye on the dressing room, one eye on the stage, one eye on the crowd and one eye on your opponent from now on... and we all know that's too many eyes for one human being. Even the "Greatest Of All Earth Time". You *beep*ed up, Denrol. I'm still alive. And I'm watching you. And Alexandra. And the baby... (Kraven chuckles, the horrible, insane laughter of those who have stared into the abyss...) (...And liked what they saw.) (We fade to black.)
