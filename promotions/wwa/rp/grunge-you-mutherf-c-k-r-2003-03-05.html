Grunge you mutherf*(c)K£R

5 Mar 2003

By Joey K

Item 2 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 5 Mar 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
FADE IN Video clips of Meltdown, in black and white.... Jimmy: Both men trying to get the fans behind them. Fat Tony: It won’t work. Jimmy: Why? Fat Tony: Because they all support Joey K. Jimmy: Yeah right. To the surprise of Adams, even though a weak Adams chant has started, much of the seats start chanting for Grunge. Adams can't reach the ropes and he has no other choice but to submit. DING DING Jimmy: Adams submits! Fat Tony: Joey K wins! Jimmy: And Grunge. Fat Tony: Sure he helped. Gary Trudeau: Ladies and gentlemen your winners are Grunge and Joey K! Grunge releases his hold on Nick Adams who rolls to the outside despondent and heads up the rampway by himself. Joey is in the ring and is trying to shake Grunge’s hand. Grunge refuses and scoops up Joey K and hit him with his own urange finisher, . MAKING WAY. Fat Tony: What gratitude? Joey gets the victory and Grunge repays him like that. Jimmy: Sometimes I wonder if we are on the same planet. Grunge yells at Joey not to get in his business again. Grunge then leaves the ring and heads to the back.... The video suddenly spins into rewind. then begins to play again. Grunge releases his hold on Nick Adams who rolls to the outside despondent and heads up the rampway by himself. Joey is in the ring and is trying to shake Grunge’s hand. Grunge refuses and scoops up Joey K and hit him with his own urange finisher, . MAKING WAY. Once again, it goes into rewind. Joey is in the ring and is trying to shake Grunge’s hand. Grunge refuses and scoops up Joey K and hit him with his own urange finisher, . MAKING WAY. The video fades out, then in again. Joey is sitting alone in an empty locker room. Beside sits a television and VCR. Joey doesn't bring his head up, instead he sits low with his head between his legs. He is wearng a light blue shirt, dark pants a shades. He begins to speak, almost a whisper. 'Thats what i get for winning the match? That's what i get for being the good sportsman. Once again i've been made a fool out of, and once again Grunge, you've proven what a damn fool you are. Did you sell your ability to the devil when you were younger? Those wrestling manuevers i saw on Meltdown i'm sure came from the lowly and rank pits of hell.' 'Grunge............sounds like something I've stepped in on the way home from a night out, and can't scrape of my shoe......heh.' He looks up at the camera. 'Oh, and Venny, don't think i have forgotten about you, I'll be seeing you real soon.In the mean time, LeTrav, Grunge and even that two bit piece of piss Nick Adams......DAMMIT, the WHOLE of the WWA!' He suddenly stands, and moves out of view of the camera. 'Make way.......for Joey K!'
