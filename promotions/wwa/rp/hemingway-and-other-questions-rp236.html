Hemingway and other questions

4 Jul 2003

By Grunge

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
FADE IN Grunge: Ernest Hemingway was a drunk. (Grunge is sitting in a library reading books all around him. The books are piled high at the desk wear he sits.) Grunge: Truth be told he’s my favorite author. It takes a true drunk to know how to take it to someplace where the average internet dimwit thinks you've created something magical. (Grunge smiles as he slams his book shut.) Grunge: He wrote about war. Make not mistake the Independence Day Rumble will be all about war. I think that’s something that most of you don’t understand. (Grunge pulls out a water bottle, almost as if he is hiding it from whoever’s in charge and takes a huge swig.) Grunge: Because I’ve listen to all of you speak, how you know your going to win. Nick is the only one with a thought in his head. He realizes that everyone thinks they are going to win…even me. (Grunge chuckles.) Grunge: But I’ll tell you I’m going to do my best to win. (Grunge gets up from the table and starts to walk outside.) Grunge: Mostly we are in this battle royal to settle scores. I have a few even though I believe if that’s why your there your going to lose. But I would be amiss if I didn’t mention you Leviathan. (Grunge goes to a candy machine at the outside of the building.) Grunge: I’ve been craving a Crunch Bar. Anyway yes Levi my wife is concerned about me as she usually is. But unlike my wife I don’t think you’re a completely evil man. You’ve never seen completely evil. Yes, you're accumulating existence but it's all just imitation. We're all just born of molecules so clearly. We’re all the same. But you think you’re superior so you alienate people with violence. Being alienated is not human. We thrive on acceptance, curiosity and intimacy; that is how we define ourselves. (Grunge places the dollar in and makes his selection. He gets the candy out of the slot at the bottom of the machine and begins to unwrap it.) Grunge: Or maybe you have a different motivation. Maybe you’re from the school of Darwin. Maybe you think your separating the wheat from the shaft. Better to call yourself evil. This way you have an excuse for your little exercise. Either way you and Lockdown both are not killers. True killers do not brag about their crimes. Look at the worst of all time, Ted Bundy, Son of Sam, etc. Both of you couldn’t hold a candle to either. (Grunge takes a bite of the chocolate.) Grunge: But now I’m rambling. (Grunge begins to walk on till he reaches the street corner.) Grunge: And these facts won’t sink in now and you will forget this. You will spend the rest of your life trying to remember this. You'll just drink till you remember. I’m assuming you drink. (Grunge begins to wave for a cab.) Grunge: Of course it could be worse; you could be reincarnated as a ATM Machine. That’s how much you figure into IDR for me. Because I’m going to do everything in my power to win. (A cab stops and Grunge opens the door but right before he turns to the camera.) Grunge: Rock and roll. (And as he gets in the camera begins to…) FADE OUT
