*BSB* Sting Like A Bee

1 Jun 2004

By Odium

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What a strange week it has been. Storms have been non-stop, Odium burns down his own house just to get rid of his father, obviously not dead but just to be away from him. Who could blame him, what an ass his father had been. Maybe that's why he grew up to be so damn cocky... Odium: The weather is truly unpredictable. Just yesterday it was an "Oh crap, it's raining, let's stay inside and play scrabble while being excited by lightning", and today is more of an "It's too sunny, let's stay inside and play scrabble while being excited by lightning!" Not much difference between them really when you think about it, but I wouldn't expect much of you to be able to do that would I? [The camera fades on screen about a metre away from the face of Odium, who instantly flashes a grin that seems to be both sarcastic yet also sadistically evil. Odium whips his tongue out of his mouth, and quickly licks his lips before the tongue goes back into his mouth.] Odium: Of course not. The only thing that I expect you people at home to remember is who to fear, who to be afraid of. Who am I referring to? No, not Satan, no, not Beelzebub, but the person who is strong enough to outwit both of these fine gents. Me. What makes me that powerful? Well I don't have any godlike powers, but I do have these. [The camera zooms away from Odium's face to see him pulling up the short sleeves of his navy green shirt, and showing off his extravagant upper arm biceps. He flashes an "I'm better than you all" smile before letting go, and allowing the sleeve to roll back down to his elbow.] Odium: Now consider the bee. Tis extremely small, yet it can cause great harm to anyone who dares to annoy the little devil. Like me, minus the fact that I am big not small, and I can cause harm to anybody just for the sheer fun of it. [The camera zooms out even further to show that Odium is in a room that leads to more rooms, each and every one of them painted light red and white on the walls. This particular room that Odium is in is, assumingly, the bedroom. A bed is located to the right of Odium, to the left of the camera.] Odium: You like my new place? Wait, don't answer that, I don't actually give a rat's ass if you do or not. I do. If you're wondering, no this isn't a house I just bought, it's a hotel, I'm going to be moving around the country from arena to arena, I thought I might aswell just stay in a hotel from now on, I've had it with houses. [Odium smiles as a cocky jerk would, and then continues to speak.] Odium: Enough about me, let's hear about you. Actually, scrap that, let's hear more about me. Bourbon Street Brawl is coming up, and for all the people who are taking part, they should know that, before they go getting excited about it, they do not deserve to be a part of it. They shouldn't be in the Bourbon Street Brawl, I should. But no, I don't get chosen by Tracer Bullet, yet all of the wrestlers on Meltdown get to take part. And to top it off, I don't even have a match for Bourbon Street Brawl, so I have to take the night off? I'm not having it. [The camera once again closes in to be in it's original position, just a metre away from his face, and you can slowly see his lips start to move.] Odium: People like Muppet Man are allowed to take part. Why should he get to take part? He's a wrestler who actually enjoys being a clown for crying out loud! Rocky Adams, why should he be taking part? He's an alcoholic and he's probably going to drop into a pub during the match! Someone with a drinking problem has actually been allowed in the match!?! And then to cap it off, people like Kid Cool get to be entered into the match? How old is he anyway, eleven? He hits like a girl, and he can't actually wrestle to save his life, how ridiculous when you could have had me in the match. [Odium groans maniacally and then the camera closes in so that the lips of Odium are nearly, but not quite, pressed against the camera screen] Odium: I'll show them. You see, it's.......... GAME OVER!
