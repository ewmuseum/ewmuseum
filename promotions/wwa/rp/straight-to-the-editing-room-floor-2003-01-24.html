WWA (1997–2019) Roleplays

"Straight to the editing room floor."

24 Jan 2003

By Jimmi Zion

Item 1 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 24 Jan 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
*[Fade In] *[Cut to the interior of a promo production and editing room. We see "Sky Flyin" Jimmi Zion standing over overseeing a promo with two other gentlemen. They are currently viewing the most recently done latest promo of Enigma from the 01/23/03 WWA Insider in the production room computer.] "Sky Flyin" Jimmi Zion: "Okay boys, take this straight from the top and I'll tell you when to freeze it and make the the neccessary editing corrections." "Sure thing Mister Zion." [They press the play button to allow the promo to play through.] {We hear clanging against metal. It sounds like metal on metal. It is dark so we can not see what is going on} What a sweet sound. It is the sound of metal clashing against metal. To be more precise it is the sound of a lead pipe smashing against steel. This is the sound that will be heard numerous times on Sunday February Second. On February 2nd 3 Men will enter this ring. One Man will walk out victorious. Two Men will walk out with nothing to show for their pain, Their suffering, Their sacrifice, Nothing One Man will be showered with praise Two others will be ridiculed, Laughed at, Why? Because they are losers One man will get the job done Two men will fail One will be victorious One will have the titles One will receive the accolades One will rise above the rest The One will be Jimmi Zion: "Wait a minute, freeze it right here boys." {We see a picture of Enigma, the men press pause right at that shot of his face.} Zion: "Okay here's what I want you do do." [At Zion's request, one of the man takes the mouse and clicks on an the picture of Enigma. The mouse soon begins acting like an eraser and causes the picture to disappear.] Zion: "Now this is what I want you to do next boys." [Following Zion's instructions, then then pull up a picture of "Sky Flyin" Jimmi Zion from a file on the computer, and places it in the spot where Enigma's picture used to be.] Zion: "Good picture I do say so myself. I always thought I was a naturally photogenic person." "Oh yes sir Mister Zion, it is a great picture. It's way better than Enigma's." Zion: "Thanks, now on this last part, here's what I want you to do." NOTHING CAN STOP ME NOW [Zion instructs the two production editors to draw a huge red arrow pointing right next to "NOTHING CAN STOP ME NOW" and puts a message right next to it.] ==============------------------->>>>>> "EXCEPT...FOR...ME...B****!" {fade to ?} Zion: "Now for that little empty question mark just sitting there at the end, something's missing. [He pauses as he thinks to himself for a moment.] Oh yes I got it. You still have that picture of Enigma?" "Yeah it's in the recycle bin Mr. Zion, why?" Zion: "Get it, I have a use for that to afterall." "Okay you're the boss." [They pull out the picture of Enigma and cut and paste it onto the part of the promo where it says, "{fade to ?}" following Zion's directions. They replace the question mark with the picture of Enigma, but then they use the mouse and draw a big red circle around the picture, and then dry a line through it. Of course they draw the line through the picture after they highlight Enigma's face with a drawn red beard, glasses, cigarette, and devil horns on his head.] [After their finished editing the changes onto the Enigma promo, they all admire the promo and it's new look.] "Excellent work Mister Zion." Zion: "Yeah sometimes I amaze even myself." "Well what do you want us to do with it now?" Zion: "What do you think I should do with it? I want you all to send it over the airwaves to everybody. I want this thing broadcast on the feed every hour on the hour to start things out, and then after that give it to the insomniacs late at night something to chew on." "What about the master copy Mister Zion, would you like to keep that one for yourself?" Zion: "No, send that one personally gift wrapped to Enigma. I want him to what's coming his way at Rage in a Cage. I will not be denied my title. Chance is the true champion, but after the PPV. There will be no doubt, not even in from Enigma. I will be THE ONE!" "We'll do Mister Zion." "We'll get right on it." Zion: "Thank you gentlemen so much for your hard work. Your work is greatly appreciated. You make me proud." "Thank you Mister Zion." Zion: "Now I hate to cut this short boys, but I need to go. I have to go home to my recovering wife back at the hotel room, so I must bid adeiu." "Of course." "Give your wife our regards." Zion: "I will, thank you." "Good luck in your match at the Rage in a cage." Zion: "Thank you...like I need luck, but thank you." [Jimmi Zion walks out of the promo room leaving the two gentlemen to do the work he has instructed them to do. The camera fades out.] *{FADE OUT]*
