X Corp: Inside The Wrestlers Studio

30 Jul 2003

By Dave Harley

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The scene begins with the soles of four dirty sneakers facing the screen. The heels touch the ground as the feet sway back and forth, rocking on the heels. The camera zooms out to reveal Dave Harley and Mal Somers, the Extreme Corporation, or X-Corp lazing back in two fold out chairs, drinking beers and chuckling with each other. We can tell they have been here for some time as there are several empty bottles lying around the place. Dave Harley wears his customary shades and “Too Hot for Monday Night" T-Shirt, and Mal Somers wears his usual “Laying the CRAXXT Down” T-Shirt. He also has a Title Belt draped across his shoulder, which appears to have macaroni facial features stuck to it as well as woollen hair. Sound strange? Then read on…] Voice: So how did you guys meet? Somers: [Pointing to the Title Belt draped across his shoulder] Who? Me and "Rookie" here? I beat Harley here for him back in November. The Belt was later retired and we became really good buddies so... Harley: Hold on there Chairboy, he's not talking about you and Rookie, he's talking about you and me! Somers: I knew that. Harley: Me and Mal met at the EWA. I beat him for the EWA Extreme Title on the night the EWA folded and he followed me to the WWA. Somers: I did not! Harley: Well whatever. Nobody's really interested in the 'truth' here Mal! Voice: So when did you set aside your differences and form X Corp? Harley: Well that would be when Mal's little floosie turned up with Leviathan and Lockdown, forming The Cell. Somers: The only way we could take The Cell down was to team up. Voice: And this was when X Corp was formed? Somers: Yup. Voice: I hear you play a lot of games - Scrabble being a favourite - tell us about that. Somers: What can I say? We're Scrabble champions! Harley has a season ticket to the World Championship! Harley: [nods] I got it on eBay! Somers: it's really been quite a challenge playing against Dave. A lot of the words he uses are so up-to-date that they're not even in the Dictionary. Harley: That's right. [Cue dreamy music as the screen goes all fuzzy and we flashback to an X Corp Scrabble game, backstage at a WWA Event around January 2003.] Somers: “CRAXXTT” is not a word, Harley and you know it! How convenient, putting that X on the Triple Word Score! Harley: Hold on, hold on…it is a word…”CRAXXT” is the sound made when you hit your “Somers Assault” finisher on your opponents! Yeah...imagine hitting those big oafs in the Cell with stereo Somers Assaults…it’d be CRAXXT! CRAXXT! 1…2…3! Somers: (Looking into the distance, smiling) You know what Harley, you’re right. There is no sweeter word in the world than CRAXXT! I love it! Harley: (adding up their final tallies, grinning) Carry the one…OK Somers you got a respectable score of 288 and lets see…oh…I got 290! I win! Hahahahah!! Somers: (Tipping over the board in frustration) Forget about Scrabble… [We fade out of that flashback sequence where Dave is cackling with laughter and Somers looks a little peterbed.] Harley: Ahhhh, Good Times. Somers: Yeah whatever! Voice: Now, I've noticed that Dave refers to you as "Chairboy" from time to time. Please explain. Somers: Well it all began when... Harley: (interrupting) You see, when Mal started out in the EWA, and then in the WWA, when I wasnt beating him up, he carried a chair around and called himself La Parka! I mean "The Chairman". So "Chairboy" is a bit of a play on that. Voice: Thanks for clearing that up. Apart from Scrabble, are there any other games you guys are known for playing? Somers: (looks at Harley) Well, if Dave here doesnt cut me off... (Harley shrugs and shakes his head.) Somers: Good. Well another game we are... (Suddenly Somers is cut off by another dreamy flashback sequence, from around March 2003. X-Corp and fellow WWA Superstar Eddie Van Dorn chat backstage.) Somers: Ah Eddie, punctual as always. Harley: Hey Eddie my man, good to see you. EVD: Now that those formalities are out of the way... your decision, gentlemen? Harley: Well we thought it over, we agreed that we wanted to give it a shot. Somers: But we couldn't agree on who would be the best representative. Harley: Yeah, we both have different talents we can bring to the table. I've got my charm, good looks, and skill. Somers has his...ummm...CRAXXT. EVD: Very well. So which of the two of you will be accepting the challenge? No wait... let me guess. I requested it to be a hardcore rules match... it's going to be Harley, right? (The crowd cheers the concept of the former WWA Extreme champion taking on the last EWA Extreme champion in a hardcore match Monday night.) Harley: (looking at ground) No Eddie, it wont be me...it will be Mal representing us at Meltdown. (The crowd cheers again, this time at the opposite concept. Easy to please, aren't they?) EVD: Hmm... interesting. Might I ask why? Harley: Well, as we couldn’t decide who it was going to be, we had to seek further advice. Somers: Yes we had to track down HHH. (The crowd boos upon hearing the name of a non WWA wrestler, especially one as loathesome as HHH.) Harley: (smirking) HHH is the game. EVD: You mean you guys know that moron HHH? You asked him for advice? Somers: Yes we let HHH decide who would face EVD on Monday Meltdown. Do you want to see HHH? EVD: Sure. I'll put that roided up jackass in his place. (the crowd pops at the thought.) Somers: OK then, Dave, go get HHH for Eddie. (Harley nods and reaches into his backpack. He pulls out HHH. Hungry Hungry Hippos.) Harley: You see, Rock Paper Scissors didn’t work, neither did Scrabble. But HHH did the job. Somers: That'd be a first! (chuckles to himself) Anyways, I won Hungry Hippos 3-2 and as a result, I'll be representing X-Corp at Meltdown. EVD: Very well. All right, Mr. Somers... Eddie jumps off of the crate and offers a handshake. EVD: I'll be looking forward to Monday night. Eddie and Mal shake hands, all but guaranteeing that the match will take place. [And we fade back to X-Corp, Dave laughing at Mal being cut off by the flashback, Somers again looking slightly annoyed.] Voice: And now the name "Extreme Corporation". How did that come about? Harley: You go Mal. Somers: Thanks man. Well it was Jill, Dave's P.A. who came up with that one. Harley: What are you smoking!? It was my idea! Somers: No it wasn't! Harley: Yes it was! If you don't believe me, roll the footage! [The dreamy music starts up again and we fade back to January 2003, to the X-Corp locker room.] Harley: Anyway Somers, it looks like we are teaming up so we can take care of that piece of s**t, Leviathan. What we need is a team name. Something that will instill fear in the heart of Leviathan and Lockdown. Something that takes a bit of each of our personalities and creates something new. Something like... Somers: Team Badass! Harley: That sucks! What about Harley and Co? Somers: Yeah right! How about Mal Somers to the Extreme! Harley: No! No! That's too Vanilla Ice! What about Harley and Chairboy! (Somers casts an annoyed glance in Harleys direction and sighs) Jill: Extreme Corporation? Harley: Oh I got it, I got it...EXTREME CORPORATION! Somers: (standing up as Jill rolls her eyes) Sounds great...I like it! Harley: (standing up also) Well its settled then. Man I am a genius or what? [The scene cuts back to the interview.] Harley: That footage was obviously doctored! Voice: Since forming the Extreme Corporation, you have captured a lot of Gold... Harley: [Holding up his GenX Title] I sure have! Voice: I meant as a team! Harley: Oh right. Yeah we beat the sucky Cell at March Madness. Somers: Yeah. Hey, how about a dreamy flashback to us winning the Titles? That way we can show Homo/Phobic just how cool we are! Harley: Yeah right Mal. What do you think this is? A movie? Somers: [sighs] I guess you're right... Harley: I'll give you points for being creative though. [Just as Mal opens his mouth to reply, we cut to another dreamy flashback. We see a ring surrounded by a Hell In The Cell. The Cell door has been smashed open by a black golfcart - otherwise known as the Extreme Mobile. Dave Harley fights Leviathan on the roof of the cell as Mal Somers and Lockdown spill out through the door and start an ascent. A spark flies from the dented bonnet of the Extreme Mobile and lands in the ever increasing puddle of gasoline, igniting it...] Jimmy: HOLY SH*T! THERE'S A FIRE INSIDE THE CELL!!! IT'S SPREADING ALONG THAT TRAIL OF GASOLINE!!! IF IT REACHES THAT GASOLINE CAN IT'LL... BOOOOOOMMMMM!!!! [The can of gasoline explodes...and the cell walls go up in flames!!!] Jimmy: OH MY GOOD GOD! THE CELL IS ON FIRE! DID ANYONE TEST WHETHER THAT CHEMICAL THAT WAS SUPPOSED TO STRENGTHEN THE CELL WAS FLAMMABLE?!!! Fat Tony: It's a good job Somers and Lockdown got up on the roof before that explosion or they'd be toast right now! [The scene cuts to a few mintes later. The cell walls begin to swell and twist due to the heat. Suddenly they slowly buckle and the cage sways one way and then the other. All four men momentarily stop brawling as the cell wall closest to the ramp gives way first. The cell collapses in on itself as all four men leap from the wreckage amidst a plume of smoke and fire. CRASSSSSSSSSSSSSSHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHBOOOOOOOOMMMMMMMMMM!!!! Jimmy, Fat Tony, Rookie, The Crowd: [stunned silence.] The dust and smoke settles and the twisted wreck of the cell lays there as a stream of officials and suits swarm from the back, standing bewildered as to what to do. Jimmy: Fans, there is no contingency for this, this is … unbelievable. [The scene fades back to Dave and Mal.] Voice: The WWA Tag Team Titles, the Rookie Title, the TV Title, the GenX Title...any last comments about the PTC Tag Team Titles? Somers: Naturally! Dave and I have proven time and time again that we are the most fightingest team in wrestling history. We fought our way up through both the EWA and the WWA and became a force to be reckoned with. Harley: Homo/Phobic, you have no idea what you're facing on Fiesta. You've seen us play Scrabble, you've seen us play Hungry Hungry Hippos... Somers: ...You've seen us step out of the rubble left by a burning cell with Gold in our hands. You think you're any kind of threat to us? Harley: On Fiesta, you will step out in front of all the HARLettes... Somers: ...and MALcoholics... Harley: ...and you're gonna lose those Belts like Mal loses his lunch after a rollercoaster! Somers: Hey! What you tell them that for? Harley: Sorry. Just slipped out. In conclusion, Homo/Phobic are nothing... Somers: ...and X Corp are a Triple Word Score!!! [The scene begins to fade out.] Harley: Triple word score? That was so freakin' corny!
