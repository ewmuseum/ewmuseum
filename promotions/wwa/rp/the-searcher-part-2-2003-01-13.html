The Searcher (Part 2)

13 Jan 2003

Author not credited in the source

Item 2 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 13 Jan 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Dirk Flint, Private Investigator is looking for a missing person. He has arrived in La Pas, Bolivia to continue the hunt. He is walking down a street lined with big white houses. This is the most exclusive district in the whole of Bolivia. He eventually arrives at a large house with a high wall right round it. He pushes the button in the gate to activate the intercom.) Voice: Ola? Flint: Hello, My name is Mr Flint, I have an appointment. Voice: Good afternoon, Mr Flint, Please come in. (The gate swings open and Flint walks up the driveway. He arrives at the door to the house which is opened by a butler. Flint is shown into a wood panelled study. The guy behind the desk in short, clean shaven and completely bald. He is dressed in a bright yellow shirt.) Flint: Hello, are you Mr Sanchez? Sanchez: That’s me buddy! Flint: Nice place, you got here. Sanchez: That is a virtue of being the “Most Popular Man in Bolivia”! My butler said you were a Private Investigator, what can I do for you? (Flint takes out a photo and slides it across the desk.) Flint: Do you know this guy? Sanchez: Yes, I do. We used to work together, we were very good friends. Flint: Do you know where he is? I’m trying to locate him. Sanchez: I haven’t heard from him in a long while. Flint: When did you last see him? Sanchez: He stayed here for a couple of months last summer but then he returned to the USA. He left towards the end of September. Flint: Did he leave a forwarding address? Sanchez: No, he just said he wanted to visit his Mother. He did leave a couple of things though. You can take them if you want, I mean they are no use to me! (Sanchez pulls a box from under the table and passes it to Flint.) Sanchez: I don’t know if they will be helpful or not. (Flint takes the items from the box one at a time. There is a 1997 street map of Brooklyn and a key with the number 7203.) Flint: These are both very interesting. Now you said he was going to visit his mother? Sanchez: Yes, he said he hadn’t seen her for years and thought it was about time he dropped by. She lives in Brooklyn, I think he’s even circled her street. (Sanchez points out some markings on the map of Brooklyn. Flint smiles.) Sanchez: Yes, here it is, look it says number 191 Johan Road. Flint: Thank-you Mr Sanchez, you have been extremely helpful. I’ll see myself out. (Fade To Black.)
