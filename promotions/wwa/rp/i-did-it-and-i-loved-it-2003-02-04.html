I did it and I LOVED it!

4 Feb 2003

By Denrol

Item 4 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 4 Feb 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene opens with Denrol. He is dressed in his street clothes, wearing a WWA T-Shirt under his black leather jacket. He is still quite bandaged up. He is standing in front of a Rage in the Cage logo, clearly this is the backstage interview spot. He is laughing.) Denrol: I did it. I did what Vik ventressa, what Craven Moore, what all of them tried to do and failed. I destroyed Jackson Kraven. I lost the match, but I won the war. I locked Kraven into that Cell, I made him think he has it all well in hand and than I showed him what fear is. I made him pay. For two years that man terrorized this federation. Everyone feared him. He shall be feared no more. I proved that I am the monster of the WWA. Think about it Kraven. Now every time you threaten someone, they will not shake their knees. They will remember that you were destroyed by a 5'9" Cruiserweight. They will remember the way I just tore into you. They will remember that you were not that scary after Rage in the Cage 2003. The monster of Jackson Kraven is no more. (Denrol grins, than turns serious.) Denrol: That brings me to the big question. Why Denrol? Oh sure, we understand why you destroyed Kraven. You had been promising to do so for months. But why did you go after Chance? Why did you give him that Evil Killer? Why did you turn your back on the fans? (Denrol chuckles.) Denrol: To send a message to every single one of those snot-nose punks out there. You know them. Jimmi Zion, Nick Adams, The Cell, X-Corp, Grunge & Rick Venom, The Veteranz, and American Luchadores. I was sending them all a message. A message that they are NOTHING! Nothing compared to the Greatness that is I, Denrol. I have been in this federation for four and a half years. I've seen a lot of people come and go. But the WWA stays together no matter what. Why is that? Why is the WWA one of the oldest federations there is? It's very simple. Three core people. These three people make the WWA. They are Shaman, Tracer Bullet, and ME! We are the WWA Franchise Players. Without us, the WWA would not exist. Shaman knows what I'm talking about. I'm sure if Tracer thought about it, he would agree with me. Just look at the past. Randy Cross comes and goes and makes less of an impact each time. Craven Moore is no more and does anybody care? The Four Horsemen are now a joke and the WWA lives on. Mark Harrington passes the reigns on to Matthew Warr. The WWA lives on. This is not an accident. We the WWA Franchise Players stay. We keep this federation alive, no matter what happens. Walk up to any causal fan. Tell them you just went to a WWA event. What do they ask you? They don't ask if you saw Ian Tense. They don't ask if Wes Sanders was on the card. They don't ask if Damian Thorne kicked butt. They ask you if you saw Tracer Bullet wrestle. They ask you if Shaman is still there. They ask you if Denrol had a match. The rest of the WWA could all get fired tomorrow and no one would care, as long the WWA Franchise Players remained. That is why I attacked Chance. The fans picked him as the next guy to breakout in the WWA. I attacked him to show that he will never be a WWA Franchise Player. I attacked him to prove that I am. Chance now knows that I am the Greatest of All Earth Time. He knows that he is my b*tch. If he ever forgets that lesson, I'll just have to teach him again. (Denrol chuckles again.) Denrol: I am the Past. I am the Present. I am the Future. I am the WWA Franchise. And you're NOT! (The scene fades to black.)
