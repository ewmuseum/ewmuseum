Curzon Disguised

27 Feb 2003

By James Curzon

Item 3 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 27 Feb 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Referee Silas Fagin is sat in a roadside cafe. He is wearing a long dark coat, a false beard and some large dark sunglasses. He peers around the smoke-filled gloom of the bar and check his watch impatiently.) Fagin: He said he would be here by now! He said he would come disguised as a lady in a red dress. Where is he? The frikkin' idiot! (He looks up to see a very attractive blond in a short red dress. She looks around the restaraunt and her eyes come to rest on Fagin. She smiles and glides seductively towards him.) Fagin: That is a bloody good disguise! (As she approaches the table, Fagin stands up and offers his hand to her. She ignores him and greets the man at the next table with a big kiss.) Fagin: Oh no. (Fagin has seen the real person he was waiting for and it's not such a good disguise. The figure in the red dress is very obviously a man, the full-beard kind of gives it away. He is dressed in a flowery red dress with two soccerballs shoved up inside, his bright yellow wig is skewed to the left and his lipstick is all over the place. He walks slowly over to the table and sits down.) Fagin: Hello Mr Curzon. Curzon: SHHH! I am in disguise, no-one must know it is me! Fagin: Sorry, boss. Curzon: Address me as Darlene! (Fagin shakes his head.) Fagin: I don't know why we have to be so secretive. Curzon: It's because of the conspiracy, they are trying to get me. I don't know who's involved, it could be anyone. I must keep my true identity a secret when I am out in public! Fagin: OK, OK, whatever. Curzon: Have you done the research I asked for? (Fagin looks into his breifcase and takes out a red folder.) Curzon: Tell me. Fagin: James Beam has been talking about the match, saying how he is a much better wrestler than you and that you can't win without cheating. Curzon: What? Well, that's patently absurd! I think you will find that he was the one disqualified for using a weapon in our previous match. Me? A cheat? I find that highly ridiculous! Fagin: Yes, Darlene, Beam is totally ridiculous! Curzon: What about Global Warfare? How has the response been to that? (Fagin pulls out another sheet of notes.) Fagin: Pretty good, of course, you did well. Curzon: Of course. In fact I was never officially eliminated. Fagin: No, you ran away half-way through. Curzon: Yes. Fagin: Can I ask why? Curzon: Yes. (Fagin waits for the answer. One is not forthcoming.) Fagin: OK, why? Curzon: Did you see the guy at ringside, in a red shirt, greasy hair. Fagin: I can't say I noticed him. Curzon: He was in the conspiracy. He kept staring at me and whenever something bad happened to me, he cheered! I decided he was going to try and get me before the end so I just had to escape whilst I could! Fagin: Right. You don't think he was just cheering on his favourite wrestlers? Curzon: No, he wasn't cheering for me! Thats how I knew. (Fagin takes out a photo and yet another sheet of notes.) Fagin: Next up, is Fiesta on Saturday night. Curzon: Shhh! I don't want my location to be revealed! Fagin: But it's all over the website. Curzon: That's just great then! I.. (A waitress walks by and Curzon stops speaking until she is out of range.) Curzon: I am trying to work underground and people publicise where I am! Crazy! Fagin: Yeah, totally. Curzon: So what's going on at Fiesta? Fagin: It's me and you against Beam and Showtime. Curzon: No, it isn't! Fagin: Yes, it is. Look on this programme, me and you! Curzon: It is actually you and I, not me and you. (Fagin takes a deep breath and counts to 10 before carrying on.) Fagin: Anyway, the two of us are teaming up again. Now I have been practicing and I have got my sleeper-hold perfected. Curzon: Now I seem to think this Showtime is one of the greatest wrestlers of all time, is that right? Fagin: Err, not exactly, no. Curzon: I beg to differ, young Mr Fagin. I had a conversation with not so long ago and he assured me that he was one of the top 4 wrestlers ever to grace the WWA. Fagin: The other three being? Curzon: Well, me, obviousley and 2 other guys I'd never heard of. I'm sure Mr Showtime and I will have a tremendous match, closely and cleanly fought. (Fagin takes the final piece of paper from his bag.) Fagin: So do you want me to deal with Beam? Curzon: Oh no, I have a couple of suprizes for James Beam, you just concentrate on your sleeper-hold and the victory will be ours! Fagin: One final thing, Darlene, this letter arrived today. (Curzon takes the letter and opens it. He read aloud.) Curzon: Mr Curzon, we have your mother, you must pay £500,000 or we kill her. Deliver the money under the clock at Waterloo Station, 12pm on Sunday or she will die! Fagin: Oh no! That is terrible! Curzon: I know! It ought to be "we will kill her" not "we kill her", that is awful grammar! (Curzon screws up the letter and throws it away. He gets up to leave, straightening his wig.) Curzon: That letter is a trap laid by the conspiracy. I will not fall for it they will not catch me! Fagin: I think you might be a bit paranoid. Curzon: I'm not paranoid, I'm right! (Curzon totters outr of the cafe on his high-heels and Fagin bangs his head on the tabel in frustration.) Fade to black
