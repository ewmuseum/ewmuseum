Champions of the past

6 Feb 2003

By Ian

Item 6 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 6 Feb 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
November 11th, 2002. (A voice of realization. Of satisfaction) It's been five days short of 3 full months. (The voice of the WWA World Heavyweight Champion) 3 months since I stormed the pinnacle of this business. This company. This world. 3 months. (The voice of 'The Prototype' Ian Tense) They said it wouldn't last. They said I wouldn't last. They said it was a paper reign. (Ian spins around in a leather executive chair, facing his computer) Which leads me to this. (Ian pulls up the WWA World Title history, found exclusively at www.wwa-online.com) The greats of the greats of the World Wrestling Alliance. The legends. The trailblazers that brought wrestlers such as myself to this business. The small "fraternity", if you will, of people lucky enough to have reigned as World Champion. (Ian double-clicks on his name next to the World Title. A smirk of arrogance creeps onto his face) Let's look at these 'Legends'. Let's take the *beep*ing time to examine their reigns. Let's do what everyone else is afraid to do. (And scrolls up and down the screen) Realize my legitimacy. (The camera zooms out momentarily, showing 4 framed title belts hanging on his wall; the TV titles, 2 US titles, and 1 World title. Ian smiles devilishly as he reads the information on the screen) Mark Lynch - 12/12/97 to 12/26/97. 14 small days at the top. Randy Cross - 2/5/99 to 2/26/99. 21 days. He's a legend. 4/17/98 to 6/26/98. 2 months, 9 days. 2/5/99 to 2/26/99. 11 *beep*ing days. Nik Seiger - 3/6/98 to 4/17/98. 1 month, 11 days. That's it. Black Scorpian - 6/26/98 to 7/10/98. All of 360 hours. All of it. Every *beep*ing second of it. Gregory O'Hara - 11/8/98 to 1/15/99. 2 months, 7 days. Close, but not enough. Darken - 1/15/99 to 1/31/99. 16 slow, agonizing days. Steihl - 1/31/99 to 2/5/99. Less than 1 *beep*ing week. Outlaw - 5/9/99 to 7/1/99. 1 month, 22 days, and a loss to *beep*ing Denrol? 7/4/99 to 8/6/99. 1 month, 2 days. Horrible. Plain and simple. Denrol - 7/1/99 to 7/4/99. The Greatest of All Earth Time? 3 Goddamned days? And THIS is a legend? Kris Kassanova - 8/6/99 to 9/10/99. 1 month, 4 days. Just enough to get his name in the books. I hope he's proud. Jim Black - 7/2/00 to 8/11/00. 1 month, 9 days of *beep*ing SH*beep*it. Pure *Beep*ing Sh*beep*it. Mantis - 8/11/00 to 10/3/00. 1 month, 8 days from the legendary Mantis. Somehow, I expected more. 1/28/01 to 3/3/01. 1 month, 3 days on his pathetic excuse for an encore. Shaman - 12/15/00 to 12/23/00. I shouldn't even *beep*ing need to address this one. 8 days. That's all. 8 black days. Tracer Bullet - 10/3/00 to 12/15/00. 2 months, 12 days. Not bad. Not too bad from Mr. Whiney. Too bad he lost it to Shaman. Pathetic. 12/23/00 to 1/28/01. 1 month, 5 days. 3/3/01 to 4/16/01. 1 month, 13 days. What a *beep*ing JOKE! TJ Donaldson - 4/16/01 to 5/27/01. 1 month, 11 days. Nothing more. Nothing less. Vik Ventressa - 10/15/01 to 12/10/01. 1 month, 25 days. And this is a *beep*ing Big Shot, huh? Jackson Kraven - 12/10/01 to 1/14/02. 1 month, 4 days. 8/3/02 to 8/10/02. His last reign was for one *beep*Ing week? And I'm running out of tommorrows? Craven Moore - 8/10/02 to 11/10/02. 2 months from 'the most dominant big man in the WWA'. Bull sh*beep*it. (Ian spins his chair around again to address the camera) Amd then there is me. 3 months. 3 *beep*ing months. Only Jackson Kraven, Randy Cross, and Shawn Cage have ever held it longer in an un-interrupted reign. 3 out of 19. And I'm paper? I've beaten Wes Sanders more than I can count on both mine and his hands and feet. I beat Craven Moore. I beat almost the whole enitre roster at Birthday Brawl. I've fought Mighty Kraven, and had Tracer Bullet nailed in a strap match. It's time you all open your eyes and see what is happening. It's time you all realize that I have held the WWA TV title, something some of you will never be able to boast. I've held the US title TWICE, something only 3 other people in the HISTORY of this fed can claim. And I am holding strong on the 4th longest title reign EVER. I'm the real *Beep*ing legend. Everyone else is pretend.
