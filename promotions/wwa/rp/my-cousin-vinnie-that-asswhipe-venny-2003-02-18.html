My cousin Vinnie, that asswhipe Venny!

18 Feb 2003

By Joey K

Item 1 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 18 Feb 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
FADE IN Joey K is dressed smartly in a black suit, with a black shit and a dark red tie. He pases up and down the room, which appears to be a lavish hotel suite. He takes a cellular phone out of his breast pocket, quickly dials a number and waits. JOEY: *unders his breath* C'mon! pick up, pick up, pick up... click! JOEY: Vinnie! Howz it goin cous'? pauses JOEY: yeah, i know i never phone, sorry. pauses JOEY: Yeah, well.........Listen, i need a favour! You saw what that piece of horse crap LeTrav did last night right? Well, with YOUR help, that aint never gonna happe again. pauses again JOEY: That's right, i need someone! Joey looks up at the camera, as if only just realising they are there. JOEY: Listen, cous', I've got something i need to deal with. Talk to ya later man. Joe ends the call and shoves it back in his pocket. He turns towards the WWA camera. JOEY: Jimmi Zion........you said after Meltdown that you thought i was gonna say you got lucky. WHAT THE HELL????!!!! You were down, you were out! I hit you with the 'Making Way' and you were flat out on the canvas! You didn't get lucky, bull! And if it wasn't for that little chicken lover LeTrav, I'd be carrying those belts around. Calms himself, and straightens his suit blazer. JOEY: *ahem* now, down to more important matters, Veny LeTrav. Brother, you will not get away with that. I mean, I always knew you were dumb, but I never thought you would make a mistake like that. But don't worry LeTrav, it aint gonna happen again. You see, i was just on the phone to my cousin Vinnie, and he's gonna get me some help. Let's say, someone who can watch my back while I'm kicking ass! Joe walks over to the bedside cabinet, and pics up a glass, half full with Baileys. He takes a sip, and continues addressing the camera. JOEY: That's right, this week I'll be holding interviews for a manager. Someone who can tell me when little willy-slaps like LeTrav are running it with a shot from beind. So, anyone who thinks they are worthy to accompany me to the ring, call me. He turns to the large window and looks out at the sunset. JOEY: Oh and Venny......... Turns back to the camera. JOEY: MAKE WAY......FOR JOEY K! Joey once again turns back to the window. Bu the camera keeps rolling, after afew seconds he looks over his shoulder at us. JOEY: You still here? Turn that s&*t off! Joey pounces forward into the camera, which falls on the floor, before fading out.
