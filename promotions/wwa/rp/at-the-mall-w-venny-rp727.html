At The Mall (w/ Venny)

21 Nov 2003

By Dave Harley

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[We open on a huge Mall somewhere in America. Hundreds of bustling shoppers rush from store to store. We see two particular shoppers emerge from a tailors and walk towards our position. Both men are wearing gold around their waists. One is the Intercontinental Title, the other is the GenX Title.] Venny: So now that you’ve made me buy a new tux do you wanna tell me what it’s for? Harley: What? You don’t wanna look smart? Venny: Define "smart" Harley. Harley: Well, just look at me! [Venny looks down at Dave’s age-old jeans and X Corp hockey jersey.] Harley: OK, OK, it’s for a little surprise I have planned. You see, I wanted to apologise for what Mal did to you before the Bridge Barnstormer last week on Fiesta. We’ve got the Birthday Bash coming up and I guess we’re all gonna be fighting it out for these titles. As a former GenX Champion myself, I thought I’d plan a little surprise for ya. Venny: If it’s anything like that last surprise then forget it! Harley: What? Are you still bitter about that clown? C’mon bud, trust me on this one. We’ve got it all planned out! Venny: [Sighs] Alright Dude, what is it? Harley: It’s a surprise! Me and Mal will pick ya up on Monday Night. We’ve got it all worked out. Venny: I dunno about that. Can't you just leave Mal at home? Harley: [aghast] What?! C'mon, he's my best bud! I couldn't do that to him. Venny: No, but I could. I'd be happy to force-feed him that belt he keeps talking to. [Both men walk on quietly for a moment.] Venny: So, umm…what are you gonna do about Mal? Harley: Huh? Venny: This title shot he wants. Harley: Jeez, not you too! Venny: I’m just asking Dude. You gonna give him a shot? It’d be one hell of a match! Harley: No way! Our friendship is much more important than that. Venny: I hear what you’re saying man. But remember, you can’t dodge him forever. You’re both a part of the Birthday Brawl. What if he chooses to go after your belt? Harley: Mal wouldn’t do that. He knows what this title means to me. He’d probably have his sights set on another one entirely. Venny: You sure about that? [Dave stops and stares straight ahead blankly, wondering. Venny glances over at Harley and looks as if >he'd rather not said that at all] Venny: …Nah, you know what? Forget I said anything. You’re probably right. Mal wouldn’t want to jeopardise your friendship any more than you would. [Venny slaps Dave on the back in reassurance as we fade out…]
