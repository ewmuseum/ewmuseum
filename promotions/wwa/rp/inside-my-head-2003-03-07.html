Inside my head

7 Mar 2003

By Grunge

Item 7 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 7 Mar 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
FADE IN (The camera shoots down on Grunge as he lays on a couch. We focus in on his face looking down at him. He looks tired.) Grunge-I have a copy of the new Weezer CD. It’s not released yet, and it’s the unremastered studio version, all 14 tracks though. Don’t ask where I got it. And yes FBI and RIAA I will buy a copy when it comes out so look at this as backing up a copy I don't own YET. (Grunge pauses his face seems to show that he’s shifting from one idea to another. He then continues.) Grunge-My life needs a director's commentary track so I can figure out what is going on. I can pull off such a good mood when I’m around other people. I’m not faking it either, I just seem to be running on fumes by the end of the day to feel good around myself during these long sleepless nights I stare blankly at my own personal gateway to the information superhighway. (Grunge adjusts himself on the couch. He begins to roll over but remembers the camera above him so stays in one place.) Grunge-Too many things going on. Too much worrying about things and managing others, putting off others still and ignoring some altogether. Matches and such all run through my head like an all night Wallmart. Monday I have the chance to get back the TV title but I have to go through three men to do it. Those being LeTrav, Adams, and Joey K. (We pan over to see Julie dressed in a white business suit with her hair up in a bun sitting in a large chair. She holds a pad and pen writing things down.) Julie-How about we play some word association. Grunge-OK. Julie-Black Grunge-Motown Julie-Over Grunge-The Rainbow. Julie-Love Grunge-Wife Julie-Joey K Grunge-NSYNC Julie-Venny LeTrav Grunge-Gumbo Julie-Nick Adams Grunge-Villain. (Julie has been jotting down notes as Grunge gives answers. He looks up from the couch and stares.) Grunge-Well what’s up doc? (Julie takes off her glasses and smiles.) Julie-My professional opinion? You’re a wackjob. Grunge-Is that a medical term? (Julie just laughs and Grunge lays back down on the couch. The camera once again shifts to the view staring down at him. He closes his eyes.) Grunge-I need to catch up on life. I need to sleep. I feel better already. The music is fading out. FADE OUT
