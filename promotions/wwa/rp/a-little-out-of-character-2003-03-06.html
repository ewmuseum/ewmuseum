A little Out of Character.

6 Mar 2003

By James Beam

Item 5 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 6 Mar 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The screen opens to a small room, somewhat of a living room. There is a TV, with VCR above it, as small black leather lounge chair and a very intent man sitting in said chair. He is ooking at the TV with a remote control in his hand. As the TV pans away from the TV, you see it is Jimmi Zion in a recent match.) James: Awh... I've got it. This is a very interesting match Jimmi. You use the same move in this situation. In fact (beam looks at the screen) you seem to use alot of the same moves. (James pauses the VCR and leans back in his chair, putting his hands behind his head.) James: I've been in this room, studying you Zion. Studying your tendancies, your weaknesses. (laughs) Trust me, my friend, there are MANY of them. But, this match is something I really look forward too. It will be my first for a few things.... a) It will be my first Extreme match. b) It will be my first Title match and Finally.... It will be my first title reign. (James smiles at his arrogance. Then dismisses it with a wave of his hand.) James: Seriously though... Jimmmi, buddy... You and I are not that much different. Except for the fact that I am a rising star and that you are a fading light falling from the sky. I mean, eventually you and I will be at the same level as you fall right past me. I figure I will pass you on my ascent somewhere in the entrances and introductions Saturday night. I mean you and I use the same move to finish our opponents. Something has to be said for that... right? Granted you do it wrong... But I think that me being superiour to you has been established. (James smiles at his jest only to get more serious.) James: So... Zion, this Saturday... Me and You... Man to Mouse, respectively of course. (laughs lightening up) So, I've gotta wonder how your wife and my kid are doing? I hope she is feeling better, as she needs to be ready for my big party Saturday night. I am sure you won't mind is she is in attendance, heh I know she won't. Make sure she wears that cute little number with the flowers on it. I love that outfit. (James gets up from his chair and stretches. Then looks back to the camera.) James: I will probably chat with you again before Saturday Zion. But in case I have more important matters, like washing my @$$, I will see you Saturday. (Fade)
