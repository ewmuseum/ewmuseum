"Feel my rage in a cage."

23 Jan 2003

By Jimmi Zion

Item 1 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 23 Jan 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
*[Straight from the WWA archives. What happened when the cameras stopped rolling. My cameras started rolling.] *[Fade In] *[Houston, Texas] *[The Compaq Center] *[Fiesta] *[Cut to the backstage area following Jimmi Zion loss to Lockdown, and then the attack by The Cell to himself and his beautiful wife Mrs. Mikalia Rush-Zinn. We see a still injuried "Sky Flyin" Jimmi Zion helping to carry his injuried wife and manager along with a couple of security guards, a couple or WWA road agents, over to an EMT standing by. They set her down so the EMT can assess and tend to her injuries.] Another EMT: [Trying to assess Zion's injuries while the other one takes care of Mikalia.] "Here sir, let me take see if you're alright." Jimmi Zion: "Get your hands off me sunshine! Take care of Mikalia, I'm fine. Leave me alone and take care of here." EMT: "She's in good hands, we still have to take care of you." Jimmi Zion: "I don't need your bloody goddamn help, now get the hell away from me." EMT: "Sorry sir, of course." [While Zion looks on while the EMTs tend to his injuried wife. You can see the rage in his eyes building up everytime he looks at her. The sweat drips and pours off of his body. You can see a little bit of blood on his brow from where he has began bleeding again. A possible reopened wound. He wipes it off slightly to examine it. The catches a little bit of blood reflecting in his big brown eyes. Zion paces back and forth like a caged animal ready to attack at any moment. Finally a WWA reporter approaches him with his half of the WWA X-Treme/Cruiserweight title.] WWA Reporter: "Excuse me Mister Zion, but you forgot this out in the ring area." [Zion glares as he stares down at the championship gold starring back at him. He knocks it down out of the reporter's hand.] Jimmi Zion: "My half? My half? What do you know? I'm just a paper champion right now. I haven't done anything to earn that half...YET! I was supposed to be the man to beat Enigma and win the title. Not Chance. ME! Now, I am a co-champion? What the hell is a co-champion? Right now I am NO champion. Chance is the only champion. I never really accepted that ruling. I don't know if you noticed, but I don't deserve to wear that half right now. I lost tonight! I f[**]kin bloody lost. To of all people some guy named Lockdown. It doesn't matter if his name was Cockjaw. I lost to him, and then I couldn't do anything while they took my wife's head and spike her head down in a DDT on the concrete floor." [Zion glares as he turns full face and stares directly into the camera.] Zion: "Make NO mistake about it. Leviathan, Lockdown...when you attacked me that was one thing, but when you lay even a finger on my beloved wife, you just signed your death warrent. You can do whatever you want to me, but you never EVER touch my wife. You started this war, but I will take you both and your boss straight to HELL for what you did. Cruz, you will pay for making the order for your boys to attack my wife. You will reap the whirlwind. I'll hit you all with a lethal dose of EXTREME, and you won't know what hit you." [Zion backs off as he starts to calm down as he steps back allowing room to continue on, he changes the subject.] Zion: "Now, it's go time. Rage in a Cage is waiting in the rings. That's when Enigma, Chance, and Myself are locked inside a steel cage and we fight for the right to become the UNIFIED WWA X-treme/cruiserweight champion. There will be no such thing as co-champion after that night. There will be only one. Mark my words. That champion will be me. Now everybody thinks that I'm washed up. They think I don't have what it takes to compete in a WWA ring anymore. They think that I don't deserve to win this title. They think that Chance deserves to perservere just because of a few bumps and bruises, he should be champion. They think that Enigma will regain what WAS rightfully his. They don't think I can win it all again. I'm gonna prove how wrong they all are. I'm going to win it all. I don't care what the odds are. I will win. I will prove that I am the best of all the best in the entire WWA. This is my vow. A 12 foot cage. I don't give a goddamn if it's a 15 foot, 20 foot, hell I don't care if it's a 90 foot steel cage. I will succeed. Sometimes you fall before you fly, and I will fly high above the rest in the end. Mark my words, Sky Flyin Jimmi Zion will be the champion or I will die in Calgary-Alberta Canada." Zion: "Now Chance, let's start with you, my esteemed 'co-champion'. One thing I agree with Enigma on is I am NOT the co-champion. I got lost in the moment. I wanted to defeat Enigma and win the gold. Not you. As far as I am concerned. I am not the co-champion. You are the whole champion. However, I'm gonna do in that cage what I said I was gonna do all along. I'm going to beat you and beat Enigma to become the ONE TRUE CHAMPION. I will be the WWA Undisputed X-treme/Cruiserweight champion. This is my vow. I hold you in high regard Chance, but in that steel cage, you will have no chance in hell of beating Sky Flyin Jimmi Zion." "Enigma. You have said that you think that I am not the champion. I couldn't agree more. Right now I am not the champion. After Rage in a Cage, I will be the undeniable champion. I will remove all doubts from your mind. The truth will be more clear in your mind more than anything. You will know exactly that I am the man I have said I am all along. I am ready...willing...&...able to go literally to hell and back with you to become the best in this federation. I'm ready to be dragged all over. Even if I end up dying in that ring, it will be worth it if it means I become the true champion. I will prove you and every other doubter wrong. This is my vow. You will know who Mister Millenium is. You will know X-Treme Incarnate. You will know Sky Flyin Jimmi Zion." "You always end with NOTHING CAN STOP YOU. I end this by saying unto you Enigma... I can and I will FINALLY stop you! Now go f[**]k yourself and get ready for hell b[***]h!" [Zion motions to cut the camera out and that he is done talking. He walks back over to his beloved wife who is finally starting to come to. The camera fades out with the letters flash on the screen.] RELEASE THE RAGE WWA 2003 RAGE IN A CAGE LIVE From The Saddledome Calgary, Alberta, Canada Sunday, 2nd February 2003 *FADE OUT
