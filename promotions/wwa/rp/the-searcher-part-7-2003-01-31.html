The Searcher (Part 7)

31 Jan 2003

Author not credited in the source

Item 1 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 31 Jan 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Dirk Flint has been tracing 2 missing men for the WWA. One has been located, the other was last heard of in Moosejaw, Canada. Flint has followed the trail of clues to a Glue Factory. Flint walks up the drive towards the imposing building. Two burly men are leading horses from a lorry into the factory.) Flint: Hey! Is this the glue factory, buddy? Man 1: Sure is. Flint: What do you used the horses for? (The men smile at each other.) Man 2: We use them to pull the turbine engine. Flint: That's OK then, because you know it's illegal to use horses to make glue these day, don't you? Man 1: Us? Use horses for glue! Hahahahahahahahahahahahahahahahaha! Man 2: Hahahahahahahahahahahahahahahahahahahhahahhahahahahahahahaha! (The men stop laughing abruptly.) Man 1: You know it's veyr easy for snoopy stranger to be met with accidents in a busy factory like this. Man 2: Who are you? What do you want? Flint: I'm looking for this man. (He holds up a photo.) Man 2: Oh, the Boss's son! He's not here. Flint: The Boss's son?! Where can I find the Boss? Boss: Right here. (Flint turns round and a large fat bald guy with a bushu moustache is behind him.) Boss: Let's take a walk. First of, why do you want to find my boy? Flint: The WWA want him back. Boss: I know. Flint: You do? Boss: In fact, He's on his way to Calgary right now. Flint: So what am I doing here? Boss: I don't know, He's not missing anymore. Flint: So I guess my work here is done. Boss: Now quite. (Flint sighs, shakes his head and looks concerned.) Flint: What? Boss: There is still something you could do. Flint: I was hired deliver your Son to the WWA in Calgary. Boss: Exactly, the Boy took his Ford Cortina and he's broken down just outside Chicago. Flint: And you want me to go and pick him up? Boss: I think you should, or else he'll never make it to Calgary in time and then you won't get paid. Flint: Hold on, why is he anywhere near Chicago? Boss: He's never had a good sense of direction. Flint: I thought he was in the Marine Corp? Boss: He was, until he got lost on manuveres in the South Pacific. In fact, I think he's Officially still MIA! Flint: Well, thank-you for your help. I must go If I want to drive from here to Chicago and from there to Calgary before Sunday. Boss: Good luck, Kid. Flint: Thanks, buddy. (Fade to black.)
