Be-Heading

30 May 2003

By myZery

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The scene opens. It is dark and gloomy, there is a light breeze that makes the leaves rustle. Rats and mice are heard scurrying along the grass and the sound of the owl echoes throughout the field. The moonlight reflects off some type of metal frame. It is a long frame that is driven into the ground and then turns at the top. There is a rope hanging from the end with some type of knot in it. The camera zooms in to reveal a hangman’s noose. It is worn from being used so often and beside it is an old mask that the executioner would of worn. It is black and worn with two holes for the eyes. I walk into the scene wearing my black baggy jeans, black boots, my black hoody with the hood up which is partly covering my face, and over that I have my long black coat on. Around my waist is the Gen X title. It begins to rain slightly and I slowly walk up to the hanging area. Wolf’s howls are heard in the background. I slowly near the frame and rest my arm on it as I fiddle with the hangman’s noose. “This really brings back old memories. I used to be the one standing here. But not one of you would understand what I am talking about. I am like a lone wolf. Always seem to be alone no matter how much I try to convince myself I have anyone. No one cares for me, no one is there for me. The only people who give a damn about if I am breathing or not is my WWA fans. I have to go thru life thinking how much pain will I have to go thru today, but then I think to myself… how much pain am I going to cause! I am sick of being so gullible to actually think that someone actually cares, that my life is worth living. The only thing that means anything to me is wrestling. Wrestling is my life and there is just one question to ask after that. WWA, are you ready for Revolution? Are you able to cope the excitement? Can you even bare thinking about how much blood is going to be split in the WWA ring on Sunday? There is one hell of a line up. The WWA title is on the line. There is a King of the hill match including four powerful tag teams. There is a boiler room match thrown in there and even a Himalayan pit match! Has anyone ever heard of a casino match? You will know all about them at Revolution. But best of all there is a four-way elimination match for a shot at the TV title. This four way match is included the best of the new wrestlers here at WWA. Anyways it is going to be mental! I think to my self ‘What if I lose? What happens then?’ but then I realise that isnt going to happen. No one is capable of taking me out that I am facing. There is a little eminem wannabe called Tommy Starr. There is some holy freak called Tweak and to top it all off some guy that thinks he is a god! I can’t wait to step into the ring with these four rejects! Ah well, that’s life! I have fought in matches that are beyond your wildest dreams. I have lost and drawn so many litres of blood I have lost count. I am capable of much more then what you have actually seen of me. You don’t know me, you don’t know how I deal with my matches and no one ever will! Tommy Starr, you better watch your cocky arrogant back. I have been keeping an eye on you. I know how you work; I know exactly how you wish to operate at Revolution. But Tommy, I am going to have to spoil your plans. You are not going to win; you are not going to get a shot at the TV title. You are going to lose. You are going to be leaving on a god damn stretcher and that’s if your lucky because if I get things the way I want you will be leaving in a coffin! Reverend Tweak, what type of sick freak are you. You might call me a freak because I am original and I am different. But what type of sicko goes around calling himself a part of the church? I might not have that much of a religion but at least I have respect for whoever made this world. You go around acting as if you are a member of a religious order. With your little bible? Who the hell do you think you are? Is god going to save you at Revolution? Is that bible going to win you the match? No because not even a miracle would make you win! Prometheus, I have the most respect for you out of the three. I don’t know much about you and I don’t want to. All I know is that you are going to be the biggest challenge of this match. So just in case you think you can act like a fish and swim your way to safety, you are wrong because I am a great white shark and I am going to devour you on Sunday!” I kneel down as a raven lands on my shoulder. The raven squawks loudly. I then signal to the sky and it flies off into the midnight sky. “Boys, you do not understand what you are going up against on Sunday and if you do not realize this, you are going to suffer! Tweak, I am going to knock you out with the myZcue. Starr, you’re going down with the myZerable as Syn! And last but not least, Prometheus you are going to taste the canvas and your own blood when I hit the Traumatic Cerebral Stress on your carcass!” I walk over to the hanging mechanism and then I pick up the mask in my hand. “You all may think I am just your opponent, but I got news for you.” I slip the black worn mask over my head. “I am also your EXECUTIONER!” Scene fades to nothingness.
