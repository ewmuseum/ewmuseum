Look at me Doe

11 Feb 2003

By Damien Ryan

Item 1 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 11 Feb 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The screen opens up outside some facility. It is snowy out, a small blizzard, with about 5 inches of snow on the ground. The building is a wide one, brick outside. There is a small parking lot of cars, but it is just covered in snow. There is only one vehicle there and it is a navy blue pick up truck, with a snowplow on the front. Behind it we can see the trail it left to get through from the plow. Then on the bottom left hand side of the screen, words pop up: Ryan Facility Toronto, Ontario, Canada 10:17 AM The camera then fades to inside the building, with the camera making its way down the corridor. It moves through a set of double doors and finds its way into a large gym. The lights are out, but the light from outside glares in through the windows from the ceiling. Rays of light shine through, showing dust particles flying through the air, and leaving shadows on the gym floor from the rafter beams on the ceiling. The gym is full of different training equipment. There is one light on in the building and it is shining from another hallway. The camera moves over there, and sees it's a locker room. WWA's new superstar, Damien Ryan is standing in there with one foot on a bench tying a boot. He has long, snow pants on, but without a shirt yet, which is lying on the bench next to him. His hair is wet, and a towel is around his neck as if he had just gotten out of the shower. He looks up and sees the camera, continuing to tie his snow boots. "So, I make my WWA debut here in the great country of Canada. Like a hometown show for me. My very first match for this organization is only about an hour drive away from where I grew up, where I worked hard everyday to become what I am now. Ontario, Canada. The snow falls hard here, and nobody knows the snow better than I do, for I have trenched from here to Ottawa in the snow for training. At the age of eighteen, I was dragging weights from border to border, now I'm twenty-four and look what I am now. I was privileged to have the correct training, with the best trainers, who hailed from the best country," says Ryan putting on his shirt, with his muscle tone being revealed as he pulls the shirt up over his head. "Now WWA begins its Canadian tour, what a way for Canada's greatest superstar to sign with the organization. Now I can travel from city to city, in each providence, waving the Canadian flag around, and each and every person in that crowd will be cheering, because they know that one of their natives is in that very ring, not only to win, but defend their dear country! I don't have to worry about being yelled at, or threatened, or hated in Canada, because I am one their own. Yet when I head down to the States, I get ridiculed and screamed at, the lousy American, trailer park heroes shout their insults at me. Yet, if an American came up to Canada, we'd treat them with respect and welcome them into our lovely country." Ryan walks over to a locker and pulls out a heavy, winter jacket and a winter hat and carries them under his arm as he walks into a different door, with the camera following him. He turns on the light and it is a small break room, or kitchen. He opens up the refrigerator and pulls out a Molson and snaps the cap off and takes a large gulp of the Canadian brewed beer. He walks over to the table, tosses his jacket and hat on a chair and sits down to relax and enjoy his drink. "That Other Guy. What a name, huh? Well, I'll call you John Doe, since that's an easier way to go by. Well, John. Look at me; this is what your go up against on Saturday. Yes, I've had my share of matches in the past, yet I'm still fairly new. I went into FAW as a fresh rookie, but went off four consecutive matches in a row winning, won the Hardcore Title. AW, walked in and dropped two of their superstars like it was my job, put one of them fifteen feet off a zamboni. Now I'm here, after a short break of resting a neck injury. I'm fresh again, with one hundred percent ability to drop your sorry ass. You are stepping in my neck of the woods on Saturday, John. And when I'm in my country, I'm in charge of things and I'm at the best of my game. With all those fans cheering for me, I cannot go wrong, John, you're stepping into your own prison on Saturday." Ryan chugs down the rest of the beer then shoots it in a bucket near the refrigerator and gets up. He puts on his jacket and hat and leaves the kitchen. He walks out of the locker room, through the dark gym and to outside. Ryan makes his way through the thick snow and gets into the truck. He turns on the engine and rolls down the window to talk to the camera again. "Doe, you and the rest of WWA is going to the sense that maybe Canada is really better than the United States. Saturday night, I will head into Ottawa; walk into the Corel Center, then leave a winning Canadian." He closes the window and lowers the snowplow and drives his way through the thick snow as the camera fades out.
