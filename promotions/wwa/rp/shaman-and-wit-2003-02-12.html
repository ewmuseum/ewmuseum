Shaman and wit??

12 Feb 2003

By Shaman

Item 8 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 12 Feb 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
** Shaman is seen sitting on a bench in a park as a kid comes up and sits down and speaks with Shaman as Shaman just raises his head and looks on.** Kid: Hey Shaman how you going to beat Wainright?? [Shaman] "Im going to beat him like a three legged dog in a frisby contest!" Kid: A three legged dog?? [Shaman] " Not good enough how about a fenchmen in a round room to piss in a corner contest??" Kid: Huh what the heck are you trying to come up with?? **Shaman stops and rubs his chin as he begins to think for a few moments as the kid is pondering what Shaman was trying to say and begins laughing a bit.** Kid: I see what you were trying to say Shaman. Stick a frenchmen in a round room tell him to piss in the corner to win right? [Shaman] " Yeah that was what i was trying to get at." Kid: How about this one ok. There once was a man named Wainright...who couldn't even fly a kite...!?!?! [Shaman] " Whoa kid thats sick, But i like it! Don't say it on television though might get plenty of fines from it though." Kid: I think Wainrights ego is bigger than the WWE's Rakishi's backside! [Shaman]" Thats was cruel kid. I like it!!" Kid: Hmm how about this one Wainrights momma is so old she used a stick and two leaves as a tampon?? [Shaman] " Nah she isn't that old boy. She is only about as old as Abe linclns crusty underwear thats still hiding in her closet!" Kid: Well gotta go have a good day Shaman and i will look forward to seeing you on tv in Ottawa! [Shaman] " Yep have a good one yourself kiddo!" ** The next person walks by Shaman as Shaman seems to be in deep thought and then sits down next to Shaman poking him in the arm as he rudely interupts Shamans thoughts.** Guy: Aren't you Shaman?? [Shaman] " Yeah what do you want? " Guy: Just wanted to tell you that you suck and that Wainrights going to bury you at Fiesta! [Shaman] " Is that a fact." Guy: Thats damn right you old out of shape twisted lame ol freak! ** Shaman reaches down and grabs something off the ground and smiles as he sits back up.** Guy: ...And another thing your not as sick and twisted as everyone say you are...!?!?! ** Shaman suddenly shoves a pile of dog crap into the guys face and mouth as it goes up his nose as the guys seems to be in shock.** [Shaman] " Eat S!#@ !!" Guy: Holy S!#@!! [Shaman] " No its dog s!#@!!" Guy: Your a sick f#@& !!! [Shaman] " Yep thats sums it up in about four words!" Guy: Im going to call the cops. Hey police!! ** Suddenly Shaman clocks the guy as he falls off the park bench and falls face first into another pile of dog crap as a few ladies walk up as Shaman is seen thinking and not even noticing the man. The ladies look at the man in disgust as Shaman looks at them and then at the man and then speaks.** [Shaman] " He seems to have taken his study of s!#@ happens a bit too seriously hasn't he??" Woman: Yeah no s!#@! ** Shaman chuckles as the ladies walk away and Shaman gets up and walks away as the camera jogs and catches up to him as Shaman begins speaking.** [Shaman] " Wainright i trust your preparing for the enevitable possibility you will lose! Im preparing for the task of hurting you severely and making you bleed buckets full of blood. You see this Saturday i have a surprise for you. its called a surprise for a couple of reasons. Firast it is to surprise you plain and simple. Secondly its a surprise because not even the head guys are ging to expect me to do this! This shall be a new pinnacle for you wainright! The man with the plan, the master of ego, the one eyes one horned purple people eating freak is going to get a shot at destiny! Uhm yes thats you Wainright don't lose yourself here. Its not a safe place at all! Anyways i think you need to wake up and prepare for the painful task of trying to beat me as you state with ease. No Wainright Shaman thats me has a new plan for you. How can you prepare for me Wainright im not the same person i used to be!" ** Shaman stops and picks up two sticks as people are riding bikes and skating by as Shaman tosses one of the sticks down into the path of an oncoming skater as he watches the man trip over the stick and go crashing into some bushes and then turns quickly and shoves the stick into the spokes of a front tire as the man goes headfirst into the asphalt.** [Shaman] " Pure joy from that one. Makes me feel good. Hmm perhaps i will take somne joy in hurting you Wainright or i'll make it my mission to show you why Shaman is the Psychotic freak you claim to believe he is. Welcome to my world Wainright better pack a lunch its going to be a longday!" ** Shaman walks off as he steps on the two men fallen before him taking careful step one on the guys head and then next right onto his nads as he walks off whistling peacefully. As these words appear on the screen.** GOT BLOOD!!
