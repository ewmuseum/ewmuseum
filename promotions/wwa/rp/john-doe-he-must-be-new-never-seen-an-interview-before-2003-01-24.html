(John Doe): He must be new, never seen an interview before

24 Jan 2003

Author not credited in the source

Item 9 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 24 Jan 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
|The scene opens in a wrestling school, or at least in a plain gymnasium room with a wrestling ring. In the middle of that ring stands an ordinary guy, the man who could walk through an empty room and still not have his face remembered. A man who when in a crowd looks pretty much like everyone, and yet no one. You know, boring, normal, lame. But he stands there with a stoic expression on his face, looking beyond the camera, beyond the ring, as if he is looking ahead to something nobody else can see... his greatness to come, and nobody will see that because, let's face it, this guy doesn't come across as anything special.| Man: Yes, the future will be oh so wonderful for me. I see the stars glowing ahead of me as the past falls behind leaving me to move furthur into the greatness. |The man has a drawling voice, as if reading from a script or just having no emotions what so ever. You want him to shut up.| Someone needs to get this guy a nametag: I will shine brightly as a star in the WWA. I will defeat men such as Denrol and Tracer Bullet when they come to stand before me. But until I am great enough to do so I will stand here, in the ring alone. I will stand alone because the bottom of the WWA roster has no challenge for me. |You would think he would make that remark snidely, or snobbishly, but he still drawls on.| Hi, my name is:? : I know the WWA only invests its time in talented up and comers, but I can think of nothing of them because my future shines brightly even now. I have to force myself to listen to the boring- |Yeah, he's one to talk| - interviews of the other men simply so I know who my opponents may or may not be. I understand that the WWA has great talent, but I also understand that the confidence of my wrestling teacher means my talents are indeed great. Which means the WWA Bookers must work doubly hard to find for me a worthy opponent. I understand their hesitation, as I understand the hesitation of the talent to undertake battling me in this, the squared circle. |Shoot him, someone shoot him.| Man who needs to shut up: I will wait, because I know the WWA will undoubtedly find for me worthiness and talent which will be my stepping stones to the stars above, with whom I shall be forever renown. John Doe, the name will be famous. |Whether by decision of the man, or the passage of the camera man into sleep, the screen fades to black after the lens moves from the man to the floor.|
