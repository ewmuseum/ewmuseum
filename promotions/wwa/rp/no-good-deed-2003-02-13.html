No Good Deed

13 Feb 2003

By Truck

Item 4 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 13 Feb 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The camera begins to fade into view to a cloud of slow swirling smoke. A cough is head off in the background to the right of a camera. The drop of a quarter clinking and clanging it way through the mechanical workings of a jukebox sounds from off to the left. A loud dreary ballet from a Hank Williams begins to play as the camera slowly starts to pan down out of the smoke, passing pictures placed frame to frame on the wall. Down further, a line of booth tables comes into view, and a pool table in the foreground. A couple stands talking to each other between the camera and the pool table. Their words muffled out by the tune of the jukebox. The booth tables are bare except a lone table nestled in the dark corner. The jukebox almost hiding its view. In it sits a muscular figure dawning a familiar straw cowboy hat, hiding his face from the camera's view. The man in the corner picks up a bottle of golden labeled beer and sips it without revealing his face to the camera. The couple in the foregrounds words begin to get louder and louder. Finally the music can't withstand their discussion and it becomes background noise to the couples now fierce and heated argument. The tall slender man, standing with a pool cue in hand, stands as the girl spouts off obscenities at him. He fires back once she is finished. Boyfriend: JESUS CHRIST!!! IT'S BAD ENOUGH I GOTTA HEAR THIS CRAP ALL WEEK AND THE ONE NIGHT WE CAN GO OUT YOU CAN'T REFRAIN FROM BEING A BITCH JUST ONCE!!! Girlfriend: GOING OUT!!! YOU CALL THIS GOING OUT!!! YOUR SUCH AN ***HOLE. I'M GOING HOME!! The girl turn toward the door and manages to get to the jukebox when the boyfriend catches her arm and spins her around. She winces as he jerks her arm. Boyfriend: DON'T YOU WALK AWAY FROM ME!! DON'T YOU EVER WALK AWAY FROM ME!!! The dark figure in the corner raises his head and the cowboy hat releases the cold stair of who is now revealed to the camera as Mac Maloy. Girlfriend: **** YOU!!! The girl slings her arm out of the grip of her boyfriend. He drops his cue and grabs her again this time nearly jerking her arm out of the socket. As she spins around, the boyfriend takes his other hand and grips her other arm detaining her from exiting the bar. Mac springs out from the booth coming up behind the boyfriend who is near the jukebox. Mac reaches up and slings the man around. Mac: Why don't you take it easy on the lady!!! Boyfriend: WHY DON'T YOU MIND YOUR OWN DAMN BUSINESS COWBOY!!! Mac takes a step into the boyfriend the brim of his cowboy hat only centimeters from the boyfriend's forehead. Mac: I suggest you calm down before you do something you regret!!! Boyfriend: Oh, is that so!! The man slowly begins to turn back to his girlfriend as Mac takes a step back feeling he's diffused the situation. The boyfriend feigns the turn and come back around swinging a hard right roundhouse that plants itself on the left cheek of Mac Maloy. Mac's head snaps back then returns to it's original position. Mac stairs deeply into the eyes of his assailant who's eyes are wide in surprise that Mac is still standing. Mac ducks his chin turning his gaze to the ground. He puckers his lips and slowly lets a string of blood ridden spit trickle down from his mouth. The saliva spatters on the floor as Mac brings his eyes back to the boyfriend. Without hesitation the boyfriend swings again this time Mac quickly ducks under the roundhouse. Mac fires a hard right hand straight into the gut of the boyfriend doubling him over at the waist. Mac grabs the back of the boyfriend's shirt with his left hand and and the belt of the boyfriend's pants with his other. Mac spins the man around like a pinwheel and then drives the boyfriend's head into the jukebox. The music skips and scratches before finding its tracking again this time settling on a tune from Metallica's "Enter Sandman". The boyfriend falls to the floor on his back his head split open spilling blood down onto his face. The blonde girlfriend nearly shoves Mac into the wall as she rushes past him and crouches to the floor of her fallen lover. She moans words of sympathy to her boyfriend when she raises back to her feet and walks over to Mac. Girlfriend: YOU SON OF A BITCH!!! WHO DO YOU THINK YOU ARE!! Before allowing Mac to answer she swing her left hand and lands a firm slap across Mac's face. Mac's head snaps back again and he takes a step toward the girl. The bartender appears quickly from off camera and interrupts Mac from his destination. Bartender: Why don't you call it a night, Truck!! Come back tomorrow when you've sobered up a little!! Mac steps back from the bartender almost nodding a gesture of approval back at him. Mac reaches up and fixes the hat back to it's perfect position and then stairs back at the girl. Mac: Ungrateful Bitch!! Mac turns and walks past the jukebox to the exit door on the other side of the partition. He walks outside out of the camera's view. The bartender turns and helps the young girl gather up her puzzled boyfriend from the floor. Bartender: That's about the stupidest thing you could have done!!! The camera fades to black.
