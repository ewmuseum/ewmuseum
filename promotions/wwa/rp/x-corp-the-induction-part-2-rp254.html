X Corp: The Induction Part 2

18 Jul 2003

By Dave Harley

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[As the scene opens up we hear the sounds of 'Louie, Louie' blasting over a stereo. Dave Harley is stood wearing a Sea Captains hat and sunglasses. As the camera pans out, we see that he is at the helm of a large speedboat. sat in the back is Chance. He is wearing a brightly coloured Hawaiian Shirt and is holding a bottle of beer. Sat opposite him is Mal Somers, who is wearing a Pirate Hat and an Eye Patch. He is also holding a bottle of beer.] Somers: Yo Ho Yo Ho A Pirates Life For Meeeeee! Harley: How's he doing back there? Chance: He's still holding on. He's doing pretty well. I don't think I managed this long when I joined X Corp! [We see a line attached to the back of the boat. At the end of the rope, Rookie is being dragged through the water.] Somers: Arrr, he be a fine sailor me hearties! Harley: You think he's a member of X Corp yet? Chance: Nah. He will be by the time I've finished drinking his beer though! Harley: Good call! [Suddenly the boat hits a wave and all three men turn to check on their comrade.] Somers: Shiver me timbers! Man overboard! Chance: Don't you mean 'Belt Overboard'? MUAHAHAHA! Somers: ROOOOOOOOOKKKKIIIIIIIIEEEEEEEEEE!!!!! [And with that, Mal dives into the calm blue waters and starts to swim back towards his Golden companion.] Chance: You sure you're allowed to drive a boat whilst drinking? Harley: Sure, why not? Look at me, I'm as fiddle as a fit. Chance: You're what? Harley I said I'm as fit as a fiddle! Wonder where that expression came from anyways? Chance: I wonder what roster we'll end up on then? Harley: I would guess the Meltdown roster. i hear the TV Title will be a permanent fixture there. Let's face it, there's no way Lockdown will be able to beat me for it on Monday! Chance: Good point. Well, wherever we end up at least we know we're all going together. Harley: Yup. Let the good times roll! Chance: Have you told Mal that his match against Enigma and Sanders is now a Handicap Match? Harley: Not yet. He's bound to find out sooner or later! Chance: You want me to tell him? Harley: Nah, don't worry about it Dude. Besides, you've got the Main Event to worry about. Kash and Tracer Bullet. Man, that team would be sweet! Chance: I'm not on their team! Harley: Oh yeah...they must really suck. [In the background we see Mal clutching on to Rookie. We hear cries of 'Don't worry Rookie! It's gonna be okay! I've saved you!' Dave and Chance take a few swigs of beer.] Chance: Damn, I think we're out of beer. Harley: [Burps] Let's go get some. [Harley fires up the engine and shoots off towards the beach.] Somers: Guys! Wait for us! Guys! Guys? [As the scene fades out, you could swear you hear the Jaws Theme.]
