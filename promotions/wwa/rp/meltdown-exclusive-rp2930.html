WWA (1997–2019) Roleplays

Meltdown Exclusive

1 Feb 2006

By Khaled

Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Across a blank screen reads the words.) Footage You Weren’t Supposed to See (With blood dripping from his head, we see Mal Somers laying face down on the cold floor of the concrete car park … cinder smashed all around. After a few seconds the feed goes, and after around half a minute of darkness, the feed opens up again. The blood is still seeping from the head of Mal Somers, but rather then focus on that, we see a sadistically smiling Khaled standing victoriously over Mal.) Khaled: Do you see? (He shouts out for all to hear, his voice reeking of satisfaction.) Khaled: Do you see what is in store for you Mal! (Still out cold, Mal can’t move.) Khaled: Well you can’t see it, can you Mal. (Laughing to himself Khaled continues.) Khaled: But I’m sure somewhere in your subconscious you can hear me, so just to make sure these words get through to you I’m going to speak real slowly, because this is the last time we’ll be this close before Total Meltdown, and this is important. (With those words said Khaled takes a knee, while in the background we can see some road agents, almost a little too scared to get close.) Khaled: Now you’re probably unaware of this Mal, but I just smashed a cinder block over your head. Yep, that’s right, a cinder block. All those little pieces lie around your head … right now. Some of them are soaking up your blood. Yep, bad news Mal … you’re bleeding. (Khaled chuckles to himself before reaching down closer.) Khaled: But there’s some good news Mal, there’s a host of agents that have circled me right now, so unfortunately I won’t be able to finish the job tonight. No worries though because Mal, I’m really looking forward to our match at Total Meltdown. I’d call it the biggest match of my career, except for the fact that I don’t envision much of a match. (Again, in a very soft tone Khaled chuckles.) Khaled: No regrets though, because it doesn’t need to be the biggest match of my career to be the biggest moment. (His voice starts to get deeper and he gets up to his feet so everyone can hear him, although he still talks directly at Mal.) Khaled: Because ending your career will surpass ending the career of Leviathan. And it will surpass ending the career of Mick Vain. Simply put, ending your career is the biggest moment of my life! (The laughing become slightly unhinged as he backs away from Mal. Khaled continues to talk at him, as some paramedics go to check on Mal.) Khaled: Think about it. These people bare witness Mal. They bare witness to what I can do to you in an instant. Of what I am capable of Mal! They can see your blood and they can see my eyes and they know that I am capable of doing so much more. (The road agents start positioning themselves in between Khaled and Mal.) Khaled: So be prepared Mal, because at Total Meltdown, I’m not going to be hitting an arm bar on you or working over your leg, I’m just going to be battering you with anything I can get my hands on. It’s going to be beautiful Mal and it truly will be good to be Khaled! (He laughs as the road agents try to back him away. Khaled finally takes notice of the road agents as he walks away from the scene, laughing in their faces before laughing as he walks away.)
