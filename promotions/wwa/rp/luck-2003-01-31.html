Luck

31 Jan 2003

By Grunge

Item 6 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 31 Jan 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
FADE IN "Here's a token of my openness. Of my need to not disappear." (Grunge smiles as he takes off his headphones. Julie is walking through their small apartment as he talks.) Grunge-Pearl Jam No Way (He smiles and blows a kiss at Julie as she walks into their bedroom.) Grunge-I'm sick of drifting though people's lives and no one taking notice of me. Shaman, Nick Adams, Christyan Payne, all of them. Sure some people think I'm great while I'm around, and if I'm brought up in conversation, people say nice things but other than that, I don't really factor into anyone's equation (and that includes my own). Most would say I don't even belong in this match Sunday. Some say I'm not deserving of the TV title. (Grunge begins to smirk.) Grunge-One of my best friends, Rick Venom, once told me, "You know you'd make a good eunuch." He said this out of the blue in the middle of Dallas. No warrant, no reason. Now I understand. He's right. (Grunge looks out at the camera. He lights up a cigarette.) Grunge-I could spell it out for you in metaphors, if you think it would help. (He takes a few puffs and then puts it out.) Grunge-You have to know when to play your hand...But the key is to be dealt a hand to play in the first place. Luck and chance are such fickle theories. I hate them. I'd love to bind myself to the idea of synchronicity (and not the album by The Police, though it is quite good). However, current events dictate to me that synchronicity doesn't always occur. That luck and chance seem to have effects on the metaphysical principals that most of us ignore. I really have never believed in chance or luck. I do believe that these concepts are the result of our collective myopic vision of reality. These may be your opinions Adams, Payne and you Shaman. I don't care. (Grunge laughs.) Grunge-The key to working through whatever it is that is troubling me is to make note of what I sense, though what may be presented to me is in direct conflict. Sunday is all about conflict and well that title. Recognize my intuition, though "common sense" contradicts it. (Grunge gets up to leave.) Grunge- I need to wait for it. Wait for it. Wait for it... And...wait for it. (Grunge exits into his bedroom and closes the door as we begin to fade.) FADE OUT
