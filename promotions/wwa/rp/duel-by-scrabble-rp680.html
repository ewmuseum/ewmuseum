Duel By Scrabble

11 Nov 2003

By Dave Harley

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[We open on a scene that should be so familiar to us by now. Dave Harley and Mal Somers sit at opposite ends of a Scrabble board deep in word playing meditation. Suddenly Dave leaps from his seat.] Harley: HA! Beat that one Chairboy! [He picks up a handful of tiles and spells them out on the board.] C O N T I N E N T Harley: Oh yeah! The Extreme Machine rules the Scrabble seas! [He does a merry little dance to himself as Mal stares at the board. Suddenly a surprised grin spreads across his face.] Somers: Oh yeah? You think you’re tough huh? Well hows about this? [He adds a few letters to the start and end of Dave’s word.] I N T E R C O N T I N E N T A L Harley: You gotta be kidding! You’re cheating! Somers: How am I cheating? I used 7 letters, you used 9! You had 2 extra letters on your rack! Harley: I did not! And besides, don’t you think it’s a bit of a coincidence that you’ve spelt out ‘Intercontinental’? Somers: What of it? Harley: I thought we’d talked about this! Somers: What? The belt? Oh no, why would I be thinking of the belt? Hadn’t even crossed my mind! Harley: No Mal! Somers: Why not?! Think about it, a match the likes of which we used to have in the EWA. You and me, one on one. Think of the ratings! Harley: No! What about Chance? Or Jill? How are we ever gonna keep X Corp running if we start fighting over a belt? And what bout Venny? He’s like an extended member of X Corp. Somers: But it would only be in the spirit of competition! Just like a game of scrabble! Harley: You still got Rookie there? Somers: He’s sitting over there. Harley: Maybe it’s about time you listened to him. I’m sure he would agree with me! [Dave gets up and walks out. Mal glances over at Rookie, who sits silently on the coffee table.] Somers: Would he? [Fade out]
