Fun with Video

16 Jan 2003

By Lockdown

Item 3 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 16 Jan 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Lockdown is sitting in a director’s chair. Next to him is a 27 inch sony Trinitron television on a wooden stand.. Lockdown holds a remote in his hand.) Lockdown: Good evening and welcome to a very special episode of “Fun With Video” I am your host with the most, Lockdown. Today we are going to take a look at some of the clowns in the WWA that think they have what it takes to knock Yours Truly off the top of the food chain. Lets begin with an especially pathetic dumb bastard, Shaman. Roll it boys! (Lockdown points the remote at the television. It plays segments of Shaman’s promo from the January 15 episode of Insider) [Shaman] " Directly threatening me Lockdown shortens your life extremely. You are not the only man to ever grace the halls and cages of prison. No i have been there in my youth and guess what i hurt people who messed with me. You are nothing and even less in the eyes of myself. You wanna cross paths with me i will gaurantee you will get hurt in more ways than even your befuddled mind and concieve!! No Lockdown your claim to hurt and threaten has caused you to be forewarned. I will get to you when i deem it necessary as for now you are less than a knat an annoyance to ,yself and others!! You should be more concerned with whats going to happen to you in the near future. I do not play fairly. mess with me your dealing with your own funeral!!" Lockdown: My claim to hurt and threaten has caused me to be forewarned?? What the hell is that supposed to mean? They must not teach English on the reservation, which leads me to ask where did you learn English? Retard U? So you have spent some time behind bars, huh? For what? Drinking too much firewater and passing out in a puddle of your own vomit in the gutter? You hurt people who messed with you, huh. Good for you. I SLAUGHTERED people for THINKING about messing with me. I swear Shaman is so dumb, he’d trade Manhattan for a few glass beads all over again. Well, moving right along, our next subject is none other than Mr. Millennium himself. Soon to be dyin Jimmi Zion. First of all let me just say that the whole millennium hype is sooo 3 years ago. Jimmi had some interesting things to say yesterday on Insider. Let’s take a look. (footage is shown of Jimmi Zion’s January 15 segment of Insider.) Zion: …. Like you really scare me. Not even when you were beating me down did you scare me… Lockdown: Well, Jimmi, I’m glad you weren’t scared. But your bravery didn’t stop us from pummeling you now did it? Zion: …My knees didn't go weak on me… Lockdown: Lets pause it right there boys. Your knees didn’t go weak? Jimmy, I beg to differ. Lets take a look at what happened on fiesta. Is it ready, guys? Ok , roll it. (Footage of last week’s Fiesta is shown) Lockdown motions to Leviathan to hold Zion up as he rears back the chair… THWACKT! And brings it down forcefully on Zion’s head. Zion drops to a knee before Leviathan yanks him to his feet again. THWACKT! Only to have Lockdown smash the chair into Zion’s skull again. Lockdown throws the chair down and paintbrushes Zion across the face a couple of times before Leviathan throws him into the turnbuckle… Lockdown: Jimmi, you said your knees didn’t go weak, but it seems to me your knees buckled right after that first chairshot. But You’re probably right. It wasn’t your knee that was weak. Ok lets go back to Insider. Zion: …All I am is bloody… Lockdown: Gotta agree with ya there. Zion: I will show you what GRADE A...USDA...PRIME...X-TREME...INCARNATE...ARSS WHOOPIN is all about… Lockdown: Well, Mr. 3 years ago, why didn’t you show us last Saturday. I mean Hell, you got the first chairshot in. I’ll tell you why. It’s because you simply don’t have what it takes. You know it, We know it, hell the whole WWA knows it. Zion: …You better finish me off all the way. You have to kill me to beat me… Lockdown: Jimmi, what you don’t seem to understand is Killing IS my business, and business is good. [Zion falls on his knees and does a mock prayer… Lockdown: OOHH! See that? Your knees just went weak again. Zion: "This is what will happen. I will just keep getting up and keep coming at you… Lockdown: AHA!! So you admit that I will put you down over and over again! See Jimmi, even when you try to talk tough, you know deep down that you cant handle us. Zion: I'm like Superman only I don't have any Kryptonite weaknesses. Lockdown: No Jimmi, you’re not like superman just yet, but after Fiesta, you will be. Just like him… In a wheelchair!!!! Fade out
