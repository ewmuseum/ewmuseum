"Finally my time has arrived."

17 Feb 2003

By Jimmi Zion

Item 4 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 17 Feb 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
*[Fade In] *[Kelowna, Brittish Columbia Canada] *[Sometime during the morning time] *[Cut to the interior of a hotel room penthouse suite over looking the city below. The WWA camera crew reveals WWA X-Treme and Cruiserweight champion, "Sky Flyin" Jimmi Zion. He is sitting on the large leather sofa polishing his two coveted titles with polish and a rag. While he is polishing he glances up through his long dreadlocks hanging down slightly over his eyes. He pushes them out of his face to address the camera. Lets listen in.] "Sky Flyin" Jimmi Zion: "Well done Joey K. The former superhero gave me a run for these belts. You prove to me the reason why I am the X-Treme and Cruiserweight champion. Oh I'm sure you'll probably say I got lucky like Enigma does, ask anybody in this business and they'll tell you the same thing that it doesn't matter if you win by an inch or a mile, a screw job or a squash...winning's winning. The way I see it Joey K dug his own grave with LeTrav so he deserved to get screwed in the end out of his title win. Well at least I don't have to worry about fighting Joey K again for a while because LeTrav will be more than enough on his plate." "However, I gotta speak on Vinny LeTrav. I almost appreciate you helping me keep my titles and all, but quite frankly I'm not thankful. If you want me to say thank you for helpin me. Okay You've got it Vinny. Thanks...FOR NOTHING! All I'm going to say to you Vinny is that in the future you stay out of my life and I'll stay out of your's. I didn't ask for your help, and if I wanted it I wouldn't asked for it. Guess what I didn't ask for it so next time know your role and mind your own bloody business. Stay away from me. If I'm gonna lose my title then I'm gonna lose it...on my own. If I'm going to win it, then I'm going to win it on my own. You got that Vinny? I hope so because I don't want to repeat this issue with you or anybody again." "Now onto more pressing matters." [He pauses while he listens to the camera man ask him a question.] Zion: "What? What about The Cell? Well I'll worry about them in due time. Right now the Cell are the least of my worries. I am a fighting champion. I have two issues that concern me. One is my soon-to-be, yet to be named match date with Chance for my X-Treme/Cruiserweight titles. They haven't set a date but when they do I will be more than ready for you Chance. It won't be luck. I don't care what Enigma or you call it. I call it skill. Forture will not smile upon you this time and I will finish what I started just a few months ago Chance. I will prove to you that I am the dual champion for a reason. I don't care what people think. I earned these champions and when they book our match I will do what I almost did before Enigma threw himself in out business. I will prove to you why I am the best. Lady luck will not even recognize you after I'm through with you. Simple as that. I can't wait until I fight you so I can take care of this unsettling feeling in my heart of who is the better man one on one between you and I. I will prove that I am the better man." "Now about my match for this week. It may be a NON title match, but it is just as big. For two reasons; one, it's a main event on Fiesta. It's the main event. Two, it's a best two out of three fall match between Mal Somers and Me. That means that I get to kick Mal's bloody arse 2 maybe three times in one single night. Now that is a luxery in itself. I respect Somers, but nothing more. Somers and I have one common enemy, we both hate and want to kill the Cell. We're both champions. That's a treat for everyone, seeing the best of the WWA climb into the ring and give it their all three matches in one night. All I gotta say is one thing...ZION TIME IS OFFICIALLY HERE IN CANADA! Hell I wanna say Zion Time's going global, but I'll take things one step at a time. Mal Somers will know what it means when I reiterate being X-Treme incarnate, having violence encoded into my DNA. I am the living, breathing, eathing, drinking, highlight reel. I am the immortal one. I am the champion of champions, and on Fiesta I will prove it to. How do I know, well for one thing I'm the guy who STOPPED ENIGMA, and I will stop you. Get ready for Fiesta because I know I am." [Zion goes back to putting on the final touches on the shine of his belt when he hears the flushing of a toilet come from the next room. The door opens up and his wife and manager, Mrs. Mikalia Rush-Zion walks out. She is wearing only a long XX-large black Invinciblewear.com shirt engulfing the smaller petite english vixon, no pants, and white laced underwear. Her long black hair is down. She looks like she is ill holding her stomach, she walks over to Zion and lays down on her side with her head on Zion's lap. Zion gives her a kiss on the forehead.] Zion: "Good morning sleepy head. You feel alright? You don't look so good baby." Mikalia: "I'm fine, but I think I may of caught some sort of flu bug or maybe it's some sort of left over air sickness, maybe it's jet lag or something." Zion: "I don't know, but you feel a little warm. Do you need to go see a doctor?" Mikalia: "Oh I'll be fine. I just need some rest I guess." Zion: "Will you be alright for my big match on Fiesta this week?" Mikalia: "The biggest match of your entire career up to this point. This match could be bigger than your title win at Rage In a Cage. Baby I wouldn't miss it for the world." Zion: "Good because I don't know what I would do if you weren't at ringside. Like I said, you bring out the best in me. Liability my arss, they know nothing." Mikalia: "Yeah." Zion: "You want me to call up something for breakfast?" Mikalia: "Yeah could you?" Zion: "For you anything." [Zion gives Mikalia a kiss on the lips but she stops him suddently.] Mikalia: "Not on the lips baby, if it's a virus, I don't want you to get what I've got. Afterall we don't want to give Somers any unfair advantages." Zion: "Even if he did have that unfair advantage, like it wouldn't have mattered. I could be Helen Keller, blind and deaf and still would beat him. Not even death himself could keep me from winning this match. Now how about breakfast?" Mikalia: "That't would be great." Zion: "Alright, let's try this again." [Zion gives Mikalia another kiss on the cheek and forehead before he slides off the couch and walks to the telephone. Mikalia just remains there quietly holding her stomach looking up at the ceiling. A look of concern can be seen in her big brown beautiful eyes. The camera leaves Mikalia and Jimmi making it's way toward the direction of the bathroom. It walks in scanning for signs of clues until it glances inside the wastebasket and finds something of interest. It finds the crushed box of a home pregnancy test laying in the trashcan. The camera fades out on this sight.] *[Fade Out]*
