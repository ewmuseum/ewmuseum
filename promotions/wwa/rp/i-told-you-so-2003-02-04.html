I told you so

4 Feb 2003

By Grunge

Item 1 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 4 Feb 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
FADE IN (The screen is completely dark. David Bowie’s voice pierces the darkness.) "You're too old to lose it, too young to choose it And the clocks waits so patiently on your song" (Eventually the darkness fades into a image of Grunge sitting in a folding chair. He’s dressed in his colors of red and white. He holds his newly won TV title over his shoulder, stroking it. Have you ever seen Desperado? It’s like the quote, “he held it like a woman.”) Grunge-David Bowie Rock 'N' Roll Suicide. I’m not really the type to say I told you so but I did. I told all of you. I told you I was the rightful champ and guess what? I was right. Grunge-Shaman, Shaman, Shaman. You didn’t listen to me did you? Did you think everything I said was crap? When I said I was going to win I knew it. I had a plan all along. Have pity on Shaman, the frozen man. He’s cold product. (The camera focuses in on the new champ and he smiles while codling the belt.) Grunge-Nick, Rainmaker, I got to give you the props. If was a matter of seconds with you. Photo finish. If you had been only a bit faster… (Grunge snaps the fingers of his free hand.) Grunge-You would be sitting here with this belt and for that I salute you. (Grunge gives a army salute, quickly raising and moving his hands.) Grunge-Of course that’s no surprise. You were the only one I was really concerned about. Christyan Payne, what Shaman did in breaking you, a thing of beauty. But what I did was worse. I showed the world you aren’t what you think. Your no puppet master, just a broken marionette, a little wooden boy with no strings. Bring that camera in Mike. (Mike does as he says. The camera moves close into Grunge’s face.) Grunge-Something else happened at Rage in the Cage I want to address. Tiffani, you screwed up. I know Rick has…his problems and I have never had any desire to interfere in your personal life. This is a warning gold dust woman. You take up with vipers and you will dig your own grave. I wipe my hands of you. (Grunge looks off.) Grunge-Hmm, I thought I had something to say, something deep. But I don't. The music is fading out. (And at that…) FADE OUT
