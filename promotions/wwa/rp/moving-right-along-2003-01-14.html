Moving Right Along

14 Jan 2003

By Shane Dorian

Item 4 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 14 Jan 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Scene opens to Shane Dorian, sitting in fron of a WWA backdrop. in the old days of wrestling, to voice their opinions they would sit in front of a poster featuring the company's logo. That's all they needed to get their point across. No flashy locations, and no guest cameos. Shane fancies himself a no-nonsense type of wrestler, hence his old school approach to the promo. He is dressed in a t-shirt that hyped the return of Tracer Bullet from Trent LeBleaux pergatory oh so many months ago. Shane's hair is neatly slicked back, and it is apparent that he has a new pair of 'burns. Dorian strokes his chin and begins to address the Insider crowd.) Shane Dorian: Tracer, week after week, I have made you the most ridiculed man in the World Wrestling Alliance. I have bloodied you, and left you for dead on so many occasions, fans feel like they're watching the same Meltdown every week. When are you going to step things up? (Shane seems a bit annoyed at the seemingly easy time he's had with Tracer.) Shane Dorian: The first week, you collapsed, the next, in a battered stupor you challenged me an I Quit match, and the most recent confrontation ended in you shackled to the fan's barrier while a washed up Mr. Cool was put out by your's truly. I gotta tell ya, Tracer, when will you give me you're all. I know you are not used to dealing with one person, but you'd think that after a year of duking it out with Denrol, Jackson Kraven, and Craven Moore, I should be a breeze. But week after week, the fans are left scratching their heads asking "How come Tracer Bullet is bleeding so much?" Now, next week will be by coup de grace. I get to manhandle Tracer one more time before I make him scream "I quit", and I finally get to have a shot at that lying piece of sh!t, Damien Thorne. Thorne, when you conned me into joining the HoP you filled me with delusions of granduer. You told me behind closed doors that this would be the best for my career. That I would be the biggest thing since New Star. Well, you lying sack of arse, you told a half truth. And now, I'm going to tie up those loose ends. Kash might have to watch his back too, because I'm not opposed to knocking him on his Shaman-wannabe ass to get to you. So this is to all three men that will be in the ring with me next week; be prepared for anything. (Screen fades to black.)
