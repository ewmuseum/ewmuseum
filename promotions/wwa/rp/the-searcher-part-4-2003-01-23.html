The Searcher (Part 4)

23 Jan 2003

Author not credited in the source

Item 5 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 23 Jan 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The world's 2nd best Private Investigator, Dirk Flint, is on the case! He's been hired to find a missing person. The hunt has taken him to New Orleans, Bolivia, Brooklyn and now finally to Lincoln, Nebraska. He found a leaflet for Gentle Hills Hopsital for the Mentally Insane. The camera follows his '56 Caddilac as it pulls up to the large grey building. Strangely there are no hills and it is a decidedly ungentle atosphere as two burly men drag a skinny guy kicking and screaming back inside. Flint enters and saunters up to the attractive receptionist, her name tag reads Cindy.) Flint: Hi Cindy! Cindy: My name is Nadine, I just borrowed thi badge. Flint: Hi Nadine! Nadine: Hello, welcome to Gentle Hills, how can I help you? Flint: I am a Private Investigator, I'm looking a missing person. I have reason to believe he is currently in this very institution! Nadine: Really? (Flint passes his file on the man to Nadine. She looks at it and frowns.) Nadine: You know, I think he is here. Hold on, I'll just go check with Dr Kahn. (Nadine walks out of the reception area and Flint sits down on a little plastic chair to wait. He leans back but the rickety chair almost falls over so he sits up straight. He picks up a magazine, Just 17, October 1989. He flicks through the various articles, about Kylie Minogue and Bad Boys INC. Finally he is saved from this horrendous tedium by the return of Nadine and a man, who is Dr Kahn.) Kahn: Mr Flint? Flint: I am that man. Kahn: This way please. (Dr Kahn starts walking down a murky corridor and Flint hurries to keep up.) Kahn: This man is a very interesting case, he's a John Doe, we have no idea who he really is or where he comes from. You probably know more about him than we do! Flint: Why is he here? Kahn: This is a hospital for the Mentally Insane, your man here has a very serious condition. Flint: What's wrong with him? Kahn: Basically it manifests itself in a number of ways, regular chronic bouts of short-term dementia, especially in stressful situations, occassional hallucinations, short-term memory loss and an almost constant state of confusion with only short bursts of lucidity. Flint: That sounds like my man! (Dr Kahn stops at a door.) Kahn: This is the door. (Kahn unlocks the door and pushes it open. The room is dark but in the shadows a figure is just visible. Flint peers at the man and checks his folder.) Flint: That is the man! That's him! Kahn: Good, I'm glad to hear that! There are some very large bills to be paid on his behalf. Flint: No problem, My employer will pay them. Kahn: By the way, Mr Flint, who was it that hired you? Flint: I'm afriad I can't say but I do need to take him with me. He needs to be in Calgary on Sunday night. Kahn: Calgary on Sunday but that is Rage In The Cage! Is he a wrestler? Flint: I can't say. Kahn: But he is Mentally Insane, you can't put him in the ring, he could seriously injure himself and others! Flint: I think that is the idea! (Flint walks in and takes the man by the hand, we still can't quite make out who it is.) Kahn: Well, he's your responsibility now! Flint: No, he is the WWA's responsibility! (Flint leads the man down the corridor, as he passes the camera we catch a glimpse of his face. Not quite long enough to be sure but the face is familiar, very familiar and it bring 3 words to mind....Those 3 words will be revealed at the PPV. Fade to black.)
