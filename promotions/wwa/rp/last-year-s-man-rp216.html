Last Year's Man

1 Jul 2003

By Grunge

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
FADE IN Grunge: It seems Levi is trying to make me scared… (We focus into the scene to see Grunge sitting in a computer lab at the University of Oklahoma. He’s reading Sun Tzu’s “The Art of War.”) Grunge: Figured I might as well do some boning up on the subject since Enigma has been babbling on about this for some days. And of course he is one of the favorites of the Rumble. (Grunge nods to Dusk who is sitting over at the next table reading.) Grunge: So I'm sitting here because I moved this weekend and I'm not able to sign online. I just can’t live without the Internet. I forgot to call and schedule the appointment to have my Cox account transferred. (Grunge points over to Dusk.) Grunge: There is a gentleman to my left at a far table. He's been sitting in the same position since I got here...reminds me of Last Year's Man by Leonard Cohen. "An hour has gone by and he hasn't moved his hand..." (Grunge laughs but of course the Liberian shushes him to be quiet.) Grunge: He’s a lot like you Levi, the way you were when you were locked in the motel room with a rather large tiger. (Grunge laughs again and once again gets shushed by the Liberian which is making Grunge a bit angry by now.) Grunge: For a while I thought you were dead. “Should I go poke him with my pen, or no?” That was the question. For to poke him with my pen...blah blah...referencing Hamlet and lo, he moves. And then with the help of your lackeys you escaped! The man I feared had felt death's wintry kiss is ALIVE! Blessed by thy Dell that I sit near. (Grunge runs his fingers across the computer keyboard.) Grunge: Heh...and this is what's passing for entertainment in my mind... (Grunge then does his swivel chair around and faces the Liberian.) Grunge: Of course that may have been funny Levi but your comments about my wife, not so much so. You want revenge at the next IDR. Well you can try and come get it. But I tell you this… (The camera closes in on Grunge’s face.) Grunge: …and this goes for everyone. I am going to win this thing. If I have to move Heaven and Hell itself I will win. (Grunge smirks.) Grunge: Rock and roll baby. (And at that we begin to…) FADE OUT
