Krazy Golf

16 Oct 2003

By Dave Harley

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[A golf ball rolls towards a monkey’s mouth. Weren’t expecting that for an opening sentence were you? It’s a big steel monkey. Its mouth is opening and closing slowly as it lays on the green. We zoom out a bit to reveal a sign that reads ‘Mad Monkey’s Krazy Golf!’ That would explain it then…] Somers: WOO! Eat that ball you crazy monkey! [The ball rolls down the monkey’s throat.] Somers: Score one for the Malster! Harley: your turn bud. [Dave nudges Venny LeTrav, who looks pretty embarrassed to be out with Dave and Mal on a miniature golf course. He steps up and takes a shot. The ball rolls down the green and hits the closed mouth of the monkey.] Harley: Unlucky Dude. It’s all about timing! Venny: [Sighs] This is so stupid! Why am I stood here playing golf with you half-baked idiots?! Harley: We’re not idiots, we’re X Corp! Besides, as the newly crowned WWA GenX Champion I need to show you how to take proper care of your title. Venny: By playing mini-golf? Harley: [Shrugs] It’s what I did! Look how successful my run was! We all need a little relaxation. You’ve got a big IC Title match on Monday, Mal finally gets his hands on Wes Sanders and I get a shot at the World Champion! Venny: Why can’t you just leave me alone? Why did you take me to that arcade and make me look like a complete jackass in front of that clown? Somers: Relax Champ! Consider this a prize for winning the Tokyo Showdown! Venny: [Mumbles] great… I'd just LOVE to see what you get me for Christmas. Dave: Oh, that'll be a BIG surprise. (sniggers) Somers: Oh, yeaahhhh... Now take another shot at that monkey! [Venny looks down at his golf ball with utter contempt. He swings as hard as he possibly can and the ball launches at breakneck speed towards the mouth of the monkey. But timing is once again against our GenX Champion. The ball ricochets off the monkey and whacks Dave Harley straight in the mouth.] Harley: AAAAAARRRRRRGGGGGHHHHH!!! Somers: BWAAAHAHAHA!!! WRONG MONKEY!!! AAAAAAAAHAHAHAHA!!! Venny: Umm…sorry Dave. Guess I need to chill out a bit. [Venny steps aside and lets Dave set up his ball. His lip has swollen up. Mal can hardly breathe from laughing. Dave swings and the ball rolls straight down the monkey’s throat…only it doesn’t come out the other end.] Harley: Dammit! Where the hell is my ball?! Somers: AAAAHAHAHAHA!!! Harley: Shut up! Venny: Go have a look down the other end Dave, I’ll see if I can poke it through with my club. [Dave goes around to the back of the monkey and peers through as Venny wedges open the monkeys mouth and jabs his golf club through. Unfortunately for Dave, it goes right through to the other side and takes out his eye.] Somers: BWAAAAAAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHA!!! Venny: Jeez Dave, are you OK? Dave? [Venny looks over the monkey’s head and sees an unconscious Dave Harley laying on the green. He turns to Mal to get some help, just in time to see Mal pass out from the pain of laughing at Dave. Venny stands alone.] Venny: To hell with this! [He throws his golf club in the air and heads off in the direction of the exit. We see the golf club fall behind the monkey and bounce off something hard.] Harley: (muffled) Oof!
