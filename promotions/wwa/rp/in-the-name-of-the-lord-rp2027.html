In The Name Of The Lord

3 Dec 2004

By Odium

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
{My crusade has brought me here. My willingness to be subjected to your will, Lord. I have traveled through the Great Plains, through the Mason-Dixie Line, through the Everglades…all for you, my savior.} . . . . Somewhere in America, a streetlight comes to life with a dim, yellowish light. The buzzing…infuriates me. Talks to me, while I find safe haven beneath the shrubbery. {I know, Dear Lord, that you move in ways so mysterious, so beguiling. So much more, than a mere mortal could ever know. Know? To know…I know the chants still ring in my ears. Bastard. Freak. They’re all the same just with more syllables. They call me brutal. They say that I am wicked, they call me wretched…when it is they who are the lost.} Somewhere in America, a lone tomcat prowls the lonesome streets and back-alleys of a quieted neighborhood; scrounging for scraps. Just trying to live in this insipid world, that is until something more interesting comes along. A scent captivates the tabby, bringing his inquisitive mind and tickling his scene of curiosity. Instinctively, the tabby indulges as animals do, without the bonds and constraints of society. What one SHOULD do…what is acceptable. {Still they judge, Dear Lord. Have not they learned the lesson? Have not they understood that they are no better than I, that they have no right, whatsoever, to even THINK about passing judgment, while their computers are laden with pornographic images of children and their minds rapt with cruel intentions. Then, they have the audacity to drag my good name, my irrevescent cause through the manure-filled fields. Oh God, why am I the only one who sees your vision? Why am I the only one guided by your warm hand?} Quietly, the tabby trots across the dimly lit, quiet street – following his nose, his impulse. The calico, cautiously, moves toward the large shrub that sits under a window, alive with light. The young feline pauses, refreshing the scent in his mind. Meanwhile, a group of storm clouds begin rolling overhead. [KRAK-THOOM!] {Heh, I know why you have chosen me, dear Lord. It’s not because of my sense of style, nor my astounding mental acumen. It’s because I know that I know nothing…because I am weak and it is you, Lord, who makes me strong. It is you that I live my life for…not any man-made idolatry. No feeble dreams or man-made substance that fills my veins, and makes my blood pulse. Just you, God, only you.} {I smell your sorrow, Father. It is rapt in this world, created by your hands. I feel your displeasure, Father. You are angry with your creations. They tarnish your image, and your grace…and it is I, who will show the error of their path.} [KRAK-THOOM!] Somewhere in America, the rain begins to pelt the hot pavement. Washing the world clean of any transgressions, any injustices and any wrong doings…except one. A wailing sequence of a cat’s meow begins to ring out amid heavy falling rain and dark neighborhood, before long the sounds of an impetuous rustling of leaves are heard. Next comes, the solemn, dreary echoes of slow, sloshing footsteps begin to sound underneath the coming thunderstorm. (splish…slosh) (splish…slosh) (splish…slosh) {Is this where you want me, Father?} . . . Somewhere in America, a lonely and confused young man by the name of Mal Somers moves about his apartment, dulled by the sounds of a loud television and confusion reigning his head, not knowing there are eyes upon him. Watching his solemn expressions, following his footsteps as he positions himself before a trophy room. A room laden with statuettes and displayed golden and leather straps. He knows not that his tears are being taken in as someone else’s. He knows not that as he begins to slice the packaging tape on the ends of a small parcel box…his destiny is being gauged. He doesn’t know that as he holds another golden strap, this one grandiose and more extravagant than the others, in his nubile arms that someone is waiting for him, waiting for him to come to Birthday Bash. He wants the golden strap. His mouth, almost, salivating at his task at hand. His chest, heaving with anticipation. His biceps, almost, rigid with excitement…He doesn’t know that his pain has become someone else’s pleasure. Somewhere in America, Mal's cries will be answered. Though, it’s quite possible, it may not be the answer he was seeking.
