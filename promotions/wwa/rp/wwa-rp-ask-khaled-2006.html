WWA — Roleplay

Ask Khaled

5 March 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
5 March 2006 Stealing a page from everyones book ... ask Khaled (in character of course) anything you want. Be warned of evil responses! ---- 5 March 2006 I have heard that you have been with the WWA for quite some time and that your biggest claim to fame is that you are the longest reigning member of the WWA roster to never win a title. Why is that and do you think that you will ever 'lost' that accolade? -Rick James (former PWW reporter) ---- 5 March 2006 Why is that? Are you implying that its because I cant win the big one? That I choke? Because Rick you should have seen Total Meltdown where I beat Mal Somers 1 ... 2 ... 3! The fact is I could be a champion. Time has gone by where maybe I wasn't ready, maybe there was something missing. Now there isnt. I step into the ring with anyone, champion or chump and the end result is going to read 'Khaled wins'. And in answer to your question I could be a champion this week ... on Meltdown. I go up against Jonathon Winters and I draw you back to January where I was a referee screwing me away from taking his precious little belt away from him. Yep indeed, its looking pretty likely that 'accolade' as you put it is going to be lost very soon. ---- 6 March 2006 Who is the toughest opponent you've ever faced? Who is the most bitter rival you've ever made? What is your favorite type of match? Who, other than yourself of course, do you think is the greatest WWA superstar of all time? Will you have some sort of over the top and wild celebration when you finally add that first title win to your WWA resume? ---- 6 March 2006
Who is the toughest opponent you've ever faced? Who is the most bitter rival you've ever made? What is your favorite type of match? Who, other than yourself of course, do you think is the greatest WWA superstar of all time? Will you have some sort of over the top and wild celebration when you finally add that first title win to your WWA resume?
The toughest guy I ever faced ... well to be honest I'll give him some credit, but I had a match with Mal Somers a few weeks back and the things we done to each other ... well we'll never be the same. Nobody has ever done to me what he did ... but I still walked out victorious. My most bitter rival ... I'm looking at Leviathan. He pushes from the top of the WWATron, he acted as a catalyst for everyone turning on me by helping Harley winning the World Title, and then he turned and decided he wanted to steal my spotlight. So I pushed him 30 feet to the concrete floor, but the son of a bitch still got up. So I went after his family, and he retired ... but if I know him one day he'll be back, and thats what makes it the greatest rivalry of mine. My favourite type of match? Well to be honest I work for the World Wrestling Alliance. And I'm perhaps the most accomplished technical wrestler in the WWA. I don't need fancy weapons and gimmicks to end careers, I just need my hands and a referee to tell me when to stop. Give me a one on one straight singles match and I'm a happy man. Other then myself, the greatest WWA superstar is none other then Ember. In one year he's shown me the error of my ways, he's made the kind of impact people can only dream about, and he's won the World Title by beating everyone at the Birthday Brawl. The WWA has never seen a wrestler like him When I win my first title ... I don't really know how I'll react. To be perfectly honest there is so much more to me that even though a lot of wreslting dirtsheets focus on it, its not something at the forefront who I am. When I win, we'll just have to wait and see how I react ... so I'd advise you to tune in to Meltdown this week when I go up against Jonathon Winters for the US Title if you want to see how I celebrate becoming Champion. ---- 7 March 2006 Is it true that you force all new WWA recruits to play your kinky bondage games? ---- 7 March 2006 Are you responsible for there being no t&a on WWA's shows? Well? ARE YOU? Grrrrr. ---- 7 March 2006 Actually to go along with Argyle's question I'd like to know what you think about there being no female wrestlers in WWA. ---- 7 March 2006
When I win my first title ... I don't really know how I'll react. To be perfectly honest there is so much more to me that even though a lot of wreslting dirtsheets focus on it, its not something at the forefront who I am. When I win, we'll just have to wait and see how I react ... so I'd advise you to tune in to Meltdown this week when I go up against Jonathon Winters for the US Title if you want to see how I celebrate becoming Champion.
I just want to point out that not only are the Deadly Alliance - myself and Khaled - the most dominant force in the WWA today but also the most prohpetic. Before Birthday Brawl I guarenteed that I would walk into the PPV, face the whole roster, and walk out as champion. I did that. Then we have Khaled sttating that everyone would see how he celebrated his first title win after taking the US title from Winters. He did that. Our time is here and now.
Are you responsible for there being no t&a on WWA's shows? Well? ARE YOU? Grrrrr.
I suppose that you are one of those people that would rather see no talent, silicone enhanced freakshows take centre stage rather than watching pure wrestling excellence in action? You know I feel so sorry for you that I may just send you a couple of dollars so that you can buy yourself the latest issue of Playboy and then you can go and amuse yourself in the confines of your own bathroom!
Actually to go along with Argyle's question I'd like to know what you think about there being no female wrestlers in WWA.
What is it with you guys? The WWA has some of the top talent in the industry - and by that I do indeed mean myself and Khaled, the WWA World and US champions respectively - and you are worried about giving your right wrist some exercise? Though, thinking about it, the WWA actually does have some female talent - Mal Somers and Jonathon Winters are our bitches anyway. ---- 7 March 2006 As for my Deadly Alliance partner said ... the fact is why waste time on no talent whores when you can spend it with top talent such as myself and Ember? The WWA doesn't need women, it needs guy like me and Ember ---- 7 March 2006 Are you being Khaled by proxy or something Ember? :smiley: ---- 8 March 2006 Intresting. Is that why those no talent "whores" like Karina do better than you super powerful athletes in PTC events? I believe the real answer to the question of why there are none in WWA is that the easily fragile egos of men like yourselves simply couldn't handle having a woman whip your ass. ---- 8 March 2006 How does it feel to be the chump of the most useless World Champion in WWA history? Oh, and congratulations on your US Title win. How long did it take you to get a Title? ---- 8 March 2006 Dave ... are you still alive? I thought you did the whole world a favour and put us out of our misery by ending it once and for all. But don't worry Dave, you decide you want to play with the big boys then maybe the Deadly Alliance will give you a helping hand on your quest to the next life. And in regards to women ... Katrina whatever her name is ... well if I got in the ring with her I'd just lay down. I'm a man ... I don't get my kicks and build my reputation off beating women. The fact is this is a mans game, and in this mans game theres no room for novelty acts or for equality. Thats the way it is, and thats the way it should be. ---- 8 March 2006 Actually, we tried having women get involved physically in the WWA...they wound up getting seriously hurt. Ironically enough, Damian Thorne destroyed two of the women that I can recall getting physically involved... Either way, out of sheer curiousity, Khaled, do you still hate Europe? ---- 8 March 2006 To be perfectly honest I used to hate Europe ... but now, when I compare it to the US where I have lived for the past two and then some years ... I realise its not so bad ---- 8 March 2006 I have been wondering Khaled how a loser like yourself is finally gaining some notoriety off of Embers success. How it feel to be second best to Ember who hasn't even become a Franchise player yet? ---- 9 March 2006 Your Bastardry is somewhat legendary, how did you manage to create this level of bastardry and do you plan to write a book on how the common man can become a bigger bastard? :smiley: ---- 9 March 2006
Are you being Khaled by proxy or something Ember? :smiley:
I am off the opinion that there is no 'I' in team, and as the Deadly Alliance are first and foremost a team I have no problem voicing my opinion on Khaled, or vice versa. Ember and Khaled - World and US champions - the most dominant force in the WWA. Get used to it Mr PTC.
Intresting. Is that why those no talent "whores" like Karina do better than you super powerful athletes in PTC events? I believe the real answer to the question of why there are none in WWA is that the easily fragile egos of men like yourselves simply couldn't handle having a woman whip your ass.
Hmmm, just remind me of something please - where was Karina in the GTT5 event? Has a woman EVER won that event? What about Infinite Guantlet? In her home fed, where she can be protected, she is marketed just like any other cheap Playboy reject and if that is the way that they want to go then fine. The fact is the that WWA is a no-nonsense wrestling fed where 99.9% of people need not apply - because they just won't hack it. Now I am not saying that women cannot be talented athletes at all, but when you get someone like Karina stepping foot in the ring with real wrestlers who don't care about her welfare - she will get hurt.
How does it feel to be the chump of the most useless World Champion in WWA history? Oh, and congratulations on your US Title win. How long did it take you to get a Title?
*SIGH* I honestly feeling nothing but sympathy for bitter people that know that their best is behind them so have nothing better to do than bitch and moan. The most useless champion Davey? I could have taken the title weeks before I decided to, but instead made the choice to send out a message by stating that I would go through EVERY member of the roster at Birthday Brawl to win the belt - and I did. This was at the same time as representing the WWA in teh GTT5. Where were you my friend? Oh yeah, you were looking for your smile weren't you, while talking to your new best friends, the Samaritans. I used to donate to them but I have cancelled that after they actually did their job too well and talked you down! Did I also mention that it only took me one year - to the day - to win my title and that I did it without any help whatsoever? Jealousy is such a petty thing, Davey, especially when the person bleating is so obviously inferior. As for how long it took Khaled to get the title - what does that matter? He beat the second most successful man (after me of course) in the WWA, a man that no-one else had managed to beat, to take that title. You should be asking yourself how long he will hold it, actually, because that will be a very long time!
I have been wondering Khaled how a loser like yourself is finally gaining some notoriety off of Embers success. How it feel to be second best to Ember who hasn't even become a Franchise player yet?
Shaman, have you ever heard of 'pot' and 'kettle'? I know that you are going to point out that you have held titles yourself, but c'mon big ugy you have been with the WWA for over eight years, you were bound to get lucky sometime! The truth is that you are a has-been and almost-was - when you held the title the WWA was desperate and its star was falling, just like the ratings when the strap was put on you. How dare you call Khaled a loser! It doesn't matter at all that Khaled has become successful since joining with me, all that matters is that he made the right choice and has profitted from it. It also doesn't matter to Khaled that he is second best to me, for you see we are a team - the Deadly Alliance are strong together! When you are second best to THE best then you are still great Shaman, as Khaled knows - and it is better second than to be so far down the list as to be nearly forgotten, like you are. Franchise Player - why the Hell would I want to join your little boy's club of broken and faded has-beens? I have beatend Tracer Bullet so often that he has earned free membership to the Ember fan club - Khaled has made Denrol contemplate retirement again, which I think is the fifteenth time this week, and as for you - well really what can I say? Nothing at all - just like how much you, your words, or your Franchise Players, mean to me. ---- 9 March 2006 Oh look Khaleds momma decides to speak up for him. Ember you as usefull as teets on a bull. Go back somehwere and play with your matches. Khaled mark the words of someone who knows. Your tenure with Mr. Oh so agreeable there is doomed to fail and when it does i am goingto laugh all the way to the bank. ---- 12 March 2006 Ember and Khaled, the 'Deadly Alliance'. Hope you're enjoying paying me the royalties for the use of the name 'Deadly'. It's still licensed to myself and Phil Flaws, after all. But enough of the bickering. I'd like to thank you both. Thank You from the bottom of my heart. You've kept my seat warm at the top of the card. But like all good seat warmers, it's time for you to both step aside. It's such a shame that EWA rip-off feeder fed isn't still around. I think you'd both make great curtain jerkers for them. ---- 13 March 2006 I could drop to the level of Shaman and Dave Harley but quite frankly when you're perched atop of the card you realise you can only spend so much time meddling with stars of yester year. Father time has caught up to you boys ... understand it, accept it and move on. ---- 14 March 2006 Don't throw me in to the company of Shaman, you peon! First you were Matt Warr's bitch and when I sent him packing you became Ember's bitch. My question is, when we piss all over the current World Champion, whos bitch will you be then? Or will you become 'Hardcore Khal' and become the fans' bitch like you have before? And let's see if Khaled can answer this one. I wonder if Mommy Ember can keep her mouth shut. ---- 14 March 2006 Dave, Dave, Dave ... lest you forget how you ran and begged for the Deadly Alliance to come help you in your little War Games match. Face it Dave you can front all you want, but you know your time has gone. Sure I may have played second fiddle to people before, but right now I'm at the top with Ember ... looking down on has beens like yourself. Deep down you know it Dave ... it just scares you to admit your time has gone and your nothing more then the nostalgia act who people use to care about. ---- 15 March 2006 Ember...World Champion. Khaled... United States Champion. *puts out hands to make a scale like structure* Yeah...ummm...that still means you're playing second fiddle to someone. ---- 15 March 2006 If I'm playing second fiddle to Ember then does that mean every one is playing second fiddle to us? Ember may be World Champion but the fact is I've never had a World Title shot, so how could I be World Champion? I am what I am ... and Ember is what he is, and we're both champions, thats the bottom line ---- 16 March 2006
I am what I am ...
Popeye? ---- 16 March 2006 Again with the humour Dave ... are we trying to mask the pain of being mid card mediocrity? ---- 18 March 2006 Says the man facing Mick Vain at Rage In The Cage. Mick Vain who didn't even have what it took to be in Devil's Own. As for myself and Damian Thorne, we're above the need for trivial 'grudge matches'. With such crappy Champions defending at Rage In The Cage, we might just not show up at all. ---- 19 March 2006 So in other words Dave, yourself and Thorne haven't even been booked for Rage in the Cage. Not even advertised as a special attraction. Don't worry though, I hear they're letting anybody in the Open Inviatational Battle Royal. Maybe yourself and Damien could go for that ... hell you never know you could be something resembling a champion like Ember or myself by the end of the night. Although after watching you recently, I'd say thats unlikely ---- 20 March 2006 *Shaman listens and shakes his head and says soemthing to Dave.* Shaman: "As much as i dislike you i am inclined to agree with you. Khaled is jealous he didn't get to your status. maybe he will next year." *Chuckling he leans back against the wall as Samuelsson walks forward and looks at him.* Samuelsson: "Unlike you always staying when you should have taken the hint to leave? I'll enjoy hurting you at Rage in the Cage"
