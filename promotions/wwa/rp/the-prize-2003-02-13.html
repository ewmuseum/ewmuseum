The Prize

13 Feb 2003

By Leviathan

Item 2 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 13 Feb 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Leviathan can be seen sitting in a chair. He is bent with his elbows on his knees and the Cell’s trademark shirt “Brutally Truthful can be seen. His eyes are focused on the camera, his hands are clasp.) Malcolm your insults amuse me Yet they are never as insulting as the sight of you still breathing. The analogies are encouraging. Only, for there childish exuberance. Try this one. Rage in the Cage was only an appetizer; The Cell has not finished feasting on the carcass known as X-corp. David, is the desire still there? Can you still make it to the ring? Do you fear heights now? Can you still compete? How is the back? Finally for X-corp. Who are the number one contenders? Now for Havoc, the children of Ares. Fear may grip other teams in your presence. They do not know your weakness. They do not know their destiny. They do not have the power to conquer their fear. The Cell has all three. The battle will be fierce, it will remind your father of Salamis and Marathon. But know that the outcome will be the same as the Greeks at Thermopylae. The loss of valuables. And Defeat.
