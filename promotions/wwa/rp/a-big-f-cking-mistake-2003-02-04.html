A Big F*cking Mistake

4 Feb 2003

By Jackson Kraven

Item 3 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 4 Feb 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(We see Jackson Kraven sitting in a chair, his face being stitched up by a white-coated doctor-type. His face is a mess of bloodied wounds, but we see that someone had already tried to bandage him up.) F*CK!!! (Now we realise where this footage was taken- the Calgary Saddledome, the night after Kraven defeated Denrol in the first-ever Cell In The Frozen hell match... and the same night Vik Ventressa savaged Kraven's thunder.) F*CK! (Kraven's blood smears the doctor's hands, and his coat, and just about everything else he can get his hands on.) F*CK! (Kraven's eyes are wild in the crimson mess that was his face. That huge gash from forehead to lip has started spewing fresh gore down him, and the doctor's actions are useless. The more he rants and raves, the more stitches he will eventually need.) F*CK! (Suddenly, Kraven catches sight of the WWA camera. Jerking himself to his feet, he pushes the doctor away, staggering towards us like a monster in a horror movie.) VIK! VIK, YOU F*CKER! YOU F*CKER! I'M GONNA F*CKING KILL YOU, YOU F*CKER! (We watch, horrified at this bloodied, maddened mockery of a human being.) VIK! I'M GONNA F*CKING KILL YOU! THEY WON'T BE ABLE TO STOP ME, VIK! YOU THOUGHT WHAT I DID TO DENROL WAS BAD? JUST YOU F*CKING WAIT, YOU F*CKER! JUST YOU F*CKING WAIT! (As we watch, the Tomorrow Man does something truly revolting- deliberately bites at his own lip, bursting through it, filling his mouth with his own blood.) (As we watch, the Tomorrow Man hawks a huge wad of blood and mucus and saliva over the camera. It trickles down the lens, washing everything in a surreal, nightmarish red.) (As we watch, the Tomorrow Man grins, blood dripping from his mouth and the savage injuries all over his face. When he speaks again, his voice is in total contrast to the howling tempest it was mere seconds previously.) Just you f*cking wait, Vik. I'm gonna f*cking kill you, and there's not a thing you, or Warr, or anyone in this federation can do about it... (Steel hidden behind velvet.) (That's what his voice is now.) And when I finish with you this time... (Kraven chuckles nastily.) There won't be any comebacks... (We fade to black.)
