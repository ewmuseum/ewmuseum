The Best Surprise

4 Jul 2003

By "N.A.I." Nicholas Adams

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[We see a black screen, but then, a red light comes on in the background, showing a silohuetted man. When he suddenly decides to speak, it's more like a voice-over, and he's lip-synching the words. But from the voice quality, we know that it is none other than Nicholas Adams.] (Adams V/O: Some days, you don't exactly know what's happening to you. In fact, you tend to have the feeling that nothing is quite real. You begin to think that everything from this point on is a dream. I never quite had that sensation, up until...well, honestly, I have had that sensation, but only in the wrestling ring. In real life, I've been sure of what was reality and what was not. Every single time I've been led to question whether something is actually happening, I either immediately jolt out of bed and onto the ground - not a pleasant experience, I can tell you - or I just look around, shrug, and then figure, okay, it's real, but it's still pretty damn weird. Normally, I don't even do the talking for myself, but for some reason, the producers got lazy and told me to say something. So this situation is pretty weird to me, even though I know that it's real. But something even more strange was what happened to me the day after the Bourbon Street Brawl...) [A flash illuminates the screen, and we see Nicholas Adams walking through a book store.] (Adams V/O: Okay, so the camera guys actually got this on tape. Honestly, I was going to do a little video there. It was going to be the same thing as I did in the library. But since it was a public place, you can't exactly know what's going to happen. Just so you're not confused about what I was doing looking in the fantasy section in Utah, I was in Seattle at the time. It's amazing what air travel can do for you.) [The book store is rather nice, but all the store logos are scrambled, much like people's faces that television stations wish to hide. The shelves only go to about waist level in the middle of the store, but on the walls, they go all the way to a height which only Kevin Nash could reach standing on his toes. Anyway, Nick is walking through the science fiction section. Obviously, he's searching for some specific books. Finally, he stops and bends down. The camera comes around the shelf so that we still see Adams. The video suddenly freezes.] (Adams V/O: Then came the part that none of us expected. Of course, I was kind of hoping for something like this to happen...just not on tape. But it sure makes for good television. I'm still not quite sure why I told the producers to keep this off the air. Maybe I did it just because it's my personal life, you know? But maybe, just this time, the time for caution is over.) [The video starts playing again, and just as Adams is about to grab a book from the shelf, somebody in jeans, presumably a woman, comes up.] Woman: Excuse me, sir. Adams: Oh, sorry. [Adams moves in closer to the books to let the woman pass, but instead, she just stands there.] Woman: Actually, I was sort of hoping that you could give me one of the copies of the book you're about to grab. This...friend of mine...well, he told me it was a good series. He's not much for fantasy, so I figured he was telling the truth. Adams: Oh, sure. Sorry about that. [Adams pulls out the book carefully, stands, and begins to hand the book to the woman without even looking at her, but suddenly...] Woman: Nick? [Adams' immediate knee-jerk physical reaction is visible as the video yet again pauses.] (Adams V/O: Okay, you would normally think that being addressed by my first name would be a common occurrence, right? It usually isn't when I go to public places. People tend to say, "Hey, you're Nicholas Adams, right? That WWA superstar." When I nod, they say that I'm pretty good in the ring, and for a man that's approaching thirty, I'm pretty damn fast. So being addressed as "Nick" by somebody I don't think I know is sort of a shock. I swear, if I were older, I would have had a heart attack then and there. I would have had a heart attack because I knew that my fans knew not to address me as simply Nick, and I knew no woman besides Julie with whom I had been in recent contact that would call me Nick. Of course, after the shock of the moment, it turned out to be a pleasant surprise.) [The video resumes, and Adams turns. The camera comes around to see a woman with brown hair slightly longer than shoulder-length. She's somewhat attractive by magazine standards, but then again, most magazines air brush their models' pictures anyway. Adams finally realizes that he recognizes her.] Adams: Anna? [The woman nods, and on the spur of the moment, Adams just gives her a huge hug. Well, actually, from a man six feet tall, it isn't exactly gigantic, but the woman is only about five-foot-six, so it's still considerably overwhelming. The woman, obviously named Anna, just hugs him back. As is usual, the video pauses for one of Nick's monologues.] (Adams V/O: To put my thoughts to words, I was overjoyed to see her. We had split on amicable terms, and I had been feeling miserable about it ever since. We hadn't kept in touch, so it was that much more pleasantly surprising. Of course, I normally don't do things on impulse, but upon seeing her...well, let's just say this is probably going to be my last monologue until the end of this, unless I missed something.) [The video resumes, and Nick and Anna separate. Anna is the first to speak.) Anna: What are you doing in Seattle? Don't you have to be in Salt Lake City this Sunday? Adams: Actually, yes. But how did you know that? I haven't sent you a WWA schedule for months now. Anna: Well, I still get cable. I just felt like watching you, even if you didn't see me. [Nick smiles a warm smile and nods. It isn't sarcastic, either. This smile-and-nod movement is sincere, though simple. Adams thinks for a couple of seconds, and then, apparently on impulse...] Adams: Hey, Anna, do you want to come watch the Independence Day Rumble live? It would be really great if I could know you were in the crowd watching me. It would be a real motivation to win the match. Plus...well, I'd get to see you after the show. Maybe we could get dinner or something. [Anna stares at Adams with a slightly confused yet desiring look. She's probably counting the benefits over the negatives. After all, the original reason she broke up with Nick was that he wasn't willing to change. So far, he really hasn't. But the greater the risk, the greater the gain.] Anna: I would love to. Just promise me one thing, okay? Adams: Depends upon what I have to promise you. Anna: Don't let anybody, and I mean the Devil's Own, the Forsaken Souls, not even Jimmi Zion...don't let anybody get you so angry that you make a mistake. Remember, your rage is what causes you to make a mistake. One mistake, and you're going to end up on the outside of the ring, wishing you had been smarter. Adams: That's a promise I can definitely keep. I promise I will keep my emotions in check. [Anna nods as they walk over to the cashier.] Anna: Good. So, you feel like grabbing some food? Adams: Actually, yeah. But I'm buying... Anna: Oh, come on. I'm the one who invited you... [The scene fades, and we see Nicholas Adams standing in front of a white cloth in a white room. He's wearing all black, just for the sake of contrast.] Adams: So I guess life does have a way of working out sometimes. Hey, if people can find happiness at my age, they should be grateful for it. And I have definitely found the joy in my life. [The scene fades to the WWA logo, and then goes to black.]
