A million bucks and th Trio

23 Jul 2003

By Matt Keith

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
FADE IN: EXT. DANNY’S GARAGE –DAY [Danny’s garage, Matt and the gang’s usual hangout. Like usual there is Danny out front looking under the hood of a car wearing his brown mechanic overalls, this time he’s looking at an old rusted out BMW, Danny’s planning to restore it and maybe race it sometime in the future. VROOOOOOMMMM! Danny turns and sees Matt pull up on his brand new neon green Honda motorcycle. Matt makes a quick U-Turn and parks the bike next to sidewalk then gets off, he’s wearing the usual baggy black jeans, and a white tank top. Matt turns off the bike then walks over to Danny, they slap five] Danny: What’s up man? Matt Keith: Looks like you’ve got a new job on your hands. [Matt glances at the hoopty BMW thinking to himself “What a pile of s*it”] Matt Keith: To be honest with you bro you better send that thing back to the junkyard, it’s a pile of crap. [Matt kicks the front tire and the front bumper fall off the car with a raggedly boom and the trunk swings opens, Matt starts laughing his ass off, he’s laughing so hard that tears are coming from his eyes, the look on Danny’s face only make him laugh harder.] Danny: Come on Matt you’re going to f*ck it up. Matt Keith: [Still laughing] Little to late for that dude. Danny: Don’t worry about that, it’s still going to be one sweet ride. Matt Keith: [Sarcastic] Yeah. Danny: [Slightly angry] What are you doing here anyway. Matt Keith: I’m here to tell you that I’ll be in the next race. [Matt reaches into his pocket and pulls out a stack of one hundred dollar bills and hands it to Danny] Matt Keith: there’s the race fee. [Danny puts it into his pocket] Danny: All right, for a second I thought you gave up racing. Matt Keith: You know me better than that, I may have taken a few weeks off but that’s all. [Unexpectedly here comes Dantes in his Aston Martin, he parks and gets out.] Dantes: What up guys. Matt Keith: Long time no see. Dantes: Boy do I have some news for you. Danny: What kind of news? Dantes: There is a race that will be going on in Chicago. Matt Keith: So, what’s the big deal about that? Dantes: The big deal is the prize for winning is one million dollars. Danny: WHAT? Matt Keith: That’s a lot of cash money. [Dantes takes out the flyer from his pants pocket and hands it to Matt] Matt Keith: What’s the catch? There’s always a catch when this kind of money is on the line. Danny: You know, he’s right. Dantes: Well the race is not limited to just cars you can also use a motorcycle as you can see on the flyer, but the catch is, they have it on good authority that the cops know everything about it and will be out in force on the streets of Chicago. Danny: Man. Matt Keith: I think it’s worth a shot for a million bucks, cops or no cops. Danny: Are you serious? Matt Keith: Hell yeah. Dantes: I’m entering too. [Matt folds up the flyer] Matt Keith: Do you mind if I keep this flyer. Dantes: Sure go ahead. [Dantes sticks his hand out and shakes Matt’s hand] Dantes: Oh and congratulation on getting a contract in the WWA. Matt Keith: Thanks bro. [Matt snicker to himself] Matt Keith: Funny, before I could even enjoy the moment I’ve been thrown into a match. Dantes: With who? Matt Keith: You know that guy that barely beat that Lynn clown at the pay per view? Dantes: Alex Knight? Matt Keith: That’s the guy. [Matt crosses his arms and snickers again] Matt Keith: The f*cking guy is nothing more than a mouth piece, all he does is run off at the mouth like it’s a reflex action. Danny: Well they tend to do that when fear stricken. Matt Keith: You’re not as dumb as you look, are you? Danny: Hey! Matt Keith: [Laughs] I’m just screwing with you don’t have a fit. [He slaps Danny on the shoulder] Matt Keith: You do make a good point though, Alex Knight is so afraid of what I have in store for him that he’s spewing out all these things to make himself feel better. Nonetheless I want him to keep on talking, keep telling himself that he’s the best thing since porn, because I’m telling you right now, that beating I gave Mystical Illusion will look like a small scuffle when it’s all said and done. [Matt slaps hands once again with his laughing friends] Matt Keith: And I want both of you at Fiesta. Danny: Don’t worry about it we’ll be there. [Matt arrogantly smirks and walks back over to his motorcycle then gets on, he sticks the key in turns it and pulls off while his buddy’s watch.] INT. MATT’S BEDROOM, LATER THAT DAY- NIGHT [Cameron’s sitting on the edge on the bed wearing her white night gown and setting the clocks alarm, Matt’s in the bathroom taking a shower] Cameron: [Calling to Matt] Matt’s are you finished yet? Matt Keith: Almost. Cameron: [Calling out to Matt] Hurry up. [Cameron puts the clock back down on the nightstand but a piece of paper catches her eye. She opens it and it’s that very same race flyer that Dantes gave Matt early that day, she reads it and the first thing that catches her attention is the million-dollar prize.] Cameron: [Whispering] A million dollars? [Suddenly the thoughts of what Matt told her after the accident rings in her head. “Please never race again, I don’t want you to get hurt”.] [She folds the paper up and places it back on the night stand, Matt comes out of the bath room wearing plaid boxers.] Matt Keith: What’s wrong? Cameron: Nothing. FADE TO BLACK
