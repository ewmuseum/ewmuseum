Decisions

26 Feb 2003

By Chance

Item 2 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 26 Feb 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Six Playing Cards On a varnished table That is all we see That and a hand twirling a coin Faster and faster, round and round Until the coin leaves the hand and spins across the table And lands in the centre of the cards, lands in the centre of luck And now the hand moves the cards Organising them, shepherding them, putting them into the necessary pattern “These cards show our fate Denrol” The shadowy face of Chance leans over the table, his hands flipping over the top three cards as he does so “One for each of you, one for each of your Franchise” Indeed each of the cards is a picture card, and each picture is of a WWA Franchise Player. Denrol is The King of Hearts, Tracer the Kind of Spades, and Shaman is the King of Clubs. Yes, they are all there. “There you have the experienced of the WWA, the veterans of its wars” Chance moves one hand towards the other three cards “And here you have the youth of the WWA, its fresh blood” Chance flips the middle card of the three to reveal a joker styled as himself, then moves his hand to the second “This man I chose because I know what he can do, I have felt it first hand, and I know that he is fighting for his own reasons in this match, I know he is fighting for his own selfish reasons, which is why I chose him, for I know what he will do, and I don’t have to like it, but this way I can control it.” Chance flips the card to reveal Enigma, shown as the Jack of Spades “The second is a man I chose almost randomly, out of all the young talent to choose from I swirled the pool until one man emerged, a man who has already proven himself, a man who is still hungry to make his name” Chance flips the final card to show Jason Wainwright, shutting off the destiny, cancelling the randomness of that match. Forever. “So now the teams are decided Denny, and the battle lines are drawn. The old will take on the new, and remember Denny, this was your idea, your decision. I simply took the decision to accept your terms. Just as every other man in the match has made their decision to take part.” We move away from the table to see Chance again standing over the table, smiling slightly, a light from somewhere illuminating the coin on the table, and that light reflecting dangerously in his eyes “I have chosen my own destiny, I have made my choice. Lady Luck will do the rest”
