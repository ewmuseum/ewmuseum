Pushing the boat out Part 1

23 Jul 2003

By Wes Sanders

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene opens up to Enigma and Wes Sanders training. Enigma is holding up a board making Wes stretch his legs high and lightly tap on the board. Enigma has his famous trench coat off for a change and looking very frustrated. Wes’ body is profusely pouring sweat, it looks like he has been training for hours. None the less Wes continues to fight through his showing pain hitting the board constantly again and again. Wes then stops for a moment.) “AGAIN!” Enigma cries “I didn’t tell you to stop” (Wes tries to psyche himself up, hopping and bouncing on his feet but the fatigue has kicked in and he is struggling to perform his best. Wes stop after a few small weak kicks and walks away.) “Hey where the hell do you think your going?” Enigma snap with anger in his voice (Wes presses with his hands against a wall gasping for breath, Enigma like a shadow stands behind Wes. Wes spits onto the floor taking in each breath as if it was his last. Enigma places a hand on Wes shoulder.) “I… can’t… go… any… further…” Wes sounds exasperated “Look I know I’ve been working you hard this week Wes but it’s for a reason, and a good reason at that.” (Enigma takes a step back and waits for Wes to turn around.) “We are the laughing stock of the WWA currently, we cannot have this we’ve got to re think our plans.” (Wes ponders for a moment whilst Enigma walks away quoting his particular “Art of War”.) “The first step in a great battle plan is to identify your objective.” (Wes knows this already he has heard it all before but still listens on.) “My old objective was Chance, and he has been shown the light, the dark eternal light of the Forsaken Souls, but Wes my friend. My TRUSTED friend… We have a new objective, a new lease of life” (Wes wondered for a moment why Enigma liked to behave in such an odd way but realized that Enigma stands by his own basic principles and meanings. Enigma carried on.) “Fiesta is new territory, it’s as if we’ve been given a completely new federation to compete in. We WILL show our dominance. Last week we made some terrible mistakes we will have to rectify.” (Wes walks up behind Enigma who is still in his stride. Wes can almost feel the brain storming ideas flow from Enigma’s head. Enigma stop and pauses, almost waiting for Wes to answer.) “And how…” Wes said… “I knew you were going to ask that Wes, follow me and I will show you exactly how we are going to push the boat out.” “Push the what now…?” Wes seemed confused “Come Wes, we both have much to talk about.” (With this Enigma and Wes lead out of there training ground.)
