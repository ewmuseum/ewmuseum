I am Not Handicapped

30 Dec 2002

By Enigma

Item 4 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 30 Dec 2002, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
{The scene begins in the familiar setting for the WWA Extreme and WWA Cruiserweight Champion Enigma. Except there are a couple of differences. The first being that there is a table in from of his chair. On the table lies his two title belts, a cigar, ashtray, and a bottle of wine with a glass. Enigma is standing up in front of the table.} Monday Night Meltdown appears to be a night where the House of Pain is going to dominate once again. Jackson Kraven is in the main event and will dominate Ian Tense and Wes Sanders, Damian Thorne will crush Kash and I will go on my way to fight both Chance and Jimmi Zion. Remember the last time we were in the ring together? Chance, I destroyed your knee to pieces and Zion I put your ass through the table. {Still photos of December 16th, 2002, Meltdown is shown specifically Zion being powerbombed through the table and Chance getting the Eko-Lizer onto the title belt.} Chance, I was definitely looking forward to meeting you in a tag team match. The challenge is still out there. It does not matter that it is not this week. By the way, Crow and you are going to perform? I need a good laugh. {Enigma smirks and lights up his cigar} Now to concentrate on this coming Monday. Handicap Match? Which side is Handicapped? It sure as hell is not me. If anyone is handicapped it is Chance and his bum knee and Jimmi Zion. You both want my titles? Let me tell you two something, these titles are not going anywhere. Let me begin with this FORMER Cruiserweight Champion by the name of Jimmi Zion. Ever since you have come back to the WWA, you have not lived up to your hype. What have you done? Nothing! You have done nothing this year. You are not impressive at all. You were the WWA Cruiserweight Champion over a year ago. You had the title for four months? BIG DEAL! You are not going to take this title from me. You can not beat me. You may have been good before but as of right now you are nothing to me. I am stronger than you. I hope you enjoyed Christmas because you are not going to get any presents in the form of titles from me. {Enigma sits down and pours some red wine and takes a drink} Chance on the other hand, is different from you Jimmi. You see Chance I am going to finish you off once and for all this coming Monday. You and I have met on a few occasions and it appears that we are at a stalemate. Chance, on Monday I am going to finish you off. On Monday {Enigma holds up his two title belts} Are not going anywhere. Chance, you will not survive Monday. NOTHING CAN STOP ME NOW!!! {The scene fades}
