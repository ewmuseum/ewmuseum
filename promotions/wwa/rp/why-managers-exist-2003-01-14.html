Why Managers Exist

14 Jan 2003

Author not credited in the source

Item 1 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 14 Jan 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
|The scene is set inside a wrestling school, all around men of all sizes are performing different feats inside rings while elsewhere people are talking. The camera follows one man who is finishing up instructions in one of the rings and then departs, approaching a seemingly forgotten individual who is practicing body slam's with a "dummy".| Teacher: You... uh... you there. |One man, with short light brown hair, wearing black wrestling trunks and sporting an average physique, points to his chest.| Man: Me? |His voice is long, slow, emotionless. He is as monotoned as Ben Stein ever was for a Clear Eyes commercial.| Teacher: Yeah, you'll do. Come on up here... uh... you. |The man gets up and walks over to the teacher. He shakes his hand and introduces himself.| Man: Name's John. Teacher: Great. You've been here in the training center for... uh... well you've been here a while now I'm sure. And I think he's good enough to wrestle professional now to. What do you think... uh... Man... who's name escapes you: John. And I think it would be incredible to wrestle professionally. |Still the man talks in a monotoned voice like Ben Stein, and he does not, in any way, show his enjoyment.| Teacher: Well that's good, because I got a request from the WWA to send over a wrestler to help out at one of their shows. Seems one of the jobbers got sick and they come to me for a replacement. Think you can handle it? |The man... who's name you just can't think of... does not display any emotion still.| Man... what was his name?: That sounds wonderful. I have always thought it would be interesting to wrestle professionally, ever since I saw such wonderful people in the wrestling ring as Hulk Hogan, Chris Benoit, and Dean Milenko. I loved nothing more than watching great men in the ring, unless you consider my love of their interviews. To talk so emotionally, so powerfully, as men like the Iceman and the Crippler. I thought that would be great if only it were me, speaking with those strong emotions, in front of millions of people. And finally, I am given the chance to speak to the world, speak powerfully and emotionally... |Still the man drones on as would the voice of Ben Stein were he to talk for long times.| The other guy on the screen: ... I could be the next Iceman. I could be the next Jerry Flynn. As a wrestler, I always considered myself rather average, but of course if you think I am good enough, who am I to argue. I shall be the stable rock, the safe ground, from which the fans might hang and gain strength. John Doe, the name shall forever be known as the most electrifying wrestler in history. The man who rocks the mic. |If you aren't asleep by this point, you notice the teacher is a little more than lightly interested in silencing the guy. But that other guy seems to refuse to shut up.| Didn't he just give his name?: Wrestling is such a great sport, where the top athletes in the world gather with one singular purpose in mind, and that is to entertain and to win. It was one thing to wrestle in high school and in college. There was me and my opponent and a referee, but there were no screaming fans and no reason to take a microphone into your hand. Now, in front of thousands and thousands and millions and millions of wrestling fans around the world, I will be able to speak. I will bring respect to the bad men, and against the good guys I will have lay down a maelstrom of insults against the ever so good men who think it takes vitamins, milk and prayers to make yourself something. I will be the wrestler everyone longs to fight. I will be the man who takes a microphone to silence the crowd so his words of wisdom might be heard more clearly. I will be the next sex symbol of wrestling, the next icon of sports, and with me, wrestling will be raised to a whole new level. |This guy has spent the last ten minutes saying what most men could have said in five. Finally, though, his teacher slaps a hand over what's his name's mouth.| Teacher: Glad you're excited, now why don't you go shower up and get ready. |The man walks off slowly, the camera happily does not follow.| Teacher: Everybody listen up. Starting today we'll spend one hour working on interviews. I think we're going to need it. |Not understanding, the men all return to their work or approach the teacher as the camera fades.|
