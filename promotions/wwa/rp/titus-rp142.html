Titus

13 Jun 2003

By Grunge

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
FADE IN (We focus in on a TV screen showing the movie “Titus.” Anthony Hopkins, who plays the title character, is in mid-speech.) Titus: Oh villains, Chiron and Demetrius. Here stands the spring whom you have stained with mud, this goodly summer with your winter mixed. You killed her husband, and for that vile fault two of her brothers were condemned to death, my hand cut off and made a merry jest, both her sweet hands, her tongue, and that more dear than hands or tongue, her spotless chastity, inhuman traitors, you constrained and forced. What would you say if I should let you speak? Villains, for shame, you could not beg for grace. Hark, wretches, how I mean to martyr you. This one hand yet is left to cut your throats whilst that Lavinia, 'tween her stumps doth hold the basin that receives your guilty blood. You know, your mother means to feast with me and calls herself Revenge and thinks me mad. Hark, villains. I shall grind your bones to dust, and with your blood and it I shall make a paste, and of the paste a coffin I will rear and make two pastries of your shameful heads. And bid that strumpet, your unhallowed dam, like to the earth, swallow her own increase! This is the feast I have bid her to, and this the banquet she shall surfeit on. ...And now prepare your throats. (We pull out to see Grunge sitting in his chair watching the movie. To the left of him sits Chris Crenshaw.) Grunge: Popcorn? (Grunge passes the large red bowel of his favorite snack food over to the WWA interviewer. Chris begins to pop some into his mouth.) Crenshaw: You know Grunge I love to come over and watch movies with you but why are there cameras here? Grunge: I felt that this movie is something Enigma should see. Crenshaw: So this is an interview? Grunge: No I just don’t like watching movies alone. Crenshaw: So where’s Julie? Grunge: Health Food Store. (A moment passes as Chris munches loudly on some of the popcorn in his mouth. He finally gets the nerve to speak.) Crenshaw: So what does this have to do with Monday’s match? Grunge: Enigma is so focused with falling from a state of grace and I felt this matches that term. This movie is about the fall of an empire, the descent of man. Crenshaw: How so? (Grunge sighs.) Grunge: War begets revenge. Victorious general, Titus Andronicus, returns to Rome with hostages: Tamora queen of the Goths and her sons. He orders the eldest hewn to appease the Roman dead. He declines the proffered emperor's crown, nominating Saturninus, the last ruler's venal elder son. Saturninus, to spite his brother Bassianus, demands the hand of Lavinia, Titus's daughter. When Bassianus, Lavinia, and Titus's sons flee in protest, Titus stands against them and slays one of his own. Saturninus marries the honey-tongued Tamora, who vows vengeance against Titus. There's irony in which two sons survive. (Chris mouths oh…) Grunge: Enigma, like Titus, is so engrossed with revenge on the world and his own pitiful condition that he doesn’t see that there are forces aligned to defeat him. His story, like Shakespeare, is a tragedy. Crenshaw: And how do Nick Adams and Dave Harley fit into the equation? Grunge: Simply more nails for Enigma’s coffin. Crenshaw: Well in the interview I did with him he said there’s no way he can lose. Grunge: If he’s counting on help from Joey K and Se7en he should just save the effort. He says Dave Harley is a comedy act, maybe, but everyone needs laughter. It’s not smart to underestimate the man. I’m not. And just because Nick hasn’t beaten me doesn’t mean he can’t beat you Enigma. I’ve seen what the man is capable of. I have beat him. It doesn’t mean you can. Besides you’re the king of the choke matches. Tracer Bullet has beaten you every time he stepped in the ring with your sorry carcass. Besides… (Grunge twists his face to his best Keanu Reeves impersonation.) Grunge: …Nick knows Kung-Fu. Crenshaw: Still that Ace he spoke of… Grunge: He’s not the only one who has planned ahead. Don’t try and match wits with me, you’ll end up with a broken psyche. Crenshaw: Well I guess we can go back to the movie. Grunge: Of course. (Grunge pulls out two Cokes from the mini-fridge and hands one to Crenshaw. Chris pops one open and takes a swig while Grunge taps the top of his can.) Grunge: Just remember Enigma one who has never existed in a state of grace to begin with cannot fall. (Grunge pops his open and slurps a drink.) Grunge: Rock and roll. (Grunge grabs the remote and begins to turn the sound up to full blast as he and Crenshaw once again begin to enjoy their movie. And at that we begin to…) FADE OUT
