Aftermath

7 Jan 2003

By Dave Harley

Item 3 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 7 Jan 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The scene opens on a hotel suite. The TV is on and we can see last night’s Insider playing on Primetime Central. As the camera pans across the room we see an icepack sitting on the bed. Bloodied bandages litter the floor.] Voice 1: AAAAAAARRRGGGHHHH!!! Voice 2: Stop screaming! Voice 1: Well stop doing that! Voice 2: This isn’t in my job description by the way! Voice 1: Well I didn’t exactly ask for any help! [As the camera reaches the head of the bed we see the first voice – Dave Harley – sat holding an ice pack to his ribs. The second voice – Jill – is stood over him and is redressing Harley’s head wounds.] Harley: The f*ckin’ Cell! They think they’re gonna get away with this because that asshole Cruz is in their corner? They’ve made two very dangerous enemies. Me and Mal were dangerous when we were in opposing corners but combined we’re gonna take the Cell to a whole new level of Extreme. PA: This head wound is taking longer than usual to heal. Just how many chairshots did you take? Harley: A lot! Let’s face it, it’s nothing compared to what I’m gonna do to Commissioner Cruz when I get hold of him. We had Lockdown and Leviathan right were we wanted them. That match was over! So how is Somers anyways? PA: They haven’t released him from hospital yet. Needless to say he won’t be competing this week but he told me to tell you he’ll be there at Fiesta this week when you take on Chance. Harley: [laughs] Harley and Somers. Who’d have thought it, huh? As for Chance, as far as I’m concerned our hatchet was buried at Seasons Beatings. I gave him as good as I took in that Steel Cage but it’s obviously not enough for Cruz. He wants us to go at each other one more time? Fine by me. Let’s face it, Chance is gonna be ready. But if the Cell even thinks of sticking their noses around the curtain this Saturday, they’re gonna fall foul of the most extreme coalition the WWA has ever seen. PA: Aren’t you getting a bit carried away? Both you and Mr Somers are in a bad condition. How can you even be sure that he’ll be fit enough to accompany you down to the ring this week? [Fade out]
