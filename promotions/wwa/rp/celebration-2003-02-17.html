Celebration

17 Feb 2003

By X Corp

Item 3 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 17 Feb 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Scene - Backstage after the most recent Fiesta. The funky disco beats of “Celebrate” by Kool and the Gang reverberate down the empty corridors. The cameraman wanders up the corridor as the music gets louder as we approach the source. We reach a half opened door with a piece of A4 paper sticky taped to it, reading “Xcorp’s Dressing Room”. Scrawled underneath it in pencil are the words “WWA’s Third Best Tag Team”. The camera continues into the room where Dave Harley and Mal Somers are relaxing in LAY-Z-BOY recliners, drinking Dom Perignon champagne from bottles, wearing party hats and popping party poppers. Dave’s PA, Jill, uncharacteristically, is dancing on a table, drinking from her own bottle of champagne. She appears to be pretty trashed. Somers and Harley aren’t far behind.) Jill (singing) - “Celebrate…good times…come on!” Harley - (in best Dr. Evil voice) “One for me…” (sips from bottle) “and one for my homies!” (pours a little bit on the floor.) Somers - “Hey man, that’s from Austin Powers, The Spy Who Shagged Me isnt it?” Harley - “Damn straight homey. Word up and to your mother also.” Somers - “Hey your drunk.” Harley - “So are you Chair boy!” Somers - “Am not!” Harley - “I’m not the one letting a full bottle of champagne spill on my crotch!” (Somers pauses for a moment and looks down.) Somers - “$hit.” Harley - “Hey if you like I’ll get Jill to clean it up.” Somers - (white as a ghost) “Nah I’ll be OK.” (Somers grabs his WWA Rookie Title and places it on his lap partially covering the spillage and takes another swig from the bottle as Jill half stumbles off the table.) Jill - (looking at Mal) “I wont ask. (hiccup) I’m just going out to the (hiccup) Extreme Mobile I’m sure I left (hiccup) a bottle of scotch out there. (Jill stumbles out of the dressing room as Mal and Dave look gobsmacked.) Harley - “I wouldn’t want to be her in the morning.” Somers - “I wouldn't want to be her in the evening either! Oh well compadre. The night is young and it is truly a night for partying.” Harley - “No doubt buddy. Our efforts against Commissioner Cruz have been rewarded. Fired as the Fiesta Commissioner. BOO-YAH!” Somers - “And it gets better. The new Commissioner Raine has already put us down for two matches on Fiesta.” Harley - “After weeks of trying everything in the book, we finally get a chance to do our thing!” Somers - “Yeah I’m sure you would have got the main event slot if I wasn’t booked.” (Harley briefly cuts an angry glance at Somers but then grins.) Harley - “Indeed both causes for celebration!” (Harley pops a party popper near Somers’ ear.) Somers - “Aaaarrggghh!” Harley (grinning) - “Sorry bout that Chairboy. Just caught up in the moment!” (Jill stumbles back in and sits on the floor.) Jill - “What’d I miss?” Somers - (rubbing his ear) “Just talking about all the good things that have happened tonight.” Harley - “We’ve yet to mention the best news. Our “support” for the Cell helped spur them on to victory.” Somers (grinning) “That’s right…Leviathan and Lockdown. The Cell. The guys we detest the most. Are now the WWA Tag Team Champions.” Harley - “Even more incentive to whip their asses!” Somers - “Oh hell yeah. I think we need to update the sign on our door!” Jill - “I’ve got (hiccup) to go to the bathroom!” (Jill stumbles back out the door as the Xcorp members toast each other with their bottles and begin laughing as the scene fades to black.)
