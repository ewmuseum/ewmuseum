Arguing

1 Mar 2003

By Kash

Item 3 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 1 Mar 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(We open in an abandoned church. Yes, it is the same abandoned church that we have seen many a time before.) Kash: Yes, it is the same church as we have seen before, why do you always say that? Its always going to be the same church. (I apologise. As I was saying, an abandoned church, in the mi...) Kash: Middle of the night, yes, as always, you realise no-one has ever rarely seen me in sunlight? (Again, nothing but apologies from this humble voice in the sky.) Kash: Good, now, ignoring the cliche's, shall we continue, Muppet reject. (Next week on Veterinarian Hosp.....) Kash: Dont even think about it. (Ok, so will you let me finish?) Kash: Yes, go on. (Ok...ahem...we are in a church .. SSHHH) Kash: Saying nothing. (In the middle....middle of the night....) Kash: Im not doing anything, what? (We find our hero Kash....) Kash: HERO? What have you been smoking? (Nothing, just trying to add a litle brevity to the precedings.) Kash: Well don't, Im not a hero and we both know it. (You fight against Thorne, doesnt that make you a good guy? As a result, a hero?) Kash: Maybe in some way, it does, but call me that again, you shall get that African American member you call a microphone inserted somewhere painful. (Ok, sorry. Although, being an omnipresent voiceover, I dont have anywhere to....) Kash: Id find somewhere, dont you worry about that. (I dont like the sound of that, shall I continue?) Kash: Indeed. (Ok Funaki...) Kash: (glares) Watch it. (We find ourselves here, usual time, usual place, usual wrestler. And he has something to say.) Kash: Yes, I .... (fade to black, but audio continues) Kash: You mean to tell me, I just lost my flash time arguing with you? (Im afraid so.) Kash: I need a break from this shit.
