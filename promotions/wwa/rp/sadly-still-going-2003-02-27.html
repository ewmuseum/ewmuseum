Sadly... still going

27 Feb 2003

By Showtime

Item 8 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 27 Feb 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(In the top floor office of what was once the WWA Head Office in Minneapolis, Showtime sits at what was Mark Harrington's desk and talks angrily into the phone. He seems oblivious to the wrecking ball swinging behind him.) Gary the Cameraman: I really have to tell him this building was condemned one day. Showtime: Don't you hang up on me Warr! I made this company! I want my world title shot!! Yeah? Same to you, buddy! (Showtime slams the phone down on the hook, and leans back in the chair. He lights a cigar and laces his hands behind his head.) Showtime: That told him. Gary: You probably would have made more of an impact if the phone was plugged in. Showtime: Yeah... well I was pratising. Yeah. That's it. Gary: I suppose he'll know once he sees this tape. Showtime: Oh don't worry. You can edit it out later. Gary: Uh huh. Well, since I'm here, might as well talk about your tag team match this weekend on Fiesta. Showtime: Oh right. This Saturday on the show I single-handedly resurrected, I get to help three other guys get a taste of the spotlight, and once again remind the world why I'm the world's greatest wrestler in the... er, world. Gary: Yeah, isn't that nice of you. Showtime: I'm a great guy. What can I say. So that of course makes me a target. Guys like Denrol, and Tracie Van Bullet have puttered around in the midcard, all the while watching me rise up into the stratosphere. Gary: Inflated egos do make a person rise. Showtime: hat's for... HEY! Don't be snarky, Wally! could snap you in half. Just like that! (Showtime tries snapping his fingers, but all he does is break the cigar. The burning end lands in his pants. After brushing off the ashes, and patting out the small fire, he sits down again.) Showtime: Yeah, it's the jealousy that I can't stand. It's bad enough I have to put up with Denny and VD, but even those who could never even amount to comparing to me are jealous of me. Gary: Do tell. Showtime: Ian Thorne and Damien Kash aren't even in the league of people who aren't in my league. Come on! Why are you two jealous of me? Just because you weren't cut out to be a member of Reborn, why hate me? I mean, are ants jealous of God? Don't bother with it fellas! It'll only hurt you if you try to match up to me. Gary: Well now, I wouldn't be that hard on them. can't compare sh*t to ice cream. Showtime: Oh, I wouldn't go as far as calling them sh*t. Gary: Neither did I. Showtime: There. You unders... HEY!! SHUT UP! You're just another person that's jealous of me. All the time, I'm fighting off those losers who what notoriety by saying "I touched Showtime!" Those four dorks I already mentioned come to mind immediately. And everyone that floats under me in the ranking. Gary: Floats under you? Showtime: Yep, I want to make sure it'll be a fair contest, so me and my peers like... uh, well, so I can have a good scientific match against James Curzon and Silas Fagin. Gary: You forgetting someone? (His pants begin to smoke again, so Showtime dumps a glass of water onto his crotch.) Showtime: Oh, who can forget my new partner James Beam? I like to take on a pet project every once in awhile. Remember how I made Mantis a household name? And how I gave a lick of the superstar ring to Troy Walcott? Now I'll try to make James Beam into a star! One day, just maybe, I'll help him get the Rookie title! Or! Or maybe even... dare I say it? The Extreme title! Yep, aim big, my liquor-obsessed little pal! The Sky's the limit! Gary: What about the world title? Showtime: Oh that? Well, since I'll be winning that title myself, I don't think he'll hold it. Not until I retire as the champ of course. Gary: You saying you wouldn't give him a shot? And why the hell am I hypothetically giving you the world title? Showtime: Of course I would grant my new protege a title shot, but out of respect to the man who got him this far, I doubt he'd accept it. Gary: Man, you're like reading a Harry Turtledove novel. Showtime: Quit dropping name Larry, it's makes you look like a star-f*cker. Gary: I'd need a Swiffer for all the ones you tossed down earlier... Showtime: Apology accepted. So yes, James Beam, welcome to the big leagues. Don screw it up, it's the only chance you've got. Like that candy guy. Gary: Aren't you dismissing your opponents out of hand a bit? Showtime: Nonsense! They too get to ride on my Main-Event coattails. (A 'whoomp' is heard from the pants region of Showtime.) Gary: You do know that was vodka you poured on your crotch, right? (The flame rising from Showtime's pants illustrates why this is a mad thing to do.) Showtime: Er... Gary: This interview's over? Showtime: Quick! Which of them "The More You Know" things do I do when my clothes are on fire? Gary: Say "no" and tell someone you trust. (Gary fades to black.) Showtime: No! No! No!
