Raven...

5 Jun 2004

By Jack Griffiths

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Sitting on the floor in the corner of a room, not flinching in an otherwise empty room sits a lone man and his LDA DIABLO Title Belt, the man is thinking not knowing what is coming next merely anticipating what may come... what may happen, for tommorrow is another day and what happens is unknown only to god, and the people with whom he makes contact. These people are known only as crazy persons, the light is flickering signifying the instability of the surroundings and how nothing can be on all the time. God makes it that everything needs a rest else they would be Him, their lack of faults are what make things not of heavenly origin.) Jack: The Blind man can see nothing and the deaf man can hear nothing but which is worse off? Neither knows of what is happening around them, each has one piece of information that the other doesn't yet neither really knows enough, without total knowledge no-one is perfect. The question of which form of information is more important is unanswerable. The deaf man acnnot hear what is shouted at him as my car is about to hit him, but the blind man cannot see me coming and thus doesn't know which way to dive out of my way. Both get hit, both get injured but who is the worse off... the man who lives as though he is constantly sleep walking or the man who lives with constant ear muffs on. (Breathing slowly the man, gentle runs his hand through his beard and then his hair, yet he is still not really moving other than that. Only time is moving at that moment.) Jack: There is one type of person worse off than both of these... not the dumb man, nor the man without the sense of sensitivity... his touch. Rather the man whom has no knowledge, the kind of man who can get himself confused with Homer Simpson, the man whom is unable to use the toliet without aid. Many would immediately guess that I am talking of the old and geriatric, and I cannot blame them: for the old should not be old they should either be dead or sentanced so. When people reach a certain age they become useless merely drawing from an economy to which they don't contribrute, the taxes that the UK government make me pay and keeping a dozen old people alive when they could be doing something useful with my money like investing in new jobs for the homeless and those single mothers drawing benefits because they got shacked up with a boyfriend who then left her. It is their problem let them deal with it, give them a job make them work for their money. Some of these mothers make more money a week doing nothing than I do putting my life on the line in a square object with ropes. Something that for some reason you call a ring... was the inventor of the wrestling "ring" a direct relative of Jurgen Raymann. (Jack begins to smile sadistically and lets out a little sadistic laugh.) Jack: Ah... Jurgen that stupid boy, will he ever learn. He is a complete airhead, you'd think he would be able to get it into his head that I am not a person to mess with... after all there is alot of room in there. Now that I gave him brain damage or a concussion there must be more room, I must have knocked some of the very little knowledge out of his head, maybe that blood. Oh... the blood. (Jack in a more sadistic moment than ever before licks his lips from the mention of Jurgen's blood, before regaining composure) Jack: Maybe that was the knowledge he has escaping from his body, they say tears are fear escaping the body. His blood, the sight of his blood was one of those experiences that every man should experience, a moment of absolute fullness. At that precise moment my life no longer felt empty, no longer felt that I was being held down because of my anti-rule feelings, why should a man be held back because he refuses to accept what the people define as the normal. The crowd booing was the most gratifying moment in my life, knowing that the people hate me for beating upon a man they take sympathy for... they finally realised that I am not about an image, I am about doing what I want, when I see fit. I am the man they call el Diablo. Jesus was God's Human form... I am Satan's Human form, learn to fear me. For when the clock strikes and the bell rings I am coming for you... you Judgement Day is coming, your time for fear is here. (Jack breathes deeply, he is becoming more and more sadistic almost possesed by an inner demon) Jack: Odium, it is your time next... your number has been called and we aren't playing bingo, so when you step up bring your A-Team because you can't hold me down, when your trial begins I will be the one sitting on the council, I will be the one handing out the punishment. I will be the one who makes you bleed, the one who makes you scream for salvation but there will be no salvation for what comes will come and I have no doubts that your fate for that night is to go home without my title belt. For you are not as evil, as destructive as me. You can't stand up against me and expect to write the next chapter, there is none I am the beginning and the end, the Alpha and the Omega. I am your Judge, Jury and Executioner, I am the devil. The time for words is over, now is the time for my fists to do the talking and my fists are why I am the bringer of the death... much like the Raven and in the immortal words of the worlds most famous Raven. Quote the Raven... nevermore (Jack fall silent and droops his head between his legs)
