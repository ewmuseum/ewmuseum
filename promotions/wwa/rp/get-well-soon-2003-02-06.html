Get well soon!

6 Feb 2003

By Xcorp/Cell

Item 5 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 6 Feb 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Scene – A hospital room. Dave Harley lies on his back in a bed. You would be hard pressed to tell that, though, because his forehead is bandaged, his eyes, closed, are puffy and swollen. His cheeks are bruised. His lower lip is stitched up. The rest of him is under the covers and he is connected to a drip. The steady beat of the heart monitor is heard in the background. On a chair beside him sits his X-corp tag partner, and WWA Rookie Champion, Mal Somers. His arm is in a sling and his forehead is stitched up, like a railway track. With his spare arm, Somers is holding his WWA Rookie Title belt up to the sun filtering through the window, as the belt glistens in all its glory.) Somers: “Wakey Wakey Harley! Rise and shine!” (The light reflects off the belt, concentrated on Harley’s eyes.) Somers: “Man, he is out cold. Slipping in and out of consciousness. I almost feel sorry for the guy. The doctors told me that he has come to, sometimes even managing to wander the halls. Unfortunately, whenever I come in, he is out to it.” (puts the belt down) “I mean at Rage in the Cage he took a freakin POSIEDEN ADVENTURE from the top of the cage all the way down to the announcers table! He was beat up prior to our match. Exposed to ladders, barbed wire, tables, and chairs. Not to mention the beating that I took. He needs his rest.” (shakes his head) “We need to heal up and regroup, buddy. The X-corp will come back bigger and badder than ever, you better believe it. Fiesta is just over a week away. The playpen of the Cell. Well not for much longer. We’ll show them what the X-corp is all about. It’ll make the beating we took look like touch rugby.” (stands up) “Rest up, Harley. Godspeed!” (Mal walks to the door and looks back at Dave, who is oblivious to the inspirational pep talk just given by his tag partner. Somers gives him a final thumbs up as he closes the door gently behind him, causing the room to be semi-dark.) (Fade Out) (Oh wait…fade back in!) (Harley stirs and starts laughing to himself even though it hurts.) Dave: Man, Mal can be such a nursemaid sometimes. I think it was better when we just belted each other’s brains out. Voice: But you’re having this nice friendship thing now, aren’t you? (Dave looks around but the darkness of the room fails to provide any human form for the voice.) Dave: I hope this is not one of those (beep)ing Christmas carol moments. I must be really drugged up to start hearing voices. Voice: The warnings are over David. The retribution has commenced. (Dave realizes whom the voice belongs to as fear comes over his face. He is unable to move due to the drugs and injuries. His demeanor changes to show the true grit of Dave Harley as Leviathan emerges from the shadows next to the doorway wearing a white suit with a blue trident on the jacket pocket.) Dave: Come over here and I will bite your legs off. Leviathan: I expected no less David. But why don’t I just take a seat. (Leviathan grabs a chair and places it next to Dave’s bed and sits down.) Leviathan: Nice quotation from the Holy Grail. That has to be my favorite part. Although, I do enjoy the Knights who say Ni or Nit. That always makes me laugh. Dave: You’re a (beep)ing Nit. Leviathan: Touche David. Dave: What do you want? It’s just your style to attack like this. Leviathan: I am hurt David. It would be more like my associate Lockdown to attack you here. I just wanted to have a small conversation with you. How are you David? Dave: You (beep)ing threw me off the cage. How do you think I feel? Oh, I’m Jim Dandy, fish head. Leviathan: Good one David, fish head. (Leans closer to the bed) Know this, David, the fish head can and will destroy you at anytime he chooses. Rage in the Cage was a walk in the park compared to what we have in planned for you and Malcolm. You failed to heed my advice and walk away from Rage in the Cage and this is the price you paid. (Leviathan motions to the hospital bed and Dave’s neck brace. Leviathan then reaches into his jacket and pulls out an envelope which is marked David.) Leviathan: Here you go David. Dave: You can shove that up your… Leviathan: Again I am hurt David. We go to all the trouble to get you a card and you offend us. Dave: The sight of you offends me. (Leviathan opens the envelope and reads it.) Leviathan: David, get well soon. From, The Cell. P.S so we can break your (beep)ing neck. That last bit was what Lockdown wanted to put in. (Leviathan laughs at the card, and then tosses the card onto Dave Harley. He stands and walks out the door. Dave’s face is filled with rage and he grabs the card, only to rip it to pieces.) Dave: We will see who has the last laugh. (Fade Out)
