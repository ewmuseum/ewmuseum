Worlds collide!!

25 Jul 2003

By Shaman

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
** Shaman is sitting back and watching videos of Eddie Van Dorn wrestling. He quietly observes each move and stops the tape and advances it slowly and surely. Bit by bit he begins to make notes on a notepad as he watches the move advance through its paces as he stops it at certain points and takes notes. He then turns and places the pad down upon the table and speaks.** Shaman: " Eddie Van Dorn about time our paths crossed. Our match had been set up for months and nobody got to us. Well finally its come for us to put on a match. Well as you can tell i am taking you very seriously. I respect your abilities and skills and im watching for the flaws. Unlike Nik Segier believed everyone has flaws. His flaw was fighting me in an Extreme match. Anyways enough about the past lets get to the here and now. You and i are facing off in the next meltdown. Im sure your going to bring an arsenal of moves as well as i. Just want it to be known if it goes to the extreme aspect i am more than capable of the same!!" ** Shaman leans back as the video continues to play through numerous segments and moves and such as Shaman looks on with interest.** Shaman: " I could sit here and brag on how i could kick your ass, But instead im going to address you achievements. You stated you had always been the bridesmaid not the bride. You know i have been to the pinnacle and back. Though my ride at the top was short im on my way back to it. I have grown tired of waiting to go back to the top. The champions have been dodging me till recently. Kash is world champion. Whoop dee friggin doo. Ive beaten him before and would do so again if given the opportunity. Well enough of that. Ive seen you kick plenty of ass across the world in one form or another had a helluva match at Bourbon Street and did really well in the Rumbles. Ive got respect for you. In the ring obviously all the respect in the world goes out the window. We will be two men out to kick each others ass. Just the way i like it!" ** Shaman pulls the video tape from the tape player and pushes another one in as he begins to consentrate on the next one.** Shaman: " Damn i have to tell you with all the action going around these next few weeks. Im glad i get a match with you Eddie. Just the fact for some action to get the blood flowing is pure adrenaline. See you in the ring on Meltdown. Im not going to drag this interview out any longer. Im just waiting on you!" ** Shaman turns back towards the tv and watches more tapes and picks up the pad taking more notes as scribbled across the wall behind Shaman and the TV is two words..** GOT BLOOD!!
