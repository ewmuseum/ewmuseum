Damian Thorne? Who's time?

18 Jan 2003

By Damian Thorne

Item 1 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 18 Jan 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Clock faces, some racing, some ticking, some broken, spinning from the darkness. There is obviously some symbolic meaning to this particular piece of tv production.... A voiceover splits the dark, and the images fade away..replaced by a shaking, sepia style screen, filled with images of wrestling history, and wrestling future from the WWA. "It's all about time. Who's had theirs and who's it is now. It affects us all, the ebb and flow of time. We only have a finite time to do all we need to, to carve or legacy, to prove ourselves. Some of us have that luxury of time, some of us don't. Some of us have squandered it, p*ssed it all away by virtue of their own self importance. These are the types of people who refuse to let time change them. They can't evolve, they're too stubborn to. If you cast your eyes over the recent history of the WWA, you will see where these rules apply. The "big dogs" of the roster, the top tier, the old guard. These are the people who won't change with time, who still think that it's their time. All.....but one. That man, is Jackson Kraven. He deserves his position, his respect, and most of all, his shot. He has allowed time to change him, and still he remains "the man", possibly more dangerous than ever, and best of all,....he runs with me. See, we have the ability to defy time... Call us immortal? No.. Call us gods. (The sepia begins to burn away, as if made of paper, finally burning away to reveal darkness again. A digital timer appears, it's numbers glowing a eerie red. It begins to count down, running ever closer to zero) Time should dictate it's will to everyone, force them to conform to it's rules. But it doesn't. There are those among us who can make our own rules, enabling us to carve our legacy, outside the rules of time. People like Chance, Nick Adams, Christian Payne, Venny Letrav, Havoc, Enigma, ME...and yes, even Kash. WE are the people who will rewrite WWA history WE are the people who will carry the company on our backs WE are the people who will become legends through our hard work and determination, and will underline it at the misfortune of others. If you need a label to explain it all, call us... THE FUTURE. (the digital readout begins to countdown more quickly) For some, time has run out. It's gone, long gone. These people crave the spotlight once again, and try to get there off the backs of others, unable to get there on their own. The wannabes..... and the has-beens. Wannabes? People like Thornhill, Kassanova...like Randy Cross. They reappear, just pop out of the woodwork, and expect that they can just step right back into the spotlight, pick up where they left off. What they don't realise, is they just don't deserve to be there anymore. Some of them just vanish when they realise that nobody cares who they were in the WWA history, because that's all they are...history, belonging in the past. Some, have to be reminded that there time was over long ago, and it costs them...dearly. They don't get to hit the next stop down the ladder, they don't get to become has-beens. They just get forgotten. Has-beens on the other hand, still litter up every promotion's roster. Powered by ego and very little else, they still feel they deserve the spotlight that they held when they once "had it", when they still knew their way. Deluded Sad Finished Do I have a point to make in all of this? My point is this. Has-beens meddle in the affairs of others, try to leech the spotlight from the deserving.... (the readout suddenly races to zero, the numbers burning red) 00:00:00 Not on my f**king watch they don't. Tracer f**king Bullsh*t... Shane Dorian Time, for you two, is up. I should allow the f**king booking team to give the Tracer v Dorian feud a shot of quick heat off the back of me and Kash? Kash should allow it? No.. F**K NO I'm sick and tired of watching roster leeches suck the life from the younger, better talent to attempt to revive themselves. I'm f**king sick, PERIOD, of not be recognised for the main event talent I am. Wes f**king Sanders? Ian "Mongo" Tense? Why am I even mentioning these names in a main event capacity? Tracer f**king Bullet and Shane Dorian? Why am I even being saddled with carrying them...yes CARRYING them in a match on Meltdown? What is it Warr? Punishment? Am I being punished for once again being one half of the hottest feud the WWA has seen? Punished for making you money you rat bastard? Don't push me too far Matthew... Why should I have to contend with Dorian's overwhelming apathy? The very thing that cost him his spot in the HoP. He had an oppertunity to revive his dying career, and he p*ssed that away. He had an oppertunity to revive himself again by feuding with Mr Bullsh*t, the king of has-beens. An easy fight right? Well, he's managed to p*ss that away too, hence why Kash and I are saddled with carrying their feud too. So where is Tracer f**king Bullshit in all of this? NOWHERE Never has been, never will be again. Times up, career over, never gonna be a headliner again. If I've told you once Tracer, I've told you a million times. Just leave. End it. The president hates you The booking team hate you The roster hate you EVRYONE..hates you. We could all care less if you live or die Tracer. Nobosy wants to see you hit the comeback trail, nobosy wants to watch you wrestle. We just want to see you go. What's it gonna take huh? What's it gonna take until you get it through your thick head that it's time to go? (The clock begins to run in reverse, going into minus figures) See Tracer, you OWE us. You owe the WWA for all the times we've put up with your sh*t. You owe the young guys for all of your non selling. You owe your wife the chance to not see her man making an arse of himself each time he hits the ring. Hell, you owe it to yourself to not stare at the lights again, and again, and again. But most of all, you owe it to me and you owe it to Kash, to just stay the f**k out of our way on Monday. You and Dorian don't even belong in the same ring as us, let alone teamed with us. So don't rely on a tag, don't expect me to save you, don't expect to win. Win or lose...it doesn't really matter to me. All that matters, is that I get my hands on the tattooed freak who'll be standing across the ring from me. And Kash? Well...Kash feels as strongly about this situation as I do. How do I know? Because he told me himself............
