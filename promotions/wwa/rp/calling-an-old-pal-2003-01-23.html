Calling an old pal

23 Jan 2003

By Extreme Corp

Item 4 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 23 Jan 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Camera opens at a bar, “Murphy’s." The patrons are your usual middle class, drinking their troubles away. The bartender knows most by sight and even remembers most names. At the bar are three regulars. All three are looking at a corner booth.) Bartender : At first I thought I was in the twilight zone. Barfly 1 : Yeah the other day they came in, they didn’t even insult each other. Barfly 2 : I know, it’s different to when they put the pinball machine through the window. Barfly 3 : Hrmmph. (Barfly 3 dives into his pint of beer. We pan to show that the people in the booth are Mal Somers and Dave Harley, “The Extreme Corporation”. Both are wearing street clothes.) Mal : What the hell is Cruz up to? Dave : Warr probably stopped paying him in tequila. (Both laugh.) Mal : Well he can kiss my … (A mobile phone rings. Mal Somers reaches inside his jacket and retrieves a mobile phone.) Mal : Hello (pause) Yeah, this is Mal Somers. (pause) Oh, hi Jack! Good to see you have arrived. (Long pause as Mal looks at Dave) Mal : Dave did you send in Jack’s immigration papers? Dave : Yeah I had them here in this pocket and...(pads the pocket) Hold on. (reaches in the pocket). $hit, here they are. Mal : Um, Jack, Dave forgot. But don’t worry we’re on our way. (Long pause. Both take a final swig from their glasses of beer and leave the bar.) Dave : What did he say? Mal : Well, Dave... the words were if we don’t get there ASAP, as soon as he merges the two butt cheeks in front of him into an ar$@hole, we’re next. (Fade Out)
