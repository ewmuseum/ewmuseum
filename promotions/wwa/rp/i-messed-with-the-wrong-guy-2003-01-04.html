I messed with the wrong Guy?

4 Jan 2003

By Enigma

Item 4 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 4 Jan 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
{Darkness} I messed with the wrong guy? {Enigma’s face appears in the darkness} Not a chance. Jimmi, you don’t seem to realize is that I don’t care that you seem to think you revolutionized the Cruiserweight Division. I created my own division. I am in a league of my own. {Holds up the two titles} This is my division. Zion, I am the current Cruiserweight and Extreme Champion. You are calling this your time? Your Time? The clock struck midnight for you along time ago. This is my time. You are an extreme cruiserweight? You have yet to show me anything that would prove that fact. You want to talk about what happened a year ago? WHO CARES! What happened a year ago, does not matter. That was then and this is now. {Enigma’s face appears to be filled with rage} Sky Flyin Jimmi Zion, I plan on sending you to the moon. I am not going to let you take my titles. Mister Millenium you call yourself? Well that’s just fine, I am going to bust your ass up. Humans can fly Zion, but remember what goes up must come down. I plan on winning this match and doing whatever it takes. Jimmi, I said you ass was in the wrong place at the wrong time before. And on Monday you are in the wrong place once again because you are in the ring with me. You may have a tag team partner and that’s all good, because you could not beat me on your own. You could and never will beat me one on one Jimmi and that’s a fact. {Enigma pauses for a second and regroups} As for my other opponent, Chance, you seem to be a little slower then usual. I wonder why? {Enigma smirks} You know what Chance, I am starting to think that your buddy Crow is a smart man. He does not want to step foot into the ring with myself. Who can blame him? He might have been known as Everybody’s victim, but if he were to step into the ring with me, he would be another one of my victims. Chance, I know that your knee is hurting still and after this match you may not be able to walk again. Chance, I have been prepared for you for awhile. I know your strengths and I know your weaknesses. I have exploited them before and I will do it again. {Enigma clears his throat} Zion You are the next one to feel my wrath. Chance You are going to feel it again. Enjoy your time before Monday, because after January 6th, you might not be able to fight again. {Enigma smirks} NOTHING CAN STOP ME NOW {fade out}
