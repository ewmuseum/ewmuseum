Livin' on the Edge

3 Jul 2003

By Grunge

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
FADE IN (We open to find Julie Wilson standing on the roof of a large building. She’s wearing a white dress with a white neck choker. As we pan into her frame we hear a voice-over begin to play.) Grunge V/O: Yeah that’s not me. Sorry to disappoint you. (The camera decides now is a good place to stop as Julie looks around. She reaches into her purse and pulls out a pack of camels. She lights it with her old silver Zippo lighter and begins to take a drag till a hand reaches on screen and yanks it away.) Grunge: Stop… (Grunge holds the cigarette up for her to see till he flings it to the ground and rubs it out with his heel.) Grunge: I’m going to make you stop if it’s the last thing I do. Julie: It just might be… (Grunge sighs and the two go over to the railing were Grunge folds his arms and leans over.) Julie: I could have married a normal man… Grunge: (interrupting) Excuse me. (Julie puts her finger up to Grunge’s lips. Grunge does as he’s asked.) Julie: Let me finish. I could have married a normal man. I could have been happy living with an English Lit Professor as long as he had tenure. Instead I married a professional wrestler. Grunge: Well excuse me for changing your plans. (Julie once again presses her ring finger against his lips but this time she smiles.) Julie: Just let me finish. The life we have now isn’t your fault. I knew your dream before we married. I don’t like seeing your body be put on the line every night. But I knew it would be this way, I accept it. Grunge: So what’s the problem? Me confused. Julie: The problem is your…moral. (Grunge throws his arms in the air.) Grunge: Moral! Woman I swear… Julie: If you’d listen I like the fact that you’re a minister’s son. I love the fact that you do the right thing, even if others don’t agree with it. The problem is that when dealing with the Devil’s Own being moral can get you killed. (Grunge wraps his arm around Julie’s shoulder.) Grunge: Leviathan is just full of hot air. I’m more interested in winning the Independence Day Rumble. He’s just another goon laying claim to my title. Julie: I know you. You can’t stand men like him. You can’t stand people who are violent against women. You hate monsters. (Grunge looks a bit concerned.) Julie: The problem is that when you deal with people like that your living on the edge. Walking a fine line between your moral stance and what needs to be done to save yourself. I mean the Tiger… Grunge: Levi was never in any danger if he just followed the rules. Besides, like I said, if we have to face fine. He’s just another cocky jerk who got some payback for hurting my singing voice. Julie: You sure about that? (Grunge kisses Julie on the forehead.) Grunge: First we worry about me winning the Independence Day Rumble and then we’ll jump off that edge when we get there. (Julie smiles as she stares into the eyes of her husband.) Julie: Rock and roll. (And at that the two shares a passionate kiss as the camera begins to…) FADE OUT
