The Morning After the Night Before

4 Jul 2003

By Chance

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
‘Argh, what happened to me last night?’ I wonder this as I gradually begin to become aware of my surroundings over my thumping hangover. Honest to God it feels as if my head is going to explode. No, not just explode, it feels like it is being squeezed very slowly in a vice, while the Big Show is sitting on it, and I’m being forced to watch the Triple H vs. Steiner Royal Rumble match. I shudder as the thought of the match goes through my mind. I look around me to find that I appear to be at someone’s apartment. Wooden floor, covered in places with a rug or two, a dark doorway leading off to what appears to be a bedroom. The room has ugly brown wallpaper, or maybe it’s just dirty, I cannot really tell. It also has a small TV, that looks as if it came out of the 50’s and a fridge and stove, next to each other, in one corner. It also has a sofa, which I am currently waking up on. The most disturbing detail of this room, which I am listing last, but occurred to me first, is that on the sofa with me is a large, muscular and somewhat hairy man who has one muscular arm around my waist, while his other arm is firmly down my boxer shorts. From the feel of things I am not wearing anything except my boxers (I can’t check because my bedmate is sort of on top of me) and by the feel of things he is sleeping Au Natural. Oh dear. There also appears to be a bottle of Absinthe on the coffee table that I’m pretty certain has never been used for coffee. I wonder if the break up with Lucy had scarred me into becoming gay, I breathe in and take a whiff of the guy’s body odour and decide it didn’t. ‘Ok, how do I get out of this without waking the guy?’ I try to hold the sentence in for fear of waking him, but the hangover makes any sort of self-control hard. If it weren’t for the hangover I probably would have leapt off the bed and tried to haul the guy off me. As it is I just hold my breath and hope that if he does wake up he isn’t in an amorous mood. I think about my situation and reason that being a pro wrestler I could probably fend him off if he tried anything, so I try to get up. I hit a stumbling block in my plan when I realise that despite being a pro wrestler I’m not strong enough to lift 300+ pounds of dead weight off of myself. Oh dear, oh dear. ‘How in the bloody hell did I wind up here?’ I cast my mind back to the night before, and draw a blank. Nothing. Zilch. Kaput. Damn absinthe! Ok, ok don’t panic, what’s the last thing I can remember? Winning the Bourbon Street Brawl! Yeah, that’s it, I won the Bourbon Street Brawl! Let’s see, that was on Monday, and it is now … I manage to lift one arm up to my eyes, it’s big effort, and I look at my watch … Friday morning! What the *beep*? I’ve lost three days! How the hell? Ok, let’s see, what did I do after winning the brawl? Um … er … I’m pretty sure it involved drinking! And a bar! And … I have a vague recollection of Mal and Dave as well. So I was … celebrating with X-Corp! Yes, that’s it! For some reason I think Jill was there too, how odd. ‘Oh God, my head!’ The guy cuddling me stirs and I shudder again. At that moment someone walks through the door to the bedroom that I am extremely glad to see. Mal Somers: “Oh, I’m sorry guys, I’ll come back when you’re ready.” I struggle to raise my head out of the sofa cushions and shout out at Mal. Chance: “No! Mal! Wait! Come back! HELP ME!” Mal looks perplexed as he tries to figure out which of the two guys are shouting at him, he then recognises what appears to be one of my Hawaiian shirts, stained with various substances, and he then looks straight at me. Mal: “Chance? Is that you? Man, I knew the break-up with Lucy hit you hard, but I didn’t think you’d go this far! Not that there’s anything wrong with that!” I marvel at how similar Mal’s reasoning is to my own, then marvel at how stupid it is to not help someone who’s trapped beneath a guy about twice their weight. Chance: “Yes it’s me you idiot! Now get this guy off me! Please! Mal is very reluctant to lift the guy off me, which is understandable considering the fact that he’s overweight, very hairy, and he smells foul. Eventually though we manage to get him off me, and he tumbles onto the floor, still out cold. Mal: “Wow, that guy’s hairier than A-Train!” I take a closer look at the man who was lying on me. Chance: “Mal, that is A-Train. I think I’m going to be sick.” Mal: “Uh, me too, race you to the bathroom?” I get there first and am violently sick in the toilet. I hear Mal throwing up in his sink, and am oddly glad that he managed to get somewhere. I’m then sadistically glad when I hear Dave shouting at Mal, then wonder how Dave managed to fall asleep in the sink, and wonder more at how he fitted in the sink. A few coffees and showers later and I decide it’s time to get some answers. Chance: “Mal, what the hell happened to me over the last few days?” Mal: “Why would I know?” Chance: “Look, the last thing I remember is celebrating winning the Bourbon Street Brawl with you guys on Monday. I have no idea what I've been doing the past three days, and even less idea why I just woke up on your sofa with A-Train! What’s been happening?” Mal: “Um, well the night of the Bourbon Street brawl’s pretty much a blur to me too, all I remember is waking up here. Not sure how I got home from New Orleans, but I guess it’s just a good thing I got here! The next few days passed quietly, except that someone stole the X-Mobile, and then you and Jill arrived here last night, you said you’d invited a few people over for a party. So I consulted Rookie and we decided we could do with a good party after all our hard training recently. And well, for some reason someone invited A-Train, and you guys both got pretty drunk, and you had a few dances, I guess one thing led to another …” Chance and Dave: “Do not finish that sentence!!!” I begin to wonder why I turned up with Jill, then memories of the past twenty-four hours come flooding back … Chance: “Oh dear God, let’s find Jill guys, I think I need to ask her some questions! I have to figure out what happened or I’ll never be able to fight Enigma, I’ll just be too distracted. Come on, let’s go!” Mal and Dave look at each other, confused, then hurry out after me. Mal suddenly heads back inside and grabs Rookie. He looks at his living room for a second and suddenly yells out: “What the hell happened to my walls? They’re brown! I’M SURE THEY WERE BLUE YESTURDAY!!!”
