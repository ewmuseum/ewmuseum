Mightier Than The Gods

22 May 2003

By Se7en

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The scene opens up and we see nothing but blue sky, there are a few white clouds in the distance, but as far as we can tell it looks like a perfect day. The sun is shining brightly and we can hear little birds squarking in the distance. Slowly the camera scrolls down and we Se7en, in natural sunlight. His clothes look dirty and ragged, his mask looks worn and horrible. He has his head down but we can see his eyes staring at the camera. He begins to speak in his husky and deep voice. Se7en: "This week on Meltdown, I will gladly show Madison how well her Daddy wrestles. I will show her how un-immune you are to pain. Venny? I will grab your throat and drag it to the nearest camera, shove it right into the lens, so that your Daughter can see just how her Daddy acts in the face of Death, Your wife will see your lives together flash before her eyes as she ponders the worst.." Se7en isn't laughing, but you an tell he's starting to get confindent. Se7en: "..Your fear and embarrassment will show through, Your Family will watch in terror as i attempt to break your spine. I may not have locked you in the Painfull and Torturess 'Nevermore', but i will gladly show you at Meltdown. Jimmi Zion? Shaman? No, no, no.. its just me and you, LeTrav" He slowly lifts his head up and the camera slowly zooms in closer towards his mask. His eyes are peircing threw like red laser beams. Se7en: "The God of Sun, Apollo. His gaurd will be let down at Meltdown and the Pay-per-view, Darkness will surround you and your family. God of the Underworld, Hades. He will be prepared to welcome you when i enter you. The Slayer of the Sphinx, Oedipus. I will gain his strength and Power as we march towards the ring, at Meltdown and at the Pay-per-view. Venny LeTrav? The worlds Lord and saviour will not even be able to guide you through the pain" Suddenly, behind Se7en we can see dark clouds start to slowly appear. We could be in for some light showers. Se7en: "King Nidud, said to be the most insane King in his time. He has shown me the shining darkness of his ways.. He will be watching over me when we face each other, he will show me the next way to make you suffer. Odin, The God of War.. The war that we will make, will be nothing he could ever create. Odin idolizes my strength and power, he respects and fears my ruthless agression.. Venny LeTrav? We are all around you, We will bring the best out of you and bring your horror and pain to the surface, We will give you a heart of Hatred, a soul of vengeance. I am Susanoo, God of Sea. I am Jesus, God of Land.. I read your thoughts, i am your thoughts.. I am all around you Venny.. I will bring the real sadistic nature of Venny LeTrav out into the open. I will be your guide" Se7en raises his left hand and fixes his glove with the other. And then he adjusts his mask. Se7en: "I am the Harvester of your sorrow, The Prince of eternal Darknes, The Prince of pain, The oppitamey of evil. The 8th Sin. I am Se7en" The scene slowly fades to black with the darkened clouds getting closer to Se7en. FADE OUT.
