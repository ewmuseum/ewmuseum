Got any carrots?

25 Jul 2003

By Bonafide

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(It is another wet and windy day out on the Yorkshire Moors. The rain pours down and the land is shrouded in grey mist. Through the fog we can just make out a single tree, standing all alone in the middle of a field. Beneath that tree two rabbits sit, sheltering from the horrible weather and munching on the long grass. The larger rabbit has pale brown fur and a white stripe down his face, his name is Tyrone. The smaller rabbit has dark fur and a limp, her name is Erica. Erica grew up in the burrow beneath the tree. She was the third child in a family of 44. Her mother never really had time for her as a child and this led to Erica growing distant from both her parents. She began spending a lot of time with the 'Bad Bunnies'. These gang of juvenile rabbits regularly left the burrow and raided a nearby old ladies' garden. To start with it just seemed like a bit of fun, the rabbits sneaked into the yard and ate a bit of cabbage, no harm done! But over time the raids became more regular, they all ate a bit more cabbage, they stayed a little longer. Soon it became an addiction, they just had to have cabbage every day! They couldn't live without it. As these addictions often do, one thing led to another, the progressed from cabbage to 'hard' vegetables, like marrow and cucumbers. It was always destined to end in tragedy. After about 9 months of almost constant raiding the Bad Bunnies went out for their daily fix but the Old Lady had bought two alsatains! The dogs attacked the rabbits killing 6 of the bunnies. The remaining two were injured but managed to limp back to the burrow. Erica's companion died the next day leaving her alone, her family had rejected her, her friends were all dead. Erica was in 'Cold Lettuce' trying to recover from her addiction and was contemplating suicide. Then her saviour arrived, Tyrone. Tyrone was several weeks older than Erica had been born in warren just across the hill. He was the youngest in a family of 72 but got on well with all of his siblings. The warren was a happy place to be with all the little rabbits living joyfully in harmony. Then just a few days before Tyrone's 3rd birthday, the warren was destroyed to make way for a new road. The rabbits scatterd, Tyrone made it to Erica's burrow. Tyrone and Erica felt a kinship. He helped her to overcome her addiction to cucumber and she helped him over the emotional wrench of seeing his home destroyed and his family disappear. The two rabbits fell in love and were married on this very spot just 3 weeks ago. Now Erica is pregnant with 6 little bunnies all of their own. Tyrone and Erica are happy, they are excited about the brith of their children and are full of bright hope for tomorrow and the joy that day will bring!) Tyrone: Got any carrots? Erik: No, no carrots at all. (Suddenly a 4x4 speeds across the screen, leaving nothing but churned up mud and 2 very flat, very dead rabbits in it's wake. Slowly a note drifts to the ground. The camera pans over it.) "I am in hiding, they are searching for me! I cannot reveal myself in public. Dave Harley is part of the conspiracy I will get my revenge on the conspiracy by defeating Dave Harley on Monday! Maybe! James Curzon." (The screen fades to black as the note lands on the ground between the unrecognisable bodies of Erica and Tyrone.)
