Interview in a box

26 Feb 2003

By The Other Guy

Item 7 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 26 Feb 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
|The WWA has gratiously provided the guy on screen, a man of average height and weight- as far as wrestlers are concerned that is- and of no remark, with the WWA's hottest sales item... the Interview-in-a-box. Complete with a bland background scene, featuring the WWA's logo on a mat, a picture in a picture on the screen with a picture of the same bland man as on the screen, and since the WWA lacks a picture of the man the picture is of him looking exactly as he does on the screen, and a generic interviewer known only as "the interviewer". So thank you WWA for providing such top quality programs as interview-in-a-box.| The Interviewer: Welcome WWA fans and I am standing here with the up and coming WWA star- |After a dramatic, unplanned pause, the other guy finishes the sentence.| The other guy: John Doe. The Interviewer: Yeah, what he said. I'm here with... him... to talk about his upcoming match. |Whispered, from the interviewer to John, "You do have an upcoming match, right?"| Um...: Yes, my upcoming match with the great WWA wrestler named Ender. I consider myself to be a well educated man, a man of many talents and diverse interests. And one of those is my ability to read. I know of the book titled "Ender's Game", in which a child prodigy goes from genius to unconquerable general in a matter of years. |Such emotion, such passion, such- blandness- has never been heard.| He does have a name, right?: Unconquerable. Which means undefeatable. A man who cannot be beaten. Too smart, too skilled, too everything. A man without a weakness, and with strengths a hundred fold higher than any other man. The Interviewer: Yeah, that's right. He's undefeatable. |The interviewer is rolling his eyes.| The Interviewer: So what do you plan to do against such an opponent, an "undefeatable general" as you put it. That other guy there: Why, I will do what anyone in my position would do. I will find a way around those defenses, flank his forces, outsmart his plans, and defeat him. |Still no passion, nothing at all.| Mr. Charisma: There is no such thing as undefeatable. There is no such thing as unconquerable. I understand a man who would name himself after an undefeatable child general must have incredible skills. I accept that such a man will be a powerful opponent to overcome. I know and believe that the man may be everything that he thinks he is, everything everyone thinks he is, and perhaps a little more. But everyone knows the talent of John Doe, the great wrestler of the WWA. Everyone knows that John Doe is a man of limitless talents and grandoise charisma. The Interviewer: Oh, yeah, everyone knows that. So you're fighting "the great and powerful Ender", a man who has no equal in the WWA. And you're going to win. |Interview-in-a-box is a far from perfected item of the WWA, and thus the inexperienced interviewer is having more than a small issue with not laughing at the man beside him.| The man beside the interviewer: That is exactly right. I am going to show the WWA just what it is like when the undefeatable is defeated. I understand I have a grueling match ahead of me, a match that may go the full distance of its own time limit. I respect that Ender and I will match up in the middle of the squared circle and struggle for supremecy. I'll pound him, he'll slam me. I'll drop him and he'll throw me around. It is the way of wrestling. Only when one man is completely outmatched will the match go otherwise. But I am not outmatched, and I can expect neither will my great opponent. |The interviewer's eyes roll like a slot machine.| The Interviewer: So we're looking at a match to remember? The great and memorable... nobody: And more. In the end, though, I will have Ender in the middle of the ring, his arm wrenched around in the armbar, and he'll scream his submission. |The interviewer lets out a snort.| The Interviewer: Well there you have it. A clash of titans, as it were. And you'll see it only on the WWA. |The screen, thanks to interview-in-a-box, cuts rather than fades.| This interview brought to you by the World Wrestling Alliance and Interview-in-a-Box.
