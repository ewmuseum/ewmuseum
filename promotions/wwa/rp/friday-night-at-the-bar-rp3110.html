FRIDAY NIGHT AT THE BAR

20 May 2006

By Belial Kahn

Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A warm Friday evening in Buffalo New York greets 'KING KONG" Belial Kahn (as the fans of Japan have nicely titled him) and Tommy Starr The Definition of Sports Entertainer.. On there way back just getting off of there return flight to the states visiting Japan. Kahn is representing WWA in the Hokori & Meiyo 06 tournament as the limelight loving Tommy Starr accompanies him. Walking in to the bar they can't help but to feel some exhaustion from their trip. Dressed down in sweats, both take in a deep breath of that New York air with a childish glee. Upon entering the famous Merlin’s Bar, Starr receives an important phone call about a potential role. Waving Kahn off as if to annoyed by his presence he stands outside where it is more accommodating to the conversation then the ruckus inside the bar. Belial enters and walking over to the bar must stand, as there are no seats to support his massive frame. He then leans on the counter and request ” 2”Incredible Hulks” please.” A free seat on either side of the standing giant , over walks a familiar face whom takes the seat to Kahn’s left. Old Friend [7:36 PM]: Long time no see Belial[7:36 Pm sipping his just received drink]: Yes long time no see. what goes on , since we last toured together in Capcom’s wrestling school. Old Friend [7:37 PM]: You do know who I am, right? Belial [7:37 Pm with a look of confusion still sipping his drink]: ummmmmmmm yeah! You do know who I am right? Old Friend [7:37 PM]: 'Lord' Zuriel Kahn, (gesturing to himself with his fist to his chest) Sam Livingston. Belial [7:37 Pm with a look of confusion again]: Yes aka Master Yoshi Yawakuzi. Livingston [7:38 PM]: What’s going on, man?(extending his hand for a handshake) Belial [7:38 PM]: Not much, do you still speak German? (Engulfing Livingston’s hand in a handshake) {During wrestling school, Samuel Livingston went thru many a gimmick. One was the very popular Japanese masked assassin by the name of “Yoshi Yawakuzi” and the least popular was a German by the name of “Lupin Lazentalfenburger”. After he got tired of wrestling he put his hat in the arena of promotion, promoting small indy feds.} Livingston [7:38 PM]: Not really. I only know the basic words, Wilkommen , Guten-Tag Belial [7:39 PM]: So Cool I can speak jack sh#$ of it , I want to learn to speak German… Livingston [7:39 PM]: Why? Belial [7:40 PM]: Because I want to read a book but it is only in German, plus it is a strong language, has such strength to it . Livingston [7:40 PM]: True, But don't pick up the accent. Belial [7:41 PM]: There is a accent? HA, ill try not too Livingston [7:41 PM]: It's the worst accent ever. Belial [7:41 PM]: So how goes Samuel Livingston the original Cerebral Assassin Livingston [7:42 PM with head some what lowering in wat appears to be shame]: In one of my old feds, I was castrated, and have now quit wrestling or being on screen. Belial [7:42 PM spitting out his drink and knocking one over]: Castrated!!!!!? Good god , what happened , why ? You slept with your bosses wife or something? Livingston [7:43 PM]: It was at the end of one of the shows. Belial [7:44 PM]: What organization were you running? Livingston[7:45 PM]: It was Indy wrestling federation you should join. Belial [7:45 PM]: You need to join WWA . Livingston [7:46 PM]: Ha Ha , nah man, I'm a independent fed kind of guy. Belial [7:47 PM]: I have to be honest with you boss ,WWA is the top gun right now specially with that other one closed down. Livingston [7:48 PM]: O, yeah, very true. Belial [7:56 PM]: So do you remember what happened that crazy night can you give me details of what happened . Livingston [7:57 PM]: Well, I was on the phone in my office, when the lights went out. You could hear these scuffles, and then the next thing I was dragged out. It turned out to be Payne, who's disappeared since then. Belial[7:58 PM]: I remember what he looks like., and if I see him Il l be sure to get him back for you little buddy.{patting Livingston on the top of his head like he was a little brother.) Livingston [8:00 PM]: I have never shown my face since I have my son be the man for the camera, whilst I stay working backstage Before they could continue further in the conversation there came three hecklers to Kahn. singing at one point in unison "WELLLLLL IT"S THE BIG NOOOOOOOOOO,,," They began to taunt the giant whom continued to ignore him as he forewarned. “Walk away while you boys can.” Yet they persisted, with taunts such as… “Faker..” “Phony.” “Kyle James is going to mop the floor with you “ It all hit the fan when accidentally one of the guys in an attempt to get closer tipped over the giants 5th “Incredible Hulk” to which Kahn responded. “That does it.” The men took off running in a flash able to get into there pretty white GMC ENVOY. Locking the doors the giant tried his best to open the door. Starr coming up to Kahn “Calm down big guy before you do something...” Kahn interrupted “Get the truck now were leaving.”, telling Starr with an aggravated tone. “It’s going down.” Starr rushes off to do as requested Belial then still frustrated bends down and with a freakish strength Kahn lifts the truck toppling it over into the middle of the streets causing a major traffic jam as Starr pulls up on to the side walk in movie star fashion yelling with Busta Rhymes new hit playing in the background “New York Sh!^%” “Get in, let’s get the hell out of here.” Kahn jumps in the car and as they drive off he turns to Tommy. “You listen to hip hop now?” as the song plays in the background IF YOU FROM NEW YORK STAND UP RIGHT NOW IF YOU FROM NEW YORK STAND UP RIGHT NOW IF YOU FROM NEW YORK STAND UP RIGHT NOW GET IT UP GET IT UP GET GET GET IT UP GET IT UP GET IT UP GET IT GET IT GET IT UP Designed By [name withheld] | © World Wrestling Alliance 1997 - 2005
