The Lion's Den

24 Jan 2003

By Leviathan

Item 3 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 24 Jan 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(A lion is shown swiping at a trainer from within his cage.) The beast does not liked to be caged. (Scenes of Wild cats attacking trainers who have used their whips too many times.) When trapped they will lash out at anybody within their space. (Pictures of a Lion tearing at flesh from a dead animal.) Devouring those who fall victim as their prey. (Leviathan is standing in the center of a ring. The steel cage surrounds him, yet there is a sinister smile on his face.) Leviathan : Malcolm and David, bravo on your bravado. Mr Warr has seen it fit, to allow the two ex-WWA stars an audience within this fine structure with the CELL. An example has to made does it Mr Warr. And these two new pets of yours, have to be part of that example. I assure you Mr Warr they will but not as you plan. The Cell is rising and all in our way will merely be flies on a windscreen. (Leviathan grabs the bars and rattles them.) Leviathan : Do you really know why Cruz, call us the Cell? (pause) Leviathan : He believes we are nothing more than animals needing to be cage. So how fitting our first PPV match is in the confines of a Cage, a Cell if you wish. Lockdown was born for these matches. He has fought in them not for gold but for his very life. So Malcolm and David you are walking into Rage in The Cage but you are not walking out. (Leviathan stops then turns his attention to the camera.) Leviathan : Now to address the other people who have attempted to ruin our name. Zion, I will talk you in the schoolboy language you know. She hit me first! So live with it. If are paths cross again, the outcome will be more serious. Shaman, the time will come when we face again. I fear the outcome will be apocalyptic, but the result won’t change. As the CELL is always Brutally Truthful. Fade to black
