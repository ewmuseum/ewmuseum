Number Of The Beast

15 Jan 2003

By Jackson Kraven

Item 5 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 15 Jan 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
WOE TO YOU O EARTH AND SEA (The words appear white on black, in writing that jitters and shakes as if afraid of what it entails.) FOR THE DEVIL SENDS THE BEAST WITH WRATH BECAUSE HE KNOWS THE TIME IS SHORT (There is a voice reading the words, a voice unrecognisable due to a robotising effect, a voice which strips away all humanity from the words until all they are is a cold and impersonal threat.) LET HIM WHO HATH UNDERSTANDING RECKON THE NUMBER OF THE BEAST LET HIM WHO HATH UNDERSTANDING RECKON THE NUMBER OF THE BEAST (The one voice fractures into thousands, the general effect being that of a thousand mosquitos angrily buzzing in the listener's skull.) FOR IT IS A HUMAN NUMBER (The voice becomes a single voice again, and we see a wrestling ring now, as a single spotlight blazes down upon it. In the centre of the ring is the logo for Monday Night Meltdown, flecked with blood.) ITS NUMBER... ITS NUMBER... (The voice loops back on itself over and over again, madly chanting the same two words over and over again, until...) (Blackness.) (Blackness and silence.) (Until...) Its number, WWA, is up. (Jackson Kraven's voice can be heard somewhere in the darkness.) Just like the WWA's number is up and the caretaker champion of the WWA's number is up. Just like Denrol's number is up. Just like "Kevin"'s number is up. (Kraven suddenly appears in the darkness, lit by a red strobe light.) I guess you all saw Meltdown. I guess you all saw what happened. I guess you all saw what *should* have happened. (Kraven vanishes as the strobe dies to black. His voice remains.) Ian Tense, what you did on Monday Night Meltdown has lit a fuse within my brain. What you did on Monday Night Meltdown has sparked a countdown, which can only end one way. (Kraven appears again, this time lit by a blue strobe, in a different place from last time, nearer, infinitely nearer and infinitely scarier, his face made up to resemble a death's-head.) You can fight against it, and you deal out all the chairshots you want, but it only ends one way. It's as inevitable as tomorrow... (Kraven vanishes again, and when he speaks again his voice has been altered, so traces of that hideous, robotic voice from earlier creep into his words.) And you have No More Tomorrows...
