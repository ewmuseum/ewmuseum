Avoided Facing my Fears

29 Dec 2006

By Chris Macbeth

Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene opens up in a dark room you can just about see a bed in the corner and dark silloette of Chris Macbeth's aching body sat on a chair) Chris Macbeth: Everybody Fears something or someone, They may try to deny it but they are talking s##t. Fear isn't something to be ashamed of it's a part of everybodys life and there is nothing anyone can do or tell themselves to erase it from there Brains, Hearts and souls. Some People are afraid of Dying, Some people are afraid of war some people are afraid of Love and some people are afraid of waking up in the morning next to the same boring person they have been waking up next to everyday for the last 20 years. These people need to stop denying they have these fears but also don't let themselves get to down over it. They are only thoughts in your head or tight knots in your stomach nothing more nothing less your brain just working in overdrive all the time and you cant stop it. Most people never have to face up to there fears so whats the point of being afraid in the first place, Because there is a small quantity of people who's fears eventully catch up to them. My worst fear is failing. I have had to face this fear before when I failed in life and I made a mistake and ended up locked up in jail. Thats one place where I never want to be again and I am determined never to fail again in anything I do. When your fear does catch up with you it's like a black fog decending on your body. It seems like the whole world as turned dark and cold, you just have to suck it up and move on always look forward there will be a better place in the near future to aim for to reach out and grab to pull yourself through the darkest of days. Just under a week ago I avoided my worst fear because I entered into the Birthday Bash the rookie, the underdog the odds stacked against me and I may not have climbed the tron and been the last man standing and grabbed that WWA World Heavyweight title belt. Kade Williams did that and yes we had a brief run in the weeks before the Bash but I'll hold my hands up and say that Ted deserved that belt he worked his ass off and showed alot of these other teds whos boss. But back to me the odds were stacked right up against me being the rookie and all that but I went into Maddison Square Garden Confident and I new I was ready to make an impact in the WWA and didn't I just do that Winning the WWA United States title. One more thing. I would like to say. Christian Heights you earned yourself the Number one contendership for my United States Championship belt. And I'll tell you now you deserve it just like Kade Williams deserved to win the World Heaveyweight title and everyone else deserved to get whatever they got out of the Brawl. But Christian Heights you climbed up above most others and showed what you were made of. So I am looking forward to Facing up against you, It doesn't matter when it is, doesn't matter when it is, doesn't matter if its on Fiesta or on PPV or in the middle of the Macy's department store just down the road. I have this belt now and I am proud to be wearing it and I will defend it against anyone who has earned the right to face me for it . You people may think. Why Chris Macbeth why are you sat here in this dark room a couple of days after you have won the WWA United States title, Whay are you not out around New York celebrating. Well I can tell you this every good man needs some time on there own some time to reflect on what has been done. This has been my time to do just that. This being said I am now going to go enjoy myself (With that Chris Macbeth gets up and walks over to the door holding his ribs. He reaches out and pulls the door open. On the otherside you can see a crowd of people dancing, drinking having a good time. Chris Macbeth walks through the door and the crowd turn to him and cheer. Deborah walks over to him with the United States Title and Hands it to him.) Deborah: Here you go champ. You want a drink? Chrs Macbeth: Yeah why not.
