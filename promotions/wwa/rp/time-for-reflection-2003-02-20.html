Time For Reflection

20 Feb 2003

By Havoc

Item 5 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 20 Feb 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
In the dim light of the room, he sat upon a marble bench. The orange glow of the candlelight danced across his stern face. His long, dark hair fell in waves about his pensively bowed head. With a deep breath, Phobos spoke in a voice like boots crushing gravel. “This upcoming Monday night is the Global Warfare event. You heard about that, didn’t you? Of course, you did.” He glanced upward for a moment, although not seriously expecting a reply from his victim. “Five teams, each with four men on them, will come together in a bloodbath to represent their homes. The South gets representatives, the East Coast, the West Coast, and Great Britain, all have teams to represent them.” Phobos paused for a moment, reaching down to collect a bottle of water. While slowly unscrewing the cap, he continued speaking to his captive who hung from the ceiling by chains. “Imagine my surprise when Denrol, of all people, approached me about including Havoc in the team that would represent the rest of the world. He wanted us to join the World Team, alongside of both him and Kash. Of course, they’re both from Canada. Apparently, there aren’t enough Canadian wrestlers in the WWA right now to create an all-Canadian team.” He pulled the bottle to his lips; drawing from the cool waters therein, slaking the dryness in his mouth. He glanced up again, at the shadowed figure that hung with his wrists overhead. “Therefore, he came to us, the most powerful tag team that the WWA has known.” A small sound, perhaps of protest, came from the shadowed figure. A small smile crept its way across Phobos’ face as he surveyed the long, angry, red streaks that criss-crossed the victim’s heavy torso. Lowering his head again, Phobos stared at the whip that curled on the floor, like a cruel, black, menacing snake. “Of course, you would disagree with that. I respect the fact that you continue to cling to your delusions. As I was saying though, Denrol recruited us for the World Team. At first, I was reticent to join a band that would place us alongside of the enemy of one of our own.” Phobos glanced up again at the man that remained suspended. Deep, purple bruises marred the sufferer’s body like rotting fruit. “Of course, you were already unconscious by then, weren’t you? You weren’t fortunate enough to hear the good news. Havoc has joined the House of Pain. We, the most formidable tag team in the WWA, have joined with the only people in the WWA that are as commanding, and dangerous, as we are.” Again, a muffled grunt of protest sprang from the battered body of his prey. Another smile snaked across Phobos’ face. “Yes, we have united with the House of Pain to bring about a new era of dominance. The entire WWA shall understand what you will soon understand, that this shall be the year of pain.” He stood, rising to his full over-seven-foot height. His black, silk shirt hid the blood that his quarry had spilled. Only the dull reflections of the flickering candlelight gave away the presence of his victim’s precious fluid of life. “The real question that lies before me however is whether I can treat Kash as an equal. Thorne hates him with an unyielding passion. Can I really stand side-by-side with the enemy of my friend? Can I stand with Kash, knowing that it means standing against my colleague?” He bowed his head with a deep sigh, placing his hands on his hips. “But then, my first concern has always been for the contest, hasn’t it? I thirst for competition. I hunger for battle. I burn with desire to see my opponents driven before me in fear. Because of this, my need to prevail and be triumphant, there really is no choice. I must win, and in order to win, I must set aside my differences with Kash. I must rely on him as an equal, just as he will be forced to rely on me.” He paused, accepting his own words as truth. With a renewed sense of purpose, his gaze returned to his victim. He reached above, pulling a small chain until it clicked. Light burst forth, blinding in contrast to the previous murkiness. It shone brightly upon the face of the victim, bruises and dried blood clinging to his face, his left eye swollen grotesquely to obscure his vision, and a ball gag filling his mouth. “But, I suppose none of this really matters to you, Harley. The only thing that should matter to you now is the punishment that you’ve brought upon yourself. You failed us when we were the Dogs of War. You were an embarrassment. Then, you dared to bring that worthless insect partner of yours with you to Rage In The Cage. And you even went so far as to cause the Championship belts to slip from our grasp on Fiesta. For all of these things, Trapper, you must suffer. You must learn that your actions have consequences, both for you and for your partner. Shrapnel too, shall learn fear.” Again, a smile crossed his face, but this time no mirth shone from the smile, only predatory malice. “There will be no revolution, Harley. There will be only fear.” Trapper’s one usable eye stared back from the mask of pain, burning with defiant hate. End
