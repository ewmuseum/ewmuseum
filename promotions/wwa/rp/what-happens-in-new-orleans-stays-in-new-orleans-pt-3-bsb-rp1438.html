What Happens in New Orleans Stays in New Orleans (pt. 3) *BSB

4 Jun 2004

By Desolation

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Paid for by the New Orleans Tourism Commision (Two cops stroll down Bourbon Street. One is eating a small ice cream cone) Cop #1: I saw that new Harry Potter movie. It was fabulous. Cop #2: You mean fabulous as in gay or fabulous as in... (Desolation darts past the camera wearing only a pixalated square on his posterior and a wide grin on his face. One officer grabs his walkie-talkie) Cop #1: We've got a 412 in progress. That's right, drunk naked guy. We've got a drunk naked guy. Stand by. (The two cops approach were Desolation ran, but he is no were in sight. They look up at the top of a lamp post and the camera follows there gaze. Desolation has shimmied up the post and clasped onto it) Desolation: Look, I'm a monkey! No wait... I'm Spiderman. Look, I'm Spiderman! Cop #2: Sure you are. Now come on down. Desolation [singing]: Spiderman, Spiderman, does whatever a spider... (Desolation dives off the lamp and lands face first on the ground) Desolation: Oooof! (The scene fades to a black screen with the words "What happens in New Orleans stays in New Orleans". Desolation: Oh... I guess my super Spidey strength doesn't cover the nether regions... oh! -------- Later that night... (A police car slows to a halt on an unpaved side street. Illuminated by the head lights is a man lying on the ground. It's Desolation, and he's finally found something to cover up his nudity: he's wrapped the state flag of Louisiana around his waist. He's grabbing at his knee, in pain) Desolation: Ow... my knee! Please help me I'm hurt. (The cops exit the car and one runs over to Desolation. He kneels down by Desolation) Cop #1: You'll be alright. [to Cop #2] There's a first aid kit in the back seat. (With that, Desolation throws a handle full of dirt in Cop #1's eyes. He gets up and runs. Cop #2 follows him and reaches out to grab him, but only gets a handful of the flag around his waist, pulling it off. The flag is replaced by a pixalated square) (The scene fades to a black screen with the words "What happens in New Orleans stays in New Orleans") Desolation: FREEDOM! FREEDOM! ------- And finally, it's all over... (The two New Orleans cops are standing in the local jail. To there right is a jail cell. From this angle, we can't see who's in it, but we can guess) Cop #1: Finally got this looney off the street. (Cop #2 turns to the jail cell and taps his nightstick on the bars) Cop #2: You can sober up in here tonight, fella. (Cop #2 turns his back to the jail cell and towards his partner) Cop #2: I'm going out for some coffee. Do you want... (The sound of water hitting the floor) Cop #2: GOD DANG IT, ALL OVER MY SHOES! (The scene fades to a black screen with the words "What happens in New Orleans stays in New Orleans") Cop #1: HaHa, I guess we can add public urination to his list of offenses... haha.
