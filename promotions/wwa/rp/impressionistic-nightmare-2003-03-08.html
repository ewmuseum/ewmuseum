Impressionistic Nightmare

8 Mar 2003

By Grunge

Item 2 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 8 Mar 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
FADE IN V/O: This is a story but you don’t have to listen. (The black screen jumps to life showing the ending fatal-four-way match between Shaman, Christyan Payne, Grunge, and Nick Adams at Rage in the Cage. With Adams and Payne brawling, Grunge and Shaman are hurting on the floor. Grunge gets up slowly while Shaman is still out. He motions to Julie and she reaches under the ring to get some bolt-cutters. He clutches his back as Julie begins to open the cage with the cutters. Jimmy: What is this all about? Fat Tony: Maybe Grunge isn’t so stupid after all! Jimmy: Maybe not! (Meanwhile Adams gets the best of Payne and begins to climb the cage. Julie finally gets the cage open and Grunge dives through the door a split second before Adams hit the floor.) DING DING DING (Adams thinks he has won and raises his hands but the ref pulls them down and points to Grunge standing and holding his back. Adams argues with the ref but it is shown on replay.) Jimmy: Wow! A photo Finish! Fat Tony: I haven’t seen that since that trip to Miami when I won that money on those horse races. Jimmy: You mean lost money. Fat Tony: Yea, whatever. (Grunge grabs the belt and he and Julie hurry up the ramp as fast as they can while Adams argues with the ref. He pauses at the top with Julie and they kiss. The crowd gives a standing ovation.) Fat Tony: Well there is our new WWA TV Champion! (The clip seems to hit rewind and in super-fast motion and we show the whole footage backwards in seconds. Once again we are back to the black screen.) V/O: weshowrespect - evenwhenwedisagree – weshowrespect (The screen once again springs to life this time with the match from last Meltdown. Venny and Joey are still recovering. Grunge locks in the Cayhill Clutch, a combination Dragon Sleeper, Cobra Clutch. Adams thrashes and thrashes about. He tries to get the fans support but Grunge clamours too.) Jimmy: Both men trying to get the fans behind them. Fat Tony: It won’t work. Jimmy: Why? Fat Tony: Because they all support Joey K. Jimmy: Yeah right. (To the surprise of Adams, even though a weak Adams chant has started, much of the seats start chanting for Grunge. Adams can't reach the ropes and he has no other choice but to submit.) DING DING Jimmy: Adams submits! Fat Tony: Joey K wins! Jimmy: And Grunge. Fat Tony: Sure he helped. Gary Trudeau: Ladies and gentlemen your winners are Grunge and Joey K! (Again the screen rewinds in super fast time till we reach a black screen.) V/O: There is no luck, no cosmic accident. What will be is. (The screen begins to shift and we see static of a TV screen. We pull out to see Grunge’s face. His face is twist of calm rage.) Grunge: Welcome to my impressionistic nightmare. The thoughts of the past wash away and all I think of is the future. I know you are upset Nick. Let’s see how bad we each want it. Venny and Joey K don’t matter, not really, just you and me. (Once again the footage goes backwards just of Grunge speaking. While it does we can hear Grunge’s voice over the footage.) Grunge: Rock and roll. (We fast forward until we reach the end and then we simply…) FADE OUT
