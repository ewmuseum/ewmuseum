Boys Will Be Boys

12 Feb 2003

By Denrol & Bullet

Item 7 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 12 Feb 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(WEDNESDAY - 7:03 p.m. The home of Tracer Bullet and Melissa LeBeaux is empty, with the exception of Denrol's five pet cats. Extreme and US are sitting on the floor of the hallway facing each other.) Extreme: Mew? (A subtitle appears at the bottom of the screen. For ease of reading, all the cats' lines from now on will be shown in subtitles.) Extreme: This is a nice place. US: Yeah it is. Not as cold as our home up north. Extreme: Shame about Tracer's Kurt Angle collector's plate, though. US: Hehe. That was kind of fun. But believe me, the baldhead just scared me. Extreme: You really should have apologized. That's one of daddy's favorites, too. Not as much as Flair, but still... US: Well I tried, but you know humans. It's like we speak a different language or something. (US licks his paw a couple of times.) US: So you get the impression that Tracer is not very happy we are here? Extreme: Well, he seems to like me pretty well. But I am getting that for some of the rest of us, yes. US: Everyone loves you of course, you're a cute little kitten. But soon you'll grow up and get yelled at like the rest of us. Extreme: You only got yelled at because you broke the plate. I haven't seen anyone upset at TV, World, or Light Heavyweight yet. I was actually referring mainly to mommy and daddy. US: Yeah, I think so. Daddy seems to like Tracer, but I don't get the impression it's mutual. Extreme: Actually, I do think they're friends. It's just that he was kind of caught off guard by daddy's sudden appearance here. Not that he doesn't like him, he just doesn't want him in his house for a week. It's like Jack's friend on The Osbornes. US: Hmmm. No, I think Tracer really has issues with Daddy. From my knowledge of humans, I predict that Tracer will attempt to put Daddy to sleep within three days. Extreme: You really think it'll take that long? You live with him same as I do, and you know how he can get at night. US: Well Missy seems to restrain Tracer somewhat. Yes, I think it'll take 3 days. Extreme: Care to make a wager on that? US: Sure. I bet you my green mouse toy. Extreme: You're on. I'll put up my stash of catnip that it'll happen sooner. And if it doesn't happen at all, what then? US: Let's not talk about impossibilities. Well the toys are out. Up for a game of Mouse Checkers? Extreme: Sure thing. When do you think the people will get back from their night out? US: A couple of hours I think. Best out of five? Extreme: So three games. Gotcha. (US shakes his head in disgust and gets up, walks to the living room.) Extreme: Like it's my fault that I own him at Mouse Checkers. (Extreme follows US into the living room. Cut to a semi-popular nightspot somewhere in downtown New Orleans. I say semi-popular because it's not totally crowded, but the people there are really making themselves known. And by making themselves known, I mean making complete jackasses out of themselves. The camera focuses in on Melissa LeBeaux and Alexandra Korth.) ML: Should we put a stop to this? Alexandra: Questionable. I think it's obvious they are mature adults and realize what they are doing. ML: If you really think so... (The camera pans over to the karaoke stage where Denrol and Tracer Bullet are in mid-song.) Denrol: From this moment as long as I live... Tracer: I will love you. I promise this.. Denrol: There is nothing I wouldn't give. (The camera goes back to Melissa and Alexandra. In the background behind them, you can see a couple guys with the most horrified and astonished looks on their faces imaginable.) ML: You were saying? Alexandra: I say we lose the rings and flirt so we can pretend we don't know them. ML: If their next song is either "Endless Love" or "Just the Two of Us," I'm with you. Alexandra: We got a deal. (On stage, the two men finish up. Suddenly the camera shows a familiar face in the crowd. It's Simon of American Idol fame. He has a horrified look on his face and can be heard saying to himself...) Simon: They are the worst singers of all Earth time. (Simon gets up and leaves and we see Tracer and Denrol make his way back to the bar.) TB: Hey ladies. How'd ya like it? Alexandra: If I didn't already have to save my vomit for the morning sickness I'll have tomorrow, I would show you exactly what we thought of your performance. TB: Hey, it's like this... if I'm going to make an ass of myself by singing karaoke, I'm going to go all out and make as big an ass of myself as possible. And did you see the way that British guy was looking at us? We must have been REALLY bad! Denrol: That's the last time I do a dare with him. (Six guys who look like preppy college kids that had a few too many, walk over to the foursome. They mockingly clap and yell catcalls like "Bravo" and "Good show".) TB: Look, Gordon. It's the local Nowinskis. (Alexandra grabs Missy's arm and pulls her away, whispering in her ear. Denrol and Tracer don't seem to notice.) Denrol: I wonder if they can spell that name. TB: Not like it matters. At least we only did something vaguely homoerotic for shits and giggles. I can picture what their frat parties are like. I don't want to, but I can. (The leader, wearing the classic college letterman jacket stops laughing and speaks.) Guy: Aren't you two "rasslers"? TB: Maybe if you're from West Virginia, or out on the bayou, that's what you'd call us. But since you look rather upper class, I'd say your inbreeding happens in a different part of the country. Guy: Hey look, this guy is not just a wrestler and a bad singer, he is a smart ass too. (The rest of the gang laugh.) TB: Feh. I'm only a bad singer when I want to be. Unlike you, my poor qualities are quite voluntary, whereas your being ugly and retarded, well... (Denrol motions to the bartender and whispers something to him. The leader gets in Tracer's face and shoves him back with one hand.) Guy: At least I'm going to make something out of myself. I'm not going to be a fake wrestler. (Tracer steps forward, looking right into the guy's face. He then backs away, fanning the air in front of his own face.) TB: Whoo, I should have known better than to get up close to a pile of shit where I could get a good strong whiff of it. So I guess I'll tell you from back here... (Tracer takes a pack of cigarettes and a lighter out of his pocket and lights one up.) TB: Fake wrestler or not, that's what I did make of myself, and I work for a living. Whereas people like you... you get your way paid through... (Tracer walks around behind the guy, puffing a smoke ring into his face as he does so.) TB: Yeah, that's what I thought. You get your way paid through Tulane by your parents, and then go into business. I don't see where anyone needs to be talented or intelligent to make it in business, and I see that you prove my point nicely. (The bartender puts two bottles of Jack Daniels on the bar, Denrol thanks him and pays the man.) Guy: Business? I'm going to make good money. I'm going to be a quarterback in the NFL. That's right, I'm a REAL athlete. TB: Excuse me a minute, I have to put this out before I do what I'm gonna do next... (Tracer snuffs his cigarette in a nearby ash tray. The guy goes into a really piss-poor fighting stance. Tracer just stares at him for a second... then breaks out into raucous laughter.) TB: HAHAHAHAHAHAHA!!!!!!! Denrol: (is laughing with Tracer) Hehe. Maybe we should get the girls to take care of this. TB: Hee hee... yeah, what with the big tough football players and all. Oh, I was having a good time thinking he was an idiot for going into business, but this... that was just too much. (The leader just glares at Tracer, the rest get right behind him and get into their own stance, some decent, some just as awful. One in the back steps away and leans against the bar.) Denrol: So Trent, how many do you want? (Tracer looks at the bottles of Jack in Denrol's hand.) TB: I think one should be enough. I could handle both of them, but what's the point? I've got a busy day tomorrow. Denrol: I meant our friends here. I'm sure you want the one with the mouth. (hands Tracer one of the bottles) Guy: How about you two fakes get out of here before I decide I'm sick of your faces. TB: Oh, you're going to be a lot sicker in a minute, I promise. But really, Gordon, I'm with you... I say that Missy and Alex could take these guys... after all, I can tell just by the way they're standing that those two are pathetic street fighters, those two have seen a few too many Jackie Chan movies and... (Tracer looks at the leader of the group.) TB: This one appears to be constipated. Guy: You know this is real entertaining, but I am willing to give some charity. I'm a nice guy like that. How about you and your lover-boy here get out of here before you have to get carted out on a stretcher. I promise you this injury won't be fake. (Tracer turns to Denrol.) TB: You wanna tell him how fake your neck injury last year was before we show him how fake theirs are going to be? Denrol: Yeah, but if I wanted to talk to a brick wall, I would just do that. Guy: (shakes his head) If you bozos think you got a chance in hell, well I'll give you a free shot. TB: I'll take that free shot! (Tracer goes to the bar.) TB: Cuervo. Compliments of Dr. Douchebag over there. (The bartender chuckles, but doesn't move.) Bartender: Just try not to wreck the stools, I just bought them last week. Denrol: I think our little moron over here meant a free punch. I guess he wants a massacre. (Tracer walks back over to the crowd.) TB: Well shucks, Gordie, if he wants us to hit him first, the fight's going to be over extremely quickly. But tell you what... if you're feeling like you want to expend that amount of energy, fella, I suggest we take it out back into the alley. I'm kind of a regular here and I wouldn't want to see the place wrecked. Follow me, please... (Tracer walks over to the rear entrance to the establishment.) Guy: Sure. You first. (Denrol shakes his head and seems to be laughing to himself. Tracer shrugs and turns, the leader shoves him as hard as he can, but Tracer only manages to move a couple of feet.) TB: Now see... (Tracer opens the bottle of Jack and takes a shot from it, then screws the lid back on.) TB: That was kind of a mistake. Not because I'm going to retaliate right away, mind, but rather because you clearly put a lot of effort into that and I barely moved. Are you satisfied that even if this fight actually starts out, you can't win? Guy: That's it asshole. Hut! (The gang starts to charge forward, but a female voice yells out.) Alexandra: Whoa. Hold on there guys. (Alexandra and Missy steps between the two sides.) Alexandra: You have had enough fun already. Now get out. Guy: Who's going to make us? Alexandra: This will. (Alexandra pulls something out her purse and shows it to the gang. They grumble, than turn and leaves. Alexandra puts the object back into her purse.) TB: What the hell was that? Bar of soap? (Alexandra smiles and turns to Tracer.) Alexandra: You had your fun. You didn't want to go to jail and neither did they. TB: Well, you're right, of course. I'm not Shaman, I can't go around beating the daylights out of any stupid guy I find standing around. But it's fun to bait them. Alexandra: And I let you have that fun. Denrol: This is why I never go to bars with her anymore. She ruins everything. Her and that stupid FBI badge. Alexandra: it's called credentials and I only bring it with because I know when men are going to be stupid. ML: And when you two get together, stupidity is pretty much a guarantee. Denrol: I guess we better head back. The fun is over for the night. TB: I suppose so. After you. (Denrol and Alexandra start walking away and Missy takes Tracer aside.) ML: I thought you didn't like having them around? TB: (shrugs) Having them in our house is quite a bit different than hitting the town with them. Gordon and I always have a blast at places like this. (The guy in the back who left the group walks over to Tracer.) Pete: Nice work with Jimmy there. I'm Pete, big fan. (Pete offers his hand. Tracer smiles and shakes Pete's hand.) TB: The ring isn't the only place I'm quick on my feet. And yeah, I noticed how you pulled away from the back when things started to escalate. Smart one. Nice to meet you. Pete: Well Jimmy is a real moron, but his father is a pretty rich guy, so I guess he's spoiled that way. TB: So just like I thought, then. Pete: Yeah. So are you and Denrol really friends? I thought that was all an angle. TB: To tell you the truth, it wasn't always this way. Quite a lot of the stuff you see on TV has more to it than just an angle. Pete: Right. That's cool. Aren't you guys up in Canada right now? TB: That's what I thought. But sure enough, this morning... knock-knock... Pete: Well what are you doing here in New Orleans? TB: I live here, of course. And I'm not booked on a show until Monday night. Go ahead, ask what Denrol's doing in New Orleans. Pete: Well I knew he'll be here. (Tracer raises an eyebrow.) TB: How's that? Pete: It was in the paper, Denrol is opening a new Denrol's House of Pizza this week, right here in New Orleans. (Tracer looks out toward the front of the establishment, where Denrol and Alexandra are stepping into the car they rode in.) TB: "A vision" my ass... Pete: Yeah, Grand Opening is this Friday. You going to be there? (Tracer grins conspiratorially.) TB: Oh yeah... wouldn't miss it for the world... Pete: That's cool. Maybe I'll see you there. Hey got to go, the guys must be wondering where I went. Cool meeting you. TB: Thanks. See you around. (Pete walks outside to catch up to his companions. Tracer and Missy also begin to walk out of the bar as well.) TB: Oh, he is SO going to get it... (The scene fades to black as some guy gets up and begins to perform Endless Love.)
