Deep and Meaningful

14 Oct 2003

By Mal Somers

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(An A4 piece of white 80 gsm paper sits atop a kitchen benchtop. Upon it is scrawled several words in red marker. DEVILS OWN (a thick line is drawn diagonally across it. Next to that a smiley face.) C.P.E (with a big question mark through it.) X-CORP (three stick figures, one short, one medium sized, and one like a monkey are drawn around it.) FORSAKEN SOULS (a rectangle is drawn around it in red, and within, the box is 50% coloured in. A pair of hands, worn by the scars of battle, picks up the piece of paper. Mal Somers begins to speak.) Just a few weeks ago, the WWA was jam packed with stables of wrestlers, all vying to be the most dominant in the WWA. There is strength in numbers and many saw that as the way to get up the ladder so to speak. But sticking to cliches, a chain is only as strong as its weakest link. The Devils Own was a force in the WWA. They provided Dave and I with the stiffest competition we had ever faced. But then they went to Meltdown, and like a fire can only burn strong when given life by the oxygen around it, they fizzled out. Was it because X-Corp were the “yin” to their “yang”? I guess we will never know, but the fact is they are no longer. C.P.E. Cold Pain Enterprises. Who knows what they are up to? One week they are strong, the other they are weak. Now with their leader jumping ship to Fiesta, a big question mark lingers over their unity. Now onto the Forsaken Souls. Once they were dominant. Once. Then Se7en vanished back to wherever the heck he came from, and now Joey K has decided to hide from the man who crippled him, Dave Harley. Which leaves them as just two buffoons looking for a clue. Well I’ll give them a clue! Wes Sanders. You have been a thorn in my side for the past few months, and enough is enough. Bridge was right. I need to make a change and stop running from my worries, I’ve got to face them head on. And that’s what’s going to happen this Monday at Fiesta. You and me, one on one. And, quite frankly, I’m looking forward to it. You see, Wesley, you may not know this, but my recent loss to Enigma was a blessing in disguise. All the MALcoholics, everyone, even Blind Freddy could see that I had Enigma beat. But then, during the match, something to occurred to me. Remember the stipulations? If I beat Enigma, I would advance in the Intercontinental Tournament, but I wouldn’t have a chance to get my hands around your pencil neck. But, if I lost, I would be out of the Tourney but our singles match would be assured. Once you came down and once AGAIN stuck your big nose in my business, pulling the referee out of the ring, I made a decision. So what if I lost to Enigma? I’d get a chance to get my revenge on him after the Tourney, possibly even get a shot at the Title in the process if he manages to get past Venny. Make no mistake, I want that Title. But the clincher was…I would get you one on one and get the chance to get you out of my life. It was an easy choice in the end, and I lost to Enigma fair and square. As for Rookie, he slipped up and hit Chivo on the head instead of you, but again, blessing in disguise as I get to face you. See what I mean? A win-win situation. A match against my tormentor, a chance to face my fears, an opportunity to step up to the table. I can take care of Enigma at a later date. Right now, its all about you. So Wes, the past two months of torment…all your nefarious actions…will come back to haunt you this Monday at Fiesta. And that’s a promise. (Mal puts the paper down and grabs the red marker. We fade to black as he colours in another quarter of the Forsaken Souls box. Only the name X-Corp remains untarnished…)
