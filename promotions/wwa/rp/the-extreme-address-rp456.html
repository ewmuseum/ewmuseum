The Extreme Address

3 Sep 2003

By Dave Harley

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[A graphic of the GenX Title comes spinning on to the screen.] V/O: The following Public Service Announcement is brought to you by the WWA GenX Champion. [The scene opens on a presidential podium. An X Corp logo has been crudely stuck over the presidential seal. Suddenly The Extreme Machine Dave Harley steps into view and takes his place behind the podium. He is wearing a ‘Dave Harley: Brand X’ T Shirt and the GenX Title around his waist. He adjusts the microphone.] Harley: Fellow citizens, I stand before you now on the brink of war. This Sunday, I go in to battle against three men. There is a good chance that I may not leave in one piece. After all, the odds are hardly in my favour. You can be sure that there will be casualties. This is unavoidable. You see, I’ve come too far and worked too hard to get where I am today without one of the three stooges taking it all away from me. So let’s examine the enemy more closely [Harley pulls out a badly drawn picture of Fuel. Well, it must be Fuel – it’s a stick man playing with fire. Go figure.] Harley: First we have Fuel. A certified pyromaniac. This is the man I will face in the first round of the GenX Gauntlet Match – in an Inferno Match. Some may call this a very stupid move, fighting a pyromaniac in an Inferno. Well, as a great man once said in one of my favourite movies ‘there’s a fine line between stupid and clever’. Let’s face it; it’s got to be safer than going out in a pair of tights to take on Homo/Phobic! Fuel loves to set things on fire. Well, let’s hope he likes it when he can smell the flesh from his body cooking. Burn, Baby, Burn! [Harley shows the camera another stick drawing. This one appears to have dreadlocks…or snakes on their head. Could be Medusa, but it’s most likely Jimmi Zion.] Harley: Once I’ve dealt with Fuel I take on Mr Extreme Days Jimmi Zion. You’re right Jimmi, we are living in Extreme days – the Extreme days of the GenX – Treme Machine. But if you think you’re walking out of Labour Day Brawl as the GenX Champion then you’re wrong. In fact, you might say you’re living in an extreme daze! Ha! Get it? Jimmi, you are part of the reason that this belt fell in to disrepair. I’ve picked up the pieces. You think I’m gonna let you mess it up again? [We are enthralled with another stick drawing.] Harley: Yeah, sorry about these. I couldn’t find any pictures of these guys to bring with me! Just use your imagination! So when I’ve defeated Mr Entrance, the cell is lowered around the ring and out comes Joey Gay. Me and Joey have a bit of history. I beat his sorry ass at Independence Day Rumble for the TV Title. It was Joey that unleashed Brand X on to the WWA. I’ve been watching you training Joe. Believe me, it’s not gonna be worth a damn once you step into that cell. I’ve been through Hell once before and I walked out of it with Gold around my waist. They say lightning strikes twice Joe. There’s no easy way to leave that cell Joey, only the ex… Man: HEY YOU! What are you doing in here?! SECURITY!!! Harley: Oh, err…hey! Must have got separated from the tour group! Will the President mind if I borrow this podium for a few minutes? [The broadcast is brought to an abrupt halt as security march into the room and dive on top of Harley. We fade out to a picture of the GenX Title.] V/O: This Public Service Announcement was brought to you by Brand X! Now with added Extreme! ONE MAN. THREE CHALLENGERS. THREE DEGREES OF EXTREME. WWA LABOUR DAY BRAWL 2003.
