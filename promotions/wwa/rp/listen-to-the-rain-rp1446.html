Listen To The Rain

6 Jun 2004

By Odium

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
{Your screen goes black for a second and the WWA Logo surges forth. The screen goes black again for a second before we are met with a scene of a field, it looks like a scene out of a movie. The scene is almost completely black and then suddenly a lightning bolt shoots down and hits not more than tewnty feet away from the camera. We hear a creeking from behind the camera. The cameraman turns around and standing in the doorway is none other than the man who hates everything, Odium. He is wearing a pair of athletic shorts and a black top. He walks over and takes a seat in front of the camera. He is leaning back as we hear nothing but the distant rumblings of thunder and the sound of rain drops hitting the roof. It appears that we are on a porch or something. The scene is very sinister looking as we can see Odium sitting there, grinning, with lightning striking in the distance behind him. He begins talking.} Odium: You know, when I was in there, I saw something on the television that grabbed my attention. You see, there were a few words from a certain Jack Griffiths on the television. He seemed to be claiming that he will be the person handing out all of the punishment at Fiesta, he seemed to think that he is the better of us two, he believes he is El Diablo. You see, I knew automatically that this must have been fake because we all know that I am the one who carried his ass on my debut not too long ago at Los Diablos Danzante, he just ended up winning because he got away with cheating. There is no way in hell that sorry excuse for a wrestler carried me in a wrestling match. The man can't even be brave enough to wrestle Jurgen Raymann, he tries to be cool be knocking him off the stage, but the truth is actually that he is afraid of wrestling anybody here. Believe me, it takes a helluva lot more than just watching people wrestle to learn how. Right now Jack is nothing more than a glorified backyard wrestler. {Odium sits back in his chair and a strike of lightning hits directly behind him. He maintains his composure while we can visibly see the cameraman shake. Odium smirks and then begins talking again.} Odium: You see Jack, there's a little thing in the World Wrestling Alliance that we call 'hopeless wannabes'. It's what you are. Jack, you are the worm and I am the early bird so to speak of. I call you out, you so predictably crawl out of the little hole that you live in, I beat you down, or in the bird's case eat you, and your wrestling career is no more, or for the bird, your life is no more. I am the predator you are the prey, and I am going to get you soon Jack. {Odium sits furhter back into the chair as another bolt of lightning strikes, seemingly right behind him. He remains calm again and we can see no visible signs of fear in his eyes.} Odium: Now Jack, if you look at our opponents there are a couple of people who you have been watching rise to superstardom here in WWA, one of which is, let's say someone like Kash. Now, let's face it, placing you in the same ring as someone like him wouldn't be the brightest of ideas, you'd get your butt kicked, just like I am going to do to you at Fiesta. But the point is, it is still fun to watch you get the crap kicked out of you, let alone beating you up myself. Fiesta is going to be an enjoyable night, oh yes indeed, it is. {Odium lets out a bit of a chuckle and listens to the rain drop on the roof for a couple of seconds.} Odium: Jurgen Raymann. Who had the bright idea of putting him in the ring with anybody other than, oh I don't know, Scorpion. The only time Jurgen can even lay a finger on a fellow WWA wrestler is when he fights someone who seems as if they're not even there, E.G Scorpion. Don't get me wrong Jurgen, I don't have anything against you, but why are you even here in the WWA, you have no hope here. {Odium starts chuckling a little bit harder, as though the thought of Jurgen Raymann wrestling is hilarious.} Odium: Anyway, back to what I was saying, oh yes, Jack Griffiths. What was it he was saying about who's worse off between a blind and deaf man? That really isn't hard at all, and it isn't all that philosophical if that's what you were aiming for. The deaf man is worse off. Let's take your scenario, both of them going to be hit by a car. With the blind man, the person in the car can poke his head out and say "Dive to your left!" Simple. However, the deaf man won't be able to hear the car coming, therefore he won't turn around to see it coming, and the driver is unable to shout "Dive To Your Left!" because the deaf man can't hear. So next time you go and try to be clever with things to try and make you think, don't, because you're going to screw it up. {Odium stands up and goes over to the corner of the porch as lightning begins striking more rapidly outside. In the corner of the porch there is a ladder leaning against one of the walls. Odium picks it up and looks at it. He then places it in the up-right position. It is as tall as the porches ceiling. He locks the legs into position and sits on the third rung. He then looks at the camera.} Odium: Now, to continue speaking to and about the man I am facing on the 14th June episode of Fiesta. It seems as though I am going to be facing Jack Griffiths yet another time. This time, another special stipulation has been added. It will be more on the line as we fight for the Diablo Title, and I will win it from his hands, trust me. I will gladly walk into Fiesta on 14th June and beat the hell out of Jack again. The results, I know will change from last time we fought. Because this time, rather than being declared the loser, I will be declared the Winner and the new Diablo Champion. You know what, that has a bit of a ring to it... Odium, WWA Diablo Champion... damn, that does sound nice. {Odium gets off of the ladder as the scene fades to black.}
