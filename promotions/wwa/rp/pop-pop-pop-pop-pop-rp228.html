Pop Pop Pop Pop Pop....

4 Jul 2003

By Mal Somers

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(We begin Insider with the unusual image of Mal Somers sitting on a chair, crouched over, his head bobbing up and down, blowing away....) (And we suddenly cut to the commercials! Maybe I'll have a quick word with the producers, hold on, I'll give them a call.) . .. ... .... ..... ...... ....... RESUME TRANSMISSION (We recommence Insider, where we can see Mal Somers sitting upright, blowing up baloons! He gives a final puff and ties a knot on a big pink balloon. He pulls out a black marker and writes "PSYCHO" on it, and taps the balloon in the air. We follow the balloon as it rises up and across, until it lands atop some more balloons of varying colours and sizes which are on top of a large net spanning the width of the room. Somers stands up and walks to the centre of the room, his shoes squeaking on the polished timber floor.) Somers: When we last left Steven "Psycho" Murphy, he was "picking up the pieces". How apt. Because after the Rumble this Sunday, you will be picking up the pieces of your career, your body and your life as you know it... which will be destroyed if I have anything to do with it. You talk about how X-Corp will be "Just Another Victim of the Psycho!" Highly original catchphrase by the way...must have taken you weeks to come up with that one! (Somers smirks as the camera zooms in closer.) Somers: Murphy, this Sunday, I'm going to be trying something original myself. And its not going to involve gimmicks or catchphrases....honestly! Would I lie to you? (Somers chuckles to himself.) Somers: Yes, this Sunday, I'm going to walk right up to you...and throw you over the top rope! As well as a few other nasty people here in the WWA. The Forsaken Souls, the Devils Own, and so on and so forth. Why? Because I can. Why do I know that? It's simple, but let me take the time to explain it. (Somers takes a deepe breath and begins his rant.) Everyone else in the Rumble rants and raves about their strategies and their game plans...they read books and quote authors. This may help when you are cramming for your next remedial English class, but the fact of the matter is, it won't amount to a lump of Sanders if you cant throw people over the fricking TOP ROPE! Heck, I used to run my own company all but a few years ago, and almost on a weekly basis I'd have some anal retentive clown waltz in with degrees and diplomas coming out the ying yang. I'd ask them how their weekend was and they would start quoting obscure motivational and team building tactics that would help benefit the triple bottom line in the June quarter! (Somers drops his head down and just as quickly looks back up.) Somers: Whoop-di-do! It all amounts to nothing if you CAN'T GET THE JOB DONE! Rookie and I have been watching all the Rumbles the past few days, and you know what? Throwing people over the top rope....and preventing yourself from suffering the same fate...is all that is required. It's not rocket science! And if that fails... (Somers reaches up and grabs a piece of cord attached to the net and yanks it downward. Twenty nine balloons float to the ground. We can see a green one with "LEVI" on it, a yellow one with "ZION" on it, a lilac one with "Sanders" scrawled on it amongst the others. Somers pulls a pin from his shirt pocket and starts poking away.) POP! POP! POP! POP! etc. (Soon enough, Somers is standing alone, with many popped balloons littering the floor.) Somers: WWA...the past few weeks have not been kind to me. I've lost my WWA Tag Team Title, been beaten up by Leviathan, Imperceptus and Wes Sanders..to name a few...I was the only X-Corp member pinned at the Brawl, and whilst Dave and Chance are booked in singles matches, I'm treading water. (The camera zooms in to Somers' steely gaze. It is the first time we have seen his stare without even a hint of frivolity to it.) Somers: I'm going to collect all this rage and bundle it up...and take it with me to the Rumble...make a change for the better. And WWA, (holds up the pin) if I have to be a PRICK to win the Rumble...trust me...I will. Sunday will be my INDEPENDENCE DAY!!!! (Somers stands there staring at the pin, breathing heavily as we fade to black.)
