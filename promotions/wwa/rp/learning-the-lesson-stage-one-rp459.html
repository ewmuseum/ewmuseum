Learning The Lesson - Stage One

3 Sep 2003

By Dave Harley

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
LESSON 1: FIRE [We open on a rather dirty looking old warehouse. We can hear sounds from within. Being the curious audience that we are, we peer in through an open window and stare at the scene within. A makeshift wrestling ring has been set up along with a few punching bags and such like. It looks a bit like Mickey’s Gym from the Rocky movies, only Sylvester Stallone is not training – The Extreme Machine Dave Harley is. He stands jabbing at the punching bag. In the far corner stands another man – WWA Assistant Commissioner and X Corp Trainer Jack ‘Bridge’ Knight.] Harley: So I took a look at some of the predictions on the WWA website… Bridge: Oh yeah? Harley: There’re not a lot of people who think I can pull this off y’know. Bridge: I know Dave don’t worry about it. I’ve trained you up for every big match you’ve participated in. You’ll be ready by Sunday. Harley: Can’t you even the odds or something? After all, you are the Assistant Commissioner… Bridge: Don’t think I’m gonna be going easy on you boys just because I’m calling the shots. You’re gonna earn your keep the way I always trained you. Harley: No problemo Jack my friend. By Sunday Night I’ll be ready to take those three assholes out. I’m the GenX-Treme Machine! Bridge: That’s the spirit! Now turn around! Harley: Huh? AAAAAAAAAAAAARRRRRRRRRRGGGGGGHHHHHHHH!!!! [Dave lets out an incredibly girly scream as he sees his trainer stood with a flamethrower in hand. He shoots a huge fireball at Dave’s face. When the flames have diminished Dave just stands there looking rather dazed.] Bridge: Fire tolerance! That’s the key to the Inferno Match! Harley: …wha?…What’s cooking Jack? It smells nice! Bridge: That would be your eyebrows Dave. [With a yelp, Harley douses the flames.] Harley: Umm, Jack, I’m feeling a little…‘well done’. Can I have a lie down? Bridge: Sure. But I’ve got big plans for you tomorrow! Harley: Great. I’m just gonna… [Dave passes out on the floor as we begin to fade out.] Bridge: Dave? Dave? You think this is bad you should see what I’m doing to Mal and Chance! ONE MAN. THREE CHALLENGERS. THREE DEGREES OF EXTREME. WWA LABOUR DAY BRAWL 2003
