Breaking news

1 Mar 2003

By Lockdown

Item 1 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 1 Mar 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
We interrupt your regularly scheduled program to bring you this breaking news bulletin “Ladies and gentleman, this is Linda Yiu, reporting live from Walt Disney World. As you may be able to tell from the mayhem behind me, Disney World’s most well known building, Cinderella’s Castle has erupted in flames.” Behind the reporter, a scene of utter mayhem has erupted. Park officials and firefighters scurry to and fro. Also standing just behind her and slightly to the left is Lockdown. Lockdown is carrying a large gas can, and is making every manner of rude and vulgar gestures. “The blaze broke out about 20 minutes ago and is spreading like…well, wildfire! Authorities suspect foul play, but currently have no suspects.” Lockdown looks around for a moment, then hurls the gas can. “As I said, I am here live and I am going to try to get an interview with one of the park officials.” Lockdown promptly steps up and tells the reporter he is ready for the interview. The reporter is taken aback as Lockdown does not exactly look like a park official OR a firefighter. Reporter: Uhhhh… Sir, would you please tell me who you are and what we know at this time. Lockdown: Don’t worry about it babe, OF COURSE I work here. As a matter of fact, just gimmie the fu**in mic Lockdown pries the microphone from the reporter’s hand. Lockdown: WHHHOOOO-HOOO!!! This IS the happiest G*ddamn place on earth!!! That’s right, Linda, the authorities suspect foul play. But nothing is as foul as the current state of the WWA. (Aside to Linda: Pretty fu**in good segway, huh! Ha! And you went to school for this sh*t) How many times do we have to kick the sh*t outta the Extreme Corporation before they realize they aren’t even on the same level with The Cell? Time and time again they run their little mouths to every weak ass b*tch who will listen about how they are gonna mop the ring with me and my partner. Yet time and time again they step into the ring and get beat like a rug! I mean sh*t!! They’re like the Cincinnati Bengals of the WWA! Tonight they’re trying something a little different, but the result will be the same. Who are they fu**in kidding? Does anyone really think a cruiserweight and little Mal Somers can take us out? (Aside to Linda: Hey, b*tch, check out this segway. You might learn a thing or two.) Folks, the fire behind me is big news, but not as big as the announcement I have to make right now. For months my partner, Leviathan, and I have been warning the other WWA superstars not to meddle in our affairs. Most of you have been good little girls and done as you were told. Unfortunately there are always some who need to learn the hard way. Shaman… Jimmi Zion… there will be no more warnings. Tonight on Fiesta, the bloodbath begins. Especially for you, Zion. You are about to learn the hard way that the best way for a man to protect his “family” is not to put them in danger in the first place. Ya see, Jimmi, while my partner may be a bit reluctant, I have no problem whatsoever with ending your little boy’s life right along with his mother’s. This is not another fu**in warning, Jimbo. I’m telling ya this so you’ll know that it’s all your fault. If only you had listened to your dear sweet wife, this story could have had a happy ending. You could have prevented it, “daddy!” (Out of the corner of his eye, Lockdown sees Linda Yiu, the reporter, motioning some police officers over.) Lockdown: This is Lockdown, signing off. Back to you, Linda!!! (Lockdown drops the mic and hits the afterburners. He is outta here.)
