X Corp: The Induction Part 1

17 Jul 2003

By Dave Harley

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The scene opens in a rather large New York apartment - presumably belonging to X Corp member Mal Somers. Seated in the living room area is Dave Harley. He is wearing a party hat and is holding the WWA Television Title. He looks at his silly grin in the reflection of the gold as Mal Somers walks in carrying two beers.] Somers: Here ya go buddy! A beer for the Champ! Harley: Thanks Mal ol' buddy. Somers: Finally Rookie gets a playmate! He was starting to get a little grouchy. You know how it is when you're living in each others pockets. Harley: Or around each others waists! BWAHAHAHA! Somers: Huh? Harley: Umm...nothing. So where's Chance? Somers: Should be here any minute. [Guess what? That's right. The doorbell rings.] Somers: That must be him now. [Mal leaves to answer the door. Dave gets up and places the TV Title on to a nearby recliner. He picks Rookie up off the couch and sits him next to him...I mean 'it'. Chance walks in with a small present under his arm.] Chance: Hey Dave. Congratulations on the TV Title win! Here, I brought you a little something... Harley: Hey thanks man. You guys are the best! [Dave rips open the parcel and pulls out...] Harley: [Gasp] A magic 8 Ball! I always wanted one of these things! Chance: Heh. Thought you'd like it! [Mal walks back in with an extra beer for Chance.] Somers: So you ready for the big Title match? Chance: Yep. Pretty much... Harley: Wait a minute, I'll ask the 8 Ball! [Harley shakes it up.] Harley: Will Chance leave Truth or Consequences as the World Champion? 8 Ball: 'My sources say Yes' Somers: Do me! Do me! Harley: Will Mal beat the Forsaken Souls on Monday? 8 Ball: 'Definitely' Harley: Will I beat Lockdown and become the Gen X and TV Champion? 8 Ball: 'Doubtful' Harley: What?!? Wise ass 8 Ball! Chance: So how do you think the roster split is gonna work out guys? Somers: Well, I hear X Corp will probably be kept together as a group... Harley: Whew, that's a relief. I guess things will work out alright. Somers: But...but... Chance: What is it Mal? Somers: Well there's one amongst us who hasn't exactly pledged his X Corp allegiance...Rookie! You don't think they'll split us up do ya? Harley: Hmmm...gentlemen, I have a plan. Chance: We should induct Rookie in to X Corp before the split? Harley: ...No, but that's an even better plan! Somers: Then it's settled then! We induct Rookie! Harley: Well, um, how about my TV Title? Somers: Huh? I dunno. I'm not sure how well they get along. Rookie: ... TV: ... Somers: You see? Chance: Yeah. Imagine the divorce proceedings if the two of them didn't get along. Probably best to keep the TV Title as an honorable member for now. Harley: You guys are probably right...OK then! We have a ceremony to prepare for! TO BE CONTINUED...
