Finding The Extreme

30 Aug 2003

By Dave Harley

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[We see an empty cinema. Empty bar one seat. From behind we can see the occupant has long brown hair that has been tied back in to a ponytail. The occupant also has his arms over the seats either side of him. He is wearing what appears to be a long sleeved T-Shirt. The sleeves read ‘Extreme’ and ‘Machine’ in silver lettering. No prize for guessing who this is then. On the screen in front of him is a familiar scene. A scene which has been rewound and watched countless times. The last Hell In The Cell match Dave Harley participated in. The Tag Team Title match from March Madness.] Harley: This was it. The match that everyone talked about. The night that me and Mal proved to the rest of the WWA that we were more than just Scrabble and Happy Meals. The match which garnered me my first WWA Title. It’s a pretty familiar scene now. The Extreme Mobile igniting a trail of gasoline, the gasoline igniting the chemical coating of the cell, the four of us fighting for our lives on the cell roof… [As we see the scene played out before Dave Harley, we watch as the cell swells and buckles. It crashes down to the floor. The arena full of people descends into a stunned silence. We watch as a head emerges from a dumpster. It is the brutally beaten head of Dave Harley. But the crowd’s cheers are silenced as we see Leviathan, hands clenched around Harley’s neck as he rises from the wreckage. With his final ounce of strength, he chokeslams Dave into the rubble.] Harley: See that? I fell almost forty feet when that cell collapsed and was then choke slammed by Leviathan. To tell you the truth, I don’t remember much of that night. I remember hearing an explosion whilst I was on the roof of the cell…that’s it. Next thing, I’m lying in a hospital bed with the Tag Team Title draped across my waist. [As the match ends, the scene cuts to another clip. This time in a converted car park with a huge scaffold in the centre. On the third tier of the scaffold, we see Dave Harley and Deimos trading punches.] Harley: Next PPV, me and Mal raised the bar again. King of the Hill. Three other tag teams… [We see Deimos pick up Harley with a military press and toss him over the side. He sails through the air, landing with a bloodcurdling thump in the flatbed of a truck.] Harley: Another horrific fall. I didn’t even know we’d lost the Tag Team Titles until I came around in the hospital. I guess the point I’m trying to make with all this is that I’ve taken one hell of a beating over the course of my career. I’ve been thrown from scaffolding, dropped through a table from the top of a cage, burnt alive on top of a cell…and look at me. I’ve enjoyed ever minute of it! The 21st of July 2003 will always stand out as an important day for me. Look, I think it’s next… [He’s right. The clip starts to play. We see Harley pick up Lockdown in the Harley Bomb position. He runs at the turnbuckle, forcing Lockdown’s back to smash a crate of beer on the turnbuckle. Lockdown rolls around on the mat, covered in beer and alcohol as Dave goes for the cover.] Harley: …right there. I thought I’d done it all. I thought that losing the WWA Tag Team Titles was the end of the ‘Extreme’ part of the Machine. But I was wrong. I lost the TV Title because of Joey K. He reintroduced me to the ‘Extreme Machine’ and in return I brought glory back to the GenX Title. [Scenes from Harley’s recent battles are displayed on the screen. Bonafide, Enigma, Joey K…] Harley: I put the X back in GenX, and I’m keeping it that way. I face my toughest challenge ever this Sunday. Fuel, Jimmi Zion and Joey K in a Gauntlet Match. This time I have no tag team partner. This time it’s just me. I’ve been burnt before, I’ve taken the fall before, and I’ve taken it to new levels of Extreme. I’m gonna make this belt of mine proud. I’m gonna take those GenX wannabes to places they never imagined. I’m gonna drag them through Hell and back. I am the GenX-Treme Machine. For these men there is no longer an easy way out… …there is only the EXTREME way out… [We fade out to a picture of the GenX Title.] ONE MAN. THREE CHALLENGERS. THREE DEGREES OF EXTREME.
