The Searcher (part 5)

24 Jan 2003

Author not credited in the source

Item 7 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 24 Jan 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Private Investigator Dirk Flint is sat in his office. He looks contented, he is smiling with the knowledge of a job well done. He lights a cigarette and puts his feet up on the table. He found the missing man and delivered him to his employers. Now he can sit back and relax....) (The Phone rings) Flint: Hello? You again, what? I did the job, I found the guy, now what? (We can't quite make out the reply from the phone, it doesn't sound pretty.) Flint: Whoa! Calm down, you say the job's not complete? Why the hell not? Two guys! You never said there was a second individual for me to locate. (The voice speaks again.) Flint: I'm not finding another guy for you, my job is done. I'm taking a break now. (The voice speaks. Flint looks stunned and the cigarette drops out of his mouth.) Flint: How much? Now that's like 8 zeroes! You got yourself a detective, buddy. So where should I start.... (The picture fades out and comes again on Flint's famous red '56 caddy, it's parked outside a set of huge wrought iron gates. Through them a long drive leads up to a very large house. Flint presses the intercom.) Voice: Hello, how can I help you? Flint: Hi, is that the owner of the house? Voice: No, sir, this is the butler. Flint: I'd like to speak to the owner, please. Voice: What business do you have with the Master? Flint: I'm a Private Investigator, I've been hired to find him. Voice: In that case you probably know that the Master has not been here for several months. Flint: Yeah, I did know that. Voice: So why did you ask? Flint: You got to check all the angles! Voice: Quite right, sir. (Flint takes out his notebook.) Flint: Do you have any idea where he might have gone? Voice: He did not leave a fowarding address. After he left his last job he became a little depressed. I believe he has gone on something of a journey of self discovery. Flint: A journey of self discovery? Great, another wacko. So you have absolutely no idea where he could be? Voice: The last information I have had is that he made several phonecalls to Moscow. Flint: Moscow? You mean I gotta go to god-damn Russia now?! Voice: No, sir, Moscow, Idaho. He has an old friend there. A man by the name of Levi Steel. Flint: "Jewish Wolverine" Levi Steel? The wrestler? Voice: I believe so. He runs a training school in Moscow, I believe the Master was planning to visit there first. (Flint writes this information down.) Flint: Of course, He trained under Steel. If he wants to "find himself" it's logical to start there! Voice: Indeed, sir. Flint: Thank-you, you've been a great help. Voice: I know, sir. Goodbye. Flint: Ciao! So he has gone to "find himself" therefoe I must go find himself too. (Flint gets back in his car and drives off into the distance. Next stop: Idaho! Fade to black.)
