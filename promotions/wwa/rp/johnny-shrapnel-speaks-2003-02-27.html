Johnny Shrapnel Speaks

27 Feb 2003

Author not credited in the source

Item 5 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 27 Feb 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(It is raining. A lot. WE are in the middle of a muddy field. Johnny Shrapnel is stood in the middle with a dazed expression on his face. He stares upwards with his mouth open. He has sunk into the mud up to his ankles.) JS: Ughuf! (He coughs as his mouth has filled with rain water and he was about to drown.) JS: Havoc! I hate Havoc! (He falls backwards into the mud and lies still for a moment.) JS: They stopped my from getting my 5th Tag Team Title, they kidnapped my partner and they smell of cheese! (Johnny pauses a moment to sniff his armpit.) JS: Although the smell could actually be me. (He sits up quickly and mud splatters across the camera. Johnny wipes it off and we can now barely see him through the dirt smeared across the glass.) JS: Havoc do all this stuff and now I am in a field of mud. Typical! This always happens to me. (JOhnny stands up and starts walking away.) JS: I have to go. I've got two fat asses to kick. (He pauses and turns back.) JS: And I need to take care of Havoc! So I will journey across the whole country to take care of business. I will rescue Trapper and together the Veteranz will walk all over you, like a knife through butter! (Johnny is pleased with this and runs off towards Meltdown.) Fade to black.
