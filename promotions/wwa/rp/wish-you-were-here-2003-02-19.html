Wish you were here

19 Feb 2003

By Lockdown

Item 6 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 19 Feb 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Scene opens and we see Lockdown standing, with tag team title belt draped over his shoulder, in a beautiful room of what must be a mansion. The room is laden with priceless antiques. Lockdown is standing in front of an immense rosewood bookshelf, filled with well worn classics. Next to Lockdown and behind him is a large wooden door. From behind the door, the sound of bedsprings squeaking, grunting, moaning, and a knocking noise can be heard. Lockdown: (to cameraman) Ok are you ready? Cameraman: Whenever you are. Lockdown: Ahem. Hi Mal! I haven’t had the pleasure of kicking your sorry ass in a while, so I thought it would be nice to send you this video post card. Ya know just to let ya know I still care. I wanna let ya know how bad me and Leviathan feel about what happened to your computer. We feel kinda responsible ya know. After all you broke it cause you cared enough to vote for us. Anyway, Leviathan wanted to be here too, but Miss Wise called him into an emergency strategy session. (Points over his shoulder to the door.) They wouldn’t let me in, though. That’s kinda strange. Oh well she never liked me much anyway. I’m sure Levi will run everything by me for approval. (Miss Wise’s voice is heard faintly through the heavy door: OH YYEESS, BABY!! JUST LIKE THAT! YOU’RE SO BIG) Lockdown: So Mal, The Cell is the WORST tag team in history? Maybe you lost too many brain cells with all the beating we have given you, but ya don’t get these for sellin Girl Scout cookies. (Points to his belt). You get these when you’re the BEST! You can run around all day with your pathetic little rookie title belt, but you know and I know the rookie belt don’t mean sh*t. By the way how long have you held that little toy? 3 months? How long were you in the WWA before you won it? See Mal, the fact that you still have it is no fu**in big accomplishment. All it means is that the powers that be here in the WWA still see you as nothing more than a rookie. You STILL haven’t stepped up to the next level. You should be thanking us, Mal. You still hold that title because we made sure you never had to defend it. (Miss Wise can be heard as the knocking grows faster and louder: YOU”RE THE BEST! GIVE IT TO ME!!) Lockdown: Now these belts on the other hand mean The Cell is the top of the food chain, Kings of the Hill. Not a single man has bested us. Not you, not Harley, The Cool Guy and Bane Edwards were a joke; Zion couldn’t do it, nor the Veteranz, not even Havoc. If you have any delusions about Shaman and his “mystery partner” derailing the fastest rising tag team ever, keep dreaming. It just doesn’t fu**in matter who he brings. I don’t care who it is. Nobody can stop us. Hell, he can even bring the World Champion Ian Tense and it wouldn’t change a fu**in thing. Me and my esteemed colleague are gonna leave their broken bodies and spirits lying in a pool of their own blood in the center of that ring. If you have any plans to interfere, I say come on with it. I’ll be glad to give you some too. (Miss Wise’s moans grow louder and louder until they become a scream, then a long, deep sigh. She says: Oh my god! Mal NEVER gave it to me like that.) Lockdown: Before I go, I wanna say I hope you’ve enjoyed watching this video post card as much as I enjoyed making it. Leviathan says he’s sorry he couldn’t be here but he sends his regards.
