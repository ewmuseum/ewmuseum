Times are changing in the W.W.A...........

21 Oct 2005

By Stan "The Pit-Fighter" Jones

Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Stan opens the door to his pick up truck, takes his gym back from the passenger seat and climbs out. He looks up at the E centre with wide eyes. He shakes his head then walks towards the building when a man approaches him, he looks like a reporter of some kind) Man: Hi my name is Dan, I report for the official WWA meltdown magazine… (He is a reporter) Dan: I understand that you are the newest member to the WWA locker room. What are your thoughs on your future in the WWA? (For a minute Stan looked like he was just going to walk away but then he stopped and he had a think about it. Why should he get his say? Everyone is entitled to there own opinion) Stan: I think my future is pretty bright. Don’t you see it, cause I do. I see all the rookies infiltrating the WWA, Xavier Anderson, Ramone and the rest. People like Mr Darkness and Dave Harley are gone for the moment, who actually knows if they will come back or if anyone would actually want Dave Harley back. It’s a new dawn for the WWA; it’s a new era. It’s the perfect time to stand up and be counted, time to shine, to show guys like Bullet, Khaled, Denrol and Ember what we are made of. Time to show them that we can run go toe to toe with the best of them. That’s why the debutants ball is happening this Monday, all the rising stars looking to make there mark, looking to show there worth. There’s something missing from that picture on Monday night, me. What would you do? Sitting in the front row Monday night watching the new guys showing off their skills, getting to grips with the fans. At first I had a game plan, find one of the new debutants and beat him senseless, so bad the guy cant wrestle so old Stan will be drafted in. Then I had to think again, because that’s not the road to travel down. I don’t want people to think that I am a vicious monster. I don’t want to be labelled as a lunatic, a madman. Not yet anyway. I personally hope all the new guys do well, because each and every one of us has something to prove. You better believe People like our new T.V champion will be watching these match’s, this affects everyone. Its like a new lease of life for the WWA, fresh blood. Time to cut off the dead wood and begin a new chapter. I watch guys like Tracer Bullet, and I see that their time is nearly up. Tracer has a family; he has a beautiful child and a mighty fine looking wife. When I see WWA tapes of old, I see the intensity on Tracer’s faceq when he steps into that ring; I see the hunger and the passion for winning. The desire for that world championship belt. Now when I look at him I see a tired man, I see an old dog on his last legs. That is in no way meant to disrespect the man because he is a legend and always will be one of the best in this company, but the point I am trying to make is that all things must come to an end at some point and time. The reason I am picking Tracers out of the bunch is because he is a prime example. A prime example, that even the best of us have to bow down at some point and time, have to make way for the new guys. Tracer doesn’t want that belt anymore, he doesn’t want this life. He wants a quiet life with his family now, which is of course understandable. Infact if I was him I would just retire that belt right now and go home to my wife. I look at that him and he has everything, everything that I never had. If I had a wife like that, I wouldn’t be here, I would be with her. Anyway that’s not the point, the point is this is the best possible time for the rookies to stand up, the WWA is entering anew chapter, this coming PPV should not be called Day of the dead, it should be called day of the rookie……… (The reporter is talking everything down. Stan looks at him) Stan: I know what your thinking, just another big mouth right. Well that’s not the case at all, I have studied the WWA past and present, and I see what’s going on. Why are so many rookies being drafted in if the company is pleased with what they have got. There not. Vik Ventressa knows it, that’s why he’s after something new. That’s why guys like me are getting there chance, sure the older WWA superstars, the ones who have been here for quite some time, they mite not appreciate my point of view but they know its true. The way I see it, the rookies are the ones who will be running the show soon. Sounds stupid right, well it is. It is madness. But that doesn’t mean it cant happen. That doesn’t mean that Stan Jones isn’t going to go out there and fight for his life, and in a year or two become world champion, oh no. You see I have eyes, and if everyone opens there’s then tis not hard to see that the WWA needs new talent. Well here we are, time for guys like Tracer Bullet to bow down and take a final look around at there surroundings. Take a final look into the ring, take a final look at there championship belts, because its my time, and it’s the rookies time. Times are changing, some guys are on their last ride, and some are just beginning their adventure here. One thing is for sure; Stan Jones is here to make an impact. (Stan walks away from the reporter and through the doors of the E Centre) Should I have said all that, maybe not, but that never stopped you making mistakes before. Either way im not going to sit down and play nice little rookie, if want to say something I am going to say, if I don’t then people are just going to walk all over me. I aint in no mood for that, I am in a fighting mood, and its feels dame good. (Stan smiled as he walked down the corridor towards the locker room) Designed By [name withheld] | © World Wrestling Alliance 1997 - 2005
