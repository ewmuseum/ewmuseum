LAX to Ronald Reagan Washington Airport

3 Jan 2007

By Chris Macbeth

Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene opens up on a plane Chris Macbeth is sat in business class next to his trainer Harry. Harry has the window seat and he has his earphones in watching the movie in the back of the seat in front. Chris Macbeth is sat by the isle reading a book. The camera focus' on the cover and shows that he is reading Michael Crighton's Novel Prey. His head lifts up as the pilots voice comes over the speakers through the cabin) Pilot: We are now approaching Washington DC would you please take not of the fasten seatbelt sign above your heads. We will be starting our decent in just a few moment. Hope you have enjoyed your Flight this morning and have a very pleasant stay in Washington. (The speakers crackle and Chris Macbeth closes his book first folding over the corner of the page to keep his place. Harry pulls off his earphones and places them in the compartment in the back of the seat in front. He then turns to look at Chris) Harry: How you doing? Macbeth: Good, I'm doing good. How was the movie? Harry: I've seen better but it was ok. I hope this isn't as rough of a landing as when we went to New York for the Birthday Bash cause I hated that. Macbeth: I think it should be ok. The flight has been good so far. (The air hostess walks down the isle doing the final check of seatbelts before heading back to there own seats and buckling up ready for landing. Harry turns and looks out of the window and Macbeth leans out from his seat spotting a kid a few rows down the isle reading a wrestling magazine. He spots the page the kid is looking at and focus' on the heading.) US CHAMP & #1 CONTENDER TO TEAM UP. (He notice's a picture of himself holding up the WWA United States Title at Birthday Bash and also a picture of Christian Heights from the same event. He can't make out any of the other writing on the page so he just sits back up in his seat and gives Harry a light elbow in the ribs.) Harry: Hey quit it. Macbeth: You see anything out there yet? Harry: Yeah. Clouds (Harry leans back letting Chris lean across him to look out. The plane bursts through the clouds and Washington DC comes into view) Macbeth: Here we go. Harry: Finally my legs are starting to get stiff and I cant stand this guy behind keep kneeing me in the back. (Chris Macbeth is now looking down the isle at the kids magazine again. He can see the page is open on a picture of Heavyweight Champion Kade Williams holding up his belt, another picture of himself holding his US Title and Cleary with the TV Title.) Harry: What you nosing at? (Macbeth looks round to Harry.) Macbeth: Ah nothing. This kids just got a WWA magazine. What do you think this is all about? Putting me in a tag match with the # 1 contender for my US Title? Harry: I don't know but I would watch yourself. Heights is more than likely to turn on you. Try and weaken you for his title shot whenever it maybe. Macbeth: Yeah I know. (Chris Macbeth looks down reading the back cover of his book and Harry again peers out the window seeing the ground getting closer and closer until the wheels finally touch down and the brakes raw trying to slow te plane down. The pilot's voice comes over the speakers again.) Pilot: We have successfully landed in Washington it is a nice day out there today. We are just moving on round to the docking area. PLease remain seated with your seatbelts fastened until we have reached a complete stop. Again on behalf of myself and my crew I hope you have had a pleasant flight and you enjoy your stay in Washington. Thank you for flying with us today. (Harry turns away from the window) Harry: Good landing. Macbeth: I told you it would be. (Macbeth reaches under the seat in front and pulls his bag up onto his lap. He opens it up and places his book in and zips it back up) Macbeth: I hate this part. Just waiting for he plane to stop. Harry: Tell me about it. (With that the plane stops and the seatbelt sign turns off. As always everyone gets up at the same time trying to grab there bags from the over head storage. Macbeth gets up placing his bag he cleverly just stowed under the seat onto his seat and grabs Harry's bag out of the storage compartment hands it to him the picks his own back up. They then just wait for the doors to open.) Harry: Here we go. (The doors open nd the queue of passengers stood in the isle begin to filter out. Chris and Harry both stand up throwing there bags over there shoulders. An elderly couple let them into the queue in front of them and they start to make the slow walk off of the plane.)
