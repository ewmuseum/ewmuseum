Soon they will be mine once again

22 Jan 2003

By Enigma

Item 6 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 22 Jan 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
{Darkness} February Second, 2003 will be a great night for the entire House of Pain. On February second, the House of Pain will dominate. Damian Thorne will destroy Kash and take his United States Title. Jackson Kraven will make sure that Denrol never has another tomorrow. And I will make sure that I walk out of Rage in the Cage as the New Extreme and Cruiserweight Champion! {We finally see a figure and it is Enigma, but he is not in his normal area. Instead we see Enigma inside of a Cage. The cage is not very big and appears to be one of those cages that divers go in when they go to swim with the sharks} At Rage in the Cage, I will be enclosed in as twelve foot high steel cage. There will be nowhere to run, nowhere to hide. There are no escapes from the cage. There is no way that you are going to be able to get out. It will be a night where I reclaim the titles that I should have never lost. This time instead of it being two on one, it will be one on one on one. This time it will be every man for themselves. It will be a time to raise some hell. The Cage is a form of punishment. It is a weapon that is to be used for evil. Nothing good can ever come out of the steel cage. The cage is unforgiving. The cage is merciless. The cage takes no prisoners. The cage can end careers or at least shorten them. The cage draws blood from the men who are in it. The Cage is just like me. I take no prisoners, I end careers and shorten others. I make people bleed. I make people suffer. I will destroy my opponents. As for the two men that I am going to face, it appears that both men definitely will be looking to gain the titles for themselves. Let me begin with Chance. Chance I saw you on Meltdown. I saw you in the ring fighting that over the hill loser Denrol. I saw you out there and I could not let you have all the fun. I had to get out there and have a little fun at your expense Chance. You see Chance, you should actually be thanking me. There are two reasons for this. The first being that because of me you beat a former World Champion, albeit by disqualification, but you did get the “win” and we all know that a win is a win. Do I hear a thank you? Your welcome Chance. Secondly you should be thanking me that I did not try and end your damn career last night. You are lucky that I let up on you for a bit. Instead of destroying your knees again, I decided to let you off easy on Monday. That was just a warning, because I knew if I injured you then the match at Rage in the Cage will be called off. I did not want that to happen. Chance I want you to be at near one hundred percent come next week. The reason being is that I don’t want any excuses from you after I beat your ass. I have beaten you before and I will do it again. At Rage in the Cage, what you will witness is the end of your reign as champion. I ended your first Extreme Title reign and I plan on ending your second title reign in February. I hope that you have had your fill of holding that title. Because after Rage in the Cage, the title will no longer belong to you. It will no longer be in your possession as the title will return to its rightful owner, that being me! Hold that title for now, sleep with it, caress it, because after the second, you will not have it any longer. {Enigma shakes the cage with his arms} Strong. The cage is strong. That is how I will have to be at Rage in the Cage. Why? Because I have two men that I need to take out. The second man is Jimmi Zion, or as I like to call him “Mr. Paper Champion.” Zion you and I will meet up again in the ring and this time the outcome will be a little different. Rather than having Chance beat me in the match, I am gonna tear you apart. After I am finished doing that I am going to walk out of the ring with the titles held above my head. Zion, you have done nothing but be a disappointment ever since your return. You are a champion in name only. You did not earn those titles. You did not do anything that suggests that you are worthy of holding those titles. You have, to put it bluntly, DONE NOTHING! The only reason you have any victories under your belt is because of someone else, like Chance and myself. Zion, I hope you realize that your loss to Lockdown on Fiesta was the beginning of the end for you. You are not going to walk out of the Cage with the titles. I will not allow you to walk out of the ring with the titles. This time it will be different. As Lockdown proved on Fiesta, you are weak. You are a feeble human being. You are a disgrace to wrestling and to my titles. After Rage in the Cage, I will have back my titles and you will be forgotten once again. You will be nothing more than a vague memory in the minds of all the men in the WWA. You are done. Rage in the Cage is just around the corner. I will regain my belts. NOTHING CAN STOP ME NOW {fade to ?}
