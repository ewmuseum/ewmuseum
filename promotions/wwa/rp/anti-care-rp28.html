Anti-Care

18 May 2003

By Se7en

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
We enter a dark room, hardly anything can be seen except for a shining light coming in from a crack in the wall. We can also hear someone breathing behind the camera's view, and slowly that breathing becomes more husky. It must be another one of Se7en's promo's, his husky breathing, the dark and morbid location. It must be Se7en. Se7en: "By the last breath of Venny LeTrav, the Seventh World will have made its fortunes known. Venny Better raise his ears, his sight and strenth, because all senses will be put to the ultimate test, The sound of my silent footsteps will try and decieve you, The sound of your heart will make you fear" Slowly Se7en comes out into view, but its another one of those promo's were we can hardly see him. Se7en: "Lock up your wife and children now, It's my time to wield the blade, For now you have got some company, and that company is much more deadlier than the devil, much more lustfull than Lucifer. Much more Horrific than Hell. Se7en is right in front of the camera, his mask is almost touching the camera lens. Se7en: "The Soldiers of War are drawing nearer, They have come to take your life On through the dead of night. The dead of night will be when all thoughts of survival will be lost for you LeTrav. You can choose your fate now, Venny. Leave and you will survive, Stay and you will rot in the damnations of my hell. But, remember this, You have been dying since the day You were born LeTrav, ever since you looked into my bloodshot eyes, You know it has all been planned. your demise and destruction, it's all apart of the Seventh World's plan. My plan. You have become part of my Harvest" The husky voice and horrific mask is scaring the hell out of everyone, but just like that he dissapears and the scene fades out.
