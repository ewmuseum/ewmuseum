Dream On

20 Feb 2003

By Mal Somers

Item 4 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 20 Feb 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Scene – Mal Somers, WWA Rookie Champion and member of the Extreme Corporation is asleep in a hotel room in Kelowna, BC. The WWA Rookie Title lies next to him, and Somers has a weird smile on his face. I wont ask what that’s all about. Anyways, Somers is dreaming. His dream is reaching the final moments. Lets see what he is dreaming about.) Jimmy – “Oh my god! Somers just hit the Somers Assault on Brian Cappelletto!” Fat Tony – “Through a burning table no less!” Somers rolls out of the ring and reaches underneath. He pulls out a rectangular box and holds it high in the air as the crowd erupts in a frenzied cheer. Jimmy and Fat Tony – “OH. MY. GOD! Somers has the Scrabble set! Look out Brian! Somers rolls back into the ring. Jimmy – "It looks to be game,set and match for Brian Cappelletto, 2002 Scrabble World Champion." Fat Tony – I totally agree. Somers clubs Cappelletto repeatedly with the scrabble box and goes for the pin. Crowd – 1! 2! 3! Gary Trudeau – Your winner, and UNIFIED WWA Rookie and World Scrabble Deathmatch Champion...Mal Somerrrrrrrrrrrrrrrrrsssssssssssssssssssssss!!! Jimmy – I don’t need to SPELL that one out. Mal was just too dominant. Fat Tony – I cant speak. Words fail me! I am a MALcoholic for LIFE. Jimmy – Me too. Me too. (The commentators embrace as the crowd gives Somers a standing ovation. Tears well in Somers' eyes as he holds the belt and Scrabble set aloft. Suddenly Dave Harley runs from backstage and into the ring. Somers goes to embrace his tag partner but Harley backs off, pointing at the Scrabble set. Harley pulls a microphone from his back pocket and holds it up ready to speak.) Harley – Somers! I’ve... RING RING RING RING Somers is awoken by his hotel phone. He fumbles in the dark to answer it. Somers – “Hello?” Caller – “This is your early morning wake up call. You have overslept and missed Fiesta. You suck.” Somers – “Wha?” Caller – “Heh. Its just Dave here. Have you tried the Continental Breakfast here? Its great!” Somers – “Harley! Cant a man get some sleep?" Harley – “Not when there’s a big main event match to prepare for. See you in the jogging track in one hour. (click) Somers – (hanging up) Great. Just great. Oh well its all for a good cause. Winning my first Fiesta main event. But that’s not the only thing happening at Fiesta. My favourite tag team, The Cell will be in the house. That’s if they aren’t too busy looking for Maxwell's sausage or whatever kinky thing they are up to next on Insider. Well don’t think that Dave and I wont be paying a keen interest in what you guys are up to this week. See those things around your waist? No, I don’t mean the love handles, I mean the WWA Tag Team Titles. The Xcorp wants them. And we were going to get around to kicking you’re a$$es anyways so it will be like killing two birds with one stone. Its only a matter of time. (The phone rings again) Harley – You STILL there? 58 minutes! (Somers hangs up and sighs as the scene fades to black)
