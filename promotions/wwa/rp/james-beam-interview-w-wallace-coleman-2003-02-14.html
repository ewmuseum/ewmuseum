James Beam Interview w/ Wallace Coleman

14 Feb 2003

By James Beam

Item 6 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 14 Feb 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene opens to WWA logo. Then pafes out to show Wallace Coleman standing there with a mic in hand. He is on a stage with 100s of rabid WWA fans in the audience. He has a huge smile on his face.) Wallace: And now... Let's talk to another rising star in the WWA... He is none other than... James Don't call me Jim Beam!!! (The crowd pops at the annouce of James Beam. From the back James walks out in a nice sports jacket, with black jeans on. His face is covered with a smile that betrays his emotions.) Wallace: Thanks for stopping by James... James: Great to be here in Ottawa!! (cheap pop) Some of the greatest wrestling fans are right here! (another cheap pop) Wallace: So James... Let's talk about your upcoming match with James Curzon, only two days away. James... What is your stategy going into this one?! James: awh come on now Wallace. You don;t expect me to spill the preverbial beans now do you? (Wallace shrugs ans nods his head yes) Well... In that case. I plan to win. I plan to give these fans out here (points out to the crowd, to a huge pop) something to rememeber. Ottawa will be the place James Beam started his rise to the TOP!! Wallace: Very good. So I gotta thank you for at least shoing up. Curzon simply left me a note, to not read out loud apparently. James: Yeah... Honestly, the whole WWA wishes you had NOT read it aloud. I think we are all a little more stupid from having heard it. (Some fans laugh, James smiles, that his joke didn;t completely bomb.) James: James Curzon is a great competitor, but... The kid is just looney! I hope he comes ready to wrestle Sunday night. Because, as always, I WILL be! Wallace: Fair enough. Assuming you win this match against Curzon this Sunday on Fiesta... What is next for James Beam?! James: Obviously first... I need to win Sunday. (Wallace nods) Either way this turns out, I would think the winner would be in the running or would have to at least be considered for a TV Title shot. We are near the top of the new rookies and I dare say we are near the best. Wallace: So... Something a little personal... What did you get Ms. Schafer for Valentine's day? (Both Wallace and James smile, James a little more.) James: Well... I got her alot. Something I will NOT mention. But she is real into stuffed animals. So I got her a little pink bear stuffed animal. (A mocking AWWHWHH from the fans. James gives them a goofy look and a smile. They is a shout from the near the back from a woman fan, "Marry me James!!" James smiles from the comment.) James: I got her this "other" teddy... But that might not be appropriate for family TV! (The crowd cheers this, all imagining Rebecca in a hot teddy. WOW! James gives a wink to the camera and waves to the crowd as he backpedals behind the curtains.) Wallace: James Beam everyone!!! Give it up!!!! (The crowd cheers for the favorite.) Wallace: The next guest here in Ottawa... (Fade)
