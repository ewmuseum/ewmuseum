No Place Like Home

27 Apr 2006

By Tracer Bullet

Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene opens inside the Toronto Airport at the security checkpoint. A frustrated Tracer Bullet watches helplessly as security goes through his luggage, searching for God knows what. Denrol is standing beside Tracer with his own bag, looking bored.) Bullet: Is this really necessary? (An airport security agent turns to Tracer.) Guard: Yes it is, sir. Your ID says you are Trent LeBeaux, but your tickets say you are “Tracy Bullet.” We have to be sure. (Tracer turns to Denrol.) Bullet: That’s the last time you get to book our tickets. Ever. (Denrol shrugs, but a small grin does appear. Tracer turns back to the agent.) Bullet: Let me just put it to you this way... you know how celebrities will often book flights and hotel rooms under assumed names to avoid public scrutiny? Same thing. Tracy... excuse me, TRACER Bullet is my professional name. Guard: That may be true, but again, we need to be safe. (The guard gets on his radio.) Guard: Do we have a go or no go on the full body cavity search yet? Bullet: Guys, really... this is CANADA. The last bastion of civility and intelligence, with the exception of my partner... (Denrol glares at Tracer.) Bullet: ... do we really need to go through the same things American airports do? (The security guard ignores Tracer as he waits for a response. The radio comes alive.) Radio: It's a no go. Let him through. And Dan wants you to make sure you get both of their autographs for his kid. (Tracer folds his arms and looks smugly at the guard.) Bullet: Dan wouldn't happen to be your supervisor, would he? (The guard still ignores Tracer.) Guard: Let's wrap this up. (The other guards begin tossing Tracer's stuff back into his luggage, pretty sloppily.) Denrol: Are you going to let them do that to your stuff? Bullet: Why not? That's how I packed it. (Denrol shrugs and picks up his stuff.) Denrol: Well we better hurry, don't want to miss our flight. (Tracer grabs his bags and starts to follow, but the guard stops him.) Guard: Sir, your... um... Bullet: I ask again... is Dan your supervisor? Guard: (sigh) Yes. Bullet: Well, it's not my place to disappoint children, but I'll tell you what I WILL do... (Tracer takes an unmatched sock out of his carry-on bag and a pen out of his pocket.) Bullet: What's the kid's name? (The guard just glares at Bullet for many long seconds before speaking.) Guard: Tim. (Tracer begins signing the sock. It reads as follows: To Tim, It's easy to see why your dad is the supervisor around here. Tracer Bullet.) Bullet: You want to sign the sock, too, Gordon? (Denrol grabs the sock and just signs: "Tim, Yes, Tracer Bullet is as dumb as he looks. Denrol".) Bullet: Now go ahead and give it to this fella here. But I think he's going to have to check it for poison or plutonium or something before he gives it to Dan. (The guard looks like he really wishes he could have had a chance to hurt Tracer. Denrol hands the sock back and then picks his bags back up and walks towards the gate.) Denrol: You know, you really shouldn't have insulted that guy. He's just going to give you more hell when we come back through here. (Tracer looks like his mind is elsewhere.) Bullet: Uh, yeah. Silly me. Denrol: What's your problem? (Denrol waves over one of the courtesy carts, which drives over to them. Denrol and Tracer put their bags on it and hop on.) Bullet: Look, don't worry about it. I say we just enjoy the flight, yeah? Denrol: You're worse than my wife, you know that? Bullet: I have my moments. But anyway, you'll find out eventually, and we'll cross that bridge when we come to it. Denrol: Fine. (We fast forward a while, now both men are sitting on the plane as it's in the air. Denrol is reading a paper while Tracer is staring out the window.) Denrol: So when are you going to say sorry? Bullet: I'm sorry? Denrol: Thank you. Bullet: I meant I wasn't following you. Why must I apologize? Denrol: For stabbing me in the back. Bullet: Eh? Denrol: Going to Vik Ventressa and having our belts put on the line in the Brawl. Bullet: Oh, that. Come on, don't you think that makes for good television? (Denrol puts his paper down.) Denrol: It's not about what makes for good TV. You've made life pretty tough for us. Bullet: What's the matter? Don't think you can win? Denrol: I want to focus on winning this as a whole, but since either of us can get pinned, I have to babysit your ass. Bullet: Look, I have my own plan going into this match, so don't worry about me. Denrol: I have to. You can lose me my tag team titles. Bullet: "Your" tag team titles? Please allow me to explain the concept of those belts to you real quick... (Denrol holds up a hand.) Denrol: It's about teamwork. Included in that is agreeing on stuff together beforehand. Bullet: Like being injected with morphine, for instance? Denrol: That was a personal health decision. Bullet: And it affected the team. Not to mention your long-term health. Denrol: It only affected the team because I let it. It didn't affect us Monday. Bullet: And that's because I didn't let it. Though we did have a close call when you faked that collapse. Masterfully played, by the way. Denrol: Thank you for acknowledging my genius. (Denrol goes back to his paper. Tracer checks his watch.) Bullet: Hmm... if everything is as it should be, we should be getting there at about the same time. Denrol: Of course we'll be there at the same time. We're on the same plane, dimwit. Bullet: Oh, I didn't mean us. Denrol: You said "we". Do you now acknowledge the voices in your head? Bullet: Even if I were doing so, it would still go without saying that we'd get there at the same time. I meant us and... well, you'll see. Denrol: Really, I can live without the strippers you know. Bullet: Bah. Well, anyway... once we get down to New Orleans, I should show you a few of the local hangouts. This is your first Brawl, I believe, and you're definitely going to want to know the layout of the city. Denrol: It's my second. During my first one, Vik threw me off a roof. Bullet: Then I suppose you're well-acquainted with that building. But trust me, the more you know about the city, the better your chances. Denrol: Fine. Fine. Can I read my paper please? Bullet: For now. Save your energy. (Denrol shoots a quizzical glance at his partner before returning to his newspaper. We flash forward to see Tracer and Denrol sitting in the back seat of a taxi.) Bullet: I can't believe we had to go through the same crap at THIS airport. They KNOW me here! (Denrol just chuckles as he watches the city go by.) Denrol: I've seen better and I've seen worse. Bullet: Ah, it was better before all the water damage. We're not quite back on our feet city-wide yet. Denrol: of course. We almost there? Bullet: Better than that... (The taxi comes to a stop.) Bullet: We ARE there. (Denrol nods and exits the taxi. Tracer tosses a few dollars extra at the cab driver, then opens the trunk to pull out some luggage as Denrol unstraps the same from the top of the cab.) Denrol: So what are our plans today? Bullet: Well... (Tracer points behind his partner. Denrol turns around and is met with an interesting sight... a large moving van in front of a white-painted antebellum home. This house looks rather familiar. In fact...) Bullet: I'm home. (Denrol turns back to him.) Denrol: Great. I guess I'll go to the hotel. Bullet: What hotel? Denrol: The hotel we were supposed to stay at, but I guess only I will. Bullet: Wasn't it my job to book the hotel? Denrol: Yeah. Bullet: Yeah, well... welcome to Chez LeBeaux! Denrol: There's going to be a porn star staying here, too? Bullet: Not Shayla LaVeaux, fool! Denrol: Fine. I hope the beds are at least set up. Bullet: Besides, she hasn't released anything since 2004. Denrol: And you know this how? Bullet: Um... hey, good news! You're going to get paid to help me move back in! Denrol: Run that by me again? Bullet: Well, see, I couldn't very well live in an empty apartment, so I couldn't send my stuff down too early. I had the moving company haul it out early this morning, several hours before our flight left. And even though Missy is already here... (Tracer indicates the Dart sitting in the driveway in front of the moving van.) Bullet: ... she couldn't very well do it all herself. So you're going to help us. Denrol: And you were going to tell me when? Bullet: At the first moment when you could no longer refuse. Denrol: What makes you think I can't refuse? Bullet: Because I know how cheap you are. It's either help us put stuff back in our house or take another taxi ride to a hotel, where you will pay for whatever room they have available. Besides, don't worry. Most of the big furniture never left. The only major thing is the plasma screen TV I bought back in Toronto. (Denrol sighs.) Denrol: And what about my bad knee. It's acting up again. Bullet: Don't hand me that, "winner." I know you came down to New Orleans prepared for the worst on the streets. Consider this a prelude. Or a workout. Or whatever. Denrol: Whatever. Let's get this show going. (Tracer nods and opens the back of the moving van, where sit boxes upon boxes of...) Denrol: I thought you said the TV was the only major thing. Bullet: What can I say? I'm a DVD freak. Now are you going to help, or are you going to stand there and bitch, which will only make it take longer? Denrol: Fine. Hand me the "Action" box, I know you're not big on that. Your past performances prove that. Bullet: I only have three words in response to that... Ha, ha... (Tracer picks up the box Denrol asked for and puts it in his arms.) Bullet: ... and here. (Denrol carries the box of DVDs to the front door as Tracer unloads a couple of the "Drama" boxes. Denrol shifts the box in his arms so he can ring the doorbell. It is promptly answered by Melissa.) Missy: Ah, you're here. Denrol: And I really hope this is all worth it. (Tracer makes it to the front door as well.) Bullet: Don't worry... it will be. More than you know. (The three enter the house as we fade out.) Designed By [name withheld] | © World Wrestling Alliance 1997 - 2005
