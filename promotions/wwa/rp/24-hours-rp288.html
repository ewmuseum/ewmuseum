24 Hours

24 Jul 2003

By "The Psycho" Steven Murphy

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
4:20am (The moonlight shines down onto Suburban America. The typical middle class neighbourhood. Big houses, all painted white to show some class. The garage are at the side of the houses, but the cars a parked outside. Why? To show how good they are. The camera zooms in onto one house, and zooms slowly through the window. In the background, a dog is barking loudly. The man in bed is restless. It was Psycho Steven Murphy.) (He turns to see his alarm clock at the side of the bed. The red digitised numbers glaringly read out 4:21am. Psycho quickly gets up, and exits the bedroom, slamming the door behind him.) (As the camera focuses on the front of the house, Murphy comes charging out of the front door, with empty bud bottle in hand, in his boxer shorts.) Psycho: SHUT THE F*CK UP!!! (Psycho throws the bottle at the neighbour’s wall, where the dog is barking its head off, and the smash triggers two more of the neighbourhood’s hounds to join in.) Psycho: Ah, for F*ck sak…. (As Psycho is muttering, he notices his Jet-black Subaru Impreza in his drive. His jaw drops as he looks at the many scratches all over his motor. As he is surveying the damage, on the window screen, a note is left.) “Hey Steve! Like the motor man! But I think it needed a new Paint Job. Hope Ya like it! PEACE ChuckSTARR” (Psycho rips off the note, and is looking at it in anger, as he storms back into his house.) 5:34am (Psycho is sitting in his Living room, on his own with a beer in his hand. He still has the note, and is looking at it closely. He is still in his boxers, as the sun starts to rise in the distance out the window. Psycho Takes a swig of his drink, but just throws it at the wall.) Psycho: He can mock me, that’s fine. He can mock my gimmick, im happy with that. He can even skit DO, I would allow that. But this? Now he will see my f*cking angry side. (Psycho grabs his mobile from the table next to him, and starts to ring someone.) Voicemail: Tommy Starr is getting some Beauty sleep. Leave a message after the beep. BEEP. Psycho: F*ck it, ill call Lockdown. (Psycho tries to phone Lockdown, but he is not answering.) Psycho: He’s Probably at the bar. Levi would know what to do. (Psycho presses his mobile, and it starts ringing.) Psycho: Ah sh*t, not now. (Slowly, his Mobile’s battery ran out. In anger, he throws his mobile at the window, smashing it instantly. He stands and walks out of the room, and runs upstairs.) 9:00am (Murphy is now clean-shaven, in clothes, and has cleaned up the mess he made earlier. He has the yellow pages out, and is looking under Car Repair section. As he is, he is drinking some red bull, to keep himself awake after a long night.) Psycho: There! They will fix my car. Dusty’s Garage, Franconia Road. Where the f*cks that? (Murphy grabs his leather jacket and rips the page out of the yellow pages, as he walks out the door, and drives off down the road in his badly scratched Impreza.) 10:30am (The roar of the impreza engine sounds, as it turns into a street, where Dusty’s Garage is placed. Psycho drives in, as he stops the car, a short, fat man with white hair and glasses comes out of the garage. He is wearing a cowboy hat, a chequered shirt and some blue jeans. The typical southerner.) Dusty: I see you have a problem with y’car there? (Psycho gets out of the car, and looks at the man, and with sarcasm in his voice…) Psycho: D’ya think? (Dusty looks at some of the scratches. He takes a good look all around the car, as Murphy stands looking concerned.) Psycho: Can you sort it out? (Dusty stands again, and rubs his chin, with his fat chubby fingers.) Dusty: We can………But it will cost you. Psycho: How Much? (Dusty is rubbing his hands gleefully as he mutters the words…) Dusty: 4 Thousand dollors. (Psycho looks blankly into Dusty’s eyes.) Psycho: Are you serious? (Dusty nods as Psycho now leans on his car, with his face in his hands.) Psycho: I am having a bad day as it is, and 4 Thousand Dollars for a paint job is taking the piss. Dusty: You take it or get out of my property. Psycho: What, are you giving me an ultimatum? Dusty: Sure as hell am, now give me an answer goddamnit! (Psycho changes his concerned look to a mad look, as he opens his car door.) Psycho: First off, I do not take Ultimatums very nicely. (Psycho reaches for something in his car.) Psycho: Second, I don’t like being forced to do something that I don’t like. (Psycho pulls out the baseball bat.) Psycho: And thirdly, Not from f*cking rednecks like you! (Psycho swings, but Dusty moves out of the way, and runs into the store, with Psycho closely behind.) Dusty: Get away from me you mad bastard! Or ill blow your f*cking head off! (As Psycho runs into the store, Dusty is standing holding a shotgun, pointing in Psycho’s direction.) Psycho: What, you think that scares me? (Psycho continues to advance.) Dusty: Don’t think I won’t shoot! (Psycho stops and thinks.) Psycho: Dusty, don’t think I won’t do this… (Psycho swings and takes out car parts which where hanging up on the wall. Dusty is now shaking, as Psycho continues to advance, with his eyes bulging, he looks like a maniac.) Psycho: Come on! Shoot Me!! Ive had a bad day already! And you tipped me over the edge!! So make my bad day! SHOOT ME!!! (Dusty is shaking still, as Psycho has his arms at his sides, waiting for Dusty to unload a cap in his ass. J dusty hesitates.) Psycho: I thought you wouldn’t. (Psycho then swings his bat, and knocks the shotgun out of his hand, as Dusty lays unarmed, as Psycho advances.) 12:00pm (The WWA headquarters, the empire that is run at this building. The loud screeches of a car speeding is heard in the distance. The noise gets closer, as we see Psycho’s car speeding to WWA Headquarters. He comes to the security check point.) Security man: Man, what happened to your car. Psycho: Just Let me in, I don’t want to talk about it. Security: Ok, oh and Chuckster was looking for you. (Psycho’s face went serious, and looked crazed. He put his hands on the steering wheel, with his knuckles covered in blood, and his top full of it. The security man looked at it, but thought it was best to say nothing. He opened up the gate, as Psycho drove in. The security man sat back down in his chair, eating a burger and listening to the radio. A news report was on.) Radio: And in other news, a man was found brutally attacked in his own garage down Franconia Road, Virginia. The man is said to be in a critical condition, with 10 ribs broken, severe fracture of jaw, kidney damage and severely cut face. The man was named Dusty Howard. Police are now looking for the man who committed this horrific assault, and have issued this statement. Police: The man who performed this attack is ruthless, and is still at large. We have reports he is driving around in a scratched Subaru Impreza, and that he is over 6ft in height, and has a shaven head. One distinct feature is a scar across his right eye. If anyone has any information, please inform the police straight away. (The Security drops his burger, and looks at the security camera, to see Psycho’s scratched Impreza. He picks up the phone straight away.) 1:00Pm (Psycho is prowling around the WWA Headquarters. He is holding his bat in hand, which is covered in blood. It drips as he walks along the corridors, with WWA officials looking at him strangely.) Psycho: What the F*ck are you looking at?! (The officials quickly turn, seeing Psycho is in a bad mood. Psycho then comes to the locker room, and he opens the door. In the room, Levi and Lockdown are talking when Psycho walks in, with a crazed look in his eye.) Psycho: Have you seen Chuckster? Levi: What’s up Steve? Psycho: Have you f*cking seen chuckster? Levi: Calm down. Now tell us what has happened? Lockdown: And why the f*ck your covered in blood? Psycho: Chuckster f*cking scratched my car to bits last night. I went to get the f*cker fixed, but a man named Dusty gave me sh*t, so I f*cked him up. Levi: What, you assaulted him? Psycho: You got it. Lockdown: Well done. Now he wont give you f*cking sh*t again. Levi: You assaulted someone outside of WWA. You’re going to get arrested for this! Lockdown: So what! I spent f*cking time in prison. (Psycho looks behind the two DO members, and sees someone in the backroom. It looks like Chuckster. He pushes past Levi, and wields his bat high in the air. Chuckster Is there, training.) SMACK (Chuckster moved, with Psycho narrowing missing.) Chuckster: What are you doing?! Psycho: Getting Revenge for my Car. (Psycho, cold and brutal, swings again, and again, Chuckster moved. Levi comes running and holds back the fellow DO member, who is struggling, and tries to get Chuckster.) Psycho: Let..Me Go!! Lockdown: Let him go! Lets see him f*ck up Chuckster! (As Levi struggled, Policemen charge into the locker-room, and cuff Psycho.) Policeman: Steven Murphy, you are being arrested on accounts of assault and 2 counts of bodily harm to one Dusty Howard. You have the right to remain sil… Psycho: F*ck you! And you, you’re a deadman! (Psycho, talking to Chuckster, is pulled out of the locker-room, and stuffed into the back of a Police car, as Lockdown and Levi look on shocked, and Chuckster is cracking a smile.) 4:20am (The scene is a dirty, damp prison cell, with an old bed, and a bog full of s*it next to it. No mirrors, not photos, not small windows. Just darkness. Psycho is sitting on the floor, covered in dry blood, and banging his head against the wall,) Psycho: I am having a bad day. (Psycho is left I his Cell, as the camera slowly fades to ?)
