Kings, Bishops, Knights, and Pawns

10 Jun 2003

By Grunge

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
FADE IN (Sound emanates from the large interview room that Grunge usually preoccupies.) If I get old, I will not give in But if I do, remind me of this. Remind me that, once I was free, Once I was cool, once I was me. And if I sat down, and crossed my arms, Hold me into, this song. Knock me out, smash out my brains, If I take a chair, start to talk..... If I get old, remind me of this: That night we kissed, and I really meant it. Whatever happens, if we're still speaking. Pick up the phone, play me this song. (We focus in on Grunge where he sits at a table. On the table is a chess board with all the pieces already set.) Grunge: Radiohead, A Reminder (Grunge picks up a chess piece and holds it for everyone to see.) Grunge: Recently Enigma you used a deck of cards in one of your interviews. Using cards to tell the fortunes of others has gone back to the gypsies and even to the pagan religions and Wicca. As you can plainly see though this is not a deck of cards. (The camera pulls into Grunge.) Grunge: I don’t play cards. However you did mention chess and I do play that. (Grunge holds up a knight.) Grunge: Nick Adams is a knight or perhaps a better analogy is that of the weapon the knight carries a sword. When he entered he was simply tempered steel with unlimited potential. Now forged in the fires of competition he claims to be a new man and I can see that in him. If he does credit me with some of that then I take it as a great complement. We each know each others moves better than anybody but since Revolution it’s a whole new ballgame. And he sure hates you Enigma. We both agree with that. (Grunge sets down the piece and picks up the Bishop.) Grunge: Dave Harley, the Bishop of the Church of Extreme. He is a extreme being. Anyone whose seen his matches with the Cell knows that. He’s one to worry about. I look forward to facing the man to see what he can do. (Grunge sets down the Bishop.) Grunge: And Enigma…do you want to know what you are? (Grunge picks up the Pawn.) Grunge: You’re a Pawn. Your whole career you’ve been used. First when you were Ekosyde you were simply a tool of Se7en and if he hadn’t of left there is no doubt you would still be one today. (Grunge smiles.) Grunge: Then the House of Pain. You sure were Thorne’s loyal little monkey. That must be the only reason you were able to take the Extreme Title from him. Because he let you. Then when he got tired of you he simply let you go and who did you hook up with then? (Grunge begins to laugh.) Grunge: Shane Dorian? What did that get you? After he left you were all alone and left with the disdain of Tracer Bullet. You claim to be a mastermind? Well would a real mastermind have fallen for Tracer Bullet’s trick in the I Quit match? You must have been slapping yourself in the head for days. Still there’s no shame in being beaten by the master. (Grunge sits the Pawn down.) Grunge: But not much has changed. Now you’re just a follower leading a bigger collection of mindless automatons. Your no mastermind, you’re a plebian. (Grunge gets the pawn and flicks it at the screen.) Grunge: And after Meltdown the whole world will see you for the pathetic wretch you really are. So I suggest that after Nick and Dave take turns beating the hell out of you, you should just lay down and let the three stars battle it out. (Grunge begins to laugh again.) Grunge: Cause even though Nick and Dave have a chance, you have none. Rock and Roll baby. (And at that we begin to…) FADE OUT
