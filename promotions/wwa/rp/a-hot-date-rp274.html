A Hot Date!

22 Jul 2003

By Tommy Starr

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Tommy Starr walks arm in arm with what can only be described as a bonafide HOTTIE. Five foot – eleven, long blonde hair, curvaceous and leggy. She wears a short black dress that leaves very little to the imagination, and is all over Tommy like a rash. Tommy is loving it, nodding at all the passers by who have frumpy partners or none at all. Tommy is in the zone. Tommy is part of one of the most destructive stables in WWA History..THE DEVILS OWN. Tommy is the man. Tommy is out on the town with this goddess. Tommy is taking her to see Terminator 3 : Rise of the Machines. They arrive at the ticket window.) Starr: Two tickets numbnuts, and make it snappy...I want to get good seats! Cashier: Is that one adult and a child? Starr: What in the heck are you on about? Cashier: Are you here with your mommy, little fella? Starr: What the hell? I’m freaking Tommy Starr! Don’t you know you I am? Cashier: Yes, you are Tommy Starr! Starr: Right. And how do you know that? My utter domination of the EWA? My movie star charisma and good looks, or my impact in the WWA? Cashier: Uhhh, none? You just said “I’m freaking Tommy Starr...” I just thought you were a kid, being short and all. Starr: Why you little punk, I’m going to crawl in there and rip you a new dirt chute..that is, if I wasn’t with my little STARRgazer here. Two tickets, and you better make them one adult and one child because its cheaper! (The cashier accepts some cash and hands over the tickets. Starr hands one to the woman and prepares to walk in. Suddenly she stops him.) Starr: What now Angie? Angie: Look honey, these tickets say the movie goes for almost three hours. Starr: So? (Looks at the cashier, grins and then back at Angie) I don’t plan to be watching the whole movie if you know what I mean, hey...hey...wink wink, nudge nudge, if you're lucky a bit later on, you will be seeing a shooting STARR if you know what I mean!! Angie: (shaking her pretty head) Look sugar, you’ve only paid me for two hours, if you want me there for the whole movie you gotta pay extra! (Starr goes bright red as the cashier and the people waiting in the queue laugh hysterically. He tells Angie to get lost and she does so obligingly, able to go home, or back on the streets early.) Starr: No-one gives me ANY respect! None of these plebs here...Lockdown...Psycho...Imperceptus...none of them! Not even Angie. I used to have a tab running but then she...oh why the heck am I even telling you morons at home about this! (Starr shakes his head and tries to compose himself. He looks up to the billboard above him that has “ARNOLD SCHWARZENEGGER” up in lights. He pouts and looks back at the camera.) Starr: Well I guess I may as well not waste my ticket, I’m looking forward to this movie, but goodness knows why someone as talented as myself wasn’t asked to be the star of this movie! I can play dumb and monosyballic for a while...it would be a stretch but I am just that talented. Maybe I could get some tips off the Shmuckster! Have you seen how that guy talks? Meltdown will have to expand to four hours just for one of his inane interviews! You all know how it goes... (Starr rips off his cap and jacket and throws them on the ground. A small crowd has gathered to watch him rant and rave and he is enjoying the audience. Even though he hates them all. He coughs a few times then speaks.) I… AM… THE… FRANCHISE… SIKE… LIKE… SUBWAY… LIKE… IT… WAS… KHUL!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! (After that impersonation, Terminator 3 is half over and Starr realises this. He picks up his jacket and cap and some dimes and a chewed piece of chewing gum fall out of it.) Starr: See Suckster, now I am really mad, you’ve made me miss half of my movie. I don’t know why I am even facing you...maybe its one of those “legend/veteran of the WWA gets to face the crappy Rookie kinda things”, I dunno. In fact, this kinda reminds me of T-3, the supporting cast may change but the super-STARR stays the same throughout! That’s me! This Meltdown, Tommy Starr, and my uh...sidekick, Psycho are going to show you what the Devils Own are all about. I’ll speak slowly so you can comprehend. I mean heck, it took you months and months and months to follow me into the WWA. Huckster, just like I used to do to you on a regular basis in the EWA, I am going to give you the STARR TREATMENT!!! (Fade into…) DEVIL'S OWN! DEAL WITH IT!
