Even This Sh*t Is Better Than RAW

17 Feb 2003

By Denrol & Tracer

Item 2 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 17 Feb 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
DAY 3: FRIDAY, 5:30 P.M. (As the camera fades in on the LeBeaux homestead, we once again see cats scattered around. TV seems to have fallen asleep in a sunbeam, US is grooming himself on a chair, World is... well, he seems to be nowhere to be found at the moment. But Light Heavyweight is stalking the pull string of the draperies and Extreme seems to be looking around for something. We once again see subtitles that translate the kitty-talk.) Extreme: Now where did I leave that thing? US: What are you looking for, little one? Extreme: I left a hairball somewhere around here... I wanted to get it cleaned up before Tracer or Missy found it... seeing as I'm still the only one who hasn't done anything to bug the hell out of them... US: That reminds me. (US gets up and bounces over to Extreme.) US: It's three days so you lost our bet. I want the catnip. Extreme: Well, I did say that it would take less than three days, but... wait a minute, what are you trying to pull? It's the third day of our visit that means it's only been two days since we got here! US: It's 3 days. Want us to help you count? (World enters the room and comes up behind Extreme. Light Heavyweight moves from the window and joins the other cats. The three cats surround Extreme.) Extreme: Just what are you three up to, anyway? US: I promised my pals here a piece of the prize. Now you're going to get my catnip or you're going to find yourself in some real trouble. Extreme: Despite the fact that it's only been like two and a half days since we got here, there's yet another reason why you shouldn't be getting my stash just yet. US: Oh let me guess, you have it hidden somewhere back in the other house. Extreme: Well, that's part of it. But I was referring more to the fact that even though I'll have lost the bet my morning, that doesn't mean you'll have won yet either. Remember, you bet that it would happen in three days' time. If it doesn't happen by morning, you lose as well. US: Hehe. You just wait and see little one. Just wait and see. (The three cats break up and go off in different directions.) Extreme: (to self) Heh... not like you'll be getting my stuff anyway... it's more than just coincidence that I got named Extreme... (Singing can be heard coming from the kitchen. Very bad singing. Tracer Bullet comes walking into the living room. He stops and sniffs the air before walking over into the kitchen. In there we find Denrol singing "When I'm Gone" by 3 Doors Down as he is filling a pot with water. On the stove, two pans are being used to fry and simmer some food.) TB: And what, may I ask, are you attempting to do? Denrol: Cook of course. I figured I'd make dinner tonight. (Denrol finishes with the pot and puts it on the counter. He picks up a bottle of Bourbon and takes a sip.) TB: I mean the song. Who are you butchering now? Denrol: It's 3 Doors Down. The song is called "When I'm Gone". From their new album. (in song) "Hold me while I'm here, Love me when I'm gone." (Tracer cleans his ear with his finger in an attempt to clear up the distortion. It doesn't help.) TB: Yeah yeah, real nice. Could you stop? Denrol: Sure. (Denrol chuckles and pours some of the Bourbon into one of the pans. It seems Denrol doesn't know much about cooking, because you're not suppose to add alocholic drinks to food on an open flame. Thus of course, the pan's contents light on fire.) Denrol: Oh sh*t! (Before Tracer can react, Denrol compounds his mistake by grabbing the pot of water and pouring it over the fire. The flame changes from a small fire to a huge electrical fire than engulfs the stove. Tracer sighs forlornly and sprays the entire stove down with the fire extinguisher that he picked up as soon as he had saw that Denrol was trying to use the stove.) Denrol: Oh well... ummm... good work work there. Fast thinking Trent. (Denrol surveys the now ruined stove.) Denrol: Hmm, maybe I need to watch more Food Network. TB: Frankly, I think you should avoid Food Network. All the stuff they do assumes that someone has already mastered the basics. Matter of fact, I think I'm going to go block that channel on the dish before Iron Chef comes on... (Alexandra and Missy comes into the kitchen.) Alexandra: What is that smell... Oh God. You guys okay? TB: Depends whether you mean physically or mentally. Does he try flambéing his food at home? Alexandra: No, I don't let him cook. Denrol: Well maybe this works out for the best. We should probably go out to eat. I know it's Friday night, but I'm sure we'll find a place without a long wait. TB: In New Orleans? Yeah, McDonald's. Denrol: No, I know a place. Just opened today. I know the owner. Get your stuff, we'll leave in five minutes. (Denrol smiles and leaves the kitchen. Alexandra just shakes her head.) TB: You know a... oh, hell... I heard about this... Alexandra: Heard about what? (Tracer looks at Alexandra as if she just spit at him.) TB: You mean to tell me that you don't know? I mean sure, I didn't hear about it until that guy Pete brought something up at the bar the other day, but you're his WIFE. Alexandra: (smiles) Maybe I'm playing the dumb bimbo wife. Denrol: (Yelling from elsewhere in the house) Hurry up guys, don't want to get out too late, we'll never find a table than! (Tracer rolls his eyes and sighs.) TB: Fine, let's go. Lucky we don't need coats in New Orleans... (Fast forward a few moments as the foursome leaves the house and goes to the driveway. But Tracer's infamous green '78 Dodge Dart is nowhere to be seen. Instead there is a brand new, banana yellow Hummer H. Tracer looks a little confused. He casts his eyes up and down the street.) TB: Dammit, where did my car go? Denrol: It's right there. (points at the H2) Isn't she a beauty? TB: No, I mean the good, reliable car that was here last night. What's the deal with this blazing monstrosity? Denrol: Well see I sold your car to a fan on eBay and used the money to get you a brand new Hummer. She's a very good automobile. You can take it anywhere really. (Tracer's eyes get as wide as dinner plates as he turns toward Denrol.) TB: You... WHAT??!? (In the window we see Extreme and US watching.) US: Here we go. Extreme: Not good. (Back in the driveway, Denrol just smiles and holds up the keys for the Hummer.) Denrol: I got a good $40,000 for a real old piece of crap. I think it worked out well. The guy who bought it was a big fan of yours. TB: You... SOLD... MY... CAR. (Tracer falls to his knees, his arms outstretched as if he's strangling something immediately in front of him. Denrol glances at Missy.) Denrol: What's wrong with him? Missy: *shrugs* You know men and their cars. I think you may have made a tremendous mistake here, Gordon. Denrol: (turns back to Tracer) Listen, you drive this baby for just a few minutes and you'll completely forget about that wreck you were driving. (Tracer is still wringing his hands around an imaginary neck about three feet off the ground.) TB: Not... the POINT... lots of work... put into it... used it... on first date... with wife... had it... on honeymoon... for Christ's sake... Denrol: Hey man, you got one hell of a William Shatner impression there. (Tracer jerks his head around to look up at Denrol, a big grin on his face.) TB: You'd see an even better one if I had a phaser on me right now. Denrol: That's great. I guess I'll drive. Come on girls. (Denrol and Alexandra get into the Hummer. Tracer is still on the ground, on the verge of either tears or murder, whichever is more therapeutic.) TB: My car... my wonderful, hideous car... (The camera pans over to the window again.) US: About ready to give up that stash yet? Extreme: Screw off. (Another fast forward, this one to downtown New Orleans, where the Hummer seems to be stuck in a traffic jam.) Denrol: Jesus, you'd think something big was happening or something. (Tracer has his arms crossed indignantly in the back seat.) TB: God forbid you actually give up the charade and tell us where you're taking us, you ham... Denrol: What you talking about there buddy? Alexandra: He knows Gordie. Denrol: He knows? You told him? Alexandra: I didn't tell him. I played dumb. TB: You think just because I don't read the paper I don't find things out? Denrol: Well ummm. Listen, it's a great photo op. You get to see your adoring public. I'm sure that'll cheer you up. TB: Not unless one of them bought my f***ing Dart, you bastard. Denrol: Come on man, you're the fan favorite here. Almost there. TB: Hmph... all those memories... all that emotional value... all gone... Denrol: Missy, can you do something to cheer him up? Missy: Not in front of company... (Tracer casts a warning glance at his wife.) Missy: Besides, he is right. That car held a lot of meaning to both of us. He's had it since... actually, since well BEFORE we met, but the point is that all of our road trips and so forth were done in that car. You can't just ignore that. Denrol: It's just a car guys. Just a car. Anyway, we're here. (The hummer takes a turn and pulls into the Denrol House of Pizza parking lot. Some people in the parking lot see the persons in the Hummer and begin to cheer.) Missy: It really is kind of impressive just how many people will show up at one of these openings. (As the Hummer is being directed through the crowd to the private backlot, they pass a familar looking green Dodge Dart.) TB: STOP!!! (Tracer doesn't even wait for the Hummer to stop before opening the door and jumping out, heading straight for the car. Of course, a crowd soon forms and surrounds Tracer, but he manages to make it to the Dart. Behind him Denrol parks the Hummer and the other three follow him. Tracer falls to his knees, caressing the Dart.) TB: This has got to be mine... yes... oh, thank God I've found you... (Someone from the crowd yells out.) Voice: Get away from my CAR! (Tracer is busy hugging and running his hands over the body of the car. He stops short and tilts his head to the side.) TB: I know that voice... (Some in the crowd with good memories boo as Father Denrol; Denrol's "brother" comes through the crowd, twirling a set of keys. He is dressed in his classic priest outfit, but lacks the glowing red eyes. Tracer stands up and immediately gets right in Father Denrol's face.) TB: You bought... my car... my precious... well I've got just one thing to say to you... Father Denrol: And what is that, sinner? Denrol: That guy never goes away. Talk about annoying. (Tracer pulls out his checkbook and a pen, and flips the book open.) TB: Fifty grand. Right now. Father Denrol: I am not a cheap whore, like your wife. I am a man of God and will not take your dirty money. (Tracer pauses for what seems like an excruciatingly long while before suddenly laughing and putting his checkbook away.) TB: Ha ha, you're right of course. You won't take my money. But you know what? Father Denrol: What? TB: I WILL take the car... (Tracer hauls off and plants a hard fist in Father Denrol's face. The crowd cheers as Father Denrol hits the floor. Denrol shakes his head and turns to Missy.) Denrol: He always that violent? Missy: Not usually, no. But somehow, I doubt that quickness to action was a result of the car. (Tracer shakes his hand off a little bit, staring down at Father Denrol.) TB: That's for insulting my wife, asshole. Missy: See? Father Denrol: (rubbing his chin) You shall pay for that sinner! TB: You're damn right I will... (Tracer pulls his checkbook back out, and scribbles something down before ripping off the check, dropping it on Father Denrol, and snatching the keys from his hands. Denrol shakes his heads as a slight breeze comes and blows away the check, Father Denrol goes running after it.) Denrol: Now that THAT is resolved, can we go in? (Tracer caresses his newly reacquired car yet again.) TB: Somehow, I doubt it's fully resolved... soon as he sees the amount on that check, I'm sure he'll be back. But yes, let's. Denrol: Why, how much did you sign it for? (The four go back to the Hummer and get back in, drive to the private parking lot.) TB: Well, I originally offered more than he paid for it. You heard me. Denrol: Right. $50,000 I think. TB: Yep. Well, let's just say that after his initial refusal to sell it back, I made it out in an amount that I thought was fair. Denrol: That being? (Tracer laughs again.) TB: You know how coupons always say that the actual monetary value of the coupon is 1/20th of a cent? Denrol: Yeah. So you gave him 1/20 of tthe price you offered? $2,500 TB: Nope! (They get in the lot and park.) Denrol: Than? TB: I would have thought you could figure it out by now, but all right... that check has a zero value on it, but I'm sure the paper is worth something. Denrol: That's low, even for you. You do know he could forge a number on there. TB: Don't worry about it. Have no idea how he can forge a number over the word "Bupkiss." Denrol: Right. Well I'm sure you'll be thrilled by the next piece of news. I've agree that you and I will do a 4 hour autograph session here after dinner. TB: Don't care, I've got my car back! I'd be more than willing to sign stuff for these people. Denrol: Ooookay... (The scene fades to black.)
