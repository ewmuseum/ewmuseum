Third Eye

28 Jun 2004

By The Muppet Man

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Think for yourself Question authority Throughout human history, as our species has faced the frightening, terrorizing fact that we do not know who we are, or where we are going in this ocean of chaos, it has been the authorities, the political, the religious, the educational authorities who attempted to comfort us by giving us order, rules, regulations, informing, forming in our minds their view of reality. To think for yourself you must question authority and learn how to put yourself in a state of vulnerable, open-mindedness; chaotic, confused, vulnerability to inform yourself. Think for yourself. Question authority. - Timothy Leary ------------------------------------------ In... Out... In... Out... Myself, the sea before me, the whole world around me moves to this steady rhythm. We are as one. In... Out... In... Out... In and out. The rhythm of life. Everything, from breathing to making love, requires this one, simple, essential motion. The Independence Day Rumble is no different. Everyone comes in, hopeful and happy. Everyone goes out, dejected and depressed. Everyone except one. In... Out... In... Out... My eyes flash open, my concentration lost. I take in a breath sharply, cursing myself, cursing my weakness, as I sit here, staring out at the ocean before me. The fresh sound of the waves against the sand calms me, reassures me. It is a gentle, pleasant sound. Try as I might, I could never quite clear my mind of all thought. I could never become like they were, and deep down I hated myself for it. The wind whistles past me, blowing my hair to and fro, softly caressing my face. It was so many years ago that I first saw it. That face, bright, blue and shimmering. That unnatural, almost holy face, grinning down on me, comforting me. Was it a dream? It must have been...yet it had seemed so real. In the days that followed, I was unsettled. Who wouldn't be? I had to find out more, but no matter how hard I looked, I could never find anything. I could not find anyone who had a similar experience either. The memory is hazy, and it is as such that I can't remember where or when I saw it. But I did. And it shook me, shook me to my very core. Now, in the wake of the Bourbon Street Brawl, and with the Independence Day Rumble drawing ever closer, when I am most in need of something, anything to assure my place in the WWA, I relive that memory...if you could call it as much as a memory. I am at a turning point in my life, and in front of me lies the biggest obstacle yet...the Independence Day Rumble. I must overcome the odds, I have to win, if only to satisfy my ever growing appetite for success. But how can I win? I must defeat 29 other men, one of them being my colleague, Ross, or Quietus as you would know him. This is more than a simple hurdle I can leap over. This is a mountain, a mountain I must clear with a single bound. I think you will agree that it is impossible for an ordinary human to clear a mountain with a single. An ordinary human. An ordinary, mundane human being. I must break down the walls, and cross into territory entered only by a select few humans, all with exceptional ability. I must exceed the limits set to me by this physical body, I must surpass what any other competitor in this match has ever done. For lack of a better word, I must ascend. In the past, I have said that exact same thing, but never have I meant it in such a literal sense. I must ascend, evolve to my next level. And I will. All those years ago, when I saw that higher body, that evolved consciousness, I was, to be frank, a pathetic junkie. Drugs were my life, my whole being. I witnessed that heavenly vision on an acid trip one night, and it changed my whole perspective on life. I began to drift away from drug use, particularly acid. Luckily, I was not too absorbed in them, too connected to them to give it up. I managed it, but in the struggle I forgot about the vision, or so it seemed. But now, in my hour of need, it returns to me. Finally, I comprehend that this being I viewed was an evolution of myself. I have seen my potential, come across it in a chance meeting. It looked down on me, smiling, beckoning me to follow it, to ride the spiral further and further. To see where I would end up, and what lay ahead. But I was scared, and I ran away. I ran away. I ran as fast as I could. I could almost sense it calling after me, even though I heard nothing. "Why are you running away? Why are you running? Why?" Those figmental words echoed through my brain as if they were as real as real could be. Now, as I sit on this desolate beach, staring out at the blue vastness before me, shimmering in the sun, hazy visions of that face calling after me drift into my mind, as clear as day. But visions are not enough. I must make it reality, and not only must I see it...I must become it. Meditation is the key to releasing one's spiritual potential. Or, at least, it's supposed to be. Many years ago, I tried meditation as a way to detach myself from the horrible drudge that was my life. I failed. Now, I return to these old ways, which I discarded all that time ago. Success has been rather hard to come by, and only in small patches. I run my hand slowly along the sand beneath me. Soft, very soft. I take some up in my hand, watching it trickle down as smoothly as a liquid. I sigh. I am somewhere I don't want to be, chasing something I don't want to see. Am I scared? Am I afraid of what I might see, and what I might become? With my eyes closed, I see myself in this exact position, the ground around me shrouded in darkness. Unaided visions. I'm getting there. Slowly but surely, I'm getting there. IDR is a few short steps away. I feel a change in the wind; the pace has picked up. The first drop of rain glances softly off my cheek, followed by another, and another. There is a nother strong gust of wind, blowing my hair strongly to one side. I let the rain fall unhindered onto my masked face. The strong whistling of the wind drowns out all the calming noise of the waves. I smile. I have been given a test, and I'll be damned if I'm going to give up now. IDR is a few short steps away, and all I have to do now is wait. With my eyes closed, I see all. Smiling, I look upon him...again. I look upon myself...again. It comforts me, with it's three warm and wild eyes, blazing like burning coals. And I sit on, Watching that shimmering face swim before me, grinning, beckoning me on. Just like before. I cannot help but grin back. And I sit on, Prying open my third eye.
