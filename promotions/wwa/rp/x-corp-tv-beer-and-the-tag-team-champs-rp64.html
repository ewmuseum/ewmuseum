X Corp: TV, Beer and the Tag Team Champs

29 May 2003

By Dave Harley

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The scene opens in Dave Harley's Chicago apartment. Seated in two huge recliners in front of the TV are Dave Harley and Mal Somers. They each have a beer in their hand as they watch the latest WWA Insider. There are empty beer cans strewn around the room. A Cell promo plays.] Somers: Ha! Look at that! 'We are the Demons you cannot beat'! Harley: Yeah right. More like we are the Demons who always get beat! Somers: I dunno Harley, can we actually beat The Cell? Harley: If The Cell are unbeatable, are we really the Tag Team Champions? Somers: Woah, that's like that 'tree-falling-in-the-woods' thing. Harley: Exactly. If Leviathan fell in the woods would his brain fall out? Somers: How about this one; If Lockdown fell in the woods, would you smell it? Harley: Ha Ha! [The screen now shows the latest Havoc promo.] Harley: Hey Chairboy, can you believe it? It was all a dream! Somers: Aw, c'mon Harley! Don't spoil the ending for me! Harley: It's a bit like that TV show...umm, what was it? Twin Peaks? Somers: No way. Twin Peaks was the one where the midget spoke funny! Harley: Heh Heh. Sounds just like Fiesta to me! Mini-showtimes, Mini-Curzons... Somers: It was Dallas. Harley: What? Somers: Dallas! Remember? Who shot JR? Harley: Somebody shot JR?! I always liked that guy. "He's drinkin' beer! He's drinkin' beer! He's drinkin' beer!" Somers: Should we really be drinking all this when we've got a title match on Sunday? Harley: Relax Chairboy! This is a celebration! X Corp has been through a lot recently. We need to relax and unwind before our big match. Somers: But three out of the six guys we're facing think they're gods! Harley: Hey! I don't recognise them as gods! Not in the Harleytarian Church of Extreme! Somers: That's not a church, it's a strip club down the road! Harley: It is not! (grins) Alright, you want to do some training, how about some co-ordination training? Somers: Say what? Harley: EVD was right. That is your new catchphrase. Anyhoo, stand up. [Somers groans as he puts his beer down and stands up. Harley pulls a plastic funnel and a coin from behind his recliner and stands up also.] Harley: OK. Put this in the front of your pants. Somers: The coin? Harley: No, Chairboy, the funnel. Jack taught me this coordination exercise. You put the funnel in the front of your pants and tilt your head back, and put the coin on your forehead. Count to three, and see if you can drop the coin in the funnel. It may take some practice, but it will improve your coordination skills. Somers: No problemos mah boy! It'll be a walk in the park! [Somers puts the funnel in the front of his pants and tilts his head back, placing the coin on his forehead.] Somers: OK..ONE, TWO..AAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAARRRRRRRRRRGGGGGHHHHHHH!!!!!!!!! [Harley cracks up as he pours an icy cold beverage down the funnel and into Mal's pants.] Harley: HAHAHAHAHAHAHAH! You fell for it! What a looooosseeerr! Somers: [Looking at the big wet stain on the front of his pants and laughing too.] You (beep)! Harley: Its OK, I fell for it too when Jack did it to me. It is supposed to be training, but more of a "being aware of surprise attacks" training. [The two chuckle as a key is turned in the door. Dave Harley's PA Jill walks in with a bag of groceries under her arms.] Jill: Good evening gentlemen. (looks at Mal) Maybe I should come back later. Somers: (Covering himself up with his Tag Team Title). No! Its not what you think! Jill: Well then, thank you for all your help carrying these groceries back from the store! Somers: Did we help her carry groceries back from the store? Harley: I guess so. Somers: No problem Jill! Jill: [sighs] Mr Harley, Jack Knight called earlier. He said to meet him in the usual place tomorrow morning. Somers: Tomorrow morning?! Man, that's enough to sober me! Harley: Hey, don't worry about it Chairboy, I'll just give him a ring and tell him we're not coming. Somers: I dunno Harley, we both know Jack's philosophy! [Harley staggers over to the phone and dials.] Harley: Hey Jack! Listen, about tomorrow morning...nah, we can't make it man...whaddya mean my speach is slurred?! Hello?...Hello? Somers: What did he say? Harley: He says he'll pick us up at six! Somers: Well, that sure drained the alcohol out of my system! Harley: Yup...wanna order a pizza? [Fade out]
