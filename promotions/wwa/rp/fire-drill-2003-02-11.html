Fire Drill?

11 Feb 2003

By Shaman

Item 6 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 11 Feb 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
** Scene opens to a firehouse somewhere in new York City as fire trucks are heading out very fast and slicing the traffic like butter as a man dressed in firemens clothes quickly bolts into the firehouse and runs over to the stairs and then heads up them only to momentarily be seen coming down the firepole very fast. Smiling he walks into another room as the firetrucks pull back into the firehouse.** Jeff: You know that was one of the most idiotic calls ive seen to date! Jason: Well nobody got hurt was all that i cared about! Jeff: You are about as new as anyone can be! Jason: Wheres the beast? Jeff: Who cares where the dog is! Jason: Man you need to relax! ** A figure comes stepping out fo the back room smiling as he steps forward it is seen that its Shaman.** [Shaman] " precisely the reason i showed up. Let me take you gentlemen out on the town. My treat i'll take care of all the expenses. Now the question is which vehicle do we take?" ** Shaman looks over his shoulder at the ladder truck.** [Shaman] " And we have a winner. So shut up and climb on in!" Jeff: We can't use this truck! ** Shaman smacks the guy upside the head and looks at the other fireman and gives a stern look as he leans forward.** [Shaman] " Any other questions or telling me i cannot do something??" Jeff: Uh no sir! Jason: Woohooo lets get rolling i haven't had no fun in months! [Shaman] " thats the excitement i was looking for. I'll drive and you two can make asses of yourselves while we have a little fun." ** Shaman starts the ladder truck up and blasts through the front bay doors as Shaman yells out in joy as the go screaming down fifth avenue with the sirens blaring and Shaman seems to be enjoying himself.** [Shaman] " Yeeee haw this is a blast. No wonder you guys became firemen. This stuff is a blast. Hey whatta you boys say we go down to wall street. have some fun destroy some stocks!" Jeff: Hot damn yeah. We need to destroy some schmuck boy yuppee damn stock!! Jason: Uhm Jeff your not stable! Jeff: Ive said the same about you for weeks. lets go have some fun at everyone elses expense! [Shaman] " Damn you guys are a barrel of fun here. lets get a move on!!" ** The fire engine screams down wall street and stops in front of a Taco Bell where Shaman gets out and walks over and orders some food.** [Shaman] " You know we have got to have some food here. No use in having too much fun on an empty stomach." ** Suddenly as jason gets out of the fire engine it screams off down the road again as Shaman grows visibly angry at this and turns to Jason.** [Shaman] " That fruitcake friend of yours juststole our wheels and went to have fun all by himself. Now im going to have to beat him severely. How many bl;ocks are we from the nearest fire station??" Jason: About 2 blocks! [Shaman] " Good we go get an ambulance where i can practice first aid!" Jason: Uh ok! ** Both men hail a cab at the sametime as Shaman pokes the glass in the cab and grows annoyed as he kicks the window seperating the driver and the passengers as Shaman grabs the driver by the turban and speaks.** [Shaman} " Head to the nearest fire station. I hope we have an undertsnading Ali baba!!" Cab Driver: Oh you american so funny! no worries sah im great indian cab driver i weel get you there very very quickly!! [Shaman] " just shut up and drive! Man damn foreigners never know how to shut up!" ** Shaman sits in the back rubbing his head as he listens to the cab driver ramble on and on. As Jason seems to be holding his head as well.** Cab Driver: We are here that will be....!?! ** Suddenly Shaman reaches through and slams the mans face down upon the steering wheel and gets out of the car as Jason claps his hands in an applause for Shamans doings.** [Shaman] " Man that guy was annoying. To hell with this lets get that ambulance!" Jason: Lets get going shall we! ** Shaman and Jason climb into an ambulance as Shaman drops the shifter into drive as he halls down the street as the siren wails and he seems to be heading straight for Wall Street. They get to the commercial and trades building and they both get out as Shaman steps out and smiles as he reaches into the back of the ambulance and pulls out several rolls of gauze.** [Shaman] " Time for first aid experience. I think your friendly neighborhood Shaman has somethings to do." ** Shaman walks into the building and comes back out with a folding chair andf walks up to Jeff who is ranting and raving and not seeing Shaman walk up behind him and gets clocked by the chair as Shaman smiles and wraps him in the gauze and then turns dragging the man back to the ambulance and motions for some duck tape and then begins taping the man to the front of the ambulance as Jason is seen looking on with a straight jacket on as Shaman grins and walks over to Jason and walks him over to a park bench and sits him down and walks away climibing back into and ambulance as Shaman speaks.** [Shaman] " You stay here and enjoy the view i'll be back to give you a drive later. Right now Jeff and i have a highway to catch. If i comeback you will have nothing to fear but fear itself. Unpredictable ain't i. I'll send a christmas card for you at the funny farm. I would go there, but they'd send me home with a diploma! Anyways have a good day and enjoy yourself and your few days of vacationing." ** Shaman smiles as he sticks a lolli pop into Jasons mouth and climbs into the van and grins as he punches the gas and speeds down the street as the sirens wail and a man is heard screaming as the ambulance draws closer it is seen that a man is duck taped to the front as Shaman is seen behind the wheel laughing and slapping the steering wheel as the camera fades these words can be seen.** GOT BLOOD!!
