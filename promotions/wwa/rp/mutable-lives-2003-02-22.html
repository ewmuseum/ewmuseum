Mutable lives

22 Feb 2003

By Nicholas Adams

Item 5 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 22 Feb 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A man once said that time was mutable. He wrote about a group of time traveling police. They kept history in the order that it has been known. They also created a future that their commanders, who will live over fifteen millennia after our current lives, wanted. Some people transfer this idea to other things. For example, victory can be mutable if the victorious person is defeated soon after their victory. Destiny is also mutable. Otherwise, why would God give humanity free will? Otherwise, why would a person develop the concept of multiple timelines existing on different tangents? Otherwise, why would the Roman Empire, who seemed to be destined to rule the world, suddenly become just another crumbling group of countries without anything but a memory of Pax Romana? It is something that many ask. Nicholas Adams isn't such a person. He just acknowledges that he can change his destiny, and leaves it at that. At this moment, Adams is actually sitting at a table in a café, drinking some...well, it's an unidentified drink, but it's in a tea glass, so it's either coffee or tea. Whatever it is, it's unimportant, since it doesn't really relate to anything. Adams takes a sip of his copy, then lifts up his newspaper. The name is blotted out, since the paper has not paid to advertise on WWA channels. A man walks up and nods at Adams in recognition. Nick looks up, smiles, and nods back, then goes back to his paper. The man had obviously been thinking that Nick was just constantly a public persona, as he is sort of taken aback by how Nick's response was like that of any other person. The man decides not to press it, and then walks off to another table. About ten seconds later, Wallace Coleman walks in and takes a seat across from Nick. A waitress quickly comes up and Wallace orders coffee before turning back to Adams. "So, Nick, why are we here, exactly?" "Well, depending on what you believe, humanity was just an accident, or God created us," Nick replies calmly, then turns the page. "As for why we’re at this café, it's just because it's got good tea and it's just amusing to see people walk up to me, expecting me to act like I do in the ring, and then discover that I'm just another guy." "Uh, Nick, that's sort of a problem, at least with the higher-ups," Wallace replies. Nick shakes his head, turns the page of the newspaper, and then looks up and retorts, "Well, if Warr has a problem with it, he can tell me himself. After all, Grunge said that I was 'normal'." "Speaking of which, the man did challenge you to a rematch on the Meltdown after Global Warfare. Doesn't that mean anything to you?" "Wallace, right now, I'm reading the news and drinking tea. Do you think it matters to me all that much?" Adams then closes the newspaper, folds it in half, and sets it down on the table. "But if you want me to send a memo or something, you send it to the booking staff. Write these statements to them: 'Match to be booked on next Meltdown - Nicholas Adams versus Grunge for the Television title. All other peoples besides referees and announce crew are banned from ringside.'" Wallace is slightly confused, and looks at Adams as if to say, "What the hell?" but does not voice his opinion. Instead, he says, "Are you sure about that stipulation, Nick? Are you absolutely sure?" Nick simply nods, takes a gulp of his tea, then lifts the paper, and reopens it to the exact page that he was reading, and continues to scan the news. Wallace, realizing that Nick is doubtful to say anything else, stands up, drops a five-dollar bill on the table, takes a gulp of his coffee, and walks out of the café. Nicholas Adams, a man who believes that destiny can be changed, lowers the paper and smiles. FADE OUT
