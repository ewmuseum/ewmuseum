The Searcher (part 8)

31 Jan 2003

Author not credited in the source

Item 2 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 31 Jan 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Private Detective Dirk Flint has finally found the missing man, at a truck-stop just outside Chicago. The man is unrecognisable to WWA fans but to Dirk's heightened PI senses he knows he has found his man. The guy is dressed entirely in black including a black balaclava. Flint openms the passenger door and the guy gets in.) Guy: Hi, Mr Flint? Flint: That's me. Guy: Thank's for coming to get me. Flint: Why are you dressed like that? Guy: I have mild facial leprosy, which is why I left wrestling in the first place! However after a lot of treament it is finally cured but the doctor gave me strict instructions not remove the balaclava for another 2 days. Flint: That still doesn't explain why you aren't wearing any pants. (The guy shrugs.) Guy: How long will it take to get to Ottawa? Flint: Calgary. Guy: Canadian Cities aren't a recognised measurement of distance. Flint: No, we are going to Calgary! Guy: But what abotu Rage In The Cage? Flint: That is in Calgary! Guy: Oh. So they moved it. (Dirk decides it is easier just to leave it.) Flint: Yes, they moved it. Guy: I wish they'd told me! I'd already gone as far as Chicago and Ottawa is only a couple of miles from there. (The two men sit in silence as trees whizz by on either side.) Guy: Do you mind if I put some music on? Flint: Go ahead. (The man puts a tape into the cassete player. Bohemian Rhapsody starts to play. It's just about to get to the rock bit.) Guy: Here we go! (The man starts head-banging like he's having a fit. His arms flail madly and he accidentally punches Flint in the head. The car swerves across 2 lanes of traffic, crashes through a fence and comes to rest in a haystack.) Flint: Thanks for that. Guy: This soprt of thing always happens to me. Are we still going to get to Calgary on time, only I've got some important business to take care of. Flint: We'll get there, my assitant will come and pick us up in the helicopter. NO worries. Guy: OK, That's cool, I've got to get there. Because Sunday is V-Day and this multiple time champion needs to whup some ass! (Fade to black.)
