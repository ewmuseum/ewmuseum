Don't Ask Jonathon Winters (IC)

6 March 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
6 March 2006 Don't ask me anything. I don't much care about what you need to know and I have no time for such trivialities. Ventressa made me do this. ---- 6 March 2006 Ooooh someone’s got a temper :smiley: ---- 6 March 2006 Why didn't you show up for Global Wrestling's 2004 Showdown in Tokyo? You were my teammate and your complete lack of respect for myself and our teammates was rather disheartening. Your carelessness almost cost me a victory, you're just lucky I can carry a dead weight team by myself. What happened that night? Why did you never come back and then suddenly show up in WWA? ---- 6 March 2006
Why didn't you show up for Global Wrestling's 2004 Showdown in Tokyo? You were my teammate and your complete lack of respect for myself and our teammates was rather disheartening. Your carelessness almost cost me a victory, you're just lucky I can carry a dead weight team by myself. What happened that night? Why did you never come back and then suddenly show up in WWA?
Pffft. Like I need to answer to you. ---- 6 March 2006
Ooooh someone’s got a temper :smiley:
You have a high estimation of yourself. I wouldn't waste my anger on insignificants. I regard you - all of you - with great indifference. Get over it and move on. ---- 6 March 2006 *Ducky sits in Dave's palm finding it difficult to comprehend how it is that something can be greatly indifferent, when being indifferent in itself is a statement of apathy.* *Dave just looks a bit miffed as to what the big words mean.* ---- 7 March 2006
You have a high estimation of yourself. I wouldn't waste my anger on insignificants. I regard you - all of you - with great indifference. Get over it and move on.
Really? So just how do you view the only two men that you haven't been able to beat since you joined the WWA then my friend - myself and the new US Champion, Khaled? By the way, commisserations on your loss. ---- 7 March 2006
Really? So just how do you view the only two men that you haven't been able to beat since you joined the WWA then my friend - myself and the new US Champion, Khaled? By the way, commisserations on your loss.
Someone was bound to do it in the end. Not to defeat me, but realise that the only way anybody can beat me is with the use of a foreign object. If I were in Khaled's position I couldn't - no wouldn't - live with the dishonour. As for you, you only manager to draw with me via outside interference. At least your buddy Khaled managed to get the job done when he cheated. ---- 8 March 2006 Hrrrmm lemme ask you something Mr. Winters are you disappoiinted by the graces of PTC guys that Jorge Samuelsson made it further than you in the tournament called GTT5? Yet your only advancement came when you got reinstated through someone elses tragic exit? Oh by the way yes i know you beat Jorge Samuelsson, But trust me the video say barely very barely and you got your ass handed to you in the process. ---- 9 March 2006 I was asked back into the GTT5 because everybody inviolved knew I was screwed in the first round. You only managed to advance because you had the luck of the draw. As me beating you...well yes it was close, but a victory is still a victory. You just go ahead and askb Khaled. By the way Samuelsson, do you still have my TV title? I say my because, I was never pinned for that title and I'm asking because that belt hasn't had stability since I held the damn thing! ---- 9 March 2006
Hrrrmm lemme ask you something Mr. Winters are you disappoiinted by the graces of PTC guys that Jorge Samuelsson made it further than you in the tournament called GTT5? Yet your only advancement came when you got reinstated through someone elses tragic exit? Oh by the way yes i know you beat Jorge Samuelsson, But trust me the video say barely very barely and you got your ass handed to you in the process.
Jorge, my little viking friend, I am not sticking up for Winters at all here - because when you think of it he is the only two time loser that the WWA had in the GTT5 - but something that you have to consider here is that you and Somers had the PTC powers-that-be pulling for you. You have to admit that you two had the much easier ride through the tournament and if you had faced guys of the calibre that Winters or myself did you would - like Winters but without the second chance I suspect - have vacated early. ---- 9 March 2006 (Smiling Shaman grins) Shaman: Even you Ember with the great intellect of yours should have been wise enugh tor realise it was not Samuelsson asking the questions there. He isn't patient enough to ask he would only beat them out of you. Besides you seem to try and speak for everyone in the WWA no a wise move my friend. Why don't you let Mr. Winters answer for hismelf i am sure he will stomp your ass when opportunity presents itself without you cheating. ---- 9 March 2006 "Besides you seem to try and speak for everyone in the WWA no a wise move my friend." I am the WWA World champion Shaman, that gives me the right to speak for the federation - there is no-one better suited to do so. ---- 9 March 2006
"Besides you seem to try and speak for everyone in the WWA no a wise move my friend." I am the WWA World champion Shaman, that gives me the right to speak for the federation - there is no-one better suited to do so.
Fear not. You're only the transitional world champion and soon you have to - or won't be able to, I haven't decided yet - to speak for anyone ever again. ---- 9 March 2006 Sorry aren't you the guy that just lost the US title, the guy that just got beaten by Khaled??? Yes, yes you are. Tell me something, do the numbers 12-1-1 mean anything to you? ---- 9 March 2006
Sorry aren't you the guy that just lost the US title, the guy that just got beaten by Khaled??? Yes, yes you are. Tell me something, do the numbers 12-1-1 mean anything to you?
Yes. Those numbers do mean something to me. It's the win/loss record everyone else in the WWA locker room can only dream about. ---- 11 March 2006 Murphy: *Laughs* Oh, the joys of listening to self-indulgence on a grand scale. ---- 11 March 2006 (Listening to the conversation Shaman laughs.) Shaman: Winters put those numbers up for ten years then bring up something to be proud of. (Looking around he turns and walks away.) Shaman: Not even worth the effort. ---- 11 March 2006
Murphy: *Laughs* Oh, the joys of listening to self-indulgence on a grand scale.
Perhaps an admittance of our superiority? ---- 11 March 2006

The green-eyed monser doth toll for you. Perhaps you should concentrate on your own career than to stare, starry eyed, at the meteoric rise of mine. Perhaps then, you'll rekindle whatever talent you believe you have and escape your reverie. ---- 11 March 2006 *Ducky regards Jonathon Winters with great mediocrity* ---- 11 March 2006 *Rolls eyes* ---- 11 March 2006 (Shaman looks over at Winters) Shaman: Quit making googly eyes at Hurst someone is going to think stuff is a bit fruity between you two! (Shaman shrugs and throws a condom over at Winters) Shaman: Well at least use protection! ---- 12 March 2006 This post was brought to you by Vasaline Intensive Care --- "If the shoe fits, you need more of this! ... just watch out for the stilettos, that's an accident waiting to happen!" ---- 13 March 2006 Murphy: Ah, so you could not see past that Ego of yours, Winters? Soon you will learn. Very soon. ---- 13 March 2006 Dave: That's not his ego, that's his nose. ---- 13 March 2006 *Murphy looks a bit closer* Darn, your right. *Hands Winters a plastic surgeon business card.* They are good, look at the job they did with Sha-woman. ---- 13 March 2006 Notice the simpletons turn to worthless name-calling in order to have attention to them. *Shakes head* How sad. ---- 14 March 2006 *licks window*
