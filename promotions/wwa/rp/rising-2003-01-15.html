Rising

15 Jan 2003

By Leviathan

Item 8 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 15 Jan 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(THE CELL – BRUTALLY TRUTHFUL flashes on the screen. A familiar voice is heard as the graphics fade into Leviathan standing in front of a white wall wearing a white suit.) Leviathan : First, Fiesta will be a statement of our intentions. (Pictures of James Curzon and his assistant, Fagin appear on the screen. Then the view switches back to Leviathan.) Leviathan : At Rage in the Cage, there will be the confrontation. (The “Extreme Corporation” of Mal Somers and Dave Harley is shown standing triumphantly last Fiesta after there revenge attack.) Leviathan : And lastly when the time is right, there will be the revelation. (The word “CONGRATULATIONS” is flashed on the screen. A close up of Leviathan is shown and his once green eyes are now blood red and the rage can be felt through the camera.) Leviathan : It’s TIME TO INCREASE THE BEAST!!
