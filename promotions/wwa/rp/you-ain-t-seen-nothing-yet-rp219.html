You Ain't Seen Nothing Yet!

2 Jul 2003

By Mal Somers

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(We open in Mal Somers' apartment where he stands back to camera, in front of a fold out card table. He is mumbling to himself, and does this for a little while before stepping back. The camera zooms in and we can see that Mal has torn out four pictures from magazines, and has placed them down flat at each caorner of the table. Somers crouches down and picks up his beloved Rookie, which shimmers briefly in the sunlight coming through the window.) Somers: Well well well Rookie, three holes in the ground! Nyuk Nyuk! We did well at the Bourbon Street Brawl didn't we? We made it to the final five, which was OK...but the best news is that an X-Corp member won! (Mal taps his belt friend a couple of times, clapping in appreciation.) Somers: Well done Chance old buddy, you deserved the win! And our good friend here, Rookie, was able to predict the outcome halfway through the match!! (Looking at Rookie) At first, I was a little miffed that when we played "Spin the Rookie" you picked Chance, but now I realise you weren't playing favorites...at least I hope you weren't!! (Somers grins at the camera and then at Rookie.) Somers: You just have an uncanny ability to see into the future! So with that in mind, I'm going to put you to the test. I've cut out four pictures of some random hot mamas...we've got Kylie Minogue, Shannon Elisabeth, Cameron Diaz and Heather Graham! (Somers puts Rookie on the table.) Somers: So, I'me going to get Rookie to do some breakdancing, and I'll ask him which one will end up with me some day!! (Somers grabs the belt and spins it. It spins round and round and round and round. It then spins wildly off the table and lands on the floor next to a copy of National Geographic. Rookie's macaroni facial features smile back at Somers.) Somers: Doh! Some fine tuning may be required. I mean, heck...Rookie was the one that told me to play paper rock scissors with Dave to determine who would pin who when there were still several enemies left in the match! (Somers shakes his head, then stares down the camera lens.) Somers: There wont be any tactical errors when I step into the Rumble this Sunday, no siree! You see, I have drawn a number between one and thirty, so I have a good chance of stepping out with the win. I'm going to watch all the old Rumbles today with Rookie and I'm going to analyse and scrutinise til I can't analyse and scrutinise no more!!! Everyone saw how well X-Corp fared in the Brawl...just wait til the Rumble...you ain't seen nothing yet! (We fade out as Somers places Rookie on the fold out card table and spins one more time. Rookie again slides off the desk as we go to black.)
