Contemplation

15 Feb 2003

By Nicholas Adams

Item 2 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 15 Feb 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Nighttime always causes the senses to become more alert. That was one thing about which the composer Andrew Lloyd Webber was correct. It also causes a person to become fearful of what is around the next corner. It makes a person suddenly become more aware of everything around them, even the slightest rustle of the leaves. It's one reason that Nicholas Adams likes being in the ring at a house show. Most of the arena is completely dark, and he's just staring at his opponent, with darkness around them. Nothing else matters except for the light. It makes him more alert, and yet...it's not the same as appearing on a television show. That's why he's recently pushing himself to the limit, taking on whoever comes his way. What's more, he thinks that he's been doing it well. The problem is, right now, he's walking through Ottawa, Canada in the middle of the day, wondering what in the world he's going to do at Fiesta and Meltdown. What if Grunge loses on Fiesta, but not to him? What if his match against Grunge suddenly becomes just another fight in a long list of fights? What if it suddenly becomes insignificant? What if? Well, it doesn't really matter. He's just walking along gathering his thoughts. Suddenly, a ring comes from his pocket. Nick pulls out a cellular phone and presses a button. "Adams here," Nick says, waits a couple of second then says. "Hey, Juan. How are you holding up? ... Wait, she called? I thought she said we were over. ... Well, if she said she's be at Meltdown, the only thing is, who's getting her the...? ... Oh, you're getting the tickets. All right, then. See you at Meltdown, along with Anna." Nick presses another button lowers the phone slowly, puts the electronic device in his pocket, and sighs. He wasn't even expecting a call from her. He let Juan watch over the house for the next couple of months because he was going to be away, travelling around Canada and the Eastern US. He had told Juan not to expect Anna to call. And now, here he is. "Well, that was unexpected. Looks like I'm going to have to give Grunge more than just a...no, being brutal to my opponents without a second though was why Anna left me in the first place. She said that she didn't want to be around somebody that potentially explosive." Nick keeps on walking, and sees that he's actually come around in a circle, winding up back at his hotel. Adams goes and sits down at a nearby bench. "Well, I think that I can handle Grunge just by wrestling him. I've almost forgotten how to do that. But I still remember my match with Fantasma. I remember all too well. I took him out by just wrestling him. That's all. No other problems. So that's what I'm going to have to do with Grunge. Regardless of what he tries to do...I'm keeping it strictly wrestling." Nick gets up and walks into the hotel silently, with only his shoes making a sound.
