Tootles?

11 Jun 2003

By Grunge

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
FADE IN (We enter the white hall which leads to Grunge’s interview room. Mike the cameraman stops at the door at the end of the hall and knocks on the door. A tiny voice echoes out.) Voice: Who goes there? Mike: Uh the cameraman. I’m here to interview Grunge. (The bottom half of the door opens and out comes a man of very small stature with long black hair and goatee. He’s wearing a black trench coat, boots, and glasses. The camera has to point down to get a good shot of him.) Voice: Nobody gets in to see the boss. (Mike, obviously not in a good mood kicks the little fellow in the knee and opens the door. Grunge sits in a hot-tub in the middle of the room.) Grunge: Daddy was right you just can’t get good help these days. Mike: Y’know your doorman looks a little familiar. (Grunge takes a sip of white wine that he has sitting next to the tub. He then sits the glass down.) Grunge: You mean Steve Bordon without the makeup? Mike: No I mean… Grunge: (interrupting) Or perhaps the Crow without the makeup. After all that’s were WCW got the idea for. (Mike slaps himself in the head.) Mike: No I mean somebody who works for the WWA. Somebody who you’ll be facing at Meltdown… (Grunge gets out of the hot-tub and Mike changes the camera’s position while Grunge puts on a red bathrobe.) Grunge: Nick Adams? Mike: No. Grunge: Dave Harley? Mike: NO! (Grunge begins to chuckle as he beckons Mike to turn the camera back onto him.) Grunge: I know who you mean, Goth Boy. The artist formally known as Ekosyde. What the hell did that name mean anyway? Kill the world or something. Eco meaning world and side meaning murder and all. Mike: I’m guessing your not to worried about the fatal four-way on Meltdown then? Grunge: Not at all. As far as Adams and Harley goes it’s just a friendly competition. Mike: What about Enigma? Grunge: Enigma should remember if you're going to be goth, there's one rule you must learn now: Boots first, then corset. (Once you put on a good corset, you won't be able to lace up your boots!) I mean the one you’re wearing now must be much more constrictive. (Grunge goes over to the corner and picks up the United States Title and places it on his shoulder.) Grunge: Enigma will finally learn that his so called “catchphrase” nothing can stop me now is very far from the truth. In fact he doesn’t have to worry about things like that. He’s reached his peak. (Grunge begins to laugh.) Grunge: Rock and roll boys. And I’ll see you on Meltdown. (Grunge begins to wave.) Grunge: Tootles. (And at that we begin to…) FADE OUT
