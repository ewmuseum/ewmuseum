The Odd Couple

29 Dec 2002

By Dave Harley

Item 2 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 29 Dec 2002, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The scene opens on a hospital bed. Sitting at bedside is Dave Harley’s PA Jill. The camera pans across to Harley, who is lying in the bed with taped up ribs. He tries to sit up and grimaces with pain.] PA: I did warn you not to participate in this Hardcore Steel Cage Match at Seasons Beatings. Now look at you. Are you sure you’re going to be able to compete in the Tag Match on Fiesta? Harley: Hey, it wasn’t just the Cage Match that did this to me. That asswipe Leviathan caused half of this on Fiesta. This Saturday I have a chance to get even with both Leviathan and Lockdown. So who’s my tag team partner? PA: Errr…I don’t think you’ll like this… Harley: Spit it out. Who is it? PA: Mal Somers. Harley: WHAT!!! Mal Somers! Which asshole booked this match?!! PA: Well he did save you from Leviathans attack on Fiesta. Harley: You think he saved me? I had Leviathan beat. I had him right where I wanted him! I was just biding my time. But Chairboy ran out and ruined everything! This match on Saturday is MY match, so Somers better stay out of my way! PA: Oh, I forgot. This Christmas Card arrived for you yesterday. [She hands him the card and he smiles, ripping open the envelope and reading the greeting inside. As he reads, the smile on his face fades and is replaced by sheer contempt.] PA: Well who is it from? Harley: ‘To Dave Harley. I’m looking forward to outshining you in our match next week. Have a Merry Christmas Loser! From Mal Somers.’ That’s it! Get Commissioner Cruz on the phone, I want this match cancelled! There is no way I’m competing side by side with the Chairman. I want this match changed to Harley v Leviathan! PA: You can’t change matches; it’s not in your contract. Like it or not you and Mal Somers are going to have to work together this Saturday. Or would you rather get beaten down by Lockdown and Leviathan again? [The camera closes in on Harley’s face. His expression has now changed to one of frustration. He rips up the Chairman’s Greetings Card.]
