Brand X

20 Aug 2003

By Dave Harley

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[We hear the sounds of a cheesy little jingle. Suddenly, a picture of the GenX Title comes spinning on to the screen, followed by the grinning face of Dave Harley.] V/O: And now a word from our sponsor! [The scene opens in a dark graveyard. I know, not quite what you were expecting. But hey, Dave Harley is stood there with a bikini-clad babe on each arm. Not bad for a graveyard scene huh? Each babe is holding a box in front of her. One has ‘Brand X’ written on it, the other has ‘Rival Brand’ written on it. Dave flashes a stupid grin at the camera and a spark tingles from his teeth in a DDP kind of moment.] Harley: Do you have trouble shining up the gravestones of your relatives? Hell, maybe you have your own grave set out ready and waiting for you. Is it grimy and filthy? Not any more thanks to Brand X! Take a look at our nearest competitor… [The babe holding the ‘Rival Brand’ box walks over to a moss covered gravestone as the jingle continues to play in the background. She pulls out a cloth and magically wipes away the dirt with a ‘woosh!’ The result? Well, the grave still looks pretty murky, but we can make out the name at least. It reads ‘Enigma’.] Harley: As you can see there’s certainly an improvement. But a little Brand X… [The ‘Brand X’ babe magically wipes away the dirt left behind on Enigma’s grave. ‘Magically’ the name has changed to ‘EkoSyde’.] Harley: Now that’s a real improvement! You can clearly see the difference in using Brand X! Brand X Babe: Wow Dave! It’s so easy! Harley: That’s right! Brand X is incredibly easy to use! But for a limited period, you can get your Brand X with a little added Extreme! [Harley picks up a sledgehammer and smashes the grave into pieces.] Harley: Giving you the option of doing things the easy way…or the EXTREME way! [The picture of the GenX Title spins back on to the screen.] V/O: Brand X. Because 9 out of 10 Extreme fans can clearly see the difference! [Fade out.]
