Words On The Wings Of The Approaching Storm

29 Dec 2002

By Jackson Kraven

Item 4 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 29 Dec 2002, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Monday Might Meltdown draws closer, Tense. Can you feel it? (The Tomorrow Man sits behind his desk for the first time in months, his hair smoothed back and his eyes hidden by wraparound sunglasses. Outside a snowstorm rages.) The storm approaches... a bigger man than you once told me that, and I took him to the limit, despite outside interference. Now it's my turn to warn you of the storm. And I don't think you're big enough or man enough to stop me. (Kraven grins.) Oh, sure, the fans can say that Wes Sanders will be in the ring, and that Wes Sanders will emerge from Meltdown victorious, but you and know both know what that means. Absolutely nothing. The fans are brain-dead, overweight, in-bred retards who would cheer for a turd if it danced and pranced for them. Stupid *beep*ers. No, Ian. You and I both know exactly who will be the winner of our clash on Meltdown. Me. Oh sure, you can hide behind your non-title stipulation, but you know what will happen. You know who will be standing victorious when that bell rings, and who will be lying broken in the ring, knowing he has No More Tomorrows. (Kraven stands, blocking out the dim light from the window, surrounded himself with a strange, almost halo-like effect.) Ian, I promise you this. On Monday Night Meltdown, you will see the future. And you will see that you... ...Just like your reign as caretaker of the WWA World Heavyweight Championship... (The halo seems to grow brighter, as if the light is coming out of Kraven, as if it was some sort of strange feedback from the pent-up hate within the man.) ...Just like the WWA... (The halo grows still brighter, almost blindingly bright now. We can barely see Kraven now, his shape a dark spot in the brightness.) ...have No More Tomorrows. (The entire screen fills with white.)
