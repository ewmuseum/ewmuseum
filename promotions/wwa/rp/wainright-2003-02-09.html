Wainright!!

9 Feb 2003

By Shaman

Item 1 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 9 Feb 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
** Shaman walks across the street dressed as "Wainright" and walks into a store and waves his hands up and down like an idiot impersonating someone else very poorly.** [Shaman] " Im Jason Wainright i get everything i want and believe i can beat everyone up on my road to prosperity. Ignore me the former has been who couldn't cut it my first time around and had to leave and come back and pray i get to face a big super star and get Shaman who is going to hurt me three ways from Sunday. Ignore the fact im an ignorant jack ass who has no chance in hell to get above mid reject status. Ignore the fact that im about as useful as Showtime!!" ** Shaman waves his arms around again and grabs the hostess at the store counter and gives her a big ol kiss.** [Shaman} " Remember me im Jason Wainright the superfluos jackass who hit on you about six months ago when i was a nobody with no road of hope. Will ya have sex with me now that i get to face a big superstar!!" ** The woman begins laughing as Shaman does a little Wainright dance.** Woman: Man your a loon!! [Shaman] " yep thats me the loonie Wainright. egotistical less than spectacular Jason Wainright!!" Woman: Not even close. That guy has the creative gumption of a gnat!! [Shaman] " You know lady your right about that one. He couldn't even do something original. I mean look at this he dresses up like a Native American runs around trying to be me and in reality Troy Walcott and New Star both did this and failed. Lets talk about his talent rating shall we!!" ** Suddenly Shaman walks over to a curtain and yanks it shut as somehow or way a spotlight shines on Shaman and he has a mic and a cheap suit on looking like a game show host.** [Shaman] " Ladies and Gentleman i am your host Shaman!!" ** The woman looks at Shaman as she wonders what the heck he has been smoking and just watches very amused.** [Shaman] " Todays show is called where the idiot turns!! Our target today is Jaaaaaaason Waaaaaainright!! Yes folks the louse of the WWA. The armpit of wrestling the one and only lint from your very own belly button Jason Wainright!! Yes thats right its guess what he has done wrong and where did he steal it from. The problem with todays show is simply this. He has just about said and done everything everyone before him has said. So let me put this into perspective for you. This is have you done anything original besides being self centered anbd annoying!!" ** Shaman stands next to a tv screen and the lady is looking around wondering where all this stuff had come from.** [Shaman] " Okay WWA Shamanites...Nah no that doesn't sound right anyways the lackluster performances of Wainright and his less than stellar appearance at Rage in the Cage has left him wanting!! Desiring the opportunity to defeat the upper guys and i believe we have a winner!!" ** Shaman grins and takes a screwed up game show host pose.** [Shaman] " Johnny tell the fans at home what our prize winner Jason Wainright has won!!" ** A voice booms out over the air waves not seen as the Lady looks around confused.** Voice: Well Shaman our winner Jason Wainright gets to underestimate you and believe you to be defeated with ease. His prize will be the fact he knows that you can be very dangerous. The fact you can hurt him three ways more than he can think of. Only a man who underestimates his opponents abilities and skills will win nothing more than a hospital visit and permanent stay in the world of pain!!" [Shaman] " Well that looks quite an interesting prospect. Maybe Wainright ill act his age quit trying to be someone he is not and really try to work his efforts to someone who really gives a damn!! Wainright your words fall upon deaf ears today. You are about as bothersome as the rash you get from it being too hot and you sweating and your skin gets bothered and festered from the sweat and wet clothes. You think this will be easy. Good i like the cockiness so i can rip it from your throat like someone lancing a boil!! Welcome to my world Wainright im going to make you wish you had never met me ever!! Dorian will pale in comparison as to what im going to do. You better hope i don't end your career mouth!!" **Shaman turns and walks away agitated as he tosses the mic to the lady and disappears as these words appear on the screen after the camera fades to black.** GOT BLOOD!!!
