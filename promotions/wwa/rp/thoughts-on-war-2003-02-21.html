Thoughts on War

21 Feb 2003

By Grunge

Item 3 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 21 Feb 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
FADE IN (The screen fills up with a gigantic bold red word.) WAR (Then it fades away to leave the viewers to see Grunge sitting in his steel-folding chair. He’s listening to his CD player. A strange choice is being played, John Mayer’s comforting tones fills the room and Grunge sways along to the beat and rhythm.) Am I living it right? Am I living it right? Am I living it right? Why Georgia, why? Grunge: Why Georgia. A favorite song of mine. I find John Mayer’s music peaceful and comforting much like James Taylor or Carly Simon. Funny that I would want to find peace so close to a time of war. War that will consume us all. (Grunge hits stop on the CD player.) Grunge: War is survival and most of us simply aren’t built for it. Unlike animals we go far and beyond that which we MUST do to survive. Forget the concept of obtaining a career, the most simplistic example is as follows: we need toilet paper, but even toilet paper needs a marketing scheme. Because we must have the softest toilet paper with the cutest baby on the packaging. The only marketing animals need is whether or not they will live or die if they eat it. What does this have to do with purpose, you ask? As humans, we strive for the better. A constant struggle for the top, or at least another notch nearer to the top and not to make our species better, or even our families better...to make our own lives better. "MY life is meaningful because I accomplished x." Not to mention the abundance of evidence that people suffer depression when the purpose factor is removed. (Grunge looks at the camera.) Grunge: Most would say we’ve evolved but most of us have lost something too. That spark. The will to find our way through things. We aren’t built for survival. It’s all about are comfort and the new MTV loving generation isn’t any better. It could make a poke at Nick Adams there but I won’t because I’m such a nice guy. (Grunge looks serious still wearing his red and white army fatigues.) Grunge: Maybe the athletes, especially the wrestlers are the last to be interested in the concept of testing ourselves through the war, no the art, that we create. But even we are interested in personal gain. Even me as well. So whose right, me or the masses? We’ll see Monday. (Grunge begins his sick laugh once again.) Grunge: The music is fading out. FADE OUT
