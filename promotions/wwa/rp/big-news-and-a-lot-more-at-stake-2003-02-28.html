WWA (1997–2019) Roleplays

"Big news and a lot more at stake."

28 Feb 2003

By Jimmi Zion

Item 4 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 28 Feb 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
*[Fade In] *[Cut to the interior of a locker room where see WWA X-Treme and Cruiserweight champion, "Sky Flyin" Jimmi Zion sitting in a recliner watch the latest promos of the X-Corp on a TV moniter across from him, with the focus on his tag team partner, Mal Somers, who one week ago was his oponant on Fiesta in the Champion verses Champion best two out of three falls main event match. Now their partners going up against a common enemy with the Cell. Zion is dressed in normal non wrestling attire such as; black zipper warm-up pants from Invinciblewear.com, a tight shiney gold Gazooks rockstar t-shirt, white Nike tennis shoes, and red Oakley sunglasses. His long dreadlocks each individually braided. He has his two titles resting on his lap as he watches with intent interests. He glances over at the camera as if he's ready to respond, pausing the tape so he can speaklet's listen in.] "Sky Flyin" Jimmi Zion: "I can't believe that one week ago Mal Somers and I were fighting Mal Somers in a champion verses Champion match last week...yeah like you really want to call Mal a champion. They call him the WWA Rookie Champion, but can you honestly call Mal a rookie? He's almost been in this federation as long as I have now. I still don't understand this." [He unpauses the tape to show a scene from Somer's promo.] *(Scene – Mal Somers, WWA Rookie Champion and one half of the Extreme Corporation is sitting at home on his sofa, watching television. A bottle of Budweiser sits half consumed beside him. On the other side of the two seater sofa lies his Rookie Title, with a full bottle of beer next to it.) Somers – C’mon Rookie, you aren't thirsty today? Drink up! [Zion pauses the tape again to respond.] Zion: "Oh bloody come on here. This man is trying to feed his championship title belt a beer. Is this man insane? I have two titles, do you see me pouring alcaholic beverages all over it? No, because their bloody real? Just like his Rookie Title, it's just a title that they give everybody to make them feel important. Like that match last week, they should of just made it for my WWA X-Treme and Cruiserweight titles because it was not a champion verses champion match. It was a challenger verses champion match. He is not a champion. I am! You are not a champion Somers so quit thinking that you are." [He pauses while he calmly recollects his thoughts and realizes what he just said almost like he was subconsciously correcting himself.] Zion: "However, I gotta give credit to you sunshine, you did fight like a champion. In my book the Rookie champion may not look like a true championship title...for someone of your caliber, but you did fight me like a true champion. If it was for my titles, you could of beaten me. Keep in mind nutmunch, I said COULD of. If this was a title defense, I should be thankful for the Cell for interfering, but I'm not. I'm bloody pissed off. Now granted I really got their attention and pissed them off, but can you blame me? You better not. The Cell messed up when they laid a hand on my wife, I defended the honor by getting them back. They started this, but I pushed it as far as it will go. Then to make matters worse Antonio Cruz almost blinded me, but that didn't matter because I got my vision back just in time. Now we get to fight the Cell either once and for all or to see how much further we will get to push this." "I will say this Somers, you never once really pissed me off, but by the sound of your promos, it appears as if I did a pretty damn good job of pissing all over you...metaphorically speaking of course. However, I'm going to set my problems aside for one moment and side with you again our common enemy, the Cell." "Leviathan and Lockdown, each time when we appear like we could end this before it gets worse, we all find a way to push it even further. This will never end. Well mark my words, one way or the other I will make sure it ends. Even if it means the death of me, I will personally end this war between us once and for all. I will not lose to the likes of you. I do not fear you and you should know what lengths I will go to win this. I never lose. I am a winner, and I'm going to win. I never aim for defeat and this will not be a first. For one night only, I will go along with the rules of the WWA and side with some HELP in order to end this between us. I hate to even think of the thought that I have to have help in doing my work, but if it means beating you, then even I can make a small sacrifice...for now. I just hope that Somers doesn't make this a habit." "Somers anytime if you wanna pick up this match where we left off before The Cell interfered, then let me know and I will be more than ready and able to show you just why I am the WWA X-Treme and Cruiserweight champion. Until that time I will be your partner. I'm not in this business to make friends, only gain wins, titles, and fame. Catch my drift." "So in short, Mal in the future do not talk about my wife in the manner that you did." "Levaithan, Lockdown, don't even conceive of the thought of touching my wife ever again." "Thirdly, The Cell better get ready because Jimmi Zion is coming into Fiesta to beat the bloody hell out of your once and for all...oh yeah maybe I'll have Mal Somers who might want a little help to." [Interupting Zion, getting his attention, his wife and manager, Mrs. Mikalia Rush-Zion walks into the room. She is wearing a light gray Armani ladies business suit, black high heel pumps, her hair is down, and she seems like she is slightly preoccupied with lots on her mind. Zion gets up from his chair to greet her with a kiss and a hug.] Zion: "Hello love, where have you been?" Mikalia Rush-Zion: "I need to talk to you Jimmi, I need to sit down." Zion: "Sure love, what's this about?" [Zion escorts his wife to sit down on the leather sofa near his chair and then sits down right down next to her holding her hand.] Mikalia: "Well you know I went to the doctor's office last week. Well the results came back with what was going on with me." Zion: [With a genuinly concerned look on his face.] Mikalia: [Fighting back the tears but trying to show feelings of joy.] "I found out that I'm pregnant." Zion: ......... Mikalia: "You're gonna be a daddy Jimmi." Zion: "Me...a daddy...like as in little Jimmi Zions flying all over the world?" Mikalia: "Yeah." Zion: "I don't know what else to say other than thank you God, I'm gonna be a father. I have to admit that this is a little sudden because of everything that has happened to us." Mikalia: "Well I guess this means I have to wait and get my hands on Ms. Wise at a later date." Zion: "AH yeah love like you're gonna have to wait for at least nine months before you have to fight her." Mikalia: "Which also means that you're gonna have to quit messing with The Cell...it's for the both of us...er I mean all three of us." Zion: "Goddamn it. I knew that there was a catch, but then again it's a small price to pay, but anything for my wife and my child on the way." Mikalia: "So you're not not mad." Zion: "Of course not. This is the greatest news I've ever heard, and I thought nothing was better than my title win at Rage In a Cage. Nothing can be better than this. This is the greatest day of my life." Mikalia: "I'll have to tell daddy that he's gonna be a grandfather." Zion: "Amoung others." Mikalia: "Jimmi I love you." Zion: "I love you too." [Zion and Mikalia celebrate with a kiss, and then Zion bends down on Mikalia's stomach to listen to his unborn child as the camera fades out.] *[Fade Out]
