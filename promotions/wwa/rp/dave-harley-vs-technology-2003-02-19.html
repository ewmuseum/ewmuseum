Dave Harley vs Technology

19 Feb 2003

By Dave Harley

Item 10 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 19 Feb 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The scene opens with The Extreme Machine Dave Harley sat in front of his Home Computer. The lights are turned down low and the main source of illumination comes from the monitor in front of him. He types a word in and presses enter. The computer beeps in disapproval. He grunts and types in the word again. The computer again makes a disapproving beep. During this battle of wills, Dave Harley's PA Jill has entered the room and has sat down in an armchair opposite the computer. She watches as her employer struggles to maintain his composure against the artificial intelligence.] Jill: ...Something wrong? Harley: Oh...Hey Jill...just trying to amuse myself on this computer... Jill: Oh Mr Harley, not again. Not after your credit card bill last month... Harley: No! Not that! And I told you that wasn't me that was Mal! I've been playing Scrabble. Jill: By yourself? Harley: I've been playing against the computer. It doesn't cheat as often as Mal so it's easier to beat. [Dave looks at his letters on the screen and types in 'CRAXXT' and presses Enter. The computer beeps again.] Harley: What the f*ck is wrong with this thing?!! "'CRAXXT' is not a word"? Of course it's a word! I've got it written on my T-Shirt! Jill: So where is Mr Somers anyway? Harley: Bah, he's gotta prepare for his big two out of three falls match against Jimmi Zion this week. Jill: Shouldn't you be preparing for your match against Mac Maloy? Harley: Who, the Truckster? He still around? Relax Jill. Part of match preparation is mental. By playing Scrabble against a computer, I'm preparing myself mentally to go into battle against Mac Maloy. Hey, Mac MALoy. MAL-oy. MAL Somers. You think they're related? Jill: [sighs] I sincerely doubt it. Harley: Anyways, in many respects, this computer reminds me of Mac Maloy. It's out of date. It needs a lot of work to do anything meaningful. It has no personality. It can only do very basic tasks...there is one thing it does that Mac Maloy can't do. Jill: What's that? Harley: It plays a mean game of Scrabble. Jill: But Mac Maloy isn't the only person you should be worrying about. How about The Cell? Harley: The Tag Team Champs? Without Cruz they're nothing. Nobody is holding us back from Fiesta now. Cruz's ban has been lifted. The Extreme Corporation are free to kick ass. Hey, what's this? [He stands up and dances around in front of Jill.] Jill: Errrr... Harley: I'll give you a clue; it's in honour of our new Commissioner. Jill: ... Harley: A Raine Dance!!! HAHAHAHAHA!!! Jill: Maybe you should just stick to Scrabble. Harley: Maybe you're right. [sits back down] Why won't this damn thing accept 'CRAXXT'? ...Maybe it's spelt with three X's. [He types in 'CRAXXXT'. The computer beeps once again and a message pops up on the screen reading 'CRAXXXT is not a word either dumbass'.] Harley: WHAT?!!! Did you let Mal tamper with this program or something?!!! Jill: No. Harley: Wise-ass piece of trash! [He picks up the computer in a Harley Bomb-like maneuvre and drills the computer into the ground. It smashes into many pieces.] Harley: ...I'm gonna go find Mal. [Exits]
