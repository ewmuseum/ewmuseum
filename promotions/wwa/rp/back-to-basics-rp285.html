Back To Basics

24 Jul 2003

By Dave Harley

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The scene opens with Dave Harley in a darkened room. A shaft of light illuminates the GenX Title that he holds in his arms. His face is illuminated by the glistening gold.] Harley: Joey, you thought you could just waltz out on Meltdown and use K Moments to cost me my match. Well that may be true Joey. You cost me the TV Title, but I gained a new prize…the GenX Title! [Harley stands up and starts walking towards the camera.] Harley: Now this Monday on Fiesta I’m defending my new title against James Curzon. With you as the Special Guest Referee. I warn you now Joey, I don’t think you quite know what you’re getting yourself into. You think you’ve gotten inside my head. That may be true, but all you’ve done is change my priorities. All I wanted was to beat you and become the new TV Champion. Well I did that. But then you did something really stupid. You introduced me to this. [He holds up the GenX Title as he walks.] Harley: And by introducing me to this, you reintroduced me to Extreme. You see, you’re only problem was that I was gonna beat your ass. Now you not only have to worry about getting your ass beaten, but you have to worry about what state I’m gonna leave your ass in once I’ve finished. [Harley flicks on a lightswitch and the room is finally revealed. He is standing in his workshop surrounded by steel chairs, barbed wire, tables and Dumpsters.] Harley: The Extreme Machine is going back to his roots Joey. The question is will you survive? [Harley flashes his trademark grin as the scene fades to black.]
