WWA (1997–2019) Roleplays

Insight

23 Jan 2003

By Kevin Straves

Item 2 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 23 Jan 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene opens. A widescreen shot. Zoomed in at incredible detail is a pair of deep blue eyes. The irises are narrow as the big black pupils are the focal point of the shot. They blink once or twice, but the shot stays still.) (We hear a "Snip... then another "Snip", as a chunk of long dark hair falls across the eyes. We see a glint of metal as the object flashes across the screen. The eyes blink, and the pupils re-adjust to the light. A voice starts to talk as the "snipping" continues in the background) "Did I catch your attention yet? Did I catch your attention enough to merit more than one line in your next so called promo? Did you enjoy the ending of the match on Meltdown? Did you recognize me?" (More hair falls across the shot, as he blinks again) "Sure jack, I understand you’re a busy man. What with baby killing and everything..... I tell you something, I may hate Denrol, but you crossed a line. And as the 'Greatest of all earth time' Said, you can’t go back from it. Sure, he's given you the opportunity to apologize, but still. We all know you won’t take that chance. After all, you are the big man on campus now Moore has stepped aside, and you finally have a little spotlight." (More snipping, the eyes blink again, and this time we see a thumb enter the shot.) "After all, before he left, you were cast back in the shadows. You had your first run as champion, and then what happened? Moore returned and you were passed down the line, back to the basement you went. Back down the card. Moore eclipsed you Jack, and if your not careful, Denrol will eclipse you too." (We hear a buzzing sound, a sound which resembles hair clippers) "And if Denrol doesn't, then I will." (The shot cuts to see a barber clipping the mans hair from behind. We see a reflection of the mans head from the eyes up in the mirror, and he stares into the camera) "You've been getting your way for too long Jack. World Titles, bullying, scare tactics, baby killing, and joining the House Of Pain. The self push you seem to give yourself week after week is phenomenal. Forcing yourself down the fan's throats. You ought to be ashamed of yourself. You can't get over the traditional way. being a good wrestler, showing off your technical abilities..." (Pause) "Well, not that you have any technical abilities....." (Another pause. The barber moves out of the way now, and returns with some shaving cream. He rubs it in his hands and begins rubbing it over the short beard of the man known as "Kevin") "But when you get to Rage In The Cage, your brute strength and hatred for Denrol won’t be enough. Trapped inside a barb wire wrapped frozen steel cell against a man whose baby you may have killed. You think Denrol is going to show mercy after that? You think he'll hold back? Do you HONESTLY thing that what you did on Meltdown wouldn’t affect Denrol to the point where he would DESTROY you in that match for what you did to his wife? Any halfwit could see what you did was a dumb move. But you did it anyway. Another prime example of Jack displaying his power an unreserved ignorance of other people and there emotions. other people's families. Other people’s entire lives. other peoples destinies..." (The barber retracts a razor and begins shaving) "Destinies - a word I haven't used in a while. For nearly a year I've been hidden away in Iowa, trying to work out exactly what my destiny was. Trying to work out exactly what my purpose in life was. What were my dreams. What were my ambitions. What did I want to achieve. I've visited some of the best psychiatrists in the world to figure out exactly what went wrong with me. Where the hill started taking its downward slope. It drove me to drink. it drove me to drugs. It drove me to hating all the people that cared for me, and eventually, drove me to attempting suicide." (The shave continues. The man tilts his head to see the shave quality of the barber, but reveals no more of his face) "But the fact I failed to even kill myself properly made things even worse. The abuse against my body continued. My family disowned me. I went on a rampage. Stole from my old friends, robbed a convenience store, and turned into an egotistic loser who would do anything he could to be noticed. Whatever it took to look like I was frightening." (Pause) "Sound familiar Jack?" (The camera returns to the deep blue eyes) "And that Jackson is when it hit me. That is what drove me to recovery. I had become Jackson Kraven. A small minded asshole that would cheat lie and harass to prove a point. But that wasn't me. I could not be that man, and in one quick thought that entered my head whilst watching Meltdown through a TV store window, I was determined to be the man I was. The man everyone loved. The man that could take Jackson Kraven, give him the run for his money that NO ONE had ever given you, and yet still lose to you." (Blink. We hear a splash as some more fingers and thumbs enter the shot.) "I had to become that man again. In the hunt to become that man, I needed some support. My family disowned me. I phoned all my old WWA buddies - none of them were interested. I couldn't even afford a Meltdown ticket to get in to see the show from the auditorium, yet alone speak to some guys about help, but one night, someone saw me hanging around in the parking lot. I was sat down, face buried in my hands. I looked up and saw him. Not a man I knew - he was around before my time, but a man I recognized. He had been abused by the WWA, and turned into a jobber. A man who everyone laughed at. A man with no respect. We were in the same boat really, in different ways. he had the contacts, I had the knowledge to make him big. And so the road began.” (Pause) "What, you think I could plan something like this on my own? I needed insider information. your address, telephone number - WWA Headliners aren't listed in the yellow pages you know. But believe me Jack when I say that the road is over. I am trim. I am lean. I am a god damn wrestling machine again. I am back to being the man that nearly beat you oh so many times. But this time, I will be the man who WILL beat you. I'll be the man that put the legend of Jackson Kraven into the casket forever." (Pause) "Ashes to ashes, Dust to dust." (We return to the shot from behind. The camera backs away and we see the back of the man. The reflection still only shows his eyes upward, but the ragged hair has been styled. It's a transformation) "You can worry about Denrol for now, but sooner or later, your going to have to focus on me. 'Kevin Straves'. And believe me when I say, you should not have underestimated me thus far. And I will make you pay for my near destruction." (He takes out a cell phone and dials. he holds it to his ear and awaits an answer) "Hello, is that American Airlines Booking? Yes, I'd like a ticket to Calgary...." (As we fade to black.)
