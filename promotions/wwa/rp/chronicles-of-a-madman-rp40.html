Chronicles of a Madman

23 May 2003

By Enigma

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
{The scene begins in a dark dreary place. Nothing is able to be seen through the mess or smoke and darkness. A small light begins to flicker from the distance and it is barely able to be scene from the camera. As the scene pans over, we see a number of different lights be scene. They appear to be organized in a way of a triangle of lights. We get closer and we see that there is a picture between the lights as we come even closer we see a candle light vigil with a picture of Chance in the middle. Below the vigil is a black wreath. It has a sash on it and it reads “Fall From Grace”. Finally we are able to see very clearly and there is a man sitting in a chair just to the left of the vigil. Dressed in all black, but we can clearly tell that this is Enigma. He is sitting on his chair. The camera pans to him} “Beautiful isn’t it? Maybe a little confusing to the untrained eye, but there is nothing to be confused about. This is my friend Chance, a lost, misguided fool. Chance, you are the bringer of light, a man who brings hope to the world. You are a man who people wish to be like; you are going down the wrong road, like so many before you. I am the way out Chance. I am the only way that you can get out of this path to a life that no one wants to live. Chance you have very few choices. You can continue down your path, or you can change you course. You can make the right decision for once in your miserable life. I am the way Chance. I am the way to get away from the light. Joey K has seen the light, pardon the pun, and has joined me, now you must do the same or be ready for the wrath and fury that I can impose on you.” “Chance, I understand you are a tortured soul. A man who lost his parents so tragically, so early, no one can fathom what that has done to you. But I understand Chance. I can see it in your eyes that hate and evil lurk within your soul. You know how I can see that? Whether you agree but, you and I are the same. It’s true. You cannot deny that fact. I have felt your hatred numerous times. You have made me bleed before; you have made others feel great pain. You are dangerous, ruthless, and I love it. I am seeing what you are doing to X Corp, showing you dark side.” “Chance, you need to feed that dark side. Chance, everyone has the black spot in their heart and they can deny it all they want, but it’s there. Chance, your heart is black. No matter how hard you try you cannot conceal it.” “Chance, the choice is simple. Join me, or you shall be destroyed. Joey K was not the only man on my side, there are others and believe me you would be wise to join. Why?” Because… Nothing…and I mean…Nothing Can Stop Us… {With that Enigma begins to blow out the candles one by one and as he is able ½ way done the scene fades to ?}
