Fiesta

30 Dec 2002

By Mal Somers

Item 1 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 30 Dec 2002, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Scene – Mal Somers sits alone at his desk, sorting through his mail.) Somers – Bill, bill..junk mail..bill..hmmm whats this? A Christmas card? (Somers opens up the envelope and several torn pieces of Christmas card fall to the desk.) Somers – Damn you Harley! I was just trying to clear the slate..start afresh..oh well. If Harley hates Leviathan half as much as I do, then we should hopefully work well as a team this Saturday at Fiesta. And then throw a bit of Lockdown into the mix and I’m pretty thankful that I’ll have Harley in my corner watching my back...If you know where I’m coming from. Leviathan. Lockdown. Both of them are sick S.O.B.s. (throws mail into his top drawer and then rests his head on his hands) Somers – I mean the last time Lockdown was involved in a tag team was with the other inmates at Cell Block Five! Man I crack myself up! Anyways, Lockdown...Leviathan...the L & L Connection..you’ve both seen me take out my frustrations on Wes Sanders at Seasons Beatings. You’ve seen what I am capable of against the major players of the WWA. And I didn’t even have anything personal against Wes. Leviathan, you especially, you know how personal this has become. And now with all the sneak attacks you and that Ho Ho Ho Miss Wise have been pulling...its just making me madder and madder. And that just makes me BADDER and BADDER. (Somers smiles) Somers – So, this Saturday boys...you losers in one corner, and me, Harley...and all the MALcoholics out there on our side...well, it looks like 2003 is going to be the shortest year of your lives! Business is picking up...CHAIRMAN Style! (Fade Out)
