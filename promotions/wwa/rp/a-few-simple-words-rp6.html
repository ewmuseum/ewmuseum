A few simple words

13 May 2003

By myZery

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I stand outside the huge hotel. The rain drips down my forehead onto my face. The light wind blows the rain in all directions. I watch as people rush in and out of cars and buildings trying not to get wet. It is amusing to see that people think that if they run up and down the streets they wont get wet. I watch as a young blond lady rushes out of a cab and she rushes straight thru a puddle, soaking her tight skirt. I try to ignore her stupidity and continue towards the huge chrome doors of the hotel. I open the doors to reveal the five star interior of the place I am staying. The fountain in the middle of the lobby sprinkles water up high into the air. I look around to see wealthy people flashing their money around. I nearly get sick to see an old grey and wrinkled man, with a young attractive model on his lap. Does he not know the term, gold digger? I slowly walk towards the long and winding marble stairs until a bellboy interrupts me. ‘Can I help you sir?’ says the nervous young bellboy. ‘No its ok, thanks though.’ I reply. I look down to see the hand of the young man is shaking and his lip is trembling. I am quite confused at this point and look around to see that everyone in the lobby is staring right at me. ‘What is wrong with you? Are you ok?’ I say to the shaking boy. ‘Em nothing eh sir. I know this is against the rules here and all but, can I’ve your autograph myZery!’ I smirk and reply, ‘Yeh, here ya go. So you’re a fan then!?’ ‘Yes I love the WWA, have been a fan four years.’ ‘That is great to hear. Anyways I better get going I don’t think they are taking kindly to a man wearing a long black leather coat and white face paint.’ I turn and proceed up the stairs. Taking step by step. The water drips from my long dripping hair to the marble steps. My boots make a loud noise as they hit the marble steps. I finally get to the top of the stairs and begin to walk down the corridor. I reach my suite, number thirteen and proceed into he room. I head straight for the coach and fall right onto it. I hear giggles coming from the bedroom and then a ladies voice begins to speak. ‘myZery come to bed, we got a surprise for you!’ ‘Yeh come on babe! Come to bed.’ As soon as I heard what the two women said I leapt up from the coach and rushed toward the bedroom. I slowly open the bedroom door to see two absolutely gorgeous women standing on my bed, only wearing a bikini of cream. ‘Hey babes! How are you two?’ I say with a sound of pure delight in my tone. ‘We’re fine, but we missed you!’ say the two practically naked model women. ‘So what we doing now?’ I say already half knowing what their plan was. ‘Well how do I put it?’ says the beautiful blond woman. The two women kiss and then the brunet turns to me and says, ‘You like cream?’ I slam the door shut. Scene fades. I lie in bed. I am so exhausted from all the ‘activities’ I have been doing. I look to the left to see Hailey, who is so fine. She looks like a model; in fact I think she is. She has her arms wrapped around the whole left side of my body. Her brown hair rubs up against my face. I scratch my face and she begins to stir. I then look to the right of me to see Jane, who is Hailey’s best friend. I think to myself could life be better. Hailey wakes up and looks up to me, ‘Hey. Your awake. What are you doing now?’ ‘I’ve got things to do honey, but I will be back later.’ I kiss Hailey and then kiss Jane on the forehead. I slowly maneuver out of the bed and get dressed. A few minutes later I emerge from the bedroom and walk towards the door. I grab my training bag on the way out. I walk out of my room and slowly shut the door behind me making sure not to wake Jane. I then begin to walk towards the stairs that leads into the lobby. I leave the hotel and get into my 1.8l injected Mini Cooper. It has all the extras including two-bucket seats, sports suspension; five spoke alloys, a sunroof, sport stripes and one hell of a cd player. I rev the car up as the bore exhaust makes a sound like a monster truck. I take my foot off the clutch and leave burn off marks behind me outside the hotel. A few minutes passed and then I park up my new mini outside the Ammunation store in town. I turn the ignition off as the car turns to a silent nothing. I slowly open the door and step out of the sports cooper. I look around to see an almost deserted street. I shut the door, and lock it with the zapper on my set of keys. I slowly but steadily pace towards the door of Ammunation. I enter the door to see huge selections of handguns, riffles. You name if they have it. I walked up to the man behind the counter with my hands closed tightly. ‘Excuse me. Where’s the shooting range?’ I looked at the man as he loudly chewed on his chewing gum. ‘Em… Over there.’ Said the obviously uneducated man. I couldn’t get over the fact that they put a guy like that in charge of so many powerful weapons. It puzzled me intensely. I proceeded onto the shooting range where there was a young man with an AK47 assault rifle blowing the living hell out of a few pieces of cardboard. I was shocked to see such a young man have so much anger. I was able to control my anger with means of medication but this option obviously worked for him. I loaded up my Venom self-revolver handgun and started to blow the cardboard men away with sturdy shots to the head and midsection. I blew up one by one until they stopped coming, I then got bored and started aiming for marks on the wall. A little old lady had entered the range at this point, she had a double barrel shotgun in her hand and she began to shoot all the cardboard cut out men she could see. She then began screaming ‘SEXIST PIG!’. I was pushed into a fit of laughter but that didn’t go down to well with the little old lady who knew how to use her handbag. A few minutes passed and all I could think about was the opportunity I have of winning the Gen X title at Fiesta. I have to face Showtime. I didn’t know what to think about him. The FFWA kicked him out for being the worst wrestler in their history, but then again if he wasn’t good how could he be a champion at the WWA? I thought about the possibilities that could arise for me if I became the Gen X champion; it would only be the start of my rein. There was no way I was going down without a fight. And to prove that to Showtime I think I should show this little demonstration of what will happen to him when he crosses me! I then take a cardboard cut out of Showtime in his tuxedo and hang it up in the firing range. I take my handgun and begin to blow the cut out to smithereens. I feel a pure taste of adrenaline all over me, my nerves are flying high and I couldn’t be any tenser. I walk over to the left over of Showtime’s cut out. ‘Showtime, can you see that? That is what I am going to do to you at Fiesta. I will have no remorse or pity for you, as soon as I get you in the squared circle YOUR MINE! I am over the moon about getting a chance to win a title here at WWA, and I know that I am not going to disappoint my boss by losing to a lame wrestler like you. I have put up with cocky, arrogant morons all my life and I know that you are no different. So Showtime you can get you little dick boy and shove it up your ass! After I am done with you it won’t matter where you are or who you are, I am going to give you such a beating you will never ever forget it. I trained in Japan for a long time and learned how to cope with pain, but you my friend are just a one-win man. You seem to win once and then lose, just like you lost the tag titles in the FFWA. You are a failure as a wrestler and a bad asset to this company. I honestly cannot understand how they signed you in the first place. You finisher has to be one of the worst aerial moves I have ever come across, if I remember correctly it looks like a messed up frog splash. Yes that’s right, you look like a fish out of water whilst doing it. Anyways just wanted to get my point across to you that I am not to be taken lightly and after Fiesta when I am the one with the Gen X title on my shoulder, you will realise this. But Fiesta isnt your only worry, remember… pain is fleeting, myZery lasts an eternity.’ The scene fades. Design and Graphics © World Wrestling Alliance 2002
