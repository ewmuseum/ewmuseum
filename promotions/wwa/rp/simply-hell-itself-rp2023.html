Simply, Hell Itself

2 Dec 2004

By Hell Itself

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The hospital is crawling with men and women all around. Outside of the windows in the waiting room, you can see that the sky has began to take on a shade of dark grey. The people inside are coughing, weezing, sneezing and look as if they are about to pass away. On the other end, the receptionist is answering phone calls left and right, trying to keep everything organized in the midst of the chaos. Nurses keep pouring into the place behind the receptionist, checking files, taking files, and reading them aloud in attempt to memorize what is on them. An older woman up against her steel walker makes her way to the counter, "When will I be able to see the doctor miss? I've been waiting here for so long now..." "I'm sorry, but were greatly under staffed tonight, and the doctor is busy with another patient right now but as soon as he's available, I'll call for you mam." "Thank you mam, you're very kind." The receptionist just smiles back as the woman heads back to the rows of blue plastic chairs along the wall. From the patient area, a nurse emerges and is walking away, as the receptionist calls her over, "Honey, please stay another hour, we really need you tonight." "I've been working overtime for three weeks now and I just need some rest tonight, I'm sorry." The nurse continues walking down the tiled hallway, as the receptionist sighs that she has lost another worker to aid with the incoming patients. The people in the waiting area are becoming restless, talking about how the state of hospitals now are horendous and all the workers there are lazy. The receptionist behind the counter continues going through papers on her desk and mutters to herself after hearing the people, "If they only knew what we go through for them..." Then, the door leading into the waiting area slowly creeks open, and all the people waiting cringe their heads to see who the newcomer is out of pure curiosity of human nature. The door has one fragile, trembling hand on the handle as the door continues to push open, and when it does, it reveals a tall brunette in a flannel jacket and blue jeans who has her left hand over her stomach, pregnant. She finally gets through the doorway and is breathing heavily with each step, with nobody around who will help her. Before the receptionist can even notice her, she stops walking and a glazed look appears on her eyes, then she drops to the floor with a smack, which cathches the receptionist attention. "Oh my God!" The receptionist dissapears into the back hallway, then opens the door into the waiting area and rushes to the aid of the woman. She crouches down to her knees, and calls for another nurse to come from the back to help her pick the woman up to get her to a doctor. "Judy! Shonda! Anybody, come quick!" From the back, a man comes running in his scrub and helps her lift the woman off of the tile as he speaks to the receptionist while they bring her to the back, "When did she come in?" "She just appeared out of nowhere!" "So who is this?" "I told you, she just came out of the blue and I haven't gotten any information from her yet!" The pregnant lady seems to be nearly unconscious as they bring her to a room where a doctor is tending to a little child, "So what did we learn today?" "Paste is bad for you!" The receptionist yells out to the doctor, "We need you now!" The doctor ushers the little child out of the room as fast as he can, then helps the woman onto the table, "Go get doctor katzenmyer, he's an OBGYN!" "Sir, this woman looks like she has to deliver," the receptionist replies. "But," "But nothing! You have to take this baby now or I will have to do it for you, you big wuss!" The doctor stands aside and motions for the receptionist to do what she said she would do. The receptionist sighs angrily, and goes to hold the woman's hand as the other nurse goes to the front ready to take the child, and he asks the receptionist, "What do I do Kayla?" "Just, like, catch it!" Kayla begins to lean over the woman telling her to push with everything she has left in her, the woman moans with what little energy she has left, as the sky outside begins to rain, "Come on baby you can do it!" After a momentary pause, you can hear the wailing of a newborn child, "Alright, let's get this baby down to the nursery asap, they'll know what to do!" The nurse and receptionist Kayla head out into the waiting room, then are seen running down the hallway then dissapear behind a wall, leaving the woman alone in the room. Kayla and the nurse are shown heading down to the maternity ward, and rush by the receptionist down there and head down to the break room where a doctor by the name of Katzenmyer is standing, "Woah, I didn't deliver this kid!" "Yeah, we know." Kayla responds, "But we need you to take care of it, we weren't trained for it." "Sure thing, I'll have it ready for it's mother in a matter of minutes." Kayla and the nurse walk casually back to there ward of the hospital, and go to check on the new mother, "Huh?" The nurse asks, He asks that, because the woman is gone. "She left him..." "Oh my..." Kayla covers her mouth with her left hand and has her right on her side and she begins to tear up. ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ The van was screaming with children, running about in the short aisle. They have planes and other various toys in their hands as they continue to be obnoxiously loud, there seems to be about seven of them. There is a woman at the front of the van, gut against the steering wheel pointing her finger into the back, yelling at the children, "I told you this last time, I will not have you kids being loud anymore!" The children pay her no mind as they continue running about and jumping on the seats out of their seatbelts. The obese woman driving the van turns back around to the road and begins to talk to herself with a face showing sadness and pity, "I wish they didn't have to transfer, but our home is just to full, I am going to miss them." One of the kids is especially excitedly giddy with a jet fighter in his right hand as he pretends to fly it around making noises, "Woooooshhhhh, woooooooooshhhhhh" The kid keeps hopping over the front two rows of the van back and forth, until he gets bored and decides to hope into the back row, where another child is sitting in his seatbelt, silent as he watches outside the window. "Watcha doing??" The other kid turns his head to him and responds, "What does it look like I am doing?" "Loooking outda winnndow!" "Right. Now go away." The happy child looks on confused as to why the other kid won't play with him, so he does what all younger children do, and begins to query the other child even further. "So what are you looking at that is outside?" "Nothing." "Then why are you doing it? It seems silly to me!" "I don't know, just leave me alone." The kid looking out of the window looks like he is angry about something other than the child, "Want to play with my super cool jet!?" The child turns his face away from the window and he looks angrily at the child, and after a pause of silence, he yells out, "JUST LEAVE ME ALONE!" All the kids in the van then stop doing what they were doing, and turn around to see what caused the outburst in a frightened manner. The lady driving the van turns around and screams to the two kids in the back, "WHAT DID I TELL YOU ABOUT YELLING!" And the instant she turns around, you can feel the van slightly turn to the right of the road as she continues her scolding, "YELLING DOESN'T MAKE THINGS BETTER!" Then, a sudden force takes the van off the road, the woman rolled straight off the road. The van continues tumbling and tumbling as all the children being to fly and smack against the windows, blood splattering everywhere with every child that hits a door or window, except for the child in the back who had his seatbelt on, but he is yelling out of fear at the top of his lungs. The van keeps flipping for what seems an enternity, until it comes to a rest on its back. The child in his seatbelt seems shaken, and is bruised on the shoulders, but manages to squirm out of his seatbelt, and he opens the back latch of the van and crawls out. He keeps crawling on his belly, and peers into the side windows of the van. It is a sickening sight, as the bodies of dead children are on the floor, with lifeless glares into nothingness that blends in with the scary silence. The child that survived the crash is staring at the kids, until he hears a sound on the other side of the van. He hurries, and hits the ground again to see the source of the noise, only to find out, that it is only blood dropping from the top side of the van hitting the vinyl seats. The stays there on his belly for a while, not knowing what to think of the carnage, until he starts to walk away. He keeps heading up the side of the road, until he hears the voice of the woman calling out to him, "Please....help me...." The child turns around at the van, and stares at the woman who is cut on the forehead badly...then turns his back away from the crash site. ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- The barn is casting a beautfiful shadow against the browning grass of the plains. The wind is dancing throughout the wheat field playfully, as the sun is starting to set and dissapear behind the horizon. A feeling of chill begins to set around the area, like a curtain of invisible ice. There is a long, colonial style home around the barn, wooden porch painted green with white railings along it. Central is the door, and there are two large windows of both sides of it. On the porch are a few rocking chairs, moving in rhythm with the wind. Out in the distance, you can hear the sound of a vehicle approaching as it shatters the peacefulness. Over one of the many hills, an ATV appears with two men riding. They pull into a path that connects the barn and the house. One older man steps off of his ATV, and a younger male around his teens hops off the back. "You know kid, I am glad you came across my home. You're like a son I never had." "It's been a pleasure living here with you sir, all the food and shelter a guy could ask for." The teenager responds. The two men walk towards the house with their hands in the pockets of their blue jeans trying to keep warm from the chill. The teenager walks up the steps on to the porch, and opens the screened in front door for the older man who nods his head to him in thanks. They both walk inside, and take off their jackets then set them on a coatrack in the corner of the roomy kitchen. "So Mr. Jones, what are we having tonight?" Mr. Jones just turns around and smiles with a somewhat wicked grin on his face, "I don't know yet, I suppose we will have to get something from the meat locker tonight because there is nothing I want to eat in the pantry." The teenager looks on curiously as he speaks while the man fixes himself a cup of coffee, "I didn't know you had a meat locker." "Really? Well I guess I must show it to you, it's quite nice." The older man sets his cup of coffee on the counter, and motions for the teenager to join him outside. They both put on their coats again, as they venture into the night towards the back of the house through the tall grass. "Hmm, I always thought this was a shelter if a tornado was to ever come." The teenager states. The older man begins to pull a key out of his pocket, and he speaks as he unlocks the keypad to what looks very similar to a wine cellar. "Haha, that's inncorrect though sonny." The man unlocks the doors, and reaches the handles about to pull them open, until he stops and stands up, as the teenager has a somewhat confused look upon his face. "Now son, you have to promise me you won't tell anybody what I've got down here." "Sure sir, I won't tell any animal activists that you have some dead cow in your freezer!" The two men laugh as the older one pats the shoulder of the teenager as they head into the cellar. The room is dark and damp, as the ground squishes with each step the men take, until you hear a chain being pulled and a central light turns on, illuminating the room. It is completely barren, except a steel meat locker in the far corner. The men venture over there, and the older one struggles to open the steel handle, but manages to get it open. The teenager is standing behind the older man as he is searching for some ground beef, and he notices an abnormally large frozen structures in the corner. "Mr. Jones...what is that?" Mr. Jones looks up, and there are two frozen bodies in the corner, completely iced over. They have their eyelids sown together, and their lips. You can see the signs of a fight on their bodies as there are multiple cut wounds, and even the outline of frozen blood on their necks. "Son, those are tresspassers! They snuck on to my property!" The old man laughs sadistically. The teenager backs out of the meat locker slowly, petrified as the man continues to search for some food. "What's wrong kiddo?" The teen simply watches on, as he grabs the steel door handle and locks the insane man in. There is a window in the steel door, and the old man screams on, "Hey! Come on, now open up that door. I know you came on to my property to, but I swear I wasn't going to do it to you! Now just open the door and we'll pretend this never happened." The teenager is scared out of his mind, but simply shakes his head no, then proceeds to walk out of the cellar, then turns the light out as the old man screams, "PLEASE COME BACK! I am sorry! I didn't mean to kill them, heck, they made me! Yeah that's it, they made me!" The cellar doors close with an eerie and slow squeak, leaving the cellar cut off from the rest of the world in darkness. ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- The club is bouncing with the tones of heavy metal all around with the sounds of guitars and singing. Outside, there are amazing amounts of people waiting outside in the dark. Most of the people are dressed in black jeans with chains everywhere, and leather jackets with an equal amount, or even more chains. There hair is black as well, some with facial piercings some without. The flow of people is going smoothly. At the front of the line, there is a man around his twenties, he is obviously the bouncer. He is checking people as they go into the establishment, "Alright dude, you can go in." The man who enters the building flashes the rock on hand signal, then the man closes the door. The flow of the line is stopped, as the rockers are in line talking to each other waiting to get in. One of the men at the front of the door however, can stand to wait in line evidently. "Come on, just let me in already." The man begs to the bouncer. "I'm sorry man, I can only let one person in every three minutes." "What kind of fuc*ing rule is that?" "The kind that my job makes me follow." The bouncer laughs. The angry person in line begins to pick at the bouncer. "You ain't so tough," as he touches the bouncers jacket, messing with his arms. This is obviously a foolish mistake, because the young bouncer seems fairly built, standing around six feet tall with arms that could kill. He pays the man no mind, until he pushes with one arm. "Watcha gonna do you little bitch!?" The bouncer seems calm and collected, until he reaches over and grabs the man by the neck with both hands, and slams him on to the cold concrete below. All the people in line are astonished at the strength of the man, then out of the line comes rushing another man, slightly larger than the bouncer. The bouncer cocks back ready to fight, until the man speaks, "Woah man, I just saw what you did, and that was amazing." "Thanks..." the young man replies, "Kid, have you ever thought about becoming a wrestler?" "No, no I haven't." "Well are you happy being a bouncer?" "No, no I'm not." "Then just hear me out for a minute." The man puts his arm around the young bouncer as both walk off, leaving the club to be rushed in by the tons of people who were in line. ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- There is a tall muscular man sitting down on a chair that is set against a black curtain. He looks very, very similar to the child, the teenager, and the younger man. He is talking into a cell phone, crunched over looking nervous, "Yes, I'm sure I am ready. You've trained me for this moment for years, for that I thank you." On the other end of the line, you can hear the person he is talking to, "Yea, yea I did. Just don't let me down. Make them feel your suffering. Are you sure you are ready to give them everything you've got, everything I've taught you?" You hear a sudden noise from behind the black curtain, as the man speaks before hanging up the phone, "Hey, I've got to go. Wish me luck." The phone beeps, as he hangs up then you see the man walk out of sight not knowing where he has gone, until, Trudeau: Ladies and gentlemen, coming down to the ring weighing in at 266 pounds, HELL ITSELF!
