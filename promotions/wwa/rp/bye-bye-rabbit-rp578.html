Bye-Bye Rabbit

11 Oct 2003

By "Bonafide" James Curzon

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(James Curzon is sat in a dark room. Everything is covered in dust, even him. There is a knock at the door and the bulk of Dead Rabbit enters.) JC: Good evening! DR: Howdy. (Dead Rabbit pulls up a chair and sits down.) JC: Don't sit on that! (Dead Rabbit pushes the chair away.) DR: Why not? Is it booby trapped? JC: No. I just want you to stand up. DR: So, like, why did you want to see me? JC: To fire you? DR: To fire me?! JC: Yes, to fire you! (Dead Rabbit looks stunned, his mouth wide open in shock.) JC: You see when you said you had WWA experience I though that was a good and you a big tough guy which I also thought was a good thing. This makes it all ther harder to let you go. (Dead Rabbit looks more stunned, his mouth even wider in shock.) JC: I'm sorry it had to be this way but this relationship just doesn't give me the buzz it used to. (Dead Rabbit starts drooling because his mouth is so wide open.) JC: So you must leave, goodbye! DR: But..but..why? (Bonafide goes over and opens the door.) DR: Is there someone else? JC: No! It's just not working out. DR: I don't understand, why didn't you say anything before? JC: Listen, Rabbbit, it's not you, it's me. I'm sorry. (Rabbit bursts into tears.) JC: Oh, don't cry! I'm sorry I just think we need some time apart. DR: You just want to go off and spend time with other sidekicks! JC: No, of course not. I just think that we've got a little stale and need a break from each other. A long break. DR: How long? JC: Pretty long. Forever. (Rabbit wipes the tears away.) DR: But I love you! JC: No, you don't, I'm sure you'll find someone else soon, PLenty more fish in the sea. (Dead Rabbit bursts into tears again.) JC: What now? DR: I can't swim! (Bonafide checks his watch.) JC: Anyway you'd better go, start fishing! Come on, hurry along now! (Bonafide pushes Dead Rabbit towards the door. At the same time an Asian man walks in.) Man: Hello, Mr Curzon. DR: Who's this guy? Man: I am The Gurkha, Mr Curzon's assitant and loyal retainer. DR: Oh james! You saud there was no-one else! Were you seeing him behind my back? JC: No, of course not! (A Pause) Well, only once but it meant nothing. (They stop and think a moment.) JC: OK, OK, the plotting with him was much better than it was with you, it was just more exciting! I'm sorry, Rabbit, goodbye. DR: I'M GOING TO KILL YOU!!! JC: AAAAAARRRGGGHHHHHH! (Curzon leaps away and runs out of the room. Rabbit starts to give chase but The Gurkha whacks him with a crate. Knocking him out. The Asian man rolls the fat man out of the room and Curzon returns.) JC: Oh, very good job. TG: All my work is good. JC: This could be the beginning of a beautiful friendship! (Fade out.)
