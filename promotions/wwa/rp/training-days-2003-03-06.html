Training Days

6 Mar 2003

By Brothers DeLucci

Item 3 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 6 Mar 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
..::The scene opens up to a isolated room with a wrestling ring in the middle, its old, the flooring is shabby and looks a little torn in one corner and the ropes hang loosely from not being tightend up at the corner posts::.. ..::We see the DeLucci two arms folded watching as two other men go at it at their feet, they look at the men closely twisting their bodies to get a better view of the situations, both men break look up at Tommy and Tony who look at each other::.. Tommy: ‘Ey I’s thought you said this wrestling’s was easy? Trainer: It is we’ve only been practicing the basics for the last two day with you guys, we have been wrestling for years now Tony: But er… ..::Tommy slaps Tony round the back of the head quickly and sharply. Tony flinches in pain and rubs the back of his bald head::.. Tommy: Shudd’up you, you’s not the mastermind behind this here project Trainer: So you ready to give the arm drag another quick go ..::Tony shuffles up and whips the trainer into the corner, the trainer bumbles out and Tommy locks the arm and flips the trainer over, he keeps the momentum going by floating over and locking in a chin lock. The trainer claps his hands and shouts good::.. Trainer: Very good Tony, Tommy you want to try now? You ready for this? Tommy: You’s kidding me? I’s was born ready, ‘ey you’s, don’t go easy on me yeah? ..::The trainer nods and smiles, he looks at the other trainer and winks at him. Tommy grabs a hold of the trainers arm and attempts to whip him across the ring. The trainer however at the last second has a rush of strength and reverses at the last possible second. Tommy twists and takes the force of the turnbuckle. He stumbles out and the trainer hooks him up in a front face lock. He then takes Tommy over with a snap suplex both trainers laughs::.. Trainer: Not to go easy on you eh Tommy? ..::Tommy just lay flat on the floor his eyes staring up at the ceiling, he breaths in and out heavily trying to grasp every breath like it was it last. He finally gets to his feet::.. Tommy: Look’s I knew what I was doing. He just surprised me that’s all. Trainer: Maybe so but when your match comes up there not going to be so nice to you as we are. Tommy: Look’s me and Tony can’s handle ourselves in the ring, we’s just needs you to gives us some extra skills. Trainer: Were trying our best here but you’ve given us very little time. Also the resources you have are, well… Very limited… Tommy: Look’s were’s not made of money were getting into wrestling so we’s can get some money to pay for better things. Look’s stick with us and we’ll take’s you places. Trainer: Look if this is some way of telling us were not getting paid were out of here. Tommy: No’s you’s misunderstand’s me, you’ll get’s your money. All I’m saying is you teach us everything you know, we’ll take on the WWA and. All 4 of us we’ll reap the rewards. Trainer: You sure my partner and me shouldn’t just go into the match and win it for you. It would be a lot quicker and easier Tommy: You’s kidding me, beating up people is our speciality aint it Tony ..::Tony is about to speak when Tommy slaps him round the back of the head::.. Tommy: ‘Ey Shuddup your face, looks guys me’s and Tony will sort out this Showtime and James Curzon on Saturday. We’s knows exactly what your doing Trainer: You sure? Ok well for the rest of this afternoon well get you through some submission holds and grasps that should hold us up for the rest of the day. You guys want to take five. ..::Tony grasps his trainer in a simple collar lockup, Tony the applies a simple headlock and twists it into a waistlock. From there he rolls up his training partner in a schoolboy::.. Trainer: Tommy you want to continue or do you want to take a break Tommy: I’ m going to take a breather’s and watch my idiot of a brother get beaten to a pulp ..::Tommy and his training partner leave the ring watching Tony and his training partner doing some mat wrestling. Tony is in a scissor leg hold and is being instructed the best way to counter such a hold. The trainer turns to Tommy::.. Trainer: So who are these James Curzon and Showtime? Tommy: Two bum’s me and Tony have to take out, I’s thinks they are nobody important but I haven’t done my research on them. Trainer: Don’t you think you should. I mean you got to know what attacks they might produce, I mean you’ve been working with us now for 2 days and your learning our skills and attacking motions. Your going to need to know your opponents and work them out. Get a feel for them. Tommy: Get’s a feel for them eh? Trainer: Yeah find out their weaknesses and strengths. A bit of a scouting mission. It will certainly help you in defeating your opponent. ..::Tommy scratches his rough chin for a moment, his fingers brush gently over the rough and stiff bristles emerging from his chin.::.. Tommy: Ok, supposed I say’s goes to these Showtime and James’ Curzon’s you think’s that will help me defeat them? Trainer: Totally, I don’t see why it would do any harm to your match Tommy: Ok’s I wills then. Tony’s you stay here’s practicing. I’m going’s to see our opponents. And see’s what were up against. Tony: Ok’s Tommy Tommy: Shuddup! I didn’t say’s you could talk! You just stay here and practice! ..::Toom puts on his shirt and does up his dark navy blue tie, before slipping on his jacket, he ruffles his short hair and exits out of the door::.. Trainer: Right now that Tommy is out of the way were going to show you some power moves Tony. You seem like you got the right kind of build and stature to pull off some good things. Tony: You’s think I have? Trainer: Of course but we’ve probably only got a couple of hours before Tommy comes back. So lets get going on this the first major power move we are going to learn is… The Powerbomb Tony: Oh, that’s sounds good Trainer: It is, deadly, powerful and can quite easily end the match. Here is how it looks ..::Tony looks closely as the two trainers lock up. One does a mock knee to the stomach and then quickly whips the trainer up onto his shoulders. Holding steady the trainer drops the other on his neck gently::.. ..::Tony’s face fills with concentration perform the move time and time again. He watches closely, examining every possibility. Tony himself then gives the move a go. He is gentle at first taking his time in every aspect. The trainers liked Tony, he was supple, his brain seemed to absorb every word and danger they said::.. ..::The scene slowly fades out on Tony then practicing the move further and further. The scene finally darkens with a final shudder of the ring. Tony was almost ready::..
