"Giving Thanks and Re-united at last"

2 Jan 2007

By True Living Colors

Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene opens inside a large cemetary in St. Louis, Missouri as a 2006 black Cadilliac Escalade pulls in through the front entrance and drives down the road. Its a cloudless sunny sky very surprising for winter in Missouri, but not always common as sunny winter days are not uncommon. The birds are chirping on this early January 2007 afternoon. The Escalade comes to a full, complete stop shutting off its engine. The tinted doors open as as the current reigning WWA World Tag Team Champions and new number one contender for the WWA World Heavyweight Championship title, Jevon White and David Black of True Living Colors.) (Jevon White, is wearing a pure white Armani suit with a matching white overcoat, white shoes, and white Al Capone/mobster like hat on, and white Oakley sunglasses His bleached blond dreadlocks are individually braided and flowing down past his back but wrapped in a ponytail. Jevon is carrying a three red roses each one to signify him, his wife, and his daughter.) (David Black is wearing black leather pants, a tight black t-shirt, black leather jacket, black steel-toed boots, and black tinted sunglasses. His head is shaved all shiney and clean and his trademark gotee is trimmed. David is carrying a brown paper bag of what appears to be a wine bottle of somekind.) (They walk toward the familer grave site area of where Jevon's wife Lillian and daughter are buried.) David Black: You sure we got time to make a detour like this. You do know that we have to be in D.C. for Fiesta to take on Kade Williams and Richard Cranium on Fiesta? Jevon White: I always make time for my baby. Don't worry I won't be long. Black: Well that's good. Hate to be late. White: Me too. (They approach the gravesite of Lillian Bethany White. Jevon unsheethes the three roses from their plastic covering order to lay them onto the mound, but as he does Jevon stops as he gazes upon the top mound underneath her tombstone and immediately sees beams down at the sight with rage in his eyes. Jevon can only look with silent, pent up, rage and anger building within him as he begins squeezing the stems of the roses to the point that his hands grip the thorns of the stems. Small drippings of blood begin to fall from Jevon's tightly gripped fist down onto the blue flowers on the mound that have spelled the following word in an accronym like fashion.) W.H.O.R.E. (David Black looks down at the sight and sees the pretty blue flowers freshly stained with small drippings of Jevon's blood. Jevon begins kicking off the flowers from the mound of dirt. The blue flowers scatter away off in the cool breezy wind. Jevon bends down and angrily begins picking out and throwing the rest of the flowers away from the mound that he couldn't kick away trying to create room for his three roses to lay in their place.) White: (Muttering outloud to himself.) Ballard. F(FCCbeep)king Ballard. Does your disrespect know any bounds. (David Black walks over and catches one of the blue flowers trying to blow away in the breeze. he picks it up and looks at it curiousity. He then brings it to his nose to sniff it and take in its refreshing scent. He smiles as he removes it looking at it stained with a drop of Jevon's blood.) Black: (Singing out loud to himself a slowed down version of The Rolling Stones song "Time Is On My Side".) Time is on my side. Oh yes it is. Time is on my side. Oh yes it is. White: What did you say Dave? Black: Oh nothing, just thinking to myself. (Jevon contines with what he is doing by clearing off the last of the flowers from the mount and then laying the three flowers neatly on the mound. He lowers his head and closes his eyes for a moment taking a moment of silence in in a prayer like fashion before standing up again and looking down. He turns his attention to David.) White: Hey Dave, hand me that bottle of wine will ya. Black: Sure thing J. (Black hands Jevon the wine bottle in the brown bag. Jevon takes it out and pops the cork. He holds it into the air. David holds the bloodstained blue flower into the air in a toast like fashion.) White: We did it baby. You did it. I said that I no matter what, I wasn't gonna go out like a b(FCCbeep)tch. Here's to the angels on my shoulder and may you always smile down on me even when I do wrong. (Jevon takes a long big drink from the five year old bottle of White Zinfidale, then passes it over to David so he can take a drink from it before handing back to Jevon. David crushes the blue flower in his hand with ease. Jevon takes the remaining amounts of the wife and pours it onto the mound. He sets the now empty bottle of wine upright next to the three roses on the mound.) Black: We ready to go, we've got to get back to the airport and catch a plane to D.C for Fiesta. White: Yeah I'm ready. (Jevon walks over to the head of the tombstone and kisses the top of the stone right above her name before walking off with his tag team partner.) White: Hey Dave, stop for a second, we need to talk. (David Black stops and looks down at his tag team partner and best friend. Their reflection bounces off the front of one another's sunglasses.) White: I appreciate everything that you've done for me from last year and all my life. But I got one thing to say to you. Black: What's up? White: Do whatever you want to Tarin Ballard. I don't care what you do to him whether it was like what you did to him on Fiesta last month or anything else you plan on doing to him after this week...BUT...I say to you bro. Don't lay a hand on his brother Ry. Black: Oh? How come? White: Because he's mine. Tarin is free reign to do whatever the blueberry hell you wanna do to him, but don't touch Ry. He's mine. Black: Okay...COOL. So anything else that you wanna talk about Cranium and Williams while we're at it? White: The same thing we always do. We're gonna get... Graphic... Colorful... and Artistically Homicidal on their asses. Black: I just realized something, this will mark the first time in almost three months...maybe longer that we've faught in the same ring together. White: Yeah like since the last time we've faught together as True Living Colors was when we first one the WWA World Tag Team titles back in July, and nobody has beaten us yet. Black: We're almost unstoppable when we're apart, imagine just how much more stronger we are when we're working together as a complete unit. You've faught and defended the tag team titles by yourself for the last three months at least. I single-handedly took the entire WWA tag team division by myself and beat them in the center of the ring at the 2006 Birthday Bash...and I was on the shelf with a concussion for three months. We're known each other since we was grasshoppers growing up in the hood right here in St. Louis. We've wrestled and helped almost every single tag team title strap in this entire industry all over the world, and now here we are holding the richest tag team straps on the top of the mountain, and nobody can beat us. Not to meantion that at you...Jevon White...THE EPITOMY OF DESTINY...could very well walk into Rage In The Cage 10 and become the next WWA World Heavyweight Champion. I got one question man, when you take the title off of Williams, you gonna hook my ass up with some of that world title love right? White: Dude you know it. Ain't no title gonna come between us and our friendship. We've been through too much to let that destroy us now. Trust me, you're gonna get hooked up. Black: Cool, but I'm in no hurry. White: And neither am I. Now come on, we've got a plane to catch. We gotta get to D.C. for Fiesta. This is history in the making. For it marks the return of the Epitomy of Destiny and The Charismatic Caucasion together in the ring again. (Jevon and David shake hands tightly then begin walking back toward the Escalade as the scene slowly fades out.) Designed By [name withheld] | © World Wrestling Alliance 1997 - 2005
