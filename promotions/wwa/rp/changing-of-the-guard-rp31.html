Changing Of The Guard

21 May 2003

By "Bonafide" James Curzon

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(James Curzon are sat in his secret hiding place. He is sat on a crate, biting his finger nails. Every so often Curzon glances through the blinds just to make sure no one is outside.) Curzon: Where are they? Where are they? (Suddenly there is a knock at the door. Nervously Curzon gets to his feet and stands beside the door.) Curzon: I see it's sunny outside. Voice: But it's raining in my head. Curzon: What? Voice: I said, but it's raining in my head! Curzon: Go away, Conspiracy Spy, the password is incorrect, there's no way you are getting in here! Voice: Mr Curzon, it's me, Fagin. Let me in. Curzon: Haha! You don't fool me, Spy, Fagin would know the real password! Now go away, I don't know why you are knocking at this door, there isn't anyone here. (Curzon sits back on his crate and nods at a job well done. He starts peering out the window again. There is another knock at the door.) Curzon: I see it's sunny outside. Fagin: But it's raining in my heart! Curzon: Aha! Do come in, Mr Fagin! (He rushes over to the door and undoes the top lock, then the middle then the bottom. He disables the alarm and switiches off the lazer warning system. He detaches the pressure pad and the infa red scanner. Then switches off the poison darts in the ceiling and the machine gun hidden in the wall. Finally he pulls back the bolt and pulls the door open.) Curzon: Hello! Come in, my man, Come in. (Fagin enters looking irritated. He is carrying a thick folder. He sits on a crate and Curzon joins him.) Curzon: Why the long face? Have the conspiracy got to you? Fagin: No, I am here to offer my resignation. Curzon: What? Why? What? Fagin: I'm fed up with being treated like a slave and having to indulge your every whim! Curzon: But that's what I pay you for. Fagin: Yes but you don't pay me enough to sneak around like theif and keep everthing secret from everyone! I haven't seen my wife in 3 weeks! I just can't carry on like this. It's not about the money, I just don't like the work. (Curzon stands up and wanders around the room, thinking.) Curzon: What if offered you more money? Fagin: It's not about the money. Curzon: Hmmm. Well, what can I do to change your mind on this? Fagin: Nothing! Curzon: So what if I offered you more money? Fagin: No, my mind is made up, I am leaving. (Curzon strokes his chin as he considers.) Curzon: How about if I offer you more money? Fagin: It's too late, I am retiring. I have found a replacement. Curzon: Now, would you stay if I offered you more money? Or how about some extra cash? Fagin: The new guy should arrive in a few minutes. Curzon: You told him where I am! What if he is a conspiracy agent! Fagin: He will be able to do all things I have done in the past, with the exception of refereeing. Curzon: So who will ref my matches now? Fagin: The normal referees. Curzon: But that will make it fair! That's so unfair on me! (Curzon sits back down and looks Fagin in the eyes.) Curzon: I have an idea, would you stay if I gave you a raise? Some more bread in your pocket? Fagin: NO! My replacement can do the rest of my duties. He will be your personal trainer and is a qualified bodygiard in the peak of athletic form. He also has something of a reputation as a wrestler, perhaps you've heard of him, K2? (Curzon's eyes widen in horror.) Curzon: K2? K2! Oh no, please no! The man is a vicious giant! Didn't he get permanently suspended from wrestling? Fagin: Yes for beating a guy unconscious and then biting his ears off. Curzon: He'll murder me! Fagin: No, he won't. Curzon: But he's a vicious mecenary! Fagin: True, but you are paying him a hell of a lot. He will be loyal to you as long as the money keeps coming. Curzon: Are you sure you won't stay? I'll give you loads more greenbacks? Fagin: K2 will be a more than ample replacement for me. He will fulfill my duties, keep the conspiracy away from you and help you to achieve your aims. (Curzon sits back and smiles.) Curzon: Achieve my aims? Sucess in the WWA? You think he'll help me become a WWA Hall of Famer? Fagin: Yes, I do. Curzon: And he'll keep the conspiracy away? Fagin: Yes, he will. Curzon: And he'll do whatever I say? Fagin: Pretty much, yes. He'll make sure you achieve great things in the WWA. Curzon: Excellent! (There is a knock at the door. Fagin opens it and a huge shadowy figure enters the room. he ducks under the doorway and stands up in the centre of the room.) Fagin: This is K2. Curzon: Holy s**t! (K2 is a huge black guy, easily 7 feet tall and very muscular. He is dressed in a black suit and black sunglasses. He cuts a very imposing figure. He speaks in a low, gravelly voice.) K2: Mr Curzon, It's an honour to be working for you. Curzon: Good afternoon. Now I just want to test your loyalty. Fagin: Oh, he's very loyal, Mr Curzon. Curzon: Let's find out. K2 dispose of Mr Fagin for me, please. Hurry along, there is much to do! (K2 grabs Fagin around the neck and throws him int the corridor, slamming the door in his face.) Curzon: Excellent work, swift and accurate. This could be the beginning of beautiful friendship. We just need to make sure the conspiracy don't catch us! K2: A conspiracy? That sounds a bit paranoid! Curzon: I'm not paranoid, I'm right! K2: OK, You're right. You're in charge! Curzon: Yes I am. From this moment on I am in charge! Absolutely in charge.
