"Final WARNING from me, leave my family alone."

3 Mar 2003

By Jimmi Zion

Item 1 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 3 Mar 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
*[Fade In] *[It's the next morning. In just a few short hours, WWA Fiesta will be showing live tonight on television. We cut to the interior of a hotel room penthouse suite overlooking the city. Inside we see "Sky Flyin" Jimmi Zion sitting by himself watching the morning news and eating a bowl of Total cornflakes cereal. As he is watching, he catches a reprise of the incident that happened at Walt Disney World where a reporter is informing the public about the fire at Cinderella's castle going up in flames. The reporter on the scene, Linda Yiu, gives her station the full skinny. Zion watches intently while he eats his bowl of cereal, he appears to have a genuine concerned look on his face.] “Ladies and gentleman, this is Linda Yiu, reporting live from Walt Disney World. As you may be able to tell from the mayhem behind me, Disney World’s most well known building, Cinderella’s Castle has erupted in flames.” "Sky Flyin" Jimmi Zion: "Oh No, DisneyWorld caught on fire. Man and I was thinking about taking my child there after it's born...he I don't know if it's a boy or a girl because it's too soon to tell, but either way is good for me. Oh well, it's a billion dollar a year business, I am sure that they will have that place rebuilt and running in no time. In fact I think that it'll be better than ever. Give it nine months to a year, it'll be back and so will my new family and me." Behind the reporter, a scene of utter mayhem has erupted. Park officials and firefighters scurry to and fro. Also standing just behind her and slightly to the left is Lockdown. Lockdown is carrying a large gas can, and is making every manner of rude and vulgar gestures. Zion: "Oh bloody hell Lockdown's there, and he's walking toward the camera. I think he's gonna talk to, well there goes my breakfast." [He pushes his bowl of cereal across the coffee table so that he can listen to the incoming report doing a semi mock motion like he is going to vomit before listening on.] “The blaze broke out about 20 minutes ago and is spreading like…well, wildfire! Authorities suspect foul play, but currently have no suspects.” *Lockdown looks around for a moment, then hurls the gas can. “As I said, I am here live and I am going to try to get an interview with one of the park officials.” Lockdown promptly steps up and tells the reporter he is ready for the interview. The reporter is taken aback as Lockdown does not exactly look like a park official OR a firefighter. Zion: "Well no bloody sh*t!" Reporter: Uhhhh… Sir, would you please tell me who you are and what we know at this time. Zion: "No Ms. Yiu, don't interview him. Anybody but that nutmunch." *Reporter: Uhhhh… Sir, would you please tell me who you are and what we know at this time. Lockdown: Don’t worry about it babe, OF COURSE I work here. As a matter of fact, just gimmie the fu**in mic Lockdown pries the microphone from the reporter’s hand. Zion: "Goddamn it, he took the mic, now we have to listen to this putz!" Lockdown: WHHHOOOO-HOOO!!! This IS the happiest G*ddamn place on earth!!! That’s right, Linda, the authorities suspect foul play. But nothing is as foul as the current state of the WWA. (Aside to Linda: Pretty fu**in good segway, huh! Ha! And you went to school for this sh*t) How many times do we have to kick the sh*t outta the Extreme Corporation before they realize they aren’t even on the same level with The Cell? Time and time again they run their little mouths to every weak ass b*tch who will listen about how they are gonna mop the ring with me and my partner. Yet time and time again they step into the ring and get beat like a rug! I mean sh*t!! They’re like the Cincinnati Bengals of the WWA! Tonight they’re trying something a little different, but the result will be the same. Who are they fu**in kidding? Does anyone really think a cruiserweight and little Mal Somers can take us out? (Aside to Linda: Hey, b*tch, check out this segway. You might learn a thing or two.) Zion: "Well it was a great place until you plum arss screwed it up sunshine. Oh well, they're rich, The Disney people can have it fixed back up in no time. Unlike what some people are going to do to you." "Folks, the fire behind me is big news, but not as big as the announcement I have to make right now. For months my partner, Leviathan, and I have been warning the other WWA superstars not to meddle in our affairs. Most of you have been good little girls and done as you were told. Unfortunately there are always some who need to learn the hard way." Zion: "News flash big boys, you attacked me first. Your boy, Cruz started this mess. I just pushed the issue. If you wanna blame somebody for me intervening, then you need to beat the crap out of him, and leave my wife...and now my unborn child out of this. Wait a bloody minute! Haven't I been saying that all along since the beginning? Why yes I have. You mess with my wife, you mess with me. You got me so live with it." "Shaman… Jimmi Zion… there will be no more warnings. Tonight on Fiesta, the bloodbath begins. Especially for you, Zion. You are about to learn the hard way that the best way for a man to protect his “family” is not to put them in danger in the first place. Ya see, Jimmi, while my partner may be a bit reluctant, I have no problem whatsoever with ending your little boy’s life right along with his mother’s. This is not another fu**in warning, Jimbo. I’m telling ya this so you’ll know that it’s all your fault. If only you had listened to your dear sweet wife, this story could have had a happy ending. You could have prevented it, “daddy!” (Out of the corner of his eye, Lockdown sees Linda Yiu, the reporter, motioning some police officers over.) Lockdown: This is Lockdown, signing off. Back to you, Linda!!! (Lockdown drops the mic and hits the afterburners. He is outta here.) [Back in the hotel room. Zion, who listened to every single word that Lockdown said about him echoed and reverberated in his ears and penatrated his mind. You can see the change in emotion in Zion's face to more of a pent up, emotional rage. He glares for a moment but then regains his cool. He turns off the television with the remote and then looks up at the camera looking at him.] Zion: "I reiterate from my statement that I said before. Hell I regress to what I said from the beginning when all this just started. Lockdown you and your boys started this. This was all Cruz's fault, and then he sicked his boys on me for no reason. WHY? Because he wanted to make a point and show that his Cellmate boys were all big and bloody bad. Then the next week, you make the biggest mistakes of your lives. You attacked my wife. I don't care what you do to me, but you never touch my wife. You say that she tried to hit you, but she was defending herself from Leviathan. If he didn't get in her face, then she wouldn't of hit him, and he wouldn't of DDTed her on the ring floor. Then I wouldn't of attacked you at Rage In a Cage because I was so damn pissed off. So again I wanna say that this was all because of you. You are the only ones blame while I am dragging you off to hell in a handbasket." "Now your right, and this needs to end Lockdown. Even I am mature enough to admit this. I'll tell you what, after this match, I will be more than gladly to leave you and Leviathan alone. Trust me it's not up to me, but it's up to my wife and my child on the way. Nothing else other than that. I will be more than happy to forget all this. I hate the idea of leaving you alone, but when it comes to my wife and NOW my kid, I will do the man thing. I will turn my back on this feud. So like it or not Lockdown, I will leave you alone and focus on more important things. However don't expect me to forget what you did, but for the sake of my family, I will FINALLY obey your warnings. It took me long enough." "Keep this in mind, I will walk into this match on Fiesta not only ready to fight but ready to win. I will fight to win. I will not lose to you. Do whatever you want to me, but leave my wife and unborn child out of this. This is our fight, so quit trying to bring them into it. This is between us. Keep it that way. I'm telling you to leave my family alone once and for all...THIS IS YOUR FINAL WARNING FROM ME!" "Did you get all that Lockdown? I hope so, because I hate to repeat myself, so comprehend the words that are coming out of my mouth sunshine, now fade to black, I'm done. I have a match to fight and win in just a few hours from now." [Zion takes the bowl of NOW soggy cereal and puts it in the kitchenette sink to be washed and then walks off toward the bedroom. The camera fades out.) *[Fade Out]
