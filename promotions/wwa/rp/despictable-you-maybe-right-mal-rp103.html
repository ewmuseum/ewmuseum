Despictable? You maybe right Mal

9 Jun 2003

By Enigma

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
{Darkness} Despicable? “You are probably right on the money Mal. But you see know one really knows what myself; Joey K and Se7en are capable of.” “You actually believe that the Extreme Corporation has become stronger with the addition of Chance?” {Smoke fills the scene and we begin to be able to see Enigma sitting in his lair} “That is where you are wrong as usual Mal. For you see with your addition of the Bringer of Light, the Extreme Corporation has actually become weaker. How do I know this? Because I am the one who made him weak. Chance has fallen by the waste side. He has yet to understand that he has fallen from grace. He may still be walking but in my mind he has been destroyed. Chance is a non-factor. I made it so, remember that Mal, become Chance is nothing but one of many people to have a bright future and have it all crashes down on one day.” “Sound familiar Mal? Well it should because you are going along the same path. You three may have been able to trick that simpleton Eddie Van Dorn into giving you back your precious, and I use that term loosely, little piece of tin you call rookie. But you will never be able to outsmart myself, Joey K or Se7en.” “You may think that I will get involved in your match? Do you think I would do something so predictable? Something so simple?” {Enigma smirks} “Of course not. For you see I am not like that. I will not do anything predictable. In fact Mal, I would say you should expect the unexpected. Because you can never tell when we will strike. Enjoy that thought while you wait for your match. NOTHING CAN STOP US NOW {Fade to ?}
