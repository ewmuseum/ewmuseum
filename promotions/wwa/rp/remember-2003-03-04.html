Remember

4 Mar 2003

By Grunge

Item 3 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 4 Mar 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
FADE IN (Once again we find Grunge sitting and listening to music.) Yes, they're sharing a drink they call loneliness But it's better than drinkin' alone (Except this time is different. Grunge is playing the Piano. He’s been playing the Piano Man by Billy Joel and singing along. He stops and looks at the camera.) Grunge- It's odd how things from the past can well up after you think you've forgotten them, isn't it? (Grunge runs his fingers the keys before shutting it.) Grunge-Julie and I and I were at the eastside Wal-Mart picking up some speaker wire. As she looked up and down the isle, I turned to check out the $15 DVDs that to find that rare find. As I stepped toward the DVD display, a girl pushed her shopping cart between said display and me. I looked and was surprised to see that it was the girl from my Spanish class who I knew from High School. I said hello, and she was just as surprised to see me. (Grunge almost looks nostalgic.) Grunge-We had started messing around after Spanish classes on Tuesday and Thursday nights, going to each other's places and not leaving until something like 4 a.m. After a month and a half, another guy from our class started hanging out. He always dropped subtle hints about wanting to hook up with her, which she always rebuffed. However, one weekend she decided to make out with him for lack of anything better to do. She then called me and talked to me about it rather matter of factly. To clarify my position at the time...I definitely wanted to go out with her on an exclusive basis, but out of respect for her recent break-ups (not to mention my own) I decided to give her space. (Grunge hits the piano and smiles.) Grunge-It hurt. It really did. And what hurt worst is that she called me, telling me about it. She said she was bored and didn't care enough to say no to him. She also mentioned how she wanted to kiss me, but I wasn't there...etc. Seeing her today brought all of this back. It's like in the movie Swingers; they don't call or show back up until you've forgotten about them. But I did get the better deal. I’m married now, happy. (Grunge starts to laugh.) Grunge-But things are different now. I don’t plan on ever being forgotten or passed up. And now this starts with Monday. It was supposed to be Nick Adams and me for TWO WEEKS NOW! But instead two other guys have been left in. (Grunge walks over and pulls back a black curtain to see pictures of all three men, Venny LeTrav, Joey K, and TV Champion Nick Adams. Grunge pulls out a laser pointer and aims it at Venny.) Grunge-First in this fourway we have the Cajun. He’s Gambit from the X-Men, Remy from the movie the Big Easy and TNT all mixed up into one package. I have respect for his skills and truth be told he could win this. I may actually have to wrestle this one. Truth me told I’d give him a rematch when I happen to win Monday. His big problem is he’s too concerned with this guy. (Grunge points over at Joey K.) Grunge-Next we have Joey K a fine..heh..a fine..Hehha. (Grunge starts laughing hysterically. He laughs so hard he doubles over and clutches his ribs. He manages to stop and wipe a tear from his eye.) Grunge-Sorry I thought I could say that with a straight face. And finally we have the holder of all the cards. (Grunge points at the poster of Nick Adams, who has a mustache drawn on the poster.) Grunge-I tried to give you your props Nick. I tried to make this just a friendly encounter but you come out on Insider and say that I DON’T DESERVE MY REMATCH! WHO DO YOU THINK YOU ARE? (Grunge calms himself and rubs his hands through his hair.) Grunge-How does it feel Nick? How does it feel to know that I’ve beaten you twice? I’ve beaten you out of a cage and I’ve made you tap. (Grunge smiles a smile which it looks like he borrowed from his friend Eddie Van Dorn. As he does so a loud echoing can be heard.) TAP TAP TAP Grunge-Now all that’s left is to pin you. I know why you’re so mad Nick; you think I cheated you out of your win at Rage in the Cage. Poor thing. I did what anyone of you including Venny and Joey, well if Joey was smart enough, would do. Oh and one more thing before I go Nick. How did it feel to hear those fans urging me on? How did it feel knowing they wanted me to make you tap? Just food for thought. Unlike that story everyone will remember me. The fans will because I’ve defended them and I’ve given my ability and you will because you will lose. (The echoing begins again.) TAP TAP TAP (We begins to fade as we settle on Grunge’s face, still smiling.) FADE OUT
