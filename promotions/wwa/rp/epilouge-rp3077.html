Epilouge

27 Apr 2006

By Kid Cool

Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
He sat alone at the edge of his bed, clutching a picture of his once beloved girl. His blonde highlighted locked untamed and brushing down over his ears. His stubble growing in patches over his fair skin. In Boston, Massachusetts there were a lot of emotions running high, and the tension had gone on far too long. Frank Erik’s was all alone on a dismal, Wednesday night in his apartment, having moved away from his long time best friend Liam Jenson, the arguments became petty and almost violent. It was Frank who decided best to leave his former friend and his newly acquainted girlfriend to their own devices, much to his own dismay. Being unemployed did not suit Frank, he was a young man, perhaps a little naive in the world today but he stood for what he felt was right. His skills were not meant for office work sitting around shuffling paper on general labour heaving bricks around a construction site, he was designed for one purpose, entertainment. He looked out the window at the cloudy night sky and sighed to himself, perhaps this was a moment to reflect on the past year. He shook his head in disgust with himself, this was the moment when he could put things right what he did wrong in the past, maybe he was all alone when he got to the WWA and they forced him to partner up with a backstabbing loser. But now he was confident, ready for the next challenge, ready to face up to the letter his real father sent him many years ago. Dear Son, I know this will come as quite a shock to you, for I have never spoken to you at all before this letter. But there are a few truths you need to know before you throw this away. Your mother and I love you very much but understand that we were underage when we had you and your Mothers parents were very religious. We had to run away, not only to protect ourselves and our love but to also protect you as a baby. We knew we wanted you from the first moment your mother found out she was expecting, but this wasn’t what both our parents, your grandparents wanted. They said it would ruin our lives, they said we would never amount to anything. We were going to prove them wrong son, were going to show, that if you ever fall off the horse you just get back on it again. I know this doesn’t explain who we are or why we did what we did. But know that we both love you and are very proud of whatever you become. Signed Mum and Dad. The letter lay crumpled on the bed next to the picture which had been carefully placed by Frank’s hand. A tear welted in his eye but he held back his emotions for a moment giving him time to think. He needed to plan his next move carefully, his only option was to return to the place where the problems took place. Face his fears and conquer the demons that lay there to haunt him. Getting in wouldn’t be easy, it would take a lot of favours from people Frank never owed, wrestling was hardly the most forgiving business, the WWA hardly ever forgave anyone that came back and usually branded them a “wash up” generally brought back to boost ratings when they were low. Kid Cool was a washed up gimmick, a loser, a nobody. Kid Cool needed to expand, to grow up. But in Franks mind this was never going to happen, he loved the fact he was always an underdog, it lured many wrestlers into a false sense of security. Frank picked up his cellphone and looked through the phonebook, Daniel W. Ebb was etched near the top along and just a short way underneath was Liam. He looked at the number for a moment, before going back to the first name and lifting the receiver to his head. “Hello Daniel? It’s Francis. I want back in.” Whether it was cold night air or Franks lasting hangover something within him began to lift slowly. Frank knew he was stepping in the right direction, stepping towards a better good. The phone call ended quickly he knew the snowball had begun to roll back, and again he put the cellphone in his lap looking at one number. “Liam Jenson” the name rang alarm bells in his head, his fingers poised on the call button, thoughts of telling him to get back into the WWA passed through his head, then his finger quickly switched over to the opposite side of the phone and his finger hung above the “Delete number”. He clicked a button, and another to confirm, this time he was going in alone. This time things were going to be different. Designed By [name withheld] | © World Wrestling Alliance 1997 - 2005
