Niño Bonito

7 Mar 2003

By Venny LeTrav

Item 2 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 7 Mar 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Shot of a nice hotel room with a pregnant woman laying down on the floral-sheeted bed. As the camera pans around, it is seen that Karine Malbrough is laying on the bed, napping. As the cameraman zooms in a bit on her features, her tan skin, her long reddish-brown hair, her supple lips… a door opens offscreen and the camera whips up, facing a dripping wet Venny LeTrav. He looks a little miffed at the sudden intrusion, and besides that, he’s wearing nothing but a hotel towel around his waist.) Venny: (whispering) Psst! C’mere! (Venny waves off to the side and the camera follows him to the other side of the room, facing the window that overlooks the busy front lot of the hotel. Venny leans against the wall and crosses his arms over his chest, looking out the window for a second, then back at the camera.) Venny: (whispering) I finally got her to fall asleep. She’s been wigged out lately, and I figured some comfort food would help her relax. She had me running all over town getting Greek Gyros, Chinese Takeout, Mexican Pizza, Torellini… I mean, there’s four or five-odd countries in my wife’s stomach right now. It’s not like it isn’t worth it… I love her and I love to make her happy. But seriously, she’d be expensive to feed whether or not we were at home. If I tried to cook her breakfast, lunch, and dinner she’d die of malnutrion. I can’t even boil water right sometimes and she expects to be fed about 9 times a day. After she pops this kid out, I’m gonna talk her into getting her cords mismatched, or I’m gonna have to do this AGAIN. (Venny shakes his head and runs his hands through his wet, platinum blonde hair. He strokes his goatee slowly and heaves a sigh, looking back at his wife for a minute or so. He then looks back at the camera.) Venny: I gotta admit, though. Me, a father. Karine’s been wanting a child for the past year, even though I’m on the road, she still manages to get me rompin’ and rollin’ when I come home. I start to wonder if she doesn’t even think lewd thoughts until I get home, because as soon as I do, she’s on me like a dog in heat. It’s not like it isn’t good, though. That woman is much more than a great personality and eye candy. (Venny grins widely and looks out the window again. He looks a little stressed out, but he smiles to himself softly.) Venny: Her worrying is as big a problem. She’s so afraid that I’m gonna get killed in the ring. Can you imagine how she was when the AL went up against Havoc? Her mom had to stay with her and the whole time she was put on muscle-relaxers because she was turning into a walking bundle of stress. And she’s not too worried about Joey K. She knows anyone can kick his ass. And very few people can kick mine. Not to sound arrogant or anything… I’m no Joey K. When I say that, I mean I’m not as delusional as to dress up like a superhero, and then when I get my ass kicked, turn into a bad remake of The Sopranos. But then again, I’m in the ring with two other guys, Grunge and Adams. Nick and I get along fine, it was pretty fun tagging with him and I hope I get the chance to do it again. As for Grunge, he’s a great guy. Funny as hell, great talent, and above all else, he’s the TV Champion. I’m hoping I at least kick Joey’s ass. The TV title isn’t exactly something I’m aiming for at the moment, but it’d be nice to get it. And if I do, I’ll see how high to the top I can rise. But, I’d have those three… er, two guys nipping at my heels. I run and lift weights every day, but it’s gonna take more than muscle to beat Grunge, or Adams for that matter. Joey’ll probably just get knocked out in the first few minutes, so I’m not too worried about him. Karine: (offscreen) Mmm… Remy… Venny: (looking offscreen) Yeah, baby? Karine: (offscreen) I could really go for some Thai right now… (Venny smiles at the cameraman and nods towards the door. He walks offscreen as the camera pans outside, looking out the window at a beautiful day as we… fade to black.)
