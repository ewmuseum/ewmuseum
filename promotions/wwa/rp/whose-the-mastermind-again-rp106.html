Whose the mastermind again?

9 Jun 2003

By Grunge

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
FADE IN (Darkness, much like the way the universe began and much like it will end.) Grunge V/O: Let us do away with complacency shall we? Let us focus. Write it down. Write it down. Let us begin to weave the threads of idealism, artistic expression, and passion together to form our canvass. Let us fill this canvass with poetic images. Hypnotic images. Images filled, contradictory enough, with starkness and vitality. (A brief flicker begins to shine out of the darkness.) Grunge V/O: It is time to write. Time to express. Time to tap into the themes and motifs that you wish to bare through your most sacred and holy cinema. They projector has started, its light splayed across the screen...hair scratches and countdowns. Our feature presentation begins in 3, 2, 1... (The light begins to shine and finally Grunge comes into view. He has the United States Title draped over his broad shoulder.) Grunge: Hey Enigma BOO! (Grunge’s laughter echoes throughout the entire room.) Grunge: Mastermind huh? Idiot is more like it. Sorry dumbing it down is for your purpose. After all look who you surround yourself with. (Grunge hits a clicker in his left hand and the entire back of the room shows Joey K’s face.) Grunge: Question. Did Chance drop you on your head so many times that you developed brain damage. Perhaps you should look at the tapes. Before that last Meltdown I had defeated Joey K more times than most can count. The only reason that was different was because Nick Adams gave him the victory. I was really quite proud of him for that. In fact I knew that’s what he would do. I’m sure your confused but I’ll come back to that point. (Grunge hits the clicker and next a picture Se7en fills the screen.) Grunge: Se7en huh? Judging by the spelling he must be a David Fincher fan. A fine director. Hey Nick, Dave, does anybody else find it funny that a man who used to “own” Enigma in every sense of the word now serves him? What does that say about you Enigma? (Grunge smiles.) Grunge: See you’re not a mastermind Enigma. The only reason the TV Title is in your champ is because I wanted him to interfere in that match. (Grunge leans in close.) Grunge: (whispers) Because if you were so smart you’d realize that your going to be stuck in the ring with three men who can’t stand you. X-Corp and Youth United are now allies and I think Adams has finally come to an understanding. (Grunge’s smile goes deranged as he cocks back his head.) Grunge: (screams) AND IF YOU DARE TO TRY ANYTHING FUNNY I WILL SHOW YOU A VISION OF VIOLENCE SO SEVERE IT WOULD MAKE DANTE COWER IN FEAR! (Grunge runs his fingers through his hair.) Grunge: And that goes for everyone. Soon, instead of the dollar bill saying "In God We Trust," it will read "The winner is the one who dies with the most toys." And as you can plainly see… (Grunge taps the United States Title draped on his shoulder.) Grunge: …that would be me. Just warning you. (Grunge leans back in his chair.) Grunge: Rock and roll baby. (And at that we begin to…) FADE OUT
