Cry Hav- errr, Mighty Joe!

12 Jan 2003

By Mighty Joe

Item 2 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 12 Jan 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
*Click* A faint echoing drip bounces through the darkness, but as the clicking continues, so does the murky setting.... *Click* *Click* *Click* We are unaware of where we are, the dimness of this place screams through the night sky, the mournful room diminishing any cheerfulness. But, as the clicking continues, so does the murky setting..... *Click* *Click* *Click* ........ Suddenly, the rhythmic clicking ceases, and is instead replaced by a much loader noise, a much more threatening noise, which lights up the room! *THUNK* One huge ceiling light explodes into life. *THUNK* And another *THUNK* And another THUNK, THUNK, THUNK, THUNK! Each ‘thunk’ allies itself with a light flashing, and we immediately see the location....’Mighty Joe’s Secret Mighty Mountain Hideout’. With the cave brightened, we behold the ‘Mighty Mobile’, parked on a large circular platform in the middle of the cave, glistening under the lights, the Mighty Joe symbol shining on the hood, spikes protruding from the tyres, and blue and yellow flames painted on either side. To the right, is a large computer-like construction, the beeping and flashing LEDs turning themselves on and off in unison. Above this machine, approximately 12 television screens show recording of city streets, while a large chair watches over the proceedings.... A rope suddenly falls into view, followed by WWA crime fighter Mighty Joe. He lands with a thud, and looks menacingly toward the camera... MJ: The ‘Foul’ Phobos...The ‘Disastrous’ Deimos, you call this high-tech hideout a ‘hovel which I call home’? I’ll have you know that ‘Mighty Joe’s Secret Mighty Mountain Hideout’ is a place where yours truly can watch over the night skies, protecting city citizens from the evils of people like you! The WWA has more villainous criminals than ever before, But, the WWA also has number 1 superhero, and best crime stopper than there has ever been, Mighty Joe, on the case! Joe walks over to the T.V screens mounted on the wall, and begins to study them. MJ: Hmmm, quiet....a little TOO quiet..... Joe turns back to the camera… MJ: I witnessed the carnage, along with Citizen Letrav, I saw you lay a beating on the Texas Longhorns. I say those two citizens spread out on the floor. But, I also saw something else, something much more interesting…I saw two bullies that night…and we all know that bullies aren’t the big men they think they are, bullies are cowards! One of the screens begins to flicker, and a red light above it starts to shine. MJ: You two may be my personal Armageddon, but with my super strength, I will just blow you right out of the sky! Joe now notices the red light above the monitor, and stares at the picture. We can faintly make out the image of a young woman being attacked by a gang of youths. The superhero springs to his feet, and launches himself towards the Mighty Mobile which appears to start-up on its own accord. Mighty Joe climbs in, before leaning out of the window towards the camera. MJ: Can you hear her? She is shouting, she is crying out the name.....not Havoc, but Mighty Joe! The car suddenly speeds up and the camera fades…
