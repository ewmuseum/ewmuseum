My Apology

23 Jan 2003

By Jackson Kraven

Item 7 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 23 Jan 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(We see a black screen, and white text types up on it accompanied by the sound of an old-fashioned typewriter.) DEAR GORDON YOU WANTED AN APOLOGY WELLL... HERE'S MY APOLOGY (The screen fills with red-and-black footage of Jackson Kraven hitting Alexandra Korth with a KravenBomb.) F (We see that horrible, heart-stopping moment again, from a different angle.) U (We see that sickening atrocity perpetrated upon a pregnant woman one more time.) C (We watch horror-struck as the Tomorrow Man yet again goes too far.) K (We watch from the crowd as the most evil man in WWA history commits the most evil crime in WWA history, and we shudder.) Y (We watch from the top of the ramp, shocked by what we are witnessing, but unable to do anything.) O (We watch as if watching the match on a TV screen, appalled by the inhumanity but unable to turn our eyes from the screen.) U (The screen drops to black, and again we hear the sound of an old typewriter.) RAGE IN THE CAGE VI WILL SEE HELL UNLEASHED BODIES WILL BREAK AND BLOOD WILL BE SPILLED GORDON, I LOOK FORWARD TO IT. JACKSON (We fade to black, but it's not over.) (Oh no, not by a long shot.) (We hear a sound in the black, a horrible, hateful sound.) (The sound of a man who knows exactly what he did...) (...and doesn't give a damn.)
