M.J gets ready for Fiesta

4 Jan 2003

By Mighty Joe

Item 1 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 4 Jan 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
FADE IN The scene opens upon a dark cave. Above the entrance hangs a sign. 'Mighty Mountain Cave' in red a yellow. The camera sweeps through the entrance of the cave and into the dark and damp pits. suddenly, Chris Crenshaw stumbles into viewand begins to seak into the camera. CRENSHAW: Hello WWA fans, I'm here at....a....a secret location, and today we're attempting to get an interview with Mighty Joe. Hopefully, he'll be somewhere around here. Crenshaw turns and begins to continue deeper into the cave, the camera follows. But, almost immedediately as Crenshaw starts walking, huge spolights brighten up the cave. Now we can clearly see we are in a huge room, in the center, is a helipad type platform and parked on that is a batmobile type car, on the hood the Mighty Joe symbol is printed. A rope suddenly falls into view from the ceiling and Mighty Joe makes a his entrance by sliding down it and settling next to the WWA interviewer. MIGHTY JOE: Citizen Crenshaw! CRENSHAW: Er, Hi Joe, how are you? MIGHTY JOE: I'm good, just how a superhero should be! you? CRENSHAW: erm, yeah Joe, I'm fine, now down to business.... MIGHTY JOE: right... CRENSHAW: Well, first, we haven't seen much of you out of the ring lately, why is that? Mighty Joe steps back, away from Crenshaw at points towards the background. MIGHTY JOE: Look around you Citizen! You are looking at 'Mighty Joe Secret Mighty Mountain hide out!' CRENSHAW: Erm, the what? MIGHTY JOE: The Mighty Joe Secret Mighty Mountain Hide-out! The place where I, Mighty Joe can plan out my attacks to foil enemy activity, a place where i am able to watch the cities over-night, so that it's citizens can sleep soundly in their beds with the knowledge that migty Joe is in his Secret Mighty Mountain Hide-out, ready to stops evil doers everywhere! CRENSHAW: ohhhhhhh.........whats that? Crenshaw points towards the car in the caneter of the cave. It's black bodywork glistens in the spotlights. Net launchers are placed either side of the hood and spikes are protruding from each tyre. MIGHTY JOE: That? That's the 'Mighty Mobile'! My transport as i swoop through the night! CRENSHAW: I see. So what your saying is, you've been busy making this? MIGHTY JOE: Well, yeah..... A silence takes over the scene as both men attempt to decide if the making of Mighty Joe's Secret Mighty Mountain Hide-Out was all abit of a waste of time. CRENSHAW: Anyways..Something else I wanted to ask you was, why did you decide to join the American Luchadores? And why did you keep Venny LeTrav waiting? MIGHTY JOE: Well Citizen Crenshaw, I decided to join simply because, Ctizen LeTrav was in touble, he had lost his partner and after calling through the night sky for his hero, Mighty Joe.....well, it is my duty to help all citizens... CRENSHAW: Right, ok, so why did it take you so lopng to arrive? MIGHTY JOE: *ahem* (under his breath) the, ah, Mighty Mobile wouldn't start.... CRENSHAW: Oh, i see. Mighty Joe quickly adjusts his mask and wait for the next quesiotn. CRENSHAW: And finally joe, have you any words for your opponents for Fiesta, Mack Tenn? MIGHTY JOE: Well, all i have to say is, Mighty Joe will stop all crime, in or out of the ring. But Mack Tenn, the big problem for you is, on saturday night, it'll be IN the ring, which is where i fight crime the most. As a tantamount member of the American Luchadores.....truth and Justice will prevail! CRENSHAW: WEll, fans, thats all we have time for. Lets say thanks for Joe for- MIGHTY JOE: HARK! Can you hear it Crenshaw?! a lady in the distress is calling the name...'Mighty Joe'.......I have to helpy her, farewell citizen! Mighty Joe runs towards the Mighty Mobile and climbs in. The camera zooms in as the cuperhero turns the key and attempts to starts the car. CHUG CHUG CHUG CHUG The car depressingly chugs into silence, but Joe doesn't give up, and tries again. CHUG CHUG CHUG CHUG The same thing happens, and Joe embarrassingly leans out the window and calls for the camera crew to stop filming... FADE OUT
