"A date at the Mu Shu Buffet" (rehibilitation and preparation has officially begun.)

25 Jun 2003

By Jimmi Zion

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
*{Fade In} *{The scene opens just outside a local Oriental massage parlor just outside the front door. After a few moments, a red 2003 Mercedes SUV pulls up on the curb. The door opens and "Sky Flyin" Jimmi Zion walks out. He is wearing; black baggy Fubu jean shorts down to his knees, a red shiney red silk collarless button up shirt, white K-Swiss tennis shoes, and wire framed mirror shade sunglasses. His dreadlocks are each individually braided as the flow down passed his shoulderblades. He still sporting a nice black eye, slowly starting to turn color from when Nick Adams punched him in the face on Meltdown about a week ago.} {He walks into the massage parlor and approaches the counter, an Orietial clerk is there to greet him pleasantly, she is a middle aged but slightly attractive mature woman, but stands somewhat very dignified.} Oriental clerk: "Hello, welcome to the Bangkok massage parlor, where the treasures of the Far East meets the West for all of your theroputic needs, now how may I help you?" {Jimmi Zion pauses as he glances over at the camera, a sly but casual look comes across his face as if he wants to say something, but refrains as he bites his tongue...metaphoracally speaking before answering the clerk's question.} Jimmi Zion: "Yes, I'm looking for the works, and I hear that the Orient is the best place for a massage. From what I am told, you have the personal hands on healing touch...so to speak." Clerk: "I see, well we have a wonderful benefits package, and at a very affordable price. We take check, mastercard, visa, and sometimes if it deems appropriate, we take the occasional food stamps." Zion: "Really?" Clerk: "No...that was a joke, you'd be surprised how many times I get that response everytime I tell it. Anyway, we do everything while in the confides of privacy. Now what seems to be the problem with you?" Zion: "Well for one thing it's my knee, but my whole body is sore and my face hurts a little bit." Clerk: "I see, well say no more, we have the best full body massage with some of the best hands in the city meant to provide that much healing touch that you heard so much about." Zion: "Excellent, so when do I start?" Clerk: "Right now, first you must choose, but choose wisely." Zion: "Or else what?" Clerk: "No reason, again I just love saying that." {The clerk claps her hands, and a wall panal opens up, on the other side, much to Jimmi's surprise, his mouth drops as he eyes on the other side of some of the most beautiful Oriental women that he has ever seen. He takes a deep breath as he recollects his thoughts.} Zion: "WHOA...Wholly Mu Shu buffet batman. My goodness, now these are treasures of the Orient." {He mutters to himself in a subtle outloud voice} "If Mikalia new that I was here, she'd kill me... Clerk: {Chiming in and interupting him.} "Is Mikalia girlfriend?" Zion: "A little more than that, she's my wife, and she's at home in England pregnant with our first child." Clerk: "Well she's a lucky lady, and you have nothing to worry about, there no harm in getting massage right. You need it correct? She will understand when you go back and feeling better than ever before." Zion: "You know, you're right. It's just a massage. Now let's see choose." {Zion starts from the beginning of the wall of beautiful Oriental ladies.} Zion: "Only a small handful of who I want to work on me right?" Clerk: "That's right, now you know why I said choose wisely." Zion: "Boy this is hard, where to begin." Clerk: "If you are looking for someone to work on your knees, how about that one right there in the middle, first row." {The clerk points to a perky, dyed red-headed Oriental lady with a red halter top and tight spandex.} Zion: "Oh yeah that would be good." {Zion's cell phone starts ringing. Aggrivated, Zion pickes it up to answer it.} Zion: "Hello? Chris Chrenshaw? What is it, I'm a little bit busy preparing for my match in the Bourbon Street Brawl on Meltdown this week. If you must know it happens to be rehibiliatation purposes. What do you mean that you want me to comment on the match. Chrenshaw, don't you know not to mess with a black englishman in a Mu Shu buffet. I'm busy now. If you want, you can meet me at the Bankok Massage parlor if you wanna interview me so bad. Otherwise hang up because I am...right now. Lalalalalalalalalalalala Can't talk. Call me after I'm done or better yet come on over, even you could get some in a place like this. Bye." {He hangs up his cell phone and continues on in his pickings. He hums a little song a little louder as he picks.} Zion: {Singing} "Yes...yes...yes...no...yes...yes...yes...no...yes yes...yes...no...yes...yes...yes...YES...no...no... YES...YES... {He stops back again at the beautiful Oriental redhead that he saw earlier, he tilts his head to the side and then raises one eye brow.} Zion: "YEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEESSSSSSSSSSSSSSSS!" Clerk: "Very good choices, you made some very hard but big and bold decision." Zion: {Grinning modestly} "Yes I did, but please tell the rest of the contestants that it wasn't anything against them, but I was limited, I hope there was no hard feelings." Clerk: "Oh course not, they understand." {To the ladies in the room.} Clerk: "Please follow Mr.... Zion: "Zion, Jimmi Zion...Mister 'Sky Flyin' Jimmi Zion." Clerk: "Yes, of course, please take Mr. Zion back in the back and start him on his mineral bath, and then give him a full body massage with the focus being on his knees." {The ladies all giggle as the scurry off. The oriental redhead glances over at Zion as she walks off.} Clerk: "I think she likes you." Zion: "I know, it's almost a blummin shame that I'm married to." Clerk: "Well if you excuse me, shall we get you ready for your massage?" Zion: "Yes." Clerk: "Right this way please." {The clerk takes Zion back into the back as the camera fades out.} *{Fade Out} 2 be continued...
