Snap Back to Reality

9 Jun 2003

By "N.A.I." Nicholas Adams

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Nicholas Adams is shown staring at a chess board. He's sitting in a plain room. His chair is padded oak, the walls are white, and there is a window covered in a blue blind behind him.) Adams: This is a blank stare of a man that's off in a world of his own creation. Few can perfect it as well as logical men, for they aren't insane at all. They seem to be staring very intently upon something, and unlike people that do this and have mental deficiencies, they can continue the action and still keep contact with the outside world. (Adams keeps staring, but relaxes slightly.) Adams: You see, speaking of the world outside this continuous gaze, I think that Enigma has lost touch with it. He thinks that he's the most powerful man in the world, simply because he has two of the biggest bumbling idiots in the world on his side. I kicked Joey K's ass after he won the TV title. I held the TV title before Joey K, and it took me to give Joey K the belt. (He then absent-mindedly moves a couple pawns.) Adams: But what you really have to ask is, can you really win against the United States champion and the man that lasted twenty-five minutes in the ring, taking some brutal punishment and dealing some out. Sure, you fought Chance in a casino match, but you know as well as I do that Chance would have kicked your sorry hide from Vegas to Sacramento if Joey K hadn't interfered. (He moves out a bishop.) Adams: But what really gets me is that, at the end of the day, you might have changed your name, you might have thought you "killed" EkoSyde, but you didn't. You're still the same wrestler. The only difference is, you've brought this cold, calculating, and cruel personage to the forefront. But what I see in you is the fact that you need somebody to help you. And trust me, Enigma, you will not get my help. (He then moves out a knight.) Adams: Of course, Enigma, you aren't the only one in the ring. There are also two men whom I happen to respect. One is a founding member of the Extreme Corporation. The other is the United States champion. For the sake of ease, I think I'll speak about the man which I encountered first in my career in the WWA. (He castles his king.) Adams: His name is Dave Harley. He was quite the competitor when I went up against him in only my second match in the WWA. It took a lot to take him down, and I actually was called a giant-killer by some people that hadn't seen me in independant federations. I was giving my all to beat Harley, but he was one sick fighter. (Adams moves one of the pawns further out. The moves seem to be rather one-sided, since Adams is the only one in the room. But right as he places the pawn back on the board, a lightening bolt flashes through the shade and a loud thunderclap is heard.) Adams: He bloodied me, he gave me a beating, he gave me a chair shot, and yet, somehow, I managed to come out on top. Most would argue it as luck, and for once, I would have to say that Chance was with me...in the form of a mask, but still, it was the edge that I needed to destroy Dave Harley. He was distracted, and it gave me the time I needed to finish him for the one, two, three. (Adams absentmindedly moves one of the knights from the opposite team out in front of its line of pawns.) Adams: Unfortunately, Dave isn't distracted this time. I have to out-wrestle him. I have to manage some way to take him down. Of course, it won't be out of sheer animosity, like it will be when I go against Enigma. It will be out of sheer sportsmanship. I hope you realize that, Dave. I don't want to develop some bad blood between us. (Adams legally positions some pawns and other pieces, then looks directly at the camera.) Adams: And then there's Grunge. My counter-part, as it were. My exact opposite in the WWA. He sees rage where I see righteous anger. He thinks using emotion is the only way to win, while I think that logic and skill can be the only true paths to triumph. I trained myself, with some help in a clinic. Grunge had an actual mentor in Jack Cayhill. And while I trust few to no people, he can rely on Rick Venom and EVD. He's got a wife...I've got nobody. And yet he helped me find a new side of me. (Adams smiles, and the lightening flashes again. He then stands.) Adams: That's the side that I will show forever more. You wanna know which side I'm going to show? (Adams walks his face right up to the camera so only it is shown, but his breath is not fogging up the screen.) Adams: The true Rainmaker. (Adams walks away from the camera, and the camera moves into the chess board, then shows that Adams has arranged the chess pieces to make alpha-numeric symbols, "# 1". The screen then fades.)
