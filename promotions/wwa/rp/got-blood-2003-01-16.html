Got Blood!

16 Jan 2003

By Shaman

Item 5 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 16 Jan 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
** Shaman is seen working out behind a tv screen where its showing various shots of Shaman through out the years in the WWA and each shows Shamans failures and misgivings from the past. The first shows Shaman in the beginningof his career.** BLIND TO THE INNER WORKINGS OF THE WWA. TAKES OVER A YEAR TO GAIN FIRST TITLE!! ** Next one shows Shaman as a leader of several stables and members of many more.** FIRST THE NEW REGULATORS!! THAN WARRIORS PRIDE AS WELL AS THE MILITIA!! FINALLY THE FOUR HORSEMEN OF APOCOLYPSE!! ALL HAD THEIR REASONS FOR COMING INTO BEING, BUT ALL FAILED FOR SOME REASON LEAVING A MAN BITTER WITH RAGE!! ** The next scene focuses on Shamans titles.** MOST WERE HARD FOUGHT YET ONE HAUNTS HIS DREAM AND THE WAY IT WAS ACHIEVED! TAKING ADVANTAGE OF AN INOPPORTUNE MOMENT!! ** The next scene depicts the whole of Shamans career in the WWA.** FIVE YEARS HE IS KNOWN AND FIVE YEARS HE HAD WALKED IN OBSCURITY!! FIVE YEARS HE HAS DOMINATED EXTREME!! FIVE YEARS HE ACHIEVED EVERY TITLE ATTAINABLE BY A HEAVYWEIGHT!! FIVE YEARS TO ONLY BEGIN AGAIN!! ** Shaman sits up as he looks towards the TV as he stands and walks towards it as the screen focuses on Nick Adams, Chrystian Payne and finally the Tv Title.** WELCOME TO THE NEW BEGINNING!! NEW WARS ** Shaman speaks in a low monotone voice and very clearly so all can understand him.** [Shaman] " Don't bank your money on knowing what im going to do next. Im going to hurt everyone who gets in my path. Its nothing personal its just the way it needs to be done!! Nick Adams you will face me in the ring. I respect you and your abilities. So don't feel insulted if im going to take it to you like you have never expected before!! Im no push over kid and things are beginning to look rather interesting coming Rage in the Cage. A war is brewing and all in all im standing in the center of its storm. Because i am the eye of this storm and you and Payne are only riding it out till it passes over!!" ** Shaman turns and walks away as the scene fades to black showing the scenes from the last few months where Shaman ends Troy Walcotts WWA career and then to Chrystian Payne where Shaman is beating the living hell out of him only to lose and then to Seasons Beatings where the match is a draw.** SOON THE CHANGE WILL BE VISIBLE THIS WAS ONLY THE BEGINNING!! ** Scene fades to black leaving only these words behind in its wake.** GOT BLOOD!!
