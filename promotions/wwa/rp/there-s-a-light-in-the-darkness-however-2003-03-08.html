There's a Light in the Darkness, However

8 Mar 2003

By Tracer Bullet

Item 5 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 8 Mar 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The sound of water, slowly dripping, can be heard through the darkness that pervades the scene. The voice of Tracer Bullet then comes up over it.) TB: I don't know what the hell is going on around here lately. First I get cheated out of what would surely have been a victory for me at Rage in the Cage by the mind games of Enigma. I didn't like that. Then I have the title of a franchise player thrust upon me. I didn't ask for that. And now, before I even get a decent chance to exact any kind of justice out of Shane Dorian, he goes and gets himself fired. Marvelous. (He sighs audibly, but still nothing can be seen.) TB: I really don't like the way that things are... *pause* dammit Tyler, will you take the lenscap off? Tyler: Oh, sorry. Was wondering why it was so dark in here. (The lenscap is removed, and the bright lights of Tracer's kitchen comes flooding into the aperture. Tracer is standing by the sink, washing dishes. Every so often, the faucet lets a drop fall into the dishwater. Incidentally, you can still see burn marks on the stove from when Denrol tried to make flambee a couple weeks ago.) TB: Now as I was saying, I really don't like the way things are shaping up around here. Tyler: I don't know why you don't like it so much. You're on a roll, man. You pinned Chance in the six-man tag, you've got an opportunity to lay waste to Enigma Monday night, and you're even starting to get the smell of stale cat piss out of your house. TB: Yeah, that wasn't easy. I am pleased with that. But I'm referring more to a feeling of uneasiness that I just can't shake about the near future in the WWA. Something's going to happen soon, and I'm pretty sure I'm not going to like it. I don't know what it's going to be, or where it's going to come from, but I sense a shadow falling over all of us. Tyler: I think you've just seen Daredevil one too many times. TB: Only saw it once. Tyler: Like I said. TB: Ah, it's not a bad movie, shut up. But I'm serious. And somehow, I get the feeling that it's all going to come back to last Monday night. The WWA has been divided. The old and the new. Not that there are that many of the old guard around, but I didn't want to get into this in the first place. I got pulled into it because Denrol is a friend of mine, and because I've been here nearly four years now. It's that simple. I don't direspect the new blood around here as a whole, merely on a case by case basis. I've always been of the belief that it's the new guys that will save or kill a wrestling organization. It all falls to them. And yet we call ourselves the Franchise Players. Well, I don't, but Denrol and Shaman do. I'd be comfortable with superstar, legend, or even icon, but not franchise. To think that one person, or even a certain group of three people, have the entire industry resting on their shoulders just because they've been around longer than anyone else? (Tracer shakes his head.) TB: That's just not right. And I hope that Chance understands this. I never had anything against you. We were in a match, you were my opponent, I beat you, the end. This is not personal. Between you and Denrol, yes, it's personal. Between myself and Enigma, yes, it's personal. But that's because he MADE it personal. You and I? Everything's copasetic. Because you, and people like you, are the future of this business. I'm not going to be another Hogan, another Triple H... I just won't do it. I've struggled to keep my ego in check for years to prevent something like that from happening. And interestingly enough, it wasn't until I was called a franchise that it finally hit home... I don't like being considered a franchise. Makes me sound like a McDonald's or something. Something that tastes good at the moment, but has no real substance to it and, in the end, is really quite bad for you. I don't want to be like that. I'm not going to let myself become complacent. (Tracer picks up a saucepan and some steel wool and starts scrubbing away.) TB: Damn grease stains... (He looks up into the camera and laughs nervously.) TB: And that of course segues into Enigma, because that's all he's going to be when I'm through with him. Tyler: Good cover. TB: Shut up. Now look, Enigma... I know that you think you're some kind of big badass just because you discovered my weak point. But remember, even New Star and the idiots he hung around with figured that out. Daniel E. Nelson figured that out. It's not exactly classified knowledge. But what you need to realize is that everyone who has ever messed with that weak point has been sent packing after I beat them senseless. Or, in Shane Dorian's case, before I beat him senseless ENOUGH. And that's another thing that bothers me... the way that your cohort got fired before I got the chance to give him exactly what he deserved. So who is inconvenienced here? ME. Dorian was saved by getting his ass canned, believe me. It is I who got screwed over here. And perhaps... perhaps you as well, Enigma. After all, now that Dorian is gone, there's really only one person that's in my sights at the moment. And that's you. (Tracer drops the pan and steel wool back into the dirty dishwater.) TB: You two really thought you were clever, I suppose. Me, who had never given in to pain in my career, was beaten psychologically. If that had been any other type of match, it would have been mine. You two played me like a ten-cent flute, and I don't like it one bit. But since Shane is gone, Enigma, you'll just have to take both beatings yourself. Shane claimed that entire match was part of an illusion... I'd say the illusion was capped off with one big disappearing act. And that couldn't be more frustrating for me, or more physically damning for you. Because you see, both you and Shane always said that together there was no stopping you. And as much of a lie as that was, you are no longer together, are you? So what are you going to do, Enigma, when you look across the ring and see me... and then look back into your own corner and see no one there? There will be no escapes for you. Not now, not ever. (Tracer drains the water out of the sink and dries his hands on a nearby towel.) TB: You tell me that quitters never win, that quitters never prosper. What about cheaters, Enigma? What about cheap-ass people like you always looking for the easy way out? You may win on occasion... but it's going to come back to haunt you eventually, you know. After all, what was I expected to do in that situation? Let you injure my wife? Yes, I made a mistake in leaving her back there alone. But the point is, what kind of human being are you when you can threaten to harm an innocent woman like that, and not even give it a second thought? I'll tell you what kind, Slick... a weak one. Very weak. If you need to threaten women to make yourself feel like a big shot, you've got problems. And I think I know why you need to make yourself feel big. Two simple words, Enigma... you're jealous. (Tracer nods as he slings the towel over his shoulder.) TB: It's written all over your face. I can tell it in your speech, the way you carry yourself, and in every little thing you do. You said it yourself, Enigma... I WAS at the pinnacle of the profession, and I HAVE been sliding down the ladder of contention. But you see what I met on the way down? Two pieces of scum who were as high up as they were ever going to go... that being you and Shane. It really must be upsetting to you that the highlight of your career is going to be getting inside the head of a has-been like me and forcing him into losing a single match that he actually had the upper hand in at the time. Must be even more frustrating that you had these lofty ideals of teaming up to destroy my legacy, and then your partner went and got himself sacked. What are you going to do now, Enigma? Surely you can't do this by yourself on such short notice. I'm sure you had these big, well-laid-out plans to... (Tracer slaps his forehead.) TB: Oh, excuse me, forgot who I was talking to for a second. I'm sure you had SOME kind of plan or other laid out well in advance to slowly break my spirit. Now what do you do when your second has buggered off? (Tracer shakes his head slowly and chuckles.) TB: Honestly, I find it really interesting that my whole feud with Dorian started over my calling him lazy, and then he gets fired for being exactly that. I guess I can consider it a moral victory, since he and the management went and proved my point and all. But anyway, Enigma, I have to wish you luck for Monday night. Because while I have given Thorne an I.O.U. for a severe ass-kicking... (Tracer's eyes narrow as he looks menacingly into the camera.) TB: With you, the debt is going to be paid up front. (Tyler fades to black with Tracer still glaring into the camera.)
