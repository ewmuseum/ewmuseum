Plain and simple

19 Feb 2003

By Ian Tense

Item 3 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 19 Feb 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Mine. (No need for pictures this time-no descriptions, nothing) Its still mine. (Just read the damned words) Kraven tried, but even The Tommorrow Man couldn't take it. (They'll spell it all out for you) Say what you will. Credit Ventressa. Credit Sanders. Credit the *beep*ing beerman for all I care. I dont' give a *beep*. Because at the end of the day, I still have something that every single person in the WWA wants... AND no one can figure out a way to take it from me. Yet the still try... Damien Thorne, Kash, Wes Sanders, Jackson Kraven, ALL of them. They are ALL sitting somewhere thinking about it. Dreaming about. Salivating over it. They're like teenagers waiting for their first piece of ass. But time and time again, they will fail. Then others will rise. The Lockdowns, Leviathans, Mal Somers, Nick Adams, Venny LeTravs, but they will fail too. And they will have excuses and reasons, just like the ones that came before them. And on and on. But through it all. Every *beep*ing second, minute, hour, day, week, month, and year, one thing will be constant... (Suddenly there is a picture after all.) The Prototype Ian Tense (One of the World Heavyweight Champion. THe man everyone wants to knock off. The man who is where everyone else wants to be...) WWA World Heavyweight Champion. (At the *beep*ing top)
