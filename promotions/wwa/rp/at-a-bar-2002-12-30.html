At a bar

30 Dec 2002

By Lockdown

Item 2 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 30 Dec 2002, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Scene opens with a shot of a tall red headed bartender wiping off the old looking wooden bar. The bartender, judging by his face seems to be in his mid twenties, but the crown of his head is showing early stages of baldness. The camera zooms out and we can more fully see the room. It is one of those old “corner pub” establishments with dark, old looking wood accents and furniture. The place is empty, except for one old rummy. The light that pours in from the large window tells us that it is likely around midday. The name of the pub is painted in large green, flowing letters. Though the word is backwards on this side of the glass, it can be read: Callahan’s. The door chime rings and in walks Lockdown. Lockdown strides up to the bar, and taking a stool says... Lockdown: Wally! You crazy Irishman, pour me a Seven and Coke!!!! Bartender: Well, well! Long time no see buddy! I’ll pour the drink, but first… well you know there is some other business to take care of. Pops likes to settle these accounts as quickly as possible. Lockdown: reaching into his pocket and retrieving a large wad of cash, sets it on the bar. Yeah, I got the 3 grand right here. That’s actually why I am here. Damn Packers!!! I hate those bastards. I figure they’ll smoke the Jets, so that’s easy money, but NOOOO!!! I mean the Bears beat the Jets for God’s sake!!! Anyway tell Gary he doesn’t have to be sketchy about taking my bets anymore. I got a real job. Bartender: Yeah, I was gonna say something. We saw you on TV. On Fiesta. You sure were putting a hurt on that jackass redneck. But man, I say this as an old friend… you might wanna chill out a little. You already have that Cruz guy pissed at you. I can’t even count how many jobs you’ve had and lost because you can’t get along with management. Sets the drink down in front of Lockdown. Lockdown: What!? I’m not worried about that ESSAY! He can gather all the INFORMACION he wants on me. I hope he gets it all. Then he’ll know just who the f*ck he’s dealing with! Besides, what’s the worst he can do to me? Get me sent back to prison??? Man that’s like sending me back home. And he must know that I’ll do a year or two, tops!!! Better believe when I got out, he’d be hiding in the dark, pissing his pants cause he knows I’m comin with the heater!! Makes the trigger motion with his hand Bartender: Just be careful, man. I hate going to see you in those places. Lockdown: Man don’t worry about it. Cruz has seen my rap sheet. He doesn’t have the COJONES to pull a stunt on me. But there is something else I wanna talk to you about. I need some information. Cruz has me in a tag team match this weekend. You know I don’t like tag matches. We already know Cruz has a beef with me so I’m a little wary about who he has me partnered with. I don’t know much about him, except that he was in EWA near its collapse. His name is Thomas Connors. Goes by the name Leviathan. I heard he spent some time in the joint, but I dunno for sure. Find out all you can. Takes a pen from his pocket and scribbles on a bar napkin. You can reach me at this number til Friday morning. Bartender: You got it. Anything else? Lockdown: Well, since you asked… Tell your sister Megan to come see me!! Bartender: Jackass!! I don’t care how bad you are, you’ll never be badder than my .45! Lockdown: Alright, alright!! It was worth a shot wasn’t it? Well, I gotta run. Here’s for the drink. Lockdown stands and heads for the door. Bartender: What about my tip? Lockdown: Tip? You want a tip? Don’t drink and drive! How’s that for a tip? Just get me that info and I’ll make it worth your while! The door chime rings and Lockdown is gone.
