Dumb and dumber

21 May 2003

By myZery

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The scene opens in a luxurious hotel suite. There is music in the background; it is the sound of new metal blaring from the 700-watt speakers. We look to the ground to see empty beer cans scattered around the floor. The camera continues along the floor until it meets a pair of sexy long tanned legs. It follows the legs up to reveal Hailey standing there with a shirt on. The camera then turns to the couch where myZery is sprawled out on. Jane emerges from the bedroom and walks over to myZery and sits beside him. Jane: Are you ok now? myZery: Yeh I’m feeling a lot better, you and Hailey helped with that last night. Jane: So you had a good night then? myZery: Yeh! My muscles are killing me though. Jane: Ah well, they’ll mend. I think you should exercise them more tonight! Maybe Hailey and me could give you a hand. myZery: Sounds good to me. myZery looks over to Hailey as she smirks at him. myZery: How does that sound Hailey? Hailey: Great! Can’t wait! Hailey smirks again at myZery and struts to the bedroom. She shuts the door behind her and then Jane follows. Jane: Be back in a while babe, I’ve got something to do. myZery: I bet you do. myZery drifts off back to sleep as groans are heard from the bedroom. Scene fades. Hours later the scene opens again and I am just after waking up from a long sleep. I feel refreshed and decide to go out for a walk. I leave the room and march straight thru the lobby and out the door to make sure that I am not stopped. I stand out in the street; the sunlight hits my face and makes me close my eyes. I pull up my hood and begin to jog down the street. The sun is blazing in the sky and the smell of sweat is all over the town. I continue to jog down the street; I stop for a breather and buy a bottle of water. I drink the water down fast as I can feel the sun on my face painted skin. I head over towards the park and do a few laps of the perimeter. It takes me about ten minutes to do each lap. The park is filled with young and old people who are constantly giving me strange looks. I simply ignore them and start to think about what Hailey and Jane are doing back in the apartment. A kid runs up to me and then he stares up at me. Kid: Hey myZery can I have your autograph? I smile at the kid and sign his WWA magazine. There is a picture of me on the front holding up the Gen X title. Kid: Well done in winning the Gen X title. That was a cool match. But what is with them little Showtime’s? myZery: Thanks. And I don’t have a clue. I think he has been watching too much Austin Powers and wanted to be more like Dr. Evil. I will get them little sh**s. (Cough) Simpletons… Kid: Yeh you go kick some ass myZery! The kid runs off into the huge green of the park. I think to myself about that match and wonder how Showtime didn’t end up in hospital. The sun is still cooking the pavements and there should be steam rising from them any minute. I pull down my hood, not hiding who I am. I jog out of the park and back to the hotel. I sprint up the stairs and into my room. Hailey and Jane are making out on the couch when I walk in and I trip up on a bra and a thong while I walk towards the bathroom. I get ready for a shower then get in. as the water falls down and washes the white face paint right off me I hear a knock on the door. “Can we come in?” The two girls strut into the bathroom wearing nothing only a towel. They walk up to the shower and open the door. They drop their towels and get in beside me. Theres no point in trying to explain what happened next. A while later we all get put of the shower and dry off. I get dressed, put on my face paint and walk out of the room once more. This time I slowly walk thru the hotel. There is a group of older teenagers in the lobby. One of them spots me and tells the rest of them. They all stand up and begin to cheer and clap. I get a feeling of delight thru my veins, and it is not that often I get this feeling. I smile over to the gang and bow, messing with them. I walk out the door and get into my Mini Cooper. I drive down to the traffic lights and then turn left towards the WWA arena. I get there a few minutes later and walk in. I enter thru the big glass doors and walk down the corridor to my locker room. I then walk over and pick up my Gen X title from the chair. I sit down on the sofa with the title over my shoulder and a cameraman enters the room. I stare into the camera. “It looks like I’ve got two jackasses to deal with this time around. First I had to face that imbecile Showtime, for this title. Now I have to face James Curzon and Showtime at the same time! Damn it’s like some type of TV appearance. I didn’t know dumb and dumber were going to be competing on Fiesta. I have come to WWA and made sure I’ve made an impact. I have accomplished one of my aims, and that was winning gold here. Showtime, you and no one else will take this title off me. You can try and try your little puny hearts out but the soulless heretic will not fall into the grasps of any of you asses! I have had a hard time trying to fit in all my life and now you two have to try and make life difficult by being put in a Handicap match with me. Showtime, you can tell your little mini-you’s that I am going to kick all of their mini asses then I will come looking for you. I find it funny how on Fiesta, you could still have the energy to laugh in the ring whilst your little gay partners were trying to beat me up. You are most likely the worst wrestler I have ever come across in my whole career. You look like a disowned pimp. No one wants you and I wouldn’t blame them! A whore wouldn’t even get it on with you, those little people that follow you around are more then just some fan club, they represent the proportional size of your c**k to you. And boy is that small. I came up with a new name for you, instead of Showtime, why don’t you call yourself ‘No Show’. If you are wandering why, it is simply because it would make the fans happier if you didn’t show up at all. You are a waste of space and money here at the WWA and the sooner they realize that the better. I cannot wait to get into the ring and totally and utterly kick the living crap out of you. And then when all of your little mini-you’s come down to check if their Gaylord is alright I am going to show them what pain really is. They probably already know that though from you trying to shove your **** up their *****! Anyway enough about you. Curzon, it seems that you happen to be in this little match too. Before you get your hopes up about winning…don’t! I am going to fight to the death if I have to and believe me you will soon realize that at Fiesta! Have you ever been to Japan? Or do you just stay in that little secret hide out? If you think that that guy K2 is going to stop me from totally brutalizing your lousy carcass. I will leave the ring with bloodstains all over it. Once I hit the Traumatic Cerebral Stress, you will not be able to walk out of this arena without a pair of EMT’s carrying you out or a stretcher wheeling you to the god damn hospital! You seem to be a bit gone in the head!? You talk about some conspiracy. What the hell are you on about you paranoid freak. You make me sick dude, people like you should be locked for and the key should be thrown away. I have noticed that a lot of people around here and everywhere else seem to underestimate my ability. Well just for a little tip, I would not do this. It is not suggested, as I will come back and make you pay for your little mistake. You shall all realize this soon enough. The Gen X title is a notch on a ladder of aims. Remember that before you go signing up for matches with me. Well Showtime, Curzon… good luck in the match. Your both going to need it!” I then put my hand up to the camera and it turns to black. Scene Fades. Design and Graphics © World Wrestling Alliance 2002
