It's a mystery, myZery

20 Jun 2003

By Joey K

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Flying into Cedar Rapids, Iowa this evening I realized something. I've been sure of it for so long, everybody else thought it. Problem was, nobody had proof. Proof.....that I am the best. Ok, so I'm not the world champion. I'll leave that to Ian Tense. Afterall, I'm 22 years old, I've got plenty of time ahead of me. What I do have however, is this. This TV title over my shoulder explains to everyone, that I am something to fear, I am the best, I deserve respect and recognition. I understand that now, and I think other people are realizing it too. But one thing I don't understand is...myZery. What are you? You're quiet, you don't seem to WANT to speak, which is a good thing... nobody wants to hear you. You earned your shot at my title. Yopu beat three other men to be able to fight me. I'm hoping you bring your game with you monday night. I don't want it to be a waste of your time. Although.....I have no doubt it will be. This weekend I'm taking afew days off.....to rest, recuperate. I sujest you take that time to train your ass off....study the tapes, KNOW the moves. Because.....you'll need those tactics, I'm bringing everything I got. Unfortunately for you, everything I got has never been seen. How can you study something that has never been witnessed? Never been known. You can't bro, and on Monday Night, you either turn up and fight me, or simply make way... MAKE WAY..FOR JOEY....K! Design and Graphics © World Wrestling Alliance 2002
