V-Day Is Nearly Upon Us

13 Jan 2003

Author not credited in the source

Item 3 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 13 Jan 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Who is V? A mystery, to be sure. Is the V a name? Victor? Vik Ventressa? Villain? Is it a word? Vengeance? Verdict? Victory? Or is the V merely misleading? Could it be Mantis or Shawn Cage? Could it possibly even herald the return of Mr Cool? No, it’s not the above. It’s not Mr Cool, I’m sorry to disappoint you. This is the return of a former multiple Champion. This is a big return. This a very big comeback. V-Day is soon. V-Day is approaching. V-Day is Sunday.
