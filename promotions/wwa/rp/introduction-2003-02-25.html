Introduction

25 Feb 2003

By Brothers DeLucci

Item 4 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 25 Feb 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
9.23pm ..:: The Scene opens to a back alleyway where a shady character wearing a dark long length coat stands waiting. The wind pushes the coat around like leaves in an autumn wind. The last few drops of rain from the night sky brush against the coat and finish thier trip to the floor. The character lights up a cigar leans back against a wall. And looks around::.. ..::Trash, nothing but filthy trash everywhere, the character mutters under his breath he still waits, a trail of smoke leaves his pouting mouth and he looks towards the main road. Many people walk past the alleyway but dont even give this character a second glance, suddenly another character, slightly taller stands in the middle of the alleyway he rushes to the first character tripping on his own footing as he goes::.. ..::The taller man opens up his jacket to show the first man something we cannot see what it is, but it's obviously something important, they both continue down the alleyway further walking side by side. The clip-clop of both men's feet eccho throughout the whole alleyway. The first man stop the second man and twists, he taps on a door::.. ..::A panel opens up and a pair of eyes glare at the two men for a second and then the door opens up, both men enter. The room is small, smoke filled and has very poor lighting. All that is in the room is a small table with a unknown presence behind the table. The lighting is so bad that the shadows only outline the white suit and thick chin of this obviously very important figure::.. Unknown figure from behind the table: You get what I asked for? ..::The second character standing infront of the table unbuttons his jacket and pulls out a brown parcel he tosses it onto the table, almost instantly the first character slaps the second character round the back of the head::.. First Character: 'Ey! Be's careful that's the boss'es private stuff there Second Character: Sorry Tommy, I'll be more careful next time ..:: Tommy smiles gently at the shadowy figure as a huge hand wraps his fingers around the parcel and drags it towards him::.. Unknown figure from behind the table: You's two's have done well for me, so how can I repay you? ..::The second figure almost instantly puts his finger up, but at the same time Tommy slaps the head of the second figure::.. Tommy: Shudd'up your face Tony, boss doesn't need to hear your babbling, look we need some money to start up a new business venture. Unknown figure from behind the table: What kind of business venture? Tommy: The entertainment industry, well ya' see boss things have been going pretty slow around here, know what I mean, so I thought we need to get somewhere that's going places. Unknown figure from behind the table: The entertainment industry? Tommy: Sure, the wrestling industry to be more's precise boss' we's figured Unknown figure from behind the table: You's figured what? Tommy: We just need fifty g's to get us started getting us the proper training and stuff ya' know what I mean. We's figured that we's could earn some money whilst beating some heads down, see Tony likes the idea of it Tony: *Laughing slow and deep* I do's, I do's ..::Tommy slaps the back of Tony's head again::.. Tommy: Shudd'up whats-a-matter with your face? Tony: Sorry Tommy ..::A finger from the shadows emerges calling for tommy to come forward, Tommy leans further and further forward over the table, the finger dissapears and is replaced by a hand who shoves the head of Tommy right down onto the table nose first. Tommy instantly leaps up::.. Tommy: 'Ey what-the-hell-is-wrong with you's Unknown figure from behind the table: Your mother would turn in her grave if she heard you talk about all this wrestling crap. Now's get back to work Tommy: No! No! Listen out for me a second boss, we found this real easy egg to get into. It's called the World Wrestling Alliance. And here's the best bit of news they are's crying out for new talent. Like me and Tony here. We go there wrestle for a bit and were in the money, those guy's are on like fifty g's a month, me and Tony can pay you back every penny boss. ..::There is a long silence as smoke emits from the shadowy figure he chuckles for a bit and then another brown parcels is places on the table and pushed accross::.. Unknown figure from behind the table: Here! Here is your fifty g. Take it and use it, I want you to pay it back to me in 2 months ya here, and don't you guys screw things up like you usualy do ok. Tommy: Look you's wont regret's this boss. They only have like 4 good teams in the federation before we know it we'll over-run the whole entire place. Tony: But what about... ..::Tommy slaps Tony around the head silencing him::.. Tommy: I know, I know Havoc and Veteranz, come'on ya big's goof lets take this money and see what kind of help we can get for getting into the WWA. Unknown figure from behind the table: Hey, boy's I know a contact in the industry. Tell ya what, I'll give him a call and see what I can arrange you two just sit tight and I'll call you in a day or two. Tommy: Thankyou boss, you have no idea what this means to me. ..::Tony whimpers::.. Tommy: Us, what this means to us. Lets go. ..::Tommy and Tony leave the small room and are back in the alleyway again, the rain has started to pick back up again and rests on the hair of Tommy and shoulders of Tony, Tommy then turns to his partner::.. Tommy: Tony, the DeLucci brothers are going to the WWA and I can't wait Tony: Nor can... ..::Slapped already::.. Tommy: Sudd'up your face! Lets go goofball ..::Both men walk down the alleyway and turn off into the main street the camera looks up at the night sky and a building that has the time on it, the counter changes as the camera fades to black::.. 9:34pm
