Rough with the smooth

12 Feb 2003

By Wes Sanders

Item 5 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 12 Feb 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene opens up to a small quaint park with a pond and benches surrounding it, the air is clean and fresh and the clouds are out in their minority. Sun beams down and feeds the grass with its natural sunlight, and birds sing echoing through the bark of the old willow trees, which surround and keeps the parks privacy. The camera walks through the park looking around for something or somebody, it stops as we see a few kids playing catch, the camera zooms through the kids and focuses on one man sitting down cross legged hands in lap.) (The camera walks through getting closer and closer to the figure who is clothed in a white t-shirt and black jeans, his hair tied back in a pony tail except for the few stands which have broke free and invade his face. Any WWA fan would tell you instantly that sitting down right there was WWA rookie legend Wes Sanders. A small boy approaches Wes and taps him on the shoulder. Wes opens his left eye to see the small boy who twists his body back and forth looking innocent.) (Wes smiles as the boy speaks in his high pitch squeaky voice.) Boy: I saw you fight the other day… Wes: And how old are you? *Asked Wes inquisitively* Boy: SEVEN! *The boy pronounced with great delight* (Wes smiled and closes his eyes once again to go back to his meditation, the young boy relentless in his annoyance request, pokes Wes again in the side of the head, this obviously breaks Wes’ concentration and he adopts a more relaxed position.) Wes: So, what can I do for ya’ Boy: I saw you fight… Wes: Uh huh, and who was I fighting? Boy: A bad man… (Wes scratches his chin) Wes: Uh huuuh, that narrows it down, did this bad man have a name? Boy: Yeah… (There is a moment where Wes just looks at the boy, expecting an answer.) Wes: And… it would be… Boy: Ummm, its (Just then the boys mother calls him back he charges back like Michael Johnson and gives his mother a huge hug, the camera pans back onto the blank face of Wes, who just shrugs and goes back to his meditation, his biceps tense greatly and he grips his knees slightly digging into the skin. Wes controls his breathing constantly on top of things. He then pauses and stops, gets to his feet and rubs his side ribs.) Wes: Damn still sore (He lifts his T-Shirt and the bandages a tightly wrapped against his ribs, a bruise runs down the middle of his body all the way down to his belly button, he runs his finger down gritting his teeth as he does so.) Wes: I’m guessing you’re here for the interview (Wes doesn’t actually face the camera in-fact his back is turned to the camera, the cameraman is startled.) Cameraman: Er, well I was told just to film you for a while, you don’t usually like to talk that much Wes: True… (Wes sits back down, and crosses his legs again he sits eyes looking just beyond the camera lens, he then perks up.) Wes: So what questions do you want to ask me? Cameraman: Er, I don’t usually ask questions, I just film Wes: Aww come on I know you got some journalistic spirit with you… (The camera shakes around a bit as the cameraman adjusts his position to fit with Wes, the camera steady so do the mans words.) Cameraman: Well ok but don’t go blaming me if anything goes wrong, the names John Wes: Ok John shoot John: Are you planning anything for next weeks meltdown, its says on the WWA website that you are due backstage Wes: I will be on Meltdown doing what though is anyone’s guess I just turn up and see what happens, I see Ian Tense is supposed to be having a world title fight with somebody but I am uncertain who its against. John: Are you worried that Ian might lose? Wes: Not really it’s not about titles for me its about getting back at Ian, trying to prove to myself and the fans that no opponent is bigger than me, no opponent is invincible against me and my attacks. John: How’s the healing going at the moment Wes: Well I still have some heavy bruising on my ribs which is a real pain in the ass but the only real bad thing is I can only do light training at the moment and my physical strength is deteriorating. But I should be able to get back into action within a week or so. John: Any thoughts on the Vik Ventressa return again? Wes: Well he’s a great athlete, whom you have to have respect for, I like that kind of you ended my career I’m going to end your attitude he has against Jackson Kraven. Besides it takes a little pressure off my back. The fans really expected me to take on Kraven, Tense, and Thorne, all of those real big guys. I know I could cause some damage but I couldn’t handle all 3 look how I struggle just against the one of them. John: But doesn’t Kash handle Damian Thorne? Wes: Ahh, Kash, well he’s a great superstar, not really got his act together bit of a strange one, but he gives you 100% all the time which is something I really like about him, I remember when me and him teamed up. It was strange at first and I gave him a hard time. I was under a lot of pressure with Ian Tense being around and the House of Pain dominating everything. (Wes pauses for breath) Wes: But I’m sure if I teamed up with him in the future we would really kick some ass, he got one of those physiques that could take on almost anyone. John: And what are your thoughts on Damian Thorne? Wes: He is someone that I dislike, he has the leadership ability and the qualities of a great wrestler but his attitude needs to change towards people otherwise one day he’s going to end up all alone like one Ian Tense. You can’t go against his skills because they are second to none but the one thing that stops me and him fighting, is mind set. He is going about things all the wrong way. He could do so much more… But I wont go into any more details about that. John: Ok fair point, so last week on Meltdown, you were booked to see Ian Tense, he actually offered you some respect, don’t you think that was a little weird? Wes: A little I guess but Ian can be your best friend at times and worst enemy at others. Right now if he is offering that respect I will take it, but I am still going to call that match in 2 weeks time when I am fully healed and it will be another match for the ages. John: If you don’t beat him this time, don’t you think it’s time to move on and take other roads Wes: It’s a possibility, I’ve heard the backlash of Ian vs Wes again, people can get bored easily but remember its going to be Kash vs Damian again. Havoc against the Veteranz things like this happen. I am against Ian because I have some issues with him that need sorting out, and the only way they can be sorted is in the ring. John: So you think I got the interviewing technique? Wes: Well your not Chris Crenshaw but that isn’t bad for your first time. Stick with the cameraman work for a while I’m sure that chance will come along soon. John: So any final words for this Monday Wes: Yeah I got a couple, Ian you know I am not ready for that match yet but when the time comes and I am ready, you best be ready for another huge blast because that is what it is going to be noting more and nothing less. (The camera starts fading to black and we hear the echoing of John talking to Wes about “Who was holding onto your foot at Rage in the Cage” The scene then complete darkens.)
