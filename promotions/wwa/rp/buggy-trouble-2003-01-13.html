Buggy Trouble

13 Jan 2003

By Extreme Corp

Item 1 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 13 Jan 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Scene – Outside a nondescript garage. Voices can be heard from within) Voice – “Ok...step sixteen says use the wrench to tighten that bolt there and that should have everything fixed!” (The view of the camera enters the garage where the Extreme Corporations’ “EXTREME MOBILE” can be seen. A black golf cart loaded with assorted plunder is being worked upon from underneath by Mal Somers. Standing beside the vehicle, reading from a “GOLF CART SERVICE MANUAL” is none other than Dave “The Extreme Machine” Harley. He closes the book and puts it down.) Harley – “How's that coming along Mal? We haven’t got all day. Man that was SWEET! Last Saturday night at Fiesta, debuting the Extreme Mobile, running down those CELL morons, leaving them laying...That felt great! A few minor repaors to the Extreme Mobile and it’ll be back ready for action!” Somers – (frustrated) “Are you sure there isn’t more to step sixteen? It doesn’t seem secure!” Harley – (looks at closed book, and shrugs) “Yup that’s it. Just tighten that bolt and that’s it. Hurry up!” (Somers grunts as he tightens the bolt one last time and goes to roll out from uder the buggy) CLUNK! Somers – “OUCH!” Harley – “What was that? You done yet?” Somers – “No I am NOT done! The battery just fell on my head.” (Harley rolls his eyes and opens the book just as Somers rolls out and stands up.) Harley – “Oh yeah. Don’t forget to make sure the battery is secured. How’d you miss that?” (Somers sighs and wipes the sweat from his brow, feeling a nasty bump forming. He pours himself a glass of water.) Somers - “Anyways, have you checked to see if we are scheduled for any matches this week?" Harley – “I just got off the phone to Jill. Looks like that Gringo Cruz isnt booking us in any matches AGAIN this week at Fiesta. I mean we debuted the Extreme Mobile, we laid out his henchmen, we even debuted our Extreme Corporation theme song, and NOTHING!" (grinning) Do you think we’ve managed to pi$$ him off? Somers – (smiling) “I hope so. But this is so counter productive. I mean I am the WWA ROOKIE CHAMPION! The MALcoholics out there deserve to see me in action (hesitates momentarily)...and you too Dave! What are we gonna do? Maybe we should meet with Cruz and the Cell and bury the hatchet.” Harley – “YEAH! Right between their eyes!” (Both members of the Extreme Corporation laugh) Harley – “I’ve got a better idea. I say at Fiesta we drive the Extreme Mobile up to ringside and stage a sit in. We will not move an inch until Cruz gives us a match!” Somers – “Sounds good. (hands Dave a wrench) Now YOU get this thing working!” (Fade Out)
