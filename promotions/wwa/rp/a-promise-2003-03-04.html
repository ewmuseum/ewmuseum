A promise

4 Mar 2003

By James Beam

Item 4 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 4 Mar 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene opens to a small room filled with cheap workout equipment. There is a universal machine in the middle with a treadmill to one side and an in-place bicycle to the other. There is a TV in the corner of the room, about 8 feet in the air. Nobody is in the room, at this time, however. A few seconds pass and you can hear voices muffled from outside the room. Then the door bursts open, with Rebecca and James entering just behind. Rebecca has a towel around, as if she had just showered and James is in his workout gear.) Rebecca: Well, congrats at any rate Jim. I knew if you stuck to your guns you would be able to rise above it all and become Champion. It will be a good victory. (James grimmaces at the confidence of his lover, but he manages a smile nonetheless.) James: I still need to beat him Bec. I still need to go out there and beat Zion for his titles. Rebecca: I have no doubt in my mind Jim. There is no way you will lose. Besides (laughs) You've already lost 2 times this month. You are due to win. (She laughs louder and give James a wink. She walks off towards the other door. James knows she is just kidding with him and he forces a smile.) Rebecca: I am gonna go tan, then go for a swim. So I will see you in about an hour? Will that give you enough time? James (not really noticing): Sure... (James is definately thinking about something other than how long he will be working out this evening. Rebecca shrugs and leaves James to himself. James sits down on the bench press bench and puts his hands in his face.) James: This is what I have been working my butt off for. A Title shot. Now I have a shot at two titles in one match. Against someone that I have never had the priveledge to wrestle before. Someone that has my respect if nothing else. I see, Jimmi, that you are going through some difficult times with the Cell. (James shrugs) I am having equal difficulty shedding the mediocrity of the likes of Showtime and Curzon. They seem to be bringing me down to the depths with them. But I finally have the chance to show the other wrestlers and more to the point, the fans, that I am above and beyond them. I have a chance to show them, Jimmi, that I am above and beyond you as well. These titles are my destiny, they are what I have worked for all of my life to attain. You have been to the top of the Light Heavies for long enough. You have stolen valuable airtime from the fans of the WWA for more than your share. Your 15 minutes are up Mr Zinn. THAT is my promise to you. I have only begun my climb to the top of this great federation and you are simply the first rung in the long ladder of success. (James smiles finally, after being very serious.) James: One thing is for sure, Zion... You are looking a little to far down the road for my tastes. You are assuming you will beat me, you are looking to make me an example of what you will be doing to the cell. (James forces a laugh, then gets a little more serious.) James: I will be nobody's example, Zion! Unless you are a youngster looking to attain their potential. But, you have reached your potential, now go back to the trailer park and..... (James stops in his tracks and clears his throat. He looks a little embarassed about his tirade.) James: I am promising you one thing Zion. One thing only. I am going to Hot-Lanta and I am going to give you the best match you have EVER had. You will be taken to every limit. You will be taken to the most EXTREME, something you could never phatom before I get in the ring. Jimmi Zion.... I am taking it ALL to the ring and I am leaving it all there smack dab on top of your head! It is my time to live MY dream! Because we ALL.... DIE.... YOUNG!!!! (Fade)
