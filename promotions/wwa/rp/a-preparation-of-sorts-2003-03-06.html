A preparation of sorts

6 Mar 2003

By Vetz

Item 2 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 6 Mar 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The Veteranz are sat in a tent. Trapper is bandaged up and lying down on the floor. He still looks exhausted from his 2 week long ordeal at the hands of Havoc. Shrapnel is sat next to him, reading a book.) JS: Here is Jane, here is John. See Jane run, see John... HT: Shut-up Johnny, I'm trying to think. (Johnny looks a little offended but closes his book and puts it into a rucksack. Johnny folds his arms and stares at Trapper.) JS: What's the plan then? HT: That plan? JS: We're up against Havoc, again and Jackson Kraven, again! HT: Well, Kraven is big but we know him, we know all his tricks. He is dangerous but we beat him before and we'll do it again. (Johnny takes a large salami sausage out of his pocket and take a bite.) HT: Havoc are also big and dangerous and we've never beaten them! JS: Beaten who? HT: Havoc! We've never beaten Havoc! JS: Who is Havoc? Have I met him? HT: We are wrestling them on monday nigh, they think they are Gods, got a bit of a odour problem. JS: You mean Denrol! HT: No, I mean Havoc. JS: Why didn't you just say that then? (Trapper shakes his head. JOhnny bites a bit more off his salami.) HT: Anyway we can beat them, we have to beat them! JS: Beat who? HT: I just told you! JS: Oh yeah! HT: So you remeber? JS: Yeah, we are going to beat Denrol. HT: Not Denrol, Havoc. JS: Who's Denrol Havoc? HT: No, I mean, dear god, what am I doing? JS: You are lying in a tent talking to me! HT: Thank-you. (Johnny stuffs the rest of the salami into his mouth. He starts to chew noisely.) HT: It's going to be tough for us, I'm still injured, you may well forget what's going on, we just have to hope that Vik is well prepared! JS: Mmmphs? HT: Yeah, that's right. JS: Mmmphl phugt mmsst? HT: That would definitely be good! JS: Mmpahs snmmpult! HT: Hahahahaha! That's the funniest thing! Hahahaha! (Johnny finally finishes his salami. Trapper sits up and turns to his partner.) HT: Johnny, we are the greatest team in WWA history and yet Havoc are whupping our asses all over the shop. We need to stop the rot, we need to stop Havoc. If we can't do it, who will? (Johnny thinks for a moment.) JS: Spiderman? (Trapper sighs and lies back down.) HT: I think you have 3 little words to say to the people at home. JS: I love you! HT: The other three! JS: Viva la revolution (Fade to Black)
