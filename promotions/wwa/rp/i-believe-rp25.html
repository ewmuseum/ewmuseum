I Believe

18 May 2003

By Leviathan

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
**Leviathan, Thomas Connor stands on a cliff edge. The jagged face continues to be awash by the angry sea. He stands back from the edge wearing casual white pants and t-shirt. The wind lashes Leviathan.** Phobos believe what you want. What was it you said "stop snatching at scraps, when you should be hunting like sharks". Is this suppose to hurt me or snap my mind. Belittle me into a frenzy. This won't work. The path we have chosen has been unpredictable yet fruitful. Our work bringing us the attention of those with power. Our Chaos does not flow evenly thus the reasons for our actions can never be seen or planned for. **Leviathan takes a step closer to the edge. A mere two feet from assured death.** "It’s time to take this seriously". I am my dear godling, as I have always against you and your brother. We have step in the ring a few times now and each time it grows. The fire inside. Ask yourself what does it mean to a team that defeats Havoc. Nothing but immortality, to achieve what most teams fear to even talk about. **Another step towards the edge.** I fear Tommy, you are already broken before you enter the ring. Dave Harley, the broken down member of X-corp is not in this match. For you step in against the god of Panic and myself. You have to be afraid Tommy as your only option, if you dare to enter, is to be brutalised. A victim of circumstance. **Leviathan stands now on edge of the cliff. He pauses and produces a tape recorder out of his pocket and presses play.** """I will hurt you badly. My brother and I have cut a swath of destruction through the WWA, much like you are trying to do. We retired the Human Condition, by snapping their bodies like twigs. We crushed Extreme Impact so horribly that they will never step foot in another ring. And those are only a couple of examples that you should learn from, Thomas. They made the cardinal mistake. They didn’t fight against us as though their very lives were in danger.""" **Leviathan pauses the tape** Phobos you know we fight. The Cell fights not for self satisfying glory. We don't enter that ring to win, we enter to dominate and destroy. Victory is welcomed but brutality is enjoyed. These other teams you mentioned were soft and feared you before they entered the ring. We do not and have shown that is so. Danger, we know more of danger than most teams. Lockdown has survived time serve, knowing each corner is a concealed danger. **Leviathan presses play again.** """For your own sake, be prepared to do anything that you have to in order to survive this ordeal. I stand over seven feet tall. I am stronger than you, I am more crafty than you, and I am more dangerous than you.""" **Leviathan laughs then tosses the recorder over the cliff plunging into a watery grave.** I won't dull your senses with "size doesn't matter" or "the bigger they are, the harder they fall. I see the truth every time we enter the ring with Havoc. A truth that words can't hide. I see fear and panic before me each time and I do not do either. **Leviathan opens his arms and embraces the wind battering his body.** Am I ready to put it all on the line. Phobos I believe I am.
