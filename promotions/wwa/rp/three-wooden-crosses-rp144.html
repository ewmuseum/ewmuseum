Three Wooden Crosses

13 Jun 2003

By Enigma

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
{The scene opens outside of a building. We see the side of the road and there are three wooden crosses that are erect on the side of the road. We see Enigma walk into the seen and stand beside them.} Three crosses Three men Three men’s hope Hope to beat me Hope dies {Nicholas Adams face flashes on the screen} Young Choke Artist Rainmaker Beatable {Dave Harley’s face flash across the screen} Joke Former Champion Beatable {Grunge’s face flashes across the screen} Champion Annoying Beatable {We flash back to Enigma and the three crosses} “There are three wooden crosses on the side of the road for my opponents in my upcoming match on Meltdown. No, it does not represent their actual deaths, far from it. The crosses to represent the death or something, they represent the death of my opponents’ hope in beating me. That’s the way I feel about it.” “As for underestimating David Harley, I understand that he used to be a tough man, but he has gotten soft. I would never underestimate an opponent but Harley is soft. He is a joke. He is a common man. He is going to lose and there is no about it.” “The time is winding down, the time for talk has ended. It is time for action. On Monday I will beat you three men in the ring and there is no denying the fact that you cannot beat me.” “Grunge, you are right about one thing, those who have not gotten to the graces cannot fall from it. Have you reached grace Kenneth? If you have, then be prepared to fall from it, just like Grunge.” NOTHING CAN STOP ME NOW {fade to ?}
