Fireman

21 Oct 2005

By Ramone

Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene opens with Ramone just sitting in his computer room looking at the WWA website, researching his opponent. “The Executioner” Donovan Carter, another one of the WWA’s recent signings. This week on Meltdown will be Carter’s and Ramone’s debut match and for Ramone, his first appearance on television.) R: “Hurry up computer!” (The connection on his computer is interrupted and he is beginning to get angry. He finally comes to Donovan’s roster page and scan’s it.) R: “The Executioner Donovan Carter. Six feet four inches, two-hundred and forty-five pounds, from Miami, Florida. Great. They stick me in my first match against him. Why so small? Well, this ought to be one of the most embarrassing moments for this poor little boy. Eighteen years old with his first assignment in his career to be against me? The odds of him winning is like Bin Laden being found. I hope he has no hard feelings after this match. I suppose he’ll be alright seeing he gets to be the first step in an unforgettable career. Imagine, when I get that world title belt and I’m making my way down the ramp and to the ring, he’ll be the first person on there as the first win in the amazingly undefeated record of mine. Well, think of it like this Donnie boy, you are undefeated now. So, it will go down in history that there was always a time that you were undefeated and it just so happens that you lost to the man who makes his career seem unreal as he continues plowing his way to the top. You’ll go down in history!” (He spins around in his chair and rises, heading towards the kitchen, dressed in his usual street apparel. He peeps in the refrigerator and has a surprisingly look come over his face. Ramone then grabs his keys.) R: “Baby, you want something?” Brittany: “No…well, you can bring me back something to drink?” (Brittany Haggan, Ramone’s ex-girlfriend. She is staying the night at his house because her parents are acting in her words, “gay”. She is a very educated, very sexy twenty year old woman. She is currently in school to become a doctor, Ramone’s chosen profession before his grandmother, JJ, died.) R: “What do you want?” B: “Where you going’?” R: “McDonalds.” B: “Should you be eating that when you have this match on Monday?” R: “Woman, what threat could this Carter character possibly be? I’ve heard nothing from this cat since he’s been in the WWA. I think he just wanted to be able to say that he was once working in the same place as yours truly but now tell me what you want?” B: “Uh huh. Because you’re sooo special. But, bring me back a strawberry shake, and regular strawberries. We’ll need some later for your ‘pre-match warm-up’.” R: “Well…will do.” (He leaves and gets in his car. He drives a black & green chameleon painted Mercedes Benz 745 on chrome twenties. He pulls out the driveway as Brittany watches him burnout.) R: “It’s sad though. I was really hoping for an exciting night. I mean it’s my first meltdown, my first match, and the beginning of what will make many people do what you do after what I’m going to do with Brittany tonight when it’s over. But, wonderful things will happen tonight. It is destined to happen. There is a lot going on tonight around the WWA. Especially for the newly recruited. We all have matches. Donovan and I, Kahn and Anderson, and Samuelsson and Dunn. I wonder who will continue their legacy with me and show the world that their just as good as I am? Oh that’s right? Nobody.” (Ramone arrives at the McDonalds and places his orders, pays, gets food, and leaves. His phone rings and checks it, it’s his mother.) R: “Mom? Oh hell naw! Not about to f**k my mood up before I return home with this food. Sh*t, forgot the strawberries.” (The first grocery store he seen he immediately turned in to it. Went in and came out within thirty seconds and began making his way home again.) R: “Final thoughts are…I am driven on competition and this doesn’t fit into that category so therefore, this match will not be my only contribution to next week’s show. It can’t. Too boring. And lastly, for my buddy Donovan, like Lil’ Wayne, I'm the fireman, fire, fa, fireman. I got that fire I'm hollering I got that fire come and try me and you can spark it up and I'ma put you out. I said you can spark it up and I'ma put you out. Hot! Ha ha!” (Ramone arrives at home, pulls in the driveway and as he approaches the front door to walk in, Brittany opens the door in a silk robe, lingerie shorts and a bra. She begins to pull him in as he lets loose a very wide smile on his face…) R: “Don’t worry, I got em’!” (The scene fades as the door closes.)
