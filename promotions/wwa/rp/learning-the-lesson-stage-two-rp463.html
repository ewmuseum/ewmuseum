Learning The Lesson - Stage Two

4 Sep 2003

By Dave Harley

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
LESSON TWO: SKY FLYIN’ [There is a low humming noise. The voices of Bridge and Dave Harley are barely audible over it. We see Dave and Jack sat facing each other. Both men are wearing helmets and some kind of backpack. Did I not mention where we are? Better leave it to the guys then…] Harley: Umm, Jack? Bridge: Yeah? Harley: It’s really nice of you to have booked these flying lessons for me, but don’t you think we’re just a little far out? I mean, look out of that window…there’s no land for as far as the eye can see. Now I’m no geographicer but I’d guess that we’re out in the middle of the Atlantic right about now. Bridge: That’s right Dave. Harley: Now, the thing is…I mean, I don’t mind flying. By that I mean I’m no Mr T, but I get the feeling that you haven’t brought me all this way just for a joyride. Bridge: Nope. Harley: Then, um…why…why are we flying over the Atlantic in a rickety old plane? [Jack’s stony expression seems to indicate the worst.] Harley: …you can’t be serious! Bridge: Dave, the key to beating Sky Flyin’ Jimmi Zion in the Ladder Match is simple. Harley: I know! Climb a freakin’ ladder! I can do that! Bridge: Which is exactly why I’m not training you to climb a ladder! To be beat Sky Flyin’ Jimmi Zion, you have to be Sky Flyin’ Dave Harley! [Dave gulps. His hand instinctively goes up to his eyebrows – or what’s left of them. The remnants of the last lesson he learnt about this Gauntlet Match. Bridge gets up and walks over to the door, pulling it open. He picks Dave up by the scruff of the collar and marches him over to the door.] Harley: Wait! Can’t we talk about this?! Bridge: Jump Dave. Harley: [Whimpering] What did I ever do to you? Bridge: Well, you know when you invite yourself around to my apartment… Harley: Yeah. I drink all the beer? Bridge: After that. Harley: I eat all your food? Bridge: After that. Harley: I make a few long distance calls? Bridge: After that. Harley: … Bridge: You use my toilet… Harley: … Bridge: …and you leave the toilet seat up!!! Harley: NOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!! [Dave Harley plummets down to the ocean below as Bridge merely turns around and shuts the door behind him, drowning out the pathetic screams of Dave.] Bridge: Well, I’m glad I’ve got that off my chest! Pilot: Aren’t you skydiving yourself, Mr Knight? Bridge: Are you kidding? There’s a thunderstorm out there! I could be killed! [We fade out as we hear lightning strike somewhere outside…funny, I could have sworn I heard Dave Harley let out a scream…] ONE MAN THREE CHALLENGERS THREE DEGREES OF EXTREME WWA LABOUR DAY BRAWL 2003
