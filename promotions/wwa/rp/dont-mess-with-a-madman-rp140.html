Dont mess with a Madman

13 Jun 2003

By "The Psycho" Steven Murphy

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The Camera shows a black wall, with some graffiti on it that is quite hard to read. Suddenly, a quite down and out Psycho Steven Murphy steps into scene, and looks into the camera.) Psycho: Well, first off, I would like to apologise to my opponents in my match. I would like to apologise for my behaviour in my last appearance. I am embarrassed, and I feel a little stupid on the way I acted. Its just I get a little emotional when people like you Vice get to me. Because wrestling gets to me, Vice, as it is my life. It has been my livelihood since I was 16 years old. So for me to come out on a show like WWA Insider, and badmouth some wrestlers-well, im sorry. But, I think to understand what is going on inside my little mind, you have to understand where I have come from and what my goals where when I first come into wrestling. (The Psycho takes a drink from his bottle of water, as he looks emotionally at the ground, with his eyes full of hate and anger. He continues to talk.) Psycho: You see, back in 1989, there was a program called Watchdog, and it challenged the wrestling industry, and it kind of portrayed it in a negative light. Vice, if your listening, try to understand that I was a big wrestling fan, and for me to stand in front of the television set and see people running down a business that I loved-even though I knew little about it…To see my mother, drunk as ever, waving her Jack Daniels at me saying “That’s what you want to be when your older?” That night I went to bed not with visions of chocolate land, but of broken bones, of battered bodies and bloody corpses, saying to myself, “If it is the last thing I do, if I have to hold myself up for a human sacrifice-the world will respect professional wrestling.” (Psycho then gets a surprised look on his face.) Psycho: Oh, and that dream is slowly starting to come true, yes I have sacrificed myself for the past 11 years, leaving the better parts of my past lying on concrete floors from the sh*tholes in Liverpool to some of the greatest arenas in USA, to right in the middle of the WWA arena when winning then European gold. And what’s it really done? Where have we really come to? (Psycho then slowly changed his expression from surprised, to hate and pain. He looked painfully down at the ground.) Psycho: Lying in a hospital bed in Wiston, Liverpool-seeing the doctors stitch up my slashed eye, trying to redeem what was left of the eyeball. And after what was seemingly hours, I was about to fall back to painful sleep, when one of the nurses in the room said this. “I thought it was all fake?” Excuse me! I didn’t know you opened up a diseased lung of a smoker and said, “Oh, by golly, I thought smoking was supposed to be good for you!” Do you open my mother’s non-functioning liver and say, “Hey, I didn’t know that four decades of heavy drinking took this kind of toll?!” So, if they show that much respect for other patients, what made me any different? Because I was a wrestler. And professional wrestling will never be respected, no matter how many teeth I lose, no matter how many eyes I lose, no matter how many brain cells have to die. And so, it comes down to the point where it’s just not worth it. Its not worth it, and, Christopher Vice, you’ve got to start looking at this realistically. (Psycho then looks straight into the camera, and throws the water on the ground.) Psycho: Wrestling is a way to make a living-nothing more and nothing less-and as long as it’s strictly business, well; you may as well be going up to the worst federation out there, into their fans welcoming arms. Because the WWA fans will be the death of you, Vice. You see, they realised, and they were smarter than any of us, that they rule WWA-not Warr or Harrington. What happened, Vice? You come out from no-where and start trash talking with your mocking ways. Did Warr hand you the camera, and say “Make a fool of Psycho for me.” You’re a disgrace to the profession, Vice, and you’re a damn fool. And now, I can’t sit back and take it, because ive got a moral obligation. Chris, try to understand, I am a failed experiment in the wrestling world, and I accept that. But never in my sickest dreams did I imagine that there would be other wrestlers taking shots with Barbed wire because I busted Chuckster up in EWA. Im the patron saint to all the sick sumbitches in EWA. Is that all I stand form Vice? When I see you mocking me with your stupid words, I get anger building in me. Well, ya know, F*ck you. Who the hell do you think you are? We are not a wrestling organisation-we’re the worlds biggest puppet show. (Psycho look incensed now.) Psycho: Ill be damned if I walk into the arena and the let the sh*thead fans call my match. Jump on the Concrete! Fall on the floor! Well not me, im nobodies stooge, and Christopher Vice, if you had a little bit of pride, or a little bit of common sense, you’d understand that those people don’t love you-they laugh at you! You trash talk on insider, and they look and laugh in your face. And to think I even tried to reply with trash talk myself. I deserve to die a terrible, painful death just for that. And as I go to bed tonight, im not sure where im going to spend my eternity. And you, Vice, are my salvation. Because, by delivering you a beating on Fiesta, you will be adored by the fans for real this time, as they will hold the little amount of respect for you to even stand up to me. And with that, there even may be a chance for me too. Please Vice, for my sake, think it over even turning up to our match, as a no would make Steven Murphy a happier man, yet a yes, I will be going to the match puts a nail into the coffin for me, Vice, for my eternal damnation. Im counting on you, you selfish pr*ck. don’t make me hurt you, because I can. Don’t make me do it, because if I do, with god as my witness, it wont be in front of the WWA sh*thead fans in the Tennessee arena; it will just be you and me. And you wont know when its coming, or know where. So unless you want to damn me to the depths of hell-answer my call and say, “OK, I won’t turn up to the match.” Because if you don’t, the only other option is… (Psycho looks down for a moment, and he looks back up, with hate in his demonic eyes.) Psycho: You will become Just Another Victim of the Psycho!! (Psycho walks away from the wall, as the scene fades with the graffiti on the wall, and as it got closer, it read “Don’t mess with a Madman”)
