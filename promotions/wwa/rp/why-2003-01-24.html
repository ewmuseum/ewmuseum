WWA (1997–2019) Roleplays

Why?

24 Jan 2003

By Kash

Item 11 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 24 Jan 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(A figure, hunched in the corner, knees to the chin, head bowed. The right arm of the figure is wrapped around the legs, trying to create at least some minor form of warmth and feeling in the cold and empty body sitting there. As the light from a solitary car passes outside, we see the glimmer of a metallic object in the left hand. A razor blade. This simple solitary item that takes on a whole new meaning when its in the hands of a man as you see before you now) How can this be fair? How can this be right? (The figure twists the razor blade in his hand, flicking it between his fingers like an ordinary man would flick a coin. The light catches the razor blade as it flicks and reflects small beams of light onto the sullen face of the hunched figure, and it is a face we recognise.) How does everyone do it? How do you all go on with your lives, living this way? (The razor blade flicks between the fingers and is then brought to a sudden stop between the thumb and fore finger, the sharp edges standing out starkly, illuminated by the glare of outside headlights.) All these emotions, all this pain, how does each day end for you? Asleep in your beds, or crying down the phone. (The tortured and desperate sound in the mans voice is obvious to anyone with half a brain. The strangled sounds of a suppressed tear well in the man’s throat and the long laboured breathing indicate a man struggling to keep his emotions in check.) All this time, who was I kidding. Jokes, smiles, what was I trying to prove? That I was normal? That I belonged with them? What sort of perverse game was I playing with myself, what sort of mess was I getting myself into? I wish I knew the answers to these questions, I really do! Suddenly, the silence of the room is shattered by the ringing of a mobile phone which rings forth with the theme from Halloween. The phone is hanging by its charger off the side of a table, and despite being upside down, we can see the callers name. ~T calling The phone rings solidly for a good three minutes before it is wrenched from the charger and slung across the room, smashing in pieces on the far wall. Sorry babe, not tonight, or ever again. Its over, forget Thorne, forget the House of Pain, forget life!
