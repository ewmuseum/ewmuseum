From the heart

1 Jan 2007

By Ry "One Man Show" Ballard

Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Apologize: One of the most loathsome words in the English language. It is a simple fact of human nature that no one likes to be wrong, and even further it can leave a vile, bitter taste in one's mouth to actually admit being wrong to the world. It's the very reason the phrase "It takes a big man to admit when he's wrong" even exists. It isn't fun, it isn't pleasureable, and sometimes it's like taking a gerbil up your rectum...it just hurts. But, like so many things in life (not so much the gerbil in this case) it's necessary. Basic survival 101 states "admit when you're wrong or face your demise at the hands of people determined to show you how wrong you are." Sometimes, most times, an apology isn't enough. But... it's a start. -The shot opens on a cloudless sky. The sun is shining, birds are chirping, it's a surprisingly pleasant day for the beginning of January. Though the pleasant feeling one gets from such a day is tempered somewhat as the camera pans down and we see we're in a cemetery. A familar cemetery. One that virtually all WWA fans have visited before. The name on the tombstone is familiar as well: Lillian White . And continuing the trend of familiarity there is a familiar face standing next to the tombstone. The "One Man Show" Ry Ballard. And he has a shovel. Dear God in heaven he has a shovel.- Ry Ballard: The sound I'm sure you all just heard is the sound of Jevon White's tires squealing on the pavement as he flies towards my location. -Ry looks thoughtful for a moment.- Ballard: Can't say I blame him. If a guy I've had problems with was standing next to my wife's grave with one of these (he holds up the shovel) I'd hit the ground running too. But, believe it or not, and I realize that no one has reason to believe anything that comes out of my mouth, I mean no harm. This shovel is no more than a gardening tool. -Ry crouches down next to some flowers arranged in a small triangle shape. Freshly planted flowers, right in front of the stone. He brushes a finger against the delicate petals. They're the color of the sky, with a darker blue hue around the edges.- Ballard: I wasn't sure on the color, so I went with blue. A lot of people like blue. I hope Lillian likes blue. I've had a lot of time to think over the holidays. I guess that's a good time to do it. And I think a few things have finally sunk in. A little late for it I'm sure, but better late than never right? I realize that my actions since arriving in the WWA have been less than exemplary to say the least, and I'd just like to say... I'm sorry. I apologize to the fans, to my family, to WWA management, and most of all, to True Living Colors, Jevon White in particular. -Ry rises from his crouch and looks down at the tombstone in quiet reflection.- Ballard: I know it's probably upsetting for TLC to see me here after what I've been saying and doing these last few weeks. But I couldn't think of any other way to show how truly genuine I am. I had to make amends to Lillian first... and in person. Sure, planting flowers in the middle of January probably doesn't seem like much, but any one who knows me even a little bit will tell you just how big of a gesture it is. When it comes to apologizing, I make it a habit of not doing it. So when it comes time for me to eat my words, I'm at a loss as to how to start. I was never fortunate enough to meet Lillian in person, but being here, it's almost like I can feel her presence. And you know what? I'm sickened by it. -Ah ha! Here it is, we knew it would last long! Here comes the real Ry Ballard, ready with the burning remarks and uncouth attitude.- Ballard: It sickened me because it showed me how disgusting I had been acting. Being here let me feel how wrong I had been, more than any beating ever could. I mean, good God, I was a bastard. There's no other way to put it. I'm ashamed. I'm so sorry. Tarin, we've talked, you know I'm genuine. Hell, you're the one that suggested I come here for this. I want apologize to you for all the crap I gave you, and for that beat down by David Black. I never wanted you in the middle of that. I should have realized you'd catch the fall out. I'm sorry I wasn't there. You know where I was America? After Tarin and I argued that night after my stunt with the manequin, I needed to cool off. Yeah, while my brother was getting his ass handed to him, I was trying to score with some chick on the production crew. Great brother huh? Well it won't happen again, I promise you that. It's a new year, and while I don't think TLC will ever forgive, nor certainly forget what I've done, I just hope we can just move forward. Jevon has better things to worry about than me. He's the new number one contender to the WWA World title. That's a great thing. he earned it. I'm not going to antagonize him and distract him from something he fought so hard for. That wouldn't be right. So, Jevon... Mr. White... these flowers are a peace offering. A small gesture on my part to let you, David Black, your wife, and the rest of the world know I'm trying to change my ignorance. I've been talking to people who knew her, trying to get an idea of what she was really like. I know that she was your wife and meant the world to you. The only excuse I can offer is that I've never had someone like that in my life. I've never known how it feels. And I was jealous of you. I also knoew she was a heroine. I think any woman who sticks with a guy with a career as crazy as this one is is nothing but heroic. She was an original, that's how a lot of people thought of her. So unique and special to everyone she met. She was regal and elegant, a true queen among peasants. These are all things I didn't really know before. Things I didn't bother to learn. Jevon, I'm so sorry. I'll never forget them again. Lillian White... Wife... Heroine... Original... Regal... Elegant. -Ry smiles and picks up his shovel. As he walks off camera the shot pans up, going to an aerial view. We see that Ry has planted more flowers behind the grave. The small triangle of foliage we saw earlier was just the point of an arrow shape. The arrow points straight down to Lillian's resting place. As Ry's words echo in our heads... Wife, Heroine, Original, Regal, Elegant... we see the acronym he's chosen for them, spelled out in pretty blue flowers above the grave.- W.H.O.R.E. | | V Designed By [name withheld] | © World Wrestling Alliance 1997 - 2005
