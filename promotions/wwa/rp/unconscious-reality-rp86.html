Unconscious Reality

31 May 2003

By Kash

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The church remains the same; it is only the occupant who changes. The occupant who is before us now, silent, still, shrouded in the black of night as he kneels in front of a derelict altar in this church he has called home for so long.) Kash: I often wonder whether my life is a dream. (He rises to one knee, but continues his still vigil at the altar.) Kash: Whether I am merely the unconscious construct of some thirty+ investment banker. (He reaches up and lights a solitary candle, a monument of light in a world of darkness.) Kash: Am I what he wants to be? Am I that which he aspires to be come? Strong? Fast? Smart? Spiritual? (The candle flickers in the breeze of his words.) Kash: Maybe I am what he aspires to be? He lives his life in his real world, full of computers and suits, then comes home and dreams me into reality. (He gets to his feet and his cloak falls down, the weight forcing it to fall perfectly flat.) Kash: It’s an interesting concept. That I wasn’t born as we say, that I was merely created. (He stands, staring at the flame of the candle.) Kash: If that is the case, then it certainly raises some questions. Why, oh why, has the construct of his unconscious desires been through such pain, such misery? Why has the person he wanted to be been forced to overcome injury, and agony. (He begins to pass his hand through the flame of the candle.) Kash: Another interesting thought. If I am what he desires to be, what does that make Ian Tense? I mean, to create your ideal self, you have to create an enemy. Does Ian Tense represent all the things this man wishes he wasn’t? Arrogance? Hate? Cowardice? Is Tense every negative aspect of this man? (He places his hand in the flame and holds it there, searing his hand, but not flinching.) Kash: Maybe that is true, and then maybe that explains my existence! Maybe, I exist in his mind to destroy his negativity? Maybe I was created because Ian Tense exists? (The flame burns his hand, but he does not pull away.) Kash: If that is the case, and if that is the reason for my being, then the battle at Revolution, the war, will not just be for the WWA Title, but … surely, it is for this mans soul? (He pulls his hand out of the flame and stares at the red blister on his palm.) Kash: Two great nemeses battle it out, face to face, fist to fist, fighting for the ultimate prize. In his mind, the ultimate prize is the belt that Tense soils by his being, on the ethereal level however, we are fighting for his soul, for his essence. (He clenches his fist around the candle, extinguishing the flame.) Kash: I will not let him down. His soul, the title, in the end, it’s all the same. Ian Tense? You won’t walk out of Revolution, and your part in this dream, is over. (He gives an almost silent chuckle.) Kash: Wake up Tense. (End) Design and Graphics © World Wrestling Alliance 2002
