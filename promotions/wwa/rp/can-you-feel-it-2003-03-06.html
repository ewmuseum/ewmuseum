Can you feel it?

6 Mar 2003

By Damian Thorne

Item 1 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 6 Mar 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Can you feel it....? That coldness, that terror? That moment of clarity you suddenly experience when you realise it's about to come to an end? When you finally understand, that all you hold dear, is about to be torn away from you? You may not have felt it before, but i KNOW you're feeling it now. You know that I'm coming for you now, and out of all the people to come after you, I'm the one you least wanted to tangle with Sure, you may be undefeated, but then, you've only ever defended seriously against a paper contender. You've only ever fought a kick boy, who isn't even in the has been league, let alone main event status. You've only ever exercised that control clause in your contract, that allows you to pick your opponents. And why is that? Scared? Selfish? Or do you just realise, that once you got where you were, you didn't have the f**king minerals to stay there. Take a look at your illustrious career, and you'll see that it's just a who's who of nobodies in the WWA. But not now.....oh no, not now It's like having two savage and rabid pitbulls after you, each one intent on ripping at you until there is nothing left. And I can tell that has you peeing in your pants I could not give two shiney brass f**ks what it is you think you are to the WWA, or who it is you think you own. What you have to remember...IAN, is this. No matter how smart you think you are, there will always be somebody smarter. No matter how good you think you are, there will always be somebody better. So bearing that in mind Mongo, I ask you this... Who owns who? Doesn't it strike you as funny that I turn on youright when I was supposed to join the fraternity? That I then let you think you had my measure enough to beat me from bell to bell. Distraction or no distraction, a lesser man would have crumbled at your hand...... But...I...didn't Did it ever occur to you that i just wasn't a pushover like i made you think that night. Maybe you didn't watch me slaughter EVD? Or maybe you didn't watch me retire three men in my HITC match with Randy Cross. Or maybe you didn't watch me tear psycho-boy Kash apart at RITC? That's my point Ian, you have paid not one iota of attention to the people that really matter, the people who are a real threat to you. In fact, the first time you did, who did you turn to for help? ME So I ask again, who owns who? You've been so busy "struggling" with wins over Sanders, that you just haven't seen me coming, and now....... IT'S....TOO....LATE I'm in your life now Ian, and you'll have to do more than beat me to make me go away....you'll have to kill me. (silence) Can you feel it Ian? The icy hand of death on your title reign The prick of realisation in your mind Nowhere to run now Ian Nowhere to hide Damian Thorne is coming for you Ian Not a threat It's a promise
