Time to move on

26 Dec 2002

By Damian Thorne

Item 2 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 26 Dec 2002, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
.....Frank Sinatra.....singing Let It Snow? (The scene is recognisable as the Thorne/Law appartment. It is decked out in christmas finery and looking more like a home than you would expect for a guy like Damian. Speaking of Damian, he is sitting at the black marble dining table, a bottle of scotch, a glass and a sea of paperwork in front of him. He is pouring over something, a contract maybe? Perhaps an resume? Either way, he's engrossed. Over by the fireplace, Lady Stephanie is putting the finishing touches to a beautiful christmas tree, pinning on the last ornament and then she flicks on the lights, illuminating the tree in all it's glory. She hops excitedly, clapping her hands with glee) LS: Damian honey, isn't it wonderful? DT: *grunts* LS: Bah, you're such a Grinchy! Look at the tree Damian quickly casts his eye at the tree, then back to the papers he is hold DT: Nice, shame its a day late. It is December the twenty sixth now. LS: Yeah, I know, but the appartment wouldn't have looked right without it. Plus, if we hadn't have had to go back to Newcastle to visit your , ahem, DAD! DT: Never mention that arsehole in my company! I had to go and give him his present. LS: Oh? What did you give him? DT: A chapstick.. LS: Why? DT: So he can continue to kiss my arse for the rest of his life.. Steph's arms drop, and she saunters sexily over to Damian, running her hands over his broad back. She stoops and purrs in his ear LS: You're not gonna look at those all night are you? I got something else I want you to see (With that, she lifts her tight brushed cotton pullover a little to reveal a red bow tied around her waist. She giggles as Damian's eyes widen as he drops the papers he was holding on the table.) DT: Ok, you have my attention... Steph cuts him off by staddling his waist and throwing her arms around him. The two are about to embrace when the doorbell rings DT: What the f... *Shouts out* Jackson, is that you? No voice comes from behind the door LS: I'll go see who it is She gets up and strides to the door, clealry a little pissed off by the interuption. She reaches the door and presses her eye to the spyhole. LS: Erm, babe, it's someone for you (She opens the door to reveal a reasonably smartly dressed Shane Dorian, his hands held out in front of him in a peaceful gesture. He nods at Stephanie as he enters..) DT: Hold it right there! Did I invite you into my home? DID I? SD: Hey, I come in peace man! I'm here on HoP business DT: Forget it, you have no place in the HoP anymore Shane. SD: Thats kinda what I wanted to talk to you about... (Stephanie hovers at the door, chewing her lip and squirming in anticipation that something bad is about to happen. Damian ponders Dorian's words for a moment, then levels his gaze) DT: Princess, a glass for Shane. Oh, and close the door love, it's freezing (Both Stephanie and Dorian look relieved at Damian's words. Dorian follows Damian to the sofa and sits down, still a little edgey. Presently, Stephanie arrives with the bottle of scotch from the table and two fresh glasses. He gesture the bottle to Shane) DT: Drink? SD: What is this, a Last Call match? (chuckles) Sure, I'll take a drop (Damian pours two generous measures and reclines in his sumptuous leather sofa) DT: SO....Shane Dorian and the House Of Pain....where do we go from here? SD: Well, our seperate ways might be best at this point Damian. I feel I never really did fit in with you guys DT: Hell, even i didn't fit in with those guys Shane. That's why I did what I did. That tired old formula wasn't working, and neither where those tired old wrestlers. SD: But Randy Cross was a great champ. In fact, the greatest ever in the WWA. I know he still had it in him DT: And then some Shane. He was still on top of his game, he was just conceited and lazy. His ego had allowed him to think he didn't have to try to win, that his superiority would just make people lay down. Unfortunatley for him, he was wrong SD: Very wrong as I recall. Good move for the HoP however. DT: Well you don't seem to think so. SD: Ah, but I'm another story DT: You can say that again SD: AH, but.. DT: (chuckling) Don't! Just because it's christmas doesn't mean I won't boot your arse! Anyways, down to business SD: Yeah, the reason I came. I want out Damian. The HoP is not the place for me. My prime focus is on, as you call him, Tracer f**king Bullet. DT: Shane, if you has wanted out for any other reason, thenthe answer would have been "NO". SD: What? You agreed? DT: Hell, why not. You're still doing work on behlaf of the HoP indirectly anyways. I hate Trent Lef**king Bullet SD: Doesn't everyone? (the two laugh out loud and begin to exchange pleasentries as Stephanie re-enters the room, looking a little more relieved at what she sees and hears) LS: You boys ok? Can I get you anything? DT: Can you go to my filing cabinet and get Shane's HoP contract honey? LS: Erm, sure. Wait right there (Stephanie slips off while the two gents continue talking. She returns carrying a folder marked with the HoP insignia and bearing the words "SHANE DORIAN" She hands it to Damian and then retires to the kitchen) v DT: Here it is Shane...your file. Be sure.. SD: I'm sure DT: Ok then. (Damian tears the file inhalf, then half again and tosses the pieces into the fire) DT: Then it's official.. SD: No beatdown? I'd prefer to go out in the street now if it's gonna happen. DT: No such luck Mr. It's christmas! SD: Who would have thought they'd ever hear Mr Nasty say something like that. (Damian stands up, smiling at Shane. He thrusts his hand out towards Shane, await Shane to do the same. Shane flinches, half expecting a punch, but realises the gesture and stands also. The two clasp hands and shake firmly.) DT: Agreed? SD: Agreed. DT: Then I'll say goodnight Mr Dorian. Stephanie will see you out SD: Well, I never thought I'd say this, but it has been a pleasure. Goodnight Damian. (The handshake is still taking place as Dorian turns to leave. Damian pulls him back to meet face to face. Damain locks stares with Shane, his words low and deliberate) DT: Shane....you owe me for this (Shane muses for a second, before re-afirming the handshake and nodding his head) SD: Understood. (With that, Shane leaves without another word, and is ushered out by Stephanie) LS: Problems baby? DT: No princess. All is good in the world today LS: Goody. So...where were we? (Steph produces a sprig of mistletoe and dangles it just around her navel) LS: Kiss me under the mistletoe??? (giggles) DT: AHEM..... (scene fades)
