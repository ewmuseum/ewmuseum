Yep, I'm the BEST!

4 Jul 2003

By Tommy Starr

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(We begin in a supermarket shopping aisle, where we can see a woman shopping for canned goods, whilst pushing a pram. A voiceover can be heard.) V/O: Jacka$$! Imbicile! Moron! Mofo! Idiot! Zilch! Ignoramus! Oh God you suck! Numbnuts! Yeah thats right Zion, you are all the above and then some. You've got some nerve, superfreak! Pinning me at the Bourbon Street Brawl! What were you thinking? Don't think I wont be coming for you at the Independence Day Rumble you worthless piece of crap, and you other twenty or so punks! (None other than Tommy Starr enters the scene. As the woman stands on her tippie-toes to get an item from the top shelf, Starr takes the time to admire her long legs and then reach into the pram. He pulls out a chocolate bar, and then walks into the foreground.) Starr: Yup, winning on Sunday is going to be as easy as taking candy from a baby! And probably with a lot less drool involved. I was thinking of taking that chick out tonight, I thought she was a bit of a MILF, but when you think about it, she is used goods...been done over more times than the corner store near Fuel's house. (Starr unwraps the chocolate bar.) Starr: But thats nothing compared to what the Devils Own is going to get up to this Sunday...once we've done with you...you'll be walking funny for weeks! Now if you will excuse me...or better yet get out of my face, I've got another Rumble to worry about, and thats the one in my stomach! (Starr devours the chocolate and throws the wrapper back in the pram whilst the woman is distracted. He chuckles to himself as we fade into...) DEVILS OWN DEAL WITH IT
