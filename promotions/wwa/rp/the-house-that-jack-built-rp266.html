The House That Jack Built?

21 Jul 2003

By Tracer Bullet

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(This is the backstage area of tonight's Meltdown. This is a locker room in the backstage area of tonight's Meltdown. This is the bench that sits in the locker room in the backstage area of tonight's Meltdown. This is the man that sits on the bench that sits in the locker room in the backstage area of tonight's Meltdown. And this is the hat that is on the head of the person that... whoa whoa whoa, hold up...) Tracer Bullet: Hey there, Tyler. Funny seeing you here. Tyler: Yeah, what with me working here and being your cameraman and all. TB: Damnedest thing. Tyler: Speaking of damnedest thing... (Tyler aims the camera directly on top of Tracer's head.) Tyler: Where'd that hat come from? TB: I've always had this hat. You know that. Tyler: That's not what I mean. TB: Ah, I see. You want to know why I STILL have this hat, despite having it stolen by Kash after our match at IDR? Tyler: Yes, exactly. TB: Surely you didn't think I only had one hat, did you? Still, that act shall not go unpunished. It's the principle of the thing. And tonight, I have to team up with a petty thief to take on two guys I actually LIKE. Tyler: That should be interesting. TB: Though honestly, while I still despise Kash, I have formed some amount of respect for him after that past two matches he's had. First, he was able to come away from our match with his title still intact. And then just the other day, he fought Chance in a tremendous match in which they both took a very nasty fall... and they're still coming into work tonight. That is gumption. (This is the hand of ther person sitting on the bench... oh, the hell with it. Tracer takes off his hat and runs his fingers through his hair.) TB: However, respect or not, we're going to have to work together in this match if we hope to come away with our dignity. I can't let myself get pinned, because it would just cement everyone's opinion that I'm on a downward slope and should hang it up for good. Kash can't let himself get pinned, because he's the World Champion and that would make him lose credibility. So there's only one alternative... we have to WIN. We have to go out there, and regardless of our feelings toward each other, we need to coexist peaceably. And since I've never really been petty and vindictive, I'm willing to go through it, so long as he comes along with me. Dirty thief that he is. (Tracer chuckles.) TB: But there's been a lot of rampant thievery going on in the WWA lately. Not only does Kash steal one of my hats, but some guy who doesn't even have the sack to show his face steals the symbol of our entire industry, the WWA World Heavyweight Title. And I don't know about the rest of you, but I'm not about to sit idly by and let him get away with it. What kind of private eye would I be if I didn't make an effort to retrieve that testament to ability? So have no fear... Trace is on the case, as of tonight. Now if you'll excuse me, I have to get ready for the match. I'm not really going to do anything else, so you may as well fade to black. (This is the camera that fades to black on the man sitting on the bench that sits in the locker room of the backstage area of tonight's Meltdown.)
