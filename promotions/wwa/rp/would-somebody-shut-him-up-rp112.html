Would SOMEBODY Shut Him Up?

10 Jun 2003

By "N.A.I." Nicholas Adams

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(A video fades in, and we see Nicholas Adams...banging his head against a wall. Of course, nobody really knows why, but that's the Rainmaker. He's completely random. Of course, he would be to differ...) Adams: There's a sound reason why I'm doing this. (He continues to bang his head, and we now note that he is wearing black jeans and a black t-shirt. The people watching the video are probably confused, but Nick will hopefully get around to it.) Adams: The real reason that I'm doing this is to get the annoying and continuous voice known as Enigma. Unfortunately, this is just burning about 80 to 90 calories per hour just rocking my head back and forth...and killing even more brain cells. (He winds up particularly far back, and then slams his head against the wall with sheer velocity!) BANG! (Adams recoils in pain, shakes his head to eliminate the cobwebs and stars floating around in his eyes. He then resumes banging his head against the wall lightly.) Adams: Hopefully, the exercise will either erase enough memory so that I don't have to recall his senseless droning, or it will knock me senseless long enough to provide a respite from his continuous replies. (With one final hit, he steps away from the wall and begins to pace back and forth.) Adams: It has come to my attention that somebody that goes by the name of Enigma has not been able to shut up for the past couple of days. Something about the fact that nothing can stop him. And that stupid phrase is ringing through my years even more than the final chords of a KISS concert...well, the single KISS concert I went to, at least. (Adams walks up to a wall that says, "This Week's Opponents". It has pictures of Enigma, Grunge, and Dave Harley underneath. Adams stops right by Enigma's picture.) Adams: The unfortunate part of my life is that I have to tolerate this moron's random ramblings for the rest of the week. I have to keep myself alert to his movements, his thoughts, and his observations. To do so, I have to face his completely annoying arrogance. I wish I never heard of him. Unfortunately, I have, so I'm screwed. (Adams shakes his head.) Adams: He has no idea what he has in store for him. His abject obsession towards just throwing everything in everyone else's face really has gotten on my nerves...and I'm including his retort towards Grunge. Speaking of which, what part about, "Since he wanted it so much, I let him have it," didn't you get, Enigma? I respect Grunge. I don't respect you. (Adams looks back at Enigma's picture, then pulls out a red marker, opens it, and writes "Loser" across his picture diagonally going downwards.) Adams: And Enigma, that's the first RIGHT thing I've done about you for a while. Get over it, go back to your personal delusions, and have a nice day. (Nick nods with a grin on his face.) Adams: By the way, Enigma, before you actually have to face the fact, I'll give you a forewarning. Consider yourself... (Nick turns back to Enigma's picture and rips it in half.) Adams: Stopped. (Fade out.)
