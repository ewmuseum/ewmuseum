WWA (1997–2019) Roleplays

- The past and the present

11 Jan 2003

By Christyan Payne

Item 4 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 11 Jan 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[A young woman can be seen walking down the sidewalk, through the darkness of the night, towards a shop at the bottom of the hill. The woman seems to be in her early twenties, she's about five feet six inches, slim, with long curvy blonde hair that comes to a rest below her shoulder blades. She walks quickly along the concrete path, her heels clicking as she walks, she goes into the shop for a moment and reappears a few moments later. She looks up and down the path before walking out onto the road, suddenly a car without it's headlights on plows into her, her young blonde flipped up over the bonnet of the car and then crashing in the concrete. The car speeds away leaving the woman alone on the road, dying] [We cut to a shot of a funeral, a few mourners stand around a casket in the cemetery. The vicar stands at the head of the casket, reading from his bible while sprinkling dirt on the coffin, a young baby in a carrier is at the other end of the coffin. The child looks to be very young, perhaps only a few months old, watching without knowing what is going on. A few of the mourners walk passed the baby and mutter to themselves, looking at the child with regret and remorse. As the coffin is slowly placed into the ground and covered over with dirt by the workmen, the vicar picks up the baby carrier and walks towards the church. As we move away from the grave we get a quick glimpse at the headstone] Here lies Mary Anne Payne Born March 1957 Died January 1981 [We see the workmen continuing to cover the grave with mud as it suddenly begins to rain heavily, slowly the workmen begin to fade out, the scene suddenly grows older. Moss can be seen growing on the grave, cracks appearing in the headstones of other graves, a tree slowly growing up from nothing to the left of the grave we are looking at] [...] [Footfalls... Slow, deliberate footfalls can be heard. Each step taken is echoed by a satisfying crunch of gravel beneath shoe, the footfalls begin to grow louder and more frequent until they suddenly stop. As the camera pans backwards we see a man standing at the foot of the grave, from behind we can see that the man has long light brown, dyed blonde and red streaked hair, which is extremely shaggy and unkempt, and that stops just above his shoulders. The man also wears a tatty and torn black leather jacket, a large tear can be seen running diagonally from the top of the right side of the hip to the base of the left, along with the jacket, a pair of denim jean shorts can be seen as well as a pair of Doc Martins boots] [As the camera turns around we see that the man is Christyan Payne, he stands without any sign of emotion in his face. He slowly rubs his hands together before turning sharply on his heels and walking away slowly] "It's been twenty two years since her passing... Twenty-two years since I was left alone to face the world... Alone to fight for myself against the evils around me... Alone on this cold and bitter planet full of inane idiots who will only ever look out for themselves. Twenty two years since I was left to rot away like some dead farm yard animal, but despite it all I forged on" [Christyan continues to walk slowly through the graveyard, the sun overhead reflecting in the puddles around his feet] "Despite it all I managed to survive, clawing my way through life, searching for something that I could make meaningful.... Searching for something that could make me stand out from the crowd, and at first I thought I had found the thing... Intelligence, you see as a child I was a prodigy. Anything I turned my hand to; Math, Science, Music, Drawing, Painting, anything I did was better than the best efforts of anyone else around, I set myself up on a pedestal, I rose to the top of everything... Only to have my peers knock me down" [Christyan looks up at the sky momentarily before continuing] "You see jealousy can be a terrible thing, people began to see that I was their intellectual superior and they began to knock me, they began the jokes, they began the snide remarks, they did everything they could to ridicule me.... And for what? Because I was smarter than them? Because I understood things? No, they were jealous and I've still got people who are jealous of me today" [Christyan smirks to himself, he looks up to see Simon Mackinaw and Tenn sat in a car talking to each other] "Grunge is a man that falls into this category, a man that sees me for what I am and is scared. He sees that I am his superior and instead of accepting it, he comes out with pitiful remarks about a catchphrase, or simple adolescent remarks about my past... Don't you think I know my past? Don't you think I have to live with the thought of everything that's ever happened to me running through my mind? Don't you think that there isn't one second of a day that goes by without my past gnawing away at me?" [Christyan draws closer to the car in the parking lot] "Grunge you want to clip the strings I pull? You want to stop the puppeteer? You want to take away my title? Grunge admit it... You just want to be me. Grunge for once in your life you want be worth something in someone's eyes, you want to be known as something other than that guy who can play darts quite well, you want to be a Champion, you want to be in control, you want to hold the cards, to pull the strings... But guess what Grunge; you're just not ready for that. I could help you Grunge, but you would never open your eyes and let me show you, not like Simon and Tenn did and that's what is keeping you back" [Christyan stops about ten feet away from the car and looks up once again at the sky, before continuing to talk] "You see while others around you will always move on to greater things, you will never amount to anything because you just can't admit certain things to yourself. You can't admit that for once in your life you are outclassed, that is why you make all of these snide comments... Lex Luthor? Doctor Moriarty? Hannibal Lector? Grunge, unfortunately for you I am not a tale from a book, a fantasy created by some pissant writer or a fairytale. Grunge I'm more real that you want to admit, I'm powerful than you'll ever be and you can't accept that" [Christyan smirks once again and runs his hand through his thick hair] "You can't accept the fact that the man you are heading into face at Meltdown is a man that simply will not be beaten, a man that will always find a way to succeed, a man that cares not for the feelings and thoughts of others but only for what he himself wants. You see Grunge; you may try to intimidate me with talks off appearing on 'The Big Show' or however you like to word it, but in truth... Who cares? You think I care one iota how many people are in an arena? Or how many people are watching at home? The only thing I care about is what happens in the ring, once I am there I see nothing but my opponent, maybe you should try it one time, stop caring about everything but the match... You might do better" [Christyan walks over to the car and leans against the bonnet of the vehicle] "Ha, you doing better... Well I suppose miracles can happen, right? That's what Father O'Reily always told me. But then it wouldn't take much to improve your dismal record would it... I mean you've done what since your arrival in the WWA? Been Cruiserweight Champion? And you held it for how long? A week? Two? Pathetic... A true Champion, a man like me, is a man that manages to keep his title for a long time, that means you know, actually beating people, I've been in the WWA for what? Two months maybe more and in that time I've had the most successful reign with the Rookie Title ever and held the Television Title since my comeback. I've won matches against Nick Adams, Mighty Joe and Shaman... Men that have cemented themselves as stars in this business and yet you believe you are the one that can stop me?" [Christyan smirks once again and rubs his chin] "Grunge... Try this one on for size" [The camera zooms in at Christyan as he removes his crimson red sunglasses to reveal his emerald green eyes] "You ever danced with the Devil, in the pale moonlight?" [Christyan smirks as he gets up and gets into the car, as the scene slowly fades out]
