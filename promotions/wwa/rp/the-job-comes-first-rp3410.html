The Job comes first

22 Dec 2006

By Richard Cranium

Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Thursday Evening 7:31PM EST The scene opens to a an over the top view of a very posh looking restaurant. Everyone that is visible is wearing formal attire, elegant gowns, suits and ties, etc. There is a series of large pained windows overlooking a small waterway. Eventually a very fat man dressed appropriately in a white suit sits at one of the window seats and his companion is a very lovely dark haired woman dressing in a black gown. The waiter appears to be reciting some pleasantries and the fat man waves him off, the waiter bows slightly and leaves. The woman reaches her hands out and the fat man takes them. Suddenly a crosshair appears on the screen. The center of the cross is aimed at the fat man's head. It remains there for a few seconds. Suddenly the man stands up and leaves the table. The crosshairs disappear. After a few moments of the dark haired woman drinking a glass of red wine, in which the waiter brought when the fat man was not around, the fat man returned to his seat. Again the crosshairs appeared, focusing on the fat man's head. The crosshairs remain steady for a couple long moments. Fade =========================================== Friday Morning 8:47AM EST "Was the money deposited in the bank?", asked Cranium. "Yes it was. It was put there once the wedding was finished. We needed this Rich. Seriously... Ever since you joined this WWA Federation thing... Your face has been on TV. Even more so since you won the TV Title. The jobs have started to slow down. People are starting to recognize you.", said Sark with a slightly worried tone. "My face will be even more recognizable when this Bash is over. People will never forget who Richard Cranium is. I will simply not let them." "Rich.... You are assassinating your career, no pun intended.", a concerned Sark said. Richard countered with a short laugh, "Perhaps I am fueling a new career? This job has honestly opened my eyes to something.... This wrestling this is a challenge. Not a challenge like what we do and used to do. It is different. Making all of the those people see me and hate me. That is simply a beautiful thing. The Birthday Bash is just another way for me throw it all back in their face. HIM paying me to be here is just gravy." "You still have your sites on the goal.... On the job right?" "Of course I do. Sark, I never lose sight of a job and I never fail. That is why we have been so busy in the past years. I never fail!" "I know Richard. But this place is just crazy. So many capable athletes all going after the same thing.", said Sark. "I AM SUPERIOR IN EVERY WAY SARK! YOU WOULD DO WELL TO REMEMBER THAT!", shouting a suddenly irate Cranium. Just as quickly, Cranium has calmed, "The WWA will be at my feet. It will simply take time and it will take patience." "Understood Rich. I didn't mean to insinuate that you were not better than the other wrestlers in the WWA....." Sark apologized. "I know what you said. I have moved past that now. I am focused on the Birthday Bash now. My goal has been set and I will NOT FAIL. I never fail....", The last part was almost a whisper. "It's just I know you Richard. I've known you for years. I know how you work, how your mind works. You are getting caught up in the WWA. Will you be able to complete the mission when the time comes?", with that, Sark got up to leave the small room leaving Richard Cranium alone to think about the words. After Sark left Richard took his seat and stared out the small window in the small room through the bars covering it, a room with a computer, a vault and a large open metal door. "This is just another job, Richard. You know what you have to do....", Richard tried to convince himself. ============================================ Enter.... Tread with caution. I only see a few people in the Brawl that would even peak my interest.... Jamal Diggs has been my biggest rival and my biggest success to date here in the WWA. Now I hear that he may chicken out and stay locked up to avoid facing me on the Bash. I suppose I will have to exact all of my anger on little brother. So be it. Mal Somers is simply a joke. He is a shell of his former self. He carries that ridiculous belt around. I know it is an act.... I see a cold cunning in his eyes. When I attacked him from the cake, he had a knowing look he gave me.... Like he knew I would come.... Like he wanted me to come. Kade Williams..... I know you Mr Pist. I know you much better than you care to admit, I assure you. People don't really change. Their outward demeanor can change, there actions can change... But inside they stay the same. Just like you... Williams. You will always be that scared little kid you were the last time you were in the WWA. You may appear to be more confident, you may appear to be a hero to the fans... But you will always be the man that hide behind New Star's skirts. You know you are. The Rainmaker.... Seriously?! Since when did you decide that you would like to fight? Superhero? Well... Let me be the first to introduce you to me... Richard Cranium., your Anti-Hero. I will simply suggest that you NOT get in my way. And that you NOT make me angry... You wouldn't like me when I'm angry. And finally.... Clearly.... Son... You don't know the first thing about being the Reaper. This is something I know a little bit about. To actually SAY that I was not important enough to mention? To actually say it?! To think it is fine, but to say it out loud. Well that is just STUPID. But you are Canadian after all, so I will allow you to be learned before I introduce you to "not important enough to mention". That particular lesson will be a little... Painful. The next phase of my job here will be accomplished at the Birthday Bash. I assure you. Now leave from whence you came! Designed By [name withheld] | © World Wrestling Alliance 1997 - 2005
