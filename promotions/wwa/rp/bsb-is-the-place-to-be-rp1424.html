*BSB* ...is the place to be.

2 Jun 2004

By Rocky Adams

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(We look from the back seat of a jeep into the front containing Rocky Adams with his geeky brother Melvin Adams driving the vehicle. Rocky is frantically turning a map upside down and inside out to find out where they are going.) Melvin: Have you found Bourbon Street yet? Rocky: No… have you? Melvin: You’re supposed to be telling me where to go. (Rocky sighs and is manhandling this large paper map. It briefly blocks Melvin’s view out of the windscreen.) Melvin: Nyargh! My vision is obstructed, please remove the object obscuring my view of the windscreen. (Rocky drags it out of the way quickly to reveal them hurtling towards a wall, now how are them Adams’ boys gonna get out of this one? The breaks of the car screech, the wall grows larger and larger as they get closer and closer until the car finally halts safely. Melvin looks stressed out as Rocky looks over at him with a look of disgust.) Rocky: You are such a dork, you know that? Melvin: That was your fault you, sack of hydrogen waiting to be lit with a match. Rocky: It’s your fault you have to describe everything in your dorky mannerisms! (An awkward silence fills the air until Melvin finally reverses the car. The silence continues only to the sound of the jeep’s engine running. Melvin finally decides to break the silence.) Melvin: At least you’re not driving, drunkard. Rocky: Recovering alcoholic is the correct term you’re looking for. (Another silence hangs in the air, this time to the ruffling of the map Rocky is holding as he begins searching for Bourbon Street once again.) Rocky: I can’t find this place anywhere. Melvin: Alcohol slows the responses, you couldn’t have halted this metal beast in time. Rocky: … whatever. (He continues ruffling the map to look for it.) Rocky: I mean, it’s not here… Melvin: (Snapping) Oh take the hint like you take your coffee! Rocky: Fast, hard and whipped cream? Melvin: It’s obviously a wind up, Bourbon Street? Come on! Rocky: (Faking a laugh) Oh yeah… I don’t get it. Melvin: Bourbon, a type of whiskey, all though I doubt you read the labels before knocking it back and ending up on your ass like an… an ass! It’s another fake wrestling street name, Austin Avenue, Jabroni Drive- Rocky: (Gasps) You cant say “fake” and “wrestling” in the same sentence! Melvin: I’m just trying to save you the embarrassme- Rocky: (Interrupting) We’re here! Melvin: How can you tell? (Rocky hops out of the vehicle which has now stopped. He walks up the street slightly to a pet store window containing a dozen monkeys. Rocky narrows his eyes like a detective.) Rocky: (Que the sinister music) They come in herds like the wolves that stalk the darkest of woods… Melvin: Are you talking about Dave Harley? Rocky: (The sinister moment ends abruptly) Yeah that means Dave is here. This must be the place. (They walk into the pet store as the camera slowly pans out on the Adamses to the sound of the monkeys squeaking loudly. Rocky has arrived at Bourbon Street…)
