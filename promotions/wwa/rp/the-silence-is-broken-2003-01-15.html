The Silence is Broken

15 Jan 2003

By Denrol

Item 9 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 15 Jan 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene opens with a eagle-eye shot of the Toronto Skydome. It's at night, so it's lighted up in purple. The camera pulls back to revel it is standing in the CN Tower. As it pulls back, we see Denrol is standing by the window on the observation deck. His back is to the camera, but it can be seen that he is dressed in his street clothes, namely a leather jacket, black keans, and black sneakers. He slowly turns around and grins.) Denrol: That's right, the silence is broken. The Greatest of All Earth Time is back and that means a lot of trouble for certain people in this federation. The man who really should be scared is Jackson Kraven. He really did a number on me, didn't he? He really screwed me up with that Kravenbomb. I keep seeing that move in my head, over and over again. I keep remembering the pain. I will deal with you in a moment Kraven, first I would talk to the man I will face on Meltdown. Chance, you always talk about Fortune's Favor. Well it didn't help you too much on Meltdown. Yeah, you will get the chance to go one on one with a legend, a true headliner in this sport. But you will lose. You know that, I know that, everyone knows that. The question isn't if you win, but how well you fight before going down. I wish you the best of luck, but you need to understand why I am a legend and unless you change yourself, you never will be. You believe in lady luck and that she will be at your side. You believe this is what you can use to become a great WWA superstar. I don't believe in luck. I won all my matches via hard work and intelligence. Luck never had anything to do with it. It wasn't easy and I suffered many setbacks. But I did it. I know you can too. But you're going to have to stop this "Flip of the coin" bullsh*t. You got to start using your head. You are certainly going to need it this Monday, when you face the smartest wrestler in the business. (Denrol grins, than turns his face serious.) Denrol: That brings me to you Jackson Kraven. You have 11 days left. 11 days before I get my revenge. You thought you could ignore me. You thought I would go away. I will not Kraven. I am the nightmare you cannot wake up from. Kraven, I don't think I have ever hated anyone like I hate you. Oh sure, I couldn't stand the fraud that was Flynn Steele. Nik Segier and I will never see eye to eye. House of Pain and I went through a lot of battles, I don't even like the current piss-poor rendition that Thorne runs. Ventressa is one of the biggest pieces of crap I ever met. But you Kraven, are one of a kind. My hate for you is beyond measure. Don't get distracted by Ian Tense, Wes Sanders, or Kevin. You've let them distract you for too long. Now you have to deal with the worst threat you've ever faced. We've been battling for a year now. At Rage in the Cage, this will be settled once and for all. You tried to break my neck Kraven. Thus, on January 26, I will return the favor. I WILL break your neck. Know this Kraven. In October, you had your once in a lifetime chance. You had your chance to finish me off. You failed. Now I'm back and more pissed off than ever. When you enter my match, my version of hell, you will learn why you failed. I can't wait Kraven. (Denrol grins and the scene fades to black.)
