Special Delivery

24 Jan 2003

By Mal Somers

Item 2 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 24 Jan 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Scene – Inside the palatial home of Thomas Connor. Better known to the viewers of WWA Insider as Leviathan. The camera pans around the interior, passing by a library full of leatherbound books, down the hallway which is adorned by many ancient artifacts. The camera comes to rest in the den, where Leviathan sits back in his recliner, sipping scotch whiskey from a crystal glass as Miss Wise gently massages his aching feet.) Leviathan – “Oh that feels great Penny. You really know how to ease a man’s worries.” Miss Wise – “If you’re lucky Thomas, later on I’ll ease someth…” Miss Wise’s suggestive comment is interrupted by a knock at the door. Leviathan – “Mmmmm. That’ll be the pizza. I’m starved. Would you care to pour me another one of these and I’ll pay the delivery boy?” (Connors shifts his imposing frame forwards, shifting the recliner into an upwards position, gets up and walks down the hall to the front door. He opens the door and is greeted by the pizza boy.) Leviathan – “You do realize you are five minutes late. That means these pizza’s are free.” (The pizza boy stands there holding a stack of six pizza boxes which obscures his upper half from view.) Leviathan – “Your hearing is equal to your punctuality, I take.” Pizza Boy (with crackling voice) “Umm yes sir...I guess those Pizzas are free. Ummm how about a tip?” Leviathan – “That is very humorous. Here is a tip then, do not aggravate the situation any further or the only way your will be eating anything is through a blender”. Pizza Boy – “How about one from me then?” (Leviathan is getting increasingly agitated but also curious.) Leviathan – “Do enlighten me, Pleb.” Pizza Boy – “Don’t BEEP with the Extreme Corporation!!” (With that, the Pizza boy charges at Leviathan and the two tumble inside. Caught off guard, Leviathan is speared into the wall a couple of metres behind. His head is whiplashed back and connects with the wall. The pizza boy removes his hat and glasses to reveal Mal Somers! Leviathan is temporarily knocked out, and he falls forward from the wall revealing broken and cracked plaster. Somers pulls a pair of handcuffs from his jacket pocket and handcuffs Leviathan to the stairway railing. He then picks up an iron fire poker from the nearby fireplace and cracks it over Leviathans arm repeatedly.) Somers – SEE (crack!) YOU (crack!) AT (crack!) RAGE (crack!) IN (crack!) THE (crack!) CAGE!!!! (crack!) (Miss Wise enters the room screaming hysterically.) Somers – “Shut your mouth you stupid wench! Call those S.O.Bs Lockdown and Cruz and tell em Mal dropped in to say Hi!” (He saunters over to Miss Wise, puts his arm around her as she stops bawling, more of a whimper. He grabs her curvaceous butt cheek.) Somers – “Wow you’ve really let your self go. You should think of cutting down on the pizza!” (He looks over to see Leviathan stirring.) Somers – “Oh well sweetie pants, time for Mal to go, I don’t wanna be around when he wakes up! Bet you he’s a big old grumpy bear when he wakes up, hey?” (Somers cops another quick grab, and zips out the door, picking up a couple of pizza boxes on the way.) (Fade Out)
