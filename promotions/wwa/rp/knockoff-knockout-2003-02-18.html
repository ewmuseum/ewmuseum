knockoff knockout

18 Feb 2003

By Lockdown

Item 5 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 18 Feb 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
We find the members of The Cell, Lockdown and Leviathan, strolling down a crowded city street. Countless numbers of scam artists, thieves and vagabonds are hawking shabby looking merchandise at a number of tables and stands. It is Market Day on Maxwell Street in downtown Chicago. Good old Market day, where a person can get an absolute steal on counterfeit merchandise and various other items usually stolen earlier in the week. Lockdown: Oh man! I’m glad you finally agreed to come and try a Maxwell Street Polish Sausage. You are gonna fu**in love it! God, I could fu**in live on the damn things. I swear there is not a better taste in the whole world. A lot of places SAY they have real Maxwell Polishes, but to get the real deal, you gotta come to Maxwell Street. There’s only two places on earth you can get a real one. They are both little stands here on this street. Just don’t buy any jewelry from anyone here. Some of it’s the real deal, but most of it will turn your fu**in skin green Leviathan: I think I have all the gold I need around my waist. (They pass a table set up displaying a variety of WWA t-shirts.) Lockdown: Hey, Levi, Look at this. I didn’t know Miss Wise had her own Tshirt! Leviathan: Neither did I. We’re going to have a little discussion when I get back to the room. Hey, here’s our shirt. Hmm, the printing looks a little sloppy (to the vendor) How much for this one? Vendor: Five dollars. Leviathan: These must be rejects, that’s why they’re so cheap. Lockdown: Wait a minute, lemme see that. (inspects tag) See that? There’s no WWA logo, and it doesn’t say “Officially Licensed Product.” This is a fu**in knockoff!! (Lockdown flips the table and kicks around the vendor’s merchandise. He pimpsmacks the vendor a couple times, grabs him by the throat and says..) Lockdown: This is my motherfu**in shirt!!! Sellin this sh*t is takin money out of my pocket! Ever heard of copyright infringement?? Do you know what happens to people who steal from me, huh, do ya?! Vendor: N..n..no. Lockdown: Do ya wanna fu**in find out?! Vendor: No! Please don’t hurt me! Lockdown: Then this is what’s gonna happen. You are gonna give me all the money you have in that little lockbox, you are gonna give me all these fu**in Brutally Truthful shirts, you’re gonna clean this mess up, me and my friend here are gonna go get a sausage and you’re gonna continue business! Does that sound fair? Vendor: Yes, sir. Thank you (The vendor begins to comply with Lockdown’s demands.) Leviathan: By the way, do you have any Shaman shirts?? Vendor: I do Leviathan: How are they selling? Vendor: Bah! I can’t give them away! I had a special- buy 2 shirts get Shaman free. Still nobody wanted them. Lockdown: See, Levi? I told you he sucks. Now that our friend Cruz can’t save him, his ass belongs to me. Leviathan: Perhaps you are correct. Lockdown: Perhaps? Ever since Cruz told us to take him off that fu**in table, I have been dreaming up all kinds of new ways to torture him. Finally I get my chance. Leviathan: Good for a second I thought we were seeing the kindler, gentler Lockdown. Why did you let this guy off so easily? That’s not like you. And I have to say it surprises me a lttle to hear you talk about copyright infringement. Where did you learn about that? Lockdown: Man, this poor schmuck is just trying to get his hustle on. As long as he’s not stealin from me, why should I care if he takes some change out of the rest of the WWA’s pockets? As far as the copyright infringement goes... well when you spend as much time with lawyers as i have you pick up a few things. Anyway, are ya ready for that sausage? Leviathan: As I’ll ever be. (The two continue down the street to get their deliciously greasy treat.)
