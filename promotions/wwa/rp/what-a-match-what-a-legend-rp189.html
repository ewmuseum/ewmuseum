What a match, what a legend!

25 Jun 2003

By Joey K

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I arrived back in Chicago sporting a nasty bruise on the back of my neck. It wasn't the kind you could see from the naked eye, it was the kind that if you touch it, it hurts like hell. If you move a certain way, bend your neck a certain angle...you almost collapse from the pain. But I'm not moaning. I'm glad I have this bruise. I'm not a hardcore icon. I don't LIKE pain. But after my "match" against myZery....I needed to come away from Meltdown with some kind of pain, so I could remember that I was actually booked to fight on Meltdown. myZery. Why did you turn up Monday night? You didn't fight me.....I asked you to bring your game, to bring your best..... All I got was a warm up, I don't think I even broke sweat. But, dude. I can't blame you.....I psyched you out, I scared you....You knew you would lose and you didn't wanna appear on Meltdown. One person who DID turn up at Meltdown however was....Dave Harley. You and your little buddy Mal Somers seem to enjoy making jokes out of yourselves....comic relief maybe. But guys, I've been there, I know what its like. You feel good about yourself when you make people laugh don't you. But the difference between you and me is, they are laughing at you, not with. And as I feel the back of my head, pressing gently on that bruise I got from a Harley Bomb...I'm smiling. Because, you actually are pretty funny. Its funny that you think you can beat me Dave....I have Enigma, Se7en and now Wes Sanders at my side! You have Mal Somers and Chance.....I have beaten both. What's also funny is that you think 'Not so Late Night With Mal and Dave..' will beat 'K Moments' in the ratings. Thats not just funny, its hilarious, the stupidest thing I have ever heard in my life. When K Moments debuts, you will see the kind of affect I have on people. You will see Mr Warr's face light up when he witnesses the ratings increase sharply....you will see him frown when you and Mal hit the ring, and the ratings plummet, giving him no choice but to FIRE both of your asses. It was a lovley little trick you pulled monday night Dave. I never thought you had it in you. I never thought you would stoop so low. I assumed that you had more courage, more class. It appears that you should never assume. I bet you thought I'd be angry, didnt you Dave? But I'm not, do you know why? Because there will only ever be one outcome. There will only ever be one winner. And just like that perfect flower, that is just out of reach.....you will never get this title from me, nobody will. The next time we see each other will be at Bourbon Street Brawl. Bring all the liquor you can Dave, you'll need it to ease the pain. The pain that I will cause you. You made the mistake. Now take the punishment, and... MAKE WAY...FOR JOEY K!
