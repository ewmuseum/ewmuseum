And God said...

19 Feb 2003

By Grunge

Item 2 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 19 Feb 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
FADE IN (The screen is black and suddenly words fill up the screen.) And God said, "Let Us make living creatures out of mud, so the mud can see what We have done." And God created every living creature that now moveth, and one was man. Mud as man alone could speak. God leaned close as mud as man sat up, looked around, and spoke. "What is the purpose of all this?" he asked politely. "Everything must have a purpose?" asked God. "Certainly," said man. "Then I leave it to you to think of one for all this," said God. And He went away. (The words fade and there can be seen the man known as Grunge, free off his loss from Monday night. A towel covers his head.) Grung: Kurt Vonnegut, Jr. Cat's Cradle. I love this passage. It so succinctly states what I've always thought of as true...we are the ones who have to make sense of all of this. It is our purpose to give meaning to life. (Grunge turns and looks at the camera.) Grunge-Does life have to have any grand meaning? It certainly doesn't (as far as we can tell) for dogs, cats, dolphins, elephants, monkeys, goldfish, manta rays, and so on. Show me a kangaroo who is concerned with the balance of his good and bad karma. (Grunge laughs.) Grunge-What then makes us any different? The ability to perceive? Maybe we aren't the only ones who perceive. Maybe the rats really are performing their own experiments on us while running through their mazes, hitting buttons, receiving their reward or punishment. Perhaps, one day, we will wake up to find all the dolphins are no longer in the sea - and that they have left a note saying "So long, and thanks for all the fish." (Grunge smiles.) Grunge-We create art to express our emotions, our thoughts and fears. We create art to show the conflict between our need to validate our existence and its place in nature. Perhaps we create God through art. To me wrestling is that art and Nick Adams has just thrown paint all over it. (Grunge holds up a peice of paper.) Grunge-Nick you owe me a rematch for that belt and I want it. Sure it can't be Monday because of Global Warfare. but the week after well I'm free. What about you? (Grunge smiles again.) Grunge-I'm trying hard to learn from life what I can before I die and move on, whatever that means. I'm creating myself, my own importance, in small steps. We all are, I think. What about you Nick? What will you say when you meet are maker? (Grunge just stares blankly.) Grunge-I'm going to say I was framed. See you all at Global Warfare. FADE OUT
