Restoring The Balance

29 Jun 2004

By Mal Somers

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Tonight’s edition of Insider begins with a familiar sight to WWA fans. WWA Intercontinental Champion Mal Somers and Dave Harley are seated across from each other, engaged in a thrilling board game. Checkers, draughts, whatever it is called, they are playing it. Somers: How appropriate! Harley: What, that I’m kicking your ass? Somers: No, not that. Look at the board. (Harley looks the board up and down. Side to side. Down and up.) Harley: Hey we’re not playing Scrabble. I don’t even know what game we are playing and I’m still beating you. Somers: Dude, we are playing checkers. An ancient game. Look at the colours. Harley: Red and Black. Somers: That’s right, the colours of X-Corp, pretty cool huh. Harley: Meh. Why are we playing this crappy game again? Somers: To illustrate a point. Look at the board, full of black pieces and only a couple of red pieces. Harley: I know, I’m kicking your ass, woo-yah! Somers: (shaking his head) What I am trying to show is something that has been on my mind for a while. Harley: (downing a beer) What’s that? Somers: If you look at the WWA at the moment, it is full of bad guys, the black pieces on this board if you will. Harley: That bastard Ian Tense. Somers: Odium. Harley: Cruz, Lockdown, Leviathan… Somers: Quietus, Muppet Man, Tracer Bullet… Harley: Mr Darkness, Toxic, Jimmi Zion… Somers: Shaman, Bonafide, Desolation… Harley: Tommy Starr, Epik… Somers: K-Rupt, Tommy Turner, the list goes on and on. Harley: That sucks man. Somers: It sure does. And in turn, these red pieces symbolise the “good” that is left in the WWA. You, Chance, Kash, and I are about it. Harley: That’s a scary thought. Somers: Sure there are a few guys from SCWF and LDA who are on the right track, if they continue their ways I think the balance will be redressed. They are all looking to make an impact at the Rumble. But on paper, there is only a small amount of people who are here to fight the good fight. Harley: That’s sad. You know, out of the sad things known to man, there ain’t too much sadder than the tears of a clown, when there is no one around. Somers: ZING! Nice insult to throw at the Muppet Man! Harley: Huh? Oh yeah I guess it was. I was just quoting some old song from the Seventies. Somers: Oh I see. You know most of these bad guys are in the Rumble this weekend. After the ass whooping we took at the Bourbon Street Brawl, we had better be on our game this Sunday. Harley: True, although that said, I think we will do better this time around. Somers: Yeah me too. The Brawl was suited to the cowardly ways of the Devils Blown. They could hide in the dark and attack like vultures when the opportunity arises. The Rumble is a lot different. Once you step through those ropes, there is absolutely no escape, unless you are eliminated. There is nowhere for them to run, no where for them to hide. Harley: And that is where the Devils Own are at their weakest. Somers: Don’t we know that. After all, X-Corp closed them down once before, we can do it again. (The pair clink beers as they look at the checkers board. Somers smirks as he grabs one red piece. He leaps over one black piece. Then another. And another. And another, and he keeps doing so until he has leapt over every black piece on that board.) Harley: Holy crap. Somers: I know we can do it. At the Rumble, good will triumph over evil. It always does. You just have to know the rules. Harley: And how to bend them. Somers: Huh? Harley: Well, it was a nice visual you jumping over all my pieces, but it was my turn. (Somers examines the board, and thumps the table.) Somers: D’oh! Harley: So what about this Intercontinental Match, how are you going to go in that? Somers: It’s a tough one. I’ll want to conserve my energy for the Rumble, but I know if I hold back I am going to lose. And losing is not an option. So I am going to give it my all, and worry about my energy levels in the Rumble later. (Harley nods. He singals he needs a toilet break and leaves Mal to talk to the camera.) Somers: Actually, I could live with losing to Chance, he is a worthy champ, but if I lose to that no good Odium, I don’t know what I’d do. The thing about Odium, is he goes on about being a predator and a warrior, all the old clichés. Well you know what Odium, the last time I saw a predator he was getting his ass handed to him by Arnold Schwarzenegger. And as they said in that movie, “if it bleeds, we can kill it.” This Sunday Odium, while I am busy making sure Chance doesn’t win the belt off me, you are going to find out if you bleed for what you have done to me. And rest assured Odium, you will bleed this Sunday. By my own two hands, you will bleed...and perhaps that will mark the beginning of the balance shifting in the WWA. Restoring the balance. You know, its a shame that the WWA isn't like a show like Survivor or Big Brother. If we could eliminate people like on those shows, we know the Odiums, the Devils Owns of this world would be long gone. Funnily enough, these shows are called reality shows, but what sort of reality do they repesent? None that I know. The only reality I know is that of the WWA. The reality of trying to win the WWA Title. The Rumble this Sunday is my first step. In my reality, bad outnumbers good three to one, and you can't phone in to vote and eliminate them. The only way to eliminate them is with my own two hands. This Sunday at the Rumble. (Fade Out.)
