Last Chance

31 Jan 2003

By Leviathan

Item 7 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 31 Jan 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Leviathan stands in the middle of the ring. It is set up for a cage match. The pans around the arena the focuses in on Leviathan. He is expresionless.) Leviathan: Life is about choices. The choice to face your demons or to turn and hide. All along David, I have offered you the opportunity to leave Malcolm to his own demise but you have never accepted. (Leviathan shakes his head) Leviathan: Now before Lockdown and I terminate Malcolm and ensure no one ever has to be offended by his presence. David I offer you the last chance to save your neck. Do not show up at Rage in the Cage. Let Malcolm enter the Lions den alone. Let him suffer, let him bleed but most importantly let him realise he is nothing. I can assure you David if you reject my hospitality and arrive at Rage in the Cage, you too will suffer the same fate. Remember back in you EWA days, how much of a friend was Malcolm then? (Leviathan walks to a side of the cage and shakes it.) Leviathan : Think David, who is more capable of causing anarchy within this building. The extreme corporation or the CELL. Our name alone should tell you. Add the fact I am partnered with a known felon. It is our match, therefore victory is assured. Nothing more and nothing less. (Leviathan laughs then heads to door and heads down the steps.) Leviathan : Extreme coporation you will not escape this pen and you will not escape your fate. Once you step in that ring the hostilities commence. Your demons await and your fears will be realised. There is no stopping the brutal truth. No stopping the red tsunami that will wash away the bitter memory of the Extreme Corporation. Let's face it, you two are in TOO DEEP!
