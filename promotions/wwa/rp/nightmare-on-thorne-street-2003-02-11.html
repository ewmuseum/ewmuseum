Nightmare on Thorne Street

11 Feb 2003

By Kash

Item 7 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 11 Feb 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
1..2..Kash is coming for you……..Hey, we made a rhyme. Oh, that’s original, start quoting horror films. No wonder people call us a freak. 3..4..Lock the door……..Have we noticed how locked doors don’t seem to help in horror movies? Will we pipe down for a minute? We’re trying to think. 5..6..Get a crucifix……..You think a crucifix would work on us? We do like blood. Don’t be stupid. 7..8..Stay up late……..Considering we come out at night, staying up late wont do much good. Shut up, or we’ll shut you up. 9..10..Never sleep again……..You think Thorne sleeps? Of course he does, right next to Lady Stephanie. Hehehehe, we like her. Maybe we do, but not for the same reasons that Thorne does. We like her neck. Perfect for? We never know! Exactly, now be quiet, we’re distracting us. What are we doing? Planning. How can we plan for Thorne? All we need to know is this……..kill the sumbitch. Please, you think Thorne is going to lay down for us? He might, if we ask him nicely enough. We have had too much time to think recently, it’s not good for us. We have it all wrong. Time to think is just what we need. What are we gibbering about? Time. Planning. Forethought. All the tools we need to beat Thorne. And here we are, trying to plan, and we are distracting us from the task at hand. Ah, but are we? Are we distracting us? Think about it. How many more useful thoughts have we had whilst we are angry with us? …….. True, true. And, now we are angered with us, have you seen our fists? Closed. Yes. Closed fists. Perfect for damaging Thorne. Perfect for punching away at his flawed face, Exactly. Now, we have that image. What are we going to use it for? Making our blood boil. Yes, good, good, do we understand? We understand, 1..2..Kash is coming for you.
