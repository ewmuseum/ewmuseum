Fearless

25 Feb 2003

By Vik Ventressa

Item 6 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 25 Feb 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"Long time no see" (The scene suddenly opens to an shot of Vik Ventressa's eyes. Blue like ice, the reflection twinkles off the glazed surface. We zoom out to see him stood on the rook of a building. It is nightime, and the stars flicker faintly through the city smog. We continue the zoom. He is wearing a black leather jacket and black cargo pants, he stands with his arms crossed, slightly shivering, trying to keep himself warm.) "One month to be precise. One month since I last appeared on WWA Insider, and just less than that since I humiliated you at Rage in The Cage. Humiliation is a wonderful form of revenge." (We pan around to the the back of his head, and we see what he is looking at. A wonderful moonlight cityscape, the cars appear to be moving slowly. An aeroplane has its lights flashing up high as it glides into the distance) "After all, the whole point of my little comeback was to exact revenge on the man that ran me out of the company. Many would say I achieved that. I took the big man out. I left him for dead. Not many people can do that and get away with it. And I didn't. I didn't get away from it. Because the very next Meltdown, I left the arena in a frigging ambulance." (The camera tilts down as we get some sense of depth. He is extremely high up. The cab's are zipping along the street down there, and we hear a horn.) "Is that what you call revenge? I haven't even begun in my plan of ultimate humiliation. The master plan that will prove to the world what a fake you are. The plan to strip you down to a mere mortal. To prove you can be beaten, and beaten fairly. Until I pin your shoulders to the mat, I will never give up. 2 month contract or not, I can guarantee you that I will haunt you until I break the spell of Jackson Kraven.... Until the Legend becomes nothing more than an old wives tale." (We cut sharply back to Vik who is now walking along the edge of the building Spreading his arms out for balance, peering over the side) "And I may be living dangerously. I could be teetering on the edge of certain destruction. You tell me to be afraid." (We cut to the face, a deep stare into the camera) "Can you be afraid if you have no fear left?" (Cut to a full length shot. He still stares at the camera) "What if a man has been forgotten for so long that fear turned to anguish and pain. What if a man had lost everything but his life, and the fear turned to despair. And what if a man had been humiliated so badly that the fear had just eroded away to a point where he simply didn't care." "Who cares about the theory of action and reaction" (Cut to a new angle) "Who cares about the consequences" (Cut to a new angle) "The Afraid." (Cut to the full length shot) "But.... When a man cares not about what he is doing, says to himself 'screw the rules'. When a man doesn't care if he lives or dies just to achieve his one...true...goal...." (We cut to a shot directly behind him.) "Is that man crazy?" (We cut to his face, a little cocky grin on his face) "Or is he simply fearless?" (We cut to the shot from behind as he runs straight at the edge, and does perfect swan dive off the top of the building as we fade to black.)
