Whats so wrong with good looks?

8 Mar 2003

By Joey K

Item 3 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 8 Mar 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
We fade in… once again the setting is a hotel room. Through the window, the setting sun casts fiery shadows across The Charlotte Coliseum, which can be seen in the distance. Suddenly, Joey walks into shot, speaking into a cell phone. JOEY: Look, we can’t put this off any longer, Meltdown is when we gotta do this! The voice on the other end replies, and Joey frowns. JOEY: Look, I know that problems can come from me hiring you, but, you gotta face your fears dude. The voice begins to speak again, and Joey starts to get angry. JOEY: OK! OK! Listen, we’ll talk about this Monday night, I’ll see you then. Goodbye. The phone clicks, and Joey looks at the camera. JOEY: My biggest chance, Monday Night in that very arena… He points out of the window, towards the coliseum. JOEY: My chance to win my first singles title, all on my own. No shots from behind Venny, oh no, that won’t be happening, you see, my manager will be there. And together, we will kick your damn ass. And then, with you down on the mat, 1...2...3, the crowd go wild as Joey K is crowned the new TV champion!! He throws the phone onto the bed, and straightens his tie. JOEY: But, as much as I hate Chicken lover LeTrav, I loath Grunge. I never had any problems with him two weeks ago, but now...my god, that has all changed. You want me to stay out of your way Grunge? Well, I never wanted to come near you, I never gave a damn about you and Nick Adams, or the TV title, as far as I was concerned, the tag match last week was gonna be an in and out job. I win the match, which I did, go home and continue to kick Venny’s ass. But you changed that, you hit me with a poor copy of my finisher. So, on Monday night, when the bell signals the end of the match, and your laying flat out on the canvas, with me standing above you, holding that belt high, the fans chanting my name...you just remember, it was YOUR fault, YOU got me involved. He takes a sip of his drink, before continuing speaking to the camera. JOEY: Nick Adams...’The Rainmaker’ Dude, Monday night, I will be the rainmaker, infact, it won’t be rain, it’ll be tears....your tears. You see, I’ll kick your butt so bad, you’ll be crying in agony, you’ll cry when I pose with my new title. And this isn’t cockiness, this is confidence. Joey stands up, and puts on his jacket. JOEY: Oh, and Grunge, just for the record.... Whats so wrong with Nsync? I can’t help t if I’m naturally good looking can I? I just feel sorry for your girl, maybe she needs some of the Joey charm? Hehehe, hope that doesn’t strike a nerve. He starts to walk out of the room, but, suddenly stop and leans into the camera. JOEY: Nick Adams, Grunge, Venny LeTrav, Monday night, a fight for the TV title.... Make Way… For Joey…K!! FADE OUT
