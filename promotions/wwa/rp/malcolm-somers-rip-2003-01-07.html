Malcolm Somers RIP

7 Jan 2003

By Leviathan

Item 2 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 7 Jan 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Fiesta logo is blazing on the screen. It fades into the crumbled pile that was Mal “CHAIRman” Somers custom chair.) Leviathan : It’s fitting that your self styled chair now symbols the epitaph that is your career, Malcolm. (Mal Somers batted body laying in the center of the ring. Blood gushing from a head wound and possible internal injuries.) Leviathan : Fiesta will be symbolic to all for two reasons. The end of seeing the CHAIRman in the WWA and emergence of “The CELL”. (The screen now shows the destruction that occurred at Fiesta.) Finally the chair gives in, snapping at the hinges. Leviathan throws the remaining pieces of the chair out of the ring as he and Lockdown exchange acknowledging nods. Cruz picks up the mic again Cruz: almost drowned out by the boos This…this is what happens. This…is what happens. This…is…what…happens. From now on, jou gringos in the back are on notice. I am in control. What I say…goes. And if jou go against me, these two madmen, este dos diablos, will take jou apart. Es dat simple. Cruz tosses the mic down and the three men leave together. Leviathan : David, I will give you a second chance. You may have had too many blows to the head as you didn’t heed my advice. So David my associate and I offer you the chance to walk away. Leave Malcolm to the gutter where he belongs. Because next time, we won’t hold back. (Leviathan is standing in front of steel drum which contains a raging fire. He holds a crumbled chair, then tosses it in the fire.) Leviathan : R.I.P Malcolm, because you and anybody else that will not obey the Commissioner will be “BARRED FROM LIFE”!!!
