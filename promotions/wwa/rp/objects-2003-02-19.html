19 Feb 2003

By Curzon

Item 7 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 19 Feb 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Wallace Coleman is stood in reception at WWA Headquarters.) Coleman: Hi folks, I am here waiting for James Curzon. He's been pretty hard to get hold off recently but he should be here any second. (Nothing happens.) Coleman: Curzon has a match against James Beam on Fiesta, in fact it's a handicap match with Referee Fagin on his team. He is also in the Global Warfare match on Monday night as part of the British team. (Coleman stands uncomfortably silent again.) Coleman: I'm getting a bit bored now. (Suddenly the doors swing open and a man in a black tracksuit runs in throws a box at Coleman and runs out again.) Coleman: Was that him? (The camera peers through the glass doors as the figure fades into the distance. Coleman opens the box inside are several items with post-it notes attached. He takes one out.) Coleman: I assume he just wants me to show you these things. This is a James Beam action figure, with the head pulled off and taped to his back. The note reads "James Beam needs to watch his back." Interesting. (Coleman takes out the next object, a Union Jack flag.) Coleman: The notes reads "Team Britain, the empire exerts it's authority again! Three quarters of the globe once belonged to us and now all of the WWA will!" Curzon is obviously confident for Monday! (He takes out another object. It is an orange plastic cube with a string attached.) Coleman: It says pull here. (He pulls. The cube expands rapidly, turning into a large dingy. The boat knocks over the camera and sends coleman flying. Wallace reads the note.) Coleman: "This represents me in the WWA. It's pretty self-explanatory. That means it's even easy for Americans to understand!" (At the bottom of the box is a final note.) Coleman: Use this box to do my interview. I could not stay in case one of the conspirators saw me. They are all watching me, I have to be careful. In order to preserve my secret location this box will explode after usage. (Coleman nods. Then a look of horror crosses his face. He dives for cover as the various oibjects catch light. A security guard rushes over with an extinguisher and puts out the blaze. Coleman looks up from the ground and tries to speak, however he is in shock and no words come out. Fade to black.)
