Tap all you want...

8 Mar 2003

By Nicholas Adams

Item 4 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 8 Mar 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
FADE IN (Nicholas Adams is shown sitting on top of a ring turnbuckle. Of course, the ring is the only part of the scenery that is lighted.. This is a bit different from his usual choice of locale, but then again, he doesn't have any real "usual" locale. Wallace Coleman walks up to him slowly, since Nick seems to somehow be meditating. Just as Coleman takes a step towards Nick, Adams jumps right at the announcer in a cat-like leap. Coleman screams in fear, but just before he would have impacted Wallace, Adams flips over. This causes Nick to completely dodge Coleman and land on his feet. Meanwhile, Coleman is left completely shocked. In fact, just as Adams turns around, Coleman apparently faints. Nick looks down. Then he turns towards the camera.) Adams: Well, that was pretty much expected, though I wish they had sent Beckham...it would have been SOOO fun to piss her off. (Adams chuckles a bit, then cups his hands around the sides of his mouth and yells.) Adams: OKAY, GUYS! YOU CAN TURN ALL THE LIGHTS ON! (The entire arena is illuminated, and we see that it is the site of tonight's Fiesta. Why Adams is there is not quite understandable, but it doesn't really matter as long as Adams is there for a purpose.) Adams: At the moment, let's make the tally of what I've done. Lemme see...won my first two singles matches in the WWA...after winning my second match, went on to beat out eighteen other men to become the number one contender for the TV title. Faced Shane Dorian twice, beat him the second time in one hell of a match for the TV title. Planted Deimos through a table with a barbed-wire two-by-four to retain my title. Then was defeated by Christyan Payne because he used some loser tag team to help. Wound up repeatedly kicking the ass of that team, then went to Rage in the Cage. Too bad that Cruz and Warr decided that Payne and I needed a little company. So, Grunge wound up winning because he got help. And then in a fair match, I kicked his hide and got the title back. (Nick walks over and looks to the Fiesta stage, though the cameras only catch a profile of Adams.) Adams: But of course, a person has to bitch when they lose to their superior, so he wanted a rematch. Not to be last week, of course, though I did tap out to let him think that he beat me. It was the least that I could do. You know, make him a little confident. (Nick then begins to tap his foot against the ground and think.) Adams: Grunge did make me realize a couple of things, though. One, we have differentiating views on music. He thinks that a punk rock love ballad is an okay song for an entrance, and I personally think that metal or maybe just hard white rap works. Though, honestly, music is music. Whatever works. But I really take offense when he doesn't take the match seriously. In fact, one of these days, because he screws around, he's going to get himself seriously injured. Another thing that really bugs me is that he soiled the name of the Three Stooges. A man like him does not deserve to misuse that form of comedy. But what really pisses me off is that he called me a villain. Here's some word association for you. Coleman, wake up. (Nick walks over to the fallen interviewer, and then shakes him. Coleman begins to revive, and when he finally says, "Huh?" Adams replies.) Adams: Okay, Wallace, I'll say a word and you say the first thing that pops into your mind. Coleman: What? Adams: I think you've got it. Coleman: Understood. (Adams nods, gives a thumbs up, and then stands Wallace up before continuing.) Adams: Meltdown. Coleman: Monday. Adams: Fiesta. Coleman: Mexico. Adams: Cruz. Coleman: Bahamas. (Adams thinks for a second, then realizes that the name is a homonym.) Adams: Champion. Coleman: Ian Tense. Adams: Loser. Coleman: Crenshaw. Adams: Grunge. Coleman: Music-obsessed. Adams: Nicholas Adams. Coleman: Freaky. Adams: Julie. Coleman: Who? Adams: Okay, Coleman, we can stop now. I was just making sure of a few things. Coleman: Yeah, whatever. Later, Nick. (Wallace walks off, still a bit confused. Nick then turns back towards the camera.) Adams: You notice how he referred to us with adjectives that weren't insulting? He said you were music-obsessed, and that I was freaky. I said the word champion, and the only thing he could thing of was Ian Tense. On the radar of the WWA's number one interviewer, we're almost nothing. To him, your girlfriend is non-existant. The fans may like what we do, but compared to other people, we're not that big. I mean, come on. Chance came in to the WWA at about the same time that I did, and yet he has already had the WWA Extreme title and the WWA Cruiserweight title. Seems like neither of us can become great all that quickly. Well, Grunge, the fact is, greatness may not come for a while, but it doesn't matter. Your ass will be mine in the ring, and if Joey K and Venny get in my way, they'll have a little taste of what a storm feels like. (Nick walks back to the turnbuckle, climbs it, and sits down.) Adams: There is only one unstoppable force in the world besides God, and that force is water. A hurricane doesn't release the destructive force of ten H-bombs in its first ten minutes because it has strong winds. The winds don't destroy...the rain does. And that's why you should fear me. (The camera pans in to Nick's face.) Adams: I am the Rainmaker. FADE OUT
