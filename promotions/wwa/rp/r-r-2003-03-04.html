R&R

4 Mar 2003

By X-corp

Item 2 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 4 Mar 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Scene – The lounge room of Dave “The Extreme Machine” Harley in Chicago. A home entertainment system occupies one wall of the room and along the opposite wall sits various trophies and memorabilia. In the centre, the EWA Extreme Title takes pride of place in a trophy cabinet. In the centre of the room, Dave Harley lies on a massage table, facing down. His back appears bruised and somewhat twisted. He is silent, but we can hear a male and female voice, both arguing.) Male Voice – I don’t care if I’m his freaking tag team partner! There is NO WAY I am rubbing lotion into his back! Female Voice – Just because I am his Personal Assistant, doesn’t mean I am going to do that either! (The camera pans across slightly to reveal the other half of the Extreme Corporation, Mal Somers, and Dave’s PA, Jill having a heated discussion. Between them, on a table is a tube of prescription sports rub.) Somers – For some reason, this segment is being broadcast on WWA Insider. What will the boys in the back think? Me rubbing lotions and creams into my tag partners back? We’d never hear the end of it! Jill – Dave did not employ me to... (Jill’s complaining is stopped by a knock at the door. She shakes her head, and walks down the hall.) Dave – All I can say is that someone better get rubbing because my back is killing me! Somers – Yeah the Cell really went to town on your back at Fiesta. Not to mention Jimmi Zion earlier in the evening. You’ve got a lot of guts buddy. Dave – Thanks Mal. You know, when I challenged Jimmi, I was under the impression the match was for the tag titles. I wanted to take them from the bloodied remains of the Cell and dance all over their decomposing bodies! Somers – You’ve been watching too many horror films. Harley – But then, as usual, they chickened out of that with their stupid clause and it wasn’t even a Title match! Somers – Bastards. Harley – Its just so typical of the Cell. Since day one, they have plotted and schemed and... Jill (entering the room) – Sorry to interrupt your little rant Dave. But, gentlemen, there are a few people here to see you. Somers – Who? (The X Corps sometime trainer, and EWA alumnus Jack Bridge enters the room. He takes off his sunglasses and surveys the room.) Harley – Mal? Who is it? I cant see here! If its another autograph hunter just give them one of my stock standard 8 x 10’s, they're in a box by the front door. Bridge – Don’t worry Dave. Its just your old friend Jack. Harley – Oh $hit! That’s the last thing I need! Another one of your wacky training sessions. Cant you see I’m healing here! Bridge – Its cool Dave. I’ve got other plans for you. I’m actually here to see Mal. Somers – (gulping) Uhhhh... Bridge – I’ve been watching you two on television and you’ve shown me flashes of what you are capable of. But that’s all I’ve seen. Flashes. Somers – Hey steady on Jack… Bridge – Hear me out Mal. I wouldn’t be here if I dint want to help. I see you are booked in a singles match on this weeks Fiesta against that freak, Leviathan. v Somers – I know. I’m counting the days til I get to catch me a big fish! Bridge – Now, what you fail to see is the opportunity that you have before you. Somers – What do you mean? Bridge – (shaking his head) You still don’t get it? You call yourselves the “Extreme” Corporation, but I haven’t seen much “Extreme” stuff from you guys, you especially Mal. This match is an opportunity to redress that situation, and make an impact. You are going to throw out the challenge to Leviathan. Somers – Am I just? Bridge – Oh yes you are. You are going to challenge him to a Hardcore Rules match at Fiesta. No disqualification, no count outs, no time limits. Just you and the fish and a $hitload of plunder! Somers – (smiling) I like the way you think, Bridge. Bridge – (smiling also) I think we think alike, Mal. Now for some training. (Bridge offers his hand to Mal and the two shake on it. Suddenly Bridge kicks Somers in the gut and Jacknife Powerbombs Somers through the table. He lies there, covered in Daves lotion.) Harley – Oh damn it Bridge! Bridge – Sorry Dave. I’ve gotta work on his killer instinct for Saturday. Harley – (laughing) No not him, you destroyed my lotion! Now what am I gonna do? Bridge – Oh don’t worry about that. As I said, Ive got plans for you too. Harley – (gulping) Oh – oh. (Bridge clicks his fingers and soon after two shapely blondes, replete in nurses uniforms enter the room. One carries a bag of “lotions” and the other a couple of bottles of bourbon.) Bridge – Meet Candy and Mandy. They’ll take good care of you. Lord knows they’ve seen me through some rough times in the past! (Harley and Bridge start laughing as the ladies begin to get to work and the scene fades to black.)
