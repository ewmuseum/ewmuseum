James Beam is back. And hopefully not Bad.

13 Jan 2003

By James Beam

Item 4 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 13 Jan 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene opens to a sauna room. Through the steam you see a man sitting alone on one of the wooden benches. He is leaning back against the wall, with his eyes closed. Apparently in deep thought. As the camera moves closer, you see it is former WWA rising star, James Beam. The camera stays on him as he sits silently. Then eventually, his eyes open, then a smile appears as he recognized the WWA camera crew.) James: Hot enough in here for ya? (James laughs as he stands up recofiguring the towel around his waist. James walks by the camera without a word and out the door. The camera follows him into a bathroom of sorts. He continues on and through a sliding glass door into a large indoor swimming area. In the pool you see none other than Rebecca Lynn swimming in the pool. James moves to the side of the pool and waits for her to recognize he is waiting for her. To which she stops against the side and reaches up for a quick kiss.) James: So when can we leave? I just don;t think your father approves of this move. Becca: Of course he does! He loves you and you know it. James (chuckles): What I know is he loves you. And tolerate my "under-achieving" hide, as he so elegantly puts it. Becca: Awh com'on Jim! He just thinks you could do better than trying again in the WWA. He just wants what is best for us. James (sighing): I guess. But Becca, this is what I really want. I really think I can do it this time. I have rededicated myself to the sport and it is time to excell. Becca (smiles): So.. What is this your third comeback James Beam?! (Becca laughs as she pushes off the side back into the water and begins swimming away.) James (barely audible): Indeed. How many times will the fans welcome me back? (normal) This time will be different! The rookies in the WWA are all excellent! And I will be forced to rise up to the heights of my ability and truimph! There will be no second place this time around for James W. Beam! (James notices that he is yelling now. He quickly stands and smiles. He turns from the pool and then back to the camera.) James: It looks like the rookie division has certainly heated up. The Rookie Champ, Mal Somers and his little cronie Harley look tough. I know I will be able to better then once the chance is given. And speaking of Chance... This fellow deserves a look also. James Curzon looks like he needs to take a nap though. Perhaps I should be the one to put him into a deep sleep. (James smiles at the thought. Then comes back to his serious attitude.) James: Since I am back, I will be at the Fiesta and I will be watching, waiting... For my chance to get back into the ring. So, if anyone is feeling Froggy... As the saying goes... I will see you all at Fiesta, unless of course, I see you first. (Fade)
