Fleetingness is the Tool of the Weak

6 Feb 2003

By Shane Dorian

Item 3 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 6 Feb 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Scene opens to Shane Dorian, as always sitting in front of a WWA backdrop. He has a pretty nasty gnash on his forehead, covered by a large bandage. His hair hangs loosely in front of his face, casting shadows over his whiskers. His eyes seem distance, conjuring up the rigth words. He brings up his right hand, and runs his fingers through his mane of hair. With a deep sigh, he begins to address the fans..) Shane Dorian: Rage in the Cage, I quit match. You know, going into that match, I knew exactly what was going to happen. Every punch I took, and every submission I endured was all part of the illusion. In that sense, I guess you could call me a modern day Houdini. Doing whatever it takes to keep the wool firmly in the path of the specter's eyesight. I was about to crack. I was going to give in to the pain. Tracer Bullet was going to prove to the world that he still had it. But then, in a moment of sheer genious, the biggest rising star in the business comes in and snatches a little glory from the living legend. What hurts more? Having a closely contested affair, where the winner was the result of a trial of tremendous effort, or having your victory stollen from you, mere moments before your feat is achieved? I think the answer is obvious. There is a philisophical theory going around that states that the course of a day mirrors your entire life. A simpler example of that is if I took a large triangle and cut off the top tip, it would still be a triangle, just smaller. In essence it is saying that a day in the life is a condensed version of how your time on this earth will be spent. Tracer, on that fateful day in Canada, your day was about to reach its pinnacle, before it all came crashing down. Story of your life. You're always going to come this close {makes "this close" gesture with his hands} to glory. But there will always be people like me and Enigma to make sure your determined course of existance goes as planned. Enigma is a colorful fellow, isn't he folks? The dual-champion that rocked two divisions of competition, the very essence of his being is to cause waves. He has a mind for this industry that is un-matched. And it can only get better. Together, there is no stopping us, and this is something the WWA will have to come to terms with sooner or later.This is not to say I'm able to control this free spirit, not in the slightest. The only solace I take on the killing fields of Enigma is that I'll be the last to survive. But my association with him brings up an interesting quandry; Is Shane Dorian House of Pain, or not? This question is irrelevent in the long run. We all know Damien Thorne's position on me, and mine on him. It goes without saying. The words are out there, and can't be taken back. HoP has no bearing on the relationship that Enigma and I will carve, that's the undeniable truth. Moving on, Rage in the Cage has brought us another development in a long standing fued. Jason Wainwright is back. Jason, I have no clue what you're trying to accomplish in this tenure. You're the leech of the WWA. Sucking at my blood, mooching off of any success I attain. But no, you're not doing that this time, are you Jason? No, now you have respect for the fans? Now you're thanking people? I don't know what you hope to make of what's left of your career, but I can tell you right now that shining up the image and battling Shaman ain't gunna cut it. And finally, I want to address Kash. Listen up, you big freakin' weirdo. You can pretend to be crazy all you want, but this guy isn't buying it. You're so clouded by self perception that you can't realize how cliche you are. Talking to yourself? Voodoo? War paint? Get with it, Papa Shango, you are not going to be here in three weeks time, so why don't you just move aside and let me get where I'm destined to be. What disgusts me the most is the way you pass off our match as nothing to worry about. There is no way you've overlooked me as you want people to think. Under your visage of calm soulfulness, you're trembling at the thought of facing me. The fact that I'm in cahoots with Enigma only supports this fact. You keep thinking you're not worried, but keep in mind, fleetingness is the tool of the weak. Now if you'll excuse me, I have a match to prepare for. (As Shane walks off, the screen fades to black.)
