Silence is Golden

25 Apr 2004

By Goro Yoshida

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The black screen flickers with a burst of static until blackness strikes again.) WWA TAG TEAM CHAMPIONS (The sound of a sword being pulled from its scabbard can be heard.) ASHIKO (The screen comes alive with another burst of static and then we can see Maishito Tanaki, the manager of the mysterious Ashiko sitting behind a desk, fingers pressed against one another in the shape of a triangle. Though he wears black sunglasses in the darkened room, he appears to be deep in thought.) Tanaki: Since Ashiko destroyed the pathetic Chance and Matt Keith at Rage in Cage many have asked when will they break silence. (The camera zooms in closer.) Tanaki: That is none of your business. Ashiko walk into best wrestling federation in world and win the titles in their first match. Ashiko do not need to talk. They talk by their actions. Last Meltdown, Kid Cool and Liam Jenson suffer same fate. (A wry grin crosses Tanaki’s face.) Tanaki: This Monday, ridiculous Deluccis will return to WWA to again suffer the same fate. (The wry grin disappears.) Tanaki: In fact all who cross our path will suffer same fate. That is why Ashiko are silent. Action speak much louder than word. No-one will wrest the Tag Team Titles from our iron clad grasp. No-one. (A burst of static and then we fade to black.)
