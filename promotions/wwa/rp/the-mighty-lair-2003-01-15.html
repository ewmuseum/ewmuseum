The Mighty Lair

15 Jan 2003

By Venny LeTrav

Item 1 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 15 Jan 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(A beautiful, cloudless day over a nameless land when a tiny beep is heard. Soon a familiar voice is heard…) “Hey, baby. Yeah, I’ll be at the airport to pick you up around 5, right? Right. No, no, I’m not at the hotel, I’m at Joe’s. Uhh… I can’t tell you where exactly. The crazy tit paid someone to come to the hotel, blindfold me, and drive me out here. I think it was some kind of a joke, though…” (Venny LeTrav is leaning against a rock wall speaking into his cell-phone when suddenly the wall behind him moves. He jumps forward slightly and looks behind him as the wall rises and he finds himself looking at a grinning Mighty Joe) Venny: What the… baby, I’ll call you back in a bit. Love you. Bye. Joe: Citizen LeTrav! Welcome to ’Mighty Joe’s Secret Mighty Mountain Hideout’! (Venny just stares at him for a few moments. He places his cell-phone on his belt clip and walks into the darkened corridor. Joe walks before him strutting proudly into a large room adorned with what looks to be costumes, utility belts, and a rather nice computer system.) Joe: Welcome to ’Mighty Joe’s Secret Mighty Mountain Hideout’!! Venny: You said that already, Joe. Joe: Nevermind that, citizen! I have asked you here because— Venny: --because you want to discuss how we’re gonna kick Havoc’s ass, well Joe, I’m way ahead of ya. Joe: Well, in a way, yes! See, I will employ my Mighty gadgets and gizmos as you see adorned on the walls of ’Mighty Joe’s Secret Mighty Mountain Hideout’! Venny: Okay? Well, Joe, it isn’t a hardcore match. Joe: I don’t follow you, Citizen LeTrav. What do you mean? Venny: …look, you can’t use your little gadgets or… what is this?! (Venny holds up a slender, cylindrical device about one foot long. He stammers and Mighty Joe just looks at him puzzled.) Joe: That’s the Mighty Club! Venny: The Mighty what?? Joe: The Mighty Club! (Venny narrows his eyes and they shift from Mighty Joe to the device he now holds by two fingers. He drops it suddenly and wipes his hands on his shirt.) Venny: Whatever… thank god I wash my hands before eating… Joe: Citizen LeTrav, what are you talking about? Venny: Nevermind!! Look, do you have some kind of strategy to use against Havoc or not? Joe: Well, of course I do! Look here, citizen… (Joe leads him to a glass case hanging on a wall next to what looks to be a boomerang and suprisingly, a harpoon gun. Inside the case is a bright red wrestling singlet with the trademark “M” on the front. Joe puffs his chest out proudly and crosses his arms. Venny looks a little annoyed.) Joe: That’s one Mighty costume, citizen… Venny: You’re not actually going to wear that, are you? Joe: Surely you jest, citizen! I mean… oh… (Joe grins widely.) Venny: What the hell are you smiling about now? Joe: If you wanted to borrow it for the match, why didn’t you just say so? (Venny looks back up at the costume and then back at Joe.) Venny: If you think I’m gonna wear that, you crazy bastard, you’re out of your gourd. I’m going back to the hotel. (Venny walks off down the corridor towards the door and Joe looks back at his singlet, shaking his head.) Joe: Very well, citizen. Just mind that you don’t tell anyone about Mighty Joe’s Secret— Venny: You say that again, Joe, and I’ll ram that costume down your throat. (FADE TO BLACK)
