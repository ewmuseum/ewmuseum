Pre Match Hype

7 Mar 2003

By Mal Somers

Item 1 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 7 Mar 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene opens with a simple WWA logo backdrop. The camera zooms out to reveal none other than Chris Crenshaw, microphone in hand. Standing beside him, talking to his title belt which is draped across his shoulder, is Mal Somers. He is wearing faded blue jeans, and a “Laying the CRAXXT Down” T-Shirt.) Crenshaw – Hello folks and welcome to WWA Insider. Tonight we are in for a real treat as I get to grab a few words from the WWA Rookie Champion, and member of the Extreme Corporation, Mal Somers. Lets get straight to the point. We all know that Leviathan has accepted your challenge for the upcoming Fiesta, making it a hardcore match. Will you be accepting his request that there be no interference also? (Chris points the microphone in Somers’ general direction) Somers - ...and THAT’S why you never see Lockdown and Pee Wee Herman in the same place! (looks at Chris) Can’t you come back later? I’m trying to have a conversation here! Crenshaw – Ummm..well..uhhhh Somers – (strapping belt around his waist) Oh don’t worry, Rookie is sleeping now anyways. I just told him a bedtime story that is bound to put any one to sleep. Chris, I’ve thought long and hard about Thomas’ request and the answer is NO. I was planning on having lots and lots of interference. Leviathan’s request would disrupt those plans. Chris – You AREN’T accepting his request? This has got to be a first. If you don’t mind me asking, who were you planning on having interfere? Somers – That’s OK. Well, I was thinking of having a baseball bat, uhhh..maybe a shovel, sheets of glass and perhaps a crowbar interfere. For starters. Chris – (shaking his head) Mal, the match is hardcore rules. Those inanimate objects are legal. By “no interference”, Leviathan was referring to people. Somers – I understand this. Its just that come Saturday, I am going to be making a whole lot of inanimate objects become VERY much alive. (nudges Crenshaw and winks at camera.) In fact when all is said and done, the only inanimate object... (smirks) ...will be Leviathan himself. Crenshaw – Fighting words there from Somers. Somers – Actions speak louder than words, Chris. So OF COURSE I accept a NO INTERFERENCE stipulation at Fiesta. I wouldn’t have it any other way. I am a former EWA Extreme Champion, and co-founder of the Extreme Corporation...notice a pattern there? Chris – Why yes...yes I do. Somers – And whats with him reading big leather bound books before our hardcore match. Way to get in shape, Levi. As I said, my actions will speak louder than any words, especially those from a damn book. Although, I did catch Leviathan backstage “rattling his lance” once before and I gotta say, I laughed at that. So the mans got a point there. As well as the fact he was using tweezers. But that’s a WHOLE other story. Chris – Hmmmm...now how is your tag partner, Dave Harley? Somers – He’s on the mend, the old X-Corp spirit is coming to the fore. Although I must say he has developed an unusual habit of sending himself mail and then replying to it. Chris – Bizarre. Somers – Yeah. We like to call it “doing a Harley.” Chris – Speaking of “doing a Harley”...I see that he is getting some “special treatment” from Jack Bridges’ nurses. How is your training going? Somers – Yes, Bridge has some very unorthodox training methods but I’m sure its all for the best. Beating Leviathan into a pulp is my prime motivation. Chris – Well we are almost out of time. Any closing words? Somers – More of a question really. I was thinking. If Leviathan is a FISH, and by ducking out of giving us a tag title shot last week that would label him a CHICKEN, does that make him a PUSS... Chenshaw – Ooooooookay we are out of time! (Fade Out)
