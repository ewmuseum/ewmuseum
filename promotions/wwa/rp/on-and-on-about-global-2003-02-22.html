On and on about Global!!

22 Feb 2003

By Shaman

Item 4 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 22 Feb 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
** Shaman is seen walking through a mist as a cloouded figure is seen standing next to him. Not as big as Shaman, But large enough as he stands there looking at the figure and Shaman turns and faces the figure.** [Shaman] " You didn't do this did you?? I mean after all we get to face the Cell later this evening and then im off to Global Warfare!" ** The figure silently looks on and shakes his head as Shaman smacks his hand down upon his forehead.** [Shaman] " Great your as bad as Showtime except he has mysterious fires! I mean look at you i found you in a state of mind that would really make anyone , but me very nervous!" ** The figure stands and places a hand on Shamans shoulder as Shaman looks at the hand and grins sadistically.** [Shaman] " Guess your right this doesn't exactly mean that we are going to not enjoy our stay in the WWA. Perhaps its tme to enjoy what has been handed to us. I mean after all this is the Cell we are going after and i intend on hurting them!!" ** The figure turns and walks away from the camera into the mist as Shaman looks on as he waves his hand through the mist as everyone realises it isn't mist it is smoke.** [Shaman] " Global Warfare is just around the corner and i trust in the team i have been included as a part of, But the trust isn't exactly comforting. We do have some major problems at this juncture. We do not believe we can work together in the ring as a team. I ask myself what we have gotten into and i continually come up with the same dilemma. The fates were cruel in teaming us all together. The games afoot and suddenl;y things change. The WWA's West Coast team will go kick ass with very little left for imagination!" ** Shaman turns and walks away as the camera fades to black leaving only these words.** GOT BLOOD!!
