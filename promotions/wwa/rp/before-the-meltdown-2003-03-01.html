Before the Meltdown

1 Mar 2003

By Joey K

Item 5 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 1 Mar 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The camera doesn’t fade in. Instead we get darkness, and walk into the middle of a phone conversation. The first voice we hear is that of Joey K, the second, we do not recognize. JOEY K: Ok, so what are you gonna do about it? VOICE: That’s up to you Mr K. JOEY: So what the hell am I paying you for? Man, that’s the point of your job, you’re my manager.....so, manage! The phone line clicks dead, just as the second voice begins to reply. Joey mumbles through the darkness, and we finally fade in. The setting, a lavish hotel suite, the wall-window looks out upon the Washington sunset, dying with the rising of the moon. Joey doesn’t look at the camera, instead he walks towards the bedside table and takes a sip of his drink, before taking a blue striped tie and puts it on. A suit blazer has been laid out on the bed, which Joey also picks up and puts on. He then walks over to the window, all the while, acting as though the camera isn’t even there. He looks at the city skyline, drink in hand, pondering. ‘Monday Night.....we will all see.’ ‘Tag Team partners? Me, and.....Grunge? He thinks he’s so damn special. Well, nobody makes a fool out of me. I had nothing against him, but since he made a fool of me, i've realized, I know how much he sucks.’ ‘But something is much more important.....or should I say, someone?’ Joey turns to the camera, his eyes piercing the camera lens, his stare searching our souls. ‘LeTrav, what I do to you Monday night will be nothing! You see, I’m just waiting, waiting for the moment I get you all to myself. So far you’ve hit me from behind, and wormed your way out of a one-on-one match, but when the time comes, there will be no cheap shots. I’ll have my manager with me. I would have them with me this coming meltdown, but I thought I’d show people that I could beat you down....all on my own.’ ‘Oh, you wanna know about my manager? You’ll know soon enough, and together we are ready to destroy you Venny, and everyone else who comes in our way.’ He takes another sip off his drink, and places it firmly on a table near the window. Then, looks deep into the camera...and whispers... ‘Make way, Venny..for Joey....K’
