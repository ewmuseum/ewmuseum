Tommorrows

15 Jan 2003

By Ian Tense

Item 7 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 15 Jan 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Tommorrow? (Tommorrow? Tommorrow? Tommorrow? The word echoes like like a gunshot) Tommorrows never seem to come. (come. come. come. Still echoes.) But you Jackson, you have the rare distinction of being the one man whose Tommorrows will come up and bite him on the ass. (The echoes die, replaced by a quick clip of Jackson Kraven smashing WWA champion Ian Tense with a chair) You're strong. You're brutal. You're powerful. You're devestating. You're the former 3-time WWA Champion. So, So many attributes that send normal men into fits of fear and anxiety at the very sight and sound of your name. (clips of Jackson beating various WWA superstars senseless. Kade Williams. Denrol. Craven Moore. Tracer Bullet. Wes Sanders. The list goes on and on.) But not me. (All the clips and all the flashes die into a shot of a man sitting at a desk with the chair spun away from the camera) I'm not normal man, Jackson. But already know that, don't you? (The chair spins around to reveal the WWA World Heavyweight Champion, Ian Tense) Because as Powerful as you are, I'm twice as cunning. And as devestating as you can be, I'm three times as sly and plotting. No one ever sees me coming. (But this image is short-lived as it is replaced with a clip of Ian winning the World title at Birthday Brawl against Jackson Kraven, Craven Moore, Wes Sanders, Tracer Bullet, and almost every single other wrestler here.) Its that simple Jackson. (And that clip dies as slow death. Ian stands up, shrouded in a black 3-piece suit and sunglasses, and stares into the camera.) Everything that you've accomplished, every single wrestler you've retired, everyone you've ever destroyed, they all mean precisely sh*beep*it to me. Do you know why? (Ian pulls out the WWA World title from underneath the desk and places it on the desk top. The name 'Ian Tense' strongly engraved onto the gold name plate) Because in time Jackson, Tommorrow will come. And I'm afraid you won't find it quite as pleasing as you might think you will. (The camera zooms on to the name plate. The name 'Ian Tense' is all that is visible. A flagarant taunt.) Because Tommorrow.... (Suddenly laughter breaks a tense silence) IS MINE!!! (fade)
