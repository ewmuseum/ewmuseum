Conditioning!!

11 Feb 2003

By Shaman

Item 2 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 11 Feb 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
** Shaman is seen sitting in a British robe of sorts as he is sitting near a table with alot of books as he is reading one smoking a pipe. Quietly he looks up and puffs on the pipe and then places the book down.** [Shaman] " Ahh you've just joined me on masterpiece theater. Today we have some classic overtures from Beethoven or shall we listen to Yohan Sabastian Bach. After that we shall watch some sterling classic films. Sgt. York and possibly even classic Godfather. Mainly we will be watching classic after classic as well as listening to classic after classic." ** A camera man is waving at Shaman trying not to anger him and pointing at a picture of Jason Wainright.** [Shaman] " Jason Wainright the man who thinks i am not training. Blowing my chances he says. Thinks im taking nothing serious past my prime. He thinks im going to learn soemthing from him. Nothin impressies me from that loudmouth. Wainright i train everyday and you have nothing to off that i haven't already seen or faced. Newsflash Wainright nothing you can say or do bothers me. I'll be around and when i do show up at Fiesta im going to bury you like yesterdays news. So sit back and enjoy my misgivings and psychotic tendancies and await your little pathetic turns to recieve them. Welcome to my world Wainright your not going to be enjoying your stay!" ** Shaman gets up and tears off the robe and snaps the pipe and grins sadistically as he grabs a book and rips it in half and then looks over his shoulder at the bookshelf and drags it down and then breaks off the chairs backing and grabs a folding chair and grabs it and unfolds it as he taps it gently and smiles.** [Shaman] " This is a classic. So is the ol 2x4 where i get to beat people bloody with them. I beat anybody and everybody. From presidents to little announcer peons. Top of the heap all the way to the bottom thats a classic!" ** Shaman grins as he stands up holding the chair gently petting it as it seems to quietly speak to him as he brightens up a bit and then suddenly cracks himself over the head a few times as a trickle of blood forms and runs down his face. He then looks across the room as his cheek begins to twitch and he then looks at the camera and grins as he holds the chair tapping his head with it.** [Shaman] " There is a quiet difference between my methods and everyone elses. I don't care and im not afraid to cut loose and make my way across the playing field. Crush my competition beneath my fists. drowning them in my blood and their own. No i think im going to hurt people and i enjoy it. I like my own pain. Im going to keep making my move and when these arms and legs step forward they do not ever go backwards. I may lose i may win, But i still get to make you bleed and enjoy my own sweet pain." ** Shaman stands up and slams his head through the mirror and grins and does not shake the lil shards of mirror glass. he then smiles as he grabs a piece of mirror and pulls it free with blood upon it as he brings it to his lips and licks it clean and grins.** [Shaman] " Tastes like beautiful fear. A fear that i have is only to lose complete control and kill my oppoonent. Then again i have never really had the control. Ask all my victims these last few months. Ask Adams we have matches and i put him through Pain. Ask Chrystian Payne who bled and bled as Grunge who recieved the pain i offered at Rage in the Cage. No i think its time i turn on the charm that i have dispalyed so much lately. So Wainright what is it that you were trying to tell me again?" ** Shaman touches his lips with the blood pouring down his face and then he grins as he cracks his neck from side to side and then looks straight at the camera.** [Shaman] " Its time for me to get moving. This is Santa Cruz , California not some lame ol house in england. I like to go surf. Its a hobby for a few years and guess what? Yeah thats right i like to wipe out. I could die i could live or i could even be a crippled man. Guess what i don't care see you in the ring soon Brah! It will be nothing you have ever expected before. Welcome to the real world Wainright this will be a fight for your life. You thought you knew me. Those tapes are old and don't mean a damn thing. This time you meet the real me the one who doesn't shy away or is afraid of anything. You wanted a fight loudmouth you have one in spades!!" ** Shaman walks over to a bookshelf kicks it over and then grabs a surf board and walks away as these words are left upon the screen.** GOT BLOOD!!
