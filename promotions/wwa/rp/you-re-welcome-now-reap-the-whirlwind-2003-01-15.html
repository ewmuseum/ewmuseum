"You're welcome, now reap the whirlwind."

15 Jan 2003

By Jimmi Zion

Item 3 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 15 Jan 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Fade In] [Houston, Texas] [The next morning following last week's WWA Fiesta] [Cut to the interior of a lush, lavish, penthouse highrise suite overlooking the large city of Houston below. The bright winter sun able to peer out of it's January clouds as the rays shine brightly through the large windows of the hotel room. We see the beautiful sight in the morning as the camera reveals the wife and manager, Mrs. Mikalia Rush-Zinn sitting down on a large sofa flipping through channels on the television across from her. She is wearing a smooth, white, silky night gown with a matching robe over it, and matching fluffy house slippers. She hasn't quite changed into clothes just yet. While she is flipping through channels, the bedroom door opens and her husband, "Sky Flyin" Jimmi Zion (Zinn) walks into the room. He partly shields his eyes from the bright sunlight coming into the room. He rubs his head from there he was hit with the steelchair by Lockdown on Fiesta. Jimmi walks over to the bar, pulls out a can of Asparin, pours himself a glass of orange juice from the fridge below. He pops the Asparins into his mouth and washes them down with the juice. He then walks over to Mikalia and sits down on the couch with her. She gives him a kiss and then starts massaging his head while they watch TV. They are talking to each other. Let's listen in.] Mikalia Rush-Zinn: "Good morning baby, how did you sleep?" "Sky Flyin" Jimmi Zion: "Actually, despite the splitting headache from last night? I actually slept pretty damn good." Mikalia: "The thoughts of Lockdown didn't keep you tossing and turning last night did he?" Zion: "Nope. Like I said, despite the headache, I slept like a baby. As for what Lockdown and Leviathan attacking me. I'm not worried about it because those two cooporate b**** arsses are gonna get their's on Fiesta. I'll promise you that. Why do you ask?" Mikalia: "Oh nothing, it's just since the camera crew was here. (She gestures at the camera in front of them.) I assumed that it was promo time, so I thought you might want to get some things off your chest before Fiesta." Zion: "Well you know me love, I'm always ready to speak on such matters, and since there is plenty to talk about, this is...(He pauses as he looks at his apparel and how he is still wearing his pajamas. He is only wearing white cotton pants bottoms with no shirt exposing a nice, firm, ripped, toned, upperbody. He then glances over at Mikalia who is still looking beautiful in her Victoria's Secret white night gown wardrobe. He shrugs his shoulders and continues on.)...almost a perfect time to talk. Yeah I can find some time to speak on such matters. Let it roll." [He pauses as he repositions himself and Mikalia sits pretty and fine behind him looking at the camera.] Zion: "For your information Lockdown. I slept just bloody fine last night. Maybe too fine. Yeah I got a headache, but trust me it will pass. I should ask the same thing for you, but you already answered that how you threatened me with your senseless babbles and idle threats. Like you really scare me. Not even when you were beating me down did you scare me. My knees didn't go weak on me. I wasn't trembling with fear. All I am is bloody f[..]kin pissed off, and I won't settle for little the same cooporate b[...]h have his way with me twice in one week. You had your time in the sun. Your time to shine. Too bad it was the wrong way to get it because now you have my full...undivided attention. Right now there is actually someone who has my attention more than Enigma, Chance, the unifying the WWA X-treme and Cruiserweight titles. And that's kicking your bloody arsses from turnbuckle to turnbuckle the only way I can. That's right because the highlights reels will once again be talking for years because they will remember the day Lockdown and Leviathan got their's from Mister Millenium in the flesh. What's that? You're too damn stupid to know what I'm talking about? That's okay because I won't just tell you, but I'll show you. I will show you what GRADE A...USDA...PRIME...X-TREME...INCARNATE...ARSS WHOOPIN is all about. You will know just who is Jimmi Zion. Zion time will run wild in Houston Texas. You said my fate was sealed? Nobody can save me from you? That's what you said right? WRONG! Nobody controls me. My fate is never sealed. I control my life and my destiny. However I will say this you will reap the whirlwind and pay for what you did to me. That is my vow!" [Zion politely helps Mikalia to move over where he stands him and looks directly in the camera.] Zion: "You see this. [Gestures at his firm, ripped upper body then to his head.] "This body has literally been through hell and back. You could say that it's a road trip of pain, agony, tears, and spilt blood. I'm still alive, I'm still kickin, I'm still fighting. As long as there's breath in his body I will always fight you to the very end. I told Enigma this and now I will tell you this. You can not just beat me. You better finish me off all the way. You have to kill me to beat me, and I will never go out without a fight. More importantly, I will never give up to the likes of cooporate b[...]hes like yourself and Leviathan. So if you can, try and kill you...I'm daring you. Seal my fate. Finish what you started. In fact... [Zion falls on his knees and does a mock prayer with his hands folded together and his head slightly bowed down. After a moment, his eyes peer up through his fallen long braided dreadlocks as he looks back directly into a camera and smiles slyly.] Zion: "I'm begging. I'm peading. I'm praying for you to TRY and kill me. If you can. But you know what I think." [Zion gets off his knees standing back up straight looking into the camera again.] Zion: "This is what will happen. I will just keep getting up and keep coming at you. I will never stop. Most importantly I will show that you are just like every other Cooporate American joke...you can't finish what you start. You started something with me. I know and you know deep down that you won't beable to finish the job. You certainly can't do it all by yourself. You make yourself all big and bad, but in reality you're nothing more but a big chicken s[..]t. I don't fear you. I don't fear Leviation. I even do not fear your boss Commissioner Cruz. He is nothing but a poor, petty, pitiful man who's on a ego trip. What can Cruz do to me? Nothing. He can put any one of you sorry arsses and I will barrel them over like the trash you are. Nothing can stop me. I am an immortal. People like you can't stop me. I'm like Superman only I don't have any Kryptonite weaknesses. Oh yeah you look at me and see some little man before you, but on Fiesta you will find out soon enough that there is more to this mere little man than you realize. You will come to the truth. You will know who I am." [He walks over to the bar again and pulls out another jar of Asparins and pops one in his mouth then washes it down with another gulp of juice from the jar.] Zion: "Sorry about that, but all this talk about jokes gave me another headache. In closing I regress to your promo when you thanked all of us so-called dumb bastards for making your night. I respond and add on by saying, you're welcome dumb f[..]k, now reap the whirlwind because the Cell is going down one way or the other, and it all starts with me taking you down. Proving that you can't finish what you start. You can't and you won't do it. This is my vow. Now I've said more than enough for now. As far as my other oponants like Enigma and Chance. Don't think that for one moment I am finished with you. I just know when to cast you aside for the time being. I will beat you and unify my title once and for all. Then once when it is unified and I am proclaimed as the one true champion, then I will keep it around me until somebody beats me. Since I don't see that happening anytime soon, I will have that title on me for a long long time. Everybody has these talks about Enigma regaining the title or Chance proving he is the true champion and getting his time to shine. What people fail to realize is everyday is Zion time. Everyday is my day. I will see the prize, take it, and keep it. If you don't believe me then wait and see...you will believe. Now with that said, I have a match to get ready for. Fade out, I'm done. See you on Fiesta Lockdown." [The camera complies and cuts the camera out to black.] *FADE TO BLACK*
