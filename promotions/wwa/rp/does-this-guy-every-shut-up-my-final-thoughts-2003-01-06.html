"Does this guy every shut up? (My Final Thoughts.)"

6 Jan 2003

By Jimmi Zion

Item 2 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 6 Jan 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
*[Fade In]... *[We open up in the locker room of "Sky Flyin" Jimmi Zion (Zinn) and his beautiful wife and manager, Mrs. Mikalia Rush-Zinn. They are one of the first people who have arrived in the arena for Meltdown. Jimmi is already dressed out in his wrestling attire going through some last minute stretching while Mikalia is watching the moniter close by. Upon closer observation reveals that it is the latest promo from Enigma in his final thoughts on Zion and Chance going into tonight. Jimmi is on the ground doing full squat stretching exercises with his chest on the ground. It's more than obvious that Jimmi is listening to the promo, because you can hear laughing come from him while he is face down. Mikalia watches and listens with a little more careful intent.] Jimmi Zion: [He brings his hands in into a 90 degree angle, he brings his leg in together and then throws them up into the air until he is complete straight up in the air doing a hand stand where he does a hand standing pushups.] "My gawg, does he ever shut up? He just goes on and on about how much he's gonna kick mine and Chance's ass. I can't wait to get in the ring just so I can see just how much of him is talk and what little of him he actually means. When I talk it, I walk it. When I walk it, I do it. I can't wait to find out just how much is him blowing smoke." Mikalia Rush-Zinn: "You do know that this is not a triple threat match, it's a handicap match." Zion: "But it's for both the X-treme and Cruiserweight titles is it not?" Mikalia: "Correct." Zion: "Then it's not a handicap match. It's a triple threat match. Matty Warr needs to be a little more realistic when booking his matches. Now granted, I will say this much, Chance and I might...I said might work as a team to take out Enigma from the match. Once he's gone, I will turn all of my intentions on Chance. I will fight to win. I still want to finish my fight with Chance that I started last week before that pissant Enigma interfered. I will fight Chance and I will not rest until I am holding both of those titles high in the air, and I am being proclaimed as the new WWA X-Treme/Cruiserweight champion. Now granted, I will say this, when I win the titles in this match. I will give Chance the first shot at the titles in a true one one one winner take all affair. I am not some prima donna...well not totally. Even I have a sense of sportsmanship, and I am definately sure Chance earned it. Enigma only deserves to licks the s[..]t off my boots. He is a morlock. He is beneath me. I will give Chance credit, he is a class act. Personally, I can't wait to kick Enigma's sorry arss just to shut him up." Mikalia: "But baby, who's to say that you will shut him up? Don't you think that you will piss him off even more by taking away his titles and proving you right?" Zion: "Mikalia, we both know that he would never do that. Even when I beat him, he would never do such a thing. He's childish like that. He is an unbeliever. He is a fool. The eniviable will hit him. I will reveal the undeniable truth that he has met his match. He has no chance in heaven or hell. He will fall at my feet in defeat. It doesn't matter if he can't accept it. Nevertheless it will happen." "Now they call it a 'ego'. They say that I am suffering with some 'prima donna' complex. They think that I am not being a team player. In fact I am being very realistic. I wanna win. I am a winner. I'm going to win, and there is not a damn thing that Enigma or my so-called partner Chance can do about it. I will win. I know what it takes and I will do whatever I can to be on top. After the match, I might be a class act, but during the match it's another story." "I also believe that Chance deep down feels the same way I do. Take out the champion, and then turn his attention to me. He wants to rise above his injuries he has substained at the hands of Enigma. His motives are almost all about vengence. That will be his undoing. His motives need to be more broad and less personal. That's why I will succeed and he will fail. Chance is walking into this match filled with emotions. I have the best chance of winning this match. I am confident, but not over confident, and I am not taking this match personal. Chance has all of his attention on Enigma. He will forget about me, if even for a moment and then that's when I will have him right where I want him. Make no mistake about it. I will take him out and immediately move in for the kill." Mikalia: "What about Enigma? Doesn't he have all of his attention on you? Won't that cause a problem for you?" Zion: "No, and I'll tell you why my love because Enigma is just like Chance. He has made this all personal. I have him so damn bloody pissed off, be can't see straight. He can not see passed his blood shot eyes. He is so filled with emotion. I haven't made this match personal despite what he did to me. He even said it best when he told me last week. Nothing personal, wrong place at the wrong time. I'm not sweating what he did because I will have the last laugh. I will take the very thing that he holds dear. That will be my retribution on Enigma for what he did to me last week. I will take back his titles...er I mean my titles. He tells me nothing can stop me. Whether he likes it or not. I can and I will stop him. I will do it as soon as tonight in that very ring. I will take the titles. I will pick up where I left off last year. I can promise you that." "I'm not scared of Enigma. In fact, Enigma has given me no real reason to be scared of him. I don't take him seriously. He is a fool who is blind to the light. The light of me. Everyday is my day. He will learn of this sooner or later. I guess he'll have to learn it later...like as in later on tonight in that ring." Mikalia: "Hey tell Enigma about what you think about understanding his rage and anger. I think an object lesson will do wonders for Enigma." Zion: "With pleasure baby." [Zion walks over to a chair and sits down in it. He pretends like he is an old man in a chair, cocks his head back and says in a rouch english voice talking like the Emperor Palpitine from Star Wars Episode 6 Return of the Jedi.] Zion: "You want me don't you? Go on do it. Strike me down with all of your rage and anger then take your place by my side." [Zion stands up and takes a mock bow while Mikalia gives him a mock applause.] Zion: "Not bad huh, did it sound like him alright." Mikalia: "George Lucus will be calling you to be in the next Star Wars film." Zion: "I thought so. How did it fit Enigma's rage?" Mikalia: "He'll be pissed, like that's anything new. The more pissed off you get him baby, the easier it will be for you to beat him later on tonight." Zion: "Precisely. In other words Enigma, that's what I honest to goodness think about your anger and rage. It's a joke, just like you as a champion. You have the audacity to call me a joke and you have no room to talk. If you didn't like that impression, then maybe you might like this one. Mikalia take off your pumps for a moment." Mikalia: [Talking while taking off her pumps] "Sure baby what for." Zion: "You'll see." [Mikalia hands Zion her high heel pumps. Zion places the pumps on the ground right in front of him then gets down on his knees putting them in the shoes. Then he picks up a stick and makes it look like a cane. Zion then starts talking like Yoda from Star Wars.] Zion: "Hmmm sadness leads to hate, hate leads to anger, anger leads to suffering...or something like that hmmm. That is path of darkside hmmmm." [Zion stands up and hands the pumps back to Mikalia where she puts back on her own feet. He then looks back at the camera again.] Zion: "Again Enigma, listen to Yoda. He might be right, but then again listen to yourself. Let your anger be your weapon and you'll find out just how much it will be you're downfall. Bottomline Enigma, continue to let me get you all pissed off. Continue to let my words bubble up inside you. Continue to let your hate for me grow more and more. I want you to hate me. I want you to hate me so much you wanna kill me. Guess what b[...]h, that means I have you exactly right where I want you. It means I already got you beat." "Everybody wonders why I have 'an ego'. Well when you are so pissed off at me that it clouds your judgement, of course I am going into the match KNOWING that I have the match won. Face it Enigma, you shouldn't even show up. I already knew that I had this match won and the match hasn't even started yet, and it's all because of you Enigma. So from the bottom of my heart Enigma, I thank you for almost literally handing me your titles. That's exactly what your doing by letting the hate boil up inside you. You just handed the titles to me." "So in closing Enigma, I mean this from the bottom of my hear to, I thank you. Consider yourself truly beat Enigma." "As for you Chance, you can be my partner...for a while. Like as in only a season, but once Enigma is taken out don't think that I won't spare you from the same fate Chance. The partnership will be over and I will take you out no different than we did Enigma. In fact, don't be surprised if I do worst to you than what we did to him. This is not an ego thing, this is a me thing. This is me seizing my day. I'm seizing all of my days." [A knock is heard at the door and a head peeks in. It's a WWA road agent.] Road Agent: "Jimmi Zion, it's almost time for your match. You'll be up in about a few more minutes." Zion: "Okay thank you very much. We'll be out in a minute." [The road agent nods as he leaves.] Zion: "That's our que. It's show time. Time is up for Enigma and Chance. Zion time is officially here. Let's go." Mikalia: "Good luck baby." Zion: "Who needs luck, I have you." [Zion gives Mikalia a kiss on the lips before walking out the door with her in hand. As the door shuts behind them, the camera fades out.] *FADE TO BLACK*
