"The lines are being drawn."

13 Feb 2003

By Jimmi Zion

Item 5 of the fed's own "WWA Insider" issue dated 13 Feb 2003, a compiled multi-author RP digest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
*[Fade In] *[Vancover-BC, Canada} *[Cut to the interior of a hotel room penthouse suite over looking the city below during the evening time. It's a couple of days following the smashing Meltdown followup to the phenomenal Rage In A Cage 6. The WWA camera crew moves around the suite scanning for signs of life. After a few moments the camera follows the trail of laughter leading out onto the balcony. The camera steps out to reveal current WWA X-Treme and Cruisereight champion, "Sky Flyin" Jimmi Zion and his beautiful wife and manager, Mrs. Mikalia Rush-Zion. They are both relaxing together on a lounge beg that they are sharing. Mikalia is resting in between Zion's legs. She is wearing only a long white ladies blouse and a white thong underwear underneath the shirt exposing a nice firm thigh. She is barefoot the rest of the way. Zion is wearing light brown Khacki cargo pants and no shirt exposing a nice, firm, ripped, muscular upper body. He long black dreadlocks are loose and waving down. They are sipping on glasses of champaine and talking like true married couples do when they spend time together alone...outside the bedroom. The camera moves into position and zooms in on them, lets listen in.] "Sky Flyin" Jimmi Zion: [Holding up his glass in a toast fashion.] "To me, the X-treme/Cruiserweight champion. And to you, who I couldn't of done it without." Mrs. Mikalia Rush-Zion: "To you and me...forever." [Zion and Mikalia drink their glasses, and then Zion casually tosses his glass into Mikalia's glass knocking it into her hand causing it to shatter on the balcony floor. He gives Mikalia a sensual kiss on the cheeck.] Zion: "I think that we gave you plenty of time to pull yourself together after getting attacked by those bloody assholes on Monday night. I couldn't even tell that you were attacked." Mikalia: "Although the new make-up I applied covered up the inflicted wounds they did to me. Still it didn't change what they did to me." Zion: "I know baby and I'll get them back. They won't lay another hand on you ever again." Mikalia: "Maybe, but you have more important things to do for now like getting ready to finish what you started with Chance and since he is the number one contender, there's nothing that will stop you two from fighting again. You also have a match next week on Meltdown against Joey K. Let me take care of them. Besides I have a score to settle with that bitch, Ms. Wise." Zion: "I mean it if Lockdown or Leviathan ever lay a hand on you again they will truly know what hell and damnation is all about. I'll do whatever it takes to protect you my beloved." Mikalia: "I know you will and that's why I have nothing to worry about. I still want to tear Ms. Wise limb from limb from every other limb." Zion: "Oh you will, and I'll make sure you get your chance." In the meantime I wanna take my focus off of the Cell for now and onto my very first title defense against Joey K. Oh where to begin love? What do you know about him" Mikalia: "Well for one thing, I do know that is the type of man voted most likely to become the WWA's first resident drag queen. I hear rumors that their gonna start up a women's division, and Warr's thinking about putting him in it. He might do well in a women's division, but against you in the WWA X-Treme/Cruiserweight division, he is SO out of your league Jimmi." Zion: "So what are you saying Mikalia, you're implying that he's just some sissy ass who tries on women's clothing in his spare time?" Mikalia: [Sarcastic] "No, I'm not saying that [She grins slying while she taps her nose slightly twice winking at the camera] I'm just saying that he will be a good warm up while you prepare for your match against Chance for the title. You need a good warm up, but don't take him lightly." Zion: "I never do, but now that you think about it, he does seem like a real bloody jabroni. I mean his name is Joey K. What in the hell happened to his last name? What kind of a gimmick is Joey K? Can he even pronounce his last name? How stupid can a man get? He must be either ashamed of his namesake and his profession or totally oblivious to the absolute truth that is coming his way. In any case, he's about to get MY rude awakening. He's about to find out why I am the champion and I will not settle for losing to somebody named Joey K." Mikalia: Oh well, we know why this no name has this title match with you Jimmi. Isn't nice of the front office to give us a nice warm up to put us over? I mean they don't really think that he can beat Mister Millenium does he. He just needs to do his thing, take his bumps, and make you look really good as you prepare for the real challenge which is Chance...and anybody else that will pose a bonefide threat at your titles." Zion: "Like whom?" Mikalia: "Chance, Enigma, and hell even Leviathan and Lockdown could pose a threat against you. Since you hold the X-treme title as well you can fight them as well and they can take your title from you. We both know that won't happen...again, but we must be aware in case if the front office throws them in your path to try and make you stumble." Zion: "Those two are just a small stumbling block. They won't take me down and out. Everytime they try I just keep getting back up. Everytime they touch you it just makes me more and more pissed off. Which means when I fight this Joey K, I get to take out all of my frustrations out on him. He gets the living piss beat out of him, and I feel just a little better." Mikalia: "Do you feel any better right now Jimmi?" Zion: "With you, I always feel better, but I won't feel totally at peace until The Cell is buried and I am sitting absolutely pretty on the top of the mountain." Mikalia: "That may take a while." Zion: "Well I am not going anywhere any time soon, so I think I can set up tent on the mountain because it's gonna be a while." Mikalia: "Spoken like a true champion." [Zion kisses on Mikalia's neck, sensually tickling her with his lips as the camera fades out on their intimate time.] *[Fade Out]
