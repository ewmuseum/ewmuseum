RITC Preview/Predictions

22 March 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Rage in the Cage IX Live from The Metrodome Minneapolis, MN Monday, 26th March 2006 -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Open Invitational Battle Royale Winner Gets TV Title Shot The biggest PPV of the year will Blast Off with a bang with an over-the-top battle royale. It's every man for himself with the winner walking away with a Television Title shot, which he will claim later in the show. So who's in it? Well we don't know. It's an open invitational after all. Anyone that wants in, they just have to show up. It's a huge chance for any rookie or veteran to get themselves onto Rage in the Cage and to maybe even become a WWA champion when the night is over. "Finish Him Off" Match Danny Vista Vs Andy McMirra Ever since these two fought to a draw in their opening round tournament match, they have had nothing but contempt for each other. Last week on Meltdown Vik Ventressa finally agreed to let them fight each other. But the two of them weren't satisfied with a simple steel cage match. No, they demanded it be a "Finish Him Off" Steel Cage Match. The only way the match can end is if someone hits their finisher and than immediately exits the cage. Neither man has had a chance to show off their finishing move, but at RITC, at least one of them will. Will it be Vista's Military Justice that wins out or McMirra's Wipeout give him the "W"? Himalayan Pit in a Cage Match Shaman Vs Jorge Samuelsson Months ago Jorge make his shocking debut in the WWA by destroying the WWA legend Shaman. Right away that proved Jorge was not someone to mess with. Shaman has made his return finally and has sworn revenge on his rival. He's going to get his chance at RITC. But this time it's going to be under Shaman's rule. The match he created, only this time inside a steel cage. The Himalayan Pit is one of the most dangerous matches in WWA history and when it involves a pissed-off Shaman, it becomes almost deadly. Jorge also isn't a pushover and so this will be one hell of a Fight, but not on Friday Night. Tournament Finale Match WWA Extreme Championship Vs WWA Television Championship Silence (c) Vs Tommy Starr (c) After a month of great matches, the tournament comes to a close with two champions. Silence has marched through Mr. Internet, Ethan Cole, & Masked Messiah to reach RITC. Tommy Starr meanwhile has defeated Jorge Samuelsson and Kyle James. Neither has had it easy and now all that is left is each other. Along with winning the mysterious tournament prize, the winner will find himself a dual champion since both the Extreme and TV titles are on the line. But the winner's night will not be over. The newly crowned dual champion will have to defend his TV title right away against the winner of the Battle Royale earlier in the night. There's never any rest for the wicked and when you talk of wicked, you must be talking about Tommy Starr and Silence. Jonathan Winters Vs Jack Griffiths Only weeks ago Jonathan Winters was riding high. He was the US champion. He was the most talked about star on the WWA roster. He was defeated after 12 straight matches. Some were already wondering when he would receive his first World Title shot. Then Jack Griffiths came into his life. Jack became the first man to pin Winters. Even through it wasn't an official match, that pin started Winter's downward spiral. He failed to defeat Ember for the World Title. He lost his US title and his streak to Khaled. Now at the bottom, Winters is determined to reclaim his spot. The first step back to Glory? Destroy Jack Griffiths. It won't be easy, but if Winters fails, he'll have a new streak. A losing streak. Tag Team Warfare Match for the WWA Tag Team Championship The Detonators (c) Vs Kaos Vs Disciples of Blaze Vs The Franchise Players The Detonators were partying like there was no tomorrow, fresh off their victory over Havoc. Then those annoying Franchise Players came in and crashed the Fiesta. Followed by Disciples of Blaze. But they were not the ultimate party poopers. The Dets thought they could chose their first challengers by helping DoB defeat the Players in a Number One Contendership Match, but Vik stepped in and instead gave a shot to both teams and the returning KaOs. And he made it a Tag Team Warfare Match, where the Dets lose their championship advantage. Now on top of it all, because of the results of a Fatal Fourway, the champions have the worst entry spots in this unique match. What a buzz kill! Armaan Vs The Muppet Man The story behind this match is a disturbing one. Weeks ago the psychotic Steven Murphy kidnapped the Indian superstar Armaan. The enigmatic Muppet Man began his quest to rescue him. This lead to a number of encounters between Murphy and Muppet. But finally Muppet got his hands on Murphy. He was inches away from ending Murphy’s career when suddenly Armaan appeared and saved his abductor from harm. Thisstrange turns of events did something to Muppet’s mind. We can never be sure what exactly happened, but we know this: It was then that the evil within Muppet’s heart was reawaken. At the final Meltdown, he searched out Murphy to finish the job, but Armaan again tried to stop him, only for Muppet to turn and throw him through a glass door. And so this match was born. Will this twisted tale finally end at RITC or are there many more champions to be written? WWA United States Championship Match Khaled (c) Vs Mick Vain Ever since Khaled formed the Deadly Alliance with Ember, the two of them have bulldozed a path through the entire WWA, leaving countless victims in their wake. It sometimes seems like there is no hope, but finally there is. A former victim of Khaled’s has come back to claim payback. Mick Vain was taken out many months ago by the current US Champ and his partner. Now Vain has returned and he will not rest until he brings Khaled to justice. Khaled has finally tasted gold for the first time in his WWA career, but now the biggest test stands before him. He must win this because despite his victory, he could still go down as the biggest choker in the WWA if he loses at the biggest show. Main Event WWA World Heavyweight Championship Match Ember (c) Vs Mal Somers Ember may very well be the most dangerous wrestler in WWA history. His partner and he have earned the name Deadly Alliance. And ever since Ember’s World Title victory at Birthday Bash, he has had his sights set on the hugely popular Mal Somers. Destroying Somers would help eliminate what little hope is left and shroud the WWA in further darkness. Because of this, Ember has spent the last few weeks making sure Somers take as much physical damage as possible. But Ember also knows there is more to a man then his body. Eve4ry man has a soul and on the last Meltdown, Ember attempted to crush Mal’s. He did this by silencing Somers’ closest ally, Rookie, possibly forever. This action has made Mal’s mind snap and one must wonder if he can pull it together. Because he will need everything he has and even then, it may not be enough.
