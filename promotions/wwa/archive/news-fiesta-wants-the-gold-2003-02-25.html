Fiesta Wants The GOLD!!

25 Feb 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
First it was to be Grunge defending the WWA World Title against Wes Sanders, who earned the shot at Birthday Bash 2003. Then Matt Warr, unhappy with two of the people he hates the most duelling for the ultimate prize announced the match would become a three way, with a Meltdown representative becoming involved. With either Shaman or Nick Adams to fill this spot, Matt Warr is not happy at all! Now the news that Commissioner Bridge has decided to one up Warr by announcing the Main Event of Rage in the Cage will be a four way matchup, with a Fiesta representative to be determined! How you ask? For those who have been tuning into Insider, you will have seen most of the Fiesta roster staking their claim as to why they deserve the gold. Bridge has said he will simply choose the four most hungry for the gold and pit them in a tourney on the 1st March Fiesta for the right to the Title Shot!! So with no official lineup, and matches for all superstars to be decided at Bridge's whim at Fiesta, this is sure to be a memorable event, where each and every Fiesta superstar will have the chance to shine.
