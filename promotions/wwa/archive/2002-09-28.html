WWA Fiesta! Saturday 29th September 2002 Scene opens up inside of the office of Commissioner Cruz where Cruz is seated smoking a nice large Cuban cigar and watching the end of "Austin Powers: The Spy Who Shagged Me" laughing hysterically. A knock is heard at the door Cruz: Come in, who is?! Wallace Coleman enters with a bandage over his forehead to a nice pop Coleman: hysterically Commissioner Cruz! Oh my gosh! Oh my gosh! oh my gosh! Oh my gosh! Cruz: Wallace, please sit down, calm down! What is it chico? Coleman: Do you remember a couple of weeks ago when 'The Nefarious One' Steve Edmunds smashed that bottle of Brandy over my head? Cruz: Yes. Are you feeling okay. Cruz tries to put his backhand to Coleman's forehead, but Coleman averts his head away Coleman: that's the thing! I'm fine! I'm excellent! Cruz: Well, Wallace, thats great news! Muy bien hecho, good for you! But why are you so happy? Coleman: Well, while I was unconscious on my way to the hospital, I had this, this vision... Cruz: A vision??? Coleman: Yeah, it was as if my calling in life was playing on a projector in front of me? Cruz: What do you mean? Coleman: I mean, I now know what it is I was meant to do here in the WWA! Cruz: You do? Coleman: Yes, I do! Cruz: Wallace, I'm so happy for you! Coleman: Well, don't you wanna know what it is? Cruz: Of course I do. But Fiesta is about to start, and I have an important announcement to make regarding next weeks show. Coleman: Okay, well i'll just wait here til you get back. Cruz: No problemo. Better lock the door. Coleman: Good call. Cruz exits as Coleman locks the door. The scene switches to inside of the jam packed Reunion Arena in Dallas, Texas where the fans are absolutely deafening. "EVERYDAY" by Bon Jovi is heard but not as loud as it should be in comparison to the boisterous crowd. Several signs read "KASH WILL YOU MARRY ME" (held by a guy in a blue and white striped shirrt), "THIS IS LONGHORN COUNTRY", "GO MAVS", and "IIIIIITT'S SHOOOOOWTIIIIIME!". The scene finally switches to Mikal Shibaev and Chase Gordon seated at the announcers table Chase: barely audible FOLKS!! WELCOME TO FIESTA!!! WWA Fiesta! LIVE! We have four newcomers to the WWA here in this match.” Mikal: “Vell these men better be ready. Because they are all probably hungry.” The entranceway explodes with pyrotechnics. As the smoke clears we can see Dave Harley stood wearing a pair of black tights with 'Extreme Machine' written down the sides in silver. He also wears his obligatory sunglasses and a T-shirt which reads 'Too Hot For Monday Night'. He begins to strut down to the ring; oblivious to the chorus of boos he is receiving from the fans. As he reaches the ring, he grabs a microphone and calls for his music to be cut. Harley: Ladies and Gentleman. Here stands before you a true legend of professional wrestling. Here stands the last ever EWA Extreme Champion. I want all of you life-wasters to make a note of this night in your diaries, hell, take a picture to remember this night. Well in that case, you're gonna have to draw straws or something. You see when I'm fin. Deadly is cut short as…. The Reunion Arena goes pitch black. Electric pyro shoots from the Jumbotron down to the ramp simulating lightning bolts. Silver pyro explodes on each side of the ramp and extends from the stage down to the arena floor as the bolts make impact. The opening chords of "Ride the Lightning" by Metallica blast over the P.A. system as the lights come back up. Truck Maloy enters onto the stage wearing a black and deep green plaid shirt with the sleeves ripped out. His beat up straw cowboy hat with "Jack Daniel's" paraphernalia rests on his head. His blue jean shorts are slightly faded giving them a worn look. His boots are ordinary, black, ankle high Timberlands. Truck's wrestling attire is topped off with black shoot fighting gloves on both hands. Truck makes his way to the top of the ramp which has a line of white lights illuminating each side with a line of yellow lights down the center, simulating the look of a road. Truck stops at the top of the stage and raises his hand over his head as the crowd stands and cheers wildly nearly drowning out the hard metal sound of Metallica. Truck lowers his hands and walks down the middle of the ramp to the arena floor. Truck makes his way up to the apron using the nearest steel steps. He pauses at the turnbuckle and turns to the fans raising his right arm and then climbs into the squared circle. Truck takes off his hat and hands it to the time keeper at ringside. Truck trots around the ring to warm up, then stops near the announcer side of the ring and grabs the top rope. He leans back hard while tugging on the rope, stretching out his arms and getting the blood flowing for the match. Truck finishes his warm up and walks up and gets nose to nose with Dave Harley starring deep into his eyes. Suddenly all the lights go out for a brief second and when they come back on the arena is flooded with dark crimson light. A couple of spotlights sweep the audience quickly, back and forth. Above the entranceway pyro goes off sending a continuous waterfall of yellow sparks falling down around the jumbotron. Then an image of a coin appears on the jumbotron, it flies upwards through the air in slow motion as if tossed by someone. We can hear the slow rush of air as the coin turns over and over. Then when the coin has reached the top of its flight a voice rings out from the speakers. Voice: The hand has been dealt. The dice rolled. Now all we can do is trust to chance and play our final cards. The time for words is past, Now is the time for Fortune’s Favoured, Fortune’s fool. The coin falls to the floor and we here the metallic ring as it makes contact, and lands on its edge. The metallic ringing goes on for a second then fades away. Suddenly pyro explodes like a bomb from the entranceway, and “Staring at the Sun” by The Offspring blares out across the arena. “Maybe life is like a ride on a freeway Dodging bullets while you're trying to find your way Everyone's around, but no one does a damn thing It brings me down, but I won't let them” Brooke Prescott: “Ladies and Gentlemen! When he reaches the ring he jumps up to the apron, turns around back flips over the top rope.
