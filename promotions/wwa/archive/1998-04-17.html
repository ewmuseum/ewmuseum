WWA — 17 April 1998

17 Apr 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Friday April 17, 1998 The screen opens up to the the NCW banner. Then.... Northeast Champion Wrestling flashes through the screen and leaves the NCW Banner Then firework type explosions go off around the Banner. The Banner fades... then the screen shows the Bryce Jordan Center. The screen then goes to the inside to show the fans jumping and screaming at the Camera. The camera pans over the the Complex and then the camera finds Tony Bailey and Terry Valentine sitting behind the table in the broadcast area. Tony : Tonight's lineup is possibly worthy of PPV status. And I for one can't wait for the action to begin. Terry : You're right Tony, we've got the HOP in the ring and Randy Cross will regain the Northeastern title tonight. With those words "Thunderstruck" by AC/DC begins to blare throughout the arena and the Outlaw appears with Todd Logan who is giving instructions on their way to the ring. Terry : The bird brain will be shot done by that cowboy, mark my words! As soon as the Outlaw stepped into the ring "Fly" by Sugar Ray began to play and Griffin came down to the ring escorted by the New Regulators. The NEw Regulators take their "traditional" seats at ringside. Tony : It looks like Griffin is attempting to bow, like a martial arts type maneuver, but it doesn't look the Outlaw will return the bow. Terry : It looks like the bird is upset and Griffin flies in with a leaping side kick to the head and the Outlaw rolls outside of the ring to regroup. Johny : Outlaw rolls back in and these two are wasting no time as the Outlaw is tearing into Griffin with a series of punches, but now Griffin is fighting back. Tony : Griffin wisely backs off and makes the Outlaw come to him, that was a beautiful dropkick by Griffin who drops a quick elbow to the Outlaw's ribs. Terry : On the outside, Logan just turned to the New Regulators and is STARING a hole through them but he's not taunting or moving at them. Johny : Griffin is keeping his distance in this match and with those martial arts kicks he's in control. Tony : Outlaw ducks that spinning roundhouse kick and Outlaw catches him in a slingshot suplex. The Outlaw is now executing rolling german suplexes. Terry : After the fourth suplex the Outlaw is scooping up the roadkill ( chuckles ) and whips him into the turnbuckle but Griffin caught the Outlaw on his way in with a flying body smash. Johny : Griffin just out Outlaw on the top rope and looks like he's going for a huracanrana, but the Outlaw blocked it. Terry : The Outlaw reverses fields and superplexes Griffin, he's GREAT! Johny : Outlaw makes the cover and gets the 1-2-3. Griffin staggers to his feet and is now extending his hand and offers a hand shake which Outlaw accepts, that's amazing. Tony : Well if the rest of tonight's card is as good as the first match we're in for a magnificent night. Terry : Will you shut up, the bores are making their way to the ring. "End Of The World" by REM begins to play and the man known as Apocalypse slowly walks to the ring. Tony : Do you think Darken is still mad at the way their match went down? Terry : Who cares they both stink! In the middle of the first verse of REM's song "Can You Deliver?" by Armored Saint replaces it and Fifth Element makes his way to the action. Tony : It's plain to see that Apocalypse is definitely outweighed in this one and he ducks a charging Element and catches him with a running lariat. Terry : Element doesn't fall down and nails Apocalyse with a double axhandle blow to the skull that sends the smaller man down. Johny : Fifth Element is going for a vertical suplex but it's blocked and reversed into a snap suplex by Apocalypse. Tony : It looks like Element hurt his back coming down and Apocalypse is now putting the boots to him. Terry : If you say he's beating him like a rented mule I will personally beat the crap out of you. Johny : Easy now. Element charges in and get scooped up into the Nuclear Blast, what a smart move by Apocalypse and he scores the upset victory. Terry : Ladies and gentleman watch the House dismantle these two bit crybaby jobbers, the Birds of Praying. Tony : That's Birds of Prey. Terry : Whatever. The Birds of Prey come down to the ring and get laughed at by some of the members at ringside as Griffin holds a sign that say "Those Birds Can't Fly". Terry : I now present their executioners, Lancer and Eliem. The theme from Psycho begins to play and Big Eliem sprints down to the ring leaving Lancer in his wake. Tony : And Big Eliem is taking on both members of BoP and handling them fairly easily no less. Terry : It just goes to show one member of the House is equal to two members of the NCW! Johny : Then why are all 5 titles held by non House members? Terry : That's just temporary and I don't remember talking to you Morehouse, now shut up and do your job. Tony : It looks like Big Eliem is trying to serve notice to the champions and big name wrestlers of the NCW with this performance. He has absolutely MANHANDLED these two guys. Terry ( begins chanting ): HOP! HOP! HOP! Johny : It looks like Big Eliem is setting up for Da Sista of Mercy, the move starts off similar to a powerbomb, but the opponent is lifted up into an over the shoulder backbreaker. Tony : Now that Eagle is up on Eliem's shoulder, he's going to spin around 3, make that 4 times. He just slammed the man down dropping him chest and face first to the mat and Eliem/Lancer win easily. Terry : The big goon Jack will now fall victim to my man Kue. Johny : I highly doubt that considering the near 200 pound weight difference. Terry : Size doesn't mean anything you idiot. Johny : If that's what you've convinced yourself, I won't try to change your mind. Both, Johny and Tony laugh as Terry is left seething. A few hard rock guitar riffs blast over the PA and Jack aka Famine of the Horsemen walks out at a quick pace. Tony : It seems that Terry is upset and we had to cut off his mic due to some foul language he was using. Unforgiven II begins to play signaling the entrance of Kue who carried his pool stick to the ring once again. Tony : Jack absolutely dwarves Kue in this match and he's egging Kue on. Johny : Kue is firing martial arts blows and kicks to Jack's midsection but they're not having much of an effect. Terry ( somewhat stifled ): I'm OK, can you put my mic back on now please? Tony : Kue is giving Jack everything he's got but it's almost as if Jack is enjoying watching Kue try and take him down. Terry : What have you guys been saying about me behind my back huh? Johny : It looks like Jack has had enough and he's starting to make his move. There's one massive clothesline followed by a huge splash but Kue kicks out at 2. Tony : Jack with a scoop and a slam and there goes another, Kue is dazed. Uh oh here comes trouble, it's the Irish Hammer and Outlaw and they came carrying cue sticks. Terry : Hey they are just walking around the outside of the ring and pointing at Jack as if to say that his time will come. Johny : Kue is being mauled by this beast and he just got sent for a devastating ride Jack calls the chokeslam! Tony : Kue is out of it and suffers a defeat. It looks like the Outlaw is yelling something at Jack and the two men from the House just left the ringside area. Johny : This next match puts the Minnesota Wrecking Crew against the Anarchy. Interesting enough this is the Anarchy's first match in the NCW. Terry : Tough draw for the Anarchy in the House of Pain. Too bad, I like these guys. "Frayed Ends of Sanity" by Metalica starts to play as The Anarchy, Raiden and Chaos come to the ring. Tony : That is funny, whre is their manager Havok? Terry : Who knows.... "Hey Man, Nice Shot" replaces Metallica as the Minnestota Wrecking Crew enter the arena to a chorus of boos. Terry : There are the winners of this match. Tony : Well the Anarchy are definatly ready for this match as they rush to two smaller opponents. Johny : Raiden and Chaos are beating both Crocketts to the ground. Terry : Smart move by both members of the House of Pain as they roll out to the floor to re-evaluate their strategy. Tony : The referee reaches 8 and Zack enters the ring as the legal man against Raiden. Johny : The two men lock up, but Raiden crushes Zack to the mat with an Axe hadle to the back. Tony : Aric charges into the ring and Raiden catches him in the air. Raiden slams him right next to Zack on the mat. Terry : Raiden goes to pick up Zack. Zack with a great move.... Was that a punch to the solar plexus. Tony : Yeah if Raiden's Anatomy is different from the rest of the world. Raiden falls to the mat from an OBVIOUS low blow from Crockett. Johny : Zack tags in Aric and Aric wastes no time in capitalizing on the downed Raiden. Boots to the head followed by a couple of Elbows. Terry : That's right the Minnesota Wrecking Crew is back to their form. Tony : Who is that coming doe to the ring. Johny : It looks like Havoc, Anarchy's manager. He is yelling at the Referee.... Something about the House of Pain is coming to the ring. The referee is argueing with Havoc as Havoc jumps up onto the apron. Terry : Hey there is that masked man with the 2x4 again. What is he doing here? Tony : He is getting into the ring and DOWN goes Zack as the masked man hits him with the 2x4. Johny : Aric turns around and gets nailed the same way his brother did... Terry : SECURITY!!! Where is security.... Tony : It's not security, but here comes someone from the rafters on a zipline or something.... Jonhy : IT'S BRITAINICCA! He lands in the ring. He got a fire extinguisher... Terry : Finally someone to break this up. Britainicca starts spraying the extinguisher at the Anarchy and they flee the ring with their friend the masked man. The lights of the arena start to flicker. Eventually the lights are out. When they come back on the Anarchy is gone and the Crocketts are sprawled out in the ring and Britainicca is standing over them with his fire extinguisher. Tony : Uh, the Anarchy is gone. What is Britainicca doing? Johny : He just put the fire extinguisher Zack Crockett's shoulder. He is climbing up to the top turnbuckle. Terry : No! What is he doing?! Tony : Britainicca executes his pattented "Union Jack Bomb" onto Zack and the fire extinguisher. Zack doesn't even move as he is probably unconscious. Terry : Harrington fine that BUM!!! He had no right! Tony : Why don't you tell Britainicca yourself. Here he comes... Terry jumps up from his seat and down from the announcers stage. Britainicca just points at him and Terry cowers at the attention. Britainicca step up to Tony and begins to speak. Britainicca : Zack Crockett, Revenge is a B!^(#, and is best served with a ... FIRE EXTINGUISHER! Britainicca leaves the announcers stage by jumping towards Terry and making a feint his way. Terry just turns and runs away into the backstage area. Tony : Wow! That was quite a statement by the Englishman. I like it because he got rid of the big mouth for a few minutes. Johny : You can say that again! Tony : This next match is for the CWC NYC Title and is Shaman's first shot at a CWC title. "Devil Man" by White Zombie begins to play and Shaman walks out with his partner Mad Coil to cheers and good wishes from the New Regulators. Johny : Angel seems pretty focused on retaining his title which he won for the second time 4 weeks ago. And if he does so he will have a shot at holding two titles simultaneously this weekend. The fans who were expecting the usual Reaper entrance were surprised as he switched musical selections for tonight. L7's "The Masses Are Asses" began playing and he casually strolled to ringside wearing a t-shirt with that saying on the front and "Angel Reaper, the CWC's BEST!" on the back. Needless to say boos emitted from the crowd but he didn't seem to care one bit. Tony : We are now rejoined by yellow Terry. I guess Angel is referring to his new ranking as the CWC's most hated individual. Terry : Shut up Tony! However, I do agree with the front portion of his t-shirt though. Johny : They lock up immediately and Shaman uses his weight to power Reaper into the corner where they begin trading knife edge chops. Tony : Did you hear that thud off of Shaman's chest? I bet that Reaper's handprint will be there in the morning. Terry : This is chaos, I love it they don't seem to be "respecting" each other now as the fists are flying from all angles. Johny : Shaman seems to be getting the better of this exchange and maybe by turning this into a brawl he is capitalizing by not having Reaper use his technical skills. Tony : I agree, Reaper is one of the better technicians in the CWC and an all out brawl is definitely not what suits him. Shaman is beginning to wear down the smaller champ now with those vicious double axhandles. Terry : Maybe that punk will finally drop the title he's been hogging for like 2 months now. Did I mention he is my LEAST favorite wrestler yet tonight? Hey here comes someone I respect. Tony : It's Todd Logan again but it looks like he just came out to watch the match and he's staring at the New Regulators again. Johny : Reaper ducks a stiff clothesline attempt and shoots a double leg and flips Shaman onto his back, and that is not the place to be when facing Reaper. Tony : He's going for that Japanese armbar which he uses as a prelude for Angelic Redemption but Shaman has him well scouted and reaches the bottom ropes. Terry : If he was smart he'd just keep the hold on and get DQ'ed. Johny : That's not his style and why would he waste a loss like that? Anyhow Shaman has made it back up to his feet and is being kneed in the midsection in the corner. Tony : Shaman is fighting back with one arm as that left arm was injured in the armbar and these two are showing no signs of fatigue just yet. Terry : Reaper blocks a right and uppercuts Shaman in the corner, a swift kick to the gut and Shaman is doubled over. Johny : Reaper cinches in the butterfly and executes a beautiful double underhook suplex, he's got the bridge locked in and the victory. Tony : Reaper bends down and helps Shaman up and pats him on the shoulder before raising his hand and leaving. Terry : What a dispicable display of sportsmanship! "Gangsta's Paradise" by Coolio starts over the PA as DKC enters the arena to the boos of the audience. Terry : This is another match with great potential.... Tony : Terry? What... Terry : To MAKE ME FALL ASLEEP! You didn't let me finish. Tony ( Shaking his head ): Figures... Johny : This is DKC's third shot at the Overlords. They are definately due for a win. "Sad but True" by Metallica replaces Coolio. The Overlords enter the arena to a surprising positive responce to the CWC National and Northeast Tag Champions. Johny : Well, it will be Stiehl and Talon starting things off. Tony : The two lock up and Steihl wastes no time to power the smaller man into the corner. Terry : Steihl looks bored with this match. Oh... He gets nailed on the break with a kick to the midsection from Talon. Tony : Talon immediately backs Steihl into their corner and begin to double team Steihl. Johny : Oh NO NOt Again!!! The lights start to flicker and go out. When they comeback on the Anarchy and the masked man are standing at ringside. Terry : I am starting to like this group... Tony : Boy Terry, you sure can pick them. Johny : Inside the ring Steihl regains the upper hand with a clothesline an Tiburon who is the new legal man for DKC. Darken reaches out for the tag and Steihlt turns his back and mounts an assualt on the fallen Tiburon. Tony : What was that about? Steihl doesn't tag in his partner. Terry : Because Steihl wants to WIN! Tony : What is that commotion up on the ramp? "Hey Man, Nice Shot" by Filter starts up and the House of Pain emerges from the curtains. Randy Cross, Outlaw, Jak Lancer, Aric Crockett, Chris Rogers, Big Eliem and Kue are all walking to the ring pointing at the Anarchy. Johny : Look at Darken... It appears that he has a slight smile on his face as he nods. Terry : The House of Pain here in full force! How are we so lucky? Tony : OH MY GOD!!! The House of Pain and the Anarchy are in a FULL brawl outside of the ring. Johny : Look at the New Regulators! They are jumping up and down cheering as the brawl continues all around the ring and up the ramp. Terry : Meanwhile in the ring Steihl is in full control of the tag match still refusing to tag in Darken. Tony : That's the sign for "The Steihl". Steihl has Tiburon set up on the top rope and DOWN! Terry : Uhh.. That is going to leave a mark. Steihl with the pin and the Overlords defend thier titles again. Yippie... I'm so happy... Johny : Meanwhile the brawl is starting to die down as security is breaking it up and sending the combatants on their way. Tony : It's now time for our main event, a steel cage match between current Northeastern Heavyweight Champ, Nikodemous Segier and the former champ Randy Cross!!! The first man to enter the cage was the leader of the House of Pain, Randy "The Painmaker" Cross who made his way down to the song "Hey Man Nice Shot" by Filter. Terry : There he is the man, the myth, THE LEGEND! ( stands and applauds ) Johny : He is also the EX-champ and I was wondering Terry, are you going to ask him to go steady with you after the match? You hear Terry Valentine on the verge of a curse filled tirade when mercifully his mic is turned off again. Nikodemous Segier made his way to the cage to the Chumbawumba smash hit "Tubthumping". Tony : The bell has sounded and the match begins with a vengeance they are pummeling each other with rights and lefts. Wait, a quick thumb to the eye by Segier sends Cross a step or two away. Terry : Here comes the rest of the best damned stable alive today, the House of Pain. laughs Hey they're taunting those dweebs the New Regulators, this is good stuff. Johny : Segier is whaling away at Cross' midsection and just fired him face first into the steel and Cross is dazed. He steps backwards right into an atomic drop that propels him into the cage once more. Tony : That was pretty crafty, Segier follows in and misses the clothesline, smacking into the cage as Cross fell to the ground. Terry : Randy has now locked on a side headlock and is grinding it in to give pretty boy Segier cauliflower ears. Johny : Segier is getting up to his feet and delivers two quick elbows to the short ribs and there's a bodyslam and a quick cover but Cross kicks out at 1 1/2. Tony : It is now Segier in firm control and he has Cross up by his hair and delivers a violent punch to the adam's apple before tossing Cross into the cage once more. Terry : HEY, he can't grind Randy's face into the cage like that, what is he some kind of damn animal? Johny : If Cross had been smart enough to think of that you'd be tossing him bouquets like always. Nik is signaling to me that his new move the Segier slam is on it's way, it's a version of the powerslam where Nikodemous lifts up his opponent for an Atomic Drop and drives him to the mat on his tailbone. Tony : Nicely described and perfectly executed by Segier, Cross is wincing in pain and bloodied above his right eye. Terry : What is the ref saying about a low blow, Randy didn't SEE that punch land and assumed he caught him in the gut, which is pretty flabby if you ask me. Johny : Maybe YOUR gut is but Nik Segier is in tremendous shape when he's not being blatantly punched in the groin! Tony : Cross has taken advantage of that low blow and is working on a grapevine of the leg now. He's going to try and soften him up for that figure four finisher of his. Terry : Randy Cross is the greatest tactician I have ever seen. Cross just drive his knee to the left shin of Segier's and he's crying like a little baby now. Johny : A low blow is by no means a technical move, and Segier is in a world of trouble as Cross is cannonballing the knee across the ropes. Tony : Segier is crawling away but Cross grabs a hold of him and tosses him into the cage busting him open, and as he caroms off Cross hits a release German suplex, an excellent maneuver. Terry : Randy may have just blinded himself as he is struggling around the ring. That little cheater deliberately cut him since he knows he couldn't win otherwise! Johny : Amazingly Segier is back up to his feet and somehow executed the Segier Hammer, which is a double underhook face slam after which Nik stays in place to hold his opponent down. He's now hooking the arm and Nik applies an armlock/hammerlock in which he's pulling the elbow towards him as far and hard as he can. Tony : Cross is in a world of trouble now as Segier may hyperextend his elbow if he doesn't hurry to the ropes. Terry : Cross does reach the rope and both men are fatigued and bloody but still looking to fight. That's what is so great about Randy, he has the heart of a champ. Johny : What about Segier? He could have submitted earlier in the match but is till toughing it out. There is a blatant thumb to the eye by Cross and an inverted atomic drop by him sends Segier back before he clotheslines him to the mat. Tony : Cross is going for the Figure Four and he's got it locked in Segier has nowhere to go, the only way he can escape is to reverse the hold and put the pressure on Cross's knees instead. Terry ( sings horrendously off key ): WE ARE THE CHAMPIONS! WE ARE THE CHAMPIONS! Johny : Hold on a minute, Segier has in fact reversed the figure four and Cross wisely releases the hold. Tony : Both men are in sheer agony as they can barely stand but Cross is like a wolverine and places the figure four on Segier AGAIN! Terry : Tap out you idiot, TAP OUT! Johny : Segier will not tap and again turns the figure four over as Cross doesn't have much strength left and he lets loose of the hold once more. Tony : Neither man seems to have any energy left but they are pulling up with the aid of the ring ropes and driven by sheer hatred for one another they fight some more. Terry : Segier goes for a snap suplex, but it's blocked and Randy reverses and fires him over. YES! Johny : This match has been going on for roughly 37 or 38 minutes and it seems that last suplex by Cross knocked the wind out of both of them. Tony : Randy Cross is stirring as the blood continues to ooze from above his right eye. Terry : That's it Randy GET HIM! Cross is crawling slowly over to Segier who has passed out from the beating he has received and puts his arm across Segier's chest. The referee counts....1.......2.............3!!! HE DID IT! HE DID IT! I KNEW HE WOULD, RANDY'S THE BEST!!! Johny : As nausea sets in, here come the House into the cage to pick up their leader and celebrate. We're out of time, we'll see you next time!
