The Gamblers Table — Birthday Bash 2002

16 Aug 2003

Bylined "The Gambler" (Kevin/Vic Sage), the fed's own in-house prediction columnist persona.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Welcome to the third installment of the Gambler’s Table. This is the column where I, “The Gambler” Kevin Sage, share my predictions for the winners. Along the way, I’ll do what I can to explain my reasons for the picks. Here we are, just days away from Birthday Bash 2002, and I can honestly say that I am anxious to see the outcome of the glorious nineteen-man battle royal. Four titles, five if we count both of the Tag Titles individually, will be up for grabs, and every competitor will be hungry to get a piece of them. Before we get to that, we’ll be treated to a number of other great matches. Let’s take a look at the astonishing lineup that the WWA is serving up. Mack Tenn vs. The Texas Longhorns The newcomers of Mack Tenn, Simon Mackinaw and Kenneth Tenn, step into the ring against the mammoth Texas Longhorns, Floyd and Ace Murdoch. Mackinaw has potential as a great cruiserweight and Tenn has already displayed his incredible strength in the ring. Despite the attention that this team has already grabbed, they have been unsuccessful as yet due to what appears to be a lack of experience. On the other hand, the Texas Longhorns have decades of experience in the squared circle in innumerable federations around the world. Factor in the fact that they weigh over half a ton combined and things begin to look grim for Mack Tenn. I expect great things out of Mack Tenn in the future, but unfortunately not tonight. If this match were taking place a few months from now, when Mack Tenn gains more experience, I would be happy to place my bets with them. But, this time I’m pinning my hopes on TXL. Rookie Title WWA Rookie Champion Barrett Sinclair vs. Dave Harley vs. Mac Maloy vs. Mal Somers Without a doubt, these are some of the hottest rookies in the WWA today. The reigning WWA Rookie Champion, “Hurricane” Barrett Sinclair, will certainly have his hands full as he attempts to retain that belt against three other hungry, up-and-coming stars. “The Extreme Machine” Dave Harley comes to us from the popular, but now defunct, EWA. With a versatile arsenal of in-ring strategies and the strength to back it up, Dave Harley is not one to be underestimated. Unfortunately, his recent performance, while impressive, has not yet been enough to overcome his opponents. Don’t discount him yet, though. The last ever EWA Extreme Champion may just be getting warmed up so far in the WWA. Mac “Truck” Maloy is another wrestler that the WWA is privileged to have gained from the EWA. With his fireplug physique and the support of the fans, he has fought in some great matches already and a great future is almost assured. The upper echelons had best keep an eye on this one. The fourth competitor in this match, and yet another recruit from the EWA, is “The Chairman” Mal Somers. While searching for the whereabouts of his missing valet, Miss Wise, Somers had an inauspicious start in the WWA. Since those first couple of matches though, Somers has begun to gain momentum with his silver chair in hand. That building momentum could be in jeopardy though, now that he has found Miss Wise. It turns out that she’s working as the personal assistant to Commissioner Cruz on Fiesta! This revelation may prove to be too much of a distraction in the Rookie Title matchup, or it may be the focusing drive that he needs. With these talented grapplers in the ring, it’s tough one to call, but I’ll be placing my money on the incumbent, Barrett Sinclair. Cruiserweight Title Two Out of Three Falls Cruiserweight Champion Ekosyde vs. Grunge Grunge has his sights set on regaining the Cruiserweight Championship that was once his. With his impressive collection of aerial attacks and kickboxing knowledge, Grunge is easily one of the best cruiserweights in the division today. Add to this his impressive win over the legendary Tracer Bullet and the Youth United member looks to be poised for further gold in the WWA. But in order to regain the Cruiserweight Title, he’ll have to go through another talented competitor, Ekosyde. Ekosyde, with his own impressive collection of daring aerial attacks, wrested the chance at the title away from Grunge a month ago during what should have been a three-way dance with then-Champion Ramsey Kaine. Kaine’s unfortunate trouble with controlled substances left the belt up in the air for Grunge and Ekosyde, and Ekosyde earned the belt in one of the hardest-fought battles of his career. Last week on Meltdown though, Ekosyde walked away from the ring and the fans, stating “Maybe I should just leave.” Although we’re not sure what this means for the Cruiserweight Title, I can’t see Ekosyde walking away that easily. After all, regardless of the capricious affection of the fans, Ekosyde is still a top-notch competitor. Of course, Grunge has been eager to reclaim the belt ever since losing it. Despite Ekosyde’s recent winning streak, Grunge took him to the limit before and I think he’ll do it again. I’ll bet on Grunge to reclaim the Cruiserweight Title in this phenomenal matchup. Extreme Title WWA Extreme Champion ??? vs. Chance The big question on everyone’s lips is, of course, who is this Mystery Man? With a string of seemingly random victims in the WWA, and his signatory question mark emblazoned card, the Mystery Man has made quite an impact. Is he a returning superstar? Or is he just a talented newcomer? Who is the man that captured the WWA Extreme Title over several other men, including Damien Thorne, in the brutal, blood-soaked battle on Alcatraz Island? After losing his opportunity in that same battle on Alcatraz Island, Chance gets another shot at the Mystery Man and the Extreme Title in St. Louis, Missouri. Chance is definitely one of the most talented newcomers in the WWA, and has shown that he is indeed Fortune’s Favored. His acrobatic, risk-taking style has endeared him to the fans, and gained the respect of the locker room. Not surprisingly, Chance is a personal favorite of mine, but we all know that Lady Luck can be a fickle mistress. Additionally, it’s nearly impossible to prepare for a match when one doesn’t even know whom the opponent is. That difficulty is compounded by the brutal nature of this extreme matchup. Unfortunately, I think the Mystery Man will retain the title, and that’s where I’ll put my money. Shaman vs. Magician Unknown Stipulations The Four Horsemen of the Apocalypse have seen their share of fits and starts in the past year. The original War, James Thornhill, proved to be a disappointment, and was replaced by one of the most promising rookies, Christyan Payne. Unfortunately, Payne disappeared soon after. Death, after months of cryptic warnings finally revealed himself to be the legendary Darken. Again unfortunately, we haven’t heard from him since. The only consistency in the Horsemen was the former Tag Team Champions, Shaman and Magician, Famine and Pestilence respectively. Their extraordinary teamwork, coupled with Shaman’s ferocious strength and Magician’s cunning, rocketed them to the top of the Tag Team division. Regrettably, the only constant of the Horsemen seems to have unraveled. On this past Meltdown, egos exploded into a backstage brawl. Both men claim to be the better, and a challenge was issued to settle the question. While Magician is certainly a wily one, Shaman can be just as devious. Factor in his nearly unstoppable power, his experience, and his mean streak, and Shaman is one dangerous competitor. In light of their performances in the Tag ranks (and I’ll probably pay for this), it seems to me that this match is a foregone conclusion. Magician added his part to the team, but Shaman really carried them. Despite the unknown stipulations in this match, I’ll bet on Shaman. Anything else would be sucker bet. Randy Cross vs. Damian Thorne Hell in a Cell Winner Takes Control of The House of Pain, Loser Leaves the WWA Possibly the hottest match on the card, outside of the Brawl itself, this will be one for the hall of fame. The legendary Randy Cross, four-time WWA World Champion, returned to the WWA at Labor Day Brawl, and immediately resurrected the infamous House of Pain. With Damian Thorne, the Minnesota Wrecking Crew, and Shane Dorian, the House looked ready to set the WWA on its ear. But then, Damian turned on Cross, vying for control of the HoP. Thorne issued a challenge, with the winner of the match gaining control of the HoP, and Cross accepted. Unfortunately, due to a highly suspect injury, Cross could not compete. In his place, Craven Moore handed a defeat to Damian Thorne. By no means was that to be the last of this heated war. Angered by the entire situation, Thorne challenged Randy Cross again, this time to a Hell in a Cell match with the victor gaining control of the HoP, and the loser leaving the WWA forever! The war escalated when Cross viciously attacked Damian Thorne during the Extreme Title Alcatraz brawl on Halloween, causing him to drop the Extreme Title and a lot of blood. On the following Meltdown, Randy Cross wrestled Tracer Bullet in an incredible display of technical excellence. Not content with allowing Cross to get away with the attack on Alcatraz, Thorne handed the victory to Tracer with a brutal brass knuckle attack on Cross. These two men hate each other with every fiber of their being. Now, they will meet in the steel cage to settle this once and for all. One man takes control of the House of Pain, and the other fades away into the obscurity of the past. Randy Cross is a legend and a genius, but Damian Thorne is one of the most dangerous men in the WWA. With the return of his manager, Stephanie, Damian Thorne may have just become the WWA’s worst nightmare. Despite Randy Cross’ incredible accomplishments, I wouldn’t bet against Damian Thorne at this point in his career. My bet goes with the “Baddest Mofo on the Show.” Birthday Brawl The list of competitors in this match pits the greatest athletes in the WWA against each other. Wes Sanders, Jackson Kraven, Tracer Bullet, Aaron Alongi, Axel Brundage, Phobos, Deimos, Showtime, Jim Black, Venny LeTrav, Justin Bourne, Craven Moore, Ian Tense, Shane Dorian, Nicholas Adams, Kash, Troy Walcott, Harley Trapper, and Joe Keane all have an opportunity to claim their dreams by stepping on the dreams of every other man in the Bash. This is gonna be great! Of course, there is no reliable way to make predictions about who will come out of the Birthday Bash with the gold, but that won’t stop the bookies from taking bets on it. Here are my own predictions, but remember that I have been wrong before. Tag Team Titles To begin with, the WWA Tag Team Titles are on the line. The first of these two belts will go to the man who draws first blood on an opponent. The second will go to the man that is able to score the first pinfall. This means that any two men could be thrust together as Tag Team Champions! We could even end up with Venny LeTrav and Phobos as the champs, or Showtime and Aaron Alongi! Now, that would be an odd couple. This year is one of the hottest years in memory for the Tag Team division of the WWA. With the four top teams in the WWA competing in the Birthday Bash, anything could happen. First, we have Reborn, the team of Jim Black and Showtime. Reborn captured the gold from Havoc a few weeks ago in a pitched battle. While Reborn is surprisingly effective as a team, Jim Black’s recent feud with Troy Walcott may become a barrier to their retention of Tag Team gold. Should Walcott choose to pursue his anger toward Jim Black, Showtime would be left out to dry against some very overpowering opponents. Havoc, Phobos and Deimos, have dominated the Tag Team scene for several months, but the bar has been raised. With the recent inclusion of Reborn and the American Luchadores, as well as the return of The Human Condition, Havoc is facing a whole new level of competition. Having lost their Tag Titles to Reborn, these giants will be looking for a little payback. The American Luchadores, Venny LeTrav and Justin Bourne, are the team with the most to prove, being the only team involved that has not held Tag gold in the past. Despite this, these two men are both former Rookie Champions, and their innovative styles are likely to carry them much further than expected. Then we have The Human Condition. Aaron Alongi and Axel Brundage have returned to the WWA after a nearly six month hiatus, after losing the belts to Havoc at the Independence Day Rumble. The WWA’s resident mercenaries have entered the exclusive employ of Damian Thorne and his House of Pain, giving the HoP a lethal entry into the Tag Team ranks. Despite The Human Condition’s reputation, I can’t consider them to be a safe bet. They’ve been out of the ring for a long time, and ring rust may become a detriment to their performance. The American Luchadores are as impressive as they come, and I could see this team becoming one of the greatest in history...but not at Birthday Bash. Still relatively new to the Tag Team division, I believe that bit of inexperience will hinder their chances. Havoc would love to become three time Tag Title holders, but their recent losing streak leaves me hesitant to place my hard-earned money on them. In the end, the kind of chaos that is inevitable in the Birthday Brawl could very well act in the favor of Reborn. As masters of chaos and disorder, I think the team of Jim Black and Showtime will find themselves right at home. My money’s on Reborn to retain the titles. Television Title The next title on the line is WWA TV Title. This hotly contested Fiesta belt can only be won by being the first to put someone – anyone – through a table. The reigning TV champion wouldn’t even have to be involved in the table destruction to lose that title. Such are the vagaries of the Birthday Brawl. Some of the WWA’s most promising young talent will be vying for Fiesta’s top belt. The current champion, Nicholas Adams will have his hands full with Kash, Mighty Joe Keane, and House of Pain member Shane Dorian. After losing the TV Title to Adams on Fiesta, I suspect that Dorian will be going into the Brawl in a rage. Shane Dorian’s mix of power and submission wrestling has carried him far in the WWA, and I would almost consider him the favorite going into the Brawl, but he’s got some serious competition. Kash nearly had the TV Title a few weeks ago in a match against Steve Edmunds, but Edmunds’ disqualification robbed him of the opportunity. Now he has the chance to show the fans that the Title should have been his all along. Kash’s well-rounded offense and tactical planning will present a challenge for every man in the Brawl. The cruiserweight Adams carries a no-nonsense approach with him to the ring, allowing him to focus on the match at hand. That kind of focus is a boon in any match, but it’s doubly important here. Unfortunately, I don’t think that Adams’ speed will be to his benefit in this table match, considering that he’ll have to contend with the power of Kash and Dorian. Although he’ll add excitement, I don’t see him with the win. Mighty Joe’s technical skill adds another element to this bout. The WWA’s resident superhero and friend to the World Champ always puts on a great show. While his talent can never be discounted, Mighty Joe seems to have become inconsistent lately. Perhaps with a little more time, Joe could be one of the greatest TV Title Champions we’ve ever known. Joe’s inconsistency will provide just the opportunity that any of these stars need. Adams will put up a great fight, but I believe the competition will simply be too much for his inexperience. Although Shane Dorian is probably the most dangerous man in the TV Title race, I think Kash can overcome him with a little planning and perhaps with a little aid from his “magic.” In the end, I expect to see Kash crowned as the well-deserved TV Champ. United States Title Next up, we have the WWA US Title. This one should be a lot of fun. The first man to place both feet outside of the arena will be awarded this prestigious title. I am certain that the competition will be fierce as a number of men attempt to prevent each other from exiting the building. The likely contenders for this title include such luminaries as the current US Champion, Ian Tense, the man with the longest winning streak in the WWA, Wes Sanders, and Troy Walcott. The animosity here runs deeper than the Mississippi River that winds its way through St. Louis. “The Rage” Wes Sanders nearly lost his spot in the Birthday Brawl after suffering his first-ever loss to Jackson Kraven on Meltdown. However, with an attack on Ian Tense during a bout with Craven Moore, Sanders reclaimed his place in the Brawl. His feud with Ian Tense has been heating up quickly and will finally boil over as they both struggle, in an anything-goes environment, for the US Title. With his educated feet and the support of the fans, Sanders has more than enough to get the win. By no means however, should Ian Tense ever be overlooked. The current US Title holder earned that belt with tenacity and a wicked deviousness. Ian Tense has turned himself into one of the most hated men in the WWA during the past year, and shows no signs of slowing down. The war between Tense and Sanders has escalated during the last few months into an unadulterated hatred. Tense even went so far as to turn Sanders over to the less-than tender mercies of Havoc. And make no mistake; Ian Tense loves his US Title. When he lost the belt to Shane Dorian, he returned to reclaim it in spite of injuries. Say what you will about him as a human being, but never undersell one of the most passionate and gifted players in the game. One day soon, I expect to see this man competing for the coveted World Title. But in the meantime, he’ll also have deal with his former partner, Troy Walcott. At one time, Walcott and Tense gave rise to a new incarnation of the Fraternity. When egos clashed however, Walcott was left hospitalized by Tense’s betrayal. Now, the most flamboyant competitor in the WWA gets a chance not only to take the title from his former partner, but also to exact his revenge. With unmatched speed, agility, and in-ring awareness, this three-time WWA TV Champion is more than qualified to take any man in the WWA to the absolute limit. I would place my bets on Ian Tense in almost any situation. He’s tough, methodical, and crafty. Unfortunately, he may be looking at an impossible situation in the Brawl. He has earned the enmity of both Sanders and Walcott, meaning that they’ll both be gunning for him. As much as I respect Tense’s abilities, I don’t believe that he can fend off both of them to claim the win. Troy Walcott’s inimitable style makes him a great competitor in anything he does. If the third competitor were anyone other than Wes Sanders, I would go with Walcott. But, Wes Sanders is part of the Brawl, and I think that he has what it takes to capitalize on the situation and capture the US Title. I would love to back Ian Tense, but my money will be riding on “The Rage” Wes Sanders. World Heavyweight Title Last, but certainly not least, the WWA World Championship Title will be suspended in midair above the Jumbotron. In order to claim the sport’s greatest prize in this Mile High match, the winner will have to climb the stage to the top of the Jumbotron, erect the ladder that is laying there, and scale that ladder to retrieve the belt, all while fending off the very best that the WWA has to offer! This match is just plain dangerous, but I’m sure everyone on the talent list would be more than willing risk their very lives to take the top belt in the world. And what a talent list it is! After suffering an attack by his ex-stablemates in the Dogs of War, Trapper has decided to hang up the boots. Before he leaves though, he’s treating the fans to one last night of action at the Birthday Bash. Trapper’s many accomplishments in the Tag Team division as one half of the Veteranz, including the distinction of being the only team to have earned the Tag Titles four times, have earned him the respect of the fans and fellow wrestlers alike. Thanks to his sudden reversal of fortunes and a newfound friendship with Craven Moore, he is back in the good graces of the audiences that pack the WWA shows. Doubtlessly, Trapper will make every effort to put on the performance of his career, insuring that he’ll go out with a bang. Unfortunately, I don’t think that it will be enough. After all, every man vying for the World Title will have come to fight. Looming retirement just doesn’t seem to be enough of a reason to believe that he can gain the victory over the other hungry competitors. Despite a few recent disappointments, notably a loss at Labor Day Brawl that seems to have affected him deeply, Tracer Bullet remains one of the top stars of the WWA. With incomparable talent and resourcefulness, Bullet has already captured the World Title three times in his career. Obviously, becoming the second ever four-time World champion is not outside of the realm of possibilities. Normally, I would be willing to put money on Bullet, but his recent bout of introspection may prove to be a personal distraction that costs him his chance. Craven Moore’s strength and determination have carried him to the World Championship and he shows no signs of letting up. With a string of impressive title defenses, including the World Title tournament at Labor Day Brawl, Craven Moore continues to prove that he is a fighting champion. The fans love him, and Jackson Kraven hates him. Once again, we’ll be treated to the inevitable carnage that comes from pitting both of these mammoth superstars against each other. Kraven, another three-time World Champion, is easily one of the most dangerous men ever to step foot into the ring. Very little seems to be able to slow him down once he gets going. To add an exclamation point to the testament of Kraven’s talent, he’s the man who ended Wes Sanders’ undefeated streak, making him the man that beat the unbeatable. A tense situation has developed around Kraven’s place in the Brawl, though. Alexandra Korth, the wife and manager of the Five Star Champion, Denrol, has stated that if Kraven competes in the Brawl, then we will never see Denrol again! Furthermore, she has insisted, beyond demanding Kraven’s termination from the WWA, that Denrol receive a World Title shot upon his return from his injuries. We haven’t heard from Denrol, due to an extended hospital stay caused by Kraven. Will he leave the WWA forever if Kraven walks to that ring for the Birthday Brawl? Only time will tell, but I would hate to see Denrol go. With competitors of this calibre, things could go almost anywhere, and probably will. Trapper’s final match will certainly be one to remember, but I don’t believe that his long and impressive career will be capped with a World Title win. Tracer Bullet’s confidence may be shaken by his loss at Labor Day Brawl, and that confidence is sometimes the single most important determining factor in a match. When all is said and done, I think the monumental battle between Moore and Kraven will end up with the current champion retaining his title. For what it’s worth, I’ll be betting on Craven Moore while crossing my fingers. But wait, there’s more! After the pinfall that places a Tag Team belt around one competitor’s waist, each additional pinfall will result in the elimination of the person pinned. Whoever manages to score the most pinfalls during the night earns a World Title shot on the next Meltdown! This is an even more impossible bet to make, but you’d be amazed at what some of the people in Las Vegas are willing to bet on. Despite my misgivings about placing a bet like this, I’ll be looking for Tracer Bullet to pull together enough pinfalls to challenge the World Champion on the following Meltdown. This year’s Birthday Bash is going to be great. With six incredible matches followed by the Brawl itself, do yourself a favor and order the Pay-Per-View right now! The past year has been a mind-blowing spectacle and this is one celebration that is not to be missed. Until Rage in the Cage, this is Kevin Sage (I’m sure Doc Bebox would be proud of that one), reminding you that anybody can win...unless there happens to be a second entry.
