As Quietus walks over to the announcer’s table, the referee climbs out of the ring, motioning for him to get back in. Dave can’t believe his luck, inconspicuously climbing into the ring and jumping on Muppet’s exposed back with both knees - the Crab still locked in. Somers shoots a weary glance over to ringside where the referee is still detained with Quietus, who seems to be looking for something. Harley shrugs, leaves Somers with Muppet Man, and walks to the ring apron himself, smiling down at Quietus. As the Australian finally turns around, Harley actually waves, before coming down with a tremendous Flying Cross Body. As Quietus thuds off the floor, the Muppet Man escapes from the crab in the ring, and looks to make a tag with his missing partner. Quickly realizing that, indeed, Quietus isn’t where he should be, Muppet Man turns around, clobbering a running Mal Somers with a Spear all the way from some place unpleasant. Fat Tony: OUCH! Jimmy: Muppet Man doesn’t look all that pleased, staring out here. Fat Tony: Would you be if you had to constantly keep an eye on your stable mate so he won’t do anything stupid? Jimmy: Well, for once you’re right; Quietus HAS been acting foolishly - as usual - so far. Fat Tony: Who said anything about Quietus? I mean Dave Harley. Maybe Tony does, but Muppet doesn’t look too pleased with the fallen Quietus either. Forgetting about Somers, he climbs out of the ring, sneaking up on Harley from behind, who is leaning over Quietus, smiling. The former NWC man connects with a German Suplex that sees Harley’s head wallop precariously off the ring steps. There are immediate boos as Muppet Man, shaking his head, brings Quietus to his feet. A dazed Australian stares at his partner, who motions with the air of a teacher to the apron. Jimmy: Still angry with Dave Harley, is he? Fat Tony: Hell yeah! Did you see, or at least HEAR that German? Quietus nods as Muppet Man climbs back into the ring, back facing Mal, still motioning for Quietus to go to his corner. Spotting Somers on his feet, Quietus makes a wild gesture with his hands for MM to turn around. He does, and is hit with an impressive Powerbomb, Mal putting all his strength into it. Quietus shakes his head, shrugs his shoulders, makes a grasp for his United States title belt, and finally struts back to the corner. The referee, having all but given up trying to cater for Quietus’ every mood swing, gets back into the ring, to spot Mal covering the Muppet Man. ONE... TWO... THRRR... No! Fat Tony: Whoa! Close, close, close. Jimmy: His partner doesn’t seem all that bothered about that. Fat Tony: Look Jim, you just don’t understand, when you’re the United States Champion and the winner of the Independence Day Rumble, you don’t even need to bother yourself with people like Mal Somers or Dave Harley, because they can only do so much. Jimmy: Are you finished? Somers brings Muppet to his feet, and grabs his arm to swing him to the ropes, but Muppet Man reverses it, and sends Somers to his rope. On his way back, Somers attempts to hit Muppet Man with a flying clothesline, but Muppet Man simply steps to the side, allowing Somers to crash to the mat. Muppet Man tags in Quietus without Quietus’ permission, but he reluctantly climbs into the ring. Fat Tony: Trouble for Somers! Quietus lifts him up to his feet, and kicks him in the stomach twice. Ramming another kick into the stomach of Somers’, Quietus stands and poses over his X-Corp counterpart, flexing his muscles just for the hell of it. The crowd begins booing and a “Quietus Sucks!” chant begins. Quietus shoots a look of disbelief over to the Muppet Man; as if unable to comprehend what he could have ever done wrong to annoy these lovely folk. Muppet doesn’t return the stare. Shrugging, the Australian turns back to Somers, still down, and bounces off the opposite ropes, coming back to drop a leg over his throat. From the canvas Mal is heard groaning - the leg having seemingly hit the spot. Harley complains to the referee, who says something into Quietus’ ear, who doesn’t seem to hear. Smiling an insincere smile in Dave’s direction, Quietus lifts Mal up, putting his head between his legs. Turning to the crowd, he holds his hands up for a moment, before looking for some sort of Powerbomb. Mal resists. Grunting with displeasure, Quietus attempts again, but is unsuccessful once more. As he tries for a third time, Somers manages to wriggle free, jumping to his feet. Quietus, too slow to react, is Irish Whipped into a German Suplex. He isn’t given time to reflect on the move, however, as Mal lifts him back up, Russian Leg sweeping him, before applying the Indian Deathlock - a combination known as the World Tour! Jimmy: Quietus was certainly taken for a ride around the world there. Fat Tony: Good one. Leave the humor to me, or else we’ll both be out of a job. Quietus screams in agony in the Deathlock, seemingly too far away from the ropes to make a genuine attempt at grabbing them. With a desperate look at the Muppet Man, he buries his head in the canvas. MM bolts into the ring, taking Somers out with a hard boot to the skull. Dave climbs into the ring now, protesting. The referee, however, attempts to keep him out, giving the Muppet Man the opportunity to wear Mal down some more with a Snap Suplex. He turns Mal over into an Ankle Lock, and still the referee is distracted - intentionally or not - by Dave Harley, who continues to point at MM but doesn’t get out of the ring. Fat Tony: Good tactics from Harley there, giving Muppet Man the opportunity to knock the stuffing out of Mal. Mal, having been in the ring for a considerable length now, isolated, grabs the ropes from the Ankle Lock, but it means nothing. Quietus, rising finally, struts over to Harley, and as clear as day spits at him. Incensed, the X-Corpian-cum-Devil’s Own man makes a lunge at Quietus, but is blocked off by the referee. Quietus turns back around; sitting on top of Mal’s back, applying a Camel Clutch while the Ankle Lock is still fully locked in by his partner. Jimmy: Could the Devil’s Own get any sicker or sink any lower? Fat Tony: I know! That Dave Harley really is using all the dirty tricks this evening. And to think, a few short weeks ago Mal was his friend! Mal’s scream of agony finally brings the attention of the referee back to the action in the ring. Quietus, spotting him, walks out of the ring, despite the fact that he is still the legal man. The referee doesn’t seem to notice, but the Muppet Man does. He shoots a “what are you doing?” look at Quietus, who simply nods at him, and gives him the thumbs-up sign. Fat Tony: Quietus preserving himself for a more testing time in the match. Muppet Man holds in the ankle lock, and Somers makes a desperate attempt to reach the ropes. He has his hand half a foot away from grabbing the bottom rope, until Muppet Man grabs him, pulling him back into the ring. Somers finally manages to crawl to the bottom rope near Harley, who hits Muppet Man with a punch to the back of the head, giving Somers the opportunity to rise to his feet at the same time as Muppet Man. Somers runs at Muppet Man, who moves out of the way, before swinging him into the corner next to where Quietus is standing. Quietus hits Somers around the back of the head, making Harley try to stop him, but the referee holds Harley back. Fat Tony: Look, Quietus and Muppet Man are double-teaming Mal Somers, how smart of them! Quietus and Muppet double team Mal in the DO corner. Dave tries to get in the ring but the referee holds him back. Crowd: BOOOO!!! Fat Tony: You see that? Harley’s trying to join in! But Harley sees red. He pushes the ref out of the way and clubs both DO members in the back of their heads, giving Mal the opportunity to kick both of them square in the groin. As the referee grabs Dave Harley and forces him to go back to his corner, Somers takes the opportunity to double clothesline them to the mat. Somers stumbles over to the X-Corp corner, and tags in Dave Harley. Fat Tony: No! Now it’s going to be DO vs. DO! Jimmy: Harley is X-Corp for life Tony. Harley runs in at Muppet Man, before knocking him to the floor with a hard right fist. Harley attempts to lift Muppet Man to his feet, but Muppet kicks him in the stomach, and then in the head, knocking him to the floor. Muppet Man swings Harley into the corner of the ring, before making his way to Quietus. Fat Tony: It looks like it is Quietus’ turn! Muppet makes the tag to Quietus, who charges across the ring towards Harley. But Harley ducks the clothesline, hooks Quietus’ legs and rolls him up. Jimmy: Harley goes for the pin! 1! 2! Fat Tony: No! Harley wouldn’t pin a teammate for three anyways. He jumps to his feet before Quietus, grabs him by the hair and charges him towards the DO corner. He flies headfirst into Muppet Man, who in turn flies from the apron. Jimmy: Quietus tags out with his head! Harley, seeing Muppet Man laying on the outside of the ring, walks towards the edge of the ring, rolls outside, before picking up The Muppet Man and rolling him back into the ring. Quietus tries to kick Harley in the head from the apron, but Harley dodges, before pulling Quietus’ leg, making him fall to the outside of the ring. Harley rolls Muppet Man into the ring, before following himself. Fat Tony: Devil’s Own against Devil’s Own! Referee, stop the madness! Jimmy: Harley is NOT Devil’s Own! Fat Tony: I’ll be willing to bet he’ll say otherwise. Jimmy: Who? Fat Tony: That man on the ramp. Voice: Hold it! Hold it! Crowd: BOOOO!!! Jimmy: It’s Ian Tense! What the hell does he want? Ian Tense stands microphone in hand with a sadistic grin on his face. Dave Harley holds Muppet Man in a Camel Clutch as he listens to his new leader. Tense: I thought this was supposed to be DO v X Corp? Jimmy: It is DO v X Corp! Tense: Dave ... don’t tell me you’re defecting! I’d hate to have to sue you for breach of contract! If you fail in your duties as a DO member, I’ll file suit. I’m sure you’d hate to lose your Extreme Corporation stock… Harley pulls back harder on the Camel Clutch, fire burning in his eyes. Tense: So be a good DO member and get out of the ring. Your part in this match is over…for now! Jimmy: What? Can he do that? Fat Tony: He’s the DO leader; he can do what he likes! The crowd boos as Dave reluctantly releases his grip and rolls out of the ring. He looks at Mal apologetically from ringside. Jimmy: This has just become a Handicap Match! As Tense walks down to ringside to stand next to Harley, who clearly wants to hit Tense, Quietus and Muppet whip Mal into the ropes and prepare to meet him with a double clothesline, but Mal ducks under and springboards off the ropes. He catches both men in the face with flying elbows. Jimmy: Mal clears house! Dave shouts in approval on the outside. Tense closely watches him as he cheers his partner on. Mal tosses Quietus to the outside and turns his attention to the approaching Muppet. He drops him with a huge spinebuster and covers. Tense whispers something to Harley. 1! 2! Fat Tony: Muppet has his feet on the ropes! Jimmy: Dave Harley did it! Ian Tense told him to! Fat Tony: He did not! Mal shoots a confused glance at Dave before Quietus stomps on him. Harley tries to run in and help his friend, but Tense grabs him, and they exchange a few words. Quietus lifts Somers to his feet, before swinging him into the corner of the ring. Finally, the referee manages to get Muppet Man to stand on the apron, so that the tag rules still apply. Jimmy: Finally, some fairness in this match! Fat Tony: I know what you mean, what with Mal no longer having a DO man on his team. Somers comes bouncing off from the corner of the ring, before Quietus knocks him to the floor with a clothesline. He lifts Somers to his feet, before swinging him to the ropes. This time, Quietus lifts Somers into the air. Fat Tony: The running Powerslam? Jimmy: No, he’s hit The Human Violation! Indeed he has. Quietus crawls towards Somers, before managing to get a pin on him, and the referee starts the count. One!!! Two!!! Three!!! Ding! Ding! Ding! Gary Trudeau:: The winners of this match…Quietus and The Muppet Man! Jimmy: This match is over! Fat Tony: Not yet it ain’t! Ian Tense jumps up on the apron and shouts commands to Quietus and Muppet Man. They pounce on the exhausted Mal Somers and start booting him. Jimmy: Get them out of here! C’mon! Tense shouts to Harley, who climbs up on the apron. Tense: Get in there. Hold your friend up… With a sigh, Dave follows his orders. The crowd erupt into a frenzied boo as Dave picks his long time partner up and holds him out for the DO pack of dogs to devour. Jimmy: He can’t make him do this! Fat Tony: Make him? He’s loving every minute of it! Muppet Man grabs Somers by the hair and pulls him across to the ropes as Quietus urges him on and Harley looks on. Somers is draped throat first over the second rope as Muppet Man straddles him and looks at the crowd who are now throwing litter in the ring. Jimmy: This is reprehensible! Fat Tony: If that means "awesome" then I totally agree!! Muppet gestures at the crowd and then time seems to stand still for a moment, until Muppet drops to his knees and in doing so slams Somers throat across the rope. Somers falls to the mat, grasping his throat as DO stand triumphant over the Intercontinental Champion. Jimmy: This sucks! Suddenly two figures tear down the ramp at full tilt, and the crowd gets to its feet now that there is something to cheer about. Chance and Bonafide near the bottom of the ramp, wielding chairs and The Muppet Man, Dave Harley and Quietus all slide out to the opposite side. Chance and Bonafide slide in and charge around the ring as the crowd roars with approval. Jimmy: Why did Harley slide out? They are his friends in the ring! Fat Tony: That's because he is DO, baybee! DO for life! Jimmy: I don't know about that Tony, I do know he is not too keen on Bonafide taking his place in X-Corp. Chance tends to Somers as Harley stares down Muppet and Quietus on the outside. Bonafide climbs the corner turnbuckles and raises his arms in a big "X" formation, some of the crowd cheer but the rest are a little unsure. Dave Harley climbs up on the apron to have some words with Bonafide who leaps back to retreat, landing on top of Chance and Somers. He picks himself up, and dusts himself off as Chance also rises and has some words with X-Corps latest inductee. Jimmy: Something’s not right here. Folks, we are about out of time. Tune in next Monday for Meltdown! I'm Jimmy Rose and ... Fat Tony: I'm Tony Valentino, thanks for joining us! Muppet Man and Quietus look very pleased with themselves, Chance and Bonafide argue, and Mal Somers lays out on the mat, bleeding. Dave Harley shakes his head as we fade to the WWA logo.
