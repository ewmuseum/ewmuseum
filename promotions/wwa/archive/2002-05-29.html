The Eagle does indeed seem to be in some degree of pain from the Cruiserweight Champion's submission finisher. EVD yells at the ref to make the Eagle quit. He won't. EVD yells at Ray Wilson, and we make out the words "cheap" and "disgrace" in th tirade, along with others that really ought not to be heard before 9pm. Wilson just smiles. Jimmy: EVD is really fired up, Tony. Fat Tony: He's just inches away from beating the American Eagle! Wouldn't you be fired up? Wilson rises from his seat and heads towards the ring. Jimmy: What's he doing now? Fat Tony: He's just trying to get a close-up look at the look on the American Eagle's face when he loses! The ref notices Wilson and begins yelling at him to get lost. Wilson stays put. Whilst the ref's back is turned, the Eagle taps out, but there's no-one to see it and so it doesn't count! EVD is appalled and breaks the hold on the Eagle, heading out of the ring to badmouth the Extreme Champion. Fat Tony: Eddie! Don't! No need! Don't do it! Jimmy: Ray Wilson has possibly cost EVD the match! There's every need, Tony! EVD shoves Wilson in the chest. Wilson responds by flipping the Cruiserweight Champion the bird and walking away. Incensed, EVD strikes, clotheslining the Extreme Champion to the ground. The referee begins to count EVD out as he stomps on Wilson. Jimmy: EVD has snapped. Ray Wilson has taken this too far and paid the price. Fat Tony: EVD better watch he doesn't get counted out. There's a thin line between revenge and stupidity. Jimmy: For once I agree. The American Eagle is back up to his feet, and as Eddie turns back to the ring the Eagle runs across the ring and gives him a baseball slide to the face! EVD's nose is broken by the impact, and splatters blood across his face as goes down and the crowd cheer. Wilson is back up as the American Eagle rolls EVD back into the ring. Jimmy: That's more like it. Sportmanship costs nothing. The ref is busy checking EVD's nose as Wilson picks up his chair from earlier. The Eagle is up on the ring apron about to get into the ring when... WHACK! The crunch of steel on knee is drowned out by the crowd's roar of horror as Wilson blasts Eagle's wounded limb with the chair. Eagle yelps and falls over the ropes into the ring. The ref turns, but all he sees is the Eagle clutching his knee and Ray Wilson heading backwards up the ramp. Jimmy: Assault! Ray Wilson has just assaulted the American Eagle! This just isn't on! This is worse than not on! This is... words fail me! I am disgusted! Fat Tony: What? What? All I saw was the Eagle fall over the ropes! What happened? In the ring Eagle howls with pain, clutching his knee. EVD has wiped away most of the blood, but his nose is a ruin. He goes to the Eagle and starts to apply the spinning toehold again. The Eagle submits almost immediately. The ref motions for the timekeeper as he shoves EVD off his fallen foe. DING, DING, DING! Gary Trudeau: Your winner, as the result of a submission... Eddie Van Dorn! EVD doesn't hang around for the victory party as his music plays. Instead he leaps out of the ring and heads straight for Wilson. Wilson is ready for him, however, and powerslams the Cruiserweight Champion on the ramp, before laying into him with lefts and rights! Jimmy: The American Eagle has been screwed here tonight! Ray Wilson may have shattered the Eagle's kneecap irrevocably! As EVD's music cuts out, we see the Eagle roll out of the ring and try to hobble over to where the Cruiserweight champ is being beaten. It's a painful spectacle. Fat Tony: Looks like hanging out with Michael Stryfe and the Tomorrow Man has given Wilson the edge he needed! Jimmy: Given him infinitely more dirty tricks, don't you mean! This is despicable. Ray Wilson is despicable! Wilson picks up the bloodied EVD and signals for The Dominator. The crowd boo. The American Eagle has grabbed Wilson's chair but is too far away to help as the Cruiserweight champion falls victim to the Extreme Champion's finisher. Wilson picks up the fallen EVD and spits in his face, before mouthing something. We can't hear what it is, but it looks like "the belt is mine". Jimmy: Somebody call security. Wilson has EVD set up for a second Dominator, but the Eagle is finally in position. As the Extreme Champion gets ready to give EVD his finisher a second time... WHACK! Wilson goes down from the Eagle's chairshot. To cheers from the crowd, the wounded hero tries to pick up EVD as WWA security finally get here. To even bigger cheers, the Eagle refuses all help offered to him, helping his bloodied foe to his feet as the security march Wilson away. EVD looks quizzically at the Eagle, but then offers him a handshake. It is now Eagle's turn to look quizzical. Fat Tony: Is this another sneak attack right here? The Eagle is cautious about shaking Van Dorn's hand, but does it anyway. The crowd roar their appreciation for both men. Van Dorn mouths something to Eagle, and Eagle nods. Both men head backstage under their own steam, the Eagle limping horribly. Jimmy: Folks, this one will definitely have repercussions, but we have to take a break! We'll be right back with more WWA action, right after this! We fade to commercial on a slow-motion replay of the American Eagle blasting Ray Wilson with the steel chair. As we return from commercial, we see Wallace Coleman backstage and stood with him, Kasanova. Not that he gets a word in edgeways..... Kasanova: Harrington! Ha, you thought you had everything planned didn't you? You thought that Kris Kas was on your side. I find it absolutely hilarious that were completely wrong. Now a steady chorus of boos coming from the crowd. Kas pays little to no attention to this and continues.. Kasanova: Now it is time for the WWA to pay. I despise the way things have been going for the last year or so, and there is really nothing that I could have done to help save this federation. Instead, I'm going to see fit that everyone in this federation, from Pretty Boy Troy to Craving More gets there share of Kasanova coming to them. I will rise to the top of this federation, as I have done in the past. Some fans cheer at this, others are still in awe that Kasanova attacked Harrington.. Kasanova: Ray Wilson! I'm coming for you and for the Extreme Belt. You better watch your ass sister boy. I'm outta here. Kasanova walks off picture as Coleman shrugs his shoulders Jimmy: I am physically disgusted by Kasanova's actions tonight. Fst Tony: Oh will you get over it! The man just want's to win again! Jimmy: Beating on the owner isnt a way to win.... Fat Tony: Jackson Kraven seemed to do it..... Gary Trudeau: And now for your main event. Introducing first, hailing from San Diego, California, ladies and gentlemen…I give to you... KADE WILLIAMS! The opening bars of "No More Tears" by Ozzy Osbourne begin to play. "Mr. Pist" Kade Williams emerges from the entranceway. He is bandaged up from Kraven's earlier attack. He has his chain mail entrance attire on, and the give him a face reaction. Kade walks slowly to the ring and waits. Gary Trudeau: His opponent, weighing in at three-hundred and ten pounds... The crowd boo as blue strobes begin to flicker around the WWATron for the second time tonight. As the World Champion's theme music plays, words appear on the WWATron. Trudeau doesn't even get to finish his speech, the crowd are booing so loudly. JACKSON KRAVEN NO MORE TOMORROWS A wall of white pyro detonates at the front of the stage, and as the smoke rises towards the ceiling in an eerie blue haze, the Tomorrow Man strides out from under the WWATron. His eyes are hidden by wraparound sunglasses, and he grins as he heads to ringside. The World Title belt glitters atop his shoulder. Jimmy: Jackson Kraven is an animal. I hope Kade Williams takes him down at May Mayhem. Fat Tony: Williams has to get through the Tomorrow Man tonight first, Jimmy. Kraven stalks up the ring steps as the arena lights come back up. The Tomorrow Man's grin fades as he Diesels over the top rope. Kraven hands the belt to the ref and steps back as he removes his jacket. Fat Tony: The World Champion is all business tonight. Williams remains unmoved center-ring. Kraven strides over and faces up to his foe. Jimmy: Williams may be outmatched physically, but he's all heart. Kraven's a gutless wretch. Fat Tony: A master tactician, more like! That attack earlier may have repercussions! Williams may have internal injuries that we don't know about! The referee looks to both men. Before he can signal the timekeeper, Kraven slams the WWA World Title belt into Williams' face again. Williams drops like a sack of potatoes. DING, DING! Jackson Kraven straddles the fallen and begins to rain blow after blow onto Kade's ruined face. After the fifth monstrous blow Kraven's fist comes back sticky with Kade's blood. A diabolic smile spreads across the champion's face. Jimmy: Kraven's lost his mind! Fat Tony: That may be, but Williams is about to lose his life. Kraven pulls Williams up violently. Blood streams down Kade's face. Kraven backs up and bounces off the ropes. He nails the wobbly Williams with a titanic lariat that sends Williams head over heels for 360 degrees. Williams lands awkwardly on the side of his head and doesn't move. Jimmy: Someone has to stop this! Fat Tony: Doesn't look like Williams has a lot of friends in the back. Kraven kicks Williams twice in the side of the head. Williams' head snaps to the right twice with sickening force. The referee tells Kraven to stop the assault. The Tomorrow Man tosses the ref over the top rope. The referee calls for the bell. Jimmy: Jackson Kraven has been disqualified! Fat Tony: But he's not done with Williams! Kraven then grabs a handful of Williams' black trunks and yanks him off the mat. Kraven puts him in position, takes him up and drops him with a KravenBomb. Williams bounces lifelessly across the canvas. Jimmy: This is just inhumane. Fat Tony: Yeah! You'd think Williams would at least put up a fight. Kraven takes Williams up and nails him with a second KravenBomb. The audience gasps as Williams lands in a heap on the mat. The Tomorrow Man stands over the lifeless body of Kade Williams and slowly shakes his head. The crowd begins to boo as Kraven lifts Williams up one more time. Fat Tony: He warned Williams! Jimmy: NO MORE TOMORROWS! The crowd begins to cheer. Craven Moore sprints down the aisle and slides into the ring. Before Jackson Kraven can react, Moore nails him with a running spear. The Tomorrow Man bellows in agony and clutches at his injured ribs. Kraven pulls himself onto his hands and knees. Craven Moore launches a giant kick into Jackson Kraven's ribs. The kick knocks Kraven into the air with another scream of pain. Jimmy: Shades of Al Del Greco! Fat Tony: This is ridiculous! Someone stop him! As if on cue, Ray Wilson and Michael Stryfe rush down the aisle. Before they get there, Craven has Jackson up. He whips the Tomorrow Man into the ropes and drives a knee into his mid-section on the bounce. Jackson Kraven flips over the big Texan's knees and lands on the mat clutching his ribs. Wilson and Stryfe hit the ring and go straight after Moore. Moore throws a quick series of fists the size of canned hams. The first two take Wilson and Stryfe back a step. The second set knock them to the mat. The crowd erupts into cheers for the big Texan. Jimmy: So much for Team Kraven! Fat Tony: He's only one man! He can't stop them all. You'd think Moore heard Fat Tony. He grabs Stryfe and Wilson and bangs their hands together, shades of the Stooges. Both men take a staggered step back. Moore stands between them launching elbow after elbow into their foreheads. Jenny Calloway rushes down the aisle and tosses something to Jackson Kraven. Moore finishes the assault with a flourish and a Bionic Elbow for each man. They both go down. Jimmy: Moore is getting funky like a monkey in there! Fat Tony: But he forgot one thing!
