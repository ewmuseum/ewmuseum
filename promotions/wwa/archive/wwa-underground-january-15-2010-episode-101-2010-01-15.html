WWA: Underground, January 15, 2010 (Episode #101)

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is Full show script of the first WWA: Underground webcast, episode 101, from the Hammerstein Ballroom in New York, including a four-way match, a retirement speech and the Adams versus Watkins WWA Championship main event.
WWA: Underground January 15, 2010 - Episode #101 Live from the Hammerstein Ballroom in New York, NY Chapter I Intrepid Vs Black w/ Special Guest Ref Somers No opening music video. No fancy pyro. No screaming announcers. The show simply opens inside the Hammerstein Ballroom in new York City, where 2,500 fans are standing and chanting. Crowd: WWA! WWA! WWA! WWA! WWA! The camera zooms in on the regal gray-haired Gary Trudeau, who is standing in the middle of the ring. The crowd is making so much noise and motion, the hard camera is noticably shaking. Trudeau brings the house mike to his mouth. Trudeau: Ladies and gentlemen, welcome to WWA: Underground! The crowd stops chanting and just goes nuts cheering. Trudeau: This match, the first Underground match ever, is to be decided by one fall. Here now is the special guest referee for this bout. Can I Play With Madness booms over the speakers, and Mal Somers pops out of the curtain to a thunderous ovation from the crowd in attendance. Mostly the same as when he left, except he appears a bit on the pudgy side, with shorter hair. He says something out to the crowd, and the fans nearby respond loudly. Somers: How are you guys doing tonight? Crowd: Pretty good! Jack: How appropriate the first man to enter the ring for the first match of the WWA's new life is Mal Somers, even if he is the official. Mikal: Not very appropriate. He's very old compared to the others. Jack: Well, that's certainly one way of looking at it, I suppose. Mal rolls into the ring and wipes his hands on his referee's shirt. He leans in the corner and sets his sights on the entryway. "Must Kill" by Cavalera Conspiracy begins playing and not even a second after that the entrance begins flashing with a quick burst of white strobe light. Intrepid comes out quickly but stops in front of the entrance curtain, staring out into the crowd and grooving to the beat of the music. He wears not only his trademark mask but his trademark smile. Trudeau: Introducing first, weighing in at 216 pounds, he is WWA's Hellbilly from Hot Springs... INTREPID! Jack: And here he is, Intrepid! Shelved by Lucas Black oh so long ago, he's returned to exact revenge on the very man who nearly ended his career and the crowd is pumped for him! Mikal: I would certainly not call them eclectic for this man. They are merely eclectic for the return of the WWA, and the return of Mikal! Jack: It certainly is pretty eclectic in here tonight, folks! Intrepid thrusts his arm into the air. As soon as the crowd reacts, Intrepid runs down the entire aisle all the way to the ring. He dives onto the ring apron and slides into the ring on his stomach. When he reaches the opposite side of the ring he pushes himself into a standing position. He goes to high-five Mal, but Mal raises his hands as if to say, "Whoa now," and Intrepid gets the hint, dropping the raised hand and waiting in anticipation for his opponent. Jack: Mal already showing he plans to be unbiased. Mikal: As unbiased as Fox News. Or any news network for that matter, I suppose. Except those in Soviet Russia. They are unbiased under threat of DEATH. Flashing red strobes engulf the arena, leaving the assembled crowd in staccato, jerking movement. "Paint it Black" by The Rolling Stones echoes throughout the small venue. Lucas Black, with short hair as black as night, steps from behind the curtain, clad in black leather pants, black boots and black elbow pads. Trudeau: Ladies and gentlemen, now coming to the ring, weighing in at 220 pounds... Lucas Black! I see a red door and I want it painted black No colors anymore I want them to turn black The flashing red strobes change to flashing black light as Tiffani Andrews, clad in black leather, steps from the behind the curtain to stand at his side. He places his black gloved hands on his hips and glares at the assembled masses, the worms that writhe beneath his notice. Jack: Former World Champion Lucas Black is certainly a devastating force to be reckoned with. Mikal: An utter monster. Jack: Yes he is. Mikal: I meant Tiffani Andrews. Ha! Jack: I suppose that's true too. She did ally herself with a psychopath. I see the girls walk by dressed in their summer clothes I have to turn my head until my darkness goes I see a line of cars and they’re all painted black With flowers and my love both never to come back As he moves toward the ring, Tiffani falls into step behind him, head held high. His lips curl into a morbid grin as he moves ever closer to the ring. Intrepid eyes them both with cold hatred. I see people turn their heads and quickly look away Like a new born baby it just happens every day I look inside myself and see my heart is black I see my red door and it has been painted black The worms boo and hiss, spewing their blind judgments. But then the cattle always hate their predators. Maybe then I'll fade away and not have to face the facts It's not easy facing up when your whole world is black Jack: The question is whether or not Intrepid is physically and mentally ready for what will be a monstrous confrontation with Lucas Black, and if his neck will stand up to the pain. Mikal: The real question, Jack, is whether or not I can stand up after a gallon of vodka. My name will become Vodka Drunkinski. Lucas enters the ring nimbly, hopping over the top rope and slowly advancing on his prey. Intrepid moves forward and his forehead is immediately up against Black's as they stare each other down. The music drops off quickly and the crowd swells in anticipation. Jack: Intrepid has been waiting a long time for this match, and Black and everyone else in here knows it. Tiffani laughs and mock applauds from ringside. Mal lumbers over and steps between Black and Intrepid, holding them back from each other. He looks to each man in turn, and suddenly moves and gestures toward Tiffani. He grins, and she frowns in confusion. Mikal: What does he want with her? Jack: I don't think Mal has been that desperate lately. With a flourish, he boldly gestures that Tiffani leave ringside, and the crowd cheers madly as she looks shocked. Black looks at Mal with a face of bemused curiosity. Jack: And Mal has already made his precense known in this match, folks! He doesn't want any unfair advantage to Black whatsoever. Mikal: Like he himself being in the match isn't an unfair advantage for Intrepid. Tiffani shakes her head and does as she was asked to Na Na Na Na Hey Hey Hey Goodbye chants.
