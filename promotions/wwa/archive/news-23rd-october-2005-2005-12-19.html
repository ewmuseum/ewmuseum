Ramone's Dressing Room Trashed?? It was reported On Monday, October 17, 2005 during WWA Monday Night Meltdown Mr. Ramone's dressing room was trashed backstage during Meltdown's commission break. There hasn't been no official word of who trashed Mr. Ramone's dressing room but rumors going around that it was either Mr. Anderson known as Xavier Anderson or The Eternal Warrior and or Mr. Kahn known as Belial Kahn. A Reporter for World Wrestling Alliance caught up with Mr. Kahn as he entered into The E Center to prepare for his match against Mr. Anderson later on tomarrow. This is what he had to say. "You got some damn nerve asking me that. I know what you need to do." Mr. Kahn got into the Reporter's face and grabbed him by the tie. Mr. Kahn then went onto to say "You need to take your little microphone and your funny little glasses out of my face before I forget your not a wrestler. If I had a problem with that nobody. I would have trashed him face to face not his dressing room or anything like that. You got that?!! Now get gone punk ass." Mr. Kahn then proceeds to slap the Reporter as Mr. Kahn walks away grinning and begins laughing as he entered into the E Center. Apparently Mr. Kahn took offense to the rumor about him trashing Mr. Ramone's dressing room. We also later on got the chance to catch up with Mr. Anderson as he also arrived to The E Center to prepare for his match against Mr. Kahn. This is what he had to say about the situation concerning him trashing Mr. Ramone's dressing room. "Yes I've heard about this rumor going around Backstage. I even found some online wrestling resource sites talking about the same rumor going around. You see things like this make me sick and I don't even know how my name got involved. Anyways I will say this like I've stated all week long and even sent a exclusive video to World Wrestling Alliance to put up on the site. I will not say I did or didn't and you can take that anyway you want. I don't care if you think I did it or not. As far as I am concern this can go on for months or just days. Either way I don't care and I won't let this bother me. If Ramone wants to know if I did it or not. He can walk his little no for good ass to my dressing room, knock on the door, and ask me to my face. He can be a man about this and ask me man to man or he can be a little pussy and start something out of nothing. Now if you excuse me, you have wasted my time with this non sense. You have taking a few minutes out of my time which I could be using it for preparation for my match against this Kahn kid." Well it seems Mr. Anderson doesn't want to answer the simple question and will only give his answer to Mr. Ramone if he approaches Mr. Anderson face to face. Well folks we should have more information concerning this as we get it. Designed By [name withheld] | © World Wrestling Alliance 1997 - 2005
