WWA — 28 Apr 1999

WWA Slam is in effect Hardcore wrestling at it's best Which of you can past the test. Piledriver, armbar, Tennessee Jam. Get ready folks.. HERE COMES THE SLAM! ::The screen shatters, showing the WWA Arena. The camera pans over such signs as 'Flynn takes Viagra'. 'Hang the Outlaw', and 'I'm a Frat Brother'. At the announcers table is Terry and Tony.:: Tony: Welcome to WWA Slam! Tonight we have a great lineup. The main event is a rematch for the TV title between Mantis and Duke Dar.. :: 'Real World' by Matchbox 20 plays as the scrawny nephew of Mark Harrington, Rick, walks down to the announcers table. Next to him is a somewhat handsome gentleman. The pair stands in front of the announcers. :: Rick: Ok you old farts, this is MY show so you two can take off. Terry: Now wait just a minute! :: The other gentleman yanks the chair out from under Terry. The two announcers throw down their headsets, shouting back at Rick as they leave. Rick and his partner sits down and put the sets on. :: Rick: Now that that is taken care of. Welcome to WWA SLAM! I'm Rick Harrington. And my co-host is Steve Collen, a veteran announcer. Steve: Great to be back behind the mike. This is a great premiere lineup. The giant Annihilation will face Albatross. We have the debut of 'Genocide' Jason Miller. Sir Michael against Lantern and a great main event. Rick: Yes folks, I put it together, the rematch between Mantis and Duke Daring. This time it is for the TV title. Mantis barely beat Duke last time, lest hope for just a great of a match. Let's get the show started! Gary Trudeau: First. from Vancouver, British Columbia, weighing in at 260 lbs..Albatross! :: 'Crop Circle' plays as Albatross strides to the ring. He stands dead in the middle of the ring, waiting. :: Gary Trudeau: And his oppenent..standing at seven feet four, weighing 405 lbs..from the streets of Chicago..Annihilation! ::Three tolls from a gong are heard as Annihilation steps out to a mixed reaction from the fans. He is wearing a WWA t-shirt and torn jeans. He slaps some hands as he walks to the ring.:: Rick: The first match of this, My first show, is Annihilation against Albatross. Steve: Albatross is a big man..but Annihilation is just plain HUGE! Seven feet four. Rick: Well the smaller Canadian isn't taking any steps back. In fact, Albatross is right in Annihilation's face. Steve: Slap by Albatross! He wants to show this big man that size doesn't matter. Rick: Annihilation with a kick to the gut, grabs the bent over Albatross and Crippler!! Steve: That quick! He makes the cover. Rick: Three..THREE! No! God damn it. This match was supposed to at least last five minutes..not five seconds. How the hell am I going to fill up the time. ::Annihilation steps over the top rope and walks to the back while Albatross slowly slides out, following to the back:: Rick: Yeah you Loser! Get out of here! ::Albatross turns to look at Rick, who looks very scared:: Rick: Umm..I was yelling at Annihilation. You'll get him next time. ::Albatross spits at Rick and walks out:: Rick: Well..I guess I can go to a commercial. When we return, The Man of Metal faces the newcomer Miller. The screen fades to black, then the silhouettes of two men appear in the screen. The two men appear and they are none other than "The Crusader" Tommy Lazer of the WWA and Kobe Bryant of the Los Angeles Lakers. The two men have bottles of Sprite in their hands. LAZER:As you can see, I am a new spokesman for Sprite. My friend Kobe and I were just enjoying the lemon lime fresh taste. KOBE:Yes, but remember: Image is nothing, thirst is everything, ob... Lazer lays Kobe out with a swinging neck breaker and picks up his fallen bottle. LAZER:It looks like image IS something Kobe! I am the image of Sprite from now on brother. Lazer walks back down the direction he came from as the camera fades to black. Rick: The next match we have scheduled for you tonight is a dandy. Steve: That's right, Rick. Although I wouldn't use the word "dandy". That went out with Disco. Rick: Yeah, whatever. Anyway, let's go to the ring. Take it away Gary... {inside ring} Gary Trudeau: Ladies and Gentleman .. The following match up is scheduled for one fall, with a twenty minute time limit. Introducing first .. From Columbus, Ohio. Weighing in at 235 pounds ... here is "Genocide" .. Jason Miller! "Santeria" by Sublime begins to play over the PA system. Jason Miller steps out from back stage and looks over all the WWA fans. He makes his way to the ring to a mixed reaction. The newcomer slides into the ring under the bottom rope, and pops to one knee. He slowly rises, and waits for his opponent. Gary Trudeau: And his opponent .. He hails from Toronto, Ontario, Canada. He weighs in at 246 pounds ... the "Man of Metal" .. Jon Cole! The lights dim slightly, as the sound of metal crashing is heard. It then goes into a Bill Goldberg TYPE music. Jon steps out from the locker room area to a chorus of "boos". He tries to ignore them, and makes his way to the ring. He enters by the ring steps. The lights come fully back up. Steve: Okay. This is fixing to be a decent Rookies match up right here. {ding...ding...ding} Rick: Here we go! Steve: Cole comes a charging.. OH. Drop toehold by Genocide. Rick: Cole looks a bit surprised by that, and slowly gets to his feet laughing. Steve: In neutral position now .. and they lock it up. Cole with a side head lock applied. Rick: Not for long folks.. Miller with three stiff elbows to the side of Cole. He's re-positioning himself now .. Back Suplex! Steve: Genocide quick to the cover .. only a one count. Rick: Both men back up .. They tie it up again, and Cole showing some power by pushing Miller into the turnbuckle. Steve: Cole pounding away now, rights .. lefts. OH! Genocide wasn't going to take much more of that .. reversal .. and now Cole is in the turnbuckle, being stomped and beat like a dusty old rug! Rick: Genocide making a rookie mistake here .. he's not capitalizing on the downed Cole. Now he is .. OH! Cole with a thumb to the eye, and Genocide backs off, holding his face.. Steve: And look at this .. Jon Cole is climbing the turnbuckle.. Rick: Ouch! There goes his sex life! Genocide just hit the turnbuckle and Cole fell crotch first... Steve: Owww... Rick: Genocide taking advantage now .. Hip Toss off the top! Genocide going up top now .. elbow drop across the chest. Steve: ..and he hooks the leg .. 1...2..KICKOUT! Cole's still alive folks. Rick: Genocide not letting any time go .. pulling the dazed Cole to his feet .. OH! He just caught Cole in the jaw with a spin wheel kick! Steve: It's time .. looks like Cole has to face.. The Truth! He's got it locked on... Rick: There it is ref .. ring the bell. Cole was forced to submit after that painful locking Dragon Sleeper. Gary Trudeau: Ladies and Gentleman .. your winner ... "Genocide" .. Jason Miller!! "Santeria" by Sublime begins to play over the PA System again. Jason exits the ring, and heads back towards the locker rooms. Rick: Some match that was .. Genocide making a nice impression here in the WWA. Steve: ..and out next match .. oh, this is going to be one hell of a battle. Up next folks, we have Sir Michael versus Lantern. Rick: .. but, before that .. lets go to a commercial break. The camera fades back in after a couple short commercials. Steve: Welcome back people. As we promised.. to the ring... Gary Trudeau: WWA fans. The following match is scheduled for one fall .. with a twenty minute time limit. Introducing first .. from 'The Middle Ages'. Weighing in at 205 pounds .. Sir Michael! Sir Michael and Lady Rachel step out from back stage as "Princes of the Universe" by Queen begins to play on the PA system. Sir Michael is in his traditional armor and has his sword and shield in hand. They walk down to the ring, as the fans cheer and reach out to try to touch them. Sir Michael removes his armor outside the ring. He hopes up on the apron and then jumps over the top rope. Lady Rachel is standing at ringside by his corner. Gary Trudeau: ..And his opponent. From Calgary, Alberta, Canada . weighing in at 233 pounds .. Lantern!! The lights dim. "Indestructible" by Matthew Good Band blares through the arena. In the entrance way pyrotechnics go off to form the lantern symbol. It lasts for a few moments before Lantern steps through. The crowd begins to boo and he smiles. He wears his black trench coat and Oakley shades. He also carries a dozen long stemmed red roses in his hand. He walks down the ramp and ignores the fans and the signs that bear such slogans as "CPE is dead, and so is Lantern". He slips his shades into his jacket pocket, shrugs off the coat and steps between the ropes. The lights come back up. Rick: That was cool effects. Steve: They were explosives, my man. {ding...ding...ding} Rick: What in the hell is Lantern doing? Steve: Looks to me like he's making a move on Lady Rachael .. I don't blame him.. Rick: Lantern just gave Rachael the roses .. and she accepted them! Steve: Oh no, Lantern. Wrong move buddy boy .. Ouch! Rick: Damn! Sir Michael leaves his feet, and catches Lantern in the back of the head with a drop kick .. sending him outside in front of us.. Steve: The ref is starting his ten count ... Rick: Sir Michael ... OH! Suicide Dive by Michael onto the just recovering Lantern outside the ring! Steve: Sir Michael now looks very angry .. he grabs the roses and tries to hurt Lantern with them by smacking him on the back of the head with them. If you asked me, it looks like a game of cops and robbers.. Rick: Sir Michael now having a talk with Lady Rachael .. as the ref reaches eight. Steve: Sir Michael doesn't care about the count .. however, Lantern just rolled in, and back out to start a fresh count. Rick: What's Lantern going to do here .. OH! Shoulder Block to the back of the knee! Steve: Sir Michael falls like a ton of bricks! Lantern continues to stomp away .. the ref up to seven. Rick: ..and Lantern again starts a fresh count... Steve: Looks like a .. and YES! Irish Whip into the turnbuckle, and Sir Michael just got aquatinted with the steel! Rick: Lantern now, look at this guy.. Steve: He's flirting with Lady Rachael...Rachael just smacked those roses in Lantern's face. Lanterns spins in time to see Sir Michael to the rescue with a chair! KERPLUNK! Lantern meets the steel this time! Rick: Sir Michael now taking this match where it belongs .. inside the ring. He rolls Lantern back in, and follows after him .. Lantern to his feet before Michael can even get in.. Steve: Double Axe Hammer to the back of the neck as Michael rolls in.. Lantern with devastating stomps to the back now.. Rick: Sir Michael being helped up .. sent for the ride ... Beautiful Arm Drag take down by Lantern! Steve: Yippee. Anyway .. Lantern now showing some submission skills .. with that text book Arm Bar .. Ouch! I'm just waiting for a loud pop... Rick: That's sick. Steve: You wimp.. Rick: Anyway .. Lantern applying more and more pressure now... Steve: Sir Michael stenches his leg and reaches the ropes .. breaking the hold.. Rick: Michael sent for the ride .. but he manages to hold onto the ropes before making his way back... Steve: Here comes Lantern with a clothesl ... NO! Ha Ha! Lantern was tossed to the outside by a back body drop.. Rick: I don't believe it .. Lantern's trying to get to his feet... Steve: YES! Sir Michael off the ropes as Lantern was struggling to his feet, and planted him back first into the safety rail.. Rick: Sir Michael down now, and he's pummeling Lantern's face .. Steve: Yeah! He's bearing the concrete floor .. the ref reaches ten .. Rick: Michael pulling Lantern up .. it looks like a Power Bomb... Steve: Oh my god! Lantern just reversed it into a Hurricanrana! Michael's back just smack the concrete .. I think he's out. Rick: The ref is at seventeen now .. Lantern re-enters the ring now .. Oh my god! Sir Michael stumbled to his feet.. get in the ring! Steve: His foot is caught in a piece of wire. Rachael is trying to help Michael get free. {ding...ding...ding} Steve: The ref counted out Sir Michael! Lantern wins! Gary Trudeau: ..And the winner, as a result of a count out .. Lantern! The lights dim once again. "Indestructible" by Matthew Good Band blares through the arena. Lantern celebrates the victory, and leaves for the locker rooms. Steve: Despite what happened .. Sir Michael felt well enough to slap the fans hands on the way out. Rick: Well you know what? That's bull. Seeing how this is my production...Next Wednesday, those two will meet again. This time, the win can only be by submission or pinfall. And I'll even sweeten the pot..winner gets a TV title shot. Steve: Good, about time those two got a little push. Now let's get to our main event! Gary Trudeau: First..the challenger. weighing 227 from Haddon Township, NJ. Duke Daring! ::Daring comes out to 'Black Lung'. The crowd gives a mixed reaction.:: Gary Trudeau: And now the Champion! Representing the House of Pain! at 273 from Haddon Township..the TV champion Mantis! :: Mantis is all business walking down to the ring. not even his ring music. Mantis tosses the belt at the announcer:: Steve: Mantis has the microphone and points at Duke. Mantis: 'Daring, you and I, we have roots so let's do this old style way we know. No DQ..Falls count anywhere' Rick: Oh the crowd loves that. Duke looks over the crowd and nods to Mantis. Steve: And Mantis just rushed Daring and hit him with the mike. Rick: Well! I didn't expect this, what a great thing! Steve: Didn't take long for this to get outside. Both men are throwing punches. Mantis throws Duke into the ringpost. Rick: He follows with a clothesline, Missed! Duke ducks and Mantis wraps his arm around the post. Low blow by Duke crumbles the TV champion. Steve: Daring shouting a few words to the groaning Mantis. Soccer kick to the gut rolls the masked man over. Rick: Come on! Beat him down HOP style Mantis. Steve: Mantis is beating no one as Duke looks under the ring for something to use. Rick: He's found some worker's lunchbox. And turns right around into Mantis' feet as the House member hits a nice dropkick. Steve: Mantis picks up the metal lunchbox and cracks it open on Daring's forehead. Duke is bleeding early. Rick: Mantis pulls out a salt shaker from the box, and is straddling Duke. Steve: Duke is SCREAMING in pain as Mantis rubs some of that salt into his opened forehead. Rick: Eyerake by Duke gets Mantis off of him. Daring follows with a clothesline that knocks them over the railing into the fans, who quickly get out of the way. Steve: Duke takes a Coke from a nearby person and sloshes it into Mantis' face. Rick: Purely a humiliation tactic, Dang! That wasn't. Duke with an Irish whip slams Mantis into the wall. Steve: Duke grabs a chair and runs to ram it into mantis, but Mantis rolls away, Duke just speared the wall with the chair. Rick: The TV champion kicks Duke in the stomach and DDT. The ref is finally with the action, Mantis with the cover. Steve: Two only as Daring draws deep to lift his shoulder. Rick: Mantis lifts Daring to his feet and uses him as a battering ram into the closed metal door leading to the stair well. Mantis finishes up with a body slam. Steve: Well Mantis has Duke spread eagle. Looks like a nutcracker attempt as Mantis climb to the top stair. Rick: Excellent counter by Duke! Lifting the foot right into Mantis' head on the way down. Steve: Both men are pretty drained, gasping for breath. Duke manages to roll a arm onto Mantis. Ref makes the count Rick: TWO! These two men are going to kill each other as the slowly rise up. Steve: They are slugging their way up the stairs. Duke got Mantis on rubber knees with those right hands. Rick: Daring lifts the champ up and owch! Steve: Man, that was painful to even look at. Duke planted Mantis, split legged onto the hand railing up the middle. Rick: Daring grabs the back of Mantis' head, pushes him down the rail and smack into the wall head first. Mantis is busted WIDE open. Steve: Duke tells the ref to count as he covers Mantis. Rick: Somehow Mantis gets a shoulder up. This has been a great rematch so far. Steve: Duke's up, whips Mantis toward the wall. Reversal ::The last image you see is Mantis reversing and whipping Duke right into the camera, it cuts out:: Rick: ..what! Steve: Folks, we will get a camera up there soon as we can. Rick: The fans are cheering loudly up on the upper deck. Steve: Look! I see them, they are fighting up there with the fans. ::the camera zooms in on them. The ref is still on the ground level looking up.:: Rick: Duke has a steel chair, hard hit sends Mantis spinning away. Steve: Mantis picked up a hard hat one of the crew left. Duke is coming from behind, pressing the advantage. Rick: He just clock Daring in the head with that hard hat! Duke is knocked right off the edge, over the railing and down onto the floor about ten feet down! Steve: Mantis is putting on the helmet. He's flying from the balcony, NUTCRACKER WITH THE HARD HAT ON! Rick: Mantis is covering Duke..one-two-three! Mantis retains the TV Title! Steve: With that match, I think he should go for the extreme belt. Rick: Well, I must pat myself on the back for this card. What a great start to Slam! For WWA, See you next week!
