WWA — 24 April 1998

24 Apr 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Friday April 24, 1998 The screen opens up to the the NCW banner. Then.... Northeast Champion Wrestling flashes through the screen and leaves the NCW Banner Then firework type explosions go off around the Banner. The Banner fades... then the screen shows the Bryce Jordan Center. The screen then goes to the inside to show the fans jumping and screaming at the Camera. The camera pans over the the Complex and then the camera finds Tony Bailey and Terry Valentine sitting behind the table in the broadcast area. Tony : Welcome to the Bryce Jordan Complex. Apparently, something is wrong with the PA system tonight, therefore there will be no music played to introduce the combatants. Terry : It looks like Harrington went cheap on us again and bought faulty equipment or forgot to pay the bills. Johny : We have another big card tonight, hilighted by Angel Reaper defending the CWC NYC title against yet another HOP member, 'Wild Thing' Chris Rogers. Tony : Here comes Anarchy and as you would guess the fans are voicing their disapproval. Terry : And now those too good for nothing 'tough guys' Black Scorpion and the Fan are making their way to the ring. Johny : The match has begun and Raiden is taking it to the Fan with a series of lefts and rights, the Fan covers up but gets kicked in the stomach for his efforts and taken over by an armdrag. Tony : Chaos is tagged in and Anarchy are now double teaming the Fan who if I'm not mistaken didn't have a tattoo on his left arm before. Anyhow, in a powerful move the Fan double clotheslined both men down and makes a tag to the Black Scorpion. Terry : Watch bug boy get squashed here by two real men. Scorpion goes for a follow up double clothesline but both members of Anarchy duck and hit him with elbows right in the mug. Johny : I've noticed Scorpion and the Fan seem a bit off this past month, making silly mistakes and not really using the things that had made them a successful combo. The boys of Anarchy are really hammering Scorpion in the corner and Chaos has him held in piledriver position, Raiden goes upstairs and drives him into the mat. Tony : Chaos with the cover and Raiden keeps the Fan out of the ring for an impressive victory by Anarchy. Terry : Our next match is about to get started and features two bums...er, rookie sensaions in the making, yeah that sounds right. Johny : Propane and Vinnie 'The Bronx' Bomber are set to square off and this should be a better match then Terry would give it credit for. Tony : I agree and this one starts off as a brawl, with Propane gaining the early edge. He whips Vinnie into the corner and decks him with a running clothesline, he now drags Vinnie to the ropes and sends him across but Vinnie ducks the elbow attempt and hits Propane with a forearm on the far side. Terry : Vinnie scoops Propane up and powerslams him to the mat but misses a kneedrop and gets blasted by an elbow from Propane on the back of the neck. Johny : Propane with a scoop and a big bodyslam, and he follows it up with a big splash, he goes for another but this time Vinnie raises his knees and cathes Propane in the ribs. Tony : Vinnie rises and connects with the B-52 Bomber, a suplex into a pildriver, and then he follow it up with the Bronx Bomb jackhammer for the 1-2-3. Terry : (sarcastically said) Oh yeah, you guys were right that match was just SO unbelievably great, I am grateful I got a chance to call it. Tony : Must you be such a jerk all the time Terry? Never mind I know the answer to that one. Nikodemous Segier must again begin his climb towards a title shot and his first opponent is Apocalypse. Johny : Apocalypse is staring Segier down now, that he has entered the ring. Segier doesn't seem fazed by this and locks up with Apocalypse but gets taken over in an armdrag which he follows up with a spinning legdrop. Tony : Segier gets thrown head first to the outside and there goes Apocalypse with a superb plancha. He sets Segier up and stands on the ring apron before driving his elbow into the gut and Segier winces in pain. Terry : Apocalypse fires Nik back into the ring but pays for it as Segier stomps at his head on the way back in. A German suplex by Segier but Apocalypse kicks out at 2, so Segier lifts him up and drives him to the mat with a brainbuster. Johny : Segier heads to the middle turnbuckle and comes off with an elbowdrop right on target. Nik is in firm control now and tosses Apocalypse to the corner before connecting with a running kneelift to the breadbasket. Tony : Nik has grounded the high flying assault of Apocalypse with a rear chinlock and is now slowly working into that Segier Stretch of his and Apocalypse doesn't quite see it coming. Terry : Segier has his pansy little hold cinched in now and Apocalypse taps out, what a bum. The Figure Four of the Painmaker's is FAR superior to that little stretch hold! Tony : It's now time for the Three Way Big Man Match between Big Eliem, Jack and Bob. I hear that this is just the beginning to a lot of these types of matches. Terry : Big Eliem is the only big man worth watching, the rest spend more time eating then learning how to wrestle or fight. The lights start to flicker and eventually out. Then after about 10 seconds the lights come back on, Thanatos the Reaper of Souls is standing behind Big Eliem. Thanatos drops Eliem with an inverted DDT. He grabs a mic... Thanatos : "Harrington! This is the second week in a row that I haven't been scheduled to fight! I have two challenges out standing against me, one from Mad Coil and one from Deadbolt, and I have made a challenge to Jack which the coward refused to accept. I warn you Harrington, next week I'd better have a match signed or the NCW will face the wrath of the Reaper of Souls . . ." Tony : It looks like Commissioner Harrington is on his way to the ring to respond. Harrington : "First of all, YOU ARE FINED $5000. Second of all, you are also in THIS match. And just for this match, pinfalls count anywhere!." Commisioner Harrington then throws the mic to the outside and the bell sounds. Tony : As soon as Harrington moved Jack nailed Thanatos with a stiff clothesline and it appears that a second referee has been added to the fray. Big Eliem is still laid out near the edge of the ring apron and Bob has decided to help Jack double team Thanatos. Terry : Thanatos is getting pounded in the corner by those two massive idiots while my man Eliem is biding his time. Johny : He looks unconscious to me, but if you want to call it strategy be my guest. Thanatos is starting to fight Jack off as Bob goes to the far corner to his bag of cookies. Tony : Thanatos just hip tossed Jack over the top rope to the outside. Eliem is stirring finally and spots Bob with his back turned. A DEVIOUS low blow kick by Eliem and Bob is hurting after that one. Terry : That kick was CLEARLY on the inner thigh man, you need glasses. Thanatos and Jack are brawling on the outside and after a quick thumb to the eye a huge slam by Jack on the concrete. Meanwhile in the ring Eliem just dropped Bob throat first across the top rope with a stungun! Johny : Eliem spots Thanatos and runs towards the ropes before flying over the top in a plancha!!! OH MY, he's lucky he didn't break his ribs and/or Thanatos' shoulder on that one. Tony : Jack sees both men are hurt and is stomping them both, he now picks up Eliem and slams him ON TOP of Thanatos. He goes for the pin but Eliem rolled off Thanatos and the count is broken. Terry : An angry looking Bob spots Eliem on the outside and pulls him in by the hair. He's yelling something about 'YOU BREAK BOB COOKIE, NOW BOB BREAK YOU!". Johny : Thanatos has gotten up to his feet and he's rumbling with Jack again but in the ring Bob as ripping Eliem apart with tremendous powerslams and a huge elbow. Bob sets Eliem up near the corner and climbs up backwards to the middle rope before bouncing on it (think Vader) and there he goes.... Tony : Would we call that a Bob Bomb? Anyway it connected and Eliem has nowhere to go, so Bob gains the victory! Terry : It is now time for that six man tag match between Shaman, Mad Coil and Rocky Stevens vs DKC (Talon, Tiburon and Deadbolt). Johny : This is Stevens' first real chance to show what he has in a match just before a main event. It looks like Stevens and Talon will start it off and Rocky just bieled Talon halfway across the ring in an impressive showing of strength. Tony : Talon comes back at Stevens and gets shoved right back down to the canvas again, Deadbolt tags in and points directly to Shaman, who Stevens then tags. Terry : Shaman and Deadbolt lock up and Deabolt gets the quick armbar which is reversed into a hammerlock and Shaman just release German suplexed Deadbolt over. Johny : Shaman goes for the cover but Tiburon comes in and makes the save, all 6 men are in the ring now and Talon just hit Mad Coil low before tossing him out of the ring. Stevens is being held off by Tiburon as Talon and Deadbolt double team Shaman. Tony : The referee has restored some semblance of order but Shaman just gorilla press slammed Deadbolt with his last remaining strength, he can surely tag Stevens in now though. Terry : Shaman extends his arm towards his partner, but Stevens just jumped off the apron leaving Shaman to keep getting pounded on. Stevens is going underneath the ring and pulls out that 2x4 we've seen a lot of lately and gets back on the apron. Johny : At this point Deadbolt is claiming he's hurt, but he's just keeping the ref distracted and Stevens nails Shaman which allows the now miraculously healed Deadbolt to pin him. Tony : This is disgusting, Total Anarchy is here now, with the exception of Chaos and they are starting to beat on Shaman and Mad Coil. But the NEW Regulators who were seated at ringside come to help out. Terry : But Total Anarchy is holding them off fairly easily with the 2x4 and the numbers edge. It looks like security is coming down to break this one up but Total Anarchy has grown stronger tonight in a nice fashion. Johny : What was so nice about that purposeful assault?? Security has finally made it down to the ring and Total Anarchy nonchalantly leave the ringside area. Tony : It's now time for the main event as Angel Reaper prepares to make his sixth NYC title defense, fourth on his second stint as champion against the 'Wild Thing' Chris Rogers. Terry : Didn't Reaper leave Rogers on a cross the last time they fought? Johny : Yes that was at Final Friday aka Darken's Birthday Bash on February 13th. It was after that match that Wild Thing's attitude changed and he became known as 'Whining Thing' for quite a bit. Tony : Rogers attacks Reaper as he was handing the belt over to the referee, and Chris is pounding away at his head and the back of his neck. Terry : You see if Reaper had hit Rogers with the belt before the bell sounded this match would be over, but since he's not in the House, he's an idiot! Johny : This is a classic speed guy against a technical wrestler bout and right now Rogers has the edge. He just poked Reaper in the eye and raked the face, this match is about 90 seconds old and Rogers has yet to stop cheating. Tony : A slam by Rogers and he follows up with an elbowdrop but Reaper tosses him off as he went for the cover. Terry : Rogers misses a knee to the face and clutches at it as Reaper gets to his feet and pulls Rogers up by his hair. Boot to the midsection and Reaper locks on a double underhook, he muscles Rogers up and powerbombs him down hard to the mat! Johny : Reaper drops a hard elbow to Rogers nose bloodying him, wait a minute here come that masked man again from the crowd carrying his 2x4. He just dropped it at ringside and is starting to yell at Angel Reaper and in the process distracting the ref. Tony : Hey someone is coming from underneath the ring, it's Chaos!!! That's where he has been hiding and he just BLASTED Rogers from behind with the board. Terry : What the HELL??? What's with the referee didn't he hear that? Reaper turns and smacks the ref in the back getting him to count and he gets the pin. DAMNIT!!! Johny : The masked man is unmasking, it's Havoc!!! Wait a minute here comes the rest of Total Anarchy, and they seem to be here to celebrate with their two NEW recruits, Reaper and Stevens. This is a shocker!! Anarchy's, Chaos and Raiden just hoisted each of them up on their shoulders and we're desperately out of time, see ya next week.
