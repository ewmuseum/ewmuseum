WWA: Underground March 6, 2010 - Episode #108 Live from the Louis J. Tullio Arena in Erie, PA Chapter I Somers & Intrepid Promo Denrol: Good evening ladies and gentleman and welcome to another Underground. Jack: Right before we came online, notice was given that Intrepid will be going out to the ring to address the WWA. Denrol: I would like it if he would explain to me why he is such dumb hick… At the same moment Denrol was speaking, Intrepid and The Family walk up alongside on the announce desk backstage. Denrol turns around in his chair to face Intrepid as Jack reaches out and holds onto his announce partner’s shoulder. Jack: It would seem that we have visitors Denrol… Denrol: Really Sherlock what gave you that idea? Without speaking Intrepid, reaches forward, yanks the headsets off Denrol and Jack, and hand them to D.Filed and Virtue. Intrepid motions for them to leave and Denrol gets up and storms off with mic barely catching him cursing Intrepid out and Jack follows quietly with a confused look on his face. We don’t want any unneeded interruptions while WWA’s Magistrate of Mayhem is speaking. Virtue: Go and open their eyes father. 'Must Kill' by Cavalera Conspiracy begins playing and not even a second after that the entrance ramp begins flashing with a quick burst of white strobe light and Intrepid comes out quickly holding 'the' chair but stops on the entrance ramp. He stares out into the crowd grooving to the beat of the music, glaring at them through the eyes of his mask. He suddenly thrusts his arm into the air and the crowd reacts vehemently toward him. Intrepid doesn’t seem fazed by it at all, he just continues enjoying the music. Before leaving the ramp, he unfolds the badly beaten up chair, sets it down at the entrance, and then takes off running down the aisle. He dives onto the ring apron and slides into the ring on his stomach. When He reaches the opposite side of the ring, he pushes himself into a standing position. Although, when he does stand up he tends to limp slightly and reaches back at his hip. Intrepid: Kill the music! Intrepid hobble paces the ring for a minute working his hip to the sound of nothing but boos and the occasional curse word. Again, it is as if he has the Nick Adams syndrome and the audience is Ry Ballard, he continues to ignore them. Finally, Intrepid raises the mic up to where his mouth is in his mask. Intrepid: Once upon a time, there was a young rookie named Silas, who joined an International wrestling promotion. He had high hopes and big plans in this company because he immediately saw things that needed attention. The crowd is clearly not in the mood for a long rant from Intrepid. Some begin to leave while others just enjoy their time by throwing verbal assaults or even physical ones with their trash. Intrepid continues to pace eventually working out his hip. Intrepid: This company was a gaping hole of contradictions. Silas, try as he might, just couldn’t get it right. There was no room in this company for any type of justice and it ate away at anyone trying to fend against it. Crowd: Silas Sucks, Silas Sucks, Silas Sucks! Intrepid: Indeed Silas did suck, coming fresh out of an indie league in California with stars in his eyes, thinking that his brand of justice was going to help correct the evils that have long since been rooted into this company. Long rooted evils that eventually buried this company after Silas had left. Members of the crowd are starting to chant for Mal to come out. Intrepid: Do not rush the story! I will get to him when I feel like it! The opening riff of “Can I Play with Madness” reverberates over the P.A. and the crowd changes from their booing and into cheering. As the song kicks in, and the stage area begins to glow with ultraviolet light, and a figure steps from the darkness into the eerie light. The crowd roar as Mal Somers stands at the top of the ramp, arms in the air. The ultraviolet light gets brighter and brighter until the scene changes as two massive explosions of white pyro occur on either side of the ramp and Somers heads down the ramp, stopping where “the chair” is sitting. The music fades out but the crowd continues in its intensity as Mal rounds the chair as Intrepid looks on impatiently. Intrepid: So…here, WWA’s precious and pudgy Mal Somers. The crowd hoots and hollers at Intrepid’s announcement and Mal stops circling the chair and stares Intrepid down with a smug smile on his face and pats his belly. The crowd loves the simple antic and they begin chanting again. Crowd: Silas sucks, Silas sucks, Silas sucks! Suddenly Virtue pops out of the entrance with a microphone in hand, the crowd begins to react to Intrepid’s use of the child, and walks right up toward Mal and hands him the instrument. Mal tries to speak to Virtue but the child just glares at him and you can hear through the lowered microphone in Mal’s hand ; Virtue: Heathen! Intrepid: As the story goes…I wasn’t getting any traction here in the cancerous company and sought help…and who did I find…Mal Somers himself. The crowd gives a small pop with the name drop but quickly dies back out. Intrepid: Well, Mal and I were a perfect fit to start a whirlwind in this company…and it started to work, until a certain Frankenstein opened my eyes during my first and only title defense for the defunct Television title. Intrepid: He opened my eyes to the fact that this company is never going to survive with idiot wrestlers that want to hold onto some kind of Puritan ideal that competition should only thrive in a wholesome environment. The crowd reacts as does Mal as jerks his head around and stares at Intrepid. Mal: That’s enough from you Silas! You and I came together to put the fun back into entertaining these people out here…right now! The crowd cheers again. Mal: You keep blaming me that I left you in your time of need…when you ought to have been looking back over what you screwed up! Intrepid climbs up onto one of the ring posts and begins shouting at Mal. Intrepid: Entertainment is dead! The cancer melts away our ability to perform and compete in this ring! When we try to protect these idgits from the “evils” of self-serving competitors because our kids will learn to emulate them…bah! Mal leans forward in the wobbly and bent chair and puts on a more serious face. Mal: Silas, do you hear yourself? Who will you compete for if you remove those who will come to watch? Entertainment is dead? I’m beginning to think that Lucas did more than snap your neck a couple of years ago…I think he may have bent your ability to listen while you talk. Crowd: Silas sucks, Silas sucks, Silas sucks! Intrepid looks around at the crowd and becomes agitated perched atop the ring post as the crowd taunts him with his family name. Mal holds his hand up trying to calm the crowd. Mal: Hold on, hold on! Silas, what do you want from me…I mean you tried to beat my head in only after you deceived me…and then began a campaign to pull me down emotionally to your level. Intrepid: What I want...! Mal stands up immediately from the chair and begins marching down the aisle toward the ring much to the delight of the audience. Intrepid stays perched atop the ring post arrogantly watching Mal approach him. Mal walks deliberately up the ring steps and remains footed on the apron outside. He stares up at Intrepid challenging him through an almost calm stare down. Mal raises his mic and urges Intrepid to continue. Mal: You were sayin’… Intrepid: Quietly responding into his microphone I want you to admit that you were wrong… The crowd waits in tension for Mal’s response as he just smiles and shakes his head slowly, almost giving a “you’re pathetic” look with his body language. Intrepid: I want you to admit that you were wrong it what you did by abandoning me when I needed you most…admit that by turning your back on me to serve your own selfish purpose you made me who I am today. The crowd boos at Intrepid’s remarks and Mal stands there still smiling but mouths what to him. Mal: I made you huh? Intrepid: I want you to face me at Retribution in an I Quit match! Intrepid: You’ll face me in an I Quit match, but instead of saying I Quit you’ll have to admit that you were wrong. By saying this, you will validate that by hitting you with that chair a couple of months ago. I was just making things…shall we say…even. Intrepid: Name it… The crowd loves this open-ended offer by Intrepid, as does Mal who plays it up with the crowd while Intrepid still sits idly by atop of the ring post. Mal: Send the kid home…he doesn’t belong here. The fans cheer on Mal’s altruistic behavior. Intrepid: Reluctantly Fine…whatever…I don’t care ‘cause I am gonna get everything I want out of you. Mal hops off the ring apron and begins to leave but continues to talk without facing Intrepid. Mal: Would you just get out of here Silas…some of us do have matches tonight and would like to get this show on the road. Intrepid gets down off the ring post and follows after Mal and the crowd can’t wait for the two to meet. Mal: Plus, you seriously need to give Denrol his headset back…I think he’s about to wet himself backstage. Intrepid: You will never talk to me with you back turned son…I think you need a little lesson taught before Retribution. Intrepid rushes Mal but it seems that he was prepared and waiting for the masked man. Mal turns and slams the microphone into Intrepid’s face with a resounding thud. Intrepid goes down to protect himself and the security is already streaming out into the aisle way. Mal: You have seriously left the building Silas…I almost feel sorry for you… Before the security team is fully between them both Mal leans in with a sharp kick to Intrepid’s wounded hip, sending him flailing in pain on the floor. The crowd cheers him on even thought the punishment is only short. Mal: See you soon Silas…I’ll make sure I bring you that life you’ve been needing. Mal is escorted out as the crowd responds to his smiles and points towards the floored Intrepid. Jack: Ladies and gentlemen, we're back and Underground is under way.
