The Scouting Report - Issue 2

24 Sep 2003

Bylined Joe Stock - Director of Talent, in the fed's own kayfabe voice.

Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Welcome ladies and gents to the second edition of The Scouting Report. The WWA is gearing up for it's big United Kingdom tour and it should be some great fun. Meanwhile in the rest of the wrestling world, well all I have to say is that Monday night proved the saying. Never say never in this business. Let's get into it. - The WWA's last house show in the US was on Sunday and we thank our fans in Philly for giving us one hell of a send-off. We were proud to inform them they would be getting a Monday Night Meltdown in the near future. - WWA's own Doctor Taber has deemed Tracer Bullet's shoulder 100%. He managed to squeeze in an appearance at our Philly house show in a non-title match with Craven Moore. He will be in Manchester for Meltdown; through it's unknown what he'll be doing at that show. - Dr. Taber has also cleared "Mighty" Joe Keane for action. He's working at the WWA Dojo right now, getting back into ring shape. I'm not sure whether he will be in Manchester, but expect him back on TV very soon. - Where is the Definition? Troy Walcott has a mildly separated shoulder. Three specialists have ruled that surgery is in no way necessary; through he will begin intense physical therapy next week. Walcott should be out about 3 months. - All other WWA superstars are at or near 100%. Hopefully we'll get through this UK tour without any serious losses. - There is serious talk about some division changes in the WWA. The Extreme and Cruiserweight Titles may someday in the future, be combined to form a division similar to the NWA: TNA X Division. There are a number of superstars we believe have the ability to give us intense action of that style, including Eddie van Dorn, James Beam, Wes Sanders, Denrol, and others. - It's not well known, but the WWA has been working on a possible Women's Division. The head trainer is Vik Ventressa, who is spending his retirement working with some possible female wrestlers. While he showed up at IDR, there is no serious talk of bringing him back, because he doesn't seem to want to come back, telling me he prefers working with women. - TJ Donaldson has decided to give wrestling a break. I have no idea if Donaldson will be back in the ring anytime soon. His faithful sidekick Dweeb however, will be continuing in the WWA in a backstage capacity. - Death has arrived. Hopefully the superstar playing him will help out the somewhat weak Horsemen angle. I have no idea when we see Death show his face. - Damien Thorne and I have had some issues in the past, but during a honest discussion backstage at a recent house show, we managed to clear the air. I wish Thorne best of luck in the WWA. - The WWA superstars are enjoying their week off before our UK tour. Maybe next week I'll tell you what your favorite wrestlers were up to on their paid vacations. - Not much news on the new talent front. None of the recent demo tapes have impressed me. - We got a great lineup for Meltdown in Manchester. A lot of our new talent gets their television debuts in front of crowd that sold out months ago. - Ozzy O and James Beam are back on the WWA roster. Ozzy will face the impressive Christyan Payne. Payne has shown a lot of talent in his house show matches and on meltdown, he should knock the socks off everyone when he makes his television debut. Hopefully Ozzy will also show why he belongs here. - While I've never been a big fan of having more than four men in a tag match, the six-man on Meltdown could be the exception. Let's hope that all the talent gets a chance to shine. - I'm not sure how good Rip 'n' Strip Vs The Texas Longhorns will be, but it'll be a one of a kind match. - I would not be surprised if the Horsemen continue their feud with Havoc by getting involved in the Tag Team Title match on Meltdown. The only question is if Extreme Impact can take advantage of their big chance. - EVD and Thorne will have one hell of a match I'm sure, but I also think it won't settle the feud that was started at IDR. Eddie will never forgive Thorne for costing him the World Title shot and Thorne will do anything to get that Extreme Title around his waist. -While most internet fans seem to think Shane Dorion has his match with James Thornhill all wrapped up, War made a great showing at IDR for the Horsemen and will be looking to make a mark by beating the whiny Dorion. - The Triple Threat match will blow the roof off the G-Mex Arena, no doubt about it. All three men have issues with each other. Kraven hates both men. Moore knows if he loses, the winner will get a title shot very soon. Denrol is looking to end his losing streak and place himself right into the World Title situation. Blood, sweat, and tears will be shed in this match before it's all said and done. - While the Fiesta lineup hasn't been announced, I have confirmation we will see Michael Stryfe Vs Wes Sanders for the Television Title. Sanders was amazing at IDR, let's see if he can follow it up. That's it for this week, [name withheld] out.
