WWA — 25 February 1998

25 Feb 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Wednesday Febuary 25, 1998 The screen opens up to the the NCW banner. Then.... Northeast Champion Wrestling flashes through the screen and leaves the NCW Banner Then firework type explosions go off around the Banner. The Banner fades... then the screen shows Lehman College's The Apex. The screen then goes to the inside to show the fans jumping and screaming at the Camera. The camera pans over the the complex and then the camera finds Tony Bailey, Terry Valentine and Johny Morehouse sitting behind the table in the broadcast area. Tony: Welcome everybody to Wednesday Night War. Tonight we'll see the New York City title on the line as the champion, Angel Reaper, defends it against the former member of the Triple Threat, "Brutal" Brian Cairo. We'll also see the grudge between the newcomer Shaman and "Wild Thing" Chris Rogers come to a head. We've otherwise got a lot of newcomers showing their skills against one another tonight, so lets get to the ring where Gary Trudeau is, ready to announce the first match. The camera zooms down to the ring were we see Gary Trudeau in the center on the ring with a microphone. Gary: Ladies and gentlemen, the following match this evening is set for one fall with a twenty minute time limit. Introducing first, weighing in at 317 lbs, and hailing from parts unknown, we have from the Four Horsemen of the Apocolypse Gutter! "Gutter Ballet" by Savage begins to blare over the loud speakers and the behemoth known as Gutter strolls out from behind the curtain to boos and quite a few one finger salutes. Gary: And his opponent, weighing in at 264 lbs, and hailing from Oakland California, we have the Oakland Oddball! The 60's beach tune Wipeout replaces Gutter Ballet and "Maniac" Michael Gates accompanies his man The Oakland Oddball to ringside. Tony : Both men in this match are trying to turn things around here, since neither had gotten off to a good start. Terry : That's true Tony, and I think both of these guys have great attitudes. Johny : Of course you would Terry, they both like to cheat. Terry : It's not cheating if you don't get caught! Tony : Well while you guys argue about morality the match has begun and these two lock up in the center of the ring. Gutter is trying to power the Oddball down, but Oddball fires a boot to the ribs knocking Gutter down to a knee. Terry : That was a good move, you want to keep a big man down and Oddball is now pummeling Gutter in the face, which he seems to be enjoying. Johny : He's laughing at Oddball, I don't think anyone ENJOYS getting punched in the face Terry. A scoop and a slam by the Oddball and he goes for a cover but Gutter powers out at 1 1/2. Tony : Gutter is dragged to his feet and whipped to the ropes where he ducks an attempted Oddball clothesline and delivers a forearm shot which took the Oddball off his feet. Terry : This has been a slugfest and the bigger man usually wins these, Gutter with a gorilla press slam knocking the starch out of the Oddball but he doesn't go for a cover, I think he wants to toy with Oddball a bit more. Johny :Gutter goes for an Irish whip which gets reversed and Michael Gates trips Gutter but the ref didn't see it as Oddball diverted his attention. Tony : He's setting up for the Wipeout and it's perfectly executed leaving Gutter in the gutter I guess. ( smiles at his joke. ) Terry : Remind me why I have to work with such talentless commentators sometime please! Tony : Shut up Terry. Johny : The Oddball goes for the cover, 1 - 2 - 3. The Oakland Oddball gets the victory. Tony : Our next match features the suddenly sadistic Wild Thing and the man who has been quiet since losing his 1st title shot a little over 2 weeks ago, Johnny Dice. The camera zooms down to the ring were we see Gary Trudeau in the center on the ring with a microphone. Gary: Ladies and gentlemen, the second match this evening is set for one fall with a twenty minute time limit. Introducing first, weighing in at 235 lbs, and hailing from Lakeland Florida, we have "Wild Thing" Chris Rogers! A church bell rings seven times and the sound resonates through the arena with a chorus of boos for the Wild Thing. He comes out mocking the fans and they show ther "appreciation" by throwing things at him. Gary : And his opponent, weighing in at 279 pounds and hailing from New York, New York, we have Johnny Dice! "Who Do You Love?" by George Thoroughgood begins to play and the crowd boos as Johnny Dice walks out from the backstage area. Terry : I wonder if Wild Thing took one shot too many to the head, his attitude has done a complete 180 in the last month. Tony : I wonder why Dice chose that song as his entrance music, no one seems to love him. Terry : It's called irony genius, perhaps you should get some smarts between now and Friday so you don't embarass me as my broadcast partner. Johny : Enough with the senseless bickering "Wild Thing" was just tossed halfway across the ring in a tremendous biel by Dice. Tony : Maybe that will knock some sense back into Chris Rogers. Wild Thing is dragged to his feet by the hair and Dice crushes him to the mat with a big right hand. Terry : Wild Thing complains to the ref and thumbs Dice in the eye, before slamming him down to the mat. Wild Thing is climbing to the top rope and he leaps at Dice who by this time had gotten to his feet and he catches the Wild Thing in a backbreaker. Johny : Dice looks bored in the ring and he yells out "It's time for me to roll the dice" and he just knocked Wild Thing out with his finisher the Snake Eyes Slam, before covering for the 1-2-3. Terry : I will give the "Wild Thing" credit for something. Johny : And what would that be? Terry : He sure can take a beating, in fact he might do that better then anyone else in the NCW! ( laughs ) Johny : Dice with a big win, which should get him back on the road to another title shot. Tony : Speaking of Title shots, this next match may be for a title shot in the coming weeks. Either of of these two wrestlers after gaining a win here tonight will be derserving of a title shot in the near future. The camera zooms down to the ring were we see Gary Trudeau in the center on the ring with a microphone. Gary: Ladies and gentlemen, the third match this evening is set for one fall with a twenty minute time limit. Introducing first, weighing in at 240 pounds, and hailing from Chicago, Illinois, we have "Brutal" Brian Cario! The opening riff from Marilyn Manson's Beautiful People begins to play and the younger goth kids in the crowd begin a little hardcore moshing as "Brutal" Brian comes out slapping hands with the fans. Gary : And his opponent, weighing in at 245 pounds and hailing from Miami, Florida, we have Kue! "Because you're Unforgiven too...." sung by James Hetfield of Metallica is heard over the loud speakers and out comes Kue to some cheers. He just strides to the ring ignoring the adoring crowd. Tony : That's the problem with America's youth they tend to be too obsessed with violence. Terry : There's nothing wrong with some good old fashioned violence, Tony. Tony : This match should be a good test for both men, most of the NCW thinks Cairo is not nearly as good on his own as he was when he had the Triple Threat watching his back, and Kue may get a shot at gold if he wins. Terry : I don't know if Cairo has won since he left the TT, but he should be able to beat a pool player. Johny : Kue starts off with some martial arts kicks, none of which land but seem to be bothering Cairo as he doesn't know how to get in close. Tony : Kue seems to be toying with Cairo, but that's just my opinion. Terry : And it's a dumb one I might add, but Cairo does look confused here as Kue charges in and takes him down with a clothesline. It's nice to see "The Chick" again, she's looking as lovely as ever. Johny : Do you need to be alone Terry? Anyway, Cairo has fired back and is working on an armbar which gets reversed into a fireman's carry take over by Kue. Terry : I wouldn't mind if you two idiots took a walk, but I have a job to do and Kue is now dominating the Brutal one and connects with a pancake suplex. Tony : He's setting up the Double Bank now by stepping on Cairo's head, and lifting up his right arm with both hands before wrenching back and stepping on the elbow, causing extreme pain for Brian. Terry : It looks like Cairo tapped out, the referee is calling for the bell and we have a commotion going on in the background, it looks as if Angel Reaper is standing in the aisleway but he's not doing anything. Johny : Maybe he's just scouting his future competition or trying to let Kue know that he's ready. In either case Kue moves to 2-0. Tony : Our next match is the first match of round one of the CWC Rhode Island Title Tournament for the cruiserweights only. Terry : I am gonna pick "The Enforcer" to win this whole thing, he's by far the best in this 8 man tournament. Johny : I would have to dispute that, Johnny Blaze has held 2 different titles here and has the most championship experience of all 8 men. Tony : That is true but maybe "Merciless" Mark Lynch can get back on the championship track, I guess we will all see these 3 men as well as some relative newcomers vie for the chance to make it to round two! The camera zooms down to the ring were we see Gary Trudeau in the center on the ring with a microphone. Gary: Ladies and gentlemen, the next match this evening is set for one fall with a thirty minute time limit. It will be a round 1 match in the CWC Rhode Island Title Tournament. Introducing first, weighing in at 235 pounds, and hailing from Paris, France we have representing the UKA Nikodemous Segier! "I get knocked down and I get up again, you ain't never gonna keep me down..." from Tubthumping by Chumbawumba plays as the now figure fourless Nikodemus Segier walks out to boos from the crowd. Segier undeterred bows and poses as if he doesn't recognize the neagtive response. Gary : And his opponent, weighing in at 220 pounds and hailing from Chicago Illinois we have Johnny Blaze! Pepper by the Butthole Surfers begins to play and out walks Johnny Blaze to massive cheers from the fans, as he walks the aisle and slaps the fans hands. A mysterious looking figure wearing all black and a black mask emerges behind him and waits in Blaze's corner. Tony : Who is that guy? And what is he doing here? Terry : The king of the obvious statements strikes again! Johny : I really think that this would have been the ideal final for this title, but you fans lucked out and get to see this tremendous match in round one. Tony : That's right Johny and these two athletes waste no time squaring off in the center of the ring, with Segier muscling the slightly smaller Blaze to the ropes where he fires him across the ring for a beautiful dropkick. Terry : Segier is looking pleased with himself as he should be, look at the beautiful females in his entourage! Johny : Am I detecting some pent up sexual frustration Terry?? ( Terry glares at Johny ) In the ring Segier is clamping down with a nerve hold on Blaze who rolls to a standing position and fires 3 quick elbows to the breadbasket before a hiplock takeover. Tony : Blaze follows up with a bodyslam and a reverse chinlock which Segier rolls out of and converts into a modified abdominal stretch. Blaze uses his leverage to hiptoss Segier to the mat as the crowd applauds. Terry : He grabbed his tights, is the referee blind? Come on Nik, teach that goody goody a lesson! Johny : It seems Segier heard you as he delivered a dropkick to Blaze's knee and is now applying a Boston Crab, but Blaze saw it coming and crawled tp the ropes and got the break. Tony : It seems Blaze's back is a little tight and he's trying to stretch but Nik refuses to give him any time to rest and drops Blaze with a series of right hands. A scoop and a slam followed by a very arrogant cover only gets Nik a 1 count. Terry : He's as arrogant as that Reaper guy, but will he be as successful in the long run, I don't know. Segier is working Blaze's lower back like a speedbag, firing forearm shots to the kidneys. Johny : Blaze looks hurt and Segier is busy taunting the fans, that will get him nowhere against someone the caliber of Blaze. Blaze gingerly gets to a vertical base and sidesteps a dropkick by Segier and clamps on the Hell's Fire surfboard!!! Tony : Segier is squirming and manages to escape the hold as Blaze's back doesn't have the necessary strength to keep him there. Terry : Did Tony just make a valid point? It's not April Fools day is it? Anyway my man Nik is now coming at Blaze with a head full of steam, Blaze leaps over and a sunset flip rollup of Segier, Blaze with a cover and a 3 count, Blaze gets the W-with THAT move? Johny : Segier was looking for something special and got beat with an elementary move maybe that will teach him not to be so arrogant. Tony : I doubt it but Johnny Blaze advances, he will face the winner of this next match in round 2. Does anyone have some info on that man in black? Terry : It's now the Metallica portion of our show, but just remember Segier has the lovely miss Camero and Blaze doesn't! The camera zooms down to the ring were we see Gary Trudeau in the center on the ring with a microphone. Gary: Ladies and gentlemen, the next match this evening is set for one fall with a thirty minute time limit. It will be a round 1 match in the CWC Rhode Island Title Tournament. Introducing first, weighing in at 235 pounds, and hailing from Las Vegas Nevada, we have "The Ice" Jake Hetti! "Master of Puppets" by Metallica plays and the kids mosh a little more with former cop "The Ice" Jake Hetti coming down to ringside getting less of a response then his music did. Gary : And his opponent, weighing in at 215 pounds and hailing from Sweetwater, Texas we have representing the UKA, "Demented" Drake Blackthorne! The original version of Unforgiven, by Metallica begins to play and the Soul Pole wielding "Demented" Drake Blackthorne walks out to massive boos which he responds to with a double one finger salute. Tony : Kue really never gave Hetti a chance to show what he can do the last time we saw "The Ice". I think that both of these men really need a win here to gain some credibility but I don't see either getting by Johnny Blaze. Terry : Are you his cheerleader or a groupie, jeez ease up. Blackthorne with a blatant chokehold as the match begins but he got to close to Hetti he employed a belly to belly suplex, and got a quick 2 count seconds into the match. Johny : Drake gets to his feet and ducks a running lariat before delivering a dropkick and a spinning legdrop, which landed right across the bridge of Jake's nose and a little blood seeps out. Terry : YES! KICK HIM WHILE HE'S DOWN, LET'S SEE MORE BLOOD!! Tony : Please remember the NCW does not condone the comments of Terry Valentine. Blackthorne had used a hit and run style throughout the match and connects with a flying forearm and he got a near fall as a result. Terry : Hetti better use his size advantage before he sees an 0-2 record to the right of his name in the standings, and falls down the Harvester path of defeats. See, this guy is smart he's listening to me, and is now pummeling Drake who he caught when he went for another flying forearm shot and dropped in a powerslam. Johny : Hetti is in the zone now as he landed two release German suplexes in succession and almost got Blackthorne who barely kicked out before the 3 count. Tony : Hetti lifts up Drake and just delivered a brainbuster, and he's going for the cover. Terry : I don't think Drake's going anywhere, and I am right as usual as the ref's hand drops for the third time. Hetti vs Blaze in round two, should be worth watching. Johny : As is all NCW action, but we move to the lower half of the draw as match 3 of the first round is set to begin. The camera zooms down to the ring were we see Gary Trudeau in the center on the ring with a microphone. Gary: Ladies and gentlemen, the next match this evening is set for one fall with a thirty minute time limit. It will be a round 1 match in the CWC Rhode Island Title Tournament. Introducing first, weighing in at 229 pounds, and hailing from Carson California, we have Talon! "Gangsta's Paradise" by Coolio plays and the fans all boo as they have no love for rap music and no one really knows who the guy coming out is. Coolio's voice is abruptly cutoff as the song changes to a much more crowd pleasing "Hey Man Nice Shot" by Filter. Gary : And his opponent, weighing in at 235 pounds and hailing from St Paul Mionnesota we have accomponied to the ring by the House of Pain, "The Enforcer" Zack Crockett! "The Enforcer" Zack Crockett comes out with his stablemates, Aric Crockett, Cross and Outlaw. The crowd boos hysterically and the House of Pain shows BOTH belts off. Tony : We are finally getting a long look at the man known as Talon. Terry and Johny : WHO?? Tony : The H.O.P. seems to be in full force, I wonder how long it will take for them to cheat this time! Terry : The House never cheats they just give good guidance and counseling to each other, I wish you would stop accusing them otherwise. Johny : Well Talon is already on his back and the Enforcer is laying the boots to his face. Tony : Zack is yawning in the ring as he lifts Talon up for the Spinebuster and connects with it. Terry : Zack's going over to the corner and talking to his teammates, after a good 10 seconds he walks back and steps on Talon's face for the 3 count, what a pathetic display from Talon. Tony : What are you talking about, there was no need for Zack to do that! Terry : Well at least he didn't pants him and show the crowd how "inadequate" this loser Talon is. Johny : I won't even go there but the next and final match of our first round will be for the right to face the Enforcer in the semis of the Rhode Island Title Tournament. The camera zooms down to the ring were we see Gary Trudeau in the center on the ring with a microphone. Gary: Ladies and gentlemen, the next match this evening is set for one fall with a thirty minute time limit. It will be a round 1 match in the CWC Rhode Island Title Tournament. Introducing first, weighing in at 220 pounds, and hailing from South Boston Mass. Representing the UKA we have "Merciless" Mark Lynch! "Mars the Bringer of War" by Holst begins to play and the crowd who went for a bathroom break during the last match begin to file back into their seats as "Merciless" Mark Lynch steps out to a mostly negative reaction. Gary : And his opponent, weighing in at 215 pounds and hailing from London, England. Accomponied to the ring by Miss McLaine, we have Britainicca! "Princes of the Universe" by Queen replaces the Holst song and Britainicca walks down the aisle with the Union Jack raised high, leaving most fans to cheer but some begin to chant USA, USA! Terry : Hey, it's encyclopedia man! Tony : Shut up, they're is nothing wrong with being a patriot. Terry : Well nothing except the fact we kicked their ass in 1776, I guess if you want to side with losers that's your prerogative. Johny : I swear you two sound like a married couple. Lynch has already shown us 2 suplexes and Britainicca is wobbly. Lynch steps toward Britainicca and is dropped by a fierce clothesline as the Brit was playing possum, he's now locked on an armbar. Tony : Can you guys feel the excitement building, we're moments away from filling out the RI Title bracket and the main event is still waiting in the wings, as Jak "The Boil" Lancer squares off against "The Submissionist" Angel Reaper. Terry : Earth to Tony, we have a match going on! Speaking of the match Lynch has since reversed the armbar into a rear waistlock, but Britainicca rolls through and gets an anklelock submissionhold which Mark promptly broke free of with a well places kick to the jaw. Johny : It seems Britainicca chipped a tooth or bit his tongue because he just spit out some blood. Shut up Terry! ( Terry looks stunned as he was just about to say something ) Lynch with a gorgeous Northern Lights Suplex, and now he goes for the cover but Britainicca kicks out. Tony : This match continues to get more dramatic as Lynch is coming with his whole arsenal of moves but Britainicca is retaliating as best he can. Terry : Which doesn't say all that much since he's getting a wrestling lesson from the Merciless one. As I was saying Lynch goes for a snap suplex, but it's countered by that no talent hack and he gets lucky reversing the suplex on Mark. Johny : Are you a mark for Mark, Terry? Britainicca is going up top and lands a crossbody splash but Lynch rolls it into a pinning predicament and nearly got Britainicca. Tony : This is NCW action at it's finest, two bitter rivals, a great display of talent.... Terry ( Interupting Tony ): Can someone teach this guy a NEW set of cliches please? Two guys are trying to win a belt they don't care if it looks good to the fans. Johny : Once again my colleagues fail to mention that Lynch just delivered a spinning DDT but Britainicca showed tremendous heart by kicking out. Lynch looks frustrated as he drags Britainicca to his feet and sends him to the ropes, reversal and a dropkick by Britainicca sends Lynch across the ring. Tony : A scoopslam by Britainicca and he's going upstairs where he connects with a moonsault, but this time Lynch kicks out and the match continues. Terry : He's the best that's why he won't be beat. Lynch with a gouge to the eye, an excellent wrestling hold I might add. Tony : Lynch fires Britainicca to the ropes and this time he misses a clothesline and Britainicca continues gaining momentum rushing to the farside ropes before he bounces back with a massive clothesline that dumped Lynch awkwardly on his heas. Britainicca covers. Terry : GET UP MARK! Tony : 1 Johny : 2 Terry : GET UP!!! Tony : YES, that's 3 and Lynch goes down at the hands of Britainicca. Terry : That was a rip off my man Lynch is clearly better then everyone in this event. Johny : Well here's the brackets, Blaze vs Hetti and Zack Crockett vs Britainicca, I can't wait to see those two matches, but now it's time for the MAIN EVENT! The camera zooms down to the ring were we see Gary Trudeau in the center on the ring with a microphone. Gary: Ladies and gentlemen, the next match this evening is the MAIN EVENT! It is for the New York City Title. It is set for one fall with no time limit. Introducing first, the challenger weighing in at 233 pounds, and hailing from Scranton, PA. We have Jak "The Boil" Lancer! The Theme to Psycho starts up as Jak and the family walk towards the ring. The crowd gives a mixed reaction as Jak walks to the ring unaffected by the crowd. Gary : And his opponent, weighing in at 252 pounds and hailing from New York City. We have the CWC New York City Champion Angel Reaper! "Antichrist Superstar" by Marilyn Manson replaces the Psycho theme. The champion walks very confidently to the ring. The crowd isn't real fond of the Angel Reaper. He ignores the crowd and remains focussed on the title defense. Tony : Well the Angel Reaper has been reported to be spending a lot of time in one of our sister feds, Georgia Regional Wresting. He, Eric Smart and the Hardcore Legend have been making waves over there. Terry : Who cares! That's in another fed! All of those guys suck anyway! Tony : He's the NYC Champ, how does he suck? Terry : He just does! Can't I have an opinion! Johny : Ladies.... Please, in the ring it shows Jak Lancer with the upper hand with the champ in an Arm bar. Tony : Reaper gets tired of the hold and pushes Jak to the ropes. The ref asks for a clean break, but Jak nails Reaper with a kick to the midsection. Reaper is realing. Terry : Jak sends Reaper into the ropes and hits with a high drop kick. Jak immediately gets the Reaper in a reverse chin lock. Johny : Reaper stands up and nails Jak in the midsection with two elbows. Reaper pushes Jak into the ropes and sends him into the opposite ropes. Jak gets his head taken off by a beautiful clothesline from Reaper. Terry : What's all of that commotion over at the curtains? Tony : Oh god! It the Crockett's and Randy Cross. What are they doing here? Johny : Probably going to cheat somebody out of a win! Terry : Bite your tongue! The question is, who are they here for? Tony : I don't know, but what I do know is Jak is back in control of the match, he has gone back to the reverse chin lock. Johny : Jak sees the House of Pain and releases the hold and backs into a corner. Angel jumps up and looks at Jak. Jak just nods his head towards the House of Pain on the outside of the ring. Angel turns and sees them on the outside. Terry : Look at the Reaper! He is in fear! Tony : I think that look is hatred. Johny : Randy Cross just raises his hands in a shrug and tells the wrestlers to continue. Both, Angel Reaper and Jak Lancer, circle each other paying close attention to the House of Pain on the outside. Terry : Finally the two wrestlers lock up, NO! Lancer catches Reaper with a kick to the midsection. Jak follows up with an axe handle to the back of the Reaper. Look Aric is up on the ring Apron telling the referee about something. The ref is telling Aric to leave ringside. Tony : Meanwhile Jak pulls reapers hair to get him up and sends him into, no it is reversed.... Jak runs right into the referee sending the ref into Aric knocking him off on to the concrete and the ref is down in the ring. Jak is stunned on the ropes. Angel Reaper nails him with a clothesline sending him outside next to Aric Crockett. Johny : Angel Reaper kneels down to check on the referee. No Zack is on the top rope. He jumps and nails Angel Reaper in the back. Zack then picks up Angel Reaper and sends him into the ropes. Angel comes off and is nailed with the Spinebuster Slam. Terry : Cross rolls Jak Lancer into the ring and goes to the ref to help him. Inside Zack is draging Jak over on top of Angel Reaper. Tony : Zack rolls out of the ring and randy Cross has the referee stirring who looks and starts the count. NO, NO! The House of Pain! Terry : There is the three count! The new champion is Jak "The Boil" Lancer. Who is that running towards the ring? It's some masked looking freak.... Johny : That's no freak, that looks like Aguayfuero! He is a new member of the GRW! He comes running and promptly gets nailed from the now up Aric Crockett. Another man is coming through the crowd. It is the Black Scorpian. He rolls into the ring and corners Randy Cross. Cross is on his knees begging the Black Scorpian not to hurt him! Terry : Zack nails the Black Scorpian from behind and the Scorpian goes down. All three members of House of Pain start kicking the Black Scorpian. Tony : Jak Lancer is stirring and now realizes he is the NEW New York City Champion. Johny : He turns and is face to face with Randy Cross, who smiles and holds his hand out to Jak Lancer for a hand shake. Tony : We are OUT OF TIME! We've got to go!
