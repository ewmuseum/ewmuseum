The classic ladies man from the 80s that is. He's got the porn 'stache. He's got the sunglasses and the cocky smile. He's even got the blonde mullet. Butler is built solidly, resembling a tatto-less CM Punk. Ring Attire Butler wrestles with pant-tights. The design he wears varies, but usually red hearts and "B2" are incorporated in the design. He also wears knee and elbow pads, with designs that match the tights. Personality Butler thinks he's God's gift to both women and the wrestling world. He's quite happy to stand and mock those he opposes, soemtimes to the point of getting him in serious trouble.
