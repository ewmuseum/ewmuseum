The Scouting Report - Issue 16

28 Nov 2003

Bylined Josh Wilson - Director of Talent, in the fed's own kayfabe voice.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It’s time for another action packed edition of The Scouting Report with yours truly. Things have been going on recently with the WWA and I’m here to give you the lowdown. -After March Madness the WWA superstars have been given a week off to rest and recuperate. It just goes to show that the WWA cares about their employees. The front office wants them to be well rested and in excellent physical condition. -We have had a recent return to the WWA locker-room with Shaman. Shaman requested to have some time off to deal with personal affairs. It’s good to have the five-year veteran back with us. Now everybody will once again be shouting “Got Blood?” -This coming Saturday we have the first Fiesta of the month of April and what a card it will be. This is the first EWA supercard called Pain Games. -The event hopes to crown the first ever WWA European champion. It features the entire EWA locker-room, save Justin Bourne, and is the brainchild of Phil Flaws and Joe Stock. -The rules are very simple. Everyone is given a random number. The first 3 men start. When someone is eliminated, the next person comes out, and so on until everyone has entered. Eliminations are by pinfall, submission, or over the top rope. The last man standing is the winner and receives the honor of being the first champion. It should be very exciting. -Most are betting that Jarod Crow or Tommy Starr gets the nod but all of the locker-room has been impressing. I say look for the entire roster to make a good showing. -Also Justin “Air” Bourne will be Facing Jimmi Zion in a WWA/EWA inner promotional match. This is a match many have been waiting for since the return of both men. Zion and Bourne are both accomplished highflyers so expect a lot of high-risk moves. -Of course these aren’t the only matches on the card. Many other WWA superstars will be competing as well. -The former TV Champion Nick Adams will face one-half of the former Tag-Team Champions in Leviathan. Both men are coming off loses at March Madness and are hoping this match will put them back in the drivers seat. Look for title reigns once again in the near future. -A match originally supposed to be a debut match between Garrett Keith and Steven Rage has been changed with Keru no Akiro taking Keith’s place. -The reason for this is because Keith was recently let go having a contract with another promotion at the same time. This is a very sad event and we wish Keith luck in the future but some things have to be done. -Also James Beam, Showtime, James Curzon are booked to be in attendance. Were these guys going comedy usually follows. -Just two days after the big show in England the big daddy of all, Meltdown, returns to the airwaves and it promises to be one great show. -The card starts with the returning Se7en to face the Son of a Gun himself in Venny LeTrav. Se7en has a big problem on his hands with Venny being a former Tag-Team Champion and a viable contender for the TV Title. -Speaking of TV Titles, Grunge will be defending against Joey K. The former superhero is hoping to dethrone the pop culture freak. However one has to wonder, with Venny being in attendance will Joey be watching his back? And of course Grunge still has his mind on Nick Adams. This match should be a good one and pretty close to call. -Last week I mentioned that the Tag-Team division of the WWA should be starting to heat up and I was right. The champions X-Corp will be facing the Brothers Delucci, Havoc and The Cell in a non-title match. -All three teams are viable contenders with the Deluccis having the number one contender’s spot, the Cell being the former Tag-Team Champions and Havoc claiming to be the greatest of all time with their demolition of the Veteranz at Madness. -In a rematch of March Madness, Tracer Bullet will be facing Enigma. Unlike Madness the match will be contested under normal rules, no I Quits this time. One has to wonder though. Tracer has been claming to have a new target on recent Insiders. -Not only that, was on the tape that Denrol gave Tracer to give to Mr. Warr. Questions, so many questions. Guess you’ll have to check out Meltdown to find out. -And in the Main Event we have Damain Thorne facing off against the WWA Champion, Ian Tense in a non-title match. Add Kash to the equation as the special guest referee and your basically throwing gasoline on a brush fire. -One has to wonder with both Kash and Thorne after Tense’s belt and now add Jackson Kraven to the mix who will be the next champion. Also Wes Sanders has recently stated that he still wants the belt from Tense. Looks like an exciting title hunt. -We that’s all for this week. I’ll be back with more goings on in the WWA next time. Ciao.
