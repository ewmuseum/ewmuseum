Tom Sawyer Basic Information Nicknames | The Macho Ranger Real Name Height | 5'7" Weight | 183 Lbs Date of Birth | Dec. 12th, 1988 Home Town | Red Deer, Alberta, Canada Theme | "Tom Sawyer" by Rush More Details Appearance Tom is blonde and has hair long enough to reach his shoulders. He's somewhat lanky, but works out enough to have a bit of tone. However, he's too flighty to ever be truly "jacked", and packing muscle onto a frame like his won't end well. Tom is almost always smiling in the ring, because he loves wrestling wholeheartedly. Ring Attire White spandex pants with a blue streak down each hip, a Canadian Maple leaf at the top on both sides. Black shin-high boots. Personality Super friendly, outgoing, bubbly, open to his fans. Against his opponents, he tends to ignore them being scary or intimidating or powerful or monstrous, in favor of puffing himself and the Macho Madness up. History Tom started in a small Canadian wrestling company, where he and Lucas Harper ended up as a tag team randomly. It stuck, and they become a tag team known as The Foreshadowing. Eventually he was hired by DEFIANCE wrestling, won their tag titles three times, won the second Aggro Crag match, and was the last tag team champion upon the closing of DEFIANCE. Alignment | Face Manager | Lucas Harper, his former tag team partner.
