Harley and Tony DeLucci slide out of the ring to gang up on Lockdown as Leviathan rises and joins in, going straight for Adams as the five men brawl around the ring and the crowd goes insane! Chase : The crowd is blowing the roof off the arena! The referee is out cold! Mikal : This look like Mikal birthday party. Tony DeLucci elbows Lockdown who headbutts Harley who chops Leviathan, who is laying in some punches on Adams. Soon enough Adams whips Leviathan into the guardrail, and charges, but Leviathan back bodydrops the Rainmaker into the crowd. Leviathan follows him and the two brawl into the crowd. Chase : This leaves Harley, Tony DeLucci and Lockdown. Mikal : Vhat about Somers and Tommy? Chase : They still appear to be out of it! The fresher Lockdown takes Somers and DeLucci out with a double clothesline, and grabs one of the Tag Titles from the nearby timekeepers table. He awaits for Harley to rise and... CRACK! ...whacks him hard over the skull with the belt as the crowd begins to throw rubbish in the ring. Tony DeLucci picks up the other Title, and charges Lockdown, slamming him into the ring apron. He slides Lockdown into the ring, and follows after him, throwing the two tag titles in as he goes. Meanwhile Mal Somers and Tony DeLucci have come to, and have slid back into the ring also. Chase : This doesn't look good for Lockdown! All four men have risen and the DeLuccis touch knuckles as they look at the cornered Lockdown. They charge with a double clothesline, which is ducked by Lockdown, who charges across the ring and spears Somers into the corner turnbuckles. He reaches down and picks up a Tag Title. Chase : Lockdown isn't going down without a fight! Dave Harley slides into the ring and takes Lockdowns legs from under him, as the DeLuccis join in, all three stomp away on the Cell member. Despite this, Lockdown rises up and swings around like a madman. Chase : Tony's down! Mikal : And Tommy! Dave Harley manages to swing behind Lockdown and holds his arms behind him. Tommy DeLucci is first to rise, and grab the other belt from the canvas. He charges at the prone Lockdown, who ducks, making Tommy DeLucci swing and hit the unsuspecting Harley. Chase : Oh no! Harley has been on the receiving end of two hellacious belt shots. Mikal : Vhy only two? Hit him again! As Tommy DeLucci stands over Harley, Lockdown kneels down and connects with a nasty low blow. He stands over the two and holds the Tag Titles high in the air, laughing as the crowd mercilessly boos. Mal Somers gets back up and sneaks a reverse DDT on Lockdown. He goes over to the rising Tommy DeLucci and pushes him into the corner. Chase : Looks like Somers isn't taking too kindly to Tommys indiscretion! Mikal : Or vas it? Chase : This match has been insane. Mikal : Is crazy like Christmas in Shibaev house! Chase : Really? Mikal : No. Shibaev family is actually much more violent. Opening presents is like Bourbon Street Brawl. Without warning, the dark opening beats of Pink Floyd's Dogs of War assault the arena. The four combatants in the ring turn their attention to the stage, preparing for the worst. Mikal : I know that muzik! Chase : Indeed, so does everyone else in the WWA. And it looks like the DeLuccis and X-Corp are prepared to meet the interference head on. Phobos and Deimos step over the ring barrier, moving in a fluid motion to the ring apron, carrying baseball bats. Chase : They've come out of the crowd! Dave Harley is the first to notice. He turns and signals to his teammate, but Havoc has already entered the ring. CRACK! Chase : Oh no! Phobos just dropped Harley with that bat! Mikal : Yes, they are greatest tag team ever! As Harley drops to the canvas, Deimos rushes forward, swinging his bat hard into the skull of Tony DeLucci. CRACK! Chase : And now they've taken a shot at the DeLuccis! Mikal : Makes me proud. Somers and Tommy charge Havoc, both delivering simultaneous Dropkicks to the interlopers. Together, Phobos and Deimos fall to the canvas. Mal and Tommy stare at each other for a moment, unsure. That uncertainty, that distrust, proves to be their downfall, as Havoc rises. Chase : Look out! If X-Corp and the DeLuccis were getting along, none of this would be happening. Mikal : Yes, it vould. Strong people, like Havoc and Cell, vill always prey on veak. Phobos boots Tommy in the gut, doubling him over. In a deft movement, he lifts Tommy into the air, over his shoulders, in a Crucifix Slam position. Meanwhile, Deimos pulls Somers into a Powerbomb position, sitting on his shoulders. Chase : Oh no! Mikal : Oh yes! Phobos pulls Tommy end over end into a devastating DDT. Chase : The Requiem! Deimos pushes Somers up and out, catching him mid-fall with a Three-Quarter Facelock Bulldog with ruinous results. Mikal : And Panic Attack! Chase : This is just terrible. The DeLuccis earned a shot at the titles when they defeated Akiro and Motive at March Madness. Neither they, nor X-Corp, deserve the kind of treatment they've received tonight. Finally, having had enough of this, the referee calls for the bell. Phobos and Deimos exit the ring, backing away up the ramp, basking in the congealed hate of the fans. Chase : Look at those self-satisfied grins on the faces of Havoc. Mikal : Yes, they do good vork. At the top of the stage, with the four men in the ring still unmoving, Deimos draws in a deep breath. His voice, without the assistance of a microphone, echoes thunderously throughout the arena. Deimos : CRY HAVOC...! The fans become more vehement in their venomous hate, tossing cups, wadded up napkins, and half-eaten hot dogs at the pair. Content in their handiwork, Havoc departs. Chase : The ref has called this one as a no-contest, and I don't blame him one bit. We are out of time. See you next week everybody... Mikal : Or I VILL CRUSH YOU!!
