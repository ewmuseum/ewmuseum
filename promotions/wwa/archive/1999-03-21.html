WWA — 21 Mar 1999

21 Mar 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Sunday, March 21, 1999 The screen opens up to the WWA banner. The camera shows the outside of the complex, revealing it to be the Bryce Jordan Center. Yellow and red pyrotechnics explode down the ramp and out of the four ringposts. The WWA banner fades in again and is instantly stamped with the word "Extreme"! The camera fades into the arena where thousands of fans cheer loudly. As the camera pans around the Fleetcenter signs such as "Lance Crashed Bob!", "The Crusade Shall Ride!" and "Big Eliem, Who's Ya Daddy?!", are seen. The camera fades into the broadcast area where Eric Pembrook, Juan Guevesos, and John Douglass are seated. Eric : Welcome ladies and gentlemen to another episode of WWA Sunday Night Extreme! I'm your host Eric Pembrook, and with me as always are Juan Guevesos and John Douglass. Juan : Ladies and gentlemen, we are sad to announce that no tournament action will happen tonight due to some Executive problems...... John : What da dillyo, Juan? You're bringing the house down. Folks, we still got two awesome matches for you. Albatross and Coleman will have their rematch and let me tell you, Coleman is pissed and wants revenge. Eric : As our main event tonight we have Todd Logan against Jack II. This is also a revenge match as Jack II blames all HOP members for the death of Jack. John : Enough yapping people, let's get to the action.... The camera flashes to Gary Trudeau in the ring.... Gary Trudeau : The following match is schedualed for one fall with a Thirty minute time limit. Introducing first, from Vancover BC, Canada, weighing in at 260 pounds ... Albatross! "Crop Circle" by Monster Magnet plays as Albatross cooly walks down the isle, he has a hockey stick in hand. The only pyro is a few red fireworks that go off.He slides under the bottom rope and into the ring. Gary Trudeau : And his opponent. Hailing from Colordao Springs, Colorado ... weighing in at 286 pounds, "Wild Thing" Billy Coleman! The arena goes black. "Wild Thing" starts blaring over the loudspeakers, and Billy Coleman walks out on to the ramp. On his back are two baseball bats, and the look on his face looks like he is going to use them. John : Here we go.. (ding...ding...ding) Juan : Hey, look. What is Albatross doing? Eric : He's extending his hand or something. Good sportsmanship. John : Oh that was rotten! Albatross just spit in Coleman's hand! Juan : Billy didn't like that. Eric : Kick to the midsection by Coleman ... Juan : (laughing) Coleman just wiped Albatross' own spit on his face. Eric : Coleman with a beautiful drop toe hold. Right into a leg drop. Going for the cover... Juan : Not even a two. John : Coleman is pulling Albatross to his feet know, grabbing handfuls of Alby's long hair. Juan : A couple stiff punches to the midsection, and Coleman knocks Albatross back down with a short arm lariot.Coleman pulling Albatross back to his feet ... Eric : Oh! Low blow by Albatross! John : He's clearly taken over now. Albatross now with a knee drop to the chest. Coleman is really feeling the pain.. Juan : Yeah, in his nuts and his chest.. Eric : Anyway ... Albatross on the offensive now. Whip into the turnbuckle. He misses with a big splash and is dazed.. Juan : Coleman with a roll over... Eric : Barely a two.. John : Both men up starting off at the beginning again. They go in for a shoulder, neck tie up... Eric : Wait a minute! Albatross faked him out, and went up. Beautiful German Suplex! Juan : 1...2...KICKOUT! Coleman is still alive. John : Albatross pulling Coleman up to his feet. Sent for the ride ... No Reversal! Coleman with an excellent clothesline ... and Albatross is down, again. Eric : Coleman is now beating the life out of Albatross. Laying the smack down, if you will. (laughing) Coleman sending Albatross into the ropes ... OH MY GOD! Hip toss over the top rope! Albatross was just hip tossed to the outside! John : Sh*t ... he's getting up. Coleman with a cross body over the top .. right on target! Juan : Coleman is reaching for a chair. OHH! John : Coleman just laid him out again with that chair shot to the head! Eric : Coleman is uncovering the concrete now, what's he going to do now? Juan : He looks like he's going for a piledriver ... NO! John : Albatross blocked it ... and counters with a back body drop! Eric : The ref is at Eight on his ten count. Juan : Albatross rolling Coleman back into the ring. John : Meanwhile, look at Albatross going up stairs... Eric : Missile Drop Ki.... NO! Juan : Coleman counters with a drop kick of his own to Albatross' gut! Eric : Now Coleman's going up stairs. Frog Splash! 1...2....3!! John : NO WAIT! The ref says Albatross had his foot on the rope at the last second. The match goes on! Eric : This is pure Extreme! Juan : Coleman with an elbow drop. Again the cover ... John : 1...2..KICKOUT! Albatross refuses to stay down. Juan : Coleman pulling Albatross back up to his feet ... Albatross sent for the ride ... Eric : He ducks under the clothesline ... Cross Body Press! 1...2...thr ... Coleman Kicked Out! Albatross grabbing some hair and continues to drive Coleman's head into the mat! Juan : Albatross off the ropes, Leg Drop. He hooks the leg.. John : 1...2...KICKOUT! Coleman wants this! Eric : Albatross now tossing Coleman to the outside... Juan : Coleman lands face first on the concrete ... but gets back up! John : Albatross dives through the middle rope! Right on target! Juan : They flying right into the Spanish Announce table! Eric : Coleman Is limp, spread across the table. The ref at 5.. John : Oh my god! Albatross is going up stairs...! Juan : Senton right through the table! Eric : Oh my god! The table is in pieces ... lets hope these Extreme Competitors aren't.. John : The ref is at Eight ... Albatross rolls in and back out to break the count. Eric : He's reaching for his Hockey Stick ... meanwhile, Coleman is coming to.. Juan : Coleman is now grabbing hold of his baseball bat as well. Albatross swings for Coleman ... John : Coleman ducks and ... Ouch! Eric : Coleman just trust his bat into Albatross' private area! John : Coleman now beating Albatross with the bat! The ref at Seven. Eric : Coleman rolling Albatross back inside. He hooks the leg ... this could be it... Juan : KICKOUT! Just in time! Albatross up ... Powerbomb! Coleman isn't letting up! John : Coleman pulling Albatross back up to his feet .. Power Slam! Eric : Coleman pulling Alby towards the turnbuckle ... Going up top...! Juan : Moonsualt! Otherwise know as Deuces Are Wild. The cover... John : One .... Two ... Three!! Eric : Coleman has pulled off the win! Juan : What a match! Eric : What's this ... Coleman reaching for a Mic... Coleman : You thought you could burn me? How does it feel to lose to a real man? A man that does not cheat? You are a disgrace to this league Albatross. You and CPE. Coleman throws the mike in Albatross's face. Coleman climbs into the fans and disappears. Gary Trudeau : Your winner ... "Wild Thing" Billy Coleman! "Wild Thing" begins blaring over the loudspeakers once again. Meanwhile inside the ring, two EMTs are strapping Albatross to a stretcher. They slide him under the ring, and carry him off, up the ramp to the back where they have an ambulance parked. Eric : Ladies and gentlemen, the fun doesn't stop here, we're going straight into our next match.....No commercials! John : Back to you Gary for the intros.... Gary Trudeau : Ladies and gentlemen, the next match of the evening is our main event!!! It is a grudge match!!! First, defending the honor of Jack, representing the Four Horsemen of Apocalypse.....Jack II!!! Jack II walks calmly down to the ring without any special effects or music. He rolls in the ring and waits.... Gary Trudeau : And his opponent, from the depths of the HOP ranks, he is "The Hammer"....Todd Logan!!! Logan walks out with a cocky grin on his face. "Hey Man, Nice Shot" by Filter plays. He also rolls into the ring and.... Eric : Jack II is all over him from the get go! Jack just bowls him over like a rag doll. John : Jack II pulls Logan up by the hair and executes a scoop slam. Another time...this time it was a II Bomb...this early in the match? The cover....1..NO! Logan up quickly... Juan : Logan rolls out of the ring trying to avoid Jack. Jack doesn't like it and slides outside the ring delivering Todd Logan a kick to the back. John : Jack approaches Logan and Logan turns and meets Jack with a haymaker. Logan picks up the steel steps ringside and throws them on top of the downed Jack... Eric : Logan covers, which is legitimate folks, this is extreme. Logan doesn't even get a 1 count! Good God, Jack just threw Logan off of him. John : Logan looks bewildered. I think he just realized what he got himself into when he signed this match... Juan : The two tie up on the ramp and Jack II overpowers Logan. He slapjacks him right into the steel ramp. Todd's been busted open. Blood gushes from a cut above his eyebrow. Eric : He still has fight in him though, he just threw Jack's head into the guard rail. Jack is down and Logan is walking away. Juan : Jack II gets up and follows him up the ramp. Logan bends near the ramp entrance and picks something up. He turns and drills Jack with a fire extinguisher. The cover...1...2...NO!!! How the hell did he get up after that?!? John : I don't know and neither does Logan! Logan goes backstage, he's had enough.... Eric : Jack's following him....get a camera back there damn it!! Juan : Finally, here comes the feed. The wrestlers have entered the food court. Jack picks up a pan and nails Logan over the head with it. John : Logan retaliates by throwing a coffee mug all over Jack's face. Jack can't see a thing. There's an arm bar take down and Jack has no idea what is going on. Eric : Oh God, is Logan crazy?!? He's going to tilt that Coke machine so it lands right on top of the fallen Horsemen. He's going to kill Jack II just as he killed Jack! John : Hey! That was never proven!! Eric : Whatever...he tilts the machine....Oh, thank God, Jack II sensed it coming and got out of the way!! Jack II is back up! Him and Logan go back and forth until they make their way through another door. Get a camera in there!! Juan : There they are....we have visual! There in the restroom!?!? Good God man! We've seen it all! John : Jack throws Logan into a stall! Logan fall to the ground.... Eric : Holy Jesus, Jack II just ripped the sink right off the wall and now throws it right on top of Logan.... John : Logan is out.....Jack II picks Logan up and shoves his head down in a toilet... Juan : He's not letting up....is he going to kill him?!? John : Did you see what was in there before he shoved his head in that toilet?! Eric : Shut up, John! I think he's going to kill him! Somebody call Cantley and security!!! John : Jack II finally lets up. Logan is knocked out still....that didn't wake him. Jack II covers and it is easily over.....1..2..3. Juan : Jack II wins and for the second time tonight the EMT's rush to the loser of the match.... Eric : Folks, it's been short and sweet tonight, but we hope you enjoyed it. This has been your pre-Oscar countdown with the WWA. We're out of time.... John : And remember, if you're not mean, you're not extreme!!!
