SIZE DOES MATTER Gary Trudeau : Ladies and Gentlemen...now making his way to the ring... from Houston, Texas...weighing in at 390 pounds...he is the WWA World Heavyweight Champion...CRAVEN MOOOOOOOOOOOOOORE! Kraven staggers back. Moore follows up by grabbing Kraven in a headlock. Fat Tony : Now we're getting somewhere! Jimmy : Do you realize how few people can actually strap a headlock on Jackson Kraven? Moore cinches in the hold and grinds down on Kraven's head. Kraven roars and pushes Moore off, shooting him into the ring ropes. Moore tries to cover up, but the Tomorrow Man's blows find their mark. A small, but vocal, Jackson Kraven contingent in the crowd begins to count the blows. Kraven stands up. He taunts Moore daring him to get up. Moore runs a hand across his mouth and it comes back red. He looks at his own blood. Moore pushes the bloody hand through his hair and rises to his knees. Jackson Kraven charges. Moore catches him by the ankles and trips Kraven. Kraven lands throat-first across the second rope. Moore climbs to his feet. Jimmy : This is insane. These two are going to kill one another. Fat Tony : You're half right. It's all over for Moore here tonight. Moore watches as Kraven climbs once again to his feet. The pair meet in the center of the ring and begin to exchange punches. Huge fists land lefts and rights with the force of jackhammers. Both men stagger, but come right back. Moore launches a haymaker with his right fist. Kraven ducks the swing and charges Moore, taking him to the mat with a spear. Jimmy : Wow! Fat Tony : Yeah! Kraven wraps both hands around Moore's throat and chokes him unmercifully. The referee counts to 4 and Kraven breaks the hold, but only for a moment. Kraven goes right back to the chokehold, again until the 4 count. The referee admonishes Kraven for his blatant cheating. Kraven ignores him and drops an elbow across Moore's throat. Fat Tony : Moore can't win if he can't breath. Jimmy : Very methodical of the Tomorrow Man. Fat Tony : And he's just getting warmed up. Kraven grabs Moore by the hair and yanks him off the mat. He places Moore's head between his legs and lifts him up. Kraven then drives Moore into the canvas with a stiff piledriver. Moore clutches at his neck after the impact. Fat Tony : The Tomorrow Man has done his homework. He's going after that old injury. Jimmy : Sadistic doesn't begin to describe Jackson Kraven. Fat Tony : Evil genius does. Kraven dives onto Moore and locks on a rear chinlock, putting more pressure on the injured neck and throat. Moore's face quickly goes red as his oxygen supply is thinned out. The crowd is solidly behind the champion and begins to chant his name. He stretches his arm as far as he can, but can't reach the ropes. Fat Tony : Choke him out Kraven! Choke him out! Jimmy : You're an ugly little man. Kraven keeps the hold locked on. Moore's face takes on a purple tint. The referee raises his arm. It falls. The referee raises it a second time. It falls. Fat Tony : It's all over! The referee raises it a third time. This time Moore's arm stays up. The crowd erupts into cheers as the big Texan locks his arm around Kraven's neck and begins to push himself up. Kraven's eyes widen as Moore makes it all the way to his feet. Then Moore drops again, hitting a desperate and sloppy Stunner. Kraven bounces off of Moore's shoulder and hits the mat. Moore slumps down beside him, gulping air. Jimmy : What a move by the champion! Fat Tony : It smacked of desperation. Jimmy : Whatever. It worked. Both men rise slowly to their feet. Kraven grabs Moore's arm and starts to whip him toward the corner. Moore plants himself and reverses the move, whipping Kraven into the far turnbuckle. Jimmy : He almost had him. Fat Tony : Rookie mistake, not hooking the leg. Jackson Kraven rises to his hands and knees. Craven Moore grabs hold of the top rope and jumps up. He lands a hard kick to Kraven's back. He does it three more times in quick succession. The Tomorrow Man goes down again. Moore drapes Kraven's neck across the second rope and runs across the ring. He bounces off the ropes and launches himself onto Kraven's back. Jimmy : BOMBS AWAY! Fat Tony : Shades of Ray Stevens. The Tomorrow Man sags against the ropes. Moore grabs him by the back of his shirt and pulls him back. Kraven falls limply onto the mat. Moore looks at Kraven and then at the corner. He points to the corner and the crowd erupts. Moore climbs unsteadily to the top turnbuckle. The ring ropes sag. Moore points to Kraven and leaps. Jimmy : FROG SPLASH! Fat Tony : Has he ever hit this move? Nope. And he doesn't again tonight. The Tomorrow Man was faking and rolls out of the way at the last second. Moore slams onto the canvas. The crowd gasps as the World Champion lies motionless on the mat. Jimmy : This isn't good. Fat Tony : Kraven smells blood. The Tomorrow Man kicks Moore in the side of his head. Blood sprays from Moore's mouth. Kraven kicks him again, and again. Moore's head sags to the right. Kraven grabs Moore by the hair and drags the bloody champion to his feet. Then Kraven whips Moore into the ropes. Moore bounces back toward Kraven. Kraven wraps his arms around Moore and plants him with a spinebuster. Jimmy : What a move! Fat Tony : Did you see that elevation? Kraven isn't done yet though. He grabs Moore's hair again and pulls Moore to his feet. The champion is jelly-legged as Kraven whips him brutally into the corner. Moore sags against the ropes. Kraven grins maniacally and stalks across the ring. Moore throws a weak left. Kraven easily sidesteps the move and cracks Moore in the faw with a solid right. Moore sags again. Kraven raises a tree trunk sized leg and sits his boot in Moore's throat. Jimmy : No mercy from the Tomorrow Man. Fat Tony : That's as it should be. Kraven breaks the choke at the last possible moment. Then he puts his arms around Moore and somehow manages to muscle him onto the top turnbuckle. Kraven locks Moore into position and flips him backwards off the ropes with a superplex. The entire rings shakes when the two men crash to the mat. Kraven rolls onto Moore. The referee counts. 1.. 2.... KICKOUT! Jimmy : Moore's still got something left. Fat Tony : Kraven let him kick out. The crowd cheers Moore's efforts. Kraven stomps him for them. Then the Tomorrow Man drags Moore to his feet and whips him into the corner again. Moore bounces out of the corner and attempts a football tackle. Kraven sidesteps the move and Moore slams into the referee. The ref goes down. Kraven begins to pound on Moore's face again. Blood streams from the champion's face and mouth. Kraven lifts Moore from the mat and puts him in position for a powerbomb. Kraven taunts the crowd, pointing to Moore and the blood that drips onto the mat. Jimmy : He's just adding insult to injury now. This is uncalled for. Fat Tony : Shut up Jimmy. You wouldn't be complaining if the shoe was on the other foot. The house lights flicker then go out. Kraven looks around warily in the dim emergency lighting. "Where's Your Head At?' by Basement Jaxx hits the PA and the crowd rise to the feet, screaming for their superhero. The music stops and the lights rise. Jackson Kraven still has Moore's head between his legs. "Mighty" Joe stands behind them, with a large bandage wrapped around his head. The crowd go nuts. Fat Tony : What the hell is he doing here? I thought he was injured! Jimmy : He's not medically cleared for action. That's for sure Kraven drops Moore and turns to face his unknown enemy. Jimmy : But Kraven has allowed Moore to get to his feet. Kraven turns around just in time to be caught with a thunderous lariat that sends him tumbling over the top rope. Kraven falls on Mighty Joe, crushing the superhero beneath his massive frame. Fat Tony : Mighty Joe looks like the witch that the house fell on. Moore slides under the bottom rope to the outside. Jimmy : This match is about to turn ugly. Fat Tony : Well sure. Craven Moore is involved. Moore rolls Kraven off of Joe. He stands over Kraven lining up a fist. Fat Tony : This one is over, just like Moore's career. Jimmy : I don't think I've ever seen Jackson Kraven more focused, more diabolical, than he is right now.
