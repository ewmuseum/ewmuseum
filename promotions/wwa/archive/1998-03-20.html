The card prints its own header as "Friday March 20, 1998", kept above as printed. The show is dated Friday 27 March 1998 here: its Darken v. Apocalypse match is "a couple days late", after the Wednesday card of 25 March where that match broke down; Bob's Driver says "Saturday" and the card closes "See you tomorrow night", with the Saturday Night Main Event of 28 March next; the card of 25 March has the House of Pain "down to Georgia last Friday night"; and the fed's own title history dates the United States title change Johnny Blaze wins on this card 27 March 1998. Its tag-title entry for the night reads 20 March, the printed date.
