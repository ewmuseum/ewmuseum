The Scouting Report - Issue 20

24 Sep 2003

Bylined Josh Wilson - Director of Talent, in the fed's own kayfabe voice.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Greetings and salutations WWA fans, it’s your esteemed and valued Director of Talent back with all the dirt and opinions you can handle with a new Scouting Report. Well I’m afraid there’s been another gap between the columns and for that I should apologize. I just moved to a new location with my lady love and we’ve been really busy with the move and the packing. Still I’m up now and raring to go. -So Revolution has been here and gone. Man was it a show and I should comment on the matches and such. -Before that you might have noticed me on the Meltdown before the big show at the Kash/Tense contract signing. Man after the brawl that broke out there I guess you can understand why I choose to be a background player. -Also some news on Venny Letrav. He’s been suffering some bumps and bruises lately and he recently went to Omaha Memorial Hospital to get checked out. -His doctor stated that “Remy's got two bruised ribs and had a mild hematoma in the front part of his head. He's in good spirits all the same, and although we have our reservations, he's cleared to wrestle Monday. Keep an eye on him till then." -So now on to Revolution -In the first match of the night we had the four newcomers facing each other for the right at a TV Title shot. In the end though, Myzery defeated Prometheus, Tommy Starr, and Reverend Tweak to win the right. -Many thought Starr getting eliminated so early was a shock. But a better question is with Myzery being the GenX champion will he have to give up the belt if he manages to get by Joey K? Of course that’s if he gets by Joey. -Tracer sure seems to be the smarty. The three time former World Champion managed to get a title shot promise from both Ian Tense and Kash. He was fixed no matter who came up the winner in the main event. -And who knew that Tracer had such a lovely singing voice. Eminem would be proud. -The Bull Keru no Akrio managed to get the duke over Fuel in the Boiler Room Brawl. I haven’t seen one of those matches so brutal since Mick Foley and Mark Callaway. Well I have to use real names to not get sued don’t I? -That match seemed to be tailor made for Fuel what with the fire aspect and all. Maybe he should seek help for that. -Well it seems that X-Corp and Chance pulled a fast one over Eddie Van Dorn. Turns out this entire Chance against Harley thing was a ploy to get Eddie to give Rookie back to Mal Somers and boy did it work. -Now it seems X-Corp and Chance have formed an alliance with Youth United. That seems to have some of the biggest stars of the day. But man what will the name be? -Venny LeTrav faced the monster Se7en in a cage match, Se7en managed to get the win because of his new partner in crime Enigma. Still you know the resident Rajun’ Cajun isn’t going to take this defeat lying down. -So now we have a new stable featuring Enigma, Se7en and new TV Champ Joey K. These guys already like to hurt people and now they have a purpose. That’s just plain scary. -Jimmi Zion and Shaman faced each other in a Himalayan Pit Match to see who really is extreme. Zion came out over the favorite Shaman proving that Zion is at home in any situation. -And that was sure cool seeing POD play Jimmi’s new theme music live. I’ve always been a big fan of the band and they are one of the few that sounds good live. -Next Grunge and Nick Adams collided in a Submission match for the vacated United States Title. In the end, after tons of mind games, Grunge made Adams tap. Not that it was easy by any means. -And it seems that Nick faced this “test” that Grunge has been going on about. What could that mean? -Live from a Casino in Las Vegas Chance faced Enigma in a Casino match. That’s a little redundant by me isn’t it? -Enigma managed to come out on top thanks to help from Joey K and Se7en in that innovative match. -The WWA Tag-Title was defended in a King of the Hill Match, the brain child of The Cell. In the end Havoc shocked the world by becoming four time Tag Team Champions. -The end was amazing with Deimos making that suicide dive off the top of the scaffold onto Dave Harley for the win. All the men involved proved their chops on that night. -And in the Main Event of the night Kash finally made his way to the top of the mountain by ending Ian Tense’s long reign as World Champion. -Kash got the win with a Super-Kashplex off the top rope and locked in the Kashmission on the way down. That had to hurt both men. -Of course the shock on the night had to be the return of WWA owner Mark Harrington at the end of the night. One has to wonder what this means for the WWA as a whole. Well until next time this is Josh Wilson. Be sure to watch Meltdown and Fiesta. You won’t be disappointed. Ciao.
