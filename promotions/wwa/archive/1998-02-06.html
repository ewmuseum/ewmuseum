NCW Friday Night Fights — 6 February 1998

6 Feb 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
February 6, 1998 Friday Night Fights The screen opens up to the the NCW banner. Then.... Northeast Champion Wrestling flashes through the screen and leaves the NCW Banner Then firework type explosions go off around the Banner. The Banner fades... then the screen shows the Bryce Jordan Center. The screen then goes to the inside to show the fans jumping and screaming at the Camera. The camera pans over the the Complex and then the camera finds Tony Bailey and Terry Valentine sitting behind the table in the broadcast area. Tony : Welcome ladies and gentlemen to the Bryce Jordan Center on the lovely campus of Penn State in State College PA. We have a great one for you tonight folks. Terry : That's right, Tony . We have a New York City Title Match and a Northeastern Tag Team Title match. As well as other great matchups. Tony : Let's not waste anytime, here tonight. Let's go straight to Gary Trudeau in the ring for the first match introductions. The camera zooms down to the ring were we see Gary Trudeau in the center on the ring with a microphone. Trudeau : Ladies and Gentlemen... Welcome to Friday Night Fights! Our first match this evening will be set for one fall with a Ten minute time limit. Introducing first.. "Gangsta's Paradise" starts over the PA. Trudeau : Introducing first, from Los Angeles, California, weighing in at 292 lbs, we have Deadbolt. The crowd starts gives Deadbolt a mixed reaction as he walks to the ring. As Deadbolt step into the ring "Hey Man, Nice Shot" by Filter starts over the P.A.. Trudeau : And his opponent, coming down the aisle, from St Paul, Minnesota, weighing in at 239 pounds accompanied by his stablemate Aric Crockett, Zack "The Enforcer" Crockett. The crowd starts to boo as the Crocketts make their way to the ring. Zack gets into the ring. Johny : Our opening bout tonight pits Zack Crockett, the Enforcer of The House of Pain against the methodical Deadbolt of Da Killer Connection. Tony : It makes you wonder. These two factions had been side by side for awhile, with the obvious problems between them recently, this match was inevitable. Terry : Well both these men have had great starts in the fed, but have sizzled to almost nothing. Johny : This match is the first step in these two men's careers to the top. Tony : Deadbolt has the size advantage but Zack can compensate with his speed and agility. Terry : There's the bell and we are underway. Johny : Deadbolt starts things off, backing Zack into a corner. Tony : Deadbolt with a series of European uppercuts, drops Zack to the mat. Terry : The ref is trying to push Deadbolt back. Johny : Zack catches Deadbolt with a low blow behind the refs back. Tony : Zack climbs to the second turnbuckle and executes a beautiful bulldog on Deadbolt. Terry : Zack drops to his knees and unleashes a barrage of kneeling headbutts, made legendary by Terry Funk. Johny : Deadbolt rakes Zack's eyes and gets to his feet. Tony : Deadbolt doubles Zack over with a knee to the gut. Terry : Deadbolt attempts a powerbomb, but Zack counters with another low blow. Tony : That is a great countermove for Zack because besides getting out of a devestating manuever he also softens up the back of Deadbolt priming him for Zack's patented spinebuster. Johny : Zack capitalizes with 2 quick elbow drops to the small of the back, and then applies a modified Texas Cloverleaf. Tony : Deadbolt uses his power to make it to the ropes. Terry : Zack breaks the hold and waits for Deadbolt to get up. Tony : Deabdolt fakes out Zack and then nails him with a football tackle. Johny : Zack back to his feet. Deadbolt charges again. Terry : Zack catches Deadbolt and uses his momentum to drive him to the canvas with a spinebuster. Tony : Incredible. Johny : Zack hooks the leg, and grabs a handful of tights. Terry : 1-2-3. Zack wins. Tony : Deadbolt is mad he starts to go after Zack. Johny : Out of the blue comes The House of Pain. Terry : Harvester of Sorrow and Randy Cross join Aric at rindside and all storm the ring and begin to annihilate Deadbolt. Tony : Zack and Aric hold down Deadbolt while Cross applies his crippling Figure Four. Johny : Harvester grabs Cross's arms and helps give him extra leverage. Terry : Here comes security to break this all up. Terry : That was a statement from the House of Pain to Da Killer Connection. I guess it official they aren't friends anymore! Tony : Good call, Terry! Let's go to the next match. The camera zooms down to the ring were we see Gary Trudeau in the center on the ring with a microphone. Trudeau : Ladies and Gentlemen... Our next match this evening will be set for one fall with a Ten minute time limit. Introducing first, from the Frayed Ends of Sanity, we have the Harvester of Sorrow. "Harvester of Sorrow" starts over the P.A. The crowd boos as the Harvester comes out to the ring. The Harvester ignores the crowd as he walks to the ring. Metallica stops and the church bells begin. Trudeau : And his opponent, from Lakeland, Florida and weighing in at 240 pounds, we have Chris "Wild Thing" Rogers. The crowd continues to boo as Rogers comes out with his "Love" look. Rogers just walks towards the ring, ignoring the loathing fans. Then he begins to run to the ring. Johny : Wild thing hits the ring like a ball of fire and immediatly tackles Harvester to the mat. Tony : Wow. Did anyone get the number of that truck? Terry : After last match, Wild Thing better be watching his back. Tony : Wild waits til Harvester sits up and then nails his with a diving clothesline. Johny : Wild Thing in complete control, goes to the ropes and executes a beautiful in ring moonsault. Tony : Wild Thing climbs to the top. Terry : Harvester is not moving. Johny : Wild Thing with a 360 leg drop. Tony : Harvester is in deep trouble. Wild Thing chooses not to go for the pin. Terry : Wild Thing pulls Harvester to the center of the ring and climbs the ropes again. Johny : Psychedelic Splash from the top. Tony : Wild Thing hooks the legs and 1-2-3. Terry : Wild Thing gets the victory easily over Harvester. Johny : Here comes Cross and the Crockett boys. Tony : House of Pain storms the ring, and Wild Thing backs into a corner ready to take them all on. Terry : House diverts their attention from Wild Thing and begin to pummel Harvester of Sorrow. Johny : I guess they weren't done cleaning up the stables yet. Tony : Wild Thing doesn't know what is going on. He bails out of the ring in a hurry. Terry : Cross again hooks in the Figure four as Aric and Zack take turns dropping elbows on the Harvester who looks to be out cold. Tony : Somebody get security! Here they are. AGAIN, they escort the House of Pain away from ringside. Johny : Well, the House of Pain makes, yet another appearence. I guess the Harvester is no longer part of the House of Pain, either. Tony : Let's go to the ring for the next matchup. The camera zooms down to the ring were we see Gary Trudeau in the center on the ring with a microphone. Trudeau : Ladies and Gentlemen... Our next match this evening will be set for one fall with a Ten minute time limit. Introducing first from New York City and weighing in at 265 pounds, representing the Executive's Club we have The Judge. The crowd boos as The Judge makes his way to the ring. He ignores the crowd and continues on to the ring with a look of determination. "Thunderstruck" is replaced with "Cry Little Sister". Trudeau : And his opponent, from Death Valley and weighing in at 312 pounds, and one half of the CWC National Tag Team Champions we have Steihl. The crowd gives a mixed reaction to Steihl as he come from the back. He appears to be wearing gloves of some sort. Johny : What is Steihl wearing on his hands? Tony : They look like Coal Miner's Gloves! Terry : Steihl hits the ring and knocks The Judge out cold. Johny : The ref calls for the bell and dq's Steihl before the match even starts. Tony : Steihl grabs a mic. Steihl : I will make this quite simple Executive's Club. I want my staff back now. You have held the upper hand for a short time, now it is The Horsemen who deal the cards. Out walk the remaining Horseman members all dressed in their respective garb Steihl : Your time is up Executive's Club. Until you get me my staff, we will hold your Judge. Steihl reaches down and grabs the unconsious Judge and tosses him over his shoulder like a bundle of fire wood. The Horsemen of the Appocolypse leave the Ring area with the hapless Judge. Terry : Steihl has snapped. He is insane. Tony : Maybe so, but until the Overlords get their Staves back, the Judge is in dire straits. Johny : Let's go to the next match. The camera zooms down to the ring were we see Gary Trudeau in the center on the ring with a microphone. Trudeau : Ladies and Gentlemen... Our next match this evening will be set for one fall with a Twenty minute time limit. Introducing first.. "Hey Man, Nice Shot" by Filter starts over the arena. Trudeau : From St. Paul Minnesota and weighing in at 272 pounds, representing the House of Pain, we have "The Punisher" Aric Crockett. The crowd boos as Aric comes out of the back. He basks in the boos as he walks to the ring laughing at the hatred of the crowd. Trudeau : And his opponent, from parts unknown, we have the Black Scorpian. The lights go out as "Iron Man" by Black Sabbath starts up. The crowd is cheering with great enthusiasm as the laser light shows the Red Scorpian in the ring. The lights turn on and in the middle of the ring stand the Black Scorpian. Aric Crockett bails out of the ring. Johny : We haven't heard much from the Black Scorpion in the past weeks. Tony : Word has it that he has been conducting alot of business with his Fan friend. Terry : Well both men have come out alone. Johny : Let's hope it stays that way. Tony : Well after tonight's actions by the House of Pain, I have to think that Aric is ready for anything. Terry : I'm just glad The real House of Pain is back. Tony : The bell sounds and Aric charges Scorpion and shoves him back into the corner. Johny : Aric unleashes a flurry of knife edge chops. Terry : One reason Aric is underated, is because he hasn't had much chance to show everyone how agile he can be. Tony : Aric whips Scorpion into the ropes. Scorpion ducks a clothesline. Johny : Scorpion off the ropes again, nails Aric with a flying double axhandle to the chest. Tony : Aric staggers back, Scorpion hits the ropes again. Terry : Scorpion attempts a flying cross body, but Aric catches him in midair. Johny : This could be trouble for Scorpion. Tony : Aric executes a fall away slam, and quickly follows up with the STF. Terry : The STF works on both the arms, neck, and back of an opponent. Johny : Well the word back in the locker room is that Aric has taken on a new finisher which he calls The Punisher, which is a modified Brazilian armlock. Tony : And look who's coming down, everyone's favorite cowboy. Outlaw. Terry : Is he here to stir up trouble with house of Pain? Or just to annoy Scorpion? Johny : I'd have to guess the latter of the two. Tony : Scorpion gets to the ropes and Aric breaks the hold at the refs 3. Terry : Aric whips Scorpion in and attempts an enzuiguiri kick, but Scorpion hooks the ropes. Johny : Scorpion pulls Aric up and ducks behind him. Tony : It could be Equalizer time for Aric. Terry : No look the Outlaw is screaming at Scorpion. Johny : Scorpion better worry about Aric in the ring. Terry : Too late! Aric catches Scorpion in a jaw breaker and applies that Brazilian armlock submission. Tony : "The Punisher" is applied and Scorpion taps out. Johny : The Outlaw leaves the ringside area with a smile on his face, and Aric leaves with a victory. Terry : Aric Crockett puts the hurt on the Black bug! Johny : Very impressive from the "Punisher" Aric Crockertt. That takes care of the Black Scorpian's win streak. Tony : Let's go to the ring for the next match. The camera zooms down to the ring were we see Gary Trudeau in the center on the ring with a microphone. Trudeau : Ladies and Gentlemen... Our next match this evening will be set for one fall with a Twenty minute time limit. Introducing first.. "Body Count" starts over the Arena. Trudeau : From Dupont, PA weighing in at 327 pounds, we have Big Eliem. Trudeau : And his opponent, from Chicago, Illinois and weighing in at 240 pounds, we have "Brutal" Brian Cario. The fans give a mixed reaction to the brutal one. More cheers than boos, but mixed. Cairo is looking his same old pissed off self. Tony : Well as you can see by Brian's attire, he has cut all ties with Triple Threat, and has issued challenges to his former stablemates in recent weeks. Terry : It's a shame. Johny : That he left Triple Threat? Terry : No, that the beautiful girl he left with last time isn't here. Tony : There's the bell and Big Eliem immediatly starts the match by wrapping his hands around Brian's throat and backing him into the corner. Johny : He breaks the choke at 4 and reapplies it, Eliem obviously doesn't want Brian to get any early momentum. Terry : It looks like one of our cameramen is having trouble on the outside, so we apologize to you fans watching at home if we lose video for a moment. Tony : Eliem whips Brian across the ring and Brian baseball slides under the ropes, avoiding any contact with the corner. Johny : Smart move. I am impressed with this new attitude of Brian. Maybe Triple Threat was holding him back so that Mark Lynch could reign supreme. Terry : Brian rolls back in and - Screen fades to black and begins to cut in and out on the action. We see bits and pieces of the match where Eliem is regaining control of the match and devestating Brian with powerbomb after powerbomb. Tony : Are we back? Terry : I think so. Johny : Once again fans, we apologize for the technical difficulties. The cameramen have gotten things under control. Tony : To bring you up to speed, Eliem has taken complete control of this match. Terry : Eliem whips Brian into the ropes, Brian reverses it and Eliem flies out of the ring over the top rope. Johny : A cameraman goes in for an extreme close-up. Meanwhile Brian has the ref distracted. Tony : The cameraman just nailed Eliem with the camera! Terry : What the hell is going on? Johny : The ref turns around and starts the count on Eliem. Tony : Meanwhile the cameraman is removing his outfit. Terry : That's no cameraman! That's that babe Brian left with! Johny : Eliem has been counted out! Tony : Brian wins and he has the mic. The beautiful woman joins Brian in the ring. Brian : Mark Lynch. YOU want an answer from ME? Well........I have a question for you. Are YOU man enough to step in the ring with ME?? Better yet, how about next week, a scafold match. You and Drake, vs. myself and a mystery partner. Drake, we are not on bad terms, but i need to prove myself. Are you man enough Lynch?? Friday the 13th, DONT MESS. Brian throws down the mike and leaves the ring. Johny : There you have it! Brian Cairo is no longer with the UKA! Tony : Let's go to the next match.. The camera zooms down to the ring were we see Gary Trudeau in the center on the ring with a microphone. Trudeau : Ladies and Gentlemen... Our next match this evening will be set for one fall with a Twenty minute time limit. It is a non-Title match. Introducing first.. "Unforgiven" by Metallica starts over the arena. Trudeau : From parts unknown and weighing in at 320 pounds, and one half of the CWC National Tag Team Champions we have Darken. The crowd gives a positive reaction as Darken strides to the ring. He remains focused on the ring and the matter at hand. "Thunderstuck" by ACDC starts over the PA. Trudeau : And his opponent, from Houston, texas and weighing in at 225 pounds, the regning New England Heavyweight Champion, we have the Outlaw. The crowd begins to boos as the Outlaw makes his way to the ring. The Outlaw is yelling back at the crowd and waving what appears to be Darken's Staff. Johny : Outlaw puts the staff down on the ground and turns his back on Darken to remove his belt. Tony : Darken rushes for the sneak attack, but Outlaw was waiting for him. Terry : Outlaw leaps into the air before Darken can put on the brakes, and catches Darken with a backspin kick to the head. Johny : Outlaw throws his belt to the ring attendant and spins to face Darken. Tony : Darken leaps from the mat and tackles Outlaw with a vicious flying clothesline. Terry : Darken pounds away at Outlaw's head. He just reopened the wound from Outlaw's match with Blaze. Johny : Wow look at the blood flow. Tony : Darken capitalizes with a piledriver. Terry : You got to wonder if the ref will stop this match, after all it's nontitle Darken has nothing to gain. Johny : Nothing to gain? How about a victory over the current title holder? Tony : Outlaw is too proud to give up this soon, but you know that if this match goes too long he might not have the choice. Terry : Darken applies a front facelock, and goes for a suplex. Johny : Outlaw blocks it and goes for a brain buster. Tony : Darken re-reverses it and nails Outlaw with a brain buster. Outlaw hits hard on his head. Terry : The blood has made it's way to the eyes of Outlaw. He's blinded. Johny : Darken picks him up and goes for the powerbomb. Tony : Outlaw blocks it and counters with a backdrop. Terry : Outlaw delivers an axe kick to the back of Darken's head. Tony : Outlaw collapses! Outlaw collapses! Johny : The ref calls for the bell, and the medics. Terry : Darken is furious, he grabs his staff and he storms out of the ring. Terry : I didn't expect this match to end like that. Tony Darken beats the champ. Let's go to the next match. The camera zooms down to the ring were we see Gary Trudeau in the center on the ring with a microphone. Trudeau : Ladies and Gentlemen... Our next match this evening will be set for one fall with a Thirty minute time limit. It will be for the Northeast Tag Team Titles. Introducing first.. The Marine's Hymn starts up over the PA. Trudeau : From the Tun Tavern in Philadelphia PA weighing in at a total combined weight of 476 pounds we have the Commandant's Own. The crowd cheers as the two Marines march to the ring in full dress uniform. The two wrestlers take off the uniforms and enter the ring. "Money Talks" by ACDC starts over the PA. Trudeau : And their opponents, weighing in at a total of 536 pounds we have the current Northeast Tag Team champions, the Executive's Club. The crows boos as the Executive and Mr Business make their way to the ring. Mr Business makes a breaking motion with his hands, obviously directed towards the two men in the ring. Johny : It's going to be Major Havok startig things off against The Executive. Tony : Well you have to wonder if Executive is at 100% after his last battle. Terry : Well I talked with The Outlaw earlier and he assured me that Executive is fine. Johny : Time will tell. There's the bell. Tony : Executive catches Havok off guard with a dropkick. Terry : Did you see the height that Executive got? Johny : Executive continues his momentum sending Havok into the Executive's Club's corner. Tony : Mr. Business holds Havok while Executive charges the corner with a clothesline. Terry : Executive snap suplexes Havok to the mat and tags in Business. Johny : Business hits the ring and nails Havok with a running forearm smash out of the ring. Tony : That knocked Havok for a loop. Havok climbs onto the apron and Business catches him with a hard punch to the face. Terry : Great defensive ploy by Business, if he can't get back into the ring then they can't win. Johny : Havok ducks and buries a shoulder in Business's midsection, and another. Tony : Havok slides beneath the bottom rope and between the legs of Business. Terry : Havok traps Business in a back waistlock, and executes a beautiful belly to back suplex. Johny : Havok crawls to his corner and tags in Payne. Tony : Business, back to his feet, is caught in a German suplex. Terry : Payne follows up with 3 quick elbowdrops. Tony : Business back to his feet. Payne charges. Terry : Business catches Payne in a choke hold, and executes a brutal choke slam. Johny : Executive is yelling to Business to do something. Tony : Business nods and taunts Havok. Havok comes in but the ref cuts him off. Terry : Business picks up Payne and holds him while Executive takes something out of his tights. Johny : It's a bag of salt. Executive thros it at Payne. Payne breaks free and the salt nails Business in the eyes. Tony : The ref turns around and doesn't have a clue. Terry : Payne takes Business to the mat with a side suplex. Johny : Executive comes into the ring and is caught in a belly to belly suplex. Tony : Executive rolls out of the ring. Payne executes his patented Air Assault, and tags in Havok. Terry : Havok goes to the top and nails Business with Terror From Above and goes for the pin. Johny : Executive comes in to break the pin but Payne knocks him through the ropes with a tremendous dropkick. Tony : 1-2-3. The Commadants Own pull off the upset victory. We have new Champions in the Northeast! The Commandan't Own take the titles! Terry : They cheated! They cheated. Johny : No one is listing to you, Terry! Let's go see the next match. The camera zooms down to the ring were we see Gary Trudeau in the center on the ring with a microphone. Trudeau : Ladies and Gentlemen... Our next match this evening will be set for one fall with a Twenty minute time limit. It will be for the New York City Title. Introducing first.. "Who Do You Love?" by George Throughgood and the Destroyers starts over the arena. Trudeau : The Challenger, from New York, New York weighing in at 300 pounds, we have Johny Dice. The crowd boos as Johny Dice makes his way to the ring. Dice is carrying what appear to be a snow board. Dice gets into the ring and does a little pose for the crowd. "Antichrist Superstar" by Marilyn Manson starts over the PA. Trudeau : And the Champion, from New York City weighing in at 252 pounds, the current New York City Champion, Angel Reaper. The crowd cheers as Angel Reaper walks to the ring with a look of determination. Johny : Dice has worked his way through the ranks and has earned this shot against Angel Reaper for the NYC title. Tony : I have to say the odds are in Angel’s favor. Any man who can withstand a match of the Triple Threat magnitude has to have the heart of a true champion. Terry : I have to disagree. Angel took a beating at that match, and can not possibly be at 100% whereas Dice is fresh and bloodthirsty. Johny : There’s the bell and we are under way. Tony : Angel and Dice circle each other like vultures, in the center of the ring. Terry : They lock in a collar and shoulder lock up. Dice gets the advantage and applies a side headlock. Johny : Angel backs Dice into the ropes, and pushes him off into the ropes. Tony : Angel ducks for a backdrop. Terry : Dice counters with a sunset flip, but he can’t get Angel over. Johny : Angel drops down with a sit down, but Dice rolls out of the way. Tony : Dice whips Angel into the corner, and follows him in with an avalanche. Terry : Angel sidesteps, and catches Dice on the rebound with a rolling reverse cradle. Johny : Dice bridges out at 2, and turns it into a backslide. Tony : Angel’s shoulders are put down to the mat. Angel rolls out at 2. Terry : Dice rushes Angel with a clothesline. Tony : Angel ducks but Dice anticipated it. Dice executes an explosive spinning piledriver. Johny : The crowd is turning to the ramp. 2 guys are coming down the ramp. Terry : I don't recognize them. Johny, you know everything there is about wrestling who are these guys? Johny : Those two guys are Eric Smart and Hardcore Legend, one of the top rated tag teams in the GRW division of CWC. Tony : Angel Reaper has been asked to commentate on their upcoming match. Terry : So what business do they have here? Johny : Maybe they're checking out the competition here in NCW. Tony : Or maybe they're up to no good. Terry : Dice sees the men and Eric Smart starts taunting him. Johny : Dice rolls out of the ring and starts after Smart. Tony : Smart retreats to the locker room, but look Hardcore has the attention of the ref. Terry : Angel Reaper comes up from behind Dice and nails him with his own snowboard. Johny : Hardcore drops off the apron and leaves the ringside area, while Angel throws Dice back in. Tony : Angel locks on his patented Angelic Redemption. Terry : Dice taps out and Angel gets the victory. Johny : You have to give the assist to Eric Smart and the Hardcore Legend. Tony : You know that the commisioner has been watching this very close, and Dice will definitly get another shot at Reaper and the NYC Title. Terry : We're out of time.
