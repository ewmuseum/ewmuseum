Meltdown Preview - April 24, 2006

24 April 2006

What this is A Meltdown preview and card for Monday 24th April 2006 live from Atlanta, two shows before Bourbon Street Brawl, with tag and television title matches.
Live from Philips Arena Atlanta, GA Monday, 24th April 2006 After one of the most shocking Meltdowns in recent history, we are only one show away from Bourbon Street Brawl. So Atlanta, Georgia better be ready for one non-stop roller coaster of a Meltdown. First up is Mick Vain taking on the Masked Enigma. Vain hasn’t been doing well lately but a victory here would get him back on track. But a loss would mean that the rookie has scored a huge win over a WWA veteran. Another debut on Meltdown will be Kindred taking on the team of KaOs. Both teams are looking to get noticed in the tag division and a win for either would step them further up the ladder. The Television Championship will be on the line this week when Danny Vista makes his second defense, this time against the number one contender Kyle James. These are two rookies that have made a lot of waves so far in the WWA and now they’ll be in the spotlight in this big match. The Tag Titles on the line also, with the Franchise Players making their first defense. They’ll battle the team that beat them two weeks, S.O.S. The team of Jorge Samuelsson and Shaman has been steamrolling through the WWA and with Denrol’s injury, it could be very rough for the champions. In our Main Event, the very angry Jonathan Winters will warm-up for his title shot against Khaled at BSB by facing the former World Champion Dave Harley. Okay, so maybe it’ll be much more then a warm-up match. Harley is a man of a mission and Winters is determined himself. This will be the first time these two have met in the ring and thus it’ll be one for the ages. We also will witness the aftermath of last week’s Meltdown. Damian Thorne is World Heavyweight Champion, Vik Ventressa has gone over the deep end, and a massive protest took place to end the show. The fallout from these events will make this a can’t miss Meltdown, just two weeks away from Bourbon Street Brawl. You’ll regret it if you don’t see it. Main Event Jonathan Winters Vs.
