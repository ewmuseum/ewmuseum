The Scouting Report - Issue 24

24 Sep 2003

Bylined Josh Wilson - Director of Talent, in the fed's own kayfabe voice.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Greetings and salutations WWA fans, it’s your esteemed and valued Director of Talent back with all the dirt and opinions you can handle with a new Scouting Report. -So the WWA has just had its first show of the new roster split and I must say so far it’s been a resounding success. We’ll discuss this more in detail later. - Venny LeTrav is returning from paternity leave with high hopes and of course, revamp of the Ragin Cajun style. He's been having problems with a pinched nerve in his left hip as well that he's been recovering from, but he is working house shows and has been receiving explosive results from the crowd. -"Back to basics for me. We(The WWA) have so many new faces that I'm gonna have to watch every show even closer just to keep up with everyone else, but I don't mind. It's the funner part of the business." -I always have some news about the World Title match from Truth or Consequences thanks to EWA President Andy Seeley. During the WWA title match, a blown spot between Kash and Chance resulted in a nasty bump, but luckily there were no injuries. Both men are saying it was their fault. Luckily nobody has any hard feelings from it. To be honest these things happen and neither should take it so hard. -Speaking of Kash, the World Champion is reportedly very unhappy about this Ninja situation, to the point where he has contacted some sources in certain occult circles to put the matter to an end. I had to go into his locker-room to get the word from him. Never going there again. -But now on to Fiesta shall we. -The first act of the night had to do with a very big announcement. We all know that Meltdown will have two titles exclusive to their brand with the World Champion able to appear on either show. Fiesta is currently lacking a primary champion which is why the Intercontinental Championship was created. There will be a tournament held on Fiesta that will involve the entire roster. The only exception is that Enigma and Chance has both received byes in the first round. -The first match of the night was not doubt a exciting affair for both of its contestants. Matt Keith and Alex Knight were both recently called up to the WWA from the EWA. For their first WWA show they both excelled with Keith getting the cheat win. I expect to see a lot more for these rookies but their first big test will be in the tournament. -The next match had CPE: Fiesta member Jimmi Zion taking on Keru no Akiro. This was a surprising new look for Keru no Akiro, and a new, more vicious attitude. His match with Zion was a real surprise to certain people, as was the ending with Fuel and Zion offering Akiro a place in CPE. Times are changing for Keru no Akiro. -As the new GenX Champion Dave Harley promised to put the extreme back in the title and that he did in his first title defense on Fiesta. Of course he did have his work cut out for him not only facing James Curzon, sorry Bonafide, but also having to worry about Joey K as the special guest referee. Luckily Joey got his comeuppance when Fate came down and paid him back for sneaking a peak at her in the shower. Maybe Joey thought it was worth it. -Finally in the main event of the night we had Enigma and Wes Sanders facing off against Chance and Mal Somers. It had been a busy night for Enigma as he had called out Kash for the next Fiesta and had taken out Jimmi Zion. Of course the match ended with Mal and Chance winning because of a disqualification but oh the price Mal paid as Wes gave him a frog splash with Rookie giving Mal a busted head and Rookie a dent. -But the surprise of the night came from the cue that Assistant Commissioner Troy Steffy is planning for Fiesta. Aside from making Commissioner Raine late for the event he took Raine out at the end of the night with the help of the returning American Eagle. One has to wonder what Steffy promised Eagle is exchange for his help. -But this Monday Meltdown has it’s first show of the roster split. -In the first match of the night we have a special “Graduate Match.” Steven Murphy, Tommy Starr, and The Chuckster all know each other well from their EWA days and now get the chance to rekindle old hatreds in this submission elimination rules match. Of course it looks bad for Chuckster considering Starr and Murphy are both members of the Devil’s Own. Let’s hope Cold Pain Enterprises are standing by for Chuckster. -Next we have Eddie Van Dorn taking on Shaman. Both claim to be the epitome of the extreme and both have legitimate claims to that title. Of course here on Meltdown the match will be competed under normal wrestling rules. Course these two have never been the type to listen to the refs before, unless it’s a three count that is. -I suppose it’s really going to be a busy night for CPE. Their leader David “Sniper” Wattsoner has the chance to yank away the stranglehold Lockdown has on the TV Title. This is no easy feat however. One has to wonder how long it will be before the rest of the Devil’s Own and the CPE get involved in the match. -And in the main event of the match the hatred is so thick you could cut it with a knife. We already know that Leviathan and Grunge hate each other to no end. Now add Damian Thorne and Nick Adams to the mix and we have a match that’s ready to explode. Many have given Adams and Grunge the edge as they have tagged together before. Thorne and Levi are practically strangers. Still their hatred may make them work together faster. Well until next time this is Josh Wilson. Be sure to watch Meltdown and Fiesta. You won’t be disappointed. Ciao.
