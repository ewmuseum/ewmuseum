What this is Full show script of the 2010 WWA: Underground webcast, episode 107, from Calihan Hall in Detroit, with a contract signing, a Kash versus Harley match and a no-finishers WWA Championship main event.
WWA: Underground February 26, 2010 - Episode #107 Live from the Calihan Hall in Detroit, MI Chapter I Adams Promo The lights start to strobe and the fans begin to cheer as the music hits. DON'T SAVE ME, DON'T SAVE ME, 'CAUSE I DON'T CARE! Nicholas Adams walks out to the ring, this time flanked by the two security guards that seem to be shadowing him everywhere he goes. Nick has the WWA Championship on his left shoulder. He walks down to the aisle and asks for a microphone. Adams: So tonight, I've got a title match against Intrepid. The fans all boo at the mention of the man's name. Adams: Yeah, I know, I know. Now, he seems to think that something that happened about three years ago will have any impact upon what happens tonight. I gotta' say, that seems to be a real big dose of cognitive dissonance. He's been on a losing streak, whereas I happen to still be holding this belt. I accepted Intrepid's challenge to prove that I am in fact a fighting champion. No more, no less. In fact... Suddenly, the lights go completely dark. For a moment, the fans murmur among themselves. Adams: We've seen this before. Who do you think is coming out to attack me? Any guesses? When the lights return, they are accompanied by Queen's I Want It All, and Ry Ballard with a microphone, standing in the ring, just one foot from Nick's face. The fans boo. Adams: Well, that's new. I guess the WWA just couldn't pay the electric bill this time. I knew we didn't have the budget that we used to, but this is ridiculous. Ry: Stop ignoring me! I'm right here in front of you! Adams turns and paces around the ring a little. Ry follows him, but to no avail. Adams: As I was saying, I'm here tonight to prove that I'm a fighting champion. In fact .... Paint it Black by the Rolling Stones echoes throughout Calihan Hall. Jack: We know that music. But what is Lucas Black doing out here? Denrol: After his win last week over Jack Griffiths, Lucas is the official number one contender to the World title. Maybe he just wants to talk about the terms of Nick's surrender. Jack: Like that would ever happen. Lucas Black strolls to the ring in black jeans and a black button-up shirt. Tiffani Andrews falls into stride a step behind him. Ry: Now you're in for it. See? You can't just keep ignoring me. My good friend Lucas is here to make you pay attention. Jack: It would seem that Ry is claiming responsibility for Lucas Black's appearance here. Lucas enters the ring, microphone in hand, while Tiffani waits outside. Denrol: Staying outside of the ring is probably a good idea for Tiffani. This could be an explosive situation. Lucas walks directly to Nick, sparing only a moment's glance at Ry. He waits for a moment while the boos continue. Ry stands by with a cocky smirk, eager to see the fists fly. When the boos finally subside, Lucas raises the microphone and speaks plainly. Lucas: I'm not finished with you, Nicholas Adams. I'm not done making you suffer. I want a rematch. Jack: The fans don't care for Lucas Black, but they like the idea of a rematch. Ry: Woah, hold up there. I'm the one looking for a match with Nick. He insulted me by ignor-- Nick: I've heard of sore losers before, but a sore winner? You've got a match with me next week anyway, lest you forget that I'm the WWA Champion. Ry stomps his foot. Lucas: There's a potential problem there, Nicholas. If you happen to lose that shiny belt before I get a shot at it, then my number one contender status won't mean anything in getting another match with you. Nick nods and ponders for a moment. Ry: So then you move on. You take the title from whoever has it that time and you move on. That's how it's done. But now let's talk about me. You see, I'm the one who needs a match with-- Nick: You really want to take me on that badly? What, an undefeated record, the obvious victory over Intrepid should I fail tonight, and this belt on my shoulder, aren't enough for you? Adams turns to look at Tiffani. Nick: Are you really sure he shouldn't still be in that mental hospital? Ry kicks the ropes, his face contorting with anger. Jack: Ry Ballard is really getting worked up here. Denrol: I would be too if someone were ignoring me so blatantly. Ry's voice grows louder, nearly shouting now. Ry: Dammit, I'm the greatest thing that the WWA has ever seen! I'm the founder of Proof! I wrote the greatest book on tag team wrestling in the history of the sport! I'm a former World Champion myself – the former part being through no fault of my own – and I make every member of the Ultimate Glory match look like crippled old women, especially you, Nick! Because that's all you are! That's all every single one of those worthless, talentless jobbers in the Ultimate Glory match were – crippled old women – in comparison to the greatness that is The One Man Show! Denrol: Um, wasn't Lucas Black in the Ultimate Glory match? Jack: Uh, yes, he was. But so was Nick Adams. I imagine that this is Ry's attempt to insult Nick, but he kind of got Black with it, too. Nick gives no reaction to Ry's rant. Nick: I'm gonna' have to think this over, man. You see, I'm just not sure when I could squeeze you in. Nick: You seem to have misunderstood a crucial part of the "think it over" idea. Besides, that's not quite necessary. Ry: Yes, it is necessary! Hit him! Hit him! Lucas stands with the chair in hand, his eyes narrowed at Nick. Nick: I'd really reconsider if I were you, Mister Black. I don't respond well to - how did George Lucas put it? Aggressive negotiations. Ry: Take him out, Lucas! Take him out! Do as I say! Lucas turns suddenly, bending the steel chair nearly in half over Ry Ballard's skull. CRAXXT! Jack: What the--? Did Lucas Black just take out Ry Ballard? Denrol: He did indeed. Ballard inadvertently insulted Lucas with the Ultimate Glory reference, and it appears that Lucas Black is not a forgiving man. The fans cheer, not so much for Lucas taking Ballard out, but just for the fact that someone finally shut Ballard's mouth. Meanwhile, Nick has bailed out of the ring, microphone still in hand. Nick: Whoa, whoa, whoa - swinging a chair around in the air is definitely not a way to get what you want. I mean, really? Are you five or something? If you don't get what you want, you throw a temper tantrum? I know that isn't you. Everybody knows that isn't you. You're a better competitor than that. You're a better man than that. Lucas pauses, his brow creased. Conflict flashes across his face like a thunder storm. Nick: You don't have to follow this path, Lucas. You don't have to be feared and hated. You have the skills and talent to stand for something greater. You have the heart of a true champion. More than that, I think you have the potential to be a hero to these fans. Lucas looks surprised when the fans cheer for the idea. Tiffani shakes her head, unwilling to accept the waver in Black's eyes. Nick: Come on, Lucas.You could be an inspiration to people all over the world just by putting away your past and choosing a brighter future. We all deserve second chances, and you have a chance to show us all what can be accomplished. Jack: Are we witnessing a change in attitude for Lucas Black? Is he turning over a new leaf? Can The Rainmaker get through to him? Nick starts to step back towards the ring, and Black just stands there, staring at Adams. Lucas and Adams exchange a look for a long while. Black's eyes drift to Tiffani, who shakes her head no. He looks out at the fans, who cheer for him, urging him put down the chair. Finally, he drops the chair to the canvas next to him and turns to Nick. Jack: He's really going to do it! He's going to join forces with Nick Adams! Lucas motions for Nick to step back in to the ring. Adams does so, and extends his hand. Black looks like he's about to go for a handshake, but he instead goes to boot Adams in the gut! Jack: Adams caught it! Denrol: At least we know where Black really stands. Jack: On one foot, for the moment. Black just glares at Nick, and Adams glares right back. Nick shoves Lucas' foot, and Black simply steps away. The security detail has gone to check on Ry Ballard, so they aren't really paying that much attention. Adams just shakes his head, picks up the microphone, and rolls out of the ring again. Nick: I really expected more of you. You'll have your match - I'll let you know later when it is. Adams waves to the security detail, who shrug and turn to join Adams as he heads backstage. Jack: You know, for a second, I thought that Lucas was going to turn over a new leaf. Denrol: Yeah. And the Rolling Stones will suddenly get an outlook on life that speaks about teddy bears and unicorns. Jack: Still, seeing as he attacked both the champion and Ry Ballard, whose side is he on? Denrol: Nobody's side but his own, apparently. Lucas and Tiffani calmly walk away from the ring, disappearing into the back with smiles on their faces.
