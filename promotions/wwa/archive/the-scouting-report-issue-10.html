The Scouting Report - Issue 10

28 Nov 2003

Bylined Joe Stock - Director of Talent, in the fed's own kayfabe voice.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Welcome to the tenth TSR and the first of a new era, the PTC era. I am not exactly sure what the details of our new contract is, all I know is we are back on TV. To celebrate the new beginning, I decided to make this a special edition of TSR. Along with the normal news and rumors, I'm going to give a state of the WWA report, detailing my thoughts on each talent currently under contract. On to the first half of the report. -The entire WWA is thrilled with the news of our new contract. I'm told Matt Warr himself celebrated by going to Disneyworld for two days. Sadly a number of WWA fans saw him on the line for Space Mountain and decides to start throwing spitballs at our President. He chased after them, but was stopped and tackled by an employee dressed in a Donald Duck costume. As you can guess, he has returned back to our offices in a very bad mood. -Big stuff is in the works for this Monday. Expect a number of surprises and the beginning of angles that will lead straight to Rage in the Cage. I'll have my thoughts on the matches planned a little bit later. -A number of WWA wrestlers got hurt on recent house shows, but none too seriously. -It's been decided that the Horsemen stable will just shut down for the moment and Shaman will show off a new look very soon. I think the veteran has a lot to give to this federation. -I've heard a number of complaints about our World Champion Ian Tense and the giant ego he has grown since winning that belt. I think Ian needs to watch himself, because the WWA will not let his Triple H attitude run wild for long. -I'll admit that there is a current staff argument going on about a segment on Meltdown, but I think all the parties involved want what's best for the federation and it will be resolved by Monday. -The WWA is looking at a number of wrestlers to bring into the fold and we've even made direct offers to two men. I hope to hear from them soon. -The lineup for Meltdown looks good, let's run through quickly. -Somers and Maloy have been fighting quite a bit on the House Show circuit and are pretty in tune with each other, so this match should be nice and polished. I expect Somers to come out with his gold intact; through the strap stipulation changes things. -Chance Vs Enigma promises to be a great match. The two did well at Birthday Bash and I'm sure they can do it again. I see Chance retaining. -I'm iffy on the Youth United Vs HOP Match, mainly because of the lack of Damian Thorne. The Human Condition has not impressed me since their return and Shane Dorion also seems at times to be a question mark. Hopefully Youth United can carry this. Youth United for the W. -The American Luchadores have something to prove and it's up to them to prevent Havoc from becoming three-time tag team champions. I think Bourne and LeTrav have a lot of potential, but it's wasted in the tag team ranks. But I do expect them to win. -The main event should rock the house. Sanders and Tense are no strangers to each other and they're practicing the match on the house show circuit. I think the WWA should really give Tense a chance to run with the ball and I expect him to retain this Monday. -Now to my "State of the WWA" Report. Ian Tense: I'll start with our World Champion. He proved to be quite a shocker and I expect him to be the Rookie of the Year. But the easy part is winning the World Title; the hard part is keeping it. Just ask Mark Lynch, Darken, Denrol, Mantis, or Shaman. Sanders will be his first true test. Wes Sanders: The Rage gimmick was a complete failure, but that didn't hold Sanders down. He's finally earned his spot and has become a possible main event player. All he has to do to show he is the real deal is a successful feud with Tense. He doesn't have to win the gold, but he has to prove he can draw. Tracer Bullet: The Three-Time World Champion is suffering through a rough period in his career. It looked like he would bounce back from the Trent LeBeaux period, but mediocre showings at Labor Day Brawl and Birthday Bash destroyed all the momentum he had. He has to do something fresh or he will become yesterday's news. Jackson Kraven: His program with Denrol for Rage in the Cage is a moneymaker, but one must wonder where Kraven will go from there. He's doesn't have much of a history with Sanders or Tense and thus will need to find another way to the World Title than a simple blood fued. That is assuming of course he wins at RITC. A loss to Denrol could toss Kraven to the bottom of the World Title divison. Denrol: The 5-Star Champion has not held a belt since March. He's been in contendership for the World belt twice, but failed both times to score the victory. The "WOOO! Eh?" chops were getting him some good face heat, but the neck injury was a major minus. He gives him a good program for Kraven, but the future of Denrol is in question. Damian Thorne: His victory over Randy Cross may have really help this man's career, but now the going is going to be much rougher. It takes a certain something to be a main eventer in the WWA and the jury is still out on Thorne. On the other hand, his control of HOP gives him a big stick to wield. Kash: Another rookie who've proven himself quickly. Kash is certainly one of a kind, but whether he can hang in the US Division is the big question. If he can have a couple of good feuds, he could find himself in the World Title division very quickly. Shane Dorian: I always wonder about this guy. Sometimes he shows he can be a great WWA susperstar, but than he stops putting in an effort. His unreliability has only hurt him and he needs to decide if he really wants to be on top. If he does, he needs to buckle down and work at it. Shaman: The living legend seems to have decided he wants to reinvent himself. A lot of people underestimate this guy, but he has always been the rock of the WWA. You know he's always going to be around, so he can be trusted with feuds and angles. I would not be surprised if he wins gold very soon. Eddie van Dorn: EVD's career has hit a big slump and how he will get out of it will be something we all will be watching. He is able to wrestle the big guys, but there almost seems like he has hit his personal ceiling. I think creating Youth United was a step back for Eddie, because he works so much better as his own man. American Luchaodres: The quality of this tag team is amazing, but it's a very open question how well they can do together. Personally I would rather see Bourne and LeTrav facing each other and not working together, but they have decided this is their future and so good luck to them. Havoc: I was happy to hear there are no plans to break up this team. With the tag team division in the shambles, this has been the one team that has kept it going. They deserve the title belts, but for the moment I think they would work better chasing the gold and not being the champs. Chance: This rookie has proven himself very quickly. I think he has a chance to really reinvent the Extreme division. He's got the right attitude and the talent there is unbelieveable. I'm betting on this rising star. EkoSyde: He's the Cruiserweight Champion, but his feud with Grunge have left question marks about how dominant he can be. He can have some great matches, but his first feud I thought was a complete failure. Hopefully he can make the right choices and head straight to the top. Grunge & Rick Venom: They are technically a tag team, but it's clear to everyone who has the future here. Grunge can be one great superstar, but I think Venom is a serious handicap. Plus the fact they can't seem to decide if they are a tag team or singles. Christyan Payne: So much talent, the Horsemen really put a damper on his career. I think he can bounce back, but it'll take some hard work. His matches on the house show circuit have been impressive, but we've always known he can do it inside the ring. Nicholas Adams: A superstar that has done better than I thought he would. That he managed to retain that TV Title at Birthday Bash is quite a feat, but the TV Title is the toughest belts to hold for a long time. This reign could shoot him to the top and I think he can do it. Texas Longhorns: I really think this team is only still around because of our shortage. They rarely make a real effort and have not looked even remotely impressive in any of their matches. The joke that they are old and fat is over, they need to become contenders or they need to go. Mack Tenn: I think people are underestimating this team. The talent is there, they just need to show it. Here's hoping they score a victory very soon, they deserve it. They don't deserve to be held down by the Longhorns. Mal Somers: The Rookie champion does have a future, but he still is very green. The change from the EWA to the WWA has affected him, but I think he's starting to understand the WWA. I see him in TV Title land very soon. Mac Maloy: I'm not sold on his Truck gimmick, but if it works, it will work. The next few weeks are vital for him; it will decide whether or not he has a future here in the WWA. Dave Harley: Hasn't quite found his place yet, but I think he can do it. He needs to get into the Rookie Title hunt, I think winning that belt should be his number one goal right now in the WWA. Jimmi Zion: He has a storied past in the WWA and I think he will add a lot to the Cruiserweight Division. He should be debuting very soon, watch for this guy. That's all for now, I'll see you next week. Happy Thanksgiving from under the New York Yankees baseball cap.
