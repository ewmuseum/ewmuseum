WWA Retribution 2010 (March 21, 2010)

21 March 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is Full script of the WWA’s Retribution 2010 pay-per-view from Providence, Rhode Island, with a prologue hardcore triple threat, Extreme Carnage vs Blood Money Inc, the I Was Wrong match, Watkins vs Williams, a Grant video package, the United States Championship match and the three-way ladder match for the WWA Championship.
Prologue Hardcore Triple Threat Conway Vs Shaman Vs Motherboy Jack: Ladies and gentlemen of the Internet, it's time for our three-way Hardcore match, Shaman versus Chris Conway versus Blake Motherboy. Denrol: Motherboy and Shaman have completely ruined Conway's life over the course of the last couple of months. They've taken everything from him, his family, his homeland, and even made his wrestling career into a joke. Jack: You can bet that Chris Conway will be looking for revenge against these two men tonight, but against both Shaman and Motherboy, this is more like a Handicap match. Stillborn by Black Label Society blasts over the PA system. Shaman enters the arena amid blue and red lights, highlighting his form eerily. Shaman steps forward swinging his arms back in forth in a boxer's fashion. Shaman walks slowly towards the ring his eyes staring at nothing but the ring. His gaze as if in a trance as he makes his way to the ring. He reaches up and grabs the ropes. He stands on the outside as he pulls himself up. He then simply steps himself over the top ropes and walks to the center of the ring and looks around at the ring itself. He then raises his arms high into the air and yells as loud as he can. A war cry from the huge Native American. Denrol: Shaman is one of the biggest and most powerful men ever to have graced the WWA. Jack: Not to mention the longest running career in the WWA. Denrol: Chris Conway will have his hands full tonight. Stephen Marley's The Mission caresses the aural cavities of the fans. For a moment, they consider cheering, thinking that perhaps Conway has embraced his Jamaican roots, but those hopeful expectations turn to boos when Blake Motherboy steps from behind the curtain. He moves to the ring with a broad grin on his face. Shaman paces in the ring. Jack: Well, that's a little insulting. Despite being from Jamaica, Chris Conway doesn't even like Reggae music. Denrol: Of course, Stephen Marley is the second son of the iconic Bob Marley, and actually made his recording debut at the age of six with his brother Ziggy on the single “Children Playing in the Streets.” Jack: Wow, I didn't think you were into Reggae music. Denrol: I love all music. And in this match, I love Shaman. The lights flash yellow and green as the bombastic sound of Wagner's Ride of the Valkyries blasts out across the arena. The Way of the Warrior! The rotund form of Chris Conway emerges onto the stage. He looks steeled and ready for serious action. Trudeau: Now entering the ring, he's from Spanish Town, Jamaica and weighs in at 424lbs, CHRIS CONWAY!! Conway nods to the crowd, his mind clearly focused on the match ahead and achieving victory. Denrol: Chris Conway is looking as fat and ugly as ever. Good to see that his recent tribulations haven't changed him. Before the referee can start to pretend to have some control over the opening moments of this Hardcore match, Conway charges forward, nothing but wild abandon and rage in his one good eye. His ham-sized fist slams into Shaman's jaw, rocking the muscled behemoth. Jack: Conway's going after it right away. Conway continues with a few more punches, forcing Shaman to step back with each mighty swing. Motherboy chooses a different tack and exits the ring. Jack: Motherboy is already gathering a steel chair at ringside. Denrol: What, you expect him to go into a fight with these monsters without a weapon? Conway grabs hold of Shaman's arm and sends him to the ropes. Shaman rebounds and is met with a meaty clothesline, dropping him to his back. Jack: Chris Conway is really fired up. Conway bends over to grab hold of Shaman's hair, but take a chair across his back. CRAXXT! Denrol: Blake Motherboy with a chair shot. Conway arches his back with the sting, but quickly turns to stare at Motherboy. He advances slowly on Motherboy, stalking the little bastard who has made his life hell. The fans love it, raining down cheers for Motherboy's imminent destruction. Motherboy shakes his head, his eyes growing wide. Jack: Motherboy is finally going to get what's coming to him. Alas, it's not to be as Shaman clubs Conway across the back of the head with a massive forearm. Conway drops to one knee. Shaman pulls him up and sends him to the ropes. Jack: The tables appear to have turned on Chris Conway. Conway rebounds from the ropes, where Shaman catches him a thundering scoop slam. Denrol: Look at that ring shake. Shaman rises to feet and steps out of the ring. Motherboy takes the opportunity to bark some orders at Shaman – which appear to be entirely ignored – and slam that steel chair across Conway's rather large belly. FWAPT! Conway sits up, clutching at his jelly-belly, while Motherboy backs off, holding the chair in a defensive posture. On the outside, Shaman tears the ring skirting away to reveal the plethora of weapons of Conway destruction. Denrol: Shaman's not going to waste any time in this hardcore match. He's already going after the nasty stuff. Shaman begins pulling out everything that he can get his hands on. Tables, chairs, barbed wire, kitchen sinks, everything. In the ring, Conway clambers to his feet, his gaze locked on Motherboy. Motherboy looks around for an escape. Jack: Motherboy is in that ring, all alone with Conway. Motherboy dives under the ropes and starts running. Conway is quick to follow, rage in his eyes. Motherboy tosses the steel chair over his shoulder, hoping to get a lucky hit, but Conway bats it away. Motherboy makes a full lap around the ring, whimpering the whole way. He finally passes Shaman who saves his bacon with a 2x4 assisted clothesline that drops Conway in his tracks. CRACK! Jack: Oh my, Shaman just broke that 2x4 in half by hitting Conway with it! Shaman takes a few moments to start setting up tables while the fans boo. Motherboy stands over Conway, shouting invectives and dripping spittle on him. Denrol: Shaman looks like he intends to make this interesting. He's making sure that there are plenty of weapons at hand. The veritable arsenal of weaponry is not lost on Motherboy. He looks around for a moment and spies a leather strap. A delighted smile creeps across his lips as he retrieves it. Conway begins to stand, but suffers the leather strap across his back instead. SNAP! Denrol: Ouch. Take it from me, that hurts. Conway arches his back, his teeth gritted in sharp pain. Motherboy strikes again. SNAP! Conway drops to his knees again and Motherboy continues the punishment, smiling like a child on Christmas. SNAP! SNAP! SNAP! The fans boo Motherboy, just on general principle. Shaman pulls up a large bag and enters the ring. Once there, he spills the contents of the bag – thousands of sharp little thumbtacks – all over the canvas. Jack: Looks like Motherboy is having a great time out there. Motherboy drops the leather strap and tries to get Conway to his feet. Of course, he simply can't muster the strength necessary to budge the over four hundred pound beast. Instead, Conway rises on his own terms and put his meaty fist around Motherboy's throat. The fans explode with joy. Jack: Chris Conway has him right where he wants him! In the ring, Shaman hits the opposite ropes and returns at speed. He drops suddenly, sliding under the bottom rope in a baseball slide that sends Conway off of his massive feet. Motherboy rubs his neck and laughs at having again been rescued by Shaman. Denrol: That Blake Motherboy is one lucky man. Motherboy points to Shaman then to Conway, barking orders. Shaman just rolls his eyes. Denrol: I can assure you that Shaman does not follow orders. Shaman pulls Conway to his feet anyway. He pulls Conway into the air with both hands around the fat man's throat. Jack: Incredible! Denrol: Shaman may be the only man in the WWA today who can hoist four hundred twenty four pounds like that. With both hands around Conway's throat, Shaman carries him to the steel ring steps. Jack: Oh, he wouldn't. Denrol: You don't know Shaman like I know Shaman. With a devastating thud Shaman slams Conway's upper back and neck into the ring steps. Jack: Two-handed chokeslam into those steel ring steps! Denrol: Vicious. Shaman stands, looking about for his next weapon. Motherboy jumps in, smashing a steel chair across Conway's head, driving it again into the ring steps. Jack: Blake Motherboy is having a little too much fun out there. Conway slumps from the ring steps to the concrete floor. Shaman meanwhile sets up a pair of tables, one against the other, leaning against the ringside barrier. Jack: What is he doing with those? Shaman then makes his way to Conway and pulls the big man up from the ground. Motherboy starts barking orders again, prompting Shaman to give him a warning glare. Motherboy promptly silences himself. Denrol: I told you he doesn't take orders. Shaman rolls Conway into the ring and follows quickly after. Inside the ring, Shaman pulls Conway up into a body slam position. With a war cry torn from the badlands themselves, he lifts Conway ever higher, pressing his massive bulk overhead. Jack: Look at the incredible strength of Shaman! Denrol: Now imagine someone with that kind of power putting his boot in your face. Trust me, it sucks. Shaman continues his roar and tosses Conway over the top rope, directly into the two table leaning against the railing. They splinter and crack under Conway's weight, offering little resistance as his body crashes against the railing. Fans scatter as the railing buckles and falls under his missile-like girth. Jack: I guess we can say that they're literally tearing the place apart. Denrol: You are such a nerd. Shaman stands in the ring, breathing heavily and shaking out his arm. Denrol: It looks like that took a toll on Shaman, though. Jack: That gorilla press launch may have been an overexertion, even for Shaman. Motherboy is on Conway in a flash, shouting more invectives and letting fly with more spittle. Shaman makes his way out of the ring and down to where Conway lays. He drops over the lifeless lump and waits for the referee's count. One! Two! Jack: What the--? Before the referee can count three, Motherboy pulls Shaman away from the pin. Denrol: Well, that was stupid. Shaman stands and stares at Motherboy, his face a mask of disbelief. Jack: Motherboy is insisting that Conway hasn't suffered enough. Motherboy continues making his case, quite loudly, as he moves next to Conway and points down at him. Conway's eyes snap open and he grabs Motherboy's legs. Jack: Chris Conway is still in this! Denrol: The Jamaican take a beating, I'll give him that. Conway clambers to his feet, dragging Motherboy off of his. With a mighty heave, he spins around in a circle, still holding Motherboy's legs. Two full rotations and he finally lets go, launching Blake Motherboy directly into Shaman, knocking him to the ground. Jack: Incredible! Conway just used Motherboy as a weapon! Conway stumbles and rolls himself into the ring, hoping to buy a moment's respite. Shaman, though, is to his feet quickly, leaving Motherboy to writhe on the ground. Shaman slides into the ring and advances on Conway. Denrol: The two big men are in the ring now. Shaman moves quickly to Conway, but Conway meets him with a powerful right, followed by a devastating left hook. Denrol: Shaman is the fresher man, but Conway is managing to keep him busy. Jack: Fresher man? Have you seen what Conway's been through in this match? With Shaman momentarily off-balance, Conway steps in and quickly hoists the Native American onto his shoulders. He positions himself carefully, then falls backward with a Samoan drop right into the pile of thumbtacks. Jack: Oh, goodness! Shaman just got a body full of those thumbtacks that he laid out earlier! Conway rolls to his feet and looks at Motherboy who has regained his feet on the outside, leaning against an as-yet intact portion of the guard rail. Conway moves toward him and points to the fans behind him. Taking the hint, the fans grab hold of Motherboy and hold him fast. Jack: This is great! Even the fans are getting in on it. Conway lumbers out of the ring and approaches Motherboy. The little weasel struggles, but the fans keep him held, his arms pinned back. Conway raises an arm in salute to the fans all around the arena, soliciting huge cheers. With a sound like a cannon firing, he swings an open chop into Motherboy's chest. SMACK! Crowd: WHOOOO, EH? Motherboy crumbles as the fans let him go. The smile on Conway's face is short-lived as Shaman grabs him from behind and floats him over with a slow, agonizing German suplex. Denrol: Conway's added bulk just makes that German suplex all the more painful. Shaman rolls to his feet and pulls Conway up again. Conway struggles, putting a fist into Shaman's gut. Shaman snarls before Conway gives him another shot to the gut. Motherboy stops the exchange with a well-timed kick to Conway's nethers from behind. The Caucasian Jamaican drops to his knees. Jack: Blake Motherboy finally did something useful out here, even if it was despicable. Denrol: Hardcore match. Jack: I know, I know. Trying desperately to ignore Motherboy's returned penchant for barking orders, Shaman takes a few steps back, lining Conway up. He then charges forward, delivering a wicked running knee to Conway's face. Conway falls flat to his back. Jack: There's no doubt that Shaman is back in control here. Shaman guides Conway back to his feet, all the while with Motherboy shouting ignored instructions. Shaman steps up onto the ring apron, dragging Conway with him. Jack: Shaman's got Conway up on the apron with him. What is he planning? Regardless of what Shaman's planning, Conway is having none of it. Denrol: Chris Conway just delivered a piledriver from the ring apron through a table! The crowd explodes with cheers. Shaman and Conway both remain motionless amid the splintered, shattered remains of the wooden table. Jack: That may have been enough to make Shaman stop in his tracks, but I think it was a desperation move on Conway's part. Motherboy smiles a weasel-like little smile and picks up a barbed wire encrusted 2x4. With a loud peal of laughter, Motherboy brings it down on Conway's head. Conway cries out and sits up, clutching at his now bleeding forehead. Motherboy laughs and brings it down on the top of Conway's head, bringing forth another fresh source of blood. Jack: And now the blood flows. Conway has been busted wide open. Denrol: Heck, I'm surprised that it took this long. Motherboy winds up to swing again, but Conway is to his feet and grabs Motherboy by the neck with one monstrous hand. Motherboy tries to scream, but it comes out as a whisper. Jack: It looks like Motherboy woke him up. Perhaps he should have left Conway alone. Conway, pulls the barbed wire 2x4 from Motherboy's hand and prepares to show him that turnabout is fair play, but instead is forced to duck underneath a clothesline from Shaman. Of course, this leaves Motherboy right in Shaman's path. Denrol: I don't think Blake Motherboy will be getting up for a long time after that clothesline. Shaman hits the ground after the strike with the barbed wire, but is quick to pull his legs up under him. But the kind of anger and rage between them could only be satisfied under hardcore rules. Shaman moves quickly, rolling back into the ring. Motherboy's been scruffed like a kitten! Shaman growls and runs his hand over his own face, coating it in his blood.
