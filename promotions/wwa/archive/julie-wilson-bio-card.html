Julie Wilson Basic Information Nicknames Real Name | Julie Angelica Wilson Height | 5'7" Weight | 117 Date of Birth | 4/27/1981 Home Town | New York City (Upper Manhattan), New York. Now residing in Oklahoma City, OK Theme | "Pumped up Kicks" by Foster the People w/ Grunge "Volcano Girls" by Veruca Salt when by hers More Details Appearance Julie is an attractive woman in her early thirties. She's above average height for a woman and her body type would be described as lithe but fit. She has a swimmers body. Her hips have flared lightly since her pregnancy. She has skin that is lightly tanned. Julie is a natural blonde but dyes her hair brown and she wears it in loose curls called a California perm. She has striking facial features with strong bone structure. Her most remarkable feature is her eyes. They're green and described as cat-like. Most people think she bears a strong resemblance to Olivia Wilde. Julie dresses casual outside of the WWA. She's a big fan of vintage t-shirts. Even though she will wear them to the ring she hates high heels. Ring Attire When managing Grunge, Julie wears her husband's signature colors of red, black, and white. If Grunge is a champion Julie is often the one wearing the title belt. Personality Julie is the yin to Grunge's yang. While Grunge may be brilliant, Julie is the more level headed one. She often keeps Grunge in line. The wife truly wears the pants in this relationship. She's a natural "den mother," always taking care of people. She tends to "adopt" Grunge's friends and allies. Most who know her describe her as warm. She has a acid tongue and a quicker wit. Grunge and Julie's relationship is best described as devoted. She and Grunge's daughter Peyton is the most important thing to her. Like Grunge, Julie is artistic and is a extremely talented painter. She's a lifelong lover of classic cars. She owns a 1958 Chevy Biscayne and a 1959 Thunderbird Convertible. She once owned a 1966 Lincoln Continental with suicide doors. She's a vegan and a member of PETA History Although born in New York, Julie was raised in Dallas Texas and went to school there, as well as in Massachusetts, where she graduated in 2002. Her father, Alexander Monfort is Irish, giving her dual US and Irish nationality and facilitating her brief study in Dublin, Ireland. She was an accomplished swimmer and earned a scholarship to the University of Oklahoma. It was there that she met her future husband, Kenneth Wilson, someday to be known as Grunge. Most of the locker-room knows the story of Grunge trying to study by the pool and Julie's high dive and resulting splash distracting him. Theirs was a whirlwind romance. They married three months later. While Grunge left college after a semester to enroll in wrestling school, Julie finished and earned a degree in finance. She's managed Grunge's career full-time since he started. She had to pull back during Grunge's WWA World Title reign when she became pregnant. After Grunge lost the title he took some time off to be with is wife. In September 2004, they welcomed Peyton Reilly Wilson into the world. Grunge wrestled sporadically for the WWA after and toured to world. When Peyton was old enough Julie went back to managing Grunge full-time. Peyton goes on the road with them to this day, being home-schooled by her mother and father. Now the Wilson family has returned home to the WWA full-time with their grand reopening. The Bunny has come too, much to Julie's annoyance.
