The Scouting Report - Issue 17

20 Sep 2003

Bylined Josh Wilson - Director of Talent, in the fed's own kayfabe voice.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It’s time for another action packed edition of The Scouting Report with yours truly. After a small hiatus I’m back. Things have been going on recently with the WWA and I’m here to give you the lowdown. -Technical difficulties are preventing the Meltdown from airing at it regular time, last week. However we should be back on schedule soon. -However Fiesta has been on schedule and things have been looking up for the show. Anybody who hasn’t seen it should go and check it out. -EWA Pain Games was recently held in the Fiesta time slot and man was it a show. Not only was it packed with action from the WWA stars but it also featured the crowning of a new WWA European Champion. -The Psycho won became the first champion in the main event in what some were calling a surprise beating out the number one picks of Tommy Starr and Jared Crow. Congratulations to him. -We also had an exciting match featuring Justin “Air” Bourne and Jimmi Zion. Although Zion won it was a highlight for both men. Justin will make his return to the WWA on this week’s Fiesta. -Also that night Keru no Akiro beat newcomer Steven Rage and Leviathan mugged Nick Adams. Both exciting matches. -The next week things got back to normal on Fiesta with WWA packed action. -Keru no Akrio and Evan “Loco” Motive faced the returning Shaman and Fuel. Fuel and Shaman have named their new team the Horsemen and it seems that a feud may be starting between the two teams. -Of course Shaman also has to contend with Jimmi Zion. Looks the like the five-year veteran will have a lot of competition on his hands. -In one of the shocks of the night Showtime beat GenX champion James Beam for the title. The conditions were that if Beam lost he would have to be the leader of Showtime’s little group that consists of himself, “Primetime” Johnny Bedford and James Curzon and of course all the minis. -The match was originally billed as James Beam to face Venny LeTrav but Venny asked for a week off. He was with his wife who was having complications with her pregnancy. -He’s back to action now and I’m happy to report that his wife is fine. They welcomed a new daughter Madison into this world. Time to break out the cigars. -Two newcomers also faced off when NGS Jason Andrews, no relation to Tiffani, faced Myzery. Myzery picked up the win and impressed. -Unfortunately the WWA and Jason are parting ways because of some backstage problems. He’s booked in a match of Fiesta this week and it may be his last appearance here on WWA television. This a shame. -However aside from Myzery we’ve also picked up some newcomers in the form of Prometheus and the new tag-team To Be Determined. Hope to see good things from all of them. -Speaking of tag-teams, in the main event of the night the Brothers Delucci faced X-Corp for the WWA Tag-Team Championship. In what was a very impressive match, Havoc decided to enter the fray. They claim they should be the tag-team champions. Looks like the Tag-Team scene is heating up. -Aside from the late Meltdown we also have a action-packed Fiesta coming up. -In the first match of the night, To Be Determined will be making their début against the 7th Layer. The 7th Layer is coming back for one night only. God help us all. -In a match made last week Keru no Akrio and Evan “Loco” Motive will face the Horsemen in signals action. What’s interesting is that each signals match will be contested in a Japanese Gimmick match and we won’t know who faces whom until that night. -You’ve got to give the advantage to the Horsemen considering Shaman and Fuel have been competing in Japan on their time off. Only these guys would consider that a vacation. -Next we have the main event of the night, the twenty-man signal elimination tag match. - Nick Adams, NSG Jason Andrews, Tommy DeLucci, Tony DeLucci, Dave Harley, Venny LeTrav, myZery, Mal Somers, Jimmi Zion and Justin Bourne will face James Beam, James Curzon, Grunge, Joey K, Leviathan, Lockdown, Se7en, Steven Rage, Showtime and Primetime Johnny Bedford. -This should be a interesting match considering that even teammates hate each other. Not only that throw in the fact that the match features the TV and Tag-Team champions and you have one exciting match. -Well until next time this is Josh Wilson. Be sure to watch Meltdown and Fiesta. You won’t be disappointed. Ciao.
