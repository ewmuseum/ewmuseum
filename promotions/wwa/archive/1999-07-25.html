Sunday July 25, 1999 The scene opens up to the WWA banner. Then firework type explosions go off around the Banner. The camera shows the outside of the complex, revealing it to be the New Orleans Arena. The screen then goes into the stadium to show the fans jumping and screaming at the Camera. The camera pans over the the arena, where signs can be seen such as: "Lantern will light the way", "HOP Country", and "Welcome Back, Tracer"... And then the camera finds Jimmy Rose and Terry Valentine sitting behind the table in the broadcast area. Jimmy : Welcome to a special edition of Fights, coming to you the 25th day of July on Sunday.... I am your NEW host Jimmy Rose! I am here with the "honorable" Terry Valentine. Terry : Thanks Jimmy... (pauses for the crowd pop) Tonight we are going yo start things off with a special interview with the World Wrestling Alliance World Heavyweight Champion... Please welcome the Outlaw! "Thunderstruck" by AC/DC begins to play over the PA System and the crowd erupts with a chorus of cheers and boos.....more cheers than boos. On the Jumbotron appears a series of quick clips of the Outlaw's matches, his two World Title victories, dominate the series. The Outlaw appears at the top of the ramp, wearing a pair of loose fitting tan dockers, a white polo shirt with a forest green polo logo, and hush puppy saddle shoes. His gold Rolex watch glistens in the lights. The World Title is draped over his shoulder and shines as if it were recently polished. The Outlaw slowly struts to the ring where Johny Morehouse waits patiently... The Outlaw reaches the ringside and walks up the ring steps and slowly approaches Morehouse. I'm glad you're back here..... Johny : Thank you....truly it means alot. Now....without anymore hesitation, I've got to ask.... What about Denrol? But as you can see....(The Outlaw points to the belt)....I'm back on top. Denrol wasn't championship material....he could never beat the true Great One....and that was proven at the Independence Day PPV. Now, why did we tag....well I had this contract drawn up back during the WWA Tag Tourney....it basically set forth that we would be contractually obligated to tag together for enough matches to win the tourney.....I think it was 4 or 5. But nonetheless, we ended one short....so he had one more match with me to fulfill the contract and until then he couldn't tag with anyone else. Denrol being the Jackass he is forgot.....leaving himself open. My original thoughts were to leave him out to dry.....let him face two of the greatest tag wrestlers in the federation by himself. But the though of being the first World Champion and World Tag Champion of the WWA, got the best of me. Unfortunately we fell short, but it was fun watching him get his ass beat in the beginning..... Johny : Any comments with regard to his final words.... Outlaw : Only good riddance. What most people around here don't know is Denrol had contacted the HOP on 3 occasions requesting to become a member of the HOP.....and on all three we said "NO". Ever since then he has been out to undermine us....he says we are getting old....we don't do what's best for the federation and so on....it's just out of envy. He claims I'm a silent champion, but lets see here.....I won the title in May....I immediately set up a dream match....Segier, Darken, Kas and Denrol in a four corner match. That was better then the one I won the title in.....the winner got me. Then I went from there to face Shaman.....then Denrol twice. All of this in just a little over two months. I'm only required to defend this strap monthly, but I've doubled that requirement. In addition, no...I don't spend all my time out here running my mouth taping interview to reiterate the same thing over and over again......but I do run the largest and greatest stable in the WWA. That is very time consuming..... I'm always working behind the scenes......but that is neither here nor there....the reality of it is Denrol was envious of the HOP and could stand that he would always be looking in from the outside......so now he is gone...... enough said.
