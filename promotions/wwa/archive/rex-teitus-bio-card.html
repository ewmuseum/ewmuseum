Rex Teitus Basic Information Nicknames | The Howitzer Real Name | Rex Teitus Height | 6'4" Weight | 275 lbs Date of Birth | 7/4/1984 Home Town | Charleston, South Carolina Theme | Last by Nine Inch Nails More Details Appearance Teitus is a very built and muscular man, he has Military high and tight hair cut and a clean shaven face with deep blueish green eyes.He has a scar in his right eyebrow and a small scar on his chin. He is very thick with muscle and dark tanned. Ring Attire Teitus comes to the ring wearing black boots, knee pads, Gloves, and a black and red singlet that says Red Blooded American on it. Personality Teitus is a very intense individual, He is very explosive when it comes to action in the ring and is not afriad of anything. If he loses he carries the same attitude, he will get up and keep fighting until he is seperated or forced out of the ring. If it isn't worth the effort he will leave. He does not care for the safety of anyone or anything. He has been known to take thigns out on anyone closest to him at the time of anger eruption. History Teitus joined the US Army at the age of eighteen and began working out heavily while he was in the military. He was in iraq during the year of 2003. He was the only survivor of a road side bombing and never really forgave himself or anyone else for that matter of what happened to those that were with him from his unit. He always looks in the mirror and remidns hismelf everyday that the scars he received were nothing compared to the scars in his soul. While returning home in 2008 to finish his military career he found Professional wrestling and Mixed martials arts appealing. He continued heavy workouts in the gym powerlifting to make himself thicker as he liked to personally call it. Over the course of a couple years he caught the attention of several higher profile wrestling promotions around the country, but nothing significant. Taking a title here and there before he finally got a chance to wrestle in a big promotion in Japan. While he was wrestling in Japan he met a WWA scout and was told he should join them, The scout had a contract prepared until the WWA shutdown due to Vaugh Babbs inept ability to run a legit wrestling business. A few monthys had passed and out of the blue a new scout contacted him and asked if he was still interested in wrestling in the WWA. Teitus said yes and the rest will soon be written into history.
