WWA — 28 Nov 1998

Saturday November 28, 1998 The screen opens up to the the WWA banner. Then firework type explosions go off around the Banner. The Banner fades... then the screen shows the Fleet Center. The screen then goes to the inside to show the fans jumping and screaming at the Camera. The camera pans over the the Complex and then the camera finds Eric Pembrook, John Douglaus Juan Guevesos sitting behind the table in the broadcast area. Eric : Welcome ladies and gentlemen to the another episode of Saturday Night Extreme! I am your host Eric Pembrook. We've got a good one hear tonight! John : We sure do Eric! Shaman in a Warriors Cage match! And Mad coil in a Himalayan Pit fight! Juan : Along with that the Stormtrooper in his singles debut. I told you SS-X to watch out! But NO, don't listen to the Mexican! Eric : We are coming to you LIVE form the Fleetcenter, home of the Boston Bruins! In lovely Boston, Mass. Like I said, we have a great one for you tonight! Let's get right into it... Wait... I have word that there is some commotion in the back! John : Why are we still here! Eric : We are getting a camera back there right now! The camera flashes to the lockerroom area. You see the Stormtrooper being attacked... by New Star and David Briceland. The Misery is already on the ground. John : Oh yeah! A good old fashioned ass whoopin'! Juan : New Star attacks the Stormtrooper and the Stormtrooper tosses New Star headlong into the lockers! New Star falls to the ground in a heap with a bloodied forehead. Eric : Briceland attacks and Stormtrooper picks him up and tosses Briceland through the wall! Oh my god! John : This is great! Uh oh! Here come Anarchy! Chaos and Raiden both tackle the Stormtrooper through another wall! Raiden picks Stormtrooper up and powerbombs him on the floor! Juan : My god man! Chaos picks the Stormtrooper up and powerslams him through a table! Eric : There is no way the Stormtrooper can take this punishment! He has a match still tonight! Havoc : You don't f*#k with the Star Station! The scene fades and goes to a commercial for the WWA Season's Beatings... A mini PPV coming on December 15th! Then the screen goes into a commercial. The camera fades back onto the comentators desk. Eric : Welcome back ladies and gentlemen. The word in the back is that the Stormtrooper is unfit to wrestle tonight against Billy Coleman. So then let's move on to the next match of the evening... The camera flashes to the ring, wear Gary Trudeau is standing with a microphone. Gary Trudeau : The second match of the night is set for a twenty minute time limit. It is a falls count anywhere match. First, weighing in at 255 pounds, the leader of Cold Pain Enterprises, "The Sniper" David Wattsoner! "Saturday Night's Alright" begins over the loudspeaker. David Wattsoner comes out from backstage. He stops at the top of the ramp and looks around at the crowd smiling. John :What is Malkav doing? He's on top of the JumboTron! Maybe he'll jump.... Eric : No John, he's got a can of paint. Oh God! He dumps it all over Wattsoner. The Sniper looks pissed. John : Actually, he looks yellow to me. Juan : Is he crazy, he just jumped off the JumboTron and nailed Wattsoner. This could be the end of the match already. Eric : Both men are just laying at the top of the ramp. It looks like Malkav is starting to stir. Now Wattsoner gets up. He is really upset. John : Malkav is running away from The Sniper. Wattsoner is giving chase, though. He's gonna catch that wascally wabbit! Juan : That was really not funny, John. John : Shut up, all supreme luchador boy! Eric : Do I need to sit between you boys? Malkav in the ring now. Wattsoner in right after him. John : The two tie up, and Malkav is pulling something from out of his trunks. Juan : It's a scouring pad. What the hell..... Eric : It looks like the mad man is trying to scrub the CPE tatoo off of Wattsoner. John : This guy is demented!!! Eric : Wattsoner is really scratched up now and he's a flaming mad. Juan : He's nailing Malkav with haymakers left and right. John : The Sniper whips Malkav off the ropes and hits a huge clothesline. Juan : Now Wattsoner has Amaranth up, and executes a big sidewalk slam. Eric : Wattsoner with the Irish whip into the ropes but Amaranth reverses it and follows it up with a clothesline knocking The Sniper out of the ring. Juan : Malkav goes off the ropes again and throws himself over the ropes onto Wattsoner with a suicide dive. Wattsoner looks hurt. John : Malkav is now rambling something to himself and goes off looking for something under the ring. Eric : He is wasting valuable time. This guy is totally nuts. Juan : It looks like he found what he wanted. Wattsoner is up but doesn't look like he's in his right mind either. How is he standing? John : Malkav has a chair from under the ring. Malkav approaches The Sniper and takes a massive swing with the chair. Eric : Wattsoner was playing possum as he kicks Malkav in the gut. That kick made Malkav drop the chair. Juan : Some CPE cronies seem to have made their way out here. The Ungentle Giant, Joseph Stock, and El Porto Spidero are making their way down the ramp. John : Wattsoner continues with the assault he just sent the Crazy One into the guardrail. Eric : El Porto Spidero and Stock have the ref cornered and are talking to him. Giant is going around the ring to meet his leader, Wattsoner. Juan : The Sniper just blasted Malkav with an atomic drop. Ungentle Giant picks up the misplaced chair and hands it to Wattsoner. Eric : He just thwacked Amaranth with the chair. He's not stopping there either, he continues to send blows to the head of the deranged one. John : Good, maybe it will knock some sense into him. Juan : Giant heads back to his stablemates Spidero and Stock. Eric : Spidero now points outside the ring where Wattsoner is on top of Malkav with the pin. John : The ref gets there...1...2...3 and Sniper gets the win. Juan : Malkav isn't getting up, I think he may be unconscious. CPE looks happy. Wattsoner is giving high-fives to his followers as they make their way backstage. "Saturday Night's Alright" by Elton John plays as CPE heads back to the lockerroom. Malkav continues to just lay outside the ring. John : Officials have come from backstage to check on Malkav Amaranth. But right when they get to him he pops up like nothings wrong. Juan : He happily skips backstage. What a weirdo. Eric : We'll be back after this short message from our sponsors. The camera closes and a commercial starts.... The camera fades back onto the comentators desk. Eric : Welcome back ladies and gentelmen... The next match this evening will be a long awaited debut for Damien "The Wolf" Smith... John : Smith sucks! He is pretty boy wannabe wrestler! Juan : We will see about that John... The camera flashes to the ring, wear Gary Trudeau is standing with a microphone. Gary Trudeau : And our challenger hailing from England Damien " The Wolf " Smith... Camera focuses on the ramp way as the new comer Damien " The Wolf " Smith walks down the ramp as "Meet the Creeper" by Rob Zombie blasts on the PA. John : Well it seems that this newcomer has the oppertunity to bring something to this fed with the leaving of Nik Segier and Johny Tambo.. Juan : Yes he will bring the excitement which may be lacking here .. Eric : Well the ref signals for the bell and they lock up.. John : Smith with a quick arm drag take down. Juan : Sonic seems to be trying to muscle out of it but Smith is to quick and wrenches back on the arm.. Eric : Smith helping Sonic up and he applies the abdominal stretch... John : Smith reaches back and pulls on the ropes for leverage and the ref does not see him.. Juan : Smith with the release and a quick German suplex and tosses his opponent out of the ring.. Eric : Very impressive array of moves applied by Smith. John : He seems well focused on his opponent and has researched him well. Juan : Smith grabs his opponent by the hair and lifts him into the ring.. Eric : Smith with a quick body slam and a suplex.. John : He seems to be wearing the man down for something. Juan : Here it is the Wolfs Howl.. John : What a finisher it looks devestating.. Eric : Smith with the one two three and the win...welcome to the WWA this new star here may be the next Nik Segier in the WWA.. Juan : Wait he has the mic..lets see what he is going to say.. Damien " The Wolf " Smith : All right i have a surprise for all you fans..One of you lucky fans will get a picture with me....and of course a male fan will be blessed with being kissed by the beautiful Bikini clad woman who is making her way to the ring...Lady please select your male and i will choose the female.. Camera focuses on the two as they both choose someone.. Damien " The Wolf " Smith : All right dear here is your picture with me and Son your about to get the kiss of your life..Because when your good your good but when your better your Damien " The Wolf " Smith... Camera focuses on Damien " The Wolf " Smith as he gets his picture taken with the young lady and the young man is being thoroughly kissed by the bikini clad woman... John : Well dang i wish i was the kid sheesh.. Juan : Well i think Snith's got the crowds attention.. Eric :This next match is a first here in the World Wrestling Alliance, a Himilayan Pit Fight. Juan :The setup for this match is very interesting. This fight will take place in a pit that is fifteen feet deep. The pit is surrounded by concrete walls. At the top of the pit a metal pole is constructed horizontally across the opening. One rope is knotted to that pole and runs down the pit. The winner is the man that climbs that rope out of the pit first. John :This is gonna be one helluva fight with these two big men. Eric :Well these guys are in the pit and ready to go. The camera cuts to the inside of the pit. Mad Coil and Brent Watson are circling each other with demonic stares on their faces. The pit is dim and the lone feature is the hanging rope. The two men approach each other. Juan :This match is started as the two men tie up in the middle of the pit. John :Mad Coil gets the edge with his strength and backs Watson into the concrete wall. Eric :Mad Coil now driving his shoulder into the chest of Watson. This Himilayan is really out for some revenge. John :Now Mad Coil backs off and hits the Canadien with some kicks to the chest and head. Juan :Watson looks dazed. He is now sitting with his back against the wall. John :Mad Coil not stopping there though. He now goes to work on Watson's knee driving hard elbows into it. Eric :Mad Coil taunts his opponent with some harsh words. Watson looks to have had enough as he slaps Mad Coil across the face. Juan :Watson now to his feet and whips Mad Coil into the concrete wall. That's definetly gonna leave a mark. Eric :Watson now with a big scoop slam. Watson with some momentum executing another scoop slam. John :Watson picks Mad Coil up for a guerilla press but Mad Coil gets down quickly. Mad Coil picks Watson up and nails and atomic drop. He picks Watson up again and rams his back into the concrete wall. Eric :Mad Coil has a hand full of hair and drags Watson towards the rope. What is he doing? Juan :Mad Coil takes the rope and wraps it around Watson's neck. He is really pulling hard on that rope making it impossible for Watson to breathe. John :With no officials down there Mad Coil can do this as long as he wants. Juan :He doesn't though. He just released the strangle. He's got Watson on his back on the ground and grabs his legs. Eric :Mad Coil with the catapult sending Watson's body right into the concrete wall. Watson has been busted open. His face is really gushing. John :Watson has no idea where he is now. Mad Coil easily executes a sidewalk slam into the cold concrete floor. Juan :Mad Coil back to the rope and is now on his way up to proclaim himself winner of this match. John :But Watson's up and he grabs Mad Coil's leg and brings him back down into the pit. Eric :Watson with some punches to Mad Coil's face and now going for the hurricanrana. Mad Coil's head has been driven into the concrete floor. His nose is bleeding like crazy. I think it may be broken, by the looks of it. Juan :Watson lifts Mad Coil to his feet and gives him a running bulldog again driving his face into the concrete floor. Eric :Watson has this thing won if he gets to that rope. John :That looks to be what he's doing. Watson starts his ascent up the rope. He's about ten feet up now. What's he doing? Juan :Watson has a weird look on his face. What the...no way! He just dropped off the rope and landed right on Mad Coil. That had to be painful for both men. Eric :Both men lay motionless on the concrete floor, bleeding heavily I might add. John :These guys could be down for awhile. They've put everything they got into knocking the snot out of each other in this match. Juan :Mad Coil is moving, he's moving! Eric :He gets up to his feet and starts driving his big boot into the chest of Watson. John :He now concentrates on the knees of Watson sending kicks to them. Mad Coil now with elbows to the inside of the knees. Eric :He continues this assault. I don't think he ever wants Watson to leave this pit. John :Well, he did have the chance! Eric :John, I think this match goes beyond winning. These men are out to kill each other. John :I think Watson's unconcious! Juan :Mad Coil lifts the Canadien to his feet. He takes Watson's head and drives it into the concrete...1...2...3...4 times. This thing has got to be over. Eric :Mad Coil lifts Watson up and nails an Avalanche. You can definetly tell from that weak reverse jack knife from Mad Coil that he is tired. He's heading for the rope. John :He starts his climb to victory. He's about halfway up and Watson is stirring. Watson looks around and gets to his feet. He limps to the rope and begins climbing up after Mad Coil. He just doesn't seem to be moving quick enough with those weakened knees. Juan :He's not gonna get him, Mad Coil is nearing the top. He is reaching for the pole and....is up. Mad Coil wins! John :What the? He is untying the rope that one Brent Watson is currently better than halfway up. Only HOP would think of something like this, baby! Eric :He pulls the last knot out and Watson goes flying back to the bottom of the pit. Wow, that was quite a thud. Get the paramedics in here! Watson just dropped at least ten feet and landed on his back on that hard concrete. John :Mad Coil's got a microphone.... Mad Coil : Watson, you were RIGHT! Third times a charm and I just crushed you. Fight me again and you will never, and I mean never, leave the ring!! Mad Coil viciously throws the microphone down into the pit. He limps back to the lockeroom. Paramedics are coming out to tend to Brent Watson. Eric :We need a commercial break to clean this carnage up. We'll be right back with our huge main event.
