CWL SUNDAY SHOWDOWN June 25, 2000 LIVE FROM THE COREL CENTRE IN OTTAWA, ONTARIO The scene fades in after a brief introduction to a sold out, rabid Canadian crowd. Various signs are caught on camera including one that reads "I am Canadian!" and another which boldly states "Dr. Love has the cure!" The camera pans around a few seconds more before coming to rest on two individuals sitting behind a red and black announce desk. Both are WWA employees recently reassigned to work north of the border. Eric : Good evening everyone and welcome to this first edition of the Canadian Wrestling League's Sunday Showdown! We are coming to you live from the nation's capital of Ottawa, the home of the Senators. I'm Eric Pembrook alongside a colleague who really needs no introduction, the incomparable Terry Valentine. Terry : That's right folks, finally Harrington assigns me to another job and its up here in frigid Canada. I swear I almost hit four moose on my way to the show. Eric : Terry, there hasn't been a moose in Ottawa for years, at least not in metropolitan Ottawa...anyway enough talk. Its time to get on with the action. CWL fans will be pleased to hear we've brought along a familiar ring announcer, Brooke Prescott. Terry : The one good thing about working up here. The camera cuts to the ring where the lovely Ms. Prescott stands surrounded by four large men... Brooke Prescott : Ladies and gentlemen, our first bout of the evening is scheduled for one fall. Introducing first to my left the team of Truckstop Jones and "Slick" Simon Watts, Damage incorporated! (after some crowd response she continues.) And on my right, Pitt and William 'THE MANGLER' Mannheim, Torture Cha... Brooke is promptly cut off as the two teams, bored from standing in the ring for introductions, tear into each other. Luckily she manages to escape to the outside before she is hurt. Eric : Brooke almost getting a firsthand taste of the action there. Terry : Welcome to Canada sweetheart! Pitt and Watts are declared the first two legal men. Pitt immediately floors Watts with a clothesline and then follows it up by choking him with his knee. The ref administers a five count and Pitt breaks the hold. He helps Simon to his feet, only todrop him again with a fallaway slam. Watts pops back up and staggers to where he can tag in Jones. Truckstop comes running in but the huge Pitt drives him down with a catching powerslam. Pitt stands over his fallen opponent before tagging out to Mannheim. The Mangler comes in and instantly hits a leg drop and covers up his opponent. 1...2..kickout. Mannheim shows no signs of letting up. He continues to work over Jones with moves like a short clothesline and bulldog. After another unsuccessful pinfall he shakes his head in annoyance. Terry : This one has been all TC so far. Eric : Both of these teams are relatively new to competition. TC are actually full fledged members of WWA and not on the CWL roster, but the higher ups have decided they will be booked here tonight. Oh! A low blow by Jones turns the tables. As Mannheim struggles to get air into his lungs Jones tags Watts back in and The Mangler is the victim of a double clothesline. Pitt tries to get in, but the ref bars his way enabling Damage Inc. to pull of a double atomic drop. Watts covers as the ref turns. 2 count only. Watts climbs to the top rope and dives at his rising opponent, but Mannheim dodges and drives him into the canvas. A still dazed Mannheim reaches Pitt and the big monster comes in. He tackles him with a gutwrench powerbomb. Jones tries to interfere, but this time it is him cut off by the ref. Eric : A little poetic justice from what happened before. TC is not double teaming though as Simon looks to be down and out. That last powerbomb really knocked him senseless. Terry : What I'm interested to see is the hiring policy on these Canadian refs. Man this guy is uptight, he won't let anything slide. Eric : Of course referee Jack Mitchell is just doin his job, but the fans and Terry seem eager to see this thing erupt. With Watts still down and out, Torture Chamber lock Jones up in a corner and take turns blasting away at him. Watts recovers in this time and drops Pitt from behind in a school boy rollup. But the immense lower body strength of Pitt enables him to throw Simon off. Jones reverses Mannheim in the corner and goes up top to begin punching away at his exposed head. Pitt sees this and knocks Jones from the turnbuckle to the outside. Eric : Jones hits hard! His face actually struck the guardrail on the way down. Man he could be in serious trouble. Terry : Actually I think Watts is in a little more trouble right now. As the crowd gasps in awe the huge Pitt mounts the top turnbuckle and Mannheim hands him a dazed Watts. In one swift motion Pitt locks him up and drives him down with a belly to belly superplex. The ring shudders with the impact and the ref is right on top of it. Pitt gets the three count and the win for Torture Chamber. Terry : I think we need a new ring. That one isn't going to be able to stand much more after Pitt's been high flying on it. Eric : I agree. Damage Inc. may have been a bit more successful against the bigger team tonight, had Jones not completely KO'ed himself on that spill to the outside. That was brutal!
