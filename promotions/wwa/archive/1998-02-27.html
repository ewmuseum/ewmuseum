WWA — 27 Feb 1998

Friday Feb 27, 1998 The screen opens up to the the NCW banner. Then.... Northeast Champion Wrestling flashes through the screen and leaves the NCW Banner Then firework type explosions go off around the Banner. The Banner fades... then the screen shows the RIC Athletic Complex. The screen then goes to the inside to show the fans jumping and screaming at the Camera. The camera pans over the the Complex and then the camera finds Tony Bailey, Terry Valentine and Johny Morehouse sitting behind the table in the broadcast area. Tony : Welcome ladies and gentlemen to the RIC Athletic Complex on the campus of Rhode Island College in Providence, Rhode Island. We have another great card for you tonight folks. Johny : Thats right. Tonight we will crown the NEW Rhode Island Champion. Now other place better to do it than right here in the capital. Terry : We have the greatest wrestler in the NCW here tonight as Randy Cross Defends his title against Nikodemous Segier. Tony : A lot of things have been happening the past week, let's take you back to Wednesday Night War and show you what happened after we left the show.... The camera fades and then opens on a replay of last week.... The Black Scorpian has Randy Cross cornered. Terry : Zack nails the Black Scorpian from behind and the Scorpian goes down. All three members of House of Pain start kicking the Black Scorpian. Tony : Jak Lancer is stirring and now realizes he is the NEW New York City Champion. Johny : He turns and is face to face with Randy Cross, who smiles and holds his hand out to Jak Lancer for a hand shake. Terry : This is great.... Jak is reaching for a microphone. Jak Lancer has a microphone. Before Jak starts to speak a commotion from the crowd interupts the announcement. It is Big Eliem who jumps over the guard rail and into the ring. Tony : Jak is about to say something. NO! he pushes Cross into Big Eliem who in turn slaps on the "Sista of Mercy". Cross is down in the ring. The House of Pain jumps onto Big Eliem and starts to beat him down. Jak "The Boil' Lancer : GENTLEMEN! The House of Pain stop there attack and look at Lancer ready to jump him.... Jak "The Boil' Lancer : May what you have witnessed here serve as an eternal symbol of my love for you. The Master has spoken....I WILL JOIN your little party. Terry : YES! New members of the House of Pain. Jak helps up Cross and huggs him. Jak walks over to the other HOP members hugging each one.... Tony : Well, it appears that Jak Lancer the new New York City Champion is the newest member of the House of Pain. Johny : They must be stopped. Terry : The House of Pain are GREAT! You guys are just jealous. Tony : Before we go to our first match of the evening, I must re-iterate something that Commisioner Harrington announced earlier today. The Commandan'ts Own have been reassigned to the Gulf. Their time in the NCW is over. The CWC Northeastern Tag Belts are vacant. There will be a Tournament soon. Also, as a result of this, the Non-Title Match between the Commandan't Own and Da Killer Connection is cancelled. Terry : No big loss there. Johny : Well let’s go down to the ring for our opening bout. The camera flashes to the ring, and we see Gary trudeau standing in the middle of the ring with a microphone. It will be a second round match in the Rhode Island Title Tournament. Introducing first, coming down the isle... "Master of Puppets" by Metalica stats up over the P.A.. He is slapping hands of the few adoring fans on his way to the ring. Hetti gets into the ring and "Pepper" starts to play as the crowd rises to their feet. Trudeau : And his Opponent, from Chicago Illinios and weighing in at 220 pounds. We have the former New England Champion Johny Blaze! As Blaze enters the arena the fan support is defening. Blaze slaps hands and plays with the crowd on his way to the ring. Tony : NCW veteran Johnny Blaze is set to take on relative newcomer Jake Hetti. Johny : The classic battle of Fire and Ice, the question remains will Fire melt the ice? Or will the Ice extinguish Johnny’s blaze. Johny : There’s the bell and we are under way. Johny : Hetti takes Blaze to the mat with a drop toe hold. Terry : Hetti apllies a headscissors on Blaze. Tony : Blaze slides out of the headscissors and kicks to his feet. Johny : Hetti slips behind Blaze with a waistlock. Tony : Blaze answers with a series of elbows to the jaw. Terry : Blaze slips behind Hetti and attempts a belly to back suplex. Johny : Hetti lands on his feet. Tony : Blaze ducks a charging clothesline. Terry : Blaze stuns Hetti with a massive kneelift. Johny : Blaze hits the ropes and drives Hetti down with a bulldog. Tony : Blaze picks up Hetti and whips him into the ropes. Johny : Hetti catches Blaze in a crucifix. Tony : Blaze kicks out at one. Terry : I am impressed with Hetti. He’s holding his own against Blaze. Johny : I agree, but the longer this match goes the more in favor of Blaze it will be. Tony : Both men are to their feet. Terry : Hetti backs Blaze into the corner. Johny : Hetti tries to hiptoss Blaze out of the corner. Tony : Blaze blocks it, and smashes Hetti’s head into the turnbuckle. Johny : Blaze whips Hetti across the ring. Terry : Hetti reverses it, and Blaze hits hard in the corner. Tony : Hetti hits the ropes and bulldogs Blaze in the center of the ring. Johny : Hetti could have him here. Terry : Hetti goes to the top. This could be it. Tony : Hetti launches backwards at Blaze. He’s going for Ice Destiny. Johny : Blaze rolls out of the way! Tony : Blaze kicks up to his feet. Terry : I can’t hear a thing shut these fans up. Tony : Blaze hooks Hetti into Hell’s Fire. Johny : Hetti taps out. Tony : What a first match. Blaze will advance to the next round to face the winner of the next match. The camera flashes to the ring, and we see Gary trudeau standing in the middle of the ring with a microphone.
