What this is Roster bio card for the WWA wrestler Nathan Cross, giving nicknames, measurements, birth date, hometown, appearance, ring attire, personality, awards and finisher.
Nathan Cross Basic Information Nicknames | Epic, Legend of the Future Real Name Height | 6'2" Weight | 224lbs Date of Birth | 12/28/1992 Home Town | Philadelphia, Pennsylvania Theme More Details Appearance Nate is an extremely handsome young man. He has blue eyes and short dark brown hair styled into a short mohawk, has two sleeper earrings in each ear lobe and has a short goatee and a five o'clock shadow. His body shows that he is in peak physical condition, think a carbon copy of John Morrison. Ring Attire He wears black wrestling shorts with a styalized lime green cross on each side. He wears black elbow and knee pads and black and lime green boots. He also wears black and lime green BMX gloves. Personality Nate is the grandson of WWA Hall of Famer Randy Cross, he is a cocky, self centered and selfish young man who cares about nothing but money and power and has always taken things he has wanted, no matter the cost. This has lead to him making mistakes in his past and as a result has had shorts stints in juvenile hall as well as a short stretch in prison for undisclosed offences. Since his release from jail he has put his criminal activities behind him and has now focused on carving his own legacy in the professional wrestling industry and the company that his grandfather helped build. He has yet to step foot in a ring professionally, but he has voiced his intentions to anyone who will listen that he will seek to achieve success by any means necessary; meaning he will lie and cheat and use any dirty, underhanded tactic to gain wins in the new World Wrestling Alliance...he is an opportunist in ever sense of the word. He is an accomplished amature wrestler and martial artist, he has shown considerable talent and skill in Shotokan Karate having begun the practise at the very young age of three and a half and now holds a second degree black belt in the dicipline as well as a National title. He also has found great knowledge and success in building his fighting repertoire with Judo in which he holds a brown belt and more recently as of 2009 a blue belt in Brazilian Jiu-jitsu. However he is not without the wrestling (and fighting) skill required to be a true success in the WWA; in addition to his considerable martial arts talent, he has also been trained by his legendary grandfather as well as a who's who of current and former WWA superstars, champions and legends throughout his childhood and into his teens in the art of professional wrestling. He is better prepared for success than many rookies making their way into this industry. Couple those extensive years of training with his incredible agility and his hobby of parkour and Nathan Cross could very well be a mega star in years to come. He has achieved some incredible feats in his short life; including National titles in High School and College Wrestling as well as Karate, but he says he has only just begun to become great and the WWA is the next step in forging his legacy and taking the Cross name to new heights. History The future has not yet been written.
