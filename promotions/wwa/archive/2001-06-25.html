The crowd are horrified. They begin to boo, and the boos grow louder as Kraven hauls Ventressa's body out of the dumpster. A voice comes from above. Voice : You might wanna get that guy in the Saints shirt some medical help? We look up, and see the voice belonged to Denrol's cameraman. We don't pay much attention, however. As the ref radio's for some EMTs, Kraven hoists Ventressa over one of his shoulders. He winces from the effort, but soon recovers, and carries his foe out from the alleyway. Jimmy : This doesn't look good for Vik Ventressa... Fat Tony : Nope, not by a long way! The camera follows Kraven, and we see him head to a street cafe: the kind with tables on the sidewalk. Kraven grins, and gestures at the folks at one of the tables. Without speaking a word, they vacate the area. Kraven smiles at the glass table, and then turns to the camera. Jackson Kraven : You watching this, Morehouse? With that Kraven sets Ventressa unsteadily on his feet, before locking him up in position for a Jackhammer! Jimmy : Folks, this about to get might ugly! Fat Tony : You said it, Jimmy! Kraven lands the Jackhammer... right through the glass table! The ref from earlier slides in to save Ventressa from any more punishment. 1... 2...<br. 3!!! Kraven smiles, but it looks as though the move took a lot out of him. Then, a shadow falls across him. A *big* shadow. Voice ( off camera ): Well, well, well. It comes down to this, does it? Kraven looks up, and his smile fades as blood drips down his face. Jackson Kraven : Son of a... Kraven is cut off, as he is speared to the ground by Craven Moore! Jimmy : We are down to our last two competitors! Fat Tony : This is great! They've fought in almost every match imaginable, and now they're fighting for the number one contendership! Moore picks up Kraven and goes for a vertical suplex, but Kraven blocks it! Moore tries again, but is thwarted a second time! Jimmy : Kraven with the reversal! Vertical suplex on the unforgiving concrete! Kraven goes for the cover. 1... 2... Jimmy : Moore kicks out! Kraven goes to pick Moore up, but gets a kick in the face for his troubles. Moore rises and picks up his huge foe, press-slamming him across the street and into a row of garbage cans! Jimmy : What strength! Now Moore feels the momentum building. He picks Kraven up and clobbers him with a big boot to the face! Moore goes for the cover! 1... 2... Fat Tony : And now it's Kraven who kicks out! Moore manages to keep the momentum on his side however, locking up with Kraven as the Tomorrow Man gets up, before hitting a bulldog. Kraven's head bounces off the concrete, and Moore adds insult to injury by stomping on Kraven's face with both boots! Jimmy : There's that vicious streak coming through again! Suddenly, we're in Anne Rice's apartment again. TB : Hey, wait a minute... it looks like they're... Tracer quickly gets up and walks to the balcony. Sure enough, Craven and Kraven are fighting a few stories below. TB : Missy, Anne, come watch this! Best seats in the... well, street, I guess. The two women head onto the balcony as well, to watch the festivities below. Jimmy : And of course, there we see the creative... Fat Tony : Don't call him a genius, he'll be even more overbearing than usual. Jimmy : The creative man behind this match, watching the last two participants duke it out twenty or so feet below him. TB : You go, Jackson! Moore picks up Kraven and goes for a Diamond Cutter, but Kraven somehow reverses it into an Atomic Drop! Craven staggers, clutching the base of his spine. The Tomorrow Man is too weak to capitalise, however, and is hit by a Lariat from the big Texan! Before he can make the pin, a smallish young man darts out of the crowd, does a double jump from the street to a trash can, the trash can to Moore's shoulders, and then jumps down, hitting a severe Ace Crusher on his way to the street below. He then covers Craven Moore, who never even saw his attacker... the ref looks confused, but makes the count anyway... 1... 2... 3!!! Jimmy : What the hell is this? Fat Tony : That kid looks familiar, but the camera is still behind Bullet... I can't tell who it is from this distance. This scrawny-looking youth then climbs a fire escape and executes apicture perfect Swanton bomb onto the fallen Jackson Kraven. Again the ref looks confused, but again he makes the count. 1... 2... 3!!! Fat Tony : What... is going... ON?!? Jimmy : Did this guy just win the Bourbon Street Brawl? TB : Not on your life, Jimmy. Jimmy : How can you hear me? TB : Live broadcast, television not more then ten feet away. Jimmy : Oh, right. Craven Moore has since gotten up and looks rather pissed off at this intrusion, and begins stalking toward the perpetrator. The unnamed guy is celebrating like there's no tomorrow, unaware of Craven's approach... or Kraven's for that matter, who has also gotten to his feet, and is sneaking up behind the young man with bad intentions. Before any felonies can go down, Tracer puts a halt to it. TB : Guys, hold it! The two giants cease their approach and look up to see Bullet for the first time... and simultaneously give him the finger. TB : Yes, I know how you feel about me. But you'll be thankful for this one thing, though... ( pointing at skinny guy ) This man is NOT the winner of the Brawl. Skinny Guy : What the hell are you talking about? I pinned them, didn't I? TB : Look Eddie, I appreciate your wanting to join this thing... but you're not a WWA wrestler. The commentators are then shown on screen. Fat Tony is beside himself. Fat Tony : Of course! That's Eddie van Dorn, the little punk kid midcarder from the FWA! Jimmy : Hasn't that been closed for over a year now? And then we're back to the "apartment" shot of the street below. TB : If you want to join up and then enter the July Rumble, that's all well and good... but I don't need you here lousing up my brainchild by interfering at the tail end of a match that these two have worked hard in all day. Eddie : F*ck you, I'm getting my title shot. According to the rules, I just earned the right to it. TB : Then let me put it this way... this is MY match, with MY rules in place, and I can alter them at any time... Tracer, knowing the quickest route is a straight line, climbs down the fire escape to the street below, the camera following him. He comes face to face with Eddie. TB : This match is going to continue with just Jackson and Craven here. And you're going to leave them alone. You're either going to do it willingly, or... Eddie : Or what, s#!tcakes?
