What this is Script of the WWA’s webcast series Underground, episode 105, staged in Richmond, Virginia, with a lumberjack match, backstage segments, a six-way elimination match for the United States title and a closing preview of the next episode.
WWA: Underground February 12, 2010 - Episode #105 Live from the Arthur Ashe Athletic Center in Richmond, VA Chapter I Lumberjack Match #7 Shaman Vs #8 Conway The show opens inside the arena, with just about every WWA wrestler standing at ringside. Jack: Ladies and gentlemen, we have an exceptional match for you tonight to open up Underground. A Lumberjack Match between WWA veteran Shaman and WWA newcomer Chris Conway. We have every WWA competitor at ringside right now, with the exception of Jaymz Watkins and Richard Cranium. Denrol: That's because they have a match coming up after this, and they need to get ready for it. Smart decision. Jack: I'm Jack Whalen, he's Denrol. Welcome to Underground. Trudeau: This match is a lumberjack match. Coming to the ring first, weighing in at three hundred and thirty pounds, hailing from Mojave, AZ, currently ranked #7, this is SHAMAN! Stillborn by Black Label Society blasts over the PA system. Shaman enters the arena amid blue and red lights, highlighting his form eerily. Shaman steps forward swinging his arms back in forth in a boxer's fashion. Shaman walks slowly towards the ring his eyes staring at nothing but the ring. His gaze as if in a trance as he makes his way to the ring. He reaches up and grabs the ropes. He stands on the outside as he pulls himself up. He then simply steps himself over the top ropes and walks to the center of the ring and looks around at the ring itself. He then raises his arms high into the air and yells as loud as he can. A war cry from the huge Native American. Jack: That is one very large man. Denrol: And very dangerous. Even with his long and proven career, people still underestimate him. Jack: Well, tonight we're going to see if one of the WWA's newest signings can go toe to toe with the big man. The lights flash yellow and green as the bombastic sound of Wagner's Ride of the Valkyries blasts out across the arena. Jack: Here he comes. The rotund form of Chris Conway emerges, decked out in crisp, white bishop's robes, complete with purple velvet panels in the front, gold chains and rings gleaming in the light, a white do-rag covering his bald head and dark sunglasses. Trudeau: Now entering the ring, he's from Spanish Town, Jamaica, weighs in at four hundred twenty-four pounds, currently ranked #8, this is CHRIS CONWAY!! Jack: What in the world? Is this Chris Conway's-- Denrol: Ice Pope. Jack: What? Denrol: Ice Pope. That's his name now. Denrol: You know, one of the perks of holding the World Title is that you don't have to do this lumberjack crap. Jack: But that's not the kind of guy Nick Adams is. He's WWA through and through. Of course, I tried to deal with both and failed. However, I certainly have seen the error in my ways and I plan on remedying that, starting tonight with Richard Cranium. Coleman: What do you have in store for Richard Cranium tonight, Jaymz? Jaymz: Now, now, Wally: that would be telling. I'd like to wax poetic on my strategy, but what if the brute was watching? Stone spins him around and climbs to the middle rope. He drills him with a right hand to the head. Since the crowd isn’t counting for him, Stone lifts up one finger. He nails him again, and holds up two fingers. A third punch and the count continues. Four… five…As Stone holds up a hand with all fingers extended, Grant grabs him around the waist and rushes forward. He runs out to the middle of the ring and drives Stone into the canvas with every ounce of force he can muster. He keeps his hands locked around RJ’s legs and folds him in half like an accordion for the cover. Jack: Running Tigerbomb out of the corner! One! Two! Three! Ding, Ding Grant falls to the side, exhausted, as the bell rings and pandemonium ensues. He got a nice ovation when he first walked into the arena, and it’s absolutely deafening now. Trudeau: Your winner of the match and NEWWWWWWWW WWA United States Champion… ‘The Lion’ John Grant! Denrol: That is the best thing I’ve seen in a WWA ring in a long time. Jack: An incredible finish to an incredible match. John Grant, in only his third WWA match out lasts five of the best this company has to offer to capture the United States championship. The referee comes to Grant holding the United States championship belt and hands it to him. Grant looks at it as if he’s unsure it’s even real. He holds it close for a moment before getting to his feet and raising it high into the air, drawing a fresh ovation. He drops to the mat and rolls out of the ring. He heads to the barricade at ringside and climbs over, quickly getting mobbed by the fans. Jack: Let the celebrating begin. A hard fought victory for that young man. He deserves this moment. As the fans surround their new champion, the show fades to black.
