'It Was Me' - Bambito Admits Guilt Pedro Pagon, the acknowledged #30 Bambito, has come forward and admitted that it was he, not Detonators member Dynamite, that swung the sledgehammer that ended up connecting with Komodo Dragon at the American Airlines Arena in Dallas, Texas this past December at Birthday Bash. ''I dressed up like Dynamite, yes,'' said Pagon to Texas authorities when he turned himself in on Sunday, clearing the way for Dynamite's return to Meltdown last night. ''I was just trying to find the belt but I saw a Havoc and thought I could help. But I didn't hit a Havoc, I hit the KaOs.'' Authorities are holding Pagon in a Dallas detention facility without bail. The judge at Pagon's hearing declared him a flight risk him full view of the court and had him remanded straight to prison. ''I will still support The Detonators,'' Pagon said, ''because I love them very much.'' More on this story as it becomes available - Pagon's trial isn't expected to start for another few weeks.
