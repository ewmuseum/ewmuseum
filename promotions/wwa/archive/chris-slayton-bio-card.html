Chris Slayton Basic Information Nicknames | The "Sandman" Real Name | Christopher Allen Slayton Height | 6'5 Weight | 265 Date of Birth | 4/18/1976 Home Town | West Lawn, PA Theme | "For whom the bell tolls" by Metallica More Details Appearance Chris is a rather sturdy man in his mid-30s with short reddish-brown hair and green eyes. He has a solid yet not too heavy build (Wade Barrett and Sheamus are examples of this build type) with a few tattoos on his body with the most proment one being a Native American "Tree of Life" which symbolizes his journey through life in black and red ink on his right forearm. He also has several scars on his body from past hardcore matches including a few deep scars from his days in the Japanese R*Y*U "Garbage Wrestling" promotion which he doesn't like talking about. Ring Attire Chris' ring gear is pretty straight forward-a black pair of cargo pants with red trim with a black button up workshirt without the sleeves and a white wife-beater tank top on underneath that and a pair of black size 14 and 1/2 Brahma SWAT boots. He also tapes up his fists with white tape. Personality Chris outside of the ring is a very easy going guy who enjoys spending time with his friends and family and while some members of his family (older siblings Marcus and Tara along with nephew Devon) try to drag family matters into the ring, Chris works hard NOT TO and downright refuses to when he can. In the ring however, Chris' mind is stuck in one direction-the fight to come. His promos and in-ring segments reflect this as well. Side Slam Backbreaker Finisher | The "Verdict" and the "Black Rain" Finisher Description For the "Verdict", Chris picks up his opponent and lifts him into a fireman's carry across his shoulders then throws his opponent's legs out in front of him while he simultaneously falls backwards, and then it drives them into a DDT or drops them onto their face and belly as a facebuster. This move is more commonly known as the "F5". The "Black Rain" on the other hand is a combination of submission hold and a suplex which is more like a sleeper suplex but instead of a sleeper hold, Chris locks in a half-nelson choke before turning it into a suplex, however instead of releasing it-Chris keeps it locked in where his opponent is either pinned with his shoulders on the mat or submits via it's submission nature or simply passes out from the sleeper-like hold.
