Saturday Night Main Event Saturday April 18, 1998 Tony : Welcome to another action packed Saturday night as we look to see who emerges to the N.C.W ranks and head for the gold...... Eric : Thats right tony we will see if Shamans quest for the gold will come to him in the coming weeks.... Bob's Driver : Yes and also we will see the debut of Big Daddy Butler... Terry : What a name Big Daddy Butler geez the guy looks like a smurf.... Johny : How about that main event Britainicca is sure to be tested tonight... Tony : Well lets get to ringside and see the first match take it away... GARY TRUDEAU STEPS INTO THE RING AND STARTS TO ANNOUNCE THE FIRST MATCH AS PLUSH BY STONE TEMPLE PILOTS STARTS TO BLAST OVER THE P.A Trudeau : Entering the ring now weighing in at 327 lbs and the height of 6' 6 1/2" Vinnie "The Bronx" Bomber! THE CROWD SCREAMS A RESPONSE AS VINNIE IS MOBBED BY FEMALE ADORING ARMS AND HE BRUSHES THEM OFF.........AS THE AMERICAN NATIONAL ANTHEM STARTS TO PLAY OVER THE P.A AS CAPTAIN AMERICA TOMMY PRIDE WALKS DOWN TO RINGSIDE..... Trudeau : And his opponent weighing in at 240 lbs and the height of 6' 6" Captain America Tommy Pride..... Tony : This should be a good match up... Terry : The bell rings and Pride takes it to Vinnie quickly... Johny : trying for the quick win i see... Eric : pride with a suplex and cover one...two.....no he didn't even have him.... Bob's Driver : Vinnie with a quick response ..WOW what a hit that looks like it rattled a few fillings.... Tony : Vinnie with a belly to back suplex, this man is showing some talent... Terry : Sure thats all he's got going for him.... Eric : Terry, shut up... Johny : Vinnie with a submission hold .... Tony : Pride to the ropes,barely escaped that one eh Eric... Eric : Thats right Tony, Pride with a drop kick but wait Vinnie caught his leg this could get ugly... Bob's Driver : Vinnie tosses him down on his head that had to hurt... Johny : Whats this The B-52 Bomber Vinnies Finisher... Tony : 1..2..3..Vinnie " the bronx" Bomber wins by pinfall this young man has a good start in his wrestling career... Johny : Lets go back down to Gary Trudeau... CAMERA FOCUSES ON TRUDEAU AS HE PREPARES TO INTRODUCE THE NEXT MATCH...... Trudeau : Our next match has a fall count of ten minutes, our first wrestler is Big Daddy Butler Weighing in at 200 lbs and at the height of 5'5"..... DONT FEAR THE REAPER BY THE BLUE OYSTER CULT PLAYS OVER THE P.A AS BUTLER APPROACHES THE RING.....WITH A MIXED CROWD REACTION...... Trudeau : And his opponent a man with anarchy and stryfe on his mind Talon weighing in at 229 lbs and at the height of 6'1".... "GANGSTA'S PARADISE" BLARES OVER THE P.A AS TALON WALKS ALONE TO RINGSIDE AMONGST A HAIL OF BOOS..... Tony : That man gets hated worse when he joins Anarchy.... Terry : they always hated him.... Bob's Driver : For just this once i'm inclined to agree with Terry... Eric : the two wrestlers lock up but look at Butler show of strength as he tosses Talon backwards... Johny : Talon stands and strikes Butler in the head with a closed fist.... Eric : I cannot believe this these two seem to want to beat each other senseless... Terry : This is wrestling not a Barbie doll convention... Tony : Be quiet Terry, Butler drives a foot to Talons mid section.... Johny : Talon with a suplex... Bob's Driver : What a show of dexterity by Talon.... Eric : Talon with a quick body slam..... Tony : Talon sending Butler into the ropes oh he slaps on the sleeper Butler struggling to get free... Johny : Its hooked in good no escaping that... Eric : Butler seems to be fading... Tony : The ref lifts his arm it drops lifts it again it drops again.... Terry : The idiots out cold call the match.... Tony : Wait Butler catches his arm and holds it up.... Eric : Butler with a quick series of elbows on Talon... Johny : Talon breaks the hold and only gets leveled by Butler... Bob's Driver : It seems that Talon is going to hurt for that decision.... Eric : Oh no Talon is being prepared for the cookie crunch.... Johny : Ouch thats it the finisher boy that looks painful.... Terry : Who would of thunk it that that midget would have won oh well....guess thats the way the cookie crumbles... Bob's Driver : Terry, shut up or I'll bring BOB out here.... Tony : Well thats a win for Butler wait thats Big Eliem coming to ringside... Johny : Eliem picks up Butler and Chokeslams him .... Eric : He is going to speak... Big Eliem : I would like to say there is only one big man in this fed and thats me.....the big man of the N.C.W... Tony : Well i think that Eliems going to have to prove that.... Johny : Lets keep rolling.... CAMERA FOCUSES ON GARY TRUDEAU AS HE PREPARES TO ANNOUNCE THE NEXT MATCH....... Trudeau : Coming to the ring weighing in at 399 lbs and the height of 7'4" is Annihalation... A GONG TOLLS THREE TIMES AS ANNIHALATION APPROACHES THE RING.... Trudeau : And his opponent weighing in at 247 lbs and the height of 6'3" The Icon Eric Stanley..... "TRIUMPH" BY THE WOO TANG CLAN BLARES OVER THE COM SYSTEM AS STANLEY RUNS TO THE RING QUICKLY........ Tony : Stanley looks to be trying to go extreme on Annihalation.... Eric : Look its a top rope plancha dive towards Annihalation... Johny : Annihalation catches him mid air ouch what a super back breaker.... Terry : Look He's Doing it again he's manhandling Stanley... Tony : Stanley rolls away slowly and starts to punch Annihalation... Johny : Thats cheating ...... Eric : It seems Annihalations partially dazed.... Terry : Stanley off the ropes and a clothesline by Annihalation levels Stanley.... Tony : And another super back breaker.... Terry : At this rate Stanley won't walk away from this match.... Bob's Driver : Stanley always whined to much he gets what he deserves... Eric : Stanley struggling to his feet, Tries to body slam Annihalation but his back gives out... Tony : Annihalations laughing and picks up Stanley and applies the super back breaker again.... Johny : Stanley rolls out of the ring in pain... Eric : Annihalation follows quickly... Johny : Stanley with a surprise attack as he drop kicks Annihalation into the ring post.... Terry : Stanley grabs Annihalations arm and tries to sling him into the post again , But Annihalation reverses it and Stanley hits the post hard.... Tony : Anniahalation with another super back breaker on Stanley... Eric : And another on Stanley this man really wants a win..... Johny : Annihalation drives Stanley to the mat and covers him for the 1...2...3.... Tony : And your winner Annihalation... CAMERA FOCUSES ON GARY TRUDEAU AS HE STANDS IN THE RING WITH SONNY LIGHTNING PACING BEHIND HIM....... Trudeau : In the ring at this time is Sonny Lightning.....and his opponent weighing in at 256 lbs and at the height of 6'5" The Untouchable Ryan Lee... "VOODOO CHILD" BY JIMI HENDRIX BLASTS OVER THE PA AS RYAN LEE HEADS FOR RINGSIDE QUICKLY...... Johny : This match shows promise... Tony : I'll say that again Lightning and Lee lock up.... Eric : Lightning with an armbar.... Terry : Lee With an armbar take down as he reverse the armbar... Johny : Lightning pulls something out of his belt it looks like a patch of salt... Eric : Lee grabs it and throws it back into Lightnings face thats it its over.... Tony : Lightning runs around the ring blinded by his own salt.... Johny : Lee levels lightning with a bunch of clotheslines..... Eric : Lee with an ankle lock and Lightning is tapping out.... Tony : Well there you have it folks Ryan Lee is the winner .... Johny : Down to you Gary... CAMERA PANS WIDE TO SEE GARY IN THE RING READY TO ANNOUNCE ANOTHER MATCH AS JAMES ROYAL STANDS BEHIND HIM...... Trudeau : The man behind me is James Royal and his opponent is weighing in at 287 lbs and at the height of 6'3" The Psychotic.... WELCOME TO THE JUNGLE BY GUNS AND ROSES BLASTS OVER THE PA AS THE PSYCHOTIC COMES TO RINGSIDE.... Terry : Who let this guy out I bet he was shacked up with Griffon... Bob's Driver : Why don't you ask Griffon when he comes out.... Tony : There's the bell the Psychotic is taking it to Royal hard... Johny : Yes he is Tony, ouch an inverted atomic drop... Tony : Royals holding his family jewels... Terry : More like shattered glass now.... Eric : Royal with a russian leg sweep.... Tony : Psychotic lands on his head hard.... Johny : The Psychotic is laughing as Royal kicks him this mans insane... Terry : Hence the name Psychotic hello.... Eric : Royal puts him in a head lock... Tony : Psychotic picks him up and atomic drops him hard.... Johny : Psychotic sends him into the ropes for a clothesline..... Eric : Wait look its the ddt ....... Tony : Whats Psychotic doing walking around him for.... Johny : Psychotic drops and rolls him up for the 1..2..3.. Bob's Driver : And the winner is The Psychotic..... CAMERA FADES IN TO HEARING SIRENS GOING OFF AS FIDEL ENTERS THE RING...... CAMERA FADES IN TO SEE A MAN RUNNING FROM BEHIND THE CURTAINS AND ATTACKS FIDEL ITS CAPITAL.......... Tony : Well it seems Fidel has angered someone else tonight.... Terry : Capital is taking it to him... Eric : This is an all out brawl... Terry : Fidel seems to finally get his bearings and he's fighting back.... Johny : Fidel with a scoop slam on Capital.... Eric : Fidel attempts an elbow drop but Capital rolls out of the way.... Bob's Driver : Look Simons finally made it to ringside.... Terry : Ooh yippee clowns...that manager should be shot.... Tony : Shut up Terry... Terry : Make me... Bob's Driver : I will Terry. Just sign the dotted line for a match in the ring.... Johny : Fidel with a dropkick and he misses.... Eric : Capital with a series of right hands and a clothesline to down Fidel... Tony : Capital with a belly to back suplex..... Terry : Capital with his arms out this could mean only one thing.... Johny : Applies the capital punishment and brings the man down hard..... Eric : Covers for the 1..2..3..and your winner Capital..... CAMERA FADES TO SEE GARY TRUDEAU READYING HIMSELF TO MAKE HIS NEXT INTRODUCTION AS THE NEW REGULATORS TAKE RINGSIDE SEATS..... Trudeau : Introducing first The Mysterious Raven.... STINGS THEME MUSIC COMES ON THE PA AS THE MYSTERIOUS RAVEN WALKS TO THE RING..... Trudeau : And his opponent The Master of the Thunderclap SHAMAN...... THUNDER ROLLS IN THE BACKGROUND THEN BEING REPLACED BY DEVIL MAN BY WHITE ZOMBIE AS SHAMAN ENTERS AS THE CROWD CHEERS WILDLY........ Tony : Shaman releases a fury on Raven at which i'm sure Raven has never seen... Terry : Ouch Shaman just slammed Raven outside the ring to the floor... Eric : Shaman follows raven out of the ring and slams him into the ring post and then pushes him back into the ring... Johny : Shaman had a mishap a few weeks back when Jack attacked Kue and cost him his win... Bob's Driver : Well we know who should have won that one.... Tony : Shaman grabs Raven and powerslams him not once but twice.... Eric : Look he is setting him up for the thunder clap.... Johny : WOW no matter how many times i say it that moves devastating.... Tony : Shaman with the 1....2....3....and your winner is Shaman.... CAMERA ZOOMS IN FOR THE MAIN EVENT AS GARY TRUDEAU READY"S THE MIKE AS COMMISIONER HARRINGTON WALKS DOWN TO RINGSIDE.... Harrington : Well it seems Zack "The Enforcer" Crocket will not be able to make it to ringside tonight. So i have made this .... CAMERA ZOOMS IN ON BRITAINICCA AS HE WALKS DOWN TO THE RING AND STANDS IN FRONT OF HARRINGTON...... Britainicca : Well I win by forfeit well that was an easy card.... Harrington : Not so fast Britainicca , you must defend your title tonight against The Outlaw.... Britainicca : You can't do this I'm champion... Harrington : If you don't defend the belt will be awarded to Outlaw.... Britainicca : Fine bring him on for I will win anyways.... CAMERA FOCUSES AS OUTLAW WALKS TO RINGSIDE WITH NO MUSIC JUST MEANING BUSINESS..... Tony : This could mean trouble for Britainicca Outlaw was New England champion for a while and he knows what its like to wear gold... Terry : Go for it Outlaw... Johny : Outlaw slaps Britainicca and Britainicca just looks at him defiantly... Eric : Britainicca slaps him back.... Tony : Outlaw nails Britainicca with a lariot... Johny : Outlaw drops the elbow but Britainicca rolls away... Eric : Britainicca with a drop kick.... Tony : Outlaw drops to the mat hard but he is getting up.... Johny : Outlaw nails Britainicca with a right cross and a clothesline .... Eric : Outlaw with a piledriver and a roll up 1..2...Britainicca kicks out... Tony : That was close for Britainicca... Johny : Yeah maybe to close for the champ.... Eric : Britainicca is sent to the ropes and clotheslined by Outlaw.... Bob's Driver : Wake me when Terry quits drooling on himself...
