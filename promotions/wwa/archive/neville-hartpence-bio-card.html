Neville Hartpence Basic Information Nicknames Real Name | John Eastwood Height | 6'2" Weight | 240 Date of Birth | 6/17/1988 Home Town | Grand Rapids, Michigan Theme | "Uncharted" by Sara Bareilles More Details Appearance Short blonde hair with natural black highlights, a rectangular face shape, pointed nose, medium-sized lips, brown eyes set evenly apart from the bridge of his nose. Athletic body with toned musculature but nothing with much bulk to it. Skin has a light olive complexion. Ring Attire Full-length tights with a black and blue checkerboard pattern and black boots. His out-of-ring attire tends to be leather dress shoes, slacks, and either a button-up long sleeve or polo-style shirt, depending on the weather, with the top button on either style of shirt undone. Personality He's genuinely a good guy, and is approachable, for the most part. He'll try to resolve conflicts through discussion before going in to physical confrontation, gives to charity in both time and money, forgives easily, and believes people's motives to be good until proven otherwise. He'd probably meet the Devil the first time and think that Satan was joking about all of the evil plots. Neville is something of a predictable personality, and generally doesn't have a villainous bone in his body. He'll go as far as he needs to to get a victory, but won't even bend the rules to do so. He won't attack anybody backstage, or even outside of a match, he won't use weapons, and refuses to perform in hardcore matches. History Due to the fact that Hartpence is the definition of nondescript, most people would say he would make the perfect spy. The only conflicting evidence - his hair, which somehow has natural black highlights among otherwise blonde coloring. This oddly specific feature managed to disqualify him from CIA training after university, leading him to pick up odd jobs here and there. A conversation with a professional wrestler while working as a bar tender led him to consider a career in the field, and after three years working for independent promotions, he signed with the WWA. His focus on clean, wholesome family entertainment in the modern wrestling world seems to have limited his career, given that even the best of "good guy" wrestlers these days either participate in hardcore matches, but somehow, it's managed to guarantee a steady paycheck. He's known to put on decent to good straight wrestling matches, and in spite of being the typical "smiling good guy" that tends to be eventual death among crowds, it's managed to work for him due to his average appearance. Somehow, it has made the crowd sympathize with him, possibly because the average Joe wants to see himself as a good guy.
