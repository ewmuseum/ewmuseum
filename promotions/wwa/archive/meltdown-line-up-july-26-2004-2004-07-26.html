Meltdown Line-up - July 26, 2004

26 July 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A Meltdown preview and card for Monday 26th July 2004 live from Miami, headlined by a World Title League match, with a rookie-on-a-pole match and a six-man tag.
Live from Airlines Arena Miami, Florida Monday, 26th July 2004 THE WORLD TITLE LEAGUE HAS BEGUN! On last weeks Meltdown, Shaman and Tracer Bullet collided in the first match of the new World Title League. This week, two recent No. 1 contenders lock horns. Rocky Adams, winner of the Bourbon Street Brawl will clash with Quietus, winner of the Independence day Rumble. It is the chance for both of these young phenoms to shine and prove to the WWA fans that they have what it takes to grab the Gold. But who will walk out on top on Monday? The Intercontinental Champion Mal Somers will face Odium – the man who has abducted his beloved Rookie. Mal walked away with a victory at Independence Day Rumble, but will it be the same on Monday night? More importantly for him, will he be any closer to retrieving his lost belt/friend? Tommy Starr was forcibly removed from the Independence Day Rumble by Jimmi Zion. On Monday night, he will have the opportunity to settle the score. Not only that, but The Veteranz will team up with Nick Adams to take on Genesis and returning LDA rookie Jack Griffiths. Can Genesis prove their dominance? Or will the former tag champs and former TV champ get the victory? We shall also see the return to Meltdown of Jim Black! He came close to winning the Independence Day Rumble. But does he have what it takes to beat one of X Corps finest when he takes on Chance? All this and more Monday Night on Meltdown!
