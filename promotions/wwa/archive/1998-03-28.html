WWA — 28 Mar 1998

28 Mar 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Saturday Night Main Event Saturday March 28, 1998 Camera focusses in in the four announcers for the first Saturday Night Main Event Terry : Well not a bad turn out for this... Tony : not bad at all... Bob's Driver : Lets get on with it. Eric : Welcome to Saturday Night Main Event folks, This should be an action packed night... Tony : Thats right we have two new comers to the fed going at it. It seems that these two have a short fuse... Terry : How can any respectable guy call himself the bronx bomber... Eric : Or how about a guy calling himself Outlaw... Terry : Watch it Eric i'll call them out after you... Tony : Well lets go to Gary Trudeau. THE CAMERA ZOOMS IN ON GARY TRUDEAU.... TONIGHTS FIRST MATCH OF THE EVENING IS WEIGHING IN AT 327 lbs IS VINNIE " THE BRONX " BOMBER... PLUSH BY THE STONE TEMPLE PILOTS BLARES OVER THE P.A AS VINNIE AND HIS MANAGER WALK TO RINGSIDE WITH A DECENT CROWD REACTION...... AND HIS OPPONENT WEIGHING IN AT 256 lbs IS THE UNTOUCHABLE RYAN LEE.... THE ROOM BLARES WITH THE MUSIC OF JIMI HENDRIX'S VOO DOO CHILD AS ERIC STANLEY WALKS RINGSIDE WITH RYAN LEE....... Tony : Wow not even a crowd response for Ryan Lee... Terry : Ryan Lee attacks Vinnie with a couple of right crosses. Eric : But Vinnie retaliates with a series of clotheslines... Bob's Driver : Vinnie definitly has the size advantage on Ryan Lee. Tony : Lee Takes down Vinnie with a russian leg sweep... Eric : Lee applies a head lock on vinnie.... Terry : Vinnie grabs ryans hair ...hey thats cheating... Tony : Like The House of Pain doesn't cheat... Terry : No they don't! Eric : Vinnie with the advantage he has ryan lee up in a full nelson... Tony : Man thats got to hurt... Terry : Wow he's swinging him around hard.... Tony : But Lee isn't giving up.... Eric : Vinnie drops Lee and begins pummeling him.... Bob's Driver : Vinnie puts Lee in a form of a stretch what the heck is that... Tony : Looks like some sort of back breaker... Terry : Remind me to not make Vinnie mad... Tony : Sure Terry...Vinnie pulls up Ryan Lee and Body slams him... Eric : Lee fights back but seems dazed.... Tony : Vinnie with a double axe handle to the back of the head... Terry : Look at Lee drop... Tony : So i have noticed.... Eric : Here it comes Vinnie's Finisher The B-52 Bomber... Tony : Ouch that looked like it hurt.... Terry : 1...2...3... Vinnie" the bronx" Bomber gets the win... Eric : Whats this Eric Stanley has just entered the ring , he's charging Vinnie... Tony : Vinnie quickly clotheslines Stanley... Bob's Driver : Ouch Vinnie just applied his other finisher on Stanley and its called The Bronx Bomb..... Eric : Vinnie leaves the ring victourious with his manager beside him... Tony what a match, Lets go to the next one.... Terry : Down to Gary Trudeau.... OUR NEXT MATCH IS A TEN MINUTE TIME LIMIT MAKING HIS WAY TO THE RING IS GUTTER WEIGHING IN AT 317lbs.. GUTTER BALLET BY SAVAGE BLASTS OVER THE P.A AS GUTTER ENTERS WITH A SLIGHT CROWD REACTION... AND HIS OPPONENT FROM THE HOUSE OF PAIN ARIC CROCKET WEIGHING IN AT 263lbs.... HEY MAN NICE SHOT BY FILTER PLAYS AS ARIC CROPCKET WALKS DOWN TO RINGSIDE TO AN ENCOURE OF BOOS....... Terry : Man does Gutter ever bathe.. Bob's Driver : Shut up Terry... Tony : Gutter takes off. He is doing a plancha dive off the top rope outside the ring.... Eric : Crocket steps aside , Gutter hits the railing... Tony : Gutter is still moving slowly, Crocket grabs him and gets him in the ring.... Terry : Crocket is battering Gutter with a series of kicks and chops... Eric : I think Gutter is suffering for his mistakes.... Bob's Driver : Gutter grabs Crockets leg... Terry : Hey he's biting Aric, is that sanitary, has he even had his rabie shots... Tony : Crocket kicks him in the head with his free leg... Eric : Crocket Applies his finisher "The Punisher"... Terry : Gutter looks to be in extreme pain... Bob's Driver : Gutter gives up, Crocket with the win... Terry : Well that was interesting grunge against ego... Tony : Well onto our next match... GARY TRUDEAU IS STANDING IN THE RING PREPARING TO ANNOUNCE.... OUR NEXT MATCH IS SET WITH A NO TIME LIMIT ITS A GRUDGE MATCH.... WEIGHING IN AT 240lbs WILD THING CHRIS ROGERS..... SEVEN CHURCH BELLS GONG OVER THE P.A AS ROGERS WALKS TO THE RING AMONGST A HAILING OF BOO"S AND HISSES..... AND HIS OPPONENT WEIGHING IN AT 310lbs THE MASTER OF THE THUNDERCLAP SHAMAN THE CROWD GOES WILD AS THEY HEAR THUNDER OVER THE P.A AND DEVIL MAN BY WHITE ZOMBIE BLARES, AS SHAMAN WALKS DOWN WITH THE NEW REGULATORS...... THE NEW REGULATORS TAKE A SEAT IN THE FRONT ROW AND SHAMAN STEPS INTO THE RING....... Terry : Wheres that sasquatch Mad Coil he's not at ringside with the rest of the Regulators... Tony : I'm sure he isn't there for good reason.... Bob's Driver : Keep Saying things about him Terry I will have you two meet.... Eric : Shaman wastes no time. He is beating Chris Rogers.... Tony : Shaman powerslams Chris Rogers onto the floor from the ring .... Terry : Oh No an enraged indian again... Eric : I believe this is a massive grudge match... Bob's Driver : I wouldn't just say grudge... Terry : Shaman steps outside the ring and slams Rogers into the ring post..... Tony : That looks like it may have broke Rogers arm..... Eric : Shaman guerrilla presses Rogers back into the ring... Bob's Driver : Shaman grabs Rogers and slams him several times on his arm.... Terry : Rogers is begging Shaman to stop ... Eric : Look at Rogers arm its just hanging there limp, Shaman kicks him and stomps him down.... Tony : Expect no mercy from Shaman..... Terry : Shaman does his finisher...the thunder clap Tony : Ouch that looks painful, Wait he's doing it again..... Eric : thats twice the thunder clap was applied.... Bob's Driver : What he's doing it again..... Eric : Rogers must have really ticked Shaman off.... Tony : Shaman finally rolls the motionless form of Rogers over for the 1.2.3 count its over Shaman wins.... Eric : Shaman goes back to kicking and stomping Rogers.... Terry : Griffin and Britainicca go into the ring to calm down Shaman... Tony : Who's this , its Kue coming down to ringside... Bob's Driver : Look who's coming out of the crowd behind him.... Terry : Its Mad Coil, oh no get out of there Kue... Tony : Mad Coil just leveled Kue, Hey its Big Eliem behind Mad Coil... Terry : Mad Coils Gonna get it now... Bob's Driver : Big Eliem hits Mad Coil... Eric : It doesn't phase Mad Coil , her turns around there face to face.... Tony : Big Eliems surrounded by The New Regulators...... Eric : Lets go to the interview platform with Johny Morehouse.... CAMERA FOCUSES ON AN INTERVIEW STAGE AS MOREHOUSE AWAITS THE CROWD TO QUIET DOWN..... Johny : Thank you Eric, and good evening N.C.W faithful my interview tonight is with Apocalypse.. Apocalypse : Shut up Morehouse i'm sick of your ranting and raving.... Johny : Hey now... Apocalypse : First off I beat Darken and I will take his horsemen apart to... Johny : Please save us your etiquette.. Apocalypse : Thats it Morehouse you were warned... LIGHTS GO OUT AS WE SEE FOUR BOLTS OF LIGHTENING AND WHEN THE LIGHTS COME BACK ON WE SEE THE FOUR HORSEMEN.... Darken : BOY! I do not know what type of sham your attempting, but come after us you will be destroyed... Steihl : You have one chance to run now ....GO.... Apocalypse runs back to the locker rooms.... Darken : See he's not the true Apocalypse... He wouldn'y even face his own horsemen... Steihl : He's a child playing at a mans game.... Johny: Well it seems Apocalypse has bitten off more than he can chew.... For my next interview I will bring down BIG DADDY BUTLER.... Big Daddy Butler : Hello Johny, its great to be here in the NCW, let me address Bob... CAMERA ZOOMS IN TO SEE BOB WALKING TO THE STAGE.... Bob : Here have cookie, BOB like cookie.... Big Daddy Butler : I don't want no stupid cookie... Butler grabs the cookie and crushes it and throws it back at BOB.... Bob : You hurt cookie , Now BOB hurt you... Big Daddy Butler : Get away from me you freak... Bob : BOB is not freak, BOB is BOB... Johny : Gentlemen behave yourselves... Big Daddy Butler : Get away from me , you idiot.... Bob : BOB step on you like bug... CAMERA FOCUSES AS BOB CHASES BIG DADDY BUTLER TO THE LOCKER ROOMS...... Johny : Well take it away Tony..... CAMERA FOCUSES BACK TO THE ANNOUNCERS BOOTH.... Tony : Thank you Johny... Terry : He seems to be chummy for a man beaten by Anarchy.... Bob's Driver : I bet he could beat the crap out of you... Eric : What do you have to say for yourself Terry.... Terry : Well how about that main event... Bob's Driver : I thought so.... Tony : Now lets go to Gary for tonights main event... CAMERA FOCUSES ON GARY TRUDEAU.... LADIES AND GENTLEMEN TONIGHTS BOUT IS A NO TIME LIMIT TITLE DEFENSE, FIRST THE CHALLENGER WEIGHING IN AT 233lbs IS JAK " THE BOIL " LANCER..... THE PSYCHO THEME MUSIC BLARES OVER THE P.A AS LANCER WALKS RINGSIDE WITH A MINIMAL AMOUNT OF BOO'S.... AND THE NORTH EASTERN HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION WEIGHING IN AT 235lbs NIKEDEMOUS SIEGER..... TUBTHUMPING BLASTS OVER THE P.A AS NIKEDEMOUS WALKS TO RING SIDE WITH A MIXED CROWD REACTION...... Tony : This should prove to be a good match... Terry : Yes a House of Pain win and another return for the championship... Eric : Seiger and Lancer lock up Lancer with Seiger to the ropes for a clothesline... Tony : Seiger with a leg to the midsection.... Terry : Seiger with a russian leg sweep... Eric : Seiger just slapped Lancer in the back of the head... Tony : Lancer is up and hits Seiger with a flurry of rights and lefts... Terry : Look who's coming ringside its Randy Cross... Eric : Seiger turns and points at Cross and motions him to leave... Tony : Lancer nails Seiger from behind with a clothesline.... Terry : He's applying his finisher the reverse grapevine.... Bob's Driver : Seigers not giving up he's struggling to get to the rope... Eric : Seigers reached the ropes the ref calls off Lancer..... Tony : Lancer backs off as Seiger catches his breath.... Eric : Lancer Body slams Seiger and look Seiger is getting his second wind... Tony : Seigers pummeling Lancer... Terry : Man who gave that guy some sugar... Eric : Seigers got Lancer down here comes his finisher... Bob's Driver : Its the Seiger stretch.... Tony : Lancers struggling and thrashing to get towards the ropes..... Terry : Look he's not giving either... Eric : Lancers made the ropes.... Tony : The ref calls off Seiger and makes him break the hold... Seiger is back into Lancer again their exchanging blows , man this could go on for hours.... Terry : Great and i'm gonna miss BayWatch.... Eric : Terry thats as close to a real woman you could get... Tony : Seiger and Lancer both into the ropes they kris cross the ring and oh my double clothes line... Terry : Both men look exhausted... Eric : Look Seiger flops his arm over Lancer , I don't think its an intentional cover... Tony : well the ref thinks so he's counting 1....2....3.....Nikedemous Seiger successfully defends his belt... Eric : Thats all the time we have tonight see you on next Saturday and some will see Johny,Terry and Tony on Wednesday and Friday......
