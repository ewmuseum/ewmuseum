The Scouting Report - Issue 5

28 Nov 2003

Bylined Joe Stock - Director of Talent, in the fed's own kayfabe voice.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hello everyone and welcome to a late and short edition of TSR. I plan to have more news next week, but here's what I do have. -The WWA has signed two new wrestlers. The first one is Chance, a one of a king character whom also looks to have good promo skills. While he wanted at first to be a 'Tweener, I convinced him his character would work great as a face. Should be fun to watch him develop. -The other is Nicholas Adams. While not as unique as Chance, he's still a very solid worker and has a bright future ahead of him. -Looking ahead, there is a talent I hope to sign soon. He also is a one of a kind character, someone who is trying to solve a mystery. Watch for this guy very soon. -I have yet to receive a demo from a single tag team yet since I became Talent Director. What's going on? -Some sad news to report. WWA's sister federation the Euro-American Wrestling Alliance has closed it's doors. Some of the former members may be seen in the WWA very soon, if their demo tapes show they have what it takes. On this note, the WWA is looking to have a developmental promotion to help us out. Our roster is approaching full, but I wouldn't want to turn away the good wrestlers that apply. -As I stated, our roster is getting full. We have room only for about two more singles. Tag teams on the other hand are another story. We're looking for three or four good teams. -The WWA has released both the Eliminators & James Williams. We wish all three men good luck in the future. -Se7en has no resigned with the WWA, stating a wish to work in other fields. We'll miss the guy and he knows the door is open for his return. -On the medical side, Troy Walcott's recovery is coming well and I know he can't wait to get back in the ring. I expect him to feud with Ian Tense to settle The Frat breakup. -Venny LeTrav has seen doctors in England about some ribs on his right side that have been hurting. The doctors have cleared him to wrestle for Labor Day Brawl, but afterword to give his body some rest. The rookie is planning to move into the Cruiserweight Division I'm told. -Ian Tense will fight at LDB, through doctors are not recommending it. While I think we got an intense feud here, Tense should be more worried about his body. He's young and wrecking it early can make him just another forgotten flash in the pan. -It's great to see Rick Venom back. He was injuried while doing a run-in during the Human Condition/Havoc match. He requested the injury to be kept quiet until he was cleared. Grunge and him look forward to their match at LDB. -I won't run down Meltdown like I usually do, but overall it was a good show to hype the PPV. Everyone gave an A+ effort. -For those who are wondering, I'm told the WWA Weekly is on hold to resolve some technical problems. It's a good magazine that really helps the fans get into WWA action. -On the "If you are curious note" I just saw Grunge play with his band Sea Prism live. They're not too bad, better than Fozzy for sure. I'm told Grunge's wife Julie is the manager and they just finished recording an album to be released soon. It should be on stores shelves in a few weeks. -Nearly the entire WWA roster flew home on Thursday (It's somewhat well known that Fiesta is taped on Tuesdays.) I was there and unlike another federation, we had no problems. It's good to know our roster gets along. That's all for now. A much better edition next week, I promise. Including my thoughts on the LDB lineup. From underneath the New York Yankees ballcap, this is Joe Stock signing out.
