TJ is on his knees in pain, looks to Captain Amazing and mouths the word "Why?" In response, Captain Amazing removes his mask, showing him to be... Daniel E. Nelson! Nelson dropkicks TJ right in the face. Fat Tony : Perfect! What a trap. Jimmy : Wait, if Captain Amazing is Nelson, then Villain... As if in answer, Villain gives TJ Kryponite as the crowd starts getting pissed and throwing trash into the ring. Continuing the carnage, Randy Cross puts the figure four leglock on TJ and Ian Kurt stomps away at TJ's chest and face. But TJ isn't defending much anymore. At Ian Kurt's urging, Nelson and Cross hold TJ up while Villain lines up to deliver a huge haymaker. Jimmy : This has got to stop. Would someone come here and stop this? Suddenly, Lois steps in between TJ and Villain. She shields TJ and starts to beg for Villain to stop the assault. Jimmy : No! Lois, get out of there! Somebody has to save her! Villain walks up to Lois, staring her right in the eye and gleaming his sharp toothed smile. She shrinks back from the man who had been terrorizing her for the last month. Summoning a last drop of courage, she pulls back and tries to slap him. Unfortunately, Villain catches her hand, and his sharp toothed smile becomes a low rabid cackle. Jimmy : Let go of her, you monster! And he does. Villain releases Lois, and keeping his eyes locked on hers, points at the bleeding TJ Donaldson. In a sickening metamorphosis, Lois' face changes from an expression of pure terror to an expression of pure pleasure. She turns around and slaps TJ right in the face. Jimmy : No! This is sick!
