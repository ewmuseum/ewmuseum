The Gamblers Table — Independence Day Rumble 2002

16 Aug 2003

Bylined "The Gambler" (Kevin/Vic Sage), the fed's own in-house prediction columnist persona.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hello to all of the WWA faithful. This is “The Gambler” Kevin Sage with my picks for the Independence Day Rumble 2002. As a former Las Vegas bookie, I’ll be sharing my thoughts on the matchups as well my picks. These are the bets that I’ll be making as the entire world watches the Pay-Per-View. Of course, don’t hold me responsible for any wagers that you may make. Unless, of course, you win a large sum of cash, then I’ll be expecting a little kickback. Without further ado, let’s take a closer look at the Independence Day Rumble which will be live from the Fleet Center in Boston on Sunday, July 7th! James Williams vs. Venny LeTrav These two have been slinging insults and threats back and forth since their mutual debut in the WWA ended in a time limit draw. Now these two competitors have the opportunity to prove their mettle in a hardcore rules matchup. This battle between a Texas native and a child of the Bayou should be nothing short of spectacular. Orion’s strength and surprising quickness would certainly seem to overshadow Venny’s in-ring skills and extensive martial arts background, but something in my gut tells me otherwise. I learned long ago to listen to that little voice that whispers in my ear, and this time it’s telling me to go with Venny LeTrav. Se7en vs. "Mighty" Joe Keane This Fiesta Bowl qualifier promises to be a great matchup. We have Se7en, the quintessential villian, against "Mighty" Joe Keane, the superhero to the common man. However, the rumor mill says that "Mighty" Joe may not be able to compete at IDR due to injuries inflicted by Jackson Kraven at the Bourbon Street Brawl. If it's true, then Se7en may find himself with a free ride to the final match of the Fiesta Bowl, or he may be facing an unknown substitute. Maybe it's the kid in me that wants to believe in real heroes, but I have faith in "Mighty" Joe Keane to go through with the match, despite his injuries. Unfortunately, reality rears its ugly head. Joe's indomitable spirit isn't likely to be enough to defeat a man like Se7en in his current condition. The safe bet goes to Se7en. Wes Sanders vs. "Hurricane" Barrett Sinclair Both of these men are incredible athletes with bright futures in the WWA. Barrett Sinclair has displayed his impressive technical skills in the ring at Fiesta and his reputation from other federations has preceded him. A good friendship with Vik Ventressa never hurts either. In the other corner, we have the mysterious Wes Sanders. We know that Sanders has competed in the WWA before, but he wore a mask at that time. Who could he be? Where can we find such a skilled aerialist and martial artist who once wore a mask? We hope that these questions will be answered at IDR and I'm sure that many will be surprised. Although I don't have the answer to his identity, I have seen his incredible talent in the ring. That's why I'm putting my money on Wes Sanders. Fiesta Bowl Final The two men who have earned their places in the Fiesta Bowl final will compete for a $10,000 purse and a shot at the TV title on the following week's Fiesta. I'm sure that both men will work hard for this prestigious honor. Assuming that my predictions in the semi-finals come to fruition, we'll be looking at Se7en vs. Wes Sanders. What a wonderful contrast of styles. Se7en is a big powerhouse with a vicious streak and Wes Sanders is one of the quickest and most skilled combatants in the WWA today. After Se7en's victory over Ekosyde in a graveyard match on Fiesta, Ekosyde is now forced to serve as one of Se7en's soldiers. I think that will be the critical detail here. I can't see a fair match coming out of this when Ekosyde is nearby, therefore I reluctantly have to predict a victory, and a TV Title shot, for Se7en. Tag Team Champions The Horsemen vs. Havoc vs. Amazingly Simple vs. Extreme Impact The last time that the Horsemen and Havoc met, the Horsemen won the Tag Team belts and revealed their true identities. Since that time, the animosity between these two teams has grown to epic proportions. As if that weren’t enough to make this an incredible match by itself, we have the return of one of the most successful tag teams in WWA history, Amazingly Simple. And there’s still more to turn this into a contender for match of the year with the inclusion of the rookie team, Extreme Impact, who’s showing in the qualifying match at Fiesta showed that these two have what it takes to go far in the WWA tag ranks. Nonetheless, they are a rookie team and unproven. I just can’t see them taking the gold against the other teams involved. Despite TJ Donaldson's defeat of the Job Squad on Meltdown, Amazingly Simple may not be the best bet. One has to wonder if Captain Amazing will return for IDR, and if he does, how much ring rust can we expect to see? The smallest misstep or hesitation will cause their chances to come crashing down. Of course, if Amazing doesn't show up for IDR, then it's a moot point anyway. When it comes down to it, the real question lies in whether the experience of Shaman/Famine and Magician/Pestilence is enough to overcome the rage of Havoc. I say it can. My money is on the Horsemen to retain the titles. The Independence Day Rumble Around thirty athletes from the WWA roster step into the ring, separated by two-minute intervals, for a shot at the World Championship title at the Labor Day Brawl. Elimination occurs by being tossed over the top rope to the floor below. There are so many great competitors in this thing, Ian Tense, Troy Walcott, Wes Sanders, any of the Four Horsemen, EVD, Tracer Bullet, Shane Dorion, Michael Stryfe, Ray Wilson, Damian Thorne, Grunge, Harley Trapper, and all of the other incredible talents in the WWA. Any one of them, far too many to list in full, could win with a lucky placement draw. This could easily all boil down to the order in which the wrestlers appear. Last year, Shaman drew the last lot and came to the ring to claim his victory unopposed due to the dual elimination of the last two men, Trent LaBeaux and Kahn. Somehow, I don’t think that we’ll see a repeat of that kind of luck this year. When all is said and done, the intensity and determination of the current US Champion, Ian Tense, may prove to be enough to outlast the entire roster and then some. My advice is to bet on the "Prototype" Ian Tense. Cruiserweight Champion Tracer Bullet vs. Denrol Two legends of the sport and world renowned superstars face off in a "last call" match. With a fully-stocked bar and bartender at ringside, the competitors must take a drink every ninety seconds. The winner is determined by pinfall, submission, or failing to make it to the bar by the count of ten. Yes, ladies and gentlemen, it's the WWA drinking game! Denrol is among the most versatile and talented wrestlers ever to step foot in the WWA, and this match is his own brainchild, but Tracer Bullet is...well...a lush. The longer the match drags on, the more advantage goes to the man from Bourbon Street. Since I don't foresee Denrol getting an early pin in this drunken contest, I have to go with the man who has returned to the name Tracer Bullet as the swaggering victor. Of course, I would hate to be the guy who has to clean up if either man proves unable to hold their liquor. World Heavyweight Champion Craven Moore vs. Jackson Kraven Craven Moore’s victory over Jackson Kraven at Mayhem was nothing short of amazing. Craven Moore solidified his standing with the fans by bringing honor to the WWA World Championship belt. But can this seven foot, 390 pound Texan cope with the unmitigated rage and brutality of Jackson Kraven a second time? These two mountainous competitors have been at each other's throats throughout their long careers. Jackson Kraven's desire for the gold, coupled with his thirst for revenge, will mean that Jackson comes into this match at the top of his game. Jackson even insured that Moore would continue to hold the belt by helping him pin Joe Keane on Meltdown. That's how badly Kraven wants this rematch with Moore. Craven Moore's hatred for Jackson is just as intense, and his desire to prove himself the better champion is genuine. I wouldn't miss this match for the world. Regardless of the outcome, this match will be bloody. Craven Moore is an incredible powerhouse who never lost a step while in Hollywood. On the other hand, Jackson Kraven has never been hungrier and more desperate than he is now. I believe that The Tomorrow Man has what it takes to reclaim the World Heavyweight Championship in what will ultimately be a legendary battle. If you need any reason to justify ordering the Independence Day Rumble, this is it. That's all for this edition of The Gambler's Table, but what a night the Rumble is going to be. I will return in late August with my picks and pans for the Labor Day Brawl. Until then, remember that a gambler never makes the same mistake twice. It's usually three or more times.
