He looks over the edge, gulping at the prospect of a 15-foot drop, and almost loses his footing. Fat Tony: What the… Jimmy: Senior Referee Rick Kilgore has had enough! He’s come to restore some sanity to this match! Everyone in the Arena: HOLY SH*T! Jimmy: Dammit… not like that… Both men lie, completely motionless, in the centre of the ring. The referee, overcoming the initial shock, suddenly realises where he is. Just like that, all of his apparent courage withers away. Jimmy: …LOOK! In the middle of the ring, there is a slight movement… and Mal Somers’ hand lands on Harley’s chest! However, the referee is facing away from the ring, slowly trying to edge himself back down. Jimmy: There’s a cover in the ring! Make the count!! Fat Tony: You just take your time there-- Jimmy: MAKE THE COUNT! WE’VE GOT A NEW CHAMPION! Hearing the huge level of noise from the crowd, the referee drops to a hanging position from the top of the cage, closing his eyes and letting himself drop back to the mat. Finally on level ground, he whips around, dropping as fast as he can to do his job. Jimmy, Fat Tony, the crowd, and the millions watching at home: ONE! TWO! THREE! Total… And utter… Silence. Harley appears to have used up his last bit of strength just getting his shoulder up, and Somers rolls onto his back, staring up at the lights. Jimmy: They’re both totally out of it. Folks, we are out of time, and we will have news on the status of Mal Somers as soon as it comes to hand.
