Ring Attire White scrub pants, no shirt, and black boots. Personality Eugene is a loner. History Growing up in Atlanta, Georgia Eugene King was always the odd one. He was much larger than his peers and therefore for was always picked on, most notably being called a 'freak'. While nobody dared mess with the man physically, the verbal abuse caused emotional distress, sending a then fourteen year old Eugene to go off the handle so to speak. His binge of antics landed him in a hospital for the mentally ill where he stayed until he reached the age of twenty-one. Since his release, King has worked odd and end jobs trying to make a living. His main focus has been remembering to take his pills to ensure he stays in a calm state of mind. While his life has been going nowhere, Eugene came up with the idea to pursue wrestling in an effort to make a change in his life and to find a medium to channel is anger.
