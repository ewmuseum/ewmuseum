What this is Roster bio card for the WWA wrestler Lockdown, giving height, weight, birth date, hometown, entrance music, physical appearance, ring attire and personality.
Biography Wrestler's Name: Lockdown Height: 6'4" Weight: 280 lbs Date Of Birth: 12/04/75 Hometown: Chicago, Illinois Entrance Music: No Remorse by Metallica Wrestler's Quote: Physical Appearance: Lockdown is extremely muscular, and very cut. His chest and arms are gigantic, his neck is thick and his abs are ripped. Lockdown has a tough square jaw, but not to the point that he looks like a neanderthal. On his upper right arm, just a few inches below his shoulder, he has a tattoo that reads,"CFH." in the center of his back is a very large tribal tattoo. These tatoos are proffessionally done, but on the left side of his neck he bears one piece of prison ink: side by side twin lightning bolts. Lockdown sports a blood red mohawk that stands about 1 1/2 inches high. Lockdown almost always has dark 5 o'clock shadow. His eyes are blue and grim. Ring Attire: light blue collared button up short sleeve shirt with a whie wife beater undershirt. On the breast pocket his prisoner ID number, 286333, is stamped below the words,"IL Dept of Correction." The back of the shirt reads, "Illinois Dept of Correction." Dark blue jeans with a 1 3/4 inch wide white stripe running down the side of each leg. Written in dark blue down each stripe are the words, IL Dept of Correction. The bottom of the jeans are cuffed. On his feet, Lockdown wears highly polished black combat boots. Lockdown has also been known to wrestle in just his undershirt, or sometimes, no shirt at all. Personality: Lockdown cares for very few besides himself. He has a talent for using others to achieve his goals. However, once his friendship is earned he is fiercely loyal. Lockdown is supremely confidant, bordering on arrogant. He would much rather jump head first into a hopeless battle than run from a fight. One of the most amazing things about Lockdown is his inhuman tolerance for pain. Handler Name: [name withheld] Handler Email: [detail withheld] Handler AIM: Handler MSN:
