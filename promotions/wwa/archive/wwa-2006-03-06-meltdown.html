March 6th Meltdown Predictions

2 March 2006

2 March 2006 Main Event United States Title Match Jonathon Winters Vs Khaled Tag Title Number One Contendership Franchise Players Vs Disciples of Blaze Muppet Man Vs Stephen Murphy Dave Harley & Damian Thorne Vs Gargoyle & Jack Dawkins Tournament Quarter-Final Mike Katz Vs Kyle James Andy McMirra & Danny Vista Vs The Human Condition ---- 6 March 2006 Here's the preview... this might help with some of the selections! 19 days is how many will be left until Rage in the Cage IX once this Meltdown has aired. The journey just keeps speeding up as we approach the point of no return. The penultimate Meltdown before the biggest pay-per-view of the year promises to be a big one. Up first is tag team action. Andy McMirra & Danny Vista impressed many with their tournament match. Sadly that ended in a draw and both were knocked out. Now they must team together to take on the Human Condition. Human Condition has been quiet as of late, but if McMirra and Vista can’t get along, this might become quite the one-sided affair. The first match of the quarter-finals will see Mike Katz taking on Kyle James. The two rookies have a lot to prove. Katz’s win over Belial Kahn and James’ victory over Axel Brundage certainly help, but now only one can move on to the semi-finals and the chance for the big prize. The biggest surprise from Meltdown last week was the return of Damian Thorne, who will be working with Dave Harley. The two men will team up this week and take on the debuting Gargoyle and Jack Dawkins. Harley & Thorne no doubt have a lot of ring rust and that could give the wily old Dawkins and the enigmatic Gargoyle a big chance to get an upset, but anyone expecting an easy match from two former WWA World Champions isn’t thinking straight. For weeks Steven Murphy and The Muppet Man have been at each other’s throats. Murphy has even gone as far as to kidnap Armaan. But last week we learned that Armaan has escaped and will make his return at RITC. Before that happens, Murphy and Muppet will have a chance to settle the score in a straight up, no string attached, one-on-one match. This promises to be one heck of a fight. A tag team title shot at RITC. That’s what is on the line when Disciples of Blaze face The Franchise Players. DoB has been complaining for weeks that they have been ignored. Now finally they have been given a shot. The whole point of Denrol & Tracer Bullet getting together was to win those tag team titles and now here’s their chance to get their first shot at them. This high stakes match may come down to which team wants it more. Or it may come down to what the Detonators want. In the main event, the United States Title is on the line when Jonathan Winters defends against Khaled. Mal Somers screwed Winters out of his chance to be World Champion two weeks ago and now Winters must make the toughest title defense of his career. Khaled came within seconds of winning the US Title last time he tried and this time Muppet Man will not be around. On one side Winters has his belt and his streak on the line. On the other side, Khaled’s reputation for choking will haunt him forever if he once again fails to gain gold. Waiting in the wings is Mal Somers, Ember, Jack Griffiths, and the mystery man. It’s a Meltdown you can’t afford to miss.
