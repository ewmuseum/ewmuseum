The Scouting Report - Issue 13

20 Sep 2003

Bylined Josh Wilson - Director of Talent, in the fed's own kayfabe voice.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Welcome back to the Scouting Report WWA fans. Josh Wilson, Director of Talent, here with the entire goings on from the WWA recently and my humble opinions. But just remember my opinions are not endorsed in any way by the WWA. I have to say that with legal matters such as it is. Anyway this week or next week millions of college kids will be enjoying their Spring Break and for that I wish them a good time. Just remember not to get stuck in Mexico without that identification, it just leads to more problems. Speaking of Spring Break something has been bothering me lately. As you fans well know the WWA stresses wrestling instead of just straight entertainment unlike our competition. However I believe they have gone to far with their recent Girls Gone Wild crossover and Playboy in the same week. I have no problem with either thing; in fact Playboy is fine publication. My problem is this seems to be more important than the wrestling and more over they're advertising this in front of kids! This is supposed to be ok for families and sometimes they do cross the line but this is a little much. Ok, I'm done with my rant now. Let's get to the goings on of the WWA. -So on Fiesta we finally let in on Joe Stock's big new plans. Along with Phil Flaws, the former owner of the Euroamerican Wrestling Alliance, the two have formed the new European Wrestling Alliance or simply the EWA. With Joe as the owner and Flaws acting as the CEO and President the two hope the new EWA will act as a feeder to the WWA, training up and coming wrestlers to be future stars of the WWA. -Stock talked to me recently about the EWA. "The European Wrestling Alliance is something the WWA has needed for awhile. So much talent comes through the door and the WWA is major league with national television, so we really have never had a place where those who are not quite ready can develop. Now we do," he said. -President Warr is also excited about the new venture saying, "We pride ourselves on bringing the best talent to the fans this is just one step in the WWA enterprise to make ourselves stand out from the competition." -Flaws and Stock have hired trainers, which include members of the WWA staff, to help these new potentials work the kinks out of their systems. -Flaws and Stock made the announcement on Fiesta and retired the WWA Rookie Title to make way for the EWA's only belt, the WWA European Title. The retiring of the WWA Rookie Title is a good idea with the belt not being defended in a long time. I'd like to wish both men luck in the EWA. -Another title shuffle happened at Fiesta with the retiring of the Extreme and Cruiserweight Titles to make way for the new GenX title. This was bound to happen with wrestlers holding both titles recently. This should really help to resurrect the Cruiserweight division and add some much needed spice, hopefully along the lines of the X-Division in the NWA. -Jimmi Zion was the first GenX champion but dropped the belt the same night to James Beam. I sense a heel turn for Beam in the works. -I just have to say, Showtime and James Curzon continue to crack me up. Not only do we have Mini-Showtimes but now a Mini-Curzon. Will the madness never stop? -Doctors have scanned Wes Sanders legs, they are doing some tests still but it looks like a clean break. They are still treating him at this time. He could be out for months; doctors are not willing to put a time scale on this yet. This is the reason for the change in the world title scene as of late. I for one wish Wes a speedy recovery. -But it seems as one door shuts another one opens. Recently the WWA has seen the return of former superstar Se7en. Many thought the rookie would be a rising star in the WWA but shortly after left the company. He's back now and hopefully can take up where he left off. -The March Madness card is continuing to shape up. We already know of the match between Chance and Denrol and now The Cell vs. X-Corp for the Tag Team Titles has been added. The stipulation for this match is that it will be fought as a Hell in the Cell match. That kind of match can make or break a man's career. -Now on to thoughts for next weeks Meltdown. -Last Meltdown Grunge came out of the Fatal Fourway match for the TV title with the victory. Monday he has to face Venny LeTrav, the man who almost had the victory, in a non-title match. Both men are rising talents and accomplished high-flyers. Look for this to set up a title match between the two men soon. -Expect Johnny Shrapnel vs. Deimos to be a slaughter. I say so because the Veteranz will look to be the underdogs in this feud leading into March Madness. Don't be surprised to see a match between the two teams booked soon if it hasn't by the time this comes out. -Havoc and the House of Pain reminds me of a quote from the movie Ocean's Eleven. "First he'll kill ya, then he'll go to work on ya." -Vik Ventressa vs. Denrol will look to be an amazing match on paper. Both are former champions and both are on their way to legend status. However the match could just serve to further the feud between Kraven and Ventressa as the Tomorrow Man will serve as the special enforcer. -Also after last week with Father Denrol challenging Chance, don't be surprised to see Chance try to get some payback. This would be very likely as to further the feud before they're clash at March Madness. That is one match I can't wait to see. -The main event is currently listed as "to be determined." Look for it to have something to do with the convoluted World Title scene. After last week's Meltdown both Thorne and Kash both claim the belt. Maybe a three way dance at the paper-view? -As mentioned Damian Thorne, Ian Tense, and Kash are going to be in the arena. Also listed to be in attendance are Eddie Van Dorn and Mal Somers. -Check back next week for more of the goings on of the WWA and thoughts on March Madness. Till then Ciao.
