The Scouting Report - Issue 22

28 Nov 2003

Bylined Josh Wilson - Director of Talent, in the fed's own kayfabe voice.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Greetings and salutations WWA fans, it’s your esteemed and valued Director of Talent back with all the dirt and opinions you can handle with a new Scouting Report. -Well we’re just days away from the Independence Day Rumble and I’m going to run down all the fantastic matches for you. -But first I’d be amiss if I didn’t speak about this Monday’s Meltdown and the Bourbon Street Brawl. -I’ll tell you what, Tracer Bullet can put on one show. Of course I was in the local bar eating crawfish while the match was going on but I caught most of it on the camera. Of course it was just to long to go over the entire thing but that won’t stop me from giving you the highlights. -First it seems that the rivalry between Nick Adams and Jimmi Zion is heating up. Make no mistake both these men hate each other and it blew up Monday with Jimmi eliminating Nick from the Brawl. -And of course there was the fact of the returning superstar. Everyone assumed it was Denrol. Nope sorry he was too busy house-sitting for Tracer. No instead it was the mentor of our favorite Canadian, David, “The Sniper” Wattsoner. -Add to that the WWA European Champion, The Chuckster and we have a very intense contest. And of course he did impress many and he seems to be making waves here in the WWA. -The Devil’s Own continued to dominate this Monday with Leviathan lasting a long time in the match. Of course he was side-tracked earlier with Grunge capturing him. Imprisoning someone with a motel room with a Tiger is a good way to show somebody is serious. Still with the help from the “Psycho” Steven Murphy and Imperceptus he managed to get by the beast. -Of course X-Corp was up to its old tricks again as well. The funniest part of the match had to come when they eliminated WWA TV Champion Joey K after dressing up as The Village People and singing YMCA on stage. -Of course a tie with that would be Eddie Van Dorn doing his best superhero impression to take out Joey. He was just the whipping boy of the night wasn’t he? People should stop picking on Joey, he’s not a superhero anymore. -Of course at the last it came down to EVD and Chance. It came close with Chance getting the duke after a move off the top of a building. Good for him. -Of course the show ended with Ricky Harrington, Mark Harrington’s nephew, being named the new CEO and Matt Warr being demoted to Head Booker. It also seems that Matt Warr had already drawn up a contract to bring someone back to the WWA. -Of course now on to thoughts for the event of July, The Independence Day Rumble. -First off we have the Loser Leaves Town match with Toot McBiggins, Myzery, and Christopher Vice facing off. -Truth be told I hate these matches. It’s sad in my opinion to see anybody go from the WWA. Especially somebody I might have hired. But if they aren’t pulling the load then they have to go. This is a business just like any other. -Next is the TV Title with Joey K defending at his first PPV against Dave Harley. Both of these men have been arguing over who has the better talk show. -Of course I might be biased. I’m a big fan of the “Not So Late Show” with Mal and Dave. -In the “extreme” match of the night two legends will be facing off. Shaman has been challenged by David Wattsoner to show the younger guys who invented extreme. I always thought it was Terry Funk. -Anyway this will be a treat for me because I’ve never seen Wattsoner wrestle in person before. Still he’s going to have to be good. Shaman has shown time and time again to be completely at home in this type of environment. Either way there will be blood. -Finally we have Chance and Enigma. I honestly can’t say who will win this match as both of these men hate each other. All I know is that it will be a war. -And next is the big event, the Independence Day Rumble. The rules are simple, two me start off. After a set time period another men comes out and so on till all the thirty men have made their way out. Eliminations occur until only one man remains. Eliminations happen only by going over the top rope and both feet touching the floor. -But instead of going through the entire list of entrants, which is the entire locker-room except for Kash and Tracer Bullet, I’m going to list my pick. -Enigma. The leader of the Forsaken Souls is in a great position to take it this year. With his entire group in the rumble and the fact that they won’t touch their leader he’s going to be hard to get rid of. The only problem I see is that after his war with Chance will be 100 percent? -Chance. If you need any more proof that Chance is a contender then check out his match at Bourbon Street Brawl. He took that rumble but can he do it again? Like Enigma though he won’t be fresh in this match and many of the other favorites will. -Eddie Van Dorn. He said it himself, always a bridesmaid never a bride. He’s always done well in rumbles but has yet to get a win. Still Sunday may be different. -Leviathan. The leader of the Devil’s Own, the big man is going to be hard to be put out of the ring. Not only that but DO have a pack mentality and will do anything to protect Levi. The only bad thing I can see is that Levi has made a lot of enemies and he’ll have a big bull’s-eye on his back. -Nick Adams. Another name that is being mentioned a lot is the Rainmaker. Quick, fast and a new member of Youth United, Nick has been waiting for his chance and he might just pluck it out of everyone’s grasp. -Of course there are others in the match as well. You can’t count out WWA United States Champion Grunge who has been waiting for this chance. Another is Mal Somers who many have been claming is the next big thing. Either one could win this. And you can’t forget the mystery man. -And the main event of the match will be WWA World Champ Kash defending his belt against legend Tracer Bullet. -The hatred between these men goes back to the match where Shane Dorian attacked Tracer’s wife under orders from Damien Thorne. Tracer blames Kash fro not helping but after his attack from Thorne about all he could do was lie in the ring. Of course now things have gotten very petty and you wonder what means more, vindication or the title. Well until next time this is Josh Wilson. Be sure to watch Meltdown and Fiesta. You won’t be disappointed. Ciao.
