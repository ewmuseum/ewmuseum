WWA — 25 Mar 1998

25 Mar 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Wednesday 25, 1998 The screen opens up to the the NCW banner. Then.... Northeast Champion Wrestling flashes through the screen and leaves the NCW Banner Then firework type explosions go off around the Banner. The Banner fades... then the screen shows Lehman College's The Apex. The screen then goes to the inside to show the fans jumping and screaming at the Camera. The camera pans over the the Complex and then the camera finds Tony Bailey and Terry Valentine sitting behind the table in the broadcast area. Tony : Welcome ladies and gentlemen to the Apex in the Bronx, New York. We have a great show for you tonight. We have quite a few men making their singles debuts tonight, and an NYC title match as our featured bout. Terry : I am putting my money on the Punisher in that one. Johny : I would have to agree, Reaper has taken quite a few beatings at the hands of the House of Pain and can't be 100%. Tony : Let's go to the first match. "Gangsta's Paradise" by Coolio plays and out walks Deadbolt to a chorus of boos. Tony : We haven't seen much of either man in this match. A win could go a long win towards a title shot for the winner. Terry : Let's hope Deadbolt doesn't get hurt again, everytime he comes off an injury he gets another one. "Gangsta's Paradise" is replaced by the United Stated National Anthem and "Captain America" Tommy Pride comes out with the American flag in tow. Tony : Deadbolt wastes no time in attacking Tommy Pride and he just leveled him with a clothesline. Terry : There's a vicious elbow drop to the throat by Deadbolt, I liked it personally. Johny : Deadbolt is pummeling the "captain" now with a barrage of rights and lefts. The referee is now warning Deadbolt, who just shrugs him aside. Tony : While Deadbolt had his back turned Pride got up and punched him square in the kidneys. Deadbolt has dropped to a knee and now Pride is returning the fists he recieved earlier. Terry : This one has turned into a streetfight and I don't see Pride being able to win one of those since Deadbolt has 50 lbs on him. Johny : I'm...shocked, Terry just made a valid point, is that possible? Tony(stifling laughter): Deadbolt just knocked Pride down again but missed a legdrop and Pride is back to his feet. Terry : Pride whips Deadbolt to the corner and staggers him with a clothesline. Tony : He's now running to the farside ropes to try it again, but Deadbolt was waiting for him and caught him coming in with a boot to the gut. Johny : Deadbolt has the A-bomb cinched, and there it is, Pride's not getting out of that one, since Deadbolt really spiked him and Deadbolt wins. Tony : Next up two of the largest men in the NCW, square off in their singles debuts. Terry : Don't you mean two of the longest winded athletes in NCW? These guys talk to much, so I'm gonna take a nap during this one. Taps begins to blare over the arena loudspeakers and Terry jumps up and falls out of his seat as Thanatos, The Reaper of Souls steps towards the ring. Johny : Leave it to Terry to provide, the REAL entertainment. (laughs). As Terry gets his headset back in position and sits down with a bruised butt and ego, "Feed My Frankenstein" by Alice Cooper replaces Taps and the burly Mad Coil walks out. Johny : Both men stand 7 feet tall, Thanatos weighs in at 420 and Mad Coil at 380. This should be high impact throughout. Terry : You know what I'd like to see? One of these big goons sitting on Blaze and Griffin, that way we wouldn't have to hear their goody two shoes mouths anymore. Johny : You know what I'd like to see? The replay of you falling all over yourself, can someone in the truck run that on the monitors? Tony : The match has begun and they lock up, Mad Coil with one swift move has control with a rear waistlock. Terry : I'm going to call out Anarchy again Morehouse, watch yourself. Anyway, Thanatos looks to be figuring a way to counter the hold now, and fires a back elbow which Mad Coil ducks but with that he lossened up his grip and gets taken over with a tremendous belly to belly suplex. Why couldn't Thanatos be wrestling Blaze? He'd surely have cracked his ribs with that move! Johny : You're sick, Terry you should seek counseling. Mad Coil looks dazed, I doubt anyone has ever manhandled him like that before. Tony : Thanatos is showcasing his mat wrestling skills with a variety of small time submission holds. Mad Coil quickly gets to the ropes, which gets Thanatos off of him momentarily. Terry : Coil charges in again but Thanatos ducks and clotheslines him in the back. Mad Coil is wobbly, and it looks like Thanatos is attempting to lift him up for a piledriver. Johny : Thanatos connects with it and the match is over as Mad Coil couldn't power out before the 3 count. Tony : We've got the rookie Paul Valentine making his debut against Rocky Stevens who will be making his second appearance. Terry : They both make me sleepy. "Paradise City" by Guns N' Roses begins to play over the PA as Paul Valentine and his wife enter from the back. Johny : Either man is capable of winning this match so it should be a good one. When Valentine enters the ring, "Enter Sandman" by Metallica begins to play and "Rock Hard" Rocky Stevens struts down to the ring. Tony : Both men look to be in good physical shape, for this match but no one seemed to be that interested as they came down to the ring. Terry : That would be because no one cares Tony. Johny : More insightful commentary from Terry, it seems that Stevens is a bit bigger and had used that advantage to muscle Valentine into the corner. Tony : They're really slugging it out in the corner there, this should be right up your alley Terry. Terry : It would be if I cared which guy won. Valentine just DDT'ed Stevens after blocking a right and landed a knee to the stomach. Paul Valentine scores a victory in his debut, whoop de doo. Johny : Were you born with this big of a chip on your shoulder or did you have to work on it all the time? Terry : Now we get to the good stuff, my man Big Elien's gonna tear into Capital. Tony : I hear good things about Capital and as long as the House doesn't cheat I expect a victory for the rookie. Johny : Asking the House not to cheat is an awful lot Tony. "Body Count" by Body Count begins to play and HOP member Big Eliem walks out to deafening boos. Terry : Why are those idiots booing this guy, he's great! "The Beautiful People" by Marilyn Manson quickly replaces Body Count and the rookie Capital walks out for his debut match in the NCW. Terry : This guy has horrible taste in entrance music there's no way he'll beat Big Eliem. Tony : There was a guy here recently who had that same entrance music and I recall you liked him. Terry : But he was a dork, and I'm glad he's gone. Johny : Capital's wasting no time attacking Big Eliem and a headbutt to the shoulder stuns the big man. Capital scoops up Big Eliem and powerslams him down. Tony : That was an impressive display of strength by Capital, who although he's only 6'2 has 280 pounds packed on his frame. Terry : Oh will you shut up, Eliem's just playing possum and will reign supreme. Johny : Eliem is back up to his feet and they are trading blows but a thumb to the eye by Eliem stops Capital's onslaught in an instant. Tony : Eliem with a scoop and a backbreaker, he's holding him there and driving his head down and Capital's back is bent awkwardly across Eliem's knee. Terry : I told you Eliem would get the job done. Johny : Did the match end sometime while you were talking? Because I didn't see a pin or hear Capital quit. Tony : As a matter of fact Capital's back up to his feet and he just drove his forearm to the bridge of Eliem's nose and stunned the big man. A clothesline sends him staggering to the ropes and another sends Eliem over the top to the outside. Terry : Isn't that a DQ? Johny : No, Terry it's not and it looks like the "Enforcer" Zack Crockett is slinking in to the ring from behind Capital's who leaning over the ropes and lifting Eliem up. Tony : Crockett just knocked Capital out with a pair of brass knucks and exited the ring just as quickly as he entered. HOW did the referee not see that? Terry : See what? Eliem whipped his head back and the impact knocked Capital out. Johny : Do you even belive the stuff that comes out of your mouth Terry? Eliem lifts Capital up and chokeslams him down to the mat. Eliem gets the win but yet again with the aid of outside interference from a member of the HOP. Tony : Next up we have the underrated Tiburon and a newcomer Rob Thorne. Terry : This is another one of those matches I don't really care to watch. "Gangsta's Paradise by Coolio plays for the second time tonight but this time Tiburon emerged from the curtain. Johny : We know if the House isn't wrestling, you don't care Terry. Terry : You're finally learning Morehouse! Coolio is replaced by Ozzy Osbourne's song "War Pigs" and the newcomer Rob Thorne walks out. Terry : Wasn't Thorne the guy who sent in 20 tapes of the same interview? Johny : Yes, that was him Terry. Terry : Well I hope he gets hammered then! Tony : They lock up and Tiburon takes him down quickly with a leaping shoulderblock and now he's hammering the smaller Thorne with a vengeance. Terry : Hey, maybe Tiburon's not that big of a bum after all. Johny : Please excuse Terry's unprofessionalism, you get used to it after a while. Tiburon has been dominant throughout and Thorne is trying to fight back but a quick thumb to the eye stops him in his tracks. Tony : A big back body drop by Tiburon nearly sends Thorne sprawling to the outside. Terry : Tiburon with a powerslam and he's now setting Thorne up for his finisher the Tiburon Chomp. Johny : He connects and fairly easily defeats Rob Thorne in his debut. Tony : Next up Darken defends his team name and honor when he meets Apocalypse one on one. Terry : Yah, this match is a great one. "End of the world" R.E.M starts over the arena as Apocalypse walks to the ring. Tony : Apocalypse sure has his work cut out for himself tonight. Johny : I'd have to agree Tony. He is giving up over 100 pounds and about 8 inches. "Sad but True" by Metallica begins to play as Darken enter the arena with the rest of the Four Horsemen of the Apocalypse and of course YES is also attending. Terry : Yeah... It's Darken. Yippie... I wish he would just leave! And take that freaky little man, too! Tony : Yep! That's why we pay Terry the big bucks. Meanwhile in the ring, Apocalypse made a mistake by trying to match strength with the bigger man. Terry : Darken threw him on his head. He is now trying to regroup and try to get on the offensive. Tony : What is that music in the background? Johny : Oh gees! Terry : Yes... I love FILTER! "Hey Man, Nice Shot" by Filter starts over the PA. The House of Pain come out from behind the curtain. Cross, The Outlaw, Big Eliem, Jak Lancer and Aric Crockett are walking down to the ring. Terry : That's right! It's time to pay the price! Tony : The Four Horsemen at ringside see the House coming down to the ring. They all start running towards the House... Steihl tackles Cross and Outlaw. Jack and Big Eliem are brawling. Aric and Gutter are at war also. Johny : In the ring Darken is beating down Apocalypse with rights and lefts. Terry : No not again. The lights are flickering.... They are out! Tony : Is it the Anarchy again? Johny : I don't know... It is a safe assumption. There we go... The light are back on. Terry : Look up at the ramp. It is Anarchy with a... Potato bag between them. The bag appears to be moving... Havoc : Hello Horsemen... Are you missing something? Like your manager! HA-HA-HA! The Anarchy disappears behind the curtains. Darken glances around the ring and doesn't find YES anywhere. He jumps out of the ring and starts running for where the Anarchy was moments before. Terry : Look! Darken is running from Apocalypse! I knew he was a coward! Tony : I don't think that is what is going on at all. The referee is calling for the bell. Apocalypse wins by count out. But I don't think he is moving in the ring. Get some medical attention out here. Johny : I can't believe that the Anarchy stole YES from the Four Horsemen. Terry : It doesn't really matter. Darken LOST! Tony : Now we have the undefeated Irish Hammer facing off against "Lightning" Joe Clapp. Terry : The Hammer is gonna crush the Clapper! "Jump Around" by House of Pain begins to play and Terry starts hopping around insanely as "The Irish Hammer" Todd Logan walks out. Tony : Would you please sit down? Terry : Hey I'm not gonna disobey the House of Pain? Get it, hahaha. Tony and Johny just ignore Terry's attempted joke. Metallica's "My Friend Of Misery" replaces Jump Around and Terry looks visibly upset as "Lightning" Joe Clapp comes down to ringside. Terry : Those girls up there were starting to get hot for my dance steps and this jerk had to ruin it with his Metallica! Tony : Oh please, you're old enough to be anyone of their fathers Terry. Johny : I hear he likes 'em young Tony. Anyhow the Irish Hammer is now in control with a top knucklelock on the marginally taller Clapp. Tony : Clapp is now powering back to a neutral position but Logan kicks him in the gut and nails him with a DDT. Terry : I can't believe Clapp fell for that. (laughs). Johny : Lighting Joe muscles out at 2 and he's got a surge of adrenalim now, as he's taking it to Logan. Tony : A thunderous powerslam by Lightning. Terry : YOU JUST HAD TO SAY IT DIDN'T YOU! Johny : I thought it was good, and Clapp agrees as he's celebrating, but you can't turn your back on Logan. Tony : Logan just leveled Clapp with a powerful clothesline as he turned around. A vicious belly to belly suplex by Logan, and he just pulled Clapp up by the hair at 2. Terry : He wants to punish him a little more, and I can't say that I blame him. Johny : After some serious punches to the face Clapp is wobbly and now Logan is setting him up for a powerbomb. Tony : There it is, a jackknife powerbomb by Logan, he gets the cover and the 1-2-3. Terry : Clapp didn't stand a chance against a member of the House. Tony : This is our main event and it's for the CWC NYC title, a belt Angel Reaper has now held twice. Terry : Which just means he'll lose it for a second time to a member of the House. "Hey Man Nice Shot" by Filter begins to play and the boobirds come out in full force for Aric Crockett. Johny : Despite Terry's guaranteeing a victory for Crockett this match has the look of a classic. The CWC's most marked man and 2 time NYC Champion, Angel Reaper cautiously walks the aisle as "Antichrist Superstar" by Marilyn Manson rocks the PA. Tony : The House of Pain travelled down to Georgia last Friday night to attack this man, is it fear or respect that they are showing him? Johny : I don't know what it is that inspires them to go after him so much, when there is more history between Blaze and the HOP and the Overlords for that matter. Terry : He's a freak and he thinks he's better then the House that is why he deserves all the beatings he receives. Tony : The match begins as the bell sounds, and Reaper charges in landing a forearm shot to the bridge of the nose that staggers Aric to the corner. Terry : Reaper's now raining blows to the Punisher's midsection but Aric reverses it and is now putting the boots to Reaper. This is great. Johny : A knife edge chop by Crockett sends Reaper down to the mat and he's now being choked by the boot of Aric. The referee warns the challenger and he lets go, only to pick Reaper up and whip him to the ropes. Tony : Aric attempts a clothesline but Reaper ducks it and comes flying off the far side ropes with an elbow smash to the face. Reaper is now stomping away at Aric as the momentum keeps shifting. Terry : Why isn't the ref warning Angel, he's on the take I tell you! Johny : First of all he's not choking Aric and secondly Angel just picked him up and body slammed him down. Tony : Reaper attempts a legdrop but Crockett rolls aside and slaps on a reverse chinlock in an attempt to wear down the champion. Terry : Aric's got him now, it's all over but the celebration. Reaper gets to his feet and buries two illegal elbows in Aric's midsection, he just attempted a clothesline but missed and got hit with a massive right hand by Crockett that dropped him, serves him right. Johny : Reaper sweeps the leg out from underneath Aric and as Crockett falls to the mat, he locks on a Japanese armbar. Tony : Reaper is really wrenching back on that left arm of Crockett's and Reaper might hyperextend the elbow if Crockett doesn't make it to the ropes soon. Terry : Aric just made it and Reaper won't break the hold, that guy is such a CHEATER! Johny : He let go on 3 and that is perfectly legal as we all know, I would think Reaper's tactics would be right up your alley Terry. Tony : Crockett is trying to shake some feeling back into his arm and he's whaling away on Reaper yet again, this has been a great match thus far and both men still look in good shape. Terry : That's just Aric's way of setting people up for a fall Tony, WHEN will you learn that? Johny : Crockett with a bodyslam and he drops an elbow right across the windpipe, there was absolutely no call for that and the referee could call for a DQ if he wanted to. Terry : Look at Reaper squirming around and gagging, way to go Aric, keep it up. Aric connects with a legdrop and he covers...1....2...kickout by Reaper, that was yet another slow count by an NCW official. Tony : Aric goes for a splash but misses miserably as Reaper slides away and drops a knee to the back of the neck. He's grabbing a hold of Aric's left arm and tucking it between his ribs and his left arm. He's repeating the process on his right side and it looks like he just may rip Aric in half here. Terry : That hold has to be illegal, a man's spine is not meant to bend that way. Get in there and do something ref! Johny : Reaper lets go of that modified Camel Clutch and lifts Crockett up. A mule kick to the groin by Crockett went unseen by the ref and Reaper is in trouble here. Tony : A devastating spinebuster by Aric Crockett and he goes for the cover...but Reaper kicks out at 2 1/2. Terry : I can't believe it, will we ever find a ref then can count straight to three? Johny : This match is well beyond it's 40th minute at this point and both men are obviously drained but still show no signs of giving up. Tony : Both men are standing and continue punching away at each other and it seems Reaper landed a solid right as Crockett's head whipped back. Terry : A closed fist that the referee allowed to go unwarned I might add. A snap suplex by Reaper took a lot out of both men. Johny : Reaper is lifting Crockett up once again, and he seems to be setting up for a Northern Lights Suplex. He's got him up and with one last burst of strength gets Aric over. Tony : Angel has the bridge in perfect placement, the referee begins his count....1...2...3! Terry : NOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO! Johny : Angel Reaper defeats Aric Crockett to retain the title in a match that took seemingly forever to complete! I can't wait for the rematch, we're going to need the trainers because both men are totally exhausted. We are out of time.
