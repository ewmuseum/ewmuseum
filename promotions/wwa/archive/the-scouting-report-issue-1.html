The Scouting Report - Issue 1

19 Jan 2004

Bylined Joe Stock - Director of Talent, in the fed's own kayfabe voice.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hello everyone and welcome to The Scouting Report, your source for what's new in the World Wrestling Alliance. I just got moved back into my old Great lakes Wrestling Federation office in WWA Towers and still have a lot to unpack, but I did manage to get to Boston for The Independence Day Rumble. I have numerous thoughts and information to pass on to you from that great event. - "The Jackyl" Christyan Payne is a rookie that impressed a lot of people in his dark match with Mr. Internet before we went on the air for the Pay-Per-View. He also helped out with some production stuff, showing a willingness to help out this federation as a whole. This kid could go far with that attitude. I can't wait to see what he can do in front of the camera. - Along with Payne, the WWA has recently signed a number of newcomers: "The Particle Man" Nick DeLoessan, Gohan, Kash, Jay-Jay Da Silva, and "The Executioner" Edward Layman. On the tag team side is The Regulators, the second team to use that name in the WWA, and the Texas Longhorns, the two oldest wrestlers in WWA history I believe. We wish all them the best of luck. - The Boston crowd was pumped for IDR 2002. The WWA has been to Boston many times and this is the second IDR in a row to be live from the Fleet center. We promise to keep coming back if they promise to give us a great crowd as they do every time we stop by. - Wes Sanders & "The Hurricane" Barrett Sinclair had a great brawl and both men came out looking good. Through a table spot so early in the show may have been the wrong thing to do. - The fans were not happy that they didn't get the Se7en Vs "Mighty" Joe Keane match that was promised. A reasonable substitute couldn't be found and we apologize to our fans for that. - Venny LeTrav and James Williams had a good hardcore match, through the match could have used more psychology and less weapon shots. Still a good Pay-Per-View debut for both men and they both got potential. Second match in a row with a table spot. - Chase Williams shocked everyone by running in LeTrav/Williams. He instantly got himself some serious heat with the fans. Follow that up by a great showing in the rumble and we got a future superstar here. - I don't know what to make of Kade Williams' speech. The guy doesn't seem to share the same reality the rest of us do. Everything that goes wrong is someone else's fault. Maybe Kade Williams should take a long look into the mirror. Mark Harrington didn't screw Kade Williams. Kade Williams screwed Kade Williams. -Se7en Vs We Sanders could have went longer, but Sanders had THREE matches that night and couldn't afford to tire out before the Rumble. It was good while it lasted. -The four corners tag match was a bit of a mess. Each team tried their heart out, but it just didn't click. Too many men, too little time. Havoc does look like they may be the tag team of the future and their feud with the Horsemen has legs. Third table spot. -The rumble was insane and fun as always. While Tracer Bullet walked with it all, it was a rumble for the rookies. -EkoSyde/Se7en have a great feud going on, both men will benefit from this. Se7en took a nasty bump in the corner, but all reports are that he's fine and is already back to working house shows. - I've said it before; I'll say it again. Chase Williams looked great out there and that boy has a future. - Eddie van Dorn may have had his best performance ever during that rumble. The midcarders is ready to take the next step and I bet you'll see him in the main event very soon. - Which Horsemen has the best future? It might be James Thornhill, who had a great showing in the rumble. His attack angle worked and created some great heat. - A rumor that Spike would win the entire rumble was just a joke folks. But you never know in the WWA. - Damien Thorne is another guy who had a good showing. He took two sick bumps and jumpstarted a feud with Eddie van Dorn. I can't wait to see those two go at it. Thorne by the way is fine, he bumped perfect on that ladder spot and was just a little sore afterwards. - Shaman spoke for every baseball fan out there. The loss of Ted Williams is a tragic one. .402 forever. - Denrol & Tracer Bullet had a great time in there. Simply the funniest match I ever watched. It was a great way to go, but it seems it wasn't enough for the Greatest of All Earth Time. He will lace up the boots in the WWA again, maybe as soon as the next Monday Night Meltdown. - Tracer Bullet sprained his shoulder during the rumble. A MRI reveled nothing serious, but to be safe, he has been removed from the house show circuit. He will be back for Meltdown. - Kraven and Moore had another classic. The two nearly kill each other every time they get in there and Sunday was no different. There was a moment or two when I thought they were going to break the ring or one of their bodies. - "Mighty" Joe Keane is still suffering headaches and other pains, but he was able to get involved in the main event. He is expected to return to ring action late July or early August. - Jenny Calloway's turn on Kraven is shocking and is a big twist for the Moore/Kraven feud. Things have not been settled between the two big men and they will lock up again. - It should be interesting how Labor Day Brawl is promoted. While Jackson Kraven Vs Tracer Bullet would have been an easy sell, Craven Moore Vs Tracer Bullet is another story. Two faces are always hard to promote. Wrestling-wise, I'm looking forward to it. - The time off for WWA television is a good idea. Everyone is exhausted from the buildup to IDR and the event itself. House shows are still running this week, headlined by Havoc Vs The Horsemen matches. It is also a great chance for our rookies to get used to WWA rings before debuting on television. See you next week for another edition of The Scouting Report. I'll have updates on injuries, rumors, and much more.
