What this is Roster bio card for the WWA wrestler Shaman, giving height, weight, birth date, hometown, entrance music, a quote, physical appearance, ring attire and personality.
Biography Wrestler's Name: Shaman Real Name: Shaman Height: 6'9 Weight: 340 Date Of Birth: 5/30/1974 Hometown: Mojave, Arizona Entrance Music: Liberate by Disturbed Wrestler's Quote: When they are new i just show them new ways to bleed! Physical Appearance: Since Shaman had reemerged as famine and then leaving The Horsemen of apocolypse he has become rather chiselled in physique and much broader in strength and mass. Looking similar to Scott Steiner at 6'9". Shamans hair is long extending past his shoulders down to the middle of his back growing straight and black as night. His eyes seem to be of the darkest brown to black flickering very little emotion and always inciting the unknown into his opponents since they cannot guess what is on his mind. Being unpredictable about his gestures and movements has always been one of his strong suits. Shaman is sporting a new tattoo which runs from his right elbow down to his wrist and hand in a wavey jagged edged pattern. Ring Attire: Shaman has chosen to go back more towards his roots and returned to the his original styled black and blue wrestling pants. He still wears fingerless gloves at times and black wrestling boots. Personality: Shaman is a sadistic individual who relishes in the pain of others. he isn't afraid of any opponent and will never back down from anyone. He very seldomnly turns down a challenge. Only turning them down if he thinks its a waste of his time. Shaman feels that if he doesn't start something he will definately finish it. As for the fans Shaman is a bit of an enigma in this category. The fans tend to like him greatly and lately he is beginning to respect that even though he doesn't care whether they like him or not. If he has any allies it is due out of grudging respect not for trust purposes. Shaman has trusted very few people in his career in the WWA.
