He slides down this, clutching at his face as he curls up into a foetal position as once more a shadow falls across his body as someone steps forward to stand over him. Ember. Jimmy: Dear God in Heaven! Fat Tony: Khaled isn’t Ember? Jimmy: No Tony … Ember is Ember, it was a ruse all the time, and it seems that he has Bullet right where he wants him. Holding onto his side, breathing heavily as a sign of the heavy match just finished recently, Ember looks down at the man who writhes in blind agony beneath him and a slow, malicious smile appears beneath his mask as he reaches behind him and slowly waves a taser backwards and forwards in front of the sightless eyes of the fallen Champion. Ember: You wanted to see the ‘real’ me Bullet, well here you are this is the REAL me … and that is something that you will never, EVER, forget! Jimmy: … we are out of time! Fat Tony: NOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO! Quick Day of the Dead logo and than fade to black.
