Saturday Night Main Event — 15 August 1998

15 Aug 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
W.W.A Welcomes you to Saturday Night Main Event (( Camera opens to Bob's driver greeting the new and improved WWA fans )) Bob's Driver: Welcome everybody to Saturday Night Main Event. Tony: Hello everyone, welcome to the second official WWA card this fine Saturday. We'll be having a nice set for you to top off with Jack and Simply the Best Flynn Steele going at it for Jack's coveted Television title. Terry:Big deal. Randy Cross could go out and take them both down for that title, if it wasn't beneath him to hold such a pathetic title. Eric: Well tonight we also have an interview with Mad Coil and Gutter, Gutter coming off that stunning loss last night to Raven Crow. Tony: Well let's get moving with the first match setting the Claymores against the former champions the Cammandant's Own. (( Camera focuses in on Bob's Driver as he is now standing in the ring to announce the following match. )) Bob's Driver: First to the ring, from the highlands of Scottland, the Claymores! And they are accompanied to the ring by the MacGregors. (( The claymores walk to the ring amidst to a bunch of boos and hisses. They threaten the fans as they make the trip to the ring. )) Bob's Driver: And their opponent, the former NCW tag champions, The Commandant's Own! (( The Marine's Hymn plays as the former champions walk to the ring to the cheers of the fans. )) Tony: There's the bell and this match is underway. Johny: Havok and Rob to start this match off. Terry: The two lock up, and neither man can force the other back. Eric: Havok with a takedown and he applies a hammerlock on Rob. Terry: Rob switches around and throws Havok over his shoulder into an armbar. Tony: Havok climbs to his feet and goes for a clothesline. Johny: Rob ducks and makes a quick tag to Doug behind Havok's back. Terry: Havok locks up with Rob and Doug comes behind with a rollup getting the two count. Tony: Havok get's up and drives a haymaker into the side of the head of Doug, staggering him. Eric: Havok sends Doug into the ropes and drops him with a Thezs Press. Terry: Look at those punches that Havok's throwing. Eric: Rob draw's the referee's attention, and Doug drives a knee to the groin of Havok. Terry: I guess the family jewels aren't too valuable anymore, are they. Tony: Payne comes in and drops a fist to the head of Doug, but the referee makes him leave the ring. Terry: Rob comes in behind the referee and attacks Havok, allowing Doug to roll out. Johny: Rob with a choke and Payne again distracts the referee trying to come in. Terry: Rob passes Payne to his partner and begins to kick him in the stomach as Doug chokes him . Johny: The referee finally sees this and makes the break for Havok. He needs to make the tag. Tony: Rob drops an elbow to the back of Havok. Havok rolls out of the way. Terry: The former champion has a handfull of hair and is driving Rob's face into the mat. Johny: Havok rolls to his corner and makes the tag. Tony: Payne in and he's full of energy and takes Rob down with punches. Johny: Doug comes in to find a fist in the face. Eric: Payne is on a roll, he's stomping both of the Claymores himself. Terry: The referee finally gets some order and Rob is left alone in the ring with Payne. Tony: Payne drops Rob with a ddt. He's dropping the elbow. Johny: Payne with a vicious powerbomb and he makes the tag. Eric: Payne lifts Rob up and drops him with the Arial Assault as Havok instills Terror from Above! Tony: The Commandant's Own make the pin and that's the end of the match. Terry: They cheated a lot... Look at all those hairpulls. Eric: And you don't think the Claymores cheated? Terry: It was healthy tag team action from them. Eric: I'll show you healthy tag team. I'll go get Bob's Driver and you can go get yourself a partner and we'll take you apart. Terry: I'll get someone, I'll get an HOP member if you aren't careful. (( Camera focuses on Bob's Driver in the ring. )) Bob's Driver: Introducing first, "The Irish Assassin" Gregory O'Hara! (( There is no crowd reaction as nobody knows who this Irish "wrestler" is. )) Bob's Driver: And his opponent, Jeff "The Machine" Keenan! (( The crowd shows little reaction to the silent Machine. )) Terry: This should be interesting, wake me when it's done. Eric: Don't worry, we wont. We'll be sleeping too. Tony: Will you two just quiet down and do your job? Eric and Terry: ZZZZZZZZZZZ! Tony: Jesus, some people. Johny: Well, this match is under way and the two lock up. Terry: Hey Eric, can you pass the soda? Eric: Here you go. It's XTC not soda, it keeps you awake. Tony: Machine with a takedown and Gregory with a drop toe hold. Terry: This stuff is disgusting. Johny: Machine hits the ropes and hits a crossbody. Eric: It's an acquired taste. Tony: Do you two mind? Eric: No, not at all, you two aren't bothering us. Terry: Speak for yourself, I'm trying to eat. Johny: That looks pretty good, mind if I have one? Terry: Yes! Tony: Kickout by the Assassin! Eric: Here you go Johny, want one Tony? Tony: Later. Terry: Anyone want some chips? Tony: Here comes Logan, maybe he wants a chip. Terry: !@#$! You two shouldn't be acting like this, Logan's a great man and doesn't deserve this disrespect. Tony: Now can I have one of those Eric? Eric: Sure thing. Terry: Logan is at ringside and what's going on? Tony: Well over here, I'm having some chips and XTC. Terry: No, in the ring. The Assassin is distracting the referee. Tony: So. Terry: Logan is in, and he just DDTed Machine. Tony: Interesting. Where's the dip? Eric: I think Johny's hogging it. Johny: Sorry. Terry: The assassin is making the pin, and he got the three count! Tony: *Burp* Terry: Shut UP! Logan is in the ring congratulating O'Hara. Tony: Look at the rampway, it's Vinnie "the Bronx" Bomber with a mic. Vinnie: Logan!! You little Irish Green Cheese! (( He pulls out a wooden shamrock and lights it on fire )) It's about to get hot for you buddy (( He throws down the shamrock and leaves )) (( Gary Trudeau approaches the ring now and begins to announce the next match... )) Gary Trudeau: Our next match is for a 20 minute time limit, Making his way to the ring is Intensity.. (( Intensity makes his way to the ring as the crowd gives him a mixed reaction... )) Gary Trudeau: And his opponent making his return after a long absence is Big Daddy Butler.... (( Big Daddy Butler makes his way to the ring with a decent crowd reaction... )) Terry: Well the Gerbal of love is back.. Eric: Well he is proclaiming to be a championship body builder... Tony: Well Eric you are a Kickboxing and Tae Kwan Do champion... Johny: Butler and Intensity lock up.. Terry: Butler with no ground gained over Intensity... Johny: Intensity with the boot to the mid section and a hammer drop on Butlers back... Eric: Butler to the mat and back up quick with a jab to the ribs of Intensity.. Terry: Butler with a scooop slam... Tony: Butler with a headlock on Intensity... Johny: Butler with a belly to belly suplex... Eric: Wow Butler with control of this match now.. Terry: Butler with a spinning toe hold... Tony: Butler helping Intensity up and picking him up for the spinning neck breaker... Eric: This is interesting alot of classic wrestling moves being used here... Terry: Butler with a leg drop... Johny: Butler applies the Cobra Clutch on to Intensity... Tony: Butler lets him go , and helps him up and look its the Cookie Crunch... Terry: Butlers finisher Butler with the 1...2...3...and the win what a return for the Gerbal of Love... Eric: Who's next on the line up.... Tony: Well lets get back down to ringside and let you know... (( Camera focuses on Gary Trudeau at ringside ready to announce the next match.... )) Gary Trudeau: Ladies and Gentleman this next match was added at the last minute for a a special edition to Saturday Night Main Event , Making his way to the ring Shane Warner... (( Warner makes his way to the ring amongst a mixed crowd reaction.... )) Gary Trudeau: And his opponent making his way to the ring now is Big Eliem the big man of the House of Pain.... (( Big Eliem walks to the ring amongst a hail of boo's... )) Terry: Eliem starts pounding Warner and he signals to the back.... Tony: Its the HOP running to the ring and all of them begin pounding Warner... Eric: Their pounding him into the mat... Johny: It seems their holding him up for The Outlaw... Terry: Yes the Outlaw kick what a hit.. Tony: Look their picking him up again and Randy Cross is stepping forward and is screaming at him... Terry: Look Cross just slapped him... Eric: Cross is begining to start punching Warner... Johny: Look its the STB running to the ring with chairs... Terry: Look out Randy... Tony: The Outlaw ducks as ChiKlyn Nails Big Eliem with a chair... Johny: House of Pain is running back to the locker rooms as STB is checking on Shane Warner... Tony: Now down to Bob's Driver on the Platform with his first interview with Gutter... (( Camera focuses on Gutter as he makes his way to the platform.... )) Bob's Driver: Gutter what is your reaction to your disheartening loss to RavenCrow the former member of The Four Horseman.... Gutter: Well it was a match that is all... Bob's Driver: What do you have planned for your future here in the WWA... Gutter: I plan on shaking things up a bit , and bringing down the house with a surprise announcement in a few weeks.... Bob's Driver: This announcement would this include the Horsemen... Gutter: NO!! That is all i wish to talk about... Bob's Driver: Thank you Gutter , My next Interview is with Mad Coil.... (( Gutter makes his way back to the locker rooms as Mad Coil and Gutter stop face to face to one another and they look each other square in the face and nod and then step past one another.... )) Bob's Driver: Mad Coil what do you plan on doing now that you have made your decision on going after Hollywood Johny Tambo.... Mad Coil: I plan on showing the upstart what its like to issue challenges against those that he thinks that he is bigger and badder than, Time has come to take the wind out of his sails and sink his boat faster than his Movie career went down the tubes... Bob's Driver: What is the goal of Warriors Pride... Mad Coil: None of your business im not going to reveal our plans at all.. Bob's Driver: What do you think of the big man issue still going on in the WWA... Mad Coil: All know that I am one of the biggest here but the title goes to those that can hold their own and prove they are the biggest the time will soon come where i will show myself once again, But for nopw i have plans... Bob's Driver: Thank you Mad Coil for your time... (( Camera fades out as Mad Coil works the crowd as he shakes and and gives high fives on the way to the locker rooms..... )) Terry: Wait did i see that right looks like Mad Coil and Gutter have something planned.. Tony: Could be respect for two big wrestlers.... Eric: Well could be , lets get to our main event... Johny: Our main event is for the TV title... (( Gary Trudeau is in the ring ready to announce the Main Event... )) Gary Trudeau: Ladies and Gentlemen, Our Main Event is for the TV title , It is a one fall pin fall for a time limit of 20 minutes.....Approaching the ring now is Simply The Best Flynn Steele... (( Steele approaches the ring as the crowd gives him a great response.... )) Gary Trudeau: And his opponent the current Reigning TV title champion Jack of the Four Horsemen... (( Jack approaches the ring as the crowd goes wild .... )) Tony: This has the makings of a great match... Terry: They lock up and Jack throws Steele across the ring... Johny: What a power display from Jack.. Eric: Steele up slowly as Jack approaches him.. Tony: Steele clips Jacks knees.. Johny: Jack to the mat.. Eric: Steele applies a figure four quickly... Tony: Jack pushes himself up and pulls himself to the the ropes Eric: The ref tells Steele to release Jack... Terry: Jack up but he is favoring his right knee... Johny: Steele take it to Jacks knee again..... Eric: Steele seems to be kicking and stomping Jacks knee... Terry: Jack is up and delivers a Lariot to Steele leaving him stunned on the mat... Johny: Steele helped up by Jack and Jack with a powerslam ... Eric: That looks like it hurt , Jack is staggering... Tony: Jack is approaching Steele , Steele with a quick kick to the midesection.. Terry: Jack plows Steele into the corner and Steele sinks to the mat... Johny: Jack begins stomping Steele.... Eric: The ref breaks the two as he backs Jack away from Steele... Johny: Steele dives forward taking out Jacks knee again... Tony: He is taking out the foundation of the big man.. Eric: Jack standing but limping delivers a vicious blow to Steele sending him to the mat... Johny: Jack helps up Steele sending him to the rope .. Tony: Jack grabs Steele and lifts him for the famine slam... Terry: Steele kicks his legs up shifting the weight of Jack and causing him to fall and he rolls him up for the 1...2...3...and the win... Eric: We have a new TV champion Flynn Steele... Tony: Well folks thats it for Saturday see you next week.....
