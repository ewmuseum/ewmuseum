WWA Friday Night Fights October 20th, 2000 Scene opens with Tracer Bullet and Melissa Camero are walking from their car to the entrance in the parking lot. Suddenly New Star is standing in front of them. Tracer : Missy, back up. This guy is nuts. New Star just grins as Missy steps back, reaching into her purse. NS : You have nothing to fear from DEATH... TB : Right. Save it for the PPV Hannibal. NS : You shouldn't worry about me, but those around me. Tracer turns around and sees Stryker about to hit Missy with a kendo shinai. He dives and pushes her to safelty but takes the hit himself. Missy trys to pull out her weighted piano wire, but Sabbat is behind her and knocks the purse out of her hand. TB : GET HELP! Sabbat let's Missy run by. The trio triple teams Tracer. New Star motions for them to stop. NS : Now why detective, did I do this? Answer that one. They walk away as the scene fades to the WWA logo and than into the Friday Night Fights intro. We enter the Freedom Hall at the University of Louisville, Kentucky. Pyrotechnics shoot off as the camera pans around, checking out the sold out crowd and their signs. Including "Tracer vs Strawman Jones", "New Star is the Future of the AWA", and "The Doc is in the House!". The camera stops on the commentators who are in the ring. Jimmy : Ladies and gentlemen, welcome to World Wrestling Alliance Friday Night Fights in Louisville!!! Crowd pops at the mention of their town. Jimmy : I'm Jimmy Rose and this is my co-host... And our main event... Fat Tony takes the mike back again. Fat Tony : Brian Jones becomes your NEW WWA World Champion. That bastard New Star. Buck : New Star did this? I'm going to get that little... Buck walks away as EMTs catch up and look over Tracer. The scene fades to a commerical.
