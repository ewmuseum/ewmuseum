WWA — 18 Feb 1998

18 Feb 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Wednesday Febuary 18, 1998 The screen opens up to the the NCW banner. Then.... Northeast Champion Wrestling flashes through the screen and leaves the NCW Banner Then firework type explosions go off around the Banner. The Banner fades... then the screen shows Lehman College's The Apex. The screen then goes to the inside to show the fans jumping and screaming at the Camera. The camera pans over the the complex and then the camera finds Tony Bailey, Terry Valentine and Johny Morehouse sitting behind the table in the broadcast area. Tony : Welcome everybody to Wednesday Night War. Tonight we'll see the New York City title on the line as the champion, Angel Reaper, defends it against the former member of the Triple Threat, "Brutal" Brian Cairo. We'll also see the grudge between the newcomer Shaman and "Wild Thing" Chris Rogers come to a head. We've otherwise got a lot of newcomers showing their skills against one another tonight, so lets get to the ring where Gary Trudeau is, ready to announce the first match. The camera zooms down to the ring were we see Gary Trudeau in the center on the ring with a microphone. Gary : Ladies and gentlemen, the following match this evening is set for one fall with a ten minute time limit. Introducing first, weighing in at 250 lbs, and hailing from Tombstone, AZ, John Bishop! "Smoke on the water" by Deep Purple plays over the PA system as John Bishop comes down the aisle wearing a sleeveless black denim duster. He is met with a mixed reaction. Gary : And his opponent, weighing in at 295 lbs, and hailing from New Port Richey, FL, "Lighning" Joe Clapp! "My Friend of Misery" By Metallica starts over the PA as "Lightning" Joe comes from out back. He walks to the ring through the cheers from the crowd, smiling. Johny : Both men shake hands in the ring and begin circling. Tony : Bishop's debute here, but Clapp's already made his name winning two weeks ago. Terry : I don't like either of them. Johny : You wouldn't. Terry : What's that supposed to mean? Tony : Clapp and Bishop lock up. Johny : Clapp with a size advantage forces Bishop to the corner. Tony : Bishop flies to the other side of the ring and hits hard. Terry : Bishop nailed to the back of the head with a big clothesline. Johny : Clapp with the quick cover. Terry : Bishop kicks out. Bishop with a face rake. Tony : Clapp whiped to the ropes. Terry : Bishop lifts the knee. Johny : Bishop kicks the head of Clapp a few times and goes for a pin. Tony : Kickout by Clapp. Terry : Bishop to the ropes tries a tackle, Clapp catches him in a reverse headlock. Johny : Clapp nails his "snake driver" and goes for the pin. Tony : Easy one two three for Joe Clapp here tonight. Johny : Well thats just one of our debute's tonight, but both men new. Terry : We have too many unworthy new comers. Why'd Harrington let them in? Tony : Because he is the commissioner and nobody else could. Terry : Well I just can't wait, later tonight the House is in action and we will also get to see that looser Shaman shown what the real americans can do. Johny : Actually, Native Americans are the "real americans" as you so nicely put it. Terry : Who won the wars? Tony : If memories of history serve me right, the French, Spanish and English. The United States wasn't even around that far back. Terry : Who cares. Let's get this night moving. The camera zooms down to the ring were we see Gary Trudeau in the center on the ring with a microphone. Gary : Ladies and gentlemen, the following match this evening is set for one fall with a ten minute time limit. Introducing first, weighing in at 237 lbs, and hailing from Los Vegas, NV, "The Ice" Jake Hetti! "Master of Puppets" by Metallica plays over the PA system as He walks to the ring through the cheers from the crowd. Gary : And his opponent, weighing in at 245 lbs, and hailing from Miami, FL, Kue! "Unforgiven II" By Metallica starts over the PA as Kue comes from out back. He walks to the ring through the the mixed crowd, but he doesn't seem to mind at all. Terry : I don't know about you two, but there's too much Metallica in these shows for my taste. Johny : Well they are a good band, I guess they'be a big following here in the NCW. Tony : The bell and Jake hits the ropes. Johny : Kue follows him and catches him with a high knee lift. Terry : That's one way to keep a high flier grounded. Johny : Kue with a series of kicks to Jake. Tony : Jake staggers to his feet, Kue sweeps the legs and drops an elbow. Terry : He's got some speed, hasn't he. Tony : I'm impressed. His Martial skills are quite good too. Johny : Hetti lifted for a brainbuster suplex. Tony : Jake with a kickout, again staggering to his feet. Terry : Into the ropes, flying sidekick. Johny : Kue rolls under him and nails him with a spinning back kick to the head. Tony : Kue climbs the corner and waits. Terry : Hetti up again, and Kue delivers his "Corner pocket". Tony : Kue hangs up Hetti in the corner and delivers it again, barely holding the prone fore. Terry : Kue locks on his "Double bank". The ref sounds the bell. Tony : I think those two Corner Pockets knocked him out. Johny : That was all unnecessary. He should have just pinned him. Terry : Maybe he wants to show people he should have that title shot he's asking after. Johny : Well it wouldn't be the first time someone has recieved a title shot from the bottom of the ranks. Angel Reaper himself was given one early on too. Terry : And how long until he won it? Johny : It was a while, but his matches kept being interfered with. Terry : Excuses. The camera zooms down to the ring were we see Gary Trudeau in the center on the ring with a microphone. Gary : Ladies and gentlemen, the following match this evening is set for one fall with a ten minute time limit. Introducing first, weighing in at 243 lbs, and hailing from Memphis, TN, R. U. Retty! "Strutter" by Bob Seagerplays over the PA system as he struts to the ring through the cheers from the crowd. Once he hits the ring he begins to pose and strut around. Gary : And his opponent, weighing in at 300 lbs, and hailing from Death Valley, CA, Doomsday! "Bad to the Bone" starts over the PA as Doomsday comes from out back. The crowd gives a mixed reaction. Johny : Well, there's some non Metallica. Tony : Doomsday attacking Retty before the bell. Terry : That's a good man, use that size. Johny : Doomsday with massive forearms to the back of Retty. Tony : Both men debuting here. Terry : Retty beat to the ground, like he should have been at Final Friday. Johny : What do you mean? Terry : He interfered with the win Nikodemous was going to get over Payne of course. Johny : Nikodemous was disqualified. Terry : So, Retty still attacked him, when Darken specifically told everyone that there was nobody not involved to be there. Tony : Well Retty was hired as a cameraman it seemed. Maybe you should confront Darken about it. Terry : No thanks. Johny : Retty still on the mat, Doomsday kicking him. Tony : Retty finally rolls out of the ring to clear his head. Terry : Doomsday walks outside of the ring. Johny : Retty with a thumb to the eyes whips Doomsday into the post. Tony : Retty whips him to the stairs. Johny : The ref breaks the count as Retty rolls in. Slowly, Doomsday follows. Terry : Retty driving an axehandle to the neck of Doomsday. Tony : Doomsday on the recieving end of the vicious beating this time. Johny : Retty with a snap suplex on Doomsday. Tony : Retty with his "Retty Plex" but the size of Doomsday just too much for him. Terry : Thats the problem with those moves, a big man can't help but power out. Johny : Retty with an elbow drop, and another. Tony : Retty choking Doomsday with the ropes and again throws Doomsday with his Retty Plex. Johny : Doomsday didn't power out this time, Retty gets the one two three. Terry : Yeah, you just know everything. Tony : Nope, that's my job. Terry : You two make me sick. Johny : What do you say Tony, should we call Darken over to set up a match? Tony : Gee, I think a two on one handycap match would be fun. Terry : Don't even think about that. The camera zooms down to the ring were we see Gary Trudeau in the center on the ring with a microphone. Gary : Ladies and gentlemen, the following match this evening is set for one fall with a ten minute time limit. Introducing first, weighing in at 240 lbs, and hailing from Washington, D.C., "Captain America" Tommy Pride! The American National Anthem plays over the PA system as "Captain America" walks to the ring in the American colors, and flag. The crowd boos him severely. Gary : And his opponent, weighing in at 300 lbs, and hailing from parts unknown, Styx! The crowd cheers a little for Styx as he walks to the ring, unaccompanied by either manager or music. Johny : Maybe we should have put Retty against Pride and Styx against Doomsday. Terry : Why? Johny : Well, look at them, the size differences are almost identical, though Pride and Styx are almost the same size. Tony : Size didn't help Doomsday all that much, who knows if it will help Styx. Terry : Pride nails Styx as he comes into the ring. Tony : Styx on the mat outside, Pride follows him out. Terry : Styx rammed head first into the ring post not once, not twice, but five times. Johny : Pride rolls in to break the count. Tony : Styx reverses an irish whip and Pride nails the security rail. Terry : Pride moves out of the way as Styx hits the rail with a stinger splash. Johny : Pride climbs into the ring and waits. Tony : Pride breaks the count at nine. Styx climbs in at six. Terry : Styx dropped with a DDT while hanging onto the ropes getting in. Johny : Styx with a kickout, a near fall. Tony : Pride with a fistdrop to the face of Styx. Johny : Styx to his feet. Clothesline by Pride only staggers him. Terry : Styx misses the clothesline, pride jumps him from behind. Johny : It's his "American Dream". Tony : Big man down to one knee. Terry : Styx in a sitting position as the ref raises his hand. Johny : That's one. And that's two. Tony : The hand drops the third time and our winner, "Captain America" Tommy Pride. Terry : And listen to the adoration of the fans. Johny : They're booing. Terry : They don't know real talent. The camera zooms down to the ring were we see Gary Trudeau in the center on the ring with a microphone. Gary : Ladies and gentlemen, the following match this evening is set for one fall with a ten minute time limit. Introducing first, weighing in at 255 lbs, and hailing from Buffulo, NY, Brian Divine! "Back in Black" by ACDC plays over the PA system as he walks to the ring through the cheers from the crowd. Smiling and acknowledging them, Divine enters the ring and raises his hands. Gary : And his opponent escorted to the ring by his wife, Velvet Blackheart, weighing in at 195 lbs, and hailing from Portland, OR, Michael Blackheart! "Kickstart my Heart" by Motley Crue starts over the PA as comes from out back. The crowd gives a loud cheer for the former champion and his lovely wife. Johny : Blackheart a former New York City title holder. Tony : He lost it at Rage in a Cage '98 to Angel Reaper in a three way match. Johny : The ref calls the bell and this match is underway. Tony : The two men with a quick lockup and Divine with an armbar. Johny : Reversed by Blackheart with some flips, Divine in a chickenwing. Terry : Back elbow makes Blackheart release the hold. Tony : Blackheart to the ropes, and nails Divine with a flying shoulderblock. Terry : Both men out of the ring, Divine smacks the smaller Blackheart's head into a table. Tony : Blackheart blocks another attempt and elbows him. Johny : Blackheart with a snapmare onto the table. Tony : Divine rolled in by Blackheart. Johny : Blackheart in with a springboard legdrop. Terry : Kickout at two for Divine. Johny : Irish whip by Blackheart. Terry : Reversed by Divine. Tony : Divine with a standing dropkick on Blackheart. Johny : Blackheart just staggered into Velvet, she smashed into a table. Terry : She has no right to be on the apron anyways. Johny : A lot of managers go up there to encourage their wrestlers. Blackheart outside. Tony : Blackheart ignores the count as he pulls his wife from the broken table. Terry : And Divine with the win, by countout. Johny : And this is truly an upset. Tony : Divine was expected to lose to Blackheart the vetran, but it seems that outside events have cost Blackheart the match. Terry : That's what he gets for bringing a woman to the ring. Johny : It was his manager. Terry : That makes no difference. Tony : Hey, are you going to argue all night? The House is up Terry, stop complaining. The camera zooms down to the ring were we see Gary Trudeau in the center on the ring with a microphone. Gary : Ladies and gentlemen, the following match this evening is set for one fall with a ten minute time limit. Introducing first, weighing in at 285 lbs, and hailing from New York City, NY, The Neckbreaker! "Demonstrate My Style" by Madball plays over the PA.The Neckbreaker walks to the ring with Mark Lynch, boos surround them. Neither seems to mind. Gary : And his opponent, weighing in at 270 lbs, and hailing from St. Paul, MN, "The Punisher" Aric Crockett! "Hey man, Nice shot" by Filter plays as Aric and Randy Cross walk to the ring through the same round of boos that accompanied the Neckbreaker and Mark Lynch. They smile. Johny : Another debute against a vetran. Tony : Neckbreaker has an incredible win loss record in other federations, but as we all know, this is the NCW. Terry : Besides, he's against Aric, nobody beats the House. Johny : I- Terry : Don't even think of saying it. Tony : Neither man waits for the bell and we are seeing a nice display of punches right now. Johny : Neckbreaker with the upper hand using his size to drive Aric to the mat with forarms. Tony : Aric under heavy fire. Terry : Aric rolls from the ring and the ref keeps the Neckbreaker there. Tony : Aric and Cross confiring. Johny : Aric back in, a kick to the stomach of Neckbreaker. Tony : Aric with a facelock. Terry : Neckbreaker taken down with a bulldog. Johny : Aric drops the knee to the shoulder of the Neckbreaker a few times. Terry : Aric with the pickup and a big shoulder breaker. Tony : Aric with a quick cover, 1 2 kickout. Terry : Aric stomping the UKA member. Johny : Neckbreaker trips Aric, Neckbreaker on top with a series of punches. Terry : Aric rolls and starts punching. Tony : The ref calls the break. Finally the two men do. Johny : Aric with some chops sends Neckbreaker to the ropes. Terry : Aric with a nice high knee sends Neckbreaker down. Tony : Aric drops the knee to the shoulder of the Neckbreaker a few more times. Terry : There it is, Aric has on "The Punisher". Tony : Neckbreaker struggles to power out. Johny : Neckbreaker submits and Lynch dives in the ring, Aric rolls out. Tony : Seems like Cross and Crockett are taunting Lynch. Terry : Wait a minute, whats this. Johny : Nikodemous from behind nails Randy Cross with flying dropkick to the back of his right knee. Tony : Nikodemous rolls to his feet and into the ring. Johny : Now it's the UKA's turn to laugh at the House as Aric helps Randy up. Terry : That was just uncalled for. Tony : A lot of things these two stables do is uncalled for. Terry : No, just the UKA. The House is always called for. Johny : You're a hipocrit. Tony : All the men are finally out of the ring and surrounding area. Let's go to Gary Trudeau for the next match. The camera zooms down to the ring were we see Gary Trudeau in the center on the ring with a microphone. Gary : Ladies and gentlemen, the following match this evening is set for one fall with a ten minute time limit. Introducing first, escorted to the ring by his manager, Bonny McLain, weighing in at 215 lbs, and hailing from London, England, Britainicca! "Princess of the Universe" by Queen plays over the PA as Britainicca walks to the ring. The crowd cheers the englishman and his lovely manager Bonny McLain, who seems to be dressed in a hotel robe, walking to the ring. Gary : And his opponent, weighing in at 210 lbs, and hailing from Sweetwater, TX, "Demented" Drake Blackthorne! "Unforgiven" by Metallica plays as Drake walks out alone. He raises the "soul pole" at Britainicca and stips some dip onto the ramp. He then points the "Soul Pole" at Bonny and makes some actions that cause the screen to blur. Johny : Drake isn't entering the ring, he's just walking around it. Tony : He appears to be pursuing Bonny who's backing away just as fast. Terry : Nah, he's afraid of Britainicca, because all of the UKA are cowards. Tony : Britainicca sneaks out of the ring and walks up behind Drake. Terry : Drake swings the "Soul Pole" around and attacks Britainicca. Tony : Britainicca with a low blow makes Drake drop the "soul pole". Johny : Britainicca tackles Drake to the ground and plugs his nose and mouth. Terry : Oh my, he's just made Drake swallow that chewing tabacco of his. Johny : Drake coughing up on the mat, Britainicca rolls in. Tony : The ref calls the bell. Terry : What's this? Drake hasn't even entered the ring yet. Johny : He started a fight, the ref called it. Tony : Some security members grab the "soul pole" as a fan tries to make a dash for it. Terry : Thieves, they just walked off. Johny : Drake rolls in and Britainicca sends him back out with a massive shoulderblock. Tony : Drake seems to be a little bigger than his last match, but it doesn't seem to be helping here. Terry : Look, "the chick" is coming to the ring. Johny : Brian Cairo's chick? Why is she here? Tony : She draws back to throw something at Britainicca as the referee is counting on Drake. Johny : The save made by Bonny who just plain lays out "The Chick". She's got one hell of a right cross. Tony : Officials run in to take an unconcious "chick" to the back. Johny : The crowd cheers Bonny. Terry : Meanwhile, during all that, Drake has gotten into the ring and taken control. Johny : With an eye gouge none the less. Tony : Drake sends Britainicca to the ropes and Britainicca comes off with a big flying clothesline. Johny : Drake hurt now, Britainicca with a summersalt legdrop. Tony : Drake out at two. Johny : Britainicca goes up top and hits a moonsault. Terry : Again Drake barely manages a kickout. Tony : That's it, Britainicca with his "Union Jack Bomb" goes for the pin. Terry : One two three and the UKA suffers another loss tonight. Tony : Britainicca wins his debute here, with the protection, it seems, of his manager, Bonny McLain. Johny : I can still see that punch, can we see it in slow replay? A shot of Bonny McLain landing a right cross on the left temple of "the chick" sending her sprawling to the floor, a pile of white salt coming from her throwing hand. The camera zooms down to the ring were we see Gary Trudeau in the center on the ring with a microphone. Gary : Ladies and gentlemen, the following match this evening is set for one fall with a fifteen minute time limit. Introducing first, weighing in at 310 lbs, and hailing from the Navajo Desert, Shaman! The crowd goes wild for the native american who walks with purpose to the ring while "Devil Man" by White Zombie blasts over the sound system. The cheers go to boos as the music is replaced by the now infamous Church Bells. Gary : And his opponent, weighing in at 240 lbs, and hailing from Lakeland, Florida, "Wild Thing" Chris Rogers! A massive collection of boos and hisses asail rogers as he walks to the ring, smiling all the while and smoking something that looks like a native american peace pipe. Johny : Shaman not waiting for the bell nails Rogers with a plancha. Terry : Rogers down hard, his pipe shatters on the mat. Johny : Shaman picks up Rogers and the bell sounds. Tony : Shaman with a running powerslam onto that broken glass. Terry : That should get him DQed. Johny : Why? Rogers brought it. Tony : Shaman in to break the count. Terry : Rogers whipped into the ring post and a tackle drives him in farther. Tony : Rogers whipped into the stairs. Johny : Shaman again sends Rogers into the stairs, this time the bottom. Tony : I almost thought Rogers wasn't going to come out. I mean those stairs are hollow. Terry : He almost did get stuck in I think. Johny : Shaman again breaks the count. Tony : Shaman with a gorrilla press slams Rogers onto the guard rail and rolls him in. Johny : I guess Wild thing is being forsaken by any form of god in this. Terry : Wild Thing into the ropes and Shaman with a big powerslam. Johny : Shaman with the pin. No, he pulls Rogers up. Tony : This is like what Rogers did to Fidel. Terry : Rogers into the corner flips over. Johny : Shaman with another plancha nails Rogers on the outside. Terry : Wild thing whipped into the far guard rail and Shaman rolls in. Johny : Shaman with the Psychadelic Splash onto Rogers. Terry : That was stupid, if he missed and hit that cement he'd be done. Tony : I think Shaman is passed caring. Johny : Rogers into the post again. Tony : Rogers rolled in by shaman again, it doesn't look like he's concious anymore. Terry : Shaman with a powerbomb near the ropes. Tony : He reverses it and now drops Rogers throat first on the top rope. Johny : In the corner, Shaman shoves Rogers face into the turnbuckles. Tony : One two and three on the top, four five and six on the middle. Johny : Seven eight and nine on the bottom. And woa, another whole ten on the mat. Tony : Shaman sets the limp form of Rogers on the top turnbuckle. Terry : And Shaman lands his "Thunder Clap". Johny : And if you didn't hear it, you sure heard a clap of thunder. Tony : Roger's head bounces off the mat but Shaman drives him back down for the pin. Johny : An easy one two three for Shaman. Tony : Shaman rolls Rogers body out of the ring and sits in the middle, legs crossed. Johny : He raises his hands up to the ceiling and chants a few words and leaves. Terry : A big wednesday night debute match for Shaman. Tony : This is his second match with the NCW, his first at Final Friday against Steihl. Johny : Well, I think this will leave a more lasting impression. Terry : On Rogers at least. First he gets some psycho with a wooden cross to pound on him and then an enraged indian. Tony : Native American. Terry : Whatever. The camera zooms down to the ring were we see Gary Trudeau in the center on the ring with a microphone. Gary : Ladies and gentlemen, the following match this evening is set for one fall with a twenty minute time limit. Introducing first, the challenger, weighing in at 240 lbs, and hailing from the Chicago, IL, "Brutal" Brian Cairo! The crowd boos as "Beautiful People" by Marilyn Manson plays on the PA. "The Chick" walks beside him, teasing the crowd, as Brian walks without saying a thing to the ring. Gary : And now introducing, the New York City Champion, weighing in at 252 lbs, and hailing from New York City, New York, Angel Reaper! Angel Reaper walks out through the curtain as "Antichrist Superstar" by Marilyn Manson begins to play on the PA. The crowd boos him, but not nearly as severely as Cairo. Reaper stares down "the chick" and climbs into the ring. Brian, sitting in the corner, makes no move for him. The ref takes the belt. Johny : The bell is rung and Brian walks over to Reaper. Terry : Reaper stands there as Brian smacks him, he's smiling. Tony : Reaper spits right in the eyes of Brian. Johny : Reaper takes Brian down with a clothesline. Tony : "The Chick" jumps to the apron and the ref goes to reprimand her. Terry : Brian with a thumb to the eyes and then with a russian legsweep. Johny : Reaper counters with a hiptoss. Tony : Reaper kicks Brian on the mat. Terry : Here comes "Demented" Drake. He's doing the same thing to "The Chick" he did to Bonny. Johny : This man is sick. I hope Harrington fines him. Terry : For what? Johny : Lude conduct for all I care. Tony : Brian taken down with a body slam by Reaper. Terry : Drake walks away, see he caused no trouble. Johny : Brian with a european uppercut. Reaper staggers a couple steps. Terry : Brian grabs the hair of Reaper and slams his face to the mat. Tony : Reaper on his feet, Brian with another eye gouge. Terry : Suplex by Brian. Johny : Someone else is coming through the curtains. Terry : Look, Drake just came from beside the ramp and tackled him through the curtains. Johny : Drake and Outlaw brawling at the top of the ramp. Terry : Outlaw, what's he doing here? Tony : Well obviously he was coming to the ring. Johny : I guess Drake doesn't like him, or maybe he is just trying to keep the match fair. Terry : Yeah, the Outlaw is a cheater. Johny : So are all the members of the House of Pain. Tony : Outlaw and Drake go through the curtains. Terry : Brian with another scoop slam on Reaper. Tony : Now who's coming? Terry : Jack? He's going to cheat. Johny : He's even got a chair. Tony : Both Reaper and Brian see him. Brian's trying to shu him away and Reapers calling him. Terry : Jack walks to ringside and sits in his chair. Tony : Reaper with the Rollup on Brian. Terry : Brian out at two. Tony : Reaper drops the elbow, nobody home. Johny : Eye gauge by Brian, blocked and returned by Reaper. Terry : Double underhook suplex by Reaper. Tony : "The Chick" is yelling at Jack, he's only laughing at her. Terry : Brian with another eye gauge, and follows it with a DDT. Johny : Brian yelling at Jack. Tony : Reaper with a quick rollup. Johny : One two and three. Reaper retains his coveted title. Tony : Brian looks pissed, he's going over to Jack. Terry : Jack quickly chokeslams Brian who got in his face. Johny : Jack gets in the ring with his chair while Reaper is being handed his belt. Tony : Reaper crumples under the blow to his head. Johny : Jack uses his chair again as he powerbombs Reaper onto it. Terry : Look, he's shoving a cookie into Reaper's mouth. Tony : Jack looking in the camera. He's saying something about Bob. Johny : I don't think he's gotten over Final Friday. Tony : Well, that's it for tonight folks, see you this Friday.
