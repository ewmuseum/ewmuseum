Randy looks surprised and just backs off... Thinking lets hear this. ChiKylin : What the F*CK is this Bruiser? We might not be FEAR or STB anymore but I would never expect this from you. Your turning your backs on us, then JOINING THE House of Pain. The very reason FEAR exists... And for this someone will have to pay. Isn't that right Mantis? Chi hands the mic over to Mantis. He pauses for a little while but than finally speaks. Mantis : Well.......actually..... Mantis cracks the microphone over Chi's head and than kicks him quickly in the face with a thrust kick. Bruiser comes up behind Chi and lifts him up and drops him painfully with his Agony. Mantis meanwhile quickly ascends the turnbuckle and as soon as Chi hits the mat, Mantis flies of the top and nails Chi square in the groin with the Nutcracker. Mantis and Bruiser quickly roll Chi out of the ring and Mantis turns to Cross and Outlaw. Cross pulls out another shirt and tosses it over to Mantis who quickly puts it on.:: Mantis : Thank you Randy. I've been sick and tired of his sh*t for ages now. Chi has always been holding me back, and he alone is the reason I no longer have my title. But anyways, I don't want to steal the stage, so I just want you all to know one small thing. Well, mainly this is for you Young Guns. No longer will there be a FEAR kicking at your heels. No, boys....now you have a whole entire army. Only two men of course, but it's an army compared to what you have ever faced. You like speed, and power........well your looking at the Blitzkreig. You've obviously seen the speed I posses, but never has it been honored with the power such as Nick Bruiser. March Madness will be a long night for you boys. Even longer for you Midcarder after the treatment you took a few minutes ago. Knowing you have to face Nick again could be devastating. I'll let you think about what you have just witnessed Young Guns.....and oh yeh.....Fare...Thee..Well.... Mantis tosses the mic back to Randy... And Randy smiles and then he gets a look of seriousness on his face... He pauses for a long moment waiting for the crowd to stop their booing... Randy Cross ( Taking a deep breath ): Well... I come to you this evening with a heavy heart... I made the WWA into what it is today. I was hear from day one and I was champ from day one. (The Outlaw gives a round of applause for Mantis and Nik Bruiser as they finish. He shakes hands with each one of them and says something to both men that cannot be heard by everyone. The Outlaw takes the mic from Mantis and slowly walks around the ring stopping by the GREAT ONE Randy Cross.) Outlaw: WHAT.....A NIGHT for the HOP. (looking and pointing at Mantis and Bruiser) and I assure you it will only get better. (The Outlaw motions to Cross) Man.....ole....man......what a dreadful time for the WWA to hear those terrifying words come out of your mouth. Randy, you and I talked about your retirement, but I've got to tell you it didn't really sink in until I heard you say it. You are truly the greatest wrestler ever to grace this Federations roaster and as a competitor I'm going to miss knowing your out there competing every night. As a challenger for that belt of yours......(laughing) I'm damn glad to hear your going to give someone else the chance to wear it. (Outlaw laughs a little more.) Randy, it's been great stand next to you and fighting the wars we have fought and although your hanging up the boots I'm damn glad to know your not walking away from the HOP. As the founder and the heart and soul of the HOP, it's your show to run. (The Outlaw shakes hands with Cross.......it's a long handshake with an intense stare....)
