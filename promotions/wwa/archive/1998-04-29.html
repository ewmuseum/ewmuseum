NCW Wednesday Night War — 29 April 1998

29 Apr 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
NCW Wednesday Night War Wednesday April 29, 1998 The screen opens up to the the NCW banner. Then.... Northeast Champion Wrestling flashes through the screen and leaves the NCW Banner Then firework type explosions go off around the Banner. The Banner fades... then the screen shows Lehman College's The Apex. The screen then goes to the inside to show the fans jumping and screaming at the Camera. The camera pans over the the Complex and then the camera finds Tony Bailey and Terry Valentine sitting behind the table in the broadcast area. Tony: Welcome ladies and gentlemen to the Apex in the Bronx, New York. We have a great show for you tonight. Tonight we see the MAN that just beat Randy Cross in a non Title match just last week. Steihl will be here. Also, Flynn Steele the veteran from the CWCW and GCW, to just name a couple of the feds he has been in, will be here. Angel Reaper will defend against Todd Logan for the New York strap. And the main event tonight will be the first true BIG MAN match. The winner will meet another big man at the upcoming PPV. Let's go to the first match tonight.... "Triumph" by The Wu-Tang Clan starts over the PA as "The Icon" Eric Stanely come out from the dressing rooms. Eric Stanley gets booed as he walks to the ring. "Triumph" is replaced by "Cry Little Sister". Steihl comes out of the back to a rather loud fan pop. Steihl strides to the ring and just ignores the fans. Terry : Oh goody, he comes Steihl... Yippee.. Tony : Steihl wastes no time as he attacks Stanely before the bell is rang. Johny : Steihl is laying kick after kick into the Icon as he rolls out of the ring to avoid the brutal onslaught of Steihl. Terry : Steihl runs against the far ropes and catapults through the sencond and third ropes and levels Stanely on the outside. Steihl is an idiot! Tony : I will tell you one thing. Not many big men can do that. Just ask the fallen Stanley. Terry : It looks like Steihl is getting up pretty slow too. Johny : Nonetheless, Steihl is first up and he grabs Stanley and throws him back into the ring breaking the refs count at 7. Tony : Steihl sends Stanely into the ropes and goes for a closeline. Terry : Stanely ducks and rebounds off the opposite ropes and hits Steihl with a dropkick. Steihl falls to the mat. Stanely is shaking the cobwebs out and walks over to Steihl and pulls him up by the hair. Tony : Uhhh, that was the wrong thing to do. Steihl puts a chokehold on Stanely. Johny : Stanely releases the hold and Steihl takes him up for a BIG COKESLAM! Terry : Oh man... I think Stanely is dead. He isn't moving. Tony : Steihl gets Stanely back up and sets him up for "THE STEIHL". The ref drops and counts to 3. Johny : An impressive win by Steihl. He should be inline for a shot at Cross's belt. Since he already holds a victory over him. Terry : Shut UP Johny... You don't know what you are talking about! Tony : No matter... Let's go to the next match... "Machinehead" by Bush starts over the PA. The Gambler enters the ring area along with the Bookie. The crowd boos as the Gambler enter the ring. "I Want it All" by Queen replaces Bush on the Speakers... Flynn Steele enters the arena to the cheers of the fans... Terry : I have a feeling I am not going to like MR. Steele. Tony : That doesn't surprise me Terry. Steele has an impressive set of credentials stacked up over the years. Terry : Who cares... This is Steele's debut in the NCW. As it is the Gambler's. Johny : Very true Terry... Anyways, there's the bell and we are under way. Tony : The two men lock up.. Terry : The Gambler with the quick advantage with a kick to the midsection. And a couple of quick double axe handles to the back of the "WILEY" veteran. Tony : Gambler sends Steele into the ropes and and into a big dropkick. Johny : The veteran pushes the drop kick to the side. And the Gambler falls on his back. The Gambler telegraphed that on a little bit. Terry : Steele immediately attacks the Gambler on the mat. HEY! Aren't fists illegal? Tony : Yes they are Terry, the ref is counting for him to stop. Steele then drops and elbowe onto the Gambler's knee. And another and another. Johny : The comments from the Gambler about Flynn Steele being a racist obviously rubbed Steele the wrong way. As Steele drops yet another elbow.... Terry : Steele stands up and says something about being "Simply the Best"... These stupid idiot fans actually believe him! Tony : The Gambler tries to stand up but falls back to the ground with an obviously injured left knee. Johny : Steele turns to the Gambler and picks him up and executes a textbook suplex. Steele right back to his feet and there is the sign for the "Steele Driver". Terry : Steele sends the Gambler into the far corner and is right behind him. Gambler hits the corner and Steele hits him with an elbow. Tony : Steele sets the Gambler up on the top turnbuckle and then goes up and "Steele Driver". Steele covers and the ref counts to 3. The crowd gets to their feets as the ref holds up Flynn Steele's hand. Johny : Impressive debut for the veteran in the NCW.... Terry : Wait till Cross gets him! Tony : Actually, Terry... I CAN'T Wait! Johny : Well lets go to the next match... "Back in Black" starts over the PA as "Mr Extreme" Jimmy Walker enters the ring area with very little fan support. As Walker enter the ring his music is replaced by Fire Truck sirens as the fans start to cheer for the new wrestler. Fire Storm enters the ring area with his manager Blaze. Terry : I really don't think I like this Fire Turck guy! Tony : Doesn't surprise me at all Terry! Also, his name is Fire Storm. Johny : Now that we have the names correct... Both wrestlers are in their NCW debuts tonight as the bells sounds and we are ready. Tony : Both of these wrestlers are in the lightweight category, we should have an exciting match as the two men lock up collar and elbow. Terry : Fire Storm backs the much smaller Mr Extreme to the ropes. OH... Fire Storm let his guard down as MR Extreme blasts him off of the break. Great move by Mr Extreme! Tony : If you say so Terry. Walker backs Fire Storm into the ropes and sends him across with an Irish whip. Johny : Walker with a leap frog and Fire Storm stops and takes Walker's head off as he turned. Tony : Fire Storm picks him up and hits a brainbuster in the middle of the ring. It looks like Jimmy Walker is in BIG trouble. Terry : Fire Storm again picks up Mr Extreme and sends him into the far corner. Oh no.... Tony : Here it comes... Fire Storm backs to the opposite corner and starts to run and hits the "Rain of Fire". Walker falls on his face. Fire Storm rolls him over and gets the pinfall. Johny : Fire Storm with his first win in the NCW. Let's go to the next match. It is for the CWC New York Title. "Jump Around" by House of Pain starts over the Public Address as Todd "The Irish Hammer" Logan enters the ring area. Logan is followed by the Outlaw and Big Eliem. The two members of the House of Pain and Logan enter the ring to a defening chorus of boos. House of Pain is replaced by Marilyn Manson's "Antichrist Superstar". Angel Reaper walks out with Havoc and Rocky Stevens as the crowd is not real happy to see these guys either. Total Anarchy just stares at the House of Pain as Reaper enters the ring. Johny : Harrington better get some extra security down here for this one. I think we are going to have a problem with both the House of Pain and Anarchy in the same ring. Terry : I wonder when Logan will become a member of the House of Pain? Tony : Maybe he is a man of HONOR Terry! Terry : Shut up Tony! The House of Pain has more honor than... Tony : Than WHAT Terry? Terry : Uh... There is the bell and we are ready to fight! Tony : Good call... Angel Reaper charges right in and Logan nails him with a clothesline. Followed up by an Elbow. Johny : Logan picks up Reaper and whips him into the ropes and he is going for another clothesline. Tony : Reaper ducks and Logan gets hit with a perfectly executed dropkick. Reaper immediately follows up with a fisherman's suplex. The ref is down to count... Logan kicks out out at 1. Terry : It is going take more than that to get Logan! Tony : Both men up and Reaper throws Logan out of the ring at the feet of Havoc and Stevens. Angel Reaper grabs the referee and starts yelling at him. Havoc and Stevens start to pound on Logan. Terry : Hey REF!! Look at what they are doing! Johny : The Outlaw and Big Eliem start running over and attack Havoc and Stevens. The Oultaw nails Havoc and Big Eliem clotheslines Stevens. Outlaw gives his Rhode Island belt to Logan. Tony : Oh... Angel Reaper through the ropes nails the Outlaw with a flying body block of some kind. It looks like the Outlaw is hurt. Reaper jumps up and nails Big Eleim staggering the big man back into the range of Havoc, who is on his feet again. Havoc delivers a huge savate kick ala the Outlaw. Big Eliem goes down and Reaper rolls into the ring where Logan already is. Johny : The ref is yelling at Havoc and the House of Pain to leave ring side. Inside the ring, Angel picks up Logan... Logan still has the Rhode Island belt. Logan nails Reaper with it and Reaper falls lifelessly to the ground. Logan throws the belt out of the ring. Terry : Logan applies the Irish Sleeper on in the middle of the ring. The ref happy about his gaining control on the outside turns and sees what is going on. He drops to the hapless Reaper and lifts up the arm. Three times Reaper drops his arm... The Ref calls for the Bell. Logan NEW New YOrk City Champion.. Tony : So much for a man of Honor! Logan rolls out of the ring with his new belt. Terry : Watch it Tony! The House of Pain retreat with their new champion. Johny : IT is now time for the Main Event. The Five way Elimination match for the NCW Big Man award for May! The Five big men enter the ring area one by one. Fidel, Thanatos, Raiden, Jack and Bob. Terry : So what are the rules for this match? Johny : Elimination match, falls count anywhere and no DQ. Terry : I am really glad to here that. Look at that. The House of Pain is all over Bob on his way to the ring after the bell sounded. Chris Rogers nails Bob from behind in the knee. Tony : Aric Crockett hits Bob with a running clothesline which has very little affect. Johny : Outlaw hits him with an Outlaw kick and Bob staggers back and is tripped by Rogers. Then all three men start beating on Bob. Here comes Big Eliem with a chair. Terry : Eliem drops the chair next to Bob. He picks him up and locks in a double underhook DDT. RIGHT onto the chair. Bob isn't moving. Big Eliem is standing over Bob and spits into his face. Tony : Here come Thanatos out of the ring with Jack close behind. Thanatos nails Big Eliem from behind. Big Eliem runs right into Chris Rogers. Running Rogers into the metal railing. The Outlaw goes for the save and Thanatos flattens Outlaw with a big chokeslam. Johny : Jack hits Thanatos from behind with a knee to the back. Thanatos falls to the ground from the impact. Jack then rolls Bob onto his back and goes for the cover. One of the two refs in there for the count. Jack pins Bob. Terry : Back in the ring Raiden is just sitting down against the corner ropes as he has already eliminated Fidel with his "Somersault Whiplash Diamond Bomb".... Tony : Jack gets up from the fallen Bob and grabs Thanatos from the cement. Jack drops Thanatos with a Piledriver onto the metal chair. Jack turns Thanatos over and goes for the pin. The ref counts to three and Thanatos is out. Johny : Jack turns his eyes towards the relaxing Raiden in the ring. He points and gets a weird smirk on his face. He starts to run for the ring as Raiden jumps up and is ready as Jack runs and slides into the ring. Tony : Raiden meets Jack with a double axe handle. And the two men start to beat each other with fists blazing. Here come Rocky Stevens with his 2x4 to the ring. This match has no DQ rules, so Stevens will be allowed to do his will with the 2x4. Terry : Inside the ring Raiden drops Jack with a powerbomb and looks to see Stevens a ring side. Raiden begins to laugh as he pushes Jack outside the ring at the feet of Stevens. Tony : Raiden turns to the crowd and yells something about Anarchy RULES! Who is that coming from under ring? Johny : It's the Black Scorpian! He is behind Stevens. Stevens winds up to hit Jack with the 2x4. Balck Scorpian grabs the board and grabs Stevens from behind and DOWN! "The Equalizer" on the concrete. Stevens isn't moving.... The Black Scorpian checks on Jack and then rolls into the ring. Tony : Raiden turns around to see the Black Scorpian standing there. Black Scorpian strikes the classic "ERIC DRAVEN" pose as Raiden just stares at him. Terry : No... Is that the Fan coming from the crowd. These stupid mindless drones are cheering like a bunch of idiots... Johny : The Fan enters the ring behind Raiden. Raiden is still staring at Black Scorpian. The Fan locks in the "Equalizer". Reverse DDT on Raiden from behind. Both the Black Scorpian and the Fan roll out of the rind. Terry : They are RUNNING away!! What cowards! Johny : The Black Scorpian helps Jack to his feet and pushes him into the ring. Jack shakes the cobwebs out and crawls over to the fallen Raiden. The referee counts to 3. Tony : Jack is the winner of the first True Big Man match. Jack turns and locks eyes with the Black Scorpian.... We are out of time.
