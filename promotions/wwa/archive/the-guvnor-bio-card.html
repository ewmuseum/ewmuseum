Black knee pads, white wrestling boots with gold trim. White tape on wrists. Personality Brash, straight-talking London wide-boy with an anarchic streak and a penchant for hand-to-hand combat and a desire to make lots of money. History Born and bred in what is now the London Borough of Tower Hamlets, Lansbury was taught all he knows by the streets of the East End of the English capital. His formative years were an education in books and numbers, but not the Shakespeare and algebra that most kids are used to, his schooling was in the art of staying out of the wrong people’s bad books, and that usually entailed finding some way to earn those people the cash they demanded. Lansbury’s way of ingratiating himself with the names that ran London’s underworld was through his birth-given talent for street fighting. For half a dozen years Lansbury moonlighted as a bare knuckled fighter (working some de-humanizing customer service job in the day) before working his way into the British wrestling scene. Slowly, he has made his name, under the guise ‘The Guv’nor’, as a ruthless and efficient brawler, while improving his technique and showmanship along the way, and now he is coming to America, with his long-suffering girlfriend Cheryl ‘Chez’ in tow, to make the millions he has always dreamed of.
