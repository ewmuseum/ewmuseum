WWA: Underground, February 5, 2010 (Episode #104)

5 February 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is Script of the WWA’s webcast series Underground, episode 104, staged in Toledo, Ohio, with a retirement promo, a tag match, a three-way United States title qualifier, an authority-figure segment announcing the coming card and a main event, plus a closing preview of the next episode.
WWA: Underground February 5, 2010 - Episode #104 Live from the Toledo Sports Arena in Toledo, OH Chapter I Somers Promo The show opens as "Can I Play With Madness" blasts over the PA and the crowd rise to their feet with raucous cheers. Mal Somers emerges from the backstage curtaining in casual attire, blue jeans and black "MALcoholics Anonymous" t-shirt and quickly makes his way into the ring. Jack: Welcome to Underground and it looks like Mal has something to say to kick things off. Denrol: That's the sound of thousands clicking off their webcasts. Mal paces the ring a few times and looks around the crowd before addressing them. Somers: Holy Toledo, it's great to be here in TOLEDO OHIO! The crowd cheers some more and Mal grins and nods at the response. Somers: That just about felt as good as pulverizing Intrepid's squishy skull with a steel chair this time last week! The crowd cheer some more. Somers: You all know the story by now…about a year ago I left the WWA on bad terms. I let the fame, the fortune and the power that comes with being a WWA superstar get to my head…and I ran my mouth off. Somers stares down at the mat, shaking his head at himself. Somers: The WWA was right to turf me out on my boney behind. I’ve come back now and that behind is a little beefier, what do you think ladies? Somers does a little jig and gets a decent reaction from the crowd. Somers: The one thing that I’ve learned in my year in exile is that no one…No One…is bigger than the WWA. All the supposed big names…all the mega stars, could all pack up and go right now and the WWA will continue on, just as strong as ever. Somers looks up toward the entrance and grins. Somers: It’s a lesson that I have learned and been humbled by…and it’s a lesson that that treacherous scumbag Insipid….I mean Intrepid should learn as well. The crowd cheers as Mal paces the ring again. Somers: I came back for one night only, to help the fed that has helped me endless times. I hung up the books twelve months ago and I intend to keep things that way. Crowd: Noooooo! Somers: That’s right Toledo…Ohio…USA…World! Last year, I was lamenting how time was passing me by, and then they turfed me out. My knees creak, by elbows pop, I walk with a little limp that gets bigger each time I see the Doc. I see all these new guys coming through and the things they do day in…day out, puts me to shame. Mal stops pacing, and looks to the rafters. Somers: The same is true, if not more true now. My year off has added the kilos and slowed me some more and now that probably will help me absorb some of the blows, but when I look at myself in the mirror each morning, I just don’t think I have it in me any more. The crowd boos at that statement. Somers: I only intended to referee that one match, but when the Insipid Intrepid punked me out, I just knew I had to stay just a little longer! Crowd: CHEER! Somers: Lucky for me, it was only a week longer as I smashed him to smithereens last week! Revenge was sweet…so it is with a heart full of regret that I am announcing my retirement from the WWA. Crowd: Booo! Somers: I’ve accomplished more than I ever imagined and showing the world what an easy beat Intrepid is last week was the icing on the cake. I intend to go out with a bang in the three-way match later tonight, give it my all…but if I win, I intend to hand my number one contendership to one of these great up and coming superstars. Denrol: Can this guy waffle or what? Jack: Denrol, one of the greats of the WWA has just announced his retirement and that’s all you can say? There will be many MALcoholics deeply devastated by this news…I know I am! Denrol: I've done the retirement thing a few times. It's nothing special. Somers: Thanks for the memor~ Somers is cut off as …'Must Kill' by Cavalera Conspiracy begins playing and not even a second after that the entrance ramp begins flashing with a quick burst of white strobe light and Intrepid comes out quickly holding 'the' chair in one hand and a microphone in the other. He stares out into the crowd grooving to the beat of the music, glaring at them through the eyes of his mask. He suddenly thrusts his arm into the air. Crowd: Boo! You suck! As soon as the crowd reacts, Intrepid runs down the entire aisle all the way to the ring. He dives onto the ring apron and slides into the ring on his stomach. When he reaches the opposite side of the ring, he pushes himself into a standing position. As the music dies off, the two stand staring at each for a very long, tense moment. Intrepid holds his hand up in a gesture to signal: ‘Wait a minute’. Intrepid lifts the chair over the top rope and hands it down to the ring attendant. Suddenly he turns and rushes Mal, and stops within inches of his face. Intrepid: What, you have a little sit-down with the WWA Fanatics and didn’t invite me? Somers: Rolls eyes and shakes his head in disgust You’re here now…brother! The crowd hushes down into a contained anxiety as Somers presses his into Intrepid’s masked face Somers: Fine…you have twisted my arm...I’ll do it punk! The crowd roars with cheers. Jack: What a reaction, the crowd’s emotions have been up and down like a rollercoaster these past few minutes! Intrepid: Good…if you win next week, you can retire gracefully and go to the old folk’s home. Somers: Great…because that is what will happen…and I will visit the old folks home…and visit your girlfriend! Mal turns to leave the ring. Intrepid: And when I win? Mal chuckles to himself. Somers: Heh, listen to me kid…if I don’t win next week…when hell freezes over and pigs fly…I will … pfft I don’t really care…I’ll be your valet for all I care… and even wear one of those funny inflatable sumo costumes! Mal steps out onto the ring apron and hops onto the floor before turning to face Intrepid Somers: Fine…you’ve got a deal! "Can I Play With Madness" blasts over the PA and the crowd rise to their feet with raucous cheers. Mal walks all the way to the entrance before turning to look at Intrepid. This staggers Black and lets Adams slip off of the turnbuckle. Black turns and Adams lays a boot into his gut, he follows this with a three quarters facelock of his own. Jack: And now Adams is looking for a Soul Crusher! Unbelievable! Black pushes Adams off though and manages to lock him up for a double underhook reverse DDT. Denrol: Black counters into the Malediction! Jack: What a back and forth exchange! Adams manages to counter into an odd jawbreaker though. Black steps back, Adams goes for another boot to the gut, setting up for another Soul Crusher. Black grabs his foot this time though. He spins Adams around, pushing his foot away, Adams fully turns around from the counter and Black drills him square in the temple with an electric roundhouse kick. It makes a noise like a gunshot. Adams lifelessly slumps to the canvas, this kick expending all the rest of his energy. Denrol: Another kick to the head! Jack: And Nicholas Adams isn’t getting up! An exhausted Black drops down for the cover, grinding a forearm into Adams face and hooking the leg for good measure. One! Two! Three! Ding, Ding! Trudeau: Your winner, Lucas Blaaaaaaaaaack! “Paint it Black” by the Rolling Stones comes on over the PA system and Black slowly stands, looking down on Adams, craning his neck from side to side as the referee holds his hand up. Tiffani, who almost seemed a phantom during the match, makes her way into the ring to stand alongside Black. Jack: What a back and forth match by these two! Denrol: And what a win for Black, pinning the WWA champion! The crowd boos as Black takes a second to look down on his worthy foe before rolling out of the ring and making his way up the aisle with Tiffani in tow. Denrol: We could be looking at the future of the WWA title on that ramp. Jack: A chilling prediction, but I can’t disagree with you. Black turns, taking a moment to coldly glare out at the crowd. The camera zooms in on his icy stare as his music plays. He turns and steps through the curtain. Jack: As noted by Beckett, this means it will be Lucas Black versus Jack Griffiths for the number one contendership on February 19. Denrol: That match should be good, but my money is on Black. Adams leans heavily against the ropes, looking to regain some of his spent energy, while the fans give him a standing ovation. Jack: Hard fought match here tonight and what will be a tough road ahead for Nicholas Adams as the World Title picture becomes more crowded and the heavy hitters of the WWA rankings emerge. Denrol: Heh, how about that? Here's one of those hitters right now. Batter up! The crowd's cheers for Adams quickly turn to boos, alerting him to danger. Adams whirls around, only to catch a Louisville Slugger in the gut. Adams doubles over and falls to his knees in the middle of the ring, while Ry Ballard stands over him. Jack: The One Man Show is attacking the champion! He came through the crowd, sneaking up on Adams from behind. Denrol: You say that as if that's a bad thing, or unexpected for that matter. That's been the One Man Show's MO since day one. You have to respect a guy who sticks with what works. With Adams on his knees, and the fans booing him mercilessly, Ballard holds the bat in both hands and back pedals into the ropes. He uses the momentum to come off and launches himself through the air. The air is filled with a dull thunk of wood meeting bone as he delivers what amounts to a diving forearm with the weapon. Jack: Oh come on now, there's no call for this. Adams just went toe to toe with Lucas Black, he's spent. Ballard has no business out here. Denrol: Adams can only blame himself for this. If he had taken Ballard seriously and stopped playing games, this probably wouldn't have happened. Ry looks down at Adams with a cold look on his face. There's no trace of arrogance there, just fury. The referee tries to intervene but gets hurled through the ropes for his trouble, bringing a fresh round of boos from the crowd. Ballard looks around at the fans, pointing out at them and then sweeping his arm around to include the whole arena. Then he starts putting the boots to Adams, who appears to be out. After several hard kicks, Ry drops down and straddles Adam's chest. He puts the bat across the champion's throat and pushes down, cutting off his air. Jack: Security needs to stop this. Ballard has lost it. Denrol: I'd say he's in total control myself. This isn't Ballard snapping, this is completely premeditated. Adams is helpless as Ballard chokes the life out of him. A few stray bits of trash get tossed into the ring. Finally, Ballard pulls back and stands up. He's breathing heavily and his grip is white knuckle around the barrel of the bat. Slowly, he points down at Adams with the bat, resting the end of it on his forehead. He speaks to Adams and though he doesn't have a mic, the camera and most of the fans at ringside hears what he says. OMS: Ignore me now. Ballard drops the bat as his music begins to play. He steps away and as he turns, that trademark smirk of his finally makes an appearance. He exits the ring and walks to the back, completely ignoring the fans. Jack: The One Man Show strikes against Nicholas Adams, trying to make a point. Denrol: I think you can say that the point has been made. Adams has been ignoring him for weeks and now by losing, he's screwed Ballard out of a chance at the WWA title. The scene fades to black.
