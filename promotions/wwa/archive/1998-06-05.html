WWA — 5 Jun 1998

5 Jun 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Friday June 5, 1998 The screen opens up to the the NCW banner. Then.... Northeast Champion Wrestling flashes through the screen and leaves the NCW Banner Then firework type explosions go off around the Banner. The Banner fades... then the screen shows the Bryce Jordan Center. The screen then goes to the inside to show the fans jumping and screaming at the Camera. The camera pans over the the Complex and then the camera finds Tony Bailey, Terry Valentine and Johny Morehouse sitting behind the table in the broadcast area. Tony : Hello and welcome to another jam packed NCW Friday Night Fight card. Tonight the Overlords of the Dark EXPLODE! That's right Steihl will challenge his CWC National and Northeastern tag partner Darken for the CWC New England Title. Terry : Don't forget to mention the REAL tag champs! The House of Pain will be in action tonight against those Marine guys. Johny : That's right the Commandant's Own are making their NCW return tonight. As Terry so elegantly put it, it is against the House of Pain, Randy Cross and The Outlaw. Terry : Speaking of the House of Pain, let's start this show off with a look at one of the NCW greats The Outlaw and his Saloon... The camera zooms into the backstage area where we see the Outlaw standing in front of a makeshift bar covered with bottles and glasses. The Outlaw is drinking what appears to be a mug of beer. The Outlaw : Well ladies and gentlemen tonight on the Saloon I will be blessed with the presence of a man who is a member of a rival stable, one which has done battle with my beloved House of Pain time in time again. A man who is partners with a man that I can't stand. This man is 1/2 of Northest and 1/2 of CWC National Tag Team Champions and soon to be New England Champion......Stiehl from the Overlords of the Dark. Steihl walks out towards the Outlaw to a fairly negative crowd responce. His shoulders are covered with the two gold belts he currently holds. As he approaches the Outlaw, the Outlaw extends his hand which Steihl ignores. The Outlaw : Well...Well...Well....tonight the two big bad asses of the Overlords of the Dark meet one another..... "Hey Man, Nice Shot" by Filter blares out over the speakers and from back stage appears Randy Cross. Cross approaches the Outlaw. Cross : Okay Outlaw...great work but I will take it from here! Outlaw : What? Cross : You heard me...I'll do the interview....I think Mr. Steihl and I have a few things to discuss and I am sure the fans don't want to listen to you droan on about how you couldn't beat Darken... Steihl just chuckles.... The Outlaw : Just a minute Cross this is my show and I will handle the interview. If you want.... Cross grabs the mic and The Outlaw and Cross begin having an exchange of words which the mic can't pick up. The discussion is getting heated and Randy Cross continues to point towards the backstage motioning for the Outlaw to leave. From backstage appears Zack and Aric Crockett and they approach the two men. Steihl backs away expecting maybe a set up. Zack and Aric seem to be debating with Randy indicating that he should leave but to no avail. Zack and Aric turn to The Outlaw and plead with him to leave and a reluctant Outlaw walks off the Saloon followed by Zack and Aric. Cross : By the way Outlaw, it's my beloved House of Pain not yours...you are just a member of the House Randy built. Sorry Mr. Steihl....lets continue. A few weeks ago you managed to defeat me in a nontitle match and you have continued to remain undefeated. As I predicted you have proven yourself to be the best of the Overlords, so what do you think about your big match tonight against Darken? Steihl : I have great respect for Darken in the ring. The same respect Outlaw has having also lost to him every time. But this is not just any match, this is a title match, and that is my first singles title. I think we'll have a vicious match, but I think when I walk away it will be with three straps of gold. Cross : Well how to you expect to be able to compete in this match tonight and then in just one week defend the tag belts against the MWC? Steihl : Hey, it was Darken's idea to say one week later. I don't care, because as you said, I am a great wrestler. I figure if I can beat you once in a non title match, then I can easily beat the Crocketts... with Darken's help of course. Cross : Well despite our previous encounters I would like to extend the HOP as your personal protector tonight because regardless of what you think we all know Darken can't be trusted...nor can we expect others to keep there distance...so what do you say would you like for us to be at ringside for you? Steihl : You see, there is no need to worry about that. It seems to me that Jack will be in Darken's corner more than mine, expecting the "leader" of the Horsemen to win. I have Yes in my corner, and Gutter. The rumors of him leaving the Horsemen are a little exagerated. He wouldn't leave until they are done. Thanks for the offer, but I think I can handle it all myself. Cross : Suit yourself big man... Steihl of the Overlords of the Dark... Randy steps back and gives a nice little NWO Scott Hall double pointer... Steihl simply turns and walks off of the stage. Tony : WOW! Do I sense a little tension there in the House of Pain? Terry : It's all in your head Tony... No problems at all. Randy just thought that he would be better suited to do the interview... Yeah that it.. Johny : You keep telling yourself that Terry... Let's get down to the first match of the evening. "Scotland the Brave" begins to play and the Claymores come out to a negative responce from the crowd. Rob and Doug apparently don't appreciate the crowd reaction. They yell back at the fans, which envokes a larger amount of boos. Tony : The Claymores are making their debut here tonight. Terry : Aren't they in the West Coast wrestling thingy? Johny : Actually they are cousins to the MacGregors in California. The Claymores enter the ring as "Holy Wars....Punishment Due" by Megadeath reaplces "Scotland the Brave" as the Vindicators enter the arena. The Vinicators are accompanied by their manager the Angel of Darkness. Terry : What's with these guys? They look like they are confused? OR on Drugs? Johny : That's enough Terry! The Vindicators are also making their NCW debut here tonight... Terry : I like the Claymores, because they’ve got the sense to hate Shaman and his friend Mad Coil. Tony : It looks like Rob MacGregor is starting off for the Claymores and for the Vindicators its uh....? Terry : One of the freaks are starting for the Vindicators... They have the same outfit on? Johny : Actually the Vindicators are different. Blood Cross has a dagger painted on his face and Blood Cross has a cross painted on his face. So in this case, Blood Shadow is starting the match. Terry : Ahhh, I see. It is clear as mud! Tony : Anyways... Rob and Shadow lock up in the middle of the ring. Rob Macgregor clearly has the weight advantage as he pushes Shadow back to his corner. Johny : The ref asks for the break and Rob whinds up and Shadow moves. Tony : Shadow hits him with a forearm smash to Rob's head. Shadow’s immediately going to work on those legs. Johny : The crowd is cheering, but not for Blood Shadow. Shaman and Mad Coil are coming to the ring. Terry : They’re here to cheat. That’s all those New Regulators do. Tony : Rob kicks out and takes Shadow down with a kick to his own knees. A lot harder than those stomps I’d assume. Johny : Shadow gets to his feet just as Doug is tagged in. Doug catches Shadow with a big dropkick. Terry : Shaman interfrering allowing Shadow to tag in Blood Cross. Johny : Well, not interfering exactly, distracting is more like it. Terry : He was making faces at Doug. Tony : Cross with the rollup from behind, but he only get’s a two count. Terry : That’s Blood Cross. There’s only one Cross. Johny : Yeah, whatever. And Doug with a clothesline, but Cross ducks it and comes off the ropes with a flying forearm. Tony : Cross is in for the cover, and again Doug kicks out. Terry : Rob would have been in the ring already if not for Shaman. Get him from ringside. Yes! There is the tag. Johny : Rob in.. catches Cross in a bearhug and turns it into a belly to belly suplex. Tony : Shaman’s laughing. Look at Rob, he’s enraged by this. Terry : Rob tags out to Doug and jumps off the apron, the referee’s telling him to get back to it. Johny : Cross and Shadow are both in the ring behind the referee. Double ddt. Tony : Cross goes for the pin and the referee sees it. That’s it, the match is done. Terry : That was horrible, did you see how nice your "New Regulators" are? They cost the Claymores a match. Tony : Well that was a good tag match. Both of those teams have a promising career in the NCW. Johny : You are so right Tony. Let's go to the next match. It is for the CWC New York City Title. Britainicca with his second title defense... "Hey Man, Nice Shot" by Filter starts up over the PA system. Zack "The Enforcer" Crockett enters the arena with the Outlaw. The fans greet the wrestlers with a barrage of boos and obsenities... Trudeau : Ladies and gentlemen, introducing first, in this CWC New York City title matchup, weighing in at 239 lbs Zack "the Enforcer" Crockett! Tony : Hey Terry... Where is Randy Cross? Isn't it odd that he isn't out here for a huge match like this for Zack? Terry : He is getting ready for the tag match later.... Johny : Oh... The Outlaw is in that match also... How do you explain that? "Princes of the Universe" by Queen saves Terry as the CWC New York City champion emerges from the back to a huge pop. Bonny MacLaine is close behind him as they walk to the ring slapping hands and talking with the crowd. He passes by the spot in which Griffin and Bob are sitting at ringside and smiles. He enters the ring. Trudeau : And his opponent, accompanied to the ring by Bonny McLain, weighing in at 215 lbs, and the reigning CWC New York City champion, Britainicca! Tony : Britainicca is looking to defend his title for the second time after beating Todd "The Irish Hammer" Logan at May Mayhem.... If you missed it, By the Way, you can get it at the video stores. It should be out in the next week or so... Terry : A new belt for the HOP. Tony : I was waiting for you to say that. Johny : Britainicca is on the apron and Zack charges before Britainicca is even completely in the ring. Zack hits Britainicca with a double axe handle to the back sending Britainicca to the floor. Terry : Zack from the apron with another double axe handle to the back of Britainicca. Tony : Zack with a handful of hair sends Britainicca into the metal stairs. Followed by a kick to the midsection. Terry : Zack smartly rolls into the ring to break the referees count. Johny : The ref gets into the face of Zack and repremands him for taking the fight outside. Terry : The Outlaw... What a guy... He's helping Britainicca back into the ring. Tony : What are you blind, TERRY! HE just hit him with three forearms to the back. Terry : He helped him back into the ring also! Johny : Zack nudges the ref to the side and gets Britainicca to his feet. Looks like a suplex... Tony : Britainicca with the small package roll-up. Terry : Zack kicks out at one. Tony : Zack whips Brit into the corner, but he jumps over the ropes. Johny : Zack hits the corner hard and Brit goes up top for a high cross body. Terry : Zack kicks out at two. Outlaw cheering this on, of course he would. This is HOP unity. Tony : Lockup, and Zack raises the knee. He’s got a side headlock on Brit now. Terry : Brit pushes him off, but can’t get the hold broken. Johny : Come on ref, he’s holding on to the mask. Tony : Brit finally breaks the hold with a belly to back suplex. Johny : And here comes Jack of the Four Horsemen down the aisle. I don’t think he’s happy about not getting his shot. Terry : Someone’s coming after him, hey it’s Logan. Tony : Logan just smashed a chair on the head of Jack outside the ring. Jack only went to his knees. Terry : What a ddt by Logan, slapped the back and everything. And on that chair. So smart! Johny : I think he inspired Zack some because he just dropped Brit with one of his own. Tony : Brit kicks out at two. Outside Logan has the Irish Sleeper on Jack. Terry : Here comes security to break up the fun. Johny : Jack’s dropped and he just falls to the floor. Logan leaving peacefully. Tony : Back in the ring now, Brit has control of the match. Irish whip and Zack hits the corner hard. Johny : Brit follows in and raises the knee. Terry : Oh, great. Here comes Darken. Johny : He’s only taking Jack out to the lockers. Maybe they are going to burn down a building, or a House. Tony : Brit with a body slam and he’s going up top. Johny : Brit just nailed the Union Jack Bomb. This is over. Terry : NO!!! The Ref counted to fast! Tony : No he didn't! Britainicca retains his belt after a grueling match with "The Enforcer". Johny : I will agree. Great match there... Let's keep it going with the next match. Trudeau : Introducing first, from the Bronx, and weighing in at 317 lbs, he’s Pestilence of the Four Horsemen of the Apocalypse. Gutter! "Gutter Ballet" by Savage plays as Gutter walks to the ring with no Horsemen gear on and a mixed reaction rises from the crowd. Following behind him is Steihl, who seems to be accompanying him to the ring. Trudeau : And his opponent, from parts unknown, and weighing in at 437 lbs, he’s Famine of the Four Horsemen of the Apocalypse. Jack! Hard Rock guitar riffs play as Jack walks through the curtains, wearing no Horsemen garb. Shortly after he passes through the curtain, Darken follows him out, ghosting along side him to the ring. Jack receives far more cheers than Gutter, but he still has a mixed reaction. Johny : Two of the horsemen against one another for the first time. And the second time will come later tonight in the Main Even when Steihl challenges Darken for the New England belt. Terry : You mean when he takes it from him. Tony : Possibly, he almost took Randy’s belt after all. Johny : Gutter locks up with Jack, who immediately bowls over him and forces him brutally into a corner. Tony : Jack using that massive size of his to break the wind free of Gutter’s chest. Terry : This one could be over as Jack grabs Gutter by the neck. Johny : Gutter blocks the choke slam with one of those luchador moves. Tony : Modified version of the spinning wristlock takedown, but Jack managed to keep his feet. Johny : Gutter with an attempt at a cross body, but Jack just catches him with his huge arms. Powerslam. Terry : Gutter stomping away at his comrade in arms, with no qualms what so ever. Tony : Jack with a pancake, a 430 plus pound pancake. Johny : Jack goes again for the chokeslam, but Gutter just kicked out his knees and tried a mahi shral pinning combination. Terry : Jack easily out of that one. He just delivered a bone crunching punch to the face of Gutter. Tony : Jack again with the choke slam. Gutter is stunned. Johny : Big Chokeslam and Jack gets the pinfall over his stablemate. Tony : And a show of Unity as he even carries Gutter to the locker rooms. "Gangsta's Paradise" starts over the speakers as Deadbolt walks out of the back with DKC close behind. The crowd boos them as they walk to the ring ignoring the fans. Tony : Where have the DKC been lately? Haven't heard from them in a long time. Terry : They have been hiding from the Anarchy! That's where! "The Small Hours" by Danzig starts over the PA. Total Anarchy starts to come out in full force. Judas being at the front of the group. The fans are booing hysterically as the jaw and threaten the crowd. Terry : If I was DKC, I would just leave the arena right now, before this gets ugly! Tony : Total Anarchy get to the ring and the game of words start between DKC and Judas and Havoc. Johny : Look... Havoc just points at the DKC and his charges attack the DKC. Terry : Talon escapes the grasps of Judas and goes under the ring. Chaos and Raiden are mauling Deadbolt and Tiburon. Judas bails out of the ring and can't find Talon. Tony : Talon emerges behind Havoc from under the ring. Talon grabs a metal chair and plants it over Havoc's head. Terry : Havoc drops to the floor lifelessly. Talon jumps up to the apron and nails Raiden from behind. Johny : Raiden staggers and turns and grabs Talon around the neck. Which makes Talon drop the chair. Tony : Deadbolt comes to his senses and nails Raiden from behind with a tackle, which sends all three men over to the floor. WHERE is security! Johny : Here they come... Terry : You better get more than that, these guys are really going at it. Tony : Security finally breaks things up... Look here comes Harrington with a microphone. The camera goes to Harrington and he walks straight over to the now recovering Havoc. Harrington : HAVOC! You and your little CRONIES are hereby SUSPENDED FOR TWO WEEKS! Starting right NOW! And you DKC! You are also suspended for two weeks. GET THEM OUT OF HERE! Tony : Wow! Harrington cracking down on the DKC and Total Anarchy! Suspended for two weeks. Johny : That was pretty brave of Harrington considering Anarchy has threatened him on more than one occassion. Terry : I've always thought that THINKING isn't one of Harrington's strong suits... Tony : Be careful Terry. He signs your check! Johny : Let's go to the next match... Trudeau : The following match is set for tag team action. Introducing first, the former CWC Northeastern tag champions, The Commandant’s Own! "The Marine's Hymn" starts over the PA. Havok and Payne receive a huge pop as they walk to the ring. The fans remember and love them. Trudeau : And their opponents, Randy Cross and the Outlaw, the House of Pain! "Thunderstruck" blares over the speakers and the Outlaw approaches the ring alone. As he gets near the ring he starts looking behind him as if to see where Cross is located. As Randy's music starts there is no Cross. As the music comes to an end Aric Crockett will appears from backstage and makes his way to ringside where the Outlaw is waiting. Terry : Looks like Randy is too sick to come down or something, looks like Aric is taking his place. Johny : Outlaw doesn’t like the idea and he’s sending Aric away. Outlaw’s going to take them on alone? Tony : Outlaw’s looking again for Randy, and oh, he just attacked Commadants Own with a double clothesline which sent Havok right out of the ring. Terry : Payne on the mat, Outlaw’s gonna teach him a few things. Tony : Outlaw helping Payne to his feet, and doubles him with a back leg side kick. Johny : Outlaw with a ddt. Terry : This is what HOP is all about. He’s winning a tag match alone. Tony : Outlaw cuts Payne’s ring in half as he tries for a tag. Payne sent for the ride. Terry : Big splash in the opposite corner by the Outlaw. Tony : Outlaw with a bulldog. Terry : Outlaw with the cover, but Payne manages to weakly kick out. Johny : Outlaw picking up Payne, but Payne counters with a leg sweep. Tony : Payne with the tag and Havok is in now. Terry : Havok with a clothesline and Outlaw goes down. Tony : Havok sends outlaw to the ropes and a textbook dropkick. Johny : Powerslam by Havok and Outlaw’s in trouble here. Tony : Top rope splash connects and Havok goes for a cover. Terry : Outlaw out at two, as I knew he would be. Johny : Outlaw helped up, and Payne tagged back in. Outlaw to the ropes. Tony : Beautiful belly to belly suplex by Payne. Johny : Quick tag by the former champions and Havok goes upstairs. Terry : Payne isn’t leaving the ring. Payne just german suplexed Outlaw. Johny : Havok with a moonsault and goes for the pin. Terry : Randy Cross just made the break. Randy’s there. Johny : Payne and Cross are going at it, all hell’s broken loose and the ref can’t stop it. Tony : Havok sends Outlaw to the ropes and misses a clothesline. Terry : Outlaw with the outlaw kick. Johny : Havok ducked, Randy just got nailed. Tony : Payne just clotheslined Outlaw right out of the ring. Johny : Payne with the cover and he gets the 1...2...3! on Randy Cross thanks to the help of the Outlaw. Terry : That’s outrageous. The referee should have cleaned everything up but didn’t. Johny : Cross finally getting to his feet, it’s about time. Terry : Cross is yelling rightfully at the Outlaw. Johny : Well Outlaw doesn’t like Cross’s attitude, considering he was late. Tony : Pushing matchup, and here comes the Crockett’s hauling ass to stop this. Terry : It’s Outlaw’s fault. See they are at least leaving together... Johny : If that's what you want to call it... Let's go to the next match Trudeau : Introducing first, weighing in at 256 lbs, and hailing from Pittsburgh, PA, Flynn Steel! The crowd cheers it’s approval of Flynn Steel as "I want it all" by Queen takes the air. Trudeau : And his opponent, weighing in at 233 lbs, from Scranton, PA, Jak "The Boil" Lancer! The crowd boos and hisses Jak as the theme to Psycho plays. Things are thrown at him, and hit him on the head, but he only smiles at it all. Johny : We haven’t seen Lancer in a while, I wonder what he’s been upto. Terry : House business. Tony : Lockup, and Jak goes to the eyes. Terry : Side headlock by Jak and Flynn is going no where. Johny : Low blow by Jak, how’d the referee miss that one? Terry : He was checking to see if Flynn quit. Johny : Jak with a bulldog. Tony : Jak goes for a scoop slam but Flynn counters with a small package. Terry : Lancer out at one, easily. Johny : Easily? That was two at least! Terry : Both men back up, waistlock by Flynn. Johny : Lancer to the ropes, but Flynn catches the victory roll and bridges back. Tony : Where’s the referee? Terry : He’s checking something with Gary Trudeau? Tony : Now he’s on duty, and Lancer kicks out easily. Johny : Jak with a low blow, but the referee’s busy cleaning his eyes out. Terry : Hey, you have to be seeing clear to referee properly. Tony : Jak goes for a powerbomb, but Flynn counters with a hurcanrana roll up. Johny : Again the referee’s off duty. Terry : I think all referee’s are allowed five minute breaks. Johny : Shut up. Now what’s going on? Aric and Zack are coming to ringside. Terry : Just to watch. Lancer’s doing great on his own. Tony : Lancer holds Flynn for Zack who sends him to the ropes. Johny : Spinebuster Slam by Zack and Jak goes for the cover. Terry : Zack informing the referee of the pin attempt. Johny : Informing him, he just went over and tapped him on the shoulder. He shouldn’t even be in the ring. Terry : Well the referee was- Tony : Shut up. Terry : And the one two three for Jak Lancer. What a return bout. Johny : That was the WORST officiating in the HISTORY of NCW... CWC for that matter! Tony : Again Larry Crosier with the terrible officiating! Harrington should ban him! Terry : Everyone has a bad day... Gees! Tony : Right Terry! Let's go to the next match. Trudeau: Introducing first, in this most unique match, the challenger. One half of the CWC Northeastern Tag title holders and one half of the CWC National title holders, he comes to the ring weighing in at 312 lbs, he is Steihl! "Cry Little Sister" plays from the public address as Steihl steps from the curtains. His cloak hanging limply against his form, he throws back his hood and spins around with his arms out. The fans give a boo reaction towards him. Quickly following him out, dressed in a blue robe and holding a giant potato is YES. Steihl pauses half way to the ring, and out comes Gutter, dressed as Pestilence. The three make their way to the ring and Steihl bounds over the top rope into the ring. "Sad But True" by Metallica rips through the air replacing Steihl's music and the crowd raises a cheer. Trudeau: And his opponent, one half of the CWC Northeastern Tag title holders and one half of the CWC National title holders, as well as the CWC New England Champion, he comes to the ring weighing in at 337 lbs, he is Darken! Darken steps through the curtains on cue, Jack in full Famine garb close behind. Darken's all black wrestling suit is missing the usual cloak as he walks straight to the ring looking straight at Steihl. He pays no attention to the fans. Jack glares a little at Gutter, but neither makes a move towards one another. Johny: As Gary said, a rather unique matchup setting tag partners against one another. Terry :That's what they get for trying to tell Harrington his job. Tony :I don't think that they meant title shot as Darken's title though. Terry :Why not? We know Steihl is better than Darken, he did beat Randy on a fluke after all. Johny :There's the bell and this match is under way. Tony : Neither man is moving, Steihl and Darken are just glaring at one another. Terry : Steihl hasn't even taken his cloak off, he doesn't need to because he's that good. Tony : There's the lockup, and Darken forces Steihl into a corner. Johny : Irish whip and Steihl goes for the ride. Terry : There goes Steihl's cloak, Gutter just removed it for him. Johny : Both men lock up again, Steihl goes to the eyes. Terry : Off the ropes, Steihl with a high cross body. Tony : Look at the strength of Darken, he just caught his 312 lbs partner in mid air. Johny : Powerslam by Darken! Terry : And Steihl get's right back up. Johny : Darken with a boot to the gut of Steihl. Tony : Darken with a hand full of hair just drove the face of Steihl into the mat. Terry : No love lost in this match, is there. Johny : Darken sends Steihl to the ropes again Darken with a flying clothesline. Tony : Darken with a quick cover, but Steihl kicks out at one. Terry : Steihl goes right back to the eyes, and now he's got Darken in a side headlock. Johny : Darken breaks the hold with a belly to back suplex. Terry : Steihl blocks the attempt and just flips to his feet. Johny : Great agility by Steihl. Terry : Of course, he's better than Darken. And Darken with a vicious lariot that sends Steihl over the top. Tony : Darken climbs out with him. I think this is a mistake, Steihl is more like to use things here. Terry : Darken sends Steihl flying into the stairs. I guess it's not below him to cheat. Johny : I thought you'd approve. Darken now rolling Steihl back in. Tony : Steihl kicks out of the lateral press at two. Johny : Steihl again goes to the eyes. Kick to the gut. Terry : Ddt by Steihl, this should do it. Johny : Darken kicks out at one. Tony : Steihl sends Darken into the corner, handspring back elbow. Johny : Wait, Darken caught him german suplex. Terry :Steihl with an easy kickout at two. Johny : Wait a minute, Randy Cross is coming to ringside. Terry : What bravery, he's come to cheer on one of his favorite wrestlers even with Jack, Gutter and Darken there. Tony : And cheer he does. The referee seems to be sending him away. Terry : Steihl with a pinning combination behind the referee's back. Johny : Pinning combination? That was a low blow. Tony : Darken just threw Steihl off of him without even a one. Johny : I don't think he's too happy anymore. Terry : Darken with closed fists backs Steihl into a corner. Tony : Back elbow, and another. Oh, three back elbows in the corner by the champion. Terry : Randy's giving Steihl advice. Johny : Steihl with a headbutt, but Darken hits him with a forearm. Terry : Darken's lifting Steihl up, what's he doing now? Tony : Darken's going for his Dark Pogostick. One hit, two. Three. Johny : Darken with the cover. Terry : Steihl will kick out. Johny : Not within the three count. Darken retains the title. Terry : Look at Randy, he's so surprised because it was a fluke. Tony : Fluke NOTHING! Nobody recovers from the Dark Pogostick!
