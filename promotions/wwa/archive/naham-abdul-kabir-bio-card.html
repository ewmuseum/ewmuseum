Naham Abdul Kabir Basic Information Nicknames | N.A.K., The Knack, Knack, The Law, The Baller Brawler, Buster (derogatory) Real Name | Kurtis Law Height | 6'10" Weight | 265 Date of Birth | 07/21/1984 Home Town | Marcus, Iowa Theme | "Intro" by DMX More Details Appearance A black man, arms covered in sleeve tattoos, massive beard, small fro-hawk atop his head. Not as thick as most wrestlers, as he was a basketball player, but still ripped. He has a face that looks as if it were cut from granite, and he has dark eyes that appear as if they can stare through anything or anyone. Ring Attire Law Breakers (his Air Jordans from his NBA rookie season endorsement deal), either track pants or jeans, taped left wrist, and warmup shooting sleeve on his right arm. Sometimes he wears a sweatband around his head, sometimes not. As for his shirt, it's either no shirt, revealing a torso as heavily tattooed as his arms (as well as noticible, mysterious scars), a tanktop of various colors, his Hawkeyes jersey, his Knicks jersey, or his Bayhawks jersey. Personality He's intense, focused. When he goes to work, it's difficult to match up with him and his brawling. However, has a very short temper, so it's easy to get him off his game. He was racking up technical fouls like they were going out of style, you know. History PRE-WWA Born to a poor family in a rough neighborhood, Kurtis Law found solace in basketball. As he grew, so did his skills, until his domination on a high school level (Macrus-Meridan-Cleghorn Eagles) and brief stint in college (Iowa Hawkeyes) was like that of few other men. He was regarded as a sure-fire superstar prospect, and "the next Shawn Kemp, but better;" he was drafted first overall by the New York Knicks. As is usually the case in stories like this, everything went downhill from there. His weight spiralled out of control, his skills diminished, and his extremely short temper reared its ugly head, and that's just in the first few games of the season. After a record pace for technical fouls and entirely ineffective play, he was dropkicked down to the Erie Bayhawks in NBA Developmental. This stint was also short-lived, after beating an opposing coach senseless mid-game. Shortly thereafter, Law converted to Islam and changed his name to Naham Abdul Kabir, receiving various nicknames from his teammates based on this. After the change in religion did not prove to be a change of heart, he was unceremoniously released by the Bayhawks. It wasn't long before the WWA began to rise from the ashes, and The Knack signed a free agent contract with them.
