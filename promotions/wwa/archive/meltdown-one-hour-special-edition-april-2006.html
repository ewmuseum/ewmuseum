Meltdown - One-Hour Special Edition (April 2006)

c. 17 April 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is Script of a one-hour edition of the WWA’s Meltdown show from Charlotte, North Carolina, opening with the Tommy Starr and Friends Show segment, then a triple-threat Television Championship match, a United States Championship match, Somers vs Thorne for the World Championship, a roster protest against the owner and a closing three-way confrontation.
The crowd falls to a hush but begin cheering as spotlights begin to strobe around the arena. Some cheesy keyboard music can be heard as well as a voice over. Voiceover: Ladies and gentlemen, live from Charlotte , North Carolina...welcome to the pilot episode of a new variety show ...welcome to history being made. Welcome to the Tommy Starr and Friends Show! The spotlights center on the ring where Tommy Starr can be seen seated behind a large desk, dressed in a suit and tie as well as wearing his custom silver tinted sunglasses. There is a pot plant to his right, and a couple of guests chairs to his left. The crowd boos and he takes a long, protracted slurp from his mug. Starr: Greetings nimrods, and welcome to my show! The crowd goes into a frenzy in booing Starr some more. Starr: I couldn't do this alone...well on second thoughts I could, but tonight, let's give it up for my tag team partner... He reads from his cue cards. Starr:...that's right, Belial Kahn! The crowd boos some more as Kahn appears at the top of the ramp to no music or fanfare. He strolls down the ramp, climbing onto the apron and over the top rope. He waves at the fans before taking a seat in the guest chair. Starr: So Belial, let's cut to the chase. What's it like tagging with the greatest wrestler in wrestling history? Kahn frowns and looks at Starr, that didn't seem to be in the script. Undeterred, Starr grins and nods at Kahn. Finally Kahn speaks. Kahn: You're the best Tommy! Starr nods, soaking in the boos. Starr: But that's enough about me, what do you think about me? Kahn shakes his head. Starr: I know, I know, no words can describe how smoking hot I am. Hold on, I think I just did. That said, there are many words that come to mind when I think about these two bozos... Starr points to the WWATron where an image of Shaman and Samuelson can be seen, it is a still from last weeks show as they stand victorious in the ring, their arms raised in victory. However thanks to the wonders of modern technology, they have tiaras and pink feather boas superimposed on them. Kahn: Awww isn't that sweet...S.O.S. Starr: S.O.S indeed...idiots in attendance and jerk offs at home, I give you Sweet Old Sistas! The crowd boo at that comment. Starr: Now let me get this straight Kahn...these two Sweet Old Sistas were beaten by us two weeks ago, and if I recall I beat Hagar a couple of weeks prior to that for the TV Title. Kahn: You did Tommy, you did. Starr: A couple of years ago I kicked Shamans leathery hiney from here to Timbuktu and back again as well. Kahn: Didn't he shave your head? The crowd laughs and Starr pouts. Starr: Shut it! Fact is, we have beaten these guys more than their boyfriends have and yet everyone is crowing about how great these disease addled Sistas are after a flukey win last week over the tag champs! The crowd boos, and Kahn nods. Kahn: That's right Tommy, where do these buffoons get off thinking they are all that, when clearly we are the best? Where do they... SOS suddenly appear at the top of the ramp walkway not too happy in expression. Starr: Give it up for our very special guest, Sisters Of Sorrow! As they walk down the aisle Jorge and Shaman stare at the ring before entering the ring to a mixed reaction of cheers and jeers. Shaman: stepping into the ring I think you two ladies are having to much fun playing with yourselves. Jorge: And I think we need to put a end to that just... Kahn gets in Jorge's face as he steps over the top rope Kahn: And do what, cause you sure not scaring anyone in this ring with your fake fur and wanna be he man boots. Starr: Easy guys, easy…getting in between the two and getting them to sit down This is a talk show not Jerry Springer. Shaman: Lets just cut to the chase, we both have something to prove. Starr No actually you two do, we already have a win over you. Jorge gets up off his chair and appears to be ready to strike when Shaman puts his arm out to restrain him and speaks. Shaman: Well, here it is then, you're undefeated and we are the team that can beat you. So how about .. Starr How about this… He then blows some powder into Shaman’s eyes sending Shaman crashing to the mat blinded as Jorge raises up but is grabbed from behind by Belial who then reverse head butts Jorge in the back of his head stunning Samuelson and leaving him open for Tommy to bring three violent chair shots to Jorge’s skull sending him then crashing to the mat. Jimmy: What is going on!? Kahn then picks up Shaman and power bombs him thru the table when Starr grabs the mic. Starr: Guess you two numb nuts thought we forgot about last week. Well we didn't .. He looks out into the crowd who is booing so loud that Tommy can barely be heard. Starr: And remember kids, guns don't kill people, We do . Kahn then licks some of the blood off his hands. Kahn: And Oh yes…there will be blood. The fans are extremely irate and booing at a deafening pitch. Kahn then steps over the top ropes leaving the ring with a devilish grin reminiscent of Beelzebub himself. He then goes under the ring and pulls out two canisters of gasoline placing them on the apron. Starr: You like to blow things up , well my boy Kahn here likes to play with matches. People they come together People they fall apart No one can stop us now Cause we are all made of stars Starr nods and saunters down the ramp, ignoring the crowd and losing himself in his misplaced self-confidence. Muppet: Two weeks ago, I told you all that I'd send a message. Two weeks ago, I sent that message. pause Yes. Do you understand now? More and more heads begin to turn. The Franchise Players look like they desperately want the fighting to end, but wherever they turn, more adversaries await them. The ringside skirmish doesn't appear to be able to end at all. One thing sets off another as the fighting continues. Muppet: It doesn't matter what I say now. The proverbial camel's back is already broken. Three men dogpile Shaman as he roars for control. Muppet: The 'peace' has been broken. The control has been destroyed. Breaking free of the melee, the World champion slides into the ring, close to the Muppet Man, who smiles at him disturbingly. It doesn't seem to affect him. Muppet: And now there is only chaos. Mal makes a movement towards Muppet, but is stopped by a roar from the crowd and a hand movement from the aggressor. From the back, a large group of WWA security and officials finally make they way out. They look organised, almost in formation. Muppet: Here come the cavalry. The security begin to restrain and force back the majority of the WWA roster. A small group run towards the ring. Mal Somers, angry at his missed opportunity, wisely gets out of there. Muppet: Don't worry, though. It's already done. With another eerie smile, the Muppet Man drops the microphone and raises his arms out, as if leaving himself completely open. Security run into the ring and tackle him to the ground, slamming his face into the mat and trying to tie his hands behind his back. Jimmy: Has it been subdued? Fat Tony: For once, I really hope so. Jimmy: Dear lord, look at Jorge Samuelsson! With the immense power that only Samuelsson could convey, he lifts up one of the security guards and flings him over his shoulder. On the other side of the ring, Tommy Starr kicks the security guard in front of him in the genital area. As the guard clutches his ‘private’ area, Starr, Caro and Kyle James launch an ambush on him. Jimmy: They’re fighting back! The protestors are fighting back! Denrol’s trademark chops are accompanied by the Independence Arena’s chants, as one half of the tag champs lays into an official. Crowd: WHOOOO! Eh? And another. Crowd: WHOOOO! Eh? The crowd give another big pop as Shaman overpowers two guards, and grabbing their heads, butts them together. Fat Tony: This is pure anarchy, Jimmy! In the ring, the Muppet Man, still handcuffed, looks on with a mixture of anger and disbelief. The two security officials holding him also look out to the carnage, too afraid as the WWA wrestlers continue to beat down the others. Jimmy: Even security can’t hold them back! One by one, the security guards are beaten down, until only the WWA’s roster is standing. Griffiths and Komodo continue to beat down an official at ringside but the majority of the protestors quickly turn their attention to the ring. Fat Tony: Is that it? That’s all the security we have? Jimmy: I guess the rest are on protest. The three men inside the ring look on in horror as the wrestlers start to surround the ring; everyone’s eyes locked on the handcuffed Muppet Man. Pyro suddenly slides into the ring and one of the officials tries to hit a right hand. Blocking it, Pyro returns one of his own and the guard is spun around, straight into the clutches of Tracer Bullet, who slid in from the other side. Jimmy: Pulp Fiction from Tracer! The second guard tries to attack Tracer, but his throat is suddenly grabbed by the big hands of Shaman. With the crowd cheering him on, Shaman lifts the guard effortlessly off his feet and dangles him over the ropes. Fat Tony: Old School Pain. Jimmy: Shaman choking the life out of that official! Shaman finally lets go and the guard crumbles onto the floor outside the ring, leaving Muppet alone without defense. Jimmy: Things do not look good for the Muppet Man right now. The rest of the protestors all climb into the ring, Mal leading the charge. Muppet shakes his head furiously in terror when, again unexpectedly, a familiar voice rings out. Voice: Wait! The arena lets out a huge pop as Armaan jumps over the guardrails. Eventually, all of the wrestlers notice Armaan standing at ringside. The Indian is in street clothes, a microphone in his hand. Fat Tony: What? Armaan’s here too?! Armaan: Leave him alone. Can’t you see? This is what he wants. The chaos agenda, the predictions, all of these will come true if we fall for his game again. The guys in the ring respond with confusion, some disagreeing with the Indian’s words. Armaan: pointing to Muppet He talks about peace being broken and only chaos surviving. But look how wrong he is. Just as quickly as chaos broke out tonight, it has ended too. He glances eyes with each man standing in the ring. Armaan: All of you men standing in the ring, you fought against Mr. Ventressa’s security guards together. And now you are all in the ring, as one unit, all standing together with one goal…stopping those who bring chaos. All eyes turn back to the Muppet Man, who seems like a beaten man. Pyro and the Komodo Dragon hoist Muppet to his feet, still handcuffed and still defenceless. Armaan’s eyes finally lock with the Muppet Man’s properly, and there is a brief, albeit extremely tense, silence. Armaan: None of your predictions have come true, Andrew. You are alone. You are defenseless. You are the one at loss right now. I could have waited until every single one of these men tore you apart before I showed up. The Indian shakes his head slowly. Armaan: But no. As much hatred as I have for you, Andrew, even I know that it will solve nothing. Only three men can end this once and for all. So long I spent wondering if this nightmare would ever end, never finding a way out. But now I know that this will end. ‘Accidents’ by Alexisonfire suddenly blasts over the PA system. Armaan’s body jolts around as Steven Murphy appears at the top of the ramp. Jimmy: And there’s the third man Armaan spoke of. Murphy remains at the top of the stage, looking down at Armaan at ringside and Muppet in the ring. Jimmy: Murphy started all this, Tony! The three men stare at each other, Murphy glaring at his adversaries with astonishment and rage. Jimmy: The balance of power has shifted…nobody is in control anymore! Fat Tony: They’re all equally nuts! Jimmy: We are well out of time, guys! After a quick shot of each of the three men, ending with Murphy at the top of the ramp, the camera fades to black.
