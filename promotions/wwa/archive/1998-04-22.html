NCW Wednesday Night War — 22 April 1998

22 Apr 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
NCW Wednesday Night War Wednesday April 22, 1998 The screen opens up to the the NCW banner. Then.... Northeast Champion Wrestling flashes through the screen and leaves the NCW Banner Then firework type explosions go off around the Banner. The Banner fades... then the screen shows Lehman College's The Apex. The screen then goes to the inside to show the fans jumping and screaming at the Camera. The camera pans over the the Complex and then the camera finds Tony Bailey and Terry Valentine sitting behind the table in the broadcast area. Tony: Welcome ladies and gentlemen to the Apex in the Bronx, New York. We have a great show for you tonight. We have quite a few making their sophmore performances tonight. The Main event puts the new and two time CWC Northeast Champion against Steihl of the CWC National and Northeast Tag Champions Overlords of the Dark. It should be a great one. On top of that we have a grudge match between Britainicca and Todd "The Irish Hammer" Logan, another great match up. Johny : Let's get to the ring for the first match. With those words "Voodoo Child" by Jimi Hendrix begins to blare throughout the arena as "Untouchable" Ryan Lee enters the arena to a negative response. Terry : This match will make a lasting impression on the side of my face. Tony : I am afraid to ask... Terry : When you fall asleep while sitting up, you lay your head against your hand. Which leaves an impession on your cheek. As soon as Lee enters the ring, "War Pigs" by Black Sabbath begins to play. Rob Thorne emerges from behind the curtain to a warm crowd response. Tony : The ref is calling for the bell.... And we are under way. Johny : Thorne is giving up a huge height and weight advantage. The wrestlers lock up and Lee bullies Thorne back into the ropes. Terry : The ref steps in for the break. Lee turns to the crowd and gives them a flex. He sucks! Tony : The two men go for the lock up... Nope Lee plants a boot to the midsection of Thorne. Terry : Lee with a double axe handle to the back of Thorne. Thorne goes down to the mat and Lee follows it up with boot after boot to Thorne's head. Johny : The ref admonishes Lee to stop the repeated blows to the head, as Thorne is in the ropes. Terry : That was an impressive display from Lee. Tony : He was kicking him in the head. There was not one wrestling move used! Terry : Thorne is laying on the mat isn't he? Johny : Lee brings Thorne up by the hair and sends him into the ropes and delivers a HUGE clothesline. Thorne practically does a 360 in midair. Tony : Here comes the "Lights Out" from Lee. He is giving the sign. Johny : The "Lights Out" is a reverse DDT. Lee wanders over to Thorne and sets him up for the reverse DDT. Terry : It's over... Next match please. Tony : WOW! Thorne counters with a sort of Diamond Cutter. Lee lies motionless on the mat. Thorne is shaking out the cobwebs as the ref starts the 10 count. Johny : That was an amazing counter by Rob Thorne. He is staggering to his feet as the ref reaches an 8 count. Lee is starting to stir as Thorne brings him to his feet. Oh... Lee falls back to the mat. Terry : Lee sure is "Untouchable". He can't stay on his feet to be touched. Johny : OK... Terry! You don't get to talk anymore. Tony : I agree. Terry shut up! Thorne just shrugs and applies "The End of the Road". His version of the STF. That's it Lee taps out after about 10 seconds. Johny : The win goes to Rob Thorne.... Let's go to the next match.... "My Friend of Misery" By Metallica starts to play as Joe Clapp enters the ring area. The crowd cheers for Clapp as he enters the ring. Axel Rose replace James Hetfield as "Paradise City" starts on the Public Address. Paul Valentine walks the aisle ignoring the crowd as he gets into the ring and just points at Clapp and says a few choice words... Tony : Both men have been pretty silent lately. Clapp got of to a great start, but recently has hit the skids. Terry : Valentine is off to a 1-0 start as well...... There is about a 5 second pause as both Johny and Tony stare blankly into the camera. Then Johny shakes his head in confusion... Johny : I DON"T BELIEVE IT!!!! Terry made a valide point, BASED on fact nonetheless. Terry : BE QUIET! And call the match! Clapp and Valentine lock up and Clapp wastes no time in gaining control with a side headlock. Tony : I am still in shock... I can't go on. Terry : OK.... Everyone KNOCK IT OFF!!! Call the match! Valentine breaks the headlock and sends Clapp into the ropes with an irish whip. Clapp runs right through Valentine sending him to the mat. Johny : Valentine quickly back to his feet, Clapp runs against the ropes again. And Again Valentine gets sent to the mat with a shoulder block from Clapp. Tony : Clapp follows with a big Elbow... Oh, he missed it and Valentine is up and is planting boot after boot into Clapp's head and shoulders. Finally, Valentine backs off and Clapp staggers to his feet. Johny : Valentine runs into the ropes and grabs the staggered Clapp into a nice Bulldog. Clapp is writhing on the mat. Terry : Valetine starts putting the boots to Clapp again. Another great exchange for Valentine. Tony : Clapp is trying to get back to his feet. Valentine grabs Clapp and sets him up for a Piledriver. Johny : Beautifully executed by Valentine. He goes over to Clapp with a handful of hair and executes a textbook vertical suplex. Terry : It's OVER!! Get Clapp a body bag. Tony : I will agree with you Terry. Valentine sets him up in his pattented "Bone Grinder". Johny : The ref could count to 100... Paul Valetine is the winner. Let's go to the next match... "The Beatiful People" by Marilyn Manson starts as Capital makes his way to the ring to a pretty good fan responce. Manson is replaced by a gong, striking three times. Annihilation enters the arena and is focused directly on Capital in the middle of the ring. Johny : This match should be interesting... Both wrestlers are 1-0 and looking to improve to a respectable 2-0 start in the NCW. Tony : I agree with you Johny. It should be a good one. The two men appear to be circling each other. Terry : Annihilation dwarves Capital. Capital hasn't a chance. Let's move to the next match and stop wasting my valueable time. Tony : Do your job and call the match Terry. Annihilation is keeping Capital back with a couple of stiff jabs to the face. Johny : Capital will have none of it as he charges in. Terry : ANNIHILATION decapitates Capital with a HUGE clothesline. Capital isn't moving on the mat. IS he dead? Tony : That was devestating! Capital is starting to stir on the mat. Annihilation gets him up just drops down in a DDT. Capital is down and out. Terry : Annihilation looks very impressive in this match. He lifts him up and Down.... Johny : Annihilation executes what he calls the Super Back Breaker to perfection. The refs counts to three and the match is history. Tony : Annihilation moves to 2-0 with an impressive win over Capital. Let's go to the next match... "Gutter Ballet" by Savage starts as Gutter emerges from the curtains... The crowd greats him with a mixed reaction as he walks to the ring. Tony : Gutter has been silent for weeks now. He will try to turn his career here in the NCW around with a victory over Propane. "More Human than Human" by White Zombie is blarring over the PA. Propane enters the arena and walks to the ring oozing with confidence. Johny : Propane is going to try and start a winning streak in his yound career. He will have his work cut out for him this week in the form of Gutter. Terry : Gutter SUCKS! Go Propane! Tony : Propane hits the ring and charges right into Gutter. The two men are brawling in the middle of the ring. Finally the ref convinces the men to have a WRESTLING match. Johny : Propane delivers a haymaker that sends Gutter to the mat. Propane starts to deliver kicks to the back of Gutter. Gutter smartly exits the ring to take a breather. Terry : Propane runs into the ropes on the oppostie side of the ring and springs into a suicide dive on the outside of the ring right into Gutter. Both men hit themselves on the guardrail and are down outside the ring. Johny : The ref is starting the count from the ring. Outside the ring Propane is up and walks over to Gutter and plants a foot across his throat. Terry : Good move on the part of Propane. Tony : It a CHOKE Terry... Nevermind. Propane has Gutter up and executes an irish whip into the guardrail. Johny : Gutter reverses it and Propane goes down. The Ref is calling for the bell.... Terry : Double count out! Johny : Gutter upon hearing the ref's decision attacks Propane and the brawl starts again. Security is on their way to break up the brawl... Propane and Gutter are still going at it, even with the security trying to break them up... Tony : It looks like we have a hatred forming there, between those two. Let's go to the next match... Sirens start to go off as Fidel comes to the ring yelling at the fans. Fidel gets to the ring and "Feed my Frankenstein" by Alice Cooper replaces the sirens. Mad Coil comes down to the ring with the Death Knight close behind. Tony : Mad Coil is a large man. I wonder why Harrington left him out of the Big man match? Terry : Do you really want to know? Johny : No we DON"T TERRY! Mad Coil is the first on the offensive as he blasts the unsuspecting Fidel with a forarm smash. Fidel falls against the ropes. Tony : Mad Coil follows it up with a kick to Fidel's leg. And Again, Fidel falls to his knees. Terry : That's got to hurt... Fidel staggers to his feet and Mad Coil again hits his leg with a kicking, sweeping thing... Johny : Mad Coil lines Fidel up and throws him down with a BIG body Slam. Tony : Fidel is helped to his feet by Mad Coil... Mad Coil locks in a bear hug as he tries to wear down the Cuban. Terry : Mad Coil releases the hold and gives the sign for the "Avalanche". He gets Fidel up and powers him down. Tony : The ref counts to three and the pinfall goes to Mad Coil. He appears to be calling for a mic. Mad Coil : HEY, Eliem! That starts the WAR of the big men! Mad Coil slams the mic to the mat and leaves.... Johny : Let's go down to the next match.... "Enter Sandman" by Metallica starts as "Hard Rock" Rocky Stevens enters the ring area. As he gets into the ring "Strutter" by Bob Segier replaces Metallica on the PA. RU Retty comes out of the back to a mixed reaction from the crowd. He ignores them and enters the ring. Terry : Both men are off to a "Rocky" start here in the NCW... Terry laughs at his WEAK joke... No One else does Terry : Don't you get it..... Rocky Stevens.... Tony : We get it Terry. It just isn't funny! Retty and Stevens lock up and Stevens powers Retty to the ropes. Johny : Stevens backs away and Retty blasts him in the face with a fist. Terry : It was an open hand... Good move from Retty. Wow... Stevens come back with an "OPEN HAND" of his own and Retty goes to the mat. Tony : Stevens jumps onto Retty and starts beating him. Johny : Retty gets him of with a rake to the eyes... Terry : Who is that coming from the curtains? Johny : It looks like it is Angel Reaper. He isn't scheduled to be here tonight? He is up on the apron and is yelling at Stevens. Stevens gets in his face and is yelling right back. Tony : Oh no, it is the Masked man again, coming fom the crowd with the 2x4. Terry : Stevens is nailed from behind by Retty. Angel Reaper is still yelling and pointing. The ref is trying to keep Reaper out of the ring. The Masked man is also up on the apron. Johny : The masked man is going to hit Stevens.... Stevens turns around and sees him and ducks as the man swings the 2x4 and it nails Retty. Retty falls lifelessly to the mat. Tony : The masked man looks upset and scurries back into the crowd. Angel Reaper just shakes his head in disgust and leaves the ring and into the back. Terry : Stevens sees Retty lying flat on his back and covers him for the win. Johny : Who is that masked man... And what does Angel Reaper have to do with it? Tony : We might never know... Check this out... The camera fades out and back on to a screen with a clip of Britainicca holding the Rhode Island Belt in the air oafter beating Blaze. Then a voice over says.... Voice Over : "NCW fans, ever want to be like your heroes? Want to show your support for your champion? Then call 1-800-BRITAINICCA to order the new BRITAINICCA luchador style mask! The picture of Britainicca in his moment of glory fades to the Mask he wears... Voice Over : Just $11.99 plus tax and shipping and handling... Allow one to two weeks for delivery, all credit cards accepted...... The screen fades to black and then flashes back to the announcers table. Tony is getting ready to speak.. Tony : We're back... Let's go to the ring for the next match... House of Pain starts with "Jump Around". Todd "The Irish Hammer" Logan with the Outlaw are walking to the ring to a chorus of boos. Todd Logan is carrying a nightstick as they enter the ring. Logan tears his shirt off as "Princes of the Universe" by Queen replaces House of Pain on the PA. Britainicca comes to the top of the ramp and then proceeds slowly to the ring looking around him as if looking for something... Tony : This match should be an all out WAR! Johny : I will say... Logan just dove out of the ring and nailed Britainicca. The two men are fighting it out.... I would say that there will not be very many wrestling moves used in this match. Terry : Britainicca isn't playing very smart here. Brawling with a bigger man. Speaking of brawling, where are they going. Hey guys the ring is the OTHER WAY! Tony : I don't think they care. Britainicca and Logan are fighting their way up the ramp and into the back. The Outlaw is right behind them, cheering for Logan. Johny : I've been told that the refereee has declared this match a no contest. I guess we go to the main event? That should make you happy Terry. Terry : It sure does... We get to see Randy trounce that crazy Steihl. Tony : We will see Terry... Let's do it. "Cry Little Sister" starts over the PA as Stiehl and Darken walk to the ring to some cheering from the crowd. Darken and Steihl enter the ring and "Hey Man, Nice Shot" by Filter starts over the PA. Randy Cross enters the arena to a onslaught of boos. Randy just smiles and shakes his head on his way to the ring. He slaps a few hands as he walks to the ring. Randy Enters the ring and takes off his robe. He points to the belt as he takes it off, tauting Steihl. Darken gets out of the ring and just flods his arms and stands at ringside. Terry : The ref needs to make that freak Darken go to the locker room. Tony : I have an idea... Why don't you tell him. I think that the fans would love to see that... I bet Darken would love to hear it from you as well. Johny : There's the bell and we are ready to go. The two men lock up and Steihl immediately overpowers Cross by throwing him to the mat. Terry : I think he pulled his hair. The ref should watch that. Tony : He did not. He just threw him down.... Cross is up and here they go again with a lock up. Johny : And Again Cross is laying on his back. Terry : See Randy is smiling... It is all part of the plan. Tony : We will see. Steihl motions for Cross to try it again. Cross shrugs and locks up again. Johny : No Cross with a kick... Caught by Steihl. Steihl has Cross hoping on one foot. Steihl levels Cross with a clothesline. Cross is back on the ground. The crowd is cheering as Cross scurries out of the ring. Terry : Randy smartly rolls out of the ring to regroup. Tony : He better do more than regroup. He better call in the cavalry. Terry : He isn't the champ for nothing... Johny : Good point Terry. He sure does cheat well... Here comes Steihl. Suicide dive through the ropes. Nails Cross on the outside. Terry : Hah! Steihl is hurt also. Tony : He sure is... Both men are slow recovering from Steihl's dive to the outaide. Steihl is first up and irish whips Cross into the guardrails. Terry : REVERSED! Steihl rams into the guardrail. He is down. Randy quickly rolls into the ring. The ref restarts the 10 count for Steihl. Tony : Darken walks over to Steihl and simple says "Get up"... to Sheihl. Steihl looks up at Darken and stands up with little effort. Johny : Steihl rolls into the ring breaking the refs count at 6. Cross starts kicking and stomping on Steihl's legs. Steihl stands up and grabs Cross around the neck... Terry : Get in there ref... DQ him! Do something... Johny : Steihl releases Cross and sends him into the corner, shaking the whole ring from the force. Steihl charges... Terry : Randy falls to the mat and Steihl rams into the turnbuckles and staggers back a couple of steps. Tony : Cross climbs to the second rope and executes a clothesline from the second rope. Steihl falls to the ground. Cross immediately follows with his figure four. Terry : YES!!! It's locked in! Steihl is FINISHED!!! Tony : Cross has the hold locked in. But Steihl is showing no signs of giving up. Johny : Cross has is locked in the middle of the ring. SO Steihl has his work cut out for him. Terry : It is only a matter of time now........... Now..... Johny : Terry, I don't think Steihl is going to submit. Steihl has been fighting the hold for about 5 minutes now. Tony : Steihl stops struggling and his shoulders are down. The referee counts 1 - 2 NO Steihl sits up and grabs Cross by the throat. Johny : Cross lets the hold go and stuggles to rid himself of the vise that Steihl's hand has become around his neck. Terry : The ref finally gets Steihl to realease the hold. Steihl stands up and falls right back down from the pain in his leg. Tony : Steihl struggles again to his feet. This time he is standing. Cross is over in the corner trying to untie the padding in the top turnbuckle. Johny : Stehil limps over to Cross and again grabs for the neck. This time throwing Cross down in a chokeslam in the middle of the ring. Terry : Get up Randy! Get up.... Tony : Here comes the House of Pain from the back.... Jak Lancer leads Big Eliem and Chris Rogers. As the trio reach the ring, a man from the crowd clotheslines Eliem to the ground. It is Jack. HE is climbing over the guardrail and Eliem and him start to brawl. Johny : Darken is blocking the way for Lancer and Rogers and they both bury their heads into Darken. All three men fall in a heap on the floor an begin to throw fists. Terry : Get up Randy! Tony : Back in the ring. Steihl has managed to get Cross up on his feet. He send him into the corner where Cross hits hard. Steihl follows him in and nails him with a body splash. Johny : That was it. That was the sign for the STEIHL. Steihl puts Cross up on the top turnbuckle and sets him up.... DOWN with the modified Outsider's Edge. Cross is down in the middle of the ring. Terry : NO!!! Somebody do something! Yes Lancer is up on the apron, blood pooring from a gash on the side of his head. Tony : Steihl turns Cross over and the ref is in position. 1 - 2 - 3.... Steihl WINS. Steihl just beat Cross! The ref raises Steihl's hand and Lancer nails him from behind. Darken is now on his feet and enters the ring.... We are out of time. See you Friday.
