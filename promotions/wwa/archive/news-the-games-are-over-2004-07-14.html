The Games Are Over

14 Jul 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
In Texas on July 4th at the Independence Day Rumble Matthew Warr made a decision which has changed the WWA immensely. As many of you loyal WWA fans are aware, the SCWF closed down and now NWC and LDA have joined it in the history books. From now on there will not be three federations under the WWA banner or even two, the World Wrestling Alliance is unified once again. Make sure you tune into the upcoming Meltdown to see not NWC Meltdown but the best superstars in the World Wrestling Alliance unified as one. From here on in the games are over, and the new era has begun!
