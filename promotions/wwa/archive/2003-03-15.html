WWA Fiesta! Saturday 15th March 2003 The scene is inside Commissioner Raine's office. Raine is there, doing some paperwork. There is a knock at the door. Raine: Come in. EWA Owner Joe Stock walks in. Raine: Hey there. What's up? Stock: Not much. Listen I need a favour. I'm running a little low on cash flow and still need some equipment for the first EWA show. Raine looks puzzled, than goes back to his work. Raine: More paperwork than anything, Cruz certainly fouled up some of these contracts. Suddenly, there is another knock at the still open door, and Raine looks up from his paperwork. Raine: Ah, Evan, Nicholas, come on in. The door widens to its fullest and in walks Keru no Akiro and Evan Motive, dressed in street clothes. Raine: Are you all set for later? Akiro: As set as we can be going into that sort of match.
