What this is Roster bio card for the WWA wrestler Miguel, giving height, weight, birth date, hometown, entrance music, physical appearance, ring attire and personality.
Biography Wrestler's Name: Miguel Height: 5'7 Weight: 160 pounds Date Of Birth: 7/13/83 Hometown: Madrid, Spain Entrance Music: Mexican Hat Dance Wrestler's Quote: Physical Appearance: Miguel isn't the big man on campus. He is rather tiny compared to others. He is 5'7" and only weighs in a 160 pounds. His legs are very musclular with how fast he moves. He has the typical six pack. He has blue eyes with blonde hair. His hair is cut army style. He has a scar on his stomach from fighting a bull and some scars on his arms from running from the bulls. Ring Attire: Miguel wears a pair a black pants with the word Miguel written down one side in red letters, and on the other, it has SCWF in Red Letters. On the front and back it has the Spanish flag. He also wears a blue Miguel t-shirt that on the front says, Tit, Tit, Gold. And on the back it says Miguel-hunting for Gold. He comes out to the ring with a sombrero. Personality: Miguel is a man of many emotions. He is usually a guy who is always happy and wants to entertain. That is the reason he joined the business, to see the fans. There are some occassions when Miguel can become very angry and hurt people. Miguel always will lend a helping hand, may it be protecting Referee Amy Trudeau, or just finding a way to help, he is always there for people.
