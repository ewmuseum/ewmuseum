WWA — 27 Oct 2000

27 Oct 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
WWA Friday Night EXTREME Fights October 27th, 2000 The black screen is replaced with Johny Morehouse talking to one of the security men for the arena. Johny : You don't udnerstand. You are not to stop anything. Tonight it's all No Holds Barred. Security Guard : That's insane Johny. The wrestlers are going to kill each other. Johny : Maybe. But the fans wants it, and they're going to get it. Morehouse turns and looks right into the camera. Johny : Live from New York...IT'S FRIDAY NIGHT! The intro fades right into Madison Square Garden, where the fans go nuts as pyro shoots all over. The camera pans around and catches some fo the signs, includning "Buck Flyod and Superman: Both Rule New York!", "Shaman is My Hero", and "Troy Walcott, Make Like A Tree and Leave!" The camera lands on Jimmy Rose and Fat Tony in the ring with mikes. Gary Trudeau is also in the ring, posing for the fans. Jimmy : Ladies and Gentleman, What about them Damn Yankees?!!! Big reaction from that, except for a few Mets fans who boo. Jimmy : This is the Big Apple, the City That never Sleeps. You had World Series action all week except for Monday, when the Jets fought in the longest game in Monday Night Football history. Tonight we're not going to let you come down from the high. Tonight it's Friday Night EXTREME Fights! The crowd goes nuts. Jimmy : I'm your host, Jimmy Rose, and this is my co-host... Fat Tony : Fat "The Yankees, the Met, the Giants, and the Jets. No one more than me likes to watch sweaty men play with balls." Tony. No one has any clue what to make of that. Jimmy : ANYWAY! Tonight, it's Dehibilitator vs Troy Walcott in a match to settle this feud that just won't die. Tricksters also takes David Drake. Fat Tony : New Star and Daniel E. Nelson will kill each other in a Barbed Wire Match and in our main event, it's a fourway for the Extreme Title, Shaman, Brian Jones, Buck Floyd, and Shawn Cage! The fans start chanting "BUCK, BUCK, BUCK". Fat Tony : You know it almost sounds like they're saying... Jimmy : To our first match, take it away Gary! The arena lights go out. "Knock Me Down" by the Red Hot Chili Peppers begins to play on the PA. A strobe light flashes on the entrance ramp. The WWA-Tron blinks to life, displaying three words in rapid succession. SIZE DOES MATTER Gary Trudeau : Ladies and Gentlemen, now making his way to the ring, from Houston, Texas... weighing in at 390 pounds... Craven Moore! White floodlights blaze to life at the rear of the entrance. Craven steps into the lights and casts a shadow all the way down the aisle. Jimmy : Not wanting to make a bad pun here folks, but it looks like the good Doctor is going to have a huge fight on his hands! Craven pushes his hair out of his eyes and walks toward the ring, not making eye contact with anyone, just staring straight ahead at the ring. When he reaches the ring Craven takes one step to the ring apron, grabs the top rope and steps over it. Fat Tony : Damn! It'd be like signing a match to fight Godzilla! He cracks his knuckles and waits for his opponent to arrive. He doesn't have to wait long. "Dr. Feelgood", by Motley Crue blares over the loudspeakers as blue & white striped pyros go off and out comes Dr. Love. The fans are going crazy. He is dressed in blue O.R. scrubs and has a white lab coat over them. He also has a surgical mask around his neck. Nurse Candy Hart comes out next to him, wearing a white nurse's outfit that's unbuttoned enough to see a black lace bra underneath. Jimmy : And here comes Dr. Love and Nurse Candy Hart! Fat Tony : Look at Candy! Boy, I wish I was sick! Jimmy : You are. Love goes to ringside and steps in the ring. Fat Tony : No giving away the surgical mask? Jimmy : Guess not. The Doc says he is all business now. Love grabs the mic. Love : New York, New York......(with crowd)...The DOCTOR IS IN!!! The crowd pops Love : Let's skip the pre-match speech tonight and wrestle! The crowd cheers as Love throws the mic into the stands and charges at Craven. Jimmy : And we are under-way ! Both men lock up centre-ring, each one trying to exert his physical superiority on his opponent. Fat Tony : You know, I wouldn’t mind getting under-way with Nurse Candy Jimmy : You are a sick man, you know that? Fat Tony : Exactly! I need a personal examination from Nurse Candy! As Tony and Jimmy banter, Craven Moore gets the upper hand, whipping Dr Love into the ropes. He bounces off the ropes himself and hits Love with a huge shoulderblock. Most men would go down. Love just soaks it up. Jimmy : Incredible! Dr Love didn’t even budge! Craven scowls, and bounces off the ropes with a second shoulderblock. Despite the fact the hit would have rattled a Mack truck, Love still keeps on his feet, much to the crowd’s approval. Craven rolls his eyes, gives a "why me" style shrug and goes for a third. Fat Tony : Went to the well once too often, the big lug! He did indeed. Dr Love drops his shoulder at just the right time and sends Moore up and over with a huge backdrop. The crowd roar their approval, and the good Doctor acknowledges them before picking his foe up. With an incredible display of strength, Love actually manages to lift his massive opponent up into the air and delivers a gorilla press slam. Jimmy : You know, with a few more moves like that, Dr Love can wrap this one up! Fat Tony : Yeah! Craven Moore really needs to turn the tide! Dr Love drops an elbow on his fallen foe. As he goes for a second, Craven manages to roll out of the way, and is up before Love, sending him crashing to the ground with a diving clothesline. Fat Tony : See? Maybe the big lug was listening to me. Jimmy : I very much doubt that Now the match begins to sway Moore’s way. Following up the clothesline, he whips the good Doctor into the ropes and catches him with a huge spinebuster. He goes for the pin, but the referee is, ahem, "distracted" Jimmy : Nurse Candy Hart is up on the apron ! Fat Tony : Talk about your outside distractions ! Craven Moore is disgusted by this setback, and stands up from the pin, going over to where Candy and the ref are jawing. Craven gets in Nurse Candy’s face, and she responds by slapping him! The crowd "oooh" as Craven stands there, unmoved, despite the red handprint on his face. Nurse Candy shows some smarts by jumping down off the apron and back to ringside. Jimmy : And Craven Moore is not a happy man, Tony! Fat Tony : Tell me about it! He’s staring at her like she’s the main course! Nurse Candy, sly little minx that she is, has noticed that her man has managed to get back to his feet whilst Craven has been distracted. She makes a sultry "come-and-get-me" style gesture. Craven appears to be thinking it over, but turns away Just in time to be sent over the top rope by a clothesline! The huge man crashes to the concrete like a felled redwood, and lies there unmoving as Dr Love steps over the top rope and down to ringside. Jimmy : Now remember folks, this match is No-Holds-Barred ! Fat Tony : If you’ve got kids watching, now’s the time to remind them that all WWA superstars are highly trained athletes, and not to copy them! Dr Love picks up Moore, who is still a little groggy from the fall he just took. The Doc measures the big man up and Jimmy : BAM! Short-arm clothesline ! Fat Tony : Moore’s head bounced off the concrete like a sack o’ potatoes! Dr Love picks up his foe and attempts to whip him into the guardrail. However, it would appear the mighty Texan was just playing possum: he reverses the whip, and the entire arena winces in pain as Dr Love goes crashing into the rail. Nurse Candy runs over to check on her man as Moore advances. Fat Tony : Oh, man! Get outta there Candy! You already made him mad! Don’t let him hurt your pretty face! Candy chooses to ignore Fat Tony, bravely standing in front of her fallen man. Fat Tony : It’s like Fay Wray and King Kong all over again! Craven Moore grabs Candy by the shoulders and bodily lifts her away from her man, but places her gently down on her feet. He then goes for a powerbomb on the concrete! Jimmy : Someone stop him! I know this is No-Holds-Barred, but for God’s sake! You don’t powerbomb a man on the concrete! You can end careers that way! Craven sets up for the powerbomb, and looks to the crowd to see if he should continue. They roar, and he is about to hit the move when Candy Hart inserts herself into the action once again. Fat Tony : Nurse Candy with the low blow! Any normal man would be rolling around on the floor in agony by now. Craven Moore just lets go of the Doc and lets him fall to the floor. Fat Tony : Run, Candy! Come over here! We’ll protect you from the big scary man! Fat Tony’s words come just too late: Craven grabs a hold of Nurse Candy’s shoulders a second time. We can’t hear what he says to her, but from the terrified look on her face it wasn’t nice. Then, as if not even thinking about it, he lifts her up over the guardrail and plonks her down in the lap of a ringside fan. Not just any ringside fan, mind Fat Tony : Ew! That guy makes Bastion Booger look like Tom Cruise! Craven Moore says something to the disgusting fan, and a grin spreads across the man’s face. Craven picks up the good Doctor and rolls him back into the ring, but the fan has a tight grip on Nurse Candy. It doesn’t look like he’s gonna break it. Jimmy : It may be a little sick, but you have to admit it: he’s effectively neutralised that particular threat! Fat Tony : Why didn’t he drop her on me? I would have been a lot less disgusting to be dropped on than We see a shot of the disgusting fan and Nurse Candy. For once, Tony is rendered speechless. Jimmy : (laughing) WWA, the only place in the world where buying a ringside seat gets you inside the action! Back in the ring, Doctor Love still lies motionless. Craven slides a chair under the bottom rope and then follows it into the ring. Jimmy : Now in a normal match, that’d be an instant disqualification, but this is No Holds Barred! Fat Tony : I wonder what he’s going to do with that chair, Jimmy? Jimmy : (deadpan) Well, he certainly isn’t going to sit on it Doctor Love still lies motionless. Moore picks him up and then just as swiftly levels him with a short-arm clothesline. Once again, he picks up the good Doctor, and follows up with an Irish Whip into the corner. Then, to the crowd’s delight, he lifts the Doc onto the top rope. Jimmy : I think we’re about to find out what Moore wanted that chair for! He places the chair somewhere in the ring where it looks as though it might be superplexed-upon. He then climbs up to the ropes and sets up for the move But the Doc has other plans! First, he punches Moore in the gut, making him break the hold, and then Jimmy : Oh my! Front-superplex onto the steel chair! Fat Tony : Forget about it ! The referee slides in to make the count. The crowd, sensing victory, count along with him 1 2 Jimmy : Wow! Craven Moore just surviving that awesome maneuver! Dr Love can’t believe it, and starts jawing out the ref for making a slow count. The ref jaws back. Jimmy : Dr Love wasting a lot of time here! If anything, the count was fast! Fat Tony : I think he’s still mad about what that moron did to poor Nurse Candy! Disgusted, Dr Love turns away from the ref and is met with a boot to the gut by the risen Craven Moore. Moore then levels the Doc with a stunner. However, he’s not finished. Without breaking the hold from the stunner, Moore rises up again and hits a Diamond Cutter! Jimmy : That’s gonna put tremendous strain on the Doc’s neck! He could have whiplash, or worse! Fat Tony : You know, that’s what I like about you Jimmy. Jimmy : What would that be? Fat Tony : Your talent for stating the blindingly obvious! Now Craven Moore has the upper hand once again and the chair in his hand. He picks up the fallen Doc, measures him up and Fat Tony : Ow! Someone call the Mets! We found the batsman they need! Jimmy : Craven nearly took off the Doc’s head with that chairshot, but he’s still standing! What will it take to bring him down? It looks like Craven Moore may be thinking the same thing. However, he’s gonna take a shot at it. He measures up with the chair and BAM! The crowd "ooohs" in awe as the Doc crumples to the ground and the now-broken chair is tossed away. Moore picks up his foe over one shoulder, carries him across the ring and lifts him onto one of the turnbuckles as if it were nothing. Fat Tony : Be careful Craven ! Remember what happened last time you did this! Craven sets up for the move Jimmy : Reverse powerbomb from the second turnbuckle ! You can forget about this one! Craven doesn’t go for the pin as we might expect him to and instead sets in the Moorelock, rocking as far back as he can. We see the tendons in Love’s neck stretching far further than they are meant to, and he quickly taps out. Jimmy : It’s over! Craven Moore is the winner! Nurse Candy extricates herself from her disgusting fan, shudders, brushes herself off and climbs over the guardrail, sliding into the ring to see if the Doc is alright. Craven Moore just stands there, impassive. The crowd gives a loud cheer as the Doc manages to get back to his feet, and he glares daggers at his victorious foe. Moore extends a hand for the Doc to shake. Dr Love looks at the hand, then at Nurse Candy, then at the hand again. Jimmy : Shake his hand! It was a good, clean fight! Fat Tony : Don’t do it, Doc! Remember what he made Nurse Candy sit on! The Doc comes to a decision, and shakes his opponent’s hand. Both men nod, and the Doc leaves the ring first, leaving Craven to soak up the applause. Jimmy : Craven Moore wanted to earn a little respect here tonight? I think he’s done a lot more than that! Moore steps over the top rope, raises a fist to the sky, accept the pop this generates, and slowly walks backstage. We go to a quick commerical. When we come back, The lights fade slowly to darkness, and in a whiny voice, not unlike you would expect to hear the World Champion's present catch phrase called out in, comes the call: I'm here to save wrestling, Dammit! Nobody in the crowd reacts of course, this entrance has never been seen before in any wrestling federation, from the feds that can't even be called indys to the big four. "Move it on Over" by George Thorogood begins to play, only the lyrics for the refrain sing though. Move it on over, Rock it on over. Move over little dog, the big bad dog is moving in. And a small man, too small to be a wrestler you'd think, standing five and a half feet tall and maybe one hundred and thirty pounds dripping wet. As the lights come up on his trip to the ring, the fans instinctively booing the man, it becomes apparent that he is dressed in a pair of brilliantly white dockers and wearing a white dress shirt of a similar color. While that would not be terribly bad, the addition of a black and red plaid vest on top of it makes for a distasteful combination. He has an intent look on his plain face, which seems to have a bit of dirt on his upper lip. On his prematurely balding head he is wearing an earpiece with a microphone attatchment. The song comes to an end, as he enters the ring, fading from the PA. The fans, not knowing the man, have continued to boo regardless, perhaps it's the obviousness of his appearance. Ian Kurt : Oh shut up. I'm here to save wresting, dammit. Stop giving me so much lip. Of course the crowd ignores him, but it does finally settle down so he can speak. He is not holding a mic, so it would appear his microphone is patched into the PA system. Ian Kurt : That's so much better. It's about time I'm given the respect I deserve. It's not every day a man puts himself on the line to protect the industry he has no stake in is it? I didn't think you'd have an answer for that. But soon you'll see that what I'm saying is all the truth. Growing up in Hartford, CT was not as easy as people would think. Sure I was born with the proverbial silver spoon in my mouth. Sure my father had all the money I would ever need to have a great life. But do you think money is everything? Well, I'm sure the poor people do, but come on. I know there's bound to be a number of well of people here, you know what I'm talking about right? The crowd does not seem to agree with the assumption, or at least the wording and handling of the topic. Ian Kurt : Oh grow up. I thought there would be some responsible adults here tonight. I guess I was wrong. No wonder crime is up, education is down, and wrestling has become a joke for the world to laugh at. That's right, wrestling has become a joke. Wrestlers are not athletes, they are actors. It's become so corrupt and poorly managed, that all I had do is pay off "President" Morehouse to have a little air time. He didn't even ask what I was going to talk about. But I'm here to reveal all of wrestling's secrets! I want the world to know what I have learned over my years watching wrestling. Wrestling is boxing with storylines. Do you think these men really love or hate one another like they show up on television? No, of course not. Do you understand me? Wrestling is fake! A commotion raises as President Johny Morehouse makes his way down to the ring quickly. He looks very upset. He walks right up to the man in the ring, and puts his fade right in his, using his microphone to speak, and making a point at the same time. Johny : Ian, your time is up. Ian Kurt : You can't do that, "President" Morehouse. You see, I paid for anywhere up to twenty minutes, and that time hasn't come yet. We have a contract, and since this is only a television series, I don't imagine you'd want to get canceled. I can do that you know, my father's a stockholder in the television network. Johny : I don't care if your father's the owner, you're out of here. The man turns his back on Johny and walks contemptiously to the ring ropes. Ian Kurt : That's right, throw me out because you can't handle the truth. Well, Johny, it's too late to throw me out. You see, I have a contract here with the World Wrestling Alliance. I have a long term contract with quite a few legal loopholes left in it specifically to let me get away with this kind of thing. I'm rich, I can afford better lawyers than the WWA usually employs. I can do anything. And you can't do anything against me. Ian Kurt :(turning on Johny with a big smile) But you're wrong again, Johny. I have a contract with the World Wrestling Alliance, but it's a contract that gives me the right to manage any wrestler in the WWA who wants to hire me. It also let's me bring in wrestlers who are already under contract with me. You see, "President" Morehouse, the WWA hasn't got the power to stop me from doing things like taking up air time, or even from bringing in a wrestler when I want to. And wouldn't you just know it? I have a wrestler all lined up to come in and fight some people. Johny : Well then, bring him out and we'll deal with you through dealing with those men who come with you! Pro wrestling hasn't changed a thing since it started, except to evolve with the times. Ian Kurt : (In a fury) LIAR! (Ian calms down quickly.) Wrestling used to be real, Captain Lou Albano took a polygraph test on the fact. Jimmy : I saw that on television too, he failed. Fat Tony : Won't you let the man finish? Ian Kurt : Wrestling was real once upon a time, not this soap opera on a stick. We didn't have grown men come out and say they have three testicals or had their penis chopped in half. We did not have to stage reasons for people to pretend to hate one another and then pretend to fight. Wrestling was once the sport of the gods, and men walked among them. Nelson, I'd say the WWA was definately in need of closure. But it shows you have some taste left. Daniel Nelson, look at this man.
