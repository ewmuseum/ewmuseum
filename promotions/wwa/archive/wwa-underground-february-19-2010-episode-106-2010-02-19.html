WWA: Underground, February 19, 2010 (Episode #106)

19 February 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is Full show script of the 2010 WWA: Underground webcast, episode 106, from the James White Civic Coliseum in Knoxville, with the U.S. title match, a number one contender main event and a preview of the next show.
WWA: Underground February 19, 2010 - Episode #106 Live from the James White Civic Coliseum in Knoxville, TN Chapter I Grant Promo The show opens inside the ring, where Gary Trudeau stands in the middle, holding a microphone. Trudeau: Ladies and gentlemen, now introducing your neeeeew WWA United States Heavyweight Champion... "The Lion" John Grant! The crowd gives a loud pop as a bell is heard, and the lights in the arena fade to black. The bell is followed by a second, then a third and a fourth. The hard guitar riffs of "Hells Bells" by AC/DC begin to play, and blue-and-yellow lasers crisscross the entrance. Finally, a spotlight comes down, and highlights the form of "The Lion" John Grant, dressed in his wrestling tights and boots, with a black T-shirt emblazoned with the letters "DV" in white. And he's wearing the U.S. Heavyweight Title belt, which he won only a week ago on this program. Jack: There he is, Denrol! The man who, at 17 years old, outlasted five other individuals last week to become the new United States Champion! Denrol: I think you mean, "boy". He's said so himself, he's not even old enough to smoke yet! Grant removes the belt from around his waist and raises it high to the sky, eliciting another loud cheer from the crowd. Jack: Be that as it may, there's no denying he earned the championship last week, and tonight he'll face his first challenge for the belt. Denrol: I can only assume that's what he's gonna talk about. Grant has now entered the ring, belt slung over his shoulder, and the lights in the arena slowly fade up to normal, and the music fades after another moment. He's obviously trying to maintain the serious air of professionalism that he usually exudes, but after a moment or two he can't anymore-- he starts smiling, the expression practically splitting his face in two. Jack: Look at that young man. You think he's not happy? Denrol: He's happy all right, but he needs to keep in mind he's got a title match tonight. No rest for the weary. Trudeau hands Grant his microphone and exits the ring, and Grant stands there for another few moments, soaking up the adulation of the crowd. Finally, he raises the microphone to his lips. Grant: What a difference two weeks make! Jack: You can say that again, kid. Grant: Two weeks ago, I entered Underground still looking for my first career win, looking ahead to the time... when I could finally give Kid Cool the beating he so richly deserved. Looking back now, I think it's safe to say my sights were set a little bit too low. Denrol: Yeah, beating Kid Cool doesn't exactly qualify you for a lifetime achievement award. Grant: So towards the end of the night-- and after Jevon and I won the tag-team match-- I was more than a little thrown for a loop when one of the guys in the back came up to me and said, 'John, did you hear? They're putting you in a six-man elimination match for the U.S. Title!' Grant smiles at that, as the crowd cheers. Grant: To be honest... I thought the guy was putting me on. I talked to one of the guys from Insider to confirm it. And then, when I heard Richie Stone was in the match as well... The crowd immediately starts jeering, and Grant chuckles softly. Grant: When I heard I'd be facing Richie Stone, my eyes lit up and I said, "I'm gonna beat that guy within an inch of his life." Richie, when you attacked your wife you committed an unforgivable sin! If you've got a beef with one of the wrestlers, go out and make an example of him, but never-- NEVER-- raise your hand to a woman, let alone your wife! Jack: You've got that right! Denrol: I'm not John Grant's biggest fan but I really enjoyed watching him beat up Stone last week. Grant: So I spent all week preparing, studying, and training for the contest. I knew it'd be a battle of endurance as much as anything-- would I be ready to take punishment as well as dish it out? Well, I think everyone here knows the answer to that question; it's readily apparent right here on my shoulder! Grant pats the U.S. Title belt with his left hand, and the crowd cheers. Grant: I promise that every night I wrestle, I'll put on the best performance I know how to, and leave everything in that ring. I promise that I'll be a fighting champion, never backing down from a challenge, just like Jack Griffiths before me. And in the spirit of never backing down from a challenge... last week, before I even won this championship, I signed up to battle Richie Stone right here tonight! And it'll be my first defense of the United States Championship. Denrol: Why does Grant keep calling him "Richie"? It's RJ, now. Jack: The champion's obviously trying to get under Stone's skin. Grant: Well, I'm not satisfied with simply winning the title, I'm aiming to keep it as long as I can. Nor am I content to just beat up Richie Stone once; I'm gonna beat him again tonight, and every night we face each other-- "Coming Undone" by Korn plays, and Grant turns to face the ring entrance. Jack: Apparently, RJ Stone's got a rebuttal! Denrol: Great. The crowd furiously boos as RJ appears at the top of the entrance aisle. He is still dressed in street clothes and has a microphone in hand. His gaze is set directly on John Grant. His music slowly fades and all that is left is the sound of the crowd showering RJ in hate. Grant and Stone stare at one another for seconds that feel like years before RJ speaks. Stone: I see you've taken a liking to your new toy. The crowd pops as Grant holds the title aloft with a smile on his face. Stone: Pity you won't be celebrating for much longer. Grant now becomes more serious. Grant: So I see you came out to make some more unfounded promises. Now Stone smiles. Stone: How much more have you thought about what happened to your mother since my actions two weeks ago, John? The crowd lets Stone have it for this comment. RJ also clearly struck a nerve with Grant. Jack: Come on! That was uncalled for! Denrol: Seems like "uncalled for" and RJ Stone are one in the same as of late. Stone: Your silence proves my point. Don't you get it now, young lion? Don't you understand what is happening? I am already beating you. In the end you are going to be defeated by me. I don't even have to raise a finger. All I have to do is merely exist in your presence. You will eventually fall to me as long as there is still a heart beat in my body. Denrol: What heart?! Grant is not amused. Grant: I could arrange for that to stop, tonight. The crowd cheers. Stone cocks his head to the side quizzically. Even though he wears sunglasses it seems as though he is a doctor or surgeon looking over a patient. Stone: You don't even realize what I've done to you, do you? I can see it from here, I can see the rage eating away at your insides. I can see how disgusted you are that you have to lay hands on me again tonight. Jack: Who wouldn't be? The Lion has clearly had enough of Stone's babbling. Grant: Is there some underlying point here, or can I just be satisfied with kicking your ass tonight? The crowd eats this up. RJ nods affirmatively, smirking. Stone: That's fine with me. Just remember though, after our match tonight, whether you win or lose, I'll still be here. I'll always just be a title match away. RJ lowers the microphone from his lips as "Coming Undone" plays him out. He slowly walks backwards, staring at Grant the entire time. The camera moves onto the United States champion, a look of confusion and anger on his face. Jack: What did Stone have to gain from that little appearance? Denrol: As much as I hate to say it Jack....I think that Stone was correct when he said that he was in Grant's head. Chapter II Somers Arrives We cut backentrance to where a taxi pulls up in the parking lot. A rear door opens and out steps Mal Somers eliciting a cheer from the Knoxville crowd in the arena. Jack: Most superstars turn up in a limo, not Mal Somers! Denrol: Times are still tough! Jack: Maybe he's above all that pretentiousness? Denrol: No, he's just a miserly bastard. He pays the driver, mainly in coins and grabs a large duffel bag from the trunk. As he hoists the heavy bag over his shoulder, his phone rings. Denrol: Hopefully that's the police tracking Mal down after his heinous attack on Intrepid last week. Jack: He was only giving back as good as he got. Somers: Hello, yeah this is Mal Somers... He nods a couple of times, grinning. Somers: What?! His grin disappears. Somers: Possible lawsuit...verbal contract...criminal damages? Look dude, that's all double Dutch to me...just get to the point. Somers grits his teeth and nods one last time. Somers: Oh, he did now! Fine! He slams the phone shut, takes a breath and then throws it hard into the nearest wall. Somers: INTREPID!!! He unzips his bag, looks inside and shakes his head. Finally, he hoists the bag over his shoulder and enters the arena. Jack: What was all that about? Denrol: I have a feeling we will find out soon. Chapter III Adams & Guevesos Vs Mr. Internet & Powers Jack: Before we move on, I have a quick programming note. Chris Conway Vs Ry Ballard was announced for tonight, but because of problems with travel arrangements, they were unable to make it to the show tonight. Denrol: I actually was looking forward to that. Hopefully we get to see it in the near future. Jack: And now it's time for the first bout of the night! We've got a special treat for you this evening... Denrol: Yeah - the most boring WWA Champion and the most boring staff member in the WWA taking on the shortest-reigning champions in the history of the WWA. Big... Jack: Besides you, you mean. Denrol: You know, if you want me to pull out the footage, I will. Tracer Bullet could sue you for bringing that up. Jack: I don't think he will, though. Either way, let's take it to the ring. The camera cuts to the arena, where Gary Trudeau is standing in the ring, microphone in hand. Trudeau: Ladies and gentlemen, the following is a tag team contest, and it is scheduled for one fall! Introducing first... Some guitar music plays, and "Renegades of Funk" by Rage Against the Machine begins to play over the speakers. Some fans give a boo, although the more long-standing WWA fans start to pull out signs, including "404 Error" and "Squadron Crash". The lights have a simple strobe effect to them. Trudeau: Hailing from the state of New York, at a combined weight of three-hundred eighty pounds, Mister Internet and Tim Powers, the Job Squad! The fans give a slightly more hearty boo as the two men come out to the aisle. Mister Internet is wearing some dress clothes, including a black tie with what appears to be UNIX code. Tim Powers seems to just be awed by the whole situation, and is wearing a black singlet with some black boots. Internet, in spite of being smaller than Powers in every way, manages to catch his attention by pulling him down to the ring. Jack: Powers seems to be unable to believe that he's competing in the WWA again. Denrol: Hey, given the rest of the WWA roster, I'm really not surprised. Heck, half the people on the roster when we started back up don't deserve to be here! Jack: I'm assuming that you're not including yourself in that group. Powers and Mister Internet step through the ropes without doing anything to acknowledge the fans, and that suddenly gets the crowd mildly against them. They don't pose, they don't do anything to put on a show - they just go to their corner and wait. Trudeau: And their opponents... huh? Jack: It appears a ring attendant is addressing Gary out there. Denrol: I wouldn't be surprised if Nick forfeits... Trudeau nods and straightens up, and then "Superman's Dead" by Our Lady Peace suddenly hits the speakers just before the chorus. Alone, alone, alone, alone, alone - I'm thinkin'... WHY IS SUPERMAN DEAD? The fans suddenly start cheering loudly as two men suddenly burst out on to the aisle, dressed in what can only be described as some kind of comic book superhero garb. The taller man on the right side fo the entrance is wearing red tights, a black tight top, and red gloves, with a stylized white "R" in the middle, and a red harlequin mask on his face. The WWA Championship is around his waist. The other man is wearing a green and white tie-dye-style one-piece long-legged setup, with a red "M2" on his chest, and a green bandana-style mask on his head, which covers his hair, as well as the top part of his face. Denrol: Oh, come on! This is totally unprofessional! Jack: Professional or not, the fans are loving it! Trudeau: And their opponents, first, at one-hundred forty-five pounds, hailing from Guadalajara, the MEXICAN MAELSTROM! The fans cheer as the man in green jumps up to the nearest turnbuckle and raises his hands. Trudeau: And his partner, hailing from the Storm Center, weigning in at two-hundred twenty-five pounds, THE RAINMAKER! The stadium practically erupts as the man in red jumps up to the turnbuckles on the other side of the ring, unlatching the belt from his waist and raising it above his head. He smiles and points out to the crowd as Trudeau steps out of the ring, and the music calms down. Denrol: For those of you who are wondering why Nick Adams and Juan Guevesos aren't facing the Job Squad, it's because those two idiots in the masks ARE Nick Adams and Juan Guevesos. Jack: Don't insult the WWA fans' intelligence. We've referred to the champion as The Rainmaker in the past. Denrol: You and Mikal, maybe. Not I. The referee hands the WWA Championship to a ring attendant and calls for the bell. Mister Internet steps in to the ring, and Rainmaker goes for a locks up. Internet rolls under Rainmaker’s arms and tries to hit a variation on a schoolboy, but The Rainmaker keeps his balance, then looks down. He grabs the arm wrapped around his thigh and pulls out and up, swinging himself to the mat as he does so. Somehow, Internet goes through 360 degrees and goes to the mat with a huge arm drag. Jack: The Rainmaker’s super-balance was in play there. Denrol: Really, Jack? You want to call the match like that. Jack: You know, you used to be more fun, from what I can see in the archives. Denrol: Yeah, and the WWA used to have real wrestlers. What’s your point? Internet stands, a little dazed, but turns and runs in to Rainmaker. He only takes another arm drag, but gets up again. However, he just winds up taking a drop kick from the crimson-clad crusader, who pulls Internet up and tosses him in to the corner, where the Mexican Maelstrom is waiting. Rainmaker tags in the Maelstrom, who climbs up to the top rope. He jumps off, does a half turn, and lands legs-first on Internet’s shoulders before hitting a rana that sends Internet flying to the center of the ring. Jack: Maelstrom, showing some surprising spring in spite of his years. Denrol: Alliteration? How many superhero cliches are you going to use tonight? Maelstrom pulls Internet off the mat and immediately places him in a wrist lock. Internet manages to use what little size advantage he has to reverse the move. Maelstrom, however, uses his agility to suddenly roll through the lock. He springs to his feet and sweeps Internet’s legs out from under him. Internet rolls to his feet, and Maelstrom is right there waiting for him. However, Internet gives Maelstrom a poke to the eye - no small feat, given the mask - which buys him enough time to tag in Tim Powers. The referee goes to admonish Internet while Powers gives a clubbing blow to Maelstrom’s back. Jack: That was blatant cheating right there! Denrol: Hey, if it doesn’t get you disqualified right away, I say use it. Jack: You would. Maelstrom goes to the ropes, mostly because he’s still somewhat blinded and can’t quite see where he is. The referee tries to get Powers to back off, but he goes and puts Maelstrom’s throat across the bottom rope. Powers then presses his shin in to the Mexican Maelstrom’s back, choking him out on the ropes. The referee begins to make the disqualification count. One! Two! Three! Four! Powers lets go, and Maelstrom takes himself off the ropes, crawling away from Internet and Powers’ corner. However, he doesn’t get enough time to recover, as the larger Powers just steps around the irate referee and hits a field goal kick right in to the Maelstrom’s ribs! The Mexican Maelstrom gets about a foot off the mat and hits hard, clutching his chest in pain. Powers doesn’t let up in spite of the referee’s count, and lifts Maelstrom to his feet, using the ropes to help him. Denrol: Powers is being brutal here! This is a new side to him. Jack: Maybe so, but the willingness to ignore the rules is the same. Before the referee ends the match on a technicality, Powers whips Maelstrom across the ring and then delivers a brutal tilt-a-whirl gutbuster, causing Maelstrom to cough heavily. Rainmaker is getting furious at this point, but Powers doesn’t seem to notice. He pulls Guevesos up and whips him to the ropes again. Jack: This is a smart tactic - Powers is letting Maelstrom do most of the work. Denrol: Yeah. A guy like Guevesos, fit or not, will still wear down after a while. Jack: Who? Denrol: Really? You’re going to just buy in to the whole “masks make me unable to recognize people I see on a regular basis” thing? Maelstom falls prey to a belly-to-belly suplex, and The Rainmaker has begun to start a small chant for the Mexican Maelstrom. Internet is in his corner pretending that he can’t hear the crowd, and Powers grabs Maelstrom off the mat. He whips Maelstrom to the ropes one more time and looks to be going for a spinebuster, but suddenly, Maelstrom counters in to a tornado DDT! Powers drops right on top of his head, and Guevesos is flat on his back, trying to catch his breath. Jack: Powers went to the same well one too many times! Denrol: Well, with the Job Squad, the well runs dry fairly quick. Powers is holding his head, and has a look that indicates that he has no idea what just happened. Meanwhile, Guevesos is slowly working his way to his corner, with the fans chanting, “Adams!” Obviously, the superhero stuff is fun, but apparently nobody is ready for more than two syllables this early in the night. Powers gets to his feet and looks over at Mister Internet, who points at Maelstom. Maelstrom gets the tag just as Powers turns around, and Rainmaker hits Powers with a huge missile drop kick! Jack: And Rainmaker is back in! Denrol: And as usual, he’s using showy moves to make up for a personality. Mister Internet rushes in, thinking he can blindside a rising Rainmaker, but the superhero is too quick, and hits him with a Manhattan Drop. With Mister Internet reeling, Rainmaker takes the opportunity to clothesline him over the top rope. Tim Powers has gotten to his feet, and Rainmaker turns around just in time to see him rushing in. He hits Powers with an arm drag and stands back up. Jack: Rainmaker is on fire! Denrol: How I wish that were literally true. Powers gets to his feet and comes in again, but this time gets a huge back body drop! The fans cheer as Adams waits for Powers to get up again. Powers takes a swing at the Rainmaker, but Rainmaker ducks ant hits Powers with an Atomic Drop! The fans are going crazy, and as Powers holds his back and circles around, Rainmaker plants his right hand firmly on Powers’ neck! Jack: He’s going for the choke slam! Denrol: Oh, come on! There’s no way that he could possibly... And Denrol is proven wrong, as Powers comes up off the mat and the Rainmaker smashes him back in to the ring with one arm! The crowd goes crazy as Rainmaker goes to tag in Maelstrom, who seems to have totally recovered. Jack: And Maelstrom goes up top! Denrol: Oh, come on, let’s just get this over with already! The Mexican Maelstrom comes off the top with a long-distance frog splash, and the fans are really chanting now - of course, it’s “Adams!”, but nobody cares about the given names. Maelstrom stays down for the pin, and the referee slides in to make the three-count. One! Two! Mister Internet comes in, but Adams cuts him off so that he can’t break up the pin. Three! The referee signals for the bell and the fans start cheering as Rainmaker once again tosses Internet out of the ring, and Maelstrom stands, one arm placed just below his rib cage. Rainmaker walks over to Maelstrom, and the referee gets between them, grabbing one wrist on each man. Trudeau: Ladies and gentlemen, your winners... Suddenly, with their free hands, Rainmaker and Mexican Maelstrom reach up and pull of their masks. Trudeau: Juan Guevesos and the WWA Champion, Nicholas Adams! The fans cheer as the referee raises one of each man’s arms, and then the two men take a bow. Jack: A great victory by these two friends, and a fun spectacle for the fans! Denrol: Crap like this is why I chose to join the commentary team. Suddenly, two large men in black suits and sunglasses rush in from the entrance entrance, and their mouths and body language as they approach the ring looks particularly annoyed. They stop Adams and Guevesos as they’re moving to exit the arena, and the camera goes in to show Nick rolling his eyes while the two oversized suits with bodies in them seem to be explaining something. Nick and Juan keep moving to the backentrance area while the men in suits keep admonishing them Jack: Apparently, the costumes were so that Nick Adams’ “security detail” wouldn’t be at ringside. Denrol: Not the smartest guards in the world, are they? Jack: Well, Ry Ballard has been known to cut costs where possible. But the disguises were actually half decent. Denrol: Sure. Except that the fans were chanting the champ’s name for most of the match. Chapter IV Backentrance with Intrepid Intrepid is sitting in an old-fashioned rocking chair with his feet propped up on one of the locker room benches. Virtue is sitting in his lap thumbing through a flipbook while D.Filed is nowhere in sight. However, the shower appears to be running in the background. Denrol: Who brings their rocking chair to a wrestling event? Jack: I have heard of celebrities asking for… Denrol: Shut up! Suddenly there is a single, quiet knock at the door. Intrepid: Cocking his head slightly, staring at the door I think our company has arrived D.Filed! D.Filed: From the shower Really? So you’ll finally tell me who it is we are expecting? Intrepid: Rolling his eyes I wouldn’t have to tell you now that they are here. You may enter if you’re fat and redheaded. No-one replies. D.Filed comes strolling out of the shower with one towel wrapped around her while using another to dry her jet-black hair. D.Filed: I thought you didn’t like redheads? Intrepid: With a frustrated tone I do not…who is it! Only a faint whisper responds. Intrepid: Clearly getting angry, he sets Virtue down before standing. Quit messing around! The person in the hallway speaks a little louder. Voice: I'm your new valet! D.Filed: Valet? Intrepid shushes D.Filed with a raised finger and cocks his head while getting to his feet. He walks over to the door and opens it to reveal... Jack: It's Mal Somers! Denrol: In a Sumo suit?! Intrepid steps back into a defensive pose as Mal enters the room and the Knoxville crowd roar with laughter as the one time Chairman of the Extreme Corporation starts to enter the room with his hair slicked back and wearing a large inflatable sumo suit. Intrepid: It’s about time you… Somers: Shut your pie hole! After some shimmying and walking sideways, Mal enters the room. Intrepid: Under normal circumstances, this would be funny… Somers: SHUT IT!!! I have just had a call from WWA Legal and it seems that two weeks ago we entered into a verbal contract. Intrepid: So they did get a hold after I filed those injunctions…so do you remember your legal obligations to me? Somers: Almost toppling over Shhhhh! The contract was as follows. If I was to beat you in our match that became the six-man US Title match, then I could retire gracefully. Intrepid: Folding his arms Like that was going to happen. Somers tries to lurch forward in an aggressive manner but instead he bumps some coffee cups and sugar off a not so nearby table. Virtue comes up next to Intrepid in a defensive pose, crosses his arms, staring coldly at Mal. Mal watches the little boy, and shakes his head in disbelief. Somers: However, if I didn't beat you...and just hold up for a minute there because I meant to say if "you beat me" but they have played the audio and unfortunately I said if "I didn't beat you"... Intrepid nods in agreement while the towel clad D.Filed smiles broadly. Somers: ...then I said I would dress up in a sumo suit and be your valet! Some of the crowd boos and others cheer, Intrepid certainly looks happy as does Virtue and D.Filed. Somers: I meant it as a joke...however, the legal department is all over it...and here I am. Intrepid: Therefore, you really are my bitch aren't you Mal! Somers seethes, but he holds back. Somers: I guess my second order of business will be acting as your valet in that teeny weenie little match you are having next week... Intrepid stops breathing and tenses firmly with the realization. Somers: Oh that's right your World Title match against Nick Adams! The crowd roars and Intrepid shakes his head. Intrepid: I'll be fine on my own! Somers: No no no...I am contractually obliged it will be my pleasure! Intrepid: Staring at through the mask What are leading me up to? Somers grins. Somers: Well, truth be told, I suggested the inflatable sumo suit stipulation because, well, I guess subconsciously there has always been a little sumo in me just dying to get out. Intrepid: Okay... Somers: I think tonight's the night! Suddenly Mal tears open a flap on the side of his massive costume and lo and behold a small midget replete in his own sumo costume emerges, rather sweaty and attacks Intrepid! Jack: Now I really have seen it all! Denrol: Can we technically call it a mini me? The mini version on Mal pounces on Intrepid, clawing at his mask. Mal stands in the doorway of the locker room with a big smile on his face. Intrepid finally gets his hands around the mini Mal and flings him across the locker room. The mini Mal slams against the lockers but is quick to his feet despite the suit. Denrol: That little bugger is fast. Before the mini Mal can get any momentum, Virtue jumps onto him while D.Filed holds him down. Intrepid jumps at Mal and pins him against the arch of the doorway as security shows up. They try to get into the locker room as best as they can since Mal’s suit is partially blocking it. Intrepid : You will NOT interfere with my match next week! Mal : Smiling Oh, I wouldn’t dream of it. The security teams finally squeeze their way into the locker room. One team separates Intrepid’s counterparts from the mini Mal while another team pulls Intrepid and Mal apart. You can hear Intrepid foaming at the mouth underneath his mask, while Mal is wearing a contented smile. Intrepid : I have woven my way into WWA’s system to get what I want and you will not dare ruin this. I will see to it that you must attend to Virtue during my match. Mal : What? Intrepid : At any time you would dare to influence my match, despite its rules, you will inflict pain upon Virtue. Virtue looks up at Intrepid, smiles coyly, and then returns his comedic gaze upon Mal. Virtue: Anything for you my father. Intrepid : To insure that this will happen, I will have D.Filed remain backentrance watching on a monitor. Anytime you would think to influence the match, in either direction…she will deliver a shock on Virtue. This time D.Filed smiles staring grimly at Mal Intrepid : You will be just as hated from the WWA Fanatics as RJ Stone is. To think you choose to do your will in spite of the young boys’ well-being. Mal is no longer smiling at Intrepid. He has a look of pure disgust for his former friend and protégé. Mal : I have no idea what happened to you Silas but you are truly sick. Intrepid lunges at Mal despite the security teams’ restraint. Intrepid : SILAS IS DEAD! I AM INTREPID! The camera returns to Jack and Denrol sitting at their announce table backentrance. Jack looks disgusted while Denrol has a look of pure anger. Denrol: I have nothing to say. Jack: I think I may need therapy. It’ll be interesting to see what happens with this entire mess next week. Denrol: Good God, I want him dead. Chapter V WWA United States Championship #4 Grant Vs #7 Stone “Coming Undone” by Korn comes on over the PA. As the song kicks into full gear, RJ Stone stalks out on to the aisle. He glares out at the audience, a cold, predatory look on his face. Trudeau : First is the challenger, from Fresno, California and ranking #7 currently within the WWA, RJ Stone! Denrol: Can we change the rules to a street fight and get away with it? Jack: That would be nice, but I doubt it. A loud bell tolls, and the lights in the arena swiftly fade to darkness. A second bell follows, followed by a third and a fourth, and as the hard guitar riffs of "Hells Bells" by AC/DC play, a spotlight appears, and John Grant stands there. His arms crossed in front of him in an X shape, with his fists balled, he holds the pose for a moment, and then stretches his arms out to his sides, as far as they'll extend. Trudeau : He is the current WWA United States champion from San Francisco, California and currently ranked #4 within the WWA, John Grant! Jack: He is not only young but also confident. I see good things in this kid’s future. Denrol: I have a hard time not liking him but since he is up against Stone…I love him. Stone stands smugly in his corner just staring at Grant. As the referee presents the belt to Stone he doesn’t bother to glance at it, he keeps his lock on Grant. Ding, Ding! Grant moves out into the center of the ring as the crowd tenses with anticipation. However, Stone continues to lean in his corner, eyes still locked onto Grant. The crowd begins to admonish Stone, as does the referee, but in a more polite manner. Denrol: Great, not even a minute into it! Jack: We may need two referees’ out there tonight. Grant remains in the center of the ring, waiting for Stone as the referee admonishes him. Suddenly, Grant takes his eyes off Stone to feed off the crowd and that is when it happens. Stone snaps out of his corner, past the referee towards Grant. In that moment however, Grant turns back to Stone with a smile on his face. He steps slightly to the side of Stone grabbing him by the wrist. He twists Stone’s arm up and over the head and spins his opponent around. Jack: Looks like Stone is going for a ride. Stone however follows through in an attempt to counter and lands on his feet, facing Grant. Except, Grant anticipates him again and pushes in with a double knife-edged chop to Stone’s chest. The blow sends Stone reeling into the corner where he catches himself on the second turnbuckle. Crowd : WHOOOOOOO! The ref tries to ask Griffiths if he wants to continue again, he is about to sound the bell and Griffiths wakes up out of seemingly nowhere. The crowd uproariously cheers as it seems like he may return the favor and break Black’s hold. He tries to stand, but Black cranks back on the hold and Griffiths falls again. Jack: There’s still some fight left in him! Where the hell did this come from? Griffiths tries to stand again and falls once more, the crowd chants for him, but to no avail. The referee asks him if he wants to stop again, and there is suddenly no response. Jack’s last bit of fight has been used up and he now lifelessly hangs in the submission hold. The referee signals for the bell. Ding, Ding! Jack: It’s over! Griffiths gave it his all, but Lucas Black is a damned machine. Denrol: And now he won’t release the hold, as is always the case. Black’s eyes close as he keeps the hold locked in while his music plays. One! Two! Three! Four! Fiv- Black lets it go and allows the referee to raise his hand as Tiffani joins him in the ring. The fans all loudly boo. Trudeau: Your winner by submission and the number one contender for the WWA title, Luuuuuuuuuucas Blaaaaaaaaack! “Paint it Black” continues to play as Tiffani and Lucas both slowly walk up the aisle, eating up the hatred the crowd is spewing at them. Denrol: Well, I predicted it and it came true. Eat that, Ewan Thornton. Jack: And now we’re going to have round two of Black versus Adams in the not too distant future. The champ is in a bad way with so many individuals nipping at his heels for his belt. Whether or not he loses his title as Jack Griffiths did a week ago is left up to time. The camera zooms in on a slowly stirring Griffiths, and then pans over to the icy glare of Lucas Black. The scene slowly fades as his stoic expression turns into a devil like grin.
