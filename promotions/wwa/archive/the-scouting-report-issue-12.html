The Scouting Report - Issue 12

21 Jan 2004

Bylined Josh Wilson - Director of Talent, in the fed's own kayfabe voice.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
You know how sometimes you go to your favorite bar, the place you feel most comfortable expecting to see your old friend Steve behind the bar slinging drinks but there’s some new guy. You’re mad because you want to know who this guy is. How do you know if this Jeff guy can fix your favorite drink? And even Jeff feels a little bad because Steve trained him and gave him his shot. Well I’m Jeff, what’ll be your order? As most of you know Joe Stock has recently resigned from position as Director of Talent to enter a new business venture and I’ve been promoted. Joe is still staff and I wish him the best of luck in the rest of his endeavors. As the new director the Scouting Report falls on me and let’s get to it shall we. -Meltdown was a successful show this week. The main event has reshuffled the World Title picture just a bit by adding Kash and Damian Thorne to the mix. Kash is a bright rising star and most have predicted that it was only a matter of time before Thorne made his way to the top spot. The double submission ending was a good way to set this up with both Kash and Thorne claiming to be the number one contender. Expect good things from this. -Dorian and Wainwright got fired, due to lack of effort. They both had many accomplishments with Dorian being a former TV and US Title holder and had the feud of the year with Wainwright in 2001. I wish the best of luck to both men in the future. -The Franchise Players vs. Chance’s team was a good match showing off the skills of all three men. It also helped to further along two feuds. -Tracer Bullet and Enigma continue to get better. One has to think that a chance at the US Title won’t be a long wait for Enigma. Also this could be the feud that not only elevates Enigma but also may be a platform for Tracer Bullet back to the World Title. -Chance and Denrol is another old versus new feud. Many have predicted that Chance’s rise to the top would meet that of Kash’s. Of course Denrol has been here on and off for many years. Let’s just hope we don’t get a retread of the New Blood storyline we have a few years back with Vince Russo. -Vik Ventressa and Jackson Kraven with their mystery partners being Spike and Mr. Cool respectively did what it set out to do, continue the feud between Kraven and Ventressa. The match had many cool spots and being a fan of both men enjoyed their encounter but Spike and Mr. Cool? I believe they were included just for the ending. -The Veteranz and Havoc storyline continues to heat up. I have said it once and I will say it again, Havoc is pound for pound the best tag-team active today. Apparently I not the only one who thinks so either with the team winning this year’s WWAmmie for best tag-team. -You want to see the future of the WWA? Check out the match with Nick Adams and Venny LeTrav squaring off against Grunge and Joey K. All four of these men have bright futures here in the WWA and expect pushes for these four soon. -Speaking of Venny, LeTrav has been battling problems with his back lately, but the 29-year-old superstar has nothing but smiles despite his pain. -"For once, I'm just glad my ribs aren't killing me. It's a pain in the ass when you're trying to lift someone or pull off a Moonsault, but I'll make it." -Venny is reportedly doing great backstage and in the locker room. He has high spirits for the future and refuses to let a sore back keep him down. -President Warr is unhappy with some of the locker-room. A lot of the Roster is currently under harsh review, particularly from the newer guys that have seemingly done nothing. This is not acceptable to be given a chance and not even try. -However for every one slacker they’re two guys who are working twice as hard. Mr. Warr is really impressed with these individuals and they will get their pushes when the right opportunity occurs. -Delucci's are impressing many backstage including the boss. Great entry angle they have for themselves of course I’ve always been a sucker for the Sopranos and the Godfather movies. -Two other rookies worth mentioning are Chuck Whipple and Guido. They have a very good showing on Fiesta and I expect great things from both men. Is it just me or does Funk the Destroyer, Guido’s manager, look like George Clinton? -Things seem to be running very smoothly on Fiesta thanks to the new leadership of Commissioner Nathaniel Raine. Trust me the man is all business and he’s been taking good care of our rookies. Check out Fiesta, you won’t be disappointed. -The next Meltdown looks to be a great lineup. -Kash vs. Ian Tense looks to be a solid technical match considering both men’s skills in the area. The X-Factor of course will be Wes Sanders. -Expect the Ventressa/Veteranz against Havoc and Jackson Kraven to be the match of the night. I say this because Ventressa and Kraven are experts at getting the fans emotionally involved; Vik being on of the most loved men in the business and Kraven being the most hated. -Chance will have a challenge when he faces a wrestler of Denrol’s choice. Could it be the guy who drove the truck on Meltdown? Me thinks so. -Tracer Bullet vs. Enigma. This ending is tough to call. I expect a controversial one to continue their feud but I have been wrong. -The four-way for the TV Title looks like a scene-stealer. Don’t seem surprised to see somebody other than Nick Adams walk out with that belt. Check back next week for more rumors, injuries and much more. Now if you’ll excuse me I have a date.
