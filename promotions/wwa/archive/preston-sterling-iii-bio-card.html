Personality Preston Sterling III is a very patriotic wrestler and proud of his British heritage. He loves the Royal Family and everything the United Kingdom stands for. However, he is very arrogant, self-centred and of the oppinion he is "better than the working American." This part of him doesn't really make him so loved by the partisan American crowds. He is driven by his hatred for the American way of life and the boo's he recieves only fuel his hatred as he believes he should be the stand out star of any promotion. A very intellegent individual and uses his higher intelegence to insult and degrade individuals without them even knowing it from time to time, which adds to his arrogance. A natural athlete gives him an added competetive streak and he hates to be second best and focuses everything he can on ataining the number one status, even if that means bending the rules occasionally. History James Preston Sterling III is an English professional wrestler, colour commentator and actor currently signed on a temporary contract with WWA competing under the name Preston Sterling III. Early Life Sterling was born initially in Bury, Greater Manchester, England before moving with his family to Cambridge. Whilst living in Cambridge, Sterling was a highly touted prospect for Rugby Union and competed regularly in Open-Water Rowing, Football and Cricket. Sterling showed his intellectual potential from an early age and was eventually given a place into a very prestigious University in England, Cambridge where he immediately made the Rugby Union and Rowing teams. He graduated from Cambridge University with a Masters in Business Development and Management, his expertise being on Marketing and wrote a Thesis on the "modern day marketing for the modern day man" exploring the usefulness of different Marketing tools. While traveling in between holidays, Sterling was introduced to Professional Wrestling on a trip to Mexico, where he was englufed with amazement for the classic Luchadores. From this point on he vowed to become a professional wrestler. Sporting Accolades Sterling, being the avid Sports man, could have had a career in different sports due to his ability to pick up any sport he seemed to be involved with. He was a member of the Cambridge University Rugby Union team throughout his tenure as a Fly-Half. He also beat rival University Oxford twice as part of various Rowing teams. Sterling was asked to trial for local Football team Cambridge United and played for the England University team. He also was asked to play Cricket for Lancashire, chosing to continue his studies and traveling. Professional Wrestling Career Early Years [2005-2009] Sterling beagan his training at a local Wrestling school in England near to the University and was the leading prospect under coach Lee Reil - formerly one half of GWA Tag Team Champions with much more succesful brother Jayson Reil - and was known to have a natural ability to create heat on the microphone. Sterling made various vacations to known Wrestling hotspots and spent a lot of time in Mexico, Japan, Puerto Rico and the United States as well as small Wrestling communities through Europe. After his initial tenure under Lee Reil had finished and he graduated the school, he left to spend a year in Japan where he trained consistantly with various Dojos throughout the Tokyo and Osaka regions. While in Japan he developed a much stronger striking game to add to his "traditional" European style taught under the watchful eye of Reil. Eventually an opportunity came from two friends who he had trained with that were living in Cleveland, Ohio and working for Wrestling Midwest. They had kept in touch with Sterling and told their management about him but there was only a managerial position available for him. He accepted with the view of improving his microphone work. Wrestling Midwest [2009] In January 2009, Sterling signed a per-appearance contract with Wrestling Midwest and began working with WMW officials to get his character right for television. The Monarchy Sterling eventually debuted three months later to help The Monarchy Tag-Team, and friends, Austin Lancaster and Bradley Williams pick up a win over WMW Team Lumberjack and GG Bruiser. Sterling immediately gained heat from the crowd due to his very anti-Amerian character and was lauded for his abilities by previous WMW roster members. However, Lancaster and Williams were in the middle of some writing issues and were given minimal roles due to no direction being decided. A Tag Team tournament was thought to help but prior to the tournament taking place they were told they were going to be released from their contracts. WMW officials wanted to keep Sterling on and offeted him a one year contract, which he declined noting that "Austin and Bradley got me here, it'd be wrong of me to stay when they're gone." Sterling was seen on WMW twice more in August 2009 accompanying The Monarchy to losses against Adam Alloco and Harry Cockles and Justin Evers and Umbrage before being released to focus on his wrestling. WWA [2011-Present] Sterling returned from his hiatus and constant training in 2011 and signed a temporary contract with WWA. He was accepted with former Japan training partner Masahiro Kojima and is poised to start his professional in-ring career. Personal Life Sterling was born in Greater Manchester to former NCW pro wrestler Jonathan "Johnny Hollywood" Sterling and Louise Thompson. They eventually moved from the area when Preston was 7 to Cambridge. Sterling is a member of the infamous Sterling Wrestling Family, who are known for their bad behaviour backstage and refusal to follow company procedures. Noteable members of the family include his father - Johnny Hollywood, former NCW Television Champion and Uncle Damien Steele who has been around the Wrestling world for some time. Sterling doesn't speak to any of his family other than his father and has asked people not to judge him from his families mistakes. Sterling is an active Christian and attends a Baptist Church regularly, he has recently been baptized and reads the word of God daily. Sterling has been in a relationship with his high school sweetheart Nancy after they met early in school.
