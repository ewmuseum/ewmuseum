Friday Night Fights — 12 Mar 1999

12 Mar 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Friday March 12, 1999 The screen opens up to the WWA banner. Then firework type explosions go off around the Banner. The camera shows the outside of the complex, revealing it to be Madison Square Garden. The screen then goes to the inside to show the fans jumping and screaming at the Camera. The camera pans over the the Complex and then the camera finds Tony Bailey and Terry Valentine sitting behind the table in the broadcast area. Tony : Welcome ladies and Gentlemen to Friday Night Fights. I am your host Tony Bailey. And to my right is the great Johny Morhouse. And to my left Terry Valentine! Tonight we are gonna bring a great one for you. Johny : That's right! Tonight is the return of New Star in a Main Event against Kristopher Kasanova! Along with that we have the Eastern brackett first round! Terry : Tonight should be a good one! So... What are we waiting for....? Tony : As you wish Terry... Let's move to the first match of the evening... Gary Trudeau : Welcome Ladies and Gentlemen to Madison Square Garden! The first match of the evening will be the first round of the Eastern Region tournament. Introducing first weighing in at 325 pounds, representing the CPE, The Crippling Canuck Brent Watson! My Hero by The Foo Fighters plays over the PA system as Watson comes down to the ring. The crowd gives a negative reaction as he walks to the ring. Gary Trudeau : And his opponent hailing from parts unknown, standing at 7 foot and 4 inches and weighing in at 394 pounds we have representing X, We have Chaos! Frayed Ends of Sanity by Metallica starts over the PA. Chaos enters the arena to a mixed reaction. He enters the ring over the top rope. Johny : BRENT WATSON is dwarfed by CHAOS, but it looks like the two are going to tie up! Terry : That's going to be a mistake. Tony : Well CHAOS was only faking, a stiff kick to the stomach bends the CRIPPLING CANUCK over, and CHAOS shoots his man in... Johny : BRENT ducks the clothesline attempt and vaults back with a flying dropkick! Terry : Yeah but CHAOS is still standing and he smiles! The crowd is eating it up! Tony : I thought you hated good guys, wait WATSON has run the ropes again and goes for a cross body block, but is caught in mid air! Johny : CHAOS is carrying him around the ring, and oh! A sidebreaker and another, and , wow! A big bodyslam! CHAOS showing his power. Terry : Yeah but WATSON showed his smarts avoiding that flying elbow drop, and now WATSON lands an elbow and covers... Tony : Two count only, and CHAOS pressed him off!!!! Johny : Did you see how far WATSON was launched? Terry : I think even he's stunned!...CHAOS is up and Whoa! A quick kick to the midsection and a clobbering single axehandle to the back! CHAOS will have reach as one of his advantages, and that was an example! Now he has WATSON bouding off the ropes, and holy mary mother of jesus! Johny : A one handed press! A one handed press! He drives WATSON down with extreme force! What apower display!..Ut oh, CHAOS is rearing back with his leg, and you know those shinguards are solid steel. Terry : I don't see why HARRINGTON allows him to wear them... Tony : Either way CHAOS shows his agility with a back brain kick that took WATSON's head off. CHAOS quickly grabs him, and he's up! Johny : OOOOOOOHHHHH MYYYYYYY GAAAAAAHHHHHHDDDDD! Terry : He just threw his man with a helicopter spin over the top rope onto the ringsteps! Not nice! Tony : The ref is laying on the count, but WATSON looks broken....The referee's at seven.... Johny : WATSON's still not stirring and it looks like the CRIPPLING CANUCK is going to be counted out... Terry : Yup it's official, CHAOS wins by countout over BRENT WATSON and advances into the next round.... Tony : Can we get a stretcher for BRENT? Johny : Here come the medics... Tony : Let's go to a commercial while they take care of Brent Watson.... The camera fades to black and then to a commercial... The commercial ends and the camera fades to Johny Morehouse in the ring... Johny : Welcome back ladies and gentlemen. Please give a warm WWA Welcome to my guest at this time. He is the reighning WWA World Television Champion. Please welcome, Nick Bruiser!! "The Unforgiven II" by Metallica rumbles the loud speakers. Nick Bruiser walks through the curtains, and stops at the top of the entrance ramp. He's wearing his 'HoP' T-Shirt, and blue jeans. He listens to a mixed reaction of 'boos', 'cheers', and appluads. He then slwoly makes his way to the ring. He enters the ring, and stands right in front of Jonhy. Johny back off a little, and starts the interview. Johny : Welcome Nick. Lets get this thing a rollin'. Nick, in the past couple of days, we have witnessed 'a new you', to make it short. Will this affect your Championship, and how? Bruiser (grabbing the mic): Listen Johny boy, I can conduct my own interview. (kicks Jonhy in the a$$ as he's walking away) That's better. (laughter from the fans) Anyways... Next week. Wednesday Night Slam. Ravage, I challenged you. Why? (shrugs) Two reasons. First reason : I thought you didn't get a fair fight the other night. That I can see. The House of Pain was banned from interfereing in this match. Second reason: I just want one last time to kick the sh*t out of you. (cheers and appluads) Ravage, listen... (climbs out side the ring to the announcers table) ...I don't care what you think of me. This Wednesday, we settle it man to man. I will persoanlly gaurantee you that I will rip you apart! Nick reaches out for the announcer's table, and tips it. Knocking the now sitting Johny to the floor. The table is laying on it's side. Nick stares into the camera, and walks way up the ramp. Tony : Are you ok Johny...? Johny (picking himself up and putting the heaset back on): Yeah... I'm ok... Thanks Tony. Terry (laughing): I am going to like this guy! Tony : Let's go to Gary Trudeau while we fix this table. Gary Trudeau : This next match will be another first round match for the Eastern Region. Introducing first from Murray Kentucky, one half of the Hot House... We have "Krazy" Karl Flood! "The Wiser Times" by the Black Crowes starts over the PA. Karl Flood enters the arena to a mild round of boos. The Krazy one ignores them and enters the ring. Gary Trudeau : And his opponent! Coming to you today from the Haddon Township in New Jersey! We have DUKE DARING! "Black Lung" by Rancid starts over the PA. Duke Daring emerges from the back to a mixed reaction from the crowd. The blue mechanics suit looking very tattered and old as Daring slapps a few hands on the way to the ring. Tony : Well this should be a great match. We have the power of "Krazy" Karl Flood squaring off against the extremist Duke Daring. Terry : I think Flood will crush the daring out of him. (Laughs at his attempted joke.) Johny : The match is underway as the two men lock up in a collar an elbow tie up. Tony : Karl with the strength advantage backs Duke into the corner. The ref calls for the clean break and the two grapplers release. Terry : But, Flood knocks Daring back into the corner with a vicious right hand. And begins opening it up with some kicks to the abdomen of the Dukester. Johny : Irish whip sends Daring slamming into the opposite corner. Terry : That'll really take the steam out of'em. Tony : Karl charges in and slams his chest into Daring. Duke slumps in the corner. Johny : Karl not letting up begins choking the downed Duke with a big boot to the throat. Terry : Choke him until his face turns colors. Tony : The ref warns Flood that he will DQ him. And the Krazy one lets up. Terry : What a wussie. Tony : Karl right back to work begins stomping Duke. He really is in control. Johny : Now he lifts up the winded and woozy Daring. He measures him up. Tony : Throws a wide forearm shot but, Daring ducks it and hits a drop kick. Knocking the gigantic Flood back a few feet. Terry : Duke to the second turnbuckle and comes off with an axe handle to the top of Karls' head. Down goes Flood. Tony : And Daring too. Both men are down as the ref starts the count. Karl shakes his head and roles toward the ropes. Terry : This isn't tag team wrestling, there's no partner to tag too. Johny : Duke up and drops a knee into the lower back of Flood. Tony : Karls' neck is now draped across the bottom rope. And Daring runs off the opposite ropes and drops down across the back Floods' neck. Johny : Daring now just jumping up and down on the neck of Flood. As Karl is being choked on the ropes. Terry : Yeah, choke him! Tony : Duke pulls Karl up by his hair and sends him into the ropes with an Irish whip. He hits Karl cleanly in the jaw with a high spinning heel kick. Terry : I love it when it goes pop! Johny : Duke to the far corner, climbs the ropes. High risk move coming up. Tony : He leaps out into the air and crashes down hitting Karl with a massive elbow drop. Terry : Daring helping Karl to his feet and off the ropes he bounces but, Flood catches him and uses Darings' momentum to hit a beautiful powerslam. Tony : Beautiful, from you. Terry : You're dreaming. Johny : Karl trying to slow the pace of the match down, applies a rear chin lock. Tony : Flood regains his breath and releases the hold. He bounces off the ropes and lands a big elbow drop. Terry : He picks up Daring and hits a standing belly-to-belly suplex. Johny : Off the ropes he goes once again and lands a knee drop across the chest of the fallen Daring. Tony : He picks up Duke and slams him down hard with a spinning side suplex and follows it up with another elbow drop. Johny : He goes for the cover but Daring kicks out at 2. Terry : Karl looks angry, as he brings Daring to his feet via Dukes' hair. Tony : But, Duke picks him up and hits a Manhattan drop. Sending Flood to his back. He follows that up with a head butt to the crotch of Flood. Terry : Flood sits up and bellows in pain, as any man would. Johny : And Daring hits him with an eye gouge. Tony : Daring will do anything to win. He kneels across Floods chest and begins raining down fists. Terry : Flood in a world of trouble. Johny : Daring climbs the ropes once again. But this time Karl stands up and motions for him to bring it on. Tony : And on he comes bowling the behemoth over with a moonsault. Terry : He pulls Flood up and sends him into the corner. Johny : Daring charges the Krazy one but, Karl was waiting for him and meets him with and elbow. Tony : Daring to one knee and what is this? Flood climbs the ropes. He goes to the top. Johny : Duke dropkicks the ropes and down goes Karl crotch first on the top turn buckle. Terry : The fool waited to long. Tony : Duke climbs the ropes. He pulls Flood up and turns him around. Johny : Down they both go as Daring hits The Daring Death Drop. Tony : Duke goes for the cover and gets the 3 count. Terry : I can't believe he won. Tony : It was a hard fought match that could have went either way. But, a mistake by Flood and Daring was able to capitalize on it. Johny : There you have it folks... Duke Daring has make it past the first round. The screen fades to black... Then you here a voice over... Voice Over : The following announcement has been paid for by the House of Pain. The chorus to "Hey Man, Nice Shot" by Filter starts over the TV. Then the screen opens to Randy Cross and the Outlaw. Randy : You know what really gets me... This f*(king Denrol guy! Who does he think he is? Outlaw : No kidding! We help the little puke out and give him the US Title. Then he tries to take our TV Title away! (Shaking his head) Unbelieveable. Randy : Denrol... Must I remind you of all who have fell before us! Must I give you a history lesson? You are playing with the wrong group of guys here sweetheart. Outlaw : Denrol you will pay... As god as my witness, you will pay. The camera fades to black. Then "Hey Man, Nice Shot" starts up again as the letters form in the screen.... FEAR US! FOR WE COME FOR YOU! The music stops and you hear the voice over... Voice Over : The preceding announcement has been paid for by the House of Pain. The screen opens to the arena, to the ring and Gary Trudeau. Gary Trudeau : The next match of the evening will be another first round match. Already in the ring at this time... We have Mr Internet! And his opponent from Hell, Ohio, he stands at 6'4" and weighs in at 291 pounds, this is Carl "100% Pain" Goldstar!! "Hard Knock Life" by Jay Z plays over the PA as Carl walks out. He's wearing a strange trenchcoat covered with objects. He also wears a "I'm Special" hat. Elena walks beside him, wearing a short little nurse outfit, getting some whistles from fans. Carl has trouble getting to the ring, he keeps bouncing off the guard rails. He makes into the ring and Elena helps him undress. Tony : Due to the most unfortunate situation with Jak Lancer, Mr Internet has been placed in the tourney as the third seed in the East! Terry : Just goes to show you that the EAST SUCKS! Johny : Mr.Internet isn't sure what to do with this nut. He watchs in almost a morbid curiosty from the other corner. Tony : Elena has grabed a chair from ringside. She swings and hits Carl Goldstar with it. What the hell is going on here?? Terry : Goldstar is smiling like a complete idiot at that chair shot. It's got him psyched for this match. Totally unbelieveable. Johny : Bell rings and Goldstar charges at Mr.Internet, starts a series of punchs to the man's face. And he chuckles loudly every time his hand smacks off Mr. Internet's face. Tony : That's just sick. The ref warns Goldstar to open the fists. Carl nods and grabs Mr.Internet in a, umm balls grip. Terry : Goldstar follows that up with a hiptoss. These moron fans actully sortof, are cheering all this. Now a legdrop across the neck. Johny : Mr.Internet risies up and Goldstar headbutts him repeatly in the midsection. Now he whips Mr.Internet into the ropes. Tony : Leapfrog by Mr.Internet, but Carl jumped up and headbutted Mr.Internet's groin area, sending him down. Terry : Jesus, he's bitting Mr.Internet's nose. The ref is really getting upset here by Goldstar's illegal actions. Goldstar stops the bite. Johny : My lord, Mr.Internet is bleeding. Now a Ttongan death grip by Goldstar. Mr.Internet smartly puts his foot on the ropes. Tony : Goldstar seems upset at this. Now he's got a Tongan death grip on the ref!!! The ref goes down to the move. Terry : Ref is out. And Mr.Internet is up. Full nelson Sulpex by Mr.Internet. He picks up and DDTs Goldstar. Good comeback. Johny : Now a sleeper by Mr.Internet. Goldstar is going out. Elena runs in with that chair. She just smacked Goldstar with it! Tony : It woke Goldstar up. Goldstar breaks the move and hiptosses Mr.Internet. Now he locks on a crossface chickenwing. Terry : He calls this move the Scream of Defeat. And Mr.Internet is certainly screaming. The dazed ref rings the bell. Johny : Carl "100% Pain" Goldstar wins by submission and advances in the WWA Tourney. Too bad he didn't face Lancer. Tony : That would have been insane. Let's go to these commercials... The screen fades to black and opens to a commercial... The commercial ends and opens to the arena and Gary Trudeau in the ring. Gary Trudeau : The next match of the evening will be set for one fall... Introducing first, coming to the ring, we have "Immaculate" Nick Jett! Nick Jett and the Executive enter the arena to the minor boos of the crowd. The two men enter the ring and await Jett's opponent. Gary Trudeau : And his opponent, coming to us from the Haddon Township in New Jersey! We have representing the House of Pain, Mantis! "Great Milenko" by Insane Clown Posse starts over the PA. Mantis emerges from the back and is met with some boos, but get a few cheers from the people that made the commute to NY from NJ. Mantis slaps some hands and jumps over the top rope and the Executive exits the ring. Tony : Looks like we have a good match up here tonight on Friday Night Fights with Mantis vs. Nick Jett. Johny : Jett, although smaller, is a ground techinicain while Mantis is bigger and a high flyer. Terry : Mantis Rules. He can beat anyone in the WWA. Tony : Whatever Terry. Mantis starts off the match with a hip toss, and he takes Jett to the ground. Johny : Jett is back up in a flash and launches a huge right hand at Mantis, but Mantis easily sidesteps the punch and lands a kidney shot. Terry : Mantis is God. Mantis whips his opponent into the ropes and hits a huge flying clothesline. Tony : Mantis grabs Jett by the hair and hauls Jett to his feet. Mantis whips Jett again across the ropes, but this time Jett scissors Mantis's legs, and Mantis hits the deck. Johny : Jett is up in a flash and starts hitting Mantis with a barage of boots to the head and back to keep Mantis grounded. Tony : Great strategy considering Mantis likes going up top. Terry : What a fairy? Can't Jett take Mantis at his own game? Tony : Anyways, Jett has now slapped on an ankle lock submission hold on Mantis. Apparently he is trying to keep him grounded. Johny : Doesn't really matter because before Jett can really crank on Mantis's ankle, Mantis grabs a hold of the rope and breaks the hold. Terry : Mantis is up now, but is limping a little. He is okay. Mantis is the man. Mantis goes for a nice roundhouse kick... Tony : Blocked by Jett. Jett while still holding one of Mantis's legs, sweeps the other one out from under Mantis. Jett really knows his ground techniques. Johny : Jett is immediately on top of Mantis, and he is pounding away at Mantis with his fists. Jett pulls Mantis to his feet and whips him across the ropes. Terry : Mantis with a huge hurricanranna. Mantis cannot be stopped. He is like Superman. Tony : Sure thing Terry. Mantis is now in control as he whips Jett into the ropes, and sends him to the mat with a spinning heel kick. Johny : Mantis climbs the ropes and nails a nice moonsault. He hops back up and nails a huge frog splash on Jett. Terry : Mantis is really giving it to Jett, as Jett can barely walk. Mantis hits Jett with a running clothesline. Tony : Jett bounces right back up, and with a second wind hits Mantis with a clothesline of his own. Right when Mantis hits the mat, Jett has applied a another ankle lock submission hold. Johny : Mantis once again grabs the ropes and stops the count. When is Jett going to learn that you have to lock it up in the center. Terry : Hopefully never. GO GET HIM MANTIS. Tony : I think I am going to scream. Mantis whips Jett into the rops and flips him over his back and out of the ring. Johny : Mantis hits a corkscrew body block from over the top rope and nails Jett. Jett is down from that one. Mantis roles Jett into the ring, and foloows himself. Terry : Mantis whips the dazed Jett into the ropes and executes a scoop slam. Mantis now hits a standing piledriver. Mantis is whooping this little punk. Tony : This match is all but over as Mantis locks up Jett for the Shocker. A Fisherman's Suplex from the second rope. Terry : Shocker by Mantis....1,2,3. Mantis with the win. Tony : Mantis will move on in this March Madness Tournament..... Terry : A great win for the House of Pain. Big surprise there! Johny : This next match tonight will put one half of the New World Tag Champions against the likes of Misery... Tony : This should be a relatively good match Johny. Terry : They both suck! But hey! At least Payne beat those incipid punks in the Youthless Rage! Tony : Let's go to ringside! Gary Trudeau : The next match of the evening will be set for one fall. Introducing first coming to the ring at this time, we have the Misery! "Dead Bodys Everywhere" by Korn starts over the PA. Misery enters to a relatively no responce.
