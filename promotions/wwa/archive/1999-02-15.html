Mack walks backstage briskly looking for a competitor. All is quiet for a few moments in the arena. Then.......New Star's music starts to play over the speakers, but no one comes out. Suddenly a hard rock version of "Saturday Night's Alright" begins to play. Sniper walks out, wearing blue jeans and cowboy boots. He walks to the ring as the fans cheer loudly. He breaks into a run, picks up the ladder and heaves it into the ring and onto Outlaw. Eric : Good lord, this match could already be over! John : Outlaw's not moving at all as Sniper enters the ring. He picks up the ladder.... Juan : Outlaw was playing possum. He kicks the ladder into the chest of Sniper. Outlaw on the ropes and nails a legdrop on the ladder, that is laying on Sniper. Eric : Sniper's up quickly and starts throwing punches to the upper chest of Outlaw. Outlaw's throwing a lot of punches of his own, but Sniper is unaffected by them. His boxing skills are really showing here. Juan : Sniper with a swinging neckbreaker that lands on top of the ladder. Sniper takes advantage and sets the ladder up under the belt while Outlaw is down. John : Sniper goes back to Outlaw and delivers multiple elbow drops. Sniper up to the second rope and goes for a flying elbow drop..... Eric : Outlaw jumps out of the way. Outlaw up now and hits an effective backbreaker. Juan : Sniper pops up and deliver a kick but....Outlaw catches the leg and hits a fisherman's suplex. Now Outlaw goes for a Northern Lights suplex, and gets it. John : Outlaw's climbing the ladder, count this one over... Eric : Oh, I wouldn't do that so soon, Sniper up and pulls Outlaw's leg, bringing him down to the mat hard. John : Sniper now stomping away on the leg of Outlaw. Outlaw's leg really looks to be in bad shape. He's going to have a tough time climbing that ladder. Juan : Outlaw unexpectantly hops and oh my, a front face ddt....how did he manage to pull that off? Eric : Outlaw follows it up with a short arm clothesline. Outlaw starts climbing the ladder again. He doesn't see Sniper...Oh my!! John : Sniper pushes the ladder over and it lands in the ropes and catapults Outlaw the other way. This one could be over! Who just hopped the guardrail..... Juan : It's Jak Lancer!!! Lancer's here! Lancer approaches the nearly unconcious Outlaw but Sniper doesn't look pleased. Eric : Sniper just slid out of the ring in front of Lancer. Sniper wants a fair match up. Lancer tries to walk by but Sniper stops him. John : Bad decision on Sniper's part, Lancer just hit with the brass knuckles and Sniper hits the ground in a heap! Lancer's out of here, he just hopped back over the guardrail. Juan : Outlaw's starting to stir. He didn't even see what happened. He slides out of the ring and easily puts the Outlaw Saddle on Sniper. Sniper's not moving. Eric : Outlaw shrugs and leaves Sniper and goes to the ladder in the middle of the ring. He positions the ladder upright under the belt again. John : Outlaw slowly but surely making his way up the ladder, Sniper's starting to stir, he looks up and sees Outlaw. Eric : Outlaw's on the last step of the ladder, Sniper slides back into the ring, Outlaw grabs the belt, it's over!!! Outlaw maintains his title! Juan : Sniper looks enraged! Eric : We're out of time....we'll see you next week! John : And remember, if you're not mean, you're not extreme!!!
