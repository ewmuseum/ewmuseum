WWA Monday Night Meltdown - Wednesday 13th March 2002 The camera sees Jackson Kraven sitting in his dressing room, stripped to the waist, his eyes hidden by wrap around sunglasses. A copy of WWA Magazine is in his hands, open to an article about "TRENT LEBEAUX: LIVING LEGEND?" Kraven looks up at the camera and without speaking tears the magazine in half before breaking into a smile. Kraven: Don't try this at home, kids. We hit the Meltdown title sequence. "Alive" by Godsmack plays as the highlights roll! WEDNESDAY Night Meltdown LIVE From Boulder CO Wednesday 13th March 2002 We open inside the arena as Godsmack and video package blasts over the PA.
