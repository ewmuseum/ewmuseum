What this is Roster bio card for the WWA wrestler Lockdown, giving measurements, birth date, hometown, theme, appearance, ring attire, personality, history, awards, moveset and finisher.
Basic Information Nicknames Real Name Height | 6'4" Weight | 280 lbs Date of Birth | 12/04/75 Home Town | Chicago, Illinois Theme | No Remorse by Metallica More Details Appearance | Lockdown is extremely muscular, and very cut. His chest and arms are gigantic, his neck is thick and his abs are ripped. Lockdown has a tough square jaw, but not to the point that he looks like a neanderthal. On his upper right arm, just a few inches below his shoulder, he has a tattoo that reads,"CFH." in the center of his back is a very large tribal tattoo. These tatoos are proffessionally done, but on the left side of his neck he bears one piece of prison ink: side by side twin lightning bolts. Lockdown sports a blood red mohawk that stands about 1 1/2 inches high. Lockdown almost always has dark 5 o'clock shadow. His eyes are blue and grim. Ring Attire | light blue collared button up short sleeve shirt with a whie wife beater undershirt. On the breast pocket his prisoner ID number, 286333, is stamped below the words,"IL Dept of Correction." The back of the shirt reads, "Illinois Dept of Correction." Dark blue jeans with a 1 3/4 inch wide white stripe running down the side of each leg. Written in dark blue down each stripe are the words, IL Dept of Correction. The bottom of the jeans are cuffed. On his feet, Lockdown wears highly polished black combat boots. Lockdown has also been known to wrestle in just his undershirt, or sometimes, no shirt at all. Personality | Lockdown cares for very few besides himself. He has a talent for using others to achieve his goals. However, once his friendship is earned he is fiercely loyal. Lockdown is supremely confidant, bordering on arrogant. He would much rather jump head first into a hopeless battle than run from a fight. One of the most amazing things about Lockdown is his inhuman tolerance for pain. History | A bad seed from birth, Lockdown was born to a female convict in an Illinois women's prison. His father was a career criminal who was executed at the very hour of Lockdown's birth. His youth was spent in one group home, or juvenile detention center after another. He soon found that the only way for a kid like him to get what he wanted was to take it. When he was 16 he was tried as an adult for crimes that became increasingly brutal. He was convicted and sent to Joliet State Prison. There he found brutality was the key to survival. Lockdown did not just survive, but thrive. After murdering several prisoners and guards, his reputation spread throughout the entire prison system in Illinois. In an effort to channel some of the most problematic convicts' rage into a semi-controllable venue, newly elected governor, Goerge Ryan, instituted his latest pet project: The Prison Wrestling Program. (PWP) For the first time in his Life, Lockdown had drive and focus. Never before had he set and acheived goals. In no time he became Champion, and his bloodlust helped him become the winningest Champion the program has seen to this day. Lockdown decimated every opponent he faced, even killing a few men in the ring. He spent much less time hanging out with the hooligans on the yard and much more time in the weight room. He also devoured every book he could get his hands on that might make him a better strategist. Finally aided by his new attitude, and help from Governor Ryan, Lockdown was granted parole. Governor Ryan had arranged a tryout with the now defunct EWA. Lockdown made the cut and quickly became their Extreme Champion. When the EWA folded, Lockdown soon returned to his old tricks, and wound up violating the terms of his parole. Fearing his beloved PWP would recieve bad press as a failure, Governor Ryan urged Lockdown to submit an application to the WWA, and promised Lockdown if he made it, that the governor would commute his sentence in return for a favor to be named later. Lockdown made the cut and the rest is history in the making. Alignment | Heel Manager Allies Rivals Awards | WWA tag team champion. (along with his partner, Thomas "Leviathan" Connors.)
