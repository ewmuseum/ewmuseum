WWA — 7 Nov 1998

7 Nov 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Saturday November 7, 1998 WORLD WRESTLING ALLIANCE... The Ultimate in Wrestling Entertainment! The Banner fades... then the screen shows Madison Square Garden. The scene then flashes to the inside of the Madison Square Garden. The lights are off in the aena and a lone spotlight lights up the ring. In the ring stands a podium. Standing behind the podium is the WWA Vice President Stan "The Action Man" Jackson. He is dressed in a tuxedo... He clears his throat and appears ready to begin. Stan Jackson : Ladies and Gentlemen... Welcome to the first annual WWAmmies awards show. Tonight we will celebrate to one year birthday of the WWA. I trust that you all enjoyed your dinners this evening... The spectators start to comment amongst themselves about the wonderful meal ealier. The lights have since been raised up to show the audience. The camera swings around to show them, among them are most of the WWA superstars, icluded are Randy Cross, the Outlaw and the House of Pain; New Star, Anarchy and the the Star Station X; and the former Brotherhood. All dressed in their "nice" clothes, be it a tuxedo or a suit. The camera moves back to Stan Jackson standing in the ring behind the podium. Stan Jackson : Outstanding... Well without further ado... Let's get on with the show. Stan looks down and picks up an envolope. And looks back at the camera and smiles... Stan Jackson : The first award of the evening with be the Most Improved wrestler of the year. Lets look at the three nominees for the WWAmmy. Stan Jackson turns to the Jumbotron and the large screen bursts into action showing Jack coming through the curtains at an arena. The sounds of thunder and hoofbeats accompanied by classical music led by a harpsichord follow him. Dressed all in black and wearing a gold "Famine" mask, he ignores the mixed crowd reaction while he approaches the steel encased ring. The screen then flashes to Jack executing a FamineSlam on Steihl. Then Jack rolls on top for the cover and the three count. Jack rolls out of the ring and grabs the US Title as the House of Pain roll into the ring. Then the screen fades to black and the camera finds Stan again in the ring. He turns to the audience... Stan : The first nominee is Jack... Famine of the Four Horsemen of the Apocalypse. He started off on his own in the beginning of his career. Eventually he was taking in by Darken and Steihl and was one of the Original members of the Four Horsemen of the Apocalypse. From that point on, his career took off with his winning of the TV Title and then on to the Gaining of the US Title which he currently holds.... And now for the second nominee... The Jumbotron comes to life again... "Stayin Alive" starts over the PA. The screen shows the MidCarder entering an arena. The Midcarder shuffles a little as the fans start the patented dance along with the Midcarder. MidCarder jumps into the ring and joins the fans in the little dance. The screen flashes to MidCarder and Vinnie Bomber on the top rope. MidCarder climbs the ropes and executes a top rope Hose Head (Brainbuster). The Midcarder gets up and dances for the crowd who are going wild. He gives the sign and executes the "Average Man Slam". He then moves in for the cover getting the three count. The screen fades to black and the camera moves back to Stan Jackson. Stan : And the MidCarder is the second nominee for the Most Improved. He started as the Hoser... And eventually changed his name to the MidCarder, which preempted an impressive winning streak. And now for the third nominee for the WAAmmy. The big Screen fires up again. This time, "Feed My Frankestein" by Alice Cooper starts over the PA. Mad Coil and the Death Knight enter the arena to a largely negative responce. The screen flashes to Mad Coil with Gutter up in the Avalanche... Gutter gets hit with the inverse Jackknife Powerbomb. Mad Coil goes for the cover and the win. Stan : And the final nominee is Mad Coil. Mad Coil started his career with the Warriors Pride. Eventually he turned his back and joined the House of Pain, definately helping his career. And the winner for the Most Imporoved Wrestler if the Year WWAmmy goes to... JACK! Hard Rock guitar riffs blare over the PA system. The crowd claps as the Jumbotron shows Jack entering an arena with the US belt over his shoulder and Darken walking next to him... The screen fades to black. Stan : Unforetunately Jack is conveleasing in the Hospital right now, so he will be unable to accept this award here tonight. However... He taped this this morning... The scene fades in to Jack's hospital room, where Jack sits up at about a 70 degree angle. Jack II is beside him on the right, and Darken, Raven Crow, and the blurred image of War is on the left. The WWA U.S. Title is drapped across his stomach, and he holds the golden figurine of the man powerbombing another man, the WWAmmy, in his right hand. Jack II looks wearily at the camera, past the camera. Jack looks at the camera. Jack : I have won a WWAmmy. For Most Improved Wrestler. Wow. Of course, I couldn't be at the ceremony tonight... Instead, I'm coming to you through this taped message from my d--- hospital bed! Thanks to a few men that sit in that crowd. I want them to know that my vengance pumps through my veins... But on a lighter note, I would like to thank all who voted for me for this award. Why do you think I have this title? It means that I am a champion, second only to the dispicable Randy Cross. I DESERVE an award. And I finally got one. Now, to close with a warning: Outlaw, Todd Logan, Mr. Business, and Once Steihl;you are all four locked into my crosshairs. Watch your backs..." With that, the camera fades to black. Stan : Well... Let me introduce an up and coming wrestling making quite a name for himself. He will be awarding the WWAmmy for Best Fued! Ladies and Gentlemen... here is El Porto Spidero! "Ratamahatta" by Sepultura starts over the PA. El Porto Spidero walks up the step to the ring and then flips into the ring. The crowd cheers for the youngster from Mexico. He walks up to the podium obviously very excited to be here and to be part of this. El Porto Spidero : WOW! Thank you everyone. I can't believe that I am here.... (Takes a deep breath). Ok.. I was so surprised when Mr. Harrington asked me to give an award tonight. It is both an honor and my pleasure. So.. The first nominee for best Fued is... The Jumbotron starts up to Randy Cross with Darken in the figure four. Steihl is getting beaten down by the other three House of Pain members the Harvester of Sorrow and the Crocketts. Darken reaches up and puts Cross in a choke hold. The Harester of Sorrow picks up a chair and slams it into the back of Darken, who barely fliches. The screen flashes to Steihl flying through the ropes nailing Cross on the outside of the ring. The Harvester of Sorrow weilding a chair nails Steihl to the ground. Inside the ring the rest of the House of Pain start to beat on Darken. Tiburon and Talon enter the ring and join the the House of Pain in the beating. The screen flashes to the Overlords with there staves in the ring. Darken swings at Aric and Zack as they bail out of the ring. Steihl nails the Harvester in the head, splashing blood all over... The rest of the House of Pain flee the ringside area. Darken and Steihl just hold up their staves as the crowd cheers. The screen fades to black and the camera finds El Porto Spidero. El Porto Spidero : Wow! That was pretty brutal. The Overlords House of Pain stared with the start of NCW. The House of Pain only consisted of the Crocketts, Randy Cross and the Harvester of Sorrow at this time. Eventualy, the House of Pain became stronger with the addition of Da Killer Connection: Deadbolt, Tiburon and Talon. Eventually the House of Pain after grabbing talent like Todd Logan and the Outlaw, ended the fued with the Overlords by convincing Steihl himself to join their ranks, which lost them the Northestern Belts. A truely great fued. Let's look at another fued... The jumbotron starts up to show Randy Cross with the figure four on Johnny Blaze. Aric Crockett grabs Randy's arms for extra leverage. The ref looks up and aric lets go... Blaze turns the figure four over and Cross is the one in pain. Cross releases the hold. The screen flashes to Cross, Blaze and the ref on the mat. Blaze is busted open as Cross begins to stir. Micheal Blackheart throws Zack Crockett out of the ring to the right of the "in ring" combatants. Cross begins to crawl over to Blaze. The ref at this point starts to come too... Cross drapes his arm over Blaze and the ref counts the three count. The screen then flashes to Blaze and Cross inside a cage. Blaze is climbing up to the top rope. He jumps off and executes a double moonsault onto Cross. And then he hooks the leg and gets the three count. The screen fades to black and the camera moves to El Porto Spidero. El Porto Spidero : Randy Cross against Johnny Blaze. Truely a good fued. I am just sorry I was not here to enjoy the matches those two had. Johnny Blaze was one of the few men to ever beat Cross. The fued ultimately ended with the retirement of the great Johnny Blaze. And now the final nominee. The Jumbotron again shows Randy Cross with the figure four locked on an opponent. This time it is Nikodemous Segier. Segier turns the figure four over. Then the screen flashes and shows Segier blooded trying to hook in the Segier Stretch. Randy nails him with a low blow. Then executing a Brainbuster in the corner. Cross getting the 3 count. The screen flashes to Segier being thrown into a wall cutting his arm. Cross gets Segier up for a back suplex and Segier escapes out and lands on his feet. Then gets Cross in a German suplex on the concrete. Cross is bleeding from the back of his head as the referee goes down for the three count. Segier falls to the ground as medics rush to attend to him. The screen fades to black. El Porto Spidero : That last match was truley one of the best the WWA had ever seen. This fued all started when Randy Cross took Segier's figure four from him. With an assist from the Outlaw I MIGHT ADD! A truley cowardly act. Then Cross managed to get a victory over Segier in a Falls count anywhere match. Fianlly Segier gets another shot at the March Madness Pay Per View also with the falls count anywhere rules. Segier with the win, proving he is the best wrestler in the WWA! This fued never truely ended... It could be said that it manifested into a Segier versus the House of Pain. Well. Without futher ado the winners of the Greatest fued are..... Randy Cross and Nik Segier! The screen splits into two parts. One showing Nikodemous Segier with a big smile on his face as he kisses Jennifer. Nik is wearing a charcoal Blazer and a white turtleneck shirt along with a pair of Blue jeans. He stands and starts to walk towards the ring. The other screen shows Randy Cross kissing his wife Dawn as he stands up straighening his tuxedo. He gives a wink to her and turns to the ring and approaches the ring. As the two men get close to the ring the screen merges into one. Both men eye each other... Randy motions for Segier to enter the ring first. Segier shakes his head mentioning something about "Age before beauty". Randy smiles and gives a little half bow and crawls through the ropes. Then Segier enters the ring quickly, smartly not trusting Cross. The two men stand about 5 feet from each other as Segier motions for Randy to speak at the podium. In the background El Porto Spidero comes up to Segier and shakes his hand. Randy Cross : Thanks everyone for the votes. I know that Nik and I have had our differences in the past. However, we put on one hell of a show! Nik and I gave you some of the best matches this business has ever seen. But then I look to the rest of the nominees... I see House of Pain or Randy Cross next to all of them. I even look back and think of all of the great fueds: Regulators/House of Pain, Executives Club/House of Pain, Unified Knights of America/House of Pain, Four Horseman/House of Pain... Do you see a trend. I will leave you with that... Randy smiles and takes a step back and motions for Segier to take it away. Segier steps up with his head cocked still watching Cross. Segier smiles to the audience and begins to speak... Nik Segier : Thank you, my adoring fans. Thank you. I knew you loved me and now I have proof. Well, I have some bad news for you, but news that should put a smile on the face of all my competition. You see, Nikodemous Segier has become a household name, more so than that. I have become one of the most popular wrestlers in history, and as such, I have been asked, and even begged, by many federations who wish my services. I have been loyal to the WWA for a long time now, and I think it is time Nikodemous moved to a new level. You see, I have been offered millions of dollars in signing bonuses as well as upwards of $20,000 a match. Even with the new payraise the WWA has offered, it can not compete with that, and to be honest, it can not afford me. Since it is my last night as a wrestler in the WWA, I would like to challenge my opponent, the Outlaw, to a real wrestling match. It seems that with all the skill this federation has trapped in it, it all believes in numbers, not skill. All of those of you who once were real men and wrestlers have turned into men who can not walk alone. Outlaw, you once walked alone, and I would like to leave the WWA in a matchup which is fair for us both. I wish to thank you all once again, and wish the WWA wrestlers the best of luck in finding their skills once again. Just because I am better than you all does not mean you are bad. Good luck, and see you in the ring, Outlaw. Nikodemous smiles again at the audience and leaves the ring. Randy follows Segier out of the ring and the two men take their seats. El Porto Spidero is standing in the ring looking like he just lost his best friend. Stan Jackson comes up behind him and whispers something in his ear. El Porto Spidero seems to not hear the Vp and just wanders to the ropes and exits the ring. Stan Jackson smiles and shakes his head. Stan Jackson : Ok... Nikodemous Segier... Thank you for your professionalism in your announcement. I am sure I speak for everyone in the WWA, when I say you will be missed... Stan Jackson starts to clap, leading the attending audience in a round of applause for Nikodemous Segier. The applause dies down and Stan Jackson step sback up to the mic. Stan Jackson : Lets keep this cerimony going... Please welcome Mantis! Awarding the Tag Team of the Year... "Great Milenko" by Insane Clown Posse starts over the PA. Mantis is not dressed up at all. He is wearing all black as the F.E.A.R. wears now... He doesn't look real pleased with being here. Mantis : This is complete crap Harrington. I don't know why I agreed to do this stuff for you. What have you done for me.....this is ridiculous, but I will give the award out just for the fans. And only for the fans. Anyways.. Now that the pleasantries are out of the way. Let's go to the first nominee for the Best Tag Team of the year. The jumbotron jumps into action showing the Overlords of the Dark walking through a set of curtains. "Sad but True" by Metallica is playing... The duo walk to the ring as Yarbleck Essuvius Suvanti runs out behind them. The screen flashes to Darken executing the Dark Pogostick on an Unknown wrestler. And the referee counting the three count. Then Darken and Steihl are awarded the CWC National Tag Team championship. The screen flashes to Darken executing a spinebuster slam on Tiburon then tagging in Steihl. Then the duo execute the "Touch of Death". Steihl goes for the cover and the three count. The screen fades to black. Mantis : Well the Overlords of the Dark! A truely great tag team. They started off as the first CWC Natiinal Champions.. Which is truely remarkable, considering that there was three other federations involved in the CWC. ( Mantis gets a weitd look on his face.) Who writes this crap...? Anyways.... Then the Overlords beat Da Killer Connection for the Northeastern titles... Let's move to the next nominees... The jumbotron starts up showing Mantis and ChiKylin walking out to the "Great Milenko". The fans are cheering and the duo slap hands on their way to the ring.. The screen flashes to ChiKylin running at Mantis and Blood Cross. Mantis to all fours and ChiKylin hits a Thai kick sending Blood Cross to the mat. Mantis up to the top and Chi lifts Blood Cross up to a squating position. Mantis nails Blood Cross with a flying clothesline. ChiKylin covers and the three count. The screen fades to black. Mantis : Now THERE is a GREAT tag team. The picture says it all... Let's go to the next loser... The screen opens up to show Raiden and Chaos entering an arena with Havok close behind. "The Small Hours" by Danzig plays as the trio get boos to the ring. The screen changes to Raiden executing his Whiplash Diamond Bomb on Sgt Payne. Then going for the cover and getting the three count. The referee awarding them the Northeast tag belts. The screen fades to black. Mantis (yawning): The Anarchy... Another one of the great tag teams in the WWA. They beat the Commandant's Own and The Minnesota Wrecking Crew in a three way tag match to gain the victory and become the Northeastern Champs. Then the NCW became WWA, the Anarchy were then the WWA World Tag Champs. And the winners of Tag Team of the Year... (Mantis gets a look of disgust on his face). The Overlords of the Dark... "Sad but True" by Metallica plays in the air as Darken walks out to claim the trophy. Steihl rushes up and tries to push past him, only to be thoroghly THUMPED with a elbow shot to the back of the neck. Both men then make the way to the ring as best they could. Darken : This award would have meant a lot to me if people were who we thought them. Steihl : Yeah, but it means something to me, because I earned it. I spent months in the tag ranks with a partner like this and I still managed to get the titles for us. Now, of course I am back with Scorpian, and we're going to kick some major ass all around the WWA. Darken : Shut up, ignorant one. You did not further the tag team alone, for I founded it and it was I who showed you how to fight. Steihl : Yeah, right. I learned how to fly from a grounded freak. Give me that trophy because it's mine as I earned it. Darken : You want the trophy? Steihl : I EARNED the trophy. Darken : Then have it. Steihl : Of cou- Darken thrusts the base of the statue into Steihl's face, knocking him down. Steihl tries to scramble out of the area, but Darken pounces on him. As the fight finally begins, Darken punching and Steihl throwing back, security rushes out, as well as the House of Pain and half the fed. HOP guides Steihl out with lines of "Save it for later" while Darken is led out with lines of "Kill him on your own time". After the ruckass is cleared and the former tag teammates are seperated the camera finds Stan Jackson with his hands on his hips with a concerned look on his face. The House of Pain back in there seats and the Four Horsemen leaving with their leader Stan Jackson speaks.... Stan : I am truely sorry for that ladies and gentlemen. I apoligize from the bottom of my heart. Now... Let us continue with the awards cerimony. Since Darken was going to award Rookie of the Year... And he is currently indeposed.. I will take the honors... The first nominee is.... The jumbotron flares to life and shows Gregory O'Hara walking out of the curtains. Vixen by his side, she just flips a couple fans off on the way to the ring as "Fuel" by Metallica starts over the PA. O'Hara gets into the ring as lets out his patented banshee cry and then green pyrotechnics shoot out of the ringposts. The screen flashes to O'Hara hitting the "Shamrock" knocking the MidCarder to the mat. He gives the sign for his finisher and climbs up the ropes. He jumps and hits the "Leprechaun Leap" and then getting the pin. The screen fades to black... Stan : "The Irish Assassin" Gregory o'Hara is nominee number one for the Rookie of the Year. He started off strong and kept it going as he is undeafeated in his professional career.. A truley remarkable feat considering the talent level that the WWA posses. And now for the second nominee... The jumbotron starts up and shows New Star coming out from behind the curtain. "Chi" by Korn blankets the WWA arena in noise. New Star with a slight swagger and runs a hand through his blond crew cut and walks to the ring. The screen flashes to New Star hitting a neckbreaker on Sonic. New Star quickly to the top rope and executes his New Star Press. Then he picks Sonic up and hits the Nova Bomb. He hooks the leg and the three count. The screen fades to black. Stan : New Star is the second nominee for the Rookie of the Year. New Star busted onto the scene not to long ago boosting to be the "Future" of the WWA. After seeing him in action, he could quite possibly be right! And on to the final nominee for the Rookie of the Year... The jumbotron shows Red and Blue spotlights racing around an arena. "More Human than Human" by White Zombie starts over the PA. A voice can be heard... "Ladies and gentlemen, introducing...from Hollywood, California, the Toast of the Coast, and multiple academy award winner...the One...THE ONLY!!! HOOOOOOLLYWOOOOOD JOHNNYYYYYY TAAAAAMBOOOO!!!" Suddenly a single beam of gold light focuses on the entranceway. The curtains part and Jennifer steps out, wearing a skin tight leopard skin outfit. She is holding the clapboard and steps right up to the camera. She says something and then claps the board. Tambo steps out from behind the curtain to a blast of fireworks. He is wearing his black robe with "Hollywood" written on the back. He and Jen walk arm in arm down to the ring and Jen holds the ropes open for him. The screen flashes to Tambo and Logan in the ring. Logan hits Tambo with a low blow and then slams his head into the mat. Jen jumps up on the apron yelling at the ref. Tambo reaches up and grabs the clapboard. He nails Logan with it. And then locks in a fisherman's suplex getting the three count. The screen fades to black. Stan : And finally "Hollywood" Johnny Tambo as the third nominee. Johnny Tambo broke onto the scene fast and furious as he quickly rose to stardom in the WWA. He took the WWA TV Title from Todd "The Irish Hammer" Logan after being in the fed for less than two months. However, tragedy struck as he announced his retirment only a week ago. Anyways... The Winner of the WAAmny for Rookie of the Year is... NEW STAR! "Chi" by Korn plays as most of the people in attendance boo. New Star rises, decked out in the uniform of a German SS Commandant. David Briceland rises, wearing an Emporer Hirohito getup while Misery resembles Mussolini. It is suddenly appaerent New Star has a black square moustache painted on. The crowd boos louder as he raises his hands in a "I LOVE YOU" hand sign, then realizing the error, turns it into a fascist salute. SS-X are all in attendance, with Havoc and The Anarchy dresses as their usual selfs. They all raise their hands with New Star, Misery and Briceland in the fascist salute. New Star : Well everyone is supposed to start with a joke, but all I could think of was NIKODEMOUS SEIGER..... (Crowd boos)..So on that thought I have a different joke. What does the universe and the WB have in coming at their beginning?......All black and no stars. (The crowd boos and a fan has to be withheld by security.) Now we know there's a man in WWA who appreciates a good role model...Veitz Heil!....And he has great taste in 40's clothes. Pal, we even bought the Rammstein CD because of you. If you want membership in SS-X, it's now your chance to accept. New Star holds up the microphone as "Du Hast" by Rammstein starts over the PA. The music starts to get lower and a voice over can be heard.... Sie nachahmer das, das Sie nicht verstehen. Sie auch zahlen. Then in a very strong balto-slavic accent these words can be heard... I accept your offer.... New Star : How nice... Somehow I knew you would. Well all I must really say is it's your pleasure to give me this honor, and since you've taken the time (all SS-X are readjusting their belts)...to ackowledge those who are strung, hung, and good with their tongue...(you see HAVOC in the backround arch an eyebrow).. we'd like to have you kiss our ...... ALL SS-X bend over, and moon the crowd.... ALL SS-X : ASS! The crowd gasps in horror and amsuement as SS-X have yet defiled the awards show. "Chi" by Korn plays some more, and SS-X take their leave, literally. Then Stan Jackson takes his place behind the podium with a sour look on his face. Stan : Again folks... I apoligize! Well, let me introduce another wrestling great. He will be awarding the WWAmmy for Wrestler of the Year! Along with a few non official awards... Ladies and Gentlemen... here is Shaman! "Devil Man" by White Zombie starts over the PA. Shaman enters the ring from the audience and takes his place behind the podium. He smiles and waits for the applause to die down. Shaman (With a smile on his face.): Thank you ladies and gentlemen. Thanks alot. I was honored when President Harrington asked me to give this award out. But first... I have a few awards of my own to give out. And since these are purely for fun, don't walk up here... First of all... Most full of himself: Nik Segier Biggest Blowhard: New Star Largest Mouth: Randy Cross Lamest stable: Star Station X Stick a fork in him hes done award: Gutter The crowd laughs at most of these... Shaman has a large grin on his face... Shaman : So enough of me babbling... Lets get right into the nominee's for the Wrestler of the Year... The Jumbotron jumps to life with, CAUSE I TOLD YOU ONCE YOU SON OF A BITCH! I'M THE BEST THERE'S EVER BEEN!! A very hard gutair riff is heard over the speakers... The screen shows Flynn Steele walking out with Black Lotus next to him. The screen flashes to Flynn Steele executing a Steele Driver on Fire Storm. He drops down and gets the win. The screen flashes to Flynn Steele in the ring with Big Eliem. Flynn drops Eliem with a drop toe hold and spins into an STF. He wrenches back on the neck. Then Flynn switches to a leg scissors and then applies the feared "Tashaskaira". Big Eliem immediately taps out. The screen fades to black. Shaman : Flynn Steele. A truely great competitor. Flynn Steele is one of the greatest technical wrestlers to even don the tights. He is a remarkable athlete as he proves time and time again his skills... And now onto the second nominee... The jumbotron shows Nikodemous Segier and Jennifer walking out from the curtains as "Tubthumpin" by Chumbawamba plays. Nik and Jennifer slap hands on the way to the ring. The screen flashes to Segier being thrown into a wall cutting his arm. Cross gets Segier up for a back suplex and Segier escapes out and lands on his feet. Then gets Cross in a German suplex on the concrete. Cross is bleeding from the back of his head as the referee goes down for the three count. Segier falls to the ground as medics rush to attend to him. The the scene shows Nik Segier holding the Championship up with Jennifer with her arms around his waist. The picture freezes and then fades to black. Shaman : Nikodemous Segier... Another one of the great technicians of our sport. Well... I guess he would be more the submisionist, but at any rate one of the best. Let's see the final nominee for the Wrestler of the Year. The scene opens to Randy Cross walking out to "Hey Man, Nice Shot" by Filter. He has the WWA World title around his waist as he walks to the ring. The screen flashes to Randy Cross with the Figure Four locked on Mark Lynch. Randy starts to bounce up and down adding pressure to the hold. Mark Lynch is fighting the hold. Eventually Lynch starts to fade and his shoulders are counted out for the pin for Randy Cross. The screen shows Randy Cross holding the belt in the air with blood in his hair and over his face. Then the screen fades to black. Shaman : I hate to say it, but Randy Cross. He was the three time and current World Champion. He has one of the best records in the WWA. His tactics may be a little questionable at times, but he is the champ! And the Wrestler of the Year is.... Randy Cross! "Hey Man, Nice Shot" by Filter starts over the PA. Randy stands up and gives his wife a hug. The Outlaw, Jak Lancer, Todd Logan and the Crockett all shake his hand. He walks over to Nik Segier and offers a handshake. Segier accepts and Randy moves on to Flynn Steele. Flynn Steele reluctantly accepts the hand shake as well. Randy makes his way to the podium. He clears his throat as the music stops. Randy Cross : Thank you everybody. I really mean that. Tonight, I am glad we were able to put our differences behind and just celebrate the gifts god gave us. Celebrate the fact we earn money for doing what we love. This award is not only mine... But it goes out to the rest of the House of Pain. You guys made me what I am today! I thank you! Dawn.. You are a rock. Putting up with the late night HOP strategy meetings, and just plain putting up with Outlaw! (Randy winks and points to the Outlaw. The HOP tables laughs a little. The Outlaw smiles and points back). But I don't want to drag this out anymore... Thank you to all! Oh.. One more thing... O'Hara... Make the right choice. Thank you..
