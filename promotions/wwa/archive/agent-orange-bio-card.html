Date of Birth | May 9, 1988 Home Town | An Undisclosed Location Theme | "Hell March" by composer Frank Klepacki More Details Appearance Agent ORANGE is slim and well-toned, rather like a ferret. He seldom blinks, and has an aura of menace about him; as though he is a second away from snapping. Ring Attire He wears camo pants, bright orange boots, wristbands and an intensely orange balaclava, obscuring all but his mad eyes and thin lips. Personality Agent ORANGE is slightly mad. No wait, not slightly, what's that other word...? Completely. He speaks is single shouted words, constantly loses his train of thought, and is only marginally controlled by his manager General Mayhem. History Little is known [kayfabe] of the person known as Agent ORANGE. Rumour has it a "Security Consultant Firm" contracted in an unofficial 'dirty war' wanted to created a stronger, faster and smarter soldier. The result of their experiment was a spectacular failure. His name is Agent Orange. In real life, ORANGE's real name is Peter Meyers, younger brother of longtime WWA personality Lily Meyers.
