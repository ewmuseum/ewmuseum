Villain bounces off the ropes and tries for a cross bodyblock, but Walcott falls to the floor and Villain hits nothing but mat. Taking quick advantage of this situation, Walcott locks Villain into a double armbar submission hold. Fat Tony: Oh no! How is he supposed to grab onto the ropes like that? Jimmy: It doesn’t have to be a hand, numb nuts. Jimmy is right, because after a few seconds in this painful hold, Villain rolls on top of Walcott and drapes a foot across the bottom rope. Walcott is forced to break the hold. Jimmy: This has been a pretty good match so far. Fat Tony: Considering that Walcott’s in it. Jimmy: I’d like to see you do better. Fat Tony: I can. Again both men are back up. Villain kicks Troy in the stomach and gets him into a front facelock. Jimmy: I think he’s going for a DDT. Fat Tony: You think of that all by yourself, or did you read it off your monitor? Villain attempts a DDT, but Troy Walcott counters with a Northern Lights suplex... or rather, he would have had Villain not shifted his weight and came back down in front of Walcott, still holding the front facelock, still trying for the DDT. Troy tries again and again fails to get Villain over, but Villain comes down with a vicious spinning DDT. Jimmy: My only regret is not seeing Rob van Dam and Jerry Lynn do that aside from in the title sequence. Both men look out of it right now, but Villain is the first one to get up. He walks over to Troy Walcott and picks him up by the hair, then whips him into the ropes. Villain leapfrogs Troy on the return, but on the second pass catches him right under the chin with a spinning heel kick they can hear in the 99th row. Jimmy: Wow, did you hear that impact? Fat Tony: Gee, um... no? Jimmy: You know, sarcasm is only funny when the person using it is intelligent. Fat Tony: I always wondered why you didn’t use it. Again Villain picks Walcott up, and again Troy is whipped into the far ropes. Villain attempts a clothesline, but Walcott ducks and continues running, bouncing off the opposite ropes and hitting Villain with a flying forearm that knocks him out of the ring. Walcott backs up and takes a running start at the ropes. He leaps over them, and Villain moves out of the way expecting a plancha. That’s not what happens, however, as Troy holds onto the ropes and lands on the outside ring apron. As soon as Villain turns back around, he is met with an excellent moonsault. Jimmy: WHAT A MOVE BY WALCOTT!!! Fat Tony: It wasn't that good. I used to do that move all the time. Walcott rolls off of Villain, then mounts him and starts laying into him with punches to the head. After a succession of these, Walcott pulls Villain to his feet. Walcott whips Villain toward the guardrail, but Villain puts on the brakes and comes back with a flying headscissors takedown. Jimmy: So many counters and reversals, I love this match. Villain and Walcott get to their feet at about the same time. Villain punches Walcott in the face, stunning him just long enough to try to Irish whip Walcott into the cornerpost. It doesn’t work out as planned, however, because Walcott reverses the whip and Villain is sent to the post instead. Villain hits the post and stays there, his arm draped around the post. Fat Tony: Oh no! Jimmy: Walcott with the reversal and Villain goes shoulder first into the steel pole. Fat Tony: What is Walcott doing now? Jimmy: Walcott setting him up, and he just yanked Villain's injured shoulder causing it to hit the steel pole again! Fat Tony: Villain now really favoring that shoulder! Indeed he is. But he doesn’t get to favor it alone for too long as Walcott forces Villain back into the ring, then climbs the ropes. Fat Tony: What’s that idiot doing now? Jimmy: I think he’s looking to take to the air, dipstick. As soon as Villain gets up, he is met with a flying clothesline. Walcott rolls through into a crouching position in the opposite corner. Jimmy: This could be bad for Villain. It is bad for Villain. With Villain facing down and Troy having him by both legs, he just rocks back and nails Villain with a wheelbarrow suplex. Troy plays to the crowd a little bit, then smirks and creeps up on the fallen Villain. Fat Tony: This doesn’t look good! Troy grabs one of Villain’s arms and attempts to lock in the Trojan Reins, but Villain sees it coming and bails out of the ring as quickly as possible. Jimmy: Like a scalded dog, as another JR would say. Fat Tony: Maybe, but it’s smart, too. Villain is now on the outside chatting with Lois Lame. Villain apparently doesn’t think this is enough to pull out a victory, because he quickly jumps up to the top rope and comes off with a frog splash. Ref: 1... 2... thr... kickout! Jimmy: Oh!
