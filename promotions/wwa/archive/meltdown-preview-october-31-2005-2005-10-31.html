Meltdown Preview - October 31, 2005

31 October 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A Meltdown preview and card for Monday 31st October 2005 live from Las Vegas, with a six-man main event and an Extreme Title Tournament match.
Live from MGM Grand Garden Arena Las Vegas, Nevada Monday, 31st October 2005 On October 31st, the WWA rolls into the MGM Grand Garden Arena in Las Vegas, Nevada for the final show before Day of the Dead. The night will start off with tag team action as former Tag Team Champions KaOs take on two members of the newly formed High Society, Armani Decano and Aaron Chandler. Next up, the Detonators continue the tag team action against The WORST. Considering the recent tensions between KaOs and the Detonators, anything could happen during these two opening matches. Last week, newcomer Ramone ruined Xavier Anderson’s debut match in the WWA with a brutal attack, prompting Anderson to ask for a match against his newfound nemesis this week. Newcomer Stan “The Pitfighter” Jones gets the chance to prove himself against WWA veteran Nick Adams. Jones turned a number of heads in the WWA during his tryout match, but can he compete with the former three-time Television Champion? The Extreme Title Tournament continues with newcomer La Sombra trying to advance over the sometimes hapless Kid Cool. This match will be fought under WWA Extreme rules and the winner will go on to fight at in the final rounds of the tournament at Global Warfare. Speaking of the Extreme Title Tournament, because one of the matches had to be cancelled two weeks ago, Khaled has gained a bye week. In our main event, WWA World Champion Tracer Bullet teams up with Denrol and the WWA US Champion, Xian Phillipse to meet Ember, Ian Tense, and Freddie Arson. Ember was recently named the number one contender to the World Title, Ian Tense has made it his personal mission to destroy Denrol’s career, and Freddie Arson is the former WWA TV Champion whose undefeated streak ended only two weeks ago. When all six of these men step into the ring together, we can certainly expect fireworks. Numerous other WWA stars will be in attendance, and we expect to be able to announce even more incredible matches for Day of the Dead. Don’t miss out. MAIN EVENT Tracer Bullet / Denrol / Xian Phillipse versus Ian Tense / Ember / Freddie Arson Extreme Title Tournament Match La Sombra vs. Kid Cool Stan Jones vs. Nick Adams Xavier Anderson vs. Ramone The Detonators vs. The WORST KaOs vs. High Society (Armani Decano and Aaron Chandler)
