WWA — 7 Apr 1999

7 Apr 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Wednesday April 7, 1999 The screen opens up to the WWA banner. Then firework type explosions go off around the Banner. The camera shows the outside of the complex, revealing it to be the Continental Arena. The screen then goes into the arena to show the fans jumping and screaming at the Camera. The camera pans over the the inside, and then the camera finds Eric Pembrook and Johny Morehouse sitting behind the table in the broadcast area. Eric : Good Evening everybody. Welcome to the new home of the Wednesday Slam. We are here in East Rutherford New Jersey inside the Continental Arena. Johny : That's right... This is the new homebase for the WWA operations. Tonight we have a great one in store for you. Tonight we see the tag team of Enfeugo go against the House of Pain's Minnesota Wrecking Crew. Eric : The winner gets a shot at the Commandant's Own this Friday night. Along with that we have Sir Micheal against "Pretty Boy" Troy Walcott... Johny : A long awaited match for those two... We also have a couple matches from the Cancelled Sunday Extreme from this last Sunday... We get Billy Coleman versus Lantern and Rage versus the Fan. Both of these matches will be EXTREME rules! Eric : In that case... Let's get right down to it! Gary... Gary Trudeau : Ladies and Gentlemen... Welcome to the WWA studios in East Rutherford New Jersey... The first match this evening with be set for one fall... Inroducing first, we have The Misery! "Dead Bodies Everywhere" by Korn starts over the PA. The Misery enters the arena to relatively no response. Misery walks to the ring and plants himself into the corner. Gary Trudeau : And his opponent... Coming to us by way of Toronto, Ontario, Canada... We have Timothy Micheals! "Come get Some" starts over the PA. Timothy Micheals enters the arena to a nice reception. The ladies especially are liking this newcomer... HE slaps hands on his way to the ring. Eric : Look out Kasanova... We got another ladies man out here... Johny : Misery flies out of the corner and nails Micheals from behind with a double axe handle... Eric : Micheals is down and Misery is laying the boots to the back and head of Micheals... Misery gets Micheals to his feet and sends him across the ring... Micheals ducks a clothesline attempt by Misery... Johny : Micheals puts on the brakes! Turns and dropkick to the chest of Misery. Misery jumps back to his feet and Micheals with another dropkick. Misery up again and this time Misery lays him out with jumping clothesline. Eric : Misery quickly gets Micheals up hits a big bodyslam and the cover. 2 only as Micheals kicks out with authority. Misery gets Micheals up and- Johny : Inside cradle by Micheals and the near fall! Misery kicks out at the last second. Both men are up now and Micheals takes Misery over with an armdrag... Misery is slow getting up as he yells at the crowd.. Eric : Micheals is standing ready and SUPERKICK! Misery is laying flat on his back and Micheals goes for the cover and... 3! Johny : Timothy Micheals with an outstanding show here... Moves on to victory over Misery... Shall we keep moving...? Eric : Yes I thi- Voice Over : The following announcement has been paid for by the House of Pain. The screen opens to a field of somesort.. A baseball diamond to be exact. As the camera closes in on the field it nears the pitchers mound... It is obvious that the man on the mound is none other than Andy Pettite of the NY Yankees... He is pitching the ball to very large man in the batters box. As the screen moves closer it is obvious that it is Mantis of the House of Pain... Then the camera finds Jon Cole, Zack Crockett, Aric Crockett, Todd Logan and the Fan all with baseball bats waiting their turn... Randy Cross and the Outlaw are watching from the dugout... The camera then moves back to Mantis who is standing ready.. Pettite pitches the ball and Mantis nails the ball and it flies over the left field fence... The camera flashes to Randy Cross and Outlaw in the dugout... Outlaw : As you can see Griffin... My boys will be more than willing to beat the tar out of you, and more than capable (motioning back towards Mantis). Randy : You see Denrol... Griffin and the House of Pain go back to the beginning of the WWA... Back to the days of NCW. The HoP has no desire to cripple Griffin. He is a great foe, and it would be a shame to see his career ended in a match developed because you are afraid to fight him. Outlaw : Tis true Denrol... Your fear and your cowardice shines forth with every match that you sign for this Pain Games of yours... Denrol... I suggest you take my advice with the modifications I brought forth yesterday... Both men get up and walk out of the dugout... Randy : But in the mean time... We will be ready to hit you out of the Park Griffin... The screen flashes back to the plate where Jon Cole sends one into the cheap seats... The camera focuses on Cole's face, where he mouths the words... "You're Dead".. Voice Over : The preceeding announcement has been paid for by the House of Pain. The camera fades into the arena and to Eric Pembrook and Johny Morhouse.... Eric : That was more than a little rude! Let's go to Gary Trudeau and the next match of the evening... Gary Trudeau : Ladies and Gentlemen.. The next match of the evening will be set for one fall with a 10 minute time limit. Introducing first, from Juno Alaska.. We have Ice Tank! "Last Ride in" by Green Day starts over the Pa. Ice Tank walks into the arena to a negative responce... HE runs his hand through his blueish hair and simply smiles... He strides to the ring and awaits his opponent... Gary Trudeau : And his opponent... Coming by way of Quincy, Mass! He is "The Crusader" Tommy Lazer! "Don't Tread on Me" by Metallica blares from the loud speakers. Tommy Lazer appears at the top of the ramp. He dumps a bottle of water over his head and sprints to the ring. He does a belly slide three-quarters of the way across the mat. Eric : Lazer in a hurry to get this one started as the ref calls for the bell... Johny : Lazer immediately nails the big Ice Tank with a drop kick staggering the big Man... Ice Tank still on his feet swings a big right at Lazer, who quickly ducks and fires a few quick kicks to the midsection of Ice Tank. Eric : The big guy staggers back a few steps and Lazer bounces of the far ropes and gets head of steam... Big drop kick.. FINALLY knocking Ice Tank to the mat. Lazer runs over and drops an elbow to the back of Ice Tank's head. Johny : Uh Oh! That just seemed to make the Tank made... He is standing up... Lazer is throwing rights and lefts, but Ice Tank just pushes Lazer back across the ring like a rag doll... Eric : Ice Tank moves over to grab Lazer again, but he smartly bails out of the ring.. Ice Tank through the ropes and grabs Lazer by the neck! Ice Tank sends Lazer headlong into the steel railing... Johny : Ice Tank threatens a couple of the fans as he moves over to the downed Lazer. Ice Tank picks up the smaller man and lauches him into the ring. Lazer is in trouble now as the bigman climbs up the ropes.... Eric : This could be trouble if Ice Tank hits this move! Ice Tank up now and off... NO! Lazer moved! Good thing to! Ice Tank would have killed him! Johny : I think so Eric... Ice Tank is alot of man. Lazer, getting his second wind staggers to his feet and over to the downed Ice Tank... Lazer helps Ice Tank up and sets up a RUSSIAN Leg Sweep! Eric : Nicley done by Lazer. Lazer again gets Ice Tank up and sends him across the ring... Belly to Belly! Again nicely executed by Lazer! Ice Tank in trouble now as Lazer is feeling it now... Johny : Lazer skips the pin attemp pulling Ice Tank up and DDT! Lazer is complete comtrol of this match now... Lazer again picks Ice Tank up... There is the sign for the Lazer Cutter... Ice Tank is a lot of man for this move! Eric : Well Lazer locks in the front face lock... And up... Lazer Cutter! Increadible power shown by the lightheavyweight! Johny : Lazer hooks the leg and gets the 3 count... I will have to say, Impressive victory by The Crusader! Let's move on... President Harrington is going to make an announcement... Mark Harrington walks to the ring in a black suit with a microphone in his hand. He climbs into the ring, and waits for the crowd to quiet down. Mark Harrington : Ladies and gentlemen... Recently, there has been some rather blood-thirsty challenges from one "Famine" of the Four Horsemen of the Apocalypse, Jack II. He has declared that he wants Steihl, or Once Steihl, in a career match. Well, I want you BOTH to come out here RIGHT now. The arena goes dark as "The House That Jack Built" by Metallica starts up over the P.A. system. Slowly, Jack II, dressed in his black Famine robes and his golden mask, walks to the ring. He climbs the steps, and stands in one corner, looking at Harrington. The voice of Steihl, twisted somehow into a hissing, rasping but recognizable voice,: Lasciate ogni speranza, voi ch'entrate! The lights turn into a red strobe as "Dragula" by Rob Zombie rips through the PA. Steihl strides through the curtains as the music plays. Down the aisle he goes, his cloak billowing out behind him, and the ramp burns as his footsteps pass, leaving a trail of fire in his wake. Outside the ring, Steihl tosses back his hood, his yellow eyes ablaze, his face clearly angry. He smiles sardonically, and jumps upto the apron, and flips over the ropes, to land on his feet in the center of the ring. As "I can never die" sings, Steihl throws a fist into the air in a jump spinning uppercut and the corners of the ring errupt in brilliant flames. The strobe does not return after the move, and the lights raise as he lands. The voice of Steihl, not so twisted, but somehow corrupt almost beyond recognition: "Leave all hope, you that enter!" Mark Harrington : Now, you two want a career match, in the Famine rules, for the Famine role in the Four Horsemen of the Apocalypse, correct? (both men nod) Well, I'm sorry, but I can't allow one of you to leave the World Wrestling Alliance. You are both too valuable of assets to have one leave. BUT, I WILL allow a Famine rules, Famine role on the line match NEXT WEEK right here on FRIDAY NIGHT FIGHTS! The crowd gasps and starts cheering. Harrington exits the ring and leaves to the back. Meanwhile, Steihl and Jack II just stare at each other through angry eyes. Then, Bob runs out, grabs Jack II, and carries him all the way back to the locker rooms before anything can happen. The cheers from the crowd are almost deafening as the scene breaks for commercial. The screen opens to a commerical. The commercial ends and the screen fades into the arena, the ring and Gary Trudeau... Gary Trudeau : Ladies and Gentleman! The following match up is sheduled for one fall, with a Twenty Minute time limit, it will be Extreme Rules! Introducing first .. He hails from Calgary, Alberta, Canada. Weighing in at 233 pounds .. Lantern! The lights go down low. "Indestructable" by Matthew Good Band blares through the arena. In the entrance way pyrotechnics go off to form the lantern symbol. As they die down a solitary swaying light passes through the entrance. Its swinging motion suggests it is being carried very uneasily by its bearer. As the lights go up it is revealed to be Lantern, dressed in his trench coat and Oakley shades, carrying the light. He sets the kerosene lantern down on the ring steps, slips his shades and jacket, and steps into the ring. Gary Trudeau : And his oppoenent. From Colorado Springs, Colorado. Weighing in at 286 pounds .. "Wild Thing" Billy Coleman! The lights go out and "Crazy" by Aerosmith. Everyone looks toward the top of the ramp to catch a glimpse of Billy Coleman. Nobody comes out. Lantern looks toward the ramp in a bewildered look. The camera then flashes to the announcers desk where Juan Guevesos and John Douglass have joined Eric, in the stead of Johny Morehouse. Juan : Maybe Coleman was to distraught over his father's death that he did not want to show up. John : Hey look into the rafters is that Coleman...? All of a sudden "Wild Thing" is heard over the loudspeakers, and the crowd responds with a deafening roar. All of a sudden Coleman is found swinging from the rafters on a rope with two baseball bats on his back. Coleman is wearing a Midcarder shirt in honor of the Man. Coleman puts both of his feet square on Lantern's back, sending Lantern out of the ring. Juan : Here we go! Eric : The bell hasn't even sounded yet.. John : Coleman follows Lantern to the outside. He's beating him with his bats! Juan : The ref finally seperates the two. Eric : The ref took his bats .. that's not fair. John : They're both in the ring now, Lantern goes for the shoulder neck tie up.. Eric : Coleman ducks under .. drop kick to the face! Juan : Coleman with the advantage.. Eric : Coleman pulling Lantern to his feet.. Oh! Low Blow .. the ref didn't see it.. John : Lantern stomping away on the downed Coleman. Off the ropes, Leg Drop to the back of the head.. Juan : Look at Lantern go .. Snap Suplex.. John : Lantern with the upper hand. Eric : Now what? Lantern going for a Running Bulldog .. NO. Coleman sent him face first into the turnbuckle. Juan : Coleman takes the staggering Lantern down with a back slide .. 1.. KICKOUT! John : Lantern with a DD'T out of no where.. Elbow Drop .. and the cover.. 1..2..KICKOUT! Eric : Both man standing .. They lock up in mid ring .. Lantern with a Side HeadLock.. Juan : Coleman backing Lantern into the ropes .. Damn! What a tremendous clothesline by 286 pounder. That rocked Lantern back into the '80s! Eric : Coleman going up top .. Flying Headbutt! Coleman with the cover .. 1..2..KICKOUT. John : Coleman pulling Lantern to his feet .. PowerBomb! Juan : Lantern holding his back in pain .. he slips under the bottom rope, he's holding his back ... Eric : What's this? Lantern's got the baseball bat .. Coleman doesn't see it .. OH! Juan : Lantern just clocked Coleman in the head with the bat .. the ref didn't see it! John : Lantern with the cover .. 1...2...Coleman kicks out! After the shot, he kicks out! Eric : Lantern can't believe his eyes .. he looks pissed. Juan : Oh, that's the last thing he wants to do .. He's argueing with the damn ref .. Eric : Coleman from behind! He just laid him out with a Belly to Back suplex that sent Lantern down hard! Juan : He's got Lantern set up .. Aspen Extreme!! He hooks the leg. 1...2...3! And Billy Coleman just scored the upset! Coleman (grabbing a mic): What a sucker you are Lantern. You thought that you could hang with the big boys did you. Nice try Lantern. The last things my Dad said to me was fight harder than you ever had. So this is the what he wants and I want. To New Star or Outlaw. I am coming for my title. Coleman throws the mike down and leaves the ring. Halfway up the ramp he leaps into the crowd and disappears. Eric : Well... Billy Coleman with quite a statement... John : Yeah! I would be worried if I was Outlaw or New Star... Whoever wins the Extreme Title... Juan : Definately... Billy Coleman means business... Eric : Lets go to the ring for the next match... Gary Trudeau : The following match will be set for one fan, with Extreme rules. Introducing first... From the crowd.. WE have The Fan! "Snap Your Fingers, Snap your neck!" by Prong starts over the PA. The Fan emerges from the curtains to the booing of the crowd. The Fan ignores the crawd and proceeds to the ring, uncaring... He enters the ring and awaits his opponent. Gary Trudeau : And his opponent, from parts Unknown.. He is RAGE! "Death Row" by Judist Preist starts over the PA. Rage enters the arena to a mixed reaction. Rage stares intently on the ring and steps over the tope rope. Eric : We have to enormous men squaring off in the ring right now. Rage and The Fan are both devastating creatures that somehow got on this earth. John : That is for sure. Rage starts things off by smashing The Fan with a huge headbutt. Rage grabs The Fan by the hair and wings him across the ring. Juan : The Fan is helpless when it comes to his hair. Rage starts smashing his huge hands onto The Fan. Somehow I cannot see The Fan winning this match. Eric : Rage sure is dominating the match. Rage picks The Fan up and lands a huge powerbomb. With The Fan's weight and Rage's strength, The Fan almost went through the mat. John : Rage is not stopping there as he picks The Fan up and slides him into a guerilla press. Juan : Rage throws The Fan clear out of the ring and onto the cement ramp. He must have thrown The Fan almost 10 FEET! Eric : It is clear to say that Rage is dominating The Fan like it is his job. The Rage is up on the ringpost and flies off, but The Fan somehow finds the energy to roll away. John : The Fan is up and now has a chair. Rage is up also and the two look at each other. Juan : The Fan swings but Rage just takes the chair shot and laughs at The Fan. The Fan swings again but this time Rage punches the chair and it rebounds to smack The Fan in the face. Eric : Rage is loving this as he lays The Fan up on a table he found under the ring. Rage shakes his head and drags The Fan off the table and brings out another table, and then another. John : Rage has set up 3 tables outside the ring. He brings The Fan into the ring and hits another horrible powerbomb to ensure that The Fan is going nowhere. Juan : Rage places The Fan on top of the three tables and then goes back under the ring for something else. He brings out a ladder and places it in the ring. Eric : Rage climbs the ladder and... John : Huge elbow drop onto The Fan slamming them both threw three tables. Rage stands up as if nothing has happened. The crowd is going crazy. Juan : Rage hauls The Fan into the ring, and places one boot on The Fan's stomach. Eric : 1,2,3! Amazing victory by Rage over The Fan. This kid will be a contender someday. John : Someday... How about NOW! That was incredible! Eric : We'll be right back! The screen fades on the body of the Fan and then to black. A commercial opens up on the screen.... The commercial ends and the screen fades into Eric and Johny, who has rejoined him at ringside. Eric : Welcome back Johny... Johny : Looks like I missed a couple of good ones...? Eric : True! A couple of great ones! Let's keep it moving and go to the next match... Gary Trudeau : The next match of the evening will be set for one fall... It will have a 20 minute time limit... Introducing first from the Middle Ages, representing the Crusade, Sir Micheal! "Princes of the Universe" starts over the PA. Sir Micheal and Lady Racheal enter the arena to a nice responce from the fans... Sir Micheal slaps hands as Lady Racheal smiles and waves. Sir Micheal takes off his breast plate and jumpe over the top rope. Gary Trudeau : And his opponent... From the Pretty City.. WE have "Pretty Boy" Troy Walcott! "If You Think I'm Jiggy" by The LOX starts over the PA. Walcott and the Executive enter the area to a mixed reaction from the crowd. Walcott enter the ring as the Executive stands at ringside. Eric : We are ready to go here... Walcott blows a kiss to Lady Racheal.. Johny : Sir Micheal flattens Walcott with a double axe handle... Sir Micheal didn't take to kindly to the kiss I guees...? Eric : I would guess not as Sir Micheal is planting boots to the back of Walcott's head. Sir Micheal gets Walcott up and sends him across the ring with an irish whip. Sir Micheal catches Walcott in a tilt-a-whirl backbreaker! Johny : Nice move by the Crusade member. Sir Micheal quickly over and gets Walcott to his feet. Swinging neckbreaker by Sir Micheal. Sir Micheal quickly with the cover and 1 count only as Walcott kicks out strongly. Eric : Sir Micheal again over to Walcott. This time Walcott fires a right to the jaw of Micheal staggering the knight.... Walcott again with a big right hand sending Sir Micheal straight to the mat! Sir Micheal quickly up and Walcott nails him with a spinning heel kick. Johny : The tides have certainly turned here.... Walcott this time with an irish whip of his own... SPINEBUSTER! Walcott smiles to the fans and yells at the Executive... Eric : The Executive sneers and nods his agreement... Walcott moves over to Sir Micheal and lock in the "Pretty Predicament"! The scorpian deathlock is locked in the middle of the ring! Sir Micheal has got no place to go. Johny : Sir Micheal is shaking his head as the ref is down there checking on him.... Sir Micheal is fighting it... He pulls towards the roeps... Walcott senses this and leans way back! Sir Micheal yells out in pain and keeps the pressure on! Eric : Sir Micheal REACHES THE ROPES! I can't believe it! The ref taps Walcott on the back and tells him what happened... He releases the hold, turns and drops a viscious elbow to the back of Micheal's head! Johny : Walcott pulls Sir Micheal back to the middle of the ring... Sir Micheal drug to his feet fires a shot to the midsection of Walcott. And again... Sir Micheal fires yet another elbow to the midsection... Sir Micheal runs against the far ropes and nails Walcott with a dropkick. Eric : Walcott jumps up and levels Sir Micheal with a clothesline! Sir Micheal is on the mat now as Walcott drops an elbow across the chest of Sir Micheal... Walcott starts spouting off about the rules of Pretty City and whatnot as he picks Sir Micheal up... Johny : Walcott picks up Sir Micheal and Inverted Atomic drop! Sir Micheal is feeling that... Walcott quickly over and locks in a front face lock... Slingshot... PRETTY PLEX! And 3! "Pretty Boy" Troy Walcott gets the upset victory over Sir Micheal of the Crusade! Eric : Let's keep this moving with the main event of the evening... The Minnesota Wrecking Crew against Enfuego! The winner gets a shot at the Commandant's Own this Friday. Gary Trudeau : Ladies and Gentlemen... It is time for the Main Event of the evening... It will be set for one fall... A 30 minute time limit. Coming to you first and representing the HOP. . . Zack and Aric Crockett, also known as The Minnesota Wrecking Crew! Hey Man, Nice Shot...by Filter pumps over the crowd as MWC comes out to a chorus of boos from the crowd. Neither wrestler pays much attention to the crowd and are talking to eachother on the way to the ring. They almost look as if they are bored to be here as they enter the ring... Gary Trudeau : And the newly formed team that call themselves Enfuego! Here in their first match in the WWA.....Steve "The Snake" Stabler and Jordan Statler! Eye of the Tiger plays over the PA as Enfuego comes out from the back to a mixed crowd reaction...It's more of a "who's this" mumble that the crowd gives the two. They shake the hands of the fans on the way down to the ring... Johny : Zack Crockett over the ropes and MISSES!!! Eric : Haha, Zack Crockett's head just knocked the concrete as he attempted a suicide dive. Johny : Stabler is laying boots into the body of Zack, as Aric comes through the ropes and hits a diamond cutter on Statler! Eric : Statler's turn to eat concrete! Johny : Aric now going after Stabler and kicks him in the gut. Aric with a short arm clothesline. Eric : Aric now tending to the still fallen Zack...helping him up and getting him into the ring. Johny : Stabler from behind!!! Double Axehandle Smash! Right in the center of Aric's back. Eric : Stabler grabbed the hair of Aric and slamming his head against the apron! 3, 4, 5!!! Johny : The ref stepping in and telling Stabler to get this one in the ring or else he'll have a double dq...before the match even starts. Eric : Stabler rolls Aric into the ring. Statler is slowly getting up on the outside, and Zack is sitting in the corner taking a breather. Johny : The ref just signalled for the bell?!!! Stabler is on his back, and Statler isn't even up off the ground yet!! Eric : Aric quickly over to Stabler with some vicious kicks to the gut of Stabler, who is trying to get up. Johny : Aric picks Stabler up and hits a vertical suplex. Eric : Aric off of the ropes with a big elbow to the chest of Stabler. Johny : So far, here in the early going, it's been all Aric Crockett, taking care of Enfuego single handedly. Eric : Crockett over to Stabler...LOWBLOW! Lowblow by Stabler. He looks over to Statler who is up on the apron, but passes on the tag. Johny : Stabler pulls Aric up by the hair and hits a quick and powerful snap suplex. Eric : Stabler over and hits the tag to Statler. Both are in and each takes one arm of Aric. Double irish whip, into a double clothesline! Johny : Nice showing of team work by the youngsters. Statler with the cover! 1...2... Zack Crockett with the save! Eric : There was the match right there ladies and gentleman. I don't think that Aric would have got up in time. Johny : Nevertheless, Statler picking up Aric and whipping him into the ropes....NO, reversal by Aric, sending Statler chest first into the turnbuckle. Eric : Aric with a SUPERKICK to the back of Statler's head. Here's the cover! 1...2... Kick out by Statler! Johny : Lazy cover by Aric there...If he would have at least wrapped a leg MCW could have got the victory! Eric : Aric frustrated with this and grabs Statler by the arm. Makes the tag to Zack who jumps the ropes and punches the upper arm of Statler. Johny : Zack and Statler tie up....Zack with the upperhand. Applies a half nelson, drops it into a back-belly suplex!
