With frightening speed and precision, Thorne releases the Suplex and catches Grunge around the waist. Thorne sits out, adding to the effects of gravity to shake the ring with a crushing Piledriver. Fat Tony: CROWN OF THORNE! CROWN OF THORNE! Jimmy: That high impact move has the potential to end this match! Thorne drops across his prey, grinding his forearm into Grunge's face. The referee drops for the count. ONE... TWO... To delight of the arena, Grunge snaps to life, forcing Thorne to break the pin. Jimmy: I don't believe it! Grunge just kicked out of the Crown of Thorne! Fat Tony: It's only a matter of time now. Thorne is about to end this! Damian lifts Grunge's leg and steps around it, preparing for a Figure Four. Jimmy: Looks like Thorne is taking a page out of Grunge's book. Fat Tony: Why does it have to be Grunge's? It could be Ric Flair's, or Buddy Rogers', or... Jimmy: Shut up or I'll beat you with your own shoes. Confidently, Thorne continues his ministrations on setting up the lethal hold. Grunge though, knows this hold. He knows every angle, every nuance, every point of pressure and leverage. And he knows the counter. Grunge rolls swiftly, using his now entwined legs to bring Thorne down to the mat. Quickly pulling himself from the mat and grabbing hold of Thorne's leg, Grunge leans back. Jimmy: Grunge reversed the Figure Four into a Sharpshooter! After all of the punishment that he's taken in this match, he's finally giving as good as he gets! Crowd : ROCK AND ROLL! ROCK AND ROLL! ROCK AND ROLL! Outside of the ring, Alec applauds and cheers loudly. Grunge leans back hard into the lethal submission hold, but Thorne's fingers reach out, managing to find the ropes. Fat Tony: There's the mark of a true ring general. Thorne knew where the ropes were the entire time. Upon the referee's say-so, Grunge releases the hold. As Thorne pulls his legs under him to rise, Grunge moves in quickly, slamming a hard roundhouse kick into Thorne's face. SMACK ! Crowd : OOOOHHH! Seeing Thorne reel, Grunge pulls him up onto his shoulders in a Fireman's Carry. Jimmy: Grunge showing some strength of his own here. Fat Tony: Are you kidding? Look at his legs shaking? How can expect to pull of a decent DVD like that? Jimmy: I don't think he will, Tony. Fat Tony: What, you're agreeing with me? It's about time you came over to the Almighty's side! Grunge steps back into the corner, and uses a combination of leverage, impeccable balance, and sheer iron-willed determination to ascend to the second rope with the heavier man on his shoulders. Fat Tony: What the heck is he doing? Grunge leaps from the second rope, tossing Thorne's weight forward and rolling in midair to slam Thorne's body into the mat with Grunge's back on top of him. Jimmy: I was not coming over to Thorne's side, incidentally. I meant that Grunge was probably not going for the DVD, but rather his patented Code Blue! Fat Tony: I am really starting to hate that guy. Grunge rolls over onto Thorne's chest, hooking the leg and leaning back. ONE... TWO... Thorne kicks out, prompting a collective sigh of disappointment from the audience. Fat Tony: There's no way that Grunge can put Thorne down for the three. There's just no way. Jimmy: I don't know, Tony. So far, Grunge has taken everything that Thorne has handed out, and he just keeps coming back for more! Grunge rolls back to his feet, his body heaving, gasping for breath to help clean away the fatigue in his muscles. Thorne rises, but his eyes leave Grunge, snatching a quick glimpse at the entranceway at the top of the ramp. Jimmy: Thorne is getting back up. These two men have put on an incredible battle tonight. Grunge moves forward, but Thorne steps in and slams him down to the mat with a Spinebuster. Not content with only this, Thorne straddles Grunge's body and begins to slam his fist repeatedly into Grunge's head, over and over, bone crunching against flesh and skull. Again and again, his fist finds its mark, until it comes back dripping with Grunge's blood. Jimmy: Oh my God! Damian Thorne just viciously tore into Grunge, beating him until he broke skin and made Grunge bleed! Fat Tony: Thorne warned him! He said that he would make Grunge bleed! Thorne stands, triumphantly pacing the ring with his bloody fist held in the air. The fans boo with renewed vigor and vitriol. Grunge pulls himself up to his elbow. Blood pours from a cut over his eye, and from his nose. He drags the back of his hand across his nose, pulling it back to see the streak of blood on his hand. He smiles and his eyes narrow. Jimmy: Grunge is actually smiling! Fat Tony: What is he, crazy? Grunge rises, standing on his own two feet. Thorne stops his parade to stare up at the entranceway on the stage again. He shakes his head and rubs his ear. Fat Tony: What is he doing? Why does he keep looking at the stage? Jimmy: I have no idea, but he'd better keep his eye on the match. Thorne turns to witness his handiwork, his artistry of gore, on Grunge's bruised, bloodied face. Thorne smiles with satisfaction. Grunge charges, launching himself into the air, wrapping his body around Thorne's and forcing him down to the mat. Jimmy: Grunge just exploded into a Lou Thesz Press! I don't think Thorne expected that kind of aggression at all! Grunge grinds his fists into Thorne's face over and over again, beating at his skull, shattering cartilage, and tearing bruised flesh. Soon, his fists come back sticky with Thorne's blood. Fat Tony: Holy crap! Jimmy: Grunge has just proved that he can give out anything that Thorne can! He's just shown Thorne that he can be just as vicious, just as brutal as him! This time, its Grunge's turn to strut. He moves to the turnbuckle, ascending and holding his fists out for the fans to see. Crowd : ROCK AND ROLL! ROCK AND ROLL! ROCK AND ROLL! Thorne stirs on the mat, rolling over and looking toward the entranceway. He cocks his head toward the ramp. Jimmy: He's doing it again. Fat Tony: What's wrong with him? Abruptly, Thorne regains his feet, blood dripping down from the cut above his eye and dripping from his nose. His lip begins to swell, even as blood escapes from the corner of his mouth. He faces Grunge. Grunge instinctively drops from the turnbuckle and faces him. Fat Tony: It's like watching two bulls squaring off before they charge each other. Jimmy: Well, they're certainly both bull-headed. Both men burst forth, their bodies dripping with blood and sweat. Thorne gains the upper hand with a devastating clothesline. Fat Tony: And the bigger bull won. Thorne pulls Grunge up and flings him across his shoulders, lifting him into a Fireman's Carry. With the brutal roar of the dominant beast, he throws Grunge's legs over his head and out to the side, dropping suddenly to the mat with a ¾ Facelock, smashing Grunge's head into the canvas. Fat Tony: PENNANCE! PENNANCE! This has to be over! The referee drops to count as Thorne rolls over onto Grunge, again grinding his forearm into his bloodied face. ONE... TWO... The arena detonates in a chorus of jubilant cheers as Grunge kicks out. Jimmy: Amazing! Grunge just refuses to quit! Any other man would have been done for, but Grunge refuses to give up! Fat Tony: How can he possibly keep this up? Thorne pulls himself from Grunge and stands staring at the entranceway. Again, he cocks his head toward the entranceway. This time, he shakes his head and mouths the word, no. Jimmy: I'm really quite concerned about Damian Thorne. What is he saying 'no' to? There's no one there. Fat Tony: Um...it's all part of the plan. Yeah. Thorne returns to Grunge and lifts him from the mat. With a clubbing forearm to keep his opponent stunned, Thorne tucks his head under Grunge's arm while tucking Grunge's head under his own. He hooks Grunge's leg and lifts. Jimmy: Cradle Suplex coming! Fat Tony: Not exactly, Jimmy! This is the setup for the most devastating finishing move in the world of wrestling, THE HAND OF GOD! Jimmy: Oh no! Thorne's face twists into a dark grin. His body stands solidly; sweat running in frantic streams and rivulets from his skin. The salty sweat mixes with the blood on his face, smearing and streaking, bringing a deranged, horrifying visage to the blue-eyed Almighty. Jimmy: My goodness, Thorne is such a powerful, dominant, frightening man. Fat Tony: He's well beyond mere man, Jimmy. Thorne stands there, holding Grunge in position at the apex of the Suplex, upside-down, his near leg being held in place by Thorne's powerful arm. His legs begin to move, struggling against Thorne's grip. Thorne tries to hold him, but Grunge's struggles bear fruit as Thorne is forced to release the near leg. Grunge swings his legs hard to the side, rotating him and his arm around Thorne's head. With blistering speed, Grunge spins around Thorne's head and locks in a ¾ Facelock while continuing the momentum of his thrown legs as a counterweight to pull Thorne off balance, and bring him down to the mat with his own version of a ¾ Facelock Bulldog! Jimmy: G SHARP! G SHARP! Grunge quickly reaches over to hook Thorne's massive, tree trunk-like leg, rolling back across Thorne's chest. ONE... TWO... THREE! Fat Tony: WHAT?! NO! IT'S IMPOSSIBLE! Jimmy: Grunge has done it! He's overcome the impossible odds! The arena bursts into cheers as Grunge rolls away from Thorne. Crowd : ROCK AND ROLL! ROCK AND ROLL! ROCK AND ROLL! Thorne sits up, staring at the entranceway with his bloodied mouth open in disbelief. Grunge grabs hold of the ropes, trying to pull himself to his feet. Jimmy: This is incredible! The unstoppable has been stopped! Gary Trudeau : Ladies and Gentlemen, the winner of this match, and your new WWA WORLD CHAMPION...GRUNGE! The thunderous roar of euphoria that sweeps through the arena, shakes the very foundations of the steel and concrete building, causing the ring to shake. Upon the WWATron, overshadowed by the incredible victory of the Ultimate Disciple of Rock and Roll, words appear. The only man who sees the words is Thorne himself. IT'S TIME. Grunge blinks hard, trying to accept what he's just heard from the ring announcer. Even as the belt is brought to him, he stares in shock. Jimmy: This is a great moment for the WWA! Fat Tony: But wait, what is Thorne doing? Thorne has limped his way up the ramp, until he stands in the exact same spot from which he entered the arena. He stares straight down the ramp, his eyes locked on Grunge. Feeling the gaze of Thorne like a palpable beam, Grunge turns. Their eyes meet. With blood and sweat coating their bodies, both men nod. Jimmy: Wha...? Was that respect? Suddenly, Thorne throws his head back in an ear-shattering bellow. A blinding white light follows, obscuring him from view, leaving only an outline of his form visible through the intense light. The light grows brighter, more intense, more urgent. And Thorne rises. Fat Tony: Holy crap! What the hell is going on? Is he floating? Jimmy: I...I don't know... Damian Thorne's body rises into the blinding white light. Without warning, massive columns of white fire erupt from the stage, eclipsing the beam of light and engulfing Thorne within their torturous, burning fingers. Through the flames, Thorne's body flails in agony, writhing against the searing of his flesh. His screams punctuate the air, rising above the terrified, panicked screams of the crowd. Jimmy: He's on fire! Get the emergency team out here! Fat Tony: WHAT THE HELL IS GOING ON?! Carmina Burana cleaves the silence. Thorne's body rises higher, nearly to the ceiling of the arena. The flames change to a deep, blood red. As the music hits a crescendo, Thorne's writhing form plummets through the fire, searing and screaming, and then... ...nothing. All goes dark and silent. No flames, no music, no screams from Thorne. Only the fearful tears of the audience remain. More than that, there is no Damian Thorne to be seen. Smoke slowly wafts over the metal stage, carried on the slow currents of air. And the Almighty Damian Thorne is nowhere to be found. Jimmy: What the hell just happened? Fat Tony: I...I don't know. He looks down at the title in his hand, and sees his own name already carved into the gold plate. Jimmy: Without a doubt, this is the most frightening and unusual thing that I have ever witnessed in all my years of commentary. One word fills the screen REMEMBER… . Jimmy: Ladies and gentlemen, I have no idea what we've just witnessed. I assure you that this was not planned. Fat Tony: I...I'm in shock. Jimmy: I think we all are, Tony. But, the fact remains that we have a new World Champion, Grunge, after an incredible match that saw him take every brutality that Damian Thorne had to give, and still refuse to give in. Fat Tony: And now, Thorne has disappeared under amazing and weird circumstances. Jimmy: We have so many unanswered questions, fans. Be here with us in two weeks.
