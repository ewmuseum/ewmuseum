The Scouting Report - Issue 9

24 Sep 2003

Bylined Joe Stock - Director of Talent, in the fed's own kayfabe voice.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Well things are not looking up. As far as I know, Warr is still a long way from getting a new television contract. Since the next edition of this column is planned to be November 28. Since if we don't have a contract by than, it's obvious we won't get one by December 1, so there is no reason to write a column for a federation that closes in two days. Thus, unless we have a new contract by than, this will be the last edition of The Scouting Report ever. It's been quite a run hasn't it? -Thankfully, if we do have a new contract, we have everyone under contract, or rather nearly everyone. Only Shaman has not yet signed a new contract. -The WWA has decided to let The Magician go and we are also not resigning Michael Stryfe. We feel at the moment neither man is ready to give the WWA what we require in our wrestlers. -A good portion of our superstars will be in action for a final set of house shows this weekend, we still have tickets out there, but it looks like this will be a rare case where we sell out all three house shows in a weekend. No doubt our fans want to see the final three WWA events. -A number of matches look good on those shows, including the main event of Kraven Vs EVD and the Extreme Title Match between Chance and Grunge. -We have signed a former WWA superstar to a new contract. I'll give you a hint and than revel the name at the end of this report. He's a former WWA Cruiserweight Champion. -Not much news on the injury front, except that Jackson Kraven is just about 100% now. He recovered quite well from his injured ribs. -Our WWA superstars are mostly on vacation, at least until the house shows. Some, like Venny LeTrav are even training to get ready for their return. -WWAmmies Nomination forms have gone out to various WWA officials. If we do get a new contract, the WWAmmies Awards ceremony should take place in January, shortly before Rage in the Cage. -An award for the best agent will be given out backstage. The agents are the one who take the ideas of all the men involved and mold them into a quality match. It's a tough job and our three guys each deserve an award, through only one will get one. So let's give a round of applause for Andrew, John, and Troy S, they deserve it. -Talking about Rage in the Cage, we've managed to book the Saddledome in Calgary, Alberta, Canada as the site for this year's event. Of course, it all depends on our contract. -I think if we do get a new contract, you'll see some of our superstars with a new look. A few people have decided to go with a makeover. That along with our hope to hire a number of new people, could help change the face of the WWA. -Rage in the Cage will be a great event I'm sure, but we have something very cool in the works for March Madness. I can't say anymore than that, but it will be one of a kind. -There are some cool items being produced for our new WWArehouse, I can't spill too much, but DVD box sets, CDs, t-shirts, and magazines are only a few things. -Just a quick note, our art and photography director works his tail off, with little credit. I think that needs to change. -Okay, whom did we sign? "Sky Flyin" Jimmi Zion has returned, along with his wife, Mikalia, who will be managing him. He was the first WWA Cruiserweight Champion and should really add to that division. The sky is the limit for Jimmi I think. That's it. I hope this really isn't the last TSR, but if it is, thank you all for reading and watching. The WWA lasted an unbelievable five years, I'm hoping for at least five more. From under the New York Yankees baseball cap, this is Joe Stock signing out... maybe for the last time.
