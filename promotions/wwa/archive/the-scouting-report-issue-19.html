The Scouting Report - Issue 19

24 Sep 2003

Bylined Josh Wilson - Director of Talent, in the fed's own kayfabe voice.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Greetings and salutations WWA fans, it’s your esteemed and valued Director of Talent back with all the dirt and opinions you can handle with a new Scouting Report. -As you may have noticed there have been some gaps between the reports and I have to apologize for that. Recently my laptop has been acting up and it seems everyone is too busy to help with the fixin’. However I know a Computer Tech who owes me a few favors and I’m now back and running. -We open with some news that affects the WWA as a whole and one of our biggest stars, Jackson Kraven. -After Jackson Kraven finished his match, a doctor examined his face and ordered him to stop wrestling to allow it to recover. The doctor recommended some minor surgery. -However as we well know Kraven isn’t know to adhere to the advice of doctors. Not only that most would consider Kraven an unstoppable monster. -So the real question is what does the former three-time World Champion think? Rest assured we will find out soon. -Now on to the happenings on Meltdown. -It seems that things have just not been going Chance’s way lately. After being attacked viciously by Joey K and Enigma we find out that Chance is behind the attempt to break up X-Corp. Now not only does he have the disdain of two dangerous men but now the Tag-Team champions as well. -Speaking of Joey K it turns out he was the one driving the pickup that tried to run over Chance those few months ago. I didn’t know he had a driver’s license. -Se7en beat James Curzon in an impressive match-up. I suppose the Conspiracy that haunts Curzon has struck again. -Oh and speaking of that match where was Mini-Curzon? That guy is the reason to watch the Admiral’s matches. He’s money. -Jimmi Zion and Justin Bourne faced the impressive team of the veteran Shaman and James Beam. Shaman didn’t seem to want Beam’s help as he took out his own partner. Maybe not the best move as it cost the man the match as Beam got hit with Zion’s Supernova. -Of course Shaman got his revenge. Bourne and Zion were both busted open by the end of the match. Just a reminder to never turn your back on the Horseman. -There was also a bit of animosity between Bourne and Zion. Everyone seemed to see this coming and in fact most want to see the two come to blows again. Wait and see fans, wait and see. -Well President Warr said he only accepts Denrol’s challenge for Rage in the Cage if Denrol challenged the man face to face. The thing is that Denrol is still banned from WWA television. Everyone knew that Denrol would eventually make his presence known but how? -By pretending to be the White Ninja of course. And Warr accepted as expected. Oh and We The People got fired. Guess that’s important. -The match with the Tag-Team Champions, X-Corp facing off against Havoc, The Brothers Delucci, and The Cell in a elimination match was what we expected, a all out brawl. -A DQ ended the match and we all know that any of these times could come out on top of the tag-team race. -In a great match Grunge unveiled his Oklahoma Metal Match. Grunge came out on top although he did have the University of Oklahoma’s Football team and Youth United acting as lumberjacks. So he did have help. That and he had chairs, that can’t hurt. -Still a brawl erupted after with a Chokeslam to Grunge and a LawBreaker to Adams. Somehow this is far from over. After all hatred doesn’t die, it only festers. -Speaking of Adams he’s been asking anybody who will listen for a shot at the vacated US Title. One has to wonder if President Warr will give him the shot. I think so but that doesn’t mean it will be on his terms. -In the Main Event of the night Kash and Tracer Bullet squared off against Ian Tense and Jackson Kraven. Tracer and Kash won thanks to a Kashmission on Tense. -Although maybe the credit goes to Kraven. After all it was he that hit Tense with the No More Tomorrows. It was an accident though with Kraven being temporarily blinded. -Now what about the next Meltdown? -Shaman will be facing Jimmi Zion in the continuation of their bitter rivalry. Both claim to be masters of the extreme but this match is billed as a regular match. Still I think we might see some hardcore action from these two Monday. -Venny LeTrav will be facing Se7en in a submission match. Venny has been itching to get a hold of the gothic madman since Se7en’s defeat over the Cajun several weeks ago. Both men are masters of offensive, especially submission wrestling. This will be one to watch. -Next Phobos, Tommy Delucci, and Leviathan will square off in a three-way dance. Phobos and Levi have shown in the past to not have a problem involving their partners in singles match-ups. Tommy had better have eyes in the back of his head. -Speaking of targets, Chance will have a big bull’s-eye on his back in his tag-team match. He’ll be joining Dave Harley to face Enigma and Joey K. Joey and Enigma seems to be on the same page and Harley seems to be on that page too. All of them want to hurt Chance. Chance is a lucky guy but is he that lucky? -In the main event of the night Kash will square off against his partner from last week Tracer Bullet. Make no mistake these two men hate each other. Both are extremely talented and have loyal fan followings. Who will win? Tune in to Meltdown to find out. Well until next time this is Josh Wilson. Be sure to watch Meltdown and Fiesta. You won’t be disappointed. Ciao.
