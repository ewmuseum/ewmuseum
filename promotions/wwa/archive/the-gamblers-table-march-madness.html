The Gamblers Table — March Madness

16 Aug 2003

Bylined "The Gambler" (Kevin/Vic Sage), the fed's own in-house prediction columnist persona.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
With WWA's March Madness just around the corner at the HSBC Arena in Buffalo, New York, it's time for another installment of the Gambler's Table. I'm Kevin Sage, former Las Vegas bookie, here to share my views and picks for the incredible lineup of matches at March Madness. With nine mind-blowing matches to cover, let's jump right in. The DeLuccis vs. Akiro and Motive Tag Team Number One Contender's Match One of the most talked about tag teams to come along in ages, the DeLucci brothers will take on Commissioner Raine's personal enforcers, Keru no Akiro and Evan "Loco" Motive. The winner of this match becomes the number one contender for the soon-to-be hotly contested Tag Team Titles. Akiro and Motive have proven their skills since dumping their masquerade as Mr. Cool and Bane Edwards, respectively. The DeLucci brothers have impressed a lot of people lately, this reporter included. With two consecutive victories over the team of Showtime and Curzon, the DeLuccis have earned this opportunity. My money says that not only are the DeLuccis destined to win this match, but also destined for greatness. James Beam vs. Jimmi Zion GenX Title Tables, Ladders, and Chairs When the Extreme and Cruiserweight titles were united into the GenX title, Jimmi Zion had the honor of becoming the first GenX champion of the WWA. His pride was cut short when James Beam managed to win the title from him. A change in Beam's outlook has accompanied his recent winning streak, including wild parties and questionable victories. If the previous two matches between these guys is any indication, this bout will shock and amaze us all. Jimmi Zion continues to live up to his "Sky Flyin'" moniker admirably, but Beam isn't far behind, having shown great prowess with both aerial and technical skills. Look for this match to be great from start to finish. Regardless of Beam's latest accomplishments, I'll have to place my bet on the determination of the WWA's first GenX champion, Jimmi Zion. Grunge vs. Joey K vs. Nick Adams vs. Venny LeTrav TV Title The WWA TV Title has always been a source of great matches, and heated contention. This four-way match at March Madness will be no different. The current champion, Grunge, has skyrocketed lately, winning the TV Title back at Rage in the Cage. His aerial style, coupled with his arsenal of submission moves, and his Lawbreaker finisher have served him well in his TV Title reign. Nick Adams has held the TV Title before, but seems more interested in taking on Grunge than in the title. In fact, word has it that Adams intends to shoot for bigger titles soon, perhaps the US Title or even the World Title. Venny LeTrav and Joey K heat up this match with a mutual disdain born from Joey K's attack on Venny to break up the American Luchadores. Joey K is quickly becoming a force to be reckoned within the TV Title race, with some of the best technical skills in the WWA today. Venny LeTrav has kept Joey's attention squarely focused by not allowing a single slight to go unanswered. All four of these men are incredible competitors, and they all deserve recognition, regardless of the outcome of this match. In the end though, I don't see Nick Adams having the necessary drive to give this match his full attention. He doesn't want it enough. If Venny LeTrav and Joey K are unable to look beyond each other, then they'll both lose out on the TV Title, leaving Grunge to pick up the win. That's why my bet goes with the Oklahoma native, Grunge. The Cell vs. X-Corp Tag Team Titles Hell in the Cell At Rage in the Cage, these two teams gave us a great match, filled with brutality and heart, in an attempt to settle their differences. Nothing was settled. In fact, their differences have continued to escalate, threatening to tear Fiesta apart week after week. Now, we include the Tag Team Titles, a steel cage, and throw the rules out the window. This match is set to blow the roof off of the arena. Lockdown and Leviathan have continued to terrorize Fiesta with unprovoked attacks and match interference. The Cell is perhaps one of the cruelest teams in the WWA, certainly living up to their tag line of "Brutally Truthful." In this respect, it may seem that the Cell has the advantage in this type of match, but X-Corp has proven unarguably that they can get extreme with the best. The versatility and sheer willpower of X-Corp have carried them far, despite the Cell's attacks. Mal Somers and Dave Harley will be coming to Buffalo for the fight of their lives, and I'm willing to bet that they'll walk away as the new Tag Team Champions. Havoc vs. The Veteranz The much-beloved Veteranz announced their impending retirement at the most recent Meltdown, but they have one more goal before they leave the squared circle forever; to stop the reign of terror that is Havoc. Havoc defeated the returning Veteranz, as well as the American Luchadores, at Rage in the Cage to win the tag titles. When the Veteranz took on the Cell to establish the number one contenders for the tag belts, Phobos, acting as the special guest referee, cheated the Vetz out of their victory. When the Cell got their chance at Havoc, the Veteranz repaid Havoc by costing them their titles. Since that time, Havoc has embarked on a campaign of terror, kidnapping and torturing Harley Trapper, and forcing Johnny Shrapnel's family to watch helplessly as Deimos systematically tried to cripple him. A win over the Veteranz would give Havoc the right to proclaim themselves as the greatest tag team in the history of the WWA, a designation currently claimed by the Vetz. Havoc are, without a doubt, two of the most dominant men ever to have found their way into the tag ranks, but the Veteranz feel nothing but hate for Havoc, and they'll bring everything in their legendary arsenal to bear on the so-called gods of war. My money goes with tradition; the Vetz will go out on a high note. Chance vs. Denrol This feud started shortly before this year's epic Rage in the Cage, when Chance managed to get a DQ win over the veteran Denrol. Apparently disgusted by the lack of respect that some of the young talent displays toward the more established competitors of the WWA, as well as their perceived lack of work ethic, Denrol appears to have chosen Chance to make an example of. Unfortunately, Chance has managed to escape every "lesson" that Denrol has thrown his way, until March Madness that is. Denrol finds himself as the smaller man in nearly all of his WWA matches, but this time he'll be in unfamiliar territory against an opponent who is much smaller and lighter. No one can deny that Denrol is the WWA franchise, a man whose name has come to be synonymous with the WWA. His technical skills and experience are nearly unmatched in the entire wrestling world. On the other hand, Chance is one of the most skilled acrobats that the wrestling world has ever seen, combining speed and death-defying aerial attacks into a powerful arsenal that few could actually handle. This will be a close one, folks. The Franchise versus the Future. In this match, I'll be betting on tradition to overtake luck, I'll be betting on Denrol. Tracer Bullet vs. Enigma Enigma made a dangerous enemy when he threatened Tracer's wife at RITC to force Tracer to submit in his I Quit match against Shane Dorian. This act of extortion, and the ensuing attacks from Enigma on Dorian's behalf, has haunted Tracer since. Since being expelled from the House of Pain, and seeing his partner, Shane Dorian, fired from the WWA, Enigma has found plenty of time to focus his anger on the Cajun detective. Of course, Enigma has Tracer's full attention, as well. With everything that's happened, a few weeks ago Tracer enacted a little brutality of his own on Enigma in a shocking attack that left us all wondering if Tracer had finally snapped. Now, they'll meet in an I Quit match at March Madness. Tracer Bullet is a legend in this sport, a phenomenal ring general and martial artist, with a string of accomplishments longer than this article. Enigma is no less impressive, as one of the premier aerialists of the WWA, having won the TV Title, the Cruiserweight Title, and the Extreme Title twice. Again, we are forced to choose between experience and youth. This time around, my money says that Tracer's new motto, No Regrets, will lead him to victory. Vik Ventressa vs. Jackson Kraven Since Vik Ventressa's return to the WWA back at Rage in the Cage, his sole purpose, his single goal, has been to defeat Jackson Kraven. Starting with the mysterious letters signed with the anagram Kevin Straves, to recently leading Kraven into a trap that resulted in Kraven's arrest, Ventressa has lived up to his stated intent, becoming a serious thorn in Kraven's side. Needless to say, Ventressa is playing with fire. Jackson Kraven is probably the most feared man in all of the WWA, and has remained so for quite some time. He is a three-time former WWA World Champion holder. Ventressa brings intelligence and flair to the ring, and his confidence has served him well many times in the past. But, tonight I believe that he'll learn a little humility. Vik Ventressa is a great competitor, but I'm betting that Jackson Kraven stops him in his tracks. WWA World Champion Ian Tense vs. Kash vs. Damian Thorne WWA World Title The WWA World Champion, Ian Tense, is perhaps one of the most scheming and conniving men in the history of the WWA. Damian Thorne, the man who forcibly ripped the House of Pain from the clutches of Randy Cross, and who has earned his shot at the World Title with viciousness and ruthlessness, recently had his US Title stripped from him on Meltdown. The mad occultist, former US Champion, Kash, has also earned his shot at the World Title, at the same time that his hated nemesis, Thorne, did. These three men, perhaps the most impressive collection of talent in the WWA today, the biggest current names that are on everyone's lips, will meet in the squared circle to do battle for the WWA World Title. What words could possibly do justice to the match that we will witness on the evening of March 30th? Without a doubt, this may be the most exciting main event of the year. We've seen the lengths to which all three of these men are willing to go. We've seen the machinations of Ian Tense come to fruition over and over. We've seen Damian Thorne do terrible damage to his enemies. We saw Kash come out of Global Warfare victorious, his head held high. We saw the RITC match where Thorne and Kash nearly ended each other's careers. Not a single one of these men are willing to back down. They will fight tooth and nail, until their bodies literally stop dropping breath. If I were a smart man, I wouldn't bet on this match at all, because it is simply too close to call. Unfortunately, I like the thrill. When it comes down to it, Thorne and Kash know each other. They know each other's style, and how they're likely to react to a number of situations. I don't see either of them getting the upper hand on the other. If past performance is any indication, Ian Tense has a plan in mind, something devious. That deviousness is why I'll put my money on Ian Tense retaining the World Title. This PPV event is filled with some of the biggest names in the sport; in some of the most heated matches we've seen on WWA programming. You do not want to miss a moment of this soon-to-be historic night. And that's brings to a close, the Gambler's Table for March Madness 2003. Be sure to come back in May, when the WWA will have revealed the name of their new May Pay-Per-View extravaganza. In the meantime, remember the words of R.E. Shay: "Depend on the rabbit's foot if you will, but remember it didn't work for the rabbit." This is Kevin Sage. See you in two months. Design and Graphics © World Wrestling Alliance 2003
