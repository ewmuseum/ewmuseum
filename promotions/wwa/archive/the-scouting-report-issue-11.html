The Scouting Report - Issue 11

21 Jan 2004

Bylined Joe Stock - Director of Talent, in the fed's own kayfabe voice.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It's good to be back. Florida was nice, but I certainly missed the WWA. I hope everyone enjoyed their holidays, no matter which one they celebrated. I don't have too much news to give this week, mostly because our PTC reporter is doing such a great job, so this will be more my opinions on various subjects than past columns. -A quick reminder than all opinion given are my own and not the official position of the WWA as a whole. Also I'm a bit funky still from a virus, so this won't be one of my better columns. -Talking about the PTC, let me start with that. The WWA has been part of the PTC for over a month and I have formed an opinion of the group in that time. My opinion is that the PTC is a waste of our time. The WWA started as part of an inter-fed or rather the WWA joined an inter-fed about a month after it opened and renamed itself Northeastern Championship Wrestling. The only people who joined NCW during that time and made an impact were Shaman and Denrol. Only once NCW became the WWA did it really take off. Even when we became an inter-fed ourselves, it didn't work out well. I don't really think the third time will be the charm. But I'm willing to be wrong. -I would be proud that we quickly became a network federation, but we never really had any competition. A lot was made of UBC Vs WWA, but it really was always one-sided. So what are the benefits of being a network fed? Almost nothing I see. I haven't noticed an increase in applications. I do think we are a bit more likely to sign free agents, but that's about it. -As for our fellow network federations, I thought the first set of ratings with us involved was bogus. We lost 1 point simply because we lack what? One column? As for the show ratings, I think it says something when our secondary show comes within .7 of a point of taking the lead from LWO's two shows. DWO has a PPV and we came within of half a point of beating them. -But enough on the PTC. Matt Warr is happy we are network and it's his opinion that sets policy with the WWA. -In WWA news, we are happy to have signed a number of new stars. Welcome Leviathan, Lockdown, "The Devious One" Bane Edwards, James Curzon, and The Cool Guy to the federation. Best of luck to them all. -Time for my thoughts on Meltdown. -Good Handicap Match, and I like the angle with the Extreme and Cruiserweight Titles. Let's hopefully get a good match at Rage in the Cage out of this. -Havoc simply have dominated the tag team ranks. I'm not sure how long the American Luchadores can play games to keep the belts. The big question is what is left for Havoc after they win the belts once again. -Tracer Bullet and Shane Dorian are putting together a good feud and I can't wait for their big match at Rage in the Cage. Tracer will want some serious revenge after the beating he took on Meltdown. -Very nice technical wrestling match between Thorne and Kash. That match showed why the WWA has the best wrestlers in the business today. -Good main event and did a great job advancing our World Title scene. Ian tense will not have an easy time keeping his belt at Rage in the Cage. -As for the next meltdown, we have a good lineup coming up in my opinion. The main event promises to involve two big angles at the least. -Who will be Bullet's and Dorian's partners? I'm not in the know, but I'm guessing it'll be Kash and Damian Thorne. I just wouldn't bet money on it. -Jarod Crow is making a one time appearance to tag up with Chance, I think he might impress us. I wonder how well Enigma and Zion will work together. -I expect Payne to retain his TV Title, but you never know. Grunge could surprise us. That's it for now, I promise next week to have a better column. From under the New York Yankees baseball cap, this is Joe Stock signing out.
