WWA — 7 Mar 1999

7 Mar 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Sunday March 7,1999 The screen opens up to the WWA banner. The camera shows the outside of the complex, revealing it to be the Bryce Jordan Center. Yellow and red pyrotechnics explode down the ramp and out of the four ringposts. The WWA banner fades in again and is instantly stamped with the word "Extreme"! The screen fades into the arena where thousands of fans are jumping up and down and screaming. As the camera pans over the audience signs such as "Say It Ain't So, Randy!", "Outlaw is Immortal!", and "HOP SUCKS!", are seen. The camera makes it's way to the announcers table where Eric Pembrook, Juan Guevesos, and John Douglass are sitting. Eric : Hello, and welcome ladies and gentleman to another exciting edition of Sunday Night Extreme! I'm your host Eric Pembrook and with me as always are Juan Guevesos and John Douglass. John : Tonight we've got a US Title defense along with first round action in the March Madness Tournament which has sixty-four contestants in it and the winner will be decided at March Madness Pay-Per-View. Juan : Enough talk boys, let's get to the ring and see one of the best luchadors around, El Porto Spidero! Gary Trudeau : Welcome ladies and gentlemen to WWA Extreme!! The first match of the evening is first round action in the tournament of 64 to see who gets a World Title shot! The matches tonight are from the Southern region of the tournament brackets. The first match of the night is set for one fall with a thirty minute time limit. First, making his way to the ring, the #2 seed in the Southern bracket, representing CPE.....El Porto Spidero!!! "Ratamahatta" by Sepultura begins to blast over the PA system. The tribal drums begin to pick up in volume and tempo and finally El Porto Spidero steps out through the curtain with his bright red cape flowing behind him. He stands at the entranceway for a while looking at the ring and the fans as they loudly boo him in his CPE shirt. He nods with acceptance and a large smile grows across his face. Out of no where Spidero bursts into a run and charges into the ring. He steps into the center and jumps up into the air with both arms raised. Gary Trudeau : And his opponent, the #15 seed representing HOP.....Mr. Business!!! Mr. Business walks out from back stage to the sounds of "Look Who's Back" by House of Pain. He enters the ring and stretches out. Eric : The following match up is the #2 seed El Porto Spidero vs. the #15 seed Mr. Business. With the way this tournament is going you never know who will pull off an upset. Juan : I think that I am going to go with Spidero on this one because that little Mexican has some amazing moves. He is really one of the best luchadors in all of professional wrestling. John : Well here is the start of the match as Mr. Business uses his size to slam Spidero to the ground with a sharp roundhouse. Eric : Spidero is up in a flash and uses his speed to get around Mr. Business and lock in a Crucifix. 1,... and only a one count as Spidero went for the early pin in this match. Juan : Spidero is up quickly again and nails Mr. Business with a huge brainbuster. Spidero then comes off the ropes and lands a nice bulldog on Mr. Business. Eric : Mr. Business does not seem to be able to catch up with this luchador because Spidero seems to be running Mr. Business into the ground. John : Spidero now whips Mr. Business into the ropes and follows up with a huge hurricanrana. Mr. Business is lying on the ground on the brink of being knocked out. Juan : Spidero slides out of the ring and grabs a chair. He sets it up in the ring and launches himself into a huge Triple Jump Moosault. John : Spidero is just dominating this match. Everyone should have just penciled in Spidero to win this match because he is kicking Mr. Business's ass. Juan : Spidero uses the chair to his advantage as he slides it under Mr. Business face, and then climbs the ropes. Huge leg drop on to Mr. Business. John : Some thought that Spidero might have used his finisher on that position there, but I guess he justs wants to add more pain. Eric : Spidero kicks the chair out of the ring. Wait, it looks as if Mr. Business is starting to stand up. Spidero climbs the ropes while Mr. Business is stumbling aroung looking for Spidero. Juan : Shooting Star Press by Spidero and Mr. Business is once again on the mat not knowing where he is. Wait...Spidero is looking into the crowd. Wait we are hearing a chant of some sort forming from some guy in the crowd. Crowd : SPIDERO, SPIDERO, SPIDERO, SPIDERO, SPIDERO, SPIDERO... Eric : That looks like it is Albatross causing that ruckus in the crowd. Juan : Spidero is on the top ropes, and I think that he is going to finish Mr. Business off this time. And there it is ladies and gentlemen. The Cross of Fire which is really a 540 leg drop from the top ropes. Eric : Spidero with the cover. 1,2,3... and there is a pin for Spidero. A very impressive tune-up for whomever his opponent might be. John : As Spidero leaves the ring, Albatross enters the ring and starts pounding on Mr. Business. Spidero is on the ramp, and is leaving the dirty work to Albatross. Wait.. there is someone in the crowd. Eric : It is "Wild Thing" Billy Coleman. Coleman picks up the chairas he is entering the ring. Coleman slams the chair into the back of Albatross's head. Albatross is out cold. Juan : Coleman is setting the chair up beside a turnbuckle. Coleman has Albatross up on the top turnbuckle, and... a massive fall-away DDT. Albatross's head really smacked that chair. John : Coleman up on the top rope again, and the big man hits a huge moonsault. Coleman just performed his patented Deuces Are Wild finisher on the dazed Albatross. He might need medical help. Eric : Spidero just turned around and saw the carnage that Coleman just did to Albatross. Coleman waves and runs into his fans. Spidero comes back down and directs the medics to the fallen Albatross. John : What a great match to get things started here on Extreme! Eric : Let's not waste any time and get to the next match up. This next match will put a rookie against a veteran here in the WWA. Let's go to Gary with the ring introductions.... Gary Trudeau : The next match is also March Madness action. First, coming to the ring, the #3 seed in the South bracket, representing the Four Horsemen.....Bob!!! Bob walks to the ring followed closely by Bob's Driver. "Bob's Theme" plays over the PA. Bob is wearing a pink dress. He enters the ring and awaits his opponent. Gary Trudeau : And his opponent, in his first match here in the WWA, the #14 seed in the South bracket....Lance Crash!!! The arena goes quiet as a plane is heard screeching through the sky. It's tone becomes higher and higher until at last the noise of it crashing into the hard earth is heard. As it crashes, the sounds of "Faith" by Limp Bizkit. Lance Crash comes out wearing red and yellow and his short blonde hair is spiked up. He sprints to the ring and slides in. Eric : This match should be an interesting one, eh Juan? Juan : Yes Eric. BIG number 3 vs. little number 14....literally. John : I don't think that Bob will have many problems putting this youngster away. Eric : The two lock up in the center of the ring as the bell rings. John : Bob grabs ahold of Crash by the neck and throws him hard into the ropes. Juan : Bob raises both hands and does a little dance for the crowd in his pretty pink dress brought to you by Kristopher Kasanova. Eric : Bob over to Crash and grabs him by the neck. John : Chokeslam by Bob, delivering the back of Lance's head into the mat hard! Eric : Bob off the ropes, cross body pin! 1...2... 2 count only! Juan : Much to Bob's disbelief there, Crash kicked out just in time. John : Crash got lucky there, Bob won't let that happen again. Eric : Bob off the ropes again, attempted a clothes but misses... John : Crash gaining speed off the ropes, connects with a spinning heel kick to the front of Bob's face. Juan : That'll set the big guy down in a hurry. Bob getting back up to his feet. Eric : Crash over to Bob and kicks him in the gut, setting him up for a hurricanranna! John : That was an ugly hurricanranna, but it got the job done nevertheless. Eric : Bob is exiting the ring now?! What's this? Juan : Crash to the outside, but perhaps that wasn't such a good idea, Bob now has a chair! Eric : BIG CHAIR SHOT to the head of Crash! John : Fans! Only here in the WWA could you see a 7 foot monster, in a pink frilly dress, absolutely clober someone with a chair that hard! Eric : Haha. Good point John! Bob does some more dancing for the crowd and points his attention back towards Crash. Juan : Bob rears back and lets Crash have another chair shot to his right shoulder. John : Bob continues this horrible dance of his for the crowd. Eric : The crowd doesn't really know how to react to this Bob character, he was such a tough guy before, but now he's wearing a dress! Juan : Yeah, but he seems to be having fun with it anyhow. John : That's cause the man doesn't know any better! Eric : Crash is stirring around, but Bob is still a dancin fool. Juan : Crash over to Bob, from behind...LOW BLOW! John : The fans are really laughing at this idiot now. Eric : Crash grabs the chair from Bob and whacks him in the back of his left knee. Juan : With Bob crouching over, that move just enabled Crash to connect with Bob's forehead with a LOUD chair shot. John : Crash pushes Bob into the ring and throws the chair in too. Eric : Bob seems to be gaining some momentum back. Juan : Crash in the ring now, and goes for the chair; however, Bob got to him first with a Bob's clothesline. John : Bob is now trying to lock on Bob's move. Eric : If Bob hits this move, it will all be over. Juan : Wait a minute! That chair just grabbed Bob's attention again. Eric : Bob over to the chair slowly, giving Crash just enough time gain some much needed energy back. John : Bob coming after Crash with the chair raised to his chest... Eric : SUPERKICK BY CRASH!!! That just sent that steel chair straight into the nose of Bob! Juan : Crash wasting no time, wraps the leg! 1...2...3!!! Bob kicked out one second too late! John : Crash the winner here with an upset victory over Bob!!! Eric : The ref raises Lance Crash's hand for the win, Crash is looking like he gave it his all in this match...Barely able to stand up! Juan : We will be definitely be hearing more things from this newcomer in the future. John : There you have it! Lance Crash with an upset victory over Bob. Eric : Don't leave, we'll be right back after this! After a few, pointless commercials the camera fades back in to the announcer's booth. Eric : Welcome back ladies and gentlemen. What a first two matches we've had tonight!! We've seen an impressive win by Spidero of the CPE and a rookie, Lance Crash take out a veteran in Bob. Juan : And there's a lot more in store for the fans here tonight. Man, if Crash can win, this tourney is wide open for the taking! John : Let's go to Gary with the intros... Gary Trudeau : The next match of the evening pits the #6 seed against the #11 seed of the Southern bracket. First, coming to us from the Midevel Times.... Sir Micheal!!! Sir Michael moves down to the ring with his breastplate on and his shield. Lady Rachel is on his right hand side. He assists her into the ring and then leaps over the top rope, into the ring. He keeps his breastplate on and puts the shield in his corner. Lady Rachel gives him some final instructions and then exits the ring. Gary Trudeau : And his opponent, the #6 seed....From the Frayed End of Sanity... The Mimic!!! The arena goes black as the intro to "Save Yourself" by Stabbing Westward starts over the PA. Green laser lights start shooting around the arena. The music starts on the third verse of the song as the laser lights concentrate in the ring. The laser lights form the words: Sir Micheal. The words start to rotate as the musician says "You Can't even save YOURSELF". Then the Jumbotron bursts into action showing Sir Micheal executing the Joust and getting the pin on Apocalypse. Then it shows Sir Micheal sitting in his library... The jumbotron focuses on the face of Sir Micheal. And again the musician says "Save Yourself...Self...Self"... "Self" echoing into nothingness as the music stops and the lights come with the Mimic standing in the ring. John : Interesting ring entrance there from the Mimic. Eric : Michael salutes the Mimic. Nice show of sportsmanship from the two. Juan : Michael and Mimic lock up and it's the knight with early advatange with a belly-to-belly suplex. Nicely done. Eric : Michael and the Mimic locking up again this time Mimic takes him down with a hip toss. John : The Mimic following it up quickly with some elbow drops to Michael's knee. Juan : Michael trying to get to his feet, grabs Mimic, and sends him to the ropes with an Irish whip. John : Wow nice tilt-a-whirl backbreaker by Michael. Juan : Michael going to the ropes...Moonsault! Nice! I like this guys style. Eric : Michael picking up Mimic and nails a hurricanrana! Some nice high flying moves from the knight! John : You might say Michael is going medivial on the Mimic! Eric : John that was really lame! John : I try. Juan : Sir Michael definatly has the early advantage here. Mimic's on the ground in some pain right now. Michael climbing the ropes. Eric : Michael with a diving headbutt!!! John : NO!!!! Mimic rolls out of the way just in time! Michael lands right on his face that has got to hurt! Juan : The Mimic trying to slow things down a bit now as he locks on a boston crab. Eric : He let's go of that hold and quickly locks on side headlock. John : Man this is boring. I thought this show was called Extreme? Eric : It may be boring John, but it's definatly a good strategy by the Mimic to try to keep Michael grounded. Juan : Mimic let's Michael up, and he nails a perfect drop kick! You can't keep a good cruiser weight down baby! Eric : Michael let's him have it with a sunset flip! Juan : Doesn't look like he's grounded just yet. John : Michael climbing to the top again, and hits a nice frog splash. Eric : Mimic might be in trouble as Michael set's him up for what could be a powerbomb... but Mimic reverses it into a hurricanrana. John : The Mimic back to the submission holds this time locking on a camel clutch! Juan : Michael looks to be fading. He's got to make it to the ropes or he's done for! Eric : Uh oh! Here comes Lady Rachael! She's got Michael's shield!! John : Yes! Rachael just nailed Mimic with the shield forcing him to drop the hold! Juan : Rachael tosses Michael his shield as she climbs back out on to the apron. Eric : And Michael making good use of it as he goes for the Mimic head... John : NO! The Mimic with a beautiful drop kick to the shield sending it right in to Michael's face! I love it! Juan : Mimic looks at the shield and tosses it out of the ring. John : Ah man! Just when this was getting good! No Mimic back to another submission hold going for a ankle lock. Eric : Michael writhing in pain. I don't think he's going to be high flying anymore in this match. Sorry Juan. Juan : The Mimic let's up though and drags Michael to his feet. Michael is really hobbling. Favoring one lag drastically. John : The Mimic executes a nice powerslam, and follows it up with another submission hold. Eric : This time he's using a bear hug. Mimic is obviously trying to slow this down again. John : Great. Juan : I know the feeling big guy. Mimic picks up Michael and nails a reverse DDT! Eric : Or should we say Equilizer? Perhaps Scorpion has been traing the Mimic on the side? John : In any case the Mimic is climbing to the top ropes. Juan : Michael trying to get to his feet... Eric : And just as he does Mimic nails him with the Joust! Flying spear! John : Mimic going for the cover...1...2...3! Juan : Well the Mimic get's another win by using his opponents own finisher, and by using some great strategy. Eric : No sense in wasting time. We've got one more tournament match for you here tonight! Let's go to Gary in the ring... The camera flashes to Gary in the ring armed with his microphone. Gary Trudeau : First, making his way to the ring, the #7 seed....Ken Steele!!! "More Human Than Human" by White Zombie erupts over the loud speakers. Ken Steele burst through the curtains. He's kind of in a way jogging down to the ring. He's looking very phyced for this match. Gary Trudeau : And his opponent, the #10 seed....Scott Haggerty!!! Wiser Time" by Black Crowes begins to play over the PA System. The camera is focused on the curtain at the top of the entrance ramp. A few seconds later, "The Bad Dog" Scott Haggarty walks through, and down to the ring. Hitting a couple fans hands on the way. He enters the ring, and is ready for his opponent. Eric : Well, ladies and Gentlemen. I hope you're ready for this match. I sure am. I'm glad to see the return of Ken Steele in the ring. Juan : But don't you think the ring rust will show? John : Of course it will. Eric : Remember folks, the winner of this match goes on to the second round. (ding...ding...ding..) John : Both men seem ready for this match. Juan : Oh, look at that. Just after the bell rang, Ken Steele launched forward and took Scott Hggarty down with a single leg take down. He followed it up with a leg drop. John : Well, for somebody being out as long as him, you think there would be a little rust. Juan : There is, look at him show boating.. Ken stands up and cheers himself on. Not paying much attention to his opponent. Scott quickly gets to his feet, and drops kicks Ken in the back of the head. Eric : Ken goes tumbling over and out. He's obviously forgot to never turn his back on anyone. Scott suddenly leaps the top rope and lands on Ken outside the ring. Juan : Damn, I love this! John : Scott's pulling him back up. Eric : You got to love that clotheline. John : Boy, Ken is getting clobbered. Scott pulls Ken back to his feet and sends him into the ring post head first. Eric : Ken is busted wide open, folks. John : I didn't know this was a. Extreme Match. Eric : It isn't, the ref is at 7 on the count. 8. Scott rolls Ken back inside, and re-enters himself as well. John : Scott pulling Ken up again. Sent for the ride, Oh! Eric : Beutiful back body drop, by Scott. Juan : Ken's really feeling that move. Scott is pulling Ken back up to his feet. Scott looks to be attempting a power bomb. Eric : Oh my god! Some how Ken managed to roll up and over, ending up behind Scott. Juan : Drop kick to Scott's knee. Scotty is down. Eric : Ken practically making Scott eat his fist. Look at this young man go. John : The former T.V. Champ is really coming back. Scott is sent for the ride, but he reverses it. He clothelines Ken. Eric : Oh, and there's a counter of his own by Scott. Juan : Hey man, what's this? A masked man leaps the steele gaurd rail and stands at ring side. John : Who the hell is that, and what does he want? Eric : Back in the ring, Ken Steele is sent for the ride. What the hell is that guy doing?! The masked man pulled down the top rope, and Ken Steele came tumbling out. Scott is standing in the ring laughing. The masked man doesn't like that, and enters the ring. Eric : Oh my god, Scott and this Masked man are standing nose to nose. Ken Steele gets to his feet, and climbs the turnbuckle. Juan : Fly baby, fly! John : Ken's going to take them both out. Ken leaps... Ken Steele leaps off the turnbuckle, and both Scott and the masked man catch him in the air by his neck. Eric : Oh my god, double choke slam! Come on ref, DQ him! John : The damn ref is just watching this. Not really trying to stop the assault on Ken Steele. The masked man pulls Ken up, and Power Bombs him. He leaves the ring, and disappears through the crowd. Eric : The damage has been done. Scott is not complaining either. A basically free win for Scott here tonight. John : He's not done though. Choke Slam! Ken is out cold! Now he's pinning the man. Eric : 1 ...2...3. No surprise. Scott Haggarty gets the win. Give the assist to that masked man. Juan : What a cheap win. Eric : Nevertheless, it's a win! We're going to commercial, we'll be right back! A few utterly repulsive Vagisil commercials play and the screen fades back into the WWA's announcer's booth. Eric : Ladies and gentlemen, a very interesting happening went down during commercial. Check this out. The scene fades in to Ken Steele walking out of the arena with duffel bag over his shoulder. President Mark Harrington is close behind following Steele. Harrington : Ken, listen man, I know you got screwed, I'll make it up to you! Ken Steele : Forget it, Mark! My dad warned me about the way you run things. I'm fed up with this fed. I QUIT!!! Harrington continues pleading with Ken Steele as he jumps into a car and races off. Harrington walks back into the arena looking very upset. Eric : There you have it folks, Ken Steele is fed up with the WWA and has resigned! Jesus, this has been one controversial night. Can it get any worse, let's find out. We've got the big US Title match next putting Crosscheck against the champ, Denrol. Give it to us Gary.... Gary Trudeau : Ladies and Gentlemen... The main Event of the evening will be for the United States Heavyweight Championship. It will be one fall and it will be television time remaining. Introducing first... The challenger coming to us by way of Toronto, Canada and weighing in at 275 pounds, we have Crosscheck! "Gonna Fly Now," the Rocky theme song plays over the PA as Crosscheck walks down the ring with actor Sylvester Stallone and brings a stretcher and hockey stick down to the ring with him. Crosscheck is wearing a Canadian Olympic jersey and a Canadian flag over his shoulders. Gary Trudeau : And his opponent, he is the WWA United States Champion. He represents Cold Pain Enterprises...Denrol! The lights dim as static fills the arena's PA System, than "Slow Ride" by Foghat starts to play. The fans erupt with boos and wait for D.E.N.R.O.L. and gang to show up. But nothing. Finally the lights come up and Crosscheck is being held by Lantern, Spidero, and a Masked Man all wearing gray trenchcoats. D.E.N.R.O.L. is standing in a corner, with a black trenchoat on. The ref is yelling at the CPE members, but they don't react. D.E.N.R.O.L. grabs a mic. D.E.N.R.O.L. : Crosscheck, how do you like your big chance to win it all and make yourself a star? (D.E.N.R.O.L. laughs) You thought you had it all wrapped up, the match being on Extreme and all, knowing I'm the farthest thing from a hardcore wrestler. You can be a nut and kill yourself by jumping off cages and balconies. Sorry, I'm a wrestler, not a wannabe. But don't worry Crosscheck, you'll get your shot. I made that promise. But it won't be tonight. It'll be in the second round of the World WRESTLING Alliance Title Tourney. can't wait. But I did make apromise that you'll leave on a stretcher and you were nice enough to bring one. Boys! CPE drags Crosscheck over to the strecher and straps him into it. He keeps fighting, but it's useless. They push the strecher away. D.E.N.R.O.L. : See you later, alligator. I guess now, since my oppent cannot countine the match, your winner and stil Canadian Champion... D.E.N.R.O.L.!!! He smiles as the fans boos loud as they can and start throwing garbage into the ring. D.E.N.R.O.L. : Pieces of trash throwing trash, how ironic. He points to a fan in the crowd. D.E.N.R.O.L. : Why look, Yokoazuna is back! (He laughs and points to another fan.) Damn, if I was that ugly, I would have myself arrested for being an eyesore. He laughs again and start strutting around the ring. Suddenly the fans' boos turn to cheers. D.E.N.R.O.L. looks confused, he doesn't see Gregory O'Hara coming through the crowd. O'Hara slides into the ring and D.E.N.R.O.L. takes the low road, running the hell out of there and to the back as fast as his legs can take him. "Youth Gone Wild" by Skid Row starts playing over the speakers. O'Hara picks up D.E.N.R.O.L.'s droped mic and his United States Title. O'Hara : Oh Denny, I think you lost something. O'Hara holds up the US Title as the camera flashes over to D.E.N.R.O.L., who's mad at hell and jawing at O'Hara, but doesn't muster up the courage to go back and get his belt. Eric : We are out of time folks... See you next week! John : And remember, if you're not mean, you're not extreme!!!
